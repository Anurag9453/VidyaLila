# Topic 2: Paani Mein Ions: H⁺(aq), OH⁻(aq), Dilution aur Strength

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki chahe hydrochloric acid (HCl) ho, sulphuric acid (H₂SO₄) ho, ya nimbu ka citric acid — **sabhi acids** blue litmus ko laal karte hain, methyl orange ko laal karte hain, aur phenolphthalein ko rangheen rakhte hain. Isi tarah NaOH, KOH aur Ca(OH)₂ jaise **sabhi bases** red litmus ko neela aur phenolphthalein ko gulabi kar dete hain. Ab sochne wali baat hai: in sab acids ke andar **aisi kaun-si ek cheez saanjhi (common) hai** jiske kaaran sab ek jaisa vyavhaar karte hain? Aur kya bina paani ke bhi koi acid apna asar dikha sakta hai? Aaj hum isi raaz ko kholenge.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Hydrogen ion, H⁺(aq)** | Paani mein acid ke tootne se nikalne wala dhan-aveshit (positive) hydrogen ion | HCl(aq) → H⁺(aq) + Cl⁻(aq) |
| **Hydronium ion, H₃O⁺** | Jab akela H⁺ ion paani ke molecule (H₂O) se jud jaata hai to banne wala ion | H⁺ + H₂O → H₃O⁺ |
| **Hydroxide ion, OH⁻(aq)** | Paani mein base ke ghulne par milne wala rin-aveshit (negative) ion | NaOH(aq) → Na⁺(aq) + OH⁻(aq) |
| **Alkali (kshaar)** | Wo base jo **paani mein ghul jaata hai** (water-soluble base) | NaOH, KOH, Ca(OH)₂ (thoda), NH₄OH |
| **Ionisation / Dissociation** | Paani mein ghulne par compound ka apne ions (+ aur −) mein alag ho jaana | HNO₃(aq) → H⁺(aq) + NO₃⁻(aq) |
| **Strong acid (prabal aml)** | Wo acid jo paani mein **lagbhag poora (100%)** toot kar dher saare H⁺(aq) ions deta hai | HCl, H₂SO₄, HNO₃ |
| **Weak acid (durbal aml)** | Wo acid jiske paani mein **bahut thode se molecules** hi toot kar H⁺(aq) ions dete hain | CH₃COOH (acetic acid), H₂CO₃, citric acid |
| **Basicity of an acid** | Ek acid ke **1 molecule** se paani mein milne wale ziyada-se-ziyada replaceable H⁺ ions ki ginti | HCl = 1 (monobasic), H₂SO₄ = 2 (dibasic), H₃PO₄ = 3 (tribasic) |
| **Acidity of a base** | Ek base ke **1 formula unit** se milne wale replaceable OH⁻ ions ki ginti | NaOH = 1 (monoacidic), Ca(OH)₂ = 2 (diacidic) |
| **Dilution (tanukaran)** | Acid ya base mein paani milakar ikai aayatan (per unit volume) mein ions ki ginti kam karna | Concentrated H₂SO₄ ko paani mein dheere-dheere milana |

## Part A: Sabhi Acids mein kya common hai? H⁺(aq) aur Hydronium Ion (H₃O⁺)

Agar hum alag-alag acids ke formulas dekhein — HCl, H₂SO₄, HNO₃, CH₃COOH — to sab mein **Hydrogen (H)** dikhta hai. Par rukna! Hydrogen to **glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆)** aur **alcohol (C₂H₅OH)** mein bhi hai! Kya glucose aur alcohol bhi acid hain? Chalo ek school-lab demo se parakhte hain.

> **Activity 2.1 (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo): Kaun bijli chalata hai — Acid ya Glucose?**
> Class 9 ke [chemical-bonding-part4.md](../../../class9/chemistry/chemical-bonding/chemical-bonding-part4.md) ki tarah teacher ek beaker mein do graphite rods lagate hain aur unhe **6 V ki battery**, switch aur ek chhote **bulb (ya LED)** se jodte hain *(kabhi bhi ghar ke 220 V mains plug se nahi!)*.
> 1. Pehle beaker mein baari-baari se **dilute HCl** aur **dilute H₂SO₄** daal kar switch ON karte hain: **Bulb chamak uthta hai!**
> 2. Ab beaker dho kar usmein **glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆) ka ghol** aur phir **alcohol (C₂H₅OH) ka ghol** daalte hain: **Bulb bilkul nahi jalta!** (Aur na hi ye dono blue litmus ko laal karte hain.)

```
     BEAKER 1: Dilute HCl(aq)               BEAKER 2: Glucose C₆H₁₂O₆(aq)

        +---[6V Battery]---( * )               +---[6V Battery]---(   )
        :                  BULB JALA!          :                  BULB BHUJA
     +--+--+                                +--+--+
     :  :  :                                :  :  :
     : |:  :|                               : |:  :|
     :  H⁺ Cl⁻                              :  C₆H₁₂O₆ molecules
     :  Cl⁻ H⁺                              :  (koi ions nahi bane!)
     +--------+                             +--------+
     Ions tair rahe hain -> current chala   Sirf neutral molecules -> current nahi
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Do beakers mein 6 V battery aur bulb se chalakta (conductivity) ki jaanch. Baayen beaker mein dilute HCl paani mein toot kar H⁺(aq) aur Cl⁻(aq) ions banata hai, isliye bulb jalta hai. Daayen beaker mein glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆) paani mein ghulta to hai, par toot kar H⁺ ions nahi deta, isliye bulb nahi jalta.

**Nateeja:** Sirf formula mein H hona kisi cheez ko acid nahi banata! **Acid wo hai jo paani mein ghul kar Hydrogen ions, H⁺(aq), paida kare:**

- HCl(aq) → H⁺(aq) + Cl⁻(aq)
- HNO₃(aq) → H⁺(aq) + NO₃⁻(aq)
- H₂SO₄(aq) → 2H⁺(aq) + SO₄²⁻(aq)
- CH₃COOH(aq) ⇌ H⁺(aq) + CH₃COO⁻(aq)

Yahi **H⁺(aq) ion** har acid ki asali pehchaan hai! Glucose aur alcohol mein hydrogen covalent bond se itni mazbooti se bandha hota hai ki paani mein H⁺ ban kar alag nahi hota, isliye unmein hydrogen hote hue bhi we **acid nahi hain**.

### Paani ke bina Acid kaam kyun nahi karta? (Dry HCl vs Wet Litmus)

Ab Palak ke sawaal par aate hain: kya sookhi HCl gas neele litmus ko laal karegi?

> **Activity 2.2 (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo): Sookhi HCl gas aur geela litmus.**
> Teacher ek saaf, sookhi test tube mein thoda namak (NaCl) lete hain aur usmein dropper se thoda **concentrated H₂SO₄** daalte hain *(Safety: concentrated H₂SO₄ aur HCl gas dono khatarnak hain; ye kaam sirf teacher fume-hood ya khuli hawa mein dastane aur safety goggles pehan kar karte hain)*. Reaction se **hydrogen chloride (HCl) gas** nikalti hai, jise teacher **anhydrous calcium chloride** wali guard tube se guzaar kar sukha lete hain (CaCl₂ drying agent hai, Class 9 [water-part4.md](../../../class9/chemistry/water/water-part4.md) yaad karo):
> 2NaCl(s) + H₂SO₄(conc.) → Na₂SO₄ + 2HCl(g)↑
> - Jab teacher nikalne wali sookhi HCl gas ke munh par **sookha (dry) blue litmus paper** laate hain: **Rang NAHI badalta (neela hi rehta hai)!**
> - Jab teacher **geela (moist/wet) blue litmus paper** laate hain: **Turant LAAL (Red) ho jaata hai!**

**Kyun?** Kyunki sookhi HCl gas mein hydrogen aur chlorine covalent bond se jude molecules (H–Cl) hain; wahan koi khula H⁺ ion nahi hai. Jaise hi HCl gas ko geela litmus (yaani **paani**) milta hai, paani ke molecules HCl ko tod kar **H⁺(aq) aur Cl⁻(aq) ions** bana dete hain, aur tabhi litmus laal hota hai!

### Hydronium Ion (H₃O⁺) kya hai?

Ek chhoti si sachchai aur: paani ke andar akela **H⁺ ion** (jo sirf ek nanga proton hai, kyunki hydrogen ka 1 electron to chhoot gaya!) akela tair nahi sakta. Wo turant paani ke ek molecule (H₂O) ke saath chipak kar **Hydronium ion (H₃O⁺)** bana leta hai:

> **H⁺ + H₂O → H₃O⁺ (Hydronium ion)**
> Isliye HCl aur paani ki reaction ko hum poori tarah aise bhi likhte hain:
> **HCl + H₂O → H₃O⁺ + Cl⁻**

Asal mein jab hum **H⁺(aq)** likhte hain, to uska matlab **H₃O⁺** hi hota hai. Dono tarah se likhna sahi hai! *(H₂O aur H⁺ ke beech banne wale khas 'coordinate bond' ki banawat hum aage Chapter 7 mein dekhenge.)*

## Part B: Bases paani mein kya dete hain? OH⁻(aq) aur Alkali

Jaise acids paani mein H⁺(aq) (ya H₃O⁺) dete hain, waise hi jab koi **base** paani mein ghulta hai, to wo **Hydroxide ions, OH⁻(aq)** deta hai:

- NaOH(s) + paani → Na⁺(aq) + OH⁻(aq)
- KOH(s) + paani → K⁺(aq) + OH⁻(aq)
- Mg(OH)₂(s) + paani ⇌ Mg²⁺(aq) + 2OH⁻(aq)

### Base aur Alkali mein kya farq hai?

Ye ek bahut zaroori baat hai jahan aksar galti hoti hai: **Saare bases paani mein nahi ghulte!**
- Kuch bases (jaise copper hydroxide Cu(OH)₂, iron(III) hydroxide Fe(OH)₃, zinc oxide ZnO, copper oxide CuO) paani mein **nahi ghulte** (insoluble hain). Par we acid ke saath reaction karke salt aur paani banate hain, isliye we **bases** hain.
- Jo bases **paani mein ghul jaate hain** aur ghol mein OH⁻(aq) dete hain, unhein ek khas naam diya gaya hai: **Alkali (kshaar)**. Pramukh alkalis hain: **NaOH, KOH, Ca(OH)₂** (thoda ghulta hai, jise limewater kehte hain) aur **NH₄OH**.

```
   +--------------------------------------------------------+
   : SAARE BASES (acid se milkar salt + paani banate hain)  :
   : CuO, ZnO, Fe(OH)₃, Cu(OH)₂  (paani mein nahi ghulte)   :
   :                                                        :
   : +---------------------------------------------+        :
   : :  ALKALIS (paani mein ghulne wale bases)     :        :
   : :  NaOH, KOH, Ca(OH)₂, NH₄OH                  :        :
   : +---------------------------------------------+        :
   +--------------------------------------------------------+
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Bases aur Alkalis ka rishta: Alkalis, Bases ke bade parivaar ke andar ka wo chhota hissa hain jo paani mein ghul jaate hain. Isliye **"Sabhi alkalis base hote hain, par sabhi bases alkali nahi hote!"**

## Part C: Basicity of Acids aur Acidity of Bases

Naam thode ulte lagte hain, isliye dhyan se dekho:
1. **Acid ki Basicity:** Kisi acid ke **ek molecule** ke paani mein tootne par ziyada-se-ziyada kitne **H⁺ ions** nikal sakte hain, use us acid ki **basicity** kehte hain:
   - **Monobasic acid (Basicity = 1):** 1 molecule se **1 H⁺** milta hai. Example: HCl, HNO₃, CH₃COOH. *(Khas dhyan do: CH₃COOH mein kul 4 H atoms dikhte hain, par unmein se sirf aakhri -OH wala **1 H** hi paani mein H⁺ ban kar nikalta hai! Carbon se jude 3 H nahi nikalte. Isliye CH₃COOH ki basicity 4 nahi, **1** hai!)*
   - **Dibasic acid (Basicity = 2):** 1 molecule se **2 H⁺** milte hain. Example: H₂SO₄ (2H⁺ + SO₄²⁻), H₂CO₃ (2H⁺ + CO₃²⁻).
   - **Tribasic acid (Basicity = 3):** 1 molecule se **3 H⁺** milte hain. Example: Phosphoric acid, H₃PO₄ (3H⁺ + PO₄³⁻).

2. **Base ki Acidity:** Kisi base ke **ek formula unit** se kitne **OH⁻ ions** milte hain:
   - **Monoacidic base (Acidity = 1):** NaOH, KOH, NH₄OH (1 OH⁻).
   - **Diacidic base (Acidity = 2):** Ca(OH)₂, Mg(OH)₂, Cu(OH)₂ (2 OH⁻).
   - **Triacidic base (Acidity = 3):** Al(OH)₃, Fe(OH)₃ (3 OH⁻).

## Part D: Strong vs Weak banaam Concentrated vs Dilute

Bache aksar **"Strong acid"** aur **"Concentrated acid"** ko ek hi baat samajh lete hain, jabki chemistry mein ye dono bilkul alag baatein hain!

### 1. Strong (Prabal) aur Weak (Durbal): Kitne pratishat molecules toote?

- **Strong Acid:** Jab ise paani mein daalo, to iske **lagbhag saare (100%) molecules toot kar H⁺(aq) ions** bana dete hain. Examples: **Hydrochloric acid (HCl), Sulphuric acid (H₂SO₄), Nitric acid (HNO₃)**. (Equation mein seedha teer → lagate hain.)
- **Weak Acid:** Jab ise paani mein daalo, to iske **bahut kam (maan lo 100 mein se sirf 1 ya 2) molecules hi toot kar H⁺(aq) ions** dete hain; baaki 98–99 molecules bina toote ghol mein tairte rehte hain! Examples: **Acetic acid (CH₃COOH), Citric acid, Carbonic acid (H₂CO₃), Oxalic acid, Methanoic acid**. (Equation mein do-tarfa teer ⇌ lagate hain.)
- Bilkul isi tarah:
  - **Strong Base (Alkali):** Paani mein lagbhag 100% toot kar OH⁻ deta hai — **NaOH, KOH**.
  - **Weak Base:** Bahut kam hissa OH⁻ deta hai — **NH₄OH**. *(Ca(OH)₂ aur Mg(OH)₂ paani mein bahut kam ghulte hain, isliye unke ghol mein OH⁻ kam hote hain; par jitna hissa ghulta hai wo lagbhag poora toot jaata hai. Inhe "weak" kehna galat hoga; inhe "kam ghulne wale" bases kehna sahi hai.)*

```
   STRONG ACID (jaise HCl paani mein)     WEAK ACID (jaise CH₃COOH paani mein)
   (10 molecules daale -> 10 ke 10 toote!) (10 molecules daale -> sirf 1 toota)

    H⁺   Cl⁻    H⁺   Cl⁻    H⁺             CH₃COOH    CH₃COOH    CH₃COOH
    Cl⁻  H⁺     Cl⁻  H⁺     Cl⁻            CH₃COOH    H⁺         CH₃COO⁻
    H⁺   Cl⁻    H⁺   Cl⁻    H⁺             CH₃COOH    CH₃COOH    CH₃COOH
    Cl⁻  H⁺     Cl⁻  H⁺     Cl⁻            CH₃COOH    CH₃COOH

    Ginti: 10 H⁺ aur 10 Cl⁻ (0 saboot HCl) Ginti: 9 saboot CH₃COOH, 1 H⁺, 1 CH₃COO⁻
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Strong acid aur Weak acid ka particle-sketch (10 molecules ke hisaab se). Baayen box mein 10 HCl molecules paani mein jaate hi poore toot kar 10 H⁺ aur 10 Cl⁻ ions banate hain. Daayen box mein 10 CH₃COOH molecules mein se 9 bina toote rehte hain aur sirf 1 toot kar 1 H⁺ aur 1 CH₃COO⁻ deta hai. (Samjhane ke liye 10 mein se 1 dikhaya hai; asli dilute sirke mein to 100 mein se lagbhag 1 hi toot-ta hai.)

### 2. Concentrated (Gaadha) aur Dilute (Patla): Paani kitna hai?

- **Concentrated:** Ghol mein acid (ya base) ki matra **zyada** hai aur paani **kam** hai.
- **Dilute:** Ghol mein paani **bahut zyada** hai aur acid (ya base) **kam** hai.

**Sabse kaam ka niyam:**
- Agar tum **HCl** mein khoob saara paani mila do, to wo **Dilute HCl** ban jaayega, par wo rahega **Strong Acid** hi (kyunki uske jitne bhi HCl molecules hain, we paani mein 100% toote hue hain!).
- Agar tum **Acetic acid (CH₃COOH)** mein bahut kam paani rakho, to wo **Concentrated Acetic Acid** hoga, par rahega **Weak Acid** hi (kyunki uske molecules abhi bhi bahut kam tootenge)!

## Part E: Dilution aur Suraksha: Acid ko Paani mein, ya Paani ko Acid mein?

Jab concentrated acid (khaaskar concentrated H₂SO₄ ya HNO₃) paani se milta hai, to **bahut zyada garmi nikalti hai** — ye ek **highly exothermic** prakriya hai ([chemical-changes-and-reactions-part4.md](../../../class9/chemistry/chemical-changes-and-reactions/chemical-changes-and-reactions-part4.md)).

> **Jaan bachane wala Lab Niyam: Hamesha ACID ko PAANI mein dheere-dheere milao (lagatar hilate hue); KABHI BHI paani ko concentrated acid mein mat daalo!**

```
   SAHI TAREEKA (Safe)                    GALAT TAREEKA (Khatarnak!)

       Acid (boond-boond)                     Paani (upar se)
            :                                      :
            v   (glass rod se hilao)               v   (bhaap ban kar uchhalega!)
        +-------+                              +-------+
        :       :                              : \ ! / :  <-- Tezab ke chhinte
        :~~~~~~~:                              :~~~~~~~:      munh/haath par!
        : PAANI :                              : CONC. :
        : (bada :                              : ACID  :
        : hissa):                              :       :
        +-------+                              +-------+
   Paani garmi sokh leta hai              Paani turant ubal kar acid uchhalta hai
```

> **Figure 2.4** – Acid ko dilute karne ka sahi aur galat tareeka. Baayen: zyada paani mein boond-boond acid daal kar hilane se paani saari garmi sokh leta hai (Physics ke [heat-and-temperature-part3.md](../../../class9/physics/heat-and-temperature/heat-and-temperature-part3.md) mein dekha tha ki paani ki specific heat bahut zyada hoti hai). Daayen: gaadhe acid mein paani daalne par paani achanak ubal kar bhaap banta hai aur khaulata hua acid bahar uchhal kar chehre aur haath ko jala sakta hai.

**Dilution se ions ki ginti par kya asar padta hai?**
Jab hum kisi acid ya base mein aur paani milate hain (**dilution** karte hain), to **ikai aayatan (per unit volume, jaise per mL ya per L) mein H₃O⁺ (ya OH⁻) ions ki saandrata (concentration) GHAT jaati hai**. Ghol pehle se halka ho jaata hai.

## Part F: Solved Examples

### Example 1. Dilution se concentration kaise ghatti hai?

**Sawaal:** Ek beaker mein **50 mL** dilute acid hai jismein har 1 mL mein maan lo **4 × 10¹⁸** H₃O⁺ ions hain. Teacher ismein **150 mL** shuddh paani aur mila dete hain.
(a) Beaker mein kul aayatan kitna ho gaya?
(b) Kya beaker mein H₃O⁺ ions ki kul ginti badli?
(c) Ab har 1 mL mein kitne H₃O⁺ ions honge?

**Hal:**
- (a) Naya kul aayatan = 50 mL + 150 mL = **200 mL**.
- (b) Pehle kul H₃O⁺ ions = 50 × (4 × 10¹⁸) = **2 × 10²⁰** ions. Strong acid pehle se poora toota hua tha, to paani milane se kul ions ki ginti lagbhag wahi (2 × 10²⁰) rahi, bas jagah (volume) 4 guna badh gayi.
- (c) Ab har 1 mL mein H₃O⁺ ions = (2 × 10²⁰) ÷ 200 mL = **1 × 10¹⁸** ions per mL.

**Jaanch:** Aayatan 50 mL se 200 mL yaani **4 guna** hua, to prati mL ions ki ginti 4 × 10¹⁸ se ghat kar ek-chauthaai (1 × 10¹⁸) reh gayi ✅.

---

### Example 2. Basicity aur H⁺ ions ki ginti

**Sawaal:** Agar **0.2 mole H₂SO₄** aur **0.2 mole HCl** ko alag-alag paani mein poori tarah ionise kiya jaaye, to kis ghol mein zyada H⁺(aq) ions banenge aur kitne moles?

**Hal:**
- HCl ek **monobasic** acid hai: HCl → H⁺ + Cl⁻ (1 mole HCl deta hai 1 mole H⁺).
  Isliye 0.2 mole HCl dega = **0.2 mole H⁺(aq) ions**.
- H₂SO₄ ek **dibasic** acid hai: H₂SO₄ → 2H⁺ + SO₄²⁻ (1 mole H₂SO₄ deta hai 2 moles H⁺).
  Isliye 0.2 mole H₂SO₄ dega = 2 × 0.2 = **0.4 mole H⁺(aq)** ions!
- Nateeja: **H₂SO₄ wale ghol mein dugne (0.4 mole) H⁺(aq) ions** banenge.

**Jaanch:** 0.4 mole ÷ 0.2 mole = 2, jo H₂SO₄ aur HCl ki basicity ka anupaat (2 : 1) hai ✅.

---

### Example 3. Kaun-sa kathan sahi hai: Alkali vs Base

**Sawaal:** Neeche diye gaye compounds mein se **Bases** chuno, aur phir unmein se **Alkalis** alag karo:
NaOH, CuO, H₂SO₄, KOH, Fe(OH)₃, C₂H₅OH, NH₄OH.

**Hal:**
- H₂SO₄ to acid hai, aur C₂H₅OH (ethanol) neutral covalent compound hai (na acid, na base — iska OH paani mein OH⁻ ion nahi banta!).
- **Bases (5 hain):** NaOH, CuO, KOH, Fe(OH)₃, NH₄OH.
- **Inmein se Alkalis (jo paani mein ghulte hain, 3 hain):** **NaOH, KOH, NH₄OH**. (CuO aur Fe(OH)₃ paani mein nahi ghulte, isliye we base to hain par alkali nahi.)

**Jaanch:** Sabhi 3 alkalis (NaOH, KOH, NH₄OH) bases ki list ke andar maujood hain ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: C₂H₅OH (alcohol) ko OH dekh kar Base samajh lena, ya C₆H₁₂O₆ (glucose) ko H dekh kar Acid samajh lena.**
   Alcohol aur glucose covalent compounds hain; we paani mein toot kar OH⁻ ya H⁺ ions **nahi** dete, aur na hi unka ghol bijli chalata hai.
2. **Galti 2: CH₃COOH (acetic acid) mein 4 hydrogen dekh kar uski basicity 4 likh dena.**
   CH₃COOH mein sirf -COOH hisse ka **1 hydrogen** hi paani mein H⁺ ban kar nikalta hai (CH₃COOH ⇌ CH₃COO⁻ + H⁺). Isliye acetic acid **monobasic (basicity = 1)** hai!
3. **Galti 3: "Dilute HCl ek weak acid hai" keh dena.**
   Bilkul galat! HCl hamesha **strong acid** hai (chahe concentrated ho ya dilute), kyunki wo paani mein 100% ionise hota hai. Paani milane se wo **dilute** hota hai, **weak** nahi!
4. **Galti 4: Concentrated acid mein upar se paani daalna.**
   Kabhi nahi! Hamesha paani mein boond-boond karke acid milana chahiye aur lagatar hilana chahiye, warna exothermic garmi se acid ubal kar bahar uchhal sakta hai.
5. **Galti 5: Sookhi HCl gas se sookhe blue litmus ke laal hone ki umeed karna.**
   Bina paani ke HCl gas mein H⁺(aq) ions nahi bante, isliye sookha litmus rang nahi badalta.

## Quick Recap

- Sabhi **acids** paani mein ghul kar **H⁺(aq)** ya **hydronium ions (H₃O⁺)** dete hain (H⁺ + H₂O → H₃O⁺).
- **Paani ke bina** acid apne ions nahi bana sakta: sookhi HCl gas sookhe blue litmus ka rang nahi badalti, par geele blue litmus ko turant laal kar deti hai.
- **Glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆)** aur **alcohol (C₂H₅OH)** mein hydrogen hota hai, par paani mein we H⁺ ions nahi dete, isliye unke ghol mein bulb nahi jalta aur we acid nahi hain.
- Sabhi **bases** paani mein **OH⁻(aq) ions** dete hain. Paani mein ghulne wale bases ko **alkali** kehte hain (NaOH, KOH, Ca(OH)₂, NH₄OH). **"All alkalis are bases, but not all bases are alkalis."**
- **Strong acids** (HCl, H₂SO₄, HNO₃) paani mein ~100% ionise hote hain; **weak acids** (CH₃COOH, H₂CO₃, citric acid) bahut kam ionise hote hain.
- **Dilution** (Acid → Paani, lagatar hilate hue) ek **exothermic** prakriya hai aur isse prati mL H₃O⁺ ya OH⁻ ions ki saandrata (concentration) ghatti hai.

## Practice Questions

1. Why do aqueous solutions of hydrochloric acid and nitric acid conduct electricity, whereas aqueous solutions of glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆) and ethyl alcohol (C₂H₅OH) do not?
2. Dry ammonia gas (NH₃) has no action on dry red litmus paper, but a solution of ammonia in water turns red litmus paper blue. Explain why.
3. Write the balanced equation showing how a hydrogen ion (H⁺) forms a hydronium ion in water. Why can a bare H⁺ ion not exist freely in aqueous solution?
4. State the basicity of each of the following acids and write the ions formed when one molecule of each ionises completely in water:
   (a) Nitric acid (HNO₃)
   (b) Sulphuric acid (H₂SO₄)
   (c) Phosphoric acid (H₃PO₄)
   (d) Acetic acid (CH₃COOH)
5. "All alkalis are bases, but all bases are not alkalis." Justify this statement with two examples of bases that are alkalis and two examples of bases that are not alkalis.
6. While diluting a concentrated acid in the laboratory, why is it recommended that the acid should be added slowly to water with constant stirring, and NOT water to the acid?
7. How does the concentration of hydronium ions (H₃O⁺) per millilitre change when a solution of an acid is diluted with water?
8. A student says: *"If I add lots of water to concentrated hydrochloric acid, it becomes a weak acid."* Is the student right or wrong? Explain the difference between a **weak acid** and a **dilute acid**.
9. Equal lengths of magnesium ribbon are dropped into two test tubes: Tube A contains dilute HCl and Tube B contains dilute CH₃COOH (both at the same concentration and temperature). In which test tube will fizzing (bubbling) occur more vigorously, and why?
10. A beaker contains **40 mL** of potassium hydroxide (KOH) solution containing **6 × 10¹⁹** OH⁻ ions per mL. If **160 mL** of distilled water is added to the beaker, find:
    (a) the final volume of the solution,
    (b) the total number of OH⁻ ions in the beaker, and
    (c) the new number of OH⁻ ions per mL after dilution.

### Answers

1. Vidyut dhara (electric current) ghol mein **free ions** ke chalne se behti hai. HCl aur HNO₃ paani mein toot kar H⁺(aq) aur Cl⁻(aq) / NO₃⁻(aq) ions banate hain, isliye unka ghol bijli chalata hai. Glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆) aur ethyl alcohol (C₂H₅OH) paani mein ghulne par bhi covalent molecules hi rehte hain aur H⁺ ions mein nahi tootte, isliye unke ghol mein bijli nahi chalti.

2. Base apna asar (OH⁻ ions) **sirf paani ki maujoodgi mein** dikhata hai. Sookhi NH₃ gas mein koi OH⁻ ions nahi hote, isliye sookha red litmus nahi badalta. Par paani mein ghulne par ammonia paani se abhikriya karke ammonium ions aur hydroxide ions banati hai (NH₃ + H₂O ⇌ NH₄⁺ + OH⁻), aur inhi OH⁻(aq) ions ke kaaran red litmus neela ho jaata hai.

3. Samikaran: **H⁺ + H₂O → H₃O⁺** (aur HCl ke liye: HCl + H₂O → H₃O⁺ + Cl⁻).
   Akela H⁺ ion sirf ek chhota-sa dhan-aveshit nucleus (proton) hai jiske paas ek bhi electron nahi bacha. Isliye wo paani mein akela nahi reh paata aur turant paani ke molecule (H₂O) se jud kar **hydronium ion (H₃O⁺)** bana leta hai.

4. (a) **HNO₃:** Basicity = **1 (Monobasic)**; Ions: H⁺(aq) aur NO₃⁻(aq).
   (b) **H₂SO₄:** Basicity = **2 (Dibasic)**; Ions: 2H⁺(aq) aur SO₄²⁻(aq).
   (c) **H₃PO₄:** Basicity = **3 (Tribasic)**; Ions: 3H⁺(aq) aur PO₄³⁻(aq).
   (d) **CH₃COOH:** Basicity = **1 (Monobasic)** (4 H hone ke bawajood sirf -COOH wala 1 H hi H⁺ banta hai); Ions: H⁺(aq) aur CH₃COO⁻(aq).

5. **Base** wo har padarth hai jo acid se milkar salt aur paani banata hai. Un bases mein se **sirf we jo paani mein ghul jaate hain**, **alkali** kehlate hain.
   - Do bases jo **alkali hain** (paani mein ghulansheel): **Sodium hydroxide (NaOH)** aur **Potassium hydroxide (KOH)**.
   - Do bases jo **alkali nahi hain** (paani mein aghulansheel): **Copper(II) hydroxide (Cu(OH)₂)** aur **Iron(III) hydroxide (Fe(OH)₃)** (ya CuO, ZnO).

6. Concentrated acid aur paani ka milna ek **bahut tez exothermic** (garmi chhodne wali) prakriya hai. Agar concentrated acid mein upar se paani daala jaaye, to nikalne wali achanak garmi se paani turant ubal kar bhaap banta hai aur khaulata hua acid bartan se bahar chehre ya haath par uchhal sakta hai, tatha kaanch ka beaker bhi chatak sakta hai. Jabki paani mein boond-boond acid daal kar hilane se paani ka bada hissa us garmi ko surakshit roop se sokh leta hai.

7. Acid mein paani milane (**dilution** karne) par kul aayatan badh jaata hai, jisse **prati mL (per unit volume) mein hydronium ions (H₃O⁺) ki saandrata (concentration) GHAT jaati hai**.

8. Vidyarthi **galat** keh raha hai!
   - **Strong vs Weak acid** is baat par nirbhar karta hai ki acid paani mein **kitne pratishat ionise (tootta) hai**: HCl paani mein ~100% ionise hota hai, isliye wo hamesha **strong acid** hai; CH₃COOH bahut kam ionise hota hai, isliye wo **weak acid** hai.
   - **Concentrated vs Dilute acid** is baat par nirbhar karta hai ki ghol mein **paani kitna hai**. Concentrated HCl mein khoob paani milane par wo **dilute strong acid** banta hai, weak acid nahi!

9. **Tube A (dilute HCl)** mein bulbule (fizzing) **bahut zyada tezi se** uthenge! Kyunki HCl ek **strong acid** hai jo paani mein poori tarah toot kar CH₃COOH (weak acid) ke muqable kahin zyada H⁺(aq) ions deta hai (dono ka concentration barabar hone par bhi). Zyada H⁺ ions hone se magnesium ke saath hydrogen gas (H₂) banne ki raftaar Tube A mein bahut tez hoti hai.

10. (a) Naya kul aayatan = 40 mL + 160 mL = **200 mL**.
    (b) Kul OH⁻ ions ki ginti = 40 mL × (6 × 10¹⁹ ions/mL) = **2.4 × 10²¹** ions.
    (c) Dilution ke baad prati mL OH⁻ ions = (2.4 × 10²¹) ÷ 200 mL = **1.2 × 10¹⁹** ions per mL ✅ (Jaanch: Aayatan 40 mL se 200 mL yaani 5 guna hua, aur prati mL ions 6 × 10¹⁹ ÷ 5 = 1.2 × 10¹⁹ yaani ek-paanchvan reh gaye ✅).
