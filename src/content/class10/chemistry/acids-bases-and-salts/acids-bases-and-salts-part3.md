# Topic 3: Acids aur Bases Ke Chemical Reactions

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki paani mein utarte hi acid apne **H⁺(aq) ions** khol deta hai aur base apne **OH⁻(aq) ions**. Ab socho: jab ye chust-durust ions kisi **dhatu (metal)**, **baking soda**, **chune ke patthar**, ya **ek-doosre** se takrate hain, to kya hota hai? Class 9 ke [chemical-changes-and-reactions-part3.md](../../../class9/chemistry/chemical-changes-and-reactions/chemical-changes-and-reactions-part3.md) mein humne reactivity ladder aur neutralisation ki pehli jhalak dekhi thi. Aaj hum use repeat nahi karenge, balki usse ek qadam aage badh kar **paanch bade rasayanik parivaaron** ke reactions, unmein nikalne wali gases ki pehchaan, aur unke peeche ka hisaab samjhenge.

## Pital ka katora aur nimbu-soda ke bulbule

Garmi ki dopahar mein Kairav apni mami Mrs. Patwardhan ke saath rasoi mein dahi jamaane ki taiyari kar raha tha. Kairav ne almari se ek chamakdar peela **pital (brass) ka katora** nikala aur usmein khatta dahi daalne hi wala tha ki Mrs. Patwardhan ne turant uska haath rok liya!

"Ruko Kairav! Pital ya taambe (copper) ke bartan mein dahi, chhaachh, nimbu ya achaar **kabhi nahi rakhte**!"

Kairav ne poochha, "Kyun mami? Pital ka katora to kitna sundar aur mazboot hai!"

Mrs. Patwardhan ne katora wapas rakhwate hue kaha, "Sundar hai, par dahi aur nimbu mein **acid** hota hai. Agar bina kalai (tin coating) wale pital ya taambe ke bartan mein khatti cheez rakhoge, to acid dhatu ke saath reaction karke **nila-hara kasaila (zeherila) salt** bana dega, jisse khana kharab ho jaayega aur pet bimar pad sakta hai. Isliye dahi hamesha mitti, kaanch, chini-mitti ya stainless steel ke bartan mein hi jamate hain."

Phir Mrs. Patwardhan ne kaanch ke glass mein nimbu ka ras aur paani milaya, aur jaise hi usmein ek chutki **meetha soda (baking soda)** daala — *shhhhhh!* — glass mein jhaag aur bulbulon ka toofan uth khada hua!

Kairav hans pada: "Mami, ye bulbule kaun-si gas ke hain? Kya ye wahi gas hai jo acid aur dhatu ke milne par nikalti hai, ya koi doosri?"

Mrs. Patwardhan muskurayin: "Bilkul alag gas hai! Dhatu ke saath **Hydrogen (H₂)** nikalti hai, aur soda ke saath **Carbon dioxide (CO₂)**. Chalo, dono ki pehchaan aur saare reactions ek-ek karke dekhte hain."

## Part A: Reaction 1 — Dhatu (Metals) ke saath Acids aur Bases ki Abhikriya

### 1. Active Metal + Dilute Acid → Salt + Hydrogen Gas (H₂↑)

Ye niyam tum Class 9 mein do jagah dekh chuke ho: reactivity ladder ([chemical-changes-and-reactions-part3.md](../../../class9/chemistry/chemical-changes-and-reactions/chemical-changes-and-reactions-part3.md)) aur hydrogen banane ki lab vidhi ([hydrogen-part2.md](../../../class9/chemistry/hydrogen/hydrogen-part2.md)), jahan Zn, Mg aur Fe ke dilute HCl aur H₂SO₄ ke saath equations bhi likhe the. Ek line mein: **ladder mein H se upar wali dhatu + dilute acid → salt + H₂↑**. Yahan bas ek naya example, aluminium ka, jismein coefficients thode bade hain:

   2Al(s) + 6HCl(aq) → 2AlCl₃(aq) + 3H₂(g)↑
   *(Jaanch: Al 2=2, H 6=6, Cl 6=6 ✅)*

Ab aage ki baat: Topic 2 ki nazar se asal khel **H⁺(aq)** ka hai. Dhatu ke atom apne electrons H⁺ ions ko de dete hain, H⁺ ions H₂ gas ban jaate hain, aur dhatu ion (jaise Zn²⁺) ghol mein chala jaata hai. Isliye jis ghol mein H⁺(aq) zyada (strong acid), usmein bulbule bhi zyada tez uthte hain.

*(Dhyan rahe: Copper Cu, Silver Ag aur Gold Au reactivity ladder mein H se neeche hain, isliye we dilute HCl ya dilute H₂SO₄ se hydrogen gas **nahi** nikalte! Phir Kairav ke pital/taambe ke katora mein dahi kyun kharab hota hai? Kyunki khuli hawa ki **oxygen (O₂)** ki maujoodgi mein dahi/nimbu ke organic acids dheere-dheere taambe ki satah par bani copper oxide ki parat ke saath ghul kar hara-neela zeherila copper salt bana lete hain!)*

> **Activity 3.1 (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo): Soap-bubble Pop Test (H₂ ki pehchaan).**
> Teacher ek stand mein lagi test tube mein **zinc granules (Zn)** aur **dilute H₂SO₄** milate hain. Nikalne wali gas ko ek delivery tube ke zariye **sabun ke paani (soap solution)** se bhare trough mein bheja jaata hai:
> - Sabun ke paani se **H₂ se bhare bulbule** hawa mein upar uthte hain (kyunki H₂ hawa se bahut halki hai!).
> - Teacher ek lambi chhadi par lagi jalti mombatti ko test tube se **door** hawa mein uthte sabun ke bulbule ke paas chhuwate hain: bulbula **"PHAT!" (pop sound)** ki aawaz ke saath jalta hai!
> *(Safety: Hydrogen gas behad jwalansheel hai. Kabhi bhi jalti tili seedhe test tube ke munh par nahi lagate; sabun ke bulbulon par hi teacher door se dikhate hain.)*

```
      Stand        Delivery tube
        :          +-------------+
        :          :             :
       +:+         :             :        ( H₂ )  <-- Jalti mombatti laane par
       : :         :             :      (H₂)          "POP!" ki aawaz!
       : : Dilute  :             v    (H₂)
       : : H₂SO₄   :         +--------------+
       :o: + Zn    :         :~~~~~~~~~~~~~~:
       +_+ granules          : Sabun ka ghol:
                             +--------------+
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Teacher demo: Zinc aur dilute H₂SO₄ se nikli H₂ gas ko sabun ke paani mein bhejne par H₂ se bhare halke bulbule upar uthte hain, jo jalti mombatti chhune par 'pop' sound ke saath jalte hain.

### 2. Kya Bases bhi Metals ke saath Hydrogen dete hain? (Zn aur Al ka khas kissa)

Saare metals bases ke saath reaction **nahi** karte (jaise Fe, Cu, Mg par NaOH ka asar nahi hota). Par kuch khas dhatuein — jaise **Zinc (Zn)** aur **Aluminium (Al)** — garam **Sodium Hydroxide (NaOH)** ke ghol ke saath abhikriya karke **Hydrogen gas (H₂↑)** deti hain:

> **2NaOH(aq) + Zn(s) → Na₂ZnO₂(aq) (Sodium zincate) + H₂(g)↑**
> *(Jaanch: Na 2=2, O 2=2, H 2=2, Zn 1=1 ✅. Yahan ZnO₂²⁻ zincate radical hai jiski valency 2 hai, isliye Na₂ZnO₂ bana!)*

## Part B: Reaction 2 — Metal Carbonates (CO₃²⁻) aur Hydrogencarbonates (HCO₃⁻) + Acid

Ab Kairav ke nimbu-soda ke bulbulon par aate hain! Jab bhi koi **acid** kisi **metal carbonate** (jaise washing soda Na₂CO₃, chuna-patthar CaCO₃) ya **metal hydrogencarbonate / bicarbonate** (jaise baking soda NaHCO₃) par girta hai, to tez bulbulahat (**brisk effervescence**) ke saath **Carbon dioxide (CO₂) gas** nikalti hai:

> **Aam Sutra:**
> Metal Carbonate / Hydrogencarbonate + Acid → Salt + Water (H₂O) + Carbon dioxide (CO₂↑)

Teen sabse zaroori equations:

1. **Sodium carbonate (Washing soda) + Dilute HCl:**
   Na₂CO₃(s) + 2HCl(aq) → 2NaCl(aq) + H₂O(l) + CO₂(g)↑
   *(Jaanch: Na 2=2, C 1=1, O 3=1+2=3, H 2=2, Cl 2=2 ✅)*
2. **Sodium hydrogencarbonate (Baking soda) + Dilute HCl:**
   NaHCO₃(s) + HCl(aq) → NaCl(aq) + H₂O(l) + CO₂(g)↑
   *(Jaanch: Na 1=1, H 1+1=2, C 1=1, O 3=1+2=3, Cl 1=1 ✅)*
3. **Calcium carbonate (Chuna-patthar / sangmarmar / ande ka chhilka) + Dilute HCl:**
   CaCO₃(s) + 2HCl(aq) → CaCl₂(aq) + H₂O(l) + CO₂(g)↑
   *(Jaanch: Ca 1=1, C 1=1, O 3=1+2=3, H 2=2, Cl 2=2 ✅)*

### Carbon dioxide (CO₂) ki do-charan wali Limewater Jaanch!

Nikalne wali gas CO₂ hi hai, iski pakki jaanch hum **chune ke saaf paani (limewater, Ca(OH)₂(aq))** se karte hain:

- **Charan 1 (Thodi der CO₂ guzarne par — Dudhiya hona):**
  Jab CO₂ gas ko saaf limewater mein se guzara jaata hai, to paani mein na ghulne wala **safed CaCO₃ precipitate** banta hai, jisse limewater **dudhiya (milky)** ho jaata hai:
  **Ca(OH)₂(aq) + CO₂(g) → CaCO₃(s)↓ (Safed dudhiya precipitate) + H₂O(l)**
  *(Jaanch: Ca 1=1, O 2+2=3+1=4, H 2=2, C 1=1 ✅)*

- **Charan 2 (Bahut zyada / Excess CO₂ guzarne par — Dudhiyapan GAYAB hona!):**
  Agar hum usi dudhiya paani mein **lagatar aur CO₂ gas** guzarte rahein, to safed CaCO₃ paani aur CO₂ se milkar **Calcium hydrogencarbonate, Ca(HCO₃)₂** bana leta hai. Class 9 ke [water-part3.md](../../../class9/chemistry/water/water-part3.md) mein humne dekha tha ki Ca(HCO₃)₂ paani mein **ghulansheel (soluble)** hota hai! Isliye **dudhiyapan gayab ho jaata hai aur ghol wapas paani jaisa saaf (colourless)** ho jaata hai:
  **CaCO₃(s) + H₂O(l) + CO₂(g) → Ca(HCO₃)₂(aq) (Paani mein ghulansheel — saaf ghol)**
  *(Jaanch: Ca 1=1, C 1+1=2, O 3+1+2=6, H 2=2 ✅)*

```
   Saaf Limewater         Thodi CO₂ guzari          Excess (zyada) CO₂ guzari
   Ca(OH)₂(aq)      --->  CaCO₃(s)↓ + H₂O     --->  Ca(HCO₃)₂(aq)
   (Rangheen/Saaf)        (DUDHIYA / Milky!)        (Wapas SAAF / Colourless!)
                          [aghulansheel thos]       [paani mein ghulansheel]
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Limewater test ke dono qadam: pehle CO₂ se aghulansheel CaCO₃ banne ke kaaran paani dudhiya hota hai; phir excess CO₂ se ghulansheel Ca(HCO₃)₂ banne ke kaaran dudhiyapan gayab ho jaata hai.

*(Mazedaar baat: Chuna-patthar (CaCO₃), khadiya (chalk) aur sangmarmar (marble) — ye teeno dikhne mein alag hain, par rasayanik roop se teeno ek hi compound **CaCO₃** hain!)*

## Part C: Reaction 3 — Acid + Base (Neutralisation ka Ionic Roop)

Class 9 ke [chemical-changes-and-reactions-part3.md](../../../class9/chemistry/chemical-changes-and-reactions/chemical-changes-and-reactions-part3.md) mein humne dekha tha ki HCl(aq) + NaOH(aq) → NaCl(aq) + H₂O(l) ek double-displacement reaction hai. Ab Topic 2 ke ions ki nazar se dekho ki beaker ke andar **sachmuch kya badla**:

- Reaction se pehle: H⁺(aq) + Cl⁻(aq) + Na⁺(aq) + OH⁻(aq)
- Reaction ke baad: Na⁺(aq) + Cl⁻(aq) + H₂O(l)

Dhyan do! Na⁺(aq) aur Cl⁻(aq) to reaction se pehle bhi paani mein alag-alag tair rahe the, aur baad mein bhi waise hi tair rahe hain (darshak ions)! Asali khel to sirf **H⁺(aq) aur OH⁻(aq)** ke beech hua:

> **Neutralisation ka Mool Ionic Sutra:**
> **H⁺(aq) + OH⁻(aq) → H₂O(l) + Garmi (Exothermic)**

> **Activity 3.2 (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo): Phenolphthalein ka Gulabi-se-Rangheen khel.**
> 1. Teacher ek conical flask mein 10 mL dilute NaOH lete hain aur usmein 2 boond **phenolphthalein** daalte hain: ghol **gehra gulabi (pink)** ho jaata hai (kyunki OH⁻ ions hain).
> 2. Ab teacher dropper se boond-boond **dilute HCl** milate hain aur flask hilate hain. Ek boond aisi aati hai jab saare OH⁻ ions ko H⁺ ions ne milkar H₂O bana diya — aur ghol achanak **bilkul rangheen (colourless)** ho jaata hai!
> 3. Ab agar teacher **1 boond NaOH** wapas daalein, to ghol phir se **gulabi** ho jaata hai! Aur phir 1 boond HCl daalo, to phir **rangheen**!

## Part D: Reaction 4 aur 5 — Metal Oxides aur Non-metal Oxides ki Prakriti

### 1. Metal Oxide + Acid → Salt + Water (Metal Oxides BASIC hote hain!)

*(Sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo.)* Teacher ek beaker mein thoda sa **kaala Copper(II) oxide (CuO) powder** lete hain aur usmein dheere-dheere **dilute HCl** milate hue hilate hain. Dekhte-dekhte kaala powder ghul jaata hai aur ghol ka rang khubsurat **neela-hara (bluish-green)** ho jaata hai!

**Kyun?** Kyunki **Copper(II) chloride (CuCl₂)** ban gaya:

> **CuO(s) (Kaala) + 2HCl(aq) → CuCl₂(aq) (Neela-hara) + H₂O(l)**
> *(Jaanch: Cu 1=1, O 1=1, H 2=2, Cl 2=2 ✅)*

Zara is equation ki tulna Base + Acid → Salt + Water se karo! CuO ne bilkul ek **base** ki tarah acid (HCl) ko neutralise karke salt (CuCl₂) aur paani (H₂O) banaya. Isse ek bada niyam nikalta hai:

> **Dhatuon ke oxides (Metal oxides, jaise CuO, MgO, CaO, Na₂O) prakriti mein BASIC (kshaariya) hote hain!**

### 2. Non-metal Oxide + Base → Salt + Water (Non-metal Oxides ACIDIC hote hain!)

Ab Part B ka limewater reaction dobara dekho:
**CO₂(g) (Non-metal oxide) + Ca(OH)₂(aq) (Base) → CaCO₃(s) (Salt) + H₂O(l) (Paani)**

Yahan carbon (ek non-metal) ka oxide CO₂, base Ca(OH)₂ ke saath milkar salt (CaCO₃) aur paani (H₂O) bana raha hai — bilkul ek **acid** ki tarah! Isi tarah sulphur dioxide (SO₂) bhi NaOH se milkar Na₂SO₃ + H₂O banata hai. Isliye:

> **Adhatuon ke oxides (Non-metal oxides, jaise CO₂, SO₂, SO₃) prakriti mein ACIDIC (amliya) hote hain!**

## Part E: Solved Examples

### Example 1. Chuna-patthar (CaCO₃) se kitni CO₂ gas bani?

**Sawaal:** Agar **10 g shuddh sangmarmar (CaCO₃)** par poora dilute HCl daala jaaye, to equation CaCO₃ + 2HCl → CaCl₂ + H₂O + CO₂ ke hisaab se kitne gram **CO₂ gas** aur kitne gram **CaCl₂** banega? *(Atomic masses: Ca = 40, C = 12, O = 16, H = 1, Cl = 35.5)*

**Hal:**
- Sabhi molar masses nikalo:
  - CaCO₃ = 40 + 12 + (3 × 16) = **100 g/mol**.
  - 2HCl = 2 × (1 + 35.5) = **73 g**.
  - CaCl₂ = 40 + (2 × 35.5) = **111 g/mol**.
  - H₂O = 2 + 16 = **18 g/mol**.
  - CO₂ = 12 + (2 × 16) = **44 g/mol**.
- 100 g CaCO₃ se banta hai 44 g CO₂ aur 111 g CaCl₂.
- To **10 g CaCO₃** (jo 100 g ka dasvan hissa yaani 0.1 mol hai) se banega:
  - CO₂ ka mass = 0.1 × 44 = **4.4 g**
  - CaCl₂ ka mass = 0.1 × 111 = **11.1 g**
  - (Aur H₂O = 0.1 × 18 = 1.8 g, jabki HCl kharch hua = 0.1 × 73 = 7.3 g.)

**Jaanch (Dravyaman Sanrakshan Niyam):**
Reactants ka kul mass = 10 g (CaCO₃) + 7.3 g (HCl) = **17.3 g**.
Products ka kul mass = 11.1 g (CaCl₂) + 1.8 g (H₂O) + 4.4 g (CO₂) = **17.3 g** ✅!

### Example 2. Do gases X aur Y ki pehchaan

**Sawaal:** Zinc ke tukdon par dilute H₂SO₄ daalne par gas **X** nikalti hai, aur baking soda (NaHCO₃) par dilute H₂SO₄ daalne par gas **Y** nikalti hai. Gas X aur Y ke naam, balanced equations, aur dono ko pehchanne ka ek-ek test likho.

**Hal:**
- **Gas X = Hydrogen (H₂):**
  Equation: Zn(s) + H₂SO₄(aq) → ZnSO₄(aq) + H₂(g)↑ *(Jaanch: Zn 1, H 2, S 1, O 4 ✅)*
  Pehchaan: Sabun ke paani se guzar kar banne wale bulbulon ke paas jalti mombatti laane par **'pop' sound** ke saath jalti hai.
- **Gas Y = Carbon dioxide (CO₂):**
  Equation: 2NaHCO₃(s) + H₂SO₄(aq) → Na₂SO₄(aq) + 2H₂O(l) + 2CO₂(g)↑ *(Jaanch: Na 2=2, H 2+2=4, C 2=2, O 6+4=4+2+4=10, S 1=1 ✅)*
  Pehchaan: Chune ke saaf paani (Ca(OH)₂) mein guzarne par use **dudhiya (milky)** kar deti hai (aur jalti tili ko bujha deti hai).

**Jaanch:** Dono equations mein baayen aur daayen taraf har tatva ke parmanu barabar hain (Zn=1, H=2, S=1, O=4 aur Na=2, H=4, C=2, O=10, S=1) ✅.

### Example 3. Kaala powder P aur Neela-hara ghol Q

**Sawaal:** Ek kaala metal oxide **P** dilute hydrochloric acid ke saath ghul kar neela-hara (bluish-green) ghol **Q** aur paani banata hai. P aur Q ke naam aur formula batao, tatha P ki prakriti (acidic ya basic) likho.

**Hal:**
- Kaala metal oxide **P = Copper(II) oxide (CuO)**.
- Neela-hara ghol **Q = Copper(II) chloride (CuCl₂(aq))**.
- Equation: CuO(s) + 2HCl(aq) → CuCl₂(aq) + H₂O(l).
- Kyunki CuO ek dhatu ka oxide hai aur acid (HCl) ko neutralise karke salt aur paani banata hai, isliye **P ki prakriti Basic (kshaariya)** hai.

**Jaanch:** CuO + 2HCl → CuCl₂ + H₂O mein Cu = 1, O = 1, H = 2, Cl = 2 dono taraf poori tarah santulit hain ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: NaOH + Zn mein product NaZnO₂ likh dena.**
   Zincate ion par **−2 charge (ZnO₂²⁻)** hota hai aur sodium par **+1 (Na⁺)**. Criss-cross se sahi formula **Na₂ZnO₂ (Sodium zincate)** banta hai, NaZnO₂ nahi!
2. **Galti 2: "Har metal NaOH ke saath H₂ gas deta hai" maan lena.**
   Sirf kuch khas dhatuein jaise **Zinc (Zn)** aur **Aluminium (Al)** hi NaOH ke saath H₂ deti hain; iron (Fe), copper (Cu) ya magnesium (Mg) NaOH ke saath reaction nahi karte.
3. **Galti 3: Excess CO₂ guzarne par limewater ko "aur zyada dudhiya" likh dena.**
   Thodi CO₂ se limewater dudhiya (CaCO₃↓) hota hai, par **excess (zyada) CO₂** guzarne par ghulansheel **Ca(HCO₃)₂** banne ke kaaran **dudhiyapan gayab (colourless)** ho jaata hai!
4. **Galti 4: Metal carbonate + Acid mein H₂ gas nikal dena.**
   Dhatu (Zn, Mg) + Acid se **H₂** nikalti hai; par Carbonate/Bicarbonate (Na₂CO₃, NaHCO₃, CaCO₃) + Acid se hamesha **CO₂ + H₂O** banta hai!

## Quick Recap

- **Active Metal + Dilute Acid → Salt + H₂(g)↑** (jaise Zn + H₂SO₄ → ZnSO₄ + H₂↑). H₂ gas sabun ke bulbulon mein **'pop' sound** ke saath jalti hai.
- **Dahi, nimbu aur khatte achaar** ko pital (brass) ya taambe (copper) ke bartan mein nahi rakhte, kyunki unke acids dhatu ki satah se abhikriya karke **zeherile salts** bana lete hain.
- **Zinc (Zn) + 2NaOH → Na₂ZnO₂ (Sodium zincate) + H₂(g)↑**.
- **Metal Carbonate / Hydrogencarbonate + Acid → Salt + H₂O + CO₂(g)↑**.
- **Limewater test:** Ca(OH)₂(aq) + CO₂(g) → CaCO₃(s)↓ (milky) + H₂O(l); excess CO₂ par: CaCO₃(s) + H₂O(l) + CO₂(g) → Ca(HCO₃)₂(aq) (colourless, soluble).
- **Neutralisation ka ionic roop:** H⁺(aq) + OH⁻(aq) → H₂O(l).
- **Metal oxides (CuO, MgO, CaO) BASIC** hote hain (Metal oxide + Acid → Salt + H₂O); **Non-metal oxides (CO₂, SO₂) ACIDIC** hote hain (Non-metal oxide + Base → Salt + H₂O).

   (c) What will happen if this gas is passed through freshly prepared limewater first in a small amount, and then in excess? Write both equations.
4. When dilute hydrochloric acid is added to a small amount of black copper(II) oxide powder in a beaker with stirring, the black powder dissolves.
   (a) What is the colour of the new solution formed, and which compound gives it this colour?
   (b) Write the balanced chemical equation.
   (c) What does this reaction tell you about the acidic or basic nature of copper(II) oxide?
5. Write the net ionic equation for any neutralisation reaction between a strong acid and a strong alkali in water. Why are the remaining ions called spectator (darshak) ions?
6. Give one chemical test to distinguish between **Hydrogen gas (H₂)** and **Carbon dioxide gas (CO₂)**.
7. Why do non-metal oxides such as CO₂ and SO₂ react with alkalis like NaOH or Ca(OH)₂ to form salt and water? Write the balanced equation for the reaction between SO₂ and NaOH(aq) (forming sodium sulphite, Na₂SO₃, and water).
8. In a conical flask containing dilute KOH solution and two drops of phenolphthalein:
   (a) What is the initial colour of the solution?
   (b) Why does the solution become colourless when dilute H₂SO₄ is added drop by drop until neutralisation is just complete?
   (c) How can you bring the pink colour back without adding any more indicator?
9. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
   (a) Copper metal reacts vigorously with dilute hydrochloric acid to liberate hydrogen gas.
   (b) Chalk, marble, and limestone are different physical forms of the same chemical compound, calcium carbonate (CaCO₃).
   (c) Calcium hydrogencarbonate, Ca(HCO₃)₂, is insoluble in water.
   (d) Metallic oxides are generally basic in nature, whereas non-metallic oxides are acidic in nature.
10. If **16.8 g of baking soda (NaHCO₃)** reacts completely with dilute hydrochloric acid according to the equation NaHCO₃ + HCl → NaCl + H₂O + CO₂, calculate:
    (a) the number of moles of NaHCO₃ taken,
    (b) the mass of common salt (NaCl) formed, and
    (c) the mass of carbon dioxide (CO₂) gas released.
    *(Atomic masses: H = 1, C = 12, O = 16, Na = 23, Cl = 35.5)*

### Answers

1. Dahi (lactic acid), nimbu (citric acid) aur imli (tartaric acid) mein **acids** hote hain. Pital (brass, jo copper aur zinc ka alloy hai) ya taambe (copper) ke bartan mein rakhne par ye acids hawa ki maujoodgi mein dhatu ke saath abhikriya karke **zeherile (toxic) copper salts** bana lete hain, jisse khana kasaila aur sehat ke liye hanikarak ho jaata hai.

2. Chaar balanced equations:
   (a) 2Al(s) + 3H₂SO₄(aq) → Al₂(SO₄)₃(aq) + 3H₂(g)↑ *(Jaanch: Al 2, H 6, S 3, O 12 ✅)*
   (b) Zn(s) + 2NaOH(aq) → Na₂ZnO₂(aq) + H₂(g)↑ *(Jaanch: Zn 1, Na 2, O 2, H 2 ✅)*
   (c) K₂CO₃(s) + 2HCl(aq) → 2KCl(aq) + H₂O(l) + CO₂(g)↑ *(Jaanch: K 2, C 1, O 3, H 2, Cl 2 ✅)*
   (d) NaHCO₃(s) + HNO₃(aq) → NaNO₃(aq) + H₂O(l) + CO₂(g)↑ *(Jaanch: Na 1, H 2, C 1, N 1, O 6 ✅)*

3. (a) Ande ke chhilke (egg shells) **Calcium carbonate (CaCO₃)** ke bane hote hain, aur nikalne wali gas **Carbon dioxide (CO₂)** hai.
   (b) Equation: CaCO₃(s) + 2HCl(aq) → CaCl₂(aq) + H₂O(l) + CO₂(g)↑ ✅.
   (c) **Thodi matra mein CO₂ guzarne par:** Limewater aghulansheel safed CaCO₃ banne ke kaaran **dudhiya (milky)** ho jaata hai:
   Ca(OH)₂(aq) + CO₂(g) → CaCO₃(s)↓ + H₂O(l)
   **Excess (zyada) CO₂ guzarne par:** Ghulansheel **Calcium hydrogencarbonate (Ca(HCO₃)₂)** ban jaata hai, jisse **dudhiyapan gayab ho jaata hai** aur ghol wapas saaf ho jaata hai:
   CaCO₃(s) + H₂O(l) + CO₂(g) → Ca(HCO₃)₂(aq) ✅.

4. (a) Naye ghol ka rang **neela-hara (bluish-green)** hota hai, jo paani mein bane **Copper(II) chloride (CuCl₂)** salt ke kaaran aata hai.
   (b) Equation: CuO(s) + 2HCl(aq) → CuCl₂(aq) + H₂O(l) ✅.
   (c) Ye reaction dikhata hai ki CuO ek **Basic oxide (kshaariya oxide)** hai, kyunki wo acid (HCl) ke saath milkar salt aur paani banata hai.

5. Net ionic equation: **H⁺(aq) + OH⁻(aq) → H₂O(l)** (ya H₃O⁺(aq) + OH⁻(aq) → 2H₂O(l)).
   Baaki ions (jaise Na⁺ aur Cl⁻) reaction se pehle aur baad mein ghol mein bina badle waise hi tairte rehte hain, isliye unhein **spectator (darshak) ions** kehte hain.

6. **Limewater test (ya Pop test):** Gas ko chune ke saaf paani (Ca(OH)₂) mein guzaro: **CO₂ limewater ko dudhiya (milky) kar degi**, jabki **H₂ limewater mein koi badlav nahi karegi**. (Aur sabun ke bulbulon ke paas jalti tili laane par H₂ 'pop' ki aawaz ke saath jalti hai, jabki CO₂ aag ko bujha deti hai.)

7. CO₂ aur SO₂ adhatuon (non-metals) ke oxides hain, jo prakriti mein **acidic** hote hain. Isliye we bases/alkalis ke saath neutralisation jaisi abhikriya karke salt aur paani banate hain.
   Balanced equation: **SO₂(g) + 2NaOH(aq) → Na₂SO₃(aq) + H₂O(l)** *(Jaanch: S 1=1, O 2+2=3+1=4, Na 2=2, H 2=2 ✅)*.

8. (a) Shuru mein KOH (base) ke kaaran phenolphthalein ka rang **gulabi (pink)** hoga.
   (b) Boond-boond dilute H₂SO₄ daalne par acid ke H⁺ ions base ke saare OH⁻ ions ko neutralise karke paani (H₂O) bana dete hain. Jab ghol neutral (ya halka acidic) ho jaata hai, to phenolphthalein **rangheen (colourless)** ho jaata hai.
   (c) Bina aur indicator daale, bas usmein **thodi-si boondein KOH (base) ki** wapas mila do: ghol phir se basic hote hi **gulabi** ho jaayega!

9. (a) **False** ❌. Copper (Cu) reactivity series mein Hydrogen se neeche hai, isliye wo dilute HCl ke saath reaction **nahi** karta.
   (b) **True** ✅. Chalk, marble aur limestone teeno ka rasayanik sutra **CaCO₃** hi hai.
   (c) **False** ❌. CaCO₃ paani mein aghulansheel hai, par **Ca(HCO₃)₂ paani mein ghulansheel (soluble)** hai.
   (d) **True** ✅. Metal oxides basic aur non-metal oxides acidic hote hain.

10. Molar masses:
    - NaHCO₃ = 23 + 1 + 12 + (3 × 16) = **84 g/mol**.
    - NaCl = 23 + 35.5 = **58.5 g/mol**.
    - CO₂ = 12 + 32 = **44 g/mol**.
    - (HCl = 36.5 g/mol; H₂O = 18 g/mol.)
    (a) NaHCO₃ ke moles = 16.8 g ÷ 84 g/mol = **0.2 mol**.
    (b) Equation NaHCO₃ + HCl → NaCl + H₂O + CO₂ mein sabka ratio 1 : 1 : 1 : 1 : 1 hai.
        Isliye 0.2 mol NaHCO₃ se **0.2 mol NaCl** banega → Mass = 0.2 × 58.5 = **11.7 g**.
    (c) **0.2 mol CO₂** banegi → Mass = 0.2 × 44 = **8.8 g**.
    *(Jaanch: Kharch hua HCl = 0.2 × 36.5 = 7.3 g; bana hua H₂O = 0.2 × 18 = 3.6 g. Reactants = 16.8 + 7.3 = 24.1 g; Products = 11.7 + 3.6 + 8.8 = 24.1 g ✅!)*
