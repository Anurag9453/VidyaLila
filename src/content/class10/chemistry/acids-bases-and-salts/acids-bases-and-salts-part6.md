# Topic 6: Namak Se Banne Wale Ratan 1: Common Salt, Caustic Soda, Bleaching Powder aur Baking Soda

[Part 5](acids-bases-and-salts-part5.md) mein humne dekha ki Hydrochloric acid (HCl) aur Sodium hydroxide (NaOH) ke milne se jo neutral salt **Sodium chloride (NaCl)** banta hai, wahi humare bhojan ka **Common Salt (sadharan namak)** hai. Par NaCl ki kahani rasoi ki namak-daani par khatam nahi hoti — wahan se to asli industrial chemistry shuru hoti hai! Aaj ke topic mein hum dekhenge ki isi sadharan namak se **Caustic Soda (NaOH)**, **Bleaching Powder (CaOCl₂)**, aur **Baking Soda (NaHCO₃)** kaise bante hain.

## Alibaug ke namak ke khet aur rasoi ke pakodon ka raaz

Garmiyon ki chhuttiyon mein **Ketaki** apne chacha **Mr. Borgaonkar** ke saath Alibaug ke samudri kinare ghoomne gayi thi. Chacha ek chemical plant mein senior engineer the. Kinare par chhote-chhote chaukor khet (shallow salt pans) bane the jinmein dhoop se samudra ka paani sookh kar safed namak ke dher ban raha tha.

Mr. Borgaonkar ne ek mutthi safed namak uthakar kaha, "Ketaki, jo namak (NaCl) rasoi mein 20 rupaye kilo milta hai, wahi desh ki sabse badi chemical factories ka 'King Raw Material' hai! Tumhare nahane ke sabun se lekar paani saaf karne wale bleaching powder aur cake phulane wale baking soda tak — sab isi namak ki aulaad hain."

Shaam ko jab we ghar laute, to Ketaki ki maa rasoi mein garma-garam pakode aur vanilla cake bana rahi thin. Ketaki ne dekha ki maa ne cake ke ghol mein **Baking Powder** daala jisse cake ekdam naram aur jaledar (spongy) phoola. Ketaki ko yaad aaya ki pichhle mahine jab usne galti se cake mein sirf **Baking Soda** zyada daal diya tha, to cake swaad mein **kadwa (bitter)** aur peela ho gaya tha!

Ketaki ne turant poocha, "Chacha ji, samudra ke namak se ye baking soda kaise banta hai? Aur baking soda aur baking powder mein aisa kya farq hai ki ek se cake kadwa ho jaata hai aur doosre se bilkul swadisht?"

Mr. Borgaonkar muskuraye: "Bahut badhiya sawaal! Chalo, namak ki tijori se nikalne wale in ratnon ko ek-ek karke samajhte hain."

## Part A: Common Salt (NaCl) aur Chlor-Alkali Process (NaOH)

### 1. Common Salt (NaCl) Kahan Se Milta Hai?

1. **Samudra ke Paani Se (From Sea Water):** Samudra ke paani mein kai ghulansheel salts hote hain. Uthle khet (shallow salt pans) mein samudra ka paani rok kar dhoop aur hawa se bhaap banakar udaya (evaporate kiya) jaata hai, aur piche thos namak bach jaata hai, jise saaf karke shuddh NaCl milta hai.
2. **Chattani Namak Se (Rock Salt):** Hazaron saal pehle jab prachin samudra sookh gaye, to unke namak ki moti parat zameen ke neeche dab gayi. In chattano ko **Rock Salt (Sendha Namak)** kehte hain. Ashuddhiyon (impurities) ki wajah se ye aksar **bhoore (brown) rang** ka hota hai aur ise koyle ki tarah khod (mine) kar nikala jaata hai.

```
   COMMON SALT (NaCl): EK RAW MATERIAL, CHAR BADE CHEMICALS

                                ┌──► 1. Caustic Soda (NaOH) + Cl₂ + H₂ [Part 6]
                                │
                                ├──► 2. Bleaching Powder (CaOCl₂) [Part 6]
    [ Common Salt: NaCl ] ──────┤
     (Raw Material Hub)         ├──► 3. Baking Soda (NaHCO₃) [Part 6]
                                │
                                └──► 4. Washing Soda (Na₂CO₃·10H₂O) [Part 7]
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Sadharan namak (NaCl) kai rozmarra ke bade chemicals banane ka mool raw material hai.

### 2. Sodium Hydroxide (NaOH — Caustic Soda): Chlor-Alkali Process

Jab Sodium chloride ke gaadhe jaliya ghol (**concentrated aqueous solution of NaCl**, jise **Brine** kehte hain) mein se bijli (electric current) guzari jaati hai, to wo toot kar (electrolysis se) **Sodium hydroxide (NaOH)**, **Chlorine gas (Cl₂)**, aur **Hydrogen gas (H₂)** banata hai.

- **Ise "Chlor-Alkali Process" kyun kehte hain?**
  Kyunki iske products mein **Chlor** ka matlab hai **Chlorine (Cl₂)** aur **Alkali** ka matlab hai **Sodium hydroxide (NaOH)**!
- **Balanced Chemical Equation:**
  2NaCl(aq) [Brine] + 2H₂O(l) --(Electricity / Electrolysis)--> 2NaOH(aq) + Cl₂(g)↑ + H₂(g)↑

```
   CHLOR-ALKALI PROCESS CELL: KAHAN KYA BANTA HAI?

              (+) ANODE                         (–) CATHODE
           [ Cl₂(g) Gas ↑ ]                  [ H₂(g) Gas ↑ ]
                  ▲                                 ▲
        ┌─────────┴───────────────┬─────────────────┴─────────┐
        │   (+)                   │                     (–)   │
        │  Anode                  │                   Cathode │
        │  ║║║                    │                     ║║║   │
        │  ║║║    Brine In ──►    │     ──► NaOH(aq)    ║║║   │
        │  ║║║   (NaCl + H₂O)     │    (Near Cathode)   ║║║   │
        │                         │                           │
        │        Cl⁻ ions         │      Na⁺ aur H⁺ ions      │
        │     move to Anode       │      move to Cathode      │
        └─────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────┘
                           (Membrane Cell)
```

> **Figure 6.2** – Brine ke electrolysis mein Anode (+) par Cl₂ gas, Cathode (–) par H₂ gas, aur Cathode ke paas ghol mein NaOH banta hai.

#### Teenon Products Kahan Milte Hain aur Unke Pramukh Uses:

| Product | Cell Mein Kahan Milta Hai? | Pramukh Upyog (Important Uses) |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Chlorine gas (Cl₂)** | **Anode (+ electrode)** par mukt hoti hai | Peene ke paani aur swimming pool ko disinfect/sterilise karne mein; **PVC** (polyvinyl chloride), **Bleaching powder (CaOCl₂)**, pesticides aur CFCs banane mein. |
| **2. Hydrogen gas (H₂)** | **Cathode (– electrode)** par mukt hoti hai | Rocket fuel, **Margarine** (vanaspati ghee / hydrogenation of oils), aur khaad (fertilizers) ke liye **Ammonia (NH₃)** banane mein. |
| **3. Sodium hydroxide (NaOH)** | **Cathode ke paas** ghol mein banta hai | Metals se tel-grease saaf karne (**degreasing metals**), **soaps aur detergents** banane, **paper making** (kaagaz udyog), aur artificial fibres (jaise **rayon**) banane mein. |
| **Bonus: HCl aur Bleach** | H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl; Cl₂ + 2NaOH → NaCl + NaOCl + H₂O | Hydrochloric acid (HCl) steel ki safaai (pickling), medicines aur cosmetics mein kaam aata hai; NaOCl household bleach mein kaam aata hai. |

## Part B: Bleaching Powder (CaOCl₂ — Calcium Oxychloride)

Jab Chlor-alkali process ke anode se nikli **Chlorine gas (Cl₂)** ko sookhe bujhe hue chune (**dry slaked lime, Ca(OH)₂**) ke upar se guzara jaata hai, to **Bleaching Powder** banta hai.

- **Chemical Name:** Calcium oxychloride
- **Chemical Formula:** **CaOCl₂** (Formula unit mass = 40 + 16 + 2(35.5) = 127 u)
- **Preparation (Balanced Equation):**
  Ca(OH)₂(s) [Dry slaked lime] + Cl₂(g) → CaOCl₂(s) [Bleaching powder] + H₂O(l)

### Bleaching Powder ke Pramukh Gunn aur Uses:

1. **Halka Peela Powder aur Chlorine ki Teekhi Gandh:** Ye ek yellowish-white powder hai jisme se chlorine ki gandh aati hai, kyunki khuli hawa ki CO₂ ya dilute acid ke sampark mein aane par ye chlorine gas chhodta hai:
   CaOCl₂(s) + H₂SO₄(aq) (dilute) → CaSO₄(aq) + Cl₂(g)↑ + H₂O(l)
2. **Textile aur Paper Industry mein Bleaching:** Suti (cotton) aur linen ke kapdon ko textile industry mein, lakdi ke gude (**wood pulp**) ko paper factories mein, aur laundry mein dhule kapdon ko safed (bleach) karne ke liye.
3. **Oxidising Agent:** Kai chemical industries mein ek taakatwar **oxidising agent** ke roop mein.
4. **Drinking Water Disinfectant:** Peene ke paani ko kitanu-mukt (**germ-free / disinfecting water**) banane ke liye.

> **Suraksha (bahut zaroori):** Bleaching powder ya ghar ka bleach **kabhi bhi** kisi doosre cleaner (jaise toilet cleaner, jismein acid hota hai, ya ammonia wale glass cleaner) ke saath mat milao. Milte hi zeherili **chlorine** (ya usse milti-julti) gas nikal sakti hai, jo saans ki nali ko jala deti hai. Bleaching powder ko sirf bade, dastane pehen kar, khuli hawa wali jagah par istemal karte hain.

## Part C: Baking Soda (NaHCO₃ — Sodium Hydrogencarbonate)

Ab Ketaki ke sawaal par aate hain! Rasoi mein pakode kurkure banane ya chhole jaldi ubalne ke liye jo safed powder daala jaata hai, wo **Baking Soda** hai.

- **Chemical Name:** Sodium hydrogencarbonate (ya Sodium bicarbonate)
- **Chemical Formula:** **NaHCO₃** (Formula unit mass = 23 + 1 + 12 + 3(16) = 84 u)
- **Industrial Preparation (Solvay / Ammonia-Soda Process):**
  Jab thande aur concentrated brine (NaCl + H₂O) mein se **Carbon dioxide (CO₂)** aur **Ammonia (NH₃)** gas guzari jaati hai, to NaHCO₃ (jo thande paani mein bahut kam ghulansheel yaani sparingly soluble hai) safed crystals ke roop mein neeche baith jaata hai:
  NaCl + H₂O + CO₂ + NH₃ → NH₄Cl (Ammonium chloride) + NaHCO₃↓ (Baking soda)

### Baking Soda ke Gunn aur 3 Sabse Bade Uses:

#### 1. Khana Pakate Waqt Garam Karne Par Kya Hota Hai? (Action of Heat)
Baking soda ek **mild non-corrosive basic salt** (pH ≈ 8.3) hai. Jab ise khana pakate waqt garam kiya jaata hai, to ye toot kar (thermal decomposition) **Sodium carbonate (Na₂CO₃)**, bhaap (H₂O) aur **Carbon dioxide gas (CO₂)** deta hai:
2NaHCO₃(s) --(Heat)--> Na₂CO₃(s) + H₂O(g) + CO₂(g)↑

#### 2. Use 1 – Baking Powder Banane Mein (Baking Soda vs Baking Powder ka Raaz!)
Agar hum cake ya bread mein **sirf Baking Soda (NaHCO₃)** daal kar bake karein, to CO₂ gas se cake phool to jaayega, par piche banne wala **Sodium carbonate (Na₂CO₃)** cake ko **kadwa (bitter, soapy taste)** aur **peele rang** ka bana dega (jaisa Ketaki ke saath hua tha)!
- Is dikkat ko door karne ke liye **Baking Powder** banaya jaata hai, jo **Baking Soda (NaHCO₃)** aur ek halke khane yogya thos acid (**mild edible acid, jaise Tartaric acid**) ka mishran hota hai.
- Jab baking powder mein paani milta hai ya use garam kiya jaata hai, to tartaric acid ke H⁺ ions NaHCO₃ (aur kisi bhi Na₂CO₃) ko neutralise karke CO₂ gas, paani aur us acid ka sodium salt banate hain (jo kadwa nahi hota):
  NaHCO₃ + H⁺ (from edible tartaric acid) → CO₂(g)↑ + H₂O(l) + Sodium salt of acid (Neutral, not bitter!)
- Nikalne wali **CO₂ gas** ke bulbule maide ke andar se nikalte hue cake aur bread ko **soft aur spongy (jaledar)** bana dete hain, aur Na₂CO₃ ka kadwapan bhi bilkul nahi rehta!

> **Activity 6.1 (ghar par adult ke saath).** Do chhoti katoriyon mein barabar maida aur paani gholo. Katori A mein chauthaai chammach **sirf baking soda** daalo, aur Katori B mein chauthaai chammach **baking powder** (ya baking soda + 2 boond nimbu ka ras). Bade inhe bhaap mein (idli stand mein) pakayein; garam bartan aur bhaap sirf bade sambhalein. Thanda hone par dono ko kaat kar **dekho aur chhoo kar** tulna karo (chakhna zaroori nahi): Katori B ka tukda zyada phoola, jaledar aur naram hoga, jabki Katori A wala halka peela-sa dikhega aur usmein soda jaisi gandh aa sakti hai (wahi Na₂CO₃ jo kadwapan deta hai).

#### 3. Use 2 – Antacid Ki Tarah (Pet ki Acidity Door Karne Mein)
Kyunki NaHCO₃ mildly alkaline hai, ye pet ke extra Hydrochloric acid (HCl) ko neutralise karke jalan aur dard se turant aaram deta hai:
NaHCO₃(s) + HCl(aq) → NaCl(aq) + H₂O(l) + CO₂(g)↑

#### 4. Use 3 – Soda-Acid Fire Extinguisher Mein (Aag Bujhane Wale Yantra Mein)
Soda-acid fire extinguisher ke andar metal cylinder mein NaHCO₃ ka ghol hota hai aur upar ek kaanch ki shishi mein Sulphuric acid (H₂SO₄) hota hai. Knob dabane par shishi toot-ti hai aur tezaab NaHCO₃ se milkar bhaari matra mein **CO₂ gas** tezi se bahar fenkta hai:
2NaHCO₃(aq) + H₂SO₄(aq) → Na₂SO₄(aq) + 2H₂O(l) + 2CO₂(g)↑
- CO₂ gas hawa se bhaari hoti hai aur na khud jalti hai na jalne mein madad karti hai — ye aag ke chaaron taraf kambal (blanket) ki tarah chha kar **oxygen (O₂) ki supply kaat deti hai** aur aag bujh jaati hai!

## Part D: Solved Examples

### Example 1. Chlor-Alkali Process mein Mass aur Products ki Ganna

**Sawaal:** Ek Chlor-alkali plant ke electrolysis cell mein **234 g pure Sodium chloride (NaCl)** paani (H₂O) ke saath poori tarah electrolyse hota hai.
1. Balanced equation likho aur batao anode aur cathode par konsi gases niklengi.
2. Is reaction mein kitne gram Caustic soda (NaOH), kitne gram Chlorine gas (Cl₂), aur kitne gram Hydrogen gas (H₂) banegi?
*(Atomic masses: Na = 23 u, Cl = 35.5 u, O = 16 u, H = 1 u)*

**Hal:**
- **Step 1 (Equation aur Electrodes):**
  2NaCl(aq) + 2H₂O(l) --(Electricity)--> 2NaOH(aq) + Cl₂(g)↑ + H₂(g)↑
  - **Anode (+):** Chlorine gas (Cl₂)
  - **Cathode (–):** Hydrogen gas (H₂)
- **Step 2 (Equation Masses):**
  - 2NaCl = 2 × (23 + 35.5) = 2 × 58.5 = 117 u
  - 2H₂O = 2 × 18 = 36 u
  - 2NaOH = 2 × (23 + 16 + 1) = 2 × 40 = 80 u
  - Cl₂ = 2 × 35.5 = 71 u
  - H₂ = 2 × 1 = 2 u
- **Step 3 (Mass Calculation):**
  - 117 g NaCl se 80 g NaOH, 71 g Cl₂ aur 2 g H₂ banta hai.
  - Humare paas 234 g NaCl hai, jo 117 g ka theek **234 ÷ 117 = 2 guna** hai!
  - Isliye:
    - Mass of NaOH formed = 80 × 2 = **160 g**
    - Mass of Cl₂ gas at anode = 71 × 2 = **142 g**
    - Mass of H₂ gas at cathode = 2 × 2 = **4 g**

**Jaanch:** Reacted H₂O = 36 × 2 = 72 g. Kul reactants = 234 + 72 = 306 g; Kul products = 160 + 142 + 4 = 306 g ✅.

### Example 2. Baking Soda ko Garam Karne Par Mass Loss aur CO₂ ki Matra

**Sawaal:** Lab mein teacher ne (teacher demo) ek khule test tube mein **8.40 g pure Baking soda (NaHCO₃)** lekar use tab tak garam kiya jab tak uska decomposition poora nahi ho gaya.
1. Balanced equation likho.
2. Test tube ke andar kitne gram thos Sodium carbonate (Na₂CO₃) bachega, aur kitne gram CO₂ gas bahar niklegi?
*(Atomic masses: Na = 23 u, H = 1 u, C = 12 u, O = 16 u)*

**Hal:**
- **Step 1 (Balanced Chemical Equation):**
  2NaHCO₃(s) --(Heat)--> Na₂CO₃(s) + H₂O(g)↑ + CO₂(g)↑
- **Step 2 (Formula Unit Masses):**
  - 2NaHCO₃ = 2 × (23 + 1 + 12 + 48) = 2 × 84 = 168 u
  - Na₂CO₃ = 2(23) + 12 + 3(16) = 106 u
  - H₂O = 18 u; CO₂ = 44 u
- **Step 3 (Mass Calculation):**
  - Scaling factor = 8.40 ÷ 168 = 0.05.
  - Solid Na₂CO₃ left in test tube = 106 × 0.05 = **5.30 g**.
  - Mass of CO₂ gas evolved = 44 × 0.05 = **2.20 g**.
  - Mass of H₂O vapour evolved = 18 × 0.05 = **0.90 g**.

**Jaanch:** Products ka kul mass = 5.30 + 2.20 + 0.90 = 8.40 g, jo shuruati NaHCO₃ (8.40 g) ke barabar hai ✅.

### Example 3. Slaked Lime se Bleaching Powder ka Nirmaan

**Sawaal:** Ek bleaching powder unit mein **18.50 g dry slaked lime (Ca(OH)₂)** par excess Chlorine gas (Cl₂) guzari gayi.
1. Kitne gram Bleaching powder (CaOCl₂) banega?
2. Is reaction mein kitne gram Chlorine gas (Cl₂) kharch hogi?
*(Atomic masses: Ca = 40 u, O = 16 u, H = 1 u, Cl = 35.5 u)*

**Hal:**
- **Step 1 (Balanced Chemical Equation):**
  Ca(OH)₂(s) + Cl₂(g) → CaOCl₂(s) + H₂O(l)
- **Step 2 (Formula Unit Masses):**
  - Ca(OH)₂ = 40 + 2(16 + 1) = 74 u
  - Cl₂ = 2(35.5) = 71 u
  - CaOCl₂ = 40 + 16 + 71 = 127 u
  - H₂O = 18 u
- **Step 3 (Mass Calculation):**
  - Moles of Ca(OH)₂ = 18.50 ÷ 74 = 0.25 mol.
  - Mass of Bleaching powder (CaOCl₂) formed = 0.25 × 127 = **31.75 g**.
  - Mass of Cl₂ gas consumed = 0.25 × 71 = **17.75 g**.

**Jaanch:** Banne wale H₂O ka mass = 0.25 × 18 = 4.50 g. Reactants = 18.50 + 17.75 = 36.25 g; Products = 31.75 + 4.50 = 36.25 g ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: Chlor-alkali process mein Anode aur Cathode ke gases ko ulta likh dena.**
   Yaad rakho: Chloride ion (Cl⁻) negative hai, isliye wo positive electrode **Anode (+)** par jaakar **Cl₂ gas** deta hai! Aur **H₂ gas** negative electrode **Cathode (–)** par nikalti hai.
2. **Galti 2: Bleaching powder (CaOCl₂) aur Calcium chloride (CaCl₂) ko ek samajhna.**
   Bleaching powder **Calcium oxychloride (CaOCl₂)** hai jo dry Ca(OH)₂ + Cl₂ se banta hai; CaCl₂ mein koi bleaching gunn nahi hota!
3. **Galti 3: Baking Soda (NaHCO₃) aur Baking Powder ko ek hi cheez maan lena.**
   **Baking soda** sirf NaHCO₃ hai, jabki **Baking powder** = NaHCO₃ + Mild edible acid (Tartaric acid) ka mishran hai taaki Na₂CO₃ ka kadwapan khatam ho sake.
4. **Galti 4: Baking soda ko garam karne par NaOH bana dena.**
   NaHCO₃ ko garam karne par **Na₂CO₃ (Sodium carbonate)**, H₂O aur CO₂ banta hai (2NaHCO₃ → Na₂CO₃ + H₂O + CO₂), NaOH nahi!
5. **Galti 5: "Brine" ka matlab pighla hua (molten) NaCl likh dena.**
   **Brine** paani mein NaCl ka gaadha ghol (**concentrated aqueous solution of NaCl**) hota hai. Pighle hue (molten) NaCl ke electrolysis mein paani nahi hota, isliye wahan NaOH aur H₂ nahi bante (sirf Na metal aur Cl₂ banta hai)!

## Quick Recap

- **Common Salt (NaCl):** Samudra ke paani ke evaporation aur zameen ke neeche **Rock Salt** ki khudai se milta hai. Ye NaOH, CaOCl₂, NaHCO₃, aur Na₂CO₃·10H₂O banane ka mukhya raw material hai.
- **Chlor-Alkali Process:** Brine (NaCl(aq)) ke electrolysis se teen cheezein milti hain:
  2NaCl(aq) + 2H₂O(l) --(Electricity)--> 2NaOH(aq) (near cathode) + Cl₂(g) (at anode) + H₂(g) (at cathode)
- **Bleaching Powder (CaOCl₂ — Calcium oxychloride):**
  Ca(OH)₂ (dry slaked lime) + Cl₂ → CaOCl₂ + H₂O. Kapdon/kaagaz ki bleaching, oxidising agent, aur peene ke paani ko disinfect karne ke kaam aata hai.
- **Baking Soda (NaHCO₃ — Sodium hydrogencarbonate):**
  - Preparation: NaCl + H₂O + CO₂ + NH₃ → NH₄Cl + NaHCO₃
  - Heating: 2NaHCO₃ --(Heat)--> Na₂CO₃ + H₂O + CO₂↑
  - **Baking Powder** = NaHCO₃ + Mild edible acid (Tartaric acid) taaki CO₂ se cake spongy bane aur Na₂CO₃ ka kadwapan neutralise ho jaaye.
  - Antacid aur **Soda-Acid Fire Extinguisher** mein bhi mukhya bhoomika nibhata hai.

8. In a Chlor-alkali plant, **35.10 g of pure NaCl** is completely electrolysed in aqueous solution. Calculate (a) the mass of NaOH produced, and (b) the mass of Cl₂ gas liberated at the anode. (Na = 23 u, Cl = 35.5 u, O = 16 u, H = 1 u)
9. Calculate the mass of dry slaked lime (Ca(OH)₂) required to react completely with Chlorine gas (Cl₂) to manufacture **50.80 g of Bleaching Powder (CaOCl₂)**. Also find the mass of water formed as a by-product. (Ca = 40 u, O = 16 u, H = 1 u, Cl = 35.5 u)
10. A soda-acid fire extinguisher contains **25.20 g of Sodium hydrogencarbonate (NaHCO₃)** which reacts completely with excess dilute Sulphuric acid (H₂SO₄) when triggered. Calculate the mass of CO₂ gas released and the mass of Na₂SO₄ formed in the solution. (Na = 23 u, H = 1 u, C = 12 u, O = 16 u, S = 32 u)

### Answers

1. Sodium chloride (NaCl) ke concentrated aqueous solution (gaadhe jaliya ghol) ko **Brine** kehte hain.
   Iske electrolysis ko **Chlor-alkali process** isliye kehte hain kyunki ismein banne wale do mukhya products **Chlor** (Chlorine, Cl₂) aur **Alkali** (Sodium hydroxide, NaOH) hain:
   2NaCl(aq) + 2H₂O(l) --(Electricity)--> 2NaOH(aq) + Cl₂(g)↑ + H₂(g)↑

2. (a) **At Anode (+):** **Chlorine gas (Cl₂)** — Uses: (i) Peene ke paani ko disinfect karne mein, (ii) Bleaching powder (CaOCl₂) aur PVC banane mein.
   (b) **At Cathode (–):** **Hydrogen gas (H₂)** — Uses: (i) Rocket fuel ke roop mein, (ii) Fertilizers ke liye Ammonia (NH₃) aur vanaspati ghee (margarine) banane mein.
   (c) **Near the Cathode:** **Sodium hydroxide (NaOH)** — Uses: (i) Soaps aur detergents banane mein, (ii) Paper industry aur artificial fibres (rayon) banane mein.

3. - **Chemical Name:** Calcium oxychloride; **Formula:** CaOCl₂.
   - **Preparation:** Dry slaked lime (Ca(OH)₂) par Chlorine gas (Cl₂) pass karke:
     Ca(OH)₂(s) + Cl₂(g) → CaOCl₂(s) + H₂O(l)
   - **Uses:** (i) Textile industry mein cotton aur linen ko bleach karne ke liye, (ii) Paper factories mein wood pulp ko bleach karne ke liye, (iii) Peene ke paani ko germ-free (disinfect) karne aur chemical industries mein oxidising agent ke roop mein.

4. Khuli hawa mein maujood Carbon dioxide (CO₂) aur nami (moisture) Bleaching powder (CaOCl₂) ke saath dheere-dheere abhikriya karke **Chlorine gas (Cl₂)** mukt karti rehti hai (CaOCl₂ + CO₂ → CaCO₃ + Cl₂↑). Isi nikalne wali Cl₂ gas ki wajah se usmein teekhi gandh aati hai. Agar ise airtight dibbe mein na rakha jaaye, to saari active chlorine ud jaayegi aur bleaching powder apni bleaching taakat kho dega.

5. - **Balanced Equation:** NaCl + H₂O + CO₂ + NH₃ → NH₄Cl + NaHCO₃↓
   - **Kaaran:** Thande paani (cold solution) mein Sodium hydrogencarbonate (NaHCO₃) **sparingly soluble** (bahut kam ghulansheel) hota hai, jabki Ammonium chloride (NH₄Cl) khoob ghulansheel hota hai. Isliye NaHCO₃ safed crystals bankar pehle neeche baith jaata hai jise chhaan (filter) kar alag kar liya jaata hai.

6. - **Baking Soda** ek akela compound hai — pure **Sodium hydrogencarbonate (NaHCO₃)**.
   - **Baking Powder** ek mishran (mixture) hai — **Baking Soda (NaHCO₃) + ek mild edible solid acid (jaise Tartaric acid)**.
   - Agar baker cake mein **sirf Baking Soda** daalega, to garam hone par:
     2NaHCO₃(s) --(Heat)--> Na₂CO₃(s) + H₂O(g) + CO₂(g)↑
     Banne wala **Sodium carbonate (Na₂CO₃)** cake ko **kadwa (bitter)** aur peela bana dega!
   - Jabki **Baking Powder** mein maujood tartaric acid ke H⁺ ions us basic Na₂CO₃ ko neutralise kar dete hain (NaHCO₃ + H⁺ → CO₂↑ + H₂O + neutral sodium salt of acid), jisse cake bina kadwe swaad ke naram aur spongy banta hai.

7. Soda-acid fire extinguisher mein Sodium hydrogencarbonate (NaHCO₃) ghol aur Sulphuric acid (H₂SO₄) alag-alag rakhe hote hain. Knob dabane par dono aapas mein milte hain aur tezi se **CO₂ gas** nikalte hain:
   2NaHCO₃(aq) + H₂SO₄(aq) → Na₂SO₄(aq) + 2H₂O(l) + 2CO₂(g)↑
   Ye CO₂ gas jalte hue padarth ke chaaron taraf kambal (blanket) ki tarah chha kar **hawa (oxygen) ka sampark kaat deti hai**, jisse aag turant bujh jaati hai.

8. Balanced Equation: 2NaCl(aq) + 2H₂O(l) → 2NaOH(aq) + Cl₂(g)↑ + H₂(g)↑
   - Masses in Equation: 2NaCl = 117 u, 2NaOH = 80 u, Cl₂ = 71 u, H₂ = 2 u.
   - Scaling factor = 35.10 ÷ 117 = 0.30.
   - (a) Mass of NaOH produced = 80 × 0.30 = **24.00 g**.
   - (b) Mass of Cl₂ liberated at anode = 71 × 0.30 = **21.30 g** ✅ (Jaanch: Reacted H₂O = 36 × 0.30 = 10.80 g, H₂ = 2 × 0.30 = 0.60 g; Reactants 35.10 + 10.80 = 45.90 g = Products 24.00 + 21.30 + 0.60 = 45.90 g ✅).

9. Balanced Equation: Ca(OH)₂(s) + Cl₂(g) → CaOCl₂(s) + H₂O(l)
   - Formula Unit Masses: Ca(OH)₂ = 74 u, Cl₂ = 71 u, CaOCl₂ = 127 u, H₂O = 18 u.
   - Moles of CaOCl₂ to be manufactured = 50.80 ÷ 127 = 0.40 mol.
   - Mass of Ca(OH)₂ required = 0.40 × 74 = **29.60 g**.
   - Mass of H₂O formed = 0.40 × 18 = **7.20 g** ✅ (Jaanch: Cl₂ used = 0.40 × 71 = 28.40 g; Reactants 29.60 + 28.40 = 58.00 g = Products 50.80 + 7.20 = 58.00 g ✅).

10. Balanced Equation: 2NaHCO₃(aq) + H₂SO₄(aq) → Na₂SO₄(aq) + 2H₂O(l) + 2CO₂(g)↑
    - Equation Masses: 2NaHCO₃ = 168 u, H₂SO₄ = 98 u, Na₂SO₄ = 142 u, 2H₂O = 36 u, 2CO₂ = 88 u.
    - Scaling factor = 25.20 ÷ 168 = 0.15.
    - Mass of CO₂ gas released = 88 × 0.15 = **13.20 g**.
    - Mass of Na₂SO₄ formed = 142 × 0.15 = **21.30 g** ✅ (Jaanch: Reacted H₂SO₄ = 98 × 0.15 = 14.70 g, 2H₂O = 36 × 0.15 = 5.40 g; Reactants 25.20 + 14.70 = 39.90 g = Products 21.30 + 5.40 + 13.20 = 39.90 g ✅).
