# Topic 7: Namak Se Banne Wale Ratan 2: Washing Soda, Plaster of Paris aur Chapter Ka Saar

Class 9 ke Water chapter ([water-part4.md](../../../class9/chemistry/water/water-part4.md)) mein humne dekha tha ki kai chamakdar salt crystals — jaise neela CuSO₄·5H₂O aur hara FeSO₄·7H₂O — chhune mein bilkul sookhe lagte hain, par unke har formula unit ke andar paani ke molecules ki ek fixed sankhya (**Water of Crystallisation**) chhupi hoti hai. Aaj ke aakhri topic mein hum isi Water of Crystallisation ke do sabse mashhoor industrial sitaron — kapde dhone aur paani ki kathorta door karne wale **Washing Soda (Na₂CO₃·10H₂O)** aur tooti haddiyon ko jodne wale **Plaster of Paris (CaSO₄·½H₂O)** — ki poori kahani samjhenge.

---

## Dr. Nerkar ka plaster room aur 15 minute mein patthar banne wali patti

Football match ke dauran girne se **Soumil** ki daayin kalai (wrist) mein hairline fracture ho gaya. Orthopedic clinic ke plaster room mein **Dr. Nerkar** ne ek kas kar band kiye hue moisture-proof tin ke dibbe mein se safed powder lagi patti (bandage roll) nikali, use gungune paani ke katori mein sirf 5 second duboya aur Soumil ki kalai ke chaaron taraf saleeqe se lapet diya.

Soumil ne hairani se dekha ki 10 se 15 minute ke andar wo naram geeli patti halka-sa garam hokar bilkul **patthar jaisi sakht (hard solid cast)** ban gayi!

Soumil ne poocha, "Doctor sahab, paani lagne se to kagaz aur aata naram ho jaate hain, phir ye safed powder paani milte hi itna sakht kaise ho gaya? Aur aapne is patti ko hawa-band tin ke dibbe mein kyun chhupa rakha tha?"

Dr. Nerkar muskuraye: "Soumil, is safed powder ka naam hai **Plaster of Paris (CaSO₄·½H₂O)**. Ye asal mein pyaasa **Gypsum** hai! Bhatti mein garam karte waqt isse iska paani chheen liya gaya tha. Jaise hi maine ise paani mein duboya, isne apna khoya hua **Water of Crystallisation** wapas pi liya aur **Gypsum (CaSO₄·2H₂O)** ke sakht crystals ka jaal bana liya! Agar main ise khuli hawa mein chhod deta, to ye hawa ki nami pi kar dibbe ke andar hi patthar ban jaata." Chalo, Soumil ke plaster aur Washing soda ke is poore vigyan ko samajhte hain.

## Part A: Water of Crystallisation (Class 9 se Tez Bridge) aur Washing Soda (Na₂CO₃·10H₂O)

### 1. Water of Crystallisation: Ek Tez Dohraav
[Class 9 Water Topic 4](../../../class9/chemistry/water/water-part4.md) mein humne seekha tha ki jab **neele CuSO₄·5H₂O crystals** ko ek sookhi boiling tube mein garam kiya jaata hai, to paani ki boondein tube ki upari deewar par jam jaati hain aur neela salt **safed anhydrous CuSO₄** ban jaata hai. Us safed powder par paani ki 2-3 boondein daalte hi wo wapas **neela** ho jaata hai:
CuSO₄·5H₂O(s) [Blue] ⇌ CuSO₄(s) [White] + 5H₂O

Yahi fixed paani ke molecules (CuSO₄·5H₂O mein 5, FeSO₄·7H₂O mein 7, Na₂CO₃·10H₂O mein 10, aur CaSO₄·2H₂O mein 2) crystals ko unka aakaar (shape) aur kabhi-kabhi unka rang dete hain.

---

### 2. Washing Soda (Na₂CO₃·10H₂O — Sodium Carbonate Decahydrate)

[Part 6](acids-bases-and-salts-part6.md) mein humne dekha tha ki Baking soda (NaHCO₃) ko garam karne par anhydrous **Sodium carbonate (Na₂CO₃ — Soda Ash)** banta hai. Jab isi Soda Ash ko paani mein ghol kar dobara crystallise (**recrystallisation**) kiya jaata hai, to humein **Washing Soda (Na₂CO₃·10H₂O)** milta hai!

- **Chemical Name:** Sodium carbonate decahydrate (deca = 10)
- **Chemical Formula:** **Na₂CO₃·10H₂O**
- **Formula Unit Mass:**
  - Na₂CO₃ hissa = 2(23) + 12 + 3(16) = 46 + 12 + 48 = 106 u
  - 10H₂O hissa = 10 × (2 × 1 + 16) = 10 × 18 = 180 u
  - **Total Formula Unit Mass of Na₂CO₃·10H₂O** = 106 + 180 = **286 u**

#### Namak (NaCl) se Washing Soda Banane ke 3 Kadam:
1. **Step 1 (Brine se Baking Soda):**
   NaCl + H₂O + CO₂ + NH₃ → NH₄Cl + NaHCO₃↓
2. **Step 2 (Baking Soda ko Garam karke Soda Ash banana):**
   2NaHCO₃(s) --(Heat)--> Na₂CO₃(s) [Anhydrous Soda Ash] + H₂O(g)↑ + CO₂(g)↑
3. **Step 3 (Soda Ash ka Recrystallisation karke Washing Soda banana):**
   Na₂CO₃(s) + 10H₂O(l) → Na₂CO₃·10H₂O(s) [Washing Soda]

#### Washing Soda ke 4 Pramukh Uses:
1. **Glass, Soap aur Paper Industries:** Kaanch (glass), sabun (soap) aur kaagaz (paper) udyog mein badi matra mein kaam aata hai.
2. **Sodium Compounds Banana:** **Borax (Na₂B₄O₇·10H₂O)** jaise mahatvapoorna sodium compounds ke nirmaan mein.
3. **Gharelu Safaai (Domestic Cleaning Agent):** Ghar mein kapde dhone aur ziddi tel-grease ke daag saaf karne ke liye.
4. **Paani ki Permanent Hardness Door Karne Mein:** Jaisa humne [Class 9 Water Topic 3](../../../class9/chemistry/water/water-part3.md) mein seekha tha, khare paani (hard water) mein ghule Ca²⁺ aur Mg²⁺ ions ke chlorides aur sulphates ko Washing soda insoluble carbonates (CaCO₃↓, MgCO₃↓) banakar neeche bitha deta hai aur paani ko mridu (**soft water**) bana deta hai:
   CaCl₂(aq) [Hard water] + Na₂CO₃(aq) [Washing soda] → CaCO₃(s)↓ + 2NaCl(aq)

## Part B: Plaster of Paris (CaSO₄·½H₂O — Calcium Sulphate Hemihydrate)

Ab Dr. Nerkar ke plaster room ke safed powder ki poori chemistry dekhte hain:

- **Chemical Name:** Calcium sulphate hemihydrate (hemi = aadha, hydrate = paani)
- **Chemical Formula:** **CaSO₄·½H₂O** (ya **(CaSO₄)₂·H₂O**)
- **Formula Unit Masses:**
  - **Gypsum (CaSO₄·2H₂O):** 40 + 32 + 4(16) + 2(18) = 136 + 36 = **172 u**
  - **Plaster of Paris (CaSO₄·½H₂O):** 40 + 32 + 4(16) + 0.5(18) = 136 + 9 = **145 u** (ya (CaSO₄)₂·H₂O = 290 u)

### 1. Plaster of Paris Kaise Banta Hai? (373 K Temperature ka Niyam!)

Jab **Gypsum (CaSO₄·2H₂O)** ko bhatti (kiln) mein bilkul niyantrit taapmaan **373 K (100 °C)** par garam kiya jaata hai, to wo apne 2 paani ke molecules mein se **1½ (3/2) paani ke molecules kho deta hai** aur safed powder **Plaster of Paris (CaSO₄·½H₂O)** ban jaata hai:

CaSO₄·2H₂O(s) [Gypsum] --(Heat carefully at 373 K / 100 °C)--> CaSO₄·½H₂O(s) [Plaster of Paris] + 1½H₂O(g)↑

*(Ya poornank sankhyaon mein: 2(CaSO₄·2H₂O) --(373 K)--> (CaSO₄)₂·H₂O + 3H₂O↑)*

> **Sabse Zaroori Sawal — Agar Gypsum ko 373 K (100 °C) se Zyada Garam Kar Diya To Kya Hoga?**
> Agar taapmaan 373 K (100 °C) se upar chala gaya, to Gypsum ka **saara ka saara water of crystallisation** ud jaayega aur piche **anhydrous Calcium sulphate (CaSO₄)** bachega, jise **"Dead-Burnt Plaster" (Mrit Jala Plaster)** kehte hain. Dead-burnt plaster mein paani milane par wo dobara jamne (set hone) ka gunn kho deta hai!

---

### 2. Formula Mein Aadha Paani Ka Molecule (½H₂O) Kaise Ho Sakta Hai?

Kya paani ka aadha molecule (½H₂O) akela kat kar reh sakta hai? **Bilkul nahi!**
CaSO₄·½H₂O likhne ka asli matlab ye hai ki crystal mein **CaSO₄ ke do (2) formula units milkar paani ke ek (1) molecule (H₂O) ko aapas mein baant-te (share karte) hain**:

```
   CaSO₄·½H₂O KA ASLI MATLAB: 2 CaSO₄ UNITS SHARE 1 H₂O MOLECULE!

         [ CaSO₄ ] ───────┐
                          ├───► [ H — O — H ]   (1 Water Molecule Shared)
         [ CaSO₄ ] ───────┘

   2 Formula Units of CaSO₄  +  1 Molecule of H₂O  =  (CaSO₄)₂·H₂O
   Isliye PRATI EK (per 1) CaSO₄ unit hissa aaya   =  CaSO₄·½H₂O !
```

> **Figure 7.1** – Do CaSO₄ formula units ke beech ek H₂O molecule sajha (shared) hota hai, isliye prati CaSO₄ unit paani ka hissa ½H₂O likha jaata hai.

---

### 3. Paani Milane Par Plaster of Paris Sakht Kyun Ho Jaata Hai? (Setting of PoP)

Jab Plaster of Paris ke safed powder mein paani milaya jaata hai, to wo wapas 1½ paani ke molecules sokh kar **10 se 15 minute** mein **Gypsum (CaSO₄·2H₂O)** ke aapas mein uljhe hue sakht suin-jaise crystals (**interlocking needle-like hard solid mass**) bana leta hai:

CaSO₄·½H₂O(s) [Plaster of Paris] + 1½H₂O(l) → CaSO₄·2H₂O(s) [Gypsum — Hard solid]

- **Moisture-Proof Container mein kyun rakhte hain?** Kyunki agar Plaster of Paris ko khuli hawa ya nami (moisture) mein chhod diya jaaye, to wo hawa ki nami sokh kar dheere-dheere wahin **Gypsum (CaSO₄·2H₂O)** bankar patthar jaisa jam jaayega aur baad mein kisi kaam ka nahi rahega!
- **Pramukh Uses:**
  1. Doctors dwara tooti hui haddiyon (**fractured bones**) ko sahi jagah par jodne ke liye plaster chadhane mein.
  2. Khilaune (**toys**), moortiyan (**statues**), saanche (casts) aur sajawati samaan banane mein.
  3. Paint karne se pehle deewaron aur chhat (ceilings) ko barabar aur chikna (**smooth**) banane mein.
  4. Blackboard ki chalk aur aag-rodhi (**fire-proofing**) sheets banane mein.

## Part D: Solved Examples

### Example 1. Washing Soda mein Water of Crystallisation ka Percentage aur Mass Calculation

**Sawaal:**
1. Class 9 mein nikala tha ki washing soda (Na₂CO₃·10H₂O) mein paani ka mass percentage kitna hai. Us nateeje ko yaad karke batao ki 14.30 g crystal mein lagbhag kitna paani hoga.
2. Agar **14.30 g pure Washing soda crystals** ko tez garam karke poori tarah anhydrous Soda ash (Na₂CO₃) mein badal diya jaaye, to kitne gram anhydrous Na₂CO₃ bachega aur kitne gram paani bhaap bankar udega?
*(Atomic masses: Na = 23 u, C = 12 u, O = 16 u, H = 1 u)*

**Hal:**
- **Step 1 (Class 9 ka nateeja yaad karo):** Na₂CO₃·10H₂O = 106 + 180 = 286 u, aur paani = **62.94%** ([water-part4.md](../../../class9/chemistry/water/water-part4.md), Example 2). To 14.30 g crystal mein paani lagbhag 14.30 × 0.6294 = **9.00 g** hona chahiye. Ab ise equation se pakka karte hain:
- **Step 2 (14.30 g Washing Soda ko Garam Karne Par):**
  - Equation: Na₂CO₃·10H₂O(s) --(Heat)--> Na₂CO₃(s) + 10H₂O(g)↑
  - Moles of Washing soda = 14.30 ÷ 286 = 0.05 mol.
  - Mass of anhydrous Na₂CO₃ left = 0.05 × 106 = **5.30 g**.
  - Mass of water vapour lost = 0.05 × 180 = **9.00 g**.

**Jaanch:** 5.30 g (Na₂CO₃) + 9.00 g (H₂O) = 14.30 g (Shuruati Washing soda ka mass!), aur (9.00 ÷ 14.30) × 100 = 62.94% ✅.

### Example 2. Gypsum se Plaster of Paris Banane aur Setting ka Numerical

**Sawaal:** Ek factory mein **34.40 g pure Gypsum (CaSO₄·2H₂O)** ko bilkul 373 K (100 °C) par garam karke Plaster of Paris (CaSO₄·½H₂O) banaya gaya.
1. Balanced equation likho.
2. Kitne gram Plaster of Paris (CaSO₄·½H₂O) banega, aur kitne gram paani bhaap bankar niklega?
*(Atomic masses: Ca = 40 u, S = 32 u, O = 16 u, H = 1 u)*

**Hal:**
- **Step 1 (Balanced Chemical Equation):**
  CaSO₄·2H₂O(s) --(373 K)--> CaSO₄·½H₂O(s) + 1½H₂O(g)↑
- **Step 2 (Formula Unit Masses):**
  - Gypsum (CaSO₄·2H₂O) = 40 + 32 + 64 + 2(18) = 136 + 36 = 172 u
  - Plaster of Paris (CaSO₄·½H₂O) = 136 + 0.5(18) = 136 + 9 = 145 u
  - Water lost (1.5 H₂O) = 1.5 × 18 = 27 u
- **Step 3 (Mass Calculation):**
  - Moles of Gypsum = 34.40 ÷ 172 = 0.20 mol.
  - Mass of Plaster of Paris formed = 0.20 × 145 = **29.00 g**.
  - Mass of water vapour lost = 0.20 × 27 = **5.40 g**.

**Jaanch:** 29.00 g (Plaster of Paris) + 5.40 g (Water lost) = 34.40 g (Initial Gypsum) ✅.

### Example 3. "Kaun Hu Main?" — Compound Identification Puzzle

**Sawaal:** Ek safed sodium compound **'A'** (Formula mass = 84 u) cakes ko naram banane aur antacid ke roop mein kaam aata hai. Jab **'A'** ko garam kiya jaata hai, to ek naya thos compound **'B'** (Formula mass = 106 u) banta hai aur ek gas **'C'** nikalti hai jo limewater ko doodhiya kar deti hai. Jab **'B'** ka paani ke saath recrystallisation kiya jaata hai, to kapde dhone aur paani ki permanent hardness hatane wala hydrated salt **'D'** (Formula mass = 286 u) banta hai.
**'A'**, **'B'**, **'C'** aur **'D'** ko pehchano aur teeno badlavon ki balanced equations likho.

**Hal:**
1. **Compound 'A' (84 u, antacid & baking):** **Baking Soda — Sodium hydrogencarbonate (NaHCO₃)** (23 + 1 + 12 + 48 = 84 u).
2. **Compound 'B' (106 u) aur Gas 'C':** NaHCO₃ ko garam karne par:
   2NaHCO₃(s) [A] --(Heat)--> Na₂CO₃(s) [B] + H₂O(g) + CO₂(g)↑ [C]
   - **'B'** = Anhydrous **Sodium carbonate (Na₂CO₃, Soda ash)** (46 + 12 + 48 = 106 u).
   - **'C'** = **Carbon dioxide gas (CO₂)**, jo limewater ko milky karti hai:
     Ca(OH)₂(aq) + CO₂(g) → CaCO₃(s)↓ + H₂O(l)
3. **Compound 'D' (286 u, removes permanent hardness):** Na₂CO₃ ke recrystallisation se:
   Na₂CO₃(s) [B] + 10H₂O(l) → Na₂CO₃·10H₂O(s) [D]
   - **'D'** = **Washing Soda — Sodium carbonate decahydrate (Na₂CO₃·10H₂O)** (106 + 180 = 286 u).

**Jaanch:** Formula unit masses: A (NaHCO₃) = 84 u, B (Na₂CO₃) = 106 u, C (CO₂) = 44 u, D (Na₂CO₃·10H₂O) = 286 u — saare clues 100% match karte hain ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: Washing soda ka formula sirf Na₂CO₃ likh aana.**
   Sirf Na₂CO₃ to **Soda Ash** (anhydrous sodium carbonate) hai! **Washing Soda** mein 10 molecules water of crystallisation ke hote hain: **Na₂CO₃·10H₂O**.
2. **Galti 2: Gypsum (CaSO₄·2H₂O) aur Plaster of Paris (CaSO₄·½H₂O) ke formula ulte kar dena.**
   Yaad rakho: **Gypsum** zameen se milne wali badi chattan hai jisme **poora 2H₂O** hota hai; use 373 K par garam karke paani udaya jaata hai tab **aadha paani (½H₂O)** wala **Plaster of Paris** banta hai!
3. **Galti 3: Gypsum ko garam karne ka taapmaan 373 °C likh dena.**
   Taapmaan **373 K** hai, jo Celsius mein **100 °C** (373 - 273 = 100 °C) hota hai! 373 °C par to Gypsum jal kar **dead-burnt plaster (CaSO₄)** ban jaayega!
4. **Galti 4: CaSO₄·½H₂O + ½H₂O → CaSO₄·H₂O likh dena.**
   Gypsum mein 2H₂O hote hain! Isliye ½H₂O ko 2H₂O banane ke liye **1½H₂O (3/2 H₂O)** joda jaata hai: CaSO₄·½H₂O + 1½H₂O → CaSO₄·2H₂O.
5. **Galti 5: "Water of crystallisation salt ko geela (wet) rakhta hai" likh dena.**
   Bilkul nahi! Water of crystallisation crystal ke andar ion-lattice ka hissa hota hai; jab tak salt ko garam na karo, crystals chhune mein bilkul **sookhe (dry)** hote hain.

## Quick Recap

- **Water of Crystallisation:** Salt ke ek formula unit mein maujood fixed water molecules (CuSO₄·5H₂O mein 5, FeSO₄·7H₂O mein 7, Na₂CO₃·10H₂O mein 10, CaSO₄·2H₂O mein 2).
- **Washing Soda (Na₂CO₃·10H₂O, 286 u):** Baking soda ko garam karke bane Na₂CO₃ ke recrystallisation (Na₂CO₃ + 10H₂O → Na₂CO₃·10H₂O) se milta hai; glass, soap, paper, borax aur permanent hardness hatane mein kaam aata hai.
- **Plaster of Paris (CaSO₄·½H₂O, 145 u):** Gypsum (CaSO₄·2H₂O, 172 u) ko 373 K (100 °C) par garam karne se banta hai; paani milane par wapas Gypsum bankar 15 minute mein sakht ho jaata hai (CaSO₄·½H₂O + 1½H₂O → CaSO₄·2H₂O).

   - **Gypsum:** CaSO₄·2H₂O — har CaSO₄ ke saath 2 H₂O; ye pehle se set (sakht) roop hai, isliye paani milane par dobara 'set' nahi hota.
   - **Plaster of Paris:** CaSO₄·½H₂O — har CaSO₄ ke saath ½ H₂O; paani milte hi 1½H₂O lekar gypsum banta hai aur **set** ho jaata hai.
   - **Dead-burnt plaster:** CaSO₄ — koi paani nahi; ye paani milane par **set nahi** hota.

2. Washing soda do charano (steps) mein Baking soda se banaya jaata hai:
   - **Step 1 (Thermal decomposition of Baking soda):**
     2NaHCO₃(s) --(Heat)--> Na₂CO₃(s) [Soda ash] + H₂O(g)↑ + CO₂(g)↑
   - **Step 2 (Recrystallisation of Soda ash):**
     Na₂CO₃(s) + 10H₂O(l) → Na₂CO₃·10H₂O(s) [Washing soda]
   - **Four Uses:** (i) Glass, soap aur paper industries mein, (ii) **Borax** jaise sodium compounds banane mein, (iii) Gharelu safaai (cleaning agent) mein, (iv) Paani ki **permanent hardness** door karne mein.

3. - **Chemical Name:** Calcium sulphate hemihydrate (CaSO₄·½H₂O).
   - **Preparation:** Gypsum (CaSO₄·2H₂O) ko 373 K (100 °C) par garam karke:
     CaSO₄·2H₂O(s) [Gypsum] --(373 K / 100 °C)--> CaSO₄·½H₂O(s) [Plaster of Paris] + 1½H₂O(g)↑
   - **373 K Temperature Control Kyun Zaroori Hai:** Agar Gypsum ko 373 K (100 °C) se zyada garam kar diya jaaye, to uska saara water of crystallisation ud jaata hai aur anhydrous CaSO₄ (**Dead-burnt plaster**) ban jaata hai, jo paani milane par dobara set (sakht) nahi hota.

4. Aadha paani ka molecule akela nahi hota; CaSO₄·½H₂O likhne ka arth hai ki crystal mein **CaSO₄ ke do (2) formula units paani ke ek (1) molecule (H₂O) ko aapas mein share (sajha) karte hain** ((CaSO₄)₂·H₂O). Isliye prati ek (per 1) CaSO₄ formula unit paani ka hissa 1/2 (½H₂O) aata hai.

5. Plaster of Paris (CaSO₄·½H₂O) nami (moisture) ke sampark mein aate hi paani sokh kar sakht **Gypsum (CaSO₄·2H₂O)** mein badal jaata hai:
   CaSO₄·½H₂O(s) + 1½H₂O(l) → CaSO₄·2H₂O(s) [Gypsum — Hard solid mass]
   Agar ise khule mein rakha jaaye, to ye pehle hi jam kar bekar ho jaayega aur baad mein saanche ya plaster chadhane ke kaam nahi aayega.

6. Washing soda (Na₂CO₃) hard water mein ghule Ca²⁺ aur Mg²⁺ ions ke saath double displacement karke unhein **insoluble carbonates (CaCO₃↓ ya MgCO₃↓)** ke roop mein neeche bitha (precipitate kar) deta hai:
   CaSO₄(aq) + Na₂CO₃(aq) → CaCO₃(s)↓ + Na₂SO₄(aq)
   MgCl₂(aq) + Na₂CO₃(aq) → MgCO₃(s)↓ + 2NaCl(aq)
   Jab Ca²⁺ aur Mg²⁺ ions hat jaate hain, to paani soft ho jaata hai aur sabun ke saath aasani se jhaag deta hai.

7. (a) **CaSO₄·½H₂O:** Formula unit mass = 136 + 9 = 145 u; paani ka hissa = 9 u.
   **% of Water** = (9 ÷ 145) × 100 = **6.21%** (lagbhag). Gypsum mein ye 20.93% tha (Class 9), yaani PoP mein paani bahut kam bacha hai.
   (b) Har 172 g gypsum se 1½H₂O = 27 g paani udta hai, isliye **mass mein kami** = (27 ÷ 172) × 100 = **15.70%** (lagbhag) ✅.
   (Jaanch: 172 g mein se 27 g gaya to 145 g bacha, jo PoP ka formula unit mass hai ✅.)

8. Balanced Equation: CaSO₄·½H₂O(s) + 1½H₂O(l) → CaSO₄·2H₂O(s)
   - Formula Unit Masses: CaSO₄·½H₂O = 145 u, 1.5 H₂O = 27 u, CaSO₄·2H₂O = 172 u.
   - Moles of Plaster of Paris = 58.00 ÷ 145 = 0.40 mol.
   - (a) Mass of water absorbed = 0.40 × 27 = **10.80 g**.
   - (b) Total mass of hardened Gypsum cast = 0.40 × 172 = **68.80 g** ✅ (Jaanch: 58.00 g + 10.80 g = 68.80 g ✅).

9. Balanced Equation: Na₂CO₃(s) + 10H₂O(l) → Na₂CO₃·10H₂O(s)
   - Formula Unit Masses: Na₂CO₃ = 106 u, 10H₂O = 180 u, Na₂CO₃·10H₂O = 286 u.
   - Moles of Washing soda to be produced = 71.50 ÷ 286 = 0.25 mol.
   - Mass of anhydrous Na₂CO₃ required = 0.25 × 106 = **26.50 g**.
   - Mass of water incorporated = 0.25 × 180 = **45.00 g** ✅ (Jaanch: 26.50 g + 45.00 g = 71.50 g ✅).

10. Overheating par gypsum ka saara paani ud jaata hai: CaSO₄·2H₂O(s) --(373 K se kaafi zyada)--> CaSO₄(s) + 2H₂O(g)↑
    - Formula Unit Masses: CaSO₄·2H₂O = 172 u, CaSO₄ = 136 u, 2H₂O = 36 u.
    - Moles of gypsum = 8.60 ÷ 172 = 0.05 mol.
    - Mass of dead-burnt plaster (CaSO₄) = 0.05 × 136 = **6.80 g**.
    - Mass of water lost = 0.05 × 36 = **1.80 g** ✅ (Jaanch: 6.80 g + 1.80 g = 8.60 g ✅). Ye CaSO₄ paani milane par dobara set nahi hota.
