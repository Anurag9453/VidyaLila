# Topic 1: Acids, Bases aur Indicators: Pehchaan Ka Tareeka

Class 9 mein humne dekha tha ki jab ek **acid** (jaise HCl) aur ek **base** (jaise NaOH) milte hain, to dono apne saathi badal kar **salt** (NaCl) aur **paani** (H₂O) bana lete hain ([chemical-changes-and-reactions-part3.md](../../../class9/chemistry/chemical-changes-and-reactions/chemical-changes-and-reactions-part3.md)). Par wahan ek bada sawaal baaki reh gaya tha: aakhir **acid aur base hote kya hain**, aur bina chakhe ya chhue hum kisi ghol ko dekh kar kaise pehchanein ki wo acid hai ya base? Aaj ke pehle topic mein hum rasoi se lekar school lab tak isi pehchaan ka raaz kholenge.

## Safed rumaal par haldi ka jaadu

Sunday dopahar ka khana chal raha tha. Shaunak apni chachi Ms. Chitnis ke paas baith kar garam-garam dal-chawal kha raha tha. Baaton-baaton mein chammach hila aur peeli dal ki ek boond Shaunak ke safed suti rumaal (cotton handkerchief) par gir gayi. Rumaal par **chamakdar peela daag** lag gaya.

Shaunak turant washbasin ki taraf bhaaga aur daag par **nahane ka sabun** ragad diya. Jaise hi sabun ka jhaag haldi ke daag se chhua, Shaunak ki aankhein phati reh gayin: peela daag achanak **gehra laal-bhoora (reddish-brown)** ho gaya!

"Chachi, dekhiye! Sabun lagate hi peeli haldi laal kaise ho gayi? Rumaal to aur kharab ho gaya!" Shaunak ne ghabra kar kaha.

Ms. Chitnis, jo ek dawa-company ki quality lab mein kaam karti thin, hanste hue washbasin ke paas aayin. Unhone pahle rumaal ko khub saare paani se dhulwaya, aur phir rasoi se **nimbu ka ek chhota tukda** laakar us laal-bhoore daag par do boond ras nichod diya. Palak jhapakte hi laal-bhoora rang gayab ho gaya aur daag wapas **peela** ho gaya!

Shaunak hairaan tha: "Sabun se laal-bhoora, aur nimbu se wapas peela? Chachi, haldi ke andar koi switch laga hai kya?"

Ms. Chitnis muskurayin, "Switch hi samajh lo, beta! Chemistry mein aisi cheezon ko **indicator** kehte hain. Sabun mein **base** hai aur nimbu mein **acid**, aur haldi apna rang badal kar chugli kar deti hai ki uske saamne kaun aaya hai." Chalo, Shaunak ke rumaal se shuru karke is poore khel ko samajhte hain.

## Part A: Rasoi ke acids aur bases banaam lab ke chemicals

Shuruat mein logon ne cheezon ko unke **swaad** aur **chhune ke ehsaas** se pehchana tha:

1. **Acids:** Shabd *acid* Latin shabd *acidus* se aaya hai, jiska matlab hai **khatta (sour)**. Nimbu, santra, imli, kachcha aam, dahi aur sirka (vinegar), in sab mein prakritik (organic) acids hote hain.
2. **Bases:** Khaane wala meetha soda (baking soda) agar ungliyon ke beech paani ke saath maslo to **chikna (soapy/slippery)** lagta hai, aur swaad mein **kadwa (bitter)** hota hai.

> **Sabse zaroori suraksha niyam (Safety Rule):** Rasoi mein nimbu ya dahi chakhna aam baat hai, par **science lab mein ya kisi anjaan botal ki kisi bhi cheez ko KABHI chakhna (taste) ya nange haath se chhuna mana hai!** Lab ke mineral acids (jaise HCl, H₂SO₄, HNO₃) aur bases (jaise NaOH, KOH) chamdi ko jala dete hain (**corrosive** hote hain). Isliye pehchaan ke liye hum jeebh ya ungli nahi, balki **indicators** ka upyog karte hain.

### Kudrat ke khazaane mein kaun-sa acid kahan milta hai?

Ye table yaad rakhne mein bahut kaam aati hai, kyunki ye rozmarra ki cheezon ko unke andar ke organic acid se jodti hai:

| Prakritik srot (Source) | Usmein paya jaane wala Acid | Khaas baat |
|---|---|---|
| Sirka (Vinegar) | Acetic acid (CH₃COOH) | Achaar aur chaat mein khattapan deta hai |
| Nimbu aur Santra (Citrus fruits) | Citric acid | Vitamin C ke saath-saath khatta swaad |
| Imli (Tamarind) aur Angoor | Tartaric acid | Baking powder banane mein bhi kaam aata hai |
| Tamatar (Tomato) aur Palak | Oxalic acid | Kapdon par lage zang ke daag hatane mein bhi upyogi |
| Khatta doodh ya Dahi (Curd) | Lactic acid | Doodh ke lactose se bacteria banate hain |
| Seb (Apple) | Malic acid | Kache seb mein zyada hota hai |
| Laal cheenti (Ant sting) aur Bichchhu-booti (Nettle leaf) | Methanoic acid (Formic acid) | Dank lagne par jalan aur dard isi acid se hota hai |

Aur kuch jaane-pehchane **bases** aur unke formula (Class 9 Ch 3 ke criss-cross niyam se, [language-of-chemistry-part5.md](../../../class9/chemistry/language-of-chemistry/language-of-chemistry-part5.md)):

- **Sodium hydroxide (NaOH)** aur **Potassium hydroxide (KOH):** sabun aur paper banane mein (bahut tez/corrosive).
- **Calcium hydroxide, Ca(OH)₂ (Slaked lime / bujha chuna):** paan mein lagne wala chuna aur deewaron ki safedi (whitewash).
- **Magnesium hydroxide, Mg(OH)₂ (Milk of magnesia):** pet ki acidity door karne wali safed dawa (antacid).
- **Ammonium hydroxide, NH₄OH:** khidki ke kaanch saaf karne wale kuch sprays mein (tez gandh hoti hai, aankh se door rakhein).

## Part B: Rang badalne wale jasoos: Prakritik aur Synthetic Indicators

Jab hum chakh nahi sakte, to hum ghol mein ek boond **indicator** daalte hain, ya indicator laga kaagaz dubote hain. Indicator do tarah ke hote hain: **prakritik (natural)** jo paudhon se milte hain, aur **manav-nirmit (synthetic)** jo lab mein bante hain.

### 1. Litmus: sabse mashhoor prakritik indicator

Litmus **lichen** (fungus aur shaival ki saajhedaari se bane chhote jeev, jo chattano aur pedon ki chhaal par ugte hain) se nikala gaya ek **baingani (purple)** rang ka ark (dye) hai. Jab na koi acid ho na base (jaise shuddh paani), to litmus ka asali rang **purple** hi rehta hai. Lab mein suvidha ke liye ise do rang ke kaagaz ki pattiyon (strips) mein banaya jaata hai: **Blue litmus paper** aur **Red litmus paper**.

- **Acid** neele (blue) litmus ko **laal (red)** kar deta hai. *(Yaad rakhne ki chhoti trick: **A**cid → **B**lue to **R**ed, yaani **ABR**; khatre ka rang laal!)*
- **Base** laal (red) litmus ko **neela (blue)** kar deta hai. *(Trick: **B**ase → **B**lue!)*
- Agar **neutral** ghol (jaise shuddh paani ya namak ka paani) ho, to na neela litmus badalta hai, na laal litmus.

### 2. Haldi (Turmeric), Gudhal (China Rose) aur Lal Patta-gobhi (Red Cabbage)

- **Haldi (Turmeric):** Asli rang **peela (yellow)**. Acid aur neutral paani mein **peela hi rehta hai**, par **base** milte hi **laal-bhoora (reddish-brown)** ho jaata hai! Yahi Shaunak ke rumaal par hua tha: sabun (base) ne haldi ko laal-bhoora kiya, aur nimbu (acid) ne sabun ke base ko khatam karke wapas peela kar diya.
- **Gudhal ke phool (China rose) ka ghol:** Garam paani mein gudhal ki pankhudiyan bhigone par halka gulabi ark milta hai. Ye **acid** mein **gehra gulabi (dark pink / magenta)** aur **base** mein **hara (green)** ho jaata hai!
- **Lal patta-gobhi (Red cabbage) ka ark:** Asli rang baingani-laal. **Acid** mein **laal (red)** aur **base** mein **hara/peela (greenish-yellow)** ho jaata hai.

> **Activity 1.1 (ghar par adult ke saath): Haldi ka greeting card.**
> Ek chammach haldi powder mein thoda paani milakar gaadha peela paste banao. Ise ek safed chart paper par phaila kar sukhne do — tumhara **haldi indicator paper** taiyaar hai! Ab ek katori mein thoda sa **kapde dhone ke sabun ka paani** lo (ise peena ya aankh ke paas le jaana mana hai). Rui ki teeli (cotton bud) sabun ke paani mein dubokar peele card par phool banao: jahan-jahan sabun lagega, wahan **laal-bhoora** phool ubhar aayega! Ab doosri saaf cotton bud ko **nimbu ke ras** mein dubokar us laal phool ke beech mein chhuo: wahan rang wapas **peela** ho jaayega. Kaam ke baad haath dho lo.

### 3. Lab ke do pakke Synthetic Indicators: Phenolphthalein aur Methyl Orange

School lab mein sabse zyada ye do manav-nirmit indicators dikhte hain:

- **Phenolphthalein:** Iska khud ka ghol paani jaisa **rangheen (colourless)** hota hai. **Acid** ya **neutral** ghol mein ye **rangheen (colourless)** hi rehta hai, par jaise hi **base** milta hai, ye khubsurat **gulabi (pink)** ho jaata hai!
- **Methyl orange:** Iska asali ghol **narangi (orange)** hota hai. **Acid** mein ye **laal (red/pinkish-red)** ho jaata hai, aur **base** mein **peela (yellow)** ho jaata hai!

Sabhi 5 mukhya indicators ka poora chart ek nazar mein:

```
   INDICATOR KA RANG-CHAKRA (ACID vs NEUTRAL vs BASE)

   Indicator          Acid mein          Neutral mein       Base mein
   --------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Litmus             Laal (Red)         Baingani (Purple)  Neela (Blue)
   Haldi (Turmeric)   Peela (Yellow)     Peela (Yellow)     Laal-bhoora
   Gudhal             Gehra Gulabi       Halka Gulabi       Hara (Green)
   Phenolphthalein    Rangheen           Rangheen           Gulabi (Pink)
   Methyl Orange      Laal (Red)         Narangi (Orange)   Peela (Yellow)
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Paanch pramukh indicators ke rang: Acid, Neutral aur Base mein. Dhyan do ki Haldi aur Phenolphthalein dono Acid aur Neutral mein ek hi jaisa rang dikhate hain (peela aur rangheen), isliye sirf unse Acid aur Neutral ke beech farq karne ke liye ek chhota step aur lagta hai (Part D mein dekhenge).

## Part C: Naak se pehchaan: Olfactory Indicators

Maan lo kisi vidyarthi ko dikhayi nahi deta (visually impaired student hai). Kya wo lab mein acid aur base ki pehchaan kar sakta hai? Bilkul! Kuch padarth aise hote hain jinki **khas gandh (smell)** acid mein to bani rehti hai, par **base milte hi gayab ho jaati hai**. Inhe **olfactory indicators** (*olfactory* = soonghne se juda) kehte hain.

Teen sabse achhe olfactory indicators:

1. **Barik kata pyaaz (Onion)-basa kapda** (acid aur base wala test **sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo)**): Ek saaf suti kapde ki pattiyon ko barik kate pyaaz ke saath plastic thaili mein raat bhar band karke fridge mein rakh do (ye hissa **ghar par adult ke saath** ho sakta hai). Subah pattiyon mein pyaaz ki tez gandh bas jaati hai.
   - Ek patti par **patla acid** (jaise nimbu ka ras ya dilute HCl) daalo: pyaaz ki **gandh bani rehti hai**.
   - Doosri patti par **base** (jaise sabun ka ghol, ya lab mein teacher dwara dilute NaOH) daalo aur paani se halka dho lo: pyaaz ki **gandh bilkul gayab ho jaati hai**!
2. **Vanilla essence** (jo cake aur ice-cream mein dalta hai):
   - **Acid** ke saath hilane par vanilla ki meethi **khushboo bani rehti hai**.
   - **Base (NaOH)** ke saath hilane par vanilla ki **khushboo gayab ho jaati hai**.
3. **Laung ka tel (Clove oil):**
   - **Acid** mein laung ki **gandh bani rehti hai**.
   - **Base (NaOH)** mein laung ki **gandh khatam (ya bahut halki) ho jaati hai**.

**Niyam ek line mein:** Pyaaz, vanilla aur laung ke tel — teeno ki gandh **Acid mein BANI REHTI hai**, aur **Base mein GAYAB ho jaati hai**!

> **Activity 1.2 (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo).** Teacher do test tubes mein thoda-thoda dilute HCl aur dilute NaOH lete hain. Dono mein 2-2 boond **vanilla essence** daal kar hilaate hain. HCl wali tube ke paas haath se hawa jhalane (wafting) par vanilla ki khushboo saaf aati hai, jabki NaOH wali tube se vanilla ki khushboo gayab ho chuki hoti hai. *(Safety: Lab mein kisi bhi tube ko naak se chipka kar nahi soonghte; haath se hawa apni naak ki taraf jhalate hain, jise **wafting** kehte hain.)*

## Part D: Solved Examples: Jasoosi Paheliyan (Detective Puzzles)

### Example 1. Teen bina-label ki test tubes aur sirf Red Litmus!

**Sawaal:** Tumhare paas teen test tubes **A, B aur C** hain. Ek mein **dilute HCl (acid)** hai, ek mein **NaOH (base)** hai, aur ek mein **shuddh paani (neutral)** hai. Tumhein **sirf ek Red Litmus paper ki patti** di gayi hai (aur koi indicator nahi!). Tum teeno tubes ki pehchaan kaise karoge?

**Hal (Step-by-step):**
1. **Qadam 1 (Base dhoondho):** Red litmus patti ke teen chhote tukde kar lo (ya patti ka ek kona-kona bari-bari dubo). Teeno tubes A, B, C ki ek-ek boond alag-alag tukde par daalo. Jis tube ki boond se **Red litmus Neela (Blue)** ho jaaye, wo pakka **Base (NaOH)** hai! Maan lo **Tube B** ne use neela kar diya. To **B = Base (NaOH)**.
2. **Qadam 2 (Ab tumhare paas Blue litmus ban gaya!):** Jis tukde ka rang abhi-abhi **neela (blue)** hua hai, wahi ab tumhara **Blue litmus paper** ban gaya!
3. **Qadam 3 (Acid aur Paani alag karo):** Is naye bane neele tukde ke do hisse karo aur bachi hui dono tubes **A aur C** ki boond daalo. Jo tube is neele tukde ko wapas **Laal (Red)** kar de, wo **Acid (HCl)** hai! Aur jis tube se neela tukda neela hi rahe, wo **Shuddh Paani (Neutral)** hai!

**Jaanch:** Kya humein bahar se doosra indicator laga? Nahi! Pehle Red → Blue (Base mila), phir usi Blue → Red (Acid mila), aur jisme koi badlav nahi wo Neutral ✅.

---

### Example 2. Sirf Haldi (Turmeric) se teeno tubes (Acid, Base, Paani) pehchanna

**Sawaal:** Phir se wahi teen tubes **X, Y aur Z** (ek mein Acid, ek mein Base, ek mein shuddh Paani) hain, aur is baar tumhare paas **sirf Haldi paper (peela)** hai. Kaise pehchanoge?

**Hal (Step-by-step):**
1. **Qadam 1:** Teeno tubes X, Y, Z ki ek-ek boond haldi paper ke teen alag hisson par daalo. Acid aur paani dono mein haldi **peeli** hi rehti hai, par **Base** mein haldi **laal-bhoori (reddish-brown)** ho jaati hai. Maan lo **Tube Z** ne haldi ko laal-bhoora kar diya. To **Z = Base** pakka ho gaya!
2. **Qadam 2:** Ab us **laal-bhoore** ho chuke haldi ke daag ke do hisse karo. Ek par **Tube X** ki kuch boondein daalo, aur doosre par **Tube Y** ki.
   - **Acid** us laal-bhoore base ko neutralise karke haldi ko turant wapas **peela** kar dega!
   - **Paani** se laal-bhoora rang wapas peela nahi hoga (sirf halka phail sakta hai).

**Jaanch:** Haldi (peeli) + Base (Z) → Laal-bhoora; Laal-bhoora + Acid → Wapas Peela (bilkul Shaunak ke rumaal aur nimbu ki tarah!) ✅.

---

### Example 3. Phenolphthalein aur Methyl Orange ka milaan

**Sawaal:** Ek anjaan ghol **P** mein phenolphthalein daalne par wo **rangheen** rehta hai, aur methyl orange daalne par **laal** ho jaata hai. Doosre ghol **Q** mein phenolphthalein **gulabi (pink)** ho jaata hai, aur laung ke tel (clove oil) ki gandh **gayab** ho jaati hai. P aur Q ki prakriti (nature) batao.

**Hal:**
- **Ghol P:** Phenolphthalein rangheen hai (matlab P base nahi hai; wo acid ya neutral ho sakta hai). Methyl orange **laal** ho gaya — methyl orange sirf **acid** mein laal hota hai (neutral mein narangi rehta hai). Isliye **Ghol P = Acidic**.
- **Ghol Q:** Phenolphthalein **gulabi** ho gaya aur clove oil ki gandh **gayab** ho gayi — ye dono baatein **base** ki pakki pehchaan hain. Isliye **Ghol Q = Basic**.

**Jaanch:** Figure 1.1 aur Part C ke niyam se dono pehchaan 100% mail khaati hain ✅.

### Example 4. Indicator drops ka hisaab

**Sawaal:** Ek school lab mein 40 vidyarthiyon ke group ko 5 alag-alag gholon (nimbu ras, sirka, sabun ka paani, chuna paani, aur namak ka paani) ki jaanch **4 indicators** (blue litmus, red litmus, phenolphthalein aur methyl orange) se karni hai. Agar 2 vidyarthi milkar 1 set banate hain aur har test mein ghol ki **2 mL** matra lagti hai, to har ghol ka kul kitna aayatan (mL mein) poore class ke liye chahiye?

**Hal:**
- Kul vidyarthi = 40; har group mein 2 vidyarthi → Kul groups = 40 ÷ 2 = **20 groups**.
- Har group ek ghol ko 4 indicators se alag-alag test tube mein jaanchta hai → 1 group ko ek ghol ke **4 samples** chahiye.
- Har sample mein 2 mL lagta hai → 1 group ke liye ek ghol = 4 × 2 mL = **8 mL**.
- Poore 20 groups ke liye har ghol ki kul matra = 20 × 8 mL = **160 mL**.

**Jaanch:** 160 mL ÷ 2 mL = 80 test tubes (har ghol ke liye); 80 ÷ 4 indicators = 20 groups; 20 × 2 = 40 vidyarthi ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: "Phenolphthalein rangheen hai, to ghol pakka Acid hi hoga" maan lena.**
   Phenolphthalein **Acid** mein bhi rangheen rehta hai aur **Neutral (shuddh paani)** mein bhi rangheen rehta hai! Isliye sirf phenolphthalein ke rangheen rehne se ye sabit hota hai ki ghol **Base nahi hai**, par Acid aur Neutral mein farq karne ke liye blue litmus ya methyl orange chahiye.
2. **Galti 2: Haldi ke rang-badlav ko ulta likhna.**
   Haldi **Base** mein laal-bhoori hoti hai, Acid mein nahi! Acid mein haldi apna asali **peela** rang hi rakhti hai.
3. **Galti 3: Olfactory indicators mein "Base mein gandh rehti hai" likh dena.**
   Bilkul ulta! Pyaaz, vanilla aur clove oil — teeno ki gandh **Acid mein bani rehti hai** aur **Base (jaise NaOH) mein gayab ho jaati hai**.
4. **Galti 4: Litmus ka asali (neutral) rang neela ya laal samajhna.**
   Lichen se nikle litmus ka asali neutral rang **baingani (purple)** hota hai. Blue aur Red litmus papers to lab ki suvidha ke liye pehle se rang kar banaye jaate hain.
5. **Galti 5: Lab mein kisi ghol ko chakh kar ya ungli dubokar dekhna.**
   Lab ke acids aur bases tez corrosive hote hain; unhe kabhi chakhna ya nange haath se chhuna nahi chahiye.

## Quick Recap

- Khaane wale safe **organic acids** (jaise nimbu mein citric acid, dahi mein lactic acid, imli mein tartaric acid, sirke mein acetic acid) swaad mein khatte hote hain; **bases** (jaise baking soda, sabun) kadwe aur chhune mein chikne (soapy) hote hain.
- Lab ke **mineral acids** (HCl, H₂SO₄, HNO₃) aur strong bases (NaOH, KOH) chamdi jala dete hain (**corrosive**), isliye unhe kabhi chakhna ya chhuna nahi chahiye.
- **Litmus** (lichen se): Acid → Blue ko **Red** karta hai; Base → Red ko **Blue** karta hai; Neutral mein koi badlav nahi (asali ark **purple**).
- **Haldi (Turmeric):** Acid aur Neutral mein **Yellow**; Base mein **Reddish-brown**.
- **Gudhal (China rose):** Acid mein **Dark Pink (Magenta)**; Base mein **Green**.
- **Phenolphthalein:** Acid aur Neutral mein **Colourless**; Base mein **Pink**.
- **Methyl Orange:** Acid mein **Red**; Neutral mein **Orange**; Base mein **Yellow**.
- **Olfactory indicators** (Pyaaz, Vanilla essence, Clove oil): Acid mein gandh **bani rehti hai**; Base mein gandh **gayab ho jaati hai**.

   (d) All acids found in fruits and vegetables are mineral acids.

Each group tests 6 different household solutions using 3 liquid indicators (litmus solution, phenolphthalein, and methyl orange) in separate test tubes, using 3 mL of solution per test tube. How many millilitres of each household solution must the lab assistant prepare in total?

### Answers

1. (a) **Tartaric acid** (imli mein), (b) **Oxalic acid** (tamatar mein), (c) **Lactic acid** (dahi mein), (d) **Methanoic acid / Formic acid** (cheenti ke dank mein), (e) **Acetic acid** (sirke mein), (f) **Citric acid** (nimbu mein).

2. Lab ke acids (jaise HCl, H₂SO₄, HNO₃) aur bases (jaise NaOH, KOH) bahut **corrosive** (jala dene wale) ya zeherile hote hain. Unhe chakhne ya nange haath se chhune par munh, jeebh ya chamdi buri tarah jal sakti hai. Isliye lab mein hamesha indicators ka hi upyog kiya jaata hai.

3. (a) Nimbu ke ras mein citric acid hota hai, isliye **neela litmus laal (red)** ho jaayega.
   (b) Sabun ka ghol basic hota hai, isliye peela haldi paper **laal-bhoora (reddish-brown)** ho jaayega.
   (c) Limewater Ca(OH)₂ ek base hai, isliye phenolphthalein **gulabi (pink)** ho jaayega.
   (d) Dilute HNO₃ ek acid hai, isliye methyl orange **laal (red)** ho jaayega.

4. **Step 1 (Acid pehchano):** Blue litmus patti ke teen chhote tukde karke teeno tubes ki ek-ek boond daalo. Sirf **dilute HCl (acid)** neele litmus ko **laal (red)** karega. Isse Acid wali tube pehchan mein aa gayi!
   **Step 2 (Ab laal tukda taiyaar hai):** Jo tukda abhi laal hua hai, wahi ab **Red litmus paper** ka kaam karega.
   **Step 3 (Base aur Paani alag karo):** Is laal tukde ke do hisse karke bachi hui dono tubes ki boond daalo. Jo tube is laal tukde ko wapas **neela (blue)** kar de, wo **KOH (base)** hai! Aur jis tube se laal tukda laal hi rahe, wo **distilled water (neutral)** hai ✅.

5. (a) Curry mein **haldi (turmeric)** hoti hai aur sabun **basic** hota hai. Base ke sampark mein aate hi haldi indicator apna rang peele se **laal-bhoora (reddish-brown)** kar leta hai.
   (b) Khoob saare paani se dhone par sabun (base) beh jaata hai, isliye laal-bhoora rang hat kar daag wapas **peela** ho jaata hai.
   (c) Sirke (vinegar) mein **acetic acid** hota hai, jo bache-khuche sabun ke base ko turant neutralise kar deta hai aur daag ko wapas **peela** kar deta hai.

6. **Olfactory indicators** wo padarth hain jinki **gandh (smell)** acidic aur basic madhyam mein badal jaati hai, isliye visually impaired vidyarthi bhi inse acid aur base pehchan sakte hain. Do udaharan: **Vanilla essence** aur **Clove oil (laung ka tel)** (ya pyaaz).
   - (i) **Dilute HCl (acid)** ke saath: dono ki khas gandh **bani rehti hai** (khatam nahi hoti).
   - (ii) **Dilute NaOH (base)** ke saath: dono ki khas gandh **gayab ho jaati hai**.

7. **Nahi**, hum pakka nahi keh sakte ki **M** acid hi hai, kyunki phenolphthalein **acid** aur **neutral** dono tarah ke gholon mein **rangheen (colourless)** hi rehta hai. Pakka karne ke liye **M** ki ek boond **blue litmus paper** par daalo (ya methyl orange daalo): agar blue litmus **laal** ho jaaye (ya methyl orange laal ho jaaye), to **M** pakka acid hai; warna agar koi badlav na ho, to **M** neutral hai ✅.

8. **Tube 1 (Window cleaner = Ammonium hydroxide, base):** China rose indicator **hara (green)** ho jaayega.
   **Tube 2 (Imli ka paani = Tartaric acid, acid):** China rose indicator **gehra gulabi / magenta (dark pink)** ho jaayega.

9. (a) **True** ✅. Lichen se nikala gaya neutral litmus ark baingani (purple) hota hai.
   (b) **False** ❌. Methyl orange **acid** mein laal (red) aur **base** mein **peela (yellow)** hota hai.
   (c) **True** ✅. Base (NaOH) ke saath laung ke tel ki gandh gayab ho jaati hai.
   (d) **False** ❌. Phalon aur sabziyon mein paaye jaane wale acids **organic acids** (prakritik aur halke acids) hote hain, mineral acids nahi.

10. Kul vidyarthi = 36; har group mein 3 vidyarthi → Kul groups = 36 ÷ 3 = **12 groups**.
    Har group ek ghol ko 3 alag test tubes mein (3 indicators ke liye) jaanchta hai → 1 group ko har ghol ke **3 test tubes** chahiye.
    Har test tube mein 3 mL ghol lagta hai → 1 group ke liye ek ghol = 3 × 3 mL = **9 mL**.
    Poore 12 groups ke liye har ghol ki kul matra = 12 × 9 mL = **108 mL** ✅ (Jaanch: 108 ÷ 3 mL = 36 tubes; 36 ÷ 3 indicators = 12 groups; 12 × 3 = 36 vidyarthi ✅).
