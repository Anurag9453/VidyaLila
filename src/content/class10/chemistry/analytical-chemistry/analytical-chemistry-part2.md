# Topic 2: Sodium Hydroxide Ka Jaadu: Kaun Sa Rang, Kaun Sa Ghulna

Topic 1 mein humne seekha ki analysis ka matlab hai "kya hai andar?" ka pata lagana: ek reagent daalo, dekho kya badla (rang, precipitate, gas), aur fir socho ki isse kya pata chala. Wahin humne dekha tha ki metal ion aur OH⁻ milkar hydroxide ka precipitate banate hain, aur baaki ion (spectator) bas tamasha dekhte hain. Ab sawaal ye hai: **kya har metal ka precipitate ek jaisa dikhta hai?** Agar nahi, to ek hi reagent, sodium hydroxide, saat alag ions ko alag-alag pehchaan sakta hai. Aaj hum wahi "jaadu" kholenge.

## School function ka jaadu

Mr. Medhekar apni bhanji Amruta ko school ke annual function mein le gaye the. Stage par "Science Magic" ka item tha. Science wali madam ne mez par teen saaf glass rakhe. Madam ne aur boondein daaleen: **dhodha ghul gaya** aur glass phir saaf ho gaya! Teesre glass mein boondein daalin to bhura-laal dhodha bana, aur kitni bhi boondein daalo, wo wahi raha.

Amruta ne Mama ka haath kheencha. "Mama, wahi dropper, wahi liquid, par teen glass ka teen alag natija! Aur doosre glass mein dhodha gayab kaise ho gaya?" Mr. Medhekar muskuraye. Har metal ion ka apna swabhav hota hai: kisi ka rang alag, kisi ka dhodha 'zyada' daalne par ghul jaata hai. Ye hi swabhav ek **jasoos ka fingerprint** hai."

"To Mama, agar hum natija yaad kar lein, to bina label dekhe bata sakte hain ki glass mein kaun sa ion tha?" "Bilkul. Chalo, ek-ek karke dekhte hain."

## Part A: NaOH dhodha kaise banata hai

Class 10 ke Chapter 1 mein salts ka part tha: NaOH, KOH aur NH₄OH **alkali** hain, aur Cu(OH)₂, Fe(OH)₃, Zn(OH)₂, Al(OH)₃ jaise hydroxide **paani mein lagbhag nahi ghulte**. NaOH ek **strong alkali** hai (Chapter 4 ka strong electrolyte): paani mein lagbhag poora Na⁺ aur OH⁻ mein tootta hai, isliye ghol mein OH⁻ bahut hote hain. Jaise hi koi metal ion Mⁿ⁺ in OH⁻ se milta hai, agar uska hydroxide nahi ghulta to wo dhodha ban kar baith jaata hai:

Mⁿ⁺ + nOH⁻ → M(OH)ₙ↓

### Lab ka tareeka (teacher ke saath)

1. Saaf test tube mein namak ka ghol thoda sa (lagbhag 2 mL).
2. Dropper se NaOH ki **kuch boondein**, har boond ke baad hilao: rang dekho.
3. Ab **excess**: aur NaOH daalo, dhodha bacha ya ghul gaya?
4. Likho: kya daala, kya dikha, isse kya pata chala (Topic 1 ke teen sawaal).

### Example 1. CuSO₄ + NaOH (Topic 1 se yaad, ab excess ke saath)

**Dekha:** halka neela dhodha.
**Ionic equation:** Cu²⁺ + 2OH⁻ → Cu(OH)₂↓
**Molecular equation:** CuSO₄ + 2NaOH → Cu(OH)₂↓ + Na₂SO₄
Spectator ions: Na⁺ aur SO₄²⁻. Excess NaOH mein dhodha **nahi** ghulta: neela hi rehta hai.

**Jaanch:** Atom ginti: Cu 1 = 1, S 1 = 1, O 4 + 2 = 6 = 2 + 4 = 6, H 2 = 2, Na 2 = 2 ✅. Ionic mein charge: (+2) + 2 × (−1) = 0 = Cu(OH)₂ ka charge 0 ✅.

### Example 2. FeCl₃ + NaOH (Topic 1 se yaad, ab excess ke saath)

**Dekha:** bhura-laal (reddish-brown) dhodha.
**Ionic:** Fe³⁺ + 3OH⁻ → Fe(OH)₃↓
**Molecular:** FeCl₃ + 3NaOH → Fe(OH)₃↓ + 3NaCl
Excess NaOH mein dhodha **nahi** ghulta.

**Jaanch:** Fe 1 = 1, Cl 3 = 3, Na 3 = 3, O 3 = 3, H 3 = 3 ✅. Charge: (+3) + 3 × (−1) = 0 ✅.

### Example 3. FeSO₄ + NaOH (do rang ek hi metal ke)

Lohe ke do ion hain: Fe²⁺ aur Fe³⁺. Dono NaOH se dhodha dete hain, par rang alag.

**Dekha:** Fe²⁺ ka **gande hare rang (dirty green)** ka dhodha.
**Ionic:** Fe²⁺ + 2OH⁻ → Fe(OH)₂↓
**Molecular:** FeSO₄ + 2NaOH → Fe(OH)₂↓ + Na₂SO₄
Excess NaOH mein **nahi** ghulta. Ek baat: ye hara dhodha kuch der khula rehne par upar se **bhura** hone lagta hai, kyunki hawa ki oxygen use Fe(OH)₃ jaisi cheez mein badal deti hai (ek line, aage ki class mein vistaar).

**Jaanch:** Fe 1 = 1, S 1 = 1, O 4 + 2 = 6 = 2 + 4 = 6, H 2 = 2, Na 2 = 2 ✅. Charge: (+2) + 2 × (−1) = 0 ✅.

**Is Part ka sabak:** pehle teen ion Cu²⁺, Fe²⁺, Fe³⁺ ka dhodha excess NaOH mein **bacha rehta hai**. Rang se hi ye teeno alag pehchane jaate hain: neela, hara, bhura-laal.

## Part C: Jo dhodha ghul jaata hai: amphoteric hydroxide

Pehli baar dekho to ajeeb lagta hai: dhodha pehle banta hai, phir **aur** NaOH daalne par ghul jaata hai! Ise samjhne ke liye sochte hain ki hydroxide ne NaOH ke saath **doosra reaction** kiya.

Zn(OH)₂, Pb(OH)₂ aur Al(OH)₃ ko Chapter 2 mein dekhe amphoteric oxides ka bhai samjho: wahan Al₂O₃ aur ZnO acid ke saath bhi reaction karte the aur NaOH ke saath bhi. Yahan hydroxide bhi aisa hi karte hain. Isliye inhe **amphoteric hydroxide** kehte hain. Ek taraf wo **base** ki tarah acid se milte hain, aur doosri taraf wo **acid** ki tarah ek strong alkali se.

### Example 5. Zinc: pehle dhodha, phir ghol

**Kadam 1 (kuch boondein):** ZnSO₄ + 2NaOH → Zn(OH)₂↓ + Na₂SO₄. Ionic: Zn²⁺ + 2OH⁻ → Zn(OH)₂↓.
**Kadam 2 (excess):** Zn(OH)₂ + 2NaOH → Na₂ZnO₂ + 2H₂O. Sodium zincate bina rang ke ghol mein rehta hai.
**Acid ke saath (teacher demo, dilute acid):** Zn(OH)₂ + H₂SO₄ → ZnSO₄ + 2H₂O.

**Jaanch:** Kadam 1: Zn 1 = 1, S 1 = 1, O 4 + 2 = 6 = 2 + 4 = 6, H 2 = 2, Na 2 = 2 ✅. Kadam 2: Zn 1 = 1, Na 2 = 2, O 2 + 2 = 4 = 2 + 2 = 4, H 2 + 2 = 4 = 4 ✅. Acid wala: Zn 1, S 1, O 2 + 4 = 6 = 4 + 2 = 6, H 2 + 2 = 4 = 4 ✅.

### Example 6. Lead: pehle dhodha, phir ghol (zehreela!)

(Lead ke compounds zehreele: sirf padhne ya teacher demo.)
**Kuch boondein:** Pb(NO₃)₂ + 2NaOH → Pb(OH)₂↓ + 2NaNO₃. Ionic: Pb²⁺ + 2OH⁻ → Pb(OH)₂↓. Safed dhodha.
**Excess:** Pb(OH)₂ + 2NaOH → Na₂PbO₂ + 2H₂O. Sodium plumbite, bina rang ka ghol.
**Acid ke saath:** Pb(OH)₂ + 2HNO₃ → Pb(NO₃)₂ + 2H₂O. (Yahan nitric acid isliye ki sulphuric acid se insoluble PbSO₄ ki parat ban jaati hai: Chapter 1 ka sabak.)

**Jaanch:** Pb 1 = 1, N 2 = 2, O 6 + 2 = 8 = 2 + 6 = 8, H 2 = 2, Na 2 = 2 ✅. Excess: Pb 1, Na 2, O 2 + 2 = 4 = 2 + 2 = 4, H 4 = 4 ✅. Acid wala: Pb 1, N 2, O 2 + 6 = 8 = 6 + 2 = 8, H 2 + 2 = 4 = 4 ✅.

### Example 7. Aluminium: jelly jaisa dhodha

**Kuch boondein:** Al³⁺ + 3OH⁻ → Al(OH)₃↓. Molecular: Al₂(SO₄)₃ + 6NaOH → 2Al(OH)₃↓ + 3Na₂SO₄. Safed, jelly jaisa (gelatinous) dhodha.
**Excess:** Al(OH)₃ + NaOH → NaAlO₂ + 2H₂O. Sodium aluminate, bina rang ka ghol.
**Acid ke saath:** 2Al(OH)₃ + 3H₂SO₄ → Al₂(SO₄)₃ + 6H₂O.

**Jaanch:** Al 2 = 2, S 3 = 3, O 12 + 6 = 18 = 6 + 12 = 18, H 6 = 6, Na 6 = 6 ✅. Excess: Al 1, Na 1, O 3 + 1 = 4 = 2 + 2 = 4, H 3 + 1 = 4 = 4 ✅. Acid: Al 2 = 2, S 3 = 3, H 6 + 6 = 12 = 12, O 6 + 12 = 18 = 12 + 6 = 18 ✅.

(Ye zincate, plumbite aur aluminate ke andar ka ion **complex ion** hota hai, aage ki class mein.)

```
  Zn²⁺ ka ghol
      :
      : kuch boondein NaOH
      v
  Zn(OH)₂ dhodha (safed)     <-- dhodha badhta gaya
      :
      : aur NaOH (excess)
      v
  Na₂ZnO₂ ka ghol (bina rang ka)  <-- dhodha ghul gaya
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Zn²⁺ ke saath NaOH: pehle dhodha, phir excess mein ghol. Pb²⁺ aur Al³⁺ bhi yahi raasta lete hain, apne-apne namak (plumbite, aluminate) ke saath.

### Example 8. Ek numerical: kitna dhodha banega?

**Sawaal:** FeCl₃ ki sahi matra (8.125 g) NaOH se poori tarah reaction karti hai. Kitna NaOH lagega, kitna Fe(OH)₃ aur NaCl banega? (Fe 56, Cl 35.5, Na 23, O 16, H 1.)

**Hal:** FeCl₃ = 56 + 3 × 35.5 = **162.5 g/mol**, to 8.125 ÷ 162.5 = **0.05 mol**.
FeCl₃ + 3NaOH → Fe(OH)₃ + 3NaCl: 0.05 mol se 0.15 mol NaOH, 0.05 mol Fe(OH)₃ aur 0.15 mol NaCl.
NaOH: 0.15 × 40 = **6.0 g**. Fe(OH)₃ = 56 + 51 = 107, to 0.05 × 107 = **5.35 g**. NaCl: 0.15 × 58.5 = **8.775 g**.

**Jaanch:** pehle 8.125 + 6.0 = 14.125 g; baad mein 5.35 + 8.775 = 14.125 g ✅.

## Part D: Calcium ka alag raasta

Ca(NO₃)₂ + NaOH mein bhi **safed dhodha** banta hai, lekin **kam sa** (slight):

Ca²⁺ + 2OH⁻ → Ca(OH)₂↓
Ca(NO₃)₂ + 2NaOH → Ca(OH)₂↓ + 2NaNO₃

Kam isliye ki Ca(OH)₂ (chuna ka paani, Chapter 1) thoda-bahut paani mein ghulta hai. Aur sabse zaroori baat: **excess NaOH mein ye dhodha nahi ghulta.** Calcium hydroxide amphoteric nahi hai, wo sirf base hai.

**Jaanch:** Ca 1 = 1, N 2 = 2, O 6 + 2 = 8 = 2 + 6 = 8, H 2 = 2, Na 2 = 2 ✅. Charge: (+2) + 2 × (−1) = 0 ✅.

## Part E: Sirf NaOH se kaun sa ion?

Ab hum "ulta" sochte hain: tube mein kuch tha, aur NaOH ke do natije mile. Kya pata chala?

```
  NaOH ki kuch boondein
        :
   dhodha ka rang?
        :
   neela -------> Cu²⁺
   hara  -------> Fe²⁺
   bhura-laal --> Fe³⁺
   safed -------> excess NaOH daalo:
                     :
                     ghula? ---haan---> Zn²⁺, Pb²⁺ ya Al³⁺

```

> **Figure 2.4** – NaOH se pehchaan ka flowchart: rang se Cu²⁺, Fe²⁺, Fe³⁺ seedhe pata chalte hain. Safed dhodhe ke liye excess NaOH daalte hain: ghulne wale teen ion, na ghulne wala Ca²⁺.

### Example 9. Paanch tubes ki pehchaan

Teacher ne paanch tubes diye (A se E), sab mein sirf in saat mein se koi ek ion. Natije:

| Tube | Kuch boondein NaOH | Excess NaOH |
|---|---|---|
| A | gande hare rang ka dhodha | nahi ghula |
| B | safed dhodha (kam sa) | nahi ghula |
| C | safed dhodha | ghul gaya |
| D | bhura-laal dhodha | nahi ghula |
| E | halka neela dhodha | nahi ghula |

**Hal:** A = Fe²⁺ (hara). B = Ca²⁺ (safed, na ghulne wala). C = Zn²⁺, Pb²⁺ ya Al³⁺ (teen mein se koi: NaOH akela inhe alag nahi kar sakta; Topic 3 mein NH₄OH Zn²⁺ ko alag karega, aur Pb²⁺ aur Al³⁺ ke liye ek extra clue lagega). D = Fe³⁺. E = Cu²⁺.

**Jaanch:** har tube ka natija Part B ki table ke ek hi row se milta hai ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti: "Saare safed dhodhe excess NaOH mein ghul jaate hain."**
Sach lagta hai kyunki Zn(OH)₂, Pb(OH)₂ aur Al(OH)₃, teeno safed hain aur teeno ghulte hain. Par **Ca(OH)₂** bhi safed hai aur wo **nahi ghulta**. Demo teen kadam mein:

1. **Teen safed dhodhe lo:** tube P (Ca²⁺), tube Q (Zn²⁺), tube R (Pb²⁺). Sabme kuch boondein NaOH: teeno mein safed dhodha, dekhne mein ek jaise.
2. **Excess NaOH daalo:** Q aur R **saaf** ho gaye, P mein **dhodha jyon ka tyon**.
3. **Niyam:** safed dhodha dikhe to rang par mat ruko, excess daalo. Ghulne wala dhodha = Zn, Pb ya Al; na ghulne wala = Ca.

Ek chhota test: ek tube mein safed dhodha excess ke baad bacha hai. Kya wo Zn²⁺ ho sakta hai? Nahi, Zn(OH)₂ to ghul jaata. Isliye **Ca²⁺**.

## Quick Recap

- NaOH ek **strong alkali** hai, OH⁻ bahut deta hai: Mⁿ⁺ + nOH⁻ → M(OH)ₙ↓.
- Tareeka: kuch boondein, phir **excess**; sirf teacher ke saath, goggles aur gloves. NaOH corrosive, lead zehreela, copper namak harmful.
- Rang: Cu²⁺ halka neela, Fe²⁺ gande hare rang ka (khule mein bhura hone lagta hai), Fe³⁺ bhura-laal, Zn²⁺, Pb²⁺, Al³⁺ safed (Al jelly jaisa), Ca²⁺ safed (slight).
- **Excess NaOH:** Zn(OH)₂, Pb(OH)₂, Al(OH)₃ **ghul jaate hain** (zincate Na₂ZnO₂, plumbite Na₂PbO₂, aluminate NaAlO₂, bina rang ka ghol). Ca(OH)₂, Fe(OH)₂, Fe(OH)₃, Cu(OH)₂ **nahi ghulte**.
- **Amphoteric hydroxide:** acid ke saath base ki tarah, strong alkali ke saath acid ki tarah.
- Fe²⁺ aur Fe³⁺ ko rang se alag karo; Ca²⁺ aur Zn²⁺ ko excess NaOH se alag karo.
- NaOH akela Zn²⁺, Pb²⁺, Al³⁺ ko alag nahi kar sakta.
- Mistake: "saare safed dhodhe ghul jaate hain": Ca(OH)₂ nahi ghulta.

## Practice Questions

(Sab sawaal paper par hal karne ke liye hain.)

1. Write the balanced molecular equation for Al₂(SO₄)₃ with NaOH giving the white precipitate, and name the spectator ions.
2. A colourless solution gives a white precipitate with a few drops of NaOH, and the precipitate vanishes in excess. Which of the seven ions could it be? Why can NaOH alone not decide?
3. Four tubes P, Q, R, S each hold one ion. P gives a reddish-brown precipitate, Q a pale blue one, R a dirty green one that slowly turns brown at the top on standing, and S a white one that does not dissolve in excess NaOH. Name the ion in each tube.
4. 14.85 g of Zn(OH)₂ is dissolved completely in NaOH (exact amounts). Find the mass of NaOH needed, the mass of sodium zincate and of water formed, and check conservation of mass. (Zn 65, Na 23, O 16, H 1.)
5. Write the equation of Pb(OH)₂ with dilute nitric acid and with NaOH. What does this show about lead hydroxide?
6. Write the molecular equation and the ionic equation for Ca(NO₃)₂ with NaOH. Which ions are spectators?
7. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) Cu(OH)₂ dissolves in excess NaOH. (b) Fe(OH)₃ is reddish-brown. (c) Al(OH)₃ dissolves in excess NaOH to form sodium aluminate. (d) Every white precipitate dissolves in excess NaOH.
8. A student adds excess NaOH to a white precipitate; it does not dissolve. He says "the solution must have contained zinc". Is he right?
9. Why does Zn(OH)₂ dissolve in excess NaOH while Cu(OH)₂ does not? Answer in terms of acid and base behaviour.
10. A student wants to repeat the colour magic at home using a kitchen drain cleaner because it contains NaOH. Give three reasons why this is unsafe.
11. 3.9 g of Al(OH)₃ is dissolved in NaOH (exact amounts). Find the mass of NaOH needed, the mass of NaAlO₂ and of water formed, and check conservation of mass. (Al 27, Na 23, O 16, H 1.)
12. Describe in words the two test tubes for Al³⁺: after a few drops of NaOH and after excess. Mark what is at the bottom.

### Answers

1. Al₂(SO₄)₃ + 6NaOH → 2Al(OH)₃↓ + 3Na₂SO₄. Spectator ions: **Na⁺ aur SO₄²⁻**. Jaanch: Al 2 = 2, S 3 = 3, O 12 + 6 = 18 = 6 + 12, H 6 = 6, Na 6 = 6 ✅.
