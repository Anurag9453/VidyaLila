# Topic 5: Anjaan Namak Ki Khoj: Sab Tests Saath Mein

Topic 2 aur Topic 3 mein humne **cation** (positive ion) ke tests seekhe: NaOH aur NH₄OH ke saath kaun sa rang, kaun sa precipitate, aur excess mein kya. Topic 4 mein **anion** ke tests seekhe. Ab tak har test ek-ek karke tha, jaise ek-ek auzaar alag-alag. Aaj hum sab auzaar ek saath uthayenge aur ek **anjaan namak** ka poora naam nikalenge: kaun sa cation, kaun sa anion, aur formula kya.

## Part A: Paanch kadam ki yojana

Anjaan namak ki khoj mein hum hamesha ek hi kram se chalte hain. Kram badalne se galti hoti hai (aage Aam galtiyan mein dekhenge).

1. **Dekho.** Thos namak ka rang, phir ghol ka rang. Ye sirf ek **clue** hai, saboot nahi.
2. **Ghulo.** Thoda namak saaf distilled water mein ghol lo. Tests ke liye ions chahiye, aur ions ghol mein hi aazad hote hain.
3. **Cation pehchaano.** Ghol ke ek hisse mein NaOH ki kuchh boondein, phir excess. Doosre hisse mein NH₄OH ki boondein, phir excess. Shared table se milao (Topic 2 aur 3).
4. **Anion pehchaano.** Naye hisse mein Topic 4 ke tests: acid pehle, phir AgNO₃ ya BaCl₂.
5. **Formula likho.** Cation aur anion ke charge se criss-cross karke, phir equations.

```
   Kadam 1          Kadam 2        Kadam 3              Kadam 4          Kadam 5
   DEKHO     --->   GHULO    --->  CATION: NaOH,   --->  ANION: acid  ---> FORMULA
   (rang)           (paani        phir NH₄OH            pehle, phir       (charge
                    mein)         (drops, excess)       AgNO₃ / BaCl₂     balance)
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Anjaan namak ki khoj ka kram: rang dekho, paani mein ghulo, cation, phir anion, aur aakhir mein formula.

Safety ki baat: ye saare tests **school lab mein teacher ke saath** (goggles, gloves, patle ghol, boondein teacher ya dropper se) hote hain. Barium aur silver ke compounds zeherile hote hain, lead ke bhi, aur NaOH ka ghol patla hone par bhi aankh aur twacha ke liye tez hota hai. Tube mein jhaank kar ya naak se soongh kar kabhi nahi dekhte. Heavy-metal waste ko student nali mein nahi daalte, usse teacher sambhalte hain.

### Ghol ke rang ka clue

Kuchh ions ghol ko apna rang de dete hain. Aur baaki ghol ko **rangheen** chhodte hain:

| Ghol ka rang | Kaun sa cation ho sakta hai |
|---|---|
| Neela | Cu²⁺ |
| Halka hara | Fe²⁺ |
| Peela-bhura | Fe³⁺ |
| Rangheen | Ca²⁺, Zn²⁺, Pb²⁺, Al³⁺ (ya NH₄⁺ aur doosre) |

Dhyan do: rang sirf **cation** ke baare mein clue deta hai, anion ke baare mein nahi, aur rangheen ghol mein 4 cation ek jaise dikhte hain. Isliye rang akela kaafi nahi.

## Part B: Charge-balance se formula

Class 9 ka criss-cross yaad karo ([language-of-chemistry-part5.md](../../../class9/chemistry/language-of-chemistry/language-of-chemistry-part5.md)): cation ka charge number anion ke neeche aur anion ka charge number cation ke neeche. Ab ye hum tests ke baad likhenge.

### Example 1. Fe³⁺ aur SO₄²⁻

Charge numbers 3 aur 2. Criss-cross: Fe ke neeche 2, SO₄ ke neeche 3. Formula Fe₂(SO₄)₃ (bracket zaroori, kyunki SO₄ ek jhund hai, 3 baar).

**Jaanch:** positive 2 × (+3) = +6, negative 3 × (−2) = −6, kul 0 ✅. Atoms: Fe 2, S 3, O 12.

### Example 2. Fe²⁺ aur Cl⁻

Charge numbers 2 aur 1. Criss-cross: Fe ke neeche 1, Cl ke neeche 2. Formula FeCl₂.

**Jaanch:** (+2) + 2 × (−1) = 0 ✅.

### Example 3. Cu²⁺ aur NO₃⁻

Charge numbers 2 aur 1. Formula Cu(NO₃)₂ (NO₃ do baar, bracket).

**Jaanch:** (+2) + 2 × (−1) = 0 ✅. Formula mass: 63.5 + 2 × (14 + 48) = 63.5 + 124 = 187.5.

## Part C: Chhe anjaan namak, chhe jasoosi

Ab store room ki botalon ko ek-ek karke kholte hain. Har baar hum ek observation table dete hain, phir tarkeeb se jawab nikalte hain. Shared table ka koi rang naya nahi: wahi hai jo Topic 2 aur Topic 3 mein tha.

### Puzzle 1: botal 1 (peela-bhura ghol)

| Test | Kya dikha |
|---|---|
| Ghol ka rang | Peela-bhura |
| NaOH ki boondein | Laal-bhura precipitate |
| NaOH excess | Precipitate nahi ghula |
| Dilute HCl pehle, phir BaCl₂ | Safed precipitate, acid mein bhi nahi ghula |

**Tarkeeb.** Peela-bhura ghol Fe³⁺ ki taraf ishara karta hai. Laal-bhura precipitate Fe(OH)₃ ka rang hai aur excess mein nahi ghulta: shared table ki Fe³⁺ wali row se mel hai. Anion: safed precipitate jo acid mein bhi nahi ghula, matlab BaSO₄, to anion SO₄²⁻. (Barium zeherila hai, ye test sirf teacher demo mein.)

Ionic equations:
- Fe³⁺ + 3OH⁻ → Fe(OH)₃↓
- Ba²⁺ + SO₄²⁻ → BaSO₄↓

Formula: Fe³⁺ aur SO₄²⁻ se **Fe₂(SO₄)₃** (Example 1). Molecular equation: Fe₂(SO₄)₃ + 6NaOH → 2Fe(OH)₃ + 3Na₂SO₄.

**Jaanch:** Fe 2 = 2, S 3 = 3, O 12 + 6 = 18 = 6 + 12, Na 6 = 6, H 6 = 6 ✅.

### Puzzle 2: botal 2 (halka hara ghol)

| Test | Kya dikha |
|---|---|
| Ghol ka rang | Halka hara |
| NaOH ki boondein | Gande hare rang ka precipitate |
| NaOH excess | Nahi ghula |
| NH₄OH ki boondein aur excess | Gande hare rang ka precipitate, excess mein nahi ghula |
| Dilute HNO₃ pehle, phir AgNO₃ | Safed dahi jaisa precipitate, NH₄OH mein ghul gaya |

**Tarkeeb.** Halka hara ghol aur gande hare rang ka precipitate dono Fe²⁺ ki taraf. Fe(OH)₂ excess NaOH aur excess NH₄OH dono mein nahi ghulta, to Fe²⁺ pakka (Topic 2 mein bataya tha ki ye precipitate khade rehne par hawa se bhura hone lagta hai). Anion: safed dahi jaisa precipitate jo NH₄OH mein ghul jaye, matlab AgCl, to Cl⁻. (Silver nitrate zeherila hai aur twacha par kaale daag chhodta hai, sirf teacher dispense karta hai.)

- Fe²⁺ + 2OH⁻ → Fe(OH)₂↓
- Ag⁺ + Cl⁻ → AgCl↓

Formula **FeCl₂**. Molecular equation: FeCl₂ + 2NaOH → Fe(OH)₂ + 2NaCl.

**Jaanch:** Fe 1 = 1, Cl 2 = 2, Na 2 = 2, O 2 = 2, H 2 = 2 ✅.

### Puzzle 3: botal 3 (neela ghol), elimination ke saath

Is puzzle mein ek fact diya gaya hai: shelf par sirf chloride, sulphate aur nitrate namak rakhe hain. (Nitrate ka apna test aage ki class mein.)

| Test | Kya dikha |
|---|---|
| Ghol ka rang | Neela |
| NaOH ki boondein | Halka neela precipitate |
| NaOH excess | Nahi ghula |
| NH₄OH ki boondein | Halka neela precipitate |
| NH₄OH excess | Ghul kar gehra (syahi jaisa) neela ghol |
| Dilute HNO₃ pehle, phir AgNO₃ | Koi precipitate nahi |
| Dilute HCl pehle, phir BaCl₂ | Koi precipitate nahi |

```
   Test tube 1          Test tube 2           Test tube 3
   (sirf ghol)          (NH₄OH ki boondein)   (NH₄OH excess)

   :            :       :            :        :            :
   :   neela    :       :   neela    :        :   gehra    :
   :   ghol     :       :   ghol     :        :   neela    :
   :            :       :            :        :   ghol     :
   :            :       :  ########  :        :            :
   '------------'       '------------'        '------------'
                        halka neela ppt       tale mein kuch nahi
                        (tale mein)
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Puzzle 3 ki NH₄OH wali tubes: sirf neela ghol; boondein daalne par halka neela precipitate tube ke **tale** mein baith gaya; excess daalne par precipitate ghul gaya aur gehra neela ghol bana. Tube 3 mein tale mein koi precipitate nahi.

**Tarkeeb.** Neela ghol, halka neela precipitate jo excess NH₄OH mein gehre neele ghol mein ghule: Cu²⁺ (shared table). Anion: na AgNO₃ se precipitate (Cl⁻ nahi), na BaCl₂ se (SO₄²⁻ nahi). Shelf par sirf chloride, sulphate ya nitrate hain, to **bacha nitrate**: NO₃⁻.

Gehra neela ghol ek "complex ion" hai: [Cu(NH₃)₄]²⁺ (complex ion, aage ki class mein).

Formula **Cu(NO₃)₂** (Example 3). Equation: Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2NaOH → Cu(OH)₂ + 2NaNO₃.

**Jaanch:** Cu 1 = 1, N 2 = 2, O 6 + 2 = 8 = 2 + 6, Na 2 = 2, H 2 = 2 ✅.

### Puzzle 4: botal 4 (rangheen ghol), do saathi ek jaise

| Test | Kya dikha |
|---|---|
| Ghol ka rang | Rangheen |
| NaOH ki boondein | Safed gelatinous precipitate |
| NaOH excess | Ghul gaya, rangheen ghol |
| NH₄OH ki boondein aur excess | Safed gelatinous precipitate, excess mein nahi ghula |
| Kuchh boondein KI | Peela precipitate nahi banta |
| Dilute HCl pehle, phir BaCl₂ | Safed precipitate, acid mein bhi nahi ghula |

**Tarkeeb.** NaOH mein ghulne wale 3 cation hain: Zn²⁺, Pb²⁺, Al³⁺. NH₄OH excess mein Zn²⁺ ghul jata, to Zn²⁺ nahi. Bache Pb²⁺ aur Al³⁺. Dono ek jaise dikhte hain, to Topic 3 ka extra test: Pb²⁺ + 2I⁻ → PbI₂ (peela precipitate). Peela precipitate nahi bana, to Pb²⁺ nahi: **Al³⁺**. (Lead ke saare compounds zeherile hain: ye KI test sirf teacher demo mein ya sirf padhne ke liye.)

Anion: BaCl₂ (dilute HCl ke baad) se safed precipitate jo acid mein nahi ghula: SO₄²⁻. Is puzzle mein hum sirf ions batate hain: **Al³⁺ aur SO₄²⁻**.

- Al³⁺ + 3OH⁻ → Al(OH)₃↓
- Al(OH)₃ + NaOH → NaAlO₂ + 2H₂O

**Jaanch:** Al 1 = 1, O 3 + 1 = 4 = 2 + 2, H 3 + 1 = 4 = 4, Na 1 = 1 ✅.

### Puzzle 5: ek aur rangheen ghol, Zn ya Al?

| Test | Kya dikha |
|---|---|
| Ghol ka rang | Rangheen |
| NaOH ki boondein | Safed precipitate |
| NaOH excess | Ghul gaya |
| NH₄OH ki boondein | Safed precipitate |
| NH₄OH excess | Ghul gaya, rangheen ghol |
| BaCl₂ (dilute HCl ke baad) | Koi precipitate nahi |
| Dilute HNO₃ pehle, phir AgNO₃ | Safed dahi jaisa, NH₄OH mein ghula |

**Tarkeeb.** NaOH excess mein ghulne wale: Zn²⁺, Pb²⁺, Al³⁺. NH₄OH excess mein ghulne wala sirf **Zn²⁺** (Pb²⁺ aur Al³⁺ nahi ghulte). Anion: AgNO₃ se safed dahi jaisa, NH₄OH mein ghula: **Cl⁻**. Ions: **Zn²⁺ aur Cl⁻**.

- Zn²⁺ + 2OH⁻ → Zn(OH)₂↓
- Zn(OH)₂ + 2NaOH → Na₂ZnO₂ + 2H₂O

**Jaanch:** Zn 1 = 1, O 2 + 2 = 4 = 2 + 2, H 2 + 2 = 4 = 4, Na 2 = 2 ✅.

### Puzzle 6: kab aage nahi badh sakte

| Test | Kya dikha |
|---|---|
| Ghol ka rang | Rangheen |
| NaOH ki boondein | Halka safed precipitate |
| NaOH excess | Nahi ghula |
| NH₄OH ki boondein aur excess | Koi precipitate nahi, ya bahut halka |
| Dilute HNO₃ pehle, phir AgNO₃ | Safed dahi jaisa, NH₄OH mein ghula |

**Tarkeeb.** Halka safed precipitate jo excess NaOH mein nahi ghula, aur NH₄OH se nahi banta: **Ca²⁺** (Zn, Pb, Al excess NaOH mein ghulte). Anion **Cl⁻**. Ions: **Ca²⁺ aur Cl⁻**.

**Ek aur baat (honest note):** agar thos namak paani mein bilkul nahi ghulta, to ye yojana yahin rukti hai kyunki ions aazad hi nahi hote. Kuchh aise namak (jaise CaCO₃) par seedha dilute acid daal kar carbonate dekha ja sakta hai (Topic 4, teacher demo), par aise namakon ki poori jaanch aage ki class ka vishay hai. Isi tarah agar ghol NaOH aur NH₄OH dono se koi precipitate hi na de, to koi bhi un 7 ions mein se nahi: "koi ion fit nahi baithta", ye bhi ek sahi jawab hai.

### Puzzle 7: gas wala test (sirf padhne ke liye)

| Test | Kya dikha |
|---|---|
| Ghol ka rang | Rangheen |
| NaOH ki boondein (thanda) | Koi precipitate nahi |
| NaOH ke saath halka garam karna | Teekhi gandh wali gas, geele laal litmus ko neela karti hai |
| Dilute HCl pehle, phir BaCl₂ | Safed precipitate, acid mein bhi nahi ghula |

**Tarkeeb.** Thande NaOH se koi precipitate nahi, par garam karne par ammonia gas nikli: **NH₄⁺** (Topic 4). Anion: BaSO₄ ka precipitate, to **SO₄²⁻**. Ions: **NH₄⁺ aur SO₄²⁻**.

NH₄⁺ + OH⁻ → NH₃ + H₂O (garam karne par).

**Jaanch:** N 1 = 1, H 4 + 1 = 5 = 3 + 2, O 1 = 1; charge (+1) + (−1) = 0 = 0 ✅. (Ammonia ki gas teekhi hai: ye test sirf fume cupboard mein teacher karta hai, ya sirf padhne ke liye.)

## Part D: Jodi mein pehchaan: kaun kisse alag

Kuchh ions ke tests lagbhag ek jaise lagte hain. Unhe alag karne ka sabse chhota tareeka ek jodi ke liye ek hi test hota hai.

| Jodi | Alag karne wala test | Kya dikhega |
|---|---|---|
| Fe²⁺ aur Fe³⁺ | Ghol ka rang ya NaOH ka precipitate | Gande hare ya laal-bhura |
| Zn²⁺ aur Al³⁺ | Excess NH₄OH | Zn²⁺ ghulta hai, Al³⁺ nahi |
| Pb²⁺ aur Al³⁺ | KI ki boondein (padhne ke liye) | Pb²⁺ se peela precipitate, Al³⁺ se nahi |
| Ca²⁺ aur Zn²⁺ | Excess NaOH | Zn²⁺ ghulta hai, Ca²⁺ nahi |

```
   Ghol rangheen: NaOH ki boondein, phir excess
                     :
         +-----------+------------+
         :                        :
     precipitate ghula         nahi ghula
         :                        :
   Zn, Pb ya Al                  Ca²⁺
         :
   NH₄OH excess
         :
   +-----+------+
   :            :
 ghula       nahi ghula
   :            :
  Zn²⁺      Pb ya Al
                :
              KI test
```

> **Figure 5.4** – Rangheen ghol ke liye cation ka flowchart: pehle NaOH ki boondein aur excess, phir NH₄OH excess, aakhir mein KI. Har mod par ek hi sawaal.

## Part E: Ek chhoti matra wali ginti

Sirf ek hi simple numerical hai, kyunki ye chapter lab-description ka hai. Chapter 5 ke mole method se.

### Example 4. Fe(OH)₃ kitna banega?

**Sawaal:** Teacher ne 40 g Fe₂(SO₄)₃ ko paani mein ghola aur uska poora Fe³⁺ NaOH se precipitate kiya (NaOH exact reacting ratio mein). Kitne gram Fe(OH)₃ banega, aur kitna NaOH lagega?

**Equation:** Fe₂(SO₄)₃ + 6NaOH → 2Fe(OH)₃ + 3Na₂SO₄ (balanced, upar jaanch ki thi).

**Masses (Class 9 values):** Fe₂(SO₄)₃ = 2 × 56 + 3 × (32 + 64) = 112 + 288 = 400. Fe(OH)₃ = 56 + 3 × 17 = 107. NaOH = 40.

**Moles:** 40 ÷ 400 = 0.1 mol Fe₂(SO₄)₃.

**Ratio:** 1 mol Fe₂(SO₄)₃ se 2 mol Fe(OH)₃ aur 6 mol NaOH. To 0.1 mol se 0.2 mol Fe(OH)₃ aur 0.6 mol NaOH.

**Mass:** Fe(OH)₃ = 0.2 × 107 = **21.4 g**. NaOH = 0.6 × 40 = **24 g**.

**Jaanch:** reactants ka mass 40 + 24 = 64 g, products ka mass 21.4 + (0.3 × 142 = 42.6) = 64 g ✅ (Na₂SO₄ = 46 + 96 = 142).

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Ghol ke rang se hi namak ka naam pata chal jata hai."**
Demo: teen botal. Botal A mein neela ghol. Maan lo tum kehte ho "neela, to ye wohi namak hai jo pichhli baar dekha tha." Par neela rang sirf **Cu²⁺** ka hai, anion kuch bhi ho sakta hai: sulphate, chloride ya nitrate, aur teeno ke ghol neele dikhenge. Rang cation ka clue hai, anion ka nahi. Ab teen aur botal: ek mein Zn²⁺, ek mein Pb²⁺, ek mein Al³⁺. Teeno **rangheen**, to rang kisi ka naam nahi batata. Rang dekhne ke baad tests se pehchaan zaroori hai. Faisla: rang + cation test + anion test.

**Galti 2: "Anion ka test thos namak par hi kar lo, ghulne ki kya zaroorat?"**
Demo: ek student sochta hai ki thos namak par seedha BaCl₂ chhidak de. Par thos mein ions lattice mein jakde hote hain, aazad nahi, aur dono thos ek doosre ko chhu bhi nahi paate jaise ghol mein paas aate hain. Isliye precipitate nahi banta aur student galti se kehta hai "sulphate nahi hai". Ghol mein wahi namak ions mein toot kar aazad ho jata hai, aur Ba²⁺ + SO₄²⁻ → BaSO₄↓ turant ho jata hai.

```
   Thos namak                         Paani mein ghola hua namak
   (ions lattice mein jakde)          (ions aazad, ghoomte hain)

   +  -  +  -  +  -                    +     -      +
   -  +  -  +  -  +                 -     +       -
   +  -  +  -  +  -                      -      +
```

> **Figure 5.5** – Thos mein ions regular jaali mein jakde hain, ghol mein ions aazad hain. Isliye pehle ghulate hain, phir tests karte hain.

## Quick Recap

- Anjaan namak ki khoj ka kram: **dekho, ghulo, cation, anion, formula**.
- Ghol ka rang sirf cation ka clue hai: neela Cu²⁺, halka hara Fe²⁺, peela-bhura Fe³⁺; baaki rangheen.
- Cation: NaOH ki boondein aur excess, NH₄OH ki boondein aur excess; shared table se milao.
- Anion: acid pehle, phir AgNO₃ (Cl⁻) ya BaCl₂ (SO₄²⁻); carbonate ke liye dilute acid; ammonium ke liye garam NaOH.
- Elimination: jab ek vikalp ke tests na mile to use hatao; jab list diya ho to bacha hua uska jawab.
- Formula criss-cross se: Fe₂(SO₄)₃, FeCl₂, Cu(NO₃)₂.
- Jodiyaan: Zn²⁺/Al³⁺ excess NH₄OH se; Ca²⁺/Zn²⁺ excess NaOH se; Pb²⁺/Al³⁺ KI se; Fe²⁺/Fe³⁺ rang se.
- Rang akela kaafi nahi, aur anion test thos par nahi, ghol par.

## Practice Questions

(Sab sawaal paper par hal karne ke liye hain. Koi test student ghar par ya akela nahi karta.)

1. A pale green solution gives a dirty green precipitate with NaOH. On standing in air the top of the precipitate turns brown. With dilute HCl and then BaCl₂ it gives a white precipitate that does not dissolve in acid. Name the cation and the anion.
2. A colourless solution gives a white precipitate with NaOH that dissolves in excess. With NH₄OH the white precipitate does not dissolve in excess. On adding a few drops of KI a yellow precipitate forms. Name the cation. Why are lead compounds not handled by students?
3. Write the formula of the salt made of Al³⁺ and NO₃⁻ by charge balance, and find its formula mass (Al 27, N 14, O 16).
4. A teacher dissolves 25.4 g of FeCl₂ in water and adds exactly the NaOH needed to precipitate all Fe²⁺. Write the balanced equation and find the mass of Fe(OH)₂ formed and the mass of NaOH used (Fe 56, Cl 35.5, Na 23, O 16, H 1).
5. A blue solution gives a pale blue precipitate with NH₄OH drops, which dissolves in excess giving a deep blue solution. With dilute HCl and then BaCl₂ a white precipitate forms that is insoluble in acid. Name the ions. Why can the colour alone not give the anion?
6. A colourless solution gives a white precipitate with a few drops of NaOH that dissolves in excess NaOH. With NH₄OH it gives a white precipitate that also dissolves in excess NH₄OH. With dilute HNO₃ and then AgNO₃ nothing forms, but with dilute HCl and then BaCl₂ a white precipitate forms that does not dissolve in acid. Name the ions and write the formula of the salt.
7. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) A yellow-brown solution contains Fe²⁺. (b) Excess NH₄OH dissolves the precipitate of both Cu²⁺ and Zn²⁺. (c) To test for sulphate, BaCl₂ is sprinkled on the dry solid. (d) A salt whose solution gives no precipitate with NaOH can still contain NH₄⁺.
8. Put these steps of identifying an unknown salt in the correct order: test the anion, dissolve the salt in water, test the cation with NaOH and NH₄OH, write the formula, observe the colour.
9. A student wants to separate Zn²⁺ and Al³⁺ solutions, both colourless and both giving a white precipitate that dissolves in excess NaOH. Which single reagent should he add in excess, and what will he see in each tube?
10. Calcium and zinc solutions are both colourless and both give a white precipitate with NaOH drops. Which reagent in excess separates them, and what is seen?
11. Write the ionic equation for the reaction of Fe³⁺ with OH⁻ and show that charge and atoms balance.
12. A teacher dissolves 20 g of Fe₂(SO₄)₃ in water and adds exactly enough BaCl₂ solution to precipitate all the sulphate. Write the balanced equation and find the mass of BaSO₄ formed and of BaCl₂ used. Check by conservation of mass (Fe 56, S 32, O 16, Ba 137, Cl 35.5).
13. A salt solution gives no precipitate with AgNO₃ after HNO₃, no precipitate with BaCl₂ after HCl, and no gas with dilute acid. The shelf holds only chlorides, sulphates, nitrates and carbonates. Which anion remains? Why is carbonate excluded?

### Answers

1. Halka hara ghol aur gande hare rang ka precipitate: **Fe²⁺**. Khade rehne par upar se bhura hona (hawa Fe²⁺ ko aage badalti hai). BaCl₂ se acid mein bhi na ghulne wala safed precipitate: **SO₄²⁻**. Ions: Fe²⁺ aur SO₄²⁻.
2. NaOH excess mein ghulne wale: Zn²⁺, Pb²⁺, Al³⁺. NH₄OH excess mein nahi ghula, to Zn²⁺ nahi (Zn ghulta). Bache Pb²⁺ aur Al³⁺. KI se peela precipitate: Pb²⁺ + 2I⁻ → PbI₂. To **Pb²⁺**. Lead ke saare compounds zeherile hain aur body mein jama hote hain, isliye student unhe nahi chhoote, sirf teacher demo.
3. Charge numbers 3 aur 1. Criss-cross: Al ke neeche 1, NO₃ ke neeche 3. Formula **Al(NO₃)₃**. Jaanch: (+3) + 3 × (−1) = 0 ✅. Mass: 27 + 3 × (14 + 48) = 27 + 186 = **213**.
4. FeCl₂ + 2NaOH → Fe(OH)₂ + 2NaCl. Jaanch: Fe 1 = 1, Cl 2 = 2, Na 2 = 2, O 2 = 2, H 2 = 2 ✅. FeCl₂ = 56 + 71 = 127, to 25.4 ÷ 127 = **0.2 mol**. Fe(OH)₂ = 56 + 34 = 90, NaOH = 40. 1 : 1 aur 1 : 2, to Fe(OH)₂ 0.2 mol = 0.2 × 90 = **18 g**, NaOH 0.4 mol = 0.4 × 40 = **16 g**. Jaanch: mass 25.4 + 16 = 41.4 aur 18 + 0.4 × 58.5 = 18 + 23.4 = 41.4 ✅.
5. Halka neela precipitate jo excess NH₄OH mein gehre neele ghol mein ghule: **Cu²⁺**. BaCl₂ se acid mein na ghulne wala safed precipitate: **SO₄²⁻**. Rang akela anion nahi batata, kyunki Cu²⁺ ke saare ghol (sulphate, chloride, nitrate) neele hote hain.
6. Safed precipitate jo excess NaOH aur excess NH₄OH dono mein ghula: shared table mein sirf **Zn²⁺** (Pb²⁺ aur Al³⁺ excess NH₄OH mein nahi ghulte). AgNO₃ se kuchh nahi, to Cl⁻ nahi; BaCl₂ se acid mein na ghulne wala safed precipitate: **SO₄²⁻**. Formula: charge (+2) aur (−2), to **ZnSO₄**. Jaanch: (+2) + (−2) = 0 ✅.
7. (a) **False** ❌: peela-bhura ghol Fe³⁺ ka hai; Fe²⁺ halka hara. (b) **True** ✅: dono NH₄OH excess mein ghulte hain (Cu gehre neele mein, Zn rangheen mein). (c) **False** ❌: pehle namak ghulao, phir dilute HCl ke baad BaCl₂. (d) **True** ✅: NH₄⁺ thande NaOH se precipitate nahi deta, garam karne par ammonia gas.
8. Sahi kram: observe the colour, dissolve in water, test the cation with NaOH and NH₄OH, test the anion, write the formula.
9. **Excess NH₄OH**. Zn²⁺ wali tube mein pehle safed precipitate, phir excess mein ghul kar rangheen ghol. Al³⁺ wali tube mein safed precipitate bana rehta hai, nahi ghulta.
10. **Excess NaOH**. Zn²⁺ ka safed precipitate ghul jata hai (rangheen ghol). Ca²⁺ ka halka safed precipitate nahi ghulta.
11. Fe³⁺ + 3OH⁻ → Fe(OH)₃↓. Atoms: Fe 1 = 1, O 3 = 3, H 3 = 3. Charge: (+3) + 3 × (−1) = 0 = 0 ✅.
12. Fe₂(SO₄)₃ + 3BaCl₂ → 3BaSO₄↓ + 2FeCl₃ (Fe 2, S 3, O 12, Ba 3, Cl 6 dono taraf ✅). Fe₂(SO₄)₃ = 400, to 20 ÷ 400 = **0.05 mol**. Ratio 1 : 3, to BaSO₄ = 0.15 mol × 233 = **34.95 g**, BaCl₂ = 0.15 × 208 = **31.2 g**. FeCl₃ = 0.1 × 162.5 = 16.25 g. Jaanch: 20 + 31.2 = 51.2 g aur 34.95 + 16.25 = 51.2 g ✅. (Barium zehreela: sirf teacher demo.)
13. Bacha **NO₃⁻**. Carbonate isliye nahi ki dilute acid se koi gas nahi nikli (carbonate se brisk CO₂ aati). Chloride aur sulphate ke tests bhi negative the. Nitrate ka apna test aage ki class mein.
