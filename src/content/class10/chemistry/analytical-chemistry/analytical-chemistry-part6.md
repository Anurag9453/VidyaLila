## Part A: Chaar-kadam ka master method

Anjaan ghol ya thos namak milne par ye chaar kadam lo, hamesha isi kram mein.

1. **Dekho.** Thos ka rang aur ghol ka rang: neela matlab Cu²⁺ ka ishara, halka hara matlab Fe²⁺, peela-bhura matlab Fe³⁺, rangheen matlab baaki ions (Ca²⁺, Zn²⁺, Pb²⁺, Al³⁺, Na⁺, K⁺, NH₄⁺ ke ghol rangheen hote hain). Rang sirf ishara hai, saboot nahi (Topic 5).
2. **Cation ka test.** Ghol ke thode hisse mein pehle NaOH ki **kuch boondein**, phir **excess**. Alag saaf test tube mein NH₄OH ki kuch boondein, phir excess. Dhodhe ka rang aur excess mein kya hua, likho (Topic 2 aur Topic 3 ke table).
3. **Anion ka test.** Alag hisse mein anion ke test: Cl⁻ ke liye dilute HNO₃ phir AgNO₃, SO₄²⁻ ke liye dilute HCl phir BaCl₂, CO₃²⁻ ke liye dilute acid aur chuna paani, NH₄⁺ ke liye NaOH ke saath halka garam (Topic 4).
4. **Naam aur formula likho.** Cation aur anion ka charge dekh kar criss-cross se formula, phir har test ki balanced equation (pehle ionic, phir molecular).

```
   Kadam 1          Kadam 2             Kadam 3            Kadam 4
   DEKHO     --->   CATION ka    --->   ANION ka    --->    NAAM aur FORMULA
   (rang)           test (NaOH,         test (AgNO₃,        (charge balance,
                     NH₄OH)              BaCl₂, acid)        equations)

   Har kadam par teen sawaal: kya daala? kya dikha? kya matlab?
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Master method: chaar kadam ek ke baad ek. Har kadam par wahi teen sawaal puchho jo Topic 1 mein seekhe the: kya daala, kya dikha, uska matlab kya.

Ek zaroori niyam: **cation aur anion ke test alag-alag hisson mein** kiye jaate hain, kyunki ek test tube mein sab milane se rang aur dhodhe aapas mein ulajh jaate hain.

### Dhodhe ka figure: kaise likhein

Test tube mein dhodha **neeche** baithta hai, upar saaf ghol rehta hai. Aisa figure hum bahut baar banayenge.

```
    NaOH ki boondein daalne ke baad          Excess NaOH daalne ke baad

         :   saaf ghol   :                         :   saaf ghol   :
         :   (rangheen)  :                         :   (rangheen)  :
         :               :                         :               :
         :  safed dhodha :                         :  koi dhodha   :
         :_______________:                         :_______________:
              (a)                                       (b)
```

> **Figure 6.2** – Zn²⁺, Pb²⁺ ya Al³⁺ ke ghol mein NaOH: (a) boondein: neeche safed dhodha, (b) excess: dhodha ghul gaya, saaf rangheen ghol bacha.

Ye wahi picture hai jo Topic 2 mein dikha tha: Zn(OH)₂, Pb(OH)₂ aur Al(OH)₃ excess NaOH mein ghulte hain (amphoteric swabhav, Chapter 2 ka Part E).

## Part C: Mile-jule puzzles (worked)

**Poison line (aaj ke puzzles ke liye):** lead ke compound zehreele hain, barium ke compound zehreele hain, silver nitrate ki boondein bhi chamdi par kaale daag chhodti hain, tamba ke namak khaane par nuksaan karte hain, NaOH ka patla ghol bhi aankh aur chamdi ko jalata hai, aur ammonia gas ki tez gandh jalan karti hai. **Is chapter mein koi bhi test student akele ya ghar par nahi karta.** Heavy metals ka bacha hua ghol teacher alag dibbe mein jama karta hai, naali mein kabhi nahi.

### Puzzle 1. Safed dhodha jo ghul gaya (observation table se)

Ek rangheen ghol P mein NaOH ki kuch boondein: **safed dhodha**. Aur NaOH daalne par dhodha **ghul gaya**. Doosre test tube mein NH₄OH ki boondein: **safed dhodha**, excess daalne par **nahi ghula**.

**Kadam 1:** ghol rangheen, to Cu²⁺, Fe²⁺, Fe³⁺ nahi.
**Kadam 2:** NaOH mein safed dhodha jo excess mein ghule: Zn²⁺, Pb²⁺, Al³⁺ (teeno). NH₄OH ke excess mein **nahi** ghula: Zn²⁺ nahi (Zn ghulta hai). Bacha: **Pb²⁺ ya Al³⁺**.
**Kadam 3:** in dono ko NaOH aur NH₄OH se alag nahi kar sakte (Topic 3). Isliye ek aur suraag chahiye: ghol P mein **dilute HCl** ki boondein daalne par safed dhodha aaya (sirf teacher demo). Ye Pb²⁺ ka suraag hai (lead chloride thande paani mein kam ghulta hai, Chapter 1).
**Kadam 4:** ion **Pb²⁺**. Equations: Pb²⁺ + 2OH⁻ → Pb(OH)₂↓ aur Pb(OH)₂ + 2NaOH → Na₂PbO₂ + 2H₂O.

**Jaanch:** Pb²⁺ + 2OH⁻: Pb 1 = 1, O 2 = 2, H 2 = 2, charge (+2) + 2 × (−1) = 0 ✅. Doosri equation: left mein Pb 1, Na 2, O 2 + 2 = 4, H 2 + 2 = 4; right mein Na₂PbO₂ (Na 2, Pb 1, O 2) aur 2H₂O (O 2, H 4): O 4, H 4 ✅.

### Puzzle 2. Neela ghol, dhodha, aur gehra neela (flowchart se)

Ek **neela** ghol Q hai. Kaun si cheezein dikhti hain, flowchart mein lagao.

```
   Ghol Q (neela)
        :
        v
   NaOH ki boondein  --->  halka neela dhodha
        :
        v
   Excess NaOH       --->  dhodha jaisa ka taisa (nahi ghula)
   
   Alag hissa :
   NH₄OH ki boondein --->  halka neela dhodha
        :
        v
   Excess NH₄OH      --->  dhodha ghula, GEHRA (siyahi jaisa) neela ghol
```

> **Figure 6.4** – Flowchart: Q mein NaOH aur NH₄OH ke natije. NH₄OH ke excess mein gehra neela ghol Cu²⁺ ki khaas pehchaan hai.

**Kadam 1:** ghol neela: Cu²⁺ ka ishara (sirf ishara).
**Kadam 2:** halka neela dhodha dono se, excess NaOH mein nahi ghula, excess NH₄OH mein **gehra neela ghol** bana: Cu²⁺ ke siwa shared table mein koi nahi.
**Kadam 3:** label par likha hai "nitrate". Anion ke tests (AgNO₃, BaCl₂) par kuch nahi aaya: ye Cl⁻ aur SO₄²⁻ ke tests ka natija **nahi** hai, label ke hisaab se NO₃⁻ (uski pakki pehchaan aage ki class mein).
**Kadam 4:** Cu²⁺ aur NO₃⁻: charge (+2) aur 2 × (−1): formula **Cu(NO₃)₂**. Equation: Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2NaOH → Cu(OH)₂↓ + 2NaNO₃.

**Jaanch:** Cu 1 = 1, N 2 = 2, O: left 6 (nitrate) + 2 (NaOH) = 8, right 2 (Cu(OH)₂) + 6 (2NaNO₃) = 8 ✅. Na 2 = 2 ✅, H 2 = 2 ✅.

### Puzzle 3. Halka hara ghol jo bhura ho gaya

Ghol R **halka hara** hai. NaOH ki boondein daalne par **ganda hara dhodha**, excess mein **nahi ghula**. NH₄OH ke saath bhi **ganda hara dhodha**, excess mein **nahi ghula**. Kuch der khula chhodne par upar ka hissa **bhura** hone laga.

**Kadam 1:** halka hara ghol: Fe²⁺ ka ishara.
**Kadam 2:** ganda hara dhodha, dono ke excess mein nahi ghula: **Fe²⁺**. (Fe³⁺ ka dhodha laal-bhura hota, hara nahi.)
**Kadam 3 aur 4:** bhura hona: hawa Fe²⁺ ko dheere-dheere Fe³⁺ mein badal deti hai (Topic 2, ek line). Ionic equation: Fe²⁺ + 2OH⁻ → Fe(OH)₂↓. Jis anion ke saath ye aaya, use alag test se dekhenge.

**Jaanch:** Fe 1 = 1, O 2 = 2, H 2 = 2, charge (+2) + 2(−1) = 0 ✅.

### Puzzle 4. Halka safed dhodha jo ghula hi nahi

Rangheen ghol S mein NaOH ki boondein: **halka safed dhodha** (bahut kam). Excess NaOH mein **nahi ghula**. NH₄OH ki boondein: **koi dhodha nahi ya bahut halka**.

**Kadam 2:** NaOH mein safed dhodha jo excess mein nahi ghula: Ca²⁺ (Zn²⁺, Pb²⁺, Al³⁺ to ghul jaate). NH₄OH par dhodha nahi: Ca²⁺ ki pehchaan paki.
**Kadam 4:** Ca²⁺ + 2OH⁻ → Ca(OH)₂↓.

**Jaanch:** Ca 1 = 1, O 2 = 2, H 2 = 2, charge +2 − 2 = 0 ✅.

### Puzzle 5. Anion ka puzzle: kaun sa ion safed curdy dhodha deta hai?

Ek rangheen ghol T. Pehle dilute HNO₃ ki thodi boondein, phir AgNO₃ ghol: **safed, curdy (dahi jaisa) dhodha**. Is dhodhe mein NH₄OH daalne par dhodha **ghul gaya**. (AgNO₃ zehreela hai aur chamdi par kaale daag chhodta hai: sirf teacher demo.)

**Kadam 3:** safed curdy dhodha jo NH₄OH mein ghule: **Cl⁻**.
**Kadam 4:** Ionic equation: Ag⁺ + Cl⁻ → AgCl↓.

**Jaanch:** Ag 1 = 1, Cl 1 = 1, charge (+1) + (−1) = 0 ✅.

### Puzzle 6. Thos namak: gas ke do suraag

Thos U (safed). **(i)** Teacher ne dilute acid daala: tez bulbule, gas jo chuna paani ko **doodhiya** karti hai. **(ii)** Doosre hisse ko NaOH ke saath halka garam kiya: **tez gandh wali gas**, jo gili laal litmus ko **neela** karti hai. (Ammonia gas ki gandh tez aur jalan wali hai: sirf fume cupboard mein teacher, student sirf padhta hai.)

**Kadam 3:** (i) CO₂ aur doodhiya chuna paani: **CO₃²⁻**. (ii) NH₃ gas, gili laal litmus neela: **NH₄⁺**.
**Kadam 4:** NH₄⁺ (+1) aur CO₃²⁻ (−2): criss-cross se **(NH₄)₂CO₃**. Equation: (NH₄)₂CO₃ + 2NaOH → Na₂CO₃ + 2H₂O + 2NH₃.

**Jaanch:** N 2 = 2 ✅. H: left 8 + 2 = 10, right 4 (2H₂O) + 6 (2NH₃) = 10 ✅. C 1 = 1 ✅, O 3 + 2 = 5 = 3 + 2 ✅, Na 2 = 2 ✅.

### Puzzle 7. BaCl₂ ke saath safed dhodha: kaun sa anion?

Ghol V mein **dilute HCl pehle** daala: koi bulbule nahi. Phir BaCl₂: **safed dhodha** jo acid mein **nahi ghula**. Cation ke liye: V peela-bhura hai, NaOH ki boondein: **laal-bhura dhodha**, excess mein **nahi ghula**. (Barium ke compound zehreele hain: sirf teacher demo.)

```
   Ghol V :  dilute HCl  --->  koi bulbule nahi
                  :
                  v
             BaCl₂ daala --->  safed dhodha, acid mein NAHI ghula
                  :
                  v
             anion = SO₄²⁻

   Alag hissa :  NaOH  --->  laal-bhura dhodha, excess mein nahi ghula
                  :
                  v
             cation = Fe³⁺
```

> **Figure 6.5** – Puzzle 7 ka flowchart: anion aur cation alag-alag hisson mein nikale gaye.

Ionic equations: Ba²⁺ + SO₄²⁻ → BaSO₄↓ aur Fe³⁺ + 3OH⁻ → Fe(OH)₃↓. Ghol mein **Fe³⁺ aur SO₄²⁻** ions hain.

**Jaanch:** Ba 1 = 1, S 1 = 1, O 4 = 4, charge (+2) + (−2) = 0 ✅. Fe 1 = 1, O 3 = 3, H 3 = 3, charge (+3) + 3(−1) = 0 ✅.

### Puzzle 8. Do bottles, do pehchaan (Zn²⁺ ya Al³⁺)

Do rangheen bottles X aur Y hain. Dono mein NaOH ki boondein daalne par **safed dhodha**, excess mein **dono ghul jaate hain**. NH₄OH ki boondein dono mein **safed dhodha**. Excess NH₄OH: X ka dhodha **ghul gaya**, Y ka dhodha **nahi ghula**. Ye maloom hai ki dono mein se **koi lead nahi** hai.

**Kadam 1:** dono rangheen, kuch bhi kahna mushkil.
**Kadam 2:** NaOH ke excess mein ghule: Zn²⁺, Pb²⁺, Al³⁺. Lead nahi (diya hua). Bacha Zn²⁺ ya Al³⁺.
**Kadam 3:** NH₄OH ke excess mein ghule to **Zn²⁺**, na ghule to **Al³⁺**. X = **Zn²⁺**, Y = **Al³⁺**.
**Kadam 4:** X: Zn²⁺ + 2OH⁻ → Zn(OH)₂↓, Zn(OH)₂ + 2NaOH → Na₂ZnO₂ + 2H₂O. Y: Al³⁺ + 3OH⁻ → Al(OH)₃↓, Al(OH)₃ + NaOH → NaAlO₂ + 2H₂O.

**Jaanch:** Zn(OH)₂ + 2NaOH → Na₂ZnO₂ + 2H₂O: Zn 1, Na 2, O 4 = 2 + 2, H 4 = 4 ✅. Al(OH)₃ + NaOH → NaAlO₂ + 2H₂O: Al 1, Na 1, O 4 = 2 + 2, H 4 = 4 ✅.

## Part D: Kitna dhodha banega? (Chapter 5 ka mole method)

### Example 1. Cu(NO₃)₂ aur NaOH se dhodhe ka mass

**Sawaal:** **18.75 g** Cu(NO₃)₂ ke ghol mein NaOH ki bilkul zaroori matra daali gayi. Kitna Cu(OH)₂ ka dhodha banega, aur ghol mein kitna NaNO₃ rahega? (Cu = 63.5, N = 14, O = 16, H = 1, Na = 23.)

**Equation:** Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2NaOH → Cu(OH)₂↓ + 2NaNO₃

**Kadam 1 (molar mass):** Cu(NO₃)₂ = 63.5 + 2 × (14 + 3 × 16) = 63.5 + 2 × 62 = **187.5 g/mol**. Cu(OH)₂ = 63.5 + 2 × 17 = **97.5 g/mol**. NaNO₃ = 23 + 14 + 48 = **85 g/mol**.
**Kadam 2 (mole):** 18.75 ÷ 187.5 = **0.1 mol**.
**Kadam 3 (ratio):** 1 mol Cu(NO₃)₂ se 1 mol Cu(OH)₂ aur 2 mol NaNO₃. To 0.1 mol se 0.1 mol Cu(OH)₂ aur 0.2 mol NaNO₃.
**Kadam 4 (mass):** Cu(OH)₂ = 0.1 × 97.5 = **9.75 g**. NaNO₃ = 0.2 × 85 = **17 g**.

```
       :  saaf ghol (NaNO₃ ghula)  :
       :                           :
       :   Cu(OH)₂ dhodha : 9.75 g :
       :___________________________:
```

> **Figure 6.6** – Test tube: Cu(OH)₂ ka halka neela dhodha neeche baitha hai, upar saaf ghol mein NaNO₃ ghula hai.

**Jaanch:** NaOH ka mass = 0.2 mol × 40 = 8 g. Left: 18.75 + 8 = 26.75 g. Right: 9.75 + 17 = 26.75 g ✅ (mass conservation).

(Ye numerical sirf "kitna dhodha" jaanne ke liye hai; asli lab mein aisa tola-bhar kaam teacher hi karta hai.)

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Excess NaOH ek saath dher sa daal diya, dhodha dikha hi nahi."**
Demo: Zn²⁺ ya Al³⁺ ke ghol mein ek student ne ek saath poori dropper-bhar NaOH daal di. Test tube mein **saaf rangheen ghol** hi dikha. Usne likh diya: "koi dhodha nahi, to koi ion nahi!" Galat. Dhodha bana tha, par itni jaldi excess aaya ki turant ghul gaya.

```
   Galat tareeka                         Sahi tareeka
   (ek saath dher NaOH)                  (boond-boond, phir excess)

   ghol  --->  saaf ghol                 boondein  --->  safed dhodha dikha
   (dhodha dikha hi nahi)                excess    --->  dhodha ghul gaya
   
   nateeja: Zn²⁺, Pb²⁺, Al³⁺ chhoot gaye  nateeja: teeno ka pata chala
```

> **Figure 6.7** – Excess ek saath daalne se Zn²⁺, Pb²⁺, Al³⁺ ka dhodha-stage chhoot jaata hai. Hamesha pehle **kuch boondein**, dekho, phir excess.

Niyam: pehle teacher kuch boondein hi deta hai, dhodha dekhne ke baad hi excess. Excess ka matlab "dhodha ghulne tak thoda-thoda aur".

**Galti 2: "NaOH aur NH₄OH ke natije aapas mein badal diye."**
Demo: ek Al³⁺ ke ghol mein safed dhodha aaya aur excess daalne par ghul gaya. Student ne notebook mein sirf likha "excess mein ghula", aur bina yaad kiye ki wo NaOH tha ya NH₄OH, use "NH₄OH mein ghula" maan liya. Natija: usne ghol ko Zn²⁺ likh diya (kyunki "Zn NH₄OH mein ghulta hai"). Asal mein wo NaOH ka natija tha: Al³⁺ **NaOH** ke excess mein ghulta hai, **NH₄OH** ke excess mein **nahi**.

Sudhaar ka tareeka: table mein har natije ke saamne **reagent ka naam** likho.

| Ghol ka natija | Galat likha | Sahi likha |
|---|---|---|
| Safed dhodha, excess mein ghula | "ghula" | "ghula NaOH mein" ya "ghula NH₄OH mein" |
| Al³⁺ ke saath excess NH₄OH | ghula | **nahi ghula** |
| Al³⁺ ke saath excess NaOH | nahi ghula | **ghula** |

Aadat: har kholi mein reagent ka naam likho, taaki Zn²⁺ aur Al³⁺ ya Cu²⁺ jaise ions ulajhein nahi.

**Galti 3: "Ionic equation mein spectator ions bhi likh diye."**
Demo: Cu(NO₃)₂ aur NaOH ki molecular equation Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2NaOH → Cu(OH)₂↓ + 2NaNO₃ hai. Ek student ne ionic equation likhi: Cu²⁺ + 2NO₃⁻ + 2Na⁺ + 2OH⁻ → Cu(OH)₂ + 2Na⁺ + 2NO₃⁻. Ye **poori ionic equation** hai aur galat nahi, par jo **net ionic equation** maanga jaata hai wo chhoti hai. Dono taraf 2NO₃⁻ aur 2Na⁺ hain: ye spectator hain, kaat do. Bacha: Cu²⁺ + 2OH⁻ → Cu(OH)₂↓.

Saath hi, spectator ion hamesha **dono taraf ek jaise** hote hain. Agar koi ion sirf ek taraf ho, to wo spectator nahi hai.

**Jaanch:** Na⁺ 2 = 2, NO₃⁻ 2 = 2 kat gaye ✅. Bache: Cu 1 = 1, O 2 = 2, H 2 = 2, charge +2 + 2(−1) = 0 ✅.

**Galti 4: "Dhodhe ko (aq) likh diya, ya state symbol hi nahi likha."**
Demo: ek student ne likha: Cu²⁺(aq) + 2OH⁻(aq) → Cu(OH)₂(aq). Phir usne test tube ki tasveer banayi: dhodha neeche baitha. Tasveer aur equation mein jhagda! (aq) ka matlab ghol mein ghula hua, jabki dhodha **ghulta nahi**, wo neeche baithta hai. Sahi: Cu²⁺(aq) + 2OH⁻(aq) → Cu(OH)₂(s)↓.

Niyam: ghulansheel salt aur ions (aq), dhodha (s), gas (g), paani (l). Dhodhe ke aage ↓ bhi laga sakte ho.

## Quick Recap

- **Master method:** dekho (rang), cation ka test (NaOH phir NH₄OH: boondein aur excess), anion ka test (alag hisse mein), naam aur formula (charge balance) aur equations.
- **Cation table:** Ca²⁺ halka safed (nahi ghulta); Fe²⁺ ganda hara; Fe³⁺ laal-bhura; Cu²⁺ halka neela (NH₄OH excess mein gehra neela); Zn²⁺ safed (NaOH aur NH₄OH dono ke excess mein ghulta); Pb²⁺ aur Al³⁺ safed (NaOH excess mein ghulte, NH₄OH excess mein nahi).
- **Anion:** Cl⁻ safed curdy AgCl (NH₄OH mein ghulta); SO₄²⁻ safed BaSO₄ (acid mein nahi ghulta); CO₃²⁻ bulbule aur chuna paani doodhiya; NH₄⁺ garam NaOH se tez gandh wali gas.
- **Equations:** ionic pehle, spectator ions kaat kar; dhodha (s)↓, ghulansheel (aq).
- **Kitna dhodha:** molar mass, mole, ratio, mass (Cu(NO₃)₂ 18.75 g se Cu(OH)₂ 9.75 g).
- **Galtiyan:** excess ek saath mat daalo; reagent ka naam har natije ke saath likho; spectator ions kaato; dhodhe ko (s) likho.
- Har test **school lab mein teacher ke saath** ya **teacher demo**; lead, barium, silver zehreele; ammonia fume cupboard mein; bacha hua ghol teacher ke hisse.

## Practice Questions

(Sab sawaal paper par hal karne ke liye hain. Atomic masses: H 1, N 14, O 16, Na 23, Al 27, Fe 56.)

1. Solution A is yellow-brown. With a few drops of NaOH it gives a reddish-brown precipitate that does not dissolve in excess NaOH. With a few drops of NH₄OH it gives a reddish-brown precipitate that does not dissolve in excess NH₄OH. Name the cation and write the ionic equation for the precipitate.
2. Solution B is colourless. With a few drops of NaOH it gives a white precipitate that dissolves in excess NaOH. With a few drops of NH₄OH it gives a white precipitate that also dissolves in excess NH₄OH. Which of the seven ions is present? Write the equation for the dissolving of the precipitate in NaOH.
3. A teacher uses 4.26 g of Al(NO₃)₃ in solution and adds exactly the right amount of NaOH. Write the balanced equation, and find the mass of Al(OH)₃ precipitate and the mass of NaNO₃ formed. Check that mass is conserved.
4. How many grams of Fe(NO₃)₂ are needed to give 4.5 g of Fe(OH)₂ precipitate with NaOH? Write the equation first, then find the mass of NaOH that reacts.
5. Solution C gives a slight white precipitate with a few drops of NaOH, which stays unchanged in excess NaOH. NH₄OH gives no precipitate. Separately, the silver nitrate test (after dilute HNO₃) and the barium chloride test (after dilute HCl) give no precipitate, and dilute acid gives no gas. The bottle is known to hold a chloride, a sulphate, a carbonate or a nitrate. Name the cation and the anion, and write the formula of the salt.
6. Solid D, with warm NaOH, gives a pungent gas that turns moist red litmus blue. A solution of D, with dilute HNO₃ and then silver nitrate, gives a white curdy precipitate that dissolves in NH₄OH. Name the two ions in D, and write the ionic equation for the gas-forming test.
7. Two colourless solutions E and F both give a white precipitate with a few drops of NaOH. In excess NaOH, E's precipitate dissolves but F's does not. With NH₄OH, E gives a white precipitate that stays in excess NH₄OH, while F gives no precipitate. It is known that neither solution contains aluminium. Name each ion and explain your logic.
8. A student adds a large dropper-full of NaOH to a colourless solution and sees only a clear solution. Which of the seven ions could be present? Why can copper not be one of them? What should the student do differently?
9. One bottle contains a pale green solution and another a yellow-brown solution. Both give precipitates with NaOH that do not dissolve in excess. Name the ions, write the two ionic equations, and say which solution may slowly change colour on standing in air.
10. Draw a flowchart, using only NaOH and NH₄OH, to separate Zn²⁺, Al³⁺ and Ca²⁺ in three colourless solutions.
11. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) A white precipitate with NaOH proves the ion is Zn²⁺. (b) In the net ionic equation for Fe²⁺ with NaOH, Na⁺ is a spectator ion. (c) Cu(OH)₂ is written with (aq) because it forms in a solution. (d) Excess NH₄OH dissolves the precipitates of both Cu²⁺ and Zn²⁺.
12. Complete this table from memory for Pb²⁺, Cu²⁺, Fe²⁺ and Ca²⁺: colour of precipitate with NaOH drops, effect of excess NaOH, effect of excess NH₄OH.
13. A student writes the formula of the salt of Fe²⁺ with the nitrate ion as FeNO₃. Use charges to write the correct formula and say what is wrong with the student's formula.

### Answers

1. Peele-bhure ghol aur laal-bhura dhodha jo dono excess mein nahi ghula: **Fe³⁺**. Ionic equation: Fe³⁺ + 3OH⁻ → Fe(OH)₃↓. Jaanch: Fe 1, O 3, H 3, charge (+3) + 3(−1) = 0 ✅.
2. Rangheen ghol, safed dhodha jo NaOH aur NH₄OH dono ke excess mein ghula: shared table mein sirf **Zn²⁺** aisa karta hai (Pb²⁺ aur Al³⁺ NH₄OH ke excess mein nahi ghulte). Equation: Zn(OH)₂ + 2NaOH → Na₂ZnO₂ + 2H₂O. Jaanch: Zn 1, Na 2, O 4 = 2 + 2, H 4 = 4 ✅.
3. Equation: Al(NO₃)₃ + 3NaOH → Al(OH)₃↓ + 3NaNO₃. Molar mass: Al(NO₃)₃ = 27 + 3 × 62 = 213, Al(OH)₃ = 27 + 3 × 17 = 78, NaNO₃ = 85. Mole = 4.26 ÷ 213 = **0.02 mol**. Al(OH)₃ = 0.02 × 78 = **1.56 g**. NaNO₃ = 0.06 × 85 = **5.1 g**. NaOH = 0.06 × 40 = 2.4 g. Jaanch: 4.26 + 2.4 = 6.66 g, 1.56 + 5.1 = 6.66 g ✅. Atom ginti: Al 1 = 1, N 3 = 3, O 9 + 3 = 12 = 3 + 9, H 3 = 3, Na 3 = 3 ✅.
4. Equation: Fe(NO₃)₂ + 2NaOH → Fe(OH)₂↓ + 2NaNO₃. Fe(OH)₂ = 56 + 34 = 90. 4.5 ÷ 90 = **0.05 mol**. 1 mol Fe(NO₃)₂ se 1 mol Fe(OH)₂, to 0.05 mol Fe(NO₃)₂. Fe(NO₃)₂ = 56 + 124 = 180, to **0.05 × 180 = 9.0 g**. NaOH: 2 × 0.05 = 0.1 mol, 0.1 × 40 = **4.0 g**. Jaanch: NaNO₃ = 0.1 × 85 = 8.5 g. Left: 9.0 + 4.0 = 13.0 g, right: 4.5 + 8.5 = 13.0 g ✅.
5. NaOH se halka safed dhodha jo excess mein nahi ghula aur NH₄OH se dhodha nahi: cation **Ca²⁺**. Anion: AgNO₃ se kuch nahi (Cl⁻ nahi), BaCl₂ se kuch nahi (SO₄²⁻ nahi), acid se gas nahi (CO₃²⁻ nahi); diye gaye chaar mein se bacha **NO₃⁻** (iska apna test aage ki class mein). Formula: Ca²⁺ aur 2 NO₃⁻, to **Ca(NO₃)₂**. Jaanch: (+2) + 2 × (−1) = 0 ✅.
6. Garam NaOH se tez gandh, gili laal litmus neela: **NH₄⁺**. AgNO₃ se safed curdy dhodha jo NH₄OH mein ghula: **Cl⁻**. Equation: NH₄⁺ + OH⁻ → NH₃ + H₂O. Jaanch: N 1 = 1, H 4 + 1 = 5 = 3 + 2, O 1 = 1, charge (+1) + (−1) = 0 = 0 ✅.
7. **E:** NaOH ke excess mein ghula, to Zn²⁺, Pb²⁺ ya Al³⁺. NH₄OH ke excess mein nahi ghula, to Zn²⁺ nahi. Aluminium nahi (diya hua), to **E = Pb²⁺** (lead zehreela: sirf teacher demo). **F:** safed dhodha jo excess NaOH mein nahi ghula aur NH₄OH se dhodha nahi: **F = Ca²⁺**. Jaanch: E ke natije table ki Pb²⁺ row se, F ke Ca²⁺ row se milte hain ✅.
8. Ek saath dher NaOH daalne se dhodha ghul gaya ho sakta hai, to wo ion jo excess NaOH mein ghulte hain: **Zn²⁺, Pb²⁺, Al³⁺**. Cu²⁺ ka dhodha excess NaOH mein **nahi ghulta**, to wo saaf ghol nahi rehta (aur Cu²⁺ ka ghol neela hota). Student ko **kuch boondein** daalni chahiye, dhodha dekhna chahiye, phir excess.
9. Halka hara ghol: **Fe²⁺**, equation Fe²⁺ + 2OH⁻ → Fe(OH)₂↓. Peela-bhura: **Fe³⁺**, equation Fe³⁺ + 3OH⁻ → Fe(OH)₃↓. Hara wala (Fe²⁺) khule mein dheere-dheere bhura hone lagta hai kyunki hawa use Fe³⁺ mein badalti hai. Jaanch: Fe(OH)₂: Fe 1, O 2, H 2, charge 0 ✅; Fe(OH)₃: Fe 1, O 3, H 3, charge 0 ✅.
10. Teeno mein NaOH ki boondein: Zn²⁺ aur Al³⁺ mein safed dhodha, Ca²⁺ mein halka safed. Excess NaOH: Zn²⁺ aur Al³⁺ ghulte hain, Ca²⁺ nahi: Ca²⁺ alag. Ab Zn²⁺ aur Al³⁺ ke naye hisse mein NH₄OH excess tak: ghule to Zn²⁺, na ghule to Al³⁺. Flowchart:

```
   NaOH ki boondein  --->  teeno mein safed dhodha (Ca²⁺ mein halka)
          :
          v
   Excess NaOH       --->  ghula : Zn²⁺ ya Al³⁺
          :                nahi ghula : Ca²⁺
          v
   NH₄OH excess      --->  ghula : Zn²⁺
                           nahi ghula : Al³⁺
```

   Jaanch: teeno ions alag-alag pehchane gaye ✅.
11. (a) **False** ❌: safed dhodha Ca²⁺, Zn²⁺, Pb²⁺, Al³⁺ sab dete hain; excess aur NH₄OH ke test se hi pata chalta hai. (b) **True** ✅: Na⁺ dono taraf ek jaisa, badalta nahi. (c) **False** ❌: Cu(OH)₂ dhodha hai, to (s). (d) **True** ✅: dono ke dhodhe excess NH₄OH mein ghulte hain (Cu: gehra neela, Zn: rangheen).
12. Pb²⁺: safed dhodha, excess NaOH mein ghulta, excess NH₄OH mein nahi. Cu²⁺: halka neela dhodha, excess NaOH mein nahi ghulta, excess NH₄OH mein ghulta (gehra neela). Fe²⁺: ganda hara dhodha, excess NaOH mein nahi ghulta, excess NH₄OH mein nahi ghulta. Ca²⁺: halka safed dhodha, excess NaOH mein nahi ghulta, excess NH₄OH mein koi badlaav nahi.
13. Fe²⁺ ka charge +2 aur NO₃⁻ ka −1: do NO₃⁻ chahiye, to **Fe(NO₃)₂**. FeNO₃ mein charge (+2) + (−1) = +1, neutral nahi, isliye galat. Bracket zaroori hai kyunki 2 poore NO₃ jhund chahiye. Jaanch: Fe 1, N 2, O 6; charge +2 + 2(−1) = 0 ✅.
