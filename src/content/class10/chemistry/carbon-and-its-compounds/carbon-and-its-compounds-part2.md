# Topic 2: Homologous Series Aur Functional Group

Topic 1 mein dekha ki carbon chaar haath wala tatva hai, wo lambi chain, branch aur ring bana sakta hai, aur isiliye carbon ke **lakhon compounds** hain. Ab ek bada sawaal: itne saare compounds ko **yaad kaise rakhein**? Kya har ek ko alag se ratna padega? Nahi. Chemists ne inhe **parivaaron** mein baant diya, jahan ek parivaar ke saare members ek jaisi chaal chalte hain aur har member pichhle se bas **ek CH₂ lamba** hota hai. Aaj hum yahi parivaar (homologous series) aur unki pehchaan (functional group) seekhenge.

---

## Metro ki gaadi ek coach lambi

Mr. Kodre apne bhatije Yogesh ko lekar Sunday ko metro station gaye. Platform par ek bada poster laga tha: "Line ka vistaar: ab 3 coach ki jagah 6 coach ki gaadi". Thodi der mein 3 coach wali gaadi aayi, aur Yogesh ne apni chemistry ki copy khol li, jisme usne kal hi kuch gas ke formula likhe the.

"Chacha, ye poster dekho. 3 coach aur 6 coach ki gaadi mein kya same hota hai, aur kya alag?" Mr. Kodre hans diye. "Dhyan se dekho. Har coach ek hi design ka hai: wahi darwaze, wahi seat. Gaadi ke dono sire par wahi engine wali cabin hai. Same hai: **gaadi ki jaat**. Alag hai: lambai, kul log, kul vajan, aur ye bhi ki lambi gaadi ko rukne mein zyada doori lagti hai. Aur ek baat: gaadi **ek coach ek baar mein** badhti hai. Aadha coach kabhi nahi judta."

Yogesh ne copy ki taraf dekha: CH₄, C₂H₆, C₃H₈, C₄H₁₀. "Chacha, ye gas bhi aisi hi lag rahi hain! CH₄ mein 1 carbon, C₂H₆ mein 2, C₃H₈ mein 3... har agle mein ek carbon zyada. Aur ye sab jalti bhi hain." Mr. Kodre ne copy uthayi. "Bahut accha dekha, Yogesh. Isi ko kehte hain **homologous series**: ek **metro line** jaisi, jahan har agla member ek **CH₂ coach** zyada lekar aata hai. Chalo samjhte hain ki ye gaadi kaise bani hai, aur ye ki gaadi ko ek 'line' kaun banata hai."

## Part A: "Series" ka matlab: ek metro line

Pehle dekhte hain ki carbon ke compounds ko ginne ka sabse aasaan tareeka kya hai: **sirf carbon aur hydrogen** se bane hydrocarbon, jinme sirf single bond ho (alkane). Ginti shuru karo: 1 carbon, 2, 3, 4, 5, ...

| Naam | Formula | Ek aur coach? | Molar mass (H 1, C 12) |
|---|---|---|---|
| methane | CH₄ | pehla member | 16 |
| ethane | C₂H₆ | +CH₂ | 30 |
| propane | C₃H₈ | +CH₂ | 44 |
| butane | C₄H₁₀ | +CH₂ | 58 |
| pentane | C₅H₁₂ | +CH₂ | 72 |

Dekho: methane se ethane banane ke liye ek **C** aur do **H** judte hain, yaani ek **CH₂ coach**. Wahi ethane se propane mein, propane se butane mein. CH₂ ka mass **12 + 2 × 1 = 14**. Isliye har agla member pichhle se **14 zyada** bhaari hai: 16, 30, 44, 58, 72 (har kadam par 14).

Figure 2.1 ko metro ki tarah padho: dono sire par ek-ek H (engine ki cabin), beech mein jitne chahe CH₂ coach.

```
  CH₄   :   H-[CH₂]-H
  C₂H₆  :   H-[CH₂]-[CH₂]-H
  C₃H₈  :   H-[CH₂]-[CH₂]-[CH₂]-H
  C₄H₁₀ :   H-[CH₂]-[CH₂]-[CH₂]-[CH₂]-H
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Alkane series ko "ginti ke nazariye" se dekha: har formula = do sire ke H + n coach CH₂. CH₄ mein 1 coach, C₂H₆ mein 2, C₃H₈ mein 3, C₄H₁₀ mein 4. Atoms ginkar: 4 coach = 4 C aur 8 H, upar se 2 sire ke H = 10 H, yaani C₄H₁₀. Asli structure agli figure mein.

Ab asli structure dekho. Har carbon ke **chaar** bond aur har hydrogen ka **ek** bond gino (Topic 1 ka niyam).

```
      H                 H   H                 H   H   H
      I                 I   I                 I   I   I
  H - C - H         H - C - C - H         H - C - C - C - H
      I                 I   I                 I   I   I
      H                 H   H                 H   H   H

     CH₄                 C₂H₆                     C₃H₈
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Pehle teen alkane ke structural formula. Har C ke chaar bond: CH₄ ka C = 4 H; C₂H₆ ke har C par 3 H + 1 C–C = 4; C₃H₈ ke beech wale C par 2 H + 2 C–C = 4, aur sire wale C par 3 H + 1 C–C = 4. Har H ka ek bond. Ek coach (CH₂) badhne par ek C aur do H jude.

**Homologous series ki paanch pehchaan:**
1. Sabka **functional group** ek hi hota hai.
2. Sabka **general formula** ek hi hota hai.
3. Agla member pichhle se ek **CH₂ (mass 14)** zyada hota hai.
4. Sabke **rasayanik gun lagbhag ek jaise** hote hain.
5. Sabke **bhautik gun** (boiling point jaise) **dheere-dheere** badalte hain.

### Example 1. Alkane ke mass ka farak

**Sawaal:** C₃H₈, C₄H₁₀ aur C₅H₁₂ ka molar mass nikaalo aur dikhao ki agle member mein farak CH₂ ka mass hai.

**Kadam 1:** C₃H₈ = 3 × 12 + 8 × 1 = 36 + 8 = **44**.
**Kadam 2:** C₄H₁₀ = 4 × 12 + 10 = 48 + 10 = **58**.
**Kadam 3:** C₅H₁₂ = 5 × 12 + 12 = 60 + 12 = **72**.
**Kadam 4:** farak: 58 − 44 = **14**, 72 − 58 = **14**.

**Jaanch:** CH₂ = 12 + 2 = 14 ✅. Teeno farak CH₂ ke mass ke barabar hain ✅.

**Karke dekho (sirf kaagaz aur kainchi, bade ke saath):** kaagaz ki 4 chhoti patti kaato, har par "CH₂" likho, aur do patti par "H" likho. Un se H–CH₂–H, H–CH₂–CH₂–H, ... banao. Ek ek patti jodkar apne aap likho ki formula kaise badhta hai. Gino: n patti ke liye hamesha 2n + 2 H nikalega, kyunki har CH₂ mein 2 H aur sire ke 2 H alag.

## Part B: General formula: ek formula, saare members

Yogesh ne poochha: "Chacha, kya C₁₀ wale alkane ka formula ginte-ginte likhna padega?" "Nahi. Valency se ek formula nikaalte hain."

**Alkane (CₙH₂ₙ₊₂):** n carbon ek seedhi chain mein hon to un ke beech **n − 1** C–C bond hote hain. Har bond **2 valency** kharch karta hai (ek-ek dono carbon ki). Kul valency = 4n, kharch = 2(n − 1) = 2n − 2. Bachi hui valency hydrogen se bharti hai: 4n − (2n − 2) = **2n + 2**. Isliye alkane = **CₙH₂ₙ₊₂**.

**Alkene (CₙH₂ₙ):** ek **double bond** hai, yaani ek C–C ke beech do bond. Isse 2 valency aur kharch hui, to hydrogen 2 kam: (2n + 2) − 2 = **2n**. Alkene = **CₙH₂ₙ** (n ≥ 2).

**Alkyne (CₙH₂ₙ₋₂):** ek **triple bond**: 4 valency extra kharch, hydrogen 4 kam: (2n + 2) − 4 = **2n − 2** (n ≥ 2).

**Alcohol (CₙH₂ₙ₊₁OH):** alkane ka ek H hatao aur uski jagah –OH lagao. Alkane mein 2n + 2 H the, ek gaya to 2n + 1, aur OH alag: **CₙH₂ₙ₊₁OH**.

**Carboxylic acid (CₙH₂ₙ₊₁COOH):** alkane ke ek H ki jagah –COOH. Yahan acid mein n carbon "COOH" ke pehle aur ek carbon COOH ke andar, yaani kul n + 1 carbon. n = 0 se shuru karne par HCOOH milta hai.

| Series | General formula | Pehle member |
|---|---|---|
| Alkane | CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ | CH₄, C₂H₆, C₃H₈ |
| Alkene | CₙH₂ₙ | C₂H₄, C₃H₆, C₄H₈ |
| Alkyne | CₙH₂ₙ₋₂ | C₂H₂, C₃H₄, C₄H₆ |
| Alcohol | CₙH₂ₙ₊₁OH | CH₃OH, C₂H₅OH, C₃H₇OH |
| Carboxylic acid | CₙH₂ₙ₊₁COOH | HCOOH, CH₃COOH, C₂H₅COOH |

### Example 2. H ki ginti se carbon ki ginti

**Sawaal:** Ek alkane mein 12 hydrogen atom hain. Uska formula aur molar mass batao.

**Kadam 1:** 2n + 2 = 12, to 2n = 10, **n = 5**.
**Kadam 2:** formula **C₅H₁₂** (pentane).
**Kadam 3:** mass = 5 × 12 + 12 = **72**.

**Jaanch:** valency: 5 carbon × 4 = 20 hai; 4 C–C bond ne 8 valency li; bache 12 ne 12 hydrogen liye ✅.

### Example 3. Mass se series ka member

**Sawaal:** Ek alkene ka molar mass 56 hai. Uska formula nikaalo.

**Kadam 1:** CₙH₂ₙ ka mass = 12n + 2n = **14n**.
**Kadam 2:** 14n = 56, to **n = 4**.
**Kadam 3:** formula **C₄H₈** (butene).

**Jaanch:** 4 × 12 + 8 × 1 = 48 + 8 = 56 ✅.

### Example 4. Alcohol series ke mass

**Sawaal:** CH₃OH, C₂H₅OH aur C₃H₇OH ka molar mass nikaalo aur batao ki farak kitna hai. (O 16)

**Kadam 1:** CH₃OH = 12 + 3 + 16 + 1 = **32**.
**Kadam 2:** C₂H₅OH = 24 + 5 + 16 + 1 = **46**.
**Kadam 3:** C₃H₇OH = 36 + 7 + 16 + 1 = **60**.
**Kadam 4:** farak: 46 − 32 = 14, 60 − 46 = 14.

**Jaanch:** general formula se: CₙH₂ₙ₊₁OH ka mass = 14n + 18. n = 1, 2, 3 ke liye 32, 46, 60 ✅.

### Example 5. Acid series ke mass aur ek ajeeb sanyog

**Sawaal:** HCOOH, CH₃COOH aur C₂H₅COOH ka molar mass nikaalo.

**Kadam 1:** HCOOH = 12 + 1 + 32 + 1 = **46**.
**Kadam 2:** CH₃COOH = 24 + 4 + 32 = **60**.
**Kadam 3:** C₂H₅COOH = 36 + 6 + 32 = **74**.

**Jaanch:** farak 60 − 46 = 14, 74 − 60 = 14 ✅. Acid series mein bhi har kadam par CH₂.

**Ek dilchasp baat:** ethanol C₂H₅OH aur methanoic acid HCOOH dono ka mass **46** hai, aur propanol C₃H₇OH aur ethanoic acid CH₃COOH dono ka mass **60**. Phir bhi inke **formula** alag hain (C₂H₆O aur CH₂O₂) aur **functional group** alag, isliye ye alag series ke member hain. Mass barabar hone se series ek nahi ho jaati: series ka ghera **functional group aur general formula** banate hain.

## Part C: Boiling point: gaadi jitni lambi, utni der se rukti hai

Metro ki lambi gaadi ko rukne mein zyada doori lagti hai. Series mein bhi **jitni lambi chain, utna zyada boiling point**. Kam se kam yahi Yogesh ne notebook mein likha:

| Alkane | Formula | Boiling point (lagbhag, °C) | 25 °C par haalat |
|---|---|---|---|
| methane | CH₄ | −162 | gas |
| ethane | C₂H₆ | −89 | gas |
| propane | C₃H₈ | −42 | gas |
| butane | C₄H₁₀ | −1 | gas |
| pentane | C₅H₁₂ | 36 | liquid |
| hexane | C₆H₁₄ | 69 | liquid |

```
  methane    -162 C   #
  ethane      -89 C   ########
  propane     -42 C   #############
  butane       -1 C   #################
  pentane      36 C   #####################
  hexane       69 C   ########################
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Alkane ke boiling point ka bar chart. Har bar ki lambai = (boiling point + 170) ÷ 10, taaki sab bar positive aur saaf dikhen: methane 1, ethane 8, propane 13, butane 17, pentane 21, hexane 24 chinh. Bar dheere-dheere lambe hote hain: badlaav **gradual** hai, achanak koi chhalang nahi.

Kya dheere-dheere badla? Gino: −162 se −89: **+73**; −89 se −42: **+47**; −42 se −1: **+41**; −1 se 36: **+37**; 36 se 69: **+33**. Har kadam par boiling point **badha**, par badhne ki raftaar dheere-dheere kam hoti gayi. Kyun? Class 9 mein dekha tha ki covalent molecule ek-dusre ko bahut kamzor kheenchte hain ([chemical-bonding-part4.md](../../../class9/chemistry/chemical-bonding/chemical-bonding-part4.md)). Lambi chain wale bhaari molecule ke beech ye **aakarshan thoda zyada** hota hai, isliye unhe door karke gas banane ke liye zyada garmi chahiye. Is aakarshan ki poori kahani **aage ki class mein**.

**Alcohol series mein bhi yahi:** methanol 65 °C, ethanol 78 °C: agla member **13 °C zyada**. **Acid series:** ethanoic acid 118 °C. Alag series ki tulna: do carbon wale teen compound ethane (−89 °C), ethanol (78 °C) aur ethanoic acid (118 °C) alag series hone ki wajah se bahut alag hain: functional group bhi boiling point badalta hai.

### Example 6. Boiling point se haalat batana

**Sawaal:** Ek ghar mein kamre ka taapman 25 °C hai. Butane (−1 °C) aur pentane (36 °C) kis haalat mein honge? LPG cylinder mein propane aur butane **liquid** kyun rakhe jaate hain?

**Kadam 1:** butane ka boiling point −1 °C hai, jo 25 °C se **kam** hai: kamre mein wo **gas** banta hai.
**Kadam 2:** pentane ka boiling point 36 °C hai, jo 25 °C se **zyada**: kamre mein wo **liquid** rahta hai.
**Kadam 3:** propane (−42 °C) aur butane gas hain, par cylinder mein **dabaav (pressure)** badha kar unhe liquid bana ke rakha jaata hai taaki kam jagah mein bahut gas aa sake. Kholne par dabaav ghatta hai aur wo gas ban kar burner tak jaati hai.

**Jaanch:** 25 > −1 ✅ gas, 25 < 36 ✅ liquid. Series ka niyam: C₄ gas aur C₅ liquid, ek hi coach ka fark, par haalat badal gayi.

## Part D: Functional group: wo "tag" jo swabhaav tay karta hai

Yogesh: "Chacha, ek hi series ke sabhi members ek jaisi chaal kyun chalte hain?" "Kyunki unme ek **vishesh hissa** hota hai, jo alag-alag nahi badalta. Use kehte hain **functional group**." Alkane ke kisi bhi H ko hatao aur uski jagah koi group lagao: ek **naya parivaar** ban jaata hai.

```
      H   H                   H   H
      I   I                   I   I
  H - C - C - H    ---->  H - C - C - O - H
      I   I                   I   I
      H   H                   H   H

     ethane                 ethanol
```

> **Figure 2.4** – Ethane (C₂H₆) ka ek H hata kar uski jagah –OH lagane se ethanol (C₂H₅OH) ban gaya. Ginti: ethane ke har C par 4 bond, har H par 1. Ethanol mein O ke do bond (ek C se, ek H se), dono C ke chaar-chaar bond, 5 hydrogen C se aur 1 hydrogen O se. Naya H sirf OH ka hai, isliye C₂H₆ se C₂H₆O bana.

Isi tarah methane CH₄ ke ek H ko –OH se badlo to CH₃OH (methanol), ethane ke ek H ko –OH se badlo to C₂H₅OH (ethanol), propane C₃H₈ se C₃H₇OH. Teeno ek hi series (alcohol) ke member hain kyunki teeno mein **–OH** hai.

| Functional group | Parivaar ka naam | Ek example (kewal naam aur formula) |
|---|---|---|
| –OH | alcohol | ethanol, C₂H₅OH |
| –CHO | aldehyde | ethanal, CH₃CHO |
| >C=O | ketone | propanone, CH₃COCH₃ |
| –COOH | carboxylic acid | ethanoic acid, CH₃COOH |
| –Cl, –Br | halogen compound (haloalkane) | chloromethane CH₃Cl, bromoethane C₂H₅Br |

Aldehyde aur ketone ke naam aur structure ka poora tareeka **Topic 3** mein aayega; yahan sirf group pehchanna seekhna hai. Alkene ka **double bond** (C=C) aur alkyne ka **triple bond** (C≡C) bhi apne parivaar ke functional "chinh" ki tarah kaam karte hain.

```
      H       H
       \     /
        C = C                     H - C ≡ C - H
       /     \
      H       H

     ethene C₂H₄                 ethyne C₂H₂
```

> **Figure 2.5** – Ethene (double bond) aur ethyne (triple bond). Ethene: har C par 2 H + double bond (2) = 4. Ethyne: har C par 1 H + triple bond (3) = 4. Har H ka ek bond.

```
      H   O
      I   II
  H - C - C - O - H
      I
      H

    ethanoic acid CH₃COOH
```

> **Figure 2.6** – Ethanoic acid. Baayein C par 3 H + 1 C–C = 4 bond; daayein C par ek C–C (1) + double bond O (2) + –O–H wala ek bond (1) = 4. Upar wale O ke do bond (double), niche wale O ke do bond (C aur H se). –COOH group yahin dikhta hai.

### Example 7. Group pehchano

**Sawaal:** In compounds mein functional group aur parivaar batao: (a) CH₃OH, (b) CH₃COOH, (c) C₂H₅Br, (d) CH₃CHO, (e) CH₃COCH₃.

**Kadam 1:** (a) group –OH, parivaar **alcohol**.
**Kadam 2:** (b) group –COOH, parivaar **carboxylic acid**.
**Kadam 3:** (c) group –Br, parivaar **halogen compound** (bromoethane).
**Kadam 4:** (d) group –CHO (aakhri carbon par), parivaar **aldehyde**.
**Kadam 5:** (e) group >C=O (beech ka carbon), parivaar **ketone**.

**Jaanch:** formula se hi group nikalta hai: (a) mein O–H alag dikhta hai, (b) mein COOH, (d) mein CHO, (e) mein CO beech mein ✅.

## Part E: Saturated aur unsaturated

**Saturated** matlab "bhara hua": carbon ki saari valency **hydrogen ya doosre atoms** se bhari hui hai, aur carbon-carbon ke beech sirf **single bond** hain. Ethane aur ek alkane ki poori series saturated hai. **Unsaturated** matlab "abhi aur le sakta hai": ek **double** ya **triple** bond hai, jo khul kar naye atoms le sakta hai (Topic 5 mein dekhenge).

Ginti se pehchan: alkane mein H ki ginti **sabse zyada** (2n + 2). Agar ek seedhi chain wale hydrocarbon mein H kam hain, to wo unsaturated hai.

### Example 8. Saturated ya unsaturated

**Sawaal:** C₄H₁₀, C₄H₈ aur C₄H₆ mein kaun saturated hai? (Seedhi chain maan lo.)

**Kadam 1:** n = 4 ke liye alkane mein H = 2 × 4 + 2 = **10**. C₄H₁₀ mein 10 hai, to **saturated** (butane).
**Kadam 2:** C₄H₈: 8 = 10 − 2, ek double bond, **unsaturated** (butene).
**Kadam 3:** C₄H₆: 6 = 10 − 4, ek triple bond, **unsaturated** (butyne).

**Jaanch:** 10 − 8 = 2 ✅ (ek double bond), 10 − 6 = 4 ✅ (ek triple bond).

## Part F: Teen tarah ke formula aur agla member kaise likhein

Ek hi compound ko teen tarah se likha ja sakta hai. Ethanol ke liye:

- **Molecular formula:** C₂H₆O (sirf ginti).
- **Condensed formula:** CH₃CH₂OH ya C₂H₅OH (group aur chain ek line mein).
- **Structural formula:** Figure 2.4 jaisa, har bond dikha kar.

Condensed likhne ka niyam: har carbon ke saath uske H likho (CH₃, CH₂), phir agla carbon, aur aakhir mein group (OH). Ek hi molecular formula ke **kabhi-kabhi ek se zyada** structure bhi ho sakte hain: iski kahani **Topic 3** mein.

**Agla member likhne ke do tareeke:**
1. **CH₂ jodo:** formula mein ek C aur do H badhao.
2. **General formula mein n + 1 rakho.**

### Example 9. Agla member

**Sawaal:** Propene ke baad kaun sa alkene aata hai? Propyne ke baad kaun sa alkyne?

**Kadam 1:** propene C₃H₆ + CH₂ = **C₄H₈** (butene).
**Kadam 2:** propyne C₃H₄ + CH₂ = **C₄H₆** (butyne).

**Jaanch:** alkene n = 4: 2n = 8 ✅. Alkyne n = 4: 2n − 2 = 6 ✅. Mass ka farak: C₄H₈ = 56, C₃H₆ = 42, farak 14 ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti: "Ek hi series ke members ke physical gun (bhautik gun) bhi ek jaise hote hain."**

Soch kar dekhte hain. Yogesh ne apni copy mein alkane likhe aur socha ki sab ek jaise hain, kyunki sab "alkane" hain. Par table dekho:

- **Butane (C₄H₁₀)** kamre ke taapman par **gas** hai (boiling point −1 °C), **pentane (C₅H₁₂)** **liquid** hai (36 °C), aur **hexane (C₆H₁₄)** bhi liquid, par uska boiling point 69 °C hai. Ek-ek coach ke farak par haalat badal gayi aur boiling point har kadam par badha. Agar sab ke bhautik gun ek jaise hote, to cylinder ki gas aur ek liquid ek hi tarah behave karti.
- **Methane** gas ek khuli jagah ki taraf udta hai, jabki **liquid pentane** kisi bartan se ulat jaaye to farsh par faila rehta hai: dono ke saath alag sawdhaani.

Par **rasayanik swabhaav ek jaisa rehta hai.** Teeno alkane jalte hain aur CO₂ aur H₂O banate hain:

CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O (C 1 = 1, H 4 = 2 × 2, O 4 = 2 + 2 ✅)
C₃H₈ + 5O₂ → 3CO₂ + 4H₂O (C 3 = 3, H 8 = 4 × 2, O 10 = 6 + 4 ✅)

Isi tarah alcohol series ka ethanol bhi jalta hai: C₂H₅OH + 3O₂ → 2CO₂ + 3H₂O (C 2 = 2, H 6 = 3 × 2, O 1 + 6 = 7 = 4 + 3 ✅). Ye combustion Topic 4 mein poora aayega, abhi sirf ye dekhne ke liye ki **gun do tarah ke hain**: **chemical gun ek jaise** (functional group ki wajah se), **physical gun dheere-dheere badalte hain** (chain ki lambai ki wajah se). Ek line mein: *Series mein chemical gun ek jaise, physical gun badalte hue.*

## Quick Recap

- **Homologous series:** carbon compounds ka parivaar jisme ek functional group, ek general formula, aur agla member ek **CH₂ (mass 14)** zyada.
- **General formulas:** alkane CₙH₂ₙ₊₂, alkene CₙH₂ₙ, alkyne CₙH₂ₙ₋₂, alcohol CₙH₂ₙ₊₁OH, carboxylic acid CₙH₂ₙ₊₁COOH.
- **Boiling point** series mein **dheere-dheere badhta** hai (alkane: −162, −89, −42, −1, 36, 69 °C; alcohol: 65 aur 78 °C).
- **Functional group:** –OH (alcohol), –CHO (aldehyde), >C=O (ketone), –COOH (carboxylic acid), –Cl/–Br (halogen compound).
- **Saturated:** sirf single bond; **unsaturated:** double ya triple bond.
- **Agla member:** ek CH₂ jodo (ek C aur do H).
- **Galti se bacho:** series mein chemical gun ek jaise, physical gun badalte hain.
- **Suraksha:** methanol zehreela, hexane tak ke alkane aur ethanol jwalanshil.

## Practice Questions

(Sab sawaal kaagaz par hal karne ke liye hain. Mass: H 1, C 12, O 16.)

1. Write the formulas and molar masses of the alkanes with 6 and 10 carbon atoms. By how many CH₂ units do they differ, and what is the difference in their molar masses?
2. An alkane has 18 hydrogen atoms in its molecule. Find the number of carbon atoms, the formula and the molar mass.
3. An alkane has a molar mass of 128. Find n and its formula.
4. The alkane series has members C₇H₁₆ and C₉H₂₀. Write the member between them and show that each step increases the molar mass by 14.
5. Write the formula of the alcohol with 5 carbon atoms and the formula of the next member. Find both molar masses.
6. A member of the alcohol series CₙH₂ₙ₊₁OH has molar mass 130. Find n and write the formula.
7. A member of the acid series CₙH₂ₙ₊₁COOH has molar mass 116. Find n, write the formula and give the total number of carbon atoms in the molecule.
8. Which of C₆H₁₄, C₇H₁₆, C₈H₁₆, C₉H₂₀, C₁₀H₂₂ do not belong to the alkane series? To which general formula does the odd one fit?
9. Name the functional group (with its symbol) in each, or say "none": (a) C₆H₁₃OH, (b) C₄H₉COOH, (c) C₉H₂₀.
10. Hexane boils at about 69 °C. Without looking up any data, say whether octane (C₈H₁₈) is likely to boil above or below 69 °C, whether it is a gas or a liquid at 25 °C, and how decane (C₁₀H₂₂) compares with octane.
11. Two alkanes of the same series have molar masses 86 and 114. Find their formulas, and the formula and mass of the member between them.
12. Find the percentage of carbon (by mass) in C₆H₁₄ and in C₁₀H₂₂ (give to one decimal place). Which is higher? What value does the percentage approach for a very long chain? What does this tell you about physical properties across a series?
13. State true or false and correct the false ones: (a) C₃H₇COOH and C₅H₁₁COOH belong to the same series and differ in molar mass by 28. (b) C₆H₁₄ and C₆H₁₃OH are members of the same series because both have 6 carbons. (c) Every member of the alcohol series has the same boiling point. (d) C₉H₂₀ is a saturated hydrocarbon.
14. Draw the structural formula of the straight-chain alcohol C₄H₉OH with the –OH on an end carbon. Count the valency of every C, H and O.
15. A cleaning-product bottle in a store room has a faded label that says only "methanol". A student says it "looks like water and smells like the sanitiser, so it must be safe to taste". Explain why the student is wrong and what he should do.

### Answers

1. C₆H₁₄ (n = 6, H = 2 × 6 + 2 = 14): 6 × 12 + 14 = 72 + 14 = **86**. C₁₀H₂₂ (n = 10, H = 22): 120 + 22 = **142**. Farak: 10 − 6 = **4 CH₂** unit; mass ka farak 142 − 86 = **56** = 4 × 14. Jaanch: 4 × 14 = 56 ✅.
2. 2n + 2 = 18, to n = **8**. Formula **C₈H₁₈**. Mass = 8 × 12 + 18 = 96 + 18 = **114**. Jaanch: 14n + 2 = 112 + 2 = 114 ✅.
3. Alkane ka mass = 12n + 2n + 2 = 14n + 2. 14n + 2 = 128, to 14n = 126, **n = 9**. Formula **C₉H₂₀**. Jaanch: 9 × 12 + 20 = 108 + 20 = 128 ✅.
4. C₇H₁₆ aur C₉H₂₀ ke beech ek CH₂ wala member **C₈H₁₈**. Masses: C₇H₁₆ = 84 + 16 = **100**, C₈H₁₈ = 96 + 18 = **114**, C₉H₂₀ = 108 + 20 = **128**. Farak: 114 − 100 = 14 ✅, 128 − 114 = 14 ✅.
5. n = 5: C₅H₁₁OH. Mass = 60 + 11 + 16 + 1 = **88**. Agla member (n = 6): **C₆H₁₃OH**, mass = 72 + 13 + 17 = **102**. Farak 102 − 88 = 14 ✅.
6. Alcohol ka mass = 12n + (2n + 1) + 16 + 1 = 14n + 18. 14n + 18 = 130, 14n = 112, **n = 8**. Formula **C₈H₁₇OH**. Jaanch: 96 + 17 + 17 = 130 ✅.
7. Acid ka mass = 12(n + 1) + (2n + 1) + 32 + 1 = 14n + 46. 14n + 46 = 116, 14n = 70, **n = 5**. Formula **C₅H₁₁COOH**. Kul carbon = n + 1 = **6** (C₆H₁₂O₂). Jaanch: 72 + 12 + 32 = 116 ✅.
8. Alkane ke liye H = 2n + 2: C₆ → 14 ✅, C₇ → 16 ✅, C₈ → **18** (diya hai 16), C₉ → 20 ✅, C₁₀ → 22 ✅. To **C₈H₁₆** alkane series mein nahi. Wo CₙH₂ₙ (n = 8) ke roop mein baithta hai, yaani alkene ka general formula.
9. (a) C₆H₁₃OH: **–OH**, alcohol. (b) C₄H₉COOH: **–COOH**, carboxylic acid. (c) C₉H₂₀: **none**, ye sirf single bond wala saturated hydrocarbon hai (H = 2 × 9 + 2 = 20 ✅).
10. Series mein chain lambi to boiling point zyada. Octane (C₈) hexane (C₆) se do coach lamba hai, to uska boiling point **69 °C se zyada** hoga. Kamre ke taapman (25 °C) par wo **liquid** hoga, kyunki uska boiling point 25 °C se bahut upar hoga. Decane octane se do coach aur lamba hai, to uska boiling point octane se bhi **zyada** hoga.
11. Alkane ka mass 14n + 2. 86 = 14n + 2, n = **6**: **C₆H₁₄**. 114 = 14n + 2, n = **8**: **C₈H₁₈**. Beech ka member n = 7: **C₇H₁₆**, mass = 84 + 16 = **100**. Farak 100 − 86 = 14 ✅, 114 − 100 = 14 ✅.
12. C₆H₁₄ mein carbon = 72 ÷ 86 × 100 = **83.7 percent**. C₁₀H₂₂ mein carbon = 120 ÷ 142 × 100 = **84.5 percent**. **C₁₀H₂₂ mein zyada.** Percentage = 12n ÷ (14n + 2) × 100. Bahut lambi chain mein "+ 2" bahut chhota ho jaata hai, to ye 12 ÷ 14 × 100 = **85.7 percent** ke paas pahunchta hai. Matlab: series mein **composition bhi dheere-dheere badalti hai**, isliye physical gun bhi dheere-dheere badalte hain; saare ek jaise nahi.
13. (a) **True** ✅: n = 3 aur n = 5, do CH₂ ka farak, 2 × 14 = 28 (88 aur 116: farak 28 ✅). (b) **False** ❌: dono mein 6 carbon hain par functional group alag (C₆H₁₄ saturated hydrocarbon, C₆H₁₃OH alcohol), general formula alag; sirf carbon ki ginti series nahi tay karti. (c) **False** ❌: boiling point chain ke saath badhta hai (jaise methanol 65 °C, ethanol 78 °C). (d) **True** ✅: 2 × 9 + 2 = 20 H, sirf single bond.
14. Structure:

```
      H   H   H   H
      I   I   I   I
  H - C - C - C - C - O - H
      I   I   I   I
      H   H   H   H
```

> **Figure 2.7** – Seedhi chain wala C₄H₉OH. Har C ke chaar bond: sire ke baayein C par 3 H + 1 C = 4; beech ke do C par 2 H + 2 C = 4; daayein C par 2 H + 1 C + 1 O = 4. O ke do bond (C aur H se), har H ka ek bond. H ki ginti: 8 (carbon par) + 1 (O par) = **9** ✅ = 2 × 4 + 1.

15. Student **galat** hai. Methanol **zehreela** hai: thoda sa bhi pee lene se andhapan ya maut ho sakti hai. Dekhne mein wo ethanol ya paani jaisa **bina rang ka** hota hai, isliye rang ya haath se pehchanna **sambhav nahi**. Gandh se pehchanne ki koshish bhi khatarnak hai. Student ko bottle **kholna, sungna, chakhna nahi** chahiye; use wahin rakh kar kisi **bade ya teacher** ko batana chahiye jo bottle ko surakshit tareeke se sambhal sakein. Methanol jwalanshil bhi hai, isliye paas mein koi lau ya chingaari nahi honi chahiye.
