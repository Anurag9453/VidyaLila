# Topic 3: Naam Aur Isomer: IUPAC Ka Pehla Kadam

Topic 2 mein hamne carbon ke compounds ko parivaaron (series) mein baanta, aur functional group se unki pehchaan seekhi. Wahan ethanal, propanone, chloromethane, bromoethane jaise naam aaye the, par ye naam **bane kaise**, ye nahi dekha tha. Aur Topic 1 mein ek ajeeb baat bhi dikhi thi: C₄H₁₀ ki **do alag shakal**. Aaj hum dono sawaal sulajhate hain: compound ko **sahi naam** kaise dein (taaki duniya bhar mein ek hi compound ka ek hi naam ho), aur ek hi formula ke **kai compound** kaise ban jaate hain.

## TEA, EAT, ATE

Mr. Rokade apni bhanji Apoorva ke saath Sunday ki shaam chai peete hue ek khel khel rahe the. Apoorva ne kaagaz par teen shabd likhe: **TEA, EAT, ATE**. "Mama, dekho! Teeno mein wahi teen akshar hain: T, E, A. Par teeno ka matlab bilkul alag: chai, khana, aur 'kha liya'! Isko anagram kehte hain." Mr. Rokade ne taali bajayi. "Bahut khoob. Aur batao, kya koi bhi teen akshar liye aur jo marzi shabd ban gaya?" "Nahi Mama, akshar wahi, par **kram** badalna padta hai, aur har kram ka matlab alag."

Thodi der baad Apoorva apni chemistry ki copy khol kar baith gayi. Usne do chitra banaye the, dono ke neeche wahi likha: **C₄H₁₀**. "Mama, ye kya ho gaya? Teacher ne kaha ki ye do alag gas hain, par formula to ek hi hai. 4 carbon, 10 hydrogen, dono mein!" Mr. Rokade muskuraye. "Wahi anagram wali baat, Apoorva. **Akshar (atom) wahi, par jodne ka kram (structure) alag**, to compound alag. TEA aur EAT ki tarah."

Phir baahar gali mein dekhte hue unhone ek aur sawaal pooch liya. Dudh wale bhaiya ek line mein 6 ghar ginte ja rahe the: "Meri dukaan is gali ke 5 number ghar mein hai". Mr. Rokade bole, "Par agar gali ke doosre sire se ginti shuru karo to wahi ghar 2 number ka hoga! Postman kaunsa number likhega?" Apoorva ne socha, "Jo chhota ho, Mama: 2." "Ye 'chhota number' wala niyam hi chemistry ke naam mein bhi kaam aata hai. Chalo dekhte hain kaise."

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **IUPAC naam** | Duniya bhar ke chemists ka tay kiya hua niyam: har compound ka ek hi, saaf naam | CH₃CH₂OH ka naam ethanol |
| **Root (mool naam)** | Naam ka pehla hissa: sabse lambi chain mein kitne carbon hain | meth-, eth-, prop-, but- |
| **Suffix (anth)** | Naam ka aakhri hissa: compound kis parivaar ka hai | -ane, -ene, -yne, -ol |
| **Prefix (agla hissa)** | Naam ke shuru mein lagne wala hissa: shakha ya halogen ka naam aur uski jagah | 2-methyl, bromo |
| **Longest chain** | Carbon ki sabse lambi lagataar chain (mod ke saath bhi ginti hoti hai) | CH₃–CH₂–CH₂–CH₃ mein 4 |
| **Branch (shakha)** | Mukhya chain se nikla hua chhota hissa | –CH₃ |
| **Methyl** | –CH₃ wali shakha: methane ka ek H hata kar | 2-methylpropane |
| **Isomer** | Wo compound jinka **molecular formula ek hi**, par **structure alag** hai | butane aur 2-methylpropane |
| **Chain isomer** | Isomer jinme carbon ki chain ka dhancha (seedhi ya shakha wali) alag hai | C₄H₁₀ ke do compound |
| **Position isomer** | Isomer jinme chain wahi, par double bond ya functional group ki **jagah** alag hai | but-1-ene aur but-2-ene |
| **Functional isomer** | Isomer jinme **functional group hi alag** hai | ethanol aur methoxymethane |

## Part A: Naam ke teen hisse: kahan, kitna aur kaun sa

IUPAC naam ek chhote vakya jaisa hai, jisme teen sawaalon ke jawab hote hain. Figure 3.1 ko dekho.

```
      2   -   methyl   +   but   +   ane
      I          I          I         I
   kahan?    kaun si     kitne     kaun sa
  (number)   shakha?   carbon?    parivaar?
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Naam "2-methylbutane" ke chaar tukde: number (shakha kis carbon par), shakha ka naam (methyl), root (but- yaani 4 carbon ki lambi chain) aur suffix (-ane yaani alkane). Naam sidha ek shabd mein likha jaata hai: 2-methylbutane.

### Root: carbon ginne wala hissa

| Carbon ki ginti | Root | Alkane ka naam | Formula (CₙH₂ₙ₊₂) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | meth- | methane | CH₄ |
| 2 | eth- | ethane | C₂H₆ |
| 3 | prop- | propane | C₃H₈ |
| 4 | but- | butane | C₄H₁₀ |
| 5 | pent- | pentane | C₅H₁₂ |
| 6 | hex- | hexane | C₆H₁₄ |
| 7 | hept- | heptane | C₇H₁₆ |
| 8 | oct- | octane | C₈H₁₈ |
| 9 | non- | nonane | C₉H₂₀ |
| 10 | dec- | decane | C₁₀H₂₂ |

Pehle chaar (meth, eth, prop, but) bas yaad karne padte hain. Baaki ke liye roz ki cheezein madad karti hain: **pent**agon (5 kone), **hex**agon (6), **hept**athlon (7 khel), **oct**agon (8), **non**agon (9), **dec**ade (10 saal).

### Suffix aur prefix: parivaar ka naam

| Parivaar | Kya lagta hai | Naam ka tareeka | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alkane | -ane | root + ane | ethane C₂H₆ |
| Alkene (double bond) | -ene | root + ene | ethene C₂H₄ |
| Alkyne (triple bond) | -yne | root + yne | ethyne C₂H₂ |
| Alcohol (–OH) | -ol | root + ol | ethanol C₂H₅OH |
| Aldehyde (–CHO) | -al | root + al | methanal HCHO, ethanal CH₃CHO |
| Ketone (>C=O) | -one | root + one | propanone CH₃COCH₃ |
| Carboxylic acid (–COOH) | -oic acid | root + oic acid | methanoic acid HCOOH, ethanoic acid CH₃COOH |
| Haloalkane (–Cl, –Br) | chloro-, bromo- (prefix) | prefix + alkane | chloromethane CH₃Cl, bromoethane C₂H₅Br |

Do chhote niyam yaad rakho:

1. **Alkane ka aakhri "e" gir jaata hai** jab suffix vowel (a, o) se shuru ho: ethane + ol = **ethanol** (ethaneol nahi), ethane + al = **ethanal**, propane + one = **propanone**, ethane + oic acid = **ethanoic acid**.
2. **–CHO aur –COOH ka carbon bhi chain ki ginti mein aata hai.** CH₃COOH mein 2 carbon hain (ek CH₃ ka, ek COOH ka), isliye eth-: ethanoic acid, ethane se aaya, "methanoic" nahi.

### Example 1. Naam se formula

**Sawaal:** In naamon se formula likho: (a) nonane, (b) propanoic acid, (c) bromoethane, (d) ethanal.

**Kadam 1:** (a) non- = 9 carbon, alkane: H = 2 × 9 + 2 = 20: **C₉H₂₀**.
**Kadam 2:** (b) prop- = 3 carbon (COOH ka carbon mila kar), –COOH: **C₂H₅–COOH**, yaani C₃H₆O₂.
**Kadam 3:** (c) eth- = 2 carbon, alkane ka ek H hat kar Br: **C₂H₅Br**.
**Kadam 4:** (d) eth- = 2 carbon, –CHO: **CH₃CHO**, yaani C₂H₄O.

**Jaanch:** (b) C₂H₅ mein 2 C, 5 H, COOH mein 1 C, 2 O, 1 H: C = 3, H = 6, O = 2 ✅. (c) ethane C₂H₆ mein 6 H, ek H gaya: 5 H + Br ✅. (d) CH₃ (1 C, 3 H) + CHO (1 C, 1 H, 1 O): C = 2, H = 4 ✅.

## Part B: Branch wali alkane ka naam

Topic 1 mein dekha tha ki carbon seedhi chain ke alawa **shakha wali chain** bhi banata hai. Aise compound ka naam teen kadam mein nikalta hai. Gali wala niyam yaad rakhna: **number us sire se jo chhota number de.**

1. **Sabse lambi chain dhundo.** Ungli se carbon par chalo, kisi bhi mod par mud sakte ho; jo sabse zyada carbon ki lagataar chain mile, wahi root hai.
2. **Number do.** Chain ko ek sire se 1, 2, 3 ... ginti karo; jis sire se **shakha ko chhota number** mile, wahi sahi sira.
3. **Likho:** number, dash, shakha ka naam, aur phir root: **2-methylbutane**. Alkane ka naam "-ane" hata kar "-yl" lagane se shakha ka naam banta hai: methane → **methyl** (–CH₃). Agar ek hi shakha do baar ho to **di-** (do), aur numbers comma se: **2,2-dimethylpropane**.

### Example 2. 2-methylpropane

**Sawaal:** Figure 3.2 ke structure ka naam batao.

```
              H
              I
          H - C - H
              I
      H       I       H
      I       I       I
  H - C ----- C ----- C - H
      I       I       I
      H       H       H

      1       2       3
```

> **Figure 3.2** – C₄H₁₀ ka shakha wala roop. Beech wale carbon ke teen carbon aur ek H se bond (4); baaki teeno carbon ke 3 H aur 1 C se bond (4). Kul 4 C aur 10 H (3 + 3 + 3 + 1). Neeche chain ke numbers: 1, 2, 3.

**Kadam 1 (lambi chain):** upar wale carbon se chalo: upar ka C, beech ka C, phir kisi bhi sire ka C: **3 carbon**. Chaar ki chain kisi raaste nahi milti. To root **prop-**, propane.
**Kadam 2 (shakha):** upar wala –CH₃ ek **methyl** shakha hai, beech wale carbon (**C2**) se judi hui. Dono sire se ginne par beech wala 2 hi hai.
**Kadam 3 (naam):** **2-methylpropane**.

**Jaanch:** carbon = 3 (chain) + 1 (methyl) = 4, hydrogen = 10, yaani C₄H₁₀ ✅. Aur "1-methylpropane" kyun nahi? Agar methyl sire ke carbon par laga hota to chain 4 ki ban jaati: wo butane hota. **Isliye "1-methyl" naam kabhi sahi nahi hota**: ye likhna pad jaaye to lambi chain galat chuni hai.

### Example 3. 2-methylbutane: number kis taraf se?

**Sawaal:** Figure 3.3 ka naam batao. Number baayen se ya daayen se dein?

```
              H
              I
          H - C - H
              I
      H       I       H       H
      I       I       I       I
  H - C ----- C ----- C ----- C - H
      I       I       I       I
      H       H       H       H

      1       2       3       4
      4       3       2       1
```

> **Figure 3.3** – C₅H₁₂ ka ek compound. Neeche do numbering: pehli baayen se (1, 2, 3, 4), doosri daayen se (4, 3, 2, 1). Beech ke baayen wale carbon ke chaar bond: teen carbon + ek H; baaki sab ke 3 ya 2 H aur chain ke bond milakar 4. Kul 5 C aur 12 H (3 + 3 + 1 + 2 + 3).

**Kadam 1 (lambi chain):** chain mein 4 carbon (baayen se daayen tak): **but-ane**.
**Kadam 2 (shakha):** ek methyl.
**Kadam 3 (numbering):** baayen se ginne par methyl **C2** par hai; daayen se ginne par **C3** par. Dukaan wali gali ki tarah **chhota number**: 2.
**Kadam 4 (naam):** **2-methylbutane**.

**Jaanch:** 4 + 1 = 5 carbon, H = 3 + 3 + 1 + 2 + 3 = 12 = 2 × 5 + 2 ✅. "3-methylbutane" galat hai kyunki 3 > 2.

### Example 4. Naam se structure: 2,2-dimethylpropane

**Sawaal:** 2,2-dimethylpropane ka structure banao.

**Kadam 1:** "propane": 3 carbon ki chain, C1–C2–C3.
**Kadam 2:** "2,2-dimethyl": **do methyl, dono C2 par**.
**Kadam 3:** carbon ke chaar bond poore karo. C2 ke bond: do chain ke carbon + do methyl = 4, isliye C2 par koi H nahi. Baaki chaaron carbon (C1, C3 aur do methyl) har ek par 3 H.
**Kadam 4:** gino: 5 carbon, 4 × 3 = 12 H.

```
              H
              I
          H - C - H
              I
      H       I       H
      I       I       I
  H - C ----- C ----- C - H
      I       I       I
      H       I       H
              I
          H - C - H
              I
              H
```

> **Figure 3.4** – 2,2-dimethylpropane (C₅H₁₂). Beech ke carbon ke chaar bond chaar alag carbon se, kisi H se nahi. Chaaron CH₃ ke 3 H aur 1 C se bond (4). Kul 5 C aur 12 H.

**Jaanch:** formula C₅H₁₂ ✅ (2 × 5 + 2 = 12), aur beech ke C par chaaron bond carbon se: 4 ✅.

### Example 5. Halogen bhi shakha ki tarah

**Sawaal:** CH₃–CHBr–CH₃ aur CH₃–CH₂–CH₂Br ke naam batao. (Ek H ki jagah Br hai.)

**Kadam 1:** dono mein 3 carbon ki chain: root **prop-**, parivaar alkane, aur Br ek shakha jaisa **bromo-** prefix.
**Kadam 2:** pehle mein Br beech wale carbon par hai: dono sire se ginne par 2: **2-bromopropane**.
**Kadam 3:** doosre mein Br ek sire par hai: us sire se ginne par 1, dusre sire se 3: chhota number 1: **1-bromopropane**.

**Jaanch:** dono ka formula C₃H₇Br (pehle mein H = 3 + 1 + 3 = 7, doosre mein 3 + 2 + 2 = 7) ✅. Dono ke naam alag, kyunki Br ki **jagah** alag hai.

## Part C: Isomer: wahi akshar, alag shabd

TEA aur EAT ki tarah, **ek hi molecular formula** ke kai compound ho sakte hain: ye **isomer** hain. Compound ko **chain ki banawat** ya **bond ki jagah** ya **functional group** badal kar alag banaya ja sakta hai. Sabse pehle chain wale (chain isomer).

### Butane ke do isomer

Figure 3.5 mein seedhi chain wala butane hai. Uske bagal mein Figure 3.2 ka 2-methylpropane rakho: dono ka formula C₄H₁₀.

```
      H   H   H   H
      I   I   I   I
  H - C - C - C - C - H
      I   I   I   I
      H   H   H   H
```

> **Figure 3.5** – Butane (C₄H₁₀), seedhi chain. Beech ke do carbon ke 2 H aur 2 C se bond, kinare ke carbon ke 3 H aur 1 C se bond: har C par 4. 4 C aur 3 + 2 + 2 + 3 = 10 H.

**Fark kahan hai?** Butane mein **koi bhi carbon teen carbon se nahi judta**; 2-methylpropane mein beech wala carbon **teen carbon** se judta hai. Atoms wahi (4 C, 10 H), molar mass wahi (4 × 12 + 10 = 58), par jodne ka kram alag: ye **chain isomer** hain.

### Example 6. C₅H₁₂ ke saare isomer dhundna (kram se)

Yunhi andaaze se chitra banane par koi isomer chhoot sakta hai ya ek hi do baar gin liya ja sakta hai. Isliye **sabse lambi chain se shuru karke ek-ek carbon ghatate** jao.

**Sawaal:** C₅H₁₂ ke kitne isomer hote hain? Sab ke naam aur structure batao.

**Kadam 1 (5 carbon ki chain):** saare 5 carbon ek line mein: **pentane**. Koi shakha nahi (Figure 3.6).
**Kadam 2 (4 carbon ki chain):** ek carbon shakha ban gaya (methyl). Methyl sire par lagaya to chain phir 5 ki ho jaati hai, isliye sirf **C2** par lagega. C3 par lagana C2 par lagane jaisa hi hai (doosre sire se ginti). Ye **2-methylbutane** (Figure 3.3).
**Kadam 3 (3 carbon ki chain):** do carbon shakha bane (do methyl). Dono beech wale C2 par hi lag sakte hain (sire par lagne se chain lambi ho jaati). Ye **2,2-dimethylpropane** (Figure 3.4).
**Kadam 4 (2 carbon ki chain):** ethane ke dono carbon sire hain, koi beech ka carbon hai hi nahi: shakha lagne se chain lambi ho jaati. **Koi naya isomer nahi.**

```
      H   H   H   H   H
      I   I   I   I   I
  H - C - C - C - C - C - H
      I   I   I   I   I
      H   H   H   H   H
```

> **Figure 3.6** – Pentane (C₅H₁₂), seedhi chain. Beech ke teen C ke 2 H aur 2 C se bond (4), kinare ke do C ke 3 H aur 1 C se bond (4). 5 C aur 3 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 3 = 12 H.

| Sabse lambi chain | Shakha | Naam | Condensed formula |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | koi nahi | pentane | CH₃–CH₂–CH₂–CH₂–CH₃ |
| 4 | methyl, C2 par | 2-methylbutane | CH₃–CH(CH₃)–CH₂–CH₃ |
| 3 | do methyl, dono C2 par | 2,2-dimethylpropane | CH₃–C(CH₃)₂–CH₃ |

**Jaanch:** teeno ka formula C₅H₁₂ (5 C; H = 12) aur molar mass 5 × 12 + 12 = **72** ✅. Kul **teen** isomer. (Ginti tez badhti hai: C₄H₁₀ ke 2, C₅H₁₂ ke 3, aur C₁₀H₂₂ ke 75.)

## Part D: Double bond aur –OH ki jagah: position isomer

Ab compound mein sirf alkane nahi, double bond ya –OH bhi ho sakta hai. Niyam wahi: **chain wo lo jisme double bond (ya –OH) aaye**, aur **number wo sira jo double bond (ya –OH) ko chhota number de.** Number naam ke andar suffix se pehle likhte hain: **but-1-ene**.

### Example 7. Butene ke do isomer

**Sawaal:** C₄H₈ ki seedhi chain mein double bond do jagah ho sakta hai. Dono ke naam batao.

```
      H   H   H   H
      I   I   I   I
      C = C - C - C - H
      I       I   I
      H       H   H

      H   H   H   H
      I   I   I   I
  H - C - C = C - C - H
      I           I
      H           H
```

> **Figure 3.7** – Upar: CH₂=CH–CH₂–CH₃ (double bond pehle aur doosre carbon ke beech). Neeche: CH₃–CH=CH–CH₃ (double bond doosre aur teesre ke beech). Har C par 4 bond (double bond ka matlab 2) aur har H par 1. Dono mein 4 C aur 8 H.

**Kadam 1:** dono mein chain 4 carbon: but-.  Double bond hai: **-ene**.
**Kadam 2 (upar wala):** baayen se ginne par double bond C1 aur C2 ke beech: chhota number **1**. Naam **but-1-ene**.
**Kadam 3 (neeche wala):** double bond C2 aur C3 ke beech: dono sire se **2**. Naam **but-2-ene**.

**Jaanch:** dono ka formula C₄H₈ = CₙH₂ₙ ✅. Ginti: upar 2 + 1 + 2 + 3 = 8 H, neeche 3 + 1 + 1 + 3 = 8 H ✅. Ye **position isomer** hain: chain wahi, sirf double bond ki jagah alag. Agar upar wale ko ulte likho: CH₃–CH₂–CH=CH₂, to bhi wo **but-1-ene** hi hai (daayen se ginte hain, 1): ulte likhne se naam nahi badalta.

(Cis aur trans naam ke aur kisi tarah ke isomer aage ki class mein.)

### Example 8. Propanol ke do isomer

**Sawaal:** C₃H₇OH mein –OH do tarah ke carbon par lag sakta hai. Dono ke naam batao.

```
      H   H   H
      I   I   I
  H - C - C - C - O - H
      I   I   I
      H   H   H


          O - H
          I
      H   I   H
      I   I   I
  H - C - C - C - H
      I   I   I
      H   H   H
```

> **Figure 3.8** – Upar: CH₃–CH₂–CH₂–OH (–OH sire ke carbon par). Neeche: CH₃–CH(OH)–CH₃ (–OH beech ke carbon par). Har C par 4 bond, har H par 1, O par 2 (ek C se, ek H se). Dono mein 3 C, 8 H, 1 O.

**Kadam 1:** teen carbon: prop-, saath mein –OH: suffix **-ol**.
**Kadam 2 (upar):** –OH sire wale carbon par: C1: **propan-1-ol**.
**Kadam 3 (neeche):** –OH beech wale carbon par: C2: **propan-2-ol**.

**Jaanch:** dono C₃H₈O: upar 3 + 2 + 2 + 1 = 8 H, neeche 3 + 3 + 1 (beech ke C par) + 1 (O par) = 8 H ✅. Molar mass 36 + 8 + 16 = **60** dono ki. Ethanol aur methanol ko number nahi chahiye kyunki 1 ya 2 carbon mein –OH ki **ek hi jagah** hoti hai.

## Part E: Functional isomer: parivaar hi badal gaya

Teesri tarah ke isomer mein **functional group hi alag** hota hai. Sabse chhota example C₂H₆O ka hai.

```
      H   H                 H       H
      I   I                 I       I
  H - C - C - O - H     H - C - O - C - H
      I   I                 I       I
      H   H                 H       H

    ethanol               methoxymethane
```

> **Figure 3.9** – C₂H₆O ke do compound. Baayen: ethanol (CH₃CH₂OH, –OH group, alcohol). Daayen: methoxymethane (CH₃OCH₃), jisme oxygen do carbon ke beech hai (ether). Har C par 4 bond, har O par 2, har H par 1. Dono mein 2 C, 6 H, 1 O.

Dono ka formula ek, par functional group alag, isliye gun bhi alag-alag; ethanol ka poora paath Topic 6 mein. **Teen tarah ke isomer ek nazar mein:**

| Kism | Kya badla | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Chain isomer | chain ki banawat (seedhi ya shakha wali) | butane aur 2-methylpropane |
| Position isomer | double bond ya group ki jagah | but-1-ene aur but-2-ene; propan-1-ol aur propan-2-ol |
| Functional isomer | functional group hi | ethanol aur methoxymethane |

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti: "Chain ko seedha banana zaroori hai: mud gayi ya L-shape mein bani chain naya isomer hai."**

Apoorva ne pentane ko kaagaz par do tarah se banaya (Figure 3.10): ek seedhi line, ek L-shape mein mudi hui. "Mama, dono alag lag rahe hain! Kya ye bhi isomer hain? To pentane ke 4 isomer ho gaye!" Mr. Rokade ne kaha, "Test karte hain. Dono mein carbon ginte hue ungli se chalo."

```
  CH₃-CH₂-CH₂-CH₂-CH₃            CH₃-CH₂-CH₂
                                         I
                                         CH₂
                                         I
                                         CH₃
```

> **Figure 3.10** – Pentane ke do chitra. Baayen: seedhi line. Daayen: wahi chain neeche ki taraf mud gayi. Dono mein 5 C: CH₃ (3 H + 1 C = 4 bond), CH₂ (2 H + 2 C = 4 bond). Kul H = 3 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 3 = 12.

Dono chitron mein ungli se **C1 → C2 → C3 → C4 → C5** tak chalo: **5 carbon ki ek lagataar chain**, har carbon dusre se wahi jodta hai. Bonds paper par nahi, space mein hote hain aur ghoom sakte hain (jaise ek mala ya chain mod dene se wahi mala rehti hai): sirf **kaun kis se judta hai** matter karta hai, kaagaz par kaisi dikhti hai nahi. Isliye dono **pentane** hain: ek hi compound. Isomer tab milte hain jab **jodne ka kram** badle (Part C ke teen compound), na ki chitra mud jaye.

Ulta galti bhi hoti hai. Figure 3.11 mein dekho.

```
  CH₃-CH₂-CH₂-CH₂             CH₃-CH-CH₃
              I                   I
              CH₃                 CH₂
                                  I
                                  CH₃
```

> **Figure 3.11** – Baayen: chain ke aakhir mein neeche mudta hua CH₃ shakha nahi hai. Chain C1 → C2 → C3 → C4 → neeche ka CH₃ = 5 carbon, ye pentane hai. Daayen: seedhi dikhne wali upar ki line mein sirf 3 C hain, par asli sabse lambi chain C1 (baayen ka CH₃) → beech ka CH → neeche ka CH₂ → CH₃ = 4 carbon hai. Is mein chaar bond har C par: CH₃ 3 H + 1 C; CH 1 H + 3 C; CH₂ 2 H + 2 C.

- **Baayen:** mudta hua sira "shakha" lagta hai, par ungli se chalo to chain 5 hi hai: naam **pentane**, "methylbutane" nahi.
- **Daayen:** upar ki seedhi line sirf 3 carbon ki hai, par neeche utarte hue ungli 4 carbon par chalti hai. Sabse lambi chain 4: root **but-**. Beech ke carbon par ek methyl: **2-methylbutane** (Example 3 wahi compound). Agar kisi ne seedhi dikhti line ko hi chain maan kar "ethyl" shakha wala naam diya, to wo galat hai.

**Niyam:** chitra ka seedha ya mud jaana dekh kar naam ya isomer mat tay karo. **Ungli se chalo, sabse lambi chain gino, number sahi sire se do.**

## Quick Recap

- **IUPAC naam** = number + shakha + root + suffix (2-methylbutane). Root: meth-, eth-, prop-, but-, pent-, hex-, hept-, oct-, non-, dec- (1 se 10 carbon).
- **Suffix:** -ane (alkane), -ene (double bond), -yne (triple bond), -ol (–OH), -al (–CHO), -one (>C=O), -oic acid (–COOH). **Prefix:** chloro-, bromo-, methyl. Alkane ka aakhri "e" vowel se shuru hone wale suffix se pehle gir jaata hai (ethanol, ethanal, propanone).
- **Branch wali alkane:** (1) sabse lambi chain, (2) wo sira jo shakha ko chhota number de, (3) naam: number-shakha-root. 1-methyl kabhi nahi; do ek jaisi shakha ho to di-, 2,2-dimethylpropane.
- **Isomer:** wahi molecular formula, alag structure. **Chain isomer:** C₄H₁₀ ke 2 (butane, 2-methylpropane), C₅H₁₂ ke 3 (pentane, 2-methylbutane, 2,2-dimethylpropane). **Position isomer:** but-1-ene aur but-2-ene, propan-1-ol aur propan-2-ol. **Functional isomer:** ethanol aur methoxymethane.
- **Saare isomer dhundna:** sabse lambi chain se shuru karo, ek-ek carbon shakha banate jao, sire par shakha nahi.
- **Galti se bacho:** chain mud gayi to naya isomer nahi; ungli se lambi chain gino.
- **Suraksha:** sab kaagaz par; propanone, ethanol, ether jwalanshil; methanal zehreela; methanol kabhi nahi chakhna.

## Practice Questions

(Sab sawaal kaagaz par hal karne ke liye hain. Mass: H 1, C 12, O 16.)

1. Hexane has a straight chain of 6 carbon atoms. Write its molecular formula and its condensed formula, and say how many CH₃ groups and how many CH₂ groups it has.
2. Name the compound CH₃–CH(CH₃)–CH₂–CH₂–CH₃ by the numbering rule. Show the position of the methyl branch when numbered from the left end and from the right end.
3. Draw the full structural formula of 3-methylpentane (C₆H₁₄) and show that every carbon has 4 bonds and every hydrogen has 1.
4. (a) A student calls the compound of Question 2 "4-methylpentane". What is wrong? (b) Another student writes "1-methylpentane". Explain why this name can never be correct, and say which compound it really is.
5. Find all the isomers of C₆H₁₄ and name each one. (Hint: start from the longest chain and shorten it one carbon at a time.)
6. For each pair say "same compound", "isomers" or "neither", with a reason: (a) hexane and a 6-carbon chain drawn with a bend in the middle; (b) 2-methylpentane and 3-methylpentane; (c) 2-methylpentane and 2-methylbutane; (d) hexane and 2-methylpentane.
7. A chemist writes three structures of C₄H₈ with a double bond: (a) CH₃–CH₂–CH=CH₂, (b) CH₂=CH–CH₂–CH₃, (c) CH₃–CH=CH–CH₃. Name each, and say how many different compounds there are.
8. A sample has 28 g of C₄H₈. Find the number of moles and the masses of carbon and hydrogen in it. Would 28 g of the other position isomer give the same answers? Why?
9. Methoxyethane has the structure CH₃–O–CH₂–CH₃. Show that its formula is C₃H₈O and check the valency of each C and O. Is methoxyethane a functional or a position isomer of propan-1-ol, and of propan-2-ol? Which pair among these three compounds is of the position kind?
10. Find the number of moles in 15 g of C₃H₈O, and the masses of carbon, hydrogen and oxygen in it. Are the answers the same for propan-1-ol, propan-2-ol and methoxyethane?
11. For a straight chain of six carbon atoms write the name and molecular formula of (a) the alkene with its double bond between C2 and C3, (b) the alcohol with –OH on the end carbon, (c) the alkyne with its triple bond at the first carbon.
12. A compound has the formula C₆H₁₄, only methyl branches, and its longest chain has exactly 4 carbon atoms. (A branch on an end carbon would lengthen the chain, so it is not allowed.) How many methyl branches does it have, and how many such compounds exist? Name them.
13. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) Isomers always have the same molar mass. (b) A 6-carbon chain drawn with a bend is a new isomer of hexane. (c) Propan-1-ol and propan-2-ol are functional isomers. (d) Methoxyethane and propan-1-ol are position isomers. (e) The ending -ol shows that a compound is an alcohol.
14. On a street the houses are numbered 1 to 8 from the west end. A bakery stands at house number 6 from the west end. What number does the bakery get from the east end, and which number will the postman use if he follows the "lowest number" rule? How does this help in numbering a methyl branch on the fifth carbon of a six-carbon chain counted from one end?

### Answers

1. n = 6: H = 2 × 6 + 2 = 14, formula **C₆H₁₄**. Condensed: **CH₃–CH₂–CH₂–CH₂–CH₂–CH₃**. **2 CH₃** (dono sire) aur **4 CH₂**. Jaanch: H = 2 × 3 + 4 × 2 = 6 + 8 = 14 ✅.
2. Sabse lambi chain 5 carbon: **pentane** (CH₃–CH–CH₂–CH₂–CH₃). Baayen se ginne par methyl **C2** par, daayen se ginne par **C4** par. Chhota number 2, isliye **2-methylpentane**. Jaanch: C = 5 + 1 = 6, H = 3 + 1 + 3 + 2 + 2 + 3 = 14 = C₆H₁₄ ✅.
3. Chain 5 carbon, methyl C3 par (beech wala).

```
                      H
                      I
                  H - C - H
                      I
      H       H       I       H       H
      I       I       I       I       I
  H - C ----- C ----- C ----- C ----- C - H
      I       I       I       I       I
      H       H       H       H       H
```

> **Figure 3.12** – 3-methylpentane (C₆H₁₄). Beech ke carbon ke chaar bond: do chain ke carbon, ek methyl, ek H. Baaki carbon ke 3 ya 2 H aur chain ke bond milakar 4. H ki ginti: 3 (methyl) + 3 + 2 + 1 + 2 + 3 = **14** ✅, 6 carbon.

4. (a) Methyl baayen se ginne par C2 par aur daayen se ginne par C4 par hai. Niyam hai **chhota number**, isliye 2: "4-methylpentane" galat, sahi **2-methylpentane**. (b) Methyl sire ke carbon par laga hota to chain 6 carbon ki ban jaati (methyl bhi chain mein aa jaata), isliye wo asal mein **hexane** hai. "1-methyl" ka matlab hai lambi chain galat chuni gayi.
5. Sabse lambi chain se: (i) 6 carbon: **hexane** CH₃–CH₂–CH₂–CH₂–CH₂–CH₃. (ii) 5 carbon, ek methyl: C2 par **2-methylpentane** CH₃–CH(CH₃)–CH₂–CH₂–CH₃, ya C3 par **3-methylpentane** CH₃–CH₂–CH(CH₃)–CH₂–CH₃ (C4 par lagana C2 jaisa hi hai). (iii) 4 carbon, do methyl: **2,2-dimethylbutane** CH₃–C(CH₃)₂–CH₂–CH₃ aur **2,3-dimethylbutane** CH₃–CH(CH₃)–CH(CH₃)–CH₃ (3,3 wala 2,2 ke barabar, aur 2,4 wala chain lambi kar deta). Kul **5 isomer**. Jaanch: har ka H = 14, C = 6 ✅.
6. (a) **Same compound**: mud jaane se sirf chitra badla, jodne ka kram wahi (hexane). (b) **Isomers**: dono C₆H₁₄, methyl C2 aur C3 par. (c) **Neither**: 2-methylpentane C₆H₁₄ aur 2-methylbutane C₅H₁₂: formula hi alag. (d) **Isomers**: dono C₆H₁₄, ek seedhi chain, ek shakha wali.
7. (a) Double bond ko dekho: C3 aur C4 ke beech baayen se, par daayen se C1 aur C2 ke beech: chhota number 1: **but-1-ene**. (b) Baayen se C1 aur C2: **but-1-ene**. (c) C2 aur C3: **but-2-ene**. (a) aur (b) ek hi compound (sirf ulta likha gaya). Kul **2 alag compound**.
8. C₄H₈ = 4 × 12 + 8 = **56**. Moles = 28 ÷ 56 = **0.5 mol**. Carbon = 0.5 × 48 = **24 g**, hydrogen = 0.5 × 8 = **4 g**. Jaanch: 24 + 4 = 28 g ✅. Doosre isomer ka bhi molecular formula wahi (C₄H₈), isliye molar mass 56 aur wahi **0.5 mol**, wahi 24 g aur 4 g: formula wahi hai, mass ka hisaab formula se hota hai.
9. C: 3 (CH₃, CH₂, CH₃), H = 3 + 2 + 3 = 8, O = 1: **C₃H₈O** ✅. Valency: CH₃ (baayen) 3 H + 1 O = 4; CH₂ 2 H + 1 O + 1 C = 4; CH₃ (daayen) 3 H + 1 C = 4; O ke do bond (ek CH₃ se, ek CH₂ se) = 2 ✅. Propan-1-ol aur propan-2-ol ke saath **functional** (–OH ki jagah –O– group), kyunki parivaar hi alag. Propan-1-ol aur propan-2-ol aapas mein **position** isomer hain (Part D).
10. C₃H₈O = 36 + 8 + 16 = **60**. Moles = 15 ÷ 60 = **0.25 mol**. Carbon = 36 ÷ 60 × 15 = **9 g**, hydrogen = 8 ÷ 60 × 15 = **2 g**, oxygen = 16 ÷ 60 × 15 = **4 g**. Jaanch: 9 + 2 + 4 = 15 g ✅. **Haan**, teeno isomer ka molecular formula wahi hai, isliye ye saare numbers wahi.
11. (a) **hex-2-ene**, C₆H₁₂ (CₙH₂ₙ, n = 6). (b) **hexan-1-ol**, C₆H₁₃OH (CₙH₂ₙ₊₁OH). (c) **hex-1-yne**, C₆H₁₀ (CₙH₂ₙ₋₂). Jaanch: 2 × 6 = 12 ✅, 2 × 6 + 1 = 13 ✅, 2 × 6 − 2 = 10 ✅.
12. Chain 4 ki, to 6 − 4 = **2 carbon** shakha mein, sirf methyl hain, to **2 methyl**. Dono sire par nahi lag sakte, to C2 ya C3 par. Dono C2 par: **2,2-dimethylbutane**. Ek C2 par aur ek C3 par: **2,3-dimethylbutane**. Dono C3 par (3,3) ulte ginne par 2,2 ban jaata hai, wahi pehla. Isliye **2 compound**: 2,2-dimethylbutane aur 2,3-dimethylbutane. (Ye Question 5 ke teen aur compound (hexane, 2- aur 3-methylpentane) se alag hain, kyunki unki lambi chain 6 ya 5 ki hai.)
13. (a) **True** ✅: formula wahi to molar mass wahi. (b) **False** ❌: mud jaane se jodne ka kram nahi badla, wahi hexane hai. (c) **False** ❌: ye **position** isomer hain (sirf –OH ki jagah). (d) **False** ❌: ye **functional** isomer hain (–OH aur –O–). (e) **True** ✅: -ol alcohol (–OH) ka suffix hai.
14. Daayen (east) end se bakery **3** number par hai (8 + 1 − 6 = 3). Postman **chhota number 3** likhega. Chemistry mein: chain ke paanchve carbon par methyl baayen se 5 hai par daayen se 2, to hum **2** likhte hain: wahi sire se number dete hain jo shakha ko chhota number de.
