# Topic 4: Alkanes: Methane Se Octane Tak

Topic 2 mein hamne series ka "train" wala idea dekha, aur Topic 3 mein naam aur isomer. Ab hum us train ke **sabse pehle aur sabse seedhe parivar** ke andar jaate hain: **alkane**, jinme carbon ke saare bond **single** hote hain. Yahi wo gas hain jo aapke ghar ke chulhe mein jalti hain, aur yahi petrol ka bada hissa hain. Aaj hum ye samjhenge ki ye kaise bane hain, kaise jalte hain (aur kabhi kabhi galat tareeke se kyun jalte hain), aur unse kaise bachna hai.

## Gobar-gas ka chulha

Mr. Salunkhe apne bhatije Rajas ko apne gaon ke ghar le gaye. Aangan ke ek kone mein zameen se ek gol, cement ka dhakkan sa ubhra tha, aur ek moti nali seedhe rasoi ke chulhe tak jaati thi. "Chacha, ye kya hai?" Rajas ne pucha. "Ye **biogas plant** hai," Mr. Salunkhe ne kaha. "Roz subah hum gai ka gobar aur thoda paani ek bade gaddhe mein daalte hain. Gaddhe ke andar hawa nahi jaati, aur wahan chhote jeev (bacteria) gobar ko sadate hain. Us sadne se ek gas banti hai jo oopar ikattha hoti hai."

Shaam ko Mr. Salunkhe ki bahu ne chulha jalaya. Rajas ne dhyan se dekha: lau **neeli** thi, kaalikh ka naam nahi, aur bartan neeche se saaf rehta tha. Rajas ko apni shehar wali rasoi yaad aayi: "Chacha, hamare yahan jab kabhi lau peeli ho jaati hai to patila neeche se kaala ho jaata hai. Yahan itni neeli kyun?" Mr. Salunkhe ne muskura kar kaha, "Do sawaal ek saath: gas kaun si hai, aur lau neeli kyun? Dono ka jawab ek hi parivar mein hai, **alkane**."

## Part A: Alkane: saare bond single

**Alkane** wo hydrocarbon hai jisme har carbon apne chaaron bond **single** bond se banata hai: ya doosre carbon ke saath, ya hydrogen ke saath. Topic 2 mein dekha tha ki general formula **CₙH₂ₙ₊₂** hai. Isiliye inhe **saturated** kehte hain: har carbon ke chaaron haath "bhare" hue hain, ab kisi aur atom ko jodne ke liye haath baaki nahi.

Figure 4.1 mein pehle teen alkane ki shakal dekho. Har C ke paas bonds ginte jao: **chaar**; har H ke paas: **ek**.

```
        H              H   H              H   H   H
        I              I   I              I   I   I
    H - C - H      H - C - C - H      H - C - C - C - H
        I              I   I              I   I   I
        H              H   H              H   H   H
     methane          ethane              propane
      CH₄             C₂H₆                 C₃H₈
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Pehle teen alkane ki structure. Methane: 1 C, 4 H (C ke 4 bond). Ethane: 2 C, 6 H, ek C–C single bond; har C ke 3 H aur 1 C ke saath bond, yaani 4. Propane: 3 C, 8 H; beech wale C ke 2 H aur 2 C ke saath bond (4), kinare wale C ke 3 H aur 1 C (4). Har H ke paas bas 1 bond.

Lambi chain bhi wahi kahani hai. Butane (C₄H₁₀) ke liye do alag structure ban sakte hain: ek seedhi chain aur ek shakha wali (Topic 3 ke isomer):

```
      H   H   H   H                    H
      I   I   I   I                    I
  H - C - C - C - C - H            H - C - H
      I   I   I   I                    I
      H   H   H   H            H       I       H
                               I       I       I
     butane                H - C ----- C ----- C - H
     (seedhi chain)            I       I       I
                               H       H       H
                          2-methylpropane
                          (shakha wali chain)
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Butane aur 2-methylpropane: dono ka formula C₄H₁₀ (4 C aur 10 H). Seedhi chain mein beech ke do C ke 2 H aur 2 C se bond, kinare wale ke 3 H aur 1 C se. Shakha wale mein beech wale C ke teen C aur ek H se bond (4); baaki teeno C ke 3 H aur 1 C se (4). Dono mein 4 C aur 10 H gin lo.

Ab ek mazedaar jaanch. Alkane ke saare bond single hain. Aise **kitne bond** hote hain?

### Example 1. C₈H₁₈ mein bond ginna

**Sawaal:** Octane, C₈H₁₈, mein kitne C–C bond aur kitne C–H bond hain? Kul single bond kitne?

**Kadam 1:** 8 carbon ek seedhi chain mein hain: beech ke gaps = 8 − 1 = **7 C–C bond**.
**Kadam 2:** har H ka ek bond C se: 18 H = **18 C–H bond**.
**Kadam 3:** kul bond = 7 + 18 = **25**.

**Jaanch:** kul valency = 8 × 4 (C) + 18 × 1 (H) = 32 + 18 = 50. Har bond do atomon ki ek-ek valency use karta hai, to bond = 50 ÷ 2 = **25** ✅. (Seedha formula: n carbon ke alkane mein bond = 3n + 1 = 3 × 8 + 1 = 25 ✅.)

### Example 2. Series ki molecular mass

**Sawaal:** CH₄ se C₈H₁₈ tak ki molecular mass likho aur dekho ki har kadam par kitna badhta hai. (H 1, C 12.)

CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ ki mass = 12n + (2n + 2) = **14n + 2**.

| Alkane | n | Mass | Pichhle se badhat |
|---|---|---|---|
| CH₄ | 1 | 16 | - |
| C₂H₆ | 2 | 30 | 14 |
| C₃H₈ | 3 | 44 | 14 |
| C₄H₁₀ | 4 | 58 | 14 |
| C₅H₁₂ | 5 | 72 | 14 |
| C₆H₁₄ | 6 | 86 | 14 |
| C₇H₁₆ | 7 | 100 | 14 |
| C₈H₁₈ | 8 | 114 | 14 |

**Jaanch:** C₈H₁₈ = 8 × 12 + 18 × 1 = 96 + 18 = 114 ✅; formula se 14 × 8 + 2 = 114 ✅. Har kadam par CH₂ (mass 14) ka jodna wahi series ka niyam hai.

## Part B: Methane aur LPG kahan se aate hain?

Rajas ke chulhe ki gas **methane (CH₄)** hai. Teen jagah se methane ya alkane hamein milte hain:

1. **Biogas:** gobar, kheti ka kachra ya rasoi ka geela kachra bina hawa ke bacteria se sadta hai. Banne wali gas mein lagbhag **55 se 65 percent methane** hota hai, baaki mukhya roop se CO₂. Gaddha (digester) band hota hai: andar hawa nahi jaati. Gas halki hai, isliye oopar gumbad mein jama hoti hai.
2. **Natural gas:** zameen ke neeche tel ke saath ya akeli milti hai; mukhya gas methane. Ise dabaakar **CNG** banti hai (Class 9: saaf fuel).
3. **LPG:** tel (petroleum) ko saaf karne par mile **propane (C₃H₈) aur butane (C₄H₁₀)**. Inhe dabaav se liquid karke cylinder mein bharte hain.

```
                                              gas ki nali
                                           .----------------> chulha
                                           I                 (neeli lau)
                                        .--+--.
   zameen ka star  ____________________/ gas   \_________________________
                                      :  (CH₄ +  :
   gobar + paani                      :   CO₂)   :       khaad (nikasi)
   ---> [ inlet ]------+              :          :  +------[ outlet ]---->
                       I     +--------+----------+--+--+
                       I     :  ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~   :
                       +-----:     gobar ka ghol        :
                             :     (bina hawa ke,       :
                             :      bacteria sadate)    :
                             +--------------------------+
```

> **Figure 4.3** – Biogas plant ka kat (section): gobar aur paani inlet se gaddhe mein jaate hain. Band gaddhe mein hawa nahi, bacteria gobar ko sadate hain, aur halki gas gumbad mein oopar jama hoti hai, jahan se nali chulhe tak jaati hai. Sada hua ghol outlet se khaad ke roop mein bahar aata hai. Gas ko jalane ka kaam sirf chulhe par, gumbad ke paas kabhi aag nahi.

### Methane banane ka ek lab tareeka (sirf padhne ke liye)

Lab mein methane **sodium ethanoate (CH₃COONa)** ko **soda lime** (NaOH aur CaO ka mix) ke saath garam karke banti hai. Bas naam aur equation dekho (sirf padhne ke liye; NaOH corrosive hai, gas jalne wali):

CH₃COONa + NaOH → CH₄ + Na₂CO₃

**Jaanch:** baayein C 2, H 3 + 1 = 4, Na 1 + 1 = 2, O 2 + 1 = 3; daayein C 1 + 1 = 2, H 4, Na 2, O 3 ✅.

Ye "kaise bani" ki sirf jhalak hai: ghar ki gas ka asli source biogas, natural gas aur petroleum hai.

## Part C: Physical gun: chain badi, boiling point oonchi

Topic 2 mein alkane series ka boiling point badhta dekha tha. Ab hum "kyun" aur "kitna" dekhte hain. Alkane **non-polar** hain: C–H bond lagbhag non-polar hai (farq 0.4) aur shape symmetric hai ([chemical-bonding-ii-part2.md](../chemical-bonding-ii/chemical-bonding-ii-part2.md)). Chapter 7 ka niyam yaad karo: non-polar padarth **paani mein nahi ghulte**, non-polar solvent mein ghulte hain ("like dissolves like"). Isliye alkane paani se alag rehte hain aur paani se halke hote hain (tel ki tarah paani par tairte hain). Molecule jitna lamba, uske beech ki halki pakad utni zyada, aur use ubalne ke liye utni zyada garmi.

| Alkane | Boiling point (lagbhag, °C) | Kamre ke taapman par |
|---|---|---|
| CH₄ | −162 | gas |
| C₂H₆ | −89 | gas |
| C₃H₈ | −42 | gas |
| C₄H₁₀ | −1 | gas |
| C₅H₁₂ | 36 | liquid |
| C₆H₁₄ | 69 | liquid |

Octane (C₈H₁₈) ek liquid hai, jo petrol ka ek mukhya hissa hai (Class 9 mein petrol ke liye octane ko maana tha: [atmospheric-pollution-part6.md](../../../class9/chemistry/atmospheric-pollution/atmospheric-pollution-part6.md)). Aur bahut lambi chain wale alkane (mom) thos hote hain.

```
   Boiling point (−162 °C se kitna oopar): har # = lagbhag 10 °C

   CH₄     #                  (0 °C oopar: −162 hi shuruaat hai)
   C₂H₆    #######            (73)
   C₃H₈    ############       (120)
   C₄H₁₀   ################   (161)
   C₅H₁₂   ####################   (198)
   C₆H₁₄   #######################   (231)
```

> **Figure 4.4** – Alkane ke boiling point, −162 °C se naapkar (CH₄ ka boiling point shuruaat mana gaya, isliye uski bar sirf ek chhota nishaan). Bar ki lambai = −162 se uska farq, lagbhag 10 °C har #. Chain badhne par bar lambi hoti jaati hai, par har naye CH₂ par badhat kam hoti jaati hai.

### Example 3. Har kadam par kitna badhat?

**Sawaal:** Table ke boiling point se CH₄ se C₆H₁₄ tak har kadam par badhat nikalo.

- CH₄ → C₂H₆: −89 − (−162) = **73** °C
- C₂H₆ → C₃H₈: −42 − (−89) = **47** °C
- C₃H₈ → C₄H₁₀: −1 − (−42) = **41** °C
- C₄H₁₀ → C₅H₁₂: 36 − (−1) = **37** °C
- C₅H₁₂ → C₆H₁₄: 69 − 36 = **33** °C

**Jaanch:** 73 + 47 + 41 + 37 + 33 = **231**, aur kul badhat 69 − (−162) = **231** ✅. Dekho, badhat har baar kam hui: shuruaat mein ek CH₂ ka asar bada, baad mein chhota.

### Example 4. LPG cylinder mein liquid kyun?

**Sawaal:** Pahaadi sardi mein subah taapman −5 °C hai. Butane (boiling point −1 °C) aur propane (−42 °C) us samay gas hain ya liquid, agar dabaav sirf hawa ka ho?

Butane: −5 °C, boiling point −1 °C se **neeche**: butane **liquid** (ubalne ke point se thanda). Propane: −5 °C, boiling point −42 °C se **oopar**: propane **gas**.
Isliye cylinder mein inhe **dabaav** se liquid rakhte hain: kam jagah mein bahut gas aa jaati hai, aur khulne par gas ban kar chulhe tak aati hai.

**Jaanch:** −5 > −42 (propane gas) ✅; −5 < −1 (butane liquid) ✅.

## Part D: Jalna: poora aur adhoora

Alkane ki sabse zaroori aadat: **jalna**. Kaafi oxygen ho to **complete combustion**: CO₂ aur paani, saath mein garmi. Ye Class 9 aur Chapter 5 mein dekha tha:

- CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O
- C₃H₈ + 5O₂ → 3CO₂ + 4H₂O
- 2C₈H₁₈ + 25O₂ → 16CO₂ + 18H₂O (octane, petrol ka hissa)

**Jaanch:** octane: baayein C 2 × 8 = 16, H 2 × 18 = 36, O 25 × 2 = 50; daayein C 16, H 18 × 2 = 36, O 16 × 2 + 18 = 50 ✅.

Oxygen **kam** ho to jalna **adhoora** hota hai, aur CO ya carbon ke kaale kan bhi bante hain:

2CH₄ + 3O₂ → 2CO + 4H₂O  (CO zehreeli)

**Jaanch:** baayein C 2, H 8, O 6; daayein C 2, H 4 × 2 = 8, O 2 + 4 = 6 ✅.

Soot ka saral roop: CH₄ + O₂ → C + 2H₂O (C = kaalikh).

**Jaanch:** baayein C 1, H 4, O 2; daayein C 1, H 4, O 2 ✅.

### Lau neeli ya peeli kyun?

Chulhe ke burner mein gas ek chhote chhed se nikalti hai aur burner ki nali ke side ke **hawa wale chhedon** se hawa ko kheench kar usse mil jaati hai. Hawa ki oxygen kaafi ho, to jalna poora: **neeli** lau, kam kaalikh. Chhed band ya gandagi se ruk jayein, to oxygen kam: jalna adhoora, lau **peeli**: peeli rang kaale carbon ke garam kaano ke chamakne se aata hai. Wahi kan jab patile se takrate hain to **kaalikh** ban kar chipakte hain. Aur saath mein **CO** bhi banti hai.

```
   Hawa wale chhed KHULE               Hawa wale chhed BAND
   (neeli lau, poora jalna)            (peeli lau, adhoora jalna)

         neeli lau                           peeli, lambi lau
           /\                                  /\    /\
          /  \                                /  \  /  \
       +--------+                         +--------+
       :  nali  :                         :  nali  :
   hawa:        :hawa                   X :        : X     (chhed band)
   --->:        :<---                     :        :
       +---::---+                         +---::---+
           ::                                 ::
           ::  gas (jet)                      ::  gas (jet)
```

> **Figure 4.5** – Chulhe ke burner ke paas (sirf padhne ke liye, burner ko bachche na chhuen): baayein hawa wale chhed khule hain, hawa nali mein jaakar gas se milti hai, lau neeli aur chhoti; daayein chhed band (X) hain, oxygen kam, lau peeli aur lambi, kaalikh banti hai.

### Example 5. Butane ka poora jalna: kitni CO₂?

**Sawaal:** 29 g butane (C₄H₁₀) poori tarah jalti hai (kagaz par): 2C₄H₁₀ + 13O₂ → 8CO₂ + 10H₂O. Kitni oxygen lagegi, kitni CO₂ (mass aur STP par volume) aur kitna paani banega? (H 1, C 12, O 16; 22.4 L per mol.)

**Kadam 1:** C₄H₁₀ = 4 × 12 + 10 = **58** g/mol. Moles = 29 ÷ 58 = **0.5 mol**.
**Kadam 2 (ratio 2 : 13 : 8 : 10):** 1 mol butane ke liye O₂ = 6.5 mol, CO₂ = 4 mol, H₂O = 5 mol. 0.5 mol ke liye O₂ = **3.25 mol** = 3.25 × 32 = **104 g**; CO₂ = **2 mol** = 2 × 44 = **88 g**, volume 2 × 22.4 = **44.8 L**; H₂O = **2.5 mol** = 2.5 × 18 = **45 g**.

**Jaanch:** atoms: C 8 = 8, H 20 = 10 × 2, O 26 = 16 + 10 ✅. Mass: 29 + 104 = 133 g; 88 + 45 = 133 g ✅.

### Example 6. Octane ka hisaab (petrol ka hissa)

**Sawaal:** 57 g octane (C₈H₁₈) poori tarah jalti hai: 2C₈H₁₈ + 25O₂ → 16CO₂ + 18H₂O. CO₂ ka STP volume, O₂ ka mass aur paani ka mass? (C 12, H 1, O 16.)

**Kadam 1:** C₈H₁₈ = 96 + 18 = **114** g/mol; moles = 57 ÷ 114 = **0.5 mol**.
**Kadam 2:** ratio 2 : 25 : 16 : 18, to 1 mol octane ke liye O₂ = 12.5, CO₂ = 8, H₂O = 9 mol. 0.5 mol ke liye: O₂ = 6.25 mol = **200 g**; CO₂ = 4 mol = 4 × 22.4 = **89.6 L**; H₂O = 4.5 mol = 4.5 × 18 = **81 g**.

**Jaanch:** 57 + 200 = 257 g; CO₂ 4 × 44 = 176 g, plus 81 = 257 g ✅.

### Example 7. Methane: kam hawa, alag products

**Sawaal:** 3.2 g methane ek baar poori hawa mein (CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O) aur ek baar kam hawa mein (2CH₄ + 3O₂ → 2CO + 4H₂O) jalti hai. Dono mein oxygen aur carbon wala product kitna? (CH₄ 16, CO 28, CO₂ 44, O₂ 32.)

**Kadam 1:** moles = 3.2 ÷ 16 = **0.2 mol**.
**Kadam 2 (poora):** O₂ = 2 × 0.2 = 0.4 mol = **12.8 g**; CO₂ = 0.2 mol = **8.8 g**.
**Kadam 3 (adhoora):** O₂ = 1.5 × 0.2 = 0.3 mol = **9.6 g**; CO = 0.2 mol = **5.6 g**; H₂O = 0.4 mol = 7.2 g.

**Jaanch:** adhoora: 3.2 + 9.6 = 12.8 g; 5.6 + 7.2 = 12.8 g ✅. Poora: 3.2 + 12.8 = 16 g; CO₂ 8.8 + H₂O 7.2 = 16 g ✅. Dhyan do: carbon ke ek hi atom ke liye **ek** CO ya **ek** CO₂ bana: carbon wala product dono mein ek jitni mole (0.2 mol), par wo alag padarth hain.

## Part E: Alkane aur chlorine: substitution

Alkane jalne ke alawa **ek aur** reaction dete hain. Sunlight mein **chlorine** ke saath **methane ke ek H ki jagah ek Cl** aa jaata hai, aur H aur Cl milkar HCl banate hain (sirf padhne ke liye: chlorine zehreeli hai, aur ye sunlight mein tez ho sakta hai):

CH₄ + Cl₂ → CH₃Cl + HCl  (sunlight mein)

```
        H                             H
        I                             I
    H - C - H   + Cl - Cl   -->   H - C - Cl   + H - Cl
        I        (sunlight)           I
        H                             H
     methane                    chloromethane
```

> **Figure 4.6** – Substitution: methane ke ek H ki jagah ek Cl aa gaya (chloromethane, CH₃Cl); C ke paas ab bhi 4 bond (3 H aur 1 Cl). Hataya hua H doosre Cl se milkar HCl bana. Molecule ka dhancha (C aur uske saath jude atom) wahi raha.

Agar chlorine aur ho, to ek ek karke aur H badalte hain: CH₃Cl → **CH₂Cl₂** (dichloromethane) → **CHCl₃** (trichloromethane, chloroform) → **CCl₄** (tetrachloromethane). Har kadam mein ek H hat kar ek Cl aata hai: CH₃Cl + Cl₂ → CH₂Cl₂ + HCl.

**Jaanch:** CH₃Cl + Cl₂: C 1, H 3, Cl 3; CH₂Cl₂ + HCl: C 1, H 2 + 1 = 3, Cl 2 + 1 = 3 ✅.

### Example 8. 1.6 g methane se CH₃Cl

**Sawaal:** 1.6 g methane poora CH₃Cl mein badla (sirf kagaz par): CH₄ + Cl₂ → CH₃Cl + HCl. Kitna Cl₂ lagega aur kitna CH₃Cl aur HCl banega? (C 12, H 1, Cl 35.5.)

**Kadam 1:** CH₄ = 16, moles = 1.6 ÷ 16 = **0.1 mol**. Ratio 1 : 1 : 1 : 1.
**Kadam 2:** Cl₂ = 71 g/mol, 0.1 mol = **7.1 g**; CH₃Cl = 12 + 3 + 35.5 = 50.5, 0.1 mol = **5.05 g**; HCl = 36.5, 0.1 mol = **3.65 g**.

**Jaanch:** 1.6 + 7.1 = 8.7 g; 5.05 + 3.65 = 8.7 g ✅.

### Alkane kyun kam reactive?

Alkane mein C–C aur C–H bond mazboot aur lagbhag non-polar hain (C: 2.5, H: 2.1, farq 0.4: [periodic-properties-part4.md](../periodic-properties/periodic-properties-part4.md)). Koi bhi siraa itna +ve ya −ve nahi ki ions ya acid/alkali use pakad lein. Isliye ye **dilute acid, alkali aur metal** ke saath kuch nahi karte. Par ek tez aag (jalna) ya chlorine + sunlight jaisi cheez inhe tod deti hai.

| Cheez | Methane ya alkane ke saath |
|---|---|
| Dilute HCl ya NaOH ka ghol | koi reaction nahi |
| Oxygen aur chingari | jalte hain |
| Chlorine aur sunlight | substitution |

## Part F: Gas fuel: upyog aur suraksha

**Upyog:**

| Alkane | Kahan kaam aata hai |
|---|---|
| CH₄ | biogas, natural gas, CNG (gaadi) |
| C₃H₈ aur C₄H₁₀ | LPG (ghar ka chulha, lighter) |
| C₈H₁₈ aur sathi | petrol |
| bahut lambi chain | mom (wax) |

**Achha fuel** kise kahte hain? Jo asani se jale, kam dhuan dekar zyada garmi de, aur jiski raakh na bache. Alkane (khaaskar methane) ye teeno achhi tarah karte hain, isliye ghar mein saaf fuel maane jaate hain (Class 9 mein CNG aur LPG ka saaf fuel hona dekha tha).

### Gas leak ke niyam

LPG mein bahut thodi si **badboo wali cheez** milayi jaati hai taaki leak ka pata chale. Agar rasoi mein gas ki badboo aaye:

1. **Switch, pankha, lighter, maachis kuch nahi.** Ek chingari bhi dhamaka kara sakti hai.
2. **Khidki aur darwaze kholo.**
3. **Regulator band karo** (agar bina aag ke paas jaaye safely ho sake), aur bahar jaao.
4. **Bade ko, gas agency ko ya emergency number ko batao.** Chhota bachcha khud kuch nahi karta.

Ek aur jaankari: LPG hawa se **bhaari** hai (propane 44, hawa lagbhag 29), isliye leak hone par **neeche farsh ke paas** jama hoti hai. Methane (16) halka hai, isliye oopar jama hota hai ([mole-concept-and-stoichiometry-part2.md](../mole-concept-and-stoichiometry/mole-concept-and-stoichiometry-part2.md)).

**Biogas plant ke niyam:** gas gumbad ya nali ke paas aag nahi; plant ke gaddhe ke andar kabhi nahi jhaankna ya utarna (andar hawa nahi hoti aur gas bhari hoti hai: dam ghut sakta hai); gas ki nali ka koi bhi kaam bade karte hain.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Alkane bilkul reactive nahi hote, isliye safe hain."**
Ye aadha sach hai. Alkane **dilute acid, alkali aur metal** ke saath reaction nahi dete, isliye "kam reactive" kahte hain. Par dekho: **LPG, methane aur petrol** sab alkane hain, aur ye **jalte** hain, bahut garmi dete hain, aur dhamaka bhi karte hain. Ek kahani: Rajas ne kaha, "Chacha, saturated matlab shaant na?" Mr. Salunkhe ne pucha, "Chulha kya jalata hai?" "Methane." "To shaant kaise hua? Dekho: methane chlorine aur sunlight mein bhi badalta hai: CH₄ + Cl₂ → CH₃Cl + HCl. Aur oxygen mein jalta hai: CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O."

| Cheez | Alkane ka behaviour |
|---|---|
| Dilute acid ya alkali | kuch nahi |
| Oxygen + chingari | jalta hai |
| Chlorine + sunlight | substitution hota hai |

**Niyam:** "saturated" ka matlab **kam reactive**, **bilkul reactive nahi** nahi. Jalna aur substitution alkane ke do reactions hain, isliye gas fuel ko hamesha sambhal ke rakhna hai.

**Galti 2: "Poora aur adhoora jalna, dono mein ek jaise products banti hain, bas kam ya zyada."**
Ye galat hai. Demonstration: Example 7 mein **3.2 g methane** ko dekho. Poori hawa mein: **CO₂ (8.8 g)** aur paani. Kam hawa mein: **CO (5.6 g)** aur paani, aur kuch kaalikh bhi ban sakti hai. CO aur CO₂ alag padarth hain: CO **zehreeli**, jo khoon ke haemoglobin se chipak kar oxygen ko rokti hai (Class 9); CO₂ nahi (ye zehreeli nahi, par greenhouse gas hai). Rajas ke chulhe ki neeli lau poore jalne ki nishani hai; peeli lau, patile par kaalikh aur kamre mein dam ghutna adhoore jalne ke sanket hain.

| Jalna | Lau | Carbon wala product | Zehreeli? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Poora (kaafi oxygen) | neeli | CO₂ | nahi |
| Adhoora (kam oxygen) | peeli | CO aur kaalikh | CO haan |

**Niyam:** products **oxygen kitni mili** par nirbhar karte hain. Peeli lau dikhe to burner saaf karwao aur kamre mein hawa ka intezaam rakho.

## Quick Recap

- **Alkane**: saare bond single, saturated, general formula CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ (CH₄, C₂H₆, C₃H₈, C₄H₁₀, ..., C₈H₁₈). n carbon ke alkane mein bond = 3n + 1.
- **Source:** biogas (gobar, bina hawa ke bacteria, lagbhag 55 se 65 percent methane), natural gas aur CNG (methane), LPG (propane aur butane), petrol (octane jaise).
- **Boiling point** chain ke saath badhta hai (CH₄ −162, C₂H₆ −89, C₃H₈ −42, C₄H₁₀ −1, C₅H₁₂ 36, C₆H₁₄ 69 °C), har naye CH₂ par badhat kam hota jaata hai. Non-polar, paani mein nahi ghulte, paani se halke.
- **Poora jalna:** CO₂ + H₂O, neeli lau. **Adhoora:** CO (zehreeli), kaalikh, peeli lau.
- **Substitution:** CH₄ + Cl₂ → CH₃Cl + HCl (sunlight); phir CH₂Cl₂, CHCl₃, CCl₄.
- **Gas fuel:** leak ki badboo par switch nahi, khidki kholo, regulator band, bahar se bade ko batao. LPG bhaari (neeche jama), methane halki (oopar).
- **Galtiyan:** alkane bilkul unreactive nahi (jalte hain, substitution dete hain); adhoora jalna alag products (CO, kaalikh) deta hai.

## Practice Questions

(Sab sawaal paper par hal karne ke liye hain; saare reactions sirf padhne ke liye. Mass: H 1, C 12, O 16, Cl 35.5. Molar volume 22.4 L at STP.)

1. For butane (C₄H₁₀) and ethane (C₂H₆), count the C–C bonds and the C–H bonds, and check your total with the formula 3n + 1.
2. Draw the full structural formula of straight-chain octane (C₈H₁₈) and show that every carbon has 4 bonds and every hydrogen has 1. How many carbons carry 3 hydrogens and how many carry 2?
3. Burn 15 g of ethane completely: 2C₂H₆ + 7O₂ → 4CO₂ + 6H₂O. Find the masses of oxygen needed, CO₂ formed and water formed. Check the total mass.
4. 22 g of propane burns completely: C₃H₈ + 5O₂ → 3CO₂ + 4H₂O. Find the volume of CO₂ formed at STP and the mass of oxygen used.
5. 285 g of octane burns completely: 2C₈H₁₈ + 25O₂ → 16CO₂ + 18H₂O. Find the moles of octane, the mass of CO₂ and the mass of water formed, and check the total mass.
6. A burner with blocked air-holes burns 12.8 g of methane incompletely: 2CH₄ + 3O₂ → 2CO + 4H₂O. Find the mass of CO formed, and compare it with the mass of CO₂ that the same methane would give with plenty of air.
7. What mass of butane is needed to produce 67.2 L of CO₂ at STP? (2C₄H₁₀ + 13O₂ → 8CO₂ + 10H₂O.)
8. The boiling points are CH₄ −162 °C, C₂H₆ −89 °C, C₃H₈ −42 °C and C₄H₁₀ −1 °C. In a freezer room at −50 °C, which of the four are gases and which liquids? Give reasons.
9. 9.6 g of methane reacts with chlorine in sunlight (on paper): CH₄ + Cl₂ → CH₃Cl + HCl. Find the mass of chlorine used and of CH₃Cl and HCl formed, and check the total.
10. Show that C₄H₁₀ and C₈H₁₈ both fit CₙH₂ₙ₊₂, find their molecular masses, and show that the difference is a whole number of CH₂ units.
11. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) Alkanes burn because they are unsaturated. (b) The yellow flame of a burner shows incomplete burning. (c) CH₄ + Cl₂ → CH₃Cl + HCl is an addition reaction. (d) Methane collects near the floor when it leaks. (e) Carbon monoxide has a strong smell, so a leak is easy to notice.
12. A student says: "Ethane is saturated, so it is inert and cannot burn." Give two reactions of alkanes that prove him wrong, and explain what saturated really means.
13. The smell of gas is noticed in a kitchen. Give four steps in the right order and explain why we must not switch on the light. Which gas, LPG (propane M = 44) or biogas (methane M = 16), will collect near the ceiling? Air has an average M of about 29.
14. In a gas cylinder, butane is a liquid. Using its boiling point (−1 °C), explain why a lighter with butane may not light well at −8 °C.

### Answers

1. Butane: 4 C ki chain, C–C = 4 − 1 = **3**, C–H = **10**, kul 13; 3n + 1 = 3 × 4 + 1 = **13** ✅. Ethane: C–C = **1**, C–H = **6**, kul 7; 3 × 2 + 1 = **7** ✅.
2. Octane: 8 C ki seedhi chain, CH₃–CH₂–CH₂–CH₂–CH₂–CH₂–CH₂–CH₃ (drawing mein har C ke upar-neeche H aur dono sire par ek-ek H). Kinare ke **2** C par 3 H + 1 C = 4 bond; beech ke **6** C par 2 H + 2 C = 4 bond; har H ka 1 bond. Kul H = 2 × 3 + 6 × 2 = **18** ✅ (C₈H₁₈). Jaanch: valency 8 × 4 + 18 = 50, bond 25 = 3 × 8 + 1 ✅.
3. C₂H₆ = 30, moles = 15 ÷ 30 = **0.5 mol**. Ratio 2 : 7 : 4 : 6. O₂ = 3.5 × 0.5 = 1.75 mol = **56 g**; CO₂ = 2 × 0.5 = 1 mol = **44 g**; H₂O = 3 × 0.5 = 1.5 mol = **27 g**. Jaanch: 15 + 56 = 71; 44 + 27 = 71 ✅.
4. C₃H₈ = 44, moles = 22 ÷ 44 = **0.5 mol**. CO₂ = 3 × 0.5 = 1.5 mol = 1.5 × 22.4 = **33.6 L**. O₂ = 5 × 0.5 = 2.5 mol = 2.5 × 32 = **80 g**. Jaanch: 22 + 80 = 102 g; CO₂ 66 + H₂O (2 mol = 36) = 102 g ✅.
5. C₈H₁₈ = 114, moles = 285 ÷ 114 = **2.5 mol**. 1 mol ke liye CO₂ = 8, H₂O = 9, O₂ = 12.5 mol. CO₂ = 20 mol = 20 × 44 = **880 g**; H₂O = 22.5 mol = 22.5 × 18 = **405 g**; O₂ = 31.25 mol = 1000 g. Jaanch: 285 + 1000 = 1285 g; 880 + 405 = 1285 g ✅.
6. CH₄ = 16, moles = 12.8 ÷ 16 = **0.8 mol**. 2CH₄ → 2CO: CO = 0.8 mol = 0.8 × 28 = **22.4 g**. Poori hawa mein CO₂ = 0.8 mol = 0.8 × 44 = **35.2 g**. Dono mein carbon ek jitni mole (0.8 mol), par CO **zehreeli** hai aur CO₂ nahi: products alag. Jaanch: CO 0.8 × 28 = 22.4 ✅; mass: 12.8 + O₂ (1.2 mol = 38.4) = 51.2 g; CO 22.4 + H₂O (1.6 mol = 28.8) = 51.2 g ✅.
7. 67.2 L ÷ 22.4 = **3 mol CO₂**. 2C₄H₁₀ : 8CO₂ = 1 : 4, to butane = 3 ÷ 4 = **0.75 mol** = 0.75 × 58 = **43.5 g**. Jaanch: 0.75 mol × 4 C = 3 mol C = CO₂ mein 3 mol C ✅.
8. −50 °C par jo gas ka boiling point −50 °C se **neeche** hai wo gas rahega. CH₄ (−162) aur C₂H₆ (−89): boiling point −50 se neeche, to **gas**. C₃H₈ (−42) aur C₄H₁₀ (−1): boiling point −50 se oopar, to ye us temperature par ubalne ke point se thande, yaani **liquid**. Jaanch: −162 < −50, −89 < −50 ✅; −42 > −50, −1 > −50 ✅.
9. CH₄ = 16, moles = 9.6 ÷ 16 = **0.6 mol**. Ratio 1 : 1 : 1 : 1. Cl₂ = 0.6 × 71 = **42.6 g**; CH₃Cl = 0.6 × 50.5 = **30.3 g**; HCl = 0.6 × 36.5 = **21.9 g**. Jaanch: 9.6 + 42.6 = 52.2 g; 30.3 + 21.9 = 52.2 g ✅.
10. C₄H₁₀: n = 4, 2n + 2 = 10 ✅; C₈H₁₈: n = 8, 2n + 2 = 18 ✅. Masses 4 × 12 + 10 = **58** aur 8 × 12 + 18 = **114**. Farq = 114 − 58 = **56** = 4 × 14, yaani **4 CH₂ units** ✅.
11. (a) **False** ❌: alkane **saturated** hain; ve isliye jalte hain ki carbon aur hydrogen oxygen se mil sakte hain. (b) **True** ✅: peeli lau kam oxygen ka sanket hai. (c) **False** ❌: ye **substitution** hai (ek H ki jagah Cl). (d) **False** ❌: methane hawa se halki hai, **oopar** jama hoti hai; LPG neeche. (e) **False** ❌: CO bina gandh ki hai, isliye chup-chaap khatarnak.
12. "Saturated" ka matlab saare bond single hain, ye **kam reactive** hota hai, **bilkul unreactive** nahi. Do reactions: (i) jalna: CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O (ethane bhi jalti hai, Q3); (ii) substitution: CH₄ + Cl₂ → CH₃Cl + HCl (sunlight). Isliye ethane unreactive nahi.
13. Steps: (1) koi switch, lighter ya maachis nahi; (2) khidki-darwaze kholo; (3) regulator band karo (agar safely ho sake) aur bahar jaao; (4) bade ya gas agency ko batao. Switch isliye nahi kyunki switch ki chingari gas-hawa ke mix ko jala sakti hai. Methane M = 16 < 29, halka, to **ceiling ke paas**; LPG (44 > 29) bhaari, neeche.
14. Butane ka boiling point −1 °C hai. −8 °C us se thanda hai, isliye lighter ke andar ki butane **ubalti nahi**, kam gas banti hai aur lau kamzor ya nahi hoti. Sardi mein lighter ya cylinder ko aag ya garmi se garam karne ki koshish kabhi nahi karni (dhamaka): bade bas halka gunguna kamra ya thodi der ruk jaayen.
