# Topic 5: Alkenes Aur Alkynes: Double Aur Triple Bond

Topic 4 mein hamne dekha ki alkane "bhare hue" (saturated) hydrocarbon hain: har carbon ke chaaron haath single bond mein laga hai, isliye wo kam reactive hain (haan, jalte zaroor hain). Ab sawaal ulta hai: agar do carbon ke beech **ek ki jagah do ya teen jodi** share ho jaye, to kya hoga? Class 9 mein tumne N₂ mein triple bond aur O₂ mein double bond dekha tha. Carbon bhi aisa kar sakta hai, aur tab ek bilkul naya parivaar banta hai: **alkene** (double bond) aur **alkyne** (triple bond). Aaj hum dekhenge ki ye "extra jodi" in gaso ko kaise zyada reactive, zyada garam jalne wala aur plastic banane layak bana deti hai.

## Welder ki workshop aur dustbin ki thaili

Mr. Pitre apni bhatiji Shruti ko gali ke mode par ek **welding workshop** dikhane le gaye. Dukaan ke andar ek kaarigar ne kaala goggle pehna, do cylinder ke paas se ek patli nali wali torch uthayi, aur achanak **chamakdaar neeli-safed lau** nikli jiski taraf dekhna mushkil tha. Loha ke do tukde us lau ke neeche laal hokar pighle aur aapas mein jud gaye. Shruti aur Chacha **kaafi door**, sadak ke kinare khade the, kyunki bachchon ko aisi lau ke paas jaane ki ijazat nahi hoti, aur lau ko seedha dekhna bhi nahi chahiye.

"Chacha, ye lau itni tez kyun hai? Hamare gas stove ki lau to halki neeli hoti hai!" Mr. Pitre bole, "Is torch mein **ethyne** gas jalti hai, shuddh oxygen ke saath. Ethyne ke do carbon ke beech **teen bond** hain, aur wahi gas ko itna garam jalne wala banata hai." Shruti ne poochha, "Aur ye cylinder mein alag-alag nishaan kyun hain?" "Kyunki ek mein jalne wali gas hai aur ek mein oxygen: dono ko mila kar hi ye lau banti hai. Ye kaam sirf prashikshit log karte hain."

Wapas aate waqt Shruti ne dekha ki ek aadmi ne ek khaali **plastic (polythene) ki thaili** dustbin ke paas rakhi hai aur uske saath kuch kachra jalane ki baat kar raha hai. Mr. Pitre turant ruke. "Ruko bhai! Plastic mat jalao, uska dhuan zehreela hota hai." Phir Shruti se bole, "Jaanti ho, ye thaili bhi ek gas se bani hai? Ek aisi gas jiske carbon ke beech **do bond** hain: **ethene**. Wahi ek gas welding wali ethyne ke bhai-behen jaisi hai. Aaj chalo, ek hi dhaage se dono kahaniyan samajhte hain."

## Suraksha (pehle ye padho)

> **Suraksha: is topic ke gas aur chemicals**
> - **Ethene aur ethyne** jwalansheel (bahut aasani se jalne wali) gas hain. Hawa ke saath inka mixture jaldi dhamaake se jal sakta hai. Inke paas **koi aag ya chingaari nahi**.
> - **Gaadha H₂SO₄** bahut corrosive hai (Chapter 9, Topic 5). **Calcium carbide** paani se milkar garmi aur jwalansheel ethyne deta hai. **Bromine aur bromine water** zehreele aur corrosive hain, unki bhaap saans ko jalati hai.
> - Isliye is topic ka **har reaction sirf padhne ke liye ya sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo)** hai: fume cupboard, goggles, gloves, door khade bachche, aur paas koi naked flame nahi. **Koi student ye ghar par ya akele kabhi nahi karega.**
> - **Plastic ko kabhi nahi jalana:** uska dhuan zehreela hota hai. Aur "carbide gun" jaisi cheezein ya carbide se kuch banane-chalane wale kaam **kabhi nahi**: ye chot aur aankh ki andhta tak kar sakte hain.
> - Cylinder, welding ya gas ka koi kaam sirf bade, prashikshit log karte hain. Kisi gas ya jale ka sandeh ho to **turant bade ko batao**.

## Part A: Extra jodi ka matlab: unsaturated

Class 9 mein tumne dekha tha ki jab dono atom ko electron chahiye to wo jodi **share** karte hain: O₂ mein do jodi (O=O), N₂ mein teen jodi (N≡N). Carbon ke chaar haath hain. Agar do carbon aapas mein **do jodi** share karen to unke paas baaki hydrogen ke liye sirf **do-do haath** bachte hain. Agar **teen jodi** share karen to **ek-ek haath** bachta hai. Isliye jaise-jaise carbon-carbon bond badhta hai, hydrogen ki ginti ghatti hai.

```
   Ethane C₂H₆ (single)    Ethene C₂H₄ (double)     Ethyne C₂H₂ (triple)

       H   H                 H         H
       :   :                  \       /
   H - C - C - H               C  =  C               H - C ≡ C - H
       :   :                  /       \
       H   H                 H         H
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Ethane, ethene aur ethyne ki structure. Har C ke chaar bond ginti mein (ethane mein har C: 3 H + 1 C-C = 4; ethene mein har C: 2 H + double bond ke 2 = 4; ethyne mein har C: 1 H + triple bond ke 3 = 4) aur har H ka ek bond. Jaise-jaise C-C bond badhta hai, hydrogen 6 se 4 se 2 ho jaate hain.

Dhyan do: ethene aur ethyne mein bhi carbon ke chaar haath **poore** hain, kuch bhi "adhura" nahi. Phir inhe unsaturated kyun kehte hain? Isliye ki carbon ke saath **aur hydrogen** judne ki jagah hai: agar double bond ki ek jodi khul jaye to har C ko ek-ek naya haath mil jata hai. Wahi "bharne ki jagah" inhe zyada reactive banati hai (Part E).

```
   Ethene                           Ethyne

      H     H
      :     :
      C :: C                        H : C ::: C : H
      :     :
      H     H
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Electron-jodi ki tasveer. Har ":" ek shared jodi (2 electron) hai. Ethene mein har C ke paas 4 jodi: 2 H ke saath aur 2 doosre C ke saath, yaani octet (8). Ethyne mein har C: 1 H ke saath aur 3 doosre C ke saath = 4 jodi = 8. Har H ke paas 1 jodi = 2 electron.

### Example 1. Ethene mein ginti

**Sawaal:** C₂H₄ ke figure mein har atom ki valency gino. Kul kitne bond hain?
**Hal:** Har C: 2 H ke saath 2 single bond + C=C ke 2 = **4** ✅. Har H: 1 bond = **1** ✅. Kul bond ginti: C–H 4, C=C do jodi = 2. Kul **6** jodi.
**Jaanch:** valency ka jod = 2 × 4 + 4 × 1 = 12. Har bond do atom ko chhoota hai, to bond ginti = 12 ÷ 2 = **6** ✅.

### Example 2. Ethyne mein ginti

**Sawaal:** C₂H₂ mein kul kitne bond? Har atom ki valency?
**Hal:** Har C: 1 H ke saath 1 + C≡C ke 3 = **4** ✅. Har H: 1. Bond: C–H 2 + triple 3 = **5**.
**Jaanch:** 2 × 4 + 2 × 1 = 10, 10 ÷ 2 = **5** ✅. Aur ethane C₂H₆ mein 2 × 4 + 6 = 14, 14 ÷ 2 = **7** bond. Is tarah bond ginti 7 → 6 → 5 gir rahi hai kyunki C-C ki jodi badhi par hydrogen ghate.

### Alkene aur alkyne parivaar

Topic 2 ka "gaadi ke dibbe" wala vichar yahan bhi chalta hai: har agla member **CH₂** zyada (mass 14), aur general formula wahi rehta hai. Double bond ke liye kam se kam **do carbon** chahiye, isliye "methene" naam ka koi alkene nahi hota.

| Parivaar | General formula | Pehla member | Doosra | Teesra |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alkene (C=C) | CₙH₂ₙ | ethene C₂H₄ | propene C₃H₆ | butene C₄H₈ (but-1-ene aur but-2-ene) |
| Alkyne (C≡C) | CₙH₂ₙ₋₂ | ethyne C₂H₂ | propyne C₃H₄ | butyne C₄H₆ |
| Alkane (single, tulna ke liye) | CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ | methane CH₄ | ethane C₂H₆ | propane C₃H₈ |

Naam ka niyam Topic 3 se: pehle carbon ki ginti ka prefix, phir **-ene** (double bond) ya **-yne** (triple bond). Aur ek chhota kaam: ethane se ethene banane mein **2 hydrogen** ghate, ethene se ethyne banane mein phir **2 hydrogen** ghate.

## Part B: Banane ke tareeke (sirf padhne ke liye, teacher demo)

### Ethene: ethanol ka paani chheen kar

Chapter 9 mein tumne dekha tha ki gaadha H₂SO₄ ek **dehydrating agent** hai: kisi padarth se paani chheen leta hai (cheeni se paani nikal kar carbon bacha deta hai). Wahi swabhaav yahan kaam aata hai. **Ethanol** (C₂H₅OH, jisse Topic 6 mein poori tarah milenge) ko gaadhe H₂SO₄ ke saath lagbhag **170 °C** tak garam karne par uske **OH aur ek H** paani ke roop mein nikal jaate hain, aur do carbon ke beech **double bond** ban jaata hai:

C₂H₅OH → C₂H₄ + H₂O (gaadha H₂SO₄, lagbhag 170 °C)

```
              thermometer
                   :
              +----:----+   cork
              :    :    :===================+   delivery tube
              +----:----+                   :
                :  :  :                     :
             ,--'  :  '--.                  :             gas jar (ulta,
            /      :      \                 :             paani se bhara)
           :  ethanol +    :                :              +---------+
           :  gaadha H₂SO₄ :                :              :  ethene :
           :   (bulb) *    :                :              :  (gas)  :
            \             /                 :              :         :
             `-----------'       ~~~~~~~~~~~:~~~~~~~~~~~~~~:~~~~~~~~~:~~~~
                   ^             ~~~~~~~~~~~:~~~~~~~~~~~~~~:~~~~~~~~~:~~~~
               tez aanch         ~~~~~~~~~~~'------------------>  ~~~~~~~~
               (teacher)         ~~~~~~~~ trough ka paani ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Ethene ki lab preparation (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo, fume cupboard mein): flask mein ethanol aur gaadha H₂SO₄ ko cork se jude thermometer (bulb liquid mein) se lagbhag 170 °C par rakha jaata hai. Ek delivery tube gas ko trough ke paani mein le jaati hai, jahan ulte gas jar mein ethene paani ko neeche dhakelti hai (ethene paani mein nahi ghulti). **Suck-back:** heating rokne se pehle delivery tube ko paani se bahar nikaal liya jaata hai. Apparatus band nahi, tube khuli hai. Asli set mein SO₂ aur CO₂ jaisi ashuddhiyan (acid se kuch ethanol ke jalne aur kaala padne se bani) ek NaOH wash bottle mein rok li jaati hain; yahan drawing saral rakhi hai.

Is demo mein gaadha acid kuch ethanol ko jala bhi deta hai, to kaala dhuan aur tez gandh wali SO₂ bhi banti hai. Isliye ye **fume cupboard** ke bina kabhi nahi hota (Chapter 9, Topic 5).

### Example 3. Ethanol se ethene ka hisaab

**Sawaal:** Teacher demo mein 4.6 g ethanol poora C₂H₄ aur H₂O mein badal gaya. Kitna ethene (g aur STP par L) aur kitna paani bana? (H 1, C 12, O 16.)
**Hal:** C₂H₅OH ka M = 24 + 6 + 16 = **46**. Moles = 4.6 ÷ 46 = **0.1 mol**. Ratio 1 : 1 : 1. Ethene = 0.1 × 28 = **2.8 g**, aur 0.1 × 22.4 = **2.24 L**. Paani = 0.1 × 18 = **1.8 g**.
**Jaanch:** 2.8 + 1.8 = 4.6 g ✅. Atoms: C₂H₆O = C₂H₄ + H₂O: C 2 = 2, H 6 = 4 + 2, O 1 = 1 ✅.

### Ethyne: calcium carbide aur paani

Doosra tareeka bilkul alag hai: **calcium carbide (CaC₂)** paani se milte hi tez reaction karta hai aur ethyne gas deta hai:

CaC₂ + 2H₂O → C₂H₂ + Ca(OH)₂

```
        dropping funnel (paani, boond-boond)
               :
          +----:----+  cork
          :    v    :==================+   delivery tube
          +----:----+                  :
             :   :                     :            gas jar (ulta,
         ,---'   '---.                 :            paani se bhara)
        /   CaC₂ ke    \               :             +---------+
       :    tukde       :              :             :  ethyne :
        \              /               :             :  (gas)  :
         `------------'    ~~~~~~~~~~~~:~~~~~~~~~~~~~:~~~~~~~~~:~~~~
                           ~~~~~~~~~~~~:~~~~~~~~~~~~~:~~~~~~~~~:~~~~
           koi aag nahi    ~~~~~~~~~~~~'------------------>  ~~~~~~~
           aas-paas        ~~~~~~~~ trough ka paani ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
```

> **Figure 5.4** – Ethyne ki preparation (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo): flask ke cork mein ek dropping funnel (paani boond-boond) aur ek delivery tube hai. CaC₂ ke tukdon par paani girne se ethyne bani, jo trough mein ulte gas jar mein paani ko neeche dhakelti hai. Paani ek-ek boond hi daalte hain, kyunki reaction tez aur garam hota hai. Koi aag paas nahi: ethyne bahut jwalansheel hai. Apparatus band nahi.

Industrial carbide mein kuch ashuddhi hoti hai, isliye is gas ki gandh **lahsun jaisi** hoti hai; shuddh ethyne lagbhag bina gandh ki aur bina rang ki gas hai.

### Example 4. Carbide ka hisaab

**Sawaal:** Teacher demo mein 6.4 g CaC₂ poora paani ke saath react hua. Kitna ethyne (g aur STP par L), kitna Ca(OH)₂ aur kitna paani kharch hua? (H 1, C 12, O 16, Ca 40.)
**Hal:** CaC₂ ka M = 40 + 24 = **64**; moles = 6.4 ÷ 64 = **0.1 mol**. Ratio CaC₂ : H₂O : C₂H₂ : Ca(OH)₂ = 1 : 2 : 1 : 1. Ethyne = 0.1 × 26 = **2.6 g**, 0.1 × 22.4 = **2.24 L**. Ca(OH)₂ (M = 40 + 34 = 74) = **7.4 g**. Paani = 0.2 mol × 18 = **3.6 g**.
**Jaanch:** 6.4 + 3.6 = 10.0 g; 2.6 + 7.4 = 10.0 g ✅. Atoms: Ca 1, C 2, H 4, O 2 dono taraf ✅.

Factory mein ethene mukhya taur par petroleum ke bade hydrocarbons ko chhote tukdon mein tod kar (cracking naam ka kaam) milti hai; yahan hum sirf lab ke do tareeke dekh rahe hain.

## Part C: Bhautik gun aur jalna: sooty lau kyun

Ethene aur ethyne dono **bina rang ki gas** hain, paani mein kam ghulti hain (isi liye paani ke upar collect hoti hain). Ye hydrocarbon hain to jalte hain (Topic 4 jaise):

C₂H₄ + 3O₂ → 2CO₂ + 2H₂O
2C₂H₂ + 5O₂ → 4CO₂ + 2H₂O

### Example 5. Ethene ka jalna

**Sawaal:** 8.4 g ethene poori tarah jalti hai (STP). Kitna O₂ (L), kitni CO₂ (g) aur kitna paani (g)? (H 1, C 12, O 16.)
**Hal:** n(C₂H₄) = 8.4 ÷ 28 = **0.3 mol**. Ratio C₂H₄ : O₂ : CO₂ : H₂O = 1 : 3 : 2 : 2. O₂ = 0.9 mol × 22.4 = **20.16 L**. CO₂ = 0.6 mol × 44 = **26.4 g**. Paani = 0.6 mol × 18 = **10.8 g**.
**Jaanch:** baayein 8.4 + (0.9 × 32 = 28.8) = 37.2 g; daayein 26.4 + 10.8 = 37.2 g ✅.

### Lau sooty kyun hoti hai?

Topic 4 mein dekha tha ki jab hawa kam padti hai to **incomplete combustion** se soot (kaala carbon) bachta hai. Ab dekho kaun si gas ke andar "carbon ki **bhari bhid**" hai: ek hi gas mein carbon ka hissa jitna zyada, jalne ke liye utni hi zyada oxygen chahiye, aur khuli hawa mein utni hi zyada soot.

### Example 6. Carbon ka percent

**Sawaal:** C₂H₆, C₂H₄ aur C₂H₂ mein carbon ka mass percent nikalo aur batao kiski lau sabse zyada sooty hogi. (H 1, C 12.)
**Hal:**
- C₂H₆: M = 30, carbon = 24 ÷ 30 × 100 = **80 percent**.
- C₂H₄: M = 28, carbon = 24 ÷ 28 × 100 = **85.7 percent**.
- C₂H₂: M = 26, carbon = 24 ÷ 26 × 100 = **92.3 percent**.

Carbon sabse zyada **ethyne** mein, to uski lau khuli hawa mein sabse zyada sooty.
**Jaanch:** har ek mein C + H ka percent = 100 (80 + 20, 85.7 + 14.3, 92.3 + 7.7) ✅.

| Gas | Khuli hawa mein lau | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| Alkane (jaise ethane) | lagbhag saaf, halki neeli (agar hawa kaafi ho) | carbon ka hissa kam |
| Ethene | peeli, thodi sooty | carbon ka hissa zyada |
| Ethyne | peeli, bahut sooty, kaala dhuan | carbon ka hissa sabse zyada |

Ab welder ki torch: wahan ethyne ko **shuddh oxygen** ke saath jalate hain (hawa ke saath nahi). Hawa mein 78 percent nitrogen "bekaar" mein garmi sokh leta hai aur oxygen kam padti hai. Shuddh oxygen ke saath ethyne poori jalti hai, **soot nahi banti**, lau **neeli-safed** ho jaati hai aur uska taapman **3000 °C se bhi upar** pahunchta hai (lagbhag), jo loha pighlane ke liye kaafi hai. Isiliye welding ke liye do cylinder: ek ethyne ka aur ek oxygen ka.

## Part D: Addition reactions: extra jodi khulti hai

Ye is topic ki sabse badi baat hai. Alkane mein har haath bhara hua tha, isliye wo kisi naye atom ko apne beech mein jagah nahi de paate: bas **ek atom hata kar uski jagah doosra** laga sakte hain (substitution, Topic 4). Par ethene mein double bond ki ek jodi khul sakti hai, aur har carbon ko ek naya haath mil jata hai. Naya atom **seedha judta hai**, koi atom nikalta nahi: isse **addition** kehte hain.

### 1. Hydrogen ke saath (nickel catalyst)

Class 9 mein ek jhalak aayi thi ki tel mein hydrogen jod kar vanaspati banta hai ([hydrogen-part5.md](../../../class9/chemistry/hydrogen/hydrogen-part5.md)). Wahi ethene ke liye:

C₂H₄ + H₂ → C₂H₆ (nickel catalyst, garam)

```
     H       H                                 H   H
      \     /                                  :   :
       C = C    +   H - H    ---Ni, garam--->  H - C - C - H
      /     \                                  :   :
     H       H                                 H   H
     ethene      hydrogen                        ethane
```

> **Figure 5.5** – Ethene par hydrogen ka addition: double bond ki ek jodi khulti hai, har C ko ek H milta hai. Ginti: ethene mein har C ke 4 bond, ethane mein bhi har C ke 4 bond (3 H + 1 C-C). Kuch nikla nahi, sirf ek product (ethane) bana.

Ethyne mein do kadam hote hain: pehle **C₂H₂ + H₂ → C₂H₄**, phir **C₂H₄ + H₂ → C₂H₆**. Dono milakar C₂H₂ + 2H₂ → C₂H₆.

### Example 7. Ethyne se ethane

**Sawaal:** 13 g ethyne ko nickel ke saath poora ethane mein badla gaya. Kitna H₂ (STP par L) lagega aur kitna ethane (g) banega?
**Hal:** n(C₂H₂) = 13 ÷ 26 = **0.5 mol**. C₂H₂ + 2H₂ → C₂H₆: ratio 1 : 2 : 1. H₂ = 1.0 mol × 22.4 = **22.4 L**. Ethane = 0.5 mol × 30 = **15 g**.
**Jaanch:** 13 + (1.0 × 2 = 2) = 15 g ✅. Atoms: C 2, H 2 + 4 = 6 ✅.

### 2. Bromine ke saath aur bromine water ki jaanch

Bromine (Br₂) bhi addition karta hai. Ethene ko bromine water (bhoora-narangi ghol) mein guzaarne par:

C₂H₄ + Br₂ → C₂H₄Br₂

Narangi rang **bina rang ka** ho jaata hai, kyunki Br₂ khatam ho gaya aur banne wala dibromo-compound bina rang ka hai. Ethyne mein do Br₂ judte hain:

C₂H₂ + 2Br₂ → C₂H₂Br₄

Alkane (jaise ethane) ke saath bromine water mein andhere mein **koi badlaav nahi**: wahan substitution ke liye tez roshni chahiye aur wo bhi dheere hota hai (Topic 4 ka chlorine wala). Isliye bromine water **unsaturation ki jaanch** hai: rang gaya to double ya triple bond hai.

```
     Test tube A                    Test tube B
     (alkane, jaise ethane)         (ethene)

     gas andar                      gas andar
        :                              :
        :                              :
     +--:--+                        +--:--+
     :  :  :                        :  :  :
     : o:o :  <- bulbule            : o:o :  <- bulbule
     :~~~~~:                        :~~~~~:
     :~~~~~:  narangi (rang         :     :  bina rang ka
     +-----+  nahi badla)           +-----+  (rang gaya)
```

> **Figure 5.6** – Bromine water ki jaanch (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo, fume cupboard, goggles aur gloves ke saath): gas ek delivery tube se bromine water mein jaati hai. Tube A mein alkane ke saath narangi rang nahi badalta. Tube B mein ethene ke saath rang gayab ho jaata hai. Tube khuli hai, band nahi, aur bachi gas fume cupboard se bahar nikalti hai.

Ek chhoti baat: ethene aur ethyne **dono** bromine water ka rang udaate hain, to ye test sirf itna batata hai ki "unsaturated hai ya nahi", ye nahi ki double hai ya triple.

### Example 8. Bromine ka hisaab

**Sawaal:** Teacher demo mein 7.0 g ethene poori bromine ke saath react hui: C₂H₄ + Br₂ → C₂H₄Br₂. Kitna Br₂ (g) kharch hua aur kitna C₂H₄Br₂ bana? (H 1, C 12, Br 80.)
**Hal:** n(C₂H₄) = 7.0 ÷ 28 = **0.25 mol**. Ratio 1 : 1 : 1. Br₂ (M = 160) = 0.25 × 160 = **40 g**. C₂H₄Br₂ (M = 24 + 4 + 160 = 188) = 0.25 × 188 = **47 g**.
**Jaanch:** 7.0 + 40 = 47 g ✅. Atoms: C 2, H 4, Br 2 dono taraf ✅. Aur kuch nikla nahi: ek hi product, addition ka nishaan.

## Part E: Polymerisation: thaili ki kahani

Ethene ke double bond ki ek jodi agar kisi dusre ethene ke saath khule, aur phir uske baad ek aur, aur ek aur, to **hazaron** ethene molecules ek lambi chain mein jud sakte hain. Is kaam ko **polymerisation** kehte hain, aur ethene yahan **monomer** hai. Ban jaane wali chain **polythene** hai (poora naam polyethene):

n C₂H₄ → (–CH₂–CH₂–)ₙ

```
   ethene ke chhote molecules               lambi chain (polythene)

   CH₂=CH₂  CH₂=CH₂  CH₂=CH₂  ...    --->   –CH₂–CH₂–CH₂–CH₂–CH₂–CH₂–  ...

    (har ek ki double bond khulti hai aur dono kinare doosre ethene se jud jaate hain)
```

> **Figure 5.7** – n ethene se polythene: double bond ki ek jodi khulti hai aur har CH₂ unit apne padosi se judta hai, bina kuch nikale. Chain mein har C ke 4 bond hain (2 H + 2 C-C), har H ka 1. Polymerisation bahut oonche dabaav aur catalyst ke saath factory mein hota hai (sirf naam ke liye).

### Example 9. Polythene ki chain

**Sawaal:** Ek polythene chain mein 500 ethene units judi hain. Chain ka formula, uska mass aur ye ek ethene ke mass se kitna guna hai? (H 1, C 12.)
**Hal:** Har unit CH₂–CH₂ = C₂H₄, M = 28. 500 units = C₍₂×₅₀₀₎H₍₄×₅₀₀₎ = **C₁₀₀₀H₂₀₀₀**. Mass = 500 × 28 = **14000** u. Ek ethene (28) se **500 guna**.
**Jaanch:** 1000 × 12 + 2000 × 1 = 14000 ✅.

Polythene halki, majboot, paani ko rokne wali aur bijli na chalne wali hai: thaili, bottle, pipe, taar ki insulation. Par wahi baat uski samasya hai: wo **sadti nahi**. Isliye:
- Polythene ki patli thaili baar-baar kaam mein lo aur kachre ke sahi dabbe mein hi daalo; nalon aur nadiyon mein nahi.
- **Plastic ko kabhi nahi jalana**: dhuan zehreela hota hai aur saans, aankh aur gale ko nuksan pahunchata hai. Yahi Mr. Pitre ne dustbin ke paas rukne ko kaha tha.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti: "Addition aur substitution ek hi tarah ki reaction hain, dono mein koi atom jud hi to jata hai."**
Sach lagta hai kyunki dono mein bahar se atom aate hain. Par farq bahut bada hai. Ek mini kahani: ek bench par chaar bachche baithe hain. **Substitution**: ek bachcha uthta hai aur doosra uski jagah baith jaata hai: bench par chaar ke chaar, aur uthne wala bachcha bahar nikla. **Addition**: bench par ek khaali jagah thi (double bond ki khuli jodi), aur ek naya bachcha seedha baith gaya: bench par ab paanch, aur koi bahar nahi gaya. Ab ginti se dekho:

| Reaction | Reactants | Products | Kuch nikla? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Substitution: CH₄ + Cl₂ → CH₃Cl + HCl | 2 | **2** (CH₃Cl aur HCl) | haan: HCl |
| Addition: C₂H₄ + Br₂ → C₂H₄Br₂ | 2 | **1** (sirf C₂H₄Br₂) | nahi |

Atom ka hisaab: CH₄ ke ek H ki jagah Cl aaya, aur H alag ho kar Cl ke saath HCl bana (C 1, H 4, Cl 2 dono taraf). C₂H₄ mein koi H nahi gaya, Br₂ ke dono Br seedhe do carbon par lag gaye (C 2, H 4, Br 2 dono taraf). Ek aur farq jo tum dekh sakte ho (teacher demo): bromine water ethene mein turant bina rang ka ho gaya, par ethane mein andhere mein badla hi nahi.

**Niyam:** unsaturated (double ya triple bond) = **addition**, ek hi product. Saturated (sirf single bond) = **substitution**, saath mein ek chhota product (jaise HCl).

## Quick Recap

- **Unsaturated** hydrocarbon mein C=C (alkene, CₙH₂ₙ) ya C≡C (alkyne, CₙH₂ₙ₋₂) hota hai; ethene C₂H₄, ethyne C₂H₂, propene C₃H₆, butene C₄H₈ (but-1-ene aur but-2-ene), propyne C₃H₄, butyne C₄H₆.
- Har C ke 4 bond aur har H ka 1 bond hi rehta hai; bas C-C bond badhne se hydrogen ghatte hain (6, 4, 2).
- **Banane ke tareeke (teacher demo):** C₂H₅OH → C₂H₄ + H₂O (gaadha H₂SO₄, lagbhag 170 °C); CaC₂ + 2H₂O → C₂H₂ + Ca(OH)₂.
- **Jalna:** C₂H₄ + 3O₂ → 2CO₂ + 2H₂O; 2C₂H₂ + 5O₂ → 4CO₂ + 2H₂O. Carbon ka hissa zyada = lau sooty; ethyne + shuddh oxygen = welding ki neeli-safed, 3000 °C se upar lau.
- **Addition:** C₂H₄ + H₂ → C₂H₆ (Ni, garam); C₂H₄ + Br₂ → C₂H₄Br₂; C₂H₂ + 2Br₂ → C₂H₂Br₄; C₂H₂ + H₂ → C₂H₄. Bromine water ka rang udna = unsaturation ka test.
- **Polymerisation:** n C₂H₄ → (–CH₂–CH₂–)ₙ polythene. Plastic kabhi nahi jalana.
- **Galti:** addition mein ek product, kuch nikalta nahi; substitution mein ek chhota product bhi (jaise HCl).
- **Suraksha:** har reaction sirf teacher demo; koi flame, carbide, bromine ya gaadha acid student ke liye nahi.

## Practice Questions

(Sab sawaal paper par hal karne ke liye hain. Mass: H 1, C 12, O 16, Ca 40, Br 80. Molar volume 22.4 L at STP.)

1. Draw the structure of propene, C₃H₆, showing every bond, and write its condensed formula. Count the bonds on each carbon to show that every carbon has valency 4.
2. In a teacher demo, bromine adds to but-1-ene (CH₂=CH–CH₂–CH₃) and, separately, to but-2-ene (CH₃–CH=CH–CH₃). Write the condensed formula of each product, check that every carbon has valency 4 in the products, and say whether the two products are the same compound.
3. Draw propyne, C₃H₄, with every bond shown. Find the total number of bonds using the valency-sum method and check it against your drawing.
4. State whether each is saturated or unsaturated and which family it belongs to, using the hydrogen count: C₃H₆, C₂H₂, C₄H₈, C₃H₄, C₂H₄. (Use CₙH₂ₙ₊₂, CₙH₂ₙ and CₙH₂ₙ₋₂.)
5. A teacher demo burns 8 L of propene gas completely in oxygen: 2C₃H₆ + 9O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O. Check that the equation is balanced. At the same temperature and pressure, find the volume of oxygen needed and of CO₂ formed.
6. Butene burns completely: C₄H₈ + 6O₂ → 4CO₂ + 4H₂O. For 14 g of butene, find the mass of CO₂ and of water formed and the mass of oxygen used. Verify using mass conservation.
7. In a teacher demo, 11.2 g of butene reacts with hydrogen over nickel: C₄H₈ + H₂ → C₄H₁₀. Find the volume of hydrogen at STP and the mass of the product. Do the two isomers of C₄H₈ give the same product?
8. In a teacher demo, 16 g of calcium carbide reacts completely with water: CaC₂ + 2H₂O → C₂H₂ + Ca(OH)₂. Find the volume of ethyne at STP and the mass of Ca(OH)₂. Then, at the same temperature and pressure, find the volume of oxygen needed to burn all this ethyne (2C₂H₂ + 5O₂ → 4CO₂ + 2H₂O).
9. A teacher bubbles 8.0 g of propyne through excess bromine: C₃H₄ + 2Br₂ → C₃H₄Br₄. Find the mass of bromine used and the mass of product. Is this addition or substitution, and why?
10. An unknown gas has the general formula CₙH₂ₙ₋₂. If 0.25 mol of it has a mass of 13.5 g, find n and write its formula and name. Would it decolourise bromine water?
11. A polythene sample has an average molar mass of 56000 u. (a) How many ethene units are in one chain? (b) How many grams of ethene are needed to make 1.4 kg of polythene, and how many moles is that?
12. Classify each as addition, substitution or combustion and give a reason: (a) C₄H₈ + H₂ → C₄H₁₀; (b) CH₄ + Cl₂ → CH₃Cl + HCl; (c) C₂H₂ + H₂ → C₂H₄; (d) C₃H₆ + Br₂ → C₃H₆Br₂; (e) 2C₂H₂ + 5O₂ → 4CO₂ + 2H₂O.
13. Find the percentage of carbon by mass in propyne (C₃H₄) and in butyne (C₄H₆). Which should give the sootier flame in open air, and why?
14. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) Ethene has no hydrogen left to react, so it is saturated. (b) Bromine water stays orange when shaken with an alkane in the dark. (c) Ethyne is burnt in air to get the hottest welding flame. (d) Addition reactions of ethene give two products, one of them small. (e) Burning old polythene bags is a safe way to dispose of them.

### Answers

1. Propene: CH₃–CH=CH₂. Dash form: H–C(H)(H)–C(H)=C(H)(H). Pehla C: 3 H + 1 C-C = 4; beech wala C: 1 H + 1 C-C + C=C ke 2 = 4; aakhri C: 2 H + C=C ke 2 = 4 ✅. Kul bond: valency jod = 3 × 4 + 6 = 18, 18 ÷ 2 = **9** ✅. Condensed formula **CH₃CH=CH₂**.
2. Double bond ki ek jodi khulti hai aur dono Br usi bond ke do carbon par judte hain. But-1-ene se: **CH₂Br–CHBr–CH₂–CH₃**. But-2-ene se: **CH₃–CHBr–CHBr–CH₃**. Valency (pehle product mein): CH₂Br 2 H + 1 Br + 1 C = 4; CHBr 1 H + 1 Br + 2 C = 4; CH₂ 2 H + 2 C = 4; CH₃ 3 H + 1 C = 4 ✅. Doosre mein: CH₃ 3 + 1 = 4; dono CHBr 1 H + 1 Br + 2 C = 4 ✅. Dono ka formula C₄H₈Br₂ hai, par Br alag carbon par hain: **alag compound** (isomers), kyunki shuru ke double bond ki jagah alag thi. Jaanch: C 4, H 8, Br 2 dono mein ✅.
3. Propyne: **CH₃–C≡CH**. Pehla C: 3 H + 1 C-C = 4; beech wala C: 1 + 3 = 4; aakhri C: 3 (triple) + 1 H = 4 ✅. Valency jod = 3 × 4 + 4 × 1 = 16, 16 ÷ 2 = **8** bond. Drawing mein: C–H 4, C–C 1, C≡C 3 = 8 ✅.
4. Saturated ke liye CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ chahiye. C₃H₆: n=3, 2n = 6 → **alkene, unsaturated**. C₂H₂: 2n−2 = 2 → **alkyne, unsaturated**. C₄H₈: 2n = 8 → **alkene, unsaturated**. C₃H₄: 2n−2 = 4 → **alkyne, unsaturated**. C₂H₄: 2n = 4 → **alkene, unsaturated**. (Saturated 3 carbon par 8 hydrogen hota, par yahan sab mein kam hain: saari paanchon unsaturated.)
5. Atoms: C 6 = 6, H 12 = 12, O 18 = 12 + 6 ✅. Gaso ke volume mole ke ratio mein: 2 : 9 : 6. Propene 8 L = "2 hissa", to 1 hissa = 4 L. O₂ = 9 × 4 = **36 L**. CO₂ = 6 × 4 = **24 L**. Jaanch: 36 ÷ 8 = 4.5 aur 9 ÷ 2 = 4.5 ✅.
6. Atoms: C 4 = 4, H 8 = 8, O 12 = 8 + 4 ✅. n(C₄H₈) = 14 ÷ 56 = **0.25 mol**. CO₂ = 1.0 mol × 44 = **44 g**. H₂O = 1.0 mol × 18 = **18 g**. O₂ = 1.5 mol × 32 = **48 g**. Jaanch: baayein 14 + 48 = 62; daayein 44 + 18 = 62 ✅.
7. n(C₄H₈) = 11.2 ÷ 56 = **0.2 mol**. H₂ = 0.2 × 22.4 = **4.48 L**. C₄H₁₀ (M = 48 + 10 = 58) = 0.2 × 58 = **11.6 g**. Jaanch: 11.2 + 0.4 = 11.6 g ✅. Haan, dono isomer **butane** hi dete hain: double bond khulkar dono carbon par H judta hai, to chain ek jaisi saturated ho jaati hai.
8. n(CaC₂) = 16 ÷ 64 = **0.25 mol**. Ethyne = 0.25 × 22.4 = **5.6 L**. Ca(OH)₂ = 0.25 × 74 = **18.5 g**. Oxygen: ratio C₂H₂ : O₂ = 2 : 5, to 5.6 L × 5 ÷ 2 = **14 L**. Jaanch: 16 + 0.5 × 18 = 25 g; 0.25 × 26 + 18.5 = 6.5 + 18.5 = 25 g ✅.
9. M(C₃H₄) = 36 + 4 = **40**; n = 8.0 ÷ 40 = **0.2 mol**. Br₂ = 0.4 mol × 160 = **64 g**. C₃H₄Br₄ (M = 36 + 4 + 320 = 360) = 0.2 × 360 = **72 g**. Jaanch: 8 + 64 = 72 ✅. Ye **addition** hai: do reactant se ek hi product bana, kuch alag nahi nikla, aur triple bond par Br judte gaye.
10. M = 13.5 ÷ 0.25 = **54**. 14n − 2 = 54 → n = **4**. Formula **C₄H₆**, naam **butyne**. Jaanch: 4 × 12 + 6 = 54 ✅. Haan, **bromine water ka rang udega** (unsaturated, triple bond).
11. (a) Ek ethene unit 28, to n = 56000 ÷ 28 = **2000**. (b) Polymerisation mein kuch nikalta nahi, to mass bachta hai: ethene bhi **1400 g**, moles = 1400 ÷ 28 = **50 mol**. Jaanch: 50 × 28 = 1400 ✅.
12. (a) **Addition**: ek alkene (C₄H₈) + H₂ → ek hi product; (b) **Substitution**: Cl ne H ki jagah li, saath mein HCl bana; (c) **Addition**: C≡C par H judta hai, ek hi product (ethene); (d) **Addition**: Br₂ ne double bond par judkar ek hi product diya; (e) **Combustion**: oxygen ke saath jal kar CO₂ aur paani. Ginti: (b) mein 2 product, (a), (c), (d) mein 1 product.
13. Propyne: M = 40, carbon = 36 ÷ 40 × 100 = **90 percent**. Butyne: M = 54, carbon = 48 ÷ 54 × 100 = **88.9 percent**. Propyne mein carbon ka hissa zyada, to uski lau thodi zyada sooty hogi (khuli hawa mein, kyunki carbon ke hisaab se oxygen kam padti hai). Jaanch: H ka percent 10 aur 11.1, jod 100 ✅.
14. (a) **False** ❌: ethene unsaturated hai, kyunki C=C ki ek jodi khul sakti hai aur uspar aur H judte hain. (b) **True** ✅: alkane andhere mein bromine water ke saath nahi badalta. (c) **False** ❌: ethyne ko shuddh oxygen ke saath jalate hain, hawa mein nahi; hawa mein sooty aur kam garam lau. (d) **False** ❌: addition mein ek hi product hota hai, kuch nikalta nahi (do product wala substitution hai). (e) **False** ❌: plastic jalane se zehreela dhuan banta hai, kabhi nahi jalana.
