# Topic 6: Ethanol: Sanitiser Se Fermentation Tak

Ab tak is chapter mein hamne **hydrocarbons** dekhe: sirf C aur H wale (alkanes, alkenes, alkynes). Topic 2 mein functional group ki baat hui thi: ek chhota sa hissa jo poore molecule ka swabhaav tay karta hai. Aaj hum **pehla functional group wala padarth** gehrai se padhenge: **ethanol, C₂H₅OH**, jisme hydrocarbon ke saath **–OH** jud gaya hai. Wahi ek chhota sa hissa isse paani mein ghulne wala, sanitiser banne wala, aur kai dusre padarthon mein badalne wala bana deta hai. Aur kyunki is topic mein ek **zehreela bhai (methanol)** bhi aayega, hum safai aur suraksha ko poora dhyan denge.

## School ke gate par "70 percent"

Mr. Karve apne bhanje Sumedh ko lene uske school gaye. Gate par ek steel stand par sanitiser dispenser laga tha, aur har bachcha andar jaate hue ek pump daba kar haath par thodi si cheez malta tha. Sumedh ne bhi haath par mal li. "Mama, ye thanda kyun lagta hai? Aur dekho, kuch hi second mein haath **sookh** gaya, na gila, na chipchipa!"

Mr. Karve ne dispenser par laga label padha: **"Ethyl alcohol 70 percent v/v. Jwalansheel (flammable). Aag se door rakhein. Peene ke liye nahi."** Sumedh ne bhi padha. "Mama, 70 percent alcohol ka matlab kya? Aur baaki 30 percent kya hai? Sirf 100 percent alcohol kyun nahi daalte, wo aur accha hoga na?"

Mr. Karve hanse. "Teen bade sawaal ek saath. Pehle ye dekho: haath thanda isliye laga ki alcohol **tezi se bhaap banta hai** aur bhaap banne ke liye garmi haath se leta hai. Alcohol ka boiling point paani se kam hai, ye tumne Class 9 mein bhi dekha tha. Aaj hum ye alcohol ka poora khandan dekhenge: ye kya hai, kaise banta hai (cheeni se!), kya kya karta hai, aur **kab bahut khatarnak ho jaata hai**."

"Khatarnak? Sanitiser?" "Sanitiser sahi tareeke se haath par lagaana theek hai. Par ek **bilkul alag, aur dikhne mein usi jaisa** alcohol bhi hota hai jo jaan le leta hai. Uska naam aaj yaad kar lo: **methanol**."

## Suraksha (pehle ye padho)

> **Suraksha: ethanol, methanol aur is topic ke reactions**
> - **Ethanol jwalansheel (flammable) hai:** uski bhaap hawa mein aag se door se bhi pakad leti hai. Sanitiser aur spirit **kabhi diya, gas ya matches ke paas nahi**. Sanitiser lagane ke baad haath sookhne do, tabhi aag ke paas jao.
> - **Methanol zehreela hai.** Kabhi nahi: use sungho, chakho, haath par lagao ya peeo. Dikhne mein ethanol jaisa hi hota hai, isliye bina label wali kisi bhi bottle ko kabhi mat chhuo.
> - **Sanitiser aur spirit peene ke liye nahi.** Aankh mein chale jaye to saaf paani se dhoo kar bade ko batao.
> - **Alcohol ki kisi bhi peene wali cheez ko bachche bilkul nahi.** (Is topic mein hum sirf cheeni ke fermentation ka **chemistry** padhenge.)
> - Is topic ke saare reactions: sodium ke saath, jalana, gaadhe H₂SO₄ ke saath, K₂Cr₂O₇ ke saath aur distillation **sirf padhne ke liye (ya teacher demo)** hain. Sodium aur gaadha H₂SO₄ (Chapters 2 aur 9) khatarnak hain; K₂Cr₂O₇ zehreela hai. Koi student ye kuch nahi karega; sirf teacher, goggles aur fume cupboard ke saath, aur bina khuli aag ke paas ethanol ke.

## Part A: Ethanol ka molecule: ek hydrocarbon aur ek –OH

Ethanol ka molecular formula **C₂H₆O** hai. Topic 2 ke hisaab se ye alcohol series ka dusra member hai: **C₂H₅OH**, yaani ethane (C₂H₆) ka ek H hata kar uski jagah **–OH** laga hua. Topic 3 mein tumne dekha tha ki C₂H₆O ke do isomers hote hain: **ethanol** aur **methoxymethane (CH₃OCH₃)**. Aaj ka padarth pehla wala hai. Structure figure 6.1 mein hai: **har C ki valency 4 aur har H ki 1**, gin kar dekho.

```
         H   H
         I   I
    H -- C - C -- O -- H
         I   I
         H   H
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Ethanol (C₂H₅OH) ka structure. Pehla C: teen H aur ek C = 4 bond. Dusra C: do H, ek C aur ek O = 4 bond. O: ek C aur ek H = 2 bond. Kul H = 6 (5 C par, 1 O par): C₂H₆O.

Ye O–H wala hissa polar hai: O ki electronegativity lagbhag 3.5 aur H ki lagbhag 2.1, farq **1.4** ([chemical-bonding-ii-part5.md](../chemical-bonding-ii/chemical-bonding-ii-part5.md) ne yahi tuka bataya tha). Baaki C–H aur C–C lagbhag non-polar hain. To ethanol ke molecule ke **do hissa** hain:

```
       C₂H₅ --------- O -- H
       :                 :
       non-polar         polar –OH
       hissa             hissa (paani se mel)
```

> **Figure 6.2** – Ethanol ke molecule ke do hissa: C₂H₅ wala non-polar hissa aur –OH wala polar hissa. Polar hissa paani ke polar molecules ke saath mel khata hai ("jaisa jaise se ghulta hai"), isliye ethanol paani mein **kisi bhi anupaat mein** mil jaata hai (Topic 8 mein ek aur two-ended molecule aayega: sabun).

### Alcohol series ke teen pehle member

```
      H                  H   H                  H   H   H
      I                  I   I                  I   I   I
 H -- C -- O -- H   H -- C - C -- O -- H   H -- C - C - C -- O -- H
      I                  I   I                  I   I   I
      H                  H   H                  H   H   H
```

> **Figure 6.3** – Baayen se daayen: methanol (CH₃OH), ethanol (C₂H₅OH) aur propanol (C₃H₇OH, propan-1-ol). Har C par chaar bond aur har H par ek bond. Har agle member mein ek CH₂ zyada hai (mass 14): ye homologous series ka wahi pattern hai jo Topic 2 mein dekha tha.

Dekho: methanol ka M = 12 + 4 + 16 = **32**, ethanol **46**, propanol **60**: har kadam par **14** ka farq. Boiling point bhi isi kram mein badhta hai: methanol lagbhag **65 °C**, ethanol lagbhag **78 °C**, paani 100 °C, aur chain badhne par boiling point badhta hai (Topic 2).

### Ethanol ke physical gun

| Gun | Ethanol |
|---|---|
| Rang aur gandh | bina rang ka liquid, tez khaas gandh |
| Boiling point | lagbhag 78 °C (paani se kam, isliye jaldi udta hai) |
| Melting point | lagbhag −114 °C |
| Paani mein | **kisi bhi anupaat mein mil jaata hai** (miscible) |
| Bijli | **nahi chalata** (covalent, ion nahi) |
| Litmus par | koi asar nahi: **neutral** |
| Jalna | jwalansheel; neeli, lagbhag dhuen rahit lau |

### Example 1. "70 percent" ka hisaab

**Sawaal:** Ek sanitiser ki 250 mL ki bottle par likha hai "70 percent v/v ethyl alcohol". Isme kitna ethanol aur kitna paani (baaki) hai? (Ye "v/v" ka matlab hai **volume mein percent**.)

**Kadam 1:** 70 percent v/v matlab 100 mL ghol mein lagbhag **70 mL ethanol**.
**Kadam 2:** 250 mL mein ethanol = 250 × 70 ÷ 100 = **175 mL**.
**Kadam 3:** baaki = 250 − 175 = **75 mL** (paani aur thoda sa aur kuch).

**Jaanch:** 175 ÷ 250 = 0.70 = 70 percent ✅. (Class 9 mein dekha tha ki paani aur alcohol milane par total volume thoda kam aata hai, isliye ye hisaab **lagbhag** hai.)

## Part B: Ethanol kaise banta hai?

### Tareeka 1: Fermentation (cheeni se, yeast ke saath)

Cheeni (glucose) wale ghol mein **yeast** daali jaye to yeast ke **enzymes** glucose ko todkar **ethanol aur CO₂** bana dete hain:

C₆H₁₂O₆ → 2C₂H₅OH + 2CO₂ (yeast ke enzymes, lagbhag 30 se 35 °C)

Atom check: baayein C 6, H 12, O 6. Daayein C = 2 × 2 + 2 = 6, H = 2 × 6 = 12, O = 2 + 4 = 6 ✅.

Teen cheezein dhyan do:
- **Temperature:** lagbhag **30 se 35 °C** sabse achha. Bahut thanda to yeast sust, **bahut garam to yeast ke enzymes kharab ho jaate hain** aur kuch nahi banta (Class 9 mein aate ke phoolne mein dekha tha ki garam jagah par yeast tez kaam karta hai; par bahut zyada garmi uske enzymes ko bigaad deti hai).
- **CO₂ bhi banti hai:** wahi bulbule aur wahi jhaag. Ye gas chuna paani (lime water) ko doodhiya kar deti hai (Class 9).
- **Cheeni kahan se?** ganne ka ras aur uska **molasses** (cheeni banane ka bachha hua gaadha ras), anaaj, aur phal ka ras: sab mein cheeni hai.

```
          delivery tube (khuli nali)
        .----------------------.
        :                      :
     [cork]                    :
      :  :                  :  :       :   <-- khula beaker
      :  :                  :~~:~~~~~~~:   <-- chuna paani (lime water)
      :  :                  :~~o~~~~~~~:       CO₂ se doodhiya hota hai
   ___:  :___               :~~~~~~~~~~:
  /          \              +----------+
 :  cheeni + :
 :  yeast ka :   gunguna kamra, lagbhag 30 se 35 °C
 :  ghol     :
  \__________/
```

> **Figure 6.4** – Fermentation ka teacher demo (sirf dekhne ke liye): cheeni aur yeast ka ghol flask mein, cork se judi delivery tube ka sira chuna paani mein dooba hua. Bani CO₂ nali se chuna paani mein jaati hai aur use doodhiya karti hai. Apparatus kasa hua band nahi hai: nali CO₂ ko bahar nikalne ka raasta deti hai. Ethanol kuch nahi nikalta, wo ghol mein rehta hai.

### Example 2. Cheeni se kitna ethanol?

**Sawaal:** Ek teacher demo mein 90 g glucose poori tarah fermentation se ethanol aur CO₂ mein badalta hai. Kitna ethanol aur CO₂ (mass) bana, aur CO₂ ka STP par volume kitna? (H 1, C 12, O 16; molar volume 22.4 L.)

**Kadam 1:** glucose C₆H₁₂O₆ = 72 + 12 + 96 = **180** g/mol. Moles = 90 ÷ 180 = **0.5 mol**.
**Kadam 2:** ratio glucose : ethanol : CO₂ = 1 : 2 : 2. To ethanol = 1.0 mol, CO₂ = 1.0 mol.
**Kadam 3:** ethanol = 1.0 × 46 = **46 g**. CO₂ = 1.0 × 44 = **44 g**, volume = 1.0 × 22.4 = **22.4 L**.

**Jaanch:** 46 + 44 = 90 g = glucose ka mass ✅. (Asli fermentation mein poora glucose nahi badalta; ye adarsh hisaab hai. 46 g ethanol ka volume lagbhag 58 mL hai, kyunki ethanol ka density lagbhag 0.79 g/mL hai: 46 ÷ 0.79 ≈ 58.)

### Fermentation se milne wale ethanol ko "shuddh" kaise karte hain?

Fermentation ke baad ghol mein ethanol **kam** (lagbhag kuch percent) aur paani **bahut** hota hai. Ek aur dikkat: ethanol ki lagbhag ek had se zyada matra hone par yeast khud mar jaati hai. Isliye ghol se ethanol alag karne ke liye **distillation** hoti hai: ethanol **78 °C** par aur paani **100 °C** par ubalte hain, to garam karne par pehle zyada ethanol wali bhaap nikalti hai, jo thandi hokar boondon mein badal jaati hai.

```
        thermometer
             :
          +--+--+
          :cork :-----bhaap---->\
   _______:     :_______         \_____________________
  /        +---+       \           condenser (thanda paani ka jacket)
 :  ethanol + paani     :           paani BAHAR  ^   (upar wali nali)
 :  (kuch boiling       :                       :
 :   chips)             :   nali thodi dhalan se neeche:
  \___________________/           paani ANDAR  :   (neeche wali nali)
   ~ garam paani ka bath ~                       :
   (bijli ka garam karne wala,                    v
    khuli aag nahi)                          [ receiver: khula ]
                                             (boondein ethanol-zyada)
```

> **Figure 6.5** – Distillation (sirf padhne ke liye, teacher demo): flask garam paani ke bath mein hai (ethanol ke paas khuli aag nahi). Thermometer ka bulb side-nali ke saamne hai, taaki bhaap ka temperature naapa jaaye. Condenser mein thanda paani **neeche se andar aata aur upar se bahar jaata hai**, aur condenser dhalan se neeche ki taraf hai. Receiver khula hai (band nahi). Bhaap ethanol-zyada hoti hai kyunki ethanol zyada udta hai.

Is tareeke se bhi ethanol lagbhag 95 percent se zyada seedhe nahi aata (uske liye alag tareeke aage ki class mein). Usse "absolute" (lagbhag 100 percent) bhi kehte hain jab bahut shuddh ho.

### Tareeka 2: Ethene se (sirf naam)

Industry mein ethene (C₂H₄) ko bhaap (paani) ke saath ek acid catalyst par, ooncha temperature aur pressure par milakar bhi ethanol banate hain:

C₂H₄ + H₂O → C₂H₅OH (acid catalyst, ooncha temperature aur pressure)

Ye ek **addition reaction** hai (Topic 5): C=C bond khulta hai aur H aur OH judte hain. Atom check: C 2 = 2, H 4 + 2 = 6 = 6, O 1 = 1 ✅.

| Tulna | Fermentation | Ethene se banana |
|---|---|---|
| Kaccha maal | cheeni (ganna, anaaj): **naya hone wala** (renewable) | ethene: aksar petroleum se |
| Condition | halka, lagbhag 30 se 35 °C, jeev (yeast) | ooncha temperature aur pressure, catalyst |
| Product | ethanol thoda aur paani bahut | zyada shuddh ethanol |
| Aur kya nikalta hai | CO₂ | kuch nahi |

## Part C: Ethanol ke reactions (sirf padhne ke liye)

```
                       C₂H₄ + H₂O
                            ^
        dehydration: conc. H₂SO₄,
        lagbhag 170 °C      :
                            :
   C₂H₅ONa + H₂  <---  C₂H₅OH  --------->  CO₂ + H₂O
    (sodium ke saath)      :     (jalna: oxygen ke saath)
                           :
                           v
                    CH₃COOH + H₂O
                 (oxidation: [O] dene wale se)
```

> **Figure 6.6** – Ethanol ke chaar raaste. Upar: dehydration se ethene. Baayen: sodium se sodium ethoxide aur hydrogen. Daayen: jalne par CO₂ aur paani. Neeche: oxidation se ethanoic acid (Topic 7).

### 1. Jalna (combustion)

C₂H₅OH + 3O₂ → 2CO₂ + 3H₂O

Ethanol **neeli, lagbhag saaf lau** ke saath jalta hai aur bahut garmi deta hai. Isliye spirit lamp mein aur petrol ke saath milane (blend karne) mein bhi iska upyog hota hai. Atom check: C 2 = 2, H 6 = 6, O 1 + 6 = 7 = 4 + 3 ✅.

**Example 3. 9.2 g ethanol ka jalna**
**Sawaal:** 9.2 g ethanol poora jalta hai. Kitni oxygen lagi, kitni CO₂ aur kitna paani bana? CO₂ ka volume STP par? (H 1, C 12, O 16.)

**Kadam 1:** ethanol = 24 + 6 + 16 = **46** g/mol. Moles = 9.2 ÷ 46 = **0.2 mol**.
**Kadam 2:** ratio ethanol : O₂ : CO₂ : H₂O = 1 : 3 : 2 : 3. To O₂ = 0.6 mol, CO₂ = 0.4 mol, H₂O = 0.6 mol.
**Kadam 3:** O₂ = 0.6 × 32 = **19.2 g**, CO₂ = 0.4 × 44 = **17.6 g** (volume = 0.4 × 22.4 = **8.96 L**), H₂O = 0.6 × 18 = **10.8 g**.

**Jaanch:** baayein 9.2 + 19.2 = 28.4 g; daayein 17.6 + 10.8 = 28.4 g ✅.

### 2. Sodium ke saath (teacher demo, sirf padhne ke liye)

Sodium ek bahut reactive dhatu hai (Chapter 2): kerosene mein rakhte hain, aur sirf teacher sambhalta hai. **Bilkul sookhe** ethanol mein sodium ka chhota tukda daalne par **hydrogen** ke bulbule aate hain aur **sodium ethoxide** banta hai:

2C₂H₅OH + 2Na → 2C₂H₅ONa + H₂

Atom check: C 4 = 4, H 12 = 10 + 2, O 2 = 2, Na 2 = 2 ✅. Dhyan do: ethanol ke **6 H mein se sirf ek (O se jaka)** sodium se badalta hai, baaki 5 C se jude rehte hain. Ye reaction paani ke saath sodium se **dheema** hai, aur nikli hydrogen ka test wahi hai jo Class 9 mein dekha tha: jalti tili paas lane par "pop".

**Suraksha:** sodium khuli aag ya paani ke paas nahi; ethanol jwalansheel hai, isliye ye teacher demo bahut chhote paimane par hota hai, kaanch ke sash ke peeche, goggles ke saath. Student kabhi nahi.

**Example 4. 2.3 g sodium**
**Sawaal:** 2.3 g sodium ethanol ke saath poora react hota hai. Kitna ethanol chahiye, kitni H₂ (STP) aur kitna sodium ethoxide banega? (Na 23.)

**Kadam 1:** Na = 2.3 ÷ 23 = **0.1 mol**. Ratio Na : ethanol : ethoxide : H₂ = 2 : 2 : 2 : 1.
**Kadam 2:** ethanol = 0.1 mol × 46 = **4.6 g**. C₂H₅ONa = 24 + 5 + 16 + 23 = **68**, to 0.1 × 68 = **6.8 g**. H₂ = 0.05 mol, **1.12 L** (0.05 × 22.4).

**Jaanch:** baayein 2.3 + 4.6 = 6.9 g; daayein 6.8 + (0.05 × 2 = 0.1) = 6.9 g ✅.

### 3. Oxidation: ethanol se ethanoic acid

Oxidising agent (jaise **acidified potassium dichromate, K₂Cr₂O₇**, ya **alkaline potassium permanganate, KMnO₄**) ethanol ko **ethanoic acid** mein badal deta hai. Equation mein oxygen ko [O] likhte hain (oxidising agent se aaya hua):

C₂H₅OH + 2[O] → CH₃COOH + H₂O

Atom check: baayein C 2, H 6, O 1 + 2 = 3; daayein C 2, H 4 + 2 = 6, O 2 + 1 = 3 ✅.

Teacher demo mein **K₂Cr₂O₇ ka naarangi ghol hare rang ka ho jaata hai** (jaise oxidising agent kharch hua), aur KMnO₄ ka **baingani rang gayab** hota hai. K₂Cr₂O₇ **zehreela** hai (chromium ka yaugik), isliye sirf teacher, gloves aur goggles ke saath. Bina oxidising agent ke bhi ethanol wale ras ko **khula chhod do** to hawa ke bacteria ethanol ko dheere dheere ethanoic acid mein badal dete hain (sirka banne ka ek raasta); Topic 7 mein hum yahin se aage badhenge.

**Example 5. 4.6 g ethanol ka oxidation**
**Sawaal:** 4.6 g ethanol poora oxidise hota hai. Kitna ethanoic acid aur kitna paani banta hai, aur kitna [O] lagta hai? (CH₃COOH = 60.)

**Kadam 1:** ethanol = 4.6 ÷ 46 = **0.1 mol**. Ratio ethanol : [O] : CH₃COOH : H₂O = 1 : 2 : 1 : 1.
**Kadam 2:** CH₃COOH = 0.1 × 60 = **6.0 g**, H₂O = 0.1 × 18 = **1.8 g**, [O] = 0.2 mol × 16 = **3.2 g**.

**Jaanch:** baayein 4.6 + 3.2 = 7.8 g; daayein 6.0 + 1.8 = 7.8 g ✅.

### 4. Dehydration: ethanol se ethene

Topic 5 mein ethene ki preparation ke taur par ye dekha tha. Ab ethanol ki taraf se: **gaadha H₂SO₄** (dehydrating agent, Chapter 9) ethanol ko lagbhag **170 °C** par garam karne par uska paani chheen leta hai:

C₂H₅OH → C₂H₄ + H₂O (gaadha H₂SO₄, lagbhag 170 °C)

Atom check: C 2 = 2, H 6 = 4 + 2, O 1 = 1 ✅. Yahan gaadha H₂SO₄ sirf paani chheenta hai (jaise cheeni ke demo mein). Temperature mahatvapurn hai: kam temperature par kuch aur banta hai (aage ki class mein). **Teacher demo, fume cupboard mein**: gaadha H₂SO₄ bahut corrosive hai aur ethanol jwalansheel.

**Example 6. 11.5 g ethanol ka dehydration**
**Sawaal:** 11.5 g ethanol ka poora dehydration hota hai. Kitni ethene (mass aur STP volume) aur kitna paani bana? (C₂H₄ = 28.)

**Kadam 1:** ethanol = 11.5 ÷ 46 = **0.25 mol**. Ratio 1 : 1 : 1.
**Kadam 2:** ethene = 0.25 × 28 = **7.0 g**, volume = 0.25 × 22.4 = **5.6 L**. Paani = 0.25 × 18 = **4.5 g**.

**Jaanch:** 7.0 + 4.5 = 11.5 g ✅.

## Part D: Ethanol ke upyog

| Upyog | Ethanol ka kaun sa gun kaam aata hai |
|---|---|
| **Sanitiser aur disinfectant** | germs ko maarta hai aur tezi se udkar haath sookha chhodta hai |
| **Fuel** | acchi garmi deta hai; petrol mein lagbhag 20 percent tak milaya jaata hai (blend), spirit lamp |
| **Solvent** | paani aur kai non-polar padarth dono ko ghulata hai (polar –OH aur non-polar C₂H₅) |
| **Medicines, cough syrup, tincture** | dawai ke ghatak ko ghulane ke liye |
| **Perfume, deodorant, paint, varnish** | khushboo ko ghulana aur jaldi sukhna |
| **Kaccha maal** | ethanoic acid (Topic 7), ethene, aur aur kai chemicals banane mein |

**Sanitiser mein 70 kyun, 100 kyun nahi?** Aksar bataya jaata hai ki lagbhag 60 se 70 percent ethanol wala ghol germs ke liye bahut asardaar hai, kyunki thoda paani ethanol ko germ ke andar jaane mein madad karta hai aur ghol ko itni jaldi udne nahi deta ki germ par asar hone se pehle hi sookh jaye. (Aage ki class mein iska poora kaaran padhoge.) Dhyan rakho: sanitiser **sabun aur paani ka vikalp nahi**, jab haath bahut gande ya chikne hon to sabun se dhona behtar hai (Topic 8).

## Part E: Methanol, denatured spirit aur sharab ka khatra

### Methanol (CH₃OH): ethanol ka zehreela bhai

Methanol alcohol series ka sabse chhota member hai: CH₃OH, boiling point lagbhag 65 °C. Dikhne mein bina rang ka, gandh bhi kuch ethanol jaisi. Class 9 mein tumne dekha tha ki ise CO aur H₂ se industry mein banaya jaata hai ([hydrogen-part5.md](../../../class9/chemistry/hydrogen/hydrogen-part5.md)). Par sharir ke andar methanol ko **zehreele padarthon** mein badal diya jaata hai jo **nasein (khaaskar aankh ki nas)** kharab karte hain.

> **Kabhi nahi:** methanol ko peena, sungna, ya haath par lagana. Sirf **bahut thodi** matra bhi andhapan ya jaan ka khatra hai. Methanol ko bina label ke kisi bhi bottle mein mat rakho aur kisi aisi cheez ko mat peeo jise tum nahi jaante.

### Denatured spirit

Industry aur jalane ke liye jo ethanol bikta hai, usme jaanbujhkar **zehreele ya bahut kadwe padarth (methanol ya anya)** aur kabhi rang bhi milate hain, taaki koi use peeya na sake. Ise **denatured spirit** kehte hain. **Denatured spirit sirf jalane ya safai ke liye hai: peene ke liye kabhi nahi**, chahe wo ethanol jaisa dikhe.

### Naqli (adulterated) sharab

Kai baar sunne mein aata hai ki **milawati ya chhup kar banayi gayi sharab**, jisme methanol mila hota hai, peene wale logon ki jaan le leti hai ya unhe andha kar deti hai. Isliye: **kabhi nahi**.

### Sharab sharir ko nuksan kyun pahunchati hai (chhota sa factual paragraph)

Ethanol **dimaag aur nason ko sust** kar deta hai: sochne ki shakti, santulan aur reaction time kam ho jaata hai, jisse haadse hote hain. Zyada dino tak peene se **jigar (liver)** kharab hota hai, kyunki ethanol ko todne ka kaam jigar karta hai, aur aadat (addiction) lag sakti hai. Bachchon aur kishoron ka dimaag aur sharir abhi bad rahe hote hain, isliye unke liye ye **aur bhi nuksandeh** hai. **Bachon ke liye bilkul nahi.** Ghar par kisi ko ethanol jaisa dikhne wala kuch peete dekho to bade ko batao.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Methanol aur ethanol ek jaise hain, dono safe."**
Dono ke formula bhi lagbhag ek jaise hain, dono bina rang ke, dono jalte hain. Par dono ka asar sharir par bilkul alag hai:

| Sawaal | Ethanol | Methanol |
|---|---|---|
| Formula | C₂H₅OH | CH₃OH |
| Molar mass | 46 | 32 |
| Farq | methanol se ek CH₂ zyada (46 − 32 = 14) | ek CH₂ kam |
| Boiling point | lagbhag 78 °C | lagbhag 65 °C |
| Sharir ke andar | dimaag aur jigar par asar, par sanitiser jaisi **bahut kam matra** haath par alag baat hai | **zehreela:** aankh ki nas aur jaan ka khatra |

**Ek kahani:** ek dukaan mein do bina label ki bottles pad gayi thi, ek ethanol ki, ek methanol ki. Dono bina rang ki, dono ek jaisi gandh. Ek naya karmchari bola, "ek ka formula ek CH₂ zyada hai, wahi to farq hai, safe hoga." Sabak: **sirf ek CH₂ ka farq sharir ke liye zindagi aur maut ka farq ho sakta hai.** Pehchaan **label aur supplier** se hoti hai, gandh ya rang se nahi, aur bina label ki bottle kabhi nahi chhoo.

**Niyam:** dono alag padarth hain. **Methanol zehreela hai.** Ethanol ko bhi kabhi peene ki cheez ya sanitiser ko paani samajhna galat hai.

**Galti 2: "Ethanol mein –OH hai, to ye base hoga (jaise NaOH)."**
Naam mein "OH" dekhkar ye sach lagta hai. Par teen jaanch (sirf padhne ke liye):

1. **NaOH ionic hai:** Na⁺ aur **OH⁻ ion** alag-alag. **Ethanol covalent hai:** C–O aur O–H dono **covalent bond**, koi charge nahi. Paani mein ethanol ke molecule **OH⁻ ion nahi chhodte** (Chapter 7: covalent molecule ion nahi banata). Isliye OH⁻ wala base wala swabhaav nahi aata.
2. **Litmus ki jaanch:** ethanol ka ghol na laal litmus ko neela karta hai, na neele ko laal: **neutral**.
3. **Bulb wali jaanch** ([acids-bases-and-salts-part2.md](../acids-bases-and-salts/acids-bases-and-salts-part2.md)): ethanol ke ghol mein tester ka bulb **nahi jalta**, kyunki ghol mein koi ion nahi (Chapter 4 ki cheeni ki tarah: non-electrolyte).

Ek aur uljhan: ethanol sodium ke saath H₂ deta hai, jaise acid deta hai. To kya ye acid hai? **Nahi:** ethanol litmus ko laal nahi karta, aur uski H paani mein H⁺ nahi deta. Wo ek **neutral** yaugik hai jisme O–H ke H ko sodium **dhatu** hata sakti hai.

| Padarth | Ion aate hain? | Litmus | Kaun sa group |
|---|---|---|---|
| NaOH | haan: Na⁺ aur OH⁻ | laal se neela | base |
| Ethanol | nahi | koi asar nahi | neutral covalent |

**Niyam:** "–OH" ka matlab "OH⁻ ion" nahi. **Ethanol na acid hai na base; neutral covalent yaugik.**

## Quick Recap

- **Ethanol C₂H₅OH:** alcohol series ka dusra member, hydrocarbon + **–OH** functional group; C₂H₆O ka dusra isomer methoxymethane CH₃OCH₃.
- **Gun:** bina rang ka, bp lagbhag 78 °C, paani mein **kisi bhi anupaat mein miscible** (polar –OH), bijli nahi, litmus par neutral, jwalansheel.
- **Banana:** fermentation C₆H₁₂O₆ → 2C₂H₅OH + 2CO₂ (yeast ke enzymes, lagbhag 30 se 35 °C); ethene se hydration C₂H₄ + H₂O → C₂H₅OH. Distillation se shuddh.
- **Reactions:** jalna C₂H₅OH + 3O₂ → 2CO₂ + 3H₂O; sodium se 2C₂H₅OH + 2Na → 2C₂H₅ONa + H₂; oxidation C₂H₅OH + 2[O] → CH₃COOH + H₂O; dehydration C₂H₅OH → C₂H₄ + H₂O (gaadha H₂SO₄, lagbhag 170 °C).
- **Upyog:** sanitiser, fuel blend, solvent, medicines, perfumes, kaccha maal.
- **Suraksha:** ethanol jwalansheel; **methanol zehreela, kabhi nahi**; denatured spirit sirf jalane aur safai ke liye; sharab bachon ke liye bilkul nahi.
- **Galtiyan:** methanol aur ethanol ek jaise safe nahi; ethanol base nahi, neutral hai.

## Practice Questions

(Sab sawaal paper par hal karne ke liye hain. Mass: H 1, C 12, O 16, Na 23. Molar volume 22.4 L at STP.)

1. A school buys 5 litres of hand sanitiser that is 60 percent ethanol by volume. How much ethanol and how much of the remaining liquid does it contain (assume volumes add)?
2. 36 g of glucose is completely fermented: C₆H₁₂O₆ → 2C₂H₅OH + 2CO₂. Find the mass of ethanol and of carbon dioxide formed, and the volume of the carbon dioxide at STP.
3. Find the percentage by mass of oxygen in methanol (CH₃OH), ethanol (C₂H₅OH) and propanol (C₃H₇OH). What trend do you notice as the chain gets longer?
4. 3.45 g of sodium reacts completely with ethanol: 2C₂H₅OH + 2Na → 2C₂H₅ONa + H₂. Find the mass of ethanol needed, the volume of hydrogen at STP and the mass of sodium ethoxide formed. (Teacher demo only; paper work here.)
5. 18.4 g of ethanol is completely oxidised: C₂H₅OH + 2[O] → CH₃COOH + H₂O. Find the mass of ethanoic acid and of water formed and of oxygen [O] used. Check that mass is conserved.
6. 34.5 g of ethanol is dehydrated completely by hot concentrated sulphuric acid. Find the mass of ethene and its volume at STP, and the mass of water formed.
7. 13.8 g of ethanol burns completely in air. Find the mass of oxygen needed and the masses of carbon dioxide and water formed. (Paper work only: never burn ethanol in a closed room.)
8. Propanol is the next member after ethanol. (a) Write its formula and draw the structure of propan-1-ol, counting the bonds on each carbon, each oxygen and each hydrogen. (b) By how much does its molar mass differ from ethanol? (c) Predict whether its boiling point is higher or lower than that of ethanol and say which rule you used.
9. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) Ethanol is a base because its formula contains OH. (b) Ethanol mixes with water in all proportions. (c) The yeast used in fermentation works best at about 80 °C. (d) Denatured spirit is safe to drink because it is mostly ethanol. (e) Ethanol and methanol are equally safe.
10. Two colourless liquids both have the molecular formula C₂H₆O. Liquid X gives bubbles of hydrogen with a small piece of sodium. Liquid Y gives no bubbles with sodium. Name X and Y and name the type of isomerism.
11. A student keeps a sugar and yeast mixture at 80 °C and finds no gas bubbles and no ethanol. The mixture was fine at room temperature. Using the fact that yeast is a living organism whose enzymes are damaged when it is very hot, explain what happened.
12. A student writes "C₂H₅OH is a base" and gives sodium as proof (it gives hydrogen). Give two pieces of evidence that ethanol is neutral, and explain why giving hydrogen with sodium does not make it an acid or a base.
13. A teacher mixes 90 mL of ethanol with 60 mL of water (assume volumes add). What is the percentage by volume of ethanol in the mixture? Is it enough for a sanitiser that needs 60 to 70 percent?
14. Ethanol boils at 78 °C and water at 100 °C. A mixture of the two is slowly heated to 85 °C. Which liquid is more in the vapour, and why is the vapour collected in a condenser richer in ethanol than the original mixture?
15. List three safety rules for using hand sanitiser at home and explain in one line why a bottle with no label must never be tasted, smelled or used.

### Answers

1. 60 percent v/v matlab 100 mL mein 60 mL. 5 litre = 5000 mL; ethanol = 5000 × 60 ÷ 100 = 3000 mL = **3 L**, baaki = **2 L**. Jaanch: 3 ÷ 5 = 0.6 = 60 percent ✅.
2. Glucose = **180** g/mol; 36 ÷ 180 = **0.2 mol**. Ratio 1 : 2 : 2: ethanol 0.4 mol × 46 = **18.4 g**; CO₂ 0.4 mol × 44 = **17.6 g**, volume = 0.4 × 22.4 = **8.96 L**. Jaanch: 18.4 + 17.6 = 36 g ✅.
3. CH₃OH = 32: 16 ÷ 32 × 100 = **50 percent**. C₂H₅OH = 46: 16 ÷ 46 × 100 = **34.8 percent** (lagbhag). C₃H₇OH = 60: 16 ÷ 60 × 100 = **26.7 percent** (lagbhag). Chain badhne par oxygen ka percent **ghat** jaata hai, kyunki hydrocarbon wala hissa zyada bhari hota jaata hai.
4. Na = 3.45 ÷ 23 = **0.15 mol**. Ratio Na : ethanol : ethoxide : H₂ = 2 : 2 : 2 : 1. Ethanol = 0.15 × 46 = **6.9 g**; sodium ethoxide = 0.15 × 68 = **10.2 g**; H₂ = 0.075 mol × 22.4 = **1.68 L**. Jaanch: 3.45 + 6.9 = 10.35; 10.2 + (0.075 × 2 = 0.15) = 10.35 g ✅.
5. Ethanol = 18.4 ÷ 46 = **0.4 mol**. Ethanoic acid = 0.4 × 60 = **24.0 g**; paani = 0.4 × 18 = **7.2 g**; [O] = 0.8 mol × 16 = **12.8 g**. Jaanch: 18.4 + 12.8 = 31.2 g; 24.0 + 7.2 = 31.2 g ✅.
6. Ethanol = 34.5 ÷ 46 = **0.75 mol**. Ratio 1 : 1 : 1. Ethene = 0.75 × 28 = **21 g**, volume = 0.75 × 22.4 = **16.8 L**; paani = 0.75 × 18 = **13.5 g**. Jaanch: 21 + 13.5 = 34.5 g ✅.
7. Ethanol = 13.8 ÷ 46 = **0.3 mol**. Ratio 1 : 3 : 2 : 3. O₂ = 0.9 × 32 = **28.8 g**; CO₂ = 0.6 × 44 = **26.4 g**; H₂O = 0.9 × 18 = **16.2 g**. Jaanch: 13.8 + 28.8 = 42.6; 26.4 + 16.2 = 42.6 g ✅.
8. (a) Formula C₃H₇OH (C₃H₈O). Structure (propan-1-ol): sabse baayen C par 3 H aur ek C = 4 bond; beech ke C par 2 H aur do C = 4; daayen C par 2 H, ek C aur O = 4; O par ek C aur ek H = 2; kul H = 3 + 2 + 2 + 1 = 8 ✅.
   ```
        H   H   H
        I   I   I
   H -- C - C - C -- O -- H
        I   I   I
        H   H   H
   ```
   (b) 60 − 46 = **14**, ek CH₂ ka farq. (c) **Zyada**, kyunki homologous series mein chain lambi hone par boiling point badhta hai (Topic 2).
9. (a) **False** ❌: ethanol covalent hai, OH⁻ ion nahi deta; neutral hai. (b) **True** ✅. (c) **False** ❌: lagbhag 30 se 35 °C par; 80 °C par enzymes kharab ho jaate hain. (d) **False** ❌: denatured spirit mein zehreele ya kadwe padarth hote hain, peene ke liye kabhi nahi. (e) **False** ❌: methanol zehreela hai.
10. X = **ethanol** (sodium ke saath H₂ deta hai, kyunki isme O–H hai). Y = **methoxymethane (CH₃OCH₃)**, jisme O–H nahi, to sodium se H₂ nahi. Dono ka formula C₂H₆O par structure alag: **functional isomerism**.
11. Yeast ek **jeevit jeev** hai aur uske enzymes bahut garam hone par **kharab ho jaate hain**. 80 °C par enzymes ne kaam karna band kar diya, isliye cheeni ethanol aur CO₂ mein nahi tooti. Sahi temperature lagbhag 30 se 35 °C hai.
12. Saboot: (i) **litmus** par koi asar nahi (na laal na neela); (ii) **bulb nahi jalta**, kyunki ghol mein ion nahi. (Ya: C–O–H covalent bond, OH⁻ ion nahi.) Sodium se H₂ isliye aati hai ki sodium dhatu O–H ke **H ko hata sakti hai**; isse ghol ka H⁺ ya OH⁻ dena sabit nahi hota. Ethanol neutral covalent yaugik hai.
13. Kul = 90 + 60 = 150 mL. Ethanol percent = 90 ÷ 150 × 100 = **60 percent**. Haan, 60 percent ki **nichli seema** par hai (lagbhag). Jaanch: 60 × 150 ÷ 100 = 90 mL ✅.
14. 85 °C par **ethanol zyada** bhaap mein hota hai, kyunki uska boiling point (78 °C) kam hai, wo zyada **udnewala (volatile)** hai. Condenser mein ye bhaap thandi hokar liquid banti hai, jisme ethanol ka hissa shuru ke mixture se **zyada** hota hai. Isi tarah distillation ethanol aur paani ko lagbhag alag karti hai.
15. Teen niyam (koi teen): (i) aag, diya, gas aur matches se door; (ii) lagane ke baad haath sookhne ke baad hi rasoi ya aag ke paas jao; (iii) peene ke liye nahi, aankh mein na lage, bachche bade ke saath hi use karein; (iv) dhoop ya garam jagah (gaadi) mein na chhodein. Bina label ki bottle mein **methanol** jaisa zehreela padarth ho sakta hai, jo ethanol jaisa dikhta hai aur gandh bhi lagbhag ek jaisi: isliye kabhi nahi.
