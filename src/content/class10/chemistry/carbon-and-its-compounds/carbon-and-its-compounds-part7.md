# Topic 7: Ethanoic Acid: Sirka Aur Uske Saathi

Topic 6 mein hamne dekha ki ethanol ko oxidise karo to ethanoic acid banta hai: C₂H₅OH + 2[O] → CH₃COOH + H₂O. Ab hum us **ethanoic acid** ko seedhe milenge. Ye wahi weak acid hai jise tumne Chapter 1 aur Chapter 4 mein ek **formula** ki tarah dekha tha: CH₃COOH ⇌ CH₃COO⁻ + H⁺. Ab hum us formula ke andar jhaankenge: kaun sa H acidic hai, kaun sa nahi; ye acid kaise carbonate se jhaag banata hai, base se namak, aur **alcohol se ek khushbudaar nayi cheez**. Aaj ke topic ke do sawaal: ye khattapan kahan se aata hai, aur ye khushboo kahan se?

## Do jar, do kahaniyan

Mr. Agashe apne bhatije Pushkar ko lekar padosi ki Aaji ke ghar gaye. Aaji ke ghar ki khidki par kaanch ka ek bada jar rakha tha: **nimbu ka achaar**, aur uske upar tairta hua halka peela sirka. Aur Aaji kehti hain ye sirka achaar ko kharab hone se bachata hai. Kaise?"

Wahan se wapas aate waqt Pushkar ko doosre ghar ki rasoi ka ek band steel ka dabba dikha. Dabba kholte hi ek bilkul **alag** gandh aayi: halki, meethi, **phal jaisi khushbu**, jaise kachche-pakke nashpati ya ananas ki. Dabbe mein purane ghar-ke-bane jamun ke sirke ki botal thi, jise kai mahine se bandh rakha gaya tha. Pushkar ne dono gandh yaad ki. "Chacha, sirke mein to khatti gandh hoti hai. Ye meethi khushbu kahan se aa gayi?"

Mr. Agashe ne hans kar kaha: "Khatti gandh **ethanoic acid** ki hai: wahi acid jo sirke mein hota hai. Aur khushbu? Wo ek bachcha hai: ethanoic acid aur alcohol ka. Chal, dono kahaniyan samajhte hain: pehle acid ki, phir uske bachche ki."

## Suraksha (pehle ye padho)

> **Suraksha: ethanoic acid, ethanol aur gaadha H₂SO₄**
> - **Rasoi ka sirka** (lagbhag 5 se 8 percent acid) halka hai aur ghar par fizz wali activity ke liye theek hai, par **aankh mein na jaaye**, aur kaam ke baad haath dho lo. Kuch chakhna nahi hai.
> - **Glacial ethanoic acid** (lagbhag shuddh) **corrosive** hai: skin jalata hai, bhaap aankh aur gale ko jalati hai, aur wo **jwalansheel (flammable)** bhi hai. Sirf **teacher** ise fume cupboard mein sambhalta hai.

## Part A: Ethanoic acid ki banawat: –COOH ka raaz

Chapter 1 mein tumne dekha tha ki CH₃COOH mein 4 hydrogen hain, par **sirf ek** H⁺ ban kar niklta hai. Ab dekhte hain kyun.

```
         H       O
         I       II
    H -- C ----- C ----- O -- H
         I
         H
```

> **Figure 7.1** – Ethanoic acid, CH₃COOH ki structure. Baayen carbon ke saath 3 H aur ek C juda hai (valency 4: 3 + 1). Dusre carbon ke saath: ek C, ek double bond O (II = double bond, valency 2) aur ek –O– (valency 1): 1 + 2 + 1 = 4. Oxygen (=O) ki valency 2 aur –O–H ke oxygen ki valency 2 hai; har H ki valency 1.

Ginti: kul C 2, H 3 + 1 = 4, O 2: **C₂H₄O₂**. Dhyan se dekho: **–COOH** wala hissa ek "naya samooh" hai, jisme **ek hi carbon** ek =O aur ek –O–H se juda hai. Chaaron H mein se **teen H carbon se** jude hain (CH₃– hissa) aur **ek H oxygen se** juda hai. Oxygen bahut electronegative hai, to O–H ka bond polar hai: us **ek H** ko paani mein H⁺ bana kar chhod dena aasaan hai. Carbon se jude H nearly non-polar C–H bonds (Chapter 7 ka electronegativity wala picture) mein hain, isliye wo ion banke nahi nikalte. **Acid ka khattapan sirf –COOH ke ek H se** aata hai.

Ek series ki tarah (Topic 2 ka sutra CₙH₂ₙ₊₁COOH) teen pehle member:

```
   HCOOH                    O
                            II
                       H -- C ----- O -- H


   CH₃COOH             H    O
                       I    II
                  H -- C -- C ----- O -- H
                       I
                       H


   C₂H₅COOH            H    H    O
                       I    I    II
                  H -- C -- C -- C ----- O -- H
                       I    I
                       H    H
```

> **Figure 7.2** – Teen carboxylic acids: upar methanoic acid HCOOH (C 1, H 2, O 2), beech mein ethanoic acid CH₃COOH, neeche propanoic acid C₂H₅COOH (C 3, H 6, O 2). Har C ke paas chaar bond aur har H ke paas ek bond ginne se milta hai (HCOOH mein C: =O ke 2, –OH ke 1 aur H ke 1). Har agla member ek CH₂ (14) zyada hai: 46, 60, 74.

### Example 1. Ethanoic acid ka hisaab

**Sawaal:** CH₃COOH ka molar mass aur har tatva ka mass percent nikalo. Phir uska empirical formula batao. (H 1, C 12, O 16.)

**Kadam 1:** CH₃COOH = C₂H₄O₂ = 2 × 12 + 4 × 1 + 2 × 16 = 24 + 4 + 32 = **60** g/mol.
**Kadam 2:** C = 24 ÷ 60 × 100 = **40 percent**; H = 4 ÷ 60 × 100 = **6.67 percent**; O = 32 ÷ 60 × 100 = **53.33 percent**.
**Kadam 3 (empirical):** C : H : O ke moles = 2 : 4 : 2 = **1 : 2 : 1**, to empirical formula **CH₂O** (uska mass 30), aur n = 60 ÷ 30 = 2, molecular formula **C₂H₄O₂**.

**Jaanch:** 40 + 6.67 + 53.33 = 100.00 ✅. (CH₂O)₂ = C₂H₄O₂ ✅. (Interesting: yahi empirical formula glucose ka bhi hai, par wo alag yaugik hai: formula se sirf anupaat milta hai, yaugik nahi.)

## Part B: Bhautik gun: sirka, glacial aur ek thandi subah

**Sirka:** paani mein ethanoic acid ka lagbhag 5 se 8 percent ka ghol. Ghar ka sirka ganne ya phal ke ras se banta hai: pehle **fermentation** (cheeni → ethanol, Topic 6), phir ek aur dhire kaam mein ethanol ka **oxidation** (C₂H₅OH + 2[O] → CH₃COOH + H₂O). Isliye ek sirke ki botal mein dono kahaniyan dikhti hain: ethanol bhi (thoda) aur acid bhi.

Ab shuddh ethanoic acid ki kuch baatein (lagbhag):

| Gun | Ethanoic acid |
|---|---|
| Rang aur gandh | bina rang ka liquid, tez khatti gandh |
| Paani mein | har anupaat mein mil jaata hai (miscible) |
| Melting point | lagbhag **17 °C** |
| Boiling point | lagbhag **118 °C** |

Melting point ki ek maze ki baat: lagbhag **17 °C** par shuddh ethanoic acid **jam jaata** hai. Thandi subah, thande kamre ya fridge mein ek lab ki botal mein **barf jaise safed crystal** dikhte hain: isi liye ise **glacial** (barf jaisa) ethanoic acid kehte hain. Garam kamre mein wo phir liquid ban jaata hai.

```
   temperature  --->  upar badhti hai

   17 °C se neeche         17 °C           118 °C
   ------------------------+---------------+------------>
   thos (barf jaisa)  pighla, liquid   ubal kar bhaap
   (glacial)          (kamre ka)       (bhaap ban gaya)
```

> **Figure 7.3** – Shuddh ethanoic acid ki avasthayen: lagbhag 17 °C se neeche thos (barf jaise crystal), 17 °C aur 118 °C ke beech liquid, lagbhag 118 °C par ubalkar bhaap. Pichhle topic ke boiling points se milao: methanol 65, ethanol 78, **ethanoic acid 118**, paani 100 °C.

Dhyan do: ethanoic acid (60) ka boiling point ethanol (46) se kaafi upar hai, aur paani (18) se bhi upar. Mass ka farak itna nahi hai ki ye samjhaaye: ye acid ke kan ek doosre ko **kaafi kas kar** pakadte hain. (Kaise: aage ki class mein.)

**Weak acid:** paani mein ye acid thoda hi H⁺ deta hai, jaisa Chapter 1 aur 4 mein dekha. Ek dhyan rakhne wali baat: sirka (ghola hua) aur glacial acid (lagbhag shuddh), dono mein **ek hi** acid hai: ghola hona ya gaadha hona **kitna acid hai** batata hai, jabki **weak ya strong** ye batata hai ki **kitne molecule toote**. Galti 1 mein iska demo.

## Part C: Acid ki reactions: carbonate, base aur dhaatu

### 1. Carbonate aur bicarbonate ke saath: ghar ki fizz wali activity

Chapter 1 se yaad: koi bhi acid carbonate ya bicarbonate se CO₂ nikalta hai. Ethanoic acid bhi:

2CH₃COOH + Na₂CO₃ → 2CH₃COONa + H₂O + CO₂
CH₃COOH + NaHCO₃ → CH₃COONa + H₂O + CO₂

Namak CH₃COONa ko **sodium ethanoate** kehte hain.

**Khud karke dekho: sirka aur baking soda (ghar par, adult ke saath)**

**Zaroorat:** ek bada, saaf kaanch ka glass, 2 chammach sirka, aadha chhota chammach baking soda, ek tray. **Adult paas mein, kuch chakhna nahi.**

1. Glass ko tray mein rakho aur usme baking soda daalo.
2. Dheere dheere sirka daalo, ek baar mein thoda. Glass ko aankhon se door rakho.
3. **Dekho:** jhaag aur bulbule uthte hain (CO₂ gas).
4. Kaam ke baad ghol ko sink mein bahao, haath dho lo.

```
                  CO₂ ke bulbule upar
                    o   o  o   o
                  o   o   o  o
           +--------------------------+
           :                          :
           :  ~~~~ jhaag + sirka ~~~~ :
           :  ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ :
           :        . . . . .         :   <-- neeche baking soda
           +--------------------------+
                  tray ke andar
```

> **Figure 7.4** – Ghar ki fizz wali activity: glass mein baking soda par sirka dalne se CO₂ ke bulbule uthte hain. Ye carbonate test hai: sirf acid hi (alcohol nahi) bicarbonate se CO₂ nikalta hai. Glass khula hai, band bottle mein ye kabhi nahi karte.

Ye reaction ek **test** bhi hai: ethanoic acid aur ethanol, dono bina rang ke liquid hain aur dono sodium ke saath H₂ dete hain, par **sirf acid** baking soda se fizz karta hai. Alcohol bicarbonate ko hilata tak nahi.

### Example 2. Na₂CO₃ ke saath: kitna CO₂?

**Sawaal:** 15.0 g ethanoic acid ek teacher demo mein Na₂CO₃ ke saath poora react hua: 2CH₃COOH + Na₂CO₃ → 2CH₃COONa + H₂O + CO₂. Kitna Na₂CO₃ lagega, kitna CO₂ (STP par, litre mein) banega, aur kitna sodium ethanoate? (H 1, C 12, O 16, Na 23; molar volume 22.4 L.)

**Kadam 1 (moles):** CH₃COOH = 15.0 ÷ 60 = **0.25 mol**. Ratio acid : Na₂CO₃ : CH₃COONa : CO₂ = 2 : 1 : 2 : 1.
**Kadam 2 (ratio se):** Na₂CO₃ = 0.125 mol × 106 = **13.25 g**. CO₂ = 0.125 mol × 22.4 = **2.8 L**. CH₃COONa (82) = 0.25 × 82 = **20.5 g**.

**Jaanch:** atoms: C 4 + 1 = 5 = 4 + 1, H 8 = 6 + 2, O 4 + 3 = 7 = 4 + 1 + 2, Na 2 ✅. Mass: 15.0 + 13.25 = 28.25 g; daayein 20.5 + (0.125 × 18 = 2.25) + (0.125 × 44 = 5.5) = 28.25 g ✅.

### 2. Base ke saath: neutralisation

CH₃COOH + NaOH → CH₃COONa + H₂O

Ye Chapter 1 wali neutralisation hai: acid + base → namak + paani. **Sodium ethanoate** ek ionic namak hai (Na⁺ aur CH₃COO⁻). Chapter 1 mein tumne dekha tha ki weak acid aur strong base ka namak halka **basic** ghol deta hai: yahan bhi.

### Example 3. NaOH ki neutralisation (sirf padhne ke liye)

**Sawaal:** 2.0 g NaOH ko poora neutralise karne ke liye kitna ethanoic acid lagega, aur kitna CH₃COONa banega? (H 1, C 12, O 16, Na 23.)

**Kadam 1:** NaOH = 40 g/mol, 2.0 ÷ 40 = **0.05 mol**. Ratio 1 : 1 : 1 : 1.
**Kadam 2:** CH₃COOH = 0.05 × 60 = **3.0 g**. CH₃COONa = 0.05 × 82 = **4.1 g**. Paani = 0.05 × 18 = 0.9 g.

**Jaanch:** 3.0 + 2.0 = 5.0 g; daayein 4.1 + 0.9 = 5.0 g ✅. Atoms: C 2, H 4 + 1 = 5 = 3 + 2, O 2 + 1 = 3 = 2 + 1, Na 1 ✅. (NaOH tez alkali hai: ye hisaab sirf kaagaz par hai.)

### 3. Active dhaatu ke saath

Active dhaatu (jaise Mg, Zn, aur bahut tez Na) acid se H₂ nikalte hain:

2CH₃COOH + Mg → (CH₃COO)₂Mg + H₂

Par ye bahut **dheere** bulbule dete hain: Chapter 1 ke weak acid ki tarah, HCl se kaafi dheere. Sodium ke saath reaction tez aur khatarnak hoti hai, to wo **sirf padhne ke liye** hai.

**Jaanch (atoms):** 2CH₃COOH: C 4, H 8, O 4. (CH₃COO)₂Mg: C 4, H 6, O 4, Mg 1, aur H₂: H 2: kul H 8 ✅.

### Ek chhoti tulna: HCl ya ethanoic acid?

| Test (barabar mol aur barabar quantity) | Dilute HCl | Dilute ethanoic acid |
|---|---|---|
| Mg ribbon ke saath | tez bulbule | dheere bulbule |
| Conductivity tester (Chapter 4) | LED tez | LED halki |
| Ion banne ka hissa | lagbhag poora | sirf thoda |
| pH (0.1 mol/litre ghol, lagbhag) | lagbhag 1 | lagbhag 3 |

## Part D: Alcohol ke saath: khushbu ka janm

### Esterification: sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo)

Ab doosri kahani: wo phal-jaisi khushbu. Jab ethanoic acid aur ethanol ko ek chhote se gaadhe H₂SO₄ (catalyst) ke saath halka garam karte hain:

CH₃COOH + C₂H₅OH ⇌ CH₃COOC₂H₅ + H₂O

Naya yaugik **ethyl ethanoate** hai: ek **ester**. Naam ki chaal: alcohol ka hissa pehle (**ethyl**, ethanol se) aur phir acid ka hissa (**ethanoate**, ethanoic acid se). Gaadha H₂SO₄ yahan catalyst hai, aur saath mein bane paani ka kuch hissa bhi sokh leta hai (Chapter 9 ka dehydrating swabhaav), jisse thoda zyada ester milta hai.

> **Suraksha: esterification demo (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo)**
> Ethanol jwalansheel hai, glacial ethanoic acid aur gaadha H₂SO₄ corrosive hain. Teacher fume cupboard mein, goggles aur gloves ke saath, **garam paani ka bath (electric kettle ya electric plate se) hi** use karta hai. **Naked flame bilkul nahi.** Test tube ka mooh khula hota hai aur kisi student ki taraf nahi hota. Bachche door rehte hain.

```
                       test tube (mooh khula, cork nahi)
                            :     :
                            :     :
          +-----------------:-----:-----------------+
          :   ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~:     :~~~~~~~~~~~~~~   :   <-- garam paani ka bath
          :   ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~:     :~~~~~~~~~~~~~~   :
          :   ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~:#####:~~~~~~~~~~~~~~   :   <-- ##### = mixture
          :   ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~+-----+~~~~~~~~~~~~~~   :
          +-------------------------------------------+
            [ electric hot plate ]   koi khuli aanch nahi
```

> **Figure 7.5** – Esterification demo (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo): chhoti test tube mein ethanoic acid, ethanol aur kuch boondein gaadhe H₂SO₄ ko garam paani ke bath mein halka garam kiya jaata hai. Bath ka paani test tube ke andar ke mixture ke star se upar hai. Test tube ka mooh khula hai (band apparatus kabhi nahi) aur koi khuli aanch nahi.

Kuch der baad teacher is mixture ko ek **thande paani ke beaker** mein daalta hai. Upar ek patli, tairti **tel jaisi parat** dikhti hai (ester paani mein bahut kam ghulta hai aur halka hai) aur usse hawa mein **meethi, phal jaisi khushbu** aati hai: yahi Pushkar ke steel ke dabbe ki khushbu thi. Kyun? Un dabbe ki purani sirke ki botal mein thoda ethanol aur bahut sara ethanoic acid pada tha, aur mahino ki dheemi reaction mein thoda ester ban gaya.

```
   CH₃CO-OH   +   H-OC₂H₅    ---->    CH₃CO-OC₂H₅   +   H₂O
   (acid)         (alcohol)            (ester)           (paani)
        \_________/
      acid ka OH aur alcohol ka H milkar H₂O
```

> **Figure 7.6** – Esterification ka "kat-jod": acid ke –OH se OH aur alcohol ke –OH se H nikalkar H₂O banate hain, aur bacha hua CH₃CO– hissa C₂H₅O– se jud jaata hai: ester. Kul C 4, O 2, H 8: ginti: baayen CH₃COOH (C₂H₄O₂) + C₂H₅OH (C₂H₆O) = C₄H₁₀O₃; daayen ester C₄H₈O₂ + H₂O = C₄H₁₀O₃.

Ester ki banawat dekho: acid ka H hat gaya, uski jagah alcohol ka C₂H₅ aa gaya.

```
         H       O                H      H
         I       II               I      I
    H -- C ----- C ----- O ------ C ---- C -- H
         I                        I      I
         H                        H      H
```

> **Figure 7.7** – Ethyl ethanoate, CH₃COOC₂H₅ ki structure. Har C ke paas chaar bond: pehla C (3 H + 1 C), dusra C (1 C, double bond O = 2, aur –O– = 1), CH₂ wala C (O, C, 2 H), aakhri CH₃ (C, 3 H). Dono O ki valency 2, har H ki valency 1. Kul C 4, H 8, O 2.

### Example 4. Ethyl ethanoate ki sambhavit matra

**Sawaal:** Ek teacher demo mein 12.0 g ethanoic acid aur 9.2 g ethanol bilkul sahi anupaat mein mile. Agar reaction poori ho jaye, to kitna ethyl ethanoate aur kitna paani banega? (H 1, C 12, O 16.)

**Kadam 1:** CH₃COOH = 12.0 ÷ 60 = **0.2 mol**; C₂H₅OH (46) = 9.2 ÷ 46 = **0.2 mol**. Ratio 1 : 1 : 1 : 1 (barabar).
**Kadam 2:** ester C₄H₈O₂ = 48 + 8 + 32 = **88** g/mol, to 0.2 × 88 = **17.6 g**. Paani = 0.2 × 18 = **3.6 g**.

**Jaanch:** 12.0 + 9.2 = 21.2 g; daayein 17.6 + 3.6 = 21.2 g ✅. Atoms: C 2 + 2 = 4, H 4 + 6 = 10 = 8 + 2, O 2 + 1 = 3 = 2 + 1 ✅.
**Dhyan:** reaction ⇌ hai (dono taraf ja sakti hai), isliye asli demo mein ester is **adarsh** matra se kam milta hai.

### Doosre ester: methanol aur propanol ke saath

Wahi chaal, alag alcohol:

CH₃COOH + CH₃OH ⇌ CH₃COOCH₃ + H₂O (methyl ethanoate, C₃H₆O₂)
CH₃COOH + C₃H₇OH ⇌ CH₃COOC₃H₇ + H₂O (propyl ethanoate, C₅H₁₀O₂)

**Sutra:** ester ka formula = acid + alcohol, minus H₂O. Dono ki **chaal ek hi**: alcohol ka hissa naam ke aage, acid ka hissa peeche (-oate).

Alag alag ester ki alag khushbu (nashpati, kela, sab): isliye khaane-peene ki cheezon mein artificial flavour aur perfume ke liye ester use hote hain (kaise ye bante hain, ek hi chaal se). Methanol ka ester banane ka kaam sirf kaagaz par hai: methanol zehreela hai (Topic 6).

### Hydrolysis: ulti disha

Reaction ⇌ hai, to ulti disha bhi ho sakti hai: **ester + paani → acid + alcohol** (thoda acid ya alkali ki madad se). Isi ulti reaction ko **hydrolysis** kehte hain. Jab hydrolysis **NaOH** jaise alkali se karte hain to alcohol ke saath **acid ka namak** milta hai: ye khaas reaction ko **saponification** kehte hain: uski poori kahani Topic 8 mein.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Ethanoic acid strong acid hai kyunki wo khatta hai."**
Khatta swaad sirf ye batata hai ki **H⁺ hai**, ye nahi ki kitna. Do jaanch:

1. **Sau molecule ki ginti.** Paani mein 100 HCl molecule daalo to lagbhag **100** toot jaate hain. Paani mein 100 CH₃COOH molecule daalo (ghola hua) to lagbhag **1 ya 2** hi tootte hain, baaki 98 ya 99 **poore molecule** ke roop mein rehte hain (Chapter 1 aur 4 ka figure).
2. **Test (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo).** Barabar Mg ribbon: HCl mein tez bulbule, ethanoic acid mein dheere. Barabar LED tester: HCl mein tez, ethanoic acid mein halki.

Ab ek dilemma: glacial ethanoic acid to skin jalata hai, to kya wo strong hai? Nahi: wo **gaadha** hai (zyada acid, kam paani). **Strong** ka matlab hai ki jitne molecule hain, unmein se lagbhag sab toot jaate hain: glacial mein to paani hi nahi, isliye kam hi toote hote hain. Shabd: **gaadha ≠ strong, khatta ≠ strong**; strong matlab paani mein lagbhag poora ion mein toot jaana.

**Niyam:** ethanoic acid **weak** hai, chahe sirke ki tarah ghola ho ya glacial ki tarah gaadha.

**Galti 2: "Esterification bhi neutralisation hai: acid + kuch milakar namak jaisa kuch banta hai."**
Dono mein "acid + X → kuch + paani" dikhta hai, isliye ye mix-up hota hai. Par dono bilkul alag hain. **Ek test case:** ek test tube mein ethanoic acid + NaOH, doosri mein ethanoic acid + ethanol (aur H₂SO₄).

| Sawaal | Neutralisation (acid + NaOH) | Esterification (acid + alcohol) |
|---|---|---|
| Doosra reactant | base (NaOH): OH⁻ deta hai | alcohol: **neutral**, koi OH⁻ nahi (Topic 6) |
| Product | namak CH₃COONa (ionic) + paani | ester CH₃COOC₂H₅ (covalent) + paani |
| Kaise hoti hai | turant, thandi mein | dheere, halki garmi aur catalyst ke saath |
| Gandh | koi khaas nahi | **khushbu** (phal jaisi) |
| Ghol mein bijli | namak ke ions: bijli chalti hai | ester covalent: bijli nahi |
| Kitni poori | lagbhag poori (→) | adhoori, ⇌ |

Ab ek chhota proof: alcohol neutral hai (litmus bina badle), to wo base ki tarah acid ko **neutralise** nahi kar sakta. Alcohol **acid ke –OH ke saath ek hissa** milata hai aur paani banata hai: ye ek alag raasta hai.

**Niyam:** neutralisation = acid + **base** → namak + paani. Esterification = acid + **alcohol** → **ester** + paani.

## Quick Recap

- **Ethanoic acid CH₃COOH** (C₂H₄O₂, 60 g/mol): –COOH samooh; 4 H mein se **sirf O se juda ek H** acidic hai. Series: HCOOH (46), CH₃COOH (60), C₂H₅COOH (74).
- **Sirka** = ethanoic acid ka lagbhag 5 se 8 percent ka ghol. **Glacial** = lagbhag shuddh, **melting point lagbhag 17 °C**, boiling point lagbhag 118 °C, corrosive aur jwalansheel.
- **Weak acid:** thode hi molecule toote; HCl se dheeri reaction, halki LED.
- **Reactions:** carbonate/bicarbonate → namak + H₂O + CO₂ (ghar ki fizz); NaOH → CH₃COONa + H₂O; active dhaatu → namak + H₂ (dheere).
- **Esterification:** acid + alcohol ⇌ ester + paani (gaadhe H₂SO₄ catalyst ke saath, halki garmi); ester ki **phal jaisi khushbu**; teacher demo. Methanol ya propanol ke saath bhi: methyl ethanoate, propyl ethanoate.
- **Hydrolysis:** ester + paani → acid + alcohol; NaOH se saponification (Topic 8).
- **Upyog:** sirka, achaar, rang, dawaiyan, solvent, artificial flavour.
- **Galtiyan:** khatta ≠ strong; esterification ≠ neutralisation.

## Practice Questions

(Sab sawaal paper par hal karne ke liye hain. Mass: H 1, C 12, O 16, Na 23, Mg 24. Molar volume 22.4 L at STP.)

1. Find the molar masses of methanoic acid HCOOH, ethanoic acid CH₃COOH and propanoic acid C₂H₅COOH. What is the difference between consecutive members, and which group of atoms does it correspond to?
2. Using the general formula CₙH₂ₙ₊₁COOH, write the formula and molar mass of the member that follows propanoic acid in the series.
3. Draw the structural formula of propanoic acid C₂H₅COOH and show by counting that each carbon has valency 4 and each hydrogen valency 1. Which single hydrogen is the acidic one?
4. In a teacher demo, 14.8 g of propanoic acid reacts completely with sodium carbonate: 2C₂H₅COOH + Na₂CO₃ → 2C₂H₅COONa + H₂O + CO₂. Find the mass of Na₂CO₃ used, the mass of sodium propanoate formed and the volume of CO₂ at STP.
5. Methanoic acid (4.6 g) is neutralised completely by NaOH: HCOOH + NaOH → HCOONa + H₂O. Find the mass of NaOH needed and the mass of sodium methanoate formed.
6. Propanoic acid (7.4 g) and methanol (3.2 g), in exactly reacting amounts, form an ester in a teacher demo: C₂H₅COOH + CH₃OH ⇌ C₂H₅COOCH₃ + H₂O. Name the ester, give its molar mass and find the ideal masses of ester and water formed.
7. Propanol, C₃H₇OH (6.0 g), reacts with exactly the right amount of ethanoic acid to form an ester. Write the equation, name the ester, and find the ideal mass of ester formed. Check that mass is conserved.
8. Methanoic acid and methanol form an ester. (a) Write the balanced equation and name the ester. (b) Find its molar mass. (c) The ester has the same molecular formula as ethanoic acid: say what word is used for such pairs and whether they would behave alike with sodium bicarbonate.
9. Methyl propanoate is hydrolysed by water: write the equation for the products formed and check that atoms balance. Name the two products.
10. A sample of 300 g of vinegar contains 6 percent ethanoic acid by mass. How many moles of acid does it contain, and what mass of baking soda NaHCO₃ and what volume of CO₂ at STP would fully react with it (CH₃COOH + NaHCO₃ → CH₃COONa + H₂O + CO₂)?
11. Three colourless liquids are named P, Q and R. Given facts: P turns blue litmus red and fizzes with baking soda. Q and R do not change litmus and do not fizz. Q has a sweet fruity smell and does not mix with water. R has the smell of a hand sanitiser and mixes with water in every ratio. The candidates are ethanol, ethanoic acid and ethyl ethanoate. Identify P, Q and R with reasons.
12. Pure ethanoic acid melts at about 17 °C and boils at about 118 °C. A lab bottle is found half-frozen on a 12 °C morning. (a) What is the physical state at 12 °C and at 25 °C? (b) Put ethanol (78 °C), water (100 °C) and ethanoic acid in order of boiling point.
13. A student says: "Glacial ethanoic acid burns the skin, so it must be a strong acid." Use the difference between concentrated and strong to explain in 3 or 4 lines, and name one test with an acid of the same concentration that shows the real strength.
14. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) Ethanol also fizzes with baking soda. (b) Esters are the products of acid with a base. (c) Sodium reacts with both ethanol and ethanoic acid. (d) Ethyl ethanoate is ionic. (e) Ethanoic acid has four acidic hydrogens.
15. (Optional, with an adult.) Read (without opening) the label of a vinegar bottle: what percentage of acid is printed? Use the fizz activity to explain why this is a carbonate test and say what you would expect if you replaced vinegar with water.

### Answers

1. HCOOH = 1 + 12 + 32 + 1 = **46**; CH₃COOH = **60**; C₂H₅COOH = 36 + 6 + 32 = **74**. Farak **14, 14**, matlab ek **CH₂** (12 + 2). Jaanch: 60 − 46 = 14, 74 − 60 = 14 ✅.
2. n = 3: **C₃H₇COOH**, C₄H₈O₂ = 48 + 8 + 32 = **88** g/mol. Jaanch: 88 − 74 = 14 ✅.
3. Structure (figure ki tarah):

```
         H      H      O
         I      I      II
    H -- C ---- C ---- C ----- O -- H
         I      I
         H      H
```

   CH₃ ka C: 3 H + 1 C = 4. CH₂ ka C: 2 H + 2 C = 4. –COOH ka C: 1 C + double bond O (2) + O (1) = 4. H ki valency 1 sab ki. **O se juda aakhri H** acidic hai.
4. C₂H₅COOH = **74**, 14.8 ÷ 74 = **0.2 mol**. Ratio 2 : 1 : 2 : 1. Na₂CO₃ = 0.1 mol × 106 = **10.6 g**. C₂H₅COONa = 0.2 × 96 = **19.2 g**. CO₂ = 0.1 × 22.4 = **2.24 L**. Jaanch: 14.8 + 10.6 = 25.4 g; daayein 19.2 + (0.1 × 18 = 1.8) + (0.1 × 44 = 4.4) = 25.4 g ✅.
5. HCOOH = 4.6 ÷ 46 = **0.1 mol**. NaOH = 0.1 × 40 = **4.0 g**. HCOONa = 0.1 × 68 = **6.8 g**. Jaanch: 4.6 + 4.0 = 8.6 g; daayein 6.8 + 1.8 = 8.6 g ✅.
6. Ester **methyl propanoate**, C₄H₈O₂ = 48 + 8 + 32 = **88** g/mol. C₂H₅COOH = 7.4 ÷ 74 = 0.1 mol; CH₃OH = 3.2 ÷ 32 = 0.1 mol: 1 : 1. Ester = 0.1 × 88 = **8.8 g**; paani = **1.8 g**. Jaanch: 7.4 + 3.2 = 10.6 g; 8.8 + 1.8 = 10.6 g ✅; atoms: C 3 + 1 = 4, H 6 + 4 = 10 = 8 + 2, O 2 + 1 = 3 = 2 + 1 ✅. (Methanol zehreela: sirf kaagaz par.)
7. CH₃COOH + C₃H₇OH ⇌ CH₃COOC₃H₇ + H₂O; ester **propyl ethanoate**. Propanol = C₃H₈O = 36 + 8 + 16 = **60**, 6.0 ÷ 60 = **0.1 mol**; ethanoic acid bhi 0.1 mol (6.0 g). Ester C₅H₁₀O₂ = 60 + 10 + 32 = **102**, 0.1 × 102 = **10.2 g**; paani 1.8 g. Jaanch: 6.0 + 6.0 = 12.0 g; 10.2 + 1.8 = 12.0 g ✅.
8. (a) HCOOH + CH₃OH ⇌ HCOOCH₃ + H₂O, ester **methyl methanoate**. (b) HCOOCH₃ = C₂H₄O₂ = 24 + 4 + 32 = **60**. Atoms: C 1 + 1 = 2, H 2 + 4 = 6 = 4 + 2, O 2 + 1 = 3 = 2 + 1 ✅. (c) Molecular formula ek (C₂H₄O₂), banawat alag: **isomers** (functional isomers, Topic 3). Behave alike nahi: ethanoic acid baking soda se **fizz** karta hai, methyl methanoate nahi.
9. C₂H₅COOCH₃ + H₂O ⇌ C₂H₅COOH + CH₃OH. Products: **propanoic acid** aur **methanol**. Atoms: baayen C 4, H 8 + 2 = 10, O 2 + 1 = 3; daayen C 3 + 1 = 4, H 6 + 4 = 10, O 2 + 1 = 3 ✅.
10. Acid = 300 × 0.06 = **18 g** = 18 ÷ 60 = **0.3 mol**. NaHCO₃ = 0.3 × 84 = **25.2 g**. CO₂ = 0.3 × 22.4 = **6.72 L**. Jaanch: 18 + 25.2 = 43.2 g; daayein CH₃COONa 0.3 × 82 = 24.6, H₂O 5.4, CO₂ 0.3 × 44 = 13.2: 24.6 + 5.4 + 13.2 = 43.2 g ✅.
11. **P = ethanoic acid** (litmus laal aur baking soda se fizz: sirf acid ye karta hai). **Q = ethyl ethanoate** (phal jaisi khushbu, paani mein nahi milta, neutral). **R = ethanol** (neutral, sanitiser jaisi gandh, paani mein har anupaat mein milta hai). Teeno kahani se alag hain.
12. (a) 12 °C par (17 se neeche) **thos/aadha jama**; 25 °C par **liquid**. (b) Ethanol (78) < paani (100) < ethanoic acid (118).
13. Glacial acid **gaadha** (kam paani, bahut zyada acid) hai, par **strong** ka matlab hai paani mein lagbhag poora ion mein tootna, jo wo nahi karta. Barabar concentration par HCl aur ethanoic acid mein Mg ribbon (HCl mein tez bulbule) ya LED tester (HCl mein tez) se asli taaqat dikhti hai.
14. (a) **False** ❌: sirf acid fizz karta hai, alcohol nahi. (b) **False** ❌: acid + base se namak aur paani; ester acid + **alcohol** se. (c) **True** ✅: dono H₂ dete hain. (d) **False** ❌: ester covalent hai. (e) **False** ❌: sirf **ek** (O se juda H).
