# Topic 8: Sabun Aur Detergent: Safai Ka Chemistry

Topic 7 mein hamne ethanoic acid dekha aur ye ki acid aur alcohol milkar **ester** banate hain, jiski gandh fruity hoti hai. Ester ek aur jagah chhupa hai: **tel aur charbi (fat)** bhi ester hain, bas unke ander ki lambi carbon zanjeeren bahut lambi hain. Aur jab in lambi zanjeeron wale padarth ko NaOH ke saath tod diya jaata hai, to wo cheez milti hai jise tum roz haath par ragadte ho: **sabun**. Class 9 mein tumne dekha tha ki kathor paani mein sabun **scum** banata hai aur detergent nahi banata ([water-part3.md](../../../class9/chemistry/water/water-part3.md)): wahan sabun ka formula "lamba hai, yaad karna zaroori nahi" kaha tha, aur detergent ka formula "aage ki class mein" kaha tha. Wo "aage" aaj hai: sabun asal mein **hai kya**, wo **gandagi kaise nikalta hai**, aur detergent kyun scum nahi banata.

## Hostel ka bathroom aur oily thali

Mr. Shelar apne bhanje Dhananjay se milne uske school ke hostel gaye. Ravivaar ki subah Dhananjay unhe apna bathroom dikhane le gaya. "Mama, yahan ka paani borewell se aata hai. Mr. Shelar ne dekha: sachmuch, mug ke andar bhi ek safed pappdi jami thi.

Phir dono mess mein gaye. Wahan ek bhaiya steel ki thaliyan dho rahe the. Pehle unhone sirf **nal ke paani** se ek thali dhoi: thali chamak to gayi, par ungli lagao to **chikni, tel ki parat** abhi bhi wahin thi. Phir unhone usi paani mein **thoda sa dhulai ka liquid** daala, aur thali **kachar-kachar** saaf ho gayi.

Dhananjay ne sir khujlaya. "Mama, Class 9 mein padha tha ki kathor paani mein sabun calcium ka scum banata hai. Par ye sawaal ab bhi hai: sirf paani se tel kyun nahi jaata? "Teeno sawaal ek hi cheez se judte hain: sabun aur detergent ke **do-mooh wale** molecule. Chalo, unka asli chehra dekhte hain."

## Part A: Gandagi kya hai, aur paani akela kyun haar jaata hai

Thali ka tel, kapde par ghee ka daag, haath ki chikni: ye sab **grease** hain. Grease ke molecule lambi carbon-hydrogen zanjeeron se bane hain. C aur H ki electronegativity ka antar sirf **2.5 − 2.1 = 0.4** hai ([periodic-properties-part4.md](../periodic-properties/periodic-properties-part4.md) ka Pauling maan), to C–H bond lagbhag **non-polar** hai. Matlab grease **non-polar** hai.

Ab Chapter 7 ka niyam yaad karo: **jaisa jaise ko ghulata hai** ([chemical-bonding-ii-part2.md](../chemical-bonding-ii/chemical-bonding-ii-part2.md)). Paani **polar** hai. Polar paani non-polar grease se nahi milta: tel aur paani ki jar mein tel upar alag parat bana leta hai. Isliye sirf paani grease ko pakad kar kapde se **alag nahi kar paata**. Aur dhool? Dhool aksar **grease se chipki** hoti hai: grease ek chipkane wali gond jaisa kaam karti hai. Grease nahi hati, dhool bhi nahi hatti.

Kya chahiye? Ek aisa "dost" jiska **ek sira paani se dosti kare aur doosra sira grease se**. Wo beech ka mediator hoga.

## Part B: Sabun kya hai: do-mooh wala molecule

**Fat ya tel** (jaise ghee, mungfali ka tel) lambi zanjeer wale **fatty acids** ke **ester** hote hain (Topic 7 ka ester, par teen lambi zanjeeron wala). NaOH ke saath inhe todne par **sabun** milta hai. Ek aam sabun ka ion hai **stearate**: **C₁₇H₃₅COO⁻**, aur sabun ka formula **C₁₇H₃₅COONa** (sodium stearate). Iska acid, **stearic acid C₁₇H₃₅COOH**, bilkul wahi carboxylic acid wala CₙH₂ₙ₊₁COOH formula hai (Topic 2) jisme n = 17. Aur ethanoic acid se CH₃COONa banta tha, waise hi yahan H ki jagah Na aa gaya.

Figure 8.1 stearate ion ki poori zanjeer dikhata hai.

```
      H   H         H   H
      I   I         I   I
  H – C – C – ... – C – C – C = O
      I   I         I   I   I
      H   H         H   H   O⁻   Na⁺
```

> **Figure 8.1** – Sodium stearate, C₁₇H₃₅COONa. Baayen se: CH₃, phir CH₂ ... aur "..." ki jagah 13 aur CH₂ ke group hain (kul 17 carbon ki zanjeer: 1 CH₃ aur 16 CH₂), phir aakhir mein –COO⁻ (C = O aur O⁻) wala sir, jiske saath Na⁺ hai. Har carbon ki valency 4 aur har H ki 1 gin li gayi: pehla C (3 H aur 1 C), har CH₂ (2 H aur 2 C), sir wala C (ek C, ek C = O dabal bond yaani 2, aur ek O⁻: kul 4).

Is lambi zanjeer ko roz likhna mushkil hai, isliye **C₁₇H₃₅–** likhte hain. Ye zanjeer ek alkane (heptadecane, C₁₇H₃₆: CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ mein n = 17 se 36) ka ek H kam hissa hai.

### Example 1. Sodium stearate ka molar mass aur poonchh

**Sawaal:** Sodium stearate C₁₇H₃₅COONa ka molar mass nikalo. Phir bataao ki non-polar poonchh C₁₇H₃₅ ka mass kitna hai. (C 12, H 1, O 16, Na 23.)

**Kadam 1:** formula mein kul C = 17 + 1 = **18**, H = **35**, O = **2**, Na = **1**.
**Kadam 2:** mass = 18 × 12 + 35 × 1 + 2 × 16 + 23 = 216 + 35 + 32 + 23 = **306** g/mol.
**Kadam 3:** poonchh C₁₇H₃₅ = 17 × 12 + 35 = 204 + 35 = **239**. Sir COONa = 12 + 32 + 23 = **67**.

**Jaanch:** 239 + 67 = 306 ✅. Matlab sabun ke ek ion ka **lagbhag 78 percent mass (239 ÷ 306)** non-polar poonchh ka hai: sabun mukhya roop se "lambi poonchh" hi hai.

### Do sire, do swabhaav

Sodium stearate paani mein **ionic** namak ki tarah ghulta hai (Chapter 7): C₁₇H₃₅COONa → C₁₇H₃₅COO⁻ + Na⁺. Ion ke do sire hain:

- **Sir (COO⁻):** charge wala, polar. **Paani se dosti** (hydrophilic).
- **Poonchh (C₁₇H₃₅–):** lambi hydrocarbon zanjeer, non-polar. **Paani se doori, grease se dosti** (hydrophobic). Grease bhi to hydrocarbon zanjeer hai: "jaisa jaise ko ghulata hai".

```
      sir                        poonchh
      COO⁻                       C₁₇H₃₅–  (lambi hydrocarbon zanjeer)
     ( - )~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
   paani se dosti             grease (tel) se dosti
   (polar, hydrophilic)       (non-polar, hydrophobic)
```

> **Figure 8.2** – Sabun ka ek ion tadpole jaisa: gol sir (COO⁻) paani ki taraf aur lambi poonchh grease ki taraf. Yahi do-mooh wala swabhaav safai ki jadd hai.

Ek aur mazedaar baat: sabun **weak acid (fatty acid) aur strong base (NaOH)** ka namak hai, isliye sabun ka ghol thoda **base** jaisa hota hai (lagbhag pH 9 se 10, Chapter 1 mein haldi ke rumaal par sabun se rang badalna yaad karo: [acids-bases-and-salts.md](../acids-bases-and-salts/acids-bases-and-salts.md); sabun ke paani ka pH: [acids-bases-and-salts-part4.md](../acids-bases-and-salts/acids-bases-and-salts-part4.md)).

## Part C: Micelle aur safai ka tareeka

Jab sabun paani mein ghulta hai aur kapde par grease ki boond hoti hai, to sabun ke ions **grease ki taraf apni poonchh** kar dete hain (poonchh ko grease pasand hai) aur **sir paani ki taraf** rakhte hain. Bahut se ions ek saath grease ki boond ko ghere lete hain aur ek **gola** banta hai: **micelle**. Poonchhein andar (grease ko pakde), sir bahar (paani ko chhute).

```
                  (-)  (-)  (-)
                   ~    ~    ~
                   ~    ~    ~
         (-) ~~~~              ~~~~ (-)
         (-) ~~~~    grease    ~~~~ (-)
         (-) ~~~~     ki boond ~~~~ (-)
                   ~    ~    ~
                   ~    ~    ~
                  (-)  (-)  (-)
              bahar: paani (polar)
```

> **Figure 8.3** – Micelle ka kaat: 12 sabun ke ion (upar 3, neeche 3, baayen 3, daayen 3). Har ion ka sir (-) bahar paani ki taraf aur poonchh (~) andar, grease ki boond ke chaaron taraf. Bahar ki sataha par sirf charge wale sir hain, isliye micelle paani mein ghul kar tairta hai.

Ek aur baat: har micelle ke bahar **ek jaisa (negative) charge** hai, isliye micelle ek doosre ko **door dhakelte** hain aur jama hokar phir se bade grease ke tukde nahi banate. Wo paani mein **bikhre hue** rehte hain.

```
 1. Kapde par grease          2. Poonchhein grease mein      3. Malne se micelle bana,
    paani mein sabun             sir paani ki taraf             paani ke saath bah gaya

 paani ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~       paani ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~       paani ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
   (-)    (-)    (-)            (-)   (-)   (-)                 (-) (-)
    ~      ~      ~              ~     ~     ~                 (-)#####(-)  <-- micelle
         #####                    ###########                     (-) (-)       bah gaya
 kapda =============          kapda =============          kapda =============
 (grease kapde par)            (poonchhein grease mein)       (kapda saaf)
```

> **Figure 8.4** – Safai ke teen kadam. (1) sabun ke ion paani mein ghoomte hain, grease kapde se chipki hai. (2) ion apni poonchh grease mein ghusa dete hain aur sir paani ki taraf rehte hain. (3) kapda malne ya hilane se grease kapde se chhoot kar micelle ban jaati hai aur paani ke saath bah jaati hai.

**Kyun malna padta hai?** Micelle bananey aur grease ko kapde se uthane ke liye halka dhakka chahiye: malna ya hilana wo dhakka deta hai. Phir saaf paani se dhone par micelle (aur uske andar ki grease) bah jaate hain.

## Part D: Sabun kaise banta hai: saponification (sirf padhne ke liye)

Fat ya tel ka ek molecule **teen lambi zanjeer wale fatty acid + glycerol** ka ester hai. NaOH ke saath garam karne par ester toot-ta hai (Topic 7 mein ester ka ulta rasta, hydrolysis, ek line mein aaya tha; yahan ye NaOH se hota hai): fatty acid ka **sodium namak (sabun)** aur **glycerol** banta hai. Is reaction ka naam **saponification** hai.

fat (ya tel) + 3NaOH → 3 C₁₇H₃₅COONa (sabun) + C₃H₅(OH)₃ (glycerol)

(Yahan fat ko stearic acid wala maan kar likha hai: asli fat aur tel mein alag-alag lambi zanjeeron ka mel hota hai.)

```
       glass rod (teacher hilate hain)
                \
                 \
        +---------\--------+
        :   ~~~~~~~\~~~~~~ :   <-- tel + NaOH ka ghol
        :  ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ :
        +------------------+
     [====  electric hot plate  ====]
```

> **Figure 8.5** – Saponification ka teacher demo (sirf dekhne ke liye): beaker mein tel aur NaOH ka ghol, niche electric hot plate se halki garmi aur glass rod se hilana. Teacher goggles aur gloves pehente hain, bachche door.

Demo mein kuch der garam karne par mishran gaadha hota jaata hai. Phir **namak (NaCl) ka ghol** milate hain: sabun namak ke paani mein kam ghulta hai, isliye wo **upar aa kar jam jaata hai** (isse "salting out" kehte hain). Glycerol paani mein ghula reh jaata hai. Sabun ko chhaan kar nikal lete hain. Glycerol saaf kiya jaata hai aur **cosmetics aur dawaiyon** mein kaam aata hai.

### Example 2. 8.9 g charbi (fat) se sabun

**Sawaal:** Ek sirf-padhne-ke-liye maan lo: stearic acid wali charbi **tristearin** (C₅₇H₁₁₀O₆, molar mass 890) ke 8.9 g ka NaOH se saponification hota hai: C₅₇H₁₁₀O₆ + 3NaOH → 3C₁₇H₃₅COONa + C₃H₅(OH)₃. Kitna NaOH lagega, kitna sabun aur kitna glycerol banega? (H 1, C 12, O 16, Na 23.)

**Kadam 1 (moles):** 8.9 ÷ 890 = **0.01 mol**.
**Kadam 2 (ratio 1 : 3 : 3 : 1):** NaOH = 3 × 0.01 = 0.03 mol × 40 = **1.2 g**. Sabun = 0.03 mol × 306 = **9.18 g**. Glycerol = 0.01 mol × 92 = **0.92 g** (C₃H₈O₃ = 36 + 8 + 48 = 92).

**Jaanch:** baayein 8.9 + 1.2 = 10.1 g; daayein 9.18 + 0.92 = 10.1 g ✅. Atoms: C 57 = 3 × 18 + 3, H 110 + 3 = 113 = 3 × 35 + 8, O 6 + 3 = 9 = 3 × 2 + 3, Na 3 = 3 ✅.

## Part E: Kathor paani mein sabun: scum ki asli kahani

Class 9 ([water-part3.md](../../../class9/chemistry/water/water-part3.md)) mein dekha ki Ca²⁺ ya Mg²⁺ wale paani mein sabun **na ghulne wala scum** banata hai. Ab hum ye saaf dekh sakte hain **kyun**. Do stearate ion ek Ca²⁺ ko pakad lete hain:

2C₁₇H₃₅COONa + Ca²⁺ → (C₁₇H₃₅COO)₂Ca + 2Na⁺

```
 ~~~~~~~~~~(-) Ca²⁺ (-)~~~~~~~~~~
   poonchh    sir        sir   poonchh

 charge ka hisaab: (-1) + (+2) + (-1) = 0
```

> **Figure 8.6** – Calcium stearate: do stearate ion ke sir ek Ca²⁺ ko beech mein pakad lete hain aur dono poonchhein bahar nikalti hain. Kul charge 0 hai: na koi charge wala sir paani ki taraf bachta hai, na paani se mel. Isliye ye **na ghulne wala** namak hai aur scum ban kar jam jaata hai.

Matlab: **sabun ke ion ka sir Ca²⁺ ne bandh liya**, to wo na micelle banata hai, na safai karta hai. Jab tak saare Ca²⁺ aur Mg²⁺ khatam nahi hote, sabun scum banane mein hi kharch hota rehta hai (Class 9 wali baat, ab asli kaaran ke saath).

### Example 3. Kitna sabun scum mein gaya?

**Sawaal:** Ek balti ke kathor paani mein 1.11 g CaCl₂ ghula hai. Poora Ca²⁺ sodium stearate se scum ban jaata hai: 2C₁₇H₃₅COONa + CaCl₂ → (C₁₇H₃₅COO)₂Ca + 2NaCl. Kitna sabun (sodium stearate) bekaar gaya aur kitna scum bana? (Ca 40, Cl 35.5, C 12, H 1, O 16, Na 23.)

**Kadam 1:** CaCl₂ = 40 + 71 = **111** g/mol; moles = 1.11 ÷ 111 = **0.01 mol**.
**Kadam 2 (ratio 2 : 1 : 1 : 2):** sabun = 2 × 0.01 = 0.02 mol × 306 = **6.12 g**.
**Kadam 3:** scum (C₁₇H₃₅COO)₂Ca = 2 × 283 + 40 = **606** g/mol (stearate C₁₇H₃₅COO = 18 × 12 + 35 + 32 = 283). Scum = 0.01 × 606 = **6.06 g**. NaCl = 0.02 × 58.5 = **1.17 g**.

**Jaanch:** 6.12 + 1.11 = 7.23 g; 6.06 + 1.17 = 7.23 g ✅. Atoms: C 36, H 70, O 4, Ca 1, Cl 2, Na 2 dono taraf ✅.

Dhyan do: sirf **1.11 g** CaCl₂ ne **6.12 g** sabun bekaar kar diya. Isliye kathor paani mein sabun bahut lagta hai.

## Part F: Detergent: do-mooh wala, par alag sir ke saath

**Synthetic detergent** bhi sabun jaisa hi do-mooh wala hai: lambi hydrocarbon **poonchh** aur charge wala **sir**. Fark sir mein hai: sabun ka sir **–COO⁻ (carboxylate)** hai, detergent ka sir **sulphate (–O–SO₃⁻)** ya **sulphonate (–SO₃⁻)** hota hai. Do aam naam:

- **Sodium lauryl sulphate**, C₁₂H₂₅–O–SO₃Na: kai shampoo aur dhulai ke liquid mein.
- **Sodium alkylbenzene sulphonate**: kapde dhone ke powder mein; iske beech mein ek ring wala hissa (benzene ring) hota hai, uska poora hisaab aage ki class mein.

```
      H   H         H   H
      I   I         I   I
  H – C – C – ... – C – C – O – SO₃⁻  Na⁺
      I   I         I   I
      H   H         H   H
```

> **Figure 8.7** – Sodium lauryl sulphate, C₁₂H₂₅–O–SO₃Na. Baayen CH₃, phir CH₂ ... "..." ki jagah 8 aur CH₂ ke group, aakhir mein do CH₂ aur uske baad O–SO₃⁻ ka sir (kul 12 carbon: 1 CH₃ aur 11 CH₂). Har carbon ki valency 4 aur har H ki 1 gin li gayi: pehla C (3 H, 1 C), har CH₂ (2 H, 2 C), aakhri CH₂ (2 H, 1 C aur 1 O).

### Example 4. Lauryl sulphate ka molar mass

**Sawaal:** C₁₂H₂₅OSO₃Na ka molar mass aur uski poonchh C₁₂H₂₅ ka mass nikalo. (H 1, C 12, O 16, Na 23, S 32.)

**Kadam 1:** atoms: C 12, H 25, O = 1 + 3 = 4, S 1, Na 1.
**Kadam 2:** 12 × 12 + 25 × 1 + 4 × 16 + 32 + 23 = 144 + 25 + 64 + 32 + 23 = **288** g/mol.
**Kadam 3:** poonchh C₁₂H₂₅ = 144 + 25 = **169**. Sir OSO₃Na = 16 + 32 + 48 + 23 = **119**.

**Jaanch:** 169 + 119 = 288 ✅. Poonchh C₁₂ ki hai, stearate (C₁₇) se chhoti: lagbhag 169 ÷ 288 ≈ 59 percent.

### Detergent kathor paani mein kyun chalta hai?

Detergent ka sir sulphate ya sulphonate hai. Isliye Ca²⁺ ya Mg²⁺ ke saath jo namak banta hai, wo **paani mein ghula rehta hai** (Class 9 mein bhi yahi dekha tha: "detergent ka calcium-magnesium wala namak ghul jaata hai"). Scum nahi banta, to detergent **bekaar nahi jaata**: micelle banta hai, grease nikalta hai, aur jhaag bhi aata hai. (Sulphate ke sir ka Ca²⁺ ke saath itna aaram se kyun ghulna: ye aage ki class mein.)

### Biodegradability aur paryavaran

- **Biodegradable:** bacteria ke liye **seedhi, shakha-rahit lambi zanjeer** tod dena aasaan hai. Purane zamane ke detergent mein **shakha wali (branched)** zanjeeren thi, jinhe bacteria nahi tod pate the: nadiyon aur talabon mein **jhaag ke pahaad** bante the. Ab seedhi zanjeer wale detergent bante hain, jo aasaani se tootte hain. Sabun bhi biodegradable hai.
- **Phosphate:** kuch dhulai ke powder mein phosphate hota hai (paani ko mridu banane ke liye). Nadi ya talab mein pahunch kar wo **algae (kai) ko khaad** deta hai: algae bahut badh jaate hain, marne par sadne mein paani ki oxygen kharch hoti hai, aur machhliyan marti hain. Isliye ab phosphate-free powder bante hain.

## Part G: Sabun aur detergent: ek tulna

| Baat | Sabun | Synthetic detergent |
|---|---|---|
| Kahan se | fat ya tel + NaOH (prakritik fat se) | factory mein bane (aksar petroleum ke rasayanon se) |
| Sir | –COO⁻ (carboxylate) | –O–SO₃⁻ ya –SO₃⁻ (sulphate, sulphonate) |
| Kathor paani mein | **scum** banata hai (Ca ya Mg ka sabun, nahi ghulta) | **scum nahi**, calcium wala namak ghulta hai |
| Safai | micelle banane se, par kathor paani mein kam | micelle banane se, kathor paani mein bhi |
| Biodegradable | haan | seedhi zanjeer wale haan |
| Sabun ka ghol | thoda base (pH lagbhag 9 se 10) | detergent ke hisaab se alag |

## Khud karke dekho: lather ki jaanch (adult ke paas)

(Class 9 mein tum ne tap, ubla aur distilled paani se jhaag naapa tha. Ye activity **kathor paani khud banakar** sabun aur detergent ko saath rakhti hai.)

> **Activity 8.1 (adult ke saath).** Teen saaf, same size ki **chhoti bottle** (dhakkan wali) lo. Har mein **100 mL** saaf paani (distilled ya ubal kar thanda kiya hua).
> - **Bottle P:** sirf paani. Usme **sabun ke 5 chhote barabar tukde**.
> - **Bottle Q:** paani mein **2 se 3 chutki Epsom salt** (dukaan se, adult laye) ghol kar kathor paani banao. Usme **wahi 5 sabun ke tukde**.
> - **Bottle R:** wahi kathor paani (Epsom salt wala). Usme **detergent (jis par label par "detergent" likha ho) ki 3 boondein**.

> **Safety:** Epsom salt aur detergent ko **kabhi chakhna nahi**, aankh se door rakho. Ye paani peena nahi. Kaam ke baad paani sink mein baha do aur haath dho lo. (Chalk ka powder bahut kam ghulta hai, isliye Epsom salt zyada saaf asar dikhata hai.)

Tum dekhoge: **P** mein kaafi jhaag, paani saaf. **Q** mein kam jhaag aur **dahi jaisi safed parat (magnesium ka sabun)** jo paani ko gadla karti hai. **R** mein jhaag aata hai aur scum nahi, kyunki detergent ka Mg²⁺ wala namak ghula rehta hai.

**Hazard label detective (adult ke saath):** ghar ke dhulai ke powder ya liquid ke label par bina kholein padho: kya "aankh mein jaye to dho lo" ya "bachcho se door" likha hai? Ek line likh lo.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti: "Sabun aur detergent ek hi cheez hain: kisi bhi paani mein ek jaise kaam karte hain."**

Ye isliye sahi lagta hai ki dono jhaag dete hain aur dono kapde saaf karte hain, aur dono do-mooh wale hain. Par **paani ki kismat** farq laati hai. Dhananjay ke hostel ki kahani se dekhte hain.

1. **Mridu paani mein:** baarish ka saaf paani lo. Sabun bhi achha jhaag deta hai aur detergent bhi. Yahan farq **nahi** dikhta.
2. **Hostel ka kathor paani:** borewell ke paani mein sabun ragdo: jhaag nahi, safed scum (Figure 8.6). Usi paani mein dhulai ka detergent daalo: jhaag aata hai, thali saaf ho jaati hai (Figure 8.7 ka sir: sulphate).
3. **Dono ke scum ka hisaab:** Example 3 mein sirf 1.11 g CaCl₂ ne 6.12 g sodium stearate khatam kar diya. Detergent ka calcium wala namak ghulta hai, to wo sabun jaisa "kharch" nahi hota.

| Paani | Sabun | Detergent |
|---|---|---|
| Mridu (saaf baarish ka) | achha jhaag, saaf paani | achha jhaag, saaf paani |
| Kathor (Ca²⁺, Mg²⁺ wala) | scum, kam jhaag, sabun bekaar | jhaag, scum nahi |

**Niyam:** dono ka kaam ek jaisa (do-mooh wale ion, micelle). Par **kathor paani mein sabun scum banata hai, detergent nahi**: isliye "kisi bhi paani mein ek jaise" kehna galat hai.

## Quick Recap

- Grease **non-polar** hai (lambi hydrocarbon zanjeer, C–H ka antar 0.4): paani akela use nahi hata sakta (jaisa jaise ko ghulata hai).
- **Sabun** = lambi zanjeer wale fatty acid ka sodium namak, jaise **C₁₇H₃₅COONa** (molar mass 306). Do sire: **sir COO⁻ (hydrophilic)**, **poonchh C₁₇H₃₅– (hydrophobic)**.
- **Micelle:** poonchhein andar (grease ko pakdein), sir bahar (paani ki taraf). Malne se grease micelle ban kar bah jaati hai. Micelle ek doosre ko dhakelte hain, isliye bikhre rehte hain.
- **Saponification:** fat (ya tel) + 3NaOH → 3 sabun + glycerol C₃H₅(OH)₃. NaCl se sabun upar aata hai ("salting out"). Sirf teacher demo: NaOH corrosive hai.
- **Kathor paani:** 2C₁₇H₃₅COONa + Ca²⁺ → (C₁₇H₃₅COO)₂Ca + 2Na⁺: na ghulne wala **scum**, sabun bekaar.
- **Detergent:** do-mooh wala, par sir **sulphate ya sulphonate** (jaise sodium lauryl sulphate C₁₂H₂₅OSO₃Na, molar mass 288); Ca²⁺ aur Mg²⁺ wale namak **ghulte hain**, to kathor paani mein bhi chalta hai.
- **Seedhi zanjeer** wale detergent biodegradable; phosphate se algae badhte hain (paryavaran).
- **Galti:** "sabun aur detergent kisi bhi paani mein ek jaise" galat hai: mridu paani mein haan, kathor paani mein nahi.

## Practice Questions

(Sab sawaal paper par hal karne ke liye hain. Mass: H 1, C 12, O 16, Na 23, Mg 24, S 32, Cl 35.5, Ca 40. Koi bhi reaction ghar par nahi karna.)

1. Palmitic acid, C₁₅H₃₁COOH, is converted into sodium palmitate by NaOH: C₁₅H₃₁COOH + NaOH → C₁₅H₃₁COONa + H₂O. Find the mass of sodium palmitate and of NaOH for 25.6 g of the acid. Check by mass conservation.
2. In a teacher demo, 40.3 g of tripalmitin, C₅₁H₉₈O₆ (molar mass 806), is saponified: C₅₁H₉₈O₆ + 3NaOH → 3C₁₅H₃₁COONa + C₃H₅(OH)₃. Find the masses of NaOH needed, sodium palmitate formed and glycerol formed.
3. Hard water in a bucket contains 2.22 g of CaCl₂. All the Ca²⁺ is removed as scum by sodium palmitate: 2C₁₅H₃₁COONa + CaCl₂ → (C₁₅H₃₁COO)₂Ca + 2NaCl. Find the mass of sodium palmitate wasted and the mass of scum formed.
4. A hostel tank holds hard water with 3.6 g of MgSO₄. Sodium stearate removes the Mg²⁺: 2C₁₇H₃₅COONa + MgSO₄ → (C₁₇H₃₅COO)₂Mg + Na₂SO₄. Find the mass of sodium stearate used up and the mass of the magnesium scum.
5. Sodium lauryl sulphate has the formula C₁₂H₂₅OSO₃Na. Find the mass of its non-polar tail C₁₂H₂₅ and of its head OSO₃Na, and show that they add up to the molar mass.
6. The tail of sodium palmitate is C₁₅H₃₁–. Show that it is a 15-carbon alkane with one hydrogen removed, and write the formula of palmitic acid in the form CₙH₂ₙ₊₁COOH.
7. Explain in 3 to 4 lines why the tails of soap ions point inwards and the heads outwards in a micelle, using polar and non-polar ideas.
8. A steel plate is smeared with butter (a fat). Why does cold water alone not clean it, and what does warm soapy water do differently? Answer in four lines using the words polar, non-polar and micelle.
9. Three buckets are filled at a village: bucket X with rainwater and a bar of soap, bucket Y with well water containing Ca²⁺ ions and the same soap, bucket Z with the same well water and a washing powder (the calcium salt of this detergent dissolves in water). Predict the lather and the water's appearance in each bucket, with a reason.
10. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) A soap ion has two different ends. (b) Scum is sodium stearate. (c) Soap and detergent behave the same in hard water. (d) Glycerol is formed when a fat is saponified. (e) A soap solution is acidic.
11. After saponification, common salt solution is added to the mixture and the soap floats up. Given that soap is less soluble in salty water and glycerol stays dissolved, explain this step in two lines.
12. A town lake turns green and fish die after heavy use of a phosphate-containing washing powder nearby. Given that algae grow fast on phosphate and that dying algae use up the water's oxygen as they rot, explain in 3 lines what happened.
13. Why must children never make soap at home with caustic soda, and name two safety rules for the lather activity.

### Answers

1. Palmitic acid C₁₅H₃₁COOH = 16 × 12 + 32 + 32 = 192 + 32 + 32 = **256**. Moles = 25.6 ÷ 256 = **0.1 mol**. Ratio 1 : 1 : 1 : 1. Sodium palmitate C₁₅H₃₁COONa = 192 + 31 + 32 + 23 = **278**; 0.1 × 278 = **27.8 g**. NaOH = 0.1 × 40 = **4.0 g**. **Jaanch:** 25.6 + 4.0 = 29.6 g; daayein 27.8 + (0.1 × 18 = 1.8) = 29.6 g ✅.
2. Moles = 40.3 ÷ 806 = **0.05 mol**. Ratio 1 : 3 : 3 : 1. NaOH = 0.15 × 40 = **6.0 g**. Sabun = 0.15 × 278 = **41.7 g**. Glycerol = 0.05 × 92 = **4.6 g**. **Jaanch:** 40.3 + 6.0 = 46.3 g; 41.7 + 4.6 = 46.3 g ✅. Atoms: C 51 = 3 × 16 + 3, H 98 + 3 = 101 = 3 × 31 + 8, O 6 + 3 = 9 = 3 × 2 + 3, Na 3 ✅.
3. CaCl₂ = 111; moles = 2.22 ÷ 111 = **0.02 mol**. Ratio 2 : 1 : 1 : 2. Sabun = 0.04 mol × 278 = **11.12 g**. Scum (C₁₅H₃₁COO)₂Ca = 2 × 255 + 40 = **550** (palmitate C₁₅H₃₁COO = 192 + 31 + 32 = 255); 0.02 × 550 = **11.0 g**. NaCl = 0.04 × 58.5 = 2.34 g. **Jaanch:** 11.12 + 2.22 = 13.34 g; 11.0 + 2.34 = 13.34 g ✅. Atoms C 32, H 62, O 4, Ca 1, Cl 2, Na 2 dono taraf ✅.
4. MgSO₄ = 24 + 32 + 64 = **120**; moles = 3.6 ÷ 120 = **0.03 mol**. Ratio 2 : 1 : 1 : 1. Sodium stearate = 0.06 mol × 306 = **18.36 g**. Scum (C₁₇H₃₅COO)₂Mg = 2 × 283 + 24 = **590**; 0.03 × 590 = **17.7 g**. Na₂SO₄ = 0.03 × 142 = 4.26 g. **Jaanch:** 18.36 + 3.6 = 21.96 g; 17.7 + 4.26 = 21.96 g ✅.
5. Poonchh C₁₂H₂₅ = 144 + 25 = **169**. Sir OSO₃Na = 16 + 32 + 48 + 23 = **119**. Jod: 169 + 119 = **288**. Seedha: 144 + 25 + 64 + 32 + 23 = 288 ✅.
6. Pentadecane (15 carbon alkane) = CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ mein n = 15 se **C₁₅H₃₂**. Usme se ek H hata do to **C₁₅H₃₁–** ✅. Palmitic acid CₙH₂ₙ₊₁COOH mein n = 15: **C₁₅H₃₁COOH** (kul 16 C, 32 H, 2 O) ✅.
7. Poonchh non-polar hai: grease ko pasand karti hai, paani ko nahi, isliye grease ke andar rehti hai. Sir charge wala polar hai: paani ko pasand karta hai, isliye bahar paani ki taraf. Is tarah micelle ka bahar polar paani ke saath mel khaata hai aur grease andar band hoti hai.
8. Butter **non-polar** hai aur thanda paani **polar**: wo nahi milte (jaisa jaise ko ghulata hai). Sabun ke ion ki **non-polar poonchh** butter mein ghusti hai, polar sir paani mein rehta hai. Garam paani mein butter narm hoti hai aur malne se **micelle** ban kar bah jaata hai.
9. **X (baarish ka mridu paani + sabun):** achha jhaag, saaf paani (Ca²⁺ nahi). **Y (kathor kuan ka paani + sabun):** kam jhaag aur safed scum (calcium ka sabun), paani gadla, kyunki sabun scum mein kharch ho gaya. **Z (kathor paani + washing powder):** jhaag aata hai aur scum nahi, kyunki detergent ka Ca²⁺ wala namak ghulta hai.
10. (a) **True** ✅: sir polar, poonchh non-polar. (b) **False** ❌: scum **calcium ya magnesium ka sabun** hai (sodium stearate paani mein ghulta hai). (c) **False** ❌: kathor paani mein sabun scum banata hai, detergent nahi. (d) **True** ✅. (e) **False** ❌: sabun ka ghol thoda **base** hai (pH lagbhag 9 se 10).
11. Namak ka ghol sabun ko kam ghulne wale halat mein le aata hai, isliye sabun **alag hokar upar tair** aata hai. Glycerol namak ke paani mein ghula rehta hai, isliye sabun glycerol se alag kiya ja sakta hai (salting out).
12. Phosphate algae ke liye khaad hai, isliye **algae bahut tezi se badhe** (paani hara hua). Marne par algae ke sadne mein **paani ki oxygen kharch hui**. Oxygen kam hone se **machhliyan mar gayi**. Yaani phosphate wala powder talab mein pahunch kar nuksan karta hai.
13. NaOH **bahut corrosive** hai (skin aur aankh ko jalata hai, nigalne par jaanlewa): isliye ghar par kabhi nahi, sirf trained teacher demo. Lather activity ke do niyam: **adult paas rahe**, **kuch bhi chakhna nahi, aankh se door rakhna, paani peena nahi** (aur kaam ke baad haath dho lo).
