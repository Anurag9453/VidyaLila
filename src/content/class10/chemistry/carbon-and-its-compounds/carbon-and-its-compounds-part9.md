## Part A: Chapter ka naksha: ek compound se doosre tak

Pichhle topics mein tumne ek ke baad ek **stations** dekhe. Ab Figure 9.1 mein ek hi naksha par saare stations aur unke beech ke teer dikhte hain. Dhyan do ki naksha ethene aur ethanol ke "parivaar" par bana hai, kyunki yahi chapter ke beech ka mool raasta hai.

```
   ALKYNE                    ALKENE                    ALKANE
   C₂H₂ =============>       C₂H₄ =============>       C₂H₆       CH₄ =====> CH₃Cl
         addition                  addition                       substitution
         (+H₂, Ni)                 (+H₂, Ni, garam)               (+Cl₂, sunlight)

                               I    ^
                   hydration   I    I  dehydration
                   (+H₂O,      v    I  (gaadha H₂SO₄,
                acid catalyst)         lagbhag 170 °C)

                             ALCOHOL                   ACID
                             C₂H₅OH =============>     CH₃COOH
                                   oxidation
                                   (K₂Cr₂O₇ ya KMnO₄)    I    ^
                                         esterification  I    I  hydrolysis
                                           (+ ethanol,   v    I  (+ paani)
                                         gaadha H₂SO₄)
                                                       ESTER
                                                       CH₃COOC₂H₅

   Har padarth + O₂ ---> CO₂ + H₂O (combustion): ye raasta har dabbe se nikalta hai
```

> **Figure 9.1** – Chapter ka reaction map. Upar ki line: alkyne se alkene se alkane tak H₂ jodna (addition). Alkane ke saath chlorine ki reaction substitution hai. Alkene aur alcohol ke beech do teer: neeche hydration (paani jodna), upar dehydration (paani nikalna). Alcohol se acid oxidation se, aur acid se ester esterification se; ester se wapas acid hydrolysis se. Har padarth jalne par CO₂ aur paani deta hai. Dhyan do: alkane se seedhe alkene ka koi teer is chapter mein nahi hai (wo industry ki alag tarkeeb hai: aage ki class mein).

Ab teer ko **teen sawaal** se padho aur table mein dekho:

| Raasta | Reaction ka naam | Reagent aur condition | Kahan padha |
|---|---|---|---|
| ethyne se ethene | addition | H₂, Ni catalyst | Topic 5 |
| ethene se ethane | addition | H₂, Ni, garam | Topic 5 |
| ethene se ethanol | addition (hydration) | bhaap, acid catalyst, ooncha temperature aur pressure | Topic 6 |
| ethanol se ethene | dehydration | gaadha H₂SO₄, lagbhag 170 °C | Topic 6 |
| ethanol se ethanoic acid | oxidation | acidified K₂Cr₂O₇ ya alkaline KMnO₄ | Topic 6 |
| ethanoic acid aur ethanol se ester | esterification | gaadha H₂SO₄ (catalyst), halki garmi | Topic 7 |
| ester se acid aur alcohol | hydrolysis | paani (NaOH ke saath saponification) | Topics 7 aur 8 |
| methane se chloromethane | substitution | Cl₂, sunlight | Topic 4 |
| koi bhi hydrocarbon ya alcohol se CO₂ aur paani | combustion | O₂, jalana | Topics 4, 5, 6 |
| ethene se polythene | polymerisation | n C₂H₄ | Topic 5 |

Ab Vikrant ki uljhan saaf hai. **Gaadha H₂SO₄** teer par do jagah aata hai, par uska kaam alag hai: ALCOHOL se ALKENE ke teer par wo **paani chheenta** hai aur condition hai lagbhag 170 °C. ACID aur ESTER ke teer par wo **catalyst** hai, aur condition halki garmi, aur saath mein **doosra reactant ethanol** bhi hai. Card ka naam ek jaisa dikhe to **teer ke dono sire** dekho: alcohol ek sire par aur alkene doosre par ho, to paani nikla (dehydration). Acid aur ester ke beech ho, to ek **doosra reactant (alcohol)** bhi judta hai aur paani bhi banta hai.

## Part B: Mixed sawaal: ek-ek karke

### Example 1. Naam se structure banana: 2-methylpentane

**Sawaal:** 2-methylpentane ka structure banao, formula likho aur gin kar jaancho.

**Kadam 1 (naam padho):** "pentane" ka matlab 5 carbon ki seedhi chain, sirf single bond. "2-methyl" ka matlab chain ke **doosre carbon par ek CH₃ (methyl) shakha**.
**Kadam 2 (chain likho):** C₁–C₂–C₃–C₄–C₅. C₂ par ek C (methyl) jodo.
**Kadam 3 (H bharo):** har C ke chaar bond poore karo, har H ka ek bond (Figure 9.2).

```
            H
            I
    H   H - C - H   H       H       H
    I       I       I       I       I
H - C ----- C ----- C ----- C ----- C - H
    I       I       I       I       I
    H       H       H       H       H
```

> **Figure 9.2** – 2-methylpentane. Neeche seedhi chain ke paanch C, aur doosre C ke upar ek methyl C. Valency ki ginti: pehle C par 3 H + 1 C = 4; doosre C par 2 C (chain) + 1 C (methyl) + 1 H = 4; teesre aur chauthe C par 2 H + 2 C = 4; paanchve C par 3 H + 1 C = 4; methyl wale C par 3 H + 1 C = 4. Har H ka ek bond.

**Formula:** C = 6, H = 3 + 1 + 2 + 2 + 3 + 3 = **14**, yaani C₆H₁₄.
**Jaanch:** 2 × 6 + 2 = 14 ✅ (alkane CₙH₂ₙ₊₂). Bond: C–C = 5, C–H = 14, kul 19 = 3 × 6 + 1 ✅. Ye hexane ka ek isomer hai (Topic 3: wahi formula, alag structure).

### Example 2. Formula dekh kar parivaar pehchano

**Sawaal:** Teen hydrocarbon ke formula C₇H₁₆, C₇H₁₄ aur C₇H₁₂ hain. Har ka parivaar aur saturated ya unsaturated batao.

**Kadam 1:** 7 carbon ke liye CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ = 2 × 7 + 2 = 16 H, CₙH₂ₙ = 14 H, CₙH₂ₙ₋₂ = 12 H.
**Kadam 2:** C₇H₁₆ **alkane** (heptane, saturated), C₇H₁₄ **alkene** (unsaturated, ek C=C), C₇H₁₂ **alkyne** (unsaturated, ek C≡C).

**Jaanch:** 16 − 14 = 2 aur 14 − 12 = 2: har extra jodi par 2 H kam ✅.
**Ek sawdhaani:** sirf formula kabhi-kabhi dhokha deta hai. **Cyclohexane**, C₆H₁₂, ek ring wala saturated compound hai jiska formula CₙH₂ₙ jaisa dikhta hai, par usme koi double bond nahi. Pakka faisla **bromine water** ki jaanch se hota hai (Topic 5).

### Example 3. Puzzle: compound A, B, C, D (Relay ka antim card)

**Sawaal:** Ek bina rang ka liquid **A** (formula C₂H₆O) sodium ke saath bulbule (H₂) deta hai. A ko gaadhe H₂SO₄ ke saath lagbhag 170 °C par garam karne par gas **B** banti hai, jo bromine water ka rang uda deti hai. A ko oxidising agent se oxidise karne par **C** banta hai, jo baking soda ke saath fizz karta hai aur jiski sirke jaisi gandh hai. A aur C ko gaadhe H₂SO₄ ke saath halka garam karne par **D** banta hai, jisse phal jaisi khushbu aati hai. A, B, C, D pehchano aur teen reaction likho.

**Kadam 1:** C₂H₆O ke do isomer hain: ethanol aur methoxymethane. Sodium ke saath H₂ sirf **O–H** wale ethanol se aata hai (Topic 6). **A = ethanol, C₂H₅OH.**
**Kadam 2:** paani nikalna (dehydration) aur bromine water ka rang udna matlab **unsaturated gas**: **B = ethene, C₂H₄.**
**Kadam 3:** oxidation se acid, aur baking soda se fizz matlab carbonate test: **C = ethanoic acid, CH₃COOH.**
**Kadam 4:** acid aur alcohol se fruity gandh wali cheez: **D = ethyl ethanoate, CH₃COOC₂H₅.**

C₂H₅OH → C₂H₄ + H₂O (dehydration)
C₂H₅OH + 2[O] → CH₃COOH + H₂O (oxidation)
CH₃COOH + C₂H₅OH ⇌ CH₃COOC₂H₅ + H₂O (esterification)

**Jaanch:** atoms: pehli: C 2 = 2, H 6 = 4 + 2, O 1 = 1; doosri: C 2 = 2, H 6 = 4 + 2, O 1 + 2 = 3 = 2 + 1; teesri: C 4 = 4, H 4 + 6 = 10 = 8 + 2, O 2 + 1 = 3 = 2 + 1 ✅.

### Example 4. Combustion ko balance karna: hexane

**Sawaal:** C₆H₁₄ + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O ko balance karo.

**Kadam 1 (carbon):** 6 C hain, to 6 CO₂.
**Kadam 2 (hydrogen):** 14 H hain, to 7 H₂O.
**Kadam 3 (oxygen):** daayein O = 6 × 2 + 7 = 19. To baayein O₂ = 19 ÷ 2 = 9.5.
**Kadam 4 (aadha hatao):** poori equation ko 2 se guna karo.

2C₆H₁₄ + 19O₂ → 12CO₂ + 14H₂O

**Jaanch:** C 12 = 12, H 28 = 14 × 2 = 28, O 38 = 24 + 14 = 38 ✅.

### Example 5. Hexane ke 8.6 g: kitni CO₂, kitni oxygen?

**Sawaal:** 8.6 g hexane poori tarah jalti hai (kagaz par). CO₂ ka mass aur STP par volume, aur oxygen ka mass batao. (H 1, C 12, O 16; molar volume 22.4 L.)

**Kadam 1 (molar mass):** C₆H₁₄ = 72 + 14 = **86**, to moles = 8.6 ÷ 86 = **0.1 mol**.
**Kadam 2 (ratio):** 2 : 19 : 12 : 14 (hexane : O₂ : CO₂ : H₂O).
**Kadam 3:** CO₂ = 0.1 × 12 ÷ 2 = **0.6 mol** = 0.6 × 44 = **26.4 g**, volume = 0.6 × 22.4 = **13.44 L**. O₂ = 0.1 × 19 ÷ 2 = **0.95 mol** = 0.95 × 32 = **30.4 g**. Paani = 0.7 mol = 12.6 g.

**Jaanch:** baayein 8.6 + 30.4 = 39.0 g; daayein 26.4 + 12.6 = 39.0 g ✅. (Ye wahi mole-method hai jo Chapter 5 mein tha: mass se mole, ratio, phir mass ya volume.)

### Example 6. Ek hi ethene se do raaste

**Sawaal:** 5.6 g ethene (C₂H₄) naksha ke do raaston par jaata hai. (a) H₂ ke saath ethane tak. (b) Bhaap se ethanol, phir oxidation se ethanoic acid. Har raaste ke product aur zaroori reagent ka hisaab karo. (H 1, C 12, O 16.)

**Kadam 1:** ethene = 28, to 5.6 ÷ 28 = **0.2 mol**. Teeno reaction mein ratio 1 : 1.
**Raasta (a):** C₂H₄ + H₂ → C₂H₆. H₂ = 0.2 mol = **4.48 L** (STP) = 0.4 g. Ethane = 0.2 × 30 = **6.0 g**.
**Raasta (b), pehla kadam:** C₂H₄ + H₂O → C₂H₅OH. Paani = 0.2 × 18 = 3.6 g. Ethanol = 0.2 × 46 = **9.2 g**.
**Raasta (b), doosra kadam:** C₂H₅OH + 2[O] → CH₃COOH + H₂O. [O] = 0.4 mol × 16 = **6.4 g**. Ethanoic acid = 0.2 × 60 = **12.0 g**, paani 3.6 g.

**Jaanch:** (a) 5.6 + 0.4 = 6.0 g ✅. (b) pehla: 5.6 + 3.6 = 9.2 g ✅; doosra: 9.2 + 6.4 = 15.6 g aur 12.0 + 3.6 = 15.6 g ✅. Sabak: ek hi ethene se, **raasta chunne** par bilkul alag family ka product milta hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Carbon ke compound ionic hote hain: carbon bhi sodium ki tarah electron deta ya leta hai."**
Topic 1 mein dekha tha ki carbon C⁴⁺ ya C⁴⁻ nahi banata, wo covalent bond banata hai. To phir sodium ethanoate (Topic 7) aur sabun (Topic 8) ionic kaise? Maan lo teacher ne pencil-LED jaisa 2 cell ka tester ([chemical-bonding-part4.md](../../../class9/chemistry/chemical-bonding/chemical-bonding-part4.md)) lagakar paanch ghol jaanche (sirf dekhne ke liye):

| Ghol | LED | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| ethanol paani mein | nahi jalti | covalent, ion nahi |
| glucose paani mein | nahi jalti | covalent, ion nahi |
| halka ethanoic acid | dheemi jalti hai | thode hi molecule ion banate hain (weak acid) |
| sodium ethanoate paani mein | tez jalti hai | Na⁺ aur CH₃COO⁻ ion |
| sabun (sodium stearate) paani mein | jalti hai | Na⁺ aur C₁₇H₃₅COO⁻ ion |

Dhyan se dekho: **charge carbon par nahi, oxygen par hai**. CH₃COO⁻ ke andar C–C, C–H aur C–O bond sab **covalent** hain; ionic jod sirf Na⁺ aur O⁻ ke beech hai. **Niyam:** carbon apne saathi atoms ke saath hamesha **covalent** bond banata hai. Kisi organic namak mein ionic bond bhi ho sakta hai, par wo carbon ke electron dene-lene se nahi.

**Galti 2: "CO aur CO₂ ek jaisi gas hain: bas ek oxygen ka farak hai."**
Ek atom ka farak bhi bahut bada ho sakta hai. Dono ko 11.2 L (STP) lo: ye 0.5 mol hai. CO ka mass 0.5 × 28 = **14 g**, CO₂ ka 0.5 × 44 = **22 g**.

| Gun | CO | CO₂ |
|---|---|---|
| Molar mass | 28 | 44 |
| Lime water | koi badlaav nahi | doodhiya |
| Jalna | khud jalti hai (neeli lau) | jalti nahi, aag bujhane mein kaam aati hai |
| Gandh aur asar | bina rang, bina gandh, zehreeli | bina rang, bina gandh, zehreeli nahi (par bahut zyada ho to saans ke liye oxygen kam) |
| Kahan banti hai | adhoora jalne se (Topic 1 aur 4) | poora jalne se, saans, fermentation |

**Niyam:** formula ke ek atom ka farak poora swabhaav badal deta hai. Band kamre mein koyla jalne par CO banti hai, jo jaan le sakti hai; CO₂ se wo khatra nahi (par hawadaar kamra phir bhi zaroori).

**Galti 3: "Double bond to do guna mazboot hota hai, isliye ethene ethane se kam reactive hona chahiye."**
Ye sahi lagta hai, par ulta hota hai. Demonstration ke liye Topic 5 ka bromine water yaad karo: ethane ke saath **koi badlaav nahi**, par ethene ka rang **turant** ud jaata hai. Ab dekho ki reaction mein hota kya hai (Figure 9.4).

```
    H       H
    I       I
    C ===== C      +    Br ---- Br
    I       I
    H       H

        v   (addition: ek jodi khuli)


              H       H
              I       I
      Br ---- C ----- C ----- Br
              I       I
              H       H
```

> **Figure 9.4** – Ethene ke saath bromine ka addition. Upar: ethene ke har C par 2 H + double bond (2) = 4; Br–Br ke har Br ka 1 bond. Neeche: product C₂H₄Br₂ mein har C par 2 H + 1 Br + 1 C = 4, har Br ka 1 bond. C–C ab **single bond** hai: double bond ki sirf **ek jodi khuli**, chain toot kar bikhri nahi.

Double bond ke dono jode barabar nahi hain: ek jodi seedhi ethane jaisi mazboot jodi hai, aur doosri **extra** jodi hai jo zyada khuli hui aur kamzor hai. Poora C=C todne ke liye zyada energy lagti hai, par reaction poora bond todti hi nahi: wo bas extra jodi kholti hai, jo aasaan hai. Socho ek parcel do dhaage se bandha hai: poora parcel phaadna ho to dono dhaage kaatne padenge (zyada mehnat), par andar sirf ek naya saaman rakhna ho to ek dhaaga kholna kaafi hai. Isliye alkene aur alkyne **zyada reactive** hain, aur alkane sirf jalne ya substitution mein bhaag lete hain.
**Niyam:** "zyada bond" ka matlab "zyada mazboot **ek poora** bond" nahi, balki "ek **extra, aasaani se khulne wali** jodi".

**Galti 4: "Isomer ka matlab ek jaise gun: formula ek hai, to padarth ek jaisa hoga."**
Same formula, same molar mass, par structure alag, to gun bhi alag. Demonstration 1: **butane** aur **2-methylpropane**, dono C₄H₁₀, molar mass 58. Butane lagbhag −1 °C par ubalta hai, 2-methylpropane lagbhag −12 °C par. Matlab −5 °C ki thandi subah mein butane **liquid** hogi aur 2-methylpropane **gas** (Topic 4 ka pahaadi sardi wala Example 4 yaad karo). Demonstration 2: **ethanol** aur **methoxymethane** dono C₂H₆O (molar mass 46): ethanol 78 °C par ubalta liquid hai aur sodium se H₂ deta hai; methoxymethane kamre ke taapman par **gas** hai (sodium se H₂ nahi, kyunki O–H nahi). Dono ka formula ek, par ek sanitiser banta hai aur doosri gas hai.
**Niyam:** isomer = **ek formula, alag structure, alag gun** (kuch isomer alag family ke bhi). Naam aur structure dono dekho, sirf formula nahi.

## Quick Recap

- **Naksha:** alkyne → alkene → alkane (H₂ jodna, addition); alkene ⇄ alcohol (hydration, dehydration); alcohol → acid (oxidation); acid + alcohol → ester (esterification, gaadha H₂SO₄ catalyst); ester → acid + alcohol (hydrolysis); alkane + Cl₂ (sunlight) → substitution; sab jalne par CO₂ + H₂O.
- **Teer padhne ke teen sawaal:** kaun se do compound, kya badla (juda, badla, paani nikla, oxygen mila), kaun sa reagent aur condition.
- **Gaadha H₂SO₄** ke do kaam: ethanol se paani chheenna (lagbhag 170 °C) aur esterification ka catalyst (halki garmi).
- **Mixed sawaal:** naam se structure (valency 4 aur 1 gino), formula se family (CₙH₂ₙ₊₂, CₙH₂ₙ, CₙH₂ₙ₋₂), balancing (C, phir H, phir O), mole method se mass aur volume, clues se compound, test table se bottle.
- **Galtiyan:** carbon ke compound apne atoms ke saath covalent hain (charge oxygen par); CO aur CO₂ alag; double bond ki extra jodi aasaan khulti hai isliye zyada reactive; isomer ke gun alag.
- **Suraksha:** reaction sirf teacher demo, bina label bottle kabhi nahi, LPG ki badboo par switch nahi, plastic kabhi nahi jalana.
