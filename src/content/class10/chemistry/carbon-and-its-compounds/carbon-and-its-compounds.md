# Topic 1: Carbon Ki Khaasiyat: Chaar Haath Wala Tatva

Chapter 9 ke aakhir mein ek sawaal chhoda tha: cheeni, sabun, plastic aur petrol, ye sab alag-alag cheezein hain, par inke peeche ek hi tatva hai, **carbon**. Chapter 7 mein tumne covalent bond aur electronegativity dekhi thi, aur Class 9 mein ye bhi ki carbon ke atom mein 4 valence electron hote hain. Is chapter ka pehla topic ek simple sawaal se shuru hota hai: **ek hi tatva itna khaas kyun hai?** Aaj hum carbon ke "chaar haath", uske lakhon compounds ki wajah, aur ek hi tatva ke bilkul alag dikhne wale roopon (heera, graphite, koyla) ko samjhenge.

## Angithi, pencil aur heere ki angoothi

December ki thandi shaam thi. Mr. Dhaygude ne aangan ke khule kone mein purani **koyle ki angithi** jalayi thi, haath sekne ke liye. Unki bhanji Gayatri school ka kaam copy mein kar rahi thi, aur beech-beech mein apne thande haath angithi ki taraf badha kar sek leti thi. Mr. Dhaygude ne akhbaar ka ek panna uthaya: poore panne par **heere ki angoothi** ka vigyapan tha, chamakdar, bina rang ka patthar.

Gayatri ne apni pencil box se pencil nikali, nok ko ungli par ghisa to ungli kaali ho gayi. Phir usne angithi ke koyle ko dekha, jo bhi kaala tha, aur akhbaar ke heere ko dekha. "Mama, ye teen cheezein ek jaisi kaise ho sakti hain? Koyla kaala aur bhurbhura hai, pencil ki nok kaali aur chikni hai, aur heera ek dum bina rang ka aur itna kadak ki sheeshe ko kaat de. Vigyapan ke neeche likha hai 'heera shuddh carbon se bana hai'. Ye to galat chhapa hoga!"

Mr. Dhaygude hans pade. "Bahut accha sawaal. Par vigyapan galat nahi hai. Koyla, pencil ki nok aur heera, teeno mein **wahi ek tatva** hai: carbon. Fark kisi aur tatva ka nahi hai, fark **carbon ke atomon ke jude hone ke tareeke** ka hai. Aur isi wajah se carbon ke saath aur bhi kai kamaal jude hain: cheeni, sabun, petrol, plastic, tumhari copy ka kaagaz. Chalo, pehle dekhte hain ki carbon mein aisa kya khaas hai."

Ek aur baat: angithi mein koyla jal raha tha, aur Mr. Dhaygude ne use **aangan mein khule mein** rakha tha, kamre ke andar nahi. Gayatri ne poocha kyun, to wo bole: "Ye baat aage Suraksha wale box mein, pehle wahi padh lo."

## Part A: Carbon ke "chaar haath": tetravalency

Class 9 mein carbon ka electron arrangement dekha tha: **2, 4**. Bahari shell mein 4 electron hain, aur wo shell tab poori hoti jab 8 electron ho: yaani **4 aur chahiye**. Ek tatva ke atom ke paas do hi tareeke hote hain: electron lena ya dena (ionic bond), ya electron **share** karna (covalent bond). Carbon ke liye ionic raasta lagbhag band hai:

- **4 electron dena** (C⁴⁺ banane ke liye): nucleus mein sirf 6 proton hain, jo bahari electron ko bahut kas kar pakadte hain. Ek-ek karke 4 electron nikalne mein itni zyada energy chahiye ki ye practically nahi hota.
- **4 electron lena** (C⁴⁻ banane ke liye): 6 proton ko ab 10 electron sambhalne padenge, aur electron aapas mein ek dusre ko dhakelte hain. Ye bhi bahut mushkil hai.

Isliye carbon ne **share karne wala** raasta chuna: wo apne chaar electron chaar aur atomon ke saath share karke **4 covalent bond** banata hai. Ye hi uski **tetravalency** hai. Hydrogen ke saath: methane CH₄ (Class 9 mein banaya tha). Carbon aur hydrogen ki electronegativity **2.5** aur **2.1** hai (Chapter 6), farq sirf 0.4, to C–H bond lagbhag **non-polar** hai (Chapter 7 ka rule).

```
   Carbon ka parmanu (Z = 6)            Methane CH₄: 4 bond

   nucleus: 6 proton                           H
   shell 1: 2 electron                         I
   shell 2: 4 electron                    H -- C -- H
   (arrangement 2, 4)                          I
   4 aur chahiye -> 4 bond                     H
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Baayen, carbon ka electron arrangement 2, 4: bahari shell mein 4 electron, 4 aur chahiye. Daayen, methane mein carbon ke 4 bond (valency 4: chaaron lakeer gini gayi) aur har hydrogen ka 1 bond (valency 1). Har atom ki shell poori ho gayi.

### Example 1. Bond gin kar formula banana

**Sawaal:** Ek chain mein 3 carbon sidhe jude hain, baaki sab valency hydrogen se poori hoti hai. Hydrogen kitne honge, aur kul bond kitne?

**Kadam 1 (valency ka bajat):** 3 carbon ki kul valency = 3 × 4 = **12**. Beech wale 2 C–C bond mein 2 × 2 = 4 valency lagi (har bond mein dono atomon ki 1-1 valency). Bachi valency = 12 − 4 = **8**, aur har hydrogen ki valency 1 hai, to **8 hydrogen**: C₃H₈.
**Kadam 2 (kul bond):** 2 C–C + 8 C–H = **10 bond**. Doosre tareeke se: (kul valency 12 + 8) ÷ 2 = 20 ÷ 2 = 10.

```
        H   H   H
        I   I   I
    H - C - C - C - H
        I   I   I
        H   H   H
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Teen carbon ki sidhi chain (C₃H₈): har carbon ke chaar bond aur har hydrogen ka ek bond gine ja sakte hain. Beech wale carbon ke upar-neeche 2 H aur do taraf C hai; kinaaron ke carbon ke paas 3 H aur 1 C hai.

**Jaanch:** har carbon: upar-neeche 2 H (ya 3 H kinaare par) + C se judna = 4 ✅; H ki ginti: 3 + 2 + 3 → upar 3, neeche 3, aur dono sire par 1-1 = 8 ✅; bond 10 ✅.

## Part B: Chhota size, mazboot bond: catenation aur lakhon compounds

Kai tatva (jaise oxygen, nitrogen) 2 ya 3 valency wale hain, par unke atom lambi chain nahi banate. Carbon kyun banata hai? Do karan:

1. **Carbon ka atom chhota hai**, to jab do carbon atom aapas mein jude to unka bond bahut paas se bana, aur wo **mazboot** hota hai. Mazboot C–C bond ko todna mushkil, isliye chain **sthir** (stable) rehti hai.
2. **Chaar valency** hain, isliye ek carbon do taraf se chain mein judta hai aur phir bhi usse do aur haath bache hote hain: dusre carbon ke liye, ya hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen, chlorine ke liye.

Isi gun ko **catenation** kehte hain: carbon ke atomon ka aapas mein **chain, shakha aur ring** banana. Silicon (carbon ke neeche wala tatva) kuch chain banata hai, par uske atom bade hain, bond kamzor, isliye uski chain chhoti hi reh jaati hai. Carbon ki chain bahut lambi ho sakti hai: sau se zyada carbon tak.

```
   Seedhi chain                  Shakha (branch) wali           Ring
   (4 carbon, C₄H₁₀)             (4 carbon, C₄H₁₀)              (5 carbon, C₅H₁₀)

    H   H   H   H                   H                             CH₂
    I   I   I   I                   I                            /    \
H - C - C - C - C - H           H - C - H                    CH₂        CH₂
    I   I   I   I                   I                          I          I
    H   H   H   H           H       I       H                CH₂ ------ CH₂
                            I       I       I
                        H - C ----- C ----- C - H
                            I       I       I
                            H       H       H
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Carbon ki teen tarah ki banawat. Baayen, sidhi chain C₄H₁₀ (10 H: upar-neeche 8 aur sire par 2). Beech mein, shakha wali chain, wahi formula C₄H₁₀ (teen CH₃ ek beech wale carbon se jude, 3 + 3 + 3 + 1 = 10 H). Daayen, 5 carbon ki ring (har CH₂ mein 2 H, 2 ring bond: valency 4; kul 10 H). Har carbon ki valency 4 gini gayi.

Dhyan do: Figure 1.3 ki pehli aur doosri chain ka **formula ek hi** (C₄H₁₀) hai par **shakal alag**: iska poora khel Topic 3 mein.

### Example 2. Chain aur ring mein H ka farq

**Sawaal:** 5 carbon ki sidhi chain mein 12 H hote hain (C₅H₁₂). Wahi 5 carbon ring mein jod do to H kitne? Bond kitne kam ya zyada?

**Kadam 1:** Ring banane ke liye dono sire ke carbon ek dusre se jude. Ek naya C–C bond banane ke liye har sire ke carbon ne **ek hydrogen chhoda**. Isliye ring mein 12 − 2 = **10 H**: C₅H₁₀.
**Kadam 2 (bond):** chain: (5 × 4 + 12) ÷ 2 = **16 bond** (4 C–C + 12 C–H). Ring: (5 × 4 + 10) ÷ 2 = **15 bond** (5 C–C + 10 C–H).

**Jaanch:** ring mein 5 C–C + 10 C–H = 15 ✅; chain mein 4 + 12 = 16 ✅. Ring mein ek C–C bond zyada, par 2 C–H kam: kul ek kam bond.

### Kitne compounds? Aur kyun?

Catenation ke saath teen cheezein aur jodo: (a) chain **kisi bhi lambai** ki ho sakti hai, (b) shakha **kahin bhi** lag sakti hai, (c) carbon, carbon ke saath **ek, do ya teen bond** bhi bana sakta hai (C–C, C=C, C≡C: Topic 5), (d) carbon ke baaki haath **hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen, chlorine, bromine** se judte hain, aur C–H, C–O, C–Cl bond bhi mazboot hain. Ye saare vikalp milakar itne alag-alag compound ban sakte hain ki **ek karod se zyada carbon ke compounds** jaane ja chuke hain (lagbhag), jabki baaki saare tatvon ke milakar bahut kam. Ye ek Lego block jaisa hai: ek hi tarah ka block (carbon), par chain, shakha, ring, pul aur rang (alag atom) ke saath jode jaate hain.

### Organic aur inorganic

Pehle log sochte the ki carbon wale compound sirf **jeevit cheezon** (paudhe, jaanvar) hi bana sakte hain, isliye unhe "organic" kaha gaya. 1828 mein Friedrich Wöhler ne lab mein ek aise compound (urea) ko bana diya, jo pehle sirf jeevon mein mila tha. Tab se ye "jeevit shakti" wali baat khatam ho gayi, par **naam bacha reh gaya**.

| Gun | Organic compound | Inorganic compound (namak, acid, dhaatu) |
|---|---|---|
| Mukhya tatva | carbon aur hydrogen (saath O, N, Cl, Br) | alag-alag, aksar dhaatu |
| Bond | aksar covalent | aksar ionic (par covalent bhi) |
| Jalna | aksar jalte hain | aksar nahi jalte |
| Melting point | aksar kam (cheeni, wax) | aksar ooncha (NaCl) |
| Paani mein | aksar nahi ghulte, ya kam | namak aksar ghulte hain |
| Bijli | solid ya liquid mein aksar nahi chalate | ghol ya pighle roop mein aksar chalate hain |

(Table mein "aksar" isliye kyunki apwaad hote hain: cheeni organic hai aur paani mein khoob ghulti hai.) Ek line: kuch carbon wale yaugik, jaise **CO, CO₂ aur carbonate**, organic nahi gine jaate: wo inorganic maane jaate hain.

## Part C: Ek hi tatva, alag roop: allotrope

Carbon ke atomon ke jude hone ka tareeka kai ho sakta hai, aur har tareeke se alag **gun** wala padarth banta hai. Ek hi tatva ke aise alag roopon ko **allotrope** kehte hain.

### Heera (diamond)

Heere mein **har carbon ke atom ke 4 padosi carbon** hote hain (chaaron valency carbon se hi puri). Ye 4 padosi ek poore **teen-aayami (3D) jaal** mein faile hote hain: poora crystal ek bada molecule jaisa hai (Chapter 7 mein "giant covalent" kaha tha). Har bond mazboot aur har taraf se jakda hua.

```
                       C(+3)
                       I
           C(+3) ----- C ----- C(+3)
                       I
                       C(+3)
```

> **Figure 1.4** – Heere ka ek tukda, chapte (2D) roop mein dikhaya gaya: beech ke carbon ke 4 bond (valency 4 gini gayi). Har bahri carbon ke bhi 4 bond hain: ek dikhaya, "(+3)" bataata hai ki baaki 3 bond aage jaal mein jaate hain. Asli heere mein ye 4 bond teen aayam mein alag-alag disha mein fail kar poora jaal banate hain.

Isse heere ke gun nikalte hain:
- **Sabse kadak prakritik padarth**: har atom 4 mazboot bond se jakda hai.
- **Bijli ka kuchalak (non-conductor)**: har carbon ke saare 4 electron bond mein bandhe hain, koi electron chalne ke liye khula nahi.
- **Bahut ooncha melting point**: poora jaal todna padega.
- **Bina rang ka, chamakta** (light ko khoob modta hai: isi liye jewellery mein).
- **Upyog:** jewellery, glass kaatna, rock drilling ke bits, polishing ka powder.

### Graphite

Graphite mein **har carbon ke sirf 3 padosi** carbon hote hain, aur wo chhote-chhote **chhe-kone (hexagon)** banate hain. Ye hexagon milkar ek **chapti parat (layer)** banate hain, aur aisi kai parat ek ke upar ek rakhi hoti hain.

```
                 \             /
                  C ========= C
                 /             \
       ------- C                 C -------
                 \\           //
                  C --------- C
                 /             \
```

> **Figure 1.5** – Graphite ki ek parat ka ek hexagon: chhe carbon, har carbon ke 3 padosi (2 hexagon mein, 1 bahar parat mein). Valency 4 ginne ke liye har carbon ka ek bond dohra (=) dikhaya gaya: dohra + ek ikhra + bahar ka ikhra = 4. Asli graphite mein ye "dohre" bond ke electron kisi ek jagah nahi bandhe, poori parat mein ghoom sakte hain.

Graphite ki **chauthi valency** wala electron kisi ek bond mein nahi bandha, poori parat mein ghoom sakta hai. Isliye graphite **bijli ka achha conductor** hai (heera nahi). Aur parat ke andar bond mazboot, par **parton ke beech** sirf **kamzor** aakarshan hai:

```
   parat 1     o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o     <-- parat ke andar mazboot bond
               . . . . . . . . . . . .     <-- parton ke beech kamzor jod
   parat 2     o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o
               . . . . . . . . . . . .
   parat 3     o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o-o

   dabane ya ragadne par ek parat dusri par phisal jaati hai  ----->
```

> **Figure 1.6** – Graphite ko side se dekhne par: parat ke andar atom mazboot bond (o-o) se jude hain, par parat aur parat ke beech kamzor jod (.) hai. Isliye parat aasani se phisal jaati hai.

Isse graphite ke gun: **narm, chikna, phisalne wala; kaala, halka chamak; bijli ka conductor; ooncha melting point.**
- **Upyog:** pencil ki nok (asli mein graphite aur chikni mitti ka mishran: "lead" naam galat hai, isme lead (Pb) hota hi nahi), **electrode** (Chapter 4 mein carbon electrode dekhe the), **lubricant** (mashin ke parts ko phisalne wala), dry cell ka carbon rod.
- Pencil mein "H" ka matlab zyada mitti (kadak, halki lakeer), "B" ka matlab zyada graphite (narm, gehri lakeer): ye bhi parat ke phisalne ka kamaal hai.

### Example 3. Heere aur graphite mein kaun zyada ghana

**Sawaal:** 0.35 g carbon heere ke roop mein hai, aur utna hi 0.35 g graphite ke roop mein. Heera ka density lagbhag 3.5 g per cm³ aur graphite ka lagbhag 2.2 g per cm³. Dono ka volume kitna, aur kaun zyada jagah leta hai?

**Kadam 1 (volume = mass ÷ density):** Heera: 0.35 ÷ 3.5 = **0.10 cm³**. Graphite: 0.35 ÷ 2.2 = **0.159 cm³**, yaani lagbhag **0.16 cm³**.
**Kadam 2:** graphite lagbhag 3.5 ÷ 2.2 = 1.59 guna jagah leta hai.

**Jaanch:** 3.5 × 0.10 = 0.35 ✅; 2.2 × 0.159 = 0.35 ✅. Matlab heere mein atom zyada kasoke baithe hain, aur graphite mein parton ke beech khaali jagah bachti hai.

### Fullerene (C₆₀) aur nayi khoj

1985 mein carbon ka ek aur roop mila: **C₆₀ fullerene**. Ek molecule mein **60 carbon atom**, jo **football** jaisi shakal banate hain: **12 panchkon (pentagon) aur 20 hexagon**. Har carbon ke 3 padosi, aur ye ek **alag molecule** hai (heere ke jaal jaisa bada nahi). Ek mazedaar jaanch: 12 × 5 = 60 aur 20 × 6 = 120; har carbon 3 shaklon (1 pentagon + 2 hexagon) ka kinaara hai, to carbon = (60 + 120) ÷ 3 = **60** ✅.

Ek line mein aur: **graphene** graphite ki **sirf ek parat** hai (ek atom moti), aur **nanotube** us parat ko nali ki tarah lapetne se bani hai: ye abhi vigyan ki nayi khoj hain, bahut hi mazboot aur bijli conduct karne wale.

### Example 4. Ek molecule C₆₀ ka hisaab

**Sawaal:** C₆₀ ka molar mass kya hai? 7.2 g C₆₀ mein kitne mol C₆₀ aur kitne mol carbon atom? Poora jalaya (C₆₀ + 60O₂ → 60CO₂) to kitna CO₂? (C 12, O 16.)

**Kadam 1:** molar mass = 60 × 12 = **720** g/mol.
**Kadam 2:** 7.2 ÷ 720 = **0.01 mol** C₆₀. Carbon atom = 0.01 × 60 = **0.6 mol**.
**Kadam 3:** reaction ke ratio se 1 mol C₆₀ se 60 mol CO₂: 0.01 × 60 = **0.6 mol CO₂** = 0.6 × 44 = **26.4 g** (sirf padhne ke liye).

**Jaanch:** atoms: C 60 = 60 ✅, O 120 = 60 × 2 ✅. Mass: 7.2 g carbon + O₂ (0.6 × 32 = 19.2 g) = 26.4 g ✅.

## Part D: Bina regular banawat wale roop: amorphous carbon

Carbon ke kuch roop ki koi lambi, regular banawat nahi hoti: unhe **amorphous** kehte hain. Unme carbon ke chhote-chhote tukde (graphite jaise bahut chhote tukde) beqaayda jude hote hain.

| Roop | Kaise banta hai | Upyog |
|---|---|---|
| **Koyla (coal)** | Zameen ke neeche dabe paudhe lakhon saalon mein (lagbhag) | eendhan, bijli ghar |
| **Coke** | Koyle ko hawa ke bina garam karne se: kadak, shuddh carbon | blast furnace (Chapter 3), eendhan |
| **Charcoal** | Lakdi ko kam hawa mein garam karne se | eendhan, paani aur gas ke rang-gandh ko sokhne wala (activated charcoal) |
| **Lamp-black (soot)** | Diya ya tel ke adhoore jalne ka kaala dhuan | ink, paint, shoe polish, tyre |

Koyla poora shuddh carbon nahi hota (isme aur cheezein bhi hoti hain), coke aur charcoal zyada shuddh hote hain. Jab inhe oxygen ki kaafi hawa mein jalate hain, to saari carbon **CO₂** ban jaati hai:

C + O₂ → CO₂

Aur oxygen kam ho to **CO** (zehreeli gas): isiliye Suraksha ka box.

## Part E: Saboot: ek hi tatva

Ab Gayatri ke sawaal ka jawab: kaise pata chale ki heera aur graphite dono carbon hi hain? Saboot **jalane se** milta hai. 1770 ke dashak mein **Lavoisier** ne badi lens se dhoop ko heere par kendrit karke use band bartan ki hawa mein jalaya (heera gaayab ho gaya), aur baad mein vaigyanikon ne ye bhi dekha ki **shuddh heera, shuddh graphite aur shuddh charcoal** teeno **sirf CO₂ gas** dete hain, aur jab **mass barabar** ho to **CO₂ ki mass bhi barabar** hoti hai. Agar teeno alag tatva hote, to alag-alag product milte.

```
   Teacher demo (sirf padhne ke liye): nal se oxygen ke saath
                                          garam karne par CO₂

   oxygen    kaanch ki nali (dono sire khule)             chuna ka paani
   cylinder   :   namoona              garam nali              (lime water)
    [  ]----->   =====[ carbon ]=======>=====\
    [  ]                 ^                     \
                  teacher ki garam lau          \__ delivery tube ka sira
                                                    chuna paani mein dooba
                                              +-------------------+
                                              :  ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ :
                                              :   (doodhiya hota)  :
                                              +-------------------+
```

> **Figure 1.7** – Carbon ke jalne ka teacher demo (sirf padhne ke liye): oxygen khuli kaanch ki nali mein jaati hai, jahan teacher ek garam lau se namoone ko garam karta hai. Nikli gas delivery tube se chuna paani (lime water) mein jaati hai, jo doodhiya ho jaata hai: CO₂ ki pehchaan. System band nahi hai: ek sira oxygen ke liye khula, doosra paani ke andar. Heating rokne se pehle delivery tube ko chuna paani se bahar nikaal lete hain taaki paani peeche na chadh aaye (suck-back).

### Example 5. Heere aur graphite, barabar mass, barabar CO₂

**Sawaal:** 0.12 g shuddh heera (lagbhag 0.6 carat; 1 carat = 0.2 g) aur 0.12 g shuddh graphite alag-alag jalaye gaye (C + O₂ → CO₂). Har ek se kitna CO₂ (mass aur STP par volume) aur kitni oxygen chahiye? (C 12, O 16; molar volume 22.4 L.)

**Kadam 1:** carbon = 0.12 ÷ 12 = **0.01 mol** (dono mein, kyunki dono sirf carbon).
**Kadam 2:** ratio 1 : 1 : 1. CO₂ = 0.01 mol × 44 = **0.44 g**; volume = 0.01 × 22.4 = **0.224 L** (224 mL). Oxygen = 0.01 × 32 = **0.32 g**.

**Jaanch:** atoms: C 1 = 1, O 2 = 2 ✅. Mass: 0.12 + 0.32 = 0.44 g ✅. **Dono ke liye ek hi jawab**: yahi saboot hai ki dono ek hi tatva hain.

Aur ek aur baat: bahut zyada pressure aur garmi par graphite ko heere mein badla bhi ja sakta hai (kuch lab mein banaye heere aise bante hain). **Tatva ek hi, atomon ka jaal badal gaya.**

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti: "Heera aur graphite alag tatva hain, kyunki ek bina rang ka kadak aur doosra kaala narm."**
Ye galat sach jaisa lagta hai kyunki gun itne alag hain. Par tatva uske **atoms ki kism** se tay hota hai, shakal se nahi. Teen jaanch (sirf padhne ke liye):

1. **Jalane par:** 0.12 g heera aur 0.12 g graphite, dono jale to **sirf CO₂**, aur dono se **0.44 g** (Example 5). Alag tatva hote to alag product aur alag mass aati.
2. **Mass se atom ginti:** 12 g heera aur 12 g graphite dono mein **1 mol carbon atom** (12 ÷ 12 = 1). Atom ek hi kism ke, bas jude alag tareeke se.
3. **Ek se doosra:** pressure aur garmi se graphite ko heere mein badla ja sakta hai. Agar alag tatva hote, to ek tatva dusre mein nahi badal sakta tha.

| Sawaal | Heera | Graphite |
|---|---|---|
| Kis tatva ka bana | carbon | carbon |
| Jalane par product | CO₂ | CO₂ |
| Har C ke padosi | 4 | 3 |
| Bijli | nahi | haan |

**Niyam:** **allotrope = ek hi tatva, atomon ke jude hone ka alag tareeka.** Gun alag hone se tatva alag nahi hota.

## Quick Recap

- Carbon ka arrangement **2, 4**: 4 valence electron, **tetravalent**; electron dena ya lena bahut mushkil, isliye wo **covalent** bond banata hai. C–H lagbhag non-polar (2.5 aur 2.1).
- Chhota size, mazboot C–C bond aur chaar valency se **catenation**: **chain, shakha aur ring**. Saath mein multiple bond aur H, O, N, Cl, Br ke jod: isliye lakhon (ek karod se zyada) compound.
- **Organic** compound = carbon wale (aksar covalent, jalne wale, kam melting point); CO, CO₂, carbonate organic nahi gine jaate.
- **Allotrope**: **heera** (har C ke 4 padosi, sabse kadak, non-conductor, jewellery aur cutting), **graphite** (har C ke 3 padosi, parat wala, conductor, pencil, electrode, lubricant), **C₆₀** (60 carbon, football; 12 pentagon + 20 hexagon). Graphene aur nanotube: ek line mein.
- **Amorphous**: koyla, coke, charcoal, lamp-black.
- Jalne par C + O₂ → CO₂; heera aur graphite barabar mass par barabar CO₂ dete hain: **saboot ki dono ek hi tatva**. Kam oxygen mein CO: zehreeli, angithi khule mein.
- Pencil tester (2 AA, 220 ohm, adult ke saath): graphite bijli chalata hai.
- **Galti:** heera aur graphite alag tatva nahi, ek hi tatva ke allotrope hain.

## Practice Questions

(Sab sawaal paper par hal karne ke liye hain. Mass: C 12, O 16. Molar volume 22.4 L at STP. Heera 3.5 g per cm³, graphite 2.2 g per cm³ lagbhag.)

1. A carbon atom has electron arrangement 2, 4. Explain in 3 to 4 lines why carbon does not form C⁴⁺ or C⁴⁻ ions easily and so forms covalent bonds.
2. In graphite, each carbon atom is joined to 3 other carbon atoms. How many valencies of each atom are left over after counting one bond to each neighbour? What does this leftover valency do for the properties of graphite?
3. 0.6 g of lamp-black (assume it is pure carbon) is burnt completely: C + O₂ → CO₂. Find the mass of CO₂ and its volume at STP.
4. 0.3 g of diamond and 0.3 g of graphite are burnt separately in excess oxygen. Find the mass of CO₂ from each. What does this show about the two substances?
5. A sample of C₆₀ has a mass of 14.4 g. Find the number of moles of C₆₀, the number of moles of carbon atoms, and the mass of CO₂ obtained on burning it completely (C₆₀ + 60O₂ → 60CO₂).
6. A 0.7 g piece of diamond and a 0.7 g piece of graphite are compared. Find the volume of each and say which one has its atoms packed closer together, and by what factor (ratio).
7. A sample of coke is 90 percent carbon by mass (the rest does not burn to CO₂). Find the mass of CO₂ formed when 5.0 g of this coke is burnt completely.
8. In C₆₀, each carbon is joined to 3 other carbons and has no hydrogen. (a) How many carbon-carbon links are there in total? (b) Every carbon needs one more unit of valency: how many double bonds are there? (c) How many single bonds?
9. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) Diamond is a good conductor of electricity. (b) The "lead" of a pencil is graphite and clay, not the metal lead. (c) Charcoal, coke and lamp-black are different elements. (d) C₆₀ has 60 carbon atoms arranged like a football. (e) Graphite and diamond give different products when burnt.
10. Why is graphite used as a lubricant, while diamond is used on tools that cut glass? Use the arrangement of atoms in each.
11. A student connects a 2 AA cell LED tester (with the 220 ohm resistor, adult nearby) with two wires touching a pencil: the LED glows when the wires touch the two sharpened ends but not when both wires touch the wooden part. Explain. Would a diamond piece in the circuit light the LED?
12. A black solid X, when burnt in oxygen, gives only a gas that turns lime water milky. 0.24 g of X gives 0.88 g of this gas. Show whether X could be pure carbon, and say whether this test can tell if X is graphite, charcoal or lamp-black.
13. Why is a closed room unsafe for a burning charcoal angithi? Name the gas, and give two reasons why it is dangerous.
14. Why is lamp-black called amorphous but diamond crystalline? Give one use of lamp-black.

### Answers

1. C⁴⁺ banane ke liye 4 electron nikalne padenge: nucleus mein sirf 6 proton hain jo bahari electron ko kas ke pakadte hain, to bahut zyada energy lagti hai. C⁴⁻ banane ke liye 4 electron lene padenge: 6 proton ko 10 electron sambhalne padenge aur electron aapas mein dhakelte hain, ye bhi bahut mushkil. Isliye carbon apne 4 electron **share** karta hai aur **covalent** bond banata hai.
2. Har carbon ki valency 4 hai, aur 3 padosi ke saath ek-ek bond ginne se 3 hi ho pati hai: **1 valency bachti hai**. Ye chauthi valency wala electron ek jagah nahi bandha, parat mein ghoom sakta hai, isliye graphite **bijli ka conductor** hai.
3. C = 0.6 ÷ 12 = **0.05 mol**. Ratio 1 : 1: CO₂ = 0.05 mol × 44 = **2.2 g**; volume = 0.05 × 22.4 = **1.12 L**. Jaanch: C 1 = 1, O 2 = 2 ✅; oxygen = 0.05 × 32 = 1.6 g, 0.6 + 1.6 = 2.2 g ✅.
4. Dono mein carbon = 0.3 ÷ 12 = **0.025 mol**. CO₂ = 0.025 × 44 = **1.1 g** (dono se). **Dono ke liye barabar**: matlab dono sirf carbon hain, yani ek hi tatva (allotrope). Jaanch: 0.3 + (0.025 × 32 = 0.8) = 1.1 g ✅.
5. C₆₀ ka molar mass = 60 × 12 = **720** g/mol. Moles = 14.4 ÷ 720 = **0.02 mol**. Carbon atom = 0.02 × 60 = **1.2 mol**. CO₂ = 1.2 mol × 44 = **52.8 g**. Jaanch: C 60 = 60, O 120 = 120 ✅; mass: 14.4 + oxygen (1.2 × 32 = 38.4) = 52.8 g ✅.
6. Heera: 0.7 ÷ 3.5 = **0.2 cm³**. Graphite: 0.7 ÷ 2.2 = **0.318 ≈ 0.32 cm³**. **Heere** mein atom zyada paas-paas hain: ratio graphite ÷ heera = 3.5 ÷ 2.2 = **lagbhag 1.6 guna**. Jaanch: 3.5 × 0.2 = 0.7 ✅.
7. Carbon = 5.0 × 0.9 = **4.5 g**. Moles = 4.5 ÷ 12 = **0.375 mol**. CO₂ = 0.375 × 44 = **16.5 g**. Jaanch: 0.375 × 44 = 16.5 ✅ (baaki 0.5 g carbon-bina hissa CO₂ nahi banata).
8. (a) Har carbon ke 3 padosi: 60 × 3 ÷ 2 = **90 links** (har link do atomon ne gina). (b) Har carbon ki ek valency baaki: ek double bond mein 2 carbon ki ek-ek valency lagti hai, to double bond = 60 ÷ 2 = **30**. (c) Single bond = 90 − 30 = **60**. Jaanch: har carbon: 1 double (2) + 2 single (2) = 4 ✅.
9. (a) **False** ❌: heera bijli nahi chalata, har electron bond mein bandha hai. (b) **True** ✅: graphite aur chikni mitti; lead (Pb) hai hi nahi. (c) **False** ❌: teeno carbon ke hi roop hain, ek hi tatva. (d) **True** ✅. (e) **False** ❌: dono sirf CO₂ dete hain.
10. Graphite mein parat ke beech kamzor jod hai, isliye parat phisalti hai: lubricant. Heere mein har C ke 4 mazboot bond poore jaal mein hain, isliye sabse kadak: glass kaat deta hai.
11. Pencil ki nok graphite hai jo bijli chalata hai (chauthi valency ka electron ghoomta hai), isliye circuit poora hua. Lakdi ek kuchalak hai: circuit khula rahta hai. Heera ke saare 4 electron bond mein bandhe hain, to **LED nahi jalti**.
12. CO₂ = 0.88 ÷ 44 = **0.02 mol**. Isme carbon = 0.02 × 12 = **0.24 g**, jo X ke mass 0.24 g ke barabar: X **shuddh carbon ho sakta hai** (poora X carbon). Par ye test sirf bata sakta hai ki X **carbon** hai; X graphite, charcoal ya lamp-black mein se kaun hai, ye nahi, kyunki teeno barabar mass par barabar CO₂ dete hain.
13. Band kamre mein oxygen kam padti hai, to carbon se **carbon monoxide (CO)** banti hai. Khatra: (1) bina rang ki aur bina gandh ki hai, pata nahi chalta; (2) khoon mein oxygen ki jagah le leti hai aur jaan le sakti hai.
14. Lamp-black mein atomon ka koi regular lambi duri tak pattern nahi (amorphous); heere mein atomon ka regular doharaya jaal (crystalline). Lamp-black ka upyog: ink, paint, shoe polish ya tyre (koi ek).
