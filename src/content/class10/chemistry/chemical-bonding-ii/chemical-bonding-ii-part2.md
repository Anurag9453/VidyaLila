# Topic 2: Polar aur Non-polar Molecule: Shape Ka Khel

Topic 1 mein humne **ek bond** ko dekha: do atom, ek shared pair, aur electronegativity ka farq batata hai ki pair kis taraf thoda zyada khisakta hai (δ⁺ aur δ⁻). Par asli cheezein sirf ek bond ki nahi hoti: paani mein do bond hain, CO₂ mein do, methane mein chaar. Ab sawaal bada ho gaya hai: **poore molecule** ka kya? Kya har polar bond wale molecule ke ek siray par δ⁻ aur doosre par δ⁺ hota hai? Is topic ka jawab chhupa hai ek cheez mein jo hum ab tak bhool rahe the: molecule ka **shape**.

## Kanghi aur paani ki dhaar

Mr. Pangarkar apne bhanje Varad ke saath subah wash-basin ke paas khade the. Varad ne apne sukhe baalon mein plastic ki kanghi (comb) kuch baar tezi se ghumayi, phir bade ki nigrani mein nal ko bahut halka sa khola, jisse paani ki ek **patli dhaar** girne lagi. Kanghi ko dhaar ke paas, bina chhue, le jaate hi dhaar **kanghi ki taraf jhuk gayi**, jaise koi use kheench raha ho.

"Mama, ye kya jaadu hai? Paani to bijli ka saaman nahi hai, na wo kanghi se chipka hai, phir bhi mud gaya!" Mr. Pangarkar hanse. "Jaadu nahi, chemistry hai. Dekh, ye dhaar mudi kyunki paani ke molecule ke **do siray** hote hain, ek thoda positive aur ek thoda negative. Kanghi par rubbing se charge aa gaya hai, wo kisi na kisi siray ko kheench leti hai." Varad ne turant poochha, "Par CO₂ mein bhi to C=O bond hain, aur oxygen carbon se zyada kheenchta hai, to wahan bhi oxygen thoda negative hoga. To kya CO₂ ki dhaar bhi mud jaati?"

Mr. Pangarkar ne sar hilaya. "Achha sawaal hai. Dekh, bond kai hote hain, par molecule ek hota hai. Jaise tug-of-war: har khilaadi ki apni taakat hoti hai, par jeet-haar poori team ke **mile-jule zor** se tay hoti hai. Aur mile-jule zor ka sawaal sirf zor ka nahi, **kis taraf** ka bhi hota hai. Aaj hum molecules ki tug-of-war dekhenge."

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Polar molecule** | Wo molecule jiske ek siray par δ⁻ aur doosre par δ⁺ hota hai, kyunki bonds ki kheench milkar ek taraf bachti hai | H₂O, NH₃, HCl |
| **Non-polar molecule** | Wo molecule jisme bonds ki kheench milkar zero ho jaati hai (ya koi bond polar hi nahi), isliye koi δ⁻ siray aur δ⁺ siray nahi bante | CO₂, CH₄ |
| **Kheench ka teer (pull arrow)** | Bond mein shared pair ki kheench dikhane wala teer: δ⁺ wale atom se δ⁻ wale atom ki taraf | H → O |
| **Kul kheench (net pull)** | Molecule ke saare bonds ki kheench ko jodne ke baad bachi hui kheench, jisme taakat aur disha dono ginti mein aati hain | H₂O mein O ki taraf bachti hai |
| **Symmetrical shape** | Aisa shape jisme saare ek jaise atom central atom ke chaaron taraf barabar faile hon, ki saare teer ek-doosre ko kaat dein | CO₂, CH₄ |
| **Solvent** | Wo padarth (aksar liquid) jisme doosra padarth ghulta hai | Paani, petrol |
| **Solute** | Wo padarth jo solvent mein ghulta hai | Namak paani mein |
| **Like dissolves like** | "Jaisa jaise ko ghulata hai": polar aur ionic padarth polar solvent mein, non-polar padarth non-polar solvent mein ghulte hain | Mom petrol mein ghulta hai |

## Part A: Bond polar, molecule non-polar: ho kaise sakta hai?

Topic 1 mein ek bond ka swabhav tay karne ke liye humne electronegativity ka farq dekha. Ab har polar bond par ek **teer** lagayenge: teer ki **lambai** (kheench kitni badi) farq se, aur **disha** δ⁺ wale atom se δ⁻ wale atom ki taraf (yaani us taraf jahan shared pair khisakta hai).

**Do bond wale molecule mein do teer hote hain.** Dono ko ek hi jagah jodne ka matlab: dono kheench milkar kitna aur kis taraf zor lagati hain. Ye bilkul tug-of-war ya do bachchon dwara ek gaadi kheenchne jaisa hai:

```
   1) Seedhi line mein, ulti taraf, barabar zor (jaise CO₂)

         <=== A    [ gaadi ]    B ===>           gaadi nahi hilti: kul zor = 0


   2) Ek hi taraf jhuke hue, barabar zor (jaise H₂O)

          A                 B
           \               /
            \             /
             \           /
              [  gaadi  ]                         gaadi A aur B ke beech, upar ki taraf khisakti hai
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Do barabar kheench ka jod: ulti seedhi taraf ho to kat jaati hai (gaadi ruki rehti hai); ek taraf jhuki ho to milkar ek taraf kul zor deti hai. Molecule ke bonds bhi isi tarah milte hain.

Is se ek niyam nikalta hai:

> **Kul kheench = saare bond-teeron ka jod (taakat aur disha dono).**
> Kul kheench **zero** to molecule **non-polar**. Kul kheench **bachi** to molecule **polar**: wo taraf δ⁻ siray, doosri taraf δ⁺ siray.

Do bonds ke teer kat sakte hain **sirf tab** jab shape aisa ho ki teer ulti disha mein hon. Isliye molecule ka shape bahut mayne rakhta hai: polar bonds ka hona kaafi nahi, **unki disha** bhi dekhni padti hai.

**Do atom wale molecule ki baat sabse aasan:** sirf ek bond, to sirf ek teer, jise kaatne wala koi doosra teer hai hi nahi. Isliye do-atom molecule mein **bond polar hai to molecule polar**, bond non-polar hai to molecule non-polar. Jaise HCl: farq 3.0 − 2.1 = 0.9, Cl taraf δ⁻, H taraf δ⁺: **polar molecule**. Jo kuch interesting hai wo **teen ya zyada atom** wale molecules mein shuru hota hai.

## Part B: CO₂ aur H₂O: seedha aur tedha

Dono molecules mein ek central atom aur do O ya do H hain, bond bhi polar hain, par kahani alag hai.

### CO₂: seedha (linear)

Class 9 se yaad karo: C ke 4 bond-electron aur do O ke saath do double bonds, **O=C=O**. Teeno atom ek **seedhi line** mein hain (shape ka naam "linear": angle aage ki class mein; abhi tasveer maan lo). C ke upar koi lone pair nahi hai, har O par 2 lone pairs.

C aur O ka farq: 3.5 − 2.5 = **1.0**, to har C=O bond **polar** hai, O taraf δ⁻, C taraf δ⁺. Do teer, dono O ki taraf:

```
        ..                ..
        O  ====  C  ====  O                 (har O par 2 lone pairs)
        ..                ..

      δ⁻          δ⁺          δ⁻

      <=== (C se O ki taraf)       (C se O ki taraf) ===>
              bahar ki taraf, ulti disha, barabar lambai

      Kul kheench = 0   =>   CO₂ NON-POLAR
```

> **Figure 2.2** – CO₂: do polar C=O bond, par seedhi line mein ulti disha ke barabar teer, isliye kat jaate hain. Molecule ke beech ka C δ⁺ hai, par molecule ke siray par koi "ek taraf" δ⁻ ya δ⁺ nahi: dono siray ek jaise (O, δ⁻).

### Example 1. CO₂ ko step mein check karo

1. Dot diagram: 4 + 6 + 6 = 16 valence electrons. O=C=O mein 4 shared pairs (8 e) aur har O par 2 lone pairs (4 + 4 = 8 e), kul 8 + 8 = **16** ✅.
2. Har bond ka farq: 3.5 − 2.5 = 1.0, polar, teer C se O ki taraf.
3. Shape: seedhi line, teer ulti disha mein, barabar lambai: kul kheench **0**.
4. Nateeja: bond **polar**, molecule **non-polar**.

**Jaanch:** 16 = 16 ✅; do teer, barabar, ulti: 1 − 1 = 0 ✅.

### H₂O: tedha (bent)

O: 2 shared pairs (do O–H bond) aur **2 lone pairs**. H aur O ka farq: 3.5 − 2.1 = **1.4**, har O–H bond polar, O taraf δ⁻. Ab shape: lone pairs ki wajah se paani ka molecule seedha nahi, **tedha** (bent, V jaisa) hota hai. (Kyun tedha: angle aage ki class mein. Abhi tasveer maan lo.) Dono teer H se O ki taraf, aur dono ek hi taraf jhuke hue:

```
                  O        ..  ..                 (O par 2 lone pairs, upar)
                /   \
              /       \
            H           H

         δ⁺               δ⁺     <- H taraf (neeche, dono)
                 δ⁻              <- O taraf (upar)

      Kul kheench: ek hi taraf, O ki taraf (upar)   =>   H₂O POLAR
```

> **Figure 2.3** – H₂O: do polar O–H bond, tedha shape, dono teer H se O ki taraf, to kat nahi sakte, mil jaate hain. O wala siray δ⁻ aur H wale dono hisse milkar δ⁺ siray. (O ke lone pairs ko yahan sirf ".." se ishara hai; unki asli jagah aage ki class mein.)

### Example 2. H₂O ko step mein check karo

1. Dot diagram: 6 + 1 + 1 = 8 valence electrons. 2 shared pairs (4 e) + 2 lone pairs (4 e) = **8** ✅.
2. Har bond ka farq: 3.5 − 2.1 = 1.4, polar, teer H se O ki taraf.
3. Shape tedha, to dono teer ulti disha mein nahi: do bachche ek gaadi ko V aakaar mein kheench rahe hain, gaadi beech ki taraf jaati hai: kul kheench **bachi**.
4. Nateeja: **polar molecule**, O wala siray δ⁻, H wala δ⁺.

**Jaanch:** 8 = 8 ✅; do teer ek taraf jhuke hue, ye kat nahi sakte (Figure 2.1, teer 2) ✅.

**Ek bada sabak:** CO₂ aur H₂O dono mein ek central atom aur do ek jaise atom hain, dono mein bond polar hain. Fark sirf **shape** ka hai: seedha ya tedha. Isliye "Shape Ka Khel".

## Part C: NH₃ aur CH₄: pyramid aur chaar-kone wala

### NH₃: pyramid

(Ammonia gas teekhi aur zehreeli hai: sirf padhne ke liye.) N ke 3 shared pairs aur **1 lone pair**. Farq 3.0 − 2.1 = **0.9**, har N–H bond polar, teer H se N ki taraf. Lone pair ki wajah se NH₃ ek **pyramid** ki tarah hota hai: N upar, teeno H neeche ek tripod ki tarah. Teeno teer upar N ki taraf jhuke hue: kat nahi sakte, **mil jaate hain**.

```
              ..                      NH₃: N upar, uske upar 1 lone pair
              N
            / | \                     teeno teer: H se N ki taraf (upar)
           /  |  \
          H   H   H                   Kul kheench: upar, N ki taraf

                                      NH₃ POLAR (N siray δ⁻, H wala hissa δ⁺)
```

> **Figure 2.4** – NH₃ ka pyramid: N upar, teeno H neeche. Teeno teer upar N ki taraf jhuke, mil kar ek bada teer banate hain. (Asli molecule 3D hai; kaagaz par sirf tasveer.)

### CH₄: chaar-kone wala (tetrahedral-by-picture)

C ke 4 shared pairs aur **koi lone pair nahi**. C–H ka farq 2.5 − 2.1 = **0.4**, bahut chhota: bond lagbhag non-polar (Topic 1 ki thumb rule: 0.4 ya usse kam, barabar baantna). Phir bhi ek chhota teer H se C ki taraf maan lein. Chaaron H, C ke chaaron taraf **barabar** faile hote hain: chaaron teer apne-aap kat jaate hain.

```
             H
             :                         Kaagaz par "plus" jaisi tasveer:
        H -- C -- H                    chaaron teer (H se C ki taraf) barabar lambai,
             :                         jodo to 0.
             H

     Asli molecule 3D ek pyramid jaisa chaar-kone ka dhancha (tetrahedron-jaisa):
     chaaron H, C se barabar doori par, ek-doosre se jitna door ho sake. Teer phir bhi kat jaate hain.

     Kul kheench = 0   =>   CH₄ NON-POLAR
```

> **Figure 2.5** – CH₄: chaar barabar bond, chaaron taraf barabar. Kuch bhi polar ho ya na ho, symmetric shape ki wajah se kul kheench 0 hai.

### Example 3. NH₃ aur CH₄ ko step mein check karo

NH₃: dot diagram: 5 + 3 = 8 valence electrons; 3 shared pairs (6 e) + 1 lone pair (2 e) = **8** ✅. CH₄: 4 + 4 = 8 valence electrons; 4 shared pairs = **8** ✅.

| Molecule | Bond ka farq | Central atom par lone pairs | Shape ki tasveer | Teer kat jaate hain? | Molecule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NH₃ | 3.0 − 2.1 = 0.9 | 1 | pyramid | Nahi | Polar |
| CH₄ | 2.5 − 2.1 = 0.4 | 0 | chaar-kone wala, barabar | Haan | Non-polar |

**Jaanch:** 3.0 − 2.1 = 0.9 ✅; 2.5 − 2.1 = 0.4 ✅.

### Symmetry ki chhoti jaanch

Is chapter ke molecules ke liye teen sawaal poochho:

1. Dot diagram banao. **Central atom par lone pair** hai? (H₂O mein 2, NH₃ mein 1, CO₂ aur CH₄ mein 0.)
2. Bahar ke atom **saare ek jaise** hain?
3. Agar central atom par koi lone pair nahi **aur** bahar ke saare atom ek jaise hain, to shape symmetrical hota hai aur molecule **non-polar**. Lone pair ho ya bahar ke atom alag ho to teer aam taur par nahi katte: **polar**.

Ye is chapter ke molecules ke liye hai (jaise ek jaise-atom wale CO₂, CH₄); aage ki class mein aur shapes aur apwaad dekhenge.

### Example 4. Do aur molecules par jaanch

**Sawaal:** HCl aur CO₂ mein se kaun polar molecule hai? Dono mein bond polar hai.

- HCl mein sirf ek bond, ek teer, kaatne wala koi nahi: **polar**.
- CO₂ mein do teer, par ek jaise bahar ke atom aur central par koi lone pair nahi: teer katte hain: **non-polar**.

**Jaanch:** HCl: teer H se Cl, kul kheench = 0.9 ke baraabar bachi ✅; CO₂ kul 0 ✅.

## Part D: Dhaar aur kanghi: asli gawah

Ye sab shape aur teer kaagaz ke khel lag sakte hain. Gawah kahan hai? Kanghi aur nal ki dhaar mein.

Paani **polar** hai: ek taraf O (δ⁻) aur doosri taraf H (δ⁺). Jab kanghi ko rub karke charge dete hain (jaise sukhe baalon ya oon par rub karne se), to wo **ya to negative ya positive** ban jaati hai. Charge kisi bhi tarah ka ho, paani ke molecule ek **sahi siray** se kanghi ki taraf ghoom jaate hain:

```
   Kanghi negative (−) hai:                 Kanghi positive (+) hai:

     kanghi (−)      dhaar                    kanghi (+)      dhaar
        −              δ⁺  δ⁻                     +             δ⁻  δ⁺
        −              H---O  <-- molecule        +             O---H
        −       <---                               +      <---
                H wala siray (δ⁺) kanghi ki                 O wala siray (δ⁻) kanghi ki
                taraf ghoom gaya                            taraf ghoom gaya

   Dono mein: kanghi ke paas wala siray, kanghi se kheencha jaata hai.
   Dhaar kanghi ki taraf mudti hai, chahe kanghi (−) ho ya (+).
```

> **Figure 2.6** – Charged kanghi ke paas paani ka molecule apna ulta-charge wala siray kanghi ki taraf ghuma leta hai: (−) kanghi ko H wala (δ⁺) siray aur (+) kanghi ko O wala (δ⁻) siray paas aata hai. Aise ghume hue bahut saare molecule dhaar ko kanghi ki taraf kheench lete hain.

### Example 5. Dhaar ke bare mein sochna

**Sawaal:** Agar H₂O seedhi line wala (CO₂ jaisa) hota aur uske bond ulte disha ke barabar hote, to kya kanghi ki dhaar mudti?

- Seedha H₂O ke teer katte, kul kheench 0, molecule non-polar. Phir uske do siray δ⁻ aur δ⁺ nahi bante.
- Non-polar liquid ko charged kanghi kheench nahi paati (kam se kam wo bahut kam mudta hai).
- Par asli paani ki dhaar saaf mudti hai: yaani **paani ka molecule seedha nahi, tedha hai**.

**Jaanch:** dhaar mudti hai (dekhi hui baat) ✅ aur tedha shape ke teer kat nahi sakte (Figure 2.3) ✅. Ye ek tarah se paani ka tedha shape **dikhane wala saboot** hai.

### Khud karke dekho: kanghi aur dhaar (ghar par adult ke saath)

1. Plastic ki saaf, sookhi kanghi lo. Sukhe baalon ya oon ke kapde par 20 se 30 baar tezi se ghumao. (Mausam sookha ho: nami wale din ya geele baalon par kam chalta hai.)
2. **Bade ki maujoodgi mein** nal ko bahut halka sa kholo, taaki paani ki **patli dhaar** aaye. Sirf nal ka paani, koi bijli ka saaman paas nahi.
3. Kanghi ko dhaar ke paas bina chhue le jao: dhaar kanghi ki taraf jhuk jaati hai.
4. Phir kanghi ko wapas rub kiye bina doobara laao, ya kapde par ek baar aur rubo: dekho dhaar jitni mudi, wo kanghi ke charge par nirbhar hai.

Ye non-polar liquid ke saath school lab mein teacher dikhate hain (kisi non-polar liquid ki patli dhaar sidhi gir jaati hai). Ghar par non-polar liquid, petrol ya kisi jalne wale ya poison wale liquid ke saath **ye na karein**. Cooking oil ki patli dhaar banana mushkil hai, aur wo bhi bahut kam hi mudti hai: isliye ghar par sirf paani ka hissa karna.

## Part E: Paani aur tel kyun nahi milte? "Jaisa jaise ko ghulata hai"

Ab polar aur non-polar ka sabse kaam ka upyog: **kaun kisme ghulega**. Niyam:

> **Like dissolves like.** Polar aur ionic padarth **polar solvent** (paani) mein ghulte hain. Non-polar padarth **non-polar solvent** (petrol, kuch cleaning liquids) mein ghulte hain.

Kyun? Paani ke polar molecule ek-doosre ko δ⁺ aur δ⁻ siray se kheenchkar paas rehna pasand karte hain. Koi aur polar ya ionic padarth aaye to paani ke molecule use bhi **kheench kar ghere** lete hain, aur wo bhi unke beech mein mil jaata hai. Par **non-polar** molecule paani ko kheenchne ka koi "pakadne wala siray" nahi deta. Paani ke molecule ek-doosre ko pakde rehte hain, non-polar molecule beech mein ghus nahi pata, aur wo alag hi jama ho jaata hai. Dono ka alag-alag rehna ek tarah ka **"jahan jodna mushkil, wahan alag rahna"** hai.

Class 9 mein namak paani mein ghula tha aur tel mein nahi (Class 9 mein dekha: chemical-bonding-part4.md). Ab hum ek aur sawaal samajh sakte hain: kyun?

### Khud karke dekho: tel aur paani ki jaar (ghar par adult ke saath)

(Kitchen ki cheezein, bade ki maujoodgi mein. Kuch chakhna nahi.)

1. Ek saaf kaanch ki jaar mein aadhi tak paani lo. Uske upar usi jitna cooking oil dhire se daalo.
2. Dekho: oil upar ek alag parat mein tairta hai. Paani aur oil milte nahi.
3. Ab jaar ka dhakkan lagakar zor se hilao. Oil ke chhote boondein paani mein dikhengi, par kuch der mein wo phir upar jama ho jaengi.
4. Dhakkan kholkar ek **chutki namak** aur ek chutki cheeni daalo (alag-alag samay par ya ek saath). Namak aur cheeni oil ki parat se **neeche girti hain**, aur paani wali parat mein jaakar dhire-dhire ghul jaati hain. Halka sa hilane par jaldi.

```
   Hilane ke baad, kuch der rakhne par:

     +----------------------------+
     :  oil  (non-polar)          :     <- upar, alag parat mein
     +----------------------------+
     :  paani (polar)             :     <- neeche; namak aur cheeni yahin ghulte hain
     :  namak, cheeni ghule hue   :
     +----------------------------+
```

> **Figure 2.7** – Tel aur paani ki jaar: non-polar oil upar alag parat mein, polar paani neeche. Namak (ionic) aur cheeni (polar hisse wala) paani mein ghulte hain, oil mein nahi.

### Example 6. Kya ghulega? "Like dissolves like" se bolo

**Sawaal:** Tel ke saath ek chutki namak mix karenge, to ghulega? Aur paani ke saath tel?

- Namak ionic (Na⁺ aur Cl⁻), polar solvent chahiye. Tel non-polar solvent hai.
- Tel (non-polar) ke molecule Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ ko kheenchne wale nahi, to **namak tel mein nahi ghulta**.
- Paani polar, tel non-polar: **tel paani mein nahi ghulta**.

**Jaanch:** jaar ke experiment mein tel upar alag, namak paani mein ghula ✅.

### Kuch aur baatein (ek-ek line mein)

- **Dry-cleaning:** tel ya grease ke daag (non-polar) paani se nahi nikalte; unhe non-polar solvent ghula dete hain. Isliye dry-cleaning mein paani ki jagah aisa cleaning liquid use hota hai. (Jalne wale ya poison wale liquids ko ghar par kabhi nahi: ye sirf dukaan ya machine mein.)
- **Tel ke daag aur sabun:** sabun tel ke daag ko nikaalne mein isliye kaam aata hai kyunki uske ek siray ko paani pasand hai aur doosre ko tel. Ye sabun ki kahani **Chapter 10** mein, abhi sirf ek jhalak.
- **Paani "universal solvent":** paani polar hai aur tedha shape ka hai, isliye bahut saare ionic aur polar padarth uske saath aasani se mil jaate hain: namak, cheeni, kai gases. Isliye use "universal solvent" kehte hain. Par naam thoda bada hai: **tel, mom aur petrol** jaise non-polar padarth paani mein nahi ghulte: "sabhi kuch" nahi, "bahut kuch".

## Part F: Namak paani mein kaise ghulta hai: siray ka khel

Class 9 mein humne kaha ki paani ke molecule namak ke ions ko alag kar dete hain. Ab hum jaante hain ki **kaun sa siray kis ion ko**. Paani ke molecule ka O siray δ⁻, H siray δ⁺ hai. Isliye:

- **Na⁺** (positive ion) ko paani ka **O wala (δ⁻) siray** kheenchta hai, paani ke molecule Na⁺ ki taraf O siray karke ghere khade ho jaate hain.
- **Cl⁻** (negative ion) ko paani ka **H wala (δ⁺) siray** kheenchta hai: paani ke molecule Cl⁻ ki taraf H siray karke ghere khade ho jaate hain.

```
   Namak ke crystal ke kinare par:           Ghulne ke baad ghol mein:

     Na+  Cl-  Na+  Cl-                             (O)                (H)
     Cl-  Na+  Cl-  Na+                              :                  :
                                              (O) : Na+ : (O)    (H) : Cl- : (H)
   Paani ke molecule kinare ke ions ko                :                  :
   kheenchte hain:                                   (O)                (H)
        H₂O  (δ⁻ O siray) --> Na+
        H₂O  (δ⁺ H siray) --> Cl-               (O) = paani ka molecule, O wala δ⁻ siray ion ki taraf
                                                (H) = paani ka molecule, H wala δ⁺ siray ion ki taraf

   Na+ ke paas 'O' siray, Cl- ke paas 'H' siray. Ions ab azaad, paani ke molecule ghere hue.
```

> **Figure 2.8** – Namak ghulne ka siray-wala khel: Na⁺ ko paani ka O (δ⁻) siray kheenchta hai, Cl⁻ ko paani ka H (δ⁺) siray. Ghere hue ions crystal se alag ho jaate hain aur ghol mein ghoomte hain. (Ye sirf ek jhalak hai, ghere paani molecules ki sankhya asli se kam di hai.)

### Example 7. Siray dhoondo

**Sawaal:** Mg²⁺ (positive) aur O²⁻ ya K⁺ aur Br⁻ jaise ions paani mein hon, to Mg²⁺ ke paas paani ka kaun sa siray hoga? Aur Br⁻ ke paas?

- Mg²⁺ positive ion hai, usse ulta-charge wala δ⁻ siray: **O wala siray** paas aayega.
- Br⁻ negative ion hai, usse ulta-charge wala δ⁺ siray: **H wala siray** paas aayega.

**Jaanch:** ulta charge hi kheenchta hai (positive ko δ⁻, negative ko δ⁺) ✅; Na⁺, Cl⁻ ka Figure 2.8 wala niyam hi dobara lagaya ✅.

## Part G: HCl paani mein ions kyun banata hai, aur cheeni kyun nahi?

Chapter 4 (Electrolysis, Topic 1) mein humne dekha ki paani ke molecule HCl ko itna kheenchte hain ki wo H⁺ aur Cl⁻ mein toot jaata hai (ionisation). Chapter 1 (Topic 2) mein H⁺ ka asli roop H₃O⁺ bataya tha. Ab polar bond aur polar paani ki nazar se dekhte hain ki paani HCl ko **kyun** kheench paata hai, aur cheeni ko kyun nahi todta.

HCl ek polar molecule hai: H taraf δ⁺, Cl taraf δ⁻ (farq 0.9). Jab HCl paani mein jaata hai:

1. Paani ka **O siray (δ⁻)** HCl ke **H (δ⁺)** ko kheenchta hai, aur paani ka **H siray (δ⁺)** HCl ke **Cl (δ⁻)** ko kheenchta hai.
2. Cl ne pehle se hi shared pair apni taraf zyada kheencha hua tha. Paani ki kheench ke saath Cl **shared pair ko poora le leta hai** aur Cl⁻ ban jaata hai.
3. H, apna electron jodi Cl ke paas chhodkar, ek nanga H⁺ ban jaata hai, jo paani ke O se jud jaata hai aur H₃O⁺ banta hai.

```
        δ⁺    δ⁻                          paani ka O siray, HCl ke H ko kheenchta hai
        H --- Cl      +    H₂O    --->     H₃O⁺    +    Cl⁻
        (polar bond)      (polar)          (hydronium)    (chloride ion)

   atom ginti:   H: 1 + 2 = 3     O: 1 = 1     Cl: 1 = 1        (dono taraf barabar)
   charge ginti: 0 = (+1) + (−1) = 0
```

> **Figure 2.9** – HCl ka paani mein toot-na: polar HCl aur polar paani milkar H₃O⁺ aur Cl⁻ bana dete hain. Dono taraf ke atoms aur charge barabar hain.

HCl + H₂O → H₃O⁺ + Cl⁻.

### Example 8. Equation ko check karo

**Sawaal:** HCl + H₂O → H₃O⁺ + Cl⁻ balanced hai? Charge bhi barabar hai?

- Baayen taraf: H = 1 + 2 = 3, O = 1, Cl = 1.
- Daayen taraf: H = 3 (H₃O⁺ mein), O = 1, Cl = 1.
- Charge: baayen taraf 0 + 0 = 0; daayen +1 + (−1) = 0.

**Jaanch:** atoms 3 = 3, 1 = 1, 1 = 1 ✅ aur charge 0 = 0 ✅.

(H₂O ka ek lone pair H⁺ ko "dono electron" kaise deta hai, ye ek naya bond hai: **Topic 3** mein.)

### Cheeni ke saath fark kyun?

Cheeni bhi covalent hai aur paani mein ghulti hai. Par uske molecule ke andar ki bonds **toot-te nahi**: paani cheeni ke poore molecule ko apne beech mein jagah de deta hai (cheeni ke molecule ka kuch hissa polar hai, isliye wo paani ke saath mil jaata hai), par **koi bond ion banane ke liye nahi tootta**. Isliye cheeni ka ghol bijli nahi chalata. HCl ke saath ek bond **polar** tha aur H itna δ⁺ tha ki paani ke O ne use utha liya.

| Padarth | Kis kism ka | Paani mein kya hota hai | Ghol bijli chalata hai? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Namak (NaCl) | Ionic | Ions paani se ghere jaate hain, azaad (dissociation) | Haan |
| HCl | Polar covalent | Bond toot-ta hai, H₃O⁺ aur Cl⁻ bante hain (ionisation) | Haan |
| Cheeni | Covalent (kuch polar hissa) | Poore molecule alag ho jaate hain, koi ion nahi | Nahi |
| Tel | Non-polar covalent | Ghulta hi nahi, alag parat | Nahi |

### Example 9. Bijli chalegi ya nahi?

**Sawaal:** Namak, HCl, cheeni aur tel mein se paani mein daalne par kis-kis ka ghol LED jalayega?

- Namak: ions azaad, jalayega.
- HCl: bond ke toot-te hi H₃O⁺ aur Cl⁻ ions, jalayega. (Sirf padhne ke liye: HCl ke saath ghar par ya student ko kuch nahi karna; ye poison hai aur jalaata hai.)
- Cheeni: koi ion nahi, nahi jalayegi.
- Tel: ghulta hi nahi, nahi jalayega.

**Jaanch:** jahan ions bane wahan bijli, baaki mein nahi ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti: "Jis molecule mein polar bond ho, wo molecule hamesha polar hota hai."**
Ye galat hai, kyunki molecule ki polarity sirf bonds par nahi, unke **shape** par bhi nirbhar hai.

*Demonstration 1 (tug-of-war):* do barabar-taakat wale bachche ek gaadi ko ulti seedhi disha mein kheench rahe hain. Har bachche ka zor bada hai, par gaadi hilti nahi. CO₂ mein ye hi hai: har C=O bond polar (farq 1.0), par seedhi line mein ulti disha ke do barabar teer: 1 − 1 = 0. Molecule non-polar hai.

*Demonstration 2 (paani se tulna):* H₂O mein bhi do polar bond hain (farq 1.4 har ek), par tedha shape hai, to teer ek taraf jhukte hain aur kat nahi pate. Isliye H₂O polar hai: paani ki dhaar kanghi ki taraf mudti hai. **Dono molecules mein bond polar hain, par ek polar aur doosra non-polar**: sirf shape ka fark.

*Ek aur dekho:* CH₄ mein bond lagbhag non-polar hi hain (0.4), phir bhi sachchi baat ye hai ki symmetric shape se kul kheench 0 hai.

Isliye jab bhi polar molecule ka sawaal aaye, ye mat kehna "bond polar hai, to molecule polar". Teer banao aur dekho kat rahe hain ya nahi.

## Quick Recap

- **Bond polar** ek bond ki baat hai. **Molecule polar** poore molecule ke saare bonds ke teer jodne ke baad ki baat hai.
- Har polar bond ka teer δ⁺ se δ⁻ ki taraf. Kul kheench = saare teeron ka jod (taakat aur disha).
- Kul kheench **0** to non-polar molecule (CO₂, CH₄). Kul kheench **bachi** to polar molecule (H₂O, NH₃, HCl).
- CO₂ seedha (linear): ulte, barabar teer kat jaate hain. H₂O tedha (bent) aur NH₃ pyramid: teer ek taraf jhukte hain. CH₄ chaar-kone wala, barabar: teer kat jaate hain. Angles aage ki class mein.
- Do atom wale molecule mein bond polar hai to molecule polar.
- Paani (polar) charged kanghi ki taraf mudta hai: (−) kanghi ko H siray, (+) kanghi ko O siray. Ye paani ke tedhe shape ka saboot hai.
- **Like dissolves like:** polar aur ionic padarth paani mein ghulte hain; non-polar (tel, mom, petrol) paani mein nahi, non-polar solvent mein ghulte hain. Isliye tel aur paani nahi milte, aur dry-cleaning mein non-polar solvent kaam aata hai.
- Namak paani mein: Na⁺ ko O siray, Cl⁻ ko H siray. HCl paani mein: HCl + H₂O → H₃O⁺ + Cl⁻ (ionisation). Cheeni paani mein poore molecule ke roop mein ghulti hai, bijli nahi chalati.
- Paani ko "universal solvent" kehte hain, par tel, mom aur petrol ke liye wo kaam nahi karta.

## Practice Questions

(Sab sawaal paper par hal karne ke liye hain. Use: H 2.1, C 2.5, O 3.5, Cl 3.0. Koi chemical ghar par nahi aazmana.)

1. For CCl₄ (poisonous, named only): draw the dot diagram with all lone pairs and count the valence electrons used. Find the C–Cl electronegativity difference, say which atom is δ⁻, and then decide whether the molecule is polar or non-polar. Explain with arrows.
2. CHCl₃ (chloroform: poisonous, named only) has one C–H and three C–Cl bonds. Find the electronegativity difference for each bond. Carbon has no lone pair, so why is the molecule still polar?
3. Ethanol mixes with water in every ratio. What does "like dissolves like" suggest about ethanol molecules? Do not use any new idea; just use the rule.
4. A student pours petrol into water in a bottle (thought question only: never do this at home) and then drops a small piece of wax in. Where will the petrol layer be, and where will the wax end up and why?
5. A wax drop falls on a cotton shirt and plain water does not remove it, but a non-polar cleaning liquid does. Explain.
6. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) A molecule with polar bonds is always polar. (b) A polar molecule is always ionic. (c) A non-polar molecule cannot contain any polar bond. (d) A thin stream of water bends towards a charged comb whether the comb is positive or negative. (e) Petrol dissolves in water because both are covalent.
7. In the comb and tap experiment, why must the stream be thin, and why does the stream bend towards the comb for both signs of charge?
8. A student says: "CCl₄ and HCl are both covalent, so CCl₄ also splits into ions when put in water and conducts electricity." Find the mistake.
9. A linear molecule X–Y–X has two equal polar bonds from Y to each X, where each pull arrow has a size of 3 units. What is the net pull? What is the net pull if the same molecule were bent (arrows not opposite)? (Give your answer in words: zero or not zero.)
10. Why is water called the universal solvent? Give one substance for which the name is not true.
11. Why do dry-cleaners use non-polar liquids instead of water for greasy stains? Mention the rule you use.
12. For each, say "dissolves in water" or "does not dissolve in water", using only polar or non-polar: (a) a polar substance (b) an ionic substance (c) a non-polar substance such as petrol.

### Answers

1. CCl₄: C 4 + 4 Cl × 7 = **32** valence electrons. Dot diagram mein C ke chaaron taraf 4 Cl; 4 shared pairs (8 e) aur har Cl par 3 lone pairs (4 × 6 = 24 e): 8 + 24 = **32** ✅. C–Cl ka farq 3.0 − 2.5 = **0.5**, Cl taraf δ⁻ (halka polar bond, thumb rule ki neeche ki seema). Central C par koi lone pair nahi aur chaaron bahar ke atom ek jaise: chaaron teer C se Cl ki taraf, chaaron taraf barabar faile, kat jaate hain: kul kheench 0. **Molecule non-polar**, bond halka polar.
2. CHCl₃: C valence 4 + H 1 + 3 Cl × 7 = 26 electrons; 4 shared pairs (8 e) + 3 × 3 lone pairs on Cl (18 e) = **26** ✅. C–H: 2.5 − 2.1 = **0.4**, C–Cl: 3.0 − 2.5 = **0.5**. Bahar ke atom ek jaise nahi (1 H aur 3 Cl), isliye teer barabar nahi faile aur kat nahi pate. C–Cl wale teen teer Cl ki taraf, C–H ka chhota teer bhi Cl wali taraf ja kar usi disha mein juda: kul kheench bachi, **polar molecule**. (Cl wala hissa δ⁻, H wala δ⁺.) Poison hai: sirf padhne ke liye.
3. Ethanol paani mein har anupaat mein milta hai, to rule ke hisaab se ethanol ke molecule **polar** hone chahiye (kam se kam ek polar hissa), kyunki paani polar hai aur polar hi polar mein ghulta hai.
4. Petrol non-polar hai aur paani polar, to **petrol paani ke upar alag parat** mein rahega (milega nahi). Wax non-polar hai, to **wax petrol wali parat mein ghulegi**, paani mein nahi. Rule: like dissolves like. (Petrol bahut jalne wala hai: ye thought question hi rahega.)
5. Mom (wax) non-polar hai, paani polar, to mom paani mein nahi ghulti, isliye plain water se daag nahi jaata. Non-polar cleaning liquid mom ko ghula deta hai (jaisa jaise ko ghulata hai).
6. (a) **False** ❌: CO₂ mein polar bond hain par molecule non-polar. (b) **False** ❌: HCl polar hai par covalent molecule hai. (c) **False** ❌: CO₂ non-polar hai par uske bond polar hain. (d) **True** ✅: (−) kanghi ko H siray, (+) kanghi ko O siray paas aata hai. (e) **False** ❌: petrol non-polar hai aur paani polar; sirf covalent hona ghulne ke liye kaafi nahi.
7. Dhaar patli ho to kheench sab molecules par asar karke use mod sakti hai, mota dhaar ka bhaar zyada hoga aur kam mudegi. Dono sign ke liye: (−) kanghi ke paas paani ke molecule H (δ⁺) siray ghumate hain, (+) kanghi ke paas O (δ⁻) siray ghumate hain. Dono hi tarah molecule kanghi ki taraf kheenche jaate hain.
8. CCl₄ **non-polar** hai (symmetric, teer kat jaate hain, aur bond bhi sirf 0.5 farq ke). Paani ke polar molecule ko pakadne ke liye koi siray nahi, to na wo paani mein ghulta hai, na bond tootte hain, na ion bante hain. HCl mein H itna δ⁺ hai ki paani ka O use kheench leta hai. Isliye CCl₄ paani mein ions nahi banata aur bijli nahi chalata. (CCl₄ poison hai, sirf padhne ke liye.)
9. Seedhi line ho to do barabar, ulte teer: 3 − 3 = 0, **kul kheench zero**, non-polar. Tedha ho to teer ulte nahi, kat nahi sakte: kul kheench **zero nahi**, polar molecule.
10. Paani polar hai aur tedha, isliye bahut saare ionic aur polar padarth (namak, cheeni) usme ghul jaate hain: isliye "universal solvent". Par tel, mom aur petrol jaise non-polar padarth usme nahi ghulte, to naam poori tarah sach nahi.
11. Tel ya grease non-polar hota hai aur paani polar, to paani use ghula nahi paata. Isliye non-polar liquid use hota hai, jo like dissolves like ke hisaab se grease ko ghula deta hai. (Jalne wale ya poison wale liquid ghar par nahi.)
12. (a) polar substance: **paani mein ghulta hai** (polar polar mein). (b) ionic substance: **paani mein ghulta hai** (paani ke O aur H siray ions ko ghere lete hain). (c) petrol (non-polar): **paani mein nahi ghulta**.
