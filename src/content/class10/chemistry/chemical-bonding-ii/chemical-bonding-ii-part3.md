# Topic 3: Coordinate Bond: Ek Hi Ne Diye Dono Electron

Topic 1 mein humne dekha ki shared pair kabhi barabar baanta jaata hai aur kabhi ek taraf khisak jaata hai, aur Topic 2 mein ki isse poore molecule ka swabhav kaise badalta hai. Un sab bondon mein ek baat ek jaisi thi: **har atom ne ek-ek electron diya**, tab jaakar ek shared pair bana (H aur Cl ne 1-1, N aur H ne 1-1). Class 9 ke covalent bond ka yahi "aadha-aadha" niyam tha. Ab ek naya sawaal: kya ek shared pair aisa bhi ho sakta hai jisme **dono electron ek hi atom ke ho**? Aur agar ho sakta hai, to kya wo bond kuch alag nikalega? Chapter 1 mein ek chhoti line chhod di gayi thi ki H⁺ aur H₂O ke beech ek "khaas coordinate bond" banta hai. Aaj hum us line ka poora hisaab chukayenge.

## Ek bill, do plate

Mr. Ghaisas apni bhatiji Madhura ko lene uske school gaye the. Annual Day ki practice abhi khatam hui thi, aur Madhura ne kaha, "Chacha, canteen chalein? Wahan ka vada-pav mashhoor hai." Counter par Madhura ke do classmates ek-saath khade the. Ek ke haath mein wallet tha, doosre ka wallet ghar par reh gaya tha. Kaunter wale bhaiya ne kaha, "Do plate vada-pav, bill ₹60."

Jiske paas wallet tha, usne **poore ₹60 akele de diye**. Doosre ne ek bhi rupaya nahi diya. Phir bhi plate dono ke haath mein aayi, aur dono ne saath baith kar vada-pav khaya.

Mr. Ghaisas ne Madhura se poochha, "Ab bata, plate dekh kar kya tu bata sakti hai ki kiska vada-pav 'udhaar ka' hai?" Madhura hansi. "Nahi Chacha! Do plate ek jaisi hain. Bill kisi ne bhi bhara ho, vada-pav dono ke liye barabar hai." Mr. Ghaisas ne sir hilaya. "Bas, yahi hai aaj ka topic. Class 9 mein bond aise bante the jaise **dono dost aadha-aadha bill** dete hain. Par kabhi-kabhi **ek hi atom poora bill** bhar deta hai: dono electron wahi deta hai, aur doosra atom sirf **le leta hai**. Ye bhi bond hai, aur bond banne ke baad **dono ko aadha-aadha wala bond** hi milta hai. Par ye tabhi ho sakta hai jab ek ke paas 'paise' hon aur doosre ka 'wallet khaali' ho."

Madhura ki aankhein chamak gayi. "Chacha, matlab pehle chemistry mein 'paise' kya hain, aur 'khaali wallet' kya?" "Wahi to dekhna hai," Mr. Ghaisas ne kaha. "Aur ek hairaan karne wali baat: is tarah ke bond se banne wale **ammonium ion** aur **hydronium ion** ke baare mein tum pehle hi sun chuki ho."

```
   Normal bill:      Dost A  ---- Rs 30 ---->  counter  <---- Rs 30 ----  Dost B
                     (dono aadha-aadha dete hain)

   Ek ne bhara:      Dost A  ---- Rs 60 ---->  counter  <---- Rs 0 -----  Dost B
                     (A ke paas paise, B ka wallet khaali)

   Plate:            A ki plate   B ki plate     dono ek jaisi, kisi par 'udhaar' ka nishaan nahi
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Bill ka analogy: normal bond mein dono aadha-aadha dete hain, coordinate bond mein ek hi poora bill bharta hai. Bill ke baad dono plates ek jaisi hoti hain, bilkul jaise bond ke baad dono electrons ka koi nishaan nahi rehta ki kaun kahan se aaya.

## Part A: Normal covalent bond aur coordinate bond mein fark

Class 9 ka ek covalent bond yaad karo: H aur Cl, **ek-ek electron**, ek shared pair. Ginti ka tareeka ye tha ki shared pair ke 2 electron dono atoms ki octet (ya duplet) mein ginte jaate hain.

Ab coordinate bond: wahi shared pair, wahi 2 electron, wahi ginti. Bas **ye 2 electron ek hi atom ke the**. Isliye ise **donor se acceptor ki taraf ek teer (→)** se likhte hain: D → A. Teer sirf ye batata hai ki "paise kahan se aaye". Bond ke baad ye ek **normal covalent bond** hi hai.

Dot diagram mein ek atom ke electron **o** se aur doosre ke **x** se dikhate hain (Class 9 wali shaili). Dekho kaise alag dikhta hai:

```
   Normal covalent bond                 Coordinate bond

        H x o Cl                            N o o H
        x   H ka electron                   o   dono electron N ke
        o   Cl ka electron                  H+ ke paas koi electron nahi

   Shared pair: 1 x aur 1 o              Shared pair: 2 o aur 0 x
   Dono atoms ne ek-ek diya              N ne akele dono diye (N -> H)
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Dono bond mein shared pair mein 2 electron hain. Farq sirf ye hai ki pehle mein ek x aur ek o hai (dono ne diya), doosre mein dono o hain (ek hi atom ne diya).

### Ye kab ho sakta hai? Do shartein

Bill wali kahani se samajh aata hai ki dono shartein ek saath chahiye:

1. **Donor ke paas ek lone pair ho** (paise hon).
2. **Acceptor ke paas ek khaali jagah ho** (wallet khaali, yaani wo dono electron le sake aur bhar jaaye).

Agar dono ke paas paise hain, to koi bill ka bojh uthane ki zarurat nahi (aur na hi khaali jagah). Agar dono ke wallet khaali hain, to bill bharega kaun? Dono hi halat mein coordinate bond **nahi** banega.

| Donor ke paas lone pair? | Acceptor ke paas khaali jagah? | Nateeja |
|---|---|---|
| Haan | Haan | Coordinate bond banta hai |
| Haan | Nahi | Nahi banta (lene ki jagah nahi) |
| Nahi | Haan | Nahi banta (dene ko kuch nahi) |
| Nahi | Nahi | Nahi banta |

### Example 1. Kaun donor, kaun acceptor?

**Sawaal:** NH₃, H₂O aur H⁺ mein se donor aur acceptor pehchano.

- **NH₃:** N ke 5 baahari electron hain, 3 H ke saath 3 shared pairs banaye (3 electron N ke), bache 5 − 3 = 2 electron = **1 lone pair**. Lone pair hai, to donor ban sakta hai.
- **H₂O:** O ke 6 baahari electron, 2 H ke saath 2 pairs (2 electron O ke), bache 6 − 2 = 4 electron = **2 lone pairs**. Ye bhi donor ban sakta hai.
- **H⁺:** H atom ka ek hi electron tha, wo chala gaya. Ab **koi electron nahi**, aur H ki shell (duplet tak) **2 electron** le sakti hai. Isliye H⁺ ke paas khaali jagah hai: **acceptor**.

**Jaanch:** NH₃ mein lone pairs = 1 ✅, H₂O mein = 2 ✅, H⁺ mein electron = 1 − 1 = 0 ✅ (kuch dene ko nahi, lene ko jagah hai).

## Part B: Ammonium ion: NH₃ + H⁺ → NH₄⁺

(Ammonia gas teekhi aur zehreeli hai: sirf padhne ke liye, kabhi sunghna ya ghar par istemaal nahi.) Ek ammonia molecule (NH₃) ko H⁺ mil jaaye to kya hoga? N par ek lone pair hai, aur H⁺ ko 2 electron chahiye. N apni **poori lone pair** H⁺ ko de deta hai, aur ab wo pair N aur H dono ka shared pair ban jaata hai.

```
         H                                           H
         x                                           x
         o                                           o
   H x o N o o       +     H+     -->        [ H x o N o o H ]+
         o                                           o
         x                                           x
         H                                           H

   NH3: N par 1 lone pair (o o)            NH4+: chaar shared pairs, koi lone pair nahi
   H+: koi electron nahi                   teen pairs (x o) aur ek pair (o o)
```

> **Figure 3.3** – NH₃ + H⁺ → NH₄⁺. N ke 5 electron (o) hain, har H ka 1 electron (x). Baayen, upar aur neeche ke teen pairs mein ek x aur ek o hai (normal bond). Daayen pair mein **dono o** hain: wo coordinate bond hai, jisme N ne dono electron diye. Bracket aur + poore ion ka charge dikhate hain.

Ab ginti. **Pehle:** NH₃ mein N par 3 bond pairs + 1 lone pair = 4 pairs = 8 electron. H⁺ par 0 electron. **Baad mein:** NH₄⁺ mein N par 4 bond pairs, lone pair 0, yaani 4 × 2 = 8 electron. Har H par 2 electron (duplet).

**Dhyan do:** H⁺ ne jab se 2 electron paye (apne nahi, N ke), uski shell duplet ban gayi, helium jaisi. Aur N ke paas ab bhi 8 hain: octet **nahi tooti**.

Dash mein dono tarike se likh sakte hain:

```
              H                                H
              :                                :
      [ H --- N ---> H ]+              [ H --- N --- H ]+
              :                                :
              H                                H

      teer se (kahan se aaya)          sab dash barabar (bond ke baad)
```

> **Figure 3.4** – NH₄⁺ ke dash diagram. Pehle mein coordinate bond ko teer (→) se dikhaya (donor N se acceptor H ki taraf), doosre mein chaaron N–H ko ek jaise dash se. Dono picture ek hi ion ki hain. (Is figure mein khadi lakeer ko colon se dikhaya hai.)

### Example 2. NH₄⁺ ke electron ginti se check

**Sawaal:** NH₃ + H⁺ → NH₄⁺ mein kul baahari electron ginke dikhao ki kuch bhi extra nahi aaya, aur sab ke octet/duplet poore hain.

- NH₃: N ke 5 + 3 H ke 3 = **8** electron.
- H⁺: **0** electron.
- NH₄⁺ mein kul electron chaahiye: 8 + 0 = **8**. Check: 4 shared pairs × 2 = 8 ✅.
- N par 8 ✅. Har H par 2 ✅.

**Jaanch:** Cheezein aise nikli: 5 + 3 = 8, aur 4 pairs × 2 = 8, dono barabar ✅. Matlab ek bhi nayi electron nahi aayi, sirf ek lone pair ne apni jagah badli (N ke paas se N aur H ke beech).

### Example 3. NH₄⁺ ka +1 charge kaise aaya?

**Sawaal:** NH₃ ka koi charge nahi, to NH₄⁺ ka +1 charge kahan se aaya?

- Protons ginti karo: N ke 7, 4 H ke 4 = **11 protons**.
- Electron: 8 baahari ke alawa N ka andar wala shell (2 electron) = 8 + 2 = **10 electrons**.
- Net charge = protons minus electrons = 11 − 10 = **+1**.

Charge **N par nahi, poore ion par** hai. Ye sirf ek proton (H⁺) ke aane se hai, jo apne saath koi electron nahi laya.

**Jaanch:** NH₃ (0) + H⁺ (+1) = +1 ✅. Protons: NH₃ mein 7 + 3 = 10, H⁺ ka 1, kul 11 ✅.

## Part C: Bond ke baad sab bond barabar kyun?

Ek bahut zaroori baat. NH₄⁺ mein N ke saath **chaar** H lage hain. Teen bond "normal" bane, aur ek coordinate. To kya wo ek alag hai? **Nahi.** Chaaron N–H bond bilkul ek jaise hain.

Kyun? Bill wali kahani yaad karo: bill ek ne bhara, par vada-pav ki plate dekh kar **bata nahi sakte** ki kiska udhaar ka hai. Electron bhi aise hi hain: **electron sab ek jaise hote hain**. Shared pair banne ke baad usme koi label nahi chipka ki "ye N ka tha". Dono electron N aur H dono ke beech hain, aur wahi dono ko jodte hain. Ek N–H bond ke andar ye dekhne ka koi tareeka nahi ki ye pair pehle kahan se aaya.

Isi liye NH₄⁺ ka koi bhi H alag nahi: agar ion se ek H⁺ wapas nikle, to wo **chaaron mein se koi bhi** ho sakta hai, aur jo beech mein rahe wo phir se NH₃ hi hai. (Mapne par bhi chaaron N–H ki lambai barabar nikalti hai; ye kyun, wo aage ki class mein.)

### Khud karke dekho: kaagaz ki chits (ghar par, kisi bade ke saath)

Ek kaagaz par N likho aur 5 **laal chits** (N ke electron) rakho. Teen H ke liye 3 **neeli chits** (har ek ka 1). Teen N–H pairs banao: har mein 1 laal + 1 neeli. Bachi hui 2 laal chits ek lone pair hain. Ab ek chhota kaagaz H⁺ ka bana ke rakho: **koi chit nahi**. Dono laal chits ko N aur H⁺ ke beech rakh do. Ab pehle teen pairs (laal + neeli) aur chauthi pair (laal + laal) hain.

Ab ek dost se kaho aankhein band kare, tum chaaron pairs ko mila ke ek-ek dabbe mein bhar do aur chits ke rang **chhupa do**. Dost ke samne sirf "N–H" ki chaar dabbian rahengi. Kya wo bata sakta hai kaun si coordinate thi? **Nahi.** Asli electron mein to koi rang hai hi nahi, isliye asli ion mein bhi koi nahi bata sakta. Kaagaz ke rang sirf hamari ginti ke liye the.

## Part D: Hydronium ion: H₂O + H⁺ → H₃O⁺

Ab wahi kahani paani ke saath. H₂O mein O par **2 lone pairs** hain. Jab H⁺ paas aata hai, to O ek lone pair H⁺ ko de deta hai.

```
         oo                                          oo
   H x o O o o       +     H+     -->        [ H x o O o o H ]+
         o                                           o
         x                                           x
         H                                           H

   H2O: O par 2 lone pairs                H3O+: O par 1 lone pair, 3 shared pairs
   (ek upar o o, ek daayen o o)           coordinate pair (o o) daayen taraf
```

> **Figure 3.5** – H₂O + H⁺ → H₃O⁺. O ke 6 electron (o), har H ka 1 (x). H₂O mein do lone pairs hain: ek upar, ek daayen. Daayen wali pair H⁺ ko de di jaati hai aur wo coordinate bond ka shared pair ban jaati hai. Ab O par ek hi lone pair bachti hai (upar), aur teen shared pairs hain (dono x-o wale aur ek o-o wala).

Is figure ko dhyan se padho: baayen wali pair (x o) aur neeche wali pair (o x) normal bond hain, daayen wali (o o) coordinate bond hai, aur ek lone pair upar bachti hai.

### Example 4. H₃O⁺ ki ginti

**Sawaal:** H₂O + H⁺ → H₃O⁺ mein dikhao ki O aur har H ka octet/duplet poora hai, aur ion ka charge nikalo.

- H₂O mein O: 2 bond pairs + 2 lone pairs = 4 pairs = 8 electron.
- H⁺ ko O ne ek lone pair di. Ab O par: **3 bond pairs** + **1 lone pair** = 4 pairs = **8 electron** ✅.
- Har H par 2 electron (duplet) ✅, naya H⁺ bhi.
- Charge: protons = 8 (O) + 3 (H) = 11; electrons = 2 (andar) + 8 (baahari) = 10; net = 11 − 10 = **+1**.

**Jaanch:** H₂O (0) + H⁺ (+1) = +1 ✅. Octet: 3 × 2 + 1 × 2 = 8 ✅.

### Chapter 1 se rishta: "H⁺(aq)" asal mein H₃O⁺ kyun hai

Chapter 1 (Topic 2) mein tumne padha tha ki acid paani mein H⁺ deta hai, par akela H⁺ paani mein tair nahi sakta: wo turant H₂O se chipak kar H₃O⁺ ban jaata hai, aur ye bhi likha tha ki is "khaas coordinate bond" ki banawat Chapter 7 mein dekhenge. Ab uska poora kaaran saaf hai:

- H⁺ ke paas **khaali jagah** hai (koi electron nahi, 2 tak le sakta hai). Wo bahut **bhookha** hai.
- Paani ke har molecule ke paas O par **do lone pairs** hain, bahut saare.
- Jaise hi H⁺ paani mein aata hai, aas-paas ka koi H₂O molecule apni ek lone pair de deta hai. Coordinate bond **turant** ban jaata hai.

Isliye acid ke paani mein jab hum **H⁺(aq)** likhte hain, to uska asli matlab **H₃O⁺** hota hai. Chapter 1 ke equation HCl + H₂O → H₃O⁺ + Cl⁻ mein ye wahi H₃O⁺ hai (HCl paani mein kyun tootta hai, wo Topic 2 mein dekha). Dono tarah se likhna sahi hai: H⁺(aq) chhota roop hai aur H₃O⁺ poora.

(Tez acid ko haath se mat chhuo: ye sirf padhne ke liye hai.)

## Part E: Pehle aur baad ki ginti: ek tulna

Ab chaaron species ko saath rakhte hain. Dhyan do ki lone pair kahan gayi aur bond pair kahan aayi.

| Species | Central atom | Bond pairs | Lone pairs | Atom ke paas kul electron |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NH₃ | N | 3 | 1 | 8 |
| NH₄⁺ | N | 4 | 0 | 8 |
| H₂O | O | 2 | 2 | 8 |
| H₃O⁺ | O | 3 | 1 | 8 |

Teen baatein table se:

1. **Donor ne apni ek lone pair khoi** aur wahi ek **naya bond pair** bani (NH₃ mein 1 → 0; H₂O mein 2 → 1).
2. Central atom ki **kul ginti 8 hi rahi**: octet na tooti, na bani.
3. H₃O⁺ ka central atom NH₃ jaisa dikhta hai: 3 bond pairs aur 1 lone pair. (Dono mein 8 electron ek hi tarah baante hain; ye sirf ginti ki tulna hai, shape ki baat nahi.)

### Example 5. Kya NH₄⁺ ek aur H⁺ le sakta hai?

**Sawaal:** NH₄⁺ ko ek aur H⁺ mil jaaye to kya coordinate bond banega?

- N par ab **koi lone pair nahi** (table dekho: 4 bond pairs, 0 lone pairs). Dene ko kuch nahi.
- Aur N ke 8 electron poore ho chuke hain: octet bhar gayi.
- Donor ki shart pehli hi toot gayi. Isliye **nahi**, NH₄⁺ aur H⁺ ke beech coordinate bond nahi banega. (Dono +1 hain, to door bhi bhagenge.)

**Jaanch:** shart 1: donor ke paas lone pair chahiye. NH₄⁺ ke N par 0 hain ✅ (nahi bana).

### Example 6. Do ek jaise: kya bond banega?

**Sawaal:** Do NH₃ molecule paas aayein, to kya unke beech coordinate bond banega?

- Dono ke N par **1-1 lone pair** hai (dene ko hai).
- Par dono ki octet poori hai, kisi ke paas **khaali jagah** nahi (lene ko kuch nahi).
- Dono wallet bhare, dono hi bill bharna chahenge, par koi lene wala nahi.
- **Nahi banega.** (Wahi bill wali kahani: do log ke paas paise hon par koi bhi khaali nahi.)

**Jaanch:** shart 2 (acceptor ke paas khaali jagah) dono mein toot gayi ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "NH₄⁺ mein coordinate bond baaki teen se kamzor ya alag hota hai."**
Demo: ek ion ko do tarah se kaat kar dekho. Bill ki kahani mein ek ne ₹60 diye, doosre ne ₹0, par jab plate dono ke haath mein aa gayi, to dono vada-pav ek jaise hain: kisi par "udhaar" likha nahi. Ab chits wali activity se dekho: chaar pairs ko dabbon mein bhar do aur rang chhupa do. Ab chaar dabbe ek jaise hain: har dabbe mein 2 electron, jo N aur H ke beech hain. Koi bhi pair "adha N ka aur adha H ka" nahi, aur koi "poora N ka" nahi dikhta. Isliye jab NH₄⁺ ek H⁺ khota hai, to **koi bhi ek H** ja sakta hai, aur bachne wala NH₃ ek jaisa hi banta hai. Matlab: coordinate bond sirf **banne ki kahani** hai, bond ke baad wo ek normal covalent bond hi hai.

**Galti 2: "H⁺ apna electron saath lata hai."**
Demo: H atom mein 1 proton aur 1 electron hota hai, to charge **0**. H⁺ ka matlab hai us atom ne apna electron **kho diya**: proton 1, electron 0, charge **+1**. Agar H⁺ apne electron ke saath aata, to wo to normal H atom hota (charge 0), aur NH₃ + H ka net charge 0 + 0 = **0** hota, +1 nahi. Par humne NH₄⁺ ka charge **+1** dekha (11 proton, 10 electron). Isliye H⁺ **koi electron nahi laata**: bond ke dono electron N ne diye. Isi wajah se ion par +1 bacha.

## Quick Recap

- **Coordinate bond:** wo covalent bond jisme shared pair ke **dono electron ek hi atom (donor)** se aaye. Likhne ke liye D → A, donor se acceptor ki taraf teer.
- **Do shartein:** donor ke paas **lone pair**, acceptor ke paas **khaali jagah**. Bill ki kahani: ek ke paas paise, doosre ka wallet khaali.
- **H⁺** ke paas koi electron nahi hota (proton 1, electron 0); wo **acceptor** hai.
- NH₃ + H⁺ → **NH₄⁺**: N ki ek lone pair H⁺ ko gayi; N par 4 bond pairs, 0 lone pairs, 8 electron.
- H₂O + H⁺ → **H₃O⁺**: O ki ek lone pair H⁺ ko gayi; O par 3 bond pairs, 1 lone pair, 8 electron.
- Dono ions ka net charge **+1** hai (11 protons, 10 electrons). Charge **poore ion** ka hai.
- Bond banne ke baad **chaaron N–H (aur teeno O–H)** barabar hain: shared pair mein electron ka koi label nahi hota.
- Acid ke paani mein **H⁺(aq)** ka asli matlab **H₃O⁺**.
- NH₄⁺ (lone pair nahi) aur do NH₃ (khaali jagah nahi) coordinate bond nahi bana sakte.

## Practice Questions

(Sab sawaal paper par hal karne ke liye hain. Atomic masses: H 1, N 14, O 16.)

1. State the two conditions required for a coordinate bond. Explain each in a line using the "one friend pays the whole bill" idea.
2. Four pairs of friends are at the counter: (a) both have money, (b) one has money, one has an empty wallet, (c) both have empty wallets, (d) both have money but both want to pay half. Match each situation with: a coordinate bond, an ordinary shared pair, or no bond at all, and say why. Which atom or ion in this lesson plays the "empty wallet" friend?
3. Using protons and electrons, find the net charge of (a) H₂O and (b) H⁺ and (c) H₃O⁺ (O has 8 protons and 8 electrons; each H atom has 1 proton and 1 electron). Then show that (a) + (b) gives (c).
4. Find the mass in u of NH₄⁺ and of H₃O⁺ (ignore the mass of electrons). Which is heavier and by how much? What does that say about the mass added when H⁺ joins?
5. Count the total valence (outer shell) electrons of H₃O⁺ (O has 6, each H has 1, subtract 1 for the +1 charge). Then count the electrons shown in your dot diagram and check that they match.
6. Which of NH₃, NH₄⁺, H₂O, H₃O⁺ and H⁺ has no lone pair on its central atom? Which of these species can **donate** a lone pair, and why can NH₄⁺ not take one more H⁺?
7. A student says, "Two NH₃ molecules can form a coordinate bond because both have a lone pair on N." Find the missing condition and correct the student.
8. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) In H₃O⁺, the coordinate O–H bond is the weakest of the three. (b) H⁺ has no electrons. (c) In NH₃, N has two lone pairs. (d) In NH₄⁺, N has 8 electrons around it. (e) A coordinate bond is one in which both shared electrons come from the same atom. (f) NH₄⁺ has 11 electrons.
9. Explain in three sentences why the symbol H⁺(aq) for an acid in water really stands for H₃O⁺. Use the words lone pair and empty place.
10. Draw H₂O + H⁺ → H₃O⁺ in dot-cross form. Mark which pair is the coordinate pair, and write the donor, the acceptor and the direction of the arrow. How many lone pairs does the donor have before and after?
11. In the reaction NH₃ + H⁺ → NH₄⁺, how do the number of lone pairs on N and the number of bond pairs on N change? Write the numbers before and after, and say what stays the same.
12. The bill story: "the bill was paid by one friend, but both plates are the same." Which part of the NH₄⁺ ion does the plate stand for, and which part does the bill stand for?

### Answers

1. Do shartein: (i) **donor** ke paas ek **lone pair** ho (jaise bill bharne wale ke paas paise), (ii) **acceptor** ke paas **khaali jagah** ho (jaise doosre dost ka khaali wallet, taaki wo dono electron le sake). Dono mein se ek bhi na ho to bond nahi banega.
2. (a) Dono ke paas paise, koi khaali wallet nahi: **normal shared pair ya bond nahi**; yahan dono bill baant lenge, ye ordinary sharing jaisa hai. (b) Ek ke paas paise, ek ka wallet khaali: **coordinate bond** (donor aur acceptor). (c) Dono khaali: **koi bond nahi**, dene ko kuch nahi. (d) Dono paise dekar half-half: **ordinary shared pair**, har atom ek electron deta hai. "Khaali wallet" wala dost is lesson mein **H⁺** hai (koi electron nahi).
3. Protons aur electron ginte hain. (a) H₂O: protons 8 + 2 = 10, electrons 10, net 0. (b) H⁺: proton 1, electron 0, net +1. (c) H₃O⁺: protons 8 + 3 = 11, electrons 10, net +1. Jaanch: 0 + (+1) = +1 ✅. (H₂O ke 10 electron = O ke 8 + do H ke 2; H₃O⁺ mein naye H⁺ ne koi electron nahi joda, isliye electron 10 hi rahe.)
4. NH₄⁺: 14 + 4 × 1 = **18 u**. H₃O⁺: 16 + 3 × 1 = **19 u**. H₃O⁺ **1 u** bhaari hai, kyunki O (16) N (14) se 2 u zyada bhaari hai par H₃O⁺ mein ek H kam hai: 16 + 3 = 19, 14 + 4 = 18. Jaanch: NH₃ (14 + 3 = 17) + H⁺ (1) = 18 ✅. H₂O (16 + 2 = 18) + H⁺ (1) = 19 ✅. H⁺ judne par mass **1 u** badhta hai.
5. Valence electrons: 6 (O) + 3 × 1 (3 H) − 1 (charge +1) = **8**. Diagram mein: O par 3 bond pairs (6 electron) + 1 lone pair (2 electron) = 8 ✅. Dono barabar, matlab ek bhi electron na zyada, na kam.
6. Bina lone pair wale central atom: **NH₄⁺** (N par 0 lone pairs). H⁺ ka koi central atom nahi, par uske paas koi electron nahi, to lone pair nahi. Lone pair wale: **NH₃** (1), **H₂O** (2) aur **H₃O⁺** (1 bachi hai). Isme se H⁺ ko donate karne wale asli donor **NH₃** aur **H₂O** hain; H₃O⁺ khud +1 hai, to H⁺ use door dhakelta hai (Example 5 jaisa). NH₄⁺ ek aur H⁺ nahi le sakta kyunki N par koi lone pair nahi bachi, aur 8 electron poore ho chuke hain.
7. Missing shart: **acceptor ke paas khaali jagah**. Dono NH₃ mein N ki octet poori hai, dono sirf dene wale hain. Lene wala koi nahi, isliye coordinate bond nahi banega. Sahi hota ki ek donor aur ek acceptor ho, jaise NH₃ aur H⁺.
8. (a) **False** ❌: bond banne ke baad teeno O–H bond barabar hain. (b) **True** ✅. (c) **False** ❌: NH₃ mein N par **1** lone pair hai (H₂O mein O par 2). (d) **True** ✅: 4 bond pairs × 2 = 8. (e) **True** ✅. (f) **False** ❌: NH₄⁺ mein 7 + 4 = 11 proton hain aur **10** electron, 11 nahi.
9. H⁺ ke paas koi electron nahi, to wo ek **khaali jagah** wala bahut bhookha ion hai. Paani ke har H₂O molecule ke O par **lone pair** hai. Isliye H⁺ turant ek H₂O se chipak kar H₃O⁺ ban jaata hai: H⁺(aq) ka asli rup H₃O⁺ hai.
10. Dot-cross: O par 6 electron (o), 2 H ke 2 electron (x). Do pairs (x o), do lone pairs (o o, o o). H⁺ ko ek lone pair (o o) mili: ye **coordinate pair** hai. Donor: **O (H₂O)**. Acceptor: **H⁺**. Teer **O se H⁺ ki taraf**. Donor par lone pairs: **pehle 2, baad mein 1**. Jaanch: O par 3 bond pairs + 1 lone pair = 8 ✅.
11. N par lone pairs: **1 se 0**. N par bond pairs: **3 se 4**. Jo nahi badla: N ke paas kul electron **8** hi hain (3 × 2 + 2 = 8 pehle, 4 × 2 = 8 baad mein) ✅.
12. **Bill** = coordinate bond ke dono electron jo N ne diye (aur banne ki kahani). **Plate** = banne ke baad ka bond: chaaron N–H bond jo ek jaise hain, aur kisi par "udhaar" ka nishaan nahi.
