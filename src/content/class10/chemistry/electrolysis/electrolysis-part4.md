# Topic 4: Electroplating: Dhatu Par Dhatu Ki Chamakdar Parat

Pichhle topic mein humne seekha ki jab ek electrode par kai ions pahunchte hain to kaun pehle discharge hota hai, aur ye bhi ki **active anode** (jaise copper ka anode CuSO₄ mein) anion ko discharge hone hi nahi deta: wo khud ghulta hai, aur ghol ka neela rang aur concentration wahi rehte hain. Wahi ek line is topic ki neev hai. Ab hum us khoj ko **kaam par** lagayenge: kisi cheez par doosri dhatu ki patli, chamakdar, majboot parat bijli se chadhana, yaani **electroplating**. Dekhenge ki cheez cathode kyun banti hai aur anode kyun nahi, silver aur nickel ke cell mein asal mein kya hota hai, ek-ek electron gin kar kitni dhatu jamti hai, cyanide aur chromium ke baths itne khatarnak kyun hain, aur galvanising se plating kaise alag hai.

## Polish-wale ki dukaan mein dull thali

Itwaar ko **Mr. Dhamdhere** apne bhanje **Shivansh** ko ek purani gali ki polish-wale ki dukaan par le gaye. Unke ghar ki steel ki pooja thali ki chamak utar gayi thi: jis nickel ki chamakdar parat se wo kabhi shishe jaisi dikhti thi, wo ghis kar patli ho gayi thi aur thali dhundhli, dagi-dagi dikh rahi thi. Dukaan ke andar bade-bade plastic ke tub rakhe the, bijli ke taar latak rahe the, aur ek kaarigar chimte se thali ko ek tub mein latka raha tha. Kuch der baad usne thali nikaali: wo **chaandi jaisi chamak** rahi thi!

Shivansh ki aankhen phail gayi. "Mama, ye to jaadu hai! Dhundhli thali ek tub mein dali aur nikli to nayi! Kya usne thali par chaandi ka rang pot diya?"

Mr. Dhamdhere hanse. "Rang nahi, dhatu. Ye **electroplating** hai. Wo bijli se thali par nickel ki ek bahut patli parat chadha raha hai, baal se kai guna patli. Par pehle ek baat: tub ke paas mat jaana, aur kuch chhoona mat. Ye ghol harmful hai aur kaarigar poori training ke saath kaam karta hai. Hum doosri taraf se dekhenge."

Shivansh ne ek kadam peeche liya. "Theek hai Mama, par ek sawaal: thali tub mein latki thi aur ek chamakdar patti bhi tub ke andar thi. Dono taar se kyun jude the? Aur thali ko taar ke kis sire se jodna hai, kaise tay hota hai?"

"Wahi sawaal asal mein poora topic hai," Mr. Dhamdhere ne kaha. "Chalo, ek-ek niyam samajhte hain."

## Part A: Electroplating ke chaar niyam

Class 9 ke electricity wale chapter mein tumne ek line yaad ki thi: "jis cheez par parat chadhani ho wo cathode, plating ki dhatu anode." Ab hum **kyun** samajhte hain, ek-ek niyam ke saath.

**Niyam 1: Cheez (article) cathode (−) banti hai.**
Cathode par hamesha **cations gain electrons** karke dhatu banate hain (Topic 2: Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu, Ag⁺ + e⁻ → Ag). Dhatu tabhi jamti hai jahan cations pahunchte aur electron lete hain: yaani cathode par. Isliye jis cheez par parat chahiye usse battery ke **negative** sire se jodte hain.

**Niyam 2: Plating ki dhatu anode (+) banti hai.**
Anode wo electrode hai jahan oxidation hoti hai. Agar anode plating ki apni dhatu ka ho (active anode, Topic 3), to wo ghulta hai: Ag → Ag⁺ + e⁻. Isse ghol mein naye Ag⁺ bante rehte hain jo cathode par jam rahe Ag⁺ ki jagah lete hain.

**Niyam 3: Electrolyte plating dhatu ke namak ka ghol hota hai.**
Silver ke liye silver ka namak (AgNO₃), nickel ke liye nickel ka namak (NiSO₄), copper ke liye copper ka namak (CuSO₄). Isme **wahi dhatu ke ions** hote hain jinhe cathode par jamna hai. Ghol mein kisi aur dhatu ke ions ho to wo bhi jam sakte hain aur parat kharab ho jaati hai.

**Niyam 4: Kam, steady current, aur saaf cheez.**
Current dheema aur ek jaisa rakhte hain, aur cheez ko pehle **saaf, grease-free** kiya jaata hai. Ye dono niyam is topic mein aage "aam galtiyan" mein dikhaye hain.

> **Yaad rakhne ka tareeka:** *cheez = cathode = negative sire se juda; plating ki dhatu = anode = positive sire se juda; ghol = plating dhatu ka namak.*

Electroplating koi alag cheez nahi: ye **wahi electrolysis hai** jo tumne Topic 2 aur 3 mein seekhi, bas ek chalaak trick ke saath. Anode plating dhatu ka rakha jaata hai (taaki wo ghul kar ghol ko bharta rahe), aur cathode wo cheez jise chamkana hai.

## Part B: Silver plating, ek chamach ka safar

Ek **steel ya brass ke chamach** par silver ki parat chadhani hai.

- **Cathode (−):** saaf kiya hua chamach (article).
- **Anode (+):** silver ki patti ya rod.
- **Electrolyte:** silver nitrate (AgNO₃) ka ghol.

> **Safety:** silver nitrate **zehreela aur nuksaandayak** hai: skin par kaale daag chhod deta hai aur aankhon ke liye bahut kharab hai. Isliye ye plating **sirf teacher demo** (low-voltage cell, dastaane, chashma, ventilation) ya **sirf padhne ke liye** hai. Students sirf kaagaz par cell banate hain.

```
               e⁻ -->  [ battery ]  e⁻ -->
          +-----------(+)--------(−)-----------+
          :                                    :
          :                                    :
    +-----:------------------------------------:-----+
    :     :                                    :     :
    :  +--:-----+                       +------:--+  :
    :  :        :                       :         :  :
    :  : ANODE  :   Ag⁺ --->            : CATHODE :  :
    :  : silver :   Ag⁺ --->            : chamach :  :
    :  : patti  :   Ag⁺ --->            : (steel) :  :
    :  :        :                       :         :  :
    :  +--------+                       +---------+  :
    :                                                :
    :         AgNO₃ ka ghol (silver nitrate)         :
    +------------------------------------------------+

 Anode par   : Ag → Ag⁺ + e⁻      (silver ghulta hai)
 Cathode par : Ag⁺ + e⁻ → Ag      (chamach par silver jamta hai)
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Chamach par silver plating ka cell. Silver ka anode (+) ghulta hai, Ag⁺ ions ghol mein cathode (−) ki taraf chalte hain aur chamach par Ag ban kar jamte hain. Taar mein electron anode se battery se hote hue cathode tak jaate hain. Chitra mein 3 Ag⁺ dikhaye gaye hain: har Ag⁺ ko 1 electron chahiye, to 3 electron lagte hain aur 3 Ag atom jamte hain.

Half-equations (atom aur charge dono barabar):
- Anode: Ag → Ag⁺ + e⁻ (charge: 0 = (+1) + (−1))
- Cathode: Ag⁺ + e⁻ → Ag (charge: (+1) + (−1) = 0)

### Silver plating ka asli industrial tareeka (sirf padhne ke liye)

Dukaan aur factory mein silver plating ke liye aksar **silver cyanide ke complex** wale baths chalte hain, kyunki unse parat ek-si, chikni aur chipki hui aati hai. Cyanide **bahut hi zehreela** hota hai: thoda sa bhi jaan le sakta hai, aur acid se milne par aur bhi khatarnak gas banti hai. Isliye aisi plating **sirf licensed factory mein, poore safety system ke saath** hoti hai. Hum ise **sirf naam se** jaante hain: kaagaz par cell banane ke liye silver nitrate ka naam kaafi hai, cyanide bath kabhi ghar, school ya aam dukaan mein nahi.

### Ghol ka concentration kyun nahi badalta?

Ye plating ki sabse sundar baat hai. Topic 3 mein tumne dekha tha ki platinum anode ke saath CuSO₄ mein neela rang ghatta hai. Yahan silver anode hai, aur dekho kya hota hai:

- Cathode par har 1 Ag⁺ jam kar 1 Ag banta hai: ghol mein se 1 Ag⁺ **kam**.
- Anode par har 1 Ag ghul kar 1 Ag⁺ banata hai: ghol mein 1 Ag⁺ **zyada**.
- Dono barabar: ghol ka AgNO₃ **utna ka utna**.

Hamare chitra ke hisaab se: 3 Ag⁺ cathode par jame, 3 Ag atoms anode se ghule: kharch 3 = jama 3, isliye ghol mein koi badlaav nahi. **Silver ka anode patla hota jaata hai aur chamach moti parat leta jaata hai**: silver ek jagah se doosri jagah ghoom gaya, ghol ne bas raasta diya.

## Part C: Nickel aur copper plating, lohe ki chaabi

Ek **lohe ki chaabi** par nickel ki parat chadhani hai: chamak ke liye aur zang se bachane ke liye.

- **Cathode (−):** saaf lohe ki chaabi.
- **Anode (+):** nickel ki patti.
- **Electrolyte:** nickel sulphate (NiSO₄) ka ghol.

> **Safety:** nickel ke namak **nuksaandayak** hain, zyada der chhoone par kuch logon ko skin ki allergy ho jaati hai, aur ghol peena zehreela hai. Isliye ye bhi **sirf teacher demo ya sirf padhne ke liye** hai.

```
               e⁻ -->  [ battery ]  e⁻ -->
          +-----------(+)--------(−)-----------+
          :                                    :
          :                                    :
    +-----:------------------------------------:-----+
    :     :                                    :     :
    :  +--:-----+                       +------:--+  :
    :  :        :                       :         :  :
    :  : ANODE  :   Ni²⁺ --->           : CATHODE :  :
    :  : nickel :   Ni²⁺ --->           :  chaabi :  :
    :  : patti  :                       :  (loha) :  :
    :  :        :                       :         :  :
    :  +--------+                       +---------+  :
    :                                                :
    :        NiSO₄ ka ghol (nickel sulphate)         :
    +------------------------------------------------+

 Anode par   : Ni → Ni²⁺ + 2e⁻     (nickel ghulta hai)
 Cathode par : Ni²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Ni     (chaabi par nickel jamta hai)
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Lohe ki chaabi par nickel plating. Chaabi cathode (−) hai aur nickel ki patti anode (+). Chitra mein 2 Ni²⁺ dikhaye gaye hain: har Ni²⁺ ko 2 electron chahiye, to 4 electron lagte hain aur 2 Ni atom jamte hain. NiSO₄ ka ghol utna ka utna rehta hai, kyunki jitna nickel jamta hai utna anode se ghulta hai.

Half-equations: Anode Ni → Ni²⁺ + 2e⁻ (0 = 2 + (−2)); cathode Ni²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Ni ((+2) + (−2) = 0).

### Copper plating (teacher demo, kaagaz par samajhna)

School lab mein teacher aksar **copper plating** ka demo dikhate hain: copper ka anode, ek brass ki chaabi cathode, aur CuSO₄ ka ghol (**harmful if swallowed**: chakhna ya peena nahi, haath dho lo). Half-equations: anode Cu → Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻, cathode Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu. Dheere se cathode par laal-bhoori copper ki parat dikhti hai.

> **Ek dilchasp baat:** agar tum seedha **lohe** ki cheez bina bijli ke CuSO₄ mein dubo do, to bhi uspar laal copper chipak jaata hai (Chapter 2 ka displacement: Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu). Par ye parat **dheeli, bhurbhuri** hoti hai aur chhil jaati hai. Isliye lohe ki cheez par pakki copper plating ke liye aksar pehle ek patli **neev-parat (strike layer)** chadhayi jaati hai, tab asli plating. Bijli se banayi parat bahut zyada kasi hui aur chipki hoti hai.

Atom aur charge check: Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu mein dono taraf Fe 1, Cu 1, S 1, O 4 ✅.

## Part D: Plating mein konsi dhatu kahan, aur kyun?

Ek table jisme sabki jaankari saath hai. (Cyanide aur chromium(VI) wale baths sirf padhne ke liye hain: dono **zehreele** hain.)

| Cheez | Plating ki dhatu | Anode | Electrolyte (naam) | Kyun plate karte hain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chamach, thali | Silver | Silver | Silver nitrate ya silver cyanide bath (cyanide: sirf factory mein) | Sundar dikhna, sasti dhatu par chaandi ki chamak |
| Lohe ki chaabi, bolt | Nickel | Nickel | Nickel sulphate | Chamak, zang se bachav, sakht parat |
| Electronics ka board, chamach | Copper | Copper | Copper sulphate | Bijli ka achha raasta, neev-parat |
| Tap, car bumper, cycle handle | Chromium (aksar nickel ki neev par) | Aksar lead ka anode | Chromium(VI) bath: **bahut zehreela** | Shishe jaisi chamak, bahut sakht, zang se bachav |
| Khaane ke dabbe (cans) | Tin | Tin | Tin ka namak ka ghol | Khaana loha se bacha rehta hai, zang nahi lagti |
| Imitation gehne, connector | Gold | Aksar anode alag | Gold ke complex baths (aksar cyanide ke): sirf factory | Sone jaisa dikhna, bijli ke contact kharab nahi hote |

> **Dhyan do:** chromium aur gold ke baths mein anode aksar plating dhatu ka **nahi** hota; wahan dhatu ke ions ghol mein alag se milaye jaate hain, isliye "anode ghulta hai" wali baat har bath mein laagu nahi. Isliye is topic mein hum **silver, nickel aur copper** ke simple cells par dhyan dete hain, jahan niyam ekdam saaf hain. Chromium plating mein aksar pehle nickel ki parat aati hai aur uske upar patli chromium ki.

### Plate kyun karte hain? Chaar kaaran

1. **Zang se bachav.** Loha par nickel ya tin ki parat usse hawa-paani se door rakhti hai (barrier, Chapter 3 ka Topic 7).
2. **Sundar dikhna.** Sasti dhatu par chaandi ya sone ki chamak.
3. **Sakht, ghisne-rodhi parat.** Chromium ya nickel ki parat tap aur bumper ko ghisne se bachati hai.
4. **Sasta, par mehnga dikhne wala.** Poora silver ka chamach bahut mehnga hota hai, par plated chamach bahut sasta: parat bahut patli hoti hai (baal se kai guna patli) isliye silver kam lagta hai.

## Part E: Galvanising aur tin plating se tulna

Chapter 3 ke Topic 7 mein tumne dekha tha ki **galvanising** mein loha par zinc ki parat chadhti hai, aur **tin plating** mein tin ki. Ek line mein yaad karo: galvanising ke baad scratch lage to zinc pehle ghulta hai (loha bachta hai), aur tin ke baad scratch lage to loha zyada tez zang khata hai (Fe > Sn). Ab dekho ki **electroplating** aur **hot-dip galvanising** mein farq kya hai.

| Baat | Electroplating | Hot-dip galvanising |
|---|---|---|
| Parat kaise chadhti hai | Bijli se, electrolyte ke ghol mein | Cheez ko **pighle zinc** mein dubo kar |
| Bijli lagti hai? | Haan (low voltage) | Nahi |
| Parat ki motai | Bahut patli, ek-si (kaabu mein) | Aksar moti aur khurdari |
| Kaun-kaun si dhatu? | Silver, nickel, copper, chromium, tin, gold... | Mukhya taur par zinc |
| Aam upyog | Gehne, tap, chamach, chaabi, dabbe | Chaadar, balti, bijli ke khambhe, pipe |

> **Ek line mein farq:** electroplating mein parat **bijli** se chadhti hai, galvanising (hot-dip) mein **pighli dhatu** mein dubone se. Dono mein barrier banta hai, par plating patli aur sundar, galvanising moti aur takatwar.

## Part F: Ek-ek electron ginte hain (kitni dhatu jamegi?)

Is chapter mein hum **sirf electron ginte hain**. Bijli ke charge ko naap kar mass nikalne wale niyam aage ki class mein aayenge. Teen cheezein yaad rakho:

- Har **Ag⁺** ko cathode par **1 electron** chahiye: Ag⁺ + e⁻ → Ag. To 1 mol electrons se 1 mol Ag.
- Har **Cu²⁺** ya **Ni²⁺** ko **2 electrons** chahiye. To 1 mol Cu ya Ni ke liye 2 mol electrons, yaani 1 mol electrons se **aadha mol** Cu ya Ni.
- Anode par utni hi dhatu ghulti hai jitni cathode par jamti hai (jab anode plating dhatu ka ho aur sirf wahi ghule).

Atomic masses (Class 9 ki table): Ag 108, Ni 59, Cu 63.5.

### Solved example 1: Silver, electrons se mass

**Sawaal:** Ek silver plating cell mein 0.025 mol electrons cathode par pahunchte hain. Kitni silver jamegi?

**Hal:**
1. Ag⁺ + e⁻ → Ag: 1 electron se 1 Ag.
2. Ag ke moles = electrons ke moles = **0.025 mol**.
3. Mass = 0.025 × 108 = **2.7 g**.

**Jaanch:** 2.7 ÷ 108 = 0.025 ✅.

---

### Solved example 2: Nickel, aur anode ka kya hua?

**Sawaal:** Nickel plating cell mein 0.30 mol electrons cathode par pahunchte hain. Nickel kitna jamega? Anode ka mass kitna ghatega? NiSO₄ ke ghol ka kya hoga?

**Hal:**
1. Ni²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Ni: 2 electrons se 1 Ni. Ni ke moles = 0.30 ÷ 2 = **0.15 mol**.
2. Mass = 0.15 × 59 = **8.85 g** jamega.
3. Anode par Ni → Ni²⁺ + 2e⁻: wahi 0.15 mol Ni ghulega, yaani anode ka mass **8.85 g ghatega**.
4. Ghol mein Ni²⁺ jitne gaye utne aaye: **koi badlaav nahi**.

**Jaanch:** 0.15 × 59 = 8.85 ✅; anode ke ghulne se aaye electron = 0.15 × 2 = 0.30 mol, wahi jo cathode ko mile ✅.

---

### Solved example 3: Ulta hisaab, copper ki chaabi

**Sawaal:** Brass ki ek chaabi par 1.27 g copper chadhana hai. Kitne mol electrons chahiye?

**Hal:**
1. Cu ke moles = 1.27 ÷ 63.5 = **0.02 mol**.
2. Har Cu ke liye 2 electrons: electrons = 0.02 × 2 = **0.04 mol**.

**Jaanch:** 0.04 ÷ 2 = 0.02 mol Cu = 0.02 × 63.5 = 1.27 g ✅.

---

### Solved example 4: Anode ka ghulna silver mein

**Sawaal:** Silver plating cell mein cathode par 4.32 g silver jamti hai. Kitne mol electrons lage aur silver anode ka mass kitna ghata?

**Hal:**
1. Ag ke moles = 4.32 ÷ 108 = **0.04 mol**, electrons = **0.04 mol** (1 se 1).
2. Anode se bhi utna hi silver ghula: **4.32 g** ghata.

**Jaanch:** 0.04 × 108 = 4.32 ✅. Ghol ka AgNO₃ utna ka utna.

---

### Solved example 5: Do cell ek hi taar mein (series)

**Sawaal:** Ek silver cell aur ek copper cell ek hi taar mein jude hain, to dono mein se **utne hi electrons** guzarte hain. Agar 0.10 mol electrons guzre, to har cathode par kitna jamega?

**Hal:**
1. Silver: 0.10 mol electrons se 0.10 mol Ag. Mass = 0.10 × 108 = **10.8 g**.
2. Copper: 0.10 ÷ 2 = 0.05 mol Cu. Mass = 0.05 × 63.5 = **3.175 g**.

**Jaanch:** silver ka mass copper se zyada kyunki Ag ka ek ion 1 electron leta hai (zyada atom jamte) aur Ag ka atomic mass bhi bhaari hai ✅. 0.05 mol Cu × 2 = 0.10 mol electrons ✅.

## Part G: Sahi aur galat set-up, saamne-saamne

Ab wahi sawaal: kaarigar ne thali ko cathode kyun banaya, anode kyun nahi? Do set-up saath rakhte hain.

```
   SAHI TAREEKA                          GALAT TAREEKA   (cross)

   battery (+) --- silver patti          battery (+) --- chamach
                   = ANODE                               = ANODE         X
   battery (−) --- chamach               battery (−) --- silver patti
                   = CATHODE                             = CATHODE       X
   ghol: AgNO₃                           ghol: AgNO₃

   result: silver chamach par jamti hai  result: chamach hi ghulta hai   X
```

> **Figure 4.3** – Sahi aur galat set-up. Baayein: silver patti anode (+) aur chamach cathode (−): silver chamach par jamti hai. Daayein (cross lagaya hua, galat): chamach anode (+) aur silver patti cathode (−). Dono set-up mein battery aur ghol wahi hain, bas taar ulte jude hain.

**Galat set-up mein kya hota hai (words mein):** chamach anode ban jaata hai, to uski apni dhatu ghulne lagti hai (jaise lohe ka chamach ho to Fe → Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻). Chamach patla aur kharab hota jaata hai, aur ghol mein Fe²⁺ jaise ions aa jaate hain jo ghol ko gandha kar dete hain. Silver patti cathode ban kar uspar silver hi jamti hai: kuch kaam ka nahi. Yaani plating ki jagah **ulta kaam** ho jaata hai: cheez ghulti hai aur plate nahi hoti.

Isliye ek niyam pakka: **jis cheez ko bachana ya chamkana hai usse cathode (−) se jodo.** Yahi wajah hai ki kaarigar ne thali ko taar ke us sire par latkaya jo battery ke negative se juda tha.

## Part H: Achhi plating ke liye teen tips

1. **Cheez saaf aur grease-free ho.** Plating se pehle cheez ko ragad kar, saaf karke, kabhi acid ke halke dip se saaf karte hain. Ungli ki chikni parat bhi parat ko chipakne se rokti hai.
2. **Current kam aur steady ho.** Itna dheema ki ions ko cathode par ek-si jagah par baithne ka samay mile. Bahut tez current se parat khurdari, bhurbhuri aur kamzor aati hai.
3. **Cheez cathode se achhi tarah judi ho.** Cheez taar se pakki juda ho, warna current uske kuch hisse tak pahunchta hi nahi aur wahan parat nahi chadhti.

| Lohe ki chaabi (teacher demo) | Copper |

## Part J: Plating sirf dhatu par? Aur dukaan mein kya hua?

Ek aur dilchasp baat: plating tabhi hoti hai jab cheez **bijli ki chaalak** ho, kyunki cathode ko electron chahiye. Plastic ke tap-handle ya car ke plastic badge par chrome chamak dikhti hai, par pehle plastic par ek **bahut patli chaalak parat** (graphite ya dhatu ki) chadhayi jaati hai, tab usse cathode banakar plating hoti hai.

Ab wapas dukaan par. Kaarigar ne thali ko pehle ragad kar aur dho kar saaf kiya, phir ek halke ghol mein dubo kar grease hataya. Phir thali ko cathode (−) se jod kar nickel ke ghol mein latkaya aur nickel ki patti anode (+) se judi thi. Kuch der baad thali ki chamak wapas aa gayi. Shivansh ne paas aa kar dhyan se dekha. "Mama, ab samajh aaya. Thali cathode thi, isliye nickel uspar jam gaya. Aur nickel ki patti dheere-dheere ghul rahi thi, isliye ghol ka rang aur taakat badli nahi."

"Bilkul," Mr. Dhamdhere ne kaha. "Aur dhyan do ki ye chamak bahut patli parat se hai. Isliye thali ko kabhi tez ragad ya tez powder se mat ragadna, warna parat utar jaayegi aur phir polish-wale ke paas jaana padega."

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: "Cheez ko anode se jodo, kyunki wahi 'plus' hai aur plus yaani jodna."**
   Ek lohe ke chamach ko galti se anode (+) se jod do aur silver ki patti ko cathode (−) se. Chamach apni hi dhatu ghulne lagta hai (Fe → Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻), patla hota jaata hai, aur ghol mein Fe²⁺ aa jaate hain. Silver patti par silver hi jamti hai. Plating nahi hui, ulta cheez kharab hui. Cathode par hi cation dhatu banta hai, isliye cheez hamesha **cathode** hoti hai.

2. **Galti 2: "Current jitna zyada, utni jaldi aur achhi plating."**
   Do kaarigaron ne ek jaisi thali plate ki. Pehle ne tez current lagaya aur 2 minute mein parat chadha di: parat khurdari, kaali-bhoori aur ungli se ragadne par jhad gayi. Doosre ne dheema, steady current lagaya aur kuch der zyada samay diya: parat chamakdar aur kasi hui thi. Ions ko jagah par baithne ka samay chahiye, tez current mein ve bhaag-bhaag kar bhurbhuri parat bana dete hain.

3. **Galti 3: "Plating hote-hote electrolyte halka hota jaata hai, jaise copper sulphate platinum ke saath."**
   Ek cell mein 10 Ag⁺ cathode par jam kar 10 Ag bane. Anode (silver) se bhi 10 Ag ghul kar 10 Ag⁺ bane. Ghol mein Ag⁺ pehle jitne hi: 10 gaye, 10 aaye. Topic 3 ke platinum anode mein CuSO₄ ka neela rang isliye ghatta tha ki anode par oxygen banti thi aur naye Cu²⁺ nahi aate the. **Plating dhatu ka anode** ho to ghol utna ka utna.

4. **Galti 4: "Cheez ko bina saaf kiye plate karna thik hai, parat sab dhak degi."**
   Ek thali par haath ki chikni ungli ka nishaan reh gaya. Plating ke baad wahan parat nahi chipki: daag jaisa gola reh gaya aur kuch hi dino mein parat phool kar chhil gayi. Grease wale hisse par ions jam hi nahi paate. Saaf aur grease-free cheez hi theek se plate hoti hai.

5. **Galti 5: "Plating aur galvanising ek hi cheez hai."**
   Ek chhoti chaabi nickel se plate hui (bijli se, patli parat), aur ek lohe ki balti pighle zinc mein dubo kar galvanise hui (bina bijli, moti parat). Dono loha ko bachati hain, par tareeka, motai aur use alag hain. Galvanising mein scratch par bhi zinc loha ko bachata hai, aur nickel jaisi kam reactive dhatu ki plating scratch par aisa nahi karti.

6. **Galti 6: "Plating sirf dhatu par hi ho sakti hai."**
   Ek plastic ka tap handle. Plastic bijli nahi chalata, to wo seedha cathode nahi bana sakta. Par agar usse pehle ek patli chaalak (graphite ya dhatu) parat se dhak diya jaaye, to wo cathode ban sakta hai aur uspar chrome ki chamak aa jaati hai. Isliye plating ke liye cheez **chaalak** honi chahiye, dhatu ho ya pehle chaalak banayi gayi.

## Quick Recap

- **Electroplating** = bijli se kisi cheez par doosri dhatu ki patli parat chadhana.
- Niyam: **cheez = cathode (−)**; **plating ki dhatu = anode (+)**; **electrolyte = plating dhatu ke namak ka ghol**; kam, steady current; saaf, grease-free cheez.
- Silver: Ag → Ag⁺ + e⁻ (anode), Ag⁺ + e⁻ → Ag (cathode). Nickel aur copper: Ni → Ni²⁺ + 2e⁻ aur Ni²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Ni (Cu bhi aise hi).
- Anode jitna ghulta hai cathode par utna jamta hai, isliye **ghol ka concentration nahi badalta** (platinum anode se farq, Topic 3).
- Electron gino: 1 Ag⁺ = 1 electron; 1 Cu²⁺ ya Ni²⁺ = 2 electrons.
- Cyanide (silver, gold ke baths) aur chromium(VI) baths **bahut zehreele** hain: sirf naam se jaano. Silver nitrate, nickel aur copper ke ghol bhi nuksaandayak hain: demo sirf teacher ka.
- Plate kyun: zang se bachav, sundar dikhna, sakht parat, sasta par mehnga dikhne wala.
- Electroplating (bijli, patli parat) hot-dip galvanising (pighla zinc, moti parat) se alag hai.

## Practice Questions

1. A brass door handle is to be silver plated. Name the cathode, the anode and the electrolyte, and write the half-equation at each electrode.
2. A student connects a plain iron nut to the positive terminal and a nickel strip to the negative terminal of a battery, both dipped in a concentrated nickel sulphate plating solution. Will the nut be nickel plated? What happens to the nut and the nickel strip? Use the rule that an active anode dissolves.
3. In a silver plating cell, 0.075 mol of electrons reach the cathode. Find the moles and the mass of silver deposited. By how much does the silver anode lose mass, and what happens to the concentration of the silver nitrate solution?
4. In a copper plating cell with a copper anode and a copper sulphate solution, 0.50 mol of electrons reach the cathode. Find the mass of copper deposited.
5. A steel bolt gains 2.95 g of nickel during nickel plating. Find the moles of nickel deposited and the moles of electrons that passed.
6. A silver spoon-plating cell deposited 16.2 g of silver. How many moles of electrons were needed?
7. A silver cell and a nickel cell are joined in series, so the same number of electrons passes through both. If 0.20 mol of electrons passes, find the mass deposited in each cell and say in which cell the cathode gains more mass.
8. A copper anode of mass 50.0 g is used to plate a brass tray in copper sulphate solution. After plating, the tray has gained 25.4 g of copper. Assume all the copper that dissolved from the anode was deposited. Find the mass of the anode left, the moles of electrons that passed, and say what happened to the blue colour of the solution.
9. Three cells each have a CuSO₄ solution and an iron cathode. Cell A has a copper anode, cell B has a platinum anode and cell C has a graphite anode. Use the rules of Topic 3. In which cell (a) does the cathode gain mass, (b) does the anode lose mass, (c) is a gas produced at the anode, (d) does the blue colour fade?
10. State two differences between electroplating and hot-dip galvanising. Which one would you choose for a thin, shiny, decorative coat on a key, and why?
11. A plastic toy car part has a chrome-like finish on it. Plastic does not conduct electricity. In two or three sentences, explain how such a part can be electroplated at all.
12. A friend wants to try silver plating a ring at home with silver nitrate and a mobile charger. Give three reasons to say no.
13. Draw and label the cell for nickel plating a steel spoon: mark the anode, cathode, electrolyte, battery terminals, the direction of the electrons in the wire and the direction in which the cations move in the solution.
14. State whether each statement is true or false and correct the false ones: (a) In electroplating the article is the anode. (b) The electrolyte is a salt of the plating metal. (c) In a silver plating cell with a silver anode the concentration of Ag⁺ in the solution changes a lot. (d) Cyanide baths may be handled by anyone as they are plating solutions.

### Answers

1. **Cathode (−):** the brass handle (cleaned). **Anode (+):** a silver rod or strip. **Electrolyte:** silver nitrate solution (AgNO₃). Cathode: Ag⁺ + e⁻ → Ag. Anode: Ag → Ag⁺ + e⁻. Dono equations mein atom aur charge barabar ✅. Safety: AgNO₃ zehreela aur daag chhodne wala, isliye sirf teacher demo ya sirf padhne ke liye.

2. Nut **plate nahi hoga**: wo anode (+) par hai. Anode par nut ki apni dhatu ghulti hai (iron active anode): Fe → Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻, nut patla hota jaata hai. Nickel strip cathode hai, to Ni²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Ni usi nickel strip par nickel jamta hai. Solution mein Fe²⁺ aa jaate hain. Sahi set-up: nut ko cathode aur nickel strip ko anode banao.

3. Ag⁺ + e⁻ → Ag: 1 electron se 1 Ag. Moles = **0.075 mol**, mass = 0.075 × 108 = **8.1 g**. Anode se utni hi silver ghulti hai: **8.1 g ghatega**. AgNO₃ ka concentration **nahi badalta** (jitne Ag⁺ jame utne anode se aaye). Jaanch: 8.1 ÷ 108 = 0.075 ✅.

4. Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu: 2 electrons se 1 Cu. Cu ke moles = 0.50 ÷ 2 = 0.25 mol. Mass = 0.25 × 63.5 = **15.875 g** (lagbhag 15.9 g). Jaanch: 0.25 × 2 = 0.50 ✅.

5. Ni ke moles = 2.95 ÷ 59 = **0.05 mol**. Har Ni ke liye 2 electrons: electrons = 0.05 × 2 = **0.10 mol**. Jaanch: 0.10 ÷ 2 × 59 = 2.95 ✅.

6. Ag ke moles = 16.2 ÷ 108 = 0.15 mol. 1 electron se 1 Ag, to electrons = **0.15 mol**. Jaanch: 0.15 × 108 = 16.2 ✅.

7. Silver cell: 0.20 mol electrons se 0.20 mol Ag, mass = 0.20 × 108 = **21.6 g**. Nickel cell: 0.20 ÷ 2 = 0.10 mol Ni, mass = 0.10 × 59 = **5.9 g**. **Silver cell** ke cathode par zyada mass jamta hai (21.6 g > 5.9 g). Jaanch: 0.10 × 2 = 0.20 mol electrons ✅.

8. Cu ke moles = 25.4 ÷ 63.5 = **0.40 mol**. Utna hi anode se ghula: 25.4 g. Anode bacha = 50.0 − 25.4 = **24.6 g**. Electrons = 0.40 × 2 = **0.80 mol**. Neela rang **utna ka utna rehta hai**, kyunki jitna Cu²⁺ gaya utna anode se aaya. Jaanch: 0.40 × 63.5 = 25.4 ✅; 24.6 + 25.4 = 50.0 ✅.

9. (a) **Teeno cells (A, B, C)** ke cathode par copper jam kar mass badhta hai (Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu). (b) Sirf **A** ka anode ghulta hai (active copper anode: Cu → Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻); platinum aur graphite inert hain. (c) **B aur C** mein anode par gas banti hai: OH⁻ discharge hota hai, 4OH⁻ → 2H₂O + O₂ + 4e⁻, yaani oxygen. A mein koi gas nahi. (d) Blue colour **B aur C** mein fade hota hai (Cu²⁺ kam hota hai, ghol acidic: 2CuSO₄ + 2H₂O → 2Cu + 2H₂SO₄ + O₂); **A** mein nahi. Atom aur charge check: 2CuSO₄ + 2H₂O → 2Cu + 2H₂SO₄ + O₂ mein Cu 2 = 2, S 2 = 2, O 10 = 8 + 2 = 10, H 4 = 4 ✅.

10. Farq: (i) electroplating mein parat bijli se chadhti hai, hot-dip galvanising mein cheez pighle zinc mein dubayi jaati hai (bijli nahi). (ii) Plating ki parat bahut patli aur ek-si, galvanising ki parat moti aur khurdari. Chaabi par patli, chamakdar, sundar parat chahiye, isliye **electroplating** (jaise nickel) behtar.

11. Plastic seedha cathode nahi ban sakta kyunki wo electron nahi pahunchata. Isliye pehle plastic par ek bahut patli chaalak parat (jaise graphite ya dhatu ki) chadhayi jaati hai. Phir wo cathode ban sakta hai, aur usi par chromium ya nickel ki parat jam jaati hai.

12. (i) Silver nitrate zehreela aur daag chhodne wala hai, aur aankhon ke liye bahut kharab hai. (ii) Silver plating ke bahut-se asli baths mein cyanide hota hai jo jaanleva hai, aur bacha hua ghol ghar mein safely phenkna bhi mushkil hai. (iii) Mobile charger mains se judta hai: mains se judi koi bhi cheez kabhi kisi ghol mein nahi jaati; bijli, ghol aur geele haath jaanleva combination hain (Topic 1 ka mains-safety sabak). Students sirf kaagaz par cell banate hain.

13. Cell mein ek beaker NiSO₄ ka ghol, uska anode nickel ki patti battery ke **(+)** se judi, cathode steel ka chamach battery ke **(−)** se juda. Taar mein electrons **anode se battery se hote hue cathode tak** jaate hain. Ghol mein **Ni²⁺ cations cathode ki taraf** chalte hain. Cathode par Ni²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Ni, anode par Ni → Ni²⁺ + 2e⁻. Label sab: anode, cathode, electrolyte, (+), (−), electron arrow, cation arrow.

14. (a) **False** ❌. Article cathode hota hai. (b) **True** ✅. (c) **False** ❌. Silver anode se utna hi Ag⁺ aata hai jitna cathode par jaata hai, isliye concentration lagbhag wahi rehta hai. (d) **False** ❌. Cyanide bahut zehreela hai: sirf licensed factory mein, poori safety ke saath, aur students ke liye sirf naam se.
