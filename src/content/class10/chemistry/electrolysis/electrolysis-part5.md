# Topic 5: Electrolysis Ke Aur Upyog: Refining, Chlor-Alkali aur Anodising

[Topic 4](electrolysis-part4.md) mein humne electroplating seekhi: ek patli parat, ek chhota sa cell. Par electrolysis sirf thali chamkane ka kaam nahi karti. Duniya ke sabse bade chemical kaarkhaane isi par chalte hain: bijli ki taar ke liye copper ko shuddh karna, namak ke ghol se chlorine, hydrogen aur caustic soda banana, aur aluminium ke bartan aur frame ko rangeen aur sakht banana. Pichhle chapter "Metallurgy" mein in sab ke naam aaye the aur kaha gaya tha ki "theory Chapter 4 mein hai". Ab wo theory hamare paas hai: cell, anode-cathode, aur **selective discharge** (Topic 3). Is topic mein hum purane naamon ko naye nazariye se dekhenge: **kyun** Au aur Ag neeche gir jaate hain, **kyun** brine se sodium nahi balki NaOH milta hai, aur **kyun** anodising mein cheez ko cathode nahi, anode banate hain.

## Neeli bottle aur neeli khidki

Sharvari ke school ka pehla din tha, aur uske haath mein ek chamakdar **neeli** water bottle thi. Uske **Mama, Mr. Pimpale**, use apne flat mein ek ghante ke liye le aaye the. Sharvari ne bottle table par rakhi aur achanak kamre ki khidki ki taraf dekha.

"Mama, ye dekho! Meri bottle aur aapki khidki ka frame, dono **ek hi neele shade** ke hain!" Sharvari hansi. "Par paint to bottle par kab ka ghis jaata hai. Meri purani bottle ka paint to ek mahine mein utar gaya tha. Ye rang kyun nahi utarta?"

Mr. Pimpale ne bottle uthayi aur uske neeche ki chhoti si chhaap padhi: *anodised aluminium*. "Bhanji, ye rang paint hai hi nahi. Ye aluminium ki apni **oxide ki parat** hai, jise bijli ki madad se jaan-boojh kar moti banaya gaya, aur phir uske chhote-chhote chhedon mein rang bhar diya gaya. Frame aur bottle, dono ek hi tareeke se bane hain: **electrolysis** se."

"Electrolysis? Wahi jisse thali par chandi chadhti hai?" Sharvari ne poocha.

"Usi ke bhai-behen. Chalo, dekhte hain ki electrolysis aur kya-kya karti hai: namak ke ghol se gas bhi banati hai, copper ko shuddh bhi karti hai, aur aluminium ko armour bhi pehnati hai."

## Part A: Copper ki refining: selective discharge se samjho

[Metallurgy Part 6](../metallurgy/metallurgy-part6.md) mein hum ek line yaad kar chuke hain: ashuddh copper anode, shuddh copper cathode, acidified CuSO₄ ka ghol, aur Au, Ag anode ke neeche gaad ban kar bethte hain. Wahan reactivity se ek line ka kaaran mila tha; ab hum use selective discharge ke niyam se poora samjhte hain: anode par kaun ghulta hai aur cathode par kaun jamta hai. (Copper sulphate ka ghol nuksaandayak hai: ye **sirf padhne ke liye** hai; school mein bhi ye cell teacher ke saath hi.)

### Cell mein kaun-kaun hai?

- **Anode (+):** ashuddh copper ka mota tukda: Cu ke saath thoda Zn, Fe, Ag, Au jaisi gandagi.
- **Cathode (-):** shuddh copper ki patli chadar.
- **Electrolyte:** acidified CuSO₄. Ghol mein ion: Cu²⁺, H⁺ (acid aur paani se), SO₄²⁻, OH⁻ (paani se).

```
   taar mein electron: anode se battery ke raaste cathode ki taraf
         +------------(+)[ battery ](-)-------------+
         :                                          :
         :                                          :
 +-------:------------------------------------------:---------+
 : +-----+-----+                              +-----+-----+   :
 : : ANODE (+) :  Cu²⁺ ------------------>    :CATHODE (-):   :
 : :ashuddh Cu :  Cu²⁺ ------------------>    : shuddh Cu :   :
 : :Zn Fe Ag Au:                              :  (patli   :   :
 : :  (mota)   :  Zn²⁺, Fe²⁺: ghol mein       :  chadar)  :   :
 : +-----------+  hi rehte hain               +-----------+   :
 : . . . . . . . .                                            :
 : ^ anode mud (Ag, Au, Pt)                                   :
 :                                                            :
 :                  CuSO₄ ghol (thoda acid)                   :
 +------------------------------------------------------------+
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Copper ki refining ka cell. Ashuddh copper anode ghulta hai (Cu²⁺ ghol mein jaate hain), shuddh copper cathode par jamta hai. Zn²⁺ aur Fe²⁺ ghol mein hi rehte hain; Ag, Au, Pt anode ke neeche anode mud ban kar bethte hain. Taar mein electron anode se battery ke raaste cathode ki taraf chalte hain, ghol mein ion chalte hain.

### Cathode par kaun chunta hai?

Cathode par positive ion aate hain: Cu²⁺, H⁺, aur anode se ghule hue Zn²⁺, Fe²⁺. [Topic 3](electrolysis-part3.md) ka discharge order yaad karo (sabse aasaan pehle): Ag⁺, Cu²⁺, H⁺, ..., Fe²⁺, Zn²⁺, ... Cu²⁺ in sab mein H⁺, Fe²⁺ aur Zn²⁺ se **pehle** hai, isliye cathode par sirf copper jamta hai:

Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu

Zn²⁺ aur Fe²⁺ discharge order mein neeche hain, isliye wo ghol mein **ghule hue** hi rehte hain. Isi se cathode ka copper lagbhag 99.9% se zyada shuddh milta hai.

### Anode par kaun ghulta hai?

Anode **active** electrode hai (Topic 3 ka teesra factor): yahan SO₄²⁻ ya OH⁻ ko chunne ke bajay **dhatu ke atom khud electron chhodte hain**. Aur electron chhodne mein aage kaun hai? **Zyada reactive dhatu.** Is cell mein bijli itni halki aur dheere rakhi jaati hai ki copper aur usse zyada reactive dhatuein hi ghulti hain:

- Cu → Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ (mukhya kaam)
- Zn → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻ aur Fe → Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻ (copper se zyada reactive: ye bhi ghulte hain)
- Ag aur Au copper se **kam reactive** hain: wo apne electron copper se zyada kass ke pakde rehte hain, isliye ghulte nahi. Anode ke tukde-tukde hote hi ye kan neeche gir jaate hain: **anode mud**.

| Gandagi | Copper se reactivity | Anode par kya hota hai | Aakhir mein kahan |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zn | Zyada reactive | Ghulti hai (Zn²⁺) | Ghol mein hi rehti hai |
| Fe | Zyada reactive | Ghulti hai (Fe²⁺) | Ghol mein hi rehti hai |
| Ag | Kam reactive | Nahi ghulti | Anode mud |
| Au | Kam reactive | Nahi ghulti | Anode mud |
| Pt | Kam reactive | Nahi ghulti | Anode mud |

**Ghol ka kya?** Jitna copper anode se ghulta hai, utna hi cathode par jamta hai, isliye Cu²⁺ ki matra lagbhag wahi rehti hai (Topic 4 ki plating jaisa hi, aur neela rang bana rehta hai). Par Zn²⁺, Fe²⁺ dheere-dheere badhte jaate hain, isliye refinery ghol ko samay-samay par saaf karti ya badalti hai. Aur **anode mud** bekaar nahi: usme sona aur chandi hote hain, jo alag nikaale jaate hain.

### Reactive dhatuein: ghol se kyun nahi?

Copper ke liye aqueous ghol chal gaya. Par Na, Mg aur Al? Topic 3 ka sabak: aqueous ghol mein ye ion discharge order mein H⁺ ke **baad** aate hain. Agar hum Al₂(SO₄)₃ ka ghol electrolyse karein, to cathode par H⁺ pehle chun liya jaata hai aur **hydrogen gas** milti hai, aluminium nahi. Isliye in dhatuon ko nikaalne ke liye **paani hataana padta hai**: pighla hua salt ya oxide.

| Dhatu | Kya electrolyse hota hai | Cathode par | Kahan dekha |
|---|---|---|---|
| Na | Pighla NaCl | Na⁺ + e⁻ → Na | [Metallurgy Part 4](../metallurgy/metallurgy-part4.md) |
| Mg | Pighla MgCl₂ | Mg²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Mg | [Metallurgy Part 4](../metallurgy/metallurgy-part4.md) |
| Al | Alumina Al₂O₃ pighle cryolite mein | Al³⁺ + 3e⁻ → Al | [Metallurgy Part 5](../metallurgy/metallurgy-part5.md) |

Aluminium wale cell mein anode par 2O²⁻ → O₂ + 4e⁻ hota hai: yaani Topic 3 ke rules wahi, bas paani ki jagah pighla salt. **Chetavni:** pighle salt saikdon digree par garam hote hain (aluminium ke cell mein lagbhag 950 °C) aur sirf kaarkhaane mein, training wale log chalate hain. Ye sab **sirf padhne ke liye**.

## Part B: Chlor-alkali process: namak ke ghol se teen cheezein

[Acids, Bases and Salts Part 6](../acids-bases-and-salts/acids-bases-and-salts-part6.md) mein hum ne dekha tha ki brine ke electrolysis se NaOH, Cl₂ aur H₂ milte hain, aur unke uses. Wahan "kya bana" dekha tha; yahan **kyun** aur **kaise** dekhte hain. (Chlorine, sodium hydroxide aur ye sab industrial cell **sirf padhne ke liye** hain; school mein ye electrolysis students nahi karte.)

### Do-ion-ki-ladai ka jawab

Brine mein ye ion hote hain: Na⁺, Cl⁻ (NaCl se) aur H⁺, OH⁻ (paani se). Topic 3 ke rules lagao:

- **Cathode (-):** Na⁺ aur H⁺ mein se H⁺ aasaan chun jaata hai (Na⁺ discharge order mein H⁺ ke baad hai): 2H⁺ + 2e⁻ → H₂. Sodium dhatu **nahi** banta.
- **Anode (+):** Cl⁻ aur OH⁻ mein se, **gaadhe** brine mein Cl⁻ ki sankhya bahut zyada hoti hai, isliye Cl⁻ chun jaata hai (concentration ka factor): 2Cl⁻ → Cl₂ + 2e⁻.
- **Peeche kya bacha?** Na⁺ (jo discharge hua hi nahi) aur OH⁻ (kyunki H⁺ nikal gaye): ghol mein **Na⁺ + OH⁻ = NaOH**.

Dono half-equation aur bacha hua Na⁺ + OH⁻ jodkar poora equation:

2NaCl + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + Cl₂ + H₂

Electron ginti: 2 Cl⁻ ne 2 electron diye (anode), 2 H⁺ ne 2 electron liye (cathode): barabar ✅. Teen products ka mole anupaat **Cl₂ : H₂ : NaOH = 1 : 1 : 2**.

> Ek zaroori note: bahut halke brine mein Cl⁻ kam hota hai aur OH⁻ ka discharge hone lagta hai, isliye plant mein brine bahut gaadha aur shuddh rakha jaata hai.

### Jhilli (membrane) kyun?

Agar teeno products ek hi tub mein mil jaayein to do dikkatein hoti hain:

1. **Cl₂ aur NaOH aapas mein react kar lete hain:** Cl₂ + 2NaOH → NaCl + NaOCl + H₂O (ghar wala bleach). Atom ginti: Na 2 = 2, Cl 2 = 2, O 2 = 2, H 2 = 2 ✅ aur charge dono taraf 0 ✅. Matlab humne jo NaOH banayi thi wo kharch ho gayi aur namak waapas aa gaya.
2. **H₂ aur Cl₂ ka mishran** chingaari ya roshni se phat sakta hai (H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl, bahut tezi se).

Isliye aaj ke **membrane cell** mein beech mein ek patli jhilli lagti hai. Ye jhilli **Na⁺ ko paar jaane deti hai** par Cl⁻ aur gases ko rokti hai. Brine **anode wale hisse** mein jaata hai, thoda paani **cathode wale hisse** mein. Anode par bana Cl₂ upar se alag nikalta hai, cathode par bana H₂ doosri taraf se, aur Na⁺ jhilli paar karke cathode wale hisse mein OH⁻ ke saath NaOH ban jaata hai. (Kuch purane plant mercury wale cell chalate the; mercury zehreela hai, isliye ab ye tareeka kai jagah band ho raha hai.)

```
             Cl₂ gas (bahar)                    H₂ gas (bahar)
                    ^                                  ^
              +-----+---------------------+------------+--------------+
              : ANODE (+)                 : CATHODE (-)               :
              : graphite ya titanium      : steel ya nickel           :
              : 2Cl⁻ → Cl₂ + 2e⁻          : 2H⁺ + 2e⁻ → H₂            :
brine -->     :                           :                           :<-- thoda
(gaadha)      : ghol: Na⁺, Cl⁻, H₂O       : ghol: Na⁺, OH⁻, H₂O       :    paani
              :                           :                           :
              : Na⁺ ------------------>   > Na⁺ paar aa gaye          :
              :                           : Na⁺ + OH⁻ = NaOH          :
              :                           :                           :
              +---------------------------+---------------------------+
              kam gaadha brine bahar        NaOH ghol bahar

              Beech ki ':' rekha jhilli (membrane) hai: sirf Na⁺ paar jaata hai
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Chlor-alkali ka membrane cell (simple chitra). Gaadha brine anode wale hisse mein jaata hai: yahan Cl⁻ se Cl₂ gas banti hai. Cathode wale hisse mein H⁺ se H₂ gas banti hai aur Na⁺ jhilli paar karke OH⁻ se milkar NaOH banata hai. Jhilli Cl⁻ aur gases ko paar nahi jaane deti.

### Teeno products aur unke upyog

| Product | Kahan banta hai | Kuch upyog |
|---|---|---|
| Chlorine (Cl₂) | Anode (+) par | Paani ko germ-free karna, PVC pipe aur plastic, bleaching powder ([Part 6 ka Part B](../acids-bases-and-salts/acids-bases-and-salts-part6.md)), HCl aur dawaiyon ka raw material, paper ka bleaching |
| Hydrogen (H₂) | Cathode (-) par | Ammonia (khaad), vanaspati ghee jaisi hydrogenation, HCl banane mein (H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl), fuel; [Class 9 ka hydrogen chapter](../../../class9/chemistry/hydrogen/hydrogen-part3.md) ise by-product bata chuka hai |
| Sodium hydroxide (NaOH) | Cathode wale ghol mein | Sabun aur detergent, kaagaz ki pulp, rayon, aluminium ke ore ka shuddhikaran ([Baeyer process](../metallurgy/metallurgy-part5.md)), tel-grease saaf karna |

### Chlorine aur NaOH: kaise sambhalte hain (sirf padhne ke liye)

- **Chlorine** hari-peeli, teekhi gandh wali, **zehreeli** gas hai. Ye hawa se lagbhag 2.5 guna bhaari hai (71 ÷ 29), isliye leak hone par neeche ki taraf bhar jaati hai. Plant mein leak detector hote hain, workers **hawa ke virudh** aur **oonchai** ki taraf bhaagte hain, aur gas ko pressure mein steel ke cylinder mein rakha jaata hai. Ghar mein bleach ko kabhi toilet cleaner (acid) ya ammonia wale cleaner ke saath mat milao: zehreeli gas (jaise chlorine) nikal sakti hai.
- **Sodium hydroxide** (caustic soda) gaadha ghol skin ko jala deta hai; ise sirf chashme, dastane aur teacher ya trained log sambhalte hain.
- **Hydrogen** jalne wali gas hai, isliye plant mein ise chlorine se aur chingaari se door rakhte hain.

## Part C: Anodising: aluminium ki apni parat ko moti karna

### Pehle ek yaad

Aluminium hawa mein khud ek bahut patli (kuch nanometre ki) **Al₂O₃** ki parat bana leta hai ([Metals and Non-metals](../metals-and-non-metals/metals-and-non-metals-part2.md)); ye parat andar ke dhatu ko aage ghisne se rokti hai. Anodising mein hum is parat ko **jaan-boojh kar kai guna moti** banate hain.

### Setup: cheez anode, cathode nahi

Plating mein cheez **cathode** thi aur nayi dhatu uspar jam rahi thi. Anodising mein **ulta** hai: aluminium ki cheez **anode (+)** hoti hai, kyunki is baar hamein uske **apne atomon se oxide** banana hai, ghol se koi nayi dhatu jamaani nahi.

- Anode: aluminium ki cheez (bottle ka dhancha, frame).
- Cathode: ek aluminium ya doosri sthir (inert) dhatu ki patti.
- Electrolyte: **dilute sulphuric acid** (kai factory mein lagbhag 15 se 20 percent). Acid aur bijli ka ye cell **sirf factory** ya teacher ke saath ka kaam hai: **students ise kabhi ghar ya school mein nahi karte.**

### Electrode par kya hota hai?

- **Cathode:** 6H⁺ + 6e⁻ → 3H₂ (hydrogen gas ke bubble).
- **Anode:** aam anode par OH⁻ se oxygen banti (4OH⁻ → 2H₂O + O₂ + 4e⁻), par yahan jo oxygen bani wo **gas banke ud nahi jaati**: wo aluminium ki surface se turant jud jaati hai. Simple roop mein:

2Al + 3H₂O → Al₂O₃ + 6H⁺ + 6e⁻

Dono jodo: 2Al + 3H₂O → Al₂O₃ + 3H₂. Atom ginti: Al 2 = 2, H 6 = 6, O 3 = 3 ✅. Charge: anode par 0 = 0 + 6 − 6 ✅.

Yaani **oxide parat aluminium ke apne atomon se** bani (kuch hissa dhatu ke andar, kuch bahar ki taraf badhta hai), isliye wo dhatu ka hi hissa hai, upar se lagayi hui parat nahi. Is wajah se wo paint ki tarah **chhilti nahi**.

```
  PEHLE (kachcha aluminium)             BAAD MEIN (anodised aluminium)
  hawa                                  hawa
                                        ------------------------------------
  --------------------------------         :   :   :   :   :   :   :   :
  ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~         :   :   :   :   :   :   :   :
  ================================         :   :   :   :   :   :   :   :
                                           :   :   :   :   :   :   :   :
                                           :   :   :   :   :   :   :   :
  ALUMINIUM (dhatu)                     ....................................
                                        ====================================
                                        ALUMINIUM (dhatu)
                                        (ab thoda patla)
  --------------------------------      ------------------------------------
  ~~~ = oxide: bahut patli (kuch nm)
                                        oxide: moti (kai µm); chhed (:) mein rang
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Anodising se pehle aur baad ka cross-section (scale se nahi). Pehle: bahut patli natural oxide parat. Baad mein: moti oxide parat jisme honeycomb jaise chhote chhed (:) hain; neeche patli sthir parat aur uske neeche thoda patla hua aluminium.

### Rang aur sealing

Moti oxide parat mein bahut chhote chhed hote hain. Garam dye ke ghol mein cheez dubone par ye chhed rang soakh lete hain, aur phir cheez ko **garam paani** mein rakhne se oxide phoolkar chhedon ko band kar deta hai (**sealing**). Isliye rang parat ke **andar** hai: ghisne se nahi utarta. Ye rang aur sealing ke kaam bhi **sirf factory** mein hote hain.

### Anodised parat ke gun

| Gun | Kaaran | Upyog |
|---|---|---|
| Sakht aur ghisne se bachav | Al₂O₃ dhatu se kai guna sakht hai | Hard-anodised kadhai aur cookware |
| Zang aur corrosion se bachav | Moti sthir parat | Khidki ke frame, bahar ki fittings |
| Bijli ka kharab conductor | Al₂O₃ insulator hai | Isliye anodised surface par electrical contact ke liye parat hatani padti hai |
| Rang | Chhedon mein dye | Neeli bottle, mobile aur laptop ke dhanche |

Aluminium ki cheez ki parat patli rehti hai: lagbhag **5 se 25 micrometre** (ek baal ki motai se kaafi kam). Isliye anodising se cheez ka size bahut nahi badalta.

### Plating aur anodising ka farq

| Sawaal | Electroplating (Topic 4) | Anodising |
|---|---|---|
| Cheez kaun sa electrode? | Cathode | Anode |
| Nayi parat kisse banti? | Ghol ke ion se nayi dhatu | Cheez ke apne aluminium ke atom se oxide |
| Parat kya hai? | Dhatu | Oxide (insulator) |
| Electrolyte | Plating dhatu ka salt | Dilute sulphuric acid |

## Part D: Paani se hydrogen, aur electrolysis ki bijli ka bill

### Green hydrogen: Class 9 ka waada

[Class 9 ke hydrogen chapter](../../../class9/chemistry/hydrogen/hydrogen-part3.md) mein green hydrogen ka naam aaya tha: solar ya wind ki bijli se paani ko H₂ aur O₂ mein todna. Ab uske electrodes ka hisaab hamare paas hai: cathode par 2H⁺ + 2e⁻ → H₂ aur anode par 4OH⁻ → 2H₂O + O₂ + 4e⁻; overall 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂ (volume mein H₂ : O₂ = 2 : 1). Bade electrolyser mein paani mein bijli chalane ke liye alkali (jaise KOH) ka ghol ya ek khaas membrane electrolyte ka kaam karta hai. Chlor-alkali mein bhi hydrogen milti hai, par wahan **by-product** hai: asli lakshya Cl₂ aur NaOH hain.

### Bijli ka bill

Electrolysis mein ion ko **zabardasti** electrode par chunna padta hai, isliye bijli bahut lagti hai. Neeche lagbhag wale aankde (plant ke hisaab se badalte hain):

| Process | Bijli ki khapat (lagbhag) | Kyun? |
|---|---|---|
| Copper ki refining | 0.3 kWh har kg copper | Dhatu sirf ek electrode se doosre par jaati hai: koi pakka compound todna nahi |
| Chlor-alkali | 2500 kWh har tonne chlorine | NaCl aur paani ke bandhan todne padte hain |
| Aluminium nikaalna ([Metallurgy Part 5](../metallurgy/metallurgy-part5.md)) | 13 se 15 kWh har kg | Bahut pakka Al₂O₃ todna, aur pighlaaye rakhne ki garmi |
| Paani se hydrogen | 50 se 55 kWh har kg hydrogen | Paani ke pakke bandhan todna |

Isliye aise plant **sasti aur lagatar milne wali bijli** ke paas lagte hain, aur green hydrogen jaise kaam tab hi sasta hoga jab saaf bijli sasti ho.

### Metallic aur electrolytic conduction: ek saath

[Topic 1](electrolysis.md) mein humne do tarah ki conduction dekhi thi. Ab dono ek hi cell mein dekho:

| Baat | Metallic conduction (taar) | Electrolytic conduction (ghol) |
|---|---|---|
| Kaun bijli le jaata hai | Free electrons | Ions (positive aur negative) |
| Chemical change | Koi nahi | Electrode par hota hai |
| Garmi badhane par | Conduction ghatti hai | Aksar badhti hai |
| Cell mein kahan | Anode se battery ke raaste cathode tak taar mein | Anode aur cathode ke beech ghol mein |

**Electron ghol ke andar nahi tairte.** Anode par ion ya dhatu ke atom electron chhodkar taar mein bhejte hain, aur cathode par taar se electron lekar ion discharge hote hain. Ghol mein ion **bijli ka raasta poora** karte hain.

## Part E: Solved examples

### Example 1. Chlor-alkali plant ka hisaab

**Sawaal:** Ek plant mein **17.55 kg** NaCl (brine mein) electrolyse hota hai. 2NaCl + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + Cl₂ + H₂. Kitna NaOH, Cl₂ aur H₂ banega? (Na 23, Cl 35.5, O 16, H 1.)

**Hal:**
- NaCl ka molar mass = 23 + 35.5 = 58.5 g/mol. 17550 g ÷ 58.5 = **300 mol**.
- Anupaat NaCl : NaOH : Cl₂ : H₂ = 2 : 2 : 1 : 1. To NaOH = 300 mol, Cl₂ = 150 mol, H₂ = 150 mol.
- NaOH = 300 × 40 = 12000 g = **12.0 kg**.
- Cl₂ = 150 × 71 = 10650 g = **10.65 kg**.
- H₂ = 150 × 2 = 300 g = **0.30 kg**.

**Jaanch:** Paani 300 mol × 18 = 5400 g = 5.4 kg. Reactants 17.55 + 5.4 = 22.95 kg, products 12.0 + 10.65 + 0.30 = 22.95 kg ✅. Electron: 300 Cl⁻ ne 300 electron diye, 300 H⁺ ne 300 electron liye ✅.

### Example 2. Copper ki refining: electron ginti

**Sawaal:** Ek refinery tank ke cathode par **38.1 kg** shuddh copper jama hua. (a) Kitne mole Cu? (b) Kitne mole electron cathode ne liye? (c) Agar ghol mein Cu²⁺ ki matra badli nahi, to anode se kitna copper ghula?

**Hal:**
- (a) 38100 g ÷ 63.5 = **600 mol**.
- (b) Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu: har Cu ke liye 2 electron, to 600 × 2 = **1200 mol electron**.
- (c) Cu²⁺ ki matra nahi badli, to jitna jama utna hi ghula: **600 mol = 38.1 kg**.

**Jaanch:** anode par Cu → Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ mein bhi 600 mol Cu se 1200 mol electron nikle, jo cathode par laute ✅. 600 × 63.5 = 38100 g ✅.

### Example 3. Gandagi kahan gayi?

**Sawaal:** Ek anode ka mass **20.0 kg** hai: copper 19.4 kg, zinc aur iron milakar 0.4 kg, aur silver aur gold milakar 0.2 kg. Maan lo poora copper cathode par jam jaata hai. Cathode par kitna copper, anode mud kitna, aur ghol mein kitni gandagi gayi? Copper ka percent kitna?

**Hal:**
- Cathode par copper = **19.4 kg**.
- Anode mud (Ag, Au: kam reactive) = **0.2 kg**.
- Ghol mein (Zn²⁺, Fe²⁺: zyada reactive) = **0.4 kg**.
- Copper ka percent = 19.4 ÷ 20.0 × 100 = **97.0%**.

**Jaanch:** 19.4 + 0.4 + 0.2 = 20.0 kg ✅.

### Example 4. Anodising: kitni oxide, kitne electron?

**Sawaal:** Anodising mein **4.05 g** aluminium oxide mein badal jaata hai (2Al + 3H₂O → Al₂O₃ + 6H⁺ + 6e⁻). Kitna Al₂O₃ banega, kitne electron nikle, aur oxide ka mass aluminium se zyada kyun? (Al 27, O 16.)

**Hal:**
- Al ke mole = 4.05 ÷ 27 = **0.15 mol**.
- Al₂O₃ ka molar mass = 2 × 27 + 3 × 16 = 102 g/mol. 2Al se 1 Al₂O₃, to 0.075 mol × 102 = **7.65 g**.
- Har Al se 3 electron: 0.15 × 3 = **0.45 mol electron**.
- Oxide mass zyada: 7.65 − 4.05 = **3.60 g** oxygen aluminium se judi (0.075 × 48 = 3.6 ✅).

**Jaanch:** 4.05 + 3.60 = 7.65 g ✅. Matlab bottle ki surface par oxide ka mass shuruaati aluminium se zyada hai, kyunki paani ka oxygen dhatu ka hissa ban gaya.

### Example 5. Chlor-alkali ka bijli bill

**Sawaal:** Ek plant roz **40 tonne** chlorine banata hai. Maan lo 1 tonne chlorine mein **2500 kWh** bijli lagti hai aur bijli **Rs 6 per kWh** ki hai. Roz ki bijli aur kharch kitna? Saath mein kitna NaOH banta hai? (Cl₂ 71, NaOH 40.)

**Hal:**
- Bijli = 40 × 2500 = **100000 kWh**.
- Kharch = 100000 × 6 = **Rs 6,00,000**.
- 40 t Cl₂ = 40,000,000 g ÷ 71 = lagbhag 5,63,400 mol. NaOH = Cl₂ ka double mole = lagbhag 11,26,800 mol; × 40 g = lagbhag 4,50,70,000 g = **lagbhag 45.1 tonne**.

**Jaanch:** Anupaat NaOH : Cl₂ = 80 : 71 mass mein, 40 × 80 ÷ 71 = 45.07 ✅ (lagbhag 45.1). Isi liye plant ko NaOH ke bhi kharidaar chahiye, warna bekaar jaayegi.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: "Anodising bhi plating hai: bottle par kisi dhatu ki parat chadhti hai."**
   Example 4 dekho: oxide **aluminium ke apne atomon** se bani, aur anode par bani (2Al + 3H₂O → Al₂O₃ + 6H⁺ + 6e⁻). Agar bottle cathode hoti to wahan sirf H₂ bubbles aate (6H⁺ + 6e⁻ → 3H₂) aur koi oxide parat nahi banti. Isliye plating mein cheez cathode, anodising mein anode.
2. **Galti 2: "Brine ke electrolysis se sodium dhatu banta hai."**
   Cathode par Na⁺ aur H⁺ dono hain. Topic 3 ke order mein H⁺ pehle chunta hai: 2H⁺ + 2e⁻ → H₂. Agar kisi tarah Na bana bhi, to wo turant paani se react karke NaOH aur H₂ de deta (2Na + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + H₂; Na 2 = 2, H 4 = 4, O 2 = 2 ✅). Isliye sodium dhatu brine se nahi, **pighle NaCl** se milta hai (Metallurgy Part 4).
3. **Galti 3: "Copper refining mein saari gandagi anode ke neeche mud ban jaati hai."**
   Example 3 mein 0.4 kg gandagi (Zn aur Fe) mud mein nahi gayi, wo **ghol mein** chali gayi, aur sirf 0.2 kg (Ag, Au) mud bani. Zyada reactive dhatuein ghulti hain, kam reactive gir jaati hain. Aur cathode par Zn²⁺ nahi jamta kyunki discharge order mein Cu²⁺ pehle hai.
4. **Galti 4: "Chlor-alkali cell mein jhilli ki zaroorat nahi, sab milte rehne do."**
   Bina jhilli ke Cl₂ + 2NaOH → NaCl + NaOCl + H₂O ho jaata hai: banai hui NaOH kharch aur namak wapas. Dono gases mil jaayein to H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl chingaari se phat bhi sakta hai. Isliye jhilli sirf Na⁺ ko paar jaane deti hai.
5. **Galti 5: "Anodised rang paint hai jo pakka ho gaya."**
   Paint upar baithi parat hai. Anodised rang **oxide parat ke chhedon ke andar** hai aur chhed garam paani se seal ho jaate hain. Parat dhatu ka hissa hai, upar se chipki hui nahi, isliye ghisne par rang nahi utarta (par bahut gehra khoncha dhatu tak pahunch sakta hai).

## Quick Recap

- **Copper refining:** ashuddh Cu anode (+), shuddh Cu cathode (-), acidified CuSO₄. Anode: Cu → Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻; cathode: Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu (Cu²⁺ discharge order mein H⁺, Fe²⁺, Zn²⁺ se pehle). Zyada reactive Zn, Fe ghulte hain aur ghol mein rehte hain; kam reactive Ag, Au, Pt **anode mud** banate hain. Ghol ka Cu²⁺ lagbhag constant, neela rang bana rehta hai.
- Na, Mg, Al aqueous ghol se nahi, **pighle salt ya oxide** se milte hain, kyunki ghol mein H⁺ pehle chunta hai.
- **Chlor-alkali:** brine ke electrolysis se anode par Cl₂ (2Cl⁻ → Cl₂ + 2e⁻), cathode par H₂ (2H⁺ + 2e⁻ → H₂), ghol mein NaOH. 2NaCl + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + Cl₂ + H₂; Cl₂ : H₂ : NaOH = 1 : 1 : 2 mole. **Membrane** sirf Na⁺ ko paar jaane deti hai taaki Cl₂, NaOH aur H₂ na milein.
- Chlorine zehreeli aur hawa se bhaari hai; NaOH jalata hai; H₂ jalti hai: sab **sirf padhne ke liye**.
- **Anodising:** aluminium ki cheez **anode** (dilute sulphuric acid mein); oxide parat cheez ke apne atom se banti hai (2Al + 3H₂O → Al₂O₃ + 6H⁺ + 6e⁻); chhedon mein rang, garam paani se sealing. Parat sakht, corrosion se bachati hai, insulator hai.
- Electrolysis mein **bahut bijli** lagti hai (copper refining kam, aluminium aur hydrogen zyada). Green hydrogen: Class 9 ka waada, saaf bijli se.
- Electron taar mein chalte hain, ion ghol mein: **electron ghol mein nahi tairte.**
