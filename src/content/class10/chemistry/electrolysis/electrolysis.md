# Topic 1: Electrolyte aur Non-electrolyte: Bijli Kaun Chalata Hai?

Chapter 3 mein hum ne bahut baar kaha: "ab yahan **electrolysis** hoti hai". Sodium, magnesium aur aluminium aise hi ore se nikle, aur copper aise hi refine hua. Par ek sawaal hum ne har baar aage ke liye tala: bijli ek **liquid** ke andar se kaise guzarti hai? Taar ke andar to ho jaata hai, par liquid mein? Kuch liquids bijli chalate hain aur kuch nahi, aur jo chalate hain unmein bhi **kuch bahut achhi tarah** aur kuch **bas thodi si**. Is chapter ke baaki topics (electrolysis kaise hoti hai, kaun sa ion pehle aata hai, plating, factory ke upyog, aur predict karna) tabhi samajh aayenge jab pehle ye pata ho ki **liquid mein bijli ko le jaane wala kaun hai**. Aaj yahi seedhi aur kaam ki baat: **kaun si cheez liquid ko "bijli ka raasta" bana deti hai?**

## Geyser ki switch aur ek sawaal

Mr. Kolhatkar ke bathroom mein ek electric geyser laga hai. Uski switch ke paas ek chhota sa label chipka hai: **"geele haath se mat chhuo"**. Garmiyon ki chhutti mein unki bhanji Aditri aayi hui thi. Nahane jaate waqt usne wo label dekha aur baahar aakar poochha, "Mama, ye label to har bathroom mein hota hai. Par paani aur bijli ki aisi dushmani kyun? School mein to padha tha ki **saaf paani bijli nahi chalata**. Phir ye dar kis baat ka?"

Mr. Kolhatkar ne muskura kar kaha, "Tumne sahi padha tha, aur ye label bhi sahi hai. Dono ek saath sach hain. Farak ek shabd ka hai: **saaf**. Nal ka paani 'saaf' dikhta hai, par wo **shuddh paani nahi** hota. Usme zameen aur pipe se aaye namak aur minerals ghule hote hain. Aur wahi ghule hue namak paani ko **bijli ka raasta** bana dete hain." Aditri ne bhaunhein uthayin, "To paani nahi, uske andar ka namak bijli chalata hai?" "Lagbhag yahi," Mr. Kolhatkar bole. "Isi baat ka naam hai **electrolyte**. Aaj hum use samjhenge. Aur ek baat saaf: ye label ek **chetawani** hai, koi experiment nahi. Bijli ke saath paani ka koi bhi test kabhi nahi karna, na ghar mein, na bahar."

Aditri ne ek alag sawaal poocha, "Mama, to kya Class 9 ka wo chhota tester bhi khatarnak hai?" "Nahi," Mr. Kolhatkar ne kaha. "Wo sirf **do AA cells (3 volt)** se chalta hai, aur uska **ghar ki bijli (mains) se koi lena-dena nahi**. Wahi sahi, bade ki nigrani mein karne wali activity hai. Chalo, pehle zaroori shabd, phir tester par ghar ke kuch naye liquids."

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Electrolyte** | Wo padarth jo pighla hone par ya paani mein ghola jaane par bijli chalata hai, kyunki usme free ions hote hain | Acids, alkalis, salts |
| **Non-electrolyte** | Wo padarth jo pighla ho ya ghola ho, bijli nahi chalata, kyunki usme ions nahi hote | Cheeni, glucose, alcohol, urea |
| **Ion** | Charge wala kan (atom ya atoms ka group jisne electron diye ya liye hain) | Na⁺, Cl⁻, SO₄²⁻ |
| **Cation, anion** | Positive charge wala ion, negative charge wala ion | Na⁺ cation; Cl⁻ anion |
| **Free ions** | Ions jo ghol ya pighle liquid mein idhar-udhar chalne ke liye azaad hain | Namak ke ghol mein Na⁺, Cl⁻ |
| **Strong electrolyte** | Wo electrolyte jo ghol mein **lagbhag poora** ions mein toot jaata hai | HCl, NaOH, NaCl |
| **Weak electrolyte** | Wo electrolyte jiske **bahut thode** molecules hi ions mein tootte hain, baaki molecule ki tarah rehte hain | CH₃COOH, H₂CO₃, NH₄OH |
| **Ionisation** | Covalent molecule ka paani mein ions mein toot jaana (ions pehle se nahi the, bane) | HCl → H⁺ + Cl⁻ |
| **Dissociation** | Ionic compound ke ions ka alag (azaad) ho jaana (ions pehle se lattice mein the) | NaCl → Na⁺ + Cl⁻ |
| **Metallic conduction** | Dhatu mein free electrons ke behne se bijli ka chalna | Taamba ka taar |
| **Electrolytic conduction** | Ghol ya pighle liquid mein free ions ke behne se bijli ka chalna | Namak ka ghol |

## Part A: Bijli chalane ke liye kya chahiye?

Class 9 Electricity mein tumne padha tha ki bijli ka matlab **charge ka behna** hai ([electricity.md](../../../class9/physics/electricity/electricity.md)). Aur Class 9 Chemical Bonding mein dekha tha ki ionic compound solid mein bijli nahi chalata par pighla hone par ya paani mein ghule hone par chalata hai ([chemical-bonding-part4.md](../../../class9/chemistry/chemical-bonding/chemical-bonding-part4.md)). Wo kahani yahan dobara nahi sunayenge. Bas ek line: **charge ko behne ke liye ya to free electron chahiye (dhatu ke taar mein) ya free ion (liquid mein).** Ab hum aage badhte hain: **ek poori list** banayenge ki kaun se padarth is gun wale hain aur kaun se nahi, aur dekhenge ki ye "kitni achhi tarah" chalate hain.

Is poori baat ka ek-line formula:

> **Kisi liquid ke bijli chalane ki kshamta = us mein maujood free ions ki ginti (aur unka charge).**

Jahan free ions nahi, wahan liquid circuit ko poora nahi karta. Jahan thode, wahan current thoda. Jahan bahut, wahan current zyada.

## Part B: Activity 1.1: Ghar ke liquids par tester

> **Ghar par adult ke saath** (koi bada paas mein rahe). Ye activity sirf 2 AA cells (3 volt) se hogi. Ghar ki bijli (mains), socket ya plug ka is activity mein kabhi, kisi bhi roop mein, kaam nahi.**

**Safety (zaroor padho):**
- Tester mein **sirf 2 AA cells (kul 3 V)**, ek chhoti LED aur uske saath **series mein ek 220 ohm ka resistor** hoga. Resistor LED ko bachata hai (Example 5 mein dekhenge).
- **Kuch bhi chakhna nahi** (nimbu aur sirka bhi nahi: sirf dekhna aur LED dekhna hai). Har glass par label lagao.
- **Koi jalne wala liquid (kerosene, petrol, spirit) nahi.** Aur koi **acid, alkali, bleach ya safai wala liquid** bhi nahi: sirf wahi liquids jo neeche likhe hain.
- Taar ke dono khule sire (probes) aapas mein **chhune nahi chahiye**, aur har liquid ke baad unhe **paani se dho kar poonchh lo**.
- Taar ko liquid mein **sirf kuch second** ke liye dubao. Agar taar ke sire par chhote bubbles dikhein, to chinta nahi, par ise zyada der mat chalao (iska raaz agle topic mein).
- Kaam ke baad haath dho lo.

**Samaan:** 2 AA cells (holder ke saath), ek chhoti LED, ek 220 ohm ka resistor, do taar, 7 glass (ya katori), distilled paani (agar na mile to chhod do), nal ka paani, nimbu ka ras, sirka, soda water (naya, band bottle se), baking soda, cheeni, chamach, label.

**Taiyari:** (1) nimbu: **ek chamach ras aadha glass paani mein**; (2) sirka: **ek chamach sirka aadha glass paani mein**; (3) soda water: **taaza, bina milaaye**, bas aadha glass; (4) baking soda: **ek chamach aadha glass paani mein**, ghol kar; (5) cheeni ka syrup: **chaar chamach cheeni aadha glass paani mein**, ghol kar; (6) nal ka paani aadha glass; (7) distilled paani aadha glass. (Matra ek jaisi rakhne ki koshish karo, warna tulna theek nahi hogi.)

A aur B ko jis liquid mein dubaate hain, wahi circuit ka bacha hua hissa ban jaata hai. Taar mein electron chalte hain, liquid ke andar ions chalte hain. ## Part C: Teen tarah ke padarth

Natijon se teen tarah ke padarth saamne aaye:

1. **Strong electrolyte:** liquid mein bahut free ions, LED tez. Baking soda ka ghol (ek salt, NaHCO₃) aisa hi tha.
2. **Weak electrolyte:** thode free ions, LED halki. Nimbu, sirka, soda water (citric acid, CH₃COOH, H₂CO₃).
3. **Non-electrolyte:** koi free ion nahi, LED nahi. Cheeni, distilled paani.

### Strong aur weak: ek hi baat: "kitne molecule toote?"

Ye farak **ek hi sawaal** se aata hai: ghulne par padarth ka **kitna hissa** ions mein tootta hai?

- **Strong electrolyte:** lagbhag **poora** hissa ions mein toot jaata hai. Ghol mein almost koi "poora molecule" nahi bachta. Example: HCl, H₂SO₄, HNO₃ (strong acids, Chapter 1 se yaad karo), NaOH, KOH (strong alkalis), NaCl, CuSO₄ (salts).
- **Weak electrolyte:** sirf **bahut thode** molecules tootte hain, baaki **molecule hi rehte hain**. Example: CH₃COOH, H₂CO₃, NH₄OH. Inke saath aksar ek **aage-peeche wala** tir (⇌) likha jaata hai: kuch toot-te hain, kuch phir jud jaate hain, aur ghol mein ek santulan rehta hai.

Ye **concentrated ya dilute hone ka farak nahi** hai. Dilute HCl bhi strong electrolyte hai (jo bhi toota, lagbhag poora toota), aur concentrated CH₃COOH bhi weak hi hai (bahut kam toota). Strong ya weak hona **padarth ka apna swabhav** hai.

```
   Strong electrolyte                   Weak electrolyte
   (10 molecule HCl)                    (10 molecule CH₃COOH = HAc)

   H⁺  Cl⁻  H⁺  Cl⁻                    HAc  HAc  HAc
   Cl⁻ H⁺   Cl⁻ H⁺                     HAc  HAc  HAc
   H⁺  Cl⁻  H⁺  Cl⁻                    HAc  HAc  HAc
   Cl⁻ H⁺   Cl⁻ H⁺                     H⁺   Ac⁻
   H⁺  Cl⁻  H⁺  Cl⁻

   10 H⁺ + 10 Cl⁻ = 20 ions            9 HAc + 1 H⁺ + 1 Ac⁻ = 2 ions
   Poora toota: LED tez                Bahut thoda toota: LED halki
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Dono beakers mein 10-10 molecule dale gaye. **Strong:** saare 10 HCl toot gaye: 10 H⁺ aur 10 Cl⁻ = 20 free ions. **Weak:** 10 mein se sirf 1 CH₃COOH toota: 1 H⁺ aur 1 Ac⁻ (Ac = CH₃COO) mile, baaki 9 poore molecule (HAc) bache. Sketch hai: asli sirke jaise ghol mein 100 molecule mein se **lagbhag 1 ya usse bhi kam** toota hota hai; yahan dikhane ke liye 10 mein se 1 liya hai. Paani ke molecule nahi banaye.

### Ionisation aur dissociation: ions kahan se aaye?

Ye ek shabd ka farak hai, par dhyan se samjho.

**Dissociation (ionic compound):** NaCl jaise ionic compound mein ions **pehle se** hain, lattice mein jakde hue (Class 9). Paani lattice ko todta hai aur ions ko **azaad** kar deta hai. Ions bane nahi, bas free hue.

NaCl → Na⁺ + Cl⁻  (dissociation)  
NaOH → Na⁺ + OH⁻  
CuSO₄ → Cu²⁺ + SO₄²⁻  

**Ionisation (covalent compound):** HCl ek covalent molecule hai (H aur Cl electron baante hain). Isme **ions pehle se nahi hain**. Paani ke molecule HCl ko itna kheenchte hain ki wo **ions mein toot jaata hai**: ions **ghol mein bante hain**. Ye wo "apwaad" hai jo Class 9 ne "aage ki class" ke liye chhoda tha.

HCl → H⁺ + Cl⁻  (ionisation; asal mein H⁺ paani se jud kar H₃O⁺ banta hai, Chapter 1 mein dekha)  
CH₃COOH ⇌ CH₃COO⁻ + H⁺  (ionisation, thoda hi)  
H₂CO₃ ⇌ H⁺ + HCO₃⁻  
NH₄OH ⇌ NH₄⁺ + OH⁻  

Dono mein **ghol ke free ions** ka natija ek hi hai: bijli chalti hai. Aur ek hi baat dono mein saaf hai: **ion banne ya azaad hone ke liye bijli ki zaroorat nahi padti**, wo paani ya garmi se hi ho jaata hai. Bijli to bas unhe **kisi ek disha mein chalane** ka kaam karegi (Topic 2).

Ek aur cheez dekho: **dono taraf ke charge barabar hote hain.** HCl → H⁺ + Cl⁻ mein +1 − 1 = 0. CuSO₄ → Cu²⁺ + SO₄²⁻ mein +2 − 2 = 0. Ghol bahar se hamesha **neutral** hi rehta hai: kul positive charge = kul negative charge.

## Part D: Kaun si cheezein electrolyte hain? Ek table

| Kism | Strong electrolyte | Weak electrolyte | Non-electrolyte |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acid | HCl, H₂SO₄, HNO₃ | CH₃COOH, H₂CO₃, citric acid | (koi acid nahi) |
| Alkali (base) | NaOH, KOH | NH₄OH | (koi nahi) |
| Salt | NaCl, CuSO₄, K₂SO₄, NaHCO₃ | (aam taur par koi nahi) | (koi nahi) |
| Covalent, bina ion wale | (koi nahi) | (koi nahi) | Cheeni, glucose, alcohol (ethanol), urea, kerosene |
| Paani | (koi nahi) | Distilled paani: bahut hi kam | (lagbhag non-electrolyte) |

(Acids, alkalis aur salts ke ionic ya covalent roop ki baat upar hui. Lab mein concentrated acid ya alkali **kabhi ghar par nahi**: sirf padhne ke liye. **CuSO₄ ka ghol nigalne par nuksaan karta hai: sirf padhne ke liye, aur chakhna nahi.**)

Ek sawal jo tum shayad poochh rahe ho: **"distilled paani kya bilkul nahi chalata?"** Poora zero bhi nahi: **paani ke bahut thode molecule khud bhi ions mein tootte hain** (H₂O ⇌ H⁺ + OH⁻). Ye matra itni kam hai ki lagbhag **55 crore molecule mein se sirf ek** (ek litre paani mein lagbhag 55.5 mol paani ke molecule, par H⁺ sirf 10⁻⁷ mol; Chapter 1 ka pH 7 isi baat ka nishaan hai). Isliye distilled paani ko **non-electrolyte ke kareeb** ya "bahut hi kamzor electrolyte" maana jaata hai: tester ki LED isse nahi jalti.

Aur **dry cheeni** kyun nahi? Kyunki cheeni ke **molecule neutral** hain, usme ion hain hi nahi. Paani mein ghulne par bhi cheeni **molecule hi rehti hai**, ions nahi banati: ghulna aur ion banana alag-alag baatein hain. Isliye cheeni ka syrup bhi LED nahi jalata.

## Part E: Dhatu ki bijli aur ghol ki bijli: ek hi cheez nahi

Dhatu ka taar bhi bijli chalata hai, aur namak ka ghol bhi. Par **kaise** chalate hain, ye alag hai.

| Baat | Metallic conduction (dhatu) | Electrolytic conduction (ghol, pighla) |
|---|---|---|
| Charge le jaane wale | **Free electrons** | **Free ions**: cations aur anions |
| Kaun chalta hai | Sirf electron; dhatu ke ion apni jagah par | Cations ek taraf, anions doosri taraf |
| Chemical badlaav | **Nahi**: dhatu wahi ki wahi | **Haan**: dono sire (electrodes) par naye padarth bante hain (Topic 2) |
| Garam karne par | Bijli chalane ki kshamta **ghatti** hai | Bijli chalane ki kshamta **badhti** hai (ions tez chalte hain) |
| Example | Taamba, aluminium, loha | Namak ka ghol, pighla NaCl |

Dhyan do: dhatu ke taar se bijli guzarne par **taar ka koi padarth nahi badalta** (kuch gaya nahi, kuch aaya nahi). Lekin ghol mein ions ke chalne ke saath **ions apna charge electrode par chhodte ya lete hain**, aur isse **naya padarth** bantaa hai. Isi chemical badlaav ko **electrolysis** kehte hain, aur uske baare mein hum Topic 2 mein padhenge.

```
  A. Dhatu ka taar                      B. Electrolyte ka ghol

  (−) ------------------ (+)           (−) cathode       anode (+)
                                        :                      :
     +    +    +    +    +              :   ←  Na⁺   Cl⁻  →    :
  e⁻→  e⁻→  e⁻→  e⁻→  e⁻→               :   ←  Na⁺   Cl⁻  →    :
     +    +    +    +    +              :   ←  Na⁺   Cl⁻  →    :

  + = dhatu ke atom ka fixed ion        Cation (+) negative taraf,
      (apni jagah par)                  anion (−) positive taraf
  e⁻ = free electron (chalta hai)       dono ghol ke andar chalte hain
```

> **Figure 1.3** – **A:** dhatu ke taar mein fixed + core (+) apni jagah par rehte hain, free electrons (e⁻) battery ke negative taraf se positive taraf chalte hain. **B:** ghol mein Na⁺ (cation) negative electrode ki taraf aur Cl⁻ (anion) positive electrode ki taraf chalte hain; ghol ke andar electron nahi chalte. Dono mein charge hi behta hai, par le jaane wale alag hain.

Ek chhota apwaad: **graphite** non-metal hai, par uske andar free electrons hain, isliye wo dhatu ki tarah (metallic conduction se) bijli chalata hai. Topic 2 mein hum ise electrode ki tarah dekhenge.

## Part F: Solved examples

**Solved Example 1 (tester ka natija predict karo).** Ek glass mein ek chamach baking soda ghola gaya. Doosre mein ek chamach cheeni. Teesre mein ek chamach sirka aadha glass paani mein. Chauthe mein distilled paani. LED ka natija batao: tez, halki ya nahi.

- Baking soda (NaHCO₃) ek **salt** hai: ionic, Na⁺ aur HCO₃⁻ free: **tez** (strong electrolyte).
- Cheeni: covalent, ions nahi: **nahi jalegi** (non-electrolyte).
- Sirka (CH₃COOH): weak electrolyte, kuch hi ions: **halki**.
- Distilled paani: lagbhag koi ion nahi: **nahi jalegi**.

**Solved Example 2 (ion ginna).** 100 formula unit Na₂SO₄ paani mein ghul gaye aur poori tarah dissociate ho gaye. Kitne Na⁺, kitne SO₄²⁻ aur kul kitne ions bane? Kul + aur − charge ka hisaab lagao.

- Na₂SO₄ → 2Na⁺ + SO₄²⁻: ek unit se 2 Na⁺ aur 1 SO₄²⁻.
- 100 unit se Na⁺ = 100 × 2 = **200**; SO₄²⁻ = 100 × 1 = **100**; kul = **300 ions**.
- Positive charge = 200 × (+1) = +200. Negative charge = 100 × (−2) = −200. Dono barabar, ghol neutral hai.
- *(Jaanch: 200 + 100 = 300 ✅, +200 − 200 = 0 ✅)*

**Solved Example 3 (equation likho aur jaancho).** HNO₃ (strong acid) aur CH₃COOH (weak acid) ke ions mein tootne ke equation likho. Har ek mein atoms aur charge ginkar jaancho.

- HNO₃ → H⁺ + NO₃⁻ (ek taraf, kyunki lagbhag poora toota). Atoms: H 1, N 1, O 3 dono taraf. Charge: 0 = (+1) + (−1) ✅.
- CH₃COOH ⇌ CH₃COO⁻ + H⁺ (⇌ kyunki thoda hi toota). Atoms: C 2, H 4, O 2 dono taraf. Charge: 0 = (−1) + (+1) ✅.
- Farak: HNO₃ ke liye ek seedha tir, kyunki saare molecule toot jaate hain. CH₃COOH ke liye ⇌, kyunki zyada molecule jaise-ke-taise rehte hain.

**Solved Example 4 (Figure 1.2 se tulna).** Figure 1.2 ke dono beakers mein ions ki ginti tulna karo aur LED ke baare mein kya kehna sahi hoga?

- Strong beaker: 10 H⁺ + 10 Cl⁻ = **20 ions**.
- Weak beaker: 1 H⁺ + 1 Ac⁻ = **2 ions**.
- Strong mein ions 20 ÷ 2 = **10 guna** zyada hain, isliye (baaki baatein barabar hon to) LED zyada tez jalegi.
- Dhyan do: sirf **ginti** bataata hai, "strong ya weak" naam nahi. Agar weak electrolyte bahut zyada ghola jaaye aur strong bahut hi kam, to ions ki ginti ulti bhi ho sakti hai, isliye fair tulna ke liye matra barabar rakho.

**Solved Example 5 (resistor kyun chahiye).** Lal LED par lagbhag 2 V girta hai. Do AA cells 3 V dete hain. Agar tester mein 220 ohm ka resistor series mein hai to current lagbhag kitna hoga (agar ghol ka resistance bahut kam maan lo)? Bina resistor ke kya hota?

- Resistor par girne wala voltage = 3 − 2 = **1 V**.
- Current = V ÷ R = 1 ÷ 220 ≈ **0.0045 A ≈ 4.5 mA** (lagbhag): LED ke liye surakshit.
- Bina resistor ke LED ke saath current ko rokne wala kuch nahi, to wo bahut zyada ho sakta hai aur LED **jal ya kharab** ho sakti hai. Isliye resistor zaroori hai.
- (Asal mein ghol ka apna resistance bhi hota hai, isliye current aur bhi kam hoga.)

**Solved Example 6 (anjaan liquid pehchano).** Ek saaf liquid ko tester se jaancha: LED nahi jali. Phir usme ek chamach namak ghola gaya: LED saaf jali. Kya hum kah sakte hain ki pehla liquid distilled paani tha?

- Pehle LED nahi jali: liquid mein free ions nahi (ya bahut kam).
- Namak daalne ke baad jali: ab free Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ aa gaye.
- Ye distilled paani ho sakta hai, ya koi aur non-electrolyte (jaise cheeni ka ghol): in sab mein LED nahi jalti.
- **Nishkarsh:** sirf "LED nahi jali" se ye tay nahi hota ki wo distilled paani hi hai; wo koi bhi non-electrolyte ho sakta hai. Pakka hone ke liye aur tests chahiye (jaise gandh, ubalne ka bindu).

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Jo paani mein ghul jaaye, wo electrolyte hai."** Galat. *Demonstration:* cheeni paani mein khoob ghulti hai, par cheeni ke syrup se LED nahi jalti (Activity 1.1). Cheeni ke **poore molecule** ghole jaate hain, koi ion nahi banta. Bijli ke liye **ions** chahiye, ghulna kaafi nahi.

**Galti 2: "Ions tab banate hain jab bijli lagayi jaati hai."** Galat. *Demonstration:* baking soda ko paani mein ghol kar rakh do: bina kisi battery ke bhi us mein Na⁺ aur HCO₃⁻ ghol mein tairte hain. Tester ki battery un ions ko **banati nahi**, sirf unhe **chalne ka raasta** deti hai. Ions to ghulne ya pighalne par pehle hi azaad ho chuke hain.

**Galti 3: "Strong electrolyte ka matlab concentrated ghol hai."** Galat. *Demonstration:* ek bahut dilute HCl ka ghol bhi strong electrolyte hai, kyunki jo HCl molecule maujood hain wo lagbhag **sab** toot-te hain. Aur concentrated CH₃COOH bhi weak hai kyunki uska zyada hissa molecule hi rehta hai. Strong ya weak ka matlab **kitna hissa tootta hai**, kitna ghola hai ye nahi (Figure 1.2: dono beakers mein 10-10 molecule the).

**Galti 4: "Paani bijli ka dushman hai: paani bijli nahi chalata."** Adhi baat. *Demonstration:* distilled paani se LED nahi jalti, par nal ka paani kabhi-kabhi halki roshni deta hai, kyunki usme ghule minerals ke ions hain. Isi liye geyser ke label ka dar sahi hai: bathroom ka paani **shuddh paani nahi**, ionon wala paani hai. Is baat ka koi bhi test **kabhi mains ke saath nahi hota**: hamesha sirf 3 V ka tester.

**Galti 5: "Dhatu aur ghol mein bijli ek hi tareeke se chalti hai."** Galat. *Demonstration:* Figure 1.3 dekho: dhatu ke taar mein sirf **electron** chalte hain aur taar wahi ka wahi rehta hai; ghol mein **ions** chalte hain aur electrode par naya padarth ban sakta hai. Taar ko garam karne par uski bijli chalane ki kshamta ghatti hai, aur ghol ko garam karne par badhti hai: dono ulte asar, kyunki carrier alag hain.

## Quick Recap

- **Electrolyte:** pighla ho ya ghola ho, free ions ke kaaran bijli chalata hai (acids, alkalis, salts). **Non-electrolyte:** koi ion nahi, bijli nahi (cheeni, glucose, alcohol, urea, kerosene).
- Liquid ki bijli chalane ki kshamta = free ions ki ginti. Distilled paani lagbhag nahi chalata (lagbhag 55 crore mein se 1 molecule toota); nal ka paani halka sa chalata hai kyunki usme ghule namak hain.
- **Strong electrolyte:** lagbhag poora ions mein toot jaata hai (HCl, H₂SO₄, HNO₃, NaOH, KOH, NaCl, CuSO₄). **Weak electrolyte:** bahut thoda toota, baaki molecule (CH₃COOH, H₂CO₃, NH₄OH); ⇌ chinh. Ye **concentration ka nahi, padarth ka** swabhav hai.
- **Dissociation:** ionic compound ke ions pehle se hain, bas azaad hote hain (NaCl → Na⁺ + Cl⁻). **Ionisation:** covalent molecule ghol mein ions banata hai (HCl → H⁺ + Cl⁻). Ions banne ke liye bijli nahi chahiye.
- **Metallic conduction:** electron chalte hain, koi chemical badlaav nahi; garam karne par ghatti hai. **Electrolytic conduction:** ions chalte hain, electrodes par chemical badlaav (Topic 2); garam karne par badhti hai.
- Tester: sirf 2 AA cells + LED + 220 ohm resistor, adult ke paas, kuch chakhna nahi. **Mains bijli ka kabhi koi test nahi** (geyser ka label sirf chetawani hai).

## Practice Questions

1. Classify each as strong electrolyte, weak electrolyte or non-electrolyte: (a) HBr solution; (b) formic acid (HCOOH) solution; (c) urea solution; (d) K₃PO₄ solution; (e) glycerol solution.

2. Write the ionisation or dissociation equation of each, and check atoms and charge: (a) HBr; (b) K₃PO₄; (c) Mg(NO₃)₂; (d) HCOOH (weak: use the double arrow).

3. 50 formula units of Mg(NO₃)₂ dissociate completely in water. Find the number of Mg²⁺, the number of NO₃⁻ and the total number of ions. Show that the total positive and negative charge are equal.

4. 30 formula units of K₃PO₄ dissociate completely. How many K⁺ and PO₄³⁻ ions, and how many ions in all? Compare the total number of ions with what you would get from 30 units of HBr (completely ionised).

5. Two identical beakers each hold 20 molecules of a dissolved substance. In beaker P, 19 molecules have split into ions; in beaker Q, only 1 has split. Count the ions in each (the substance splits into exactly 1 positive and 1 negative ion). Which beaker has a brighter LED and which is the strong electrolyte?

6. Explain in two or three lines why a solution of glycerol does not make an LED glow, though glycerol dissolves very well in water.

7. Explain why the label "geele haath se mat chhuo" near a bathroom geyser switch is correct although distilled water is said to be a very poor conductor of electricity. Do not suggest any test.

8. Give two differences between metallic conduction and electrolytic conduction, using the words electrons, ions and chemical change.

9. A solution of a substance X lights an LED dimly. X is a covalent compound of which, when dissolved, only 3 molecules out of every 300 split into ions. Is X a strong or a weak electrolyte? What percent of its molecules have split? Write ⇌ or → in X's equation and give your reason.

10. Puzzle: four unlabelled colourless solutions P, Q, R, S are, one each, of: HBr (a gas dissolved in water), formic acid, urea, K₃PO₄ (a solid salt of a metal). Facts: (i) P and S make the LED glow brightly; (ii) Q makes it glow dimly; (iii) R does not make it glow at all; (iv) the solute of S contains a metal. Name P, Q, R and S.

11. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) A strong electrolyte must be a concentrated solution. (b) The ions of an electrolyte form only after the current is switched on. (c) HBr is a covalent compound but its solution conducts. (d) Heating a metal wire makes it conduct better. (e) A solution can conduct and still be electrically neutral overall.

12. Paper activity: draw a simple version of Figure 1.2 for a solution with 8 molecules of a weak electrolyte in which exactly 1 molecule has split, and a solution of 8 molecules of a strong electrolyte. Label the number of ions in each.

### Answers

1. (a) HBr: **strong electrolyte** (strong acid, lagbhag poora ionise). (b) HCOOH: **weak electrolyte** (weak acid, thoda hi ionise). (c) Urea: **non-electrolyte** (covalent, ions nahi). (d) K₃PO₄: **strong electrolyte** (salt, poori tarah dissociate). (e) Glycerol: **non-electrolyte** (covalent molecules ki tarah ghulta hai, ions nahi).

2. (a) HBr → H⁺ + Br⁻: H 1, Br 1 dono taraf; charge 0 = +1 − 1 ✅. (b) K₃PO₄ → 3K⁺ + PO₄³⁻: K 3, P 1, O 4 dono taraf; charge 0 = +3 − 3 ✅. (c) Mg(NO₃)₂ → Mg²⁺ + 2NO₃⁻: Mg 1, N 2, O 6 dono taraf; charge 0 = +2 − 2 ✅. (d) HCOOH ⇌ HCOO⁻ + H⁺: H 2, C 1, O 2 dono taraf; charge 0 = −1 + 1 ✅.

3. Ek unit se 1 Mg²⁺ aur 2 NO₃⁻. 50 unit se Mg²⁺ = **50**, NO₃⁻ = 50 × 2 = **100**, kul = **150 ions**. Positive charge = 50 × (+2) = +100; negative = 100 × (−1) = −100: barabar ✅.

4. Ek unit se 3 K⁺ aur 1 PO₄³⁻. 30 unit se K⁺ = 30 × 3 = **90**, PO₄³⁻ = **30**, kul = **120 ions**. Charge: +90 aur 30 × (−3) = −90 ✅. 30 HBr se 30 H⁺ + 30 Br⁻ = **60 ions**. K₃PO₄ ke 30 unit 60 ke bajay 120 ions dete hain: yani **2 guna** zyada.

5. Beaker P: 19 molecule toote, har ek se 2 ions: 19 × 2 = **38 ions**. Beaker Q: 1 × 2 = **2 ions**. P mein ions 38 ÷ 2 = **19 guna** zyada hain, isliye **P ki LED tez** aur P (lagbhag poora toota) **strong electrolyte** hai; Q weak hai.

6. Glycerol covalent hai: uske molecule neutral hain. Paani mein ghulne par bhi wo **molecule ki tarah hi** rehta hai, ions nahi banata. Ions nahi to charge ko le jaane wala koi nahi: isliye LED nahi jalti. Ghulna aur bijli chalana alag baatein hain.

7. Distilled paani mein lagbhag koi ion nahi hota, par bathroom ka (nal ka) paani **shuddh nahi** hai: usme ghule minerals aur namak ke ions hain, jo use bijli ka raasta bana dete hain. Isliye **geele haath ya geele farsh** ke saath bijli ka khatra sach hai, aur label sahi chetawani hai. (Is baat ko ghar par jaanchna nahi hai: sirf padhna.)

8. (i) Metallic mein **electrons** charge le jaate hain, electrolytic mein **ions**. (ii) Metallic mein **koi chemical badlaav nahi**; electrolytic mein electrodes par naye padarth bante hain. (Teesra: garam karne par metallic ghatta hai, electrolytic badhta hai.)

9. 3 ÷ 300 × 100 = **1%** molecule toote. Dim LED aur 1% ke aadhar par X **weak electrolyte** hai. Equation mein **⇌** likhenge, kyunki zyadatar molecule toote nahi aur toote hue ions phir se jud sakte hain.

10. (iv) se S mein dhatu hai: wo **K₃PO₄** hai (HBr, formic acid, urea mein dhatu nahi). (iii) se R: LED nahi jalti: **urea** (non-electrolyte). (ii) se Q: dim: **formic acid** (weak electrolyte). (i) mein P aur S dono tez: S = K₃PO₄ (pehle se), to P = **HBr** (strong acid). Jaanch: chaaron facts is ek hi assignment se sach hote hain: (i) P (HBr) aur S (K₃PO₄) tez, (ii) Q dim, (iii) R nahi, (iv) S mein dhatu K hai. Koi doosra tarika (jaise S = HBr) (iv) ko tod deta hai.

11. (a) **False** ❌: strong ka matlab "lagbhag poora toota", concentration nahi. (b) **False** ❌: ions ghulne ya pighalne par pehle se azaad hote hain; battery unhe sirf chalati hai. (c) **True** ✅: paani ke saath ions mein ionise hota hai. (d) **False** ❌: dhatu ko garam karne par uski bijli chalane ki kshamta ghatti hai. (e) **True** ✅: kul + aur − charge barabar rehte hain.

12. Strong: 8 molecule, saare toote: 8 positive + 8 negative = **16 ions**. Weak: 1 toota: 1 + 1 = **2 ions**, aur 7 poore molecule bache. Strong mein ions 16 ÷ 2 = 8 guna zyada hain. (Sketch ki tarah bana sakte ho: ye hamesha sketch hai, asli weak mein 1% se bhi kam tootta hai.)
