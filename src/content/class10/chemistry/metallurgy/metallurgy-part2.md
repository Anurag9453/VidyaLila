# Topic 2: Ore Ki Safai: Concentration

Topic 1 mein humne dekha ki khaan se jo ore nikalta hai, usme kaam ki dhatu wala mineral aur **gangue** (mitti, ret, patthar) mila hota hai. Metallurgy ke flow mein khaan se nikaalne ke baad ka agla kadam bhi wahin likha tha: **concentration**, yaani ore ko "saaf" karna, taaki gangue zyada se zyada nikal jaaye aur kaam ka mineral bacha rahe. Aaj hum dekhenge ki ye safai kitne tareeke se hoti hai, aur har tareeka mineral aur gangue ke beech ke **kis fark** par tika hai. Ye wahi Class 9 wala niyam hai: pehle dhundho ki hisson mein kya alag hai, phir tareeka chuno.

---

## Nadi kinare ki thali

Mr. Upasani apni bhatiji Falguni ko Jharkhand ki ek nadi ke kinare le gaye. Wahan kuch auratein ghutne tak paani mein khadi thi. Har ek ke haath mein ek chauda, chhichhla thaal tha. Wo thaal mein nadi ki ret bharti, use paani mein dubakar halke se **gol-gol** ghumati, thoda tirchha karti, aur ret ka ek hissa paani ke saath bah jaata. Thaal mein ek hi pal mein ret kam hoti jaati thi.

"Chacha, ye log kya kar rahe hain? Ret to bas ret hai!" Falguni ne poochha. Mr. Upasani muskuraye. "Ye sone ke **bahut chhote kan** dhundh rahi hain. Is nadi ka naam Subarnarekha hai: **suvarna** yaani sona, rekha yaani lakeer. Kaha jaata hai ki yahan kuch jagah log aaj bhi ret dhokar sone ke kan nikaalte hain (lagbhag, aur bahut kam matra mein)." Falguni ne ek aurat ke thaal mein jhaanka. Neeche, thaal ke ek kinare par, kuch **peele, chamakdaar zarre** aa gaye the, baaki ret bah chuki thi.

"Par Chacha, sone ko ret se alag karne ke liye na magnet laga, na filter!" "Bilkul," Mr. Upasani bole. "Isme **vajan ka fark** kaam karta hai. Sona ret se lagbhag **saat guna bhaari** hota hai. Paani ke ghoomne se halki ret bah jaati hai, bhaari sona thaal ki tali par tik jaata hai. Aur ye to sirf ek tareeka hai. Jis dhatu ka ore jaisa ho, safai ka tareeka bhi waisa hota hai. Chalo, saare tareeke ek-ek karke dekhte hain."

## Part A: Safai ka niyam: "fark dhundho"

Topic 1 mein ek ore ka naksha banaya tha: **mineral + gangue**. Concentration ka kaam sirf itna hai ki gangue ka bada hissa nikal jaaye. **Dhatu abhi nahi nikalti**: concentrate mein dhatu abhi bhi compound (oxide, sulphide ya carbonate) ke roop mein hai. Hum bas aage ke mehange kadam (garam karna, reduction, electrolysis) mein bekaar gangue ko dhone se bachate hain.

Class 9 ke Topic 4 wala sawaal yahan bhi har baar poochna hai: **ore ke mineral aur gangue mein kaun si property alag hai?** Paanch tareeke, paanch properties:

| Tareeka | Kis property ka fark | Kahan kaam aata hai |
|---|---|---|
| Hand-picking | Rang aur dikhne wala aakaar (bade tukde) | Bade tukdon wale ore, kam matra |
| Hydraulic washing | Density (bhaari ya halka) | Gold; oxide ores jaise haematite, cassiterite |
| Magnetic separation | Magnet se lagaav | Magnetite; (jaise tin ore ke saath wolframite, sirf naam) |
| Froth flotation | Tel se geela hona ya paani se geela hona | Sulphide ores: ZnS, PbS, CuFeS₂ |
| Leaching | Kisi chemical mein ghulna ya na ghulna | Bauxite (NaOH se); gold (sirf naam) |

## Part B: Haath se aur paani ki dhaar se

### 1. Hand-picking

Jab ore ke tukde **bade** hon aur mineral aur gangue ke **rang alag dikhte** hon, to khaan ke kaamgaar ya machine ki conveyor belt ke paas khade log gangue ke tukde **haath se hata dete hain**. Class 9 mein tumne daal se kankad chunna isi tarah dekha tha. Idhar bhi wahi baat hai: dhyan se dekho, chun lo. Par ye sirf **kam matra** aur **saaf pehchan** wale ore mein chalta hai.

### 2. Hydraulic washing (gravity separation)

Jab mineral aur gangue **density** mein bahut alag hon, to paani ki dhaar kaam aati hai. Barik kuti hui ore ko ek **halki dhalan wali, chaudi mez** par rakhkar upar se paani ki tez dhaar bahai jaati hai. **Halka gangue paani ke saath bah jaata hai, bhaari mineral mez par ruk jaata hai.**

Kuch density (lagbhag, g per cm³):

| Padarth | Density (lagbhag) |
|---|---|
| Quartz (SiO₂, aam ret) | 2.65 |
| Magnetite (Fe₃O₄) | 5.2 |
| Haematite (Fe₂O₃) | 5.3 |
| Cassiterite (tin ka ore, SnO₂) | 7.0 |
| Galena (PbS) | 7.5 |
| Gold (Au) | 19.3 |

Dekho gold ret se kitna bhaari hai: 19.3 ÷ 2.65 ≈ **7.3 guna**. Isiliye panning mein sone ke kan thaal ki tali par tik jaate hain, ret bah jaati hai. Quartz (2.65) aur haematite (5.3) mein bhi fark itna hai (lagbhag 2 guna), isiliye oxide ores ke liye bhi ye tareeka kaam karta hai.

```
        halki ret paani ke saath bah jaati hai
                  ~~~~>  ~~~~>
         ______________________________
        /  ~    ~    ~    ~    ~    ~   \      <- paani
       /   . .  . .  . .  . .  . .      \     <- halki ret (SiO₂)
      /     .  .   .   .   .   .   .     \
     /______________________________* * *_\    <- bhaari kan (sona) tali par
          thaal ko tirchha karke gol-gol ghumate hain
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Panning (hydraulic washing ka chhota roop): thaal ko paani mein ghumane se halki ret bah jaati hai (upar-upar), aur bhaari kan (*) thaal ki tali par ruk jaate hain.

> **Activity 2.1 (ghar par adult ke saath): Apni panning.**
> Kisi khuli jagah (chhat, aangan, bagiche ke paas) ek chauda, chhichhla thaal lo. Usme do-teen mutthi saaf, sookhi ret lo aur usme **5 se 6 chhote steel ke nut ya bolt** milao (kuch bhaari, chhoti cheezein; kaanch ke moti nahi, wo ret jitne hi halke hote hain). Thaal mein itna paani daalo ki ret doob jaaye, aur use dheere se ghumao aur halka sa tirchha karo. Dekho ki ret paani ke saath ek kinare se bah kar niklegi aur bhaari nut tali par bache rahenge. Kaam ke baad ret bagiche mein daalo (nali mein nahi), aur haath dho lo. Kisi ko kuch bhi mooh ya aankh mein nahi lena hai.

## Part C: Magnet se (Magnetic separation)

Class 9 mein tumne magnet se loha aur ret alag ki thi. Ab wahi idea ek bade scale par ore ke saath dekhte hain. Agar ore ka mineral **magnet se chipakta hai** aur gangue nahi chipakta, to ore ko ek conveyor belt par bahaya jaata hai, jiske ek siri par **magnet wala roller** hota hai.

- **Magnetite** (Fe₃O₄) magnet se kaafi mazboot chipakta hai, aur uska gangue (zyadatar silica) nahi chipakta. Isliye magnetite ka concentration magnet se bahut aasaan hota hai.
- **Haematite** (Fe₂O₃) bhi iron ka ore hai, par wo magnet se bahut kamzor chipakta hai. Ise alag karne ke liye bahut mazboot magnet lagte hain, aur kai baar paani ki dhaar bhi saath mein lagti hai.
- Kabhi **ulta** bhi hota hai: **tin ore** (cassiterite) magnet se nahi chipakta, par uske saath mili **wolframite** (tungsten ka ore) chipakta hai. Wahan magnet gangue (wolframite) ko hatata hai aur tin ore peeche rehta hai. Hum ise sirf naam se jaante hain.

```
        crushed ore (magnetite + gangue)
                   :
                   v
        =================================( M )   <- magnet wala roller
           conveyor belt chal rahi hai ->   :  \
                                            :    \   gangue (non-magnetic):
      magnetite (magnetic): roller se       :      \  roller se aage uchhal
      chipki rehti hai, phir neeche girti   :        \ kar door girta hai
                                            v          v
                                     +-----------+ +----------+
                                     : MAGNETITE : :  GANGUE  :
                                     +-----------+ +----------+
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Magnetic separation: belt ke siri par magnet wala roller hai. Non-magnetic gangue belt ki raftaar se aage uchhal kar door wale dher mein girta hai, jabki magnetite roller se chipki rehti hai aur thoda ghoom kar roller ke neeche wale dher mein girti hai.

> **Activity 2.2 (ghar par adult ke saath): Magnet se sample sorting.**
> Ret aur keel wala sorting tum Class 9 mein kar chuke ho. Is baar plate mein thodi ret ke saath **teen dhatuein** milao: steel ke paper-clip, aluminium foil ki chhoti goliyan, aur taambe ke taar ke chhote tukde (purani wire se, adult kaate). Magnet ko ek patli polythene ki thaili ya kagaz mein lapetkar plate ke upar ghumao: **sirf steel ke clip chipakte hain**; aluminium aur taamba ret ke saath peeche reh jaate hain. Phir ek steel ki keel aur aluminium foil ka tukda bari-bari magnet ke paas laao: steel chipakti hai, aluminium nahi. Ye isliye ki magnet har dhatu ko nahi kheenchta, aur isi liye magnetic separation sirf magnetic ore (jaise magnetite) ke liye kaam aata hai. Magnet ko aankh, kaan ya chhote bachchon se door rakho.

## Part D: Froth flotation (jhaag wala tareeka)

Sulphide ores, jaise **zinc blende (ZnS), galena (PbS), copper pyrites (CuFeS₂)**, ke saath ek chaalaaki wala tareeka hota hai. Inki ek khaas aadat hai: inke kan **tel se chipakte hain, paani se nahi**; jabki gangue (silica, mitti) ke kan **paani se geele hote hain**, tel se nahi. Isi fark par **froth flotation** tika hai.

Kaise kaam karta hai (sirf padhne ke liye; students ise kabhi nahi karte, ye khaan ke plant mein hota hai):

1. Ore ko bahut **barik pees** liya jaata hai (paudar jaisa).
2. Ek bade tank mein **paani** bharte hain aur usme ye paudar daalte hain.
3. Usme **pine oil** (jo ore ke kano ko tel ki parat se dhak deta hai) aur ek **frothing agent** (jhaag banane wala) milate hain.
4. Neeche se **hawa ke bulbule** phoonkte hain aur andar ek ghoomta hua pankha ise milata rehta hai.
5. **Ore ke kan** bubbles se chipak jaate hain aur bubbles unhe **upar jhaag mein** le aate hain. **Gangue ke kan** paani se geele hone ke kaaran **neeche baith jaate hain**.
6. Upar ka jhaag chhaan kar sukhaya jaata hai: wahi **concentrate** hai.

```
                 jhaag ke bubbles ore ko upar le jaate hain
                      O o O o O o O o
                    O o *O o *O o O *o O
        ============================== +--> jhaag nikalta hai (concentrate)
        :  ~  ~  ~  ~  ~  ~  ~  ~  ~  :
        :  ~ *o ~  ~ *o ~  ~ *o ~  ~   :        * = ore ke kan
        :  ~  ~  ~  ~  ~  ~  ~  ~  ~  :        o = hawa ke bubble
        :  #  ~  #  ~  #  ~  ~  #  ~   :       # = gangue ke kan
        :_#__#__#___#__#__#__#___#__#_:
              ^     ^     ^     ^
              neeche se hawa ke bulbule
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Froth flotation: tank mein barik ore, paani, pine oil aur neeche se hawa. Ore ke kan (*) bubbles (o) se chipak kar jhaag mein upar aate hain, gangue ke kan (#) neeche baith jaate hain.

Ye tareeka ek zarooratmand baat bhi sikhata hai: **barik peesna** isliye zaroori hai ki kan itne halke ho jaayein ki bubbles unhe utha sakein. Aur ye isliye bhi chalta hai ki sulphide ores aur gangue ki **satah ki aadat** (tel ya paani se dosti) bilkul ulti hai. Isse bahut kam kharche mein kai ton ore ka kachra alag ho jaata hai, aur duniya ke zyadatar zinc, lead aur copper ke sulphide ores isi se saaf hote hain.

## Part E: Leaching (ghulana, ya chemical se chhaantna)

Kabhi mineral aur gangue mein **na density ka bada fark** hota hai, na magnet ka, na tel ya paani ka. Tab ek **chemical** dhoondha jaata hai jo **sirf ore ko ghula de aur gangue ko chhod de**. Ye **leaching** hai. Phir ghol ko filter karke alag kar lete hain: kaam ka hissa ghol mein, gangue filter par.

### Bauxite aur sodium hydroxide (Chapter 2 wali amphoteric kahani)

Aluminium ka ore **bauxite** hai (Al₂O₃·2H₂O). Bauxite ke saath mile gangue mein mukhya roop se **Fe₂O₃** (lohe ka oxide, jo use lal-bhoora rang deta hai) aur silica jaisi cheezein hoti hain. Inme se **Al₂O₃ amphoteric** hai: Chapter 2 ([metals-and-non-metals-part2.md](../metals-and-non-metals/metals-and-non-metals-part2.md)) mein tumne dekha tha ki ye NaOH ke saath bhi react karta hai. Yahi ore ko saaf karne ka rasta hai: garm, **concentrated** sodium hydroxide ke ghol mein bauxite ko daalo.

Al₂O₃ + 2NaOH → 2NaAlO₂ + H₂O

Sodium aluminate (NaAlO₂) paani mein **ghul jaata hai**. Lekin **Fe₂O₃ ek basic oxide hai**, amphoteric nahi: wo NaOH se react nahi karta aur **ghulta nahi**. Kuch aur ashuddhiyan bhi gangue ke saath ghulti nahi. Ye **na ghulne wala lal-bhoora kachra** hi **red mud** kehlata hai. Ghol ko filter karne par red mud filter par rah jaata hai aur sodium aluminate ka saaf ghol neeche aata hai.

Concentrated sodium hydroxide ka ghol **jalan karne wala aur khatarnak** hota hai: ye **sirf padhne ke liye** hai. **Isko sirf trained log, plant mein, chashme-dastane pehen kar sambhalte hain.** Pura aluminium ka kissa (red mud ke baad kya, aur electrolysis kaise) **Topic 5** mein aayega.

### Gold ka leaching: sirf naam, aur ek zehr ki chetawani

Gold aur silver ke ore ko saaf karne ka ek tareeka ek **cyanide ke ghol** ka use bhi hai. **Cyanide bahut zehreeli hai: thodi si matra bhi jaan le sakti hai.** Isliye ye kaam sirf bade, kadi nigrani wale plant mein hota hai, aur **students ke liye ye sirf naam hai**: na equation, na koi activity. Hum yahan aage nahi badhte.

## Part F: Solved examples

**Solved Example 1 (tareeka chuno).** Neeche teen ore aur unka gangue diya hai. Har ek ke liye batao kaun sa tareeka kaam karega.

| Ore | Gangue | Diya gaya fark |
|---|---|---|
| Zinc blende (ZnS) | Quartz (SiO₂) | ZnS tel se chipakta hai, quartz paani se geela hota hai |
| Magnetite (Fe₃O₄) | Silica ki ret | Magnetite magnet se chipakta hai, ret nahi |
| Cassiterite (SnO₂, 7.0) | Quartz (2.65) | Density mein bada fark (lagbhag 2.6 guna) |

- **ZnS aur quartz:** fark "tel se chipakna ya paani se geela hona" hai, isliye **froth flotation**.
- **Fe₃O₄ aur ret:** fark "magnet se lagaav" hai, isliye **magnetic separation**.
- **SnO₂ aur quartz:** fark "density" hai (7.0 ÷ 2.65 ≈ 2.64), isliye **hydraulic washing**.

**Solved Example 2 (flotation ke baad gangue kitna gaya).** 1500 kg galena ore mein 8% PbS aur baaki gangue hai. Flotation ke baad jo concentrate milta hai, usme 64% PbS hai. Maan lo ki saara PbS concentrate mein aa gaya. Concentrate ka mass aur nikla hua gangue nikalo.

- PbS = 8% of 1500 = **120 kg**. Gangue = 1500 − 120 = **1380 kg**.
- Concentrate mein PbS 64% hai, to concentrate = 120 ÷ 0.64 = **187.5 kg**.
- Concentrate mein bacha gangue = 187.5 − 120 = **67.5 kg**.
- Ore se **nikla hua gangue** = 1380 − 67.5 = **1312.5 kg**.
- Ore ki jagah concentrate le jaana **1500 ÷ 187.5 = 8 guna** kam bhaar hai.
- *(Jaanch: 120 + 67.5 = 187.5, aur 120 ÷ 187.5 = 0.64 ✅; 1312.5 + 187.5 = 1500 ✅)*

Dhyan do: concentrate mein bhi **64% hi PbS** hai, 100% nahi: gangue ka kuch hissa saath chala aata hai. Isi se agla tareeka ya agla kadam bhi saaf karta hai.

**Solved Example 3 (magnetic separation ka hisaab).** 500 kg ke crushed sample mein 60% magnetite hai. Magnet saari magnetite kheench leta hai, lekin 10 kg gangue bhi chipak kar saath aa jaata hai. Concentrate ka mass aur usme magnetite ka percent nikalo.

- Magnetite = 60% of 500 = **300 kg**. Gangue = 200 kg.
- Concentrate = 300 + 10 = **310 kg**.
- Magnetite ka percent = 300 ÷ 310 × 100 ≈ **96.8%** (lagbhag).
- Gangue jo alag hua = 200 − 10 = **190 kg**.
- *(Jaanch: 190 + 310 = 500 ✅)*

**Solved Example 4 (pehchan karo).** Ek ore ke baare mein: "Ye pehle chhota kuta hua hai; ghol mein NaOH mila diya, to kuch ghul gaya aur lal-bhoora kachra bacha." Is ore ka kaun sa tareeka laga?

**Leaching.** Kyunki ek chemical ne ore ka ek hissa **ghula** diya aur baaki bacha. Ye wahi bauxite wali kahani hai: Al₂O₃ ghula, Fe₂O₃ (red mud) nahi.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

- **"Concentration ke baad dhatu mil jaati hai."** Galat. Demonstration: galena ke concentrate ka 64% PbS hai (Example 2). PbS **sulphide compound** hai, dhatu lead (Pb) nahi. PbS mein Pb ka hissa 207 ÷ (207 + 32) = 207 ÷ 239 ≈ 86.6% hai (lagbhag), par wo lead **sulphur se juda hua** hai. Dhatu nikalne ke liye aur kadam chahiye (Topic 3 aur 4).
- **"Froth flotation har ore ke liye chalta hai."** Galat. Ye mukhya roop se **sulphide ores** ke liye hai, jinke kan tel se chipakte hain. Demonstration: quartz ki ret paani se geeli hoti hai, tel se nahi: use jhaag mein daalo to wo neeche baith jaati hai, upar nahi aati. Isi tarah har ore ke liye uska alag fark dhundhna padta hai.
- **"Magnet har dhatu ke ore ko kheenchta hai."** Galat. Magnet sirf magnetic padarth (jaise iron ka Fe₃O₄) ko kheenchta hai. Demonstration: Activity 2.2 mein steel ki keel chipakti hai par aluminium ki foil nahi. Isi tarah zinc blende ya galena magnet se alag nahi hote.
- **"Hydraulic washing mein halka padarth neeche baithta hai."** Galat, ulta. Panning mein **bhaari** sone ke kan tali par ruk jaate hain, halki ret paani ke saath **bah jaati hai** (Figure 2.1).
- **"Leaching mein gangue ghulta hai."** Aksar ulta hota hai. Bauxite ke leaching mein **ore (Al₂O₃) ghulta hai** aur gangue (Fe₂O₃ wali red mud) filter par rehta hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Concentration** = ore se gangue hataana. Dhatu ab bhi compound ke roop mein hai.
- Tareeka **property ke fark** par: hand-picking (rang, bade tukde), hydraulic washing (density), magnetic separation (magnet), froth flotation (tel ya paani se geela hona, sulphide ores), leaching (ghulna).
- Gold ret se lagbhag **7 guna** bhaari hai: panning ka raaz.
- Magnetite magnet se mazboot chipakta hai; haematite kamzor. Tin ore ke saath wolframite sirf naam se.
- **Froth flotation:** barik ore + paani + pine oil + hawa. Ore jhaag mein upar, gangue neeche. Zinc blende, galena, copper pyrites.
- **Bauxite ka leaching:** Al₂O₃ + 2NaOH → 2NaAlO₂ + H₂O; Fe₂O₃ aur anya ashuddhiyan red mud (amphoteric wali kahani).
- Gold ka cyanide leaching **sirf naam**, bahut zehreeli.
- Magnet aur panning ghar par adult ke saath; baaki sab (flotation, NaOH, cyanide) **sirf padhne ke liye**.

## Practice Questions

1. Name the best concentration method for each pair, and the property used: (a) native gold grains in river sand; (b) copper pyrites (CuFeS₂) in white quartz; (c) magnetite grains in a pile of non-magnetic silica; (d) big bright blocks of ore among dull grey rock pieces on a conveyor belt; (e) bauxite mixed with Fe₂O₃ and silica.

2. A zinc blende ore lot weighs 2400 kg and contains 10% ZnS, the rest gangue. After froth flotation the concentrate holds 80% ZnS, and all the ZnS is recovered. Find the mass of ZnS, the mass of the concentrate, the gangue left in the concentrate and the gangue removed.

3. A 800 kg sample has 25% magnetite and the rest non-magnetic gangue. The magnet picks up 90% of the magnetite and also drags 20 kg of gangue. Find the mass of the concentrate and the percent of magnetite in it, and the mass of magnetite lost with the gangue.

4. In a leaching calculation on paper, 40.8 g of Al₂O₃ is treated with enough hot concentrated NaOH solution (Al₂O₃ + 2NaOH → 2NaAlO₂ + H₂O). Find the mass of NaOH used, the mass of NaAlO₂ formed and the mass of water formed (Al = 27, O = 16, Na = 23, H = 1). Check that mass is conserved.

5. A tray holds a mixture of quartz sand (density 2.65 g per cm³), tiny bits of galena (7.5), and tiny bits of magnetite (5.2), all of the same size. Water is swirled gently in the tray. Which one is washed away first, and which one stays longest at the bottom? Give the order and the reason.

6. Three unlabelled powders A, B and C are the three: magnetite, zinc blende and quartz. Facts: (i) only magnetite is attracted by a magnet; (ii) in a flotation tank with pine oil and air, zinc blende goes up into the froth while quartz stays down; (iii) powder A is attracted by a magnet; (iv) powder B stays at the bottom of the flotation tank. Name A, B and C.

7. A mine sends 5000 tonnes of crude copper-pyrites ore per day. The ore has 4% CuFeS₂ and the flotation concentrate holds 40% CuFeS₂ (all recovered). Find the mass of the concentrate per day and how many 20-tonne trucks are needed for the ore and for the concentrate.

8. Why is the ore ground to a fine powder before froth flotation but a crushed, coarser sample is enough for magnetic separation?

9. Explain, using the word "amphoteric", why NaOH dissolves Al₂O₃ in bauxite but leaves Fe₂O₃ behind. What is the left-over solid called?

### Answers

1. (a) **Hydraulic washing (panning)**: gold bahut bhaari hai, ret halki. (b) **Froth flotation**: CuFeS₂ ek sulphide hai, tel se chipakta hai, quartz paani se geela hota hai. (c) **Magnetic separation**: magnetite magnet se chipakta hai. (d) **Hand-picking**: dikhne wala rang aur bade aakaar ka fark. (e) **Leaching** (NaOH ke ghol se): Al₂O₃ ghulta hai, Fe₂O₃ aur zyadatar baaki ashuddhiyan nahi (red mud).

2. ZnS = 10% of 2400 = **240 kg**; gangue = 2160 kg. Concentrate = 240 ÷ 0.80 = **300 kg**. Concentrate mein gangue = 300 − 240 = **60 kg**. Nikla hua gangue = 2160 − 60 = **2100 kg**. Jaanch: 2100 + 300 = 2400 ✅.

3. Magnetite = 25% of 800 = **200 kg**; gangue = 600 kg. Magnet ne 90% × 200 = **180 kg** magnetite uthayi aur 20 kg gangue: concentrate = 180 + 20 = **200 kg**. Magnetite ka percent = 180 ÷ 200 × 100 = **90%**. Jo magnetite gangue ke saath chhoot gayi = 200 − 180 = **20 kg**. Jaanch: concentrate 200 + baaki (580 gangue + 20 magnetite = 600) = 800 ✅.

4. Al₂O₃ = 102 g per mol, to 40.8 ÷ 102 = **0.4 mol**. 1 mol Al₂O₃ ke liye 2 mol NaOH, to NaOH = 0.8 mol × 40 = **32 g**. NaAlO₂ (23 + 27 + 32 = 82): 0.8 mol × 82 = **65.6 g**. Paani = 0.4 mol × 18 = **7.2 g**. Jaanch: 40.8 + 32 = 72.8 aur 65.6 + 7.2 = 72.8 ✅.

5. Quartz sabse halka hai (2.65) to **pehle bahta hai**. Phir magnetite (5.2). **Galena (7.5) sabse ant tak** tali par tikta hai. Order bahne ka: quartz, magnetite, galena. Kaaran: bhaari kan ko paani ki dhaar utni aasani se nahi bahati.

6. (iii) se **A = magnetite**. (ii) mein zinc blende upar aati hai aur quartz neeche rehta hai, to B (jo neeche baitha) **quartz** hai. Isliye **C = zinc blende**. 8. Flotation mein kan itne halke hone chahiye ki bubbles unhe utha saken, aur ore aur gangue ke kan alag-alag hone chahiye, isliye **barik peesna** zaroori hai. Magnetic separation mein bas itna chahiye ki magnetite ke tukde gangue se alag ho chuke hon, to coarser kuta hua sample bhi chalta hai.

9. **Al₂O₃ amphoteric** hai: wo acid ke saath bhi aur base (NaOH) ke saath bhi react karta hai, isliye NaOH usse react karke ghulne wala NaAlO₂ banata hai. **Fe₂O₃ basic oxide** hai, amphoteric nahi, isliye NaOH se koi reaction nahi aur wo ghulta nahi.
