# Topic 3: Garam Karke Oxide Banana: Roasting aur Calcination

Pichhle topic mein humne ore ko **saaf** kiya: gangue hataya, aur ab hamare paas **concentrated ore** hai, yaani ZnS, PbS, ZnCO₃ jaise yaugik ka lagbhag shuddh dher. Par dhatu abhi bhi **yaugik ke andar chhupi** hai. Metallurgy ke flow (ore, concentration, **oxide banana**, reduction, refining) mein ab agla padaav aata hai: is saaf ore ko **garam karke oxide** mein badalna. Kyun? Kyunki aage dhatu nikalne wale tarike (Topic 4) **oxide** ke saath sabse aasaani se chalte hain. Aaj hum dekhenge ki garam karne ke do tarike hain, **roasting** (hawa ke saath) aur **calcination** (kam ya bina hawa ke), aur ye bhi ki roasting ki ek **gandi, zehreeli gas** ka kya kiya jaata hai.

## Nakli sona aur chubhti gandh

School ke annual exhibition mein Mr. Lavate apne bhatije Kaivalya ko lekar science wing mein pahunche. Ek stall ke bahar bada board laga tha: "**Dekho, ye sona hai?**" Mez par chamakdar, peele-sunehre, kone-wale pathar ke tukde rakhe the. Kaivalya ki aankhein phail gayi.

"Chacha, itna saara sona! Ye to crorepati banne wali baat hai!"

Stall ki teacher muskuraayi. "Ye **sona nahi** hai, beta. Ise **fool's gold** (nakli sona) kehte hain, asli naam **iron pyrites** (FeS₂). Sone jaisa dikhta hai, par sone se bahut sasta aur bahut alag." Phir unhone kaha, "Ek pal ruko. Main aap sabko dikhati hoon ki ye sona kyun nahi hai. Lekin sab **teen-chaar kadam peeche** khade ho jaana, aur khidki ke paas wali jagah khaali karo."

Teacher ne **fume cupboard** (jisme gas bahar nikalne ka pankha lagta hai) ke andar ek chhoti si **chutki** pyrites ka powder ek patli glass ki nali (test tube) mein liya aur garam kiya. Kuch hi der mein powder ka rang badal kar laal-bhoora ho gaya, aur nali ke munh se ek **tez, chubhti, dam ghotne wali gandh** aayi, jaise jalte hue maachis ki teeli ki gandh, par kai guna zyada tez. Kaivalya ne turant naak dabaayi aur do kadam peeche hat gaya.

"Chacha, ye kya?" wo khaansa. "Sona to garam karne par aisa nahi karta!"

"Bilkul sahi," Mr. Lavate ne kaha. "Sona garam karne par **badalta hi nahi**, jaisa tumne Chapter 2 mein dekha tha ki sone-platinum ko hawa ka kuch nahi bigaadta. Ye pathar badal gaya, kyunki isme **sulphur** hai. Garam karne par sulphur hawa ki oxygen se mila aur **sulphur dioxide** gas bani. Wahi gas, jo Class 9 mein acid rain ki kahani mein thi. Aur ek mazedaar baat: **jo teacher ne abhi kiya, wahi kaam asli metallurgy ke factory mein bade pairane par hota hai**, bas sone ke naqli pathar par nahi, zinc, lead aur copper ke sulphide ores par."

Kaivalya ne naak abhi bhi dabaayi hui thi. "Par factory mein itni gandh... wahan ke log kaise?"

"Yehi to aaj ka sawaal hai," Mr. Lavate bole. "Chalo, dekhte hain ki **ore ko garam kyun karte hain**, kaise karte hain, aur us gandh wali gas ka kya karte hain."

> **Suraksha ki baat:** Pyrites ko garam karna **sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo)** hai, fume cupboard mein, teacher ke dwara. Sulphur dioxide **zehreeli** gas hai, aankh, naak aur phephdon ko jalati hai. Students **kabhi** koi sulphide ore ya khanij garam nahi karte, na ghar par, na lab mein bina teacher ke.

## Part A: Pyrites ne kya kiya? Ek equation

Teacher ke demo mein pyrites (FeS₂) hawa ki oxygen ke saath mila. Nateeja: ek **laal-bhoora oxide** (iron(III) oxide, Fe₂O₃) aur wo **chubhti gas** (SO₂):

4FeS₂ + 11O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃ + 8SO₂

Atom count (Jaanch): Fe 4=4; S 8=8; O 22 = 6 + 16 = 22 ✅.

Dhyan do: sulphur ne oxygen se milkar gas banayi, aur loha oxygen se milkar oxide bana. **Do** cheezein ek saath hui, aur ye kaam **hawa ke bina** nahi ho sakta tha. Yehi **roasting** ka seedha-saadha tareeka hai.

**Sona aur pyrites ka farq:** asli sona **narm** hota hai (chadar aur taar mein dhal jaata hai), jabki pyrites **kadak aur bhurbhura** hai: thokne par toot kar powder ban jaata hai. Ye farq jaanne ke liye ek safe test padhte hain.

### Safe activity: streak test (school lab mein teacher ke saath)

Teacher ek **bina chamak wali (unglazed) ceramic tile** ka peeche wala khurdura hissa lete hain. Pyrites ke tukde ko usse halke se ragda jaata hai: tile par **hara-kaala** nishaan padta hai. Asli sone ka nishaan **sunehra-peela** hota hai. Isse **streak** kehte hain. **Dekhoge:** dono dikhne mein ek jaise, par streak ka rang alag. Kaam khidki khuli rakh kar, hawadaar lab mein hota hai; ragadne ki dhool ko phoonkna ya soonghna nahi. Kaam ke baad haath dho lo. Pyrites ko garam nahi karna.

## Part B: Do tarike, ek maksad: oxide

Concentrated ore teen mukhya roop mein milta hai: **sulphide**, **carbonate**, aur **hydrated oxide**. Teeno mein ek hi cheez chahiye: shuddh **oxide**. Par tarika alag hota hai.

### 1. Roasting: sulphide ore + zyada hawa

Sulphide ore ko **barik powder** karke bhatti mein garam kiya jaata hai, jahan **hawa ki oxygen bahut maatra mein** pahunchti hai. Sulphur oxygen se milkar SO₂ gas ban kar nikalta hai, aur dhatu oxide mein badal jaati hai:

2ZnS + 3O₂ → 2ZnO + 2SO₂   (zinc blende se zinc oxide)

2PbS + 3O₂ → 2PbO + 2SO₂   (galena se lead(II) oxide)

2Cu₂S + 3O₂ → 2Cu₂O + 2SO₂   (copper(I) sulphide se copper(I) oxide)

2HgS + 3O₂ → 2HgO + 2SO₂   (cinnabar se mercury oxide; **sirf padhne ke liye**, mercury aur uske yaugik zehreele hain)

Atom count (Jaanch): ZnS: Zn 2=2, S 2=2, O 6 = 2 + 4 = 6 ✅. PbS: Pb 2=2, S 2=2, O 6 = 2 + 4 = 6 ✅. Cu₂S: Cu 4=4, S 2=2, O 6 = 2 + 4 = 6 ✅. HgS: Hg 2=2, S 2=2, O 6 = 2 + 4 = 6 ✅.

Dhyan do: **chaaron mein ek hi dhaancha** hai: sulphide + O₂ → oxide + SO₂. Isliye ek equation yaad ho to baaki khud ban jaate hain. (Ek chhoti baat: agar hawa kam ho, ya garmi bahut tez na ho, to kabhi-kabhi sulphate jaisa dusra yaugik bhi ban sakta hai, jaise ZnSO₄. Bhatti mein hawa aur garmi ka dhyaan rakhne ka ek kaaran yeh bhi hai.)

Roasting ka ek aur faayda: ore mein chhupi **kuch gandi impurity** (jaise sulphur, aur kuch arsenic jaise tatva) bhi **gas ban kar** nikal jaati hain. Isliye roasting ek tarah ki "safai" ka kaam bhi karti hai. Aur ek aur baat: sulphide ka jalna **khud garmi deta hai**, isliye ek baar shuru hone par bhatti ko bahut kam bahari fuel lagta hai (jaise ek baar jalne ke baad kagaz ki aag khud aage badhti hai).

### 2. Calcination: carbonate ya hydrated ore + kam hawa

Carbonate ya paani wale ore ko bhatti mein garam karne par wo **tootta hai**: carbonate se **CO₂** nikalti hai, aur hydrated ore se **paani ki bhaap**. Peeche **oxide** bachta hai. Isme oxygen ki zaroorat nahi, isliye hawa **kam ya bilkul nahi** di jaati:

ZnCO₃ → ZnO + CO₂   (calamine se zinc oxide)

Al₂O₃·2H₂O → Al₂O₃ + 2H₂O   (hydrated bauxite se aluminium oxide aur paani ki bhaap)

CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂   (limestone: **wahi Class 9 ki chuna bhatti**, [chemical-changes-and-reactions-part2.md](../../../class9/chemistry/chemical-changes-and-reactions/chemical-changes-and-reactions-part2.md) mein. Wahan ek hi example tha: aaj wahi idea ore par laga rahe hain.)

Atom count (Jaanch): ZnCO₃: Zn 1=1, C 1=1, O 3 = 1 + 2 = 3 ✅. Al₂O₃·2H₂O: Al 2=2, O 5 = 3 + 2 = 5, H 4=4 ✅. CaCO₃: Ca 1=1, C 1=1, O 3 = 1 + 2 ✅.

Ye **thermal decomposition** hi hai (Class 9): ek yaugik garmi se **tootkar** dusre yaugik banata hai. Farq itna hai ki yahan **ore** par laga rahe hain aur maksad **oxide** banana hai.

### Ek teacher demo: calcination ki ek jhalak (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo)

Teacher ek hawadaar lab mein (ya fume cupboard mein) ek **khule munh wali** test tube mein thoda **hara copper carbonate** (CuCO₃ ke jaisa) garam karte hain, aur tube ki nali ko **chuna-paani (lime water)** ki doosri tube mein dalte hain. Garmi band karne se **pehle** teacher nali ko chuna-paani se bahar nikaal lete hain, taaki thanda hota paani garam tube mein waapas na kheench jaaye.

CuCO₃ → CuO + CO₂

**Dekhoge:** hara powder **kaala** ho jaata hai (kaala CuO), aur chuna-paani **doodhiya** ho jaata hai, yaani CO₂ nikli (Atom count: Cu 1=1, C 1=1, O 3 = 1 + 2 ✅). Copper ke yaugik thodi maatra mein bhi nuksaan kar sakte hain, isliye students door se, goggles pehen kar dekhte hain, powder ko ungliyon se nahi chhoote, aur kaam ke baad haath dho lete hain.

## Part C: Roasting aur calcination: aamne-saamne

| Mudda | Roasting | Calcination |
|---|---|---|
| Kis ore ke liye | Sulphide ore (ZnS, PbS, Cu₂S) | Carbonate aur hydrated ore (ZnCO₃, Al₂O₃·2H₂O) |
| Hawa | **Zyada** hawa (oxygen chahiye) | **Kam ya bina** hawa (oxygen zaroori nahi) |
| Kya hota hai | Sulphur oxygen se jalta hai, dhatu oxide banti hai | Ore tootta hai, gas ya bhaap nikalti hai |
| Nikalne wali gas | **SO₂** (zehreeli, tez gandh) | **CO₂** ya **paani ki bhaap** (sulphur dioxide jaisi chubhti nahi) |
| Reaction ka prakaar | Oxidation (oxygen judti hai) | Thermal decomposition (todna) |
| Peeche bachta hai | Metal oxide | Metal oxide |
| Maksad | Oxide banana, impurity gas ban kar nikalna | Oxide banana, CO₂ ya paani hataana |

```
   ROASTING                                  CALCINATION
   (sulphide ore)                            (carbonate ya hydrated ore)

   +------------------------+                +------------------------+
   :                        :                :                        :
   :   ZnS  (powder)        :                :   ZnCO₃  (lump)        :
   :                        :                :                        :
   :  bahut garam           :                :  bahut garam           :
   +------------------------+                +------------------------+
        ^   ^   ^                                   (hawa: KAM / NAHI)
        :   :   :
     hawa (O₂) ZYADA                         neeche se sirf garmi
        :   :   :                                   :
        v   v   v                                   v
   ZnO  +  SO₂ gas                           ZnO  +  CO₂ gas
   (zehreeli, tez gandh)                     (zyada chubhti nahi)
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Roasting aur calcination: roasting mein **hawa zyada** (O₂ andar jaati hai) aur gas SO₂ nikalti hai; calcination mein **hawa kam ya nahi** aur gas CO₂ (ya paani ki bhaap) nikalti hai. Dono mein peeche **oxide** bachta hai.

> **Jaanch:** Ek ore ko garam karne par SO₂ jaisi chubhti gandh aati hai, to wo **sulphide** hai aur kaam **roasting** ka hai. ✅ Kyunki sulphur oxygen se jalta hai.

## Part D: Furnace andar se kaisa dikhta hai?

Roasting ka kaam bade furnace mein hota hai. Ek sabse saral roop: ek **lambi chaudi bhatti**, jisme ore ka powder ek patli parat mein **farsh (hearth)** par bichha rehta hai. Upar se **garam gas** guzarti hai, neeche ya side se **hawa** pahunchti hai, aur nikalne wali **SO₂ gas** ek **chimney (flue)** se ek alag jagah le jaayi jaati hai. Aajkal bahut jagah **fluidised bed** furnace bhi hoti hain, jisme hawa neeche se itni tezi se fenki jaati hai ki powder ek **ubalte paani ki tarah** hawa mein tairne lagta hai, aur powder ka har kan oxygen ko achhi tarah chhoota hai. (Ye sirf naam hai.)

```
               ore (powder) hopper se
                      :
                      v
            +----------------------+
   garam    :  ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~   :     SO₂ gas
   gas -->  :  ore ki patli parat  : --->  chimney se
            :  =================   :     (aage ka safar: Part F)
            :   HEARTH (farsh)     :
            +----------------------+
                  ^   ^   ^   ^
                  :   :   :   :
              hawa (O₂) andar
                      :
                      v
            roasted ore (oxide)
              neeche se nikalta hai
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Roasting furnace ka saral roop: ore ka powder hopper se girta hai, hearth par patli parat mein bichhta hai, garam gas aur hawa uspar se guzarte hain, SO₂ chimney se nikalti hai aur taiyaar oxide neeche se nikaal liya jaata hai. (Asli furnace isse kahin bade aur zyada jatil hote hain.)

Student ke liye ye furnace **sirf padhne ke liye** hai: koi bhi **ore ko furnace mein khud nahi garam karta**. Ye factory ke trained log, mashinon aur gas-trap ke saath karte hain.

## Part E: Oxide hi kyun chahiye?

Teen aasaan kaaran:

1. **Oxide ko reduce karna aasaan hai.** Aage (Topic 4) dekhenge: carbon (coke) ya carbon monoxide jaisi cheezein dhatu ke **oxide** se oxygen kheench leti hain. Sulphide ya carbonate ke saath ye seedha kaam nahi karta, ya bahut mehnga aur mushkil padta hai. Jaise zinc blende (ZnS) ko seedha carbon se nahi, pehle **ZnO** banakar hi aage badhate hain.
2. **Impurity nikal jaati hai.** Roasting mein sulphur gas ban kar jaata hai, calcination mein CO₂ ya paani ki bhaap: ye sab **gaye**, to bacha hua oxide **zyada shuddh** aur **zyada dhatu-wala** hota hai.
3. **Ore chhidradaar (porous) aur bhurbhura ho jaata hai.** Gas nikalne se ore mein chhote-chhote ched ban jaate hain, jisse aage ka reduction aasaani se aur tez hota hai.

Yahan dhyan rakhna: **oxide ban jaana abhi dhatu mil jaana nahi hai.** Dhatu to oxide ke andar **ab bhi oxygen ke saath bandhi** hai. Use oxygen se alag karna agla topic hai.

Ek chhoti yaad: Chapter 2 mein dekha tha ki ZnO, Al₂O₃ **amphoteric** oxide hain (acid aur base dono ke saath reaction). Metallurgy mein ye oxide hi Topic 5 mein bauxite ki kahani ka dil hain: wahan se **Al₂O₃** nikal kar aage badhenge.

## Part F: SO₂ ka kya karein? Teen raaste

Roasting se **bahut saari SO₂** nikalti hai. Ek hisaab (neeche Example 1 mein): 970 kg ZnS se **640 kg** SO₂. Ek bade smelter mein ye roz kai sau tonne ban sakti hai. Isse chimney se seedhe hawa mein chhod dena **bahut galat** hoga, kyunki:

- SO₂ **zehreeli** hai: aankh, naak, gale aur phephdon ko jalati hai (Class 9: atmospheric pollution).
- SO₂ hawa mein SO₃ aur phir **H₂SO₄** banti hai, aur **acid rain** ka kaaran banti hai: imaarat, marble, jheel, fasal sab ko nuksaan ([atmospheric-pollution-part2.md](../../../class9/chemistry/atmospheric-pollution/atmospheric-pollution-part2.md)):

2SO₂ + O₂ → 2SO₃, SO₃ + H₂O → H₂SO₄

Atom count (Jaanch): S 2=2, O 4 + 2 = 6 = 6 ✅; S 1=1, O 3 + 1 = 4 = 4, H 2=2 ✅.

Isliye aajkal SO₂ ke saath teen cheezein (ya inme se kuch) ki jaati hain:

1. **Rokna aur fasaana (scrubber):** gas ko chuna (lime) ya limestone ke ghol ya powder se guzarte hain, jisse SO₂ **thos namak** (CaSO₃ ya CaSO₄) ban kar chipak jaati hai aur hawa mein nahi jaati. Ye wahi scrubber hai jo Class 9 mein power plant ke liye aaya tha: SO₂ + CaCO₃ → CaSO₃ + CO₂ (Atom count: S 1=1, Ca 1=1, C 1=1, O 2 + 3 = 5 = 3 + 2 = 5 ✅).
2. **Kaam ki cheez banana:** wahi SO₂ jo pehle bekaar jaati thi, ek aur factory mein **sulphuric acid (H₂SO₄)** banane mein lagti hai. Sulphuric acid ek bahut kaam ka **industrial chemical** hai: khaad (fertiliser), battery, rang aur kai aur cheezon mein lagta hai. Iska poora tareeka **Chapter 9** mein padhenge; yahan sirf ek soch: gandi gas ko **kaam ki cheez** mein badal dena.
3. **Chimney ko oonchi aur kadi nigrani mein rakhna:** SO₂ ki maatra naapi jaati hai aur had se zyada hone par factory rokni padti hai.

```
                    SO₂ gas  (roasting ke baad)
                          :
         +----------------+----------------+
         :                :                :
         v                v                v
   Seedha hawa      Scrubber mein      Sulphuric acid
   mein chhodi       pakadi gayi        banane ke kaam
   (GALAT)           (SAHI)             (SABSE ACHHA)
         :                :                :
         v                v                v
   Zehar: aankh,     Thos namak          H₂SO₄
   phephde           (CaSO₃, CaSO₄)      (khaad, battery,
   Acid rain:        hawa saaf           rang mein kaam)
   imaarat, jheel
```

> **Figure 3.3** – SO₂ ke teen raaste: seedha hawa mein (zehar aur acid rain, galat), scrubber mein fasaana (hawa saaf), ya sulphuric acid banana (kaam ki cheez). Aajkal roasting ki gas ko pakad kar aage kaam mein lagana hi sahi tareeka maana jaata hai.

> **Jaanch:** Calcination mein bhi sulphur dioxide chhodna zaroori hai. ❌ Calcination mein carbonate ya hydrated ore garam hota hai: CO₂ ya paani ki bhaap nikalti hai, SO₂ nahi (SO₂ sirf sulphide ki roasting se aati hai).

## Part G: Solved Examples

### Example 1. Zinc blende ki roasting

**Sawaal:** 970 kg shuddh ZnS ko hawa mein poori tarah roast kiya jaata hai (2ZnS + 3O₂ → 2ZnO + 2SO₂). Kitna ZnO aur kitni SO₂ banegi, aur kitni oxygen lagegi? (Zn = 65, S = 32, O = 16.)

**Hal:**
- ZnS = 65 + 32 = **97**; ZnO = 65 + 16 = **81**; SO₂ = 32 + 32 = **64**; O₂ = **32**.
- Equation ke hisaab se: 2 × 97 = **194** (ZnS) : 3 × 32 = **96** (O₂) : 2 × 81 = **162** (ZnO) : 2 × 64 = **128** (SO₂).
- 970 ÷ 194 = **5** guna. To ZnO = 5 × 162 = **810 kg**; SO₂ = 5 × 128 = **640 kg**; O₂ = 5 × 96 = **480 kg**.

Jaanch: 970 + 480 = 1450; 810 + 640 = 1450 ✅.

**Nateeja:** 970 kg ZnS se **810 kg** ZnO aur **640 kg** SO₂. Dhyan do: ZnO ka mass ZnS se **kam** hai (810 < 970), bhale hi oxygen judi hai, kyunki zyada bhaari **sulphur gas ban kar** nikal gaya.

### Example 2. Galena aur sulphuric acid

**Sawaal:** 478 kg shuddh PbS roast hoti hai (2PbS + 3O₂ → 2PbO + 2SO₂). (a) Kitna PbO banega? (b) Kitni SO₂ nikalegi? (c) Agar saari SO₂ ko sulphuric acid mein badal diya jaaye (har 64 kg SO₂ se 98 kg H₂SO₄), to kitna H₂SO₄ milega? (Pb = 207, S = 32, O = 16, H = 1.)

**Hal:**
- PbS = 207 + 32 = **239**; PbO = 207 + 16 = **223**; SO₂ = **64**.
- 2 × 239 = **478** (PbS) : 2 × 223 = **446** (PbO) : 2 × 64 = **128** (SO₂). 478 kg ka matlab ek poora equation, yaani guna 1.
- (a) PbO = **446 kg**. (b) SO₂ = **128 kg**.
- (c) 128 ÷ 64 = 2 guna, to H₂SO₄ = 2 × 98 = **196 kg**. (H₂SO₄ = 2 + 32 + 64 = 98.)

Jaanch (reaction ka): O₂ = 3 × 32 = 96 kg. 478 + 96 = 574; 446 + 128 = 574 ✅.

**Nateeja:** 478 kg galena se 446 kg lead oxide **aur** 196 kg sulphuric acid ka mauka: gandi gas ek kaam ka product ban gayi. (Lead aur uske yaugik zehreele hain: ye sirf kaagaz ka hisaab hai.)

### Example 3. Calamine ki calcination

**Sawaal:** 250 kg shuddh ZnCO₃ ko calcine kiya jaata hai (ZnCO₃ → ZnO + CO₂). Kitna ZnO milega aur kitni CO₂ nikalegi? (Zn = 65, C = 12, O = 16.)

**Hal:** ZnCO₃ = 65 + 12 + 48 = **125**; ZnO = **81**; CO₂ = 12 + 32 = **44**. Ratio 125 : 81 : 44. 250 ÷ 125 = **2** guna. ZnO = 2 × 81 = **162 kg**; CO₂ = 2 × 44 = **88 kg**.

Jaanch: 162 + 88 = 250 ✅.

**Nateeja:** 250 kg calamine se **162 kg** ZnO. Dhyan do: ZnS se roasting ka ZnO (Example 1) aur ZnCO₃ se calcination ka ZnO **ek hi cheez** hai; bas raasta alag.

### Example 4. Hydrated ore ka paani

**Sawaal:** 207 kg shuddh Al₂O₃·2H₂O ko calcine kiya jaata hai. Kitna Al₂O₃ milega aur kitna paani (bhaap) nikalega? Ore ka mass kitne percent kam hua? (Al = 27, O = 16, H = 1.)

**Hal:** Al₂O₃·2H₂O = 102 + 36 = **138**; Al₂O₃ = **102**; 2H₂O = **36**. 207 ÷ 138 = **1.5** guna. Al₂O₃ = 1.5 × 102 = **153 kg**; paani = 1.5 × 36 = **54 kg**.

Jaanch: 153 + 54 = 207 ✅. Mass ka ghatna: 36 ÷ 138 × 100 = lagbhag **26.1%**.

**Nateeja:** 207 kg se **153 kg** Al₂O₃ aur 54 kg paani ki bhaap, yaani lagbhag **26%** mass ghat gaya.

### Example 5. Roasting ya calcination? Reasoning

**Sawaal:** Chaar concentrated ores diye gaye hain: (a) PbS (b) ZnCO₃ (c) Cu₂S (d) Al₂O₃·2H₂O. Har ek ke liye batao ki roasting chalega ya calcination, aur ek line mein kaaran do.

**Hal:**

| Ore | Kis kism ka | Kaam | Kaaran |
|---|---|---|---|
| PbS | Sulphide | Roasting | Sulphur ko oxygen se jalana hai, SO₂ ke roop mein nikalna hai |
| ZnCO₃ | Carbonate | Calcination | Garmi se CO₂ nikalna, oxygen ki zaroorat nahi |
| Cu₂S | Sulphide | Roasting | Wahi sulphide wala dhaancha: sulphide + O₂ → oxide + SO₂ |
| Al₂O₃·2H₂O | Hydrated | Calcination | Garmi se paani ki bhaap nikalna |

**Nateeja:** pehchaan ka shortcut: **S** hai (sulphide) to **roasting**; **CO₃** ya **H₂O** hai to **calcination**.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Roasting aur calcination ek hi cheez ke do naam hain"**
Nahi. Demonstration (padhne ke liye, ek mini-example): do ore, ek hi tarah ki garmi. **ZnS** garam karo hawa mein: chubhti gandh aati hai (SO₂). **ZnCO₃** garam karo: gas nikalti hai par gandh jaisi chubhti nahi; chuna-paani doodhiya karti hai (CO₂). Do ores, do alag gasein, do alag naam: ek mein oxygen **jalane** ka kaam karti hai (roasting), doosre mein ore **khud tootta** hai (calcination).

**Galti 2: "Calcination mein bhi bahut hawa chahiye"**
Nahi. Calcination mein **oxygen ki zaroorat nahi**. Demonstration: upar ke teacher demo mein hara copper carbonate ek patli test tube mein garam hua, jiske andar hawa bahut kam thi aur nikalti CO₂ ne bachi hawa ko bhi bahar dhakel diya, aur CuCO₃ → CuO + CO₂ phir bhi ho gaya. Oxygen ke bina bhi reaction chala, kyunki wo todna hai, jalna nahi.

**Galti 3: "Roasting mein oxygen judti hai, to ore ka mass badhna chahiye"**
Nahi, aksar **ghatta** hai. Demonstration: 194 kg ZnS roast karne par 162 kg ZnO bacha (Example 1 ka hisaab): 32 kg ki kami, kyunki **sulphur** (jo oxygen se bhaari tha) SO₂ ban kar nikal gaya. Oxygen judi bhi, par ek bhaari cheez ke jaane ka asar bada tha. Class 9 mein kuch **dhatu** ke jalne par mass badhta tha; yahan ore ke saath ulta ho sakta hai.

**Galti 4: "SO₂ chimney se chhod do, hawa bahut badi hai, kuch nahi hoga"**
Nahi. Demonstration: Example 1 ki ek batch mein 640 kg SO₂. Agar aisi 100 batch ek mahine mein hon, to **64 tonne** SO₂ hawa mein, jo baarish ke saath khatti ho kar kheton aur jheel mein girti hai. "Hawa badi hai" se gas **gayab** nahi hoti; wo bas door ja kar gir jaati hai (Class 9: acid rain kai sau kilometre door tak jaa sakti hai).

**Galti 5: "Oxide ban gaya, matlab dhatu mil gayi"**
Nahi. Demonstration: ZnO ko hath mein lo to wo safed **powder** hai (kai jagah dawa aur cream mein bhi), koi chamakta hua zinc nahi. Dhatu ki chamak aur taar banne wale gun tab aate hain jab oxygen **hat** jaaye, aur wo **reduction** ka kaam hai (Topic 4).

## Quick Recap

- Concentrated ore ko pehle **oxide** mein badalte hain, kyunki oxide ko reduce karna aasaan hai.
- **Roasting:** sulphide ore + **zyada hawa** → oxide + **SO₂**. 2ZnS + 3O₂ → 2ZnO + 2SO₂; 2PbS + 3O₂ → 2PbO + 2SO₂; 2Cu₂S + 3O₂ → 2Cu₂O + 2SO₂.
- **Calcination:** carbonate ya hydrated ore + **kam ya bina hawa** → oxide + CO₂ ya paani ki bhaap. ZnCO₃ → ZnO + CO₂; Al₂O₃·2H₂O → Al₂O₃ + 2H₂O; CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂.
- Pehchaan: **S** hai to roasting; **CO₃** ya **H₂O** hai to calcination.
- SO₂ **zehreeli** hai aur acid rain banati hai: use **scrubber** se fasaate hain ya **sulphuric acid** banane mein lagate hain (Chapter 9).
- Ore ko furnace mein garam karna **sirf padhne ke liye**; students kabhi sulphide ya khanij nahi bhunte.
- Mass ka hisaab: 970 kg ZnS → 810 kg ZnO + 640 kg SO₂; 250 kg ZnCO₃ → 162 kg ZnO + 88 kg CO₂.

2. (a) FeS₂: **roasting** (sulphide, sulphur ko jalana hai). (b) MgCO₃: **calcination** (carbonate, CO₂ nikalni hai). (c) Cu₂S: **roasting** (sulphide). (d) PbCO₃: **calcination** (carbonate; ek zehreeli lead yaugik, isliye bhi padhne ke liye). (e) Malachite: **calcination** (carbonate aur paani wale OH ke saath, garmi se CO₂ aur paani nikalte hain). (f) HgS: **roasting** (sulphide; sirf padhne ke liye).

3. Cu₂S = 2 × 63.5 + 32 = **159**; Cu₂O = 127 + 16 = **143**; SO₂ = **64**. Equation: 2 × 159 = 318 (Cu₂S) : 96 (O₂) : 2 × 143 = 286 (Cu₂O) : 128 (SO₂). 318 kg se ek poora equation (guna 1): **Cu₂O = 286 kg, SO₂ = 128 kg, O₂ = 96 kg**. Jaanch: 318 + 96 = 414; 286 + 128 = 414 ✅.

4. MgCO₃ = 24 + 12 + 48 = **84**; MgO = **40**; CO₂ = **44**. 42 ÷ 84 = **0.5** guna. **MgO = 20 kg, CO₂ = 22 kg** (20 + 22 = 42 ✅). Mass ka ghatna: 22 ÷ 42 × 100 = lagbhag **52.4%** (ya 44 ÷ 84 same).

5. FeS₂ = 56 + 64 = **120**; Fe₂O₃ = 112 + 48 = **160**; SO₂ = **64**; O₂ = 32. To FeS₂ se prati kg lagbhag 1.07 kg SO₂ (512 ÷ 480), Cu₂S se prati kg lagbhag 0.40 kg SO₂ (128 ÷ 318). **FeS₂ zyada SO₂ deta hai**, isliye uski roasting ki factory ko **bada gas-trap aur sulphuric acid plant** chahiye.
