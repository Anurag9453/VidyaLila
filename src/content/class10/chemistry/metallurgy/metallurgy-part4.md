# Topic 4: Oxide Se Dhatu Tak: Reduction

Topic 3 mein hum ne ore ko garam karke **oxide** bana liya (sulphide ko roast karke, carbonate ko calcine karke). Par oxide abhi dhatu nahi hai: ZnO ek safed powder hai, Fe₂O₃ ek laal-bhura patthar. Dhatu paane ke liye hume oxide se **oxygen nikalna** hai. Class 9 mein is kaam ka naam tha **reduction**. Aaj ka sawaal: **kaun si dhatu ke liye kaun sa tareeka**, aur uska jawab hai aapki purani dost, reactivity series.

## Part A: Reduction kya hai, ek line mein (aur kaun kaun reducing agent ban sakta hai)

Class 9 ([oxidation aur reduction](../../../class9/chemistry/chemical-changes-and-reactions/chemical-changes-and-reactions-part5.md)) se yaad karo: jis padarth ka **oxygen chala jaaye** wo **reduce** hota hai, aur jo oxygen le le wo **reducing agent** hai (khud oxidise hota hai). Dhatu nikalne ka matlab: oxide **reduce** karna. Reducing agent ke liye teen kisam ki cheezein kaam aati hain.

- **Carbon (coke) aur carbon monoxide**: sasta, bahut milta hai, par sirf **beech wali** dhatuon ke liye.
- **Zyada reactive dhatu** (jaise aluminium): thermite jaisa displacement, mehnga, par kai awkward dhatuon ke liye kaam ka.
- **Bijli (electrolysis)**: sabse zyada taaqatwar "reducing agent", kyunki bijli electron seedha deti hai. **Sabse upar** wali dhatuon ke liye.

(Hydrogen bhi Class 9 mein oxide ko reduce kar chuka hai, par bade paimane par mehnga aur khatarnak hota hai, isliye dhatu-udyog mein kam hi aata hai.)

Ab dekhte hain ki teen "group" kaise bante hain.

```
   REACTIVITY SERIES (upar se neeche: kam hoti reactivity)

   GROUP 1: BAHUT REACTIVE        K  Na  Ca  Mg  Al
   oxygen ko bahut kasa ke
   pakadti hain
        ==> tareeka: pighle chloride ya oxide ki ELECTROLYSIS

   GROUP 2: BEECH WALI            Zn  Fe  Sn  Pb
   oxygen ko madhyam taaqat
   se pakadti hain
        ==> tareeka: oxide banao (Topic 3), phir CARBON
            ya CARBON MONOXIDE se REDUCTION

   GROUP 3: KAM REACTIVE          Cu  Hg  Ag  Au
   oxygen ko halke se
   pakadti hain
        ==> tareeka: sirf garam karna ya mili hi
            mili hai (Ag, Au aksar free)
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Reactivity series ke teen group aur har group ka tareeka. Jitni upar ki dhatu, utna zyada taaqatwar tareeka chahiye (bijli); jitni neeche, utna kam (sirf garam karna).

## Part B: Group 3, kam reactive dhatu, sirf garam karna kaafi

Seedhi ke bilkul neeche ki dhatu ke oxide itne **kamzor** hain ki sirf **garam karne se** hi tootkar dhatu aur oxygen ban jaate hain. Kisi reducing agent ki zaroorat nahi.

**Mercury** (sirf padhne ke liye): mercury ka ore cinnabar HgS hai. Pehle roast karke HgO banta hai, aur HgO ko aur garam karne par:

2HgO → 2Hg + O₂

Atom ginti: Hg 2 = 2, O 2 = 2 ✅.

> **Warning:** Mercury aur uski bhaap **zehreeli** hain. Ye sirf padhne ke liye hai; students mercury ya iske compound ko kabhi na chhoote, na garam karein. Ye kaam sirf bahut surakshit, band system mein trained log karte hain.

**Copper** ki kahani thodi alag hai, kyunki copper ka mukhya ore sulphide hai (copper pyrites CuFeS₂, ya Cu₂S). Topic 3 ke roasting se Cu₂S ka **ek hissa** Cu₂O ban jaata hai (poora nahi). Fir hawa band karke aur garam karte hain: bacha hua Cu₂S aur bana hua Cu₂O **aapas mein hi** ek dusre ko reduce kar dete hain, bina carbon ke:

Cu₂S + 2Cu₂O → 6Cu + SO₂

Atom ginti: Cu (2 + 4) = 6 = 6, S 1 = 1, O 2 = 2 ✅. Isse **auto-reduction** kehte hain: Cu₂S mein sulphur ne oxygen liya (SO₂ bana), aur Cu₂O ka oxygen gaya (Cu bana). (Pighla hua kaccha copper "blister copper" kehlata hai aur use refining ki zaroorat padti hai: Topic 6.)

**Gold** (aur kabhi-kabhi **silver**) itne kam reactive hain ki wo zameen mein **free (native)** milte hain, unhe reduce karna nahi padta: bas gravity se alag karke (Topic 2) chhaanna hota hai.

## Part C: Group 2, beech wali dhatu, carbon ka kamaal

Zn, Fe, Sn, Pb aur isi tarah ki dhatu ke oxide ko **carbon** ya **carbon monoxide** reduce kar sakte hain. Carbon wahi kaam karta hai jo seedhi mein koi upar wali dhatu karti: oxide se oxygen kheench leta hai.

Pehle ZnS ko roast karke ZnO banaya (Topic 3, 2ZnS + 3O₂ → 2ZnO + 2SO₂), ab:

ZnO + C → Zn + CO

Atom ginti: Zn 1 = 1, O 1 = 1, C 1 = 1 ✅. Isi tarah:

PbO + C → Pb + CO

Atom ginti: Pb 1 = 1, O 1 = 1, C 1 = 1 ✅. (Lead aur uske yaugik zehreele hain: sirf padhne ke liye.) Aur lohe ke liye:

Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂

Atom ginti: Fe 2 = 2, C 3 = 3, O (3 + 3) = 6 = (3 × 2) = 6 ✅. Yahan reducing agent **carbon monoxide** hai: CO ne oxygen liya aur CO₂ bana.

Tin ke liye bhi wahi (SnO₂ + 2C → Sn + 2CO). Atom ginti: Sn 1 = 1, O 2 = 2, C 2 = 2 ✅.

> **Activity 4.1 (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo): CuO aur carbon.** Teacher kaale CuO powder aur carbon powder ka mixture ek **hard glass** test tube mein garam karte hain (2CuO + C → 2Cu + CO₂, Atom ginti: Cu 2 = 2, O 2 = 2, C 1 = 1, O 2 = 2 ✅), aur nikalti gas ko chuna paani mein bahaate hain jo **doodhiya** ho jaata hai. Ye demo hawadaar lab ya fume cupboard mein hota hai (thodi CO bhi ban sakti hai, jo zehreeli hai), aur garmi band karne se pehle teacher nali ko chuna paani se bahar nikaal lete hain. Students bunsen burner ya garam test tube ko **na chhoote**, safety chashme aur doori rakhein; test tube mein bacha laal-bhoora copper kuch minute baad dekha jaata hai.

Carbon ye kaam **Group 1 ki dhatuon** ke liye nahi kar paata: aage dekho.

## Part D: Blast furnace, lohe ki bhatti (sirf padhne ke liye)

Sabse bada udyog: iron. Iron ka ore haematite Fe₂O₃ hai (lagbhag 70% iron by mass, 112 ÷ 160 = 0.70), kabhi kabhi magnetite Fe₃O₄. Isse loha banane ki bhatti **blast furnace** kehlati hai. Ye ek khadi, bahut lambi, andar se refractory eent se bani chimney hai, jisme **upar se teen cheezein** daali jaati hain:

1. **Iron ore** (Fe₂O₃, saath mein gangue jisme mukhya roop se silica SiO₂ hota hai).
2. **Coke** (carbon): eendhan bhi aur reducing agent bhi banane ka kaam.
3. **Limestone** (CaCO₃): flux.

Neeche se **garam hawa** (bahut tez dhaar se, isliye "blast") phoonki jaati hai.

```
              ore + coke + limestone
                        :
                        v
                  +-----------+
   gas bahar  <-- :           :   ZONE 1 (upar): lagbhag 200 se 400 °C
   (CO, CO₂, N₂)  :  sukhna   :   ore sookhta, nami nikal jaati hai
                 /             \
                /  ZONE 2       \ lagbhag 400 se 900 °C
               /   Fe₂O₃ + 3CO   \ Fe₂O₃ ko CO reduce karta hai
              /    ---> 2Fe + 3CO₂\ limestone: CaCO₃ ---> CaO + CO₂
             /                     \
            /  ZONE 3               \ lagbhag 900 se 1500 °C
           /   CO₂ + C ---> 2CO      \ CO banti hai, loha pighalne lagta hai
          /                           \
         /  ZONE 4 (neeche, garam)     \ lagbhag 1500 se 2000 °C
        /   C + O₂ ---> CO₂             \ coke jalta hai, garmi milti hai
       /    CaO + SiO₂ ---> CaSiO₃       \ slag banta hai
        \                               /
 garam ==>\                           / <== garam hawa
 hawa      :  SLAG (halka, upar)     :  <-- slag nikalne ka chhed
 (blast)   :-------------------------:
           :  PIGHLA LOHA (bhaari)   :  <-- loha nikalne ka chhed
           :_________________________:
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Blast furnace ka kaat (cross-section), zone-wise. Upar se ore, coke, limestone girte hain; neeche, pighle loha aur slag ke theek upar, garam hawa phoonki jaati hai. Gas upar se nikalti hai. Neeche pighla loha bhaari hone ke karan tali par baithta hai aur halka slag uske upar tairta hai; dono ke alag-alag nikasi dwar hote hain. Taapman lagbhag hain aur furnace ke hisaab se badalte hain.

### Furnace ke andar ki kahani, chhote chhote kadam mein

1. **Neeche, hawa aur coke:** garam hawa ka oxygen coke se milkar jalta hai aur bahut garmi nikalta hai.

   C + O₂ → CO₂

   Atom ginti: C 1 = 1, O 2 = 2 ✅.
2. **Upar uthti hui CO₂ garam coke se milti hai** aur **carbon monoxide** ban jaati hai, jo hi asli reducing agent hai:

   CO₂ + C → 2CO

   Atom ginti: C (1 + 1) = 2 = 2, O 2 = 2 ✅.
3. **CO, ore ko reduce karti hai** (upar ki taraf, kam garam hisse mein):

   Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂

   Ye wahi reaction hai jo hum Part C mein dekh chuke. (Class 9 ke Fe₂O₃ + 3CO ka bada roop.)
4. **Limestone ka kaam (flux):** garmi se CaCO₃ toot jaata hai:

   CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂

   Atom ginti: Ca 1 = 1, C 1 = 1, O 3 = (1 + 2) = 3 ✅. Ye CaO ek **basic oxide** hai aur gangue ki silica SiO₂ ek **acidic oxide** hai (Class 9 aur Chapter 1 ka acid-base yaad karo). Dono mil jaate hain:

   CaO + SiO₂ → CaSiO₃

   Atom ginti: Ca 1 = 1, Si 1 = 1, O (1 + 2) = 3 = 3 ✅. Ye **CaSiO₃ slag** hai.

**Slag kyun upar tairta hai aur ye kitna zaroori hai?** Gangue ko alag karna hi to samasya thi: silica ka melting point bahut zyada hota hai. Lekin CaO ke saath milkar wo **kam taapman par pighalne wala** slag banta hai, jo **halka** hai (density kam) aur pighle loha ke **upar tairta** hai. Isliye slag ek parat ki tarah loha ko hawa ke oxygen se bhi bachata hai, aur dono ko alag-alag chhedon se nikaal liya jaata hai. Ye **flux** ka idea hai: ek aisi cheez daalo jo gangue ko pighla ke dhatu se alag kar de.

**Slag ka kya hota hai?** Ye kachra nahi hai: sadak banane aur cement ke kaarkhane mein kaam aata hai.

### Pig iron aur aage ki kahani

Furnace se nikla loha **pig iron** hai: usme lagbhag **4% carbon** aur kuch aur ashuddhiyan hoti hain. Aisa loha **bhurbhura (brittle)** hota hai, isliye aksar use ek aur kadam mein sudhaar kar **steel** banaya jaata hai (carbon ko kam karke). Steel aur alloys ke bare mein Topic 6 mein padhenge.

## Part E: Group 1, bahut reactive dhatu: carbon kaam nahi karta

Ab asli sawaal. Agar carbon itna achha reducing agent hai to Al₂O₃ ya MgO ya Na₂O ko carbon se kyun nahi reduce kar lete?

**Jawab (seedhi se):** thermite ([metals-and-non-metals-part4.md](../metals-and-non-metals/metals-and-non-metals-part4.md)) yaad karo: Al ne Fe₂O₃ se oxygen le liya kyunki Al, Fe se zyada reactive hai. Yani **oxygen ko pakadne ki taaqat** dhatu ki reactivity se judi hai: zyada reactive dhatu **oxygen ko zyada kasa ke** pakadti hai. K, Na, Ca, Mg, Al ye taaqat bahut zyada rakhte hain. Carbon ki taaqat in sab ke upar nahi hai: carbon ki taaqat Zn, Fe, Pb ke oxide ko todne jitni hai, Al aur Mg ke oxide ko todne jitni **nahi** (is chapter ke level par hum yahi yaad rakhte hain: carbon ki jagah Al aur Zn ke beech kahin hai). Isliye carbon in dhatuon ke oxide se oxygen **nahi chheen sakta**.

To in dhatuon ke liye carbon se **zyada taaqatwar** reducing agent chahiye. Inse zyada reactive koi dhatu milti nahi (ya bahut mehngi padti hai). To bachta hai **bijli**.

### Electrolysis ek jhalak (Chapter 4 mein poori kahani)

Class 9 ([chemical-bonding-part4.md](../../../class9/chemistry/chemical-bonding/chemical-bonding-part4.md)) mein padha tha ki **pighla hua ionic compound bijli chalata hai**, kyunki uske ions ghoom sakte hain. Isi par ye tareeka tika hai: pighle compound mein bijli guzaarte hain.

- **Cathode (negative plate)** par positive ion electron leta hai aur dhatu banta hai.
- **Anode (positive plate)** par negative ion electron deta hai aur gas banta hai.

**Sodium, pighle sodium chloride se** (sirf padhne ke liye: pighle namak aur pighli dhatu bahut khatarnak):

- Cathode: Na⁺ + e⁻ → Na
- Anode: 2Cl⁻ → Cl₂ + 2e⁻
- Kul: 2NaCl → 2Na + Cl₂

Atom ginti: Na 2 = 2, Cl 2 = 2 ✅. Charge ginti: left par 0, right par 0 ✅. Chlorine gas **zehreeli** hai.

**Magnesium, pighle magnesium chloride se** (sirf padhne ke liye): isi tarah Mg²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Mg (cathode) aur 2Cl⁻ → Cl₂ + 2e⁻ (anode), kul: MgCl₂ → Mg + Cl₂. Atom ginti: Mg 1 = 1, Cl 2 = 2 ✅.

**Aluminium** isi tarah **pighle Al₂O₃** se banta hai, par uski kahani bahut dilchasp hai: Topic 5 mein.

(Electrolysis ka poora siddhant, kaun sa ion pehle chhoot-ta hai, aur zyada examples Chapter 4 "Electrolysis" mein aayenge. Yahan sirf "ek jhalak" hai: kaun sa tareeka, kyun.)

**Pighla compound hi kyun, sookha ya ghol kyun nahi?** Namak ke **paani wale ghol** mein dhatu (sodium) nahi banti kyunki ban kar wo paani se turant react kar jaati (Chapter 2): isliye pighle, paani-rahit compound ki zaroorat hai. Aur pighlane ke liye **bahut garmi** lagti hai: isi liye ye tareeka mehnga padta hai.

## Part F: Beech ka raasta: zyada reactive dhatu se reduction (thermite wali vidhi)

Kuch dhatuon (jaise Mn, Cr) ke liye carbon se kaam banta nahi ya dhatu mein carbon mil kar use kharab kar deta hai, aur bijli bhi mehngi padegi. Unke liye **zyada reactive dhatu** (aluminium) ko reducing agent banate hain. Ye wahi thermite hai jo hum Chapter 2 mein dekh chuke:

Fe₂O₃ + 2Al → Al₂O₃ + 2Fe (ek line ka recall, railway ki patriyan jodne ka kaam)

Isi tarah:

- 3MnO₂ + 4Al → 3Mn + 2Al₂O₃ (manganese). Atom ginti: Mn 3 = 3, O 6 = 6, Al 4 = 4 ✅.
- Cr₂O₃ + 2Al → Al₂O₃ + 2Cr (chromium). Atom ginti: Cr 2 = 2, O 3 = 3, Al 2 = 2 ✅.

Ye reactions **bahut ooncha taapman** paida karte hain (pighli dhatu banti hai), isliye sirf **trained log** aur surakshit furnace mein hote hain; students ke liye **sirf padhne ke liye**. (Aluminium mehnga padta hai, isliye ye tareeka sirf khaas dhatuon ke liye chunte hain, jaise Mn, Cr, aur railway-weld mein loha.)

## Part H: Solved examples (sab kagaz par; sirf padhne ke liye ki bhatti ke hisaab)

Atomic mass: C 12, O 16, Fe 56, Zn 65, Cl 35.5, Ca 40, Si 28.

### Solved Example 1: Haematite se iron

**Sawaal:** Ek haematite ore ka 1000 kg dher 80% Fe₂O₃ rakhta hai (baaki gangue). Blast furnace mein Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂ se kitna iron banega, kitni CO lagegi, kitni CO₂ banegi? Agar nikla pig iron 96% iron ka ho to pig iron ka mass kya hoga?

**Hal:**
1. Fe₂O₃ ka mass = 1000 × 0.80 = 800 kg. Fe₂O₃ ka molar mass = 2 × 56 + 3 × 16 = 160. Isliye 800 kg = 800 ÷ 160 = **5 kmol** (kilo-mole).
2. Equation: 1 mol Fe₂O₃ → 2 mol Fe, 3 mol CO → 3 mol CO₂. To Fe = 2 × 5 = 10 kmol = 10 × 56 = **560 kg**. CO = 3 × 5 = 15 kmol × 28 = **420 kg**. CO₂ = 15 × 44 = **660 kg**.
3. Jaanch: 800 + 420 = 1220 kg left, 560 + 660 = 1220 kg right ✅.
4. Pig iron mein 96% Fe ho to total pig iron = 560 ÷ 0.96 = **lagbhag 583 kg**. (Baaki 23 kg lagbhag carbon aadi.)

**Matlab:** ek tonne ore se lagbhag 0.56 tonne iron milta hai (shuddh).

### Solved Example 2: Zinc oxide se zinc

**Sawaal:** 405 kg ZnO ko carbon se reduce kiya jaata hai: ZnO + C → Zn + CO. Zn, carbon aur CO ka mass batao.

**Hal:** ZnO = 65 + 16 = 81. 405 ÷ 81 = **5 kmol**. 1 : 1 : 1 : 1 anupaat se: Zn = 5 × 65 = **325 kg**, C = 5 × 12 = **60 kg**, CO = 5 × 28 = **140 kg**. Jaanch: 405 + 60 = 465 kg, 325 + 140 = 465 kg ✅.

### Solved Example 4: Carbon Al₂O₃ ko kyun nahi reduce karta?

**Sawaal:** Ek dost kehta hai: "Hum Fe₂O₃ ko carbon se reduce karte hain, to bauxite se Al₂O₃ ko bhi carbon se kyun nahi?" Jawab do.

**Hal (teen kadam):**
1. Thermite se hum jaante hain ki Al, Fe₂O₃ se oxygen le leta hai (Al zyada reactive hai). Yani Al ka oxygen se baandh Fe se **mazboot** hai.
2. Carbon ka baandh oxygen se Fe aur Zn jitna kamzor hai (isliye carbon Fe₂O₃ aur ZnO se oxygen le leta hai), par Al jitna **mazboot nahi**.
3. Isliye carbon Al₂O₃ se oxygen kheench nahi sakta. Chahiye ek aisa reducing agent jo Al se bhi zyada taaqatwar ho, aur wo hai bijli (electrolysis).

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Carbon har dhatu ke liye kaam karta hai."**
Maano ek student kehta hai: "Bauxite ko coke ke saath garam karenge aur aluminium mil jaayega." Kaagaz par equation balance bhi kar lo: Al₂O₃ + 3C → 2Al + 3CO. Par balance hona reaction hone ki guarantee nahi: aam bhatti ke taapman par ye reaction chalta hi nahi. Seedhi mein Al, Zn aur Fe se upar hai, aur Al, Fe ke oxide ko thermite mein hara chuka hai; carbon sirf Zn, Fe, Pb jaise **beech** wale ke liye theek hai. Aluminium ke liye bijli chahiye (Topic 5).

**Galti 2: "Slag kachra hai."**
Ek student pooche: "Slag fenk dete honge?" Sahi baat: slag **kaam ka** hai: sadak, cement. Aur bhatti ke andar slag ek **parat** hai jo pighle loha ko oxygen se bachati hai aur gangue ko alag karti hai. Agar flux (limestone) na daalein to silica pighalti nahi aur bhatti ruk jaayegi.

**Galti 3: "Blast furnace mein coke seedha Fe₂O₃ ko kheench leta hai."**
Zyaadatar kaam **CO gas** karti hai: gas ore ke tukdon ke beech har kone mein pahunch jaati hai, jabki thos coke ore ke tukdon ko sirf kuch jagah chhoota hai. Pehle C + O₂ → CO₂, fir CO₂ + C → 2CO, aur ab CO ne reduce kiya. Hum ne tino reactions upar ginti se check kiye.

**Galti 4: "Pig iron = shuddh loha."**
Pig iron mein lagbhag 4% carbon hota hai. Maano 500 kg pig iron: carbon = 500 × 0.04 = 20 kg, iron = 480 kg. Itna carbon use **bhurbhura** bana deta hai; isliye steel banane ke liye carbon aur kam karna padta hai.

## Quick Recap

- Reduction: oxide se oxygen hatana. Dhatu paane ka asli kadam.
- Tareeka dhatu ki **reactivity series mein jagah** se tay hota hai.
- Neeche (Cu, Hg, Ag, Au): sirf garam karna, ya free dhatu. Cu₂S + 2Cu₂O → 6Cu + SO₂ (auto-reduction); 2HgO → 2Hg + O₂ (sirf padhne ke liye, mercury zehreela).
- Beech (Zn, Fe, Sn, Pb): oxide banao, phir carbon ya CO se reduce karo: ZnO + C → Zn + CO, PbO + C → Pb + CO, Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂.
- Blast furnace: ore + coke + limestone upar se, garam hawa neeche se; C + O₂ → CO₂, CO₂ + C → 2CO, Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂, CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂, CaO + SiO₂ → CaSiO₃ (slag). Slag halka hai, loha ke upar tairta hai. Pig iron mein lagbhag 4% carbon.
- Upar (K, Na, Ca, Mg, Al): carbon nahi, pighle chloride ya oxide ki electrolysis; jaise 2NaCl → 2Na + Cl₂ (Na⁺ + e⁻ → Na cathode par, 2Cl⁻ → Cl₂ + 2e⁻ anode par).
- Zyada reactive dhatu (Al) se reduction: thermite wali vidhi, Mn aur Cr jaise dhatuon ke liye.
- Furnace, pighle dhatu aur pighle namak sirf padhne ya dekhne ke liye; mercury, chlorine zehreele.

## Practice Questions

1. 464 kg of magnetite Fe₃O₄ is reduced by carbon monoxide in a furnace: Fe₃O₄ + 4CO → 3Fe + 4CO₂. Find the mass of iron obtained, the mass of CO used and the mass of CO₂ formed. Verify the conservation of mass.
2. 669 kg of lead(II) oxide PbO is reduced by coke: PbO + C → Pb + CO. Find the mass of lead, the mass of carbon needed and the mass of CO formed. Verify the conservation of mass.
3. In an aluminothermy process 3MnO₂ + 4Al → 3Mn + 2Al₂O₃, 261 g of MnO₂ is used. Find the mass of manganese formed, the mass of aluminium needed, and the mass of Al₂O₃ formed. Verify the conservation of mass.
4. Molten magnesium chloride (190 kg) is electrolysed: MgCl₂ → Mg + Cl₂. Find the mass of magnesium and the mass of chlorine. Write the reaction at each electrode. Why is chlorine gas handled with great care?
5. 755 kg of tin oxide SnO₂ is reduced by carbon: SnO₂ + 2C → Sn + 2CO. Find the mass of tin, the mass of carbon needed and the mass of CO formed. Verify the conservation of mass.
6. In a blast furnace 180 kg of silica SiO₂ (gangue) must be turned into slag with CaO from limestone: CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂ and CaO + SiO₂ → CaSiO₃. Find the mass of pure limestone needed and the mass of slag formed.
7. Molten sodium chloride (351 g) is electrolysed: 2NaCl → 2Na + Cl₂. Find the mass of sodium and of chlorine. Check that the masses add up.
8. Copper is extracted by auto-reduction: Cu₂S + 2Cu₂O → 6Cu + SO₂. If 477 g of Cu₂S reacts, find the mass of Cu₂O needed, the mass of copper obtained and the mass of SO₂ formed. Verify the conservation of mass.
9. Choose the method (heating alone, carbon or CO reduction after roasting or calcination, or electrolysis of a molten compound) for each metal and give a one-line reason by its position in the reactivity series: calcium, tin, chromium, mercury, potassium, lead. 10. Three metals P, Q, R are studied. Facts: (i) a more reactive metal can reduce the oxide of a less reactive metal; (ii) P reduces the oxide of Q; (iii) Q reduces the oxide of R; (iv) carbon can reduce only the oxide of R, not of P or Q. Arrange P, Q, R in decreasing order of reactivity and say which metals would need a method stronger than carbon.
11. 750 kg of pig iron contains 4% carbon. Find the mass of carbon and the mass of iron in it. Why is pig iron not used directly for making tools?
12. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) Slag is heavier than molten iron and sinks to the bottom of the furnace. (b) Carbon reduces the oxides of metals at the top of the series. (c) Mercury(II) oxide gives mercury just on heating. (d) In the blast furnace the main reducing agent for Fe₂O₃ is carbon monoxide. (e) Electrolysis of molten NaCl gives sodium at the anode.
13. Why is hot air blown from the bottom of the blast furnace and not from the top? Give two reasons from the reactions you learnt (heat and CO). Also, why must NaCl be molten, and not a solid, for its electrolysis?

### Answers

1. Fe₃O₄ = 3 × 56 + 4 × 16 = 232. 464 ÷ 232 = **2 kmol**. 1 mol Fe₃O₄ → 3 mol Fe, 4 mol CO → 4 mol CO₂. Fe = 6 kmol × 56 = **336 kg**. CO = 8 kmol × 28 = **224 kg**. CO₂ = 8 kmol × 44 = **352 kg**. Jaanch: 464 + 224 = 688 kg; 336 + 352 = 688 kg ✅.
2. PbO = 207 + 16 = 223. 669 ÷ 223 = **3 kmol**. Anupaat 1 : 1 : 1 : 1. Pb = 3 × 207 = **621 kg**. C = 3 × 12 = **36 kg**. CO = 3 × 28 = **84 kg**. Jaanch: 669 + 36 = 705 kg; 621 + 84 = 705 kg ✅.
3. MnO₂ = 55 + 32 = 87. 261 ÷ 87 = **3 mol**. 3 mol MnO₂ ke liye 4 mol Al aur 3 mol Mn, 2 mol Al₂O₃. Mn = 3 × 55 = **165 g**. Al = 4 × 27 = **108 g**. Al₂O₃ = 2 × 102 = **204 g**. Jaanch: 261 + 108 = 369 g; 165 + 204 = 369 g ✅.
4. MgCl₂ = 24 + 71 = 95. 190 ÷ 95 = **2 kmol**. Mg = 2 × 24 = **48 kg**. Cl₂ = 2 × 71 = **142 kg**. Jaanch: 48 + 142 = 190 kg ✅. Cathode: Mg²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Mg. Anode: 2Cl⁻ → Cl₂ + 2e⁻. Chlorine **zehreeli** gas hai, saans ke raste ko nuksaan pahunchati hai, isliye band system mein jama kar li jaati hai.
5. SnO₂ = 119 + 32 = 151. 755 ÷ 151 = **5 kmol**. Sn = 5 × 119 = **595 kg**. C = 2 × 5 = 10 kmol × 12 = **120 kg**. CO = 10 kmol × 28 = **280 kg**. Jaanch: 755 + 120 = 875 kg; 595 + 280 = 875 kg ✅.
6. SiO₂ = 28 + 32 = 60. 180 ÷ 60 = **3 kmol**. 1 mol SiO₂ ko 1 mol CaO chahiye, aur 1 mol CaO ke liye 1 mol CaCO₃. To CaCO₃ = 3 kmol × 100 = **300 kg**. CaO = 3 × 56 = 168 kg. Slag CaSiO₃ = 3 × 116 = **348 kg**. Jaanch: 180 + 168 = 348 kg ✅.
7. NaCl = 23 + 35.5 = 58.5. 351 ÷ 58.5 = **6 mol**. 2 mol NaCl → 2 mol Na + 1 mol Cl₂. Na = 6 × 23 = **138 g**. Cl₂ = 3 mol × 71 = **213 g**. Jaanch: 138 + 213 = 351 g ✅.
8. Cu₂S = 2 × 63.5 + 32 = 159. 477 ÷ 159 = **3 mol**. 1 mol Cu₂S ke liye 2 mol Cu₂O, to 6 mol Cu₂O. Cu₂O = 2 × 63.5 + 16 = 143. Cu₂O = 6 × 143 = **858 g**. Cu = 18 mol × 63.5 = **1143 g**. SO₂ = 3 mol × 64 = **192 g**. Jaanch: 477 + 858 = 1335 g; 1143 + 192 = 1335 g ✅.
9. **Ca**: Group 1 (bahut upar), carbon oxygen nahi chheen sakta: pighle chloride ki electrolysis. **Sn**: Group 2 (beech), SnO₂ ko carbon se reduce karte hain. **Cr**: oxide ko aluminium se (aluminothermy): Cr₂O₃ + 2Al → Al₂O₃ + 2Cr. **Hg**: Group 3, roast karne par HgO ban jaata hai, jise sirf garam karke Hg milta hai (sirf padhne ke liye, zehreela). **K**: Group 1, pighle chloride ki electrolysis. **Pb**: Group 2, PbS ko roast karke PbO, phir carbon se.
10. (ii) se P > Q, (iii) se Q > R. To order **P > Q > R**. (iv): carbon sirf R ka oxide reduce karta hai, P aur Q ka nahi: to **P aur Q** ke liye carbon se zyada taaqatwar tareeka (electrolysis ya zyada reactive dhatu) chahiye. Dusre kisi bhi order se fact (ii) ya (iii) toot jaate hain: sirf P > Q > R sahi baithta hai.
11. Carbon = 750 × 0.04 = **30 kg**. Iron = 750 − 30 = **720 kg** (iron = 96%). Jaanch: 30 + 720 = 750 ✅. Itne carbon se loha kadak par **bhurbhura** ho jaata hai: hathode se tutega, isliye pehle usme carbon kam kiya jaata hai (steel).
12. (a) **False** ❌: slag **halka** hai aur pighle loha ke **upar** tairta hai. (b) **False** ❌: carbon sirf beech ki dhatuon ke oxide reduce karta hai, K, Na, Mg, Al jaise ke nahi. (c) **True** ✅: HgO sirf garam karne par Hg aur O₂ deta hai (sirf padhne ke liye). (d) **True** ✅: CO hi mukhya reducing agent hai. (e) **False** ❌: sodium **cathode** par banta hai (Na⁺ + e⁻ → Na); anode par chlorine banti hai.
13. Neeche coke garam hawa ke oxygen se jalta hai (C + O₂ → CO₂), isse furnace ke sabse garam hisse ko **garmi** milti hai (pighle loha aur slag ke liye zaroori). Wahin se CO₂ + C → 2CO se **CO** banti hai, aur ye gas upar uthte hue ore ke tukdon ke beech se guzarti hui unhe reduce karti jaati hai. Hawa upar se daali jaati to garmi aur CO dono neeche nahi pahunchte.
