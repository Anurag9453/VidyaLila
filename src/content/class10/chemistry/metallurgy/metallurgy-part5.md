# Topic 5: Aluminium Ki Kahani: Baeyer aur Hall-Héroult

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki dhatu ko oxide se bahar nikaalne ka raasta uski **jagah reactivity series mein** tay karti hai: neeche wali dhatu garam karne se, beech wali carbon se. Par jo dhatu series ke **sabse oopar** baithi hain (Na, Mg, Al), unka oxygen se rishta itna pakka hai ki carbon bhi unhe oxygen se chhuda nahi paata. Un par sirf **electrolysis** kaam karti hai. Aaj hum aisi hi ek dhatu ki poori yatra dekhenge: **aluminium**, jo bauxite ki laal mitti se nikalkar cola ke can tak pahunchti hai. Do bade padav hain: pehle **bauxite ko shuddh alumina banana (Baeyer)**, phir **alumina se dhatu nikalna (Hall-Héroult)**. Aur sabse bada sawaal: itni mehnat ke baad bhi hum can ko kabaad mein kyun nahi phenkte?

## Cola ke can ki wapsi

School ke mele mein ek stall par bada-sa hara dibba rakha tha jis par likha tha **"Khaali can yahan daalein"**. Mr. Ranade apni bhatiji Tanmayi ke saath mele mein ghoom rahe the. Tanmayi ne apne haath ka khaali cola can us dibbe mein daala, aur ek pal ruk kar poochha.

"Chacha, ye can recycle hoke kahan jaata hai? Aur ye banta kaise hai? Ye to bilkul halka hai, par chamkeela aur kabhi zang bhi nahi lagta."

Mr. Ranade muskuraye. "Tanmayi, ye aluminium ka bana hai. Aur isse zyada dilchasp dhatu kam hi hain. Zameen ki parat (crust) mein aluminium sabse zyada milne wali **dhatu** hai, lagbhag 8% (mass ke hisaab se). Phir bhi ek zamaane mein ye bahut mehngi thi."

"Mehngi? Itni zyada hai to mehngi kyun?"

"Kyunki wo kabhi **akeli** nahi milti. Wo hamesha oxygen se bandhi hui **Al₂O₃** ki shakal mein hoti hai, aur Al-O ka bandhan itna majboot hai ki carbon use todh nahi paata. Pichhle topic ki table yaad karo: Al series mein oopar baithi hai. Isliye use bijli se hi nikaalna padta hai. Aur wo bijli bahut lagti hai."

"To phir can ko recycle karna aur bhi zaroori hua!" Tanmayi ne hanste hue kaha.

"Bilkul. Chalo, pehle dekhte hain ki wo laal mitti se chamakte can tak kaise pahunchta hai. Wapsi ka kissa baad mein."

## Part A: Baeyer process: bauxite se shuddh alumina (sirf padhne ke liye)

Bauxite seedha electrolysis mein nahi jaata, kyunki usme **Fe₂O₃, SiO₂** jaisi ashuddhiyan hoti hain. Aluminium itni bijli kharch karke banti hai, isliye alumina **bahut shuddh** hona chahiye. Isliye pehle ek **rasayanik chhanai** hoti hai. Ye wahi **leaching** hai jiska zikr Topic 2 mein hua tha, aur isme Chapter 2 ki wahi **amphoteric** baat kaam aati hai.

> **Safety:** Garam, gaadhe (concentrated) NaOH ka ghol bahut khatarnak hai (jalaata hai aur aankh ko nuksan pahunchaata hai). Ye sab **sirf padhne ke liye** hai. Is padav ka koi bhi hissa school ya ghar mein nahi kiya jaata.

**Padav 1: Garam, gaadhe NaOH mein ghulana (digestion).** Pisa hua bauxite garam, gaadhe NaOH ke ghol mein dabaav ke saath pakaya jaata hai. Amphoteric Al₂O₃ **base ke saath bhi reaction karta hai** (Chapter 2 mein humne ZnO aur Al₂O₃ ke liye ye dekha tha), isliye wo ghul jaata hai:

Al₂O₃ + 2NaOH → 2NaAlO₂ + H₂O

(Bauxite ka paani wala hissa, 2H₂O, bas ghol ke paani mein mil jaata hai.) **NaAlO₂** (sodium aluminate) paani mein ghulta hai. Par **Fe₂O₃** ek basic oxide hai: wo amphoteric nahi hai, to NaOH ke saath reaction karke nahi ghulta. Isliye wo thos laal keechad ke roop mein bacha rehta hai.

**Padav 2: Chhaan lo.** Ghol ko chhana jaata hai. Ghulne wala NaAlO₂ chhanne ke baad **filtrate** (neeche aaya ghol) mein hai; **laal keechad (red mud)** filter par reh gaya. Class 9 ki chhanai (filtration) yahan hai, bas bahut bade pariman par.

**Padav 3: Ghol ko patla karo, thanda karo, seeding karo.** Ghol mein paani milakar usse patla kiya jaata hai, thanda kiya jaata hai aur usme pehle se banaye hue **Al(OH)₃ ke kan** daale jaate hain. Tab NaAlO₂ paani se reaction karke **Al(OH)₃** ka safed thos bana deta hai jo neeche baith jaata hai:

NaAlO₂ + 2H₂O → Al(OH)₃ + NaOH

Dhyan do: yahan **NaOH wapas aa gaya**. Use dubara bauxite ghulane mein kaam mein liya jaata hai.

**Padav 4: Chhaan kar garam karo (calcination).** Chhana hua Al(OH)₃ bahut garam kiya jaata hai (Topic 3 ki calcination), aur wo paani chhod kar shuddh alumina ban jaata hai:

2Al(OH)₃ → Al₂O₃ + 3H₂O

```
   Pisa hua bauxite  (Al₂O₃·2H₂O + Fe₂O₃, SiO₂ ...)
        :
        V
   +------------------------------------------+
   : 1. Garam, gaadha NaOH ke saath pakao     :
   :    Al₂O₃ + 2NaOH -> 2NaAlO₂ + H₂O        :
   +------------------------------------------+
        :
        V
   +------------------------------------------+
   : 2. Chhaan lo                             : ---> laal keechad
   :    (NaAlO₂ ghol mein, Fe₂O₃ filter par)  :      (Fe₂O₃ aadi)
   +------------------------------------------+
        :
        V
   +------------------------------------------+
   : 3. Patla karo, thanda karo, seeding      : ---> NaOH wapas
   :    NaAlO₂ + 2H₂O -> Al(OH)₃ + NaOH       :      (dobara kaam)
   +------------------------------------------+
        :
        V
   +------------------------------------------+
   : 4. Chhaan kar bahut garam karo           : ---> bhaap (H₂O)
   :    2Al(OH)₃ -> Al₂O₃ + 3H₂O              :
   +------------------------------------------+
        :
        V
   Shuddh alumina Al₂O₃ (safed powder)
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Baeyer process ek flow ke roop mein: bauxite se shuddh alumina. Laal keechad chhanne par alag ho jaata hai, aur NaOH wapas ghol mein aakar dobara istemaal hota hai.

Equations ki atom-jaanch (Jaanch): Al₂O₃ + 2NaOH → 2NaAlO₂ + H₂O: Al 2=2, O 3+2=5 aur 4+1=5, Na 2=2, H 2=2 ✅. NaAlO₂ + 2H₂O → Al(OH)₃ + NaOH: Na 1=1, Al 1=1, O 2+2=4 aur 3+1=4, H 4=4 ✅. 2Al(OH)₃ → Al₂O₃ + 3H₂O: Al 2=2, O 6=3+3, H 6=6 ✅.

> **Jaanch:** Baeyer process ke baad bacha hua laal keechad bhi alumina hai. ❌ Wo Fe₂O₃ aadi hai, jo NaOH mein nahi ghulta; alumina to filtrate se nikalta hai.

## Part B: Hall-Héroult: alumina se aluminium (sirf padhne ke liye)

Ab hamare paas shuddh Al₂O₃ hai. Par **carbon use nahi tod sakta**, ye hum Topic 4 mein dekh chuke hain. Rasta sirf **electrolysis** hai, jise **1886 mein do logon ne alag-alag, ek hi saal mein** khoja: **Charles Hall (USA)** aur **Paul Héroult (France)**. Isliye iska naam **Hall-Héroult process** hai.

> **Safety:** Pighli dhatu, pighle namak (lagbhag 950 °C) aur bahut badi bijli: ye **sirf padhne ke liye** hai.

### Seedha alumina ko kyun nahi pighlate?

Alumina ka gala-baindu (melting point) **lagbhag 2072 °C** hai: itna ki bhatti banana bahut mehnga aur mushkil hai. Aur Class 9 mein humne dekha tha ki **thos** ionic yaugik bijli nahi chalate; **pighle** hue chalate hain, kyunki tab ion aazaad ho jaate hain ([chemical-bonding-part4.md](../../../class9/chemistry/chemical-bonding/chemical-bonding-part4.md)). Isliye alumina ko **pighla hua ya ghola hua** hi chahiye, par 2072 °C par nahi.

### Cryolite ka jaadu

Is samasya ka hal hai **cryolite, Na₃AlF₆**. Pighle cryolite (thodi fluorspar ke saath) mein alumina **ghul jaata hai**, bilkul jaise paani mein namak. Is mishran ke teen fayde hain (lagbhag):

1. Gala-baindu **2072 °C se ghatkar lagbhag 950 °C** ho jaata hai: bahut kam garmi, bahut kam bijli bhi.
2. Pighla mishran **bijli ko achchhe se chalata hai** (ions bahut hain).
3. Alumina ghulkar ions ke roop mein poore ghol mein phail jaata hai, isliye electrolysis lagatar chal sakti hai.

Cryolite aluminium ka ore **nahi** hai: wo sirf ek **ghulaane wala madhyam** (solvent) hai.

### Tank ki banawat

Ek bahut bada **steel ka tank** hota hai. Uski andar ki deewaren aur tala **carbon (graphite) ki parat** se dhake hote hain, aur ye carbon lining hi **cathode (−)** hai. Oopar se **carbon ke mote rod** ghol mein latke hote hain: ye **anode (+)** hain. Beech mein hota hai pighle cryolite mein ghula alumina ka ghol.

```
           (+)         (+)         (+)       <- bijli ka + sira
           ##          ##          ##        <- carbon ke rod = ANODE
   +-------##----------##----------##-------+  <- steel ka tank
   :# =====##==========##==========##===== #:
   :# ~~~~~##~~~~~~~~~~##~~~~~~~~~~##~~~~~ #:
   :# ~~ pighla cryolite + ghula Al₂O₃ ~~  #:
   :# ~~ lagbhag 950 °C; anode par O₂      #:
   :# ~~ banti hai jo carbon ko jalati hai #:
   :# ------------------------------------ #:
   :# pighla aluminium (bhaari, neeche)    #:  ---> tap karke nikaala
   :########################################:
   +----------------------------------------+
     # = carbon: upar ke rod ANODE (+), lining CATHODE (−)
     = = upar jami thos alumina ki parat
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Hall-Héroult cell ka cross-section (sketch, maap se nahi): steel ke tank ki deewar aur tala carbon lining (#) se dhake hain, jo cathode (−) hai; upar se latakte carbon ke rod anode (+) hain. Beech mein pighle cryolite aur alumina ka ghol, upar thos alumina ki parat, aur neeche cathode par jama bhaari pighla aluminium jise tap karke bahar nikaala jaata hai.

### Electrode par kya hota hai ("ek jhalak")

Pighle ghol mein alumina **ions mein toota** hota hai: Al³⁺ aur O²⁻. Bijli chalane par:

- **Cathode (carbon lining, −):** Al³⁺ ko electron milte hain: **Al³⁺ + 3e⁻ → Al**. Pighla aluminium bhaari hai, isliye ghol ke neeche baith jaata hai aur kabhi-kabhi **tap** karke bahar nikaala jaata hai.
- **Anode (carbon rod, +):** O²⁻ electron chhodta hai: **2O²⁻ → O₂ + 4e⁻**.

Dono ko jodo: 4 Al ke liye 4 × 3 = 12 electron chahiye, aur 6 O²⁻ milkar 3 O₂ banate hue 3 × 4 = 12 electron chhodte hain, yaani electron barabar:

4Al³⁺ + 6O²⁻ → 4Al + 3O₂, yaani **2Al₂O₃ → 4Al + 3O₂**.

Atom-jaanch (Jaanch): Al 4=4, O 6=6 ✅. Electron: 4 × 3 = 12 aur 6 O²⁻ × 2 = 12 (yaani 3O₂ banne mein 3 × 4 = 12) ✅.

(Ye sab **ek jhalak** hai. Electrolyte kya hota hai, kaun sa ion kahan jaata hai aur electrode par kya tay karta hai, ye sab ka poora siddhant agle chapter **Electrolysis** mein aayega. Yahan bas dekho ki aluminium **cathode par** banta hai.)

### Anode kyun jalta hai?

Anode par jo O₂ bani, wo bahut garam (lagbhag 950 °C) carbon ke rod se milti hai, aur carbon use **jala deta hai**:

C + O₂ → CO₂

Isliye anode ke carbon rod **dheere-dheere ghisgar chhote hote jaate hain** aur kuch dino mein **badalne** padte hain. Poora hisaab milakar likhein (3 O₂ ko 3 C jala deta hai):

**2Al₂O₃ + 3C → 4Al + 3CO₂**

Jaanch: Al 4=4, O 6=3 × 2=6, C 3=3 ✅. Mass-jaanch: 2 × 102 + 3 × 12 = 204 + 36 = 240 aur 4 × 27 + 3 × 44 = 108 + 132 = 240 ✅.

> **Jaanch:** Hall-Héroult cell mein cathode par aluminium banta hai aur anode par oxygen se carbon jalta hai. ✅

## Part C: Itni bijli, itna kabaad: recycling ka hisaab

Aluminium banane mein **bahut bijli** lagti hai: **lagbhag 13 se 15 kWh bijli har kilogram aluminium par** (lagbhag; ye sankhya plant ke hisaab se badalti hai). Hum aage hisaab ke liye **14 kWh/kg** maan lete hain. Par **kabaad (scrap) aluminium ko dubara pighlakar** naya aluminium banane mein bauxite se banane ki **lagbhag sirf 5%** bijli lagti hai (lagbhag).

```
   1 kg aluminium banane mein bijli (kWh)

   Bauxite se : ########################################  14 kWh
   Scrap se   : ##                                          0.7 kWh

   (1 # = lagbhag 0.35 kWh; 5% = 14 ka 0.7)
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Naya aluminium (bauxite se) aur recycled aluminium (scrap se) ki bijli ki khapat ki tulna, lagbhag maan ke saath: recycling mein lagbhag 5% bijli lagti hai, yaani lagbhag 95% bachat.

Kyun itni bachat? Kyunki scrap mein aluminium pehle se **dhatu** hai: use sirf pighlana hai, na bauxite khodna, na Baeyer, na Hall-Héroult. Bas pighlao aur dhalo. Isiliye school ke mele ka "khaali can yahan daalein" dibba sirf safaai nahi, **bijli bachaane** ka bhi kaam hai.

**Ghar par adult ke saath (activity):** Ek khaali, dhoya hua cola can lo aur kitchen scale par uska bhaar (lagbhag 12 se 16 g) naap lo. Phir apne ghar ke ek mahine ke khaali can ginkar, ye **bijli ki bachat** Example 4 ke tareeke se nikalo (14 kWh/kg aur 5%). (Can ko kaatna ya todna nahi: kinaare tez hote hain.)

## Part E: Solved examples (hisaab ki baat)

Atomic masses: Al 27, O 16, C 12, H 1. Al₂O₃ = 2 × 27 + 3 × 16 = 102; 2Al = 54. Dhyan do: ye **mass-se-mass** ka hisaab bas equation ke ratio par chalta hai.

**Example 1 (alumina se aluminium):** 306 tonne alumina se kitna aluminium milega?

- Equation: 2Al₂O₃ → 4Al + 3O₂. 2 × 102 = 204 g Al₂O₃ → 4 × 27 = 108 g Al. Ratio: 102 g Al₂O₃ → 54 g Al.
- 306 ÷ 102 = 3. Isliye Al = 3 × 54 = **162 tonne**.

**Jaanch:** 162 ÷ 306 × 100 = 52.9% ✅ (Al₂O₃ mein aluminium 54 ÷ 102 = 52.94%).

**Example 2 (1 tonne aluminium ke liye alumina aur bauxite):** 1 tonne Al ke liye kitna alumina chahiye? Aur agar bauxite shuddh Al₂O₃·2H₂O hota to kitna?

- 54 g Al ke liye 102 g Al₂O₃: 1 tonne Al ke liye 102 ÷ 54 = **1.889 tonne** alumina (lagbhag).
- Al₂O₃·2H₂O = 102 + 2 × 18 = 138. 102 g alumina 138 g bauxite se aata hai: 1.889 × 138 ÷ 102 = **2.556 tonne** shuddh bauxite (lagbhag).
- (Asli bauxite mein ashuddhiyan hoti hain, isliye asal mein isse zyada, lagbhag 4 tonne ya usse bhi kuch zyada, lagti hai.)

**Jaanch:** 2.556 × 102 ÷ 138 = 1.889 ✅; 1.889 × 54 ÷ 102 = 1.0 ✅.

**Example 3 (anode ka carbon):** Cell mein 1 tonne aluminium banane par kitna carbon jalta hai, aur kitna CO₂ banta hai, agar poora oxygen carbon ko CO₂ bana de? Equation: 2Al₂O₃ + 3C → 4Al + 3CO₂.

- 4Al = 108 g ↔ 3C = 36 g ↔ 3CO₂ = 132 g.
- 1 tonne Al ke liye C = 36 ÷ 108 = **0.333 tonne = 333 kg** (lagbhag).
- CO₂ = 132 ÷ 108 = **1.222 tonne** (lagbhag).

**Jaanch:** Mass: 2 × 102 + 36 = 240 aur 108 + 132 = 240 ✅. Dhyan do: **333 kg equation se nikla kam se kam hisaab hai.** Asli plant mein lagbhag **400 kg ya usse zyada** carbon lagta hai (lagbhag), kyunki kuch carbon CO ban kar jaata hai aur anode ka upri garam hissa hawa mein bhi jalta hai.

**Example 4 (ek cola can ki bijli):** Ek can ka bhaar lagbhag 15 g maan lo. Naya aluminium banane mein (14 kWh/kg) aur recycling mein (5%) kitni bijli lagi?

- 15 g = 0.015 kg. Naya: 0.015 × 14 = **0.21 kWh**. Recycled: 5% = 0.21 × 0.05 = **0.0105 kWh**.
- Bachat = 0.21 − 0.0105 = 0.1995 kWh (lagbhag 0.2 kWh): yaani ek 10 W ka LED bulb lagbhag 20 ghante jalane jitni bijli, **har ek can se**.

**Jaanch:** 0.21 ÷ 0.01 = 21 ghante (10 W = 0.01 kW) aur bachat 0.1995 ÷ 0.01 = 19.95 ghante ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Cryolite aluminium ka ore hai."** Nahi: bauxite ore hai. Cryolite sirf pighlaane ka madhyam hai. Dekho: Hall-Héroult mein aluminium sirf alumina se aata hai. 2Al₂O₃ → 4Al + 3O₂ mein cryolite hai hi nahi. Agar cryolite hi ore hota, to equation mein Na₃AlF₆ dikhta. Cryolite bas "paani" ka kaam karta hai jisme alumina ghulta hai, jaise chai ka paani cheeni ko ghulata hai par cheeni nahi banta.

**Galti 2: "Baeyer process mein aluminium dhatu ban jaati hai."** Baeyer process ka aakhri maal **Al₂O₃** hai, dhatu nahi. Dekho: Part A ke chaaron padav (NaAlO₂, Al(OH)₃, Al₂O₃) mein kahin Al ka akela atom nahi. Dhatu sirf Hall-Héroult cell mein, cathode par banti hai (Al³⁺ + 3e⁻ → Al).

**Galti 3: "Anode jaisa ka waisa rehta hai, sirf cathode par kaam hota hai."** Anode ke carbon rod roz ghisgar chhote hote hain, kyunki anode par bani oxygen unhe jalati hai: C + O₂ → CO₂. Example 3 ne dikhaya: equation ke hisaab se 1 tonne Al par kam se kam 333 kg carbon jalta hai (asli plant mein lagbhag 400 kg ya zyada). Isliye rod badalne padte hain.

**Galti 4: "Recycling mein bijli lagti hi nahi."** Lagti hai, par bahut kam: lagbhag 5%. Dekho: 1 kg ke liye 14 kWh ki jagah 0.7 kWh. Kabaad ko ikattha karna, chhaantna aur dobara pighlaana mehnat aur bijli leta hai, par bauxite khodne, Baeyer aur Hall-Héroult se bahut kam.

## Quick Recap

- Aluminium reactivity series mein oopar hai, isliye carbon se nahi, **electrolysis** se bani hai (Topic 4).
- **Baeyer process:** bauxite ko garam, gaadhe NaOH mein ghulao (Al₂O₃ amphoteric hai), laal keechad (Fe₂O₃ aadi) chhaan do, ghol ko patla, thanda aur seed karo, Al(OH)₃ chhano aur garam karke shuddh Al₂O₃ lo.
- **Hall-Héroult:** alumina (gala-baindu lagbhag 2072 °C) ko pighle cryolite Na₃AlF₆ mein ghulao (lagbhag 950 °C par), carbon lining = cathode, carbon rod = anode.
- Cathode: Al³⁺ + 3e⁻ → Al; anode: 2O²⁻ → O₂ + 4e⁻; oxygen carbon ko jalati hai: C + O₂ → CO₂.
- Poora: 2Al₂O₃ → 4Al + 3O₂ (aur carbon ke saath 2Al₂O₃ + 3C → 4Al + 3CO₂).
- Bijli lagbhag 13 se 15 kWh/kg; recycling mein lagbhag 5%.
- 102 g Al₂O₃ se 54 g Al; yaani 102 t alumina se 54 t Al.
- Garam NaOH aur pighle namak: sirf padhne ke liye.

9. A class collects 20 empty cola cans of 12 g each. How much energy would the cans have needed if made fresh (14 kWh/kg), and how much is needed to recycle them (5%)? For how many hours would the saving run an 8 W LED bulb?
10. Why does the carbon anode need to be replaced regularly while the carbon lining of the tank does not burn? Use the reaction at each electrode.
11. State true or false and correct: (a) Red mud is mostly the aluminium compound. (b) NaOH is wasted in the Baeyer process. (c) Molten aluminium collects at the bottom of the cell. (d) Aluminium is produced at the anode.
12. Match each property of aluminium with a use: (i) low density, (ii) good electrical conductor, (iii) forms a thin protective oxide layer, (iv) very reactive, strong reducing agent. Uses: cooking foil that does not rust, thermite welding, overhead cables, aircraft body.

### Answers

1. 2Al(OH)₃ → Al₂O₃ + 3H₂O (Al 2=2, O 6=3+3, H 6=6 ✅). 2 × 78 = 156 kg → 102 kg Al₂O₃ + 54 kg paani. 390 ÷ 156 = 2.5, isliye Al₂O₃ = 2.5 × 102 = **255 kg**, paani = 2.5 × 54 = **135 kg**. Jaanch: 255 + 135 = 390 ✅.
2. Al₂O₃·2H₂O = 138. 276 ÷ 138 = 2. Al₂O₃·2H₂O → Al₂O₃ + 2H₂O: Al₂O₃ = 2 × 102 = **204 tonne**, paani = 2 × 36 = **72 tonne**. Jaanch: 204 + 72 = 276 ✅.
3. 102 t Al₂O₃ → 54 t Al. 612 ÷ 102 = 6. Al = 6 × 54 = **324 tonne**. Jaanch: 324 ÷ 612 = 52.94% ✅.
4. (a) Alumina = 81 × 102 ÷ 54 = **153 tonne**. (b) 108 t Al ↔ 36 t C, to 81 t Al ke liye C = 81 × 36 ÷ 108 = **27 tonne**. (c) CO₂ = 81 × 132 ÷ 108 = **99 tonne**. (d) 108 t Al ↔ 96 t O₂ (3 × 32), to 81 t ke liye O₂ = 81 × 96 ÷ 108 = **72 tonne**. Jaanch: C + O₂ → CO₂: 27 + 72 = 99 ✅; 153 + 27 = 180 aur 81 + 99 = 180 ✅.
5. P dono acid aur base se reaction karta hai, to **amphoteric** hai (jaise Al₂O₃). P NaOH mein ghulta hai, isliye **filtrate mein** hai. Q NaOH mein nahi ghulta, isliye **residue (laal keechad ki tarah)** hai. Dono ke liye diye gaye facts se hi ye nikalta hai.
6. Naye ke liye: 250 × 14 = 3500 kWh. Recycling: 5% × 3500 = **175 kWh**. Bachat = 3500 − 175 = **3325 kWh** (lagbhag 95%).
7. (i) Mishran lagbhag 950 °C par pighal jaata hai, jabki akela alumina lagbhag 2072 °C par, yaani bahut kam garmi aur bijli lagti hai. (ii) Pighla mishran bijli achchhe se chalata hai, jabki thos alumina nahi chalata (Class 9). (iii) Alumina ghulkar ions ke roop mein har jagah phailta hai, jisse electrolysis chalti rehti hai.
8. Cathode: Al³⁺ + 3e⁻ → Al. 4Al³⁺ ke liye 4 × 3 = **12 electron** chahiye. 2O²⁻ → O₂ + 4e⁻, to 3O₂ ke liye 6O²⁻ lagenge aur 3 × 4 = **12 electron** nikalenge. Dono barabar ✅. Poora: 4Al³⁺ + 6O²⁻ → 4Al + 3O₂.
9. 20 × 12 g = 240 g = 0.24 kg. Naya: 0.24 × 14 = **3.36 kWh**. Recycling: 5% = **0.168 kWh**. Bachat = 3.36 − 0.168 = 3.192 kWh. 8 W = 0.008 kW: 3.192 ÷ 0.008 = **399 ghante** (lagbhag 400 ghante). Jaanch: 0.008 × 399 = 3.192 ✅.
10. Anode par O²⁻ ions se O₂ banti hai (2O²⁻ → O₂ + 4e⁻), aur wo garam carbon ko jala deti hai: C + O₂ → CO₂, isliye rod ghisgar chhote hote hain. Cathode (lining) par oxygen nahi banti: wahan sirf Al³⁺ + 3e⁻ → Al hota hai (dhatu banti hai), isliye lining nahi jalti.
11. (a) **False** ❌: laal keechad mostly Fe₂O₃ aadi hai; aluminium wala hissa NaAlO₂ ban kar ghol mein gaya. (b) **False** ❌: seeding wale step mein NaOH wapas banta hai aur dobara kaam aata hai. (c) **True** ✅: pighla aluminium bhaari hai aur neeche baithta hai. (d) **False** ❌: aluminium cathode par banta hai; anode par oxygen banti hai.
12. (i) low density: **aircraft body** (alloy ke roop mein). (ii) achchha bijli-chaalak: **overhead cables**. (iii) patli kavach wali oxide parat: **cooking foil jo zang nahi pakadti**. (iv) bahut reactive aur reducing agent: **thermite welding**.
