# Topic 6: Refining aur Alloys: Dhatu Ko Pure Banana aur Mel Se Mazboot Banana

Pichhle topics mein humne ore se dhatu tak ka lamba safar dekha: concentration, roasting ya calcination, reduction, aur aluminium jaisi dhatuon ke liye electrolysis. Par reduction ya electrolysis ke baad jo dhatu milti hai wo aksar **thodi ashuddh** hoti hai: usme ore ki bachi hui gandagi, doosri dhatuein aur carbon jaisi cheezein mili rehti hain. Safar ka aakhri padaav hai **refining** (dhatu ko shuddh karna). Aur uske baad ek naya sawal: kya shuddh dhatu hamesha sabse achhi hoti hai? Jawab chaunka sakta hai: kai kaamon ke liye **do ya zyada dhatuon ko milakar** banaya gaya **alloy** shuddh dhatu se behtar nikalta hai. Is topic mein hum ye dono baatein seekhenge.

## Shaadi ki baraat, band-baaja aur mandir ki ghanti

Gaon mein ek shaadi thi. **Mr. Bhave** apne bhanje **Pratham** ko saath lekar baraat dekhne gaye. Aage-aage band-wale chal rahe the: trumpet, bugle aur bade bass drum ke saath ek lamba chamakta hua baaja. Dhoop mein sab sunehre-peele chamak rahe the. Raaste mein ek purana mandir pada, aur uski badi ghanti kisi ne baja di: "dhaaaang!" aur wo awaaz der tak goonjti rahi.

Pratham ne poocha, "Mama, ye baaje itne sundar kyun chamakte hain? Aur ghanti ki awaaz itni lambi kyun hai?"

Mr. Bhave hanse, "Bhanje, ye baaje **peetal (brass)** ke hain, yaani copper aur zinc ka mel. Aur ye ghanti **bronze** ki hai, yaani copper aur tin ka mel. Dulhan ke gale ka haar dekha? Usme jo sona hai wo 22 carat ka hai, aur wo bhi sone ka mel hai!"

"Mel? Mama, sona seedha pura sona kyun nahi?" Pratham ne poocha.

"Yahi to is topic ka sawal hai," Mr. Bhave ne kaha. "Pehle ye dekhte hain ki dhatu ko **shuddh** kaise karte hain, aur phir ye ki use **milakar** kyun banate hain."

## Part A: Refining kyun, aur kaun-kaun si vidhi?

Reduction ya electrolysis ke baad jo dhatu aati hai usme thodi gandagi hoti hai. Kuch kaamon mein ye chalti hai (jaise gaadi ki body mein loha), par kai kaamon mein shuddhta bahut zaroori hai. Misaal: bijli ki taar ke liye copper chahiye jo lagbhag **99.9% se zyada** shuddh ho, kyunki thodi si gandagi bhi uski bijli ki conductivity kaafi ghata deti hai. Chip ke liye silicon to isse bhi kai guna pura chahiye.

Refining ki vidhi is baat par chunte hain ki dhatu aur gandagi ke **kis physical ya chemical gun mein farq** hai (Class 9 ke separation tareekon ki tarah). Chaar aam vidhiyan:

| Vidhi | Kis farq par tiki hai | Kis dhatu ke liye | Ek line mein tareeka |
|---|---|---|---|
| Electrolytic refining | Dhatu ka bijli se ghulna aur jamna | Copper (aur aur bhi kai dhatuein) | Ashuddh dhatu anode, shuddh dhatu cathode (Part B) |
| Distillation | Dhatu ka ubaal-ank gandagi se kam hai | Zinc (ubaal-ank lagbhag 907 °C), mercury (lagbhag 357 °C) | Pighli dhatu ko garam karke bhaap banate hain aur thanda karke shuddh dhatu jamate hain |
| Liquation | Dhatu ka gala-ank gandagi se kam hai | Tin (lagbhag 232 °C), lead (lagbhag 327 °C) | Dhalwaan farsh par ashuddh dhatu ko garam karte hain, kam gala-ank wali dhatu pighal kar beh jaati hai, ooncha gala-ank wali gandagi peeche reh jaati hai |
| Zone refining | Gandagi pighli patti mein zyada ghulti hai, jami hui dhatu mein kam | Bahut pure silicon aur germanium (chips ke liye) | Rod par ek chhota garam chhalla dheere-dheere chalta hai |

> **Sankshep mein zone refining:** silicon ki ek lambi rod ke ek sire par ek chhota garam chhalla (heater ring) rakha jaata hai, jo rod ka ek patla hissa pighlata hai. Chhalla dheere-dheere doosre sire ki taraf chalta hai. Pighle hisse mein gandagi zyada ghulti hai, isliye wo us hisse ke saath-saath aage badhti jaati hai aur aakhir mein ek sire par jama ho jaati hai. Us sire ko kaat kar phenk dete hain. Kai baar chalane par rod bahut pure ho jaati hai.

> **Chetavni:** mercury aur lead **zehreeli dhatuein** hain. Unka vaashp aur dhool sehat ke liye khatarnaak hai. Distillation aur liquation yahan **sirf padhne ke liye** hain: school ya ghar mein inhein kabhi mat karna aur mercury ya lead ko haath mat lagana.

## Part B: Copper ki electrolytic refining (ek jhalak)

Copper ke liye sabse mashhoor refining vidhi hai **electrolytic refining**. Isme bijli se ashuddh copper ko ghula kar shuddh copper ko doosri jagah jama dete hain. Poori theory (electrolyte kya hai, kaun sa ion kab chunta hai) **Chapter 4 "Electrolysis"** mein hai. Yahan sirf ek jhalak, aur wo bhi **sirf padhne ke liye**: copper sulphate ka ghol aur bijli ka cell school mein bhi teacher ki dekh-rekh mein hi hota hai.

Set-up:
- **Anode (+):** ashuddh copper ka mota tukda.
- **Cathode (-):** shuddh copper ki patli chadar.
- **Electrolyte:** thode acid mile hue **copper sulphate** (CuSO₄) ka ghol (acid se ghol ki conductivity badhti hai). Ye neela ghol zehreela aur nuksaandayak hai.

```
                bijli ka source
       (+) o------[ battery ]------o (-)
           :                   :
+----------:-------------------:-------------+
:      +---:---+           +---:---+         :
:      :       :   Cu²⁺    :       :         :
:      : ANODE :  ------>  :CATHODE:         :
:      :ashuddh:   Cu²⁺    : shuddh:         :
:      : copper:  ------>  : copper:         :
:      :(mota) :           :(patli):         :
:      :       :           :chadar :         :
:      +-------+           +-------+         :
:  CuSO₄ ghol (thoda acid mila hua)          :
:                                            :
:     . . . . .  <-- anode mud (Au, Ag, Pt)  :
+--------------------------------------------+
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Copper ki electrolytic refining ka cell. Ashuddh copper anode (+) dheere-dheere ghulta hai aur Cu²⁺ ghol mein jaate hain; ye Cu²⁺ shuddh copper cathode (-) par Cu ban kar jam jaate hain. Anode ke neeche na ghulne wali gandagi **anode mud** ban kar bethti hai.

**Electrode ki reactions (ek jhalak):**

- Anode par: Cu → Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ (copper ke atom electron chhodkar ghol mein ion ban jaate hain; ashuddh copper ghulta hai).
- Cathode par: Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu (ghol ke ion electron lekar shuddh copper banke chadar par jam jaate hain; chadar mooti hoti jaati hai).

Dono reaction mein charge barabar hai: 0 = (+2) + (-2) aur 0 = (+2) + (-2) ✅, aur Cu ke atom 1 = 1 ✅. Net result: **copper anode se uthakar cathode par rakh diya gaya**, aur ghol mein CuSO₄ lagbhag utna hi bana rehta hai kyunki jitna Cu²⁺ ghola utna hi jama.

**Gandagi ka kya hota hai?**
- **Sona, chandi, platinum** copper se kam reactive hain, isliye wo bijli se bhi nahi ghulte. Ye anode ke neeche **anode mud** (gaad) ki tarah gir jaati hain. Ye mud bekaar nahi hai: isse sona aur chandi alag nikaale jaate hain, aur kai refineries ke liye ye achhi kamai hai.
- **Zinc, iron** jaise zyada reactive impurities anode se ghulkar ghol mein chali jaati hain par cathode par jam nahi hoti. (Kyun? Ye Chapter 4 ka sawal hai.)

Cathode par jo copper jamta hai wo lagbhag 99.9% se zyada pura hota hai.

## Part C: Alloy kya hai aur kyun banate hain?

Class 9 mein hum dekh chuke hain ki alloy **mixture** hai, compound nahi ([is-matter-around-us-pure-part6.md](../../../class9/chemistry/is-matter-around-us-pure/is-matter-around-us-pure-part6.md)): dhatuein pighlakar milayi jaati hain, anupaat badal sakta hai, aur har dhatu ke apne gun bane rehte hain. Ab ye dekhte hain ki mel banane se milta kya hai.

**Alloy kyun banate hain?**

| Fayda | Kaise | Misaal |
|---|---|---|
| Zyada sakht aur mazboot | Alag aakaar ke atom parton ko phisalne se rokte hain (Part D) | Steel loha se mazboot, bronze copper se sakht |
| Zang aur corrosion se bachav | Mel se surface par dhatu ka behtar vyavhaar | Stainless steel mein zang nahi lagti |
| Gala-ank kam | Mel ka gala-ank aksar dono dhatuon se kam | Solder tin aur lead dono se kam gala-ank par pighalta hai |
| Rang ya awaaz behtar | Mel se chamak ya goonj badal jaati hai | Peetal sunehra, bronze ki ghanti ki lambi goonj |
| Kaam ke hisaab se gun | Hardness, halkapan ya kisi aur gun ko apni zaroorat ke hisaab se chunna | Aircraft ke liye halka aur mazboot duralumin |

Ek baat dhyan se: alloy aamtaur par shuddh dhatu se **bijli ke kharaab conductor** hote hain. Isliye bijli ki taar shuddh copper ki banti hai, brass ki nahi.

Neeche ek table jisme saath hi composition aur upyog hain. Percentage **lagbhag** diye gaye hain, kyunki alag-alag nirmaata thoda alag anupaat rakhte hain.

> **Figure 6.2** – Alloys ka chart: naam, mukhya dhatu, doosre ghatak (lagbhag percent) aur upyog. Lead aur mercury zehreele hain, isliye lead-free solder aur naye materials ab zyada chalte hain.

> **Chetavni:** solder mein lead aur amalgam mein mercury hai: dono zehreele hain. Aaj kal pani ke nal aur electronics mein **lead-free solder** (tin ke saath chandi ya copper) ka chalan badh raha hai. Ye dono alloy yahan sirf padhne ke liye hain: inhein kabhi haath mat lagana ya pighlane ki koshish mat karna.

Figure 6.2 ke steel par dhyan do: carbon ka thoda sa hissa (0.1 se 1.5%) loha ko kaafi badal deta hai: kam carbon (jaise 0.1%) wala steel naram aur aasani se mudne wala hota hai, zyada carbon wala sakht par bhurbhura. Loha mein carbon blast furnace se hi aata hai (Topic 4: pig iron mein lagbhag 4% carbon), aur steel banate waqt us carbon ko kam kiya jaata hai.

## Part D: Alloy sakht kyun hoti hai? (ek picture)

Shuddh dhatu mein sab atom **ek hi aakaar** ke hote hain aur ek ke upar ek parton mein bichhe hote hain, jaise taash ke patte. Kyunki atom ek jaise hain, ek parat doosri par aasani se **phisal** jaati hai. Isi liye shuddh dhatu naram aur malleable hoti hai: peetne par parat phisal kar naya aakaar le leti hai.

Alloy mein doosri dhatu ke atom **alag aakaar** ke hote hain. Ye bade (ya chhote) atom parton ke beech phans kar unke rastey mein rukavat daal dete hain. Parton ka phisalna mushkil ho jaata hai, aur isi se alloy zyada sakht aur mazboot ban jaati hai.

```
SHUDDH DHATU                     ALLOY
(sab atom ek jaise, o)           (kuch bade atom, O)

  ----> parat phisal gayi          ----> X parat ruk gayi
 o o o o o o o o                  o o o O o o o o
 o o o o o o o o                  o o o o o O o o
 o o o o o o o o                  o O o o o o o o

Parat aasaani se phisalti hai    Bade atom rukawat daalte hain
```

> **Figure 6.3** – Shuddh dhatu (24 chhote atom o) aur alloy (21 chhote atom o aur 3 bade atom O) ki parton ki tulna. Shuddh dhatu mein parat aasaani se phisalti hai (kam sakht). Alloy mein bade atom parton ko phisalne se rokte hain (zyada sakht). Yaad rakho: ye sirf sakhti ka ek simple picture hai, alloy abhi bhi mixture hai, compound nahi.

## Part E: Carat aur hallmark ki ginti

Sona bahut naram hota hai. Saaf, 24 carat sone ka gehna haath se bhi mud sakta hai. Isliye gehne mein sone mein thoda copper ya chandi milate hain. **Carat** batata hai ki 24 hisson mein kitne hisse sona hai.

- 24 carat: 24 hisse sona = 100% (shuddh sona; aksar sikke ya chhoti eent (bar) ke roop mein).
- 22 carat: 22 hisse sona, 2 hisse doosri dhatu = 22 ÷ 24 × 100 = **91.67%** sona.
- 18 carat: 18 ÷ 24 × 100 = **75%** sona.
- 14 carat: 14 ÷ 24 × 100 = **58.33%** sona.

Gehnon par ek **hallmark** ki chhaap hoti hai jo sone ki shuddhta batati hai. Wo sone ke **1000 hisson mein kitne hisse** ke roop mein likhi hoti hai: 22 carat par **916** (lagbhag 91.6%), 18 carat par **750** (75.0%), 14 carat par **585** (lagbhag 58.5%). Hallmark likha dekh kar tum jaanch sakte ho ki gehne mein kitna sona hai.

**Activity 6.1: Hallmark padho (ghar par adult ke saath).** Ghar ke kisi sone ke gehne ya sikke par bade adult ki madad se dekho ki koi number (jaise 916 ya 750) khuda hai. Chhaap bahut chhoti hoti hai, isliye magnifying glass se dekho. Gehne ko mat kaatna, mat ghisna, koi chemical mat lagana. Number ko 1000 se bhaag karke percent nikalo aur 24 se guna karke carat.

## Part F: Solved examples

### Example 1. Brass ki composition (peetal ka percent)

**Sawaal:** Ek 250 g ke brass ke baaje mein 70% copper aur 30% zinc hai. Har dhatu kitni hai?

**Hal:**
- Copper = 250 × 70 ÷ 100 = **175 g**.
- Zinc = 250 × 30 ÷ 100 = **75 g**.

**Jaanch:** 175 + 75 = 250 g ✅ aur 70% + 30% = 100% ✅.

---

### Example 2. 22 carat ki angoothi

**Sawaal:** 10 g ki 22 carat angoothi mein kitna sona aur kitni doosri dhatu hai?

**Hal:**
- Sone ka percent = 22 ÷ 24 × 100 = 91.67%.
- Sona = 10 × 22 ÷ 24 = **9.17 g** (lagbhag).
- Doosri dhatu = 10 − 9.17 = **0.83 g** (lagbhag), yaani 2 ÷ 24 × 10 = 0.83 g.

**Jaanch:** 9.17 + 0.83 = 10 g ✅. Dulhan ke haar jaise bhaari gehne par bhi yahi anupaat lagta hai: jitna bhaari gehna, utna hi uska 22 ÷ 24 hissa sona.

---

### Example 3. Ashuddh copper ki refining ka hisab

**Sawaal:** Ek refinery ke anode ka mass 40 kg hai aur usme 97.5% copper hai. Maan lo poora copper ghul kar cathode par jam jaata hai. Cathode par kitna shuddh copper milega aur kitni gandagi gir jayegi ya ghol mein jayegi?

**Hal:**
- Copper = 40 × 97.5 ÷ 100 = **39 kg**. Ye cathode par shuddh copper ke roop mein jamega.
- Gandagi = 40 − 39 = **1 kg** (2.5%): sona, chandi jaise kam reactive hissa anode mud ban jaata hai, aur zinc, iron jaise zyada reactive hissa ghol mein chala jaata hai.

**Jaanch:** 39 + 1 = 40 kg ✅, aur 97.5% + 2.5% = 100% ✅. Reaction mein Cu ke atom: anode par jitne Cu atom Cu²⁺ ion bane, cathode par utne hi Cu²⁺ ion wapas Cu bane.

---

### Example 4. Steel mein carbon

**Sawaal:** 2000 kg steel mein 0.8% carbon hai. Carbon aur loha kitna hai? Loha kitne percent hai?

**Hal:**
- Carbon = 2000 × 0.8 ÷ 100 = **16 kg**.
- Loha = 2000 − 16 = **1984 kg**.
- Loha percent = 100 − 0.8 = **99.2%**.

**Jaanch:** 16 + 1984 = 2000 kg ✅. Kitna thoda carbon, par loha ka swabhaav kitna badal jaata hai: yahi alloy ka jaadu hai.

---

### Example 5. Mel ya compound? (Class 9 ki baat, naye sabut se)

**Sawaal:** Class 9 mein brass ke do namoonon se dekha tha ki alloy mixture hai. Ab sunaar ki dukaan ka ek naya sabut: 22 carat aur 18 carat, dono ko "sona" kehte hain, aur dono sone aur copper ke mel hain. Kya sona aur copper milkar koi compound bana rahe hain?

**Hal:** Compound mein tatvon ka mass anupaat fixed hota hai (Class 9: paani mein H : O hamesha 1 : 8). Yahan sona : doosri dhatu = 22 : 2 = 11 : 1 ek gehne mein, aur 18 : 6 = 3 : 1 doosre mein. Anupaat badal gaya, phir bhi dono gold alloy hain, isliye ye **mixture** hai, compound nahi.

**Jaanch:** 22 ÷ 2 = 11 aur 18 ÷ 6 = 3, dono alag ✅ (alloy ka anupaat badal sakta hai).

---

### Example 6. Paper alloy-composition task (sirf kaagaz par)

**Kaam:** Ek kaarigar ko 2 kg brass chahiye jisme 63% copper ho. Aur uske paas sirf 1 kg copper hai.

**(a)** 2 kg brass mein copper aur zinc kitna?
- Copper = 2000 × 63 ÷ 100 = **1260 g** (1.26 kg).
- Zinc = 2000 − 1260 = **740 g**.

**(b)** Sirf 1 kg copper ho, to zyada se zyada kitna brass (63% Cu) ban sakta hai?
- 1 kg (1000 g) copper 63% hai, to kul brass = 1000 ÷ 0.63 = **1587 g** (lagbhag 1.59 kg).
- Zinc chahiye = 1587 − 1000 = **587 g** (lagbhag).

**Jaanch:** (a) 1260 + 740 = 2000 g ✅. (b) 1000 ÷ 1587 × 100 = 63.0% ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: "Alloy ek naya compound hai."**
   Example 5 mein dekha: gold alloy 22 : 2 bhi hota hai aur 18 : 6 bhi (Class 9 mein brass ke saath bhi yahi dekha tha). Compound jaise paani mein anupaat kabhi nahi badalta (1 : 8). Alloy mein dhatuein pighla kar milayi jaati hain, unke apne gun bane rehte hain, aur anupaat badal sakta hai. Isliye alloy mixture hai.
2. **Galti 2: "24 carat sona gehne ke liye sabse achha hai."**
   Pure sona itna naram hai ki gehna haath se bhi mud sakta hai ya ghis jaata hai. 22 carat mein 91.67% sona hai aur baaki 8.33% copper ya chandi use mazboot banati hai, isliye gehne ke liye wahi chalta hai. Shuddhta aur mazbooti ka santulan chahiye.
3. **Galti 3: "Anode mud kachra hai."**
   Example 3 mein 1 kg gandagi mili. Isme sona, chandi, platinum jaisi keemti dhatuein hoti hain. Refinery ye gaad alag se nikaalti hai aur sona chandi alag karti hai. Is by-product se refinery ko achhi kamai hoti hai.
4. **Galti 4: "Anode aur cathode kuch bhi laga do."**
   Maan lo tumne galti se pure copper ki chadar ko anode (+) bana diya. To wahi chadar ghulegi (Cu → Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻) aur ashuddh copper par jam jayegi. Matlab pure copper ashuddh ho jayega: ulta kaam. Isliye ashuddh dhatu hamesha anode, shuddh dhatu hamesha cathode.
5. **Galti 5: "Alloy ka gala-ank dono dhatuon ke beech mein hota hai."**
   Solder is baat ko galat sabit karta hai. Tin lagbhag 232 °C par pighalta hai, lead lagbhag 327 °C par, par ye mel (lagbhag 60 : 40) lagbhag 190 °C ke aas-paas pighal jaata hai, jo dono se kam hai. Isiliye taar jodte waqt bijli ke component zyada garmi se bach jaate hain.
6. **Galti 6: "Alloy hamesha zyada bijli conduct karti hai."**
   Ulta: alloy aamtaur par shuddh dhatu se kam conductor hoti hai. Isliye bijli ki taar shuddh copper ki hoti hai, brass ki nahi.

## Quick Recap

- Dhatu nikaalne ke baad bhi use **refine** karte hain, kyunki usme gandagi bachi hoti hai.
- Refining ki vidhiyan gun ke farq par chali hain: **electrolytic** (copper), **distillation** (zinc, mercury, ubaal-ank kam), **liquation** (tin, lead, gala-ank kam), **zone refining** (bahut pure silicon, germanium).
- Copper ki electrolytic refining: ashuddh copper anode (+), shuddh copper cathode (-), acidified CuSO₄ ghol. Anode: Cu → Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻, cathode: Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu. Sona, chandi, platinum **anode mud** mein jaate hain. (Theory Chapter 4 mein.)
- **Alloy mixture hai**, compound nahi (anupaat badal sakta hai). Fayde: zyada sakht, zang se bachav, kam gala-ank, behtar rang ya awaaz.
- Steel (iron + 0.1 se 1.5% carbon), stainless steel (iron, chromium, nickel), brass (copper + zinc), bronze (copper + tin), solder (tin + lead), duralumin (aluminium + copper, magnesium, manganese), amalgam (mercury ka alloy).
- Alloy sakht isliye hoti hai kyunki alag aakaar ke atom parton ko phisalne se rokte hain.
- 22 carat sona = 22 ÷ 24 × 100 = **91.67%** sona, hallmark 916.
- Mercury aur lead zehreele hain: sirf padhne ke liye.
