# Topic 7: Corrosion Se Bachav aur Chapter Ka Saar

Pichhle topics mein humne dhatu ko dharti se nikaala, saaf kiya aur alloy bana kar kaam ka banaya. Par ek sawaal baaki hai: itni mehnat se bani dhatu **tikti kitni der hai**? Loha hawa aur paani ke beech rakho to wo dheere-dheere waapas "ore" jaisa ban jaata hai: zang. Is aakhri topic mein dekhenge ki corrosion andar se kaise kaam karta hai, usse bachne ke tareeke kaun-se hain aur kaun kis jagah behtar hai, aur ek lagbhag 1600 saal purane khambhe ne kaise hamein hairan kiya hai. Aakhir mein poore chapter ka saar.

## Part A: Corrosion ko andar se dekhna

Class 9 mein tumne dekha tha ki loha hawa aur paani dono ke saath milkar zang banata hai ([chemical-changes-and-reactions-part6.md](../../../class9/chemistry/chemical-changes-and-reactions/chemical-changes-and-reactions-part6.md)): **ek ke bina zang nahi**. Ab usi baat ko gehrai se dekhte hain.

**Zang ka asli formula.** Wahan hamne 4Fe + 3O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃ likha tha, jo simplified tha. Asli zang mein paani bhi judta hai, isliye uska formula hai **Fe₂O₃·xH₂O**. Yahan **x** ek sankhya hai jo hawa ki nami aur samay ke saath badalti rehti hai, isliye aamtaur par x ki ek pakki value nahi likhte. Poori equation:

4Fe + 3O₂ + 2xH₂O → 2Fe₂O₃·xH₂O

Atom count (kisi bhi x ke liye): baayein Fe = 4, O = 6 + 2x, H = 4x; daayein Fe = 2 × 2 = 4, O = 2 × (3 + x) = 6 + 2x, H = 2 × 2x = 4x ✅. Ye humne x = 1, 2, 3 aur 5 ke liye ginkar bhi check kiya.

```
    LOHA  +  OXYGEN (hawa)  +  PAANI   =>   ZANG
    Fe       O₂                H₂O          Fe₂O₃·xH₂O

    Teeno maujood        : loha ghat-ghat kar bhoora zang banta hai
    Loha + oxygen        : paani nahi, to zang bahut dheema
    Loha + paani         : oxygen nahi, to zang bahut dheema
    Loha akela, sukha    : kuch nahi hota, loha jaisa ka taisa
```

> **Figure 7.1** – Zang ke liye loha ke do saathi chahiye: hawa ki oxygen aur paani. Dono saath mile to zang, ek bhi na mile to zang bahut dheema.

### Zang itna khatarnak kyun, aur kaun use tez karta hai?

- **Zang bhurbhura aur chhidradaar hai.** Wo jhad jaata hai aur neeche ka taaza loha phir hawa-paani ke saamne aa jaata hai, isliye zang andar tak badhta jaata hai. Aluminium ki oxide parat ulti hai: wo kasi hui hai, isliye aage ka rasta band kar deti hai (Chapter 2: [metals-and-non-metals-part2.md](../metals-and-non-metals/metals-and-non-metals-part2.md)).
- **Namak wala paani zang tez karta hai.** Samundar ke paas ke jahaaz, pul aur railing jaldi zang khate hain. Namak ke ions paani ko bijli ka achha conductor bana dete hain, aur zang ki prakriya mein ye madad karta hai (poori vajah Chapter 4 "Electrolysis" mein).
- **Acid wali baarish zang tez karti hai.** Fuel jalne se bani SO₂ aur nitrogen ki oxide hawa ke paani mein ghul kar acid wali baarish karti hain ([atmospheric-pollution-part2.md](../../../class9/chemistry/atmospheric-pollution/atmospheric-pollution-part2.md)). Ye parat ko khaa jaati hai aur dhatu ko nanga kar deti hai.
- **Garam aur nam mausam** mein zang tez, sukhe aur thande mein dheema.

Dusri dhatuon ke corrosion ka ek-ek line mein recall (poora vivaran Class 9 mein): taambe par hara coat (basic copper carbonate) aur chaandi par kaala Ag₂S, dono ek patli parat hain jo aage ka kaam kaafi dheema kar deti hai; aluminium ki Al₂O₃ parat poori tarah rakhwali hai. Farq ye hai ki **zang apni hi suraksha nahi karta**, baaki parat karti hain.

## Part B: Teen safe rust-test (ghar par adult ke saath)

Class 9 ka teen keel wala test tum kar chuke ho. Ab teen naye tests, jo ye batayenge ki **bachne ke tareeke asal mein kaam karte hain ya nahi**. Har test mein adult paas rahe, kuch bhi chakha nahi jaata, aur har cheez dekh kar haath dho lo.

### Test 1: Coating ka farq (ghar par adult ke saath)

Chaar saaf, naye loha ke keel lo aur chaar glass mein thoda sa **tap water** daalo, jaise keel aadha doobe:

1. Keel A: bilkul nanga.
2. Keel B: poora tel ya grease se lipta hua.
3. Keel C: poora paint (ya nail polish) se dhaka, sukhne ke baad.
4. Keel D: paint ki hui, par ek jagah **sandpaper ya kisi kate se paint khurch di** (adult kare).

5 se 7 din dekho. **Aamtaur par dikhega:** A par sabse zyada zang, B aur C par lagbhag nahi, aur D par us khurchi hui jagah par hi zang, jo kabhi-kabhi paint ke neeche bhi rengti hai. Natija: **barrier tab tak kaam karta hai jab tak poora hai**.

### Test 2: Namak ka asar (ghar par adult ke saath)

Do bilkul ek jaise loha ke keel lo. Ek glass mein tap water, doosre mein utne hi paani mein ek chammach namak ghola hua. Dono ko ek jaisi jagah par rakho. Kuch din mein **namak wale glass ka keel zyada aur jaldi zang khayega**. Paani ko chakhna nahi hai, bas dekhna hai.

### Test 3: Alloy ka asar (ghar par adult ke saath)

Ek loha ka keel aur ek **stainless steel ka purana chamach** ek hi namak wale paani ke glass mein rakho. Kuch hi dinon mein keel par zang dikhne lagegi, par chamach lagbhag saaf rahega. Natija: loha ke saath chromium milane se alag cheez ban jaati hai (Topic 6 ka alloys).

> **Safety:** kisi bhi test mein acid, bleach ya bijli nahi lagti. Glass khaane-peene ki cheezon se alag rakho, paani ya keel chakho nahi, aur kaam ke baad haath dho lo.

## Part C: Bachne ke tareeke ek table mein

Corrosion se bachne ka seedha niyam: **dhatu ko hawa aur paani se door rakho**, ya phir kisi aur ko pehle "kurbaan" hone do. Neeche saare tareeke saath-saath hain.

| Tareeka | Kaise kaam karta hai | Scratch ho jaye to | Kahan istemal hota hai |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Paint** | Hawa aur paani ke beech parat | Wahan zang shuru, aur paint ke neeche failti hai | Gate, railing, pul |
| **Tel ya grease** | Chikni parat jo paani ko hataati hai | Dubara lagana padta hai | Cycle ki chain, tools, kabza |
| **Galvanising (zinc)** | Barrier, aur zinc khud pehle ghul jaata hai | Phir bhi loha bachta hai | Chaadar, balti, bijli ke khambhe |
| **Tin plating** | Sirf barrier (tin kam reactive hai) | Loha aur tez zang khata hai | Khaane ke dabbe |
| **Chromium plating** | Chamakdar kasi parat | Barrier toot gaya, wahan zang shuru | Cycle ka handle, bumper |
| **Anodising** | Aluminium ki moti oxide parat | Parat gehri hai, kam chhilti hai | Khidki ke frame, rangeen bartan |
| **Alloying (stainless steel)** | Chromium ki khud ki surakshit parat | Naye khule hisse par parat khud ban jaati hai | Rasoi ke bartan, chamach |
| **Sacrificial block** | Zyada reactive dhatu pehle ghulti hai | Kaam karti rehti hai jab tak block hai | Jahaaz ki hull, zameen ke neeche pipeline |

> Ek bada farq: **barrier** wale tareeke tab tak kaam karte hain jab tak parat poori ho. **Sacrificial** tareeka scratch hone par bhi loha ko bachata hai. Ab dekhte hain ye kaise.

## Part D: Galvanising versus tin plating, reactivity series ka sawaal

Tum jaante ho ki reactivity series (Chapter 2: [metals-and-non-metals-part4.md](../metals-and-non-metals/metals-and-non-metals-part4.md)) mein **zinc loha se upar** hai. Chapter 2 ki seedhi mein tin nahi tha, par is chapter ki seedhi mein (Topic 1, Figure 1.3) tin Fe aur Pb ke beech aata hai, yaani **tin loha se neeche**:

Zn  >  Fe  >  Sn  (zyada reactive se kam reactive)

Isi ek line se galvanising aur tin plating ka poora farq nikalta hai. Dono mein loha ki chaadar par ek aur dhatu ki parat hai. Jab tak parat poori hai, dono barrier hain. Asli farq tab dikhta hai jab **parat chhil jaaye** aur loha, parat wali dhatu ke saath, paani ke samne khul jaaye.

```
    GALVANISED (zinc ki parat)          TIN-PLATED (tin ki parat)

    zzzzzzzz   zzzzzzzzzzzzzz           tttttttt   tttttttttttttt
    zzzzzzzz\ /zzzzzzzzzzzzzz           tttttttt\ /tttttttttttttt
    ==========================          ==========================   <- loha (Fe)
             ^                                   ^
          scratch                              scratch
          paani ki boondein                    paani ki boondein

    Zn  >  Fe : zinc zyada reactive     Fe  >  Sn : loha zyada reactive
    zinc pehle ghulta hai               loha pehle ghulta hai
    Zn → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻                     Fe → Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻
    loha BACHTA hai                     loha ZYADA tez zang khata hai
```

> **Figure 7.2** – Dono taraf loha par ek dhatu ki parat hai aur dono mein scratch lagi hai. Baayein: zinc, jo loha se zyada reactive hai, pehle ghulta hai aur loha bachta hai. Daayein: tin, jo loha se kam reactive hai, ghulta nahi aur loha pehle ghul kar zang banta hai, nanga loha se bhi tez.

Yahan ek zaroori baat: Zn → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻ mein zinc **electron deta hai** (Chapter 2 ka reactivity ka kaaran). Ye electron kaise chalte hain aur kyun loha bachta hai, uski poori kahani Chapter 4 "Electrolysis" mein.

### Solved reasoning example: kaun si chaadar chuni jaaye?

**Sawaal:** Ek gaon mein baarish ka paani jama karne ke liye do tanki hain. Tanki P galvanised loha ki hai, tanki Q tin-plated loha ki hai. Dono tanki mein ek-ek jagah par patthar se khurch lag gayi. Do saal baad ek tanki ki chaadar khurch ke paas se phoot gayi aur ek ab bhi theek hai. Kaun si tanki phooti, aur kyun?

**Hal:**
1. Series: Zn > Fe > Sn. P par zinc hai, Q par tin.
2. P mein khurch par loha ke saath zinc ki parat bhi khuli hai. Zinc, loha se zyada reactive hai, to zinc pehle ghulta hai aur loha bachta hai. **Tanki P theek rahegi.**
3. Q mein khurch par loha tin ke saath khula hai. Tin, loha se kam reactive hai, to loha pehle ghulta hai, aur wahi "chhote chhed" se andar zang badhti hai. **Tanki Q phooti.**
4. Jawab: **Q phooti, P theek hai.**

**Jaanch:** Agar khurch na lagti to dono tanki aur kai saal barrier ki wajah se chalti. Farq sirf khurch ke baad dikhta hai ✅. Aur zinc ka ghulna Zn → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻: charge 0 = (+2) + (−2) ✅.

> **Zinc ki parat lambe samay tak kyun tikti hai?** Zinc khud hawa mein ek patli, safed, kasi parat (oxide aur carbonate) banata hai, jo aage ghulne ko bahut dheema kar deti hai. Isliye galvanised chaadar kai saal tak chalti hai.

## Part E: Sacrificial protection aur anodising

### Jahaaz ki hull par magnesium ka block

Samundar ka namak wala paani steel ki hull ka bada dushman hai. Paint kaafi nahi: scratch to lagti hi rehti hai. Isliye jahaaz ki hull ke neeche, paani mein, **magnesium ya zinc ke bade block** bolt ya weld se jode jaate hain. Magnesium, loha se zyada reactive hai (Mg > Fe), to wo dheere-dheere ghulta hai: Mg → Mg²⁺ + 2e⁻. Steel bachta rehta hai. Block "kurbaan" hota hai, isliye isko **sacrificial anode** kehte hain.

```
    SAMUNDAR KA PAANI (namak wala)
    ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

    #########                   JAHAAZ KI HULL (steel, loha ka alloy)
    #########  =[ Mg ]=         <- magnesium block, hull se judha hua
    #########
    #########  =[ Mg ]=         <- doosra block
    #########
    #########

    Mg → Mg²⁺ + 2e⁻ : block dheere-dheere ghulta hai
    steel hull : bachi rehti hai
    Block chhota hote-hote ghisa to naya laga do (kuch saal mein)
```

> **Figure 7.3** – Jahaaz ki steel hull se magnesium ke block jude hain. Magnesium loha se zyada reactive hai, isliye samundar ke paani mein wahi pehle ghulta hai aur hull bachti hai. Ghis jaane par block badal diya jaata hai.

Yahi tareeka **zameen ke neeche ki loha ki pipeline** aur **paani ki tanki** ke liye bhi hai: gaadi hui pipeline ke saath magnesium ya zinc ke block jode jaate hain. Block ke liye wahi dhatu chalti hai jo loha se **upar** hai (Mg, Zn). Taambe ya tin ka block kaam nahi aayega, kyunki wo loha se kam reactive hain.

> **Yaad rakho:** block ka kaam tabhi hai jab wo **hull se jura ho**. Alag rakha hua block kuch nahi karta.

### Anodising: aluminium ki apni parat ko moti banana

Aluminium hawa mein khud ek bahut patli Al₂O₃ ki parat bana leta hai (Chapter 2). **Anodising** mein is parat ko **jaan-boojh kar kai guna moti** banaya jaata hai: aluminium ko dilute acid ke ghol mein **anode** banakar bijli guzaari jaati hai. Anode par oxygen banti hai jo aluminium se mil kar oxide parat ko aur moti karti hai. Phir parat mein rang bhi bhara ja sakta hai, isliye anodised aluminium ke bartan aur frame rangeen aur chikne hote hain. (Ye prakriya **sirf padhne ke liye** hai: acid aur bijli ka saath students nahi chalate. Anode kya hota hai, ye Chapter 4 mein aayega.)

## Part F: Delhi ka Iron Pillar, 1600 saal ka raaz

Ab Vrinda ke sawaal par wapas. Delhi ke Qutub parisar ka Iron Pillar lagbhag **1600 saal purana** (Gupta kaal ka) aur lagbhag **7 meter** ooncha hai. Isne saalon ki baarish aur dhoop jhel li, aur aaj bhi uspar zang bahut kam hai. Ye jaadu nahi hai aur stainless steel bhi nahi. Jaanch karne walon ke hisaab se:

- Ye bahut **shuddh wrought iron** (peete hue loha) ka bana hai, jisme carbon bahut kam hai, aur **phosphorus ki matra aam loha se zyada** hai (lagbhag).
- Iske upar ek **patli, kasi hui parat** ban gayi hai jisme loha, oxygen aur phosphorus ke yaugik hain, aur wahi parat **passive layer** ki tarah neeche ka loha bachati hai.
- Delhi ki hawa ke **zyadatar sukhe mausam** ne bhi madad ki hai.

Ye khamba poori tarah zang-rodhi nahi hai, bas bahut dheere zang khata hai. Aur ye usi baat ki kahani hai jo hum seekh rahe hain: **kasi aur surakshit parat** corrosion rok sakti hai. Zang jhadne wali aur chhidradaar hoti hai, isliye wo suraksha nahi deti, par is khambhe ki parat alag hai. Ab khamba railing se ghira hua hai, taaki log ise chhoo kar parat na kharab karein.

## Part G: Corrosion ki keemat aur recycling

Corrosion sirf dikhne ki cheez nahi: pul, pipeline, jahaaz, gaadi aur machine ko badalna padta hai. Kuch anumaan ke hisaab se kai deshon mein corrosion ki keemat poore desh ki aay ka lagbhag 3 se 4 pratishat tak jaati hai (lagbhag, anumaan). Is liye bachaav ke saath **recycling** bhi zaroori hai:

- Dhatu ke ore **seemit** hain: ek baar kharch hue to naye ore dhundhne padte hain.
- Naye ore se dhatu nikaalne mein bahut energy lagti hai (Topic 4, 5). **Purani dhatu pighlane mein kam**: scrap steel ko dobara pighla kar naya steel banane mein lagbhag 60 se 70 pratishat energy bachti hai (lagbhag), aur aluminium mein Topic 5 ki tarah lagbhag 5 pratishat energy lagti hai.
- Kooda kam hota hai aur khadano ki zaroorat bhi.
- Zang khayi hui loha ki cheezein bhi **kabadiwale ke paas** jaaye to dobara pighal kar naya steel ban jaata hai.

Kabadiwale ke dher ko tum ferrous aur non-ferrous mein baant sakte ho (Chapter 2 ka aakhri topic), aur ab jaante ho ki wo dher "kooda" nahi, **kam energy mein naya dhatu** hai.

## Part I: Mixed problems

### Mixed 1: Kitna zang banta hai? (mass ki ginti)

**Sawaal:** Maan lo x = 3, yaani zang Fe₂O₃·3H₂O hai. 8.4 g loha poora zang ban jaye to kitna zang banega, aur kitni oxygen aur kitna paani judega?

**Hal:** Equation (x = 3): 4Fe + 3O₂ + 6H₂O → 2Fe₂O₃·3H₂O.
- Fe ke moles = 8.4 ÷ 56 = 0.15 mol.
- Zang ke moles = 0.15 × 2/4 = 0.075 mol. Zang ka molar mass = 2 × 56 + 3 × 16 + 3 × 18 = 112 + 48 + 54 = 214 g/mol, to mass = 0.075 × 214 = **16.05 g**.
- O₂ ke moles = 0.15 × 3/4 = 0.1125 mol, to mass = 0.1125 × 32 = **3.6 g**.
- H₂O ke moles = 0.15 × 6/4 = 0.225 mol, to mass = 0.225 × 18 = **4.05 g**.

**Jaanch:** 8.4 + 3.6 + 4.05 = 16.05 g ✅. Atom count: Fe 4 = 4, O 6 + 6 = 12 = 2 × (3 + 3) = 12 ✅, H 12 = 2 × 6 = 12 ✅. Zang ka bhaar loha se zyada hai, kyunki oxygen aur paani judte hain.

---

### Mixed 2: Magnesium ka block kitna loha bachata hai?

**Sawaal:** Ek pipeline ke saath 9.6 kg ka magnesium block jura hai. Maan lo ki block ke har Mg atom ki jagah ek Fe atom ghulta (dono 2 electron dete hain: Mg → Mg²⁺ + 2e⁻ aur Fe → Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻). Block poora ghulne par kitna loha bachta?

**Hal:**
- Mg ke moles = 9600 ÷ 24 = 400 mol.
- Itne hi mol Fe ghulta (1 Mg ke badle 1 Fe), to Fe ka mass = 400 × 56 = 22 400 g = **22.4 kg**.

**Jaanch:** Mg: 400 × 24 = 9600 g ✅. Dono ke 2 electron ka hisaab barabar ✅. Magnesium halka hai (24), loha bhaari (56), isliye 9.6 kg block lagbhag 22.4 kg loha ko bacha sakta hai.

---

### Mixed 3: Galvanised keel mein zinc kitna percent?

**Sawaal:** Ek 5.0 g ke galvanised keel mein 0.2 g zinc hai. Zinc ka mass percent aur loha ka mass nikalo.

**Hal:** Zinc = 0.2 ÷ 5.0 × 100 = **4%**. Loha = 5.0 − 0.2 = **4.8 g** (96%).

**Jaanch:** 4% + 96% = 100% ✅.

---

### Mixed 4: Recycling se kitni bijli bachi?

**Sawaal:** Maan lo ki 1 kg naya steel banane mein 20 units bijli lagti hai aur 1 kg scrap se naya steel banane mein 8 units. Kitne percent ki bachat hui? 1800 kg steel ke liye kitne units bachenge?

**Hal:** Bachat per kg = 20 − 8 = 12 units. Percent = 12 ÷ 20 × 100 = **60%**. 1800 kg ke liye = 1800 × 12 = **21 600 units**.

**Jaanch:** 60% ke hisaab se 20 × 0.6 = 12 ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: "Tin plating bhi galvanising jaisi hi hai."**
   Do khurchi hui cheezein socho: ek galvanised balti aur ek tin-plated dabba. Zinc loha se zyada reactive hai, to khurch par zinc pehle ghulta hai aur loha bachta hai. Tin loha se kam reactive hai, to khurch par loha pehle ghulta hai. Isliye ek saal baad balti theek, dabba khurch ke paas zang se bhara. Dono parat dikhne mein ek jaisi hain, par khurch ke baad kaam ulta hai.

2. **Galti 2: "Paint lagaya, ab loha hamesha ke liye surakshit hai."**
   Ek gate par paint ki, par ek jagah se cycle ki takkar se paint chhil gayi. Wahan zang shuru hui aur paint ke neeche failti gayi, aur paint phool kar chhilne lagi. Paint tab tak kaam karta hai jab tak poora hai. Isliye dobara paint karna padta hai.

3. **Galti 3: "Zang bhi aluminium ki oxide parat ki tarah suraksha deta hai."**
   Ek loha ka tar aur ek aluminium ki foil, dono hawa mein kai saal rakho. Foil ki oxide parat kasi aur patli hai, isliye foil andar se bachi rehti hai. Zang bhurbhura aur chhidradaar hai, jhad jaata hai aur neeche ka loha dobara khul jaata hai. Dono "oxide" hain par alag tarah ke.

4. **Galti 4: "Stainless steel kabhi zang nahi khata."**
   Samundar ke kinare ki stainless steel ki railing par kabhi-kabhi bhoore daag dikhte hain, kyunki namak wale paani aur nami mein uski parat kabhi-kabhi toot jaati hai. Stainless steel bahut kam zang khata hai, par **kabhi nahi** nahi khata.

5. **Galti 5: "Delhi ke Iron Pillar mein kabhi zang nahi lagti ya wo stainless steel hai."**
   Pillar mein shuddh wrought iron hai, stainless steel nahi (usme chromium nahi). Usme bhi bahut dheere zang lagti hai. Bas uski surface par ek patli, kasi parat hai, aur Delhi ke sukhe mausam ne madad ki hai. Yahi khamba agar samundar ke nam, namak wale kinare par hota, to shayad itna na tikta.

6. **Galti 6: "Magnesium ka block ek baar laga do, jahaaz ke saath hamesha chalega."**
   Block dheere-dheere ghulta hai: ye uska kaam hi hai. Kuch saal baad wo bahut chhota ya gaayab ho jaata hai, aur tab hull ko bachane wala koi nahi hota. Isliye dock mein jaakar naya block lagaya jaata hai.

## Quick Recap

- Zang ka formula **Fe₂O₃·xH₂O**: 4Fe + 3O₂ + 2xH₂O → 2Fe₂O₃·xH₂O. Loha ko oxygen **aur** paani dono chahiye. Namak aur acid wali baarish zang ko tez karte hain.
- Zang bhurbhura aur chhidradaar hai, isliye andar tak badhta hai. Aluminium ki oxide parat kasi hai, isliye wo suraksha deti hai.
- Barrier tareeke: paint, tel, grease, plating. Scratch par barrier toot jaata hai.
- **Galvanising** (zinc) scratch par bhi loha bachata hai, kyunki Zn > Fe, zinc pehle ghulta hai. **Tin plating** scratch par loha ko aur tez zang dilata hai, kyunki Fe > Sn.
- **Sacrificial block** (Mg, Zn): hull ya pipeline se jode jaate hain aur pehle ghulte hain. Anodising aluminium ki oxide parat ko moti banata hai.
- Delhi Iron Pillar: lagbhag 1600 saal purana, shuddh loha, zyada phosphorus aur ek kasi parat, aur sukhe mausam ki madad.
- Recycling ore bachati hai aur energy bhi: steel mein lagbhag 60 se 70 pratishat, aluminium mein lagbhag 95 pratishat tak (lagbhag).

## Practice Questions

1. Write the balanced equation for rusting with x = 2, that is, rust of formula Fe₂O₃·2H₂O. If 33.6 g of iron rusts completely, find the mass of rust formed, the mass of oxygen and the mass of water that combine. Show that mass is conserved.
2. Why does an iron nail rust faster in salty water than in tap water? Why does the rust of iron keep spreading inwards while an aluminium object does not corrode inwards?
3. Four nails are kept in tap water for a week: (a) a bare nail, (b) a nail coated with grease, (c) a nail wrapped with a zinc strip, (d) a nail wrapped with copper wire. Use this rule: "A more reactive metal in contact with iron corrodes first and protects the iron; a less reactive metal in contact makes iron corrode faster." Which nails rust least and which rust more than the bare nail? Use the series Zn > Fe > Cu.
4. A galvanised bucket and a tin-plated can are both dented, so the iron underneath is exposed. Explain which one will rust at the dent and why. Which one is better, from the point of view of rusting only, for a bucket that is often dented?
5. A scratched coating on an iron sheet made the sheet rust faster than a bare sheet kept next to it. The coating metal M is one of Zn, Mg, Sn. Using the rule in Question 3 and the series Mg > Zn > Fe > Sn, find M.
6. A zinc block of 32.5 kg is attached to an underground pipeline. Assume each Zn atom that dissolves saves one Fe atom (both give 2 electrons). Find the moles of zinc and the mass of iron that the block can save.
7. A galvanised wire weighs 25.0 g in all, and 1.5 g of that is zinc. Find the percentage of zinc and the mass of iron.
8. A 400 g stainless steel kadhai contains 18% chromium and 8% nickel by mass, the rest being iron. Find the mass of chromium, nickel and iron in it. Why does chromium in the alloy help against corrosion?
9. A recycling plant receives 800 kg of aluminium cans and recovers 760 kg of metal. Find the percentage recovered and the mass lost. Why is the recycling of aluminium so useful compared with making it from bauxite?
10. Choose the best protection and give one reason for each: (a) the steel tower of a high-voltage electricity line, (b) the handlebar of a bicycle, (c) an underground iron oil pipeline, (d) the blade of a plough, kept in the shed between seasons, (e) an aluminium window frame at a seaside house.
11. A plumber joins an iron pipe directly to a copper pipe. At the joint, which metal will corrode faster and why? Suggest one way to avoid this problem.
12. State whether each statement is true or false and correct the false ones: (a) Rust is simply iron mixed with water. (b) Tin plating protects iron even when it is scratched. (c) A sacrificial block must be in contact with the iron it protects. (d) The Delhi Iron Pillar is made of stainless steel.
13. In your own words, explain the difference between a barrier method and a sacrificial method of protection, with one example of each.
14. Explain, with the Chapter 3 flow (ore, concentration, conversion to oxide, reduction, refining), why recycling a metal object saves more energy than making new metal. Name the steps that are skipped.

### Answers

1. Rust x = 2: **4Fe + 3O₂ + 4H₂O → 2Fe₂O₃·2H₂O.** Atom count: Fe 4 = 4, O 6 + 4 = 10 = 2 × (3 + 2) = 10 ✅, H 8 = 2 × 4 = 8 ✅. Fe ke moles = 33.6 ÷ 56 = 0.6 mol. Rust ke moles = 0.6 × 2/4 = 0.3 mol. Rust ka molar mass = 112 + 48 + 36 = 196, to mass = 0.3 × 196 = **58.8 g.** O₂ = 0.6 × 3/4 = 0.45 mol = 0.45 × 32 = **14.4 g.** H₂O = 0.6 × 4/4 = 0.6 mol = 0.6 × 18 = **10.8 g.** Jaanch: 33.6 + 14.4 + 10.8 = 58.8 g ✅.

2. Namak wala paani bijli ka achha conductor hai, aur namak ke ions zang ki prakriya ko tez karte hain, isliye zang jaldi lagti hai (poori vajah Chapter 4 mein). Zang bhurbhura aur chhidradaar hota hai, jhad jaata hai aur neeche ka taaza loha hawa-paani se milta hai, isliye wo andar tak badhta hai. Aluminium ki Al₂O₃ ki parat patli par kasi hui hoti hai, jo hawa aur paani ko andar jaane nahi deti.

3. (a) Nanga: reference. (b) Grease: barrier, bahut kam zang. (c) Zinc strip: Zn > Fe, zinc pehle ghulta hai, to loha **bachta hai** (kam zang). (d) Copper wire: Cu kam reactive hai, to loha pehle ghulta hai aur **nanga keel se bhi tez zang khata hai.** Sabse kam: (b) aur (c). Nanga se zyada: **(d)**.

4. Galvanised balti: Zn > Fe, zinc pehle ghulta hai, loha bachta hai: dent par zang **nahi** (ya bahut kam). Tin-plated dabba: Fe > Sn, loha pehle ghulta hai, to dent par zang **tez** hai. Zang ki nazar se, aksar dabne wali bucket ke liye **galvanised** behtar hai.

5. Jo coating loha se **kam** reactive hai wahi khurch par loha ko tez zang dilayegi. Series Mg > Zn > Fe > Sn mein loha se kam reactive sirf **Sn** hai. Isliye **M = Sn.** Zn aur Mg loha se upar hain, wo loha ko bachate.

6. Zn ke moles = 32 500 ÷ 65 = **500 mol.** Utne hi mol Fe bachte, to Fe ka mass = 500 × 56 = 28 000 g = **28 kg.** Jaanch: 500 × 65 = 32 500 g ✅.

7. Zinc = 1.5 ÷ 25.0 × 100 = **6%.** Loha = 25.0 − 1.5 = **23.5 g** (94%). Jaanch: 6% + 94% = 100% ✅.

8. Chromium = 18% of 400 = **72 g.** Nickel = 8% of 400 = **32 g.** Loha = 400 − 72 − 32 = **296 g** (74%). Jaanch: 72 + 32 + 296 = 400 ✅. Chromium hawa mein kasi, patli, surakshit oxide parat banata hai, aur parat chhil jaaye to naye khule hisse par phir ban jaati hai, isliye loha bachta hai.

9. Percent recovered = 760 ÷ 800 × 100 = **95%.** Lost = 800 − 760 = **40 kg** (5%). Aluminium ko bauxite se banane mein purification aur Hall-Héroult electrolysis ki bahut bijli lagti hai (Topic 5), jabki recycling mein sirf pighlana hai, isliye lagbhag 5 pratishat energy hi lagti hai.

10. (a) **Galvanising** (zinc ki parat): scratch par bhi suraksha, sasti aur lambi chalti hai. (b) **Chromium plating**: chamakdar, kasi parat, dikhne mein accha (par chhilne par dhyan). (c) **Paint ya coating aur magnesium ya zinc ke block**: gaadi hui pipeline ki jaanch roz nahi hoti, to scratch par bhi kaam karne wali suraksha chahiye. (d) **Tel ya grease**: hal ka blade mitti mein ghis kar paint utaar deta, jabki shed mein rakhne se pehle tel ya grease lagana aur agle season mein saaf karna aasaan hai. (e) **Anodising** (aluminium ki moti parat): namak wali hawa mein bhi parat majboot.

11. Copper loha se kam reactive hai (Fe > Cu), to joint par **loha** pehle ghulega aur tez zang khayega. Bachne ke liye dono pipe ke beech plastic ya rubber ka insulating jod lagao, ya loha ke pipe par galvanising/coating rakho.

12. (a) **False** ❌. Zang ek naya padarth Fe₂O₃·xH₂O hai jisme iron, oxygen aur paani ke kan jude hain. (b) **False** ❌. Tin loha se kam reactive hai, scratch par loha aur tez zang khata hai. (c) **True** ✅. Judna zaroori hai taaki block pehle ghule. (d) **False** ❌. Wo shuddh wrought iron hai, jisme phosphorus zyada hai; usme chromium nahi hai.

13. **Barrier:** dhatu aur hawa-paani ke beech ek parat (jaise paint). Parat tootne par suraksha khatam. **Sacrificial:** zyada reactive dhatu (jaise Mg) jaan-boojh kar pehle ghulne di jaati hai, jo scratch par bhi loha bachati hai (jaise jahaaz ki hull par magnesium block). Galvanising dono hai: pehle barrier, scratch par sacrificial.

14. Naya dhatu: ore khodo, concentration, roasting ya calcination, reduction ya electrolysis, refining (Topic 1 se 6). Recycling mein **ore khodna, concentration, roasting ya calcination, aur reduction ya electrolysis chhoot jaate hain**; sirf scrap ko jod kar pighlana aur zaroorat par refining karni hoti hai. Isliye kam energy lagti hai, jaise aluminium mein lagbhag 5 pratishat.
