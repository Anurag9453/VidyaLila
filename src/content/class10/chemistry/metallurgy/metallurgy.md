# Topic 1: Dhatu Dharti Mein Kahan Milti Hai: Mineral, Ore aur Gangue

Pichhle chapter mein humne dhatuon ko khoob parakha: chamak, bijli, awaaz, oxygen, paani, acid, aur ek lambi seedhi (reactivity series). Par ek sawaal baaki reh gaya tha: ye dhatuein **aati kahan se hain?** Hamare ghar ka loha, taamba, aluminium ka foil, chandi ke sikke: koi dukaan mein ugta to nahi. Is chapter ka naam **Metallurgy** hai: dhatuon ko unke **ore** se nikaalne, saaf karne aur kaam ke laayak banane ka vigyaan. Aaj ka pehla kadam: dharti ke andar kya milta hai, aur use kya kehte hain.

## Museum ki gallery mein "boring" patthar

Mr. Pethe apne bhanje Devansh ko shahar ke museum ki **mineral gallery** mein le gaye, kyunki Devansh ke school ne agle hafte yahan ki trip rakhi thi. "Mama, yahan to bas patthar hi patthar hain!" Devansh ne munh bana kar kaha. Kaanch ke dibbon mein dhool-jaise bhure, kaale aur laal-bhure tukde rakhe the, aur har ek ke neeche ek chhoti si patti: *Haematite*, *Magnetite*, *Bauxite*, *Galena*, *Zinc blende*, *Malachite*.

Mr. Pethe muskuraye. "Ye patthar tumhari cycle ki frame, ghar ke bartan aur wiring ka **pehla roop** hain. Ye **Haematite** dekho: laal-bhura sa, bilkul bekaar. Isse hi duniya ka bahut saara loha nikalta hai. Ye **Bauxite**: laal mitti jaisa tukda, aur isi se aluminium ka foil banta hai. Ye hara **Malachite**: isme taamba hai."

"Par Mama, loha to chamakta hai, ye to bilkul nahi chamak raha!"

"Wahi to baat hai. Zameen ke andar dhatu aksar **chamakti hui dhatu ke roop mein nahi**, kisi **yaugik (compound)** ke roop mein milti hai, jaise loha, oxygen ke saath mila hua." Phir wo gallery ke ek kone mein gaye jahan ek chhota sa dibba alag chamak raha tha: peele rang ka ek tukda. "Ye **sona** hai, seedha dharti se nikla hua, bina kisi mehnat ke saaf. Baaki sab patthar dull dikhte hain, par ye chamakta hai."

"To sona alag kyun hai?" Devansh ki aankhein chamak uthin. "Kyunki sone ko dharti ke andar kisi se milne ki jaldi nahi thi," Mr. Pethe ne hans kar kaha. "Is baat ka jawab seedhi (reactivity series) mein chhupa hai. Chalo, ek-ek patthar ka raaz kholte hain."

## Part A: Mineral, ore aur gangue: teen alag shabd

### 1. Mineral

Dharti ki parat (crust) mein kai hazaar tarah ke mineral milte hain. **Mineral** wo padaarth hai jo **kudrat mein apne aap** milta hai aur jiski banawat aur formula tay hota hai. Kuch mineral mein dhatu hoti hai (haematite mein loha), aur kuch mein koi dhatu nahi hoti (quartz, jo ret ka mukhya ghatak hai).

### 2. Ore

Har mineral se dhatu nikaalna **munafe ka sauda** nahi hota. **Ore** wo mineral hai jisse dhatu **sasti, aasaan aur kaafi matra mein** nikaali ja sake.

Ek misaal: aluminium **chikni mitti (clay)** mein bhi hota hai, aur bauxite mein bhi. Par chikni mitti se aluminium nikaalna itna mehenga hai ki koi nahi karta, jabki bauxite se nikaalna sasta padta hai. To **bauxite ore hai, chikni mitti nahi**, jabki dono mein aluminium hai.

Dhyan do: ore ki parimaasha **samay ke saath badal** sakti hai. Dhatu ki keemat badhne par ya naya tareeka aane par, jo patthar kal bekaar tha wo kal ore ban sakta hai.

### 3. Gangue

Khaan se nikla ore **kabhi shuddh nahi hota**. Usme mitti, ret aur kankar hote hain. Is bekaar hisse ko **gangue** (ya matrix) kehte hain. Aage ke topics mein ye dekha jaayega ki gangue ko kaise alag karte hain; abhi bas itna samjho ki **ore = kaam ka mineral + gangue**.

```
   ZAMEEN KI SATAH
   ==========================================
   :  ghaas, mitti                          :
   :  ----------------------------------    :
   :   chattan (bekaar)                     :
   :        ########                        :
   :      ##  ORE  ##     <-- ore ki nas    :
   :        ########          (khaan)       :
   :   chattan (bekaar)                     :
   ==========================================

   ore ke ek tukde ko bada karke dekhein (O = ore ka daana, . = gangue ka daana)

        . . . . O . . .
        . . O . . O . O
        O . . O . . . .
        . O . . . . O .
        . . . O . . O .
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Upar: zameen ke andar ore ki ek nas, jiske charon taraf bekaar chattan hai. Neeche: ore ke ek tukde ka bada roop. 40 daanon mein sirf 10 ore ke hain (O) aur 30 gangue ke (.), yaani sirf chauthai hissa kaam ka: isliye ore ko "saaf" karna padta hai (Topic 2).

## Part B: Dhatu akeli kab milti hai, aur yaugik kab?

Chapter 2 (part 2 aur part 4) mein tumne dekha ki zyada reactive dhatu hawa ke oxygen, paani aur kaarbon dioxide se **turant** ladti hai. Zameen ke andar bhi karodon saalon se wahi ho raha hai.

- **Kam reactive dhatuein** (sona, chandi, platinum, kabhi-kabhi taamba) kisi se milti hi nahi. Isliye wo **akeli (native)** milti hain. Gold seedhi mein sabse neeche hai, jaise Chapter 2 ki seedhi mein dekha.
- **Zyada reactive dhatuein** (sodium, magnesium, aluminium, zinc, loha) oxygen, sulphur, carbonate aur chloride se mil chuki hain. Isliye wo **yaugik** (ore) ke roop mein milti hain, kabhi akeli nahi.

Kaun si dhatu kis roop mein milti hai, usse ore ka **prakaar** bhi tay hota hai:

| Dhatu | Ore ka naam | Formula | Prakaar | Dikhta kaisa hai (lagbhag) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sodium | Rock salt (namak) | NaCl | Chloride | Safed ya halka rangeen |
| Magnesium | Magnesite, carnallite | MgCO₃, KCl·MgCl₂·6H₂O | Carbonate, chloride | Safed |
| Aluminium | Bauxite | Al₂O₃·2H₂O | Hydrated oxide | Laal-bhura mitti jaisa |
| Zinc | Zinc blende | ZnS | Sulphide | Bhura-peela ya kaala |
| Zinc | Calamine | ZnCO₃ | Carbonate | Safed-bhura |
| Loha | Haematite | Fe₂O₃ | Oxide | Laal-bhura |
| Loha | Magnetite | Fe₃O₄ | Oxide | Kaala, chumbak se chipakta hai |
| Lead | Galena | PbS | Sulphide | Bhaari, chamakta hua dhoosar |
| Taamba | Cuprite | Cu₂O | Oxide | Gehra laal |
| Taamba | Copper pyrites | CuFeS₂ | Sulphide | Peela, pital jaisa chamakta |
| Taamba | Malachite | CuCO₃·Cu(OH)₂ | Carbonate wala | Chatak hara |
| Mercury | Cinnabar | HgS | Sulphide | Laal |

Ek dilchasp baat: **loha ka ek mineral iron pyrites (FeS₂)** hota hai, jo peela chamakta hai aur sone jaisa dikhta hai, isliye usse **"fool's gold"** (bewakoof banane wala sona) kehte hain. Par ise aam taur par loha ka ore nahi maana jaata (isme sulphur bahut hota hai, aur ek jhalak Topic 3 mein aayegi).

**Dhyan rakho:** galena (lead), cinnabar (mercury) aur kai sulphide ore **zeherile** hote hain. Museum mein inhein sirf kaanch ke dibbon mein dekhte hain; chhoote, sunghte ya muh mein nahi daalte.

## Part C: Dhatu nikaalne ka poora safar (metallurgy ka flow)

Khaan se nikla ore seedha dhatu nahi deta. Poore safar mein paanch kadam hote hain (har dhatu mein paanchon kadam zaroori nahi: kai dhatuon mein kuch kadam chhoot jaate hain):

1. **Ore nikaalna** (mining): khaan se ore bahar laana.
2. **Concentration (ore ki safai):** gangue hatana. (Topic 2)
3. **Oxide banana:** sulphide ya carbonate ore ko garam karke **oxide** mein badalna: roasting ya calcination. (Topic 3)
4. **Reduction:** oxide se dhatu nikaalna (oxygen chheen kar). (Topic 4)
5. **Refining:** nikli hui dhatu ki ashuddhiyan hatana. (Topic 6)

```
   +------------------+
   :  1. ORE KHAAN    :   mitti, ret ke saath
   :  SE NIKALNA      :
   +------------------+
             :
             v
   +------------------+
   :  2. CONCENTRATION:   gangue hata do
   :  (ore ki safai)  :
   +------------------+
             :
             v
   +------------------+
   :  3. OXIDE BANANA :   roasting ya calcination
   :                  :   (agar ore oxide nahi hai)
   +------------------+
             :
             v
   +------------------+
   :  4. REDUCTION    :   oxide se dhatu
   :                  :
   +------------------+
             :
             v
   +------------------+
   :  5. REFINING     :   ashuddhi hatao
   :                  :
   +------------------+
             :
             v
        SHUDDH DHATU
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Metallurgy ka flow: ore se shuddh dhatu tak paanch kadam. Agar ore pehle se oxide hai (jaise haematite), to teesra kadam chhoot sakta hai; kai dhatuon mein reduction ke baad bhi refining zaroori hoti hai. Har kadam ek alag topic mein padhoge.

## Part D: Kaun si dhatu, kaun sa tareeka? (Ek jhalak)

Reduction ka tareeka dhatu ki **reactivity** par tay hota hai: dhatu jitni zyada reactive, oxygen se uska rishta utna mazboot, aur use todna utna mushkil. Seedhi ko teen hisson mein baanta ja sakta hai.

```
   K    potassium     \
   Na   sodium         \   UPAR WALI: bahut reactive
   Ca   calcium         >  oxygen se rishta bahut mazboot
   Mg   magnesium      /   ==> pighle yaugik ki ELECTROLYSIS
   Al   aluminium     /
   ------------------------------------------------
   Zn   zinc          \
   Fe   iron           >  BEECH WALI: madhyam reactive
   Sn   tin            /  ==> roasting ya calcination
   Pb   lead          /       phir CARBON se reduction
   ------------------------------------------------
   [H]  hydrogen (sirf tulna ke liye)
   ------------------------------------------------
   Cu   copper        \
   Hg   mercury        >  NEECHE WALI: kam reactive
   Ag   silver         /  ==> sirf garam karna (ya akeli hi milti)
   Au   gold          /      sone, chandi, platinum akele bhi milte hain
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Reactivity series ke teen hisse aur har ke liye mukhya tareeka (sirf ek jhalak, poori kahani Topic 4 mein). Hydrogen ko seedhi mein sirf tulna ke liye rakha gaya hai, wo dhatu nahi hai.

**Ek nayi dhatu seedhi mein:** Class 9 aur Chapter 2 ki seedhi mein tin (Sn) nahi tha. Is chapter ki seedhi mein **tin bhi aata hai, Fe aur Pb ke beech**: yaani tin loha se kam reactive hai aur lead se thoda zyada. Ye baat Topic 4 aur Topic 7 mein kaam aayegi.

| Hissa | Dhatuein | Ore ka tareeka | Mukhya tareeka |
|---|---|---|---|
| Upar (bahut reactive) | K, Na, Ca, Mg, Al | Chloride, oxide ya carbonate; kabhi akeli nahi | Pighle yaugik ki electrolysis |
| Beech (madhyam) | Zn, Fe, Sn, Pb | Oxide, sulphide, carbonate | Oxide banao, phir carbon se reduction |
| Neeche (kam reactive) | Cu, Hg, Ag, Au | Kabhi akeli, kabhi sulphide ya oxide | Sirf garam karna, ya akeli hi milti hai |

Ye ek **shuruaati nakshaa** hai. Chapter ke aage ke topics isko kadam-ba-kadam kholenge, har tareeke ki equation aur factory ke saath.

## Part F: Solved examples (ore chunne ka hisaab)

Atomic masses: O 16, Al 27, S 32, Fe 56, Zn 65, H 1, C 12. Tareeka (Class 9, percentage composition): **dhatu ka mass percentage = (yaugik mein dhatu ka kul mass ÷ yaugik ka formula mass) × 100.**

### Solved Example 1: Loha ke ore: kaun sa sabse "ameer"?

Chaar loha ke mineral: Fe₂O₃, Fe₃O₄, FeCO₃, FeS₂.

| Mineral | Formula mass | Fe ka mass | Fe ka percentage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fe₂O₃ | 112 + 48 = 160 | 112 | 112 ÷ 160 × 100 = **70%** |
| Fe₃O₄ | 168 + 64 = 232 | 168 | 168 ÷ 232 × 100 = **72.41%** |
| FeCO₃ | 56 + 12 + 48 = 116 | 56 | 56 ÷ 116 × 100 = **48.28%** |
| FeS₂ | 56 + 64 = 120 | 56 | 56 ÷ 120 × 100 = **46.67%** |

**Nateeja:** sabse zyada loha **Fe₃O₄ (magnetite)** mein hai (72.41%), phir Fe₂O₃ (70%), phir FeCO₃ (48.28%), aur sabse kam FeS₂ (46.67%).

**Par sirf percentage kaafi nahi.** Duniya mein sabse zyada loha **haematite** se nikalta hai, kyunki wo bahut badi matra mein milta hai. FeS₂ mein percentage kam hai aur sulphur ka jhanjhat bhi hai, isliye aam taur par ore nahi maana jaata. **Ore chunne ke liye percentage, matra (kitna milta hai), aur nikaalne ka aasaan tareeka teeno dekhte hain.**

### Solved Example 2: Kachche ore se asli loha kitna?

250 kg haematite ore mein 64% Fe₂O₃ hai (baaki gangue). Is mein se kitna loha nikal sakta hai (sirf kagaz par, matlab maximum)?

- Fe₂O₃ ka mass = 64% × 250 = 0.64 × 250 = **160 kg**.
- Fe₂O₃ mein 70% Fe (Example 1) hai, to Fe = 0.70 × 160 = **112 kg**.

Jaanch: 160 kg Fe₂O₃ = 1000 mol (kyunki 1 mol = 160 g, to 160 kg = 1000 mol); har mol mein 2 mol Fe, to 2000 mol Fe = 2000 × 56 = 112 000 g = **112 kg** ✅.

Asli factory mein itna nahi milta, kyunki kuch loha slag mein reh jaata hai.

### Solved Example 3: Zinc blende se zinc

970 kg zinc blende ore mein 50% ZnS hai. Zinc kitna mil sakta hai?

- ZnS ka mass = 0.50 × 970 = **485 kg**.
- ZnS ka formula mass = 65 + 32 = 97. Zn ka percentage = 65 ÷ 97 × 100 = **67.01%**.
- Zn = 485 × 65 ÷ 97 = **325 kg** (maximum).

Jaanch: 485 ÷ 97 = 5 aur 5 × 65 = 325 ✅.

### Solved Example 4: Bauxite se aluminium

Bauxite ka mineral Al₂O₃·2H₂O hai. Formula mass = 54 + 48 + 36 = **138** (2H₂O = 36). Aluminium ka mass = 2 × 27 = 54.

- Al ka percentage = 54 ÷ 138 × 100 = **39.13%**.
- 27 kg aluminium ke liye bauxite mineral = 27 ÷ (54 ÷ 138) = 27 × 138 ÷ 54 = **69 kg** (shuddh mineral maanein).

Jaanch: 69 kg × 39.13% = 27 kg ✅. Asli ore mein gangue bhi hota hai, isliye asli matra zyada lagegi.

## Part G: Ek data-padhne ka kaam

Ek factory ko do zinc ore ke beech chunna hai: **ZnS** (zinc blende) aur **ZnCO₃** (calamine). Dono ka bhaav aur matra barabar maano. Kaun sa chunna chahiye?

ZnCO₃ ka formula mass = 65 + 12 + 48 = 125, to Zn % = 65 ÷ 125 × 100 = **52%**. ZnS ka Zn % = **67.01%** (Solved Example 3).

- **Dhatu ki matra:** ZnS mein zyada zinc hai (67.01% bamukabil 52%), to 100 kg shuddh mineral se ZnS zyada zinc dega.
- **Pehla kadam:** ZnS ko hawa mein garam karte hain (roasting), jisse ZnO banta hai aur sulphur dioxide (zehreeli gas) nikalti hai; ZnCO₃ ko garam karne par (calcination) ZnO banta hai aur carbon dioxide nikalti hai. Dono ka natija ek hi hai: **ZnO**, jise aage reduce karte hain. Is par detail Topic 3 mein.
- **Faisla:** sirf percentage dekho to **ZnS**; par agar sulphur dioxide ko sambhalne ka kharcha bhi gino, to nateeja badal sakta hai. Isliye factory ke liye ore ka chunav **percentage, matra, aur tareeke ka kharcha** teeno dekh kar hota hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Mineral aur ore ek hi cheez hai."** Galat. Ek chhoti kahani: Devansh ne gallery mein quartz ka tukda dekha aur socha ki wo bhi ek "ore" hai. Mr. Pethe ne poochha: "Isme kaun si dhatu hai?" Quartz mein sirf silicon aur oxygen hain (SiO₂), koi kaam ki dhatu nahi. To quartz **mineral hai, ore nahi**. Aur clay mein aluminium hai par nikaalna mehenga, to wo bhi mineral hai, ore nahi. **Har ore mineral hai, har mineral ore nahi.**

**Galti 2: "Khaan se sirf ore nikalta hoga, gangue wahin chhoot jaata hoga."** Galat: gangue **ore ke saath hi mila hota hai**. Devansh ne socha: "To khaan mein sirf ore khodte honge, gangue chhod dete honge?" Mr. Pethe ne kaha: "Khaan mein ore aur gangue **ek hi patthar mein mile hote hain**, jaise namak aur ret mile hote hain. Pehle ek saath nikaalte hain, phir alag karte hain." Isliye concentration ka kadam zaroori hai.

**Galti 3: "Sab dhatuein dharti mein akeli milti hain."** Galat. Sirf **sona, chandi, platinum** (aur kabhi-kabhi taamba) akele milte hain. Loha, aluminium, sodium aur zinc kabhi akele nahi milte. Devansh ne poochha: "To mere ghar ki loha ki rod kahan se aayi?" Jawab: **haematite jaise ore se, kai kadam se nikaali gayi.**

**Galti 4: "Jis ore mein dhatu ka percentage zyada, wahi hamesha chuna jaayega."** Galat (Solved Example 1). Percentage ke saath **matra** aur **tareeke ki aasaani** bhi dekhte hain. Magnetite mein haematite se thoda zyada loha hai (72.41% versus 70%), par duniya ki zyada khudaai haematite ki hoti hai kyunki wo zyada milta hai.

**Galti 5: "Ore ka matlab sirf oxide."** Galat. Ore oxide, sulphide, carbonate, chloride kuch bhi ho sakta hai (table dekho). Isliye aage roasting aur calcination ki zaroorat padti hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Mineral:** dharti mein milne wala tay banawat wala padaarth. **Ore:** wo mineral jisse dhatu munafe se nikaal sakein. **Gangue:** ore ke saath mili mitti aur ret.
- Kam reactive dhatuein (sona, chandi, platinum, kabhi taamba) **akeli** milti hain; zyada reactive dhatuein oxide, sulphide, carbonate, chloride ke roop mein.
- Ore ke udaharan: bauxite Al₂O₃·2H₂O, haematite Fe₂O₃, magnetite Fe₃O₄, zinc blende ZnS, calamine ZnCO₃, galena PbS, cuprite Cu₂O, copper pyrites CuFeS₂, cinnabar HgS, rock salt NaCl.
- Metallurgy ke paanch kadam: ore nikaalna, concentration, oxide banana, reduction, refining.
- Tareeka reactivity se tay hota hai: upar wali (K, Na, Ca, Mg, Al) electrolysis se; beech wali (Zn, Fe, Sn, Pb) carbon se reduction; neeche wali (Cu, Hg, Ag, Au) sirf garam karna ya akeli.
- Dhatu ka percentage = (dhatu ka kul mass ÷ formula mass) × 100. Fe₂O₃ 70%, Fe₃O₄ 72.41%, ZnS 67.01%, Al₂O₃·2H₂O 39.13%.
- India ke ore (lagbhag): loha Odisha, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh mein; bauxite Odisha, Gujarat, Jharkhand mein; taamba Khetri mein; zinc aur lead Zawar aur Rampura Agucha mein.

3. Find the mass percentage of copper in Cu₂O and in CuFeS₂ (Cu 63.5, Fe 56, S 32, O 16). Which is richer in copper?
4. A tin ore sample of 500 kg has 30% SnO₂. Find the mass of SnO₂ in it and the maximum mass of tin it can give (Sn 119, O 16).
5. Magnesite is MgCO₃ and one chloride source of magnesium is MgCl₂. Find the mass percentage of Mg in each (Mg 24, C 12, O 16, Cl 35.5). Which has more magnesium per 100 g?
6. Rock salt from a mine is 90% NaCl, the rest being gangue. In Class 9 you found that NaCl has about 39.3% sodium. What is the maximum mass of sodium that 650 kg of this rock salt can give (Na 23, Cl 35.5)?
7. Silver glance (argentite) is Ag₂S. Find the mass percentage of silver in it (Ag 108, S 32), and the mass of silver in 124 g of pure Ag₂S.
8. A pyrolusite ore of 1000 kg contains 87% MnO₂. Find the maximum mass of manganese it can give (Mn 55, O 16).
9. A factory has a rule: it accepts a mineral only if the pure mineral has at least 60% metal by mass. The minerals offered are Cr₂O₃, MnO₂, MgCO₃ and NaCl. Using your calculations (Cr 52, Mn 55, Mg 24, Na 23, Cl 35.5, C 12, O 16), say which are accepted and which are rejected.
10. Place each of Mg, Hg, Sn, Ag, Al and Pb in the correct group of Figure 1.3 and name the method you expect for it.
11. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) Gold is found free because it is very reactive. (b) Every mineral is an ore. (c) Zinc blende is a sulphide ore. (d) In metallurgy, refining comes before concentration. (e) Gangue is the earthy impurity found with an ore.
12. Arrange these steps in the correct order: refining, reduction, mining, concentration, conversion to oxide. Then explain in two lines why clay is not an ore of aluminium although it contains aluminium.

### Answers

1. **Mineral:** dharti mein milne wala tay banawat wala padaarth. **Ore:** wo mineral jisse dhatu munafe ke saath nikaali ja sake. **Gangue:** ore ke saath mili mitti aur ret jaisi bekaar cheez. Mineral jo ore nahi: **quartz (SiO₂)**, kyunki usme koi kaam ki dhatu nahi (ya clay, kyunki usse aluminium nikaalna mehenga hai).
2. Cu₂O: **oxide** ore (copper). CuFeS₂: **sulphide** ore (copper). ZnCO₃: **carbonate** ore (zinc). NaCl: **chloride** ore (sodium). Fe₃O₄: **oxide** ore (iron). PbS: **sulphide** ore (lead). MgCO₃: **carbonate** ore (magnesium).
3. Cu₂O ka formula mass = 127 + 16 = 143. Cu ka % = 127 ÷ 143 × 100 = **88.81%**. CuFeS₂ ka mass = 63.5 + 56 + 64 = 183.5. Cu ka % = 63.5 ÷ 183.5 × 100 = **34.60%**. **Cu₂O zyada ameer hai.**
4. SnO₂ = 0.30 × 500 = **150 kg**. SnO₂ ka formula mass = 119 + 32 = 151. Sn = 150 × 119 ÷ 151 = **118.21 kg** (lagbhag, maximum).
5. MgCO₃ = 24 + 12 + 48 = 84; Mg % = 24 ÷ 84 × 100 = **28.57%**. MgCl₂ = 24 + 71 = 95; Mg % = 24 ÷ 95 × 100 = **25.26%**. **MgCO₃ mein zyada magnesium** hai per 100 g.
6. NaCl = 0.90 × 650 = **585 kg**. NaCl ka formula mass = 23 + 35.5 = 58.5. Na = 585 × 23 ÷ 58.5 = 10 × 23 = **230 kg** (maximum). Jaanch: 230 ÷ 585 × 100 = 39.3% ✅ (Class 9 wala percent).
7. Ag₂S ka mass = 216 + 32 = 248. Ag % = 216 ÷ 248 × 100 = **87.10%**. 124 g mein: 124 ÷ 248 = 0.5, to Ag = 0.5 × 216 = **108 g**.
8. MnO₂ = 0.87 × 1000 = **870 kg**. MnO₂ ka formula mass = 55 + 32 = 87. Mn = 870 × 55 ÷ 87 = 10 × 55 = **550 kg** (maximum).
9. Pehle percentage nikalo. Cr₂O₃ = 104 + 48 = 152, Cr % = 104 ÷ 152 × 100 = **68.42%** (accept). MnO₂ = 87, Mn % = 55 ÷ 87 × 100 = **63.22%** (accept). MgCO₃ = **28.57%** (reject). NaCl = lagbhag **39.3%** (Class 9 mein nikala tha; reject). To **Cr₂O₃ aur MnO₂ accept**, baaki reject.
10. **Mg aur Al:** upar wala hissa, pighle yaugik ki electrolysis. **Sn aur Pb:** beech wala hissa, oxide banao aur carbon se reduce karo. **Hg aur Ag:** neeche wala hissa, sirf garam karna ya akeli hi milti (Ag kabhi akeli bhi milti hai).
11. (a) **False** ❌: sona free isliye milta hai kyunki wo bahut **kam** reactive hai. (b) **False** ❌: har ore mineral hai, par har mineral ore nahi. (c) **True** ✅: ZnS sulphide hai. (d) **False** ❌: refining sabse aakhir mein aati hai, concentration pehle. (e) **True** ✅.
12. Sahi order: **mining, concentration, conversion to oxide, reduction, refining.** Clay mein aluminium hai par uska nikaalna bahut mehenga padta hai, jabki bauxite se sasta padta hai. Ore wo mineral hai jisse dhatu **munafe ke saath** nikaali ja sake, isliye clay ore nahi hai.
