# Topic 2: Dhatuon Ka Oxygen Se Rishta

Pichhle topic mein humne dhatuon ko unki **shakal-soorat aur bhautik gun** se pehchana: chamak, taar banna, bijli ka chalna. Ab sawaal hai unke **swabhaav** ka: jab dhatu hawa ki **oxygen** se milti hai to kya hota hai? Kuch dhatu hawa mein chup-chaap baithi rehti hain, kuch dheere-dheere rang badalti hain, aur kuch itni utaawli hain ki unhe tel mein dubo kar rakhna padta hai. Dhatu ke **oxides** ki basic prakriti hum Chapter 1 mein dekh chuke hain; yahan hum dekhenge ki **oxide banta kaise hai, kitna banta hai, aur uska dhatu par kya asar padta hai**. Aakhir mein ek dilchasp kirdaar: wo oxide jo acid aur base **dono** se milta hai.

## Taambe ka bartan aur aluminium ka foil

Mr. Chitale ke bhanje Bhargav ne rasoi ka purana sandook kholkar ek taambe ka bartan nikala. Wo kaala-hara-sa tha, jaise kisi ne usse bhula diya ho. Usi shelf par aluminium foil ka naya roll chamak raha tha, jabki wo bhi hawa mein roz rakha rehta hai.

"Mama, ye kaisi baat hai?" Bhargav ne poochha. "Dono dhatuein hain, dono ek hi hawa mein hain. Taamba kaala-hara ho gaya, aur foil aaj bhi aaina jaisa!"

Mr. Chitale ne bartan ko ek haath mein, foil ko doosre mein uthaya. "Yehi to aaj ka raaz hai, Bhargav. Hawa mein lagbhag 21% oxygen hoti hai (lagbhag har paanch mein se ek hissa), aur oxygen bahut milansaar hai: bahut saari dhatuon se jud jaati hai. Par har dhatu ka **oxide** alag tarah ka hota hai. Kisi ka oxide dhatu ko tod-phod kar bura bana deta hai. Kisi ka oxide ek **kavach** (armour) ban kar dhatu ko bacha leta hai. Chalo, ek-ek karke dekhte hain."

## Part A: Dhatuon aur hawa ki oxygen: ek nazar

Dhatu + oxygen → **dhatu ka oxide**. Class 9 mein hum isse **oxidation** keh chuke hain (oxygen judna). Par raftaar bahut alag-alag hoti hai: **tez jalna**, **dheema rang badalna**, ya **bilkul na badalna**.

| Dhatu | Hawa mein kya hota hai | Equation | Dhyan rahe |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sodium (Na) | Kate hue taaze tukde ki chamak kuch hi pal mein dhundhli ho jaati hai; garam karne par jalta hai | 4Na + O₂ → 2Na₂O | Sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo); kerosene mein rakhte hain |
| Magnesium (Mg) | Ribbon par halki dhundhli parat; garam karne par chakachaundh safed roshni ke saath jalta hai | 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO | Sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo); roshni seedhi na dekho |
| Aluminium (Al) | Turant patli, pakki parat; aage nahi badhta | 4Al + 3O₂ → 2Al₂O₃ | Parat bachati hai |
| Iron (Fe) | Lohe ke taar ka gucchha (iron wool) garam karne par chingaariyan deta hua jalta hai | 3Fe + 2O₂ → Fe₃O₄ | Sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo) |
| Copper (Cu) | Garam karne par kaali parat; purane bartan par kaala-hara coat | 2Cu + O₂ → 2CuO | Garam karna teacher demo |
| Silver (Ag) | Oxygen se nahi, hawa ki sulphur-gas se kaali parat | 4Ag + 2H₂S + O₂ → 2Ag₂S + 2H₂O | Dheere hoti hai |
| Gold (Au), platinum (Pt) | Hawa mein kuch nahi | koi reaction nahi | Isliye hamesha chamakte hain |

Har equation ka atom count (Jaanch): Na 4=4, O 2=2; Mg 2=2, O 2=2; Al 4=4, O 6=6; Fe 3=3, O 4=4; Cu 2=2, O 2=2; Ag 4=4, H 4=4, S 2=2, O 2=2 ✅.

Ye ek tarah ki **reactivity ladder** hai jo hum Class 9 mein dekh chuke hain: ladder mein jo dhatu upar, wo oxygen ke saath zyada utaawli; jo neeche, wo sust. Sodium aur magnesium upar hain, taamba aur chaandi neeche, aur sone-platinum ko oxygen chhoota bhi nahi.

## Part B: Sodium kerosene mein kyun?

Sodium itna utaawla hai ki kate hue naye tukde par hawa ki oxygen aur nami se **kuch hi pal mein** parat ban jaati hai, aur wo paani se to **tezi se** reaction karta hai (ye Topic 3 mein). Isliye school ke store mein sodium aur potassium **kerosene** (ya mitti ke tel) mein dubo kar rakhe jaate hain: tel hawa aur nami ko door rakhta hai.

4Na + O₂ → 2Na₂O

Sodium oxide **paani mein ghulne wala** oxide hai, aur paani se milkar **kshaar (alkali)** banata hai:

Na₂O + H₂O → 2NaOH

Isi tarah K₂O + H₂O → 2KOH, aur CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂ (chuna, jo chuna-paani banata hai). Atom count: Na 2=2, O 2=2, H 2=2 ✅; K 2=2, O 2=2, H 2=2 ✅; Ca 1=1, O 2=2, H 2=2 ✅.

Dhyan do: **saare** dhatu-oxide paani mein nahi ghulte; zyada-tar (jaise CuO, Fe₃O₄, Al₂O₃) nahi ghulte. Sirf wahi ghulte hain jo sodium, potassium, calcium jaisi upar wali dhatuon se bane hain, aur ghulne par kshaar banate hain. (Ye oxide basic kyun hote hain, wo [acids-bases-and-salts-part3.md](../acids-bases-and-salts/acids-bases-and-salts-part3.md) ke Part D mein hai: yahan dohraya nahi.)

> **Jaanch:** Sodium oxide ko paani mein ghola gaya, to ghol lal litmus ko neela kar dega. ✅ Kyunki NaOH ek kshaar hai.

**Magnesium ka jalna (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo):** Teacher chimte se magnesium ribbon ka ek tukda pakad kar jalate hain. Wo bahut tez safed roshni ke saath jalta hai (usse seedha dekhna nahi chahiye) aur peeche safed raakh jaisa **MgO** bachta hai.

## Part C: Aluminium ka kavach

Ab Bhargav ke foil par wapas aayein. Aluminium, magnesium ki tarah, utaawla dhatu hai. Hawa mein khule rakhte hi uski satah par oxygen turant judkar **Al₂O₃** ki **bahut patli** parat bana leti hai (kuch nanometre, matlab ek mm ka kuch lakh-wan hissa; ye andaaza hai). Ye parat itni **kasi hui aur chipki** hoti hai ki na paani, na hawa **andar tak** pahunchne deti hai. Neeche ki aluminium surakshit rehti hai.

```
        HAWA (O₂ aur nami)
             :   :   :
             v   v   v
   +--------------------------------+
   : O  Al  O  Al  O  Al  O  Al  O  :   <- Al₂O₃ ki kasi hui parat
   +--------------------------------+      (bahut patli, andar nahi jaane deti)
   Al  Al  Al  Al  Al  Al  Al  Al  Al
   Al  Al  Al  Al  Al  Al  Al  Al  Al      <- neeche ki shuddh aluminium
   Al  Al  Al  Al  Al  Al  Al  Al  Al         (surakshit)
   Al  Al  Al  Al  Al  Al  Al  Al  Al
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Aluminium ki satah par Al₂O₃ ki patli, kasi hui parat. Ye parat hawa aur nami ko neeche ki dhatu tak nahi pahunchne deti, isliye aluminium aage reaction nahi karta.

**Loha ke zang se bada farq:** loha ka zang (Class 9 mein dekha: [chemical-changes-and-reactions-part6.md](../../../class9/chemistry/chemical-changes-and-reactions/chemical-changes-and-reactions-part6.md)) **bhurbhura** hota hai, jhad jaata hai aur neeche ka loha phir khul jaata hai. Aluminium ka oxide **kasa hua** hota hai, jhadta nahi: ye **madad** karta hai. Isi liye aluminium ke bartan, foil aur khidki ke frame saalon tak sahi-salaamat rehte hain.

**Anodising (sirf padhne ke liye):** factory mein aluminium ki is parat ko **bijli ki madad se aur mota** banaya jaata hai. Mote oxide-coat se saaman aur mazboot, kam ghisne wala aur zyada samay chalne wala ho jaata hai. Isme rang bhi bhare ja sakte hain, isliye rang-birange anodised aluminium ke bartan aur frame milte hain. Ye kaam factory ka hai: ghar par ya school mein nahi karna.

### Safe activity: foil ki chamak (ghar par adult ke saath)

Aluminium foil ka ek tukda lo aur ek **nayi chamakdar** taraf ko dekho. Usse kuch hafte khule mein rakh do. **Dekhoge:** foil par kuch bhi bura nahi hota, bas halka dhundhla-sa rehta hai. Ab usse ek purane taambe ke sikke ya bartan se milao: wo kitna alag dikhta hai. (Foil ko ragad kar sikke mat saaf karo: foil ke kinare kaat sakte hain, bade ke saath dhyan se.)

Isi baat ka ek jaana-pahchana sabak: **aluminium ke bartan mein bahut khatti cheez (jaise imli) ko der tak mat rakho**, kyunki acid us kavach ko dheere-dheere ghula deta hai. Ye amphoteric oxide wali baat hai, jo hum Part E mein padhenge.

## Part D: Taamba aur chaandi: rang badalna

Class 9 ([chemical-changes-and-reactions-part6.md](../../../class9/chemistry/chemical-changes-and-reactions/chemical-changes-and-reactions-part6.md)) mein corrosion ke roop mein ye teen kisse ek-ek line mein aaye the: taambe ka hara coat, chaandi ka kaala hona aur aluminium ki surakshit parat. Yahan hum unhe oxygen ke rishte ki nazar se jodte hain aur ginti ke saath aage badhte hain.

**Taamba (copper).** Taambe ko garam karne par uski chamak kaali parat se dhak jaati hai. Ye kaala **copper(II) oxide** hai:

2Cu + O₂ → 2CuO

Garam karna **sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo)** hai. Par purane taambe ke bartan **bina garam kiye bhi** rang badalte hain: hawa ki oxygen, CO₂ aur nami se, saalon mein, unpar pehle **bhoora-kaala** aur phir **hara** coat jam jaata hai. Is hare coat ko **patina** kehte hain, aur ye mukhyatah **basic copper carbonate** hai (sirf naam, formula yaad nahi rakhna). Isi wajah se purane mandir ke kalash aur kai purani moortiyan hari dikhti hain. Ye hara patina bhi neeche ke taambe ko aage ghisne se **kuch had tak** bachata hai.

**Chaandi (silver).** Chaandi **oxygen se nahi**, balki hawa mein bahut kam maatra mein maujood **hydrogen sulphide (H₂S)** se kaali padti hai. Ande, pyaaz aur kuch pollution wali jagahon ki hawa mein sulphur wali gas hoti hai (Class 9 ka yahi equation, upar wale link mein):

4Ag + 2H₂S + O₂ → 2Ag₂S + 2H₂O

Kaali parat **silver sulphide (Ag₂S)** hai, oxide nahi. Dhyan do ki oxygen is reaction mein bhi shaamil hai. Tarnish, patina aur oxide-parat tino **alag** cheezein hain, par teeno ka matlab ek hi hai: hawa ki gas ne dhatu ki satah badal di.

```
   TARNISHED (kaala)                    SAAF kiya hua
   hawa ki gas se bani parat            parat hata di gayi

   ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~             ------------------------
   xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx  <- Ag₂S    Ag  Ag  Ag  Ag  Ag  Ag
   Ag  Ag  Ag  Ag  Ag  Ag               Ag  Ag  Ag  Ag  Ag  Ag
   Ag  Ag  Ag  Ag  Ag  Ag               Ag  Ag  Ag  Ag  Ag  Ag
   Ag  Ag  Ag  Ag  Ag  Ag

   dull, kaali dikhti hai               chamakdar, asli dhatu dikhti hai
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Tarnished satah par Ag₂S (ya CuO, patina) ki parat dhatu ko dhak deti hai, isliye wo dull dikhti hai. Parat hata dene par neeche ki chamakdar dhatu dikh jaati hai.

### Safe activity: taambe ke sikke ki safai (ghar par adult ke saath)

Ek purana kaala taambe ka sikka (ya bartan ka chhota hissa) lo. Ek katori mein **nimbu ka ras** ya **imli ka paani** daalo aur usmein sikka kuch minute rakho, phir halke haath se ragdo. **Dekhoge:** kaali parat hat jaati hai aur chamak laut aati hai. Kaaran: nimbu-imli ka acid taambe ke oxide aur carbonate wali parat ko ghula deta hai (oxide basic hai, Chapter 1 ka Part D). Kaam ke baad sikke ko paani se dho lo aur haath dho lo. Kuch bhi chakhna nahi hai.

### Silver spoon ka tarnish (ghar par adult ke saath)

Ek chaandi ka chammach (ya chaandi ki payal) kate pyaaz ya ek uble ande ke saath kuch din ek band dibbe mein rakho (wo anda baad mein khaana nahi, phenk dena; dibba garam nahi karna). Chamach par kaali parat dikhne lagegi: ye Ag₂S hai. Safai nimbu se ya soft kapde se karo.

**Sona aur platinum:** inpar hawa ka koi asar nahi hota, isliye ye hazaaron saal baad bhi chamakte hain (jaise museum ke sone ke gehne). Yehi wajah hai ki unhe gehne aur sikkon ke liye pasand kiya jaata hai.

## Part E: Amphoteric oxides: acid aur base dono ke dost

Chapter 1 mein humne seekha: dhatuon ke oxide **basic**, adhaatuon ke oxide **acidic**. Wahin ye bhi dekha tha ki zinc **dhatu** garam NaOH ke saath sodium zincate (Na₂ZnO₂) aur H₂ deti hai. Ab uske **oxide** ki baari. Kuch dhatuon ke oxide **beech ke** hote hain: wo acid ke saath bhi reaction karte hain aur base ke saath bhi. Aise oxide ko **amphoteric** kehte hain. Aluminium oxide (Al₂O₃) aur zinc oxide (ZnO) iske sabse jaane-pahchane example hain.

**Acid ke saath** (basic oxide ki tarah):

Al₂O₃ + 6HCl → 2AlCl₃ + 3H₂O
ZnO + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂O

**Base ke saath** (acidic oxide ki tarah):

Al₂O₃ + 2NaOH → 2NaAlO₂ + H₂O   (sodium aluminate)
ZnO + 2NaOH → Na₂ZnO₂ + H₂O   (sodium zincate)

Atom count: Al 2=2, O 3=3, H 6=6, Cl 6=6 ✅; Zn 1=1, O 1=1, H 2=2, Cl 2=2 ✅; Al 2=2, Na 2=2, O 3+2=5 = 4+1=5, H 2=2 ✅; Zn 1=1, Na 2=2, O 1+2=3 = 2+1=3, H 2=2 ✅.

```
    ACID ke saath                         BASE ke saath

    Al₂O₃  +  6HCl                        Al₂O₃  +  2NaOH
       \       /                             \        /
        v     v                               v      v
    2AlCl₃  +  3H₂O                       2NaAlO₂  +  H₂O
    (salt)    (paani)                     (sodium aluminate)

    oxide BASIC ki tarah               oxide ACIDIC ki tarah
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Amphoteric oxide (jaise Al₂O₃) acid ke saath salt aur paani banata hai (basic oxide ki tarah), aur base ke saath bhi salt aur paani banata hai (acidic oxide ki tarah).

Isi wajah se aluminium ki parat acid ya base mein **ghul** sakti hai, aur tab kavach toot jaata hai. Isliye aluminium ke bartan mein bahut khatti ya bahut kshaari cheez mat rakho.

> **Jaanch:** Basic oxide sirf acid se milta hai, acidic oxide sirf base se, aur amphoteric dono se. ✅

### Ek bahut chhota rule: kaun sa oxide kaisa?

| Oxide ka test | Acid (HCl) se | Base (NaOH) se | Nateeja |
|---|---|---|---|
| CuO, MgO, Na₂O | Reaction | Nahi | Basic |
| CO₂, SO₂ | Nahi | Reaction | Acidic (adhaatu ka) |
| Al₂O₃, ZnO | Reaction | Reaction | Amphoteric |

## Part F: Solved Examples

### Example 1. Aluminium ke foil se kitna oxide?

**Sawaal:** 5.4 g aluminium poori tarah oxygen se judkar Al₂O₃ banata hai. Kitna Al₂O₃ banega, aur kitni oxygen judi?

**Hal:**
- Al ka mole = 5.4 ÷ 27 = **0.2 mol**.
- 4Al + 3O₂ → 2Al₂O₃: 4 mol Al se 2 mol Al₂O₃, yaani 0.2 mol Al se **0.1 mol Al₂O₃**.
- Al₂O₃ ka molar mass = 2 × 27 + 3 × 16 = **102 g/mol**. To mass = 0.1 × 102 = **10.2 g**.
- Judi hui oxygen: 10.2 − 5.4 = **4.8 g**. Check: 3 mol O₂ har 4 mol Al ke liye, yaani 0.15 mol O₂ = 0.15 × 32 = 4.8 g ✅.

**Nateeja:** dhatu ke saath oxygen judne se **mass badhta hai** (5.4 g → 10.2 g).

### Example 2. Magnesium ribbon ka jalna (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo)

**Sawaal:** 2.4 g magnesium ribbon poora jalta hai. Kitna MgO bachta hai?

**Hal:** Mg ka mole = 2.4 ÷ 24 = 0.1 mol. 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO: 0.1 mol Mg se **0.1 mol MgO**. MgO = 24 + 16 = 40 g/mol, to mass = **4.0 g**. Yaani 1.6 g oxygen judi (4.0 − 2.4). Raakh ka mass ribbon se **zyada** hota hai ✅.

### Example 3. Aluminium oxide aur HCl

**Sawaal:** 10.2 g Al₂O₃ (Example 1 wala) ko HCl ke saath poora reaction karwaya jaye. Kitna HCl lagega aur kitna AlCl₃ banega?

**Hal:** Al₂O₃ = 10.2 ÷ 102 = 0.1 mol. Al₂O₃ + 6HCl → 2AlCl₃ + 3H₂O: 0.1 mol se **0.6 mol HCl** lagega, yaani 0.6 × 36.5 = **21.9 g**. AlCl₃ ka mole 0.2, molar mass = 27 + 3 × 35.5 = 133.5, to mass = **26.7 g**. Paani 0.3 mol = **5.4 g**. Jaanch: reactants = 10.2 + 21.9 = 32.1 g; products = 26.7 + 5.4 = 32.1 g ✅.

### Example 4. Data padhkar oxide ki pehchaan

**Sawaal:** Ek lab mein chaar alag oxide P, Q, R, S ko HCl aur NaOH ke ghol ke saath test kiya gaya.

| Oxide | HCl mein | NaOH mein |
|---|---|---|
| P | Ghul gaya, ghol hara | Nahi ghula |
| Q | Ghul gaya | Ghul gaya |
| R | Nahi ghula | Ghul gaya |
| S | Nahi ghula | Nahi ghula |

Har ek ki prakriti batao, aur kaun sa oxide shayad aluminium ka hoga.

**Hal:**
- **P**: sirf acid se, to **basic oxide** (jaise CuO, jiska ghol hara-neela hota hai).
- **Q**: dono se, to **amphoteric oxide**. Aluminium ka oxide ya zinc ka oxide yahi hoga.
- **R**: sirf base se, to **acidic oxide** (jaise CO₂ ya SO₂, adhaatu ka).
- **S**: kisi se nahi, to is test mein **na acidic na basic** dikha. Ye ek **neutral oxide** ho sakta hai (jaise CO ya N₂O, jo adhaatuon ke hain; Topic 5 mein), ya koi aisa oxide jo is test ke samay mein bahut dheere ghulta hai. Pakka kehne ke liye aur test chahiye.

Isliye **Q** aluminium ka oxide hoga.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Aluminium oxygen se reaction nahi karta, isliye chamakta hai"**
Karta hai, aur turant! Par ban gayi patli oxide-parat aage ka reaction rok deti hai.

**Galti 2: "Har oxide dhatu ko kharab karta hai, jaise loha ka zang"**
Nahi. Aluminium ka oxide **bachata** hai, loha ka zang jhadta hai aur dhatu ko khata hai.

**Galti 3: "Saare dhatu-oxide paani mein ghulkar kshaar banate hain"**
Sirf kuch (Na₂O, K₂O, CaO). CuO, Fe₃O₄, Al₂O₃ jaise zyada-tar nahi ghulte. Demonstration (sirf soch kar ya teacher demo; chuna aur CuO students nahi chhoote): kaala CuO powder paani mein daalo to wo neeche baith jaata hai aur laal litmus laal hi rehta hai; par chuna (CaO) paani mein daalo to garmi ke saath chuna-paani banta hai jo laal litmus ko neela kar deta hai.

**Galti 4: "Chaandi ka kaalapan oxide hai"**
Nahi, wo silver sulphide (Ag₂S) hai, jo hawa ki sulphur-gas se banta hai.

**Galti 5: "Amphoteric matlab oxide na acid na base"**
Amphoteric matlab **dono** ke saath reaction karna.

**Galti 6: "Sodium ko paani mein rakhte hain"**
Bilkul ulta! Sodium ko kerosene mein rakhte hain, kyunki paani se wo tezi se reaction karta hai.

## Quick Recap

- Dhatu + oxygen → **dhatu ka oxide**; raftaar dhatu ke reactivity par nirbhar: Na, K bahut tez, Mg garam karne par chakachaundh, Al turant patli parat, Fe aur Cu garam karne par, Ag, Au, Pt nahi (Ag sulphur-gas se kaala).
- Sodium ko kerosene mein rakhte hain kyunki wo hawa aur nami se turant reaction karta hai; **Na₂O + H₂O → 2NaOH** (soluble oxide kshaar banata hai).
- **Aluminium** par Al₂O₃ ki patli, kasi hui parat andar ki dhatu ko bachati hai; **anodising** usse mota banati hai.
- **Taamba:** garam karne par kaala CuO; purane bartan par hara **patina**. **Chaandi:** Ag₂S se kaali (tarnish), oxide se nahi. Sona aur platinum nahi badalte.
- **Amphoteric oxides** (Al₂O₃, ZnO) acid aur base dono se salt aur paani dete hain.
- Dhatu ke oxide banne par mass badhta hai: 5.4 g Al → 10.2 g Al₂O₃.

## Practice Questions

3. Write the balanced equation for the burning of iron wool in air. Why is this shown only by a teacher?

4. 16.8 g of iron is converted completely into Fe₃O₄. Calculate the mass of Fe₃O₄ formed and the mass of oxygen that combined.

5. A copper wire of mass 12.7 g is heated strongly in air until it is fully black (CuO). Find the mass of CuO formed. Why is the black coating different from the green coat seen on old copper statues?

6. A silver anklet kept for months in a drawer next to rubber bands (rubber contains sulphur) turns black. Name the black substance, write the equation, and explain why it is not an oxide even though oxygen takes part.

7. 10.8 g of silver turns completely into silver sulphide. Find the mass of Ag₂S formed.

8. Explain why an aluminium sheet does not "rust" like iron even though aluminium is more reactive than iron. Draw a simple labelled sketch of the protective layer.

9. What is anodising? Give two benefits and one use.

10. Define an amphoteric oxide. Write two equations to show that zinc oxide is amphoteric.

11. 8.1 g of ZnO is treated with enough HCl. Find (a) the moles of ZnO, (b) the mass of HCl needed, and (c) check that the total mass is conserved. (Atomic mass of Zn is 65.)

12. 8.1 g of ZnO is also treated with NaOH solution. How many grams of NaOH are needed, and what mass of sodium zincate is formed?

13. 20.4 g of Al₂O₃ is dissolved in NaOH solution. Find the mass of NaOH required and the mass of NaAlO₂ formed. Verify the conservation of mass using the mass of water formed.

14. A teacher shows four oxides W, X, Y and Z. W reacts only with NaOH, X reacts with both HCl and NaOH, Y reacts only with HCl, and Z dissolves in plain water to give a solution that turns red litmus blue. Classify W, X and Y as acidic, basic or amphoteric. What kind of oxide is Z, and name one metal whose oxide could behave like Z?

15. State whether true or false, and correct the false ones:
    (a) Gold forms a black oxide on exposure to air.
    (b) The layer of Al₂O₃ protects aluminium from further reaction.
    (c) Tarnish on silver is silver oxide.
    (d) All metal oxides dissolve in water to form alkalis.

### Answers

1. Sodium hawa ki oxygen aur nami se turant reaction karta hai (aur paani se tezi se), isliye usse kerosene mein dubo kar rakhte hain: tel hawa aur nami ko door rakhta hai. Taaza kate tukde par: **4Na + O₂ → 2Na₂O** (Na 4=4, O 2=2 ✅).

2. Na ka mole = 4.6 ÷ 23 = 0.2 mol. 4Na + O₂ → 2Na₂O: 0.2 mol Na se 0.1 mol Na₂O. Na₂O = 2 × 23 + 16 = 62 g/mol, to mass = **6.2 g**. Na₂O + H₂O → 2NaOH: 0.1 mol se 0.2 mol NaOH = 0.2 × 40 = **8.0 g** (NaOH = 23 + 16 + 1 = 40). Check: 6.2 g + 1.8 g paani (0.1 mol) = 8.0 g ✅.

3. **3Fe + 2O₂ → Fe₃O₄** (Fe 3=3, O 4=4 ✅). Garam taar chingaariyan deta hua jalta hai, isliye sirf teacher demo mein dekhte hain.

4. Fe ka mole = 16.8 ÷ 56 = 0.3 mol. 3Fe → 1Fe₃O₄, to 0.1 mol Fe₃O₄. Fe₃O₄ = 3 × 56 + 4 × 16 = 232 g/mol, to mass = **23.2 g**. Judi oxygen = 23.2 − 16.8 = **6.4 g** (0.2 mol O₂ × 32 = 6.4 g ✅).

5. Cu ka mole = 12.7 ÷ 63.5 = 0.2 mol. 2Cu + O₂ → 2CuO: 0.2 mol CuO. CuO = 63.5 + 16 = 79.5 g/mol, to mass = **15.9 g**. Kaala coat garam karne par bana oxide hai (CuO), jabki purani moortiyon ka hara coat saalon mein hawa ki oxygen, CO₂ aur nami se bana basic copper carbonate (patina) hai.

6. Rubber bands se dheere-dheere sulphur wali gas (jaise H₂S) nikalti hai. Kaala padarth **silver sulphide Ag₂S** hai: **4Ag + 2H₂S + O₂ → 2Ag₂S + 2H₂O** (Ag 4=4, H 4=4, S 2=2, O 2=2 ✅). Oxygen reaction mein hissa leti hai, par wo H₂S ke hydrogen ke saath paani (H₂O) ban kar nikal jaati hai; chaandi ke saath **sulphur** judta hai. Isliye kaali parat **oxide nahi, sulphide** hai.

7. Ag ka mole = 10.8 ÷ 108 = 0.1 mol. 4Ag → 2Ag₂S, to 0.05 mol Ag₂S. Ag₂S = 2 × 108 + 32 = 248 g/mol, to mass = **12.4 g**.

8. Aluminium zyada reactive hai, isliye wo turant oxygen se judkar **Al₂O₃** ki patli, kasi hui, chipki parat banata hai. Ye parat hawa aur nami ko andar ki dhatu tak nahi pahunchne deti, isliye aage reaction ruk jaata hai. Loha ka zang bhurbhura hota hai aur jhad jaata hai, to neeche ka loha phir khul jaata hai. Sketch (Figure 2.1 jaisa): oopar "hawa", beech mein "Al₂O₃ parat", neeche "shuddh Al".

9. **Anodising**: aluminium ki oxide parat ko bijli ki madad se aur mota karna. Fayde: dhatu ghisne aur corrosion se zyada surakshit, aur rang bhare ja sakte hain. Use: khidki ke frame ya rang-birange bartan.

10. **Amphoteric oxide** wo hai jo acid aur base dono ke saath reaction karke salt aur paani banata hai. ZnO + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂O (Zn 1=1, O 1=1, H 2=2, Cl 2=2 ✅) aur ZnO + 2NaOH → Na₂ZnO₂ + H₂O (Zn 1=1, Na 2=2, O 3=3, H 2=2 ✅).

11. (a) ZnO = 65 + 16 = 81 g/mol; 8.1 ÷ 81 = **0.1 mol**. (b) ZnO + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂O: 0.2 mol HCl = 0.2 × 36.5 = **7.3 g**. (c) ZnCl₂ = 65 + 71 = 136 g/mol, 0.1 mol = 13.6 g; paani 0.1 mol = 1.8 g. Reactants: 8.1 + 7.3 = 15.4 g; products: 13.6 + 1.8 = 15.4 g ✅.

12. ZnO + 2NaOH → Na₂ZnO₂ + H₂O: 0.1 mol ZnO ke liye 0.2 mol NaOH = 0.2 × 40 = **8.0 g**. Na₂ZnO₂ = 2 × 23 + 65 + 2 × 16 = 143 g/mol, to 0.1 mol = **14.3 g**. Check: 8.1 + 8.0 = 16.1 g; products 14.3 + 1.8 (paani) = 16.1 g ✅.

13. Al₂O₃ = 20.4 ÷ 102 = 0.2 mol. Al₂O₃ + 2NaOH → 2NaAlO₂ + H₂O: 0.4 mol NaOH = 0.4 × 40 = **16.0 g**; NaAlO₂ = 23 + 27 + 32 = 82 g/mol, 0.4 mol = **32.8 g**; paani 0.2 mol = 3.6 g. Reactants: 20.4 + 16.0 = 36.4 g; products: 32.8 + 3.6 = 36.4 g ✅.

14. **W**: sirf base se, to **acidic oxide**. **X**: dono se, to **amphoteric oxide**. **Y**: sirf acid se, to **basic oxide**. **Z**: paani mein ghulkar kshaar banata hai (laal litmus neela), to **basic oxide jo paani mein ghulta hai**, jaise Na₂O, K₂O ya CaO (sodium, potassium ya calcium ka oxide). Na₂O + H₂O → 2NaOH.

15. (a) **False** ❌. Sona hawa mein koi reaction nahi karta. (b) **True** ✅. (c) **False** ❌. Chaandi ka tarnish silver sulphide (Ag₂S) hai. (d) **False** ❌. Sirf kuch (Na₂O, K₂O, CaO) ghulte hain; CuO ya Al₂O₃ nahi ghulte.
