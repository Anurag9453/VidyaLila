# Topic 3: Dhatuon Ki Paani Aur Acid Se Takkar

Topic 2 mein humne dekha ki hawa ki oxygen se dhatuon ka rishta alag-alag hota hai: sodium turant, magnesium jalne par, iron dheere-dheere, aur gold bilkul nahi. Ab hum oxygen ki jagah do aur "takkar wale" saathi lete hain: **paani** (thanda, garm aur bhaap) aur **acid**. Class 9 mein tumne dekha tha ki sodium paani par daudta hai aur Chapter 1 mein ki dhatu dilute acid se hydrogen deti hai. Aaj hum un sabko ek **seedhi par** rakhenge, ek ladder, aur do naye kirdaar milenge: **nitric acid**, jo hydrogen deta hi nahi, aur **aqua regia**, jo wo gold bhi ghula deta hai jise koi akela acid nahi chhoota.

## Part A: Dhatu aur paani: tezi ki seedhi

### 1. Seedhi ke upar se neeche tak

Class 9 mein tumne dekha tha ki sodium paani par daudta hai, calcium dheere react karta hai aur copper bilkul nahi karta. Ab hum poori seedhi bana lete hain. Is seedhi par **jitni zyada reactive dhatu**, utni **kam garmi** mein wo paani se react kar leti hai, aur **jitni kam reactive**, utni **zyada garmi** (bhaap) chahiye. Jo bahut hi kam reactive hain, unhe bhaap bhi nahi hilati.

```
   DHATU        PAANI KE SAATH KYA HOTA HAI
   --------------------------------------------------------
   K  (potassium)   thande paani se BAHUT tez, hydrogen
                    hamesha jal uthti hai
   Na (sodium)      thande paani se bahut tez, hydrogen
                    aksar jal uthti hai
   Ca (calcium)     thande paani se dheere, bubbles ke saath
                    upar uthta hai
   Mg (magnesium)   thande paani se bahut dheere, GARM paani
                    se, aur bhaap mein jalta hai
   Al (aluminium)   sirf bhaap se (oxide parat hatne par)
   Zn (zinc)        sirf bhaap se
   Fe (iron)        sirf bhaap se (aur wo bhi garm iron)
   --------------------------------------------------------
   Pb (lead)        paani ya bhaap se kuch nahi
   Cu (copper)      kuch nahi
   Hg (mercury)     kuch nahi
   Ag (silver)      kuch nahi
   Au (gold)        kuch nahi

   ^ upar: kam garmi chahiye, tez reaction
   v neeche: zyada garmi ya koi reaction nahi
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Ladder of vigour: dhatuon ka paani ke saath vyavhar. Upar se neeche jaate hue reaction dheema hota hai, thande paani se garm paani, phir bhaap, aur aakhir mein koi reaction nahi.

### 2. Har pagdandi ka equation

Ye saare reactions ek hi dhaage mein bandhe hain: **dhatu + paani → dhatu ka hydroxide (ya oxide) + hydrogen**. Thande ya garm paani ke saath aksar hydroxide banta hai, aur jab paani ki jagah bhaap ho aur dhatu thodi kam reactive ho, to oxide banta hai (bhaap ki garmi mein hydroxide tik nahi paata).

| Dhatu | Condition | Equation |
|---|---|---|
| Potassium | Thanda paani | 2K + 2H₂O → 2KOH + H₂ |
| Sodium | Thanda paani | 2Na + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + H₂ |
| Calcium | Thanda paani | Ca + 2H₂O → Ca(OH)₂ + H₂ |
| Magnesium | Garm paani | Mg + 2H₂O → Mg(OH)₂ + H₂ |
| Magnesium | Bhaap | Mg + H₂O → MgO + H₂ |
| Aluminium | Bhaap | 2Al + 3H₂O → Al₂O₃ + 3H₂ |
| Zinc | Bhaap | Zn + H₂O → ZnO + H₂ |
| Iron | Bhaap | 3Fe + 4H₂O → Fe₃O₄ + 4H₂ |

*(Jaanch, har equation ka atom-count: 2K + 2H₂O: K 2, H 4, O 2 aur 2KOH + H₂: K 2, O 2, H 2 + 2 = 4 ✅. Ca + 2H₂O: Ca 1, H 4, O 2 aur Ca(OH)₂ + H₂: Ca 1, O 2, H 2 + 2 = 4 ✅. 2Al + 3H₂O: Al 2, H 6, O 3 aur Al₂O₃ + 3H₂: Al 2, O 3, H 6 ✅. 3Fe + 4H₂O: Fe 3, H 8, O 4 aur Fe₃O₄ + 4H₂: Fe 3, O 4, H 8 ✅. Baaki chaar bhi isi tarah balanced hain.)*

Teen baatein dhyan se:

- **Jab bhi paani ke saath dhatu react kare, hydrogen milti hai.** Dhatu paani ke H ko chhudaakar apni jagah le leti hai (displacement, Class 9 wali kahani).
- **Calcium thande paani mein neeche nahi baithta, upar tairta hai.** Kyunki hydrogen ke bubbles dhatu ke tukde ke saath chipak jaate hain aur use oopar utha lete hain. Paani doodhiya ho jaata hai, kyunki Ca(OH)₂ paani mein bahut kam ghulta hai.
- **Sodium aur potassium ka paani se reaction itna tez hota hai** ki bani hui garmi hydrogen ko jalne ke liye kaafi hoti hai. Potassium mein ye hamesha hota hai, sodium mein aksar.

> **Activity 3.1 (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo): Dhatu aur paani.**
> Teacher ek bade, chaude kaanch ke trough mein thanda paani lete hain aur chhoti si, daal ke dane jitni sodium ya calcium ki tikki daalte hain (sirf thodi si matra, safety screen ke peeche, bachche door). Sodium paani par daudta hai aur sisakne ki awaaz aati hai; calcium bubbles ke saath upar neeche hota hai. Sodium aur potassium ke baare mein teacher alag se batayenge: wo hamesha kerosene ke neeche rakhe jaate hain (Topic 2) aur wahi se nikaalkar kaate jaate hain. **Students sodium, potassium, calcium ya magnesium ko kabhi nahi chhoote, na ghar par, na lab mein.**

> **Activity 3.2 (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo): Magnesium aur bhaap.**
> Garm bhaap mein magnesium ka ribbon jalta hai, aur teacher sirf screen ke peeche se dikhate hain. Roshni itni tez hoti hai ki use **seedhe nahi dekhna**. Iron, zinc aur aluminium ke saath bhaap ke reactions sirf trained log, khas apparatus mein, karte hain: student inhe kabhi nahi karte, aur band container ko kabhi garm nahi karte.

Magnesium thande paani mein kyun dheere hai? Kyunki uski satah par **oxide ki patli parat** hoti hai (Topic 2), jo paani ko dhatu tak pahunchne nahi deti. Garm karne par ya bhaap mein, ye parat toot jaati hai aur reaction chalu ho jaata hai. Aluminium ke saath bhi aisa hi hota hai: apni Al₂O₃ parat hatne par hi bhaap ke saath react karta hai.

### 3. Hydrogen ka hisaab (sirf mass aur moles)

Ab ek naya sawal: ek hi mass ki do dhatu lein, to kaun zyada hydrogen degi? Iske liye **mole ratio** chahiye. Atomic masses: Na 23, Mg 24, Al 27, Ca 40, Fe 56, Zn 65, H 1, O 16.

**Solved Example 1.** 4.6 g sodium thande paani se kitni hydrogen aur kitna NaOH deta hai?

2Na + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + H₂

- Sodium ke moles = 4.6 ÷ 23 = **0.2 mol**.
- Equation mein 2 mol Na se 1 mol H₂ banti hai, to 0.2 mol Na se **0.1 mol H₂** = 0.1 × 2 = **0.2 g hydrogen**.
- 2 mol Na se 2 mol NaOH, to 0.2 mol NaOH = 0.2 × 40 = **8 g NaOH** (NaOH = 23 + 16 + 1 = 40).
- *(Jaanch, mass bachti hai: 4.6 g Na + 0.2 mol × 18 g paani = 3.6 g, kul 8.2 g. Aage: 8 g NaOH + 0.2 g H₂ = 8.2 g ✅)*

**Solved Example 2.** 10 g ki chaar dhatuon ko bhaap ke saath garm karein to kaun kitni hydrogen degi?

Chaaron equations ka mole ratio (dhatu : hydrogen): Mg 1 : 1, Al 2 : 3, Zn 1 : 1, Fe 3 : 4.

| Dhatu (10 g) | Moles dhatu | Moles H₂ | Mass H₂ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Magnesium | 10 ÷ 24 = 0.4167 | 0.4167 | 0.83 g |
| Aluminium | 10 ÷ 27 = 0.3704 | 0.3704 × 1.5 = 0.5556 | 1.11 g |
| Zinc | 10 ÷ 65 = 0.1538 | 0.1538 | 0.31 g |
| Iron | 10 ÷ 56 = 0.1786 | 0.1786 × 4 ÷ 3 = 0.2381 | 0.48 g |

Padhai: **aluminium sabse zyada hydrogen deta hai (1.11 g)**, kyunki uske atom halke hain (27) aur har 2 atom 3 H₂ dete hain. **Zinc sabse kam (0.31 g)**, kyunki uska atom bhaari hai (65).

### Jaanch (Part A)

"Copper bhaap se hydrogen deta hai." ❌ Nahi. Copper ladder ke neeche hai: bhaap se bhi nahi. "Calcium thande paani mein tairta hai kyunki wo paani se halka hai." ❌ Nahi. Calcium paani se bhaari hai (density 1.55 g per cm³); wo hydrogen ke bubbles ke saath chipakne se uthta hai.

## Part B: Dhatu aur dilute acid: raftaar ki seedhi

Zn, Mg aur Fe ke dilute acid ke saath **equations** Class 9 ([hydrogen-part2.md](../../../class9/chemistry/hydrogen/hydrogen-part2.md)) mein, aur Al ka equation Chapter 1 ke Topic 3 ([acids-bases-and-salts-part3.md](../acids-bases-and-salts/acids-bases-and-salts-part3.md)) mein aa chuka hai: dhatu + dilute acid → salt + hydrogen. Class 9 mein ek moti baat bhi kahi thi ki Mg tez, Zn madhyam aur Fe dheere hai. Yahan wo equations dobara nahi. Hum ek naya kaam karenge: **ginti ke data se tezi ki seedhi padhna**.

> **Activity 3.3 (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo): Raftaar ki daud.**
> Teacher chaar test tube mein barabar maatra ka dilute HCl lete hain. Har mein barabar size ka ek tukda dalte hain: magnesium ka ribbon, aluminium ki patti (satah saaf karke), zinc ka tukda aur iron ki keel. Ek paanchve tube mein copper ka tukda. Bachche thodi doori se 30 second tak har tube ke bubbles ginte hain. **Students sirf dekhte aur ginte hain; acid aur dhatu sirf teacher sambhalte hain.**

Ek classroom ke demo se mile bubbles (30 second mein, yeh sample data hai, taaki hum padhna seekhein):

| Dhatu | Bubbles (30 s mein) |
|---|---|
| Magnesium | 60 |
| Aluminium | 42 |
| Zinc | 18 |
| Iron | 6 |
| Copper | 0 |

```
   Dhatu       Bubbles (30 s mein), har # = 3 bubbles
   ----------------------------------------------------
   Mg    60    ####################
   Al    42    ##############
   Zn    18    ######
   Fe     6    ##
   Cu     0
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Raftaar ki seedhi: dilute acid ke saath dhatuon ke bubble-count ka bar chart (sample data). Mg sabse tez, phir Al, Zn, Fe, aur Cu mein koi bubble nahi.

Padhne ki baat: bubble ginti jitni zyada, dhatu utni **zyada reactive**. Ye order wahi hai jo Class 9 ki reactivity series mein tha: **Mg > Al > Zn > Fe > (H) > Cu**. Aluminium shuru mein dheere chalta hai kyunki uski oxide parat acid ko rokti hai (Topic 2); jab acid parat ko ghula deta hai, bubbles tez ho jaate hain.

Copper aur silver se **hydrogen nahi** banti, kyunki ye ladder mein hydrogen ke **neeche** hain. Gold aur platinum ko dilute acid hilata hi nahi. Mercury ek chamakdaar **taral dhatu** hai: wo bhi dilute acid se hydrogen nahi deta. **Mercury ko sirf describe kiya jaata hai**: iski bhaap zehreeli hoti hai, isliye thermometer toot jaaye to chhoona nahi, teacher ko batao.

**Solved Example 3 (data padho).** Teacher ne chaar anjaan dhatuon P, Q, R, S ko dilute HCl mein dala. 30 second ke bubbles: P: 24, Q: 0, R: 9, S: 15. Dhatuon ko tezi ke order mein likho aur batao kaun copper jaisi hai.

- Order (zyada se kam): **P (24) > S (15) > R (9) > Q (0)**.
- **Q** mein koi bubble nahi, isliye Q hydrogen ke neeche, copper ya silver jaisi hai.
- Agar ek aur jaankari mile ki P ne thande paani ko bhi chhua tha, to P ladder ke bahut upar hai; hum us tak Topic 4 mein wapas aayenge, jab hum seedhi se **anjaan dhatu ki jagah** dhundhna seekhenge.

## Part C: Nitric acid: jo hydrogen nahi deta

Ab tak lagta hai ki acid ka kaam hi dhatu se hydrogen dena hai. Par **nitric acid (HNO₃)** alag hai. Wo ek **strong oxidising acid** hai: wo apne andar ke nitrogen se dhatu ko oxidise kar deta hai, aur bani hui hydrogen ko khud paani mein badal deta hai. Isliye **dhatu + nitric acid se hydrogen nahi milti**, balki nitrogen ke oxides milte hain.

Taambe ke saath concentrated nitric acid:

Cu + 4HNO₃ (concentrated) → Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2NO₂ + 2H₂O

*(Jaanch: Cu 1=1, H 4=4, N 4 = 2 + 2 = 4, O 12 = 6 + 4 + 2 = 12 ✅)*

Is reaction mein **bhoori, zehreeli gas nitrogen dioxide (NO₂)** nikalti hai, aur ghol **hara-neela** ho jaata hai (copper(II) nitrate). Ye **sirf describe** kiya ja raha hai: concentrated nitric acid aur NO₂ ka kaam sirf trained log hawadaar cupboard (fume cupboard) mein karte hain. Bahut hi dilute nitric acid kuch dhatuon, jaise magnesium, ke saath thodi hydrogen de sakta hai, par ye ek apvaad hai.

Gold aur platinum par nitric acid bhi asar nahi karta. To kya karein? Yahin se aqua regia ki kahani shuru hoti hai.

## Part D: Aqua regia: shahi paani aur noble metals

**Aqua regia** banane ka nuskha: **concentrated nitric acid ka 1 hissa** aur **concentrated hydrochloric acid ke 3 hisse**. Ye mix taaza banaya jaata hai, kyunki thodi der baad apne aap badal jaata hai.

Kaam kaise karta hai (simple tasveer): nitric acid gold ko thoda-sa oxidise karke uske atoms ko ion banata hai. Hydrochloric acid ke chloride ions in ions ko pakad kar ghol mein le jaate hain. Ye pakad itni mazboot hoti hai ki gold apni jagah chhod deta hai aur ghulta chala jaata hai. Aakhir mein **peele-sunehre rang ka ghol** milta hai (isme gold ka chloride compound hota hai; uska naam sirf padhne ke liye chloroauric acid hai). **Ek akela acid ye kaam nahi kar sakta**: nitric acid akela oxidise kar leta hai par ghol mein le jaane wala koi nahi, aur HCl akela oxidise hi nahi kar sakta. Dono saath milkar kamaal karte hain.

```
        1 hissa conc. HNO₃  +  3 hisse conc. HCl
                         \     /
                          v   v
                +-----------------------+
                :    AQUA REGIA         :   <- shahi paani
                :   (taaza mix)         :
                :                       :
                :        [Au]           :   <- gold ka tukda
                +-----------------------+
                           :
                           v
                +-----------------------+
                :  peela-sunehra ghol   :   <- gold ghul gaya
                +-----------------------+
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Aqua regia gold ko ghulata hai (sirf describe, teacher bhi nahi karte): 1 hissa nitric acid aur 3 hisse HCl milakar shahi paani banta hai, jisme gold ka tukda ghul kar peele-sunehre ghol mein badal jaata hai.

Aqua regia gold aur platinum ko ghula deta hai, jo koi akela acid nahi karta, isiliye ye **noble metals** kehlaate hain: raja jaise, dusron ke saath mel nahi khaate. Sonar aur refinery isse **purane gold ko saaf karne** aur ratna-aabhushan ke gold ko alag karne mein use karte hain. Ye sab **sirf trained logon** ka kaam hai: aqua regia bahut khatarnak, rasayanik jalan karne wala aur zehreeli gas deta hai. **Student ke liye ye sirf padhne ke liye hai**, aur isko kabhi band bottle mein nahi rakhte (gas bante-bante bottle phat sakti hai).

Sonar ek aur kaam bhi karta hai: **kasauti** par gold ghis kar nitric acid ki ek boond se parakhta hai. Sachcha gold nitric acid se nahi hilta, par nakli, taambe wala, ghul kar hara ho jaata hai. Ye bhi sirf describe hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

- "Har acid dhatu se hydrogen deta hai." Galat: **nitric acid** hydrogen nahi deta. Demonstration: Cu + 4HNO₃ → Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2NO₂ + 2H₂O ke products padho: Cu(NO₃)₂, NO₂, H₂O. H₂ kahin nahi hai; acid ka saara hydrogen paani mein chala gaya.
- "Calcium paani se halka hai isliye tairta hai." Galat: calcium ki density lagbhag 1.55 g per cm³ hai, paani (1) se zyada; akela tukda doobna chahiye. Wo upar isliye aata hai kyunki hydrogen ke bubbles us par chipak kar use utha lete hain.
- "Aqua regia koi ek bahut tez acid hai jo sab kuch ghula deta hai." Galat: ye do acid ka **mix** hai (1 : 3), jisme dono ka alag kaam hai: HNO₃ oxidise karta hai, HCl ke chloride ions ghol mein le jaate hain. Isliye na akela HNO₃ gold ghulata hai, na akela HCl. Aur ye sab kuch nahi ghulata: kaanch par iska asar bahut kam hota hai.
- "Ek hi mass ki dhatu ho to sabse reactive dhatu sabse zyada hydrogen degi." Galat: hydrogen ki maatra **mole ratio aur atomic mass** se tay hoti hai, reactivity se nahi. Solved Example 2 dekho: 10 g Al (kam reactive) 1.11 g H₂ deta hai, jabki 10 g Mg (zyada reactive) sirf 0.83 g.
- "Sodium paani se react karta hai, isliye uska ek chhota tukda ghar par dekh sakte hain." Galat aur khatarnak: daal ke dane jitna tukda bhi aag pakad sakta hai aur garam NaOH ke chheente uda sakta hai. Kabhi nahi; ye sirf teacher demo hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Paani ke saath seedhi:** K, Na (thanda paani, bahut tez) > Ca (thanda, dheere) > Mg (garm paani, bhaap) > Al, Zn, Fe (sirf bhaap) > Pb, Cu, Hg, Ag, Au (kuch nahi).
- Dhatu + paani → hydroxide (ya oxide) + **hydrogen**.
- Ek hi mass se **aluminium sabse zyada hydrogen** deta hai (Al, Mg, Fe, Zn mein), kyunki uska atom halka hai.
- Dilute acid ke saath raftaar: Mg > Al > Zn > Fe > Cu (Cu mein koi nahi). Equations ke liye Chapter 1.
- **Nitric acid** ek oxidising acid hai: hydrogen ki jagah NO₂ jaisi gas deta hai. Cu + 4HNO₃ → Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2NO₂ + 2H₂O.
- **Aqua regia** = 1 hissa HNO₃ + 3 hisse HCl: gold aur platinum ko ghulata hai. Dono noble metals hain.
- Sodium, potassium, calcium, magnesium, bhaap, nitric acid, aqua regia aur mercury: **sirf padhne ya teacher demo ke liye**.

## Practice Questions

1. Lead, aluminium, calcium, silver and zinc: for each say whether it reacts with cold water, only with steam, or not at all, and give the reason from the reactivity series.

2. Lithium, like sodium, reacts with cold water to give lithium hydroxide (LiOH) and hydrogen. Balance Li + H₂O → LiOH + H₂ and show the atom count on both sides. Where would you expect lithium to sit on the ladder of Figure 3.1 compared with sodium, if lithium reacts more gently?

3. 7.8 g of potassium reacts with cold water (2K + 2H₂O → 2KOH + H₂). Find the moles and the mass of hydrogen formed, and the mass of KOH (K = 39, O = 16, H = 1).

4. 5.4 g of aluminium is heated in steam (2Al + 3H₂O → Al₂O₃ + 3H₂). Find the mass of hydrogen and of aluminium oxide formed (Al = 27, O = 16). Check that mass is conserved.

5. 20 g of calcium reacts with cold water. Find the mass of hydrogen and of calcium hydroxide, and explain why the calcium piece rises to the top.

6. Compare 4 g of calcium and 4 g of sodium reacting with cold water. Which gives more hydrogen? Show the numbers.

7. A teacher drops four unknown metals W, X, Y and Z in dilute HCl and counts bubbles in 30 seconds: W 55, X 0, Y 20, Z 7. Arrange them from most to least reactive. Which one could be silver or copper? Which one is likely magnesium-like?

8. In the reaction Cu + 4HNO₃ → Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2NO₂ + 2H₂O, 3.175 g of copper reacts (Cu = 63.5, N = 14, O = 16, H = 1). Find the moles of copper, the mass of NO₂ and the mass of HNO₃ used. Why is this reaction only described?

9. A trained worker in a gold refinery needs 120 mL of aqua regia. How much concentrated nitric acid and how much concentrated hydrochloric acid should be mixed? Why can neither acid alone dissolve gold?

10. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) Mercury reacts with dilute HCl to give hydrogen. (b) Aqua regia dissolves gold. (c) Iron and steam give Fe₃O₄ and hydrogen. (d) Nitric acid usually gives hydrogen with metals. (e) A student may test sodium in a glass of water at home.

11. Gold ornaments stay shiny in sweat and rain for years, while silver spoons turn black. Use today's topic and Topic 2 to explain.

### Answers
