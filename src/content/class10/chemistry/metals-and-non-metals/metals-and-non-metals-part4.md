# Topic 4: Reactivity Series Ka Poora Istemal

Topic 3 ke aakhir mein humne dhatuon ko paani aur acid se milaya aur ek seedhi bani. Aaj us seedhi ko **kaam par** lagate hain. Ek seedhi sirf ratne ke liye nahi hoti: usse kagaz par hi **bhavishyavaani** ki ja sakti hai (kaun si reaction hogi), usse **anjaan dhatuon ka order** data se nikala ja sakta hai, aur usse **gram aur mole ke hisaab** bhi lagte hain. Ant mein ek dhamakedaar application: railway ki patriyon ki welding.

---

## Railway yard mein chingariyon ka phool

Mr. Hardikar apne bhatije Harshal ko ek Sunday railway yard ke bahar wali pagdandi par le gaye. Door ek khaali patri par kuch mazdoor safed helmet aur kaale chashme pehne khade the. Unke beech do patriyon ke sire kuch milimetre ke gap ke saath rakhe the, aur gap ke upar ek mitti-jaisa saancha (mould) bandha tha. Ek mazdoor ne ek chhoti si cheez jalayi, aur achanak saancha **chingariyon ke phool** ki tarah phat pada: laal-peeli roshni, dhuaan, aur kuch hi der mein saancha ke neeche se pighla hua, tapta laal loha behta dikha. Harshal ne awaaz mein "waah!" kaha. Mr. Hardikar ne uska kandha pakda: "Peeche hi rehna. Ye bahut hi zyada garm hota hai, aur is kaam ko sirf trained log, poore safety ke saath karte hain."

Kuch minute baad ghati jam gayi, saancha hataya gaya, aur dono patriyan **ek hi lambi patri** ban chuki thi. "Chacha, sab kuch itni jaldi? Loha pighlane ke liye to bahut badi bhatti chahiye." Mr. Hardikar ne ek paper par seedhi bani: "Yahan koi bhatti nahi thi. Mazdoor ne **aluminium ka powder** aur **loha ka oxide** milakar jalaya. Aluminium ne loha ke oxide se **loha nikaal liya**, aur is jagah-badal ki ladai mein itni garmi nikli ki loha khud pighal gaya. Yahi hai displacement ka sabse bada, garm roop."

"Par aluminium ko loha se zyada taakatwar kyun maana?" Mr. Hardikar muskuraye: "Wahi sawaal hai jisse aaj ka topic shuru hota hai. Aao reactivity series ko kaam par lagate hain."

## Part A: Seedhi ek baar phir (aur uska ek hissa: hydrogen)

Class 9 ([chemical-changes-and-reactions-part3.md](../../../class9/chemistry/chemical-changes-and-reactions/chemical-changes-and-reactions-part3.md)) mein tumne reactivity series banayi thi, aur Topic 3 mein paani aur acid ke saath uska vyavhar dekha. Wahi seedhi yahan ek tasveer mein, aur is baar hydrogen ko saaf **chinhit** kiya gaya hai, kyunki hydrogen hi dilute acid se hydrogen nikalne wali dhatuon ki **sarhad** hai.

```
   SABSE ZYADA REACTIVE
          ^
     K    potassium     paani se turant, bahut tez
     Na   sodium        paani se turant, tez
     Ca   calcium       thande paani se dheere
     Mg   magnesium     garm paani, bhaap se
     Al   aluminium     bhaap se
     Zn   zinc          bhaap se
     Fe   iron          bhaap se
     Pb   lead          paani se kuch nahi
   ----------------------------------------------
     [H]  hydrogen      <== SARHAD: dilute acid se
   ----------------------------------------------   bulbule yahi tak
     Cu   copper        dilute acid se kuch nahi
     Hg   mercury       dilute acid se kuch nahi
     Ag   silver        dilute acid se kuch nahi
     Au   gold          kisi akele acid se nahi
          v
   SABSE KAM REACTIVE
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Reactivity seedhi (12 dhatu aur hydrogen): jo upar hai wo neeche wali ko uske salt ke ghol se nikaal sakti hai. Hydrogen ki lakeer ke upar ki dhatuein dilute acid se hydrogen deti hain, neeche ki nahi. Order Topic 3 ki paani aur acid wali seedhi se mel khata hai.

**Niyam ek hi line mein:** seedhi mein **upar** wali dhatu, **neeche** wali dhatu ko uske salt ke ghol se bahar nikaal deti hai. Ulta kabhi nahi.

**Do zaroori sawadhaniyan:**

1. **K, Na, Ca** ko salt ke ghol mein nahi daalte. Ye paani se hi turant ladne lagte hain (Topic 3), to salt ki dhatu ko hatane se pehle paani ke saath hi hydrogen ban jaati hai. Isliye displacement ke liye hum aksar **Mg, Al, Zn, Fe, Cu** jaisi dhatuein lete hain.
2. **Aluminium** ki strip par hawa ki Al₂O₃ ki patli parat hoti hai (Topic 2). Shuru mein wo reaction rok sakti hai; parat ragad kar ya ghol mein kuch der rakhne par reaction dikhta hai.

## Part B: Displacement ki bhavishyavaani, gehri nazar

### 1. Class 9 wala tareeka, ek line mein

Class 9 mein (Fe + CuSO₄, Zn + CuSO₄, Cu + ZnSO₄ jaise examples se) tum ye kar chuke ho: dono dhatuon ko seedhi mein dhundo; akeli dhatu upar ho to reaction hoga, warna nahi; hone par akeli dhatu salt mein ja baithti hai aur equation balance karo. Yahan hum naye salts (nitrate aur chloride) aur zyada mushkil jodiyon par chalte hain.

### 2. Solved Example 1: Pehchano, hoga ya nahi

| Dhatu (strip) | Salt ka ghol | Seedhi mein kaun upar | Reaction? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zn | AgNO₃ | Zn upar hai | Haan |
| Cu | Pb(NO₃)₂ | Pb upar hai | Nahi |
| Al | CuCl₂ | Al upar hai | Haan |
| Ag | Cu(NO₃)₂ | Cu upar hai | Nahi |
| Zn | Pb(NO₃)₂ | Zn upar hai | Haan |
| Cu | AgNO₃ | Cu upar hai | Haan |

Jahan reaction hota hai, equations (har ek ka atom count kiya hai):

- Zn + 2AgNO₃ → Zn(NO₃)₂ + 2Ag. Zn 1 = 1, Ag 2 = 2, N 2 = 2, O 6 = 6 ✅
- 2Al + 3CuCl₂ → 2AlCl₃ + 3Cu. Al 2 = 2, Cu 3 = 3, Cl 6 = 6 ✅
- Zn + Pb(NO₃)₂ → Zn(NO₃)₂ + Pb. Zn 1 = 1, Pb 1 = 1, N 2 = 2, O 6 = 6 ✅
- Cu + 2AgNO₃ → Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2Ag. Cu 1 = 1, Ag 2 = 2, N 2 = 2, O 6 = 6 ✅

> **Activity 4.1 (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo): Taambe ki taar aur silver nitrate.** Teacher ek test tube mein bahut patla silver nitrate ka ghol lete hain aur usme saaf taambe ki taar latkate hain (dastane pehne, safety chashme ke saath). Kuch minute mein taar par **chamakti chandi ke patle sui-jaise kristal** ugte hain, aur ghol dheere-dheere **halka neela** ho jaata hai kyunki Cu²⁺ ban raha hai. Silver nitrate chamdi par kaale daag chhodta hai aur ankhon ke liye nuksandayak hai, isliye **students ise khud nahi chhoote**. Lead ke salts bhi zeherile hain: Pb(NO₃)₂ wale sawaal **sirf kagaz par** hain.

### 3. Doori ka asar: kitni "ladai" tez hogi?

Seedhi mein dhatuein jitni **door-door** hon, displacement utna **tez** aur zyada garmi ke saath hota hai. Mg ko tutiya jaise Cu salt ke ghol mein dalo to hilchal turant dikhti hai; Cu ko silver nitrate mein dalo (dono paas-paas hain) to kristal dheere-dheere ugte hain. Ye ek **gunatmak** (qualitative) baat hai: kaun upar hai, ye pakki baat; kitna tez, ye halka-sa sanket.

### 4. Ionic equation: darshak ko hata do

Ghol mein salt ions ke roop mein tairta hai. Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu mein SO₄²⁻ ko koi farq nahi padta: wo **darshak ion** hai. Use hatakar sirf asli badlav likhte hain:

Fe + Cu²⁺ → Fe²⁺ + Cu

Charge check: left par +2, right par +2 ✅. Isi tarah Cu + 2Ag⁺ → Cu²⁺ + 2Ag: left par +2, right par +2 ✅ (do Ag⁺ ke liye ek Cu).

Ye ek bahut kaam ki baat hai: **ek hi ionic equation kai salts par chalti hai**. Zn + Cu²⁺ → Zn²⁺ + Cu chahe CuSO₄ ho ya CuCl₂, ek hi rahegi, kyunki darshak ion badalte hain, asli kahani nahi. Aisi reaction ko **redox** (Class 9 ke [oxidation aur reduction](../../../class9/chemistry/chemical-changes-and-reactions/chemical-changes-and-reactions-part5.md) wali baat) kehte hain: Fe ne electron khoye (oxidise hua), Cu²⁺ ne electron paaye (reduce hua).

## Part C: Zyada reactive ka matlab: electron dene ki aasani

Seedhi **kyun** aisi hai? Class 9 ke [periodic-table-part4.md](../../../class9/chemistry/periodic-table/periodic-table-part4.md) ka metallic character yaad karo: jis atom ka baahari electron **door** ya **kam** hota hai wo use aasani se **de deta hai**. Dhatu ke har atom ke liye reaction ka matlab hai: electron khona aur ion banana.

- Zn → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻ (Zn do electron khota hai)
- Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu (Cu²⁺ do electron paata hai)

Zn + Cu²⁺ mein **Zn electron dene mein zyada tez** hai, isliye Cu²⁺ wo electron le leta hai aur Cu ban kar bahar aa jaata hai. Matlab: **zyada reactive = electron aasani se dene wali dhatu**, aur "displacement" asal mein **electron ki khinchtaan ka nateeja** hai.

```
   Zn atom   -->  Zn²⁺ ion  +  2 electron      (Zn khota hai)
                                  :
                                  v
   Cu²⁺ ion  +  2 electron  -->  Cu atom        (Cu²⁺ leta hai)

   Kul:   Zn + Cu²⁺  -->  Zn²⁺ + Cu
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Displacement ko electron ki nazar se: Zn ke 2 electron Cu²⁺ ke paas jaate hain. Zn ion ban kar ghol mein jaata hai aur Cu atom ban kar strip par jamta hai. Dono taraf charge +2 hai.

Trend dekho: Na aur K jaise Group 1 ke atom mein sirf **1 baahari electron** hota hai, jo nucleus se door hai, isliye wo bahut aasani se nikalta hai; K mein Na se zyada door, isliye K aur zyada reactive. Gold ke atom ko apne electron khone mein **mehnat** lagti hai, isliye gold seedhi ke sabse neeche hai. (Seedhi ka har kadam is trend se saaf nahi nikalta: Al aur Zn jaise beech wale kadam prayog se tay hote hain. Isliye seedhi ek **prayog ka nateeja** hai aur trend uski sirf ek wajah.)

## Part D: Anjaan dhatuon ki seedhi data se banana

Ab ek detective ka kaam. Hum ek asli seedhi nahi dekh rahe: sirf **prayog ke nateeje** hain, aur unse order **saabit** karna hai. Har baat ke peeche ek **proof** hona chahiye.

### Method

1. Har "haan" (displacement hua) se ek **taaqat ka teer** likho: jo strip ki dhatu hai, wo salt wali dhatu se **upar** hai.
2. Har "nahi" se ek **ulta** teer: strip wali dhatu salt wali se **neeche** hai (ya koi proof nahi, agar strip par oxide thi).
3. Teeron ko jod kar **ek line** banao. Chain: A > B aur B > C, to A > C (bina test kiye).
4. Hydrogen ki jagah dilute acid ke bubbles se tay hoti hai; paani ke reaction se aur upar ka hissa.

### Puzzle 1: Chaar anjaan dhatu A, B, C, D (grid)

Teacher ne chaar dhatuon ki strip ko unke **sulphate ke ghol** mein dala (sirf dhatu A, B, C, D ke naam hain, asli dhatu nahi). "Haan" ka matlab strip par nayi dhatu jami; "nahi" ka matlab koi badlav nahi.

```
   Strip      A-salt     B-salt     C-salt     D-salt
   --------------------------------------------------
     A         -          haan       nahi       haan
     B         nahi       -          nahi       nahi
     C         haan       haan       -          haan
     D         nahi       haan       nahi       -
```

> **Figure 4.3** – Displacement ka natija grid: har kataar ek strip hai, har column ek salt ka ghol. A-salt mein A ki strip dalne ka koi matlab nahi, isliye "-". Is grid se A, B, C, D ka order banta hai.

**Hal.**

- Kataar C mein teen "haan": C ne A, B aur D teeno ko unke salt se hataya, to **C sabse upar** (C > A, C > B, C > D).
- Kataar B mein teen "nahi": B kisi ko nahi hata saka, to **B sabse neeche**.
- Bachi A aur D: A ne D ko hataya (A-kataar, D-salt: haan), aur D ne A ko nahi (D-kataar, A-salt: nahi). To **A > D**.
- Order: **C > A > D > B**.

Jaanch (har haan aur nahi par order tick karo): A ne B ko hataya ✅ (A > B), A C ko nahi ✅, D ne B ko hataya ✅, B ne kisi ko nahi ✅, C sab ko hataya ✅, D ne C ko nahi ✅. Koi bhi nateeja order ke khilaaf nahi hai.

### Puzzle 2: Asli dhatuein, kaagaz par (Zn, Pb, Cu, Ag)

Ek lab record mein yeh likha hai (sirf kagaz par; Pb ke salt zeherile hain):

- Zn ki strip Pb(NO₃)₂ mein: lead jam gayi. Pb ki strip ZnSO₄ mein: kuch nahi.
- Pb ki strip AgNO₃ mein: chandi jami.
- Cu ki strip AgNO₃ mein: chandi jami. Cu ki strip Pb(NO₃)₂ mein: kuch nahi.

Order nikalo.

**Hal.**

- Zn ne Pb ko hataya aur Pb ne Zn ko nahi: **Zn > Pb**.
- Pb ne Ag ko hataya: **Pb > Ag**. Cu ne Ag ko hataya: **Cu > Ag**.
- Cu ne Pb ko nahi hataya: Cu, Pb se upar nahi. To **Pb > Cu**.
- Jodo: Zn > Pb > Cu > Ag. Ye wahi hai jo Figure 4.1 mein hai (Zn, Pb, Cu, Ag ke sthaan) ✅.

Dhyan do: Pb > Ag aur Cu > Ag se Pb aur Cu ka aapas ka order nahi nikalta. Wo **Pb > Cu** sirf is "nahi" se pakka hua ki Cu ne Pb ko nahi hataya.

### Puzzle 3: Paani aur acid se hydrogen ki jagah (A, B, C, D, E)

Paanch anjaan dhatuein. Notes:

- E thande paani se bulbule deta hai; baaki chaar thande paani se kuch nahi karti.
- Dilute HCl se: A, B, D bulbule dete hain; C nahi deta.
- A ki strip B ke chloride ke ghol mein: B jami.
- B ki strip D ke chloride ke ghol mein: D jami.
- D ki strip C ke chloride ke ghol mein: C jami.

Order, aur hydrogen ki jagah batao.

**Hal.**

1. E akela thande paani se react karta hai, baaki nahi: **E sabse upar** (E > A, B, C, D).
2. Displacement chain: A > B, B > D, D > C. Chain: **A > B > D > C**.
3. Dilute HCl mein A, B, D bulbule dete hain: ye teeno **hydrogen se upar** hain. C nahi deta: **C hydrogen se neeche**.
4. Pura order: **E > A > B > D > [H] > C**. Hydrogen D aur C ke beech hai.

Jaanch: D ne C ko hataya, D hydrogen se upar aur C neeche: D > H > C ke saath mel khata hai ✅. C ek copper jaisi dhatu hai; E ek sodium ya calcium jaisi.

### Puzzle 4: Jab data poora nahi hota

Teen dhatu X, Y, Z ke tests: X ki strip Y-salt mein: Y jami. Z ki strip Y-salt mein: Y jami. Y ki strip X-salt mein: kuch nahi. Y ki strip Z-salt mein: kuch nahi. Sawal: order kya hai?

**Hal.**

- **X > Y** aur **Z > Y** pakka hai (do alag haan, do alag nahi).
- Par **X aur Z mein kaun upar hai?** Data mein X aur Z kabhi aamne-saamne nahi hue. To order **sirf aadha** pata hai: **{X, Z} > Y**.
- Poora karne ke liye ek test chahiye: **X ki strip Z-salt mein**. Agar Z jami to X > Z, nahi to (aur Z ki strip X-salt mein haan) Z > X.

Sabak: **jo data nahi hai, uska jawab nahi nikala ja sakta.** "Y sabse neeche hai" kehna theek hai, "X > Z" kehna **galat** hai.

### Puzzle 5: Kaunsa note galat hai?

Ek student ne likha: **P ne Q ko hataya**, **Q ne R ko hataya**, aur **R ne P ko hataya**. Ye sab ek saath sach ho sakta hai kya?

**Hal.**

- Pehli do se P > Q > R. Teesri se R > P. Ek hi seedhi mein P, R se upar bhi aur neeche bhi nahi ho sakta: **ye ek chakkar hai, asambhav hai.**
- To **kam se kam ek observation galat** hai. Sambhav wajah: strip par oxide ki parat, galat salt, ya ghol aur strip ka sahi se na milna.
- Hum kehte hain: seedhi **ek seedhi line** hai, gol chakkar nahi. Naya test dobara karna hoga.

## Part E: Mass aur mole ka hisaab

Atomic masses: Fe 56, Cu 63.5, Zn 65, Ag 108, Al 27, O 16, N 14, S 32.

### Solved Example 2: Loha, tutiya aur 0.1 mol

Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu. Equation 1 : 1 : 1 : 1 hai.

- 0.1 mol Fe = 0.1 × 56 = **5.6 g** Fe.
- 0.1 mol Cu banega = 0.1 × 63.5 = **6.35 g** Cu.
- Strip ka mass: 5.6 g Fe gaya, 6.35 g Cu chadha: **0.75 g badhta hai**.

Matlab loha ki strip **bhaari** ho jaati hai, kyunki Cu ka atom Fe se bhaari hai (63.5 versus 56). Class 9 ke 56 g wale hisaab ki hi chhoti copy.

### Solved Example 3: Zinc ki strip aur tutiya

Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu (1 : 1). 6.5 g Zn = 6.5 ÷ 65 = **0.1 mol**. 0.1 mol Cu = **6.35 g**. Strip: 6.5 g gaya, 6.35 g chadha, **0.15 g ghat gaya**. Yahan strip **halki** ho jaati hai, kyunki Zn (65) Cu (63.5) se thoda bhaari hai. Dono ek hi dhatu ke saath alag-alag natije dete hain: ye saabit karta hai ki **strip ka mass badhega ya ghatega, ye atomic masses par depend karta hai**, seedhi par nahi.

### Solved Example 4: Taamba aur silver nitrate

Cu + 2AgNO₃ → Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2Ag. 6.35 g Cu = 0.1 mol. 1 mol Cu se **2 mol Ag**, to 0.2 mol Ag = 0.2 × 108 = **21.6 g** Ag. Strip ka mass: 6.35 g gaya, 21.6 g aaya: **15.25 g badha**. Silver ka atom bahut bhaari hai (108), aur ek Cu do Ag nikalta hai, isliye strip ka mass bahut badhta hai.

Mass conservation check (poora system): reactants Cu 6.35 g + AgNO₃ (0.2 mol × 170 = 34 g) = 40.35 g. Products Cu(NO₃)₂ (0.1 × 187.5 = 18.75 g) + Ag 21.6 g = 40.35 g ✅.

## Part F: Thermite aur railway ki patriyan

Ab Mr. Hardikar ka rahasya. Aluminium seedhi mein **Fe se upar** hai, to Al, Fe ke **oxide** se loha nikaal sakta hai:

2Al + Fe₂O₃ → Al₂O₃ + 2Fe

Atom count: Al 2 = 2, Fe 2 = 2, O 3 = 3 ✅.

Mass check: 2Al = 54 g, Fe₂O₃ = 112 + 48 = 160 g, kul 214 g. Al₂O₃ = 54 + 48 = 102 g, 2Fe = 112 g, kul 214 g ✅.

**Ye reaction itni garmi kyun deti hai?** Aluminium ko oxygen se jodna Fe ko oxygen se jodne se **zyada** garmi deta hai (Topic 2: Al ka oxygen se rishta mazboot hai). Is faraq ki garmi bahar nikalti hai, aur wo itni hai ki loha **pighal** jaata hai (taapmaan kai hazaar °C ke aas-paas, anumaanit lagbhag 2500 °C). Isko **thermite reaction** kehte hain.

```
          chhota sa shuru karne wala (trained log)
                     :
                     v
          +----------------------+
          :  Al powder           :
          :  +  Fe₂O₃ powder     :   <-- thermite mix, saancha ke andar
          +----------------------+
                     :
                     v     bahut zyada garmi
          +----------------------+
          :   Al₂O₃ (slag)       :   <-- halka, upar tairta hai
          :   pighla hua Fe      :   <-- bhaari, neeche behta hai
          +----------------------+
                     :
                     v
            patri ke gap mein behta hai
     ===== patri 1 =====[ pighla Fe ]===== patri 2 =====
                         <-- gap -->
```

> **Figure 4.4** – Thermite ka schematic (sirf describe): Al aur Fe₂O₃ ka mix jalne par pighla loha aur halka slag (Al₂O₃) banta hai. Bhaari loha neeche behkar patriyon ke beech ke gap ko bharta hai aur thanda hokar dono sire jod deta hai. Ye kaam sirf trained log, poore safety ke saath karte hain.

**Thermite kabhi activity nahi hai.** Ye sirf padhne ke liye hai. Aag, 2000 °C se zyada garmi aur chingariyan: koi student ise na ghar par, na lab mein, na kabhi bhi dekhne ke liye paas jaye.

### Solved Example 5: Thermite ka mass-hisaab (sirf kagaz par)

280 g Fe banane ke liye kitna Al aur Fe₂O₃ chahiye?

- 280 g Fe = 280 ÷ 56 = **5 mol** Fe.
- 2Al + Fe₂O₃ → Al₂O₃ + **2**Fe: 2 mol Fe ke liye 2 mol Al aur 1 mol Fe₂O₃. 5 mol Fe ke liye **5 mol Al** aur **2.5 mol Fe₂O₃**.
- Al = 5 × 27 = **135 g**. Fe₂O₃ = 2.5 × 160 = **400 g**.
- Al₂O₃ = 2.5 mol × 102 = **255 g**.

Jaanch: 135 + 400 = 535 g; 255 + 280 = 535 g ✅.

Asli railway weld mein kai kilo mix lagta hai. Thermite ke baare mein ek aur baat: Al ka Fe se upar hona **hi** is reaction ki shart hai. Agar Cu ka powder lete (Cu, Fe se neeche), Fe₂O₃ se Fe nikalne ka koi sawal hi nahi.

## Part G: Seedhi ka kaam: kya kahan rakhein?

**1. Kaun si dhatu kis salt ke ghol ko "kha" jaayegi.** Tutiya (CuSO₄) ka ghol **loha ke tank** mein rakhne se Fe, Cu ko hata dega (Class 9 ka sprayer): tank galta hai. Isliye tutiya plastic mein rakhte hain. Iske ulta, **ZnSO₄ ka ghol taambe ke bartan** mein ghul jaata hai bina kuch hue, kyunki Cu, Zn se neeche hai.

**2. Dhatu ko dhatu se bachana.** Loha ko **zinc** ki parat se dhakte hain (galvanising). Agar parat mein kharonch bhi aa jaaye, to Zn, Fe se zyada reactive hone ki wajah se **pehle** khud corrode hota hai aur Fe bacha rehta hai. Ye **balidaan wala kavach** (sacrificial protection) Chapter 3 mein poora aayega, yahan sirf ek paragraph.

**3. Chandi ka dobara paana.** Chandi ke salt ke purane ghol mein **taamba** dalo to Cu, Ag ko hata dega: Ag wapas dhatu ban kar jam jaayegi.

**4. Dhatuon ko unke oxide se nikaalna.** Thermite wahi sikhata hai: **zyada reactive dhatu** kam reactive ke oxide se use **nikaal** sakti hai. Dhatu kaise nikalti hain (ores se), uska poora tareeka Chapter 3 (Metallurgy) mein aayega.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

## Quick Recap

- Seedhi: K, Na, Ca, Mg, Al, Zn, Fe, Pb, [H], Cu, Hg, Ag, Au. Upar wali dhatu neeche wali ko uske salt ke ghol se nikaalti hai.

## Practice Questions

1. Using the reactivity series, predict whether each reaction happens, and write the balanced equation where it does: (a) Mg + CuCl₂ (b) Fe + ZnCl₂ (c) Al + AgNO₃ (d) Cu + FeSO₄ (e) Zn + MgSO₄.
2. Five unknown metals A, B, C, D, E were tested in nitrate solutions: E displaces B; B displaces A; A displaces C; C displaces D; D does not displace C; C does not displace A. Build the order from most to least reactive and say which comparisons were proved directly and which by chain.
3. Four unknown metals W, X, Y, Z: Y reacts with cold water and the other three do not; W gives slow bubbles with dilute HCl; X and Z give none; Z displaces X from X-salt; W displaces Z from Z-salt. Give the full order and the position of hydrogen.
4. Metals A, B, C, D: A displaces B; C displaces B; D does not displace B (all three tested only in B-salt solution). What can you say about the order, and what test would you do to complete it?
5. A student's lab record for four metals says: M displaces N, N displaces R, R displaces S, and S displaces N. Show that the record contains a contradiction, name the three metals that form the loop, say which pairs should be tested again, and suggest two reasons for the error.
6. 11.2 g of iron is placed in excess copper chloride solution: Fe + CuCl₂ → FeCl₂ + Cu. Find the mass of copper formed and the change in the mass of the strip (Fe 56, Cu 63.5).
7. 13 g of zinc is placed in excess silver nitrate solution: Zn + 2AgNO₃ → Zn(NO₃)₂ + 2Ag. Find the mass of silver formed and the change in the mass of the strip (Zn 65, Ag 108).
8. 3.175 g of copper wire reacts completely with silver nitrate solution. Find the moles of Cu, the mass of Ag formed, and the mass of AgNO₃ needed (Cu 63.5, Ag 108, N 14, O 16). Check mass conservation using Cu(NO₃)₂ = 187.5 g per mol.
9. In thermite, 27 g of aluminium reacts completely with iron oxide: 2Al + Fe₂O₃ → Al₂O₃ + 2Fe. Find the mass of iron and of aluminium oxide formed, and the mass of Fe₂O₃ needed. Check mass conservation.
10. Why can copper sulphate solution not be stored in an iron vessel, while zinc sulphate solution can be kept in a copper vessel? Why is aluminium, and not copper, used in thermite?
11. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) Hydrogen is a metal because it is in the reactivity series. (b) Zn + Cu²⁺ → Zn²⁺ + Cu is balanced in charge. (c) The strip always gains mass in a displacement. (d) Thermite is a safe class demonstration. (e) If A displaces B and B displaces C, then A displaces C.
12. Explain, with the idea of ease of losing electrons, why potassium is more reactive than zinc and why gold stays shiny. Then, as a paper task, copy Figure 4.1 from memory and mark the position of hydrogen.

### Answers

1. (a) **Haan.** Mg seedhi mein Cu se upar. Mg + CuCl₂ → MgCl₂ + Cu. Mg 1 = 1, Cu 1 = 1, Cl 2 = 2 ✅. (b) **Nahi.** Fe, Zn se neeche. (c) **Haan.** Al + 3AgNO₃ → Al(NO₃)₃ + 3Ag. Al 1 = 1, Ag 3 = 3, N 3 = 3, O 9 = 9 ✅ (Al par oxide parat shuru mein roak sakti hai). (d) **Nahi.** Cu, Fe se neeche. (e) **Nahi.** Zn, Mg se neeche.
2. Teer: E > B, B > A, A > C, C > D. Order: **E > B > A > C > D**. Seedhe sabit: E > B, B > A, A > C, C > D (chaar). Chain se: E > A, E > C, E > D, B > C, B > D, A > D. "D ne C ko nahi" aur "C ne A ko nahi" bhi order ke mel mein hain ✅.
3. Y thande paani se reaction karta hai aur baaki nahi: **Y sabse upar**. W bubbles deta hai: hydrogen se upar. X, Z bubbles nahi dete: hydrogen se neeche. W > Z (displacement), Z > X. Order: **Y > W > [H] > Z > X**.
4. A > B aur C > B pakka; D, B ko nahi hata saka to D, B se neeche: **{A, C} > B > D**. A aur C ka aapas ka order **pata nahi**. Test: **A ki strip C-salt mein** (aur ulta). Agar C jami to A > C.
5. Pehle teen se M > N > R > S, yaani chain se N > S. Par chautha note S > N kehta hai. To **N, R aur S** ek chakkar banate hain (N > R > S > N), jo ek seedhi mein asambhav hai. Kam se kam ek observation galat hai, par data se ye nahi pata ki kaunsa: chakkar ke teeno note (N-R, R-S, S-N) mein se koi bhi ek hata do to baaki sab mel kha jaata hai. Isliye **teeno jodiyan** (N aur R, R aur S, S aur N) dono taraf se dobara test karni hongi. M wala note (M > N) chakkar mein nahi hai, use dohrane ki zaroorat nahi; par M ki poori jagah tabhi pakki hogi jab chakkar suljhega. Kaaran: strip par oxide ki parat, galat salt ya strip ka chunav, ya ghol ka label badal jaana.
6. Fe = 11.2 ÷ 56 = **0.2 mol**. Fe + CuCl₂ 1 : 1, to Cu = 0.2 mol × 63.5 = **12.7 g**. Strip: 11.2 g gaya, 12.7 g aaya: **1.5 g badha**.
7. Zn = 13 ÷ 65 = **0.2 mol**. 1 mol Zn se 2 mol Ag, to Ag = 0.4 mol × 108 = **43.2 g**. Strip: 13 g gaya, 43.2 g aaya: **30.2 g badha**.
8. Cu = 3.175 ÷ 63.5 = **0.05 mol**. Ag = 2 × 0.05 = 0.1 mol × 108 = **10.8 g**. AgNO₃ = 0.1 mol × 170 = **17 g** (108 + 14 + 48). Cu(NO₃)₂ = 0.05 × 187.5 = 9.375 g. Jaanch: 3.175 + 17 = 20.175 g; 10.8 + 9.375 = 20.175 g ✅.
9. Al = 27 ÷ 27 = **1 mol**. 2 mol Al se 2 mol Fe: Fe = 1 mol × 56 = **56 g**. Al₂O₃ = 0.5 mol × 102 = **51 g**. Fe₂O₃ = 0.5 mol × 160 = **80 g**. Jaanch: 27 + 80 = 107 g; 51 + 56 = 107 g ✅.
10. Fe, Cu se upar hai, to Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu: loha ka bartan galta hai aur ghol kharab hota hai. Cu, Zn se neeche hai, to taambe ke bartan mein ZnSO₄ par koi reaction nahi. Thermite mein Al, Fe se upar hai, isliye Fe₂O₃ se Fe nikaal sakta hai; Cu, Fe se neeche, isliye nahi nikaal sakta.
11. (a) **False** ❌: hydrogen dhatu nahi hai, tulna ke liye seedhi mein rakha gaya hai. (b) **True** ✅: left par +2, right par +2. (c) **False** ❌: Zn + CuSO₄ mein strip ka mass ghatta hai. (d) **False** ❌: thermite bahut khatarnak hai, sirf trained log ke liye. (e) **True** ✅: chain se A > C.
12. K ka baahari electron nucleus se door hai aur kam bandha hai, isliye wo aasani se nikalta hai: K zyada reactive hai Zn se. Gold apne electron mushkil se deta hai: hawa, paani aur paseene se react nahi karta, isliye chamakta rehta hai. Paper task: Figure 4.1 mein hydrogen Pb aur Cu ke beech likhna hai.
