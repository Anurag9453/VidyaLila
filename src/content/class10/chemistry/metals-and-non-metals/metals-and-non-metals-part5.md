# Topic 5: Adhaatuon Ke Rasayanik Gun aur Upyog

Pichhle topic mein humne dhatuon ki line lagayi: kaun kisko displace karega, aur thermite se railway track tak ke jode. Ab dhatuon ki duniya se bahar nikalte hain. **Adhaatu (non-metals)** ka swabhav dhatuon se bilkul alag hai: ye na paani se ladte hain, na dilute acid se; par oxygen, hydrogen aur dhatuon ke saath inke apne tareeke hain. Aur ek chhoti si cheez, jise tum roz dekhte ho lekin shayad kabhi socha nahi, in sab ka sabse achha example hai: **ek maachis ki teeli**.

---

## Maachis factory ki ek subah

Mr. Inamdar ki ek maachis (matchbox) factory mein jaan-pehchan thi, aur ek chhutti wale din wo apni bhanji Janhavi ko wahan dikhane le gaye. Gate par ek board tha: "Andar sirf guide ke saath jaayein; kuch bhi na chhuen." Janhavi ne dekha ki ek lambi belt par hazaaron lakdi ki teeliyan ek ke baad ek aa rahi hain, aur unke sire par ek rangeen "sir" lag raha hai.

Guide ne ek khaali dibbi uthakar dikhayi. "Is dibbi ki side mein ek **bhoori-laal patti** hai, ye striker kehlati hai. Teeli ka sir akele kisi bhi sateh par nahi jalta. Sir aur patti **dono** milen, tabhi aag lagti hai." Janhavi ne poochha, "Par ek hi chiz mein dono kyun nahi daal dete?" Guide muskuraye, "Isi sawaal ke jawab mein poora rasayan shaastra chhupa hai: patti mein ek adhaatu (phosphorus) hai, sir mein doosra adhaatu (sulphur) aur ek oxygen dene wala padarth hai, aur teeno ka alag-alag kaam hai."

Mr. Inamdar ne dheere se kaha, "Aaj hum maachis ke bahaane **adhaatuon** ko samjhenge: ye oxygen ke saath kya karte hain, hydrogen ke saath kya, dhatuon ke saath kya, aur ek doosre ke saath kya. Aur ye sab sirf padhne ke liye: factory mein bhi bachchon ko kuch chhoone nahi diya jaata, aur ghar par maachis sirf bade jalate hain."

## Part A: Adhaatu aur paani, dilute acid, oxygen

**Paani aur dilute acid se:** Topic 3 mein humne dekha ki dhatuon ki line: potassium se lekar iron tak paani ya bhaap se hydrogen nikalti hai, aur magnesium se iron tak dilute acid se bhi. **Adhaatu aisa kuch nahi karte.** Sulphur ka powder, carbon (koyla) ya phosphorus: dilute HCl ya paani mein daalo to koi hydrogen nahi nikalta. Wajah simple hai: dhatu **electron dene** ki wajah se H⁺ ko hydrogen mein badalte hain; adhaatu ko electron dena pasand hi nahi, unhe to **lena ya baantna** hai (Class 9 ka electron wala picture). Isliye dhatuon ki reactivity series mein adhaatu shamil hi nahi hote.

**Oxygen ke saath:** adhaatu jalkar apne oxide banate hain. Dhyan do ki ye dhatuon ke oxides se ulta hai:

- C + O₂ → CO₂ (koyla ya lakdi jalti hai; hawa kam ho to 2C + O₂ → 2CO, jo zehreeli gas hai)
- S + O₂ → SO₂ (sulphur neeli lau se jalta hai, tej chubhne wali gandh wali gas banti hai)
- P₄ + 5O₂ → P₄O₁₀ (phosphorus ki safed dhuaan jaisi dhool)

*(Jaanch: C 1=1, O 2=2; S 1=1, O 2=2; P 4=4, O 10=10 ✅)*

Ye oxide aam taur par **acidic** hote hain: paani mein ghulkar acid dete hain, jaise P₄O₁₀ + 6H₂O → 4H₃PO₄. Aur kuch **neutral** hote hain: **CO, N₂O aur H₂O** na acid jaisa na base jaisa behave karte hain. Ye acidic aur basic oxide wali baat hum Chapter 1 ke [acids-bases-and-salts-part3.md](../acids-bases-and-salts/acids-bases-and-salts-part3.md) ke Part D mein dekh chuke hain: wahan non-metal oxide + base → salt + water tha. Yahan hum use dohrate nahi, sirf yaad rakhte hain: **dhatu ke oxide basic, adhaatu ke oxide aam taur par acidic.**

**SO₂ ka ek bura roop:** sulphur wala koyla ya petrol jalne par SO₂ hawa mein jaati hai, wahan aur oxidise hokar barish ke paani mein ghulti hai aur **acid rain** banati hai. Ye kahani Class 9 ke [atmospheric-pollution-part2.md](../../../class9/chemistry/atmospheric-pollution/atmospheric-pollution-part2.md) mein thi. (Chaandi ko kaala SO₂ nahi, H₂S karti hai: Topic 2.)

**Teeli jalne ki gandh:** jab bada maachis jalata hai, to teeli ke sir ka sulphur jalkar SO₂ banata hai, isliye wo chubhne wali gandh aati hai. Maachis ko ghar mein bachchon ki pahunch se door aur hawadaar jagah par hi jalate hain.

> **Jaanch:** Sulphur dilute HCl mein daalne par hydrogen dega. ❌ Nahi: adhaatu electron dene wale nahi hain, isliye dilute acid se H₂ nahi banti.

## Part B: Adhaatu aur hydrogen: covalent hydrides

Adhaatu hydrogen ke saath milkar **hydrides** banate hain, jisme dono electron **baantte** hain. Isliye ye **covalent** yaugik hain (Class 9 ka covalent bond), aur aam taur par gas ya liquid milte hain:

| Adhaatu ki valency | Hydride | Reaction ya sutra |
|---|---|---|
| Cl (1) | HCl | H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl |
| S (2) | H₂S | H₂ + S → H₂S |
| O (2) | H₂O | 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O |
| N (3) | NH₃ | N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ |
| C (4) | CH₄ | C + 2H₂ → CH₄ |

*(Jaanch: Cl 2=2, H 2=2; S 1=1, H 2=2; O 2=2, H 4=4; N 2=2, H 6=6; C 1=1, H 4=4 ✅)*

Pattern dekho: hydrogen ki sankhya adhaatu ki valency ke barabar hai: Cl ke saath 1, S ke saath 2, N ke saath 3, C ke saath 4. Ye bilkul wahi cross-multiply wala tareeka hai jo tum Class 9 mein seekh chuke ho.

Ammonia wali equation mein **⇌** ka chinh hai: reaction dono taraf chal sakta hai, aur ise bade pairane par khaad banane mein use karte hain (aage ki class mein poori kahani). **H₂S** sade hue ande jaisi gandh wali zehreeli gas hai. **HCl gas** paani mein ghulkar hydrochloric acid banati hai (Chapter 1 ki kadi).

## Part C: Adhaatu aur dhatu: ionic yaugik

Jab ek **dhatu** (jise electron dena hai) aur ek **adhaatu** (jise electron lena hai) milte hain, to electron ek taraf se doosri taraf jaata hai aur **ionic yaugik** banta hai:

- 2Na + Cl₂ → 2NaCl (sodium chlorine ke saath tez jalta hai: *sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo)*, kyunki chlorine zehreeli hai)
- Fe + S → FeS (iron aur sulphur ka mixture garam karne par kaala FeS banta hai: *sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo)*)
- 2K + Br₂ → 2KBr, Ca + S → CaS

*(Jaanch: Na 2=2, Cl 2=2; Fe 1=1, S 1=1 ✅)*

Dhyan do: Fe aur S ka mixture magnet se alag ho jaata hai (iron magnet se chipakta hai), par jab FeS ban jaata hai to **ab ye alag nahi hota**, kyunki naya padarth ban chuka hai (Class 9 ka mixture aur compound ka farak).

**Adhaatu aur adhaatu:** jab do adhaatu milte hain to dono ko electron chahiye, isliye **baantkar** covalent yaugik bante hain: CO₂, SO₂, HCl, NH₃. Ek mashhoor udaharan: bijli kadakne par hawa ke N₂ aur O₂ milkar N₂ + O₂ → 2NO banate hain (bahut kam matra mein).

```
              ADHAATU (non-metal)
                      :
    +-----------------+-----------------+-----------------+
    :                 :                 :                 :
 PAANI/dilute      OXYGEN se         HYDROGEN se       DHATU se
 ACID se
    :                 :                 :                 :
 koi reaction      oxide:            hydride:          ionic yaugik:
 nahi (H₂ nahi)    aam taur par      covalent          NaCl, FeS, KBr
                   acidic            HCl, H₂S, NH₃
                   (CO₂, SO₂, P₄O₁₀) CH₄, H₂O
                   neutral
                   (CO, N₂O, H₂O)
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Adhaatuon ka swabhav ek chart mein: paani aur dilute acid se koi reaction nahi; oxygen ke saath aam taur par acidic (kabhi neutral) oxide; hydrogen ke saath covalent hydride; dhatu ke saath ionic yaugik.

> **Jaanch:** FeS ke andar iron ko magnet se kheench sakte hain. ❌ Nahi: FeS naya yaugik hai, usme iron apni purani pehchaan kho chuka hai.

## Part D: Halogens ki seedhi (displacement ladder)

Class 9 ([periodic-table-part5.md](../../../class9/chemistry/periodic-table/periodic-table-part5.md)) mein tumne dekha tha ki halogens group mein neeche jaate hue **kam reactive** hote hain: **F₂ > Cl₂ > Br₂ > I₂**. Ek adhaatu ki reactivity ka matlab hai: kitni aasani se wo **electron khinch** leta hai. Isliye **zyada reactive halogen, kam reactive halogen ko uske namak (salt) se bahar nikaal deta hai**, bilkul waise jaise dhatuon mein Topic 4 mein hota tha. Bas yahan nikalne wala **adhaatu** hai.

- Cl₂ + 2KBr → 2KCl + Br₂ (chlorine bromine se upar hai: bromine bahar aata hai, ghol peela-naarangi ho jaata hai)
- Cl₂ + 2KI → 2KCl + I₂ (iodine bahar aata hai, ghol bhoora ho jaata hai)
- Br₂ + 2KI → 2KBr + I₂ (bromine iodine se upar hai)
- Br₂ + 2KCl → koi reaction **nahi** (bromine chlorine se neeche hai, use nikaal nahi sakta)

*(Jaanch: Cl 2=2, K 2=2, Br 2=2; Cl 2=2, K 2=2, I 2=2; Br 2=2, K 2=2, I 2=2 ✅)*

```
   Reactivity (electron khinchne ki taakat)

   F₂   sabse upar        ^   ye saare neeche walon ko
   Cl₂                    :   unke namak se bahar nikaal
   Br₂                    :   sakte hain
   I₂   sabse neeche      :

   Cl₂ + KBr  ->  Br₂ bahar      (Cl upar, Br neeche: ho jaata hai)
   Cl₂ + KI   ->  I₂ bahar       (ho jaata hai)
   Br₂ + KI   ->  I₂ bahar       (ho jaata hai)
   I₂  + KBr  ->  koi reaction nahi (I neeche, Br upar)
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Halogen displacement ladder: upar wala halogen neeche wale ko uske namak se nikaal deta hai, ulta nahi hota. Ye sirf padhne ka chitr hai.

**Safety:** chlorine aur bromine **zehreeli** hain, aur bromine ki bhaap aankhon aur phephdon ko jalati hai. Ye reactions **sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo)** hote hain, wo bhi bahut chhoti matra mein, hawadaar jagah ya fume cupboard mein. Koi bachcha ghar par chlorine (bleach) ya aisi cheez milane ki koshish nahi karega: bleach ko kisi aur safai wale ke saath milane se zehreeli chlorine gas ban sakti hai.

**Equation likhne ka chhota tareeka:** namak (jaise KBr) mein jo halogen baitha hai, agar aane wala halogen usse zyada reactive hai to wo uski jagah le leta hai aur apna namak (KCl) banata hai; purana halogen (Br₂) bahar aa jaata hai. Teeno equations mein K sirf "darshak" hai: wo wahin rehta hai, bas uska saathi badal jaata hai (Topic 4 ke darshak ion jaisa).

## Part E: Maachis ke andar ka rasayan

Ab Janhavi ke sawaal par aate hain. Safety maachis (jo hum ghar mein dekhte hain) ki do jagah mein rasayan baanta gaya hai:

```
   TEELI KA SIR (head)                  DIBBI KI SIDE KI PATTI (striker)

   +------------------------+           +------------------------+
   :  potassium chlorate    :           :  LAAL PHOSPHORUS       :
   :  (KClO₃, oxidiser:     :           :  (red phosphorus)      :
   :  oxygen dene wala)     :           :  + paisa hua kaanch    :
   :  + SULPHUR             :           :  + chipkane wala gond  :
   :  + paisa hua kaanch    :           :                        :
   :  + chipkane wala gond  :           :  (ye patti sirf        :
   +------------------------+           :  dibbi par hoti hai)   :
        ye sir lakdi ki                 +------------------------+
        teeli par laga hota hai
                       
   teeli ko patti par ragdo:  ragad ki garmi  ->  thoda sa phosphorus
   aag pakadta hai  ->  KClO₃ oxygen deta hai  ->  sir jalta hai
   ->  sulphur jalta hai (SO₂)  ->  lakdi jalti hai
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Safety maachis ke do hisse: sir mein oxidiser (KClO₃) aur sulphur, patti mein laal phosphorus. Dono alag rehte hain, isliye teeli kisi bhi sateh par nahi jalti.

**Kaam kaise hota hai (sirf padhne ke liye; maachis jalana sirf bade ka kaam):**

1. Teeli ko patti par ragdne se **ragad (friction)** ki thodi garmi paida hoti hai. Isse patti ka thoda sa laal phosphorus **tez reactive roop** mein aa jaata hai aur aag pakad leta hai.
2. Sir mein **potassium chlorate** hota hai (aur sulphur, ya kuch maachison mein sulphur ki jagah antimony ka ek yaugik). Garm karne par ye oxygen deta hai: **2KClO₃ → 2KCl + 3O₂**. Isliye teeli ka sir hawa ke bina bhi achhi tarah jalna shuru kar deta hai.
3. Is oxygen se **sulphur** jalta hai: S + O₂ → SO₂, aur ye lakdi ki teeli ko jalana shuru kar deti hai.
4. Lakdi par aisi dawa lagi hoti hai jisse teeli jal jaane ke baad "afterglow" (bujhne ke baad ka angaara) na rahe.

*(Jaanch: K 2=2, Cl 2=2, O 6=6 ✅)*

**Safety maachis ko "safety" kyun kehte hain?** Purane zamane ki maachis mein **safed phosphorus (white phosphorus)** hota tha, jo hawa mein khud-ba-khud aag pakad leta hai aur bahut zehreela hai: jin mazdooron ne use bahut samay sambhala, unhe gambhir bimariyaan hui. Isliye uski jagah **laal phosphorus** aaya, jo kam khatarnak hai, aur use teeli ke sir se **alag**, sirf dibbi ki patti par rakha gaya. Teeli akeli jalti hi nahi, patti akeli jalti hi nahi: isse chingaari ki sambhavna kam ho gayi.

**Adult-only note:** maachis ki teeli ragadna sirf bade ka kaam hai, kisi bachche ka nahi. Bada bhi ise khuli, hawadaar jagah par jalaye, aur aas-paas koi jalne wali cheez (kapda, kagaz, parda, gas, petrol, deodorant spray) na ho. Maachis jalane ka koi prayog bachchon ke liye nahi hai.

## Part F: Allotropes: ek hi tatva, kai roop

Ek hi tatva ke alag-alag roop, jinke gun alag hote hain, **allotropes** kehlate hain:

- **Carbon:** **heera (diamond)** sabse kathor prakritik padarth hai aur bijli nahi guzarta; **graphite** narm, phisalne wala hai aur bijli guzarta hai (pencil ki lead, electrode). Dono carbon hi hain. Carbon ki poori kahani Chapter 10 ("Carbon and Its Compounds") mein.
- **Phosphorus:** **safed phosphorus** hawa mein khud jal jaata hai, zehreela hai, isliye **paani ke neeche rakha jaata hai**; **laal phosphorus** hawa mein khud se aag nahi pakadta, kam zehreela hai, aur maachis ki patti mein kaam aata hai.

Isse ek sabak milta hai: tatva ka naam hi poori kahani nahi batata; **kaise jude hue hain**, ye bhi utna hi important hai.

## Part G: Adhaatuon ke upyog aur surakshit istemal

| Adhaatu | Upyog | Dhyan rakhne ki baat |
|---|---|---|
| Carbon | Eendhan (koyla), pencil lead, electrode, heera aabhooshan | Band kamre mein koyla jalane se CO banti hai: zehreeli |
| Sulphur | Maachis, baaroot, rubber ko majboot banane (vulcanising), sulphuric acid banane mein | Jalne par SO₂ banti hai |
| Phosphorus | Maachis ki patti, khaad (fertilisers) | Safed phosphorus paani ke neeche; kabhi nahi chhuna |
| Nitrogen | Khaad aur ammonia banane mein, khaane ke packet mein bharne ke liye | Hawa ka lagbhag 78 percent; zehreeli nahi, par band jagah mein bahut zyada ho to oxygen ki kami kar deti hai |

| Iodine | Chot par antiseptic tincture | Sirf bade ke saath |
| Oxygen | Saans, aspatal, welding | Aag ko badhaata hai |

Ye saari baatein padhne ke liye hain. Kisi bhi adhaatu ko chakhna ya soonghna nahi hai: Chapter ke safety rules wahi hain jo Topic 3 mein the.

## Part H: Solved examples

**Example 1: 8 g sulphur poora jalne par kitna SO₂ banega?**
S + O₂ → SO₂. Moles of S = 8 ÷ 32 = 0.25. Mole ratio 1 : 1, isliye 0.25 mol SO₂. Molar mass of SO₂ = 32 + 2 × 16 = 64. Mass = 0.25 × 64 = **16 g**. (Oxygen ne 8 g joda, 8 + 8 = 16 ✅.)

**Example 2: In oxides ko acidic ya neutral batao: CO, CO₂, P₄O₁₀, N₂O, SO₂.**
CO₂, P₄O₁₀ aur SO₂ adhaatu ke oxide hain jo base ke saath neutralise karte hain, isliye **acidic**. CO aur N₂O **neutral** hain (CO ke baare mein yaad rakho ki wo zehreeli hai, par uska acidic ya basic hona alag sawaal hai).

**Example 3 (sirf kagaz par; chlorine zehreeli hai): 23.8 g KBr ke ghol mein chlorine ki zyada matra daali gayi. Cl₂ + 2KBr → 2KCl + Br₂. Br₂ aur KCl kitne banenge?**
Molar mass KBr = 39 + 80 = 119. Moles = 23.8 ÷ 119 = 0.2. Mole ratio: 2 KBr se 1 Br₂ aur 2 KCl. Br₂ ke moles = 0.1; mass = 0.1 × 160 = **16 g**. KCl ke moles = 0.2; molar mass 74.5; mass = 0.2 × 74.5 = **14.9 g**. Mass sanrakshan: 23.8 g KBr + 7.1 g Cl₂ (0.1 mol × 71) = 30.9 g, aur 14.9 + 16 = 30.9 g ✅.

**Example 4: Teen halogens X₂, Y₂, Z₂ ko unke potassium namak ke saath milaya gaya.**

| Mixture | Natija |
|---|---|
| X₂ + KY | Y₂ bahar aaya |
| Z₂ + KX | Koi reaction nahi |
| Z₂ + KY | Koi reaction nahi |

X₂ ne Y₂ ko nikaala, isliye X reactive hai Y se. Z₂ ne X aur Y dono ko nahi nikaala, isliye Z sabse kam reactive hai. Kram: **X₂ > Y₂ > Z₂**. (Jaanch: agar ye X₂ + KZ lete, to Z₂ bahar aana chahiye.)

**Example 5: 14 g iron ko poora FeS banane ke liye kitna sulphur chahiye, aur kitna FeS banega? (sirf kagaz par; garam karna teacher demo)**
Fe + S → FeS. Moles Fe = 14 ÷ 56 = 0.25. Ratio 1 : 1, isliye sulphur bhi 0.25 mol = 0.25 × 32 = **8 g**. FeS ka molar mass = 56 + 32 = 88, to FeS = 0.25 × 88 = **22 g**. Check: 14 + 8 = 22 ✅. (Agar sulphur 8 g se kam ho, to kuch iron bina reaction ke bach jaata hai aur magnet se alag ho sakta hai.)

**Example 6: 12.4 g phosphorus (P₄) hawa mein poora jalta hai. Oxygen kitni lagegi aur P₄O₁₀ kitna banega? (P = 31)**
P₄ ka molar mass = 4 × 31 = 124. Moles = 12.4 ÷ 124 = 0.1. P₄ + 5O₂ → P₄O₁₀: oxygen ke moles = 0.5; mass = 0.5 × 32 = **16 g**. P₄O₁₀ ke moles = 0.1; molar mass = 124 + 160 = 284; mass = **28.4 g**. Check: 12.4 + 16 = 28.4 ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Adhaatu bhi dilute acid se hydrogen dete hain"**
Nahi. Hydrogen dhatu dete hain (jo electron dete hain), adhaatu nahi. Demonstration: Topic 3 ki raftaar wali daud (teacher demo) mein ek tube mein magnesium ki jagah sulphur powder ya koyle ka tukda daalo: 30 second mein bubble ginti **0** aati hai, bilkul copper jaisi, jabki Mg wali tube mein bubbles hi bubbles.

**Galti 2: "Saare adhaatuon ke oxide acidic hote hain"**
Aam taur par haan, par **CO, N₂O aur H₂O neutral** hain.

**Galti 3: "Halogen displacement mein neeche wala upar wale ko nikaal sakta hai"**
Nahi. Sirf zyada reactive, kam reactive ko nikaalta hai: Cl₂ KBr se Br₂ nikaalti hai, par Br₂ KCl se Cl₂ nahi nikaal sakta.

**Galti 4: "Maachis ke sir mein hi phosphorus hota hai"**
Safety maachis mein laal phosphorus **patti** par hota hai, sir mein sulphur aur KClO₃.

**Galti 5: "Heera aur graphite alag tatva hain"**
Dono carbon hi hain, sirf jude hone ka tareeka alag hai.

**Galti 6: "Safed aur laal phosphorus dono utne hi khatarnak hain"**
Safed phosphorus khud jal jaata hai aur zehreela hai; laal kam khatarnak hai.

## Quick Recap

- Adhaatu paani aur dilute acid se **hydrogen nahi** dete.
- Adhaatu + oxygen: aam taur par **acidic oxide** (CO₂, SO₂, P₄O₁₀); **CO, N₂O, H₂O** neutral.
- Adhaatu + hydrogen: **covalent hydride** (HCl, H₂S, NH₃, CH₄, H₂O).
- Adhaatu + dhatu: **ionic yaugik** (2Na + Cl₂ → 2NaCl, Fe + S → FeS).
- Halogens ki reactivity: **F₂ > Cl₂ > Br₂ > I₂**; upar wala neeche wale ko uske namak se bahar nikaalta hai.
- Safety maachis: sir mein KClO₃ aur sulphur, patti mein laal phosphorus; chlorine, bromine aur safed phosphorus zehreele ya khatarnak: sirf teacher demo.
- Allotropes: heera aur graphite; safed aur laal phosphorus.

## Practice Questions

1. 9 g of charcoal (take it as pure carbon) is burnt completely in excess air to form carbon dioxide. Calculate the mass of CO₂ formed and the mass of oxygen used.
2. Explain why non-metals such as sulphur and carbon do not liberate hydrogen from dilute hydrochloric acid, while zinc does.
3. Chlorine gas is passed through a solution containing 33.2 g of potassium iodide (KI). Write the balanced equation and calculate the mass of iodine liberated.
4. State whether a reaction takes place in each case, and write the balanced equation where it does: (a) Br₂ + NaCl, (b) I₂ + KBr, (c) Cl₂ + NaI, (d) Br₂ + NaI.
5. Three halogens P₂, Q₂ and R₂ are tested with the potassium salts of the other two. Q₂ displaces P from KP, R₂ displaces both P and Q from KP and KQ, and P₂ displaces neither. Arrange P₂, Q₂, R₂ in decreasing order of reactivity.
6. Write the formulas of the hydrides of (a) phosphorus (valency 3), (b) silicon (valency 4), (c) bromine (valency 1). State what type of bond each hydride has.
7. 6.4 g of sulphur reacts with hydrogen to form H₂S (H₂ + S → H₂S). Calculate the mass of H₂S formed and the mass of hydrogen used.
8. Classify each product as ionic or covalent: KBr, CaS, SO₂, CH₄, NH₃. Give a reason for the rule you use.
9. 14 g of nitrogen reacts with hydrogen as N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ (assume complete reaction). Find the mass of hydrogen needed and the mass of ammonia formed.
10. A coal sample contains 2 percent sulphur by mass. If 1 tonne (1000 kg) of this coal is burnt completely, what mass of sulphur dioxide is released? Why is this a problem for the environment?
11. 6.2 g of white phosphorus (P₄) burns completely in air. Find the mass of oxygen consumed and the mass of P₄O₁₀ formed. (P = 31)
12. Why does a safety match not light when its head is rubbed on a wooden table, but lights when rubbed on the box striker? Name the substance in each part.
13. Why is white phosphorus stored under water, and why was it replaced by red phosphorus in matches?
14. Which of these oxides are neutral: N₂O, SO₃, CO, H₂O, CO₂? Which are acidic?
15. State true or false and correct the false ones: (a) Fluorine can displace chlorine from potassium chloride. (b) Diamond and graphite are different elements. (c) Bleach must never be mixed with other cleaning liquids at home.

### Answers

1. C + O₂ → CO₂. Moles C = 9 ÷ 12 = 0.75; mole ratio 1 : 1 : 1. CO₂ = 0.75 × 44 = **33 g**; oxygen = 0.75 × 32 = **24 g**. Check: 9 + 24 = 33 ✅.

2. Dilute HCl se hydrogen tab nikalta hai jab koi tatva **electron dekar** H⁺ ko H₂ mein badle. Zinc ke atom aasani se electron dete hain, isliye wo reaction karta hai. Sulphur aur carbon adhaatu hain: unhe electron lena ya baantna hota hai, dena nahi. Isliye unse H₂ nahi banti.

3. Cl₂ + 2KI → 2KCl + I₂ (Cl 2=2, K 2=2, I 2=2 ✅). Molar mass KI = 39 + 127 = 166. Moles = 33.2 ÷ 166 = 0.2. Ratio 2 KI : 1 I₂, isliye I₂ = 0.1 mol. Mass = 0.1 × 254 = **25.4 g**.

4. (a) Br₂ + NaCl: **koi reaction nahi**, kyunki bromine chlorine se kam reactive hai. (b) I₂ + KBr: **koi reaction nahi**, iodine bromine se kam reactive hai. (c) Cl₂ + 2NaI → 2NaCl + I₂ (Cl 2=2, Na 2=2, I 2=2 ✅). (d) Br₂ + 2NaI → 2NaBr + I₂ (Br 2=2, Na 2=2, I 2=2 ✅).

5. R₂ ne P aur Q dono ko nikaala, isliye R₂ sabse reactive. Q₂ ne sirf P ko nikaala, isliye Q₂ beech mein. P₂ ne kisi ko nahi nikaala, isliye sabse kam reactive. Kram: **R₂ > Q₂ > P₂**.

6. (a) **PH₃**, (b) **SiH₄**, (c) **HBr**. Teeno mein hydrogen aur adhaatu electron baantte hain, isliye **covalent bond** hai. Hydrogen ki sankhya adhaatu ki valency ke barabar hai (3, 4, 1).

7. Moles S = 6.4 ÷ 32 = 0.2. H₂S ke moles = 0.2, molar mass = 2 + 32 = 34; mass = 0.2 × 34 = **6.8 g**. Hydrogen = 0.2 × 2 = **0.4 g**. Check: 6.4 + 0.4 = 6.8 ✅.

8. **Ionic:** KBr (K dhatu + Br adhaatu) aur CaS (Ca dhatu + S adhaatu): dhatu electron deta hai, adhaatu leta hai. **Covalent:** SO₂, CH₄ aur NH₃, jinme sirf adhaatu hain jo electron baantte hain. Niyam: dhatu + adhaatu → ionic; adhaatu + adhaatu → covalent.

9. Moles N₂ = 14 ÷ 28 = 0.5. Ratio N₂ : H₂ : NH₃ = 1 : 3 : 2, isliye H₂ = 1.5 mol = 1.5 × 2 = **3 g**; NH₃ = 1 mol = **17 g**. Check: 14 + 3 = 17 ✅.

10. Sulphur = 2 percent of 1000 kg = 20 kg. S + O₂ → SO₂, mass ratio 32 : 64, isliye SO₂ = 20 × 2 = **40 kg**. SO₂ hawa mein jaakar barish ke paani ke saath acid rain banati hai, jo imaaraton, paudhon aur taalab ke jeevon ko nuksaan pahunchati hai (Class 9 ki kadi).

11. Moles P₄ = 6.2 ÷ 124 = 0.05. P₄ + 5O₂ → P₄O₁₀: oxygen = 0.25 mol = 0.25 × 32 = **8 g**; P₄O₁₀ = 0.05 mol × 284 = **14.2 g**. Check: 6.2 + 8 = 14.2 ✅.

12. Teeli ke sir mein **sulphur aur potassium chlorate** hai, par aag shuru karne wala **laal phosphorus** sirf dibbi ki patti mein hai. Mez par ragadne se phosphorus nahi milta, isliye ragad ki garmi se aag nahi lagti. Patti par ragadne par phosphorus aag pakadta hai aur sir ko jalata hai.

13. Safed phosphorus hawa mein khud-ba-khud jal jaata hai, isliye use paani ke neeche rakhte hain. Wo bahut zehreela bhi hai. Laal phosphorus kam khatarnak hai aur hawa mein nahi jalta, isliye maachis mein uski jagah le li.

14. **Neutral:** N₂O, CO, H₂O. **Acidic:** SO₃ aur CO₂ (dono adhaatu ke oxide jo base ke saath neutralise karte hain).

15. (a) **True** ✅: fluorine chlorine se zyada reactive hai. (b) **False** ❌: dono carbon hain, sirf allotropes hain. (c) **True** ✅: bleach ko doosre cleaners ke saath milane se zehreeli chlorine gas ban sakti hai.
