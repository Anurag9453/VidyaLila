# Topic 6: Dhatu ya Adhaatu? Pehchaan, Ionic Yaugik aur Chapter Ka Saar

Pichhle paanch topics mein humne dhatuon aur adhaatuon ko alag-alag kone se dekha: chamak aur sakhti, oxygen aur paani se takkar, reactivity series, aur adhaatuon ke apne rasayanik gun. Ab aakhri topic mein sab kuch ek saath jodna hai. Ek anjaan cheez haath mein aaye to hum kaise tay karein ki wo dhatu hai ya adhaatu, uska oxide kaisa hoga, aur uska yaugik ionic hoga ya covalent? Ye ek detective ka kaam hai, aur is topic mein tum detective banoge.

## Part A: Dhatu aur adhaatu ka poora comparison

Pichhle topics ke saare gun ek jagah:

| Gun | Dhatu (metals) | Adhaatu (non-metals) | Mashhoor apvaad (exceptions) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Roop | Zyadatar thos | Thos ya gas, bromine liquid | Mercury liquid dhatu hai, gallium aur caesium haath ki garmi se pighalne ke kareeb |
| Chamak | Chamakdar (lustre) | Aamtaur par phiki | Graphite aur iodine chamakte hain |
| Sakhti | Zyadatar sakht | Thos adhaatu aksar bhurbhure ya naram | Sodium, potassium chaaku se kat jaate hain, diamond sabse sakht |
| Malleable aur ductile | Haan, peette ya kheench sakte hain | Nahi, bhurbhure (brittle) | Metalloid (silicon) chamakdar hokar bhi bhurbhura hai; adhaatu mein koi malleable apvaad aam nahi |
| Bijli ka sanchaalan | Achhe conductors | Bure conductors | Graphite bijli conduct karta hai, lead ek dhatu hokar kamzor conductor hai |
| Garmi ka sanchaalan | Achhe conductors | Bure conductors | Diamond garmi ko achhi tarah conduct karta hai |
| Awaaz (sonorous) | Thokne par bajte hain | Nahi bajte | Sodium jaisi naram dhatu nahi bajti |
| Density | Aamtaur par zyada | Kam | Lithium bahut halka, osmium sabse bhaari |
| Gala-ank aur ubaal-ank | Aamtaur par bahut ooncha | Aamtaur par neeche | Tungsten ka gala-ank lagbhag 3400 °C, graphite aur diamond bhi bahut ooncha |
| Oxide | Basic ya amphoteric | Acidic ya neutral | CO, H₂O aur N₂O neutral oxide hain |
| Reaction paani aur acid se | Kai dhatuein deti hain H₂ | Nahi deti | Copper aur silver dilute acid se H₂ nahi dete |
| Bond banana | Electron dekar positive ion banate hain | Electron lekar negative ion banate hain ya share karte hain | Hydrogen dono tarah vyavhaar karta hai |

> Yaad rakho: har test ek sanket deta hai, par **kisi ek test se pakka faisla nahi hota**. Isliye detective hamesha kai tests milata hai.

## Part B: Ionic yaugik, ek tez dohraav

Class 9 mein ([chemical-bonding-part2.md](../../../class9/chemistry/chemical-bonding/chemical-bonding-part2.md)) humne dekha tha ki dhatu apne baahari electron dekar **positive ion (cation)** banti hai aur adhaatu electron lekar **negative ion (anion)**. In dono ions ke beech ka aakarshan hi ionic bond hai. Yahan sirf chaar jodiyon ka recap:

- **Na + Cl:** Na (2, 8, 1) 1 electron dekar Na⁺ (2, 8) banta hai, Cl (2, 8, 7) 1 electron lekar Cl⁻ (2, 8, 8). Yaugik: **NaCl** (23 + 35.5 = 58.5 u).
- **Mg + 2Cl:** Mg (2, 8, 2) 2 electron dekar Mg²⁺ banta hai, har Cl 1 electron leta hai. Yaugik: **MgCl₂** (24 + 71 = 95 u).
- **Ca + O:** Ca (2, 8, 8, 2) 2 electron deta hai, O (2, 6) 2 electron leta hai. Yaugik: **CaO** (40 + 16 = 56 u).
- **Al + O:** Al (2, 8, 3) 3 electron deta hai (Al³⁺), O 2 electron leta hai (O²⁻). Dene aur lene ke electron barabar karne ke liye 2 Al aur 3 O chahiye (6 electron diye, 6 liye). Yaugik: **Al₂O₃** (54 + 48 = 102 u).

Ye ionic yaugik **dhatu ke electron adhaatu ko dene** se bante hain, isliye **dhatu aur adhaatu ke milan se** ionic yaugik banta hai. Do adhaatuon ke beech electron **share** hote hain aur covalent yaugik banta hai (jaise H₂O, CH₄, HCl).

Ionic yaugikon ke gun ([chemical-bonding-part4.md](../../../class9/chemistry/chemical-bonding/chemical-bonding-part4.md)) ek recall table mein:

| Gun | Ionic yaugik | Covalent yaugik (aamtaur par) |
|---|---|---|
| Roop | Thos, kadak crystal | Gas, liquid ya naram thos |
| Gala-ank aur ubaal-ank | Ooncha (NaCl lagbhag 801 °C) | Neecha |
| Kathor ya bhurbhura | Kathor par bhurbhura, hathode se chatakta hai | Naram ya bhurbhura |
| Bijli ka sanchaalan | Thos mein nahi, pighle hue ya paani mein ghule hue roop mein haan | Aamtaur par kisi roop mein nahi |
| Paani mein ghulna | Aamtaur par ghulte hain | Aamtaur par nahi ghulte (sugar jaisi cheezein apvaad) |

> Kyun? Ionic crystal mein ions apni jagah par jakde hote hain, isliye thos mein bijli nahi chalti. Pighalne ya ghulne par ions aazaad ho jaate hain aur chal sakte hain, isliye bijli chalti hai.

## Part D: Pehchaan ke puzzle (har jawab ka poora tark)

### Puzzle 1. Figure 6.2 ke paanch samples

**Sawaal:** Figure 6.2 ke P, Q, R, S, T ko dhatu, adhaatu, metalloid ya apvaad mein baanto.

**Hal:**
- **P:** chamak, bijli, awaaz, peetna: chaaron dhatu ke gun. **Dhatu**, chumbak nahi kheenchta, isliye non-ferrous (jaise aluminium ya copper).
- **Q:** na chamak, na bijli, na awaaz, toot jaata hai: **adhaatu** (jaise sulphur).
- **R:** P jaisa hi, par chumbak kheenchta hai. **Dhatu**, ferrous: iron ya steel.
- **S:** chamakdar par bijli sirf thodi, toot jaata hai: **metalloid** (jaise silicon). Ye dhatu aur adhaatu ke beech hai.
- **T:** chamak nahi par bijli chalti hai, dheela parat nikalta hai (pencil jaisa): **graphite**, adhaatu hokar bijli conduct karne wala apvaad.

**Jaanch:** P aur R mein sirf chumbak ka farq hai, aur wahi farq unhe do alag dhatu-parivaaron mein baantta hai ✅.

---

### Puzzle 2. Chamakdar par bhurbhura

**Sawaal:** Ek chamakdar, bhurbhura thos sample bijli ko bahut kam conduct karta hai. Kya ye dhatu hai? Kaunsa shabd sabse sahi hai?

**Hal:** Dhatu bhurbhuri nahi hoti (peetne par chadar banti hai), aur achhe conductor hoti hai. Ye sample chamakta hai par bhurbhura hai aur bijli kam conduct karta hai, to ye **na poora dhatu hai na poori adhaatu**: **metalloid** (jaise silicon, germanium).

**Jaanch:** Chamak dhatu jaisi, bhurbhurapan aur kam conduction adhaatu jaise, to beech ki shreni ✅.

---

### Puzzle 3. Do oxide, do ghol

**Sawaal:** Do tatvon X aur Y ke oxide paani mein ghole gaye. X ka ghol laal litmus ko neela karta hai. Y ka ghol neele litmus ko laal karta hai. Kaun dhatu hai?

**Hal:** Dhatu ka oxide aamtaur par basic hota hai, isliye alkali (jaise Na₂O + H₂O → 2NaOH) bana kar laal litmus ko neela karta hai. Isliye **X dhatu** hai. Adhaatu ka oxide acidic hota hai (jaise CO₂ ya SO₂ paani mein acid banate hain), isliye **Y adhaatu** hai.

**Jaanch:** X: basic, dhatu. Y: acidic, adhaatu ✅. (Neutral oxide jaise CO aur amphoteric jaise Al₂O₃ is test mein alag dikhenge, aage dekho.)

---

### Puzzle 4. Ek oxide jo acid aur base dono se reaction kare

**Sawaal:** Ek sample ka oxide dilute HCl se bhi reaction karta hai aur NaOH ke ghol se bhi. Ye kaun-sa oxide hai, aur uska tatva dhatu hai ya adhaatu?

**Hal:** Jo oxide acid aur base dono se reaction kare wo **amphoteric** hota hai. Amphoteric oxide dhatuon ke hote hain, jaise Al₂O₃ aur ZnO. Isliye tatva **dhatu** hai. Uske do reaction (Topic 2 ki tarah):
- Al₂O₃ + 6HCl → 2AlCl₃ + 3H₂O
- Al₂O₃ + 2NaOH → 2NaAlO₂ + H₂O

**Jaanch:** Al: 2 aur 2 ✅, O: 3 aur 3 ✅, Cl: 6 aur 6 ✅, Na: 2 aur 2 ✅.

---

### Puzzle 5. Pighle namak ka raaz

**Sawaal:** Teen safed thos yaugik K, L, M ke data neeche hain.

| Yaugik | Gala-ank | Thos mein bijli | Pighle ya ghol mein bijli | Hathode se |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| K | 801 °C | nahi | haan | chatak jaata hai |
| L | 55 °C | nahi | nahi | naram, dabta hai |
| M | 714 °C | nahi | haan | chatak jaata hai |

Kaun ionic hai, kaun covalent?

**Hal:** Ionic yaugik ka ooncha gala-ank hota hai, wo thos mein bijli nahi deta par pighalne ya ghulne par deta hai, aur hathode se chatak jaata hai. **K aur M ionic** hain (K ka data NaCl jaisa, M ka MgCl₂ jaisa). **L covalent** hai: neecha gala-ank, kisi roop mein bijli nahi, naram.

**Jaanch:** K aur M ke teeno ooncha gala-ank, thos mein nahi, pighle mein haan: ionic ✅. L ke teeno ulte ✅.

---

### Puzzle 6. Dhatu ki seedhi mein jagah

**Sawaal:** Ek dhatu Z ne copper sulphate ke neele ghol se copper nikaal diya (lal parat), par zinc sulphate ke ghol se zinc nahi nikala. Z dilute acid se H₂ deti hai. Z reactivity series mein kahan hai?

**Hal:** Z ne Cu ko hataya, to Z, Cu se zyada reactive hai. Z ne Zn ko nahi hataya, to Z, Zn se kam reactive hai. H₂ dene ka matlab Z hydrogen se upar hai. Isliye **Zn aur Cu ke beech, hydrogen ke upar**: iron ya lead jaisi jagah.

**Jaanch:** Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu ke jaisa reaction, aur Fe + ZnSO₄ mein koi reaction nahi ✅ (Topic 4).

---

### Puzzle 7. Pehchaano ye tatva kaun-sa hai

**Sawaal:** Ek peela, bhurbhura, bijli na dene wala powder hai. Garam karne par (sirf teacher demo mein) neeli lau se jalta hai aur ek tez gandh wali gas banata hai jo neele litmus ko laal karti hai. Tatva ka naam aur uska oxide?

**Hal:** Peela bhurbhura bijli na dene wala: **adhaatu**. Jalne par SO₂ (tez gandh), jo paani mein acid deti hai. Ye **sulphur** hai: S + O₂ → SO₂.

**Jaanch:** S: 1 aur 1, O: 2 aur 2 ✅. Oxide acidic hai, jaisa adhaatu ke liye apekshit tha ✅.

### Puzzle 8. Kabadiwale ki tray

**Sawaal:** Chacha ki tray mein paanch cheezein hain. Unke chumbak aur thokne ke results:

| Cheez | Chumbak | Thokne par |
|---|---|---|
| Tin ki purani peti | kheenchta hai | khokhli tang |
| Aluminium ki dibbi ka dhakkan | nahi | halki, oonchi tang |
| Plastic ki botal | nahi | dabi hui tak |
| Copper ka tukda | nahi | gehri, lambi tang |
| Seeshe ka sinker | nahi | dabi thup |

Har ek ko dhatu ya adhaatu (ya plastic) mein baanto, aur ferrous ya non-ferrous batao.

**Hal:**
- **Tin ki peti:** chumbak kheenchta hai, bajti hai: **ferrous dhatu** (lohe ki chadar).
- **Aluminium ka dhakkan:** chumbak nahi, bajta hai: **non-ferrous dhatu**.
- **Plastic ki botal:** bajti nahi: **adhaatu padarth** (plastic, dhatu nahi).
- **Copper ka tukda:** chumbak nahi, bajta hai: **non-ferrous dhatu**.
- **Seeshe ka sinker:** chumbak nahi par bajta nahi bhi (lead naram aur bhaari hai, dabi thup): phir bhi **dhatu** hai, kyunki chhilne par chamakti hai, aur wo non-ferrous hai. Ye ek apvaad hai: sab dhatuein tez nahi bajti.

**Jaanch:** Chumbak sirf ferrous ko pakadta hai, isliye ek hi cheez (tin ki peti) us dher mein gayi ✅.

## Part E: Mixed problems, saare topics ek saath

### Mixed 1. Aluminium aur oxygen (Topic 2 + mole mass)

**Sawaal:** 2.7 g aluminium poori tarah oxygen mein jalta hai. Kitna Al₂O₃ banega aur kitna oxygen lagega?

**Hal:** 4Al + 3O₂ → 2Al₂O₃.
- Al ke moles = 2.7 ÷ 27 = 0.1 mol.
- Al₂O₃ ke moles = 0.1 × 2/4 = 0.05 mol, to mass = 0.05 × 102 = **5.1 g**.
- O₂ ke moles = 0.1 × 3/4 = 0.075 mol, to mass = 0.075 × 32 = **2.4 g**.

**Jaanch:** 2.7 + 2.4 = 5.1 g ✅. Atom count: Al 4 = 4, O 6 = 6 ✅.

---

### Mixed 2. Magnesium aur chlorine (Topic 5, ionic yaugik; sirf paper par)

**Sawaal:** 4.8 g Mg ke saath chlorine ki poori matra milaane par kitna MgCl₂ banega aur kitna Cl₂ chahiye? (Chlorine zehreeli hai, ye sirf kaagaz ki ginti hai.)

**Hal:** Mg + Cl₂ → MgCl₂.
- Mg ke moles = 4.8 ÷ 24 = 0.2 mol.
- MgCl₂ = 0.2 × 95 = **19.0 g**, Cl₂ = 0.2 × 71 = **14.2 g**.

**Jaanch:** 4.8 + 14.2 = 19.0 g ✅. Mg: 1 = 1, Cl: 2 = 2 ✅.

---

### Mixed 3. Anjaan dhatu ka naam (Topic 3 + moles)

**Sawaal:** Ek trivalent dhatu M (jiske ion M³⁺ hote hain) ke 0.54 g dilute HCl se 0.06 g hydrogen dete hain. Dhatu ka atomic mass nikalo aur naam batao.

**Hal:** 2M + 6HCl → 2MCl₃ + 3H₂.
- H₂ ke moles = 0.06 ÷ 2 = 0.03 mol.
- M ke moles = 0.03 × 2/3 = 0.02 mol.
- Atomic mass = 0.54 ÷ 0.02 = **27 u**: **aluminium**.

**Jaanch:** 2Al + 6HCl → 2AlCl₃ + 3H₂: Al 2 = 2, H 6 = 6, Cl 6 = 6 ✅.

---

### Mixed 4. Kitna hissa dhatu (ionic yaugik ka mass percent)

**Sawaal:** CaO mein calcium ka mass percent kya hai?

**Hal:** 40 ÷ 56 × 100 = **71.43%**.

**Jaanch:** Ca 40 + O 16 = 56 ✅, aur oxygen ka hissa 28.57%, kul 100% ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: "Saari dhatuein sakht hoti hain."**
   Sodium aur potassium chaaku se kat jaate hain, lead naram hai, mercury liquid hai. Sakhti ek aamtaur par sahi baat hai, niyam nahi.
2. **Galti 2: "Saari adhaatuein bijli ki kharaab conductor hain."**
   Graphite bijli conduct karta hai (electrodes isi se bante hain). Diamond bijli ka kharaab par garmi ka achha conductor hai.
3. **Galti 3: "Saari dhatuein bijli ki achhi conductor hain."**
   Lead aur mercury dhatuon mein kamzor conductor hain, aur silver sabse achha.
4. **Galti 4: "Hydrogen adhaatu hai to reactivity series mein kyun?"**
   Hydrogen adhaatu hi hai, par wo dhatuon ki tarah H⁺ ion banata hai. Seedhi mein wo ek maapdand hai: usse upar ki dhatuein dilute acid se H₂ deti hain, neeche ki nahi.
5. **Galti 5: "Chumbak se kheenchne wali sab dhatuein hain, aur har dhatu ko chumbak kheenchta hai."**
   Ulta sach hai: kai dhatuein (aluminium, copper, silver, gold) ko chumbak nahi kheenchta. Chumbak sirf iron, steel, nickel aur cobalt jaisi dhatuon ko kheenchta hai.
6. **Galti 6: "Adhaatu ka oxide hamesha acidic hota hai."**
   CO, H₂O aur N₂O neutral oxide hain.
7. **Galti 7: "Ek hi test kaafi hai."**
   Chamak graphite aur iodine mein bhi hai, bijli lead mein kam hai. Kai tests milao.

## Quick Recap

- Dhatu: chamakdar, malleable, ductile, achhe conductor, sonorous, basic ya amphoteric oxide, electron dekar positive ion. Adhaatu: aamtaur par phiki, bhurbhuri, kharaab conductor, acidic ya neutral oxide, electron lekar ya share karke bond.
- Apvaad: mercury, gallium, sodium, potassium, lead, graphite, diamond, iodine, bromine, hydrogen.
- Ionic yaugik dhatu aur adhaatu ke milan se bante hain (NaCl, MgCl₂, CaO, Al₂O₃): ooncha gala-ank, bhurbhure, pighle ya ghol mein bijli.
- Pehchaan ke liye kai tests milao: chamak, bijli, thokna, peetna, chumbak, oxide ka litmus test.
- Mole aur mass ki ginti saare topics ko jodti hai.

## Practice Questions

1. Classify each as metal, non-metal or metalloid: iodine, silicon, tungsten, graphite, gallium, bromine. Give one reason for each.
2. A dark red powder is brittle and does not conduct electricity. When burnt (teacher demo only), it gives white fumes whose solution in water turns blue litmus red. Is the element a metal or a non-metal? What kind of oxide is it?
3. Compound U melts at 734 °C, does not conduct as a solid but conducts when molten, and shatters when hit. Compound V melts at 80 °C, does not conduct in any state and does not dissolve in water. Decide whether each is ionic or covalent and justify.
4. A kabadiwala tests five items: a steel spoon, a brass bell, a lead sinker, a zinc sheet and a glass bottle. Predict for each whether a magnet attracts it and how it sounds when tapped.
5. Show by electron arrangement how potassium and sulphur form K₂S. Give the formula unit mass.
6. Show by electron arrangement how aluminium and chlorine form AlCl₃. Give the formula unit mass.
7. State whether each statement is true or false and correct the false ones: (a) All metals are hard. (b) All non-metals are poor conductors of electricity. (c) Hydrogen is a metal because it sits in the reactivity series. (d) The oxide of every non-metal is acidic.
8. 2.0 g of calcium burns completely in oxygen on paper. Write the equation, and find the mass of CaO formed and the mass of oxygen used.
9. 16.2 g of zinc oxide reacts completely with sodium hydroxide: ZnO + 2NaOH → Na₂ZnO₂ + H₂O. Find the mass of NaOH required and of Na₂ZnO₂ formed. What does this reaction tell you about ZnO?
10. Four metals P, Q, R, S give these results with solutions of each other's sulphates: Q displaces P from P sulphate. P displaces R from R sulphate. R displaces S from S sulphate. S does not displace any of the others. Arrange them from most to least reactive.
11. Hydrogen is a non-metal, yet it appears in the reactivity series. Explain why.
12. What percent of Al₂O₃ is aluminium by mass? How much aluminium is present in 51 g of Al₂O₃?
13. Give the name for each: (a) a metal stored under kerosene, (b) a non-metal stored under water, (c) a metal protected by its own oxide layer, (d) a mixture that dissolves gold, (e) a non-metal that conducts electricity.

### Answers

1. - **Iodine:** non-metal (shiny par bhurbhura aur bijli nahi deta, sublime hota hai).
   - **Silicon:** metalloid (chamakdar, bhurbhura, kam conductor).
   - **Tungsten:** metal (sabse ooncha gala-ank lagbhag 3400 °C, bijli conduct karta hai).
   - **Graphite:** non-metal (carbon ka roop) jo bijli conduct karta hai.
   - **Gallium:** metal (lagbhag 29.8 °C par pighalta hai, haath ki garmi se).
   - **Bromine:** non-metal (laal-bhura liquid, zehreela, kabhi nahi chhoo sakte).

2. Bhurbhura aur bijli na dene wala powder **non-metal** hai (red phosphorus jaisa). Jalne par safed dhuan P₄O₁₀ hai, aur paani mein ghol (acid) neele litmus ko laal karta hai: **acidic oxide**. (Ye sirf teacher demo ka vivaran hai.)

3. **U ionic hai:** ooncha gala-ank (734 °C), thos mein bijli nahi par pighle mein hai, hathode se bikhar jaata hai. **V covalent hai:** neecha gala-ank (80 °C), kisi roop mein bijli nahi, paani mein nahi ghulta. (U ka data KBr jaisa hai, V naphthalene jaisa.)

4. - **Steel spoon:** aam steel ko chumbak kheenchta hai (lohe wali), tang ki awaaz. (Kai stainless steel ke chammach ko chumbak bahut kam ya bilkul nahi kheenchta, isliye kabadiwala awaaz aur wazan bhi dekhta hai.)
   - **Brass bell:** chumbak nahi (copper-zinc alloy), lambi tang.
   - **Lead sinker:** chumbak nahi, naram aur bhaari, dabi thup.
   - **Zinc sheet:** chumbak nahi, tang.
   - **Glass bottle:** chumbak nahi, bajti hai par dhatu nahi, aur tootne wali cheez.
   Isliye sirf steel spoon ferrous dher mein jayega.

5. K (2, 8, 8, 1) 1 electron deta hai (K⁺), S (2, 8, 6) 2 electron leta hai (S²⁻). Barabar karne ke liye 2 K aur 1 S: **K₂S**. Formula unit mass = 2 × 39 + 32 = 78 + 32 = **110 u**.

6. Al (2, 8, 3) 3 electron deta hai (Al³⁺), har Cl (2, 8, 7) 1 electron leta hai (Cl⁻), to 1 Al ke liye 3 Cl: **AlCl₃**. Mass = 27 + 3 × 35.5 = 27 + 106.5 = **133.5 u**.

7. (a) **False** ❌: sodium, potassium chaaku se katte hain, lead naram hai, mercury liquid. Sahi: zyadatar dhatuein sakht hoti hain. (b) **False** ❌: graphite bijli conduct karta hai. (c) **False** ❌: hydrogen adhaatu hai, seedhi mein wo ek maapdand ki tarah hai (acid se H₂ dene wali dhatuein uske upar). (d) **False** ❌: CO, H₂O aur N₂O neutral hain.

8. 2Ca + O₂ → 2CaO. Ca ke moles = 2.0 ÷ 40 = 0.05 mol, to CaO = 0.05 mol = 0.05 × 56 = **2.8 g**. O₂ ke moles = 0.025, to mass = 0.025 × 32 = **0.8 g**. Jaanch: 2.0 + 0.8 = 2.8 g ✅. Atom: Ca 2 = 2, O 2 = 2 ✅.

9. ZnO ke moles = 16.2 ÷ 81 = 0.2 mol. NaOH 0.4 mol = 0.4 × 40 = **16.0 g**. Na₂ZnO₂ = 0.2 mol, 46 + 65 + 32 = 143, to **28.6 g**. Paani bhi bana: 0.2 mol = 3.6 g. Jaanch: 16.2 + 16.0 = 32.2 g aur 28.6 + 3.6 = 32.2 g ✅. Atom count: Zn 1 = 1, O 3 = 3, Na 2 = 2, H 2 = 2 ✅. Ye reaction batata hai ki **ZnO amphoteric hai**, kyunki wo base (NaOH) se bhi reaction karta hai (aur acid se bhi karta hai).

10. Q displaces P, to Q > P. P displaces R, to P > R. R displaces S, to R > S. S kisi ko nahi hataata, to sabse kam. Kram: **Q > P > R > S**.

11. Hydrogen adhaatu hai, par dhatuon ki tarah H⁺ ion banata hai. Reactivity series mein usse upar wali dhatuein dilute acid se H₂ nikaal sakti hain aur neeche wali nahi, isliye wo maapdand ke roop mein rakha gaya hai.

12. Al₂O₃ = 102 u, aluminium = 54 u. Percent = 54 ÷ 102 × 100 = **52.94%**. 51 g mein Al = 51 × 54 ÷ 102 = **27 g**. Jaanch: 27 g Al = 1 mol, aur 51 g Al₂O₃ = 0.5 mol jisme 1 mol Al ✅.

13. (a) **Sodium** (ya potassium), kerosene mein. (b) **White phosphorus**, paani mein (hawa mein jal jaata hai). (c) **Aluminium**, apni patli oxide parat se. (d) **Aqua regia** (1 hissa concentrated HNO₃, 3 hisse concentrated HCl), sirf padhne ke liye (ye teacher demo bhi nahi hai), jo gold aur platinum ko ghulata hai. (e) **Graphite**.
