# Topic 1: Dhatu aur Adhaatu: Bhautik Gun

Class 9 mein humne tatvon ko teen parivaar mein baanta tha: dhatu, adhaatu aur upadhaatu, aur dekha tha ki dhatu chamakdar hoti hain, peetne par chadar banti hain aur ghanti jaisi awaaz deti hain. Pichhle chapter mein humne dekha ki zinc dilute acid se H₂ nikaalta hai aur copper nahi. Ab hum is poore parivaar ko **gehrai se** dekhenge, aur isse yahan se shuru karenge: **dhatu ko haath se chhoo kar, tod kar, bajaa kar aur bijli lagaa kar kaise pehchaante hain**, yaani unke **bhautik gun (physical properties)**. Rasayanik gun (oxygen, paani, acid ke saath vyavhaar) agle topics mein aayenge. Is topic ke aakhir mein hum ye bhi seekhenge ki taar, bartan, ghanti ya pul banaane ke liye kaun si dhatu kyun chuni jaati hai.

## Mithai ki dukaan par chaandi ka warq

Mr. Ambekar apni bhatiji Advika ko Dussehra ki mithai dilwane le gaye the. Dukaan ke kaanch ke case mein barfi ke tukde saje the, aur har tukde par chamakdar **chaandi ka warq** laga tha, itna patla ki barfi ke upar chipka hua sheesha jaisa lagta tha.

Advika ne dukaandaar se warq ki ek chhoti sheet dekhne ko maangi. Dukaandaar ne kagaz ke beech rakhi sheet ko dheere se kholne ki koshish ki, aur Advika ne jaise hi zor se saans chhodi, warq udd kar muda-tuda ho gaya aur uske haath mein chipak gaya.

"Arre! Ye to ek phoonk mein ud gaya!" Advika hansi.

Mr. Ambekar ne kaha, "Isi liye dukaandaar kagaz ke beech mein rakhta hai. Yaad hai, Class 9 mein kya padha tha? Dhatu **malleable** hoti hai: peetne par chadar ban jaati hai. Chaandi ko kaarigar kai ghanton tak halke hathodon se peette hain, jab tak wo itni patli na ho jaaye ki phoonk se ud jaaye. Koi lakdi, koyla ya chaak ke tukde ke saath aisa karke dikhaao to wo chura ho jaayega."

"To sirf chaandi aisi hoti hai?"

"Nahi. Sone ke warq bhi bante hain, jo aur patle hote hain. Aur sona to itna ductile bhi hai ki ek gram se kai kilometre lambi taar kheench lo. Par aisa kyun hai? Aur kya **har** dhatu aisi hoti hai? Kya koi dhatu naram bhi hoti hai, ya paani jaisi behti bhi?" Mr. Ambekar muskuraaye. "Chalo, ek-ek gun ko khol kar dekhte hain. Aur ghar chal kar hum ek chhoti si jaanch kit bhi banayenge."

## Part A: Dhatuon ke gun, ek-ek karke

Dhatuon ke aam bhautik gun ye hain. (Har gun mein "aam taur par" lagaa hai, kyunki thodi der mein hum apvaad bhi dekhenge.)

1. **Lustre.** Taaza kaati ya polish ki hui dhatu chamakti hai. Isi liye gehne, bartan aur sikke dhatu ke bante hain.
2. **Malleable.** Hathode se peetne par chadar ban jaati hai. Sone ka warq lagbhag **0.1 micrometre** moti (**lagbhag** maan) tak peeta ja sakta hai. 1 micrometre = 10⁻⁶ m, yaani ek baal ki motaai ka sau-hissa se bhi kam!
3. **Ductile.** Kheench kar taar ban jaati hai. **Lagbhag** 1 gram sona kheench kar lagbhag **2 km** lambi taar mein badla ja sakta hai.
4. **Garmi aur bijli ki achhi suchalak.** Sabse achhi chaandi (silver), phir copper, phir gold, phir aluminium. Isiliye ghar ki wiring copper ya aluminium ki hoti hai. **Apvaad:** lead aur mercury dhatuon mein bhi **kamzor** conductor hain.
5. **Sonorous.** Chot par ghanti jaisi awaaz: mandir ki ghanti, school ki ghanti, kadhai ki thankaar.
6. **Aam taur par kathor aur ghani (hard aur dense).** Lohe ka pul, hathoda, ghar ke taale.
7. **Aam taur par ooncha melting point aur boiling point, aur kamre ke taapman par thos.** Isiliye tawa, kadhai aur ghar ke bartan aanch par pighalte nahi.

### Dhatu aise kyun hoti hai? (Ek paragraph mein "electron ke sagar" ka vichar)

Class 9 ([chemical-bonding-part4.md](../../../class9/chemistry/chemical-bonding/chemical-bonding-part4.md)) mein humne dekha tha ki ionic aur covalent compounds ke alag gun kyon hote hain. Dhatu ke andar ek **teesra hi** manzar hota hai, jise abhi hum sirf naam se jaanenge. Dhatu ke atoms apne sabse bahari electrons ko loose rakhte hain, aur ye electrons ek jagah baandhe na hokar poore tukde mein **ghoomte rehte** hain, jaise atoms ke beech electrons ka **sagar**. Ye sagar saare atoms ko ek saath jode rakhta hai (isliye dhatu aam taur par kathor aur ooncha melting point wali hai). Jab hathoda padta hai, atoms ki parat **sarak** jaati hai par sagar unhe jode rakhta hai, isliye dhatu toot kar bikharti nahi, balki chadar ya taar ban jaati hai. Aur jab bijli ya garmi lagti hai, ye ghoomte electrons use **tezi se aage le jaate hain**, isliye dhatu achhi conductor hai. Roshni par ye electrons chhote-chhote hilte hain aur roshni ko wapas bhejte hain: yahi lustre hai. Adhaatuon mein electrons aise ghoomte nahi, isliye unke gun alag hain. (Is vichaar ka poora vistaar aage ki class mein.)

## Part B: Dhatu aur adhaatu ka aamne-saamne chart

Adhaatu (non-metal) aam taur par **dull**, **bhangur**, **kamzor conductor**, **halke** aur **kam melting point wale** hote hain, aur thok par **sonorous nahi**. Jaise sulphur ka tukda ya coal.

| Gun | Dhatu (aam taur par) | Adhaatu (aam taur par) | Kaaran (ek line mein) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lustre | Chamakdar | Dull (nirjeev) | Dhatu mein ghoomte electrons roshni ko wapas bhejte hain |
| Peetne par | Malleable, chadar banti hai | Bhangur, chura ho jaata hai | Dhatu ki parat sarak sakti hai, adhaatu mein nahi |
| Khinchne par | Ductile, taar banti hai | Taar nahi banti | Wahi: parat sarakna |
| Bijli ki conduction | Achhi | Kamzor (zyadatar insulator) | Dhatu mein electrons chalne ko azaad hain |
| Garmi ki conduction | Achhi | Kamzor | Wahi electrons garmi bhi le jaate hain |
| Chot par awaaz | Sonorous | Sonorous nahi | Kathor, ek saath jude atoms kampan ko aage badhaate hain |
| Density | Aam taur par zyada | Aam taur par kam | Dhatu ke atoms paas-paas aur bhaari hote hain |
| Melting aur boiling point | Aam taur par ooncha | Aam taur par kam | Dhatu mein atoms ko sagar ne kasa hua rakha hai |
| Kamre ke taapman par | Aam taur par thos | Thos, liquid ya gas | Dono parivaar mein mixed |

```
   DHATU                                  ADHAATU
   (jaise copper, silver)                 (jaise sulphur, coal)

   chamakdar ............................ dull
   peetne par chadar .................... bhangur, chura
   khinch kar taar ...................... taar nahi
   bijli: achha conductor ............... bijli: bura conductor
   garmi: achha conductor ............... garmi: bura conductor
   chot par: ghanti jaisi awaaz ......... chot par: koi awaaz nahi
   ghani, kathor, ooncha melting ........ halka, kam melting
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Dhatu aur adhaatu ke bhautik gun ek chart mein: har line mein baayein dhatu ka gun, daayein adhaatu ka ulta gun. Ye aam niyam hain; kuch apvaad aage Part C mein hain.

## Part C: Apvaad (exceptions): jahan niyam todne waale kisse hain

Dhatu aur adhaatu ke ye gun "aam taur par" sach hain. Har gun ka kuch na kuch apvaad hai, aur yahi apvaad sabse mazedaar hain: inse niyam ki asli **samajh** banti hai.

### Dhatuon mein apvaad

| Dhatu | Kya alag hai | Kuch number |
|---|---|---|
| **Mercury** | Kamre ke taapman par **liquid** hai (aur kamzor conductor) | Sirf padhne ke liye; students ise nahi chhoote, bhaap bahut zehreeli hai |
| **Gallium** | Haath ki garmi se pighal jaata hai | Melting point lagbhag **29.8 °C** |
| **Caesium** | Garam din mein pighal sakta hai | Melting point lagbhag **28.5 °C** |
| **Sodium aur potassium** | Itne naram ki chaaku se kat jaate hain, aur paani par **tairte** hain | Density lagbhag 0.97 aur 0.86 g per cm³ |
| **Lithium** | Sabse halki dhatu | Density lagbhag **0.53** g per cm³ |
| **Osmium** | Sabse ghani dhatu | Density lagbhag **22.6** g per cm³ |
| **Tungsten** | Sabse ooncha melting point; isliye bulb ke filament mein | Lagbhag **3400 °C** |
| **Lead** | Dhatu hai par bijli ka kamzor conductor | Isiliye wiring ke liye nahi |

**Sodium aur potassium sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo).** Ye dono dhatu hawa aur paani se tezi se react karte hain (Topic 2 aur Topic 3), isliye students inhe kabhi nahi chhoote; kerosene mein rakhe jaate hain. Hum yahan sirf padh rahe hain ki ye naram hote hain aur paani par tairte hain.

**Gallium ke baare mein:** ye chamakdar, dhatu jaisa dikhne wala thos tukda hota hai jo haath mein rakhne par kuch hi der mein pighal jaata hai, kyunki haath ka taapman (lagbhag 37 °C) uske melting point (29.8 °C) se zyada hai. Ye **sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo)** ka example hai.

Sabse halki dhatu lithium (0.53) aur sabse ghani osmium (22.6) ka fark: 22.6 ÷ 0.53 = **lagbhag 42.6 guna**. Yaani osmium ka ek chhota sa tukda utne hi size ke lithium se lagbhag 43 guna bhaari hoga.

### Adhaatuon mein apvaad

| Adhaatu | Kya alag hai | Kyun mazedaar |
|---|---|---|
| **Graphite** (carbon ka ek roop) | Bijli ka **achha conductor** | Pencil ki lead aur battery ke electrode graphite se bante hain |
| **Diamond** (carbon ka doosra roop) | Sabse **kathor** prakritik padaarth, par bijli ka conductor **nahi** | Ek hi tatva, do alag gun: Chapter 10 mein carbon ki poori kahani |
| **Iodine** | **Chamakdar** thos, aur garam karne par seedha gas (sublimation) | Adhaatu hokar bhi chamakdar |
| **Bromine** | Kamre ke taapman par **liquid** adhaatu | Mercury ke saath, ye dono hi liquid tatva hain (Class 9). Sirf padhne ke liye: bromine zehreela hai |

Class 9 mein humne **upadhaatu (metalloids)**, jaise boron, silicon, germanium, arsenic, bhi dekhe the, jo dhatu aur adhaatu ke **beech** ke gun rakhte hain. Aam niyam dono taraf ke tatvon par lagte hain, par beech ke tatvon par nahi.

## Part D: Ghar par karne wali jaanchein (safe activities)

Ab Mr. Ambekar ki jaanch kit ki baari. Har activity ke upar label dekhna.

### Activity 1: Bijli ki jaanch (conductivity tester)

**Ghar par adult ke saath.** Sirf **2 chhote cells (3 V)** use karo. Gharelu bijli (mains, plug ya socket) se kabhi nahi.

Saman: 2 AA cells (cell-holder mein), ek chhoti **LED**, uske saath ek **100 ohm ka resistor** (ya ek chhota torch ka bulb), insulated taar aur 2 alligator clip. LED ke saath resistor lagaana zaroori hai, nahi to LED jal sakti hai. (3 V mein LED ke 2 V ke baad bacha 1 V, 100 ohm par lagbhag 10 mA, jo LED ke liye surakshit hai.)

```
        cell 1   cell 2
       +--[+ -]--[+ -]--+
       :                :
      LED               :
       :                :
   resistor (100 ohm)   :
       :                :
    clip A           clip B
        \             /
       [ jaanchne wali cheez ]

   LED jali  ->  cheez bijli guzarne deti hai (conductor)
   LED nahi  ->  cheez bijli nahi guzarne deti (insulator)
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Conductivity tester ka saral circuit: 2 cells, LED aur uske saath series mein resistor, aur do clip. Clip A aur clip B ko jaanchne wali cheez ke do sirhon par lagao. Agar cheez conductor hai to circuit poora ho jaata hai aur LED jalti hai.

Kuch cheezein ek-ek karke jaancho: steel ka chammach, aluminium foil, copper ki taar, pencil ki lead, pencil ka lakdi wala hissa, eraser, rubber band, plastic ka kangha, kaanch. Dhyan: pencil ki lead graphite ki hoti hai, isliye LED jalti hai.

### Activity 2: Chumbak (magnet) ki jaanch

**Ghar par adult ke saath.** Ek chhota chumbak lekar steel ka chammach, loha ka keel, copper ka taar, aluminium foil, peetal ka sikka, plastic ki cheez ke paas laao.

Tum paoge ki **sirf loha (aur steel, jo lohe se banta hai) kheenchta hai**. Copper aur aluminium nahi kheenchte. Isliye "chumbak dhatu ko kheenchta hai" ye **galat** hai: kuch hi dhatuein (loha, nickel, cobalt) kheenchti hain. (Dhyan: kai stainless steel ke chammach ko chumbak bahut kam ya bilkul nahi kheenchta, kyunki usme loha doosri dhatuon ke saath ek khaas tarah se mila hota hai. Isliye ek chammach na chipke to bhi wo dhatu hi hai.)

### Activity 3: Ghanti ki jaanch (ringing test)

**Ghar par adult ke saath.** Alag-alag cheezein ek dhaage se latka kar ek chhoti steel ki chammach se halke se thok kar awaaz suno.

```
   dhaage se latki steel ki thaali
              :
         +----+----+
         :  thaali :     <--- chamach se halka tap
         +---------+

   steel / peetal: lambi, ghanti jaisi "tingg..."  (sonorous)
   lakdi / plastic: chhoti "thak"                  (sonorous nahi)
   kaanch: kabhi kabhi ghanti jaisa, par dhatu nahi
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Ringing test: dhaage se latki cheez ko halke se thokne par dhatu ki cheezein (steel, peetal) lambi ghanti jaisi awaaz deti hain, lakdi aur plastic nahi. (Kaanch bhi bajta hai, isliye awaaz akeli dhatu ki pehchaan nahi hai.)

### Activity 4: Lachak ki jaanch (flexibility test)

**Ghar par adult ke saath.** Teen cheezein lo: **aluminium foil**, **copper ki patli taar** (insulation hati hui) aur **pencil ki lead**.

- Aluminium foil ko mod kar chhoti gend bana lo; wo bina phate mud jaati hai (malleable).
- Copper ki taar ko 3 se 4 baar halke se aage-peeche modo; wo bina toote mud jaati hai (ductile), par bahut zyada mod-mod kar karoge to akhir mein toot sakti hai.
- Pencil ki lead ko halke se modne ki koshish karo; wo **toot** jaati hai (bhangur). Lead ko zor se mat dabao ki chubh jaaye.

Jaanch ka nateeja: dhatu mud jaati hai, adhaatu (graphite) bhangur hai, kyunki graphite adhaatu hai, bhale hi bijli le jaata ho.

### Activity 5: Chaaku ki jaanch (sirf dekhne ke liye)

**Sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo).** Teacher kerosene mein rakha sodium ka chhota tukda nikaal kar chaaku se kaatta hai: wo mom ki tarah naram kat jaata hai, aur andar ka taaza hissa chamakdar hota hai, jo kuch hi der mein hawa se react kar ke dull ho jaata hai. Students door se dekhte hain.

## Part E: Solved examples (numbers ke saath)

Is topic mein density = mass ÷ volume. Sone ki density lagbhag **19.3 g per cm³** lo.

### Example 1. 1 g sone ka warq kitna bada hoga?

Warq ki motaai lagbhag 0.1 micrometre = 0.1 × 10⁻⁴ cm = 10⁻⁵ cm. (Lagbhag maan.)

- 1 g sone ka volume = 1 ÷ 19.3 = **0.0518 cm³**.
- Volume = area × motaai, to area = 0.0518 ÷ 10⁻⁵ = **5181 cm²** (lagbhag).
- 1 m² = 10,000 cm², to ye lagbhag **0.52 m²**, yaani aadhe metre ki sheet.

Matlab 1 gram sone se lagbhag aadha vargmeter ka warq ban sakta hai.

Jaanch: area × motaai = 5181 × 10⁻⁵ = 0.0518 cm³, aur 0.0518 × 19.3 = 1.0 g ✅

### Example 2. Warq ki gaddi

Maan lo 0.1 mm = 100 micrometre motaai mein warq ki sheetein ek ke upar ek rakhi hain. Har sheet 0.1 micrometre moti hai.

- Sheetein = 100 ÷ 0.1 = **1000**.

Jaanch: 1000 × 0.1 µm = 100 µm = 0.1 mm ✅. Yaani 1000 warq milkar bhi sirf 0.1 mm moti, ek kagaz ki sheet jitni.

### Example 3. 1 g sone ki taar ki motaai

Maan lo 1 g sone se lagbhag **2 km** lambi taar kheenchi gayi (lagbhag maan).

- Volume = 0.0518 cm³, lambai = 2 km = 200000 cm.
- Cross-section ka kshetrafal = 0.0518 ÷ 200000 = 2.59 × 10⁻⁷ cm².
- Taar ka vyas (diameter) lagbhag **5.7 µm** nikalta hai.

Jaanch: π × (diameter ÷ 2)² × lambai se wapas volume nikaalne par 0.0518 cm³ milta hai ✅. Ye insaani baal (lagbhag 70 µm) se bhi kai guna patli hai.

### Example 4. Kaun bhaari, kaun halka?

Sodium ki density 0.97, osmium ki 22.6 g per cm³. Dono ke 10 cm³ ke tukde:

- Sodium ka mass = 10 × 0.97 = **9.7 g**.
- Osmium ka mass = 10 × 22.6 = **226 g**.
- Paani ki density 1 g per cm³ hai: sodium (0.97) us se thodi halki hai, isliye paani par **tairta** hai (par uska paani se react karna Topic 3 mein; ise students nahi karte). Osmium (22.6) doobta hai.

Jaanch: 226 ÷ 9.7 = 23.3 guna ✅ (density ratio 22.6 ÷ 0.97 = 23.3).

### Example 5. Gallium kitna garam hone par pighalta hai?

Gallium ka melting point 29.8 °C hai, haath ka taapman lagbhag 37 °C.

- Haath ka taapman − melting point = 37 − 29.8 = **7.2 °C**.

Yaani haath gallium ke melting point se 7.2 °C zyada garam hai, isliye tukda pighal jaata hai.

Jaanch: 29.8 + 7.2 = 37.0 °C, yaani haath ka taapman ✅

### Example 6. Tester ki reading padhna

Ek bacche ne conductivity tester par ye nateeje note kiye.

| Cheez | LED jali? |
|---|---|
| Steel ka chammach | Haan |
| Pencil ki lead | Haan |
| Plastic ka kangha | Nahi |
| Aluminium foil | Haan |
| Rubber ka tukda | Nahi |
| Lakdi ki chhadi | Nahi |

Sawaal: kin cheezon mein dhatu hai? Kya LED ka jalna hamesha dhatu hone ka saboot hai?

- Steel aur aluminium dhatu hain, to LED jali. Plastic, rubber aur lakdi insulator hain, to nahi jali.
- Pencil ki lead mein LED **jali**, par wo **graphite (adhaatu)** hai. Isliye LED jalna **sirf** dhatu hone ka saboot nahi: graphite jaise apvaad bhi hain.

### Example 7. Kaun si cheez kisse banti hai? (Reasoning)

| Cheez | Material | Kaaran |
|---|---|---|
| Ghar ki bijli ki taar | Copper ya aluminium | Achhi conductor, ductile (taar ban sake) |
| Taar ke upar ka khol | Plastic ya rubber | Insulator, taaki jhatka na lage |
| Kadhai ya tawa ka tala | Aluminium, steel ya copper | Garmi ki achhi conductor, ooncha melting point |
| Kadhai ka handle | Plastic ya lakdi | Garmi ka kamzor conductor, haath nahi jalega |
| Ghanti | Peetal ya bronze | Sonorous, kathor |
| Pul aur railway ke tracks | Loha ya steel | Kathor, majboot |
| Khane ki foil | Aluminium | Malleable, halka |
| Gehne | Sona, chaandi | Lustre, malleable, ductile |
| Bulb ka filament | Tungsten | Ooncha melting point (lagbhag 3400 °C) |
| Pencil ki lead aur electrode | Graphite | Adhaatu hokar bijli ka conductor, aur ragadne par chikna (slippery) |

Dhyan do: bartan ke liye **garmi ka conductor** chahiye (taaki khana pake), par handle ke liye **insulator**. Ek hi bartan mein dono cheezein mil jaati hain.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Saari dhatuein kathor hoti hain"**

Sodium aur potassium chaaku se kat jaate hain, aur mercury to liquid hai. "Aam taur par" kathor kehna sahi hai.

**Galti 2: "Saare adhaatu bijli ke bure conductor hain"**

Graphite adhaatu hai par bijli ka achha conductor hai.

**Galti 3: "Saari dhatuein bijli ki achhi conductor hain"**

Lead dhatu hai par kamzor conductor hai. Mercury bhi dhatuon mein kamzor conductor hai.

**Galti 4: "Chumbak sabhi dhatuon ko kheenchta hai"**

Chumbak sirf loha, nickel, cobalt jaisi kuch dhatuon ko kheenchta hai. Copper, aluminium, chaandi nahi.

**Galti 5: "Saari dhatuein thos hoti hain"**

Mercury kamre ke taapman par liquid hai, aur gallium aur caesium kamre ke taapman ke bahut paas pighalte hain.

**Galti 6: "Sirf dhatuon ki chamak hoti hai"**

Iodine adhaatu hai par chamakdar hai.

**Galti 7: "LED jali to dhatu hai"**

LED sirf bijli guzarne ka saboot hai. Graphite bhi LED jala deta hai. Dhatu ki pehchaan ke liye kai gun saath dekhne chahiye.

**Galti 8: "Warq aur taar ka matlab hai ki dhatu naram hai"**

Malleable aur ductile hona naram hone se alag hai. Demonstration: lohar garam lohe ko peet kar patli chadar aur taar bana deta hai, phir bhi thanda loha itna kathor hai ki usse pul bante hain. Matlab "peetne par na tootna" ek alag gun hai, "naram hona" alag.

## Quick Recap

- **Dhatu aam taur par:** chamakdar, malleable, ductile, garmi aur bijli ki achhi conductor, sonorous, ghani aur kathor, ooncha melting point, thos.
- **Adhaatu aam taur par:** dull, bhangur, kamzor conductor, sonorous nahi, halke aur kam melting point.
- **Electron ka sagar:** dhatu mein bahari electrons ghoomte hain; isi se chamak, conduction aur malleability aati hai (poora vistaar aage).
- **Conduction ka kram:** silver, copper, gold, aluminium. Lead aur mercury kamzor.
- **Apvaad (dhatu):** mercury (liquid), gallium (29.8 °C) aur caesium (28.5 °C) pighalte hain, sodium aur potassium naram aur paani par tairte hain, lithium sabse halki (0.53), osmium sabse ghani (22.6), tungsten ka melting point sabse ooncha (lagbhag 3400 °C).
- **Apvaad (adhaatu):** graphite bijli ka conductor, diamond sabse kathor, iodine chamakdar aur sublime hota hai, bromine liquid.
- **Gold:** warq lagbhag 0.1 µm moti, 1 g se lagbhag 2 km taar (lagbhag).
- **Activities:** conductivity tester (2 cells + LED ke saath resistor), chumbak, ringing test, flexibility test. Sodium ka kaatna sirf teacher demo. Kuch bhi chakhna nahi.
- **Material ka chunaav:** kaam ke gun dekho (taar: ductile conductor; handle: insulator; ghanti: sonorous; filament: ooncha melting point).

## Practice Questions

1. A silver bar has a volume of 20 cm³. If the density of silver is 10.5 g per cm³, what is its mass?

2. A jeweller beats 0.5 g of gold (density 19.3 g per cm³) into a leaf about 0.25 micrometre thick. Estimate the area of the leaf in cm².

3. About 1 g of gold can be drawn into a wire of length about 2 km. Estimate the length of wire that can be drawn from 0.25 g, assuming the wire thickness stays the same.

4. Lithium has density 0.53 g per cm³. Find the mass of 100 cm³ of lithium. Also arrange lithium, sodium (0.97), potassium (0.86) and iron (7.87) in increasing order of density, and say which are lighter than water.

5. Osmium has density 22.6 g per cm³. A cube of side 2 cm is made of osmium. Find its mass.

6. On a hot day, the room temperature is 31 °C. Will a piece of gallium (melting point 29.8 °C) and a piece of caesium (melting point 28.5 °C) be solid or liquid? By how many degrees is the room above each melting point?

7. A student's conductivity tester gave these results. Copper wire: LED glows. Iron nail: LED glows. Chalk: LED does not glow. Graphite (from an old pencil): LED glows. Dry wood: LED does not glow. Name the conductors, and say why the graphite result needs a careful explanation.

8. Why are cooking pans made of metals like aluminium, but their handles of plastic or wood?

9. A bell made of lead is tapped and it does not ring well, while one made of bronze rings clearly. Which property is this, and what does it tell you about lead?

10. Why is tungsten used for the filament of an electric bulb and not copper or gold?

11. A magnet attracts an iron nail but not an aluminium foil piece. Does this mean aluminium is a non-metal? Explain.

12. Name one non-metal that is a good conductor of electricity, one that is a liquid at room temperature, and one that is a shiny solid. Give one use of the first.

13. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) All metals are solid at room temperature.
    (b) Diamond is the hardest natural substance and a good conductor of electricity.
    (c) Sodium can be cut with a knife.
    (d) Iodine is a lustrous non-metal.

### Answers

1. Mass = density × volume = 10.5 × 20 = **210 g**. Jaanch: 210 ÷ 20 = 10.5 ✅

2. Volume = 0.5 ÷ 19.3 = 0.0259 cm³. Motaai = 0.25 µm = 0.25 × 10⁻⁴ cm = 2.5 × 10⁻⁵ cm. Area = volume ÷ motaai = 0.0259 ÷ (2.5 × 10⁻⁵) = **lagbhag 1036 cm²**. Jaanch: 1036 × 2.5 × 10⁻⁵ = 0.0259 ✅

3. Taar ki motaai wahi rahe to lambai mass ke barabar anupaat mein badhti hai: 0.25 g ke liye 0.25 × 2 km = **0.5 km = 500 m** (lagbhag).

4. 100 cm³ lithium ka mass = 100 × 0.53 = **53 g**. Badhta hua kram: lithium (0.53), potassium (0.86), sodium (0.97), iron (7.87). Paani ki density 1 g per cm³ hai, isliye lithium, potassium aur sodium (teeno 1 se kam) paani se halke hain; iron bhaari hai.

5. Volume = 2 × 2 × 2 = 8 cm³. Mass = 22.6 × 8 = **180.8 g**. Jaanch: 180.8 ÷ 8 = 22.6 ✅

6. 31 °C dono ke melting point se zyada hai, isliye dono **liquid** honge. Gallium: 31 − 29.8 = **1.2 °C** zyada. Caesium: 31 − 28.5 = **2.5 °C** zyada.

7. Conductors: copper wire, iron nail, graphite (LED jali). Chalk aur dry wood insulator hain. Graphite adhaatu (carbon) hai par bijli ka conductor hai, ye apvaad hai; isliye LED jalne se hi dhatu hone ka faisla nahi hota.

8. Pan ko aanch ki garmi khane tak pahunchaane ke liye garmi ka achha conductor (aluminium) chahiye. Handle haath mein pakda jaata hai, isliye wahan garmi ka kamzor conductor (plastic ya lakdi) chahiye, taaki haath na jale.

9. Ye **sonorous** hone ka gun hai. Lead naram aur kamzor sonorous hai, isliye uski ghanti achhi nahi bajti; peetal ya bronze kathor hai, isliye lambi awaaz deta hai.

10. Bulb ka filament bahut garam hota hai, isliye uska melting point bahut ooncha hona chahiye. Tungsten ka melting point lagbhag 3400 °C hai, jabki copper aur gold bahut kam taapman par hi pighal jaate hain.

11. Nahi. Chumbak sirf loha, nickel, cobalt jaisi kuch hi dhatuon ko kheenchta hai. Aluminium dhatu hai (chamakdar, malleable, conductor) par magnetic nahi.

12. Bijli ka conductor: **graphite** (pencil ki lead, electrode). Liquid: **bromine** (sirf padhne ke liye, zehreela). Chamakdar thos: **iodine**.

13. (a) **False** ❌. Mercury kamre ke taapman par liquid hai. (b) **False** ❌. Diamond sabse kathor hai par bijli ka conductor nahi hai. (c) **True** ✅. Sodium chaaku se kat jaata hai (sirf teacher demo). (d) **True** ✅. Iodine chamakdar thos adhaatu hai.
