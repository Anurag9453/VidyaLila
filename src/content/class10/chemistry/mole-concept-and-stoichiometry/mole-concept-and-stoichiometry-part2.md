# Topic 2: Vapour Density: Gas Ka Halka-Bhari Naap

Topic 1 mein humne Avogadro ka niyam dobara parakha: **same taapman (T) aur dabao (P) par barabar volume ki gas mein barabar molecules** hote hain, chahe gas koi bhi ho. Us niyam se volume ka anupaat mole ka anupaat nikla. Ab ek naya sawaal: agar **barabar volume mein barabar molecules** hain, to un barabar volumes ke **vazan** mein jo fark aayega, wo kiska fark hoga? Jawab seedha hai: **ek-ek molecule ke vazan ka**. Is topic mein hum isi se ek chhota, tez aur bahut kaam ka naap banate hain: **vapour density (VD)**, yaani kisi gas ko **hydrogen se tol kar** bataana ki wo kitni guna bhaari hai. Isse hum gas ka molecular mass nikaalenge, aur ye bhi bata paayenge ki kaun si gas hawa mein upar jaayegi aur kaun neeche baithegi.

---

## Mela ka gubbare wala

Mr. Sapkal apne bhatije Ranveer ko gaon ke mela mein le gaye. Ek jagah bhaari bheed thi: ek **gubbare wala** apne stall par rang-birange gubbare bech raha tha. Ranveer ne ek ajeeb baat dekhi. Kuch gubbare dhaage se bandhe hue **seedhe upar** tair rahe the, jaise aasman ko chhoo rahe hon. Aur kuch gubbare stall ke bans se bandhe hue **neeche jhool** rahe the, bas kuch inch upar, aur zara hawa chali to idhar-udhar ladkhadate rahe.

Stall ke peeche ek bhaari dhaatu ka **cylinder** rakha tha. Gubbare wale bhaiya ne ek gubbare ka munh cylinder ke nozzle se lagaya, valve haule se khola, aur gubbara phoolkar tairne laga. Dusre gubbare unhone **hath ke pump** se phulaye the. "Upar jaane wale **helium** ke hain, bachcha," unhone Ranveer se kaha. "Neeche wale mein sirf **hawa** hai."

Ranveer ne Mr. Sapkal ki kameez kheenchi. "Chacha, dono mein gas hi hai. Phir ek upar, ek neeche? Aur bhaiya helium ko 'halki' kehte hain, to ye **kitni halki** hai? Hum ise kaise naap sakte hain?"

Mr. Sapkal muskuraye. "Class 9 mein tune gas ki density padhi thi, grams per litre mein. Aaj ek aur aasan naap seekhte hain: kisi bhi gas ka **ek litre** lo, aur usi halat mein **hydrogen ka ek litre** lo. Dono ko tolo. Jo ratio aayega, wahi us gas ki halki-bhaari ka naap hai. Aur Avogadro ka niyam ise ek bahut simple number bana dega."

> **Safety:** gubbare wale bhaiya cylinder khud sambhalte hain; hum sirf door se dekh rahe the. Gas cylinder koi bachcha kabhi nahi chhuta, na ghar par kabhi aisa kuch kiya jaata hai. Is poore topic mein har gas ka kaam **sirf kagaz par** hai. Jo gas zehreeli hain (H₂S, SO₂, Cl₂, NO₂, F₂, NH₃, HCl), unka zikr **sirf padhne ke liye** hai.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Relative density** | Ek gas ka vazan, kisi doosri gas ke **barabar volume** ke vazan se kitna guna hai (same T, P) | Gas hydrogen se kitni guna bhaari hai |
| **Vapour density (VD)** | Kisi gas ka relative density, jab **doosri gas hydrogen** ho | CO₂ ki VD = 22 |

## Part A: Naya naap kyun? Hydrogen se tolna

Class 9 mein tumne gas ki density **M ÷ 22.4** (g per L, STP par) padhi thi. Par wo ek **alag number** hai, jo taapman aur dabao badalne par badalta rehta hai. Chemist ko kabhi-kabhi ek aisa number chahiye jo gas ki **apni** pehchaan bataye, aur taapman-dabao par nirbhar na kare. Iska tareeka: **do gas, ek hi halat mein, ek hi volume le kar tolna.**

Kisse tolein? Hum sabse **halki** gas, **hydrogen (H₂)**, ko ek maan (standard) banate hain. Phir:

**Vapour density (VD) = (gas ke ek volume ka vazan) ÷ (usi T aur P par hydrogen ke utne hi volume ka vazan)**

Ye dono vazan ek hi unit mein hote hain, isliye ratio ek **bina unit wala number** hai, bas "kitni guna". (Naam ka "vapour" sirf purani aadat hai: har gas ya vapour ke liye ye kaam karta hai. Naye naam se ise **relative density** bhi kehte hain, jab doosri gas hydrogen ho.)

Hydrogen ki apni VD? Khud ko khud se tolo to ek barabar ek: **H₂ ki VD = 1**.

```
          Hydrogen ka 1 litre                 Gas X ka 1 litre
          (T aur P wahi)                      (T aur P wahi)

          +-----------+                       +-----------+
          :           :                       :           :
          :   1 L     :                       :   1 L     :
          :   H₂      :                       :   gas X   :
          :           :                       :           :
          +-----------+                       +-----------+
          vazan = w₁                          vazan = w₂

                 VD (gas X) = w₂ ÷ w₁
```

> **Figure 2.1** – VD ka matlab: wahi volume, wahi T aur P, ek mein hydrogen aur ek mein gas X. Dono ka vazan tol kar ratio le lo. Ye ratio hi gas X ki VD hai.

### Example 1: Flask ko tol kar VD

**Sawaal:** Ek hi flask ko ek baar hydrogen se aur ek baar anjaan gas X se bhara gaya (dono baar wahi T aur P, kagaz par socho). Gas ka vazan hydrogen se **0.5 g** aur X se **8.5 g** nikla. X ki VD, aur uska molecular mass kya hai? (Aage Part B ka formula use hoga.)

**Hal:**
- VD = 8.5 ÷ 0.5 = **17**. (X ka ek litre hydrogen ke ek litre se 17 guna bhaari hai.)
- Part B ka formula M = 2 × VD se M = 2 × 17 = **34 g per mol**.
- **Jaanch:** M = 34 hai, H₂S ka M = 2 × 1 + 32 = 34. Gas H₂S ho sakti hai (zehreeli, sadi hui anday jaisi badboo, sirf padhne ke liye). 17 × 0.5 = 8.5 g ✅

## Part B: VD = M ÷ 2: Avogadro se seedha nateeja

Ab wo jaadu jo Avogadro ka niyam karta hai. Maan lo ek hi volume mein, ek hi T aur P par, **N molecules** hain. Avogadro ke niyam se hydrogen ke volume mein bhi N molecules hain aur gas X ke volume mein bhi **N molecules**.

- Hydrogen ke volume ka vazan = N × (ek H₂ molecule ka vazan).
- Gas X ke volume ka vazan = N × (ek X molecule ka vazan).

Ratio lene par **N kat jaata hai**:

VD = (N × ek X molecule ka vazan) ÷ (N × ek H₂ molecule ka vazan) = (ek X molecule ka vazan) ÷ (ek H₂ molecule ka vazan)

Ab molecule ka vazan (u mein) = molecular mass M hota hai (Class 9, [atoms-and-molecules-part4.md](../../../class9/chemistry/atoms-and-molecules/atoms-and-molecules-part4.md)). H₂ ka M = 2 × 1 = 2. Isliye:

**VD = M(gas) ÷ M(H₂) = M ÷ 2**, yaani **M = 2 × VD**

Yaani VD, molecular mass ka **aadha** hota hai. Ye "2" kahan se aaya? **Hydrogen ka molecule H₂ hai** (do atoms), uska M = 2, 1 nahi. Ye wahi diatomic baat hai jo Topic 1 mein Avogadro ne kahi thi.

```
   Box 1: hydrogen                       Box 2: koi gas (yahan CO₂)
   (volume, T, P barabar)                (volume, T, P barabar)

   +------------------------+            +------------------------+
   : H-H      H-H           :            : O=C=O    O=C=O         :
   :                        :            :                        :
   : H-H   H-H              :            : O=C=O  O=C=O           :
   :        H-H             :            :       O=C=O            :
   :                        :            :                        :
   : H-H                    :            : O=C=O                  :
   +------------------------+            +------------------------+
   6 molecules                           6 molecules
   har ek ka M = 2                       har ek ka M = 44
   box ka vazan: 6 x 2 = 12 u            box ka vazan: 6 x 44 = 264 u
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Barabar volume ke do box, wahi T aur P: dono mein 6-6 molecules (Avogadro ka niyam). Box ka vazan ek molecule ke vazan par tika hai. 264 ÷ 12 = 22, yaani CO₂ ki VD = 22 = 44 ÷ 2.

**Class 9 se doosra raasta (jaanch ke liye):** STP par density = M ÷ 22.4 g per L. Hydrogen ki density = 2 ÷ 22.4, gas ki = M ÷ 22.4. Ratio = (M ÷ 22.4) ÷ (2 ÷ 22.4) = **M ÷ 2** ✅. Dono raaste ek hi jagah pahunchte hain.

### Example 2: VD se M, par naam nahi

**Sawaal:** Ek gas ki VD 23 hai. Uska molecular mass kya hai? Kya hum pakka keh sakte hain ki wo kaun si gas hai?

**Hal:**
- M = 2 × VD = 2 × 23 = **46 g per mol**.
- NO₂ ka M = 14 + 2 × 16 = 46. Ye ek laal-bhoori, zehreeli gas hai (sirf padhne ke liye).
- Par ethanol ke vapour (C₂H₅OH) ka M = 2 × 12 + 6 × 1 + 16 = 46 bhi hai. Dono ki VD 23 hai!
- **Seekh:** VD sirf **M** batata hai, gas ka **naam nahi**. Naam tay karne ke liye rang, gandh, ya formula ka aur saboot chahiye (Topic 3 mein hum formula ki taraf badhenge).
- **Jaanch:** 46 ÷ 2 = 23 ✅

## Part C: Aam gaason ki VD

VD nikalna bas M ko do se baant dena hai. Kuch workings: CH₄: M = 12 + 4 = 16, VD = 16 ÷ 2 = **8**. HCl: M = 1 + 35.5 = 36.5, VD = 36.5 ÷ 2 = **18.25**. Cl₂: M = 2 × 35.5 = 71, VD = 71 ÷ 2 = **35.5**. (Cl₂ zehreeli hai, sirf padhne ke liye.)

| Gas | Formula | M (g per mol) | VD = M ÷ 2 | Hawa se (VD 14.5) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hydrogen | H₂ | 2 | 1 | Bahut halki |
| Helium | He | 4 | 2 | Bahut halki |
| Methane | CH₄ | 16 | 8 | Halki |
| Ammonia | NH₃ | 17 | 8.5 | Halki |
| Nitrogen | N₂ | 28 | 14 | Lagbhag barabar, zara halki |
| Oxygen | O₂ | 32 | 16 | Zara bhaari |
| Hydrogen chloride | HCl | 36.5 | 18.25 | Bhaari |
| Carbon dioxide | CO₂ | 44 | 22 | Bhaari |
| Sulphur dioxide | SO₂ | 64 | 32 | Bahut bhaari |
| Chlorine | Cl₂ | 71 | 35.5 | Bahut bhaari |

Helium **monoatomic** hai (ek atom ka molecule), isliye uska M = 4 hi hai, 8 nahi (Topic 1 ki atomicity). Aur dhyaan do: sab VD **hydrogen ke 1** se naape gaye hain, isliye H₂ upar ki list mein sabse chhoti hai.

```
   VD ka paimana (har # lagbhag 1 VD)     = hawa ka nishaan

   H₂            # 1
   He            ## 2
   CH₄           ######## 8
   NH₃           ######### 8.5
   N₂            ############## 14
   hawa (ausat)  =============== 14.5
   O₂            ################ 16
   HCl           ################## 18.25
   CO₂           ###################### 22
   SO₂           ################################ 32
   Cl₂           #################################### 35.5
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Common gaason ki VD ek paimane par. Hawa ke nishaan (14.5) se kam wale gas halki, usse zyada wale gas bhaari. Bar ki lambai VD ke barabar hai (rounded).

## Part D: Hawa kitni bhaari hai? Hawa se muqabla

Hawa koi ek gas nahi, **mixture** hai: lagbhag **78 percent nitrogen, 21 percent oxygen aur lagbhag 1 percent argon** (volume mein). Kyunki **barabar volume = barabar molecules (Avogadro)**, volume ka percent ab **molecules ka percent** (mole ka percent) bhi hai. Isliye hawa ka ausat M nikaalna aasan hai: har gas ke M ko uske percent se guna karo aur jod do.

### Example 3: Hawa ka ausat M aur VD

**Sawaal:** 100 molecules ke ek namune mein 78 N₂, 21 O₂, 1 Ar (M = 40) hain. Hawa ka ausat M aur VD nikalo.

**Hal:**
- Kul vazan (u mein) = 78 × 28 + 21 × 32 + 1 × 40 = 2184 + 672 + 40 = **2896 u**.
- Ausat M = 2896 ÷ 100 = **28.96**, yaani **lagbhag 29**.
- VD = 28.96 ÷ 2 = 14.48, yaani **lagbhag 14.5**.
- **Jaanch:** 78 + 21 + 1 = 100 molecules ✅ aur 29 ÷ 2 = 14.5 ✅

(Asli hawa mein carbon dioxide, paani ki bhaap jaise bahut kam matra wale gas bhi hain. Unke saath bhi answer lagbhag 29 hi rehta hai.)

**Niyam (hawa mein upar ya neeche):** kisi gas ki VD 14.5 se **kam** ya M 29 se **kam** hai to wo **hawa se halki** hai aur hawa mein **upar uthegi**; VD 14.5 se **zyada** (M 29 se zyada) hai to wo **hawa se bhaari** hai aur **neeche baithegi**. (VD ko 14.5 se aur M ko 29 se milana hai, **dono ko dono se nahi**: Aam galtiyan mein dekhenge.)

### Example 4: Hawa se kitni guna bhaari ya halki?

**Sawaal:** Butane (C₄H₁₀, jo LPG mein hota hai) aur methane (CH₄, jo natural gas mein hota hai): dono hawa se kitni guna bhaari ya halki hain? (Hawa ka M = 29.)

**Hal:**
- Butane: M = 4 × 12 + 10 = 58. Hawa se 58 ÷ 29 = **2 guna bhaari**.
- Methane: M = 16. 16 ÷ 29 = 0.55, yaani hawa se lagbhag **aadhi (0.55 guna)**, halki.
- **Jaanch (VD se):** butane ki VD = 58 ÷ 2 = 29, 29 ÷ 14.5 = 2 ✅. Methane ki VD 8 ÷ 14.5 = 0.55 ✅.

## Part E: VD ke asli kaam

### 1. Gubbare: upar kyun, neeche kyun

Ab Ranveer ke gubbaron ka raaz. Cylinder ka gas **helium (VD 2)** hawa (VD 14.5) se bahut halki hai, isliye uske gubbare ko hawa **upar dhakel** deti hai. Pump se bhari gayi gas **hawa hi** hai, jiski VD 14.5 hai, wahi bahar ki hawa ki bhi hai: koi uthaav nahi, upar se gubbare ki rubber aur dhaaga bhaari, isliye ye stall ke bans se **neeche jhoolte** hain.

Hydrogen bhi bahut halki hai (VD 1), par wo **aag pakadne wali** gas hai, isliye mela aur party ke gubbaron mein **helium** hi bharte hain, wo bhi sikhaaye hue log. (Garm hawa wale bade balloon ek alag kahani hain: wahan **wahi hawa garam kar ke halki** ki jaati hai, kisi aur gas ki VD ka mamla nahi.)

### Example 5: Helium aur hydrogen mein se kaun zyada uthaata hai?

**Sawaal:** Barabar volume (kagaz par, STP jaisi halat) ke gubbare mein helium ya hydrogen bharne par hawa dhakelti hai. Helium kitna prati-shat uthaav deta hai (hydrogen ke muqable)? Aur 4 g vazan ke rubber aur dhaage wale gubbare ko tairane ke liye kam se kam kitne litre helium chahiye?

**Hal:**
- 1 mole (22.4 L) hawa ka vazan 29 g. Helium ka 4 g, to uthaav = 29 − 4 = **25 g per 22.4 L**. Hydrogen se 29 − 2 = **27 g per 22.4 L**.
- Helium ka uthaav ÷ hydrogen ka = 25 ÷ 27 = 0.926, yaani **lagbhag 93 percent**. Helium bina aag ke hydrogen ka lagbhag 93 percent kaam kar deta hai.
- Helium ka uthaav per litre = 25 ÷ 22.4 = **1.12 g per L** (lagbhag).
- 4 g ke liye volume = 4 ÷ 1.116 = **3.58 L**, yaani kam se kam **lagbhag 3.6 L** helium.
- **Jaanch:** 3.58 × 1.116 = 4.0 g ✅

(Asli mela garam hota hai, STP nahi, isliye asli numbers thode alag honge; kagaz ka hisaab idea samjhane ke liye hai.)

### 2. Carbon dioxide wala fire extinguisher

CO₂ ki VD = 22, hawa se **lagbhag 1.5 guna** bhaari. Ek CO₂ type fire extinguisher se nikli CO₂ jalte hue saman par **neeche baith kar chaadar (blanket)** ki tarah faili hai aur hawa ki oxygen ko jalti cheez tak aane se rokti hai. Isse aag bujh jaati hai. (Extinguisher chalana sikhaaye gaye bade ka kaam hai: aag lage to bade ko batao, bahar niklo aur fire brigade ko bulao.)

### 3. Gas leak: natural gas upar, LPG neeche (sirf describe)

**Natural gas** mein mukhya taur par **methane (VD 8)** hota hai: hawa se halki, to leak hone par **upar** (chhat ke paas) jama hoti hai. Ghar ki **LPG** mein propane aur butane (M lagbhag 44 se 58, yaani VD lagbhag 22 se 29) hote hain: hawa se **bhaari**, to leak hone par **neeche** (farsh ke paas, naali mein) jama hoti hai. Isiliye gas-leak alarm aam taur par natural gas ke liye **upar** aur LPG ke liye **neeche** lagaye jaate hain.

```
     NATURAL GAS (CH₄, VD 8)             LPG (propane-butane, VD 22 se 29)

   +----------------------------+     +----------------------------+
   :  [alarm]  ^  ^  ^  ^  ^    :     :                            :
   :           ^  ^  ^  ^       :     :                            :
   :              ^  ^  ^       :     :                            :
   :              ^  ^          :     :                            :
   :            [chulha]        :     :            [chulha]        :
   :                            :     :           v  v  v  v       :
   :                            :     :        v  v  v  v  v  v    :
   :                            :     :  [alarm]  v  v  v  v  v    :
   +----------------------------+     +----------------------------+
       halki: upar jama hoti hai           bhaari: neeche jama hoti hai
```

> **Figure 2.4** – Gas leak ka naksha (sirf describe): methane (halki) upar jama hoti hai, isliye alarm upar; LPG (bhaari) neeche jama hoti hai, isliye alarm neeche. Dono chulhe ek hi unchai par hain; gas wahin se phailti hai.

**Safety line:** agar ghar mein gas ki gandh aaye, to **khidkiyan-darwaze kholo, koi switch mat dabao, regulator band karo aur gas agency ko phone karo**. Ye kaam bade karte hain; bachche ka kaam ek hi hai, turant bade ko batana. Cooking gas ki poori kahani Topic 5 mein aayegi.

## Part F: VD se formula ki taraf: pehla kadam

VD se M mil jaata hai. Agar hum ye bhi jaante hain ki gas mein **kaun se atoms** hain, to M se bata sakte hain ki **kitne atoms** hain.

### Example 6: Sulphur ka oxide, SOₓ

**Sawaal:** Sulphur aur oxygen ka ek gas SOₓ hai (x = oxygen atoms ki sankhya), uski VD 32 hai. x kitna hai? (S = 32, O = 16.)

**Hal:**
- M = 2 × 32 = **64**.
- 32 + 16x = 64, to 16x = 32, **x = 2**. Gas **SO₂** hai (jali hui matchstick jaisi chubhne wali gandh, zehreeli, sirf padhne ke liye).
- **Jaanch:** SO₂ ka M = 32 + 2 × 16 = 64, 64 ÷ 2 = 32 ✅

Ye "guess-and-check" tareeka chhote formulas par chalta hai. **Topic 3** mein hum iska poora tareeka seekhenge: pehle **empirical formula** (atoms ka sabse chhota ratio) percentage se nikalna, phir VD ya M se **molecular formula** tak jaana.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "VD aur M ek hi cheez hai."**
Maano kisi ne kaha ki methane ki VD 16 hai. Check karte hain: STP par 22.4 L hydrogen 2 g ka hota hai aur 22.4 L methane 16 g ka. Ratio 16 ÷ 2 = **8**, 16 nahi. Methane ka ek litre hydrogen ke ek litre se **8 guna** bhaari hai. VD **M ka aadha** hai, kyunki hum H₂ (M = 2) se tol rahe hain, H (1) se nahi.

**Galti 2: "VD ko hawa ke M (29) se milana."**
Ek dost ne kaha: "CO₂ ki VD 22 hai aur hawa ka M 29, 22 < 29, to CO₂ hawa se halki." Galat! 22 ek **VD** hai (hydrogen ke 1 ke hisaab se), 29 ek **M** hai. Do alag paimane. **VD ko VD se milao:** 22 > 14.5 (hawa ki VD), to CO₂ bhaari. Ya **M ko M se:** 44 > 29, bhaari. Dono tarah ek hi jawab: CO₂ hawa se bhaari.

**Galti 3: "Vazan kisi bhi T aur P par tol lo."**
Maano H₂ ko 273 K par 22.4 L ke flask mein tola: 1 mol = **2 g**. CO₂ ko usi 22.4 L ke flask mein **546 K** par tola, dabao wahi: ab garm hone se gas phail kar aadhi hi rah jaati hai, 0.5 mol, vazan 0.5 × 44 = **22 g**. Ratio 22 ÷ 2 = **11**, jabki asli VD **22** hai. Galat jawab isliye aaya ki **do alag taapman** par tole gaye. VD ke liye **dono gas ek hi T aur P par** honi chahiye (Topic 1 ka Avogadro ka niyam bhi isi shart par chalta hai).

## Quick Recap

- **Vapour density (VD)** = gas ke ek volume ka vazan ÷ hydrogen ke utne hi volume ka vazan (wahi T aur P). Ye bina unit wala number hai.
- Avogadro ke niyam se: **VD = M ÷ 2**, **M = 2 × VD** (kyunki H₂ ka M = 2).
- VD sirf M batati hai, gas ka naam nahi (NO₂ aur ethanol vapour dono VD 23).
- Hawa ka ausat M lagbhag **29**, VD lagbhag **14.5**: kam VD (ya M) wali gas **upar** uthti hai, zyada wali **neeche** baithti hai.
- Halki: H₂, He, CH₄, NH₃, N₂ (zara halki). Bhaari: O₂ (zara), HCl, CO₂, SO₂, Cl₂.
- Kaam: gubbare (He, hydrogen se lagbhag 93 percent uthaav), CO₂ ka fire extinguisher, gas leak (methane upar, LPG neeche), aur M se formula ki taraf.
- VD nikalte waqt **dono gas ek hi T aur P** par hon.

## Practice Questions

Saare gas wale sawaal **sirf kagaz par** hain. Ghar par koi gas banaane ya jalane ki koshish kabhi nahi.

1. Find the vapour density of (a) ethyne, C₂H₂, (b) neon, Ne (M = 20) and (c) ethane, C₂H₆. Which of these are lighter than air and which is heavier? (Air has VD about 14.5.)
2. A gas has a vapour density of 24. Find its molecular mass. If the gas is made only of oxygen atoms (O = 16), how many atoms are there in one molecule? What is its formula?
3. At the same temperature and pressure, a flask holds 0.1 g of hydrogen. The same flask holds 1.9 g of an unknown gas. Find the VD and the molecular mass of the gas. If the gas is made only of fluorine atoms (F = 19), find the formula. (Fluorine is poisonous: paper work only.)
4. A gas mixture contains 9 mol of nitrogen and 1 mol of helium. Find its average molar mass and its VD. Is the mixture lighter or heavier than air?
5. A gas has the formula CₓH₄ and a VD of 20. Find x and write the formula. (C = 12, H = 1.)
6. A large decoration balloon holds 44.8 L of helium (take STP-like conditions on paper). The rubber and thread together weigh 36 g. Find the mass of air displaced, the mass of helium, and the net lift. Will the balloon rise?
7. How many times heavier or lighter than air (M = 29) are (a) CO₂, (b) SO₂, (c) NH₃?
8. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) The VD of oxygen (O₂) is 32. (b) A gas with VD 10 rises in air. (c) Two gases with the same VD have the same molecular mass. (d) The VD of hydrogen (H₂) is 2. (e) You can find a gas's VD by weighing equal volumes at different temperatures.
9. Puzzle: four balloons A, B, C and D are filled one each with the gases helium, methane, carbon dioxide and chlorine, at the same T and P. The following facts are known. Air has VD about 14.5. Balloons A and B rise, balloons C and D sink. The gas in B is 4 times as heavy as the gas in A for equal volumes. The gas in C is lighter than the gas in D for equal volumes. Identify the gas in each balloon, with the VD of each. (Chlorine is poisonous: paper work only.)
10. Natural gas (mostly methane) alarms are usually placed high on the wall, while LPG alarms are placed low. Using VD values, explain why. (Describe only: never test with a real gas.)
11. Explain in three or four lines, using Avogadro's law, why VD = M ÷ 2 and not M.
12. At the same T and P, a flask holds 2 g of hydrogen. Find the mass of nitrous oxide, N₂O, in the same flask. (N = 14, O = 16.)
13. A balloon-seller fills one balloon from a pump (air) and one with helium. Using VD, explain why the pump balloon does not rise even if its rubber is imagined to have no weight.

### Answers

1. (a) C₂H₂: M = 2 × 12 + 2 = 26, VD = 26 ÷ 2 = **13**. (b) Ne: M = 20, VD = **10** (neon ek atom ka monoatomic gas hai). (c) C₂H₆: M = 24 + 6 = 30, VD = **15**. Hawa ki VD 14.5: ethyne (13) aur neon (10) **halki**; ethane (15) **thodi si bhaari** (30 > 29 bas). Jaanch: 26 ÷ 29 = 0.90, 20 ÷ 29 = 0.69, 30 ÷ 29 = 1.03 ✅
2. M = 2 × 24 = **48**. 48 ÷ 16 = **3 atoms**, formula **O₃** (ozone). Jaanch: 3 × 16 = 48, 48 ÷ 2 = 24 ✅
3. VD = 1.9 ÷ 0.1 = **19**. M = 2 × 19 = **38**. 38 ÷ 19 = 2 atoms, formula **F₂**. Jaanch: 2 × 19 = 38 ✅
4. Ausat M = (9 × 28 + 1 × 4) ÷ 10 = (252 + 4) ÷ 10 = **25.6**. VD = 25.6 ÷ 2 = **12.8**. 12.8 < 14.5, to mixture hawa se **halka** hai (par bahut kam: sirf 3.4 g per mole ka fark). Jaanch: 9 + 1 = 10 mol ✅
5. M = 2 × 20 = 40. 12x + 4 = 40, 12x = 36, **x = 3**. Formula **C₃H₄**. Jaanch: 3 × 12 + 4 = 40, 40 ÷ 2 = 20 ✅
6. 44.8 L = 44.8 ÷ 22.4 = 2 mol. Hawa ka vazan = 2 × 29 = **58 g**. Helium = 2 × 4 = **8 g**. Uthaav = 58 − 8 = **50 g**. Rubber aur dhaaga 36 g, to net lift = 50 − 36 = **14 g upar**. Gubbara **uthega**. Jaanch: 44.8 × 25 ÷ 22.4 = 50 g ✅
7. (a) CO₂: 44 ÷ 29 = **1.52 guna bhaari**. (b) SO₂: 64 ÷ 29 = **2.21 guna bhaari**. (c) NH₃: 17 ÷ 29 = **0.59 guna**, yaani halki. Jaanch (VD se): 22 ÷ 14.5 = 1.52 ✅, 32 ÷ 14.5 = 2.21 ✅, 8.5 ÷ 14.5 = 0.59 ✅
8. (a) **False** ❌: O₂ ki VD 16 hai (M = 32, aadha). (b) **True** ✅: 10 < 14.5. (c) **True** ✅: VD = M ÷ 2, to barabar VD matlab barabar M (par naam alag ho sakte hain, jaise NO₂ aur ethanol vapour). (d) **False** ❌: H₂ ki VD 1 hai (khud ko khud se tolo to 1). (e) **False** ❌: dono gas ek hi T aur P par honi chahiye, warna molecules ki ginti badal jaati hai.
9. Hawa ki VD 14.5 se kam wale do: helium (2) aur methane (8): A aur B. Bhaari do: CO₂ (22) aur Cl₂ (35.5): C aur D. B ki gas A se 4 guna bhaari: 8 = 4 × 2, to **A = helium (VD 2)**, **B = methane (VD 8)** (ulta hota to A = 8, B = 2, 2 ≠ 4 × 8). C halki, D bhaari: **C = CO₂ (VD 22)**, **D = Cl₂ (VD 35.5)**. Jaanch: 8 ÷ 2 = 4 ✅, 22 < 35.5 ✅
10. Methane ki VD 8 (< 14.5): hawa se halki, leak hone par upar jama hoti hai, isliye natural gas ka alarm **upar**. LPG (propane-butane) ki VD lagbhag 22 se 29 (> 14.5): hawa se bhaari, neeche jama hoti hai, isliye LPG ka alarm **neeche**. Gas aakhirkar hawa mein mil bhi jaati hai, isliye ye "aam taur par" wali baat hai.
11. Barabar volume, wahi T aur P par, Avogadro ke niyam se barabar molecules (N) hote hain. Gas ke volume ka vazan N × M(gas) aur hydrogen ka N × M(H₂) hai, ratio mein N kat jaata hai: VD = M(gas) ÷ M(H₂). Hydrogen ek **diatomic** molecule H₂ hai, to M(H₂) = 2, isliye VD = M ÷ 2.
12. 2 g H₂ = 1 mol. Same flask, T aur P par N₂O bhi **1 mol**. N₂O ka M = 2 × 14 + 16 = 44, to mass = **44 g**. Jaanch (VD se): VD = 44 ÷ 2 = 22, 22 × 2 g = 44 g ✅
13. Pump ki hawa ki VD 14.5 hai, jo bahar ki hawa ki bhi hai: gubbare ke andar ki gas aur dhakelne wali hawa ke vazan mein koi fark nahi, uthaav = 0. Helium ki VD 2 hai, hawa se bahut halki, to uthaav hota hai. Pump ke gubbare mein rubber ke vazan se **neeche** hi jhoolta hai.
