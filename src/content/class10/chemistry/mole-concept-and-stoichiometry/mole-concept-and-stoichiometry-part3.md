# Topic 3: Empirical aur Molecular Formula

Topic 2 mein humne seekha ki vapour density se gas ka **molecular mass** nikalta hai (M = 2 × VD). Par ek sawaal baaki reh gaya: agar lab mein koi anjaan gas ya liquid mile, to uska **formula** kaise pata chalega? Molecular mass to sirf ek sankhya hai. Formula tab milta hai jab hum ye bhi jaanein ki usmein **kaun se tatva** hain aur **kis ratio** mein. Is topic mein hum do formula seekhte hain: ek **sabse chhota ratio** batata hai (empirical), doosra **asli ginti** batata hai (molecular). Aur ek 5-step method seekhte hain jo percentage se formula tak le jaata hai. Class 9 mein hamne ulta kiya tha: formula se percentage nikaala tha ([atoms-and-molecules-part7.md](../../../class9/chemistry/atoms-and-molecules/atoms-and-molecules-part7.md)). Ab percentage se formula tak ulta safar.

## Tile-fitter ka chhota sa nakshaa

Mr. Bhagwat ke ghar ke drawing room ki nayi flooring ho rahi thi. Unki bhanji Manasi chhutti mein aayi hui thi, aur wo tile-fitter ka kaam dekh rahi thi. Har badi tile par ek chhota sa nakshaa chhapa tha: neela chhota diamond aur uske saath do safed chhote bindu. Tile-fitter ne bataya ki ek badi tile par ye chhota nakshaa kai baar dohraya gaya hai.

Manasi ne tile ginni shuru ki: "Mama, is badi tile par 3 neele diamond aur 6 safed bindu hain. Par dukaan wale ne kaha ki nakshe ka design '1 neela, 2 safed' hai. To asli design kya hai: '3 neele aur 6 safed', ya '1 neela aur 2 safed'?"

Mr. Bhagwat muskuraye. "Dono sahi hain, bas alag sawaal ke jawab hain. Sabse chhota **dohrane wala hissa** '1 neela, 2 safed' hai: usse pata chalta hai ki **ratio** kya hai. Par tumhare haath mein jo tile hai, usmein **asli ginti** '3 neele, 6 safed' hai. Ek tile par wo chhota hissa 3 baar aaya hai." Phir unhone ek line joda: "Chemistry ke formulae mein bhi bilkul yahi hota hai. Ek formula sabse chhota ratio batata hai, doosra asli ginti. Chalo, dono ko naam dete hain."

```
   Chhota nakshaa (1 hissa)           Poori badi tile (3 hisse)

        B  w  w                        B  w  w    B  w  w    B  w  w

   B = neela diamond, w = safed bindu
   ratio B : w = 1 : 2                B : w = 3 : 6 = 1 : 2
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Ek chhota nakshaa (1 B, 2 w) aur poori tile (3 B, 6 w). Dono ka ratio B : w = 1 : 2 hi hai, par ginti alag hai. Sabse chhota ratio empirical formula jaisa hai, asli ginti molecular formula jaisi.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Empirical formula** | Kisi yaugik mein atoms ka sabse chhota poora-ankon (whole number) wala ratio | Glucose ka CH₂O |
| **Molecular formula** | Ek molecule mein har tatva ke atoms ki asli ginti | Glucose ka C₆H₁₂O₆ |
| **Empirical formula mass** | Empirical formula ke atomic masses ka jod | CH₂O ka 12 + 2 + 16 = 30 |
| **n (multiplier)** | Wo poori sankhya jisse empirical formula ko guna karke molecular formula milta hai | n = 6 glucose ke liye |
| **Whole number ratio** | Aisa ratio jismein har sankhya poori ho, bhinn (fraction) nahi | 2 : 3, na ki 1 : 1.5 |
| **Formula unit** | Ionic yaugik ka sabse chhota ratio wala hissa (ionic yaugik ke molecule nahi hote) | NaCl, Na₂SO₄ |

## Part A: Do formula, ek hi yaugik

**Empirical formula** (empirical = sabse chhota ratio) batata hai ki atoms ka **sabse chhota poora-ankon wala ratio** kya hai. **Molecular formula** batata hai ki **ek molecule mein asli mein kitne atoms** hain.

**Molecular formula = (empirical formula) × n**, jahan **n = molecular mass ÷ empirical formula mass**.

Kuch jode dekhte hain:

| Yaugik | Molecular formula | Empirical formula | n |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ethane | C₂H₆ | CH₃ | 2 |
| Benzene | C₆H₆ | CH | 6 |
| Hydrogen peroxide | H₂O₂ | HO | 2 |
| Glucose | C₆H₁₂O₆ | CH₂O | 6 |
| Acetic acid (sirke ka khatta tatva) | C₂H₄O₂ | CH₂O | 2 |
| Paani | H₂O | H₂O | 1 |

Dhyan do: glucose aur acetic acid ka **empirical formula ek hi** (CH₂O) hai, par ye **do bilkul alag padarth** hain. Isiliye sirf empirical formula se yaugik ki pehchaan nahi hoti; molecular mass bhi chahiye. (Benzene aur hydrogen peroxide sirf padhne ke liye hain: benzene zehreeli hai, aur gaadha hydrogen peroxide tez rasayan hai. Sirf dawaakhane ka halka H₂O₂ ghar mein kabhi-kabhi milta hai, wo bhi bade ke saath hi use hota hai.)

```
   HO  (empirical)                    H₂O₂  (molecular)

     H - O                              H - O - O - H

   1 H aur 1 O ka ratio              2 H aur 2 O: asli molecule
   (1 hissa)                          (HO ka 2 guna)
```

> **Figure 3.2** – H₂O₂ ke molecule mein 2 hydrogen aur 2 oxygen hain (kul 4 atoms). Sabse chhota ratio 1 : 1 hai, isliye empirical formula HO hai. Asli molecule HO ke 2 hisson jaisa hai.

**Kabhi empirical aur molecular formula ek hi hote hain:** jab ratio pehle se hi sabse chhota ho, jaise H₂O (2 : 1), CO₂ (1 : 2), NH₃ (1 : 3). Wahan n = 1.

## Part B: Percentage se empirical formula: 5 steps

Maano ek lab report mein likha hai: "yaugik mein carbon 80% aur hydrogen 20%". Formula kaise nikalein? Yaad rakho: percentage **mass** ki baat karta hai, aur formula **atoms ki ginti** ki. Mass ko ginti mein badalne ke liye **atomic mass se bhaag** dena padta hai (Class 9 ka mole wala idea: moles = mass ÷ atomic mass).

```
   Step 1  Percent ko gram maano (100 g yaugik mein)
              :
              v
   Step 2  Har tatva ke gram ÷ atomic mass = moles
              :
              v
   Step 3  Sabse chhote mole se sabko bhaag do
              :
              v
   Step 4  Agar ratio poora-ankon mein nahi, to 2 ya 3 se guna karo
              :
              v
   Step 5  Subscript likho: empirical formula
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Empirical formula ke 5 step. Step 1 ke liye hum 100 g ka sample sochte hain, kyunki 100 g mein "percent" aur "gram" ek hi sankhya hote hain.

### Example 1. Ek hydrocarbon: C 80%, H 20%

- Step 1: 100 g mein C = 80 g, H = 20 g.
- Step 2: C = 80 ÷ 12 = 6.67 mol. H = 20 ÷ 1 = 20 mol.
- Step 3: sabse chhota 6.67. C = 6.67 ÷ 6.67 = 1. H = 20 ÷ 6.67 = 3.
- Step 4: dono poori sankhya hain, guna ki zaroorat nahi.
- Step 5: **empirical formula = CH₃**.

**Jaanch:** CH₃ mein C ka percent = 12 ÷ 15 × 100 = 80% aur H ka 3 ÷ 15 × 100 = 20% ✅.

Ab ye yaugik ek gas hai jiska vapour density 15 hai (Topic 2). To M = 2 × 15 = **30**. Empirical formula mass CH₃ = 12 + 3 = 15. n = 30 ÷ 15 = **2**. Molecular formula = CH₃ × 2 = **C₂H₆** (ethane).
**Jaanch:** C₂H₆ ka mass = 24 + 6 = 30 ✅ aur 30 ÷ 2 = VD 15 ✅. (Ethane jalne wali gas hai: sirf padhne ke liye.)

### Example 2. Ek aur hydrocarbon: C 92.3%, H 7.7%

- Step 2: C = 92.3 ÷ 12 = 7.69 mol. H = 7.7 ÷ 1 = 7.7 mol.
- Step 3: sabse chhota 7.69. C = 1. H = 7.7 ÷ 7.69 = 1.00 (lagbhag 1).
- Step 5: **empirical formula = CH**.

Agar iska molecular mass 78 ho: CH ka mass = 13. n = 78 ÷ 13 = **6**. Molecular formula **C₆H₆** (benzene).
**Jaanch:** C₆H₆ = 72 + 6 = 78 ✅. C ka percent = 72 ÷ 78 × 100 = 92.3% ✅.

### Example 3. Whole-number trick: Fe 70%, O 30%

- Step 2: Fe = 70 ÷ 56 = 1.25 mol. O = 30 ÷ 16 = 1.875 mol.
- Step 3: sabse chhota 1.25. Fe = 1. O = 1.875 ÷ 1.25 = **1.5**.
- Step 4: 1 : 1.5 poori sankhya nahi. Dono ko **2** se guna karo: 2 : 3.
- Step 5: **Fe₂O₃**.

**Jaanch:** Fe₂O₃ ka mass = 2 × 56 + 3 × 16 = 160. Fe ka percent = 112 ÷ 160 × 100 = 70% ✅.

Ye yaugik ionic hai (dhatu + oxygen), isliye **Fe₂O₃** ek **formula unit** hai: ye hi uska sabse chhota ratio hai aur isme "molecular formula" ka alag matlab nahi hota (Part D).

### Example 4. Ek aur trick: Fe 72.4%, O 27.6%

- Step 2: Fe = 72.4 ÷ 56 = 1.293 mol. O = 27.6 ÷ 16 = 1.725 mol.
- Step 3: Fe = 1. O = 1.725 ÷ 1.293 = **1.33**.
- Step 4: 1.33 = 4/3. **3** se guna karo: Fe = 3, O = 4.
- Step 5: **Fe₃O₄**.

**Jaanch:** Fe₃O₄ = 168 + 64 = 232. Fe ka percent = 168 ÷ 232 × 100 = 72.4% ✅.

**Kis decimal par kaun sa guna?** Ratio mein jo bhinn dikhe: 0.5 ya 1.5 ho to × 2; 0.33 ya 1.33 (ya 0.67) ho to × 3; 0.25 ya 1.25 ho to × 4. Ye teeno yaad rakho.

### Example 5. Teen tatva: Na 32.4%, S 22.5%, O 45.1%

- Step 2: Na = 32.4 ÷ 23 = 1.409. S = 22.5 ÷ 32 = 0.703. O = 45.1 ÷ 16 = 2.819.
- Step 3: sabse chhota 0.703. Na = 2.00, S = 1, O = 4.01 (lagbhag 4).
- Step 5: **Na₂SO₄**.

**Jaanch:** Na₂SO₄ = 46 + 32 + 64 = 142. Na = 46 ÷ 142 × 100 = 32.4% ✅, S = 32 ÷ 142 × 100 = 22.5% ✅, O = 64 ÷ 142 × 100 = 45.1% ✅.

### Example 6. Sidhe gram diye ho (Step 1 chhoot jaata hai)

**Sawaal:** Sulphur ke 1.6 g jalne par 2.4 g oxygen se mil kar ek oxide banta hai. Empirical formula? (Ye gas zehreeli hai: sirf padhne ke liye.)

- S = 1.6 ÷ 32 = 0.05 mol. O = 2.4 ÷ 16 = 0.15 mol.
- Sabse chhota 0.05: S = 1, O = 3.
- **Empirical formula = SO₃**.

**Jaanch:** SO₃ = 32 + 48 = 80. S = 32 ÷ 80 × 100 = 40%; diya hua 1.6 ÷ (1.6 + 2.4) × 100 = 40% ✅.

## Part C: Molecular formula: empirical formula, phir n

Jab empirical formula mil jaaye, to molecular formula ke liye ek aur jaankari chahiye: **molecular mass**. Ye ya seedha diya hota hai, ya gas ho to **vapour density** se milta hai (M = 2 × VD, Topic 2).

1. Empirical formula ka mass nikalo.
2. n = molecular mass ÷ empirical formula mass (hamesha poori sankhya aani chahiye).
3. Empirical formula ke har subscript ko n se guna karo.

### Example 7. Ek hi CH₂O, do alag padarth

- **Glucose:** M = 180. CH₂O ka mass = 12 + 2 + 16 = 30. n = 180 ÷ 30 = **6**. Formula **C₆H₁₂O₆**.
- **Acetic acid:** M = 60. n = 60 ÷ 30 = **2**. Formula **C₂H₄O₂**.

**Jaanch:** C₆H₁₂O₆ = 72 + 12 + 96 = 180 ✅. C₂H₄O₂ = 24 + 4 + 32 = 60 ✅.

Dono ka empirical formula ek, par molecular mass alag. Isliye n alag, aur yaugik alag. Tile ki kahani yaad karo: ek hi chhota nakshaa 3 baar ya 6 baar dohraya ja sakta hai.

### Example 8. H₂O₂ percentage se

Ek yaugik mein H = 5.88%, O = 94.12%, aur M = 34.

- H = 5.88 ÷ 1 = 5.88 mol. O = 94.12 ÷ 16 = 5.88 mol. Ratio 1 : 1. Empirical formula **HO** (mass 17).
- n = 34 ÷ 17 = **2**. Molecular formula **H₂O₂**.

**Jaanch:** H₂O₂ = 2 + 32 = 34 ✅. H ka percent = 2 ÷ 34 × 100 = 5.88% ✅.

### Example 9. Poora safar: percent + VD

**Sawaal:** Ek hydrocarbon gas mein C = 85.7%, H = 14.3%. Uska vapour density 28 hai. Molecular formula? (Sirf padhne ke liye: jalne wali gas.)

- C = 85.7 ÷ 12 = 7.14. H = 14.3 ÷ 1 = 14.3. H ÷ C = 14.3 ÷ 7.14 = 2.00. Empirical formula **CH₂** (mass 14).
- M = 2 × 28 = 56.
- n = 56 ÷ 14 = **4**. Molecular formula **C₄H₈**.

**Jaanch:** C₄H₈ = 48 + 8 = 56 ✅. C = 48 ÷ 56 × 100 = 85.7% ✅.

```
   Percent / gram        Empirical formula        Molecular formula
   (5 step)         -->  (sabse chhota ratio) --> (asli ginti)
                                                   ^
                          M ya VD (M = 2 x VD)     |
                          n = M / empirical mass --+
```

> **Figure 3.4** – Poora raasta: pehle 5 step se empirical formula, phir molecular mass (ya VD se M = 2 × VD) aur n se molecular formula.

## Part D: Jab empirical aur molecular formula ek hi hon, aur ionic yaugik

**Jab ek hi hon:** H₂O, CO₂, NH₃. Inka ratio pehle se sabse chhota hai, isliye n = 1. Jaise paani: H : O = 2 : 1, aur 1 se chhota poora-ankon ka ratio nahi ban sakta.

**Ionic yaugik sirf empirical formula rakhte hain.** NaCl mein ek hi "NaCl molecule" nahi hota. Ye Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ ions ka bahut bada jaal (lattice) hai jisme har Na⁺ ke baad ek Cl⁻ aata hai. NaCl sirf ratio 1 : 1 batata hai, isliye ise **formula unit** kehte hain. Isi liye NaCl ka "molecular mass" nahi, **formula unit mass** 58.5 kehte hain (Class 9 mein bhi yahi kaha tha).

```
   Na+ Cl- Na+ Cl- Na+ Cl-
   Cl- Na+ Cl- Na+ Cl- Na+
   Na+ Cl- Na+ Cl- Na+ Cl-
   Cl- Na+ Cl- Na+ Cl- Na+
```

> **Figure 3.5** – NaCl ke jaal ka ek chhota 2D tukda: 12 Na⁺ aur 12 Cl⁻, har ek apne padosi ulte ion ke saath. Gino to Na⁺ : Cl⁻ = 1 : 1 aata hai: yahi formula NaCl hai. Koi alag "NaCl molecule" nahi dikhta.

## Part E: Ulta, thoda sa: formula se percent aur empirical formula

Percent nikalna Class 9 mein tha, to yahan sirf ek jhalak:

- **Fe₂O₃ mein Fe ka percent:** 112 ÷ 160 × 100 = 70% (Example 3 ka ulta).
- **Formula se empirical formula:** subscripts ko unke sabse bade saajha (common) bhaajak se bhaag do. C₆H₁₂O₆: 6, 12, 6 sabko 6 se bhaag do: C₁H₂O₁ = **CH₂O**. C₂H₂: 2 se bhaag do: **CH**. C₄H₁₀: 2 se bhaag do: **C₂H₅**.

Ye bhi dhyan rakho: **ek hi percentage kai molecular formulae se aa sakta hai**, kyunki percentage sirf ratio batata hai. Isiliye molecular mass ki zaroorat padti hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Percent ko sidhe bhaag kar dete hain, atomic mass se nahi."**
Example 1 mein C = 80%, H = 20%. Agar koi seedha 80 : 20 = 4 : 1 likh de, to formula C₄H nikalega. Ye galat hai: ye **mass** ka ratio hai, atoms ka nahi. Ek C atom 12 u ka hai aur H sirf 1 u ka, isliye 80 g C mein kam atoms aate hain aur 20 g H mein bahut zyada. Sahi tareeka: 80 ÷ 12 = 6.67 aur 20 ÷ 1 = 20, ratio 1 : 3, yaani CH₃.

**Galti 2: "1.33 ko round off karke 1 kar dena."**
Fe 72.4%, O 27.6% mein ratio 1 : 1.33 aaya. Agar koi 1.33 ko 1 kar de to FeO likhega. Par FeO ka Fe percent 56 ÷ 72 × 100 = 77.8% hota hai, jo diye hue 72.4% se door hai. Fe₃O₄ (3 : 4) ke saath percent 72.4% aata hai. Chhota sa nuksaan aksar bada farq banata hai: 1.33 jaise bhinn ko poora karne ke liye hamesha 3 se guna karo (Example 4).

**Galti 3: "NaCl ek molecule hai, uska molecular formula NaCl hai."**
Agar NaCl ek alag molecule hota, to figure 3.5 mein Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ ke joda-joda alag dikhte. Par asli mein har Na⁺ ko 6 Cl⁻ ghere hote hain aur har Cl⁻ ko 6 Na⁺ (3D mein), aur koi ek jodi alag nahi hoti. Isliye ionic yaugik ke liye "formula unit" kehna sahi hai: NaCl, Na₂SO₄, Fe₂O₃ ye sab formula unit hain.

**Galti 4: "Molecular formula nikaalte waqt sirf ek subscript ko n se guna karna."**
Glucose mein n = 6. Agar koi sirf C ko 6 se guna kare: C₆H₂O, to galat. **Har** subscript ko n se guna karna hai: CH₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆. Jaanch karo: C₆H₂O ka mass = 72 + 2 + 16 = 90, jo diye hue 180 se aadha hai. Hamesha last mein molecular mass jaanch lo.

## Quick Recap

- **Empirical formula** = atoms ka sabse chhota poora-ankon ratio. **Molecular formula** = ek molecule mein asli ginti.
- **Molecular formula = (empirical formula) × n**, n = molecular mass ÷ empirical formula mass.
- **5 step:** percent ko gram maano, atomic mass se bhaag (mole), sabse chhote se bhaag, zaroorat ho to 2 ya 3 se guna, formula likho.
- Bhinn ratio ke tricks: 1.5 → × 2, 1.33 → × 3, 1.25 → × 4.
- VD se molecular mass: M = 2 × VD (Topic 2). Phir n nikalo.
- Ionic yaugik (NaCl, Na₂SO₄, Fe₂O₃) ke sirf empirical formula (formula unit) hote hain. H₂O, CO₂, NH₃ mein empirical aur molecular formula ek hain.
- Ek hi empirical formula alag yaugiko ka ho sakta hai (glucose aur acetic acid, dono CH₂O).

## Practice Questions

(Sab sawaal paper par hal karne ke liye hain; jalne wali, zehreeli ya tez gasein sirf padhne ke liye hain.)

1. A compound contains 29.1% sodium, 40.5% sulphur and 30.4% oxygen by mass. Find its empirical formula. (Na = 23, S = 32, O = 16.)
2. A hydrocarbon has 83.7% carbon and 16.3% hydrogen by mass. Its vapour density is 43. Find its empirical formula, its molecular mass and its molecular formula.
3. An organic compound contains 54.5% carbon, 9.1% hydrogen and 36.4% oxygen. Its molecular mass is 88. Find the empirical formula and the molecular formula.
4. A poisonous gas contains 30.4% nitrogen and 69.6% oxygen, and its vapour density is 46. Find its molecular formula. (Described only: never handle it.)
5. A compound has 31.8% potassium, 29.0% chlorine and 39.2% oxygen by mass. Find its empirical formula. Is it a molecule or a formula unit? Why? (K = 39, Cl = 35.5.)
6. A sample of an oxide of nitrogen contains 2.8 g of nitrogen combined with 1.6 g of oxygen. Find its empirical formula. (N = 14.)
7. Write the empirical formula of each: (a) C₈H₁₈ (b) P₄O₁₀ (c) C₁₂H₂₂O₁₁ (P = 31).
8. The empirical formula of a liquid is NH₂ and its vapour density is 16. Find its molecular formula. (Do not handle it: poisonous.)
9. A student says: "The empirical formula of a compound is CH₂O, so its molecular mass can be 100." Is that possible? Explain with n.
10. NO₂ and N₂O₄ both contain 30.4% nitrogen (rounded). Show this with a calculation. Why does this prove that the percentage alone cannot give the molecular formula?
11. A student says that the formula NaCl means a molecule with one sodium atom and one chlorine atom. Correct the student.
12. Pattern puzzle: a floor tile carries 5 blue diamonds and 15 white dots. The smallest repeating pattern keeps the same blue : white ratio as the whole tile. Find the smallest pattern, the multiplier n, and say which part is like the empirical formula and which like the molecular formula.
13. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) A compound's molecular formula can be smaller than its empirical formula. (b) n must be a whole number. (c) The empirical formula of C₂H₂ is CH. (d) NaCl has a molecular mass of 58.5 u. (e) Divide the percentage directly by the percentage of the other element to find the formula ratio.

### Answers

1. Na = 29.1 ÷ 23 = 1.265. S = 40.5 ÷ 32 = 1.266. O = 30.4 ÷ 16 = 1.9. Sabse chhota 1.265: Na = 1, S = 1, O = 1.9 ÷ 1.265 = **1.5**. Poori sankhya nahi, to sab ko 2 se guna: 2 : 2 : 3. **Empirical formula Na₂S₂O₃**. Jaanch: Na₂S₂O₃ = 46 + 64 + 48 = 158. Na = 46 ÷ 158 × 100 = 29.1% ✅, S = 64 ÷ 158 × 100 = 40.5% ✅, O = 48 ÷ 158 × 100 = 30.4% ✅.
2. C = 83.7 ÷ 12 = 6.975. H = 16.3 ÷ 1 = 16.3. H ÷ C = 16.3 ÷ 6.975 = 2.34, lagbhag 2.33. 1 : 2.33 ko 3 se guna: 3 : 7. **Empirical formula C₃H₇** (mass 43). M = 2 × 43 = **86**. n = 86 ÷ 43 = 2. **Molecular formula C₆H₁₄**. Jaanch: C₆H₁₄ = 72 + 14 = 86 ✅. C = 72 ÷ 86 × 100 = 83.7% ✅.
3. C = 54.5 ÷ 12 = 4.54. H = 9.1 ÷ 1 = 9.1. O = 36.4 ÷ 16 = 2.275. Sabse chhota 2.275: C = 2.0, H = 4.0, O = 1. **Empirical formula C₂H₄O** (mass 24 + 4 + 16 = 44). n = 88 ÷ 44 = 2. **Molecular formula C₄H₈O₂**. Jaanch: C₄H₈O₂ = 48 + 8 + 32 = 88 ✅. C = 48 ÷ 88 × 100 = 54.5% ✅.
4. N = 30.4 ÷ 14 = 2.17. O = 69.6 ÷ 16 = 4.35. O ÷ N = 2.0. **Empirical formula NO₂** (mass 46). M = 2 × 46 = 92. n = 92 ÷ 46 = 2. **Molecular formula N₂O₄**. Jaanch: N₂O₄ = 28 + 64 = 92 ✅. N = 28 ÷ 92 × 100 = 30.4% ✅.
5. K = 31.8 ÷ 39 = 0.815. Cl = 29.0 ÷ 35.5 = 0.817. O = 39.2 ÷ 16 = 2.45. Sabse chhota 0.815: K = 1, Cl = 1, O = 3. **Empirical formula KClO₃**. Ye ek ionic yaugik hai (K⁺ aur ClO₃⁻), isliye **formula unit** hai, molecule nahi. Jaanch: KClO₃ = 39 + 35.5 + 48 = 122.5. K = 39 ÷ 122.5 × 100 = 31.8% ✅. (KClO₃ ek tez oxidiser hai: sirf padhne ke liye.)
6. N = 2.8 ÷ 14 = 0.2 mol. O = 1.6 ÷ 16 = 0.1 mol. Ratio N : O = 2 : 1. **Empirical formula N₂O**. Jaanch: N₂O = 28 + 16 = 44. N = 28 ÷ 44 × 100 = 63.6%; diya hua 2.8 ÷ 4.4 × 100 = 63.6% ✅.
7. (a) 8, 18 ko 2 se bhaag: **C₄H₉**. (b) 4, 10 ko 2 se bhaag: **P₂O₅**. (c) 12, 22, 11 ka koi saajha bhaajak 1 ke alawa nahi, isliye **C₁₂H₂₂O₁₁** wahi rehta hai (empirical aur molecular formula ek hi hain). Jaanch (b): P₄O₁₀ = 124 + 160 = 284, P₂O₅ = 62 + 80 = 142 aur 284 ÷ 142 = 2 ✅.
8. NH₂ ka mass = 14 + 2 = 16. M = 2 × 16 = 32. n = 32 ÷ 16 = 2. **Molecular formula N₂H₄**. Jaanch: N₂H₄ = 28 + 4 = 32 ✅.
9. CH₂O ka mass = 12 + 2 + 16 = 30. n = 100 ÷ 30 = 3.33, jo poori sankhya nahi. **Nahi**, ye possible nahi: molecular mass 30 ka poora guna hona chahiye (30, 60, 90, 120...). 100 in mein nahi aata.
10. NO₂ = 14 + 32 = 46, N = 14 ÷ 46 × 100 = 30.4%. N₂O₄ = 28 + 64 = 92, N = 28 ÷ 92 × 100 = 30.4%. Dono ka percent ek hai kyunki dono ka ratio (N : O = 1 : 2) ek hai. Isliye percentage se sirf empirical formula milta hai; molecular formula ke liye molecular mass (ya VD) bhi chahiye.
11. NaCl ionic hai: usmein Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ ions ka bahut bada jaal hota hai (Figure 3.5), koi alag molecule nahi. NaCl **formula unit** hai: sirf Na⁺ : Cl⁻ = 1 : 1 ka ratio. Mass ko "formula unit mass" 58.5 u kehte hain, "molecular mass" nahi.
12. Ratio B : w = 5 : 15 = 1 : 3. Sabse chhota pattern **1 B aur 3 w** (BW₃). n = 5. 1 : 3 wala sabse chhota hissa **empirical formula** jaisa hai, aur poori tile (B₅W₁₅) **molecular formula** jaisi. Jaanch: 1 × 5 = 5 aur 3 × 5 = 15 ✅.
13. (a) **False** ❌: molecular formula hamesha empirical ka n guna hota hai, n ≥ 1, to chhota nahi ho sakta (ya barabar hota hai). (b) **True** ✅. (c) **True** ✅: 2 : 2 ko 2 se bhaag do to 1 : 1. (d) **False** ❌: NaCl ka "molecular mass" nahi hota; **formula unit mass** 58.5 u hota hai. (e) **False** ❌: mass ratio se atoms ka ratio nahi milta; pehle atomic mass se bhaag (moles) karna zaroori hai.
