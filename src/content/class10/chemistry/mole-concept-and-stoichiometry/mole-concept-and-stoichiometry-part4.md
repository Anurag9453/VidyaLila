# Topic 4: Equation Ek Recipe Hai: Mass Se Mass Tak

Pichhle topic mein humne kisi yaugik ka **formula** nikaalna seekha: percentage ya vapour density se, ek-ek atom ginkar. Ab ek naya sawaal: formula to mil gaya, par jab **do ya zyada yaugik aapas mein reaction karein**, to ek ka mass pata ho to doosre ka mass kaise nikaalein? Topic 1 mein humne dekha tha ki gas ke **volume** ek simple ratio mein reaction karte hain. Is topic mein wahi idea **mass** ke liye khulta hai, aur iska pul wahi hai jo Class 9 se hamare paas hai: **mole**. Is topic ke baad kisi bhi balanced equation ko dekh kar tum bata sakoge: "itne gram se itne gram banega."

## Cake ka order: 1 se 40

Mr. Tendulkar ki bhatiji Anagha ko school-fete (mela) mein ek stall milega, aur stall ke liye **40 cakes** ka order hai. Mr. Tendulkar ne purani recipe-card nikaali: **1 cake ke liye 2 ande, 200 g maida, 100 g cheeni, 50 g butter.** Anagha ne turant likha: "Chacha, 40 cake matlab sab kuch **40 guna**: 80 ande, 8000 g yaani 8 kg maida, 4 kg cheeni, 2 kg butter." Mr. Tendulkar muskuraye: "Bilkul. Recipe ek **ratio** hai. Cake kitne banane hain, bas wahi **guna karne wali sankhya** (scale factor) hai."

Phir unhone ek doosra card dikhaya, jo unki nani ke zamane ka tha: **"2 ande, 1 katori maida, 1 katori cheeni."** Anagha ne kaha: "Is mein maida aur cheeni ka ratio 1 : 1 hai, to dono ka **vazan** bhi barabar hoga." Mr. Tendulkar ne rasoi ki taraazu par ek **barabar katori** maida aur ek **barabar katori** cheeni tole. Maida lagbhag **100 g** nikla, cheeni lagbhag **150 g**. "Dekha? **Ginti** (katori) mein ratio 1 : 1 hai, par **vazan** mein ratio 100 : 150, yaani 2 : 3 hai. Kyunki cheeni ki ek katori maida ki katori se bhaari hoti hai."

Anagha ne aankhein badi kar ke kaha: "Chacha, ye to bilkul chemistry jaisa hai! Equation ke aage ke numbers bhi **'katori'** (ginti) hote honge, vazan nahi!" Mr. Tendulkar ne taali baja di: "Yahi aaj ka poora topic hai. Equation ek **recipe** hai, aur uski 'katori' ka naam **mole** hai."

**Ghar par kar sakte ho (bade ke saath, 5 minute):** ek hi katori se chawal, atta aur cheeni tolo (har baar katori barabar bhari, upar se saaf). Ginti barabar, par vazan alag-alag aayega. Taraazu na ho to haath mein uthakar mehsoos karo: cheeni aur chawal bhaari lagenge, atta halka.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Stoichiometry** | Reaction mein kaun kitna lagta hai aur kitna banta hai, iska hisaab | 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O ke hisaab se paani ka mass nikaalna |
| **Coefficient** | Balanced equation mein formula ke aage ka number | 2H₂O mein 2 |
| **Mole ratio** | Do substances ke coefficients ka ratio, yaani unke moles ka ratio | CaCO₃ : CaO = 1 : 1 |
| **Given aur asked** | Jis substance ka mass diya hai (given) aur jiska poochha hai (asked) | Diya CaCO₃, poochha CaO |
| **Scale factor** | Recipe ko kitne guna karna hai | 1 cake se 40 cake: scale factor 40 |
| **Mass-mass problem** | Ek substance ke mass se doosre substance ka mass nikaalna | 400 g CaCO₃ se kitna CaO |
| **Conservation of mass** | Reaction mein kul mass na badhta hai na ghatta hai | Reactants ka mass = products ka mass |
| **Exact reacting ratio** | Reactants ko bilkul recipe ke ratio mein lena, taaki dono poore khatam hon | Is topic ke saare sawaalon mein yehi maana gaya hai |

## Part A: Equation kya batati hai, kya nahi

Ek balanced equation ko **recipe** ki tarah padho. 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O ka matlab:

- **Molecules:** 2 molecule H₂ + 1 molecule O₂ → 2 molecule H₂O.
- **Moles:** 2 mol H₂ + 1 mol O₂ → 2 mol H₂O (har molecule ko 6.022 × 10²³ se guna karo, to mole mil jaata hai: ye ginti ki baat hai, [atoms-and-molecules-part5.md](../../../class9/chemistry/atoms-and-molecules/atoms-and-molecules-part5.md) wali).
- **Gas ka volume** (same T aur P par): 2 : 1 : 2 (Topic 1 wala Avogadro ka kamaal).

Aur **mass** (gram)? Mass ka rishta seedha equation mein nahi likha hota. Wo mole se **molar mass** ke zariye nikalta hai:

```
            2H₂      +      O₂       →      2H₂O

 molecules   2              1                2
 moles       2              1                2
 mass (g)    2 x 2 = 4      1 x 32 = 32      2 x 18 = 36
```

> **Figure 4.1** – 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O ki recipe teen tarah se. Molecules aur moles ka ratio coefficients wala hi hai (2 : 1 : 2). Mass ka ratio 4 : 32 : 36, yaani 1 : 8 : 9 hai: coefficient 2 : 1 : 2 se bilkul alag. Phir bhi kul mass bachta hai: 4 + 32 = 36.

Anagha ki katori ki tarah: coefficient 2 : 1 hai (ginti ki katori), par H₂ ki ek katori (1 mol) sirf **2 g** ki aur O₂ ki ek katori **32 g** ki. Isliye mass kabhi seedha coefficient se nahi nikalte. **Pehle mole, phir mass.**

(Chapter 4 mein jo electrons gine the, wahan bhi yahi ginti ka idea tha: "2 electron ek Cu ke liye". Wahan ginti electrons ki thi, yahan reactants aur products ki. Charge ya bijli ka hisaab yahan nahi aayega.)

## Part B: Mole ratio method: paanch kadam

Class 9 mein ek hi substance ke liye mass aur mole ka rishta tha: n = m ÷ M. Ab do substances hain, aur unke beech ka **pul equation ke coefficients** banate hain. Poora raasta ek hi figure mein:

```
    GIVEN ka mass       GIVEN ke moles      ASKED ke moles      ASKED ka mass

    [ m (given) ] --> [ n (given) ] --> [ n (asked) ] --> [ m (asked) ]
              ÷ M            mole ratio              x M
                    (equation ke coefficients)
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Mass-mass ka raasta: given ke mass ko uske molar mass M se bhaag karke moles mein badlo (÷ M), equation ke coefficients se asked ke moles nikalo, phir asked ke molar mass M se guna karke (× M) mass mein wapas aao. Beech ka pul sirf moles par bana hai.

**Paanch kadam:**

1. **Balance karo** aur har formula ka molar mass likho (Jaanch: har atom ka count dono taraf barabar).
2. **Mole ratio likho:** diya hua (given) aur poochha hua (asked) substance, unke coefficients.
3. **Given ko moles mein badlo:** n = m ÷ M.
4. **Ratio lagao:** asked ke moles = given ke moles × (asked ka coefficient ÷ given ka coefficient).
5. **Asked ko mass mein badlo:** m = n × M. Aakhir mein **Jaanch**: kul mass reactants ka = kul mass products ka.

Is topic mein hum maante hain ki doosre reactants **bilkul sahi ratio mein** maujood hain (ya "baaki sab kaafi hai", jab sawaal bole). Kam ya zyada reactant wale sawaal aage ki class mein aate hain.

## Part C: Solved examples

(Jahan koi khatarnak reaction ya gas hai, wahan "sirf padhne ke liye" likha hai. Hum sirf kagaz par hisaab kar rahe hain.)

### Example 1: Chuna-patthar se CaO (limestone ko garam karna)

Ek chuna-bhatti (lime kiln) mein shuddh CaCO₃ ko garam kiya jaata hai (calcination, [metallurgy-part4.md](../metallurgy/metallurgy-part4.md) ke sath wahi idea): **CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂**. (Sirf padhne ke liye: bhatti bahut garam hoti hai.)

**Sawaal:** 400 g shuddh CaCO₃ poori tarah garam kiya gaya. Kitna CaO aur kitni CO₂ banegi?

**Hal:**
1. Equation balanced hai: Ca 1 = 1, C 1 = 1, O 3 = 1 + 2 ✅. Molar mass: CaCO₃ = 40 + 12 + 48 = 100, CaO = 40 + 16 = 56, CO₂ = 12 + 32 = 44 g/mol.
2. Mole ratio CaCO₃ : CaO : CO₂ = 1 : 1 : 1.
3. CaCO₃ ke moles = 400 ÷ 100 = **4 mol**.
4. CaO = 4 mol, CO₂ = 4 mol.
5. CaO ka mass = 4 × 56 = **224 g**. CO₂ ka mass = 4 × 44 = **176 g**.

**Jaanch (mass conservation):** pehle 400 g; baad mein 224 + 176 = 400 g ✅.

### Example 2: Ek aur kadam: CaO se bujha chuna (chain)

Example 1 ka CaO ab paani se milta hai (bujha chuna, whitewash wala): **CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂**. Sawaal: 224 g CaO mein kitna paani lagega aur kitna Ca(OH)₂ banega?

**Hal:**
- Balanced: Ca 1 = 1, O 1 + 1 = 2 = 2, H 2 = 2 ✅. Ca(OH)₂ = 40 + 2 × 17 = 74 g/mol.
- CaO ke moles = 224 ÷ 56 = **4 mol**. Ratio 1 : 1 : 1, to paani 4 mol aur Ca(OH)₂ 4 mol.
- Paani = 4 × 18 = **72 g**. Ca(OH)₂ = 4 × 74 = **296 g**.

**Jaanch:** 224 + 72 = 296 ✅. Poori kahani: 400 g CaCO₃ → 224 g CaO → 296 g Ca(OH)₂ (aur raaste mein 176 g CO₂ hawa mein gayi).

### Example 3: Loha, CO se (ratio 1 : 3 : 2 : 3)

Blast furnace mein ([metallurgy-part4.md](../metallurgy/metallurgy-part4.md)): **Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂**. (Sirf padhne ke liye: CO ek zehreeli gas hai, bina dikhe aur bina boo ke nuksan karti hai.)

**Sawaal:** 480 g Fe₂O₃ se kitna Fe milega, aur kitni CO lagegi, kitni CO₂ banegi?

**Hal:**
1. Balanced: Fe 2 = 2, O 3 + 3 = 6 = 6, C 3 = 3 ✅. Fe₂O₃ = 112 + 48 = 160, CO = 28, Fe = 56, CO₂ = 44 g/mol.
2. Ratio Fe₂O₃ : CO : Fe : CO₂ = 1 : 3 : 2 : 3.
3. Fe₂O₃ = 480 ÷ 160 = **3 mol**.
4. CO = 3 × 3 = 9 mol, Fe = 3 × 2 = 6 mol, CO₂ = 9 mol.
5. Fe = 6 × 56 = **336 g**. CO = 9 × 28 = **252 g**. CO₂ = 9 × 44 = **396 g**.

**Jaanch:** pehle 480 + 252 = 732 g; baad mein 336 + 396 = 732 g ✅.

Dhyan do: coefficient 1 : 3 : 2 : 3 hai, par mass 480 : 252 : 336 : 396. Ratio of **moles** 1 : 3 : 2 : 3 pakka hai, mass ka ratio nahi.

### Example 4: Ulta sawaal: product diya, reactant poochha (H₂O₂)

Chhote sawaal mein bhi raasta wahi, sirf ulta chalte hain. Ghar mein dawai ki dukaan ka dilute hydrogen peroxide (H₂O₂) dheere-dheere toot kar paani aur oxygen deta hai: **2H₂O₂ → 2H₂O + O₂**. (Bade ki nigrani mein hi use hota hai.)

**Sawaal:** 4.8 g oxygen banane ke liye kitna H₂O₂ toot-na chahiye, aur kitna paani banega?

**Hal:**
1. Balanced: H 4 = 4, O 4 = 2 + 2 ✅. H₂O₂ = 2 + 32 = 34, H₂O = 18, O₂ = 32 g/mol.
2. Ratio H₂O₂ : H₂O : O₂ = 2 : 2 : 1.
3. **Given ab O₂ hai:** 4.8 ÷ 32 = **0.15 mol**.
4. H₂O₂ = 0.15 × 2 = 0.3 mol. H₂O = 0.3 mol.
5. H₂O₂ = 0.3 × 34 = **10.2 g**. H₂O = 0.3 × 18 = **5.4 g**.

**Jaanch:** 10.2 = 5.4 + 4.8 ✅.

### Example 5: Dhatu aur acid, aur kanon (particles) ki ginti

Aluminium dilute HCl se hydrogen deta hai: **2Al + 6HCl → 2AlCl₃ + 3H₂**. (Teacher demo ke liye hi, aur H₂ ko kabhi jalana ya jama karna nahi.)

**Sawaal:** 10.8 g aluminium se kitna HCl lagega, kitna AlCl₃ aur kitna H₂ banega? H₂ ke kitne molecules banenge?

**Hal:**
1. Balanced: Al 2 = 2, H 6 = 6, Cl 6 = 6 ✅. Al = 27, HCl = 1 + 35.5 = 36.5, AlCl₃ = 27 + 106.5 = 133.5, H₂ = 2 g/mol.
2. Ratio Al : HCl : AlCl₃ : H₂ = 2 : 6 : 2 : 3. (Chhota karo to 1 : 3 : 1 : 1.5.)
3. Al = 10.8 ÷ 27 = **0.4 mol**.
4. HCl = 0.4 × 3 = 1.2 mol. AlCl₃ = 0.4 mol. H₂ = 0.4 × 1.5 = 0.6 mol.
5. HCl = 1.2 × 36.5 = **43.8 g**. AlCl₃ = 0.4 × 133.5 = **53.4 g**. H₂ = 0.6 × 2 = **1.2 g**.
6. **Kan:** H₂ ke molecules = 0.6 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **3.613 × 10²³** (Class 9 wala n × 6.022 × 10²³).

**Jaanch:** 10.8 + 43.8 = 54.6 g; 53.4 + 1.2 = 54.6 g ✅. Aur 0.6 mol H₂ = 0.6 × 2 = 1.2 g ✅.

### Example 6: Rasoi ka reaction: baking soda aur sirka (ghar par safe)

Baking soda (NaHCO₃) aur sirka (acetic acid) ka jhaag aapne dekha hoga: **NaHCO₃ + CH₃COOH → CH₃COONa + H₂O + CO₂**. (Bade ke saath, khuli jagah par, aankhon se door.)

**Sawaal:** 8.4 g baking soda ke saath kitna shuddh acetic acid chahiye? Kitni CO₂ nikalegi? Sirka lagbhag 5% acetic acid hota hai, to kitna sirka?

**Hal:**
1. Balanced: Na 1 = 1, C 1 + 2 = 3 = 2 + 1 ✅, H 1 + 4 = 5 = 3 + 2 ✅, O 3 + 2 = 5 = 2 + 1 + 2 ✅. NaHCO₃ = 23 + 1 + 12 + 48 = 84, CH₃COOH = 24 + 4 + 32 = 60, CO₂ = 44.
2. Ratio sab 1 : 1 : 1 : 1 : 1.
3. NaHCO₃ = 8.4 ÷ 84 = **0.1 mol**.
4. CH₃COOH = 0.1 mol, CO₂ = 0.1 mol.
5. Acetic acid = 0.1 × 60 = **6 g**. CO₂ = 0.1 × 44 = **4.4 g**.
6. Sirka mein 5% acid hai (lagbhag): 6 ÷ 0.05 = **120 g sirka**, jo lagbhag 120 mL ke barabar hai.

**Jaanch:** products: CH₃COONa 0.1 × 82 = 8.2 g, H₂O 1.8 g, CO₂ 4.4 g, kul 14.4 g. Reactants: 8.4 + 6 = 14.4 g ✅.

**Taraazu wali baat:** agar ye sab ek khuli katori mein rakhein, to **CO₂ hawa mein chali jaayegi** aur taraazu par mass lagbhag **4.4 g ghata** dikhega. Mass gayab nahi hua, gas ne bhaag kar kamra chhoda. (Ye reaction kabhi bandh bottle mein mat karo: gas ka dabav bottle ko phaad sakta hai.)

## Part D: Mass bachta hai, moles nahi

Har Jaanch ka sabse bada hathiyar hai **conservation of mass**: reaction mein atom na banta hai na khatam hota hai, sirf nayi tarah judta hai, isliye **kul mass pehle aur baad mein barabar**. Par ek rochak baat: **moles ki ginti barabar hona zaroori nahi.** Dekho N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃ (Topic 1 ka same equation, ab mass ke saath), jab 1 mol N₂ lete hain:

| Cheez | Moles | Mass (g) |
|---|---|---|
| N₂ (1 mol × 28) | 1 | 28 |
| H₂ (3 mol × 2) | 3 | 6 |
| **Reactants kul** | **4** | **34** |
| NH₃ (2 mol × 17) | 2 | 34 |
| **Products kul** | **2** | **34** |

**Moles 4 se 2 ho gaye, par mass 34 g ka 34 g.** Kyun? Kyunki mole ginti ki ikai hai (molecules ki ginti badal sakti hai: do molecule milkar ek bada bana sakte hain), par **atoms ki ginti aur unka mass** nahi badalte. Isiliye Jaanch **mass** se karte hain, moles se nahi.

**Ek galat jawaab pakadna:** maan lo kisi ne Example 1 mein 4 mol CaCO₃ se CaO 224 g aur CO₂ **44 g** likha (4 mol ki jagah 1 mol ka mass likh diya). Jaanch karo: 224 + 44 = 268 g, jo 400 g se bahut kam hai. Jaanch ne turant batadiya ki **kuch chhoot gaya**: 400 − 224 = 176 g sahi hona chahiye tha, aur 176 ÷ 44 = 4 mol: yaani CO₂ ke 4 mol the, 1 nahi. Isi Jaanch se galti pakadne ki aadat dalo.

## Part E: Scale karna: gram se kilo aur tonne tak

Cake ki tarah, ek baar mass-ratio nikal liya to **kisi bhi unit mein** scale kar sakte ho. Example 1 se: 100 g CaCO₃ → 56 g CaO + 44 g CO₂. Ye **hamesha** (sirf is reaction ke liye) sahi hai, kyunki ye moles ke raaste se aaya hai. Isliye:

**Sawaal:** Ek chuna-bhatti mein 5 tonne shuddh CaCO₃ garam hua. Kitna CaO aur kitni CO₂?

**Hal:** 100 g CaCO₃ se 56 g CaO, yaani har 1 tonne CaCO₃ se 0.56 tonne CaO (kyunki 56 ÷ 100 = 0.56, aur ye ratio kisi bhi unit mein wahi rehta hai). Isi tarah CO₂ ka ratio 44 ÷ 100 = 0.44.
- 5 tonne ke liye: CaO = 5 × 0.56 = **2.8 tonne**. CO₂ = 5 × 0.44 = **2.2 tonne**.
- **Jaanch:** 2.8 + 2.2 = 5.0 tonne ✅. (Aur yaad rakho: ye ratio sirf is reaction ka hai. Kisi aur reaction mein ye 56 : 44 nahi hoga.)

**Seekh:** mass ka ek pair ka ratio fixed hota hai, par wo ratio **molar masses aur coefficients** se aata hai, "coefficient ko seedha gram samajhne" se nahi.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Equation mein jo numbers hain, wahi **gram** ka ratio hain."**

Anagha ne soch liya: "2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O hai, to 2 g hydrogen ko 1 g oxygen chahiye." Isse test karte hain, teen tarah.

**Test 1: cake ki tarah.** Nani wali recipe thi 1 katori maida : 1 katori cheeni. Agar koi bole "100 g maida ke saath 100 g cheeni", to katori ka matlab hi kho gaya: 100 g cheeni lagbhag 2/3 katori hai, 1 nahi. Ratio **katori (ginti)** mein tha, gram mein nahi.

**Test 2: paani ka fixed ratio.** Class 9 ([atoms-and-molecules-part2.md](../../../class9/chemistry/atoms-and-molecules/atoms-and-molecules-part2.md)) mein dekha tha ki paani mein H aur O ka mass hamesha **1 : 8** hota hai (Figure 4.1 mein bhi 4 g H₂ ke saath 32 g O₂). Agar "2 g H₂ ke saath 1 g O₂" paani banata, to us paani mein H : O = 2 : 1 hota, jo 1 : 8 se bilkul alag hai. Aisa paani asli paani nahi hota. Isliye "gram ka ratio = coefficient" **ghalat** hai. Sahi: 2 g H₂ = 1 mol, to 0.5 mol O₂ = **16 g O₂** chahiye, aur 1 mol H₂O = **18 g** banta hai (Jaanch: 2 + 16 = 18 ✅).

**Test 3: dhatu aur acid.** Mg + 2HCl → MgCl₂ + H₂ mein koi kahe "24 g Mg ko 2 g HCl chahiye (coefficient 1 : 2)". Sahi raasta: 24 g Mg = 1 mol, to HCl = 2 mol = 2 × 36.5 = **73 g**. Galat jawaab 2 g aur sahi 73 g mein lagbhag 36 guna ka farak hai: itne kam acid se Mg ka bahut chhota hissa hi ghulta.

**Sahi aadat:** coefficient ko hamesha **moles** padho, "kitni katori." Gram tak pahunchne ke liye har substance ko apne molar mass se guna karo.

## Quick Recap

- Balanced equation ek **recipe** hai: coefficients batate hain **molecules, moles aur gas volume** (same T, P) ka ratio. **Mass** ka ratio seedha coefficients se nahi, molar mass se milta hai.
- **Mass-mass ke paanch kadam:** balance aur molar mass, mole ratio, given ko moles mein (m ÷ M), ratio lagao, asked ko mass mein (n × M), phir Jaanch.
- Raasta: given mass → given moles → asked moles → asked mass (Figure 4.2). Beech ka pul **mole ratio**.
- **Conservation of mass:** kul mass reactants = kul mass products. Moles ki ginti ka barabar hona zaroori nahi (N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃: 4 mol se 2 mol, par mass 34 g = 34 g).
- Mass ka ek pair ka ratio fixed hai, to kisi bhi unit mein scale kar sakte ho (100 g → 56 g to 5 tonne → 2.8 tonne).
- **Kan:** moles × 6.022 × 10²³ se molecules ki ginti.
- **Aam galti:** coefficient ko gram ka ratio samajhna: 2 g H₂ ko 1 g O₂ nahi, 16 g O₂ chahiye.
- Khatarnak reaction (CO, HCl, acid, dhatu ki aag) sirf padhne ke liye; rasoi ka reaction bade ke saath.

## Practice Questions

Har sawaal mein reactants ko bilkul sahi ratio mein maano (ya "baaki sab kaafi hai"). Jahan khatarnak reaction hai, wo sirf padhne ke liye. Use H 1, C 12, N 14, O 16, Na 23, Mg 24, Al 27, S 32, Cl 35.5, K 39, Ca 40, Fe 56, Cu 63.5, Zn 65, I 127, Pb 207, P 31.

1. 2NaOH + H₂SO₄ → Na₂SO₄ + 2H₂O. A school lab neutralises 30 g of NaOH. Find the mass of H₂SO₄ needed, and the masses of Na₂SO₄ and water formed. Check that mass is conserved.
2. Na₂CO₃ + 2HCl → 2NaCl + H₂O + CO₂. Find the mass of NaCl and of CO₂ formed from 15.9 g of Na₂CO₃. How many molecules of CO₂ is that?
3. Ethanol burns as C₂H₅OH + 3O₂ → 2CO₂ + 3H₂O. For 23 g of ethanol, find the mass of oxygen needed and of CO₂ and H₂O formed. Check conservation of mass. (Ethanol is inflammable: paper work only.)
4. Pb(NO₃)₂ + 2KI → PbI₂ + 2KNO₃. A solution containing 16.6 g of KI is mixed with exactly enough Pb(NO₃)₂ solution. Find the mass of Pb(NO₃)₂ needed and of yellow PbI₂ formed. (Lead compounds are poisonous: paper work only.)
5. Copper(II) carbonate decomposes on strong heating: CuCO₃ → CuO + CO₂. How much CuO and CO₂ come from 37.05 g of CuCO₃? Check by adding the masses.
6. Mg + H₂SO₄ → MgSO₄ + H₂. A teacher uses 18 g of magnesium ribbon with enough dilute acid. Find the mass of MgSO₄ formed and the number of H₂ molecules released.
7. In the thermite reaction Fe₂O₃ + 2Al → Al₂O₃ + 2Fe, 84 g of iron must be produced. Find the masses of Fe₂O₃ and Al needed, and of Al₂O₃ formed. (Paper work: the reaction throws molten metal and sparks.)
8. A student says: "In 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O, 10 g of hydrogen needs 5 g of oxygen." Find the actual mass of oxygen needed and the mass of water formed, and explain the student's mistake in one line.
9. Quicklime slowly takes up carbon dioxide from the air: CaO + CO₂ → CaCO₃. 14 g of CaO takes up the exact amount of CO₂. Find the mass of CO₂ taken up and of CaCO₃ formed. Compare the total moles before and after the reaction, and the total mass before and after.
10. A pancake recipe for 8 pancakes needs 2 cups flour, 1 cup milk and 1 egg. How much of each is needed for 24 pancakes? Which part of a chemical equation plays the role of "cups", and why can you not add the masses of the ingredients directly as cups?
11. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) In 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO, 2 g of Mg needs 1 g of O₂. (b) The total number of moles is always the same before and after a reaction. (c) The total mass is always the same before and after a reaction. (d) If 1 mol of CaCO₃ gives 1 mol of CaO, then 100 g of CaCO₃ gives 100 g of CaO. (e) The mole ratio of reactants and products comes from the coefficients of the balanced equation.
12. 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO. Magnesium ribbon of mass 30 g burns completely in oxygen (teacher demo only: never look at the bright flame directly). Find the mass of oxygen used, the mass of MgO formed, and the number of O₂ molecules used. Why is the white ash heavier than the ribbon?

### Answers

1. NaOH = 40 g/mol, to 30 ÷ 40 = **0.75 mol**. Ratio NaOH : H₂SO₄ : Na₂SO₄ : H₂O = 2 : 1 : 1 : 2. H₂SO₄ = 0.375 mol × 98 = **36.75 g**. Na₂SO₄ = 0.375 mol × 142 = **53.25 g**. H₂O = 0.75 mol × 18 = **13.5 g**. Jaanch: 30 + 36.75 = 66.75 g; 53.25 + 13.5 = 66.75 g ✅.
2. Na₂CO₃ = 46 + 12 + 48 = 106, to 15.9 ÷ 106 = **0.15 mol**. Ratio Na₂CO₃ : NaCl : CO₂ = 1 : 2 : 1. NaCl = 0.3 mol × 58.5 = **17.55 g**. CO₂ = 0.15 mol × 44 = **6.6 g**. Molecules CO₂ = 0.15 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **9.033 × 10²²**. Jaanch: HCl 0.3 mol × 36.5 = 10.95 g, H₂O 0.15 × 18 = 2.7 g. Reactants 15.9 + 10.95 = 26.85 g; products 17.55 + 2.7 + 6.6 = 26.85 g ✅.
3. C₂H₅OH = 24 + 6 + 16 = 46, to 23 ÷ 46 = **0.5 mol**. Ratio 1 : 3 : 2 : 3. O₂ = 1.5 mol × 32 = **48 g**. CO₂ = 1 mol × 44 = **44 g**. H₂O = 1.5 mol × 18 = **27 g**. Jaanch: 23 + 48 = 71 g; 44 + 27 = 71 g ✅.
4. KI = 39 + 127 = 166, to 16.6 ÷ 166 = **0.1 mol**. Ratio Pb(NO₃)₂ : KI : PbI₂ = 1 : 2 : 1. Pb(NO₃)₂ = 0.05 mol, molar mass 207 + 2 × 62 = 331, to **16.55 g**. PbI₂ = 0.05 mol, molar mass 207 + 254 = 461, to **23.05 g**. KNO₃ = 0.1 mol × 101 = 10.1 g. Jaanch: 16.6 + 16.55 = 33.15 g; 23.05 + 10.1 = 33.15 g ✅. Lead ke yaugik zehreele hain: sirf kagaz par.
5. CuCO₃ = 63.5 + 12 + 48 = 123.5, to 37.05 ÷ 123.5 = **0.3 mol**. Ratio 1 : 1 : 1. CuO = 0.3 × 79.5 = **23.85 g**. CO₂ = 0.3 × 44 = **13.2 g**. Jaanch: 23.85 + 13.2 = 37.05 g ✅.
6. Mg = 18 ÷ 24 = **0.75 mol**. Ratio sab 1 : 1 : 1 : 1. MgSO₄ = 0.75 mol, molar mass 24 + 32 + 64 = 120, to **90 g**. H₂ = 0.75 mol, molecules = 0.75 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **4.517 × 10²³**. Jaanch: H₂SO₄ = 0.75 × 98 = 73.5 g, H₂ = 1.5 g. 18 + 73.5 = 91.5 g; 90 + 1.5 = 91.5 g ✅.
7. Fe = 84 ÷ 56 = **1.5 mol**. Ratio Fe₂O₃ : Al : Al₂O₃ : Fe = 1 : 2 : 1 : 2. Fe₂O₃ = 0.75 mol × 160 = **120 g**. Al = 1.5 mol × 27 = **40.5 g**. Al₂O₃ = 0.75 mol × 102 = **76.5 g**. Jaanch: 120 + 40.5 = 160.5 g; 76.5 + 84 = 160.5 g ✅.
8. H₂ = 10 ÷ 2 = 5 mol. Ratio H₂ : O₂ : H₂O = 2 : 1 : 2, to O₂ = 2.5 mol = 2.5 × 32 = **80 g**, aur H₂O = 5 mol × 18 = **90 g**. Jaanch: 10 + 80 = 90 g ✅. Student ki galti: usne coefficients 2 : 1 ko **gram** ka ratio maan liya; coefficient **moles** ka ratio hai (5 mol H₂ ke liye 2.5 mol O₂), aur 1 mol O₂ 32 g ka hota hai, 2 mol H₂ sirf 4 g ka.
9. CaO = 14 ÷ 56 = **0.25 mol**. Ratio CaO : CO₂ : CaCO₃ = 1 : 1 : 1. CO₂ = 0.25 mol × 44 = **11 g**. CaCO₃ = 0.25 mol × 100 = **25 g**. Moles: pehle 0.25 + 0.25 = **0.5 mol**, baad mein **0.25 mol** (moles badle). Mass: pehle 14 + 11 = **25 g**, baad mein **25 g** (mass wahi). Jaanch: atom Ca 1 = 1, C 1 = 1, O 1 + 2 = 3 ✅.
10. 24 ÷ 8 = scale factor **3**. Flour 2 × 3 = **6 cups**, milk 1 × 3 = **3 cups**, egg 1 × 3 = **3 eggs**. "Cups" ka role **moles** (coefficient) ka hai. Ek cup flour aur ek cup milk ka vazan barabar nahi hota (flour halka, milk bhaari), waise hi 1 mol ek substance ka aur 1 mol doosre ka mass alag hota hai (H₂ 2 g, O₂ 32 g). Isliye ginti se vazan tak jaane ke liye har ek ka apna molar mass chahiye.
11. (a) **False** ❌: 2 mol Mg = 48 g ko 1 mol O₂ = 32 g chahiye; 2 g Mg ko 1.33 g O₂ lagega (2 ÷ 24 = 0.0833 mol Mg, to O₂ = 0.0417 mol × 32 = 1.33 g). (b) **False** ❌: moles badal sakte hain (N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃ mein 4 se 2). (c) **True** ✅. (d) **False** ❌: 1 mol CaCO₃ = 100 g, 1 mol CaO = 56 g, to 100 g CaCO₃ se **56 g** CaO. (e) **True** ✅.
12. Mg = 30 ÷ 24 = **1.25 mol**. Ratio 2 : 1 : 2. O₂ = 0.625 mol × 32 = **20 g**. MgO = 1.25 mol × 40 = **50 g**. O₂ molecules = 0.625 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **3.764 × 10²³**. Jaanch: 30 + 20 = 50 g ✅. Raakh bhaari isliye hai ki Mg ke saath **hawa ki oxygen judi**: 30 g ribbon + 20 g oxygen = 50 g MgO (mass ghata nahi, oxygen ka mass jud gaya).
