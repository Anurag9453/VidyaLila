# Topic 5: Gas Ke Volume Aur Mole Ke Saath Calculations

Topic 4 mein humne seekha ki balanced equation moles ki recipe hai, aur mass se mass tak ka rasta **mass → mole → ratio → mole → mass** hai. Par bahut si reactions mein **gas** nikalti hai ya lagti hai, aur gas ko tarazu par tolna mushkil hai: usse naapna aasaan hota hai **volume** (litre) mein. Class 9 mein humne jaana tha ki STP par **1 mole gas = 22.4 L**. Is topic mein wahi 22.4 L mole ke pul ke **doosre kinare** par lagta hai: ab hum **mass se volume**, **volume se mass** aur **volume se volume** tak ke sawaal hal karenge, ghar ke cooking-gas burner ko kitni hawa chahiye ye bhi, aur aakhir mein ek poora **naksha (map)** banayenge jisme mass, mole, volume aur kan, sab ek jagah hain.

3. **Koi bhi switch mat dabao** (na light, na fan, na mobile charger), aur **koi matchstick ya lighter nahi.**

## Part A: Ek mole ke teen roop

Class 9 mein humne ek hi mole ke teen cheezein jodi thi: **mass** (molar mass, g), **kan** (6.022 × 10²³) aur **gas ka volume** (22.4 L, STP par). Ab inhe **ek saath** dekhte hain.

| 1 mole ka... | Mass | Gas ka volume (STP par) | Kan (molecules) |
|---|---|---|---|
| H₂ | 2 g | 22.4 L | 6.022 × 10²³ |
| O₂ | 32 g | 22.4 L | 6.022 × 10²³ |
| CO₂ | 44 g | 22.4 L | 6.022 × 10²³ |
| Liquid paani H₂O | 18 g | lagbhag 18 mL (22.4 L nahi) | 6.022 × 10²³ |
| Carbon (thos) | 12 g | lagbhag 5 mL (22.4 L nahi) | 6.022 × 10²³ (atoms) |

Gas ke teen row dekho: **mass sabka alag**, par **volume aur kan sabke barabar**. Wajah wahi Avogadro ka niyam: barabar volume mein barabar kan. Liquid aur thos ke row ka volume **bahut chhota** hai: wahan kan paas-paas jame hain, gas mein door-door (Aam galtiyan mein demonstration).

Ye teeno cheezein **mole** se jud'ti hain, aur mole hi pul hai:

```
  MASS (g)                   MOLES (n)                    VOLUME (L)

  +---------+ ÷ molar mass  +---------+    × 22.4    +---------+
  :    m    : ------------> :    n    : -----------> :    V    :
  :         : <------------ :         : <----------- : gas, STP:
  +---------+ × molar mass  +---------+    ÷ 22.4    +---------+
                                :   ^
              × 6.022 × 10²³    :   : ÷ 6.022 × 10²³
                                v   :
                          +-----------------+
                          :  KAN (particles):
                          +-----------------+
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Mole map. Beech mein **mole**: baayein mass (molar mass se aana-jaana), daayein gas ka volume (22.4 L se aana-jaana, sirf gas aur STP par), neeche kan (6.022 × 10²³ se aana-jaana). Mass se volume (ya kan) seedha nahi jaate: pehle mole.

**Niyam jo yaad rakhna hai:** map mein **do dibbon ke beech hamesha mole** aata hai. Mass se volume chahiye? mass → mole → volume. Kan se mass? kan → mole → mass.

## Part B: Mass se volume (equation ke saath)

Topic 4 ka rasta ek aur kinare tak badh gaya hai. Paanch step:

1. Equation **balance** karo.
2. Diye hue **mass ko mole** mein badlo (m ÷ M).
3. Equation ke **coefficient** se mole ratio lekar poochhi hui gas ke mole nikalo.
4. Un moles ko **22.4 se guna** karke volume nikalo (STP par).
5. **Jaanch**: volume ÷ 22.4 se wapas mole, aur mass conservation.

### Example 1: Sodium carbonate par acid: kitni CO₂

**Sawaal:** 21.2 g Na₂CO₃ par dilute HCl daalne par CO₂ nikalti hai (teacher demo mein bubbles). STP par kitni CO₂ banegi? (Na₂CO₃ + 2HCl → 2NaCl + H₂O + CO₂; HCl kaafi hai.)

**Hal:**
- Equation balanced hai: Na 2 = 2, C 1 = 1, O 3 = 1 + 2, H 2 = 2, Cl 2 = 2 ✅.
- M(Na₂CO₃) = 2 × 23 + 12 + 3 × 16 = 106 g/mol. Mole = 21.2 ÷ 106 = **0.2 mol**.
- Mole ratio Na₂CO₃ : CO₂ = 1 : 1, to CO₂ = **0.2 mol**.
- Volume = 0.2 × 22.4 = **4.48 L** (STP par).
- **Jaanch (mass conservation):** left: Na₂CO₃ 21.2 g + HCl (0.4 mol × 36.5 = 14.6 g) = 35.8 g. Right: NaCl (0.4 × 58.5 = 23.4 g) + H₂O (0.2 × 18 = 3.6 g) + CO₂ (0.2 × 44 = 8.8 g) = 35.8 g ✅. Volume ÷ 22.4 = 4.48 ÷ 22.4 = 0.2 mol ✅.

### Example 2: Metal par acid se hydrogen (sirf teacher demo)

**Sawaal:** 5.4 g aluminium dilute H₂SO₄ ke saath poori tarah react karta hai: 2Al + 3H₂SO₄ → Al₂(SO₄)₃ + 3H₂. STP par kitni H₂? Aur kitna H₂SO₄ lagega? (Hydrogen jalne wali gas hai: ise kabhi jalana nahi; ye sirf kagaz ka hisaab aur teacher demo hai.)

**Hal:**
- Atom count: Al 2 = 2; S 3 = 3; O 12 = 12; H 6 = 6 ✅.
- Al = 27 g/mol, mole = 5.4 ÷ 27 = **0.2 mol**.
- Ratio Al : H₂ = 2 : 3, to H₂ = 0.2 × 3/2 = **0.3 mol**. Volume = 0.3 × 22.4 = **6.72 L**.
- Ratio Al : H₂SO₄ = 2 : 3, to H₂SO₄ = **0.3 mol** = 0.3 × 98 = **29.4 g**.
- **Jaanch:** left: 5.4 + 29.4 = 34.8 g. Right: Al₂(SO₄)₃ (0.1 mol × 342 = 34.2 g) + H₂ (0.3 × 2 = 0.6 g) = 34.8 g ✅.

## Part C: Volume se mass (ulti disha)

Kabhi gas ka volume diya hota hai aur **kitna reactant chahiye** poochha jaata hai. Rasta ulta: **volume ÷ 22.4 → mole → ratio → mole → mass.**

### Example 3: Oxygen banane ke liye KClO₃ (sirf padhne ke liye)

**Sawaal:** 2KClO₃ → 2KCl + 3O₂. STP par 10.08 L oxygen chahiye. Kitna KClO₃ garam karna padega? (KClO₃ ek **tez oxidiser** hai: sirf padhne ke liye, student kabhi nahi garam karte.)

**Hal:**
- Atom count: K 2 = 2; Cl 2 = 2; O 6 = 6 ✅.
- Mole O₂ = 10.08 ÷ 22.4 = **0.45 mol**.
- Ratio KClO₃ : O₂ = 2 : 3, to KClO₃ = 0.45 × 2/3 = **0.3 mol**.
- M(KClO₃) = 39 + 35.5 + 48 = 122.5 g/mol. Mass = 0.3 × 122.5 = **36.75 g**.
- **Jaanch:** products: KCl (0.3 × 74.5 = 22.35 g) + O₂ (0.45 × 32 = 14.4 g) = 36.75 g ✅ (reactant ka mass).

## Part D: Volume se volume: mole mein badalna zaroori nahi

Agar **sab gas** hain aur **same T aur P** par, to Topic 1 ka rasta chalta hai: **coefficient ka ratio hi volume ka ratio hai**. Kyun? Kyunki barabar volume mein barabar kan (Avogadro), isliye volume ratio = mole ratio. Mole mein badalne ka kaam ek hi factor (22.4) se hota hai, jo dono taraf kat jaata hai.

### Example 4: Ethene ko kitni O₂

**Sawaal:** Ethene (C₂H₄) jalti hai: C₂H₄ + 3O₂ → 2CO₂ + 2H₂O. 6 L ethene ke liye kitni O₂ chahiye, aur kitni CO₂ banegi? Sab gas ek hi T, P par maano (paper reasoning).

**Hal:**
- Atom count: C 2 = 2; H 4 = 4; O 6 = 4 + 2 ✅.
- Volume ratio C₂H₄ : O₂ : CO₂ = 1 : 3 : 2.
- O₂ = 6 × 3 = **18 L**. CO₂ = 6 × 2 = **12 L**.
- Garam bhaap (steam) ko bhi gas gino to H₂O = 12 L; thanda hone par wo liquid ban jaati hai aur bahut chhota volume leti hai.
- **Jaanch (atoms gino, volume = molecule ginti):** left O atoms: 18 × 2 = 36. Right: CO₂ 12 × 2 = 24 aur steam 12 × 1 = 12, kul 36 ✅.

**Dhyan:** ye shortcut tabhi chalta hai jab **jo diya hai aur jo poochha hai, dono gas hon**. Agar ek taraf thos ya liquid ho (jaise Mg ya Na₂CO₃ ya paani), to mole se hi jaana padega (Part B aur C).

## Part E: Burner ko kitni hawa chahiye?

Ab Naiysha ka sawaal. Cooking gas (LPG) mein mukhya roop se propane aur butane hote hain. Hum sirf **propane (C₃H₈)** lete hain (kagaz ka hisaab).

C₃H₈ + 5O₂ → 3CO₂ + 4H₂O. Atom count: C 3 = 3; H 8 = 8; O 10 = 6 + 4 ✅.

Volume ratio C₃H₈ : O₂ : CO₂ : H₂O (steam) = 1 : 5 : 3 : 4.

```
  1 hissa = 2.1 L (ek dabba)

  C₃H₈          [ ]
  O₂ chahiye    [ ][ ][ ][ ][ ]
  CO₂ banti     [ ][ ][ ]
  H₂O (bhaap)   [ ][ ][ ][ ]
```

> **Figure 5.2** – 2.1 L propane ke liye (kagaz par): 10.5 L oxygen lagti hai (5 dabbe), 6.3 L CO₂ aur 8.4 L bhaap bante hain. Coefficient 1 : 5 : 3 : 4 hi dabbon ka ratio hai.

Par burner ko oxygen **hawa** se milti hai, aur hawa sirf lagbhag 21% oxygen hai:

```
  100 L hawa ke 100 chhote hisse (1 hissa = 1 L):

   N N N N N N N N N N
   N N N N N N N N N N
   N N N N N N N N N N
   N N N N N N N N N N
   N N N N N N N N N N
   N N N N N N N N N N
   N N N N N N N N N N
   N N N N N N N N O O
   O O O O O O O O O O
   O O O O O O O O O A

   N = 78 (nitrogen), O = 21 (oxygen), A = 1 (argon aur baaki)
```

> **Figure 5.3** – 100 L hawa ki ek tasveer: lagbhag 78 L nitrogen (N), 21 L oxygen (O), aur 1 L argon aur baaki gas (A). Isliye hawa ka volume = oxygen ka volume ÷ 0.21.

### Example 5: 2.1 L propane ke liye hawa

**Sawaal:** 2.1 L propane poori tarah jalne ke liye kitni hawa chahiye? (Hawa mein 21% O₂ by volume, sab ek hi T, P par.)

**Hal:**
- O₂ = 2.1 × 5 = **10.5 L** (volume ratio 1 : 5).
- Hawa = 10.5 ÷ 0.21 = **50 L**.
- **Jaanch:** 50 L hawa ka 21% = 50 × 0.21 = 10.5 L O₂ ✅.
- Isse ek mazedaar baat: **1 L propane ke liye lagbhag 24 L hawa** (50 ÷ 2.1 = 23.8). Gas jitni, hawa uski **24 guna** chahiye.

**Safety ki baat (sirf samajhne ke liye):** agar kitchen band ho aur hawa kam mile, to burner ki aag peeli ho jaati hai, bartan par kaalikh jamti hai, aur **carbon monoxide (CO)** bann sakti hai, jo **zehreeli aur bina badboo ki** gas hai. Isliye kitchen mein khidki ya chimney zaroori. Burner ke paas koi experiment nahi.

## Part F: Kan aur volume ka rishta

Volume → mole → kan. Seedha map ka neeche wala teer.

### Example 6: 1.12 L methane mein kitne kan

**Sawaal:** 1.12 L methane (CH₄) STP par hai. Kitne molecules, aur kitne kul atoms?

**Hal:**
- Mole = 1.12 ÷ 22.4 = **0.05 mol**.
- Molecules = 0.05 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **3.011 × 10²²**.
- Har CH₄ molecule mein 1 C + 4 H = **5 atoms**. Atoms = 3.011 × 10²² × 5 = **1.5055 × 10²³**, lagbhag **1.506 × 10²³**.
- **Jaanch:** 0.05 mol × 5 = 0.25 mol atoms; 0.25 × 6.022 × 10²³ = 1.5055 × 10²³ ✅.

## Part G: Do-step sawaal: ek reaction ki gas doosre mein

### Example 7: H₂O₂ se oxygen, aur us oxygen se magnesium

**Sawaal:** 5.1 g H₂O₂ ke toot'ne (2H₂O₂ → 2H₂O + O₂) se jo oxygen bani, wo 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO mein poori tarah kaam aa jaati hai. STP par oxygen ka volume kitna, aur kitna magnesium jalta hai, aur MgO kitna? (Magnesium bahut tez chamak ke saath jalta hai: sirf padhne ke liye. H₂O₂ ghol aankh aur haath ke liye nuksandeh ho sakta hai.)

**Hal:**
- Atom count: pehli equation H 4 = 4, O 4 = 2 + 2 ✅. Doosri: Mg 2 = 2, O 2 = 2 ✅.
- M(H₂O₂) = 34. Mole = 5.1 ÷ 34 = **0.15 mol**.
- Ratio H₂O₂ : O₂ = 2 : 1, to O₂ = **0.075 mol**. Volume = 0.075 × 22.4 = **1.68 L**.
- Doosri equation: Mg : O₂ = 2 : 1, to Mg = 0.075 × 2 = **0.15 mol** = 0.15 × 24 = **3.6 g**. MgO = **0.15 mol** = 0.15 × 40 = **6 g**.
- **Jaanch (mass conservation):** Mg 3.6 g + O₂ (0.075 × 32 = 2.4 g) = 6.0 g = MgO ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "22.4 L har cheez ka ek mole hota hai, liquid aur thos ka bhi."**
Naiysha ki ek dost ne kaha: "36 g paani ka volume 2 × 22.4 = 44.8 L hoga." Par 36 g paani 2 mol hai, aur **liquid paani** ka volume lagbhag **36 mL** hota hai (1 g paani = lagbhag 1 mL). Yaani 44.8 L se lagbhag **1200 guna** chhota: ek chhoti katori mein samaa jaata hai. Wajah: liquid mein kan **paas-paas** hote hain, gas mein bahut **door-door**; 22.4 L sirf **gas** ke liye hai. Bhaap ke liye bhi 22.4 L nahi: 273 K par paani gas hi nahi hota, aur 100 °C (373 K) par 1 mol bhaap 1 atm par 22.4 × 373 ÷ 273 = lagbhag **30.6 L** leti hai (Class 9 ka Charles ka niyam). Thos ke saath bhi wahi: CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂ mein sirf **CO₂** ka volume gas ke roop mein ginte, CaO thos hai, uska volume litre mein nahi.

**Galti 2: "Volume 22.4 L ka matlab hamesha 1 mole."**
Ek student ne kaha: "546 K par 44.8 L gas = 2 mol." Par 273 K par 1 mol = 22.4 L, aur 546 K par (do guna taapman, wahi dabao) 1 mol ka volume **44.8 L** ho jaata hai (Class 9 ka [gas-laws-part3.md](../../../class9/chemistry/gas-laws/gas-laws-part3.md) wala niyam). Isliye 546 K par 44.8 L = **1 mol**, 2 mol nahi. Aur kamre ke taapman (lagbhag 300 K) par 1 mol ka volume lagbhag **24.6 L** hai (22.4 × 300 ÷ 273), 22.4 nahi. Isliye sawaal mein hamesha dekho: "**STP par**" likha hai? Agar nahi, to ya to taapman aur dabao diye hone chahiye, ya fir sawaal **volume-volume** (same T, P) hona chahiye, jisme 22.4 ki zaroorat hi nahi padti.

## Quick Recap

- STP par **1 mol gas = 22.4 L** (sirf gas, aur sirf STP par). 1 mol ke teen roop: mass (molar mass), volume (22.4 L), kan (6.022 × 10²³).
- **Mole map (Figure 5.1)**: mass, volume aur kan ke beech hamesha **mole** hai.
- Mass-volume aur volume-mass: balance karo, mole banao, coefficient ka ratio, 22.4 se aana-jaana, jaanch.
- Volume-volume (sab gas, same T, P): coefficient ka ratio hi volume ka ratio; mole mein badalne ki zaroorat nahi.
- Hawa mein lagbhag 21% O₂: **hawa = O₂ ka volume ÷ 0.21**. Propane ke 1 L ke liye lagbhag 24 L hawa.
- Liquid ya thos ka volume 22.4 L nahi hota (36 g paani lagbhag 36 mL). Taapman ya dabao badle to 22.4 L bhi badalta hai.
- Cooking gas ka leak: badboo, khidki kholo, switch nahi, regulator off, gas agency ko phone. Baaki sab kagaz ka hisaab.

Jaanch: left: 29.4 + HCl (0.35 × 36.5 = 12.775) = 42.175 g; right: NaCl 20.475 + H₂O (0.35 × 18 = 6.3) + CO₂ (0.35 × 44 = 15.4) = 42.175 g ✅.

Jaanch: 114.3 × 0.21 = 24 ✅; O atoms: 24 × 2 = 48 = 15 × 2 (CO₂) + 18 × 1 (steam: 6 × 3 = 18 L) ✅. (Pentane jalne wala liquid hai: sirf kagaz par.)

Per litre butane: 130 ÷ 4.2 = lagbhag **31 L hawa** (propane se zyada, kyunki butane mein zyada atom hain). Jaanch: 130 × 0.21 = 27.3 ✅. Butane ka leak ya burner ka kaam kagaz tak, ghar mein sirf safety card.

Jaanch: 0.3 × 44 = 13.2 g CO₂; NaHCO₃ 50.4 = Na₂CO₃ (0.3 × 106 = 31.8) + H₂O (0.3 × 18 = 5.4) + CO₂ 13.2 = 50.4 g ✅.
