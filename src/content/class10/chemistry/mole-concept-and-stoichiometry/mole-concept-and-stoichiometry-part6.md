> **Figure 6.1** – Paanch kadam ka master method (ek udaaharan ke saath). Teeno cheezein (mass, volume, kan) kadam 3 aur kadam 4 mein **mole** ke raste hi aati-jaati hain; kadam 2 mein ratio **mole ka** hai, mass ka nahi.

Har kadam ek line mein:

1. **Balance:** equation likho aur har element ke atom left aur right par gino ([language-of-chemistry-part6.md](../../../class9/chemistry/language-of-chemistry/language-of-chemistry-part6.md) wala kaam). Bina balance ke ratio galat hoga.
2. **Mole ratio:** equation se wo do padarth chuno jo sawaal mein hain (given aur asked), aur unke coefficients ka ratio likho. Ye ratio **mole mein** hai (Topic 4). Agar sab gas hain aur T, P wahi hain, to ye ratio **volume mein** bhi hai (Topic 1).
3. **Given ko mole mein badlo:** mass hai to mass ÷ molar mass; gas ka volume at STP hai to volume ÷ 22.4; kan diye hain to kan ÷ 6.022 × 10²³ (Topic 5 ka mole map).
4. **Asked ka mole nikalo, phir asked ki shakal mein badlo:** mole × ratio, phir mass = mole × M, ya volume = mole × 22.4 L, ya kan = mole × 6.022 × 10²³.
5. **Check:** unit sahi hain? **Mass conservation** (left ka kul mass = right ka kul mass) chalta hai? Jawab mein sensible size hai? Kabhi-kabhi ulta chalna: asked se wapis given tak.

## Part B: Mixed solved examples

**Safety note:** is part ki saari reactions **sirf padhne ke liye** hain ya school lab mein **teacher demo**. Hum yahan sirf **kaagaz par ginti** kar rahe hain: kisi gas ko khud nahi banana, nahi jalana. Dilute acid, thermite (Chapter 2) aur CO jaisi cheezein bina teacher ke kabhi nahi.

### Example 1: Dhatu aur acid: magnesium aur HCl (Chapter 2 ki reaction)

**Sawaal:** 3.6 g magnesium dilute HCl ke saath poori tarah react karta hai (HCl itna hai ki Mg poora ghul jaaye). Kitni H₂ gas (volume at STP aur mass) banegi? Kitna HCl lagega aur kitna MgCl₂ banega?

**Hal (paanch kadam):**
- **Kadam 1:** Mg + 2HCl → MgCl₂ + H₂. Mg 1 = 1, H 2 = 2, Cl 2 = 2 ✅.
- **Kadam 2:** Mg : HCl : MgCl₂ : H₂ = 1 : 2 : 1 : 1.
- **Kadam 3:** n(Mg) = 3.6 ÷ 24 = **0.15 mol**.
- **Kadam 4:** H₂ = 0.15 mol; volume = 0.15 × 22.4 = **3.36 L**; mass = 0.15 × 2 = **0.3 g**. HCl = 0.15 × 2 = 0.3 mol; M(HCl) = 36.5, to 0.3 × 36.5 = **10.95 g**. MgCl₂ = 0.15 mol; M = 24 + 71 = 95, to 0.15 × 95 = **14.25 g**.
- **Kadam 5, Jaanch:** left: 3.6 + 10.95 = 14.55 g. Right: 14.25 + 0.3 = 14.55 g ✅.

### Example 2: Carbonate aur acid: Na₂CO₃ aur HCl (Chapter 1 ki reaction)

**Sawaal:** 10.6 g sodium carbonate dilute HCl se poori tarah react karta hai. Kitni CO₂ (volume at STP) nikalegi, kitna NaCl banega, aur kitna HCl lagega?

**Hal:**
- **Kadam 1:** Na₂CO₃ + 2HCl → 2NaCl + H₂O + CO₂. Na 2 = 2, C 1 = 1, O 3 = 1 + 2 = 3, H 2 = 2, Cl 2 = 2 ✅.
- **Kadam 2:** Na₂CO₃ : HCl : NaCl : CO₂ = 1 : 2 : 2 : 1.
- **Kadam 3:** M(Na₂CO₃) = 46 + 12 + 48 = 106. n = 10.6 ÷ 106 = **0.1 mol**.
- **Kadam 4:** CO₂ = 0.1 mol; volume = 0.1 × 22.4 = **2.24 L**. NaCl = 0.2 mol × 58.5 = **11.7 g**. HCl = 0.2 mol × 36.5 = **7.3 g**.
- **Jaanch:** left 10.6 + 7.3 = 17.9 g. Right: NaCl 11.7 + H₂O (0.1 × 18 = 1.8) + CO₂ (0.1 × 44 = 4.4) = 17.9 g ✅.

### Example 3: Nikaalna (extraction): Fe₂O₃ aur CO (Chapter 3 ka blast furnace)

**Sawaal:** Chhote paimane par kaagaz ki ginti: 80 g Fe₂O₃, CO ke saath, poori tarah reduce hota hai: Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂. Kitna loha (Fe) milega? Kitni CO lagegi, aur kitni CO₂ banegi (mass, aur volume at STP)? Ye sirf padhne ke liye hai: CO zehreeli hai.

**Hal:**
- **Kadam 1:** Fe 2 = 2, C 3 = 3, O 3 + 3 = 6 = 6 ✅.
- **Kadam 2:** Fe₂O₃ : CO : Fe : CO₂ = 1 : 3 : 2 : 3.
- **Kadam 3:** M(Fe₂O₃) = 112 + 48 = 160. n = 80 ÷ 160 = **0.5 mol**.
- **Kadam 4:** Fe = 2 × 0.5 = 1 mol = **56 g**. CO = 1.5 mol × 28 = **42 g**. CO₂ = 1.5 mol × 44 = **66 g**, volume = 1.5 × 22.4 = **33.6 L**.
- **Jaanch:** left 80 + 42 = 122 g; right 56 + 66 = 122 g ✅.

Dhyan do: 1 mol Fe₂O₃ se **2 mol** Fe mila: ye **equation ka coefficient** hai. (Part D mein ek galti is par.)

### Example 4: Paani ka electrolysis: mass se volume tak (Chapter 4)

**Sawaal:** Electrolysis (teacher demo) mein 7.2 g paani poori tarah toot jaata hai: 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂. H₂ aur O₂ ke mole, mass aur volume (STP) batao. Volume ka ratio kya hoga? ([electrolysis-part3.md](../electrolysis/electrolysis-part3.md) mein ye reaction electrons ke saath thi; yahan sirf gino.)

**Hal:**
- **Kadam 1:** H 4 = 4, O 2 = 2 ✅.
- **Kadam 2:** H₂O : H₂ : O₂ = 2 : 2 : 1.
- **Kadam 3:** n(H₂O) = 7.2 ÷ 18 = **0.4 mol**.
- **Kadam 4:** H₂ = 0.4 mol = 0.4 × 2 = **0.8 g**, volume 0.4 × 22.4 = **8.96 L**. O₂ = 0.2 mol = 0.2 × 32 = **6.4 g**, volume 0.2 × 22.4 = **4.48 L**.
- **Jaanch:** 0.8 + 6.4 = 7.2 g ✅. Volume ratio 8.96 : 4.48 = **2 : 1** ✅ (Topic 1 ka Gay-Lussac ka ratio).

### Example 5: Gas aur gas: ethene ki combustion, sirf kaagaz par (Topic 1 aur Topic 5)

**Sawaal:** Ethene (C₂H₄) ki 15 L ki ginti kaagaz par poori tarah jalti hai: C₂H₄ + 3O₂ → 2CO₂ + 2H₂O (steam). Sab gas ek hi T aur P par hain. Kitni O₂, kitni CO₂ aur kitni steam banegi? Aur hawa kitni lagegi (hawa mein lagbhag 21% oxygen, volume mein)?

**Hal:**
- **Kadam 1:** C 2 = 2, H 4 = 4, O 6 = 4 + 2 = 6 ✅.
- **Kadam 2:** gas hain, same T aur P, isliye volume ratio = mole ratio = 1 : 3 : 2 : 2. **Mole mein badalne ki zaroorat nahi** (Topic 1).
- **Kadam 4:** O₂ = 3 × 15 = **45 L**. CO₂ = 2 × 15 = **30 L**. Steam = 2 × 15 = **30 L**.
- **Hawa:** 45 L oxygen, hawa mein 21%: 45 ÷ 0.21 = 214.3, yaani lagbhag **214 L hawa**.
- **Jaanch:** left 15 + 45 = 60 L aur right 30 + 30 = 60 L ban gaya, par ye is reaction ka sanyog hai (volume conserve hone ka niyam nahi: sirf ratio fixed hota hai). Asli check atoms ka hai: oxygen ke "atom-hissa" left 45 × 2 = 90; right 30 × 2 (CO₂) + 30 × 1 (steam) = 90 ✅.

### Example 6: Formula pehle, phir mole: C₃H₆ (Topic 2 aur 3)

**Sawaal:** Ek gaseous hydrocarbon mein 85.7% carbon aur 14.3% hydrogen (mass se) hai, aur uski vapour density 21 hai. Uska molecular formula batao.

**Hal:**
- Maano 100 g: C = 85.7 g, H = 14.3 g.
- Mole: C = 85.7 ÷ 12 = 7.14; H = 14.3 ÷ 1 = 14.3.
- Sabse chhote se bhaag: C = 7.14 ÷ 7.14 = 1; H = 14.3 ÷ 7.14 = 2.0. Empirical formula **CH₂**, uska mass 12 + 2 = 14.
- Molecular mass = 2 × VD = 2 × 21 = **42**.
- n = 42 ÷ 14 = **3**, to molecular formula = (CH₂) × 3 = **C₃H₆**.
- **Jaanch:** M(C₃H₆) = 36 + 6 = 42 ✅. Carbon percent = 36 ÷ 42 × 100 = 85.7% ✅.

### Example 7: Wahi gas jalti hai: mass, volume aur kan (Topic 4 aur 5)

**Sawaal:** Example 6 ki gas C₃H₆ ka 4.2 g poori tarah jalta hai: 2C₃H₆ + 9O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O. Kitna O₂ lagega (g), kitni CO₂ banegi (g aur L), kitna paani banega (g), aur paani ke kitne molecules?

**Hal:**
- **Kadam 1:** C 6 = 6, H 12 = 12, O 18 = 12 + 6 = 18 ✅.
- **Kadam 2:** C₃H₆ : O₂ : CO₂ : H₂O = 2 : 9 : 6 : 6.
- **Kadam 3:** n(C₃H₆) = 4.2 ÷ 42 = **0.1 mol**.
- **Kadam 4:** O₂ = 0.1 × 9 ÷ 2 = 0.45 mol = 0.45 × 32 = **14.4 g**. CO₂ = 0.1 × 6 ÷ 2 = 0.3 mol = 0.3 × 44 = **13.2 g**, volume 0.3 × 22.4 = **6.72 L**. H₂O = 0.3 mol = 0.3 × 18 = **5.4 g** (liquid, ghar ke thande paani ki tarah). Molecules = 0.3 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **1.807 × 10²³**.
- **Jaanch:** left 4.2 + 14.4 = 18.6 g; right 13.2 + 5.4 = 18.6 g ✅.

### Example 8: Thermite (Chapter 2 ki yaad), sirf padhne ke liye

**Sawaal:** Railway track ki welding ki thermite reaction: 2Al + Fe₂O₃ → Al₂O₃ + 2Fe. 13.5 g aluminium ke liye Fe₂O₃ kitna chahiye (exact ratio mein), aur kitna loha aur Al₂O₃ banega? Loha ke kitne atoms? (Ye reaction bahut garam hoti hai: sirf professional, aur hum sirf kaagaz par.)

**Hal:**
- **Kadam 1:** Al 2 = 2, Fe 2 = 2, O 3 = 3 ✅.
- **Kadam 2:** Al : Fe₂O₃ : Al₂O₃ : Fe = 2 : 1 : 1 : 2.
- **Kadam 3:** n(Al) = 13.5 ÷ 27 = **0.5 mol**.
- **Kadam 4:** Fe₂O₃ = 0.25 mol × 160 = **40 g**. Al₂O₃ = 0.25 mol × 102 = **25.5 g**. Fe = 0.5 mol × 56 = **28 g**; atoms = 0.5 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **3.011 × 10²³**.
- **Jaanch:** left 13.5 + 40 = 53.5 g; right 25.5 + 28 = 53.5 g ✅.

### Example 9: Ulta sawaal: gas ke volume se reactant ka mass

**Sawaal:** Kaagaz par: 26.88 L hydrogen (at STP) banani hai, aluminium aur dilute HCl se: 2Al + 6HCl → 2AlCl₃ + 3H₂ (sirf padhne ke liye; hydrogen jalne wali gas hai). Kitna Al aur kitna HCl lagega, aur kitna AlCl₃ banega?

**Hal:**
- **Kadam 1:** Al 2 = 2, H 6 = 6, Cl 6 = 6 ✅.
- **Kadam 2:** Al : HCl : AlCl₃ : H₂ = 2 : 6 : 2 : 3. Ab **asked** pehle ke reactants hain, given H₂ hai: ratio ko ulta padho.
- **Kadam 3:** n(H₂) = 26.88 ÷ 22.4 = **1.2 mol**.
- **Kadam 4:** Al = 1.2 × 2 ÷ 3 = 0.8 mol = 0.8 × 27 = **21.6 g**. HCl = 1.2 × 6 ÷ 3 = 2.4 mol = 2.4 × 36.5 = **87.6 g**. AlCl₃ = 0.8 mol × 133.5 = **106.8 g**.
- **Jaanch:** left 21.6 + 87.6 = 109.2 g; right 106.8 + (1.2 × 2 = 2.4) = 109.2 g ✅.

Ye nau problems alag-alag dikhti hain, par har ek mein **kadam 3 mein mass ya volume mole bana** aur **kadam 4 mein mole se wapis** gaya. Pul hamesha mole hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

Topic 4 ki galti ("masses ka seedha ratio") aur Topic 5 ki galti ("22.4 L har cheez ke liye") alag hain: yahan wo galtiyan hain jo mixed problems mein sabse zyada hoti hain.

**Galti 1: "2 mol H₂O ka mass 18 g hai."**
Saee ke buzzer wali galti. Dekho kaise pakadni hai: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O mein **do mole** paani bana. 18 g **ek** mole ka mass hai. Do mole = 2 × 18 = **36 g**. Jaanch: reactants 2 mol H₂ (4 g) + 1 mol O₂ (32 g) = 36 g. Agar paani ka mass 18 g hota, to 18 g gayab ho jaate, jabki mass banta-bigadta nahi (conservation). Niyam: jab bhi koi coefficient 2, 3 ... ho, **mole × M** ek hi baar karo, aur coefficient ko mass mat samjho.

**Galti 2: "Atomic mass aur molar mass ek hi cheez hain, unit se kya farq padta hai."**
Magnesium ka atomic mass 24 u hai: **ek** atom ka. Ek Mg atom ka asli mass 24 ÷ 6.022 × 10²³ = lagbhag 3.99 × 10⁻²³ g hota hai: bahut chhota. **Molar mass** 24 g/mol hai: **6.022 × 10²³ atoms** ka mass. Sawaal: "0.5 mol Mg ka mass?" Galat jawab: 0.5 × 24 u = 12 u (ye ek atom ka hota hai, aur 0.5 mol ke liye bekaar). Sahi: 0.5 × 24 g = **12 g**. Gram wala hisaab tab, jab **mole** ginte ho; u wala jab **ek atom ya ek molecule** ginte ho. Dono ke number wahi, par ikai alag aur matlab alag.

**Galti 3: "Empirical formula hamesha dikhne wale formula se chhota hota hai."**
Kai students empirical formula ko "kuch kaat-chhaant" samajhte hain aur har formula ko chhota karne ki koshish karte hain. Dekho: glucose C₆H₁₂O₆ sabko 6 se bhaag lagta hai, isliye empirical CH₂O. Par **H₂O**: 2 : 1 ko aur chhota nahi kar sakte (whole number mein), isliye uska empirical formula bhi H₂O hai. CO₂, NH₃, CH₄ ka bhi yahi haal hai. Empirical formula **sabse chhota whole-number ratio** hai, koi zabardasti ka kaata hua nahi. Ionic compounds (NaCl, CaCl₂) ke formula pehle se hi empirical hote hain, kyunki wahan alag molecule hota hi nahi.

**Galti 4: "Vapour density ka bhi unit hota hai, g ya g per L."**
Ek student ne likha: "CO₂ ki VD = 22 g." Dekho: VD do **mass ka ratio** hai: CO₂ ke kisi volume ka mass ÷ hydrogen ke utne hi volume ka mass. Gram ÷ gram = **koi unit nahi**. Aur ye **density** (g per L) se alag hai: STP par CO₂ ki density = 44 ÷ 22.4 = 1.96 g per L, jabki VD = 22. Density mein 22.4 L ka bhaag hai, VD mein hydrogen se tulna. Isliye VD ke aage "g" ya "g per L" kabhi mat likho: sirf number likho (VD = 22).

**Galti 5: "Equation ka coefficient aur formula ke andar ka chhota number ek hi cheez hain."**
Fe₂O₃ mein neeche likha chhota 2 hai: **ek unit mein 2 Fe atom**. Equation mein Fe ke aage 2 coefficient hai: **2 mole Fe**. Example 3 dekho: 1 mol Fe₂O₃ mein 2 mol Fe atoms **maujood hain** (formula ka chhota number), aur reduce hone par **2 mol Fe** bane (coefficient): dono sach, par alag jagah. Ek student ne 3 mol CO ke liye socha "3 g CO" kyunki coefficient 3 hai: **coefficient mole hai, gram nahi**. 3 mol CO = 3 × 28 = 84 g. Formula ka chhota number kisi molecule ke andar atom ginata hai; coefficient ye batata hai ki us molecule ke kitne mole reaction mein hain.

## Quick Recap

- **Master method:** (1) balance, (2) mole ratio, (3) diye hue ko mole mein, (4) asked ka mole phir uski shakal, (5) check.
- **Pul mole hai:** mass ÷ M, volume ÷ 22.4 L (gas, at STP), kan ÷ 6.022 × 10²³.
- **Coefficient mole hai**, gram nahi; **sab gas aur same T, P** ho to volume ratio = mole ratio.
- **Check:** mass in = mass out; unit; sensible size.
- Mixed sawaal mein Topic 2 aur 3: VD se M (M = 2 × VD), phir empirical aur molecular formula.
- Galtiyan: 2 mol paani 36 g hai; u aur g/mol alag; empirical formula kabhi-kabhi wahi rehta hai; VD ka koi unit nahi; coefficient aur formula ka chhota number alag.
- Safety: CO, thermite aur dilute acid ki reactions sirf padhne ke liye ya teacher demo.

## Practice Questions

Sab reactions **sirf padhne ke liye** ya teacher demo hain. Atomic masses Class 9 wali, molar volume 22.4 L at STP.

1. 16.2 g of aluminium reacts completely with dilute sulphuric acid: 2Al + 3H₂SO₄ → Al₂(SO₄)₃ + 3H₂. Find the volume of hydrogen at STP, the mass of H₂SO₄ needed and the mass of Al₂(SO₄)₃ formed. Check that mass is conserved.
2. 42 g of magnesium carbonate (MgCO₃) reacts completely with dilute HCl: MgCO₃ + 2HCl → MgCl₂ + H₂O + CO₂. Find the volume of CO₂ at STP, the mass of MgCl₂ and the mass of HCl needed.
3. In a paper exercise, 39.75 g of copper(II) oxide is reduced by carbon monoxide: CuO + CO → Cu + CO₂. Find the mass of copper, the mass of CO needed and the volume of CO₂ formed at STP. (CO is poisonous: paper only.)
4. In an electrolysis demonstration, 8.4 L of hydrogen is collected at STP from 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂. Find the volume of oxygen, the mass of water decomposed and the mass of oxygen. Check with mass conservation.
5. A liquid contains 24.24% carbon, 4.04% hydrogen and 71.72% chlorine by mass, and its vapour has a vapour density of 49.5. Find its empirical formula and its molecular formula. (Cl = 35.5; the liquid is poisonous: paper only.)
6. 4 g of methane burns completely: CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O. Find the mass of oxygen needed, the mass of carbon dioxide formed, and the number of water molecules formed. Check mass conservation.
7. 36 L of carbon monoxide gas is burnt in a paper exercise: 2CO + O₂ → 2CO₂, all gases at the same temperature and pressure. Find the volume of oxygen needed, the volume of carbon dioxide formed and the volume of air needed (air has about 21% oxygen by volume).
8. A compound has 56.4% phosphorus and 43.6% oxygen by mass, and its vapour density is 110. Find its empirical and molecular formulas. (P = 31, O = 16.)
9. In a teacher demonstration, 28 g of potassium hydroxide is neutralised completely by dilute sulphuric acid: H₂SO₄ + 2KOH → K₂SO₄ + 2H₂O. Find the moles of KOH, the mass of H₂SO₄ needed, the mass of K₂SO₄ formed and the mass of water formed. Check mass conservation. (KOH is caustic.)
10. 12.25 g of potassium chlorate is heated (a paper question only: KClO₃ is a strong oxidiser, never heated by students): 2KClO₃ → 2KCl + 3O₂. Find the mass of KCl left and the volume of oxygen at STP.
11. A gas collected from a decomposition has a mass of 7.0 g in a volume of 2.24 L at STP. Find its molar mass and its vapour density. Is it lighter or heavier than air (take the vapour density of air as about 14.5)? Would a balloon filled with it float up or sink?
12. State True or False, and correct the false ones: (a) The mass of 3 mol of water is 18 g. (b) The vapour density of oxygen is 16 g. (c) The empirical formula of ethane, C₂H₆, is CH₃. (d) A coefficient 2 in front of HCl means 2 g of HCl. (e) In 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O (steam) at the same T and P, 10 L of H₂ gives 10 L of steam. (f) The atomic mass of Cu is 63.5 g.

### Answers

1. Kadam 1: 2Al + 3H₂SO₄ → Al₂(SO₄)₃ + 3H₂ (Al 2 = 2, S 3 = 3, O 12 = 12, H 6 = 6 ✅). n(Al) = 16.2 ÷ 27 = **0.6 mol**. Ratio Al : H₂SO₄ : Al₂(SO₄)₃ : H₂ = 2 : 3 : 1 : 3. H₂ = 0.9 mol, volume = 0.9 × 22.4 = **20.16 L**. H₂SO₄ = 0.9 mol × 98 = **88.2 g**. Al₂(SO₄)₃ = 0.3 mol × 342 = **102.6 g** (M = 54 + 96 × 3 = 342). H₂ mass = 0.9 × 2 = 1.8 g. **Jaanch:** left 16.2 + 88.2 = 104.4 g; right 102.6 + 1.8 = 104.4 g ✅.
2. MgCO₃ + 2HCl → MgCl₂ + H₂O + CO₂ (Mg 1 = 1, C 1 = 1, O 3 = 1 + 2, H 2 = 2, Cl 2 = 2 ✅). M(MgCO₃) = 24 + 12 + 48 = 84, n = 42 ÷ 84 = **0.5 mol**. CO₂ = 0.5 mol, volume = **11.2 L**. MgCl₂ = 0.5 × 95 = **47.5 g**. HCl = 1 mol × 36.5 = **36.5 g**. **Jaanch:** left 42 + 36.5 = 78.5 g; right 47.5 + 9 (0.5 mol H₂O) + 22 (0.5 mol CO₂) = 78.5 g ✅.
3. CuO + CO → Cu + CO₂ (1 = 1 ✅). M(CuO) = 63.5 + 16 = 79.5, n = 39.75 ÷ 79.5 = **0.5 mol**. Ratio 1 : 1 : 1 : 1. Cu = 0.5 × 63.5 = **31.75 g**. CO = 0.5 × 28 = **14 g**. CO₂ volume = 0.5 × 22.4 = **11.2 L**. **Jaanch:** 39.75 + 14 = 53.75 g; Cu 31.75 + CO₂ (0.5 × 44 = 22) = 53.75 g ✅.
4. H₂ = 8.4 L ÷ 22.4 = **0.375 mol**. 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂ (H 4 = 4, O 2 = 2 ✅): H₂O : H₂ : O₂ = 2 : 2 : 1. Oxygen = 0.1875 mol; volume = **4.2 L** (volume ratio 2 : 1: 8.4 ÷ 2 = 4.2 ✅). Mass of water = 0.375 × 18 = **6.75 g**. Mass of O₂ = 0.1875 × 32 = **6 g**. **Jaanch:** water 6.75 g = H₂ (0.375 × 2 = 0.75 g) + O₂ 6 g ✅.
5. Maano 100 g: C = 24.24 ÷ 12 = 2.02; H = 4.04 ÷ 1 = 4.04; Cl = 71.72 ÷ 35.5 = 2.02. Sabse chhote se bhaag: C = 1, H = 2, Cl = 1. Empirical formula **CH₂Cl**, mass 12 + 2 + 35.5 = 49.5. Molecular mass = 2 × 49.5 = 99. n = 99 ÷ 49.5 = **2**, molecular formula **C₂H₄Cl₂**. **Jaanch:** C₂H₄Cl₂ = 24 + 4 + 71 = 99 ✅; C percent = 24 ÷ 99 × 100 = 24.24% ✅. (Ye liquid zehreela hai: sirf kaagaz par.)
6. CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O (C 1 = 1, H 4 = 4, O 4 = 2 + 2 ✅). n(CH₄) = 4 ÷ 16 = **0.25 mol**. O₂ = 0.5 mol × 32 = **16 g**. CO₂ = 0.25 mol × 44 = **11 g**. H₂O = 0.5 mol (18 × 0.5 = 9 g); molecules = 0.5 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **3.011 × 10²³**. **Jaanch:** left 4 + 16 = 20 g; right 11 + 9 = 20 g ✅.
7. 2CO + O₂ → 2CO₂ (C 2 = 2, O 4 = 4 ✅). Sab gas, same T aur P: ratio 2 : 1 : 2. O₂ = 36 ÷ 2 = **18 L**. CO₂ = **36 L**. Hawa = 18 ÷ 0.21 = 85.7, yaani lagbhag **86 L**. **Jaanch:** left 36 + 18 = 54 L; right 36 L (volume ghata, jo is reaction mein hota hai); C atoms 36 = 36 ✅. (CO zehreeli hai, sirf kaagaz par.)
8. Maano 100 g: P = 56.4 ÷ 31 = 1.819; O = 43.6 ÷ 16 = 2.725. Sabse chhote se bhaag: P = 1; O = 2.725 ÷ 1.819 = 1.5. Ratio 1 : 1.5 ko 2 se guna karo: **2 : 3**. Empirical **P₂O₃**, mass 62 + 48 = 110. Molecular mass = 2 × 110 = 220. n = 220 ÷ 110 = **2**, molecular formula **P₄O₆**. **Jaanch:** P₄O₆ = 124 + 96 = 220 ✅; P percent = 62 ÷ 110 × 100 = 56.4% ✅. (Phosphorus ke oxide zehreele aur tez hote hain: sirf kaagaz par.)
9. H₂SO₄ + 2KOH → K₂SO₄ + 2H₂O (H 4 = 4, S 1 = 1, O 6 = 6, K 2 = 2 ✅). M(KOH) = 39 + 16 + 1 = 56, n(KOH) = 28 ÷ 56 = **0.5 mol**. Ratio H₂SO₄ : KOH : K₂SO₄ : H₂O = 1 : 2 : 1 : 2. H₂SO₄ = 0.25 mol × 98 = **24.5 g**. K₂SO₄ = 0.25 mol × 174 = **43.5 g** (M = 78 + 32 + 64 = 174). Water = 0.5 mol × 18 = **9 g**. **Jaanch:** left 28 + 24.5 = 52.5 g; right 43.5 + 9 = 52.5 g ✅.
10. 2KClO₃ → 2KCl + 3O₂ (K 2 = 2, Cl 2 = 2, O 6 = 6 ✅). M(KClO₃) = 39 + 35.5 + 48 = 122.5, n = 12.25 ÷ 122.5 = **0.1 mol**. KCl = 0.1 mol × 74.5 = **7.45 g**. O₂ = 0.15 mol, volume = 0.15 × 22.4 = **3.36 L** (mass 0.15 × 32 = 4.8 g). **Jaanch:** 7.45 + 4.8 = 12.25 g ✅. (Garm karne ka kaam sirf teacher ka.)
11. n = 2.24 ÷ 22.4 = 0.1 mol. M = 7.0 ÷ 0.1 = **70 g/mol**. VD = 70 ÷ 2 = **35** (koi unit nahi). Hawa ki VD lagbhag 14.5 hai, to ye gas **bhaari** hai, isliye gubbara **neeche baithega (sink)**. **Jaanch:** 0.1 × 70 = 7.0 g ✅.
12. (a) **False** ❌: 3 mol paani = 3 × 18 = 54 g (18 g ek mol ka hai). (b) **False** ❌: VD ka koi unit nahi; O₂ ki VD 16 hai. (c) **True** ✅: C₂H₆ ko 2 se bhaag, CH₃. (d) **False** ❌: coefficient 2 matlab 2 **mole** HCl, yaani 2 × 36.5 = 73 g. (e) **True** ✅: H₂ : H₂O = 2 : 2, to 10 L H₂ se 10 L steam (volume ratio mole ratio ke barabar, same T aur P par). (f) **False** ❌: atomic mass 63.5 **u** hai; molar mass 63.5 g/mol hai.
