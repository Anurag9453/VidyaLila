# Topic 1: Gay-Lussac aur Avogadro: Gas Ka Ginti Ka Niyam

Pichhle chapter ke aakhir mein sawaal tha: chemist ginta kaise hai? Class 9 mein humne ginti ka ek pul banaya tha: **mole**. 1 mole mein 6.022 × 10²³ kan, aur STP par 1 mole gas ka volume 22.4 L. Electrolysis mein bhi 2 : 1 volume ka H₂ : O₂ dikha tha. Ab hum is ginti ko **equation** se jodte hain. Iska pehla kadam sabse seedha hai: **gaso ke volume**. Kuch gaso ke reaction mein volume hamesha **chhoti, poori sankhyaon** ke ratio mein milte hain, aur is topic mein hum dekhenge ki ye kyun hota hai. Isi se ye bhi pata chalega ki hydrogen aur oxygen jaisi gas **do-do atom ke molecule** kyun hoti hain.

## Chai wale ka "do paani, ek doodh"

Mr. Zende apni bhanji Sanchi ko bus-stand ke paas ek chai stall par le gaye. Chaiwale kaka ke paas ek hi recipe thi: **2 cup paani aur 1 cup doodh**. Ek chhote pot mein 2 cup paani aur 1 cup doodh; bade pot mein 6 cup paani aur 3 cup doodh; shaadi ke order mein 60 cup paani aur 30 cup doodh. Pot kitna bhi bada ho, **ratio 2 : 1 hi rehta hai**.

Sanchi ne hisaab lagaya: "Mama, chhote pot mein kul 3 cup, bade mein 9 cup. Par ratio hamesha 2 : 1. Kaka ko kabhi 'saade teen cup paani' ya 'ek aur chauthai cup doodh' nahi chahiye hota!" Mr. Zende hanse: "Bilkul. Ab ek ajeeb baat suno. Gaso ke reaction mein bhi aisa hi hota hai. Hydrogen aur oxygen milkar steam banate hain: **2 litre hydrogen ko hamesha 1 litre oxygen chahiye**, 2 litre steam bante hain. 20 litre hydrogen ho to 10 litre oxygen. Kabhi 'saade 1.37 litre' nahi. Chai ki recipe ki tarah **gaso ki bhi ek recipe hoti hai, aur wo volume mein likhi jaati hai**."

Sanchi ne puchha: "Par Mama, chai ka ratio to kaka ki marzi se hai. Gas ko kaun batata hai ki 2 : 1 hi lena hai?" Yahi is topic ka sawaal hai: gaso ki recipe **kaun likhta hai**, aur wo hamesha poori sankhya mein kyun aati hai?

**Ghar par adult ke saath (chai banaye bina):** do alag size ke glass ya katori lo. Ek ko "ek cup" maan kar thanda paani aur thanda doodh 2 : 1 mein milao. Phir doosri size ki katori ko "ek cup" maano aur wahi ratio dohrao. Jab tak **ek hi katori** se dono naapte ho, ratio 2 : 1 rehta hai. Do alag size ki katori se naapoge to ratio bigad jayega. Is topic mein bhi, **sab gaso ke volume ek hi taapman aur dabao par** naapne honge, warna ratio bigad jayega.

(Hot chai ya gas par kuch bhi sirf adult banata hai. Is topic mein koi gas ka reaction karna nahi hai: sab **sirf padhne ke liye, kagaz par** hai.)

## Part A: Gay-Lussac ka niyam: gaso ki volume recipe

**Pehle ek imaandari ki baat.** Class 9 ke [gas-laws-part4.md](../../../class9/chemistry/gas-laws/gas-laws-part4.md) mein "Gay-Lussac ka niyam" ka matlab tha **dabao aur taapman** ka rishta (P₁/T₁ = P₂/T₂). Yahan Gay-Lussac ka **doosra** niyam hai, jo gaso ke **milte hue volumes** ke baare mein hai. Ek hi vaigyanik, do alag niyam. Dono naam dekh kar ghabrao mat: is chapter mein "Gay-Lussac ka niyam" ka matlab hamesha **volumes ka ratio** hoga.

Fransisi vaigyanik Joseph Louis Gay-Lussac ne **1808** mein prayog karke dekha ki gaso ke reaction mein volumes ka ratio chhoti poori sankhyaon mein aata hai:

**Niyam:** same taapman aur dabao par naapne par, reaction mein bhaag lene wali gaso ke volumes, aur banne wali gas ke volume, aapas mein **simple whole-number ratio** mein hote hain.

| Reaction (equation) | Volume ratio | Ek line mein |
|---|---|---|
| H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl | 1 : 1 : 2 | 1 L H₂ aur 1 L Cl₂ se 2 L HCl |
| 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O (steam) | 2 : 1 : 2 | 2 L H₂ aur 1 L O₂ se 2 L steam |
| N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃ | 1 : 3 : 2 | 1 L N₂ aur 3 L H₂ se 2 L NH₃ |
| CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O (steam) | 1 : 2 : 1 : 2 | 1 L methane ko 2 L O₂, 1 L CO₂ aur 2 L steam |

Dekho, har ratio mein sankhyayein **wahi hain jo equation ke coefficient** hain. Ye sanyog nahi hai: Part C mein dekhenge ki kyun.

**Teen savdhaniyan (paper reasoning ke niyam):**
1. Sabhi volume **ek hi taapman aur dabao** par ginte hain. Steam ko bhi usi taapman par steam hi rehna hai.
2. Ratio sirf **gaso** ke liye hai. Agar product liquid ya solid ho (jaise thanda hone par paani), to uska volume bahut chhota hota hai aur ratio mein nahi ginte.
3. Ratio **volume** ka hai, **mass** (gram) ka nahi. Mass ka ratio alag cheez hai, wo Topic 4 mein dekhenge.

```
  H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl : teen dibbe-bhar ki ginti, har dibba barabar size ka

  +---------------+   +---------------+         +---------------+  +---------------+
  :    H₂ H₂ H₂   : + :  Cl₂ Cl₂ Cl₂  :  ====>  :  HCl HCl HCl  :  :  HCl HCl HCl  :
  +---------------+   +---------------+         +---------------+  +---------------+

    1 hissa H₂         1 hissa Cl₂                   2 hissa HCl
    (3 molecule)       (3 molecule)                  (3 + 3 = 6 molecule)
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Barabar size ke dibbe, same taapman aur dabao: 1 hissa H₂ (3 molecule) aur 1 hissa Cl₂ (3 molecule) se 2 hissa HCl bante hain. Har dibbe mein 3 molecule hain (Avogadro ka niyam), isliye 3 + 3 = 6 molecule HCl do dibbon mein baat gaye. Drawing sirf nidarshan hai, asli ginti 10²³ ke paimane par hoti hai. (Chlorine zehreeli hai: ye sirf kagaz ka chitra hai.)

## Part B: "Aadha molecule" ki paheli, aur Avogadro ka jawab

Gay-Lussac ke nateeje bahut saaf the, par ek **ulajhan** thi. Us samay maana jaata tha ki hydrogen, chlorine jaisi gas ke kan **akele atom** hote hain (H, Cl). Is soch se dekho kya hota:

- 1 volume hydrogen + 1 volume chlorine → **2 volume** HCl.
- Barabar volume mein barabar kan (jaise Class 9 mein dekha). Maan lo 1 volume mein n kan hain. To n H aur n Cl se 2 volume HCl, yaani **2n kan HCl** bane.
- 2n HCl ke liye 2n H aur 2n Cl atom chahiye, par hamare paas sirf n H aur n Cl hain. Har H aur Cl atom ko **do bhaagon mein tootna** padega: "aadha atom". Aur atom ko kaatna possible nahi!

Is paheli ko **1811** mein Italy ke **Amedeo Avogadro** ne suljhaya. Unka vichar: hydrogen aur chlorine ke kan **akele atom nahi, do-do atom ke jode (molecule)** hain: H₂ aur Cl₂. Ab ek molecule ke toot kar do bhaag hona koi ajeeb baat nahi: **molecule toot sakta hai, atom nahi**.

```
        1 molecule H₂        1 molecule Cl₂         2 molecule HCl

            H-H        +        Cl-Cl     ====>      H-Cl     H-Cl

   H₂ ke do atom alag hue, Cl₂ ke do atom alag hue, aur
   H-Cl ke do naye jode bane. Atom kul: 2 H aur 2 Cl, pehle bhi, baad mein bhi.
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Ek H₂ aur ek Cl₂ ke do-do atom alag hote hain aur do HCl molecule banate hain. Har HCl mein ek H aur ek Cl. 1 molecule H₂ + 1 molecule Cl₂ yaani volume mein 1 : 1, aur 2 molecule HCl yaani volume mein 2: Gay-Lussac ka 1 : 1 : 2 isi se aata hai. Koi "aadha atom" nahi.

**Avogadro ka kadam (1811):** (1) same taapman aur dabao par barabar volume mein **barabar molecule**; (2) hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen, chlorine ke molecule **do atom ke** (diatomic). In do baaton se Gay-Lussac ka niyam seedha nikalta hai. Dilchasp baat: Avogadro ki baat **kai dashak tak** maani nahi gayi (lagbhag 50 saal baad hi sab ne apnayi). Itna waqt lag gaya ek simple vichar ko jagah milne mein.

**Niyam kyun chalta hai (sabse mukhya baat):**
Barabar volume = barabar molecule (Avogadro). To gas ka **volume ratio = molecule ratio = mole ratio = equation ke coefficient ka ratio**. Isliye Gay-Lussac ke ratio hamesha poori sankhya mein hote hain: kyunki molecules gine jaate hain, aur molecule ki ginti poori sankhya hoti hai. Chai wale ke "2 cup paani" ki tarah, gas ka "2 volume" bhi "2 hissa" hai.

## Part C: Atomicity: molecule mein kitne atom?

Ab ye bhi samjho ki oxygen **O₂** kyun likhte hain, **O** nahi. Ek reaction lo: 2 L hydrogen + 1 L oxygen → 2 L steam. Maan lo 1 L mein 2 molecule hain (sirf ginti ke liye, jaisa Figure 1.3 mein):

- Hydrogen: 4 molecule. Oxygen: 2 molecule. Steam: 4 molecule.
- 4 steam molecule mein har ek mein kam se kam 1 oxygen atom, yaani **kam se kam 4 oxygen atom** chahiye.
- Par oxygen ke sirf **2 molecule** the. 2 molecule se 4 atom tabhi aa sakte hain jab har oxygen molecule mein **2 atom** ho.

```
  Volume mein:   2 hissa H₂    +   1 hissa O₂   →   2 hissa H₂O (steam)

  Molecule (har hissa = 2 molecule):

   +---------------+     +---------------+       +---------------+
   :   H-H   H-H   :     :               :       : H-O-H   H-O-H :
   +---------------+     :   O=O   O=O   :       +---------------+
   +---------------+     :               :       +---------------+
   :   H-H   H-H   :     +---------------+       : H-O-H   H-O-H :
   +---------------+                             +---------------+

   H atom: 8 (4 molecule x 2)    O atom: 4 (2 molecule x 2)    H atom: 8, O atom: 4
```

> **Figure 1.3** – 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O (steam). Barabar size ke dibbe (har ek mein 2 molecule). Baayein: 2 dibbe H₂ (4 molecule, 8 H atom) aur 1 dibba O₂ (2 molecule, 4 O atom). Daayein: 2 dibbe steam (4 molecule), har molecule mein 2 H aur 1 O: 8 H aur 4 O atom. Atom bhi bache, volume bhi 2 : 1 : 2. Har O₂ molecule do steam molecule mein ek-ek O atom deta hai, isliye 1 hissa O₂ se 2 hissa steam banti hai.

**Atomicity ki table:**

| Prakar | Molecule mein atom | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Monoatomic | 1 | He, Ne, Ar (noble gas) |
| Diatomic | 2 | H₂, O₂, N₂, Cl₂ |
| Triatomic | 3 | O₃ (ozone) |
| Polyatomic | 3 se zyada | P₄ (4 atom), S₈ (8 atom) |

Ek hi tatva ki alag atomicity alag cheez deti hai: O₂ (hum saans mein lete hain) aur O₃ (ozone, kam matra mein, tez gandh) dono sirf oxygen hain. Noble gas jaise helium akele atom ki tarah chalti hain, isliye He ka formula bas He hai, He₂ nahi.

## Part D: Equation ko volume ki recipe ki tarah padhna

Ab chai wale ki recipe wapas. Equation ek recipe hai, aur gaso ke liye uske coefficient **volume ka ratio** bhi dete hain:

**Tareeka (3 kadam):**
1. **Balanced equation** likho (atom ginkar).
2. Gaso ke coefficient padho: wahi **volume ratio** hai (same taapman aur dabao par).
3. Diye hue volume ko ratio se **scale** karo (jaise 2 cup paani : 1 cup doodh se 60 : 30).

Is tareeke mein **mole ya gram nikaalne ki zaroorat nahi** padti, jab sab kuch gas ho aur volume diye ho. Ye Topic 5 mein bhi kaam aayega.

## Part E: Solved examples (sab kagaz par, sab same taapman aur dabao par)

(Sab gas wale reaction **sirf padhne ke liye** hain. Koi bhi gas jalana, collect karna ya cylinder chhoona student ka kaam nahi.)

### Example 1: Methane ko kitni oxygen chahiye?

**Sawaal:** 20 L methane (CH₄) poori tarah jalti hai (sirf kagaz par). Kitni oxygen lagegi, aur kitni CO₂ aur steam banegi? Kul volume pehle aur baad mein kya hai?

**Hal:**
- CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O(g). Atom ginti: C 1 = 1, H 4 = 4, O 4 = 2 + 2 = 4.
- Ratio 1 : 2 : 1 : 2. 20 L CH₄ (1 hissa = 20 L): O₂ = 2 × 20 = **40 L**, CO₂ = **20 L**, steam = 2 × 20 = **40 L**.
- Pehle kul: 20 + 40 = 60 L. Baad mein kul: 20 + 40 = 60 L. Volume **jaisa ka taisa**.
- **Jaanch:** coefficient ka kul 1 + 2 = 3 baayein, 1 + 2 = 3 daayein: volume nahi badla ✅

### Example 2: Ethyne (acetylene) aur oxygen

**Sawaal:** Welding mein jalne wali ethyne (C₂H₂) gas ke 10 L ko poori tarah jalane ke liye kitni oxygen chahiye aur kitni CO₂ aur steam banegi? (Sirf padhne ke liye: ye gas bahut jwalansheel hai.)

**Hal:**
- 2C₂H₂ + 5O₂ → 4CO₂ + 2H₂O(g). Atom: C 4 = 4, H 4 = 4, O 10 = 8 + 2 = 10.
- Ratio 2 : 5 : 4 : 2. 10 L C₂H₂ ka matlab "1 hissa = 5 L" (kyunki 2 hissa = 10 L).
- O₂ = 5 × 5 = **25 L**, CO₂ = 4 × 5 = **20 L**, steam = 2 × 5 = **10 L**.
- Kul pehle 10 + 25 = 35 L, baad mein 20 + 10 = 30 L: volume **ghata**, kyunki coefficient 2 + 5 = 7 se 4 + 2 = 6 hue.
- **Jaanch:** 10 : 25 : 20 : 10 = 2 : 5 : 4 : 2 ✅

### Example 3: Carbon monoxide ka CO₂ banna

**Sawaal:** 30 L carbon monoxide (CO) se, oxygen mein jal kar CO₂ banti hai. Kitni oxygen lagegi aur kitni CO₂ banegi? Kul volume kitna ghata? (CO **zehreeli** gas hai: sirf padhne ke liye.)

**Hal:**
- 2CO + O₂ → 2CO₂. Atom: C 2 = 2, O 2 + 2 = 4 = 4.
- Ratio 2 : 1 : 2. 30 L CO = 2 hissa, to 1 hissa = 15 L. O₂ = **15 L**, CO₂ = 2 × 15 = **30 L**.
- Pehle 30 + 15 = 45 L. Baad mein 30 L. Volume **15 L ghata**, yaani ek tihai.
- **Jaanch:** 30 : 15 : 30 = 2 : 1 : 2 ✅

### Example 4: Ratio dekh kar equation aur atomicity nikalo

**Sawaal:** Bijli kadakne par hawa ki nitrogen aur oxygen milkar nitric oxide banati hain (sirf padhne ke liye). Prayog mein 25 mL nitrogen aur 25 mL oxygen se **50 mL** nitric oxide mila (same taapman aur dabao par). Ratio nikalo. Dono gas ki atomicity kya hai, agar nitric oxide ka formula NO hai?

**Hal:**
- Ratio 25 : 25 : 50 = **1 : 1 : 2**.
- Barabar volume mein barabar molecule. Maan lo 1 hissa = n molecule. To 2n molecule NO bane, har ek mein 1 N aur 1 O: kul **2n N atom** aur **2n O atom** chahiye.
- Nitrogen ke sirf n molecule the, to har nitrogen molecule mein 2n ÷ n = **2 atom**. Wahi oxygen ke liye. Dono **diatomic**: N₂ + O₂ → 2NO.
- **Jaanch (atom):** baayein N 2, O 2; daayein 2 × (N 1, O 1) = N 2, O 2 ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Gay-Lussac ka niyam" ko dabao-taapman wala samajh lena.**
Maano ek dost ne likha: "Gay-Lussac ke niyam se 20 L H₂ ko 10 L O₂ chahiye kyunki P₁/T₁ = P₂/T₂." Is formula mein volume hai hi nahi! Ye wahi Class 9 wala niyam hai jisme volume constant rehta hai. Yahan volumes ka **ratio** chahiye. Pehchaan: jahan do **gas milte** hain aur coefficient ki baat hai, wahan combining volumes wala niyam; jahan ek hi gas ka P aur T badal raha hai, wahan Class 9 wala.

**Galti 2: "Reaction ke baad volume wahi rehna chahiye."**
Example 1 mein volume 60 se 60 raha, to koi sochega ki hamesha ayega. Par Example 2 mein 35 se 30 hua, Example 3 mein 45 se 30. Volume **molecule ki ginti** ke saath chalta hai: jahan coefficient ka kul ghata, volume ghata; jahan badha (jaise 2NH₃ → N₂ + 3H₂, 2 se 4), volume badha. Volume ka nahi, **atoms ka bachna** niyam hai.

**Galti 3: Oxygen ko "O" likh kar ginti karna.**
Maano ek student ne likha: H₂ + O → H₂O, aur kaha "1 L H₂ aur 1 L O se 1 L steam". Par prayog mein 2 L H₂ ko sirf **1 L** oxygen lagti hai aur steam 2 L banti hai. Student ka ratio 1 : 1 : 1 hai, prayog ka 2 : 1 : 2. Sahi equation 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O hai: oxygen **diatomic O₂** hai, aur ek O₂ molecule do steam molecule ko ek-ek atom deta hai.

**Galti 4: Taapman badalkar ratio lagana, ya steam ko thanda kar ke gin lena.**
Example 1 mein 20 L CH₄ ke saath 40 L steam bani, jo tabhi hai jab sab kuch ek hi garam taapman par naapa jaaye (steam gas hi rahe). Agar hum products ko thanda kar den, to steam wapas paani ban jaati hai aur uska volume bahut chhota ho jata hai: sirf CO₂ ke 20 L bachte hain. Isliye ratio "2 L steam" tabhi sahi hai jab steam, steam hi ho aur taapman dabao wahi hon.

## Quick Recap

- Gay-Lussac (1808): same taapman aur dabao par, gaso ke reaction mein volumes **simple whole-number ratio** mein hote hain (H₂ : O₂ : steam = 2 : 1 : 2).
- Class 9 wala Gay-Lussac ka niyam (P₁/T₁ = P₂/T₂) **alag** niyam hai.
- Avogadro (1811): barabar volume mein barabar molecule, aur H₂, O₂, N₂, Cl₂ **diatomic** hain. Isse "aadha atom" ki paheli suljhi.
- Volume ratio = molecule ratio = mole ratio = coefficient ratio (gaso ke liye).
- Atomicity: He, Ne, Ar monoatomic; H₂, O₂, N₂, Cl₂ diatomic; O₃ triatomic; P₄, S₈ polyatomic.
- Tareeka: balanced equation, coefficient se volume ratio, diye hue volume ko scale karo.
- Volume badh bhi sakta hai, ghat bhi sakta hai, aur wahi bhi reh sakta hai. Atoms hamesha bachte hain.
- Sab gas wale reaction sirf padhne ke liye hain.

## Practice Questions

(Sab volume same taapman aur dabao par hain; sab gas reaction sirf kagaz par hain.)

1. 60 L of sulphur dioxide reacts with oxygen to form sulphur trioxide gas: 2SO₂ + O₂ → 2SO₃. Find the volume of oxygen needed and of SO₃ formed. By how many litres does the total volume change?
2. 12 L of hydrogen sulphide burns completely: 2H₂S + 3O₂ → 2SO₂ + 2H₂O(g). Find the volume of oxygen needed and of SO₂ and steam formed. Does the total volume change? (Both H₂S and SO₂ are poisonous: paper work only.)
3. 8 L of ammonia reacts with oxygen to give nitrogen and steam: 4NH₃ + 3O₂ → 2N₂ + 6H₂O(g). Find the volumes of oxygen used, nitrogen and steam formed.
4. 4 L of methanol vapour (CH₃OH, kept hot so that it stays a gas) burns completely: 2CH₃OH + 3O₂ → 2CO₂ + 4H₂O(g). Find the volume of each gas involved and the total volume before and after. (Methanol is poisonous: paper work only.)
5. 30 mL of hydrogen combines with 30 mL of bromine vapour to give 60 mL of hydrogen bromide gas. Write the ratio, deduce the equation and say what this tells you about the atomicity of hydrogen and bromine (HBr has one H and one Br).
6. 6 L of ethane (C₂H₆) burns: 2C₂H₆ + 7O₂ → 4CO₂ + 6H₂O. Find the volume of oxygen needed. If the products are then cooled so that the steam turns to liquid water, what gas volume is left?
7. Ozone breaks down to oxygen: 2O₃ → 3O₂. If 10 L of ozone breaks down completely, what volume of oxygen forms? Check that the number of atoms is the same on both sides, and say what the change in volume tells you.
8. 3 L of a gas A contains 7.5 × 10²² molecules. At the same temperature and pressure, how many molecules are there in 5 L of a different gas B? Name the law you used.
9. Two gases combine in the volume ratio 2 : 1 and give 2 volumes of a gas product. Name two reactions from this topic that follow this ratio. Why can the ratio not be 1 : 1 : 2 for them?
10. 18 mL of nitric oxide reacts with oxygen to make nitrogen dioxide: 2NO + O₂ → 2NO₂. Find the oxygen needed and the NO₂ formed. Does the volume go up or down?
11. State two differences between the Gay-Lussac law of this chapter and the Gay-Lussac law of Class 9 (the pressure-temperature law). Which of them would you use for the reaction H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl?
12. True or false, and correct the false ones: (a) 1 L of helium and 1 L of hydrogen at the same temperature and pressure have the same number of molecules. (b) In a reaction of gases the total volume must stay the same. (c) Oxygen gas is made of single oxygen atoms. (d) Gay-Lussac's combining volumes ratio is the same as the coefficient ratio for gases. (e) The ratio of volumes of H₂ : O₂ in 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O is 2 : 2.

### Answers

1. Ratio 2 : 1 : 2. 60 L SO₂ = 2 hissa, 1 hissa = 30 L. O₂ = **30 L**, SO₃ = **60 L**. Pehle 60 + 30 = 90 L, baad mein 60 L: volume **30 L ghata**. Atom: S 2 = 2, O 4 + 2 = 6 = 6 ✅. Jaanch: 60 : 30 : 60 = 2 : 1 : 2 ✅ (SO₂ aur SO₃ zehreeli hain: sirf kagaz par.)
2. Ratio 2 : 3 : 2 : 2. 12 L H₂S = 2 hissa, 1 hissa = 6 L. O₂ = 3 × 6 = **18 L**, SO₂ = 2 × 6 = **12 L**, steam = 2 × 6 = **12 L**. Pehle 12 + 18 = 30 L, baad mein 12 + 12 = 24 L: volume **6 L ghata**. Atom: H 4 = 4, S 2 = 2, O 6 = 4 + 2 = 6 ✅. (H₂S aur SO₂ zehreeli: sirf kagaz par.)
3. Ratio 4 : 3 : 2 : 6. 8 L NH₃ = 4 hissa, 1 hissa = 2 L. O₂ = 3 × 2 = **6 L**, N₂ = 2 × 2 = **4 L**, steam = 6 × 2 = **12 L**. Pehle 8 + 6 = 14 L, baad mein 4 + 12 = 16 L. Atom: N 4 = 4, H 12 = 12, O 6 = 6 ✅ (Ammonia tez gandh wali, zehreeli gas hai: sirf kagaz par.)
4. Ratio 2 : 3 : 2 : 4. 4 L CH₃OH vapour = 2 hissa, 1 hissa = 2 L. O₂ = 3 × 2 = **6 L**, CO₂ = 2 × 2 = **4 L**, steam = 4 × 2 = **8 L**. Pehle 4 + 6 = 10 L, baad mein 4 + 8 = 12 L: volume **2 L badha**. Atom: C 2 = 2, H 8 = 8, O 2 + 6 = 8 = 4 + 4 ✅
5. Ratio 30 : 30 : 60 = **1 : 1 : 2**. Equation: H₂ + Br₂ → 2HBr. Har HBr mein 1 H aur 1 Br: n molecule H₂ aur n molecule Br₂ se 2n HBr, to 2n H atom n molecule H₂ se aaye, yaani har molecule mein **2 atom**; wahi bromine ke liye. Dono **diatomic**. Atom: H 2 = 2, Br 2 = 2 ✅ (Bromine zehreeli hai: sirf kagaz par.)
6. Ratio 2 : 7 : 4 : 6. 6 L C₂H₆ = 2 hissa, 1 hissa = 3 L. O₂ = 7 × 3 = **21 L**, CO₂ = 4 × 3 = 12 L, steam = 6 × 3 = 18 L. Thanda karne par steam liquid paani ban jaati hai (volume bahut chhota), to bachi gas sirf **CO₂, 12 L**. Atom: C 4 = 4, H 12 = 12, O 14 = 8 + 6 = 14 ✅
7. 2 : 3 ratio. 10 L O₃ = 2 hissa, 1 hissa = 5 L. O₂ = 3 × 5 = **15 L**. Atom: baayein 2 × 3 = 6, daayein 3 × 2 = 6 ✅. Volume **5 L badha** kyunki 2 molecule se 3 molecule bane: volume ginti ke saath chalta hai. Jaanch: 10 : 15 = 2 : 3 ✅
8. Avogadro ka niyam: barabar volume mein barabar molecule, to 3 L mein 7.5 × 10²². 1 L mein 7.5 × 10²² ÷ 3 = 2.5 × 10²². 5 L mein 5 × 2.5 × 10²² = **1.25 × 10²³** molecule. Jaanch: 1.25 × 10²³ ÷ 5 = 2.5 × 10²² = 7.5 × 10²² ÷ 3 ✅
9. 2 : 1 : 2 wale reaction: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O (steam) aur 2CO + O₂ → 2CO₂ (ya 2SO₂ + O₂ → 2SO₃). Ratio coefficient se tay hota hai: in reactions mein 2 molecule ek gas ke aur 1 molecule doosri gas ka lagta hai, tabhi atom bachte hain. 1 : 1 : 2 likhne par atom nahi bachte, jaise H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O mein baayein O 2 aur daayein O 2 ok, par H baayein 2 aur daayein 4. Isliye ratio hamari marzi se nahi, balanced equation se aata hai.
10. Ratio 2 : 1 : 2. 18 mL NO = 2 hissa, 1 hissa = 9 mL. O₂ = **9 mL**, NO₂ = **18 mL**. Pehle 18 + 9 = 27 mL, baad mein 18 mL: volume **ghata**. Atom: N 2 = 2, O 2 + 2 = 4 = 4 ✅ (NO₂ zehreeli gas hai: sirf padhne ke liye.)
11. Class 9 wala: ek hi gas ka P aur T, volume constant, formula P₁/T₁ = P₂/T₂; ek hi gas, reaction nahi. Is chapter wala: alag-alag gaso ke volume ka ratio, reaction mein, same T aur P par, coefficient jaisa. Reaction H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl ke liye **combining volumes wala** (1 : 1 : 2).
12. (a) **True** ✅: barabar volume, barabar molecule, kan ka size alag ho tab bhi. (b) **False** ❌: volume ghat ya badh sakta hai (2CO + O₂ → 2CO₂ mein 3 se 2). (c) **False** ❌: oxygen **O₂** hai, do atom ka. (d) **True** ✅. (e) **False** ❌: ratio **2 : 1** hai, 2 : 2 nahi.
