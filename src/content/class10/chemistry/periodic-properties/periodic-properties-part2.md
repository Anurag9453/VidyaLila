# Topic 2: Ionisation Energy: Electron Nikalne Ki Keemat

Topic 1 mein humne atom ka **size** naapa aur dekha ki do hi ideas sab kuch samjha dete hain: **shells ki ginti** (doori) aur **nuclear pull** (nucleus ka kheenchav, beech ki shells ke parde ke saath). Ab ek naya sawaal: **agar baahari electron ko atom se bahar nikaalna ho, to kitni mehnat lagegi?** Ye mehnat har tatva ke liye alag hoti hai, aur isse bataya ja sakta hai ki kaun sa tatva electron aasani se chhodta hai (aur isliye cation banata hai), aur kaun nahi. Yahi "electron nikalne ki keemat" **ionisation energy** hai. Wahi do ideas, naye sawaal par.

## Khilone ki kheenchatani

Mr. Tembe ke ghar Sunday ko rishtedaaron ki baithak thi. Unka bhanja Hrishikesh chhutti mein aaya hua tha. Baithak mein ek dedh saal ki bachchi apna nanha sa rabar ka khilona pakde baithi thi. Hrishikesh ne mazaak mein haath badhaya: "Mujhe de do, thodi der ke liye." Bachchi ne khilona seene se chipka liya aur dono haathon se kas kar pakad liya. Hrishikesh ne halka sa kheencha, to bachchi aur kas gayi.

Thodi der baad bachchi ek kone mein khel rahi thi aur khilona haath ki lambi bahon mein dheele se latka hua tha. Hrishikesh ne wahi halka kheench kiya aur khilona jhat se nikal aaya. Bachchi ko pata bhi nahi chala.

"Mama, ye kya? Pehle itna mushkil, ab itna aasaan. Khilona wahi, main wahi!" Hrishikesh hairaan tha.

Mr. Tembe hanse. "Farq **pakad** ka tha. Jab khilona seene ke bilkul paas aur kas ke pakda tha, to nikalne ke liye bahut zor lagta. Jab door aur dheela tha, to bahut kam. Chemistry mein bhi yahi hota hai: ek atom apne baahari electron ko kitni zor se pakde hai, ye tay karta hai ki use nikalne mein kitni **energy** lagegi. Aur is pakad ko tay karne wale do hi cheezein hain, jo tumne pichhle topic mein dekhi: **doori** aur **nuclear pull**."

```

        _____                                  _____
       ( ^ ^ )                                ( ^ ^ )
        \___/ [khilona]                        \___/
         / \  tight                             / \  ---------- [khilona]
                                                    door aur dheela

   Nikalne mein zyada zor              Nikalne mein kam zor
   (paas aur kas ke = badi ionisation   (door aur dheela = chhoti ionisation

```

## Part A: Ionisation energy kya hai?

Maan lo ek sodium ka atom gas ki tarah akela hai. Isse baahari ek electron nikaalna hai. Ye khud nahi nikalega: bahar se **energy deni padegi**, kyunki nucleus us electron ko kheench raha hai. Jitni energy dene par ye electron nikal jaaye, wahi **ionisation energy** hai.

**Na (gas) + energy → Na⁺ (gas) + e⁻**

Ye general roop mein: **M (g) + energy → M⁺ (g) + e⁻**

Yaad rakho: yahan energy **andar jaati hai** (kharch hoti hai), isliye ionisation energy hamesha **positive** hoti hai: electron ko nikalne ke liye mehnat lagti hai, mufat mein nahi milta.

```
     Pehle: Na atom                         Baad mein: Na⁺ ion aur electron

      +11 nucleus                             +11 nucleus
     shells: 2, 8, 1                          shells: 2, 8
        o                                                     o --> e⁻ (bahar)
     ( o )  ( o o ... )                       ( o )  ( o o ... )
      1 electron baahar                        ab sirf 2 aur 8 bache

     11 proton, 11 electron                   11 proton, 10 electron
     kul charge 0                             kul charge +1
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Na → Na⁺ + e⁻: baahari akela electron nikal gaya, 11 proton wahi hain, ab 10 electron bache, isliye ion +1 hai. Nikalne mein lagi energy hi ionisation energy hai.

**Gas ki haalat kyun?** Agar atom thos mein doosre atoms ke bahut paas ho, to padosi atoms bhi us electron par asar daalte hain. Ek **akele** atom ki ionisation energy tabhi saaf-saaf naapi ja sakti hai jab wo gas ki tarah akela ho. Bas itna hi yaad rakho.

**Ikaai kJ per mol kya hai?** Ek atom se ek electron nikaalne ki energy itni chhoti hoti hai ki use sidhe likhna asaan nahi. Isliye chemist **1 mol atoms** (bahut bade, fixed ginti ka dher: Class 9 aur Chapter 5 ka mole) ke saare atoms se ek-ek electron nikalne ki kul energy likhte hain. Ikaai kilojoule per mol (kJ per mol) hai. Ek kilojoule = 1000 joule. Isse zyada ikaai ka matlab yahan nahi chahiye.

**Doosri ionisation energy (ek line):** pehla electron nikalne ke baad jo ion bachta hai, usse ek aur electron nikaalne ki energy **doosri ionisation energy** kehlaati hai, aur wo hamesha pehli se badi hoti hai (Part C mein dekhenge ki kitni badi).

## Part B: Trends: group mein aur period mein

Ab wahi do ideas lagate hain. Khilone ki kahani yaad karo: pakad kasi ho (nuclear pull zyada) ya door ho (shells zyada) to nikalne ka zor kitna badalta hai.

### Group mein upar se neeche: IE ghat-ti hai

Group 1 mein Li, Na, K, ..., Cs. Neeche utarte hue **shells badhti jaati hain**, baahari electron nucleus se door hota jaata hai, aur beech mein zyada shells ka **parda** bhi hota hai. Pakad dheeli hoti jaati hai, isliye electron nikaalna aasan hota jaata hai.

Lagbhag values (first IE, kJ per mol): **Li 520, Na 496, K 419, Cs 376.**

```
   Group 1: pehli ionisation energy (lagbhag)     1 # = 20 kJ per mol

   Li   ##########################   520
   Na   #########################    496
   K    #####################        419
   Cs   ###################           376

   Neeche jao: shells badhti hain, baahari electron door, IE ghat-ti hai
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Group 1 mein IE neeche utarte hue kam hoti jaati hai: Li ke baahari electron ki pakad sabse kasi, Cs ki sabse dheeli. (Cs sirf group ke order mein naam ke liye hai.)

Isi tarah Group 2 mein Mg lagbhag 738 aur Ca lagbhag 590: Ca neeche hai, usse electron aasani se nikalta hai.

### Period mein baayen se daayen: IE badhti hai

Ek hi period mein shells ki ginti wahi rehti hai, par **proton badhte jaate hain**. To nucleus ki kheench badhti hai, atom chhota hota jaata hai (Topic 1), aur baahari electron aur kas ke pakda jaata hai. IE **badhti hai**, aur period ke **end par noble gas mein sabse zyada** hoti hai.

Period 3: **Na lagbhag 496, Mg lagbhag 738, Ar lagbhag 1521.**
Period 2: **Li lagbhag 520, Ne lagbhag 2081.**

```
   Period 3 (shuruat se ant tak)                  1 # = 50 kJ per mol

   Na   ##########                 496
   Mg   ###############            738
   Ar   ##############################   1521     (noble gas: sabse oonchi)

   Period 2

   Li   ##########                 520
   Ne   ##########################################   2081
```

> **Figure 2.4** – Ek period mein shuruat ke metal (Na, Li) se noble gas (Ar, Ne) tak ionisation energy bahut badh jaati hai: same shells, zyada proton, kasi pakad.

**Ek line aage ki class ke liye:** Period ke beech ke kuch tatvon mein (jaise Mg se Al) IE ek chhote ulte kadam se girti hai, jiska reason aage ki class mein aata hai. Bada trend wahi rehta hai: **period mein badhna**.

**Pakad ka mantra:** group mein neeche = door + parda = IE kam. Period mein aage = zyada proton, wahi shells = IE zyada.

## Part C: Ek ke baad ek electron: successive ionisation energies

Ab ek dilchasp sawaal: pehla electron nikal gaya, to doosra? Wo kuch aur zyada mehnat maangega, par kitna zyada? Isi mein poori kahani chhupi hai ki **Na sirf Na⁺ kyun banata hai, Na²⁺ kyun nahi**.

### Na ka case

Na ke shells 2, 8, 1. Lagbhag values:

| Kaun sa electron | Ionisation energy (kJ per mol, lagbhag) |
|---|---|
| Pehla (baahari 1 electron) | 496 |
| Doosra (ab 2, 8 wali bhari shell se) | 4562 |

Doosra electron pehle se **lagbhag 9 guna** mehnga hai (4562 ÷ 496 = 9.2).

**Kyun?** Pehla electron teesri shell ka akela electron tha, nucleus se door aur 2, 8 ke parde ke peeche. Wo nikal gaya. Ab doosra electron **doosri shell ki bhari hui, nucleus ke bilkul paas wali shell** se aana hai. Nucleus mein abhi bhi 11 proton hain, par ab sirf 10 electron unhe baand rahe hain (pehle 11 the). Aur ye electron bahut paas hai, parde ke andar. Pakad bahut kasi hai, isliye energy bahut lagti hai.

### Mg ka case

Mg ke shells 2, 8, 2. Lagbhag values:

| Kaun sa electron | Ionisation energy (kJ per mol, lagbhag) |
|---|---|
| Pehla | 738 |
| Doosra | 1451 |
| Teesra (ab 2, 8 wali bhari shell se) | 7733 |

Pehle se doosra lagbhag 2 guna (1451 ÷ 738 = 1.97), par doosre se teesra lagbhag **5.3 guna** (7733 ÷ 1451 = 5.3). Bada chhalaang **teesre electron par** aaya, jab bhari shell tootne lagi.

```
   Successive IE ke bar (1 # = 200 kJ per mol, lagbhag)

   Na   1st    ##                                      496
        2nd    #######################                       4562

   Mg   1st    ####                                    738
        2nd    #######                                1451
        3rd    #######################################   7733

```

> **Figure 2.5** – Sodium mein pehle electron ke baad ek bahut bada ooncha kadam aata hai (2nd), magnesium mein do chhote kadam ke baad bada ooncha kadam (3rd). Bada kadam tab aata hai jab bhari hui andar ki shell tootne lagti hai.

### Ye kya batata hai?

- Na: pehla aasan, doosra bahut mushkil, isliye Na **sirf 1 electron** deta hai. Valence electron 1 hain, ion **Na⁺**.
- Mg: pehle do (738, 1451) mein chhota farq, teesra bahut zyada, isliye Mg **2 electron** deta hai. Ion **Mg²⁺**.
- Isse seedha pata chalta hai ki **valence electron kitne hain**: jahan IE mein **bada chhalaang** aaye, us se pehle jitne electron nikale gaye, utne valence electron.

Class 9 mein valency aur group ka rishta tha: ye usi ka **energy wala saboot** hai.

## Part D: Noble gases sabse oonchi chotiyon par

Noble gas ka baahari shell **bhari hui** hoti hai (He ki 2, Ne ki 2, 8, Ar ki 2, 8, 8). Electron nucleus ke paas aur kas ke pakde hain, isliye inki IE apne period mein **sabse zyada** hoti hai: **He lagbhag 2372, Ne lagbhag 2081, Ar lagbhag 1521 kJ per mol.**

Dhyan do: noble gas ki IE apne **group mein neeche** bhi ghat-ti hai (He > Ne > Ar), kyunki neeche shells badhti hain. Ye group wala trend wahi hai jo Part B mein tha.

```
   Group 18: IE neeche utarte hue ghat-ti hai          1 # = 50 kJ per mol

   He   ###############################################   2372
   Ne   ##########################################        2081
   Ar   ##############################                    1521
```

> **Figure 2.6** – He, Ne, Ar: IE bahut oonchi hai (isliye ye gas aasani se react nahi karti: Class 9 ka "inert" ka ek reason), par neeche utarte hue phir bhi thodi ghat-ti hai.

## Part E: Cation kaise bante hain aur Group 1 ki reactivity

### Cation ka bantaa

Jis tatva ki IE **kam** hoti hai, wo electron aasani se chhodta hai aur **cation** banata hai. Isliye Group 1 aur 2 ke tatva (K, Na, Mg, Ca) ke metals cation banane mein maahir hain: inki IE kam hai. Ye wahi cations hain jo Chapter 2 ke ionic compounds mein aur Chapter 4 mein **cathode** ki taraf jaate hain (Electrolysis).

### Group 1 ki reactivity

Class 9 mein hum ne dekha tha ki Li < Na < K reactivity badhti hai ([periodic-table-part5.md](../../../class9/chemistry/periodic-table/periodic-table-part5.md)). Ab uska energy-wala reason: reaction mein alkali metal ko electron **dena** hota hai. **Jis ki IE kam, wo electron jaldi deta hai, isliye zyada tezi se react karta hai.** K ki IE (419) Na ki (496) se kam hai, isliye K, Na se zyada tezi se react karta hai. Ye vyavhaar paani ke saath teacher ke demo mein dikhaya jaata hai: **potassium zyada tez, hinsak tareeke se**. **Sirf padhne ke liye ya sirf teacher demo:** koi student kabhi sodium ya potassium ko nahi chhuega, aur na hi koi prayog karega.

## Part F: Solved examples

### Example 1. Li, Na, K ko IE ke order mein likho (sabse kam se sabse zyada)

- Teeno Group 1 mein hain. Li ke 2 shells, Na ke 3, K ke 4.
- Group mein neeche: shells badhti hain, baahari electron door, parda zyada, pakad dheeli.
- Isliye IE ghat-ti hai: **K < Na < Li**.

**Jaanch:** values lagbhag 419 < 496 < 520 ✅.

### Example 2. Mg aur Ca mein kiski IE zyada?

- Mg: 3 shells (2, 8, 2). Ca: 4 shells (2, 8, 8, 2). Dono Group 2.
- Ca ka baahari electron zyada door aur zyada shells ke parde ke peeche hai.
- Isliye **Mg ki IE zyada**, Ca ki kam.

**Jaanch:** Mg lagbhag 738, Ca lagbhag 590, aur 738 > 590 ✅.

### Example 3. Na, Mg, Ar: period mein badhta hua order

- Teeno Period 3 mein: 3 shells. Proton Na 11, Mg 12, Ar 18.
- Shells wahi, proton badhe, nuclear pull badhi, electron kas ke pakda hai.
- Order: **Na < Mg < Ar**.

**Jaanch:** lagbhag 496 < 738 < 1521 ✅. (Ar noble gas hai, isliye sabse oonchi.)

### Example 4. Na⁺ hi kyun, Na²⁺ kyun nahi?

- Na: 1st IE lagbhag 496, 2nd IE lagbhag 4562.
- Pehle electron ke baad 2nd bahut mehnga, kyunki wo ab bhari hui 2, 8 shell se aata hai.
- Reaction mein itni zyada energy dene ka koi faayda nahi, isliye Na **sirf ek** electron deta hai.

**Jaanch:** Na → Na⁺ + e⁻ likho. Total electron 11 − 1 = 10 aur proton 11, charge +1 ✅.

### Example 5. Mg²⁺ kyun?

- Mg: 738, 1451, 7733 lagbhag.
- Pehle do ke baad bada chhalaang. Mg sirf 2 electron deta hai.
- Mg → Mg²⁺ + 2e⁻.

**Jaanch:** shells 2, 8, 2 mein se 2 electron nikle, bache 2, 8 (10 electron) aur 12 proton, charge +2 ✅.

### Example 6. Ek anjaan tatva: IE ke numbers se valence electron

Ek tatva ke lagbhag IE (kJ per mol) hain: **578, 1817, 2745, 11577.**

- 578 → 1817: lagbhag 3.1 guna.
- 1817 → 2745: lagbhag 1.5 guna.
- 2745 → 11577: lagbhag **4.2 guna**, bada chhalaang.
- Bada chhalaang chautha electron nikalte hue aaya, to pehle 3 electron aasani se nikle: **3 valence electron**.
- Group 13 ka tatva, ion X³⁺. (Ye Al hai: Al³⁺.)

**Jaanch:** Al ke shells 2, 8, 3. 3 nikalne par 2, 8 bachti hai, 10 electron aur 13 proton, charge +3 ✅.

### Example 7. Kitni energy chahiye? (chhota sa numerical)

**Sawaal 1:** Sodium ke 2 mol atoms se ek-ek electron nikalne mein kitni energy lagegi? (Na ki first IE lagbhag 496 kJ per mol.)

- 2 × 496 = **992 kJ**.

**Jaanch:** 1 mol ke liye 496, to 2 mol ke liye double = 992 ✅.

**Sawaal 2:** Magnesium ke 1 mol atoms ko Mg²⁺ ions mein badalne mein kul kitni energy? (Lagbhag 738 + 1451.)

- 738 + 1451 = **2189 kJ**.

**Jaanch:** 2189 − 738 = 1451 ✅.

### Example 8. Group 18 ki tulna

He, Ne, Ar: kiski IE sabse zyada?

- He ka sirf 1 shell hai, electron nucleus ke bilkul paas aur beech mein koi parda nahi.
- Neeche utarte hue (Ne, Ar) shells badhti hain, IE ghat-ti hai.
- Order: **He > Ne > Ar**.

**Jaanch:** lagbhag 2372 > 2081 > 1521 ✅.

### Example 9. Ek hi period ka sabse bada aur sabse chhota

Period 3 ke Na, Mg, Ar mein se kis ka atom electron sabse aasani se chhodta hai, kiska sabse mushkil se?

- Sabse aasani se: **Na** (IE sabse kam, 496).
- Sabse mushkil se: **Ar** (IE sabse zyada, 1521).

**Jaanch:** Na ke atom ka size sabse bada hai (Topic 1), isliye pakad dheeli ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti: "Jiski ionisation energy zyada, wo electron aasani se chhodta hai."** Ye ulta hai.

Ionisation energy electron **nikalne ki keemat** hai, yaani zor jo lagana padta hai. Keemat zyada = nikalna mushkil. Khilone ki kahani mein: jab khilona kas ke pakda ho, to nikalne mein zyada zor lagta hai; aasani se kabhi nahi nikalta.

**Demonstration:** teen tatva lo: Na (496), Ne (2081), K (419). Kaun electron sabse aasani se chhodega?

- Kam IE = aasan nikalna. K sabse kam (419), phir Na (496), phir Ne (2081).
- Isliye order electron chhodne ki aasaani mein: **K > Na > Ne**.
- Ne ki IE sabse zyada hai, to wo electron chhodne mein sabse **kamzor** hai, isliye hi Ne aasani se react nahi karti (Class 9 ka inert gas).

**Do chhote sawaal se jaanch:**

1. K aur Ne: kaun sa cation banata hai? K⁺ (aasani se), Ne⁺ lagbhag kabhi nahi.
2. Agar IE zyada ka matlab "aasani se chhodta" hota, to Ne sabse zyada electron chhodne wala hota, par asli mein wo sabse nishkriya gas hai.

Yaad rakhne ka tareeka: **IE ka number paise ki tarah socho: jitni zyada keemat, utna kam aasan.**

## Quick Recap

- **Ionisation energy** = gas ki haalat ke ek akele atom se baahari electron nikalne ki energy: M → M⁺ + e⁻. Ikaai kJ per mol.
- Ye energy **dene** par hi electron nikalta hai, isliye IE positive hoti hai.
- **Group mein neeche:** shells badhti hain, electron door aur parda zyada, IE **ghat-ti** hai (Li 520, Na 496, K 419, Cs 376, lagbhag).
- **Period mein aage:** shells wahi, proton zyada, pull zyada, IE **badhti** hai (Na 496, Mg 738, Ar 1521, lagbhag). Beech ke tatvon mein chhote ulte kadam aage ki class mein.
- **Noble gas** ki IE apne period mein sabse zyada (He 2372, Ne 2081, Ar 1521, lagbhag).
- **Successive IE:** doosri hamesha pehli se badi. Bada chhalaang tab aata hai jab bhari andar ki shell tootne lagti hai: Na ka 2nd (4562), Mg ka 3rd (7733).
- Chhalaange se **valence electron** ki ginti aur ion ka charge pata chalta hai: Na⁺, Mg²⁺, Al³⁺.
- **Kam IE = electron aasani se chhodta hai** = achha cation banane wala metal. Group 1 mein K, Na se zyada tez reactive kyunki IE kam.
- **Zyada IE ka matlab electron aasani se nikalta hai? Nahi, bilkul ulta.**

### Answers

1. K aur Ca Period 4 mein hain (4 shells), Ar Period 3 mein (3 shells). K aur Ca mein shells wahi, par Ca mein ek proton zyada, to pull zyada aur IE zyada: K < Ca. Ar mein sirf 3 shells (electron paas), 18 proton, aur bhari baahari shell, isliye uski IE sabse zyada. Order: **K (lagbhag 419) < Ca (lagbhag 590) < Ar (lagbhag 1521)**. **Jaanch:** 419 < 590 < 1521 ✅.
2. **Cs** zyada aasani se chhodta hai. Cs mein shells bahut zyada hain, baahari electron nucleus se bahut door aur bahut shells ke parde ke peeche hai, isliye pakad dheeli. Lagbhag values: Cs 376 < Li 520 ✅.
3. 590 → 1145: lagbhag 1.9 guna. 1145 → 4912: lagbhag 4.3 guna, bada chhalaang. Isliye pehle **2 electron** aasani se nikle: **2 valence electron**, **Group 2**, ion **X²⁺**. (X Ca hai, Ca²⁺.) **Jaanch:** Ca ke shells 2, 8, 8, 2: 2 nikalne par 2, 8, 8 bachti hai, charge +2 ✅.
4. Li ke shells 2, 1: 1 valence electron. 520 aasan, 7298 bahut mushkil (lagbhag 14 guna), kyunki doosra electron bhari hui paas wali 1st shell se aata. Isliye ion **Li⁺**, Li²⁺ nahi. **Jaanch:** 7298 ÷ 520 = 14.0 ✅.
5. 3 × 419 = **1257 kJ**. **Jaanch:** 1257 ÷ 3 = 419 ✅.
6. Har atom ke liye 590 + 1145 = 1735 kJ per mol. 2 mol ke liye 2 × 1735 = **3470 kJ**. **Jaanch:** 3470 ÷ 2 = 1735 ✅.
7. (a) **True** ✅: Ne lagbhag 2081, Na lagbhag 496, Ne ki shell bhari aur electron kas ke pakda. (b) **False** ❌: K ki IE (419) Na (496) se kam, kyunki K mein shells zyada. (c) **False** ❌: noble gas ki IE sabse zyada hai, isliye wo electron sabse mushkil se chhodte hain. (d) **False** ❌: doosri hamesha badi hoti hai, kyunki ab ion se electron nikalna hai. (e) **False** ❌: cation banne mein energy **lagti** hai (di jaati hai), mukt nahi hoti.
8. He mein sirf 1 shell hai, electron nucleus ke bilkul paas hain aur beech mein koi doosri shell ka parda nahi. Ne aur Ar mein shells aur parde badhte hain, isliye IE ghat-ti hai. **He lagbhag 2372 > Ne 2081 > Ar 1521** ✅.
9. Ar mein 3 shells aur sabse baahari shell 8 se bhari hai. K mein 19 proton hain par uska 19th electron ek **nayi, 4th shell** mein akela hai, jo bahut door hai aur 18 electron (2, 8, 8) ke parde ke peeche hai. Doori aur parda is ek extra proton ki kheench ko bahut zyada kam kar dete hain. Isliye IE 1521 se girkar 419 ho gayi. **Jaanch:** 1521 > 419 ✅.
10. Student galat hai. **Zyada IE ka matlab electron nikalna mushkil hai**, aasan nahi. Ar ki IE lagbhag 1521 hai, to Ar⁺ banane mein bahut energy lagti hai, isliye Ar aasani se ion nahi banata. K jaise kam IE wale metal aasani se cation banate hain.
11. Na ki IE (lagbhag 496) Ca ki (lagbhag 590) se kam hai, isliye Na apna electron aasani se chhodta hai aur thande paani ke saath zyada tezi se react karne ki ummeed hai. (Asli mein bhi Na thande paani se tez react karta hai aur Ca dheere: Chapter 2 ki seedhi.) Ye bas ek anuman hai, prayog sirf teacher ka. **Jaanch:** 496 < 590 ✅.
12. Aasani se chhodne ka order (kam IE pehle): **K (419), Na (496), Ca (590), Mg (738)**. K aur Na: dono Group 1, K mein ek shell zyada, electron door, isliye K aasani se chhodta hai. Na aur Mg: dono Period 3, Mg mein ek proton zyada aur shells wahi, isliye pull zyada aur IE zyada. **Jaanch:** 419 < 496 < 590 < 738 ✅.
13. Seene se kas ke pakda khilona: wo chhota atom hai jo electron ko kas ke pakda hai (nucleus ki kheench zyada, doori kam): iski **IE zyada** hai. Haath ki lambaai par dheele se pakda khilona: wo bada atom hai jiska baahari electron door hai: iski **IE kam** hai. Ye wahi do ideas hain: doori aur nuclear pull.
