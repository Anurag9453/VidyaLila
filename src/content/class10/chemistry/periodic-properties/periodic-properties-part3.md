# Topic 3: Electron Affinity: Electron Lene Ka Josh

Topic 1 mein humne atom ka size dekha aur Topic 2 mein ek atom se electron **nikaalne** ki keemat (ionisation energy). Ab sikke ka doosra pehlu: kuch atom electron dene mein nakhre dikhate hain, par kuch to electron **lene** ke liye utaawle rehte hain. Is topic mein hum naapte hain ki ek atom electron pakadne par kitni energy **chhodta** hai, aur dekhte hain ki halogen itne "josheele" aur noble gas itne "bepervah" kyun hain. Dono topic mein bas ek hi do ideas kaam karenge: **shells ki ginti (doori)** aur **nuclear pull (shielding ke baad)**.

Mr. Ubale ki chhutti thi aur unki bhatiji Sayali society ke maidan mein gully cricket dekhne aayi thi. Chacha bhi saath mein kursi daal kar baith gaye. Match ke beech ball ooncha uchhla. ## Part A: Electron affinity kya hai?

Ek **gaseous atom** socho: akela, door-door koi padosi nahi. Usmein ek electron aakar judta hai. Agar atom us electron ko pasand karta hai (uski nuclear pull electron ko khinch leti hai), to judte hi **energy bahar nikalti** hai. Yahi hai **electron affinity**:

**X(g) + e⁻ → X⁻(g) + energy**

Unit wahi hai jo Topic 2 mein tha: **kJ per mol** (1 mol atoms ke liye). Dhyan do: yahan ye energy **chhodi** jaati hai, jabki ionisation energy mein energy **lagani** padti thi.

```
   Ionisation energy (Topic 2)             Electron affinity (Topic 3)

   Na  +  energy  -->  Na+  +  e-          Cl  +  e-  -->  Cl-  +  energy

   Atom se electron NIKAALNA               Atom mein electron JODNA
   Energy LAGTI hai (andar jaati)          Energy NIKALTI hai (bahar aati)
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Ionisation energy aur electron affinity ek-doosre ke ulte kaam hain: ek mein electron bahar jaata hai (energy andar daalni padti hai), doosre mein electron andar aata hai (energy bahar nikalti hai).

Kuch lagbhag values (kJ per mol mein, energy jo atom ne **chhodi**):

| Atom | Electron affinity (lagbhag) |
|---|---|
| Li | 60 |
| O (pehla electron) | 141 |
| F | 328 |
| S | 200 |
| Cl | 349 |
| Br | 325 |
| I | 295 |
| N | lagbhag 0 (aage ki class mein) |
| Ne, Ar | lagbhag 0 (electron judne mein koi fayda nahi) |

Is table se do cheezein dikhti hain. Pehli: **non-metals** (O, F, S, Cl, Br, I) ki affinity badi hai. Doosri: **Cl sabse upar** hai. Ye sab aage ek-ek kar ke samjhenge.

Ek chhoti baat: "Cl ki affinity 349" ka matlab ye **nahi** ki Cl ke andar 349 electron hain. Ye bas energy ki **maatra** hai jo 1 mol Cl atoms ek-ek electron pakadne par chhodte hain.

## Part B: Josh kahan se aata hai? Do ideas

Topic 1 aur 2 ki tarah yahan bhi do ideas hain:
1. **Shells ki ginti:** bahar aane wala electron nucleus se kitni door ke shell mein baithega?
2. **Nuclear pull:** nucleus ka positive charge (protons) shielding ke baad kitna pull de raha hai?

Jab aane wala electron **paas** wale shell mein baithta hai aur nucleus ki pull **zyada** hai, to wo electron mazbooti se pakda jaata hai aur zyada energy chhoot-ti hai. Jab electron **door** wale shell mein jaata hai, ya pull kam hai, to kam energy chhoot-ti hai.

### Example 1. Cl aur F ke liye bahar ka electron kahan jaata hai?

- Cl ka configuration 2, 8, 7. Bahar ke shell mein 7 electron hain, ek ki **jagah khaali** (8 ki seema tak). Aane wala electron isi jagah mein baith kar shell ko **8 se poora** kar deta hai: 2, 8, 8 (jaise Ar).
- Cl ke nucleus mein 17 protons hain aur bahar ka shell 3rd hai. Pull acchi-khaasi hai.

**Jaanch:** Cl⁻ mein 18 electron: 2, 8, 8 ✅ (Ar ka configuration, jo stable hai).

### Example 2. Li, O, F: ek period mein EA ka badhna

Teeno Period 2 ke hain: bahar ka shell **2nd** hai, shells ki ginti ek hi. Bas protons badhte jaate hain: Li (3), O (8), F (9).

- Li (2, 1): aane wale electron ke liye nucleus mein sirf 3 protons. Pull kam: EA lagbhag **60**.
- O (2, 6): 8 protons. Pull zyada: EA lagbhag **141**.
- F (2, 7): 9 protons, aur sirf **ek** electron ki kami. Pull aur zyada: EA lagbhag **328**.

**Jaanch:** 60 < 141 < 328 ✅ (protons 3 < 8 < 9). Ek period mein baayen se daayen **EA badhti hai** (lagbhag).

Ek honest note: Period 2 mein N (2, 5) ki EA lagbhag zero hai, ye order mein theek se nahi baithta. Isi ko "aage ki class mein" padhenge. Abhi hum halogens (Group 17) par dhyan rakhte hain, jahan trend saaf hai.

### Example 3. Group 17 mein neeche jaate hue: Cl, Br, I

Cl (3 shells), Br (4 shells), I (5 shells). Neeche jaate ho to aane wala electron **door ke shell** mein baithta hai (Topic 1 ki "doori" wali baat) aur andar ke shells ki **shielding** bhi badhti hai. Nuclear pull bahar tak kam pahunchti hai.

- Cl: lagbhag **349**
- Br: lagbhag **325**
- I: lagbhag **295**

**Jaanch:** 349 > 325 > 295 ✅. Group mein neeche jaate hue EA **ghatti** hai (halogens mein).

```
   Group 17: electron ka naya ghar (door hota jaata hai)

   Cl   [ 2 ] [ 8 ] [ 7+1 ]            EA ~ 349   (3rd shell, paas)
   Br   [ 2 ] [ 8 ] [ 18 ] [ 7+1 ]     EA ~ 325   (4th shell, thoda door)
   I    [ 2 ] [ 8 ] [ 18 ] [ 18 ] [ 7+1 ]
                                       EA ~ 295   (5th shell, aur door)

   +1 = aane wala electron
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Cl, Br, I mein aane wala electron kramshah 3rd, 4th aur 5th shell mein baithta hai. Jitna door, utni kam pull, utni kam energy chhoot-ti hai. Dhyan do: shell mein 18 electron wala figure bas shells ki ginti dikhane ke liye hai.

### Example 4. F ke baare mein honest note

Ye dekh kar lagega ki F sabse upar hona chahiye, kyunki ye Group 17 mein sabse upar hai (Example 3 ke hisaab se). Par asli values mein F (lagbhag 328) Cl (349) se **thoda kam** hai. Iska reason bahut chhote 2nd shell mein electrons ka ek-doosre se tang hona hai: ye **aage ki class mein** samjhenge. Is chapter mein hum bas ye yaad rakhenge: **Cl sabse upar** hai, aur Cl, Br, I mein trend saaf neeche ki taraf hai.

## Part C: Noble gases ki bepervahi aur metals ka thanda swabhav

**Noble gases** (He, Ne, Ar) ka bahar ka shell **bhara hua** hai: Ne ka 2, 8 aur Ar ka 2, 8, 8. Koi jagah khaali nahi. Agar aap Ne ko ek aur electron do, to wo naye shell mein jaayega (Ne ke liye 3rd shell), jo bahut door hai, aur andar ka bhara hua shell aane wale electron ko door dhakelta hai. Isliye **koi fayda nahi**: EA lagbhag **zero** (Ne, Ar). Noble gas "Varun" jaise hain: ball haath mein aati hi nahi.

**Metals** (jaise Li, Na) ka bahar mein sirf 1 electron hota hai aur unka size bada, nuclear pull kam. Aane wale electron ke liye pull kam hai, isliye EA **chhoti** hoti hai (Li ki lagbhag 60, jabki F ki 328). Metals ko electron **dene** mein zyada dilchaspi hai (Topic 2), lene mein nahi. Isliye metals aam taur par cation bante hain, anion nahi.

| Group | Bahar ke electrons | Electron ka rujhaan | EA |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (jaise Li) | 1 | dena chahta hai | chhoti |
| 17 (F, Cl, Br, I) | 7 | lena chahta hai | badi |
| 18 (Ne, Ar) | 8 (He mein 2) | na dena, na lena | lagbhag 0 |

### Example 5. Kaun sa atom electron zyada pasand karega?

**Sawaal:** Ar aur Cl mein se kaun electron ko zyada khushi se lega? Kyun?

- Cl (2, 8, 7): ek jagah khaali hai, aur nuclear pull acchi hai. Electron lene par shell poora ho jaata hai (2, 8, 8).
- Ar (2, 8, 8): shell pehle se bhara hua. Aane wala electron naye 4th shell mein jaayega, door aur andar ke bhare shells se dhakela hua.

**Jawab: Cl.** **Jaanch:** Cl ki EA lagbhag 349 aur Ar ki lagbhag 0 ✅.

### Example 6. Ek hi shell mein kaun zyada?

**Sawaal:** Ne (2, 8) aur F (2, 7): dono Period 2 mein hain. Kaun sa atom electron zyada khushi se lega?

- F: ek electron aate hi 2, 8 ban jaata hai (Ne jaisa). Nuclear pull bhi acchi (9 protons).
- Ne: bhara hua shell, EA lagbhag 0.

**Jawab: F.** **Jaanch:** F lagbhag 328 aur Ne lagbhag 0 ✅.

## Part D: Anion ban-na aur "Na gives, Cl takes"

Jab koi atom electron pakadta hai, wo **anion** ban jaata hai. Cl + e⁻ → Cl⁻. F + e⁻ → F⁻. O ko do electron chahiye: O + 2e⁻ → O²⁻ (Class 9 mein yahi dekha tha). Pehla electron O ko energy de kar jaata hai (EA lagbhag 141), aur doosra electron lene mein O ko ulta energy lagani padti hai: ye **aage ki class mein**.

Ab Chapter 4 ka ek link. Electrolysis mein hum ne dekha tha ki **anion anode** (positive electrode) ki taraf jaate hain ([electrolysis-part2.md](../electrolysis/electrolysis-part2.md)). Cl⁻ bhi isiliye anode par ja kar ek electron chhodta hai. Ye wahi electron hai jo Cl ne pehle pakda tha: yani Cl⁻ ban-na aur Cl⁻ ka wapas Cl banna ek-doosre ke ulte hain.

**Ionic bond ka ek pehlu: "Na gives, Cl takes".** NaCl mein Na apna bahar ka electron dene ko taiyaar hai (Topic 2: ionisation energy kam), aur Cl us electron ko lene ko taiyaar hai (EA badi, lagbhag 349). Dono ek-doosre ke liye bane hain: **ek dene wala, ek lene wala.**

```
   Na (2, 8, 1)        Cl (2, 8, 7)

       Na  ----- e- ----->  Cl
                  v
       Na+ (2, 8)           Cl- (2, 8, 8)
       Dene wala             Lene wala

       Na+ aur Cl- aapas mein aakarshan se jude rehte hain: NaCl
```

> **Figure 3.4** – Na ka bahar ka ek electron Cl ke paas jaata hai. Na ban jaata hai Na⁺ (2, 8) aur Cl ban jaata hai Cl⁻ (2, 8, 8). Dono ke bahar ke shell bhare hue hain.

Isi tarah MgO mein Mg do electron deta hai aur O do leta hai (Mg²⁺ aur O²⁻). CaF₂ mein Ca do electron deta hai, aur do F ek-ek lete hain (Ca²⁺ aur 2 F⁻). Ek baat saaf rakhna: ionic yaugik ka banna sirf dene wale ki IE aur lene wale ki EA par nahi tikta, ions ka jaal (lattice) bhi bahut madad karta hai. Ye poora hisaab **aage ki class mein** hai. Yahan sirf "dene wala aur lene wala" yaad rakho.

(Ghar mein jo namak khate ho wo NaCl hi hai. Par Cl₂ gas zehreeli hai: use koi student kabhi nahi chhuega, sirf padhne ke liye.)

### Example 7. Kaun dene wala, kaun lene wala?

**Sawaal:** CaF₂ mein Ca aur F ki bhumika batao.

- Ca (2, 8, 8, 2): 2 electron dena aasan, **dene wala**.
- F (2, 7): ek electron lene par 2, 8: **lene wala**. Ek Ca ke do electron do F ko mil jaate hain.

**Jaanch:** Ca²⁺ ka +2 aur do F⁻ ka −2: kul charge 0 ✅.

## Part E: Energy ke chhote numericals

Electron affinity "per mol" di jaati hai. To agar 1 mol se alag matra ho, to bas guna kar do.

**Energy chhodi gayi = (moles) × (EA per mol)**

### Example 8. 3 mol Cl atoms

Har mol Cl ek-ek electron pakadne par lagbhag 349 kJ chhodta hai. 3 mol Cl ke liye: 3 × 349 = **1047 kJ** (lagbhag).

**Jaanch:** 1047 ÷ 3 = 349 ✅.

### Example 9. Aadha mol F aur 0.2 mol Cl

- 0.5 mol F: 0.5 × 328 = **164 kJ** (lagbhag).
- 0.2 mol Cl: 0.2 × 349 = **69.8 kJ** (lagbhag).

**Jaanch:** 164 ÷ 0.5 = 328 ✅ aur 69.8 ÷ 0.2 = 349 ✅.

(Ye sab sirf kaagaz par hisaab hai: koi bhi F ya Cl gas ko hath nahi lagata.)

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Electron affinity aur ionisation energy ek hi cheez hai."**

Dono mein electron ka lena-dena hai, isliye ye dhokha deta hai. Par dono **ulte** kaam hain. Cl ko hi lo:

- **Electron affinity:** Cl + e⁻ → Cl⁻ + energy. Lagbhag **349 kJ per mol** energy **nikalti** hai.
- **Ionisation energy:** Cl → Cl⁺ + e⁻. Lagbhag **1251 kJ per mol** energy **lagani** padti hai.

Ek hi atom, dono process alag, aur dono ki sankhya bhi bahut alag (349 aur 1251). Ek mein electron **andar** aata hai (energy nikalti hai), doosre mein electron **bahar** jaata hai (energy lagti hai). Ek aur dekho: Na ki ionisation energy lagbhag 496 hai (Topic 2), par Na ki EA sirf lagbhag 53 hai (chhoti, kyunki metal electron lena nahi chahta). Dono ka mix-up kiya to jawab ulta ho jaayega.

Ek chhoti kahani: Sayali ne apni copy mein likha: "Cl ki EA = 1251." Mr. Ubale ne poocha: "Kya Cl electron lene par 1251 kJ per mol chhodta hai?" Sayali ne table dekhi: lene par 349 kJ per mol. 1251 to electron **nikaalne** par lagti hai (IE). Sayali ne kaat kar sahi likha: EA = 349, IE = 1251.

Ek aur tareeke se dekho. Cricket mein "ball pakadna" aur "ball fenkna" bilkul alag kaam hain: pehle mein haath mein ball **aati** hai, doosre mein haath se **jaati** hai. Ninad ke liye catch pakadna aasan (EA badi), par wo apni batting mein wicket se door khada rehta hai, matlab ek kaam ka josh doosre kaam ke josh se kuch nahi kehta. Isi tarah ek atom ki EA aur IE ek-doosre se alag sankhyaein hain.

## Quick Recap

- **Electron affinity** = gas ke ek atom mein ek electron judne par jitni energy **bahar nikalti** hai. X + e⁻ → X⁻ + energy. Unit: kJ per mol.
- EA aur IE ulte process hain: IE mein electron nikalta hai (energy lagti hai), EA mein electron judta hai (energy nikalti hai).
- Do ideas: **shells ki ginti** (door ka shell, kam pull) aur **nuclear pull**.
- Ek period mein baayen se daayen EA lagbhag badhti hai (Li 60, O 141, F 328). Group 17 mein neeche jaate hue ghatti hai (Cl 349, Br 325, I 295).
- **Cl** ki EA sabse badi hai (common elements mein). F thodi kam hai: "aage ki class mein".
- **Noble gases** (Ne, Ar): bhara shell, EA lagbhag 0. **Metals**: EA chhoti, wo dene mein khush.
- Anion ban-na: Cl⁻, F⁻, O²⁻. Cl⁻ electrolysis mein anode par jaata hai.
- NaCl mein "Na gives, Cl takes": Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ ka jodi.

### Answers

1. Order: **Cl > Br > I** (349 > 325 > 295, lagbhag). Reason: Cl mein aane wala electron 3rd shell mein baithta hai (paas), Br mein 4th, I mein 5th (door). Door ka shell aur zyada shielding matlab nuclear pull kam, isliye kam energy chhoot-ti hai. **Jaanch:** 349 > 325 > 295 ✅.
2. Energy = 6 × 325 = **1950 kJ** (lagbhag). **Jaanch:** 1950 ÷ 6 = 325 ✅.
3. Ar ka configuration 2, 8, 8: bhara hua shell, koi khaali jagah nahi. Naya electron door ke 4th shell mein jaata, jahan bhare hue andar ke shells use dhakelte hain, isliye koi fayda nahi aur EA lagbhag 0. S (2, 8, 6) mein baahari shell mein do jagah khaali hain: aane wala electron isi 3rd shell mein, nucleus ke 16 protons ke paas baithta hai, isliye energy nikalti hai aur EA badi (lagbhag 200).
4. (a) **S**: bahar ke shell mein 2 jagah khaali, EA lagbhag 200; Ne mein bhara shell, EA lagbhag 0. (b) **Br**: Br halogen hai, baahari shell mein sirf ek jagah khaali, EA lagbhag 325; Li metal hai, baahari 1 electron, pull kam, EA lagbhag 60. (c) **Br**: Br ka electron 4th shell mein, I ka 5th mein, to door wale I ki pull kam: Br 325 > I 295. **Jaanch:** 200 > 0 ✅, 325 > 60 ✅, 325 > 295 ✅.
5. **False** ❌. Dono ulte process hain. IE mein electron atom se **nikalta** hai aur energy **lagti** hai (jaise Cl → Cl⁺ + e⁻, lagbhag 1251). EA mein electron atom mein **judta** hai aur energy **nikalti** hai (jaise Cl + e⁻ → Cl⁻, lagbhag 349). Do alag sankhya, do alag process.
6. 2.5 × 200 = **500 kJ** (lagbhag). Cl ke liye 2.5 × 349 = **872.5 kJ** (lagbhag). Cl se 872.5 − 500 = 372.5 kJ zyada nikalti hai, kyunki Cl ki EA badi hai. **Jaanch:** 500 ÷ 2.5 = 200 ✅ aur 872.5 ÷ 2.5 = 349 ✅.
7. Li: 2, 1. Li⁺: 2 (He jaisa, ek electron diya). O ka anion O²⁻: 2, 8 (O: 2, 6, plus 2 electron). Li ke liye "**gives**" (Li⁺), O ke liye "**takes**" (O²⁻). **Jaanch:** O²⁻ mein 8 + 2 = 10 electron = 2 + 8 ✅.
8. Se Group 16 mein hai, S ke neeche: bahar 6 electron, do jagah khaali. Isliye ye electron lene ka rujhaan rakhta hai: EA **badi** (par S se lagbhag barabar ya thodi kam, kyunki neeche jaate hue door ka shell). Ye **anion** (Se²⁻) banata hai, cation nahi.
9. X (17 protons, 2, 8, 7) Cl hai: ek jagah khaali, **sabse badi EA** (lagbhag 349). Y (3 protons, 2, 1) Li hai: sirf 1 bahar ka electron, EA chhoti (lagbhag 60). Z (10 protons, 2, 8) Ne hai: bhara shell, EA **lagbhag zero** (sabse chhoti ya zero). Order: X > Y > Z ya zero. **Jaanch:** 349 > 60 > 0 ✅.
10. Li: 2 × 60 = **120 kJ**. O: 1 × 141 = **141 kJ**. Aadhe moles ke bawajood O zyada energy chhodta hai (141 > 120). Matlab non-metal (O) electron lene mein metal (Li) se kahin zyada josheela hai; metal ka josh electron dene mein hai (Topic 2). **Jaanch:** 120 ÷ 2 = 60 ✅ aur 141 > 120 ✅.
11. **Galat.** Table: Cl 349, Br 325, I 295. I sabse bada hai (5 shells), par uski EA sabse **chhoti** hai, kyunki aane wala electron door ke shell mein jaata hai aur pull kam hai. Bada size matlab badi EA nahi.
12. Energy = 4 × 295 = **1180 kJ** (lagbhag). Cl (349), Br (325) aur F (328) sab ki EA I (295) se **zyada** hai, to koi bhi I se kam nahi. Isliye **none**. **Jaanch:** 1180 ÷ 4 = 295 ✅.
13. **Na giver**, **Cl taker**. Na (2, 8, 1) → Na⁺ (2, 8), Cl (2, 8, 7) → Cl⁻ (2, 8, 8). Dono ke bahar ke shell bhare (noble gas jaise: Ne aur Ar), isliye dono stable. **Jaanch:** Na⁺ ka +1 aur Cl⁻ ka −1: kul 0 ✅.
