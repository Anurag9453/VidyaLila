# Topic 4: Electronegativity: Bond Mein Kheenchatan

Topic 2 mein humne dekha ki **akele** gaseous atom se electron nikalne mein kitni energy lagti hai (ionisation energy), aur Topic 3 mein ye ki **akela** gaseous atom ek electron pakadta hai to kitni energy nikalti hai (electron affinity). Dono baatein tab ki hain jab atom bilkul akela hai, koi padosi nahi. Par asli duniya mein atom aksar **kisi doosre atom ke saath bandhe** hote hain: H₂O mein, HCl mein, NaCl mein. Wahan ek naya sawaal uthta hai: do atom jab ek bond mein electrons **baantte** hain, to un shared electrons ko **kaun apni taraf zyada kheenchta hai**? Is topic ka jawab ek chhoti si sankhya hai, jise **electronegativity** kehte hain.

## Ek kambal, do bachche

Sardi ki raat thi. Mr. Vaidya apne bhatije Swanand ke saath ek rishtedaar ke ghar gaye the. Drawing room mein sofa par padosi ke do chhote bachche ek hi kambal mein dubke baithe the, aur tivi par kuch dekh rahe the. Swanand ne dekha ki kambal ek taraf **khisakta ja raha tha**: ek bachcha apni taraf zor se kheench raha tha, doosre ka kandha khula reh gaya aur wo thithurne laga.

Swanand ne dheere se kaha, "Chacha, kambal to dono ka hai, phir ek ke paas zyada kyun?" Mr. Vaidya muskuraye. "Kyunki dono ki **kheenchne ki taakat** barabar nahi hai. Agar dono barabar kheenchte, to kambal theek beech mein rehta aur dono ko aadha-aadha milta. Ek zyada zor lagaata hai, to kambal uski taraf khisakta hai. Aur agar ek itna zor lagaye ki **poora kambal** apni taraf kar le, to doosre ke paas kuch bacha hi nahi."

Swanand ki aankhein chamak gayi. "Chacha, ye to bond jaisa hai na? Do atom, aur beech mein shared electrons ka kambal!" Mr. Vaidya ne shabaashi di. "Bilkul. Har atom ki ek **kheenchne ki aadat** hoti hai. Use hi chemistry mein **electronegativity** kehte hain. Chalo, dekhte hain ki kis atom ki aadat kitni zor ki hai, aur wo table mein kahan kaise badalti hai."

```
   1) Dono barabar kheenchte hain       A  ----[ kambal ]----  B    kambal beech mein
   2) B thoda zyada kheenchta hai       A  ------[ kambal ]--  B    kambal B ki taraf khisak gaya
   3) B poora kheench leta hai          A         [ kambal ]-  B    kambal poora B ke paas
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Kambal ki tarah shared electrons: A aur B ki kheench ka farq jitna bada, kambal utna hi ek taraf. Yahi baat bond mein electrons ke jode (shared pair) ke saath hoti hai.

## Part A: Electronegativity kya hai, aur scale kaise padhein

**Electronegativity** ek atom ki wo **tendency** (jhukaav) hai ki jab wo kisi bond mein hai, to shared electrons ko **apni taraf** kheenche. Dhyan do do shabdon par: **bond mein** (akele atom ki baat nahi) aur **kheenchna** (electron ko poora lena nahi, bas zyada paas rakhna).

Iski koi **unit nahi** hoti: ye sirf ek tulna ki sankhya hai, jaise "is bachche mein kheench 3 numbers ki hai aur us mein 2 ki". Ise Pauling scale par naapte hain, aur hum sirf kuch jaane-pehchane **lagbhag** maan lenge (kuch jagah H ke liye 2.2 bhi likha milta hai; hum is chapter mein 2.1 lenge).

| Atom | H | Li | C | N | O | F | Na | Mg | Cl | K | Br |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Electronegativity (lagbhag) | 2.1 | 1.0 | 2.5 | 3.0 | 3.5 | 4.0 | 0.9 | 1.2 | 3.0 | 0.8 | 2.8 |

Teen cheezein dhyan se dekho:

- **Fluorine (F)** sabse upar hai: lagbhag **4.0**. Scale ka sabse bada number isi ka hai.
- **Sabse neeche** wo atom hain jo electron dene mein khush hain: caesium (Cs) aur francium (Fr), dono lagbhag **0.7 se 0.8** ke paas (ye dono Group 1 ke bahut neeche ke tatva hain; yahan sirf naam kaafi hai).
- **Hydrogen (2.1)** beech ka hai: na bahut zyada kheenchne wala, na bahut kam. Isliye wo kuch ke saath "dene wala" aur kuch ke saath "lene wala" ban jaata hai.

### Example 1. Table padhna

**Sawaal:** Na (0.9) aur Cl (3.0) mein se kiski kheench zyada hai? Farq kitna hai?

- Cl ki number 3.0 hai aur Na ki 0.9, to **Cl** zyada kheenchta hai.
- Farq = 3.0 − 0.9 = **2.1**.

**Jaanch:** 0.9 + 2.1 = 3.0 ✅.

Ye farq bahut bada hai. Aage dekhenge ki aisa farq kya batata hai.

## Part B: Table mein electronegativity ka pattern

Ye pattern bhi usi do-vichaar se samajh aata hai jo is chapter mein baar-baar aaye hain: **shells ki ginti** (doori) aur **nuclear pull** (protons ki kheench, beech ke shells ki rukavat ke baad).

Ek atom ko bond mein shared electron kheenchne hain. Ye kaam tab achha hota hai jab:

1. nucleus mein **protons zyada** hon (kheench badi), aur
2. shared electrons **nucleus ke paas** hon (shells kam, doori kam).

```
   Period 2:   Li 1.0   Be 1.5   B 2.0   C 2.5   N 3.0   O 3.5   F 4.0    (baayen se daayen BADHTI)
   Period 3:   Na 0.9   Mg 1.2   ...                              Cl 3.0
   Period 4:   K  0.8   ...                                       Br 2.8

   Group 1 mein Li 1.0 > Na 0.9 > K 0.8   (neeche jaate hue GHATTI)
   Group 17 mein F 4.0 > Cl 3.0 > Br 2.8  (neeche jaate hue GHATTI)

   Sabse zyada: F (daayen-upar)       Sabse kam: Cs, Fr (baayen-neeche)
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Electronegativity ka map (lagbhag values). Period ke saath baayen se daayen badhti hai, group mein upar se neeche ghatti hai. Noble gases (Group 18) ko yahan chhod diya hai: ve lagbhag koi bond hi nahi banate, isliye unki usual values nahi di jaati.

### Across a period: Li se F tak

Period 2 mein Li se F tak shells **wahi 2** rehte hain, par protons 3 se 9 tak badhte hain. Kheench badhti hai, atom chhota hota hai (Topic 1), shared electrons nucleus ke aur paas aa jaate hain. Isliye electronegativity 1.0 se 4.0 tak chadhti hai.

```
   Li  1.0  ##########
   Be  1.5  ###############
   B   2.0  ####################
   C   2.5  #########################
   N   3.0  ##############################
   O   3.5  ###################################
   F   4.0  ########################################
```

> **Figure 4.3** – Period 2 mein electronegativity (har # = 0.1). Li se F tak har kadam par bar lamba hota jaata hai.

### Example 2. Period 2 ka krama

**Sawaal:** Li, O aur C ko electronegativity ke badhte krama mein likho, aur karan bhi do.

- Teeno Period 2 mein hain, shells ki ginti ek jaisi (2).
- Protons: Li 3, C 6, O 8. Zyada protons, zyada kheench.
- **Krama: Li (1.0) < C (2.5) < O (3.5).**

**Jaanch:** 1.0 < 2.5 < 3.5 ✅, aur protons 3 < 6 < 8 ✅.

### Down a group: F se Br tak

Group 17 mein F → Cl → Br neeche jaate hue shells 2, 3, 4 hote hain. Shared electrons nucleus se door hote jaate hain, aur beech ke bhare shells kheench ko **rok** lete hain (bonfire ki beech ki rows jaisa). Isliye electronegativity ghatti hai.

```
   F   4.0  ########################################
   Cl  3.0  ##############################
   Br  2.8  ############################
```

> **Figure 4.4** – Group 17 mein neeche jaate hue electronegativity ghatti hai (har # = 0.1): F sabse upar aur sabse zyada, Br usse kam.

### Example 3. Group ka krama

**Sawaal:** Group 1 ke Li (1.0), Na (0.9) aur K (0.8) mein kiski kheench sabse kam hai, aur kyun?

- K ke 4 shells hain, Na ke 3, Li ke 2.
- K ka baahari electron nucleus se sabse door aur bahut shielded hai.
- Isliye K shared electron ko sabse kam zor se kheenchta hai: **K sabse kam**, krama **K (0.8) < Na (0.9) < Li (1.0)**.

**Jaanch:** 0.8 < 0.9 < 1.0 aur shells 4 > 3 > 2 ✅. (Ek aur decimal lein to K 0.82 aur Cs 0.79: Cs usse bhi thoda kam, jaisa group trend ka hukm hai.)

### Example 4. Do alag raaste ek saath

**Sawaal:** K, Mg aur Br mein se kiski kheench sabse zyada hai? (Period 4: K, Br. Period 3: Mg.)

- **K aur Br** ek hi period (4) mein hain: Br mein 35 protons, K mein 19. Br ki kheench zyada.
- **Mg** (Period 3, sirf 3 shells) aur **K** (4 shells): Mg ki kheench K se zyada.
- Sabse zyada **Br**.

**Jaanch:** values K 0.8 < Mg 1.2 < Br 2.8 ✅.

**Ek aur baat:** noble gases ki alag se usual electronegativity nahi di jaati, kyunki ye bond banate hi nahi (ya bahut kam). Aage ki class mein isi par aur baat hogi.

## Part C: Electronegativity aur electron affinity: alag cheezein

Ye dono naam milte-julte lagte hain, par ye **do alag** cheezein hain.

| Baat | Electron affinity (Topic 3) | Electronegativity (ye topic) |
|---|---|---|
| Atom kahan hai? | **Akela**, gaseous, koi padosi nahi | **Bond ke andar**, padosi ke saath |
| Kya hota hai? | Atom ek **poora electron le leta hai** | Shared electrons ko **zyada paas** kheenchta hai |
| Kaise naapte hain? | Energy se (kJ per mol) | Tulna ki sankhya (**koi unit nahi**) |
| Kiska sawaal? | "Is atom ko electron lena kitna pasand hai?" | "Is bond mein is atom ki kheench padosi se kitni zyada hai?" |

### Example 5. Ek hi sankhya, alag kahani

**Sawaal:** Nitrogen (3.0) aur chlorine (3.0) ki electronegativity ek hi hai. To kya dono ki electron affinity bhi ek jaisi hogi?

- Nahi. Chlorine ki electron affinity bahut badi hai (Topic 3: sabse zyada, lagbhag 349 kJ per mol).
- Nitrogen ki electron affinity zero ke bahut paas hai.
- Matlab ek hi bond-kheench par do atomon ka akele rehkar electron lene ka josh alag ho sakta hai.

**Jaanch:** electronegativity bond-vaali tulna hai (koi unit nahi) aur electron affinity gaseous atom ki energy hai (kJ per mol): dono ke naap hi alag hain ✅.

Ek honest line: fluorine ki electron affinity chlorine se thodi kam hai, par uski electronegativity sabse zyada hai. Ye kyun hai, isse **aage ki class mein** samjhenge. Abhi itna kaafi hai ki ye do alag cheezein hain.

## Part D: Bond mein negative end kaun?

Ab electronegativity ka sabse bada **upyog**: kisi bhi bond mein pata karna ki **shared pair kis taraf khisakta hai**.

Teen step:

1. Bond ke dono atomon ki electronegativity table se dekho.
2. Jiski number **zyada**, wo shared pair ko apni taraf kheenchta hai: wahi **negative end**.
3. Doosra atom **positive end** hai.

### Example 6. HCl

H = 2.1, Cl = 3.0. Cl ki number zyada, to **Cl** shared pair ko apni taraf kheenchta hai. Cl thoda negative, H thoda positive end. Farq = 3.0 − 2.1 = **0.9**.

**Jaanch:** 3.0 > 2.1 ✅ aur 3.0 − 2.1 = 0.9 ✅.

### Example 7. H₂O

H = 2.1, O = 3.5. Paani mein **do** O–H bond hain. Har ek mein O zyada kheenchta hai. Farq = 3.5 − 2.1 = **1.4**. Isliye O **negative end** hai aur dono H positive end.

**Jaanch:** 3.5 > 2.1 ✅ aur 3.5 − 2.1 = 1.4 ✅.

### Example 8. CO₂

C = 2.5, O = 3.5. Har C=O bond mein O ki number zyada, to **O negative end**, C positive end. Farq = 3.5 − 2.5 = **1.0**.

**Jaanch:** 3.5 − 2.5 = 1.0 ✅.

### Example 9. H₂ aur Cl₂

H₂ mein dono atom H hain (2.1 aur 2.1): farq **0.0**. Dono ki kheench barabar, shared pair theek beech mein. Cl₂ mein bhi Cl (3.0) aur Cl (3.0): farq **0.0**. Koi negative end ya positive end nahi.

**Jaanch:** ek hi tatva ke do atom, to farq hamesha 0.0 ✅.

```
   H2      H : H          pair theek beech mein       farq 0.0
   HCl     H  :  Cl       pair Cl ki taraf khisaka    farq 0.9    H thoda +, Cl thoda -
   H2O     H :  O  : H    pair O ki taraf             farq 1.4    H thoda +, O thoda -
```

> **Figure 4.5** – Teen bond mein shared pair ki jagah: H₂ mein beech mein, HCl aur H₂O mein zyada electronegative atom (Cl, O) ki taraf. (Ye sirf bond ki jhalak hai: molecules ka asli shape yahan nahi dikhaya.)

## Part E: Farq bada ho to bond ka swabhav

Class 9 mein humne **ionic** bond (electron poora dena-lena) aur **covalent** bond (electron baantna) alag-alag seekhe the. Electronegativity ka farq in dono ko **ek hi line par** laa deta hai:

- **Farq 0** (ek jaise atom): shared pair bilkul beech mein. Shuddh covalent bond.
- **Chhota farq:** shared pair thoda ek taraf khisakta hai, par baanta zaroor hai. Covalent bond, par barabar nahi.
- **Bahut bada farq (lagbhag 2 ya usse zyada):** ek atom itna zor lagaata hai ki shared pair **lagbhag poora** usi ke paas chala jaata hai. Ab wo atom electron le chuka jaisa lagta hai: bond **ionic** jaisa ban jaata hai.

Ye koi sakht deewar nahi hai, ek **dheere-dheere badalti line** hai: farq badhne par bond barabar baantne se, ek taraf jhukne tak, aur phir ionic ki taraf jaata hai.

```
   farq = 0         chhota farq                    bahut bada farq
   H : H            H  :  Cl                       Na+   Cl-
   barabar          ek taraf jhuka hua baantna     electron lagbhag poora ek taraf
   (covalent)       (covalent, barabar nahi)       (ionic)
```

> **Figure 4.6** – Farq badhne par bond ka swabhav: pehle barabar baantna, phir ek taraf jhuka hua baantna, aur bahut bade farq par ionic. Beech ki jagah ka matlab hai ki bond sirf do dabbon mein nahi baante jaate.

### Example 10. NaCl: bahut bada farq

Na = 0.9, Cl = 3.0. Farq = 3.0 − 0.9 = **2.1**, bahut bada. Cl shared pair ko lagbhag poora apni taraf kheench leta hai, aur Na ek electron deta hua dikhta hai: **Na⁺ aur Cl⁻**. Yahi hamari Class 9 ki "Na deta hai, Cl leta hai" kahani hai, aur ab ye number se bhi dikhta hai. Ye Topic 2 (Na ka ionisation energy kam) aur Topic 3 (Cl ka electron affinity bahut zyada) se bhi milta hai: dono baatein ek hi disha mein ishara karti hain.

**Jaanch:** 3.0 − 0.9 = 2.1 ✅ aur ye 2 se zyada hai ✅.

### Example 11. HF: sabse zyada jhukaav

H = 2.1, F = 4.0. Farq = 4.0 − 2.1 = **1.9**. Ye HCl ke 0.9 se bahut bada hai, isliye F shared pair ko aur zyada apni taraf kheenchta hai. Phir bhi HF ek **molecule** hai: farq 2 se thoda neeche hai, to ye bond covalent aur ionic ke beech ki dhundhli seema ke paas hai.

**Jaanch:** 4.0 − 2.1 = 1.9 ✅ aur 1.9 > 0.9 (HCl) ✅.

### Example 12. Kaun sa bond zyada ek taraf?

**Sawaal:** HCl (0.9), HF (1.9) aur H₂O ke O–H (1.4) mein shared pair sabse zyada kis bond mein ek taraf khisakta hai?

- Jis bond mein farq sabse bada, wahan pair sabse zyada ek taraf.
- Krama (badhta jhukaav): **HCl (0.9) < O–H (1.4) < HF (1.9)**.
- HF mein pair F ki taraf sabse zyada khisakta hai.

**Jaanch:** 0.9 < 1.4 < 1.9 ✅.

**Ek line aage ke liye:** jab shared pair ek taraf khisakta hai to bond ko ek khaas naam milta hai, aur uska asar molecule ke gun par padta hai. Ye **Chapter 7 "Chemical Bonding II"** mein detail mein aayega. Yahan sirf itna dhyan rakho ki farq hi sab kuch batata hai.

## Khud karke dekho: kambal ki jagah tauliya

(Bade ki maujoodgi mein, aur halke haath se. Koi chemical nahi.)

Do doston ko ek chhoti tauliya ke dono sire pakadne do. Pehle dono **barabar** halke se kheenchein: tauliya beech mein rehti hai (jaise H₂). Phir ek dost thoda zyada kheenche: tauliya uski taraf khisakti hai (jaise HCl). Ab ek dost itna kheenche ki poori tauliya uske paas chali jaaye: doosra khaali haath (jaise NaCl). Dhyan rahe: **kheenchna halka rakhein**, jhatka nahi, kisi ko chot na lage.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Sabse zyada electronegative tatva sabse reactive metal hota hai."**
Dekho: sabse zyada electronegative **F** hai, aur wo ek **non-metal** hai (halogen). Sabse kam electronegative tatva **Cs aur Fr** hain, aur ve **bahut reactive metals** hain (Group 1). Matlab "bahut kheenchne wala" aur "reactive metal" ek hi cheez nahi hai: reactive metal wo hai jo electron **dene** mein khush hai, aur uski kheench kam hoti hai. Demo: F (4.0) ko "sabse bada kheenchne wala" maan lo, to wo electron lene wala non-metal hai, dene wala nahi. Ab K (0.8) ko dekho: bahut kam kheench, isliye wo apna baahari electron aasani se de deta hai (Topic 2), aur isi wajah se Group 1 ke metals tez react karte hain (sirf padhne ke liye). Matlab: bada number = lene wala, chhota number = dene wala, aur "reactive metal" chhote number wala hota hai.

**Galti 2: "Electronegativity wo energy hai jo electron lene par nikalti hai."**
Ye definition **electron affinity** ki hai (Topic 3), electronegativity ki nahi. Demo: Cl ki electronegativity 3.0 hai. Agar koi kahe "3.0 kJ per mol", to galat: electronegativity ke saath **koi unit nahi** hoti, aur Cl ki electron affinity lagbhag 349 kJ per mol hai: dono alag sankhya hain (Example 5). Electronegativity ek **tulna** hai bond mein, energy nahi.

## Quick Recap

- **Electronegativity** = bond mein shared electrons ko apni taraf kheenchne ki atom ki tendency. Koi unit nahi.
- Scale par **F lagbhag 4.0** sabse upar; **Cs aur Fr lagbhag 0.7 se 0.8** sabse neeche; H lagbhag 2.1, C 2.5, N 3.0, O 3.5, Cl 3.0, Na 0.9, K 0.8 (sab lagbhag).
- **Period mein baayen se daayen badhti hai** (protons badhe, shells wahi). **Group mein neeche ghatti hai** (shells badhe, shielding badhi).
- Electronegativity (bond ke andar, unit nahi) aur electron affinity (akela atom, kJ per mol) alag hain.
- Bond mein **zyada number wala atom negative end** hota hai.
- Farq 0: barabar baantna. Chhota farq: ek taraf jhuka hua baantna. Bahut bada farq (lagbhag 2 ya zyada): ionic jaisa, jaise NaCl (2.1).
- Polar aur non-polar naam **Chapter 7** mein.

2. (a) Br (2.8 > 2.1). (b) Cl (3.0 > 2.5). (c) F (4.0 > 3.5). (d) O (3.5 > 3.0). (e) F (4.0 > 0.8). Jaanch: har case mein bade number wala negative end ✅.
3. (a) Cl–Cl: 3.0 − 3.0 = 0.0, equal sharing. (b) K–F: 4.0 − 0.8 = 3.2, very large, ionic-like. (c) N–H: 3.0 − 2.1 = 0.9, slightly unequal. (d) C–Br: 2.8 − 2.5 = 0.3, slightly unequal (bahut chhota). Jaanch: 0.0, 3.2, 0.9, 0.3 ✅.
4. C–H: 2.5 − 2.1 = 0.4. N–H: 3.0 − 2.1 = 0.9. Br–H: 2.8 − 2.1 = 0.7. Krama (kam se zyada jhukaav): **C–H (0.4) < Br–H (0.7) < N–H (0.9)**. Sabse zyada positive hydrogen **N–H** mein. Jaanch: 0.4 < 0.7 < 0.9 ✅.
5. (a) **False** ❌: electronegativity ki koi unit nahi hoti. (b) **True** ✅. (c) **False** ❌: Cs ki Li se kam hai, kyunki Cs ke zyada shells hain. (d) **False** ❌: Cl–Cl mein dono ki kheench barabar hai (0.0 ka farq). (e) **True** ✅.
6. Br ke 4 shells hain aur Cl ke 3. Br mein shared electrons nucleus se door hote hain aur beech ke bhare shells kheench rok lete hain, to Br ki kheench Cl se kam (2.8 < 3.0).
7. C aur O dono Period 2 mein hain, shells wahi 2. O ke 8 protons hain aur C ke 6, to O ki kheench zyada. Shared electrons O ke nucleus ke aur paas rehte hain. Isliye O (3.5) > C (2.5).
8. Period 3 mein Cl (3.0) sabse zyada (table mein Period 3 ka Cl). Na, K, Mg mein sabse kam **K (0.8)**: Na 0.9, Mg 1.2.
9. Electron affinity akele gaseous atom ki baat hai aur kJ per mol mein naapte hain; electronegativity bond ke andar ki baat hai aur isme koi unit nahi. Pehli ek poora electron lene ki energy hai, doosri shared pair ko zyada paas kheenchne ki aadat.
10. (a) N₂: N–N, farq 0.0, **equal sharing**. (b) MgO: 3.5 − 1.2 = 2.3, **ionic-like**. (c) CH₄: 2.5 − 2.1 = 0.4, **unequal sharing** (bahut thoda). (d) KCl: 3.0 − 0.8 = 2.2, **ionic-like**. Jaanch: 0.0, 2.3, 0.4, 2.2 ✅.
11. Neon ke baahari shell bhare hain aur wo lagbhag koi bond nahi banata. Electronegativity bond ki baat hai, isliye usual table mein Ne ke liye value nahi di jaati.
12. Farq = 4.0 − 0.7 = **3.3**, bahut bada. Cs–F ka bond **ionic-like** hoga: F shared pair lagbhag poora le lega. Jaanch: 4.0 − 0.7 = 3.3 ✅.
13. Kambal = shared electrons ka jodaa. Kheenchne ki taakat = electronegativity. Ek bachcha poora kambal le le to doosre ke paas kuch nahi bachta: yaani bahut bade farq par electron lagbhag poora ek atom ke paas, ionic jaisa bond.
