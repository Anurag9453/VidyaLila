# Topic 5: Metallic aur Non-metallic Character: Sab Ek Saath

Pichhle topics mein humne ek hi atom ko kai angle se dekha: kitna bada hai (size), electron nikalne mein kitni mehnat lagti hai (ionisation energy), electron lene mein kitna josh hai (electron affinity), aur bond mein electron kitne zor se kheenchta hai (electronegativity). Ab sawaal hai: **in sab ka asli nateeja kya hai?** Koi tatva "dhatu jaisa" kyun vyavhaar karta hai aur koi "adhaatu jaisa"? Is topic mein hum chaaron ko ek saath jodte hain, aur table mein sirf **jagah** dekh kar tatva ke baare mein lagbhag sab kuch bata dene ki kala seekhte hain: usse reactivity, oxide ki prakriti aur ion tak.

## Lending library ka khel

Mr. Yewale ki bhanji Kaveri chhuttiyon mein unke yahan aayi thi. Mohalle ki ek chhoti lending library mein Mr. Yewale ka khaata tha, aur Kaveri ko wo wahan le gaye. Library ke bahar ek board laga tha: "Dene wale sadasya" aur "Lene wale sadasya".

Kaveri ne dekha ki kuch sadasya hamesha kitaab **dene** ke liye taiyaar rehte the: bas koi maange, to haath badha kar de dete. Doosre sadasya hamesha kitaab **lene** ke chakkar mein rehte, aur ek baar le lene ke baad kabhi waapas nahi dete. Aur kuch beech wale the, jo kabhi dete, kabhi lete.

"Mama, ye sab alag-alag kyun hain?" Kaveri ne poochha. "Ek hi library mein sab ek jaise kyun nahi?"

Mr. Yewale muskuraye. "Dekho, jinki kitaab unse door, ooperi manzil par rakhi hai aur jinki pakad dheeli hai, wo dene mein aasaan hain. Aur jinka sanduk bas **ek ya do kitaab se adhoora** hai aur jo use poora karna chahte hain, wo lene mein utsuk hain. Tatvon mein bhi bilkul yahi hota hai. **Electron wo kitaab hai.** Kuch atom use dene mein khush rehte hain, kuch lene mein."

```
   Library ka board

   DENE WALE (lender)                     LENE WALE (borrower)
   kitaab door aur halki pakad            kitaab ki kami, pakad majboot

   Na  Mg  Al   <----  beech ke  ---->   P   S   Cl
   (dhatu)            sadasya (Si)     (adhaatu)

   dete hain  ----------------------->  lete hain
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Period 3 ko library ka board maano: baayen taraf ke tatva electron (kitaab) **dene** mein aasaan, daayen taraf ke **lene** mein aasaan, aur beech mein Si jaise tatva donon ke beech jhoolte hain.

Wapas ghar jaate hue Kaveri ne ek line apni copy mein likhi: "Dhatu = dene wala. Adhaatu = lene wala." Mr. Yewale ne kaha: "Bilkul sahi. Ab isi line ko poori table tak failate hain."

## Part A: Metallic character: electron dene ki aasani

**Metallic character** matlab atom electron **kitni aasani se chhodta** hai. Ye seedha **ionisation energy** (Topic 2) se juda hai: jis atom ki ionisation energy kam, wo electron aasani se deta hai, to uska metallic character zyada.

**Ek period mein baayen se daayen: metallic character ghatta hai.** Reason wahi do idea hain jo poore chapter mein chale: shells (doori) aur nuclear pull (kheench, jismein beech ke shells ka parda bhi gina jaata hai). Period mein shells wahi rehte hain, par protons badhte jaate hain, to kheench badhti hai, baahari electron zor se pakda jaata hai aur nikalna mushkil ho jaata hai.

**Ek group mein upar se neeche: metallic character badhta hai.** Neeche shells badhte hain, baahari electron nucleus se door aur beech ke shells ke parde ke peeche hota hai, to aasani se nikalta hai.

Lagbhag values se dekhte hain:

| Tatva | Pehli ionisation energy (kJ per mol, lagbhag) | Atomic radius (pm, lagbhag) |
|---|---|---|
| Li | 520 | 152 |
| Na | 496 | 186 |
| K | 419 | 227 |
| Mg | 738 | 160 |
| Ca | 590 | 197 |
| Cl | 1251 | 99 |

Group 1 mein Li se K ki taraf: ionisation energy 520 → 496 → 419 (ghatti hai) aur radius 152 → 186 → 227 (badhta hai). Dono ek hi kahani kehte hain: baahari electron door aur dheela, to **metallic character badhta hai**. Period 3 mein Na (496), Mg (738) aur Cl (1251): ionisation energy badhti hai, to metallic character ghatta hai. (Beech ke kuch tatvon mein ionisation energy ke chhote utaar-chadhav aate hain; unka kaaran aage ki class mein.)

**Non-metallic character** iska ulta hai: atom electron **lene** mein kitna utsuk hai. Ye electron affinity (Topic 3) aur electronegativity (Topic 4) se juda hai, jo dono bade hote hain to non-metallic character zyada. Isliye ye trend metallic character ka **ulta** chalta hai:

- Period mein baayen se daayen: **badhta** hai (kheench badhti hai).
- Group mein upar se neeche: **ghatta** hai (shell door hoti jaati hai).

```
   Metallic character                 Non-metallic character

   Period 3:  Na  Mg  Al  Si  P  S  Cl
              most ----------> least    (metallic)
              least ---------> most     (non-metallic)

   Group 1:   Li           Group 17:  F
              Na                      Cl
              K                       Br
              v  metallic badhta      v  non-metallic ghatta
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Period mein dono character ek-dusre ke ulte chalte hain. Group 1 mein neeche utarte hue metallic character badhta hai; Group 17 mein neeche utarte hue non-metallic character ghatta hai.

Ye yaad rakhne ka sabse aasaan tareeka: **table mein baayen aur neeche: sabse zyada metallic (Cs, Fr jaise). Daayen aur upar: sabse zyada non-metallic (F).** Noble gases ki baat alag hai: unka shell pehle se poora hai, na wo dene aate hain na lene.

Table ki seedhi (staircase) aur metalloids Class 9 mein aaye the ([periodic-table-part4.md](../../../class9/chemistry/periodic-table/periodic-table-part4.md)). Ab unka reason saaf hai: seedhi wahi jagah hai jahan dene aur lene ki pravritti lagbhag barabar ho jaati hai. B aur Si metalloid hain. Group 13 mein ek mazedaar baat dekho: **B metalloid hai, Al dhatu, aur uske neeche Ga (sirf naam) bhi dhatu**. Group mein neeche utarte hue tatva dhatu-pan ki taraf badhta gaya.

### Example 1. Na, Mg, Al: kaun zyada metallic?

- Teeno ke 3 shells. Protons 11, 12, 13. Valence electrons 1, 2, 3.
- Shells wahi, protons badhe, to kheench badhi, atom chhota hua (186, 160, 143 pm lagbhag), aur electron nikalna mushkil.
- **Na > Mg > Al** metallic character mein.

**Jaanch:** radius 186 > 160 > 143 aur electron ginti 1 < 2 < 3 dono isi order ko dete hain ✅.

### Example 2. Li, Na, K: kaun zyada metallic?

- Li (2 shells), Na (3 shells), K (4 shells). Ionisation energy 520, 496, 419 lagbhag.
- Ionisation energy sabse kam K ki hai, to wo electron sabse aasani se deta hai.
- **K > Na > Li**.

**Jaanch:** 419 < 496 < 520 ✅ aur radius 227 > 186 > 152 ✅.

### Example 3. Ca aur Mg: do valence electron wale dhatu

- Dono ke 2 valence electrons. Ca ke 4 shells, Mg ke 3.
- Ca ka baahari electron zyada door, to ionisation energy kam: 590 lagbhag (Mg ki 738 lagbhag).
- **Ca zyada metallic**.

**Jaanch:** Class 10 Chapter 2 ki reactivity series mein bhi Ca, Mg se upar hai ✅.

### Example 4. Si, P, S, Cl: kaun zyada non-metallic?

- Chaaron 3 shells wale, valence electrons 4, 5, 6, 7.
- Protons 14, 15, 16, 17 badhte hain, to kheench badhti hai aur electron lene ki taqat badhti hai.
- **Cl > S > P > Si** non-metallic character mein.

**Jaanch:** radius lagbhag 118, 110, 103, 99 pm: atom jitna chhota, utna kheench ka asar zyada. Order ek-doosre ke saath milta hai ✅.

## Part B: Reactivity: kaun kitni tezi se react karta hai

**Dhatu ki reactivity** matlab wo kitni aasani se electron dekar react karti hai. Isliye **dhatu ki reactivity usi order mein chalti hai jis order mein metallic character chalta hai.**

- Group mein neeche: **badhti** hai (K > Na > Li).
- Period mein baayen se daayen: **ghat-ti** hai (Na > Mg > Al).

**Adhaatu ki reactivity** matlab wo kitni tezi se electron **lekar** react karti hai. To ye non-metallic character ke saath chalti hai.

- Group mein upar se neeche: **ghat-ti** hai (F > Cl > Br > I).
- Period mein baayen se daayen: **badhti** hai (Si < P < S < Cl).

(Dhyan do: noble gases ki baat yahan nahi, kyunki wo apni poori shell ki wajah se react hi nahi karte.)

Ye wahi cheez hai jo hum Class 9 ki families wali file mein dekh chuke hain ([periodic-table-part5.md](../../../class9/chemistry/periodic-table/periodic-table-part5.md)), par ab hum reason ko ionisation energy aur electron affinity tak le ja sakte hain.

### Dhatuon ki reactivity: sirf padhne ke liye (teacher demo)

Ye sab **sirf padhne ke liye ya teacher ke dikhane ke liye** hai. Alkali metals aur garam paani, steam: kabhi student ke haath mein nahi.

| Dhatu | Thande paani ke saath (sirf padhne ke liye) | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| Na | Tez, hilta-dulta, gas aur garmi | 1 electron, door aur dheela |
| K | Aur bhi tez, hydrogen ko jalaa deta hai | Na se bhi door electron |
| Ca | Dheeme se, lagataar bulbule | 2 electron, Mg se door |
| Mg | Bahut dheere (bina garmi ke lagbhag nahi dikhta) | 2 electron, Ca se paas |
| Al | Seedha nahi; ooper ki patli Al₂O₃ ki parat roke rakhti hai | 3 electron, kheench zyada |

(Ye sirf ek tasveer hai; asli prayog hamesha teacher ke upar chhodte hain. Sr, yaani strontium, sirf naam ke roop mein: Ca ke neeche hai, isliye wo Ca se bhi zyada tezi se react karta hai.)

Ek equation: **2Na + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + H₂**. Atom count: baayen Na 2, H 4, O 2; daayen Na 2, O 2, H 2 + 2 = 4 ✅.

### Adhaatuon ki reactivity: halogens ki seedhi (sirf padhne ke liye)

Class 9 mein ek displacement reaction dekha tha: Cl₂ + 2KBr → 2KCl + Br₂ ([periodic-table-part5.md](../../../class9/chemistry/periodic-table/periodic-table-part5.md)). Ab usi se **poori seedhi** bana lete hain. Ye sirf teacher ke dekhne ke liye hai: chlorine aur bromine zehreeli hain.

Ek zyada reactive halogen kam reactive halogen ko uske compound (salt) se bahar nikaal deta hai:

- Cl₂ + 2KBr → 2KCl + Br₂ (Cl₂ ne Br₂ ko nikaala)
- Cl₂ + 2NaI → 2NaCl + I₂ (Cl₂ ne I₂ ko nikaala)
- Br₂ + 2KI → 2KBr + I₂ (Br₂ ne I₂ ko nikaala)
- I₂ + KBr → **koi reaction nahi** (I₂ kamzor hai, Br₂ ko nahi nikaal sakta)
- Br₂ + KCl → **koi reaction nahi**

```
   Halogens ki seedhi (displacement)

        F₂   ------  sabse zyada reactive (upar)
         :
        Cl₂  ------  F₂ se kam, Br₂ se zyada
         :
        Br₂  ------  Cl₂ se kam, I₂ se zyada
         :
        I₂   ------  sabse kam reactive (neeche)

   Upar wala neeche wale ko uske salt se nikaal sakta hai.
   Neeche wala upar wale ko nahi nikaal sakta.
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Halogens ki displacement seedhi. Upar wala halogen apne neeche wale halogen ko uske salt (jaise KBr, KI) se bahar nikaal deta hai; ulta kabhi nahi hota.

Ye seedha **non-metallic character ke trend** se milta hai: Group 17 mein neeche utarte hue electron lene ki taqat ghatti hai.

### Example 5. Kya reaction hoga?

**Sawaal:** (a) Cl₂ + KI (b) Br₂ + NaCl (c) I₂ + KBr. Reaction hoga ya nahi?

- (a) Cl₂, I₂ se upar hai: **hoga**. Cl₂ + 2KI → 2KCl + I₂.
- (b) Br₂, Cl₂ se neeche hai: **nahi hoga**.
- (c) I₂ sabse neeche: **nahi hoga**.

**Jaanch (a):** baayen Cl 2, K 2, I 2; daayen K 2, Cl 2, I 2 ✅. (b), (c) mein neeche wala halogen upar wale ke salt ko chhed raha hai, jo seedhi ke niyam ke khilaf hai ✅.

## Part C: Oxide ki prakriti Period 3 mein: dhatu-pan se adhaatu-pan ka safar

Ab ek mazedaar jagah jahan dhatu-pan aur adhaatu-pan **dikhte** hain: tatva ke **oxide** ki prakriti. Chapter 1 mein humne seekha tha ki dhatu ke oxide basic hote hain aur adhaatu ke acidic (jo paani mein ghulne par kshaar ya amla dete hain). Chapter 2 mein Al₂O₃ amphoteric dekha. Ab Period 3 mein ek hi qatar mein sabko dekhte hain:

| Tatva | Oxide | Prakriti | Paani ke saath (jahan ghulta hai) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Na | Na₂O | Basic | Ghulkar NaOH (kshaar) |
| Mg | MgO | Basic | Bahut kam ghulta hai, kamzor kshaar |
| Al | Al₂O₃ | Amphoteric | Nahi ghulta; acid aur base dono se milta hai |
| Si | SiO₂ | Kamzor acidic | Nahi ghulta (ret, kaanch ka mukhya hissa) |
| P | P₄O₁₀ | Acidic | Ghulkar H₃PO₄ |
| S | SO₃ | Acidic | Ghulkar H₂SO₄ |
| Cl | Cl₂O₇ | Bahut zyada acidic | Ghulkar HClO₄ |

```
   Dhatu  ------------------------------------------>  Adhaatu

   Na₂O    MgO    Al₂O₃       SiO₂      P₄O₁₀   SO₃    Cl₂O₇
   basic   basic  amphoteric  kamzor    acidic  acidic  bahut
   (tez)   (kam)              acidic                    acidic

   <---- basic-pan ghatta hai --------------------------->
                  ---------------------> acidic-pan badhta hai
```

> **Figure 5.4** – Period 3 ke oxides: Na₂O aur MgO basic, Al₂O₃ beech mein (amphoteric), aur SiO₂ se Cl₂O₇ tak acidic-pan badhta hai. Ye wahi safar hai jo dhatu-pan se adhaatu-pan tak ka hai.

Reason kya hai? Baayen ke tatva (Na, Mg) electron dete hain, to unke oxide mein dhatu ion hota hai, jo paani mein ghulne par OH⁻ jaisa kshaar banata hai. Daayen ke tatva (P, S, Cl) electron lene ya baantne wale hain, to unka oxide paani mein ghulne par H⁺ (amla) deta hai. Beech ka Al dono taraf ka vyavhaar karta hai.

Kuch equations (sirf padhne ke liye; SO₃ aur Cl₂O₇ bahut tez aur khatarnak hote hain):

- Na₂O + H₂O → 2NaOH. Atom: Na 2, H 2, O 2 → Na 2, O 2, H 2 ✅.
- SO₃ + H₂O → H₂SO₄. Atom: S 1, O 4, H 2 → S 1, O 4, H 2 ✅.
- P₄O₁₀ + 6H₂O → 4H₃PO₄. Atom: P 4, O 16, H 12 → P 4, O 16, H 12 ✅.
- Cl₂O₇ + H₂O → 2HClO₄. Atom: Cl 2, O 8, H 2 → Cl 2, O 8, H 2 ✅.
- Al₂O₃ + 6HCl → 2AlCl₃ + 3H₂O (acid ke saath: base jaisa vyavhaar). Atom: Al 2, O 3, H 6, Cl 6 ✅.
- Al₂O₃ + 2NaOH → 2NaAlO₂ + H₂O (base ke saath: acid jaisa vyavhaar). Atom: Al 2, O 5, Na 2, H 2 ✅.

Dhyan do: SiO₂ ko paani ke saath nahi, **garam, tez NaOH** ke saath milaane par hi kuch hota hai (SiO₂ + 2NaOH → Na₂SiO₃ + H₂O, sirf padhne ke liye). Isliye usse "kamzor acidic" kehte hain. Cl₂O₇ bahut asthir aur khatarnak hai: sirf padhne ke liye.

**Group 1 ke hydroxides:** Group mein neeche utarte hue dhatu-pan badhta hai, to uska hydroxide **aur zyada tez base** banta hai: LiOH < NaOH < KOH (lagbhag, tez basic-pan). Reason: dhatu jitna zyada electron dene mein tatpar, utna wo OH⁻ ko chhodne mein asaani deta hai. (NaOH aur KOH tez kshaar hain jo tvacha ko jalate hain: kabhi chhona nahi, sirf padhne ke liye.)

### Example 6. Oxide pehchano

**Sawaal:** Ek tatva Period 3 mein hai aur uska oxide paani mein ghulkar tez kshaar banata hai. Kaun sa tatva ho sakta hai?

- Tez kshaar banane wala oxide Period 3 ke baayen kone ka hota hai.
- **Na**: Na₂O + H₂O → 2NaOH.

**Jaanch:** Na ke 1 valence electron, Na₂O mein 2 × (+1) = +2 aur O ka −2, total 0 ✅.

### Example 7. Order mein likho

**Sawaal:** Na₂O, SO₃, Al₂O₃, MgO ko sabse zyada basic se sabse zyada acidic ke order mein likho.

- Na₂O (sabse basic), MgO, Al₂O₃ (beech), SO₃ (acidic).
- **Na₂O > MgO > Al₂O₃ > SO₃** basic-pan mein.

**Jaanch:** Figure 5.4 mein yahi order baayen se daayen hai ✅.

## Part D: Sab ek saath: position dekh kar sab kuch bata do

Ab tak hum alag-alag properties dekh rahe the. Ek poora "trend map" banate hain jismein chhah properties ek saath hain (sab ke liye lagbhag, general trend; chhote apwaad aage ki class mein):

```
   Ek PERIOD mein baayen se daayen  ------------->

   Atomic size:            ghatta hai
   Ionisation energy:      badhti hai
   Electron affinity:      badhti hai
   Electronegativity:      badhti hai
   Metallic character:     ghatta hai
   Non-metallic character: badhta hai

   Ek GROUP mein upar se neeche (v)

   Atomic size:            badhta hai
   Ionisation energy:      ghatti hai
   Electron affinity:      ghatti hai
   Electronegativity:      ghatti hai
   Metallic character:     badhta hai
   Non-metallic character: ghatta hai
```

> **Figure 5.5** – Trend map: ek period mein baayen se daayen aur ek group mein upar se neeche chhah properties kaise badalti hain. Dhyan do: jahan size ghatta hai wahan ionisation energy, electron affinity aur electronegativity badhti hain; dono "chhote atom" ke saath chalte hain.

Chhoti si yaadgaar: **size, metallic character aur dhatu ki reactivity ek taraf chalte hain; IE, EA, EN aur non-metallic character doosri taraf.**

### Example 8. Anjaan tatva X: configuration 2, 8, 3

Is tatva ki sab kuch bataiye: group, period, dhatu ya adhaatu, valency, banne wala ion, uska size aur oxide ki prakriti.

- Electrons = 2 + 8 + 3 = 13, to Z = 13: **aluminium (Al)**.
- 3 shells, to **Period 3**. 3 valence electrons, to **Group 13** (3 + 10).
- Baayen taraf aur beech ka: **dhatu** (seedhi ke baayen).
- Valency 3 (3 electron dene hain).
- Ion: **Al³⁺** (3 electron nikal jaate hain). Naya configuration 2, 8 (neon jaisa), 10 electrons.
- Size: **Al³⁺, Al atom se chhota** (3 electron gaye, ek poori shell kam, aur 13 protons ab sirf 10 electron ko kheench rahe hain). Al ka radius lagbhag 143 pm.
- Oxide: **Al₂O₃**, amphoteric.

**Jaanch:** Al₂O₃ mein 2 × (+3) = +6 aur 3 × (−2) = −6, total 0 ✅. Al ka valency 3 aur Group 13: 13 − 10 = 3 ✅.

### Example 9. Anjaan tatva Y: clues se pehchano

**Sawaal:** Tatva Y Period 3 mein hai, wo anion Y⁻ banata hai jiske 18 electron hain, aur uska oxide bahut tez acidic hai. Y kaun hai, aur kaisa hai?

- Y⁻ mein 18 electron, to Y ke 17 electron: Z = 17, **chlorine (Cl)**, configuration 2, 8, 7.
- 7 valence electrons: **Group 17**, valency 1. **Adhaatu**, halogen.
- Ion **Cl⁻** (ek electron liya), bada hai Cl se (electron zyada, protons wahi 17).
- Oxide **Cl₂O₇**, bahut acidic, paani mein HClO₄.
- Non-metallic character Period 3 mein sabse zyada (noble gas ko chhodkar), aur reactivity bhi Period 3 ke non-metals mein sabse zyada.

**Jaanch:** Cl₂O₇ + H₂O → 2HClO₄: Cl 2, O 8, H 2 dono taraf ✅. Cl⁻ ka configuration 2, 8, 8 (argon jaisa), 18 electron ✅.

### Example 10. B, Al aur Ga: ek hi group, alag swabhav

**Sawaal:** B, Al aur Ga Group 13 mein hain, upar se neeche. Dhatu-pan ka order aur kaaran batao.

- B (2 shell), Al (3 shell), Ga (4 shell, sirf naam).
- Neeche utarte hue baahari electron door aur dheela, to **metallic character badhta hai**: B < Al < Ga.
- B metalloid hai, Al dhatu, Ga dhatu.

**Jaanch:** Group mein neeche metallic character badhta hai ✅ aur B seedhi par baitha hai ✅.

### Example 11. Br₂ aur KI ka milna

**Sawaal:** Br₂ (laal-bhoora liquid, sirf dekhne ke liye) KI ke ghol mein mile to kya hoga? Likho.

- Br₂, I₂ se upar hai (Figure 5.3), to Br₂ I₂ ko nikaal dega.
- Br₂ + 2KI → 2KBr + I₂.

**Jaanch:** baayen Br 2, K 2, I 2; daayen K 2, Br 2, I 2 ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti: "Neeche utarte hue dhatu aur adhaatu dono ki reactivity badhti hai."**

Ye galti isliye hoti hai ki "reactivity" shabd dono ke liye ek hi hai, par dono ka matlab alag hai. Dhatu ki reactivity = electron **dene** ki taiyari. Adhaatu ki reactivity = electron **lene** ki taiyari. Group mein neeche utarte hue baahari electron **door** hota hai: ye electron dene ko aasaan banata hai par lene ko mushkil.

*Demonstration 1: library se.* Maano library ki ek nayi shakha khuli jahan kitaabein ooperi manzil se bhi door, teesri manzil par rakhi hain. Jo sadasya kitaab **dena** chahta hai, uske liye ab aur aasaan: kitaab uske haath se door, pakad dheeli. Par jo sadasya kitaab **lena** chahta hai, use wo kitaab ab door se kheenchni padegi: kheench kamzor, utsukta kam. Neeche utarte hue shell door hoti jaati hai: dene wala aur tatpar, lene wala aur kamzor.

*Demonstration 2: asli reactions se.* Group 1: K, Na se zyada tezi se react karta hai (teacher demo; electron door, ionisation energy 419 < 496 lagbhag). Group 17: Cl₂ + 2KBr → 2KCl + Br₂ hota hai, par Br₂ + 2KCl → kuch nahi hota. Agar adhaatu ki reactivity neeche badhti, to Br₂ Cl₂ ko uske salt se nikaal deta. Par aisa nahi hota: **Cl₂ Br₂ se zyada reactive hai**. Is tarah ek hi direction (neeche) mein dhatu ki reactivity **badhti** hai aur adhaatu ki **ghatti**.

Jaanch: Li < Na < K (badhti) aur F > Cl > Br > I (ghatti) ✅.

## Quick Recap

- **Metallic character** = electron dene ki aasani (ionisation energy kam). **Non-metallic character** = electron lene ki aasani (EA aur EN zyada).
- Period mein baayen se daayen: metallic character **ghatta**, non-metallic **badhta**. Group mein neeche: metallic **badhta**, non-metallic **ghatta**.
- Dhatu ki reactivity: group mein neeche **badhti**, period mein daayen **ghat-ti**. Adhaatu ki reactivity: group mein neeche **ghat-ti** (F > Cl > Br > I), period mein daayen **badhti**.
- Halogens ki seedhi: upar wala halogen neeche wale ko uske salt se nikaalta hai (Cl₂ + 2KBr → 2KCl + Br₂); ulta nahi hota. Sirf padhne ke liye.
- Period 3 ke oxide: Na₂O, MgO basic; Al₂O₃ amphoteric; SiO₂ kamzor acidic; P₄O₁₀, SO₃ acidic; Cl₂O₇ bahut acidic.
- Group 1 ke hydroxide neeche utarte hue tez base: LiOH < NaOH < KOH.
- Table mein jagah dekh kar group, period, dhatu ya adhaatu, valency, ion, size aur oxide ki prakriti sab bata sakte hain.

## Practice Questions

(Sab sawaal paper par hal karne ke liye hain; alkali metals, halogens aur tez acid ya alkali wale sawaal sirf padhne ke liye hain.)

1. Arrange K, Ca and Mg in decreasing order of metallic character. Give a reason using shells and the number of electrons.
2. Atomic number 20 is an element. Find its configuration, group, period, valency, the ion it forms, whether the ion is bigger or smaller than the atom, and the nature of its oxide.
3. Chlorine water (Cl₂ dissolved in water) is added to separate solutions of KCl, KBr and KI. In which solutions will a reaction take place? Write the equation(s) and explain using the halogen ladder. (Fact given: Cl₂ is more reactive than Br₂ and I₂.)
4. Name the oxide of each element with its type: Na, Al, S, P. Which one reacts with both an acid and a base?
5. Three metals P, Q and R are Na, Mg and Al in some order. P reacts violently with cold water (teacher demo), Q reacts very slowly with cold water but faster with hot water and steam, and R does not react with cold or hot water and reacts only with steam (its oxide layer protects it). Arrange them in decreasing order of reactivity and identify each.
6. Why does the metallic character increase from B to Al to Ga? Why is B called a metalloid?
7. Compare the reactivity of metals and non-metals as we go down a group. A student says both increase. Show the error with examples.
8. Arrange LiOH, NaOH and KOH in order of increasing basic strength. Give the reason in terms of metallic character.
9. Arrange MgO, Cl₂O₇, SiO₂ and P₄O₁₀ in increasing order of acidic nature.
10. Which is the most non-metallic element among Cl, Br and I, and which is the most metallic among Mg, Ca and Sr (strontium, just below Ca in Group 2)? Give reasons using the number of shells.
11. The first ionisation energy of K is about 419 kJ per mol and of Ca about 590 kJ per mol. (a) How much energy is needed to remove one electron from each atom in 5 mol of K atoms? (b) The same for 5 mol of Ca atoms. (c) Which of the two metals is more reactive from this data, and why? (Gaseous atoms, first ionisation energy only.)
12. Why is I₂ unable to take Br⁻ out of KBr, though both are halogens? Give two reasons based on the trend.
13. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) Metallic character increases from Na to Cl. (b) Cl₂O₇ is the most acidic oxide in Period 3. (c) Al₂O₃ is a basic oxide. (d) Reactivity of F₂ is higher than that of Cl₂. (e) An element with configuration 2, 8, 1 is more metallic than one with 2, 8, 8, 1.
14. An element Z has configuration 2, 3. Find its group, period, valency, and whether it is metal, non-metal or metalloid. Predict whether its oxide is acidic or basic, and whether it is more or less metallic than Al.

### Answers

1. K: 4 shells, 1 valence electron. Ca: 4 shells, 2 valence electrons. Mg: 3 shells, 2 valence electrons. K aur Ca ke shells barabar, par Ca mein 20 protons aur K mein 19, to Ca ka kheench zyada, K sabse aasani se electron deta hai. Mg ke 3 hi shells, to electron nucleus ke paas aur kheench zyada. **K > Ca > Mg**. Jaanch: ionisation energy lagbhag 419 < 590 < 738 ✅.
2. Z = 20: configuration **2, 8, 8, 2**. 4 shells, to **Period 4**; 2 valence electrons, **Group 2** (2 valence electron). Valency 2. Ion **Ca²⁺** (2, 8, 8, argon jaisa, 18 electron). Ion atom se **chhota** (4th shell gayi, 20 protons 18 electron ko kheench rahe hain). Oxide **CaO**, basic (Ca dhatu hai). Jaanch: CaO mein +2 aur −2 ✅. Ye element Ca hai.
3. Cl₂ Br₂ aur I₂ dono se zyada reactive hai. **KBr aur KI** mein reaction hoga, KCl mein nahi (Cl⁻ ko Cl₂ nikaal hi nahi sakta, wo pehle se Cl hai). Cl₂ + 2KBr → 2KCl + Br₂ (K 2, Cl 2, Br 2 dono taraf ✅). Cl₂ + 2KI → 2KCl + I₂ (K 2, Cl 2, I 2 ✅). **KCl:** koi nayi cheez nahi bani.
4. Na: **Na₂O**, basic. Al: **Al₂O₃**, amphoteric. S: **SO₃**, acidic. P: **P₄O₁₀**, acidic. **Al₂O₃** acid aur base dono se reaction karta hai (Al₂O₃ + 6HCl → 2AlCl₃ + 3H₂O aur Al₂O₃ + 2NaOH → 2NaAlO₂ + H₂O). Jaanch: Al 2, O 3, H 6, Cl 6 dono taraf ✅.
5. Reactivity: P > Q > R. Period 3 mein baayen se daayen reactivity ghatti hai, to **P = Na, Q = Mg, R = Al**. Jaanch: ionisation energy lagbhag Na 496 < Mg 738, aur Al ke 3 electron ko nikalna aur mushkil ✅. (Na ke 1 electron aasani se, Mg ke 2 thoda mushkil se, Al ke 3 aur mushkil se, aur oxide ki parat bhi roke rakhti hai.)
6. Group 13 mein B ke 2 shell, Al ke 3, Ga ke 4. Neeche utarte hue baahari electron door hota gaya aur parde ke peeche, to metallic character badha: **B < Al < Ga**. B ka electron dene ka aur lene ka vyavhaar lagbhag barabar hai, to wo seedhi par baitha **metalloid** hai.
7. Dhatu ki reactivity group mein neeche **badhti** hai (Li < Na < K) kyunki electron dene mein aasani badhti hai. Adhaatu ki reactivity group mein neeche **ghatti** hai (F > Cl > Br > I) kyunki electron lene ki taiyari ghatti hai. Student ki baat galat: Cl₂ + 2KBr → 2KCl + Br₂ ho jaata hai par Br₂ + 2KCl → kuch nahi, to Cl₂ Br₂ se zyada reactive hai, yaani neeche Br₂ kam reactive.
8. **LiOH < NaOH < KOH**. Group 1 mein neeche utarte hue metallic character badhta hai: dhatu electron dene mein tatpar, to hydroxide mein wo OH⁻ ko chhodne mein aasani dikhata hai, aur hydroxide zyada tez base banta hai. (Ye sab tez kshaar hain: sirf padhne ke liye.)
9. MgO (basic), SiO₂ (kamzor acidic), P₄O₁₀ (acidic), Cl₂O₇ (bahut zyada acidic). **Increasing acidic: MgO < SiO₂ < P₄O₁₀ < Cl₂O₇**. Jaanch: Period 3 mein baayen se daayen Mg, Si, P, Cl ka order wahi hai ✅.
10. Non-metallic: **Cl** (3 shells; Br ke 4, I ke 5). Cl mein aane wala electron nucleus ke sabse paas baithta hai, to electron lene ki kheench sabse zyada. Metallic: **Sr** (5 shells; Mg ke 3, Ca ke 4). Sr ka baahari electron sabse door aur sabse zyada shells ke parde ke peeche, to sabse aasani se nikalta hai. Jaanch: group mein neeche non-metallic character ghatta aur metallic character badhta hai ✅.
11. (a) K ki 1 mol ke liye 419 kJ, to 5 mol ke liye 5 × 419 = **2095 kJ**. (b) Ca ke liye 5 × 590 = **2950 kJ**. (c) K ko kam energy chahiye (2095 < 2950), to **K zyada reactive**: wo electron aasani se deta hai, jo shells aur kheench ke hisaab se bhi theek hai (K ka ek hi valence electron aur kam proton). Difference 2950 − 2095 = 855 kJ. Jaanch ✅.
12. Pehla: I₂ Br₂ se neeche hai, to uske electron lene ki taiyari kam. Doosra: I (5 shells wala) ka baahari shell Br (4 shells) se door hai, to naya electron kheenchna mushkil. Isliye **I₂ Br⁻ ko nahi nikaal sakta**; ulta Br₂ I⁻ ko nikaal sakta hai.
13. (a) **False** ❌: Na se Cl tak metallic character **ghatta** hai. (b) **True** ✅. (c) **False** ❌: Al₂O₃ **amphoteric** hai. (d) **True** ✅. (e) **False** ❌: 2, 8, 1 wala Na hai (3 shells) aur 2, 8, 8, 1 wala K hai (4 shells); **K zyada metallic**.
14. Configuration 2, 3: electron 5, to Z = 5, **boron (B)**. 2 shells: **Period 2**. 3 valence electrons: **Group 13**. Valency 3. **Metalloid**. Oxide B₂O₃, kamzor acidic (non-metal ki taraf). Al se **kam metallic** (group mein upar). Jaanch: B ki valency 3 aur O ki 2: B₂O₃ mein 2 × 3 = 6 = 3 × 2 ✅.
