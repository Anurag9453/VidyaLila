## Part A: Chaar kadam ka tareeka

Class 9 mein bonfire ka picture tha ([periodic-table-part4.md](../../../class9/chemistry/periodic-table/periodic-table-part4.md)); is chapter ne use do shabdon mein pakka kar diya: **shells (doori)** aur **nuclear pull (nucleus ki kheench, shielding ke baad)**. Ab kisi bhi sawaal mein ye chaar kadam chalaao:

1. **Shells ginno.** Dono tatva kis period mein hain? Zyada shells matlab baahari electron door.
2. **Nuclear pull dekho.** Same period mein protons zyada hain to pull zyada. Neeche jaane par inner shells shielding dete hain, isliye pull utni nahi badhti jitne protons.
3. **Trend likho.** Size, IE, EA, EN ya character: ek shabd mein "badhta hai" ya "ghat-ta hai".
4. **Reason likho.** Ek line mein: "shells ... hain, isliye ... ; protons ... hain, isliye pull ... ."

```
   Sawaal  -->  Step 1: shells (same row ya alag row?)
                   :
                   v
                Step 2: pull (protons aur shielding)
                   :
                   v
                Step 3: trend (badha ya ghata)
                   :
                   v
                Step 4: ek line ka reason
```

> **Figure 6.2** – Chaar kadam. Step 1 aur 2 "kyun" ki tayyari hain, Step 3 aur 4 jawab ka form. Reason ke bina jawab adhura maana jaata hai.

### Example 1. Period 2 mein size: B, N, F, Be

**Sawaal:** B, N, F, Be ko atomic size ke ghat-te order mein lagao aur kaaran batao.

- Step 1: chaaron Period 2 mein hain: sab ke 2 shells.
- Step 2: protons Be 4, B 5, N 7, F 9. Shells wahi, to jis mein protons zyada, pull zyada.
- Step 3: pull badhne par size ghat-ta hai.
- Step 4: **Be > B > N > F**. Reason: shells ek jaisi (2), par protons 4 se 9 tak badhte hain, isliye baahari electrons zyada khinchte hain.

Lagbhag values (Topic 1 wala set): Be 111, B 88, N 74, F 64 pm.
**Jaanch:** 111 > 88 > 74 > 64 ✅.

### Example 2. Group 17 mein: F, Cl, Br, I

**Sawaal:** Electronegativity ke ghat-te order mein lagao, aur ek aur property batao jo isi order mein chalti hai.

- Step 1: F Period 2, Cl Period 3, Br Period 4, I Period 5. Neeche jaate hue shells 2, 3, 4, 5.
- Step 2: protons badhte hain (9, 17, 35, 53), par inner shells ki shielding bhi badhti hai; asar yeh ki baahari electron door aur kam khincha hua.
- Step 3 aur 4: **F > Cl > Br > I**. Reason: zyada shells matlab baahari shell door, aur bond ke electrons ko kheenchne ki taqat kam.

Lagbhag electronegativity: F 4.0, Cl 3.0, Br 2.8, I 2.5. Ek aur property jo isi order mein ghat-ti hai: first ionisation energy, lagbhag F 1681, Cl 1251, Br 1140, I 1008 kJ per mol.
**Jaanch:** 4.0 > 3.0 > 2.8 > 2.5 ✅ aur 1681 > 1251 > 1140 > 1008 ✅.

### Example 3. Isoelectronic ions: N³⁻, F⁻, Al³⁺

**Sawaal:** In teeno ions mein 10 electrons hain. Size ka order batao.

- Step 1: teeno ke electrons ek hi 2-shell wale hain (2, 8).
- Step 2: protons N 7, F 9, Al 13. Electrons ki ginti same, par nucleus mein protons alag.
- Step 3: jis mein protons zyada, wo electrons ko zyada kheencha hai, isliye chhota.
- Step 4: **N³⁻ > F⁻ > Al³⁺**. Reason: electrons 10 hi hain, par 7, 9 aur 13 protons unhein alag-alag zor se khinchte hain.

Lagbhag radii: N³⁻ 146, F⁻ 133, Al³⁺ 54 pm.
**Jaanch:** 146 > 133 > 54 ✅ aur protons 7 < 9 < 13 ✅.

## Part B: Nau puzzles, ek ke baad ek

Har puzzle ke tathya sawaal mein hi diye gaye hain. Dhyan se padho kya diya hai, kya poocha hai.

### Puzzle 1. Clues se anjaan tatva

**Sawaal:** Tatva X Period 3 mein hai. Uske valence electrons ek kam hain poori shell se. Tatva Y bhi Period 3 mein hai aur uski radius apne period mein sabse badi hai (noble gas ko chhod kar). X aur Y se bana yaugik kaisa hoga?

- Period 3 mein baahari shell mein 8 electron chahiye; ek kam matlab 7 valence electrons: **X = Cl**.
- Period 3 mein left par size sabse bada: **Y = Na**. (Class 9 ka Na aur Cl yaad hai; yahan naya ye hai ki hum bond ki baat karte hain.)
- Electronegativity lagbhag: Na 0.9, Cl 3.0. Difference = 3.0 − 0.9 = **2.1**, bahut bada.
- Bada difference matlab Cl electron kheench leta hai: **ionic**, NaCl.

**Jaanch:** Na ⟶ Na⁺ + e⁻ aur Cl + e⁻ ⟶ Cl⁻; dono ion ek jaisa noble gas configuration lete hain (Ne aur Ar jaisa) ✅.

### Puzzle 2. Successive ionisation energy: do anjaan tatva

**Sawaal:** X aur Y dono Period 2 ke tatva hain. Unki successive ionisation energies (kJ per mol, lagbhag) yeh hain. Group, valency aur naam batao.

| Energy | X | Y |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | 900 | 801 |
| 2nd | 1757 | 2427 |
| 3rd | 14849 | 3660 |
| 4th | 21007 | 25026 |

- X: pehli do energy chhoti (900, 1757), teesri achanak bahut badi (14849). Do electron aasani se niklenge, teesra ek **bhare hue inner shell** se. Valence electrons 2: **Group 2, valency 2**. Period 2 ka Group 2 tatva: **Be**.
- Y: teen energy chhoti-chhoti (801, 2427, 3660), chauthi 25026 par kood. Valence electrons 3: **Group 13, valency 3**: **B**.
- Dono ke ions: Be²⁺, B³⁺ (B asal mein aksar bond share karke yaugik banata hai; yahan sirf ginti dekhi).

Ek numerical: 1 mol gaseous Be atoms ko Be²⁺ banane ke liye energy = 900 + 1757 = **2657 kJ**. To 2 mol ke liye 2 × 2657 = **5314 kJ**. B ke 0.5 mol ko B³⁺ banane ke liye: (801 + 2427 + 3660) × 0.5 = 6888 × 0.5 = **3444 kJ**.

**Jaanch:** X ki teesri ÷ doosri = 14849 ÷ 1757 lagbhag 8.5 guna, aur Y ki chauthi ÷ teesri = 25026 ÷ 3660 lagbhag 6.8 guna: dono jagah bada kood ✅.

### Puzzle 3. Mystery element: family ka description

**Sawaal:** Teen anjaan tatva:
(a) Ek gas, 2 shells, baahari shell poori bhari hui, lagbhag koi reaction nahi.
(b) Ek gehra baingani-kaala thos non-metal, 5 shells, Group 17 mein.
(c) Ek chamakdar-sa, bhurbhura thos, Period 3, 4 valence electrons, thodi bijli conduct karta hai, aur uska oxide retili (sand) cheez hai.

- (a) Period 2, poori shell (2, 8): **Ne**.
- (b) Group 17, 5 shells, thos: **I** (iodine). (Iodine ki vapour saans ke liye nuksaandeh hai: sirf padhne ke liye.)
- (c) Period 3, valence 4 = Group 14, metalloid: **Si**, oxide SiO₂ (sand).

**Jaanch:** Ne ke electrons 2 + 8 = 10 ✅ (Z = 10). I ka period 5 ✅. Si Z = 14 aur 2, 8, 4 ✅.

### Puzzle 5. Oxide ki clues se tatva ki jagah

**Sawaal:** Chaar Period 3 tatvon ke oxide: A ka oxide sirf acid se react karta hai; B ka acid se bhi aur alkali se bhi; C ka oxide sand ki tarah hai aur sirf alkali se (garam karne par) react karta hai; D ka oxide paani mein ghul kar bahut tez acid banata hai. Choose from Mg, Al, Si, Cl, aur inhein table mein left se right order mein likho.

- A sirf acid se: basic oxide = dhatu: **Mg**.
- B dono se: amphoteric: **Al**.
- C sand, weakly acidic: **Si**.
- D strongly acidic: **Cl**.
- Order left se right: **Mg, Al, Si, Cl** (Z = 12, 13, 14, 17).

**Jaanch:** basic ⟶ amphoteric ⟶ weakly acidic ⟶ strongly acidic, matlab dhatu wala swabhav kam hota gaya aur non-metal wala badhta gaya ✅ (Topic 5 ka ye picture).

### Puzzle 6. Bond ka swabhav: EN ka difference

Thumb rule (sirf shuruaat ke liye, lagbhag): difference **0** matlab dono atom barabar kheenchte hain; difference **lagbhag 2 ya usse zyada** matlab ek atom electron poora kheench leta hai (ionic); beech ka difference matlab bond covalent hai par electrons **barabar nahi** bante (iska poora naksha Chapter 7 mein).

| Jodi | EN values (lagbhag) | Difference | Swabhav | Negative kinara |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Br–Br (Br₂) | 2.8, 2.8 | 0 | barabar sharing | koi nahi |
| P–Cl (PCl₃) | 2.1, 3.0 | 0.9 | unequal sharing | Cl |
| S–H (H₂S) | 2.5, 2.1 | 0.4 | thodi unequal | S |
| Si–O (SiO₂) | 1.8, 3.5 | 1.7 | unequal sharing | O |
| K–Br (KBr) | 0.8, 2.8 | 2.0 | ionic | Br |
| Na–F (NaF) | 0.9, 4.0 | 3.1 | ionic | F |

Jahan sharing unequal hai, atom jis ki EN badi hai (Cl, S, O) electrons ko apni taraf zyada kheenchta hai, to wahan thoda negative hissa banta hai. Si–O (1.7) dekho: farq bada hai par phir bhi sharing hai, isliye seema dhundhli hai, sakht deewar nahi. (H₂S zehreeli gas hai: sirf padhne ke liye.)

**Jaanch:** sabse bada difference NaF (3.1) aur KBr (2.0): dono dhatu + non-metal ✅ ionic; sabse chhota Br₂ (0) dono same atom ✅.

### Puzzle 7. Group ke neeche: ek hi tatva-family ki do ginti

**Sawaal:** Group 17 mein neeche jaate hue Cl, Br, I ki lagbhag radius 99, 114, 133 pm aur EN 3.0, 2.8, 2.5 hain. Bataiye (a) kaun sabse badi atom, (b) kaun electron sabse mushkil se chhodega, (c) ek student kehta hai "Br ki radius Cl se bhi bada hai aur IE bhi bada hai". Kya sahi?

- (a) **I**, 5 shells, sabse door baahari electron.
- (b) Yahan "mushkil se chhodna" ka matlab ionisation energy bada. Group mein neeche IE ghat-ta hai (shells badhne se): Cl 1251 > Br 1140 > I 1008. Sabse mushkil **Cl**.
- (c) Radius wali baat sahi (114 > 99), par IE wali baat **galat**: Br ka IE (1140) Cl (1251) se chhota hai. Do trend ulte chalte hain.

**Jaanch:** radius badhi (99 → 114 → 133) aur IE ghati (1251 → 1140 → 1008) ✅.

### Puzzle 8. Ek hi electron count, alag size

**Sawaal:** P³⁻, S²⁻, Cl⁻ mein 18 electrons hain. Size order aur reason.

- Step 1: sab ke 3 shells (2, 8, 8).
- Step 2: protons P 15, S 16, Cl 17.
- Step 3 aur 4: **P³⁻ > S²⁻ > Cl⁻**. Zyada protons wala ion electrons ko zyada khinchta hai, isliye chhota.

Lagbhag radii: P³⁻ 212, S²⁻ 184, Cl⁻ 181 pm.
**Jaanch:** 212 > 184 > 181 ✅ aur protons 15 < 16 < 17 ✅. (Ar ko is list mein jaan-boojh kar nahi rakha: Aam galti 1 dekhein.)

### Puzzle 9. Ek jagah se paanch jawab: chalk table par

**Sawaal:** Tanuja chalk table mein Period 3, Group 15 ke dabbe mein khadi hai. (i) Wo kaun sa tatva hai? (ii) Uska valency? (iii) Uske left wale dabbe ka tatva usse bada ya chhota? (iv) Uske neeche wale (Period 4) tatva ka EN zyada ya kam hoga? (v) Wo metal hai ya non-metal?

- (i) Period 3 Group 15: **P** (2, 8, 5).
- (ii) Valence 5, valency = 8 − 5 = **3**.
- (iii) Left mein Si: protons kam, pull kam, to size **bada** (Si 118 > P 110 pm).
- (iv) Neeche Period 4 mein shells zyada: EN **kam**.
- (v) Table ke right-upar ka hissa: **non-metal**.

**Jaanch:** Group 15 matlab 5 valence electrons ✅ aur P ke 15 protons ✅.

## Part C: Chalk-chart activity (maidan mein, aur ghar mein bhi)

Ye activity school ke maidan mein ya ghar ke aangan mein ki ja sakti hai. Koi hazardous cheez nahi, sirf chalk aur cards.

**Chahiye:** chalk (zameen par chalne wali), rassi ya measuring tape, 18 card jin par tatva ke symbol aur proton count hon, aur ek bada (adult ya teacher) paas mein.

**Safety:** chalk ki dhool saans mein na jaaye (phoonk kar na udaao), kaam ke baad haath dho lo. Zameen geeli ya phisalan wali ho to activity na karo. Daudna nahi, sirf chalna.

**Karna kya hai:**
1. Figure 6.1 jaisa 3 row × 8 column ka table banao (har dabba 1 metre).
2. Har bacha ek card leta hai aur apne dabbe mein khada hota hai.
3. **Period walk:** Na se Cl tak dheere chalo. Har dabba par ruk kar bolo: "protons ek zyada, shells wahi." Bacha jo Cl par khada hai apne haath ko Na wale bacche se thoda kam failaata hai (radius kam): chhota size dikhane ke liye.
4. **Group walk:** Li se Na tak jao (Group 1 mein neeche). Har kadam par ek aur "shell ring" (rassi ka gol) badhao: baahari electron aur door ho gaya, to size bada.
5. **Pull game:** do bacche, ek Na wala aur ek Cl wala, ek rassi ko dono taraf se halke se kheenchte hain (adult paas mein, kheench sirf thodi). Cl wala zyada "pull-token" (cards) pakadta hai: use rassi apni taraf laana aasaan lagega. Yeh EN ka khel hai: jis ke paas zyada pull, wo shared rassi apni taraf zyada laata hai.

```
   Na  Mg  Al  Si  P   S   Cl
   --> protons badhte hain, shells ek jaisi (3)
   --> size ghat-ta hai, EN badhti hai
   --> baayen se daayen: dhatu se non-metal
```

> **Figure 6.4** – Ek period ke par chalne ka summary: protons badhte hain, shells ek jaisi hain. Isi liye size ghat-ta hai, EN badhti hai, aur character dhatu se non-metal ki taraf jaata hai.

Chacha ne jab bacchon ko baar-baar "Na se Cl" chalte dekha, to bola: "Ab ek aur dimaagi kaam: kisi ek tatva ke dabbe par khade ho kar bolo ki apne padosi se tum kaise alag ho: left, right, upar, neeche. Chaar disha, chaar jawab." Tanuja ne N ka dabba chuna: left C (bada, kam EN), right O (chhota, zyada EN), neeche P (bada, kam EN), upar koi nahi (Period 1 mein is Group mein koi nahi). Chacha ne taali bajai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Noble gas ke paas size ka trend toot jaata hai, kyunki Ne F se bada dikhta hai."**
Table mein F ka bonded radius lagbhag 64 pm hai aur Ne ka koi bond banta hi nahi. Ne ki size ke liye alag tareeka use hota hai: do Ne atoms bina bond ke ek-doosre ke kitne paas aate hain, aur isse Ne ki radius lagbhag 154 pm aati hai. Agar 64 aur 154 ko seedhe tulna karein to Ne F se lagbhag dhai guna bada lagega: ye bhram hai. F ko usi tareeke se naapo to lagbhag 147 pm aata hai, yaani dono paas-paas; baaki barik baatein aage ki class mein. **Sabak:** size ke trend mein hamesha ek hi tarah ke naap wale values compare karo; noble gas ko isi liye aksar alag rakha jaata hai. Isi wajah se Puzzle 8 mein Ar ko ions ki list mein nahi rakha.

**Galti 2: "Ek group mein neeche jaane par har property period wali property ka ulta chalti hai."**
Zyaadatar ulta hai (size, IE, EN, character), par har ek nahi. Dekho:

| Property | Period mein left se right | Group mein upar se neeche |
|---|---|---|
| Valence electrons | 1 se 8 tak badhte hain | wahi rehte hain |
| Shells | wahi rehti hain | badhti hain |
| Size | ghat-ta hai | badhta hai |
| IE | badhti hai | ghat-ti hai |

Valence electrons group mein neeche **wahi** rehte hain (Li, Na, K sab mein 1), isliye "ulta" ka matlab "ghat-na" nahi ho sakta. Shells period mein **wahi** hain, isliye "ulta" ka matlab "ghat-na" yahan bhi galat. **Sabak:** har property ka apna trend padho, ulta-seedha ratne se kaam nahi chalega.

**Galti 3: "Valence electrons aur total electrons ek hi cheez hain."**
Cl mein total electrons 17 hain, par valence sirf 7 (2, 8, 7). Agar koi kahe "Cl ka valency 17", to galat. Sahi: Cl 1 electron leta hai (valency 8 − 7 = 1) aur Cl⁻ banata hai. Isi tarah Mg mein total 12 hain par wo sirf 2 chhodta hai: Mg²⁺. Puzzle 2 ke kood (jump) isi baat ke saboot hain: Be mein do electron ke baad ka teesra electron andar ki bhari shell se aata hai, isliye uski keemat achanak badh jaati hai. **Sabak:** group number baahari shell se tay hota hai, total electrons se nahi.

**Galti 4: "Staircase ke saare tatva ya to sab metal hain ya sab non-metal."**
Staircase par ek alag family hai: metalloid. B aur Si (aur aage Ge, As, Sb, Te) dikhne mein chamakdar par bhurbhure hain, aur bijli thodi conduct karte hain: na pakke metal, na pakke non-metal. Al staircase ke bilkul paas hai, par wo metal hai: chamakdar, bijli ka achha conductor, taar mein khichne wala. Si ko hammer se maaro to wo toot jaata hai, jabki Al patla-sa chadar ban jaata hai. **Sabak:** staircase ke paas hona aur staircase par hona alag baatein hain.

## Quick Recap

- 4 kadam: shells ginno, nuclear pull dekho, trend likho, ek line ka reason likho.
- Period mein left se right: size ghat-ta hai, IE, EA, EN aur non-metallic character badhte hain.
- Group mein neeche: size badhta hai, IE, EA, EN aur non-metallic character ghat-te hain, metallic character badhta hai.
- Isoelectronic ions mein jis ke protons zyada, wo chhota (N³⁻ > F⁻ > Al³⁺).
- Successive ionisation energies mein bada kood batata hai ki valence electrons kitne hain (Be: 2, B: 3).
- EN difference se bond: 0 barabar, lagbhag 2 ya zyada ionic, beech wala unequal sharing (Chapter 7).
- Valence electrons aur total electrons alag; staircase par metalloid; noble gas ki size alag tareeke se naapi jaati hai.

## Practice Questions

(Saare sawaal paper par hal karne ke liye hain; koi bhi reaction ghar ya school mein na karein.)

2. Arrange C, N and O in increasing order of electronegativity and in increasing order of atomic size. Give one reason that explains both orders.

6. For each of the following bonds, calculate the electronegativity difference using H 2.1, I 2.5, Na 0.9, O 3.5, N 3.0, C 2.5, Cl 3.0, and say whether the bond is ionic, equally shared or unequally shared. Name the atom that pulls harder in each unequal case. (a) N–N in N₂ (b) H–I (c) Na and O in Na₂O (d) C–Cl in CCl₄
7. State True or False and give a reason: (a) Si has 14 valence electrons. (b) Every element next to the staircase is a metalloid. (c) The electronegativity of S is greater than that of P. (d) Valence electrons increase as we go down Group 17.
8. Element W is a yellow solid non-metal in Period 3 with 6 valence electrons. Name it. Compare it with its Group 16 neighbour in Period 2 (O) for atomic size and electronegativity (use S: 103 pm, 2.5 and O: 66 pm, 3.5).
9. A student stands in the chalk table at the box for Period 2, Group 16. Name the element, then state which neighbouring element (left, right or below) is (a) larger in size and has lower electronegativity than the student's element, and (b) smaller and has higher electronegativity. Use the first 18 elements.
10. How much energy (first ionisation) is needed to remove one electron from each atom in 0.5 mol of gaseous Br atoms, and from 3 mol of gaseous Cl atoms? Use Br 1140 and Cl 1251 kJ per mol. Which of the two elements loses its outer electron more easily?
11. Na⁺ and Al³⁺ both have 10 electrons. Which ion is smaller, and why? (Facts given: Na has 11 protons and Al 13; lagbhag radii 102 and 54 pm.)
12. Three oxides: oxide A reacts only with acids; oxide B reacts with acids and with alkalis; oxide C reacts only with alkalis and gives an acid with water. They belong to Na, Al and Cl. Match each oxide to its element and arrange the three elements in the order of decreasing metallic character.
13. Four Period 2 elements have 1, 3, 5 and 6 valence electrons. Name them, arrange them in increasing order of electronegativity, and say which forms a positive ion.
14. A student says: "Aluminium has 13 electrons, so its valency is 13." Correct the student, and give the actual valency with the working.

### Answers

1. Step 1: sab Period 3. Step 2: protons Al 13, Si 14, P 15, S 16. Step 3 aur 4: protons badhne se pull badhti hai, isliye size ghat-ta hai: Al > Si > P > S. **Increasing order: S < P < Si < Al.** Lagbhag radii 103 < 110 < 118 < 143 pm. Jaanch: 103 < 110 < 118 < 143 ✅.
2. EN: C 2.5 < N 3.0 < O 3.5, **increasing order C < N < O**. Size: protons C 6, N 7, O 8, shells same (2): size ghat-ta hai, to **increasing size O < N < C** (lagbhag 66 < 74 < 77 pm). Ek hi reason: shells wahi hain par protons badhte hain, to pull badhti hai: size chhota aur electrons ko kheenchne ki taqat zyada. Jaanch: 66 < 74 < 77 ✅ aur 2.5 < 3.0 < 3.5 ✅.
3. Period 3, valence 2 (2, 8, 2): **Magnesium (Mg)**. Valency **2** (2 electron deta hai, Mg²⁺). Table ke left par hai, to **metal**. Oxide MgO **basic**. Jaanch: Z = 2 + 8 + 2 = 12 ✅.
4. (a) 1st se 4th tak ek ke baad ek barhti, 5th (16091) mein bada kood: **4 valence electrons**. (b) 4 valence electrons: **Group 14** (ye Si hai, 786 lagbhag). (c) 1 mol ke liye 786 + 1577 + 3232 + 4356 = 9951 kJ. 2 mol ke liye 2 × 9951 = **19902 kJ** (lagbhag). Jaanch: 16091 ÷ 4356 lagbhag 3.7 guna, bada kood 4th ke baad ✅.
5. Radius badhti hai (74 → 110): N ke 2 shells, P ke 3. IE ghat-ti hai (1402 → 1012): P ka baahari electron door aur zyada inner shells se shielded, to nikalna aasaan. EN ghat-ti hai (3.0 → 2.1): P ki baahari shell door hai, to bond ke electrons ko kam kheenchta hai. Jaanch: 110 > 74, 1012 < 1402, 2.1 < 3.0 ✅.
6. (a) N–N: 3.0 − 3.0 = 0: **equally shared**, koi atom zyada nahi kheenchta. (b) H–I: 2.5 − 2.1 = 0.4: **unequally shared**, I zyada pulls. (c) Na₂O: 3.5 − 0.9 = 2.6: **ionic** (O electron leta hai). (d) C–Cl: 3.0 − 2.5 = 0.5: **unequally shared**, Cl zyada pulls. Jaanch: sab differences sahi ✅.
7. (a) **False** ❌: Si 2, 8, 4 mein valence sirf 4 hain, 14 total electrons hain. (b) **False** ❌: B aur Si metalloid hain, par Al staircase ke bilkul paas hone par bhi metal hai. (c) **True** ✅: S 2.5, P 2.1, left se right EN badhti hai. (d) **False** ❌: Group 17 mein sab ke valence electrons 7 hi rehte hain.
8. Period 3, valence 6: **Sulphur (S)**. Size: S 103 pm > O 66 pm: S ke 3 shells, O ke 2: **S bada**. EN: O 3.5 > S 2.5: O zyada kheenchta hai. Reason: Group mein neeche shells badhti hain, isliye size badhta aur EN ghat-ti hai. Jaanch: 103 > 66 ✅ aur 3.5 > 2.5 ✅.
9. Period 2, Group 16: **O**. Left mein N: protons kam, to **bada** aur EN kam (3.0 < 3.5). Right mein F: protons zyada, to **chhota** aur EN zyada (4.0 > 3.5). Neeche S (Period 3): zyada shells, to **bada** aur EN kam (2.5). To (a) larger and lower EN: N aur S dono; (b) smaller and higher EN: F. Jaanch: size N 74 > O 66 > F 64 pm ✅.
10. Br: 0.5 × 1140 = **570 kJ**. Cl: 3 × 1251 = **3753 kJ**. Per mole Br ka IE chhota (1140 < 1251), isliye **Br** apna baahari electron zyada aasani se chhodta hai (zyada shells, door baahari electron). Jaanch: 570 ÷ 0.5 = 1140 ✅ aur 3753 ÷ 3 = 1251 ✅.
11. Dono ke 2 shells aur 10 electrons (2, 8) hain, par Al³⁺ mein 13 protons hain aur Na⁺ mein 11. Zyada protons wala ion apne 10 electrons ko zyada kheenchta hai, to **Al³⁺ chhota**. Lagbhag 54 pm aur 102 pm. Jaanch: 54 < 102 ✅.
12. A sirf acid se: basic: **Na** (Na₂O). B dono se: amphoteric: **Al** (Al₂O₃). C sirf alkali se aur acid banata hai: **Cl** (Cl₂O₇). Metallic character decreasing: **Na > Al > Cl** (Z = 11, 13, 17). Jaanch: basic ⟶ amphoteric ⟶ acidic ✅.
13. 1 valence e⁻: **Li**; 3: **B**; 5: **N**; 6: **O** (sab Period 2). EN: Li 1.0 < B 2.0 < N 3.0 < O 3.5, **increasing order Li < B < N < O**. Positive ion sirf **Li** banata hai (Li⁺: ek electron chhodna aasan; B metalloid hai aur aksar share karta hai). Jaanch: 1.0 < 2.0 < 3.0 < 3.5 ✅.
14. Student galat hai: 13 total electrons hain, valence sirf **3** (2, 8, 3). Valency = **3** (Al 3 electron deta hai aur Al³⁺ banata hai). Jaanch: 2 + 8 + 3 = 13 ✅.
