# Topic 1: Atom Kitna Bada? Atomic aur Ionic Size

Class 9 mein humne table ko ek "bonfire circle" ki tarah padha tha: nucleus aag hai, shells rows hain, aur baahari electron aakhri row mein baitha hai ([periodic-table-part4.md](../../../class9/chemistry/periodic-table/periodic-table-part4.md)). Wahan sirf itna dekha tha ki ek period mein atom chhota hota hai aur ek group mein neeche bada. Is chapter mein hum us picture ko **gehra** karte hain: kaun si do cheezein size tay karti hain, aur kitna? Hum asli (lagbhag) sankhyaein bhi dekhenge, aur ye bhi dekhenge ki jab atom electron deta ya leta hai (ion banta hai), to size ke saath kya hota hai.

---

## Fridge ka magnet aur kaapi ke panne

Mr. Kshirsagar ki bhanji Shalaka chhutti mein unke ghar aayi thi. Fridge ke darwaaze par ek chhota sa gol magnet laga tha. Shalaka ne uske neeche ek kaapi ka panna daba diya aur magnet ne use pakad liya. Phir doosra, phir teesra. Teen panne tak magnet ki pakad theek thi.

"Mama, ab main aur panne daalti hoon," Shalaka ne kaha. Mr. Kshirsagar ne haath neeche rakh liya: "Dhyan se, magnet gir sakta hai. Aur ye sirf bade ke saamne karna." Shalaka ne 10 panne rakhe, phir 20. Ek samay aaya ki magnet **dheela** pad gaya aur khisak kar haath mein aa gira, jabki fridge ka darwaaza wahi tha aur magnet bhi wahi.

"Magnet kamzor to nahi hua, Mama. Phir chhoot kyun gaya?" Mr. Kshirsagar ne samjhaya: "Magnet aur fridge ke beech jitni zyada **parten** (layers) aati gayin, magnet ki khinchne ki taaqat fridge tak utni kam pahunchi. Taaqat wahi thi, par beech mein rukawat aur doori badh gayi."

Shalaka ne chunauti li, "To Mama, ye atom mein bhi hota hoga?" Mr. Kshirsagar muskuraye. "Bilkul. Nucleus ek magnet jaisa hai jo baahari electron ko kheenchta hai. Beech ki bhari shells wo panne hain. Chalo, dekhte hain ki is picture se atom ka size kaise tay hota hai."

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Atomic radius** | Atom ke nucleus se baahari shell tak ki lagbhag doori, picometre (pm) mein | Na ka lagbhag 186 pm |
| **Picometre (pm)** | Bahut chhoti lambai: 1 pm = 10⁻¹² m | 100 pm = 0.1 nm |
| **Nuclear pull** | Nucleus ke protons ka baahari electron ko apni taraf kheenchna | Zyada protons, zyada pull |
| **Shielding (parda)** | Beech ki bhari shells ke electrons ka baahari electron tak pahunchne wali pull ko kam kar dena | Panne jo magnet aur fridge ke beech hain |
| **Ionic radius** | Ion ka size (nucleus se uski baahari shell tak ki lagbhag doori) | Na⁺ ka lagbhag 102 pm |
| **Cation** | Electron khokar bana positive ion | Na⁺, Mg²⁺ |
| **Anion** | Electron lekar bana negative ion | Cl⁻, O²⁻ |
| **Isoelectronic** | Aise atoms ya ions jinke electrons ki ginti barabar ho | O²⁻, F⁻, Na⁺, Mg²⁺: sab ke 10 electron |

## Part A: Size tay karne wale do vichaar

Is chapter ke har trend ko hum sirf **do vichaaron** se samjhenge. Magnet-aur-panne wali kahani inhi ko dikhati hai.

**Vichaar 1: Shells ki ginti (doori).** Jitni zyada shells, baahari electron nucleus se utna door. Door wali cheez par pull kam. (Magnet se fridge jitni door, pakad utni dheeli.)

**Vichaar 2: Nuclear pull aur shielding.** Baahari electron ko nucleus ke protons kheenchte hain. Par beech ki bhari shells ke electrons us pull ka bahut hissa **rok** lete hain, bilkul un panno ki tarah. Isliye baahari electron ko "poori" pull nahi milti, bas bachi hui pull milti hai.

Ek **mota hisaab** is bachi hui pull ka: **baahari electron ko lagbhag (protons) − (andar ki shells ke electrons) jitni pull mehsoos hoti hai.** Ise hum "bachi hui pull" kahenge. (Asli numbers nikaalne ke tareeke aage ki class mein aate hain; yahan ye sirf ek aasan ginti hai jo trend samajhne ke liye kaafi hai.)

| Atom | Protons | Andar ke electron | Bachi hui pull (mota hisaab) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Li | 3 | 2 | 3 − 2 = 1 |
| Na | 11 | 10 | 11 − 10 = 1 |
| K | 19 | 18 | 19 − 18 = 1 |
| Cl | 17 | 10 | 17 − 10 = 7 |

Dhyan do: Li, Na, K tino mein "bachi hui pull" lagbhag **barabar (1)** hai. To group mein neeche jaate hue size kyun badhta hai? Kyunki ek aur shell judti hai, aur baahari electron **door** chala jaata hai. Doosri taraf Na se Cl tak "andar ke electron" wahi 10 hain, par protons 11 se 17 ho gaye: bachi hui pull **1 se 7** ho gayi, aur shells wahi 3 rahin.

```
   Group mein neeche (Li, Na, K)             Period mein aage (Na, Cl)

   Li:  (+3) ))   e                           Na:  (+11) ))) e
        2 shells, pull 1, e paas                   3 shells, pull 1
   Na:  (+11) )))   e                         Cl:  (+17) ))) e
        3 shells, pull 1, e door                   3 shells, pull 7
   K:   (+19) ))))   e                        shells wahi, pull zyada:
        4 shells, pull 1, e aur door          rows nucleus ke paas khinch gayin

   (+n) = nucleus aur uske n protons      ) = ek shell (baahari shell bhi gini)
   e = baahari electron (door ya paas)
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Do vichaar ek nazar mein. Baayen: group mein neeche jaate hue shells badhti hain, "bachi hui pull" lagbhag wahi (1) rehti hai, par baahari electron door ho jaata hai, isliye atom bada. Daayen: period mein Na se Cl tak shells wahi (3) hain, par bachi hui pull 1 se 7 ho gayi, isliye atom chhota. Ye sirf sketch hai: asli atom gol hota hai.

**Ek sachchi baat is analogy ke baare mein:** panne magnet ko door karte hain (doori aur parten), jo vichaar 1 aur 2 ko samjhane ke liye kaafi hai. Asli atom mein andar ke electron baahari electron ko **dhakelte** (repel) bhi hain, isliye pull ghatti hai. Dono tasveerein ek hi nateeje par pahunchti hain.

## Part B: Atomic radius kaise naapte hain (ek chhoti si baat)

Atom ki koi seedhi "deewar" nahi hoti, isliye uska radius hum ek tareeke se **tay** karte hain: jaise do ek jaise atoms jab chipak kar baithe hon (dhatu ke jaal mein ya bond mein), to unke nucleus ke beech ki doori ka aadha. Isliye tables mein sankhyaein thodi alag-alag milti hain, jo is baat par nirbhar karti hain ki kis tareeke se naapa gaya. **Trend (order) har table mein wahi rehta hai**, isliye hum kuch mashhoor, lagbhag values ka ek hi set use karenge. Ye values 1 pm = 10⁻¹² m mein hain.

**Noble gases ke baare mein ek line:** He, Ne, Ar jaise atoms kisi se bond hi nahi banate, isliye unka size doosre tareeke se naapte hain aur wo values dusre atoms se seedhi milti nahi. Isliye is topic mein trend Li se F aur Na se Cl tak dekhte hain (jaise Class 9 mein bhi kiya tha).

## Part C: Group mein neeche: size badhta hai

Group 1 ke lagbhag atomic radius (pm):

| Atom | Shells | Lagbhag radius (pm) |
|---|---|---|
| Li | 2 | 152 |
| Na | 3 | 186 |
| K | 4 | 227 |
| Rb | 5 | 248 |

Group 17 mein bhi yahi: F (2 shells) lagbhag 64 pm, Cl (3 shells) lagbhag 99 pm. Neeche jaate hue har naye shell ke saath radius badhta hai. Badhne ki raftaar ek jaisi nahi hoti (Li se Na tak lagbhag 34 pm, Na se K tak lagbhag 41 pm, K se Rb tak lagbhag 21 pm), par **direction** hamesha wahi: neeche bada.

### Example 1. Li, Na, K: kaun bada, aur kyun?

- Li: 2 shells (2, 1). Na: 3 shells (2, 8, 1). K: 4 shells (2, 8, 8, 1).
- Teeno mein bachi hui pull lagbhag barabar (1) hai (Part A ka table).
- To faisla shells se hoga: zyada shells wala bada.
- **K > Na > Li.**

**Jaanch:** values 227 > 186 > 152 pm, order wahi hai ✅

### Example 2. Be aur Mg: ek hi group, alag period

- Be: 2, 2 (2 shells). Mg: 2, 8, 2 (3 shells).
- Dono Group 2 mein hain (valence electrons 2), par Mg mein ek shell zyada hai.
- **Mg bada.** Values lagbhag 111 pm aur 160 pm.

**Jaanch:** Group mein neeche shells badhti hain aur radius badhta hai, Mg neeche hai ✅

## Part D: Period mein aage: size ghat-ta hai

Period 2 aur Period 3 ke lagbhag radius (pm):

```
   Period 2:   Li    Be    B     C     N     O     F
   Protons     3     4     5     6     7     8     9
   Shells      2     2     2     2     2     2     2
   Radius      152   111   88    77    74    66    64

   Period 3:   Na    Mg    Al    Si    P     S     Cl
   Protons     11    12    13    14    15    16    17
   Shells      3     3     3     3     3     3     3
   Radius      186   160   143   118   110   103   99

   -----> shells wahi, protons badhte hain, radius ghat-ta hai ----->
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Period 2 aur Period 3 ke atomic radius (lagbhag, pm). Har naye tatva mein ek proton aur ek electron zyada hai, par shells ginti wahi hai, isliye radius har kadam par ghat-ta hai.

Ek rochak nateeja: Li se F tak radius lagbhag 152 se 64 pm ho gaya, yaani lagbhag **88 pm** ghata, aur Na se Cl tak lagbhag 186 se 99, yaani lagbhag **87 pm**. Dono periods mein "paas khinchne" ka asar lagbhag ek jaisa hai.

Kyun? Period mein aage jaate hue naya electron **wahi** baahari shell mein baithta hai. Andar ki shells wahi rehti hain, to shielding lagbhag wahi rehti hai. Par nucleus mein ek proton aur judta hai, isliye bachi hui pull har kadam par badhti hai (Na par 1, Cl par 7) aur wahi shells nucleus ke paas khinch jaati hain.

### Example 3. Li se F tak ka size, protons ke saath

- Li (3 protons) se F (9 protons) tak sab ke 2 shells.
- Zyada protons = zyada bachi hui pull = chhota radius.
- **Li > Be > B > C > N > O > F.**

**Jaanch:** 152 > 111 > 88 > 77 > 74 > 66 > 64 ✅ (Class 9 mein bhi "Li se F" ka trend yahi tha).

### Example 4. Period 3: ek kadam ka farak

Na se Mg tak: radius 186 se 160 pm, farak lagbhag **26 pm**. Mg se Al tak: 160 se 143 pm, farak lagbhag **17 pm**. Ye ghatav protons se kaise samjhaate hain?

- Na (11 protons) aur Mg (12 protons), dono ke 3 shells.
- Mg mein ek proton zyada hai, to wahi 3 shells paas khinchin: radius ghata.
- Aur aage Al mein ek aur proton, to aur ghata.

**Jaanch:** Na → Mg → Al ke radius 186 → 160 → 143, har kadam par ghata ✅. (Ghatne ki matra har kadam par ek jaisi nahi, par order hamesha wahi.)

## Part E: Cation, atom se chhota

Class 9 mein hum jaante hain: Na ek electron khokar Na⁺ banta hai, Mg do electron khokar Mg²⁺.

Na (2, 8, 1) → Na⁺ (2, 8). Mg (2, 8, 2) → Mg²⁺ (2, 8).

Do kaaran milkar ion ko chhota banate hain:

1. **Poori baahari shell hat jaati hai.** Na ki teesri shell (jisme 1 electron tha) khaali ho gayi, to ion ki sabse baahari shell ab doosri shell hai.
2. **Bache hue electron ko zyada pull milti hai.** Protons wahi rahe (Na⁺ mein bhi 11), par electrons 11 se 10 reh gaye. Kam electron, zyada pull per electron.

```
   Na  (11 protons, 11 electrons)         Na+  (11 protons, 10 electrons)

        (+11) ))) e                            (+11) ))
        3 shells, radius ~186 pm               2 shells, radius ~102 pm

   Mg  (12 protons, 12 electrons)         Mg2+ (12 protons, 10 electrons)

        (+12) ))) ee                           (+12) ))
        3 shells, radius ~160 pm               2 shells, radius ~72 pm
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Cation ban kar atom chhota ho jaata hai. Na → Na⁺: teesri shell gayi, radius lagbhag 186 se 102 pm. Mg → Mg²⁺: teesri shell gayi aur 12 protons ab sirf 10 electrons ko kheench rahe hain, radius lagbhag 160 se 72 pm.

### Example 5. Na aur Na⁺

- Na: 11 electrons, 3 shells, lagbhag 186 pm. Na⁺: 10 electrons, 2 shells, lagbhag 102 pm.
- **Na⁺ chhota hai.** Ye lagbhag 102 ÷ 186 = 0.55, yaani atom ka lagbhag aadha.

**Jaanch:** shell ek ghati aur electron ek ghata, dono hi radius ko chhota karte hain ✅

### Example 6. Mg aur Mg²⁺: aur bhi bada farak

- Mg: 12 electrons, lagbhag 160 pm. Mg²⁺: 10 electrons, 12 protons, lagbhag 72 pm.
- Ratio 72 ÷ 160 = 0.45: Mg²⁺ atom se aadhe se bhi chhota.
- Mg²⁺ ka farak Na⁺ se zyada hai, kyunki Mg²⁺ mein **12 protons** 10 electrons ko pakde hain (Na⁺ mein 11 protons).

**Jaanch:** Mg²⁺ (72 pm) < Na⁺ (102 pm) ✅, aur dono apne atom se chhote ✅

Isi liye ionic yaugikon (jaise Class 10 ke Chapter 2 mein dekhe the) mein har cation apne atom se chhota hota hai, aur Chapter 4 mein jab Na⁺ jaise cation cathode ki taraf jaate hain, to wo apne atom se chhote hote hain.

## Part F: Anion, atom se bada

Ab ulta: Cl ek electron lekar Cl⁻ banta hai, O do electron lekar O²⁻.

Cl (2, 8, 7) → Cl⁻ (2, 8, 8). O (2, 6) → O²⁻ (2, 8).

Yahan **shell ki ginti nahi badli** (Cl⁻ ke bhi 3, O²⁻ ke bhi 2), par:

1. Protons wahi rahe (Cl⁻ mein 17, O²⁻ mein 8), par electrons zyada ho gaye (18, 10). **Wahi pull, zyada electrons mein baant gayi**, to har electron ko kam pull mili.
2. Naye electron ek doosre ko **dhakelte** (repel) hain. Baahari shell phail jaati hai.

```
   Cl  (17 protons, 17 electrons)         Cl-  (17 protons, 18 electrons)

        (+17) ))) 7e                           (+17) ))) 8e
        radius ~99 pm                          radius ~181 pm

   O   (8 protons, 8 electrons)           O2-  (8 protons, 10 electrons)

        (+8) )) 6e                             (+8) )) 8e
        radius ~66 pm                          radius ~140 pm
```

> **Figure 1.4** – Anion ban kar atom bada ho jaata hai. Cl → Cl⁻: shells wahi (3), protons wahi (17), par electron 17 se 18, radius lagbhag 99 se 181 pm. O → O²⁻: shells wahi (2), protons wahi (8), par electrons 8 se 10, radius lagbhag 66 se 140 pm.

### Example 7. Cl aur Cl⁻

- Cl: 17 electrons, lagbhag 99 pm. Cl⁻: 18 electrons, lagbhag 181 pm.
- **Cl⁻ bada.** Lagbhag 181 ÷ 99 = 1.83 guna.

**Jaanch:** protons barabar (17), electron zyada: pull baant gayi aur electrons ne ek doosre ko dhakela ✅. Isiliye namak ke ghol mein Cl⁻ (anion) Na⁺ (cation) se bada hota hai.

### Example 8. O aur O²⁻: do electron ka asar

- O: 8 electrons, lagbhag 66 pm. O²⁻: 10 electrons, lagbhag 140 pm.
- Lagbhag 140 ÷ 66 = 2.1 guna.
- **O²⁻ bada.** Jitne zyada electron liye, utna zyada phailaav (aur wahi protons).

**Jaanch:** Cl → Cl⁻ mein 1 electron se lagbhag 1.8 guna, O → O²⁻ mein 2 electron se lagbhag 2.1 guna ✅

Chhota sa yaad rakhne ka tareeka: **"Cation: kam electron, chhota. Anion: zyada electron, bada."**

## Part G: Isoelectronic ions: electron barabar, size alag

**Isoelectronic** matlab: electrons ki ginti barabar. O²⁻, F⁻, Na⁺ aur Mg²⁺ sab ke **10 electrons** hain (configuration 2, 8). Par unke protons alag-alag hain:

| Ion | Protons | Electrons | Lagbhag radius (pm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| O²⁻ | 8 | 10 | 140 |
| F⁻ | 9 | 10 | 133 |
| Na⁺ | 11 | 10 | 102 |
| Mg²⁺ | 12 | 10 | 72 |

Sab ke electron aur shells barabar, to farak sirf **nuclear pull** ka hai: jitne zyada protons, utni zyada pull aur utna chhota ion.

```
   Size:   O2-  >  F-  >  Na+  >  Mg2+
   Protons: 8       9      11      12
           (sab ke 10 electron, 2 shells)

   protons badhte hain  ---->  ion chhota hota hai
```

> **Figure 1.5** – 10 electron wale ions: O²⁻ (8 protons) sabse bada, Mg²⁺ (12 protons) sabse chhota. Electron barabar hone par protons hi size tay karte hain.

### Example 9. Ek aur isoelectronic jodi

Cl⁻ aur K⁺ dono ke 18 electrons hain (2, 8, 8). Cl⁻ mein 17 protons, K⁺ mein 19 protons.

- Electrons barabar, K⁺ mein protons zyada: **K⁺ chhota.**
- Lagbhag values: Cl⁻ 181 pm, K⁺ 138 pm.

**Jaanch:** 138 < 181 ✅. Dekho, K atom (227 pm) Cl atom (99 pm) se bahut bada tha, par ion ban kar order ulta ho gaya: K⁺ (138) ab Cl⁻ (181) se chhota hai. Ye cation aur anion ke asar ka ek khaas nateeja hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Jis atom mein zyada electrons hain, wo hamesha bada hota hai."**

Ye galat hai, aur ye is topic ki sabse badi galti hai. Gin ke dekho:

- Mg mein 12 electrons aur S mein 16 electrons. Par Mg lagbhag 160 pm aur S lagbhag 103 pm: **kam electron wala bada.** Kaaran: dono ke 3 shells hain, par S mein 16 protons hain aur wahi shells zyada khinchi hain.
- Li (3 electrons) lagbhag 152 pm, F (9 electrons) lagbhag 64 pm: yahan bhi zyada electron wala chhota.
- O²⁻ mein 10 electrons aur Mg²⁺ mein bhi 10 electrons: **electrons barabar, par size lagbhag 140 aur 72 pm** (Part G).
- K⁺ mein 18 electrons aur K mein 19 electrons: **zyada electron wala (K) bada** hai (227 pm aur 138 pm). Yahan kaaran ye hai ki ek poori shell zyada hai.

To size electrons ki ginti se nahi, **shells aur bachi hui pull** (Part A) se tay hota hai. Electrons ki ginti kabhi-kabhi sahi nateeja deti hai, par sirf sanyog se.

**Galti 2: "Cation bana to size nahi badalta, sirf charge badalta hai."**

Na ki 3 shells thi, Na⁺ ki 2 hain. Radius lagbhag 186 se 102 pm ho jaata hai. Cation hamesha apne atom se chhota hota hai.

**Galti 3: "Anion mein protons badh jaate hain, isliye wo chhota ho jaana chahiye."**

Protons kabhi nahi badalte, kyunki ion banne mein sirf electron aate-jaate hain. Cl⁻ mein bhi 17 protons hain. Zyada electron wahi pull ko baant lete hain, to anion bada hota hai.

**Galti 4: "Periodic table mein neeche jaate hue protons badh rahe hain, to atom chhota hona chahiye."**

Protons zaroor badhte hain (Li 3, Na 11, K 19), par saath hi andar ke electron aur shells badh jaati hain. Bachi hui pull Li, Na, K mein lagbhag barabar (1) rehti hai (Part A), isliye shells ka asar jeetata hai aur atom bada hota hai.

## Quick Recap

- Size do vichaaron se tay hota hai: **shells ki ginti** (zyada shells = door = bada) aur **nuclear pull** (protons ka khinchna, jise andar ki shells ka **shielding** kam kar deta hai).
- Mota hisaab: bachi hui pull ≈ protons − andar ke electrons. Group mein ye lagbhag barabar rehti hai, period mein badhti hai.
- **Group mein neeche:** shells badhti hain, atomic radius badhta hai (Li 152, Na 186, K 227, Rb 248 pm, lagbhag).
- **Period mein aage:** shells wahi, protons badhte hain, atomic radius ghat-ta hai (Na 186 se Cl 99 pm, lagbhag).
- Noble gases ka size alag tareeke se naapte hain, isliye unhe is trend mein nahi jodte.
- **Cation atom se chhota** (shell hati, electron kam). **Anion atom se bada** (electron zyada, wahi protons, dhakka).
- **Isoelectronic ions** (jaise O²⁻, F⁻, Na⁺, Mg²⁺): zyada protons = chhota ion.
- "Zyada electron = bada atom" galat hai.

## Practice Questions

(Sab sawaal paper par hal karne ke liye hain. Sab values lagbhag hain, aur ek hi table ke set se li gayi hain.)

1. Rubidium has 5 shells and sodium has 3 shells. Which atom is larger, and how does the "net pull" idea (protons minus inner electrons) apply to the two? Use radius Rb about 248 pm and Na about 186 pm.
2. Arrange O, S and F in decreasing order of atomic radius and give a reason in terms of protons and shells. (Lagbhag radii: O 66, S 103, F 64 pm.)
3. Arrange S, Al and Mg in increasing order of atomic radius. Mention the number of shells and protons for each.
4. Aluminium atom has a radius of about 143 pm and Al³⁺ has a radius of about 54 pm. Write the electronic configuration of both and explain, with the two ideas, why the ion is so much smaller.
5. Sulphur forms S²⁻. Its atomic radius is about 103 pm and S²⁻ is about 184 pm. How many protons and electrons does each have? Explain the increase in size.
6. Arrange K⁺, S²⁻ and Ca²⁺ in decreasing order of size, using lagbhag radii 138, 184 and 100 pm. Show that all three are isoelectronic and say why their sizes differ.
7. A student says: "Nitrogen has 7 electrons and beryllium has only 4 electrons, so a nitrogen atom must be larger than a beryllium atom." Show with the data (Be about 111 pm, N about 74 pm) that she is wrong and explain the reason.
8. Beryllium and fluorine both belong to Period 2. Which atom is larger? Which ion (Be²⁺ or F⁻) is larger? Use lagbhag values Be 111 pm, F 64 pm, Be²⁺ 45 pm, F⁻ 133 pm. Explain why the order flips for the ions.
9. Radii of the alkali metals (lagbhag, pm): Li 152, Na 186, K 227, Rb 248. Find the increase in size for each step from one element to the next. Does the size increase by the same amount each time? Is the direction of the change always the same?
10. Rb has about 248 pm radius and Rb⁺ has about 152 pm. How many shells does each have? By what percentage does the radius fall? (Rb has 5 shells.)
11. Rb has 37 protons and Be has only 4 protons, yet Rb is much larger. Explain using shells and the net pull idea. Does adding protons ever make an atom bigger if shells stay the same?
12. State True or False and give a reason: (a) A cation is always smaller than its atom. (b) An anion has more protons than its atom. (c) Mg²⁺ and Ne have the same number of electrons. (d) The first element of Period 2 (Li) is larger than the last element (F). (e) Na⁺ is larger than Mg²⁺ because it has fewer protons.
13. Activity (adult nearby, nothing hazardous): stick a magnet on a refrigerator door with 1 sheet of paper, then add sheets one at a time, holding your other hand below. Note how many sheets it holds. Which part of an atom does the magnet play, and which part do the sheets play? Name one thing the analogy does not show about real atoms.

### Answers

1. **Rb bada hai.** Rb ke 5 shells aur Na ke 3, to Rb ka baahari electron door hai. Dono mein bachi hui pull lagbhag barabar hai (Rb: 37 − 36 = 1, Na: 11 − 10 = 1), isliye faisla shells se hota hai. Radius 248 > 186 pm ✅.
2. S ke 3 shells hain, O aur F ke sirf 2: isliye S sabse bada. O aur F dono Period 2 mein (2 shells), par F mein 9 protons aur O mein 8: shells wahi, protons zyada = chhota. **S > O > F** (lagbhag 103, 66, 64 pm) ✅.
3. Mg: 3 shells, 12 protons (160 pm). Al: 3 shells, 13 protons (143 pm). S: 3 shells, 16 protons (103 pm). Shells barabar, protons zyada = chhota. Increasing order: **S < Al < Mg** ✅ (103 < 143 < 160).
4. Al: 2, 8, 3 (3 shells). Al³⁺: 2, 8 (2 shells). Teesri shell chali gayi, aur 13 protons ab sirf 10 electrons ko kheench rahe hain. Radius lagbhag 143 se 54 pm. Ratio 54 ÷ 143 = 0.38: atom ka lagbhag 38 pratishat ✅.
5. S: 16 protons, 16 electrons. S²⁻: 16 protons, 18 electrons. Protons wahi, electrons 2 zyada: pull zyada electrons mein baant gayi aur electrons ne ek doosre ko dhakela, to size 103 se 184 pm ✅.
6. S²⁻: 16 protons, 18 electrons. K⁺: 19 protons, 18 electrons. Ca²⁺: 20 protons, 18 electrons. Teeno ke 18 electrons (2, 8, 8), to isoelectronic. Zyada protons = zyada pull = chhota ion. **S²⁻ > K⁺ > Ca²⁺** (184 > 138 > 100 pm) ✅.
7. Be (4 electrons) lagbhag 111 pm aur N (7 electrons) lagbhag 74 pm: N **chhota** hai. Dono ke 2 shells hain, N mein 7 protons aur Be mein 4, to N ke electron zyada kheeche hain. Isliye "zyada electron = bada" galat hai ✅.
8. Atoms: Be (111 pm) F (64 pm) se **bada**. Ions: Be²⁺ (45 pm) aur F⁻ (133 pm): **F⁻ bada**. Order ulta ho gaya kyunki Be ne 2 electron khoye (shell hati, 4 protons ab 2 electrons ko kheench rahe hain) aur F ne 1 electron liya (wahi 9 protons ab 10 electrons ko baant rahe hain). Cation chhota hua, anion bada ✅.
9. Li se Na: 186 − 152 = **34 pm**. Na se K: 227 − 186 = **41 pm**. K se Rb: 248 − 227 = **21 pm**. Increase har baar barabar nahi hai. Par **direction hamesha wahi** (har baar badha): neeche jaate hue size badhta hai ✅.
10. Rb: 5 shells. Rb⁺: 4 shells (ek baahari shell gayi). Radius 248 se 152 pm: kami 96 pm, 96 ÷ 248 × 100 = lagbhag **38.7 pratishat** ✅ (lagbhag 39 pratishat).
11. Rb: 5 shells, Be: 2 shells. Bachi hui pull Rb mein 37 − 36 = 1 aur Be mein 4 − 2 = 2 hai, yaani dono ki lagbhag ek jaisi (kam), par Rb ka baahari electron 3 shells door hai, to Rb bahut bada (248 pm aur 111 pm). Agar shells **wahi** rahein (jaise Na se Cl), to protons badhne par atom **chhota** hota hai, bada nahi. Protons bada karne ka asar tabhi dikhta hai jab shells nahi badhti ✅.
12. (a) **True** ✅: shell hat jaati hai ya electron kam hone se bacha electron zyada kheencha jaata hai. (b) **False** ❌: protons wahi rehte hain, sirf electrons badhte hain. (c) **True** ✅: Mg²⁺ ke 12 − 2 = 10 electrons, Ne ke bhi 10. (d) **True** ✅: Li lagbhag 152 pm, F lagbhag 64 pm. (e) **True** ✅: dono ions ke 10 electrons hain (isoelectronic), Na⁺ mein 11 protons aur Mg²⁺ mein 12, to Na⁺ par pull kam hai aur wo bada hai (lagbhag 102 aur 72 pm).
13. Magnet **nucleus** hai (kheenchne wala). Panne **andar ki bhari shells** hain (parda aur doori badhate hain). Jaise-jaise parten badhti hain, magnet ki pakad kam hoti hai, bilkul baahari electron ki pull jaise. Analogy ye nahi dikhati ki andar ke electron baahari electron ko **dhakelte** bhi hain, aur ye bhi ki atom mein electron ek jagah par jame nahi hote, wo nucleus ke aas-paas ghoomte hain.

**Aage kya?** Topic 2 mein hum ek electron ko atom se bahar **nikaalne ki keemat** naapenge, yaani ionisation energy, ek bachhe ke haath se khilauna kheenchne wali kahani ke saath. Aur dekhenge ki ye keemat group aur period mein kaise badalti hai, aur usse ye bhi samjhenge ki K, Na se zyada tezi se kyun react karta hai.
