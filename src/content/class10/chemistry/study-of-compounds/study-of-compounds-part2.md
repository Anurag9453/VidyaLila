# Topic 2: Ammonia: Lab Se Khet Tak

Topic 1 mein humne ek **acidic gas**, HCl, ko lab mein banate dekha: mishran garam karo, gas ko dry karo, heavier hone ki wajah se upward displacement se jar mein bharo. Aaj ki gas HCl ki bilkul ulti hai: ek **basic gas**, ammonia NH₃. Jab dono gas paas aati hain to safed dhuan banta hai (Topic 1 ka NH₃ + HCl → NH₄Cl). Aaj dekhenge ki ammonia lab mein kaise banti hai, kaise sookhai aur bharee jaati hai (aur kyun HCl wale tareeke yahan galat hain), kaarkhane mein hawa se kaise banti hai, aur khet tak kaise pahunchti hai.

## Khaad ki dukaan aur hawa mein chhupa nitrogen

Gaon ki khaad ki dukaan par subah-subah bheed thi. Mr. Khadilkar apni bhatiji Netra ko saath lekar wahan kuch kaam se aaye the. Ek kisaan ne boriyaan khareedi: ek ek bori par bada sa "N" aur kuch percent likha tha. Dukaandaar ne boriyan gaadi mein rakhwayi, kisaan ne paise diye aur chala gaya.

Netra ne dhyan se dekha. "Chacha, ye kisaan nitrogen kyun khareed raha hai? Hamari science book kehti hai ki hawa ka lagbhag 78 percent hissa nitrogen hai. Khet ke upar to hawa hi hawa hai. Phir paise dekar kyun khareedna?"

Mr. Khadilkar muskuraye. "Sawaal bilkul sahi hai. Hawa mein nitrogen bahut hai, par wo **N₂ ke roop mein** hai, jisme do N atom bahut mazbooti se jude hain. Paudhe use seedha nahi le sakte. Unhe nitrogen chahiye aisa jo kisi **yaugik** mein ho, jaise ammonium ya nitrate. To kisi ko hawa ke nitrogen ko tod kar yaugik mein badalna padta hai. Wo kaam ek bahut mashhoor gas karti hai, jisse ye saari boriyan bani hain."

"Konsi gas, Chacha?" "Wahi, jiski gandh tumne kabhi kisi purane saaf-safai ke ghol mein mehsoos ki hogi: **ammonia**. Aur ye bhi bata doon: Class 9 mein tumne N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ naam se dekha tha, aaj uski poori kahani hai." Netra boli, "To hawa ka nitrogen kisi tarah ammonia ban jaata hai, aur ammonia se ye boriyan?" "Bilkul. Chalo, pehle lab mein ye gas banti kaise hai wo dekhte hain, phir kaarkhana."

```
   Hawa ka N₂ (paudhe seedha nahi le sakte)
        :
        :  hydrogen ke saath, Haber process
        v
   Ammonia NH₃
        :
        :  acid ke saath (ya aur kadam)
        v
   Khaad: ammonium sulphate, ammonium nitrate, urea
        :
        v
   Khet  -->  fasal  -->  hamari thali
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Nitrogen ka safar: hawa se ammonia, ammonia se khaad, khaad se khet. Is topic ka baaki hissa isi safar ke har kadam ko kholta hai.

## Part A: Lab mein ammonia kaise banti hai

> **Suraksha (isse pehle ki aage padho):**
> - Ammonia gas **zehreeli** hai aur aankh, naak, gale ko jalati hai. Isko student kabhi nahi banata, nahi sunghta, nahi chhoota.
> - Ye sab **sirf padhne ke liye** ya **sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo)** hai, jo school lab mein teacher fume cupboard, goggles aur gloves ke saath karta hai.
> - Sunghna ho to teacher sirf haath se halki hawa apni taraf laata hai ("waft"), seedha nahi sunghta.
> - Ghar ka ammonia cleaner bachche kabhi na chhuen. Ise bleach ya toilet cleaner ke saath **kabhi mat milana**: zehreeli gas banti hai.

Sabse seedha tareeka: ek **ammonium namak** aur ek **base** ko garam karo. Ammonium namak NH₄Cl, aur base **sookha bujha hua chuna Ca(OH)₂**. Analytical Chemistry mein humne dekha tha ki NH₄⁺ ko OH⁻ milte hi NH₃ nikalti hai. Yahi reaction yahan **banane ke liye** hota hai:

2NH₄Cl + Ca(OH)₂ → CaCl₂ + 2H₂O + 2NH₃

Dono thos **sookhe** hote hain, ek saath pisse hue (mishran), isliye pehle kuch bhi ghol nahi hota. Garam karne par reaction shuru hoti hai.

### Apparatus kaisa dikhta hai

```
   Test tube: sookha mishran NH₄Cl + Ca(OH)₂
   (band sira thoda UPAR, mooh halka NEECHE jhuka)          ulta gas jar
                                                        .-------------.
    .-----------.                                       :      .      :  <-- tube jar ke TOP tak:
   (   mishran   [cork]==.                              :      :      :      NH₃ upar jamti hai
    '-----------'         :     .-----------.           :      :      :
          ^               '====>: CaO ke    :===.       :      :      :
       burner                   : tukde     :   :       :      :      :
    (halki aanch)               '-----------'   :       :      :      :
                                drying tower    :       '      :      '
                                                :              :      <-- jar ka mooh neeche, khula:
                                                '--------------'          hawa yahan se nikalti hai
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Ammonia ki lab preparation (teacher demo): baayen taraf tube mein sookha mishran, uska mooh cork se band, jisme se delivery tube nikalti hai. Tube ka **mooh halka neeche** aur band sira thoda upar hai, taaki bani hui paani ki boondein wapas garam kaanch par na ruk-ruk kar girein. Gas pehle CaO ke tukdon wale tower se guzarti hai (sookhne ke liye) aur phir ulte gas jar ke andar upar tak pahunchti hai. Jar ka mooh neeche aur **khula** hai, isliye poora apparatus band nahi hai.

Do baatein jo drawing se samjhein:
1. **Tube neeche kyun jhuka hai?** Reaction mein paani (2H₂O) bhi banta hai. Ye paani garam test tube ke thande mooh par boond banta hai. Agar tube ka mooh neeche na ho to wo boondein garam tale par girkar kaanch tod sakti hain. Isliye mooh halka **neeche** hota hai (halka sa).
2. **Delivery tube kahan tak?** Jar ke **upar tak**: jinhe aage Part B mein samjhenge.

### Example 1. Equation aur ratio

**Sawaal:** 107 g NH₄Cl ko poore Ca(OH)₂ ke saath garam kiya. Kitni NH₃ (mole mein aur STP par litre mein) aur kitna Ca(OH)₂ lagega? Mass: N 14, H 1, Cl 35.5, Ca 40, O 16.

**Kadam 1 (molar mass):** NH₄Cl = 14 + 4 + 35.5 = **53.5** g/mol. Ca(OH)₂ = 40 + 2 × 17 = **74** g/mol.
**Kadam 2 (moles):** 107 ÷ 53.5 = **2 mol** NH₄Cl.
**Kadam 3 (ratio 2 : 1 : 2):** 2NH₄Cl se 2 mol NH₃, aur Ca(OH)₂ = 1 mol = **74 g**.
**Kadam 4 (volume):** 2 × 22.4 = **44.8 L** NH₃ (STP). Mass NH₃ = 2 × 17 = 34 g.

**Jaanch:** CaCl₂ = 1 mol = 111 g, H₂O = 2 mol = 36 g, NH₃ = 34 g. Reactants 107 + 74 = 181 g; products 111 + 36 + 34 = 181 g ✅. Atoms: N 2 = 2, H 8 + 2 = 10 = 4 + 6, Cl 2 = 2, Ca 1 = 1, O 2 = 2 ✅.

## Part B: Sookhana aur bharna: kya chalega, kya nahi

Ammonia ke saath paani ki bhaap bhi nikalti hai, to gas ko **dry** karna padta hai. Topic 1 mein HCl ko conc. H₂SO₄ se dry kiya tha. Kya ammonia ke saath bhi wahi kar sakte hain? **Nahi.** Yahin Chapter 1 ki acid-base wali baat kaam aati hai: acid aur base aapas mein react karte hain. To niyam: **acidic gas ke liye acidic drying agent, basic gas ke liye basic drying agent.**

Ammonia basic hai. Conc. H₂SO₄ ek acid hai, aur acid base ko kha jaata hai:

2NH₃ + H₂SO₄ → (NH₄)₂SO₄

Gas **namak mein badal jaati hai**: drying ke bajaye gas hi gayab. Anhydrous CaCl₂ bhi NH₃ ko jakad leta hai (ek jod bana leta hai), isliye use bhi nahi lete. Sahi drying agent hai **quicklime CaO**: ye basic oxide hai, paani ko kheench leta hai (CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂) aur ammonia se reaction nahi karta.

### Hawa se halki, isliye jar ulta

Chapter 5 se: vapour density = molar mass ÷ 2. NH₃ ka 17 ÷ 2 = **8.5**, aur hawa ka 29 ÷ 2 = **14.5**. Ammonia hawa se **halki** hai, to wo **upar** uthti hai. Isliye jar **ulta** (mooh neeche) rakhte hain aur delivery tube **jar ke top tak** le jaate hain. Gas upar jama hoti hai aur bhaari hawa ko neeche, khule mooh se dhakel kar nikaalti hai. Isse kehte hain **downward displacement of air** (hawa ko neeche dhakelna).

HCl bhaari thi to jar seedha, tube neeche tak (upward displacement of air). Ammonia halki hai to ulta jar, tube upar tak. Dono ka **ulta** hona kaaran se hai.

```
   HCl (bhaari: V.D. 18.25)             NH₃ (halki: V.D. 8.5)
   jar seedha, tube NEECHE tak          jar ulta, tube UPAR tak

       hawa upar se nikalti                 .-------------.
          ^    :                            :  NH₃    .   :  <-- NH₃ upar jamti hai
      :   ^    :      :                     :         :   :
      :        :      :                     :   hawa  :   :
      :        :      :                     :    v    :   :
      :  HCl   :      :                     '         :   '
      :  neeche.      :                         v     :      <-- mooh neeche, khula:
      :______________:                        hawa    :          hawa neeche se nikalti
         tube jar ke tale tak                    tube neeche se andar
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Do gas, do tareeke: bhaari HCl ke liye seedha jar aur neeche tak tube (hawa upar se nikalti hai); halki NH₃ ke liye ulta jar aur upar tak tube (hawa neeche se nikalti hai).

**Paani ke upar kyun nahi?** Ammonia **bahut hi zyada** ghulansheel hai: 1 volume paani mein lagbhag 700 volume tak ghul sakti hai. Paani ke upar jama karne ki koshish karein to gas bante hi paani mein ghul jaayegi, aur paani wapas tube mein khinch aayega. Isliye na paani ke upar, na paani se.

**Jar bhara ya nahi?** Teacher geela (moist) laal litmus jar ke mooh ke paas laata hai: neela ho jaaye to jar bhar gaya.

### Example 2. Kaun sa drying agent?

**Sawaal:** Ammonia ko conc. H₂SO₄, CaO aur anhydrous CaCl₂ mein se kisse dry karenge? Har ek ka reason do.

- **Conc. H₂SO₄:** nahi. 2NH₃ + H₂SO₄ → (NH₄)₂SO₄: gas namak ban jaati hai.
- **Anhydrous CaCl₂:** nahi. Ye NH₃ ko jakad leta hai.
- **CaO:** haan. Basic hai, paani kheenchta hai, NH₃ se reaction nahi.

**Jaanch:** acidic ya neutral drier basic gas ko kha jaate hain; basic drier basic gas ko chhodta hai ✅. Atoms 2NH₃ + H₂SO₄: N 2 = 2, H 6 + 2 = 8 = 8, S 1 = 1, O 4 = 4 ✅.

## Part C: Ammonia ke gun aur reactions

**Bhautik gun:** bina rang ki, tez chubhne wali gandh, hawa se halki, paani mein bahut ghulansheel. Dabane aur thanda karne par asaani se **liquid** ban jaati hai (ubaalne ka bindu lagbhag −33 °C): ye baat refrigeration mein kaam aati hai.

**Ammonia aur paani:** NH₃ + H₂O ⇌ NH₄OH, yaani ghol mein NH₄⁺ aur OH⁻ ke thode ions. NH₄OH ek **kamzor base** hai (Chapter 1: pH lagbhag 11). Isliye moist red litmus neela hota hai, par **sookhi** NH₃ sookhe red litmus par asar nahi karti (paani ke bina OH⁻ nahi bante: yahi Chapter 1 ka dry-aur-wet wala niyam).

### Fountain experiment (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo)

Teacher ek gol flask sookhi ammonia se bharta hai (downward displacement se). Flask ko ulta karke clamp se jakadta hai. Cork ke ek chhed mein ek lambi tube neeche ek beaker ke paani tak jaati hai (paani mein thoda phenolphthalein); doosre chhed mein ek dropper mein thoda paani hai.

```
       clamp se tangi hui ulti gol flask: andar sookhi NH₃
           ___________
          /   . . .   \
         /  .  jet  .  \       <-- paani fawware ki tarah upar uthta hai
        :     ( ^ )     :
         \    nozzle   /
          \___________/
              : :  :
           [cork]   dropper (thoda paani)
              :
              :  lambi tube
              :
      :       :       :
      :~~~~~~~:~~~~~~~:   <-- paani ki satah
      :       :       :   <-- tube ka sira paani mein dooba
      :     paani     :   (paani mein phenolphthalein)
      :_______________:   beaker
```

> **Figure 2.4** – Fountain experiment ka setup: ulti flask mein sookhi NH₃, cork ke ek chhed se tube beaker ke paani mein, doosre chhed mein dropper. Dropper dabate hi thoda paani flask mein jaata hai.

Kya hota hai: dropper ka thoda paani ammonia ko turant ghol leta hai, flask ke andar **dabaav girta hai**, aur bahar ki hawa beaker ke paani ko tube se dhakel kar andar fawware ki tarah bhej deti hai. Paani flask mein pahunchte hi phenolphthalein **gulabi** ho jaata hai (alkali ka saboot: Chapter 1). Isse do baatein pata chalti hain: ammonia **bahut ghulansheel** hai aur uska ghol **alkaline** hai. (Ye dono baatein HCl ke fountain mein bhi dekhi thi, bas wahan ghol acidic tha.)

### Ammonia ki reactions

| Reaction | Equation | Kya dikhta hai |
|---|---|---|
| HCl gas ke saath | NH₃ + HCl → NH₄Cl | safed ghana dhuan (NH₄Cl ke chhote thos kan) |
| Oxygen mein jalna | 4NH₃ + 3O₂ → 2N₂ + 6H₂O | peeli-hari jwala; hawa mein ye nahi jalti |
| Pt catalyst, garam | 4NH₃ + 5O₂ → 4NO + 6H₂O | NO banti hai: Topic 3 ka raasta |
| Chlorine se (ammonia zyada ho to) | 8NH₃ + 3Cl₂ → 6NH₄Cl + N₂ | safed NH₄Cl dhuan, N₂ gas |
| Acid se | 2NH₃ + H₂SO₄ → (NH₄)₂SO₄ | namak, khaad ka hissa |

Chlorine zehreeli hai: ye reaction sirf padhne ke liye. Is reaction mein agar ammonia kam aur chlorine zyada ho to ek aur khatarnaak padarth bhi ban sakta hai (**NCl₃**, jo phat sakta hai): iski kahani **aage ki class mein**.

Ammonia ki NH₃ + HCl wali reaction ka ek upyog hai: kisi ne agar ammonia ki gas ki leak ki jaanch karni ho to HCl ki botal paas laane par safed dhuan dikhne se pata chalta hai (professional log hi ye karte hain).

### Example 3. 34 g ammonia oxygen mein jalti hai

**Sawaal:** 4NH₃ + 3O₂ → 2N₂ + 6H₂O. 34 g NH₃ ke jalne se kitna N₂, kitna paani aur kitni O₂ lagegi?

**Kadam 1:** 34 ÷ 17 = **2 mol** NH₃. **Kadam 2 (ratio 4 : 3 : 2 : 6):** O₂ = 2 × 3 ÷ 4 = **1.5 mol** = 48 g; N₂ = 2 × 2 ÷ 4 = **1 mol** = 28 g; H₂O = 2 × 6 ÷ 4 = **3 mol** = 54 g.

**Jaanch:** reactants 34 + 48 = 82 g; products 28 + 54 = 82 g ✅. Atoms: N 4 = 4, H 12 = 12, O 6 = 6 ✅.

## Part D: Haber process: hawa se ammonia

Lab ki method chhote paimane par hai. Kaarkhane mein hazaaron ton ammonia chahiye, aur uske liye seedha hawa ka N₂ aur hydrogen H₂ se banate hain. Class 9 se: N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃, iron catalyst, bahut dabaav, **ulti ho sakne wali** reaction.

**Shartein (lagbhag):** dabaav lagbhag **200 atmosphere**, taapmaan lagbhag **450 °C**, iron catalyst (saath mein thoda promoter).

### Ye shartein samjhauta (compromise) kyun hain?

- Reaction **reversible** hai: bani hui NH₃ phir tootkar N₂ aur H₂ bhi ban sakti hai. To ek mishran milta hai, jisme **kuch hi** ammonia hoti hai.
- **Kam taapmaan** par mishran mein zyada ammonia milti hai, par reaction itni **dheemi** hoti hai ki kaam ka nahi. **Zyada taapmaan** par tezi aati hai par ammonia ka hissa kam hota hai. Isliye beech ka **lagbhag 450 °C**, aur catalyst tezi badhata hai (catalyst sirf **speed** badhata hai, kitni ammonia banegi wo nahi).
- **Zyada dabaav** par zyada ammonia milti hai kyunki 1 N₂ + 3 H₂ = **4 kan** milkar **2 kan** NH₃ dete hain: kan kam hote hain, to dabi hui jagah ke liye ye side behtar hai. Par bahut zyada dabaav ke liye mazboot, mehnge pipe chahiye, isliye lagbhag 200 atm par ruka jaata hai.

(Isko poori tarah samajhna aage ki class ka hai: yahan sirf "conditions samjhauta hain" itna yaad rakho.)

```
   N₂ (hawa se) + H₂
         :
         v
   Compressor : lagbhag 200 atm
         :
         v
   Converter (iron catalyst, lagbhag 450 °C)
         :
         v
   Thanda karna (cooler)
         :
         +--> NH₃ liquid ban kar alag --> store karo
         :
         +--> bina badli N₂ aur H₂ gas
                    :
                    +--> wapas compressor ke aage (recycle)
```

> **Figure 2.5** – Haber process ka flow chart: gas dabai jaati hai, iron catalyst par kuch hi hissa ammonia banta hai, thanda karne par ammonia liquid ban kar nikal jaati hai (N₂ aur H₂ gas hi rehti hain) aur wo gas wapas bheji jaati hai, taaki kuch bekaar na jaaye.

### Example 4. Ammonia ka mass-hisaab

**Sawaal:** 140 kg N₂ se (ideal, bina kami ke) kitni NH₃ aur kitni H₂ lagegi?

**Kadam 1:** N₂ = 28 g/mol, to 140 kg = 5 kmol. **Kadam 2 (ratio 1 : 3 : 2):** NH₃ = 10 kmol = 10 × 17 = **170 kg**; H₂ = 15 kmol = 15 × 2 = **30 kg**.

**Jaanch:** 140 + 30 = 170 ✅. Atoms: N 10 = 10, H 30 = 30 ✅.

## Part E: Ammonia khet tak: khaad

Ammonia gas ko seedha zameen mein daalna mushkil hai, isliye use acid se milakar **ammonium namak** banate hain, jo ghulkar paudhon ko NH₄⁺ ya nitrate dete hain:

2NH₃ + H₂SO₄ → (NH₄)₂SO₄ (ammonium sulphate)
NH₃ + HNO₃ → NH₄NO₃ (ammonium nitrate: HNO₃ ki kahani Topic 3 mein)

Teesri khaad **urea** CO(NH₂)₂ bhi ammonia se banti hai (formula yaad rakho, uska banne ka tareeka aage ki class mein). Class 9 mein humne in teeno mein nitrogen ka percent nikala tha; ab hum inse ammonia ka hisaab nikalenge.

```
   N₂ (hawa) + H₂
        :
        v
   NH₃ --+--> + H₂SO₄ --> (NH₄)₂SO₄
         :
         +--> + HNO₃  --> NH₄NO₃
         :
         +--> urea CO(NH₂)₂
         :
         +--> HNO₃ banane ke liye (Topic 3)
```

> **Figure 2.6** – Ammonia kaise-kaise khaad mein badalti hai. Neeche wali shaakha Topic 3 ki taraf jaati hai.

### Example 5. Khaad ke liye ammonia

**Sawaal:** 264 kg (NH₄)₂SO₄ banane ke liye kitni NH₃ lagegi? (NH₄)₂SO₄ ka M = 2 × 18 + 96 = 132.

**Kadam 1:** 264 ÷ 132 = **2 kmol**. **Kadam 2 (ratio 2NH₃ : 1):** NH₃ = 4 kmol = 4 × 17 = **68 kg**.

**Jaanch:** acid H₂SO₄ = 2 kmol × 98 = 196 kg; 68 + 196 = 264 ✅.

### Example 6. NH₄NO₃ ke liye ammonia

**Sawaal:** 400 kg NH₄NO₃ ke liye kitni NH₃? NH₄NO₃ ka M = 14 + 4 + 14 + 48 = 80.

400 ÷ 80 = **5 kmol**, ratio 1 : 1, NH₃ = 5 × 17 = **85 kg**.

**Jaanch:** 85 kg + HNO₃ (5 × 63 = 315 kg) = 400 kg ✅.

**Aur upyog:** nitric acid banana (Topic 3), thande bhandar ka refrigeration (liquid ammonia ke bhaap banne par thandak), aur ghar ke safai wale ghol (dilute NH₄OH: **bachche kabhi na chhuen**, bleach ya toilet cleaner ke saath kabhi nahi milana).

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti: "Ammonia ko conc. H₂SO₄ se dry kar sakte hain, aur paani ke upar jama kar sakte hain."**
HCl ke liye ye dono sahi lagte hain, isliye ammonia ke liye bhi laga dena aasaan hai. Par dono galat hain. Do chhote demo (sirf padhne ke liye):

**Demo 1: conc. H₂SO₄ mein se gas guzaarna.**

```
   Ammonia aayi --> .-------------.   --> gas jar mein kuch nahi aata
                    : conc. H₂SO₄ :
                    :  (wash bottle)
                    :_____________:
     bottle mein ab (NH₄)₂SO₄ ban gaya, gas khatam
```

> **Figure 2.7** – Galat tareeka: ammonia wash bottle ki conc. H₂SO₄ se reaction karke namak ban gayi, isliye bottle ke doosre taraf kuch gas nahi nikli.

Equation: 2NH₃ + H₂SO₄ → (NH₄)₂SO₄. Gas **sookhi** nahi hui, **gayab** ho gayi. Anhydrous CaCl₂ ke saath bhi yahi dikkat hai (ye ammonia ko jakad leta hai).

**Demo 2: paani ke upar jama karna.** Maan lo ek 1 L ki jar paani ke upar ulti rakhi hai aur usme ammonia bhari ja rahi hai. 1 volume paani mein lagbhag 700 volume NH₃ ghul sakti hai, to trough ka thoda sa paani bhi jar mein aane wali saari gas ko turant kha jaata hai. Jar ke andar dabaav gir jaata hai aur **paani jar mein chadh jaata hai** (fountain jaisa). Gas jama hi nahi hoti.

| Gas | Sookhane ke liye | Bharne ke liye |
|---|---|---|
| HCl (Topic 1) | conc. H₂SO₄ | upward displacement of air |
| NH₃ (aaj) | quicklime CaO | downward displacement of air |

**Niyam:** gas ki **prakriti** (acidic ya basic) dekho aur uske hisaab se drier chuno. Aur gas ki **ghulansheelta** dekho: bahut ghulansheel gas paani ke upar jama nahi hoti.

## Quick Recap

- Lab preparation: 2NH₄Cl + Ca(OH)₂ → CaCl₂ + 2H₂O + 2NH₃, sookha mishran, tube ka mooh halka neeche.
- Sookhane ke liye **CaO**, conc. H₂SO₄ ya CaCl₂ nahi: ye ammonia ko kha jaate hain.
- Ammonia hawa se halki (V.D. 8.5 vs lagbhag 14.5): **downward displacement of air**, ulta jar, tube upar tak. Paani ke upar nahi, kyunki bahut ghulansheel.
- NH₃ + H₂O ⇌ NH₄OH, kamzor base: moist red litmus neela.
- Fountain experiment dikhata hai ki NH₃ bahut ghulansheel aur ghol alkaline hai.
- Reactions: HCl se safed dhuan, oxygen mein jalna, Pt par 4NH₃ + 5O₂ → 4NO + 6H₂O, chlorine se NH₄Cl aur N₂.
- Haber: N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃, iron catalyst, lagbhag 200 atm, lagbhag 450 °C, shartein samjhauta hain, bina badli gas recycle hoti hai.
- Upyog: khaad (ammonium sulphate, ammonium nitrate, urea), nitric acid, refrigeration, safai ke ghol.
- Ammonia zehreeli hai: sab kuch sirf padhne ke liye ya teacher demo.

## Practice Questions

(Mass: H 1, C 12, N 14, O 16, S 32, Cl 35.5, Ca 40. Molar volume 22.4 L at STP.)

1. A teacher heats 42.8 g of NH₄Cl with enough Ca(OH)₂. Find the mass of Ca(OH)₂ used, the mass of CaCl₂ formed, and the volume of NH₃ at STP.
2. What mass of NH₄Cl must be heated with excess Ca(OH)₂ to obtain 6.72 L of ammonia at STP?
3. Explain why the test tube used to prepare ammonia from NH₄Cl and Ca(OH)₂ is tilted with its mouth slightly downward.
4. Ammonia is passed through each of (a) conc. H₂SO₄, (b) quicklime. For each, say whether ammonia is dried and write any equation.
5. How many kg of urea contain 70 kg of nitrogen? (Use the formula CO(NH₂)₂.)
6. A farmer needs the same amount of nitrogen as is present in 120 kg of urea, but wants to use NH₄NO₃ instead. How many kg of NH₄NO₃ is needed?
7. How many kg of NH₃ and of H₂SO₄ are needed to make 33 kg of (NH₄)₂SO₄ by 2NH₃ + H₂SO₄ → (NH₄)₂SO₄?
8. A bag of fertiliser is labelled "N 46 percent". A field needs 23 kg of nitrogen. How many kg of that fertiliser is needed?
9. 3.4 g of NH₃ reacts completely with HCl gas to form white fumes. Find the mass of HCl used and the mass of NH₄Cl formed.
10. 20.4 g of NH₃ burns completely in oxygen as 4NH₃ + 3O₂ → 2N₂ + 6H₂O. Find the volume of oxygen used at STP.
11. In a Haber plant, 51 kg of NH₃ is made by N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ (ideal case). Find the masses of N₂ and H₂ used. Then explain why plants run at about 450 °C and not at 100 °C, given that "a lower temperature gives a larger share of ammonia in the mixture but the reaction becomes very slow".
12. Choose and justify: which gas jar position (upright or inverted) and which method collects ammonia, and why can it not be collected over water? What would be seen if a student tried?
13. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) Ammonia is dried with conc. H₂SO₄. (b) NH₃ is lighter than air. (c) A catalyst increases the amount of ammonia in the Haber mixture. (d) Dry NH₃ turns dry red litmus blue. (e) NH₄Cl, (NH₄)₂SO₄ and NH₄NO₃ are all made from ammonia.

### Answers

1. NH₄Cl = 53.5 g/mol, 42.8 ÷ 53.5 = **0.8 mol**. Ratio 2 : 1 : 1 : 2: Ca(OH)₂ = 0.4 mol × 74 = **29.6 g**; CaCl₂ = 0.4 × 111 = **44.4 g**; NH₃ = 0.8 mol, 0.8 × 22.4 = **17.92 L**. Jaanch: H₂O = 0.8 mol = 14.4 g, NH₃ = 0.8 × 17 = 13.6 g; reactants 42.8 + 29.6 = 72.4; products 44.4 + 14.4 + 13.6 = 72.4 ✅.
2. 6.72 ÷ 22.4 = **0.3 mol** NH₃. Ratio 1 : 1 to NH₄Cl, to 0.3 mol = 0.3 × 53.5 = **16.05 g**. Jaanch: 16.05 ÷ 53.5 = 0.3 ✅.
3. Reaction mein paani banta hai jo test tube ke thande mooh par boond bankar wapas garam tale ki taraf behne ki koshish karta hai. Mooh neeche hone se wo bahar nikal jaata hai aur garam kaanch tootta nahi.
4. (a) **Nahi**: 2NH₃ + H₂SO₄ → (NH₄)₂SO₄, gas namak ban jaati hai. (b) **Haan**: CaO paani kheenchta hai, aur ammonia se reaction nahi karta (CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂). Jaanch: N 2 = 2, H 8 = 8, S 1, O 4 ✅.
5. Urea ka M = 12 + 16 + 2 × (14 + 2) = 12 + 16 + 32 = **60**, nitrogen 28. 70 ÷ 28 × 60 = **150 kg**. Jaanch: 150 kg urea mein N = 150 × 28 ÷ 60 = 70 ✅.
6. 120 kg urea mein N = 120 × 28 ÷ 60 = **56 kg**. NH₄NO₃ (M 80, N 28): 56 ÷ 28 × 80 = **160 kg**. Jaanch: 160 × 28 ÷ 80 = 56 ✅.
7. (NH₄)₂SO₄ ka M 132; 33 ÷ 132 = **0.25 kmol**. NH₃ = 0.5 kmol × 17 = **8.5 kg**; H₂SO₄ = 0.25 × 98 = **24.5 kg**. Jaanch: 8.5 + 24.5 = 33 ✅.
8. 23 ÷ 0.46 = **50 kg**. Jaanch: 50 × 0.46 = 23 ✅.
9. 3.4 ÷ 17 = **0.2 mol**. NH₃ + HCl → NH₄Cl (1 : 1 : 1). HCl = 0.2 × 36.5 = **7.3 g**; NH₄Cl = 0.2 × 53.5 = **10.7 g**. Jaanch: 3.4 + 7.3 = 10.7 ✅.
10. 20.4 ÷ 17 = 1.2 mol NH₃. O₂ = 1.2 × 3 ÷ 4 = 0.9 mol, volume = 0.9 × 22.4 = **20.16 L**. Jaanch: O₂ = 0.9 × 32 = 28.8 g; N₂ = 0.6 mol = 16.8 g; H₂O = 1.8 mol = 32.4 g; 20.4 + 28.8 = 49.2 = 16.8 + 32.4 ✅.
11. 51 ÷ 17 = **3 kmol** NH₃. N₂ = 1.5 kmol × 28 = **42 kg**; H₂ = 4.5 kmol × 2 = **9 kg**. Jaanch: 42 + 9 = 51 ✅. 100 °C par reaction bahut dheemi, isliye factory ke liye bekaar; garam karne par tezi aati hai par ammonia ka hissa ghatta hai: isliye samjhauta, lagbhag 450 °C aur iron catalyst.
12. **Ulta jar** (mooh neeche), tube **upar tak**, kyunki ammonia hawa se halki hai: downward displacement of air. Paani ke upar nahi, kyunki 1 volume paani mein lagbhag 700 volume NH₃ ghulti hai: gas jama nahi hogi, aur paani jar mein tezi se chadh jaayega.
13. (a) **False** ❌: conc. H₂SO₄ ammonia ko namak bana deta hai; CaO use karte hain. (b) **True** ✅. (c) **False** ❌: catalyst sirf speed badhata hai. (d) **False** ❌: sookhi NH₃ sookhe litmus par asar nahi karti; moist red litmus neela hota hai. (e) **True** ✅: NH₃ + HCl → NH₄Cl, 2NH₃ + H₂SO₄ → (NH₄)₂SO₄, NH₃ + HNO₃ → NH₄NO₃.
