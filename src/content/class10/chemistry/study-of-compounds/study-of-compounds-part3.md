# Topic 3: Nitric Acid: Ostwald Se Aqua Regia Tak

Topic 1 mein humne HCl gas ko NaCl aur concentrated H₂SO₄ se bante dekha, aur Topic 2 mein ammonia ko, jo Haber process se kaarkhaanon mein banti hai. Topic 2 ke uses mein ek line thi: ammonia se **nitric acid** bhi banta hai. Aaj usi acid ki kahani hai: wo lab mein kaise banta hai, kaarkhaane mein ammonia se kaise banta hai (Ostwald process), wo dhatuon ke saath "ulta" kyun chalta hai, aur wo kaun sa jodi hai jo gold ko bhi gala deta hai.

---

## Sunaar ki dukaan aur "raja ka ghol"

Mr. Kanitkar apne bhatije Nihar ko saath lekar sunaar ki dukaan par gaye. Nihar ki dadi ki ek purani anguthi thi: dhundhli, kaali-si pad gayi thi. Sunaar ne use roshni mein ghumaya, kasauti par halka sa ghisa, aur muskura kar bola, "Gold hi hai, par saath mein thoda taamba bhi mila hai. Saaf karni padegi."

Dukaan ke peeche ek chhota kamra tha, jiska darwaza band tha aur ek bada pankha bahar hawa phenk raha tha. Nihar ne khidki ke shishe se jhaanka: ek kaanch ke cupboard ke andar halki **bhoori-narangi dhundh** ghoom rahi thi. Sunaar ne dhyan se darwaza aur bhi band kiya. "Beta, wahan mat aao. Wahan mere tezaab rehte hain. Gold ko **sirf ek khaas ghol gala sakta hai: raja ka ghol**. Wo main bachchon ko kabhi nahi dikhata, aur khud bhi hawadaar cupboard aur dastaane ke bina nahi chhoota."

Nihar ki aankhen phail gayin. "Chacha, gold to sabse shaant dhatu hai, na usme jang lagta hai na paani usse kuch karta hai. Aur ye 'raja ka ghol' kya hai? Wo bhoori dhundh kya hai? Aur ye sab tezaab aate kahan se hain?"

Mr. Kanitkar ne ghar lautte hue kaha, "Teen sawaal hain, aur teeno ka jawaab ek hi acid ke paas hai: **nitric acid**. Chal, ek-ek karke ginte hain."

```
   Nihar ke teen sawaal                      Kahan milega jawaab

   1. Ye acid aata kahan se hai?       -->   Part A (lab) aur Part B (Ostwald)
   2. Bhoori dhundh kya hai?           -->   Part C (NO₂, oxidising acid)
   3. Raja ka ghol gold ko kaise gala? -->   Part E (aqua regia)
```

## Suraksha (yahin padh lo, har paragraph se pehle)

- **Concentrated nitric acid** chamdi ko jalata hai aur use **peela** kar deta hai. Aankh, naak aur gale ko nuksaan.
- **NO₂ (bhoori gas) aur NO** zehreeli hain: phephdon ko nuksaan, jo kabhi kabhi der se dikhta hai.
- Is poore topic ke saare reactions **sirf padhne ke liye** ya **sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo)** hain: fume cupboard, goggles, gloves, aur teacher. Koi student inhein banata, chhoota, sunghta ya test nahi karta.
- Concentrated H₂SO₄ bhi yahan aayega: wo bhi jalane wala hai (uska poora topic aage Topic 5 mein).
- Ghar mein koi aisi botal mile jis par khatre ka chinh ho to **bachche kabhi na chhuen**: bade se poochho aur label sirf bade ke saath padho.

## Part A: Lab mein nitric acid kaise banta hai

Topic 1 ki ek yaad: NaCl par concentrated H₂SO₄ daalo, aur HCl gas ban jaati hai, kyunki H₂SO₄ ek **bhaari, der se udne wala** acid hai jo halke acid ko uske namak se nikaal deta hai. Wahi chaal yahan: **nitrate ka namak** (KNO₃ ya NaNO₃) aur concentrated H₂SO₄:

KNO₃ + H₂SO₄ (conc.) → KHSO₄ + HNO₃

NaNO₃ ke liye bhi wahi: NaNO₃ + H₂SO₄ → NaHSO₄ + HNO₃. Garmi **200 °C se neeche** (lagbhag) aur halki rakhi jaati hai.

**Kyun halki garmi?** Nitric acid garmi aur roshni mein toot jaata hai:

4HNO₃ → 4NO₂ + 2H₂O + O₂

Is wajah se acid mein thodi NO₂ ghuli rehti hai aur acid **peela-bhoora** ho jaata hai. Isliye use **kaali (dark) botal** mein rakhte hain. Ye hi Nihar ki bhoori dhundh ka pehla suraag hai.

**Kyun poora kaanch?** Nitric acid **cork aur rubber ko khaa jaata hai**. Isliye retort aur receiver dono poore kaanch ke, aur jod bhi kaanch ke (ground-glass joint). Aur receiver ke mooh mein retort ki neck **dheeli** rehti hai, bandh nahi, taaki garam hota hua apparatus band na ho.

```
   Teacher demo (fume cupboard ke andar, sab kuch kaanch ka)

                  retort
          ___________
        /             \
       :   KNO₃ +       \_____
       :   conc. H₂SO₄         \______
        \             /               \______  <-- neck thodi neeche jhuki
         \___________/                       \_____
               ^                                    \
               :                                     v   (dheeli, bandh nahi)
          halki aanch                          :              :
          (lagbhag 200 °C se neeche)           :   HNO₃       :   <-- receiver flask
                                               :   (boondein) :       (thande paani mein)
                                               :______________:
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Nitric acid ki lab preparation (teacher demo). Poore kaanch ka retort halki aanch par, jisme KNO₃ aur concentrated H₂SO₄ hain. Bhaap neck se receiver mein jaati hai aur thande bartan mein boondon ki tarah jama hoti hai. Retort ki neck receiver ke mooh mein **dheeli** hai (koi cork nahi, koi band apparatus nahi); poora set fume cupboard ke andar hai.

### Example 1. KNO₃ se kitna HNO₃?

**Sawaal:** 20.2 g KNO₃ par concentrated H₂SO₄ ki zaroori matra daali gayi. Kitna HNO₃ aur kitna KHSO₄ banega? (K 39, N 14, O 16, H 1, S 32.)

**Kadam 1:** KNO₃ = 39 + 14 + 48 = **101** g/mol. HNO₃ = 1 + 14 + 48 = **63**. KHSO₄ = 39 + 1 + 32 + 64 = **136**. H₂SO₄ = **98**.
**Kadam 2:** moles KNO₃ = 20.2 ÷ 101 = **0.2 mol**. Ratio 1 : 1 : 1 : 1.
**Kadam 3:** HNO₃ = 0.2 × 63 = **12.6 g**. KHSO₄ = 0.2 × 136 = **27.2 g**.

**Jaanch:** H₂SO₄ chahiye 0.2 × 98 = 19.6 g. Pehle: 20.2 + 19.6 = 39.8 g. Baad mein: 12.6 + 27.2 = 39.8 g ✅. Atoms: K 1, H 2, N 1, S 1, O 3 + 4 = 7 = 4 + 3 ✅.

## Part B: Kaarkhaane ka raasta: Ostwald process

Kaarkhaane mein nitric acid **ammonia se** banta hai: wahi ammonia jo Topic 2 mein Haber process se mili thi, aur saath mein **hawa** (jisme oxygen hai). Teen kadam:

**Kadam 1: ammonia ka jalna (catalyst par).** Ammonia aur hawa ki oxygen ko **Pt-Rh gauze** (platinum-rhodium ki jaali) par lagbhag **800 °C** par guzaarte hain:

4NH₃ + 5O₂ → 4NO + 6H₂O

**Kadam 2: NO ka NO₂ banna.** Bina rang ki NO thandi hone par hawa ki oxygen se mil kar bhoori gas ban jaati hai:

2NO + O₂ → 2NO₂

**Kadam 3: NO₂ ka paani mein ghulna.** Oxygen ki maujoodgi mein NO₂ paani mein ghulkar nitric acid deti hai:

4NO₂ + O₂ + 2H₂O → 4HNO₃

Ye teesra kadam **wahi hai jo baarish ki boondon mein hota hai**: Class 9 ke acid rain mein hamne yahi equation dekhi thi. Farak sirf itna ki kaarkhaana ise niyantran mein karta hai aur usse kaam ka acid banata hai. Catalyst (gauze) sirf reaction ko **tez** karta hai, khud nahi badalta.

```
   NH₃ (Haber process se, Topic 2)  +  hawa (O₂)
                    :
                    v
        garm Pt-Rh gauze, lagbhag 800 °C
        4NH₃ + 5O₂ → 4NO + 6H₂O
                    :
                    v
        thanda karo, hawa ki O₂ milao
        2NO + O₂ → 2NO₂   (bhoori gas)
                    :
                    v
        O₂ aur paani mein absorb karo
        4NO₂ + O₂ + 2H₂O → 4HNO₃
                    :
                    v
             nitric acid (aage gaadha kiya jaata hai)
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Ostwald process ka flow chart: ammonia aur hawa pehle garm gauze par NO dete hain, phir NO₂, aur akhir mein paani aur oxygen ke saath HNO₃.

**Teeno kadam jodne par:** NH₃ + 2O₂ → HNO₃ + H₂O (har NH₃ se ek HNO₃: nitrogen ke sab atom aage chalte hain).

### Example 2. 1.7 kg ammonia se kitna acid?

**Sawaal:** 1.7 kg NH₃ ko poori tarah nitric acid mein badla gaya. Kitna HNO₃, kitni O₂ lagi, aur kitna paani net bana?

**Kadam 1:** NH₃ = 17 g/mol. 1700 g ÷ 17 = **100 mol**.
**Kadam 2:** overall NH₃ + 2O₂ → HNO₃ + H₂O, ratio 1 : 2 : 1 : 1.
**Kadam 3:** HNO₃ = 100 × 63 = **6300 g = 6.3 kg**. O₂ = 200 × 32 = **6400 g = 6.4 kg**. H₂O = 100 × 18 = **1800 g = 1.8 kg**.

**Jaanch:** pehle 1.7 + 6.4 = 8.1 kg. Baad mein 6.3 + 1.8 = 8.1 kg ✅. Atoms: N 1, H 3 = 1 + 2, O 4 = 3 + 1 ✅.

## Part C: Nitric acid ka sabse alag swabhav: wo oxidise karta hai

Nitric acid ek **strong acid** hai (Chapter 1: H⁺ aur NO₃⁻ mein poora toot jaata hai). Par ye sirf acid nahi, ek **bahut zabardast oxidising agent** bhi hai. Isi wajah se Class 9 mein hydrogen ke prayog mein ise **nahi** liya gaya tha, aur Chapter 2 (Topic 3) mein Cu + conc. HNO₃ se bhoori NO₂ dekh kar iski pehli jhalak mili thi. Yahan poori tasveer: nitric acid dhatu se hydrogen banne hi nahi deta, ya bani hui hydrogen ko turant paani mein badal deta hai. Dhatu **Cu²⁺ jaise ion** ban jaati hai aur acid ke nitrogen se **nitrogen ke oxide** nikalte hain.

### Taambe ke saath

**Concentrated HNO₃ + Cu (teacher demo, sirf dekhne ke liye):**

Cu + 4HNO₃ (conc.) → Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2NO₂ + 2H₂O

**bhoori gas NO₂** nikalti hai aur ghol hara-neela ho jaata hai. Yahi bhoori dhundh Nihar ne sunaar ke cupboard mein dekhi.

**Dilute HNO₃ + Cu (teacher demo, sirf dekhne ke liye):**

3Cu + 8HNO₃ (dil.) → 3Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2NO + 4H₂O

Yahan gas **NO** hai, jo **bina rang ki** hai. Par test tube ke mooh par hawa mili aur **2NO + O₂ → 2NO₂**: bhoori ban gayi. Isliye dilute mein bhi bhoori dhundh dikhti hai, par peechhe se.

```
   Conc. HNO₃ + Cu                       Dilute HNO₃ + Cu

      ~ ~ ~  bhoori NO₂ upar                 ~ ~  mooh par bhoori
       :          :                           :          :
       :  hara-neela ghol  :                  :  neela-hara ghol :
       : (Cu tukde neeche) :                  : (bina rang ki NO :
       :__________________:                   :  andar se uthti) :
                                              :__________________:
```

> **Figure 3.4** – Teacher demo (fume cupboard mein, test tube khula): concentrated acid mein shuru se bhoori NO₂; dilute acid mein andar bina rang ki NO, jo test tube ke mooh par hawa ki oxygen se bhoori ho jaati hai.

**Zinc?** Zinc ke saath bhi nitric acid hydrogen nahi deta; dilute ya concentrated dono mein nitrogen ke oxide milte hain. Iske equation hum sirf **naam** se jaante hain, aage ki class mein.

### Example 3. Concentrated acid aur 6.35 g copper

**Sawaal:** 6.35 g Cu concentrated HNO₃ se poori tarah react karta hai. Kitna HNO₃ lagega, kitni NO₂ (STP par volume) aur kitna Cu(NO₃)₂ banega? (Cu 63.5; STP par 1 mol gas = 22.4 L.)

**Kadam 1:** moles Cu = 6.35 ÷ 63.5 = **0.1 mol**.
**Kadam 2:** Cu : HNO₃ : Cu(NO₃)₂ : NO₂ : H₂O = 1 : 4 : 1 : 2 : 2.
**Kadam 3:** HNO₃ = 0.4 mol × 63 = **25.2 g**. NO₂ = 0.2 mol: volume = 0.2 × 22.4 = **4.48 L**, mass = 0.2 × 46 = 9.2 g. Cu(NO₃)₂ = 0.1 × 187.5 = **18.75 g**. H₂O = 0.2 × 18 = 3.6 g.

**Jaanch:** pehle 6.35 + 25.2 = 31.55 g. Baad mein 18.75 + 9.2 + 3.6 = 31.55 g ✅.

### Example 4. Dilute acid aur 19.05 g copper

**Sawaal:** 19.05 g Cu dilute HNO₃ se poori tarah react karta hai. Kitna HNO₃ lagega aur kitni NO (STP par) banegi?

**Kadam 1:** moles Cu = 19.05 ÷ 63.5 = **0.3 mol**.
**Kadam 2:** 3Cu : 8HNO₃ : 2NO, to 0.3 mol Cu ke liye HNO₃ = 0.8 mol, NO = 0.2 mol.
**Kadam 3:** HNO₃ = 0.8 × 63 = **50.4 g**. NO = 0.2 × 22.4 = **4.48 L** (mass 0.2 × 30 = 6.0 g).

**Jaanch:** Cu(NO₃)₂ = 0.3 × 187.5 = 56.25 g, H₂O = 0.4 × 18 = 7.2 g. Pehle 19.05 + 50.4 = 69.45 g. Baad mein 56.25 + 6.0 + 7.2 = 69.45 g ✅.

### Gair-dhatuon ke saath (sirf padhne ke liye)

Concentrated HNO₃ garam karne par gair-dhatuon ko bhi oxidise karta hai:

C + 4HNO₃ (conc.) → CO₂ + 4NO₂ + 2H₂O
S + 6HNO₃ (conc.) → H₂SO₄ + 6NO₂ + 2H₂O

Dono mein bhoori NO₂. Carbon CO₂ ban jaata hai, sulphur sulphuric acid ban jaata hai (Chapter 8 ke acidified BaCl₂ se sulphate pakda ja sakta hai).

### Example 5. 1.2 g carbon

**Sawaal:** 1.2 g carbon concentrated HNO₃ se poori tarah react karta hai. Kitna HNO₃ lagta hai aur kitni gas (STP par) milti hai?

**Kadam 1:** moles C = 1.2 ÷ 12 = **0.1 mol**.
**Kadam 2:** C : HNO₃ : CO₂ : NO₂ = 1 : 4 : 1 : 4.
**Kadam 3:** HNO₃ = 0.4 × 63 = **25.2 g**. CO₂ = 0.1 mol = **2.24 L**, NO₂ = 0.4 mol = **8.96 L**, kul gas 0.5 mol = **11.2 L**.

**Jaanch:** H₂O = 0.2 mol = 3.6 g. Pehle 1.2 + 25.2 = 26.4 g. Baad mein CO₂ 4.4 + NO₂ 18.4 + H₂O 3.6 = 26.4 g ✅.

| Padarth | Conc. HNO₃ se kya milta hai | Dikhne wali baat |
|---|---|---|
| Cu | Cu(NO₃)₂, NO₂, H₂O | bhoori gas, hara-neela ghol |
| C | CO₂, NO₂, H₂O | bhoori gas |
| S | H₂SO₄, NO₂, H₂O | bhoori gas |

## Part D: Fe aur Al "passive" ho jaate hain

Concentrated HNO₃ ke saath **iron aur aluminium** ek ajeeb baat dikhate hain: pehle thodi si harkat, phir **reaction ruk jaata hai**. Wajah: concentrated nitric acid in dhatuon ki satah par ek **patli, kasi hui oxide parat** bana deta hai, jo aluminium par Chapter 2 ki Al₂O₃ parat jaisi hi hai. Parat acid aur dhatu ke beech deewar ban jaati hai. Isse dhatu **passive** kehlaati hai. Is baat ka naya hissa ye hai ki **concentrated nitric acid khud** ye parat bana deta hai (Chapter 2 mein sirf hawa ki oxygen se bani parat thi).

Isi wajah se kaarkhaane mein concentrated nitric acid aluminium ke tanker mein le jaaya jaata hai. (Taambe ka tank nahi chalega: taambe ke saath to wo seedha react karta hai, Part C.)

## Part E: Aqua regia: raja ka ghol

Gold aur platinum par **akela nitric acid bhi** kuch nahi karta, aur **akela HCl bhi nahi**. Par jab **concentrated HCl ke 3 volume aur concentrated HNO₃ ke 1 volume** taaza milaye jaate hain, to ye mix **aqua regia** kehlata hai, jo gold aur platinum ko gala deta hai. Ye nuskha aur iski "kyun" hum Chapter 2 (Topic 3) mein padh chuke hain; yahan sirf ek chhoti yaad.

HNO₃ ek **oxidising acid** hai, to wo gold ke atoms ko ion banne par majboor karta hai. Par akela wo ek **taala todne wala** hai jo saaman le jaane wala saath nahi laaya. HCl ke **chloride ions** wo "porter" hain jo in ions ko pakad kar ghol mein le jaate hain. Dono saath hon to gold ghulta hai.

```
   Gold ka tukda ke saath:

   akela conc. HNO₃    -->  koi asar nahi
   akela conc. HCl     -->  koi asar nahi
   3 : 1 mix (HCl : HNO₃)  -->  peela ghol: gold ghul gaya
```

> **Figure 3.5** – Gold ke saath teen halaat (sirf describe, koi student nahi karta): akela HNO₃ kuch nahi, akela HCl kuch nahi, par 3 : 1 ka mix gold ko ghula deta hai.

Sunaar anguthi mein jo **taamba** mila hota hai, wo to akele nitric acid se bhi ghul jaata hai (Part C), aur gold ko aqua regia se alag kiya jaata hai. Ye sab sirf **trained logon** ka kaam hai: aqua regia ko **taaza** banate hain aur **band botal mein kabhi nahi** rakhte, kyunki isme zehreeli gas banti rehti hai aur botal phat sakti hai.

### Example 6. 40 mL aqua regia

**Sawaal:** 40 mL aqua regia ke liye concentrated HCl aur HNO₃ ke volume batao.

3 : 1 ka matlab 4 hisse. 40 ÷ 4 = 10 mL ek hissa. HCl = 3 × 10 = **30 mL**, HNO₃ = **10 mL**.

**Jaanch:** 30 + 10 = 40 mL ✅, aur 30 ÷ 10 = 3 ✅.

## Part F: Chhoti baatein aur uses

- **Peela nishan:** concentrated nitric acid chamdi ke protein se react karke use **peela** kar deta hai (khaas rang ki wajah se xanthoproteic reaction kehte hain). Isse chhoona kabhi nahi.
- **Fertiliser:** nitric acid aur ammonia: NH₃ + HNO₃ → NH₄NO₃ (ammonium nitrate). 63 g HNO₃ aur 17 g NH₃ se 80 g NH₄NO₃. Yani Example 2 ke 6.3 kg HNO₃ (100 mol) ko NH₄NO₃ banane ke liye 100 mol = 1.7 kg ammonia aur lagegi, aur 8.0 kg NH₄NO₃ milega.
- **Aur uses:** explosive, rang (dyes), dhatu par naqqashi (etching), dhatu ki safai, aur rocket ke fuel ka oxidiser (ek line).
- **Pradushan:** NO aur NO₂ zehreeli hain, aur NOx hawa mein jaakar **acid rain** banate hain (Class 9). Isliye kaarkhaane ki chimney ki gas saaf karni padti hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti: "Nitric acid bhi baaki acids ki tarah dhatu se hydrogen deta hai."**
Ye sach lagta hai, kyunki Class 9 mein Zn + dilute acid se hydrogen mili thi aur sab acid mein H likha hai. Chapter 2 mein concentrated acid ke liye ye galti pakdi thi; yahan dilute acid aur zinc ke saath bhi dekhte hain. Teen kadam mein dekho:

1. **Class 9 yaad karo.** Zinc par halke H₂SO₄ ya HCl: H₂ gas, aur teacher ki "pop" test. Wahan nitric acid isliye **nahi** liya gaya tha (hydrogen-part2).
2. **Ab atoms ginte hain.** Cu + 4HNO₃ → Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2NO₂ + 2H₂O mein bayen taraf 4 H hain. Dayen taraf: Cu(NO₃)₂ mein 0 H, NO₂ mein 0 H, 2H₂O mein **4 H**. Saare hydrogen **paani** mein chale gaye, **H₂ gas nahi bani**.
3. **Dilute mein bhi wahi.** 3Cu + 8HNO₃ → 3Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2NO + 4H₂O mein 8 H bayen, 4H₂O mein 8 H dayen. H₂ phir bhi nahi.

```
   4HNO₃ ke 4 H atoms           Cu + conc. HNO₃ ke baad

   H H H H   --------------->   2 H₂O mein : H H   H H
                                H₂ gas mein : koi nahi
```

> **Figure 3.6** – Hydrogen ka hisaab: 4HNO₃ ke saare 4 hydrogen atoms 2 paani ke molecules mein chale jaate hain; H₂ gas mein ek bhi nahi.

| Acid + Zn | Gas | Pop test |
|---|---|---|
| dilute H₂SO₄ ya HCl | H₂ | haan |
| HNO₃ (dilute ya conc.) | nitrogen ke oxide (NO ya NO₂), H₂ nahi | nahi |

**Niyam:** dhatu + nitric acid = namak + nitrogen ka oxide + paani. Hydrogen dhundhna galti hai.

## Quick Recap

- Lab: KNO₃ (ya NaNO₃) + conc. H₂SO₄ → KHSO₄ + HNO₃, 200 °C se neeche, **poore kaanch ke retort** mein (cork aur rubber ko acid khaata hai). HNO₃ distil hokar receiver mein aata hai.
- Peela-bhoora rang: 4HNO₃ → 4NO₂ + 2H₂O + O₂ (garmi, roshni): isliye kaali botal.
- Ostwald: 4NH₃ + 5O₂ → 4NO + 6H₂O (Pt-Rh gauze, lagbhag 800 °C); 2NO + O₂ → 2NO₂; 4NO₂ + O₂ + 2H₂O → 4HNO₃. Overall NH₃ + 2O₂ → HNO₃ + H₂O.
- Strong acid aur **oxidising agent**; dhatu se **hydrogen nahi** deta.
- Cu + 4HNO₃ (conc.) → Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2NO₂ + 2H₂O; 3Cu + 8HNO₃ (dil.) → 3Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2NO + 4H₂O; NO hawa mein bhoori ho jaati hai.
- C + 4HNO₃ → CO₂ + 4NO₂ + 2H₂O; S + 6HNO₃ → H₂SO₄ + 6NO₂ + 2H₂O.
- Fe aur Al conc. HNO₃ se passive ho jaate hain.
- Aqua regia: 3 volume conc. HCl + 1 volume conc. HNO₃, taaza; gold aur platinum ghulata hai.
- Uses: fertiliser (NH₄NO₃), explosive, dyes, etching, safai, rocket oxidiser. Chamdi par peela nishan. NO, NO₂ zehreeli, acid rain.
- Sab kuch sirf padhne ya teacher demo ke liye.

## Practice Questions

(Mass: H 1, C 12, N 14, O 16, Na 23, S 32, Cu 63.5. STP par 1 mol gas = 22.4 L.)

1. 12.75 g of sodium nitrate, NaNO₃, is warmed with concentrated sulphuric acid in a glass retort (NaNO₃ + H₂SO₄ → NaHSO₄ + HNO₃). Find the mass of HNO₃ formed, the mass of NaHSO₄ formed, and the mass of H₂SO₄ used. Check the conservation of mass.
2. Why is the nitric acid preparation done in an all-glass retort, and why is the heating kept gentle? Why is the acid stored in a dark bottle?
3. In the second step of the Ostwald process, 2NO + O₂ → 2NO₂, 5.6 L of NO at STP reacts completely. Find the volume of O₂ needed and the volume of NO₂ formed at STP.
4. 5.1 kg of ammonia is completely converted into nitric acid by the Ostwald process (overall NH₃ + 2O₂ → HNO₃ + H₂O). Find the mass of HNO₃, the mass of oxygen used and the mass of water formed.
5. 15.875 g of copper reacts completely with concentrated nitric acid (Cu + 4HNO₃ → Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2NO₂ + 2H₂O). Find the moles of Cu, the mass of HNO₃ used and the volume of NO₂ at STP.
6. 7.62 g of copper reacts completely with dilute nitric acid (3Cu + 8HNO₃ → 3Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2NO + 4H₂O). Find the mass of HNO₃ used and the volume of NO at STP. What colour change is seen at the mouth of the test tube, and why?
7. 3.6 g of carbon reacts completely with concentrated nitric acid (C + 4HNO₃ → CO₂ + 4NO₂ + 2H₂O). Find the mass of HNO₃ used and the total volume of gases formed at STP.
8. 1.6 g of sulphur reacts completely with concentrated nitric acid (S + 6HNO₃ → H₂SO₄ + 6NO₂ + 2H₂O). Find the mass of H₂SO₄ formed and the volume of NO₂ at STP. Which Chapter 8 test would show the sulphate in the solution?
9. A student says: "Zinc is above hydrogen in the reactivity series, so zinc with dilute nitric acid must give hydrogen, as with dilute sulphuric acid." Using the fact that nitric acid is an oxidising acid, state whether she is right and what is formed instead.
10. Concentrated nitric acid is carried in aluminium tankers in industry, but never in copper tanks. Explain both facts.
11. A teacher prepares 180 mL of aqua regia. Find the volumes of concentrated HCl and concentrated HNO₃. A ring is an alloy of gold and copper. Which part dissolves in concentrated HNO₃ alone, and which part needs aqua regia?
12. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) A rubber stopper is fine for the nitric acid retort. (b) The catalyst of the first Ostwald step is a Pt-Rh gauze. (c) NO is brown. (d) Aqua regia is 3 volumes of HNO₃ to 1 of HCl. (e) Cu with dilute HNO₃ gives hydrogen.
13. Two solids X and Y are each warmed with concentrated nitric acid in a teacher demo. Both give brown fumes. The solution from X gives a white precipitate with acidified BaCl₂. The gas from Y, besides brown fumes, turns lime water milky. The solutions are colourless and the only candidates are Cu, C and S. Identify X and Y and write the equations.
14. Describe the safety precautions for a teacher demo of copper with concentrated nitric acid, and why a student can never do it. Which gas is poisonous and what does it do in the air outside?

### Answers

1. NaNO₃ = 23 + 14 + 48 = **85**. Moles = 12.75 ÷ 85 = **0.15 mol**. Ratio 1 : 1 : 1 : 1. HNO₃ = 0.15 × 63 = **9.45 g**. NaHSO₄ = 23 + 1 + 32 + 64 = 120, to 0.15 × 120 = **18 g**. H₂SO₄ = 0.15 × 98 = **14.7 g**. Jaanch: pehle 12.75 + 14.7 = 27.45 g, baad mein 9.45 + 18 = 27.45 g ✅.
2. Nitric acid **cork aur rubber ko khaa jaata hai**, isliye sab kaanch ka. Garmi halki isliye ki acid **garmi se toot jaata hai** (4HNO₃ → 4NO₂ + 2H₂O + O₂), aur roshni mein bhi, jisse peela-bhoora ho jaata hai: isliye kaali botal.
3. 5.6 L ÷ 22.4 = 0.25 mol NO. Ratio 2 : 1 : 2, to O₂ = 0.125 mol = **2.8 L**, NO₂ = 0.25 mol = **5.6 L**. Jaanch: N 2 = 2, O 2 + 2 = 4 = 4 ✅.
4. NH₃ = 5100 ÷ 17 = **300 mol**. HNO₃ = 300 × 63 = 18900 g = **18.9 kg**. O₂ = 600 × 32 = 19200 g = **19.2 kg**. H₂O = 300 × 18 = 5400 g = **5.4 kg**. Jaanch: 5.1 + 19.2 = 24.3 kg = 18.9 + 5.4 ✅.
5. Cu = 15.875 ÷ 63.5 = **0.25 mol**. HNO₃ = 4 × 0.25 = 1.0 mol = **63 g**. NO₂ = 2 × 0.25 = 0.5 mol = 0.5 × 22.4 = **11.2 L**. Jaanch: Cu(NO₃)₂ 0.25 × 187.5 = 46.875 g, NO₂ 23 g, H₂O 9 g; 46.875 + 23 + 9 = 78.875 g = 15.875 + 63 ✅.
6. Cu = 7.62 ÷ 63.5 = **0.12 mol**. HNO₃ = 0.12 × 8 ÷ 3 = 0.32 mol = **20.16 g**. NO = 0.12 × 2 ÷ 3 = 0.08 mol = 0.08 × 22.4 = **1.792 L**. Mooh par **bina rang ki NO bhoori NO₂ ban jaati hai** (2NO + O₂ → 2NO₂). Jaanch: Cu(NO₃)₂ 22.5 g, NO 2.4 g, H₂O 0.16 × 18 = 2.88 g, kul 27.78 g = 7.62 + 20.16 ✅.
7. C = 3.6 ÷ 12 = **0.3 mol**. HNO₃ = 4 × 0.3 = 1.2 mol = **75.6 g**. Gas: CO₂ 0.3 mol + NO₂ 1.2 mol = 1.5 mol = 1.5 × 22.4 = **33.6 L**. Jaanch: CO₂ 13.2 g + NO₂ 55.2 g + H₂O (0.6 mol) 10.8 g = 79.2 g = 3.6 + 75.6 ✅.
8. S = 1.6 ÷ 32 = **0.05 mol**. H₂SO₄ = 0.05 × 98 = **4.9 g**. NO₂ = 6 × 0.05 = 0.3 mol = 0.3 × 22.4 = **6.72 L**. Test: dilute HCl ke baad BaCl₂: safed dhodha BaSO₄ jo acid mein nahi ghulta. Jaanch: HNO₃ 0.3 × 63 = 18.9 g; 1.6 + 18.9 = 20.5 g; 4.9 + 13.8 + 1.8 = 20.5 g ✅.
9. **Galat.** Nitric acid oxidising acid hai: hydrogen banne ke bajaay zinc ke ion ban jaate hain aur **nitrogen ke oxide** nikalte hain (hydrogen nahi); saara hydrogen paani mein jaata hai.
10. Aluminium par concentrated HNO₃ ek **patli oxide parat** bana deta hai (passive), reaction ruk jaata hai, isliye tanker surakshit. **Copper** passive nahi hota: Cu + 4HNO₃ → Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2NO₂ + 2H₂O, tank ghul jaata aur zehreeli NO₂ nikalti.
11. 3 : 1 matlab 4 hisse. 180 ÷ 4 = 45 mL. HCl = **135 mL**, HNO₃ = **45 mL**. Jaanch: 135 + 45 = 180 ✅, 135 ÷ 45 = 3 ✅. **Copper** akele HNO₃ se ghul jaata hai; **gold** ke liye aqua regia chahiye.
12. (a) **False** ❌: HNO₃ rubber ko khaata hai, poora kaanch chahiye. (b) **True** ✅. (c) **False** ❌: NO bina rang ki hai, NO₂ bhoori. (d) **False** ❌: 3 volume HCl, 1 volume HNO₃. (e) **False** ❌: NO milti hai, hydrogen nahi.
13. **X = S**, **Y = C**. Cu ka ghol hara-neela hota (question mein colourless), aur sulphate (BaSO₄) sirf S se, milky lime water (CO₂) sirf C se. X: S + 6HNO₃ → H₂SO₄ + 6NO₂ + 2H₂O. Y: C + 4HNO₃ → CO₂ + 4NO₂ + 2H₂O. Jaanch: S 1, H 6, N 6, O 18 = 4 + 12 + 2 ✅; C 1, H 4, N 4, O 12 = 2 + 8 + 2 ✅.
14. Teacher fume cupboard mein, goggles aur gloves ke saath, bahut chhoti matra mein karta hai; test tube khula rehta hai; baaki log door. **NO₂** zehreeli hai (phephdon ko nuksaan). Bahar hawa mein NOx **acid rain** banate hain. Student ghar par nahi kar sakta kyunki na fume cupboard, na goggles, na teacher, aur acid chamdi ko jalata hai.
