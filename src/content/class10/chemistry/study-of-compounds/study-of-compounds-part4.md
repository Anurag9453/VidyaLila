# Topic 4: Sulphuric Acid Ban Kaise? Contact Process

Topic 1 mein HCl banane ke liye aur Topic 3 mein HNO₃ banane ke liye ek hi cheez baar-baar lagi: **concentrated sulphuric acid**. Ek acid dusre acid ko bana raha tha! Ab sawaal naturally uthta hai: ye sulphuric acid khud kahan se aata hai? Kisi khet mein ugta nahi, kisi khaan mein seedha milta nahi. Aaj hum uski factory mein jhaankenge: **Contact process**. Chapter 3 ki roasting aur Class 9 ki acid rain, dono ke dhaage yahin aakar jud jaayenge.

---

## Battery ki dukaan aur chimney ka dhuaan

Mr. Thosar apne bhanje Sarang ko saath lekar gaadi ki nayi battery lene ek garage ki dukaan par gaye. Sarang ne kone mein rakhi ek bhaari, kaali battery dekhi, jis par laal-peele rang mein likha tha: **"DANGER: ACID. Sirf bade (adults) chhuen."** Saath mein ek khopdi-aur-haddi wala hazard ka nishaan bhi tha.

"Mama, is dabbe ke andar acid hai?" Sarang ne poochha.

"Haan, battery ke andar **dilute sulphuric acid** hota hai, aur tabhi iske upar itna bada warning hai," Mr. Thosar ne kaha. "Isliye hum dukaan wale uncle ko hi ise uthane dete hain. Hum sirf label padh rahe hain, haath nahi laga rahe."

Wapas aate waqt unki gaadi ek factory ke paas se guzri. Aasmaan mein lambi chimney se halka-sa dhuaan uth raha tha aur hawa mein ek tez, chubhti-si gandh thi, jaise kisi ne maachis jalayi ho. Sarang ne naak par rumaal rakh liya. "Mama, ye kaisi gandh hai? Aur kya ye wahi chimney hai jiske baare mein kal TV par acid rain bata rahe the?"

Mr. Thosar ne gaadi ki khidki chadha di. "Bahut sahi pakda. Ye gandh **SO₂** ki ho sakti hai: sulphur jalne se bani gas. Aur socho: wahi SO₂ agar hawa mein chhoot jaaye to baarish mein sulphuric acid ban jaati hai (Class 9). Par agar **usi SO₂ ko ek factory pakad le aur bahut samajhdari se** sulphuric acid mein badal de, to wo battery, khaad aur kitne hi kaamon ka acid ban jaata hai. Ek hi gas: ek jagah pollution, doosri jagah kamaayi. Aaj ghar chalkar wohi samajhte hain ki factory ye kaam **sahi tareeke se** kaise karti hai."

## Suraksha (ek baar dhyan se padho)

Ye poora topic **sirf padhne ke liye** hai: ye factory ka kaam hai, ghar ya school ka nahi.

- **SO₂ aur SO₃ zehreeli gas** hain: aankh, naak, gale aur phephdon ko jalati hain. SO₃ nami mein tezaabi dhundh bana deti hai.
- **Concentrated sulphuric acid aur oleum** kapde, kaagaz aur skin ko turant jala dete hain. Ye factory mein sirf trained log, goggles, gloves aur band pipe ke saath sambhalte hain.
- Jahan school lab mein sulphur ko jalane ka demo hota hai, wo **sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo)** hai: fume cupboard, goggles, thodi si matra, aur students door khade rehte hain.
- Car battery ya toilet cleaner jaisi cheezon ke andar ka acid **bachche kabhi na chhuen**; label bade ke saath padho, dabba mat kholo.

## Part A: Kachcha maal aur teen kadam ka naksha

Sulphuric acid banane ke liye factory ko chaar saadi cheezein chahiye:

1. **Sulphur ka srot:** ya to khud **sulphur (S)**, ek peela powder, jo aaj ka bahut saara petroleum aur natural gas ki safai mein bachta hai (lagbhag), ya **sulphide ore** jaise iron pyrites **FeS₂** ko roast karke nikali hui SO₂ (Chapter 3 ki roasting). Dono se nateeja ek hi: **SO₂**.
2. **Hawa:** oxygen ka sabse sasta srot.
3. **Catalyst V₂O₅** (vanadium pentoxide).
4. **Paani aur concentrated H₂SO₄:** aakhri kadam ke liye (aage samjhenge).

Poori kahani **teen kadam** mein hai:

```
   Kadam 1:  sulphur ko hawa mein jalao        S + O₂ --> SO₂
   Kadam 2:  SO₂ ko catalyst par SO₃ banao     2SO₂ + O₂ <==> 2SO₃
   Kadam 3:  SO₃ ko H₂SO₄ mein ghulao          SO₃ + H₂SO₄ --> H₂S₂O₇ (oleum)
             phir oleum mein paani milao       H₂S₂O₇ + H₂O --> 2H₂SO₄

   Sulphur --> SO₂ --> SO₃ --> oleum --> sulphuric acid
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Contact process ka bada naksha: sulphur se SO₂, SO₂ se SO₃ (catalyst par), SO₃ se oleum, aur oleum se sulphuric acid. SO₃ ko seedha paani mein nahi, pehle concentrated H₂SO₄ mein ghulaya jaata hai (kyun: aage).

### Example 1. Teeno kadam ka atom-hisaab

**Sawaal:** Contact process ke chaaron equations mein har tatva ki ginti dono taraf barabar hai, ye jaancho.

**Kadam 1:** S + O₂ → SO₂.
**Kadam 2:** 2SO₂ + O₂ ⇌ 2SO₃.
**Kadam 3a:** SO₃ + H₂SO₄ → H₂S₂O₇.
**Kadam 3b:** H₂S₂O₇ + H₂O → 2H₂SO₄.

**Jaanch:** Pehla: S 1 = 1, O 2 = 2 ✅. Doosra: S 2 = 2, O (4 + 2) = 6 = 6 ✅. Teesra (a): S (1 + 1) = 2 = 2, O (3 + 4) = 7 = 7, H 2 = 2 ✅. Teesra (b): H (2 + 2) = 4 = 2 × 2, S 2 = 2, O (7 + 1) = 8 = 2 × 4 ✅. Kadam 3 mein ek H₂SO₄ pehle kharch hota hai aur 3b mein do milte hain, to **net** mein ek hi naya H₂SO₄ bana (Part D mein ginenge).

## Part B: Kadam 1: sulphur ko jalana aur gas ko saaf rakhna

Factory mein pighla hua sulphur ek **burner** mein hawa ki dhaar ke saath jalaya jaata hai. **Hawa pehle sukhayi jaati hai** (nami ke bina), kyunki aage chalkar paani ki boond catalyst aur pipe ko kharab kar sakti hai:

S + O₂ → SO₂

Class 9 mein tumne yahi equation dekhi thi, jab fuel mein bacha hua sulphur jalta hai aur chimney se SO₂ nikalti hai. Wahan wo bekaar jaati thi aur pollution banati thi. Yahan wahi gas **kachcha maal** hai.

**Teacher demo (sirf dekhne ke liye):** school lab mein teacher fume cupboard ke andar ek gas jar mein sulphur ki bahut thodi matra jalata hai. Wo **neeli lau** ke saath jalta hai, aur chubhti gandh wali SO₂ jar mein bharti hai (teacher mooh se nahi sunghta; students door se dekhte hain). Jar mein rakhi geeli neeli litmus ki patti **laal** ho jaati hai, kyunki SO₂ paani mein ghulkar khatta ghol banati hai. Ye sirf dekhne ke liye hai: SO₂ zehreeli hai.

SO₂ factory mein burner se nikalte hi **saaf** ki jaati hai: dhool aur nami hataye jaate hain, kyunki dhool aur nami catalyst ko kam kaam ka kar dete hain aur pipe ko jang lagate hain. Sukhane ke liye **concentrated H₂SO₄** hi kaam aata hai: wahi aage ka hero hai (Topic 5 mein uske swabhav ka poora kissa).

Agar sulphur ki jagah **FeS₂** ho, to wo bhatti mein roast hota hai (Chapter 3 mein aise roasting ke equations aaye the):

4FeS₂ + 11O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃ + 8SO₂

Dhyan do: **ek FeS₂ se do SO₂** bante hain (8 SO₂ ÷ 4 FeS₂ = 2). Aur peeche **Fe₂O₃** bachta hai, jise lohe ke kaam mein lagaya ja sakta hai.

**Jaanch:** Fe 4 = 4, S 8 = 8, O 22 = 6 + 16 = 22 ✅.

```
   Hawa --> [ sukhane wala tower ]            sulphur ---+
                  (H₂SO₄ se)                            |
                      :                                 v
                      +------------------------> [ BURNER ] --> SO₂ + bachi hawa
                                                                    :
                                                                    v
                                                    [ saaf karna: dhool, nami hataana ]
                                                                    :
                                                                    v
                                                          [ garam karne wala (heat exchanger) ]
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Contact process ka shuruaati hissa: sukhi hawa aur sulphur burner mein SO₂ banate hain; phir gas ki dhool aur nami hataayi jaati hai aur gas ko converter ke liye sahi garmi par laya jaata hai. (Aage ka hissa Figure 4.3 mein.)

## Part C: Kadam 2: converter, catalyst aur 450 °C ka samjhauta

Ab asli jaadu. SO₂ ko aur oxygen ke saath **V₂O₅ catalyst** ki parton par se guzaara jaata hai. Gas catalyst ke **sampark (contact)** mein aati hai, isi se is process ka naam pada:

2SO₂ + O₂ ⇌ 2SO₃

Do baatein dhyan rakhne ki hain:

1. **Ye reversible hai (⇌).** Class 9 mein Haber process ke saath ye vichar dekha tha: reaction aage bhi chalti hai aur peeche bhi, to saari SO₂ ek baar mein SO₃ nahi ban paati.
2. **Catalyst kya karta hai?** Sirf **raftaar badhata hai**, aur ye khud khatam nahi hota (aur kuch nahi).

### Taapman ka samjhauta (compromise)

Is reaction se **garmi nikalti hai**. Isliye:

- **Bahut kam taapman** par SO₃ **zyada** banta hai, lekin reaction **bahut dheemi** chalti hai: factory ko samay par acid nahi milta.
- **Bahut zyada taapman** par reaction **tez** hai, lekin SO₃ ki **matra kam** ho jaati hai.
- Isliye factory **lagbhag 450 °C** par kaam karti hai: na bahut dheema, na bahut kam SO₃. Ye ek **samjhauta** hai.

**Dabaav (pressure):** is process mein dabaav **lagbhag 1 se 2 atmosphere** hi rakha jaata hai. Oonche dabaav ki zaroorat nahi, kyunki is catalyst aur taapman par SO₃ ki matra pehle se hi **kaafi achhi** milti hai. Isse Haber process (lagbhag 200 atmosphere) se bahut fark hai, aur plant sasta aur surakshit rehta hai.

```
        SO₂ + O₂ (garam gas andar)
                 :
                 v
        +------------------+
        :  V₂O₅ parat 1    :   <--- gas har parat se guzarti hai
        :------------------:
        :  V₂O₅ parat 2    :
        :------------------:
        :  V₂O₅ parat 3    :
        +------------------+
                 :
                 v
        SO₃ + bachi SO₂ aur O₂ (bahar)

        converter: lagbhag 450 °C
```

> **Figure 4.3** – Converter ka saral roop: garam gas upar se aati hai, V₂O₅ ki kai paraton par se guzarti hai, aur neeche se SO₃ (aur thodi bachi SO₂ aur O₂) nikalti hai. Reaction se jo garmi nikalti hai use pipe ke zariye aane wali thandi gas ko garam karne mein lagaya jaata hai.

Ab taapman ka samjhauta ek nazar mein:

```
   Taapman              Raftaar          SO₃ ki matra
   -------------------  ---------------  --------------
   Bahut kam            bahut dheemi     zyada
   Lagbhag 450 °C       theek-thaak      kaafi achhi
   Bahut zyada          bahut tez        kam
```

> **Figure 4.4** – Taapman ka samjhauta, sirf dishaayen dikhane ke liye (koi sankhya nahi): kam taapman par SO₃ zyada par dheemi raftaar; zyada taapman par tez raftaar par SO₃ kam. Factory dono ke beech lagbhag 450 °C chunti hai.

### Example 2. Gas ke volume ka hisaab

**Sawaal:** Converter mein 120 L SO₂ ek hi taapman aur dabaav par oxygen ke saath mila hai. Agar maan lo ki poori SO₂ SO₃ ban jaaye, to kitni oxygen lagegi aur kitni SO₃ banegi? (Chapter 5 ka volume-anupaat.)

**Kadam 1 (equation):** 2SO₂ + O₂ → 2SO₃. Volume ka anupaat 2 : 1 : 2.
**Kadam 2:** O₂ = 120 ÷ 2 = **60 L**.
**Kadam 3:** SO₃ = 120 L (2 : 2 ka anupaat).

**Jaanch:** Pehle 120 + 60 = 180 L, baad mein 120 L: volume **ghata** (kyunki 3 hisse se 2 hisse bane: 3 → 2). Atom: S 120 = 120 (har L ke liye) ✅. (Asli factory mein poori SO₂ nahi badalti, kyunki reaction reversible hai: ye sirf ek aadarsh hisaab hai.)

## Part D: Kadam 3: SO₃ ko H₂SO₄ mein ghulana (oleum)

SO₃ ab converter se nikal chuki hai. Ab use sulphuric acid banana hai. Seedha tareeka dikhta hai: SO₃ + H₂O → H₂SO₄. Par factory ye **nahi** karti. Wo SO₃ ko **concentrated sulphuric acid (lagbhag 98 percent)** mein ghulati hai, jisse **oleum** (dhuaan chhodne wala acid) banta hai:

SO₃ + H₂SO₄ → H₂S₂O₇

Phir oleum mein **kaabu mein paani** milaya jaata hai aur wo do H₂SO₄ ban jaata hai:

H₂S₂O₇ + H₂O → 2H₂SO₄

Isse factory jitna chahe utna **mazboot (concentrated) ya halka** acid bana sakti hai: paani kitna milaya, us par depend karta hai.

```
        concentrated H₂SO₄ (upar se spray hota hai)
                      :
                      v
             +--------------------+
             :  patthar ke tukde  :
             :  (gas aur liquid   :
             :   ka sampark)      :
             :  SO₃ upar jaati    :
             :  hai, acid neeche  :
             :  aata hai          :
             +--------------------+
               ^                :
               :                v
        SO₃ gas andar      oleum neeche jama
        (converter se)     (phir kaabu mein paani milakar H₂SO₄)
```

> **Figure 4.5** – SO₃ ka absorption tower: concentrated H₂SO₄ upar se girta hai, SO₃ gas neeche se upar uthti hai, tower ke andar bhare tukdon par dono milte hain, aur neeche oleum jama hota hai. Phir oleum mein kaabu mein paani milakar sulphuric acid banta hai.

### Example 3. Oleum aur uska paani

**Sawaal:** 40 kg SO₃ ko concentrated H₂SO₄ mein poori tarah ghulaya gaya. (a) Kitna H₂SO₄ lagega aur kitna oleum (H₂S₂O₇) banega? (b) Is oleum se kitna H₂SO₄ banega aur kitna paani lagega? (S 32, O 16, H 1.)

**Kadam 1 (molar mass):** SO₃ = 32 + 48 = **80**; H₂SO₄ = 2 + 32 + 64 = **98**; H₂S₂O₇ = 2 + 64 + 112 = **178**; H₂O = **18**.
**Kadam 2 (moles):** 40 kg ÷ 80 = **0.5 kmol** SO₃. SO₃ + H₂SO₄ → H₂S₂O₇ ka anupaat 1 : 1 : 1.
**Kadam 3 (a):** H₂SO₄ = 0.5 × 98 = **49 kg**; oleum = 0.5 × 178 = **89 kg**.
**Kadam 4 (b):** H₂S₂O₇ + H₂O → 2H₂SO₄. Paani = 0.5 × 18 = **9 kg**; naya H₂SO₄ = 0.5 × 2 × 98 = **98 kg**.

**Jaanch:** Mass bachaav: 40 + 49 = 89 kg = oleum ✅; 89 + 9 = 98 kg = 98 kg H₂SO₄ ✅. Ek aur baat: shuru mein jo 49 kg H₂SO₄ liya tha wo wapas mil gaya: **net mein sirf 40 kg SO₃ aur 9 kg paani se 49 kg naya acid bana**: 98 − 49 = 49 ✅ (40 + 9 = 49 ✅).

## Part E: Sulphur se sulphuric acid: mass ka hisaab

Teeno kadam ko jodkar ek seedha rishta milta hai (Class 9 ke acid rain wale hisaab mein bhi yahi chain thi): **1 S → 1 SO₂ → 1 SO₃ → 1 H₂SO₄** (har kadam mein ek-ek ka anupaat). Is chain ki ginti: sulphur ke ek mole se sulphuric acid ka ek mole, yaani **32 g S se 98 g H₂SO₄**.

### Example 4. 16 kg sulphur se kitna acid?

**Sawaal:** Ek factory mein 16 kg shuddh sulphur poori tarah jala kar sulphuric acid banaya jaata hai (maan lo kuch bhi bekaar nahi gaya). Kitni SO₂ banegi, kitni oxygen lagegi kadam 1 mein, aur aakhir mein kitna H₂SO₄ milega? (S 32, O 16, H 1.)

**Kadam 1:** S = 16 ÷ 32 = **0.5 kmol**.
**Kadam 2 (kadam 1):** S + O₂ → SO₂, to SO₂ = 0.5 kmol × 64 = **32 kg**; O₂ = 0.5 × 32 = **16 kg**.
**Kadam 3 (poori chain):** 1 : 1 anupaat se H₂SO₄ = 0.5 kmol × 98 = **49 kg**.

**Jaanch:** Kadam 1: 16 + 16 = 32 kg ✅. Pure chain mein S (16 kg) se H₂SO₄ (49 kg): 49 ÷ 16 = 3.06, jo 98 ÷ 32 = 3.06 ke barabar ✅.

### Example 5. Iron pyrites FeS₂ se acid

**Sawaal:** 240 kg FeS₂ ko roast karke saari SO₂ se sulphuric acid banaya jaata hai (4FeS₂ + 11O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃ + 8SO₂). Kitna H₂SO₄ milega? (Fe 56, S 32, O 16, H 1.)

**Kadam 1:** FeS₂ = 56 + 64 = **120** g/mol. 240 kg ÷ 120 = **2 kmol**.
**Kadam 2:** 4 FeS₂ se 8 SO₂, yaani 1 FeS₂ se 2 SO₂: SO₂ = 2 × 2 = **4 kmol**.
**Kadam 3:** 1 SO₂ se 1 H₂SO₄ (chain): H₂SO₄ = 4 kmol × 98 = **392 kg**.

**Jaanch:** Sulphur ki ginti: 2 kmol FeS₂ mein S = 4 kmol; 4 kmol H₂SO₄ mein bhi S = 4 kmol ✅. Sulphur ka mass 4 × 32 = 128 kg, aur 392 kg H₂SO₄ mein S = 392 × 32 ÷ 98 = 128 kg ✅.

## Part F: Chimney, acid rain aur roasting se rishta

Ab Sarang ka sawaal wapas. Contact process mein SO₂ ki jagah-jagah gadbad se bachna zaroori hai. **Poori SO₂ SO₃ nahi banti** (reaction reversible hai), isliye bachi hui SO₂ ko aksar ek baar aur catalyst ki parat par bheja jaata hai, aur aakhri gas mein jo thodi rehti hai use chimney se pehle **saaf (scrub)** kiya jaata hai: seedhe chimney se chhodna zehar aur acid rain dono ko bulava hai.

- **Roasting se link:** Chapter 3 mein sulphide ore ko roast karne par bahut si SO₂ nikalti thi, aur wahan bataya tha ki use **sulphuric acid banane** mein lagaya jaata hai. Ab tumne dekh liya ki wo raasta **Contact process** hai.
- **Acid rain se link:** Class 9 mein SO₂ hawa mein dheere-dheere SO₃ aur phir H₂SO₄ banti thi, aur baarish ki boondon mein ghulkar acid rain banati thi. Factory yahi kaam **control** mein, band pipe mein, catalyst ke saath tez raftaar se karti hai: jaisa hona chahiye waisa.

| Baat | Hawa mein chhoot gayi SO₂ | Contact process mein SO₂ |
|---|---|---|
| Kaun karta hai | Hawa, dhoop, baadal | Catalyst V₂O₅, 450 °C |
| Nateeja | Acid rain, nuksaan | Kaam ka sulphuric acid |
| Kaabu | Koi nahi | Poora kaabu, band system |

**Sulphuric acid ke kaam (lagbhag list):** khaad (superphosphate, ammonium sulphate) sabse bada hissa, car battery (dilute H₂SO₄, lagbhag 30 percent), detergent, rang, paint, petroleum ki safai, steel ko plating se pehle saaf karna, aur dusre acid (HCl, HNO₃) banana. Concentrated H₂SO₄ ke tarah-tarah ke swabhav Topic 5 mein.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti: "Contact process mein SO₃ ko seedha paani mein ghulaya jaata hai: SO₃ + H₂O → H₂SO₄."**
Equation sahi hai (Class 9 mein ye baadal mein hota tha), par factory iska upyog **seedha nahi** karti. Demo teen kadam mein (kaagaz par):

1. **Seedha paani mein:** SO₃ aur paani ka milna bahut **zabardast garmi** chhodta hai. Paani ubalne lagta hai aur ye acid **bahut chhoti-chhoti boondon ki dhundh (mist)** ban kar hawa mein tairne lagta hai.
2. **Dhundh ka nuksaan:** ye boondein jama nahi hotin, tower se bahar nikal jaati hain, aur chimney se hawa mein jaakar acid rain jaisi gadbad karti hain. Saath mein jama karna bahut mushkil hota hai.
3. **Factory ka tareeka:** SO₃ ko pehle concentrated H₂SO₄ mein ghulao (oleum), phir uske baad **kaabu mein** paani milao. Ab garmi dhire-dhire nikalti hai aur acid jama hota jaata hai, dhundh nahi banti.

```
   Galat tareeka                          Sahi tareeka

   SO₃ + paani                            SO₃ + conc. H₂SO₄  --> oleum
        :                                          |
        v                                          v
   bahut garmi, dhundh (mist)             oleum + paani, kaabu mein
   tower se bahar nikalti hai             --> H₂SO₄ jama hota hai
```

> **Figure 4.6** – Do raaste: SO₃ ko seedha paani mein ghulane par acid ki dhundh bani rehti hai aur jama nahi hoti; concentrated H₂SO₄ mein ghulane par oleum banta hai, jisme baad mein kaabu se paani milaya jaata hai.

**Niyam:** Overall equation SO₃ + H₂O → H₂SO₄ sahi hai, par **factory ka raasta** SO₃ → oleum → H₂SO₄ hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Kachcha maal:** sulphur (ya FeS₂ jaise sulphide ore ki roasting se SO₂), hawa, V₂O₅ catalyst, paani aur concentrated H₂SO₄.
- **Teen kadam:** (1) S + O₂ → SO₂; (2) 2SO₂ + O₂ ⇌ 2SO₃ (V₂O₅, lagbhag 450 °C, 1 se 2 atmosphere); (3) SO₃ + H₂SO₄ → H₂S₂O₇ (oleum), phir H₂S₂O₇ + H₂O → 2H₂SO₄.
- **450 °C ek samjhauta hai:** kam taapman par SO₃ zyada par dheemi raftaar; zyada taapman par tez par kam SO₃.
- **Dabaav ki zaroorat nahi:** SO₃ ki matra pehle se achhi hai.
- **Catalyst:** sirf raftaar badhata hai.
- **Seedha paani mein nahi:** acid ki dhundh (mist) banti hai, isliye pehle oleum.
- **Mass ka hisaab:** 1 S se 1 H₂SO₄: 32 g S se 98 g H₂SO₄ (16 kg S se 49 kg acid). 1 FeS₂ se 2 H₂SO₄.
- **Rishte:** Chapter 3 ki roasting ki SO₂ aur Class 9 ki acid rain, dono isi kahani se judte hain.
- **Suraksha:** SO₂ aur SO₃ zehreeli; oleum aur concentrated H₂SO₄ jalane wale; sab sirf padhne ya teacher demo ke liye.

## Practice Questions

(Mass: H 1, O 16, S 32, Fe 56. Sab sawaal paper par hal karne ke liye hain.)

1. A plant burns 48 kg of pure sulphur completely in air (S + O₂ → SO₂). Find the mass of SO₂ formed and the mass of oxygen used. Check that the masses balance.
2. All the SO₂ from the burning in Question 1 is turned into sulphuric acid by the Contact process. Find the mass of H₂SO₄ formed.
3. 360 kg of iron pyrites FeS₂ is roasted (4FeS₂ + 11O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃ + 8SO₂) and all the SO₂ is converted into H₂SO₄. Find the mass of H₂SO₄ and the mass of Fe₂O₃ formed.
4. In a converter 336 L of SO₂ reacts with oxygen at the same temperature and pressure (2SO₂ + O₂ → 2SO₃). Assuming full conversion, find the volume of oxygen needed and of SO₃ formed.
5. 200 kg of SO₃ is absorbed in concentrated H₂SO₄ to make oleum, SO₃ + H₂SO₄ → H₂S₂O₇. Find the mass of H₂SO₄ taken up and the mass of oleum formed.
6. 17.8 kg of oleum (H₂S₂O₇) is later mixed carefully with the exact amount of water, H₂S₂O₇ + H₂O → 2H₂SO₄. Find the mass of water needed and the mass of H₂SO₄ formed.
7. A batch of sulphur of 100 kg contains 96 percent pure sulphur (the rest is not sulphur). Find the mass of H₂SO₄ that can be made from this batch by the full Contact process.
8. A converter handles 192 kg of SO₂ per hour. Find the mass of oxygen needed and of SO₃ formed per hour if all the SO₂ reacts.
9. A student says: "Since the Contact process uses SO₃ + H₂O → H₂SO₄ in the end, we may simply bubble SO₃ into water." Explain what is wrong and what really happens in the plant.
10. The Contact process uses about 450 °C. Using the facts that the reaction gives out heat, that low temperature gives more SO₃ but is too slow, and that high temperature is fast but gives less SO₃, explain why 450 °C is a compromise.
11. Name the raw materials and the catalyst of the Contact process, and arrange these steps in the correct order: absorption in conc. H₂SO₄, burning sulphur, passing over V₂O₅, adding water to oleum, purifying the gas.
12. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) Oleum is SO₃ dissolved in concentrated H₂SO₄. (b) The Contact process needs a pressure of about 200 atmospheres. (c) The catalyst of the Contact process is V₂O₅. (d) One mole of S gives one mole of H₂SO₄ in the whole process. (e) SO₂ in the chimney of a factory is harmless to people and the environment.
13. Explain how roasting of a sulphide ore (Chapter 3) and the Contact process are connected, and why this link matters for acid rain.

### Answers

1. S = 48 ÷ 32 = **1.5 kmol**. S + O₂ → SO₂ (1 : 1 : 1). SO₂ = 1.5 × 64 = **96 kg**; O₂ = 1.5 × 32 = **48 kg**. **Jaanch:** 48 + 48 = 96 ✅.
2. 1 S se 1 H₂SO₄, to H₂SO₄ = 1.5 kmol × 98 = **147 kg**. **Jaanch:** 147 ÷ 48 = 3.06 = 98 ÷ 32 ✅.
3. FeS₂ = 56 + 64 = 120. 360 ÷ 120 = **3 kmol**. 1 FeS₂ se 2 SO₂ (8 ÷ 4), to SO₂ = **6 kmol**, to H₂SO₄ = 6 × 98 = **588 kg**. Fe₂O₃: 4FeS₂ se 2Fe₂O₃, to 3 kmol FeS₂ se 1.5 kmol Fe₂O₃; Fe₂O₃ = 112 + 48 = 160; mass = 1.5 × 160 = **240 kg**. **Jaanch:** Fe: 3 kmol FeS₂ mein Fe = 3 kmol; 1.5 kmol Fe₂O₃ mein Fe = 3 kmol ✅. S: 6 kmol = 6 kmol H₂SO₄ mein ✅.
4. Anupaat 2 : 1 : 2. O₂ = 336 ÷ 2 = **168 L**. SO₃ = **336 L**. **Jaanch:** 336 + 168 = 504 L pehle, 336 L baad (volume ghata, kyunki 3 → 2 hissa) ✅.
5. SO₃ = 200 ÷ 80 = **2.5 kmol**. H₂SO₄ = 2.5 × 98 = **245 kg**. Oleum = 2.5 × 178 = **445 kg**. **Jaanch:** 200 + 245 = 445 ✅.
6. H₂S₂O₇ = 17.8 ÷ 178 = **0.1 kmol**. Paani = 0.1 × 18 = **1.8 kg**. H₂SO₄ = 0.1 × 2 × 98 = **19.6 kg**. **Jaanch:** 17.8 + 1.8 = 19.6 ✅.
7. Shuddh S = 100 × 0.96 = **96 kg**. 96 ÷ 32 = 3 kmol. H₂SO₄ = 3 × 98 = **294 kg**. **Jaanch:** 294 ÷ 96 = 3.06 = 98 ÷ 32 ✅.
8. SO₂ = 192 ÷ 64 = **3 kmol**. O₂ = 3 ÷ 2 = 1.5 kmol × 32 = **48 kg**. SO₃ = 3 × 80 = **240 kg**. **Jaanch:** 192 + 48 = 240 ✅.
9. SO₃ aur paani milne par **bahut garmi** nikalti hai, paani ubalta hai aur acid ki **dhundh (mist)** ban jaati hai jo jama nahi hoti aur hawa mein chali jaati hai. Plant mein SO₃ ko pehle **concentrated H₂SO₄** mein ghulate hain (oleum, H₂S₂O₇), phir kaabu mein paani milakar do H₂SO₄ banate hain.
10. Garmi nikalti hai, to **kam taapman** par SO₃ zyada milta hai par raftaar bahut dheemi ho jaati hai (acid dheere bante hain). **Zyada taapman** par raftaar tez hai par SO₃ ki matra kam ho jaati hai. Isliye lagbhag 450 °C ka **samjhauta** (compromise) chunte hain, aur V₂O₅ catalyst se raftaar badhate hain.
11. Kachcha maal: **sulphur (ya sulphide ore), hawa, paani aur concentrated H₂SO₄**; catalyst **V₂O₅**. Sahi kram: (1) sulphur ko jalana, (2) gas ko saaf karna, (3) V₂O₅ par se guzaarna, (4) concentrated H₂SO₄ mein absorb karna (oleum), (5) oleum mein paani milana.
12. (a) **True** ✅. (b) **False** ❌: lagbhag 1 se 2 atmosphere kaafi hai; 200 atmosphere Haber process ka dabaav hai. (c) **True** ✅. (d) **True** ✅ (1 : 1 anupaat har kadam mein). (e) **False** ❌: SO₂ zehreeli hai aur acid rain banati hai.
13. Sulphide ko roast karne par **SO₂** nikalti hai (Chapter 3). Ye SO₂ seedha chhodne ki jagah Contact process ka **kachcha maal** ban sakti hai: V₂O₅ par SO₃, phir oleum, phir H₂SO₄. Isse chimney se SO₂ **kam** nikalti hai, to acid rain bhi kam hoti hai, aur ek kaam ka acid mil jaata hai.
