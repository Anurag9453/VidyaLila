# Topic 5: Concentrated Sulphuric Acid: Dehydration, Oxidation Aur Suraksha

Topic 4 mein hamne dekha ki factory mein sulphur se H₂SO₄ kaise banta hai, aur ki Contact process ka aakhri product **gaadha (concentrated) H₂SO₄** hota hai: lagbhag 98 percent acid, baaki thoda sa paani. Ab sawaal ye hai: gaadha H₂SO₄ **karta kya hai**? Class 9 mein aur Chapter 1 mein tumne sirf **dilute** H₂SO₄ dekha: Zn ke saath hydrogen, carbonate ke saath CO₂. Gaadha H₂SO₄ ek bilkul **alag swabhaav** dikhata hai. Aaj hum uske chaar chehre dekhenge: paani chheenne wala, oxidise karne wala, "na udne wala" aur gas sukhane wala, aur ye bhi ki use sambhalna kyun itna khatarnak hai.

## Science fair ka kaala stambh

Mr. Wable apni bhanji Rasika ko school ke science fair mein le gaye. Lab ke ek kone mein kaanch ke peeche **fume cupboard** (hawa bahar kheenchne wala bandh almirah) mein teacher ek demo kar rahe the, aur bachche aur bade **kaafi door rassi ke peeche** khade the. Fume cupboard ke andar ek beaker mein safed cheeni (sugar) ke dane rakhe the. Teacher ne dastane pehne, goggles lagaye, kaanch ka sash neeche kiya, aur bahar se khud ek chhota sa lambe haath wala kaam kiya: beaker mein gaadha acid daala.

Kuch der kuch nahi hua. Phir cheeni peeli padi, phir bhoori, phir **kaali**, aur achanak ek **kaala, jhajhra stambh** beaker se oopar uthne laga, saath mein bhaap ki dhaar aur ek tez gandh. Rasika ki aankhein phail gayi. "Mama, ye to **jal gayi**! Par aag kahan thi? Teacher ne to koi maachis bhi nahi jalayi!"

Mr. Wable muskuraye. "Bahut accha sawaal, Rasika. Aag thi hi nahi. Cheeni **jali nahi, uska paani chheena gaya**. Wo acid cheeni ke andar se paani nikaal raha hai, aur jo bachta hai wo kaala carbon hai. Aur jo bhaap tumne door se uthti dekhi, wo isi acid ki garmi ka saboot hai. Ye kaise hota hai, aaj hum yahi samjhenge."

"Aur Mama, ye wahi acid hai jo hum car ki battery mein padhte hain?" "Wahi, par battery ka acid **dilute** hota hai, aur ye gaadha. Naam ek, swabhaav bilkul alag."

## Suraksha (pehle ye padho)

> **Suraksha: gaadha sulphuric acid**
> - Gaadha H₂SO₄ **bahut corrosive** hai: skin, aankh, kapde, kaagaz, lakdi sab ko jalata aur gala deta hai. Isse sambhalna **sirf prashikshit teacher ka kaam** hai.
> - Isliye is topic ka **har demo sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo)** hai: fume cupboard, goggles, gloves, apron, aur bachche door. Koi student ye ghar par ya akele kabhi nahi karega.
> - Kuch demos mein **SO₂ gas** banti hai: zehreeli, gale aur aankh ko jalane wali. Wo fume cupboard mein hi hoti hai.
> - **Dilution rule:** acid ko paani mein, paani ko acid mein kabhi nahi (Part E mein poora kyun).
> - Battery ka acid ya kisi safai ke saaman par "danger acid" likha ho to bachche **kabhi na chhuen**: sirf bade sambhalte hain. Kisi ko acid lag jaye to **turant bade ya teacher ko batao**: ghar par khud ilaaj nahi.

## Part A: Gaadha aur dilute: ek hi formula, do swabhaav

Bottle ke andar kya hai? **Gaadha H₂SO₄** lagbhag 98 percent acid hai: **tel jaisa gaadha, chipchipa (syrupy)**, aur paani se kaafi bhaari (lagbhag 1.8 guna). **Dilute H₂SO₄** mein acid ke chhote chhote kan bahut sare paani mein ghule hote hain. Chapter 1 mein dekha tha ki paani mein H₂SO₄ ions (H₃O⁺ aur SO₄²⁻) deta hai, aur **wahi H₃O⁺ metal, carbonate aur base ke saath reaction ka kaaran hai**.

Gaadhe acid mein paani **lagbhag hai hi nahi**, to ions bahut kam banenge. Uska swabhaav alag jagah se aata hai: H₂SO₄ ke **poore kan** ka paani ke liye bahut tez "bhookh" aur uska garam hone par **oxygen dene ka** swabhaav. Isliye:

- **dilute** acid: acid wala swabhaav (ions se): Zn ke saath H₂ (Class 9 ke Zn + H₂SO₄ → ZnSO₄ + H₂ jaisa).
- **gaadha** acid: paani chheenna, oxidise karna, gas sukhana: **ek dum alag kahani**.

Figure 5.1 chaaron chehre ek nazar mein dikhata hai.

```
                      Gaadha H₂SO₄ (lagbhag 98 percent)
                                   :
        +--------------------------+---------------------------+
        :              :                       :               :
        v              v                       v               v
   1. Dehydrating   2. Oxidising        3. Non-volatile    4. Drying agent
      (paani chheene)  (garam par,         (HCl, HNO₃ ko      (nami sokhe:
                       Cu, C se)           unke namak se       HCl, Cl₂ ke liye;
      cheeni -> C      Cu -> CuSO₄ + SO₂   udane wala)         NH₃ ke liye nahi)
```

## Part B: Dehydrating agent: paani chheenne wala

**Dehydrate** ka matlab hai paani nikalna. Gaadha H₂SO₄ paani se milte hi bahut garmi deta hai, iska matlab uska paani ke saath **bahut gehra lagav** hai. Jahan bhi paani (ya H aur O ek saath, H₂O ke 2 : 1 anupaat mein) mile, wo kheench leta hai.

### Cheeni (sugar) wala demo

Cheeni ka formula **C₁₂H₂₂O₁₁**. Dhyan se dekho: H aur O ka anupaat **22 : 11 = 2 : 1**, bilkul paani (H₂O) jaisa. Isliye cheeni jaise padarthon ka naam hi **carbohydrate** (carbon + hydrate: "carbon ka paani") hai. Gaadha acid inme se paani chheen leta hai aur **carbon bacha deta hai**:

C₁₂H₂₂O₁₁ → 12C + 11H₂O (gaadha H₂SO₄ ki maujoodgi mein)

Dhyan do: acid **khud is equation mein kharch nahi hota**; wo sirf paani chheenta hai (isliye likha hai "maujoodgi mein"). Chheena hua paani acid mein ghulta hai aur isiliye bahut garmi nikalti hai, jis se paani bhaap ban kar kaale carbon ko **jhajhra, phooli hui** stambh bana kar upar dhakelta hai.

```
   Fume cupboard (kaanch ka sash neeche; teacher bahar se kaam karta hai)
   +----------------------------------------------------+
   :                                                    :
   :     pehle                       kuch der baad      :
   :                                    ^   ^  bhaap    :
   :                                   /#####\          :
   :                                  :#######:  <-- kaala,
   :       ~ cheeni ~                 :#######:      jhajhra
   :     (safed dane)                 :#######:      carbon
   :    +----------+                 +---------+     ka stambh
   :    :  beaker  :                 : beaker  :
   :    +----------+                 +---------+
   :                                                    :
   +----------------------------------------------------+
        bachche aur bade door, rassi ke peeche
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Cheeni ka dehydration (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo): fume cupboard ke andar beaker mein cheeni par gaadha H₂SO₄ daala jaata hai. Cheeni bhoori hokar kaali ho jaati hai aur bhaap ke saath jhajhra carbon ka stambh upar uthta hai. Kaanch ka sash neeche rehta hai.

Demo mein ek tez gandh bhi aati hai: kuch garam acid kuch carbon ko bhi oxidise kar deta hai (Part C), jisse **SO₂** nikalti hai. Isliye ye demo **fume cupboard** ke bina kabhi nahi hota.

### Example 1. Cheeni ke 3.42 g ka hisaab

**Sawaal:** Teacher ne beaker mein 3.42 g cheeni (C₁₂H₂₂O₁₁) li. Agar saari cheeni poori dehydrate ho jaye to kitna carbon aur kitna paani banega? (Mass: H 1, C 12, O 16.)

**Kadam 1 (equation):** C₁₂H₂₂O₁₁ → 12C + 11H₂O.
**Kadam 2 (molar mass):** C₁₂H₂₂O₁₁ = 12 × 12 + 22 × 1 + 11 × 16 = 144 + 22 + 176 = **342** g/mol.
**Kadam 3 (moles):** 3.42 ÷ 342 = **0.01 mol**.
**Kadam 4 (ratio se):** carbon = 12 × 0.01 = 0.12 mol, mass = 0.12 × 12 = **1.44 g**. Paani = 11 × 0.01 = 0.11 mol, mass = 0.11 × 18 = **1.98 g**.

**Jaanch:** atoms: C 12 = 12, H 22 = 11 × 2 = 22, O 11 = 11 ✅. Mass sangrakshan: 1.44 + 1.98 = 3.42 g ✅. (Asli demo mein kuch carbon aur jalta hai, to ye "poore dehydration" ka aadarsh hisaab hai.)

### Neela thotha ka rang udna

Class 9 mein tumne dekha tha ki neela thotha **CuSO₄·5H₂O** ke crystal mein paani (water of crystallisation) hota hai, aur use garam karne par ya paani milane par uska rang badalta hai ([water-part4.md](../../../class9/chemistry/water/water-part4.md)). Gaadha H₂SO₄ ye paani **bina garam kiye bhi** chheen leta hai (teacher demo):

CuSO₄·5H₂O → CuSO₄ + 5H₂O (gaadha H₂SO₄ ke saath)

Neela crystal **safed** (ya halka bhoora-safed) powder ban jaata hai: anhydrous CuSO₄. Ghol neela nahi: kyunki jo paani crystal ko neela banata tha, wo acid ne le liya.

```
   Pehle                              Gaadha H₂SO₄ ke baad
   (teacher demo, sookhi test tube)

     :   :                              :   :
     :***:  neela crystal               :...:  safed powder
     :***:  CuSO₄·5H₂O                  :...:  CuSO₄ (anhydrous)
     +---+                              +---+
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Neela thotha ka rang udna (sirf dekhne ke liye): gaadha H₂SO₄ crystal ke paani ko chheen leta hai, to neela CuSO₄·5H₂O safed anhydrous CuSO₄ ban jaata hai. Test tube khuli rehti hai.

### Example 2. Kitna paani gaya?

**Sawaal:** 12.475 g neela thotha (CuSO₄·5H₂O) gaadhe H₂SO₄ se dehydrate hua. Kitna paani gaya aur kitna safed CuSO₄ bacha? (Cu 63.5, S 32, O 16, H 1.)

**Kadam 1:** CuSO₄·5H₂O = 63.5 + 32 + 64 + 5 × 18 = 159.5 + 90 = **249.5** g/mol.
**Kadam 2:** moles = 12.475 ÷ 249.5 = **0.05 mol**.
**Kadam 3:** CuSO₄·5H₂O → CuSO₄ + 5H₂O. Paani = 5 × 0.05 = 0.25 mol, mass = 0.25 × 18 = **4.5 g**. CuSO₄ = 0.05 × 159.5 = **7.975 g**.

**Jaanch:** 7.975 + 4.5 = 12.475 g ✅. Atoms: Cu 1, S 1, O 4 + 5 = 9, H 10 = 5 × 2 ✅.

### Kaagaz, lakdi, kapda aur skin

Kaagaz, lakdi aur kapda (cotton) jaise padarth bhi paani wale carbon yaugik (cellulose) hain: gaadha acid inse paani chheenkar **kaale carbon ke daag** chhodta hai: kaagaz par **kaali jali-si lakeer**, kapda gal kar chhidra. **Skin mein bhi** paani aur carbon yaugik hote hain: isliye gaadha acid skin ko **jalata** hai. Ye aag se jalna nahi, **paani chheenne se** jalna hai.

## Part C: Oxidising agent: garam gaadha acid dhaatu aur carbon ko "jala" deta hai

Dilute H₂SO₄ ke saath copper **koi reaction nahi karta**: Class 9 aur Chapter 2 mein dekha tha ki copper reactivity series mein hydrogen se neeche hai, to H₂ displace nahi kar sakta. Par **garam gaadha** H₂SO₄ copper se react karta hai, aur isme **hydrogen nahi, SO₂ gas** nikalti hai. Yahan H₂SO₄ khud oxidise karne wala hai: wo copper ko oxygen deta hai aur khud SO₂ mein badal jaata hai.

Cu + 2H₂SO₄ (gaadha, garam) → CuSO₄ + SO₂ + 2H₂O

```
   Teacher demo (fume cupboard mein) : Cu + garam gaadha H₂SO₄

                   delivery tube (cork se hokar)
             .------------------------------.
             :                              :
          [cork]                            v
          :    :                        \       /   <-- ulta funnel: mooh sirf
          : Cu :                         \     /        NaOH ke ghol ko chhoota
          : +  :                       +--\~~~/--------+
          :gaadha                      :  ~~~~~~~~~~~~ :  <-- NaOH ka ghol
          :H₂SO₄:                      :  (SO₂ ko sokhta hai)
          :____:                       +---------------+
         boiling tube    ^   thodi aanch (teacher)
         (khuli nali se judi, band nahi)
```

> **Figure 5.4** – Copper aur garam gaadhe H₂SO₄ ka teacher demo (sirf dekhne ke liye): boiling tube ke cork se ek delivery tube nikalti hai, jiska sira ulte funnel mein hai jo NaOH ke ghol ki sataha ko bas chhoota hai, taaki zehreeli SO₂ ghol mein sokh li jaye (acidic gas aur alkali: Chapter 1). Apparatus band nahi hai. Teacher heating rokne se pehle funnel ko ghol se bahar nikaal leta hai taaki suck-back na ho.

Is demo mein: ghol dheere dheere **neela** ho jaata hai (CuSO₄), aur ek **tez gandh wali, bina rang ki SO₂** nikalti hai. SO₂ **zehreeli** hai (Class 9 mein acid rain wale topic mein ye gas aayi thi), isliye ye sirf fume cupboard mein hota hai.

### Example 3. Copper ke 9.525 g se SO₂

**Sawaal:** 9.525 g copper poora garam gaadhe H₂SO₄ ke saath react hua. Kitna CuSO₄ banega, kitni SO₂ (STP par, litre mein), aur kitna H₂SO₄ kharch hoga? (Cu 63.5, S 32, O 16, H 1; molar volume 22.4 L.)

**Kadam 1 (equation):** Cu + 2H₂SO₄ → CuSO₄ + SO₂ + 2H₂O. Ratio Cu : H₂SO₄ : CuSO₄ : SO₂ = 1 : 2 : 1 : 1.
**Kadam 2 (moles):** Cu = 9.525 ÷ 63.5 = **0.15 mol**.
**Kadam 3 (ratio se):**
- CuSO₄ = 0.15 mol × 159.5 = **23.925 g**.
- SO₂ = 0.15 mol × 22.4 = **3.36 L** (STP).
- H₂SO₄ = 0.3 mol × 98 = **29.4 g**.

**Jaanch:** atoms: Cu 1, S 2 = 1 + 1, O 8 = 4 + 2 + 2, H 4 = 4 ✅. Mass: baayein 9.525 + 29.4 = 38.925 g; daayein CuSO₄ 23.925 + SO₂ (0.15 × 64 = 9.6) + H₂O (0.3 × 18 = 5.4) = 38.925 g ✅.

### Carbon bhi oxidise hota hai

Garam gaadha H₂SO₄ **non-metal carbon** ko bhi oxidise karta hai. Aur hum Part B mein cheeni ke demo mein jo tez gandh aayi thi, uski jadd yahi hai: bana hua kuch carbon garam acid se aage react karta hai.

C + 2H₂SO₄ (gaadha, garam) → CO₂ + 2SO₂ + 2H₂O

### Example 4. 1.8 g carbon ka hisaab (sirf padhne ke liye)

**Sawaal:** 1.8 g carbon poora garam gaadhe H₂SO₄ se react hua. Kul gas kitni (CO₂ + SO₂, STP) aur kitna H₂SO₄ kharch? (C 12.)

**Kadam 1:** C = 1.8 ÷ 12 = **0.15 mol**. Ratio C : CO₂ : SO₂ : H₂SO₄ = 1 : 1 : 2 : 2.
**Kadam 2:** CO₂ = 0.15 mol, SO₂ = 0.3 mol, kul gas = 0.45 mol × 22.4 = **10.08 L**. H₂SO₄ = 0.3 mol × 98 = **29.4 g**.

**Jaanch:** atoms: C 1, S 2, O 8 = 2 + 4 + 2, H 4 ✅. Mass: 1.8 + 29.4 = 31.2 g; daayein CO₂ 6.6 + SO₂ 19.2 + H₂O 5.4 = 31.2 g ✅.

## Part D: Non-volatile aur drying agent

### Non-volatile: zyada udne wale acid ko bhaga deta hai

Gaadha H₂SO₄ bahut garam karne par bhi (lagbhag 300 °C se bhi upar jaakar hi ubalta hai): ye **non-volatile** hai. HCl gas, ek taraf, bahut kam temperature par hi gas ban jaati hai. Isliye jab gaadhe H₂SO₄ ko ek namak (jaise NaCl) ke saath halka garam karte hain, to **volatile HCl udkar bahar nikal jaata hai** aur H₂SO₄ peeche ruk kar namak banata hai: NaCl + H₂SO₄ → NaHSO₄ + HCl. Yahi tareeka **Topic 1 ki lab preparation** hai (HCl ke liye), aur aisa hi **Topic 3** mein nitrate ke saath HNO₃ ke liye. Dono jagah acid ka kaam: "apne se zyada volatile acid ko uske namak se bhaga dena". Yahan naya kuch nahi, sirf pehchaan ki gaadhe H₂SO₄ ka ye teesra chehra hai.

### Drying agent: gas ki nami sokhna

Gaadha H₂SO₄ nami (paani ki bhaap) ko bhi kheench leta hai. Isliye wo kai gaso ke liye **sukhane wala** hai. Class 9 mein tumne dekha tha ki hydrogen ko wash bottle mein sukhaya jaata hai ([hydrogen-part2.md](../../../class9/chemistry/hydrogen/hydrogen-part2.md)). Wahi wash bottle:

```
         gas andar               gas bahar
         (geeli)                 (sookhi)
            :                      ^
            v                      :
          +-+--+------------------+-+--+
          [  cork (do chhed)          ]
          +--:--------------------:----+
          :  :                    :    :
          :  :                         :
          :  :                         :
          :~~:~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~:   <-- gaadha H₂SO₄ ka star
          : o:o  bulbule                :
          +------------------------------+
         lambi nali acid mein dooba hai, chhoti nali acid ke upar
```

> **Figure 5.5** – Wash bottle mein gas sukhana (sirf padhne ke liye): geeli gas lambi nali se acid ke andar jaati hai aur bulbule banakar upar uthti hai; nami acid kheench leta hai, aur sookhi gas chhoti nali se bahar nikalti hai. Ye wash bottle teacher hi sambhalta hai.

Gaadha H₂SO₄ **HCl** aur **Cl₂** jaisi gaso ko sukhane ke liye theek hai (ye acidic gas hain jo acid se react nahi karte). Par **NH₃ ko kabhi nahi**: ammonia ek **base** hai, jo acid se turant react karke namak (ammonium sulphate) bana deti hai (Topic 2: NH₃ ko CaO se sukhate hain). Ek aur dekhne wali baat: **khuli bottle** mein rakha gaadha H₂SO₄ hawa ki nami sokh kar **vazan mein badhta hai aur patla hota jaata hai**: isliye bottle band rakhte hain.

## Part E: Dilution rule aur suraksha: acid paani mein, kabhi ulta nahi

Gaadhe H₂SO₄ ka paani se milna **bahut exothermic** hai (Chapter 1 mein ye garmi aur ye niyam dono dekhe the: [acids-bases-and-salts-part2.md](../acids-bases-and-salts/acids-bases-and-salts-part2.md); yahan gaadhe H₂SO₄ ke liye uski poori "kyun"). Niyam: **hamesha acid ko paani mein, dheere dheere, hilate hue; paani ko acid mein kabhi nahi.**

Ab **kyun**? Gaadha acid paani se **bhaari** (lagbhag 1.8 guna) hai, aur paani **halka**. Agar paani gaadhe acid ke upar daala jaye, to wo **upar hi tairta rehta hai**. Saari garmi us **patli sataha** par hi nikalti hai, wahan paani achanak ubalta hai aur **acid ke chhinte** uchhal kar aankh aur haath par aate hain. Ulta, agar acid dheere se bahut sare paani mein daala jaye aur hilaya jaye, to garmi poore paani mein **faili jaati** hai (paani bahut garmi sokh sakta hai: Class 9 ki specific heat) aur chhinte nahi uchhalte.

```
   GALAT (khatarnak)                       SAHI
   paani ko acid mein                      acid ko paani mein
   
      paani (boondein)                        acid (dheere dheere)
           :                                       :
           v                                       v   (glass rod se hilate hue)
   ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~  <-- halka            +-------------+
   :  ! ubalta, chhinte ! \  paani upar         : ~~~~~~~~~~~ :  <-- bahut sara
   :   uchhalte !        :  tairta (patli        : paani       :      paani
   : GAADHA ACID (bhaari) :   sataha)            :             :
   +--------------------+                        +-------------+
   saari garmi yahin, patli sataha par           garmi poore paani mein faili
```

> **Figure 5.6** – Dilution ka rule (sirf padhne ke liye): baayen, paani halka hone se gaadhe acid ke upar tairta hai aur garmi patli sataha par jama hoti hai, jisse chhinte uchhalte hain. Daayen, acid ko dheere dheere bahut paani mein hilate hue milane se garmi faili jaati hai.

**Teacher ka tareeka:** paani pehle beaker mein, acid dheere, glass rod se hilate hue, beaker haath mein nahi bhi pakadte kyunki wo garam ho sakta hai. Student ye nahi karta.

### Gaadhe H₂SO₄ ke upyog (lagbhag list)

| Upyog | Kya banta ya kya hota hai |
|---|---|
| Khaad (fertiliser) | superphosphate aur ammonium sulphate: sabse bada upyog |
| Car battery | lead-acid battery mein dilute H₂SO₄ (lagbhag 30 percent) |
| Detergent, rang, paint | banane mein |
| Petroleum refining | tel ko saaf karne mein |
| Steel ki safai (pickling) | plating se pehle zang hatana |
| Doosre acid | HCl aur HNO₃ (Topics 1 aur 3) |

Kai jagah **dilute** acid ya uske namak ka upyog hai, par bachche kabhi na chhuen: battery ka acid aur safai ka koi bhi saaman bade hi sambhalte hain.

## Part F: Chaaron chehre: ek tulna

| Gun | Kya hota hai | Kaun sa padarth / dikhta kya hai | Equation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dehydrating | paani chheenta hai | cheeni kaali, neela thotha safed, kaagaz kaala | C₁₂H₂₂O₁₁ → 12C + 11H₂O |
| Oxidising (garam) | Cu aur C ko oxidise karta hai | Cu se neela ghol aur SO₂; C se CO₂ aur SO₂ | Cu + 2H₂SO₄ → CuSO₄ + SO₂ + 2H₂O |
| Non-volatile | apne se volatile acid ko namak se bhagata hai | HCl, HNO₃ ki lab preparation | NaCl + H₂SO₄ → NaHSO₄ + HCl |
| Drying agent | nami sokhta hai | HCl, Cl₂ ke liye; NH₃ ke liye nahi | koi equation nahi |

## Khud karke dekho: hazard label detective

(Koi chemical nahi: sirf padhna aur sochna, adult ke saath.)

Kisi adult ke saath ghar ke safai ke saaman, battery ya khaad ke packet ke label **bina kholein** padho. Dekho: kya "danger", "corrosive" ya khopdi wala chinh hai? Kaun se saaman par "sirf bade" likha hai? Ek kaagaz par table banao: saaman ka naam, chinh ka naam, aur kya likha hai. Bottle kholna, sunghna ya mila-julakar dekhna **bilkul nahi**.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Dilute aur gaadha H₂SO₄ ek jaise hi behave karte hain, bas gaadhe ka asar zyada hota hai."**
Ye sach lagta hai kyunki naam aur formula ek hai. Par dono ka **swabhaav hi alag** hai. Teen jaanch (sirf padhne ke liye ya teacher demo):

1. **Copper ke saath:** copper ka tukda dilute H₂SO₄ mein ho to **kuch nahi hota** (Class 9 aur Chapter 2). Wahi copper garam gaadhe H₂SO₄ mein: ghol **neela** hota hai aur **SO₂** nikalti hai. Ek ne reaction nahi diya, doosre ne di: swabhaav alag.
2. **Cheeni ke saath:** cheeni ko paani mein ghola to meethi chaashni (ghul gayi). Wahi cheeni gaadhe acid mein **kaali**. Dilute acid mein paani bahut hai, wo cheeni ko dehydrate nahi karta.
3. **Dhaatu aur gas ka sawaal:** dilute acid se reactive dhaatu (jaise Zn) **H₂** deti hai, par garam gaadhe acid ke saath copper ne **H₂ nahi, SO₂** di. Yaani gaadhe acid mein hydrogen wala raasta band, oxidise karne wala khul gaya.

| Sawaal | Dilute H₂SO₄ | Gaadha H₂SO₄ |
|---|---|---|
| Copper ke saath | koi reaction nahi | garam karne par CuSO₄ aur SO₂ |
| Cheeni ke saath | kuch nahi (cheeni ghulti hai) | kaala carbon, bhaap |
| Kaagaz par | halka asar | kaala, jalne jaisa daag |
| Khaas gun | acid wale ions (H₃O⁺) | paani chheenna, oxidise karna |

**Niyam:** Dilute = acid wala swabhaav (ions se). Gaadha = paani chheenna aur oxidise karna. **Ek jaisa maan lena khatarnak galti hai.**

**Galti 2: "Gaadhe acid ko dilute karna ho to paani ko acid mein daal do, ek hi baat hai."**
Chhote kaam mein ye aasaan lagta hai. Par **kabhi nahi.** Ek kahani: ek lab mein ek assistant ne jaldi mein paani ka mug gaadhe acid ke beaker mein ulta diya. Gaadha acid bhaari hai, paani **upar tairne laga**; saari garmi us patli parat par hui, paani ubal kar **acid ke chhinte** uchhal diye. Assistant ne goggles pehne the, isliye aankhein bachi. Sabak: wahi acid aur wahi paani, par **kaun kis mein jaata hai** wo faisla karta hai ki garmi faili ya jamma hogi. Isliye hamesha **"A" se pehle "P"**: **A**cid ko **P**aani mein (Figure 5.6).

**Niyam:** acid ko paani mein, dheere dheere, hilate hue; paani ko acid mein **kabhi nahi**. (Aur ye ek teacher ka kaam hai.)

## Quick Recap

- **Gaadha H₂SO₄** (lagbhag 98 percent, chipchipa, paani se bhaari) aur **dilute H₂SO₄** ke swabhaav **alag** hain: dilute = H₃O⁺ wale acid ke gun; gaadha = paani chheenna, oxidise karna, non-volatile, drying.
- **Dehydrating:** C₁₂H₂₂O₁₁ → 12C + 11H₂O (cheeni kaali, jhajhra stambh, SO₂ ki gandh); CuSO₄·5H₂O → CuSO₄ + 5H₂O (neela se safed); kaagaz, lakdi, kapda aur skin bhi paani chheenne se jalte hain.
- **Oxidising (garam, gaadha):** Cu + 2H₂SO₄ → CuSO₄ + SO₂ + 2H₂O; C + 2H₂SO₄ → CO₂ + 2SO₂ + 2H₂O. Hydrogen nahi nikalta, SO₂ nikalti hai.
- **Non-volatile:** gaadha acid HCl aur HNO₃ ko unke namak se bhaga deta hai (Topics 1 aur 3).
- **Drying agent:** HCl, Cl₂ ke liye theek; **NH₃ ke liye nahi** (acid-base reaction).
- **Dilution rule:** **acid ko paani mein**, dheere dheere, hilate hue: gaadha acid bhaari hai, paani upar tairta hai, to ulta karne par chhinte uchhalte hain.
- **Suraksha:** har demo sirf teacher ka, fume cupboard mein; SO₂ zehreeli hai; bachche kabhi na chhuen.
- **Galtiyan:** dilute aur gaadha ek jaise nahi; paani ko acid mein nahi.

## Practice Questions

(Sab sawaal paper par hal karne ke liye hain. Mass: H 1, C 12, O 16, Mg 24, S 32, Cu 63.5. Molar volume 22.4 L at STP.)

1. Glucose, C₆H₁₂O₆, is dehydrated completely by concentrated sulphuric acid in a teacher demo: C₆H₁₂O₆ → 6C + 6H₂O. Find the mass of carbon and of water formed from 9.0 g of glucose.
2. Cane sugar, C₁₂H₂₂O₁₁, is dehydrated in a teacher demo. For 17.1 g of sugar, find the mass of carbon left and the mass of water removed.
3. Treat paper as made of a repeating unit C₆H₁₀O₅ that is dehydrated completely: C₆H₁₀O₅ → 6C + 5H₂O. Find the masses of carbon and water formed from 32.4 g of paper.
4. A teacher dehydrates 7.485 g of blue vitriol, CuSO₄·5H₂O, with concentrated sulphuric acid. Find the mass of water removed, the mass of anhydrous copper(II) sulphate left, and its colour.
5. In a teacher demo, 12.7 g of copper is completely reacted with hot concentrated sulphuric acid: Cu + 2H₂SO₄ → CuSO₄ + SO₂ + 2H₂O. Find the mass of CuSO₄ formed, the volume of SO₂ at STP, and the mass of H₂SO₄ used up. Why must this demo be done in a fume cupboard?
6. Carbon (0.6 g) reacts completely with hot concentrated sulphuric acid: C + 2H₂SO₄ → CO₂ + 2SO₂ + 2H₂O. Find the total volume of gases (CO₂ and SO₂) at STP and the mass of acid used.
7. Anhydrous magnesium sulphate, MgSO₄, can also take up water to become MgSO₄·7H₂O: MgSO₄ + 7H₂O → MgSO₄·7H₂O. How much water can 6.0 g of MgSO₄ absorb, and what is the mass of the hydrated salt?
8. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) Dilute and concentrated sulphuric acid react with copper in the same way. (b) Sugar turns black with concentrated sulphuric acid because it burns. (c) Concentrated H₂SO₄ is a suitable drying agent for ammonia. (d) The hydrogen to oxygen ratio in sugar is the same as in water. (e) The acid should be poured slowly into water, not water into acid.
9. Name the property of concentrated H₂SO₄ shown in each: (a) blue crystals of CuSO₄·5H₂O turn white; (b) copper dissolves on heating and a pungent gas leaves; (c) a drop on paper leaves a black hole; (d) a wash bottle of acid makes a moist gas dry.
10. Why does dilute sulphuric acid not react with copper, but hot concentrated sulphuric acid does? Which gas is formed and which gas would have been formed if copper had behaved like zinc?
11. A student claims "pouring water into concentrated acid is the same as pouring acid into water because the final mixture is the same". Explain in 3 to 4 lines why the order matters even though the final mixture is the same.
12. Ask an adult and read (without opening) the label of a battery or cleaner. List two hazard words or symbols you find, and say why children must never open or mix such products.
13. An open bottle of concentrated sulphuric acid left on a shelf for weeks is found to be heavier and thinner (less syrupy). Explain which property causes this and what should have been done.

1. Glucose = 6 × 12 + 12 + 6 × 16 = 72 + 12 + 96 = **180** g/mol. Moles = 9.0 ÷ 180 = **0.05 mol**. Ratio 1 : 6 : 6, to carbon = 0.3 mol × 12 = **3.6 g**, paani = 0.3 mol × 18 = **5.4 g**. Jaanch: 3.6 + 5.4 = 9.0 g ✅; atoms C 6, H 12, O 6 dono taraf ✅.
2. Cheeni = **342** g/mol. Moles = 17.1 ÷ 342 = **0.05 mol**. Carbon = 12 × 0.05 = 0.6 mol × 12 = **7.2 g**. Paani = 11 × 0.05 = 0.55 mol × 18 = **9.9 g**. Jaanch: 7.2 + 9.9 = 17.1 g ✅.
3. C₆H₁₀O₅ = 72 + 10 + 80 = **162** g/mol. Moles = 32.4 ÷ 162 = **0.2 mol**. Carbon = 6 × 0.2 = 1.2 mol × 12 = **14.4 g**. Paani = 5 × 0.2 = 1.0 mol × 18 = **18.0 g**. Jaanch: 14.4 + 18.0 = 32.4 g ✅; atoms C 6, H 10, O 5 dono taraf ✅.
4. CuSO₄·5H₂O = **249.5** g/mol. Moles = 7.485 ÷ 249.5 = **0.03 mol**. Paani = 5 × 0.03 = 0.15 mol × 18 = **2.7 g**. CuSO₄ = 0.03 × 159.5 = **4.785 g**, rang **safed** (ya halka bhoora-safed). Jaanch: 2.7 + 4.785 = 7.485 g ✅.
5. Cu = 12.7 ÷ 63.5 = **0.2 mol**. Ratio 1 : 2 : 1 : 1. CuSO₄ = 0.2 × 159.5 = **31.9 g**. SO₂ = 0.2 × 22.4 = **4.48 L**. H₂SO₄ = 0.4 mol × 98 = **39.2 g**. Fume cupboard isliye ki **SO₂ zehreeli, gale aur aankh ko jalane wali** hai. Jaanch: 12.7 + 39.2 = 51.9 g; daayein 31.9 + (0.2 × 64 = 12.8) + (0.4 × 18 = 7.2) = 51.9 g ✅.
6. C = 0.6 ÷ 12 = **0.05 mol**. CO₂ 0.05 mol, SO₂ 0.1 mol, kul **0.15 mol × 22.4 = 3.36 L**. Acid = 0.1 mol × 98 = **9.8 g**. Jaanch: 0.6 + 9.8 = 10.4 g; daayein CO₂ 2.2 + SO₂ 6.4 + H₂O 1.8 = 10.4 g ✅.
7. MgSO₄ = 24 + 32 + 64 = **120** g/mol. Moles = 6.0 ÷ 120 = **0.05 mol**. Paani = 7 × 0.05 = 0.35 mol × 18 = **6.3 g**. Hydrated salt = 6.0 + 6.3 = **12.3 g**. Jaanch: MgSO₄·7H₂O = 120 + 126 = 246; 0.05 × 246 = 12.3 g ✅.
8. (a) **False** ❌: dilute copper se react nahi karta; garam gaadha karta hai. (b) **False** ❌: aag nahi thi; cheeni se **paani chheena gaya**, carbon bacha. (c) **False** ❌: NH₃ base hai, acid se react karti hai; CaO se sukhate hain. (d) **True** ✅: 2 : 1 (C₁₂H₂₂O₁₁ mein 22 : 11). (e) **True** ✅.
9. (a) **dehydrating** (paani chheenna); (b) **oxidising** (garam acid, Cu ko oxidise karta hai, SO₂ nikalti hai); (c) **dehydrating** (kaagaz se paani chheenna); (d) **drying agent**.
10. Cu reactivity series mein hydrogen se **neeche** hai, to dilute acid ke H₃O⁺ se H₂ nahi bana sakta. Garam gaadha H₂SO₄ **oxidising agent** hai: wo Cu ko oxidise karta hai aur khud **SO₂** mein badalta hai. Agar Cu zinc jaisa hota (dilute acid ke saath) to **H₂** banta.
11. Final mixture ek jaisa hai, par **garmi kahan nikalti hai** wo alag hai. Acid bhaari hai aur paani halka. Paani acid ke upar daalne par wo **patli sataha par tairta** hai aur saari garmi wahin nikalti hai: paani **ubal kar acid ke chhinte** uchhalta hai. Acid ko bahut paani mein dheere hilate hue daalne par garmi poore paani mein faili jaati hai. Isliye order matters.

Sirf bade sambhalte hain. (Koi bhi uttar jo label se do alag chinh de, theek hai.)
13. **Gaadhe H₂SO₄ ka drying (nami sokhne) wala gun**: wo hawa ki nami kheench leta hai, isliye mass badhta hai aur paani badhne se wo patla ho jaata hai. Bottle ko **kas kar band** rakhna chahiye tha.
