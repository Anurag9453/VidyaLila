# Topic 6: Sab Kuch Mila Kar: Practice Aur Chapter Ka Saar

Topic 1 se 5 mein humne chaar kirdaar jaane: **HCl, ammonia, nitric acid aur sulphuric acid**. Har ek ki apni banawat, apna swabhav aur apna khatra tha. Ab sabse zaroori kaam: inhe **ek saath rakh kar** dekhna, aur seekhi hui baaton ko naye sawaalon par lagana. Aaj hum ek master table banayenge, gas jama karne aur sukhane ka ek seedha tareeka seekhenge, "kaun sa compound?" ke puzzle hal karenge, aur chaar aam galtiyan pakdenge jo abhi tak nahi aayi thi.

## Compound Pehchaan: school ka quiz

Mr. Zagade apni bhatiji Durva ke school ke annual science quiz mein baithe the. Quiz ka naam tha **"Compound Pehchaan"**. Stage par ek anchor clue cards padh raha tha, aur har team ko sirf clue sunkar compound ka naam batana tha.

Pehla card: "Bina rang ki gas, tez chubhne wali gandh, gili laal litmus ko neela kar deti hai." Durva ki team ne turant haath uthaya: "Ammonia!" Doosra card: "Bina rang ki gas, nami wali hawa mein dhuan banati hai, AgNO₃ se safed curdy dhodha." Ye bhi sahi nikla: HCl. Teesre card par team atak gayi: "Bina rang ki gas, jali hui match-stick jaisi gandh, heavy hai, paani mein bahut ghulti hai." Durva ne kaha, "Chacha, mujhe lagta hai SO₂, par kaise pakka karun?"

Ghar jaate waqt Mr. Zagade ne kaha, "Durva, quiz sirf yaad karne ka khel nahi hai. Har clue ke peeche ek **kyun** hota hai. Durva ne poochha, "Aur agar kisi gas ko ek tarah se sukhaya aur wo kharab ho gayi?" Chacha muskuraye. "Yahi to aaj ka sabse mazedaar hissa hai."

- Is topic ka har gas, acid aur reaction **sirf padhne ke liye** hai, ya **sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo)** jo school lab mein teacher, fume cupboard, goggles aur gloves ke saath hota hai. Koi student kabhi inhe banata, chhoota, sungta ya test nahi karta.

## Part A: Chaaron kirdaar ek table mein

Pehla table **banana, jama karna aur sukhana** batata hai (Topic 1, 2, 3 aur 4). Sab teacher demo ya padhne ke liye.

| Compound | Lab mein kaise banta hai | Kaise jama hota hai | Kaise sukhaya jaata hai |
|---|---|---|---|
| HCl gas | NaCl + conc. H₂SO₄, halka garam (lagbhag 200 °C se neeche): NaCl + H₂SO₄ → NaHSO₄ + HCl | Upward displacement of air (heavy: VD 18.25); paani ke upar kabhi nahi, bahut ghulti hai | conc. H₂SO₄ se |
| NH₃ gas | 2NH₄Cl + Ca(OH)₂ → CaCl₂ + 2H₂O + 2NH₃; test tube halka neeche jhuki | Downward displacement of air (halki: VD 8.5); paani ke upar kabhi nahi | quicklime CaO se; conc. H₂SO₄ ya CaCl₂ se nahi |
| HNO₃ | KNO₃ + conc. H₂SO₄ → KHSO₄ + HNO₃, lagbhag 200 °C se neeche, sirf glass ki retort mein | Liquid hai: retort se distil hokar receiver mein aata hai | liquid hai: gas ki tarah sukhane ka sawaal nahi |
| H₂SO₄ | Lab mein nahi; factory mein Contact process | Liquid | Ye khud drying agent hai |

Doosra table **pehchaan, khatra aur factory ka tareeka** batata hai (Topic 1 se 5):

| Compound | Pehchaan | Khatra | Factory mein |
|---|---|---|---|
| HCl | Bina rang ki, nami mein dhuan, gili neeli litmus laal, NH₃ se safed dhuan, AgNO₃ se safed curdy dhodha (Chapter 8) | Gas gale aur aankh ko jalati hai; conc. acid skin jalata hai | H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl |
| NH₃ | Tez gandh, gili laal litmus neeli, HCl se safed dhuan | Aankh, naak, gale ko jalati hai; bleach ke saath kabhi nahi | N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ (Haber) |
| HNO₃ | Peela-bhoora (NO₂ ghula hai), Cu se bhoori NO₂, skin par peela daag | Corrosive; NO aur NO₂ zehreeli, acid rain | Ostwald process |
| conc. H₂SO₄ | Gaadhi chaashni jaisi; cheeni ko kaala karti hai; neela CuSO₄·5H₂O safed | Gambhir jalan; paani acid mein kabhi nahi | Contact process |

Ek baat dhyan dene layak: **conc. H₂SO₄ chaaron ko jodta hai**. Conc. H₂SO₄ se NaCl ne HCl diya, KNO₃ ne HNO₃ diya: ye "non-volatile acid" wala kaam Topic 5 mein tha.

## Part B: Gas ko jama karne aur sukhane ka seedha tareeka

Ek gas ke baare mein bas **do sawaal** poochho: (1) kya wo paani mein ghulti ya paani se react karti hai? (2) kya wo hawa se bhaari hai? Hawa ka VD lagbhag **14.5** hai (Chapter 5). Jis gas ka VD 14.5 se zyada, wo hawa se bhaari.

```
   Gas teacher ke saath lab mein banayi gayi
        :
        v
   Paani mein ghulti ya react karti hai?
        :                                      :
     nahi :                                 haan :
        v                                      v
   Paani ke upar jama karo            Hawa se bhaari hai? (VD > 14.5)
   (jaise Class 9 ki hydrogen)                 :                    :
                                         haan  :                    : nahi
                                               v                    v
                                  Upward displacement     Downward displacement
                                  of air: jar seedhi,     of air: jar ulti,
                                  tube jar ke tale tak    tube jar ke upar tak
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Gas jama karne ka faisla: pehle paani wala sawaal, phir hawa se bhaari ya halki. Jo gas paani mein bahut ghulti hai use paani ke upar kabhi jama nahi karte.

```
   (a) Bhaari gas: jar seedhi                (b) Halki gas: jar ulti

       gas aayi (generator se)                    ______________
             :                                   :      .       :  <- jar ka band sira
             :  <- delivery tube                 :      :       :     (halki gas yahin jama)
       hawa  :       hawa                        :      :       :
        ^    :        ^                          :      :       :
        :    :        :                          :      :       :
       :     :      :                            :      :       :
       :     :      :  <- jar ka mooh khula      :      :       :
       :     :      :                            :______:_______:
       :     :      :                             ^     ^     ^
       :  gas jama  :                       hawa neeche      delivery tube
       :............:                       se bahar         (neeche se andar, upar tak)
```

> **Figure 6.2** – (a) Bhaari gas (HCl, Cl₂, SO₂): tube jar ke tale tak jaati hai, gas neeche jama hoti hai aur hawa ko upar se bahar dhakelti hai. (b) Halki gas (NH₃): jar ulti hai, tube upar tak pahunchti hai, gas upar jama hoti hai aur hawa ko neeche mooh se bahar dhakelti hai.

### Sukhana: acidic gas ke liye acidic agent

Drying agent gas se react nahi karna chahiye. Chapter 1 ka niyam: **acidic padarth basic padarth se react karta hai**. Isliye acidic gas (HCl, Cl₂, SO₂, NO₂) conc. H₂SO₄ se sukhti hain, aur basic gas NH₃ quicklime CaO se. Bottle mein gas lambi tube se acid ke andar jaati hai aur chhoti tube se sookhi nikalti hai:

```
        geeli gas andar               sookhi gas bahar
              :                              ^
              :                              :
        .-----:------ cork (do ched) --------:-----.
        :     :                              :     :  <- chhoti tube: cork ke
        :     :                                    :     bas neeche khatam
        :     :                                    :
        :~~~~~:~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~:  <- conc. H₂SO₄ ka level
        :     :   o  o                             :
        :     :  (bulbule)                         :
        :     :                                    :
        :__________________________________________:
         gas wash bottle (lambi tube acid mein doobi)
```

> **Figure 6.3** – Gas wash bottle: geeli gas lambi tube se conc. H₂SO₄ mein bulbule banati hai, nami acid mein reh jaati hai, aur sookhi gas chhoti tube se bahar nikalti hai. Chhoti tube liquid ke upar rehti hai.

### Example 1. H₂S: kaise jama karein, kaise sukhayein?

**Sawaal:** H₂S zehreeli gas hai (sadi hui ande ki gandh), paani mein thodi ghulti hai. Teacher demo mein ise fume cupboard mein jama karna hai. VD nikaalo, jama karne ka tareeka aur drying agent batao. (H 1, S 32.)

**Kadam 1 (M):** H₂S = 2 × 1 + 32 = **34**. **Kadam 2 (VD):** 34 ÷ 2 = **17**.
**Kadam 3:** 17 > 14.5: hawa se bhaari. To **upward displacement of air**.
**Kadam 4 (sukhana):** H₂S ko conc. H₂SO₄ **nahi** sukha sakte: wo H₂S ko oxidise karke kharab kar deta hai. Koi doosra drying agent lena padta hai.

**Jaanch:** 17 > 14.5 ✅ (hawa se bhaari, isliye jar seedhi). H₂S acidic hone par bhi conc. H₂SO₄ se nahi sukhti: iska karan aage "aam galti" mein.

### Example 2. SO₂: jama karna aur sukhana

**Sawaal:** SO₂ bina rang ki, zehreeli gas hai, paani mein bahut ghulti hai. Jama karne aur sukhane ka tareeka batao. (S 32, O 16.)

M = 32 + 32 = **64**, VD = **32** > 14.5: bhaari. Paani mein bahut ghulti hai: paani ke upar nahi. To **upward displacement of air**, conc. H₂SO₄ se sukhi (acidic gas, acid se react nahi karti). Quicklime se nahi: wo bhi acidic gas ko khaa jaata hai (Chapter 1 ka niyam).

**Jaanch:** VD 32 > 14.5 ✅, conc. H₂SO₄ acidic-acidic jodi ✅.

## Part C: "Kaun sa compound?" ke puzzle

### Example 3. Clue cards se naam

**Sawaal:** Chaar clue cards. Naam batao (sabke liye facts card mein hain).

- **Card A:** peela-bhoora fuming liquid, copper se bhoori gas nikalti hai. 
- **Card B:** gaadhi chaashni jaisa liquid, cheeni ko kaala karta hai.
- **Card C:** bina rang ki gas, jar ko HCl ke paas laane par safed dhuan, halki hai (VD 8.5).
- **Card D:** bina rang ki gas, nami mein dhuan, VD 18.25, AgNO₃ se safed curdy dhodha.

**Hal:** A = **HNO₃** (peela rang NO₂ ghulne se; Cu + 4HNO₃ → Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2NO₂ + 2H₂O). B = **conc. H₂SO₄** (dehydrating agent). C = **NH₃**: NH₃ + HCl → NH₄Cl safed dhuan. D = **HCl**.

**Jaanch:** har card ka sirf ek hi compound ✅. Equation: Cu 1, H 4 = 4, N 4 = 2 + 2, O 12 = 6 + 4 + 2 ✅.

### Example 4. Teen jar, teen gas

**Sawaal:** Teen jar 1, 2, 3 mein HCl, NH₃ aur SO₂ hain (kisi order mein). Jar 1 gili neeli litmus ko laal karti hai aur AgNO₃ se safed curdy dhodha deti hai. Jar 2 gili laal litmus ko neela karti hai. Jar 3 gili neeli litmus ko laal karti hai par AgNO₃ se curdy dhodha nahi deti, jali match-stick ki gandh hai. Kaun kaun?

**Hal:** gili laal litmus ko neela sirf **NH₃** karti hai: **jar 2 = NH₃**. Curdy AgCl sirf Cl⁻ se: **jar 1 = HCl**. Bacha **jar 3 = SO₂**.

**Jaanch:** teen jar, teen alag gas, har clue sirf ek se mel khata hai ✅. (Tests ki teacher demo wali vidhi: Chapter 8.)

## Part D: Factory ka naqsha aur hisaab

```
   Hawa se N₂, paani/gas se H₂ : N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃      (Fe, lagbhag 200 atm, lagbhag 450 °C)
        ammonia ---(Pt-Rh, lagbhag 800 °C)--> NO ---(O₂)--> NO₂ ---(O₂, H₂O)--> HNO₃
                       4NH₃ + 5O₂ → 4NO + 6H₂O   2NO + O₂ → 2NO₂   4NO₂ + O₂ + 2H₂O → 4HNO₃

   Sulphur S ---(O₂)--> SO₂ ---(O₂, V₂O₅, lagbhag 450 °C)--> SO₃ ---(conc. H₂SO₄)--> oleum ---(H₂O)--> H₂SO₄

   Namak ka ghol (electrolysis) --> H₂ aur Cl₂ ---> H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl
```

> **Figure 6.4** – Chaaron compounds ka factory naqsha: Haber aur Ostwald (NH₃ se HNO₃ tak), Contact (S se H₂SO₄ tak), aur HCl ka seedha tareeka (Chapter 4 ke chlor-alkali se aaye H₂ aur Cl₂).

### Example 5. NH₃ se HNO₃, aur ek khaad

**Sawaal:** 0.85 g NH₃ se Ostwald process mein kitna HNO₃ banega? Phir utni hi aur NH₃ HNO₃ se milaayi (neutralisation) to kitna NH₄NO₃? (H 1, N 14, O 16.)

**Kadam 1:** 4NH₃ + 5O₂ → 4NO + 6H₂O, 2NO + O₂ → 2NO₂, 4NO₂ + O₂ + 2H₂O → 4HNO₃. Teeno ko jodein to 4 NH₃ se 4 HNO₃: ratio **1 : 1**.
**Kadam 2:** NH₃ = 17, moles = 0.85 ÷ 17 = **0.05 mol**. HNO₃ = 1 + 14 + 48 = 63, to 0.05 × 63 = **3.15 g**.
**Kadam 3:** NH₃ + HNO₃ → NH₄NO₃ (1 : 1). 0.05 mol NH₄NO₃, M = 14 + 4 + 14 + 48 = 80, mass = **4 g**.

**Jaanch:** 0.85 g NH₃ + 3.15 g HNO₃ = 4.0 g = NH₄NO₃ ✅ (mass bachta hai). Atoms: N 2, H 4, O 3 dono taraf ✅.

### Example 6. Chapter 5 ka volume wala tareeka

**Sawaal:** 7.1 g Cl₂ H₂ ke saath H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl se react karti hai (teacher ki dekh-rekh mein). HCl ka volume STP par aur mass? (Cl 35.5, H 1; molar volume 22.4 L.)

Cl₂ = 71, moles = 7.1 ÷ 71 = **0.1 mol**. Ratio Cl₂ : HCl = 1 : 2, to HCl = **0.2 mol**. Volume = 0.2 × 22.4 = **4.48 L**. Mass = 0.2 × 36.5 = **7.3 g**.

**Jaanch:** Cl₂ 7.1 g + H₂ (0.1 mol × 2 = 0.2 g) = 7.3 g = HCl ✅. H 2 = 2, Cl 2 = 2 ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Saari acidic gas conc. H₂SO₄ se sukh jaati hain."**
Demo (sirf padhne ke liye): teacher teen bottle ek ke baad ek lagata hai: HCl wali, SO₂ wali aur H₂S wali gas, har ek conc. H₂SO₄ wali gas wash bottle se guzarti hai.

| Gas | Conc. H₂SO₄ se guzarne par |
|---|---|
| HCl | sirf nami nikalti hai: sookhi HCl bahar aati hai |
| SO₂ | sirf nami nikalti hai: sookhi SO₂ bahar aati hai |
| H₂S | acid H₂S ko oxidise karta hai: peela sulphur banta hai, gas kharab |

H₂S bhi acidic gas hai, par conc. H₂SO₄ ek **oxidising** agent hai (Topic 5), isliye wo H₂S ko hi badal deta hai. **Niyam:** pehle dekho ki drying agent gas se kaise react karega, sirf acidic ya basic hona kaafi nahi. (HBr aur HI jaisi gas bhi isi se kharab ho jaati hain: sirf naam.)

**Galti 2: "Ek hi acid har metal ke saath ek jaisa react karta hai."**
Demo (sirf kaagaz par, padhne ke liye): Zn aur Cu ke chaar acid ke saath natije.

| Metal + acid | Kya hota hai |
|---|---|
| Zn + dilute HCl | Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂ (hydrogen) |
| Cu + dilute HCl | koi reaction nahi (Cu hydrogen se neeche hai: Class 9) |
| Cu + conc. HNO₃ | Cu + 4HNO₃ → Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2NO₂ + 2H₂O (bhoori gas, H₂ nahi) |
| Cu + conc. H₂SO₄ (garam) | Cu + 2H₂SO₄ → CuSO₄ + SO₂ + 2H₂O (SO₂, H₂ nahi) |

Cu ek hi hai, par acid badalte hi ya to kuch nahi hota ya alag gas milti hai. **Gas dono par nirbhar karti hai: metal aur acid.** Jaanch: Cu + 4HNO₃: H 4 = 4, N 4 = 2 + 2, O 12 = 6 + 4 + 2 ✅.

**Galti 3: "Jo gas paani mein ghulti ho use bhi paani ke upar jama kar sakte hain."**
Demo: SO₂ paani mein lagbhag 40 guna ghulti hai (1 L paani lagbhag 40 L SO₂). 5 L paani ka trough **5 × 40 = 200 L** SO₂ nigal sakta hai. Teacher ki 1 L ki jar to kuch bhi nahi: gas jar mein pahunchne se pehle hi paani mein ghul jaati hai, jar bharti hi nahi, aur paani khatta ho jaata hai. Upar se paani ke tube mein wapas khinchne (suck-back) ka khatra bhi. Cl₂ bhi thodi ghulti aur react karti hai. HCl aur NH₃ to aur bhi zyada ghulti hain (Topic 1 aur 2). **Niyam:** ghulne wali gas hamesha hawa ko dhakel kar, paani ke upar sirf H₂ jaisi na ghulne wali gas.

**Galti 4: "Catalyst product ki matra badha deta hai."**
Demo: Contact process mein 2SO₂ + O₂ ⇌ 2SO₃. 128 g SO₂ (2 mol) se equation ke hisaab se zyada se zyada 2 mol SO₃ = 160 g ban sakte hain (reversible hone se asal mein thoda kam). Do plant, ek hi taapmaan aur dabaav: A mein V₂O₅ (5 g), B mein nahi. Dono mein aakhir mein SO₃ ki **matra ek jaisi** hoti hai, par A mein wo jaldi milti hai; B mein wahi matra tak pahunchne mein bahut der lagti hai. Plant band karke V₂O₅ tola: **5 g hi**, ghista nahi. Catalyst ne sirf **raftaar** badhayi, na SO₃ ki matra badli, na khud kharch hua.

## Quick Recap

- Chaar kirdaar: HCl (NaCl + conc. H₂SO₄; H₂ + Cl₂), NH₃ (NH₄Cl + Ca(OH)₂; Haber), HNO₃ (KNO₃ + conc. H₂SO₄; Ostwald), H₂SO₄ (Contact).
- Gas jama karna: paani mein ghulti hai? phir hawa se bhaari (VD > 14.5)? Bhaari: upward displacement; halki: downward displacement.
- Sukhana: acidic gas conc. H₂SO₄ se (par H₂S, HBr, HI nahi), NH₃ CaO se.
- Pehchaan clue se: rang, gandh, dhuan, litmus, AgNO₃ (Chapter 8).
- Metal aur acid ka natija dono par nirbhar: Zn aur Cu alag.
- Ghulne wali gas paani ke upar nahi.
- Catalyst sirf raftaar badhata hai, matra nahi.
- Sab gas aur acid sirf padhne ke liye ya teacher demo.

## Practice Questions

(Sab sawaal paper par hal karne ke liye. Mass: H 1, C 12, N 14, O 16, S 32, Cl 35.5; Br 80 aur I 127 sirf sawaal 2 ke liye. Molar volume 22.4 L at STP, 6.022 × 10²³ per mole. Hawa ka VD lagbhag 14.5.)

1. Chlorine, Cl₂, is a poisonous greenish-yellow gas that dissolves partly in water. Using its vapour density from Chapter 5 (35.5), say how a teacher would collect it and which drying agent is suitable. Why not over water?
2. HBr and HI (names only) are gases like HCl. Using Br 80 and I 127, find the vapour density of each. Concentrated H₂SO₄ oxidises both. Which drying agent is wrong for them, and how would they be collected?
3. Four jars P, Q, R, S hold Cl₂, SO₂, H₂S and NO₂. P is reddish-brown. Q smells like rotten eggs. R is greenish-yellow. S is colourless with a burning-sulphur smell. Name each, and say which of the four cannot be dried with concentrated H₂SO₄.
4. For 2NO + O₂ → 2NO₂ at STP, 4.48 L of NO is converted. Find the volume of O₂ needed, and the mass and volume of NO₂ formed.
5. Find the number of moles and molecules in 13.8 g of NO₂ (use 46 g/mol), and its volume at STP.
6. Urea, CO(NH₂)₂, 12 g. Find moles of urea, number of molecules, and the total number of atoms.
7. A sack is labelled ammonium phosphate (name only). Which ion is shown by warming with NaOH, which gas comes out, and how is it tested? Mention the safety rule.
8. Arrange Cl₂, SO₂, H₂S, NO₂, HBr and HI in order of increasing vapour density. Which would be collected by downward displacement of air?
9. True or False, correct the false: (a) all acidic gases can be dried with conc. H₂SO₄. (b) A catalyst increases the mass of product. (c) NO₂ can be collected over water. (d) Cl₂ is lighter than air. (e) HI can be dried by conc. H₂SO₄.
10. In a plant, a reaction gives 6.9 g NO₂ in 2 hours without a catalyst and the same 6.9 g in 10 minutes with one. What changed and what did not? How many moles is 6.9 g?
11. A leak of SO₂ and H₂S occurs in a lab. Explain, with vapour densities, why ventilation near the floor matters.
12. Describe two safe, paper-only activities for a student to revise this chapter, and say why no chemical is used.

### Answers

1. M = 71, VD = **35.5** > 14.5: bhaari. **Upward displacement of air**, conc. H₂SO₄ se sukhayi (acidic gas). Paani ke upar nahi, kyunki wo ghulti aur thodi react karti hai.
2. HBr: 80 + 1 = 81, VD = **40.5**. HI: 127 + 1 = 128, VD = **64**. **Conc. H₂SO₄ galat** (wo oxidise karta hai). Dono hawa se bhaari: upward displacement of air. Jaanch: dono > 14.5 ✅.
3. P = **NO₂**, Q = **H₂S**, R = **Cl₂**, S = **SO₂**. **H₂S** conc. H₂SO₄ se nahi sukhti (wo use oxidise karta hai).
