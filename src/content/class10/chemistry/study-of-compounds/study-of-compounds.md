## Toilet cleaner ki bottle aur pet ka acid

Mr. Dabholkar apni bhanji Vaishnavi ke saath ghar ke bathroom mein khade the. Shelf par toilet cleaner ki ek bottle rakhi thi. Vaishnavi ne dekha ki label par do nishaan bane hain: ek khopdi-haddi ka, aur ek aisa jisme haath aur dhaatu par acid ki boondein girti dikhti hain. Neeche chhote akshar mein likha tha: "sirf bade".

"Mama, ye nishaan kya kehte hain?" Mr. Dabholkar ne bottle ko shelf se uthaye bina label padha. "Pehla nishaan: zehreela ya bahut hanikarak. Doosra: **corrosive**, yaani chamdi, aankh aur dhaatu ko jalane wala. Aur likha hai: 'bachchon se door rakhein', 'kisi aur safai wale ya bleach ke saath kabhi na milayein'." "Aur andar kya hai?" "Label par ek acid ka naam hai: **hydrochloric acid**. Is bottle ko hum nahi kholenge, aur tum bhi kabhi nahi khologi."

Vaishnavi thodi der chup rahi, phir boli, "Mama, par school mein padha tha ki hamare pet mein bhi hydrochloric acid hota hai. Jo toilet saaf karta hai, wahi mere andar? Phir mera pet kyun nahi jalta?" Mr. Dabholkar muskuraye. "Bahut achha sawaal. Jawab ke kuch hisse hain: bottle ka acid pet ke acid se kai guna **gaadha** hota hai, aur pet ki deewar par ek chikni parat hoti hai jo use bachati hai. Par ek aur baat hai: jise hum 'HCl' kehte hain wo ek **gas** bhi hai aur ek **ghol** bhi. Chalo, aaj dono ki poori kahani dekhte hain."

## Part A: HCl gas aur hydrochloric acid: ek cheez ke do roop

**Hydrogen chloride** ek gas hai jiske molecule mein ek H aur ek Cl covalent bond se jude hain: H–Cl. Jab ye gas paani mein ghulti hai to **hydrochloric acid** banta hai. Class 9 mein H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl aaya tha ([hydrogen-part4.md](../../../class9/chemistry/hydrogen/hydrogen-part4.md): "paani mein ghul kar hydrochloric acid"). Ab dono roop ek-ek karke dekhte hain.

**Gas roop ke gun (sirf padhne ke liye):**
- **Bina rang ki**, **tez, dam ghutane wali** gandh (teacher bhi use seedha nahi sunghta).
- **Hawa se bhaari:** vapour density 18.25, hawa ka lagbhag 14.5 (Chapter 5, Topic 2).
- **Bahut zyada ghulansheel:** ek litre paani mein lagbhag 450 litre HCl gas ghul jaati hai.
- **Nami se dhuan-sa:** khuli hawa mein HCl gas hawa ki nami se milkar acid ki chhoti-chhoti boondein bana deti hai, jo safed kohre ki tarah dikhti hain. Wo gas ka dhuan nahi, acid ki boondein hain.
- **Geele blue litmus ko laal** karti hai (sookhe ko nahi: Aam galtiyan mein dekhenge).

**Ghol roop ke gun:** Chapter 1 mein dekha tha ki HCl ek **strong acid** hai: paani mein lagbhag poori tarah H₃O⁺ aur Cl⁻ mein tut jaati hai. Isliye wo saare acid wale kaam karti hai: metals ke saath H₂ (Chapter 2), carbonates ke saath CO₂ (Chapter 1), bases ke saath namak aur paani. Ye pehle ho chuka hai, yahan dobara nahi. Metals ke saath bani chlorides mein ek **ZnCl₂** hai: iska hum sirf naam yaad rakhte hain.

Bazaar ka **concentrated** hydrochloric acid lagbhag 36 percent HCl ka ghol hota hai aur khuli botal mein dhuan-sa chhodta hai.

### Example 1. HCl hawa se kitni bhaari?

**Sawaal:** HCl ka vapour density nikalo aur hawa se uska anupaat batao. (H 1, Cl 35.5; hawa ka average molar mass 29.)

**Kadam 1:** HCl ka molar mass = 1 + 35.5 = **36.5** g/mol.
**Kadam 2:** Vapour density = molar mass ÷ 2 = 36.5 ÷ 2 = **18.25**. Hawa ka = 29 ÷ 2 = **14.5** (dono Chapter 5 ki VD table mein the; naya kadam agla hai).
**Kadam 3:** Barabar T aur P par barabar volume ke vazan ka anupaat = 36.5 ÷ 29 = lagbhag **1.26**. HCl hawa se lagbhag 1.26 guna bhaari hai.

**Jaanch:** 18.25 ÷ 14.5 = 1.26 bhi aata hai ✅ (dono raaste ek hi jawab).

## Part B: Lab mein HCl gas kaise banti hai (sirf padhne ke liye)

**Kachcha maal:** namak **NaCl** aur **concentrated H₂SO₄**. Concentrated H₂SO₄ **non-volatile** hai: uska ubal-bindu bahut ooncha hai, to garam hone par bhi wo gas ban kar nahi udta. Isliye jab wo NaCl se milta hai to jo gas ban kar nikalti hai wo **HCl** hi hai.

**Halki garmi par (lagbhag 200 °C se neeche):** NaCl + H₂SO₄ (concentrated) → NaHSO₄ + HCl

**Zyada garmi par doosra kadam:** NaHSO₄ + NaCl → Na₂SO₄ + HCl

Dono kadam jodo to **kul reaction:** 2NaCl + H₂SO₄ → Na₂SO₄ + 2HCl

Lab mein aam taur par halki garmi mein pehla kadam hi chalta hai. Chapter 1 ke teacher demo mein bhi sookhi HCl gas isi tarah NaCl aur conc. H₂SO₄ se bani thi (wahan seedha kul equation likhi thi).

```
          \       /     <-- thistle funnel (conc. H₂SO₄ isse daalte hain)
           \     /
            \   /
             : :
       ===cork===: :===cork===.________________  delivery tube: HCl gas yahan se
       :         : :          :                   agle bartan ki taraf jaati hai
       :         : :          :
      /          : :           \
     :           : :            :
     :   ~~~~~~~~: :~~~~~~~~~   :  <-- NaCl + conc. H₂SO₄ (funnel ki nali acid ke andar)
     :      (NaCl + acid)       :
      \                        /
       \______________________/
            round-bottom flask
                  ^
                  :  halki aanch (burner)
```

> **Figure 1.1** – HCl ka generator: flask mein thos NaCl hai; thistle funnel se conc. H₂SO₄ daalte hain (funnel ki nali ka neeche ka sira acid ke andar dooba rehta hai, taaki gas funnel se bahar na nikle). Cork mein se delivery tube nikalti hai jo agle bartan tak jaati hai. Halki aanch lagti hai. Delivery tube aage khuli hai, isliye apparatus band nahi hai.

```
flask se aayi       chhoti nali: sukhi gas bahar
delivery tube       jaati hai
      :      +-------------------------------------+
      :      :                                     :
   ===:=cork=:===                                  :            <-- jar ka muh khula:
   :  :         :                                  :                hawa yahan se nikalti hai
   :  :         :                       :          :          :
   :  :         :                       :          :          :
   :~~:~~~~~~~~~:                       :          : nali jar :
   :  :         :                       :HCl gas   : ke tale  :
   :  :conc.    :                       :jar mein  : tak      :
   :  :H₂SO₄    :                       :neeche se :          :
   :  :         :                       :bharti hai:          :
   \____________/                       :          :          :
                                        :          :          :
  wash bottle                           :                     :
  (gas sukhaane ke liye)                :                     :
                                        \_____________________/
                                     gas jar (seedha khada)
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Gas ko sukhana aur ikattha karna. Wash bottle mein conc. H₂SO₄ hai: **lambi nali** (acid ke andar dooba sira) se geeli gas andar aati hai aur acid ke bulbulon se guzarkar sookhti hai; **chhoti nali** (cork ke bas neeche) se sukhi gas bahar jaati hai. Wahan se nali jar ke **tale tak** jaati hai: HCl bhaari hai, to jar mein neeche se bharti hai aur halki hawa upar ke khule muh se nikal jaati hai. Poora set-up fume cupboard mein.

**Har cheez kyun?**
- **Sukhana kyun?** Flask se nikalne wali gas mein nami hoti hai. **Concentrated H₂SO₄** paani ko kheench leta hai aur HCl se koi reaction nahi karta: isliye wash bottle mein wahi lete hain. (Chapter 1 mein teacher ne **anhydrous CaCl₂** wali guard tube se bhi sukhaya tha: wo bhi HCl ke saath reaction nahi karta, to wo bhi chalta hai.)
- **Dilute H₂SO₄ kyun nahi?** Usme paani hota hai, aur HCl itni ghulansheel hai ki turant ghul jaati, gas nikalti hi nahi.
- **Delivery tube jar ke tale tak kyun?** Taaki bhaari HCl neeche jam kar halki hawa ko upar se dhakel de.

### Example 2. Teen equations ka atom count

**Sawaal:** Check karo ki teeno equations balanced hain, aur dikhao ki kul reaction dono kadamon ka jod hai.

Kadam 1: NaCl + H₂SO₄ → NaHSO₄ + HCl. Kadam 2: NaHSO₄ + NaCl → Na₂SO₄ + HCl.
Dono ko jodo: NaCl + H₂SO₄ + NaHSO₄ + NaCl → NaHSO₄ + HCl + Na₂SO₄ + HCl. Dono taraf NaHSO₄ ek-ek baar hai, wo kat jaata hai (beech ka padarth). Bachta hai **2NaCl + H₂SO₄ → Na₂SO₄ + 2HCl**.

**Jaanch:** Kadam 1: Na 1 = 1, Cl 1 = 1, H 2 = 1 + 1, S 1 = 1, O 4 = 4 ✅. Kadam 2: Na 2 = 2, H 1 = 1, S 1 = 1, O 4 = 4, Cl 1 = 1 ✅. Kul: Na 2 = 2, Cl 2 = 2, H 2 = 2, S 1 = 1, O 4 = 4 ✅.

### Example 3. 58.5 g NaCl se kitni HCl?

**Sawaal:** Teacher ne 58.5 g NaCl par conc. H₂SO₄ ki theek utni matra daali jo pehle kadam ke liye chahiye (halki garmi). Kitni HCl banegi (mass aur STP par volume) aur kitna H₂SO₄ lagega? (Na 23, S 32, O 16.)

**Kadam 1 (moles):** NaCl = 23 + 35.5 = 58.5 g/mol, to moles = 58.5 ÷ 58.5 = **1 mol**.
**Kadam 2 (ratio):** NaCl : H₂SO₄ : HCl = 1 : 1 : 1, to HCl = **1 mol**, H₂SO₄ = **1 mol**.
**Kadam 3 (mass):** HCl = 1 × 36.5 = **36.5 g**. H₂SO₄ = 2 + 32 + 64 = 98, to **98 g**.
**Kadam 4 (volume):** STP par 1 mol = 22.4 L, to HCl = **22.4 L** (Chapter 5, Topic 5).

**Jaanch:** mass sangrakshan: reactants 58.5 + 98 = 156.5 g; products NaHSO₄ (23 + 1 + 32 + 64 = 120 g) + HCl 36.5 g = 156.5 g ✅.

## Part C: Kaise ikattha karein aur kaise ghulayein

Gas ikattha karne ka tareeka gas ke do gun se tay hota hai: wo hawa se **kitni bhaari** hai aur paani mein **kitni ghulansheel** hai.

| Gas ka gun | Collection ka tareeka | HCl par lagu? |
|---|---|---|
| Hawa se **bhaari** | Upward displacement of air: jar seedha khada, nali jar ke tale tak | **Haan**: HCl hawa se lagbhag 1.26 guna bhaari |
| Hawa se **halki** | Downward displacement of air: jar ulta, nali upar tak | Nahi |
| Paani mein **bahut kam** ghulansheel | Paani ke upar (jar paani mein ulta) | **Nahi**: HCl bahut ghulansheel hai |

HCl ko paani ke upar ikattha karne ki koshish karte to wo paani mein ghul jaati aur jar kabhi nahi bharta. Isliye HCl hamesha **hawa ke upward displacement** se, paani ke upar kabhi nahi.

### Hydrochloric acid banana: ulta funnel

HCl ka ghol banane ke liye nali ko seedha paani mein nahi dubote, kyunki gas itni tezi se ghulti hai ki paani nali mein ulta khinch kar garam flask tak pahunch sakta hai (**suck-back**). Isliye ek **ulta funnel** lagate hain:

```
               :   :   <-- rubber tube se HCl gas aati hai
               :   :
              _:___:_
              \     /
               \   /         <-- ulta funnel: patli nali upar, chauda mooh neeche
                \ /
   ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~  <-- paani ki satah: funnel ka kinara bas paani ko chhoota hai
   :                            :
   :     paani (beaker mein)    :
   :    HCl yahan ghulti hai    :
   :____________________________:
              beaker
```

> **Figure 1.3** – HCl ko paani mein ghulana: ulta funnel ka chauda kinara paani ki satah ko bas chhoota hai. Funnel ke andar paani thoda upar chadh sakta hai, par tab beaker ki satah neeche utarti hai, funnel ka kinara paani se bahar aa jaata hai, hawa andar aa jaati hai aur paani wapas gir jaata hai: suck-back nahi hota.

## Part D: Fountain experiment (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo)

Ek mazedaar demo se pata chalta hai ki HCl kitni zyada ghulansheel hai. Teacher ek **bilkul sookha** round-bottom flask HCl gas se bharta hai (mote kaanch ka, safety screen ke peeche). Flask ko ulta karke clamp mein lagate hain: gardan neeche, cork mein do nali. Ek lambi nali ka sira flask ke andar upar tak hai aur doosra sira beaker ke neele litmus wale paani mein. Doosri nali ke saath ek chhota dropper hai jisme thoda paani bhara hai.

```
            _____________
          /               \
         :          ^      :    <-- jet: lambi nali ka upar ka sira (flask ke top ke paas)
         :          :      :        yahin se paani ka fuwwara phootta hai
         : sukhi    :      :
         : HCl gas  :      :
          \         :     /
            \_      :   _/
              :     : :
              : :   : :         <-- flask ulta lagaya hai: bulb upar, gardan neeche
              : :   : :
          ======:===:======     <-- cork (===): isme se do nali guzarti hain
                :   :
              (     )
              (paani)           <-- dropper: dabane par kuch boondein paani flask mein
              (     )
             rubber bulb
                    :
      ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~:~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
      :             :     <-- lambi nali ka neeche ka sira paani mein dooba
      :             :                         :
      :   paani + neela litmus                :
      :                                       :
      :                                       :
      :_______________________________________:
                      beaker
```

> **Figure 1.4** – HCl ka fountain demo. Ulte flask mein sukhi HCl gas hai. Dropper dabane par kuch boondein paani andar jaati hain aur gas turant ghulne lagti hai. Flask ke andar ka dabao gir jaata hai, to bahar ki hawa ka dabao beaker ke paani ko lambi nali se upar dhakelta hai aur wo jet se fuwwara banke phoot padta hai. Neela litmus laal ho jaata hai.

**Kya dikhta hai:** dropper dabate hi paani lambi nali mein tezi se chadhta hai, jet se fuwwara banke flask mein phootta hai aur **neela litmus laal ho jaata hai**, kyunki ghola hua HCl acid hai. Flask **sookha** isliye hona chahiye ki pehle se nami hoti to gas pehle hi ghul chuki hoti aur dabao nahi girta.

### Example 4. Kitna paani kaafi hai?

**Sawaal:** 500 mL ke sookhe flask mein HCl gas bhari hai. Maan lo 1 mL paani mein 450 mL HCl ghulti hai. Lagbhag kitna paani flask ki saari gas ghulane ke liye kaafi hai?

**Kadam 1:** 450 mL gas ke liye 1 mL paani chahiye.
**Kadam 2:** 500 mL gas ke liye paani = 500 ÷ 450 = lagbhag **1.1 mL**.
**Kadam 3:** Matlab dropper ki thodi si boondein kaafi hain: isi wajah se turant dabao gir jaata hai.

**Jaanch:** 1.1 × 450 = 495 mL, jo 500 mL ke kareeb hai ✅.

## Part E: HCl ki pehchaan aur ammonia ke saath safed dhuan

HCl ke teen aasaan tests hain (sab teacher ke saath ya sirf padhne ke liye):

1. **Geela blue litmus laal** hota hai.
2. **Ammonia ke saath safed ghana dhuan:** jab conc. HCl ki geeli kaanch ki rod aur conc. ammonia ki geeli rod ek-doosri ke paas laate hain, beech ki hawa mein safed dhuan ban jaata hai: NH₃ + HCl → NH₄Cl (thos ammonium chloride ke chhote kan).
3. **Silver nitrate ke saath safed curdy dhodha:** HCl ke ghol mein Cl⁻ hai, to AgNO₃ se AgCl banta hai. HCl + AgNO₃ → AgCl↓ + HNO₃ (silver nitrate zehreela hai: sirf teacher demo; Chapter 8, Topic 4; wahan dilute HNO₃ se pehle acidify karna aur NH₄OH mein ghulne ki baat hai: yahan dobara nahi).

```
      geeli rod (conc. HCl)             geeli rod (conc. ammonia)
                \                                /
                 \       . o   .  o  .          /
                  \    . safed dhuan  o  .     /
                   \     (NH₄Cl ke kan)       /
```

> **Figure 1.5** – Do geeli rods ke sire paas aane par beech mein safed dhuan banta hai: gas HCl aur gas NH₃ milkar thos NH₄Cl ke chhote kan banate hain. Ye teacher demo hai: dono bahut tez gandh wale hain.

### Example 5. HCl aur NH₃ ka hisaab

**Sawaal:** 18.25 g HCl poori tarah ammonia se milti hai (NH₃ + HCl → NH₄Cl). Kitna NH₃ lagega aur kitna NH₄Cl banega? (N 14, H 1, Cl 35.5.)

**Kadam 1:** HCl = 18.25 ÷ 36.5 = **0.5 mol**. Ratio 1 : 1 : 1.
**Kadam 2:** NH₃ = 0.5 × 17 = **8.5 g**. NH₄Cl (14 + 4 + 35.5 = 53.5) = 0.5 × 53.5 = **26.75 g**.

**Jaanch:** 18.25 + 8.5 = 26.75 g ✅ (mass sangrakshan). Atoms: N 1, H 4, Cl 1 dono taraf ✅.

## Part F: Factory mein HCl kaise banti hai

Industry mein HCl ka sabse seedha tareeka: **hydrogen aur chlorine ko seedha jodna.** Class 9 mein H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl dekha tha. Chapter 4 (Electrolysis, Topic 5) mein chlor-alkali process se brine ke electrolysis se **Cl₂ aur H₂ dono** bante hain. Wahi dono gas ek burner mein jalaye jaate hain: H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl. Banne wali HCl gas ko paani mein ghulakar gaadha hydrochloric acid milta hai. Ye safal tareeka isliye hai ki dono kachche maal ek hi plant se mil jaate hain.

```
   Brine (namak ka gaadha ghol)
        :
        v
   Electrolysis (chlor-alkali, Chapter 4)
        :
        +--> Cl₂ (anode par) -------+
        :                           v
        +--> H₂ (cathode par) --> burner mein jalana: H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl (gas)
        :                           :
        +--> NaOH (alag se)         v
                            paani mein ghulana (absorption)
                                    :
                                    v
                      gaadha hydrochloric acid (lagbhag 36 percent)
```

> **Figure 1.6** – HCl ka industrial rasta: brine ke electrolysis se Cl₂ aur H₂ bante hain (NaOH alag), dono burner mein milkar HCl gas dete hain, aur gas ko paani mein ghulakar gaadha acid banta hai.

### Example 6. Volume aur mass dono se

**Sawaal:** 10 L H₂ aur 10 L Cl₂ (barabar T aur P par) se kitni HCl banegi (volume)? Aur 2 g H₂ jalne par kitni HCl (mass)? (H 1, Cl 35.5.)

**Kadam 1 (volume):** H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl mein ratio 1 : 1 : 2. Barabar T aur P par gas ke volume ka ratio mole ratio hota hai (Chapter 5, Topic 5), to 10 L + 10 L → **20 L HCl** (volume badla nahi: 20 L bachti hai).
**Kadam 2 (mass):** 2 g H₂ = 1 mol. Chahiye 1 mol Cl₂ = 71 g. HCl = 2 mol = 2 × 36.5 = **73 g**.

**Jaanch:** 2 + 71 = 73 g ✅.

## Part G: HCl kahan kaam aati hai

### Example 7. Pet ka acid kitna halka hai?

**Sawaal:** Pet ke ras mein lagbhag 0.5 percent HCl hota hai. 250 g ras mein kitni HCl hogi? Aur bazaar ka gaadha acid (lagbhag 36 percent) pet ke ras se lagbhag kitna guna gaadha hai?

**Kadam 1:** 0.5 percent matlab 100 g mein 0.5 g, to 250 g mein 250 × 0.5 ÷ 100 = **1.25 g HCl**.
**Kadam 2:** Gaadhapan ka anupaat = 36 ÷ 0.5 = **72 guna** (lagbhag).

**Jaanch:** 1.25 ÷ 250 × 100 = 0.5 percent ✅. Isliye Vaishnavi ki bottle ka acid pet ke acid se kai guna tez hota hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti: "HCl gas apne aap mein acidic hai, to sookhi HCl gas bhi neele litmus ko laal karegi."**
Ye sach lagta hai kyunki HCl ka naam hi acid hai. Par acid wala asar (H₃O⁺) **paani ke saath** aata hai. Chapter 1 ke teacher demo ko ab Chapter 7 ki nazar se dekhte hain (teacher demo, sirf dekhne ke liye):

1. **Sookhi HCl gas, sookha neela litmus:** koi badlav nahi. Sookhi gas mein H–Cl molecules hain: bond covalent hai, koi khula H⁺ nahi.
2. **Wahi gas, par litmus par paani ki ek boond:** litmus turant laal. Paani ke molecule polar hain aur HCl ke polar bond ko tod dete hain: HCl + H₂O → H₃O⁺ + Cl⁻ (Chapter 7, Topic 2). Ab H₃O⁺ hai, to asar dikha.
3. **Khuli hawa mein HCl ka safed kohra:** ye bhi isi wajah se: gas ne hawa ki nami se paani liya aur acid ki boondein ban gayeen.

```
   Case 1: sookhi HCl gas + sookha neela litmus  :  neela hi rehta hai
   Case 2: sookhi HCl gas + geela neela litmus   :  laal (paani ne H₃O⁺ banaya)
   Case 3: HCl ka ghol + sookha neela litmus     :  laal (ghol mein H₃O⁺ pehle se hai)
```

> **Figure 1.7** – Sookhi HCl gas akeli litmus ko laal nahi karti; ghol mein ya paani ke saath hi acid wala asar aata hai.

Niyam: **HCl ka asar paani ke saath hi dikhta hai.** Isliye pet mein, toilet cleaner mein aur fountain demo ke ghol mein, sab jagah paani maujood hai.

## Quick Recap

- **HCl gas** bina rang ki, tez gandh wali, hawa se lagbhag 1.26 guna bhaari (vapour density 18.25 vs hawa ka 14.5) aur bahut ghulansheel hai; nami se safed kohra banati hai.
- **Hydrochloric acid** HCl gas ka paani mein ghol hai; strong acid; bazaar ka gaadha acid lagbhag 36 percent; pet mein lagbhag 0.5 percent.
- **Lab:** NaCl + H₂SO₄ (conc.) → NaHSO₄ + HCl (halki garmi, lagbhag 200 °C se neeche); zyada garmi par NaHSO₄ + NaCl → Na₂SO₄ + HCl; kul 2NaCl + H₂SO₄ → Na₂SO₄ + 2HCl.
- Gas ko **conc. H₂SO₄** se sukhate hain aur **upward displacement of air** se ikattha karte hain (paani ke upar kabhi nahi).
- Acid banane ke liye **ulta funnel**, taaki suck-back na ho.
- **Fountain demo:** sookhe flask mein dropper se thoda paani; gas ghulti hai, dabao girta hai, paani fuwwara banke chadhta hai, litmus laal.
- **Tests:** geela blue litmus laal; NH₃ ke saath safed dhuan (NH₄Cl); AgNO₃ ke saath safed curdy dhodha (Chapter 8).
- **Industry:** H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl (chlor-alkali ki H₂ aur Cl₂). **Upyog:** pickling, toilet cleaner (dilute), chlorides, pet ka acid.
- **Galti se bacho:** sookhi HCl gas litmus ko laal nahi karti: paani chahiye.
- Sab kuch teacher ke saath ya sirf padhne ke liye; toilet cleaner kabhi bleach ke saath nahi.

## Practice Questions

(Mass: H 1, N 14, O 16, Na 23, S 32, Cl 35.5, K 39, Ca 40, Ag 108. 1 mol gas = 22.4 L at STP.)

1. Potassium chloride, KCl, is warmed gently with the exact amount of concentrated H₂SO₄ needed for the first step (KCl + H₂SO₄ → KHSO₄ + HCl). If 44.7 g of KCl is used, find the mass of HCl formed, its volume at STP and the mass of H₂SO₄ needed.
2. For 23.4 g of NaCl and the exact amount of H₂SO₄ for the overall reaction 2NaCl + H₂SO₄ → Na₂SO₄ + 2HCl, find the mass and the volume at STP of HCl, and the mass of Na₂SO₄ formed. Check by conservation of mass.
3. How much NaCl and how much H₂SO₄ (in grams, by the overall equation) are needed to prepare 15.68 L of HCl at STP?
4. A gas jar holds 3.36 L of HCl at STP. Find the mass of HCl in it. Using 22.4 L per mole, find the density of HCl in g per L and compare it with that of air (molar mass 29). Why does this decide the method of collection?
5. 67.2 L of H₂ at STP is burnt completely in chlorine. Find the volume of Cl₂ needed, the volume of HCl formed and the masses of all three gases.
6. A teacher adds acidified AgNO₃ to a solution containing 3.33 g of calcium chloride, CaCl₂ (CaCl₂ + 2AgNO₃ → 2AgCl + Ca(NO₃)₂). Find the mass of AgCl and the mass of AgNO₃ used. Why does calcium not affect the test?
7. Gastric juice contains about 0.5 percent HCl by mass. How much HCl is in 800 g of it? If a cleaner contained 10 times that percentage, what would its percentage be?
8. A dry 250 mL flask is filled with HCl in a fountain demo. If 1 mL of water dissolves about 450 mL of HCl, estimate the volume of water that can dissolve all the gas. Why must the flask be dry, and why does the litmus turn red?
9. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) Dry HCl gas turns dry blue litmus red. (b) HCl gas is collected by upward displacement of air. (c) HCl is collected over water. (d) Concentrated H₂SO₄ is used to dry HCl. (e) Dilute H₂SO₄ is used to make HCl from NaCl. (f) Dry HCl gas turns moist blue litmus red.
10. NaCl, KCl, CaCl₂ and ZnCl₂ (name only) are four chlorides. How many chloride ions are present in one formula unit of each? What common observation do their solutions give with acidified silver nitrate, and why does the cation not matter?
11. A student draws a set-up for HCl in which the gas is collected in an inverted jar with the tube reaching its top, and the delivery tube is dipped deep in water at the end. Name two errors and the correct method for each.
12. Why is it said that the hydrogen and chlorine for industrial HCl are conveniently available from one plant? Name the process and the third product.

### Answers

1. KCl = 39 + 35.5 = 74.5 g/mol, moles = 44.7 ÷ 74.5 = **0.6 mol**. Ratio 1 : 1 : 1, to HCl = 0.6 mol = 0.6 × 36.5 = **21.9 g**, volume = 0.6 × 22.4 = **13.44 L**. H₂SO₄ = 0.6 × 98 = **58.8 g**. Jaanch: reactants 44.7 + 58.8 = 103.5 g; products KHSO₄ (39 + 1 + 32 + 64 = 136; 0.6 × 136 = 81.6 g) + HCl 21.9 g = 103.5 g ✅.
2. NaCl = 23.4 ÷ 58.5 = **0.4 mol**. 2NaCl : H₂SO₄ : Na₂SO₄ : 2HCl, to HCl = 0.4 mol = 0.4 × 36.5 = **14.6 g**, volume = 0.4 × 22.4 = **8.96 L**. Na₂SO₄ = 0.2 mol × 142 = **28.4 g**. Jaanch: H₂SO₄ = 0.2 × 98 = 19.6 g; reactants 23.4 + 19.6 = 43.0 g; products 14.6 + 28.4 = 43.0 g ✅.
3. HCl = 15.68 ÷ 22.4 = **0.7 mol**. 2NaCl : 2HCl = 1 : 1, to NaCl = 0.7 mol = 0.7 × 58.5 = **40.95 g**. H₂SO₄ : 2HCl = 1 : 2, to H₂SO₄ = 0.35 mol = 0.35 × 98 = **34.3 g**. Jaanch: reactants 40.95 + 34.3 = 75.25 g; products HCl 0.7 × 36.5 = 25.55 g + Na₂SO₄ 0.35 × 142 = 49.7 g = 75.25 g ✅.
4. Moles = 3.36 ÷ 22.4 = **0.15 mol**, mass = 0.15 × 36.5 = **5.475 g**. Density HCl = 36.5 ÷ 22.4 = lagbhag **1.63 g/L**; hawa = 29 ÷ 22.4 = lagbhag **1.29 g/L**. HCl bhaari hai, isliye jar seedha khada, nali neeche tak: **upward displacement of air**. Jaanch: 1.63 ÷ 1.29 = 1.26 (Example 1 jaisa) ✅.
5. H₂ = 67.2 ÷ 22.4 = **3 mol**. H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl: Cl₂ = 3 mol = **67.2 L**, HCl = 6 mol = **134.4 L**. Mass: H₂ = 3 × 2 = **6 g**, Cl₂ = 3 × 71 = **213 g**, HCl = 6 × 36.5 = **219 g**. Jaanch: 6 + 213 = 219 g ✅.
6. CaCl₂ = 40 + 71 = 111 g/mol, moles = 3.33 ÷ 111 = **0.03 mol**. 1 CaCl₂ se 2 AgCl, to AgCl = 0.06 mol = 0.06 × 143.5 = **8.61 g**. AgNO₃ = 0.06 mol × 170 = **10.2 g**. Calcium Ca²⁺ **darshak** hai: dhodha sirf Ag⁺ + Cl⁻ → AgCl↓ se banta hai (Chapter 8). Jaanch: reactants 3.33 + 10.2 = 13.53 g; products 8.61 + Ca(NO₃)₂ (0.03 × 164 = 4.92 g) = 13.53 g ✅. (Silver nitrate zehreela hai: teacher demo.)
7. 800 × 0.5 ÷ 100 = **4 g** HCl. 10 guna = **5 percent**. Jaanch: 4 ÷ 800 × 100 = 0.5 ✅.
8. Paani = 250 ÷ 450 = lagbhag **0.56 mL**. Flask **sookha** isliye ki nami hoti to gas pehle hi ghul jaati aur dabao nahi girta, fuwwara nahi banta. Litmus laal isliye ki ghola hua HCl paani mein H₃O⁺ deta hai (acid). Jaanch: 0.56 × 450 = 252 mL ≈ 250 mL ✅.
9. (a) **False** ❌: sookhi HCl sookhe litmus ko laal nahi karti; paani chahiye. (b) **True** ✅. (c) **False** ❌: HCl bahut ghulansheel hai, upward displacement of air se. (d) **True** ✅. (e) **False** ❌: dilute acid mein paani hai, HCl ghul jaati hai; conc. H₂SO₄ chahiye. (f) **True** ✅: geele litmus par paani mil gaya.
10. NaCl 1, KCl 1, CaCl₂ 2, ZnCl₂ 2 chloride ions. Chaaron ke ghol acidified AgNO₃ se **safed curdy AgCl** dete hain, kyunki test Cl⁻ ka hai: Ag⁺ + Cl⁻ → AgCl↓, cation (Na⁺, K⁺, Ca²⁺, Zn²⁺) darshak hai (Chapter 8, Topic 4). Jaanch: formulas se Cl ki ginti: NaCl 1, KCl 1, CaCl₂ 2, ZnCl₂ 2 ✅.

(HCl ko paani ke upar bhi nahi ikattha karte: bahut ghulansheel.)
