# Topic 6: Quadratic Ke Word Problems: Kahani Se Equation Tak

Ab tak tumhare paas teen tareeke hain (Topic 1 ki factorisation, Topic 3 ka completing the square, Topic 4 ka formula) aur Topic 5 ka D, jo hal karne se pehle roots ka swabhav bata deta hai. Par asli zindagi mein equation kisi ne likh kar nahi di hoti. Wahan to bas ek **kahani** hoti hai: ek ground, ek bus, ek tanki. Is topic ka kaam hai kahani ko equation mein badalna, equation ko hal karna, aur phir dono roots ko wapas kahani ke saamne khada karna, kyunki kahani aksar ek root ko dhakka dekar bahar kar deti hai.

---

## Shobhit aur sports day ki chit

School mein sports day aane wala hai. Teacher ma'am ne Shobhit ko ek chit di: "Long jump ka naya sand-pit banana hai. Uski length breadth se 4 m zyada honi chahiye, aur sand ka area 45 m² chahiye. Breadth kitni rakhein?"

Shobhit ne breadth ko x m maana, to length (x + 4) m. Area = length × breadth, to x(x + 4) = 45. Usne x² + 4x − 45 = 0 banayi, factorise ki, aur do roots nikaale: x = 5 aur x = −9. Chit par usne likh diya: "breadth 5 m ya −9 m". Teacher ma'am hans padi: "−9 m ki breadth kahan se laoge? Sand-pit ki chaudai −9 m to kabhi nahi hoti." Shobhit ko samajh aaya ki hisaab poora tabhi hota hai jab jawab **kahani mein baith jaaye**.

Phir usne dekha ki chit par aur bhi kaam hain: relay ke runners ki speed, ek khaali tanki ko bharne wale do nal, aur ek gend jo upar ja kar neeche aati hai. Sawaal ye hai: **kahani se equation kaise banti hai, aur dono roots mein se kaun sa kahani ka hai?**

## Part A: Chaar kadam ka tareeka (numbers aur ages)

Har word problem ke liye wahi chaar kadam:

```
 1. ANJAAN: kya dhundhna hai? Use x kaho (units ke saath)
                        :
                        v
 2. EQUATION: kahani ki baatein ek-ek karke x mein likho
                        :
                        v
 3. HAL: standard form, phir factorisation ya formula, dono roots
                        :
                        v
 4. STORY CHECK: har root ko kahani mein rakh kar dekho
          :                                :
     baith gaya                       nahi baitha
          :                                :
          v                                v
     answer likho                 root reject, wajah likho
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Word problem ke chaar kadam. Aakhri kadam (story check) hi decide karta hai ki root answer hai ya bekaar root.

### Example 1: Shobhit ka sand-pit (x(x + 4) = 45)

**Anjaan:** breadth x m. Length (x + 4) m.
**Equation:** x(x + 4) = 45, to x² + 4x − 45 = 0.
**Hal:** 45 = 9 × 5 aur 9 − 5 = 4, to (x + 9)(x − 5) = 0, x = −9 ya x = 5.
**Story check:** breadth negative nahi ho sakti, to −9 reject. **Breadth 5 m, length 9 m.** 5 × 9 = 45.
**Jaanch:** roots ka jod = 5 + (−9) = −4 = −b/a, guna = −45 = c/a ✅.

### Example 2: do numbers (antar 5, squares ka jod 193)

Do positive numbers ka antar 5 hai aur unke squares ka jod 193 hai. Numbers dhundho.

**Anjaan:** chhota number x, bada x + 5.
**Equation:** x² + (x + 5)² = 193 ⇒ 2x² + 10x + 25 = 193 ⇒ 2x² + 10x − 168 = 0. 2 se divide: **x² + 5x − 84 = 0**.
**Hal:** 84 = 12 × 7 aur 12 − 7 = 5, to (x + 12)(x − 7) = 0, x = −12 ya x = 7.
**Story check:** numbers positive hain, to x = −12 reject. **Numbers 7 aur 12.** 49 + 144 = 193 aur 12 − 7 = 5.
**Jaanch:** jod = −12 + 7 = −5 = −b/a, guna = −84 = c/a ✅.

### Example 3: umar ka sawaal (x saal pehle ka guna)

Didi ki umar abhi x saal hai aur Mummy Didi se 22 saal badi hain. 4 saal pehle dono ki umar ka guna 135 tha. Abhi ki umar dhundho.

**Anjaan:** Didi abhi x saal ki. Mummy x + 22 saal ki.
**Equation:** 4 saal pehle Didi x − 4 aur Mummy x + 18 ki thin. (x − 4)(x + 18) = 135 ⇒ x² + 14x − 72 = 135 ⇒ **x² + 14x − 207 = 0**.
**Hal:** 207 = 23 × 9 aur 23 − 9 = 14, to (x + 23)(x − 9) = 0, x = −23 ya x = 9.
**Story check:** umar negative nahi hoti, to −23 reject. **Didi 9 saal ki, Mummy 31 saal ki.** 4 saal pehle 5 aur 27: 5 × 27 = 135.
**Jaanch:** jod = −23 + 9 = −14 = −b/a ✅.

## Part B: Geometry (right triangle aur border)

### Example 4: right triangle (Pythagoras se)

Ground ke ek kone par rassi se ek right-angled triangle ka marking hai. Ek chhoti side x m, doosri side usse 23 m lambi, aur teesri (kaan, hypotenuse) chhoti side se 25 m lambi. Teeno sides dhundho.

```
        :\
        : \
        :  \
 x + 23 :   \  x + 25
        :    \
        :_____\
           x
```

> **Figure 6.2** – Right angle neeche-baayein. Do sides x aur x + 23, kaan x + 25 (figure naap ke hisaab se nahi hai).

**Equation:** Pythagoras se x² + (x + 23)² = (x + 25)².
Left: 2x² + 46x + 529. Right: x² + 50x + 625. Doosri taraf bhejne par **x² − 4x − 96 = 0**.
**Hal:** 96 = 12 × 8, 12 − 8 = 4, to (x − 12)(x + 8) = 0, x = 12 ya x = −8.
**Story check:** length negative nahi, to −8 reject. **Sides 12 m, 35 m aur 37 m.** 144 + 1225 = 1369 = 37².
**Jaanch:** jod = 12 − 8 = 4 = −b/a, guna = −96 = c/a ✅.

### Example 5: ground ke andar track (area se)

Ek rectangular ground 30 m × 20 m ka hai. Uske andar, chaaron kinaaron ke saath, x m chaudai ka jogging track banana hai. Track ke baad bacha hua ground 336 m² ka hai. x dhundho.

```
 +-------------------------------+
 :  x  (track, chaudai x)        :
 :     +---------------------+   :
 : x   : bacha hua ground    :  x:
 :     :  (30 - 2x) by       :   :
 :     :  (20 - 2x)          :   :
 :     +---------------------+   :
 :              x                :
 +-------------------------------+
```

> **Figure 6.3** – Chaaron taraf x chaudai ka track. Andar ka bacha hua rectangle har direction mein 2x chhota hai (ek taraf x aur doosri taraf x), isliye (30 − 2x) aur (20 − 2x).

**Equation:** (30 − 2x)(20 − 2x) = 336 ⇒ 600 − 100x + 4x² = 336 ⇒ 4x² − 100x + 264 = 0. 4 se divide: **x² − 25x + 66 = 0**.
**Hal:** 66 = 22 × 3, 22 + 3 = 25, to (x − 3)(x − 22) = 0, x = 3 ya x = 22.
**Story check:** x = 22 par 20 − 44 = −24 m, jo ho nahi sakta. Ye root **positive hone par bhi bekaar** hai: track ki chaudai ground ki chhoti side (20 m) ke aadhe, yaani 10 m, se zyada ho hi nahi sakti. **x = 3 m.** Bacha hua: 24 × 14 = 336.
**Jaanch:** jod = 25 = −b/a, guna = 66 = c/a ✅.

## Part C: Speed, time, doori

Yaad rakho: **time = doori ÷ speed**. Aur jab sawaal mein "x km/h" aur "1 ghanta kam" ho, to do time ka **farak** equation banata hai. Time agar minutes mein diya ho to use ghanton mein badlo (30 minute = 1/2 ghanta).

### Example 6: bus ki speed (doori 90 km)

Ek bus 90 km ka raasta ek fixed speed se tay karti hai. Agar speed 15 km/h zyada hoti to safar 1 ghanta kam lagta. Original speed dhundho.

**Anjaan:** bus ki speed x km/h. Time 90/x ghante. Nayi speed x + 15, time 90/(x + 15).
**Equation:** 90/x − 90/(x + 15) = 1. Dono taraf x(x + 15) se guna (x ≠ 0, x ≠ −15): 90(x + 15) − 90x = x(x + 15), to 1350 = x² + 15x, yaani **x² + 15x − 1350 = 0**.
**Hal:** 1350 = 45 × 30, 45 − 30 = 15, to (x + 45)(x − 30) = 0, x = −45 ya x = 30.
**Story check:** speed negative nahi, to −45 reject. **Speed 30 km/h.** Time 90/30 = 3 ghante; nayi speed 45 par 90/45 = 2 ghante. Farak 1 ghanta.
**Jaanch:** jod = −45 + 30 = −15 = −b/a ✅.

### Example 7: boat, dhaara ke saath aur ulta

```
  Dhaara (2 km/h)  ---------------------->

  Downstream:  boat ->->->   speed = x + 2
  Upstream:    boat <-<-<-   speed = x - 2
```

> **Figure 6.4** – Dhaara ke saath boat ki speed jud jaati hai, dhaara ke ulta ghat jaati hai. x shaant paani ki speed hai.

Ek boat shaant paani mein x km/h chalti hai. Dhaara ki speed 2 km/h hai. Boat 15 km upstream jaati hai aur 15 km downstream wapas aati hai, kul 4 ghante mein. x dhundho.

**Equation:** Upstream speed x − 2, downstream speed x + 2. 15/(x − 2) + 15/(x + 2) = 4. Dono taraf (x − 2)(x + 2) se guna (x ≠ 2, x ≠ −2): 15(x + 2) + 15(x − 2) = 4(x² − 4), to 30x = 4x² − 16, yaani 4x² − 30x − 16 = 0. 2 se divide: **2x² − 15x − 8 = 0**.
**Hal:** (2x + 1)(x − 8) = 2x² − 16x + x − 8, to x = −1/2 ya x = 8.
**Story check:** −1/2 negative speed, reject. **Boat 8 km/h.** Upstream 6 km/h par 15/6 = 2.5 ghante, downstream 10 km/h par 15/10 = 1.5 ghante, kul 4 ghante. Upstream speed positive (8 > 2) bhi.
**Jaanch:** jod = 8 − 1/2 = 15/2 = −b/a, guna = −4 = c/a ✅.

### Example 8: cyclist aur minutes

Ek cyclist 30 km ka raasta x km/h se tay karta hai. Agar uski speed 5 km/h zyada hoti to usse 30 minute kam lagte. x dhundho.

**Equation:** 30 minute = 1/2 ghanta. 30/x − 30/(x + 5) = 1/2. x(x + 5) se guna: 30(x + 5) − 30x = x(x + 5)/2, to 150 = x(x + 5)/2, yaani x² + 5x = 300, **x² + 5x − 300 = 0**.
**Hal:** 300 = 20 × 15, 20 − 15 = 5, to (x + 20)(x − 15) = 0, x = −20 ya x = 15.
**Story check:** −20 reject. **Speed 15 km/h.** 30/15 = 2 ghante, 30/20 = 1.5 ghante, farak 30 minute.
**Jaanch:** jod = −5 = −b/a, guna = −300 = c/a ✅.

## Part D: Kaam aur samay

Agar koi akela x ghante mein kaam poora karta hai, to ek ghante mein uska **1/x hissa** hota hai. Do log saath kaam karein to ek ghante ke hisse jud jaate hain: **1/x + 1/y = 1/(saath mein lagne wala time)**.

### Example 9: do groups, line marking

Do groups track ki lines chalk se banate hain. Group A akela, group B se 5 ghante kam lagata hai. Dono saath mein 6 ghante mein kaam khatam karte hain. Har group akela kitna time lega?

**Anjaan:** A akela x ghante, to B akela (x + 5) ghante.
**Equation:** 1/x + 1/(x + 5) = 1/6. x(x + 5) se guna aur 6 se: 6(x + 5) + 6x = x(x + 5), to 12x + 30 = x² + 5x, yaani **x² − 7x − 30 = 0**.
**Hal:** (x − 10)(x + 3) = x² − 7x − 30, to x = 10 ya x = −3.
**Story check:** time negative nahi, −3 reject. **A akela 10 ghante, B akela 15 ghante.** 1/10 + 1/15 = 3/30 + 2/30 = 5/30 = 1/6.
**Jaanch:** jod = 10 − 3 = 7 = −b/a, guna = −30 = c/a ✅.

### Example 10: do nal, jawab surd mein

Ek khaali tanki ko do nal bharte hain (sprinkler ke liye). Chhota nal akela bade nal se 6 ghante zyada leta hai. Dono saath khulein to tanki 1 ghante mein bharti hai. Bada nal akela kitna time lega?

**Anjaan:** bada nal x ghante, chhota (x + 6) ghante.
**Equation:** 1/x + 1/(x + 6) = 1. x(x + 6) se guna: (x + 6) + x = x(x + 6), to 2x + 6 = x² + 6x, yaani **x² + 4x − 6 = 0**.
**Hal:** Factorise nahi hoti (6 = 2 × 3 ya 1 × 6 mein jod 4 nahi aata). Formula: a = 1, b = 4, c = −6, b² − 4ac = 16 + 24 = 40 = 4 × 10, √40 = 2√10. x = (−4 ± 2√10)/2 = −2 ± √10.
Calculator se √10 ≈ 3.162: x ≈ 1.162 ya x ≈ −5.162.
**Story check:** time negative nahi, to −2 − √10 reject. **Bada nal lagbhag 1.16 ghante (−2 + √10), chhota nal lagbhag 7.16 ghante.** 1/1.162 + 1/7.162 ≈ 0.861 + 0.140 = 1.001, rounding ke andar 1.
**Jaanch:** jod = (−2 + √10) + (−2 − √10) = −4 = −b/a, guna = 4 − 10 = −6 = c/a ✅.

## Part E: Kharcha aur ginti

### Example 11: kharcha baantna (log ghat jaayein)

Ek class ne inaam ka cup ₹ 840 mein liya, aur kharcha sab bachchon ne barabar baanta. 4 bachche kam hote to har ek ko ₹ 7 zyada dene padte. Class mein kitne bachche hain?

**Anjaan:** bachche x. Abhi har ek ka hissa ₹ 840/x. 4 kam hone par ₹ 840/(x − 4).
**Equation:** 840/(x − 4) − 840/x = 7. x(x − 4) se guna (x ≠ 0, x ≠ 4): 840x − 840(x − 4) = 7x(x − 4), to 3360 = 7x² − 28x. 7 se divide: **x² − 4x − 480 = 0**.
**Hal:** 480 = 24 × 20, 24 − 20 = 4, to (x − 24)(x + 20) = 0, x = 24 ya x = −20.
**Story check:** bachchon ki ginti negative nahi hoti, −20 reject. **24 bachche.** Hissa 840/24 = ₹ 35; 20 bachche hote to 840/20 = ₹ 42, farak ₹ 7.
**Jaanch:** jod = 24 − 20 = 4 = −b/a, guna = −480 = c/a ✅.

### Example 12: kursiyon ki rows

Ek hall mein 120 kursiyan rows mein lagi hain, har row mein barabar kursiyan. Agar 3 rows kam kar ke har row mein 2 kursiyan badha dein, to bhi total 120 hi rahe. Abhi kitni rows hain?

**Anjaan:** rows x, har row mein 120/x kursiyan.
**Equation:** (x − 3)(120/x + 2) = 120 ⇒ 120 + 2x − 360/x − 6 = 120 ⇒ 2x − 360/x − 6 = 0. x se guna (x ≠ 0): 2x² − 6x − 360 = 0. 2 se divide: **x² − 3x − 180 = 0**.
**Hal:** 180 = 15 × 12, 15 − 12 = 3, to (x − 15)(x + 12) = 0, x = 15 ya x = −12.
**Story check:** rows negative nahi hoti, −12 reject. **15 rows, har row mein 8 kursiyan.** Naya intezaam: 12 rows × 10 = 120.
**Jaanch:** jod = 15 − 12 = 3 = −b/a ✅.

## Part F: Diya hua height formula

Kabhi sawaal mein ek taiyaar formula milta hai, aur humein bas samay (ya ooncha-ayi) wali equation hal karni hoti hai. Yahan physics nahi, sirf algebra hai.

### Example 13: gend zameen par kab girti hai?

Ek gend ko seedha upar phenka gaya. t second baad uski ooncha-ayi (zameen se) h = 5 + 24t − 5t² metre hai. Gend zameen par kab girti hai?

**Equation:** Zameen par h = 0: 5 + 24t − 5t² = 0, yaani **5t² − 24t − 5 = 0**.
**Hal:** ac = −25: 5t² − 25t + t − 5 = 5t(t − 5) + 1(t − 5) = (t − 5)(5t + 1), to t = 5 ya t = −1/5.
**Story check:** phenkne ke baad ka samay t ≥ 0 hota hai, to −1/5 reject. **Gend 5 second baad girti hai.** h(5) = 5 + 120 − 125 = 0.
**Jaanch:** jod = 5 − 1/5 = 24/5 = −b/a, guna = −1 = c/a ✅.

### Example 14: jab dono roots sahi hon

Ek doosri gend ki ooncha-ayi h = 2 + 18t − 5t² metre hai. Kab uski ooncha-ayi 17 m hoti hai?

**Equation:** 2 + 18t − 5t² = 17 ⇒ **5t² − 18t + 15 = 0**. Formula: b² − 4ac = 324 − 300 = 24 (perfect square nahi, roots surd mein), √24 = 2√6. t = (18 ± 2√6)/10 = (9 ± √6)/5. Calculator se √6 ≈ 2.449: t ≈ 2.29 ya t ≈ 1.31.
**Story check:** dono roots positive hain. Gend zameen par lagbhag 3.7 second par girti hai (2 + 18t − 5t² = 0 ka positive root), to 1.31 aur 2.29 dono usse pehle hain. **Gend 17 m par do baar hoti hai: lagbhag 1.31 s par upar jaate hue, aur lagbhag 2.29 s par neeche aate hue.** Yahan **dono roots rakhne hain**.
**Jaanch:** jod = (9 + √6)/5 + (9 − √6)/5 = 18/5 = −b/a, guna = (81 − 6)/25 = 3 = c/a ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: dono roots rakh lena, jab ek kahani mein baithta hi nahi**

Sawaal: ek rectangle ki length breadth se 3 m zyada hai aur area 70 m² hai. Breadth x m: x(x + 3) = 70 ⇒ x² + 3x − 70 = 0 ⇒ (x + 10)(x − 7) = 0. Ek bachche ne likha: "Breadth 7 m ya −10 m."

Check karo −10 ko. Breadth −10 m to length x + 3 = −7 m. Guna (−10)(−7) = 70 to sahi aaya, par **−10 m lambi cheez duniya mein nahi hoti**. Math ka root hai, kahani ka nahi.
Sahi: x = −10 reject. **Breadth 7 m, length 10 m.** 7 × 10 = 70.
Ye bhi yaad rakho: har baar ek hi root nahi hota. Example 14 mein gend 17 m par do baar thi, aur dono roots sahi the. Isliye **har root ko kahani se puchho**, aap-se-aap na rakho na phenko.

**Galti 2: time = speed ÷ doori likhna (ulta)**

Sawaal: ek car 60 km tay karti hai. Speed 10 km/h zyada hoti to 1 ghanta kam lagta. Ek bachche ne time = speed ÷ doori likha: x/60 − (x + 10)/60 = 1.

Check karo. Left side = (x − x − 10)/60 = −10/60 = −1/6, jo 1 nahi hai. Yaani x kat gaya aur equation bekaar ban gayi: koi x nahi nikla. Unit se bhi dekho: speed ÷ doori = (km/h) ÷ km = 1/h, ye "ghanta" nahi hai.
Sahi: time = doori ÷ speed: 60/x − 60/(x + 10) = 1. x(x + 10) se guna: 60(x + 10) − 60x = x(x + 10), to 600 = x² + 10x, **x² + 10x − 600 = 0**. (x + 30)(x − 20) = 0, to x = 20 (−30 reject). Time 60/20 = 3 ghante, 60/30 = 2 ghante, farak 1 ghanta.
**Jaanch:** jod = −30 + 20 = −10 = −b/a ✅.

## Quick Recap

- Chaar kadam: **anjaan** (units ke saath), **equation**, **hal**, **story check**.
- **Time = doori ÷ speed.** Minutes ko ghanton mein badlo (30 minute = 1/2 ghanta).
- Boat: **downstream = x + dhaara**, **upstream = x − dhaara**. Dhaara par upstream speed positive honi chahiye.
- Kaam: **1/x + 1/y = 1/(saath ka time)**.
- Fractions wali equation ko common denominator se guna karo, aur x ko 0 banane wali value rok do (Topic 2).
- Geometry: Pythagoras (right triangle), area = length × breadth, triangle = ½ × base × height.
- Diye hue height formula mein bas h ki value rakho aur t ki quadratic hal karo.
- Har root ko **kahani mein rakho**: negative length, time, speed, ya umar reject; bachchon ki ginti poori honi chahiye. Kabhi ek, kabhi dono roots sahi hote hain.
- Jaanch: jod = −b/a, guna = c/a aur kahani ki arithmetic.

## Practice Questions

1. One number is 7 less than twice another positive number, and the product of the two numbers is 99. Find the numbers.

2. The sum of the present ages of two cousins is 27 years. Four years ago the product of their ages was 84. Find their present ages.

4. The base of a triangular flag is 3 cm longer than its height, and its area is 20 cm². Find the base and height.

5. The sides of a right triangle (in metres) are x, 2x − 1 and 2x + 1 (the longest side is 2x + 1). Find x and the three sides.

6. A truck covers 140 km at a uniform speed. If the speed were 10 km/h more, the trip would take 42 minutes less. Find the speed.

7. A boat goes 36 km upstream and 36 km downstream in a total of 9 hours. The stream flows at 3 km/h. Find the speed of the boat in still water.

8. Worker A alone takes 7 hours less than worker B alone to paint a fence. Together they take 12 hours. How long does each take alone?

9. A group of friends shares the cost of a gift worth ₹ 1260 equally. When 3 more friends join, each pays ₹ 10 less. How many friends were there at first?

10. A stone thrown from a bridge has height h = 10 + 10t − 5t² metres above the river after t seconds. (a) When does it hit the river? Give the answer to 2 decimal places (√3 ≈ 1.732). (b) When is it at a height of 15 m?

11. A hall has 400 chairs in equal rows. If there were 5 rows fewer and 4 more chairs in each row, the total would stay 400. Find the number of rows.

12. In a league each team plays every other team exactly once, and 66 matches are played in all. If there are x teams, the number of matches is x(x − 1)/2. Find x.

13. Machine A alone takes 1 hour more than machine B alone to fill an order. Together they take 1 hour. Form the equation for B's time, solve it (√5 ≈ 2.236) and give both times to 2 decimal places.

14. State True or False, with a reason:
    (a) Both roots of the equation formed from a word problem are always answers to the story.
    (b) For a time problem a root x = −3 hours is rejected.
    (c) If a bus takes 2 hours less at a speed of s km/h more, then time = speed ÷ distance.
    (d) 20 minutes should be written as 20 in an equation where the time is in hours.
    (e) A word problem can have two valid answers.

### Answers

1. Doosra number x, pehla 2x − 7. x(2x − 7) = 99 ⇒ 2x² − 7x − 99 = 0. 2x² − 18x + 11x − 99 = 2x(x − 9) + 11(x − 9) = (x − 9)(2x + 11), to x = 9 ya x = −11/2.
   **Story check:** numbers positive hain, −11/2 reject. **Numbers 9 aur 11** (2 × 9 − 7 = 11). 9 × 11 = 99.
   Jaanch: jod = 9 − 11/2 = 7/2 = −b/a ✅, guna = −99/2 = c/a ✅.

2. Ek cousin x saal ka, doosra 27 − x. (x − 4)(23 − x) = 84 ⇒ −x² + 27x − 92 = 84 ⇒ x² − 27x + 176 = 0. 176 = 16 × 11 aur 16 + 11 = 27, to (x − 11)(x − 16) = 0, x = 11 ya x = 16.
   **Story check:** dono roots positive aur 4 se bade hain. Ye **ek hi jodi ke do naam** hain: **cousins 11 aur 16 saal ke.** 4 saal pehle 7 × 12 = 84.
   Jaanch: jod = 27 = −b/a, guna = 176 = c/a ✅.

3. Breadth x cm, length x + 9. x² + (x + 9)² = 45² ⇒ 2x² + 18x + 81 = 2025 ⇒ 2x² + 18x − 1944 = 0 ⇒ x² + 9x − 972 = 0. 972 = 36 × 27 aur 36 − 27 = 9, to (x + 36)(x − 27) = 0, x = −36 ya x = 27.
   **Story check:** −36 reject. **Breadth 27 cm, length 36 cm.** 729 + 1296 = 2025 = 45².
   Jaanch: jod = −36 + 27 = −9 = −b/a ✅.

4. Height x cm, base x + 3. ½ × (x + 3) × x = 20 ⇒ x² + 3x = 40 ⇒ x² + 3x − 40 = 0. (x + 8)(x − 5) = 0, x = −8 ya x = 5.
   **Story check:** −8 reject. **Height 5 cm, base 8 cm.** ½ × 8 × 5 = 20.
   Jaanch: jod = −3 = −b/a, guna = −40 = c/a ✅.

5. Pythagoras: x² + (2x − 1)² = (2x + 1)². Left: 5x² − 4x + 1. Right: 4x² + 4x + 1. To x² − 8x = 0, x(x − 8) = 0, x = 0 ya x = 8.
   **Story check:** x = 0 par side 0 m hogi (aur 2x − 1 = −1 negative): reject. **x = 8, sides 8 m, 15 m, 17 m.** 64 + 225 = 289 = 17².
   Jaanch: jod = 8 = −b/a, guna = 0 = c/a ✅. (Yahan ek root 0 hai, aur Topic 1 ka c = 0 wala form hai.)

6. 42 minute = 42/60 = 7/10 ghanta. 140/x − 140/(x + 10) = 7/10. x(x + 10) se guna: 140(x + 10) − 140x = (7/10)x(x + 10), to 1400 = (7/10)(x² + 10x). 10/7 se guna: 2000 = x² + 10x, **x² + 10x − 2000 = 0**. 2000 = 50 × 40, 50 − 40 = 10, to (x + 50)(x − 40) = 0, x = 40 ya −50.
   **Story check:** −50 reject. **Speed 40 km/h.** 140/40 = 3.5 ghante, 140/50 = 2.8 ghante, farak 0.7 ghanta = 42 minute.
   Jaanch: jod = −10 = −b/a ✅.

7. Boat x km/h. Upstream x − 3, downstream x + 3. 36/(x − 3) + 36/(x + 3) = 9 (x ≠ ±3). 36(x + 3) + 36(x − 3) = 9(x² − 9) ⇒ 72x = 9x² − 81 ⇒ x² − 8x − 9 = 0 ⇒ (x − 9)(x + 1) = 0, x = 9 ya −1.
   **Story check:** −1 reject. **Boat 9 km/h** (9 > 3, to upstream speed positive). Upstream 6 km/h: 6 ghante, downstream 12 km/h: 3 ghante, kul 9.
   Jaanch: jod = 8 = −b/a, guna = −9 = c/a ✅.

8. A akela x ghante, to B akela x + 7 ghante. 1/x + 1/(x + 7) = 1/12 ⇒ 12(x + 7) + 12x = x(x + 7) ⇒ 24x + 84 = x² + 7x ⇒ x² − 17x − 84 = 0 ⇒ (x − 21)(x + 4) = 0, x = 21 ya −4.
   **Story check:** −4 reject. **A akela 21 ghante, B akela 28 ghante.** 1/21 + 1/28 = 4/84 + 3/84 = 7/84 = 1/12.
   Jaanch: jod = 17 = −b/a, guna = −84 = c/a ✅.

9. Pehle x friends. 1260/x − 1260/(x + 3) = 10 (x ≠ 0, x ≠ −3). 1260(x + 3) − 1260x = 10x(x + 3) ⇒ 3780 = 10x² + 30x ⇒ x² + 3x − 378 = 0. 378 = 21 × 18, 21 − 18 = 3, to (x + 21)(x − 18) = 0, x = −21 ya 18.
   **Story check:** ginti negative nahi, −21 reject. **18 friends.** Hissa 1260/18 = ₹ 70; 21 friends par 1260/21 = ₹ 60, farak ₹ 10.
   Jaanch: jod = −3 = −b/a, guna = −378 = c/a ✅.

10. (a) h = 0: 10 + 10t − 5t² = 0, −5 se divide: t² − 2t − 2 = 0. b² − 4ac = 4 + 8 = 12, √12 = 2√3. t = (2 ± 2√3)/2 = 1 ± √3. √3 ≈ 1.732: t ≈ 2.732 ya −0.732.
   **Story check:** t ≥ 0, to −0.73 reject. **Stone lagbhag 2.73 s mein river mein girta hai.**
   Jaanch: jod = 2 = −b/a ✅, guna = 1 − 3 = −2 = c/a ✅.
   (b) 10 + 10t − 5t² = 15 ⇒ 5t² − 10t + 5 = 0 ⇒ t² − 2t + 1 = 0 ⇒ (t − 1)² = 0, t = 1. **Sirf t = 1 s par** (ek hi root, do baar). Matlab 15 m stone ki **sabse zyada ooncha-ayi** hai: usse upar wo jaata hi nahi (ek hi pal 15 m par). Jaanch: h(1) = 10 + 10 − 5 = 15 ✅.

11. Rows x, har row mein 400/x kursiyan. (x − 5)(400/x + 4) = 400 ⇒ 400 + 4x − 2000/x − 20 = 400 ⇒ 4x − 2000/x − 20 = 0. x se guna aur 4 se divide: x² − 500 − 5x = 0, yaani x² − 5x − 500 = 0. 500 = 25 × 20, 25 − 20 = 5, to (x − 25)(x + 20) = 0, x = 25 ya −20.
    **Story check:** −20 reject. **25 rows, har row mein 16 kursiyan.** Naya: 20 rows × 20 kursiyan = 400.
    Jaanch: jod = 5 = −b/a, guna = −500 = c/a ✅.

12. x(x − 1)/2 = 66 ⇒ x² − x = 132 ⇒ x² − x − 132 = 0. 132 = 12 × 11, 12 − 11 = 1, to (x − 12)(x + 11) = 0, x = 12 ya −11.
    **Story check:** teams negative nahi hoti, −11 reject. **12 teams.** 12 × 11/2 = 66.
    Jaanch: jod = 1 = −b/a, guna = −132 = c/a ✅.

13. B akela x ghante, A akela x + 1. 1/x + 1/(x + 1) = 1 ⇒ (x + 1) + x = x(x + 1) ⇒ 2x + 1 = x² + x ⇒ **x² − x − 1 = 0**. Factorise nahi hoti. Formula: b² − 4ac = 1 + 4 = 5, x = (1 ± √5)/2. √5 ≈ 2.236: x ≈ 1.618 ya −0.618.
    **Story check:** −0.618 reject. **B lagbhag 1.62 ghante, A lagbhag 2.62 ghante.** 1/1.618 + 1/2.618 ≈ 0.618 + 0.382 = 1.
    Jaanch: jod = 1 = −b/a, guna = (1 − 5)/4 = −1 = c/a ✅.

14. (a) **False** ❌. Kahani aksar ek root ko reject kar deti hai (Examples 1 se 13), kabhi dono rakhne padte hain (Example 14).
    (b) **True** ✅. Time negative nahi ho sakta.
    (c) **False** ❌. Time = doori ÷ speed. Speed ÷ doori ka unit 1/h hota hai, ghanta nahi.
    (d) **False** ❌. 20 minute = 20/60 = 1/3 ghanta likhna hai.
    (e) **True** ✅. Jaise Practice 2 ke cousins ki jodi: x = 11 aur x = 16 dono sahi nikle (aur Example 14 ki gend do baar 17 m par).
