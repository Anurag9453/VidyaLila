# Topic 2: Ratio Ko Ghumana-Phirana: Invertendo Se Componendo-Dividendo Tak

Topic 1 mein humne seekha ki jab do ratio barabar hon, to unhe **proportion** kehte hain: a : b :: c : d, yaani a/b = c/d, aur tab ad = bc hota hai. Wahan hum proportion ko **jaisa hai waisa** padhte the, ya usme ek missing term dhundte the. Ab sawaal ye hai: kya hum ek sach proportion ko **ghuma-phira kar** (ulta karke, jod kar, ghata kar) naye sach proportions bana sakte hain? Aur ye naye proportions kab kaam aate hain? Is topic ka kaam yahi hai.

---

## Rupal ka map aur ghoomta hua ratio

Rupal ki class school-trip par ja rahi hai: school se pehle ek museum, aur phir museum se ek purana qila (fort). Teacher ma'am ne har bacche ko ek chhota map diya. Map par route ke do hisse (legs) hain: **school se museum 5 cm** aur **museum se fort 9 cm**. Ma'am ne bataya ki map par 1 cm asli sadak ke 7 km ke barabar hai.

Rupal ne dekha ki ticket par school se museum ka asli faasla 35 km likha hai. Usne jaldi se socha: doosra leg bhi 9 × 7 = 63 km hoga. Phir usne ye sach likha:

**5 : 9 :: 35 : 63**, yaani 5/9 = 35/63.

(Dono 5/9 hain: 35/63 mein 7 ka common factor hai, jo kat jaata hai.)

Tab ma'am ne ek paheli di: "Maano map aadha bheeg gaya aur tumhe sirf ye pata hai ki dono legs ka **jod** 98 km hai aur map par legs ka ratio 5 : 9 hai. Kya tum 63 aur 35 ko seedha bina scale ke nikaal sakti ho? Aur agar sirf **antar** (difference) pata ho, tab?"

Rupal ne apne sach proportion ko palat kar, jod kar aur ghata kar dekhna shuru kiya. Pata chala ki ek sach proportion se **paanch naye sach proportions** nikalte hain, aur har ek ka apna naam aur apna kaam hai. Chalo, ek-ek ko dekhte hain.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Invertendo** | Proportion ke dono ratios ko ek saath ulta kar dena: a/b = c/d se b/a = d/c | 5/9 = 35/63 se 9/5 = 63/35 |
| **Alternendo** | Beech ke do terms (b aur c) ki jagah badal dena: a/b = c/d se a/c = b/d | 5/9 = 35/63 se 5/35 = 9/63 |
| **Componendo** | Dono ratios mein numerator mein denominator jod dena (yaani dono mein 1 jodna): a/b = c/d se (a + b)/b = (c + d)/d | 5/9 = 35/63 se 14/9 = 98/63 |
| **Dividendo** | Dono ratios mein numerator se denominator ghata dena (yaani dono mein se 1 ghatana): a/b = c/d se (a − b)/b = (c − d)/d | 5/9 = 35/63 se (5 − 9)/9 = (35 − 63)/63 |
| **Componendo-dividendo** | Componendo aur dividendo ke natije ko bhaag karke b hata dena: a/b = c/d se (a + b)/(a − b) = (c + d)/(c − d), jab a ≠ b ho | 5/9 = 35/63 se 14/(−4) = 98/(−28) |

## Topic 1 se yaad

- **Topic 1** ([ratio-and-proportion.md](ratio-and-proportion.md)): a : b :: c : d ka matlab a/b = c/d hai, aur is par ad = bc (extremes ka guna = means ka guna). Hum yahan bhi har baar maan lete hain ki a, b, c, d koi bhi term 0 nahi hai.
- Is topic mein **a, b, c, d hamesha isi kram mein** chaar terms hain, aur har proof ki shuruaat usi se hogi: **a/b = c/d.**

## Part A: Paanch property, har ek ka kyun aur proof

Rupal ka sach proportion hum aage har jagah number-check ke liye rakhenge: **5 : 9 :: 35 : 63**, yaani a = 5, b = 9, c = 35, d = 63.

### Property 1: Invertendo (ulta karna)

**Matlab:** dono ratios ko ek saath palat do.

**Kyun sach hai:** do barabar numbers ke reciprocal bhi barabar hote hain. Agar a/b aur c/d ek hi number hain, to 1 ko us number se bhaag karne par bhi ek hi number aayega.

**Proof:** a/b = c/d. Dono taraf ka reciprocal lo: **b/a = d/c.** (Ye tab hi hota hai jab a aur c zero na hon, jo humne maan rakha hai.)

**Jaanch (numbers se):** 9/5 aur 63/35. 63/35 mein 7 kat jaata hai: 9/5. Dono barabar ✅. Map ki zubaan mein: pehle "pehla leg : doosra leg" tha, ab "doosra : pehla", aur asli doori mein bhi wahi ulta.

### Property 2: Alternendo (beech wale badal do)

**Matlab:** a : b :: c : d se a : c :: b : d. Yaani b aur c apni jagah badal lete hain (extremes wahi, means badal gaye).

**Kyun sach hai:** hum dono taraf ko b/c se guna karte hain. Left mein b kat jaata hai, aur right mein c kat jaata hai.

**Proof:** a/b = c/d. Dono taraf (b/c) se guna karo:

(a/b) × (b/c) = (c/d) × (b/c), yaani **a/c = b/d.**

**Jaanch (numbers se):** 5/35 = 1/7 aur 9/63 = 1/7 ✅.

**Rupal ke map par iska matlab:** 5/35 pehle leg ka "map : asli" hai aur 9/63 doosre leg ka. Dono 1/7 hain, yaani wahi **scale** (1 cm ke liye 7 km). Alternendo ye batata hai: agar do legs ka map-ratio aur asli-ratio barabar hai, to har leg ka "map : asli" bhi ek hi aata hai. Isi se map ka ek scale ban pata hai.

(Dhyan do: yahan a : c jaisa ratio cm ko km se milata hai, yaani alag-alag unit wali cheezein. Topic 1 ke hisaab se aisa "ratio" asal mein ek **rate** (scale) hai. Is topic mein a, b, c, d ko seedhe numbers maante hain, aur alternendo numbers ke liye sach hai; par kahani mein hamesha dekho ki har original ratio (a : b aur c : d) apne andar ek hi unit ki cheezein jode.)

### Property 3: Componendo (jod kar)

**Matlab:** a : b :: c : d se (a + b) : b :: (c + d) : d. Yaani numerator mein denominator jod do, dono ratios mein.

**Kyun sach hai:** a/b ek number hai. Usme 1 jodo, to 1 ko b/b likhkar hum a/b + b/b = (a + b)/b paate hain. Agar c/d wahi number hai, to usme 1 jodne par bhi wahi naya number banega.

**Proof:** a/b = c/d. Dono taraf 1 jodo:

a/b + 1 = c/d + 1, yaani (a + b)/b = (c + d)/d. Yahi **componendo** hai.

**Jaanch (numbers se):** (5 + 9)/9 = 14/9 aur (35 + 63)/63 = 98/63. 98/63 mein 7 kat jaata hai: 14/9 ✅.

Ek chhota rupantar: pehle invertendo (b/a = d/c), phir componendo, to **(a + b)/a = (c + d)/c** bhi sach hai. Check: 14/5 aur 98/35 = 14/5 ✅.

```
Map  (1 khana = 1 cm)   : [--5 cm--][------9 cm------]
Asli (1 khana = 7 km)   : [-35 km--][-----63 km------]
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Rupal ke route ke do legs. Upar map ki patti (1 khana = 1 cm), neeche asli doori ki patti (1 khana = 7 km). Dono patti ka shape bilkul ek jaisa hai, chhoti-badi nahi: yahi proportion ka matlab hai. Poora route map par 5 + 9 = 14 khane, asli mein 35 + 63 = 98 km; componendo wale ratio 14/9 aur 98/63 ek hi hain.

### Property 4: Dividendo (ghata kar)

**Matlab:** a : b :: c : d se (a − b) : b :: (c − d) : d. Yaani numerator se denominator ghata do, dono ratios mein, aur **dono mein ek hi kram** (pehle a, phir b; pehle c, phir d).

**Kyun sach hai:** componendo ki tarah, bas 1 ghataane se. a/b − 1 = a/b − b/b = (a − b)/b.

**Proof:** a/b = c/d. Dono taraf se 1 ghatao:

a/b − 1 = c/d − 1, yaani (a − b)/b = (c − d)/d. Yahi **dividendo** hai.

**Jaanch (numbers se):** (5 − 9)/9 = −4/9 aur (35 − 63)/63 = −28/63 = −4/9 ✅. Minus aana theek hai, yahan a chhota hai b se. Zaroori sirf ye hai ki dono taraf ka kram ek jaisa ho.

Kai baar bade term mein se chhota ghataana (b − a) zyada seedha lagta hai. Wo bhi sahi hai: (a − b)/b = (c − d)/d ko dono taraf (−1) se guna karo: **(b − a)/b = (d − c)/d.** Check: 4/9 aur 28/63 = 4/9 ✅.

Ek aur roop, invertendo ke baad dividendo: b/a = d/c se **(b − a)/a = (d − c)/c.** Check: 4/5 aur 28/35 = 4/5 ✅. (Example 3 mein yahi roop kaam aayega.)

```
Map  (1 khana = 1 cm)   : [--5 cm--][.4 cm.]   total b = 9 cm
Asli (1 khana = 7 km)   : [-35 km--][28 km.]   total d = 63 km
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Doosre leg (9 cm map, 63 km asli) ko do hisson mein baanta: pehle leg jitna (5 cm aur 35 km, patti se) aur bacha hua antar (4 cm aur 28 km, bindiyon wali patti). Antar ka ratio: antar / pehla leg = 4/5 (map) aur 28/35 = 4/5 (asli), yaani dividendo ki (b − a)/a = (d − c)/c wali shakl.

### Property 5: Componendo-dividendo (dono ek saath)

**Matlab:** a : b :: c : d se (a + b) : (a − b) :: (c + d) : (c − d).

**Kyun sach hai:** componendo aur dividendo dono ek hi sach proportion ke naye roop hain. Do barabar ratios ko bhaag karoge to bhi barabar hi niklenge, aur bhaag karte waqt dono ka b (denominator) kat jaata hai.

**Proof:** Componendo: (a + b)/b = (c + d)/d. Dividendo: (a − b)/b = (c − d)/d. Pehle ko doosre se bhaag karo:

[(a + b)/b] ÷ [(a − b)/b] = [(c + d)/d] ÷ [(c − d)/d].

Left mein b kat jaata hai, right mein d kat jaata hai: **(a + b)/(a − b) = (c + d)/(c − d).**

**Jaanch (numbers se):** (5 + 9)/(5 − 9) = 14/(−4) = −7/2. (35 + 63)/(35 − 63) = 98/(−28) = −7/2 ✅.

**Ek line ki savdhaani:** a ≠ b hona chahiye, warna a − b = 0 aur bhaag ka koi matlab nahi. Agar a ≠ b hai, to c ≠ d bhi apne aap hota hai (kyunki a/b = c/d, aur a = b hota to ratio 1 hota, jisse c = d aata). Isliye sirf ye dekhna kaafi hai ki a − b zero nahi hai.

## Part B: Yaad rakhne ki table

| Property | Dono ratios ke saath kya kiya | Nayi shakl |
|---|---|---|
| Invertendo | dono ko ulta kiya | b/a = d/c |
| Alternendo | beech wale terms b aur c badle | a/c = b/d |
| Componendo | dono mein 1 joda | (a + b)/b = (c + d)/d |
| Dividendo | dono mein se 1 ghataya | (a − b)/b = (c − d)/d |
| Componendo-dividendo | componendo ko dividendo se bhaaga | (a + b)/(a − b) = (c + d)/(c − d) |

**Kab kaun si property?** Ek chhota sa pehchaan-tareeka:

| Sawaal mein kya dikhe | Kaun si property |
|---|---|
| x denominator mein ho aur tumhe use numerator mein chahiye | invertendo |
| do terms ka jod (ya antar) diya hai aur unme se ek term ya ratio pata hai | componendo (ya dividendo) |
| ek taraf x + number aur x − number (ya 3x + number aur 3x − number) | componendo-dividendo |
| ek taraf sirf x aur doosri taraf x + number | invertendo, phir dividendo (ya componendo) |

## Part C: Numbers aur kahani mein use

### Example 1: ek proportion, paanchon roop (6 : 11 :: 18 : 33)

Pehle dekhte hain ki 6/11 = 18/33 sach hai: 18/33 mein 3 kat jaata hai, to 6/11. Ab a = 6, b = 11, c = 18, d = 33:

| Property | Left ratio | Right ratio | Barabar? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Invertendo | 11/6 | 33/18 = 11/6 | haan |
| Alternendo | 6/18 = 1/3 | 11/33 = 1/3 | haan |
| Componendo | (6 + 11)/11 = 17/11 | (18 + 33)/33 = 51/33 = 17/11 | haan |
| Dividendo | (6 − 11)/11 = −5/11 | (18 − 33)/33 = −15/33 = −5/11 | haan |
| Componendo-dividendo | 17/(−5) | 51/(−15) = 17/(−5) | haan |

**Jaanch:** har row mein dono ratio barabar nikle ✅. Paanchon property ek hi proportion se alag-alag sach roop dete hain.

### Example 2: map ka jod diya hai (componendo ki kahani)

Ma'am ki paheli: map par legs 5 cm aur 9 cm hain, aur asli legs ka **jod 98 km** hai. Dono asli legs kitne hain?

Asli legs c aur d (km) maan lo: 5 : 9 :: c : d, aur c + d = 98.

**Componendo lagao:** (5 + 9)/9 = (c + d)/d, yaani 14/9 = 98/d. Cross-multiply: 14d = 98 × 9 = 882, to **d = 63.** Phir c = 98 − 63 = **35.**

**Jaanch:** 5/9 = 35/63 ✅ (7 kat gaya), aur 35 + 63 = 98 ✅.

**Story check:** scale 1 cm = 7 km tha, 5 × 7 = 35 aur 9 × 7 = 63 aate hain, aur dono ka jod 98. Rupal ka pehla anuman ek dum theek nikla.

Dhyan do: yahan humne seedhe d nikaala, bina scale jaane. Sirf "jod" aur ratio kaafi the.

### Example 3: asli antar diya hai (dividendo ki kahani)

Doosre din ka route alag hai: map par legs **7 cm aur 10 cm**. Asli mein doosra leg pehle se **18 km** lamba hai. Dono asli legs nikalo.

Asli legs c aur d (km): 7 : 10 :: c : d, aur d − c = 18.

**Dividendo lagao, bade se chhota ghata kar:** (10 − 7)/7 = (d − c)/c, yaani 3/7 = 18/c. Cross-multiply: 3c = 126, to **c = 42.** Phir d = 42 + 18 = **60.**

**Jaanch:** 7/10 = 42/60 ✅ (6 kat gaya), aur 60 − 42 = 18 ✅.

**Story check:** scale 1 cm = 6 km nikla (7 × 6 = 42, 10 × 6 = 60), aur dono ka antar (10 − 7) × 6 = 18 km, jo diya gaya antar tha.

### Example 4: jod aur antar dono diye hain (componendo-dividendo ka ulta chalna)

Isi doosre route ke liye ab ma'am ne sirf itna bataya: asli legs ka **jod 102 km** aur **antar 18 km** hai. Unka ratio c : d kya hoga?

d bada hai, to jod d + c = 102 aur antar d − c = 18. Ratio (d + c)/(d − c) = 102/18 = 17/3.

Ab ye 17/3 ek ratio hi hai. Is par **componendo-dividendo** ko "ulta" chalate hain. Hum (d + c)/(d − c) = 17/3 par C-D lagate hain: left mein [(d + c) + (d − c)] / [(d + c) − (d − c)] = 2d/2c = d/c, aur right mein (17 + 3)/(17 − 3) = 20/14 = 10/7.

To **d/c = 10/7**, yaani **c : d = 7 : 10.**

**Jaanch:** Example 3 ka c : d = 42 : 60 = 7 : 10 ✅, aur 42 + 60 = 102, 60 − 42 = 18 ✅.

Ye chaal yaad rakho: **jod aur antar ka ratio, asli terms ka ratio bata deta hai.**

## Part D: Un sawaalon mein jahan x aata hai

Ab C-D ka sabse kaam ka roop: ek taraf x + number aur x − number ho, aur doosri taraf ek ratio. Tab C-D lagane par left ke jod mein x ke saath number kat jaata hai, aur antar mein x kat jaata hai, yaani sirf akela x ya akela number bachta hai.

### Example 5: (x + 3)/(x − 3) = 7/4

Left ko a/b maano: a = x + 3, b = x − 3. Right c = 7, d = 4. **Componendo-dividendo lagao:**

[(x + 3) + (x − 3)] / [(x + 3) − (x − 3)] = (7 + 4)/(7 − 4).

Left: numerator 2x, denominator 6. Right: 11/3. To 2x/6 = 11/3, yaani x/3 = 11/3, aur **x = 11.**

**Jaanch:** (11 + 3)/(11 − 3) = 14/8 = 7/4 ✅. Denominators bhi zero nahi hain (11 − 3 = 8).

(Seedha cross-multiply bhi karte: 4(x + 3) = 7(x − 3), 4x + 12 = 7x − 21, 33 = 3x, x = 11. Wahi jawab.)

### Example 6: coefficient wala x (2x + 7)/(2x − 7) = 5/3

Yahan a = 2x + 7, b = 2x − 7. Jod: (2x + 7) + (2x − 7) = 4x, antar: (2x + 7) − (2x − 7) = 14. Right: (5 + 3)/(5 − 3) = 8/2 = 4.

To 4x/14 = 4, yaani 4x = 56 aur **x = 14.**

**Jaanch:** 2x = 28, (28 + 7)/(28 − 7) = 35/21 = 5/3 ✅.

### Example 7: invertendo aur dividendo ki jodi (x/(x + 8) = 3/7)

Yahan x akela numerator mein hai aur x + 8 denominator mein. Pehle **invertendo:** (x + 8)/x = 7/3. Ab **dividendo** (numerator se denominator ghatao): [(x + 8) − x]/x = (7 − 3)/3, yaani 8/x = 4/3. Cross-multiply: 4x = 24, to **x = 6.**

**Jaanch:** 6/(6 + 8) = 6/14 = 3/7 ✅. Cross-multiply se bhi: 7x = 3(x + 8), 4x = 24, x = 6 ✅.

Shortcut ka faayda: jab ek taraf sirf "x" aur doosri taraf "x + number" hai, ek hi kadam mein x ka akela term hamare saamne aa jaata hai.

**Savdhaani:** C-D lagane ke baad jo x mile, use **asli equation** mein daalkar dekho ki koi denominator zero na ban jaye.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: componendo sirf ek (left) taraf lagana**

Proportion 6 : 7 :: 18 : 21 (6/7 = 18/21, dono sach). Ek bacche ne "componendo lagaya" aur likha: (6 + 7)/7 = 18/21.

Check karo. Left: 13/7 (≈ 1.86). Right: 18/21 = 6/7 (≈ 0.86). Dono barabar nahi. Galti: left mein 1 joda (6/7 + 1), right mein nahi joda. Do taraf barabar rakhne ke liye **dono taraf 1 jodna hai.**

Sahi: (6 + 7)/7 = (18 + 21)/21.

**Jaanch:** 13/7 aur 39/21 = 13/7 ✅.

**Galti 2: dividendo mein sign ka phisalna (ek taraf a − b, doosri taraf d − c jaisa kram)**

Proportion 9 : 4 :: 27 : 12 (27/12 mein 3 kat jaata hai: 9/4, dono sach). Ek bacche ne dividendo ke liye likha: (9 − 4)/4 = (12 − 27)/12. Left mein pehla minus doosra, right mein doosra minus pehla.

Check karo. Left: 5/4 = 1.25. Right: (12 − 27)/12 = −15/12 = −5/4 = −1.25. Left positive, right negative, barabar nahi.

Sahi: dono taraf **ek hi kram.** Ya to (9 − 4)/4 = (27 − 12)/12, ya (4 − 9)/4 = (12 − 27)/12.

**Jaanch:** (9 − 4)/4 = 5/4 aur (27 − 12)/12 = 15/12 = 5/4 ✅. Doosra roop: (4 − 9)/4 = −5/4 aur (12 − 27)/12 = −5/4 ✅.

## Quick Recap

- Proportion a : b :: c : d (a/b = c/d, har term 0 se alag) ko ghuma-phira kar paanch naye sach proportions milte hain.
- **Invertendo:** dono ratios ulte, b/a = d/c.
- **Alternendo:** b aur c ki jagah badli, a/c = b/d. Map par isi se scale nikalta hai.
- **Componendo:** dono mein 1 joda, (a + b)/b = (c + d)/d. **Dividendo:** dono mein se 1 ghataya, (a − b)/b = (c − d)/d, dono taraf ek hi kram.
- **Componendo-dividendo:** (a + b)/(a − b) = (c + d)/(c − d), jab a ≠ b.
- Har property ka proof a/b = c/d se shuru hota hai: kabhi reciprocal, kabhi b/c se guna, kabhi 1 jodna ya ghatana, kabhi bhaag.
- Jod ya antar diya ho to componendo ya dividendo, x + number aur x − number ho to componendo-dividendo.
- Har x ka jawab asli equation mein daalkar check karo.
- Aam galtiyan: componendo sirf ek taraf lagana, dividendo mein dono taraf ka kram alag karna.

### Answers

**1.** Invertendo: 13 : 8 :: 39 : 24, yaani 13/8 = 39/24.
**Jaanch:** 39/24 mein 3 kat jaata hai: 13/8 ✅. Aur original 24/39 mein 3 kat jaata hai: 8/13 ✅, yaani invertendo ne sach hi ulta kiya.

**2.** Alternendo: a/c = b/d, yaani 11/44 = 4/16.
**Jaanch:** 11/44 = 1/4 aur 4/16 = 1/4 ✅. Aur original 44/16 mein 4 kat jaata hai: 11/4 ✅.

**3.** Componendo: (15 + 8)/8 = (45 + 24)/24, yaani 23/8 = 69/24.
**Jaanch:** 69/24 mein 3 kat jaata hai: 23/8 ✅.

**4.** Dividendo: (17 − 5)/5 = (51 − 15)/15, yaani 12/5 = 36/15. Componendo-dividendo: (17 + 5)/(17 − 5) = (51 + 15)/(51 − 15), yaani 22/12 = 66/36.
**Jaanch:** 36/15 = 12/5 ✅ (3 kat gaya). 22/12 = 11/6 aur 66/36 = 11/6 ✅.

**5.** a/b = c/d. Dono taraf 3 jodo: a/b + 3 = c/d + 3. 3 ko b/b ke roop mein likho: 3 = 3b/b, to a/b + 3b/b = (a + 3b)/b. Isi tarah right mein (c + 3d)/d. To **(a + 3b)/b = (c + 3d)/d.**
**Jaanch (numbers se):** 9 : 2 :: 36 : 8 lo. (9 + 6)/2 = 15/2 aur (36 + 24)/8 = 60/8 = 15/2 ✅.

**6.** Componendo-dividendo: [(x + 5) + (x − 5)] / [(x + 5) − (x − 5)] = (13 + 3)/(13 − 3). Left: 2x/10, right: 16/10. To 2x = 16 aur **x = 8.**

**7.** Componendo-dividendo: [(3x + 8) + (3x − 8)] / [(3x + 8) − (3x − 8)] = (7 + 5)/(7 − 5). Left: 6x/16, right: 12/2 = 6. To 6x = 96 aur **x = 16.**
**Jaanch:** 3x = 48, (48 + 8)/(48 − 8) = 56/40 = 7/5 ✅.

**8.** (x − 9)/x = 2/5. Invertendo: x/(x − 9) = 5/2. Dividendo (numerator se denominator ghatao): [x − (x − 9)]/(x − 9) = (5 − 2)/2, yaani 9/(x − 9) = 3/2. Cross-multiply: 3(x − 9) = 18, x − 9 = 6, **x = 15.**
**Jaanch:** (15 − 9)/15 = 6/15 = 2/5 ✅.

**9.** (a + b)/(a − b) = 17/9. Componendo-dividendo: [(a + b) + (a − b)] / [(a + b) − (a − b)] = (17 + 9)/(17 − 9), yaani 2a/2b = 26/8. To a/b = 13/4, yaani **a : b = 13 : 4.**
**Jaanch:** a = 13, b = 4 lo: (13 + 4)/(13 − 4) = 17/9 ✅.

**10.** Road 1 aur road 2 asli (km): 9 : 14 :: p : q, aur p + q = 253. Componendo: (9 + 14)/14 = (p + q)/q, yaani 23/14 = 253/q. 23q = 253 × 14 = 3542, to q = 154. Phir p = 253 − 154 = 99. Asli lambai: **99 km aur 154 km.**
**Jaanch:** 9/14 = 99/154 (11 kat gaya) ✅, 99 + 154 = 253 ✅.
**Story check:** map scale 1 cm = 11 km: 9 × 11 = 99, 14 × 11 = 154, dono ka jod 253 km.

**11.** Lanes asli (km): 12 : 19 :: p : q, q − p = 63. Dividendo (bade se chhota): (19 − 12)/12 = (q − p)/p, yaani 7/12 = 63/p. 7p = 756, to p = 108. Phir q = 108 + 63 = 171. Asli lambai: **108 km aur 171 km.**
**Jaanch:** 12/19 = 108/171 (9 kat gaya) ✅, 171 − 108 = 63 ✅.
**Story check:** scale 1 cm = 9 km: 12 × 9 = 108, 19 × 9 = 171, antar (19 − 12) × 9 = 63.

**12.**
(a) **Sach** ✅. Invertendo se b/a = d/c, phir dono taraf 1 jodo: (b + a)/a = (d + c)/c.
(b) **Galat** ❌. (a + b)/b sirf left mein hai: dono ratios mein 1 jodna hota hai. Sahi (a + b)/b = (c + d)/d. Example: 3 : 2 :: 12 : 8 mein (3 + 2)/2 = 5/2 hai, par 12/8 = 3/2 hai.
(c) **Galat** ❌. a = b par a − b = 0 aur bhaag ka matlab nahi. Example: 6 : 6 :: 9 : 9 mein 12/0 nahi likh sakte.

**13.** a/b = c/d. Dono taraf 2/3 se guna karo: 2a/(3b) = 2c/(3d). Ab ye ek proportion hai jisme terms 2a, 3b, 2c, 3d hain. Is par componendo-dividendo (a ≠ b ki jagah 2a ≠ 3b): (2a + 3b)/(2a − 3b) = (2c + 3d)/(2c − 3d). Yahi siddh karna tha.
**Jaanch (4 : 5 :: 8 : 10):** left (8 + 15)/(8 − 15) = 23/(−7), right (16 + 30)/(16 − 30) = 46/(−14) = 23/(−7) ✅.

**14.** Galti: right mein kram ulta ho gaya (15 − 42 rakha, jabki left mein 14 − 5 hai). Right mein bhi pehla term minus doosra hona tha. Sahi: (14 − 5)/5 = (42 − 15)/15, yaani 9/5 = 27/15.
**Jaanch:** 27/15 mein 3 kat jaata hai: 9/5 ✅. Doosra sahi roop: (5 − 14)/5 = −9/5 aur (15 − 42)/15 = −27/15 = −9/5 ✅.

---

Ab tum ek proportion ko ghuma-phira kar apni zaroorat ke hisaab se kaam ka roop de sakte ho. Jod diya ho to componendo, antar diya ho to dividendo, dono ho to componendo-dividendo.

**Aage ka rasta:** abhi tak chaar terms ka proportion tha. Topic 3 mein dekhenge ki jab **beech ka term do baar aaye** (a : b = b : c), to kya hota hai: continued proportion, mean proportional aur third proportional.
