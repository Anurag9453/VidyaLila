# Topic 3: Teen Cheezon Ka Rishta: Continued, Mean Aur Third Proportional

Topic 1 mein tumne **chaar** numbers ki proportion a : b :: c : d dekhi aur product rule ad = bc seekha. Topic 2 mein us proportion ko ghuma-phirakar naye roop diye. Dono jagah har baar **do ratio** the, yaani chaar alag-alag numbers. Ab ek khaas halat: jab **dusre ratio ka pehla term wahi ho jo pehle ratio ka dusra term hai**. Tab chaar ki jagah sirf **teen** numbers bachte hain, aur beech wala number do kaam karta hai. Is topic mein is "beech wale" ka rishta, teen cheezon ko ek ratio mein jodna, aur chaar ki chain ki jhalak aayegi.

---

## Akhil ki Mummy ki nested katoriyan

Akhil ki Mummy ne almari ke upar se steel ki katoriyon ka ek set utaara. Teen katoriyan ek ke andar ek fit thi: sabse chhoti beech wali mein, beech wali sabse badi mein. Akhil ne ruler se unke muh (diameter) naape: **8 cm, 12 cm aur 18 cm.**

"Mummy, ye teeno sahi fit hote hain, kyunki har katori agli se ek jitni gunaa chhoti hai," usne kaha. Phir usne ratio nikaale: chhoti aur beech wali ka 8 : 12 = 2 : 3, aur beech wali aur badi ka 12 : 18 = 2 : 3. "Arre, dono ratio barabar hain!" Mummy ne hanskar poochha, "To agar sirf sabse chhoti (8) aur sabse badi (18) katori ka naap pata ho, to beech wali ka naap tum bina naape bata sakte ho?" Akhil ne copy mein 12 × 12 = 144 aur 8 × 18 = 144 likha. "Ye to barabar nikle! Ittefaq hai ya rule?"

Aaj hum is sawaal ka jawab denge. Aur ye bhi dekhenge: agar sirf chhoti aur beech wali pata ho to teesri kaise nikalein, aur do alag-alag ratio ko ek saath teen cheezon ke ratio mein kaise badlein.

## Part A: Continued proportion aur b² = ac

Topic 1 ki proportion a : b :: c : d mein rule tha ad = bc (extremes ka product = means ka product). Ab dusre ratio ka pehla term c ki jagah **b** rakho, aur aakhri term d ki jagah **c**. Proportion banti hai a : b :: b : c, yaani

a/b = b/c

**Kyun product rule yahan bhi chalega:** dono taraf bc se guna karo. Left: (a/b) × bc = ac. Right: (b/c) × bc = b². To **b² = ac.** Topic 1 ka "extremes ka product = means ka product" yahan "a × c = b × b" ban gaya.

Ulta bhi theek hai (teeno numbers non-zero hon): agar b² = ac ho, to dono taraf bc se divide karo: b²/(bc) = ac/(bc), yaani b/c = a/b. Yani

**a, b, c continued proportion mein hain ⇔ b² = ac** (teeno non-zero).

Ye wahi shart hai jo GP chapter ke Topic 3 ke Part E mein thi. Kyun ye ek hi baat hai, Part D mein dekhenge.

Ab Akhil ki katoriyan ek line mein dekho. Har bar ki lambai diameter (cm) ke barabar hai.

```
chhoti (8 cm) : ########
beech (12 cm) : ############
badi (18 cm)  : ##################
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Teen katoriyon ke diameter 8, 12 aur 18 cm, har # ek cm. Chhoti aur beech wali ka ratio 8 : 12 = 2 : 3, beech wali aur badi ka ratio 12 : 18 = 2 : 3. Dono barabar, to 8, 12, 18 continued proportion mein hain, aur 12² = 144 = 8 × 18.

### Example 1: Kya ye continued proportion mein hain?

(a) 5, 15, 45. (b) 9, 12, 16. (c) 6, 10, 15.

b² aur ac dekho (b beech wala number hai):
(a) b² = 15² = 225, ac = 5 × 45 = 225. Barabar, to **haan.** **Jaanch:** 5 : 15 = 1 : 3 aur 15 : 45 = 1 : 3 ✅.
(b) b² = 144, ac = 9 × 16 = 144. Barabar, to **haan.** **Jaanch:** 9 : 12 = 3 : 4 aur 12 : 16 = 3 : 4 ✅.
(c) b² = 100, ac = 6 × 15 = 90. Barabar nahi, to **nahi.** **Jaanch:** 6 : 10 = 3 : 5 par 10 : 15 = 2 : 3, dono ratio alag ✅.

**Kram matter karta hai.** 8, 12, 18 continued proportion mein hain, par 12, 8, 18 nahi: ab beech wala 8 hai, 8² = 64 aur 12 × 18 = 216, barabar nahi. Beech wala number wahi hona chahiye jo dono ratio mein aaye.

(Zero se bachna: 0, 0, 5 mein b² = ac ban jaata hai, par a : b ka matlab hi nahi banta. Isliye teeno numbers non-zero maane gaye hain, bilkul GP ki tarah.)

## Part B: Mean proportional: beech wala number

Do numbers a aur c ke beech ka wo number b jisme a : b = b : c. Part A se b² = ac, to

**b = √(ac)**  (a aur c positive hon to b positive lete hain)

**Kyun sirf √(ac)?** Kyunki b² = ac se b ko khud se guna karke ac milna chahiye, aur √ wahi undo karta hai. (b = −√(ac) bhi b² = ac ko poora karta hai, par GP chapter ke GM ki tarah mean proportional mein positive wala hi lete hain.)

### Example 2: 4 aur 49 ka mean proportional

ac = 4 × 49 = 196, √196 = 14 (14² = 196). To **mean proportional = 14.**
**Jaanch:** 4 : 14 = 2 : 7 aur 14 : 49 = 2 : 7 ✅.

**Ye average nahi hai.** 4 aur 49 ka average (AM) (4 + 49)/2 = 26.5 hai. Agar 26.5 ko beech mein rakhein: 4 : 26.5 = 8/53, aur 26.5 : 49 = 53/98. Ye barabar nahi, to 4, 26.5, 49 continued proportion nahi. Farak ye hai:
- **Average (AM):** beech wala number dono taraf se **utna hi door** (4 se 22.5 aage, 49 se 22.5 peechhe): barabar **gap**, jaise AP.
- **Mean proportional:** beech wala number dono taraf se **utna hi guna** (4 ko 3.5 se guna karke 14, 14 ko 3.5 se guna karke 49): barabar **ratio**, jaise GP.

Dono ek jaise tab hi aate hain jab a = c. Warna mean proportional hamesha average se **chhota** hota hai. Kyun: (a + c)/2 − √(ac) = (√a − √c)²/2, aur kisi bhi square se kam kuch nahi hota. (4 aur 49 par: (2 − 7)²/2 = 12.5, aur 26.5 − 14 = 12.5.)

### Example 3: 16 aur 81

ac = 16 × 81 = 1296, √1296 = 36 (36² = 1296). **Mean proportional = 36.**
**Jaanch:** 16 : 36 = 4 : 9 aur 36 : 81 = 4 : 9 ✅.

### Example 4: Decimal numbers: 0.3 aur 4.8

ac = 0.3 × 4.8 = 1.44, √1.44 = 1.2 (1.2 × 1.2 = 1.44). **Mean proportional = 1.2.**
**Jaanch:** 0.3/1.2 = 1/4 aur 1.2/4.8 = 1/4 ✅.

### Example 5: x nikalna

x + 6 ko x + 2 aur x + 14 ka mean proportional bana do. Shart (x + 6)² = (x + 2)(x + 14):
x² + 12x + 36 = x² + 16x + 28. Dono taraf x² kat gaya, to 36 − 28 = 16x − 12x, yaani 8 = 4x, **x = 2.**
Teen numbers: x + 2 = 4, x + 6 = 8, x + 14 = 16.
**Jaanch:** 4 : 8 = 1 : 2 aur 8 : 16 = 1 : 2 ✅.

### Example 6: Katoriyon ka set

Ek donga-set mein teen katoriyan continued proportion mein diameter wali hain. Sabse chhoti ka diameter 12 cm aur sabse badi ka 48 cm hai. Beech wali ka diameter nikalo.
Beech wali b = √(12 × 48) = √576 = **24 cm.**
**Story check:** diameter negative nahi ho sakta, to −24 reject. 12 < 24 < 48, to beech wali sach mein beech mein aati hai aur andar-bahar fit hogi. Ratio 12 : 24 = 1 : 2 aur 24 : 48 = 1 : 2.

**Ek chhoti si jyamiti baat:** ek rectangle p × q ka area pq hai, aur uske barabar area wale **square** ki side √(pq) hogi. Yani square ki side p aur q ka mean proportional hai. (5 m × 20 m ke rectangle ka area 100 m², barabar area wale square ki side 10 m, aur 5 : 10 = 10 : 20.)

## Part C: Third proportional: teesra number

Ab a aur b pata hain, aur c dhundhna hai jisme a : b = b : c. Part A se b² = ac, aur a se divide karo:

**c = b²/a**

**Kyun:** b² = ac mein c ko akela karne ke liye dono taraf a se divide kiya. Dhyan do: **dusra term b ka square, pehle term a se divide.** Naam bhi isiliye "third" hai: wo a aur b ke baad teesre sthaan par aata hai.

### Example 7: 16 aur 20 ka third proportional

c = 20²/16 = 400/16 = **25.**
**Jaanch:** 16 : 20 = 4 : 5 aur 20 : 25 = 4 : 5 ✅.

### Example 8: Ek hi triple ke teen roop

Ek continued proportion mein teen numbers ke teen "role" hote hain. 8, 12, 18 lo:

| Kya poochha | Kaise nikala | Jawab |
|---|---|---|
| 8 aur 18 ka mean proportional | √(8 × 18) = √144 | 12 |
| 8 aur 12 ka third proportional | 12²/8 = 144/8 | 18 |
| 18 aur 12 ka third proportional | 12²/18 = 144/18 | 8 |

Teesri line kaise banti hai: a : b = b : c se ulta (invertendo, Topic 2 ka) c : b = b : a bhi milta hai, yaani 18, 12, 8 bhi continued proportion mein hain. Isliye "continued proportion" ko ulte kram mein padho to bhi chalta hai (par beech wala beech mein hi rahe).

### Example 9: Parat-set ka teesra naap

Ek set ki do badi parat (nested trays) ke diameter 49 cm aur 42 cm hain, aur teesri bhi isi continued proportion mein hai (chhoti hoti jaa rahi hai). Teesri ka diameter?
c = 42²/49 = 1764/49 = **36 cm.**
**Story check:** 49 > 42 > 36, set ghatta hua hai, to teesri chhoti hi honi chahiye, aur 36 chhoti hai. Ratio 49 : 42 = 7 : 6 aur 42 : 36 = 7 : 6.
**Jaanch:** 42² = 1764 aur 49 × 36 = 1764 ✅.

## Part D: GP se rishta, aur a : c = a² : b²

GP chapter mein common ratio r = (agla term)/(pichhla term) tha. Agar a : b = b : c, to a/b = b/c. Dono ko ulta karo (invertendo): b/a = c/b. Ye b/a aur c/b wahi **common ratio r** hain. To a, b, c ek GP hain (b = ar, c = ar²), aur isiliye b² = ac, jo GP chapter mein "b² = ac ⇔ GP" kehlaya tha. Yani dono baatein ek hi hain: **continued proportion = teen terms ka GP**, aur beech wala term wahi GM.

Ek aur upyogi baat. Pehle aur teesre ka ratio beech wale ki madad se likhte hain.

**Kyun:** a/c ko do ratio ke guna ke roop mein tod do: a/c = (a/b) × (b/c). Continued proportion mein dono ratio barabar hain: a/b = b/c. To a/c = (a/b) × (a/b) = a²/b². Yaani

**a : c = a² : b²**

**Jaanch (8, 12, 18):** 8/18 = 4/9 aur 8²/12² = 64/144 = 4/9 ✅.

### Example 10: Pehle aur teesre ka ratio diya hai

Positive numbers a, b, c continued proportion mein hain aur a : c = 25 : 49. a : b : c nikalo.
Ab (a/b)² = a/c = 25/49, to a/b = 5/7 (positive numbers, to positive root). Phir b/c bhi 5/7 (dono ratio barabar). Isliye a : b = 5 : 7 aur b : c = 5 : 7. Ab b barabar karo (ye LCM wali chaal aage Part E mein poori samjhayi hai): b ke values 7 aur 5, LCM 35. a : b = 5 : 7 ko 5 se guna: 25 : 35, aur b : c = 5 : 7 ko 7 se guna: 35 : 49. To **a : b : c = 25 : 35 : 49.**
**Jaanch:** 35² = 1225 aur 25 × 49 = 1225 ✅. Aur 25 : 49 sach mein a : c hai.

## Part E: Do ratio ko jodkar a : b : c banana

Do ratio hain: a : b aur b : c. Hum chahte hain ek **teen ka ratio** a : b : c. Samasya ye hai ki b ke liye ek ratio mein ek number hai aur doosre mein doosra.

**Kyun LCM wali chaal:** ratio ko dono terms ek hi number se guna karne par ratio nahi badalta (4 : 6 aur 2 : 3 ek hi). To dono ratio ko is tarah guna karo ki **saanjha term b dono mein ek hi number ban jaaye.** Sabse chhota aisa number b ke dono values ka **LCM** hai.

### Example 11: 5 : 6 aur 9 : 4

a : b = 5 : 6 aur b : c = 9 : 4. Saanjha term b hai, uske values 6 aur 9. LCM(6, 9) = 18.
- a : b = 5 : 6, dono ko 3 se guna: 15 : 18.
- b : c = 9 : 4, dono ko 2 se guna: 18 : 8.
Ab b dono mein 18, to **a : b : c = 15 : 18 : 8.**
**Jaanch:** 15 : 18 = 5 : 6 ✅ aur 18 : 8 = 9 : 4 ✅.

```
a : ###############     15
b : ##################  18
c : ########            8
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Teen ka ratio 15 : 18 : 8, har # ek bhaag. a aur b ki bar ka ratio 15 : 18 = 5 : 6, b aur c ki bar ka ratio 18 : 8 = 9 : 4, dono wahi jo diye the.

(36 jaisa koi aur common multiple lete to 30 : 36 : 16 aata, jo sab ko 2 se divide karke wahi 15 : 18 : 8 ban jaata. LCM lene se sabse chhote numbers milte hain.)

### Example 12: Teen tankon mein tel

Ek tel-dukaan ke teen tankon mein kul 984 litre sarson ka tel hai. Pehle aur dusre tank ka ratio 5 : 6 hai, aur dusre aur teesre ka 9 : 4. Har tank mein kitna?
Example 11 se ratio 15 : 18 : 8, kul bhaag 15 + 18 + 8 = 41. 984 ÷ 41 = 24 litre ek bhaag. Tank: 15 × 24 = **360 L**, 18 × 24 = **432 L**, 8 × 24 = **192 L.**
**Jaanch:** 360 + 432 + 192 = 984 ✅, aur 360/432 = 5/6, 432/192 = 9/4 ✅.
**Story check:** teeno litre positive hain, aur dusra tank sabse bada hai (432 L), jaisa ratio mein 18 sabse bada bhaag hai.

### Example 13: Jab saanjha term dono mein dusre sthaan par ho

a : b = 3 : 4 aur c : b = 5 : 6. Yahan b dono ratio mein **dusre** sthaan par hai, to ghumane ki zaroorat nahi: seedha b ko barabar karo. LCM(4, 6) = 12.
- a : b = 3 : 4, dono ko 3 se guna: 9 : 12.
- c : b = 5 : 6, dono ko 2 se guna: 10 : 12.
To b = 12 dono mein, a = 9 aur c = 10: **a : b : c = 9 : 12 : 10.**
**Jaanch:** a : b = 9 : 12 = 3 : 4 ✅ aur c : b = 10 : 12 = 5 : 6 ✅. (Dhyan do: teen ka ratio hamesha a, b, c ke kram mein likha jaata hai, to c ka bhaag aakhir mein 10.)

### Example 14: Chaar cheezein: teen ratio jodna

a : b = 2 : 3, b : c = 4 : 5, c : d = 6 : 7. Pehle b barabar karo: LCM(3, 4) = 12. a : b = 8 : 12 aur b : c = 12 : 15, to a : b : c = 8 : 12 : 15. Ab c barabar karo: LCM(15, 6) = 30. a : b : c ko 2 se guna: 16 : 24 : 30, aur c : d = 6 : 7 ko 5 se guna: 30 : 35. To **a : b : c : d = 16 : 24 : 30 : 35.**
**Jaanch:** 16 : 24 = 2 : 3 ✅, 24 : 30 = 4 : 5 ✅, 30 : 35 = 6 : 7 ✅.

## Part F: Chaar ki chain ki jhalak

Ab Part A ko ek kadam aage badhao: **a : b = b : c = c : d.** Yani teen kadam ke teeno ratio barabar. Akhil ki chain 8, 12, 18 ko ek aur katori 27 cm se badhao: 8, 12, 18, 27.

Teeno ratio dekho: 8 : 12 = 2 : 3, 12 : 18 = 2 : 3 aur 18 : 27 = 2 : 3.

```
1st : ########
2nd : ############
3rd : ##################
4th : ###########################
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Chaar katoriyon ke diameter 8, 12, 18 aur 27 cm, har # ek cm. Har agli bar pichhli se 3/2 guna lambi: 8 se 12, 12 se 18, 18 se 27.

**Kyun teen shartein banti hain:** har padosi jodi par product rule lagao (Part A).
- a : b = b : c se **b² = ac**: 12² = 144 = 8 × 18.
- b : c = c : d se **c² = bd**: 18² = 324 = 12 × 27.
- a/b = c/d (pehla aur teesra ratio barabar) se **ad = bc**: 8 × 27 = 216 = 12 × 18.

**Pehle aur chauthe ka rishta:** a/d = (a/b) × (b/c) × (c/d), aur teeno ratio barabar hain, to a/d = (a/b)³. Jaanch: 8/27 = (2/3)³ = 8/27 ✅. Isse jab sirf pehla aur aakhri term pata ho, beech ke do nikalte hain.

### Example 15: Do beech ke terms

8, x, y, 216 continued proportion mein (chain). a/d = 8/216 = 1/27 = (1/3)³, to a/b = 1/3, yaani 8/x = 1/3, x = 8 × 3 = **24.** Phir y: 24/y = 1/3, y = 24 × 3 = **72.**
**Jaanch:** 8, 24, 72, 216: 24² = 576 aur 8 × 72 = 576 ✅; 72² = 5184 aur 24 × 216 = 5184 ✅; 8 : 24 = 1 : 3 = 24 : 72 = 72 : 216 ✅.

(Barabar ratio ko **ek hi naam** dekar sab kuch likhne ka tareeka Topic 4 mein hai: wahan teen terms ki chain a : b = b : c ek akshar k se ek line mein bandhegi. Yahan humne chaar ki chain ki baat sirf ratio ke guna se nikaali hai.)

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Mean proportional ko average le lena (3 aur 27 ke beech)**

Ek bachcha poochhta hai: "3 aur 27 ka mean proportional?" Wo "mean" shabd dekhkar average le leta hai: (3 + 27)/2 = **15.** Jaanch kare to: 3 : 15 = 1 : 5, par 15 : 27 = 5 : 9. 1/5 aur 5/9 barabar nahi, to 3, 15, 27 continued proportion **nahi** hain.
**Sahi:** b = √(3 × 27) = √81 = **9.** 3 : 9 = 1 : 3 aur 9 : 27 = 1 : 3 ✅, aur 9² = 81 = 3 × 27.
**Kyun galat hua:** average beech ka wo number hai jo dono taraf se utna hi **door** hai (gap barabar), jabki mean proportional wo hai jo dono taraf se utna hi **guna** hai (ratio barabar). Is demo mein 15, 3 se 12 aage aur 27 se 12 peechhe tha, par ratio 5 aur 9/5 alag the.

**Galti 2: Third proportional ke liye b²/a ki jagah a²/b**

Do numbers 18 aur 30 ka third proportional poochha. Bachcha dono ko ulta-sulta jod deta hai: "a²/b = 18²/30 = 324/30 = **10.8.**" Jaanch: 18 : 30 = 3 : 5, par 30 : 10.8 = 25 : 9. Alag ratio, to 18, 30, 10.8 continued proportion nahi.
**Sahi:** c = b²/a = 30²/18 = 900/18 = **50.** 18 : 30 = 3 : 5 aur 30 : 50 = 3 : 5 ✅.
**Kyun galat hua:** formula c = b²/a mein **beech wale (dusre) term ka square** upar aata hai aur **pehle term** se divide hota hai. Sahi yaad rakhne ka tareeka: ek hi sentence "dusra term ka square, pehle se divide." Jaanch hamesha wahi: b² = ac.

## Quick Recap

- Teen numbers a, b, c **continued proportion** mein: a : b = b : c, yaani a/b = b/c. Shart: **b² = ac** (non-zero). Kyun: a/b = b/c ko bc se guna karo. Kram matter karta hai: beech wala wahi jo dono ratio mein aaye.
- **Mean proportional** b = √(ac) (a, c positive). Ye average (a + c)/2 nahi hai: average barabar gap deta hai, mean proportional barabar ratio. Mean proportional hamesha average se chhota (barabar tab jab a = c).
- **Third proportional** c = b²/a: "dusra ka square, pehle se divide." Ek continued proportion a, b, c mein b mean proportional hai, c third proportional hai a aur b ka, aur a third proportional hai c aur b ka.
- Continued proportion = **teen terms ka GP**: common ratio r = b/a = c/b, shart wahi b² = ac. Ek upyogi nateeja: **a : c = a² : b².**
- Do ratio **a : b aur b : c** ko jodna: saanjha term b dono mein barabar karo (LCM se) aur phir teen ka ratio **a : b : c** padho. Teen ratio ho to doobara saanjha term barabar karo.
- Chaar ki chain a : b = b : c = c : d mein: **b² = ac, c² = bd, ad = bc** aur a/d = (a/b)³. Isi se beech ke do terms nikalte hain.
- **Galti 1:** mean proportional average nahi. **Galti 2:** third proportional c = b²/a, a²/b nahi.

## Practice Questions

2. Find the mean proportional between 5 and 80, and check it by writing both ratios.

3. Find the mean proportional between 0.2 and 1.8.

4. Find the third proportional to 50 and 70.

5. Find the value of x for which 2x + 2 is the mean proportional between x + 3 and 4x − 3. Write the three numbers and check them.

6. If a : b = 4 : 7 and b : c = 3 : 5, find a : b : c.

7. If a : b = 5 : 8 and c : b = 3 : 4, find a : b : c.

8. A florist sells marigold, rose and jasmine flowers, 810 in all. The marigold to rose ratio is 8 : 3 and the rose to jasmine ratio is 6 : 5. How many flowers of each kind are there? Do a story check.

9. Four numbers 3, x, y, 648 are in continued proportion (3 : x = x : y = y : 648). Find x and y.

10. If a, b, c are in continued proportion, show that (a + b)/(b + c) = a/b. Then check it for 49, 56, 64.

11. A rectangular garden is 4.5 m by 8 m. Find the side of a square garden that has the same area, and state which number is the mean proportional of which two numbers.

12. State True or False, with a reason: (a) The mean proportional between 8 and 32 is 20. (b) If b is the mean proportional between positive numbers a and c, then c is the third proportional to a and b. (c) The third proportional to 5 and 10 is 20. (d) If a : b = 2 : 3 and b : c = 3 : 5, then a : b : c = 2 : 3 : 5. (e) If a : b = 3 : 4 and b : c = 5 : 6, then a : b : c = 3 : 4 : 6.

13. The mean proportional between two numbers is 18, and one of the numbers is 54. Find the other number, and the ratio of the two numbers.

### Answers

1. Har ek mein b² aur ac dekho.
(a) b² = 35² = 1225, ac = 7 × 175 = 1225. Barabar, to **haan.** **Jaanch:** 7 : 35 = 1 : 5 aur 35 : 175 = 1 : 5 ✅.
(b) b² = 441, ac = 14 × 35 = 490. Barabar nahi, to **nahi.** **Jaanch:** 14 : 21 = 2 : 3 par 21 : 35 = 3 : 5 ✅.
(c) b² = 70² = 4900, ac = 100 × 49 = 4900. To **haan.** **Jaanch:** 100 : 70 = 10 : 7 aur 70 : 49 = 10 : 7 ✅.

2. ac = 5 × 80 = 400, √400 = 20. **Mean proportional = 20.** **Jaanch:** 5 : 20 = 1 : 4 aur 20 : 80 = 1 : 4 ✅.

3. ac = 0.2 × 1.8 = 0.36, √0.36 = 0.6. **Mean proportional = 0.6.** **Jaanch:** 0.2/0.6 = 1/3 aur 0.6/1.8 = 1/3 ✅.

4. c = b²/a = 70²/50 = 4900/50 = **98.** **Jaanch:** 50 : 70 = 5 : 7 aur 70 : 98 = 5 : 7 ✅.

5. Shart (2x + 2)² = (x + 3)(4x − 3). Left: 4x² + 8x + 4. Right: 4x² + 12x − 3x − 9 = 4x² + 9x − 9. 4x² kat gaya: 8x + 4 = 9x − 9, to **x = 13.** Teen numbers: x + 3 = 16, 2x + 2 = 28, 4x − 3 = 49. **Jaanch:** 28² = 784 aur 16 × 49 = 784 ✅; 16 : 28 = 4 : 7 aur 28 : 49 = 4 : 7 ✅.

6. Saanjha term b: 7 aur 3, LCM 21. a : b = 4 : 7, dono ko 3 se guna: 12 : 21. b : c = 3 : 5, dono ko 7 se guna: 21 : 35. **a : b : c = 12 : 21 : 35.** **Jaanch:** 12 : 21 = 4 : 7 ✅ aur 21 : 35 = 3 : 5 ✅.

7. b dono ratio mein dusre sthaan par hai: 8 aur 4, LCM 8. a : b = 5 : 8 jaisa hai. c : b = 3 : 4, dono ko 2 se guna: 6 : 8. To **a : b : c = 5 : 8 : 6.** **Jaanch:** 5 : 8 ✅ aur c : b = 6 : 8 = 3 : 4 ✅.

8. Marigold : rose = 8 : 3 aur rose : jasmine = 6 : 5. Saanjha term rose: 3 aur 6, LCM 6. Pehla ratio 2 se guna: 16 : 6. Dusra ratio waise hi: 6 : 5. To ratio **16 : 6 : 5**, kul bhaag 27. 810 ÷ 27 = 30. Marigold 16 × 30 = **480**, rose 6 × 30 = **180**, jasmine 5 × 30 = **150.** **Jaanch:** 480 + 180 + 150 = 810 ✅; 480/180 = 8/3 ✅ aur 180/150 = 6/5 ✅. **Story check:** teeno sankhya poori (phool aadhe nahi hote) aur marigold sabse zyada, jaisa ratio mein 16 sabse bada bhaag hai.

9. 3/648 = 1/216 = (1/6)³, to 3/x = 1/6, x = 3 × 6 = **18.** Phir 18/y = 1/6, y = 18 × 6 = **108.** **Jaanch:** 18² = 324 aur 3 × 108 = 324 ✅; 108² = 11664 aur 18 × 648 = 11664 ✅; 3 : 18 = 1 : 6 = 18 : 108 = 108 : 648 ✅.

10. a/b = b/c se b² = ac. Ab (a + b) × b = ab + b² = ab + ac = a(b + c). Dono taraf b(b + c) se divide karo: (a + b)/(b + c) = a/b. Yahi dikhana tha. (b² = ac continued proportion ki apni shart hai, isliye proof ka pehla kadam wahi hai.) **Number jaanch (49, 56, 64):** 56² = 3136 aur 49 × 64 = 3136, to continued proportion hain. (49 + 56)/(56 + 64) = 105/120 = 7/8 aur a/b = 49/56 = 7/8 ✅.

11. Area = 4.5 × 8 = 36 m². Square ki side = √36 = **6 m.** 4.5 : 6 = 3 : 4 aur 6 : 8 = 3 : 4 ✅. To **6 m, 4.5 aur 8 ka mean proportional** hai. **Story check:** side positive honi chahiye, to −6 reject; 4.5 < 6 < 8 sahi.

12. (a) **False** ❌. 8 × 32 = 256, √256 = 16, mean proportional 16 hai. 20 to average hai (8 + 32)/2. (b) **True** ✅. b² = ac se c = b²/a, wahi third proportional ka formula. (c) **True** ✅. 10²/5 = 100/5 = 20, aur 5 : 10 = 1 : 2 = 10 : 20. (d) **True** ✅. b ke values 3 aur 3 pehle se barabar hain, to seedha 2 : 3 : 5. (e) **False** ❌. b ke values 4 aur 5 alag hain, LCM 20 se a : b = 15 : 20 aur b : c = 20 : 24, to a : b : c = 15 : 20 : 24.

13. b² = ac se 18² = 54 × c, 324 = 54c, c = **6.** Ratio **54 : 6 = 9 : 1.** **Jaanch:** 54 : 18 = 3 : 1 aur 18 : 6 = 3 : 1 ✅.

**Aage kya?** Topic 4 mein hum barabar ratio ko **ek hi akshar k** denge (teen terms ki chain a : b = b : c se shuru karke). Phir dekhenge ki a aur c ko b aur d ke roop mein likhne se lambe proofs aur equations kaise chhote ho jaate hain, aur ek ratio se doosra ratio kaise nikalta hai.
