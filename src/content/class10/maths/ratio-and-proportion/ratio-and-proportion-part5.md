## Kalpesh ka tiffin startup

Kalpesh aur uske do dost apni society mein ghar ka khana pahunchane ka tiffin-service shuru karna chahte the. Teeno ne paise milaye: Kalpesh ne ₹ 45,000, ek dost ne ₹ 30,000 aur doosre ne ₹ 25,000. (Ye kahani ke apne numbers hain.) Pehle mahine ke baad Mummy ne register kholkar hisaab dekha: saare kharche ke baad **₹ 40,000 ka munafa** bacha tha. Sawaal tha: ye teeno mein kaise baante?

Kalpesh ne jhat se kaha, "Teeno mein barabar, ₹ 13,333 har ek!" Dusre dost ne virodh kiya: "Meri jeb se sirf ₹ 25,000 gaye aur tumhari jeb se ₹ 45,000. Barabar kaise?" Mummy ne paise ka ratio likha: 45,000 : 30,000 : 25,000, jo sabse chhote roop mein **9 : 6 : 5** hota hai. "Munafa isi ratio mein baantna chahiye. 9 + 6 + 5 = 20 hisse, aur har hissa ₹ 40,000 ÷ 20 = ₹ 2,000 ka."

Kalpesh ke hisse: 9 × 2,000 = ₹ 18,000, aur baaki do ke ₹ 12,000 aur ₹ 10,000. Teeno jod ₹ 40,000. "Ye to sahi baith gaya!" Par uske baad sawaal aur bhi aaye: agar ek dost aadhe mahine baad paise daalta to? Agar dahi aur paani ka mixture banana ho? Agar delivery bike ki chaal badal jaaye? Agar ek model-van banwani ho? In sab ko hal karne ke liye ek hi tareeka kaam aata hai, aur wahi aaj hum seekhenge.

## Part A: Chaar kadam aur ratio mein baantna

Chaar kadam wahi hain jo tumne Quadratic Equations aur Linear Inequations mein laga rakhe hain:

```
1. ANJAAN    : kya dhundhna hai? Use x kaho (units ke saath)
        :
        v
2. EQUATION  : ratio ko parts mein badlo, kahani ki shart likho
        :
        v
3. HAL       : parts jodo, ek part nikalo, phir har hissa
        :
        v
4. STORY CHECK: jod diya hua total hai? ratio wahi hai? jawab realistic?
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Word problem ke chaar kadam. Aakhri kadam decide karta hai ki jawab kahani mein sahi baithta hai ya nahi.

**Kyun parts se?** Agar kul raashi T aur ratio a : b ho, to ek **part** p lo. Tab pehle ka hissa a × p aur doosre ka b × p hai (k-method ka hi "ek tin ka size" wala vichaar). Jod: (a + b) × p = T, to **p = T ÷ (a + b).** Isiliye hissa "ratio ka pad × ek part" hota hai, na ki sirf ratio ka pad.

### Example 1: ₹ 91,000 ko 8 : 5 mein baantna

Do bhai ₹ 91,000 ka inaam 8 : 5 mein baantte hain. Dono ko kitna?

1. **Anjaan:** ek part = p.
2. **Equation:** (8 + 5) × p = 91,000.
3. **Hal:** 13p = 91,000, p = 7,000. Pehle ko 8 × 7,000 = ₹ 56,000, doosre ko 5 × 7,000 = ₹ 35,000.

**Story check:** 56,000 + 35,000 = ₹ 91,000 (diya hua total) aur 56,000 : 35,000 = 8 : 5 (dono ko 7,000 se divide karo). Pehle ko zyada milna tha, wahi hua.
**Jaanch:** 8/5 = 56,000/35,000 ✅.

### Example 2: Teen logon ka ratio, do ratio ko jodkar

Teeno partners A, B, C ka munafa baantna hai: A : B = 3 : 4 aur B : C = 6 : 7. Kul munafa ₹ 1,05,000. Teeno ko kitna?

1. **Anjaan:** pehle teeno ka ek hi ratio A : B : C, phir ek part p.
2. **Equation:** saanjha term B. Topic 3 ki tarah B ko barabar karo: 3 : 4 ko 3 se guna karo to 9 : 12, aur 6 : 7 ko 2 se guna karo to 12 : 14. Ab B dono mein 12 hai, to **A : B : C = 9 : 12 : 14.** (9 + 12 + 14) × p = 1,05,000.
3. **Hal:** 35p = 1,05,000, p = 3,000. A = 27,000, B = 36,000, C = 42,000.

**Story check:** 27,000 + 36,000 + 42,000 = ₹ 1,05,000, aur 27 : 36 = 3 : 4, 36 : 42 = 6 : 7, dono diye hue ratio wahi.
**Jaanch:** 27,000/36,000 = 3/4 ✅ aur 36,000/42,000 = 6/7 ✅.

## Part B: Partnership

**Kyun?** Jab sab partner **barabar samay** ke liye kaam mein rehte hain, to jisne zyada paisa lagaya uska hissa zyada hona chahiye, aur ye "kitna guna" ke hisaab se hona chahiye. Isliye **munafa = investment ke ratio mein.** (Agar kisi ne beech mein paisa daala ho, to samay bhi jodna padta hai: investment × mahine. Yahan hum barabar samay wale sawaal hi lenge.)

### Example 3: teen partners, ek munafa

P ne ₹ 72,000, Q ne ₹ 48,000 aur R ne ₹ 30,000 lagaye. Saal ke ant mein ₹ 1,25,000 ka munafa hua. Har ek ka hissa?

1. **Anjaan:** investment ratio, phir ek part p.
2. **Equation:** 72,000 : 48,000 : 30,000 ko 6,000 se divide karo: **12 : 8 : 5.** (12 + 8 + 5) × p = 1,25,000.
3. **Hal:** 25p = 1,25,000, p = 5,000. P = 60,000, Q = 40,000, R = 25,000.

**Story check:** 60,000 + 40,000 + 25,000 = ₹ 1,25,000 aur 60 : 40 : 25 = 12 : 8 : 5 (5,000 se divide). Sabse zyada paisa P ka tha, usi ka hissa sabse bada.
**Jaanch:** 60,000/72,000 = 5/6, 40,000/48,000 = 5/6, 25,000/30,000 = 5/6 ✅ (har partner ko lagaye hue har ₹ 6 par ₹ 5).

### Example 4: ek partner ka paisa nikalna

Do partners ka munafa 7 : 4 mein baanta gaya. Chhote partner (4 wale) ne ₹ 56,000 lagaye the. Bade partner ne kitna lagaya?

1. **Anjaan:** bade partner ka investment x.
2. **Equation:** investment ka ratio bhi 7 : 4 hai, to x : 56,000 = 7 : 4, yaani x/56,000 = 7/4 (Topic 1 ka fourth proportional).
3. **Hal:** x = 56,000 × 7/4 = **₹ 98,000.**

**Story check:** bade partner ka investment bada hona chahiye tha, aur 98,000 > 56,000 hai.
**Jaanch:** 98,000 : 56,000 = 7 : 4 (14,000 se divide) ✅.

## Part C: Mixture

**Kyun?** Mixture ka ratio "kaun kitna guna" batata hai, to parts wahi tareeka. Par dhyan: agar kuch **milao** ya **hatao**, to ratio badal jaata hai, kyunki ek hi cheez ki matra badli. Dusri cheez ki matra tab ek jagah **sthir** (fixed) rehti hai, use pakad kar chalo.

### Example 5: doodh aur paani, 7 : 3

Ek bartan mein 40 L doodh-paani ka mixture hai, doodh : paani = 7 : 3. Dono kitne kitne?

1. **Anjaan:** ek part p (L mein).
2. **Equation:** (7 + 3) × p = 40.
3. **Hal:** p = 4. Doodh = 28 L, paani = 12 L.

**Story check:** 28 + 12 = 40 L, aur 28 : 12 = 7 : 3, doodh zyada hai jaise ratio kehta hai.
**Jaanch:** 28/12 = 7/3 ✅.

### Example 6: paani milaya, ratio badla

Example 5 ke mixture mein kitna paani milayein ki doodh : paani = 7 : 5 ho jaye?

1. **Anjaan:** milaya gaya paani w L.
2. **Equation:** doodh 28 L **wahi** rehta hai, paani 12 + w ho jaata hai: 28/(12 + w) = 7/5.
3. **Hal:** cross-multiply (Topic 1): 7(12 + w) = 28 × 5 = 140, 12 + w = 20, **w = 8 L.**

**Story check:** naya mixture 28 L doodh aur 20 L paani ka hai, yaani 48 L. Paani badha to doodh ka hissa kam hua, jaise 7 : 3 se 7 : 5 mein hona chahiye (paani ka pad 3 se 5 bada).
**Jaanch:** 28 : 20 = 7 : 5 (4 se divide) ✅.

## Part D: Scale model

**Kyun?** Scale 1 : n ka matlab model ki har 1 cm asli n cm ke barabar hai. Model aur asli cheez **ek hi tarah ki lambai** hain, to unka ratio hamesha wahi (proportion a : b :: c : d). Bas **unit ek karna** (Topic 1) mat bhoolna.

```
Asli van  : [==============================]  4.8 m = 480 cm
Model van : [=====]                           15 cm
Scale 1 : 32  (model ka 1 cm = asli ka 32 cm)
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Kalpesh ki tiffin-van ka model. Model asli se 32 guna chhota hai, par uski har lambai (lambai, chauraai, oonchai) 32 guna chhoti hai, isliye model "sahi shakl" ka dikhta hai. (Figure mein jagah bachane ke liye lambai sirf 6 : 1 dikhayi hai, asli scale 1 : 32 hai.)

### Example 7: model aur asli

Ek van ka model scale 1 : 32 par bana hai. (a) Asli lambai 4.8 m hai, model ki lambai? (b) Model ki oonchai 6.5 cm hai, asli oonchai?

1. **Anjaan:** (a) model ki lambai y cm. (b) asli oonchai h cm.
2. **Equation:** (a) 4.8 m = 480 cm. y : 480 = 1 : 32. (b) 6.5 : h = 1 : 32.
3. **Hal:** (a) y = 480/32 = **15 cm.** (b) h = 6.5 × 32 = 208 cm = **2.08 m.**

**Story check:** model asli se chhota aana chahiye, aur 15 cm < 480 cm hai. Asli oonchai 2.08 m ek van ke liye maqool hai (model ki oonchai 6.5 cm, lambai 15 cm, aur asli 2.08 m : 4.8 m ka ratio 13 : 30, jaise 6.5 : 15 = 13 : 30).
**Jaanch:** 15 × 32 = 480 ✅ aur 6.5 × 32 = 208 ✅.

## Part E: Chaal, samay aur doori (sankshep mein)

**Kyun?** Ek hi chaal par doori samay ke ratio mein badhti hai: doguna samay to doguni doori. Ye **seedha proportion** hai (Class 9 ke gas laws mein Charles ke niyam mein dekha tha, jahan volume Kelvin temperature ke "directly proportional" tha: [gas-laws-part3.md](../../../class9/chemistry/gas-laws/gas-laws-part3.md)). Par **doori ek hi rakhi** ho to chaal badhne par samay **ghat** jaata hai: chaal 3 : 4 to samay 4 : 3 (ulta ratio).

### Example 8: ek hi chaal, kitni doori?

Delivery bike 35 minute mein 14 km jaati hai. Usi chaal par 1 ghanta 20 minute mein kitni doori?

1. **Anjaan:** doori d km.
2. **Equation:** samay minute mein: 1 ghanta 20 minute = 80 minute (unit ek). Doori ka ratio samay ke ratio ke barabar: 14 : d :: 35 : 80 (km se km, minute se minute). Extremes 14 aur 80, means d aur 35.
3. **Hal:** 35d = 14 × 80 = 1120, **d = 32 km.**

**Story check:** 80 minute 35 minute se zyada hai, to doori 14 km se zyada honi chahiye, aur 32 > 14 hai. Chaal 14/35 = 0.4 km har minute, 32/80 = 0.4 km har minute, wahi.
**Jaanch:** 32/80 = 2/5 aur 14/35 = 2/5 ✅.

### Example 9: do chaal, ek doori

Do delivery boy ek hi raaste par jaate hain. Chaal 45 km/h aur 60 km/h. Dheemi chaal wale ko 36 minute lage, to tez wale ko?

1. **Anjaan:** tez wale ka samay t minute.
2. **Equation:** chaal ka ratio 45 : 60 = 3 : 4, to samay ka ratio **ulta**: 4 : 3 (dheema : tez). 36 : t = 4 : 3.
3. **Hal:** t = 36 × 3/4 = **27 minute.**

**Story check:** tez wale ko kam samay lagna chahiye, aur 27 < 36 hai.
**Jaanch:** doori = chaal × samay. Dheema: 45 × 36/60 = 27 km. Tez: 60 × 27/60 = 27 km, dono ki doori barabar ✅.

## Part F: Umar ke sawaal

**Kyun?** Umar ke sawaal mein ratio **ek ya do alag samay** ke liye diya hota hai. Ratio ke pad umar nahi hain, umar ke **hissedaar** hain (ratio ki ek part). Isliye ek vyakti ki umar a × x aur doosre ki b × x lo. Kuch saal baad dono mein **wahi number** jodna hota hai (ya pehle dono se wahi ghatana). Ratio mein number jodna **nahi** hai.

### Example 10: Papa aur beta, ab aur 8 saal baad

Papa aur bete ki umar ka ratio abhi 10 : 3 hai. 8 saal baad ratio 12 : 5 ho jaayega. Dono ki abhi umar?

1. **Anjaan:** abhi umar 10x aur 3x saal.
2. **Equation:** 8 saal baad (10x + 8) aur (3x + 8): (10x + 8)/(3x + 8) = 12/5.
3. **Hal:** 5(10x + 8) = 12(3x + 8), 50x + 40 = 36x + 96, 14x = 56, x = 4. Papa 40 saal, beta 12 saal.

**Story check:** 8 saal baad Papa 48 aur beta 20 saal: 48 : 20 = 12 : 5 wahi diya hua ratio. Umar maqool hai (Papa 40, beta 12).
**Jaanch:** 40/12 = 10/3 ✅ aur 48/20 = 12/5 ✅.

### Example 11: Mummy aur beti, 5 saal pehle

Mummy aur beti ki umar ka ratio abhi 8 : 3 hai, aur 5 saal pehle 7 : 2 tha. Abhi umar?

1. **Anjaan:** abhi umar 8x aur 3x.
2. **Equation:** 5 saal pehle (8x − 5) aur (3x − 5): (8x − 5)/(3x − 5) = 7/2.
3. **Hal:** 2(8x − 5) = 7(3x − 5), 16x − 10 = 21x − 35, 25 = 5x, x = 5. Mummy 40 saal, beti 15 saal.

**Story check:** 5 saal pehle Mummy 35 aur beti 10: 35 : 10 = 7 : 2. Beti ki umar 5 saal pehle 10 thi, kuch negative nahi, theek.
**Jaanch:** 40/15 = 8/3 ✅.

## Part G: Kaun sa auzaar, kab?

| Sawaal ka suraagh | Kya karo |
|---|---|
| Raashi ko a : b ya a : b : c mein baantna | Parts jodo, ek part = kul ÷ parts, har hissa |
| A : B aur B : C alag diye hain | Saanjha term barabar karo, a : b : c banao (Topic 3) |
| Munafa baantna, investment diya hai | Investment ka ratio (barabar samay), phir parts |
| Mixture mein kuch milaya ya hataya | Jo cheez nahi badli use pakad kar proportion likho |
| Model, naksha, chaal, samay | Pehle ek unit, phir a : b :: c : d aur ad = bc |
| (A + B) aur (A − B) ka ratio diya hai | Componendo-dividendo: A/B = (p + q)/(p − q) (Topic 4) |
| Do ratio barabar hain, ek relation hai | k-method: a = bk, c = dk (Topic 4) |
| Beech wala term (a : b = b : c) | b² = ac, mean b = √(ac), third c = b²/a (Topic 3) |
| Umar, ratio do samay ke liye | x lo, dono mein wahi number jodo ya ghatao, phir cross-multiply |

## Part H: Purane topics ko jodne wale sawaal

### Example 12: jod aur antar ka ratio (Topic 2 aur Topic 4)

Do partners ka munafa A aur B hai. Dono ka jod ₹ 66,000 hai, aur (A + B)/(A − B) = 11/3. A aur B alag alag?

1. **Anjaan:** A aur B (rupaye mein).
2. **Equation:** Topic 4 ka niyam: (A + B)/(A − B) = p/q se A/B = (p + q)/(p − q). Yahan p/q = 11/3.
3. **Hal:** A/B = (11 + 3)/(11 − 3) = 14/8 = 7/4. To A : B = 7 : 4, 11 parts, ek part 66,000 ÷ 11 = 6,000. A = **₹ 42,000**, B = **₹ 24,000.**

**Story check:** A + B = 66,000 diya hua jod, aur A − B = 18,000, to 66,000/18,000 = 11/3 diya hua ratio.
**Jaanch:** (42,000 + 24,000)/(42,000 − 24,000) = 66,000/18,000 = 11/3 ✅.

### Example 13: investment continued proportion mein (Topic 3 aur GP)

Teen partners X, Y, Z ke investment a : b = b : c mein hain (continued proportion). X ne ₹ 20,000 aur Z ne ₹ 45,000 lagaye. Y ka investment, aur ₹ 1,52,000 ka munafa teeno mein?

1. **Anjaan:** Y ka investment b, phir munafa.
2. **Equation:** b² = ac = 20,000 × 45,000 = 90,00,00,000.
3. **Hal:** b = √(90,00,00,000) = ₹ 30,000. Investment 20,000 : 30,000 : 45,000 = 4 : 6 : 9, 19 parts, ek part ₹ 1,52,000 ÷ 19 = 8,000. X = ₹ 32,000, Y = ₹ 48,000, Z = ₹ 72,000.

**Story check:** Y ka investment 30,000 X aur Z ke beech mein hai (20,000 < 30,000 < 45,000). Ye average 32,500 nahi hai (Topic 3 ki galti na ho). Hissa 32,000 + 48,000 + 72,000 = ₹ 1,52,000.
**Jaanch:** 20,000/30,000 = 2/3 aur 30,000/45,000 = 2/3 ✅ (teeno GP mein, common ratio 3/2 ya ulte order mein 2/3).

### Example 14: relation se ratio, phir baantna (Topic 4)

A ke paise ka 2/3 hissa B ke paise ke 3/4 hisse ke barabar hai. Dono ke paise ka jod ₹ 1,19,000. Alag alag?

1. **Anjaan:** A aur B ke paise.
2. **Equation:** (2/3)A = (3/4)B, dono taraf 12 se guna: 8A = 9B. To A/B = 9/8.
3. **Hal:** A : B = 9 : 8, 17 parts, ek part 1,19,000 ÷ 17 = 7,000. A = ₹ 63,000, B = ₹ 56,000.

**Story check:** A + B = ₹ 1,19,000 aur A ko zyada milna chahiye tha (A ka coefficient chhota tha, to A bada).
**Jaanch:** (2/3) × 63,000 = 42,000 aur (3/4) × 56,000 = 42,000 ✅.

### Example 15: alag unit, phir fourth proportional (Topic 1)

Kalpesh ke tiffin mein chawal aur dal ki matra ka ratio 2 kg 400 g : 1 kg 600 g mein hai. Agar dal 8 kg ho, to chawal kitna?

1. **Anjaan:** chawal c kg.
2. **Equation:** pehle ek unit: 2.4 kg : 1.6 kg = 24 : 16 = 3 : 2. c : 8 = 3 : 2.
3. **Hal:** c = 8 × 3/2 = **12 kg.**

**Story check:** chawal zyada hona chahiye tha (3 > 2), aur 12 kg > 8 kg hai.
**Jaanch:** 12/8 = 3/2 ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Ratio ke pad hi hissa bana dena (ya total ko ek pad se divide karna)**

Sawaal: ₹ 840 do dost 3 : 4 mein baantein. Ek bachche ne likha: "Pehle ko ₹ 3, doosre ko ₹ 4." Doosre bachche ne socha: "Total 840 hai, to 840 ÷ 3 = 280 aur 840 ÷ 4 = 210."
**Ulta udaharan:** pehle bachche ka total 3 + 4 = ₹ 7, jabki baantna ₹ 840 tha. Doosre bachche ke ₹ 280 + ₹ 210 = ₹ 490, jo 840 nahi, aur 280 : 210 = 4 : 3 (ulta ho gaya, kyunki jo pad chhota tha uska hissa bada ban gaya).
Kyun galat? Ratio ke pad hissa nahi, **parts** hain. Pehle total parts jodo (3 + 4 = 7), ek part = 840 ÷ 7 = ₹ 120, phir pehle ko 3 × 120 = ₹ 360, doosre ko 4 × 120 = ₹ 480.
**Jaanch:** 360 + 480 = 840 ✅ aur 360 : 480 = 3 : 4 ✅.

**Galti 2: "A se B" ke ratio mein order ulta kar dena**

Sawaal: ek mixture 54 L ka hai. "Doodh aur paani" ka zikr pehle aaya, aur kaha gaya hai "ratio of water to milk is 2 : 7" (yaani paani **se** doodh ka ratio 2 : 7 hai). Ek bachche ne doodh ke liye 2 aur paani ke liye 7 liya: doodh 12 L, paani 42 L.
**Ulta udaharan:** is hisaab se mixture mein doodh 12 L aur paani 42 L, yaani paani doodh se bahut zyada. Par "paani se doodh" 2 : 7 ka matlab paani 2 parts aur doodh 7 parts, yaani **doodh zyada**. Bachche ne order ulta padha.
Kyun galat? "A to B" ya "A se B" ka ratio **pehle A, phir B**: ratio ka pehla pad wahi jiska naam "se" ke pehle aaya. Doosra pad wo jiska naam "se" ke baad aaya. Sahi: paani 2 parts, doodh 7 parts, 9 parts, ek part 54 ÷ 9 = 6 L. Paani 12 L, doodh 42 L.
**Jaanch:** 12 + 42 = 54 ✅ aur paani : doodh = 12 : 42 = 2 : 7 ✅.

**Galti 3: Umar ke ratio mein saalon ko ratio ke pad mein jodna**

Sawaal: Mummy aur bete ki umar ka ratio 11 : 4 hai, aur 6 saal baad 13 : 6 hoga. Ek bachche ne socha: "6 saal baad dono mein 6 jodo: 11 + 6 = 17 aur 4 + 6 = 10, to 17 : 10." Wo bola, "ye 13 : 6 se alag hai, sawaal galat hai!"
**Ulta udaharan:** agar abhi Mummy 33 aur bete 12 saal ke ho (33 : 12 = 11 : 4), to 6 saal baad 39 aur 18, yaani 39 : 18 = 13 : 6, bilkul diya hua ratio. Bachche ka 17 : 10 galat tha.
Kyun galat? Ratio ke pad (11 aur 4) umar ke **parts** hain, umar nahi. Saal **asli umar** mein jodte hain: 11x + 6 aur 4x + 6. Sahi: (11x + 6)/(4x + 6) = 13/6, 6(11x + 6) = 13(4x + 6), 66x + 36 = 52x + 78, 14x = 42, x = 3. Mummy 33, beta 12 saal.
**Jaanch:** 33/12 = 11/4 ✅ aur 6 saal baad 39/18 = 13/6 ✅.

## Quick Recap

- **Ratio mein baantna:** parts jodo, ek part = kul ÷ parts, har hissa = pad × ek part. Pad hissa nahi hote.
- **Teen ka ratio:** saanjha term barabar karke a : b : c banao, phir wahi parts ka tareeka.
- **Partnership (barabar samay):** munafa = investment ke ratio mein. Ek partner ka paisa diya ho to fourth proportional.
- **Mixture:** ratio ke parts se matra; kuch milao ya hatao to jo cheez nahi badli use pakad kar proportion likho.
- **Scale:** model : asli = 1 : n; pehle ek unit, phir proportion.
- **Chaal aur samay:** ek chaal par doori : samay seedha ratio; ek doori par chaal ka ratio ulta karke samay ka ratio.
- **Umar:** ratio ke pad x ke saath guna, saal asli umar mein jodo ya ghatao.
- **Order:** "A se B" ka ratio pehla pad A ka. **Chaar kadam** aur story check har sawaal mein.
- Galtiyan: ratio ke pad ko hissa banana, "A se B" ka order ulta karna, saal ko ratio mein jodna.

## Practice Questions

1. Divide ₹ 1,65,000 between two friends in the ratio 3 : 7.
2. Three people share a profit in a way that A : B = 2 : 5 and B : C = 3 : 4. The total profit is ₹ 1,23,000. Find each share.
3. Three partners invest ₹ 65,000, ₹ 91,000 and ₹ 39,000 for the same time. The profit is ₹ 1,50,000. How much does each get?
4. Two partners invest in the ratio 7 : 5. The bigger partner gets ₹ 8,400 more profit than the smaller one. Find the profit of each.
5. A drum holds 72 L of a curd and water mixture in the ratio 5 : 3 (curd : water). (a) Find the quantity of each. (b) How much water must be added to make the ratio 5 : 4?
6. A jug holds 64 L of aloe juice and water in the ratio 3 : 5 (juice : water). How much more juice must be added so that juice and water become equal?
7. (a) A model of a bridge is made to scale 1 : 250. The real bridge is 300 m long. Find the model length in cm. (b) A model of a water tower is 36 cm high, and the real tower is 45 m high. Find the scale in the form 1 : n.
8. A van covers 45 km in 54 minutes. At the same speed, how long will it take to cover 70 km? Give the answer in hours and minutes.
9. Two couriers travel the same distance at speeds in the ratio 5 : 7. The faster one takes 40 minutes. How long does the slower one take?
10. The ages of an uncle and his nephew are in the ratio 5 : 2. After 9 years their ages will be in the ratio 13 : 7. Find their present ages.
11. The ages of two sisters are in the ratio 8 : 5. Four years ago the ratio was 2 : 1. Find their present ages.
12. The sum of two partners' shares is ₹ 77,000, and (A + B)/(A − B) = 7/3. Find A and B.
13. Three investments a, b, c are in continued proportion. The first is ₹ 12,000 and the third is ₹ 48,000. Find the middle one, and share a profit of ₹ 1,26,000 among the three in the ratio of their investments.
14. Two people have amounts A and B with (3/5) of A = (4/7) of B. Together they have ₹ 1,64,000. Find A and B.
15. True or false (give a reason or a counter-example):
 (a) To share ₹ 750 in the ratio 2 : 3, the first gets ₹ 2 and the second ₹ 3.
 (b) If boys : girls = 3 : 4, then girls are 4/7 of the class.
 (c) If two ages are 6 and 2 now, then after 2 years their ratio is 5 : 3.
 (d) If two couriers travel the same distance at speeds in the ratio 2 : 3, their times are in the ratio 2 : 3.

### Answers

**1.** 3 + 7 = 10 parts, ek part 1,65,000 ÷ 10 = ₹ 16,500. Pehle ko 3 × 16,500 = **₹ 49,500**, doosre ko 7 × 16,500 = **₹ 1,15,500.**
**Jaanch:** 49,500 + 1,15,500 = 1,65,000 ✅ aur 49,500 : 1,15,500 = 3 : 7 ✅.

**2.** B saanjha. 2 : 5 ko 3 se guna: 6 : 15. 3 : 4 ko 5 se guna: 15 : 20. To A : B : C = 6 : 15 : 20, 41 parts, ek part 1,23,000 ÷ 41 = 3,000. **A = ₹ 18,000, B = ₹ 45,000, C = ₹ 60,000.**
**Jaanch:** 18 + 45 + 60 = 123 (hazaar) ✅, 18 : 45 = 2 : 5 ✅, 45 : 60 = 3 : 4 ✅.

**3.** 65,000 : 91,000 : 39,000 ko 13,000 se divide: 5 : 7 : 3, 15 parts, ek part 1,50,000 ÷ 15 = 10,000. **₹ 50,000, ₹ 70,000, ₹ 30,000.**
**Jaanch:** 50,000 + 70,000 + 30,000 = 1,50,000 ✅ aur 50,000/65,000 = 70,000/91,000 = 30,000/39,000 = 10/13 ✅.

**4.** Profit bhi 7 : 5 mein. Antar 7 − 5 = 2 parts = ₹ 8,400, to ek part ₹ 4,200. Bada: 7 × 4,200 = **₹ 29,400**, chhota: 5 × 4,200 = **₹ 21,000.**
**Jaanch:** 29,400 − 21,000 = 8,400 ✅ aur 29,400 : 21,000 = 7 : 5 ✅.

**5.** (a) 5 + 3 = 8 parts, ek part 72 ÷ 8 = 9 L: **curd 45 L, paani 27 L.** (b) curd 45 L wahi rehta hai: 45/(27 + w) = 5/4, 5(27 + w) = 180, 27 + w = 36, **w = 9 L.**
**Jaanch:** 45 : 36 = 5 : 4 ✅.

**6.** 3 + 5 = 8 parts, ek part 8 L: juice 24 L, paani 40 L. Paani wahi rehta hai, juice 40 L chahiye (barabar), to 40 − 24 = **16 L** aur daalo.
**Story check:** nayi mixture 80 L ki, juice 40 L aur paani 40 L, ratio 1 : 1.

**7.** (a) 300 m = 30,000 cm. Model m : 30,000 = 1 : 250, m = 30,000/250 = **120 cm.** (b) 45 m = 4,500 cm. 36 : 4,500 = 1 : n, n = 4,500/36 = 125, **scale 1 : 125.**
**Jaanch:** 120 × 250 = 30,000 ✅ aur 36 × 125 = 4,500 ✅.

**8.** Doori ka ratio samay ke ratio ke barabar: 45 : 70 :: 54 : t (km se km, minute se minute). 45t = 70 × 54 = 3,780, t = 84 minute = **1 ghanta 24 minute.**
**Jaanch:** 54/84 = 9/14 aur 45/70 = 9/14 ✅.

**9.** Chaal 5 : 7 to samay (slower : faster) ulta 7 : 5. t : 40 = 7 : 5, t = 40 × 7/5 = **56 minute.**
**Story check:** dheema ko zyada samay chahiye: 56 > 40. Doori: 5 × 56 = 7 × 40 = 280 (chaal ke parts × samay), dono barabar, theek.

**10.** Umar 5x aur 2x. (5x + 9)/(2x + 9) = 13/7, 7(5x + 9) = 13(2x + 9), 35x + 63 = 26x + 117, 9x = 54, x = 6. **Uncle 30 saal, bhatija 12 saal.**
**Jaanch:** 9 saal baad 39 aur 21: 39/21 = 13/7 ✅ aur 30/12 = 5/2 ✅.

**11.** Umar 8x aur 5x. 4 saal pehle (8x − 4)/(5x − 4) = 2, 8x − 4 = 10x − 8, 4 = 2x, x = 2. **Badi behen 16 saal, chhoti 10 saal.**
**Jaanch:** 4 saal pehle 12 aur 6: 12/6 = 2 ✅ aur 16/10 = 8/5 ✅.

**12.** A/B = (7 + 3)/(7 − 3) = 10/4 = 5/2. 7 parts, ek part 77,000 ÷ 7 = 11,000, to 5 × 11,000 = **A = ₹ 55,000**, 2 × 11,000 = **B = ₹ 22,000.**
**Jaanch:** (55,000 + 22,000)/(55,000 − 22,000) = 77,000/33,000 = 7/3 ✅.

**13.** b² = 12,000 × 48,000 = 57,60,00,000, b = **₹ 24,000.** Ratio 12 : 24 : 48 = 1 : 2 : 4, 7 parts, ek part ₹ 1,26,000 ÷ 7 = 18,000. **₹ 18,000, ₹ 36,000, ₹ 72,000.**
**Jaanch:** 12,000/24,000 = 24,000/48,000 = 1/2 ✅ aur 18,000 + 36,000 + 72,000 = 1,26,000 ✅.

**14.** (3/5)A = (4/7)B, 35 se guna: 21A = 20B, A/B = 20/21. 41 parts, ek part 1,64,000 ÷ 41 = 4,000. **A = ₹ 80,000, B = ₹ 84,000.**
**Jaanch:** (3/5) × 80,000 = 48,000 aur (4/7) × 84,000 = 48,000 ✅.

**15.** (a) **False ❌.** 2 aur 3 parts hain, 2 + 3 = 5 parts, ek part ₹ 150, to ₹ 300 aur ₹ 450. (b) **True ✅.** 3 + 4 = 7 parts, ladkiyan 4 parts. (c) **False ❌.** 6 aur 2 ki umar 2 saal baad 8 aur 4 hogi, ratio 2 : 1 hai, 5 : 3 nahi (saal asli umar mein jode jaate hain, ratio mein nahi). (d) **False ❌.** Chaal 2 : 3 to samay ulta, 3 : 2.
