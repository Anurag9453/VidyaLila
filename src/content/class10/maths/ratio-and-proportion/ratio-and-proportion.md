# Topic 1: Do Cheezon Ki Tulna: Ratio Aur Proportion

Pichhle chapter mein humne seekha ki do cheezon mein "kaun bada, kaun chhota" kaise likhte hain (inequation). Ab ek naya sawaal: "**kitna guna** bada ya chhota?" ## Amulya ka sharbat stall

Mela mein Amulya ke Papa ka sharbat ka chhota stall hai. Papa ek chhote jug mein **1 glass syrup aur 4 glass paani** milate hain, aur sharbat ka swaad bilkul sahi aata hai. Aaj bheed zyada thi, to Papa ne Amulya se kaha: "Beta, ek bada jug bana do, jisme 3 glass syrup ho. Swaad wahi rehna chahiye."

Amulya ne socha: "Syrup 1 se 3 hua, yaani 2 zyada. To paani mein bhi 2 jod deti hoon: 4 + 2 = 6." Usne bade jug mein 3 glass syrup aur 6 glass paani daale. Pehle grahak ne ek ghoont piya aur bola: "Ye to bahut meetha hai!" Doosre ne chhota jug wala piya tha, wo bola: "Pehle wala swaad kahan gaya?"

Papa ne muskura kar kaha: "Swaad tab wahi rehta hai jab syrup aur paani ka **anupaat (ratio)** wahi rahe. Chhote jug mein syrup paani ka chautha hissa tha. Tumne jodne se kaam liya, par yahan **guna** karna tha: syrup 3 guna hua to paani bhi 3 guna: 12 glass."

Amulya ne Papa ki baat maan kar naya jug banaya, aur is baar swaad bilkul sahi aaya. Ye anupaat hai kya, do anupaat "barabar" kab kahlate hain, aur ek anupaat dekh kar baaki cheezein kaise nikalte hain? Chalo seekhte hain.

## Part A: Ratio kya hai? Jodna nahi, guna

Amulya ki galti ye thi ki usne dekha "kitna zyada" (farak), jabki swaad ka raaz hai "kitna guna" (ratio). Farak se kaam kyun nahi chalta, ye ek chhote sawaal se dekho. Do ropes hain: 4 m aur 8 m. Doosri do ropes hain: 104 m aur 108 m. Dono jodi mein farak 4 m hai, par kya dono jodi ek jaisi hain? Pehli mein doosri rope pehli se **2 guna** lambi hai; doosri jodi mein 108 aur 104 lagbhag barabar hain (108/104 sirf thoda sa 1 se zyada). Isliye farak "kitna bada" bata deta hai, par "kitna guna" nahi. **Ratio, bhaag dekar, kitna guna ya kitna hissa batata hai.**

**Ratio ki paribhasha:** do cheezon a aur b ka ratio a : b hai, aur ise a/b ki tarah padhte hain (b zero nahi). **Kram (order) maayne rakhta hai:** 1 : 4 aur 4 : 1 do alag baatein hain, kyunki 1/4 aur 4/1 alag hain.

```
Chhota jug (1 : 4)    S WWWW
Bada jug (3 : 12)     SSS WWWWWWWWWWWW
```

> **Figure 1.1** – S ka matlab 1 glass syrup aur W ka matlab 1 glass paani. Chhota jug mein 1 S ke liye 4 W hain. Bade jug mein 3 S ke liye 12 W hain: har cheez 3 guna hui, isliye S aur W ka ratio wahi 1 : 4 raha. Amulya ka pehla jug 3 S aur 6 W (3 : 6) ka tha, jo alag ratio hai.

### Example 1: ratio nikalna (₹ 52 aur ₹ 91)

Dono ek hi tarah ki cheez hain (paise, dono rupaye mein), to ratio bana sakte hain. Ratio 52 : 91 hai. Dono ka HCF 13 hai (52 = 13 × 4 aur 91 = 13 × 7), to 13 se divide karo:

52 : 91 = 4 : 7.

**Jaanch:** 4 × 13 = 52 aur 7 × 13 = 91 ✅. Aur 52/91 = 4/7 (cross: 52 × 7 = 364 = 4 × 91 ✅).

### Example 2: sabse chhota roop (126 : 98)

126 = 14 × 9 aur 98 = 14 × 7, aur 9 aur 7 mein 1 ke alawa koi common factor nahi. Isliye HCF = 14.

126 : 98 = (126 ÷ 14) : (98 ÷ 14) = **9 : 7**.

**Jaanch:** 9 × 14 = 126 ✅ aur 7 × 14 = 98 ✅. Agar sirf 2 se divide karte (63 : 49) to ratio sahi hota par sabse chhota roop nahi, kyunki 63 aur 49 dono mein 7 common hai.

**Ratio ka koi unit nahi hota.** 52 rupaye ÷ 91 rupaye mein "rupaye" bhaag mein kat jaate hain, aur sirf shuddh number bachta hai (4/7). Isliye ratio ke saath "m" ya "kg" ya "₹" kabhi nahi likhte.

## Part B: Same unit mein laao, phir ratio banao

Ratio banane se pehle dono cheezein **ek hi unit** mein hon. Isliye ki bhaag tabhi sahi hai jab dono taraf "ek hi paimana" ho. Agar ek taraf cm aur doosri taraf m ho, to bhaag ka number kisi bhi hisaab se alag niklega.

### Example 3: lambai (45 cm aur 2 m)

1 m = 100 cm, to 2 m = 200 cm. Ab dono cm mein hain:

45 : 200, HCF 5, to **9 : 40**.

**Jaanch:** 9 × 5 = 45 ✅ aur 40 × 5 = 200 ✅. Aur 45 cm to 200 cm ka chhota sa hissa hona chahiye: 9/40 = 0.225, jo 1 se bahut kam hai ✅.

### Example 4: paise (₹ 4 aur 75 paise)

₹ 1 = 100 paise, to ₹ 4 = 400 paise. 400 : 75, HCF 25, to **16 : 3**.

**Jaanch:** 16 × 25 = 400 ✅ aur 3 × 25 = 75 ✅. Rupaye mein dono ko likhte: 4 : 0.75 = 400 : 75 (dono ko 100 se guna) ✅, wahi ratio.

### Example 5: samay (1 ghanta 15 minute aur 50 minute)

1 ghanta = 60 minute, to 1 ghanta 15 minute = 75 minute. 75 : 50, HCF 25, to **3 : 2**.

**Story check:** 75 minute, 50 minute se zyada hain, isliye ratio 1 se bada aana chahiye: 3/2 = 1.5, jo 1 se bada hai. Aur 50 ka derh (1.5) guna = 75.

Ek aur dhyan do ki ratio wahi do cheezein banati hain jo **ek hi tarah ki** hon. "5 kg aur 3 metre" ka ratio nahi banta, kyunki kilo aur metre ek doosre mein badle ja hi nahi sakte. (Aisi cheezon ke liye "kilo ke liye ₹" jaisa **rate** hota hai, jisme hum ek cheez ko doosri se bhaag dekar ek naya unit paate hain. Wo is topic ka hissa nahi.)

## Part C: Barabar ratio aur do ratios ki tulna

### Barabar ratio (equivalent ratios)

Ratio ek fraction hai, aur fraction ke numerator aur denominator ek hi non-zero number n se guna ya bhaag karne par uski value nahi badalti:

a/b = (a × n)/(b × n), jahan n ≠ 0.

Isliye a : b = (a × n) : (b × n). Jaise **1 : 4 = 2 : 8 = 3 : 12 = 5 : 20**. Figure 1.1 mein dikhaya tha ki 3 : 12 bhi 1 : 4 hi hai.

### Example 6: lupta pad dhundhna (9 : 7 = x : 35)

9 : 7 ko 5 se guna karne se 45 : 35 milta hai (kyunki 7 × 5 = 35). To **x = 45**.

**Jaanch:** 45/35 = 9/7 (dono ko 5 se divide) ✅. Cross: 45 × 7 = 315 aur 9 × 35 = 315 ✅.

### Do ratios ki tulna: kaun bada?

Ek ratio ko fraction maan lo, to "kaun bada" fractions ki tulna ban jaata hai. a/b aur c/d (b, d dono positive) ko dekhne ka sabse sidha tareeka:

> Dono taraf positive number bd se guna karo. a/b × bd = ad aur c/d × bd = bc. Positive number se guna karne se bada-chhota ka order nahi badalta (Chapter 6, Topic 2, Part A: [linear-inequations-part2.md](../linear-inequations/linear-inequations-part2.md)). Isliye **a/b aur c/d ka order wahi hai jo ad aur bc ka**.

Yaani a : b aur c : d ko tulna ho to **cross-multiply** karo: ad aur bc. Jahan **ad > bc** wahan a : b bada; jahan **ad < bc** wahan c : d bada; aur **ad = bc** hone par dono barabar.

### Example 7: kaun sa sharbat zyada meetha? (jug I mein syrup : paani = 2 : 7, jug II mein 3 : 10)

Syrup ka paani se ratio jitna bada, sharbat utna zyada meetha. Cross-multiply:

2 × 10 = 20 aur 3 × 7 = 21. Yahan bc = 21 bada hai, yaani c : d = 3 : 10 bada hai.

**Jug II zyada meetha hai.**

**Jaanch:** 2/7 = 0.2857... aur 3/10 = 0.3. 0.3 > 0.2857 ✅.

### Example 8: kya ye barabar hain? (6 : 15 aur 4 : 10)

6 × 10 = 60 aur 15 × 4 = 60. Barabar, to 6 : 15 = 4 : 10 (dono 2 : 5).

**Jaanch:** 6 ÷ 3 = 2, 15 ÷ 3 = 5; 4 ÷ 2 = 2, 10 ÷ 2 = 5. Dono ka sabse chhota roop 2 : 5 ✅.

### Ratio mein badhana ya ghatana (halka sa)

"₹ 80 ko 9 : 8 ke ratio mein badhao" ka matlab hai: naya : purana = 9 : 8. Naya = 80 × 9/8 = **₹ 90**. **Jaanch:** 90 : 80 = 9 : 8 ✅. Isi tarah "84 minute ko 5 : 6 ke ratio mein ghatao" ka matlab hai naya : purana = 5 : 6, to naya = 84 × 5/6 = **70 minute**. **Jaanch:** 70 : 84 = 5 : 6 (dono ko 14 se divide) ✅.

## Part D: Proportion: jab do ratio barabar hon

Amulya ka chhota jug (1 : 4) aur bada jug (3 : 12) ek hi swaad ke hain, kyunki dono ratio barabar hain. Aise do barabar ratios ko hum ek jagah likhte hain:

**a : b :: c : d**, jiska matlab hai **a/b = c/d**.

Chaar numbers a, b, c, d isi kram mein hain, to kehte hain "a, b, c, d proportion mein hain". Is chapter mein **a, b, c, d ka kram hamesha yahi rahega**. Pehle ratio a : b ke dono pad a aur b hain, aur doosre ratio c : d ke dono pad c aur d.

- **Extremes (bahar ke do):** a aur d.
- **Means (beech ke do):** b aur c.

Sharbat ke jug mein: 1 : 4 :: 3 : 12 mein extremes 1 aur 12, means 4 aur 3.

### Proportion ka sabse kaam ka niyam: extremes ka guna = means ka guna

**Kyun?** a/b = c/d hai. Dono taraf ko bd (non-zero) se guna karo:

(a/b) × bd = (c/d) × bd, yaani **ad = bc**.

Bayen taraf b kat gaya aur ad bacha; daayen taraf d kat gaya aur bc bacha. Yaani:

**Extremes ka guna = Means ka guna.**

Ulta bhi sach hai: agar ad = bc ho (b, d non-zero), to dono taraf bd se bhaag karne par a/b = c/d milta hai. Isliye "ad = bc" proportion ki ek pakki jaanch hai.

**Number se jaanch:** 4 : 9 :: 12 : 27. Extremes 4 aur 27: guna 4 × 27 = 108. Means 9 aur 12: guna 9 × 12 = 108. Barabar ✅. Aur 4/9 = 12/27 (12/27 ko 3 se divide karo to 4/9) ✅.

```
pad:       a    b     c     d
ratio:     4 :  9  :: 12 :  27
extremes:  4 aur 27, guna 4 × 27 = 108
means:     9 aur 12, guna 9 × 12 = 108
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Proportion 4 : 9 :: 12 : 27 ke chaar pad aur unke naam. Bahar ke 4 aur 27 extremes hain, beech ke 9 aur 12 means hain, aur dono jodiyon ka guna 108 ek jaisa hai.

### Example 9: kya chaar number proportion mein hain?

Rule: a, b, c, d ke liye ad aur bc nikalo. Barabar hon to proportion hai, warna nahi.

(a) 6, 8, 15, 20: ad = 6 × 20 = 120, bc = 8 × 15 = 120. Barabar, to **proportion mein hain**. **Jaanch:** 6/8 = 3/4 aur 15/20 = 3/4 ✅.

(b) 7, 9, 21, 28: ad = 7 × 28 = 196, bc = 9 × 21 = 189. Barabar nahi, to **proportion mein nahi**. **Jaanch:** 7/9 = 0.777... aur 21/28 = 0.75, alag ✅.

(c) **Kram (order) ka asar:** 6, 10, 9, 15: ad = 6 × 15 = 90, bc = 10 × 9 = 90. To 6 : 10 :: 9 : 15 sach hai. Wahi chaar number doosre kram mein 6, 10, 15, 9: ad = 6 × 9 = 54 aur bc = 10 × 15 = 150, barabar nahi. **Jaanch:** 6/10 = 0.6 aur 15/9 = 1.666..., alag ✅. Yaani chaar number "kisi bhi kram mein" proportion nahi hote; kram wahi chahiye jo pad ke ratio banate hain.

### Example 10: bazaar ki kahaani, kya ye proportion hai?

Papa jis thok dukaan se syrup lete hain, wahan 4 litre syrup ₹ 140 ka aur 10 litre ₹ 350 ka milta hai. Kya 4 : 10 :: 140 : 350?

4 : 10 mein HCF 2 hai, to 2 : 5. 140 : 350 mein HCF 70 hai, to 2 : 5. Dono barabar, to **proportion hai**. **Jaanch:** 4 × 350 = 1400 aur 10 × 140 = 1400 ✅.

**Story check:** 4 litre ₹ 140 ka matlab 1 litre ₹ 35 ka (140 ÷ 4). 10 litre = 10 × 35 = ₹ 350, jo diye gaye daam se mel khata hai.

### "Directly proportional" ka asli matlab

Jab do cheezein aisi hon ki pehli ko 2 guna karo to doosri bhi 2 guna, pehli ko 3 guna karo to doosri bhi 3 guna, to hum kehte hain ki wo "directly proportional" hain. Upar sharbat mein litre aur daam aise hi hain: litre 10 ÷ 4 = 2.5 guna hue to daam bhi 350 ÷ 140 = 2.5 guna hue. Class 9 ke Charles ke niyam mein bhi aisa hi tha: V₁ : V₂ :: T₁ : T₂, yaani volume ka ratio Kelvin temperature ke ratio ke barabar rehta hai (volume se volume, temperature se temperature) ([gas-laws-part3.md](../../../class9/chemistry/gas-laws/gas-laws-part3.md)).

## Part E: Fourth proportional: ek lupta pad dhundhna

Teen numbers a, b, c diye hain, aur chaar proportion banane ke liye chautha pad d chahiye. Rule ad = bc se:

d = bc / a.

**Kyun?** a : b :: c : d ka matlab ad = bc. Dono taraf a se divide karo (a ≠ 0): **d = bc/a**. Dhyan do ki d, c ke baad aata hai: pehle ratio a : b mein jo hua (a se b), wahi doosre ratio mein c se d.

```
a : b :: c : d
d = (b × c) ÷ a
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Fourth proportional ka rule: beech ke do pad (b aur c) ko guna karo aur pehle pad a se bhaag do. Jo mila wo d hai.

### Example 11: 7, 12, 21 ka fourth proportional

d = 12 × 21 / 7 = 252/7 = **36**.

**Jaanch:** 7 : 12 :: 21 : 36. Extremes 7 × 36 = 252, means 12 × 21 = 252 ✅. Aur 7/12 = 21/36 (21/36 ko 3 se divide) ✅.

### Example 12: 9, 4, 6 ka fourth proportional (jawab fraction mein)

d = 4 × 6 / 9 = 24/9 = **8/3**.

**Jaanch:** 9 : 4 :: 6 : 8/3. Extremes 9 × 8/3 = 24 aur means 4 × 6 = 24 ✅. Fourth proportional ka whole number hona zaroori nahi.

### Example 13: x ko mean ya extreme mein rakh kar solve karna

(a) **x : 21 :: 4 : 7.** Extremes x aur 7, means 21 aur 4. Rule: 7x = 21 × 4 = 84, to **x = 12**. **Jaanch:** 12 : 21 = 4 : 7 (dono ko 3 se divide) ✅.

(b) **(x + 2) : 6 :: 5 : 3.** Extremes (x + 2) aur 3, means 6 aur 5. Rule: 3(x + 2) = 6 × 5 = 30, to x + 2 = 10 aur **x = 8**. **Jaanch:** x + 2 = 10, aur 10 : 6 = 5 : 3 (dono ko 2 se divide) ✅.

### Example 14: dukaan ka sawaal (5 copy ₹ 85 ki, 8 copy kitne ki?)

Copy ki ginti ka ratio aur unke daam ka ratio barabar honge (kyunki daam ginti ke seedhe anupaat mein hai, Example 10 ki tarah):

5 : 8 :: 85 : x. Extremes 5 aur x, means 8 aur 85.

5x = 8 × 85 = 680, to **x = 136**.

**Story check:** 85 ÷ 5 = ₹ 17 ek copy ka. 8 copy ka daam 8 × 17 = ₹ 136, jo mel khata hai.

(Dhyan do: pehle ratio mein copy ki ginti hai aur doosre mein rupaye, par har ratio ke andar ke dono pad ek hi tarah ke hain: copy se copy, rupaye se rupaye. Isliye hi ratio bana.)

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

### Galti 1: alag-alag unit ko seedha ratio bana dena (25 minute : 2 ghanta = 25 : 2)

Amulya ne stall sajane mein 25 minute lagaye aur Papa ne 2 ghante. Usne likha: "To hamara ratio 25 : 2 hai." Papa ne turant roka.

**Galat:** 25 minute : 2 ghanta = 25 : 2 (unit dekhe bina sirf number likh diye).

Is jawab ka matlab hota 25/2 = 12.5, yaani Amulya ka samay Papa ke samay se saade barah guna hota. Par 25 minute to 2 ghante se bahut kam hain, kaise ye 12 guna ho sakte hain?

**Sahi:** pehle dono ko minute mein badlo. 2 ghanta = 2 × 60 = 120 minute. Ratio 25 : 120, HCF 5, to **5 : 24**.

**Jaanch:** 25/120 = 5/24 = 0.2083..., yaani Amulya ka samay Papa ke samay ka lagbhag ek-paanchwa hissa hai. Ye 2 ghante ke mukable 25 minute se mel khata hai ✅, aur galat jawab 25/2 = 12.5 mel nahi khata.

### Galti 2: ratio ko farak se padhna (3 : 5 aur 13 : 15 "barabar" hain kyunki dono mein farak 2 hai)

Amulya ne ek din dekha ki 3 : 5 aur 13 : 15 mein dono jagah bade aur chhote pad ka farak 2 hi hai. Usne likha: "To ye dono ratio barabar hain."

**Galat:** 3 : 5 = 13 : 15 kyunki dono mein farak 2 hai.

Dekhte hain ki sharbat mein iska kya matlab hota. Ek jug mein 3 glass syrup aur 5 glass paani, doosre mein 13 glass syrup aur 15 glass paani. Ek hi swaad hota to ratio barabar hota.

```
Jug A (3 : 5)     SSS WWWWW
Jug B (13 : 15)   SSSSSSSSSSSSS WWWWWWWWWWWWWWW
```

> **Figure 1.4** – S ka matlab 1 glass syrup aur W ka matlab 1 glass paani. Jug A mein syrup paani se kam hai (3 S aur 5 W). Jug B mein syrup paani se thoda hi kam hai (13 S aur 15 W), yaani B ka sharbat kahin zyada meetha hai. Dono jugon mein "paani minus syrup" ek jaisa 2 glass hai, par swaad ek jaisa nahi.

**Sahi:** do ratios ka barabar hona cross-multiply karke dekhte hain. 3 × 15 = 45 aur 5 × 13 = 65. 45 ≠ 65, to **3 : 5 aur 13 : 15 barabar nahi hain.**

**Jaanch:** 3/5 = 0.6 aur 13/15 = 0.8666..., alag ✅. 13 : 15 asal mein 3 : 5 mein dono pad mein 10 jodne se bana hai, aur jodne se ratio badal jaata hai (jaise Amulya ke pehle bade jug mein). Ratio ko **guna ya bhaag** se hi sambhalte hain.

## Quick Recap

- **Ratio** do samaan jaati ki cheezon ki tulna hai bhaag se: a : b = a/b (b non-zero). Ye "kitna guna" batata hai, "kitna zyada" nahi. Order maayne rakhta hai.
- Ratio banane se pehle dono cheezein **ek hi unit** mein laao. Ratio ka koi unit nahi hota.
- **Sabse chhota roop**: dono pad ko HCF se divide karo.
- **Barabar ratio**: dono pad ko ek hi non-zero number se guna ya bhaag karo, jodo ya ghatao nahi.
- **Do ratios ki tulna**: a : b aur c : d (b, d positive) mein ad aur bc cross-multiply karke dekho. ad > bc to a : b bada, ad < bc to c : d bada, ad = bc to barabar.
- **Proportion** a : b :: c : d ka matlab a/b = c/d. Extremes a, d; means b, c. **Extremes ka guna = means ka guna: ad = bc** (kyunki a/b = c/d ko bd se guna karo).
- **Fourth proportional** d = bc/a.
- Chaar number proportion mein hain ya nahi, kram ke saath ad = bc se jaancho.

### Answers

**1.** (a) 98 = 14 × 7 aur 154 = 14 × 11, HCF 14. Ratio **7 : 11**. **Jaanch:** 7 × 14 = 98 ✅, 11 × 14 = 154 ✅.
(b) 1 m 40 cm = 140 cm aur 2 m 5 cm = 205 cm. HCF 5. Ratio 140 : 205 = **28 : 41**. **Jaanch:** 28 × 5 = 140 ✅, 41 × 5 = 205 ✅.
(c) ₹ 6.40 = 640 paise. Ratio 640 : 80, HCF 80, to **8 : 1**. **Jaanch:** 8 × 80 = 640 ✅.

**2.** 2 ghanta 40 minute = 120 + 40 = 160 minute. Ratio 48 : 160, HCF 16, to **3 : 10**. **Jaanch:** 3 × 16 = 48 ✅, 10 × 16 = 160 ✅.

**3.** 7 × 14 = 98 aur 9 × 11 = 99. 99 bada hai, yaani c : d bada, to **11 : 14 bada hai**. **Jaanch:** 7/9 = 0.7777... aur 11/14 = 0.7857..., 0.7857 > 0.7777 ✅.

**4.** 5 : 6 aur 11 : 13: 5 × 13 = 65 aur 6 × 11 = 66, to 5 : 6 chhota. 7 : 9 aur 5 : 6: 7 × 6 = 42 aur 9 × 5 = 45, to 7 : 9 chhota. Isliye **7 : 9 < 5 : 6 < 11 : 13**. **Jaanch:** 7/9 = 0.778, 5/6 = 0.833, 11/13 = 0.846 ✅.

**5.** ad = 12 × 21 = 252 aur bc = 18 × 14 = 252. Barabar, to **haan, proportion mein hain.** **Jaanch:** 12/18 = 2/3 aur 14/21 = 2/3 ✅.

**6.** ad = 9 × 27 = 243 aur bc = 12 × 20 = 240. Barabar nahi, to **proportion mein nahi.** **Jaanch:** 9/12 = 0.75 aur 20/27 = 0.7407..., alag ✅.

**7.** (a) d = bc/a = 16 × 33/11 = 16 × 3 = **48**. **Jaanch:** 11 × 48 = 528 aur 16 × 33 = 528 ✅.
(b) d = 5 × 12/18 = 60/18 = **10/3**. **Jaanch:** 18 × 10/3 = 60 aur 5 × 12 = 60 ✅.

**8.** (a) 13 : x :: 39 : 51. Extremes 13 aur 51, means x aur 39. 13 × 51 = 39x, to 663 = 39x aur **x = 17**. **Jaanch:** 13 : 17 aur 39 : 51 (39/51 ko 3 se divide karo to 13/17) ✅.
(b) Extremes (3x − 2) aur 5, means 10 aur 8. 5(3x − 2) = 80, to 3x − 2 = 16, 3x = 18 aur **x = 6**. **Jaanch:** 3 × 6 − 2 = 16, aur 16 : 10 = 8 : 5 (dono ko 2 se divide) ✅.

**9.** 9 : 14 :: 117 : x. Extremes 9 aur x, means 14 aur 117. 9x = 14 × 117 = 1638, to **x = ₹ 182**. **Story check:** 117 ÷ 9 = ₹ 13 ek metre ka. 14 metre ka 14 × 13 = ₹ 182.

**10.** 2 ghanta = 120 minute. Samay ka ratio aur doori ka ratio barabar: 45 : 120 :: 18 : x. 45x = 120 × 18 = 2160, to **x = 48 km**. **Story check:** 45 minute mein 18 km ka matlab 1 minute mein 0.4 km. 120 minute mein 120 × 0.4 = 48 km.

**11.** 5 : 11 :: 20 : x. 5x = 11 × 20 = 220, to **x = 44 glass paani**. **Story check:** syrup 5 se 20 (4 guna) hua, to paani bhi 11 se 4 guna: 44.

**12.** (a) **Sahi ✅.** 14 : 21 = 2 : 3 aur 20 : 30 = 2 : 3. Cross: 14 × 30 = 420 aur 21 × 20 = 420.
(b) **Galat ❌.** 2 kg = 2000 g, to ratio 2000 : 500 = 4 : 1, jo 2 : 500 se bahut alag hai. Pehle same unit karna padta hai.
(c) **Galat ❌.** 4 × 17 = 68 aur 7 × 14 = 98, barabar nahi. Ratio farak se nahi, bhaag se banta hai (4/7 = 0.571 aur 14/17 = 0.824).
(d) **Sahi ✅.** a/b = c/d ko bd se guna karo: ad = bc.

**13.** Extremes 6 aur 35: 6 × 35 = 210. Means 14 aur 15: 14 × 15 = 210. Barabar, to proportion hai. Wajah: ad = bc ka matlab dono taraf bd se bhaag karne par a/b = c/d milta hai (b, d non-zero), yaani 6/14 = 15/35. **Jaanch:** 6/14 = 3/7 aur 15/35 = 3/7 ✅.

**14.** Naya : purana = 7 : 10, to naya = 150 × 7/10 = **₹ 105**. **Jaanch:** 105 : 150 (dono ko 15 se divide) = 7 : 10 ✅.

**15.** Galti: dono cheezein alag unit mein thi (minute aur ghanta), aur student ne unit badle bina sirf number likh diye. 3 ghanta = 180 minute, to sahi ratio 36 : 180 = **1 : 5**. **Jaanch:** 1 × 36 = 36 aur 5 × 36 = 180 ✅. Student ka 12 : 1 kehta tha ki 36 minute, 3 ghante ka 12 guna hai, jo galat hai.

Ab tum do cheezon ko "kitna guna" ki nazar se dekh sakte ho: ratio banana, use sabse chhote roop mein likhna, do ratios ki tulna karna, aur proportion mein lupta pad dhundhna. Har baar unit pehle barabar karna aur jodne-ghatane ki jagah guna-bhaag se sochna yaad rakhna.

Topic 2 mein hum proportion ko "ghumana-phirana" seekhenge: a/b = c/d se shuru karke ratio ko ulta karna (invertendo), beech ke pad badalna (alternendo), aur dono taraf 1 jodna ya ghatana (componendo aur dividendo, aur dono saath: componendo-dividendo). Ye chaar-paanch chaal ek sawaal ko aasaan bana deti hain.
