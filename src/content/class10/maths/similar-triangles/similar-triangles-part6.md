## Tahir ka school-building ka model

Tahir ke school mein ek **Model-making Contest** hai: har team ko school ki imaarat ka **scale model** banana hai, scale **1 : 50** (model ka 1 cm = asli imaarat ke 50 cm). Tahir ke saamne ek ke baad ek sawaal aaye. Imaarat ke saamne chhat ke neeche ek teen-kone wali deewar hai (isse **gable** kehte hain): uska card wala tukda kaise kaata jaaye? Pehle to Tahir ghabra gaya: itne alag sawaal, itne alag tareeke! Phir usne dekha ki har sawaal ke andar do hi cheezein chhupi hain: **do similar triangles** (jinse lambaiyan k se aur ratio se milti hain) aur **k × k** (jab areas ki baat ho). ## Part A: Chaar kadam

Chaar kadam wahi aadat hain jo tumne Quadratic Equations, Linear Inequations, Ratio aur Matrices mein banayi thi. Bas yahan pehla kadam "similar triangles dhoondho" hai:

```
1. JODI DHOONDHO   : kaun se do triangle similar hain? A↔P, B↔Q, C↔R ka kram likho
        :
        v
2. PROPORTION LIKHO: corresponding sides ka ratio (ek hi kram mein), ya area ke liye k × k
        :
        v
3. HAL KARO        : cross-multiply (ad = bc), saari lambaiyan ek hi unit mein
        :
        v
4. STORY CHECK     : jawab kahani mein sahi baithta hai? chhota-bada, unit, ulta ratio to nahi?
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Word problem ke chaar kadam. Zyada galtiyan pehle kadam (galat jodi) aur aakhri kadam (story check chhoot jaana) mein hoti hain.

**Kyun?** Har sawaal mein asli sawaal ye hota hai ki "kaun sa tool?" Agar pehle kadam mein sahi do triangles aur unka sahi kram mil gaya, to baaki kaam sirf ek proportion hai. Aur agar sawaal area ka hai, to ratio ke saath k × k lagana hai (Topic 5): lambai k guna, area k × k guna.

## Part B: Figure padhna (similar triangles kahan chhupe hain?)

Kai baar sawaal mein do triangles alag-alag diye hi nahi hote: ek figure hota hai aur usme se do triangle **dhoondhne** padte hain. Ye cheezein dekho:

```
   (a) parallel line                       (b) do parallel ke beech
       triangle mein                           do line cross

        A                                       A---B
       / \                                       \ /
    D o---o E                                     O
     /     \                                     / \
    /       \                                   /   \
   /         \                                 D-----C
B o-----------o C
```

> **Figure 6.2** – (a) DE ∥ BC: ∠A common, ∠ADE = ∠ABC (corresponding), to ΔADE ~ ΔABC. (b) AB ∥ DC: ∠OAB = ∠OCD (alternate), ∠AOB = ∠COD (vertically opposite), to ΔOAB ~ ΔOCD. Is topic ki saari figures naap ke hisaab se nahi hain.

| Figure mein kya dikha | Kaun se angle barabar | Test | Kya milta hai |
|---|---|---|---|
| Triangle ke andar ek side ke parallel line | Common angle, corresponding angles | AA | Chhota triangle bade ke similar, aur BPT ke roop |
| Do parallel lines ke beech do line ek point par cross | Alternate angles, vertically opposite angles | AA | Bow-tie ke do triangle similar (O↔O) |
| Zameen par seedhi khadi cheez (khamba, deewar, support) | 90° = 90° aur common (ya dhoop ka) angle | AA | Ek chhota right triangle bade right triangle ke similar |
| Right angle wale kone se hypotenuse par perpendicular | Common angle aur 90° | AA | Teen similar triangles, leg aur altitude relation |
| Teeno sides ka data | Koi angle nahi, bas ratio | SSS (pehle sort karo) | Similar ya nahi |
| Ek angle aur uske dono sides | Angle dono sides ke beech | SAS | Similar ya nahi |
| Sirf "dikhta hai ki ek jaise hain" | Kuch nahi | **Koi test nahi** | Data chahiye (Galti 3) |

**Ek aadat:** jab do triangle mil jaayein, to turant barabar angles ke hisaab se vertices ka kram likho (jaise ΔOAB ~ ΔOCD), phir sides ke jodi bana lo. Kram ek baar likh liya, to proportion galat jodi mein nahi banegi.

## Part D: Word problems aur mixed examples

### Example 1: kya card ki gable sahi hai? (Topic 1 aur 3)

Imaarat ki asli gable ΔABC mein AB = 8.5 m, BC = 14 m, CA = 10 m. Tahir ne scale 1 : 50 par card se ΔPQR kaata: PQ = 28 cm, QR = 20 cm, RP = 17 cm. Kya card sahi shape ka hai? Model ki gable ka perimeter kitna hoga?

```
              A                                     R
             / \                                   / \
            /   \                                 /   \
    8.5 m  /     \ 10 m                   17 cm  /     \ 20 cm
          /       \                             /       \
         /         \                           /         \
      B o-----------o C                     P o-----------o Q
            14 m                                  28 cm
```

> **Figure 6.3** – Asli gable ΔABC (meter mein) aur card ΔPQR (cm mein), dono ek hi taraf rakhe gaye.

1. **Jodi aur unit:** asli sides cm mein: 850, 1400, 1000. Card: 28, 20, 17.
2. **Proportion (SSS, sorted):** asli 850, 1000, 1400 aur card 17, 20, 28. Ratio: 17/850 = 1/50, 20/1000 = 1/50, 28/1400 = 1/50. Teeno barabar, to **similar**, k = 1/50 (asli se card), yaani card se asli tak har lambai 50 guna.
3. **Kram:** AB ↔ RP, BC ↔ PQ, CA ↔ QR. B dono AB aur BC mein hai, aur P dono RP aur PQ mein: B ↔ P. Phir A ↔ R, C ↔ Q. To **ΔABC ~ ΔRPQ.**
4. **Perimeter:** asli 8.5 + 14 + 10 = 32.5 m = 3250 cm. Model: 3250 / 50 = **65 cm**.

**Story check:** card cm mein hai aur asli m mein, par jab dono ek unit (cm) mein likhe, to har lambai 50 guna. Card ke teeno sides ka jod 17 + 20 + 28 = 65, wahi perimeter jo 1/50 se aaya.
**Jaanch:** 17 × 50 = 850, 20 × 50 = 1000, 28 × 50 = 1400 ✅. Aur 17 + 20 + 28 = 65 ✅.

### Example 2: model ke area se asli area (Topic 1 aur 5)

Model ki terrace par hare rang ke triangular patch ka card 162 cm² ka hai (scale 1 : 50). Asli terrace ka patch kitne m² ka hoga? Agar 1 litre paint 9 m² dhakta hai, to kitna paint chahiye?

1. **Jodi:** model ka patch aur asli patch similar hain, lambai ka k = 1/50.
2. **Proportion:** area ka ratio k × k = 1/2500, to asli area = 162 × 2500 = 405,000 cm².
3. **Unit badlo:** 1 m² = 10,000 cm², to 405,000 / 10,000 = **40.5 m²**. Paint = 40.5 / 9 = **4.5 litre**.
4. Story check: asli patch model se bahut bada hona chahiye, 40.5 m² ek terrace ke patch ke liye maqool hai.

**Jaanch:** ulta chalo: 40.5 m² = 405,000 cm², aur 405,000 / 2500 = 162 ✅.

### Example 3: parchhai se imaarat ki unchai, phir model ki (Topic 4, 3 aur 1)

Tahir ne level zameen par 2.1 m ka seedha khamba khada kiya. Usi waqt khambe ki parchhai 1.4 m aur imaarat ki parchhai 9.6 m thi. Imaarat kitni oonchi hai? Scale 1 : 50 par model ki unchai kitni rakhni hai?

1. **Jodi:** khamba aur imaarat dono seedhe khade, dhoop ek hi: AA se ΔABC ~ ΔPQR (ABC imaarat, PQR khamba).
2. **Proportion:** h / 2.1 = 9.6 / 1.4, to h = 2.1 × 9.6 / 1.4 = 20.16 / 1.4 = **14.4 m**.
3. **Model:** 14.4 m = 1440 cm, aur 1440 / 50 = **28.8 cm**.
4. Story check: khambe ki parchhai (1.4 m) uski unchai (2.1 m) se chhoti hai, to imaarat ki bhi (9.6 m < 14.4 m): dono taraf ek hi baat.

**Jaanch:** khambe ka unchai : parchhai = 2.1 : 1.4 = 3 : 2, imaarat ka 14.4 : 9.6 = 3 : 2 ✅.

### Example 4: ramp ka khada support (figure padhna)

Ek platform ki deewar AB 1.8 m ooncha hai. Zameen par B se C tak BC = 6 m, aur ramp A se C tak jaata hai (∠B = 90°). Ramp ke neeche zameen par D hai, CD = 2.4 m, aur wahan se ek seedha support DE ramp tak (E, AC par) lagana hai, jo BC par 90° par ho. DE kitna lamba hoga?

```
          A
          :\
          : \
1.8 m     :  \
          :   E
          :   |\
          B---D-C
```

> **Figure 6.4** – ΔCBA ke andar ek chhota right triangle ΔCDE. Baayein ki ':' wali line AB hai (platform ki deewar).

1. **Jodi (figure padhna):** ΔCDE aur ΔCBA mein ∠C common aur ∠CDE = ∠CBA = 90°. AA se **ΔCDE ~ ΔCBA** (C↔C, D↔B, E↔A).
2. **Proportion:** DE / BA = CD / CB, yaani DE / 1.8 = 2.4 / 6.
3. **Hal:** DE = 1.8 × 2.4 / 6 = 4.32 / 6 = **0.72 m**.
4. Story check: D, C ke paas hai (CD = 2.4 m, poora BC = 6 m ka 2/5 hissa), to support deewar AB se chhota hona chahiye: 0.72 m < 1.8 m.

**Jaanch:** DE : AB = 0.72 : 1.8 = 0.4 aur CD : CB = 2.4 : 6 = 0.4 ✅.

### Example 5: khet mein bagicha: BPT aur area (Topic 2, 3 aur 5)

Ek triangular khet ABC mein D (AB par) se E (AC par) tak ek fence DE lagi hai, aur DE ∥ BC hai. AD = 14 m, DB = 24.5 m, AC = 30.8 m, aur khet ka area 484 m² hai. AE aur EC nikalo, aur **DBCE ka area** (jisme phool-kyaari lagani hai) nikalo.

```
                A
               / \
              /   \
             /     \
           D o-----o E
            /       \
           /         \
          /           \
       B o-------------o C
```

> **Figure 6.5** – Khet ABC, fence DE ∥ BC. Chhota triangle ADE ke neeche trapezium DBCE hai.

1. **Lambai (BPT):** AB = 14 + 24.5 = 38.5 m. AD / AB = AE / AC, yaani 14 / 38.5 = AE / 30.8. 14 / 38.5 = 4/11, to AE = 30.8 × 4/11 = **11.2 m** aur EC = 30.8 − 11.2 = **19.6 m**.
2. **Jodi (area ke liye):** DE ∥ BC se ∠A common aur ∠ADE = ∠ABC, AA se ΔADE ~ ΔABC, k = AD / AB = 4/11.
3. **Area:** ar(ΔADE) : ar(ΔABC) = k × k = 16/121, to ar(ΔADE) = 484 × 16/121 = 4 × 16 = **64 m²**. Trapezium DBCE = 484 − 64 = **420 m²**.
4. Story check: DB (24.5 m) AD (14 m) se lamba hai, to phool-kyaari wala trapezium bada hissa hona chahiye: 420 m² > 64 m².

**Jaanch:** AD : DB = 14 : 24.5 = 4 : 7 aur AE : EC = 11.2 : 19.6 = 4 : 7 ✅. Area: 64 / 484 = 16 / 121 ✅ aur 64 + 420 = 484 ✅.

### Example 6: trapezium ka sabit karo aur phir nikalo (Topic 3 aur 5)

Ek trapezium-shape table-top ABCD mein AB ∥ DC, aur diagonals AC aur BD point O par milte hain. AB = 18 cm, DC = 27 cm, OA = 8 cm aur ar(ΔOAB) = 56 cm². (a) Sabit karo ΔOAB ~ ΔOCD. (b) OC nikalo. (c) ar(ΔOCD) nikalo.

```
        A---B
         \ /
          O
         / \
        /   \
       D-----C
```

> **Figure 6.6** – Trapezium ke do samantar bhuj AB (upar) aur DC (neeche) aur diagonals AC, BD jo O par milte hain. Trapezium ke baaki do bhuj AD aur BC figure saaf rakhne ke liye nahi kheenche.

**(a) Sabit karna:**

| Statement | Reason |
|---|---|
| 1. ∠OAB = ∠OCD | Alternate interior angles, AB ∥ DC, transversal AC |
| 2. ∠OBA = ∠ODC | Alternate interior angles, AB ∥ DC, transversal BD |
| 3. ∴ ΔOAB ~ ΔOCD | AA test (O↔O, A↔C, B↔D) |

**(b)** OA / OC = AB / CD = 18 / 27 = 2/3, to OC = 8 × 3/2 = **12 cm**.
**(c)** Area ka ratio = (AB / CD)² = 4/9, to ar(ΔOCD) = 56 × 9/4 = **126 cm²**.

**Story check:** DC (27 cm) AB (18 cm) se lamba hai, to ΔOCD bada hona chahiye: OC = 12 cm > OA = 8 cm, aur area 126 cm² > 56 cm².
**Jaanch:** 8 / 12 = 18 / 27 = 2/3 ✅ aur 56 / 126 = 4/9 ✅.

### Example 7: kaanch ki panel (Topic 4 aur 5)

Ek triangular kaanch ki panel ABC mein ∠A = 90° hai. A se hypotenuse BC par support AD (AD ⊥ BC) lagaya hai, BD = 20 cm aur DC = 180 cm. BC, AD, AB, AC aur panel ka area nikalo. Ek bilkul isi shape ki chhoti panel (similar) ki hypotenuse 50 cm hai: uska area?

```
     A
    /|\____
    /|     \_____
   / |           \____
   / |                \_____
  B--D---------------------------C
  BD = 20 cm          DC = 180 cm
```

> **Figure 6.7** – Panel ABC, ∠A = 90°, AD ⊥ BC. D hypotenuse ko BD = 20 cm aur DC = 180 cm mein baantta hai, isliye D, B ke bahut paas hai. Figure naap ke hisaab se nahi hai: bas itna dikhaya hai ki A par kona sidha lage aur D, B ke paas ho.

1. **Jodi:** ΔABC ~ ΔDBA ~ ΔDAC (Topic 4).
2. **BC:** BD + DC = 20 + 180 = **200 cm**.
3. **Altitude:** AD² = BD × DC = 20 × 180 = 3600, to AD = **60 cm**.
4. **Legs:** AB² = BD × BC = 20 × 200 = 4000, AB = 20√10 cm (lagbhag 63.2 cm). AC² = DC × BC = 180 × 200 = 36000, AC = 60√10 cm (lagbhag 189.7 cm).
5. **Area:** ½ × BC × AD = ½ × 200 × 60 = **6000 cm²**.
6. **Chhoti panel:** k = 50 / 200 = 1/4, to area = 6000 × (1/4)² = 6000 / 16 = **375 cm²**.

**Story check:** support AD (60 cm) dono legs se chhota hai aur hypotenuse se bhi, jaisa altitude hota hai. Chhoti panel ka area 375 cm² bahut kam hai kyunki lambai sirf chauthai hai.
**Jaanch:** AB² + AC² = 4000 + 36000 = 40000 = 200² ✅. Aur ½ × AB × AC = ½ × 20√10 × 60√10 = ½ × 1200 × 10 = 6000 ✅.

### Example 8: area se side (Topic 5 ulta, aur perimeter)

Tahir ke bridge-model mein do similar triangular plates hain, areas 50 cm² aur 72 cm². Chhoti plate ki ek side 15 cm hai. Bade plate ki usi jodi wali side kitni hai? Chhoti plate ka perimeter 40 cm ho to bade ka?

1. **Jodi:** dono similar diye gaye hain, side ratio k (chhoti se badi).
2. **Proportion:** area ratio = k × k = 50 / 72 = 25 / 36, to **k = 5 / 6** (root liya, square nahi).
3. **Hal:** side 15 × 6/5 = **18 cm**. Perimeter 40 × 6/5 = **48 cm** (perimeter ka ratio side ratio hi hai).
4. Story check: badi plate ka area bada hai to uski sides bhi badi: 18 > 15. Area ratio (25/36, lagbhag 0.69) side ratio (5/6, lagbhag 0.83) se chhota hai, jaisa k × k hone par (k < 1 ke liye) hona chahiye.

**Jaanch:** (15 / 18)² = (5/6)² = 25/36 = 50/72 ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: trapezium ka area bhi k × k se nikalna (kate hue hisse ko similar triangle samajhna)**

Ek triangular khet ABC ka area 600 m² hai. DE ∥ BC hai (D, AB par aur E, AC par) aur AD : DB = 2 : 3. Bagicha trapezium DBCE hai, uska area chahiye. Ek bachche ne socha: "DB / AB = 3/5, to area (3/5)² guna hoga" aur likha: ar(DBCE) = 600 × 9/25 = **216 m².**

Check karo. D, A ke paas hai (AD sirf 2/5 hissa), to neeche ka trapezium **bada** hissa hona chahiye, aadhe se zyada. Par 216 to 600 ka aadha (300) bhi nahi. Galti ye thi: k × k ka niyam sirf do **similar triangles** ke areas par chalta hai. Trapezium DBCE triangle nahi hai, ΔABC ke similar bhi nahi. Aur DB / AB wo k hai hi nahi: ΔADE ~ ΔABC mein k = AD / AB = 2/5.

Sahi: k = 2/5, to ar(ΔADE) = 600 × (2/5)² = 600 × 4/25 = **96 m².** Trapezium DBCE = 600 − 96 = **504 m².**

**Jaanch:** 96 + 504 = 600 ✅ aur 96 / 600 = 4/25 = (2/5)² ✅.
**Sahi tarika:** pehle chhota similar triangle (k = chhoti side / badi side, A se naapkar), uska area k × k se, phir poore mein se ghatao.

**Galti 2: area ke jawab mein unit bhool jaana (cm aur cm² ki gadbad)**

Ek triangular kyaari ka model scale 1 : 20 par bana hai, model ka area 45 cm². Ek bachche ne likha: asli area = 45 × 400 = **18,000 cm**. Phir usne "m mein" karne ke liye 100 se bhaag kiya: **180 m**.

Check karo: model ka kyaari base 10 cm aur height 9 cm ka triangle hai (½ × 10 × 9 = 45). Asli base 200 cm = 2 m aur height 180 cm = 1.8 m, to asli area ½ × 2 × 1.8 = 1.8 m². 180 m ka matlab ek bahut bada maidan hota. Galtiyan do thi: area ka unit cm² hota hai (cm nahi), aur cm² se m² mein jaane ke liye 10,000 se bhaag karna hai, 100 se nahi (1 m = 100 cm, to 1 m² = 100 × 100 = 10,000 cm²).

Sahi: 45 × 400 = 18,000 cm² = 18,000 / 10,000 = **1.8 m²**.

**Jaanch:** 1.8 × 10,000 = 18,000 ✅ aur 18,000 / 400 = 45 ✅.
**Sahi tarika:** jawab ke saath hamesha unit likho: lambai cm ya m, area cm² ya m².

**Galti 3: figure mein "ek jaise dikhte hain" dekhkar similar maan lena**

Ek figure mein ΔABC aur ΔPQR bilkul ek jaise dikh rahe hain: ek doosre ka bada-chhota roop. Diya hai AB = 6 cm, BC = 8 cm, CA = 11 cm aur PQ = 9 cm, QR = 12 cm, RP = 17 cm. Ek bachche ne kaha: "Aankh se dikh raha hai, to ΔABC ~ ΔPQR."

Sides se check karo (AB ↔ PQ, BC ↔ QR, CA ↔ RP): 9/6 = 3/2, 12/8 = 3/2, par 17/11 lagbhag 1.545. Do ratio barabar hain, teesra nahi. Similar hote to RP = 11 × 3/2 = 16.5 cm hona chahiye tha, par RP = 17 cm hai. **Similar nahi.** Figure naap ke hisaab se nahi hoti, aur thodi si farak aankh ko dikhta nahi.

**Jaanch:** 9/6 = 12/8 = 1.5, par 17/11 ≠ 1.5 ✅ (isliye similar nahi).
**Sahi tarika:** similar kehne se pehle koi test (AA, SSS, SAS ya BPT ka converse) poora karo. Figure sirf suraagh deti hai, saboot nahi.

## Quick Recap

- **Chaar kadam:** jodi dhoondho (kram likho), proportion likho, hal karo (ek unit, cross-multiply), story check.
- **Figure padhna:** parallel line, common angle, vertically opposite angles aur 90° se AA ke angle milte hain.
- **Method map:** lambai ke liye k ya proportion, area ke liye k × k, area se side ke liye square root, perimeter side ratio ke barabar.
- **Model:** scale 1 : 50 matlab k = 1/50. Lambai 1/50, area 1/2500.
- **Parchhai** (unchai : parchhai), **BPT** (tukda : poori side), **altitude relation** (AD² = BD × DC) mixed sawaalon mein bhi wahi kaam karte hain.
- **Unit:** area ka unit cm² ya m²; 1 m² = 10,000 cm².
- Galtiyan: trapezium ka area k × k se nikalna (chhota triangle nikaal kar ghatana tha), area ka unit bhoolna, figure dekhkar similar maan lena.

## Practice Questions

Check every answer by substituting back. Write the units, and write the similarity statement with the vertices in order wherever you prove similarity.

1. A model of a school's science block is made at scale 1 : 40. Its front gable is a triangle with real sides 6.8 m, 8.4 m and 10.4 m. Find the three sides and the perimeter of the model gable, in cm.
2. On a model made at scale 1 : 30 a triangular lawn patch has an area of 250 cm². Find the area of the real lawn patch in m².
3. A real triangular steel plate has an area of 6 m². Its model plate has an area of 96 cm². Find the scale (model : real) of the model.
4. A 1.5 m stick on level ground casts a shadow of 0.6 m. At the same time a factory chimney casts a shadow of 21 m. Find the height of the chimney, and the height of its model at scale 1 : 60.
5. In ΔPQR, X lies on PQ and Y lies on PR with XY ∥ QR. PX = 6.5 cm, XQ = 11.7 cm and PY = 5 cm. Find YR. If ar(ΔPQR) = 98 cm², find ar(ΔPXY) and the area of the trapezium XQRY.
6. Two hedges are fixed in a triangular plot ABC, with D on AB and E on AC. Hedge 1: AD = 7.5 m, DB = 12 m, AE = 8.5 m, EC = 13.6 m. Hedge 2: AD = 7.5 m, DB = 12 m, AE = 8.5 m, EC = 13 m. Which hedge is parallel to BC? Give the reason.
7. In trapezium PQRS, PQ ∥ SR and the diagonals PR and QS meet at O. PQ = 20 cm, SR = 28 cm, OP = 9 cm and ar(ΔOPQ) = 75 cm². (a) Prove ΔOPQ ~ ΔORS. (b) Find OR. (c) Find ar(ΔORS).
8. In ΔABC, ∠A = 90° and AD ⊥ BC. AD = 6 cm and BD = 4 cm. Find DC, AB and AC (surd form), and check that AB² + AC² = BC².
9. Two similar triangular tiles have areas 18 cm² and 128 cm². The longest side of the smaller tile is 15 cm and its perimeter is 33 cm. Find the longest side and the perimeter of the larger tile.
10. A triangle has sides 3.6 cm, 4.8 cm and 7.2 cm. A student must draw an enlarged similar copy whose shortest side is 6 cm. She pairs 6 cm with the 7.2 cm side, so k = 6 / 7.2, and gets the other sides 3 cm and 4 cm. What is wrong? Give the correct sides.
11. In a figure, ΔABC has AB = 8 cm, BC = 10 cm, CA = 13 cm and ΔPQR has PQ = 12 cm, QR = 15 cm, RP = 20 cm. They look like enlargements of each other, so a student writes ΔABC ~ ΔPQR. Is she right? What should RP be for the triangles to be similar?
12. True or false, with a one-line reason. (a) If two similar triangles have sides in the ratio 1 : 4, their areas are in the ratio 1 : 16. (b) A model at scale 1 : 25 has 1/25 of the real area. (c) 1 m² = 100 cm². (d) If the areas of two similar triangles are in the ratio 49 : 121, their corresponding sides are in the ratio 7 : 11. (e) If DE looks parallel to BC in a drawing, we can use BPT.
13. Two similar triangular windows have perimeters 36 cm and 54 cm. The longest side of the smaller window is 15 cm and the area of the larger window is 108 cm². Find the longest side of the larger window and the area of the smaller window.
14. Name the main tool for each situation (BPT, converse of BPT, AA, SSS, SAS, altitude relation, area ratio) and write the first line of the working. (a) A hedge DE is claimed to be parallel to the base BC, and AD, DB, AE, EC are given. (b) A card model has area 300 cm² at scale 1 : 15 and the real area is needed. (c) In a right triangle with the right angle at A and AD ⊥ BC, BD and DC are given and AD is needed. (d) The shadows of a stick and a mast are measured together.

### Answers

**1.** Pehle unit ek karo: 6.8 m = 680 cm, 8.4 m = 840 cm, 10.4 m = 1040 cm. k = 1/40: 680 / 40 = **17 cm**, 840 / 40 = **21 cm**, 1040 / 40 = **26 cm**. Perimeter = 17 + 21 + 26 = **64 cm**.
**Jaanch:** asli perimeter 25.6 m = 2560 cm, aur 2560 / 40 = 64 ✅.

**2.** k × k = (1/30)² = 1/900. Asli area = 250 × 900 = 225,000 cm². 225,000 / 10,000 = **22.5 m²**.
**Jaanch:** 22.5 × 10,000 / 900 = 250 ✅.

**3.** 6 m² = 60,000 cm². Area ratio (model : real) = 96 : 60,000 = 1 : 625. Ye k × k hai, to k = √(1/625) = 1/25. **Scale 1 : 25.**
**Jaanch:** (1/25)² = 1/625 aur 96 × 625 = 60,000 ✅.

**4.** AA se stick aur chimney ke triangle similar: unchai / 1.5 = 21 / 0.6 = 35, to unchai = 1.5 × 35 = **52.5 m**. Model: 5250 cm / 60 = **87.5 cm**.
**Jaanch:** stick ka 1.5 : 0.6 = 5 : 2, chimney ka 52.5 : 21 = 5 : 2 ✅.

**5.** BPT: PX / XQ = PY / YR, 6.5 / 11.7 = 5 / YR, YR = 11.7 × 5 / 6.5 = **9 cm**. PR = 14 cm, PQ = 18.2 cm. ΔPXY ~ ΔPQR (AA), k = PX / PQ = 6.5 / 18.2 = 5/14, k × k = 25/196. ar(ΔPXY) = 98 × 25/196 = 25/2 = **12.5 cm²**. Trapezium XQRY = 98 − 12.5 = **85.5 cm²**.
**Jaanch:** PX : XQ = 6.5 : 11.7 = 5 : 9 aur PY : YR = 5 : 9 ✅. PY / PR = 5/14 = k bhi ✅.

**6.** Hedge 1: AD : DB = 7.5 : 12 = 5 : 8 aur AE : EC = 8.5 : 13.6 = 5 : 8, ek hi kram mein barabar, to converse se **DE ∥ BC.** Hedge 2: AE : EC = 8.5 : 13 = 17 : 26, jo 5 : 8 se alag, to **parallel nahi.**
**Jaanch:** 7.5 / 12 = 0.625, 8.5 / 13.6 = 0.625 ✅ aur 8.5 / 13 lagbhag 0.654, alag ✅.

**7.** (a) ∠OPQ = ∠ORS (alternate, PQ ∥ SR, transversal PR) aur ∠OQP = ∠OSR (alternate, transversal QS). AA se **ΔOPQ ~ ΔORS** (O↔O, P↔R, Q↔S). (b) OP / OR = PQ / RS = 20 / 28 = 5/7, to OR = 9 × 7/5 = **12.6 cm.** (c) Area ratio (5/7)² = 25/49, to ar(ΔORS) = 75 × 49/25 = **147 cm².**
**Jaanch:** 9 / 12.6 = 5/7 ✅ aur 75 / 147 = 25/49 ✅.

**8.** AD² = BD × DC, 36 = 4 × DC, DC = **9 cm.** BC = 4 + 9 = 13 cm. AB² = BD × BC = 4 × 13 = 52, AB = **2√13 cm.** AC² = DC × BC = 9 × 13 = 117, AC = **3√13 cm.**
**Jaanch:** AB² + AC² = 52 + 117 = 169 = 13² ✅.

**9.** Area ratio 18 : 128 = 9 : 64, to side ratio = 3 : 8 (root). Longest side 15 × 8/3 = **40 cm.** Perimeter 33 × 8/3 = **88 cm.**
**Jaanch:** (15 / 40)² = (3/8)² = 9/64 = 18/128 ✅.

**10.** Sabse chhoti side ko sabse badi se jod diya (6 ↔ 7.2). Isse copy ki sides 3, 4, 6 aati hain, jo asli se **chhoti** hain, jabki enlarged copy chahiye. Sahi: shortest 3.6 ↔ 6, k = 6 / 3.6 = 5/3. Baaki: 4.8 × 5/3 = **8 cm** aur 7.2 × 5/3 = **12 cm.** Copy: 6, 8, 12.
**Jaanch:** 8 / 4.8 = 12 / 7.2 = 6 / 3.6 = 5/3 ✅.

**11.** **Nahi.** Sorted ratios: 12/8 = 3/2, 15/10 = 3/2, par 20/13 lagbhag 1.538, 3/2 nahi. Dikhna kaafi nahi, test poora nahi hua. Similar ke liye RP = 13 × 3/2 = **19.5 cm** chahiye.
**Jaanch:** 12 / 8 = 15 / 10 = 19.5 / 13 = 1.5 ✅.

**12.** (a) **True** ✅: area ratio k × k = (1/4)² = 1/16. (b) **False** ❌: area 1/25 ka nahi, (1/25)² = 1/625 hai. (c) **False** ❌: 1 m² = 100 × 100 = 10,000 cm². (d) **True** ✅: root 7/11 hai. (e) **False** ❌: dikhna kaafi nahi, tukdon ka ratio converse se check karna padta hai ya parallel diya hona chahiye.

**13.** Perimeter ratio 36 : 54 = 2 : 3, to k (chhoti se badi) = 3/2. Badi window ki longest side = 15 × 3/2 = **22.5 cm.** Area: chhoti = 108 × (2/3)² = 108 × 4/9 = **48 cm².**
**Jaanch:** 48 × (3/2)² = 48 × 9/4 = 108 ✅.

**14.** (a) **BPT ka converse:** AD : DB aur AE : EC ek hi kram mein barabar hain ya nahi. (b) **Area ratio:** asli area = 300 × 15² = 300 × 225 = 67,500 cm² = 6.75 m². (c) **Altitude relation:** AD² = BD × DC. (d) **AA (parchhai):** stick ki unchai / stick ki parchhai = mast ki unchai / mast ki parchhai.
**Jaanch:** (b) 67,500 / 10,000 = 6.75 m² ✅.
