# Topic 1: Ek Jaisi Shakl, Alag Size: Similar Figures

Class 9 mein tumne **congruent** figures dekhe: bilkul same shape **aur** same size, jaise ek hi saanche se bane do sikke. Wahan ek chhota sa side-note bhi tha: shape same, size alag ho to figures **similar** hoti hain, aur "poori baat aage ki classes mein aayegi". Wo "aage" ab aa gaya hai. Is poore chapter mein hum similar triangles ke kamaal dekhenge: parallel line se lambai kaatna, tower ki unchai bina chadhe naapna, aur Pythagoras ka ek naya saboot. Par pehle ek sawaal: "same shape" ka **pakka** matlab kya hai? Aankh se dekh kar kehna kaafi nahi, hume use naap-tol se pakadna hai.

## Shilpa ka banner

School ka annual function paas aa raha hai. Shilpa ko school ke **logo** ka kaam mila hai. Logo ek chhota rectangle hai: **4 cm chaura aur 6 cm lamba**. Ye T-shirts par chhapta hai. Is baar usi logo ko ek bade banner par bhi lagana hai.

Print-shop wale uncle ne banner ka pehla design-proof laaya: sheet par logo ka naya rectangle **12 cm chaura aur 6 cm lamba**. Uncle bole, "Dekho, chaudai 4 se 12 ho gayi, teen guna. Logo bada ho gaya na?"

Shilpa ne ek nazar dekha aur boli, "Uncle, bada to hua, par **mota** ho gaya! Ye hamara logo nahi lag raha."

```
T-shirt (4 x 6)    Pehla proof (12 x 6)      Sahi banner (12 x 18)

####               ############              ############
####               ############              ############
####               ############              ############
                                             ############
                                             ############
                                             ############
                                             ############
                                             ############
                                             ############
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Teen rectangles: T-shirt wala logo, uncle ka pehla proof, aur sahi enlargement. Figures not to scale, bas shape ka andaaza dene ke liye hain.

Uncle ne dobara socha. Chaudai 4 se 12 gayi (3 guna), par lambai 6 ki 6 hi rahi (1 guna). Logo **ek taraf se zyada kheencha** gaya. Jab chaudai aur lambai **dono** 3 guna kiye (12 cm aur 18 cm), tab logo bilkul apna jaisa dikha, bas bada.

Dhyan do: pehle proof mein bhi rectangle ke chaaron kone 90° hi the. Phir bhi shape bigad gayi. To "same shape" ka matlab sirf kone barabar hona nahi hai. Aakhir ye aur kya maangta hai? Chalo, seedha niyam banate hain.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Similar figures** | Do figures jinka shape same ho, size chahe alag ho: unke corresponding angles barabar aur corresponding sides ek hi ratio mein hon | 4 x 6 ka logo aur 12 x 18 ka banner-logo |
| **Corresponding (jodidaar)** | Ek figure ka wo hissa jo doosri figure ke kisi hisse se "match" karta ho, jaise chhote logo ka kona aur bade logo ka usi jagah wala kona | chhote logo ki 4 cm side aur bade logo ki 12 cm side |
| **Scale factor (k)** | Naye figure ki ek side ko purani figure ki matching side se bhaag dene par jo number aata hai: k = naya / purana | 12 / 4 = 3, to k = 3 |
| **Enlargement (bada karna)** | Similar figure banana jismein k 1 se bada ho | k = 3 |
| **Reduction (chhota karna)** | Similar figure banana jismein k 1 se chhota ho | k = 2/5 |
| **ΔABC ~ ΔPQR** | "Triangle ABC, triangle PQR ke similar hai" padhte hain. ~ ka nishaan similar dikhata hai. Vertices ka order bhi pehchaan batata hai: A ke saamne P, B ke saamne Q, C ke saamne R | ΔABC ~ ΔPQR |
| **Standard shape** | Aisa shape jiska har size similar hota hai, isliye hum use "ek shape, kai size" maante hain | circle, square (iska naam bas yaad rakho, aage kaam aayega) |

## Part A: Similar ka pakka matlab

Shilpa ke logo ne do shartein sikha di:

1. **Corresponding angles barabar** hon.
2. **Corresponding sides ek hi ratio mein** hon, yaani har jodi ka (naya / purana) ek hi number aaye.

Do cheezein dono chahiye. Pehla proof (12 x 6) shart 1 poori karta tha, shart 2 nahi: chaudai ka ratio 12/4 = 3, lambai ka ratio 6/6 = 1, dono alag. Isliye similar nahi tha. Sahi banner (12 x 18) mein 12/4 = 3 aur 18/6 = 3, dono ek: similar.

Ye ek hi number, jo har jodi par aaye, **scale factor k** kehlata hai.

> **Similar figures:** Do figures similar hain agar (1) unke corresponding angles barabar hain aur (2) unki corresponding sides ek hi ratio mein hain. Ye ratio hi scale factor k hai.

**Kyun ek hi ratio?** Soch lo ki tum ek design ko kisi machine se zoom karte ho. Machine har lambai ko **ek hi guna** se badhati hai (zoom ka number) aur kone nahi badalti. Agar kisi side ko zyada aur kisi ko kam badhaaye, to shape kheench jaata hai, bilkul pehle proof ki tarah.

### k ko samajhna

| k kitna hai | Kya hua | Naam |
|---|---|---|
| k bada 1 se (jaise 3 ya 3/2) | Figure bada hua | Enlargement |
| k = 1 | Size bhi same | Congruent figures (ye similar ka khaas case hai) |
| k chhota 1 se (jaise 2/5) | Figure chhota hua | Reduction |

Agar sahi shape ke saath k = 1 ho, to sab angles aur sab sides barabar hain, yaani figures congruent hain (Class 9 ke SSS wale tareeke se bhi). Isliye Class 9 ki baat sahi nikli: **har congruent figure similar hai, par har similar figure congruent nahi.**

Ek aur chhoti baat: agar chhote logo se bade logo tak k = 3 hai, to bade logo se chhote logo tak k = 1/3 hai. Dono ek hi sach ke do roop hain.

### Example 1: kaun si rectangles similar hain?

Rectangle R: 9 cm x 15 cm. Rectangle S: 12 cm x 20 cm. Rectangle T: 12 cm x 18 cm. Kya R aur S similar hain? Kya R aur T similar hain?

**Kadam 1:** Rectangles ke saare kone 90° hain, to shart 1 sab mein poori.

**Kadam 2 (R aur S):** Corresponding sides: 9 cm ke saamne 12 cm, 15 cm ke saamne 20 cm. Ratios: 12/9 = 4/3 aur 20/15 = 4/3. Barabar, to R aur S similar hain, k = 4/3.

**Kadam 3 (R aur T):** 12/9 = 4/3 par 18/15 = 6/5. 4/3 aur 6/5 barabar nahi (cross-multiply: 4 x 5 = 20 aur 3 x 6 = 18, 20 ≠ 18). Shart 2 fail, to R aur T similar nahi.

**Jaanch:** S ko R se banaya: 9 x 4/3 = 12 ✅ aur 15 x 4/3 = 20 ✅. T ke liye 15 x 4/3 = 20, par T ki lambai 18 hai, isliye alag ✅ (T, R ka zoom nahi hai).

## Part B: Kuch figures hamesha similar hoti hain

Kuch shapes mein itni cheezein tay hoti hain ki **koi bhi do** similar nikalte hain.

**Koi bhi do squares similar hain.** Square ki saari sides barabar, saare kone 90°. Side a aur side b ke do squares mein har jodi ka ratio b/a hi aata hai. Angle bhi barabar, to similar.

**Koi bhi do equilateral triangles similar hain.** Teeno angle 60° (Class 9 ke angle sum se: 180° ÷ 3). Side a aur side b ke do equilateral triangles mein teeno jodi ka ratio b/a. Dono shartein poori.

**Koi bhi do circles similar hain.** Circle mein koi kone ya sides nahi hain, bas radius hai. Radius r wale circle ko k guna kheenchne par radius kr wala circle milta hai, aur wo bhi circle hi hai. (Ye hum is tarah maan lete hain: circle ke har point centre se r door hain, zoom karne par kr door ho jaate hain.) Isliye har circle bas ek "standard shape" ka alag size hai.

**Par sab rectangles similar nahi hote** (Figure 1.1 wala pehla proof isi baat ka sabut hai), aur **sab rhombuses bhi nahi** (Aam galtiyan mein dekhenge).

### Example 2: do rectangles ka missing hissa

Ek rectangle ka logo 2.5 cm chaura aur 3.5 cm lamba hai. Usi shape ka bada print banana hai jiski chaudai 10 cm ho. Lambai kitni hogi?

**Kadam 1:** Chaudai se k nikaalo: k = 10 / 2.5 = 4.

**Kadam 2:** Lambai bhi 4 guna: 3.5 x 4 = 14 cm.

**Jaanch:** 10/2.5 = 4 aur 14/3.5 = 4, dono ek hi ratio ✅. Cross-multiply: 2.5 x 14 = 35 aur 3.5 x 10 = 35 ✅.

## Part C: Similar triangles aur unka naam likhne ka tarika

Ab triangles par aate hain, kyunki ye chapter inhi ka hai. Jab hum likhte hain

> **ΔABC ~ ΔPQR**

to iska matlab sirf "similar hain" nahi hai. **Letters ka order ye bhi batata hai ki kaun kiske saamne hai:**

- A ke saamne P, B ke saamne Q, C ke saamne R. Yaani ∠A = ∠P, ∠B = ∠Q, ∠C = ∠R.
- Sides bhi isi order se: AB ke saamne PQ, BC ke saamne QR, CA ke saamne RP.
- Aur AB/PQ = BC/QR = CA/RP, yaani teen ratio barabar, jo scale factor hai.

```
         A                              P
        /o\                            /o\
       /   \                          /   \
      /     \                        /     \
     /       \                      /       \
    /x       +\                    /x       +\
   B-----------C                  Q-----------R
```

> **Figure 1.2** – ΔABC ~ ΔPQR: o, x, + ek hi nishaan wale angle barabar hain (∠A = ∠P, ∠B = ∠Q, ∠C = ∠R).

**Ek order ka niyam:** pehle letter ki pehle se, doosre ki doosre se, teesre ki teesre se jodi banti hai. Isliye hum ΔABC ~ ΔPQR ko ΔBCA ~ ΔQRP bhi likh sakte hain (teeno jodi saath-saath ghoom gayi), par ΔABC ~ ΔQPR **nahi** likh sakte, kyunki wahan A ke saamne Q aa jaata hai.

(Triangles ke liye aage ek baat aayegi: do triangles ko similar sabit karne ke liye shartein kam bhi chal jaati hain. Wo Topic 3 mein hai. Abhi hum maan ke chalte hain ki diya gaya hai ki triangles similar hain.)

### Example 3: sides aur angle ek saath

ΔABC ~ ΔPQR. AB = 8 cm, BC = 10 cm, CA = 14 cm, aur PQ = 12 cm. Ye bhi diya hai ki ∠B lagbhag 101.5° hai. Scale factor k (ΔABC se ΔPQR tak), QR, RP aur ∠Q nikalo.

```
        A                          P
       / \                        / \
   8  /   \  14               12 /   \ 21
     /     \                    /     \
    B-------C                  Q-------R
        10                         15
```

> **Figure 1.3** – ΔABC aur ΔPQR (jisme PQ = 12 diya hai; QR = 15 aur RP = 21 niche ke Kadam 2 mein nikalte hain).

**Triangle bante hain?** ΔABC: 8 + 10 = 18 > 14, 8 + 14 > 10, 10 + 14 > 8, haan. 

**Kadam 1 (k):** PQ, AB ke saamne hai. k = PQ / AB = 12 / 8 = 3/2.

**Kadam 2:** QR, BC ke saamne: QR = 10 x 3/2 = 15 cm. RP, CA ke saamne: RP = 14 x 3/2 = 21 cm.

**Kadam 3:** ∠Q, ∠B ke saamne hai, aur angle badalte nahi: ∠Q = ∠B lagbhag 101.5°.

**Jaanch:** ΔPQR bhi bana sakte hain: 12 + 15 = 27 > 21 ✅. Teeno ratio: 12/8 = 3/2, 15/10 = 3/2, 21/14 = 3/2 ✅.

### Example 4: chhota karna (k kam 1 se)

School ID card par logo mein ek triangle hai ΔABC jiski sides AB = 20 cm, BC = 15 cm, CA = 10 cm (design sheet par). Card ke liye ΔDEF banana hai ΔABC ~ ΔDEF ke saath, k = 2/5. DEF ki sides nikaalo.

**Kadam 1:** DE = 20 x 2/5 = 8 cm. EF = 15 x 2/5 = 6 cm. FD = 10 x 2/5 = 4 cm.

**Kadam 2 (triangle bante hain?):** 6 + 4 = 10 > 8, haan.

**Jaanch:** 8/20 = 2/5, 6/15 = 2/5, 4/10 = 2/5 ✅ (teeno ek hi ratio). Ulta: card se sheet par jaane ka scale factor 5/2 hai: 8 x 5/2 = 20 ✅.

### Example 5: order kaise nikaalte hain? (angles se)

Do triangles similar diye gaye hain. ΔABC mein ∠A = 35°, ∠B = 75°, ∠C = 70°. ΔXYZ mein ∠X = 70°, ∠Y = 35°, ∠Z = 75°. Similarity statement likho aur corresponding sides batao.

```
        A                         X
       /35\                      /70\
      /    \                    /    \
     /      \                  /      \
    B75------C70              Y35------Z75
```

> **Figure 1.4** – Angles ke naap (not to scale): ΔABC aur ΔXYZ. Dhyan do ki ΔXYZ mein 35° wala kone Y par hai, X par nahi.

**Kadam 1 (barabar angle jodo):** 35° wala A ke saamne Y. 75° wala B ke saamne Z. 70° wala C ke saamne X.

**Kadam 2 (statement):** A, B, C ke saamne Y, Z, X: **ΔABC ~ ΔYZX.** (ΔABC ~ ΔXYZ likhna galat hota, kyunki wahan A ke saamne X aa jaata, jabki ∠A = 35° aur ∠X = 70°.)

**Kadam 3 (sides):** Order se: AB ke saamne YZ, BC ke saamne ZX, CA ke saamne XY. Ek tarkeeb: **jo side kisi angle ke saamne hai, wo doosre triangle mein barabar angle ke saamne wali side se jodti hai.** Jaise BC, ∠A (35°) ke saamne hai, aur ZX, ∠Y (35°) ke saamne.

**Jaanch:** Angle sum: 35 + 75 + 70 = 180 ✅ aur 70 + 35 + 75 = 180 ✅. Teeno jodi mein angle barabar: 35 = 35, 75 = 75, 70 = 70 ✅.

### Example 6: shabdon mein sawaal

Banner par logo ke andar ek triangle emblem hai jiski sides 12 cm, 14 cm aur 18 cm hain. Shilpa ko ek chhota badge banana hai jisme usi emblem ki sabse chhoti side 6 cm ho. Badge ki baaki do sides kitni hongi?

**Kadam 1:** k = 6 / 12 = 1/2. (Chhota karna, k < 1.)

**Kadam 2:** 14 x 1/2 = 7 cm aur 18 x 1/2 = 9 cm.

**Story check:** Badge chhota hai, to uski sides 12, 14, 18 se chhoti honi chahiye: 6, 7, 9, ye sab chhoti hain. Triangle bhi bana sakte hain: 6 + 7 = 13 > 9.

**Jaanch:** 6/12 = 7/14 = 9/18 = 1/2 ✅.

## Part D: Polygons mein bhi yehi niyam (sirf ek jhalak)

Rectangles aur triangles ke alawa pentagon, hexagon jaise **polygons** (jinki bahut si sides ho) bhi similar ho sakte hain. Niyam wahi:

- Dono mein **sides ki ginti barabar** ho (ek triangle aur ek rectangle kabhi similar nahi hote).
- **Corresponding angles barabar** hon.
- **Corresponding sides ek hi ratio mein** hon.

Polygon mein teen se zyada sides hon to **dono shartein alag-alag zaroori hain**, ek se doosri khud nahi nikalti. (Triangle ek khaas figure hai, uski baat Topic 3 mein hogi.) Isliye aage ki galtiyon mein dono ki jaanch karna sikhenge.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

### Galti 1: sirf angle ya sirf ratio dekh kar "similar" bol dena

**Demo (a): angle barabar, sides ka ratio nahi.** Rectangle P: 2 cm x 3 cm. Rectangle Q: 4 cm x 9 cm. Ek bacha kehta hai, "Dono ke chaaron kone 90° hain, to similar hain."

Sides ka ratio dekho: chaudai ka 4/2 = 2, lambai ka 9/3 = 3. 2 ≠ 3, to koi ek k nahi mila. Rectangle P ko 2 guna karo to 4 x 6 milta hai, 4 x 9 nahi. **Similar nahi hain.**

```
Rectangle P (2 x 3)     Rectangle Q (4 x 9)

####                    ########
####                    ########
####                    ########
                        ########
                        ########
                        ########
                        ########
```

> **Figure 1.5** – P aur Q ke kone barabar hain par Q kheencha hua dikhta hai.

**Jaanch:** 4/2 = 2 aur 9/3 = 3, 2 ≠ 3 ✅ (isliye similar nahi).

**Demo (b): sides ka ratio barabar, angle nahi.** Ek square jiski har side 5 cm, aur ek rhombus jiski har side 10 cm, par uske angle 60° aur 120° hain. Ek bacha kehta hai, "Chaaron sides ka ratio 10/5 = 2, har jodi mein ek hi k, to similar."

Par shart 1 dekho: square ke angle 90°, rhombus ke 60° aur 120°. Square ko 2 guna kheenchoge to 10 cm ka **square** milega, rhombus nahi. Square bada karne par kone nahi jhukte, isliye ye similar nahi.

```
Square (side 5)       Rhombus (side 10, angle 60 aur 120)

#####                      ##########
#####                     ##########
#####                    ##########
#####                   ##########
```

> **Figure 1.6** – Square aur rhombus: chaaron sides ka ratio ek hi hai (2), par kone alag hain.

**Jaanch:** Sides: 10/5 = 2 (chaaron jodi mein) lekin 90° ≠ 60° ✅ (isliye similar nahi).

**Sahi tarika:** **dono** shartein alag-alag likh kar check karo: pehle angles, phir sab jodi ka ratio.

### Galti 2: ΔABC ~ ΔPQR likhte waqt vertices ka order galat likhna

ΔABC mein AB = 6 cm, BC = 9 cm, CA = 10 cm. ΔPQR mein PQ = 18 cm, QR = 20 cm, RP = 12 cm. Dono similar diye gaye hain, aur figure mein barabar angle ek hi nishaan se marked hain: ∠A = ∠R, ∠B = ∠P, ∠C = ∠Q.

```
         A                              R
        /o\                            /o\
    6  /   \  10                   12 /   \ 20
      /     \                        /     \
     /x     +\                      /x     +\
    B---------C                    P---------Q
         9                              18
```

> **Figure 1.7** – ΔABC aur ΔPQR, jisme R upar hai aur P, Q neeche. Angle ke nishaan: o ek hi, x ek hi, + ek hi.

Ek bacha figure ko left se right padhta hai, kehta hai: **ΔABC ~ ΔPQR** (alphabet ke order mein). Isse ratios bante hain AB/PQ = 6/18 = 1/3, BC/QR = 9/20, CA/RP = 10/12 = 5/6. Teeno alag, to bacha ghabra gaya ki "ye similar nahi hain".

Galti ye hai ki A ke saamne P aaya, jabki figure mein A ke saamne **R** hai (o nishaan). Sahi order angles se banta hai: A ke saamne R, B ke saamne P, C ke saamne Q, yaani **ΔABC ~ ΔRPQ.**

Ab sahi ratios: AB/RP = 6/12 = 1/2, BC/PQ = 9/18 = 1/2, CA/QR = 10/20 = 1/2. Teeno barabar, to k = 2 (ΔABC se ΔRPQ tak).

**Jaanch:** 6 x 2 = 12 ✅, 9 x 2 = 18 ✅, 10 x 2 = 20 ✅.

**Sahi tarika:** letters ko naam ke order se nahi, **barabar angles** (ya jodi wali sides) se jodo, phir statement likho.

## Quick Recap

- **Similar figures:** corresponding angles barabar **aur** corresponding sides ek hi ratio mein. Dono shartein.
- Wo ek ratio **scale factor k** (naya / purana) hai. k > 1 bada, k < 1 chhota, k = 1 congruent.
- Har congruent figure similar hai, par har similar figure congruent nahi.
- Koi bhi do squares, koi bhi do equilateral triangles, koi bhi do circles similar hote hain. Sab rectangles ya sab rhombuses nahi.
- **ΔABC ~ ΔPQR** ka matlab A, B, C ke saamne P, Q, R. Sides: AB-PQ, BC-QR, CA-RP.
- Missing side: pehle k nikaalo (ek jodi se), phir doosri sides ko k se guna karo.
- Jaanch: har jodi ka ratio dobara nikaalo, ya cross-multiply (ad = bc).

## Practice Questions

1. Are rectangles 14 cm x 20 cm and 21 cm x 30 cm similar? Give the scale factor or the reason they are not.
2. Are rectangles 16 cm x 10 cm and 24 cm x 14 cm similar? Explain with the ratios of corresponding sides.
3. A rectangle measures 6 cm x 14 cm. Another rectangle is similar to it and is 15 cm wide (the side corresponding to 6 cm). Find the other side.
4. ΔLMN ~ ΔSTU, with LM = 14 cm, MN = 22 cm, NL = 18 cm and ST = 10.5 cm. Find the scale factor from ΔLMN to ΔSTU, then find TU and US.
5. ΔEFG ~ ΔHIJ. ∠E = 48° and ∠F = 67°. Find ∠G, ∠H, ∠I and ∠J.
6. In ΔABC, ∠A = 50°, ∠B = 60°, ∠C = 70°. In ΔDEF, ∠D = 60°, ∠E = 70°, ∠F = 50°, and the triangles are similar. Write the similarity statement in the form ΔABC ~ Δ??? and name the side of ΔDEF that corresponds to AB.
7. A sticker is 4.5 cm wide and 6.5 cm tall. Printed on a mug it is enlarged so that its width becomes 13.5 cm. Find the new height and the scale factor.
8. A rectangle is 12 cm x 21 cm. A smaller rectangle similar to it has its shorter side 8 cm. Find its longer side and the scale factor from the bigger to the smaller rectangle.
9. An emblem is a triangle with sides 9 cm, 11 cm and 15 cm. A reduced print is similar to it and its longest side is 12 cm. Find the other two sides. Is the reduced triangle possible (triangle inequality)?
10. Two circles have radii 6.5 cm and 15.6 cm. Are they similar? What is the scale factor from the smaller to the bigger?
11. For ΔABC ~ ΔPQR the scale factor from ΔABC to ΔPQR is 7/3. (a) What is the scale factor from ΔPQR to ΔABC? (b) If AB = 9 cm, find PQ.
12. True or false, with a one-line reason each: (a) Every two squares are similar. (b) Every two rectangles are similar. (c) A rhombus of side 7 cm with angles 70° and 110° is similar to a rhombus of side 14 cm with angles 80° and 100°. (d) If two similar figures have k = 1, they are congruent. (e) A triangle can be similar to a quadrilateral.
13. In ΔPQR, ∠P = 30°, ∠Q = 80°, ∠R = 70°. In ΔXYZ, ∠X = 80°, ∠Y = 70°, ∠Z = 30°. A student wrote ΔPQR ~ ΔXYZ. Find the mistake and write the correct statement.

### Answers

1. Dono rectangles ke kone 90°. Ratios: 21/14 = 3/2 aur 30/20 = 3/2. Barabar, to similar, k = 3/2.
   **Jaanch:** 14 x 3/2 = 21 ✅, 20 x 3/2 = 30 ✅.

2. Corresponding sides: 24/16 = 3/2 aur 14/10 = 7/5. Cross-multiply: 3 x 5 = 15 aur 2 x 7 = 14, 15 ≠ 14, to ratios alag. **Similar nahi.**
   **Jaanch:** 16 x 3/2 = 24 par 10 x 3/2 = 15, 14 nahi ✅.

3. k = 15/6 = 5/2. Doosri side = 14 x 5/2 = **35 cm**.
   **Jaanch:** 6 x 5/2 = 15 ✅ aur 14 x 5/2 = 35 ✅. Cross-multiply: 6 x 35 = 210 aur 14 x 15 = 210 ✅.

4. k = ST/LM = 10.5/14 = **3/4**. TU = MN x 3/4 = 22 x 3/4 = **16.5 cm**. US = NL x 3/4 = 18 x 3/4 = **13.5 cm**.
   **Jaanch:** 10.5/14 = 16.5/22 = 13.5/18 = 3/4 ✅. Triangle: 10.5 + 13.5 = 24 > 16.5 ✅.

5. ∠G = 180° − 48° − 67° = **65°**. ΔEFG ~ ΔHIJ, to E-H, F-I, G-J: ∠H = **48°**, ∠I = **67°**, ∠J = **65°**.
   **Jaanch:** 48 + 67 + 65 = 180 ✅ (ΔHIJ ka bhi sum 180).

6. 50° wala ∠A, ∠F (50°) ke saamne; 60° wala ∠B, ∠D (60°) ke saamne; 70° wala ∠C, ∠E (70°) ke saamne. A, B, C ke saamne F, D, E: **ΔABC ~ ΔFDE.** AB ke saamne **FD** hai.
   **Jaanch:** 50 + 60 + 70 = 180 ✅. Teeno jodi mein angle barabar: 50-50, 60-60, 70-70 ✅.

7. k = 13.5 / 4.5 = **3**. Height = 6.5 x 3 = **19.5 cm**.
   **Jaanch:** 13.5/4.5 = 3 ✅ aur 19.5/6.5 = 3 ✅.

8. k (bade se chhote tak) = 8/12 = **2/3**. Longer side = 21 x 2/3 = **14 cm**.
   **Jaanch:** 8/12 = 14/21 = 2/3 ✅. Cross-multiply: 12 x 14 = 168 aur 21 x 8 = 168 ✅.

9. k = 12/15 = 4/5. Doosri sides: 9 x 4/5 = **7.2 cm** aur 11 x 4/5 = **8.8 cm**.
   **Story check:** 7.2 + 8.8 = 16 > 12, aur baaki dono jodi bhi theek, to triangle bana sakte hain.
   Jaanch: 7.2/9 = 8.8/11 = 12/15 = 4/5 ✅.

10. Circles hamesha similar hote hain. k = 15.6 / 6.5 = **12/5** (yaani 2.4).
    **Jaanch:** 6.5 x 12/5 = 15.6 ✅.

11. (a) Ulta k: 1/(7/3) = **3/7**. (b) PQ = AB x 7/3 = 9 x 7/3 = **21 cm**.
    **Jaanch:** 21 x 3/7 = 9 ✅.

12. (a) **True** ✅: sab sides barabar aur sab kone 90°, har jodi ka ratio ek hi (Part B).
    (b) **False** ❌: kone to sab 90° hote hain, par sides ka ratio badal sakta hai. Jaise 5 x 8 aur 10 x 9 mein 10/5 = 2 par 9/8 ≠ 2.
    (c) **False** ❌: dono rhombuses ke angles alag hain (70°, 110° aur 80°, 100°), to shart 1 fail, chahe chaaron sides ka ratio 14/7 = 2 ho.
    (d) **True** ✅: k = 1 mein saari sides aur saare angles barabar, yaani congruent.
    (e) **False** ❌: sides ki ginti alag (3 aur 4), to koi corresponding jodi poori nahi banti.

13. Galti ye hai ki letters ko naam ke order mein jod diya. Barabar angles: 30° wala P, Z par; 80° wala Q, X par; 70° wala R, Y par. P, Q, R ke saamne Z, X, Y: **ΔPQR ~ ΔZXY.**
    **Jaanch:** 30 = 30, 80 = 80, 70 = 70 ✅ aur dono ka angle sum 180 ✅.
