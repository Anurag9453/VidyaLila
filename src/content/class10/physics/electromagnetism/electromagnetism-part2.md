# Topic 2: Motor Andar Se: Coil, Commutator Aur Spin

Topic 1 mein dekha ki magnetic field mein rakhe current wale taar par force lagta hai, jo field aur current dono se 90° par hota hai, aur uski disha left-hand rule se milti hai. Ek seedhe taar ka wo force use bas ek taraf dhakel deta hai. Aaj hum us force ko **ghumaav** mein badalna seekhenge: taar ko loop banao, to wahi force ghumne ka kaam karta hai. Yahi motor hai.

## Class 9 ka recall

Class 9 mein tumne ek rectangular coil ko magnet ke beech rakha tha: coil ki do aamne-saamne ki bhujaon par ulte force lagte hain, to wo ghoomti hai, aur **split-ring commutator** har aadhe chakkar par current ulta karta hai ([magnetism-part6.md](../../../class9/physics/magnetism/magnetism-part6.md)). Wahan likha tha ki "iski design ki bareeki aage ki class mein." Wo bareeki aaj aayegi: **kis position par kitna force, kyun aadhe chakkar par hi current ulta karna hai, aur motor kitni efficient hoti hai.**

Conventions wahi: page par right (→), left (←), up (↑), down (↓); page ke andar **⊗**, page se bahar **⊙**. Left-hand rule: forefinger = field, middle finger = current, thumb = force.

## Part A: Coil ki do bhujaon par ulte force

Coil ko aise dekho jaise uska ek sira tumhari taraf hai (end-on view). Do lambi bhujayein page ke perpendicular hain: ek mein current **page se bahar (⊙)** aur doosri mein **page ke andar (⊗)**, kyunki loop mein current ek bhuja mein aage jaata hai aur doosri mein wapas aata hai. Field N se S ki taraf (→).

```
      →  →  →  →  →  →  →  →  →  →  →  →  →
              ↑ F
              :
[N]   →       ⊙═════════ + ═════════⊗       →           [S]
                                    :
                                    ↓ F
      →  →  →  →  →  →  →  →  →  →  →  →  →
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Leta hua (flat) coil, end-on view, field → (N se S). Left-hand rule: ⊙ wali bhuja par force ↑, ⊗ wali par ↓. Axle ke ek taraf ↑ aur doosri taraf ↓ hone se coil **clockwise** ghoomti hai.

Teen baatein dhyan se:
- Ulte force, par **alag jagah** par (axle ke dono taraf). Isi se coil ghoomti hai; ek force akela hota to dhakel deta, ghumata nahi. Turning effect = force × axle se perpendicular doori (Class 9 ka moment).
- Coil ki baaki do chhoti bhujayein leta hua (flat) coil mein field ke **parallel** hain, to unpar force **zero** hai (Topic 1: parallel hone par zero). Coil ghoomne par unpar jo thoda force aata hai, wo axle ki line mein ulte-ulte hai aur kat jaata hai.
- Do lambi bhujayein hamesha field ke perpendicular rehti hain, to ghoomte waqt unpar force ka size wahi rehta hai. Jo badalta hai wo axle se perpendicular doori hai: leta hua coil par sabse zyada, khadi coil par zero. Isliye turning effect bhi isi tarah badalta hai.

### Example 1: Coil kis taraf ghoomegi?

**Given:** Leta hua (flat) coil, end-on view. Field ← (N pole right mein, S pole left mein). Coil ki **left** bhuja mein current ⊗ (page ke andar), **right** bhuja mein ⊙ (page se bahar).
**Find:** Dono bhujaon par force ki disha aur coil ghoomne ki disha.
**Formula:** Left-hand rule: forefinger = field, middle finger = current, thumb = force.
**Substitution:** Left bhuja: forefinger ←, middle finger page ke andar, thumb **↑**. Right bhuja: forefinger ←, middle finger page se bahar (apni taraf), thumb **↓**. Left bhuja ↑ aur right bhuja ↓: left upar jaati hai aur right neeche aati hai, jo ghadi ki disha hai.
**Answer:** Left ↑, right ↓, coil **clockwise** ghoomegi.
**Jaanch 1 (doosra tareeka):** Figure 2.1 mein field → aur left bhuja ⊙ thi: clockwise. Yahan field **bhi** ulta (←) aur left bhuja ka current **bhi** ulta (⊗): do cheezein ulti, to force wahi, yaani wahi clockwise ghoomna ✅.
**Jaanch 2 (taar ka apna field):** Left bhuja ⊗ hai, to right-hand thumb rule se uske chaaron taraf field ghadi ki disha mein hai: taar ke **upar** wo → hai, **neeche** ←. Magnet ka field ← hai: neeche dono ← (jud gaye, field zyada), upar → aur ← (kat gaye, field kam). Taar zyada field se kam field ki taraf, yaani **upar** dhakela jaata hai ✅.

Battery ulti lagao (left bhuja ⊙, right ⊗, field ← wahi): ab left ↓ aur right ↑, coil **anticlockwise**. Yahi hobby motor wali activity (Ghar par adult ke saath) ka raaz hai.

## Part B: Coil khadi ho jaaye to? Dead position

Figure 2.1 ka leta hua coil clockwise ghoomna shuru karta hai. Ek chauthai chakkar (90°) ke baad coil field ke **perpendicular** khadi ho jaati hai. Tab kya hota hai?

```
(a) khada coil           (b) aage: reverse nahi   (c) aage: reverse hua
→  →  →  →  →  →  →      →  →  →  →  →  →  →      →  →  →  →  →  →  →
          ↑ F                           ↑ F                      ↓ F
          ⊙                             ⊙                        ⊗
          ║                           /                        /
          +                         +                        +
          ║                       /                        /
          ⊗                     ⊗                        ⊙
          ↓ F                   ↓ F                      ↑ F
→  →  →  →  →  →  →      →  →  →  →  →  →  →      →  →  →  →  →  →  →
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Field → hai. (a) Field ke perpendicular khada coil: ⊙ upar, ⊗ neeche, force ↑ aur ↓ coil ki **apni line** mein: ghumane ka asar zero (dead position). (b) Thoda aage ghoomne par, current wahi rahe: ⊙ wali bhuja upper right mein aa gayi, uspar ↑ force coil ko wapas (anticlockwise) khinchta hai. (c) Current ulta hua: ⊗ bhuja upper right mein, force ↓, coil clockwise ghoomti rahti hai.

Figure 2.2(a): forces ↑ aur ↓ coil ki line mein hain, to wo coil ko bas khinchte hain (axle ke aar-paar), ghumate nahi. Turning effect **zero**. Isliye ise **dead position** kehte hain.

Ab maan lo coil apni chaal (jadatv) se thoda aage nikal gayi, par current wahi raha. Figure 2.2(b): upper right mein ⊙ wali bhuja hai aur uspar ↑ force. Upper right par ↑ force anticlockwise ghumata hai, yaani coil ko **wapas** khinch lega. Coil khadi position ke aas-paas dolti hui ruk jaayegi. Yaani commutator ke bina coil khadi position tak pahunch kar dolti hui ruk jaati hai.

Ilaaj: jaise hi coil khadi position paar kare, **current ulta kar do**. Figure 2.2(c): ab upper right ki bhuja mein ⊗ hai, force ↓, aur upper right par ↓ force clockwise ghumata hai: coil wahi clockwise ghoomti rahegi. Is jaadu ka kaam **commutator** ka hai.

## Part C: Commutator aur brushes: current kab ulta hota hai

Split-ring commutator ek chhalle (ring) ke do aadhe hisse A aur B hain, jinke beech chhoti si gap hai; ye coil ke saath ghoomte hain. Brushes P aur Q sthir hain, battery se jude, aur ring ko bahar se dabkar chhoote hain.

```
(a) coil leta hua       (b) coil khadi           (c) aadha chakkar baad
      .-----.                  .-----.                  .-----.
    /     :     \            /     A     \            /     :     \
P--(  A   :  B  )--Q     P--(. . . . . .)--Q      P--(  B   :  A  )--Q
    \     :     /            \     B     /            \     :     /
      '-----'                  '-----'                  '-----'

```

> **Figure 2.3** – Commutator ke do aadhe hisse A, B aur sthir brushes P (battery ka +) aur Q. (a) P-A aur Q-B: coil mein ek disha ka current. (b) Coil khadi hone par brushes dono aadhon ke beech ki gap par: current ka raasta kuch pal ke liye band (dead position). (c) Aadha chakkar baad A aur B ki jagah badal gayi: ab P-B aur Q-A, to coil mein current **ulta** hai, jabki battery se current wahi hai.

Dhyan do: battery ka current brush se ring tak hamesha wahi disha mein jaata hai. Ring ke aadhe hisse badalte hain, isliye **coil ke andar** current ulta hota hai. Aur ye ulatna theek usi pal hota hai jab coil khadi (dead) position se guzarti hai: yaani jab current ulta karne ki zaroorat hai.

```
 [N]  →  →  →  →  →  →  →  →  →  →  →        [S]
         ⊙ ═════ coil (kai turn) ═════ ⊗
                        :
                        :  axle (coil ke saath ghoomta)
                 P ─(A)─:─(B)─ Q   <- split ring ke do aadhe hisse A, B
                 :             :
                 +──[battery]──+
              brush P aur brush Q sthir hain, ring ghoomti hai

```

> **Figure 2.4** – DC motor ka saar (schematic, scale mein nahi): coil N aur S ke beech, axle par split ring (A, B), aur sthir brushes P, Q battery se. Ring ghoomti hai, brushes nahi.

**Brushes ka kaam:** ghoomti ring ko bijli dena. Ye aksar kaarbon ki hoti hain, spring se ring par dabti hain, aur dheere-dheere ghisti hain aur chingaari bhi karti hain. Isliye brush wali motor ko kuch samay baad service chahiye. **Brushless** motor mein ye hissa hi nahi hota (sirf naam; design aage ki class mein).

**Ek aur sach:** akeli coil mein turning effect har chakkar mein do baar zero tak girta hai (har dead position par). Asli motor mein bahut si coils alag-alag kone par lagi hoti hain, aur commutator ke bahut se hisse hote hain, to kisi na kisi coil ka turning effect hamesha bada rehta hai aur motor sahi chalti hai (sirf naam; design ki bareeki aage ki class mein).

## Part D: Turning effect kin cheezon par hai?

Bade turning effect ka matlab hai ki coil bhaari load ko bhi ghuma sakti hai aur tezi se speed pakadti hai. Chaar cheezein:

| Cheez badli | Turning effect |
|---|---|
| Pher (turns) 2 guna | 2 guna |
| Magnet ka field 3 guna | 3 guna |
| Current 1.5 guna | 1.5 guna |
| Coil ka area 2 guna | 2 guna |
| Battery ulti (current ulta) | Barabar, par ghumna ulta |

Chaaron cheezein ek saath badlein to unke guna aapas mein **gunit** hote hain (turning effect ∝ pher × field × current × area). Honest note: asli **speed** sirf turning effect se nahi, load aur ghisaav se bhi tay hoti hai (motor wahan tikti hai jahan turning effect load ke barabar ho jaata hai). Par chaaron cheezein badhane se motor ki shakti badhti hai.

### Example 2: Naya coil, naya turning effect

**Given:** Coil mein pehle 80 pher, 2.5 A current, field wahi, area wahi. Naya coil: 200 pher, 2.0 A current, magnet 1.4 guna tagda, area wahi.
**Find:** Turning effect kitna guna.
**Formula:** Turning effect ∝ pher × field × current × area.
**Substitution:** Pher: 200 ÷ 80 = 2.5. Current: 2.0 A ÷ 2.5 A = 0.8. Field: 1.4. Area: 1. Guna = 2.5 × 0.8 × 1.4 × 1 = 2.8.
**Answer:** Turning effect **2.8 guna**.
**Jaanch:** Pehle 80 × 2.5 = 200 ikai; ab 200 × 2.0 × 1.4 = 560 ikai: 560 ÷ 200 = 2.8 ✅. Dhyan: current kam kiya par pher aur field itne badhe ki fayda hi hua.

## Part E: Motor ki power aur efficiency

Motor bijli (electrical) energy leta hai aur ghumne wali (mechanical) energy deta hai, par kuch energy garmi, awaaz aur ghisaav mein bhi jaati hai. Koi motor 100 percent nahi hoti.

- **Input power** = V × I (battery ka voltage × current).
- **Output power** = upyogi kaam ÷ time. Agar motor load ko ooncha uthaye, to kaam = m × g × h (Class 9 ka potential energy: [work-energy-and-power-part5.md](../../../class9/physics/work-energy-and-power/work-energy-and-power-part5.md)); g = 10 m/s² (Class 9 jaisa, aasaan hisaab ke liye).
- **Efficiency** = (output ÷ input) × 100 percent ([sources-of-energy.md](../sources-of-energy/sources-of-energy.md)).

### Example 3: Chhoti motor ka winch

**Given:** Hobby winch ki motor 9 V par 1.6 A leti hai. Wo 2.6 kg ka load 1.7 m ooncha 9.0 s mein uthati hai. g = 10 m/s².
**Find:** Input power, output power, efficiency aur bekaar jaati power.
**Formula:** P_in = V × I; P_out = m g h ÷ t; efficiency = (P_out ÷ P_in) × 100 percent.
**Substitution:** P_in = 9 V × 1.6 A = 14.4 W. Kaam = 2.6 kg × 10 m/s² × 1.7 m = 44.2 J. P_out = 44.2 J ÷ 9.0 s = 4.91 W. Efficiency = (4.91 W ÷ 14.4 W) × 100 = 34.1 percent. Bekaar = 14.4 W − 4.91 W = 9.49 W.
**Answer:** Input **14.4 W**, output lagbhag **4.91 W**, efficiency lagbhag **34.1 percent**; lagbhag 9.49 W garmi, awaaz aur ghisaav mein.
**Jaanch:** 34.1 percent of 14.4 W = 0.341 × 14.4 W = 4.91 W ✅. Output input se kam ✅. Chhoti hobby motor ki efficiency aksar kam hoti hai; bade motor aksar zyada efficient hote hain.

Garmi kahan se aayi? Coil ki taar mein bhi resistance hai, to I² R garmi banti hai ([current-electricity-ii-part5.md](../current-electricity-ii/current-electricity-ii-part5.md) ki taar ki garmi). Saath mein bearing ka ghisaav, brush ka ghisaav aur core ki garmi. Isliye energy-saving (zyada star wale) pankhe aksar wahi hawa kam bijli mein dete hain: unki motor ki efficiency zyada hoti hai, to bekaar garmi kam (star rating: [current-electricity-ii-part5.md](../current-electricity-ii/current-electricity-ii-part5.md)).

## Part F: Jab motor jam jaaye

Ek motor **ghoom** rahi hai, ya usi motor ka shaft **pakad kar roka** hua hai (stalled)? Dono mein current alag hota hai. Chalti motor mein coil khud bhi field ko kaat rahi hoti hai, aur ye ek **ulta asar** banata hai jo supply ko current dhakelne se rokta hai (iska sahi naam aur niyam Topic 3 mein milega, hisaab aage ki class mein). Isliye **chalti motor kam current leti hai**. Shaft ruk jaaye to ye asar nahi bachta, aur current sirf V ÷ R ban jaata hai: bahut zyada.

### Example 4: Ruki aur chalti motor

**Given:** Chhoti DC motor 6.0 V par; coil ka resistance 3.2 Ω. Chalte waqt current 0.42 A.
**Find:** Ruki hui motor ka current aur winding ki garmi; chalti motor ki winding ki garmi aur dono ka anupaat.
**Formula:** Ruki: I = V ÷ R; garmi P = I² R. Chalti: P = I² R.
**Substitution:** Ruki: I = 6.0 V ÷ 3.2 Ω = 1.875 A. P = (1.875 A)² × 3.2 Ω = 3.516 A² × 3.2 Ω = 11.25 W. Chalti: P = (0.42 A)² × 3.2 Ω = 0.1764 A² × 3.2 Ω = 0.564 W. Current ka anupaat: 1.875 A ÷ 0.42 A = 4.46. Garmi ka anupaat: 11.25 W ÷ 0.564 W = 19.9.
**Answer:** Ruki: **1.875 A**, **11.25 W** garmi. Chalti: **0.564 W**. Current lagbhag **4.46 guna**, garmi lagbhag **20 guna**.
**Jaanch:** (4.46)² = 19.9 ✅ (garmi ∝ I²). Chalti motor ka input 6.0 V × 0.42 A = 2.52 W; usme se 0.564 W garmi, baaki 1.956 W ghumne aur ghisaav mein (maante hue ki koi aur nuksaan nahi) ✅.

Isi se samajh aata hai ki **jam hua pankha ya mixer jaldi jalta hai**: coil mein current kai guna aur garmi uske **varg (square)** ke hisaab se aur bhi zyada. Jam jaaye to bade se kehkar switch turant band karwao; motor ko jalne mat do.

## Ghar par adult ke saath: chhoti motor ki spin ulti karo

Ek chhoti hobby motor (dukaan ya school se li hui; kisi khilone ya pankhe mein se nikalni nahi) aur ek **1.5 V ka cell** lo. Bade ko pehle se dikha do.
1. Motor ke do taar cell ke dono siro se jodo (motor hi load hai). Shaft ghoomta dikhega. Dekho kis disha mein.
2. Cell ko **ulta** karke jodo (taar badal do). Ab shaft ulta ghoomega.
3. Agar ungli se shaft rok do, to **turant cell hata do**; ruki motor garm hoti hai (Part F: Jab motor jam jaaye). Cell ke dono siro ko kabhi seedhe taar se mat jodo: motor hi beech mein rahe.

Motor ko kholna nahi: andar chhota strong magnet hai.

**Sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo):** school mein ek bada model motor, jisme coil, split ring aur brushes alag dikhte hain, 6 V ki supply aur rheostat se (teacher chalaye). Teacher coil ko dheere ghumakar dikhaye ki jab brushes gap par aate hain to current ek pal ke liye kat jaata hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Commutator sirf ek switch hai jo motor ko rokta hai."** Demonstration: Motor ko 1260 rpm par ghoomte socho. 1260 ÷ 60 = 21 chakkar per second, aur har chakkar mein commutator current ko **2 baar** ulta karta hai (har aadhe chakkar par): 2 × 21 = 42 baar per second. Agar commutator motor ko rokta, to motor 42 baar per second ruki hoti! Asli kaam ulta hai: wo current ko **sahi pal par ulta** karta hai taaki force hamesha ek hi taraf ghumaye. Bina commutator ke (jaise Figure 2.2(b)) motor khadi position ke paas dolti hui rukti hai. To commutator **rukwata nahi, chalata hai**.

**Galti 2: "Motor khud energy bana leti hai."** Demonstration: Ek mixer ki motor 480 W bijli leti hai aur 372 W ki ghumne wali power deti hai. Efficiency = (372 W ÷ 480 W) × 100 = 77.5 percent. Baaki 480 W − 372 W = 108 W garmi aur awaaz ban jaati hai; 10 minute (600 s) mein 108 W × 600 s = 64 800 J. Output **hamesha input se kam** hota hai. Energy sirf ek roop se doosre mein badalti hai (Class 9: conservation of energy), banti nahi.

## Quick Recap

- Coil ki do lambi bhujaon mein current ulte, to force ulte, aur alag jagah par: coil ghoomti hai. Baaki chhoti bhujaon par force zero.
- Leta hua coil par turning effect sabse zyada; field ke perpendicular khadi coil par zero (**dead position**).
- **Commutator** coil mein current **har aadhe chakkar par ulta** karta hai (dead position par), to ghumna ek hi disha mein rehta hai. **Brushes** sthir hain aur ring ko bijli dete hain.
- Turning effect ∝ pher × field × current × area. Battery ulti, to ghumna ulta.
- Motor: input = V × I, output = upyogi kaam ÷ time, efficiency hamesha 100 percent se kam. Bekaar energy garmi, awaaz aur ghisaav mein.
- Chalti motor ka current **ruki motor** se kam hota hai; jam motor mein current bahut zyada, I² R garmi aur jalne ka khatra.
- Pankha, mixer, pump, washing machine, EV, metro: motor. Brushless aur induction motor sirf naam.

## Practice Questions

(Use g = 10 m/s². The view in Question 5 is end-on, like Figure 2.1: the coil is flat, with its two long sides at the left and the right of the axle.)

1. A winch motor on 12 V draws 3.4 A and lifts a 24 kg load through 2.3 m in 18 s. Find the input power, the output power, the efficiency and the power wasted.
2. A 750 W pump lifts 5100 kg of water from a 9.5 m deep well in 25 minutes. Find the useful output power, the efficiency and the power lost. How many kilograms of water would it lift in one hour at the same rate?
3. A small 36 V DC motor has a winding resistance of 2.1 Ω and draws 1.9 A while running freely. (a) What current would it draw if its shaft were stalled? (b) Find the heat produced per second in the winding in each case. (c) How many times larger is the heat when stalled?
4. A motor coil has 150 turns, carries 2.4 A and has an area of 26 cm². It is replaced by a coil of 90 turns, 3.0 A and 39 cm², in the same magnet. By what factor does the turning effect change? What current would the new coil need to give the original turning effect?
5. A flat coil (end-on view) has its left and right sides in a magnetic field. Give the force on each side, and say whether the coil turns clockwise, anticlockwise, or does not turn at all.
   (a) Field →; left side ⊗, right side ⊙.
   (b) Field ←; left side ⊙, right side ⊗.
   (c) Field ↑; left side ⊙, right side ⊗.
   (d) Field ↓; left side ⊙, right side ⊗.
6. A single-coil motor with a split-ring commutator turns at 1740 rpm. How many times per second and per minute does the commutator reverse the current in the coil?
7. Explain, in four short steps, why a coil without a commutator would stop near the vertical position, and what the commutator changes. Why do real motors use many coils instead of one?
8. A small 4.5 V hobby motor is held still (stalled) by a child while it is connected to its cell pack. Describe, with reasons, what happens to the current, to the heating, and what the child should do.
9. True or false, with a reason. (a) The brushes rotate with the coil. (b) A freely spinning motor draws less current than a stalled one of the same voltage. (c) If both the battery and the magnet's poles are reversed, the motor spins the other way. (d) Every motor has a split-ring commutator. (e) A motor can have an efficiency of 105 percent.
10. A 28 V DC motor of efficiency 64 percent lifts a 17 kg bucket through 6.2 m while drawing 2.9 A. Find the electrical energy it uses and the time taken.
11. An old ceiling fan uses 74 W and a new energy-saving fan uses 33 W (assume these values). Both run 11 hours a day for 30 days. Find the units saved in a month and the bill saved at Rs 6 per unit.
12. In a hobby-motor test with a 1.5 V cell, the shaft turns clockwise as seen from the front. What happens when (a) the cell is reversed, (b) the cell is reversed and the magnet is turned around? (c) Why must the cell be removed as soon as the shaft is held?

### Answers

1. **Given:** V = 12 V, I = 3.4 A, m = 24 kg, h = 2.3 m, t = 18 s. **Formula:** P_in = V I; P_out = m g h ÷ t. **Substitution:** P_in = 12 V × 3.4 A = **40.8 W**. Kaam = 24 kg × 10 m/s² × 2.3 m = 552 J; P_out = 552 J ÷ 18 s = **30.67 W**. Efficiency = (30.67 ÷ 40.8) × 100 = **75.2 percent**. Bekaar = 40.8 W − 30.67 W = **10.13 W**. **Jaanch:** 0.752 × 40.8 W = 30.67 W ✅

2. **Given:** P_in = 750 W, m = 5100 kg, h = 9.5 m, t = 25 min = 1500 s. **Substitution:** Kaam = 5100 kg × 10 m/s² × 9.5 m = 484 500 J; P_out = 484 500 J ÷ 1500 s = **323 W**. Efficiency = (323 ÷ 750) × 100 = **43.1 percent**. Kho gayi = 750 W − 323 W = **427 W**. Ek ghante (60 min) mein: 5100 kg × (60 ÷ 25) = **12 240 kg**. **Jaanch:** 323 W × 1500 s = 484 500 J ✅

3. **Given:** V = 36 V, R = 2.1 Ω, I_chalti = 1.9 A. (a) Ruki: I = V ÷ R = 36 V ÷ 2.1 Ω = **17.14 A**. (b) Ruki: P = V² ÷ R = 1296 V² ÷ 2.1 Ω = **617.1 W**. Chalti: P = (1.9 A)² × 2.1 Ω = 3.61 A² × 2.1 Ω = **7.58 W**. (c) 617.1 W ÷ 7.58 W = **81.4 guna**. **Jaanch:** (17.14 A)² × 2.1 Ω = 293.8 A² × 2.1 Ω = 617.0 W ✅; current ka anupaat 17.14 ÷ 1.9 = 9.02 aur (9.02)² = 81.4 ✅. Ruki motor mein current 9 guna aur garmi 81 guna.

4. **Formula:** Turning effect ∝ pher × current × area. **Substitution:** pher: 90 ÷ 150 = 0.6; current: 3.0 A ÷ 2.4 A = 1.25; area: 39 cm² ÷ 26 cm² = 1.5. Guna = 0.6 × 1.25 × 1.5 = **1.125**. Original ke liye: 0.6 × 1.5 × (I ÷ 2.4 A) = 1, to I = 2.4 A ÷ 0.9 = **2.667 A**. **Jaanch:** 90 × 2.667 × 39 = 9360 aur 150 × 2.4 × 26 = 9360 ✅

5. Rule: left hand; forefinger = field, middle finger = current, thumb = force. (a) Left bhuja ⊗ aur field →: thumb ↓. Right bhuja ⊙: ↑. Left neeche, right upar: **anticlockwise**. (b) Left ⊙ aur field ←: ↓. Right ⊗: ↑. **Anticlockwise**. (c) Field ↑, left ⊙: thumb ←; right ⊗: →. Force dono bhujaon par axle se door, coil ki apni line mein: coil **khinchti hai, ghoomti nahi** (dead position). (d) Field ↓, left ⊙: thumb →; right ⊗: ←. Force axle ki taraf, coil ki line mein: **ghoomti nahi** (dabti hai). **Jaanch:** (a) mein Figure 2.1 se field → wahi, current ulta: ghumna ulta (Figure 2.1 clockwise, to anticlockwise) ✅. (b) mein field ulta, current Figure 2.1 wala: ghumna ulta, anticlockwise ✅. (c) aur (d) mein dono force coil ki apni line mein hain (axle se door ya axle ki taraf), kisi ka axle ke gird ghumane wala asar nahi, to turning effect zero ✅. Ye wahi dead position hai jo Figure 2.2(a) mein field → ke saath khadi coil ki thi.
6. 1740 ÷ 60 = 29 chakkar per second. Har chakkar mein 2 baar: 2 × 29 = **58 baar per second**. Ek minute: 2 × 1740 = **3480 baar**. **Jaanch:** 58 × 60 = 3480 ✅

7. (i) Coil leta hua se khadi position (dead position) tak ghoomti hai: force coil ki line mein, turning effect zero. (ii) Thodi aage nikli to, current wahi rahe to, force use wapas khinchte hain. (iii) Isliye coil dolti hui ruk jaati hai. (iv) Commutator khadi position paar hote hi current ulta kar deta hai, to force ab aage ghumate hain. Akeli coil mein har chakkar mein do baar turning effect zero se gujarta hai; bahut si coils alag-alag kone par hon to kisi coil ka turning effect hamesha bada rehta hai, to motor ek-sa ghoomti hai.

8. Ruki motor: ghumne wala ulta asar nahi banta, to current sirf V ÷ R ban jaata hai, jo chalte waqt se bahut zyada hota hai. Garmi I² R hai, to bahut tezi se badhti hai aur winding jal sakti hai. Bachche ko **turant cell hata dena chahiye** aur shaft chhod dena chahiye. **Jaanch:** chalti motor mein current kam, to garmi (I²) bahut kam; ruki par kai guna, jaise Question 3 mein 81 guna ✅

9. (a) **False** ❌: brushes sthir hain; coil aur ring ghoomte hain. (b) **True** ✅: chalti motor ka ulta asar current ghatata hai. (c) **False** ❌: do cheezein ulti, to force wahi; ghumna wahi rehta hai. (d) **False** ❌: pankhe ki induction motor aur brushless motor mein split ring nahi hota. (e) **False** ❌: output input se kam hi hota hai; energy banti nahi.

10. **Given:** V = 28 V, I = 2.9 A, efficiency = 64 percent = 0.64, m = 17 kg, h = 6.2 m. **Substitution:** Upyogi kaam = 17 kg × 10 m/s² × 6.2 m = 1054 J. Bijli energy = 1054 J ÷ 0.64 = **1647 J**. P_in = 28 V × 2.9 A = 81.2 W. Time = 1646.9 J ÷ 81.2 W = **20.28 s** (lagbhag 20.3 s). **Jaanch:** 81.2 W × 20.28 s = 1647 J ✅; 1054 ÷ 1647 = 0.64 ✅

11. Units bachat per din = (74 W − 33 W) × 11 h = 41 W × 11 h = 451 Wh = 0.451 kWh. 30 din mein: 0.451 × 30 = **13.53 units**. Bachat = 13.53 × Rs 6 = **Rs 81.18**. **Jaanch:** 74 W × 11 h × 30 = 24.42 kWh, 33 W × 11 h × 30 = 10.89 kWh; 24.42 − 10.89 = 13.53 kWh ✅

12. (a) Cell ulta karne se current ulta, to force ulta, to shaft **anticlockwise** ghoomega. (b) Cell ulta (force ulta) aur magnet ulta (force phir ulta): do ulte, to shaft wahi **clockwise** ghoomega. (c) Ruki motor mein current bahut zyada aur coil mein I² R garmi, to motor aur cell garm ho sakti hai. **Jaanch:** (b) mein do ulte milkar seedha ✅ (Class 9 ka "dono ulta, to force wahi").
