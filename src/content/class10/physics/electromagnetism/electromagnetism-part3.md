# Topic 3: Induction Aur Lenz Ka Niyam: Current Kaise Banti Hai Aur Kyun Rukti Hai

Topic 1 aur Topic 2 mein humne current se **force** banaya: magnet ke beech rakha current wala taar dhakka khata hai, aur isi se motor ghoomti hai. Ab hum ulta raasta pakadte hain: agar current se halchal milti hai, to **halchal se current** kaise milta hai? Class 9 mein Faraday ka prayog dekha tha: magnet hilao to coil mein current bana. Aaj hum usse do kadam aage jaayenge: current ki **disha** kaise nikalein, aur wo current ek ajeeb si **rukawat** kyun paida karta hai.

## Part A: Chalte taar mein current: Fleming ka right-hand rule

**Kyun banti hai?** Taar ke andar ke chhote charge taar ke saath hi field mein chalte hain. Topic 1 ka wahi force un par bhi lagta hai (chalte charge par field ka dhakka), aur wo unhe taar ki lambai ke saath dhakelta hai. Yahi dhakka current hai.

**Rule.** Right hand ka **thumb, forefinger aur middle finger** ek doosre se 90° par kholo:

- **Thumb** = taar ki **halchal (motion)** ki disha
- **Forefinger** = **field** ki disha (N se S)
- **Middle finger** = **induced current** ki disha

```
   [N]  ──────────────────────────────>  [S]
                  forefinger: field, baayen se daayen
                       ↑
                       :   thumb: taar upar ki taraf chal raha hai
                       :
                       ⊗   taar ka kata hua sira: current page ke andar
                           (middle finger)
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Right-hand rule. Field N se S (daayen), taar upar ki taraf chal raha hai: right hand ka forefinger daayen, thumb upar, to middle finger page ke andar (⊗). Taar page ke aar-paar hai, isliye uska kata hua sira dikhta hai.

**Left aur right: kaun sa kab?** Dono rule **ek hi niyam** ko do taraf se dekhte hain: taar, field aur current ke beech ek hi rishta. Bas pooch alag hai.

| Baat | Left-hand rule | Right-hand rule |
|---|---|---|
| Machine | Motor | Generator |
| Pehle se diya hua | Current aur field | Halchal aur field |
| Jo nikalna hai | Force (halchal) | Induced current |
| Thumb | Force | Halchal |
| Forefinger | Field | Field |
| Middle finger | Current | Induced current |

**Yaad rakhne ka tareeka:** forefinger **hamesha field**. Baaki do ungliyan, thumb aur middle, aapas mein jagah badal leti hain: jo cheez aap **dete** ho wo ek ungli, jo **milti** hai wo doosri. Motor mein current deke force lete ho (middle se thumb). Generator mein halchal deke current lete ho (thumb se middle). Motor ke saath **left**, generator ke saath **right**.

### Example 1: Right-hand rule ke chhah cases

Convention Class 9 wala: page par right →, left ←, up ↑, down ↓, page ke andar ⊗, page se bahar ⊙. Case (d) se (f) mein compass ki directions hain: North, East, West, South, aur seedha Up ya Down (jaise naksha table par rakha ho aur upar aasmaan ho).

**Given:** taar ki halchal aur field di hai. **Find:** induced current ki disha. **Formula:** right-hand rule (thumb halchal, forefinger field, middle finger current).

| Case | Halchal | Field | Middle finger (current) |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) | right → | page ke andar ⊗ | **up ↑** |
| (b) | up ↑ | page se bahar ⊙ | **right →** |
| (c) | left ← | up ↑ | **page ke andar ⊗** |
| (d) | East | Down | **North** |
| (e) | Up | South | **East** |
| (f) | North | West | **Up** |

**Jaanch (doosre tareeke se):** induced current ko ab left-hand rule se parkho. Taar mein ab current hai aur field hai, to Topic 1 ke hisaab se force lagega, aur wo force **halchal ke ulta** nikalna chahiye. (a): current ↑, field ⊗: force **left ←**, halchal right → ke ulta ✅. (b): current →, field ⊙: force **down ↓**, halchal up ↑ ke ulta ✅. (c): current ⊗, field ↑: force **right →**, halchal left ← ke ulta ✅. (d): current North, field Down: force **West**, halchal East ke ulta ✅. (e): current East, field South: force **Down**, halchal Up ke ulta ✅. (f): current Up, field West: force **South**, halchal North ke ulta ✅. Yani bana hua current taar ki halchal ko **rokta** hai. Ye dekhna Part B ka bahut bada hint hai.

## Part B: Lenz ka niyam: current hamesha rokti hai

Heinrich Lenz ne 1834 mein ye niyam diya. (Right-hand rule ka naam British engineer John Ambrose Fleming ke naam par hai, 1800 ke aakhri varshon ka.)

> **Lenz ka niyam:** Induced current usi disha mein behti hai jo **us badlav ka virodh (oppose)** kare jisse wo bani hai.

Magnet aur coil par dekho. Magnet ka **N pole coil ki taraf** aata hai. Coil mein jo current banegi wo coil ke paas wale sire (near face) ko **N** bana degi, yaani magnet ke N ke saamne coil ka N aa jaata hai aur do N ek doosre ko **dhakelte** hain. Magnet ko paas laana mushkil ho jaata hai. Yahi virodh hai.

```
                          ⊗  ⊗  ⊗
                        ┌─────────┐
   [S ══════ N]  ───>   N         S
     magnet             └─────────┘
                          ⊙  ⊙  ⊙
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Magnet ka N pole coil ki taraf aa raha hai. Coil (side se): upar ke taar mein current page ke andar (⊗), neeche ke taar mein bahar (⊙). Right-hand grip rule: ungliyan upar andar ki taraf aur neeche bahar ki taraf mudti hain, to angootha **baayen** ko jaata hai: coil ka baayen (near) sira **N**, daayen sira **S**. N, N ko door dhakelta hai.

Magnet ko wapas **kheencho**, to coil ka near face ulta, yaani **S** ban jaata hai aur magnet ko **kheench kar rokne** ki koshish karta hai.

| Magnet | Halchal | Coil ka near face | Asar |
|---|---|---|---|
| N pole | paas aata | N | Door dhakelta hai |
| N pole | door jaata | S | Wapas kheenchta hai |
| S pole | paas aata | S | Door dhakelta hai |
| S pole | door jaata | N | Wapas kheenchta hai |

Har case mein coil magnet ki halchal ka **virodh** karta hai. Near face se dekhne par **N face par current anticlockwise** aur **S face par clockwise** dikhta hai (Class 9 ka clock rule).

### Example 2: Lenz ke chhah cases

**Find:** coil ka near face kaun sa pole banta hai, aur current ki disha. **Formula:** Lenz ka niyam (virodh) aur clock rule.

| Case | Kya ho raha hai | Near face | Current (near face se dekhne par) |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) | Magnet ka N pole coil ki taraf aa raha hai | N | anticlockwise |
| (b) | Magnet ka N pole coil se door ja raha hai | S | clockwise |
| (c) | Magnet ka S pole coil ki taraf aa raha hai | S | clockwise |
| (d) | Magnet ka S pole coil se door ja raha hai | N | anticlockwise |
| (e) | Magnet sthir hai, coil us ke S pole ki taraf chadhai ja rahi hai | S | clockwise |
| (f) | Magnet coil ke daayen taraf hai aur uska S pole coil ki taraf aa raha hai. Current ko coil ke **baayen** sire se dekho | Daayen (near) face S, baayen (far) face N | baayen sire se anticlockwise (daayen sire se clockwise) |

**Jaanch:** (f) mein daayen (near) face S hai, wahan se current clockwise dikhti hai. Baayen face se dekhne par wahi current ulti dikhti hai, to anticlockwise, aur baayen face N hai: N face par anticlockwise ✅. Case (a) aur (b) ko dekho: sirf halchal ulti hui, aur near face aur current dono ulte ho gaye ✅. Case (e) mein sirf relative motion ki zaroorat thi, isliye coil chalane se bhi wahi asar mila ✅.

### Ye virodh kyun? Ulta ho jaaye to?

Maan lo ulta hota: N pole paas aaya aur coil ka near face **S** ban gaya. Tab coil magnet ko **kheench** leti. Magnet aur tez aata, current aur badhti, kheench aur zor se hoti. Ek halka sa dhakka lagakar bina mehnat ke bijli aur gati dono badhti rehti, **free energy**. Aisa kabhi nahi hota. Isliye virodh hi sahi hai, aur ye energy conservation ka hi seedha asar hai (Part C).

### Taambe ke pipe mein girta magnet (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo)

Ek lamba **taambe (ya aluminium) ka pipe** seedha pakdo (lohe ka nahi). Uske upar se ek chhota tagda magnet girao. Wo pipe ke andar **bahut dheere dheere** girta hai: lagbhag kuch second mein, jabki plastic ke pipe mein ya hawa mein wo ek second se bhi kam mein gir jaata. Kaaran: girta magnet pipe ke chhallon mein gol current banata hai, aur Lenz ke niyam ke mutabiq wo current magnet ki girne ki halchal ka virodh karte hain. Magnet ke **neeche** ka chhalla use upar dhakelta hai, aur magnet ke **upar** ka chhalla use upar kheenchta hai. Magnet ki girne wali energy ka lagbhag sab hissa pipe ki halki garmi mein chala jaata hai, magnet ki gati bahut kam badhti hai.

```
              ┌──────────────┐
              │              │
              │   ┌──────┐   │       ↓  gravity ka force (neeche)
              │   │  S   │   │
              │   │  N   │   │       ↑  induced current ka force
              │   └──────┘   │          (upar, girne ka virodh)
              │              │
              └──────────────┘
                taambe ka pipe
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Taambe ke pipe mein girta magnet (N neeche): neeche ka chhalla N ko dhakelta hai, upar ka chhalla S ko kheenchta hai. Dono force **upar** ki taraf hain, to magnet dheere girta hai.

> **Teacher ke liye:** pipe ke saath hamesha ek plastic ya kaagaz ka pipe aur wahi size ka bina-magnet wala dummy rakhein taaki bachche fark dekh sakein. Magnet bahut tagda ho to bachchon ko door rakhein aur magnet girne se pehle neeche ek mulaayam gaddi rakhein.

## Part C: Energy ka hisaab: dynamo bhaari kyun lagti hai

Ab Sanskruti ka sawaal. Lamp **band** tha to circuit khula tha: dynamo mein emf to bana (taar ka voltage), par **current nahi** chali. Current nahi, to Lenz ka virodh nahi. Tab pedal halka tha (sirf bearing aur tyre ka ragad).

Lamp **on** kiya to circuit band hua aur current chali. Ab dynamo ke coil ki current par magnet ke field ka force lagta hai (Topic 1 wala force, left-hand rule). Part A ke Jaanch mein dekha ki ye force halchal ke **ulta** hota hai. Isliye pahiya ko ghumana **mushkil** ho gaya. Wo extra mehnat, jo Sanskruti ki taangon ne ki, hi light ki bijli bani. Is liye:

> **Mechanical work jo tum lagate ho = bijli ki energy + bachi hui garmi aur ragad.** Energy na banti hai na khatam hoti hai, sirf roop badalti hai. Zyada bulb jodo to zyada current, zyada virodh, zyada mehnat.

| Generator | Circuit | Current | Ghumana |
|---|---|---|---|
| Khula | Bulb ka switch off | Nahi | Halka |
| Band | Bulb on | Hai | Bhaari |
| Band, zyada bulb | Zyada bulb on | Zyada | Aur bhaari |

**Motor ke saath bhi yahi.** Topic 2 mein dekha tha ki chalti motor ruki hui motor se kam current leti hai. Ab uska sahi naam aur kaaran saaf hai: ghoomti coil khud field ko kaat-ti hai, to usme bhi induced emf banta hai, aur Lenz ke niyam se wo supply ke current ka **virodh** karta hai. Shaft ruk jaaye to ye virodh nahi bachta, aur current bahut badh jaata hai. (Hisaab aage ki class mein; motor aur generator ka ye rishta Topic 6 mein dobara.)

### Example 3: Hand-crank torch ka energy hisaab

Hand-crank torch ko 54 s tak ghumane mein haath ne 264 J ka kaam kiya. Torch ke generator se 2.2 W ki bijli us poore samay (54 s) mili. Efficiency kitni? Kitni energy ragad aur garmi mein gayi? Haath ki average power?

```
  haath ki mehnat      generator          bijli ki energy         LED
    (264 J)  ───────>  [ coil + magnet ] ───────> (118.8 J) ────> roshni aur garmi
                            :
                            v
                    ragad aur coil ki garmi: 145.2 J
```

> **Figure 3.4** – Hand-crank torch ka energy chain. Haath ki 264 J mehnat ka kuch hissa bijli banta hai, baaki ragad aur coil ke taar ki garmi mein jaata hai.

- **Given:** haath ka kaam = 264 J, power = 2.2 W, time = 54 s
- **Find:** electrical energy, efficiency, bachi energy, haath ki power
- **Formula:** E = P × t; efficiency = (output ÷ input) × 100 percent; haath ki power = kaam ÷ time
- **Substitution:** E = 2.2 W × 54 s = **118.8 J**. Efficiency = (118.8 J ÷ 264 J) × 100 = **45 percent**. Bachi = 264 J − 118.8 J = **145.2 J**. Haath ki power = 264 J ÷ 54 s = **lagbhag 4.9 W**.

**Jaanch:** 0.45 × 264 J = 118.8 J ✅; 118.8 J + 145.2 J = 264 J ✅; energy bachi hai kisi ne banai nahi ✅. Efficiency 100 percent se kam, jaise Class 9 ([machines-part2.md](../../../class9/physics/machines/machines-part2.md)) aur [current-electricity-ii-part5.md](../current-electricity-ii/current-electricity-ii-part5.md) mein dekha: kabhi 100 percent nahi.

### Safe activity: coil, magnet aur meter (school mein teacher ke saath)

**Samaan:** taar ki coil (kai pher), centre-zero galvanometer ya multimeter ki sabse chhoti current/mV range, ek school ka bar magnet. Coil ko meter se jodo. Magnet ko coil mein tezi se dhakelo aur nikalo.

Dekho: andar dhakelne par needle ek taraf, bahar nikalne par doosri taraf (Lenz ke near face ki wajah se). Magnet sthir to needle zero. **Dhyan:** haath se hilaye magnet se bani bijli bahut kam hoti hai, to LED seedhi nahi jalegi (ek LED ko chahiye kuch volt). Strong neodymium magnet bachchon ko nahi dena. **Kabhi ghar ka socket nahi.**

### Safe activity: cycle ka dynamo (ghar par adult ke saath)

Cycle stand par ho, lamp ke switch se on-off karke, wahi dhakka dekar pahiya ghumao, aur dekho ki kaun sa zyada der chalta hai. Haath kabhi chain, spoke ya dynamo ke roller ke paas na jaaye. Dynamo ya lamp **kholna nahi**.

## Part D: Emf kitna bada banega?

Faraday ke teen factor ab ek chhote sutra mein: **induced emf e ∝ N × (field badalne ki rate)**. N = coil ke pher. Rate zyada hogi jab magnet tez chale ya zyada tagda ho.

- Pher double to emf **double** (rate wahi).
- Magnet ko aadhe time mein andar dhakela to rate **double**, to emf **double**.
- Dono saath mein badlen to **guna** karte jaao.

### Example 4: Ratio se emf

Ek coil (200 pher) mein magnet 30 cm/s se dhakelne par meter 2.5 mV dikhata hai. Ab 280 pher ki coil aur magnet 45 cm/s se. Naya emf?

- **Given:** e₁ = 2.5 mV; N: 200 → 280; chaal: 30 cm/s → 45 cm/s
- **Find:** e₂
- **Formula:** e ∝ N × rate; rate chaal ke barabar badhti hai
- **Substitution:** pher ka ratio = 280 ÷ 200 = 1.4; chaal ka ratio = 45 ÷ 30 = 1.5. e₂ = 2.5 mV × 1.4 × 1.5 = **5.25 mV**.

**Jaanch:** 5.25 ÷ 2.5 = 2.1 aur 1.4 × 1.5 = 2.1 ✅. Units mV: dono taraf mV ✅.

## Part E: Eddy current aur uske kaam

Taambe ke pipe mein current kisi taar mein nahi thi: pipe ke **moti dhaatu ke andar** hi gol current ban gayi. Aisi gol, bina taar ki current ko **eddy current** kehte hain. Jahan bhi motey dhaatu ke paas field badalta hai, eddy current banti hai, aur Lenz ke niyam se wo badlav ka virodh karti hai.

- **Induction cooktop:** choolhe ke neeche coil mein tez badalta current chalta hai. Upar rakhe **lohe ya steel ke bartan** (aam taur par jinhe magnet chipakta hai) ke andar eddy current banti hai aur bartan hi garm ho jaata hai. Choolha aur kaanch khud seedhe garm nahi hote, bas bartan ki garmi se halke garm hote hain.
- **Magnetic braking:** kuch trains aur rides mein magnet dhaatu ki plate ya patri ke paas laya jaata hai. Eddy current halchal ka virodh karti hai aur gaadi **bina ragad** ke dheemi hoti hai.
- **Transformer ka core:** wahan eddy current ki garmi nuksaan hai. Isiliye core ko patli parton (laminations) se banate hain. Ye Topic 5 mein.
- **Regenerative braking:** metro aur electric gaadi ke rukte waqt motor ko generator bana diya jaata hai. Induced current gaadi ki halchal ka virodh karti hai (brake lagta hai) aur gaadi ki movement energy wapas bijli ban jaati hai. Ragad wale brake saath mein rehte hain.
- **Microphone aur card reader:** microphone mein awaaz se ek coil ya diaphragm hilta hai aur chhota induced voltage banta hai. Card reader mein card ki magnetic patti coil ke paas se guzarti hai. (Dono sirf naam.) Isi liye magnet card ko kharab bhi kar sakta hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Induced current to badlav ki madad karti hai."**
Nahi, wo virodh karti hai. Demonstration: ek N pole coil ki taraf laa rahe ho. Agar near face S banta, to coil magnet ko apni taraf kheench leti. Tab tumhe bas ek halka dhakka dena padta, aur magnet apne aap tez hoti hui andar aata, current aur badhti, kheench aur zor se hoti. Tum bina mehnat ke bijli bana lete. Aisa kabhi nahi hota: asal mein N face hi banta hai aur tumhe andar dhakelne ke liye **kaam** karna padta hai. Jis din ye "madad" mile, us din energy ban jaayegi, jo nahi banti.

**Galti 2: "Sthir magnet coil ke andar ho to bhi current banti hai."**
Nahi, field **badalna** zaroori hai. Demonstration: ek bar magnet ko coil ke andar tape se jod do, aur dono ko ek tray par rakh kar poore kamre mein ghuma lo. Magnet aur coil saath chal rahe hain, unke beech relative motion **zero** hai, to meter ki needle zero par hi rehti hai. Ab wahi tray rok kar magnet ko coil ke andar se ek jhatke se nikaalo: needle hil jaati hai. Fark sirf ek hai: magnet aur coil ke beech relative motion hui.

## Quick Recap

- **Fleming ka right-hand rule:** thumb halchal, forefinger field, middle finger induced current. Left-hand rule motor ke liye, right-hand rule generator ke liye; forefinger hamesha field.
- **Lenz ka niyam:** induced current uske paida karne wale badlav ka **virodh** karti hai. Magnet paas aaye to near face same pole (N ke saamne N), door jaaye to ulta pole.
- Virodh ka matlab: energy conservation. Jo kaam tum lagate ho wahi bijli (aur garmi) banta hai.
- Generator bulb jalate waqt bhaari kyun lagta hai: current par field ka force halchal ke ulta lagta hai. Circuit khula to current nahi, virodh nahi.
- **e ∝ N × rate of change**: pher, tezi aur field badhao to emf badhta hai.
- **Eddy current:** motey dhaatu mein gol current. Induction cooktop, magnetic braking, regenerative braking, microphone aur card reader. Transformer core mein nuksaan: Topic 5.
- Sab activity kam voltage par, mains nahi.

7. A straight wire moves West and the induced current in it flows South. Find the direction of the field (one of the six compass directions: North, South, East, West, Up or Down).

8. Using Lenz's law, state which pole each coil face becomes and the sense of the current as seen from that face.
   (a) A magnet's S pole is moving away from a coil. The magnet lies to the right of the coil. Look from the right.
   (b) A coil moves away from the stationary N pole of a magnet. The magnet lies to the right of the coil. Look from the right.
   (c) A magnet is dropped with its S pole pointing down towards a flat ring lying on a table. Look from above, as the magnet approaches the ring.
   (d) The same magnet has now fallen through and is below the ring, moving away downward. Which pole is the bottom face of the ring?

9. A coil of some turns gives 1.8 mV when a magnet is moved into it. If the magnet is moved twice as fast and the number of turns is tripled, what will the meter read?

10. A coil with 360 turns gives 3.2 mV. The coil is replaced by one with 270 turns and the magnet is moved at 60 cm/s instead of 24 cm/s. Find the new reading.

11. A hand-crank torch lights an LED for 75 s with a power of 1.6 W. The hand does 320 J of work. Find (a) the electrical energy, (b) the efficiency, (c) the energy wasted. (d) For another run the electrical energy is 84 J at the same efficiency: how much work must the hand do?

12. A magnet loses 0.40 J of potential energy while falling 1.0 m through a copper pipe in 5.2 s. In a plastic pipe it takes 0.47 s. Find the average speeds in both pipes. Where does this lost energy go in the copper pipe?

13. A 1260 kg electric car at 72 km/h brakes to a stop using regenerative braking. Find its kinetic energy. If 28 percent is returned as electrical energy, how many joules is that?

14. State true or false and give a reason: (a) Fleming's left-hand rule gives the induced current in a generator. (b) A lamp on a dynamo is easier to pedal when the lamp is switched off. (c) An induced current can help a magnet moving towards a coil. (d) Moving a coil towards a still magnet can induce a current.

### Answers

1. Given: thumb (halchal) down, forefinger (field) left. Find: middle finger. Right hand se: **page ke andar ⊗**. Jaanch: current ⊗ aur field ← par left hand ka force **up ↑**, halchal (down) ke ulta ✅.

2. Thumb ⊙ (bahar), forefinger →: middle finger **up ↑**. Jaanch: current ↑, field →: left hand ka force **⊗ (andar)**, halchal ⊙ ke ulta ✅.

3. Thumb →, forefinger ⊙: middle finger **down ↓**. Jaanch: current ↓, field ⊙: left hand ka force **left ←**, halchal → ke ulta ✅.

4. Thumb West, forefinger Up: middle finger **North**. Jaanch: current North, field Up: force **East**, halchal West ke ulta ✅.

5. Thumb Down, forefinger East: middle finger **South**. Jaanch: current South, field East: force **Up**, halchal Down ke ulta ✅.

6. Thumb South, forefinger Down: middle finger **East**. Jaanch: current East, field Down: force **North**, halchal South ke ulta ✅.

7. Thumb West, middle finger South. Forefinger **Down** (seedha neeche). Jaanch: thumb West aur forefinger Down dene par right hand ka middle finger **South** aata hai ✅. Left-hand se: current South, field Down: force **East**, halchal West ke ulta ✅.

8. (a) S pole door ja raha hai, to near face **N** (wapas kheenchne ke liye). Right se dekhne par **anticlockwise**. (b) Coil door ja rahi hai N pole se, to near (daayen) face **S**. Right se dekhne par **clockwise**. (c) S pole paas aa raha hai, to ring ka upar wala face **S**, upar se dekhne par **clockwise**. (d) Ab magnet ka upar wala N sira door ja raha hai, to ring ka neeche wala face **S** banta hai (N sira door jaane par near face S). Jaanch: har case mein face ka pole aur current ka sense clock rule se milaya: N par anticlockwise, S par clockwise ✅.

9. Given: e₁ = 1.8 mV, chaal ×2, pher ×3. Formula: e ∝ N × rate. e₂ = 1.8 mV × 2 × 3 = **10.8 mV**. Jaanch: 10.8 ÷ 1.8 = 6 ✅.

10. Given: e₁ = 3.2 mV; N: 360 → 270; chaal 24 → 60 cm/s. Pher ka ratio = 270 ÷ 360 = 0.75; chaal ka ratio = 60 ÷ 24 = 2.5. e₂ = 3.2 mV × 0.75 × 2.5 = **6.0 mV**. Jaanch: 0.75 × 2.5 = 1.875 aur 6.0 ÷ 3.2 = 1.875 ✅.

11. (a) E = P × t = 1.6 W × 75 s = **120 J**. (b) Efficiency = (120 J ÷ 320 J) × 100 = **37.5 percent**. (c) Bachi = 320 J − 120 J = **200 J** (ragad aur garmi). (d) Input = 84 J ÷ 0.375 = **224 J**. Jaanch: 0.375 × 320 J = 120 J ✅; 120 + 200 = 320 ✅; 0.375 × 224 = 84 ✅.

12. Copper pipe: v = 1.0 m ÷ 5.2 s = **lagbhag 0.19 m/s**. Plastic: v = 1.0 m ÷ 0.47 s = **lagbhag 2.1 m/s**. Plastic mein lagbhag 11 guna tez. Copper mein magnet ki girne wali 0.40 J energy induced (eddy) current ke zariye **pipe ki garmi** mein jaati hai, kyunki magnet ki gati bahut kam badhti hai. Jaanch: 0.19 × 5.2 = 1.0 ✅; 2.1 × 0.47 = 1.0 ✅.

13. Speed = 72 km/h = 72 ÷ 3.6 = 20 m/s. KE = ½ × m × v² = 0.5 × 1260 kg × (20 m/s)² = **252 000 J**. Wapas mili = 0.28 × 252 000 J = **70 560 J**. Jaanch: 70 560 ÷ 252 000 = 0.28 ✅. Baaki 181 440 J ragad aur garmi mein gayi.

14. (a) **False** ❌: induced current ke liye right-hand rule; left-hand motor ke force ke liye. (b) **True** ✅: switch off, circuit khula, current nahi, Lenz ka virodh nahi. (c) **False** ❌: virodh karti hai, madad karne par free energy ban jaati. (d) **True** ✅: sirf relative motion matter karti hai.
