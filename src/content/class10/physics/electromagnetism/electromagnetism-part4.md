# Topic 4: AC Generator Aur AC Vs DC: Bijli Ka Safar Ek Chakkar Mein

Topic 3 mein dekha ki jab taar ya coil magnetic field ko kaat-ti hai to current banta hai, uski disha Fleming ke right-hand rule se milti hai, aur Lenz ka niyam batata hai ki wo current hamesha badlav ka **virodh** karta hai. Ab wo saari baatein ek saath ek machine mein dekhte hain: **generator**. Class 9 mein iska chitra dekha tha. Aaj hum coil ko ghoomte hue **ek-ek position par rok kar** dekhenge: kab voltage zero, kab sabse zyada, aur kab ulta. Phir ise ginti se jodenge ki ghar ka 50 Hz kaise banta hai.

## Part A: Coil ki chaar positions: voltage kab zero, kab zyada

Generator ki coil mein voltage tab banta hai jab uski bhujayein field ki **lakeeron ko kaat-ti hain**. Agar kisi bhuja ki chaal field ke **saath ya ulta** (usi line mein) ho, to wo koi lakeer nahi kaat-ti: voltage zero. Agar chaal field ke **aar-paar** (90 deg par) ho, to wo sabse zyada lakeerein kaat-ti hai: voltage sabse zyada. Beech ki sthitiyon mein beech ka asar.

Chitra mein coil ko **axle ki taraf se** (axle page se bahar, tumhari taraf) dekha gaya hai, isliye har bhuja ek bindu jaisi hai. Dono bhujayein P aur Q naam se pehchani jaati hain. Coil **anticlockwise** ghoom rahi hai. Field sab chitron mein **baayen se daayen (N se S)** hai.

```
    0 deg                90 deg               180 deg              270 deg
    <-- P                                     <-- Q
        :                                         :
        :                         ↑               :                         ↑
        :              P==========Q               :              Q==========P
        :              ↓                          :              ↓
        Q -->                                     P -->

   e = 0                e = max              e = 0                e = max
   P: 0  Q: 0         P: ⊙  Q: ⊗             P: 0  Q: 0         P: ⊗  Q: ⊙

```

> **Figure 4.1** – Generator ki coil ki chaar positions (axle page se bahar ki taraf, coil anticlockwise, field baayen se daayen). 0 deg par bhujayein upar aur neeche hain aur field ki line mein chalti hain (upar wali baayen, neeche wali daayen): e = 0. 90 deg par bhujayein baayen aur daayen hain, P neeche aur Q upar jaati hai, field ko seedha kaat-ti hain: e = max, P mein ⊙ aur Q mein ⊗. 180 deg par phir e = 0. 270 deg par phir e = max, par ab P daayen taraf hai aur upar jaati hai: P mein ⊗ aur Q mein ⊙. Yaani P ka current 90 deg par ⊙ tha, 270 deg par ⊗ ho gaya: **ulta**.

Ye dekho: 90 deg aur 270 deg par daayen taraf ki bhuja **upar** ja rahi hai aur field wahi hai, isliye space mein current ki disha wahi (⊗) hai. Par daayen taraf pehle Q thi, ab P hai. Yaani coil ke **apne** hisaab se current ulta ho gaya, aur slip rings se hokar bahar ke circuit mein bhi current **ulta** aata hai. Yahi AC ka raaz hai.

| Chakkar ka hissa | Bhujayein kaise chalti hain | Voltage | Bahar ka current |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 deg (neutral position) | field ki line mein | zero | zero |
| 0 se 90 deg | field ko zyada se zyada kaat-ti | zero se peak tak badhta | ek disha (maan lo +) |
| 90 deg | field ke aar-paar | peak value | ek disha (+) |
| 90 se 180 deg | kaatna kam hota jaata | peak se zero tak ghatta | ek disha (+) |
| 180 deg (neutral position) | field ki line mein | zero | zero |
| 180 se 270 deg | phir kaat-ti, par ulta | zero se peak tak badhta | **ulti** disha (-) |
| 270 deg | field ke aar-paar | peak value | ulti disha (-) |
| 270 se 360 deg | kaatna kam hota jaata | peak se zero | ulti disha (-) |

Ek poora chakkar (360 deg) = **ek cycle**: pehla aadha chakkar ek disha, doosra aadha ulti disha. Isliye ek chakkar mein **do baar zero**, **do baar peak** (ek +, ek -), aur disha **2 baar ulti**.

### Example 1: 90 deg par current ki disha: do tareeke se

**Given:** Figure 4.1 jaisa generator. Field baayen se daayen (→). Coil anticlockwise (axle page se bahar). 90 deg par bhuja P baayen taraf hai aur neeche (↓) ja rahi hai.
**Find:** Bhuja P mein induced current ki disha (⊙ ya ⊗), right-hand rule se; phir left-hand rule se re-check.
**Formula:** Fleming ka right-hand rule: thumb = motion, forefinger = field, middle finger = induced current. Fleming ka left-hand rule: forefinger = field, middle finger = current, thumb = force.
**Substitution:** Right hand lo. Thumb neeche (↓, P ki chaal), forefinger daayen (→, field). Beech wali ungli ab page se **bahar** (tumhari taraf) aati hai.
**Answer:** Bhuja P mein current **⊙ (page se bahar)**. Bhuja Q ki chaal ulti hai, to Q mein ⊗; dono bhujayein sire par jud kar loop banati hain: ek taraf bahar, doosri taraf andar.
**Jaanch:** Ab wahi current left hand se: forefinger daayen (→, field), middle finger page se bahar (⊙, current): thumb **upar** (↑) jaata hai, yaani P par force upar. Par P neeche ja rahi hai, to force chaal ka **virodh** kar raha hai: Lenz ke niyam (Topic 3) se bilkul sahi, coil ko ghumane mein zor lagana padta hai ✅.

## Part B: Ek cycle ka graph

Ab voltage ko samay ke saath chitra mein rakhte hain. Ek poore chakkar mein voltage zero se shuru, peak (+), zero, peak (-), aur wapas zero: ek smooth lehar. Iski shape ko "sin ki shape" kehte hain (Class 10 maths ka sin): ye wahi curve hai jo coil ke 0 deg se 360 deg ghoomne par banta hai.

Chitra mein ghar ki bijli ke liye ek cycle dikhaya hai: peak lagbhag 325 V aur 50 Hz, yaani ek cycle 0.020 s mein.

```
+325 V                 *  *  *
                 *  *           *  *

              *                       *
           *                             *
0 V     *-----------------------------------*-----------------------------------*
                                               *                             *

                                                  *                       *
                                                     *  *           *  *
-325 V                                                     *  *  *
        +                 +                 +                 +                 +
        0                90                180               270               360 deg
        0               0.005             0.010             0.015             0.020 s

```

> **Figure 4.2** – Ek cycle ka voltage-versus-time graph: neeche angle (deg) aur uske neeche 50 Hz ke liye samay (s). 0 aur 180 deg par voltage zero (neutral position), 90 deg par +325 V (lagbhag) aur 270 deg par -325 V (lagbhag). 0 se 180 deg tak pehla half cycle (ek disha), 180 se 360 deg tak doosra half cycle (ulti disha). Poora 0 se 360 deg ek cycle hai.

Isse teen baatein nikalti hain:

1. **Peak tak jaane mein cycle ka chautha hissa lagta hai:** 50 Hz mein 0.020 s ÷ 4 = 0.005 s.
2. **Do half cycle opposite hain:** isliye graph zero ke upar aur neeche dono taraf jaata hai, yaani current ki disha ulti hoti hai.
3. **Value hamesha badalti rehti hai.** Neutral position se 30 deg par voltage peak ka aadha, 60 deg par peak ka lagbhag 0.87 guna, 90 deg par poora peak. Ghar ke socket ke liye: 30 deg par lagbhag 163 V, 60 deg par lagbhag 281 V, 90 deg par lagbhag 325 V.

**Ek honest baat (230 V kahan gaya?):** Socket ke liye hum "230 V" kehte hain, par voltage kisi ek pal ki value nahi hai: wo 0 se lagbhag 325 V tak (aur ulti taraf bhi) badalta rehta hai. 230 V ek **effective value** hai: wo DC voltage jo ek heater ko utni hi garmi de, jitni ye AC deta hai. Peak lagbhag 325 V hai. Is effective value ka poora hisaab aage ki class mein.

### Example 2: Graph padho

**Given:** Ek school generator ke voltage graph mein: voltage t = 0 s par zero, t = 0.0125 s par +6 V (peak), t = 0.025 s par zero, t = 0.0375 s par -6 V (peak), t = 0.05 s par phir zero, aur wahan se ye dohrata hai. Generator do-pole hai.
**Find:** Time period T, frequency f, generator ki rpm, aur kin samay par coil ki bhujayein field ki line mein chal rahi hain.
**Formula:** Ek cycle ka samay = T; f = 1 ÷ T; do-pole generator mein f (Hz) = rpm ÷ 60, yaani rpm = f × 60.
**Substitution:** Cycle t = 0 se t = 0.05 s tak, to T = 0.05 s. f = 1 ÷ 0.05 s = 20 Hz. rpm = 20 × 60 = 1200 rpm. Voltage zero (neutral position) tab: 0 s, 0.025 s aur 0.05 s.
**Answer:** T = 0.05 s, f = 20 Hz, 1200 rpm; bhujayein field ki line mein 0 s, 0.025 s aur 0.05 s par.
**Jaanch:** 0.05 s × 20 = 1 s ✅; 1200 rpm ÷ 60 = 20 chakkar per second, har chakkar ek cycle ✅; peak 0.0125 s par aata hai, jo 0.05 s ka chautha hissa hai ✅.

## Part C: Frequency aur rpm

Do-pole simple generator mein ek chakkar = ek cycle, to
**f (Hz) = rpm ÷ 60** aur **T = 1 ÷ f**.
Frequency aur time period ka rishta Chapter 6 mein dekha tha ([vibrations-and-resonance.md](../vibrations-and-resonance/vibrations-and-resonance.md)); yahan wahi hai, bas "hilna" ki jagah "chakkar" hai.

### Example 3: Hand-crank generator

**Given:** Ek school ka do-pole hand-crank generator 240 rpm par ghoom raha hai.
**Find:** Frequency, time period, 1 second mein current kitni baar disha badalta hai, aur 1 minute mein kitni cycles.
**Formula:** f = rpm ÷ 60; T = 1 ÷ f; ek cycle mein 2 baar disha ulti.
**Substitution:** f = 240 ÷ 60 = 4 Hz. T = 1 ÷ 4 Hz = 0.25 s. Disha ke badlav: 4 × 2 = 8 per second. Cycles in 1 min = 4 Hz × 60 s = 240.
**Answer:** f = 4 Hz, T = 0.25 s, 8 baar disha ulti per second, 240 cycles per minute.
**Jaanch:** 0.25 s × 4 = 1 s ✅; 240 cycles per minute = 240 chakkar per minute = rpm ✅ (do-pole machine mein cycles per minute hamesha rpm ke barabar hote hain).

## Part D: Bijli-ghar ka alternator

Bijli-ghar mein turbine ka shaft alternator ko ghumata hai: paani, bhaap ya hawa se ([sources-of-energy-part3.md](../sources-of-energy/sources-of-energy-part3.md)). Ek honest baat: bade alternator mein aksar **magnet ghoomta hai aur coil sthir rehti hai**, kyunki bahut zyada current ko ghoomte slip rings se nikalna mushkil hai. Voltage tab bhi banta hai, kyunki sirf **relative motion** matter karti hai, jaisa Class 9 mein dekha tha.

India ke grid ko **50 Hz** chahiye ([household-circuits-part5.md](../household-circuits/household-circuits-part5.md)). Do-pole machine ke liye rpm = 50 × 60 = **3000 rpm** (lagbhag; bade thermal bijli-ghar mein aksar aisa hi hota hai). Par har turbine itni tez nahi ghoomti. Paani ki turbine kaafi dheemi hoti hai (kai sau rpm tak, lagbhag), aur hawa ki chakki ka rotor to aur bhi dheema hai (lagbhag 10 se 20 rpm; aksar gearbox se generator ko tez ghumaya jaata hai). Tab **zyada pole** lagaye jaate hain: har **pole pair** ke saamne se guzarne par coil ek cycle deti hai, to zyada pole pair ho to **kam rpm** mein bhi 50 Hz mil jaata hai.

Is idea ka seedha niyam (sirf is topic ki seema mein): **f = (rpm ÷ 60) × pole pairs**. Do-pole machine mein pole pair 1 hai, to purana niyam wapas.

### Example 4: Dheemi turbine, zyada pole

**Given:** Ek kalpanik paani ki turbine ka alternator 20 pole ka hai aur 300 rpm par ghoomta hai. Ek thermal station ka do-pole alternator 3000 rpm par.
**Find:** Dono ki frequency.
**Formula:** f = (rpm ÷ 60) × pole pairs; pole pairs = poles ÷ 2.
**Substitution:** Paani ki machine: pole pairs = 20 ÷ 2 = 10; f = (300 ÷ 60) × 10 = 5 × 10 = 50 Hz. Thermal machine: pole pairs = 2 ÷ 2 = 1; f = (3000 ÷ 60) × 1 = 50 Hz.
**Answer:** Dono 50 Hz. Dheemi machine ne zyada pole se kami poori ki.
**Jaanch:** 300 rpm yaani 5 chakkar per second, har chakkar mein 10 cycles: 5 × 10 = 50 ✅; 3000 ÷ 60 = 50 ✅. Dono machine ek hi grid se saath bandh sakti hain kyunki dono ki frequency 50 Hz hai.

## Part E: 50 Hz par bulb jhapakta kyun nahi

Bulb ki roshni uski **power** ke saath badhti-ghatti hai, aur power voltage ki disha par nirbhar nahi karti: dono half cycle mein bulb utna hi tapta hai. Isliye ek cycle mein roshni **do baar** peak par jaati hai.

```
max                    #  #  #                             #  #  #
                    #           #                       #           #
                                   #                                   #
                 #                                   #
              #                       #           #                       #
0 W     #--#-----------------------------#--#--#-----------------------------#--#
        +                 +                 +                 +                 +
        0               0.005             0.010             0.015             0.020 s

```

> **Figure 4.3** – Lamp ki power ek cycle (0.020 s) mein: do hump, kyunki voltage + ho ya -, power dono mein positive. Zero par roshni sabse kam, hump ke peak par sabse zyada. Ek second mein 50 cycle, to 100 hump.

Kai ghar ke LED lamp mein roshni 1 second mein lagbhag 100 baar badalti hai (lamp ki design par nirbhar; kuch mein ye bahut kam hota hai). Purane filament bulb ka filament garm rehta hai, isliye uska jhapkana aur bhi kam hota hai. Persistence of vision ([human-eye.md](../human-eye/human-eye.md)): aankh mein image lagbhag 1/16 s tikti hai.

### Example 5: Jhapkana dikhega ya nahi

**Given:** (i) 50 Hz par ek LED lamp jo power ke saath roshni badalta hai, har cycle mein 2 baar chamakta hai. (ii) Hand-crank generator 4 Hz par (Example 3) do ulti jude LED chalata hai (ek half cycle mein ek, doosre mein doosri), jo milkar har cycle mein 2 baar chamakti hain. Persistence of vision lagbhag 1/16 s.
**Find:** Dono ka flicker rate aur do chamak ke beech ka gap; kya aankh jhapkana pakdegi?
**Formula:** Flicker rate = 2 × f; gap = 1 ÷ flicker rate; agar gap < 1/16 s to roshni lagatar lagti hai.
**Substitution:** (i) Rate = 2 × 50 Hz = 100 per second; gap = 1 ÷ 100 = 0.01 s. (ii) Rate = 2 × 4 Hz = 8 per second; gap = 1 ÷ 8 = 0.125 s. 1/16 s = 0.0625 s.
**Answer:** (i) 0.01 s < 0.0625 s: roshni **lagatar** dikhti hai. (ii) 0.125 s > 0.0625 s: jhapkana **saaf dikhega**.
**Jaanch:** (i) mein gap persistence se 0.0625 ÷ 0.01 = 6.25 guna chhota hai ✅; (ii) mein gap persistence se 0.125 ÷ 0.0625 = 2 guna lamba hai ✅. Isi liye school-model ka lamp dheere ghumane par jhapak raha tha. (Persistence ka 1/16 s moti-moti baat hai; asli aankh ko tez roshni mein thoda zyada flicker rate chahiye, par 100 per second aaram se upar hai.)

**Meter ki needle ka jawab:** Needle ek bhaari cheez hai, aur wo itni tezi se dono taraf nahi ghoom sakti. Dheere ghumane par (kam f) wo har half cycle ke saath dono taraf jaa paati hai. Tez ghumane par (zyada f) wo ek taraf jaane se pehle hi current ulta ho jaata hai, aur needle bas zero ke paas kaampti hai, kyunki ek half cycle ka asar doosre half cycle se ulta kat jaata hai. Par lamp ki roshni power se tay hoti hai, jo dono half cycle mein positive hai: isliye lamp saaf chamakta hai. Asli bijli-ghar ke generator mein bhi yahi hota hai.

## Part F: AC aur DC: kaun kya, kyun

**DC (direct current)** ek hi disha mein behta hai, aur uska voltage lagbhag tay rehta hai. **AC** ka voltage aur disha lagatar badalte hain.

```
DC (battery):

+5 V    =  =  =  =  =  =  =  =  =  =  =  =  =  =  =  =  =  =  =  =  =  =  =  =  =

0 V     -------------------------------------------------------------------------

AC (socket-type wave):
+                   *  *  *  *  *
                 *                 *
           *  *                       *  *
0 V     *-----------------------------------*-----------------------------------*
                                               *                             *
                                                  *  *                 *  *
-       samay (time) ---->                              *  *  *  *  *

```

> **Figure 4.4** – Voltage-versus-time: upar DC (battery): tay voltage, seedhi line. Neeche AC: zero ke upar aur neeche badalti lehar (schematic, voltage ke scale par nahi).

| Baat | DC | AC |
|---|---|---|
| Sources | Battery, solar panel, adapter ka output | Generator (alternator), ghar ka socket |
| Disha | Ek hi | Har half cycle mein ulti (50 Hz mein 1 second mein 100 baar) |
| Voltage badalna | Seedha aasaan nahi | Transformer se aasaani se (Topic 5) |
| Lambi doori ki line | HVDC kuch khaas lines mein (sirf naam) | Zyadatar grid mein |
| Kahan chalta hai | Phone, laptop, LED torch, solar gadgets | Ghar ke pankhe, geyser, bulb, mixer |
| Banane ka tareeka | Battery ya generator + commutator | Generator + slip rings |

**DC generator (3 line mein):** Slip rings ki jagah split-ring commutator lagao. Wo har aadhe chakkar par coil ke sire badal deta hai, to bahar ka current ek hi disha mein jaata hai. Par wo DC tay line nahi, balki upar-neeche hilta hua (pulsating) hota hai; use aur saaf karne ki baat aage ki class mein.

**Adapter ka kaam:** Ghar mein AC aata hai, par phone aur laptop **DC** par chalte hain. Adapter (charger) ke andar voltage kam karne wala hissa aur AC ko DC mein badalne wala **rectifier** hota hai. Ye sirf naam hai: charger ko **kabhi mat kholo**.

**Transmission AC mein kyun:** Bijli-ghar se shehar tak current kam rakhne ke liye voltage **bahut badhana** padta hai ([household-circuits-part5.md](../household-circuits/household-circuits-part5.md)), aur AC mein transformer se ye aasaani se hota hai. Isiliye grid zyadatar AC par chalta hai. HVDC ka naam jaan lo: kuch bahut lambi lines mein ye kaam aata hai; iski detail aage ki class mein.

### Safe activity: label padho (ghar par adult ke saath)

Apne phone ke charger ya laptop ke adapter ka label dekho (kholna nahi). Input ke saamne AC ka nishaan (ek lehar ~) aur Hz likha hoga, aur output ke saamne DC ka nishaan (ek seedhi line aur uske neeche ek tooti-tooti line). Bas padho aur likho: input AC hai ya DC? Output? Kholna ya mains se kuch jodna bilkul nahi.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "AC mein current aage-peechhe hilta hai, to electrons kahin jaate nahi, bijli bekaar hai."**
Demonstration: Mr. Dahale ne Vaibhav se aari (saw) ki baat ki. Aari lakdi par aage-peechhe hi chalti hai, kahin "jaati" nahi, par lakdi kat jaati hai: energy haath se lakdi tak pahunchi. Waise hi AC mein taar ke charge apni jagah ke aas-paas aage-peechhe hilte hain, aur energy generator se bulb tak **lagatar** pahunchti rehti hai. Saboot: Figure 4.3 mein dikha ki lamp ki power dono half cycle mein positive hai: current ulta hone par lamp ka tapna ulta nahi hota. Heater, pankha aur bulb AC par bilkul theek chalte hain.

**Galti 2: "Frequency wahi hai jo generator ki rpm hai."**
Demonstration: Vaibhav ne socha ki 180 rpm par ghoomte do-pole generator ki frequency 180 Hz hogi. Par f = rpm ÷ 60 = 180 ÷ 60 = 3 Hz: "rpm" mein **minute** ka hisaab hai aur "Hz" mein **second** ka. Aur ye bhi dekho: wahi generator agar 4 pole ka ho (2 pole pairs), to usi 180 rpm par f = 3 × 2 = 6 Hz. Frequency rpm ke saath **pole pairs** par bhi nirbhar hai.

## Quick Recap

- Generator ki coil mein voltage bhujayein field ko **kaat-ne** par banta hai: neutral position par zero, field ke aar-paar chalne par peak.
- Ek chakkar mein do baar zero, do baar peak (ek +, ek -); disha **2 baar ulti**. Do-pole machine mein ek chakkar = ek cycle.
- Ek cycle ka graph smooth lehar: pehla half cycle ek disha, doosra ulti.
- Do-pole machine mein f (Hz) = rpm ÷ 60 aur T = 1 ÷ f. Zyada pole pairs ho to kam rpm mein bhi utni f.
- Bijli-ghar mein 50 Hz: do-pole machine lagbhag 3000 rpm; paani ki dheemi turbine mein zyada pole.
- Ghar ka 230 V effective value hai; peak lagbhag 325 V.
- Lamp 1 second mein 100 baar jhapakta hai (gap lagbhag 0.01 s), jo persistence of vision (lagbhag 1/16 s) se chhota hai, isliye dikhta nahi.
- DC battery, solar, adapter se; AC generator se. AC ka fayda: transformer se voltage badalna; isliye transmission AC mein. Adapter AC ko DC mein badalta hai (rectifier). HVDC sirf naam.

## Practice Questions

1. A two-pole generator turns at 900 rpm. Find the frequency and the time period of its output.

2. A two-pole generator runs at 1500 rpm. Find its frequency and time period, and say how many times the current changes direction in one second.

3. A two-pole generator must give an AC output of 10 Hz. At what rpm must it turn?

4. A hand-crank two-pole generator is turned at 120 rpm to light an LED. Find the frequency, and the number of times the current changes direction in 13 seconds. How many complete cycles does it make in one minute?

5. The voltage of a small generator is noted at equal steps of time: at t = 0 s it is 0 V, at t = 0.1 s it is +9 V (the peak), at t = 0.2 s it is 0 V, at t = 0.3 s it is -9 V (the peak), and at t = 0.4 s it is 0 V again, after which the pattern repeats. (a) Find the time period and frequency. (b) If the generator has two poles, find its rpm. (c) At which of these times are the coil sides moving along the field lines?

6. A hand-crank two-pole generator turns at 420 rpm and lights two LEDs connected in opposite directions, so that together they glow brightest twice in each cycle. Find the frequency, the number of bright glows per second, and the gap between two glows. Using persistence of vision as about 1/16 s, will the flicker be visible?

7. Explain, using the direction in which the coil sides move compared with the field, why the voltage of an AC generator becomes zero twice in every rotation.

8. A coil rotates clockwise (axle pointing out of the page towards you) in a field that points to the left of the page. At the instant when side R is on the left of the axle, it is moving upwards (↑). Using Fleming's right-hand rule, find the direction of the induced current in R (⊙ or ⊗), and check your answer with the left-hand rule.

9. In the situation of Question 8, find the direction of the induced current in the other side, S, at that same instant. After the coil turns a further quarter turn clockwise, what is the induced current in R?

10. Say whether each of these is mainly AC or DC: (a) a dry cell, (b) the output of a solar panel, (c) the output of an alternator in a power station, (d) the output of a phone charger, (e) the socket in a house.

11. A phone charger takes power from a 230 V AC socket and gives DC to the phone. Name the two jobs the charger does, and say which one the rectifier does.

12. Say whether each statement is true or false, giving a reason. (a) In AC, the current reverses its direction, so no energy reaches the bulb. (b) The 230 V of mains is the highest voltage that appears in the cycle. (c) At the same rpm, a four-pole generator gives twice the frequency of a two-pole generator. (d) Because the power of a lamp peaks twice in every cycle of 50 Hz AC, the light of an LED lamp can rise and fall 100 times a second, yet we do not notice it.

13. A six-pole alternator must produce 50 Hz. At how many rpm must it turn? Use f = (rpm ÷ 60) × pole pairs.

### Answers

1. **Given:** two-pole, 900 rpm. **Formula:** f = rpm ÷ 60, T = 1 ÷ f. **Substitution:** f = 900 ÷ 60 = 15 Hz; T = 1 ÷ 15 Hz = 0.0667 s. **Answer:** f = 15 Hz, T lagbhag 0.067 s. **Jaanch:** 0.0667 s × 15 = 1 s ✅.

2. f = 1500 ÷ 60 = **25 Hz**; T = 1 ÷ 25 Hz = **0.04 s**; disha ke badlav = 25 × 2 = **50 per second**. **Jaanch:** 0.04 s × 25 = 1 s ✅.

3. rpm = f × 60 = 10 × 60 = **600 rpm**. **Jaanch:** 600 ÷ 60 = 10 Hz ✅.

4. f = 120 ÷ 60 = **2 Hz**. Ek second mein 2 × 2 = 4 baar disha badalti hai, to 13 s mein 4 × 13 = **52 baar**. Ek minute mein cycles = 2 × 60 = **120**. **Jaanch:** 120 cycles per minute = rpm, jaisa do-pole machine mein hona chahiye ✅; 52 ÷ 13 = 4 per second ✅.

5. (a) Cycle t = 0 se t = 0.4 s tak: **T = 0.4 s**; f = 1 ÷ 0.4 s = **2.5 Hz**. (b) rpm = 2.5 × 60 = **150 rpm**. (c) Bhujayein field ki line mein tab chalti hain jab voltage zero ho: **t = 0 s, 0.2 s aur 0.4 s**. **Jaanch:** 150 ÷ 60 = 2.5 ✅; peak 0.1 s par hai, jo 0.4 s ka chautha hissa hai ✅.

6. f = 420 ÷ 60 = **7 Hz**. Chamak per second = 2 × 7 = **14**. Gap = 1 ÷ 14 = **lagbhag 0.0714 s**. Persistence 1/16 s = 0.0625 s. Gap **lamba** hai (0.0714 s > 0.0625 s), to flicker **dikhega**. **Jaanch:** 14 × 0.0714 s = 1 s ✅.

7. Voltage tab banta hai jab bhujayein field ki lakeerein kaat-ti hain. Chakkar mein do jagah aisi aati hain jahan bhujayein field ki **line mein** (saath ya ulta) chalti hain, wahan koi lakeer nahi kat-ti: voltage zero. Ye 0 deg aur 180 deg par hota hai, yaani **ek chakkar mein do baar**. Beech mein (90 deg aur 270 deg) bhujayein field ke aar-paar chalti hain: peak.

8. Right hand: thumb = motion = ↑, forefinger = field = ← (left). Thumb ko upar aur forefinger ko left rakho; beech wali ungli page se **bahar** (tumhari taraf) aati hai. **R mein current ⊙ (page se bahar).** **Jaanch (left hand):** forefinger ← (field), middle finger ⊙ (current), to thumb **neeche** (↓) aata hai. R upar ja rahi hai aur force neeche: chaal ka virodh, Lenz ke niyam ke anusaar ✅.

9. Side S coil ke doosre sire par daayen taraf hai aur neeche (↓) ja rahi hai (R ki ulti chaal). Right hand: thumb ↓, forefinger ←: beech wali ungli page ke **andar** ⊗ jaati hai. **S mein current ⊗.** (R mein ⊙ aur S mein ⊗: loop mein ek taraf bahar, doosri taraf andar, jaisa hona chahiye.) Ab R ko clockwise ek aur quarter turn karo: R baayen se upar (top) pahunchti hai, aur wahan clockwise chalne ka matlab daayen (→) chalna hai, jo field (←) ki **line mein** hai. Bhuja field ko kaat nahi rahi, isliye R mein **current zero**. **Jaanch:** ye neutral position hai, wahan e = 0 (Part A) ✅.

10. (a) **DC** (b) **DC** (c) **AC** (d) **DC** (e) **AC**. **Jaanch:** Part F ki table: battery, solar aur adapter ka output DC; alternator aur socket AC ✅.

11. Do kaam: (i) voltage kam karna (230 V se phone ke voltage tak), (ii) AC ko DC mein badalna. **Rectifier** doosra kaam (AC se DC) karta hai. **Jaanch:** phone DC par chalta hai, to rectifier zaroori hai ✅. (Voltage kam karne wale hisse ki detail Topic 5 mein.)

12. (a) **False** ❌. Current ulta hota hai par energy phir bhi bulb tak jaati hai (power dono half cycle mein positive: Figure 4.3). (b) **False** ❌. 230 V effective value hai; peak lagbhag 325 V. (c) **True** ✅. f = (rpm ÷ 60) × pole pairs; four-pole mein pole pairs 2, to f double. (d) **True** ✅. Power voltage ke + ya - hone par nahi, uske size par nirbhar hai, to ek cycle mein do hump aate hain: 2 × 50 = 100 per second; gap lagbhag 0.01 s hai, jo persistence se bahut chhota hai. **Jaanch:** Figure 4.3 mein ek cycle mein 2 hump ✅.

13. **Given:** six poles, f = 50 Hz. **Formula:** f = (rpm ÷ 60) × pole pairs. **Substitution:** pole pairs = 6 ÷ 2 = 3; 50 = (rpm ÷ 60) × 3, to rpm ÷ 60 = 50 ÷ 3 = 16.67, rpm = 16.67 × 60 = **1000 rpm**. **Jaanch:** (1000 ÷ 60) × 3 = 16.67 × 3 = 50 Hz ✅.
