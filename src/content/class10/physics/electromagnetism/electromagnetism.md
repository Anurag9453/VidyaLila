## Class 9 ka recall

Class 9 mein teen baatein seekhi thi, bas ek-ek line mein yaad kar lo:

- **Oersted (1820):** current wale taar ke aas-paas compass ki sui ghoomti hai, yaani current ka apna magnetic field hota hai ([magnetism-part4.md](../../../class9/physics/magnetism/magnetism-part4.md)).
- **Right-hand thumb rule:** daayein haath ka angootha current ki taraf, mudi hui ungliyan taar ke field ki taraf. Page ke andar jaate current (⊗) ke liye field **clockwise**, page se bahar aate current (⊙) ke liye **anticlockwise**.
- **Fleming ka left-hand rule:** **left** hand ki forefinger = field, middle finger = current, thumb = force ([magnetism-part6.md](../../../class9/physics/magnetism/magnetism-part6.md), Example 4). Wahan force ki disha nikali thi, par wo "kyun" nahi bataya tha. (Fleming: British engineer John Ambrose Fleming, 1800s ke ant mein.)

Aaj hum "kyun" dekhenge, force kin cheezon par depend karta hai ye dekhenge, aur rule ko do tareekon se check karna seekhenge.

> **Suraksha (poore topic ke liye):** Is topic mein **sirf kam voltage** ki baat hai (battery ya 6 V ke andar ka lab supply). Bijli ke **mains socket** se kabhi koi prayog mat karo, aur phone-charger ya adapter ko **kabhi mat kholo**. **Taqatwar magnet** (jaise neodymium) nigalne ka, ungli chimatne ka aur phone, ATM card ya pacemaker ko kharab karne ka khatra hai: chhote bachon se door rakho, aur inhe sirf teacher ya bade sambhalen. **Tez awaaz** (bass-box, DJ speaker, dhol) ke bilkul paas kaan ko nuksaan pahunch sakta hai: door khade ho, kaan par haath ya earplug rakho, aur chhote bachon ko speaker ke paas mat le jao. Is topic ki har activity ya to "school mein teacher ke saath", "ghar par adult ke saath", ya "sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo)" hai.

## Part A: Dhakka lagta kyun hai?

### Do field, ek taar

Taar mein current behta hai to Oersted ke hisaab se uske aas-paas **apna gol field** ban jaata hai. Ab is taar ko ek magnet ke field mein rakho. Ab wahan **do field** hain: magnet ka seedha field, aur taar ka gol field. Ye dono ek doosre se milte hain.

Maan lo magnet ka field **right (→)** hai aur taar mein current **page ke andar (⊗)**. Right-hand thumb rule se taar ka apna field **clockwise** hai: taar ke **upar** wo **→** hota hai, taar ke **neeche** **←**.

- Taar ke **upar**: magnet ka → aur taar ka → **jud jaate** hain: field **zyada** (lines bheed mein).
- Taar ke **neeche**: magnet ka → aur taar ka ← **kat jaate** hain: field **kam** (lines khaali).

```
 (a) Sirf magnet: field barabar
   N   →   →   →   →   →   →   S
   N   →   →   →   →   →   →   S

 (b) Sirf taar, current ⊗: apna gol field clockwise
               →
           ↑   ⊗   ↓
               ←

 (c) Dono saath: taar magnet ke field ke beech
   N   →   →   →   →   →   →   S     upar: lines paas-paas, BHEED
   N   →   →   →   →   →   →   S
   N   →   →   →   →   →   →   S
   N   →   →   →   ⊗   →   →   S     taar (current page ke andar)
   N       →       ↓       →   S     neeche: lines door-door, KAM
                force neeche
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Catapult picture: taar ke upar magnet aur taar ke field jud kar bheed banate hain, neeche kat kar kam rehte hain. Bheed wali taraf se khaali taraf, yaani **neeche** dhakka lagta hai. Left-hand rule bhi isi case mein (field →, current ⊗) force neeche deta hai.

Field lines ko tum **kheenchi hui rubber** ki tarah soch sakte ho: bheed wali taraf rubber zyada kasi hui hai, wo taar ko khaali taraf **dhakel deti hai**, jaise catapult ka pattha patthar ko aage fenkta hai. Dono taraf ka field ek jaisa hota to dhakka nahi lagta.

Ek honest baat: ye ek **picture** hai jo hamesha sahi disha deti hai, aur ise hum "second method" ki tarah jaanch ke liye use karenge. Asli baat ye hai ki force un chhote chhote charges par lagta hai jo taar ke andar chal rahe hain, aur taar unhe thaame rakhta hai, isliye poora taar hilta hai.

### Dhakke ka jawab bhi hota hai

Taar magnet ko bhi utna hi dhakka ulti taraf deta hai (do cheezein ek doosre ko barabar aur ulta force deti hain). Isliye lab mein magnet ko **kasa hua (clamp)** rakhte hain, warna wo khud hil jaata. Bass-box mein bhi magnet cabinet se kasa hua hota hai, aur halka cone hilta hai.

### Sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo): jhoolta hua taar

**School mein teacher ke saath, sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo):** Teacher ek halka lachakdaar taar (ya patli patti) horseshoe magnet ke poles ke beech latkate hain. Taar ko ek key aur ek rheostat ke saath lagbhag 6 V ya usse kam ke DC supply se jodte hain, aur rheostat current ko chhota rakhta hai. Key ko **bas ek pal** dabate hain: taar ek taraf jhoolta hai. Current ulta karne par ya magnet palatne par wo **ulti taraf** jhoolta hai. Students sirf dekhte hain, kuch jodte nahi.

## Part B: Force kin cheezon par depend karta hai?

Bass-box ki awaaz tez karo to cone zyada hilta hai, yaani force badhta hai. Lab ke taar se dekhne par force chaar cheezon par depend karta hai:

1. **Current I:** zyada current, zyada force.
2. **Field ki strength B:** taqatwar magnet, zyada force.
3. **Taar ki lambai L jo field mein hai:** zyada lambai field mein, zyada force.
4. **Taar aur field ke beech ka kon θ:** 90° par force **sabse zyada**, taar field ke **parallel** (θ = 0°) ho to **zero**.

Shabdon mein: force ∝ field × current × (field mein taar ki lambai) × sin θ. Isse chhota karke likhte hain **F ∝ B I L sin θ**. Ye sirf proportionality hai (kisi ek raashi ko double karo to F double), asli formula tesla jaisi unit ke saath aage ki class mein aayega.

| θ (taar aur field ke beech) | sin θ | Force (sabse zyada force ka kitna hissa) |
|---|---|---|
| 90° (taar field ke seedha aar-paar) | 1 | poora |
| 30° | 0.5 | aadha |
| 0° (taar field ke saath-saath) | 0 | zero |

Kyun zero? Field ke saath-saath chalte taar ka gol field magnet ke field ko ek taraf bheed aur doosri taraf khaali nahi banata, isliye dhakka nahi.

### Example 1: Sirf current badla

**Given:** Ek taar field mein hai, current 1.5 A par force 0.020 N. Field, lambai aur kon wahi. Current ab 4.5 A.
**Find:** Naya force.
**Formula:** F ∝ I (baaki sab same), isliye F₂ ÷ F₁ = I₂ ÷ I₁.
**Substitution:** I₂ ÷ I₁ = 4.5 A ÷ 1.5 A = 3. F₂ = 3 × 0.020 N = 0.060 N.
**Answer:** **0.060 N**.
**Jaanch:** F ÷ I pehle 0.020 N ÷ 1.5 A = 0.0133 N/A, ab 0.060 N ÷ 4.5 A = 0.0133 N/A, dono barabar ✅.

### Example 2: Teen cheezein ek saath badli

**Given:** Pehle: I = 1.6 A, field mein lambai L = 9 cm, force F₁ = 0.024 N. Ab: magnet 1.8 guna taqatwar, current aadha (0.8 A), field mein lambai 15 cm. Kon wahi (90°).
**Find:** Naya force.
**Formula:** F₂ ÷ F₁ = (B₂ ÷ B₁) × (I₂ ÷ I₁) × (L₂ ÷ L₁).
**Substitution:** B ka factor = 1.8. I ka factor = 0.8 A ÷ 1.6 A = 0.5. L ka factor = 15 cm ÷ 9 cm = 1.667 (lagbhag). Total = 1.8 × 0.5 × 1.667 = 1.5. F₂ = 1.5 × 0.024 N = 0.036 N.
**Answer:** **0.036 N**.
**Jaanch:** 0.036 N ÷ 0.024 N = 1.5 ✅. Factors alag alag dekho: field se badha, current se ghata, lambai se badha, kul mila kar 1.5 guna.

### Example 3: Taar tirchha kiya

**Given:** Taar jab field ke 90° par hai to force 0.040 N. Ab taar ko ghumaya ki wo field se 30° par hai.
**Find:** 30° par force, aur 0° par force.
**Formula:** F ∝ sin θ.
**Substitution:** F(30°) = 0.040 N × sin 30° = 0.040 N × 0.5 = 0.020 N. F(0°) = 0.040 N × sin 0° = 0.040 N × 0 = 0 N.
**Answer:** **0.020 N** (30° par), **0 N** (0° par).
**Jaanch:** 90° se 30° par force aadha ho gaya, sin 30° bhi 1 se aadha (0.5) ✅. 0° par poora hat gaya, jo Part B ki table se milta hai ✅.

## Part C: Left-hand rule ka abhyaas, aur catapult se jaanch

Class 9 ka rule: **left hand** ki teeno ungliyan ek doosre se 90° par. **Forefinger = field**, **middle finger = current**, **thumb = force**. Dhyan rahe: ye **left** hand hai. (Right-hand thumb rule field ki disha ke liye alag cheez hai.)

Yaad rakhne ki chhoti trick: **left-hand = motor** (bijli de kar force/halchal lo), **right-hand = generator** (halchal de kar bijli lo: ye rule Topic 3 mein aayega). Dono haathon mein forefinger hamesha field hoti hai.

```
                 forefinger = FIELD (B)
                       ↑
                       :
                       :
  thumb = FORCE (F) ←──+
                      /
                     /
                    ↙
              middle finger = CURRENT (I), tumhari taraf (page se bahar)
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Left-hand rule ke teeno axes ek doosre se 90° par. Is chitra mein current page se bahar (tumhari taraf), field upar, to force left. Ye **left hand** ka rule hai.

Is topic mein dishaen aise likhi hain: **page par** right (→), left (←), up (↑), down (↓), page se bahar **⊙**, page ke andar **⊗** (Class 9 jaisa). **Lab mein** (naksha dekhte waqt) East, West, North, South aur seedha Up, Down. Dono mein "Up" aur ⊙ ek hi cheez ke do naam samjho jab nakshe ko upar se dekhte ho.

### Example 4: Chhe cases

Har case mein: pehle left-hand rule, phir catapult picture se check (taar ka apna field kis taraf magnet ke field ke saath judta hai, wahi bheed wali taraf, aur force uske **ulti** taraf).

| Case | Field | Current | Left-hand rule: thumb | Catapult jaanch: bheed wali taraf (wahan taar ka field = magnet ka field) | Force |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | North | East | Up | Neeche wali taraf (wahan taar ka field North) | **Up** |
| 2 | East | North | Down | Upar wali taraf (wahan taar ka field East) | **Down** |
| 3 | Up (⊙) | West | North | South wali taraf (wahan taar ka field Up) | **North** |
| 4 | South | Up (⊙) | East | West wali taraf (wahan taar ka field South) | **East** |
| 5 | Page ke andar (⊗) | Neeche (↓) | Right (→) | Left wali taraf (wahan taar ka field ⊗) | **Right (→)** |
| 6 | Upar (↑) | Page se bahar (⊙) | Left (←) | Right wali taraf (wahan taar ka field ↑) | **Left (←)** |

Case 1 ka poora tareeka: taar mein current East ki taraf. Right-hand thumb rule se angootha East, to taar ke **neeche** field North aur **upar** field South. Magnet ka field North hai, to **neeche** field jud kar bheed, **upar** kat kar kam. Dhakka bheed se khaali ki taraf: **Up**. Left hand se bhi forefinger North, middle finger East, thumb Up. Dono tareeke ek hi jawab dete hain.

**Jaanch:** Case 1 aur case 2 mein field aur current ki jagah badli (North, East ki jagah East, North) aur force **ulta** (Up ki jagah Down) ho gaya ✅. Har case mein force current aur field dono se 90° par hai ✅. Har case mein dono tareeke (left hand aur catapult) ek hi disha pe pahunche ✅.

### Ulta karne par

Current ulta karo to force ulta. Field ulta karo (magnet palto) to force ulta. Dono ulte karo to force pehle jaisa. Kyun? Current ulta hone par taar ka gol field ulta ho jaata hai, to bheed wali taraf badal jaati hai. Magnet ulta karne par bhi yahi hota hai.

## Part D: Do samaantar taar: ek doosre ko kheenchte ya dhakelte hain

Ab ek mazedaar baat: magnet ki zaroorat hi nahi. **Do seedhe samaantar (parallel) taar**, jinme current behta hai, ek doosre par force lagate hain. Reason wahi hai: ek taar ka field doosre taar tak pahunchta hai.

Taar P bayein aur taar Q daayein, dono mein current **page se bahar (⊙)**. P ka apna field anticlockwise hai: P ke **daayein** (jahan Q hai) wo field **upar (↑)** ki taraf hai. Ab Q par left-hand rule: field ↑, current ⊙, to thumb **left (←)**, yaani **P ki taraf**. Q, P ki taraf kheencha jaata hai. Barabar aur ulta force P par bhi lagta hai (P, Q ki taraf).

Agar Q mein current ulta (⊗) hota, to wahi field ↑ par force ulta: **right (→)**, P se **door**.

```
  Same direction: dono ⊙           Ulti direction: P ⊙, Q ⊗

       P            Q                    P            Q
       ⊙ →        ← ⊙                  ← ⊙            ⊗ →
  (ek doosre ki taraf: ATTRACT)    (ek doosre se door: REPEL)
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Do samaantar taar. Same direction ke currents ek doosre ko **attract** karte hain (force ek doosre ki taraf), ulti direction ke currents **repel** karte hain. Har taar par force barabar aur ulta hota hai.

**Yaad rakhne ka tareeka:** *same direction = attract, opposite direction = repel*. (Dhyan: magnet ke poles mein **same pole repel** karte hain, par taaron mein **same direction attract** karti hai. Dono ko mat milao.)

Force kitna? Pehle taar ka field uske current ke saath badhta hai, aur doosre taar par force doosre ke current aur us field ka guna hai. To **force ∝ I₁ × I₂** (taaron ke beech ki doori aur lambai wahi). Ampere ki paribhasha (definition) aage ki class mein.

### Example 5: Do samaantar taar

**Given:** Pehle dono taaron mein 1.2 A, doori aur lambai wahi, aur force F₁. Ab ek taar mein 3.6 A aur doosre mein 2.4 A.
**Find:** Naya force kitna guna.
**Formula:** F ∝ I₁ × I₂.
**Substitution:** Pehle: 1.2 A × 1.2 A = 1.44 A². Ab: 3.6 A × 2.4 A = 8.64 A². Ratio = 8.64 A² ÷ 1.44 A² = 6.
**Answer:** Force **6 guna** (same direction mein attract, ulti direction mein repel).
**Jaanch:** Taar 1 ka current 3.6 ÷ 1.2 = 3 guna, taar 2 ka 2.4 ÷ 1.2 = 2 guna, to 3 × 2 = 6 ✅.

## Part E: Loudspeaker aur moving-coil meter

### Kaam ka siddhant: coil par force

Ek coil mein current behta hai to uski **do aamne-saamne ki bhujaon** mein current ulti disha mein hota hai (Class 9 ke motor ki tarah), isliye un par force bhi ulte hote hain. Ek bhuja ek taraf, doosri doosri taraf: coil **ghoomti** hai.

```
                                         ↑ force upar
   [N]   →   →      ⊗ ━━━━ coil ━━━━ ⊙      →   →   [S]
                    ↓ force neeche
         (coil ko kinare se dekha: ⊗ wali bhuja neeche, ⊙ wali bhuja upar,
          field N se S ki taraf, coil anticlockwise ghoomti hai)
```

> **Figure 1.4** – Magnet ke poles ke beech coil: ⊗ wali bhuja par force neeche aur ⊙ wali bhuja par force upar (left-hand rule: field →, ⊗ se neeche, ⊙ se upar), isliye coil anticlockwise ghoomti hai.

### Moving-coil galvanometer

Is coil ke saath ek **sui** aur ek **spring** jodo. Current jaye to coil ghoomti hai, par spring use wapas kheenchti hai. Coil wahin ruk jaati hai jahan magnet ka dhakka aur spring ka kheenchna barabar ho jaaye. **Jitna zyada current, utna bada force, utna zyada ghoomna**, to sui utni zyada hilti hai (lagbhag current ke proportional). Isiliye is meter ke dial par current ke nishaan ban jaate hain. Purane analogue meter isi tarah kaam karte the.

### Loudspeaker

Loudspeaker mein ek **voice coil** cone se juda hota hai aur magnet ke field mein baitha hota hai. Music ka current coil mein **kabhi ek disha mein, kabhi ulti disha mein** behta hai, aur zyada awaaz par zyada. Current ek disha mein to force aage, ulti disha mein to force peeche: coil aur uske saath cone **aage-peeche** kaanpta hai, hawa hilti hai aur awaaz ban jaati hai. Kam current ho to chhota kaanpna (halki awaaz), zyada current ho to bada kaanpna (tez awaaz).

```
   music ka current (taar se)
            :
            v
   +----------------+        +----------------+
   :   voice coil   :<======>:      cone       :  ---> hawa hilti hai: awaaz
   +----------------+        +----------------+
            :
   coil magnet ke field ke beech baithi hai
   +----------------------------------------------+
   :                  magnet                      :
   +----------------------------------------------+
```

> **Figure 1.5** – Loudspeaker ka saar: music ka current voice coil mein jaata hai, magnet ke field mein coil par force lagta hai, coil aur cone saath aage-peeche hote hain (<=====>) aur hawa hilti hai. Current ulta to force ulta.

Bass-box wali Purva ki paheli ab hal: patle taar mein current ka apna field hai, jo magnet ke field se milkar taar ko dhakka deta hai. Coil ke bahut saare turns aur taqatwar magnet se ye dhakka itna ho jaata hai ki cone ko hila de.

### Example 6: Meter ki sui

**Given:** Ek moving-coil meter mein 0.70 mA par sui 14 khaane (divisions) hilti hai. Sui ka hilna current ke proportional hai.
**Find:** 1.5 mA par sui kitne khaane hilegi.
**Formula:** Deflection ∝ I, isliye D₂ ÷ D₁ = I₂ ÷ I₁.
**Substitution:** D₂ = 14 khaane × (1.5 mA ÷ 0.70 mA) = 14 × 2.143 = 30 khaane (lagbhag).
**Answer:** **30 khaane**.
**Jaanch:** 14 ÷ 0.70 = 20 khaane per mA, aur 20 × 1.5 = 30 khaane ✅.

### Ghar par adult ke saath: cone ko mehsoos karo

Ek chhota speaker (phone ka bluetooth speaker ya purana radio) **dheemi awaaz** par music ke saath chalao. Adult ke saath uski jaali par **halki si ungli** rakho: tum kaanpna mehsoos karoge, jo voice coil ka dhakka hai. Speaker ko **kholna mat**, kyunki andar taqatwar magnet hota hai. Cards aur phone ko speaker ke bilkul paas mat rakho.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Force taar ki taraf ya field ki taraf lagta hai"**

Mini-demo: Ek taar mein current **North** ki taraf hai, aur magnet ka field **neeche (Down)** ki taraf. Purva sochti hai ki taar North chal raha hai, to dhakka North mein lagega, ya field neeche hai to neeche. Test mein taar na North gaya, na neeche: wo **West** ki taraf jhoola. Left hand: forefinger Down, middle finger North, thumb West. Catapult picture se bhi: taar ke **East** wali taraf taar ka field Down hai (magnet ke Down se jud kar bheed), to dhakka East se West ki taraf, yaani **West**. Force hamesha current aur field dono se **90° par** hota hai.
**Jaanch:** West aur North ke beech 90° ✅, West aur Down ke beech bhi 90° ✅. Agar force current ke saath hota to taar apni hi lambai mein khisakta, jhoolta nahi.

**Galti 2: "Force ke liye right hand use karna"**

Mini-demo: Ek taar mein current **West** ki taraf hai, field **South** ki taraf. Sahi: **left hand**, forefinger South, middle finger West, thumb **Up**. Agar koi right hand se wahi ungliyan rakhe, to thumb **Down** aayega: **galat** (ulta). Catapult picture se check karo: taar ke **neeche** wali taraf taar ka field South hai (magnet ke South se jud kar bheed), to dhakka bheed se khaali ki taraf, yaani **Up**. Left-hand answer sahi hai, right-hand wala galat.
Right hand ka kaam alag hai: **right-hand thumb rule** taar ke **field** ki disha batata hai, aur **left hand** **force** ki disha. Dono ko mat milao.
**Jaanch:** Left hand aur catapult dono se **Up** ✅. Right hand se **Down**, jo dono tareekon se match nahi hota ❌.

## Quick Recap

- Magnet ke field mein rakhe current wale taar par **force** lagta hai: taar ka apna gol field magnet ke field se ek taraf jud kar **bheed**, doosri taraf kat kar **khaali** banata hai, aur dhakka bheed se khaali ki taraf lagta hai (catapult picture).
- Force **F ∝ B I L sin θ**: current, field, field mein lambai ke saath badhta hai; θ = 90° par maximum, θ = 0° (taar field ke parallel) par zero.
- Force ki disha **left-hand rule**: forefinger = field, middle finger = current, thumb = force. Force current aur field dono se 90° par hai. Current ya field ulta to force ulta; dono ulte to force wahi.
- Do samaantar taar: **same direction attract, opposite direction repel**; force ∝ I₁ × I₂; dono taaron par force barabar aur ulta.
- Moving-coil galvanometer: coil par ulte force coil ko ghumate hain, spring rokti hai, current ke hisaab se sui hilti hai.
- Loudspeaker: voice coil mein music ka current, magnet ke field mein coil aur cone aage-peeche hote hain.

## Practice Questions

1. A straight wire in a uniform magnetic field carries 3 A and feels a force of 0.12 N. The field, the length of wire in the field and the angle stay the same, and the current is raised to 5 A. Find the new force.

2. A 0.6 m length of a wire lies in a magnetic field and feels a force of 0.45 N. The magnet is moved so that only half of that length stays in the field. The current, the field strength and the angle are unchanged. Find the new force and say why it changes.

3. A wire at 90° to a field feels a force of 0.50 N. It is turned so that it makes 45° with the field (take sin 45° = 0.71, lagbhag). Find the force now, and the force when the wire lies along the field.

4. Using the left-hand rule, find the direction of the force on the wire in each case. (The wire is in a lab; North, South, East and West are horizontal, Up and Down are vertical.)
   (a) Field West, current South.
   (b) Field Down, current East.
   (c) Field North, current Down.
   Then, for case (c), check your answer with the catapult picture (say on which side of the wire the field lines crowd).

5. On a page, directions are right (→), left (←), up (↑), down (↓), out of the page (⊙) and into the page (⊗). Find the direction of the force in each case.
   (a) Field right (→), current up (↑).
   (b) Field down (↓), current out of the page (⊙).
   (c) Field up (↑), current into the page (⊗). For (c), also state on which side of the wire the field lines are crowded, and confirm the force with the catapult picture.

6. A horizontal wire carries current towards East. It feels a force vertically Down. The field is horizontal and at right angles to the wire. In which direction does the field point? Use the left-hand rule.

7. Two long parallel wires carry 2 A and 4 A in the same direction. Compared with the case where both carry 2 A (same distance, same length), by what factor does the force change? Do the wires attract or repel? Which wire feels the greater force?

8. Two long straight parallel wires, P on the left and Q on the right, stand on this page. P carries current out of the page (⊙), Q carries current into the page (⊗). First use the right-hand thumb rule to find the direction of P's field at Q, then use the left-hand rule to find the direction of the force on Q. Do the wires attract or repel?

9. The voice coil of a loudspeaker carries 0.28 A at a quiet moment and 0.84 A at a loud moment (same magnet, same coil). By what factor does the force on the coil change? What happens to the force when the current reverses?

10. A moving-coil meter shows a deflection of 24 divisions for 0.40 mA. Assuming the deflection is proportional to the current, how many divisions will it show for 0.90 mA?

11. Explain (a) why a wire lying along the field direction feels no force, and (b) why the magnet has to be clamped when a current-carrying wire swings in its field.

12. Say whether each statement is true or false, giving a reason.
    (a) The force on a current-carrying wire in a field acts along the direction of the current.
    (b) If both the current and the field are reversed, the force reverses.
    (c) Two parallel wires carrying current in the same direction repel each other.
    (d) The right-hand thumb rule gives the direction of the force on a wire in a field.

### Answers

1. **Given:** I₁ = 3 A, F₁ = 0.12 N, I₂ = 5 A. **Find:** F₂. **Formula:** F ∝ I, F₂ = F₁ × (I₂ ÷ I₁). **Substitution:** F₂ = 0.12 N × (5 A ÷ 3 A) = 0.12 N × 1.667 = 0.20 N. **Answer:** **0.20 N**. **Jaanch:** 0.12 N ÷ 3 A = 0.04 N/A, aur 0.20 N ÷ 5 A = 0.04 N/A, barabar ✅.

2. **Given:** L₁ = 0.6 m, F₁ = 0.45 N, L₂ = L₁ ÷ 2 = 0.3 m. **Formula:** F ∝ L (field mein lambai). **Substitution:** F₂ = 0.45 N × (0.3 m ÷ 0.6 m) = 0.45 N × 0.5 = 0.225 N. **Answer:** **0.225 N** (lagbhag 0.23 N). Force isliye badla ki dhakka sirf field mein bachi taar par lagta hai: lambai aadhi, to force bhi aadha. **Jaanch:** 0.225 N × 2 = 0.45 N ✅.

3. **Given:** F(90°) = 0.50 N, θ = 45°. **Formula:** F ∝ sin θ. **Substitution:** F(45°) = 0.50 N × 0.71 = 0.355 N. F(0°) = 0.50 N × 0 = 0 N. **Answer:** lagbhag **0.35 N** (45° par), **0 N** (field ke saath-saath). **Jaanch:** 0.355 N ÷ 0.50 N = 0.71, jo sin 45° hai ✅; 0.35 N poore 0.50 N se kam hai, jaisa 90° se ghumne par hona chahiye ✅.

4. (a) Left hand: forefinger West, middle finger South, thumb **Down**. (b) Forefinger Down, middle finger East, thumb **North**. (c) Forefinger North, middle finger Down, thumb **East**.
   Catapult check (c): current Down, right-hand thumb rule se taar ke **East** wali taraf ka field **South** ki taraf aur **West** wali taraf ka field **North** ki taraf aata hai (dono ke thumb aur grip ke hisaab se). Magnet ka field North hai, to **West** wali taraf field jud kar **bheed**, East wali taraf kat kar **khaali**. Dhakka bheed (West) se khaali (East) ki taraf: **East** ✅ (left-hand answer jaisa).
   **Jaanch:** har case mein force field aur current dono se 90° par hai ✅.

5. (a) Forefinger →, middle finger ↑, thumb **page ke andar (⊗)**. (b) Forefinger ↓, middle finger ⊙, thumb **right (→)**. (c) Forefinger ↑, middle finger ⊗, thumb **right (→)**.
   Catapult (c): current ⊗, to taar ka field clockwise: taar ke **left** wali taraf wo **↑** hai, magnet ke ↑ se jud kar **bheed**; **right** wali taraf ↓ hai, kat kar **khaali**. Dhakka left se right: **right (→)** ✅.
   **Jaanch:** (c) mein dono tareeke ek hi jawab dete hain ✅.

6. Current East, force Down. Left hand se forefinger (field) us disha mein hogi jo East aur Down dono se 90° par ho aur horizontal ho: North ya South. **South** try karo: forefinger South, middle finger East: thumb **Down** aata hai ✅. (North try karo to thumb Up aata, jo galat.) **Answer: South.**
   **Jaanch (catapult):** current East, to taar ke **neeche** field North aur **upar** South. Force Down, yaani bheed upar honi chahiye: upar wala wire-field South tabhi bheed banata hai jab magnet ka field bhi South ho ✅.

7. **Formula:** F ∝ I₁ × I₂. Pehle: 2 A × 2 A = 4 A². Ab: 2 A × 4 A = 8 A². Ratio = 8 ÷ 4 = 2. **Answer:** force **2 guna**, same direction hai to wires **attract** karte hain. **Dono taaron par force barabar** hota hai (ek doosre par barabar aur ulta), chahe currents alag hon. **Jaanch:** ek taar ka current double hua (2 A se 4 A), baaki wahi, to force double ✅.

8. P ⊙ hai, to P ka field anticlockwise. P ke **right** mein (jahan Q hai) field **up (↑)** hai (anticlockwise: right side par upar). Q par: field ↑, current ⊗ (page ke andar). Left hand: forefinger ↑, middle finger ⊗, thumb **right (→)**, yaani **P se door**. Wires **repel** karte hain (ulti direction ke currents).
   **Jaanch:** Figure 1.3 ke right-hand wale chitra se milta hai ✅. Dusre tareeke se: Q mein current ulta hota (⊙) to force ulta (←, P ki taraf, attract) ✅.

9. **Formula:** F ∝ I. Factor = 0.84 A ÷ 0.28 A = 3. **Answer:** force **3 guna**. Current reverse ho to force bhi **reverse**: cone ulti taraf jaata hai. **Jaanch:** 0.28 A × 3 = 0.84 A ✅.

10. **Formula:** D₂ = D₁ × (I₂ ÷ I₁). **Substitution:** D₂ = 24 × (0.90 mA ÷ 0.40 mA) = 24 × 2.25 = 54. **Answer:** **54 divisions**. **Jaanch:** 24 ÷ 0.40 = 60 divisions per mA, 60 × 0.90 = 54 ✅.

11. (a) Taar field ke saath-saath ho to θ = 0°, sin 0° = 0, force F ∝ B I L sin θ ke hisaab se zero. Catapult picture mein bhi taar ka gol field magnet ke field ke saath ek taraf bheed aur doosri taraf khaali nahi banata. (b) Taar magnet ko utna hi aur ulta dhakka deta hai (barabar aur ulta force), to magnet kasa na ho to wo khud hil jaata.

12. (a) **False** ❌: force current aur field dono se 90° par hota hai. (b) **False** ❌: dono ulte karne par force **wahi** rehta hai (sirf ek ulta karo to force ulta hota, doosra bhi ulta karo to wapas pehle jaisa). (c) **False** ❌: same direction par **attract** hote hain. (d) **False** ❌: right-hand thumb rule **field** ki disha batata hai; force ke liye **left-hand rule**.
