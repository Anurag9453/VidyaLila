# Topic 2: g Kahan Kitna? Height, Depth Aur Doosre Grah

Topic 1 mein hum aasmaan mein the: planets Sun ke gird ellipse mein ghoomte hain, aur Kepler ke niyam batate hain ki kaun kitni der mein ek chakkar lagata hai. Ab hum wapas ek hi grah par utarte hain, aur ek chhota sa sawaal poochte hain: **ek hi Earth par g har jagah ek jaisa 9.8 m/s² hota hai kya?** Class 9 mein hamne g = GM/R² seekha tha aur ye bhi dekha tha ki g Moon par kam hai, poles par thoda zyada hai, aur oopar jaane par kam hota hai. Wahan sirf "kam hota hai" kaha tha. Aaj hum **kitna kam** hota hai, wo number nikaalenge: oopar jaane par, neeche (zameen ke andar) jaane par, aur doosre grahon par.

## Part A: Recall: g = GM/R² aur doori kahan se naapte hain

Class 9 mein hamne dekha tha ki F = G M m / R² aur F = m g ko barabar karne par **g = G M / R²** milta hai. Ismein girne wali cheez ka mass m nahi aaya. Ye bhi yaad rakho ki formula mein **doori Earth ke centre se** hoti hai, surface se nahi.

Jab cheez surface par hai, to centre se uski doori = R = 6.4 × 10⁶ m. Agar cheez kisi aur jagah hai, jahan centre se doori r hai, to wahan ka g

> g(r) = G M / r²  (cheez Earth ke bahar ho)

Is formula mein M wahi Earth ka poora mass hai, aur doori r ka square neeche hai. **Isliye jab r double hota hai, g chauthai ho jaata hai** (Class 9 mein inverse-square ke saath dekha tha). Is topic ka poora hisaab isi ek vichaar se niklega.

**Ratio vidhi:** G aur M baar-baar daalne ki zaroorat nahi. Surface ka g = 9.8 m/s² hamare paas pehle se hai (jo GM/R² hi hai). Naya g bas "doori ke badlaav ka asar" se milta hai:

> g(r) / g = (R / r)²

Ye wahi cheez hai jo ek line mein, bina bade numbers ke, kaam kar deti hai. Is topic ke zyadatar numericals isi se honge.

## Part B: Oopar jaane par g kam hota hai (par zero nahi)

Cheez surface se h oopar hai, to centre se uski doori r = R + h. Ratio vidhi mein r daalo:

> g_h = g × (R / (R + h))²

Dhyan do: **h** surface se naapi jaati hai, par formula mein **R + h** aata hai. (Class 9 mein bhi "R + h" wali baat dekhi thi.)

```
                     P   <- cheez, surface se h oopar
                     :
                     : h
              .------+------.
           .'        :        '.
          /          :          \
         (           O-------Q---)   <- Q: surface se d neeche
          \          :          /
           '.        :        .'
              '------+------'
```

> **Figure 2.1** – O Earth ka centre hai. Oopar wali cheez P ki centre se doori OP = R + h hai. Zameen ke andar wale point Q ki doori OQ = R − d hai, jahan d surface se Q tak neeche ki doori hai. Aage Part C mein Q ka g dekhenge.

**Example 1 (surface se 640 km oopar):** Surface se h = 640 km oopar kisi jagah par g nikalo. (g = 9.8 m/s², R = 6400 km.)

**Given:** g = 9.8 m/s², R = 6400 km, h = 640 km
**Find:** g_h
**Formula:** g_h = g × (R / (R + h))²
**Substitution:** R + h = 6400 + 640 = 7040 km. Hissa R/(R + h) = 6400 ÷ 7040 = 0.9091. Uska square = 0.8264. g_h = 9.8 m/s² × 0.8264 = **8.10 m/s²**
**Answer:** g_h lagbhag **8.1 m/s²** (surface ke g ka lagbhag 83%)
**Jaanch:** Surface se door gaye, to g kam aana chahiye: 8.10 < 9.8 ✅. Units: m/s² × (km ÷ km)² = m/s² ✅.

**Example 2 (surface se 1600 km oopar):** h = 1600 km par g kitna hoga?

**Given:** g = 9.8 m/s², R = 6400 km, h = 1600 km (yani R/4)
**Find:** g_h
**Formula:** g_h = g × (R / (R + h))²
**Substitution:** R + h = 8000 km. R/(R + h) = 6400 ÷ 8000 = 0.8. Square = 0.64. g_h = 9.8 m/s² × 0.64 = **6.27 m/s²**
**Answer:** **6.27 m/s²**
**Jaanch:** Ulta karke: 6.27 ÷ 9.8 = 0.64 = 0.8² ✅. Aur 6.27 m/s² ke liye r = 8000 km: G M ÷ r² = 4.002 × 10¹⁴ ÷ (8.0 × 10⁶)² = 4.002 × 10¹⁴ ÷ 6.4 × 10¹³ = 6.25 m/s² ✅ (thoda sa farak 9.77 aur 9.8 ki rounding ka hai).

Ab ek chhoti table dekho (R = 6400 km, g = 9.8 m/s²):

| Surface se height h | R + h | g_h (m/s²) lagbhag | Surface ke g ka hissa |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (surface) | 6400 km | 9.80 | 100% |
| 400 km | 6800 km | 8.68 | lagbhag 89% |
| 640 km | 7040 km | 8.10 | lagbhag 83% |
| 1600 km | 8000 km | 6.27 | 64% |
| 3200 km | 9600 km | 4.36 | lagbhag 44% |

Table se ek baat saaf hai: **g ghat'ta hai, par dheere dheere.** 400 km par abhi bhi lagbhag 89% hai. Kahani wale ISS ka jawab yahin chhupa hai, jise hum Part F mein poora karenge.

## Part C: Zameen ke andar jaane par g

Ab ulta safar: hum khaan mein, ya kisi gehre kunwe mein neeche utarte hain. Intuition kehti hai ki Earth ka centre paas aa raha hai, to gravity badhni chahiye. Par aisa nahi hota. Ek simple soch se dekhte hain.

**Simple soch (isse abhi saabit nahi karenge, bas maan lenge):** Maan lo Earth ek gol gend hai jiska density har jagah ek jaisa hai. Newton ne dikhaya tha ki jab hum is gend ke andar kisi point par hain, to **hamare bahar wali parat** (jo humse oopar, surface tak hai) hum par **net force zero** daalti hai: us parat ke alag-alag hisse hamein alag-alag dishaon mein kheenchte hain, aur ye saare kheench mil kar cancel ho jaate hain. Hum par bas **andar wali chhoti gend** ka kheench kaam karta hai, jiska radius R − d hai.

Ab us chhoti gend ka mass kitna hai? Density ek jaisi hai, to mass **radius ke cube** ke saath badhta hai: (R − d)³ ÷ R³. Aur doori R − d hai, jiska square neeche aata hai. To

> g_d / g = [(R − d)³ / R³] ÷ [(R − d)² / R²] = (R − d) / R = 1 − d/R

Yaani:

> **g_d = g (1 − d/R)**

Ye ek saaf, seedha sa **rule** hai: g depth ke saath **seedhi line mein ghat'ta hai**, aur centre par (d = R) g_d = 0.

Dhyan do ki surface ke **bahar** g, doori ke square se ghat'ta hai (Part B). Surface ke **andar** g, centre se doori ke saath seedha (linearly) ghat'ta hai. Dono surface par milte hain, jahan g = 9.8 m/s².

```
 g (m/s²)
10 !                    *
   !                  *
 8 !                *     *
   !              *         *
 6 !            *             *
   !          *                 *
 4 !        *                     * *
   !      *                           * * *
 2 !    *                                   * * * * * *
   !  *                                                 * * * * *
 0 !*
   +------------------------------------------------------------- r
    O                   R                   2R                  3R
```

> **Figure 2.2** – g aur Earth ke centre se doori r. Centre O par g = 0. Surface (r = R) tak seedhi line mein badhta hai aur R par sabse zyada, 9.8 m/s². Surface ke bahar (r > R) g ghat'ta hai: 2R par 2.45 m/s² aur 3R par lagbhag 1.09 m/s². (Ye ek simple model hai: Earth ko barabar density ki gend maana gaya hai.)

**Example 3 (1600 km gehraai par):** Ek kalpanik gehra kunwa surface se d = 1600 km neeche tak jaata hai (R/4). Wahan g kitna hai?

**Given:** g = 9.8 m/s², R = 6400 km, d = 1600 km
**Find:** g_d
**Formula:** g_d = g (1 − d/R)
**Substitution:** d/R = 1600 ÷ 6400 = 0.25. g_d = 9.8 m/s² × (1 − 0.25) = 9.8 m/s² × 0.75 = **7.35 m/s²**
**Answer:** **7.35 m/s²**
**Jaanch:** Ulta: 7.35 ÷ 9.8 = 0.75 = 1 − 0.25 ✅. Limiting case: d = 0 par formula g deta hai (9.8) ✅, aur d = R par 0 ✅.

**Example 4 (640 km gehraai par):** d = 640 km par g nikalo.

**Given:** g = 9.8 m/s², R = 6400 km, d = 640 km
**Find:** g_d
**Formula:** g_d = g (1 − d/R)
**Substitution:** d/R = 640 ÷ 6400 = 0.1. g_d = 9.8 m/s² × 0.9 = **8.82 m/s²**
**Answer:** **8.82 m/s²**
**Jaanch:** 8.82 ÷ 9.8 = 0.9 = 1 − 0.1 ✅. Example 1 mein 640 km **oopar** par g 8.10 m/s² tha, 640 km **neeche** 8.82: dono 9.8 se kam hain ✅. Itni hi doori par oopar jaane se g zyada ghata (8.10), andar jaane se kam ghata (8.82).

| Surface se neeche d | d / R | g_d (m/s²) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 (surface) | 0 | 9.80 |
| 640 km | 0.1 | 8.82 |
| 1600 km | 0.25 | 7.35 |
| 4800 km | 0.75 | 2.45 |
| 6400 km (centre) | 1 | 0 |

**Ek imaandaar baat:** Asli Earth mein andar barabar density nahi hai: bahut bhaari iron wala core centre ke paas hai. Isliye asli Earth mein shuru ke lagbhag 2900 km tak g ghat'ta nahi, balki thoda sa badhta bhi hai (core ki sataah ke paas lagbhag 10.7 m/s²), aur uske baad hi ghat kar centre par zero hota hai. Hamara g_d = g (1 − d/R) ek **simple model** hai jo ek hi baat sahi dikhata hai: **centre par g zero hota hai.** Real khaan ya kunwe (kuch km gehre) mein g itna kam badalta hai ki 9.8 m/s² hi maanna theek hai (Practice mein dekhoge).

## Part D: Earth gol nahi hai: poles aur equator

Class 9 mein bola tha ki poles par g thoda zyada hai aur equator par kam. Ab number dekhte hain: **poles par lagbhag 9.83 m/s²** aur **equator par lagbhag 9.78 m/s².**

Do wajah hain, dono bina formula ke samajh sakte ho:

1. **Earth ka ubhaar (bulge).** Earth apne axis par ghoomti hai, isliye equator par thodi phooli aur poles par thodi dabi hui hai. Equator ki radius lagbhag 6378 km aur poles ki lagbhag 6357 km hai, yaani farak sirf lagbhag 21 km. Poles centre ke **thode paas** hain, to g_pole thoda zyada (g = GM/r² mein r chhota).
2. **Earth ki ghoomti hui chaal.** Equator par hum Earth ke sabse bade ghere par ghoomte hain (24 ghante mein ek chakkar), isliye wahan ghoomne ki chaal sabse zyada hoti hai. Ye ghoomna hamein oopar ki taraf "phenkne" ki thodi koshish karta hai, jisse g ka asar thoda kam mehsoos hota hai. Poles par ghoomne ka ye asar lagbhag zero hota hai. (Isi liye g latitude ke saath dheere dheere badalta hai: equator, yaani 0 degree par sabse kam, aur poles, yaani 90 degree par sabse zyada.)

```
                    N pole   (g lagbhag 9.83)
                  .-------------.
              .--'               '--.
         ----(     Earth ghoomti     )---- Equator (g lagbhag 9.78)
              '--.      hai       .--'
                  '-------------'
                    S pole   (g lagbhag 9.83)
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Earth thodi dabi hui hai: poles centre ke paas, equator door. Yahan ubhaar bahut badha-chadha kar dikhaya hai: asli Earth mein farak sirf lagbhag 21 km hai, jo 6400 km ke saamne bahut chhota hai. (Ye "g kam, g zyada" ke baare mein hai, **mass** ke baare mein nahi: mass kahin nahi badalta.)

**Example 5 (ek hi insaan, do jagah):** Ek 80 kg ke insaan ka weight poles par aur equator par kitna hoga? (g_pole = 9.83 m/s², g_equator = 9.78 m/s².)

**Given:** m = 80 kg, g_pole = 9.83 m/s², g_eq = 9.78 m/s²
**Find:** W_pole, W_eq, aur unka farak
**Formula:** W = m g
**Substitution:** W_pole = 80 kg × 9.83 m/s² = 786.4 N. W_eq = 80 kg × 9.78 m/s² = 782.4 N. Farak = 786.4 − 782.4 = 4.0 N
**Answer:** W_pole = **786.4 N**, W_eq = **782.4 N**, farak sirf **4.0 N** (lagbhag 0.4 kg ki cheez ka weight)
**Jaanch:** 786.4 N ÷ 80 kg = 9.83 m/s² ✅ aur 782.4 N ÷ 80 kg = 9.78 m/s² ✅. Mass dono jagah 80 kg hi hai.

Is farak ko hum roz mehsoos nahi karte. Bas yaad rakho: g poora-poora 9.8 sirf ek **lagbhag** value hai.

## Part E: Doosre grah aur Chaand: apna weight kitna?

g = G M / R² mein M aur R us pind ke hote hain jis par hum khade hain. Isliye har pind par g alag hai. Ye values lagbhag hain:

| Pind | g (m/s²) lagbhag | Earth ke g ka hissa |
|---|---|---|
| Earth | 9.8 | 1 |
| Moon | 1.6 | lagbhag 1/6 |
| Mars | 3.7 | lagbhag 0.38 |
| Jupiter | 25 | lagbhag 2.6 |

(Jupiter ek gas ka bada grah hai, uski koi theek-theek "zameen" nahi. Yahan g uske **badalon ki oopari parat** ke paas ka hai.)

Mass **nahi badalta**, weight badalta hai: W = m g. Class 9 mein isi ko spring balance aur beam balance ke saath dekha tha: spring balance Chaand par kam weight dikhati hai, beam balance wahi mass.

**Example 6 (ek vidyarthi, chaar jagah):** 52 kg ke vidyarthi ka weight Earth, Moon, Mars aur Jupiter par nikalo.

**Given:** m = 52 kg; g = 9.8, 1.6, 3.7, 25 m/s²
**Find:** W har jagah
**Formula:** W = m g
**Substitution:**
- Earth: 52 × 9.8 = 509.6 N
- Moon: 52 × 1.6 = 83.2 N
- Mars: 52 × 3.7 = 192.4 N
- Jupiter: 52 × 25 = 1300 N

**Answer:** Earth par **509.6 N**, Moon par **83.2 N**, Mars par **192.4 N**, Jupiter par **1300 N**. Mass sab jagah **52 kg**.
**Jaanch:** Moon wala weight Earth ka 83.2 ÷ 509.6 = 0.163 ≈ 1/6 ✅. Jupiter wala 1300 ÷ 509.6 = 2.55 ≈ 25 ÷ 9.8 ✅.

```
 Earth    ##### 
 Moon     #
 Mars     ##
 Jupiter  #############
          1 # lagbhag 100 N (52 kg ke vidyarthi ka weight)
```

> **Figure 2.4** – Ek hi vidyarthi ka weight chaar jagah: Earth lagbhag 510 N, Moon lagbhag 83 N, Mars lagbhag 192 N, Jupiter 1300 N. Bar ka size lagbhag hai, mass sab jagah 52 kg hi hai.

**Example 7 (astronaut kitna oopar kood sakta hai):** Earth par ek astronaut zameen se utha kar sirf 0.50 m oopar kood paata hai. Maan lo Chaand par bhi wo usi **chaal** (takeoff speed) se kood sakta hai. Chaand par wo kitni oonchi kood sakta hai? (g_E = 9.8 m/s², g_M = 1.6 m/s², hawa aur suit ka asar chhodo.)

Class 9 ka v² = u² + 2 a s yaad karo. Oopar chadhte hue ant mein speed v = 0 hoti hai aur a = −g.

**Given:** h_E = 0.50 m, g_E = 9.8 m/s², g_M = 1.6 m/s²
**Find:** takeoff speed u, aur Chaand par oonchai h_M
**Formula:** 0 = u² − 2 g h, yaani u² = 2 g h
**Substitution:** Earth par u² = 2 × 9.8 × 0.50 = 9.8 m²/s², to u = 3.13 m/s. Chaand par h_M = u² ÷ (2 g_M) = 9.8 ÷ (2 × 1.6) = 9.8 ÷ 3.2 = 3.06 m
**Answer:** takeoff speed lagbhag **3.13 m/s**, aur Chaand par oonchai lagbhag **3.06 m**
**Jaanch:** Chaal wahi, to oonchai g ke ulte anupaat mein: h_M ÷ h_E = 3.06 ÷ 0.50 = 6.1, aur g_E ÷ g_M = 9.8 ÷ 1.6 = 6.1 ✅. Isliye Chaand par kood lagbhag **6 guna** oonchi jaati hai.

(Asli astronaut moti suit pehne hote hain, isliye itni oonchi nahi kood paate: ye bas g ka asar dikhane ke liye ek aadarsh (ideal) sawaal hai.)

**Example 8 (ratio vidhi: ek aur grah):** Ek grah ka mass Earth ka 8 guna aur radius Earth ka 2 guna hai. Wahan g kitna hoga?

**Given:** M = 8 M_E, R = 2 R_E, g_E = 9.8 m/s²
**Find:** g
**Formula:** g = g_E × (mass ka guna) ÷ (radius ke guna ka square)
**Substitution:** g = 9.8 × 8 ÷ 2² = 9.8 × 8 ÷ 4 = 9.8 × 2 = **19.6 m/s²**
**Answer:** **19.6 m/s²**
**Jaanch:** Seedha: g = G (8 M) ÷ (2 R)² = 8 ÷ 4 × (G M ÷ R²) = 2 g_E ✅. Aur ye Jupiter ke g (25 m/s²) se kam hai, jaise hona chahiye: Jupiter ka mass Earth ka lagbhag 318 guna hai, par radius bhi lagbhag 11 guna hai, to g sirf lagbhag 2.6 guna hi aata hai.

**Khud karke dekho: kood-oonchai ka test (ghar par adult ke saath)**

Kisi sapaat, na phisalne wali jagah par deewar ke paas khade ho jao (adult paas rahe). Ek chalk se deewar par apni khade hone ki oonchai ka nishaan lagao. Ab zor se kood kar, deewar par chhoone ki jagah ka nishaan lagao. Dono nishaanon ka farak tumhari kood ki oonchai h_E hai. Maan lo ye 0.30 m nikli. Is chaal se Chaand par h_M = h_E × 9.8 ÷ 1.6 = 0.30 × 6.125 = **1.84 m**. Apna number khud nikalo. Koodne se pehle jagah ki zameen saaf aur gili na ho, aur neeche utarte waqt ghutne thode mod kar utro.

**Khud karke dekho: kaagaz ki table (ghar par adult ke saath, sirf kaagaz)**

Apna mass kilogram mein likho. Part E ki g table se dekho ki tumhara weight Earth, Moon, Mars aur Jupiter par kitna hoga. Chaaron ke saamne N likhna na bhoolo. Phir sochkar likho: jab mass wahi hai, to **kaun si cheez badli** aur **kaun si nahi**?

## Part F: Kahani ka jawab: ISS par g kitna?

Yuvraj ke sawaal par aate hain: kya ISS mein g zero hai? Surface se h = 400 km, R = 6400 km.

**Given:** g = 9.8 m/s², R = 6400 km, h = 400 km
**Find:** g_h
**Formula:** g_h = g × (R / (R + h))²
**Substitution:** R + h = 6800 km. R/(R + h) = 6400 ÷ 6800 = 0.9412. Square = 0.8858. g_h = 9.8 m/s² × 0.8858 = **8.68 m/s²**
**Answer:** lagbhag **8.7 m/s²**, surface ke g ka lagbhag 89%. Yaani g sirf lagbhag 11% kam hua.
**Jaanch:** Doori 6400 se 6800 (sirf 1.06 guna) hui, to g 1/1.06² = 0.886 guna hona chahiye ✅. Ye wahi 8.68 m/s² hai jo Class 9 mein ek satellite ke orbit ke liye mila tha.

To Yuvraj ke saamne ka sach ye hai: ISS par gravity **lagbhag poori** hai, aur wahi gravity ISS ko aur uske andar ke astronaut ko Earth ki taraf kheench rahi hai. Wo astronaut isliye tairte hue dikhte hain ki wo aur ISS **dono saath free fall** mein hain. Ye hum Topic 4 mein poori tarah dekhenge. Abhi itna yaad rakho: **tairna g zero hone ka saboot nahi hai.**

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "400 km oopar g zero ho jaata hai (isliye astronaut floating)."**

Ye sach lagta hai kyunki video mein sab kuch hawa mein tair raha hai. Par ek chhoti kahani se dekho. Yuvraj ne kaha, "Agar wahan g zero hota, to ISS ko Earth kheench hi nahi rahi hoti, aur wo seedha door ud jaata." Mr. Godbole ne poochha, "To phir ISS Earth ke gird chakkar kyun lagata hai? Gol ghoomne ke liye bhi to koi **kheench** chahiye." Yuvraj ruk gaya.

Number ne baat saaf kar di (Part F): 400 km par g = 8.7 m/s², surface ke g ke lagbhag 89%. Aur formula g_h = g (R/(R + h))² mein R/(R + h) kabhi zero nahi hota, chahe h kitna bhi bada ho: g **kam** hota jaata hai, par kisi bhi height par exact zero nahi hota. Surface se bahut door, jaise Earth ke chaaron taraf ke aasmaan mein dur-dur tak, bhi gravity ka kuch na kuch asar rehta hai.

**Niyam:** g oopar jaane par **kam hota hai par kabhi zero nahi.** Astronaut ka tairna, g zero hone se nahi, **free fall** se hai (Topic 4).

**Galti 2: "Zameen ke andar jaane par g badhta hai, kyunki centre paas aa raha hai."**

Ye dimag ko sahi lagta hai: gravity centre ki taraf kheenchti hai, to paas jaane par zyada honi chahiye (jaise 1/r² mein r chhota). Ek demonstration se dekho: maan lo Earth ke andar d = 4800 km gehra gaye, yaani centre se sirf r = 1600 km door. 1/r² wale rule se ye soch kehti hai: R/r = 6400 ÷ 1600 = 4, to g = 9.8 × 16 = **156.8 m/s²**. Par ye galat hai, kyunki hamare **bahar ki parat** ab hum par net force nahi daalti (Part C). Sahi rule: g_d = 9.8 × (1 − 0.75) = **2.45 m/s²**, jo surface ke g se bahut kam hai.

Isse bhi aasan test: Earth ke bilkul centre par chaaron taraf ka kheench barabar hai: kisi taraf se kheenchne ka koi **fayda** nahi, to net force zero, g = 0. Agar "paas jaane par g badhta hai" sach hota to centre par to infinite g hona chahiye tha, jo asambhav hai.

**Niyam:** Surface ke **oopar** g ghat'ta hai (1/r² se), surface ke **neeche** bhi ghat'ta hai (seedhi line mein, g (1 − d/R) se), aur centre par zero hai. Surface par sabse zyada hai (simple model mein; asli Earth ke bhaari core ki wajah se kuch hazaar km tak g thoda badhta hai, par "centre ki taraf g badhta hi jaata hai" galat hai: centre par g zero hai).

## Quick Recap

- **Recall:** g = GM/R² (Class 9). Doori **Earth ke centre se** naapte hain: surface se h oopar ho to r = R + h.
- **Height par:** g_h = g × (R / (R + h))². h badhne par g ghat'ta hai, par zero kabhi nahi hota. 400 km par g lagbhag 8.7 m/s² (surface ka lagbhag 89%).
- **Depth par (simple model, barabar density):** g_d = g (1 − d/R). Seedhi line mein ghat'ta hai, centre par 0. Asli Earth mein andar ka core isse thoda alag asar deta hai.
- **Poles aur equator:** g_pole ≈ 9.83 m/s², g_equator ≈ 9.78 m/s². Wajah: Earth ka ubhaar aur uska ghoomna. Mass nahi badalta.
- **Doosre grah:** g_Moon ≈ 1.6, g_Mars ≈ 3.7, g_Jupiter ≈ 25 m/s². Weight W = m g badalta hai, mass nahi.
- **Astronaut ka hop:** wahi takeoff speed, to oonchai g ke ulte anupaat mein; Chaand par lagbhag 6 guna.
- **Ratio vidhi:** g_naya = g × (mass ka guna) ÷ (radius ke guna ka square).
- **Galtiyan:** "400 km par g zero" galat (8.7 m/s² hai). "Depth par g badhta hai" galat (ghat'ta hai, centre par 0).

9. At what height above the Earth's surface does g become half of its surface value? (R = 6400 km. Hint: use g_h / g = (R / (R + h))² = 1/2 and a square root.)

10. At what depth below the surface does g fall to 7.84 m/s²? Take g = 9.8 m/s² and R = 6400 km.

11. A 100 kg person is weighed using a spring balance at the North Pole and at the equator. Find the two readings (g_pole = 9.83 m/s², g_equator = 9.78 m/s²). Will a beam balance show a different mass at these two places?

12. State True or False and correct the false statements:
    (a) At the height of the ISS (about 400 km), g is zero.
    (b) Inside the Earth, g increases steadily as we go towards the centre.
    (c) A 1 kg object has the same mass on the Moon as on the Earth.
    (d) The weight of an object on Mars is more than on Jupiter.
    (e) The value of g at the poles is slightly greater than at the equator.

13. Explain in your own words why astronauts hop to a great height on the Moon even though their mass is the same as on the Earth.

### Answers

1. **Given:** h = 100 km. **Formula:** g_h = g (R / (R + h))². **Substitution:** R + h = 6500 km. R/(R + h) = 6400 ÷ 6500 = 0.98462. Square = 0.96945. g_h = 9.8 × 0.96945 = **9.50 m/s²**. **Jaanch:** 9.50 < 9.8 ✅ aur 9.50 ÷ 9.8 = 0.969 ≈ (0.9846)² ✅. 100 km par g sirf lagbhag 3% kam hua.

2. **Given:** h = 36,000 km. **Formula:** g_h = g (R / (R + h))². **Substitution:** R + h = 6400 + 36,000 = 42,400 km. R/(R + h) = 6400 ÷ 42,400 = 0.15094. Square = 0.022783. g_h = 9.8 × 0.022783 = **0.223 m/s²** (lagbhag 0.22). Hissa = 0.022783, yaani lagbhag **2.3%** surface ke g ka. **Jaanch:** Doori 6.625 guna hui (42,400 ÷ 6400), to g 1/6.625² = 0.0228 guna ✅. Ye zero nahi hai: aur Topic 4 mein dekhenge ki isi chhote g se satellite 24 ghante mein ek chakkar lagata hai.

3. **Given:** d = 1 km. **Formula:** g_d = g (1 − d/R). **Substitution:** d/R = 1 ÷ 6400 = 0.00015625. g_d = 9.8 × (1 − 0.00015625) = 9.8 × 0.99984 = **9.7985 m/s²** (lagbhag 9.80). Farak = 9.8 − 9.7985 = **0.0015 m/s²**. **Jaanch:** Farak = g × d/R = 9.8 × 0.00015625 = 0.0015 ✅. Itna chhota farak koi nahi mehsoos kar sakta, isliye khaan mein bhi g = 9.8 maanna theek hai.

4. **Given:** d = R/2. **Formula:** g_d = g (1 − d/R). **Substitution:** d/R = 1/2. g_d = 9.8 × (1 − 0.5) = 9.8 × 0.5 = **4.9 m/s²**. **Jaanch:** 4.9 ÷ 9.8 = 0.5 = 1 − 0.5 ✅. (d = 3200 km, aadhe raaste par aadha g.)

5. **Given:** m = 48 kg, g_E = 9.8 m/s², g_Mars = 3.7 m/s². **Formula:** W = m g. **Substitution:** W_E = 48 × 9.8 = 470.4 N. W_Mars = 48 × 3.7 = 177.6 N. **Answer:** Earth par **470.4 N**, Mars par **177.6 N**, mass Mars par bhi **48 kg**. **Jaanch:** 177.6 ÷ 470.4 = 0.378 = 3.7 ÷ 9.8 ✅.

6. **Given:** m = 44 kg, g_E = 9.8, g_J = 25 m/s². **Formula:** W = m g. **Substitution:** W_E = 44 × 9.8 = 431.2 N. W_J = 44 × 25 = 1100 N. Anupaat = 1100 ÷ 431.2 = 2.55. **Answer:** **431.2 N** aur **1100 N**; Jupiter par lagbhag **2.6 guna**. **Jaanch:** 25 ÷ 9.8 = 2.55 ✅. Mass dono jagah 44 kg hi hai.

7. **Given:** m = 76 kg, h = 8 km. **Formula:** g_h = g (R / (R + h))². **Substitution:** R + h = 6408 km. R/(R + h) = 6400 ÷ 6408 = 0.998752. Square = 0.997505. g_h = 9.8 × 0.997505 = **9.7755 m/s²** (lagbhag 9.78). Sea level par W = 76 × 9.8 = 744.8 N. Peak par W = 76 × 9.7755 = 742.9 N. Farak = **lagbhag 1.9 N**. **Jaanch:** Farak ≈ 2h/R × W = 2 × 8 ÷ 6400 × 744.8 = 1.86 N ✅. Itna chhota farak (lagbhag 0.25%) bina tez instrument ke mehsoos nahi hoga. Yaad rakho: 8 km, 6400 km ke saamne bahut chhota hai.

8. **Given:** h_E = 0.40 m, g_E = 9.8, g_M = 3.7 m/s². **Formula:** u² = 2 g h (wahi u dono jagah), to h ∝ 1/g. **Substitution:** h_Mars = h_E × g_E ÷ g_Mars = 0.40 × 9.8 ÷ 3.7 = 3.92 ÷ 3.7 = **1.06 m**. **Jaanch:** Takeoff speed u = √(2 × 9.8 × 0.40) = √7.84 = 2.8 m/s. Mars par h = u² ÷ (2 × 3.7) = 7.84 ÷ 7.4 = 1.06 m ✅.

9. **Given:** g_h / g = 1/2. **Formula:** (R / (R + h))² = 1/2, to R / (R + h) = 1/√2. **Substitution:** R + h = √2 × R = 1.4142 × 6400 = 9051 km, to h = 9051 − 6400 = **lagbhag 2651 km** (yaani 0.414 R). **Jaanch:** g_h = 9.8 × (6400 ÷ 9051)² = 9.8 × (0.7071)² = 9.8 × 0.5 = 4.9 m/s² ✅.

10. **Given:** g_d = 7.84 m/s², g = 9.8 m/s². **Formula:** g_d = g (1 − d/R), to 1 − d/R = g_d ÷ g. **Substitution:** 7.84 ÷ 9.8 = 0.8, to d/R = 1 − 0.8 = 0.2. d = 0.2 × 6400 = **1280 km**. **Jaanch:** 9.8 × (1 − 0.2) = 9.8 × 0.8 = 7.84 m/s² ✅.

11. **Given:** m = 100 kg, g_pole = 9.83, g_eq = 9.78 m/s². **Formula:** W = m g. **Substitution:** W_pole = 100 × 9.83 = 983 N. W_eq = 100 × 9.78 = 978 N. **Answer:** spring balance **983 N** aur **978 N** (farak 5 N). Beam balance dono jagah **100 kg** hi dikhayegi, kyunki wo apne mass ko doosre known mass se compare karti hai (g dono taraf ek jaisa lagta hai). **Jaanch:** 983 ÷ 100 = 9.83 ✅ aur 978 ÷ 100 = 9.78 ✅.

12. (a) **False** ❌. 400 km par g lagbhag 8.7 m/s² hai (Part F). Astronaut tairte hain kyunki wo aur ISS free fall mein hain.
    (b) **False** ❌. Simple model mein g centre ki taraf seedhi line mein **ghat'ta** hai aur centre par zero hai.
