# Topic 3: Escape Velocity: Itni Tez Phenko Ki Wapas Na Aaye

Topic 2 mein dekha ki g kahin bhi ek jaisa nahi rehta: Earth se door jaate jao to g ghat-ta jaata hai, aur Moon ya Mars jaisi jagahon par uski value alag hoti hai. Class 9 mein ye bhi aaya tha ki g kabhi **bilkul zero** nahi hota. Ab ek bada sawal uthta hai. Agar g door jaakar itna kamzor hota jaata hai, to kya hum koi cheez itni **tez** phenk sakte hain ki wo gravity ki pakad se nikal jaaye aur **kabhi wapas na aaye**? Is topic mein hum is sawal ka sahi, number wala jawab dhoondhenge, aur ye bhi dekhenge ki Moon par hawa kyun nahi hai aur Jupiter par kyun hai.

## Park ka fawwara

Mr. Agarkar apni bhatiji Manjiri ko shaam ki sair ke liye shehar ke bade park mein le gaye. Park ke beech ek fawwara chal raha tha. Paani ki kai dhaarein neeche se upar jaati thin, kuch chhoti, kuch lambi, aur har dhaar oopar jaakar boondon mein bikhar kar wapas hauz mein gir jaati thi.

Manjiri dhyan se dekh rahi thi. "Chacha, jis dhaar ki raftaar zyada hai, wo zyada oopar jaa rahi hai. Wo sabse oonchi wali to kaafi tez nikli hogi!"

"Bilkul theek," Mr. Agarkar ne kaha. "Pump jitni tez dhakela, dhaar utni oonchi."

Manjiri ne kuch socha, phir boli, "Chacha, ek ajeeb sawal hai. Agar hum pump ko aur tez, aur tez karte jaayein, to kya kisi din dhaar itni tez ho jaayegi ki uski boondein **wapas hi na aayen**? Seedha aasmaan mein chali jaayen?"

Mr. Agarkar hanse. "Ye to Newton ke zamane ka sawal hai, Manjiri! Aur jawab hai: haan, ek raftaar aisi hoti hai. Par wo kitni hai, ye sunke tumhare hosh ud jaayenge. Chalo, hisaab lagate hain."

## Part A: Gend upar jaati hai, aur wapas aati hai

Class 9 mein tumne seekha tha ki upar phenki cheez ki max oonchai **H = u² ÷ (2g)** hoti hai ([gravitation-part4.md](../../../class9/physics/gravitation/gravitation-part4.md)). Fawwara ki dhaar ka hisaab lagate hain. Maan lo sabse oonchi dhaar lagbhag 5 m oonchi gayi.

**Example 1 (fawwara ki dhaar ki raftaar):**

**Given:** H = 5 m, g = 9.8 m/s².
**Find:** nikalne ki raftaar u.
**Formula:** u² = 2 g H.
**Substitution:** u² = 2 × 9.8 m/s² × 5 m = 98 m²/s², to u = **9.9 m/s** (lagbhag 36 km/h).
**Answer:** dhaar lagbhag 10 m/s ki raftaar se nikli.

**Jaanch:** H = u² ÷ (2g) = 98 m²/s² ÷ 19.6 m/s² = 5 m ✅

Ab usi formula se dekho ki zyada raftaar dene par kya hota hai (g ko har jagah 9.8 m/s² maan kar, jaisa Class 9 mein karte the):

| Phenkne ki raftaar | H = u² ÷ (2g) (g ko sthir maanne par) |
|---|---|
| 20 m/s | 20.4 m |
| 100 m/s | 510 m |
| 1000 m/s | lagbhag 51 km |
| 5000 m/s | lagbhag 1276 km |
| 11,200 m/s | 6400 km, yani poori Earth ki radius |

Raftaar badhao, oonchai badhti jaati hai. Par abhi tak **har cheez wapas aa rahi hai**, kyunki Class 9 ke formula ne maana ki g hamesha 9.8 m/s² rahega. Asli baat ye hai ki g **door jaakar ghat-ta hai** (Topic 2). Ye bahut bada farak daalta hai, aur isi se escape velocity ki kahani shuru hoti hai.

**Fawwara ka jawab abhi:** fawwara ki dhaar 10 m/s ki thi. Jo raftaar hum aage nikalenge, wo 11,200 m/s hai: 11,200 ÷ 9.9 ≈ **1130 guna** zyada! Koi pump aisa nahi bana.

## Part B: Gravity door jaakar kamzor hoti hai

Topic 2 ki yaad: Earth ke centre se doori r ho to g ka maan 9.8 m/s² ko (R ÷ r)² se guna karne par milta hai (r = R + h). Isse:

| Centre se doori | g (surface ke g ka hissa) |
|---|---|
| R (surface) | 1 (9.8 m/s²) |
| 2R | 1/4 |
| 3R | 1/9 |
| 4R | 1/16 |
| 10R | 1/100 |

```
   g (surface ke g ka hissa)

   1     ########################################  R   (surface)
   1/4   ##########  2R
   1/9   ####  3R
   1/16  ###  4R
   1/100 .  10R (bahut hi chhota)

   Har # g ke 1/40 hisse ke barabar hai (1/16 aur 1/9 ke liye lagbhag)
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Earth ke centre se doori badhne par g ka hissa (lambai ke hisaab se mota-mota). 4R par g sirf 1/16 reh jaata hai, 10R par 1/100. Ye kabhi zero nahi hota, par bahut chhota ho jaata hai.

Sochne wali baat: kisi cheez ko oopar phenkne par shuru ke kuch hazaar km mein gravity use zor se peeche kheenchti hai. Par jaise jaise wo door jaati hai, kheenchaav **kamzor** hota jaata hai. Agar cheez ke paas itni raftaar ho ki wo kamzor hote kheenchaav ko "haraate" hue door nikalti chali jaaye, to ek mod aata hai jahan gravity use wapas laa hi nahi paati. Wo mod ki raftaar hi **escape velocity** hai.

Ye zaroori baat hai ki gravity ka asar kabhi zero nahi hota, phir bhi cheez bach nikalti hai. Wajah ye hai ki kheenchaav itni tezi se kam hota hai ki **poore safar ka kul "peeche kheenchne ka kaam"** ek sheema tak hi rehta hai, anant nahi hota. Jis cheez ke paas utni gati ki energy ho, wo us kaam ko paar kar leti hai. Ab is baat ko energy ke hisaab mein badalte hain.

## Part C: Energy balance se escape velocity

Class 9 mein tumne dekha tha: oonchai h par potential energy m g h hoti hai ([work-energy-and-power-part5.md](../../../class9/physics/work-energy-and-power/work-energy-and-power-part5.md)), aur free fall mein kinetic plus potential energy barabar rehti hai ([work-energy-and-power-part6.md](../../../class9/physics/work-energy-and-power/work-energy-and-power-part6.md)). Wahi soch yahan lagayenge.

**Soch:** Phenkte waqt cheez ke paas **kinetic energy ½ m v²** hoti hai. Cheez jaise jaise oopar jaati hai, ye energy **gravity se ladne mein kharch** hoti jaati hai. Cheez tab hamesha ke liye nikal jaati hai agar shuruaati kinetic energy us poore kaam ke **barabar ya zyada** ho, jo cheez ko Earth se "bahut door" tak le jaane mein lagta hai.

Ab ye kaam kitna hai? Yahan ek **imaandar baat**: asli hisaab ke liye anant tak ka jod chahiye, jo aage ki class ke ganit (calculus) se hota hai. Hum yahan uska **nateeja** lete hain, aur use ek sundar tareeke se samajhte hain:

> Cheez ko Earth se bahut door tak le jaane ka kaam = **G M m ÷ R**

Aur kyunki g = G M ÷ R² (Class 9), to G M = g R². Isliye:

> G M m ÷ R = g R² m ÷ R = **m g R**

Dekho kitna ajeeb aur sundar nateeja hai: **m g R** wahi kaam hai jo cheez ko **R oonchai tak, poore g par** (g badle bina) uthane mein lagta, yani 6400 km. Gravity kamzor hoti jaati hai, isliye asli safar ka kul kaam sirf utna hi hai jitna **ek Earth-radius ki oonchai tak sthir g par**. Isliye Part A ki table mein 11,200 m/s par H = R aaya tha, aur wahi raftaar escape ki raftaar nikli: ye sanyog nahi, wahi energy hai.

**Ab energy balance:**

> Kinetic energy = door tak le jaane ka kaam
> ½ m v² = G M m ÷ R

Dono taraf se m kaat do (cancel) aur 2 se guna karo:

> v² = 2 G M ÷ R, yani **v = √(2 G M ÷ R)**

Aur G M = g R² daalne par:

> **v = √(2 g R)**

Ye hai **escape velocity** ka formula. Do roop, dono barabar. Dhyan do: poore formula mein **cheez ka mass m kahin nahi bacha**: Part E mein iska matlab dekhenge.

```
   KE shuru mein (surface par)            Door tak le jaane ka kaam
   +----------------------+               +----------------------+
   :        ½ m v²        :     =         :    G M m / R = m g R :
   +----------------------+               +----------------------+
              \                                      /
               \                                    /
                +--> m dono taraf kat jaata hai <--+
                          v = √(2 G M / R) = √(2 g R)
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Energy balance: surface par di gayi gati ki energy aur Earth se bahut door tak le jaane ka kaam barabar rakhne par escape velocity milti hai. Cheez ka mass m dono taraf cancel ho jaata hai.

Safety note: neeche ka hisaab sirf kaagaz par hai. Koi student ya koi bhi is raftaar par kuch phenkne ki koshish nahi karega.

## Part D: Earth ki escape velocity: hisaab

**Example 2 (g aur R se):**

**Given:** g = 9.8 m/s², R = 6.4 × 10⁶ m.
**Find:** Earth ki escape velocity v_e.
**Formula:** v_e = √(2 g R).
**Substitution:** 2 × 9.8 m/s² × 6.4 × 10⁶ m = 1.2544 × 10⁸ m²/s², to v_e = √(1.2544 × 10⁸) = 11,200 m/s.
**Answer:** v_e = **11,200 m/s = 11.2 km/s**.

**Jaanch:** 11,200² = 1.2544 × 10⁸ ✅. Units: √(m/s² × m) = √(m²/s²) = m/s ✅. Ye lagbhag 11.2 × 3600 = 40,320 km/h hai.

**Example 3 (G, M aur R se):**

**Given:** G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg², M = 6 × 10²⁴ kg, R = 6.4 × 10⁶ m.
**Find:** v_e.
**Formula:** v_e = √(2 G M ÷ R).
**Substitution:** 2 G M = 2 × 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ × 6 × 10²⁴ = 8.004 × 10¹⁴ N m²/kg. Is ko R se bhaag karo: 8.004 × 10¹⁴ ÷ 6.4 × 10⁶ = 1.2506 × 10⁸ m²/s². Phir v_e = √(1.2506 × 10⁸) = 11,183 m/s.
**Answer:** v_e = **11.2 km/s** (lagbhag; 11.18 ≈ 11.2).

**Jaanch:** Dono tareeke (Example 2 aur 3) lagbhag ek hi jawab dete hain: 11.20 aur 11.18 km/s ✅. Chhota sa farak M aur R ki rounding se hai.

Ye raftaar kitni badi hai? Fawwara ki dhaar lagbhag 10 m/s thi. Ek tez goli lagbhag 1 km/s ke aas-paas chalti hai (lagbhag), aur ISS lagbhag 7.7 km/s se Earth ka chakkar lagata hai. Escape velocity us se bhi zyada, 11.2 km/s, yani 40,000 km/h se upar.

**Example 4 (kitni energy chahiye):**

**Given:** m = 500 kg ka ek chhota antariksh yaan, v_e = 11,200 m/s, g = 9.8 m/s², R = 6.4 × 10⁶ m.
**Find:** Earth se escape karne ke liye kinetic energy.
**Formula:** KE = ½ m v_e²; check: m g R.
**Substitution:** KE = ½ × 500 kg × (11,200 m/s)² = 250 × 1.2544 × 10⁸ J = 3.136 × 10¹⁰ J.
**Answer:** **3.136 × 10¹⁰ J** (lagbhag 3.1 × 10¹⁰ J).

**Jaanch:** m g R = 500 kg × 9.8 m/s² × 6.4 × 10⁶ m = 3.136 × 10¹⁰ J ✅: dono tareeke barabar, yani energy balance sahi baithta hai.

## Part E: Mass se aur disha se kuch fark nahi

Formula v_e = √(2 g R) mein sirf **g aur R** hain, jo **grah ke** hain. Phenki jaane wali cheez ka mass m kahin nahi hai. Iska matlab:

> Ek kankar aur ek bhaari antariksh yaan, dono ke liye **escape velocity ek hi** hai: Earth par 11.2 km/s.

Ye wahi cheez hai jo Class 9 ke free fall mein dekhi thi: g par cheez ke mass ka asar nahi. Bhaari cheez par gravity zyada kheenchti hai, par use hilana bhi utna hi mushkil hai, to farak cancel ho jaata hai.

**Magar dhyan do, ek zaroori farak:** **raftaar** same hai, par **energy** alag hai. Kinetic energy ½ m v² mein m hai.

**Example 5 (alag mass, ek raftaar):**

**Given:** m₁ = 1 kg, m₂ = 1000 kg, v_e = 11,200 m/s.
**Find:** dono ko escape karwane ki raftaar aur kinetic energy.
**Formula:** v_e wahi, KE = ½ m v_e².
**Substitution:** KE₁ = ½ × 1 × 1.2544 × 10⁸ = 6.272 × 10⁷ J. KE₂ = ½ × 1000 × 1.2544 × 10⁸ = 6.272 × 10¹⁰ J.
**Answer:** raftaar dono ki **11.2 km/s**, par bhaari cheez ko **1000 guna** zyada energy deni padegi.

**Jaanch:** KE₂ ÷ KE₁ = 6.272 × 10¹⁰ ÷ 6.272 × 10⁷ = 1000 = m₂ ÷ m₁ ✅

**Disha ke baare mein:** escape velocity **kisi bhi disha** mein (zameen mein ghusne wali disha chhodkar, jahan se raasta Earth se hi takra jaaye) wahi hai. Seedha oopar ya tirchi, dono mein agar cheez Earth se na takrae, ye raftaar gravity se bachne ke liye kaafi hai. Isliye "escape velocity" ko "escape speed" bhi kehte hain. **Imaandari se do shartein:** hum hawa ka rukawat nahi maan rahe (asli hawa mein cheez jal jaati ya ruk jaati), aur Earth ke apne ghoomne ka asar bhi chhod rahe hain. Asli rocket ko jab poorab ki taraf bheja jaata hai, to Earth ki ghoomti raftaar (bhoomadhya rekha par lagbhag 0.46 km/s) thodi madad kar deti hai: isi liye launch site ka chunaav aur disha dono dhyan se chune jaate hain.

## Part F: Doosre grah, doosri escape velocity

Formula har grah par chalta hai, bas us grah ke M aur R daalo (ya g aur R).

**Example 6 (Moon):**

**Given:** Moon ka M = 7.4 × 10²² kg, R = 1.7 × 10⁶ m, G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg².
**Find:** Moon ki escape velocity.
**Formula:** v_e = √(2 G M ÷ R).
**Substitution:** 2 G M = 2 × 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ × 7.4 × 10²² = 9.872 × 10¹² N m²/kg. R se bhaag: 9.872 × 10¹² ÷ 1.7 × 10⁶ = 5.807 × 10⁶ m²/s². Root: √(5.807 × 10⁶) = 2410 m/s.
**Answer:** **2.4 km/s** (lagbhag).

**Jaanch:** 2410² = 5.808 × 10⁶ ✅. Earth se compare: 2.41 ÷ 11.2 ≈ 0.215, yani lagbhag **1/5**: Moon par escape velocity Earth ki lagbhag ek-paanchva hissa hai.

**Example 7 (ratio se: M aur R badalna):** v_e = √(2 G M ÷ R) mein M upar hai aur R neeche, dono **square-root ke andar**. Isliye v_e ∝ √(M ÷ R).

**Given:** ek grah ka mass Earth ka 4 guna, radius Earth jaisa hi. Earth ka v_e = 11.2 km/s.
**Find:** us grah ki v_e.
**Formula:** v_e (naya) = v_e (Earth) × √(mass guna ÷ radius guna).
**Substitution:** √(4 ÷ 1) = 2, to v_e = 11.2 × 2 = 22.4 km/s.
**Answer:** **22.4 km/s**.

**Jaanch:** M = 4 × 6 × 10²⁴ = 2.4 × 10²⁵ kg se seedha: √(2 × 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ × 2.4 × 10²⁵ ÷ 6.4 × 10⁶) = √(5.0 × 10⁸) = 22,360 m/s ≈ 22.4 km/s ✅

**Example 8 (diye hue M aur R wala grah):**

**Given:** ek grah ka M = 1.2 × 10²⁵ kg, R = 7.2 × 10⁶ m.
**Find:** escape velocity.
**Formula:** v_e = √(2 G M ÷ R).
**Substitution:** 2 G M = 2 × 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ × 1.2 × 10²⁵ = 1.6008 × 10¹⁵. R se bhaag: 1.6008 × 10¹⁵ ÷ 7.2 × 10⁶ = 2.223 × 10⁸ m²/s². Root: 14,911 m/s.
**Answer:** **14.9 km/s**.

**Jaanch:** Ratio se: mass 2 guna, radius 7.2 ÷ 6.4 = 1.125 guna, √(2 ÷ 1.125) = 1.333, 11.2 × 1.333 = 14.93 km/s ✅

Ab chaar jagahon ki escape velocity ek saath dekho:

```
   Escape velocity (1 # = lagbhag 1 km/s)

   Moon     ##  2.4 km/s
   Mars     #####  5.0 km/s
   Earth    ###########  11.2 km/s
   Jupiter  ############################################################  59.5 km/s
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Chaar jagahon ki escape velocity ek hi paimaane par (har # lagbhag 1 km/s). Jupiter ki bahut badi hai: Earth ki lagbhag 5.3 guna.

| Jagah | Escape velocity | Earth ke mukable |
|---|---|---|
| Moon | lagbhag 2.4 km/s | lagbhag 1/5 |
| Mars | lagbhag 5.0 km/s | lagbhag 0.45 guna |
| Earth | lagbhag 11.2 km/s | 1 |
| Jupiter | lagbhag 59.5 km/s | lagbhag 5.3 guna |

Jaanch: 59.5 ÷ 11.2 = 5.31 ✅, 5.0 ÷ 11.2 = 0.446 ✅.

## Part G: Moon par hawa kyun nahi, Jupiter par kyun hai

Class 9 ([matter-in-our-surroundings-part2.md](../../../class9/chemistry/matter-in-our-surroundings/matter-in-our-surroundings-part2.md)) mein dekha tha ki gas ke kan **bahut tezi se** idhar-udhar udte hain. Ek kamre ke taapman (lagbhag 27 °C) par gas ke kan ki **ausat raftaar** lagbhag aisi hoti hai:

| Gas | Kan ki ausat raftaar (lagbhag) |
|---|---|
| Hydrogen (H₂) | 1.9 km/s |
| Helium (He) | 1.4 km/s |
| Nitrogen (N₂) | 0.52 km/s |
| Oxygen (O₂) | 0.48 km/s |
| Carbon dioxide (CO₂) | 0.41 km/s |

Dhyan do: ye **ausat** raftaar hai: kuch kan isse kaafi tez bhi hote hain. Agar kisi kan ki raftaar grah ki escape velocity se zyada ho jaaye aur wo oopar se bahar ki taraf ud raha ho, to wo **hamesha ke liye nikal jaata hai**. Lekin gas ke kano ko bahut dheere-dheere, arbon saalon mein, bahar jaana padta hai. Is liye ek **mote taur ka niyam** (rule of thumb) maana jaata hai:

> Agar gas ke kan ki ausat raftaar grah ki escape velocity ke **1/6 hisse** se kam ho, to grah us gas ko arbon saal tak thaam leta hai. Agar zyada ho, to wo gas dheere-dheere nikal jaati hai.

(Ye niyam lagbhag hai, aur asli duniya mein aur kaaran bhi hote hain, jaise taapman aur Sooraj se aane wali hawa.)

**Example 9 (is niyam se):**

**Given:** Moon v_e = 2.4 km/s, Jupiter v_e = 59.5 km/s.
**Find:** dono jagah ke liye "1/6 ki seema".
**Formula:** seema = v_e ÷ 6.
**Substitution:** Moon: 2.4 ÷ 6 = 0.40 km/s. Jupiter: 59.5 ÷ 6 = 9.9 km/s.
**Answer:** Moon ki seema 0.40 km/s hai: nitrogen (0.52), oxygen (0.48) aur CO₂ (0.41) sab ke kan is seema se **zyada** tez hain, isliye Moon par kisi gas ka thaha rehna mushkil hai: aaj Moon par hawa lagbhag nahi ke barabar hai. Jupiter ki seema 9.9 km/s hai: hydrogen ke kan (1.9 km/s) uske **bahut neeche** hain, isliye Jupiter hydrogen thaam kar rakhta hai.

**Jaanch:** Moon: 0.52 > 0.40 ✅ (nitrogen nikal jaayegi). Jupiter: 1.9 < 9.9 ✅ (hydrogen ruki rahegi).

Earth ke liye seema 11.2 ÷ 6 = 1.87 km/s hai. Nitrogen aur oxygen ke kan iske bahut neeche hain, isliye hawa tikti hai. Hydrogen (1.9 km/s) is seema ke lagbhag barabar hai, aur oopar ki hawa ka taapman zyada hota hai, isliye Earth se halki gas **hydrogen aur helium dheere-dheere** nikalti rehti hai. Aur Mars (v_e lagbhag 5.0 km/s, seema lagbhag 0.83 km/s) par CO₂ ke kan seema se neeche hain: wahan hawa hai, par bahut patli (iska kaaran sirf escape velocity nahi, aur bhi hai).

```
   Moon (seema 0.40 km/s)              Jupiter (seema 9.9 km/s)

   N2, O2, CO2 ke kan                  H2 ke kan (1.9 km/s) seema
   (0.4 se 0.5 km/s) seema             se bahut neeche hain:
   ke upar hain:                       grah ke paas hi rehte hain

      o -->  o -->                          o   o   o
   -----------------------             =======================
   Moon ki sataah                      Jupiter ke baadal
```

> **Figure 3.4** – Moon par gas ke kan escape velocity ki seema se tez hain, isliye wo bahar nikal jaate hain. Jupiter par hydrogen ke kan seema se bahut dheere hain, isliye ruke rehte hain. (Dono taraf kan ke tir sirf dikhane ke liye hain.)

## Part H: Rocket ko 11.2 km/s ek saath nahi chahiye

Ab tak humne ek **goli** ki tarah socha: sataah se ek baar mein 11.2 km/s ki raftaar do, phir koi dhakka nahi. Asli rocket aisa nahi karta. **Imaandari se baat:**

- Rocket **lift-off ke waqt dheere** utha hai (raftaar zero se shuru) aur uska engine **lagatar dhakka** (thrust) deta rehta hai. Powered flight mein cheez 11.2 km/s ek hi baar mein nahi, dheere-dheere haasil karti hai. Dhakka lagta rehta hai, isliye gravity se ladne ka kaam engine hi kar deta hai.
- Phir bhi **kaam ka kul hisaab wahi rehta hai**: kaafi energy chahiye, kyunki gravity ke khilaaf Earth se bahut door jaana hai. 11.2 km/s wo raftaar hai jo engine bandh hone ke baad bhi cheez ko wapas nahi aane deti.
- Siddhant mein ek rocket lagbhag 100 m/s ki raftaar se bhi bahut oopar jaa sakta hai, agar uska engine chalta rahe. (Asli mein itna fuel le jaana mumkin nahi, isliye asli rocket kuch hi minute mein bahut tez raftaar pakad lete hain.) Agar engine bandh ho jaaye, to 11.2 km/s se kam raftaar par wo wapas gir sakta hai.

```
   Goli ki tarah phenka               Rocket (engine chalta rehta hai)
   (ek dhakka, phir kuch nahi)

         O  <-- sabse oopar,              ^  raftaar badhti jaati hai
         :      ruk jaati hai             ^
         :                                ^  engine chalu
         :  oopar jaate hue               ^
         :  raftaar ghat-ti hai           ^
         ^                               /#\
         :                             lift-off: raftaar zero se
   ======O========  zameen              ============  zameen
   ek baar mein phenki, phir           shuru, dheere dheere tez
   gravity wapas laati hai
```

> **Figure 3.5** – Goli ko ek hi dhakka milta hai: oopar jaate hue uski raftaar ghat-ti hai, wo ruk kar wapas aati hai. Rocket zero raftaar se shuru karta hai, par engine lagatar dhakka deta hai, isliye raftaar badhti jaati hai.

**Kahan jaana hai, usse kya farak padta hai:**

- **Satellite ke orbit tak**: raftaar 11.2 km/s nahi, orbit ki raftaar chahiye (Earth ke paas lagbhag 7.9 km/s, Topic 4 mein iska hisaab). Satellite Earth ke gravity ke andar hi rehta hai.
- **Moon tak**: Moon khud Earth ke gravity mein bandha hai, isliye Moon jaane wale yaan ko poori 11.2 km/s se thodi kam raftaar chahiye (lagbhag 11 km/s se thoda kam). Chandrayaan-3 ko 2023 mein LVM3 rocket ne uthaya, aur kai manzilon mein uski raftaar badhai gayi.
- **Mars tak**: Earth ki pakad se nikalna zaroori hai. Mangalyaan ne pehle Earth ke chaaro taraf kai chakkar lagakar apni raftaar badhayi, phir Mars ki taraf nikla (is ke baare mein Topic 5 mein).

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Bhaari cheez ki escape velocity zyada hoti hai"**

Ye sabse aam galti hai. Dekho ek chhota demonstration. Do cheezein: ek 1 kg ka patthar aur ek 1000 kg ka antariksh yaan. Dono ko Earth se hamesha ke liye bhejna hai.

- Formula: v_e = √(2 g R). Isme na 1 kg hai na 1000 kg.
- Dono ke liye v_e = √(2 × 9.8 × 6.4 × 10⁶) = **11,200 m/s**.
- **Bhaari ke liye kya zyada lagta hai?** Raftaar nahi, **energy**. 1 kg ke liye KE = 6.272 × 10⁷ J, aur 1000 kg ke liye 6.272 × 10¹⁰ J. Bhaari yaan ko 1000 guna zyada energy chahiye (Example 5), par uski raftaar wahi 11.2 km/s hi chahiye.

Ek **doosri uljhan** bhi hoti hai: "bhaari **grah** ki escape velocity zyada hoti hai". Ye baat **sahi** hai, kyunki yahan v_e ∝ √(M ÷ R) mein **grah ka** M hai: Jupiter ka M bahut bada hai, isliye uska v_e bhi bada (Figure 3.3). Farak ye hai: **grah ka mass M** formula mein hai, **phenki gayi cheez ka mass m** nahi.

Free fall se bhi yahi baat samjho: Class 9 mein sikka aur pankh vacuum mein ek saath girte the, kyunki g mein m nahi tha. Yahan v_e mein bhi m nahi.

## Quick Recap

- Upar phenki cheez wapas aati hai. Class 9 ka H = u² ÷ (2g) g ko sthir maanta hai; asli mein g door jaakar ghat-ta hai.
- **Escape velocity**: sataah se wo kam se kam raftaar jisse phenki cheez gravity se bach kar kabhi wapas na aaye.
- **Energy balance**: ½ m v² = G M m ÷ R = m g R, jisse **v_e = √(2 G M ÷ R) = √(2 g R)**. (Door tak le jaane ka kaam G M m ÷ R ka honest nateeja hai; poora hisaab aage ki class mein.)
- **Earth ke liye v_e = 11.2 km/s** (lagbhag 40,000 km/h). Moon: lagbhag 2.4 km/s, Mars: lagbhag 5.0 km/s, Jupiter: lagbhag 59.5 km/s.
- v_e **phenki gayi cheez ke mass par depend nahi** karta, aur (hawa aur Earth ke ghoomne ko chhodkar) disha par bhi nahi. Par **energy** ½ m v_e² mass ke saath badhti hai.
- v_e ∝ √(M ÷ R): mass 4 guna to v_e 2 guna; radius 4 guna to v_e aadha.
- Moon ki v_e chhoti hai, isliye hawa ke kan nikal gaye: Moon par hawa nahi. Jupiter ki v_e badi hai, isliye hydrogen ruka hai. (Mote niyam: kan ki ausat raftaar v_e ke 1/6 se kam ho to gas tikti hai.)
- Rocket ko 11.2 km/s ek hi baar mein nahi chahiye: uska engine lagatar dhakka deta hai. Moon tak thodi kam, satellite orbit ke liye lagbhag 7.9 km/s, Mars ke liye Earth ki pakad se nikalna padta hai.

## Practice Questions

1. Mars has g = 3.7 m/s² and radius 3.4 × 10⁶ m. Find the escape velocity from Mars in m/s and in km/s.

2. A 120 kg lander has to escape from the Moon, whose escape velocity is 2.4 km/s. Find the minimum kinetic energy it needs at the surface. How many times larger would the energy be for the same lander on the Earth (v_e = 11.2 km/s)?

3. A planet has mass 3.2 × 10²⁴ kg and radius 4.0 × 10⁶ m. Find its escape velocity. (G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg².)

4. A small asteroid has mass 1.0 × 10¹⁵ kg and radius 5.0 × 10³ m. Find its escape velocity in m/s and in km/h. Would a ball thrown at 30 m/s from its surface ever return (ignore everything else)?

5. Jupiter has about 318 times the mass and 11.2 times the radius of the Earth. Using the ratio method and the Earth's v_e = 11.2 km/s, find Jupiter's escape velocity and compare it with the value 59.5 km/s.

6. A hypothetical planet has 3 times the mass and 2 times the radius of the Earth. Find its escape velocity.

7. A 5 kg probe and a 15 kg probe are to be sent away from the Earth for ever. Compare (a) their escape velocities, and (b) the kinetic energies needed (give the actual values in J).

8. Find the kinetic energy needed to send a 2500 kg spacecraft out of the Earth's pull (use v_e = 11,200 m/s), and check your answer with m g R. (g = 9.8 m/s², R = 6.4 × 10⁶ m.)

9. State True or False:
   (a) The escape velocity from the Earth is different for a stone and for a rocket.
   (b) A planet with a bigger mass (same radius) has a larger escape velocity.
   (c) Ignoring air and the Earth's rotation, a ball thrown up at 12 km/s will never come back.
   (d) The escape velocity from the Moon is larger than from the Earth.
   (e) A rocket must reach 11.2 km/s at the moment of lift-off to leave the Earth.

10. A world has an escape velocity of 4.5 km/s. Use the rule "a gas stays if the average speed of its particles is less than 1/6 of the escape velocity". Particle speeds at room temperature: hydrogen 1.9 km/s, helium 1.4 km/s, nitrogen 0.52 km/s, oxygen 0.48 km/s, carbon dioxide 0.41 km/s. Which of these gases will it keep, and which will it lose?

11. A hose throws a jet of water 20 m high. Find the speed of the water leaving the hose, and say how many times smaller it is than the Earth's escape velocity. (g = 9.8 m/s².)

12. A planet has half the mass and half the radius of the Earth. Find its escape velocity and say what you notice.

13. Explain in your own words why a rocket that rises at only 100 m/s could, in principle, still leave the Earth, while a ball thrown at 100 m/s cannot.
