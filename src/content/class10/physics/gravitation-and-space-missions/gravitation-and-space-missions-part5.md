## Part A: Rocket kaise kaam karta hai

### Gubbare se rocket tak

Class 9 mein tumne dekha tha ki jab bhi ek cheez doosri par force lagati hai, to doosri bhi pehli par **barabar aur ulta** force lagati hai ([force-and-laws-of-motion-part7.md](../../../class9/physics/force-and-laws-of-motion/force-and-laws-of-motion-part7.md)). Gubbara andar ki hawa ko peeche dhakelta hai (action), aur hawa gubbare ko aage dhakelti hai (reaction). Rocket ke liye wohi hai:

- **Action:** rocket ka engine apni garam gas (exhaust) ko bahut tezi se **neeche** phenkta hai.
- **Reaction:** wo gas rocket ko **upar** dhakelti hai. Ye reaction hi **thrust** hai.

```
        ^   thrust (reaction): rocket upar
        :
       /^\
      /   \
     :     :        <-- rocket
     :     :
     +-----+
      \   /
       \ /
        v
        :   exhaust gas (action): neeche, bahut tez
        v
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Rocket aur uski exhaust gas: rocket gas ko neeche dhakelta hai (action) aur gas rocket ko upar dhakelti hai (reaction). Dono force barabar aur ulte hain, par alag cheezon par lagte hain: ek gas par, ek rocket par.

### Momentum ki nazar se

Class 9 ke Topic "Conservation of Momentum" ([force-and-laws-of-motion-part8.md](../../../class9/physics/force-and-laws-of-motion/force-and-laws-of-motion-part8.md)) mein dekha tha ki ek band system ka total momentum pehle aur baad mein same rehta hai. Rocket shuru mein ruka hai, to total momentum **0** hai. Jab wo gas ko neeche phenkta hai (gas ka momentum neeche), to rocket ka momentum utna hi **upar** hona chahiye, taaki jod phir 0 rahe:

> (rocket ka mass) × (rocket ki velocity) = (gas ka mass) × (gas ki velocity)  (ulti disha mein)

Is hisaab ko samajhne ke liye ek chhota, door space mein maana hua toy rocket lete hain, jahan gravity aur hawa ki rukawat ko ignore kar rahe hain.

**Example 1 (toy rocket ki recoil speed)**

**Sawaal:** Space mein ruka hua toy rocket (gas nikalne ke baad bacha mass 1.5 kg) apne engine se 0.05 kg gas 60 m/s ki speed se peeche phenkta hai. Rocket kitni speed se aage jayega?

- **Given:** gas ka mass m₁ = 0.05 kg, gas ki speed v₁ = 60 m/s, rocket ka mass m₂ = 1.5 kg.
- **Find:** rocket ki speed v₂.
- **Formula:** shuru mein total momentum 0 hai, to m₂ v₂ = m₁ v₁ (sirf maan lete hain, disha ulti hai).
- **Substitution:** gas ka momentum = 0.05 kg × 60 m/s = 3 kg m/s. v₂ = 3 kg m/s ÷ 1.5 kg.
- **Answer:** v₂ = **2 m/s** (gas ki ulti disha mein).

**Jaanch:** rocket ka momentum = 1.5 kg × 2 m/s = 3 kg m/s, gas ke momentum 3 kg m/s ke barabar aur ulta ✅. Unit: kg m/s ÷ kg = m/s ✅.

**Ek honest baat:** asli rocket ek hi baar mein gas nahi phenkta: wo **lagatar** phenkta rehta hai, aur jaise jaise propellant jalta hai, rocket ka mass kam hota jaata hai. Ye Example 1 sirf "ek jhatke" wala sidha hisaab hai, jo idea dikhata hai.

### Thrust: kitna zor?

Class 9 mein Newton ka doosra law bhi aaya tha: **force = momentum ke badalne ki dar** ([force-and-laws-of-motion-part6.md](../../../class9/physics/force-and-laws-of-motion/force-and-laws-of-motion-part6.md)). Engine har second **kuch kilogram gas** ek speed se bahar phenkta hai. Isliye har second gas ko kitna momentum diya, wahi gas par force hai, aur wahi (ulti disha mein) rocket par **thrust** hai:

> **Thrust = (har second nikalne wali gas ka mass) × (exhaust ki speed)**

Yaani thrust ko zyada karne ke do raaste: **zyada gas har second**, ya **gas ko aur tez phenkna**.

**Example 2 (thrust nikalna)**

**Sawaal:** Ek chhote test engine se har second 2 kg gas 500 m/s ki speed se peeche nikalti hai. Thrust kitna hai?

- **Given:** mass flow = 2 kg/s, exhaust speed = 500 m/s.
- **Find:** thrust F.
- **Formula:** F = (mass flow) × (exhaust speed).
- **Substitution:** F = 2 kg/s × 500 m/s.
- **Answer:** F = **1000 N**.

**Jaanch:** unit: kg/s × m/s = kg m/s² = N ✅. Back-substitution: har second gas ko 2 kg × 500 m/s = 1000 kg m/s ka momentum mila, yani 1000 kg m/s ÷ 1 s = 1000 N ✅.

### Kya thrust rocket ko utha dega?

Rocket tabhi upar uthta hai jab thrust uske **weight (m g) se zyada** ho. Phir bacha hua force (net force) F = m a se acceleration deta hai.

**Example 3 (lift-off)**

**Sawaal:** Ek rocket ka mass 2000 kg hai aur uska thrust 30,000 N hai. Kya wo uthega? Uthega to uski shuruaati acceleration kitni hai? (g = 9.8 m/s²)

- **Given:** m = 2000 kg, thrust = 30,000 N, g = 9.8 m/s².
- **Find:** weight, net force, a.
- **Formula:** weight W = m g; net force = thrust − W; a = net force ÷ m.
- **Substitution:** W = 2000 kg × 9.8 m/s² = 19,600 N. Net force = 30,000 N − 19,600 N = 10,400 N. a = 10,400 N ÷ 2000 kg.
- **Answer:** thrust (30,000 N) weight (19,600 N) se zyada hai, to wo uthega. a = **5.2 m/s²** (upar).

**Jaanch:** 2000 kg × 5.2 m/s² = 10,400 N, jo net force ke barabar hai ✅. Agar thrust sirf 15,000 N hota (< 19,600 N), to rocket pad par hi rehta ✅.

Ek dilchasp baat: jaise jaise propellant jalta hai, **m kam** hota jaata hai, to wahi thrust zyada acceleration dene lagta hai. Isliye rocket pehle dheere uthta hai aur phir bahut tez ho jaata hai.

## Part B: Rocket ko hawa kyun nahi chahiye?

Kalyani ka doosra sawaal tha: space mein hawa nahi, to dhakka kis par? Upar ki kahani mein jawab dikh gaya: rocket **gas ko dhakelta hai, hawa ko nahi**. Dhakka rocket aur uski apni gas ke beech ka hai. Bahar hawa ho ya na ho, dhakke par koi farak nahi padta. Hawa ho to bas thodi rukawat (air resistance) daalti hai.

Ek aur cheez jo ghar ke gubbare mein nahi dikhti: asli rocket jalane ke liye **oxygen bhi apne saath** rakhta hai. Hawai jahaaz ka jet engine bahar ki hawa se oxygen leta hai, isliye wo bahut oonchai par kaam nahi karta. Rocket ke propellant mein eendhan aur oxidiser dono hote hain, isliye space ke khaali mein bhi engine jal sakta hai.

## Part C: Multi-stage rocket: bojh phenkte jaana

Ek rocket ka zyadatar mass uske **propellant** ka hota hai, aur jab propellant jal jaata hai, to khaali tanki aur bada engine sirf ek **bojh** rah jaata hai. Agar rocket us bojh ko poora saath le jaaye, to usse bhi utni hi gravity khinchegi. Isliye rocket kai **stage** mein banta hai: jab ek stage ka propellant khatam ho jaata hai, to wo stage **alag karke gira diya jaata hai** aur agla stage apna engine chalata hai.

Kyun fayda hota hai? Newton ka doosra law: a = (thrust − m g) ÷ m. Thrust wahi, par m kam hua, to a zyada.

**Example 4 (stage girane ka fayda)**

**Sawaal:** Ek rocket ka mass 600 kg hai aur thrust 8000 N hai (g = 9.8 m/s²). Uski khaali ek tanki (200 kg) alag karke gira di jaati hai, aur thrust 8000 N hi rehta hai. Girane se pehle aur baad ki acceleration nikalo.

- **Given:** thrust = 8000 N, m₁ = 600 kg, m₂ = 600 − 200 = 400 kg, g = 9.8 m/s².
- **Find:** a₁ aur a₂.
- **Formula:** a = (thrust − m g) ÷ m.
- **Substitution (pehle):** weight = 600 × 9.8 = 5880 N. Net = 8000 − 5880 = 2120 N. a₁ = 2120 N ÷ 600 kg.
- **Substitution (baad mein):** weight = 400 × 9.8 = 3920 N. Net = 8000 − 3920 = 4080 N. a₂ = 4080 N ÷ 400 kg.
- **Answer:** a₁ = **3.53 m/s²**, a₂ = **10.2 m/s²** (lagbhag teen guna).

**Jaanch:** 600 kg × 3.53 m/s² = 2118 N ≈ 2120 N ✅ aur 400 kg × 10.2 m/s² = 4080 N ✅. Mass sirf 1/3 kam hua par acceleration lagbhag **3 guna** ho gayi, kyunki net force ka bada hissa bhi ghatne wale weight se badla ✅.

```
   Stage 3  (upar, satellite wala)      <-- aakhri, sabse halka
   -----------
   Stage 2                              <-- ek khaali stage gir gaya
   -----------
   Stage 1  (neeche, sabse bada)        <-- pehle jalta hai,
   xxxxxxxx                                 phir alag hokar gir jaata hai
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Teen stage ka rocket (sankshep mein). Sabse neeche wala bada stage pehle propellant jalata hai aur khaali hote hi alag hokar gir jaata hai; phir doosra, phir teesra. Har stage ke baad bacha hua rocket halka hota jaata hai.

### Rocket ko 7.9 km/s kaise mile?

Topic 4 mein dekha tha ki orbit mein sideways speed lagbhag 7.9 km/s ke aaspaas chahiye. Rocket ki exhaust speed isse kam hoti hai (lagbhag 2.5 se 4.5 km/s). Phir bhi rocket 7.9 km/s kaise pahunchta hai? Do kaaran: (1) engine **lagatar** gas phenkta rehta hai, to rocket ki speed badhti jaati hai, ek jhatke mein nahi; (2) **stage girne** se mass kam hota jaata hai. Isliye rocket ko Earth se jaate hue kabhi bhi ek hi pal mein 11.2 km/s ki zaroorat nahi padti (Topic 3 mein jis escape speed ki baat thi): wo dheere dheere upar badhte hue bhi door ja sakta hai, jab tak engine thrust deta rahe.

## Part F: Bharat ke antariksh mission

Bharat ke space karyakram ki shuruaat **ISRO** (Indian Space Research Organisation) ne ki, jo **1969** mein bana. Dr. Vikram Sarabhai ko Bharat ke antariksh karyakram ka janak (father) kaha jaata hai. Rocket chhodne ki jagah **Sriharikota** hai, jo Andhra Pradesh mein hai.

```
    1969    1975       2008           2013-14      2019           2023
    :       :          :              :            :              :
    o-------o----------o--------------o------------o--------------o----> saal
    ISRO    Aryabhata  Chandrayaan-1  Mangalyaan   Chandrayaan-2  Chandrayaan-3
    bana    (pehla                                                Aditya-L1
            satellite)
```

> **Figure 5.4** – Bharat ke antariksh karyakram ki sankshep timeline (sahi kram mein, par saalon ka beech ka faasla scale mein nahi). Gaganyaan abhi taiyari mein hai, isliye timeline par nahi.

**Mission ek-ek karke:**

- **Aryabhata (1975):** Bharat ka **pehla satellite**. Ise Soviet Union se chhodha gaya tha (Bharat ke apne rocket abhi nahi bane the). Aaj ke satellite ke mukable mein ye chhota tha, par ek bahut bada pehla kadam tha.
- **Chandrayaan-1 (2008):** Bharat ka pehla Moon mission. Isne Moon par **paani ke molecules ke sanket** (signs of water molecules) dhoondhe, ek probe ke saath jo Moon ki sataah par takrayi.
- **Mangalyaan, yaani Mars Orbiter Mission (launch November 2013, Mars ke orbit mein September 2014):** PSLV se chhodha gaya, lagbhag 10 mahine ka safar. **Bharat pehla desh bana jo pehli hi koshish mein Mars ke orbit mein pahuncha.** Iska kharch lagbhag 450 crore rupaye tha.
- **Chandrayaan-2 (2019):** Orbiter Moon ke orbit mein pahunch gaya aur kaam karta raha. Vikram lander surakshit utar nahi paya (Aam galtiyan mein is par ek demonstration hai).
- **Chandrayaan-3 (launch July 2023, LVM3 se):** **23 August 2023** ko Moon ke **south polar region** ke paas soft landing hui. Bharat chautha desh bana jisne Moon par soft landing ki, aur south pole ke paas utarne wala pehla desh.
- **Aditya-L1 (September 2023):** Sun ko dekhne wala mission. Ye Earth se lagbhag **15 lakh km (1.5 million km)** door Lagrange point **L1** par jaata hai, jahan se Sun ko bina rok-tok ke dekha ja sakta hai. January 2024 mein ye apne halo orbit mein pahuncha.
- **Gaganyaan:** Bharat ka **taiyaar ho raha** (planned) maanav antariksh karyakram, jisme insaan ko space bheja jaayega. Ye abhi **taiyari mein** hai.

**Example 5 (Aditya-L1 kitni door hai?)**

**Sawaal:** Aditya-L1 ka L1 point Earth se lagbhag 1.5 × 10⁹ m (15 lakh km) door hai. Earth-Sun ki doori 1 AU = 1.5 × 10¹¹ m hai aur Earth-Moon ki doori 3.84 × 10⁸ m. L1 Sun ki taraf Earth-Sun doori ka kitna hissa hai, aur Moon ki doori ka kitna guna?

- **Given:** d(L1) = 1.5 × 10⁹ m, 1 AU = 1.5 × 10¹¹ m, d(Moon) = 3.84 × 10⁸ m.
- **Find:** d(L1) ÷ 1 AU aur d(L1) ÷ d(Moon).
- **Formula:** ratio = chhoti doori ÷ badi doori (ya ulta).
- **Substitution:** 1.5 × 10⁹ ÷ 1.5 × 10¹¹ = 0.01. 1.5 × 10⁹ ÷ 3.84 × 10⁸ = 3.9.
- **Answer:** L1 Earth-Sun doori ka sirf **1 percent** hai, aur Moon ki doori ka **lagbhag 4 guna**.

**Jaanch:** 0.01 × 1.5 × 10¹¹ m = 1.5 × 10⁹ m ✅ aur 3.9 × 3.84 × 10⁸ m = 1.5 × 10⁹ m ✅. Matlab Aditya-L1 Sun ke "paas" nahi, Moon se thoda hi door (4 guna) hai: Sun abhi bhi lagbhag 99 percent door!

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Rocket ko dhakelne ke liye hawa chahiye, isliye space mein wo nahi chalega."**

Galat. Rocket hawa ko nahi, **apni hi gas ko** dhakelta hai. Ek demonstration: space mein ek astronaut (suit samet 78 kg) ke haath mein 2 kg ka thaila hai, aur wo use 3 m/s ki speed se peeche phenk deta hai. Aas-paas kuch nahi hai, na hawa, na zameen. Kya astronaut ko phir bhi koi speed milegi?

- Thaile ka momentum = 2 kg × 3 m/s = 6 kg m/s (peeche).
- Astronaut ka momentum utna hi aage: v = 6 kg m/s ÷ 78 kg = **0.077 m/s** (lagbhag, aage ki taraf, thaile ki ulti disha mein).

**Jaanch:** 78 kg × 0.077 m/s = 6.0 kg m/s ✅. Astronaut ko aage ki taraf speed mil gayi, jabki wo kisi cheez par "tika" nahi tha. Rocket bhi yahi karta hai, bas hazaaron kg gas aur bahut tez speed ke saath. Isliye hawa ke bina bhi rocket chalta hai (aur hawa na ho to rukawat bhi nahi).

**Galti 2: "Chandrayaan-2 Moon tak pahunch kar bhi lander toot gaya, isliye poora mission fail ho gaya."**

Zyadatar log ek pal dekh kar poora mission tay kar dete hain. Mission ke **teen hisse** the, aur sab ek jaise nahi gaye:

- **Launch aur Moon tak ka safar:** ho gaya. Orbiter Moon ke orbit mein pahuncha.
- **Orbiter:** Moon ke chaaro taraf ghoom kar kaam karta raha, aur uska data milta raha.
- **Lander (Vikram):** soft landing mein safal nahi hua.

Yaani ek hissa (lander) apne aim tak nahi pahuncha, par baaki hissa **chalta raha**. Aur is anubhav se seekh kar hi 2023 mein Chandrayaan-3 ne soft landing kar dikhayi. Isliye ise "poora fail" nahi, ek mission jisne kuch haasil kiya aur kuch seekha kehna sahi hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Rocket ka principle:** action-reaction aur momentum conservation: rocket gas ko peeche phenkta hai, gas rocket ko aage dhakelti hai.
- **Thrust = (har second nikalne wali gas ka mass) × (exhaust speed)**, unit N. Rocket tab uthta hai jab thrust weight (m g) se zyada ho.
- **Hawa ki zaroorat nahi:** rocket apni gas ko dhakelta hai, aur apna oxidiser saath rakhta hai.
- **Multi-stage:** khaali stage gira kar mass ghatate hain, to wahi thrust zyada acceleration deta hai (Example 4: 3.53 se 10.2 m/s²).
- **Propellant:** solid (simple), liquid (control), cryogenic (sabse tez exhaust, bahut thanda).
- **Launch vehicle:** SLV, SSLV, PSLV (chaar stage, Mangalyaan), GSLV, LVM3 (Chandrayaan-3).
- **Mission:** ISRO 1969, Aryabhata 1975, Chandrayaan-1 2008, Mangalyaan 2013-14, Chandrayaan-2 2019, Chandrayaan-3 2023 (south pole ke paas), Aditya-L1 2023 (L1 lagbhag 15 lakh km), Gaganyaan taiyari mein.
- **Satellite ke fayde:** communication, mausam, cyclone aur machhuaaron ki chetavni, remote sensing, navigation (NavIC).
- **Safety:** asli rocket aur pataakha rocket student ke liye nahi.

### Answers

1. **Given:** m₁ = 0.03 kg, v₁ = 90 m/s, m₂ = 0.6 kg. **Find:** v₂. **Formula:** m₂ v₂ = m₁ v₁. **Substitution:** gas ka momentum = 0.03 kg × 90 m/s = 2.7 kg m/s; v₂ = 2.7 kg m/s ÷ 0.6 kg. **Answer:** v₂ = **4.5 m/s** (gas ki ulti disha mein).
   Jaanch: 0.6 kg × 4.5 m/s = 2.7 kg m/s ✅

2. **Given:** mass flow = 4 kg/s, v = 2500 m/s. **Formula:** thrust = mass flow × speed. **Substitution:** 4 kg/s × 2500 m/s. **Answer:** **10,000 N**.
   Jaanch: kg/s × m/s = kg m/s² = N ✅

3. **Given:** F = 12,000 N, v = 3000 m/s. **Find:** mass flow. **Formula:** mass flow = F ÷ v. **Substitution:** 12,000 N ÷ 3000 m/s. **Answer:** **4 kg/s**.
   Jaanch: 4 kg/s × 3000 m/s = 12,000 N ✅

4. **Given:** m = 900 kg, thrust = 12,000 N, g = 10 m/s². **Find:** weight, a. **Formula:** W = m g, a = (thrust − W) ÷ m. **Substitution:** W = 900 × 10 = 9000 N; net = 12,000 − 9000 = 3000 N; a = 3000 N ÷ 900 kg. **Answer:** thrust weight se zyada hai, to rocket **uthega**; a = **3.33 m/s²**.
   Jaanch: 900 kg × 3.33 m/s² = 2997 N ≈ 3000 N ✅

5. **Pehle:** W = 400 × 10 = 4000 N, net = 6000 − 4000 = 2000 N, a₁ = 2000 ÷ 400 = **5 m/s²**. **Baad mein:** m = 300 kg, W = 3000 N, net = 6000 − 3000 = 3000 N, a₂ = 3000 ÷ 300 = **10 m/s²**. Ratio = 10 ÷ 5 = **2 guna**.
   Jaanch: 400 kg × 5 m/s² = 2000 N ✅ aur 300 kg × 10 m/s² = 3000 N ✅

6. 450 crore = 450 × 10⁷ rupaye = 4.5 × 10² × 10⁷ = **4.5 × 10⁹ rupaye**.
   Jaanch: 4.5 × 10⁹ ÷ 10⁷ = 450 crore ✅

7. **Formula:** time = doori ÷ speed. Aditya-L1: 1.5 × 10⁹ m ÷ 3 × 10⁸ m/s = **5 s**. Mangalyaan: 2.25 × 10¹¹ m ÷ 3 × 10⁸ m/s = **750 s = 12.5 minute**. Command pahunchne mein hi 12.5 minute lagte hain (aur jawab aane mein utne hi aur), isliye Mars orbiter ko pehle se bheje gaye commands aur apne computer par chalna padta hai.
   Jaanch: 3 × 10⁸ m/s × 750 s = 2.25 × 10¹¹ m ✅

8. Tool ka momentum = 1.5 kg × 8 m/s = 12 kg m/s. Astronaut ki speed = 12 kg m/s ÷ 90 kg = **0.133 m/s** (lagbhag 0.13 m/s), tool ki disha se **ulti** disha mein.
   Jaanch: 90 kg × 0.133 m/s = 12 kg m/s ✅

9. (a) **False** ❌. Rocket apni gas ko dhakelta hai, hawa ko nahi.
   (b) **True** ✅. Thrust ek force hai.
   (c) **True** ✅.
   (d) **True** ✅. a = (thrust − m g) ÷ m, m kam hone par a badhti hai.
   (e) **False** ❌. Orbiter kaam karta raha.
   (f) **True** ✅. Jaise liquid hydrogen aur liquid oxygen.

10. Rocket apna eendhan aur oxidiser saath rakhta hai aur gas ko peeche phenkta hai, jo rocket ko aage dhakelti hai (action-reaction): hawa ki zaroorat nahi. Jet aeroplane ke engine ko jalne ke liye bahar ki hawa se oxygen chahiye, jo space mein nahi hai.
