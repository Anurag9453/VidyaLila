# Topic 1: Kepler Ke Teen Niyam: Planets Ki Chaal

Class 9 ke Gravitation chapter mein hamne seekha ki gravity ek universal force hai: F = G m₁m₂/r². Newton ke cannon se ye bhi dekha tha ki Chaand aur satellite girte girte Earth ko miss karte rehte hain, aur ye ki satellite jitna paas, utna tez aur utna kam time lagata hai. Tab ek baat adhoori chhodi gayi thi: "uska poora hisaab aage ki class mein aayega." Ye chapter wahi hisaab poora karta hai: g height aur depth ke saath kaise badalta hai, kisi cheez ko hamesha ke liye door phenkne ke liye kitni raftaar chahiye, satellite kitna tez aur kitne time mein ghoomta hai, aur rocket aur Bharat ke antariksh mission kaise kaam karte hain.

Shuruaat Sooraj ke planets se karte hain. Newton se pehle ek insaan ne planets ki chaal ke **aankhon se dekhe hue numbers** se teen seedhe niyam nikaale the. Wahi teen niyam aage satellite aur Moon mission ki neev bane. Aaj ka topic unhi ka hai.

## Part A: Tycho Brahe aur Kepler ki kahani

Aaj hum Sooraj ko planets ka kendra maante hain, par ye baat insaan ko seedhi nahi mili. Copernicus ne 1543 mein kaha ki planet Sooraj ke chaaro taraf ghoomte hain, par wo bhi maanta tha ki raaste bilkul circle hain.

Phir Denmark ke **Tycho Brahe** (1546 se 1601) aaye. Unke paas telescope nahi tha (telescope tab tak bana hi nahi tha), par unhone saalon tak (lagbhag 20 saal) apni observatory mein badhiya yantron se planets ki jagah note ki: **Mars kis raat aasmaan mein kahan tha**, har raat, har mahine, har saal. Unke naap itne saaf the ki galti lagbhag **ek arcminute** (ek degree ka 60va hissa) ke aas-paas thi. Bina telescope ke ye us zamaane ka sabse achha data tha.

Tycho ke baad ye data unke saathi **Johannes Kepler** (1571 se 1630) ke haath aaya. Kepler ne saalon tak Mars ke numbers par hisaab lagaya. Pehle unhone wahi purana raasta socha: circle. Hisaab milaya to circle se nikli Mars ki jagah aur Tycho ke numbers mein lagbhag **8 arcminute** ka farak aaya. 8 arcminute matlab 8 ÷ 60 = 0.13 degree, jo poorne Chaand ke aakaar (lagbhag 0.5 degree) ka lagbhag **chauthai** hai.

Kepler ke paas bahaana tha: "itna chhota farak hai, chhodo." Par wo jaante the ki Tycho ke numbers itne achhe hain ki 8 arcminute ka farak naap ki galti nahi ho sakta. Unhone kaha: "ye mere circle ki galti hai." Aakhir mein circle chhod kar ellipse try kiya, aur data **bilkul baith gaya**. Isi se teen niyam nikle: pehle do **1609** mein aur teesra **1619** mein chhape.

Baad mein **Newton** (1687) ne dikhaya ki ye teeno niyam gravity ke universal law ka natija hain. Yaad rakho: **Kepler ne data se niyam nikaale, Newton ne un niyamon ki wajah batayi.**

```
   1543          1576 se 1597       1609          1619          1687
    :                 :               :             :             :
 Copernicus     Tycho Brahe       Kepler ke      Kepler ka     Newton ka
 Sooraj ke      ke naap           pehle do       teesra        gravity ka
 chaaro taraf   (bina telescope   niyam          niyam         law: Kepler
 planet         ke, saalon tak)                                 ki wajah
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Kepler ke niyamon ki kahani ek time-line par: Copernicus se Newton tak. Beech mein Tycho Brahe ka data aur Kepler ka hisaab hai.

## Part B: Pehla niyam: planet ka raasta ellipse, Sooraj ek focus par

> **Kepler ka pehla niyam:** Har planet Sooraj ke chaaro taraf ek **ellipse** mein ghoomta hai, aur **Sooraj ellipse ke ek focus par** hota hai.

Dhyan do: Sooraj ellipse ke **centre** par nahi hai: wo ek **focus** par hai. Doosra focus khaali hai, wahan kuch nahi hota.

```
                      .................
                .......               .......
             ....                           ....
          ....                                 ....
        ...                                       ...
       ..                                           ..
      ..                                             ..
      .                                               .
      A-----+-----------------C-----------------S-----P
      .                                               .
      ..                                             ..
       ..                                           ..
        ...                                       ...
          ....                                 ....
             ....                           ....
                .......               .......
                      .................
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Planet ka ellipse. A = aphelion (Sooraj se sabse door), P = perihelion (Sooraj ke sabse paas), S = Sooraj (ek focus), + = doosra, khaali focus, C = ellipse ka centre. Dekho: S, centre C se door hai aur P ke paas hai. Samjhane ke liye ellipse yahan kaafi dabaa banaya gaya hai (e lagbhag 0.75); asli planets ke raaste isse bahut kam dabe hain.

Drawing se do baatein saaf hain:
- Ellipse ka lamba dhura A se P tak hai. Uska aadha hissa **a** (semi-major axis) hai. Kepler ke teesre niyam mein planet ki "doori" ka matlab yahi a hai: **a = (perihelion doori + aphelion doori) ÷ 2**.
- Planet Sooraj se kabhi paas (P ke aas-paas), kabhi door (A ke aas-paas) hota hai.

### Khud banao: dhaage aur do pin se ellipse (ghar par adult ke saath)

Chahiye: cardboard, uspar tika kaagaz, do pin, pencil, lagbhag 20 cm ka dhaaga (dono sire baandh kar chhalla), scale. **Pin lagane ka kaam adult ke saath karo**: pin cardboard mein pakki lagao aur pencil chalate waqt ungliyan pin se door rakho.

1. Kaagaz par lagbhag 6 cm door do jagah chuno. Adult ke saath wahan do pin cardboard mein lagao. Ye dono **focus** hain.
2. Dhaage ka chhalla dono pin ke upar daalo.
3. Pencil ki nok chhalle ke andar rakho aur use itna kheencho ki dhaaga **tana** ho jaaye: pencil ki nok se dono pin tak dhaaga seedha hona chahiye, jaise ek tana hua triangle.
4. Dhaage ko tana rakhte hue pencil ko dheere dheere ghumao. Lakeer ek **ellipse** banayegi.

```
                       pencil ki nok
                             X
                            / \
                 r1        /   \       r2
                          /     \
                         /       \
                       (o)-------(o)
                     pin F1  d   pin F2
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Dhaage aur do pin se ellipse. Chhalle ki lambai L = d + r1 + r2 hai, aur L aur d fixed hain, isliye pencil ki nok X ke liye **r1 + r2 hamesha ek jaisa** rehta hai.

Ye ellipse ki asli pehchaan hai: **ellipse ke har point se dono focus tak ki doori ka jod ek jaisa hota hai.** Do pin = do focus.

**Example 1 (dhaage wale ellipse ke numbers):** Chhalle ki lambai L = 20 cm hai aur dono pin ke beech d = 6 cm. r1 + r2, a, aur b nikalo. (b = ellipse ke chhote dhure ka aadha hissa.)

- Chhalle se: d + r1 + r2 = L, to r1 + r2 = 20 cm − 6 cm = **14 cm**.
- Lambe dhure ke sire par pencil ho to uski dono pin se doori a − c aur a + c hoti hai (c = centre se focus ki doori = d ÷ 2 = 3 cm). Inka jod 2a = r1 + r2 = 14 cm, to **a = 7 cm**.
- Chhota dhura: ellipse ke upar ya neeche ke sabse door point par r1 = r2 = a = 7 cm hota hai, to b = √(a² − c²) = √(49 − 9) = √40 = **6.32 cm** (lagbhag).

**Jaanch:** Lambe dhure ke sire par doori 7 − 3 = 4 cm aur 7 + 3 = 10 cm hain, 4 + 10 = 14 cm = r1 + r2 ✅. Chhote dhure ke sire par 3² + 6.32² = 9 + 40 = 49 = 7² ✅.

### Eccentricity: kitna dabaa hua?

Ellipse kitna dabaa hai, ye ek chhote number se batate hain: **eccentricity e = c ÷ a** (focus ki centre se doori ÷ a). Jab c = 0 hota hai, dono focus ek hi jagah (centre par) aa jaate hain, to e = 0 aur ellipse **circle** ban jaata hai (ek pin wali baat!). Focus jitne door, ellipse utna lamba aur patla, aur e 1 ke paas pahunchta hai.

Sooraj se sabse paas aur sabse door ki doori ke liye seedhe formula: **perihelion = a (1 − e)** aur **aphelion = a (1 + e)**.

**Example 2 (dhaage wale ellipse ka e):** Example 1 ke ellipse ka e nikalo.

- e = c ÷ a = 3 cm ÷ 7 cm = **0.43** (lagbhag)

**Jaanch:** a (1 − e) = 7 × 4/7 = 4 cm aur a (1 + e) = 7 × 10/7 = 10 cm: ye wahi 4 cm aur 10 cm hain jo Example 1 ke Jaanch mein aaye the ✅.

Ab asli planets. Kai planets ke e bahut chhote hain, isliye unka raasta **lagbhag circle** jaisa dikhta hai.

| Planet | Eccentricity e (lagbhag) | Kitna dabaa |
|---|---|---|
| Venus | 0.007 | circle se alag dikhna mushkil |
| Earth | 0.017 | lagbhag circle |
| Mars | 0.093 | thoda dabaa |
| Mercury | 0.21 | saaf dabaa |
| Halley ka comet (sirf naam) | lagbhag 0.97 | bahut lamba aur patla |

**Example 3 (Mercury ka raasta):** Mercury ke liye a = 0.39 AU aur e = 0.21 (lagbhag) hai. Perihelion aur aphelion nikalo.

- Perihelion = a (1 − e) = 0.39 × (1 − 0.21) = 0.39 × 0.79 = **0.31 AU**
- Aphelion = a (1 + e) = 0.39 × 1.21 = **0.47 AU**

**Jaanch:** (0.31 + 0.47) ÷ 2 = 0.39 AU = a ✅. Mercury Sooraj se kabhi 0.31 AU aur kabhi 0.47 AU door hota hai: farak 0.16 AU, yani a ka lagbhag 40 percent.

## Part C: Doosra niyam: barabar time mein barabar area

> **Kepler ka doosra niyam:** Sooraj aur planet ko jodne wali rekha **barabar time mein barabar area** saaf karti hai.

Kalpana karo ki Sooraj se planet tak ek lambi rassi bandhi hai. Planet chalta hai to rassi jhaadu ki tarah ghoomti hai aur pie ke tukde jaisa ek wedge "jhaad" deti hai. Niyam kehta hai: **ek mahine mein rassi ne jitna area jhaada, agle mahine mein bhi utna hi jhaadegi**, chaahe planet ellipse ke kisi bhi hisse mein ho.

Ab dhyan do. Jab planet Sooraj ke paas hota hai, rassi chhoti hai. Itne chhote radius ke wedge ko utna hi area dene ke liye wedge **chaudaa** banana padta hai: yaani planet ko arc par zyada door chalna padta hai. Jab planet door hota hai, rassi lambi hai, to wedge **patla** hota hai: arc chhota. Isliye:

- **Perihelion ke paas planet tez chalta hai.**
- **Aphelion ke paas planet dheere chalta hai.**

```
                      .................
                .......               ..2:...
             ....                        :: ....
          ....                            ::   ....
        ...                                ::     ...
       ..                                   ::      ..
      .3::::::::::                           ::      ..
      .           ::::::::::::::::::::         :      .
      .                               ::::::::::S     .
      .           ::::::::::::::::::::         :      .
      .4::::::::::                           ::      ..
       ..                                   ::      ..
        ...                                ::     ...
          ....                            ::   ....
             ....                        :: ....
                .......               ..1:...
                      .................
```

> **Figure 1.4** – Barabar time, barabar area. S = Sooraj. Planet ellipse par ghadi ki ulti disha mein chal raha hai. Wedge S-1-P-2 (perihelion P wali taraf, arc 1 se P hokar 2 tak) aur wedge S-3-A-4 (aphelion A wali taraf, arc 3 se A hokar 4 tak) ka area barabar hai, to planet 1 se 2 aur 3 se 4 tak barabar time mein pahunchta hai. Perihelion ke paas ka arc (1 se 2) bahut lamba hai, aphelion ke paas ka arc (3 se 4) chhota (arcs 1 se 2 ke beech P ka raasta aur 3 se 4 ke beech A ka raasta hai): isliye wahan planet tez, yahan dheere chalta hai. (Drawing ke liye ellipse kaafi dabaa banaya gaya hai.)

### Do points par seedha hisaab

Perihelion aur aphelion par planet ki chaal Sooraj ki rekha ke bilkul seedhe-kone (90°) par hoti hai. Kam time t mein planet lagbhag v t doori chalta hai, aur wo chhota wedge ek patle triangle jaisa hai: **area ≈ ½ × (radius r) × (v t)**. Barabar time mein barabar area ka matlab:

> r_perihelion × v_perihelion = r_aphelion × v_aphelion

Yaani in do points par **speed doori ke ulte anupaat mein** hai: doori jitni kam, speed utni zyada. (Ye seedha rule sirf perihelion aur aphelion par chalta hai, kyunki sirf wahin chaal radius ke seedhe-kone par hoti hai.)

**Example 4 (ek kalpit planet):** Ek kalpit planet Z ka perihelion 1.0 AU aur aphelion 4.0 AU hai. Aphelion par uski speed 5 km/s hai. Perihelion par speed kitni hogi?

- v_perihelion = v_aphelion × (r_aphelion ÷ r_perihelion) = 5 km/s × (4.0 ÷ 1.0) = **20 km/s**

**Jaanch:** 1.0 AU × 20 km/s = 20 aur 4.0 AU × 5 km/s = 20: dono guna barabar hain ✅. Perihelion par doori 4 guna kam hai, to speed 4 guna zyada: 20 ÷ 5 = 4 ✅.

**Example 5 (Earth ki speed):** Earth ka perihelion lagbhag 147.1 million km aur aphelion lagbhag 152.1 million km hai. Perihelion par speed lagbhag 30.3 km/s hai. Aphelion par speed nikalo.

- v_aphelion = v_perihelion × (r_perihelion ÷ r_aphelion) = 30.3 km/s × (147.1 ÷ 152.1) = 30.3 × 0.967 = **29.3 km/s** (lagbhag)

**Jaanch:** 147.1 × 30.3 = 4457 aur 152.1 × 29.3 = 4457: barabar ✅. Earth ki speed kuch kam-zyada hoti hai: 30.3 aur 29.3 km/s, yani lagbhag 1 km/s ka farak. Earth ka orbit lagbhag circle hai, isliye ye farak chhota hai.

Ek aur mazedaar baat: Earth Sooraj ke sabse paas **janwari ke shuru mein** hota hai, jab India mein sardi hoti hai. Yaani mausam Sooraj se doori se nahi badalte (wo Earth ke dhure ke jhuke hone se badalte hain, jo aage ki class ka topic hai).

## Part D: Teesra niyam: T² ∝ r³

> **Kepler ka teesra niyam:** Planet ke ek chakkar ke time T ka **square**, Sooraj se uski average doori r (yani a) ke **cube** ke anupaat mein hota hai: **T² ∝ r³**.

Do planets ke liye: **T₁² ÷ T₂² = r₁³ ÷ r₂³**.

Ab Sooraj ke planets ke liye ek **seedhi list** dekhte hain. Agar T saal mein aur r AU mein naapein, to Earth ke liye T = 1 saal aur r = 1 AU, to T² ÷ r³ = 1 ÷ 1 = 1. Baaki planets bhi lagbhag yahi number dete hain:

| Planet | r (AU) | T (saal) | T² | r³ | T² ÷ r³ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mercury | 0.39 | 0.24 | 0.058 | 0.059 | 0.97 |
| Venus | 0.72 | 0.62 | 0.38 | 0.37 | 1.03 |
| Earth | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| Mars | 1.52 | 1.88 | 3.53 | 3.51 | 1.01 |
| Jupiter | 5.20 | 11.9 | 141.6 | 140.6 | 1.01 |
| Saturn | 9.58 | 29.5 | 870 | 879 | 0.99 |
| Uranus | 19.2 | 84 | 7056 | 7078 | 1.00 |
| Neptune | 30.1 | 165 | 27225 | 27271 | 1.00 |

Aathon planets ke liye T² ÷ r³ lagbhag 1 aata hai (thoda sa farak sirf diye gaye numbers ke rounding ka hai). **Ye wahi niyam hai jo Kepler ne Tycho ke numbers se pakda tha.**

Is niyam se do baatein nikalti hain:
- Jo planet Sooraj se door hai, uska chakkar **zyada lamba** hota hai: sirf isliye nahi ki raasta bada hai, balki isliye bhi ki wo **dheere** chalta hai (Part E mein ye dekhenge).
- Agar ek planet ki doori aur time pata ho, aur doosre ki sirf ek cheez, to doosri cheez nikal sakti hai.

**Example 6 (doori se time: Mercury):** Mercury ki Sooraj se average doori 0.39 AU hai. Uska time period T nikalo (saal aur din mein).

- T² = r³ = 0.39³ = 0.0593 (T saal mein, r AU mein)
- T = √0.0593 = **0.24 saal** (lagbhag; 0.2436)
- Dinon mein: 0.2436 × 365 = **lagbhag 89 din**

**Jaanch:** 0.2436² = 0.0593 ✅. Mercury ka saal Earth ke lagbhag teen mahine se bhi kam hota hai.

**Example 7 (kalpit planet, doori 4 AU):** Ek kalpit planet X Sooraj se 4 AU door hai. Uska T nikalo.

- T² = r³ = 4³ = 64, to T = √64 = **8 saal**

**Jaanch:** Earth se compare: T₁² ÷ T₂² = r₁³ ÷ r₂³ = 64 ÷ 1 = 64, to T₁ ÷ T₂ = 8 ✅. Doori Earth ki 4 guna hai, par chakkar 4 guna nahi, **8 guna** lamba hai.

**Example 8 (time se doori):** Ek kalpit planet Y ka time period 27 saal hai. Sooraj se uski doori AU mein nikalo.

- r³ = T² = 27² = 729, to r = ∛729 = **9 AU**

**Jaanch:** 9³ = 729 = 27² ✅. Ye planet Earth se 9 guna door hai (Saturn ke aas-paas).

Chhoti trick: jab r = 4, 9 jaise "achhe" numbers hon to T = r × √r hota hai: r = 4 → 4 × 2 = 8; r = 9 → 9 × 3 = 27. Aur r = 1 → T = 1.

## Part E: Kepler ke niyam satellite par bhi chalte hain

Kepler ne niyam planets ke liye nikaale the, par ye **kisi bhi cheez ke liye** sach hain jo gravity ke asar mein kisi bade pind ke chaaro taraf ghoomti hai: Sooraj ke planets, Earth ka Chaand, Earth ke artificial satellite, Jupiter ke chaand (sirf naam). Class 9 ke Newton ke cannon wale satellite bhi ellipse (ya lagbhag circle) mein chalte hain, **Earth ek focus par**. Ek farak zaroor hai: Sooraj ke planets ke liye T² ÷ r³ ek hi number hai, aur **Earth ke satellites ke liye ek doosra number**. Kyun, ye Topic 4 mein orbit ke formula se saaf ho jaayega.

**Example 9 (Chaand ke saath satellite):** Chaand ki orbit ka radius lagbhag 3.84 × 10⁸ m aur time period 27.3 din hai. Ek satellite Earth ke chaaro taraf 9.6 × 10⁷ m radius ke circle mein chalta hai (Chaand ke radius ka chauthai hissa). Uska time period nikalo.

- Radius ka anupaat: 9.6 × 10⁷ ÷ 3.84 × 10⁸ = 1/4
- (T_sat ÷ 27.3 din)² = (1/4)³ = 1/64, to T_sat ÷ 27.3 din = 1/8
- T_sat = 27.3 din ÷ 8 = **3.41 din** (lagbhag; yani 81.9 ghante)

**Jaanch:** (3.41 ÷ 27.3)² = (0.125)² = 0.0156 aur (1/4)³ = 0.0156 ✅. Chaand ki orbit ka chauthai radius, par time 8 guna kam: Class 9 ka pattern "paas wala kam time lega" sach nikla.

**Example 10 (Earth ke satellites ka constant):** (a) Chaand ke data (T = 2.36 × 10⁶ s, r = 3.84 × 10⁸ m) se T² ÷ r³ nikalo. (b) Ek geostationary satellite ka radius 4.2 × 10⁷ m hai (Earth ke centre se); uska T lagbhag 24 ghante hona chahiye. Kepler ke niyam se jaanch karo.

(a) T² = (2.36 × 10⁶ s)² = 5.57 × 10¹² s²; r³ = (3.84 × 10⁸ m)³ = 5.66 × 10²⁵ m³. T² ÷ r³ = 5.57 × 10¹² ÷ 5.66 × 10²⁵ = **9.8 × 10⁻¹⁴ s²/m³** (Earth ke satellites ke liye).

(b) Ratio se: T ÷ 27.3 din = (4.2 × 10⁷ ÷ 3.84 × 10⁸)^(3/2) = (0.109)^1.5 = 0.0362, to T = 27.3 × 0.0362 = 0.988 din = **23.7 ghante** (lagbhag).

**Jaanch:** 23.7 ghante lagbhag 24 ghante ke bahut paas hai (4.2 × 10⁷ m ek rounded value hai) ✅. Seedha constant se bhi: T = 86,160 s (23 h 56 min) aur r = 4.2 × 10⁷ m ke liye T² ÷ r³ = 7.42 × 10⁹ ÷ 7.41 × 10²² = 1.0 × 10⁻¹³ s²/m³, jo (a) ke 9.8 × 10⁻¹⁴ ke bahut paas hai ✅. Dekho, ek Chaand aur ek kritrim satellite, dono ek hi niyam par chalte hain.

Sooraj ke planets ke liye isi constant ka maan SI units mein lagbhag 2.9 × 10⁻¹⁹ s²/m³ hai (Earth: T = 3.15 × 10⁷ s, r = 1.5 × 10¹¹ m). Alag number, kyunki ghoomne wale ke beech ka "kendra" alag hai (Sooraj ya Earth).

## Part F: Teeno niyam ek saath

| Niyam | Kehta kya hai | Kab dikhta hai |
|---|---|---|
| Pehla (ellipse) | Orbit ellipse hai, Sooraj ek focus par | Sooraj se doori badalti hai |
| Doosra (barabar area) | Barabar time mein barabar area | Perihelion par tez, aphelion par dheere |
| Teesra (T² ∝ r³) | Door wale planet ka chakkar lamba, T² ÷ r³ ek hi number | Mars, Jupiter, Saturn... sab par |

Ye teeno niyam **kyun** sach hain, iska jawab Newton ke gravity ke universal law se milta hai. Pehle do niyamon ki poori wajah aage ki class mein aayegi, isliye yahan unhe derive nahi karte. Teesre niyam ka seedha hisaab Topic 4 mein satellite ke orbit ke formula se aayega.

---

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Sooraj ellipse ke centre par hota hai."**

Ye galat hai: Sooraj **ek focus par** hai. Demo: Earth ke liye perihelion lagbhag 147.1 million km aur aphelion lagbhag 152.1 million km hai. Agar Sooraj ellipse ke centre par hota to dono doori **barabar** hoti. Farak 152.1 − 147.1 = 5.0 million km hai, yaani Sooraj centre se (5.0 ÷ 2 =) 2.5 million km hat kar hai. Ek aur demo: dhaage wale ellipse mein agar dono pin ko ek hi jagah milaa do (focus ek ho jaaye, matlab centre par) to ghera **circle** ban jaata hai (Mrunmayee ka "ek pin" wala jawab). Asli ellipse tabhi banta hai jab pin ke beech doori ho. Earth ka ellipse itna kam dabaa hai ki aankh se circle lagta hai, isliye ye galti aasani se ho jaati hai.

**Galti 2: "Planet hamesha ek jaisi speed se chalta hai."**

Galat. Doosre niyam se planet perihelion ke paas **tez** aur aphelion ke paas **dheere** chalta hai. Demo: Example 4 mein kalpit planet Z ki speed 20 km/s (perihelion) aur 5 km/s (aphelion) thi: 4 guna ka farak. Example 5 mein Earth ki speed 30.3 km/s se 29.3 km/s tak badalti hai. Agar speed hamesha ek hi rehti, to chhote radius wala wedge utne hi time mein chhota area jhaadta, jo "barabar time mein barabar area" ke khilaaf hota.

## Quick Recap

- **Tycho Brahe** ne saalon tak planets (khaaskar Mars) ki jagah bina telescope ke naapi. **Kepler** ne is data se teen niyam nikaale: 1609 mein do aur 1619 mein ek. Baad mein Newton ne wajah (gravity) batayi.
- **Pehla niyam:** planet ka orbit **ellipse** hai, **Sooraj ek focus par** (centre par nahi). Ellipse ke har point se dono focus ki doori ka jod ek jaisa. Dhaage aur do pin se ellipse banta hai.
- **Eccentricity e = c ÷ a:** e = 0 circle, e jitna bada utna dabaa. Perihelion = a (1 − e), aphelion = a (1 + e). Earth ka e lagbhag 0.017.
- **Doosra niyam:** barabar time mein barabar area. Perihelion par planet tez, aphelion par dheere. Do points par r_peri × v_peri = r_aph × v_aph.
- **Teesra niyam:** T² ∝ r³, yaani T₁² ÷ T₂² = r₁³ ÷ r₂³ (r = a, average doori). T saal aur r AU mein ho to Earth ke liye T² ÷ r³ = 1.
- Kepler ke niyam **satellite aur Chaand par bhi** chalte hain (Earth ek focus par), par constant alag.
