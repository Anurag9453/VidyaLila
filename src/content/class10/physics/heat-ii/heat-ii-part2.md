## Pahaad ki chai aur ghar ka cooker

Mr. Tipnis apne bhatije Chirayu ko lekar ek chhutti mein oonche pahaad (lagbhag 3000 m) par gaye. Subah-subah ek chhote se chai ke thele par unhone dal-chawal ka order diya. Thele wale bhaiya ne patila chadha diya. Dal ubalti rahi, ubalti rahi, aur lagbhag ek ghanta nikal gaya. Dal phir bhi kachchi si thi.

Chirayu ne bhookh se bechain hokar kaha, "Chacha, paani to kabse ubal raha hai, bulbule uth rahe hain. Phir dal kyun nahi gal rahi?" Thele wale bhaiya hanse, "Beta, yahan paani garam hi kam hota hai. Ubalta hai par jalta nahi."

Ghar wapas aakar Chirayu ne dekha ki mummy ka cooker usi dal ko lagbhag pandrah minute mein gala deta hai. Mr. Tipnis cooker ko khud sambhalte hain: gas par chadhana, seeti ginna, aur band karke thanda hone dena. Chirayu door se dekhta raha. "Chacha, ek hi paani, ek hi dal. Pahaad par ubalne par bhi nahi galti, aur cooker mein itni jaldi? Ubalne ka temperature kya sab jagah ek hi hota hai?"

Mr. Tipnis ne kaha, "Nahi, aur isi mein poori kahani chhupi hai. Aaj dekhenge ki ubaal ka temperature kaun chunta hai."

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |

## Class 9 ka recall

Class 9 mein ([heat-and-temperature-part5.md](../../../class9/physics/heat-and-temperature/heat-and-temperature-part5.md)) hum dekh chuke hain: **boiling point** wo temperature hai jahan liquid poora ubalta hai (paani ke liye normal pressure par 100 °C), aur **melting point** wo jahan solid pighalta hai (barf ke liye 0 °C). **Evaporation** har temperature par sirf sateh se hota hai aur **boiling** ek fixed temperature par poore liquid mein. Wahan ek line thi ki boiling point pressure par depend karta hai, par "poori detail aage". Atmospheric pressure aur 1 atm ([pressure-and-fluids-part4.md](../../../class9/physics/pressure-and-fluids/pressure-and-fluids-part4.md)) bhi Class 9 ke hain: 1 atm lagbhag 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa hota hai. Aaj hum is "detail" ko kholte hain, aur Class 9 ke "100 °C" ko ek sahi shart ke saath likhna seekhte hain: **100 °C, jab bahari pressure 1 atm ho**.

## Part A: Paani ubalta kab hai? Bulbule ki kahani

Paani ko garam karte ho to sateh se bhaap ud rahi hoti hai (evaporation). Par **ubaal** tab aata hai jab paani ke **andar**, tali ke paas, bhaap ke bulbule bante hain. Bulbula ek chhoti si "khaali jagah" hai jo paani ki bhaap se bhari hai. Ab sawaal ye hai ki wo jagah bani kaise rahe? Bulbule ko apne aas-paas ke paani aur hawa ko **peeche dhakelna** padta hai. Bahar se jo bhi dabaav hai (hawa ka 1 atm aur upar ke paani ka thoda sa bojh), wo bulbule ko pichkane ki koshish karta hai.

```
     hawa ka pressure (1 atm) dabata hai
          v      v      v      v
   ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~   paani ki sateh
   :                                    :
   :          upar ke paani ka          :
   :          thoda bojh bhi dabata     :
   :                                    :
   :            .--------.              :
   :       --> (  bhaap   ) <--         :   bhaap ka dhakka (andar se bahar)
   :            '--------'              :
   :                                    :
   +------------------------------------+
              garam tala (aag)
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Tali par bana bhaap ka bulbula. Bahar se dabaav andar ki taraf (v) dabata hai; bulbule ke andar ki bhaap bahar ki taraf dhakka deti hai (-->  <--). Bulbula tabhi bada hota aur upar uthta hai jab bhaap ka dhakka bahari pressure ke barabar ho jaaye.

Bhaap ka dhakka paani ke temperature ke saath badhta hai: paani jitna garam, bhaap ka dhakka utna zor ka (is dhakke ka koi formula Class 10 mein nahi chahiye, bas ye samajhna hai). Paani 100 °C par pahunchta hai to bhaap ka dhakka lagbhag 1 atm ho jaata hai, jo khule bartan par hawa ke pressure ke barabar hai. Tab bulbule bachne lagte hain: **ubaal**.

Is picture se do seedhe nateeje nikalte hain:

- **Bahari pressure zyada** ho to bhaap ko zyada zor lagana padega, yaani paani ko **zyada garam** karna padega. Boiling point **badhta** hai.
- **Bahari pressure kam** ho to kam dhakka kaafi hai. Boiling point **ghatta** hai.

| Kahan | Paani par bahari pressure | Paani ka boiling point |
|---|---|---|
| Samundar ke kinare, khula bartan | 1 atm | lagbhag 100 °C |
| Lagbhag 3000 m ki oonchai, khula bartan | 1 atm se kaafi kam | lagbhag 90 °C (hedged) |
| Pressure cooker ke andar | lagbhag 2 atm (absolute) | lagbhag 120 °C |
| Pump se hawa nikaala hua jar | 1 atm se bahut kam | kamre ke temperature par bhi ubaal |

Ye saari values **lagbhag** hain: asal mein mausam aur cooker ke model se thodi badalti hain.

## Part B: Pressure cooker: seeti ke andar ki physics

Cooker mein dhakkan gasket ke saath kasa band hota hai. Garam paani ki bhaap bahar nahi nikal paati, to cooker ke andar hawa aur bhaap jama hoti jaati hai aur pressure badhta hai. Dhakkan ke upar ek chhote vent (chhed) par ek **bhaari valve** rakha hota hai. Jab tak andar ka pressure kam hai, valve vent ko dabaye rakhta hai. Pressure ek had se upar jaate hi bhaap valve ko uthakar nikalti hai: **seeti**. Isi se cooker ka pressure ek had par tika rehta hai aur dhakkan phatta nahi.

```
                   [ W ]               W = valve ka wazan (vent par)
                   ==+==               vent (chhota chhed)
   +---------------+ ↑ +---------------+
   :o                ↑                o:   ↑ = andar ki bhaap ka dhakka
   :                                   :
   :    bhaap, garam paani, dal        :
   :                                   :
   +-----------------------------------+
            gas ki aag (adult ke haath mein)
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Cooker ka kaat (scale ke hisaab se nahi). Bhaap ka dhakka vent par upar ki taraf (↑) lagta hai; valve ka wazan aur bahar ki hawa use neeche dabate hain. Dhakkan ke kinare gasket (o) use bhaap ke liye band rakhta hai. Dhakkan ki patti upar ki line hai, jisme vent ka chhed beech mein hai.

**Valve kitna pressure banata hai?** Valve tab uthta hai jab andar ki bhaap ka upar ki taraf ka force, bahar ki hawa ke force aur valve ke wazan ke jod ke barabar ho jaaye. Bahar ki hawa ka dabaav to 1 atm hai hi, isliye **extra pressure = valve ka wazan ÷ vent ka area**, yaani P = m g ÷ A (Class 9 ka P = F ÷ A, [pressure-and-fluids-part4.md](../../../class9/physics/pressure-and-fluids/pressure-and-fluids-part4.md)). Yahan g = 10 m/s² (Class 9 ki tarah) aur π = 3.14 lete hain.

**Example 1 (valve ka extra pressure):** Ek cooker ke valve ka mass 70 g hai aur vent ka diameter 3 mm hai. Extra pressure aur absolute pressure (atm mein) nikalo. 1 atm = 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa lo.

- Given: m = 70 g = 0.07 kg; d = 3 mm = 3 × 10⁻³ m, to r = 1.5 × 10⁻³ m; g = 10 m/s².
- Find: extra pressure P_extra, phir kul absolute pressure.
- Formula: A = π r²; F = m g; P_extra = F ÷ A.
- Stage 1: A = 3.14 × (1.5 × 10⁻³ m)² = 3.14 × 2.25 × 10⁻⁶ m² = 7.065 × 10⁻⁶ m².
- Stage 2: F = 0.07 kg × 10 m/s² = 0.7 N.
- Stage 3: P_extra = 0.7 N ÷ 7.065 × 10⁻⁶ m² = lagbhag 9.9 × 10⁴ Pa.
- Stage 4: atm mein: 9.9 × 10⁴ ÷ 1.013 × 10⁵ = lagbhag 0.98 atm.
- Stage 5: absolute pressure = 1 atm (bahar ki hawa) + 0.98 atm = lagbhag **1.98 atm**, yaani lagbhag **2 atm**.
- **Jaanch:** P_extra × A = 9.9 × 10⁴ Pa × 7.065 × 10⁻⁶ m² = 0.70 N ✅. Aur 1.98 atm lagbhag 2 atm hi hai, jaisa Part A ki table mein likha tha ✅.

Ab 2 atm par paani lagbhag 120 °C par ubalta hai. Dal 100 °C ki jagah 120 °C ke paani mein pakti hai. **Garam paani mein cheezein tezi se galti hain**, isliye wahi dal ghante bhar ki jagah lagbhag pandrah minute mein gal jaati hai. (Ye "kitni tez" hedged hai: dal, chawal aur sabzi sab alag raftaar se galte hain.)

**Example 2 (cooker ko garam karne ka time):** Ek electric cooker mein 1.5 kg paani (dal-chawal ke saath, paani hi maano) 30 °C par hai. Heater ki power 1350 W hai aur saari garmi paani ko jaati hai (cooker ka apna mass aur bahar nikli bhaap chhod do, wo chhoti hai). Use 120 °C tak garam hone mein kitna time lagega? Paani cooker ke andar liquid hi rehta hai.

- Given: m = 1.5 kg; start 30 °C; end 120 °C; c = 4200 J/kg °C; P = 1350 W.
- Find: t.
- Formula: Q = m c ΔT; t = Q ÷ P.
- Stage 1 (30 °C se 100 °C, ΔT = 70 °C): Q₁ = 1.5 × 4200 × 70 = 441000 J.
- Stage 2 (100 °C se 120 °C, ΔT = 20 °C): Q₂ = 1.5 × 4200 × 20 = 126000 J.
- Stage 3: Q = 441000 + 126000 = 567000 J.
- Stage 4: t = 567000 J ÷ 1350 J/s = **420 s** = **7 minute**.
- **Jaanch:** 1350 W × 420 s = 567000 J ✅. Khule patile mein sirf 100 °C tak: 441000 J ÷ 1350 = lagbhag 327 s ✅.

Dhyan do: cooker ko 120 °C tak le jaane mein khule patile se **zyada garmi** lagi (126000 J extra, lagbhag 29 percent zyada). Cooker kaam isliye jaldi karta hai kyunki **pakane ki raftaar garam paani mein bahut badh jaati hai**, isliye nahi ki wo paani ko tezi se garam kar deta hai. Electric heater ke time ka hisaab Chapter 7 mein bhi aaya tha: [current-electricity-ii-part5.md](../current-electricity-ii/current-electricity-ii-part5.md).

### Cooker ki seeti ki kahani (adult ke saath, door se)

Rasoi mein jab seeti baji, Chirayu ne dekha ki Chacha ne gas band kiya aur cooker ko ek taraf rakh diya. Chirayu ne haath badhaya, "Chacha, main khol doon?" Chacha ne use roka, "Nahi. Abhi andar ka pressure 2 atm ke aas-paas hai, aur dhakkan khulte hi paani achanak ubal kar bahar uchhalta hai. Pehle thanda hone do." Wo ek taraf khade hokar bole, "Seeti ke vent se door rehna: bhaap bahut garam hoti hai." Chirayu ne ghadi dekhi, aur door se seeti ka vent dekhta raha.

**Cooker ke safety niyam (adult ke liye, bachchon ke padhne ke liye):**
- Dhakkan ka gasket saaf aur sahi baitha ho. Vent aur seeti ka chhed daal ya chhilke se band nahi hona chahiye.
- Cooker ko kabhi tooti-footi ya dhile dhakkan ke saath nahi chalate. Cooker mein safety valve ya fusible plug bhi hota hai jo pressure hadd se badhe to bhaap nikaal deta hai (naam yaad rakho).
- Cooker ko lagbhag do-tihayi se zyada na bharein (aam salah: bhaap aur jhaag se vent band ho sakta hai).
- Dhakkan sirf tab kholein jab cooker thanda ho jaaye aur pressure nikal chuka ho, aur wo bhi adult.

## Part C: Pahaad par ubaal: kam pressure, kam temperature

Pahaad par hawa patli hoti hai, isliye vahan bahari pressure **1 atm se kaafi kam** hota hai. Part A ki bulbule wali picture se: bhaap ko kam dhakka lagana hai, to paani **kam temperature par** hi ubalne lagta hai. Lagbhag 3000 m ki oonchai par ye lagbhag 90 °C hota hai (hedged, mausam se thoda badalta hai).

Par 90 °C ka ubalta paani 100 °C ke ubalte paani se **thanda** hai. Dal wahi hai, par use 90 °C ka paani mil raha hai, to wo der se galti hai. Aur ubalte paani ko aur tez aag dene se bhi kuch nahi hota (Aam galti 1 dekho): temperature 90 °C par hi rukta hai.

**Example 3 (pahaad vs maidan):** 0.85 kg paani 21 °C par hai. Use ubaal ke temperature tak laane mein kitni garmi lagegi, (a) lagbhag 3000 m ki oonchai par (boiling point lagbhag 90 °C), (b) samundar ke kinare (100 °C)?

- Given: m = 0.85 kg; start 21 °C; c = 4200 J/kg °C.
- Find: Q (a) aur (b), aur unka fark.
- Formula: Q = m c ΔT.
- Stage 1 (pahaad, ΔT = 90 − 21 = 69 °C): Q = 0.85 × 4200 × 69 = 246330 J.
- Stage 2 (maidan, ΔT = 100 − 21 = 79 °C): Q = 0.85 × 4200 × 79 = 282030 J.
- Stage 3: fark = 282030 − 246330 = **35700 J**.
- **Jaanch:** fark ΔT = 10 °C ka hi hai: 0.85 × 4200 × 10 = 35700 J ✅.

Pahaad par paani **kam garmi mein** ubal gaya, phir bhi dal der se galegi: ubaal jaldi aaya, par pakane wala paani **thanda** hai. "Ubal gaya" aur "bahut garam hai" ek hi baat nahi.

**Cooker pahaad par bhi madad karta hai.** Cooker ka valve ka extra pressure (Example 1 jaisa, lagbhag 1 atm extra) wahan bhi wahi rehta hai, par bahar ka pressure kam hai, to absolute pressure samundar ke kinare se kam hota hai. Isliye pahaad par cooker ke andar ka temperature **120 °C se kuch kam** hota hai (lagbhag, hedged), par khule patile ke 90 °C se **kaafi zyada**. Isi wajah se pahadi ilaakon mein cooker bahut aam hai, aur samundar ke kinare cooker se dal sabse jaldi galti hai.

```
 T (°C)                                       h = pahaad (lagbhag 90 °C)
  120 +                     *======================c    p = maidan (100 °C)
      +                    *                           c = cooker (lagbhag 120 °C)
  110 +                   *
      +                  *
  100 +                 *=====================p
      +                *
   90 +               *=====================h
      +              *
   80 +             *
      +            *
   70 +          *
      +         *
   60 +        *
      +       *
   50 +      *
      +     *
   40 +    *
      +   *
   30 +  *
      + *
   20 +*
      +----------------------------------------------
       0         100       200       300       400   Q (kJ)
```

> **Figure 2.3** – 0.5 kg paani 20 °C se garam hota hai (c = 4200 J/kg °C), phir har jagah 0.1 kg ubal kar bhaap banta hai (L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg), jo har shelf (=) ki lambai hai. 1 column = 10000 J, 1 row = 5 °C (scale ke hisaab se). Shuruaat ka dhalaan (*) teeno ke liye ek hi hai, kyunki m c wahi hai. Shelf 90 °C (h), 100 °C (p) aur 120 °C (c) par aati hai. Teeno shelf ki lambai barabar hai (L_v wahi maani hai), par cooker ki shelf upar aur der se shuru hoti hai.

Is figure se dekho: **pahaad** ki shelf sabse neeche aur sabse pehle shuru, **cooker** ki sabse upar aur sabse baad mein. Shelf par temperature nahi badalta, sirf garmi bhaap banane mein jaati hai (Topic 1 ka heating curve wala sabak).

## Part D: Kam pressure ka faayda: vacuum evaporation

Chini ke kaarkhane mein ganne ke ras mein paani bahut hota hai, aur use nikaalna padta hai taaki chini ke dane (crystals) bane. Khule bartan mein ras ko 100 °C se zyada garam karne par chini **jal kar kaali** (caramel jaisi) ho sakti hai aur uska swaad aur rang bigad jaata hai. Doodh se powder banane mein bhi yahi dikkat: zyada garam karne se doodh ka swaad aur poshan bigadta hai. Isliye kaarkhane **band bade tanks (vacuum pan)** mein pump se hawa nikaal dete hain. Pressure bahut kam ho jaata hai, to ras **kam temperature** par hi ubalne lagta hai aur paani bhaap ban kar nikal jaata hai, chini ya doodh jalta nahi.

**Example 4 (chini ke ras ka vacuum pan):** 250 kg ganne ka ras (paani hi maano) 30 °C par hai. Usme se 60 kg paani bhaap banana hai. (a) Khule pan mein (ubaal 100 °C par) aur (b) vacuum pan mein, jahan maan lo ki ras 70 °C par ubalta hai, kitni garmi lagegi? L_v ko dono jagah wahi 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg maan lo (asal mein L_v kam temperature par thoda zyada hota hai, par wahi maan lena yahan theek hai).

- Given: m_ras = 250 kg; start 30 °C; bhaap banne wala paani 60 kg; c = 4200 J/kg °C; L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg.
- Find: kul garmi (a) aur (b), aur bachat.
- Formula: Q = m c ΔT (garam karna), Q = m L_v (bhaap banana).
- Stage 1 (khule pan, 30 °C se 100 °C, ΔT = 70 °C): Q₁ = 250 × 4200 × 70 = 73500000 J = 7.35 × 10⁷ J.
- Stage 2 (khule pan, 100 °C par 60 kg bhaap): Q₂ = 60 × 2.26 × 10⁶ = 1.356 × 10⁸ J.
- Stage 3 (khule pan ka kul): 7.35 × 10⁷ + 1.356 × 10⁸ = **2.091 × 10⁸ J**.
- Stage 4 (vacuum pan, 30 °C se 70 °C, ΔT = 40 °C): Q₃ = 250 × 4200 × 40 = 4.2 × 10⁷ J.
- Stage 5 (vacuum pan, 70 °C par 60 kg bhaap): Q₄ = 1.356 × 10⁸ J (wahi).
- Stage 6 (vacuum pan ka kul): 4.2 × 10⁷ + 1.356 × 10⁸ = **1.776 × 10⁸ J**.
- Stage 7: bachat = 2.091 × 10⁸ − 1.776 × 10⁸ = **3.15 × 10⁷ J**.
- **Jaanch:** bachat ek hi cheez se aani chahiye: ras ko 30 °C aur chadhane ki garmi = 250 × 4200 × 30 = 3.15 × 10⁷ J ✅.

Dhyan do: **garmi ki bachat sirf lagbhag 15 percent hai** (3.15 × 10⁷ ÷ 2.091 × 10⁸ = 0.15). Asli faayda energy ka nahi, **quality ka** hai: chini ya doodh kam temperature par rehta hai, to jalta ya bigadta nahi. Bhaap nikaalne ka bada hissa (1.356 × 10⁸ J) dono mein barabar hai, kyunki L_v wahi maana hai.

### Bell jar mein paani ubalta hai: kamre ke temperature par (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo)

**Sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo).** School ki lab mein teacher ek mazboot, moti deewar wale bell jar (gol kaanch ke jar) mein kamre ke temperature ka thoda paani rakhta hai, jar ko vacuum pump se jodta hai aur hawa nikaalta hai. Jaise-jaise pressure girta hai, paani mein bulbule uthne lagte hain: **paani ubal raha hai, par wo garam nahi hai**. Dhyan se dekho: bahut kam pressure par paani ke bhaap banne ki garmi paani se hi aati hai, isliye wo thanda bhi ho sakta hai. Bachche door se dekhte hain; jar aur pump sirf teacher sambhalta hai (kaanch ke jar mein hawa nikaalne ka kaam sirf proper jar aur guard ke saath hota hai). Ye prayog ghar par kisi bhi tarah nahi karna.

## Part E: Ubaal par aur do baatein: tez aag aur energy ka hisaab

**Example 5 (cooker ki seeti ki bhaap ka energy hisaab):** Cooker mein 0.13 kg paani 30 °C par hai. Maan lo ki 0.13 kg paani ubal kar bhaap ban gaya. Kul garmi kitni lagi, (a) khule patile mein jahan ubaal 100 °C par hai, aur (b) cooker mein jahan ubaal 120 °C par hai? L_v ko dono jagah wahi maanna hai.

- Given: m = 0.13 kg; start 30 °C; c = 4200 J/kg °C; L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg.
- Find: kul Q (a) aur (b).
- Formula: Q = m c ΔT, phir Q = m L_v.
- Stage 1 (khula, 30 °C se 100 °C, ΔT = 70 °C): 0.13 × 4200 × 70 = 38220 J.
- Stage 2 (khula, 100 °C par bhaap): 0.13 × 2.26 × 10⁶ = 293800 J.
- Stage 3 (khule ka kul): 38220 + 293800 = **332020 J**.
- Stage 4 (cooker, 30 °C se 120 °C, ΔT = 90 °C): 0.13 × 4200 × 90 = 49140 J.
- Stage 5 (cooker, 120 °C par bhaap, wahi L_v): 293800 J.
- Stage 6 (cooker ka kul): 49140 + 293800 = **342940 J**.
- **Jaanch:** fark = 342940 − 332020 = 10920 J = 0.13 × 4200 × 20 ✅ (extra 20 °C ki garmi).

## Part F: Pressure aur barf: regelation

Ab tak pressure ne ubaal par asar dikhaya. Pressure **pighalne ke point** par bhi asar daalta hai, par bahut kam. Paani ek ajeeb padaarth hai: barf paani se **kam ghani** hoti hai (isiliye tairti hai; paani ke aise ajeeb vyavhar ki ek aur jhalak Class 9 ke [heat-and-temperature-part2.md](../../../class9/physics/heat-and-temperature/heat-and-temperature-part2.md) mein anomalous expansion mein dekhi thi). Isliye barf ko dabane par wo **thoda aasani se pighalti hai**: dabaav ke neeche barf ka melting point 0 °C se **thoda sa** neeche chala jaata hai. Ye girna bahut chhota hai: kai atm ke dabaav par bhi ek degree ke chhote hisse jitna (hedged).

**Regelation ka prayog (school mein teacher ke saath):** Barf ke ek tukde par patla taar daal kar uske dono sire par bhaari wazan latka do. Taar dheere-dheere barf ke andar utarta jaata hai aur nikal bhi jaata hai, par barf ka tukda **do hisson mein nahi tootta**. Taar ke neeche ki barf dabaav se pighalti hai, paani taar ke upar nikal jaata hai, jahan dabaav kam hai, to wo wapas jam jaata hai: **regelation**. Jamne par barf latent heat nikalti hai (Topic 1 ka L_f), aur wo garmi dhaatu ke taar se hote hue neeche pighalne mein kaam aati hai. Isiliye dhaatu ka taar kaam karta hai aur dhaage jaisa kharab conductor bahut dheere kaam karta hai (ye bhi hedged hai: poori kahani mein garmi ka flow aur dabaav dono ka haath hai).

**Barf par skating: kahani ka sach.** Purani kahani ye thi ki skate ka patla blade barf par bahut dabaav daalta hai, isse barf pighalti hai aur blade phisalta hai. Ab vaigyanik maante hain ki ye **akeli kahani kaafi nahi** hai: ek skater ka dabaav barf ka melting point sirf ek degree ke chhote hisse jitna girata hai, jabki skating −10 °C ya usse thandi barf par bhi hoti hai. Ragad se bani garmi aur barf ki sateh par ek **patli paani jaisi parat** bhi kaam karti hai (poora sach abhi bhi research mein hai, hedged).

### Sublimation: 3 line mein recall

Kapoor, naphthalene ki goliyan aur dry ice seedhe solid se gas ban jaate hain ([heat-and-temperature-part5.md](../../../class9/physics/heat-and-temperature/heat-and-temperature-part5.md)). Dry ice ke liye pressure ka bhi haath hai: 1 atm par carbon dioxide ka liquid roop mil hi nahi sakta, isliye wo seedhe gas ban jaata hai. Liquid CO₂ banane ke liye 1 atm se **kaafi zyada** pressure chahiye (is poore nakshe ko aage ki class mein dekhoge).

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Zyada tez aag karne se paani zyada garam temperature par ubalta hai."**
Nahi. Khule patile mein paani ek baar ubalne lage to temperature bahari pressure ke hisaab se **fixed** rehta hai. Tez aag sirf ye badalti hai ki **kitni jaldi** paani bhaap banta hai. Demonstration: ek khule patile mein 1.6 kg paani ubal raha hai (100 °C). Pehle choti aag se 700 W ki garmi mil rahi hai, phir badi aag se 1400 W (maan lo ki saari garmi paani ko jaati hai). Ek minute (60 s) mein: chhoti aag se Q = 700 × 60 = 42000 J, to bhaap bani m = 42000 ÷ 2.26 × 10⁶ = lagbhag 0.0186 kg (lagbhag 18.6 g). Badi aag se Q = 1400 × 60 = 84000 J, to bhaap bani lagbhag 0.0372 kg (lagbhag 37.2 g). **Dono mein thermometer 100 °C par hi rahega**: extra garmi ne **double bhaap** banayi, double temperature nahi. Isliye dal ko jaldi gaalna ho to aag nahi, cooker chahiye (wo pressure badhata hai).

**Galti 2: "Paani 100 °C par hi ubalta hai, har jagah."**
Nahi. 100 °C sirf tab jab bahari pressure 1 atm ho. Demonstration: wahi paani, wahi patila, wahi dal. Samundar ke kinare patile mein paani lagbhag 100 °C par ubalta hai. Lagbhag 3000 m ke thele par wahi paani lagbhag 90 °C par ubalne lagta hai, aur cooker ke andar lagbhag 120 °C par. Teen jagah, teen alag "ubaal ke temperature", jabki teeno jagah paani "ubal raha hai" aur bulbule uth rahe hain. Isiliye "ubal raha hai" ka matlab "100 °C hai" nahi hota: ubal raha hai matlab "jis pressure par hai, uske hisaab ke boiling point par hai".

## Quick Recap

- Ubaal tab hota hai jab paani ke andar bhaap ke bulbule bahari pressure ko peeche dhakel sakein: bhaap ka dhakka bahari pressure ke barabar ho jaaye.
- **Bahari pressure zyada: boiling point zyada. Pressure kam: boiling point kam.** 100 °C sirf 1 atm par hai.
- Pressure cooker mein valve ka wazan extra pressure banata hai: P_extra = m g ÷ A. Total absolute pressure lagbhag 2 atm, boiling point lagbhag 120 °C, to dal jaldi galti hai. Cooker adult chalata hai, garam hote hue kabhi nahi kholte.
- Cooker ko garam karne mein khule patile se zyada garmi lagti hai, par pakane ki raftaar bahut badhti hai.
- Lagbhag 3000 m ki oonchai par paani lagbhag 90 °C par ubalta hai: dal der se galti hai; cooker wahan bhi madad karta hai.
- Vacuum evaporation: kam pressure, kam temperature par ubaal, to chini ya doodh jalta nahi. Energy ki bachat chhoti, quality ka faayda bada.
- Pressure se barf ka melting point sirf thoda sa girta hai: regelation. Skating ki purani "pressure melting" kahani akeli kaafi nahi.
- Kapoor, naphthalene aur dry ice sublimate hote hain; dry ice 1 atm par liquid ban hi nahi paata.
- Tez aag se ubalte paani ka temperature nahi badalta, bhaap zyada banti hai.

## Practice Questions

Constants for this set: c(water) = 4200 J/kg °C and L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg. Where a question needs them, take g = 10 m/s², π = 3.14 and 1 atm = 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa. The pressure and boiling point tables inside Questions 3, 4 and 8 are made up (maan lo) for practice only and are not exact. Each question gives every value it needs.

1. A cooker has a valve of mass 55 g sitting on a vent of diameter 2.6 mm. Taking g = 10 m/s², π = 3.14 and 1 atm = 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa, find (a) the extra pressure the valve causes, in Pa and in atm, and (b) the absolute pressure inside, taking the outside air as 1 atm.

2. An electric hot plate of power 1100 W heats 1.15 kg of water (with rice in it, treat it as water) from 23 °C to 120 °C inside a closed cooker. All the heat goes into the water and the water stays liquid. Find the time taken. Show the two stages (23 °C to 100 °C, then 100 °C to 120 °C) separately.

3. At a hill camp the outside pressure is 0.60 atm. Use this table: 0.45 atm gives 78 °C; 0.60 atm gives 86 °C; 0.75 atm gives 92 °C; 1.00 atm gives 100 °C. 0.78 kg of water at 14 °C is heated to its boiling point. (a) What is the boiling point at the camp? (b) How much heat is needed there? (c) How much heat would the same water need to reach its boiling point at 1.00 atm, and how much more is it?

4. A trekker finds that water in her open pot boils at 92 °C. Use this table: 0.45 atm gives 78 °C; 0.60 atm gives 86 °C; 0.75 atm gives 92 °C; 1.00 atm gives 100 °C. Say what the outside pressure is. Is this place higher or lower than the 0.60 atm camp of Question 3? Give one reason.

5. In an open pot, 0.28 kg of water at 100 °C is completely boiled into steam at 100 °C. In a cooker, 0.28 kg of water at 120 °C is boiled into steam at 120 °C (take L_v as unchanged). (a) Find the heat needed in each case. (b) Find the extra heat needed to bring 0.28 kg of water from 100 °C to 120 °C. (c) If the cooker case also started with water at 100 °C, which case needs more total heat to end as steam, and by how much?

6. A milk-powder plant has 900 kg of milk (treat as water) at 28 °C. 300 kg of water has to be removed as vapour. In a vacuum pan the milk boils at 55 °C (maan lo). Take L_v as unchanged. Find (a) the heat to warm the milk to 55 °C, (b) the heat to boil away 300 kg, and (c) how much less heat is needed for warming than if the pan were open and the milk had to be warmed to 100 °C.

7. A hot plate gives 1600 W to a pot in which 0.82 kg of water is already boiling at 100 °C at 1 atm. All the heat goes to the water. How long will it take to boil away 0.21 kg of water, and what will the thermometer read during this time?

8. At the coast (outside 1.00 atm) and at a hill (outside 0.75 atm), a cooker's valve adds 1.00 atm extra. Use this table: 0.75 atm gives 92 °C; 1.00 atm gives 100 °C; 1.75 atm gives 116 °C; 2.00 atm gives 120 °C. (a) Find the absolute pressure inside the cooker in each place. (b) Find the boiling point in each place. (c) By how much does the hill cooker's temperature exceed the open pot at the same hill?

9. A thin steel wire with a heavy weight at each end is hung over a block of ice at 0 °C and slowly passes through the block, leaving the block in one piece. Explain, step by step, where the water under the wire goes, what happens when it refreezes, and why a cotton thread (a poor conductor of heat) with the same weights would work much more slowly.

10. State True or False, and correct the false ones: (a) Doubling the flame under an open pot of boiling water makes the water boil at about 200 °C. (b) At about 3000 m, water boils at a temperature lower than 100 °C. (c) A cooker cooks faster because its water is hotter, not because it heats up faster. (d) The old story that "skates melt ice only by pressure" fully explains skating. (e) At 1 atm, dry ice melts into a liquid before it becomes gas.

11. A cooker designer wants the valve to add 1.2 atm extra pressure on a vent of diameter 3.2 mm. Using g = 10 m/s², π = 3.14 and 1 atm = 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa, find the required mass of the valve in grams.

### Answers

1. **Given:** m = 55 g = 0.055 kg; d = 2.6 mm, to r = 1.3 × 10⁻³ m. **Formula:** A = π r²; P_extra = m g ÷ A.
   (a) A = 3.14 × (1.3 × 10⁻³)² = 3.14 × 1.69 × 10⁻⁶ = 5.3066 × 10⁻⁶ m². F = 0.055 × 10 = 0.55 N. P_extra = 0.55 ÷ 5.3066 × 10⁻⁶ = lagbhag **1.04 × 10⁵ Pa**. atm mein: 1.04 × 10⁵ ÷ 1.013 × 10⁵ = lagbhag **1.02 atm**.
   (b) Absolute pressure = 1 + 1.02 = lagbhag **2.02 atm**.
   **Jaanch:** 1.0364 × 10⁵ Pa × 5.3066 × 10⁻⁶ m² = 0.55 N ✅. Aur 2.02 atm lagbhag 2 atm hai ✅.

2. **Given:** m = 1.15 kg; P = 1100 W; start 23 °C; end 120 °C. **Formula:** Q = m c ΔT; t = Q ÷ P.
   Stage 1 (23 °C se 100 °C, ΔT = 77 °C): Q₁ = 1.15 × 4200 × 77 = 371910 J.
   Stage 2 (100 °C se 120 °C, ΔT = 20 °C): Q₂ = 1.15 × 4200 × 20 = 96600 J.
   Stage 3: Q = 371910 + 96600 = 468510 J. t = 468510 ÷ 1100 = lagbhag **425.9 s** (lagbhag 7.1 minute).
   **Jaanch:** 1100 × 425.9 = 468490 J, lagbhag 468510 J ✅ (rounding). Total ΔT 97 °C: 1.15 × 4200 × 97 = 468510 ✅.

3. **Given:** m = 0.65 kg; start 14 °C; table se.
   (a) 0.60 atm par table: **86 °C**.
   (b) ΔT = 86 − 14 = 72 °C. Q = 0.78 × 4200 × 72 = **235872 J**.
   (c) 1.00 atm par boiling point 100 °C, ΔT = 100 − 14 = 86 °C. Q = 0.78 × 4200 × 86 = 281736 J. Fark = 281736 − 235872 = **45864 J** zyada.
   **Jaanch:** fark ΔT 14 °C ka hai: 0.78 × 4200 × 14 = 45864 J ✅.

4. Table mein 92 °C ke saamne **0.75 atm** hai. 0.75 atm, 0.60 atm se **zyada** hai, to ye jagah camp se **neeche** (kam oonchai par) hai, kyunki oonchai badhne par hawa patli hoti hai aur pressure ghatta hai. **Jaanch:** boiling point bhi 92 > 86, jo zyada pressure se mel khata hai ✅.

5. **Given:** m = 0.28 kg; L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg (dono jagah). **Formula:** Q = m L_v; Q = m c ΔT.
   (a) Dono mein paani pehle se boiling point par hai, to Q = m L_v = 0.28 × 2.26 × 10⁶ = **632800 J** dono jagah (L_v wahi maana hai).
   (b) ΔT = 120 − 100 = 20 °C: Q = 0.28 × 4200 × 20 = **23520 J**.
   (c) Agar dono 100 °C se shuru hon, to cooker wale ko pehle 100 °C se 120 °C tak le jaana hai: 632800 + 23520 = 656320 J, to **cooker** ko zyada kul garmi chahiye, 23520 J zyada.
   **Jaanch:** 656320 − 632800 = 23520 ✅.

6. **Given:** m = 900 kg; start 28 °C; vacuum pan par 55 °C; 300 kg bhaap. **Formula:** Q = m c ΔT; Q = m L_v.
   (a) ΔT = 55 − 28 = 27 °C: Q = 900 × 4200 × 27 = **1.0206 × 10⁸ J**.
   (b) Q = 300 × 2.26 × 10⁶ = **6.78 × 10⁸ J**.
   (c) Khule pan mein 100 °C tak: ΔT = 72 °C: Q = 900 × 4200 × 72 = 2.7216 × 10⁸ J. Vacuum pan mein garam karne mein kam: 2.7216 × 10⁸ − 1.0206 × 10⁸ = **1.701 × 10⁸ J** kam.
   **Jaanch:** ΔT ka fark 100 − 55 = 45 °C: 900 × 4200 × 45 = 1.701 × 10⁸ J ✅. (L_v wala hissa dono mein barabar maana hai.)

7. **Given:** P = 1600 W; m = 0.21 kg; boiling 100 °C par. **Formula:** Q = m L_v; t = Q ÷ P.
   Q = 0.21 × 2.26 × 10⁶ = 474600 J. t = 474600 ÷ 1600 = **296.6 s** (lagbhag 4.9 minute). Thermometer **100 °C** par hi rahega, kyunki ubalte paani ka temperature tab tak nahi badalta jab tak liquid bacha hai aur pressure wahi hai.
   **Jaanch:** 1600 × 296.6 = 474560 J ≈ 474600 J ✅.

8. (a) Coast: 1.00 + 1.00 = **2.00 atm**. Hill: 0.75 + 1.00 = **1.75 atm**.
   (b) Table se: coast ka cooker **120 °C**; hill ka cooker **116 °C**.
   (c) Hill par khule pot ka boiling point 0.75 atm par 92 °C hai. Cooker ka 116 °C. Fark = 116 − 92 = **24 °C** zyada.
   **Jaanch:** coast aur hill cooker ka fark = 120 − 116 = 4 °C, yaani hill par cooker thoda kam garam hai, par khule pot se kaafi zyada ✅.

9. Step 1: taar ke neeche ki barf par bahut dabaav hai, isliye wahan barf ka melting point 0 °C se thoda neeche chala jaata hai, aur 0 °C par rakhi barf wahan pighal jaati hai. Step 2: pighla hua paani taar ke upar ki taraf nikal jaata hai, jahan dabaav kam hai. Step 3: wahan paani wapas jam jaata hai (regelation), aur jamte hue wo latent heat nikalta hai. Step 4: dhaatu ka taar achha conductor hai, isliye ye garmi taar ke raaste neeche tak jaati hai aur wahan pighalne mein kaam aati hai. Isliye taar barf ko kaat kar neeche jaata rehta hai aur barf poori rehti hai. Cotton ka dhaaga conductor kharab hai, to garmi neeche tak nahi pahunchti, aur dhaaga bahut dheere kaam karta hai. (Hedged: poori kahani mein pressure aur garmi ke flow dono ka haath hai.)

10. (a) **False** ❌. Khule patile mein boiling point 1 atm par lagbhag 100 °C par hi rehta hai, tez aag se sirf bhaap zyada banti hai. (b) **True** ✅. (c) **True** ✅. Cooker ko garam karne mein to zyada garmi lagti hai (Example 2), par 120 °C ke paani mein pakna tez hai. (d) **False** ❌. Pressure se barf ka melting point sirf ek degree ke chhote hisse jitna girta hai, jabki skating −10 °C par bhi hoti hai; ragad ki garmi aur sateh ki patli paani jaisi parat bhi kaam karti hai. (e) **False** ❌. 1 atm par dry ice seedha gas banta hai (sublimation).

11. **Given:** P_extra = 1.2 atm = 1.2 × 1.013 × 10⁵ = 1.2156 × 10⁵ Pa; r = 1.6 × 10⁻³ m. **Formula:** m = P A ÷ g.
    A = 3.14 × (1.6 × 10⁻³)² = 3.14 × 2.56 × 10⁻⁶ = 8.0384 × 10⁻⁶ m². F = P A = 1.2156 × 10⁵ × 8.0384 × 10⁻⁶ = 0.9771 N. m = 0.9771 ÷ 10 = 0.09771 kg = lagbhag **97.7 g**.
    **Jaanch:** 0.0977 kg × 10 m/s² ÷ 8.0384 × 10⁻⁶ m² = 1.2155 × 10⁵ Pa = lagbhag 1.2 atm ✅.
