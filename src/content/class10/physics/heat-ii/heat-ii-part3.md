# Topic 3: Evaporation Se Thandak: Matka, Cooler Aur Pasina

Topic 2 mein dekha ki paani kab ubalta hai, ye bahar ke pressure par tikta hai. Par roz ki zindagi mein zyadatar paani ubalta nahi, phir bhi **chupchap bhaap ban kar ud jaata hai**: geela kapda sookhta hai, pasina sookhta hai, matke ka paani thanda rehta hai. Class 9 mein pata chala tha ki is evaporation se thandak milti hai. Aaj hum use **tauleinge**: kitna paani evaporate hota hai, kitni garmi le jaata hai, aur kitna thanda kar sakta hai.

## Nirgude Chacha ka gaon aur dopahar ka matka

Garmi ki chhuttiyon mein Nirgude Chacha apni bhatiji Vrushali ko apne gaon le gaye. Dopahar ke lagbhag 40 °C ke tapte din mein wo bus se utre. Vrushali ke paas shehar se laayi steel ki bottle thi, aur uska paani chai jaisa **gunguna** ho chuka tha. Gaon ke ghar ki rasoi ke kone mein mitti ka matka rakha tha, bina fridge, bina barf. Chacha ne glass bhar kar diya: paani **thanda** tha.

Vrushali ne ek ghoont pi kar kaha, "Chacha, matka subah wahi nalke ka paani bhar kar rakha gaya tha. Na usme barf, na koi machine. Phir ye mere gunguna paani se thanda kaise?"

Chacha muskuraaye. Unhone ek saaf kapda geela kiya, Vrushali ki steel bottle par lapeta, aur chhaon mein hawa ke aage rakh diya. "Ek ghante baad dekhna. Aur sunna: ye jaadu nahi, hisaab hai. Aaj hum ye bhi nikaalenge ki matka kitna paani evaporate karke baaki paani ko thanda karta hai."

## Part A: Thandak ka poora hisaab

### Garmi jaati kahan se hai?

Paani ke ek kan ko surface chhodna ho to use apne padosiyon ki khinchavat todni padti hai. Isme **energy** lagti hai, aur wahi energy bhaap ke saath chali jaati hai. Yahi hai latent heat: **1 kg paani ko bhaap banne mein L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg** lagti hai. Is chapter mein hum **boiling ya evaporation, dono ke liye yahi value** lete hain. (Asal mein thande paani par ye value thodi zyada hoti hai; hum shared value hi use karenge, isliye hamare jawab lagbhag hain.)

Ye energy do jagah se aa sakti hai: **bacha hua paani** (to wo thanda hota hai) aur **aas-paas**: hawa, matke ki deewar, dhoop ya hamari skin. Pehle hum sabse saadi soch lete hain: **saari garmi usi paani se aaye jo thanda ho raha hai** (bahar se kuch nahi aa raha). Phir dekhenge ki asal mein kya hota hai.

> **Evaporation ka hisaab:**
> Garmi jo paani se nikli = Q = m' × L_v  (m' = jitna paani evaporate hua, kg mein)
> Paani ka thanda hona = Q = m c ΔT  (m = bacha hua paani, c = 4200 J/kg °C)
> Saari garmi usi paani se aaye to: **m' × L_v = m × c × ΔT**

Bahut zaroori: **m' bahut chhota hota hai**, m ke mukabale. Isliye hum m ko "lagbhag wahi" maante hain.

### Example 1: Matke ka paani kitna ud-ana padega?

Matke mein 1.8 kg paani hai. Use 7 °C thanda karne ke liye kitna paani evaporate hona chahiye? (Bahar se koi garmi nahi aati, aur evaporate hue paani ka mass chhota hone se m ko 1.8 kg hi maano.)

- Given: m = 1.8 kg, ΔT = 7 °C, c = 4200 J/kg °C, L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg
- Find: m' (evaporate hua paani)
- Formula: m' L_v = m c ΔT
- Stage 1 (paani se kitni garmi nikli): Q = 1.8 × 4200 × 7 = 52920 J
- Stage 2 (itni garmi kitne paani ko bhaap banati hai): m' = Q ÷ L_v = 52920 ÷ 2260000 = 0.0234 kg
- Answer: m' ≈ **0.0234 kg ≈ 23.4 g**, yaani lagbhag 23 ml, **kuch hi chamach paani** 1.8 kg ko 7 °C thanda kar sakta hai.

**Jaanch:** 0.0234 kg × 2260000 J/kg = 52884 J ≈ 52920 J ✅ (rounding). Range check: 23.4 g lagbhag 1.8 kg ka 1.3% hai, to "m lagbhag wahi" maanna theek tha ✅.

> Dhyan do: 1.8 kg paani **7 °C** thanda hone mein sirf 23.4 g ka evaporate hona kaafi hai. Yahi evaporation ki taakat hai: **L_v bahut bada hai**, isliye thoda sa paani bahut garmi le jaata hai.

### Example 2: Kutta hanfta kyun hai?

Ek 12 kg ka kutta daudne ke baad garm ho gaya. Uska body temperature 0.6 °C badh gaya. Maan lo uska sharir paani jaisa (c = 4200 J/kg °C) hai: asal mein thoda kam hota hai, isliye ye **upar ki seema** hai. Use thanda karne ke liye jeebh aur saans ki nali se kitna paani evaporate hona chahiye?

- Given: m = 12 kg, ΔT = 0.6 °C, c = 4200 J/kg °C, L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg
- Find: m'
- Formula: m' = m c ΔT ÷ L_v
- Stage 1: Q = 12 × 4200 × 0.6 = 30240 J
- Stage 2: m' = 30240 ÷ 2260000 = 0.01338 kg
- Answer: m' ≈ **13.4 g** paani

**Jaanch:** 0.01338 × 2260000 = 30239 J ≈ 30240 J ✅. Range: 13 g lagbhag ek badi chamach paani hai, jo hanfne se kuch minute mein nikal sakta hai, to ye maanne layak hai ✅.

## Part B: Matka aur geela kapda

Matke ki mitti mein itne **chhote chhed** hote hain ki paani ek-ek boond kar ke **seep** kar bahar aata hai. Bahar ki sataha par ye paani ek patli parat bana kar **evaporate** hota hai. Ab dekho:

```
  ANDAR KA PAANI          MITTI KI DEEWAR         BAHAR KI HAWA
                        ┌───────────────────┐    bhaap ↑   ↑   ↑   ↑
                        │ o   o   o   o   o │    (evaporation)
                        │   (chhote chhed)  │──▶ bahari sataha par
  paani ──────────▶     │ o   o   o   o   o │    paani ki patli parat
                        │ paani seep kar    │
                        │ bahar aata hai    │
                        └───────────────────┘
  thanda hota paani                              garam, sookhi hawa
  ◀── evaporation ki garmi andar ke paani se aur
  ◀── bahar ki hawa aur dhoop se bhi garmi aati hai
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Matka: andar ka paani deewar ke chhedon se seep kar bahari sataha par aata hai aur wahan hawa mein evaporate hota hai. Evaporation ki garmi andar ke paani se aur bahar ki hawa aur dhoop se aati hai.

**Asli baat: do garmiyan ek saath chalti hain.** Evaporation garmi **nikaalta** hai, par bahar ki hawa aur dhoop garmi **andar bhejti** hain. Paani tab tak thanda hota jaata hai jab tak dono barabar na ho jaayein: us temperature par matke ka paani **tik jaata hai**. Isliye matka paani ko bahar ki hawa se **kuch degree** hi thanda kar paata hai, barf jitna nahi. Agar matke ko **hawa mein, chhaon mein** rakho to evaporation tez hota hai, to wo tikne wala temperature aur neeche aata hai.

### Example 3: Geele kapde ke saath bottle

Vrushali ki bottle mein 0.75 kg paani tha. Chhaon mein, tez hawa mein, lapete hue geele kapde se dopahar bhar mein lagbhag 85 g paani evaporate hua. Agar saari garmi bottle ke paani se hi jaati, to uska temperature kitna girta?

- Given: m' = 85 g = 0.085 kg, m = 0.75 kg, c = 4200 J/kg °C, L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg
- Find: ΔT (maan kar ki bahar se koi garmi nahi aati)
- Formula: m' L_v = m c ΔT
- Stage 1: Q = 0.085 × 2260000 = 192100 J
- Stage 2: ΔT = Q ÷ (m c) = 192100 ÷ (0.75 × 4200) = 192100 ÷ 3150 = 61 °C
- Answer: ΔT ≈ **61 °C**

**Jaanch:** 0.75 × 4200 × 61 = 192150 J ≈ 192100 J ✅. **Par ye asambhav hai**: gunguna paani 61 °C gir kar zero se neeche chala jaata, jo hua nahi. Matlab: bahar ki hawa aur dhoop se bahut saari garmi **lagatar andar aati rahi**, aur bottle sirf kuch degree thandi hui. Ye wahi "do garmiyan" wali baat hai: Example 1 jaisa saaf hisaab sirf **sabse zyada sambhav thandak** batata hai, asli thandak usse kaafi kam hoti hai.

### Ghar par adult ke saath: do bottle ka mukabla

Do ek jaisi steel ya plastic ki bottle lo, dono mein **nalke ka kamre ke temperature wala paani**. Ek par **geela** kapda lapeto, doosri par **sookha** kapda. Dono ko chhaon mein, ek hi jagah, ek ghante ke liye rakho (jahan hawa chalti ho to behtar). Phir **alcohol ya digital thermometer** se dono ka paani naapo, ya haath se chhoo kar tulna karo. Geele wali zyada thandi milegi. **Garam paani, tasting aur aag nahi.** Agle din dobara karke dekho: kapde ko dhoop mein rakho to kya badalta hai?

## Part C: Desert cooler ki cooling power

Desert cooler mein ek pump paani ko **geele pad** tak chadhata hai. Pankha garam, sookhi bahar ki hawa ko pad ke beech se kheenchta hai. Pad ka paani evaporate hota hai, hawa se garmi leta hai, aur hawa **thandi** ho kar kamre mein aati hai.

```
garam, sookhi   ┌───────────────┐       ┌─────────────┐
hawa bahar se   │ GEELA PAD     │       │             │
─────────────▶  │ (paani se tar)│       │  PANKHA     │ thandi, nam hawa
                │               │─────▶ │  (fan)      │ ─────────▶
                │ yahan paani   │       │             │ kamre mein
                │ evaporate hota│       │             │
                └───────────────┘       └─────────────┘
                    ▲             │
                    │ pump        ▼ tapakta
                ┌───────────────────────┐
                │ TANKI + PUMP          │
                │ paani ka bhandar      │
                │                       │
                └───────────────────────┘
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Desert cooler: bahar ki garam, sookhi hawa geele pad se guzarti hai, pad ka paani evaporate hokar hawa se garmi leta hai, aur pankha thandi, nam hawa kamre mein bhejta hai. Pump tanki se paani pad tak chadhata hai.

**Thandak ki taakat (power).** Cooler har second jitna paani evaporate karta hai, wo utni garmi hawa se kheenchta hai. Isliye:

> **Cooling power P = (evaporate hua paani, kg/s mein) × L_v**
> (watt ka matlab joule per second: [current-electricity-ii-part5.md](../current-electricity-ii/current-electricity-ii-part5.md) mein tha)

Dhyan do: ye evaporate hue paani se nikalta hai, tanki mein bhare paani se **nahi**. Pump jo paani wapas tanki mein tapkata hai, wo evaporate nahi hua.

### Example 4: Cooler ka 2.5 litre per ghanta

Ek cooler ka paani dopahar mein 2.5 litre per ghanta ghat-ta hai (sab evaporation se; 1 litre paani = 1 kg). Uski cooling power kitni hai?

- Given: 2.5 litre/ghanta = 2.5 kg per 3600 s, L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg
- Find: P
- Formula: P = (m' ÷ t) × L_v
- Stage 1 (kg/s mein): m' ÷ t = 2.5 ÷ 3600 = 0.000694 kg/s (yaani lagbhag 0.69 g har second)
- Stage 2: P = 0.000694 × 2260000 = 1569 W
- Answer: P ≈ **1570 W**, yaani lagbhag **1.57 kW**

**Jaanch:** ghante mein garmi = 2.5 × 2260000 = 5650000 J. Ise 3600 s se bhaago: 5650000 ÷ 3600 = 1569 W ✅.

Ek baat sambhal ke: **cooler ka asli faayda isse kam hota hai**, kyunki bahar ki hawa lagatar andar aati hai aur hawa mein nami bhi badhti hai. Phir bhi ek dopahar mein hazaar watt se zyada garmi ko hawa se kheench lena bada kaam hai.

### Hawa, surface aur temperature: kyun?

| Factor | Evaporation rate par asar | Kyun (garmi aur kan ke hisaab se) |
|---|---|---|
| Hawa tez chale | Badhta hai | Surface ke paas jo bhaap ban gayi, hawa use hata deti hai, to naye kan nikalte rehte hain |
| Surface area badi | Badhta hai | Zyada kan hawa se milte hain (thali vs katori; matka ki gol deewar vs bottle) |
| Paani garam | Badhta hai | Zyada kan itne tez hote hain ki nikal sakein |
| Hawa mein pehle se bhaap | Ghatta hai | Hawa mein jagah kam; wapas girte kan zyada (Part E) |

Isi liye **bheega ungli hawa ki disha batati hai**: ungli geeli karke upar utha lo. Jis taraf se hawa aati hai, wo taraf **sabse thandi** lagti hai, kyunki wahan evaporation sabse tez hai.

## Part D: Pasina, jaanwar aur tez evaporator

### Insaan ka pasina

Hard work mein insaan ghante mein lagbhag 0.5 se 1 litre pasina bahata hai (hedge: mausam aur insaan ke hisaab se badalta hai). **Sirf wahi pasina thandak deta hai jo evaporate hoti hai**: jo pasina boondon mein bah kar gir jaata hai, wo kuch nahi karta.

### Example 5: Pasine ki cooling power

Ek mazdoor garmi mein ghante mein 0.9 litre pasina bahata hai aur wo **poora evaporate** ho jaata hai. Evaporation se uske sharir ki cooling power kitni hai?

- Given: m' = 0.9 kg per 3600 s, L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg
- Find: P
- Formula: P = (m' ÷ t) × L_v
- Stage 1 (ghante mein garmi): Q = 0.9 × 2260000 = 2034000 J
- Stage 2 (watt mein): P = 2034000 ÷ 3600 = 565 W
- Answer: P ≈ **565 W**

**Jaanch:** 565 × 3600 = 2034000 J ✅. Hard work mein sharir kai sau watt garmi banata hai (lagbhag, hedged), to pasine ka ye 565 W hi use sambhaalta hai: isliye pasina dena **sharir ki zaroori suraksha** hai.

### Jaanwar kya karte hain?

- **Kutta** (Example 2): jeebh aur saans ki nali se evaporation: hanfna. Kutte ki skin par pasine ki gland kam hoti hain (lagbhag), isliye hanfna uska mukhya tareeka hai.
- **Bhains** paani ya keechad mein baithti hai: skin par lagi paani ki parat evaporate hoti hai (uski skin par pasine ki gland bahut kam hoti hain, aisa aksar bataya jaata hai).
- **Pakshi** chonch kholkar hanfte hain, aur kangaroo apne agle pairon ko chaat kar geela karta hai: dono mein evaporation hi kaam karta hai.

### Spirit ya acetone aur skin

Doctor ke saaf karne ke baad **spirit** lagi jagah par bahut thandi lagti hai: ye paani se **kaafi tezi se evaporate** hota hai. Isliye har second zyada garmi khinchta hai. (Iska apna L paani se kam hota hai, par evaporation itna tez hai ki cooling power zyada ho jaati hai.) Spirit ya acetone ka istemal sirf **bade aur sirf medical safai ke liye** karte hain: **kabhi saans mein nahi, kabhi spray nahi, aag ke paas kabhi nahi**, aur kisi bachche ko nahi chhoona.

## Part E: Hawa kitna paani rakh sakti hai?

Cooler aksar **barsaat mein fail** ho jaata hai aur matka bhi utna thanda nahi rehta. Kaaran: hawa mein bhaap rakhne ki ek **had** hai. Ek fixed temperature par 1 m³ hawa zyada se zyada utni hi bhaap rakh sakti hai jitni neeche table mein hai. Jab bhaap itni ho jaati hai, hawa **saturated** ho jaati hai.

**Table 3.1: air kitna paani rakh sakti hai** (1 m³ hawa mein zyada se zyada bhaap, lagbhag; standard values)

| Hawa ka temperature | Zyada se zyada bhaap (g/m³) |
|---|---|
| 5 °C | 6.8 |
| 10 °C | 9.4 |
| 15 °C | 12.8 |
| 20 °C | 17.3 |
| 25 °C | 23.0 |
| 30 °C | 30.4 |
| 35 °C | 39.6 |
| 40 °C | 51.1 |

In-between temperature ke liye **table se andaaza** lagate hain. Table mein dekho: **jitni garam hawa, utni zyada bhaap**. 40 °C par 5 °C se lagbhag 7.5 guna zyada (51.1 ÷ 6.8 ≈ 7.5).

Ab band dabbe mein paani rakho aur dekho kya hota hai:

```
  KHULA BARTAN                        BAND DABBA (hawa saturated)
  ↑   ↑   ↑   ↑                       ↑   ↑   ↑   ↑   (4 kan upar jaate)
  bhaap hawa mein door                ↓   ↓   ↓   ↓   (4 kan wapas girte)
  chali jaati hai
  ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~                ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
  paani ghatta jaata hai              paani ka level wahi rehta hai
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Khule bartan se bhaap door chali jaati hai, isliye paani ghatta jaata hai. Band dabbe mein jitne kan upar jaate hain, utne wapas aate hain (yahan 4 aur 4): hawa saturated hai, paani ka level wahi rehta hai. Kan abhi bhi aa-ja rahe hain, bas **net evaporation zero** hai.

**Dhyan do:** saturated hawa mein bhi kan nikalte rehte hain, par **utne hi wapas** aa jaate hain. Isliye paani ka level nahi ghatta, aur evaporation se **thandak bhi nahi** hoti, kyunki jitni garmi ja rahi hai, utni condensation wapas de deti hai.

### Example 6: Band bathroom mein geela tauliya

Ek band bathroom ka hawa ka volume 8 m³ hai, temperature 30 °C par fixed. Hawa shuru mein bahut sookhi hai (maan lo lagbhag 0 g/m³ bhaap). Geele tauliye mein 400 g paani hai. Zyada se zyada kitna paani evaporate ho sakta hai, aur kitna tauliye mein reh jaayega?

- Given: V = 8 m³, T = 30 °C (table: 30.4 g/m³), tauliye mein 400 g
- Find: evaporate hua paani, bacha hua paani
- Formula: zyada se zyada bhaap = V × saturation value
- Stage 1: 8 × 30.4 = 243.2 g
- Stage 2: 400 − 243.2 = 156.8 g (agar 243.2 g < 400 g)
- Answer: lagbhag **243 g** evaporate hoga, tauliye mein lagbhag **157 g** reh jaayega aur phir evaporation rukh jaayega.

**Jaanch:** 243.2 + 156.8 = 400 g ✅. Sach me: evaporation se hawa thandi hoti hai, to saturation value thodi ghat jaati hai, isliye asli evaporation 243 g se kuch kam hogi. Hum temperature fixed maan rahe hain.

**Cooler aur matka ka sabak:** humid, yaani nami bhari hawa pehle se kaafi bhaap rakhti hai, to usme paani utna evaporate nahi karta, aur thandak kam hoti hai. Hawa mein bhaap ka hisaab (aur wo kitni "bhari" hai ye ek percent mein) Topic 4 mein hoga.

## Part F: Fridge ka evaporator coil

Fridge ke andar ki thandak bhi evaporation se aati hai. Ek khaas liquid (refrigerant: iska naam abhi zaroori nahi) ek patli nali, **evaporator coil**, mein evaporate hota hai aur khane ki chamber se garmi **leta** hai. Compressor us bhaap ko dabata hai, peeche ki **condenser** coil mein wo dobara liquid banti hai aur wo garmi **kamre ki hawa ko** de deti hai (isiliye fridge ke peeche ki jaali garam hoti hai). Liquid ek patli nali se wapas coil tak jaata hai aur chakkar chalta rehta hai.

```
┌─────────────────────────────┐                  ┌─────────────────────────────┐
│ FRIDGE KE ANDAR             │                  │ FRIDGE KE PEECHE            │
│ EVAPORATOR COIL             │ ── bhaap ──▶     │ COMPRESSOR, phir            │
│ liquid yahan bhaap banta    │                  │ CONDENSER coil: bhaap       │
│ hai aur khane ki chamber    │                  │ dobara liquid banti hai     │
│ se garmi LETA hai           │ ◀── liquid ──    │ aur garmi bahar DETI hai    │
│                             │ (patli nali se)  │                             │
└─────────────────────────────┘                  └─────────────────────────────┘
  andar ka khana thanda hota hai                   peeche ki jaali garam hoti hai
```

> **Figure 3.4** – Fridge ka block diagram: andar ki coil mein liquid evaporate hokar garmi leta hai; peeche ki coil mein bhaap condense hokar wahi garmi bahar deti hai. (Sirf naam: detail aage ki class mein.)

Ye wahi evaporation hai: bas **bartan band hai** aur bhaap ko wapas liquid banaya jaata hai, taaki chakkar chalta rahe. Isiliye **fridge ka darwaza khula chhodne se kamra thanda nahi hota**: peeche ki coil andar se khinchi garmi **aur compressor ke kaam ki garmi** bhi kamre mein chhodti hai, to kamra thoda garam hi hota hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Evaporation se thandak isliye hoti hai kyunki garmi nasht (destroy) ho jaati hai."**
Nahi: garmi **bhaap ke andar** chali jaati hai (latent heat), nasht nahi hoti. Demonstration: 15 g paani evaporate hota hai to 0.015 × 2260000 = 33900 J garmi hawa aur paani se nikalti hai. Ab maan lo wahi 15 g bhaap kisi **thande glass par condense** ho jaaye: wo ye 33900 J **wapas de deti hai**, aur glass ki sataha garm hoti hai. Isiliye band dabbe mein (Figure 3.3) thandak nahi milti: garmi nikli bhi hai aur wapas bhi aa rahi hai. Energy kabhi gayab nahi hoti, wo bas jagah badalti hai.

**Galti 2: "Garmi sirf ubalne mein lagti hai, evaporation mein nahi."**
Nahi, evaporation mein bhi **L_v** jitni hi garmi lagti hai (hum is chapter mein wahi value lete hain). Demonstration: raat bhar kamre mein rakhe katore ka 6 g paani chupchap evaporate ho gaya. Kisi ne use garam nahi kiya, par 0.006 × 2260000 = 13560 J garmi **hawa aur katore ke paani se** gayi: isiliye subah katore ka paani kamre se thoda sa thanda mila. Fark sirf raftaar ka hai: ubalne mein ye garmi tezi se (kuch minute mein) jaati hai, evaporation mein dheere-dheere (kai ghante mein).

## Quick Recap

- Evaporation mein paani ke tez kan nikalte hain aur **garmi saath le jaate hain**: bacha paani aur aas-paas thanda hota hai. Garmi nasht nahi hoti, wo bhaap mein latent heat ban kar chali jaati hai.
- **m' L_v = m c ΔT** (agar saari garmi usi paani se aaye): sirf **chhota sa paani** (matke ke 1.8 kg ke liye 23.4 g) kaafi hai. Asal mein bahar se bhi garmi aati hai, isliye asli thandak kam hoti hai.
- **Cooling power P = (m' ÷ t) × L_v**: 2.5 litre per ghanta lagbhag 1570 W; 0.9 litre per ghanta pasina lagbhag 565 W.
- Hawa, surface area, garmi se evaporation badhta hai; hawa mein pehle se bhaap ho to ghatta hai.
- Hawa ek fixed temperature par ek had tak hi bhaap rakh sakti hai (table 3.1). Saturated hawa mein net evaporation zero, thandak bhi zero.
- Matka, geela kapda, pasina, kutte ka hanfna aur fridge ka evaporator coil, sab evaporation se garmi lete hain. Spirit tez evaporator hai (sirf bade, medical safai ke liye).

## Practice Questions

1. A kitchen matka holds 5.5 kg of water. Assume all the heat for cooling comes from this water and none from outside. How much water must evaporate to cool it by 3.5 °C? Use c(water) = 4200 J/kg °C and L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg.

2. A boy of 48 kg runs and his body temperature rises by 1.2 °C. Treat the body as water (c = 4200 J/kg °C). How much sweat must evaporate to cool him back? Use L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg.

3. A desert cooler tank holds 31 litres. It runs for 7.5 hours and the tank is empty at the end, with all the water having evaporated at a steady rate. Find the cooling power in watts. (1 litre = 1 kg; L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg.)

4. A small cooler evaporates 40 g of water every minute. (a) Find its cooling power in watts. (b) How much heat does it remove from the air in 18 minutes? Use L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg.

5. A cooler has a cooling power of 1340 W. How many litres of water must it evaporate in one hour? (1 litre = 1 kg; L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg.)

6. A steel pot holds 1.45 kg of water and is wrapped in a wet cloth. During the afternoon 9 g of water evaporates from the cloth. (a) If all the heat for this evaporation came from the water in the pot, by how much would the pot's water cool? (b) Why is the real fall much smaller? Use c(water) = 4200 J/kg °C and L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg.

7. A closed storeroom has a volume of 14 m³ and a fixed temperature of 20 °C. The air in it holds almost no vapour at the start. A tray holds 300 g of water. Use the saturation table in this topic. How much water can evaporate at most, and how much stays in the tray?

8. A closed box has a volume of 5 m³. Using the table in this topic, find the maximum amount of water vapour the box of air can hold at 10 °C and at 35 °C, and say how many times larger the second is than the first.

9. A wet sheet evaporates 3.1 g of water every minute. How long will it take to remove 94000 J of heat from the room air? Use L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg.

10. A worker sweats 0.65 litre per hour but only 70 percent of this sweat evaporates, while the rest drips off. (a) Find the cooling power from evaporation in watts. (b) Find the cooling power if all the sweat had evaporated. (1 litre = 1 kg; L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg.)

11. Explain in two or three lines why a refrigerator's rear grille feels warm even though its inside is cold.

12. State true or false and correct the false ones. (a) Saturated air stops all movement of water particles between liquid and air. (b) The matka's water can become much colder than the surrounding air in dry weather without any heat entering. (c) Evaporation of 1 g of water takes the same energy whether it comes from a hot cup or a cool cup in this chapter. (d) A wet finger feels coolest on the side from which the wind blows.

### Answers

1. Given: m = 5.5 kg, ΔT = 3.5 °C. Find: m'. Formula: m' L_v = m c ΔT.
   Stage 1: Q = 5.5 × 4200 × 3.5 = 80850 J. Stage 2: m' = 80850 ÷ 2260000 = 0.0358 kg.
   Answer: **lagbhag 35.8 g**.
   Jaanch: 0.0358 × 2260000 = 80908 J ≈ 80850 J ✅ (rounding).

2. Given: m = 48 kg, ΔT = 1.2 °C. Find: m'.
   Stage 1: Q = 48 × 4200 × 1.2 = 241920 J. Stage 2: m' = 241920 ÷ 2260000 = 0.107 kg.
   Answer: **lagbhag 107 g** pasina.
   Jaanch: 0.107 × 2260000 = 241820 J ≈ 241920 J ✅. Range: ek insaan ke ek ghante ke pasine (0.5 se 1 litre) se kaafi kam ✅.

3. Given: 31 kg in 7.5 h = 27000 s. Find: P.
   Stage 1: heat = 31 × 2260000 = 70060000 J. Stage 2: P = 70060000 ÷ 27000 = 2595 W.
   Answer: **lagbhag 2595 W** (lagbhag 2.6 kW).
   Jaanch: 2595 × 27000 = 70065000 J ≈ 70060000 J ✅. Rate = 31 ÷ 7.5 = 4.13 litre/ghanta, Example 4 ke 2.5 litre/ghanta se zyada, to power bhi zyada ✅.

4. Given: 40 g per min = 0.04 kg per 60 s.
   (a) Stage 1: rate = 0.04 ÷ 60 = 0.000667 kg/s. Stage 2: P = 0.000667 × 2260000 = 1507 W. 
   (b) Stage 3: 18 min = 18 × 60 = 1080 s. Heat = P × t = 1507 × 1080 = 1627000 J ≈ 1.63 × 10⁶ J.
   Jaanch: 18 × 0.04 = 0.72 kg paani; 0.72 × 2260000 = 1627200 J ✅.
   Answer: (a) **lagbhag 1507 W**, (b) **lagbhag 1.63 × 10⁶ J**.

5. Given: P = 1340 W = 1340 J/s. Find: m' per hour.
   Stage 1: heat in 1 h = 1340 × 3600 = 4824000 J. Stage 2: m' = 4824000 ÷ 2260000 = 2.13 kg.
   Answer: **lagbhag 2.13 litre** per ghanta.
   Jaanch: 2.13 × 2260000 ÷ 3600 = 1337 W ≈ 1340 W ✅.

6. (a) Given: m' = 9 g = 0.009 kg, m = 1.45 kg. Stage 1: Q = 0.009 × 2260000 = 20340 J. Stage 2: ΔT = 20340 ÷ (1.45 × 4200) = 20340 ÷ 6090 = 3.34 °C. Answer: **lagbhag 3.3 °C**.
   Jaanch: 1.45 × 4200 × 3.34 = 20341 J ≈ 20340 J ✅.
   (b) Pot ko hawa aur dhoop se lagatar garmi milti rehti hai (Part B ki do garmiyan), to asli fall isse kaafi kam hota hai.

7. Given: V = 14 m³, T = 20 °C (table: 17.3 g/m³), paani 300 g.
   Stage 1: zyada se zyada bhaap = 14 × 17.3 = 242.2 g. Stage 2: bacha paani = 300 − 242.2 = 57.8 g.
   Answer: **lagbhag 242 g** evaporate hoga, **lagbhag 58 g** tray mein bacha rahega.
   Jaanch: 242.2 + 57.8 = 300 g ✅. 242.2 g < 300 g, to hawa saturated ho gayi aur evaporation ruk gaya ✅.

8. Given: V = 5 m³, table: 10 °C 9.4, 35 °C 39.6.
   Stage 1: 10 °C par 5 × 9.4 = 47 g. Stage 2: 35 °C par 5 × 39.6 = 198 g. Stage 3: 198 ÷ 47 = 4.2.
   Answer: **47 g** aur **198 g**, doosra **lagbhag 4.2 guna** bada.
   Jaanch: 39.6 ÷ 9.4 = 4.21 ✅ (volume dono mein same, isliye ratio sirf table ki values ka hai).

9. Given: m' rate = 3.1 g/min = 0.0031 kg per 60 s, Q = 94000 J.
   Stage 1: rate = 0.0031 ÷ 60 = 5.17 × 10⁻⁵ kg/s. Stage 2: P = 5.17 × 10⁻⁵ × 2260000 = 116.8 W. Stage 3: t = 94000 ÷ 116.8 = 805 s.
   Answer: **lagbhag 805 s ≈ 13.4 minute**.
   Jaanch: 13.4 min × 3.1 g = 41.5 g; 0.0415 × 2260000 = 93800 J ≈ 94000 J ✅.

10. Given: 0.65 kg per 3600 s, evaporate hone wala 70%.
    (a) Stage 1: evaporate hua = 0.65 × 0.70 = 0.455 kg per ghanta. Stage 2: heat = 0.455 × 2260000 = 1028300 J. Stage 3: P = 1028300 ÷ 3600 = 286 W.
    (b) Stage 4: poora: 0.65 × 2260000 = 1469000 J, P = 1469000 ÷ 3600 = 408 W.
    Answer: (a) **lagbhag 286 W**, (b) **lagbhag 408 W**: boondon mein tapak kar jaane wala pasina kuch thandak nahi deta, 122 W ka nuksaan.
    Jaanch: 408 × 0.70 = 285.6 W ✅.

11. Andar coil mein liquid evaporate hoke garmi leta hai; compressor bhaap ko dabata hai aur peeche ki condenser coil mein wo condense hoti hai aur wahi garmi (aur compressor ka thoda kaam) bahar kamre ko deti hai. Isliye peeche ki jaali garam hoti hai.

12. (a) **False** ❌: kan lagatar aate-jaate rehte hain, bas dono taraf barabar. (b) **False** ❌: matka sirf tab tak thanda hota hai jab tak bahar se aati garmi aur evaporation ki garmi barabar na ho, to hawa se kuch hi degree thanda hota hai. (c) **True** ✅: is chapter mein hum dono ke liye wahi L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg lete hain. (d) **True** ✅: us taraf evaporation sabse tez hai.
