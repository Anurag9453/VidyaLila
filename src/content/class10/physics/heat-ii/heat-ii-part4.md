# Topic 4: Humidity Aur Dew Point: Hawa Mein Kitna Paani?

Topic 3 mein humne dekha ki evaporation se thandak tab milti hai jab paani hawa mein ja sake, aur hawa har temperature par sirf ek hadd tak paani ki bhaap rakh sakti hai. Us hadd ki ek table bhi thi. Ab sawal ye hai: **abhi hawa mein asal mein kitna paani hai**, aur wo us hadd ke kitne paas hai? Is ek sawal se mausam ki khabar mein bolne wale "RH" aur "dew point" ka matlab khulega.

## Part A: Class 9 se recall aur ek nayi soch

Class 9 ([heat-and-temperature-part5.md](../../../class9/physics/heat-and-temperature/heat-and-temperature-part5.md), Part G) mein hum ye jaan chuke hain: evaporation har temperature par surface se hota hai, aur wo **humidity** ke kam hone par tez hota hai. Pasine ke sookhne se thandak aati hai (wahan 10 g pasine ka hisaab bhi tha), kyunki bhaap garmi le jaati hai. Ab tak "humidity kam ya zyada" sirf shabdon mein thi. Yahan hum use **sankhya** denge.

Topic 3 ki table yahan phir rakhte hain: hawa ke 1 m³ mein kitna paani (g) **lagbhag** rakha ja sakta hai (saturation value). Beech ke temperature ke liye "table se andaaza" lagayenge.

| Temperature | 5 °C | 10 °C | 15 °C | 20 °C | 25 °C | 30 °C | 35 °C | 40 °C |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Adhiktam bhaap (g/m³), lagbhag | 6.8 | 9.4 | 12.8 | 17.3 | 23.0 | 30.4 | 39.6 | 51.1 |

Nayi soch ye hai: hawa mein bhaap ki do cheezein alag hain. Ek, **abhi kitni hai** (humidity, g/m³). Doosri, **us temperature par kitni ho sakti hai** (table). RH inka anupaat hai.

**Example 1 (kamre mein kul bhaap):** ek kamra 5 m × 4 m × 3 m ka hai aur uski hawa ki humidity lagbhag 14.5 g/m³ hai. Kamre mein kitni bhaap hai?

- **Given:** kamre ka volume = 5 × 4 × 3 = 60 m³; humidity = 14.5 g/m³
- **Find:** bhaap ka kul mass
- **Formula:** mass = humidity × volume
- **Substitution:** 14.5 g/m³ × 60 m³ = 870 g
- **Answer:** **870 g = 0.87 kg** bhaap (lagbhag 0.87 litre paani ke barabar)

**Jaanch:** 870 ÷ 60 = 14.5 ✅. Agar ye poori bhaap condense ho jaaye, to garmi niklegi 0.87 kg × 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg = 1.97 × 10⁶ J (lagbhag): wahi latent heat ki baat, ulti disha mein ✅.

## Part B: Relative humidity (RH)

**RH = (hawa mein abhi ki bhaap ÷ usi temperature par adhiktam bhaap) × 100 percent**

Isi ko dabav ki bhasha mein bhi kehte hain: RH = abhi ka vapour pressure ÷ saturation vapour pressure × 100. Hum yahan sirf upar wala (g/m³ wala) tareeka use karenge.

- RH 100 percent: hawa **saturated** hai, ab aur bhaap nahi le sakti.
- RH 50 percent: hawa apni hadd ka aadha rakhe hue hai.
- RH kam: hawa pyaasi hai, evaporation tez.

**Example 2 (subah ki hawa):** subah 15 °C par hawa ki humidity lagbhag 9.9 g/m³ hai. RH?

- **Given:** T = 15 °C, humidity = 9.9 g/m³; table: 15 °C par adhiktam = 12.8 g/m³
- **Find:** RH
- **Formula:** RH = abhi ÷ adhiktam × 100
- **Substitution:** RH = 9.9 ÷ 12.8 × 100 = 77.3 percent
- **Answer:** RH lagbhag **77 percent**

**Jaanch:** 77.3 percent of 12.8 = 9.9 ✅. Range check: 9.9 < 12.8, to RH 100 se kam ✅.

### Ek hi hawa, din bhar ka RH

Ye jaanna zaroori hai: **hawa mein bhaap badle bina bhi RH badal jaata hai**, kyunki table ki hadd temperature ke saath badalti hai. Maan lo ek hawa ka parcel band dabbe mein hai (volume fixed), aur uski bhaap 9.9 g/m³ hi rehti hai. Asal mein hawa failne se g/m³ thoda badalta hai, par ye ghatna samajhne ke liye hum use ignore karte hain.

**Example 3 (din se raat tak):** Example 2 wali hawa (9.9 g/m³) dopahar mein 35 °C, shaam ko 30 °C, phir 25 °C, 20 °C aur subah 15 °C ho gayi. Har temperature par RH?

- **Given:** bhaap = 9.9 g/m³ (sab jagah same); table ki adhiktam values 39.6, 30.4, 23.0, 17.3, 12.8 g/m³
- **Find:** har temperature par RH
- **Formula:** RH = 9.9 ÷ adhiktam × 100
- **Stage 1:** 35 °C: 9.9 ÷ 39.6 × 100 = 25.0 percent
- **Stage 2:** 30 °C: 9.9 ÷ 30.4 × 100 = 32.6 percent
- **Stage 3:** 25 °C: 9.9 ÷ 23.0 × 100 = 43.0 percent
- **Stage 4:** 20 °C: 9.9 ÷ 17.3 × 100 = 57.2 percent
- **Stage 5:** 15 °C: 9.9 ÷ 12.8 × 100 = 77.3 percent
- **Answer:** lagbhag **25, 33, 43, 57, 77 percent**

```
RH (%)    0         25        50        75      100
          +---------+---------+---------+---------+
35 C      ##########                                25 %
30 C      #############                             33 %
25 C      #################                         43 %
20 C      #######################                   57 %
15 C      ###############################           77 %
(1 block = 2.5 %; 10 C se thoda upar RH 100 % ho jaata hai)

```

> **Figure 4.1** – Ek hi hawa (9.9 g/m³ bhaap) ka RH jaise-jaise wo thandi hoti hai: dopahar ka sookha RH raat tak gila ho jaata hai. Bar ki lambai lagbhag hai (1 block = 2.5 percent).

**Jaanch:** jaise-jaise temperature ghata, adhiktam bhaap ghati aur RH sirf badha ✅ (sankhyaein ek sahi order mein: 25 < 33 < 43 < 57 < 77). 10 °C par table ki hadd 9.4 g/m³ hai, jo 9.9 se kam hai: yaani hawa 10 °C se **pehle hi** saturated ho jaayegi. Ye hi dew point ka vichaar hai.

## Part C: Dew point

Hawa ko thanda karte jao to table ki hadd girti jaati hai aur RH badhta jaata hai. Ek temperature aisa aata hai jahan hadd **abhi ki bhaap ke barabar** ho jaati hai: RH 100 percent. Wahi **dew point** hai. Isse thanda karo to extra bhaap ko condense hona padega.

Dhoondhne ka tareeka: table mein dekho kis temperature par adhiktam bhaap = abhi ki bhaap. Agar ye beech mein aaye to do pados ke temperature ke beech seedha andaaza (interpolation) lagao. Ye lagbhag sahi hai, kyunki table ke do pados mein curve thoda mudi hui hoti hai.

**Example 4 (seedha table se):** 35 °C ki hawa mein bhaap lagbhag 12.8 g/m³ hai. Dew point aur RH?

- **Given:** T = 35 °C, bhaap = 12.8 g/m³
- **Find:** dew point, RH
- **Formula:** dew point: table mein wo temperature jahan adhiktam = abhi ki bhaap; RH = abhi ÷ adhiktam (35 °C par) × 100
- **Stage 1 (dew point):** table mein 12.8 g/m³ ka row **15 °C** hai, to dew point = 15 °C
- **Stage 2 (RH):** 12.8 ÷ 39.6 × 100 = 32.3 percent
- **Answer:** dew point **15 °C**, RH lagbhag **32 percent**

**Jaanch:** 15 °C table ke 35 °C se neeche hai, jaisa hona chahiye (dew point kabhi hawa ke temperature se zyada nahi hota) ✅. 32 percent ka matlab hadd ka ek tihai: 0.323 × 39.6 = 12.8 ✅.

**Example 5 (beech ka andaaza):** 40 °C ki hawa mein bhaap lagbhag 26.5 g/m³ hai. Dew point aur RH?

- **Given:** T = 40 °C, bhaap = 26.5 g/m³; 25 °C par adhiktam 23.0 aur 30 °C par 30.4 g/m³
- **Find:** dew point, RH
- **Formula:** interpolation: T_d = 25 + 5 × (26.5 − 23.0) ÷ (30.4 − 23.0); RH = abhi ÷ adhiktam (40 °C par) × 100
- **Stage 1 (dew point):** 26.5 table ke 23.0 (25 °C) aur 30.4 (30 °C) ke beech hai. 5 × 3.5 ÷ 7.4 = 2.4; T_d = 25 + 2.4 = 27.4 °C
- **Stage 2 (RH):** 26.5 ÷ 51.1 × 100 = 51.9 percent
- **Answer:** dew point **lagbhag 27 °C**, RH lagbhag **52 percent**

**Jaanch:** 27.4 °C 25 aur 30 ke beech aur dew point 40 °C se neeche ✅. Ulta check: 27 °C par table-andaaze se adhiktam ≈ 23.0 + 0.4 × 7.4 = 26.0 g/m³, jo 26.5 ke paas hai ✅.

### RH dew point se

Dew point pata ho to RH nikalna bahut seedha hai. Dew point par hawa saturated hoti hai, yaani **abhi ki bhaap = dew point par adhiktam**. Isliye:

**RH = (dew point par adhiktam bhaap ÷ hawa ke temperature par adhiktam bhaap) × 100**

**Example 6 (dew point se RH):** hawa ka temperature 30 °C hai aur dew point 10 °C hai. RH?

- **Given:** T = 30 °C, dew point = 10 °C
- **Find:** RH
- **Formula:** RH = (adhiktam at dew point ÷ adhiktam at T) × 100
- **Substitution:** adhiktam at 10 °C = 9.4 g/m³; at 30 °C = 30.4 g/m³; RH = 9.4 ÷ 30.4 × 100 = 30.9 percent
- **Answer:** RH lagbhag **31 percent** (sookhi hawa)

**Jaanch:** 0.309 × 30.4 = 9.4 ✅; 31 percent 100 se kaafi kam aur dew point (10 °C) hawa ke temperature (30 °C) se kam ✅.

### Dew point aur RH mein kaun behtar batata hai?

RH temperature ke saath badalta rehta hai, jabki dew point hawa ki **asal bhaap** ka naap hai. Isliye mausam ki khabar mein aksar dono diye jaate hain, aur dew point jyada ho to hawa lagbhag hamesha "chipchipi" lagti hai (aam taur par bataya jaata hai: dew point 20 °C se upar zyadatar logon ko umas bhara lagta hai).

## Part D: TV ki khabar se hawa ka temperature

**Example 7 (Mama aur Saurabh ki khabar):** TV ne kaha "RH 84 percent aur dew point 24 °C". Table 20 °C (17.3 g/m³) aur 25 °C (23.0 g/m³) ke beech hai. Hawa ka asal temperature?

- **Given:** RH = 84 percent; dew point = 24 °C
- **Find:** hawa ka temperature
- **Formula:** abhi ki bhaap = adhiktam at dew point; adhiktam at T = abhi ki bhaap ÷ RH
- **Stage 1:** adhiktam at 24 °C table se: 17.3 + (24 − 20) ÷ 5 × (23.0 − 17.3) = 17.3 + 4.56 = 21.9 g/m³ (andaaza)
- **Stage 2:** adhiktam at T = 21.9 ÷ 0.84 = 26.0 g/m³
- **Stage 3:** 26.0 table ke 23.0 (25 °C) aur 30.4 (30 °C) ke beech hai: T = 25 + 5 × (26.0 − 23.0) ÷ (30.4 − 23.0) = 25 + 2.0 = 27.0 °C
- **Answer:** hawa ka temperature **lagbhag 27 °C**

**Jaanch:** 27 °C par adhiktam (andaaza) 23.0 + 0.4 × 7.4 = 26.0 g/m³; RH = 21.9 ÷ 26.0 × 100 = 84.2 percent ✅. Dew point (24 °C) hawa ke temperature (27 °C) se neeche hai, jaisa hona chahiye ✅.

Saurabh bol hi pada, "Mama, 27 aur 24: bas 3 degree ka fark. Matlab raat ko zara sa thanda hote hi hawa saturated ho jaayegi?" Mama ne sir hilaya, "Bilkul. Hawa ke temperature aur dew point ka fark jitna kam, hawa utni nami se bhari."

**Example 8 (kapde kyun nahi sookhte):** gile kapdon mein lagbhag 600 g paani hai. Ek barsaati din hawa 25 °C aur RH 92 percent hai. Ek sookhi, garam dopahar mein hawa 35 °C aur RH 30 percent hai. Dono din kapde sukhane ke liye kam se kam kitni hawa ko kapdon par se gujarna padega (sirf andaaza: har hawa ko saturated hone tak lete hain)?

- **Given:** paani = 600 g; barsaat: 25 °C, RH 92 percent; sookha din: 35 °C, RH 30 percent
- **Find:** kam se kam hawa ka volume (m³)
- **Formula:** hawa ki "khaali jagah" = (1 − RH) × adhiktam; zaroori volume = paani ÷ khaali jagah
- **Stage 1 (barsaat):** khaali jagah = (1 − 0.92) × 23.0 = 1.84 g/m³; volume = 600 ÷ 1.84 = 326 m³
- **Stage 2 (sookha din):** khaali jagah = (1 − 0.30) × 39.6 = 27.7 g/m³; volume = 600 ÷ 27.72 = 21.6 m³
- **Stage 3 (energy, dono din same):** 0.6 kg × 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg = 1.356 × 10⁶ J (lagbhag)
- **Answer:** barsaat mein **lagbhag 326 m³**, sookhe din **lagbhag 21.6 m³**: barsaat mein lagbhag **15 guna** zyada hawa chahiye

**Jaanch:** 1.84 × 326 = 600 ✅; 27.72 × 21.6 = 599 ✅ (rounding). Energy dono din barabar hai (kapde se paani utna hi evaporate hona hai), par barsaat mein hawa nami se bhari hone ki wajah se wo bahut dheere hota hai. Real mein hawa kabhi poori saturate nahi hoti, isliye ye hisaab sirf **kam se kam** batata hai.

## Part E: Garmi mein nami aur "feels-like"

Sawan ki dopahar mein 35 °C par bhi insaan jyada pareshan kyun hota hai, jabki sookhi garmi mein 40 °C bhi jhel jaata hai? Topic 3 se yaad karo: pasina tab thanda karta hai jab wo **evaporate** ho. Table se dekhna chahiye: 35 °C par RH 30 percent ho to hawa abhi aur 27.7 g/m³ le sakti hai; RH 90 percent ho to sirf 4.0 g/m³. Pasine ko jaane ki jagah hi nahi milti. Pasina boondon mein bahta hai, par evaporate nahi hota, to thandak kam milti hai (sharir ko thanda rakhne ke tareekon mein pasina ek bada tareeka hai). Isiliye shareer ko asal temperature se zyada garam mehsoos hota hai. Is asar ko mausam wale **heat index** (feels-like temperature) mein dikhate hain: ek formula se milta hai, jo hum yahan nahi seekhenge.

**Desert cooler aur AC par asar:** cooler bhi evaporation par chalta hai, isiliye nami wali hawa mein kam thanda karta hai: wahi baat, wahi table.

## Part F: Nami naapne ke yantra

### Wet-and-dry-bulb hygrometer

Isme **do thermometer** ek saath lagte hain. Ek khula (**dry bulb**, sookha). Doosre ke bulb par geela kapda lipta hota hai jiska doosra sira paani ke katore mein dooba hai (**wet bulb**, geela). Wet bulb par paani lagatar evaporate hota hai, to uska temperature kam padta hai (Topic 3: evaporation se thandak).

```
  SOOKHA BULB                               GEELA BULB
  (udaharan: 28 C)                          (udaharan: 22 C)

        ( )                                       ( )
        ( )                                       ( )
        ( )                                       ( )
       (###)  <-- bulb khuli hawa mein           (###)
                                                  ~~~   <-- geela kapda (wick)
                                                  ~~~
                                            [~~~~~~~~~~~~]
                                            [ saaf paani ]
                                            [____________]

```

> **Figure 4.2** – Wet-and-dry-bulb hygrometer ka khaka. Bulb ke aas paas hawa chalti rahe to evaporation sahi dikhta hai. Figure mein 28 °C aur 22 °C sirf udaharan hain.

- **Sookhi hawa:** zyada evaporation, wet bulb bahut neeche, **bada fark**.
- **Nami wali hawa:** kam evaporation, wet bulb dry bulb ke paas, **chhota fark**.
- **Saturated hawa (RH 100 percent):** evaporation lagbhag ruk jaata hai, **fark zero**.

Is fark ko ek chart se RH mein badalte hain (chart hum yahan nahi dete: sirf sochna hai ki **fark jitna bada, hawa utni sookhi**).

### Hair hygrometer aur cobalt chloride paper

**Hair hygrometer** mein insaani baal nami badhne par thoda lamba ho jaata hai, aur ek soi ghoomti hai. **Cobalt chloride paper** (sirf naam yaad rakho) nami lagne par neela se gulabi ho jaata hai. Silica gel ke packet mein bhi kabhi-kabhi aisa rangeen daana hota hai. Ye sirf teacher ke saath dekhne ke liye hai: chhoona ya chakhna nahi.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "RH 100 percent matlab hawa mein paani ki boondein hain, baarish ho rahi hai."**
Nahi: RH 100 percent ka matlab hawa **saturated** hai: wo aur bhaap nahi le sakti. Bhaap **bina rang ki, aankhon se dikhti nahi**. Demonstration: ek saaf, thandi subah ka temperature 5 °C hai aur hawa mein lagbhag 6.8 g/m³ bhaap hai. Table ke hisaab se 5 °C par adhiktam bhi 6.8 g/m³ hai, yaani RH = 6.8 ÷ 6.8 × 100 = 100 percent. Phir bhi abhi koi boond nahi bani, aasmaan saaf hai: boondein tab banti hain jab hawa **aur thandi ho** (ya thandi satah se mile) aur extra bhaap condense ho. RH 100 percent **sirf shuruaat** hai, baarish nahi.

**Galti 2: "Relative humidity hawa mein paani ki matra hai."**
Nahi: RH ek **anupaat** hai, matra nahi. Demonstration: do jagah ka RH barabar 60 percent hai: ek 40 °C ki garam dopahar, doosri 5 °C ki thandi subah. Garam dopahar mein bhaap = 0.60 × 51.1 = 30.7 g/m³. Thandi subah mein bhaap = 0.60 × 6.8 = 4.1 g/m³. RH barabar, par paani ki matra lagbhag **7.5 guna** alag (30.66 ÷ 4.08 = 7.5). Matra jaanni ho to humidity (g/m³) dekho, RH nahi.

## Quick Recap

- **Humidity** = hawa ke 1 m³ mein bhaap ka mass (g/m³). **Saturation value** table se milti hai aur temperature ke saath badhti hai.
- **RH = abhi ki bhaap ÷ usi temperature par adhiktam × 100 percent.** Ye anupaat hai, matra nahi. RH 100 percent = saturated, baarish nahi.
- Bhaap same ho to garam dopahar mein RH kam, thandi raat mein RH zyada (Figure 4.1).
- **Dew point**: wo temperature jahan table ki hadd abhi ki bhaap ke barabar ho jaati hai. Table mein seedha dhoondho, beech ho to andaaza (interpolation).
- **RH = adhiktam at dew point ÷ adhiktam at hawa ka temperature × 100.** Dew point jitna hawa ke temperature ke paas, utni umas.
- Wet bulb dry bulb se thanda: evaporation se. **Bada fark = sookhi hawa**, zero fark = saturated hawa. Hair hygrometer aur cobalt chloride paper ke naam yaad rakho.
- Umas mein pasina nahi sookhta, isliye jyada garam lagta hai (**heat index**). Barsaat mein kapde isi wajah se der se sookhte hain: energy barabar, par hawa ke paas "khaali jagah" kam.

## Practice Questions

Use the saturation table given in this topic (5 °C 6.8, 10 °C 9.4, 15 °C 12.8, 20 °C 17.3, 25 °C 23.0, 30 °C 30.4, 35 °C 39.6, 40 °C 51.1 g/m³). Use L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg where needed. For in-between values estimate from the table.

1. Air at 25 °C holds 11.5 g/m³ of water vapour. Find its relative humidity.
2. Air at 35 °C holds 18 g/m³ of water vapour. Find its relative humidity.
3. Air at 30 °C holds 24 g/m³ of water vapour. (a) Find its RH. (b) Estimate its dew point.
4. Air at 20 °C holds 12 g/m³ of water vapour. (a) Find its RH. (b) Estimate its dew point.
5. A room measures 3 m × 4 m × 2.5 m. Its air is at 25 °C and has RH 40 percent. Find the mass of water vapour in the room.
6. Air at 15 °C has RH 68 percent. Find the vapour content of the air in g/m³ and estimate its dew point.
7. On a hot day the air at 35 °C has RH 85 percent. Estimate its dew point and say whether sweat can evaporate easily.
8. In the evening, 40 m³ of air at 30 °C holds 15.0 g/m³ of vapour. (a) Find its RH. (b) Estimate the dew point. (c) The air cools to 10 °C during the night with its volume kept at 40 m³. Find the mass of vapour that condenses. (d) Find the heat released by this condensation.
9. Air at 30 °C has a dew point of 5 °C. Find the RH.
10. City P has air at 40 °C with dew point 20 °C. City Q has air at 25 °C with the same dew point 20 °C. Find the RH of each. Which city feels more humid, and which air holds less water in total?
11. Two days have the same dry-bulb reading of 32 °C. On day X the wet bulb shows 31 °C, and on day Y it shows 23 °C. (a) Which day is more humid? (b) Why does the wet bulb read lower than the dry bulb? (c) What would the difference be if the air were saturated?
12. Wet clothes contain 450 g of water. The air is at 20 °C with RH 35 percent. Find (a) the minimum volume of this air that must pass over the clothes to dry them (assume the air ends saturated), and (b) the heat needed to evaporate the water.
13. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) RH 100 percent means it is raining. (b) A person on a humid day feels hotter because sweat evaporates slowly. (c) If the air temperature falls and the vapour stays the same, RH rises. (d) Dew point is always higher than the air temperature.

### Answers

1. **Given:** T = 25 °C, vapour = 11.5 g/m³; adhiktam = 23.0 g/m³. **Formula:** RH = abhi ÷ adhiktam × 100. **Substitution:** 11.5 ÷ 23.0 × 100 = 50. **Answer:** RH **50 percent**. **Jaanch:** 0.5 × 23.0 = 11.5 ✅
2. **Given:** T = 35 °C, 18 g/m³; adhiktam = 39.6. **Substitution:** 18 ÷ 39.6 × 100 = 45.5. **Answer:** RH lagbhag **45 percent**. **Jaanch:** 0.455 × 39.6 = 18.0 ✅
3. (a) 24 ÷ 30.4 × 100 = 78.9, **RH lagbhag 79 percent**. (b) 24 g/m³ table ke 23.0 (25 °C) aur 30.4 (30 °C) ke beech hai: T_d = 25 + 5 × (24 − 23.0) ÷ (30.4 − 23.0) = 25 + 0.68 = 25.7, **dew point lagbhag 26 °C**. **Jaanch:** 26 °C < 30 °C ✅; 0.789 × 30.4 = 24.0 ✅
4. (a) 12 ÷ 17.3 × 100 = 69.4, **RH lagbhag 69 percent**. (b) 12 table ke 9.4 (10 °C) aur 12.8 (15 °C) ke beech: T_d = 10 + 5 × (12 − 9.4) ÷ (12.8 − 9.4) = 10 + 3.82 = 13.8, **dew point lagbhag 14 °C**. **Jaanch:** 14 °C < 20 °C ✅
5. **Given:** volume = 3 × 4 × 2.5 = 30 m³, T = 25 °C, RH = 40 percent. **Stage 1:** bhaap = 0.40 × 23.0 = 9.2 g/m³. **Stage 2:** mass = 9.2 × 30 = 276 g. **Answer:** **276 g = 0.276 kg**. **Jaanch:** 276 ÷ 30 = 9.2 ✅ aur 9.2 ÷ 23.0 = 40 percent ✅
6. **Stage 1:** bhaap = 0.68 × 12.8 = 8.70 g/m³. **Stage 2:** 8.70 table ke 6.8 (5 °C) aur 9.4 (10 °C) ke beech: T_d = 5 + 5 × (8.70 − 6.8) ÷ (9.4 − 6.8) = 5 + 3.65 = 8.7. **Answer:** **lagbhag 8.7 g/m³** aur dew point **lagbhag 9 °C**. **Jaanch:** 9 °C < 15 °C ✅
7. **Stage 1:** bhaap = 0.85 × 39.6 = 33.7 g/m³. **Stage 2:** 33.7 table ke 30.4 (30 °C) aur 39.6 (35 °C) ke beech: T_d = 30 + 5 × (33.7 − 30.4) ÷ (39.6 − 30.4) = 30 + 1.8 = 31.8. **Answer:** dew point **lagbhag 32 °C**. Hawa apni hadd ke 85 percent par hai, to adhiktam mein sirf 15 percent bacha: sweat ko kam jagah, isliye wo **dheere** sookhta hai (Part E). **Jaanch:** 32 °C < 35 °C ✅
8. (a) 15.0 ÷ 30.4 × 100 = 49.3, **RH lagbhag 49 percent**. (b) 15.0 table ke 12.8 (15 °C) aur 17.3 (20 °C) ke beech: T_d = 15 + 5 × (15.0 − 12.8) ÷ (17.3 − 12.8) = 15 + 2.4 = 17.4, **dew point lagbhag 17 °C**. (c) 10 °C par adhiktam 9.4 g/m³, to condense = 15.0 − 9.4 = 5.6 g/m³; 40 m³ mein 5.6 × 40 = 224 g = **0.224 kg**. (d) Q = m L_v = 0.224 kg × 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg = 506240 J, **lagbhag 5.06 × 10⁵ J**. **Jaanch:** dew point 17.4 °C, jo 10 °C se zyada hai, isliye hawa 10 °C tak pahunchne se pehle hi saturated ho chuki hogi ✅; 5.6 × 40 = 224 ✅
9. **Given:** T = 30 °C, dew point = 5 °C. **Formula:** RH = adhiktam at dew point ÷ adhiktam at T × 100. **Substitution:** 6.8 ÷ 30.4 × 100 = 22.4. **Answer:** **RH lagbhag 22 percent** (bahut sookhi hawa). **Jaanch:** 0.224 × 30.4 = 6.8 ✅
10. **City P:** 17.3 ÷ 51.1 × 100 = 33.9, RH lagbhag **34 percent**. **City Q:** 17.3 ÷ 23.0 × 100 = 75.2, RH lagbhag **75 percent**. City Q jyada umas wali lagegi (pasina kam sookhega). Paani ki kul matra: dew point barabar (20 °C) hai, to dono jagah bhaap **17.3 g/m³** hai: **kisi mein kam ya zyada nahi, barabar**. **Jaanch:** dono RH 100 se kam aur dew point (20 °C) dono temperature se kam ✅
11. (a) **Day X** (fark sirf 1 °C, chhota fark = nami wali hawa; day Y mein fark 9 °C, sookhi hawa). (b) Wet bulb par paani evaporate hota hai aur latent heat (m L_v) bulb se leta hai, isliye wo thanda ho jaata hai. (c) Saturated hawa mein evaporation nahi hota, to **fark zero** (RH 100 percent). **Jaanch:** 32 − 31 = 1 °C aur 32 − 23 = 9 °C ✅
12. **Stage 1:** adhiktam at 20 °C = 17.3 g/m³; khaali jagah = (1 − 0.35) × 17.3 = 11.2 g/m³. **Stage 2:** volume = 450 ÷ 11.245 = 40.0 m³. **Stage 3:** Q = 0.45 kg × 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg = 1017000 J. **Answer:** **lagbhag 40 m³** hawa aur **lagbhag 1.02 × 10⁶ J** garmi. **Jaanch:** 11.245 × 40 = 450 ✅; 0.45 × 2260000 = 1017000 ✅
13. (a) **False** ❌: RH 100 percent matlab saturated, baarish nahi. (b) **True** ✅: umas mein sweat ko jaane ki jagah kam. (c) **True** ✅: adhiktam ghatti hai aur bhaap wahi, to anupaat badhta hai. (d) **False** ❌: dew point hawa ke temperature se **kabhi zyada nahi** hota; zyada hone par to wo hawa pehle hi saturate ho chuki hogi (RH 100 percent mein barabar).
