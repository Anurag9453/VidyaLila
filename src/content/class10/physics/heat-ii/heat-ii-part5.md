# Topic 5: Dew, Fog, Baadal Aur Baarish: Humidity Ka Weather

Topic 4 mein humne hawa ke do number seekhe: **RH** (abhi ki bhaap hadd ke kitne percent) aur **dew point** (wo temperature jahan hadd abhi ki bhaap ke barabar ho jaati hai). Wahan hawa ko thanda karne par jo hota hai, wo sirf "extra bhaap condense hogi" tak raha. Ab dekhte hain ki **kudrat mein ye condensation kahan-kahan hota hai**: ghaas par, sadak par, aasmaan mein. Dew, kohra, baadal aur baarish, sab usi ek table ke alag-alag roop hain.

## Shirodkar Mama ka lawn, mirror aur highway

Sardiyon ki ek subah Shirodkar Mama apni bhanji Rashmi ko gaadi mein bithakar gaon ke ghar se shehar ki taraf nikle. Chhat se utarte waqt Rashmi ne lawn dekha: ghaas ki har patti par chhoti-chhoti chamakti boondein thi, jaise kisi ne raat bhar moti bikher diye hon. Usne ghaas ko haath lagaya aur haath geela ho gaya. Ghar ke andar bathroom ka sheesha bhi dhundhla padha tha, kyunki Mama ne abhi garam paani se nahaya tha (garam paani aur geyser bade ke kaam hain: Rashmi ne door se hi dekha).

Phir highway par gaadi dhire-dhire chali. Samne sadak sirf kuch gaadi-lambai tak dikh rahi thi: peela-safed kohra, aur headlight ki do dhundhli kiranein. Rashmi ne khidki par ungli rakhi to wahan bhi nami thi.

"Mama," Rashmi boli, "ye dew aasmaan se girti hai na? Aur kohra to baadal jaisa hi dikhta hai: kya wo baadal hi hai jo neeche aa gaya?"

Mama muskuraaye, "Ek sawal ke andar do galatfehmiyan chhupi hain, Rashmi. Aaj ke topic mein table aur dew point se hi hum dew, kohra, baadal aur baarish ka hisaab lagayenge. Aur ant mein dekhenge ki tumhara bathroom ka sheesha aur kamre ka dehumidifier bhi usi kahani ka hissa hain."

## Part A: Class 9 se recall, aur dew kahan se aati hai

Class 9 ([heat-and-temperature-part5.md](../../../class9/physics/heat-and-temperature/heat-and-temperature-part5.md), Part G) mein ye pata chala tha ki evaporation aur condensation ulte hain, aur nami kam ho to evaporation tez hota hai. Topic 4 ne wahi baat table aur anupaat se tauli. Ab ek hi bhaav: **jab bhi kisi hawa ko thanda karke uske dew point se neeche le jaaya jaata hai, to extra bhaap boondon mein badal jaati hai.** Ye "thanda karna" kai tarah se hota hai: ek thandi satah se chhoone par (dew), zameen ke paas ki saari hawa ke thanda hone par (kohra), ya oopar uthne par (baadal).

Topic 3 ki table phir yahan hai (kaam aayegi):

| Temperature | 5 °C | 10 °C | 15 °C | 20 °C | 25 °C | 30 °C | 35 °C | 40 °C |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Adhiktam bhaap (g/m³), lagbhag | 6.8 | 9.4 | 12.8 | 17.3 | 23.0 | 30.4 | 39.6 | 51.1 |

Beech ke temperature ke liye "table se andaaza" (seedha interpolation) lagayenge, jaisa Topic 4 mein kiya tha.

### Raat ko ghaas hawa se zyada thandi kyun?

Din bhar zameen aur ghaas sooraj se garmi lete hain. Raat ko ye apni garmi **infrared radiation** ke roop mein chhodte rehte hain (har garam cheez infrared deti hai: Class 9 ke [heat-and-temperature-part6.md](../../../class9/physics/heat-and-temperature/heat-and-temperature-part6.md) aur Class 10 ke [scattering-and-spectrum-part4.md](../scattering-and-spectrum/scattering-and-spectrum-part4.md) mein dekha tha). **Saaf aasmaan** ho to ye garmi seedhi oopar nikal jaati hai. **Baadal** ho to wo is garmi ka kuch hissa wapas neeche bhej dete hain, jaise ek kambal. Ghaas ki patti patli aur halki hoti hai, to wo hawa se bhi **kai degree** thandi (lagbhag, mausam par depend) ho sakti hai. Agar patti ka temperature dew point se neeche gaya, to uske paas ki hawa saturated ho jaati hai aur bhaap patti par boondein ban jaati hai: wahi **dew**.

```
  SAAF, SHAANT RAAT                     BAADAL WALI RAAT
     ↑     ↑     ↑     ↑                (~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~)
     ↑     ↑     ↑     ↑                   ↓      ↓      ↓      ↓
     ↑     ↑     ↑     ↑                   ↑      ↑      ↑      ↑
  garmi (infrared) seedhi               baadal garmi ka kuch hissa
  upar nikal jaati hai                  wapas neeche bhej deta hai

  /\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\        /\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\
  ghaas bahut thandi (dew point         ghaas kam thandi (dew point
  se neeche): ghaas par boondein        se upar): dew kam ya nahi
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Saaf, shaant raat (baayein) aur baadal wali raat (daayein). Saaf raat mein garmi (↑) seedhi oopar nikal jaati hai, ghaas dew point se neeche thandi hoti hai aur dew banti hai. Baadal garmi ka kuch hissa (↓) wapas bhej deta hai, to ghaas kam thandi hoti hai. Tez hawa chalne par bhi dew kam banti hai, kyunki hawa patti ko garam hawa se milati rehti hai aur patti itni thandi nahi ho paati.

Ek aur dilchasp baat: patti ke kinaare par kabhi-kabhi boondein **patti ke andar se** bhi nikalti hain (paudhe ka apna paani); sirf dew hi nahi. Isliye har boond ko "dew" kehna theek nahi, par zyadatar boondein hawa ki bhaap se hi banti hain.

**Example 1 (lawn par dew):** raat ko lawn ke paas ki hawa lagbhag 15 °C par hai aur usme lagbhag 9.4 g/m³ bhaap hai. Raat ke aakhir mein ghaas ki patti lagbhag 6 °C tak thandi ho jaati hai. (a) Dew point? (b) Kya dew banegi? (c) Jo hawa patti ko chhoo kar 6 °C tak thandi hui, uske har 1 m³ se kitni bhaap condense hogi? (d) Is condensation se kitni garmi nikalegi?

- **Given:** hawa 15 °C, bhaap = 9.4 g/m³; patti = 6 °C; table: 5 °C par 6.8, 10 °C par 9.4 g/m³
- **Find:** dew point, dew banegi ya nahi, condensed mass aur garmi (har 1 m³ ke liye)
- **Formula:** dew point = table mein wo temperature jahan adhiktam = abhi ki bhaap; condensed = abhi ki bhaap − (patti ke temperature par adhiktam); Q = m L_v
- **Stage 1 (dew point):** table mein 9.4 g/m³ ka row **10 °C** hai, to dew point = 10 °C
- **Stage 2 (dew?):** patti 6 °C < 10 °C, to dew banegi
- **Stage 3 (6 °C par adhiktam):** 5 °C aur 10 °C ke beech: 6.8 + (6 − 5) ÷ 5 × (9.4 − 6.8) = 6.8 + 0.52 = 7.32 g/m³
- **Stage 4 (condensed):** 9.4 − 7.32 = 2.08 g/m³
- **Stage 5 (garmi):** 2.08 g = 0.00208 kg; Q = 0.00208 × 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg = 4701 J (lagbhag 4.7 kJ)
- **Answer:** dew point **10 °C**; dew banegi; har m³ se **lagbhag 2.1 g** bhaap condense; **lagbhag 4.7 kJ** garmi

**Jaanch:** 7.32 + 2.08 = 9.40 ✅. Dew point (10 °C) hawa ke temperature (15 °C) se neeche hai ✅. Ye 4.7 kJ garmi patti aur hawa ko milti hai, isliye raat ka thanda hona **ruk-ruk kar** hota hai: condensation khud ek garam karne wala asar hai (L_v ulti disha mein, Topic 4 Example 1 jaisa).

### Frost (paala)

Agar satah ka temperature **0 °C se neeche** jaaye aur hawa ka dew point bhi kam ho, to bhaap boondon ki jagah seedhe **barf ke chhote crystal** ban jaati hai: ye **frost** hai. Ye jami hui dew nahi hai: jami hui dew alag hoti hai, jab pehle boondein banti hain aur phir jam jaati hain. Frost mein bhaap seedha thos ban jaati hai (Class 9 ke sublimation ka ulta). Ye table ke 5 °C se neeche ka maamla hai, isliye hum yahan sankhyaein nahi nikalenge: sirf samajhna hai ki "dew point 0 °C se neeche" ka matlab frost hai.

## Part B: Fog aur mist

Dew mein sirf thandi satah ke paas ki hawa saturate hoti hai. **Fog** mein **zameen ke paas ki poori hawa ki parat** dew point tak thandi ho jaati hai, aur hawa ke andar hi boondein ban jaati hain. Ye usually **lambi, saaf, shaant sardi ki raat** mein hota hai: zameen garmi chhodti rehti hai, uske paas ki hawa thandi hoti rehti hai, aur subah tak hawa ka temperature dew point ke aas-paas aa jaata hai. Ghaati wali jagah (valley) mein thandi hawa neeche baithti hai, isliye wahan fog ghana hota hai.

Boondein bahut chhoti hoti hain (lagbhag ek baal ki motai se bhi kai guna patli), isliye wo girti nahi, hawa mein tairti rehti hain. Unhe banne ke liye kuch **kan (condensation nuclei)** chahiye: dhool, dhuan, namak. Dhuen wale shehar mein kan zyada hote hain, isliye wahan kohra aksar ghana ban jaata hai (hedged: aur bhi factor hote hain).

Dikhne ki doori **lagbhag 1 km se kam** ho to usse fog, usse thodi kam ghani ko mist kehte hain. Kohra safed kyun dikhta hai? Boondein bade kan hain aur sab rang lagbhag barabar bikherti hain: ye [scattering-and-spectrum-part2.md](../scattering-and-spectrum/scattering-and-spectrum-part2.md) (Part E) mein dekha tha. Kohre mein gaadi ki **low beam** aur fog-lamp kaam aati hai: **high beam** boondon se bikhar kar seedha chalak ki aankhon mein laut aati hai aur raasta aur kam dikhta hai.

**Example 2 (highway ka kohra):** sham ko highway ke paas ki hawa lagbhag 10 °C aur RH 90 percent hai. Raat ko hawa lagbhag 8 °C tak thandi ho jaati hai. Table 5 °C (6.8 g/m³) aur 10 °C (9.4 g/m³) ke beech hai. (a) Dew point? (b) Kohra banega? (c) Har m³ hawa se kitni bhaap boondein banegi? (d) Agar kohra 2 km² ke ilaake mein 30 m oonchi parat mein ho, to boondon mein kul kitna paani aur kitni garmi?

- **Given:** T = 10 °C, RH = 90 percent; raat ka temperature 8 °C; area = 2 km², oonchai = 30 m
- **Find:** dew point, condensed mass per m³, kul mass aur garmi
- **Formula:** bhaap = RH × adhiktam; dew point = table interpolation; condensed = bhaap − adhiktam (8 °C par); Q = m L_v
- **Stage 1 (bhaap):** 0.90 × 9.4 = 8.46 g/m³
- **Stage 2 (dew point):** T_d = 5 + 5 × (8.46 − 6.8) ÷ (9.4 − 6.8) = 5 + 3.19 = 8.19 °C
- **Stage 3 (kohra?):** raat ka 8 °C < 8.19 °C, to kohra banega (bas thoda sa neeche)
- **Stage 4 (8 °C par adhiktam):** 6.8 + (8 − 5) ÷ 5 × 2.6 = 6.8 + 1.56 = 8.36 g/m³; condensed = 8.46 − 8.36 = 0.10 g/m³
- **Stage 5 (kul):** volume = 2 × 10⁶ m² × 30 m = 6 × 10⁷ m³; mass = 0.10 g/m³ × 6 × 10⁷ m³ = 6 × 10⁶ g = 6000 kg
- **Stage 6 (garmi):** Q = 6000 kg × 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg = 1.356 × 10¹⁰ J
- **Answer:** dew point **lagbhag 8.2 °C**; kohra banega; har m³ mein **lagbhag 0.10 g** paani; kul **lagbhag 6000 kg** aur **lagbhag 1.36 × 10¹⁰ J** garmi

**Jaanch:** 8.19 °C hawa ke 10 °C se neeche ✅. 6000 kg = 6 m³ paani, jo 6 × 10⁷ m³ hawa mein faila hai: har 10 m³ mein lagbhag 1 mL, yaani 0.1 g/m³ ✅. Ye bahut chhoti matra hai, phir bhi dikhne ki doori kam kar deti hai (aksar bataya jaata hai ki ghane kohre mein bhi lagbhag 0.1 se 0.5 g/m³ hi hota hai).

T jaise hi dew point ki line se neeche jaata hai (lagbhag 03h aur 06h ke beech), hawa saturated ho jaati hai aur kohra banta hai.

**Mist, haze aur smoke:** mist bhi boondon ka hi hai, bas halki; haze mein sookhe kan (dhool, dhuan) hote hain, boondein nahi. Dono mein fark bataane ke liye nami dekho: haze mein RH kam hota hai, mist aur fog mein lagbhag 100 percent.

## Part C: Baadal: hawa jab oopar uthti hai

Zameen par hawa ko thanda karne ke liye raat chahiye. Par aasmaan mein ek aur tarika hai: hawa jab **oopar uthti hai**, to wahan pressure kam hota hai (Topic 2 mein dekha tha ki pahaad par pressure kam hota hai), isliye wo **failti hai**. Failne mein hawa apni hi garmi se kaam karti hai aur bahar se garmi nahi aati, isliye wo **thandi hoti hai**. Ye thandak kaafi tez hai: sookhi hawa har kilometre par lagbhag 9 se 10 °C thandi hoti hai (hedged: din ke hisaab se thoda badalta hai).

Oopar uthne par dew point bhi thoda gir jaata hai (lagbhag 2 °C har kilometre par, hedged), par bahut kam. Isliye hawa ka temperature dew point ki taraf tezi se girta hai, aur ek oonchai par dono **barabar** ho jaate hain. Wahi hai **cloud base**: wahan se bhaap boondein ban kar **baadal** banati hai.

Yahan hum ek **simple model** lete hain: temperature aur dew point ke girne ke rate **diye hue** hain. Asal mein rate din ke hisaab se badalta hai, isliye hum hamesha **sawal mein diya gaya rate** hi lenge.

**Example 3 (baadal ki oonchai):** sham ko zameen ki hawa 25 °C par hai aur RH 70 percent hai. Maan lo oopar uthte hue hawa ka temperature har km par 9 °C girta hai aur dew point har km par 2 °C girta hai. Baadal ka aadhar kitni oonchai par hoga?

- **Given:** T = 25 °C, RH = 70 percent; temperature ka girna = 9 °C/km; dew point ka girna = 2 °C/km
- **Find:** cloud base ki oonchai, aur wahan ka temperature
- **Formula:** zameen par dew point table se; fark (T − T_d) har km par (9 − 2) °C ghatata hai; oonchai = (T − T_d) ÷ (9 − 2)
- **Stage 1 (bhaap):** 0.70 × 23.0 = 16.1 g/m³
- **Stage 2 (dew point):** 16.1 table ke 12.8 (15 °C) aur 17.3 (20 °C) ke beech: T_d = 15 + 5 × (16.1 − 12.8) ÷ (17.3 − 12.8) = 15 + 3.67 = 18.67 °C
- **Stage 3 (fark):** T − T_d = 25 − 18.67 = 6.33 °C
- **Stage 4 (oonchai):** h = 6.33 ÷ (9 − 2) = 0.904 km
- **Stage 5 (base par temperature):** 25 − 9 × 0.904 = 16.9 °C
- **Answer:** cloud base **lagbhag 0.9 km (lagbhag 900 m)** par, wahan hawa ka temperature **lagbhag 16.9 °C**

**Jaanch:** 0.904 km par dew point = 18.67 − 2 × 0.904 = 16.86 °C aur hawa ka temperature 16.86 °C: dono barabar ✅. Hawa jitni sookhi (T − T_d bada), baadal ka aadhar utna oonchai par. Isliye sookhi dopahar mein baadal oonche banenge aur nami wali hawa mein neeche.

```

0.9 km :             *  <- cloud base (lagbhag 0.9 km)
0.6 km :              D      T
0.3 km :                D            T
0.0 km :                  D                  T
       +-------------------------------------------
            14    16    18    20    22    24
       T = hawa ka temperature, D = dew point (C mein)
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Example 3: oonchai ke saath hawa ka temperature (T) aur dew point (D). Dono lagbhag 0.9 km par milte hain (*): wahi cloud base hai. Usse oopar baadal hai, jisme dew point temperature ke saath chalta hai. Figure lagbhag hai: 1 column = lagbhag 0.33 °C, 1 row = 0.3 km.

**Baadal ke andar thandak kam kyun?** Baadal ke andar bhaap boondon mein badalti hai aur L_v wali garmi chhodti hai (Part A ke Example 1 wali baat). Ye garmi hawa ko garam rakhti hai, isliye baadal ke andar oopar jaane par hawa **kam tezi se** thandi hoti hai (kai baar lagbhag 5 °C har km, hedged). Yaani baadal sirf "nami ka dher" nahi, garmi ka bhi thikana hai.

**Baadal ki boond se baarish ki boond:** baadal ki boondein lagbhag 0.01 mm jaisi chhoti hoti hain (lagbhag, hedged), aur wo girti nahi, tairti hain. Jab lakhon boondein aapas mein milti hain (ya oonchai par barf ke crystal par jamti hain) to unka wazan itna ho jaata hai ki hawa unhe sambhal nahi paati, aur wo **baarish** ban kar girti hain. Ek baarish ki boond mein lagbhag das lakh baadal ki boondein ho sakti hain (hedged). Bahut oonchai par thandi hawa mein barf ke crystal ya **hail** (ole) aur **snow** (barfbari) bhi banti hai: yahan sirf naam yaad rakhna hai.

**Example 4 (baarish ki chhupi garmi):** ek shehar ke 12 km² ilaake mein ek din lagbhag 30 mm baarish hui. (a) Kitna paani gira? (b) Ye paani jab baadal mein bhaap se boondein bana, to kitni garmi chhodi gayi? (L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg)

- **Given:** area = 12 km² = 12 × 10⁶ m²; depth = 30 mm = 0.030 m
- **Find:** mass, Q
- **Formula:** volume = area × depth; mass = volume × 1000 kg/m³; Q = m L_v
- **Stage 1 (volume):** 12 × 10⁶ × 0.030 = 3.6 × 10⁵ m³
- **Stage 2 (mass):** 3.6 × 10⁵ × 1000 = 3.6 × 10⁸ kg
- **Stage 3 (garmi):** Q = 3.6 × 10⁸ × 2.26 × 10⁶ = 8.136 × 10¹⁴ J
- **Answer:** **lagbhag 3.6 × 10⁸ kg** paani, **lagbhag 8.1 × 10¹⁴ J** garmi

**Jaanch:** 1 mm baarish 1 m² par = 1 litre = 1 kg; 30 mm = 30 kg/m²; 30 × 12 × 10⁶ = 3.6 × 10⁸ kg ✅. Ye garmi samundar ke paani ne evaporate hote waqt hawa se li thi (Topic 3 wali baat) aur ab baadal ban kar wahin chhod di: **baarish, samundar ki garmi ko door tak le jaati hai.**

## Part D: Monsoon, pahaad, samundar aur registan

**Samundar ke paas** hawa mein nami zyada hoti hai, kyunki evaporate hone ke liye paani bahut hai, isliye wahan RH aksar zyada rehta hai. **Registan** mein paani door hai, hawa sookhi hai, RH kam hota hai: wahin cooler achha chalta hai (Topic 3, Topic 4). Sookhi hawa aur saaf aasmaan mein raat mein garmi tezi se nikalti hai, isliye registan ki raat thandi hoti hai.

**Monsoon ki hawayein** samundar ke upar se nami lekar aati hain. Jab ye hawa **pahaad** (jaise Western Ghats) se takraati hai, to use pahaad ke saath **oopar chadhna** padta hai: oopar jaate hue wo failti hai, thandi hoti hai, apne dew point tak pahunchti hai aur baadal banta hai. Ye **orographic baarish** (pahaadi baarish) hai. Pahaad ki saamne wali taraf (windward) zyada baarish hoti hai. Pahaad paar karne ke baad hawa utarti hai, dabti hai aur **garam** hoti hai. Uski bhaap kam ho chuki hai aur garam hawa ki adhiktam hadd badh gayi hai, to RH kam ho jaata hai. Isliye peeche ka ilaaka kam baarish wala (rain shadow) hota hai.

## Part E: Ghar mein condensation: sheesha, kaanch, AC aur dehumidifier

Wahi dew point ki baat ab ghar mein dekhte hain.

**Bathroom ka sheesha:** nahate waqt hawa garam aur bahut nami wali hoti hai, sheesha usse kam garam. Sheesha agar us hawa ke dew point se thanda hai, to uspar boondein jam jaati hain.

**Example 5 (bathroom ka sheesha):** nahane ke baad bathroom ki hawa lagbhag 35 °C aur RH 80 percent hai. Sheesha lagbhag 25 °C par hai. (a) Dew point? (b) Kya sheesha dhundhla hoga? (c) Sheesha ke paas ki hawa 25 °C tak thandi hui to har m³ se kitni bhaap boondein banegi? (d) Agar sheesha ke peeche anti-fog pad se wo 32 °C par rakha jaaye, to?

- **Given:** T = 35 °C, RH = 80 percent; sheesha = 25 °C; table: 30 °C par 30.4, 35 °C par 39.6, 25 °C par 23.0 g/m³
- **Find:** dew point, mist ya nahi, condensed mass per m³
- **Formula:** bhaap = RH × adhiktam; dew point = table interpolation; condensed = bhaap − adhiktam (sheeshe ke temperature par)
- **Stage 1 (bhaap):** 0.80 × 39.6 = 31.68 g/m³
- **Stage 2 (dew point):** T_d = 30 + 5 × (31.68 − 30.4) ÷ (39.6 − 30.4) = 30 + 0.70 = 30.7 °C
- **Stage 3 (mist?):** 25 °C < 30.7 °C, to sheesha dhundhla hoga
- **Stage 4 (condensed):** 31.68 − 23.0 = 8.68 g/m³
- **Stage 5 (pad ke saath):** 32 °C > 30.7 °C, to boondein nahi banengi
- **Answer:** dew point **lagbhag 30.7 °C**; sheesha dhundhla; **lagbhag 8.7 g/m³**; 32 °C par **dhundh nahi**

**Jaanch:** 23.0 + 8.68 = 31.68 ✅. Mirror ke 25 °C aur 32 °C ke liye ek hi rule: **satah ka temperature dew point se upar ho to koi condensation nahi.**

**Sardi mein khidki ki andar ki taraf boondein:** kamre ki garam, saans se nami wali hawa thande kaanch ko chhoti hai. Agar kaanch ka andar ka temperature kamre ki hawa ke dew point se neeche hai, to andar boondein bantee hain. Gaadi ke **windscreen** par bhi: saans ki nami se bhari hawa thande kaanch par condense hoti hai. **Demister** kaanch par garam (aur sookhi) hawa bhejta hai: kaanch ka temperature dew point se upar chala jaata hai aur nami kam hoti hai, isliye dhundh ud jaati hai. Garmi ki umas mein ulta bhi hota hai: AC se thanda kaanch bahar ki garam, nami wali hawa ke dew point se neeche ho sakta hai, aur boondein **bahar** ki taraf bantee hain.

**Air conditioner aur dehumidifier:** AC ya dehumidifier ke andar ek bahut thandi coil hoti hai (hedged: aksar lagbhag 5 se 12 °C ke beech). Kamre ki hawa jab coil se guzarti hai to wo apne dew point se neeche thandi hoti hai, aur uski bhaap boondon mein badal kar neeche ki tray ya nali se **tapakti** hai. Isiliye AC ki nali se paani tapakta hai. Hawa thandi ke saath **sookhi** bhi ho jaati hai.

```
kamre ki garam,     +------------------+
nami wali hawa      +   THANDA COIL    +
================>   + (dew point se    +=====>  thandi, kam nami
                    +   neeche)        +        wali hawa
                    +------------------+
                            o   <- boondein tapakti hain
                            o
                    +------------------+
                    +  paani ki tray   +
                    +------------------+
```

> **Figure 5.5** – Dehumidifier ya AC ka block diagram (sirf yehi dikhaya hai ki kya hota hai, andar ke yantra nahi). Garam, nami wali hawa thandi coil se guzarti hai: bhaap boondon mein badal kar tray mein tapakti hai (o), aur thandi, kam nami wali hawa bahar nikalti hai. Compressor wale dehumidifier mein ye hawa ek garam coil se bhi guzarti hai aur kamre ko thodi garam hokar milti hai (figure mein nahi dikhaya).

**Example 6 (dehumidifier ka hisaab):** ek kamra 6 m × 4.5 m × 2.8 m ka hai. Hawa 30 °C par hai aur RH 75 percent se 50 percent karna hai. (a) Kamre se kitna paani nikaalna padega? (b) Condensation ki garmi kitni hai? (c) Agar dehumidifier lagbhag 0.25 litre per ghanta paani nikaalta ho (maan lo), to kitne ghante lagenge? (1 litre = 1 kg; L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg)

- **Given:** volume = 6 × 4.5 × 2.8 = 75.6 m³; T = 30 °C (adhiktam 30.4 g/m³); RH 75 percent se 50 percent; dehumidifier = 0.25 litre per ghanta
- **Find:** hataane wala paani, garmi, time
- **Formula:** bhaap = RH × adhiktam; hataya mass = (bhaap pehle − bhaap baad mein) × volume; Q = m L_v; time = mass ÷ rate
- **Stage 1 (pehle):** 0.75 × 30.4 = 22.8 g/m³
- **Stage 2 (baad mein):** 0.50 × 30.4 = 15.2 g/m³
- **Stage 3 (fark):** 22.8 − 15.2 = 7.6 g/m³
- **Stage 4 (kul mass):** 7.6 × 75.6 = 574.6 g = 0.575 kg
- **Stage 5 (garmi):** Q = 0.5746 kg × 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg = 1.30 × 10⁶ J
- **Stage 6 (time):** 0.575 litre ÷ 0.25 litre per ghanta = 2.3 ghante
- **Answer:** **lagbhag 575 g (0.57 litre)** paani, **lagbhag 1.30 × 10⁶ J** garmi, **lagbhag 2.3 ghante**

**Jaanch:** 22.8 − 15.2 = 7.6 ✅; 7.6 × 75.6 = 574.56 ✅. Kamre ki hawa ka dew point = 20 + 5 × (22.8 − 17.3) ÷ (23.0 − 17.3) = 24.8 °C. Maan lo coil lagbhag 10 °C par hai: coil ko chhoone wali hawa sirf 9.4 g/m³ rakh sakti hai, to us hawa se 22.8 − 9.4 = 13.4 g/m³ condense hota hai. Ye 7.6 g/m³ ke average se zyada hai, kyunki coil se guzri sookhi hawa kamre ki baaki hawa mein mil kar use dheere-dheere sookha karti hai: lagbhag 574.6 ÷ 13.4 = 43 m³ hawa coil se guzar jaaye to bhi kamre ka average 7.6 g/m³ girta hai (sirf andaaza) ✅. Ye condensation ki garmi (1.3 × 10⁶ J) bhi coil ko hataani padti hai: isliye nami wale din AC ko zyada mehnat karni padti hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Dew aasmaan se girti hai."**
Nahi: dew **hawa ki bhaap** se, **satah par** banti hai, girti nahi. Demonstration: ek raat hawa 20 °C par hai aur usme lagbhag 6.8 g/m³ bhaap hai. Table se 5 °C par adhiktam 6.8 g/m³, to dew point 5 °C hai. Khule lawn ki ghaas ki patti radiation se lagbhag 4 °C tak thandi ho gayi: 4 < 5, to dew bani. Usi lawn mein, usi khule aasmaan ke neeche, rakhi patthar ki bench din ki garmi dheere chhodti hai aur lagbhag 11 °C par rahi: 11 > 5, to bench par dew nahi bani. Agar dew aasmaan se girti, to wo har khuli satah par ek jaisi girti. Par dew sirf us satah par bani jiska temperature dew point se neeche gaya. Dew patton ke **neeche** ki satah par bhi banti hai, jahan upar se girne ka sawal hi nahi. Jo satah thandi hai, wahin boondein banti hain.

**Galti 2: "Kohra aur baadal bilkul alag cheezein hain."**
Nahi: dono **hawa mein tairti paani ki chhoti boondein** hain. Fark sirf **kahan aur kaise thandak** milti hai: kohra mein zameen ke paas ki hawa raat bhar thandi hoti hai; baadal mein hawa oopar uthkar failkar thandi hoti hai. Demonstration: ek ghaat ki sadak 800 m se 1500 m tak chadhti hai, aur us din baadal ka aadhar lagbhag 1.2 km par hai. Neeche se dekho to baadal door aasmaan mein hai. Ghaat par 1.2 km ke oopar chadhte hi gaadi **usi baadal ke andar** hai: aur wahan sadak par dikhne ki doori kohre jaise kam hai. Yaani **baadal jab zameen ko chhoo le to wahi kohra** hai, aur kohra jab oopar uthe to baadal. Fark naam ka hai, physics ek hi hai: hawa apne dew point se neeche gayi.

## Quick Recap

- Jab bhi hawa **dew point se neeche** thandi hoti hai, extra bhaap boondon (ya barf ke crystal) mein badal jaati hai.
- **Dew:** thandi satah par. **Saaf, shaant raat** mein zyada (garmi seedhi oopar jaati hai); baadal ya tez hawa mein kam. Dew aasmaan se nahi girti. **Frost:** satah 0 °C se neeche, bhaap seedhe barf.
- **Fog (kohra):** zameen ke paas ki hawa ki parat dew point tak thandi. Boondein bahut chhoti, dikhne ki doori lagbhag 1 km se kam. Condensation kan (dhool, dhuan) chahiye. **Baadal zameen ko chhoo le to wahi fog.**
- **Baadal:** oopar uthti hawa failti hai aur thandi hoti hai (diya hua rate se). **Cloud base** wahan jahan T = dew point: h = (T − T_d) ÷ (rate fark). Condensation ki garmi baadal ke andar thandak ko kam karti hai.
- **Baarish:** lakhon baadal ki boondein milkar bhaari boond; baarish ke saath samundar ki L_v garmi atmosphere mein chhodi jaati hai (Example 4).
- **Pahaadi baarish:** nami wali hawa pahaad se chadhte hue thandi hoti hai, saamne baarish, peeche kam.
- **AC aur dehumidifier:** thandi coil, hawa dew point se neeche, bhaap paani ban kar tapakti hai. Comfortable RH lagbhag 40 se 60 percent; 70 percent ke upar mould.

    (c) **Stage 1:** bacha = 15.3 − 3.0 = 12.3 g. **Stage 2:** 12.3 ÷ 0.9 = 13.67 g/m³. **Stage 3:** RH = 13.67 ÷ 23.0 × 100 = 59.4 percent.
    **Answer:** **lagbhag 59 percent**: comfortable range (40 se 60 percent) mein.
    **Jaanch:** 15.3 − 3.0 = 12.3 ✅; RH badhne ki jagah ghata ✅.

12. **Given:** T = 32 °C (table andaaza 30.4 + 0.4 × 9.2 = 34.08 g/m³).
    **Day 1:** T_d = 21 °C par adhiktam = 17.3 + (21 − 20) ÷ 5 × 5.7 = 18.44 g/m³; RH = 18.44 ÷ 34.08 × 100 = 54.1 percent. **Day 2:** T_d = 8 °C par adhiktam = 6.8 + (8 − 5) ÷ 5 × 2.6 = 8.36 g/m³; RH = 8.36 ÷ 34.08 × 100 = 24.5 percent.
    **Answer:** Day 1 **lagbhag 54 percent**, Day 2 **lagbhag 25 percent**. Day 1 zyada umas wali, Day 2 mein kapde jaldi sukhenge.
    **Jaanch:** dono dew point (21 °C aur 8 °C) hawa ke 32 °C se neeche ✅; dew point jitna kam, RH utna kam ✅.

13. (a) **False** ❌: dew hawa ki bhaap se, thandi satah par banti hai. (b) **True** ✅: zameen ko chhoone wala baadal hi fog hai. (c) **False** ❌: baadal aur hawa ghaas ko kam thanda hone dete hain, to dew **kam** banti hai; saaf, shaant raat mein zyada. (d) **True** ✅: coil dew point se thandi hai.
