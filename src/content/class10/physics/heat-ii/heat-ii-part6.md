## Part A: Kaun sa vichaar kab?

Koi bhi sawal aaye to pehle teen sawal poochho: **(1) Kya state badal rahi hai? (2) Garmi kis tarah ki hai: m c ΔT (temperature badla) ya m L (state badli)? (3) Garmi kahan se aa rahi hai aur kahan ja rahi hai (jama aur kharch barabar)?** Phir neeche ki table se auzaar chuno.

| Sawal kaisa dikhta hai? | Pehle kya poochho? | Kaun sa auzaar | Kahan seekha |
|---|---|---|---|
| Barf ya bhaap ke saath garam aur thandi cheezein mil rahi hain | Kya poori state badlegi? | Milne wali garmi aur chahiye wali garmi ki tulna (anth-avastha ki jaanch), phir hi final temperature | Topic 1 |
| Paani ko ubalna hai, jagah ya cooker ka zikr hai | Bahar ka pressure kitna hai? | Pressure zyada to boiling point zyada; kam to kam | Topic 2 |
| Kuch sookh raha hai ya thanda ho raha hai (matka, pasina, cooler) | Kitna paani evaporate hua? | Q = m L_v, aur agar paani thanda hua to Q = m c ΔT | Topic 3 |
| Hawa kitni "bhari" hai? | Hawa ka temperature aur abhi ki bhaap kitni? | RH = abhi ki bhaap ÷ table ki adhiktam × 100 percent | Topic 3 (table), Topic 4 |
| Boondein kab banengi, dew ya mist | Kis temperature par bhaap saturated hogi? | Dew point: table mein wo temperature jahan adhiktam = abhi ki bhaap | Topic 4, 5 |
| Garmi di, par temperature nahi badla | State badal rahi hai? | Q = m L; temperature badle to Q = m c ΔT | Class 9, Topic 1 |

Dhyan do: table ka koi bhi auzaar akela nahi chalta. "Sweat ka cooling" Q = m L_v se tolta hai, par kitna sweat sach mein evaporate hoga, ye hawa ka RH batata hai (Topic 4).

## Part B: Paani ke chakkar ka garmi ka khaata

Paani ki har badlav ki ek garmi hai, aur ek jagah se doosri jagah jaati hai. Ye master table ek nazar mein sab dikhati hai (L_f aur L_v waise hi jaise Class 9 mein).

| Prakriya | Kya badla | Garmi (per kg) | Garmi kahan se kahan | Roz ki misaal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Melting | Barf se paani | L_f = 3.36 × 10⁵ J/kg | Aas-paas se barf ke andar; temperature 0 °C par ruka | Cooler box ki barf |
| Freezing | Paani se barf | L_f = 3.36 × 10⁵ J/kg | Jamte paani se aas-paas ko | Freezer mein barf banna |
| Boiling | Ubalta paani se bhaap | L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg | Chulhe se bhaap ke andar; temperature ruka | Patile ka ubaal |
| Evaporation | Paani se bhaap (kisi bhi temperature par) | Hum bhi L_v lete hain (lagbhag) | Bache paani aur aas-paas se bhaap ke andar; paani thanda | Matka, pasina |
| Condensation | Bhaap se paani | L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg | Bhaap se aas-paas ko; aas-paas garm | Thande glass ki boondein, baadal |
| Sublimation | Solid se seedha gas | Alag; value hum nahi lete | Aas-paas se solid ke andar | Kapoor, dry ice |

Pattern dekho: **jahan bhaap ban rahi hai wahan garmi *li* jaati hai, jahan bhaap boondein ban rahi hai wahan wahi garmi *di* jaati hai.** Isiliye baarish wale baadal ke andar condensation se bahut garmi nikalti hai, wahi jo samundar se evaporation mein li gayi thi.

```
   BADAL (oopar, thandi hawa mein)
   bhaap -> boondein: CONDENSATION
   garmi hawa ko DETI hai: m L_v
      ↑  nam hawa oopar uthti               ↓  boondein: baarish
      ↑  (thandi hoti jaati)                ↓  nadi, talaab, zameen
   SAMUNDAR / TALAAB / GEELI ZAMEEN      (baarish ka paani wapas yahin)
   paani -> bhaap: EVAPORATION
   garmi hawa aur paani se LETI hai: m L_v
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Water cycle ka garmi ka khaata. Neeche evaporation mein garmi li jaati hai (m L_v), oopar condensation mein wahi garmi wapas di jaati hai. Ek taraf bhaap oopar jaati hai, doosri taraf baarish neeche aati hai.

## Part C: Baarish ka hisaab: rain gauge se garmi tak

Rain gauge ke seedhe deewar wali jar mein gehrai ka matlab ye hai ki **bartan chaude ya patle hone se fark nahi padta**: girne wale paani ki gehrai wahi hoti hai. Isliye kisi bhi jagah ke liye "mm" ek hi jaisi sankhya hai.

```

      │~~~~~~~~~~│  <- paani ki satah: 18 mm
16 mm │≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈│
      │≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈│
12 mm │≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈│
      │≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈│
 8 mm │≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈│
      │≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈│
 4 mm │≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈│
      │≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈≈│

      └──────────┘
```

> **Figure 6.2** – Rain gauge ka khaka. 1 row = 2 mm gehrai. Paani ki satah 18 mm par hai. Seedhi deewarein hone se ye gehrai bartan ki chaudai par nirbhar nahi karti.

**Example 1 (18 mm baarish ki garmi):** Pichhli raat chhat ke us hisse par, jiska kshetrafal 40 m² hai, rain gauge ne 18 mm baarish bataayi. (a) Is hisse par kitna paani gira? (b) Maan lo ye saara paani baadal ki bhaap se bana tha; condensation mein kitni garmi nikli? (c) Utni garmi se kitni barf (0 °C par) pighal sakti hai?

**Given:** gehrai = 18 mm = 0.018 m; kshetrafal = 40 m²; L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg; L_f = 3.36 × 10⁵ J/kg; 1 m³ paani = 1000 kg.
**Find:** paani ka mass, garmi Q, barf ka mass.
**Formula:** mass = gehrai × kshetrafal × 1000 kg/m³; Q = m L_v; m_barf = Q ÷ L_f.
**Substitution:**
Stage 1: Ayatan = 0.018 × 40 = 0.72 m³; mass = 0.72 × 1000 = 720 kg.
Stage 2: Q = 720 × 2.26 × 10⁶ = 1.627 × 10⁹ J (lagbhag).
Stage 3: m_barf = 1.627 × 10⁹ ÷ 3.36 × 10⁵ = 4843 kg (lagbhag 4.8 tonne).
**Answer:** **720 kg** paani, **lagbhag 1.63 × 10⁹ J** garmi, jo **lagbhag 4.8 tonne** barf pighla sakti hai.
**Jaanch:** 18 mm = har m² par 18 kg, aur 18 × 40 = 720 kg ✅; 720 × 2.26 × 10⁶ = 1.627 × 10⁹ J ✅; 4843 × 3.36 × 10⁵ = 1.627 × 10⁹ J ✅. Ye garmi baadal ki hawa ko garm karti hai, aur isi se toofani baadal oopar uthte rehte hain (lagbhag; ye asli baarish mein sirf ek hissa hai).

## Part D: Pasina: sookhi hawa mein aur umas mein

Topic 3 ne bataya ki sweat ka cooling m L_v se aata hai. Topic 4 ne bataya ki umas mein hawa ke paas "khaali jagah" kam hoti hai. Ab dono ko jodte hain.

**Example 2 (khilaadi ka pasina):** Ek khilaadi ko 20 minute (1200 s) tak lagbhag 240 W ki garmi pasine ke evaporation se nikaalni hai. 40 °C par do din: **sookha din** (RH 20 percent) aur **umas wala din** (RH 90 percent). Maan lo (sirf udaharan ke liye) ki sookhe din 90 percent pasina evaporate hota hai aur umas wale din sirf 40 percent (baaki boondein bankar behta hai). Table se hawa ki khaali jagah nikaalo, phir har din kitna pasina banana padega?

**Given:** P = 240 W, t = 1200 s; 40 °C par adhiktam = 51.1 g/m³; RH 20 aur 90 percent; evaporate hone ka hissa 0.90 aur 0.40; L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg.
**Find:** khaali jagah (g/m³), evaporate hone wala pasina, pasina jo banana padega.
**Formula:** khaali jagah = (1 − RH) × adhiktam; Q = P × t; m_evap = Q ÷ L_v; m_pasina = m_evap ÷ hissa.
**Substitution:**
Stage 1 (khaali jagah): sookha din = 0.80 × 51.1 = 40.9 g/m³; umas wala din = 0.10 × 51.1 = 5.1 g/m³ (lagbhag).
Stage 2: Q = 240 × 1200 = 288000 J.
Stage 3: m_evap = 288000 ÷ 2.26 × 10⁶ = 0.1274 kg = 127.4 g.
Stage 4: sookhe din: 127.4 ÷ 0.90 = 141.6 g; umas wale din: 127.4 ÷ 0.40 = 318.6 g.
**Answer:** khaali jagah **lagbhag 40.9** aur **5.1 g/m³** (8 guna ka fark); pasina **lagbhag 142 g** aur **lagbhag 319 g**.
**Jaanch:** 0.90 × 141.6 = 127.4 g ✅ aur 0.40 × 318.6 = 127.4 g ✅; 0.1274 × 2.26 × 10⁶ = 287900 J ≈ 288000 J ✅; 40.9 ÷ 5.1 = 8.0 ✅. Umas wale din 319 g, 20 minute mein, yaani lagbhag 0.96 kg per ghanta: ye mehnat ke waqt ki lagbhag upari hadd ke paas hai (0.5 se 1 litre per ghanta), isiliye umas mein khel itna thaka deta hai. Dono din energy wahi (288000 J) nikalni thi; fark sirf itna hai ki sookhi hawa ne pasina le liya, umas wali ne nahi.

```
Extra bhaap jo hawa le sakti hai (g/m³), 1 star = 2 g/m³
Sookhi hawa, RH 20 percent    ********************     40.9
Umas wali hawa, RH 90 percent ***                      5.1
```

> **Figure 6.3** – 40 °C par sookhi aur umas wali hawa ki "khaali jagah" (Example 2). Bar ki lambai lagbhag hai (1 star = 2 g/m³, 5.1 ko 3 star dikhaya).

## Part E: Kamre ka dehumidifier

**Example 3 (band kamra, dehumidifier):** Weather station ke saath ka band kamra 6 m × 3.5 m × 2.5 m ka hai. Hawa 25 °C par hai, RH 78 percent. Ek dehumidifier 3 ghante mein (10800 s) RH ko 52 percent tak laata hai, temperature 25 °C hi rakhte hue. Kitna paani nikla, condensation mein kitni garmi nikli, aur uski average power kitni hui? Table: 25 °C par 23.0 g/m³.

**Given:** ayatan = 6 × 3.5 × 2.5 m³; 25 °C par adhiktam = 23.0 g/m³; RH 78 → 52 percent; t = 10800 s; L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg.
**Find:** nikla hua paani, garmi, average power.
**Formula:** bhaap = RH × adhiktam; Q = m L_v; P = Q ÷ t.
**Substitution:**
Stage 1: ayatan = 6 × 3.5 × 2.5 = 52.5 m³.
Stage 2: shuru ki bhaap = 0.78 × 23.0 = 17.94 g/m³; ant ki bhaap = 0.52 × 23.0 = 11.96 g/m³.
Stage 3: kami = 17.94 − 11.96 = 5.98 g/m³; mass = 5.98 × 52.5 = 313.95 g = 0.314 kg.
Stage 4: Q = 0.31395 × 2.26 × 10⁶ = 7.10 × 10⁵ J (lagbhag).
Stage 5: P = 7.10 × 10⁵ ÷ 10800 = 65.7 W (lagbhag).
**Answer:** **lagbhag 314 g** paani, **lagbhag 7.1 × 10⁵ J**, average **lagbhag 66 W**.
**Jaanch:** 5.98 × 52.5 = 313.95 ✅; 0.31395 × 2.26 × 10⁶ = 709527 J ✅; 65.7 × 10800 = 7.10 × 10⁵ J ✅. Ye garmi machine ke thande coil se hokar bahar wali hawa mein chali jaati hai, aur machine ka compressor bhi garmi jodta hai, isiliye dehumidifier chalne par kamra thoda garm hi hota hai.

## Part F: Do aur mixed sawal

**Example 4 (ice-bath: kya barf bachegi?):** Fenil ki team thermometer ko 0 °C par jaanchne ke liye ice-bath banati hai: 0.7 kg paani 26 °C par aur 0.32 kg barf −6 °C par (c(barf) = 2100 J/kg °C). Garmi ka nuksaan nahi maano. Kya saari barf pighlegi? End state?

**Given:** m_paani = 0.7 kg, 26 °C; m_barf = 0.32 kg, −6 °C; c(paani) = 4200 J/kg °C; c(barf) = 2100 J/kg °C; L_f = 3.36 × 10⁵ J/kg.
**Find:** end state, final temperature, bachi barf.
**Formula:** Milne wali garmi = m c ΔT (paani 0 °C tak); chahiye wali = barf ko 0 °C tak laane ki + poori pighlane ki.
**Substitution:**
Stage 1 (paani 0 °C tak de sakta hai): 0.7 × 4200 × 26 = 76440 J.
Stage 2 (barf ko 0 °C tak): 0.32 × 2100 × 6 = 4032 J.
Stage 3 (poori barf pighlane ko): 0.32 × 3.36 × 10⁵ = 107520 J. Kul chahiye = 4032 + 107520 = 111552 J.
Stage 4 (tulna): 76440 J < 111552 J, to poori barf **nahi** pighlegi, temperature **0 °C**.
Stage 5: pighli = (76440 − 4032) ÷ 3.36 × 10⁵ = 0.2155 kg; bachi barf = 0.32 − 0.2155 = 0.1045 kg; paani = 0.7 + 0.2155 = 0.9155 kg.
**Answer:** **0 °C**, bachi barf **lagbhag 0.105 kg**, paani **lagbhag 0.916 kg**. Isiliye ice-bath thermometer ke 0 °C ke liye achha hai: barf bachi hai to temperature 0 °C par tikta hai.
**Jaanch:** paani ne di 76440 J; barf ne li 4032 + 0.2155 × 3.36 × 10⁵ = 4032 + 72408 = 76440 J ✅; temperature 0 °C aur 26 °C ke beech nahi, 0 °C par ruka (jaisa Topic 1 ka niyam) ✅.

**Example 5 (aangan ki raat):** Ek aangan ki 90 m³ hawa dopahar mein 40 °C par hai aur uska dew point 25 °C hai. Raat ko hawa 20 °C tak thandi hoti hai (maan lo ki hawa ka ayatan 90 m³ hi rahe). Kya os banegi? Kitna paani aur kitni garmi?

**Given:** 40 °C par adhiktam 51.1 g/m³; 25 °C par 23.0 g/m³; 20 °C par 17.3 g/m³; 90 m³; L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg.
**Find:** hawa ka RH, kya os banegi, kitna paani condense hoga, garmi.
**Formula:** dew point par adhiktam = abhi ki bhaap; RH = abhi ki bhaap ÷ adhiktam × 100; Q = m L_v.
**Substitution:**
Stage 1: dew point 25 °C hai, to abhi ki bhaap = 23.0 g/m³.
Stage 2: dopahar ka RH = 23.0 ÷ 51.1 × 100 = 45.0 percent.
Stage 3: raat ko 20 °C par adhiktam 17.3 g/m³ hai, aur dew point 25 °C hai jo 20 °C se zyada hai, to hawa 25 °C par hi saturated ho jaayegi: **os banegi**.
Stage 4: condense hone wali bhaap = 23.0 − 17.3 = 5.7 g/m³; 90 m³ mein 5.7 × 90 = 513 g = 0.513 kg.
Stage 5: Q = 0.513 × 2.26 × 10⁶ = 1.16 × 10⁶ J (lagbhag).
**Answer:** dopahar ka RH **lagbhag 45 percent**; os **banegi**; **lagbhag 513 g** paani; **lagbhag 1.16 × 10⁶ J** garmi (jo raat ke thanda hone ki raftaar ko dheema karti hai).
**Jaanch:** 5.7 × 90 = 513 ✅; 0.513 × 2.26 × 10⁶ = 1159380 J ✅; dew point (25 °C) < hawa ka temperature (40 °C) ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Latent heat dene se temperature badhta hai."**
Nahi: latent heat state badalne mein jaati hai, temperature nahi badhta. Demonstration: 0.16 kg barf 0 °C par hai, aur use 33600 J garmi di gayi. Agar koi Q = m c ΔT laga de to ΔT = 33600 ÷ (0.16 × 2100) = **100 °C**: barf 100 °C par! Ye bekaar hai. Sahi: pighli = 33600 ÷ 3.36 × 10⁵ = **0.1 kg**, bachi barf **0.06 kg**, temperature **0 °C**. Pehle poochho: state badal rahi hai?

**Galti 2: "Specific heat aur latent heat ek hi cheez hain."**
Nahi: specific heat c ka matlab 1 kg ka temperature 1 °C badhane ki garmi (J/kg °C); latent heat L ka matlab 1 kg ki state badalne ki garmi (J/kg), jisme temperature nahi badalta. Demonstration: 0.3 kg paani ke liye 1 °C badhane mein 0.3 × 4200 = **1260 J** lagte hain, par use 100 °C par bhaap banane mein 0.3 × 2.26 × 10⁶ = **678000 J** lagte hain. Ye 678000 ÷ 1260 = **538 guna** zyada hai: yaani wahi garmi paani ko (agar wo liquid rehta) 538 °C badha deti. Dono ki ikai aur kaam alag hain.

**Galti 3: "Evaporation se thandak ke liye thandi satah chahiye."**
Nahi: thandak evaporation se aati hai, satah ke pehle se thandi hone se nahi. Demonstration: 38 °C ki garm hawa mein ek geele kapde se dhaki 2.1 kg paani ki bottle se 11 g paani evaporate hota hai. Garmi = 0.011 × 2.26 × 10⁶ = **24860 J**. Agar ye saari garmi bottle ke paani se aaye to wo 24860 ÷ (2.1 × 4200) = **2.8 °C** thanda ho (upari hadd: asal mein hawa se bhi garmi aati hai, isliye kam). Bottle garm hawa mein rakhi thi, phir bhi sookhi bottle se thandi rahi.

**Galti 4: "Dew point wo temperature hai jis par os girti hai."**
Nahi: dew point **hawa ki** khaasiyat hai (kis temperature par wo saturated hogi), os ka temperature nahi. Demonstration: 30 °C ki hawa mein bhaap 12.8 g/m³ hai, to table se dew point **15 °C**. Agar ghaas 11 °C tak thandi ho jaaye, to usse lagi hawa 15 °C se neeche thandi hoti hai (11 °C par adhiktam 12.8 se kam hai), aur os ghaas ki 11 °C wali satah par banti hai, 15 °C par nahi. Agar ghaas sirf 17 °C tak thandi ho (dew point se upar), to os bilkul nahi banti, par dew point phir bhi **15 °C** hi rehta hai.

## Quick Recap

- Mixed sawal mein pehle teen sawal: state badal rahi hai? m c ΔT ya m L? jama aur kharch barabar?
- Mixture mein barf ya bhaap ho to pehle anth-avastha ki jaanch (Topic 1), phir hi final temperature.
- Boiling point bahari pressure ke saath badhta aur ghatta hai: cooker (lagbhag 120 °C), pahaad (lagbhag 90 °C).
- Evaporation ka garmi ka hisaab Q = m L_v; condensation mein wahi garmi wapas deti hai. Water cycle isi ka khaata hai (Figure 6.1).
- RH table se: abhi ki bhaap ÷ adhiktam × 100 percent. Dew point table se: wo temperature jahan adhiktam = abhi ki bhaap.
- Umas mein hawa ke paas khaali jagah kam hoti hai, isliye pasina kam evaporate hota hai (Example 2).
- Rain gauge: 1 mm gehrai = har m² par lagbhag 1 kg paani (Example 1).
- Dehumidifier ka paani = (RH ka antar × adhiktam) × ayatan (Example 3).
- Latent heat temperature nahi badhati; latent heat aur specific heat alag hain; thandak evaporation se aati hai, thandi satah se nahi; dew point hawa ki khaasiyat hai.

12. A desert cooler works well on a dry 40 °C afternoon but badly on a humid 40 °C afternoon. Explain in three or four lines using the idea of evaporation and RH, why, and say whether it is the cooler's L_v or the amount evaporating that changes.

### Answers

1. **Given:** gehrai 46 mm = 0.046 m, kshetrafal 72 m². **Formula:** mass = gehrai × kshetrafal × 1000; Q = m L_v. **Substitution:** Stage 1: ayatan = 0.046 × 72 = 3.312 m³; mass = **3312 kg**. Stage 2: Q = 3312 × 2.26 × 10⁶ = 7.485 × 10⁹ J. **Answer:** **3312 kg** paani aur **lagbhag 7.49 × 10⁹ J**. **Jaanch:** 46 mm = har m² par 46 kg, 46 × 72 = 3312 ✅; 3312 × 2.26 × 10⁶ = 7.485 × 10⁹ ✅.

2. **Given:** P = 300 W, t = 50 × 60 = 3000 s, hissa 0.65. **Formula:** Q = P t; m = Q ÷ L_v; m_pasina = m ÷ 0.65. **Substitution:** (a) Q = 300 × 3000 = 900000 J; m = 900000 ÷ 2.26 × 10⁶ = **0.398 kg** (398 g). (b) m_pasina = 0.398 ÷ 0.65 = 0.613 kg, 50 minute mein; ek ghante mein = 0.613 × 60 ÷ 50 = **0.735 kg per ghanta**. **Jaanch:** 0.65 × 0.613 = 0.398 ✅; 0.398 × 2.26 × 10⁶ = 899500 J ≈ 900000 ✅. 0.735 kg per ghanta mehnat ke 0.5 se 1 litre per ghanta ki hadd ke andar hai.

3. **Given:** 15 °C, 8.4 g/m³; 15 °C par adhiktam 12.8. **Formula:** RH = abhi ÷ adhiktam × 100; dew point table se interpolate. **Substitution:** RH = 8.4 ÷ 12.8 × 100 = 65.6. Dew point: 8.4 table ke 6.8 (5 °C) aur 9.4 (10 °C) ke beech: T_d = 5 + 5 × (8.4 − 6.8) ÷ (9.4 − 6.8) = 5 + 3.08 = 8.1. **Answer:** RH **lagbhag 66 percent**, dew point **lagbhag 8 °C**. **Jaanch:** 8 °C < 15 °C ✅; 0.656 × 12.8 = 8.4 ✅.

4. **Given:** 10 °C par adhiktam 9.4; dew point 5 °C par adhiktam 6.8. **Formula:** RH = adhiktam at dew point ÷ adhiktam at T × 100. **Substitution:** RH = 6.8 ÷ 9.4 × 100 = 72.3; abhi ki bhaap = 6.8 g/m³; aur le sakti hai = 9.4 − 6.8 = 2.6 g/m³. **Answer:** RH **lagbhag 72 percent**; **2.6 g/m³** aur. **Jaanch:** 0.723 × 9.4 = 6.8 ✅.

5. **Given:** m_barf = 0.135 kg, −4 °C; m_paani = 0.95 kg, 33 °C. **Formula:** pehle anth-avastha ki jaanch, phir mixture ka santulan. **Substitution:** Stage 1: paani 0 °C tak de sakta hai = 0.95 × 4200 × 33 = 131670 J. Stage 2: barf ko 0 °C tak = 0.135 × 2100 × 4 = 1134 J. Stage 3: poori barf pighlane ko = 0.135 × 3.36 × 10⁵ = 45360 J; kul chahiye 46494 J. Stage 4: 131670 > 46494, to saari barf pighlegi aur paani abhi bhi 0 °C se garm hai. Stage 5: 0.95 × 4200 × (33 − T) = 46494 + 0.135 × 4200 × T, yaani 131670 − 3990 T = 46494 + 567 T, T = 85176 ÷ 4557 = 18.69. **Answer:** saari barf pighlegi, final temperature **lagbhag 18.7 °C**. **Jaanch:** paani ne di 3990 × 14.31 = 57092 J; li 46494 + 567 × 18.69 = 57092 J ✅; 0 aur 33 ke beech ✅.

6. **Given:** m = 0.52 kg, 27 °C; boiling point 94 °C (camp), 100 °C (coast); 0.048 kg bhaap. **Formula:** Q = m c ΔT; Q = m L_v. **Substitution:** (a) ΔT = 94 − 27 = 67 °C; Q = 0.52 × 4200 × 67 = **146328 J**. (b) coast: ΔT = 73 °C; Q = 0.52 × 4200 × 73 = 159432 J; extra = 159432 − 146328 = **13104 J**. (c) bhaap: 0.048 × 2.26 × 10⁶ = 108480 J; kul = 146328 + 108480 = **254808 J** (lagbhag 2.55 × 10⁵ J). **Jaanch:** 0.52 × 4200 × 6 = 13104 ✅; 146328 + 108480 = 254808 ✅. Camp par warming ke liye kam garmi lagi, par paani sirf 94 °C tak hi garam hota hai, isliye dal der se galti hai (Topic 2).

7. **Given:** 64 m³, 15 °C (adhiktam 12.8), RH 88 → 55 percent, t = 2.5 × 3600 = 9000 s. **Formula:** bhaap = RH × adhiktam; Q = m L_v; P = Q ÷ t. **Substitution:** Stage 1: 0.88 × 12.8 = 11.26 g/m³; 0.55 × 12.8 = 7.04 g/m³. Stage 2: kami = 4.22 g/m³ (4.224); mass = 4.224 × 64 = 270.3 g = 0.2703 kg. Stage 3: Q = 0.2703 × 2.26 × 10⁶ = 6.11 × 10⁵ J. Stage 4: P = 6.11 × 10⁵ ÷ 9000 = 67.9 W. **Answer:** **lagbhag 270 g**, **lagbhag 6.1 × 10⁵ J**, **lagbhag 68 W**. **Jaanch:** 4.224 × 64 = 270.3 ✅; 67.9 × 9000 = 6.11 × 10⁵ ✅.

8. **Given:** 150 m³; 20 °C par 13.6 g/m³ (adhiktam 17.3); 10 °C par adhiktam 9.4. **Substitution:** (a) RH = 13.6 ÷ 17.3 × 100 = 78.6; dew point: 13.6 table ke 12.8 (15 °C) aur 17.3 (20 °C) ke beech: T_d = 15 + 5 × (13.6 − 12.8) ÷ (17.3 − 12.8) = 15 + 0.89 = 15.9. (b) Dew point 15.9 °C > 10 °C, to **os banegi**. Condense = 13.6 − 9.4 = 4.2 g/m³; 4.2 × 150 = 630 g = 0.63 kg. Q = 0.63 × 2.26 × 10⁶ = 1.42 × 10⁶ J. **Answer:** RH **lagbhag 79 percent**, dew point **lagbhag 16 °C**; **os banegi**, **lagbhag 630 g** paani aur **lagbhag 1.42 × 10⁶ J** garmi. **Jaanch:** 4.2 × 150 = 630 ✅; 0.63 × 2.26 × 10⁶ = 1423800 J ✅.

(c) **Evaporation ka m L_v hisaab** (Topic 3). (d) **RH = abhi ki bhaap ÷ usi temperature ki adhiktam × 100** (Topic 4). (e) **Dew point** table se (Topic 4). **Jaanch:** har jawab ko teen sawaalon (state? kaun si garmi? jama-kharch?) se mila kar dekho ✅.

10. **Given:** 0.8 kg, 100 °C. **Substitution:** (a) 0.8 × 4200 × 1 = **3360 J**. (b) 0.8 × 2.26 × 10⁶ = **1808000 J**. (c) 1808000 ÷ 3360 = **538 guna** (lagbhag). **Answer:** state badalne ki garmi 1 °C badhane ki garmi se **538 guna** zyada: yaani latent heat aur specific heat do alag cheezein hain, aur ubalne mein garmi ka bada hissa jaata hai. **Jaanch:** 3360 × 538 = 1807680 ≈ 1808000 ✅; ye ratio L_v ÷ c = 2.26 × 10⁶ ÷ 4200 = 538 ✅.
