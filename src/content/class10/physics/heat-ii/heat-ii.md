## Class 9 ka recall

Class 9 mein teen baatein seekhi thi ([part3](../../../class9/physics/heat-and-temperature/heat-and-temperature-part3.md), [part5](../../../class9/physics/heat-and-temperature/heat-and-temperature-part5.md)):

- Temperature badalte waqt: **Q = m c ΔT**. Yahan c(paani) = 4200, c(barf) = 2100 aur c(bhaap) = 2000 J/kg °C.
- State badalte waqt (temperature fixed): **Q = m L**. Yahan L_f(barf) = 3.36 × 10⁵ J/kg aur L_v(paani) = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg.
- Heating curve (Figure 5.3): do flat hisse (0 °C aur 100 °C par) aur teen dhalwan hisse.

Aur [part4](../../../class9/physics/heat-and-temperature/heat-and-temperature-part4.md) ka principle of mixtures: garam cheez ki di hui garmi = thandi cheez ki li hui garmi. Class 9 ke Example 7 mein ye line thi: "Barf poori pighal jaati hai aur paani 0 °C par hi ruk jaata hai." **Wo ek maan-na tha.** Aaj usi maan-ne ko jaanchna sikhenge: kab wo sach hai aur kab nahi.

## Part A: Mixture ka formula yahan kyun atakta hai?

Class 9 mein jab do paani mixte the, to final temperature **(m₁T₁ + m₂T₂) ÷ (m₁ + m₂)** se nikalte the. Ye formula tabhi chalta hai jab (1) dono ka c same ho (dono paani), aur (2) **kisi ki state na badle**. Ab barf + garam paani lo. Do galat intuitions dikhti hain:

- "Barf 0 °C par aur paani 78 °C par, to beech ka kuch temperature banega." Hamesha nahi. Agar paani ki garmi barf ko poori pighlane layak na ho, to mixture **0 °C par hi atka rahega**, aur barf bachi rahegi.
- "Barf ke paas hone se paani 0 °C se bhi neeche chala jaayega." Nahi. Jab tak barf aur paani saath hain, temperature **0 °C** hi rehta hai (Figure 5.3 ke flat hisse jaisa).

Isliye **pehle** ye dekhna padta hai ki kaun kitni garmi de sakta hai aur kitni chahiye. Ye hai **anth-avastha ki jaanch**:

1. **Milne wali garmi** nikalo: garam cheez ko state-badalne wale temperature tak (barf wale case mein 0 °C) thanda karne par jitni garmi nikalti hai.
2. **Chahiye wali garmi** nikalo: thandi cheez ko wahan tak laane aur poori state badalne mein lagne wali garmi (barf ko 0 °C tak garm karna, agar wo 0 °C se thandi ho, aur phir Q = m L_f).
3. **Dono ko milao.** Teen cases:
   - milne wali **>** chahiye wali: poori state badal gayi. Bachi hui garmi ab **poore mixture** ko garm karti hai: yahan mixture formula lagao.
   - milne wali **=** chahiye wali: sirf poori state badli, temperature **ruka hua** (0 °C ya 100 °C).
   - milne wali **<** chahiye wali: state **adhoori** badli. Temperature **0 °C ya 100 °C par hi** rehta hai. Kitni state badli: Q ÷ L se.

```
   Garam cheez aur thandi cheez (jiski state badal sakti hai) mile
                                  │
                                  ▼
    Step 1. Milne wali garmi = garam cheez ko 0 °C (ya 100 °C) tak
            thanda karne par nikalne wali garmi
    Step 2. Chahiye wali garmi = thandi cheez ko wahan tak laane aur
            poori state badalne mein lagne wali garmi
                                  │
                                  ▼
           Milne wali garmi ki chahiye wali garmi se tulna
          ┌────────────────────────┬────────────────────────┐
          │                        │                        │
          ▼                        ▼                        ▼
 milne > chahiye          milne = chahiye         milne < chahiye
 poori state badli        sirf poori badli        state adhoori badli
 bachi garmi mixture      T ruka hua:             T = 0 °C (ya 100 °C)
 ko garm karti hai:       0 °C (barf)             matra = Q ÷ L
 mixture formula          100 °C (bhaap)          se nikalo

```

> **Figure 1.1** – Anth-avastha ki jaanch ka flow-chart. Pehle garmi ka hisaab, phir milaan, phir hi final temperature ka formula. Bhaap ke case mein phase-change temperature 100 °C hai, aur bhaap ke liye L_v lagta hai.

**Safe activity: ghar par adult ke saath.** Ek steel ke glass mein lagbhag 200 mL **nal ka paani** (room temperature) lo aur ek thermometer daalo. **Round 1:** sirf 2 barf ke tukde daalo, chamach se dheere hilao, har minute temperature padho. Barf poori pighal jaati hai aur temperature 0 °C se **kaafi upar** rehta hai. **Round 2:** wahi glass dobara bharo aur is baar **bahut saari barf** daalo. Temperature girte girte lagbhag 0 °C par aata hai aur **jab tak barf dikhti hai, wahi atka rehta hai**. (Ghar ke thermometer mein 1 ya 2 °C ka farak aa sakta hai. Paani peena nahi.) Yahi anth-avastha ki jaanch ka seedha dekhne wala roop hai.

## Part B: Chai mein barf (ice-tea ka hisaab)

**Example 1 (Tanushree ka ice-tea: poori barf pighli?):** 0.26 kg chai (paani maan lo) 78 °C par hai. Usme 0.08 kg barf 0 °C par daali gayi. Garmi ka koi nuksaan nahi. Kya poori barf pighli? Final temperature kya hai?

- **Given:** m_chai = 0.26 kg, T_chai = 78 °C, m_barf = 0.08 kg, T_barf = 0 °C. **Find:** end state aur T.
- **Milne wali garmi** (chai 78 °C se 0 °C tak): Q_mil = 0.26 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × 78 °C = **85176 J**
- **Chahiye wali garmi** (barf 0 °C par, sirf pighalna): Q_chahiye = 0.08 kg × 336000 J/kg = **26880 J**
- **Milaan:** 85176 J > 26880 J, to **poori barf pighlegi** aur **58296 J** bach jaayegi.
- Ye bachi garmi ab 0.26 + 0.08 = 0.34 kg paani ko 0 °C se T tak garm karegi: 0.34 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × T = 58296 J, to 1428 × T = 58296.
- **Answer:** T = 58296 ÷ 1428 = **lagbhag 40.8 °C**.

**Jaanch:** Chai ne gawai: 0.26 × 4200 × (78 − 40.82) = 40597 J. Barf ke paani ne payi: 26880 + 0.08 × 4200 × 40.82 = 26880 + 13717 = 40597 J ✅. Aur 40.8 °C, 0 aur 78 ke beech mein hai ✅. Class 9 wala "weighted average" (0.26 × 78 ÷ 0.34 = lagbhag 59.6 °C) yahan galat hota: wo barf ke pighalne ki garmi bhool jaata (Galti 1 dekho).

```
Chai se milne wali garmi    ████████████████████████████  85176 J
Barf ko pighlane ko chahiye █████████                     26880 J
Bachi garmi (mixture garm)  ███████████████████           58296 J
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Ice-tea ka garmi-hisaab (1 block lagbhag 3000 J). Chai se 28 block garmi mili. Barf ko pighlane mein sirf 9 block chahiye the. Bache 19 block poore mixture ko 0 °C se 40.8 °C tak garm karte hain: isiliye ice-tea abhi bhi halki garam hai.

Mama ne Tanushree ko samjhaya, "Itni si barf (0.08 kg) chai ko thanda nahi, bas halka thanda karti hai. **Barf ko pighalne mein hi bahut garmi leni padti hai (L_f)**, par chai ke paas usse kahin zyada garmi thi." Tanushree boli, "To zyada barf daalni thi!" Wahi hum ab dekhte hain.

**Example 2 (zyada barf: ab sab nahi pighlegi):** wahi 0.26 kg chai, 78 °C par. Ab usme 0.31 kg barf (0 °C par) daali gayi. End state aur final temperature?

- **Milne wali garmi:** wahi **85176 J** (Example 1 se, chai wahi hai).
- **Chahiye wali garmi** (poori barf pighlane ke liye): 0.31 kg × 336000 J/kg = **104160 J**.
- **Milaan:** 85176 J **<** 104160 J. Poori barf nahi pighlegi: mixture **0 °C par rukega**.
- **Kitni barf pighli:** m = 85176 J ÷ 336000 J/kg = **0.2535 kg**. Bachi barf = 0.31 − 0.2535 = **0.0565 kg**.
- **Answer:** mixture **0 °C par**, jisme **0.5135 kg paani** (chai ka 0.26 kg + pighla 0.2535 kg) aur **0.0565 kg barf** saath hain.

**Jaanch:** Chai ki saari garmi (85176 J) barf pighlane mein lagi: 0.2535 × 336000 = 85176 J ✅. Energy poori khatam ho gayi, isliye temperature 0 °C se upar nahi gaya ✅.

Dhyan do: zyada barf daalne se chai bahut thandi (0 °C) ho gayi, par poori barf nahi pighli. Pighli hui barf chai mein mil gayi, isliye chai bahut halki ho jaayegi. Ice-tea ke liye bachi barf nikaal lete hain.

**Example 3 (ulta case: thandi barf, thoda garm paani):** 0.6 kg barf −18 °C par hai. Usme 0.15 kg paani 8 °C par daala gaya. Garmi ka nuksaan nahi. End state?

Yahan barf paani se **thandi** hai, to garmi paani se barf ki taraf jaayegi. Do cheezein ho sakti hain: paani thanda hokar 0 °C par jam jaaye aur garmi chhode, ya barf 0 °C tak garm ho jaaye.

- **Barf ko −18 °C se 0 °C tak laane ki zaroorat:** 0.6 kg × 2100 J/kg °C × 18 °C = **22680 J**
- **Paani 8 °C se 0 °C tak thanda ho to garmi deta hai:** 0.15 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × 8 °C = **5040 J**
- **Kami:** 22680 J − 5040 J = **17640 J**. Ye garmi paani ko **jamne** (0 °C par) se milegi: kitna paani jam sakta hai? Zyada se zyada 0.15 kg × 336000 = 50400 J ka. 17640 J < 50400 J, to sirf **kuch paani** jamega.
- **Kitna jama:** m = 17640 J ÷ 336000 J/kg = **0.0525 kg**.
- **Answer:** sab **0 °C par**: barf = 0.6 + 0.0525 = **0.6525 kg**, paani = 0.15 − 0.0525 = **0.0975 kg**.

**Jaanch:** Garmi ka hisaab: paani ne diya 5040 + 0.0525 × 336000 = 5040 + 17640 = 22680 J, barf ne liya 22680 J ✅. Dekha? Yahan temperature **0 °C se neeche nahi** gaya (jab tak paani bacha hai), aur halat ulti ho gayi: paani ka kuch hissa **barf** bana.

## Part C: Bhaap thande paani mein (steam ka hisaab)

Bhaap ka hisaab barf jaisa hi hai, bas ab phase-change temperature **100 °C** hai. Bhaap thande paani ko garm karti hai. Do sawal: kya thanda paani 100 °C tak pahunchega (to bhaap sirf aadhi condense hogi), ya bhaap poori condense hokar bhi paani ko 100 °C tak nahi laa paayegi?

**Example 4 (poori bhaap condense hoti hai):** 0.05 kg bhaap (100 °C) ko 0.9 kg paani (18 °C) mein bubble kiya jaata hai. Garmi ka nuksaan nahi. Final temperature?

- **Milne wali garmi** (bhaap poori condense, 100 °C par): 0.05 kg × 2260000 J/kg = **113000 J**.
- **Chahiye wali garmi** (paani ko 18 °C se 100 °C tak laane ke liye): 0.9 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × (100 − 18) °C = **309960 J**.
- **Milaan:** 113000 J **<** 309960 J. Bhaap poori condense hogi, par paani 100 °C tak nahi pahunchega: final T **100 °C se kam**.
- Ab mixture ka formula: condense hui bhaap ka 100 °C paani bhi T tak thanda hoga:
  - Bhaap ne di: 113000 J + 0.05 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × (100 − T) °C
  - Paani ne li: 0.9 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × (T − 18) °C
  - 113000 + 21000 − 210 T = 3780 T − 68040, to 202040 = 3990 T
- **Answer:** T = 202040 ÷ 3990 = **lagbhag 50.6 °C**.

**Jaanch:** Bhaap ne gawai: 113000 + 210 × (100 − 50.64) = 113000 + 10366 = 123366 J. Paani ne payi: 3780 × (50.64 − 18) = 3780 × 32.64 = 123366 J ✅. Aur 50.6 °C, 18 aur 100 ke beech ✅.

**Example 5 (bhaap bach jaati hai):** 0.18 kg bhaap (100 °C) ko 0.6 kg paani (22 °C) mein chhoda jaata hai. End state?

- **Milne wali garmi:** 0.18 kg × 2260000 J/kg = **406800 J**.
- **Chahiye wali garmi** (paani ko 22 °C se 100 °C tak): 0.6 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × 78 °C = **196560 J**.
- **Milaan:** 406800 J **>** 196560 J. Paani 100 °C tak pahunch jaayega aur bhaap ke paas abhi garmi bachi hai.
- **Kitni bhaap condense hui** (sirf utni jitni 196560 J de sake): m = 196560 J ÷ 2260000 J/kg = **0.0870 kg**. Bachi bhaap = 0.18 − 0.0870 = **0.0930 kg**.
- **Answer:** mixture **100 °C par**, jisme lagbhag **0.687 kg paani** (0.6 + 0.0870) aur **0.0930 kg bhaap** hai.

**Jaanch:** 0.0870 kg × 2260000 = 196620 J ≈ 196560 J ✅ (rounding). Aur T = 100 °C se upar nahi gaya ✅. (Asli khule bartan mein bachi bhaap bahar nikal jaati. Ye sawal band, insulated bartan ka ideal case hai: kaagaz par.)

## Part D: Barf aur bhaap saath mein: do jaanch

Kabhi kabhi dono hote hain: thandi barf, aur garam bhaap. Tab **do alag jaanch** karni padti hai: 0 °C par aur 100 °C par. Neeche ka Example dekho.

**Example 6 (barf + bhaap):** 0.22 kg barf −9 °C par hai. Usme 0.07 kg bhaap (100 °C) chhodi gayi. Garmi ka nuksaan nahi. Final state?

- **Jaanch 1 (0 °C par):** barf ko 0 °C tak laane aur pighlane mein: 0.22 × 2100 × 9 + 0.22 × 336000 = 4158 + 73920 = **78078 J**. Bhaap condense hokar 0 °C tak thandi hone par de sakti hai: 0.07 × 2260000 + 0.07 × 4200 × 100 = 158200 + 29400 = **187600 J**. 187600 J > 78078 J, to **poori barf pighlegi**. Mixture 0 °C par nahi rukega.
- **Jaanch 2 (100 °C par):** barf ko pighla kar paani ko 100 °C tak laane mein: 4158 + 73920 + 0.22 × 4200 × 100 = 78078 + 92400 = **170478 J**. Bhaap sirf condense hokar (100 °C par) de sakti hai: **158200 J**. 158200 J < 170478 J, to **paani 100 °C tak nahi pahunchega**: bhaap poori condense ho jaayegi, final T 100 °C se kam.
- Ab final T 0 aur 100 °C ke beech hai: bhaap ne di = barf aur uske paani ne li:
  - 158200 + 0.07 × 4200 × (100 − T) = 4158 + 73920 + 0.22 × 4200 × T
  - 158200 + 29400 − 294 T = 78078 + 924 T, to 109522 = 1218 T
- **Answer:** T = 109522 ÷ 1218 = **lagbhag 89.9 °C**. Poori barf pighli, poori bhaap condense hui, sab paani.

**Jaanch:** Bhaap ne gawai: 158200 + 294 × (100 − 89.92) = 158200 + 2963 = 161163 J. Barf ne payi: 78078 + 924 × 89.92 = 78078 + 83086 = 161164 J ✅.

## Part E: Garam dhaatu ka tukda barf mein aur paani mein

Ab ek **garam dhaatu** ka tukda lo. Dhaatu ka c bahut kam hota hai (Class 9 ke Example 4 aur 5 jaisa), isliye wo itni garmi nahi deta jitna lagta hai. Is topic mein **do dhaatu** aayengi: **aluminium c = 900 J/kg °C** aur **copper c = 380 J/kg °C** (ye is topic ki values hain. Class 9 mein copper ke liye lagbhag 400 liya tha, asli value 385 ke aas-paas hai: dono lagbhag hain).

**Example 7 (garam aluminium barf mein):** 0.46 kg aluminium 160 °C par hai. Use 0.64 kg barf (0 °C par) mein daala gaya. Garmi ka nuksaan nahi. Kitni barf pighli?

- **Milne wali garmi** (aluminium 160 °C se 0 °C): 0.46 kg × 900 J/kg °C × 160 °C = **66240 J**.
- **Chahiye wali garmi** (poori barf pighlane ko): 0.64 kg × 336000 J/kg = **215040 J**.
- **Milaan:** 66240 J < 215040 J. Barf poori nahi pighlegi, final temperature **0 °C**.
- **Pighli barf:** m = 66240 J ÷ 336000 J/kg = **lagbhag 0.197 kg**. Bachi barf = 0.64 − 0.197 = **lagbhag 0.443 kg**.
- **Answer:** aluminium aur mixture dono **0 °C par**; lagbhag 0.443 kg barf bachi.

**Jaanch:** 0.19714 × 336000 = 66240 J ✅. Aur dhyan do: ek mote 0.46 kg ke tukde ne lagbhag **200 g** barf hi pighlayi, kyunki aluminium ka c paani ke c se bahut kam hai.

**Example 8 (garam copper paani mein, pehle 100 °C par jaanch):** 1.1 kg copper 520 °C par hai. Use 0.62 kg paani (24 °C) mein daala gaya. Kya paani ubal jaayega? Final temperature?

Yahan phase-change temperature **100 °C** hai (kyunki paani ubal sakta hai).

- **Milne wali garmi** (copper 520 °C se 100 °C tak): 1.1 kg × 380 J/kg °C × 420 °C = **175560 J**.
- **Chahiye wali garmi** (paani 24 °C se 100 °C tak, ubalne se pehle): 0.62 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × 76 °C = **197904 J**.
- **Milaan:** 175560 J < 197904 J. Paani **100 °C tak pahunchega hi nahi**: ubalega nahi. Final T 100 °C se kam.
- Principle of mixtures: 1.1 × 380 × (520 − T) = 0.62 × 4200 × (T − 24), to 418 (520 − T) = 2604 (T − 24), to 217360 − 418 T = 2604 T − 62496, to 279856 = 3022 T.
- **Answer:** T = 279856 ÷ 3022 = **lagbhag 92.6 °C**.

**Jaanch:** Copper ne gawai: 418 × (520 − 92.61) = 178651 J. Paani ne payi: 2604 × (92.61 − 24) = 178651 J ✅. 92.6 °C < 100 °C ✅ (jo humari jaanch ka natija tha). Agar milne wali garmi chahiye wali se bada hoti, to paani 100 °C par ubalta aur kuch bhaap banti: tab mixture 100 °C par rukta. (Aisa asli mein bhaap aur chheente ke saath hota hai: lohar ke ya factory mein garam dhaatu paani mein bujhane ka kaam bade logon ka hai.)

## Part F: Barf ka tukda garm kamre mein: samay ka hisaab

Ab Tanushree ka doosra sawal: cooler box ki barf lunch tak bachegi? Yahan garmi ek **dar** (watt) se aa rahi hai. Power aur energy ka sambandh Ch7 ([current-electricity-ii-part5.md](../current-electricity-ii/current-electricity-ii-part5.md)) mein dekha tha: **Q = P × t**, yaani **t = Q ÷ P**.

**Example 9 (cooler box):** Box mein lagbhag 2.6 kg barf 0 °C par hai. Dhoop ki wajah se box ke andar lagbhag 55 W ki garmi lagatar ghus rahi hai (sab barf ko hi jaati hai). (a) Saari barf pighalne mein kitna samay lagega? (b) Lunch 4 ghante baad hai: us waqt tak kitni barf bachegi?

- **Given:** m = 2.6 kg, P = 55 W = 55 J/s. **Find:** t, aur 4 ghante baad bachi barf.
- (a) **Formula:** Q = m L_f, t = Q ÷ P. **Substitution:** Q = 2.6 kg × 336000 J/kg = **873600 J**. t = 873600 J ÷ 55 J/s = **lagbhag 15884 s** = 15884 ÷ 3600 = **lagbhag 4.41 ghante** (lagbhag 265 minute).
- (b) 4 ghante = 4 × 3600 s = 14400 s. 4 ghante mein garmi = 55 × 14400 = **792000 J**. Pighli barf = 792000 J ÷ 336000 J/kg = **lagbhag 2.357 kg**. **Bachi barf = 2.6 − 2.357 = lagbhag 0.243 kg.**
- **Answer:** poori barf lagbhag 4.4 ghante mein pighlegi, aur lunch (4 ghante) par lagbhag 0.24 kg barf bachi hogi.

**Jaanch:** 55 J/s × 15884 s = 873620 J ≈ 873600 J ✅. 0.243 kg barf pighlane ke liye 0.243 × 336000 = 81648 J chahiye: 873600 − 792000 = 81600 J ✅ (rounding). Mixture bhi 0 °C par rahega, jab tak barf bachi hai.

**Example 10 (barf pehle garm hoti hai):** 3.2 kg barf −14 °C par ek khali warm kamre mein hai. Kamre se lagbhag 70 W ki garmi barf mein aa rahi hai. Kab tak barf apne **0 °C** par pahunchegi aur kab tak **poori pighlegi**?

Pehle **garm hona**, phir **pighalna**: dono ko alag alag line mein likho.

- **Stage 1 (−14 °C se 0 °C tak garm hona):** Q₁ = 3.2 kg × 2100 J/kg °C × 14 °C = **94080 J**. Samay t₁ = 94080 ÷ 70 = **1344 s** = **22.4 minute**.
- **Stage 2 (0 °C par pighalna):** Q₂ = 3.2 kg × 336000 J/kg = **1075200 J**. Samay t₂ = 1075200 ÷ 70 = **15360 s** = 256 minute.
- **Kul samay:** 1344 + 15360 = **16704 s** = 16704 ÷ 3600 = **4.64 ghante**.
- **Answer:** 22.4 minute mein barf 0 °C par aayegi, aur kul **lagbhag 4.64 ghante** mein poori pighlegi.

**Jaanch:** 70 J/s × 16704 s = 1169280 J = 94080 + 1075200 ✅. Pighalna kul samay ka lagbhag 92 percent hai (15360 ÷ 16704 = 0.92): jyada samay latent heat mein jaata hai ✅.

## Asli experiment mein thoda kam pighlti hai

Hamare sawalon mein "garmi ka koi nuksaan nahi" kehte hain. Asli calorimeter mein ye sach nahi hota. Calorimeter ka bartan khud bhi **garm** hota hai (wo garam paani se garmi leta hai, ise aage ki class mein "water equivalent" kehte hain), aur kuch garmi **hawa mein** bhi chali jaati hai. Isliye barf ko **thodi kam garmi** milti hai, aur asli experiment mein **thodi kam barf pighalti** hai jitna humara hisaab batata hai. Ulta bhi ho sakta hai: agar mixture kamre se **thanda** hai, to kamre ki garmi andar aati hai aur thodi zyada barf pighal jaati hai. Isliye ye hisaab **aadarsh** (ideal) hain aur asli answer ke kareeb hote hain, bilkul wahi nahi.

---

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Final temperature hamesha weighted average hota hai"**

Ek student ne 0.14 kg barf (0 °C par) aur 0.2 kg paani (24 °C par) mix kiya aur likha: T = (0.14 × 0 + 0.2 × 24) ÷ (0.14 + 0.2) = **14.1 °C**.

Sahi jaanch: milne wali garmi = 0.2 × 4200 × 24 = **20160 J**. Poori barf pighlane ko 0.14 × 336000 = **47040 J** chahiye. 20160 < 47040, to barf poori nahi pighlegi: kitni pighli = 20160 ÷ 336000 = **0.06 kg**, bachi barf **0.08 kg**, aur **temperature 0 °C**. Weighted average ne barf ko "paani jaisa" maan liya aur L_f ki garmi bhool gaya. Ye tabhi chalta hai jab kisi ki state na badle.

**Galti 2: "Barf daalne se mixture 0 °C se neeche chala jaata hai" (jab tak barf bachi hai, mixture 0 °C par rehta hai)**

Ek student ne 0.45 kg barf (0 °C par) 0.2 kg paani (9 °C par) mein daali. Usne milne wali garmi (0.2 × 4200 × 9 = **7560 J**) mein se barf ko pighlane ki garmi (0.45 × 336000 = **151200 J**) ghata di: bacha −143640 J. Phir usne ise poore 0.65 kg paani mein baant diya: ΔT = −143640 ÷ (0.65 × 4200) = **−52.6 °C**. Kya sach mein itni si barf paani ko −52.6 °C tak thanda kar sakti hai?

Nahi. **Negative bachi garmi ka matlab ye hai ki sab barf pighalne layak garmi mili hi nahi.** Paani ki saari garmi sirf **0.0225 kg** barf pighlane mein lag gayi (7560 ÷ 336000). Bachi barf 0.4275 kg. Mixture ka temperature **0 °C par hi** hai. Paani 0 °C se neeche tabhi jaata jab wo khud jam jaata, aur wo to is case mein jama hi nahi. Isliye pehle **jaanch**, phir hisaab.

## Quick Recap

- Mixture mein state badal sakti ho to **weighted average ka formula seedha nahi lagta**: pehle anth-avastha ki jaanch.
- **Milne wali garmi** (garam cheez ko 0 °C ya 100 °C tak thanda karne par) aur **chahiye wali garmi** (thandi cheez ko wahan tak laane aur poori state badalne ki) ki tulna karo.
- Milne wali **>** chahiye wali: poori state badli, bachi garmi mixture ko garm karti hai (mixture formula). **=**: sirf poori badli, temperature ruka hua. **<**: adhoori badli, mixture **0 °C ya 100 °C par** hi rehta hai, aur bachi/pighli matra Q ÷ L se.
- Barf ke saath temperature 0 °C par jam jaata hai jab tak barf aur paani dono hain. Ulte case mein (thandi barf, thoda garm paani) **paani ka kuch hissa jam sakta hai**.
- Barf aur bhaap dono ho, to **do jaanch**: 0 °C par aur 100 °C par.
- Garam dhaatu ka tukda: c chhota hota hai, isliye garmi kam milti hai (Al c = 900, Cu c = 380 J/kg °C, is topic ke liye).
- Garmi ka bahaav P (W) ho to **t = Q ÷ P**: pighalne ka samay latent heat se zyada tay hota hai.
- Scale ke hisaab ka heating curve mein boiling ka flat hissa sabse lamba hota hai.
- Asli experiment mein calorimeter aur hawa ki wajah se thodi kam barf pighalti hai.
