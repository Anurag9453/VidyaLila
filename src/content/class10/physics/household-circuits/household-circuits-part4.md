# Topic 4: Jab Kuch Galat Ho: Short Circuit, Overload Aur Loose Contact

## Shaadi hall ka garm multi-plug

Tulpule Mama ke ghar mein shaadi thi, aur shaam ko wo apne bhanje Vinit ko lekar shaadi hall dekhne gaye, jahan sajawat chal rahi thi. Stage ke peeche decorator ke ladke ne purani peeli bulb wali jhaalar ki bahut saari ladiyan, ek speaker aur chai ka bada garm-paani wala dibba, sab ek hi multi-plug mein ghused diye the. Multi-plug ek lambi taar se deewar ke socket tak ja raha tha.

Vinit ne dekha ki multi-plug ka plastic halka chamak raha hai. "Mama, ye garm kyun lag raha hai?" Usne haath badhaya hi tha ki Mama ne uska kandha pakad kar peeche kheencha. Tab tak ek tez smell aa chuki thi: **jalte hue plastic ki**. Hall ke electrician ne bhaagkar **main switch band kiya**, aur phir tab jaakar multi-plug ko torch se dekha. Socket ke aas-paas ka plastic kaala aur thoda pighla hua tha.

Vinit ne dheere se poocha, "Mama, ghar mein to MCB hota hai na? Wo kyun nahi gira? Aur ye multi-plug jal kyun raha tha jabki koi taar nahi tooti?" Mama ne kaha, "Bahut achhe sawaal hain. Aaj inhi ka jawab milega: ghar mein bijli kab-kab aur kitni tarah se galat ho sakti hai, aur MCB kis galti ko pakadta hai aur kisko chhod deta hai."

## Part A: Overload aur short circuit: do alag bimariyan

Dono mein taar garm hoti hai, par kaaran aur raftaar bilkul alag hain.

| Baat | Overload | Short circuit |
|---|---|---|
| Kaaran | Bahut saare appliance saath chalna | Live aur neutral ka seedha, bina appliance ke milna |
| Raasta | Raasta theek hai, current badh gaya | Naya, bahut chhota raasta bana |
| Resistance | Kul resistance ghata (parallel mein branches judne se) | Resistance bahut hi kam (taar ka apna) |
| Current | Rating se kuch guna zyada | Rating se kai sau guna tak zyada |
| Garmi | Dheere badhti hai, minuton mein | Ek second ke bahut chhote hisse mein |
| Kaun bachata hai | MCB ka bimetal strip (dheema) | MCB ka electromagnet (turant) |

Overload mein har appliance theek hota hai, galti **jod** mein hoti hai: ghar ke parallel circuit mein currents jud jaate hain (Class 9), aur jod taar ki rating se upar chala jaata hai.

```
  Live (laal) taar

        +---[ 16 A MCB ]------+-----------+-----------+-----------+
        :                     :           :           :           :
  +-----------+               :           :           :           :
  :   230 V   :          +---------+ +---------+ +---------+ +---------+
  :   supply  :          :  lights : : speakers: : coolers : :   urn   :
  +-----------+          :  3.91 A : :  3.48 A : :  2.43 A : :  8.26 A :
        :                +---------+ +---------+ +---------+ +---------+
        :                     :           :           :           :
        :                     :           :           :           :
        +---------------------+-----------+-----------+-----------+
  Neutral (kaala) taar
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Ek hi 2.5 mm² ke circuit par chaar parallel branches: lights, speakers, coolers aur urn. Har branch par 230 V hai, par currents jud kar lagbhag 18.09 A banta hai, jabki 2.5 mm² taar lagbhag 16 A tak ke liye hai.

### Example 1: Hall ke circuit ka overload

**Given:** Hall ke ek circuit par 2.5 mm² copper taar (safe current lagbhag 16 A) aur 16 A ka MCB hai. Usi par: jhaalar ki 6 ladiyan, 150 W har ek; 2 speaker, 400 W har ek; 2 cooler, 280 W har ek; chai ka urn, 1900 W. Supply 230 V.
**Find:** Kul current, taar ki rating se kitna upar, aur taar mein garmi kitni guna.
**Formula:** I = P ÷ V; garmi ∝ I² (taar ka R lagbhag same).
**Substitution:** P_kul = 6 × 150 W + 2 × 400 W + 2 × 280 W + 1900 W = 900 W + 800 W + 560 W + 1900 W = 4160 W. I = 4160 W ÷ 230 V = 18.09 A. Rating se upar: 18.09 A − 16 A = 2.09 A. Garmi ka anupaat = (18.09 A ÷ 16 A)² = (1.130)² = 1.28.
**Answer:** Kul current lagbhag 18.1 A, yaani taar ki 16 A rating se lagbhag 2.1 A zyada. Taar mein lagbhag 1.28 guna garmi, jo dheere-dheere badhti hai.
**Jaanch:** Branch currents: 3.91 A + 3.48 A + 2.43 A + 8.26 A = 18.08 A (rounding ke karan 18.09 A se 0.01 A kam) ✅; 230 V × 18.087 A = 4160 W ✅

Ye halka overload hai (16 A ka lagbhag 1.13 guna). Aise chhote overload par MCB ko ghante tak bhi na girne ki ijaazat hoti hai (lagbhag; MCB ke aam standard mein rating ka lagbhag 1.13 guna ek ghante tak chal sakta hai, aur lagbhag 1.45 guna par ek ghante ke andar girna zaroori hai), kyunki bimetal ko garm hone mein samay lagta hai. Isliye "MCB laga hai to kuch nahi hoga" sochna galat hai: MCB overload ko **dheere** pakadta hai, aur wo bhi sirf apne **peeche wali** taar ke liye.

## Part B: Short circuit: jab raasta bahut chhota ho jaaye

Insulation phat jaaye, ya koi dheeli taar apni jagah se khisak kar live aur neutral dono ko ek saath chhoo le, to current ko **appliance se guzarna hi nahi padta**. Appliance ka resistance (kai Ω se hazaaron Ω) beech se hat jaata hai, aur raaste mein sirf taar ka chhota resistance bachta hai, jise hum **loop resistance** kehte hain (live jaane ki taar + neutral wapsi ki taar).

```
  Live (laal) taar

        +---[ 16 A MCB ]------+---------------------------+
        :                     :                           :
  +-----------+               :                           :
  :   230 V   :               : <-- seedha raasta    +---------+
  :   supply  :               :   live se neutral    :   lamp  :
  +-----------+               :                      : (bypass):
        :                     :                      +---------+
        :                     :                           :
        :                     :                           :
        +---------------------+---------------------------+
  Neutral (kaala) taar
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Short circuit: live aur neutral ke beech ek seedha raasta ban gaya hai (insulation phatne se). Lamp ko current ka raasta nahi milta (bypass). Poora current supply se MCB aur taar se hokar seedhe is raaste se wapas aata hai, aur sirf taar ka resistance rukawat hai.

### Example 2: 0.46 Ω ka loop

**Given:** Ek circuit mein live aur neutral ki taar milakar loop resistance 0.46 Ω hai. Supply 230 V. Circuit normal mein lagbhag 2 A leta hai aur 16 A ka MCB laga hai.
**Find:** Short circuit current, normal current se kitna guna, MCB rating se kitna guna, aur loop mein garmi ki power.
**Formula:** I = V ÷ R_loop; P = I² R.
**Substitution:** I = 230 V ÷ 0.46 Ω = 500 A. Normal ke mukable: 500 A ÷ 2 A = 250 guna. MCB ke mukable: 500 A ÷ 16 A = 31.25 guna. P = (500 A)² × 0.46 Ω = 250 000 A² × 0.46 Ω = 115 000 W.
**Answer:** Lagbhag 500 A, yaani normal ka 250 guna. Taar mein garmi ki power lagbhag 115 kW (1 kW ke lagbhag 115 heaters ek saath chalne jitni).
**Jaanch:** Alag tareeka: P = V² ÷ R = 52 900 V² ÷ 0.46 Ω = 115 000 W ✅

**Honest baat:** Asli current thoda kam hoga, kyunki supply line ka apna resistance aur galti ke point ka resistance bhi hota hai. Par kai sau ampere tak ka current asli baat hai.

**Itni bhaari garmi ko kitni der jhela jaa sakta hai?** Agar MCB ka electromagnet kuch millisecond (lagbhag 0.01 s) mein supply kaat de, to loop mein garmi = 115 000 W × 0.01 s = **1150 J**, jo kuch nahi. Agar koi protection na ho aur ye sirf 1 s chale, to 115 000 W × 1 s = **115 000 J**: taar pighal kar insulation jala dega. Isiliye short circuit ke liye **turant** girne wala electromagnet chahiye, dheema bimetal nahi.

Dhyan do: ye ek kaagaz ka hisaab hai. Short circuit ko kabhi bhi koshish karke nahi dekhte.

## Part C: Dheela connection: sabse chupa hua khatra

Ab Vinit ka doosra sawaal. Plug ki pin aur socket ke beech, ya taar aur screw ke beech, bijli ko ek **touch** se guzarna padta hai. Agar touch dheela ho, ya zang aur kaalikh lagi ho, to current ke liye wahan ka raasta bahut patla ho jaata hai: **contact resistance** badh jaata hai. Ye chhota sa resistance bhi us jagah ke **series** mein hota hai, to wahi current wahan se guzarta hai aur wahan I² R garmi banti hai.

```
  Live (laal) taar

        +-----[ taar 0.0068 Ω ]-----[ dheela contact 1.5 Ω ]--+
        :                                   **                :
  +-----------+                       hot spot: yahin garmi   :
  :   230 V   :                                         +-----------+
  :   supply  :                                         :   heater  :
  +-----------+                                         :    20 Ω   :
        :                                               +-----------+
        :                                                     :
        :                                                     :
        +-----------------------------------------------------+
  Neutral (kaala) taar
```

> **Figure 4.3** – Heater ka circuit, jisme taar ka resistance (0.0068 Ω, 1 m ke 2.5 mm² copper ka) aur ek dheela contact (1.5 Ω) heater ke series mein hain. Poora loop band hai. Garmi us chhoti si jagah par ikatthi hoti hai jahan resistance sabse zyada hai.

### Example 3: Heater ke plug ka dheela contact

**Given:** Ek 230 V, 2645 W heater ek socket par chal raha hai. Plug ki dheeli pin ka contact resistance 1.5 Ω hai. 1 m ki 2.5 mm² copper taar (ρ = 1.7 × 10⁻⁸ Ω m) se tulna karni hai.
**Find:** Heater ka R, current, contact mein garmi, heater ki asli power, aur 1 m taar ki garmi.
**Formula:** R = V² ÷ P; I = V ÷ R_kul; P = I² R; R_taar = ρ l ÷ A.
**Substitution:** R_heater = 52 900 V² ÷ 2645 W = 20 Ω. R_kul = 20 Ω + 1.5 Ω = 21.5 Ω. I = 230 V ÷ 21.5 Ω = 10.70 A. P_contact = (10.70 A)² × 1.5 Ω = 114.4 A² × 1.5 Ω = 171.7 W. P_heater = 114.4 A² × 20 Ω = 2289 W. R_taar = 1.7 × 10⁻⁸ × 1 ÷ 2.5 × 10⁻⁶ = 0.0068 Ω, P_taar = 114.4 A² × 0.0068 Ω = 0.78 W.
**Answer:** Current lagbhag 10.70 A. Contact mein lagbhag 172 W ki garmi: ek chhoti si pin ke paas jahan se 1 m taar ki sirf lagbhag 0.78 W garmi nikalti hai, yaani lagbhag 220 guna. Heater ki power bhi 2645 W se ghat kar lagbhag 2289 W reh gayi.
**Jaanch:** 171.7 W + 2288.8 W + 0.78 W = 2461.3 W; 230 V × 10.698 A = 2460.5 W ✅ (rounding)

Teen cheezein dhyan se dekho:

1. **Current normal rehta hai** (10.70 A, 16 A ke MCB se neeche). **MCB kabhi nahi girega**, phir bhi pin ke paas lagbhag 172 W ka chhota "heater" jal raha hai. Yahi hall ke multi-plug ki kahani ka bada hissa hai.
2. Garmi bahut chhoti jagah mein hai: yahi **hot spot** hai. Plastic pighalta hai, aur dheere-dheere kaala padta hai.
3. Garm hone par dhaatu par oxide parat banne lagti hai, jisse contact resistance aur badh sakta hai, aur garmi aur zyada ho jaati hai (aksar aisa hota hai). Dheela contact apne aap badtar hota jaata hai.

**Arcing:** Dheeli pin vibration se kabhi touch karti hai kabhi chhodti hai. Chhodne ke pal mein current ek chhoti hawa ki gap mein se **chingari** banke kood sakta hai. Chingari ka tapman kai hazaar °C tak ho sakta hai (lagbhag), isliye wo plastic ya aas-paas ke kapde ko jala sakti hai. Isiliye dheele plug aur hilte hue socket ko bada turant electrician se badalwaye.

### Safe activity: dheele contact ka chhota model (school mein teacher ke saath)

3 V ki battery, ek torch bulb aur do paper clip lo. Paper clips ko bulb ke circuit mein aise jodo ki ek jagah clips sirf **halke se** chhoo rahe hon. Bulb **madham** jalega; clips ko kas kar dabao, to bulb tez ho jaayega. Samjhne wali baat: dheele touch ka resistance series mein judta hai, to current ghat jaata hai. Ghar ke high-voltage plug mein wahi cheez current ghatati bhi hai aur garmi bhi banati hai. (Sirf 3 V ki battery; battery ke dono sirey kabhi seedhe taar se nahi jodna. Mains ke plug par ye kabhi nahi.)

## Part D: Aur kaaran: jo dikhte nahi

Short circuit aur dheela contact ki jadein aksar yahi hoti hain:

| Kaaran | Kya hota hai | Bachaav (bade ya electrician ka kaam) |
|---|---|---|
| Phati hui ya ghisi insulation | Live aur neutral (ya body) ke beech raasta | Taar badalwana |
| Chuhe aur keede | Taar kaat dete hain, insulation kharab | Wiring ko jaanch karwana |
| Nami aur seelan | Paani se live aur neutral ke beech leakage | Geele hisse ki jaanch, bathroom mein dhyan |
| Purani wiring | Insulation sookh kar toot jaata hai, joints dheele | Samay par rewiring |
| Sasti, nakli taar aur plug | Label se patla copper, patla plastic | Standard mark wala saaman |
| Dheele screw aur plug | Hot spot aur arcing | Plug aur socket badalna |

Ye jaanch ki cheezein **sirf electrician** karta hai.

### Example 4: Sasti, nakli taar

**Given:** Ek 24 m ki run ke liye packet par "1.5 mm²" likha hai, par nakli taar asal mein 1.0 mm² ki hai (hedged: aise maamle hote hain). Load 10 A. ρ = 1.7 × 10⁻⁸ Ω m. Taar ki rating ki table: 1.0 mm² lagbhag 6 A, 1.5 mm² lagbhag 10 A.
**Find:** Dono taaron ka R aur garmi (P = I² R), aur kaun si rating ke upar hai.
**Formula:** R = ρ l ÷ A; P = I² R.
**Substitution:** Asli 1.5 mm²: R = 1.7 × 10⁻⁸ × 24 ÷ 1.5 × 10⁻⁶ = 0.272 Ω, P = (10 A)² × 0.272 Ω = 27.2 W. Nakli 1.0 mm²: R = 1.7 × 10⁻⁸ × 24 ÷ 1.0 × 10⁻⁶ = 0.408 Ω, P = 100 A² × 0.408 Ω = 40.8 W.
**Answer:** Nakli taar mein lagbhag 40.8 W garmi (asli ke 1.5 guna), aur 10 A load uski lagbhag 6 A ki rating se bahut upar hai: har samay overload.
**Jaanch:** 0.408 ÷ 0.272 = 1.5 = 1.5 ÷ 1.0 ✅ (area ka ulta anupaat)

Isliye taar aur plug kharidte waqt **standard mark** dekho aur packet par likhi rating ke saath bharosemand dukaan se hi lo (bade ke saath).

Sookhi CO₂ gas current ko nahi chalne deti, aur wo appliance par gandagi nahi chhodti. Dry powder bhi aag ko dhak deta hai. Aag chhoti ho tab hi bade log extinguisher use karein; badhne par sab bahar niklein aur fire brigade (lagbhag 101, ya 112) ko bulayein.

## Part F: Surge, toofan, charger aur ghar ka safety audit

**Surge.** Kabhi badi machine ke on-off hone par, ya paas mein bijli girne par, voltage **thodi der ke liye** bahut badh sakta hai: ye surge hai. Computer aur TV jaise nazuk appliance ke liye **surge protector** lagta hai, jo us badhe voltage ko poora andar jaane se rokta hai. **Voltage stabiliser** alag kaam karta hai: supply dheere-dheere kam ya zyada ho (jaise Chapter 7 ke verandah mein 184 V), to wo appliance ko lagbhag tay voltage deta hai. Surge protector achanak ke spike ke liye; stabiliser dheeme ghatne-badhne ke liye.

**Toofan mein.** Bijli kadakne ke dauran TV, router aur computer ke plug nikale hon to achha hai, par ye kaam **bade** karein, aur wo bhi pakki imaarat ke andar. Tum khule mein ya bade ped ke neeche mat khade ho.

**Charger aur battery.** Sirf **standard mark wale, asli charger** lo. Nakli charger mein andar ki doori aur insulation kam hoti hai. Charger ka thoda garam hona normal hai (kuch energy garmi ban jaati hai), par **takiye ya bistar ke neeche charger chalu rakhna** garmi ko bahar nikalne nahi deta: wo kapde mein garmi jama kar sakta hai. Phone ki battery phooli lage, ya charger bahut garm, to use band karo aur bade ko batao.

### Ghar par adult ke saath: safety audit (sirf dekho aur batao)

Ek page par ye checklist banao aur bade ke saath ghar mein ghoomo. **Kuch chhoona nahi, kuch kholna nahi.** Bas dekho aur likho:

## Wapas shaadi hall mein

Ab Vinit ke saare sawal saaf hain. Hall ka multi-plug ek **overload** ki wajah se garm tha: bahut saare appliance ek hi multi-plug par, jabki wo aur uski patli lambi taar utna current sahne ke liye bani hi nahi thi. Deewar ka MCB ek bade circuit (moti taar) ke liye laga tha, to wo **nahi gira**: wo sirf apne peeche wali moti taar ko bachata hai. Multi-plug ki pin ke paas dheela contact hot spot ban gaya, aur wahi se jalte plastic ki smell aayi. Electrician ne sahi kiya: **pehle main switch**, phir jaanch. Mama ne Vinit ko samjhaya, "Beta, jab bhi koi cheez garm lage, hum haath badhane se pehle bade ko bulate hain."

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Aag lagi hai to paani daal do."** Demonstration: Vinit ne socha ki jalte multi-plug par ek balti paani daal denge. Par balti se girti dhaar ek geeli "taar" ki tarah hai: ek sira live bhaag par, doosra daalne wale ke haath aur zameen se. Nal ke paani ke ions se wo current ko raasta de sakta hai, aur jhatka lagne ka khatra hai. Sahi kram: (1) door hato, (2) bada main supply band kare, (3) CO₂ ya dry powder extinguisher, (4) aag badhe to bahar aur 101 ya 112. Supply band hone ke baad bhi, chhoti aag ke liye paani nahi, jab tak electrician "supply band hai" na bol de.

**Galti 2: "Adaptor ke upar adaptor laga do, MCB bacha lega."** Demonstration: Ek 6 A ke adaptor (lagbhag 6 A × 230 V = 1380 W tak) par ek aur adaptor lagaya gaya aur teen appliance chalaye: toaster 760 W, cooker 1040 W, dryer 910 W. Kul 2710 W, to I = 2710 W ÷ 230 V = 11.78 A. 16 A ka MCB: 11.78 A < 16 A, **nahi girega**. Par adaptor ki apni rating 6 A thi: 11.78 A ÷ 6 A = 1.96 guna current, yaani adaptor ke andar lagbhag (1.96)² = 3.86 guna garmi, aur do adaptor ke beech ek aur dheela joint (contact resistance). **MCB sirf apne peeche wali taar ko jaanta hai**, adaptor aur extension lead ki apni rating alag hoti hai, aur wo sabse kamzor kadi hai. Isliye adaptor ya multi-plug par rating se zyada load nahi, aur stacking nahi.

## Quick Recap

- **Overload:** appliance saath chalne se currents jud kar taar ya board ki rating se upar. Garmi ∝ I², dheere badhti hai. MCB ka bimetal dheere pakadta hai.
- **Short circuit:** live aur neutral seedha mil gaye, loop resistance bahut kam (jaise 0.46 Ω), current V ÷ R (230 V par 500 A). Garmi bahut tezi se; MCB ka electromagnet turant kaat deta hai.
- **Loop resistance** = live ki taar + neutral ki taar.
- **Dheela ya zang laga contact:** chhote se hot spot par I² R garmi, current normal, **MCB nahi girta**. Plug garm ya kaala ho to turant bade ko batao.
- **Arcing:** dheele sirey se chingari: kai hazaar °C (lagbhag), aag ka kaaran.
- Kaaran: insulation, chuhe, seelan, purani wiring, nakli taar aur plug. **Standard mark** dekho.
- MCB sirf apne peeche ki taar bachata hai: adaptor aur multi-plug ki apni rating hoti hai.
- **Aag mein:** door hato, bada main supply band kare, CO₂ ya dry powder, **paani nahi**; badhe to bahar aur 101 ya 112.
- **Surge protector** achanak spike ke liye, **stabiliser** dheeme badlaav ke liye.
- Charger asli aur standard mark wala; bistar ya takiye ke neeche nahi.
- Safety audit: dekho aur bade ko batao, chhoona nahi.

## Practice Questions

(Use 230 V as the household supply and ρ of copper = 1.7 × 10⁻⁸ Ω m. Treat each appliance's resistance as constant.)

1. Two faults are possible in a house. In fault A the loop resistance is 0.35 Ω, and in fault B (at a far socket) it is 0.8 Ω. Find the short-circuit current in each (ignoring the supply's own resistance) and say how many times larger fault A's current is. Find also the power of heating in fault B's loop.
2. A loose pin has a contact resistance of 2.5 Ω and carries 7 A. Find the heat power at the contact and the energy produced in 25 minutes. Compare the heat power with that in a 0.5 m piece of 1.0 mm² copper wire carrying the same current.
3. A multi-plug is rated for 10 A. Five appliances are connected to it: a microwave oven 1330 W, a toaster 870 W, a grinder 640 W, a laptop charger 95 W and a fan 330 W. Find the total current. Is the multi-plug overloaded? Which single appliance should be removed to bring the current under 10 A?
4. A 1.45 kW heater (230 V) runs through a 3 m extension lead of total resistance 0.12 Ω. (a) Find the current and the heat produced in the lead each second. (b) A loose plug adds a contact resistance of 1.8 Ω in series. Find the new current, the heat power at the contact and the actual power of the heater.
5. A cable sold as 2.5 mm² is really 1.5 mm². It carries 14 A over a 15 m run. Find the heat produced in the real cable and in a genuine 2.5 mm² cable. Is 14 A within the safe current of the real cable? Does a 16 A MCB protect it?
6. Classify each as overload, short circuit or loose contact, and give the reason. (a) A rat bites a wire, and there is a bang and the MCB trips. (b) A multi-plug with several heavy appliances becomes warm, and the circuit MCB does not trip. (c) A plug is warm and smells of plastic although only a small heater is on it.
7. A multi-plug is smoking and there is a smell of burning plastic. Arrange these steps in order: (i) call the fire brigade if the fire grows, (ii) an adult switches off the main supply, (iii) move away and tell an adult, (iv) use a CO₂ or dry powder extinguisher. Why must a bucket of water not be used?
8. State True or False with a reason. (a) A 16 A MCB protects every extension lead and adaptor after it. (b) A loose contact can overheat even when the current is below the MCB's rating. (c) The short-circuit current mainly depends on the wattage of the appliance. (d) A surge protector holds a low supply voltage steady. (e) A genuine phone charger may be used under a pillow while charging.
9. A phone charger delivers 20 W and is 80 percent efficient. Find its input power and the power lost as heat. How much energy is lost as heat in 8 hours, in Wh and in J? Why is it unsafe under a pillow?
10. A new circuit uses 2.5 mm² copper wire with a total (live plus neutral) length of 36 m. Find its loop resistance, the short-circuit current at the far end, and how many times that is of a 16 A MCB's rating. Why does a fault far from the board give a smaller current?
11. A decoration team uses 55 identical strings of lights. Each string takes 8 W if LED and 42 W if the old filament type. Find the total current in each case. If the multi-plug is rated 6 A, which case overloads it, and by what factor does the heat in the multi-plug rise compared with its rated current?
12. Explain in two lines each: the difference between a surge protector and a voltage stabiliser, and why a standard mark matters on a cable.

### Answers

1. **Given:** V = 230 V, R_A = 0.35 Ω, R_B = 0.8 Ω. **Formula:** I = V ÷ R; P = V² ÷ R. **Substitution:** I_A = 230 V ÷ 0.35 Ω = **657 A**. I_B = 230 V ÷ 0.8 Ω = **287.5 A**. Anupaat = 657.1 ÷ 287.5 = **2.29**. P_B = 52 900 V² ÷ 0.8 Ω = **66 125 W** (lagbhag 66 kW). **Jaanch:** (287.5 A)² × 0.8 Ω = 82 656 A² × 0.8 Ω = 66 125 W ✅

2. **Given:** R_c = 2.5 Ω, I = 7 A, t = 25 min = 1500 s. **Formula:** P = I² R; E = P t. **Substitution:** P = (7 A)² × 2.5 Ω = 49 A² × 2.5 Ω = **122.5 W**. E = 122.5 W × 1500 s = **183 750 J**. Taar: R = 1.7 × 10⁻⁸ × 0.5 ÷ 1.0 × 10⁻⁶ = 0.0085 Ω; P = 49 A² × 0.0085 Ω = **0.4165 W**. Anupaat = 122.5 ÷ 0.4165 = **lagbhag 294**. **Jaanch:** 122.5 W × 1500 s = 183 750 J ✅ (contact ka R ÷ taar ka R = 2.5 ÷ 0.0085 = 294 ✅)

3. P_kul = 1330 W + 870 W + 640 W + 95 W + 330 W = 3265 W. I = 3265 W ÷ 230 V = **14.2 A**, jo 10 A se zyada hai: **haan, overloaded**. 10 A par maximum power = 230 V × 10 A = 2300 W. Microwave hatane par 3265 W − 1330 W = 1935 W, I = 1935 W ÷ 230 V = **8.41 A < 10 A** ✅. Toaster hatane par 2395 W, I = 10.41 A, jo abhi bhi 10 A se zyada hai: isliye **sirf microwave** hatane se kaam chalta hai. **Jaanch:** 8.41 A × 230 V = 1934.3 W ≈ 1935 W ✅

4. R_heater = 52 900 V² ÷ 1450 W = 36.48 Ω. (a) R_kul = 36.48 Ω + 0.12 Ω = 36.60 Ω; I = 230 V ÷ 36.60 Ω = **6.28 A**; P_lead = (6.28 A)² × 0.12 Ω = 39.49 A² × 0.12 Ω = **4.74 W** (har second lagbhag 4.74 J). (b) R_kul = 36.48 + 0.12 + 1.8 = 38.40 Ω; I = 230 V ÷ 38.40 Ω = **5.99 A**; P_contact = (5.99 A)² × 1.8 Ω = 35.87 A² × 1.8 Ω = **64.6 W**; P_heater = 35.87 A² × 36.48 Ω = **1309 W** (rated 1450 W se kam). **Jaanch:** 64.6 W + 1308.6 W + 4.31 W (lead: 35.87 × 0.12) = 1377.5 W = 230 V × 5.989 A = 1377.5 W ✅

5. **Given:** l = 15 m, I = 14 A. **Formula:** R = ρ l ÷ A; P = I² R. **Substitution:** Genuine 2.5 mm²: R = 1.7 × 10⁻⁸ × 15 ÷ 2.5 × 10⁻⁶ = 0.102 Ω; P = (14 A)² × 0.102 Ω = 196 A² × 0.102 Ω = **19.99 W** (lagbhag 20 W). Real 1.5 mm²: R = 1.7 × 10⁻⁸ × 15 ÷ 1.5 × 10⁻⁶ = 0.170 Ω; P = 196 A² × 0.170 Ω = **33.3 W**. Real cable mein lagbhag 1.67 guna garmi. 1.5 mm² ki safe current lagbhag 10 A hai, to 14 A uske **bahar** hai. 16 A ka MCB 14 A par nahi girega, to wo is cable ko **nahi bachata** (16 A uski 10 A rating se upar). **Jaanch:** 0.170 ÷ 0.102 = 1.67 = 2.5 ÷ 1.5 ✅

6. (a) **Short circuit**: taar kaatne se live aur neutral seedhe mile (bang, MCB turant gira). (b) **Overload** (aur multi-plug ki apni rating ke upar): currents jud kar board ya taar ki rating se upar; MCB bade circuit ke liye hai, isliye nahi gira. (c) **Loose contact**: chhoti load par current normal hai, par dheele contact ke hot spot par I² R garmi. 

7. Sahi kram: **(iii), (ii), (iv), (i)**. Pehle door hato aur bade ko batao; bada main supply band kare; phir CO₂ ya dry powder extinguisher; aag badhe to fire brigade. Paani kyun nahi: nal ke paani ke ions se wo current guzarne deta hai, to dhaar daalne wale tak live part se current ka raasta ban sakti hai, aur jhatka lag sakta hai.

8. (a) **False** ❌: MCB sirf apne peeche wali taar ko bachata hai; adaptor aur lead ki apni rating alag hoti hai. (b) **True** ✅: hot spot par I² R garmi normal current par bhi, jaise Example 3. (c) **False** ❌: current mainly V ÷ R_loop hai (taar ka resistance), appliance ka wattage beech mein hai hi nahi. (d) **False** ❌: wo achanak spike ko rokta hai; steady voltage stabiliser deta hai. (e) **False** ❌: asli charger bhi takiye ke neeche garmi nikalne nahi paata.

9. **Given:** P_out = 20 W, efficiency 80 percent. **Formula:** P_in = P_out ÷ efficiency. **Substitution:** P_in = 20 W ÷ 0.80 = **25 W**; P_heat = 25 W − 20 W = **5 W**. 8 h mein: 5 W × 8 h = **40 Wh** = 40 × 3600 J = **144 000 J**. Takiye ke neeche ye garmi bahar nikalne ki jagah nahi paati, to charger aur kapda dono garm ho sakte hain. **Jaanch:** 5 W × 28 800 s = 144 000 J ✅

10. **Given:** l = 36 m, A = 2.5 mm² = 2.5 × 10⁻⁶ m². **Formula:** R = ρ l ÷ A; I = V ÷ R. **Substitution:** R = 1.7 × 10⁻⁸ × 36 ÷ 2.5 × 10⁻⁶ = **0.2448 Ω**. I = 230 V ÷ 0.2448 Ω = **939.5 A** (lagbhag 940 A). 939.5 A ÷ 16 A = **58.7 guna**. Board se door galti par taar lambi, to loop resistance zyada, to current kam (par phir bhi kai sau ampere). **Jaanch:** 939.5 A × 0.2448 Ω = 230.0 V ✅

11. LED: P = 55 × 8 W = 440 W, I = 440 W ÷ 230 V = **1.91 A**. Filament: P = 55 × 42 W = 2310 W, I = 2310 W ÷ 230 V = **10.04 A**. 6 A ke multi-plug ko **filament wala** overload karta hai: 10.04 A ÷ 6 A = 1.67 guna, garmi (1.67)² = **lagbhag 2.8 guna**. LED wala 1.91 A, rating ke andar. **Jaanch:** 440 W ÷ 230 V = 1.913 A ✅; 2310 ÷ 230 = 10.043 A ✅

12. Surge protector achanak aur bahut thodi der ke voltage spike ko appliance tak jaane se rokta hai; stabiliser lambe samay ke dheeme ghatne-badhne ko theek karke lagbhag tay voltage deta hai. Standard mark batata hai ki taar ya plug jaanch-parakh ke standard par khara utra hai: label ke hisaab se copper, insulation aur plug ki pin ka size sahi hone ki umeed rehti hai, aur nakli samaan se aag ka khatra kam hota hai.
