# Topic 5: Socket Ke Peeche Ki Duniya: Power Plant Se Ghar Tak

Topic 1 se 4 tak hum ghar ke **andar** ghoome: fuse aur MCB, earthing aur RCCB, ghar ke circuit aur wo galtiyan jo wiring bigaad sakti hain. Ab ek bada sawaal baaki hai: jo 230 V socket mein aa raha hai, wo **aata kahan se hai**? Aaj hum deewar ke us paar, khambon aur taaron ke us lambe safar ko dekhenge, aur ye bhi ki us safar mein **I² R** (Chapter 7 wali garmi) ek bahut bada kirdaar kyun nibhaata hai.

## Khambe par lineman

Hasabnis Mama ka gaon shehar se kuch ghante door tha, aur Sunday ko wo apni bhanji Rutuja ko wahan le gaye. Dopahar mein achanak pankhe ruk gaye, bulb bujh gaye: bijli chali gayi. Gali ke mod par ek khambe ke upar ek bade se dabbe jaisa (kaale-bhoore rang ka) **pole transformer** laga tha. Thodi der mein bijli vibhag ki ek gaadi aayi, jisse ek **lineman** helmet aur dastaane pehne seedhi lagakar khambe par chadha.

Rutuja sadak par, lagbhag 30 m door, Mama ke saath khadi thi. Wo aage badhne lagi to Mama ne uska haath pakad liya: "Yahin ruko. Khambe, taar aur transformer ke paas **koi bhi** nahi jaata, sirf prashikshit lineman, aur wo bhi line band karke aur apne vibhag ke niyam se kaam karta hai. Hum yahan se sirf dekhenge."

Aadhe ghante baad bijli wapas aa gayi. Rutuja ne haath se bulb ki taraf ishara kiya. "Mama, ye roshni **aati kahan se hai**? Us dabbe se? Aur wo dabba bhi kisi se to bijli le raha hoga na? Aur khambon par itne motey taar... mere ghar ke socket ke taar to kitne patle hain!"

Mama muskuraye. "Bahut badhiya sawaal. Chalo, bijli ka safar ulta chalte hain: pehle bijli-ghar, phir khambon ki lambi qatar, phir ye dabba, aur aakhir mein tumhara socket."

> **Figure 5.1** – Bijli ka safar, baayen se daayen (phir neeche wali line mein): bijli-ghar mein lagbhag 11 kV, step-up transformer se transmission ka bahut ooncha voltage (132, 220, 400 ya 765 kV, lagbhag), grid substation mein step-down, gali tak lagbhag 11 kV, pole transformer ke baad ghar tak 230 V. Teen-phase ka 415 V (lagbhag) pole transformer ke output par milta hai.

**Kadam 1: Bijli banti hai.** Bijli-ghar mein turbine generator ko ghumata hai. Turbine ko koyle ka bhaap, bahta paani, nuclear garmi ya hawa ghumaati hai (Chapter 3: [sources-of-energy-part2.md](../sources-of-energy/sources-of-energy-part2.md), [sources-of-energy-part3.md](../sources-of-energy/sources-of-energy-part3.md)). Generator se bijli lagbhag **11 kV** par nikalti hai. Chhat ke solar panel ki bijli alag hai: wo sidhi ghar ya local line mein judti hai (neeche net metering dekho).

**Kadam 2: Voltage badhaya jaata hai.** Bijli-ghar ke bahar **step-up transformer** voltage ko **132 kV, 220 kV, 400 kV ya 765 kV** tak (lagbhag) badhata hai. Kyun? Ye Part B ka main sawaal hai.

**Kadam 3: Transmission line.** Ye lambi lines loha-tower par kai sau kilometre tak jaati hain.

**Kadam 4: Grid substation.** Shehar ke bahar **step-down transformer** voltage ko phir ghatata hai (lagbhag 11 kV tak) aur taar alag-alag ilaakon mein baant diye jaate hain.

**Kadam 5: Distribution line aur pole transformer.** Gali ke khambon par lagbhag 11 kV ki distribution line aati hai. Pole transformer use ghatakar ghar ke liye **415 V teen-phase** (lagbhag) banata hai, aur har ghar ko usme se **230 V** (lagbhag) milta hai.

**Kadam 6: Tumhara ghar.** Service line, meter, main MCB, board: yani Class 9 aur Topic 3 wala hissa.

**Grid ek bada jaal hai.** Bahut saare bijli-ghar ek hi network ko bijli dete hain, aur network se bahut saare shehar jude hain. Desh ke zyadatar hisse aise bade jude networks ka hissa hain (kaafi had tak, lagbhag), aur sab 50 Hz par ek saath chalte hain. Isliye ek bijli-ghar band ho to dusra uski jagah madad kar sakta hai. Ek aur honest baat: bijli ko badi matra mein jama karke rakhna abhi mehnga hai, isliye lagbhag **jitni bijli ghar-factory mein kharch ho rahi hai, utni hi us pal bijli-ghar mein ban rahi hoti hai**.

**Ghar aur chhat ka solar.** Chapter 3 mein dekha tha: chhat ka panel din mein ghar ko bijli deta hai, bachi hui grid ko, aur raat mein ghar grid se leta hai; **net metering** meter dono ka hisaab rakhta hai ([sources-of-energy-part4.md](../sources-of-energy/sources-of-energy-part4.md)). Yaani ghar kabhi-kabhi grid ka **grahak** hi nahi, **supplier** bhi hai.

## Part B: Itna ooncha voltage kyun? Line loss ka hisaab

Rutuja ne poochha: "Mama, jab ghar ko sirf 230 V chahiye, to bijli-ghar se 400 kV par kyun bhejte hain? Khatarnak nahi hai?" Khatarnak hai, aur isliye lines tower par oonchi, door aur suraksha ke saath hoti hain. Par ek bahut accha **kaaran** bhi hai.

Bijli ki power **P = V I** hoti hai. Kisi shehar ko, maan lo, 3.6 MW chahiye. Wahi power do tareeke se bheji ja sakti hai: **kam V, bada I** ya **bada V, chhota I**. Taar ko garm karne wali cheez **current** hai:

line loss = I² R, aur I = P ÷ V, isliye line loss = P² R ÷ V²

Yahan P wo power hai jo bheji ja rahi hai, V line ka voltage aur R line ka kul resistance (jaana + aana). (Seedhi samajh ke liye hum line par girne wale voltage ko chhota maan rahe hain: asli lines mein ye hisaab lagbhag sahi baithta hai.) Isse do baatein nikalti hain:

1. V **10 guna** karo to I **ek-dasva (1 ÷ 10)** ho jaata hai.
2. I ek-dasva ho to I² R **ek-sau-va (1 ÷ 100)** ho jaata hai. Yani loss, V² ke ulte anupaat mein girta hai.

```
  (a) Kam voltage (10 kV) par: I = 360 A

        +-----------[ line ka kul resistance R ]-----------+
        :                                                  :
  +-----------+                                      +-----------+
  :  station  :                                      :  shehar   :
  :  10 kV    :                                      :  ka load  :
  +-----------+                                      +-----------+
        :                                                  :
        +--------------------------------------------------+
           (har taar mein wahi I = 360 A)

  (b) Dus guna voltage (100 kV) par: I = 36 A

        +-----------[ line ka kul resistance R ]-----------+
        :                                                  :
  +-----------+                                      +-----------+
  :  station  :                                      :  shehar   :
  :  100 kV   :                                      :  ka load  :
  +-----------+                                      +-----------+
        :                                                  :
        +--------------------------------------------------+
           (har taar mein wahi I = 36 A)
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Wahi 3.6 MW wahi line (R = 8 Ω, jaana aur aana milakar) se bheja ja raha hai. (a) 10 kV par current 360 A; (b) voltage 10 guna to current 36 A. Dono loops poore band hain: station se line, load, aur wapas taar.

### Example 1: Dus guna voltage, sau guna kam loss

**Given:** Shehar ko P = 3.6 MW = 3 600 000 W bhejna hai. Line ka kul resistance R = 8 Ω. Do tareeke samajhne ke liye: V₁ = 10 kV = 10 000 V aur V₂ = 100 kV = 100 000 V (ye voltage samajhne ke liye maane gaye hain, asli list nahi).
**Find:** Dono mein current, line loss, aur loss kitne percent hai.
**Formula:** I = P ÷ V; line loss = I² R; percent = loss ÷ P × 100.
**Substitution:** I₁ = 3 600 000 W ÷ 10 000 V = 360 A. Loss₁ = (360 A)² × 8 Ω = 129 600 A² × 8 Ω = 1 036 800 W ≈ 1.04 MW. Percent₁ = 1 036 800 W ÷ 3 600 000 W × 100 = 28.8 percent. I₂ = 3 600 000 W ÷ 100 000 V = 36 A. Loss₂ = (36 A)² × 8 Ω = 1296 A² × 8 Ω = 10 368 W ≈ 10.4 kW. Percent₂ = 10 368 W ÷ 3 600 000 W × 100 = 0.288 percent.
**Answer:** 10 kV par current 360 A aur loss lagbhag 1.04 MW (28.8 percent, bahut zyada). 100 kV par current 36 A aur loss lagbhag 10.4 kW (0.288 percent).
**Jaanch:** Loss₁ ÷ Loss₂ = 1 036 800 W ÷ 10 368 W = 100 ✅; I₁ ÷ I₂ = 360 A ÷ 36 A = 10 ✅. Units: A² Ω = W ✅

Ye hisaab dikhata hai ki bahut kam voltage par bhejne par to aadhi se kam bijli taar mein hi garm hokar kho jaati, aur taar ko bhi bahut motta banana padta.

### Example 2: Sirf 10 guna nahi: 132 kV aur 400 kV

Voltage ka anupaat hamesha 10 hi ho zaroori nahi. Ek gaon ko P = 1.2 MW bhejna hai, line ka R = 15 Ω. Do lines: 132 kV aur 400 kV.

**Given:** P = 1.2 MW = 1 200 000 W, R = 15 Ω, V₁ = 132 kV = 132 000 V, V₂ = 400 kV = 400 000 V.
**Find:** Dono mein I aur loss, aur loss ka anupaat.
**Formula:** I = P ÷ V; loss = I² R.
**Substitution:** I₁ = 1 200 000 W ÷ 132 000 V = 9.09 A; loss₁ = (9.09 A)² × 15 Ω = 82.6 A² × 15 Ω = 1240 W. I₂ = 1 200 000 W ÷ 400 000 V = 3.00 A; loss₂ = (3.00 A)² × 15 Ω = 9 A² × 15 Ω = 135 W. Anupaat = 1240 W ÷ 135 W = 9.18.
**Answer:** 132 kV par lagbhag 1240 W, 400 kV par 135 W. Voltage 400 ÷ 132 = 3.03 guna karne par loss lagbhag 9.18 guna kam.
**Jaanch:** (400 ÷ 132)² = (3.03)² = 9.18 ✅ (loss, V² ke ulte anupaat mein)

**Phir 765 kV ya usse bhi zyada kyun nahi?** Voltage jitna oonchha, utne oonche tower, taaron ke beech zyada doori, zyada mazboot insulator aur zyada jagah chahiye: kharcha aur khatra badhte hain. Isliye har doori aur power ke liye ek munasib level chuna jaata hai: kam doori, kam power par 132 kV, badi doori aur badi power par 400 kV ya 765 kV (lagbhag).

**Aur DC nahi, AC kyun?** Ek ghar tak bijli AC mein isliye bheji jaati hai kyunki **transformer** AC voltage ko aasaani se badha aur ghata deta hai (Chapter 9 mein iska raaz). Isi wajah se "bijli-ghar mein ooncha voltage banao, ghar mein neecha" sasti ho jaati hai.

## Part C: Transformer aur 50 Hz: naam aur kaam, raaz Chapter 9 mein

**Transformer** ek aisa device hai jo **AC** voltage ko badha (step-up) ya ghata (step-down) sakta hai, aur is topic ke liye hum use sirf **uske kaam** se jaante hain. Bijli-ghar ke bahar step-up, substation aur pole transformer par step-down. Wo andar se kaise kaam karta hai aur kitne gunaa badhata-ghatata hai, ye **Chapter 9 Electromagnetism** mein aayega. Abhi bas itna: transformer ko AC chahiye; DC par ye kaam nahi karta (aur iske ek-do aur kaaran bhi Chapter 9 mein).

**50 Hz:** poora grid ek hi frequency par chalta hai: **lagbhag 50 Hz**, yaani current ek second mein 50 baar poora chakkar lagata hai aur **100 baar disha badalta hai** (Class 9 ka recall). Ye itna tez hai ki bulb ko hamein jhapakta nahi dikhta. Isse zyada AC ki baat (kaise naapte hain, kaise teen cheezein ek saath chalti hain) aage ki class mein.

## Part D: Gali ke mod ka dabba: pole transformer aur teen-phase

Substation se gali tak ek **teen taar** wali (har taar alag phase) lagbhag 11 kV ki line aati hai. Pole transformer uska voltage ghatakar ghar ke kaam ka bana deta hai. Uske output par **chaar taar** hote hain: teen **live** (R laal, Y peela, B neela) aur ek **neutral** (N). Ye teen-phase supply hai. (Class 9 ne neutral ka rang "kaala ya neela" bataya tha; teen-phase mein neela taar B phase ka hota hai, isliye wahan neutral kaala dikhaya hai. Rang kabhi pehchaan ka bharosa nahi: wiring electrician hi padhta hai.)

```
  R (laal) -----------------------+
                                  :
                            +-----------+
                            :   Ghar A  :   230 V
                            +-----------+
                                  :
  N neutral (kaala) --------------+

  Y (peela) ----------------------+
                                  :
                            +-----------+
                            :   Ghar B  :   230 V
                            +-----------+
                                  :
  N neutral (kaala) --------------+

  B (neela) ----------------------+
                                  :
                            +-----------+
                            :   Ghar C  :   230 V
                            +-----------+
                                  :
  N neutral (kaala) --------------+

  R (laal) -----------------------+
                                  :
                            +-----------+
                            :  factory  :   400 V
                            :   motor   :
                            +-----------+
                                  :
  Y (peela) ----------------------+
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Teen-phase output se ghar kaise jude hain. Ghar A, B aur C har ek ke paas **ek live aur neutral** hai (230 V, lagbhag). Factory motor ke liye do live taar (R aur Y) hote hain, aur unke beech lagbhag 400 V hai. Har loop poora band hai. Neutral taar saare loops ke liye ek hi hai; samajhne ke liye har loop mein alag dikhaya hai.

Zyadatar ghar **ek hi live aur neutral** lete hain (**single-phase**, lagbhag 230 V). Ek mohalle ke ghar aam taur par teeno phases mein baante jaate hain, taaki har live taar par load lagbhag barabar rahe. Factory aur badi motor **teen live taar** (three-phase) lete hain: iska hisaab aage ki class mein aayega, abhi bas naam jaanna hai.

### Example 3: 400 V aur 230 V ka rishta

**Given:** Do live taaron ke beech lagbhag 400 V. Teen-phase ka ek tathya (bina saabit kiye): do live taaron ke beech ka voltage live aur neutral ke beech wale voltage ka √3 = 1.732 guna hota hai.
**Find:** Live aur neutral ke beech ka voltage.
**Formula:** V_phase–N = V_phase–phase ÷ √3.
**Substitution:** V = 400 V ÷ 1.732 = 230.9 V.
**Answer:** Lagbhag 230 V, jo ghar ke socket ka voltage hai.
**Jaanch:** 230.9 V × 1.732 = 400.0 V ✅

(Ye ek-line ka tathya hai; teen-phase mein √3 kyun aata hai, ye aage ki class mein aayega.)

## Part E: Meter: kitni bijli kharch hui?

Ghar ke entrance par **energy meter** hai (Class 9). Wo **kWh (units)** mein ginti karta hai, aur 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J. Purane **disc** wale meter mein ek chakri ghoomti hai jo jitni zyada power, utni tez ghoomti hai (iska raaz bhi Chapter 9 mein); **digital** meter number dikhata hai; **smart meter** wahi digital meter hai jo reading company ko door se bhej bhi sakta hai; **prepaid meter** mein pehle recharge karte hain (kai jagah balance khatam hone par bijli rok di jaati hai).

Reading padhne ka tareeka: **ab ki reading − pichhli reading = is dauran ke units**.

```
  Pichhla reading:   1 8 3 6 4   kWh
  Is mahine ka:      1 8 6 0 1   kWh
                     ---------
  Farak:                 2 3 7   kWh = 237 units
```

> **Figure 5.4** – Meter ki do readings ka farak: is baar ki reading mein se pichhli reading ghatao.

### Example 4: Reading aur slab wala bill

**Given:** Pichhle mahine ki reading 18364 units aur is mahine ki 18601 units. Tariff (sirf is sawaal ke liye maan liya): pehle 100 units ₹3.40 per unit, usse upar ₹5.10 per unit. Fixed charge nahi jodte.
**Find:** Units, aur bill.
**Formula:** units = ab − pichhli; bill = (pehle 100) × ₹3.40 + (baaki) × ₹5.10.
**Substitution:** units = 18601 − 18364 = 237 units. Pehle 100 units: 100 × ₹3.40 = ₹340. Baaki: 237 − 100 = 137 units, 137 × ₹5.10 = ₹698.70. Bill = ₹340 + ₹698.70 = ₹1038.70.
**Answer:** 237 units aur ₹1038.70.
**Jaanch:** 100 + 137 = 237 ✅; ₹340 + ₹698.70 = ₹1038.70 ✅

### Ghar par adult ke saath: meter ki reading (sirf dekho)

Bade ke saath meter ke paas jao (meter ke dabbe ko **chhoona nahi**, kholna nahi). Aaj shaam ki reading likho aur do din baad usi samay ki dobara likho. Farak nikalo: do din ke units. Phir 2 se bhaag do: roz ke units. Bade se poochho ki is dauran ghar mein kya zyada chala.

**Net metering** (recall): chhat ke panel ki bachi bijli grid mein jaati hai, to **net** units = jitna liya − jitna diya ([sources-of-energy-part4.md](../sources-of-energy/sources-of-energy-part4.md)).

## Part F: Bijli chali gayi: load shedding aur inverter

Rutuja ne poochha: "Mama, aaj bijli kyun gayi?" Mama ne kaaran ginaye: line par kisi ped ka girna, kisi taar ya transformer mein kharabi, mausam, ya maintenance ka kaam. Kai baar network ki maang supply se zyada ho jaati hai to company kuch ilaakon ki bijli baari-baari se kaat deti hai: **load shedding**. (Ye hamesha nahi hota, aur jagah par nirbhar hai.)

Kai ghar **inverter** (battery wali) rakhte hain. Dhyan do: ghar ke board se inverter ka judna **electrician ka kaam** hai, aur uske beech **changeover switch** zaroori hai.

```
  Bijli company ki line --> [ Meter ] ----+
                                          v
                                 +------------------+
                                 :  changeover      : -----> Ghar ka board
                                 :  switch          :
                                 +------------------+
                                          :
  Inverter + battery ---------------------+
```

> **Figure 5.5** – Changeover switch ghar ke board ko ek samay mein sirf ek hi source se jodta hai: company ki line (meter se hokar) ya inverter. Wo dono ko ek saath nahi jodta.

**Kyun zaroori hai?** Agar inverter ki bijli bina changeover ke ghar ke taaron se hote hue gali ki line mein chali jaye, to jis line ko lineman ne band samajhkar chhua, wo ghar ki bijli se **zinda** ho sakti hai: lineman ke liye jaan ka khatra. Isliye ye kaam sirf electrician ka hai: student sirf itna jaane.

## Wapas khambe ke paas

Ab Rutuja ke saare sawaalon ka jawab hai. Roshni **bijli-ghar** se aati hai, jo generator ke zariye lagbhag 11 kV par banti hai. Voltage **badhaakar** (lagbhag 132 se 765 kV) lambi lines mein bheji jaati hai, taaki current kam ho aur I² R loss bahut kam. Substation se gali tak lagbhag 11 kV, aur **pole transformer** use **415 V teen-phase** (lagbhag) aur ghar ke liye **230 V** (lagbhag) bana deta hai. Isliye khambon ke taar motey aur ooncha voltage waale, par ghar ke taar patle.

Rutuja ne ek line likhi: "Jitna oonchha voltage, utna chhota current, utna kam I² R." Mama ne kaha, "Yahi poore grid ka ek-line saar hai."

---

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Line par bahut ooncha voltage hai, to ghar mein bhi wahi voltage aata hoga."** Demonstration: Rutuja ne socha ki 765 kV ki lagbhag line se ghar ki bijli seedhi aati hai, to socket mein bhi 765 kV aata hoga. Par 765 000 V ÷ 230 V = 3326 guna: itne voltage par ghar ka bulb ek pal mein jal jaata aur insaan ke liye jaan ka khatra hota. Raaste mein **substation aur pole transformer** voltage ghatate hain (765 kV, ya 400 kV, se 11 kV, phir 415 V aur 230 V). Ghar ka voltage **ghar ke liye tay** hota hai: 230 V.

**Galti 2: "Bijli ki line mein bijli koi loss nahi hoti."** Demonstration: Mama ne ek line ka udaharan diya: V = 132 kV, I = 50 A, R = 3 Ω. Bheji gayi power P = V I = 132 000 V × 50 A = 6 600 000 W = 6.6 MW. Line loss = I² R = (50 A)² × 3 Ω = 2500 A² × 3 Ω = 7500 W = 7.5 kW. Ye P ka 7500 ÷ 6 600 000 × 100 = 0.114 percent hai: bahut kam, par **zero nahi**, aur ye din-raat chalti hai: 24 ghante mein 7.5 kW × 24 h = 180 kWh. Isliye loss ko **kam** kiya jaata hai (oonchha voltage, mota taar), khatam nahi.

## Quick Recap

- Bijli ka safar: **bijli-ghar** (lagbhag 11 kV) → **step-up transformer** → **transmission line** (132, 220, 400, 765 kV lagbhag) → **grid substation** (step-down) → **distribution line** (lagbhag 11 kV) → **pole transformer** → ghar (230 V).
- Ek hi power P = V I ke liye V badhao to I = P ÷ V ghatta hai, aur **line loss I² R = P² R ÷ V²**: voltage 10 guna to loss 1 ÷ 100.
- Ooncha voltage hamesha aur oonchha nahi: tower, insulator aur kharcha seema tay karte hain.
- Transmission AC mein isliye ki **transformer** AC voltage badhata-ghatata hai (raaz Chapter 9).
- Grid lagbhag **50 Hz**: current 1 second mein lagbhag 100 baar disha badalta hai.
- Pole transformer ke output par **teen live (R, Y, B) aur ek neutral**; do live ke beech lagbhag 400 V, live aur neutral ke beech lagbhag 230 V (400 ÷ √3).
- Meter: **ab − pichhli reading = units**; smart aur prepaid meter naam bas; net metering ka hisaab net units.
- **Load shedding** supply-maang ka antar; **changeover switch** ke bina inverter ghar mein mat jodna (electrician ka kaam).
- Khambon, transformer aur taaron ke paas kabhi nahi jaana: sirf door se dekhna.

## Practice Questions

(Use 230 V as the household supply. Treat the line resistance as constant and ignore the voltage drop along the line.)

1. A power of 6 MW is sent over a line of total resistance 12 Ω at 11 kV. Find the current, the line loss and the percentage of the power lost.
2. The same 6 MW goes through the same 12 Ω line at 110 kV. Find the current and the line loss. By what factor is the loss smaller than in Question 1?
3. A factory needs 25 MW through a line of resistance 40 Ω at 220 kV. Find the current, the loss in kW and the percentage loss.
4. Use the same 25 MW and 40 Ω as in Question 3, but suppose the line is run at 11 kV. Find the loss the formula gives and compare it with 25 MW. What does this tell you about sending this power at 11 kV?
5. An energy meter reads 07426 units at the start of a month and 07688 units at the end. The tariff is ₹4.75 per unit plus a fixed charge of ₹85. Find the units used and the total bill.
6. A prepaid meter has a balance of ₹372 and the tariff is ₹6.20 per unit. A water pump of 0.8 kW runs for 2.5 hours every day. How many units can the balance buy, and for how many days will it last if the pump is the only load?
7. Between two live wires of a three-phase supply the voltage is about 400 V. Starting from 230 V between a live wire and the neutral, check by calculation that 230 V is consistent with 400 V. Then say which voltage a single-phase house gets and which a factory motor between two live wires gets.
8. The mains frequency is 50 Hz. How many times does the current reverse direction in 3 minutes? In a country with 60 Hz, how many times does it reverse in one second, and what is the time of one cycle?
9. State True or False with a reason. (a) The 400 kV transmission line supplies a house socket directly. (b) For the same power, sending at a higher voltage means a smaller current. (c) If the voltage on a line is doubled, the line loss becomes half. (d) A smart meter can send the reading to the company.
10. Arrange in the correct order: pole transformer, generator, grid substation, step-up transformer, house, transmission line, distribution line.
11. For the same power and the same line, find the factor by which the line loss changes when the transmission voltage is raised from 11 kV to 220 kV.
12. A line carries 6 MW over a resistance of 12 Ω. The company wants the loss to be at most 3 percent of the power sent. Find the largest allowed current and the smallest voltage needed.
13. In two lines each, explain (a) why a house inverter must go through a changeover switch fitted by an electrician, and (b) why a lineman, and not a student, works on a pole transformer.

### Answers

1. **Given:** P = 6 MW = 6 000 000 W, R = 12 Ω, V = 11 kV = 11 000 V. **Find:** I, loss, percent. **Formula:** I = P ÷ V; loss = I² R. **Substitution:** I = 6 000 000 W ÷ 11 000 V = **545.5 A**; loss = (545.5 A)² × 12 Ω = 297 520 A² × 12 Ω = 3 570 000 W ≈ **3.57 MW**; percent = 3.57 MW ÷ 6 MW × 100 = **59.5 percent**. **Jaanch:** 545.5 A × 11 000 V = 6.0 MW ✅. (Ye bahut bada loss hai: asli mein itne kam voltage par itni power is line se bheji hi nahi jaati.)
2. **Substitution:** I = 6 000 000 W ÷ 110 000 V = **54.55 A**; loss = (54.55 A)² × 12 Ω = 2975 A² × 12 Ω = 35 700 W ≈ **35.7 kW**. Factor = 3 570 000 ÷ 35 700 = **100**. **Jaanch:** voltage 110 ÷ 11 = 10 guna, to loss 10² = 100 guna kam ✅.
3. I = 25 000 000 W ÷ 220 000 V = **113.6 A**; loss = (113.6 A)² × 40 Ω = 12 913 A² × 40 Ω = 516 500 W ≈ **516.5 kW**; percent = 516.5 kW ÷ 25 000 kW × 100 = **2.07 percent**. **Jaanch:** 113.6 A × 220 000 V = 25.0 MW ✅.
4. I = 25 000 000 W ÷ 11 000 V = 2272.7 A; loss = (2272.7 A)² × 40 Ω = 5 165 289 A² × 40 Ω = 206 600 000 W ≈ 206.6 MW. Ye 25 MW se bahut zyada hai: **826 percent**. Iska matlab: 11 kV par itni power us line se bheji hi nahi ja sakti (line ke resistance par hi bahut zyada voltage gir jaata; 2272.7 A × 40 Ω = 90 909 V, jo 11 000 V se bahut zyada hai). Isliye lambi, badi power ke liye ooncha voltage zaroori hai. **Jaanch:** 90 909 V ÷ 11 000 V = 8.26 ✅ (wahi 826 percent ka sanket).
5. Units = 7688 − 7426 = **262 units**. Energy bill = 262 × ₹4.75 = ₹1244.50; fixed charge ₹85; total = ₹1244.50 + ₹85 = **₹1329.50**. **Jaanch:** 262 × 4.75 = 1244.5 ✅; 7426 + 262 = 7688 ✅.
6. Units = ₹372 ÷ ₹6.20 per unit = **60 units**. Pump roz = 0.8 kW × 2.5 h = 2 kWh. Din = 60 kWh ÷ 2 kWh per din = **30 din**. **Jaanch:** 30 × 2 kWh × ₹6.20 = ₹372 ✅.
7. 400 ÷ √3 = 400 ÷ 1.732 = 230.9 V ≈ 230 V ✅ (ulta: 230 × 1.732 = 398.4 V ≈ 400 V; fark 400 − 398.4 = 1.6 V, lagbhag 0.4 percent). Single-phase ghar ko ek live aur neutral se **230 V** (lagbhag); factory motor ko do live taaron ke beech **400 V** (lagbhag).
8. 3 min = 180 s. Reversals = 100 per s × 180 s = **18 000**. 60 Hz par: 2 × 60 = **120 baar per second**, ek cycle ka samay T = 1 ÷ 60 Hz = **0.0167 s**. **Jaanch:** 100 × 180 = 18 000 ✅; 60 cycle × 0.0167 s = 1.0 s ✅.
9. (a) **False** ❌: 400 kV line pehle substation aur pole transformer se guzarti hai, tab 230 V ghar tak aata hai. (b) **True** ✅: P = V I, V badhe to I = P ÷ V ghatta hai. (c) **False** ❌: loss V² ke ulte anupaat mein hota hai, to voltage double hone par loss **ek-chauthai (1 ÷ 4)**. (d) **True** ✅: smart meter reading door se bhej sakta hai.
10. **generator → step-up transformer → transmission line → grid substation → distribution line → pole transformer → house.**
11. Factor = (220 ÷ 11)² = 20² = **400 guna kam** (loss 1 ÷ 400 ho jaata hai). **Jaanch:** V ka anupaat 20 hai, to I ek-bees-va (1 ÷ 20) aur loss 1 ÷ 20² = 1 ÷ 400 ✅. (Q1 aur Q2 mein anupaat 10 tha aur factor 100 mila tha: wahi niyam.)
12. **Given:** P = 6 MW = 6 000 000 W, R = 12 Ω, loss ≤ 3 percent. **Formula:** loss = I² R; V = P ÷ I. **Substitution:** Loss_max = 0.03 × 6 000 000 W = 180 000 W; I² = 180 000 W ÷ 12 Ω = 15 000 A²; I = **122.5 A**; V = 6 000 000 W ÷ 122.5 A = 48 980 V ≈ **49 kV**. **Jaanch:** (122.5 A)² × 12 Ω = 15 000 × 12 = 180 000 W ✅. Isliye 11 kV (Q1) bahut kam hai, 110 kV (Q2) kaafi hai.
13. (a) Changeover switch ghar ko ek samay mein ek hi source se jodta hai, to inverter ki bijli gali ki line mein wapas nahi ja sakti, jise lineman band samajhkar kaam kar raha ho. Wiring electrician ka kaam hai. (b) Transformer aur line par lagbhag 11 kV (ya zyada) hota hai, jo jaan le sakta hai; lineman ko training, helmet, dastaane aur line band karne ke niyam aate hain, student ko door se dekhna hai.
