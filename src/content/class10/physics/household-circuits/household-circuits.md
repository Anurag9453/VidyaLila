## Oturkar Mama ke ghar ka fuse box

Baarish ka mausam tha. Trupti chhutti mein apne Mama, Mr. Oturkar, ke purane ghar aayi hui thi. Shaam ko itni tez baarish hui ki poori gali ki bijli chali gayi. Mama ne diye aur torch nikaale, aur dono baramde mein baith kar baarish dekhne lage.

Ghanta bhar baad achanak gali ki bijli wapas aayi, aur saath hi ek tez **tak!** ki awaaz. Padosiyon ke ghar roshan ho gaye, par Mama ke ghar mein andhera hi raha. "Lagta hai apna fuse ud gaya," Mama bole. Unhone padosi electrician ko bulaya. Electrician ne sabse pehle ghar ka **main switch band kiya**, tab jaakar purane fuse box ka dhakkan khola. Mama aur Trupti torch ki roshni mein ek kadam peeche khade rahe: dekhne ki ijazat thi, chhoone ki nahi.

Andar ek cheeni-mitti (porcelain) ka carrier tha, jis par do screw lage the, aur unke beech ek bahut **patla** taar, jo beech se kaala hokar toot chuka tha. Paas hi ek gatte par fuse wire ka chhota roll rakha tha. Trupti hairaan ho gayi. "Mama, poore ghar ki bijli is ek baal jaise patle taar se guzarti hai? Ye to sabse kamzor cheez hai! Isse to mota taar lagana chahiye tha, taaki wo kabhi na tute!"

Electrician muskuraya, "Beti, isi kamzori mein iski taaqat hai. Mota taar lagaya, to fuse hi nahi raha." Mama ne kaha, "Chalo, kal din mein samjhte hain: patla taar poore ghar ko kaise bachata hai, aur aajkal ke MCB mein isse bhi achha kya hai."

## Part A: Fuse jaan-boojh kar kamzor kadi hai

Fuse ka ek hi idea hai: **circuit mein sabse kamzor kadi usi ko banao, jo sabse pehle toote**, taaki baaki wires, switch aur appliance bache rahein. Jaise paper ki chain mein sabse patli kadi sabse pehle fat-ti hai.

```
      Live (laal ya bhura) taar

    +---------------[ FUSE ]--------[ SWITCH ]--------+
    :                                                 :
    :             fuse: live mein, series             :
    :                                                 :
[ 230 V ]                                        [ HEATER ]
    :                                                 :
    :                                                 :
    :                                                 :
    +-------------------------------------------------+

      Neutral (kaala ya neela) taar
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Fuse ki sahi jagah: live taar mein, switch ke saath series mein, aur appliance ke baad neutral taar se wapsi: poora closed loop. Fuse udne par live taar kat jaata hai aur appliance supply se alag ho jaata hai.

```
  +--------------------------------------------------+
  :   porcelain (cheeni-mitti) ka carrier            :
  :                                                  :
  :   (o)=========  patla fuse wire  =========(o)    :
  :                                                  :
  :   screw                                screw     :
  :                                                  :
  +--------------------------------------------------+
  Live andar aati hai ->                -> Live baahar jaati hai
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Rewirable fuse ka block diagram: cheeni-mitti ke carrier mein do screw, aur unke beech ek patla fuse element. Current is patle taar se guzar kar hi appliance tak jaata hai (carrier aur screw ka sirf naksha hai: ghar par kabhi mat kholna).

### Patla taar sabse pehle kyun garm hota hai?

Fuse element aur baaki taar **series mein** hain, to dono mein **wahi current** I guzarta hai. Garmi P = I² R se milti hai, aur R = ρ l ÷ A hota hai (pichhle chapter mein dekha). Patle taar ka area A chhota hai, to R **zyada**, to usi current par garmi zyada. Isliye fuse element hi sabse pehle garm hota hai. Dusri baat: uska **melting point kam** hai, lagbhag **183 °C** (tin-lead alloy, ek illustration: aaj ke fuse ke element alag cheezon ke ban sakte hain).

Kitni garmi chahiye usse pighlane ke liye? Class 9 ka Q = m c ΔT. Is chapter mein hum tin-lead fuse taar ke liye ye maante hain: c lagbhag **200 J/kg °C**, melting point lagbhag **183 °C**, kamre ka temperature lagbhag **30 °C**. Kaam ka tareeka: current I × R × t se jitni garmi **I² R t** banti hai, use Q ke barabar rakho. (Hum do baatein chhod rahe hain: pighalne ke liye thodi aur garmi alag se lagti hai, aur kuch garmi bahar nikal jaati hai. Isliye ye **sabse tezi se** udne ka samay hai: asli samay thoda zyada hota hai.)

### Example 1: Short circuit mein fuse ka udna

**Given:** Fuse taar ke tukde ka mass m = 30 mg = 3.0 × 10⁻⁵ kg, taar ka resistance R = 0.10 Ω (ek illustration), c lagbhag 200 J/kg °C, melting point lagbhag 183 °C, kamre ka temperature 30 °C, fault current I = 40 A.
**Find:** Pighlane tak ki garmi Q aur tab tak ka samay t (garmi bahar jaana chhodkar).
**Formula:** ΔT = T_melt − T_room; Q = m c ΔT; I² R t = Q, to t = Q ÷ (I² R).
**Substitution:** ΔT = 183 °C − 30 °C = 153 °C. Q = 3.0 × 10⁻⁵ kg × 200 J/kg °C × 153 °C = 0.918 J. Garmi ki power P = I² R = (40 A)² × 0.10 Ω = 1600 A² × 0.10 Ω = 160 W. t = 0.918 J ÷ 160 W = 0.00574 s.
**Answer:** Q = 0.918 J; t lagbhag 5.74 ms.
**Jaanch:** 160 W × 0.0057375 s = 0.918 J ✅. Units: J ÷ W = s ✅

Ek aur baat dekho: current jitna zyada, samay utna kam. Wahi taar, alag alag fault current (garmi bahar jaana chhodkar):

| Current I | Garmi ki power I² R | Samay t = 0.918 J ÷ power |
|---|---|---|
| 20 A | 40 W | 22.95 ms |
| 40 A | 160 W | 5.74 ms |
| 80 A | 640 W | 1.43 ms |

```
  20 A  ################  22.95 ms
  40 A  ####              5.74 ms
  80 A  #                 1.43 ms
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Example 1 ke taar ke liye udne ke samay ke bars (ek # lagbhag 1.43 ms). Current double hua, to power chaar guna, to samay chauthai: 16, 4, 1.

Is table ke 20 A se 40 A tak ka sabak: **current 2 guna, samay ek-chauthai** (kyunki I² mein current ka square aata hai).

**Ek zaroori imaandari:** isi taar ko 5 A par rakho to P = (5 A)² × 0.10 Ω = 2.5 W, aur ye model kehta hai 0.37 s mein pighal jayega. Par asli 5 A ka fuse to hamesha chalta hai! Wajah: chhote current par taar ki garmi **bahar nikalti rehti hai** (screw aur hawa mein), aur jab garmi ki aamdani aur nikaasi barabar ho jaati hai, taar ka temperature 183 °C se neeche hi ruk jaata hai. **Fuse rating wahi current hai jahan ye santulan melting point se neeche rehta hai.** Bade current par garmi itni tezi se banti hai ki bahar nikalne ka mauka hi nahi milta, aur taar pighal jaata hai.

### Example 2: Mota fuse taar kyun nahi?

**Given:** Example 1 ka wahi taar (30 mg, 0.10 Ω, 40 A), par ab usi alloy aur usi length ka taar jiska **diameter double** hai.
**Find:** Naye taar ka mass, R aur udne ka samay.
**Formula:** Diameter double, to area 4 guna (A ∝ d²); m ∝ A (length same), R = ρ l ÷ A ∝ 1 ÷ A; t = m c ΔT ÷ (I² R).
**Substitution:** m' = 4 × 30 mg = 120 mg = 1.2 × 10⁻⁴ kg. R' = 0.10 Ω ÷ 4 = 0.025 Ω. Q' = 1.2 × 10⁻⁴ kg × 200 J/kg °C × 153 °C = 3.672 J. P' = (40 A)² × 0.025 Ω = 40 W. t' = 3.672 J ÷ 40 W = 0.0918 s.
**Answer:** t' lagbhag 91.8 ms, jabki patle taar ka 5.74 ms.
**Jaanch:** 91.8 ms ÷ 5.74 ms = 16 ✅ (mass 4 guna, power ka chauthai hissa: 4 × 4 = 16).

Matlab mota taar **16 guna der** se udta hai. Aur agar usse bhi 5.74 ms mein udwana ho, to current **4 guna** (160 A) chahiye. Ye ab fuse nahi raha: wo ek bada, dheeth fuse ban gaya, aur ghar ki patli wiring ko bachane wala koi nahi bacha. Isliye "mota taar kabhi nahi".

**Copper kyun nahi?** Ghar ke taar wala copper ka melting point tin-lead se kai guna zyada hai, aur uska resistivity bahut kam hai (ρ lagbhag 1.7 × 10⁻⁸ Ω m), to wo kam garm hota hai aur pighalta hi nahi. (Kuch cartridge fuse ke element copper ya chaandi ke bhi hote hain, par unki shakal bahut barik aur khaas design ki hoti hai: wo ghar ka aam taar nahi hai.)

### Safe activities

**Sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo):** teacher 12 V ki low-voltage DC supply (jisme current ki limit lagi ho) par, mains par kabhi nahi, tin-lead solder ki patli patti ya foil ki patti jodte hain, jiske beech ek sankari "gardan" hai. Bachhe table se door, safety goggles ke saath dekhte hain. Current badhane par gardan pehle laal hoti hai aur phir tut jaati hai: yahi fuse ka udna hai. **Ek chetavani:** solder mein lead hota hai, jo zehreela hai: sirf teacher chhuta hai, aur baad mein haath dhoye jaate hain.

**Ghar par adult ke saath (paper activity):** kaagaz ki 6 patti lekar chain banao, par ek kadi ke beech ek sankari gardan rakho. Chain ko dono sire se halke se kheencho: wo sankari gardan par hi tutegi. Fuse bhi isi tarah series circuit ki sabse kamzor kadi hai: bas yahan kamzori ka matlab hai sabse patla hissa, jo sabse zyada garm hota hai.

## Part B: Rating ka matlab, aur samay ka rishta

**Fuse rating** wo current hai jo fuse bina pighle **lagataar** jhel leta hai. Rating se thoda upar current par wo turant nahi udta: pehle garm hota hai, aur jitna zyada current, utna kam samay. Neeche ek **illustration table** hai (lagbhag aur hedged: asli fuse ka naksha uske maker ke datasheet mein aata hai):

| Current (rating ka kitna guna) | Fuse kitni der mein udta hai (lagbhag) |
|---|---|
| 1.0 guna | Nahi udta (lagataar chalta hai) |
| 1.5 guna | Kai minute se lekar ghanta |
| 2 guna | Kuch second se ek-do minute |
| 4 guna | Lagbhag ek second ya usse kam |
| 10 guna | Second ke kuch sau-wen hisse |

Isi se do taraf ki sahi rating samajh aati hai: rating **normal current se upar** ho (warna normal kaam mein hi udega), par **bahut upar nahi** (warna asli fault par bhi nahi udega).

### Example 3: Plug ka cartridge fuse chunna

**Given:** Plug mein 5 A ya 13 A ka cartridge fuse lag sakta hai. Do appliances: toaster 805 W aur room heater 2.185 kW, supply 230 V.
**Find:** Har ka normal current aur kaun sa fuse.
**Formula:** I = P ÷ V; fuse rating ≥ I, aur thodi margin.
**Substitution:** Toaster: I = 805 W ÷ 230 V = 3.5 A. Heater: I = 2185 W ÷ 230 V = 9.5 A.
**Answer:** Toaster ke liye **5 A** (3.5 A < 5 A, 1.5 A margin). Heater ke liye **13 A** (9.5 A < 13 A, 3.5 A margin; 5 A to heater ke chalte hi udta).
**Jaanch:** 3.5 A × 230 V = 805 W ✅. 9.5 A × 230 V = 2185 W ✅. Toaster par 13 A kyun nahi? Agar toaster mein fault se current 8 A ho jaaye, 13 A ka fuse use "normal" maanega, aur toaster ki patli lead garm hogi: chhote appliance ko chhoti rating ka fuse hi bachata hai ✅

### Example 4: Galat fuse ho to kya hoga?

**Given:** Wahi 2.185 kW heater ko galti se 5 A ke fuse wale plug mein lagaya, 230 V.
**Find:** Fuse kya karega?
**Formula:** I = P ÷ V; multiple = I ÷ rating; upar ki table padho.
**Substitution:** I = 2185 W ÷ 230 V = 9.5 A. Multiple = 9.5 A ÷ 5 A = 1.9.
**Answer:** Current rating ka lagbhag 2 guna (1.9) hai, table ke hisaab se fuse **kuch second se ek-do minute** mein udega: ghar ka wiring bach gaya, par heater chalega hi nahi.
**Jaanch:** 1.9 × 5 A = 9.5 A ✅. Heat ki dar ka bhi sach: rating par I² R ka 25 A² hai, aur 9.5 A par 90.25 A²: lagbhag 3.6 guna ✅ (isliye samay itna kam).

## Part D: MCB: bina jale band hone wala chowkidaar

MCB mein fuse ka jalne wala taar nahi hota. Uski jagah ek **switch jaisa mechanism** hota hai: andar contacts ek **latch** se band rakhe jaate hain, aur ek spring unhe kholne ke liye taiyaar rehti hai. Latch chhoote to spring contacts khol deti hai: MCB **trip** ho jaata hai. Current ek hi raaste se guzarta hai, aur do cheezein latch ko chhudwa sakti hain, **do alag halaat** ke liye:

```
Live IN ---[ bimetal ]---[ coil ]---( contacts )---> Live OUT
             :              :             :
             :              :             :
             :              :             :
             v              v             :
          +----------------------+        :
          :  LATCH (atka hua)    :--spring+
          +----------------------+

  bimetal: dheeme overload mein jhukta hai, latch chhodta hai
  coil: bahut bade current par magnet ban kar latch kheenchti hai
  latch chhoota, spring ne contacts khole: circuit khula (trip)

```

> **Figure 1.4** – MCB ke andar ka block diagram (sirf function): live current bimetal strip aur coil se hoke contacts tak jaata hai. Dheeme overload par bimetal, bade current par coil latch chhudwa deti hai, aur spring contacts khol deti hai.

1. **Dheeme overload ke liye: bimetal strip.** Do dhaatuein jodkar banayi patti garm hone par jhukti hai (Class 9 mein dekha). Current zyada hua, to patti garm hui, jhuk kar latch ko dhakela. Isme **kuch second se kuch minute** lagte hain, jaise fuse ke table mein (lagbhag).
2. **Achanak short circuit ke liye: coil (electromagnet).** Bahut bada current coil se guzre, to coil ek magnet ki tarah latch ko turant kheench leti hai: second ke bahut chhote hisse mein (lagbhag). Magnet kaise banta hai: ye Chapter 9 (Electromagnetism) mein aayega, abhi sirf kaam jaano.

Dono ke bina kaam nahi chalta: bimetal garm hone mein waqt leta hai, to bade fault ke liye bahut dheema hai; aur magnet chhote overload par chup rehta hai.

**MCB ki rating** 6 A, 10 A, 16 A, 32 A (aur main ke liye 63 A) hoti hai, jo uska lagataar jhelne wala current hai. Uske saath ek **curve** B, C ya D likha hota hai (sirf naam): wo batata hai ki magnet kitne guna current par kaam karna shuru kare (B sabse sensitive, D sabse kam). Ghar ke liye curve electrician chunta hai.

**Reset:** fault door hone par handle wapas ON kar sakte hain. Par agar fault abhi bhi hai, to MCB **dobara trip** ho jaayega: handle ko zabardasti pakadkar rakhna khatarnaak hai. Baar baar trip ho to electrician ko bulao.

**Ghar par adult ke saath (padhna):** apne ghar ke MCB board par bahar se dikhne wale label dekho: 6, 10, 16, 32 jaisi rating aur B ya C. Board ka dhakkan **mat kholo**, kisi MCB ko **mat chhuo**: sirf padho, aur copy mein likho.

## Part E: MCB kaise chunte hain: do seemaayein

Ek MCB do kaam ek saath karta hai. Uski rating:

- **Normal current se upar** honi chahiye, warna roz trip hoga.
- **Taar ki safe current se upar nahi** honi chahiye, warna taar overload mein garm hota rahega aur MCB chup rahega.

(Taar ki safe current ki table Topic 3 mein hai. Is topic mein ye maan lo: 1.5 mm² lagbhag 10 A, 2.5 mm² lagbhag 16 A, 4 mm² lagbhag 24 A, aur taar ke packet par likhi rating dekho.)

### Example 5: Geyser circuit

**Given:** Ek circuit ka 2.5 mm² ka taar (safe current lagbhag 16 A); uska normal load 1.7 kW, supply 230 V; MCB vikalp 6 A, 10 A, 16 A, 32 A.
**Find:** Normal current aur sahi MCB.
**Formula:** I = P ÷ V; I_normal < MCB rating ≤ taar ki safe current.
**Substitution:** I = 1700 W ÷ 230 V = 7.39 A. 6 A < 7.39 A (roz trip hoga). 10 A: 7.39 A < 10 A ≤ 16 A (theek). 16 A: 7.39 A < 16 A ≤ 16 A (seema ke barabar, theek). 32 A: 32 A > 16 A (taar ki seema se upar).
**Answer:** 10 A ya 16 A dono dono seemaayein poori karte hain. **10 A** behtar, kyunki wo chhote overload par pehle bachata hai.
**Jaanch:** 1700 W ÷ 230 V = 7.39 A ✅. 10 A tak current 7.39 A se bada hai, aur 10 A taar ki 16 A seema se neeche ✅

```
           normal 7.4 A
              N
+-----------+-------+-----------+-------------------------------+--
0           6       10          16                              32
                  MCB 10 A
                        wire ki safe limit 16 A
         6 A: bahut chhota                        32 A: wire se upar

```

> **Figure 1.5** – Example 5 ki sankhya-rekha (1 chinh lagbhag 0.5 A): normal current 7.4 A, uske upar 10 A ka MCB, aur uske upar taar ki 16 A ki seema. 6 A bahut chhota hai, 32 A taar ki seema se bahar.

### Example 6: Jab koi MCB nahi baithta

**Given:** 4 mm² ka taar (safe current lagbhag 24 A); normal load 4.83 kW; vikalp 6 A, 10 A, 16 A, 32 A.
**Find:** Kaun sa MCB?
**Formula:** I = P ÷ V; I_normal < rating ≤ taar ki safe current.
**Substitution:** I = 4830 W ÷ 230 V = 21.0 A. 16 A: 16 A < 21.0 A (roz trip). 32 A: 32 A > 24 A (taar ki seema se upar).
**Answer:** Is list mein koi MCB dono seemaayein poori nahi karta. Sahi hal: load ko **do circuit mein baant do** (aur ratings bhi milti hain: electrician bata dega). **32 A** laga dena, bas "trip na ho" ke liye, galat hal hai.
**Jaanch:** 21.0 A × 230 V = 4830 W ✅. 16 A < 21.0 A aur 32 A > 24 A: dono rule toot-te hain ✅ (is sawal ka asli sabak Topic 3 mein: circuits baantna).

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Zyada rating ka fuse zyada safe hai, wo kabhi udta hi nahi."**
Demonstration: 1.5 mm² ke taar ki safe current lagbhag 10 A hai. Kisi ne baar baar fuse udne se tang aakar 32 A ka fuse (ya mota taar) laga diya, aur circuit par 3.91 kW ka load chalaya. I = 3910 W ÷ 230 V = 17.0 A. Taar ke liye ye safe current ka 17.0 ÷ 10 = 1.7 guna hai, to heat ki dar (I² ka ratio) (17.0 ÷ 10)² = 2.89 guna. 32 A ka fuse 17.0 A ko "normal" maan kar chup raha, aur taar deewar ke andar 2.89 guna garm hota raha: wahin aag lag sakti hai, jahan koi dekh nahi sakta. Sahi 10 A fuse 1.7 guna par (table ke hisaab se kuch minute ke aas paas) udkar saath mein bata deta ki load zyada hai. **Fuse udna theek hai: wajah dhoondho (load kam karo ya electrician se milo), fuse mat badlo.**

**Galti 2: "Fuse mujhe bijli ke jhatke se bachata hai."**
Demonstration: koi insaan kharab appliance se 60 mA ka current paata hai (60 mA = 0.060 A). Ye shock ke liye bahut hai (lagbhag 30 mA ya usse zyada seene ke aar-paar khatarnaak ho sakta hai: hedged, Topic 2 mein). Par 10 A ka fuse 0.060 A ko dekhta hi nahi: 10 A ÷ 0.060 A = 166.7, yaani usse 166.7 guna current chahiye tab wo garm hoga. Fuse **taar ki garmi** par kaam karta hai, **sharir ki surakshit seema** par nahi. Sharir ko bachane wale chowkidaar alag hain: unki kahani agle topic mein.

## Quick Recap

- **Fuse = jaan-boojh kar kamzor kadi:** patla, kam melting point (illustration: lagbhag 183 °C) ka taar, live mein, series mein.
- Garmi **I² R t**; pighlane ko **m c ΔT** (hum: c lagbhag 200 J/kg °C, 30 °C se 183 °C). Garmi bahar jaana chhodkar: t = m c ΔT ÷ (I² R). Asli samay thoda zyada.
- Current double, samay lagbhag **chauthai** (garmi bahar nikalne ki jagah ke bina). Fuse rating: wo current jahan garmi ki aamdani = nikaasi, aur taar nahi pighalta.
- **Mota taar** ya **copper** fuse ki jagah nahi: wo der se (ya kabhi nahi) udta hai. Diameter double hua to 16 guna der.
- Fuse ke prakaar: rewirable, cartridge, HRC, blade (rang se rating).
- **MCB:** latch aur spring; **bimetal** dheeme overload ke liye, **coil (electromagnet)** achanak short circuit ke liye; reset ho sakta hai; rating 6, 10, 16, 32 A.
- MCB chunna: **normal current < rating ≤ taar ki safe current.**
- Fuse aur MCB **wire ko** bachate hain, **insaan ko** shock se nahi.

## Practice Questions

(Use 230 V as the supply. For fuse-heating questions use c = 200 J/kg °C, melting point 183 °C and room temperature 30 °C, ignore heat loss and the extra heat needed to actually melt. Safe currents: 1.5 mm² about 10 A, 2.5 mm² about 16 A, 4 mm² about 24 A.)

1. A fuse wire piece of mass 18 mg and resistance 0.25 Ω carries a fault current of 15 A. Find the heat needed to raise it from 30 °C to its melting point and the time it takes to reach it.
2. A fuse wire of mass 50 mg reaches its melting point in 8.5 ms when 30 A passes through it. Find its resistance.
3. A thin fuse wire of a certain alloy melts in 4.0 ms at a given current. A wire of the same alloy and length, but with 1.5 times the diameter, carries the same current. Find the new time. By what factor must the current rise to melt it in the original time?
4. A plug can hold a 5 A or a 13 A cartridge fuse. Which would you choose for (a) a 460 W appliance, (b) a 1.61 kW appliance, (c) a 2.53 kW appliance? Give the current of each.
5. An illustration table for a 13 A cartridge fuse says: at 13 A it never blows; at 19.5 A it blows in about 30 min; at 26 A in about 40 s; at 52 A in about 0.80 s; at 130 A in about 0.020 s. (a) A faulty heater draws 26 A: how many times the rating is this, and how long before the fuse blows? (b) A short circuit draws 130 A: how long? (c) Compare the rate of heating at 26 A with that at 13 A.
6. Choose the MCB out of 6 A, 10 A, 16 A, 32 A, or say none fits, for each circuit: (a) 10 A wire, load 1.012 kW; (b) 16 A wire, load 1.84 kW; (c) 24 A wire, load 3.22 kW; (d) 10 A wire, load 2.76 kW. Give the normal current in each case.
7. A circuit with a wire of safe current 16 A has a 32 A MCB. A 4.6 kW load is run on it. Find the current. Will the MCB trip? How much more current and heating is the wire carrying compared with its safe limit?
8. Explain which part of an MCB handles a slow overload and which part handles a sudden short circuit. Why would the first part alone be too slow for a short circuit?
9. True or false, with a reason: (a) A 5 A fuse blows the instant the current reaches 5.1 A. (b) An MCB that trips can be reset once the fault is cleared. (c) A fuse protects a person from electric shock. (d) Ordinary copper wire is a good fuse wire because it conducts well. (e) An MCB's rating is the current it can carry continuously.
10. A fuse element of mass 25 mg and resistance 0.040 Ω carries a short-circuit current of 120 A. Find the time to reach melting point.
11. In a car, a circuit has a normal current of 6.5 A. The blade fuses available are 5 A tan, 10 A red and 15 A blue. Which would you choose and why?
12. A fuse wire carries its rated current, and then a fault makes the current three times the rating. By what factor does the rate of heat production rise? Why does the real blowing time not fall by exactly that factor?

### Answers

1. **Given:** m = 18 mg = 1.8 × 10⁻⁵ kg, R = 0.25 Ω, I = 15 A. **Find:** Q aur t. **Formula:** Q = m c ΔT; t = Q ÷ (I² R). **Substitution:** ΔT = 183 − 30 = 153 °C. Q = 1.8 × 10⁻⁵ kg × 200 J/kg °C × 153 °C = **0.5508 J**. P = (15 A)² × 0.25 Ω = 56.25 W. t = 0.5508 J ÷ 56.25 W = 0.00979 s = **9.79 ms** (lagbhag). **Jaanch:** 56.25 W × 0.009792 s = 0.5508 J ✅

2. **Given:** m = 50 mg = 5.0 × 10⁻⁵ kg, t = 8.5 ms = 0.0085 s, I = 30 A. **Formula:** Q = m c ΔT; I² R t = Q. **Substitution:** Q = 5.0 × 10⁻⁵ kg × 200 J/kg °C × 153 °C = 1.53 J. P = Q ÷ t = 1.53 J ÷ 0.0085 s = 180 W. R = P ÷ I² = 180 W ÷ 900 A² = **0.20 Ω**. **Jaanch:** 900 A² × 0.20 Ω × 0.0085 s = 1.53 J ✅

3. Area (aur mass) 1.5² = 2.25 guna, R ki 1 ÷ 2.25. t = m c ΔT ÷ (I² R) mein m badhta hai 2.25 guna aur R ghatta hai 2.25 guna, to t **2.25 × 2.25 = 5.0625 guna**: t' = 4.0 ms × 5.0625 = **20.25 ms** (lagbhag 20.3 ms). Wahi time chahiye to P ko 5.0625 guna karo: I² 5.0625 guna, to I **2.25 guna** (√5.0625 = 2.25). **Jaanch:** 5.0625 ÷ 2.25² = 1 ✅

4. (a) I = 460 W ÷ 230 V = **2.0 A**: **5 A**. (b) I = 1610 W ÷ 230 V = **7.0 A**: 5 A turant udta, to **13 A**. (c) I = 2530 W ÷ 230 V = **11.0 A**: **13 A** (margin 2.0 A). **Jaanch:** 2.0 × 230 = 460 ✅, 7.0 × 230 = 1610 ✅, 11.0 × 230 = 2530 ✅

5. (a) 26 A ÷ 13 A = **2 guna**; table: **lagbhag 40 s**. (b) 130 A ÷ 13 A = 10 guna; **lagbhag 0.020 s** (20 ms). (c) Heating ∝ I², to (26 ÷ 13)² = **4 guna**. **Jaanch:** (130 ÷ 13)² = 100 guna, aur samay 40 s se 0.020 s: lagbhag 2000 guna kam, to samay current ke square se bhi tez ghat raha hai ✅ (kyunki bade current par garmi bahar nikalne ka waqt hi nahi milta).

6. (a) I = 1012 W ÷ 230 V = 4.4 A. 6 A: 4.4 A < 6 A ≤ 10 A ✅: **6 A**. (b) I = 1840 W ÷ 230 V = 8.0 A. 10 A: 8.0 < 10 ≤ 16 ✅: **10 A** (16 A bhi seema mein, par 10 A behtar). (c) I = 3220 W ÷ 230 V = 14.0 A. 10 A < 14.0 A (trip). 16 A: 14.0 < 16 ≤ 24 ✅: **16 A**. (d) I = 2760 W ÷ 230 V = 12.0 A. 10 A < 12.0 A (trip). 16 A > 10 A (taar ki seema se upar). **Koi nahi:** load do circuit mein baanto. **Jaanch:** 4.4 × 230 = 1012 ✅, 8.0 × 230 = 1840 ✅, 14.0 × 230 = 3220 ✅, 12.0 × 230 = 2760 ✅

7. I = 4600 W ÷ 230 V = **20.0 A**. 20.0 A < 32 A, to MCB **trip nahi** hoga. Taar ki seema 16 A: 20.0 ÷ 16 = 1.25 guna current (25 percent zyada), aur heating (20.0 ÷ 16)² = **1.5625 guna** (lagbhag 56 percent zyada). Taar overload mein hai aur MCB chup. **Jaanch:** 1.25² = 1.5625 ✅. Sahi hal: 32 A MCB hata kar load do circuit mein baanto.

8. **Bimetal strip** dheeme overload ko sambhalti hai: current se garm hokar jhukti hai aur latch chhudwa deti hai. **Coil (electromagnet)** achanak bade short-circuit current ko sambhalti hai: magnet ki tarah latch ko turant kheench leti hai. Bimetal ko garm hone aur jhukne mein waqt lagta hai (kuch second se minute), jabki short circuit mein current kuch hi ms mein taar ko khatre mein daal deta hai: isliye akela bimetal bahut dheema hota.

9. (a) **False** ❌: samay current badhne ke saath dheere dheere ghat-ta hai; 5.1 A (rating ka 1.02 guna) par lagbhag udta hi nahi ya bahut der mein. (b) **True** ✅: fault door hone par; agar fault ho to dobara trip. (c) **False** ❌: shock ke mA current fuse ko dikhte hi nahi (Galti 2). (d) **False** ❌: copper ka melting point bahut ooncha aur R kam: wo kam garm hota hai aur nahi pighalta. (e) **True** ✅ (lagbhag): rating wo current hai jo wo lagataar jhel leta hai.

10. m = 25 mg = 2.5 × 10⁻⁵ kg. Q = 2.5 × 10⁻⁵ kg × 200 J/kg °C × 153 °C = 0.765 J. P = (120 A)² × 0.040 Ω = 14 400 A² × 0.040 Ω = 576 W. t = 0.765 J ÷ 576 W = 0.00133 s = **1.33 ms** (lagbhag). **Jaanch:** 576 W × 0.0013281 s = 0.765 J ✅

11. **10 A red.** 5 A < 6.5 A, to turant udta. 15 A bahut upar hai: taar ki seema ke aas paas honi chahiye, to 10 A chuna. **Jaanch:** 5 A < 6.5 A < 10 A ✅

12. Current 3 guna, to I² R wali garmi 3² = **9 guna**. Real fuse mein garmi taar se screw aur hawa mein bahar bhi nikalti hai, aur bade current par ye nikaasi chhoti pad jaati hai: aur melting ke liye alag se thodi garmi bhi chahiye: isliye samay 9 guna kam nahi hota (aur table dikhati hai ki samay current ke square se bhi zyada tezi se ghatta hai). **Jaanch:** 3² = 9 ✅
