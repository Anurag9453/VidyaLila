# Topic 2: Myopia Aur Hypermetropia: Hisaab Se Chashme Ka Number

Topic 1 mein humne aankh ko ek asli lens system ki tarah dekha: lagbhag +60 D ki kul power, jawan aankh ki lagbhag +4 D ki accommodation, aur ek retina jo lens se lagbhag 2.3 cm peeche hai. Ab sawaal ye hai ki jab ye hisaab thoda bigad jaye, to chashme ka number **kaise nikalta hai**, aur parchi par likha "−2.50" ya "+1.75" asal mein kehta kya hai? Is topic mein hum number ke peeche ka hisaab seekhenge: ek aankh ki takleef se seedha lens ki power tak.

## Optician ki dukaan par "−2.50"

Shanivaar ko Mr. Kirloskar apna purana chashma badalwane ek optician ki dukaan par gaye, aur unka bhatija Kaustubh saath ho liya. Dukaan mein ek chhoti si machine thi, jiske saamne Mr. Kirloskar ne thodi der tak aankh tikakar rakhi. Optician bhaiya ne ek button dabaya, machine ne halki si beep ki, aur ek chhoti parchi bahar nikal aayi. Parchi par ek line chhapi thi: **SPH −2.50**.

Kaustubh ne parchi haath mein li. "Chacha, ye −2.50 kya hai? Ye kilo hai, ya size hai? Aur plus ki jagah minus kyun?"

Mr. Kirloskar muskuraye. "Ye mere lens ki **power** hai, dioptre mein, jaise tumne science mein padha hai. Minus ka matlab **concave lens**, kyunki meri aankh door ka saaf nahi dekh paati."

Optician bhaiya ne ek lens uthaya aur Kaustubh ke haath mein rakh diya. "Isse dukaan ki board ko dekho." Board chhoti dikhi. "Dekha? Par ye bata do: parchi mein sirf −2.50, −2.75 ya −2.25 hi kyun hote hain? −2.60 ya −2.37 kyun nahi?"

Kaustubh ne socha. "Hmm... shayad machine ke steps itne hi hote hain?" Mr. Kirloskar bole, "Nahi beta. Aaj dekhte hain ki ek number aata kahan se hai, aur jo chashma **zyada power** ka ho, kya wo hamesha **behtar** hota hai?" Kaustubh ne turant kaha, "Haan Chacha, zyada number matlab zyada tez, to behtar hi hoga!" "Wahi galat-fehmi hai," Mr. Kirloskar ne kaha, "jo aaj saaf karni hai. Chalo, pehle recall."

## Class 9 se recall

Class 9 mein hum dekh chuke hain ([light-refraction-part7.md](../../../class9/physics/light-refraction/light-refraction-part7.md), Part F) ki **myopia** mein door ki cheez ka image retina ke **saamne** banta hai aur **concave lens** use peeche retina par le jaata hai. **Hypermetropia** mein paas ki cheez ka image retina ke **peeche** banta hai aur **convex lens** use aage retina par laata hai. Dono ke ray diagrams wahin ke Figure 7.6 aur Figure 7.7 mein hain, hum unhein dobara nahi banayenge. Class 9 ke hisaab ke liye ye formulas yaad rakho ([light-refraction-part6.md](../../../class9/physics/light-refraction/light-refraction-part6.md)):

- Lens formula: 1/v − 1/u = 1/f, aur power P = 1 ÷ f (f metre mein, P dioptre D mein).
- **Sign convention:** lens ka optical centre 0 hai, roshni left se right jaati hai. Real object ki doori u **negative** (object light wali taraf), real image ki doori v **positive**, virtual image ki doori v **negative**, convex lens ka f **positive**, concave lens ka f **negative**.
- Chipke hue lenses ki powers **jodi jaati hain** (Class 9 ka "do lens chipke hue").

Ab humein sirf ek naya kaam karna hai: parchi ka number **kaise nikalta hai**, ye samajhna.

## Part A: Defect ko "extra power" se samajhna

Ek theek aankh ki power lagbhag +60 D hai (Topic 1). Aaram ki halat mein (relaxed) wo door ki cheez ki parallel kiranon ko seedha retina par laati hai. Maan lo retina tak ki doori v hai. Lens formula P = 1/v − 1/u mein u = −∞ rakho to 1/u = 0 aur P₀ = 1/v. Yaani **jo power retina ki doori v ko "fit" kare**, wahi theek power hai.

Ab ek **myopic aankh** lo. Ya to eyeball thodi zyada lambi hai, ya aankh ki power thodi zyada hai. Lambi eyeball ke liye "theek" power thodi kam chahiye, isliye asar wahi hota hai: kiranen retina se pehle mil jaati hain. Hum dono ko ek hi tarah likhte hain: myopic aankh ki power = P₀ + x, jahan **x extra power** hai. Ye aankh door ki cheez ka image retina ke saamne banati hai, kyunki zyada power matlab zyada tez modna.

Is aankh ka **far point** d_f wo sabse door ka point hai jo use abhi bhi saaf dikhta hai. Wahan rakhi cheez ke liye u = −d_f hai, aur image retina par hi banta hai (v wahi). Lens formula ka seedha use:

1/v = (P₀ + x) + 1/u = P₀ + x − 1/d_f

Retina par image ka matlab 1/v = P₀. Dono barabar rakho to **x = 1/d_f**. Ye ek bahut kaam ki baat hai:

> Myopic aankh ki extra power = 1 ÷ (far point, metre mein) dioptre.

To theek karne ke liye humein ek aisa lens chahiye jo **utni hi power ghata de**, yaani P = −x = −1 ÷ d_f. Ye wahi hai jo Class 9 mein f = −(far point) se aata tha: f = −d_f to P = 1 ÷ f = −1 ÷ d_f. Dono raaste ek hi jagah pahunchte hain. Kul power P₀ + x − x = P₀ wapas theek ho jaati hai (powers jodi jaati hain).

Ek honest baat: asli aankh andar se hawa nahi, paani jaisi cheez se bhari hai, isliye ye sirf ek **saaf model** hai ("effective" numbers, jaisa Topic 1 mein bhi tha). Par lens ke number nikaalne ke liye ye model bahut accha kaam karta hai.

Aur ek zaroori baat: x aksar bahut chhota hota hai. Example 1 mein dekhenge ki lagbhag 60 D mein se sirf lagbhag 1 D ya 2 D ka farq, yaani lagbhag 2 percent. **Itne chhote se farq se door ki dunia dhundhli ho jaati hai.**

## Part B: Myopia: far point se lens

Concave lens ka kaam Figure 2.1 mein dikhaya hai: door ki cheez se aayi parallel kiranen lens se guzarkar **faile hue (diverging) raste** par chalti hain, aur aankh ko aisa lagta hai ki wo kiranen **F se aa rahi hain**, jo aankh ka far point hai. Yaani lens door ki cheez ka **virtual image far point par** bana deta hai, aur aankh use aaram se saaf dekh leti hai.

```

                                      )(                   ╱──────
                                      )(          ╱────────
                                      )(   ╱──────
──────────────────────────────────────)(───
                           ·······    )(
                  ·········           )(
           ·······                    )(
──────F───────────────────────────────)(─────────────────────────────────────
           ·······                    )(
                  ·········           )(
                           ·······    )(
──────────────────────────────────────)(───
                                      )(   ╲──────
                                      )(          ╲────────

  F = far point (virtual image)    concave lens
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Concave lens parallel kiranon ko failakar aisa bana deta hai jaise wo far point F se aa rahi hon. Bindu wali lines peeche ki taraf badhai gayi virtual lines hain; ye sab F par milti hain, isliye lens ka virtual image F par banta hai.

### Example 1: Far point 90 cm

Ek myopic vyakti ka far point 90 cm hai. Use kaisa lens chahiye? Chashme ki parchi mein kya likha jayega? Aankh ki extra power bhi nikalo.

**Given:** far point = 90 cm = 0.90 m. Door ki cheez u = −∞ (u = −∞ ka matlab 1/u = 0).
**Find:** lens ka type, f, P, parchi ka number, aur extra power x.
**Formula:** 1/f = 1/v − 1/u, P = 1 ÷ f. Sign convention: u negative (object light wali taraf), virtual image v negative, concave lens ka f negative.

Lens ko door ki cheez ka virtual image 90 cm par banana hai, to v = −90 cm.

**Substitution:** 1/f = 1/(−90 cm) − 0 = −1/90 cm⁻¹, to f = **−90 cm** = −0.90 m.
P = 1 ÷ f = 1 ÷ (−0.90 m) = −1.11 D (lagbhag).

Opticians lagbhag 0.25 D ke steps mein lens dete hain, isliye parchi mein nearest 0.25 D likhte hain: −1.11 D −1.00 D ke sabse paas hai (−1.00 D se farq 0.11 D, −1.25 D se farq 0.14 D). Asli number optician ke **aankh ke test** se tay hota hai (lens badal badal kar poochna "kaunsa saaf?"); hisaab ek bahut accha andaza deta hai.

**Extra power:** x = 1 ÷ d_f = 1 ÷ 0.90 m = **1.11 D**. Ye lagbhag 60 D ka 1.11 ÷ 60 = 0.0185, yaani lagbhag 1.9 percent hai.

**Answer:** concave lens, f = −90 cm, P = −1.11 D, parchi par SPH −1.00 D (nearest 0.25 D). Extra power 1.11 D.

**Jaanch:** f = 1 ÷ P = 1 ÷ (−1.11 D) = −0.90 m ✅. Lens formula wapas: 1/v = 1/f + 1/u = −1/90 + 0, v = −90 cm, yaani image far point par ✅. Minus sign saaf kehta hai ki lens concave hai ✅.

### Parchi ka number aur far point: ek table

Extra power x = 1 ÷ d_f ko ulta bhi padh sakte hain: parchi ka number (bina minus ke) jitna bada, far point utna paas.

| Parchi (SPH) | Extra power | Far point lagbhag |
|---|---|---|
| −0.75 D | 0.75 D | 133 cm |
| −1.50 D | 1.50 D | 67 cm |
| −3.00 D | 3.00 D | 33 cm |
| −4.50 D | 4.50 D | 22 cm |
| −6.00 D | 6.00 D | 17 cm |

Dhyan do: number jitna bada, aankh mein extra power utni zyada aur far point utna paas. Aur ye bhi dekho: lens ka kaam sirf aankh ka extra cancel karna hai, usse zyada nahi. Is par Aam galtiyan mein wapas aayenge.

## Part C: Hypermetropia: near point se lens

Ab dusri takleef. **Hypermetropic** aankh ka near point 25 cm se door hota hai: kitaab ko 25 cm par rakho to image retina ke peeche banta hai. Hum chahte hain ki 25 cm par rakhi kitaab saaf padhi jaye. Convex lens ka kaam Figure 2.2 mein dikhaya hai: kitaab se aati kiranen (jo kaafi faili hui hain) lens se guzarkar **kam faili hui** ho jaati hain, aur aankh ko aisa lagta hai ki wo **near point N** se aa rahi hain, jo kitaab se door hai. Yaani lens kitaab ka **virtual image near point par** bana deta hai.

```

                                        ()                    ╱───────
                                        ()           ╱────────
                                        ()   ╱───────
                                    ·╱──()───
                            ·····╱───   ()
                   ········· ╱───       ()
           ········      ╱───           ()
      N───────────────●─────────────────()───────────────────────────────────
           ········      ╲───           ()
                   ········· ╲───       ()
                            ·····╲───   ()
                                    ·╲──()───
                                        ()   ╲───────
                                        ()           ╲────────
                kitaab (25 cm)
  N = near point (virtual image)     convex lens
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Kitaab (25 cm par) se aati kiranen convex lens ke baad kam faili hui ho jaati hain. Bindu wali lines peeche badhane par near point N par milti hain, yaani lens ka virtual image N par banta hai, jo kitaab se door aur aankh ke liye saaf dikhne layak hai.

### Failaav ka shortcut

Ek bindu aankh se d metre door ho to wahan se aati kiranon ka **failaav** 1 ÷ d dioptre kehte hain (bindu jitna paas, failaav utna zyada; door ki cheez ka failaav 0). Lens formula mein object aur image dono ek hi taraf hon (u = −d_u, v = −d_v) to
P = 1/v − 1/u = −1/d_v + 1/d_u. Yaani seedha:

> **P = (object ka failaav) − (image ka failaav)**, jahan image wahi hai jo aankh ko chahiye.

Hypermetropia mein: kitaab 25 cm par hai, uska failaav 1 ÷ 0.25 = **4 D** hai. Aankh sirf near point N tak ka failaav sambhaal sakti hai, yaani 1 ÷ N. Lens ko 4 D ko ghatakar 1 ÷ N karna hai, to **P = 4 D − 1 ÷ N**. Myopia mein kitaab ki jagah door ki cheez hai (failaav 0) aur aankh ko 1 ÷ d_f chahiye: P = 0 − 1 ÷ d_f, jo Part A ka hi jawab hai. Do alag takleefein, ek hi formula: **P = (object ka failaav) − (jo aankh ko chahiye)**.

### Example 3: Near point 55 cm

Ek vyakti ka near point 55 cm hai. Use 25 cm par rakhi kitaab padhne ke liye kaisa lens chahiye? Parchi mein kya likhenge?

**Given:** near point = 55 cm. Kitaab ki doori = 25 cm.
**Find:** lens ka type, f, P, parchi ka number.
**Formula:** 1/f = 1/v − 1/u, P = 1 ÷ f. Sign convention: u = −25 cm (real object, light wali taraf), v = −55 cm (virtual image, usi taraf, near point par), convex lens ka f positive.

**Substitution:** 1/f = 1/(−55 cm) − 1/(−25 cm) = −1/55 cm⁻¹ + 1/25 cm⁻¹ = (−5 + 11)/275 cm⁻¹ = 6/275 cm⁻¹.
f = 275/6 cm = **+45.8 cm** (lagbhag) = +0.458 m.
P = 1 ÷ 0.458 m = **+2.18 D** (lagbhag). Nearest 0.25 D: **+2.25 D**.

**Failaav se jaanch:** 4.00 D − 1 ÷ 0.55 = 4.00 D − 1.82 D = **2.18 D** ✅, wahi nikla.

**Answer:** convex lens, f = +45.8 cm, P = +2.18 D, parchi par SPH +2.25 D.

**Jaanch:** lens formula mein wapas: 1/v − 1/u = −1/55 + 1/25 = −0.01818 + 0.04 = +0.02182 cm⁻¹ = 1/45.8 cm⁻¹ ✅. f positive, yaani convex lens ✅. Limiting case: agar near point 25 cm hi hota to 1/v − 1/u = 0, power 0, chashma chahiye hi nahi ✅.

## Part D: Parchi par "−2.50" ka matlab

Ab Mr. Kirloskar ki parchi padhte hain. SPH ka number ek seedha-sa code hai:

| Parchi ka number | Matlab | Lens |
|---|---|---|
| Minus (jaise −2.50) | Myopia: door dhundhla | Concave |
| Plus (jaise +1.75) | Paas ka dhundhla (hypermetropia ya budhape ki takleef) | Convex |
| Number ka size (minus ya plus hataakar) | Power dioptre mein: lens kitna tez modta hai | 3.00 ka lens 1.00 se teen guna tez |
| 0.25 ke steps | Lens lagbhag isi taraah milte hain | Hisaab ko nearest 0.25 par round karte hain |

Parchi par aur columns bhi aate hain (CYL, AXIS): wo tedhi cornea ke liye hain, jinhe Topic 3 mein samjhenge.

### Example 2: Parchi se ulta hisaab

Mr. Kirloskar ki parchi SPH −2.50 D hai. Unka far point kitna hai? Extra power kitni? Bina chashme ke unki aankh ko kitni doori tak saaf dikhta hai (maan lo near point 25 cm hai)?

**Given:** P = −2.50 D. Near point 25 cm.
**Find:** f, far point, extra power, clear-vision range.
**Formula:** f = 1 ÷ P, far point d_f = −f (myopia mein, Part B se), x = −P.
Sign convention: concave lens ka f negative; virtual image v negative.

**Substitution:** f = 1 ÷ (−2.50 D) = −0.40 m = **−40 cm**. To far point = 40 cm. Extra power x = **2.50 D**, jo lagbhag 60 D ka 2.5 ÷ 60 = 0.042, yaani lagbhag 4 percent hai.
Bina chashme ke saaf dikhne ki range: 25 cm se 40 cm tak, yaani 40 cm − 25 cm = **15 cm** ki chaudaai.

**Answer:** f = −40 cm, far point 40 cm, extra power 2.50 D, bina chashme ke sirf 15 cm ki range.

**Jaanch:** lens formula wapas: u = −∞ (1/u = 0), 1/v = 1/f = −1/40 cm⁻¹, v = −40 cm, yaani image far point par ✅. 25 cm + 15 cm = 40 cm ✅.

Kaustubh ne kaha, "Matlab Chacha, aapko bina chashme ke sirf paas ka saaf dikhta hai!" Mr. Kirloskar hanse. "Haan, isi liye number minus mein aata hai: door ka dhundhla."

### Activity 1: Apna near point naapo (ghar par adult ke saath)

Ek card par chhote akshar likho. Ek aankh band karo, card ko aankh se door se dheere dheere paas laao, jab tak akshar **dhundhle** na ho jayein. Wahan ki doori ruler se naapo. Bade ke saath karo, aankh par card chhuaye bina. **Ye medical test nahi hai:** bachchon ka near point aksar 25 cm se kam nikalta hai, kyunki unki accommodation zyada hoti hai. Agar aankhon mein dard ya dhundhlapan rehta ho, to doctor ya optician ko dikhao.

## Part E: Chashma aur contact lens: thoda sa farq

Chashma aankh se lagbhag 1.5 se 2 cm **aage** rehta hai, jabki contact lens aankh ki sateh par hota hai. Hum ab tak lens ko aankh ke "bilkul pass" maan rahe the. Par agar lens 2 cm aage ho, to far point (jo aankh se naapa jaata hai) lens se 2 cm **kam** doori par hota hai. Ye dekhne ke liye ek example. (Hum yahan sirf lens formula ka seedha use kar rahe hain; "vertex distance" ka koi formula ki zarurat nahi.)

### Example 4: Far point 30 cm, contact lens aur chashma

Ek myopic vyakti ka far point aankh se 30 cm hai. (a) Contact lens ki power? (b) Agar chashma aankh se 2 cm aage ho, to uski power?

**Given:** far point aankh se 30 cm. Chashma aankh se 2 cm aage.
**Find:** P (contact lens) aur P (chashma).
**Formula:** 1/f = 1/v − 1/u, P = 1 ÷ f. Sign convention: u = −∞, virtual image v negative, concave lens ka f negative.

(a) Contact lens aankh par hai: v = −30 cm. 1/f = −1/30 cm⁻¹, f = −30 cm = −0.30 m. P = 1 ÷ (−0.30 m) = −3.33 D, nearest 0.25: **−3.25 D**.
(b) Chashma 2 cm aage hai, to far point chashme se 30 cm − 2 cm = 28 cm door hai: v = −28 cm. f = −28 cm = −0.28 m. P = 1 ÷ (−0.28 m) = −3.57 D (lagbhag), nearest 0.25: **−3.50 D**.

**Answer:** contact lens lagbhag −3.25 D, chashma lagbhag −3.50 D. Chashme ka number thoda zyada minus.

**Jaanch:** dono minus, yaani concave ✅. Chashma lens image ko lens se sirf 28 cm par banata hai, jo contact wale 30 cm se paas hai, isliye lens thoda tez hona chahiye (3.57 D ka size 3.33 D se bada hai) ✅. Limiting case: kam power par farq gayab: far point 4 m ho to 1 ÷ 4.00 = 0.25 D aur chashme ke liye 1 ÷ 3.98 = 0.251 D, dono nearest 0.25 D par −0.25 D hi hain ✅.

**Seekh:** contact lens aur chashme ke numbers ka farq **tez power** mein dikhta hai (jaise −6 D ke aas-paas lagbhag 0.7 D), kam power mein lagbhag nahi. Isliye contact lens ki parchi chashme ki parchi se thodi alag hoti hai, aur kabhi ek ko doosre ki jagah nahi use karna chahiye.

### Activity 2: Chashme se cheezein chhoti ya badi (ghar par adult ke saath)

Ghar mein kisi ka **minus** wala (myopia ka) chashma lo aur ek chhapa hua akshar uski taraf se dekho: akshar **chhote** dikhenge. Phir kisi ke **plus** wale (padhne ka) chashme se dekho: akshar **bade** dikhenge. Dhyan: **lens ko kabhi Suraj ya tez bulb ki taraf mat karna aur dhoop ko lens se kisi cheez par focus mat karna**. Kaanch ko girne se bhi bachao.

## Part F: Jab dono takleefein saath hon

Umar badhne par ek myopic vyakti ki accommodation bhi kam ho jaati hai (presbyopia, jiski details Topic 3 mein hain). Tab usko **do alag powers** chahiye: door ke liye ek, padhne ke liye ek. Ek hi kaanch mein dono rakhne ka tareeka **bifocal lens** hai: **upar ka hissa door ke liye, neeche ka hissa padhne ke liye** (aur aajkal ke "progressive" lenses mein ye badlaav bina line ke dheere dheere hota hai). Yahan hum sirf idea aur ek numerical dekhte hain.

### Example 5: Do alag lens, ek bifocal

Ek 56 saal ke grahak ka door ka lens (upar ka hissa) −3.50 D hai. Ye chashma pehen kar bhi unka near point 70 cm hai (accommodation kam hai). Wo 30 cm par rakhi kitaab padhna chahte hain. Padhne ka alag convex lens (reading add) kitni power ka ho, aur bifocal ke **neeche ke hisse** ki kul power kitni hogi?

**Given:** door ka lens P₁ = −3.50 D. Chashma pehen kar near point = 70 cm. Kitaab 30 cm par.
**Find:** reading add P₂, aur neeche ke hisse ki kul power P.
**Formula:** 1/f = 1/v − 1/u, P = 1 ÷ f, chipke hue lenses ki powers jodi jaati hain (P = P₁ + P₂). Sign convention: u = −30 cm, v = −70 cm (virtual image near point par), convex lens ka f positive.

**Substitution:** 1/f = 1/(−70 cm) − 1/(−30 cm) = −1/70 cm⁻¹ + 1/30 cm⁻¹ = (−3 + 7)/210 cm⁻¹ = 4/210 cm⁻¹.
f = 210/4 cm = +52.5 cm = 0.525 m. P₂ = 1 ÷ 0.525 m = +1.90 D (lagbhag), nearest 0.25 D: **+2.00 D**.
Neeche ka kul hissa: P = P₁ + P₂ = (−3.50 D) + (+2.00 D) = **−1.50 D**.

**Answer:** reading add +2.00 D (convex). Bifocal ka upar ka hissa −3.50 D, neeche ka hissa −1.50 D (ab bhi concave, par kam).

**Jaanch:** +2.00 D ke liye f = +50 cm: 1/v = 1/f + 1/u = 1/50 − 1/30 = (3 − 5)/150 = −2/150 cm⁻¹, to v = −75 cm. Ye near point 70 cm se **aage** hai, yaani image aankh ko saaf dikhta hai ✅. Aur −3.50 D + 2.00 D = −1.50 D ✅ (Class 9 ka "chipke hue lenses" ka niyam).

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Zyada power ka chashma hamesha behtar hota hai"**

Demonstration: Example 1 wale vyakti ka far point 90 cm hai, sahi number lagbhag −1.00 D hai. Maan lo use galti se **−3.00 D** ka chashma mil gaya. Door ki cheez ka image lens se 1 ÷ 3.00 D = 0.333 m, yaani **33 cm par** banta hai. Is image ka failaav 3.00 D hai, jabki aankh bina zor ke sirf 1.11 D tak ka failaav sambhal sakti hai (uska far point 90 cm hai). Aankh ko **lagataar 3.00 − 1.11 = 1.89 D** accommodation karni padegi, door dekhte waqt bhi: sir dard aur aankhon mein khinchav.
Ulta dekho: agar chashma sirf **−0.50 D** ka ho, to door ka image 1 ÷ 0.50 = 2 m par banta hai, jo far point 0.90 m ke **aage** hai, to door ka ab bhi dhundhla.

**Sahi:** chashme ka number **na zyada, na kam**, bilkul aankh ke extra (ya kami) ke barabar hona chahiye. Sahi number wahi hai jo optician ke aankh ke test se tay ho.

**Galti 2: "Concave lens cheezein bada kar deta hai"**

Demonstration: ek concave lens, f = −45 cm, aur ek object 90 cm door (u = −90 cm). 1/v = 1/f + 1/u = −1/45 − 1/90 = −3/90 = −1/30 cm⁻¹, to v = −30 cm. m = v ÷ u = (−30) ÷ (−90) = **+1/3**: image **seedha par teen guna chhota**.
Ab ek convex lens ke saath dekho: f = +45 cm aur object 30 cm par (u = −30 cm, jo f ke andar hai). 1/v = 1/45 − 1/30 = (2 − 3)/90 = −1/90, to v = −90 cm. m = (−90) ÷ (−30) = **+3**: image **seedha aur teen guna bada**. Concave lens hamesha virtual, seedha aur **chhota** image deta hai. Isi liye minus chashme se cheezein (aur dekhne walon ko aapki aankhein) thodi chhoti dikhti hain. Cheez ko bada karna convex lens ka (magnifying glass ka) kaam hai, jo Topic 4 mein aayega.

**Sahi:** concave lens cheez ko chhota dikhata hai; wo myopia ko isliye theek karta hai ki wo kiranon ko failata hai, bada karne se nahi.

## Quick Recap

- Myopic aankh = **extra power** x = 1 ÷ (far point, metre mein) dioptre. Use cancel karne ke liye P = −1 ÷ d_f, yaani f = −(far point), concave lens.
- Hypermetropia mein near point N ho aur 25 cm par padhna ho: u = −25 cm, v = −N, to P = 4 D − 1 ÷ N (N metre mein), convex lens.
- **Failaav shortcut:** P = (object ka failaav) − (jo aankh ko chahiye).
- Parchi mein minus = concave (myopia), plus = convex. Number ki power dioptre mein, lens nearest 0.25 D ke steps mein.
- Chashma aankh se lagbhag 1.5 se 2 cm aage hota hai, isliye uska number contact lens se thoda alag hota hai (tez power mein zyada farq).
- Dono takleef saath hon to bifocal: upar door ka lens, neeche door ka lens + reading add (powers jodi jaati hain).
- Zyada power behtar nahi: sahi power chahiye. Concave lens cheez ko chhota dikhata hai.

## Practice Questions

1. A myopic person has a far point of 150 cm. Find the power of the corrective lens, the prescription (nearest 0.25 D), and the extra power of his eye.

2. A person cannot see clearly beyond 60 cm. Find the focal length and the power of the lens that will correct this. What will the optician write on the prescription (nearest 0.25 D)?

3. The far point of a mildly myopic person is 4 m. (a) Find the power of the lens. (b) What fraction (in percent) of the eye's total power of about 60 D is this extra power?

4. A girl's far point is 1.2 m. Find the extra power of her eye and the focal length of the lens that cancels it. Say whether the lens is convex or concave.

5. A hypermetropic person has a near point of 40 cm. Find the focal length and the power of the lens that lets him read a book kept at 25 cm. Check your answer by the "failaav" method.

6. The near point of a person is 75 cm. Find the power of the lens (and the prescription to the nearest 0.25 D) that lets him read at 25 cm.

7. A hypermetropic person has a near point of 120 cm but is comfortable reading at 40 cm. Find the power of the lens that allows him to read at 40 cm.

8. A prescription reads −5.25 D. (a) Is the lens concave or convex? (b) Find the far point of the wearer. (c) Can he read a book at 25 cm without glasses? Explain.

9. A myopic person has a far point of 22 cm from the eye. Find the lens power for (a) a contact lens and (b) spectacles worn 1.5 cm in front of the eye. Which has the larger number?

10. A person wears a bifocal. The upper part is −2.75 D and the reading add is +1.75 D. Find the power of the lower part, its focal length, and whether it is convex or concave. Also find the far point of this person without glasses.

11. A person with far point 60 cm is mistakenly given a lens of −2.25 D. Where is the image of a very distant object formed by this lens? How much accommodation does his eye need to see it clearly? (Use the far point to decide.)

12. A concave spectacle lens of power −1.50 D is held to look at an object 2 m from it. Find the position of the image and the magnification. Is the image bigger or smaller than the object?

13. State true or false, with a reason:
    (a) A lens of −1.50 D is a convex lens.
    (b) A person with a prescription of −2.00 D has a nearer far point than a person with −0.75 D.
    (c) The prescription of a contact lens is always exactly equal to that of spectacles.
    (d) A stronger spectacle always gives clearer vision.

### Answers

1. Far point 150 cm = 1.5 m. Sign convention: u = −∞, virtual image v = −150 cm (negative). 1/f = 1/v − 1/u = −1/150 − 0, f = **−150 cm** = −1.5 m. P = 1 ÷ (−1.5 m) = **−0.67 D** (lagbhag). Nearest 0.25 D: −0.75 D (farq 0.08 D, jabki −0.50 D se 0.17 D). Extra power x = 1 ÷ 1.5 m = **0.67 D**.
   Jaanch: 1 ÷ (−0.67 D) = −1.5 m ✅. Minus yaani concave ✅.

2. Far point 60 cm = 0.6 m. u = −∞, v = −60 cm (virtual, negative). 1/f = −1/60 cm⁻¹, f = **−60 cm** = −0.6 m. P = 1 ÷ (−0.6 m) = **−1.67 D** (lagbhag). Nearest 0.25 D: **−1.75 D** (farq 0.08 D).
   Jaanch: 1 ÷ (−1.67 D) = −0.6 m ✅. Concave ✅.

3. (a) Far point 4 m, u = −∞, v = −4 m. 1/f = −1/4 m⁻¹, f = −4 m, P = **−0.25 D**. (b) Extra power 0.25 D, aur 0.25 ÷ 60 = 0.0042, yaani lagbhag **0.42 percent**.
   Jaanch: 1 ÷ (−0.25 D) = −4 m ✅. Itni chhoti power ki takleef aksar sirf bahut door ke board par pata chalti hai.

4. Far point 1.2 m. Extra power x = 1 ÷ 1.2 m = **0.83 D** (lagbhag). Cancel karne wale lens ki power −0.83 D, f = 1 ÷ (−0.83 D) = **−1.2 m** = −120 cm. Concave lens (f negative).
   Jaanch: u = −∞, v = −1.2 m: 1/f = 1/v = −1/1.2 = −0.83 m⁻¹ ✅.

5. Near point 40 cm. u = −25 cm (real object), v = −40 cm (virtual image, near point par). 1/f = 1/v − 1/u = −1/40 + 1/25 = (−5 + 8)/200 = 3/200 cm⁻¹. f = 200/3 = **+66.7 cm** (lagbhag) = 0.667 m. P = 1 ÷ 0.667 m = **+1.5 D**.
   Failaav se jaanch: 4.00 D − 1 ÷ 0.40 = 4.00 − 2.50 = **1.5 D** ✅. Plus yaani convex ✅.

6. Near point 75 cm. u = −25 cm, v = −75 cm. 1/f = −1/75 + 1/25 = (−1 + 3)/75 = 2/75 cm⁻¹, f = **+37.5 cm** = 0.375 m. P = 1 ÷ 0.375 m = **+2.67 D** (lagbhag). Nearest 0.25 D: **+2.75 D** (farq 0.08 D).
   Jaanch: failaav se 4.00 − 1 ÷ 0.75 = 4.00 − 1.33 = 2.67 D ✅.

7. Near point 120 cm, kitaab 40 cm par. u = −40 cm, v = −120 cm. 1/f = −1/120 + 1/40 = (−1 + 3)/120 = 2/120 cm⁻¹, f = **+60 cm** = 0.6 m. P = 1 ÷ 0.6 m = **+1.67 D** (lagbhag). Nearest 0.25 D: +1.75 D.
   Jaanch: failaav se 1 ÷ 0.40 − 1 ÷ 1.20 = 2.50 − 0.83 = 1.67 D ✅. Dhyan do: 25 cm ki jagah 40 cm par padhna ho to chhoti power kaafi hai.

8. (a) Minus, to **concave** (myopia). (b) f = 1 ÷ (−5.25 D) = −0.190 m = −19.0 cm, to far point lagbhag **19 cm**. (c) Near point 25 cm ek theek aankh ka hai; is vyakti ki aankh sirf 19 cm tak saaf dekh sakti hai, isliye 25 cm par kitaab **dhundhli** dikhegi aur wo bina chashme ke kitaab ko aankh ke bahut paas (lagbhag 19 cm ke andar) rakhkar hi padh sakta hai.
   Jaanch: 1 ÷ 0.190 m = 5.26 D, lagbhag 5.25 D ✅.

9. (a) Contact lens: v = −22 cm, f = −22 cm = −0.22 m, P = 1 ÷ (−0.22 m) = −4.55 D (lagbhag), nearest 0.25: **−4.50 D**. (b) Chashma 1.5 cm aage: far point lens se 22 − 1.5 = 20.5 cm, v = −20.5 cm, f = −0.205 m, P = 1 ÷ (−0.205 m) = **−4.88 D** (lagbhag), nearest 0.25: −5.00 D. **Chashme ka number zyada** (4.88 > 4.55), farq lagbhag 0.33 D.
   Jaanch: dono minus ✅; 1 ÷ 4.88 = 0.205 m ✅.

10. Lower part: P = (−2.75 D) + (+1.75 D) = **−1.00 D**. f = 1 ÷ (−1.00 D) = −1.00 m = **−100 cm**. Minus, to **concave**. Bina chashme ke far point: f = 1 ÷ (−2.75 D) = −0.364 m, yaani far point lagbhag **36.4 cm**.
   Jaanch: −2.75 + 1.75 = −1.00 ✅; 1 ÷ 0.364 = 2.75 D ✅.

11. Lens ki power −2.25 D, to f = 1 ÷ (−2.25 D) = −0.444 m = **−44.4 cm**. Door ki cheez (u = −∞, 1/u = 0) ka virtual image v = f = **−44.4 cm**: lens ke saamne (usi taraf) 44.4 cm par. Far point 60 cm hai aur 44.4 cm us se **paas** hai, to aankh ko image par focus karne ke liye accommodation chahiye: failaav 2.25 D ka hai, jabki aankh aaram se sirf 1 ÷ 0.6 = 1.67 D sambhal sakti hai. Zarurat: 2.25 − 1.67 = **0.58 D** (lagbhag) lagataar accommodation, jo door dekhte hue bhi aankh ko thakata hai.
   Jaanch: 2.25 − 1.67 = 0.58 ✅. Ek jawan aankh (lagbhag +4 D) ke liye ye chhoti hai, par lagataar rehne se thakan deti hai.

12. f = 1 ÷ (−1.50 D) = −0.667 m = −66.7 cm. u = −200 cm. 1/v = 1/f + 1/u = −1/66.7 − 1/200 = −0.015 − 0.005 = −0.020 cm⁻¹, to v = **−50 cm**. m = v ÷ u = (−50) ÷ (−200) = **+0.25**. Image **seedha** aur chaar guna **chhota** hai.
   Jaanch: m = 0.25, yaani 1 se chhota, aur v negative, yaani virtual, seedha aur chhota, jaisa concave lens ke liye hona chahiye ✅.

13. (a) **False** ❌: minus power ka matlab concave lens hai. (b) **True** ✅: −2.00 D ka far point 1 ÷ 2.00 = 50 cm, −0.75 D ka lagbhag 133 cm (1 ÷ P se). (c) **False** ❌: chashma aankh se lagbhag 1.5 se 2 cm aage hota hai, isliye tez power mein number alag aata hai (Example 4). (d) **False** ❌: zyada tez lens aankh ko lagataar accommodation karne par majboor karta hai; sahi power hi saaf dikhati hai (Galti 1).

---

Ab aap jaante ho ki chashme ka number koi rahasya nahi: ek aankh ki thodi si extra ya kam power ko cancel karne wala hisaab hai, jo aap khud far point ya near point se nikal sakte ho. Agli baar parchi padhne ka mauka mile to SPH ka number dekhkar bata dena ki wo kaunsa lens hai aur wearer ka far point kitna hai!

Par kya har aankh ki takleef sirf eyeball ki lambai ya power ka hisaab hai? Topic 3 mein hum dekhenge ki umar ke saath aankh mein kya badalta hai: **presbyopia** (dadi-nani ke padhne ke chashme), **astigmatism** (tedhi cornea) aur **cataract** (dhundhla lens).
