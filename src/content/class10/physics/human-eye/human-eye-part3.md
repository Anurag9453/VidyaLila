# Topic 3: Presbyopia, Astigmatism Aur Cataract: Aankh Ki Umar

Topic 2 mein humne dekha ki myopia aur hypermetropia mein chashme ka number "extra power" ya "kami wali power" ko cancel karne se nikalta hai, aur wo number far point ya near point se hisaab lagakar milta hai. Ab ek aur sawal: **jinki aankh bachpan mein bilkul theek thi, unhe bhi umar ke saath chashma kyun lagta hai?** Is topic mein aankh ke teen "umar aur aakar" wale dosh dekhte hain: presbyopia, astigmatism aur cataract.

## Aaji ka chashma aur Gajanan dada ke do shishe

Ravivaar ki subah Mr. Hirlekar ke ghar akhbaar ka time tha. Unki bhatiji Devyani ne dekha ki Aaji akhbaar ko bilkul haath bhar door pakde hue hain, aur phir bhi aankhein sikod rahi hain. Phir unhone chupchaap apna moti-sa chashma uthaya, pehna, aur akhbaar jhat se paas aa gaya: "Ab saaf hai!"

Devyani ko shararat soojhi. Usne Aaji ka chashma pehna aur turant chillayi, "Chacha! Sab kuch dhundhla aur ghoomta hua lag raha hai! Aaji ko isse saaf kaise dikhta hai?" Mr. Hirlekar hanse. "Isliye ki wo chashma Aaji ki aankh ke liye bana hai, tumhari ke liye nahi. Aur abhi ek chashma aur dekhna: Gajanan dada aate hi honge."

Thodi der mein Mr. Hirlekar ke cousin Gajanan dada aaye. Unke chashme ke shishe ke beech ek **saaf si lakeer** thi: upar ka hissa alag, neeche ka alag. Devyani ne ghoor kar dekha. "Dada, ye do chashme ek mein kyun? Aur Aaji ko door ka TV bina chashme ke saaf dikhta hai, par akhbaar nahi. Class 9 mein to padha tha ki paas ki cheez na dikhe to hypermetropia hota hai. To kya Aaji ko bhi wahi hai?"

Mr. Hirlekar ne kaha, "Achha sawal. Jawab 'haan' bhi hai aur 'nahi' bhi. Aaj dekhenge ki Aaji ki aankh ke andar umar ke saath kya badla, Gajanan dada ke shishe mein do power kaise rehti hain, aur do aur cheezein: aankh ka gol na hona, aur lens ka dhundhla ho jaana."

## Part A: Umar ke saath accommodation girti hai (presbyopia)

### Aankh ki "focus ki kharch-rakam"

Topic 1 mein humne accommodation ko dioptre mein naapna dekha tha. Ek jawan aankh apni power lagbhag **+4 D** tak badha sakti hai: isi se door (infinity) ki cheez se lekar 25 cm ki cheez tak saaf dikhti hai. Umar badhne par eye lens kam lachila (zyada sakht) ho jaata hai, aur 50 se 60 saal tak accommodation girkar lagbhag **+1 D** reh jaati hai. Ise kehte hain **presbyopia**: ye bimari nahi, umar ka asar hai, aur lagbhag sab ko hota hai.

Jis aankh ka far point infinity hai, uska near point nikalna aasan hai. Agar bachi hui accommodation A dioptre hai, to wo aankh sabse paas **1 ÷ A** metre par saaf dekh sakti hai.

| Bachi hui accommodation A | Near point = 1 ÷ A | Matlab |
|---|---|---|
| +4 D (jawan aankh) | 0.25 m = 25 cm | Normal near point |
| +2.5 D | 0.4 m = 40 cm | Kitaab thodi door karni padti hai |
| +1.6 D | 0.625 m = 62.5 cm | Akhbaar haath bhar door |
| +1 D (lagbhag 50 se 60 saal) | 1 m = 100 cm | Chhota print bilkul nahi padh sakte |

Dhyan do: door ka dekhna nahi bigda (far point abhi bhi infinity hai), sirf **near point door khisakta ja raha hai**. Isiliye Aaji ko TV saaf dikhta hai par akhbaar nahi.

### Reading glasses: kitni power?

Reading glass ko 25 cm par rakhi kitaab ka virtual image aankh ke **near point** par banana hai (jaise Class 9 ke Example 6 mein). Sign convention wahi: u negative (cheez lens ke saamne), v negative (virtual image usi taraf), convex lens ka f positive.

### Example 1: Aaji ka reading glass

Aaji ka near point lagbhag 62.5 cm hai (Topic 1 ke Example 4 mein isi tarah bachi hui accommodation se near point nikala tha) aur door ka dekhna theek hai. Wo 25 cm par rakhi kitaab padhna chahti hain. Kaisa lens aur kitni power?

**Given:** u = −25 cm, v = −62.5 cm (virtual image near point par)
**Find:** f aur P
**Formula:** 1/v − 1/u = 1/f, P = 1 ÷ f (f metre mein)
**Substitution:** 1/f = 1/(−62.5 cm) − 1/(−25 cm) = −0.016 cm⁻¹ + 0.04 cm⁻¹ = 0.024 cm⁻¹, to f = 41.67 cm = +0.4167 m; P = 1 ÷ 0.4167 m = **+2.4 D**
**Answer:** convex lens, f = +41.7 cm, power +2.4 D.
**Jaanch:** f positive hai, to convex ✅. Chhota raasta: jawan aankh 4 D deti hai, Aaji ke paas 1.6 D bachi hai, kami 4 − 1.6 = **2.4 D** ✅. Check: 1 ÷ 2.4 D = 0.4167 m ✅.

Dekho, reading glass sirf wo "kami" bharta hai jo accommodation se poori nahi ho rahi. Isiliye umar badhne par power badhti jaati hai: aam taur par lagbhag **+1 D se +3 D** ke beech.

### Doosre ka chashma mat pehno

Devyani ne Aaji ka chashma pehna to sab dhundhla kyun? Kyunki +2.4 D ki extra power uski jawan aankh ke liye **bahut zyada** thi. Kisi doosre ka chashma kuch minute se zyada mat pehno: aankhein thak sakti hain aur sir dard ho sakta hai.

## Part B: Bifocal aur progressive lens

### Aaji do chashme kyun badalti hain?

(Bifocal ka idea aur ek numerical Topic 2 ke Part F mein tha; yahan dekhte hain ki ek hi shishe mein do power ki zaroorat kyun padti hai.)

Reading glass pehni to paas saaf, par ab door dekho to? Dekhte hain.

### Example 2: Aaji TV dekhti hain, chashme ke saath

Aaji +2.4 D ka reading glass pehni hain aur 3 m door TV dekhti hain. Lens ke saamne TV ka image kahan banta hai?

**Given:** u = −3 m = −300 cm, f = +41.67 cm (P = +2.4 D)
**Find:** v
**Formula:** 1/v = 1/f + 1/u
**Substitution:** 1/v = 0.024 cm⁻¹ + (−0.003333 cm⁻¹) = 0.020667 cm⁻¹, to v = **+48.4 cm**
**Answer:** v lagbhag +48.4 cm: plus sign yaani kiranein lens ke baad **milne ki taraf** chal rahi hain (converging), parallel nahi.
**Jaanch:** u ka sign negative lagaya ✅. Agar TV bahut door ho (u → ∞) to v = f = +41.7 cm; 3 m par v thoda zyada (48.4 cm) ✅.

Aankh ki taraf converging kiranein aati hain, aur aankh to khud hi parallel kiranon ko retina par laati hai. Converging kiranein **retina ke saamne** hi mil jaati hain: bilkul myopia jaisa dhundhla (Class 9 ka myopia wala Figure 7.6 yaad karo). Isiliye Aaji TV dekhte waqt chashma utaar deti hain. Roz roz chashma utaarna-pehenna thaka dene wala kaam hai, aur isi ka hal hai bifocal.

### Bifocal aur progressive

**Bifocal lens** mein ek hi shishe ke upar ka hissa **door** ke liye aur neeche ka hissa **paas** ke liye hota hai. Aankh neeche dekhti hai to padhne wala hissa, seedha dekhti hai to door wala. **Progressive lens** mein bhi yahi do kaam hote hain, par power upar se neeche dheere dheere badhti hai aur beech mein koi line nahi dikhti. Dono mein asli baat ek hi hai: **do power ek chashme mein**.

```
  (a) Bifocal                         (b) Progressive

 ╭──────────────────────────╮        ╭──────────────────────────╮ 
(  door ke liye              )      (  door ke liye              )
(  (upar ka hissa)           )      (  (upar ka hissa)           )
(                            )      (     ↓  power dheere-       )
(────────────────────────────)      (     ↓  dheere badhti hai,  )
(  padhne ke liye            )      (     ↓  koi line nahi       )
(  (neeche ka hissa)         )      (  padhne ke liye            )
 ╰──────────────────────────╯        ╰──────────────────────────╯ 
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Chashme ke shishe ka samne se naksha (schematic): (a) Bifocal: shishe ke beech ek saaf line, upar ka hissa door ke liye aur neeche ka hissa padhne ke liye. (b) Progressive: koi line nahi, power upar se neeche dheere dheere badhti hai.

### Example 3: Gajanan dada ka bifocal

Gajanan dada ko door ki taraf ka myopia bhi hai aur presbyopia bhi. Unke door wale hisse ki power −3.50 D hai aur padhne ke liye **+1.75 D ka add** lagta hai. Neeche wale hisse ki power aur focal length kya hai?

**Given:** P_upar = −3.50 D, add = +1.75 D (powers add, jaise Class 9 ke Example 14 mein)
**Find:** P_neeche aur f_neeche
**Formula:** P_neeche = P_upar + add, f = 1 ÷ P
**Substitution:** P_neeche = (−3.50 D) + (+1.75 D) = **−1.75 D**; f = 1 ÷ (−1.75 D) = −0.5714 m = **−57.1 cm**
**Answer:** neeche ka hissa bhi concave hai (−1.75 D), bas upar se halka.
**Jaanch:** f negative, to concave ✅. Add wapas: (−1.75 D) − (−3.50 D) = +1.75 D ✅. Units: D ✅.

Gajanan dada ke neeche ka hissa phir bhi concave kyun? Kyunki wo myopic hain: unhe sirf padhne ke liye **kam** concave power chahiye. Dono shishe ek hi aankh ke liye hain, par kaam alag. Is hisaab mein hum lens ko aankh se chipka hua maan rahe hain; asli chashma aankh se lagbhag 1.5 se 2 cm door hota hai, isliye optician ka number thoda alag aa sakta hai (Topic 2 ki wahi baat).

Ek check aur: Gajanan dada ke paas lagbhag +1 D accommodation bachi hai. Bina chashme unki aankh ka far point 1 ÷ 3.5 = 0.2857 m = 28.6 cm hai (myopia), aur +1 D accommodation lagakar wo sabse paas 1 ÷ 4.5 = 0.2222 m = 22.2 cm tak dekh sakti hai. Yaani unki saaf dikhne ki range sirf 22.2 cm se 28.6 cm. Kitaab 40 cm par rakhi hai. Door wale hisse se u = −40 cm: 1/v = −3.5 + (−2.5) = −6 D, v = −16.7 cm: ye 22.2 cm se **paas** hai, dhundhla. Neeche wale hisse se: 1/v = −1.75 + (−2.5) = −4.25 D, v = −23.5 cm: ye 22.2 cm aur 28.6 cm ke **beech** hai, saaf ✅. Yahi hai bifocal ka kaam.

## Part C: Astigmatism: jab aankh gol nahi

### Ek chamach se samajhna

Steel ka chamach lo (ghar par adult ke saath). Uska peeth wala hissa dekho: wo **lambai ki disha** mein bhi jhuka hua hai aur **chaudaai ki disha** mein bhi, par dono jhukaav **barabar nahi** hote. Ab chamach ke peeth mein apna chehra dekho: lambai ki disha mein chehra kuch aur, chaudaai ki disha mein kuch aur fail ya sikud jaata hai. Agar cornea bhi kuch aisa hi ho (football ki jagah rugby ball jaisa), to uska focus **har disha mein alag** hoga. Ise **astigmatism** kehte hain.

Normal cornea bilkul gol gend ke ek hisse jaisa hota hai, to har disha mein barabar bending. Astigmatism mein ek disha mein zyada bending aur doosri mein kam: ek hi cheez ki seedhi aur aadi lines retina par **alag alag jagah** focus hoti hain.

### Kaun sa lens?

Gole lens (convex ya concave) har disha mein barabar bending karte hain, isliye ek disha ki kami poori nahi hoti. Isliye astigmatism ke liye **cylindrical lens** lagta hai: ek kaanch ki chhadi ke tukde jaisa, jo **sirf ek disha mein** kiranon ko modta hai (power) aur uske lambvat disha mein bilkul seedha (power zero). Doctor jis disha mein cornea kam bend karta hai, ussi disha mein cylindrical lens ki power rakhte hain, aur gol lens (myopia ya hypermetropia ke liye) saath mein jod diya jaata hai. Ismein numericals aage ki class mein; yahan sirf idea.

## Part D: Cataract: lens dhundhla ho jaaye

### Kya hota hai?

Eye lens normal mein bilkul transparent hota hai. Cataract mein wo **dhundhla (cloudy)** ho jaata hai, jaise tej dhoop mein bhaap wala shishe. Roshni lens se guzarte waqt bikhar jaati hai, to cheezein dhundhli, rang pheeke aur raat mein roshni ke aaspaas chakachaundh dikhti hai. Kaaran: **umar** (sabse aam), **diabetes**, **UV rays** ka lagataar asar (isliye UV-protected sunglasses ki salah dete hain), aur **aankh ki chot**.

Cataract koi retina ya cornea ka dosh nahi: **lens** ka dosh hai. Isiliye chashma kuch khaas madad nahi karta: aankh ke andar ka lens hi saaf nahi.

### Surgery: dhundhle lens ki jagah naya lens

Doctor dhundhle lens ko nikaal dete hain aur uski jagah ek **intraocular lens (IOL)** laga dete hain, jo ek chhota plastic ya acrylic ka lens hota hai. Ye ek aam aur surakshit surgery hai. Par ek zaroori baat: IOL ki power **fixed** hoti hai. Wo ciliary muscles ke saath apni shape nahi badal sakta, yaani accommodation nahi hoti. Isiliye surgery ke baad aksar **reading ke liye chashma** phir bhi lagta hai, jaisa agle numerical mein dekhte hain. (Kuch IOL multifocal hote hain, par wo ek alag vishay hai.)

### Example 4: Cataract surgery ke baad

Poori aankh ki power lagbhag +60 D hai, jisme natural lens ka hissa lagbhag +20 D hai. Surgery mein natural lens nikala gaya. (a) Bina lens ke aankh ki power? (b) Kitni power ka IOL lagana chahiye? (c) Door ke liye IOL lagne ke baad kitab 30 cm par padhne ke liye chashme ki power?

**Given:** P_aankh = +60 D, P_lens = +20 D, kitab u = −30 cm, aankh ab door par focused (relaxed), accommodation nahi
**Find:** (a), (b), (c)
**Formula:** powers add: P = P₁ + P₂; chashme ka lens: kitab ka virtual image infinity par (v → −∞): 1/f = 1/v − 1/u
**Substitution:** (a) 60 − 20 = **+40 D**. (b) Kami = 60 − 40 = **+20 D**, to IOL lagbhag **+20 D**. (c) 1/f = 0 − 1/(−0.30 m) = +3.33 D, to P = **+3.33 D**, f = 1 ÷ 3.33 = +0.30 m = +30 cm.
**Answer:** (a) +40 D, (b) +20 D, (c) lagbhag +3.33 D ka convex reading glass.
**Jaanch:** (b) +40 D + 20 D = +60 D ✅; (c) f = +30 cm positive, convex ✅; limiting case: kitab 30 cm par hai, f = 30 cm, to kitab lens ke focus par hai aur image infinity par ✅.

Optician aam taur par power 0.25 D ke kadam mein round karta hai (Topic 2): +3.33 D ke liye lagbhag +3.25 D.

### Aankh daan: kiski zaroorat kab?

Class 9 ke [Part H](../../../class9/physics/light-refraction/light-refraction-part7.md) mein aankh daan ke baare mein padha tha; wahan cornea kharab hone ke saath "cataract jaise kaaranon" ka bhi zikr tha. Yahan ek saaf baat: **cataract** ke ilaaj ke liye daan ki gayi aankh ki zaroorat nahi hoti, kyunki wahan **lens** badla jaata hai aur uski jagah nakli IOL lagta hai. Jinka **cornea** kharab ho gaya ho (chot ya infection se), unhe daan mein mile cornea ke transplant se roshni mil sakti hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Presbyopia aur hypermetropia ek hi cheez hai, kyunki dono mein convex lens lagta hai."**

Demonstration: Aaji (presbyopia) aur ek 12 saal ka bachcha Sarvesh (hypermetropia, eyeball chhota) ka near point dono ka 62.5 cm hai. Dono ko 25 cm par padhne ke liye ek jaisa +2.4 D ka lens lagega (Example 1 ka hisaab). Par andar ka hisaab alag hai:
- Aaji ke eyeball ka size theek hai, bas total accommodation girkar +1.6 D reh gayi hai. Door ke liye unhe koi extra zor nahi lagana padta.
- Sarvesh ki accommodation abhi +4 D hai, par chhote eyeball ki wajah se door ki cheez ke liye bhi use lagbhag +2.4 D "kharch" karna padta hai. Bachta hai 4 − 2.4 = **+1.6 D**, aur isse bhi near point 1 ÷ 1.6 = 0.625 m = 62.5 cm banta hai. Isiliye bachche ko aksar sir dard hota hai aur chashma poora samay lagta hai.

Dono ka lens ek jaisa convex, par **kaaran** alag (umar ke saath lachak ghatna alag aur chhota eyeball alag), aur **kab pehenna hai** bhi alag. Aaji ko chashma sirf padhte waqt, Sarvesh ko aam taur par sab samay.

**Sahi:** lens ka type ek, kaaran alag. Hypermetropia mein aankh ki banawat (chhota eyeball ya kam power) dosh hai, presbyopia mein umar ke saath lens ki lachak kam hona.

## Quick Recap

- **Presbyopia:** umar ke saath accommodation ghatti hai (lagbhag +4 D se +1 D, 50 se 60 saal tak); near point door khisakta hai, far point nahi.
- Reading glass ki power = 4 D − (bachi hui accommodation), yaani formula se **P = 4 − 100 ÷ (near point cm)**: convex, aur umar ke saath badhti hai (lagbhag +1 D se +3 D).
- Reading glass pehen kar door dekho to dhundhla (converging kiranein, retina ke saamne focus).
- **Bifocal:** ek shishe mein door (upar) aur paas (neeche) ki do power; **progressive:** bina line wale, power dheere dheere badhti hai. Add: P_neeche = P_upar + add.
- **Astigmatism:** cornea ya lens ek disha mein zyada, doosri mein kam bend: ek disha ki lines saaf, doosri ki dhundhli. Ilaaj: **cylindrical lens** (power sirf ek disha mein).
- **Cataract:** eye lens dhundhla (umar, diabetes, UV, chot). Ilaaj: surgery, dhundhle lens ki jagah lagbhag +20 D ka **IOL**; IOL fixed hota hai, to reading ke liye aksar chashma.
- Cataract ke liye aankh daan ki zaroorat nahi; kharab cornea ke liye cornea ka transplant.
- Presbyopia aur hypermetropia: dono mein convex lens, par kaaran alag.

## Practice Questions

1. A man's near point has moved to 60 cm; his far point is still at infinity. Find the type, focal length and power of the lens that lets him read a book kept at 25 cm.

2. Another person has a near point of 90 cm and a normal far point. Find the power and focal length of his reading glasses for a book at 25 cm.

3. A woman cannot see anything clearly nearer than 1.5 m. Find the power of the lens she needs to read at 25 cm, and the focal length.

4. Use your answers to Questions 1 to 3. For each person find the remaining accommodation A = 1 ÷ (near point in metres), and show that 4 D minus A equals the lens power you found. Who is probably the oldest?

5. A person wears bifocals with a distance part of −2.00 D and a reading add of +1.50 D. Find the power and focal length of the lower (reading) part.

6. A bifocal lens has an upper part of −2.25 D and a lower part of −1.25 D. Find the reading add and the focal length of each part.

7. A teacher wears +1.75 D reading glasses and looks at a lamp post 5 m away without taking them off. Find the image distance given by the glasses, and explain why the lamp post looks blurred.

8. After cataract surgery a patient has a monofocal IOL set for distance and no accommodation. (a) Find the power of the glasses he needs to read a newspaper at 35 cm. (b) Round it to the nearest 0.25 D.

9. The natural lens of an eye (about +20 D) is removed in a cataract operation and an IOL is placed. What power must the IOL have to restore the whole eye to about +60 D? Why is spectacle correction of cataract not preferred when the lens itself is cloudy, and why does the operation not need donated eyes?

10. A student sees the horizontal lines of a fan chart sharp and black but the vertical lines faint. (a) Which defect is this? (b) What is unequal in her eye? (c) Why will a normal convex or concave lens not fix it? (d) Which lens will?

11. Why do people usually start holding books and phones farther away around the age of 40 to 45, though their far vision is fine? Explain using accommodation in dioptres.

12. Say whether each statement is true or false, with a reason.
    (a) Presbyopia is caused by a very long eyeball.
    (b) A reading glass is a convex lens.
    (c) After a standard distance IOL the patient can still change focus like a young eye.
    (d) A cylindrical lens has the same power in all directions.
    (e) Cataract is a disease of the retina.
    (f) In a progressive lens a clear line separates the distance and reading parts.

13. Presbyopia and hypermetropia can both need a convex lens. Give one similarity and two differences between them.

### Answers

1. **Given:** u = −25 cm, v = −60 cm (virtual image at near point). **Formula:** 1/v − 1/u = 1/f. 1/f = 1/(−60 cm) − 1/(−25 cm) = −0.016667 + 0.04 = 0.023333 cm⁻¹, to f = 42.86 cm = +0.4286 m. P = 1 ÷ 0.4286 m = **+2.33 D** (convex, f = +42.9 cm).
   Jaanch: 4 − 1 ÷ 0.6 = 4 − 1.667 = 2.33 D ✅.

2. u = −25 cm, v = −90 cm. 1/f = 1/(−90) − 1/(−25) = −0.011111 + 0.04 = 0.028889 cm⁻¹, f = 34.62 cm = 0.3462 m. P = 1 ÷ 0.3462 = **+2.89 D** (convex, f = +34.6 cm).
   Jaanch: 4 − 1 ÷ 0.9 = 4 − 1.111 = 2.89 D ✅.

3. u = −25 cm, v = −150 cm. 1/f = −0.006667 + 0.04 = 0.033333 cm⁻¹, f = **+30 cm** = 0.30 m, P = 1 ÷ 0.30 = **+3.33 D**.
   Jaanch: 4 − 1 ÷ 1.5 = 4 − 0.667 = 3.33 D ✅.

4. A₁ = 1 ÷ 0.6 = **1.67 D**, A₂ = 1 ÷ 0.9 = **1.11 D**, A₃ = 1 ÷ 1.5 = **0.67 D**. 4 − 1.67 = 2.33 D, 4 − 1.11 = 2.89 D, 4 − 0.67 = 3.33 D: teeno question 1, 2, 3 ke power se milte hain ✅. Sabse kam accommodation (0.67 D) Q3 wali woman ki hai, to wahi sabse badi umar ki hone ki sambhavna hai (accommodation umar ke saath girti hai).

5. P_neeche = P_upar + add = (−2.00 D) + (+1.50 D) = **−0.50 D**. f = 1 ÷ (−0.50 D) = **−2 m = −200 cm** (concave, bahut halka).
   Jaanch: −0.50 D − (−2.00 D) = +1.50 D ✅.

6. add = P_neeche − P_upar = (−1.25 D) − (−2.25 D) = **+1.00 D**. f_upar = 1 ÷ (−2.25 D) = −0.4444 m = **−44.4 cm** (concave). f_neeche = 1 ÷ (−1.25 D) = **−0.8 m = −80 cm** (concave, halka).
   Jaanch: −2.25 + 1.00 = −1.25 D ✅.

7. **Given:** f = 1 ÷ 1.75 D = 0.5714 m = 57.14 cm, u = −5 m = −500 cm. 1/v = 1/f + 1/u = 0.0175 − 0.002 = 0.0155 cm⁻¹, v = **+64.5 cm**. Plus sign: kiranein converging hain, lens ke baad lagbhag 64.5 cm par milne chali hain. Aankh ko to parallel kiranon ki aadat hai; converging kiranein retina ke **saamne** focus hoti hain, to lamp post dhundhla (myopia jaisa) dikhta hai.
   Jaanch: u → ∞ par v = f = +57.1 cm; 5 m par thoda zyada (64.5 cm) ✅.

8. (a) Kitab u = −35 cm, image infinity par (v → −∞): 1/f = 0 − 1/(−0.35 m) = +2.857 m⁻¹. P = **+2.86 D** (f = +35 cm). (b) 0.25 D ke kadam mein: 2.75 aur 3.00 ke beech, 2.857 − 2.75 = 0.107, 3.00 − 2.857 = 0.143: nearest **+2.75 D**.
   Jaanch: 1 ÷ 2.857 = 0.35 m = 35 cm ✅.

9. IOL ki power = 60 − (60 − 20) = **+20 D** (natural lens jitni). Chashma sirf aankh ke bahar hai, aur lens ke andar ki dhundh (roshni ka bikharna) ko door nahi kar sakta, isliye aksar surgery ki jaati hai. Aankh daan sirf kharab **cornea** ke liye kaam aata hai; cataract mein lens badla jaata hai aur nakli IOL lagta hai, daan ki gayi aankh nahi.
   Jaanch: (60 − 20) + 20 = 60 D ✅.

10. (a) **Astigmatism**. (b) Cornea (ya lens) ki curvature alag alag dishaon mein barabar nahi hai. (c) Convex ya concave lens har disha mein barabar bending (ya spreading) karta hai, jabki kami sirf ek disha mein hai. (d) **Cylindrical lens**: power sirf ek disha mein.

11. Jawan aankh ki accommodation lagbhag +4 D hai, jisse near point 1 ÷ 4 = 25 cm. Umar ke saath lens kam lachila hota hai, to accommodation girti hai (lagbhag +2.5 D par near point 1 ÷ 2.5 = 0.4 m = 40 cm). Itna door ho jaata hai ki kitab ya phone ko haath se door karke dekhna padta hai. Far point infinity hi rehta hai, isliye door ka dekhna theek rehta hai.

12. (a) **False** ❌: lambi eyeball myopia mein hoti hai; presbyopia mein lens ki lachak kam hoti hai. (b) **True** ✅: convex lens extra power deta hai. (c) **False** ❌: IOL ki power fixed hoti hai, accommodation nahi. (d) **False** ❌: cylindrical lens ki power sirf ek disha mein hoti hai. (e) **False** ❌: cataract eye lens ka dosh hai. (f) **False** ❌: progressive mein koi line nahi hoti, power dheere dheere badalti hai.

13. **Similarity:** dono mein convex (positive) lens se paas ka saaf dikhta hai (jaise 25 cm par kitab). **Differences:** (i) Kaaran: presbyopia mein umar ke saath lens ki lachak ghatti hai, hypermetropia mein chhota eyeball ya kam power wala lens. (ii) Kab: presbyopia mein door ka dekhna bina zor ke theek hai aur chashma aam taur par sirf padhte waqt lagta hai; hypermetropia bachpan mein bhi ho sakta hai aur door ki cheez ke liye bhi aankh ko zor lagta hai.
