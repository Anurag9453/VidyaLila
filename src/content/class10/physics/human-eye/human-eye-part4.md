# Topic 4: Magnifying Glass Aur Microscope: Chhoti Cheez Badi

Pichhle topics mein humne aankh ko ek asli lens system ki tarah dekha, aur samjha ki umar ke saath uske kaun se dosh aate hain. Ab ek naya sawaal: **kuch cheezein itni chhoti hoti hain ki sabse tez, sabse swasth aankh bhi unhein saaf nahi dekh sakti.** Ghadi ka gear, kapde ka dhaaga, patte ki ek cell. Aise mein hum aankh ko ek **instrument ki madad** dete hain. Is topic mein ek lens wala **magnifying glass**, aur do lens wala **microscope**.

## Ghadi-saaz ki dukaan aur school ka microscope

Mr. Tambat ki purani wristwatch ruk gayi thi, to unka bhanja Aniruddh unke saath gali ke mod par ek ghadi-saaz ki chhoti si dukaan mein gaya. Ghadi-saaz ne ghadi ka piche ka dhakkan kholkar mez par rakha, aur apni **ek aankh ke aage ek chhota gol kaanch (loupe)** lagaya. Andar ke gear itne chhote the ki Aniruddh ko sirf dhundhli si chamak dikhi.

Aniruddh ne jhuk kar ghadi ko apni aankh ke bilkul paas, lagbhag 5 cm door, le aane ki koshish ki. Ulta ho gaya: gear **aur bhi dhundhle**. Phir ghadi-saaz ne usse loupe diya. Loupe aankh ke paas rakhte hi gears ke daant **saaf, bade aur chamakdaar** dikhne lage.

"Mama, ye kaise? Aankh ko paas le jaane se to dhundhla hua, par ek chhote se kaanch ke saath saaf ho gaya!" Mr. Tambat ne hanste hue kaha, "Kal tumhari school lab mein microscope hai na? Wahan ek ki jagah **do** lens milenge. Dono sawaal ek hi jawab se jude hain."

Agle din lab mein teacher ne pyaaz ke chhilke ki ek patli parat slide par rakhi. Microscope mein dekhte hi Aniruddh chilla utha: ek ek **cell** gin sakta tha, jaise chhoti-chhoti eent ki deewar! Par ek baat ajeeb thi: slide ko **left** sarkao to cells **right** bhaagte dikhte the.

Do sawaal bache: **loupe aankh ko paas laane par bhi saaf kaise dikhata hai? Aur microscope mein do lens kyun, aur picture ulti kyun?** Dono sawaal hum Part A se C ke baad "Wapas dukaan par aur lab mein" section mein solve karenge.

## Naye shabd

## Class 9 ka recall

Class 9 mein convex lens ko cheez F₁ ke **andar** rakhne par image **virtual, seedhi aur badi** mili thi: f = +15 cm, u = −10 cm par v = −30 cm aur m = +3 ([light-refraction-part6.md](../../../class9/physics/light-refraction/light-refraction-part6.md), Example 6). Lens formula 1/v − 1/u = 1/f, m = v ÷ u, aur power P = 1 ÷ f (f metre mein) wahi rahenge. Aur Class 9 ([light-refraction-part7.md](../../../class9/physics/light-refraction/light-refraction-part7.md)) mein dekha tha ki normal aankh lagbhag 25 cm se paas ki cheez saaf nahi dekh pati.

**Sign convention (Class 9 wala, hum yahi lenge):** light left se right ko jaati hai. Real object ke liye u negative. Convex lens ka f positive. Virtual image (object ki hi taraf) ka v negative, real image (doosri taraf) ka v positive.

## Part A: Magnifying glass kaam kaise karta hai

### Cheez paas, to image badi

Ek sikka haath mein lekar aankh ke paas laao: wo bada dikhta hai. Wajah: paas aane par us cheez ke kinaron se aankh tak jaati lines ke beech ka **kon** (visual angle) bada ho jaata hai, aur retina par image bhi badi banti hai. Chhote kon ke liye ye kon lagbhag **θ ≈ h ÷ doori** hota hai (h cheez ki unchai).

### Par aankh ek jagah ruk jaati hai

Topic 1 mein dekha tha ki jawan aankh ki accommodation lagbhag +4 D hoti hai. Cheez ko aankh se jitna paas laao, aankh ko utni hi **zyada extra power** chahiye: cheez d metre par ho to extra power = 1 ÷ d dioptre. +4 D tak hi ja sakte hain, to sabse paas d = 1 ÷ 4 = 0.25 m = **25 cm**. Isse paas cheez dhundhli. Yahi D hai. Aniruddh ka ghadi ko 5 cm par laana isliye ulta pada.

### Lens ki chaal

Hum chahte hain: cheez **paas** (bada kon) ho, aur phir bhi aankh ko saaf dikhe. Convex lens cheez ko F₁ ke **andar** rakhne par ek **virtual image** banata hai jo cheez se door hoti hai (u = −7 cm to v = −23 cm jaisa). Aankh ko wo image D ya usse door dikhti hai, jahan wo aaram se focus kar leti hai. Ab cheez asal mein paas hai, par aankh ko "door ki badi cheez" dikhti hai. Wahi hai magnifying glass.

Boond upar se gol ubhri hoti hai, yaani **bahut chhoti f ka convex lens**. Boond ke andar se akshar bade dikhenge. Boond sookhne par akshar wapas chhote.

### Part A ka natija

Magnifying glass image ko bada nahi karta jo screen par aaye. Wo **cheez ko aankh ke paas laane ki ijaazat** deta hai, aur virtual image D ya usse door banata hai.

## Part B: Magnifying power M

Ab ye "kitna bada" ek number mein. Hum M ko is tarah parakhte hain:

> **M = (lens ke saath dikhne wala kon) ÷ (bina lens ke, cheez D = lagbhag 25 cm par rakhne par dikhne wala kon)**

### Image D par (aankh ko sabse zyada magnify, thodi mehnat)

Image D par ho: v = −D (virtual, object ki taraf). Lens formula se u nikalo:
1/u = 1/v − 1/f = −1/D − 1/f = −(f + D) ÷ (D f), to u = −D f ÷ (D + f).
Aankh lens ke bilkul paas hai aur image D par hai, to lens ke saath kon = (image ki unchai) ÷ D aur bina lens kon = (cheez ki unchai) ÷ D. Dono ka anupaat seedha m ke barabar nikalta hai, to M = m = v ÷ u:
M = (−D) ÷ (−D f ÷ (D + f)) = (D + f) ÷ f

> **M = 1 + D ÷ f**   (image D par)

### Image infinity par (aankh ko aaram)

Cheez ko thik F₁ par rakhne se image infinity par jaati hai, aur parallel kirnein aankh mein aati hain: aankh bina zor lagaye dekhti hai. Lens ke saath kon: θ = h ÷ f. Bina lens ke D par: θ₀ = h ÷ D. To

> **M = D ÷ f**   (image infinity par)

Dono mein M ka farak sirf 1 hai: image D par ho to M thoda zyada. Hum numericals mein pehla (1 + D ÷ f) lenge, jab tak sawaal na bataye ki aankh relaxed hai.

### Example 1: f = 10 cm ka magnifying glass

Ek magnifying glass ki f = 10 cm hai. D = 25 cm lo. (a) Image D par ho to cheez kahan rakhein aur M kya hoga? (b) Image infinity par ho to M?

(a) Sign ke saath: f = +10 cm, v = −25 cm (virtual image, object ki taraf).
1/u = 1/v − 1/f = 1/(−25 cm) − 1/(+10 cm) = −2/50 cm⁻¹ − 5/50 cm⁻¹ = −7/50 cm⁻¹
u = **−50/7 cm = −7.14 cm** (cheez lens se lagbhag 7.1 cm par, F₁ = 10 cm ke andar).
M = m = v ÷ u = (−25 cm) ÷ (−7.14 cm) = **+3.5**. Yaani seedhi aur 3.5 guna badi. Formula se: 1 + 25 ÷ 10 = 3.5 ✅

(b) Cheez F₁ par: u = −10 cm. M = D ÷ f = 25 cm ÷ 10 cm = **2.5**.

Jaanch: 1/v − 1/u = 1/(−25 cm) − 1/(−7.14 cm) = −0.04 + 0.14 = 0.10 cm⁻¹ = 1/(10 cm) = 1/f ✅. Sign: u negative, v negative, f positive aur m positive (virtual, erect) ✅

### Example 2: Ghadi-saaz ka loupe "10×"

Ghadi-saaz ke loupe par "10×" likha hai (image infinity par wali value). Uski f aur power kya hai? Gear ka daant 1.2 mm ka hai: D par image ho to wo kitna bada dikhega?

M = D ÷ f = 10, to f = D ÷ M = 25 cm ÷ 10 = **2.5 cm** = 0.025 m.
P = 1 ÷ f = 1 ÷ (+0.025 m) = **+40 D** (convex lens, f positive).
Image D par: M = 1 + D ÷ f = 1 + 25 ÷ 2.5 = **11**. Daant 1.2 mm × 11 = **13.2 mm** jaisa dikhega.
Is par cheez u = −D f ÷ (D + f) = −(25 × 2.5) ÷ 27.5 cm = **−25/11 cm = −2.27 cm** par hogi.

Jaanch: 1/v − 1/u = 1/(−25 cm) − 1/(−2.27 cm) = −0.04 + 0.44 = 0.40 cm⁻¹ = 1/(2.5 cm) = 1/f ✅. Aur 1 ÷ (0.025 m) = 40 D ✅

### Example 3: Chahiye 9 guna magnification

Aniruddh ko ek aisa magnifying glass chahiye jo image D par banakar cheez ko 9 guna bada dikhaye. f kya ho aur cheez kahan rakhni padegi?

M = 1 + D ÷ f = 9, to D ÷ f = 8, f = 25 cm ÷ 8 = **+3.125 cm** (convex, f positive).
Image D par: v = −25 cm, aur m = v ÷ u = 9, to u = v ÷ 9 = (−25 cm) ÷ 9 = **−2.78 cm**.
Power P = 1 ÷ 0.03125 m = **+32 D**.

Jaanch: 1/v − 1/u = −1/25 + 9/25 = 8/25 cm⁻¹ = 1/(3.125 cm) = 1/f ✅

### Chhoti f ka matlab: zyada M, par chhota lens

M = 1 + D ÷ f dikhata hai: **f jitni chhoti, M utna zyada.** Par chhoti f ke liye lens ki surface bahut zyada **curved** (moti jaisi) chahiye, aur aisa lens chhota hone par hi bana sakte hain: usse cheez ka sirf thoda hissa dikhta hai, aankh ko lens ke bilkul paas rakhna padta hai, aur image ke kinare dhundhle padne lagte hain (lens ki kamiyan: aage ki class mein). Isliye haath ke magnifying glass lagbhag 10× tak hi aasaani se mil pate hain, aur 10× ka loupe (f = 2.5 cm, +40 D: lagbhag utni power jitni hamari cornea ki hoti hai) kaafi chhota hota hai.

**Ghar par adult ke saath (apne magnifier ki f naapo):** kamre ki khidki ya tube light se door ek saaf deewar ya safed kaagaz ke saamne magnifier pakdo (**Suraj ki taraf kabhi nahi, aur Suraj ki roshni kabhi nahi**). Magnifier ko aage-peeche karke khidki ki chhoti, ulti, real image kaagaz par saaf karo. Lens se kaagaz ki doori lagbhag f hoti hai (kyunki khidki door hai). Us f se M = 1 + 25 ÷ f nikaal lo.

## Part C: Compound microscope: do lens, do stage

Ek lens se M aasaani se lagbhag 10× ya 20× se aage nahi jaata. Pyaaz ke cell ya bacteria dekhne ke liye 100× ya 400× chahiye. Is liye **do lens** lagate hain: pehla (objective) cheez ko bada karke ek real image banata hai, doosra (eyepiece) us image ko magnifying glass ki tarah aur bada karke dikhata hai. **Do stage, to M ka guna.**

**Stage 1: objective.** Chhoti f ka lens (lagbhag kuch mm se kuch cm). Slide F_o ke thik bahar. Image real, ulti aur badi, tube ke andar banti hai: ye **intermediate image I₁** hai.
**Stage 2: eyepiece.** I₁ ko eyepiece ke F_e ke thoda **andar** rakhte hain. Eyepiece wahi karta hai jo Part A mein magnifying glass: **virtual, seedhi (I₁ ke hisaab se), badi image I₂**. I₂ aankh ko D par ya infinity par dikhti hai.

> **Total magnification = m_o × m_e**  (objective ka m aur eyepiece ka m; sign ka matlab ulti image)

Objective ki image **ulti** hai aur eyepiece usse seedha rakhta hai, to final image cheez ke hisaab se **ulti** hai. Isi liye Aniruddh ne slide left sarkayi to cells right bhaage.

Samajhne ke liye kam doori mein banaya hai: asal mein cheez F_o ke bilkul bahar hoti hai.

### Example 4: Ek microscope ke numbers

Ek microscope ke objective ki f_o = 1 cm hai aur slide us se 1.1 cm par hai. Eyepiece ki f_e = 2.5 cm hai, aur I₂ aankh ke liye D = 25 cm par banti hai. Cell ki lambai lagbhag 0.05 mm hai. Total magnification, I₁ ki jagah, aur dono lens ke beech ki doori nikalo.

Sign ke saath: objective: f_o = +1 cm, u = −1.1 cm (real object).
1/v = 1/f_o + 1/u = 1/(1 cm) − 1/(1.1 cm) = 1/11 cm⁻¹, to v = **+11 cm** (real, I₁ objective se 11 cm par).
m_o = v ÷ u = (+11 cm) ÷ (−1.1 cm) = **−10** (ulti, 10 guna badi).
Eyepiece: f_e = +2.5 cm, I₂ D par, to v = −25 cm (virtual).
m_e = 1 + D ÷ f_e = 1 + 25 ÷ 2.5 = **+11**. I₁ eyepiece se u = −D f_e ÷ (D + f_e) = −25/11 cm = **−2.27 cm** par hai.
Total m = m_o × m_e = (−10) × (+11) = **−110** (ulti, 110 guna badi).
Cell ki image 0.05 mm × 110 = **5.5 mm** ki dikhegi.
Dono lens ke beech ki doori = 11 cm + 2.27 cm = **13.27 cm**, yaani lagbhag 13.3 cm.

Jaanch: objective: 1/v − 1/u = 1/(11 cm) + 1/(1.1 cm) = 0.0909 + 0.9091 = 1.000 cm⁻¹ = 1/f_o ✅. Eyepiece: 1/(−25) − 1/(−2.27) = −0.04 + 0.44 = 0.40 cm⁻¹ = 1/(2.5 cm) ✅. Total negative: image ulti ✅. Eyepiece wahi f = 2.5 cm, jo Example 2 ka loupe tha: **eyepiece asal mein ek loupe hi hai!**

### Lab mein microscope: school mein teacher ke saath

- Microscope ko seedhi, hilti na hui mez par rakho; lens ko **ungli se mat chhuo**.
- Roshni ke liye teacher bataya hua **lamp ya built-in bulb** lo. **Mirror ko kabhi Suraj ki taraf mat modo**: Suraj ki roshni lens se ikattha hokar aankh ko nuksaan pahuncha sakti hai.
- Pehle kam M wala objective lo. Coarse knob se tube ko upar karke slide ko focus karo; **neeche karte waqt microscope ko side se dekho**, taaki lens slide se na takraye. Phir fine knob.
- Electron microscope: light ki jagah electrons ki beam, aur M lagbhag lakhon guna tak. Isse virus jaisi cheezein dikhti hain. School lab mein nahi hota.

## Wapas dukaan par aur lab mein

**Loupe ka raaz:** ghadi-saaz ne cheez (gear) ko loupe ke F₁ (f = 2.5 cm) ke thik paas, **lagbhag 2.5 cm par** rakha. Cheez aankh ke itne paas thi ki **bina loupe ke** dhundhli hoti (Part A: 25 cm se paas aankh focus nahi kar sakti), par loupe ne image ko door (D ya infinity) banaya, isliye aankh ko aaram se saaf dikhi. Aniruddh jab ghadi ko 5 cm par laya, to aankh ko zaroori extra power 1 ÷ 0.05 m = 20 D thi, jabki wo sirf lagbhag +4 D de sakti hai. Isliye dhundhla.

**Lab ka raaz:** microscope mein 2 stage hain. Objective 10 guna badi real image banata hai, eyepiece usse 11 guna aur: total 110 (Example 4). Picture ulti isliye ki objective ki image ulti hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti: "Magnifying glass screen par badi image banata hai"**

Demonstration: wahi f = +10 cm wala lens lo (Example 1).
- Cheez 7 cm par (F₁ ke andar): 1/v = 1/10 − 1/7 = −3/70 cm⁻¹, to **v = −23.3 cm**, m = v ÷ u = (−23.3) ÷ (−7) = **+3.33**. v negative: image **cheez ki hi taraf**, virtual, seedhi. Screen kahin bhi rakho, usse kuch nahi dikhega: rays milti hi nahi.
- Cheez 15 cm par (F₁ ke bahar): 1/v = 1/10 − 1/15 = 1/30 cm⁻¹, **v = +30 cm**, m = −2. **Real**, ulti aur badi image screen par aa jaati hai. Ye magnifying glass ka kaam nahi, **projector** wali position hai (Class 9, Position 4).
- Cheez Suraj jaisi bahut door ho: real image F₂ par, chhoti aur bahut chamakdaar: **burning glass**. Wahan kabhi bhi Suraj ki roshni ko haath, kapde ya kaagaz par focus mat karo (Class 9 ki savdhani).

**Sahi:** magnifying glass = cheez F₁ ke **andar**, image **virtual**, aankh se dekhi jaati hai. Real image (screen par) ke liye cheez F₁ ke bahar chahiye.

## Quick Recap

- Cheez paas aane par visual angle aur retina ki image bada hoti hai, par aankh D = lagbhag 25 cm se paas focus nahi karti (+4 D accommodation).
- **Magnifying glass (simple microscope):** convex lens, cheez F₁ ke andar, image virtual, seedhi, badi. Cheez ko aankh ke paas laane ki ijaazat deta hai.
- **M = 1 + D ÷ f** (image D par), **M = D ÷ f** (image infinity par, aankh relaxed). f cm mein, D = 25 cm.
- Image D par ho to u = −D f ÷ (D + f). f jitni chhoti, M utna zyada, par lens chhota aur zyada curved.
- **Compound microscope:** objective (chhoti f) real, ulti, badi I₁ banata hai; eyepiece (loupe ki tarah) I₁ ko virtual badi I₂ mein badalta hai. **Total M = m_o × m_e**, final image cheez ke hisaab se ulti.
- Lab safety: lens ko mat chhuo, mirror kabhi Suraj ki taraf nahi, focus karte waqt tube neeche karte hue side se dekho. Electron microscope: lakhon guna M.
- Magnifying glass real image nahi banata. Screen par real, badi image projector wali position hai (cheez F₁ ke bahar). Suraj ki roshni lens se kisi cheez par kabhi focus mat karo.

## Practice Questions

Take D = 25 cm. Use the sign convention from the lesson.

1. A loupe has a focal length of 4 cm. Find its power and its magnifying power when the image is formed (a) at the near point and (b) at infinity.
2. A magnifying glass of focal length 6.25 cm is used with the image formed at the near point. Where must the object be placed? What is the magnifying power?
3. A magnifier has a focal length of 12.5 cm. Find its power in dioptres and its magnifying power for an image at the near point and for an image at infinity. Is it suitable for a watchmaker who needs at least 6×? Give a reason.
4. A convex lens of focal length 8 cm is used as a magnifier with the object 6.4 cm from the lens. Find the image distance and the linear magnification. State whether the image is real or virtual, and whether a person with a near point of 25 cm can see it clearly.
5. A jeweller wants a loupe with a magnifying power of 16 when the image is at infinity. Find the focal length and the power. What is its magnifying power if the image is at the near point instead?
6. A compound microscope has an objective of magnification 10 and an eyepiece of magnification 15. Find the total magnification. A cell of length 0.04 mm is viewed. What is the length of its final image?
7. In the microscope of Question 6 the eyepiece is replaced by another of magnification 20. What is the new total magnification? What eyepiece magnification would be needed for a total of 240 with the same objective?
8. The eyepiece of a microscope gives a magnification of 15 when the final image is at infinity. Find its focal length and its power. Compare the power with that of a whole human eye.
9. Say true or false and give a one-line reason.
   (a) A magnifying glass is a concave lens.
   (b) For the same lens, the magnifying power for an image at the near point is greater than for an image at infinity.
   (c) The objective of a compound microscope forms a virtual image.
   (d) A magnifying glass can form a sharp image on a screen when the object is inside F₁.
   (e) The final image in a compound microscope is inverted compared with the object.
10. A watch gear is held 10 cm from the eye of a young person whose accommodation is +4 D. How much extra power does the eye need for the gear? Can the eye supply it? What is the nearest distance at which the eye can focus?
11. Two thin lenses of focal lengths 8 cm and 12.5 cm are kept in contact and used as a magnifier. Find the combined power, the focal length and the magnifying power for an image at the near point.
12. A student says, "To see more light in the microscope I will turn the mirror towards the Sun." Explain why this is dangerous and what should be done instead.

### Answers

1. f = +4 cm = +0.04 m. P = 1 ÷ 0.04 m = **+25 D**.
   (a) M = 1 + D ÷ f = 1 + 25 ÷ 4 = **7.25**. (b) M = D ÷ f = 25 ÷ 4 = **6.25**.
   Jaanch: 7.25 − 6.25 = 1 ✅

2. Sign ke saath: f = +6.25 cm, v = −25 cm (virtual, near point par).
   1/u = 1/v − 1/f = −1/25 − 1/6.25 = −0.04 − 0.16 = −0.20 cm⁻¹, to **u = −5 cm** (cheez lens se 5 cm par, F₁ = 6.25 cm ke andar).
   M = v ÷ u = (−25) ÷ (−5) = **+5**. Formula: 1 + 25 ÷ 6.25 = 1 + 4 = 5 ✅
   Jaanch: 1/v − 1/u = −0.04 + 0.20 = 0.16 cm⁻¹ = 1/(6.25 cm) ✅

3. f = +12.5 cm = +0.125 m. P = 1 ÷ 0.125 m = **+8 D**.
   M (near point) = 1 + 25 ÷ 12.5 = 1 + 2 = **3**. M (infinity) = 25 ÷ 12.5 = **2**.
   Watchmaker ko kam se kam 6× chahiye, aur ye lens sirf 3× (ya 2×) deta hai: **suitable nahi**. Use f ≈ 5 cm ya kam chahiye.
   Jaanch: 3 − 2 = 1 ✅

4. Sign ke saath: f = +8 cm, u = −6.4 cm.
   1/v = 1/f + 1/u = 1/8 − 1/6.4 = 0.125 − 0.15625 = −0.03125 cm⁻¹, to **v = −32 cm**.
   m = v ÷ u = (−32) ÷ (−6.4) = **+5**.
   v negative: image **virtual**, cheez ki taraf, seedhi, 5 guna badi. Image 32 cm par hai, jo 25 cm se door hai, to near point 25 cm wala vyakti use **saaf dekh sakta hai**.
   Jaanch: 1/v − 1/u = −0.03125 + 0.15625 = 0.125 cm⁻¹ = 1/8 ✅. Cheez F₁ = 8 cm ke andar hai ✅

5. M = D ÷ f = 16, to f = 25 ÷ 16 = **1.5625 cm** (lagbhag 1.56 cm) = 0.015625 m.
   P = 1 ÷ 0.015625 m = **+64 D**.
   Image near point par: M = 1 + D ÷ f = 1 + 16 = **17**.
   Jaanch: 25 ÷ 1.5625 = 16 ✅

6. Total m = m_o × m_e = 10 × 15 = **150** (final image ulti, 150 guna badi).
   Image ki lambai = 0.04 mm × 150 = **6 mm**.
   Jaanch: 6 mm ÷ 0.04 mm = 150 ✅

7. Naya total = 10 × 20 = **200**. Total 240 ke liye m_e = 240 ÷ 10 = **24**.
   Jaanch: 10 × 24 = 240 ✅

8. Image infinity par: M = D ÷ f_e = 15, to f_e = 25 ÷ 15 = **1.67 cm** (5/3 cm) = 0.01667 m.
   P = 1 ÷ 0.01667 m = **+60 D**. Poori insaani aankh ki power bhi lagbhag +60 D hai: ye akela lens poori aankh jitna tez modta hai.
   Jaanch: 1 ÷ (+60 D) = 0.01667 m = 1.67 cm ✅

9. (a) **False** ❌. Magnifying glass convex lens hota hai (cheez F₁ ke andar).
   (b) **True** ✅. M = 1 + D ÷ f, D ÷ f se 1 zyada.
   (c) **False** ❌. Objective **real**, ulti, badi image banata hai.
   (d) **False** ❌. F₁ ke andar ki image virtual hai, screen par nahi aati.
   (e) **True** ✅. Objective ulti, eyepiece usse seedhi rakhta hai: cheez ke hisaab se final ulti.

10. d = 10 cm = 0.10 m. Zaroori extra power = 1 ÷ d = 1 ÷ 0.10 m = **10 D**. Aankh sirf **+4 D** de sakti hai, to **nahi**: 10 D > 4 D, gear dhundhla. Sabse paas d = 1 ÷ 4 D = 0.25 m = **25 cm**.
    Jaanch: 1 ÷ 10 D = 0.1 m ✅, 10 D − 4 D = 6 D ki kami ✅

11. f₁ = 8 cm = 0.08 m, to P₁ = 1 ÷ 0.08 = +12.5 D. f₂ = 12.5 cm = 0.125 m, to P₂ = +8 D.
    Powers jud jaati hain: P = 12.5 D + 8 D = **+20.5 D**. f = 1 ÷ 20.5 D = 0.04878 m = **4.88 cm**.
    M = 1 + D ÷ f = 1 + 25 cm ÷ 4.878 cm = **6.125** (= 49/8).
    Jaanch: 1 + 0.25 m × 20.5 D = 1 + 5.125 = 6.125 ✅

12. Mirror Suraj ki roshni ko lens ke raaste aankh tak bhej dega, aur lens us roshni ko ikattha karke retina ko **gambhir nuksaan** pahuncha sakta hai. Kabhi nahi. Teacher ke bataye **lamp ya built-in bulb** use karo, ya Suraj se door ki saaf, halki khidki ki roshni; aur kam roshni ho to ujaala badhao, mirror Suraj ki taraf nahi modo.
