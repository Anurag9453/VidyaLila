## Part A: Aankhon ki dekhbhaal, ab numbers ke saath

### Jitna paas, utna zor

Topic 1 mein dekha tha: object d metre par ho to aankh ko extra power 1 ÷ d dioptre chahiye, aur jawan aankh lagbhag +4 D tak de sakti hai. Wahin 40 cm aur 30 cm par padhne ka reserve nikala tha. Ab do aur sthitiyan.

### Example 1: Phone 28 cm par

Ek jawan aankh (accommodation lagbhag +4 D) phone ko 28 cm par rakh kar dekh rahi hai. Kitni power lagti hai, reserve kitna bachta hai?

**Given:** A = +4 D, d = 28 cm = 0.28 m (u = −0.28 m, real object)
**Find:** zaroori extra power aur reserve
**Formula:** zaroori power = 1 ÷ d, reserve = A − zaroori power
**Substitution:** 1 ÷ 0.28 m = **3.57 D**, reserve = 4 − 3.57 = **0.43 D**.
**Answer:** lagbhag 3.6 D lagti hai aur sirf lagbhag 0.43 D bachta hai.
**Jaanch:** 3.57 + 0.43 = 4.00 ✅. Doori hamesha metre mein, tabhi D milta hai ✅.

Muscles lagbhag poori kshamata par chal rahe hain, isliye der tak aise dekhne se aankhein thakti hain. Isi liye **padhne ki doori lagbhag 30 se 40 cm** rakhne ko kehte hain.

### Example 2: 20 feet ka raaz

20 feet = 20 × 0.3048 m = 6.096 m, jise hum lagbhag **6 m** lete hain. Door ki cheez dekhte waqt aankh ko kitna extra zor chahiye?

**Given:** d = 6 m (u = −6 m)
**Find:** zaroori extra power
**Formula:** zaroori power = 1 ÷ d
**Substitution:** 1 ÷ 6 m = **0.17 D**. Jawan aankh ki +4 D kshamata ka 0.1667 ÷ 4 = 0.0417, yaani lagbhag **4 percent**.
**Answer:** 6 m par aankh ko lagbhag 0.17 D chahiye: focusing muscle lagbhag poora **aaram** mein.
**Jaanch:** 6.096 m lene par 1 ÷ 6.096 = 0.164 D: lagbhag wahi ✅.

Yaani 6 m ya usse door dekhne par aankh ka focusing muscle aise aaram karta hai jaise infinity dekh rahi ho. 20 second ka ye break us muscle ko saans deta hai. Yahi wajah hai ki Snellen chart bhi lagbhag 6 m par lagta hai: wahan aankh ko accommodation nahi karni padti, to **sirf aankh ka asli number** naapa jaata hai.

## Part B: Kab kaun sa formula?

Girish ki dikkat ab hal karte hain: saare formulas ek chart mein, ek hi sawaal ke saath: **"mere paas kya diya hai, aur kya poochha hai?"**

| Sthiti | Formula | Dhyan rakho | Topic |
|---|---|---|---|

| Image ki jagah | 1/v − 1/u = 1/f | Sign convention likho: u negative | Class 9 |
| Image kitni badi | m = v ÷ u | m negative: ulti | Class 9 |
| Chipke hue lenses | P = P₁ + P₂ | Powers jodo, f nahi | Class 9, Topic 3 |

To seedha lens formula, hamesha sign ke saath.

## Part C: Sab topics ek saath: mixed problems

### Example 3: Prescription, phir contact lens ka note (Topic 2)

Ek myopic vyakti ka far point aankh se 48 cm hai. (a) Contact lens ki power aur prescription (nearest 0.25 D). (b) Agar chashma aankh se 2 cm aage ho to?

**Given:** far point 48 cm aankh se. Chashma aankh se 2 cm aage.
**Find:** P (contact lens) aur P (chashma).
**Formula:** 1/f = 1/v − 1/u, P = 1 ÷ f. Sign convention: u = −∞ (door ki cheez), virtual image v negative, concave lens ka f negative.
**Substitution:** (a) contact lens aankh par: v = −48 cm. f = −48 cm = −0.48 m. P = 1 ÷ (−0.48 m) = −2.08 D, nearest 0.25: **−2.00 D**.
(b) Chashma 2 cm aage hai, to far point lens se 48 − 2 = 46 cm door: v = −46 cm, f = −0.46 m. P = 1 ÷ (−0.46 m) = −2.17 D, nearest 0.25: **−2.25 D**.
**Answer:** contact lens lagbhag −2.00 D, chashma lagbhag −2.25 D.
**Jaanch:** dono minus (concave) ✅. Chashme ki image lens se 46 cm par, contact wali 48 cm se paas, isliye chashme ka number thoda bada ✅ (Topic 2 ka niyam).

### Example 4: Padhne wale ke do lens (Topic 2 aur 3)

Ek 58 saal ke teacher ka door ka lens −3.00 D hai. Ye chashma pehen kar bhi unka near point 65 cm hai (accommodation kam hui). Wo 25 cm par kitaab padhna chahte hain. Reading add kitna ho? Bifocal ke neeche ke hisse ki kul power kya hogi?

**Given:** P₁ = −3.00 D, near point (chashma pehen kar) 65 cm, kitaab 25 cm par.
**Find:** reading add P₂ aur neeche ke hisse ki power P.
**Formula:** 1/f = 1/v − 1/u, P = 1 ÷ f, P = P₁ + P₂ (chipke lenses). Sign convention: u = −25 cm, v = −65 cm (virtual image near point par), convex lens ka f positive.
**Substitution:** 1/f = 1/(−65 cm) − 1/(−25 cm) = −0.01538 + 0.04 = 0.02462 cm⁻¹. f = **+40.6 cm** = 0.406 m. P₂ = 1 ÷ 0.406 m = +2.46 D, nearest 0.25: **+2.50 D**.
Neeche ka hissa: P = P₁ + P₂ = (−3.00 D) + (+2.50 D) = **−0.50 D**.
**Answer:** reading add +2.50 D (convex), bifocal ke neeche ka hissa −0.50 D (ab bhi concave, par bahut kam).
**Jaanch:** +2.50 D ke liye f = +40 cm: 1/v = 1/40 − 1/25 = (5 − 8)/200 = −3/200 cm⁻¹, v = −66.7 cm. Ye near point 65 cm se **door** hai, to image saaf dikhti hai ✅. −3.00 + 2.50 = −0.50 ✅.

### Example 5: Loupe aur telescope, M ki tulna (Topic 4 aur 5)

Ek loupe ki f = 3 cm hai. Ek telescope ka objective f_o = 108 cm aur eyepiece f_e = 9 cm. (a) Loupe ka M, dono tarah. (b) Telescope ka M aur lambai. (c) Kya dono M seedhe tulna ke layak hain?

**Given:** loupe f = +3 cm, D = 25 cm. Telescope f_o = 108 cm, f_e = 9 cm.
**Find:** M (loupe), M aur length (telescope).
**Formula:** M = 1 + D ÷ f ya D ÷ f; M = f_o ÷ f_e, length = f_o + f_e.
**Substitution:** loupe: image infinity par M = 25 ÷ 3 = **8.33**; image D par M = 1 + 25 ÷ 3 = **9.33**. Power P = 1 ÷ 0.03 m = **+33.3 D**.
Telescope: M = 108 ÷ 9 = **12**; length = 108 + 9 = **117 cm**.
**Answer:** loupe 8.3× (ya 9.3×), telescope 12×, lambai 117 cm.
**Jaanch:** M ki koi unit nahi, sirf ek anupaat ✅. Telescope mein f_o > f_e, isliye M > 1 ✅.
(c) **Nahi.** Dono "kon ka anupaat" hain, par alag base par: loupe ka M cheez ko **25 cm par rakhne** ke mukable hai, telescope ka M cheez ko **jahan wo hai wahin dekhne** ke mukable hai. Isliye "12 bada hai 9.3 se" ka matlab ye nahi ki telescope har kaam mein behtar hai: loupe chhoti cheez paas laata hai, telescope door ki cheez ka kon bada karta hai.

### Example 6: Camera ke lens se ek insaan ki image (Topic 1 aur 5)

Ek camera ke lens ki f = 35 mm hai. 1.7 m lamba insaan lens se 1.4 m (1400 mm) par khada hai. Image kahan banegi, kitni badi, aur sensor ko lens se f ke mukable kitna door rakhna padega?

**Given:** f = +35 mm, u = −1400 mm (real object), h = 1.7 m.
**Find:** v, m, h′, aur v − f.
**Formula:** 1/v − 1/u = 1/f, m = v ÷ u, h′ = m × h. Sign convention: u negative, f convex positive, real image v positive.
**Substitution:** 1/v = 1/f + 1/u = 1/35 − 1/1400 = (40 − 1)/1400 = 39/1400 mm⁻¹. v = 1400 ÷ 39 = **+35.9 mm**.
m = v ÷ u = (+35.9 mm) ÷ (−1400 mm) = **−0.0256**. h′ = −0.0256 × 1.7 m = **−0.0436 m = −43.6 mm**.
v − f = 35.9 − 35 = **0.9 mm**.
**Answer:** image lagbhag 35.9 mm par, ulti, lagbhag 43.6 mm lambi. Focus ke liye lens ko lagbhag 0.9 mm aage khisakna padta hai.
**Jaanch:** v positive (real), m negative (ulti) ✅. Door ki cheez ke liye v lagbhag f hota hai, yahan 35.9 mm lagbhag 35 mm ✅. Aankh mein (Topic 1) ye kaam lens khud mota karke karta hai: 1/f badhakar.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Dioptre nikalne ke liye f cm mein hi daal do"**

Demonstration: ek concave lens, f = −36 cm. Galti se P = 1 ÷ (−36) = −0.0278 "D". Ye to lagbhag zero power ka shishe ka tukda hai. Sahi: f = −0.36 m, P = 1 ÷ (−0.36 m) = **−2.78 D**, prescription −2.75 D. 100 guna ka farak! Optician ko galat number ka chashma banane par ye hi hota hai: lens sahi lagta hai par aankh ko koi madad nahi milti.
**Sahi:** P = 1 ÷ f mein f **hamesha metre** mein.

**Galti 2: "Sign of u bhool gaye"**

Demonstration: ek convex lens, f = +30 cm, cheez 12 cm par (F₁ ke andar). Sahi: u = −12 cm, 1/v = 1/30 − 1/12 = (2 − 5)/60 = −3/60, **v = −20 cm**: virtual, cheez ki hi taraf, badi (m = +1.67). Agar u = +12 cm likh do: 1/v = 1/30 + 1/12 = 7/60, v = +8.57 cm: ek **real** image jo kabhi banti nahi, kyunki F₁ ke andar rakhi cheez real image deti hi nahi (Topic 4 ki galti dekho). Jab jawab asambhav aaye to sign pe wapas jao.
**Sahi:** u hamesha negative (real object), phir hi jawab ka sign padho.

**Galti 3: "Retina par image seedhi banti hai"**

Demonstration: Topic 1 ke tarah aankh ke lens ki jagah v = +2.3 cm = +0.023 m (real image, retina) aur 1.5 m ke bachche ko 8 m par rakho (u = −8 m). m = v ÷ u = 0.023 ÷ (−8) = **−0.002875**, h′ = −0.002875 × 1.5 m = **−4.3 mm**. Minus: **ulti** image, aur sirf lagbhag 4.3 mm ki. Seedha hum isliye dekhte hain ki dimaag ise palat deta hai.
**Ghar par adult ke saath:** ek magnifying glass lo. Khidki (jo door hai) ki taraf mukh karke kaagaz ya safed deewar par uski image saaf karo: khidki ki frame ulti dikhegi. **Suraj ki taraf lens kabhi nahi.** Aankh ka lens wahi karta hai.
**Sahi:** retina par image real, ulti aur chhoti hoti hai.

**Galti 4: "Telescope aur microscope ek hi instrument ke seedhe-ulte roop hain"**

Demonstration: telescope ka f_o = 108 cm, f_e = 9 cm (Example 5). Uski M = 12 (door ki cheez ka kon 12 guna). Agar ise ulta pakdo (aankh objective ki taraf), to f_e objective ban jaata hai aur f_o eyepiece: M = 9 ÷ 108 = **1/12**, yaani door ki cheez 12 guna **chhoti** dikhegi. Aisa ulta karke binocular se door ka ped dekhne par wo chhota dikhta hai, par ise **microscope nahi** kehte, kyunki microscope paas ki chhoti cheez ko bada dikhata hai.
Asli farak: microscope ka objective **chhoti f** ka hai (kuch mm se kuch cm) aur cheez F_o ke thik bahar rakhi jaati hai, image I₁ door aur real hoti hai. Telescope ka objective **lambi f** ka hai aur cheez infinity par, to I₁ **F_o par hi** banti hai (common focus). Alag kaam, alag hisaab: microscope M = m_o × m_e, telescope M = f_o ÷ f_e.
**Ghar par adult ke saath:** binocular ya telescope ko ulta karke door ki imarat dekho: wo chhoti dikhegi. **Suraj ki taraf kabhi nahi.**
**Sahi:** dono mein do convex lens hain, par telescope mein objective lamba aur eyepiece chhota hai, microscope mein dono chhote, aur dono ke kaam alag hain.

## Quick Recap

- **Aankh ki dekhbhaal:** zaroori power = 1 ÷ d. 28 cm par lagbhag 3.57 D, 6 m par lagbhag 0.17 D. Padhne ki doori 30 se 40 cm, 20-20-20 se focusing muscle ko aaram. Screen ki blue light ka seedha nuksaan pakka nahi, par lambi doori-kaam aur kam palak jhapakna dikkat hai.
- **Bachaav:** aankh nahi masalna, welding aur lab mein goggles, UV wale sunglasses, **Suraj ya laser ko kabhi aankh se dekhna nahi**, kisi instrument se bhi nahi.
- **Formula chart:** P = 1 ÷ f (f metre mein); 1/v − 1/u = 1/f (u negative); myopia f = − far point; hypermetropia/presbyopia u = −25 cm, v = − near point; chipke lens: powers jodo.
- **Instruments:** loupe M = 1 + D ÷ f ya D ÷ f; microscope M = m_o × m_e; telescope M = f_o ÷ f_e, length f_o + f_e; camera lens formula, door ki cheez ke liye v lagbhag f.
- **Chaar galtiyan:** f cm mein power, u ka sign bhoolna, retina par seedhi image, telescope = ulta microscope. Sahi: f metre mein, u negative, retina par ulti image, aur telescope aur microscope alag instruments.

## Practice Questions

Take D = 25 cm. Use the sign convention from the lesson.

1. A person cannot see clearly beyond 2.5 m. Find the focal length and power of the lens that corrects this and the prescription (nearest 0.25 D). Is the lens convex or concave?
2. The person of Question 1 takes off his glasses and uses a loupe of focal length 5 cm with the image at the near point. Find the power of the loupe, the object distance and the magnifying power. Why can he not use the loupe with the image at infinity without glasses?
3. A telescope in normal adjustment is 84 cm long and has a magnifying power of 13. Find the focal lengths of the objective and the eyepiece, and the power of each lens.
4. A reading add of +2.25 D lets a person with a normal far point read at 25 cm. Find the focal length of the lens, the person's near point and the accommodation that remains in the eye. Check that this accommodation plus the add equals 4 D.
5. A camera lens of focal length 50 mm focuses a 1.6 m tall person standing 2 m away. Find the image distance in mm, the distance by which the lens moves from its infinity position, and the height of the image with its sign.
6. A student holds a phone 20 cm from her eyes. (a) What accommodation is needed? (b) Can a young eye with +4 D supply it? (c) At what distance is the eye's reserve 2 D?
7. Find the accommodation needed to look at a bird 12 m away and at a notice board 2 m away. Which is closer to a fully relaxed eye? What part of +4 D is each?
8. The eyepiece of the telescope in Question 3 is taken out and used as a loupe. Find its power and its magnifying power for an image at the near point and at infinity.
9. A myopic person has a far point of 26 cm from the eye. Find the lens power (a) for a contact lens and (b) for spectacles worn 2 cm in front of the eye, each to the nearest 0.25 D.
10. A person has a far point of 3.2 m. Find the exact power of the lens that corrects this, and the prescription to the nearest 0.25 D. Why is it not worth using a much stronger lens?
11. A person wears distance glasses of −1.75 D. With them on, his near point is 85 cm. Find the exact reading add needed to read at 25 cm, and the power of the lower part of his bifocal. What happens if the optician rounds the add to +2.75 D?
12. A convex lens of focal length +30 cm is used with an object 12 cm from it. Find the correct image distance. A student writes u = +12 cm by mistake: what does she get, and why can you tell that it is wrong?
13. Say true or false and give a one-line reason.
    (a) In P = 1 ÷ f, f may be put in centimetres.
    (b) In a telescope in normal adjustment the objective has the longer focal length.
    (c) 20 feet is about 6 m.
    (d) The image formed on the retina is erect.
    (e) The telescope objective and the microscope objective have the same job.
14. Explain in three lines why looking at the Sun through binoculars or a telescope, or at a laser pointer, can blind a person, and give two safe habits that follow.

### Answers

1. Far point 2.5 m. f = −far point = **−2.5 m**. P = 1 ÷ (−2.5 m) = **−0.40 D**, nearest 0.25: **−0.50 D**. f negative: **concave**.
   Sign: u = −∞, v = −2.5 m: 1/f = 1/v − 1/u = −0.4 − 0 = −0.4 m⁻¹ ✅.
   Jaanch: 1 ÷ 0.4 D = 2.5 m ✅.

2. f = +5 cm = 0.05 m, P = 1 ÷ 0.05 m = **+20 D**.
   Image D par: v = −25 cm (virtual). 1/u = 1/v − 1/f = −1/25 − 1/5 = −1/25 − 5/25 = −6/25 cm⁻¹. **u = −4.17 cm** (F₁ = 5 cm ke andar).
   M = 1 + D ÷ f = 1 + 25 ÷ 5 = **6**. Formula se: m = v ÷ u = (−25) ÷ (−4.17) = +6 ✅.
   Infinity par image nahi: uska far point 2.5 m hi hai, infinity par rakhi hui virtual image uski seema ke bahar hai, aankh use focus nahi kar sakti. Image D (25 cm) par theek hai: 25 cm ≤ 250 cm.
   Jaanch: 1/v − 1/u = −0.04 + 0.24 = 0.20 cm⁻¹ = 1/(5 cm) ✅.

3. M = f_o ÷ f_e = 13, length = f_o + f_e = 84 cm. To f_o = 13 f_e: 13 f_e + f_e = 14 f_e = 84 cm, **f_e = 6 cm**, **f_o = 78 cm**.
   Powers (f metre mein): P_o = 1 ÷ 0.78 m = **+1.28 D**, P_e = 1 ÷ 0.06 m = **+16.7 D**.
   Jaanch: 78 ÷ 6 = 13 ✅ aur 78 + 6 = 84 cm ✅. Objective dheeme, eyepiece tez: f_o ≫ f_e ✅.

4. P = +2.25 D, f = 1 ÷ 2.25 = 0.444 m = **+44.4 cm**.
   Sign: u = −25 cm = −0.25 m, 1/v = 1/f + 1/u = 2.25 − 4 = −1.75 m⁻¹, **v = −0.571 m = −57.1 cm**: near point **57.1 cm** (lagbhag).
   Bachi accommodation A = 1 ÷ 0.571 = **1.75 D**.
   Jaanch: 1.75 + 2.25 = 4.00 D ✅.

5. f = +50 mm, u = −2000 mm (real object). 1/v = 1/50 − 1/2000 = (40 − 1)/2000 = 39/2000 mm⁻¹, **v = +51.3 mm** (real, sensor par). Lens infinity par f = 50 mm par rehta, to khisakna: 51.28 − 50 = **1.28 mm** (lagbhag 1.3 mm) aage.
   m = v ÷ u = 51.28 ÷ (−2000) = −0.02564, h′ = −0.02564 × 1.6 m = **−0.0410 m = −41.0 mm**: image ulti (minus) aur lagbhag 41 mm ki.
   Jaanch: 1/51.28 + 1/2000 = 0.0195 + 0.0005 = 0.0200 = 1/50 ✅.

6. (a) d = 20 cm = 0.20 m, zaroori power = 1 ÷ 0.20 = **5 D**. (b) **Nahi**: 5 D > 4 D, kami = 1 D. (c) reserve 2 D means kaam mein 4 − 2 = 2 D, d = 1 ÷ 2 = 0.5 m = **50 cm**.
   Jaanch: sabse paas 1 ÷ 4 = 25 cm, to 20 cm usse paas ✅; 2 + 2 = 4 ✅.

7. Bird: 1 ÷ 12 = **0.083 D**, jo 4 D ka 0.083 ÷ 4 = 0.021, yaani **lagbhag 2 percent**. Board: 1 ÷ 2 = **0.5 D**, 0.5 ÷ 4 = 0.125, yaani **12.5 percent**. Bird (door wali cheez) fully relaxed aankh ke zyaada paas hai.
   Jaanch: door jaane se 1 ÷ d ghatta hai ✅.

8. f_e = 6 cm = 0.06 m, P = 1 ÷ 0.06 = **+16.7 D**. Image D par: M = 1 + 25 ÷ 6 = **5.17**. Image infinity par: M = 25 ÷ 6 = **4.17**.
   Jaanch: 5.17 − 4.17 = 1 ✅. Dhyan: ye M loupe ka M hai, telescope wale 13 se alag cheez ✅.

9. (a) Contact lens: v = −26 cm, f = −0.26 m, P = 1 ÷ (−0.26) = −3.85 D, nearest 0.25: **−3.75 D**.
   (b) Chashma 2 cm aage: far point lens se 26 − 2 = 24 cm: v = −24 cm, f = −0.24 m, P = 1 ÷ (−0.24) = −4.17 D, nearest 0.25: **−4.25 D**.
   Chashme ka number bada (−4.25 > −3.75 ka size). Sign: u = −∞, v negative, f negative ✅.
   Jaanch: |4.17| > |3.85| ✅.

10. f = −3.2 m, P = 1 ÷ (−3.2) = **−0.3125 D**, nearest 0.25: **−0.25 D** (0.3125 − 0.25 = 0.0625, jo 0.50 − 0.3125 = 0.1875 se kam). Isse bahut zyada tez lens lagaane par door ki cheez ka image far point se paas banega aur aankh ko lagataar accommodation karni padegi (Topic 2 ki galti): sir dard, thakaan.
   Jaanch: 1 ÷ 0.3125 = 3.2 m ✅.

11. Sign: u = −25 cm, v = −85 cm (virtual, near point). 1/f = 1/v − 1/u = −1/85 + 1/25 = −0.01176 + 0.04 = 0.02824 cm⁻¹. f = **+35.4 cm**, P₂ = 1 ÷ 0.354 m = **+2.82 D**.
    Neeche ka hissa: P = −1.75 + 2.82 = **+1.07 D** (ab convex).
    Agar add +2.75 D kar do: 1/v = 2.75 − 4 = −1.25 m⁻¹, v = −0.80 m = −80 cm. Ye near point 85 cm se **paas** hai, to saaf nahi dikhega. Optician is liye +3.00 D lega: v = −1 m = −100 cm, ✅ aur neeche ka hissa −1.75 + 3.00 = +1.25 D.
    Jaanch: 1 ÷ 0.354 = 2.82 D, aur 4 − 1.176 = 2.82 D (1 ÷ 0.85 = 1.176) ✅.

12. Sahi: f = +30 cm, u = −12 cm. 1/v = 1/30 − 1/12 = (2 − 5)/60 = −3/60, **v = −20 cm** (virtual, cheez ki taraf, m = +1.67).
    Galti: u = +12 cm: 1/v = 1/30 + 1/12 = 7/60, v = +8.57 cm, yaani real image. Par F₁ ke andar (12 < 30) rakhi cheez real image deti hi nahi (Topic 4), isliye ye jawab **asambhav** hai.
    Jaanch: 1/(−20) − 1/(−12) = −0.05 + 0.0833 = 0.0333 = 1/30 ✅.

13. (a) **False** ❌: f metre mein hi lena hai, warna P 100 guna kam nikalta hai.
    (b) **True** ✅: f_o lamba, f_e chhota, M = f_o ÷ f_e > 1.
    (c) **True** ✅: 20 × 0.3048 = 6.096 m.
    (d) **False** ❌: image real aur ulti hai.
    (e) **False** ❌: microscope objective chhoti cheez ki badi real image banata hai (chhoti f); telescope objective door ki cheez ki real image F_o par banata hai (lambi f).

14. Lens ya mirror Suraj ya laser ki saari roshni ko retina ke ek chhote se point par ikattha kar deta hai. Wahan roshni ki shakti itni ho sakti hai ki retina jal jaye, aur nuksaan aksar dard ke bina aur hamesha ke liye hota hai.
