# Topic 1: Aankh Ek Asli Lens System: Numbers Ke Saath

Chapter 3 mein humne dekha tha ki Suraj hi zyaadatar energy-strot ki jad hai. Is chapter mein hum usi Suraj ki roshni ko pakadte hain, us waqt jab wo hamari **aankh** mein ghusti hai. Class 9 mein aankh ke hisse, uska image banana, near point aur far point, aur do bade dosh (myopia, hypermetropia) hum padh chuke hain. Ye chapter wo sab dohrata nahi: ye aankh ko ek **asli optical instrument** ki tarah naapta hai, **numbers ke saath**, aur phir wahan tak jaata hai jahan aankh akeli kaafi nahi: chashme, magnifying glass, microscope aur telescope. Pehle topic ka kaam: aankh ke andar ke asli numbers.

## Almaari se nikla purana camera

Mr. Palande ne nani ke zamane ki almaari ka sabse neeche wala khana kholna shuru kiya, aur kapde mein lipta ek purana **film camera** nikla. Unki bhanji Sampada chhutti mein aayi hui thi, aur uske haath mein school ki copy khuli thi jisme aankh ka naksha bana tha.

Mr. Palande ne camera ka lens ghumakar kholkar andar jhaanka. "Ye dekh Sampada. Ye gol chhed **aperture** hai, roshni isi se andar aati hai. Ye parda **shutter** hai, click karte hi thodi der ke liye khulta hai. Aur sabse peeche **film**: tasveer yahin banti hai."

Sampada ne apni copy ke naksha par ungli rakhi. "Mama, ye to meri copy ka aankh jaisa hai! Pupil aperture hai, retina film hai. Par shutter kaun hai? Aur ek baat aur: camera mein focus karne ke liye lens ko aage-peeche ghumate hain. Meri aankh ka lens to kahin ghumta nahi. Phir focus kaise hota hai?"

"Achha sawaal," Mr. Palande hanse. Sampada ne ek aur poochha: "Class 9 mein padha tha ki cornea sabse zyada bending karta hai aur lens bas thoda sa. Par 'zyada' aur 'thoda' kitna? Kya iska koi number hai?"

Mr. Palande ne camera mez par rakha. "Number hai, Sampada, aur nikalna bhi aata hai. Kyunki aankh ek **asli lens system** hai, aur lens ka hisaab hum dioptre mein kar sakte hain. Chal, aankh ko camera ki tarah khol kar dekhte hain."

## Class 9 se recall (paanch line)

1. Aankh ka cornea roshni ko sabse zyada mod'ta hai, eye lens baariki se focus karta hai; image retina par **real aur ulta** banta hai ([light-refraction-part7.md](../../../class9/physics/light-refraction/light-refraction-part7.md), Part E; aankh ke hisson ka Figure 7.5 wahin hai, hum use dobara nahi banayenge).

3. Lens formula 1/v − 1/u = 1/f, magnification m = v ÷ u, power P = 1 ÷ f (f metre mein) aur "do lens chipke hue to powers jud jaati hain" Class 9 ke [light-refraction-part6.md](../../../class9/physics/light-refraction/light-refraction-part6.md) se.

**Sign convention (Class 9, har example mein yahi):** light left se right jaati hai. Real object ki doori u **negative**, real image ki doori v **positive**, convex lens ka f **positive**, concave lens ka f **negative**.

## Part A: Aankh ke asli numbers

Aankh ki poori power ek hi lens ki nahi, **do hisson** ki milli hui hai. Lagbhag numbers (har ek real-world value "lagbhag" hai):

- **Cornea:** lagbhag **+40 D**
- **Eye lens (aaraam ki halat mein, door dekhte waqt):** lagbhag **+20 D**
- **Poori aankh:** lagbhag **+60 D**
- Eye lens se retina tak ki doori: lagbhag **2.3 cm**

Do lens lagbhag chipke hue hon to unki powers jud jaati hain (Class 9 ka Example 14), isliye cornea aur lens ki powers jodkar kul power milti hai.

### Example 1: Cornea aur lens ka hissa

Cornea +40 D aur lens +20 D. Poori aankh ki power aur har ek ka hissa batao.

**Given:** P₁ (cornea) = +40 D, P₂ (lens) = +20 D (dono convex jaise, isliye positive)
**Find:** kul P, aur cornea aur lens ka percent hissa
**Formula:** P = P₁ + P₂, hissa = (apni power ÷ kul power) × 100
**Substitution:** P = 40 + 20 = **60 D**. Cornea: 40 ÷ 60 × 100 = **66.7 percent**. Lens: 20 ÷ 60 × 100 = **33.3 percent**.
**Answer:** Kul lagbhag +60 D; cornea lagbhag do-tihai, lens lagbhag ek-tihai.
**Jaanch:** 66.7 + 33.3 = 100 percent ✅. Unit D, aur dono positive kyunki dono converge karte hain ✅.

Dekha? Class 9 ka "cornea zyada, lens thoda" ab ek number hai: **do guna**. (Ek imaandaar baat: cornea aur lens ke beech thoda faasla hai, isliye powers ka seedha jodna ek achha anuman hai, exact nahi.)

### Ek imaandaar sawaal: 1 ÷ 60 D kitna hota hai?

P = 1 ÷ f se f = 1 ÷ 60 D = 0.0167 m = lagbhag **1.7 cm**. Par retina to lagbhag **2.3 cm** door hai! Do numbers match kyon nahi karte?

Wajah: lens formula mein humne maana tha ki lens ke dono taraf **hawa** hai. Aankh ke andar hawa nahi hai: wahan paani jaisa taral (aqueous humour) aur jelly jaisa vitreous humour hai. Taral mein roshni ki bending ka hisaab badal jaata hai. Isliye +60 D ek **effective power** hai: ek kaam chalau number jo aankh ke asli kaam ko lagbhag sahi bata deta hai. Ek aur baat: Class 9 ke Example 8 mein retina ki doori 2 cm lekar lagbhag 50 D mila tha; wo bhi isi tarah ek moti tulna thi. Is chapter mein hum sirf wahi numbers lenge jo upar likhe hain, aur **farak** (jaise accommodation ka +4 D) par bharosa karenge, jo dono hisaab mein ek jaise nikalte hain. Aage Part B mein ye dikhega.

```

lens   ████████████████████  +20 D
kul    ████████████████████████████████████████████████████████████  +60 D

```

> **Figure 1.1** – Power ke batware: har block 1 D ke barabar. Upar: door dekhte waqt cornea +40 D, lens +20 D, kul +60 D. Neeche: 25 cm par padhte waqt lens ne apni power +4 D badhai, to lens +24 D aur kul +64 D; cornea wahi +40 D. Lens badalta hai, par cornea ka bada hissa phir bhi wahi rehta hai.

### Example 2: 25 cm par padhne ke liye lens kitni power jodta hai?

Retina eye lens se v = +2.3 cm par hai. Aankh pehle door ki cheez (u = −∞) dekh rahi thi, ab 25 cm par rakhi kitaab (u = −25 cm) dekhti hai. Lens ko apni power kitni badhani padegi?

**Given:** v = +2.3 cm = +0.023 m (retina, real image, isliye positive), u = −25 cm = −0.25 m (real object, isliye negative)
**Find:** power ka badlaav ΔP
**Formula:** 1/f = 1/v − 1/u, P = 1/f (metre mein)
**Substitution:** Door ki cheez: P_door = 1/0.023 − 0 = **43.5 D**. Paas ki cheez: P_paas = 1/0.023 + 1/0.25 = 43.5 + 4.0 = **47.5 D**. ΔP = 47.5 − 43.5 = **+4.0 D**.
**Answer:** Lens ko lagbhag **+4 D** extra power jodni hoti hai.
**Jaanch:** ΔP = 1 ÷ 0.25 m = 4 D ✅. Positive hai, yaani lens aur zyada convex (mota) hua ✅. Aur dekho: 43.5 D kul +60 D se alag hai (Part A ki imaandaar baat), par **farak** +4 D hi aaya.

Isse ek bahut kaam ka niyam nikalta hai: retina ki doori fixed hai, isliye **d metre par rakhi cheez dekhne ke liye extra power = 1 ÷ d dioptre**. Ye 25 cm ke liye 4 D, 1 m ke liye 1 D hai.

Is niyam se Figure 1.1 ke neeche wale batware ki ginti: lens +20 D se +24 D hua, to kul 40 + 24 = **64 D**. Cornea ka hissa ab 40 ÷ 64 = **62.5 percent** hai, lens ka 24 ÷ 64 = **37.5 percent**. Paas dekhne par bhi cornea bada hissedaar rehta hai.

## Part B: Accommodation ko dioptre mein naapna

Class 9 mein accommodation ek kahani thi: "lens mota hota hai". Ab hum use **number** dete hain. Jawan aankh mein eye lens kul lagbhag **+4 D** tak extra power jod sakta hai. Part A ke niyam (extra power = 1 ÷ d) se ulta bhi nikalta hai: agar accommodation A dioptre hai, to sabse paas ki saaf doori (near point) **d = 1 ÷ A metre** hai.

Jawan aankh ke liye: d = 1 ÷ 4 D = 0.25 m = **25 cm**. Yahi Class 9 wala near point hai. Ab ye number umar ke saath girta hai:

| Umar | Accommodation | Near point (1 ÷ A) |
|---|---|---|
| Jawan aankh | lagbhag +4 D | 1 ÷ 4 = 0.25 m = 25 cm |
| Beech ki umar ka ek maana hua udaharan | +3 D | 1 ÷ 3 = 0.333 m = lagbhag 33 cm |
| 50 se 60 saal | lagbhag +1 D | 1 ÷ 1 = 1 m = 100 cm |

Eye lens umar ke saath kadak hota jaata hai, to wo utna mota nahi ho pata. Isse presbyopia hota hai (Class 9 mein naam padha tha); uska poora hisaab Topic 3 mein hoga. Yahan bas itna dekho ki near point 25 cm se 100 cm tak khisak jaata hai.

### Example 3: Padhne ki aaram ki doori

Jawan aankh (accommodation +4 D) kitab 40 cm par aur phir 30 cm par rakhkar padhti hai. Har baar kitni power lagti hai aur reserve kitna bachta hai? (Padhne ki aam doori 30 se 40 cm hai.)

**Given:** A = +4 D, d₁ = 40 cm = 0.4 m, d₂ = 30 cm = 0.3 m (u = −d, real object)
**Find:** zaroori extra power aur bacha hua reserve
**Formula:** zaroori power = 1 ÷ d, reserve = A − zaroori power
**Substitution:** 40 cm par: 1 ÷ 0.4 = **2.5 D**, reserve = 4 − 2.5 = **1.5 D**. 30 cm par: 1 ÷ 0.3 = **3.33 D**, reserve = 4 − 3.33 = **0.67 D**.
**Answer:** 40 cm par reserve 1.5 D, 30 cm par sirf lagbhag 0.67 D.
**Jaanch:** 2.5 + 1.5 = 4 ✅ aur 3.33 + 0.67 = 4 ✅. Jitna paas, utni zyada power, utna kam reserve.

Isliye kitab ko aankh ke bahut paas rakhkar der tak padhne se aankh thak jaati hai: muscles lagataar badi power par kaam karte hain aur reserve lagbhag khatam.

### Example 4: Accommodation se near point

Ek vyakti ki aankh mein accommodation sirf +1.6 D bachi hai. Uska near point kya hai? Kya wo 40 cm par chhapa akshar padh sakta hai?

**Given:** A = +1.6 D
**Find:** near point d
**Formula:** d = 1 ÷ A
**Substitution:** d = 1 ÷ 1.6 = **0.625 m = 62.5 cm**
**Answer:** Near point lagbhag 62.5 cm; 40 cm par rakha akshar uske near point se paas hai, isliye saaf nahi dikhega.
**Jaanch:** 40 cm par 2.5 D chahiye, aur 2.5 > 1.6 ✅. Unit: 1 ÷ D = m ✅.

## Part C: Pupil, aperture aur camera ki tulna

Sampada ke sawaal ka pehla hissa: shutter kaun? Camera aur aankh ko saath rakhte hain.

| Camera | Aankh | Kaam |
|---|---|---|
| Aperture (gol chhed) | **Pupil** (iris ke beech ka chhed) | Kitni roshni andar aaye |
| Shutter | **Palak** (aur retina ka asar tikna) | Kab aur kitni der |
| Film ya sensor | **Retina** | Image ka record |
| Lens aage-peeche khisakta hai | Lens **apna aakar** badalta hai | Focus |

Ye tulna ek madadgaar tasveer hai, exact nahi. Palak camera ke shutter jaisi nahi: wo mukhya roop se aankh ko dhulati aur bachati hai. Aankh ka "exposure" retina ke lagbhag 1/16 s tikne wale asar (persistence of vision) jaisa kuch hai.

Do baatein khaas hain. **Pehli:** camera mein focus karne ke liye lens ko **aage-peeche** ghumaya jaata hai, jisse lens aur film ki doori badalti hai. Aankh mein retina ki doori fixed hai (lagbhag 2.3 cm), isliye focus lens ka **aakar badalkar** (power badalkar) hota hai: yahi Part B ka accommodation hai.

**Doosri:** pupil ka size lagbhag **2 mm se 8 mm** tak badal sakta hai. Tez dhoop mein pupil chhota (roshni kam andar), halki roshni mein bada. Par dhyan se: roshni **chhed ke area** ke hisaab se aati hai, aur area diameter ke **square** ke saath badhta hai.

### Example 5: Pupil ka area

Sadak par tez dhoop mein pupil lagbhag 3 mm ka hai, aur kamre ki halki roshni mein lagbhag 6 mm ka. Kamre mein pupil dhoop wale se kitni guna roshni andar aane deta hai?

**Given:** d₁ = 3 mm, d₂ = 6 mm
**Find:** area ka anupat A₂ ÷ A₁
**Formula:** A = π d² ÷ 4
**Substitution:** A₁ = 3.14 × 9 ÷ 4 = **7.07 mm²**; A₂ = 3.14 × 36 ÷ 4 = **28.3 mm²**; A₂ ÷ A₁ = 28.3 ÷ 7.07 = **4**
**Answer:** 4 guna roshni (diameter sirf 2 guna, par roshni 4 guna).
**Jaanch:** (6 ÷ 3)² = 2² = 4 ✅. Unit mm² ✅. Isi tarah 2 mm se 8 mm tak diameter 4 guna, aur roshni 4² = **16 guna** badhti hai: jo pupil ka poora range hai.

### Example 6: Film kyun chalti dikhti hai

Ek purani hath se ghumayi jaane wali film 18 frames per second par chalti hai. Kya ek frame ka asar agli aane tak aankh mein tikta hai? (Persistence of vision lagbhag 1/16 s, Class 9.)

**Given:** 18 frames per second, persistence = 1/16 s = 0.0625 s
**Find:** ek frame ka samay aur tulna
**Formula:** t = 1 ÷ (frames per second)
**Substitution:** t = 1 ÷ 18 = **0.0556 s**; 0.0556 < 0.0625
**Answer:** Haan, asar tikta hai, isliye harkat saaf dikhti hai (bas thodi hi margin se).
**Jaanch:** 18 × 0.0556 s ≈ 1 s ✅. Agar frames 16 se kam hote to t > 0.0625 s hota aur parda jhilmilata ✅.

### Activity 1: Pupil ko chhota-bada hote dekhna (ghar par adult ke saath)

Ek kamre mein bas halki roshni rakho aur shishe ke saamne apni aankh dekho: pupil bada dikhega. Ab adult se kamre ki normal light (room light) on karwao aur shishe mein dekhte raho: kuch hi second mein pupil chhota hoga. **Kabhi torch, laser ya mobile ki flash seedha aankh par mat chamkao, aur Suraj ko kabhi seedha mat dekho.**

## Part D: Image ulta hai, phir seedha kyun dikhta hai?

Figure 1.2 mein ek khadi cheez ke upar wale sire se do kiranen chali hain: ek axis ke samantar, doosri lens ke beech se. Dono retina ke **neeche** wale point par milti hain.

Image **real aur ulta** hai: cheez ka upar wala sira image mein neeche aa gaya, aur cheez ka neeche wala sira axis par hai aur image ka bhi axis par.

### Example 7: Ped ka image retina par

2.3 cm ki retina wali aankh 20 m door khade 5 m ooche ped ko dekhti hai. Retina par ped ka image kitna bada aur kaisa banega?

**Given:** u = −20 m (real object, negative), v = +2.3 cm = +0.023 m (real image, positive), h = +5 m
**Find:** magnification m aur image ki oonchai h′
**Formula:** m = v ÷ u, h′ = m × h
**Substitution:** m = (+0.023 m) ÷ (−20 m) = **−0.00115**; h′ = (−0.00115) × (5 m) = **−0.00575 m = −5.75 mm**
**Answer:** Image lagbhag **5.8 mm** ooncha aur **ulta** (minus sign).
**Jaanch:** Minus sign: real image ulti hoti hai ✅. 20 m aur 5 m ka anupat 4 ÷ 1; 2.3 cm ÷ 4 (≈ 5.75 mm) ✅. Units m ÷ m = bina unit ✅.

5 m ka ped aankh mein 6 mm se bhi chhota ho jaata hai, aur wo bhi ulta. Phir hum ulta kyun nahi dekhte? Kyunki dekhna aankh ka nahi, **dimaag** ka kaam hai. Retina to signals bhejta hai; dimaag ne bachpan se in signals ko haath se chhone, girne aur chalne ke anubhav se milaya hai, isliye wo "ye upar hai" aur "ye neeche hai" ka matlab sahi jodta hai. Kuch prayogon mein logon ne kai din tak aise chashme pehne jo duniya ulti dikhate the, aur dimaag kuch dinon mein khud ko adjust kar leta tha. Isliye retina par ulta image koi kharabi nahi, ek theek chalta hua system hai.

## Part E: Rods, cones aur raat ki nazar

Retina mein do tarah ke roshni-sanvedi cell hote hain (Class 9 mein naam aaye the):

| Cell | Kitne (lagbhag) | Kahan zyada | Kaam |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rods** | lagbhag 12 crore | retina ke kinaaron par | Bahut halki roshni mein dekhna, **rang nahi** |
| **Cones** | lagbhag 60 lakh | retina ke beech ke chhote hisse mein | Tez roshni mein dekhna, **rang** aur baariki |

Cones teen kism ke hote hain: **laal-sanvedi, hare-sanvedi aur neele-sanvedi**. Aankh dimaag ko in teeno ke signals ka mel bhejti hai, aur usi mel se hum bahut saare rang pehchante hain.

**Raat ko rang kyun phike padte hain?** Halki roshni mein cones kaam nahi karte, sirf rods karte hain, aur rods rang nahi pehchante. Isliye chaandni raat mein bagiche ke phool kaale-bhoore dikhte hain.

**Cinema hall mein pehle sab andhera kyun lagta hai?** Tez roshni se achanak andhere mein jaao to pupil to kuch second mein bada ho jaata hai, par asli kaam rods ke andar ke rasayan ka halki roshni ke liye taiyaar hona hai. Isse **dark adaptation** kehte hain, aur ye lagbhag **20 se 30 minute** mein poora hota hai. Ek aur chhoti baat: rods kinaaron par zyada hain, isliye bahut halke taare ko seedha dekhne se wo kabhi kabhi gayab ho jaata hai, aur thoda **side** mein dekhne se dikh jaata hai.

Vitamin A rods ke kaam mein madad karta hai, isliye uski kami se raat ko dekhna mushkil hota hai: ye baat Class 9 ke [light-refraction-part7.md](../../../class9/physics/light-refraction/light-refraction-part7.md) ke Part H mein bhi thi.

## Part F: Blind spot ko khud pakadna

Class 9 mein blind spot ka naam aaya: retina ka wo hissa jahan optic nerve nikalti hai aur koi rod ya cone nahi hota. Aao use pakadte hain.

### Activity 2: Dot ka gayab hona (ghar par adult ke saath)

Ek safed card par ek **cross (+)** aur uske daayein taraf 8 cm door ek kaala **dot (●)** banao (Figure 1.3). Card ko haath ki lambai par pakdo. **Baayi aankh band** karo, daayi aankh se sirf cross ko ghoorte raho. Card ko dheere dheere apni taraf laao. Kisi doori par dot **gayab** ho jaayega.

```
      +                                   ●
   cross                              dot
      ◄────────────── 8 cm ───────────────►
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Blind spot ka card: cross aur dot ke beech 8 cm. Daayi aankh se cross ko dekhte hue card paas laane par dot kisi doori par gayab ho jaata hai, kyunki uska image blind spot par padta hai.

**Jaanch (hisaab se):** Blind spot dekhne ki seedh se lagbhag 15° ki disha mein hai. Dot ka kon 15° ho, to doori = 8 ÷ tan 15° = 8 ÷ 0.268 = lagbhag **30 cm**. Blind spot thoda chauda hai, isliye dot lagbhag **25 se 36 cm** ke beech kahin gayab hoga ✅.

Hum roz blind spot kyon nahi dekhte? Do wajah: dono aankhon ke blind spot alag jagah hain, isliye ek ki kami doosri puri kar deti hai; aur ek aankh se bhi dimaag aaspaas ki jagah ka pattern bharkar khaali jagah chhupa deta hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Eye lens hi saara focusing karta hai."**

Class 9 mein likha tha ki cornea zyada mod'ta hai aur lens thoda. Ab teen demonstration, numbers ke saath:

- **Hissa:** Part A mein cornea lagbhag +40 D aur lens +20 D tha: cornea **do-tihai** (66.7 percent) mod'ta hai. Lens sirf ek-tihai.
- **Focusing mein lens ka badlaav:** Paas dekhne par lens ne sirf **+4 D** badhai. Kul power 60 D mein se ye 4 ÷ 64 = **6.25 percent** ke barabar hai: lens "fine-tuning" karta hai, saara kaam nahi.
- **Paani ke andar:** Swimming ke waqt (pool mein, coach ya adult ke saath) bina goggles ke sab dhundhla dikhta hai. Wajah: cornea ki bending hawa aur cornea ke beech hoti hai. Paani ke andar cornea ke aage bhi paani hai, to cornea ki +40 D ki zyadatar power chali jaati hai. Lens wahi hai, uski +20 D wahi hai, par kul power bahut ghat gayi aur image retina par nahi banti. Goggles aankh ke saamne hawa ki parat laa dete hain, to cornea phir kaam karta hai aur dikhna saaf ho jaata hai.

**Sahi:** cornea lagbhag do-tihai bending karta hai aur lens baaki ek-tihai: lens ka khaas kaam **badalna** hai (paas-door), kul bending ka bada hissa uska nahi.

## Quick Recap

- Aankh ek **asli lens system**: cornea lagbhag +40 D, eye lens (aaraam mein) lagbhag +20 D, kul lagbhag +60 D; lens se retina lagbhag 2.3 cm.
- 1 ÷ 60 D = lagbhag 1.7 cm, retina 2.3 cm par hai: kyunki aankh ke andar taral hai, hawa nahi, to +60 D ek **effective** number hai.
- **Accommodation** dioptre mein: d metre par rakhi cheez ke liye extra power = 1 ÷ d. Jawan aankh lagbhag +4 D jodti hai (near point 25 cm); umar ke saath ye lagbhag +1 D tak girti hai (near point 100 cm).
- **Camera ki tulna:** aperture = pupil (2 se 8 mm), shutter ~ palak aur retina ka asar, film = retina. Camera lens **khisakta** hai, eye lens **aakar badalta** hai.
- Pupil ki roshni **area** ke hisaab se aati hai: d ka square. 2 mm se 8 mm: lagbhag 16 guna.
- Retina par image real aur **ulta** (m negative); dekhna dimaag ka kaam hai, isliye hum seedha dekhte hain.
- **Rods** halki roshni (bina rang), **cones** tez roshni aur teen kism ke rang; raat mein rang phike; dark adaptation lagbhag 20 se 30 minute.
- **Blind spot** khud pakda ja sakta hai: dono aankhen aur dimaag use chhupa dete hain.
- Galti: "lens hi saara focusing karta hai." Sahi: cornea lagbhag do-tihai, lens ek-tihai.

## Practice Questions

1. A young eye has an accommodation of +6 D. Find its near point.

2. An elderly person has an accommodation of only +0.8 D. Find his near point. Can he read a book kept at 40 cm without any help? How much power is he short of?

3. A person's near point is 32 cm. Find the accommodation of his eye in dioptres.

4. The pupil of an eye is 2 mm wide in bright light and 8 mm wide in a dark room. Find the ratio of the areas, and say what this means for the light entering the eye.

5. At dusk the pupil of an eye widens from 2.5 mm to 7.5 mm. How many times more light can it let in?

6. (a) A film runs at 60 frames per second. How long does each frame stay? (b) A hand-cranked film runs at 12 frames per second. Use the persistence of vision (about 1/16 s) to say whether it will look smooth.

7. In a model eye the cornea has a power of +39 D and the eye lens +21 D when relaxed. (a) Find the total power and the share of the cornea. (b) The eye now looks at a page 45 cm away. By how much does the lens power rise, and what is the new total power and the new share of the cornea?

8. Using P = 1 ÷ f, the eye's total power of +60 D gives f = 1.7 cm, but the retina is about 2.3 cm from the eye lens. Explain in two or three lines why these two numbers do not match.

9. A 1.8 m tall person stands 15 m from an eye whose retina is 2.3 cm behind the lens. Find the height of the image on the retina and say whether it is erect or inverted. Use the sign convention.

10. A person with +3.5 D accommodation reads a page kept 35 cm away. How much accommodation is needed, and how much is left in reserve? Why might the person's eyes tire soon?

11. Say whether each statement is true or false, with a reason.
    (a) The cornea provides most of the bending of light in the eye.
    (b) If the pupil diameter doubles, the light entering becomes twice as much.
    (c) Rods give us colour vision.
    (d) The image on the retina is erect.
    (e) To see a nearby object, the eye lens becomes more powerful.

12. In a moonlit garden, flowers look grey. Explain why. Also, how can you see a very faint star better?

13. On a blind-spot card the cross and the dot are 6 cm apart, and the blind spot lies about 15° from the line of sight. At roughly what distance from the eye will the dot vanish?

14. Name one way in which a camera and the eye are similar and one way in which they focus differently.

15. Why does a swimmer see clearly under water with goggles but not without them?

### Answers

1. Accommodation A = +6 D. Near point d = 1 ÷ A = 1 ÷ 6 = 0.1667 m = **16.7 cm** (lagbhag).
   Jaanch: 1 ÷ 0.1667 m = 6.0 D ✅.

2. A = +0.8 D. d = 1 ÷ 0.8 = 1.25 m = **125 cm**. 40 cm par padhne ke liye 1 ÷ 0.4 = 2.5 D chahiye, aur uske paas sirf 0.8 D hai, to **nahi padh sakta**. Kami = 2.5 − 0.8 = **1.7 D**.
   Jaanch: 0.8 + 1.7 = 2.5 ✅.

3. d = 32 cm = 0.32 m. A = 1 ÷ d = 1 ÷ 0.32 = 3.125 D = lagbhag **3.1 D**.
   Jaanch: 1 ÷ 3.125 = 0.32 m ✅.

4. A = π d² ÷ 4: bright: π × 4 ÷ 4 = 3.14 mm²; dark: π × 64 ÷ 4 = 50.3 mm². Anupat = 50.3 ÷ 3.14 = **16**. Yaani andhere mein pupil bright ki tulna mein **16 guna** roshni andar aane deta hai.
   Jaanch: (8 ÷ 2)² = 4² = 16 ✅.

5. (7.5 ÷ 2.5)² = 3² = **9 guna**.
   Jaanch: areas 4.91 mm² aur 44.2 mm²; 44.2 ÷ 4.91 = 9.0 ✅.

6. (a) t = 1 ÷ 60 = **0.0167 s**, jo 0.0625 s se bahut kam hai, to saaf harkat dikhegi.
   (b) t = 1 ÷ 12 = **0.0833 s**, jo 0.0625 s se zyada hai: agle frame se pehle asar mit chuka hota hai, isliye film **jhilmilayegi**, smooth nahi dikhegi.
   Jaanch: 0.0833 > 0.0625 ✅ aur 0.0167 < 0.0625 ✅.

7. (a) P = 39 + 21 = **60 D**. Cornea ka hissa = 39 ÷ 60 × 100 = **65 percent**.
   (b) d = 45 cm = 0.45 m, u = −0.45 m. Extra power = 1 ÷ 0.45 = **2.22 D**. Lens ki nayi power = 21 + 2.22 = **23.2 D**; kul = 60 + 2.22 = **62.2 D**; cornea ka hissa = 39 ÷ 62.2 × 100 = **62.7 percent**.
   Jaanch: 23.2 + 39 = 62.2 ✅. Cornea ka hissa ghata par phir bhi sabse bada hai ✅.

8. Lens formula mein hum lens ke dono taraf **hawa** maanke chalte hain. Aankh ke andar paani jaisa taral aur jelly jaisa vitreous humour hai, hawa nahi, isliye roshni ki bending ka hisaab badal jaata hai. +60 D ek **effective power** hai, ek kaam chalau number, isliye 1.7 cm aur asli 2.3 cm ka farak aaya.

9. u = −15 m (real object), v = +2.3 cm = +0.023 m (real image), h = +1.8 m. m = v ÷ u = 0.023 ÷ (−15) = −0.001533. h′ = m × h = −0.001533 × 1.8 = −0.00276 m = **−2.76 mm**. Image lagbhag **2.8 mm** ka aur **inverted** (minus sign).
   Jaanch: 0.023 × 1.8 ÷ 15 = 0.00276 m ✅.

10. Zaroori accommodation = 1 ÷ 0.35 = **2.86 D**. Reserve = 3.5 − 2.86 = **0.64 D**. Reserve bahut kam hai, isliye ciliary muscles lagbhag poora zor lagakar kaam kar rahe hain aur aankh jaldi thak jayegi.
    Jaanch: 2.86 + 0.64 = 3.5 ✅.

11. (a) **True** ✅: cornea lagbhag +40 D deta hai, kul +60 D mein se.
    (b) **False** ❌: area d² ke saath badhta hai, to diameter doguna hone par roshni **chaar guna** hoti hai.
    (c) **False** ❌: rang cones se dikhte hain; rods halki roshni ke liye hain.
    (d) **False** ❌: image real aur ulta hai.
    (e) **True** ✅: paas dekhne par eye lens mota hota hai aur uski power badhti hai (extra 1 ÷ d).

12. Chaandni mein roshni bahut kam hoti hai, to sirf rods kaam karte hain, jo rang nahi pehchante, isliye phool grey dikhte hain. Bahut halke taare ko side mein thoda hatkar dekhne se wo zyada dikhta hai, kyunki rods retina ke kinaaron par zyada hote hain.

13. Doori = 6 ÷ tan 15° = 6 ÷ 0.268 = **22.4 cm** (lagbhag 22 cm).
    Jaanch: 22.4 × 0.268 = 6.0 cm ✅.

14. Samaanta: dono mein ek chhed (aperture / pupil) roshni ki matra tay karta hai, aur ek sateh (film / retina) par real, ulta image banta hai. Farak: camera mein lens film se doori badalne ke liye **aage-peeche** khisakta hai, aankh mein lens apna **aakar** badalta hai (retina ki doori fixed).

15. Bina goggles ke cornea ke aage paani hota hai, to cornea ki lagbhag +40 D ki zyadatar power chali jaati hai aur image retina par nahi banti, isliye sab dhundhla dikhta hai. Goggles cornea ke aage hawa ki parat bana dete hain, to cornea phir normal tarah mod sakta hai.

Sampada ne camera ko wapas kapde mein lapeta, par ab uske paas apni aankh ke liye ek naya number-wala naksha tha. Shabaash!

**Aage kya?** Kabhi kabhi ye hisaab bigad jaata hai: aankh ka lens ya eyeball thoda zyada power ya galat lambai ka ho jaata hai, aur door ya paas ki cheez dhundhli dikhne lagti hai.
