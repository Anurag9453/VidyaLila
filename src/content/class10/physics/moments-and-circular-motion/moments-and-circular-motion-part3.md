# Topic 3: Centre Of Gravity: Wajan Ka Ek Hi Point

Topic 2 mein humne baar-baar ek baat maani: **uniform rod ya plank ka weight uske beech mein lagta hai**. Us waqt hume bas ek shart chahiye thi ki weight kisi ek jagah se lagta dikhe, taaki moment nikal sakein. Par sawaal bacha tha: agar rod ek sire par moti ho, ya cheez L-shape ki ho, ya ek khaali ring ho, to wo "ek jagah" kahan hogi? Aaj hum us ek point ko dhundhenge: **centre of gravity**.

## Craft table par ruler aur mitti

Mr. Karandikar ke ghar craft ka saaman bichha tha. Unki bhatiji Kshitija school project ke liye mitti ke khilone bana rahi thi. Mez par ek lambi metre scale padi thi. Kshitija ne shararat mein scale ko ungli par rakha: **beech wale 50 cm ke nishaan par** wo bilkul seedhi tik gayi, na dayein gir rahi thi, na bayein.

Phir usne mitti ki ek goli scale ke ek sire ke paas (90 cm ke nishaan par) chipka di. "Ab to gir jayegi!" Wo ungli par wapas rakhne gayi, par 50 cm par scale tik hi nahi rahi: mitti wali taraf jhuk gayi. Kshitija ne ungli ko mitti ki taraf thoda khiskaya aur... scale phir tik gayi, ek naye nishaan par.

"Chacha, ye kya jaadu hai? Scale ka wazan to ab bhi poori scale mein faila hai, par ungli ko sahi jagah chahiye. Wo jagah kaise pata chalegi?"

Mr. Karandikar ne ungli ke neeche ka nishaan dekha. "Jaadu nahi, Kshitija. Is scale ka poora wazan jaise **ek hi point** par jama hua hai, aur tab scale tikti hai jab ungli theek usi point ke neeche ho. Is point ko **centre of gravity** kehte hain. Chalo, pehle ise samjhte hain, phir mitti wale scale ka ye point numbers se nikalenge."

## Part A: Centre of gravity kya hai?

Hamari scale mein bahut saare chhote-chhote hisse hain, aur Earth har hisse ko neeche kheenchti hai. Matlab har hisse par apna chhota weight lagta hai: **bahut saare chhote parallel forces**. Ab ek bada sawaal: kya in sab ko ek hi force se badal sakte hain?

Haan, aur jahan wo ek force lagayenge, wahi hai **centre of gravity (CG)**:

> **CG wo ek point hai jahan cheez ka poora weight (mg) lagta hua maana ja sakta hai.** Us point ke around saare chhote weights ke moments ka jod zero hota hai.

Isi liye agar ek support (jaise ungli) theek CG ke neeche laga do, to support ka upar ki taraf force aur poora weight neeche ki taraf, **ek hi line mein** barabar-barabar hote hain. Na force ka jod bacha, na moment: cheez Topic 2 ki dono shartein poori karti hai aur tik jaati hai.

```
                                                 mitti 30 g
                                                 ▼
    ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    └─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
    0                        50                  90   100 cm
                                  ▲
                                  ungli: CG 60 cm par
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Mitti 90 cm par chipki hai, isliye metre scale ka CG 50 cm se mitti ki taraf khisak kar 60 cm par aa gaya (Example 3). Ungli sirf tab tikaa sakti hai jab wo CG ke theek neeche ho. Neeche ki taraf poora weight aur upar ki taraf ungli ka force ek hi line mein hain.

**Ek imaandar baat:** CG aur centre of mass alag shabd hain. Centre of mass sirf mass ki baat karta hai, CG weight ki. Jab tak g poori cheez par ek-sa ho (jaise Earth ke paas ghar ki cheezein), dono **ek hi point** hote hain. Is chapter mein hum dono ke liye ek hi jagah maante hain.

### Activity 1: Pencil ungli par (ghar par adult ke saath)

Ek lambi, seedhi pencil ya scale lo, dono haaton ki ek-ek ungli ke upar usse door-door rakho, phir dono ungliyaan dheere dheere ek doosre ki taraf khiskao. Dono ungliyaan **hamesha beech mein hi milti hain**, chahe pehle koi bhi ungli aage thi. Kyunki jis ungli ke paas CG zyaada paas hota hai, wo zyaada bhaar uthati hai aur uspar zyaada friction lagta hai, to doosri ungli khisakti hai. Is tarah dono dheere-dheere CG ke neeche aa jaati hain. Ab pencil ke ek sire par mitti ki goli chipka kar dobara karo: ungliyaan **ab bhi milengi, par naye beech (CG) par**.

## Part B: Seedhi shapes ka CG

Uniform shapes mein CG wahan hota hai jahan **symmetry** ka kendra ho:

- **Seedhi uniform chhad** (rod, metre scale): beech mein (50 cm ke nishaan par).
- **Rectangle ya square ki plate:** dono diagonals ke milne ke point par (beech mein).
- **Gol plate ya sphere:** uske kendra par.
- **Triangle ki plate:** teeno **medians** ke milne ke point par. Ye point har median ko is tarah baantta hai ki wo kone se **do-tihaai** aur samne wali bhuja se **ek-tihaai** door hota hai. Isliye **CG base se triangle ki oonchai ka ek-tihaai (⅓) oopar** hota hai.

```
                    /\
                   /  \
                  /    \          oonchai h
                 /      \
                /   ×    \       <- CG: base se ⅓ h oopar
               /          \
              /____________\
              <──  base  ──>
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Triangle ki plate. × CG hai, jo base se oonchai h ka ⅓ oopar hai (aur kone se ⅔ h neeche). Teeno medians isi point par milti hain. (Figure sirf proportion ke liye hai.)

### Example 1. Triangle ki plate ka CG

Ek uniform triangle ki plate ki oonchai 0.27 m hai. Uska CG base se kitna oopar hai?

**Given:** oonchai h = 0.27 m
**Find:** CG ki base se oonchai
**Formula:** CG ki oonchai = h ÷ 3
**Substitution:** 0.27 m ÷ 3 = 0.09 m
**Answer:** CG base se **0.09 m (9 cm)** oopar hai.
**Jaanch:** Kone se doori = 0.27 − 0.09 = 0.18 m, jo 0.09 m ki theek do guna hai ✅ (kone se do-tihaai, base se ek-tihaai).

## Part C: Lamina ka CG: plumb-line ki vidhi

Seedhi shape ke liye symmetry kaam aati hai, par tedhe-medhe cardboard ke liye nahi. Uske liye ek chalaak tareeka hai: use **latka do**.

**Kyun kaam karta hai?** Jab lamina ek pin par latakti hai, to wo tabtak hilti rehti hai jab tak CG pin ke theek **neeche** na aa jaye. Agar CG pin ke dayein ya bayein hota, to weight (neeche) aur pin ka force (upar) alag-alag lines mein hote, aur unka couple (Topic 1) lamina ko ghumata. Jab CG theek neeche aata hai, tab dono force ek hi line mein hote hain, couple zero, aur lamina ruk jaati hai. Isliye **pin se seedhi neeche ki line mein CG hota hai**.

```
              pin A
                ●
      ┌─────────┼─────────┐
      │         │         │
      │         │         │
      │         ×  CG     │
      │         │         │
      └─────────┼─────────┘
                │
                ◆ nut (plumb line)
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Cardboard pin A par latka hai. Pin A se plumb line (dhaaga + nut) bhi latka hai. CG (×) A se seedhi neeche wali line par hi kahin hota hai. Dhaage ke peeche pencil se line kheench lo.

**Tareeka (teen baar):**
1. Cardboard par teen chhote ched (A, B, C) kinaaro ke paas karo.
2. Pehle ched A mein pin pakdao, cardboard ko aur plumb line ko saath latkao. Jab dono ruk jayein, dhaage ke peeche cardboard par pencil se seedhi line kheencho.
3. Yahi B aur C ke saath karo. Teeno lines **ek hi point par milengi**: wahi CG hai.

### Activity 2: Cardboard ka CG (ghar par adult ke saath)

Ek tedhe-medhe cardboard ka tukda lo. Adult se kinaaro ke paas teen chhote ched karwao (pin ya dhaage ke liye, kaanch ya nukeela koi kaam bachche na karein). Ek pin ya mazboot pencil ko ched mein dheele se daalo, taaki cardboard khul kar ghoom sake. Dhaage ke ek sire par chhoti washer ya nut baandho aur wo dhaaga bhi usi pin par latkao. Ruk jaane par pencil se line kheencho. Teeno ched ke liye karo. Jahan lines milein, wahan pencil ki nok se cardboard ko neeche se tikao: wo **seedhi tik jayegi**.

## Part D: Jab CG cheez ke andar hi nahi hota

Kya CG hamesha cheez ke "maal" (matter) ke andar hota hai? Nahi.

- **Ring ya angoothi (circular ring):** CG ring ke **kendra** par hota hai, jahan hawa hai, koi matter nahi. Ring ka weight phir bhi kendra se lagta hua maana jaata hai: usi point ke around saare moments ka jod zero hota hai.
- **Khaali pyaala ya katora:** CG uski axis par, andar ki khaali jagah mein, kahin hota hai.
- **L-shape ki plate, boomerang ya mudi hui chhad:** CG mode ke andar ke kone ki taraf, matter ke bahar hota hai.

```
         Ring                       Khaali pyaala

        .-'''-.                      ┌          ┐
      /         \                    │          │
     (     ×     )   <- CG           │    ×     │  <- CG (hawa mein)
      \         /                     \        /
        '-...-'                        \______/
```

> **Figure 3.4** – Ring ka CG (×) uske kendra mein hai, jahan koi matter nahi. Khaali pyaale ka CG bhi uski khaali jagah mein hota hai.

Ye isliye aaram se ho jaata hai ki CG koi "cheez" nahi, ek **hisaab ka point** hai: wo point jahan se weight lagta hua maan lo, to asli weight jaisa hi asar dikhta hai.

## Part E: Do masses ka CG: x = (m₁x₁ + m₂x₂) ÷ (m₁ + m₂)

Ab asli numbers. Maan lo ek **halki** chhad (hum use "rod ko halka maano" kehte hain, yani uska apna weight ignore) par do masses hain: m₁ bayein sire se x₁ par aur m₂ x₂ par. CG ek point x par hoga. Us point par support laga dein to chhad tik jaani chahiye, yaani **moments ka jod zero**:

m₁g × (x − x₁) = m₂g × (x₂ − x)

Dono taraf g kat jaata hai. Ise x ke liye sulajhaayein:

> **x = (m₁x₁ + m₂x₂) ÷ (m₁ + m₂)**

Matlab CG **bhaari mass ke paas** aata hai. Dono masses barabar hon to x = (x₁ + x₂) ÷ 2, yaani seedha beech mein. Ek ya do se zyaada masses ho to numerator mein usi tarah ek aur term judta hai, par hum yahan do masses tak hi rahenge.

```
         m₁ = 2.5 kg                             m₂ = 1.5 kg
         ▼                                       ▼
    ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    └─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                        ▲
                        support (CG)
    0    0.10           0.40                     0.90 1.0 m
```

> **Figure 3.5** – Halki 1.0 m ki chhad par 2.5 kg 0.10 m par aur 1.5 kg 0.90 m par. Support CG (0.40 m) par laga hai, jo bhaari mass 2.5 kg ki taraf hai.

### Example 2. Do masses ki halki chhad

Ek halki 1.0 m ki chhad par 2.5 kg ka mass bayein sire se 0.10 m par aur 1.5 kg ka mass bayein sire se 0.90 m par laga hai. CG kahan hai?

**Given:** m₁ = 2.5 kg, x₁ = 0.10 m; m₂ = 1.5 kg, x₂ = 0.90 m (chhad halki)
**Find:** CG ki jagah x
**Formula:** x = (m₁x₁ + m₂x₂) ÷ (m₁ + m₂)
**Substitution:** x = (2.5 × 0.10 + 1.5 × 0.90) ÷ (2.5 + 1.5) = (0.25 + 1.35) ÷ 4.0 = 1.60 ÷ 4.0 = 0.40 m
**Answer:** CG bayein sire se **0.40 m** par hai (bhaari mass ke paas).
**Jaanch:** CG ke around moments (g ko kaat kar): bayein taraf 2.5 × (0.40 − 0.10) = 0.75 kg m, dayein taraf 1.5 × (0.90 − 0.40) = 0.75 kg m. Barabar ✅. Units mein x ki unit m aayi, jo doori ki unit hai ✅.

### Example 3. Mitti wali scale (Kshitija ka sawaal)

Metre scale ka mass 90 g hai (uniform, to uska weight 50 cm par). 30 g ki mitti ki goli scale ke 90 cm ke nishaan par chipki hai. (a) Ungli kahan rakhein to scale tik jaye? (b) Us waqt ungli kitna force lagayegi?

**Given:** scale 90 g, uska CG 50 cm par; mitti 30 g, 90 cm par; g = 9.8 m/s²
**Find:** (a) CG ki jagah x; (b) ungli ka force
**Formula:** x = (m₁x₁ + m₂x₂) ÷ (m₁ + m₂) (cm aur g mein, ratio hai); force = poora weight = Mg
**Substitution:**
(a) x = (90 × 50 + 30 × 90) ÷ (90 + 30) = (4500 + 2700) ÷ 120 = 7200 ÷ 120 = 60 cm
(b) M = 90 + 30 = 120 g = 0.12 kg, force = 0.12 × 9.8 = 1.176 N
**Answer:** (a) ungli **60 cm** ke nishaan ke neeche; (b) ungli lagbhag **1.18 N** upar ki taraf lagayegi.
**Jaanch:** 60 cm ke around: scale 90 g × 10 cm = 900 g cm, mitti 30 g × 30 cm = 900 g cm. Barabar ✅. Ungli ka force poore weight ke barabar hai ✅ (Topic 2 ki pehli shart: forces ka jod zero).

Dekha? Mitti ne CG ko 50 cm se 60 cm tak mitti ki taraf khiska diya, bilkul Kshitija ki ungli ki tarah.

## Part F: Composite shape: L-plate aur hathoda

Agar shape seedhe tukdon se judi ho to rasta wahi: **har tukde ko uske CG par rakhe ek mass maan lo**, phir wahi formula. Uniform plate mein mass uske **kshetrafal (area)** ke barabar-anupaat mein hota hai, isliye area ko hi mass ki jagah le sakte hain.

```
   y (cm)
   30  ┌───┐
       │   │
       │ B │
       │   │
   10  │   └────────┐
       │      A     │
    0  └────────────┘
       0   10       30   x (cm)
```

> **Figure 3.6** – L-shape ki plate: neeche A (30 cm lambi, 10 cm oonchi) aur oopar B (10 cm chaudi, 20 cm oonchi). Tukda A ka CG (15, 5) aur tukda B ka CG (5, 20) hai. Poori plate ka CG in dono ke beech kahin aayega.

### Example 4. L-plate ka CG

Figure 3.6 ki uniform plate ka CG nikalo. A ke baayein-neeche kone ko origin (0, 0) lo.

**Given:** A: area = 30 × 10 = 300 cm², CG (15, 5); B: area = 10 × 20 = 200 cm², CG (5, 20)
**Find:** poori plate ka CG (x, y)
**Formula:** x = (A₁x₁ + A₂x₂) ÷ (A₁ + A₂), y bhi isi tarah (area ko mass ki jagah)
**Substitution:**
x = (300 × 15 + 200 × 5) ÷ (300 + 200) = (4500 + 1000) ÷ 500 = 11 cm
y = (300 × 5 + 200 × 20) ÷ 500 = (1500 + 4000) ÷ 500 = 11 cm
**Answer:** CG **(11 cm, 11 cm)** par hai.
**Jaanch:** Ye point dono tukdon se bahar hai: A sirf y = 0 se 10 cm tak hai, B sirf x = 0 se 10 cm tak. Yaani (11, 11) L ke andar ke kone ki **khaali jagah** mein hai: bilkul waisa jaisa Part D mein kaha ✅. Ek aur check: x = 11 cm, dono CG (x = 15 aur x = 5) ke beech hai, aur bhaari A ke zyaada paas ✅.

### Example 5. Hathoda: sir bhaari, haath ka dastaa halka

Hathode ke lakdi ke dastaa ka mass 0.2 kg aur lambai 0.30 m hai (uniform). Lohe ka sir 0.6 kg ka hai aur uska CG dastaa ke khule sire se 0.32 m par hai. Poore hathode ka CG dastaa ke khule sire se kitna door hai?

**Given:** dastaa m₁ = 0.2 kg, CG x₁ = 0.15 m (beech mein); sir m₂ = 0.6 kg, x₂ = 0.32 m
**Find:** CG ki jagah x (khule sire se)
**Formula:** x = (m₁x₁ + m₂x₂) ÷ (m₁ + m₂)
**Substitution:** x = (0.2 × 0.15 + 0.6 × 0.32) ÷ (0.2 + 0.6) = (0.030 + 0.192) ÷ 0.8 = 0.222 ÷ 0.8 = 0.2775 m
**Answer:** CG khule sire se lagbhag **0.28 m** par hai, yaani dastaa ke aakhri sire ke paas, sir ke bahut karib.
**Jaanch:** x ke around moments: dastaa 0.2 × (0.2775 − 0.15) = 0.0255 kg m, sir 0.6 × (0.32 − 0.2775) = 0.0255 kg m. Barabar ✅.

```
                                ↓ CG: 0.28 m
    ══════════════════════════════█████
                   ↑                ↑
    khula sira (0) 0.15 m (dastaa)  0.32 m (sir)
```

> **Figure 3.7** – Hathoda (dastaa 0.30 m + sir). Dastaa ka apna CG 0.15 m par aur sir ka CG 0.32 m par. Poore hathode ka CG × lagbhag 0.28 m par, dastaa ke geometric beech (0.15 m) se bahut door, sir ke paas.

Isi liye hathoda sir ke paas se "bhaari" mehsoos hota hai.

## Part G: Insaan, ucchal-kood aur gaadi

- **Khade insaan ka CG:** lagbhag naabhi ke aas-paas (kamar ke thoda oopar), body ke andar. Haath oopar karo to CG oopar khisakta hai; jhukne par aage ki taraf aur kabhi-kabhi body ke bahar bhi chala jaata hai.
- **High jumper (fosbury flop):** jumper bar ke oopar piche ki taraf jhuk kar kamaan jaisa banta hai. Body ka CG bar ke **neeche** se bhi guzar sakta hai jabki body bar ke oopar hai, kyunki body mudi hui hai aur CG body ke bahar hai. Isse jumper ko CG ko kam oonchai tak hi uthana padta hai.
- **Gaadi aur bus:** CG jitna neeche, utni gaadi sthir. Heavy luggage ko bus ke floor-level hold mein rakhna aur chhat par halka saaman rakhna isi liye theek hai. Is par Topic 4 mein poori baat hogi.

**Galti: "CG hamesha cheez ke andar hota hai, aur hamesha geometric beech mein hota hai"**

Demonstration 1 (andar nahi): Example 4 mein L-plate ka CG (11 cm, 11 cm) dono tukdon se **bahar**, khaali jagah mein aaya. Ring ka CG kendra mein hai jahan hawa hai (Figure 3.4).

Demonstration 2 (beech mein nahi): Metre scale ka geometric beech 50 cm hai, par mitti lagane ke baad CG 60 cm par aaya (Example 3). Hathode ke dastaa ka geometric beech 0.15 m par hai, par poore hathode ka CG 0.28 m par aaya (Example 5): sir ki taraf bahut khisak gaya.

**Sahi:** CG wo point hai jahan weight ka asar jama maana jaata hai. Uniform aur symmetric shape mein wo beech mein aata hai, par asymmetric ya hollow shape mein wo bhaari hisse ki taraf khisak jaata hai, aur kabhi cheez ke **bahar** bhi ho sakta hai.

## Quick Recap

- **CG** wo ek point hai jahan cheez ka poora weight mg lagta hua maana ja sakta hai; CG par ek support cheez ko tika deta hai.
- Uniform rod ka CG beech mein; rectangle ka diagonals par; circle ka kendra mein; triangle ka base se oonchai ka ek-tihaai (⅓) oopar.
- **Plumb-line vidhi:** lamina ko alag-alag ched se latkao, har baar dhaage ki line kheencho; lines jahan milein, wahi CG.
- CG cheez ke **bahar** bhi ho sakta hai: ring, khaali pyaala, L-plate, mudi hui chhad.
- **Do masses:** x = (m₁x₁ + m₂x₂) ÷ (m₁ + m₂); CG bhaari mass ke paas hota hai. Composite plate mein area ko mass ki jagah lete hain.
- Insaan ya gaadi mein CG jitna neeche, utni sthirta (Topic 4).

## Practice Questions

1. A light rod has a 4.0 kg mass at one end and a 6.0 kg mass at the other end, with 1.2 m between them. How far from the 4.0 kg mass is the centre of gravity?

2. A dumb-bell has unequal balls of 5.0 kg and 3.0 kg joined by a light bar so that their centres are 0.64 m apart. Find the position of the CG from the centre of the heavier ball.

3. A uniform rod 2.0 m long and of mass 3.0 kg has a 5.0 kg lump tied at one end. How far from the lump end is the CG of the rod and lump together?

4. A triangular plate is 0.84 m high. How far above its base is its CG? How far is it from the top corner?

5. A uniform L-shaped plate is made from two rectangles. The lower bar is 36 cm long and 6 cm wide. The upright bar is 6 cm wide and rises 24 cm above the lower bar, at the left end. Taking the bottom-left corner as the origin, find the CG (x, y). Does it lie on the plate?

6. A hammer has a wooden handle of mass 0.3 kg and length 0.36 m (uniform) and a head of mass 0.8 kg whose CG is 0.40 m from the free end of the handle. How far from the free end is the CG of the whole hammer? Does it lie on the handle?

7. A metre scale of mass 150 g has a 50 g lump stuck at its 20 cm mark. At which mark should a pencil support be placed to balance the scale, and what is the support force in newtons?

8. A student hangs a cardboard lamina from holes A and B and draws two plumb-line paths that cross at a point P. She then hangs it from a third hole C. What do you expect to find? If instead the lamina were hung freely from C and the line from C passed 2 cm away from P, what would that tell you?

9. State True or False and give one line of reason:
   (a) The CG of a body must always lie inside the body.
   (b) A uniform rod's CG is at its middle.
   (c) In a triangle, the CG is one-third of the height above the base.
   (d) The CG of a system moves closer to the heavier mass.
   (e) The plumb-line method needs the lamina to hang freely.

10. A circular ring has radius 0.15 m. Where is its CG? How far is the CG from the nearest part of the ring's material?

11. A light rod 1.0 m long carries a 3.5 kg mass at 0.15 m and an unknown mass at 0.85 m. The CG of the system is at 0.60 m. Find the unknown mass.

12. Explain in two or three lines why a high jumper can clear a bar even though his CG passes below the bar, and why a bus with passengers on its roof is more likely to tip than one with passengers inside.

### Answers

1. **Given:** m₁ = 4.0 kg at x₁ = 0; m₂ = 6.0 kg at x₂ = 1.2 m (chhad halki). **Find:** x. **Formula:** x = (m₁x₁ + m₂x₂) ÷ (m₁ + m₂). **Substitution:** x = (4.0 × 0 + 6.0 × 1.2) ÷ 10.0 = 7.2 ÷ 10.0 = 0.72 m. **Answer:** CG 4.0 kg wale mass se **0.72 m** door hai (bhaari 6.0 kg se 0.48 m). **Jaanch:** moments 4.0 × 0.72 = 2.88 aur 6.0 × 0.48 = 2.88 kg m ✅.

2. **Given:** 5.0 kg aur 3.0 kg, doori 0.64 m. **Find:** CG ki bhaari ball se doori. **Formula:** bhaari ball ko origin lo, x = (3.0 × 0.64) ÷ (5.0 + 3.0). **Substitution:** x = 1.92 ÷ 8.0 = 0.24 m. **Answer:** **0.24 m** bhaari ball ke kendra se (halki ball se 0.40 m). **Jaanch:** 5.0 × 0.24 = 1.2 aur 3.0 × 0.40 = 1.2 kg m ✅.

3. **Given:** lump 5.0 kg lump-end par (x = 0); rod 3.0 kg, uska CG 1.0 m par. **Formula:** x = (5.0 × 0 + 3.0 × 1.0) ÷ (5.0 + 3.0). **Substitution:** x = 3.0 ÷ 8.0 = 0.375 m. **Answer:** lump wale sire se **0.375 m**. **Jaanch:** 5.0 × 0.375 = 1.875 aur 3.0 × (1.0 − 0.375) = 3.0 × 0.625 = 1.875 kg m ✅.

4. **Given:** h = 0.84 m. **Formula:** CG ki oonchai = h ÷ 3. **Substitution:** 0.84 ÷ 3 = 0.28 m. **Answer:** base se **0.28 m** oopar, top corner se 0.84 − 0.28 = **0.56 m** neeche. **Jaanch:** 0.56 = 2 × 0.28 ✅ (kone se do-tihaai).

5. **Given:** neeche ki bar: 36 cm × 6 cm, area 216 cm², CG (18, 3); upright: 6 cm × 24 cm (y = 6 se 30 tak), area 144 cm², CG (3, 18). **Formula:** x = (A₁x₁ + A₂x₂) ÷ (A₁ + A₂), y waise hi. **Substitution:** x = (216 × 18 + 144 × 3) ÷ 360 = (3888 + 432) ÷ 360 = 12 cm. y = (216 × 3 + 144 × 18) ÷ 360 = (648 + 2592) ÷ 360 = 9 cm. **Answer:** CG **(12 cm, 9 cm)**. Neeche ki bar sirf y = 0 se 6 cm tak aur upright sirf x = 0 se 6 cm tak hai, to (12, 9) khaali jagah mein hai: **plate par nahi**. **Jaanch:** total area 360 cm² ✅; x = 12 dono tukdon ke CG (18 aur 3) ke beech hai ✅.

6. **Given:** dastaa 0.3 kg, CG 0.18 m; sir 0.8 kg, CG 0.40 m. **Formula:** x = (m₁x₁ + m₂x₂) ÷ (m₁ + m₂). **Substitution:** x = (0.3 × 0.18 + 0.8 × 0.40) ÷ 1.1 = (0.054 + 0.32) ÷ 1.1 = 0.374 ÷ 1.1 = 0.34 m. **Answer:** **0.34 m** khule sire se. Dastaa 0.36 m ka hai, to CG dastaa par hi hai, sir se sirf 0.02 m pehle. **Jaanch:** 0.3 × (0.34 − 0.18) = 0.048 aur 0.8 × (0.40 − 0.34) = 0.048 kg m ✅.

7. **Given:** scale 150 g, CG 50 cm; lump 50 g, 20 cm; g = 9.8 m/s². **Formula:** x = (150 × 50 + 50 × 20) ÷ (150 + 50). **Substitution:** x = (7500 + 1000) ÷ 200 = 8500 ÷ 200 = 42.5 cm. Force = 0.20 kg × 9.8 = 1.96 N. **Answer:** pencil **42.5 cm** ke nishaan par; support force **1.96 N** upar ki taraf. **Jaanch:** 150 × 7.5 = 1125 aur 50 × 22.5 = 1125 g cm ✅.

8. Teeno lines **ek hi point P par milengi**, kyunki har latkaav mein CG pin ke theek neeche hota hai. Agar C wali line P se 2 cm door hoti, to koi nishaan galat hai: ya lamina swing karte hue poori tarah ruka nahi, ya pin ched mein atka, ya line seedhi nahi kheenchi gayi. **Jaanch:** CG ek hi point hota hai, to teeno lines ek hi point par milni chahiye.

9. (a) **False:** ring ya L-plate mein CG matter ke bahar hota hai. (b) **True** (agar rod uniform ho, jaise hum Topic 2 mein maan rahe the). (c) **True:** base se ek-tihaai (⅓) h oopar. (d) **True:** x = (m₁x₁ + m₂x₂) ÷ (m₁ + m₂) mein bhaari mass ke x ka hissa zyaada hota hai. (e) **True:** pin ke around freely ghoomne par hi CG neeche aata hai; ruki hui lamina galat line deti.

10. CG ring ke **kendra** par hai. Ring ke matter se uski doori radius ke barabar, **0.15 m**, kyunki kendra ring ke har point se 0.15 m door hai. **Jaanch:** hawa ke point par CG hai, to kendra par koi matter nahi hai ✅.

11. **Given:** m₁ = 3.5 kg, x₁ = 0.15 m; m₂ = ?, x₂ = 0.85 m; x = 0.60 m. **Find:** m₂. **Formula:** m₁(x − x₁) = m₂(x₂ − x) (CG ke around moments barabar). **Substitution:** 3.5 × (0.60 − 0.15) = m₂ × (0.85 − 0.60), to 1.575 = m₂ × 0.25, m₂ = 1.575 ÷ 0.25 = 6.3 kg. **Answer:** **6.3 kg**. **Jaanch:** x = (3.5 × 0.15 + 6.3 × 0.85) ÷ 9.8 = (0.525 + 5.355) ÷ 9.8 = 5.88 ÷ 9.8 = 0.60 m ✅.

12. High jumper bar ke oopar peeche ki taraf kamaan jaisa mud jaata hai, to body ke hisse bar ke oopar se guzarte hain par **CG body ke bahar, bar ke neeche** reh sakta hai. Isse CG ko utni oonchai tak nahi uthana padta jitni seedhe kood mein padti. Bus ki chhat par passengers hon to bus ka **CG oopar** chala jaata hai, aur CG jitna oopar, bus ko tilt karne par CG se seedhi neeche wali line jaldi base ke bahar nikal jaati hai; iska poora hisaab Topic 4 mein hoga.
