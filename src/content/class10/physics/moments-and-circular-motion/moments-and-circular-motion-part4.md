# Topic 4: Stability: Kab Girega, Kab Tikega

Topic 3 mein humne seekha ki kisi bhi cheez ka poora weight ek point, **centre of gravity (CG)**, par kaam karta hua maana ja sakta hai. Ab us point ka asli fayda dekhte hain. Do cheezein dono "tiki" hui hain, par ek ko zara sa dhakka do to wo seedhi wapas khadi ho jaati hai, aur doosri dhad se gir padti hai. Farq kahan hai? Jawab **CG kitna oonchaa hai, base kitna chauda hai, aur CG ki seedhi line base ke andar girti hai ya bahar**, isme chhipa hai. Isi se milta hai **stability** ka poora hisaab.

## Roly-poly gudiya aur jhuki hui bus

Mr. Pendse ke bhanje Chaitanya ke haath ek **roly-poly** (tumbler) gudiya lagi thi: neeche se gol, upar se patli, ek hansta chehra. Chaitanya use zor se dabata, wo lete-lete jhoolti, aur phir **seedhi khadi**. Usne gudiya ko pet ke bal lita diya, kone mein dhakela, mez ke kinare tak ghumaya. Har baar wahi: khadi. "Mama, ye cheating hai! Isme jaadu hai kya?"

Mr. Pendse ne akhbaar ka ek page mez par rakha. Us par ek **news photo** thi: ek bus kachche raste ke dhal par ek taraf bahut jhuki hui khadi thi, chhat par saaman ka bada dher laga tha, aur khaas baat: **sab surakshit the**, bus girne se pehle ruk gayi thi. "Is bus ko dekho, Chaitanya. Gudiya jitna bhi jhuke, wapas aa jaati hai. Ye bus thoda aur jhuki hoti to palat jaati. Kya in dono mein koi ek hi rule chhipa hai?"

Chaitanya ne gudiya ko ek baar phir dabaya, ab dhyan se dekhte hue. "Mama, gudiya ka sir to halka hai, par uska neeche wala hissa **bhaari** lag raha hai. ## Part A: Teen tarah ka equilibrium

Topic 2 mein equilibrium ka matlab tha forces aur moments balance. Ab sawal hai: balance **kitna pakka** hai? Ek simple jaanch hai: cheez ko **zara sa hila do** aur dekho kya hota hai. Teen ball ka picture lo.

```
    Stable                 Unstable               Neutral

   \         /               o                       o
    \       /               / \                ===========
     \__o__/             __/   \__             samtal mez
    katori ka tala        pahaadi ki choti
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Teen goliyan: katori ke tale mein (stable), pahaadi ki choti par (unstable) aur samtal mez par (neutral). Goli ko thoda sarkane par: katori mein wo **oopar chadhti** hai, choti par **neeche utarti** hai, mez par **barabar oonchai** par rehti hai.

- **Stable:** thoda hilane par CG **oopar uthta** hai. Weight use wapas neeche (sabse neeche wali jagah par) kheenchta hai. Cheez wapas aa jaati hai.
- **Unstable:** thoda hilane par CG **neeche girta** hai. Weight use aur neeche le jaata hai. Cheez door chali jaati hai.
- **Neutral:** CG ki oonchai **badalti nahi**. Cheez nayi jagah par bhi tiki rehti hai.

**Cone ka example.** Ek cone (jaise ice-cream cone ya topi) teen tarah se rakha ja sakta hai:

| Cone kaise rakha hai | CG ki oonchai hilane par | Equilibrium |
|---|---|---|
| Chaude base par khada | Base ka ek kinara uthaane par CG oopar jaata hai | Stable |
| Nok (tip) par khada | Girne par CG neeche aata hai | Unstable |
| Lete hue, side par | Lotne par CG barabar oonchai par chalta hai | Neutral |

**Ek rule yaad rakho:** equilibrium ki kism is par depend karti hai ki **thoda hilaane par CG ki oonchai badhti, ghatati ya wahi rehti hai**. Badhe to stable, ghate to unstable, wahi rahe to neutral.

## Part B: Teen cheezein jo cheez ko tikaati hain

Roz ki cheezein dekho: patla lamba glass jaldi gir jaata hai, chaude tale ka katora nahi. Ek lamba bamboo seedha khada rakhna mushkil hai, par ek chhoti si stool aaram se khadi rehti hai. Teen baatein mil kar tay karti hain ki cheez kitni stable hai:

1. **CG jitna neeche, utni stability.** CG neeche hai to cheez ko palatne ke liye zyaada jhukana padega.
2. **Base jitna chauda, utni stability.** Chaude base ke kinare CG se door hote hain, to CG ko kinare ke upar pahunchne ke liye aur zyaada jhukaav chahiye.
3. **CG se seedhi neeche girti line base ke andar honi chahiye.** Cheez tab tak khadi hai jab tak is line ka foot base ke andar hai. Line base ke **bahar** nikli to weight cheez ko palat deta hai.

```
                                    G2  seema par
                                    :
                                    :
           G1  shuru                :                 G3  seema ke paar
           ↓ W                      :                 ↓ W
           :                        :                 :
           :                        :                 :
 ==========a========================P=================b=========
         (andar)                    P ke theek neeche   (bahar)
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Kinara P par block ko palatte hue CG ek chaap par chalta hai (schematic chitra). G1: shuru mein CG P ke bayein, to weight ki line zameen ko a par chhooti hai, jo block ke apne base ke **andar** hai. G2: CG P ke theek oopar, line P se guzarti hai: yahi **seema** hai. G3: CG P ke daayein, line b par girti hai, base ke **bahar**: weight ka moment block ko palat deta hai.

Upar wali figure ko aise padho: weight W CG par neeche kaam karta hai. Kinara P pivot ki tarah hai. CG P ke bayein ho to W ka moment block ko **wapas** gira kar base par rakhta hai (restoring). CG P ke daayein ho jaaye to W ka moment block ko **aage** ghumata hai (toppling). Ye wahi principle of moments hai, bas pivot ek kinara hai.

Roz ki misalein:
- **Racing car:** neeche aur chaudi, CG zameen ke qareeb aur pahiye door-door. Isliye tez mod par bhi nahi palatti.
- **Table lamp:** bhaari, chaudi plate par: base chauda aur CG neeche.
- **Bus ke upar saaman:** CG oopar chala jaata hai, bus pehle se jaldi palat sakti hai (hum numbers se dekhenge).

## Part C: Kitna jhukne par palat jaayega? (tilting angle)

Ek **uniform rectangular block** lo: base chaudai b, oonchai h. Uska CG beech mein hota hai: base ke upar **h ÷ 2** par, aur base ke dono kinaron se **b ÷ 2** door (seedhi line mein). Isse kinara P par jhukaane par CG P ke theek oopar tab aata hai jab block kuch angle θ ghoom jaaye.

```
   +-----------+
   [           ]
   [           ]
   [           ]
   [           ]
   [     G     ]    G = CG
   [     :\    ]    GM = h ÷ 2 (seedhi khadi line)
   [     : \   ]    MP = b ÷ 2
   [     :  \  ]    GP = G se kinara P tak
   [     :   \ ]    GM aur GP ke beech ka kona = θ
   +-----M-----P
```

> **Figure 4.3** – Seedhe khade block ka side view (scale ke hisaab se nahi). G CG hai, M base ka beech, P wo kinara jis par block ko palatna hai. Palatne ki seema par block itna ghoomta hai ki GP seedhi khadi ho jaaye, yaani G ko P ke theek oopar aana pade. Is liye GM aur GP ke beech ka kona θ ghoomna padta hai, aur triangle GMP mein **tan θ = MP ÷ GM**.

Triangle GMP mein MP ÷ GM ke liye **tan θ = (aadha base) ÷ (aadhi oonchai) = (b ÷ 2) ÷ (h ÷ 2)**, jo kat kar **tan θ = b ÷ h** ho jaata hai (uniform block ke liye). Ye θ hi **tilting angle** hai. Isse zyaada jhukane par block palat jaayega, isse kam par wapas aa jaayega. (Hum maan rahe hain ki block **phisalta nahi**, sirf kinare par ghoomta hai.)

**Aur khaas baat:** jab body uniform nahi hoti (jaise bhaari saaman wali bus), to "aadhi oonchai" ki jagah **CG ki asli oonchai H** lete hain: **tan θ = (aadha base) ÷ H**. Aage dono hi roop kaam aayenge.

### Example 1. Block ka palatne ka angle

Ek uniform lakdi ka block hai: base chaudai 0.20 m, oonchai 0.50 m. Use palatne ke liye kitna jhukana padega?

**Given:** b = 0.20 m, h = 0.50 m
**Find:** θ
**Formula:** tan θ = (b ÷ 2) ÷ (h ÷ 2)
**Substitution:** tan θ = 0.10 m ÷ 0.25 m = 0.40, to θ = **21.8°** lagbhag (calculator se)
**Answer:** Block lagbhag 21.8° jhukane par seema par aata hai; usse zyaada par palat jaayega.
**Jaanch:** tan 21.8° = 0.3999, lagbhag 0.40 ✅. Units: m ÷ m = bina unit ka sankhya, kone ke liye theek ✅.

### Activity 1: Matchbox ya kitaab ko girne tak jhukao (ghar par adult ke saath)

Ek khaali matchbox (ya moti kitaab) lo. Adult paas mein rahe, mez par kuch tuutne wala na ho.
1. Matchbox ko **khada** (sire par) rakho aur narrow side ki taraf dheere se ek kinare par jhukao. Dhyaan do ki wo kab palatta hai. (Matchbox lagbhag 5.5 cm lamba, 3.6 cm chauda, 1.5 cm moti hota hai: narrow side par tan θ = 0.75 ÷ 2.75, to θ lagbhag 15.3°: bahut kam jhukaav mein palat jaata hai.)
2. Ab **lita hua** (flat) rakho aur lambi kinare par jhukao: tan θ = 1.8 ÷ 0.75 = 2.4, to θ lagbhag 67.4°: bahut jhukaana padega.
3. Matchbox mein 4 chhote sikke tape se **tali par** chipkao aur phir (alag baar) **oopar** chipkao. Khada rakhkar dono baar jhukao. Kisme zyaada jhukaav par palatta hai? Tali wale mein, kyunki CG neeche hai.

Tumhare matchbox ka size thoda alag ho sakta hai; angle thoda alag aayega, par rule wahi rahega.

## Part D: Jab body uniform nahi: CG ki asli oonchai

Topic 3 ka formula yaad karo: do hisse hon to CG ki position = (m₁x₁ + m₂x₂) ÷ (m₁ + m₂). Yahan x ko **oonchai** (zameen se) maan lo.

### Example 2. Bus ke upar yaatri

Ek khaali bus ka mass 9000 kg hai, CG sadak se 1.1 m oopar, aur track width t = 2.4 m. Chhat par 20 yaatri baithte hain, har ek 60 kg, aur unka CG sadak se 2.6 m oopar hai. Bus kitne angle tak jhuk sakti hai (a) khaali, (b) yaatriyon ke saath, bina palte? (Sadak ka dhal itna ho sakta hai, bus phisalti nahi.)

```
       yaatri   ○ ○ ○ ○     ← 2.6 m par (1200 kg)
      +-----------------+
      [                 ]
      [        x        ]   CG 1.1 m par khaali bus (9000 kg)
      [                 ]
      [ (O)         (O) ]
 =================================== sadak
      <------ t = 2.4 m ------>
```

> **Figure 4.4** – Bus peeche se dekhi gayi (scale ke hisaab se nahi). Khaali bus ka CG neeche hai. Chhat par yaatri aate hain to saath ka CG oopar khisak jaata hai.

**Given:** M = 9000 kg at 1.1 m; yaatri m = 20 × 60 = 1200 kg at 2.6 m; t = 2.4 m (aadha base = 1.2 m)
**Find:** CG ki naya oonchai H, aur tilting angle (a) khaali, (b) yaatriyon ke saath
**Formula:** H = (M × 1.1 + m × 2.6) ÷ (M + m); tan θ = (t ÷ 2) ÷ H
**Substitution:**
- (a) Khaali: tan θ = 1.2 ÷ 1.1 = 1.091, to θ = **47.5°** lagbhag
- (b) H = (9000 × 1.1 + 1200 × 2.6) ÷ 10200 = (9900 + 3120) ÷ 10200 = 13020 ÷ 10200 = **1.276 m**; tan θ = 1.2 ÷ 1.276 = 0.940, to θ = **43.2°** lagbhag

**Answer:** Khaali bus lagbhag 47.5° tak, yaatriyon ke saath lagbhag 43.2° tak jhuk sakti hai: CG upar jaane se seema ghat gayi.
**Jaanch:** CG oopar to angle chhota ✅. Bhaari saaman ya zyaada yaatri chhat par honge to H aur bada hoga, aur angle aur ghatega ✅.

### Example 3. Table lamp: bhaari tala aur halka tala

Ek table lamp ka stem aur shade milakar 0.8 kg ka hai, aur unka CG tale se 0.25 m oopar hai. Lamp ka tala ek patli plate hai, base ki chaudai 0.18 m, plate ka CG tale ke bilkul paas 0.01 m par. Do tale hain: (a) bhaari tala 1.2 kg, (b) halka tala 0.4 kg. Dono mein tilting angle?

**Given:** upar ka hissa 0.8 kg at 0.25 m; tala 1.2 kg (ya 0.4 kg) at 0.01 m; b = 0.18 m (aadha = 0.09 m)
**Find:** CG ki oonchai H aur θ dono ke liye
**Formula:** H = (m₁x₁ + m₂x₂) ÷ (m₁ + m₂); tan θ = 0.09 ÷ H
**Substitution:**
- (a) H = (1.2 × 0.01 + 0.8 × 0.25) ÷ 2.0 = (0.012 + 0.20) ÷ 2.0 = **0.106 m**; tan θ = 0.09 ÷ 0.106 = 0.849, to θ = **40.3°** lagbhag
- (b) H = (0.4 × 0.01 + 0.8 × 0.25) ÷ 1.2 = (0.004 + 0.20) ÷ 1.2 = **0.170 m**; tan θ = 0.09 ÷ 0.170 = 0.529, to θ = **27.9°** lagbhag

**Answer:** Bhaari tala: 40.3°. Halka tala: 27.9°. Bhaari tala lamp ko zyaada stable banata hai, kyunki wo CG ko **neeche** laata hai.
**Jaanch:** Tale ka mass badhane se H 0.170 m se ghatkar 0.106 m hua, to angle bada hona chahiye ✅ (27.9° se 40.3°).

Dhyaan do: bhaari tala is liye madad karta hai ki wo CG neeche laata hai, sirf "bhaari hone" se nahi. Ye baat Aam galtiyan mein aur saaf karenge.

## Part E: Roly-poly kaise khadi hoti hai (restoring moment)

Ab gudiya ka raaz. Uska neeche ka hissa gol (chhote se gole ka tukda) hai, aur us golai ka kendra O gudiya ke andar ek jagah hai. Gudiya ke neeche bhaari (sikka ya patthar) bhara hota hai, isliye uska **CG, O se neeche** hota hai (O se doori d).

```
           ○ sir                                       ○ sir
           :                                          /
           :                                         /
           O  kendra                                O  kendra
           :                                       /:
           x G  (O se d neeche)                 G x :
          .:.                                     ↓W:
        ,-'   '-,                             ,-'   :   '-,
 =================================      =========================
                                                    ↑ N
                                                    C = O ke theek neeche
```

> **Figure 4.5** – Gudiya jhukti hai to wo apne gol tale par ghoomti hai; sampark point C hamesha O ke theek neeche rehta hai (schematic chitra, scale ke hisaab se nahi). Normal force N (upar) C par lagta hai, weight W (neeche) CG, G par. G, C ke **bayein** taraf aa jaata hai, e doori par. W aur N barabar, ulte, par alag lines mein: ek **couple** (Topic 1), jo gudiya ko wapas seedha ghumata hai.

W aur N barabar aur ulte hain, par ek hi line par nahi: **Topic 1 wala couple**! Moment = W × e. Ye gudiya ko seedha karta hai. Agar CG, O ke **upar** hota (d ulta, yaani CG oopar), to wahi couple gudiya ko aur gira deta aur wo **unstable** hoti.

### Example 4. Gudiya ka restoring moment

Gudiya ka mass 0.15 kg hai, uska CG golai ke kendra O se d = 0.02 m **neeche** hai. Gudiya 20° jhuki hai. G ki aadi doori e = d sin 20°, aur sin 20° = 0.342. Restoring moment nikalo.

**Given:** m = 0.15 kg, d = 0.02 m, θ = 20°, sin 20° = 0.342, g = 9.8 m/s²
**Find:** W, e aur restoring moment M
**Formula:** W = mg; e = d sin θ; M = W × e
**Substitution:** W = 0.15 × 9.8 = 1.47 N; e = 0.02 × 0.342 = 0.00684 m; M = 1.47 × 0.00684 = **0.0101 N m** lagbhag
**Answer:** Moment 0.0101 N m (lagbhag), gudiya ko wapas seedha ghumane wala.
**Jaanch:** Units: N × m = N m ✅. Agar d = 0 (CG theek O par) to e = 0 aur M = 0: neutral equilibrium (gend ki tarah) ✅. Agar CG O se 0.02 m **oopar** hota to wahi 0.0101 N m, par ulti disha mein, gudiya palat jaati ✅.

## Part F: Insaan, bhaar aur lambi imaarat

### Example 5. Bhaari saaman utha kar kyun peeche jhukte hain

Ek aadmi (55 kg) seedha khada hai: uska CG pairon ke beech mein hai, aur pair us CG ki seedhi line se **aage 0.10 m** tak aur **peeche 0.15 m** tak jaate hain (yahi uska base hai). Wo 25 kg ka saaman haathon mein, CG ki line se **0.40 m aage** pakadta hai. (a) Combined CG kitna aage khisakta hai? Kya wo gir jaayega? (b) Wo apna body-CG kitna peeche kare ki combined CG wapas pairon ke oopar aa jaaye?

**Given:** m₁ = 55 kg at x = 0; m₂ = 25 kg at x = 0.40 m (aage ki taraf positive); pair ke aage ka kinara 0.10 m
**Find:** combined CG ki sthiti, aur peeche jhukne ki doori s
**Formula:** x = (m₁x₁ + m₂x₂) ÷ (m₁ + m₂)
**Substitution:**
- (a) x = (55 × 0 + 25 × 0.40) ÷ 80 = 10 ÷ 80 = **0.125 m** aage. Ye 0.10 m se zyaada hai: line base ke **bahar**, to wo **aage gir jaayega**.
- (b) Body-CG s peeche khisakta hai: x = (55 × (−s) + 25 × 0.40) ÷ 80 = 0, to 55 s = 10, s = 10 ÷ 55 = **0.18 m** lagbhag

**Answer:** Saaman utha kar combined CG 0.125 m aage aata hai (palat sakta hai); lagbhag 0.18 m peeche jhukne se wo wapas pairon ke oopar aa jaata hai.
**Jaanch:** (55 × 0.18 = 9.9 ≈ 10 = 25 × 0.40) ✅. Isi liye saaman uthate hue hum aage nahi, **peeche** jhukte hain.

### Roz ki cheezein (padhne ke liye)

- **Leaning Tower of Pisa:** lagbhag 4° jhuka hua hai (sudhaar ke baad), phir bhi khada hai, kyunki uske CG ki seedhi line abhi bhi uske base ke **andar** girti hai. Engineers ne uski neev ke neeche se thodi mitti nikaalkar jhukaav kuch kam kiya (pehle ye lagbhag 5.5° tha), par ye shart nahi todi.
- **Tightrope walker ka lamba pole:** pole ke dono sire neeche jhulte hain, isse walker aur pole ka milaa hua CG **neeche** aata hai. CG neeche aane se santulan banaye rakhna aasaan ho jaata hai.
- **Seedhi (ladder):** deewar se tikaakar chadhne se pehle adult ko neeche se pakadna chahiye; iska poora hisaab (friction aur moments) aage ki class mein aayega. Yahan itna: seedhi ka base chauda ho to wo jyada tikti hai.
- **Racing car aur bus:** car neeche aur chaudi, bus oonchi aur sankri, isliye khaali bus bhi mod par jaldi palatti hai (agle topic mein circular motion dekhenge).

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti: "Jo cheez zyaada bhaari hai, wo hamesha zyaada stable hoti hai"**

Demonstration 1: Do uniform block lo, base 0.20 m ke dono. Block A: ooncha, h = 1.00 m, mass 50 kg. Block B: chhota, h = 0.20 m, mass 2 kg.
- A: tan θ = 0.10 ÷ 0.50 = 0.20, to θ = **11.3°** lagbhag
- B: tan θ = 0.10 ÷ 0.10 = 1.0, to θ = **45°**

Bhaari A sirf 11.3° par palat jaata hai, halka B 45° tak tikta hai. Mass kisi formula mein aaya hi nahi!

Demonstration 2: Ek hi block ko bhaari (8 kg) aur halka (2 kg) banao, base 0.20 m, oonchai 0.50 m. Dono ka tilting angle **same** (21.8°, Example 1). Haan, bhaari block ko **dhakka dekar** palatne ke liye zyaada force chahiye: CG ki oonchai 0.25 m par force F lagao to F × 0.25 = W × 0.10: 8 kg ke liye F = 78.4 × 0.10 ÷ 0.25 = 31.36 N, 2 kg ke liye F = 19.6 × 0.10 ÷ 0.25 = 7.84 N. Yaani bhaarapan **dhakke ko jhelne** mein madad karta hai, par **kis angle par palatta hai** ye mass par depend nahi karta.

**Sahi:** Stability CG ki oonchai aur base par tikti hai. Sirf bhaarapan kaafi nahi; bhaar ka **kahan** hona zaroori hai (Example 3 ka bhaari tala neeche CG laaya, isliye madad mili).

## Quick Recap

- **Stable:** hilane par CG oopar uthe, cheez wapas aaye. **Unstable:** CG neeche gire, cheez door jaaye. **Neutral:** CG ki oonchai wahi, cheez nayi jagah par bhi tiki.
- Stability badhti hai: **CG neeche, base chauda, aur CG ki seedhi line base ke andar**.
- Uniform block ka tilting angle: **tan θ = (b ÷ 2) ÷ (h ÷ 2) = b ÷ h**. Aam body ke liye **tan θ = (aadha base) ÷ H**, H = CG ki oonchai.
- Zyaada oonchaa load (bus ki chhat par) CG ko oopar karta hai aur θ ghatata hai.
- Roly-poly mein W aur N ka **couple** wapas seedha karta hai kyunki CG golai ke kendra se **neeche** hai.
- Saaman utha kar peeche jhukte hain, taaki combined CG pairon ke oopar rahe.
- Bhaarapan **akela** stability nahi deta: CG aur base deta hai.

## Practice Questions

1. A uniform block has a base width of 0.18 m and a height of 0.72 m. Through what angle can it be tilted about an edge before it topples? Does its mass change the answer?

2. A brick measures 0.23 m × 0.11 m × 0.075 m. It is tilted about an edge across its 0.075 m width when standing on its smallest face (height 0.23 m), and across its 0.23 m length when lying flat (height 0.075 m). Find the tilting angle for both and say which is more stable.

3. A bus has track width 2.5 m. Its CG is 1.35 m above the road when empty, and 1.90 m when carrying heavy luggage on the roof. Find the tilting angle in each case.

4. A truck of mass 6000 kg has its CG 1.25 m above the road and a track width of 2.1 m. A load of 3000 kg is placed on it with the load's CG at 2.6 m above the road. Find the new CG height and the tilting angle with and without the load.

5. A uniform solid cylinder of diameter 0.12 m and height 0.40 m stands upright on a rough plank. The plank is slowly raised at one end. At what slope angle does the cylinder topple (if it does not slide)? Will it topple on a 20° slope?

6. A table lamp has a base plate of mass 0.9 kg whose CG is 0.01 m above the table. The rest of the lamp is 0.6 kg with its CG 0.30 m above the table. (a) Find the CG height of the lamp. (b) For a base width of 0.16 m find the tilting angle. (c) What is the minimum base width for a tilting angle of 35°?

7. A roly-poly toy of mass 0.2 kg is tilted so that the weight and the normal force are 6 mm apart horizontally. Find the weight and the restoring moment. What would happen if the CG were above the centre of curvature?

8. A person of mass 62 kg stands with the front end of the feet 0.09 m ahead of his CG line. He holds a load of mass 18 kg at 0.35 m in front of his CG line. Will he topple forward? What if the load is 30 kg instead? (Use the combined mass for the CG.)

9. Classify each as stable, unstable or neutral equilibrium: (a) a cone standing on its tip, (b) a coin lying flat on a table, (c) a ball at the bottom of a bowl, (d) a rolling ball on a level floor at rest, (e) a pencil balanced on its sharp tip.

10. A uniform block of base 0.30 m and height 0.45 m lies on a plank that is slowly tilted (no sliding). At what angle does it topple? A second block of base 0.30 m and height 0.80 m: at what angle? Which block topples first, and why?

11. Two cars of equal mass: car A has CG 0.30 m above the road and a track of 1.6 m; car B has CG 0.55 m and a track of 1.5 m. Find the tilting angle for both and say which one resembles a racing car.

12. A uniform model tower has a base diameter of 14 m and a height of 55 m. (a) Find the angle at which it would topple. (b) A real tower leans about 4° and still stands: what does that tell you about its CG line? (The real tower is not uniform, so this is only a model.)

13. State True or False with a reason:
    (a) A heavier block always has a larger tilting angle.
    (b) A lower CG gives greater stability.
    (c) A ball on a flat table is in neutral equilibrium.
    (d) If the vertical line through the CG leaves the base, the body topples.

### Answers

1. **Given:** b = 0.18 m, h = 0.72 m. **Formula:** tan θ = (b ÷ 2) ÷ (h ÷ 2) = 0.09 ÷ 0.36 = 0.25, to θ = **14.0°** lagbhag. Mass formula mein nahi aaya, to **nahi** badalta.
   Jaanch: tan 14.0° = 0.249 ✅.

2. Khada (smallest face): tan θ = (0.075 ÷ 2) ÷ (0.23 ÷ 2) = 0.0375 ÷ 0.115 = 0.326, to θ = **18.1°** lagbhag. Lita hua: tan θ = (0.23 ÷ 2) ÷ (0.075 ÷ 2) = 0.115 ÷ 0.0375 = 3.067, to θ = **71.9°** lagbhag. Lita hua zyaada stable.
   Jaanch: 18.1° + 71.9° = 90° (dono ka tan ek doosre ka ulta) ✅.

3. Khaali: tan θ = 1.25 ÷ 1.35 = 0.926, to θ = **42.8°** lagbhag. Bhaari saaman ke saath: tan θ = 1.25 ÷ 1.90 = 0.658, to θ = **33.3°** lagbhag.
   Jaanch: CG oopar, angle ghata (42.8° se 33.3°) ✅.

4. H = (6000 × 1.25 + 3000 × 2.6) ÷ 9000 = (7500 + 7800) ÷ 9000 = 15300 ÷ 9000 = **1.70 m**. Bina load: tan θ = 1.05 ÷ 1.25 = 0.84, to **40.0°** lagbhag. Load ke saath: tan θ = 1.05 ÷ 1.70 = 0.618, to **31.7°** lagbhag.
   Jaanch: 1.25 × 6000 = 7500 aur 1.70 × 9000 = 15300 ✅; angle ghata ✅.

5. Cylinder uniform hai: tan θ = (0.12 ÷ 2) ÷ (0.40 ÷ 2) = 0.06 ÷ 0.20 = 0.30, to θ = **16.7°** lagbhag. 20° ki slope par: 20° > 16.7°, to wo **palat jaayega** (agar phisle nahi).
   Jaanch: tan 16.7° = 0.300 ✅.

6. (a) H = (0.9 × 0.01 + 0.6 × 0.30) ÷ 1.5 = (0.009 + 0.18) ÷ 1.5 = 0.189 ÷ 1.5 = **0.126 m**. (b) tan θ = 0.08 ÷ 0.126 = 0.635, to θ = **32.4°** lagbhag. (c) Aadha base = H × tan 35° = 0.126 × 0.700 = 0.0882 m, to base = 2 × 0.0882 = **0.176 m** lagbhag (17.6 cm).
   Jaanch: 0.0882 ÷ 0.126 = 0.700 = tan 35° ✅.

7. W = 0.2 × 9.8 = **1.96 N**. M = W × e = 1.96 × 0.006 = **0.01176 N m** lagbhag 0.0118 N m. Agar CG golai ke kendra ke **oopar** hota to wahi couple ulta hoga aur toy palat jaata (unstable).
   Jaanch: N × m = N m ✅.

8. Combined mass 62 + 18 = 80 kg, CG line se person 0, load 0.35 m: x = (62 × 0 + 18 × 0.35) ÷ 80 = 6.3 ÷ 80 = **0.079 m** aage. 0.079 < 0.09, to **nahi girega**. 30 kg ke liye: m = 92 kg, x = 30 × 0.35 ÷ 92 = 10.5 ÷ 92 = **0.114 m** > 0.09 m, to **girega** (jab tak peeche na jhuke).
   Jaanch: 18 × 0.35 = 6.3; 30 × 0.35 = 10.5 ✅.

9. (a) **Unstable** (hilane par CG neeche aata hai). (b) **Neutral** (lotne par CG ki oonchai wahi). (c) **Stable**. (d) **Neutral** (ek jagah se doosri par bhi ruka rehta hai). (e) **Unstable**.
   Jaanch: har ek mein CG ki oonchai ka badalna dekha ✅.

10. Pehla: tan θ = 0.15 ÷ 0.225 = 0.667, to θ = **33.7°** lagbhag. Doosra: CG 0.40 m par, tan θ = 0.15 ÷ 0.40 = 0.375, to θ = **20.6°** lagbhag. **Doosra** (ooncha block) pehle palatega, kyunki uska CG oopar hai aur uska θ chhota hai.
    Jaanch: tan 33.7° = 0.667, tan 20.6° = 0.376 ✅.

11. Car A: tan θ = 0.80 ÷ 0.30 = 2.667, to θ = **69.4°** lagbhag. Car B: tan θ = 0.75 ÷ 0.55 = 1.364, to θ = **53.7°** lagbhag. **Car A** racing car jaisi hai: CG neeche, track chauri.
    Jaanch: A ka angle zyaada ✅.

12. (a) Model ke liye CG oonchai = 27.5 m, aadha base = 7 m: tan θ = 7 ÷ 27.5 = 0.2545, to θ = **14.3°** lagbhag. (b) Real tower 4° par bhi khada hai, jo 14.3° wali seema se kaafi kam hai, matlab uske CG ki seedhi line abhi bhi uske base ke **andar** hai.
    Jaanch: tan 14.3° = 0.255 ✅.

13. (a) **False** ❌: angle sirf base aur CG ki oonchai par depend karta hai, mass par nahi. (b) **True** ✅: neeche CG, zyaada jhukaana padta hai. (c) **True** ✅: goli ki oonchai nahi badalti. (d) **True** ✅: tab weight ka moment cheez ko gira deta hai.
