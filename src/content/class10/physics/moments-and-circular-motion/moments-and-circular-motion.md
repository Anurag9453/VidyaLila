# Topic 1: Couple: Ghumaane Ka Jodidaar Force

Pichhle chapter mein hum gravity ke saath Earth se Moon aur satellites tak gaye. Is chapter mein hum wapas zameen par aate hain, par ek naye sawaal ke saath: **cheezein ghoomti kaise hain, aur ghoomte hue tikti kaise hain?** Class 9 ke Machines wale chapter mein tumne **moment of force** (force × perpendicular doori) aur **principle of moments** seekha tha. Aaj usi moment ka ek bahut khaas roop dekhenge: jab **do force ek saath, ek-doosre ke ulte** lagte hain. Isse **couple** kehte hain, aur ye hamare ghar ke taps, bottle caps aur steering wheel mein chhupa hai.

---

## Tap ki chaabi aur gol knob

Mr. Pardeshi ke garden mein ek purana tap tha, aur uske paas ek **T-shaped chaabi** (key) latki rehti thi. Unki bhanji Ovee chhutti mein aayi thi. Usne tap kholne ki koshish ki: chaabi ke dono sire do ungliyon se pakde aur ghuma di. Tap aaraam se khul gaya.

"Mama, ye to bahut halka hai!" Ovee boli. Phir usne bagal ke bathroom ka tap try kiya, jisme **chhota gol knob** laga tha. Wahan uski ungliyan phisal gayin, aur knob zor lagakar bhi mushkil se hila.

"Mama, dono tap ek jaise hain. Phir chaabi se itna aasaan aur gol knob se itna mushkil kyun?"

Mr. Pardeshi muskuraaye. "Chaabi ke **dono sire par do force** lagte hain, ek upar ki taraf aur ek neeche ki taraf. Dono barabar hain, par ulte. Aise jodidaar force ko physics mein **couple** kehte hain. Aur couple ka asar sirf force par nahi, **dono force ke beech ki doori** par depend karta hai. Chaabi chaudi hai, knob chhota. Chalo, isko naap kar dekhte hain."

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Couple** | Do force jo barabar hon, ulti disha mein hon, parallel hon, par ek hi line par na hon | Tap ki chaabi ko do ungliyon se ghumana |
| **Line of action** | Wo seedhi line jis par force kaam karta hai (force ke teer ko dono taraf badhao) | Force ke teer ki line |

| **Arm of the couple (d)** | Couple ke dono force ki lines ke beech ki seedhi (perpendicular) doori | Chaabi ki chaudai |
| **Rigid body** | Aisi cheez jo force lagne par bhi apni shakal nahi badalti | Ruler, chaabi, steering wheel |
| **Net force** | Saare forces ka jod (disha ke saath) | Couple mein net force zero |

## Part A: Pehle yaad karo: ek force ka moment

Class 9 mein dekha tha ki ek force kisi pivot ke around ghumane ka asar deta hai:

> **Moment = force × pivot se force ki line tak ki perpendicular doori**, unit **N m**

Ghadi ki soi ki disha mein ghumane wala moment **clockwise**, ulti disha mein **anticlockwise**. Aur agar chhad balance mein ho, to **clockwise moments ka jod = anticlockwise moments ka jod**, yani principle of moments.

**Recall example:** Ek spanner ko nut se 0.35 m door 14 N ka force seedhe 90° par lagta hai.

**Given:** F = 14 N, d = 0.35 m
**Find:** moment
**Formula:** M = F × d
**Substitution:** M = 14 N × 0.35 m = **4.9 N m**
**Answer:** 4.9 N m
**Jaanch:** 14 × 0.35 = 4.9 ✅. Doori aadhi (0.175 m) hoti to moment aadha, 2.45 N m ✅.

Ab ek nayi baat. Yahan ek **pivot** (nut) tha jo spanner ko tikaye hue tha. Agar koi pivot na ho, ek khuli cheez par do force lagayein, to kya hoga? Wahi ab dekhte hain.

## Part B: Ek force kisi khuli cheez ke saath kya karta hai

Ek chikni mez par ruler rakho (upar se dekho). Ruler ke ek sire par ek force lagao, uske **perpendicular** (aage ki taraf).

```
                       aage ki taraf
        ↑ F
   A ═══════════════════════════════ B
   (ruler, upar se drishya, sirf A par dhakka)
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Khuli ruler ke sire A par ek hi force F. Ruler **aage sarkegi bhi** aur **ghoomegi bhi**: ek force dono kaam karta hai.

Asli mez par friction hoti hai, par agar mez bahut chikni ho, to ruler aage bhi badhti hai (Newton ka doosra niyam: net force F hai, to poori ruler F = m a se accelerate hoti hai) aur A wala sira aage aate hue ghoomti bhi hai. Ek force = **chaal bhi badle, ghumna bhi**.

Ab ek **couple** lagao: ek sire par aage ka force, doosre sire par peeche ka force, **barabar**.

```
         ↑ F
   A ═══════════════════════════════ B
                                     ↓ F
   <───────────── d ───────────────>
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Couple: ruler ke sire A par F ek taraf (upar), sire B par F ulti taraf (neeche). Dono ki lines ke beech ki seedhi doori d hai.

Dono force barabar aur ulte hain, to **net force = F − F = 0**. Isliye ruler ka beech ka hissa **kahin nahi sarkta**, ruler apni jagah par rehkar sirf **ghoomti** hai (Figure 1.2 mein A ka sira upar, B ka neeche, to ruler clockwise ghoomegi).

### Activity 1: Pencil par couple (ghar par, koi hazard nahi)

Ek pencil ko mez par seedha rakho. **Sirf ek ungli** se pencil ke ek sire ko aage dhakelo: pencil aage bhi sarkegi aur ghoomegi bhi. Ab **do ungliyan** lo: ek sire par aage ki taraf, doosre sire par peeche ki taraf, **ek saath aur barabar zor se** dhakelo. Pencil lagbhag apni jagah par rehkar **chakkar** lagayegi, jaise compass ki sui. Ye couple hai.

### Activity 2: Bottle ka cap (ghar par adult ke saath)

Ek khaali plastic bottle ka cap kasa hua hai. **Ek ungli** se cap ke kinare ko side se dhakelo: bottle hi phisalne lagti hai. Ab **angoothe aur ungli** se cap ke do ulte kinaare pakdo aur ghumao: cap aaraam se ghoomta hai, bottle apni jagah par rehti hai. Angoothe aur ungli ke force ulte aur parallel hain, yani **couple**.

## Part C: Couple ka moment = F × d

Couple ka ghumane ka asar kitna hai? Maan lo rod AB par A par F neeche aur B par F upar lag raha hai, aur A se B ki doori d hai. Rod ke andar kisi point O ko pivot maano, jo A se x door aur B se (d − x) door hai.

```
   A ═══════════ O ═══════════════ B
   ↓ F                              ↑ F
   <──── x ─────><──── d − x ─────>
   <─────────────── d ────────────>
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Couple ka moment kisi bhi point O ke around nikalna: dono force O ko **ek hi disha** (anticlockwise) mein ghumana chahte hain.

- A wala force: F × x (anticlockwise)
- B wala force: F × (d − x) (anticlockwise)
- Jod = F x + F (d − x) = F d

x kuch bhi ho, **jod F × d hi aata hai**. Matlab couple ka moment **pivot ki jagah par depend nahi karta**: chahe O beech mein ho, kinare par ho ya rod ke bahar, hamesha F × d.

> **Moment of a couple = F × d**, F = ek force (N), d = dono force ki lines ke beech ki perpendicular doori (m)
> Unit: **N m**

**Ek imaandaar line unit ke baare mein:** N m likhne mein joule (J) jaisa dikhta hai, par yahan ye ghumane ka asar hai, work ya energy nahi. Isliye couple ke moment ko "J" mein mat likhna.

Couple ka sense (clockwise ya anticlockwise) bhi hota hai: wo ghumane ki disha jo dono force milkar deti hain.

## Part D: Solved examples

**Example 1 (kahani wali chaabi):** Mr. Pardeshi ki T-chaabi ke dono sire 8 cm door hain (8 cm = 0.08 m). Ovee har ungli se 1.5 N ka force lagati hai. (a) Couple ka moment? (b) Gol knob ka vyas 3 cm hai. Wahi tap kholne ke liye wahi moment chahiye, to har ungli ko kitna force lagana padega?

**Given:** F = 1.5 N, d = 0.08 m; knob par d' = 3 cm = 0.03 m, moment wahi
**Find:** M, aur knob par F'
**Formula:** M = F × d, F' = M ÷ d'
**Substitution:**
- M = 1.5 N × 0.08 m = **0.12 N m**
- F' = 0.12 N m ÷ 0.03 m = **4 N**

**Answer:** Chaabi se 0.12 N m; gol knob par har ungli 4 N.
**Jaanch:** 4 N × 0.03 m = 0.12 N m ✅. Doori 8 ÷ 3 = 2.67 guna chhoti hai, to force 1.5 × 2.67 = 4 N ✅.

Mr. Pardeshi bole, "Dekha Ovee? Chaabi mein force kam lagta hai kyunki **d bada** hai. Knob mein d chhota hai, to force zyaada lagana padta hai."

**Example 2 (khilone ka windmill):** Ek khilone ke windmill ke do blade ke tips ek-doosre se 0.2 m door hain. Hawa har tip par blade ke perpendicular 0.4 N ka force lagati hai, ek tip par ek taraf aur doosre par ulti taraf. Net force aur moment?

**Given:** F = 0.4 N (har tip par), d = 0.2 m, forces ulte
**Find:** net force aur M
**Formula:** net force = F − F, M = F × d
**Substitution:**
- Net force = 0.4 N − 0.4 N = **0 N**
- M = 0.4 N × 0.2 m = **0.08 N m**

**Answer:** Net force 0 N, moment 0.08 N m.
**Jaanch:** M = 0.4 × 0.2 = 0.08 ✅. Net force zero hai, to windmill hawa ke saath udta nahi, apni dandi par **tika rehkar ghoomta hai** ✅.

**Example 3 (bada steering wheel):** Ek bade steering wheel ka vyas 0.50 m hai. Use ghumane ke liye 4.5 N m ka moment chahiye. Driver dono haath wheel ke ulte kinaaron par tangent ki taraf lagata hai (couple). Har haath ka force? Agar wheel ka vyas aadha (0.25 m) hota?

**Given:** M = 4.5 N m, d = 0.50 m
**Find:** F, aur d = 0.25 m par F
**Formula:** F = M ÷ d
**Substitution:**
- F = 4.5 N m ÷ 0.50 m = **9 N** (har haath)
- d = 0.25 m par: F = 4.5 ÷ 0.25 = **18 N**

**Answer:** 9 N, aur aadhe vyas par 18 N.
**Jaanch:** 9 × 0.50 = 4.5 ✅ aur 18 × 0.25 = 4.5 ✅. Doori aadhi to force double ✅.

Ye wahi baat hai jo Class 9 mein wheel-and-axle mein dekhi thi: bada wheel = kam force. Ab uska "kyun" bhi pata chal gaya: bada d.

```
              .-----------.
           .'               '.
    ↑ F  (         O          )  ↓ F
           '.               .'
              '-----------'
    <──────── d = vyas ───────>
```

> **Figure 1.4** – Steering wheel par couple: bayein kinare par F upar ki taraf, dayein kinare par F neeche ki taraf. Dono lines ke beech ki doori wheel ka vyas d hai, aur wheel clockwise ghoomta hai.

**Example 4 (turnstile):** Ek turnstile ki do ulti baajuon ke sire dhure (axle) se 0.55 m door hain. Do log har sire par 22 N ka force ulti disha mein lagate hain. (a) Couple ka moment? (b) Dhure ki friction 16.5 N m ka rukawat-moment deti hai. Turnstile ko ghumane ke liye kam se kam har sire par kitna force chahiye?

**Given:** F = 22 N, har baaju 0.55 m, to d = 0.55 + 0.55 = 1.10 m; friction moment 16.5 N m
**Find:** M, aur F_min
**Formula:** M = F × d, F_min = rukawat ka moment ÷ d
**Substitution:**
- M = 22 N × 1.10 m = **24.2 N m**
- F_min = 16.5 N m ÷ 1.10 m = **15 N**

**Answer:** 24.2 N m; kam se kam 15 N har sire par.
**Jaanch:** 15 × 1.10 = 16.5 ✅. 22 N ka moment 24.2 N m, friction se zyaada (24.2 − 16.5 = 7.7 N m bacha), to turnstile ghoomta hai ✅.

Yahan d dono baajuon ki lambai ka **jod** hai, ek baaju ki lambai nahi.

**Example 5 (ek force versus couple, khuli cheez par):** Chikni mez par 0.4 kg ka lakdi ka tukda hai. (a) Ek sire par 3.2 N ka ek force lagao. (b) Do sire par 3.2 N ke ulte force (couple). Dono mein tukde ka acceleration kitna (ghumna ignore karo)?

**Given:** m = 0.4 kg, F = 3.2 N
**Find:** a (a) mein aur (b) mein
**Formula:** a = net force ÷ m
**Substitution:**
- (a) Net force = 3.2 N, a = 3.2 ÷ 0.4 = **8 m/s²**
- (b) Net force = 3.2 − 3.2 = 0 N, a = 0 ÷ 0.4 = **0 m/s²**

**Answer:** (a) 8 m/s², tukda sarakta hai; (b) 0 m/s², tukda sirf ghoomta hai.
**Jaanch:** 0.4 kg × 8 m/s² = 3.2 N ✅. Units: N ÷ kg = m/s² ✅.

**Example 6 (bottle cap: ek ungli versus couple):** Ek cap ka vyas 3.2 cm = 0.032 m hai. (a) Angoothe aur ungli se 3 N ka couple. (b) Ek ungli se kinare par 3 N, cap ke kendra ke around. Dono ke moment aur net force?

**Given:** F = 3 N, vyas 0.032 m, radius 0.016 m
**Find:** M aur net force, dono mein
**Formula:** couple: M = F × d; ek force: M = F × r
**Substitution:**
- (a) M = 3 N × 0.032 m = **0.096 N m**, net force **0 N**
- (b) M = 3 N × 0.016 m = **0.048 N m**, net force **3 N** (bottle bhi dhakelti hai)

**Answer:** Couple 0.096 N m; ek ungli 0.048 N m aur upar se bottle par 3 N ka dhakka.
**Jaanch:** 0.096 ÷ 0.048 = 2 ✅ (couple mein dono force alag-alag r × F dete hain, jod 2 F r). Couple ka fayda: double moment aur dhakka zero.

**Example 7 (pivot kahin bhi rakho):** Rod AB par A par 4.5 N neeche aur B par 4.5 N upar, AB = 0.24 m. Moment (a) seedha F × d se, (b) A se 0.10 m door ke point O ke around, (c) A ke 0.18 m bahar bayein taraf wale point P ke around.

**Given:** F = 4.5 N, d = 0.24 m
**Find:** moment, teen tarah se
**Formula:** M = F × d; ya moments ka jod
**Substitution:**
- (a) 4.5 × 0.24 = **1.08 N m**
- (b) O par: 4.5 × 0.10 + 4.5 × 0.14 = 0.45 + 0.63 = **1.08 N m** (dono anticlockwise)
- (c) P par: A 0.18 m door (clockwise 4.5 × 0.18 = 0.81) aur B 0.42 m door (anticlockwise 4.5 × 0.42 = 1.89), net = 1.89 − 0.81 = **1.08 N m** anticlockwise

**Answer:** Teeno jagah 1.08 N m.
**Jaanch:** Teeno barabar ✅: couple ka moment pivot par depend nahi karta.

## Part E: Roz ke couples

| Cheez | Couple kahan banta hai | d kya hai |
|---|---|---|
| Tap ki chaabi | Do ungliyon ke force chaabi ke ulte sire par | Chaabi ki chaudai |
| Steering wheel | Do haath ulte kinaaron par | Wheel ka vyas |
| Screwdriver | Ungliyan handle ko ulte side se ghumati hain | Handle ka vyas |
| Bottle cap | Angootha aur ungli | Cap ka vyas |
| Darwaze ki chaabi | Angootha aur ungli chaabi ke sir par | Chaabi ke sir ki chaudai |
| Do haath se spanner | Dono haath spanner ke ulte sire par | Dono haathon ke beech ki doori |
| Kainchi kholna | Angootha aur ungli ke force ulte | Dono ke beech ki doori |
| Turnstile | Do log ulti baajuon par | Dono baajuon ki lambai ka jod |

Ek imaandaar baat: ek haath se spanner ghumao, to nut ka bolt bhi spanner par ek force lagata hai, jo tumhare force se ulta hota hai. Isliye wahan bhi ek force-jodi ban jaati hai, par **bolt par side ka dhakka bhi lagta hai**. Do haath ke couple se ye side ka dhakka nahi lagta, aur isi liye kai auzaaron mein **dono sire par do force** lagana behtar hota hai.

### Lambi doori = kam force

M = F × d hai. M fixed ho, to d jitna bada, F utna kam. Example 1 aur 3 mein dekha. Isi liye tap ki chaabi lambi, aur steering wheel bada hota hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Couple matlab ek force jo double hai"**

Maan lo ek ruler par couple F + F lagta hai, aur koi kehta hai ye 2F ke ek force ke barabar hai. Example 5 ke 0.4 kg tukde par dekho: couple (3.2 N + 3.2 N) ka net force **zero** hai, to tukda sarakta nahi. Agar ek 6.4 N ka force (3.2 + 3.2) lagao, to a = 6.4 ÷ 0.4 = 16 m/s², tukda **bhaag jaata hai**. Couple do forces ka **jod** hai, par uska asar ek double force jaisa nahi. Couple sirf ghumata hai, ek force sarkata bhi hai.

**Galti 2: "Net force zero hai, to koi asar nahi"**

Tap ki chaabi (Example 1): net force 1.5 − 1.5 = 0 N hai, phir bhi tap 0.12 N m ke moment se khul jaata hai. Net force zero ka matlab sirf itna hai ki cheez **sarakti** nahi. **Ghumne** ka asar alag cheez hai: wo moment se aata hai, aur couple ka moment zero nahi hota jab tak d zero na ho. Pencil activity yaad karo: pencil ghoomti hai, par apni jagah par rehti hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Couple:** do barabar, ulte, parallel force jo ek hi line par nahi hain.
- Couple ka **net force = 0**, isliye wo cheez ko **sarkata nahi**, sirf **ghumata** hai.
- **Moment of couple = F × d** (N m), d = dono force ki lines ke beech ki perpendicular doori.
- Couple ka moment **pivot ki jagah par depend nahi karta** (Example 7).
- Lambi d = kam force: tap ki chaabi, steering wheel, spanner.
- Ek akela force khuli cheez ko **sarkata bhi hai aur ghumata bhi**; couple sirf ghumata hai.
- Unit N m, joule nahi (ye work nahi).

## Practice Questions

1. A tap key is 6 cm wide. Each of two fingers pushes with 2.8 N in opposite directions at its ends. Find the moment of the couple in N m. How much force must each finger apply to open a stiff tap that needs 0.336 N m?

2. A steering wheel has a diameter of 0.36 m. A driver applies 4.5 N with each hand at opposite ends of a diameter. Find the moment. What force would each hand need on a wheel of diameter 0.30 m for the same moment?

3. A bottle cap of diameter 4.0 cm needs a moment of 0.26 N m to open. (a) What force must the thumb and finger each apply? (b) What moment would a single 6.5 N finger give at the rim about the centre, and why can it not open the cap?

4. In a toy windmill, the tips of two opposite blades are 0.24 m apart. The wind pushes each tip with 0.35 N in opposite directions. Find the net force and the moment.

5. A hand-drill handle has two ends 0.18 m apart. A student pushes one end up and the other down, 24 N each. (a) Find the moment. (b) How long must the handle be to give 5.4 N m with the same 24 N?

6. Two people push opposite arms of a turnstile, each at 0.45 m from the axle, with 18 N each in opposite directions. (a) Find the moment of the couple. (b) The axle friction resists with 12.6 N m. Will the turnstile turn? What is the excess moment?

7. A light plastic ruler of mass 0.06 kg lies on a very smooth table. A single push of 0.9 N acts at one end. Find the acceleration of the ruler as a whole. What is the acceleration if a second 0.9 N push in the opposite direction is applied at the other end?

8. State True or False:
   (a) The net force of a couple is zero.
   (b) A couple can be balanced by a single force.
   (c) The moment of a couple is F × d, where d is the distance between the lines of the two forces.
   (d) A couple changes the speed of the centre of the body.
   (e) The unit of moment of a couple is N m.

9. A tap key is 6 cm wide and a round knob on another tap has a diameter of 2.5 cm. For the same moment, how many times more force does each finger need on the knob? Why?

10. A rod has forces of 3.5 N at its two ends, opposite and perpendicular to it, with the ends 0.40 m apart. Find the moment about (a) the middle of the rod, (b) a point 0.15 m outside one end.

11. A driver pulls the left side of a steering wheel down with 5.2 N and the right side up with 4.0 N. Is it a pure couple? What is the net force that the steering column must supply?

### Answers

1. 6 cm = 0.06 m. M = 2.8 N × 0.06 m = **0.168 N m**. Stiff tap: F = 0.336 N m ÷ 0.06 m = **5.6 N** har ungli.
   Jaanch: 5.6 × 0.06 = 0.336 ✅. Moment double (0.336 = 2 × 0.168), to force bhi double (5.6 = 2 × 2.8) ✅.

2. M = 4.5 N × 0.36 m = **1.62 N m**. Vyas 0.30 m par F = 1.62 ÷ 0.30 = **5.4 N**.
   Jaanch: 5.4 × 0.30 = 1.62 ✅. Chhota wheel, zyaada force ✅.

3. (a) 4.0 cm = 0.04 m. F = 0.26 ÷ 0.04 = **6.5 N** har ek. (b) Radius 0.02 m, M = 6.5 N × 0.02 m = **0.13 N m**, jo zaroori 0.26 N m ka aadha hai, aur ek ungli ka net force 6.5 N bottle ko bhi dhakelta hai.
   Jaanch: 6.5 × 0.04 = 0.26 ✅; 0.13 ÷ 0.26 = ½ ✅.

4. Net force = 0.35 − 0.35 = **0 N**. M = 0.35 N × 0.24 m = **0.084 N m**.
   Jaanch: 0.35 × 0.24 = 0.084 ✅.

5. (a) M = 24 N × 0.18 m = **4.32 N m**. (b) d = 5.4 N m ÷ 24 N = **0.225 m = 22.5 cm**.
   Jaanch: 24 × 0.225 = 5.4 ✅. 4.32 N m < 5.4 N m, to lambi handle chahiye ✅.

6. (a) d = 0.45 + 0.45 = 0.90 m. M = 18 N × 0.90 m = **16.2 N m**. (b) 16.2 > 12.6, to haan, ghoomega. Bacha hua moment = 16.2 − 12.6 = **3.6 N m**.
   Jaanch: 18 × 0.9 = 16.2 ✅; 16.2 − 12.6 = 3.6 ✅.

7. a = 0.9 N ÷ 0.06 kg = **15 m/s²** (ruler poori sarakti hai). Doosra force lagane par net force 0.9 − 0.9 = 0, to a = **0 m/s²**.
   Jaanch: 0.06 × 15 = 0.9 ✅.

8. (a) **True** ✅. Barabar aur ulte force.
   (b) **False** ❌. Ek force net force zero nahi rehne dega; couple ko sirf doosra couple balance karta hai.
   (c) **True** ✅. d = dono lines ke beech ki perpendicular doori.
   (d) **False** ❌. Net force zero, to centre ki chaal nahi badalti.
   (e) **True** ✅.

9. Same M, to F ∝ 1 ÷ d. Ratio = 6 ÷ 2.5 = **2.4 guna**. Knob mein d chhota hai.
   Jaanch: chaabi par F ho to knob par 2.4 F; 2.4 × 2.5 = 6 ✅.

10. Moment = F × d = 3.5 N × 0.40 m = **1.4 N m**, dono jagah. (a) Beech: 3.5 × 0.20 + 3.5 × 0.20 = 1.4 N m. (b) Bahar ka point: door wale force ki doori 0.15 + 0.40 = 0.55 m, paas wale ki 0.15 m, to 3.5 × 0.55 − 3.5 × 0.15 = 1.925 − 0.525 = **1.4 N m**.
    Jaanch: dono 1.4 ✅.

11. Force barabar nahi (5.2 N ≠ 4.0 N), to **pure couple nahi**. Net force = 5.2 − 4.0 = **1.2 N** (neeche ki taraf), jo steering column ko ulta force dekar sambhalna padta hai.
    Jaanch: 4.0 N ke saath couple 4.0 × d, aur bacha 1.2 N akela force ✅.

Ab jab bhi tap kholo, bottle ka cap ghumao ya steering wheel ghumao, yaad rakhna: do ulte force jab ghumate hain, to cheez apni jagah par rehti hai. Tum ye kar sakte ho!

**Aage kya?** Couple ne ek cheez ko sirf ghumaya. Par jab ek lambi beam par kai force lagte hain aur wo na ghoome na sarake, to wo kaise balance mein rehti hai? Do mazdoor ek baans par bhaari bori kaise uthate hain, aur bojh kisko zyaada lagta hai? Topic 2 mein **equilibrium** dekhenge.
