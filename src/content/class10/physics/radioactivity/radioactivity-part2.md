# Topic 2: Alpha, Beta, Gamma: Teen Kism Ki Kirnein

Topic 1 mein humne dekha ki kuch nuclei unstable hote hain aur apne aap, bina kisi dhakke ke, kuch chhodkar badalte hain; is chhodne ko hi radioactivity kehte hain. Par **chhodte kya hain?** Kya har nucleus ek hi cheez chhodta hai? Is topic mein tum teen alag "kism" ki kirnein pehchanoge: unka swabhav, unki bhedne ki shakti, aur ye ki magnet unhe kaise modta hai. Unhi se aage Topic 3 ka hisaab-kitaab bhi tay hoga.

## Part A: Teen kism, teen swabhav

| Baat | Alpha (α) | Beta (β) | Gamma (γ) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kya hai | He-4 nucleus (2 protons + 2 neutrons) | Tez electron | EM wave |
| Charge | +2e | −e | 0 |
| Mass | lagbhag 4 u | lagbhag 1/1836 u | 0 |
| Chaal (lagbhag) | c ka lagbhag 5 percent | c ka 99 percent tak | c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s |
| Ionising shakti | sabse zyada | beech ki | sabse kam |
| Bhedne ki shakti | sabse kam | beech ki | sabse zyada |
| Rokne wala | kaagaz ya hawa ke kuch cm | aluminium ke kuch mm | moti lead ya concrete (kam hoti hai, shunya nahi) |
| Electric ya magnetic field mein | thoda mudta hai | zyada, ulti taraf | nahi mudti |

(Beta ke saath ek aur bahut halka, bina charge ka kan bhi nikalta hai; uske baare mein aage ki class mein. Beta electron nucleus ke andar ke ek neutron ke badalne se banta hai: Topic 3 mein.)

**Example 1. Alpha, beta se kitna bhaari?**

Alpha ka mass lagbhag 4 u aur beta ka lagbhag 1/1836 u hai. Alpha kitne guna bhaari hai? Charge mein kya fark hai?

**Given:** m(α) = 4 u, m(β) = 1/1836 u, q(α) = +2e, q(β) = −e
**Find:** mass ka anupaat, charge ka anupaat
**Formula:** anupaat = m(α) ÷ m(β)
**Substitution:** 4 ÷ (1/1836) = 4 × 1836 = **7344**; charge: (+2e) ÷ (−e) = −2
**Answer:** Alpha lagbhag **7344 guna bhaari** hai; uska charge beta ka **do guna aur ulta** (positive) hai.
**Jaanch:** 7344 × (1/1836) = 4 ✅ (wapas alpha ka mass mila).

**Example 2. Nali mein kiran ka safar**

Ek hawa nikali hui (vacuum) 0.6 m lambi nali mein alpha (c ka 5 percent), sabse tez beta (c ka 99 percent) aur gamma ko ek chhor se doosre chhor tak jaana hai. Har ek ko kitna samay lagega? (c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s)

**Given:** L = 0.6 m
**Find:** t(α), t(β), t(γ)
**Formula:** v = (fraction) × c; t = L ÷ v
**Substitution:** v(α) = 0.05 × 3 × 10⁸ = 1.5 × 10⁷ m/s; v(β) = 0.99 × 3 × 10⁸ = 2.97 × 10⁸ m/s; v(γ) = 3 × 10⁸ m/s. t(α) = 0.6 ÷ (1.5 × 10⁷) = **4 × 10⁻⁸ s**; t(β) = 0.6 ÷ (2.97 × 10⁸) ≈ **2.02 × 10⁻⁹ s**; t(γ) = 0.6 ÷ (3 × 10⁸) = **2 × 10⁻⁹ s**
**Answer:** alpha 4 × 10⁻⁸ s, beta lagbhag 2.02 × 10⁻⁹ s, gamma 2 × 10⁻⁹ s.
**Jaanch:** alpha ka samay gamma se 20 guna zyada, kyunki uski chaal 5 percent = 1/20 hai (4 × 10⁻⁸ ÷ 2 × 10⁻⁹ = 20) ✅. Beta gamma se zara hi peeche hai (99 percent) ✅. Ye hisaab nali ke andar ka hai; khuli hawa mein alpha kuch cm mein hi ruk jaata hai (Part B).

## Part C: Ionisation aur ye ulta kram kyun?

Charge wala ya bahut tez kan jab kisi atom ke paas se guzarta hai, to wo atom ke ek electron ko dhakka maar kar **bahar nikaal** sakta hai. Atom ek positive ion ban jaata hai aur ek khula electron milta hai: **ionisation.** Gamma ke liye bhi yahi hota hai, par wo kam baar ionise karti hai (Chapter 5 mein wahi seema thi).

Har ionisation mein kan ki thodi energy kharch hoti hai. To **range** (kitni door tak jaayega) lagbhag aisa hai:

**range ≈ kan ki kul energy ÷ har cm mein kharch hui energy**

Alpha bhaari aur positive hai, dheema hai (5 percent c), to wo har atom ke paas "zyada der" rehkar bahut saare atoms ionise karta hai: har cm mein bahut energy kharch, isliye jaldi ruk jaata hai. Beta halka aur bahut tez hai, bahut kam ionise karta hai, to door tak jaata hai. Gamma ka koi charge nahi, wo atoms ke paas se bina takraye nikal jaati hai aur kabhi-kabhi hi ek ionisation karti hai: sabse door.

**Example 3. Energy ke sikke (model)**

Maan lo (ye ek model hai, asli sankhyayein nahi) ek kan ke paas 60 "energy ke sikke" hain. Alpha jaisa kan har cm hawa mein 15 sikke kharch karta hai, beta jaisa kan har cm mein sirf 0.2 sikka. Dono kitni door tak jaayenge?

**Given:** kul energy = 60 sikke, kharch: alpha 15 sikke per cm, beta 0.2 sikka per cm
**Find:** range
**Formula:** range = kul energy ÷ kharch per cm
**Substitution:** alpha: 60 ÷ 15 = **4 cm**; beta: 60 ÷ 0.2 = 300 cm = **3 m**
**Answer:** alpha 4 cm, beta 3 m (model mein).
**Jaanch:** 4 cm × 15 = 60 ✅ aur 300 cm × 0.2 = 60 ✅. Alpha ka 4 cm "kuch cm" wali asli baat se milta hai; beta metres tak jaata hai ✅. Jo zyada ionise kare, wo kam door jaata hai: ye ulta kram.

**Ionisation ke do chehre:** ye cells ke DNA ko nuksaan pahuncha sakta hai (isliye safety rules Topic 6 mein), aur yahi **detector** ka kaam bhi karta hai (Part E).

## Part D: Field mein deflection: Fleming ka left-hand rule

Chalta hua charge ek tarah ka **current** hai. Rule: **positive charge ki chaal ki disha = current ki disha; negative charge ke liye current ulta (chaal se ulti disha).** To magnetic field mein left-hand rule seedha lagta hai: forefinger = field, middle finger = current, thumb = force.

- **Alpha** (+2e): current chaal ki hi disha mein.
- **Beta** (−e): current chaal se ulti disha mein, isliye force **ulti taraf**.
- **Gamma:** charge nahi, isliye koi force nahi: seedhi.

Force ki disha ke saath bend ki matra bhi: bhaari aur dheeme alpha ko mudna mushkil hai, halke aur tez beta ko aasaan: isliye **alpha thoda, beta kaafi zyada** mudta hai. (Alpha ka charge do guna hota hai, par uska mass hazaaron guna zyada hai, to nateeja yahi: alpha kam mudta hai.)

**Example 4. Magnet ke beech upar jaati kirnein**

Ek source ek lead ke dibbe ke chhote sorakh se kirnein seedhi **upar (↑)** bhejta hai. Poore ilaake mein magnetic field **page ke andar (⊗)** hai. Teeno kiran kis taraf mudengi?

**Given:** chaal ↑, field ⊗
**Find:** alpha, beta, gamma ki disha
**Formula:** left-hand rule (alpha: current chaal ki taraf; beta: ulti)
**Substitution:** alpha: forefinger ⊗ (andar), middle finger ↑, thumb **left (←)**. Beta: current ulta, yaani ↓: forefinger ⊗, middle finger ↓, thumb **right (→)**. Gamma: koi charge nahi, seedha ↑.
**Answer:** alpha **left** mudta hai (thoda), beta **right** mudta hai (zyada), gamma **seedhi upar**.
**Jaanch:** Chapter 9 ke Example 4 ka case 5 (field ⊗, current ↓ → thumb right) beta se milta hai, aur alpha ka current ulta hai to thumb ulta (left) ✅. Dono ek-doosre se ulte hain ✅.

## Part E: Pakadne ke tareeke: Geiger–Müller counter

Kiran dikhti nahi, naam sunayi deti hai ya ginti hoti hai. **Geiger–Müller counter** ek gas (kam dabav par) bhari nali hai: beech mein ek patla tar, nali ki deewar aur tar ke beech ooncha voltage. Jab koi ionising kiran gas ke atoms ionise karti hai, to bane electrons tar ki taraf bhaagte hain aur khud aur atoms ionise karke ek chhota **pulse** banate hain: counter ek **click** deta hai. Click ki ginti ek minute mein = **count rate.**

```
        ##############################################
kiran   W . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .#
~~~~~>  W ------ beech ka patla tar (high voltage) --#===> pulse
        W . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .#
        ##############################################

W = patli window (mica)     . = gas bhari nali
```

> **Figure 2.4** – Geiger–Müller counter ka schematic: ek patli window (mica) se kiran andar aati hai, gas ko ionise karti hai, aur tar se ek pulse bahar nikalta hai jo click banta hai. Alpha ko patli window chahiye, kyunki wo moti deewar paar nahi kar sakta.

Do dhyan dene wali baatein: counter **kisi bhi ionising kiran** par click deta hai, isliye wo ye nahi batata ki kiran kaun si hai. Kaun si hai, ye parde (absorber) lagakar pata chalta hai (Example 6). Aur gamma kai baar gas ko chhue bina nikal jaati hai, to counter uska sirf chhota hissa hi pakadta hai.

**Cloud chamber** bhi ek upkaran hai: usme ionising kiran ka raasta bhaap ki patli lakeer ke roop mein dikhta hai (aam taur par alpha ka nishan mota aur seedha, beta ka patla aur tedha). Isse sirf naam se jaano; badi class mein padhoge.

**Example 6. Parde laga kar pehchaano (count rate)**

Ek anjaan source ke saamne counter rakha gaya (sab values maan lo, ek kalpanik data). Neeche readings hain:

| Beech mein parda | Count rate (counts per minute) |
|---|---|
| Koi nahi | 2400 |
| Ek kaagaz | 2400 |
| 5 mm aluminium | 400 |
| Moti lead (kai cm) | 40 |

Kaun si kirnein hain?

**Given:** ghat-badh upar ki table mein
**Find:** alpha, beta ya gamma ka hona
**Formula:** parde se jitna ghate = utni matra us kiran ki jo us parde ne roki
**Substitution:** kaagaz se kuch nahi ghata (2400 → 2400): **alpha nahi hai.** Aluminium se 2400 → 400: ghata 2000, yaani 2000 ÷ 2400 = lagbhag 83 percent **beta** hai. Aluminium ke baad bhi 400 bacha: ye **gamma** ka hissa hai. Lead mein 400 → 40: 90 percent kami, par shunya nahi.
**Answer:** source beta aur gamma deta hai; alpha nahi. Beta lagbhag 2000 counts per minute, gamma lagbhag 400.
**Jaanch:** 2000 + 400 = 2400 ✅. Lead ke baad bhi 40 bacha, yaani gamma shunya nahi hui ✅ (Part B ka sabak).

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Gamma bhi ek kan hai, to usme mass hoga"**

Demonstration: Mr. Bhoir ne Devdutt se kaha, "9 m ke gaaliyare mein light aur gamma ko kitna samay lagega?" Devdutt ne nikaala: t = 9 ÷ (3 × 10⁸) = 3 × 10⁻⁸ s: wahi jo light ka. Ab sabse tez beta (mass wala, 0.99 c): 9 ÷ (2.97 × 10⁸) ≈ 3.03 × 10⁻⁸ s, zara sa peeche. Devdutt: "To gamma ka koi charge nahi: kya isse mass bhi zero ho gaya?" Chacha: "Nahi: neutron ka bhi charge nahi hota, par uska mass hota hai (U-235 se nikla neutron: Chapter 3). Mudna nahi mudna charge ka saboot hai, mass ka nahi. Gamma ke mass-heen hone ka saboot hai Chapter 5 ki table: gamma EM wave hai, radio aur roshni ki hi family ki, aur c par chalti hai; mass wala beta bahut tez ho sakta hai, par c tak pahunchna uske liye mumkin nahi (ye aage ki class mein)."

**Sahi:** gamma = EM wave: **na charge, na mass.** Beta aur alpha kan hain, wo mass wale hain.

**Galti 2: "Alpha sabse bada hai, isliye sabse zyada bhedta hai"**

Demonstration: bhaari-pan aur range ki table dekho (lagbhag values):

| Kiran | Mass | Hawa mein range |
|---|---|---|
| Alpha | lagbhag 4 u (sabse bhaari) | kuch cm |
| Beta | lagbhag 1/1836 u | kuch metre |
| Gamma | 0 | bahut door (aur lead mein ghat-ghat kar) |

Agar "jitna bhaari, utna door" sahi hota to kram ulta hota. Alpha bada hai, par bhaari aur charge wala hone se **har atom se zyada takraata hai** (Part C): har takkar mein energy jaati hai, isliye wo kaagaz par ruk jaata hai. Jaise bheed wali gali mein bada, bhaari saaman dheere aur ruk-ruk kar chalta hai, aur ek patla teer seedha nikal jaata hai.

**Sahi:** alpha **sabse kam bhedta, sabse zyada ionise karta** hai. (Ek nuance: bahar se ye kamzor hai, par saans ya khaane ke saath body ke andar chala jaaye to sidhe cells ke paas hota hai: Topic 6 mein poori baat.)

## Quick Recap

- Nucleus teen kism ki kirnein chhodta hai: **alpha (He-4 nucleus, +2e, lagbhag 4 u)**, **beta (tez electron, −e)**, **gamma (EM wave, na charge na mass)**.
- Chaal: alpha lagbhag 5 percent c, beta 99 percent c tak, gamma c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s.
- Bhedne ki shakti: gamma > beta > alpha; rokne wale: kaagaz (alpha), kuch mm aluminium (beta), moti lead ya concrete (gamma ko kam karte hain, shunya nahi).
- Ionising shakti ulta kram hai: alpha sabse zyada, gamma sabse kam; range ≈ energy ÷ kharch per cm.
- Field mein: alpha aur beta ulti taraf mudte hain (left-hand rule; beta ke liye current ulta), alpha kam, beta zyada; gamma seedhi.
- Geiger–Müller counter ionisation se pulse banata hai, count rate deta hai; kiran kaun si hai ye parde lagakar tay hota hai.
- Radioactive source kabhi chhoona nahi: trefoil dekho, door raho, bade ko batao.

## Practice Questions

1. Three rays are tested. Ray P bends towards the negative plate of an electric field and is stopped by a sheet of paper. Ray Q bends towards the positive plate, passes through paper and is stopped by a few mm of aluminium. Ray R is not bent by any field and is only reduced, never stopped, by thick lead. Name each ray and state its charge.

2. A source sends all three radiations to the right (→) and the field is out of the page (⊙). Use the left-hand rule to find the direction in which each bends. Explain why one of them bends more than the other.

3. A source above the page sends the rays down (↓) in a field that is out of the page (⊙). State, with your finger directions, which way alpha and beta bend, and whether gamma bends.

4. Two horizontal plates are connected so that the top plate is positive and the bottom plate is negative. A beam of alpha, beta and gamma rays enters from the left, moving horizontally between the plates. Which way does each ray bend? Which of alpha and beta bends more?

5. A counter is placed in front of a source and shows these count rates (maan lo): no absorber 7200 counts per minute, with a sheet of paper 4800, with paper and 4 mm of aluminium 4800, with paper, aluminium and 6 cm of lead 480. Which radiations does the source emit? How many counts per minute come from each kind?

6. Beta particles in a lab tube travel at 0.60 of c (assume this, maan lo). How long does one take to cross a 1.35 m long vacuum tube? How long does a gamma ray take for the same length? By how much is the gamma ahead? (c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s)

7. Use the energy-coins model: a particle has 66 coins. Particle X spends 22 coins per cm of air, particle Y spends 0.11 coin per cm. Find the range of each and say which one behaves like alpha and which like beta, with a reason.

8. A source emits alpha, beta and gamma. It is covered first by a sheet of paper, then by 8 mm of aluminium, then by 5 cm of lead. After each layer, which radiations are left?

9. Why does a Geiger–Müller counter that detects alpha need a very thin window, while a thick steel wall around it would still let gamma through? Answer in two or three sentences.

10. A thin aluminium plate absorbs 80 percent of the beta particles falling on it (maan lo). If 9000 beta particles per minute fall on the plate, how many pass through? Would the same plate change the count of gamma much?

11. State True or False, and correct the false ones: (a) alpha particles are helium nuclei. (b) A beta particle bends more than an alpha particle in the same field. (c) Gamma rays are stopped completely by 1 mm of lead. (d) A Geiger–Müller counter can tell which kind of radiation it has detected by itself.

12. A student shines a torch through more and more sheets of tissue paper to model radiation. State one thing this model shows correctly and one thing it gets wrong about alpha particles.

### Answers

1. **P = alpha** (negative plate ki taraf = positive charge, kaagaz rokta hai): charge **+2e**. **Q = beta** (positive plate ki taraf = negative, aluminium rokta hai): charge **−e**. **R = gamma** (field se nahi mudti, lead se sirf ghatti hai): charge **0**.
   **Jaanch:** teeno swabhav Part A ki table se mile ✅.

2. Alpha: current →, field ⊙: forefinger ⊙, middle finger →, thumb **down (↓)**. Beta: current ←: thumb **up (↑)**. Gamma seedhi. Beta zyada bend hota hai kyunki wo bahut halka aur tez hai; alpha bhaari aur dheema hai, mudna mushkil. **Jaanch:** Example 5 mein field ⊗ tha aur alpha up tha; field ulta to alpha down ✅.

3. Alpha: current ↓, field ⊙: forefinger ⊙, middle finger ↓, thumb **left (←)**. Beta: current ↑: thumb **right (→)**. Gamma nahi mudti. **Jaanch:** Example 4 (↑, ⊗: alpha left) mein chaal ulti aur field ulta: dono ulte, to force wahi **left** ✅ (alpha), beta **right** ✅.

4. Alpha positive hai, negative (neeche wali) plate ki taraf: **neeche (↓)**. Beta negative hai, positive (upar wali) plate ki taraf: **upar (↑)**. Gamma seedhi. Beta zyada bend hota hai (halka aur tez). **Jaanch:** ulte charge ulti taraf ✅.

5. **Given:** 7200, 4800, 4800, 480. Kaagaz se ghata 7200 − 4800 = 2400: **alpha 2400 counts per minute.** Aluminium se koi kami nahi (4800 → 4800): **beta nahi.** Lead se 4800 → 480: gamma ka hissa; wo 4800 gamma ke hain, aur lead ne use 90 percent ghataya (480 ÷ 4800 = 0.1). **Answer:** alpha aur gamma; **alpha 2400, gamma 4800 counts per minute** (7200 − 2400 = 4800 ✅). Lead ke baad bhi 480 bacha: gamma shunya nahi hui ✅.

6. v(β) = 0.60 × 3 × 10⁸ = 1.8 × 10⁸ m/s. t(β) = 1.35 ÷ (1.8 × 10⁸) = **7.5 × 10⁻⁹ s**. t(γ) = 1.35 ÷ (3 × 10⁸) = **4.5 × 10⁻⁹ s**. Gamma aage: 7.5 × 10⁻⁹ − 4.5 × 10⁻⁹ = **3 × 10⁻⁹ s**. **Jaanch:** 1.8 × 10⁸ × 7.5 × 10⁻⁹ = 1.35 ✅.

7. range = 66 ÷ spend. X: 66 ÷ 22 = **3 cm**; Y: 66 ÷ 0.11 = **600 cm = 6 m**. X alpha jaisa hai (zyada ionise karta hai, kam range), Y beta jaisa (kam ionise, zyada range). **Jaanch:** 3 × 22 = 66 ✅ aur 600 × 0.11 = 66 ✅.

8. Paper ke baad: **beta aur gamma** (alpha ruk gaya). 8 mm aluminium ke baad: **gamma** (beta ruk gaya; aluminium gamma ko bahut kam rokta hai). 5 cm lead ke baad: **gamma ki bahut ghati hui matra**, shunya nahi. **Jaanch:** Part A ki table ke rokne wale se milta ✅.

9. Alpha moti deewar paar nahi kar sakta, wo hawa ke kuch cm mein ya kaagaz par ruk jaata hai, isliye gas tak pahunchne ke liye patli window zaroori hai. Gamma ki bhedne ki shakti bahut zyada hai, wo moti steel ke paar bhi gas tak pahunch sakti hai. (Counter ka sirf chhota hissa gamma pakadta hai, par kuch pakadta zaroor hai.)

10. Pass hone wale = 100 − 80 = 20 percent. 9000 × 0.20 = **1800 beta per minute**. Gamma ki count lagbhag wahi rahegi, kyunki patli aluminium gamma ko bahut kam rokti hai. **Jaanch:** 1800 + (9000 × 0.80 = 7200) = 9000 ✅.

11. (a) **True** ✅. (b) **True**: beta halka aur tez hai, ✅. (c) **False:** 1 mm lead gamma ko poora nahi rokti; moti lead use ghatati hai, shunya nahi. (d) **False:** counter har ionising kiran par click deta hai; kaun si hai ye parde lagakar pata chalta hai.

12. **Sahi dikhata hai:** jitna zyada material, utni kam roshni (jaise gamma dheere-dheere ghatti hai). **Galat dikhata hai:** alpha ek patle kaagaz par ek jhatke mein ruk jaata hai (uski tay seema hai), torch ki roshni ki tarah dheere-dheere kam nahi hota; aur torch ki roshni radioactive nahi hoti.
