# Topic 3: Nuclear Equations: Decay Ka Hisaab

Topic 1 mein dekha tha ki radioactivity mein nucleus khud-ba-khud badalta hai, aur jab protons ki ginti (Z) badalti hai to **naya tatva** ban jaata hai. Topic 2 mein humne teen kirnon, alpha, beta aur gamma, ko unke gun aur mudne se pehchaana. Ab ek sawaal bacha hai: jab nucleus koi kan chhodta hai, to **baaki bacha nucleus kaun-sa hai**? Iska jawab andaaza lagakar nahi, ek chhote, saaf hisaab se milta hai: **do ginti hamesha barabar rehti hain.**

## Film ka hara pathar aur Chacha ki copy

Itwaar ki dopahar Mr. Hardas ke ghar TV par ek purani science-fiction film chal rahi thi. Scene mein ek vaigyanik ek **chamakte hare pathar** ko kaanch ke dibbe mein dekhta hai. Dekhte-dekhte pathar ka rang badalta hai, chamak bujh jaati hai aur wo ek saadharan bhoora pathar ban jaata hai. Vaigyanik chillata hai, "Tatva badal gaya! Ek pathar doosra ban gaya!"

Bhatiji Urmila ne uchhal kar poochha, "Chacha, kya sach mein koi tatva doosra tatva ban sakta hai? Ya ye sirf film ka jaadu hai? Aur agar ban sakta hai, to kaise pata chalega ki ban kya raha hai?"

"Ye ek **hisaab** hai, Urmila. Teeno ke upar-neeche ki ginti dekh. Pehle bata, ye sahi kyun hai?" Urmila ne ungli se ginti ki aur dheere se boli, "238 aur 234 + 4... Chacha, ye to barabar hai!" Mr. Hardas muskuraye, "Bas, yahi hai aaj ka poora topic: har decay ki equation mein **ginti barabar** rehni chahiye, aur isi se pata chalta hai ki naya nucleus kaun-sa banega."

## Part A: Z aur A ka ek chhota recall

Class 9 ([structure-of-the-atom-part6.md](../../../class9/chemistry/structure-of-the-atom/structure-of-the-atom-part6.md)) se chaar baatein yaad rakho:

- **Z** (atomic number) = protons ki ginti. Z se hi tatva ki pehchaan hai.
- **A** (mass number) = protons + neutrons. Neutrons = A − Z.
- Ek hi tatva ke alag A wale atom **isotopes** hain (jaise C-12, C-13, C-14).
- Naam ke saath A likhte hain: U-238 ka matlab uranium, A = 238, Z = 92.

Class 10 Physics ke [fission wale topic](../sources-of-energy/sources-of-energy-part5.md) mein ek nuclear equation pehle hi dikhi thi, aur uski jaanch bhi isi tarah thi: **235 + 1 = 141 + 92 + 3** aur **92 = 56 + 36**. Wahan bhi upar ki ginti aur protons ki ginti barabar nikli. Aaj hum yehi dhang **decay** ke liye banate hain: har equation ke liye ek chhota, do-line ka hisaab.

Tatvon ke naam aur Z ke liye [periodic table](../../../class9/chemistry/periodic-table/periodic-table.md) dekho. Is topic mein hum in Z ka use karenge: C = 6, N = 7, Co = 27, Ni = 28, I = 53, Xe = 54, Ra = 88, Rn = 86, Th = 90, Pa = 91, U = 92, Pb = 82. (Ye Z is topic ki chhoti list hai; naam aur Z periodic table se bhi milaa sakte ho.)

### Do ginti jo kabhi nahi badalti

Kisi bhi decay mein, teer ke baayein aur daayein, do cheezein barabar rehti hain:

1. **A ka jod** (nucleons ki kul ginti): nucleons na paida hote hain na khatam, bas idhar-udhar baante jaate hain.
2. **Z ka jod** (kul charge, proton ke charge ki ginti mein): kul charge na banta hai na mitta hai.

Isliye har equation ke neeche hum ye do line likhenge: **A: ... = ... + ...** aur **Z: ... = ... + ...**. Beta kan ke liye hum likhenge **A = 0** aur **Z = −1**, kyunki uska charge −e hai aur uska mass ek nucleon ke mukaable bahut hi kam hai (lagbhag 1 ⁄ 1836 u, itna kam ki ginti mein 0 maana jaata hai). Gamma ka A = 0 aur Z = 0 hai (na charge, na mass).

Ek imaandaar baat: **A ek ginti hai**, kilogram wala mass nahi. Asli mass dekhein to parent ka mass daughter aur kan ke jod se **thoda zyada** hota hai. Wo chhoti kami ki energy ban kar nikalti hai (E = mc², jaisa fission wale topic mein tha). Is topic mein hum nucleons ki ginti hi jodte hain, aur wo hamesha barabar aati hai.

## Part B: Alpha decay: A mein 4 ki kami, Z mein 2 ki kami

Alpha kan He-4 hai: 2 protons aur 2 neutrons, to **A = 4, Z = 2**. Jab nucleus ek alpha chhodta hai, to usme se 4 nucleons aur 2 protons chale jaate hain. Isliye:

> **Alpha decay:** daughter ka **A = (parent ka A) − 4** aur **Z = (parent ka Z) − 2**. Tatva do kadam peeche (periodic table mein) chala jaata hai.

### Example 1. U-238 ka alpha decay

**Given:** parent U-238 (Z = 92); alpha He-4 nikalta hai; Th ka Z = 90.
**Find:** daughter nucleus.
**Formula:** A_d = A − 4; Z_d = Z − 2.
**Substitution:** A_d = 238 − 4 = 234; Z_d = 92 − 2 = 90, yaani thorium.
**Answer:** U-238 → Th-234 + He-4
**Jaanch:** A: 238 = 234 + 4 ✅; Z: 92 = 90 + 2 ✅

Yahi wo line thi jo Mr. Hardas ne copy mein likhi thi.

### Example 2. Ra-226 ka alpha decay

**Given:** parent Ra-226 (Z = 88); alpha nikalta hai; Rn ka Z = 86 (radon, ek bina rang ki gas).
**Find:** daughter.
**Formula:** A_d = A − 4; Z_d = Z − 2.
**Substitution:** A_d = 226 − 4 = 222; Z_d = 88 − 2 = 86, yaani radon.
**Answer:** Ra-226 → Rn-222 + He-4
**Jaanch:** A: 226 = 222 + 4 ✅; Z: 88 = 86 + 2 ✅

Ek tatva (radium, thos dhaatu) se bilkul alag tatva (radon, gas) ban gaya: film ka "pathar badalna" asli duniya mein is tarah hota hai, bas chamak ke bina.

Teen rule ek saath dekhne ke liye ek chhota naksha banate hain. Parent nucleus ko (A, Z) par rakho:

```
           A - 4         A
Z + 1        .          beta
Z            .         PARENT
Z - 1        .           .
Z - 2      alpha         .
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Parent nucleus (A, Z) se teen badlav, A ko baayein-daayein aur Z ko upar-neeche padhte hue: **alpha** ek jagah baayein (A − 4) aur do jagah neeche (Z − 2) le jaata hai. **Beta** wahi A rakhta hai aur ek jagah **upar** (Z + 1) le jaata hai. **Gamma** kahin nahi le jaata: nucleus wahi (A, Z) par rehta hai, bas energy kam ho jaati hai.

## Part C: Beta decay aur gamma

### Electron nucleus se kaise nikalta hai?

Beta kan ek **electron** hai (charge −e), par wo electron **nucleus ke andar pehle se baitha nahi tha** aur wo atom ke bahar ghoomne wale electrons mein se bhi koi nahi hai. Beta decay mein nucleus ke andar ek **neutron badal kar proton ban jaata hai**, aur usi pal ek electron **paida hota hai** aur bahar nikal jaata hai:

neutron → proton + electron (+ ek aur bahut halka kan, antineutrino: iske baare mein aage ki class mein)

Charge ka hisaab: 0 = (+1) + (−1) ✅. Antineutrino ka na charge hai na ginti mein A, isliye A aur Z ke hisaab par uska koi asar nahi padta. Isliye hum use is chapter ki equations mein nahi likhenge.

Iska ek seedha nateeja: nucleons ki ginti wahi rahi (ek neutron kam, ek proton zyada), par **protons ek badh gaye**:

> **Beta decay:** daughter ka **A = parent ka A** (wahi) aur **Z = (parent ka Z) + 1**. Tatva periodic table mein ek kadam **aage** chala jaata hai.

### Example 3. C-14 ka beta decay

**Given:** parent C-14 (Z = 6); beta (e⁻) nikalta hai (A = 0, Z = −1); N ka Z = 7.
**Find:** daughter.
**Formula:** A_d = A; Z_d = Z + 1.
**Substitution:** A_d = 14; Z_d = 6 + 1 = 7, yaani nitrogen.
**Answer:** C-14 → N-14 + e⁻
**Jaanch:** A: 14 = 14 + 0 ✅; Z: 6 = 7 − 1 ✅

Dhyan do: carbon ka ek atom nitrogen ka atom ban gaya, aur Class 9 ke museum-wale carbon-14 ki kahani isi badlav par tiki hai (uske badalne ki raftaar ka hisaab Topic 4 mein).

### Example 4. Th-234 ka beta decay

**Given:** parent Th-234 (Z = 90); beta nikalta hai; Pa ka Z = 91.
**Find:** daughter.
**Formula:** A_d = A; Z_d = Z + 1.
**Substitution:** A_d = 234; Z_d = 90 + 1 = 91, yaani protactinium.
**Answer:** Th-234 → Pa-234 + e⁻
**Jaanch:** A: 234 = 234 + 0 ✅; Z: 90 = 91 − 1 ✅

Ye wahi Th-234 hai jo Example 1 mein U-238 ke alpha decay se bana tha. Is tarah **ek daughter agle decay ki parent ban jaati hai**: yahi decay series ka beej hai (Part E).

### Example 5. I-131 ka beta decay

**Given:** parent I-131 (Z = 53), jo thyroid ke ilaaj mein aata hai; beta nikalta hai; Xe ka Z = 54 (xenon, ek bina rang ki gas).
**Find:** daughter.
**Formula:** A_d = A; Z_d = Z + 1.
**Substitution:** A_d = 131; Z_d = 53 + 1 = 54, yaani xenon.
**Answer:** I-131 → Xe-131 + e⁻
**Jaanch:** A: 131 = 131 + 0 ✅; Z: 53 = 54 − 1 ✅

Ek thos tatva (iodine) se ek gas (xenon) ban gaya. Mass number 131 wahi raha, isliye Xe-131 ko I-131 ka **isobar** bhi kehte hain (Class 9 ka shabd: same A, alag Z).

### Example 6. Co-60: beta ke baad gamma

**Given:** parent Co-60 (Z = 27), jo cancer ke ilaaj ki gamma kirnon ka source hai; beta nikalta hai; Ni ka Z = 28.
**Find:** beta wali equation aur gamma wala kadam.
**Formula:** beta: A_d = A, Z_d = Z + 1; gamma: A aur Z wahi.
**Substitution:** A_d = 60; Z_d = 27 + 1 = 28, yaani nickel. Nickel ka ye nucleus **josheela** (excited) bachta hai.
**Answer:**
Kadam 1: Co-60 → Ni-60 (josheela) + e⁻
Kadam 2: Ni-60 (josheela) → Ni-60 + gamma
**Jaanch:** Kadam 1: A: 60 = 60 + 0 ✅; Z: 27 = 28 − 1 ✅. Kadam 2: A: 60 = 60 + 0 ✅; Z: 28 = 28 + 0 ✅

**Gamma ka niyam:** nucleus apni extra **energy** electromagnetic wave ki tarah chhod deta hai (Chapter 5 ke Topic 5 wali gamma ray: [scattering-and-spectrum-part5.md](../scattering-and-spectrum/scattering-and-spectrum-part5.md)). Is mein na koi nucleon jaata hai, na koi charge. Isliye **A aur Z nahi badalte, tatva wahi rehta hai**: jaise ek khiladi daud ke baad sirf saans chhodta hai, khud wahi khiladi rehta hai. Aksar alpha ya beta ke theek baad gamma aata hai, kyunki naya bana nucleus josheela hota hai.

## Part D: Anjaana daughter ya anjaana kan nikalna

Kabhi daughter pata nahi hoti, kabhi nikla hua kan. Tarika wahi: **A ka sawal** aur **Z ka sawal** alag-alag hal karo.

### Example 7. Anjaana kan: Pa-234 ka decay

**Given:** Pa-234 (Z = 91) → U-234 (Z = 92) + ? ; kan ka A aur Z nahi diya.
**Find:** kan ka A, Z aur naam.
**Formula:** A: parent = daughter + kan; Z: parent = daughter + kan.
**Substitution:** A: 234 = 234 + A_kan, to A_kan = 0. Z: 91 = 92 + Z_kan, to Z_kan = −1.
**Answer:** A = 0, Z = −1 wala kan electron hai: **beta kan**. Pa-234 → U-234 + e⁻
**Jaanch:** A: 234 = 234 + 0 ✅; Z: 91 = 92 − 1 ✅

### Example 8. Anjaani daughter: Th-230 ka alpha decay

**Given:** Th-230 (Z = 90) alpha chhodta hai; Ra ka Z = 88.
**Find:** daughter.
**Formula:** A_d = A − 4; Z_d = Z − 2.
**Substitution:** A_d = 230 − 4 = 226; Z_d = 90 − 2 = 88, yaani radium.
**Answer:** Th-230 → Ra-226 + He-4
**Jaanch:** A: 230 = 226 + 4 ✅; Z: 90 = 88 + 2 ✅

### Naam kaise nikaalein?

Daughter ka Z nikalne ke baad periodic table mein us Z ka tatva dekho. Ek chhoti table:

| Z | 86 | 88 | 90 | 91 | 92 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tatva | Rn (radon) | Ra (radium) | Th (thorium) | Pa (protactinium) | U (uranium) |

Alpha decay mein tum table mein **do kadam peeche** jaate ho (jaise U 92 se Th 90), aur beta decay mein **ek kadam aage** (jaise Th 90 se Pa 91).

## Part E: Decay series: U-238 se Pb-206 tak

Daughter bhi aksar radioactive hoti hai, to wo bhi badalti hai, aur phir wo bhi. U-238 se shuru hoke ek **lamba silsila** chalta hai, jab tak ek **stable** nucleus na mil jaaye. Is silsile ke shuru ke kadam ab tak hamare examples mein aa chuke hain. Unhe **Z (upar-neeche) aur A (baayein-daayein)** ke naksha par rakhte hain:

```
        226       230       234       238
Z=92      .         .       U-234     U-238
Z=91      .         .       Pa-234      .
Z=90      .       Th-230    Th-234      .
Z=89      .         .         .         .
Z=88    Ra-226      .         .         .
```

> **Figure 3.2** – U-238 se Ra-226 tak ke shuru ke paanch kadam, Z (neeche ki taraf kam) aur A (baayein ki taraf kam). Kadam: U-238 se Th-234 (alpha: A − 4, Z − 2), Th-234 se Pa-234 (beta: Z + 1), Pa-234 se U-234 (beta: Z + 1), U-234 se Th-230 (alpha), Th-230 se Ra-226 (alpha). Alpha baayein-neeche ki taraf aur beta seedhe upar ki taraf ek hi column mein jaata hai.

Paanch kadam mein 3 alpha aur 2 beta hue. **Jaanch:** A = 238 − 3 × 4 = 226 ✅; Z = 92 − 3 × 2 + 2 × 1 = 88 ✅ (Ra-226, wahi jo Example 2 mein tha).

### Example 9. Poore silsile mein kitne alpha aur kitne beta?

U-238 aakhir mein **Pb-206** (Z = 82) par ruk jaata hai. Silsila lamba hai (beech mein radon, polonium, bismuth jaise tatva bhi aate hain), par hume har kadam likhne ki zaroorat nahi: sirf **shuru aur ant** ka hisaab kaafi hai.

**Given:** U-238 (Z = 92) se Pb-206 (Z = 82) tak; alpha: A − 4, Z − 2; beta: A wahi, Z + 1.
**Find:** alpha ki ginti (x) aur beta ki ginti (y).
**Formula:** A: 238 − 4x = 206; Z: 92 − 2x + y = 82.
**Substitution:** A se: 4x = 238 − 206 = 32, to x = 8. Z se: 92 − 16 + y = 82, to y = 82 − 76 = 6.
**Answer:** **8 alpha aur 6 beta**.
**Jaanch:** A: 238 − 8 × 4 = 206 ✅; Z: 92 − 8 × 2 + 6 × 1 = 82 ✅

Dekha? **A sirf alpha se ghatta hai** (beta usse chhuta nahi), isliye A ka sawal pehle hal hota hai. Phir Z ke hisaab mein beta ka hissa nikalta hai.

### Silsila Pb-206 par kyun ruka?

Pb-206 **stable** nucleus hai: wo khud-ba-khud koi alpha, beta ya gamma nahi chhodta. Silsile ke har kadam par nucleus "zyada tikau" ki taraf badhta hai, aur Pb-206 par wo tikau ban jaata hai. Kyun kuch nuclei tikau hote hain aur kuch nahi, ye Topic 1 ki baat hai (protons aur neutrons ka santulan); yahan yaad rakhna kaafi hai ki **stable matlab aage decay nahi**.

### Example 10. Ek alpha aur do beta: wahi tatva, naya isotope

**Given:** U-238 (Z = 92) ek alpha chhodta hai, phir daughter do beta chhodti hai (aap Figure 3.2 mein Th-234, Pa-234, U-234 dekh chuke ho).
**Find:** antim nucleus ka A aur Z, aur kya wo U-238 ka isotope hai?
**Formula:** A: 238 − 4 (alpha) + 0 + 0; Z: 92 − 2 (alpha) + 1 + 1 (do beta).
**Substitution:** A = 234; Z = 92 − 2 + 2 = 92.
**Answer:** antim nucleus **U-234** hai: Z = 92 (wahi, to wahi tatva uranium) aur A = 234 (alag). Yaani **U-238 ka isotope**.
**Jaanch:** Z: 92 = 92 ✅; A: 238 − 234 = 4, yaani sirf alpha ka 4 ✅ (beta ne A nahi badla).

Isi ko saaf shabdon mein yaad rakho: **ek alpha aur do beta** milkar Z ko wapas wahi bana dete hain (−2 + 2), isliye tatva wahi rehta hai, sirf A chaar kam ho jaata hai. Alpha ne tatva ko do kadam peeche bheja aur do beta ne ek-ek kadam karke wapas laa diya.

## Part F: Galat equation pakdo

Ek achcha sawaal: "ye equation sahi hai ya nahi?" Jawab do ginti se milta hai.

### Example 11. Teen equation, ek-ek ki jaanch

Urmila ne Mr. Hardas ki copy mein teen equation likhin aur Chacha ne pucha, "Sahi kaun-si hai?" (Ni ka Z = 28, N ka Z = 7, Rn ka Z = 86 ho.)

(i) C-14 → N-15 + e⁻
(ii) Ra-226 → Rn-224 + He-4
(iii) Co-60 → Ni-60 + e⁻

**Formula:** har equation mein A ka jod aur Z ka jod, dono dekho.
**Substitution:**
(i) A: 14 aur 15 + 0 = 15, barabar nahi. Z: 6 aur 7 − 1 = 6, barabar.
(ii) A: 226 aur 224 + 4 = 228, barabar nahi. Z: 88 aur 86 + 2 = 88, barabar.
(iii) A: 60 aur 60 + 0 = 60, barabar. Z: 27 aur 28 − 1 = 27, barabar.
**Answer:** (i) galat (A nahi mila, sahi daughter N-14); (ii) galat (A nahi mila, sahi daughter Rn-222); (iii) **sahi**.
**Jaanch:** (i) ko sudhaar kar 14 = 14 + 0 ✅; (ii) ko sudhaar kar 226 = 222 + 4 ✅

**Dono ginti mein se ek bhi na mile, to equation galat.** Dono milti hain to equation ginti ke hisaab se sahi hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Beta decay mein mass number badal jaata hai."**
Nahi: beta decay mein **A wahi rehta hai**, sirf Z ek badhta hai. Demonstration: Th-234 mein 90 protons aur 144 neutrons hain, kul 90 + 144 = 234 nucleons. Beta decay ke baad Pa-234 mein 91 protons aur 143 neutrons hain, kul 91 + 143 = 234 nucleons. Ek neutron kam, ek proton zyada, par kul nucleons wahi 234. Agar koi likhe "Th-234 → Pa-235 + e⁻", to A ka jod 234 aur 235 + 0 = 235, barabar nahi: galat. Beta ne nucleons ki ginti nahi badli, sirf ek neutron ko proton mein badla.

**Galti 2: "Beta decay ka electron nucleus ya atom mein pehle se ghoom raha tha."**
Nahi: wo **us pal paida hota hai** jab ek neutron proton ban jaata hai. Demonstration: ek neutron neutral hai (charge 0). Wo ek proton (+1) aur ek electron (−1) mein badalta hai: 0 = (+1) + (−1), charge ka hisaab barabar. Agar electron atom ke bahar ke shell se aaya hota, to nucleus mein kuch badalta hi nahi aur tatva wahi rehta (I-131 ka Z 53 hi rehta), jabki asal mein Z badhkar 54 ho jaata hai aur xenon ban jaata hai. Taali bajane se awaaz paida hoti hai, par wo awaaz taali ke andar pehle se nahi thi: electron bhi isi tarah **ban kar** nikalta hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Nuclear equation:** parent → daughter + kan. Do ginti barabar: **A ka jod** aur **Z ka jod**. Har equation ka do-line hisaab.
- **Alpha decay:** A − 4, Z − 2 (He-4 nikalta hai). Tatva do kadam peeche.
- **Beta decay:** A wahi, Z + 1 (e⁻ nikalta hai, A = 0, Z = −1). Neutron proton banta hai. Electron us pal paida hota hai. Tatva ek kadam aage.
- **Gamma:** A aur Z wahi, nucleus bas energy chhodta hai. Aksar alpha ya beta ke baad.
- Shuru aur ant se hisaab: pehle **A** (sirf alpha se ghatta hai), phir **Z** (alpha −2, beta +1).
- U-238 se Pb-206: **8 alpha aur 6 beta** (238 − 32 = 206; 92 − 16 + 6 = 82). Pb-206 stable hai, isliye silsila ruka.
- Ek alpha aur do beta: Z wahi, A − 4 kam: **isotope**.

## Practice Questions

Use these atomic numbers where needed: Po 84, Bi 83, Pb 82, Rn 86, Y 39, Mg 12, S 16, Np 93, U 92. In every question write the equation and the two-line A and Z check.

1. Rn-222 (Z = 86) emits an alpha particle. Find the A and Z of the daughter and name the daughter element.
2. Pb-214 (Z = 82) emits a beta particle. Write the equation, and find the A and Z of the daughter and its name.
3. Sr-90 (Z = 38) decays by beta emission. Find the A, Z and the name of the daughter.
4. Na-24 (Z = 11) emits a beta particle to become Mg-24 (Z = 12), and the Mg-24 nucleus is left excited. Write both steps: the beta step and the gamma step. Does the gamma step change the element? Explain.
5. P-32 (Z = 15) decays to S-32 (Z = 16) by beta emission. (a) Write the equation. (b) Count the protons and neutrons in the parent and the daughter and show that the total number of nucleons is the same.

Which one is correct by A and Z balance? For each wrong one, say which of A or Z fails.

(d) The electron in beta decay was orbiting the nucleus before the decay. (e) One alpha and two beta emissions together give an isotope of the parent.
12. An unknown nucleus M becomes Bi-212 (Z = 83) by emitting a beta particle. Find the A and Z of M and its name.

### Answers

1. **Given:** Rn-222, Z = 86, alpha nikalta hai. **Formula:** A_d = A − 4; Z_d = Z − 2. **Substitution:** A_d = 222 − 4 = 218; Z_d = 86 − 2 = 84, yaani polonium. **Answer:** Rn-222 → Po-218 + He-4. **Jaanch:** A: 222 = 218 + 4 ✅; Z: 86 = 84 + 2 ✅
2. **Given:** Pb-214, Z = 82, beta. **Formula:** A_d = A; Z_d = Z + 1. **Substitution:** A_d = 214; Z_d = 82 + 1 = 83, yaani bismuth. **Answer:** Pb-214 → Bi-214 + e⁻. **Jaanch:** A: 214 = 214 + 0 ✅; Z: 82 = 83 − 1 ✅
3. **Given:** Sr-90, Z = 38, beta. **Substitution:** A_d = 90; Z_d = 38 + 1 = 39, yaani yttrium. **Answer:** Sr-90 → Y-90 + e⁻. **Jaanch:** A: 90 = 90 + 0 ✅; Z: 38 = 39 − 1 ✅
4. **Kadam 1 (beta):** Na-24 → Mg-24 (josheela) + e⁻. **Jaanch:** A: 24 = 24 + 0 ✅; Z: 11 = 12 − 1 ✅. **Kadam 2 (gamma):** Mg-24 (josheela) → Mg-24 + gamma. **Jaanch:** A: 24 = 24 + 0 ✅; Z: 12 = 12 + 0 ✅. **Jawab:** gamma step **element nahi badalta**, kyunki A aur Z wahi rehte hain: nucleus sirf extra energy chhodta hai, koi proton ya neutron nahi jaata.
5. (a) P-32 → S-32 + e⁻. **Jaanch:** A: 32 = 32 + 0 ✅; Z: 15 = 16 − 1 ✅. (b) P-32: protons 15, neutrons 32 − 15 = 17, kul 15 + 17 = 32. S-32: protons 16, neutrons 32 − 16 = 16, kul 16 + 16 = 32. **Answer:** ek neutron kam (17 se 16), ek proton zyada (15 se 16), nucleons kul 32 hi rahe ✅
6. **Given:** Bi-210, Z = 83, beta. **Substitution:** A_d = 210; Z_d = 83 + 1 = 84, yaani polonium. **Answer:** Bi-210 → Po-210 + e⁻. Daughter ka A **alag nahi** (210 hi). **Jaanch:** A: 210 = 210 + 0 ✅; Z: 83 = 84 − 1 ✅
7. **Given:** Am-241, Z = 95, alpha. **Substitution:** A_d = 241 − 4 = 237; Z_d = 95 − 2 = 93, yaani neptunium. **Answer:** Am-241 → Np-237 + He-4. **Jaanch:** A: 241 = 237 + 4 ✅; Z: 95 = 93 + 2 ✅
8. (a) **Formula:** A: 232 − 4x = 208; Z: 90 − 2x + y = 82. **Substitution:** 4x = 232 − 208 = 24, to x = 6. Z: 90 − 12 + y = 82, to y = 82 − 78 = 4. **Answer:** **6 alpha aur 4 beta**. **Jaanch:** A: 232 − 6 × 4 = 208 ✅; Z: 90 − 6 × 2 + 4 × 1 = 82 ✅. (b) Pb-208 **stable** hai: wo khud-ba-khud koi alpha, beta ya gamma nahi chhodta, isliye aage koi decay nahi.
9. (i) A: 200 = 196 + 4 = 200 ✅. Z: 80 = 78 + 2 = 80 ✅. **Sahi.** (ii) A: 200 = 196 + 4 ✅, par Z: 80 aur 79 + 2 = 81, **Z fail**. (iii) A: 200 = 200 + 0 ✅, par Z: 80 aur 79 − 1 = 78, **Z fail** (sahi Z = 81). (iv) A: 200 aur 201 + 0 = 201, **A fail**. (Z: 80 aur 81 − 1 = 80 ✅.) **Jaanch:** sirf (i) mein dono ginti milti hain ✅
10. **Formula:** A: 244 − 2 × 4 = 236. Z: 94 − 2 × 2 + 2 × 1. **Substitution:** Z = 94 − 4 + 2 = 92. **Answer:** A = 236, Z = 92: **U-236, uranium**. Parent ka Z = 94 tha aur ab Z = 92, to **isotope nahi** (alag tatva). **Jaanch:** Z: 94 = 92 + 4 − 2 (do alpha ke 4 wapas jodo, do beta ke 2 hatao) ✅; A: 244 = 236 + 8 ✅
11. (a) **False** ❌: gamma mein A aur Z wahi, tatva wahi. (b) **True** ✅: alpha mein Z − 2. (c) **False** ❌: beta mein A wahi rehta hai, Z + 1 hota hai. (d) **False** ❌: electron us pal neutron ke proton banne par paida hota hai. (e) **True** ✅: Z: −2 + 1 + 1 = 0, to Z wahi, A − 4: isotope.
12. **Given:** M → Bi-212 + e⁻, Bi Z = 83. **Formula:** A: A_M = 212 + 0; Z: Z_M = 83 + (−1). **Substitution:** A_M = 212; Z_M = 83 − 1 = 82, yaani lead. **Answer:** M = **Pb-212**: Pb-212 → Bi-212 + e⁻. **Jaanch:** A: 212 = 212 + 0 ✅; Z: 82 = 83 − 1 ✅
