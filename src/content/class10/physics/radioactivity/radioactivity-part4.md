# Topic 4: Half-Life: Aadha Kab Bachta Hai?

Topic 3 mein humne dekha ki ek nucleus toota to A aur Z kaise badalte hain: ek nucleus ka hisaab. Ab ek bada sawaal: ek chammach bhar radioactive padarth mein karodon-arabon nuclei hote hain, to **poora sample kitni jaldi khatam** hota hai? Topic 1 mein ye baat thi ki har nucleus ka toot-na random aur apne aap hota hai. Is topic mein dekhenge ki random hone ke baad bhi, bahut saare nuclei ke liye ek bahut pakka niyam nikalta hai: **half-life**. Isi se hospital ka injection, reactor ka kachra aur purani naav ki umar, teeno ka hisaab hota hai.

---

## Coin Decay ka khel aur lakdi ki naav

School ke science fair mein "Coin Decay" ka stall tha. Mr. Ingale apne bhanje Naveen ko wahan le gaye. Teacher ke saath ek bachchi ne ek badi tray mein 100 sikke daale, tray hilayi, aur phir jin sikkon par **tails** aaya unhe nikaal kar alag rakh diya. Bache hue sikke gine: 52. Phir wahi: sirf bache hue sikke tray mein, tray hilao, tails nikalo. Is baar 24. Naveen ne kaha, "Mama, har round mein lagbhag aadhe nikal jaate hain!"

"Haan," Mr. Ingale ne kaha, "par ye batao, kya tum bata sakte ho ki **kaun sa** sikka agle round mein hatega?" Naveen ne sir hilaya: "Nahi, wo to toss par hai." "Bas, yahi random hona hai, aur phir bhi **ginti** ka pattern pakka hai."

Stall ke baahar ek akhbaar ki cutting lagi thi: nadi ke kinare ki khudai mein mili ek purani lakdi ki naav ki photo, neeche likha: "Umar lagbhag 11,000 saal se zyada, carbon-14 se tay ki gayi." Naveen ne pucha, "Mama, ye kaise? Kisne naav ke saath janam-tithi likhi thi kya?" Mr. Ingale hanse: "Koi tithi nahi likhi thi. Ek 'ghadi' naav ki lakdi ke andar hi chalti hai, wahi sikkon wali ghadi: aadha, phir aadha, phir aadha. Chalo samjhte hain."

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |

### Half-life ki paribhasha

> **Half-life (T½):** wo samay jisme kisi sample ke **aadhe radioactive nuclei** decay ho jaate hain. Barabar baat: wo samay jisme sample ki **activity** (kitne nuclei har second toot rahe hain) **aadhi** reh jaati hai.

Activity aadhi isliye hoti hai ki jab nuclei aadhe bachte hain, to toot-ne waale bhi aadhe hi rehte hain.

Ek zaroori baat: decay ka matlab nucleus ka **gayab ho jaana nahi** hai. Co-60 → Ni-60 + e⁻ (A: 60 = 60 + 0; Z: 27 = 28 − 1 ✅) jaise decay mein nucleus naye tatva ka ban jaata hai (Topic 3). Isliye jab hum kehte hain "32 mg bacha", wo **radioactive isotope ka** mass hai; baaki 32 mg ab doosre tatva ban chuka hai.

## Part B: Halving ka staircase

Agar shuru mein N₀ nuclei hain, to har half-life ke baad bacha hua hissa pichhle ka aadha hota jaata hai.

| Half-lives ki ginti n | Bacha hua hissa | Percent | Nuclei (N₀ = 64 maan lo) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 | 100 | 64 |
| 1 | 1 ÷ 2 | 50 | 32 |
| 2 | 1 ÷ 4 | 25 | 16 |
| 3 | 1 ÷ 8 | 12.5 | 8 |
| 4 | 1 ÷ 16 | 6.25 | 4 |
| 5 | 1 ÷ 32 | 3.125 | 2 |
| 6 | 1 ÷ 64 | lagbhag 1.56 | 1 |

Ye ek simple niyam deta hai:

**N = N₀ ÷ 2ⁿ, jahan n = t ÷ T½** (jab t, T½ ka poora guna ho).

Yaani "2 ka n-th power" se bhaag do. n = 4 par 2⁴ = 16, isliye N = N₀ ÷ 16.

```
   N
  32 +*
     +
     +
     +
  24 +
     +
     +
     +
  16 +         *
     +
     +
     +
   8 +                  *
     +
     +                           *
     +                                    *        *
   0 +-----------------------------------------------
      0        1T       2T       3T       4T       5T
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Halving ka curve (N₀ = 32 maan lo): har T-half ke baad nuclei aadhe: 32, 16, 8, 4, 2, 1. Curve neeche aata hai par kabhi seedha zero nahi chhoota: 5T par bhi 1 bacha. (Aakhri do bindu, 4T aur 5T, graph ki chhoti ooNchai ki wajah se ek hi row par aa gaye.)

Curve ki ek aur pehchaan: shuru mein tez girta hai (bade N mein se zyada nuclei toote), phir dheere (kam bache to kam toot-te hain).

### Example 1. Tc-99m ke nuclei

Ek hospital ke Tc-99m sample mein shuru mein 3.2 × 10¹² nuclei the. Tc-99m ki T-half lagbhag 6 ghante hai. 18 ghante baad kitne radioactive nuclei bachenge?

**Given:** N₀ = 3.2 × 10¹², T½ = 6 h, t = 18 h
**Find:** N
**Formula:** n = t ÷ T½; N = N₀ ÷ 2ⁿ
**Substitution:** n = 18 ÷ 6 = 3; N = 3.2 × 10¹² ÷ 2³ = 3.2 × 10¹² ÷ 8
**Answer:** N = **4 × 10¹¹ nuclei**
**Jaanch:** 3.2 × 10¹² → 1.6 × 10¹² → 0.8 × 10¹² → 0.4 × 10¹² = 4 × 10¹¹ ✅ (teen baar aadha)

### Example 2. Mass ke roop mein

Ek I-131 sample ka mass 56 mg hai. I-131 ki T-half lagbhag 8 din hai. 24 din baad kitna I-131 bachega, aur kitna doosre tatva mein badal chuka hoga?

**Given:** m₀ = 56 mg, T½ = 8 d, t = 24 d
**Find:** bacha I-131 aur badla hua hissa
**Formula:** n = t ÷ T½; m = m₀ ÷ 2ⁿ
**Substitution:** n = 24 ÷ 8 = 3; m = 56 ÷ 8
**Answer:** bacha I-131 = **7 mg**; badla hua = 56 − 7 = **49 mg**
**Jaanch:** 56 → 28 → 14 → 7 mg ✅; 7 + 49 = 56 ✅ (badla hua hissa naye tatva ke atoms hain; gayab nahi hua)

### Example 3. Ulta: kitna samay beeta?

Ek Co-60 source ki activity 12.5 percent reh gayi hai. Co-60 ki T-half lagbhag 5.3 saal hai. Kitna samay beeta?

**Given:** bacha hissa = 12.5 percent = 1 ÷ 8, T½ = 5.3 y
**Find:** t
**Formula:** 1 ÷ 2ⁿ = bacha hissa; t = n × T½
**Substitution:** 1 ÷ 8 = 1 ÷ 2³, to n = 3; t = 3 × 5.3
**Answer:** t = **15.9 saal**
**Jaanch:** 100 → 50 → 25 → 12.5 percent: teen half-lives ✅; 3 × 5.3 = 15.9 ✅

Ulta hisaab karne ka tareeka: bache hue hisse ko "1 ÷ 2 ka kuch power" mein likho, ya table mein dhundo; wo power hi n hai.

## Part C: Carbon dating, ek ghadi jo lakdi ke andar chalti hai

Jeevit ped, hawa se CO₂ le kar carbon banate hain, jisme thoda C-14 (radioactive) aur zyaadatar C-12 (stable) hota hai. Jeevit rehte hue ped (aur use khaane waale jeev) mein **C-14 ka C-12 se anupaat** lagbhag ek jaisa bana rehta hai, kyunki naya carbon aata rehta hai. (Upar ki hawa mein cosmic rays se naya C-14 banta rehta hai, isliye ye hawa mein bhi lagbhag sthir rehta hai; ye aage ki baat hai.) Ped kat jaaye ya jeev mar jaaye to naya carbon aana band ho jaata hai, aur bacha hua C-14 apni T-half (lagbhag 5730 saal) ke hisaab se kam hota jaata hai:

C-14 → N-14 + e⁻
A: 14 = 14 + 0; Z: 6 = 7 − 1 ✅

Ye equation Topic 3 ke tareeke se balanced hai. Ab ginti:

```
   Kaat-ne ke samay (0 saal)    ##########################  100 percent
   5730 saal baad               #############               50 percent
   11,460 saal baad             ######                      25 percent
   17,190 saal baad             ###                         12.5 percent
```

> **Figure 4.3** – Kati hui lakdi mein C-14 ka bacha hua hissa har 5730 saal mein aadha hota hai (har # = lagbhag 4 percent). Naav ki lakdi mein jitna kam C-14, utni purani lakdi.

### Example 4. Naav ki umar

Nadi kinare ki khudai ki naav ki lakdi mein C-14 ka anupaat, abhi ke jeevit ped ke C-14 anupaat ka sirf ek-chauthai (1 ÷ 4) mila. Naav ki lakdi kitni purani hai? (C-14 ki T-half lagbhag 5730 saal lo.)

**Given:** bacha hissa = 1 ÷ 4, T½ = 5730 y
**Find:** t
**Formula:** n se, t = n × T½
**Substitution:** 1 ÷ 4 = 1 ÷ 2², to n = 2; t = 2 × 5730
**Answer:** t = **11,460 saal** (lagbhag)
**Jaanch:** 100 → 50 → 25 percent ✅, 2 × 5730 = 11,460 ✅

Yani Naveen ki akhbaar wali photo ka "11,000 saal se zyada" is hisaab se milta-julta hai. (Ye "umar", kati hui lakdi ki hai, yaani us ped ko kaate hue kitna samay hua.)

### Carbon dating ki seemayein

- Ye sirf **kisi jeev se bani** cheezon (lakdi, haddi, kapda, kagaz) par chalta hai; patthar ki umar iss se nahi nikalti. Bahut purane patthar ya chattaanon ki umar ke liye U-238 jaise lambi T-half waale padarth kaam aate hain (sirf naam).
- Lagbhag 9 half-lives (9 × 5730 ≈ 51,570 saal) ke baad C-14 sirf lagbhag 1 ÷ 512 (≈ 0.2 percent) bachta hai, jo naapna bahut mushkil ho jaata hai. Isliye carbon dating ki seema **lagbhag 50,000 saal** maani jaati hai.
- Hawa mein C-14 ka anupaat poore itihaas mein bilkul ek-sa nahi raha; vaigyanik is liye apne nateejon ko "sudhaar" (calibration) se jaanchte hain. Isliye "umar" ek **andaaza** hai, ek exact tithi nahi.

## Part D: Alag-alag T-half, alag-alag kaam

| Isotope | T-half (lagbhag) | Kyun ye kaam ka (ya chinta ka) hai |
|---|---|---|
| Tc-99m | 6 ghante | Hospital ki imaging: kaam ke baad jaldi kam ho jaata hai |
| I-131 | 8 din | Thyroid ke ilaaj ya jaanch mein; kuch hafton mein kam |
| Co-60 | 5.3 saal | Gamma radiotherapy ka source; kuch saal mein kamzor |
| Cs-137 | 30 saal | Reactor ke kachre mein; sadiyon tak chinta |
| C-14 | 5730 saal | Carbon dating |
| Ra-226 | 1600 saal | Lambi T-half: hazaaron saal mein hi bahut kam hota hai |
| U-238 | 4.5 × 10⁹ saal | Dharti jitni purani chattaanon mein abhi bhi bahut bacha |

(In sabke upyog aur khatre ka vistaar Topic 6 mein hai.)

**Chhoti T-half ka matlab:** nuclei jaldi-jaldi toot-te hain, to radiation tez nikalti hai par **jaldi khatam** bhi ho jaati hai. **Lambi T-half ka matlab:** nuclei dheere-dheere toot-te hain, to har second kam radiation, par bahut lamba samay.

Isliye hospital mein **diagnosis** (jaanch) ke liye aisa isotope chunte hain jiski T-half chhoti ho, jaise Tc-99m: ek din mein (4 half-lives) saara radiation bahut kam ho jaata hai. Ulta, Cs-137 jaise lambi T-half waale kachre ko sadiyon tak surakshit rakhna padta hai (30 saal ki T-half se ek hazaar guna kam hone ke liye 10 half-lives, yaani lagbhag 300 saal lagte hain). Plutonium jaise kuch tatvon ki T-half hazaaron saal hoti hai (lagbhag), isliye unke kachre ki chinta bahut lambi hoti hai. Kachre ko kaise sambhalte hain, ye Topic 6 mein.

### Example 5. Table se padhna

Ek khel ke "kalpanik" isotope Y ki T-half 40 minute hai (kalpanik, asli nahi). Shuru mein 800 hazaar nuclei hain. 160 minute baad kitne?

**Given:** N₀ = 800 (hazaar), T½ = 40 min, t = 160 min
**Find:** N
**Formula:** n = t ÷ T½; N = N₀ ÷ 2ⁿ
**Substitution:** n = 160 ÷ 40 = 4; N = 800 ÷ 16
**Answer:** **50 hazaar nuclei**
**Jaanch:** 800 → 400 → 200 → 100 → 50 ✅

### Example 6. Coin Decay ka hisaab

Figure 4.1 ke table mein round 3 par 14 sikke bache the. Kitne bachne chahiye the aur farak kitna?

**Given:** N₀ = 100, n = 3
**Find:** apekshit N, aur ginti ka farak
**Formula:** N = N₀ ÷ 2ⁿ
**Substitution:** N = 100 ÷ 8 = 12.5
**Answer:** apekshit **12.5** (yaani 12 ya 13 ke aas-paas); mila 14, farak **1.5**
**Jaanch:** 14 − 12.5 = 1.5 ✅. Chhota farak random hone ki wajah se hai.

## Part E: Ek nucleus ka koi half-life nahi hota

Half-life ek **statistical** (ginti ki) baat hai. Ek akele nucleus ke liye hum nahi bata sakte ki wo kab tootega: wo agle second mein bhi toot sakta hai aur lakhon saal baad bhi. Hum sirf itna keh sakte hain ki T-half ke samay mein uske tootne ka chance 1 ÷ 2 hai. Jaise ek sikke ka toss: ek toss ka nateeja nahi pata, par hazaar sikkon mein lagbhag 500 heads. **Bahut saare** nuclei hon (jaise ek chammach mein arabon), tabhi halving ka niyam ek-dum pakka dikhta hai. Sirf 20 sikke hon to ulat-pher zyada dikhegi, jaisa Figure 4.1 mein dikhi.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Do half-lives ke baad sab khatam ho jaata hai"**
Do half-lives = aadha + aadha ka khatam hona, ye soch galat hai. Pehli T-half mein **aadha** gaya; doosri T-half mein bache hue ka **aadha**, yaani shuruaat ka sirf ek-chauthai gaya. Demonstration: Ra-226 ka 36 g sample lo (T-half lagbhag 1600 saal). 3200 saal (2 half-lives) baad Ra-226 = 36 ÷ 4 = **9 g** bacha, 0 nahi. 4800 saal baad 36 ÷ 8 = **4.5 g**. 16,000 saal (10 half-lives) baad bhi 36 ÷ 1024 ≈ **0.035 g** Ra-226 bacha hai. Curve (Figure 4.2) kabhi seedha zero nahi chhoota.

**Galti 2: "Jo nucleus ek half-life se bach gaya, wo aadha decay ho chuka hai"**
Nucleus ko "yaad" nahi rehta ki wo kitna bacha. Har nucleus ke aage ke har T-half mein toot-ne ka chance wahi 1 ÷ 2 rehta hai, chahe wo kitne bhi samay se bacha ho. Demonstration: ek sikka jo Coin Decay mein teen round se lagatar heads laya hai: kya chauthe round mein uske bachne ka chance badh gaya? Nahi, ab bhi 1 ÷ 2. Isi tarah ek Cs-137 nucleus jo 120 saal (4 half-lives) se bacha hua hai, agle 30 saal mein toot-ne ka chance phir bhi 1 ÷ 2 hai. Nucleus naya hi jaisa hai: na "aadha tuta", na "purana hone se kamzor".

## Quick Recap

- **Half-life (T½):** wo samay jisme aadhe radioactive nuclei decay ho jaate hain; activity bhi aadhi ho jaati hai.
- **N = N₀ ÷ 2ⁿ, n = t ÷ T½** (poori half-lives ke liye). Bacha hissa: 1 ÷ 2, 1 ÷ 4, 1 ÷ 8, 1 ÷ 16, 1 ÷ 32.
- Curve neeche aata hai par kabhi seedha zero nahi chhoota.
- Decay ka matlab gayab hona nahi: nucleus naye tatva ka ban jaata hai; "bacha" matlab bacha **radioactive** isotope.
- Hospital ki jaanch ke liye chhoti T-half (Tc-99m, lagbhag 6 ghante); lambi T-half (Cs-137, lagbhag 30 saal) waale kachre ki chinta lambi hai.
- **Carbon dating:** C-14 ki T-half lagbhag 5730 saal; sirf jeev se bani cheezon par; seema lagbhag 50,000 saal.
- Half-life statistical hai: ek nucleus ka toot-na random hai, bahut saare ka niyam pakka.

## Practice Questions

1. A sample of Co-60 has a mass of 64 mg. Taking its half-life as about 5.3 years, find the mass of Co-60 left after 10.6 years and after 21.2 years.

2. A hospital has 160 µg of I-131 (half-life about 8 days). (a) How much remains after 32 days? (b) After how many days will only 2.5 µg remain?

3. A waste sample contains 40 g of Cs-137 (half-life about 30 years). Find the mass of Cs-137 left after 90 years and the mass that has changed into other nuclei.

4. A museum holds 12 g of Ra-226 (half-life about 1600 years). What mass of Ra-226 remains after 4800 years? Did the rest simply disappear? Explain in one line.

5. A piece of charcoal from an old campfire has one-eighth of the C-14 fraction of living wood. A second piece has one-sixteenth. Taking the half-life of C-14 as about 5730 years, find the age of each piece.

6. How long does it take for a Co-60 source (half-life about 5.3 years) to fall to one thirty-second of its starting amount?

7. A "kalpanik" (imaginary) isotope X has a half-life of 3 days. Its sample has 960 thousand nuclei on day 0 and 480 thousand on day 3. Without any formula, fill in the numbers on day 6, day 12 and day 18, and say what you notice.

8. A detector near a Tc-99m source (half-life about 6 hours) counts 720 counts per minute at noon. Assuming that the count rate is proportional to the number of radioactive nuclei and ignoring other sources, what will the count rate be 24 hours later?

9. After how many half-lives will less than 1 percent of a radioactive isotope remain? Give the fraction left at that stage.

10. A "kalpanik" isotope W falls to one sixty-fourth of its starting amount in 78 hours. Find its half-life.

11. Compare Cs-137 (half-life about 30 years) after 300 years with Tc-99m (half-life about 6 hours) after 60 hours. What fraction of each is left? Why does this make Cs-137 waste a long-term problem?

12. State True or False, and correct the false ones: (a) After 3 half-lives, three-eighths of the sample remain. (b) A nucleus that has not decayed for a long time is more likely to decay next. (c) Carbon dating can find the age of a 200,000-year-old fossil bone. (d) A radioactive isotope with a short half-life is useful for a hospital scan.

13. In a Coin Decay game with 200 coins, about how many coins would you expect after 4 rounds? Why might your actual count be a little different? What would change if you started with only 20 coins?

### Answers

1. **Given:** m₀ = 64 mg, T½ = 5.3 y. 10.6 y: n = 10.6 ÷ 5.3 = 2, m = 64 ÷ 4 = **16 mg**. 21.2 y: n = 21.2 ÷ 5.3 = 4, m = 64 ÷ 16 = **4 mg**. **Jaanch:** 64 → 32 → 16 → 8 → 4 ✅.

2. (a) n = 32 ÷ 8 = 4; m = 160 ÷ 16 = **10 µg**. (b) 160 ÷ 2.5 = 64 = 2⁶, to n = 6; t = 6 × 8 = **48 din**. **Jaanch:** 160 → 80 → 40 → 20 → 10 → 5 → 2.5 (6 kadam) ✅.

3. n = 90 ÷ 30 = 3; bacha = 40 ÷ 8 = **5 g**; badla hua = 40 − 5 = **35 g**. **Jaanch:** 40 → 20 → 10 → 5 ✅; 5 + 35 = 40 ✅.

4. n = 4800 ÷ 1600 = 3; bacha = 12 ÷ 8 = **1.5 g**. Baaki 10.5 g gayab nahi hua: wo Ra-226 ke nuclei toot kar doosre tatva ban gaye. **Jaanch:** 12 → 6 → 3 → 1.5 ✅.

5. One-eighth = 1 ÷ 2³, n = 3; umar = 3 × 5730 = **17,190 saal**. One-sixteenth = 1 ÷ 2⁴, n = 4; umar = 4 × 5730 = **22,920 saal**. Dono 50,000 saal ki seema ke andar hain. **Jaanch:** 1 ÷ 8 teen baar aadha ✅, 1 ÷ 16 chaar baar ✅.

6. 1 ÷ 32 = 1 ÷ 2⁵, n = 5; t = 5 × 5.3 = **26.5 saal**. **Jaanch:** 100 → 50 → 25 → 12.5 → 6.25 → 3.125 percent (paanch kadam) ✅.

7. T-half 3 din, har 3 din mein aadha: day 6 = 480 ÷ 2 = **240**, day 12 = 240 → 120 (day 9) → **60** (day 12), day 18 = 60 → 30 (day 15) → **15** (hazaar nuclei). Dekha: har 3 din mein aadha, raasta seedhi line nahi, curve hai, aur 18 din (6 half-lives) mein bhi 15 hazaar bache hain. **Jaanch:** 960 ÷ 2⁶ = 960 ÷ 64 = 15 ✅.

8. t = 24 h, n = 24 ÷ 6 = 4; count rate = 720 ÷ 16 = **45 counts per minute**. **Jaanch:** 720 → 360 → 180 → 90 → 45 ✅.

9. 1 ÷ 32 = 3.125 percent (n = 5), 1 ÷ 64 = lagbhag 1.56 percent (n = 6), dono abhi 1 percent se zyada. 1 ÷ 128 = lagbhag **0.78 percent**, jo 1 percent se kam hai. To **7 half-lives** ke baad, bacha hissa **1 ÷ 128**.

10. 1 ÷ 64 = 1 ÷ 2⁶, to n = 6. T½ = t ÷ n = 78 ÷ 6 = **13 ghante**. **Jaanch:** 6 × 13 = 78 ✅.

11. Cs-137: n = 300 ÷ 30 = 10; Tc-99m: n = 60 ÷ 6 = 10. Dono mein bacha hissa **1 ÷ 2¹⁰ = 1 ÷ 1024** (lagbhag 0.1 percent). Fark ye hai ki Tc-99m ko ye 60 ghante (lagbhag 2.5 din) mein milta hai, jabki Cs-137 ko 300 saal lagte hain. Isliye Cs-137 waale kachre ko sadiyon tak surakshit rakhna padta hai. **Jaanch:** 2¹⁰ = 1024 ✅.

12. (a) **False** ❌: 3 half-lives par 1 ÷ 8 bachta hai, 3 ÷ 8 nahi. (b) **False** ❌: nucleus ko bita hua samay "yaad" nahi, chance hamesha wahi 1 ÷ 2 per T-half. (c) **False** ❌: lagbhag 50,000 saal se purani cheezon mein C-14 bahut kam bachta hai; carbon dating kaam nahi karti. (d) **True** ✅: chhoti T-half se radiation jaldi kam ho jaati hai.

13. n = 4, apekshit = 200 ÷ 16 = **12.5**, yaani 12 ya 13 ke aas-paas. Sikke random girte hain, isliye ginti thodi upar-neeche ho sakti hai. Sirf 20 sikke hon to ulat-pher aur bhi bada dikhega (kai baar 1 ya 2 sikke ka farak bhi bada lagta hai), kyunki "bahut saare" ka niyam kam ginti par kamzor hota hai. **Jaanch:** 200 → 100 → 50 → 25 → 12.5 ✅.
