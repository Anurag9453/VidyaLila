## Part A: Fission ko nuclear equation ki nazar se

### Chapter 3 se recall

Chapter 3 ([sources-of-energy-part5.md](../sources-of-energy/sources-of-energy-part5.md), Part C se F) mein dekha tha ki ek dheema neutron U-235 ko todta hai, do medium tukde aur 2 ya 3 neutron nikalte hain, ye neutron aur nuclei todte hain (chain reaction), aur reactor mein control rods use kaabu mein rakhti hain. Reactor ka block diagram, 200 MeV prati fission, Tarapur aur Kudankulam aur do accident wahan hain; hum unhe dobara nahi padhayenge. Wahan ek cheez baaki thi: equation ka **hisaab** aur ye ki tukde kitne tarah ke ban sakte hain.

Wahan ki equation ko Topic 3 ke dhang se ek baar jaanch lete hain:

U-235 + n → Ba-141 + Kr-92 + 3 n
A: 235 + 1 = 236 = 141 + 92 + 3; Z: 92 + 0 = 92 = 56 + 36 ✅

Dekho, **ginti (A) aur proton (Z) dono barabar** hain. Neutron ka Z = 0 hai, isliye neutron Z ko nahi badalte.

```
A (nucleon ginti), 1 akshar = 4
baayein   UUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUn   235 + 1 = 236
daayein   BBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKn   141 + 92 + 3 = 236

Z (proton ginti), 1 akshar = 2
baayein   UUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUU   92 + 0 = 92
daayein   BBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKKK   56 + 36 + 0 = 92

U = U-235 (aur uska n)   B = Ba-141   K = Kr-92   n = neutron
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Fission ka A aur Z ka bahi-khaata. Upar A: baayein aur daayein dono taraf 236, yaani dono baar 59 akshar (neutron ke chhote hisse ko ek akshar rakha hai). Neeche Z: dono taraf 92, yaani 46 akshar; neutron ke Z = 0 ka koi akshar nahi.

### Example 1. Fission ka ek aur tareeka

U-235 sirf ek hi tareeke se nahi tootta. Ek aur sambhav tareeka (hedged: ye bahut se tareekon mein se ek hai):
U-235 + n → Cs-140 + Rb-93 + 3 n. Z of Cs = 55 aur Rb = 37. Kya ye balanced hai?

**Given:** parent U-235 (Z = 92), neutron A = 1, Z = 0; Cs-140 (Z = 55), Rb-93 (Z = 37), 3 neutron
**Find:** A aur Z ki jaanch
**Formula:** baayein ka A = daayein ka A; baayein ka Z = daayein ka Z
**Substitution:** A: 235 + 1 = 236; 140 + 93 + 3 = 236. Z: 92 + 0 = 92; 55 + 37 + 0 = 92
**Answer:** equation **balanced** hai
**Jaanch:** A: 236 = 236 ✅; Z: 92 = 92 ✅

### Tukde aksar radioactive kyun hote hain?

Bhaari nucleus mein neutron proton se kaafi zyada hote hain (Topic 1). Jab wo do medium tukdon mein tootta hai, to tukdon ko wahi neutron-bhara anupaat mil jaata hai, jo medium nuclei ke liye **zaroorat se zyada** hai. Aise neutron-bhare nuclei aksar **beta decay** karte hain (Topic 3: Z badhta hai, A wahi rehta hai) jab tak tikau nucleus na mil jaaye. Isliye fission ke tukde aur reactor ka kachra radioactive hota hai. Kachre ko kaise sambhalte hain, ye Topic 6 mein.

## Part B: E = mc² ka naya hisaab: mass defect

Chapter 3 mein dekha tha ki fission ke baad kul mass thoda kam hota hai aur wo kami energy ban jaati hai (E = mc², c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s). Us kami ka naam hai **mass defect**.

Ek zaroori baat: A (nucleon ki **ginti**) bilkul barabar rehti hai (236 = 236), par **mass** (kg mein naap) thoda kam ho jaata hai. Ginti aur mass do alag cheezein hain: isiliye equation A ke hisaab se balanced hoti hai, aur mass ki ye chhoti kami alag se energy ban jaati hai.

### Example 2. 7.5 microgram ki kami

Kisi nuclear reaction mein mass defect 7.5 µg (7.5 × 10⁻⁹ kg) hai. Kitni energy nikli, joule aur kWh mein? (1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J)

**Given:** m = 7.5 × 10⁻⁹ kg, c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s
**Find:** E (J aur kWh)
**Formula:** E = mc²; kWh = E ÷ 3.6 × 10⁶
**Substitution:** E = 7.5 × 10⁻⁹ × 9 × 10¹⁶ = 6.75 × 10⁸ J; kWh = 6.75 × 10⁸ ÷ 3.6 × 10⁶
**Answer:** E = **6.75 × 10⁸ J**, yaani **187.5 kWh**
**Jaanch:** unit: kg × (m/s)² = J ✅; 187.5 × 3.6 × 10⁶ = 6.75 × 10⁸ J ✅

### Example 3. Ulta: itni energy ke liye kitna mass?

Ek reaction se 1.8 × 10⁹ J energy nikli. Mass defect kitna?

**Given:** E = 1.8 × 10⁹ J, c² = 9 × 10¹⁶ m²/s²
**Find:** m
**Formula:** m = E ÷ c²
**Substitution:** m = 1.8 × 10⁹ ÷ 9 × 10¹⁶ = 2 × 10⁻⁸ kg
**Answer:** m = 2 × 10⁻⁸ kg = **20 µg**, aur energy 1.8 × 10⁹ ÷ 3.6 × 10⁶ = **500 kWh**
**Jaanch:** 2 × 10⁻⁸ × 9 × 10¹⁶ = 1.8 × 10⁹ J ✅

## Part C: Fusion: nuclei ko jodna

**Fusion** mein do halke nuclei jud kar ek bhaari nucleus banate hain. Chapter 3 mein ek line mein ye equation aayi thi; ab Z bhi jaanchte hain. Hydrogen ke do isotope Class 9 se: deuterium D = H-2 (1 proton, 1 neutron) aur tritium T = H-3 (1 proton, 2 neutron):

H-2 + H-3 → He-4 + n + energy
A: 2 + 3 = 5 = 4 + 1; Z: 1 + 1 = 2 = 2 + 0 ✅

```
Deuterium (D)    Tritium (T)         Helium-4          neutron
H-2              H-3                 He-4              n
P n              P n n               P P n n           n

1p + 1n     +    1p + 2n      -->    2p + 2n     +     0p + 1n
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Fusion ka D + T → He-4 + n (schematic). P = proton, n = neutron. Baayein: protons 1 + 1 = 2, neutron 1 + 2 = 3. Daayein: protons 2, neutron 2 + 1 = 3. Ginti dono taraf wahi.

### Example 4. Suraj ke ek kadam ki jaanch

Suraj mein hydrogen ka helium banna ek lambi shrinkhala hai (hum yahan poori nahi, sirf aakhri kadam dekhte hain; sachchai lambi hai, aage ki class mein). Ek kadam ye hai: He-3 + He-3 → He-4 + 2 H-1 (H-1 = ek proton). Check karo.

**Given:** He-3 (Z = 2), He-4 (Z = 2), H-1 (Z = 1)
**Find:** A aur Z ki jaanch
**Formula:** baayein ka A = daayein ka A; baayein ka Z = daayein ka Z
**Substitution:** A: 3 + 3 = 6; 4 + 1 + 1 = 6. Z: 2 + 2 = 4; 2 + 1 + 1 = 4
**Answer:** **balanced** hai
**Jaanch:** A: 6 = 6 ✅; Z: 4 = 4 ✅

### Fusion itna mushkil kyun hai?

Dono nuclei **positive** hain, isliye jab wo paas aate hain to ek-doosre ko **door dhakelte** hain (jaise ek hi naam ke charge). Nucleus ke andar ki strong force (Topic 1) sirf bahut paas se kaam karti hai. Isliye nuclei ko itni tez raftaar chahiye ki wo is dhakke ko paar karke bahut paas pahunch jayein. Tez raftaar ka matlab **bahut zyada temperature**: lagbhag **10⁷ °C ya usse zyada**. Itne garam gas mein electrons atoms se alag ho jaate hain: ise **plasma** kehte hain.

```
KAM speed (thanda gas)
pehle        (+) -->            <-- (+)
paas aaye        (+)->        <-(+)        (door hi rukte hain)
baad mein    <-- (+)                (+) -->     (wapas mud gaye)

BAHUT tez speed (10⁷ °C ya zyada)
pehle        (+) ====>          <==== (+)
paas aaye              (+)(+)              (bahut paas: jud sakte hain)
baad mein              (++)                (ek bada nucleus)
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Do positive nuclei (schematic). Kam speed par wo ek-doosre ko dhakelte hain aur laut jaate hain. Bahut tez speed par dhakka paar ho jaata hai aur ve itne paas aate hain ki jud jaate hain.

Ye sirf ek **model** hai: ek hi kism ka charge dhakelta hai (nuclei mein bhi). Isme kuch radioactive nahi hai. Chhote bachche door, gubbare ke tukde muh mein nahi.

### Suraj, aur dharti par fusion reactor

Suraj ke andar bahut bada dabaav aur 10⁷ °C ke order ka temperature hai (lagbhag), isliye wahan hydrogen helium mein badalta rehta hai, aur yahi hamari roshni ka strot hai. Dharti par itna temperature banana aur plasma ko tikaye rakhna mushkil hai. Plasma charged hai, to use **magnetic field** se hawa mein roka jaata hai (Chapter 9 aur Topic 2: charged kan field mein mudte hain). Is disha mein **ITER** naam ka bada prayog France mein ban raha hai (kai deshon ka saajha project; Bharat bhi isme bhaagidaar mana jaata hai: hedged). Abhi koi fusion plant grid ko bijli nahi deta.

## Part D: Parmanu bomb aur reactor: ek imaandar tulna

Sachchai ye hai ki **dono mein fission wali hi chain reaction** hai. Phir fark kya hai? Fark ye hai ki chain **kaise** chalti hai.

| | Reactor | Bomb |
|---|---|---|
| Chain reaction | **Kaabu mein**: ausat ek fission se ek hi naya fission | **Bina rok ke**: ausat ek se zyada naya fission |
| Fuel | Kam enriched (Chapter 3: lagbhag 3 se 5 percent U-235) | Bahut zyada enriched padarth |
| Maqsad | Dheere aur steady garmi, bijli | Achanak, bahut badi energy |
| Rokne ke tareeke | Control rods, coolant, kai suraksha parat | Aisa koi niyantran nahi |

(Is topic mein bomb ki banaavat ya fuel ke bare mein koi aur jaankari nahi di gayi: wo is lesson ka vishay nahi.)

Isliye reactor bomb ki tarah phat nahi sakta (Chapter 3 mein bhi yahi tha). Par reactor mein bhi accident ho sakte hain (jaise Chernobyl 1986 aur Fukushima 2011, Chapter 3 mein), isliye suraksha bahut zaroori hai.

Hiroshima aur Nagasaki (1945) mein parmanu hathiyaar se bahut badi sankhya mein log mare (andaaze alag-alag hain, ek-do lakh ke aas-paas): ye ek dukhad itihaas hai, jo hamein is shakti ko samajhdari se sambhalne ki yaad dilata hai.

## Part E: Bharat ka teen-charan nuclear karyakram

Bharat ke paas uranium kam hai, par **thorium** ke bade bhandar maane jaate hain (hedged: samudri retili tat par). Isiliye Dr. Homi Bhabha ne 1950 ke dashak mein ek teen-charan yojna rakhi (lagbhag):

```
[ Charan 1: kudrati uranium (zyaadatar U-238), heavy-water reactors ]
                 bijli + Pu-239 (U-238 se bana)
                              ↓
[ Charan 2: fast breeder reactors: Pu-239 fuel, U-238 se aur Pu-239 ]
                 (thorium ke saath U-233 bhi banta hai: hedged)
                              ↓
[ Charan 3: thorium se bane U-233 wale reactors ]
```

> **Figure 5.4** – Teen-charan karyakram ka seedha-saada naksha (hedged). Har charan ka kachra ya bacha hua padarth agle charan ka fuel banta hai. Teer neeche ki taraf agle charan ko dikhaate hain.

- **Charan 1:** kudrati uranium se chalne waale reactors (heavy water moderator, Class 9). Ye bijli dete hain aur Pu-239 bhi banate hain.
- **Charan 2:** **fast breeder reactor**: ye Pu-239 jalate hue U-238 se aur naya Pu-239 bana sakta hai. Kalpakkam (Tamil Nadu) mein 500 MW ka prototype fast breeder reactor (PFBR) bana hai, jiske 2026 ke shuru mein critical hone ki khabar aayi (hedged: poori commercial bijli ki sthiti akhbaar se dekho).
- **Charan 3:** thorium se banne waale U-233 par chalne waale reactors.

### Example 5. Breeder mein U-238 se Pu-239

U-238 ek neutron leta hai: U-238 + n → U-239. Phir U-239 beta decay se Np-239 aur phir Np-239 beta decay se Pu-239 banta hai. Z: U = 92, Np = 93, Pu = 94. Check karo.

**Given:** U-238 (Z = 92), n; U-239; Np-239 (Z = 93); Pu-239 (Z = 94); e⁻ (A = 0, Z = −1)
**Find:** teeno kadam ki A aur Z ki jaanch
**Formula:** baayein ka A = daayein ka A; baayein ka Z = daayein ka Z
**Substitution:** (i) U-238 + n → U-239: A: 238 + 1 = 239; Z: 92 + 0 = 92. (ii) U-239 → Np-239 + e⁻: A: 239 = 239 + 0; Z: 92 = 93 − 1. (iii) Np-239 → Pu-239 + e⁻: A: 239 = 239 + 0; Z: 93 = 94 − 1
**Answer:** teeno kadam **balanced**; U-238 do beta decay ke baad Pu-239 ban gaya
**Jaanch:** (i) 239 = 239, 92 = 92 ✅; (ii) 239 = 239, 92 = 92 ✅; (iii) 239 = 239, 93 = 93 ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Nuclear reactor bhi atom bomb ki tarah hi kaam karta hai."**
Chain reaction ka mool vichaar ek hai, par farak kaabu ka hai (Chapter 3 ki Aam galti 1 ([sources-of-energy-part5.md](../sources-of-energy/sources-of-energy-part5.md)) mein bhi yahi baat thi; yahan ek naya hisaab dekho). Demonstration: maan lo har fission ausat **2** naye fission karata hai (bina rok ke). Pidhi 0 par 1, pidhi 1 par 2, pidhi 2 par 4, ... pidhi 30 par 2³⁰ = 1,073,741,824, yaani lagbhag 1.07 × 10⁹ fission. Reactor mein control rods ausat **1** naya fission hi rakhti hain: pidhi 30 par bhi 1. Ek dhamaka, doosra steady garmi. Isiliye reactor mein fuel bhi kam enriched hota hai (Part D).

**Galti 2: "Fusion se aaj hamare ghar roshan ho rahe hain."**
Dharti par koi fusion bijlighar grid ko bijli nahi deta (ITER nirman mein hai). Par ek sachchai chhupi hai: Suraj mein fusion roz ho raha hai. Uski roshni lagbhag 8 minute mein dharti tak aati hai (1.5 × 10¹¹ m ÷ 3 × 10⁸ m/s ≈ 500 s ≈ 8.3 minute, hedged), aur solar panel ya hawa-paani se milne waali bijli usi ki den hai. Yaani **fusion ki energy ghar tak pahunchti hai, par fusion Suraj mein hoti hai, ghar ke paas nahi.** Dharti par fusion plant banana abhi bhi bhavishya ki baat hai.

## Quick Recap

- Fission ki equation A aur Z dono se balance hoti hai: U-235 + n → Ba-141 + Kr-92 + 3 n (236 = 236; 92 = 92). Tukde alag-alag tareeke ke ban sakte hain, aur ye aksar radioactive hote hain.
- **Mass defect:** products ka kul mass thoda kam; E = mc², c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s. A (ginti) barabar rehti hai, mass nahi.
- **Fusion:** halke nuclei judte hain (D + T → He-4 + n, A: 5 = 5; Z: 2 = 2). Iske liye lagbhag 10⁷ °C ya usse zyada chahiye kyunki nuclei positive hain aur ek-doosre ko dhakelte hain.
- Suraj mein fusion hota hai; dharti par abhi koi fusion bijlighar grid ko bijli nahi deta (ITER nirman mein).
- Reactor aur bomb: dono mein chain reaction, par reactor mein kaabu mein (ausat ek naya fission); reactor bomb ki tarah phat nahi sakta.
- Bharat ka teen-charan karyakram: natural uranium, fast breeder, thorium (hedged); Dr. Homi Bhabha, BARC, Tarapur, Kudankulam.
- Radioactive padarth hamesha visheshagya sambhalte hain: kabhi chhoo-na, ikattha karna ya kholna nahi.

## Practice Questions

Use c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s and 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J where needed. Atomic numbers: H 1, He 2, Sr 38, Xe 54, Ba 56, Kr 36, Th 90, Pa 91, U 92.

1. A nuclear reaction has a mass defect of 2.4 µg. Find the energy released in joules and in kWh.
2. A different reaction has a mass defect of 55 µg. Find the energy released in joules and in kWh.
3. A third reaction has a mass defect of 0.8 mg. Find the energy in joules and in kWh. How many times larger is it than the energy in Question 1?
4. A reaction releases 5.4 × 10⁸ J of energy. Find the mass defect in micrograms.
5. U-235 absorbs a neutron and splits in one possible way as U-235 + n → Sr-90 + Xe-143 + 3 n. Show by the two-line A and Z check that the equation is balanced.
6. In one possible fission, U-235 + n → Ba-144 + Kr-? + 3 n. Find the A and Z of the krypton nucleus and write its symbol in the form Kr-A.
7. In another possible fission, U-235 + n → Xe-140 + Sr-94 + k n. Find k, the number of neutrons released, by balancing A and then check Z.
8. Check by A and Z whether this fusion equation is balanced: H-2 + H-2 → He-3 + n.
9. A second way two deuterium nuclei can fuse: H-2 + H-2 → H-3 + X. Find the A and Z of X and name the particle (it is a nucleus of ordinary hydrogen).
10. Nuclei must be extremely fast to fuse. Take the flame of a gas stove as about 2000 °C (maan lo) and fusion as needing about 10⁷ °C. How many times hotter must the gas be? In two lines, why can a stove flame not fuse hydrogen nuclei?
11. In the thorium stage, Th-232 absorbs a neutron to become Th-233. Then Th-233 → Pa-233 + e⁻ and Pa-233 → (final nucleus) + e⁻. Find the A and Z of the final nucleus, name it, and write the two-line check for each beta step.
12. State True or False, and correct the false ones: (a) A nuclear reactor can explode like an atom bomb. (b) In fusion the total mass of the products is slightly more than the starting mass. (c) Fusion plants are already supplying electricity to the grid in India. (d) The Sun gets its energy from fusion. (e) Tarapur is in Tamil Nadu.
13. Match each stage of India's three-stage programme with the fuel idea: (i) Stage 1, (ii) Stage 2, (iii) Stage 3, with (a) thorium-based U-233 reactors, (b) fast breeder reactors making fuel from U-238, (c) natural-uranium reactors.

### Answers

1. **Given:** m = 2.4 × 10⁻⁹ kg. **Formula:** E = mc². **Substitution:** 2.4 × 10⁻⁹ × 9 × 10¹⁶ = **2.16 × 10⁸ J**; kWh = 2.16 × 10⁸ ÷ 3.6 × 10⁶ = **60 kWh**. **Jaanch:** 60 × 3.6 × 10⁶ = 2.16 × 10⁸ ✅.
