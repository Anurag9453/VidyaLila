# Topic 6: Radiation Ke Upyog, Khatre Aur Suraksha

Topic 5 mein dekha ki nucleus se bahut badi energy nikal sakti hai. Par radioactivity ka kaam sirf bijli banana nahi hai: wahi alpha, beta aur gamma kirnein (Topic 2) aur wahi half-life ka niyam (Topic 4) hospital, factory, khet aur museum mein roz kaam aate hain. Aur jis cheez mein ionisation hai, usse khatra bhi hai. Is topic mein dekhenge ki ye kirnein kahan kaam aati hain, kahan nuksaan karti hain, aur **samajhdari se kaise bacha jaata hai**.

## Chhat ka dibba aur dentist ki apron

Mr. Talekar apne bhatije Jaideep ko lekar ek hospital mein ek rishtedaar ko dekhne gaye. Intezaar-hall ki chhat par ek safed gol dibba laga tha. Jaideep ki nazar uske chhote sticker par gayi: "Contains radioactive material: do not open." (sticker ke shabd alag-alag company mein alag hote hain.)

"Chacha!" Jaideep ne dheere se kaha, "chhat par radioactive cheez? Hum yahan baithe hain!" Mr. Talekar muskuraye: "Wo smoke detector hai. Chalo, pehle dekh lo ki andar kya hai, phir dar ya be-dar ka faisla karna."

Wapas jaate waqt radiology-wing ke paas ek technician patli si lead wali apron pehne kuch dikhi, aur ek darwaze par peele-kaale trefoil ka nishan tha. Jaideep ne pucha, "Ye log roz isi radiation ke beech kaam karte hain. Phir bachte kaise hain?" Mr. Talekar bole, "Bachne ke teen niyam hote hain. Chalo, ek ek karke samjhte hain."

## Suraksha

> **Suraksha:** radioactive source aur smoke detector ke andar ka source **kabhi chhoote, ikattha karte, kholte ya jaanchte nahi.** Trefoil nishan ka matlab hai: door raho aur kisi bade ko batao. Koi purana dhatu ka dibba ya sticker wali cheez mile to haath mat lagao, bade ko batao. Is topic ki saari activities mein sirf **torch, kagaz, sikke aur list** hain; kuch bhi radioactive nahi hai. Chhote bachche sikke aur chhoti cheezein nigal sakte hain, isliye unhe door rakho.

## Part A: Recall, phir hospital

Topic 2 mein dekha tha ki ionising kiran atom se electron nikaalti hai; Chapter 5 mein ye bhi dekha tha ki ionising ka matlab "hamesha khatarnak" nahi: **dose aur samay** hi sab kuch hain ([scattering-and-spectrum-part5.md](../scattering-and-spectrum/scattering-and-spectrum-part5.md)). Topic 4 mein half-life ka N = N₀ ÷ 2ⁿ aaya tha. Class 9 mein I-131 aur Co-60 ke bas naam the (Tc-99m yahan naya hai). Ab "kyun" dekhte hain.

### Jaanch (diagnosis): kam half-life, bahar nikalne wali kiran

Marij ko ek aisa tracer diya jaata hai jisme **Tc-99m** hota hai (T½ lagbhag 6 ghante). Ye gamma deta hai, aur gamma sharir ke bahar tak nikal jaati hai, jahan ek special camera use pakad leta hai. Agar kiran **alpha** hoti to wo sharir ke andar hi ruk jaati (Topic 2), camera tak na pahunchti, aur sirf andar nuksaan karti. Aur T½ **chhota** isliye chahiye ki scan ke baad source jaldi kamzor ho jaye, jisse marij ko zyada dose na mile.

**Example 1. Scan ke baad kitna bacha?**

Tc-99m (T½ lagbhag 6 ghante) ka injection ho gaya. 12 ghante aur 30 ghante baad kitna percent bacha?

**Given:** T½ = 6 h, t = 12 h aur 30 h
**Find:** bacha hua percent
**Formula:** n = t ÷ T½; hissa = 1 ÷ 2ⁿ
**Substitution:** 12 h: n = 2, hissa = 1 ÷ 4. 30 h: n = 5, hissa = 1 ÷ 32
**Answer:** **25 percent** aur **lagbhag 3.1 percent**
**Jaanch:** 100 → 50 → 25 (do kadam) ✅; 100 → 50 → 25 → 12.5 → 6.25 → 3.125 (paanch kadam) ✅

```
 t      bacha hua source
 0 h    ################################  100
 6 h    ################  50
12 h    ########  25
18 h    ####  12.5
24 h    ##  6.25
30 h    #  3.125
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Tc-99m ka bacha hua source (percent mein, har # = 3.125 percent): har 6 ghante mein aadha. 30 ghante mein lagbhag 3 percent hi bachta hai.

**I-131 aur thyroid.** Thyroid gale ki wo gland hai jo iodine ko khinchti hai. Marij ko I-131 diya jaye to wo wahin jama hota hai. Zyada matra mein uski beta kiranein (Topic 3: I-131 → Xe-131 + e⁻) usi gland ke bimaar cells ko maarti hain; chhoti matra jaanch ke kaam aati hai. T½ lagbhag 8 din hai: marij ka sharir kuch dino mein sambhla jaata hai.

**Co-60 aur radiotherapy.** Co-60 (T½ lagbhag 5.3 saal) ki gamma kirnein cancer waali jagah par kasi hui beam mein daali jaati hain. Beam ko aise ghumaya jaata hai ki bimaar hisse par sabse zyada dose pade aur baaki sharir par kam. Ye ilaaj sirf **trained doctor aur physicist** karte hain. PET scan ka bhi sirf naam: usme ek aisa tracer hota hai jo apne aap kirnein deta hai; detail aage ki class mein.

**Sterilisation.** Sui-syringe jaise saaman ko band packet mein gamma se germs-free karte hain (Chapter 5). Packet khulne par saaman radioactive nahi hota, bas saaf hota hai.

## Part B: Factory, khet aur tracer

### Smoke detector: alpha hi kyun?

Zyada-tar purane smoke detector mein **Am-241** ka bahut chhota, sealed alpha source hota hai. (Aajkal bahut se detector optical hote hain aur unme radioactive padarth nahi hota: ye hedged baat hai, sticker padho.)

```
          + plate (battery ke + se juda)
   +=================================+
   :   electron  ↑   ↑   ↑           :
   :        . . . . . . . .          :
   :   Am-241 ka alpha: hawa ionise   :
   :        . . . . . . . .          :
   :   + ion    ↓   ↓   ↓            :
   +=================================+
          - plate (battery ke - se juda)
```

> **Figure 6.2** – Smoke detector ka schematic: alpha kan hawa ko ionise karte hain; positive ion neeche wali negative plate ki taraf aur electron upar wali positive plate ki taraf chalte hain, isliye ek chhota current bahta hai.

Saaf hawa mein current steady rehta hai. Dhuan aaye to ion dhuan ke bhaari kanon se chipak kar sust ho jaate hain, current **gir jaata hai**, circuit ye girawat pakadkar alarm bajata hai. Alpha isliye sahi hai ki wo hawa ko **sabse zyada ionise** karta hai (Topic 2), aur usi wajah se wo sirf kuch cm ke andar ruk jaata hai: dibbe ke bahar nahi aata. Phir bhi use kabhi kholna nahi hai: kholne se source ka seal tootega aur wo kisi ke haath ya saans tak pahunch sakta hai.

### Beta thickness gauge

Kagaz ki mill mein chalti sheet ke neeche beta source aur upar counter hota hai. Sheet **moti** hui to zyada beta ruki, counter ki ginti **kam**; patli hui to ginti zyada. Alpha sheet ko paar hi nahi karta; gamma lagbhag bina farak ke paar ho jaati hai. Isliye patli sheet ke liye beta sabse sahi hai.

```
          [ counter ]            ginti upar
              ↑ ↑ ↑
   ===================== chalti sheet (rollers par)
              ↑ ↑ ↑
          [ source ]             beta neeche, sealed
```

> **Figure 6.3** – Beta thickness gauge: source neeche, counter upar. Sheet moti to kam beta pahunchte hain, ginti ghat jaati hai.

**Example 2. Gauge ka sandesh**

Sahi sheet par counter 1500 counts per minute (maan lo) deta hai. Aaj ke roll par 1320 counts per minute mila. Sheet moti hai ya patli?

**Given:** sahi = 1500, aaj = 1320 counts per minute
**Find:** farak aur kya baat
**Formula:** (1500 − 1320) ÷ 1500 × 100
**Substitution:** 180 ÷ 1500 × 100
**Answer:** ginti **12 percent kam** hai, isliye sheet **moti** hai
**Jaanch:** moti sheet zyada beta rokti hai, isliye ginti kam ✅

**Gamma radiography.** Moti dhatu ke jod ke ek taraf gamma source aur doosri taraf film rakhte hain; weld mein daraar ho to wahan zyada gamma paar jaati hai aur film par kaala nishan banta hai. Ye kaam trained log door se karte hain.

**Kheti.** **Mutation breeding** mein beejon ko kirnein deke random badlaav paida kiye jaate hain; hazaaron paudhon mein se kuch ke gun kaam ke nikalte hain (jaise jaldi pakna) aur unhe chuna jaata hai. Beej baad mein radioactive nahi hote. **Food irradiation** mein aloo ke ankur ya masalon ke keede roke jaate hain; khana radioactive nahi banta.

**Tracer.** Pani ke pipe mein ek chhoti matra ka tracer chhodte hain aur bahar counter ghumate hain: jahan ginti achanak badhe wahan leak. Aise kaam ke liye T½ chhota, aur kiran aisi chahiye jo mitti ke paar counter tak pahunche. **Archaeology** mein Topic 4 ka carbon dating wahi "ghadi" hai.

## Part C: Khatra: kyun, kab, kitna

Ionising kiran cells mein DNA ke atoms ko bhi ionise kar sakti hai. Zyada **dose** par radiation sickness (ulti, kamzori, baal jhadna) ho sakta hai aur cancer ka khatra badhta hai. **Kam dose** par khatra bahut chhota hota hai (hedged: zero nahi mana jaata); sharir kai nuksaan khud sudhar leta hai. Dose teen cheezon par chalti hai: **kitna source, kitni der, kitni door.** Alpha bahar se skin ki mrit (bahari) parat mein ruk jaati hai, par saans ya khane ke saath **andar** chali jaaye to ye sabse zyada nuksaan karti hai (Topic 2).

Dose **sievert (Sv)** mein naapte hain, jise "khuraak ki unit" samajh lo (definition aage ki class mein). 1 Sv bahut badi hai, isliye 1 mSv = 0.001 Sv aur 1 µSv = 0.000001 Sv.

**Dose = dose rate × samay**

**Example 3. Technician ka din**

Ek kamre ke paas dose rate 7.5 µSv per hour (maan lo) hai. Technician wahan 6 ghante rehti hai. Ek din ki seema 60 µSv (maan lo, asli niyam nahi). (a) Dose? (b) Zyada se zyada kitna samay?

**Given:** rate = 7.5 µSv per hour, t = 6 h, seema = 60 µSv
**Find:** (a) dose, (b) t ka maximum
**Formula:** dose = rate × t; t = seema ÷ rate
**Substitution:** (a) 7.5 × 6 (b) 60 ÷ 7.5
**Answer:** (a) **45 µSv** (b) **8 ghante**
**Jaanch:** 7.5 × 8 = 60 ✅; 45 < 60 ✅

```
 ghante   dose (har # = 7.5 µSv)
  1       #
  2       ##
  4       ####
  6       ######        45
  8       ########      60  (seema)
```

> **Figure 6.4** – Dose = rate × samay: samay do guna to dose do guna. 8 ghante par seema (60 µSv, maan lo) poori.

## Part D: Background radiation: sabke saath rehti hai

Hum kabhi bhi **zero** radiation mein nahi hote. Is **background radiation** ke strot: **kosmik kirnein** (antariksh se), **chattan aur mitti**, **radon gas** (band kamron mein), **khana aur hamara sharir** (K-40, Topic 1; C-14) aur **medical** jaanch. Duniya bhar mein ek aam aadmi ko saal mein lagbhag **2.4 mSv** milta hai ("lagbhag 2 se 3"; jagah ke hisaab se bahut badalta hai; naye andaaze prakritik strot ke liye lagbhag 3 mSv tak bhi bataate hain, aur medical alag se judta hai; duniya ke average mein bhi radon sabse bada prakritik strot hai). Kela, kisi granite ya hawa mein bhi thodi radioactivity hoti hai, par bahut kam. Mobile phone aur Wi-Fi non-ionising hain (Chapter 5).

**Example 4. Ek parivaar ka saal**

Jaideep ne maan lo ki parivaar ke saal ki dose ki list banayi (mSv): kosmik 0.36, chattan aur mitti 0.48, radon 0.88, khana aur sharir 0.32, medical 0.16. Kul kitna? Aam se kitna kam? Radon ka hissa?

**Given:** 0.36, 0.48, 0.88, 0.32, 0.16 mSv
**Find:** kul, 2.4 se farak, radon ka percent
**Formula:** jodo; farak = 2.4 − kul; percent = 0.88 ÷ kul × 100
**Substitution:** 0.36 + 0.48 + 0.88 + 0.32 + 0.16; 2.4 − 2.2; 0.88 ÷ 2.2 × 100
**Answer:** kul **2.2 mSv**, aam se **0.2 mSv kam**, radon **40 percent**
**Jaanch:** 9 + 12 + 22 + 8 + 4 = 55 hisse × 0.04 = 2.2 ✅

```
kosmik kirnein  #########  0.36
chattan/mitti   ############  0.48
radon gas       ######################  0.88
khana, sharir   ########  0.32
medical         ####  0.16
```

> **Figure 6.5** – Maan lo wali list ki dose (mSv, har # = 0.04 mSv). Is parivaar mein sabse bada hissa radon ka hai.

**Activity 1: ghar par adult ke saath.** Ek kagaz par "background ke strot" ki apni list banao: kela, granite ki slab ya pathar ki cheez (sirf naam likho), khuli hawa, Sooraj, hawai safar. Kuch naapna nahi, sirf likhna aur samajhna ki ye sab ek hi category (prakritik) mein aate hain.

## Part E: Bachne ke teen niyam: samay, doori, shielding

1. **Samay kam:** dose = rate × samay (Example 3).
2. **Doori zyada:** source se door jaao to dose rate **bahut** ghat jaata hai (sirf yahi baat yaad rakho; exact niyam aage ki class mein).
3. **Shielding:** source aur insaan ke beech parat. Alpha ke liye kagaz, beta ke liye kuch mm aluminium, gamma ke liye moti lead ya concrete (kam hoti hai, shuddh zero nahi: Topic 2). Dentist ki lead apron aur radiology room ka lead darwaza yahi hain.

Technician apna **dosimeter badge** pehente hain, jise baar-baar jaanch kar dekha jaata hai ki kisi ki jama dose kitni hui.

**Activity 2: torch aur deewar (ghar par adult ke saath).** Kamre mein torch deewar ki taraf rakho: paas se roshni ka chakkar chhota aur tez, door se bada aur halka. Beech mein kitab ya card rakho to kam roshni. Ye sirf **model** hai, radiation nahi.

## Part F: Radioactive waste

Reactor ya hospital se kuch kachra nikalta hai. **Low level** (dastane, kagaz, use hui syringe): kuch samay rakhte hain jab tak wo kamzor na ho jaye. **Intermediate level** (reactor ke kuch purze): shield mein. **High level** (spent fuel): pehle pani ke tank mein thanda karte hain, phir mazboot sealed casks aur gehre underground store mein. Kuch ka T½ (jaise Cs-137, lagbhag 30 saal) bahut lamba hai, isliye intezaam hazaaron saal ka sochte hain (Chapter 3 mein bhi isi se jude khatre the).

**Example 5. I-131 ka kachra**

Ek hospital I-131 (T½ lagbhag 8 din) ke kachre ko tab tak rakhta hai jab tak uski activity shuruaat ke **1 ÷ 1000** se kam na ho jaye. Kitne din?

**Given:** T½ = 8 days, lakshya < 1 ÷ 1000
**Find:** t
**Formula:** 1 ÷ 2ⁿ < 1 ÷ 1000, t = n × T½
**Substitution:** 2⁹ = 512 (kam), 2¹⁰ = 1024 (kaafi), n = 10; t = 10 × 8
**Answer:** **80 din**
**Jaanch:** 1 ÷ 1024 < 1 ÷ 1000 ✅; 9 kadam par 1 ÷ 512 > 1 ÷ 1000 ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Har radiation khatarnak hoti hai."**

Chapter 5 mein dhoop wali kahani thi; yahan doosri tarah se dekho. Maan lo ek dental X-ray ki dose 5 µSv hai. 5 µSv = 0.005 mSv, aur 0.005 ÷ 2.4 × 100 = lagbhag **0.2 percent** saal bhar ki aam dose ka. Dukaan ka radio, Wi-Fi aur mobile phone to ionising hi nahi. Khatra **kaun si kiran, kitni dose, kitne samay** par hai, na ki "radiation" shabd par.

**Galti 2: "Irradiated khana radioactive ho jaata hai."**

Maan lo irradiation se pehle masale ke packet ke paas counter ne 26 counts per minute diye aur baad mein 24, dono background ke aas-paas. Matlab kuch nahi badla. Jaise X-ray lene ke baad hamara haath radioactive nahi hota, waise kirnein sirf paar hui. Tracer alag cheez hai: usme radioactive **atom hi** khane ya injection ke saath andar jaate hain.

## Quick Recap

- Medical: Tc-99m (T½ lagbhag 6 h, gamma bahar nikalti hai), I-131 thyroid (T½ lagbhag 8 din), Co-60 radiotherapy; sterilisation mein saaman radioactive nahi banta.
- Smoke detector mein sealed Am-241 alpha source: alpha hawa ionise karta hai, dhuan se current girta hai; kabhi nahi kholna.
- Beta se patli sheet ki motai; gamma se weld; mutation breeding aur food irradiation; tracer se leak.
- Dose = rate × samay; Sv, mSv, µSv. Background lagbhag 2.4 mSv saal (lagbhag 2 se 3).
- Teen niyam: samay kam, doori zyada, shielding. Dosimeter badge, lead apron.
- Waste: low, intermediate, high level; sealed casks aur deep store.
- Kabhi source na chhoo, na kholo; trefoil dikhe to door raho.

## Practice Questions

1. A Tc-99m injection (half-life about 6 hours) is given at 9 a.m. What percentage of the Tc-99m is left 36 hours later? Give one reason why a short half-life is better for a scan.
2. An I-131 capsule (half-life about 8 days) is given to a thyroid patient. What fraction and percentage are left after 16 days and after 56 days?
3. A new Co-60 radiotherapy source (half-life about 5.3 years) gives the required dose in 3.0 minutes. Assuming dose = rate × time and that the rate is proportional to the remaining Co-60, find the session time after 5.3 years and after 31.8 years.
4. A store-room has a dose rate of 12 µSv per hour (maan lo). A worker stays there 3.5 hours. Find the dose. If the limit for a shift is 150 µSv (maan lo), what is the longest time allowed?
5. A town's yearly doses in mSv (maan lo) are: cosmic 0.39, rocks and soil 0.57, radon 1.12, food and body 0.28, medical 0.19. Find the total, say how it compares with 2.4 mSv, and find radon's percentage.
6. A dosimeter badge shows 0.045 mSv in one month. Express this in µSv, find the total for 12 such months in µSv and mSv, and the percentage of 2.4 mSv.
7. A hospital stores a waste isotope (kalpanik) of half-life 20 days until its activity is below 1 percent of the start. Find the minimum time using whole half-lives.
8. A beta gauge shows 2000 counts per minute for the correct sheet but 2600 today. Is the sheet thicker or thinner, and by what percent has the count changed? Why would an alpha source not suit this job?
9. Explain in three or four lines why a smoke detector uses an alpha source, why this is safe on a ceiling, and why it must never be opened.
10. A gamma source gives a dose rate of 24 µSv per hour at one position and 1.5 µSv per hour at a farther position. A person stays 2.5 hours at each. Find both doses and their ratio. Which protection rule does this show?
11. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) Irradiated spices become radioactive. (b) A banana has a little natural radioactivity. (c) Mobile phone radiation is ionising. (d) High-level waste is thrown into rivers. (e) Lead apron reduces X-ray reaching the body.
12. A student says that wheat treated with gamma rays will make a counter click above background. Is she right? Explain how it differs from a tracer given to a patient.
13. In mutation breeding thousands of seeds are exposed to gamma rays and grown. Why are so many plants needed, and are the new seeds radioactive?
