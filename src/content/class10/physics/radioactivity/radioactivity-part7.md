## Part C: Mixed sawal: auzaar jodna

### Example 1. Daughter nikalo, phir bacha mass

Ek hospital ke I-131 sample ka mass 72 mg hai. I-131 (Z = 53) beta kan chhodta hai aur xenon (Xe, Z = 54) banta hai. I-131 ki T-half lagbhag 8 din hai. (a) Nuclear equation likho aur A, Z jaanch karo. (b) 40 din baad kitna I-131 bachega, aur kitna Xe-131 ban chuka hoga?

**Given:** I-131 (Z = 53), beta decay, Xe Z = 54, m₀ = 72 mg, T½ = 8 d, t = 40 d
**Find:** equation, bacha I-131, bana Xe-131
**Formula:** A wahi, Z + 1; n = t ÷ T½; m = m₀ ÷ 2ⁿ
**Substitution:** (a) I-131 → Xe-131 + e⁻. (b) n = 40 ÷ 8 = 5; m = 72 ÷ 2⁵ = 72 ÷ 32
**Answer:** (a) I-131 → Xe-131 + e⁻ (b) bacha I-131 = **2.25 mg**; bana Xe-131 lagbhag 72 − 2.25 = **69.75 mg** (electron ka mass bahut kam hai)
**Jaanch:** A: 131 = 131 + 0 ✅; Z: 53 = 54 − 1 ✅; 72 → 36 → 18 → 9 → 4.5 → 2.25 mg (paanch baar aadha) ✅; 2.25 + 69.75 = 72 ✅

### Example 2. Ek chain, ginti ka hisaab

Ra-226 (Z = 88) kai alpha aur beta chhod-kar aakhir mein stable Pb-206 (Z = 82) ban jaata hai. Kitne alpha aur kitne beta?

**Given:** A: 226 → 206, Z: 88 → 82
**Find:** alpha ki ginti x, beta ki ginti y
**Formula:** A sirf alpha se ghatta hai (4 har baar); Z: −2 per alpha, +1 per beta
**Substitution:** 226 − 4x = 206, to 4x = 20, x = 5. Z: 88 − 2 × 5 + y = 82, to 78 + y = 82, y = 4
**Answer:** **5 alpha aur 4 beta**
**Jaanch:** A: 226 − 5 × 4 = 206 ✅; Z: 88 − 10 + 4 = 82 ✅

Mukhya raasta (kuch jagah chhoti shaakhayein bhi hoti hain, jo aage ki class mein) ye hai; har kadam ka A aur Z jaanch liya gaya hai:

| Kadam | Kan | Naya nucleus | A | Z |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | shuruaat | Ra-226 | 226 | 88 |
| 1 | alpha | Rn-222 | 222 | 86 |
| 2 | alpha | Po-218 | 218 | 84 |
| 3 | alpha | Pb-214 | 214 | 82 |
| 4 | beta | Bi-214 | 214 | 83 |
| 5 | beta | Po-214 | 214 | 84 |
| 6 | alpha | Pb-210 | 210 | 82 |
| 7 | beta | Bi-210 | 210 | 83 |
| 8 | beta | Po-210 | 210 | 84 |
| 9 | alpha | Pb-206 | 206 | 82 |

Table mein alpha 5 baar (kadam 1, 2, 3, 6, 9) aur beta 4 baar (kadam 4, 5, 7, 8) aaye, jo upar ke hisaab se milta hai ✅. Dekho Z kaise upar-neeche hota hai: alpha Z ko 2 giraata hai, beta 1 uthata hai.

### Example 3. Dose aur samay: half-life ke saath

Ek hospital ke Co-60 source ke paas ek jagah dose rate **275 microsieverts per hour** hai (maan lo). Ek technician ko us jagah ek kaam karna hai jisme usse **55 microsieverts** se zyada dose nahi leni (ye seema maan lo, kalpanik hai). (a) Wo zyada se zyada kitni der wahan ruk sakta hai? (b) Wahi source 5.3 saal baad (Co-60 ki T-half lagbhag 5.3 saal), aur 10.6 saal baad, usi jagah par ho aur kuch aur na badle, to kitni der ruk sakta hai?

**Given:** dose rate = 275 µSv/h, seema = 55 µSv, T½ = 5.3 y
**Find:** samay t
**Formula:** dose = dose rate × time, to t = dose ÷ dose rate; dose rate bhi N ki tarah aadha hota hai
**Substitution:** (a) t = 55 ÷ 275 = 0.2 h = 12 minute. (b) 5.3 saal baad rate = 275 ÷ 2 = 137.5 µSv/h, t = 55 ÷ 137.5 = 0.4 h = 24 minute; 10.6 saal baad rate = 275 ÷ 4 = 68.75 µSv/h, t = 55 ÷ 68.75 = 0.8 h = 48 minute
**Answer:** **12 minute**, phir **24 minute**, phir **48 minute**
**Jaanch:** 275 × 0.2 = 55 ✅; 137.5 × 0.4 = 55 ✅; 68.75 × 0.8 = 55 ✅. Rate aadha hua to samay do guna: ulte anupaat mein ✅

```
   Source ki umar   Surakshit samay (har # = 4 minute)

   0 saal           ###                          12 minute
   5.3 saal         ######                       24 minute
   10.6 saal        ############                 48 minute
   15.9 saal        ########################     96 minute
```

> **Figure 7.1** – Co-60 source jaise jaise purana hota hai, usi jagah surakshit samay har 5.3 saal mein do guna hota hai: 12, 24, 48, 96 minute. Ye chitra Example 3 ke maan lo dose rate (275 µSv/h) aur seema (55 µSv) se bana hai; aakhri bar 15.9 saal (teen half-lives) ke liye hai.

Ye hisaab sirf kitaabi hai. Asli kaam mein teeno rules saath chalte hain: samay kam, doori zyada, aur beech mein shielding (Topic 6). Technician ke paas dosimeter badge bhi hota hai jo asli dose naapta hai.

### Example 4. Gamma, half-life aur ek seema

Hospital ka Tc-99m source subah 8 baje 5600 counts per minute deta hai. Tc-99m ki T-half lagbhag 6 ghante hai. Scan ke liye kam se kam 600 counts per minute chahiye (maan lo). (a) Tc-99m, Tc-99 ban kar gamma chhodta hai: equation aur jaanch likho (Tc ka Z = 43). (b) 18 ghante baad aur 24 ghante baad count rate kya hogi? Kya scan 24 ghante baad ho sakta hai?

**Given:** 5600 counts per minute, T½ = 6 h, seema = 600 counts per minute
**Find:** gamma equation, count rate 18 h aur 24 h par
**Formula:** gamma mein A aur Z wahi; n = t ÷ T½; rate = rate₀ ÷ 2ⁿ
**Substitution:** (a) Tc-99m → Tc-99 + gamma. (b) 18 h: n = 3, rate = 5600 ÷ 8 = 700. 24 h: n = 4, rate = 5600 ÷ 16 = 350
**Answer:** (a) Tc-99m → Tc-99 + gamma (b) 18 ghante par **700**, 24 ghante par **350 counts per minute**. 350 < 600, to **24 ghante baad scan nahi** ho sakta; 18 ghante tak (700 > 600) ho sakta hai.
**Jaanch:** A: 99 = 99 + 0 ✅; Z: 43 = 43 + 0 ✅ (gamma se tatva nahi badla); 5600 → 2800 → 1400 → 700 → 350 (chaar baar aadha) ✅

### Example 5. Field mein bend aur parde

Ek source ek lead ke dibbe ke chhote sorakh se kirnein **left (←)** bhejta hai. Poore ilaake mein field **page se bahar (⊙)** hai. (a) Alpha, beta aur gamma kis taraf mudenge? (b) Raste mein ek kaagaz aur 5 mm aluminium ki plate rakhi jaaye to detector tak kaun pahunchega?

**Given:** chaal ←, field ⊙
**Find:** alpha, beta, gamma ki disha; bachi hui kirnein
**Formula:** left-hand rule (alpha: current chaal ki taraf; beta: current ulta)
**Substitution:** alpha: forefinger ⊙ (bahar), middle finger ←, thumb **up (↑)**. Beta: current →, forefinger ⊙, thumb **down (↓)**. Gamma: koi charge nahi, seedhi ←
**Answer:** (a) alpha **up** (thoda), beta **down** (zyada), gamma **seedhi**. (b) kaagaz alpha ko rokta hai, kuch mm aluminium beta ko rokta hai, to detector tak sirf **gamma** (thodi ghati hui) pahunchti hai.
**Jaanch:** Topic 2 ke Example 4 mein chaal ↑ aur field ⊗ par alpha left tha. Chitra ko 90 degree anticlockwise ghumao to chaal ← ho jaati hai aur alpha ka force down; field ⊗ wahi rehta hai. Ab field ulta (⊙) karo to force bhi ulta: alpha up ✅. Alpha aur beta ulti taraf ✅

### Example 6. Fusion ki jaanch aur E = mc²

H-2 aur H-3 jud kar He-4 aur ek neutron banate hain. Ek fusion experiment mein kul **7.0 microgram** mass kam hota dikhta hai. (a) Equation ka A aur Z hisaab. (b) Mass kam hone se kitni energy mili? (c) Ye kitni kWh hai, aur agar ek ghar roz 5 kWh bijli (maan lo) kharch kare to kitne din chalegi? (c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s; 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J, jaisa Chapter 7 mein dekha)

**Given:** m = 7.0 µg = 7.0 × 10⁻⁹ kg, c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s
**Find:** A, Z jaanch; E in J; E in kWh; din
**Formula:** E = mc²; kWh = J ÷ (3.6 × 10⁶)
**Substitution:** (a) H-2 + H-3 → He-4 + n. (b) E = 7.0 × 10⁻⁹ × (3 × 10⁸)² = 7.0 × 10⁻⁹ × 9 × 10¹⁶ = 6.3 × 10⁸ J. (c) 6.3 × 10⁸ ÷ 3.6 × 10⁶ = 175 kWh; din = 175 ÷ 5
**Answer:** (b) **6.3 × 10⁸ J** (c) **175 kWh**, yaani **35 din**
**Jaanch:** A: 2 + 3 = 4 + 1 ✅; Z: 1 + 1 = 2 + 0 ✅; 175 × 3.6 × 10⁶ = 6.3 × 10⁸ ✅; 5 × 35 = 175 ✅

Dhyan do: Ch3 Topic 5 ki tarah E = mc² mein mass **kilogram** mein hona chahiye; 1 µg = 10⁻⁹ kg.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Garam karne se radioactive sample ki half-life chhoti ho jaati hai"**
Topic 1 mein hum ne dekha tha ki radioactivity garmi, dabaav ya chemical bandhan se lagbhag bina asar ke rehti hai. Demonstration: ek kalpanik isotope Q ki T-half 12 din hai. Do 80 g sample lo: ek thanda kamre mein, doosra tez garam bhatti ke paas (sirf kitaabi sochna: koi radioactive cheez garam karne ka prayog nahi karna). Dono ka 36 din baad hisaab: n = 36 ÷ 12 = 3; m = 80 ÷ 8 = **10 g** dono mein. Agar garmi se T-half chhoti hoti, to garam wale mein 10 g se kam bachta, par asal mein dono mein wahi 10 g hai. Wajah: garmi atoms ke hilne-dulne ki energy badhaati hai, par nucleus ke andar ki energy usse kahin zyada badi hoti hai, to bahar ki garmi us par lagbhag asar nahi daalti (aage ki class mein).

**Galti 2: "Radioactivity sirf insaan ke banaye padarthon mein hoti hai"**
Nahi. Topic 1 mein hum ne natural radioactivity dekhi thi, aur Topic 3 ke ladder (Figure 3.2) mein Ra-226 U-238 ke silsile mein **apne aap** aaya tha, kisi ne banaya nahi. Demonstration: U-238 ki T-half lagbhag 4.5 × 10⁹ saal hai: itni lambi ki dharti ki chattaanon mein ye aaj bhi bahut bacha hai, aur wahan se Th-234, Ra-226 jaise nuclei dheere-dheere apne aap bante rehte hain. Ye sab insaan ke aane se pehle se hai; kisi ne factory mein nahi banaya. Natural aur artificial dono hote hain (Co-60 aur Tc-99m artificial).

**Galti 3: "Gamma rays ko ek kaagaz rok deta hai"**
Nahi: kaagaz **alpha** ko rokta hai. Demonstration (counts maan lo): ek gamma source se counter 3000 counts per minute deta hai. Ek kaagaz lagao: 2985 (zara sa ghata). Moti lead ki parat lagao: 300 (bahut ghata, par shunya nahi). Agar kaagaz gamma ko rokta, to counter 0 dikhata. Gamma ko ghatane ke liye kaagaz nahi, moti lead ya concrete chahiye (Topic 2). Gamma ek EM wave hai, kaagaz ke aar-paar nikal jaati hai.

**Galti 4: "Isotopes ki chemistry same hai, to C-12 aur C-14 bilkul ek jaise hain"**
Class 9 mein (Part C) electrons ki wajah se chemistry same thi. Yahan naya nuktaa: **nuclear gun alag hote hain**, chemical gun nahi. Demonstration: C-12 aur C-14, dono ke 6 electrons hain. Dono oxygen se mil kar CO₂ banate hain: C-12 wala CO₂ lagbhag 12 + 32 = 44 u, C-14 wala lagbhag 14 + 32 = 46 u. Chemistry same hai, mass alag hai (2 u ka fark), aur sirf C-14 ka nucleus beta chhodta hai (C-14 → N-14 + e⁻). Isi liye ped ko C-14 aur C-12 dono ek jaise "khaane" mein lagte hain (carbon dating ka aadhaar, Topic 4).

**Galti 5: "Fission aur fusion ek hi cheez ke do naam hain"**
Nahi: ulte raaste hain. Demonstration: fission mein ek bhaari nucleus tootta hai: U-235 + n → Ba-141 + Kr-92 + 3 n; ek bada nucleus do madhyam nuclei aur teen neutron mein. Fusion mein do halke judte hain: H-2 + H-3 → He-4 + n; A mein 2 + 3 = 5 shuru mein, 4 + 1 = 5 baad mein. Fission ko neutron aur chain ka control chahiye, fusion ko lagbhag 10⁷ °C ya usse zyada chahiye. Dono mein mass thoda kam hota hai, par shuruat aur nateeja ulte hain.

- **Auzaar ka chunav:** A, Z jod (Topic 3); N₀ ÷ 2ⁿ (Topic 4); E = mc² (Ch3 aur Topic 5); left-hand rule (Topic 2, Ch9); dose = rate × time (Topic 6).
- **Mixed sawal:** pehle daughter ya kan nikalo (A, Z), phir bacha hissa, phir dose ya energy; har jawab ek doosre raaste se jaanch lo.
- **Chain ginti:** A sirf alpha se ghatta hai (4 har baar); phir Z se beta nikalo. Ra-226 se Pb-206: 5 alpha, 4 beta.
- **Bend:** positive ke liye current chaal ki taraf, negative ke liye ulta; alpha aur beta ulti taraf, gamma seedhi.
- **Half-life** garmi, dabaav ya chemical se nahi badalti; **natural** radioactivity dharti mein bhari padi hai; gamma kaagaz se nahi rukti; isotopes ki chemistry same par nuclear gun alag; fission aur fusion ulte hain.
- **Suraksha:** radioactive cheez kabhi chhoona, ikattha karna ya kholna nahi; trefoil dekho to door raho aur bade ko batao.

## Practice Questions

Use these atomic numbers where needed: Tc 43, Cs 55, Ba 56, Rn 86, Pb 82, U 92, H 1, He 2, Zr 40, Te 52. In every question about a nucleus write the equation and the two-line A and Z check.

1. Cs-137 (Z = 55) decays by beta emission to barium (Ba, Z = 56). The half-life of Cs-137 is about 30 years. (a) Write the equation and check A and Z. (b) A sample has 4.8 g of Cs-137. How much is left after 150 years, and about how much Ba-137 has formed?

2. A plutonium nucleus, Pu-241 (Z = 94), changes through a series of alpha and beta decays into Bi-209 (Z = 83). Find the number of alpha and beta particles emitted, and check Z at the end.

3. A hospital's Tc-99m source (half-life about 6 hours) gives 6720 counts per minute at 8 a.m. Assume the count rate is proportional to the number of Tc-99m nuclei. (a) Write the equation for Tc-99m changing to Tc-99 by emitting only a gamma ray, with the A and Z check. (b) What is the count rate at 8 p.m. the same day? (c) At what clock time does the count rate fall to 420 counts per minute?

4. At a technician's working spot the dose rate from an I-131 source is 144 microsieverts per hour (maan lo, take dose = dose rate × time). The most dose allowed for one task is 36 microsieverts (maan lo). Find how long the task may take (a) today, and (b) after 8 days, when the I-131 has decayed (its half-life is about 8 days). Take the spot and source position as unchanged.

5. A source in a lead box sends rays downward (↓) through a region where the field is into the page (⊗). Using the left-hand rule, state the direction in which alpha and beta bend, and whether gamma bends. Which one bends more, and why?

6. In a fusion reaction two tritium nuclei (H-3, Z = 1) join to give He-4 and two neutrons. (a) Check the A and Z balance of H-3 + H-3 → He-4 + 2 n. (b) In an experiment the total mass lost is 4.5 micrograms. Find the energy in joules and in kWh. (c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s; 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J.)

7. One possible fission of uranium is U-235 + n → Zr-94 + Te-139 + x n, where Zr has Z = 40 and Te has Z = 52. Find x using A, and then check that Z balances.

8. A piece of an ancient bone has one sixty-fourth of the C-14 fraction of a living bone. (a) Find its age, taking the half-life of C-14 as about 5730 years. (b) Another bone has one 1024th of the living fraction. Can carbon dating be trusted for it? Use the limit of about 50,000 years.

9. A nucleus U-235 (Z = 92) finally becomes Pb-207 (Z = 82) after a series of alpha and beta decays. Find the number of alpha and beta particles, and show that A and Z balance.

10. Kshipra says: "If I heat a radioactive sample strongly, its half-life will become shorter, because everything happens faster when hot." Explain whether she is right, and say what heating does and does not change.

11. Name the best protection rule (time, distance or shielding) for each, and why: (a) A hospital technician leaves the room before an X-ray is taken. (b) A thick lead door in a radiotherapy room. (c) A worker stays at the far end of a long corridor while a source is moved by a machine.

12. Match each idea with the Class 10 Physics chapter and topic where it is taught in detail: (a) the force on a moving charge in a magnetic field; (b) E = mc² and fission in a reactor; (c) gamma rays in the electromagnetic family; (d) the unit kWh.

### Answers

1. (a) **Equation:** Cs-137 → Ba-137 + e⁻. **Jaanch:** A: 137 = 137 + 0 ✅; Z: 55 = 56 − 1 ✅. (b) **Given:** m₀ = 4.8 g, T½ = 30 y, t = 150 y. **Formula:** n = t ÷ T½; m = m₀ ÷ 2ⁿ. **Substitution:** n = 150 ÷ 30 = 5; m = 4.8 ÷ 32 = 0.15 g. **Answer:** bacha Cs-137 = **0.15 g**; bana Ba-137 lagbhag 4.8 − 0.15 = **4.65 g**. **Jaanch:** 4.8 → 2.4 → 1.2 → 0.6 → 0.3 → 0.15 ✅; 0.15 + 4.65 = 4.8 ✅.

2. **Formula:** A: 241 − 4x = 209; Z: 94 − 2x + y = 83. **Substitution:** 4x = 32, to x = 8; Z: 94 − 16 + y = 83, to y = 5. **Answer:** **8 alpha aur 5 beta**. **Jaanch:** A: 241 − 8 × 4 = 209 ✅; Z: 94 − 8 × 2 + 5 × 1 = 83 ✅.

3. (a) Tc-99m → Tc-99 + gamma. **Jaanch:** A: 99 = 99 + 0 ✅; Z: 43 = 43 + 0 ✅ (gamma se tatva nahi badla). (b) 8 a.m. se 8 p.m. = 12 ghante, n = 12 ÷ 6 = 2; rate = 6720 ÷ 4 = **1680 counts per minute**. (c) 6720 ÷ 420 = 16 = 2⁴, to n = 4; t = 4 × 6 = 24 ghante, yaani agle din subah **8 a.m.**. **Jaanch:** 6720 → 3360 → 1680 → 840 → 420 (chaar baar aadha) ✅.

4. **Formula:** t = dose ÷ dose rate. (a) t = 36 ÷ 144 = 0.25 h = **15 minute**. (b) n = 8 ÷ 8 = 1; dose rate = 144 ÷ 2 = 72 µSv/h; t = 36 ÷ 72 = 0.5 h = **30 minute**. **Jaanch:** 144 × 0.25 = 36 ✅; 72 × 0.5 = 36 ✅; rate aadha, samay do guna (15 minute × 2 = 30 minute) ✅.

5. Alpha: current ↓, field ⊗: forefinger ⊗ (andar), middle finger ↓, thumb **right (→)**. Beta: current ↑, thumb **left (←)**. Gamma **seedhi**, kyunki charge nahi. Beta zyada mudta hai kyunki wo bahut halka aur tez hai; alpha bhaari aur dheema hai. **Jaanch:** Topic 2 ke practice mein ↓ ke saath field ⊙ tha aur alpha left; field ulta (⊗) karne par alpha right ✅.

6. (a) **Equation:** H-3 + H-3 → He-4 + 2 n. **Jaanch:** A: 3 + 3 = 6 aur 4 + 2 × 1 = 6 ✅; Z: 1 + 1 = 2 aur 2 + 2 × 0 = 2 ✅. (b) **Given:** m = 4.5 µg = 4.5 × 10⁻⁹ kg. **Formula:** E = mc². **Substitution:** E = 4.5 × 10⁻⁹ × 9 × 10¹⁶ = 4.05 × 10⁸ J; kWh = 4.05 × 10⁸ ÷ 3.6 × 10⁶ = 112.5. **Answer:** **4.05 × 10⁸ J**, yaani **112.5 kWh**. **Jaanch:** 112.5 × 3.6 × 10⁶ = 4.05 × 10⁸ ✅.

7. **Formula:** A ka jod barabar. **Substitution:** 235 + 1 = 236; 94 + 139 = 233, to x = 236 − 233 = 3. Z: 92 + 0 = 92 aur 40 + 52 + 3 × 0 = 92. **Answer:** **x = 3 neutron**. **Jaanch:** A: 236 = 94 + 139 + 3 ✅; Z: 92 = 40 + 52 ✅.

8. (a) 1 ÷ 64 = 1 ÷ 2⁶, to n = 6; umar = 6 × 5730 = **34,380 saal**. (b) 1 ÷ 1024 = 1 ÷ 2¹⁰, to n = 10; umar = 10 × 5730 = 57,300 saal, jo lagbhag 50,000 saal ki seema se zyada hai: bacha C-14 bahut kam hai, to nateeja **bharose ke laayak nahi**. **Jaanch:** 1 ÷ 2⁶ = 1 ÷ 64 ✅; 2¹⁰ = 1024 ✅; 34,380 < 50,000 ✅.

9. **Formula:** A: 235 − 4x = 207; Z: 92 − 2x + y = 82. **Substitution:** 4x = 28, to x = 7; Z: 92 − 14 + y = 82, to y = 4. **Answer:** **7 alpha aur 4 beta**. **Jaanch:** A: 235 − 7 × 4 = 207 ✅; Z: 92 − 7 × 2 + 4 × 1 = 82 ✅.

10. Kshipra **galat** hai. Garam karne se atoms zyada tezi se hilte hain aur chemical reactions tez hoti hain, par decay nucleus ke andar ki baat hai: T-half lagbhag wahi rehti hai (Topic 1 aur Galti 1). Garmi sirf **sample ko garam** karti hai (aur chemical roop badal sakti hai), decay ki raftaar nahi. Isliye kitaabi sawaal mein bhi T-half wahi li jaati hai, chahe sample garam ho ya thanda. Aur koi bhi radioactive cheez ko garam karne ka prayog **kabhi nahi** karna.

11. (a) **Shielding aur doori/samay:** technician kamre se bahar nikalkar kam se kam samay aur zyada doori mein rehti hai (Topic 6). Dentist ke X-ray mein bhi yahi (Chapter 5). (b) **Shielding:** moti lead gamma ko bahut ghatati hai. (c) **Doori:** source se door rehne par dose kaafi kam hoti hai. **Jaanch:** har jawab mein ek mukhya rule dikha ✅.

12. (a) **Chapter 9** (Electromagnetism), Topic 1: Fleming ka left-hand rule (taar ke current ke liye; chalte charge par iska istemaal is chapter ke Topic 2 mein). (b) **Chapter 3** (Sources of Energy), Topic 5. (c) **Chapter 5** (Atmospheric Refraction, Scattering and the Spectrum), Topic 5. (d) **Chapter 7** (Current Electricity II), Topic 5 (power aur energy, kW aur kWh ka farak). **Jaanch:** Part C ke Example 5 aur 6 inhi chapters se kaam lete hain ✅.
