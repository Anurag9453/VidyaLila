# Topic 1: Nucleus Aur Radioactivity: Atom Ke Andar Ki Halchal

Pichhle chapter Heat II mein garmi, nami aur mausam ka poora khel atoms aur molecules ki **bahari chaal** ka tha: kaun tezi se ud raha hai, kaun jud raha hai. Ab hum us jagah chalte hain jahan na garmi pahunchti hai, na dabaav: **atom ke bilkul beech ka nucleus**. Wahan bhi kabhi-kabhi ek halchal hoti hai jo apne aap shuru hoti hai, aur isi se ye poora chapter bana hai.

## Hospital ke gate par peela-kaala nishaan

Ravivaar ko Mr. Zope apni bhanji Pratibha ko lekar hospital gaye, kyunki Mama ko apne ek dost ki report lene jaana tha. Lambe corridor ke ek kone mein ek bhaari darwaza tha. Authorised persons only."

Ek technician, gale mein ek chhota sa badge latkaye, darwaze ke andar gaya aur darwaza dheere se band ho gaya. Pratibha ne dabi awaaz mein poochha, "Mama, ye teen patte wala nishaan kya hai? Andar kya jal raha hai? Na dhuan dikh raha hai, na kuch chamak raha hai."

Mama ne use darwaze se kuch kadam peeche kheench liya. "Yahi to baat hai, Pratibha. Andar **kuch dikhta hi nahi**. Hum corridor se hi dekhenge, andar jaane ka koi kaam nahi hai. Pratibha boli, "To phir pata kaise chalta hai?" Mama ne muskura kar kaha, "Aaj ghar jaakar wahi samjhenge: **nucleus ke andar kya hota hai, aur kuch nuclei apne aap kyun badalte hain**."

## Part A: Nucleus: chhota par bahut bhaari

Class 9 mein Rutherford ke alpha-kan wale prayog se dekha tha ki atom ke beech mein ek bahut chhota, ghana, positive **nucleus** hai, jisme protons hain (inki ginti Z, tatva ki pehchaan), aur neutrons bhi (A = Z + neutrons). Ek hi tatva ke alag-alag neutron wale atom **isotopes** hain, aur kuch isotopes (jaise carbon-14, iodine-131) **radioactive** hain. Chapter 3 ke Topic 5 mein ye bhi dekha tha ki U-235 ka nucleus toot sakta hai aur bahut energy deta hai: [Sources of Energy, Topic 5](../sources-of-energy/sources-of-energy-part5.md). Tab nucleus ko hum todte the. Aaj dekhenge ki **kuch nuclei khud kyun toot ya badal jaate hain.**

Nucleus ke andar jo kan hain unhe saath mein **nucleons** kehte hain.

| Kan | Charge | Mass (lagbhag) | Kahan |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proton | +1 | 1 u | Nucleus |
| Neutron | 0 | 1 u | Nucleus |
| Electron | −1 | 1 ÷ 1836 u | Nucleus ke bahar |

```
   Carbon-12 (6 P, 6 n)              Carbon-14 (6 P, 8 n)

         P  n  P                          n  P  n
         n  P  n                        P  n  n  P
         P  n  P                        n  P  P  n
         n  P  n                          n  P  n
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Nucleus ek guchchha hai protons (P) aur neutrons (n) ka (schematic, asli nucleus bahut chhota aur gol hota hai). Carbon-12 mein 6 P aur 6 n (kul 12 nucleons), carbon-14 mein 6 P aur 8 n (kul 14). Protons dono mein same, bas neutron ki ginti alag.

**Mass kahan hai?** Electron ka mass proton ka lagbhag 1 ÷ 1836 hissa hai. Isliye atom ka **lagbhag sara mass nucleus mein** hai, jabki nucleus atom se lagbhag ek lakh guna chhota hai (Class 9). Matlab bahut kam jagah mein lagbhag poora dravyaman: nucleus **bahut ghana** hai.

### Example 1. Co-60 ke atom mein mass ka hisaab

Cobalt ka Z = 27. Co-60 ke ek neutral atom mein protons, neutrons aur electrons ginti karo, aur batao ki electrons ka mass atom ke mass ka kitna hissa hai (har nucleon lagbhag 1 u).

**Given:** Z = 27, A = 60
**Find:** protons, neutrons, electrons; electrons ka mass-hissa
**Formula:** neutrons = A − Z; electrons (neutral atom) = Z; electrons ka mass = electrons ÷ 1836 u
**Substitution:** neutrons = 60 − 27 = 33; electrons ka mass = 27 ÷ 1836 = 0.0147 u; hissa = 0.0147 ÷ 60 = 0.000245
**Answer:** 27 protons, 33 neutrons, 27 electrons. Electrons atom ke mass ka sirf **lagbhag 0.025 percent** hain, yaani lagbhag 99.975 percent mass nucleus mein hai.
**Jaanch:** 27 + 33 = 60 ✅. 0.000245 × 100 = 0.0245 percent, jo 1 percent se kai guna chhota hai ✅.

## Part B: Kuch nuclei asthir kyun hote hain?

Nucleus mein protons **positive** hain, aur same charge ek-doosre ko dhakelte hain. To ek nucleus bikhar kyun nahi jaata? Kyunki nucleons ke beech ek bahut taqatwar aakarshan chalta hai, jise **strong force** kehte hain. Wo sirf bahut chhoti doori par kaam karta hai aur nucleons ko chipkaye rakhta hai (iska gehra hisaab aage ki class mein). Neutron dhakelte nahi, par chipkane mein madad karte hain, isliye zyada protons ke saath **zyada neutron** chahiye.

Par ye santulan nazuk hai. Aam taur par ye teen halat nucleus ko asthir banate hain:

1. **Zyada neutron** (stable bhai se bhi zyada).
2. **Kam neutron** (protons ke hisaab se bahut kam).
3. **Bahut bhaari nucleus** (bahut saare protons ek saath): Pb (Z = 82) se aage ke nuclei lagbhag sab radioactive hain.

Dhalaan par rakhi gend ki tarah socho: wo zyada oonchi jagah se neeche ki taraf lurakna chahti hai, jahan ruk sake. Asthir nucleus bhi zyada sthir arrangement ki taraf badalta hai (par "kab" badlega, ye alag baat hai: Part C).

| Tatva | Sthir bhai | Radioactive bhai | Neutron: sthir wale mein, radioactive wale mein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Carbon (Z = 6) | C-12, C-13 | C-14 | 6 aur 7, phir 8 |
| Sodium (Z = 11) | Na-23 | Na-24 | 12, phir 13 |
| Cobalt (Z = 27) | Co-59 | Co-60 | 32, phir 33 |
| Potassium (Z = 19) | K-39, K-41 | K-40 | 20 aur 22, phir beech wale K-40 mein 21 |

Dekha? C-14, Na-24 aur Co-60 mein apne sthir bhai se **zyada neutron** hain. Par K-40 (beech ki ginti 21 hone par bhi) asthir hai: yaani koi seedha formula nahi, aur poori baat aage ki class mein. Ye bas ek kaam ka sanket hai.

Bhaari nuclei ka hisaab: U-238 (Z = 92) mein neutrons 238 − 92 = **146**, aur Ra-226 (Z = 88) mein 226 − 88 = **138**. Itne saare protons aur neutrons ka bheed hi inhe asthir banata hai.

## Part C: Radioactivity kya hai: apne aap, random aur bina asar ke

> **Radioactivity:** ek **asthir nucleus** ka kuch kan ya energy chhodkar zyada sthir ban jaana. Ye nucleus ke andar ki baat hai, bahar ke electrons ki nahi.

Kya chhodta hai: kirnein ya kan, jin ke teen kism ka naam Topic 2 mein khulega. Abhi sirf itna: kuch nuclei bahut tez kan chhodte hain, aur is chhodne ke saath **nucleus badal jaata hai** (Part F).

Teen khaas baatein:

1. **Spontaneous (apne aap).** Nucleus ko kisi ne chhua nahi, jalaya nahi, kuch nahi. Wo apne aap badalta hai.
2. **Random.** Ek hi tarah ke kai nuclei saath rakho: koi nahi bata sakta ki kaunsa pehle badlega. Har nucleus ka apna "samay" hai jo pehle se tay nahi. Par **bahut saare nuclei** ek pattern par chalte hain: Topic 4 (half-life) mein ye pattern milega.
3. **Bahari cheezon se (lagbhag) bina asar ke.** Garmi, dabaav ya chemical reaction (kisi aur tatva se jodna) radioactivity ki dar ko **lagbhag nahi badalte** (bahut sookshm farak, jo aage ki class ka vishay hai, ho sakte hain).

| Cheez | Chemical reaction | Radioactivity |
|---|---|---|
| Kya badalta hai | Bahari electrons ka jodna-todna | Nucleus |
| Temperature badhane se | Aksar bahut tez | Lagbhag koi asar nahi |
| Doosre tatva se jodne se | Naya padarth banta hai | Dar lagbhag wahi |
| Z (protons) | Wahi rehta hai | Badal sakta hai |

Kaha jaata hai ki isi baat ne Curie ko chaunkaya tha: uranium kisi bhi roop (namak, oxide, dhatu) mein ho, radioactivity uranium ki **matra** se judi thi, roop se nahi. Isse unhone samjha ki ye atom ke andar ki baat hai.

**Becquerel (Bq)** unit batati hai ki har second kitne nuclei decay ho rahe hain: **1 Bq = har second 1 decay.** Ye bas ek naam hai; iske hisaab aage ki class mein.

## Part D: Natural aur artificial radioactivity

| Prakaar | Kahan se | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Natural** | Zameen aur chattanon mein pehle se | U-238, Ra-226 |
| **Natural** | Hamare aas-paas ke tatvon mein | K-40: potassium ke lagbhag har 10,000 atoms mein lagbhag 1 atom K-40 hota hai |
| **Natural** | Oopar ke vaayumandal mein | C-14: cosmic kirnein banati rehti hain |
| **Artificial** | Reactor mein | Co-60 |
| **Artificial** | Reactor ya accelerator se bane padarth se | Tc-99m (hospital ke imaging ke liye) |

Technetium (Z = 43) ka koi bhi isotope sthir nahi: isliye prakriti mein wo lagbhag nahi milta aur use banana padta hai. Artificial ka matlab ye nahi ki wo kisi aur tarah ka radioactive hai: **radioactivity ka tareeka wahi hai.** Fark sirf ye hai ki nucleus insaan ne banaya.

### Example 2. Co-60 reactor mein kaise banta hai

Reactor mein sthir Co-59 (Z = 27) ek neutron nigal leta hai aur Co-60 ban jaata hai. Equation likho aur A aur Z ka balance jaanchon. (Ye sirf padhne ke liye: reactor mein visheshagya ye kaam karte hain.)

**Given:** Co-59: A = 59, Z = 27. Neutron n: A = 1, Z = 0
**Find:** banne wala nucleus aur balance
**Formula:** dono taraf A ka jod same aur Z ka jod same
**Substitution:** Co-59 + n → Co-60
**Answer:** Co-59 + n → Co-60
**Jaanch:**
- A: 59 + 1 = 60 ✅
- Z: 27 + 0 = 27 ✅
- Neutron ginti: 32 + 1 = 33 ✅ (Co-60 mein 33, Example 1).

Z wahi (27) hai, isliye tatva abhi bhi cobalt hai: sirf neutron ginti badhi, yaani nucleus "zyada neutron" wala asthir ban gaya (Part B).

### Activity 1: Nucleus ka model (ghar par adult ke saath)

Do rang ki mitti ya clay ki chhoti goliyan banao: laal = proton, neela = neutron. Carbon-12 banao: 6 laal aur 6 neela, aur ek chhote gole mein chipka do. Phir 2 aur neele jodkar C-14 banao. Likho: kul nucleons kitne, protons kitne, tatva kyun wahi raha. **Chhoti goliyan munh mein nahi; chhote bachche door.** Isme koi radioactive cheez nahi hai: ye sirf ginti ka model hai.

## Part E: Khoj ki kahani: ek drawer, ek aurat aur kuch naye tatva

1896 mein Paris mein **Henri Becquerel** photographic plates ke saath kuch kar rahe the. Unhe laga ki uranium ke namak (jo dhoop mein chamakte hain) shayad dhoop se kuch aisi kirnein banate honge jo plate ko kaala kar deti hain. Plate ko kaale kaagaz mein lapeta gaya, upar namak aur beech mein ek dhatu ka cross rakha gaya.

Kuch din baadal chhaaye rahe, to unhone sab kuch ek **andhere drawer** mein rakh diya. Phir bhi (kaha jaata hai ki bas dekhne ke liye) unhone plate develop ki, aur dekha ki wo **gehri kaali** thi, cross ki parchhai ke saath. Matlab dhoop zaroori nahi thi: **uranium khud, bina dhoop ke, kuch aisa chhod raha tha jo kaale kaagaz ke aar-paar jaakar plate ko kaala kar deta hai.**

```
        uranium ka namak (upar)          <- sabse upar
        ==========================
              X  dhatu ka cross  X       <- beech mein, parchhai banata hai
        ==========================
        kaale kaagaz mein lipti plate    <- neeche, develop karne par kaali
        ==========================
        drawer ka andhera (poora band)
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Becquerel ka set-up (saral sketch, scale se nahi): namak upar, beech mein dhatu ka cross, neeche kaagaz mein lipti photographic plate. Develop karne par plate par cross ki parchhai dikhi, yaani drawer ke ghor andhere mein bhi kuch nikal raha tha.

**Marie aur Pierre Curie** ne aage badhkar dekha ki **pitchblende** (uranium ka ek ore) khud shuddh uranium se zyada active hai, to usme kuch aur zaroor hai. Tonnon ore se mehnat ke baad 1898 mein unhone do naye tatva khoje: **polonium** (Marie ke desh Poland ke naam par) aur **radium**. "Radioactivity" shabd bhi Marie ka diya hua kaha jaata hai. 1903 mein Becquerel aur Curie dampati ko physics ka Nobel Prize mila, aur 1911 mein Marie ko akeli ko chemistry ka Nobel Prize: do alag vishayon mein. Isi 1911 mein Rutherford ka nucleus wala prayog (Class 9) bhi hua tha.

```
   (a)     (b)                 (c)                             (d)
   *-------*-------------------*-------------------------------*
   1896    1898                1903                            1911

   (a) Becquerel: uranium ki kirnein     (b) Curie: polonium aur radium
   (c) Physics ka Nobel Prize           (d) Marie ka chemistry ka Nobel, Rutherford ka nucleus
```

## Part F: Z hi pehchaan hai: nucleus badla to tatva badla

Class 9 se: **protons ki ginti Z hi tatva ki pehchaan hai.**

| Z | Tatva | Z | Tatva |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hydrogen | 53 | Iodine |
| 6 | Carbon | 55 | Caesium |
| 11 | Sodium | 82 | Lead |
| 19 | Potassium | 84 | Polonium |
| 27 | Cobalt | 86 | Radon |
| 43 | Technetium | 92 | Uranium |

**Chemical reaction** sirf bahari electrons ko chhedti hai, nucleus ko nahi: Z wahi, tatva wahi. **Radioactivity** nucleus ko chhedti hai: agar Z badal gaya, to **naya tatva** ban gaya. Isi ko **transmutation** kehte hain. (Z kaise badalta hai, iske niyam Topic 3 mein.)

### Example 3. K-40: ek ion aur ek nucleus ka badlaav

K-40 (Z = 19) ka atom ek electron kho kar K⁺ ion banta hai. Ek alag soch: agar K-40 ke nucleus mein ek proton badh jaaye to? Dono mein tatva kya hoga?

**Given:** Z = 19, A = 40; K⁺ = ek electron kam; doosre case mein Z mein +1. Z = 20 calcium hai.
**Find:** K⁺ ka protons, neutrons, electrons; doosre case ka tatva
**Formula:** protons = Z; neutrons = A − Z; electrons (ion) = Z − 1
**Substitution:** K⁺: protons 19, neutrons 40 − 19 = 21, electrons 19 − 1 = 18. Doosra case: Z = 19 + 1 = 20
**Answer:** K⁺ mein 19 protons, 21 neutrons, 18 electrons: **tatva potassium hi hai** (sirf bahari electron gaya). Doosre case mein Z = 20, yaani **calcium**: naya tatva (transmutation).
**Jaanch:** K⁺: 19 + 21 = 40 ✅ (A wahi). Nucleus wahi, isliye chemical badlaav se tatva nahi badla. Z: 19 → 20 badla, isliye tatva badla ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Radioactive cheezein chamakti hain."**
Nahi. Radioactivity khud **dikhti nahi**. Becquerel ka uranium andhere drawer mein tha aur kuch nahi dikha: plate kaali hui. Chamak ka bhram purani ghadiyon se aaya: kuch purani ghadiyon ke numbers radium wale paint se bane the, jisme zinc sulphide jaisa ek **alag chamakne wala padarth** mila hota tha (hedged: jaisa aksar bataya jaata hai). Radium ki kirnein us padarth par padkar roshni banati thi, kirnein khud dikhti nahi thi. Wo chamakne wala padarth kuch saalon mein ghis jaata hai, to dial ki chamak chali jaati hai; par Ra-226 ki half-life lagbhag 1600 saal hai, yaani radium ka zyadatar hissa abhi bhi wahan hota hai. **Chamak gayi, radioactivity nahi gayi.** Isliye aisi purani ghadi mile to haath mat lagao, bade ko batao.

**Galti 2: "Radioactivity ek chemical reaction hai."**
Nahi. Chemical reaction mein sirf bahari electrons jud-tut te hain, nucleus wahi rehta hai. Demonstration: loha zang khata hai: 4 Fe + 3 O₂ → 2 Fe₂O₃. Fe ka Z = 26 zang lagne se pehle bhi 26, Fe₂O₃ ke andar bhi 26: nucleus ko kuch nahi hua, tatva loha hi raha. Radioactivity mein Z hi badal sakta hai (Part F). Aur chemical reaction garmi se bahut tez ho jaati hai, par radioactivity ki dar lagbhag wahi rehti hai: C-14 chaahe CO₂ mein ho, chaahe sugar mein, chaahe lakdi ki raakh mein, uska nucleus apne tareeke se badalta rehta hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Nucleus** protons aur neutrons (nucleons) ka ghana guchchha hai; lagbhag poora mass wahin hai (electron proton se lagbhag 1836 guna halka).
- **Strong force** nucleons ko chipkaye rakhti hai; zyada ya kam neutron, ya bahut bhaari nucleus ho to **asthir**. C-14, Na-24, Co-60 apne sthir bhai se zyada neutron wale hain.
- **Radioactivity:** asthir nucleus ka **apne aap**, **random** tareeke se kan ya energy chhodna; garmi, dabaav ya chemical bandhan se (lagbhag) bina asar ke.
- **Natural** (U-238, Ra-226, K-40, C-14) aur **artificial** (Co-60, Tc-99m); Co-59 + n → Co-60 (A: 59 + 1 = 60; Z: 27 + 0 = 27).
- **Khoj:** Becquerel (1896), Curie ka polonium aur radium (1898), Marie ke do Nobel (1903 physics, 1911 chemistry). Unit: **becquerel**, 1 Bq = har second 1 decay.
- **Z hi tatva ki pehchaan:** chemical reaction Z nahi badalti; nucleus ka badlaav Z badal sakta hai (**transmutation**).
- **Suraksha:** radioactive cheez ko kabhi chhoona, ikattha karna ya kholna nahi; trefoil dekho to door raho aur bade ko batao.

## Practice Questions

Use these atomic numbers where needed: O 8, F 9, Na 11, K 19, Co 27, Tc 43, Bi 83, Rn 86, Th 90. Treat each nucleon as about 1 u and the electron mass as 1 ÷ 1836 u.

5. Using the table, name the element with Z = 43, and the element whose nucleus has 83 protons and 126 neutrons. Also give the mass number of the second nucleus.
6. (a) A nucleus of oxygen (Z = 8) gains one proton. What are the new Z and the new element? (b) A separate oxygen atom gains two electrons. What is its number of electrons and is it still oxygen? (c) Which of (a) and (b) is a chemical change and which is a nuclear change?
7. (maan lo) Near a sealed lab sample, a detector's clicks in seven successive 15-second windows were 23, 38, 27, 18, 29, 25 and 31. Find the average number of clicks per window and the range. What does the variation suggest about when individual nuclei decay?
8. How many years passed between Becquerel's discovery and the Curies' discovery of polonium and radium? How many more years passed before the first Nobel Prize in the timeline of Figure 1.3?
9. State True or False, and correct the false ones: (a) Most of an atom's mass is in its nucleus. (b) Radioactive decay is a change in the outer electrons. (c) Co-60 used in hospitals is natural radioactivity. (d) Scientists can predict exactly when one particular nucleus will decay.
10. A friend finds a small metal box with a yellow sign of three black blades around a circle in a school's old cupboard. What should be done, and why must it not be opened?
11. In two or three lines explain why a nucleus with many protons packed together is in trouble, and what role neutrons play.
12. Describe in three or four lines how Becquerel found that uranium gives out something even in the dark. Why did a drawer matter?

### Answers

1. **Given:** Z = 11. **Formula:** neutrons = A − Z. **Substitution:** Na-23: protons 11, neutrons 23 − 11 = 12; Na-24: protons 11, neutrons 24 − 11 = 13. Neutrons minus protons: 12 − 11 = 1 aur 13 − 11 = 2. **Answer:** Na-23: 11 p, 12 n (1 zyada); Na-24: 11 p, 13 n (2 zyada). **Jaanch:** 11 + 12 = 23 ✅, 11 + 13 = 24 ✅.
2. **Equation:** Na-23 + n → Na-24.
   **Jaanch:**
   - A: 23 + 1 = 24 ✅
   - Z: 11 + 0 = 11 ✅

   Na-24 insaan ne reactor mein banaya, isliye wo **artificial** radioactivity hai. Z wahi (11) rahi, isliye tatva sodium hi hai.
