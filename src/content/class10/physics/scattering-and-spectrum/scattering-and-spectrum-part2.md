## Part A: Scattering aur kan ki size

### Roshni ek kan se takrati hai

Roshni ek wave hai (Class 9 ke Sound mein dekha tha: wavelength λ hoti hai). Jab ye wave kisi kan se guzarti hai, to kan ko hila deti hai, aur hilta hua kan **har dishaa mein** roshni dobara bhejne lagta hai. Isi ko **scattering** kehte hain. Beam ka kuch hissa seedhi raah se hat kar idhar-udhar chala jaata hai.

Ek zaroori sawaal: **kan ki size, roshni ki wavelength ke mukable kitni hai?** Do halat hote hain.

1. **Kan bahut chhota hai** (hawa ke molecules, lagbhag 0.3 nm; yaani wavelength 400 se 700 nm se hazaar guna se bhi chhote). Ab alag alag rang **alag alag mikdaar** mein bikharte hain: chhoti wavelength zyada, lambi wavelength kam.
2. **Kan bada hai** (badal ya kohre ki boondein, lagbhag kuch µm ya usse bhi badi; wavelength se kai guna badi). Ab sab rang **lagbhag barabar** bikharte hain.

```
                      neela: har taraf bahut bikhra
                       ↖        ↑        ↗

safed beam  ─────────►  o        o        o     ────────►  laal-narangi: zyada seedha nikla

                       ↙        ↓        ↘
                      neela: har taraf bahut bikhra

          o = hawa ke chhote kan (molecules)
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Chhote kan (o) par safed beam: neela rang har taraf bahut bikharta hai, laal-narangi rang kam bikharta hai aur zyadatar seedha aage nikal jaata hai. Teer ki disha scattered rays dikhati hai (kan ki size dikhane ke liye bahut badi banayi gayi hai).

### Ghar par dekho: doodh ka glass

**Ghar par adult ke saath.** Class 9 mein tumne doodh wale paani mein torch ki beam dekhi thi. Ab ek baar aur, par is baar **rang** par dhyan do.

1. Ek saaf, lamba glass paani se bharo. Usmein doodh ki sirf **do-teen boond** ya aate ki ek chutki daalo aur hilao. Paani halka dhundhla dikhna chahiye, safed nahi. (Zyada doodh daloge to paani doodh jaisa safed ho jaayega aur rang dikhna band.)
2. Kamre ki batti band karo. Phone ki torch ko glass ke **ek chhor par, side se** lagao, taaki beam glass ke andar ek lakeer ki tarah jaaye.
3. **Torch ko kabhi kisi ki aankh ki taraf mat karo**, aur beam mein seedha mat jhaanko.
4. Glass ko **side se** dekho: beam ka raasta halka **neela-sa** chamakta hai. Ab glass ko **doosre chhor** se (torch ke saamne wali taraf, kuch door se) dekho: beam ka rang **peela-narangi** dikhta hai.

Isse milti hai wahi kahani: **neela rang side mein bikhar jaata hai** (isliye side se neela dikhta hai), aur jo rang bikhre bina aage nikla, wo **transmitted** roshni hai, jismein neela kam bacha aur narangi-laal zyada. Aasmaan aur Sooraj ka khel isi glass ka bada roop hai.

## Part B: 1/λ⁴ ka rule aur "kitni guna" ka number

Chhote kan ke liye ek rule hai, jo hum **fact** ki tarah maan lenge (iska poora gadit aage ki class mein):

> **Bikhri hui roshni ki mikdaar ∝ 1/λ⁴**   (chhote kan ke liye)

Matlab, wavelength jitni **chhoti**, scattering utni **zyada**, aur "chauthi ghaat" ki wajah se bahut tezi se. Do rangon ka anupaat nikalne ke liye:

> **Relative scattering = (lambi λ ÷ chhoti λ)⁴**

Dono wavelength **ek hi unit** mein hon (dono nm mein), to unit kat jaata hai aur anupaat bina unit ka number hota hai.

### Example 1: Neela (400 nm) aur laal (700 nm)

Chhote kan par neela-violet chhor ka 400 nm laal (700 nm) se kitni guna zyada bikharta hai?

**Given:** λ_neela = 400 nm, λ_laal = 700 nm.
**Find:** (bikhra neela) ÷ (bikhra laal).
**Formula:** anupaat = (λ_laal ÷ λ_neela)⁴.
**Substitution:** 400 nm = 400 × 10⁻⁹ m = 4 × 10⁻⁷ m; 700 nm = 7 × 10⁻⁷ m. λ_laal ÷ λ_neela = (7 × 10⁻⁷) ÷ (4 × 10⁻⁷) = 7 ÷ 4 = 1.75. Phir 1.75⁴ = 1.75 × 1.75 × 1.75 × 1.75 = 3.0625 × 3.0625 = **9.38**.
**Answer:** neela rang laal se lagbhag **9 guna** zyada bikharta hai (≈ 9).

**Jaanch:** nm mein 700 ÷ 400 = 1.75, metre mein bhi 1.75: dono unit mein ek hi anupaat ✅. Order of magnitude: 1.75² ≈ 3, 3² ≈ 9 ✅

### Example 2: Ek aur pair (500 nm aur 700 nm)

Maan lo ek hara-neela rang 500 nm ka hai. Wo 700 nm ke laal se kitni guna zyada bikharta hai?

**Given:** λ₁ = 500 nm, λ₂ = 700 nm.
**Find:** anupaat.
**Formula:** (λ₂ ÷ λ₁)⁴.
**Substitution:** 700 ÷ 500 = 1.4. 1.4² = 1.96, 1.96² = 3.8416.
**Answer:** lagbhag **3.84 guna**.

**Jaanch:** 3.84 < 9.38 (Example 1), kyunki 500 nm, 400 nm ke mukable laal ke zyada paas hai ✅. Kisi bhi rang ke liye ye anupaat 1 se zyada nikla: chhoti wavelength zyada bikharti hai ✅

### Example 3: "Laal kitna bikharta hai" prati-shat mein

Example 1 ke hisaab se, neela agar **100 hissa** bikhre, to laal kitna?

**Given:** neela : laal = 9.38 : 1.
**Find:** laal ka hissa, neela ke percent mein.
**Formula:** laal ÷ neela = 1 ÷ 9.38.
**Substitution:** 1 ÷ 9.38 = 0.1066, yaani 0.1066 × 100 = 10.66 percent.
**Answer:** laal sirf lagbhag **11 percent**, yaani neela ka ek-dashvaan hissa bikharta hai.

**Jaanch:** 9.38 × 0.1066 = 1.000 ✅

Ye anupaat ek bar chitra mein dekho.

```
400 nm   ████████████████████████████  9.38
500 nm   ████████████  3.84
600 nm   ██████  1.85
700 nm   ███  1.00
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Chhote kan par relative scattering: 700 nm ke laal ko 1.00 maan kar, 600 nm par 1.85, 500 nm par 3.84 aur 400 nm par 9.38 guna. Bar ki lambai ≈ 3 block prati 1.00 (rounded). Wavelength ghatne par bar tezi se lamba hota hai.

## Part C: Aasmaan neela kyun, violet kyun nahi?

Ab gaur karo: 400 nm ka **violet**, 450 nm ke neele se bhi zyada bikharta hai. To agar scattering hi sab kuch hai, to aasmaan violet dikhna chahiye. Phir neela kyun? **Teen kaaran** milkar tay karte hain:

1. **Sooraj mein violet kam hai.** Sooraj ki roshni mein violet ka hissa neele-hare ke mukable kam hota hai, to bikharne ke liye violet kam milta hai.
2. **Upar ki hawa chhoti wavelength ka kuch hissa soakh leti hai.** Aksar bataya jaata hai ki upar ki parton mein UV (aur violet ke aas-paas ka thoda hissa) soakh ho jaata hai (ozone parat ki baat aage Topic 5 mein), to zameen tak violet aur kam pahunchta hai.
3. **Humari aankh violet ke liye kam sensitive hai.** Chapter 4 mein dekha tha ki cones teen kism ke hote hain ([human-eye.md](../human-eye/human-eye.md), Part E). Aankh ki sanvedanshilta 400 nm ke violet par bahut kam hoti hai aur neele-hare par kaafi zyada; dimaag teeno cones ke signals ka mel padhta hai, aur bikhre hue mix ka mel usse **neela-sa** hi dikhta hai.

Isliye bikhra hua, aankh tak aaya hua, aur aankh ko "dikha hua" mix **neela** banta hai. (Dopahar ko aasmaan **dhundhla neela** hota hai, kyunki thoda bikhra hua hara aur peela bhi mil jaata hai; saaf subah ya ooncha pahaad par aasmaan aur gehra neela hota hai.)

## Part D: Laal Sooraj, lambi raah ka kamaal

### Raasta kitna lamba?

Dopahar ko Sooraj sir ke upar hota hai, aur uski roshni ko hawa ki lagbhag **10 km** moti parat sidhe neeche aani padti hai. Sunrise ya sunset par Sooraj kshitij ke paas hota hai, aur wahi roshni hawa ki parat mein **tirchhi** ghusti hai: raasta kai guna lamba. Jitna lamba raasta, utne zyada kan jinse roshni takrati hai, aur utna zyada **neela** raaste mein hi bikhar kar alag ho jaata hai.

```
  Hawa ki parat (lagbhag 10 km)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                        Sooraj \         Sooraj ║
Sunset: Sooraj kshitij ke paas   \              ║
lamba, tirchha raasta              \            ║  Dopahar: sidha, chhota raasta
                                     \          ║
                                       \        ║
                                         \      ║
                                           \    ║
                                             \  ║
────────────────────────────────────────────────T─────────────────────────────────────────
zameen
                                              T = tum (observer)
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Hawa ki parat (lagbhag 10 km) mein roshni ka raasta: dopahar ko seedha aur chhota (║), sunset par tirchha aur lamba (\). Ye sirf samajhne ka chitra hai: parat ko sapaat maana hai aur scale se bahar hai.

Is chitra ke hisaab se raasta = moti parat ÷ sin(Sooraj ki oonchai ka kon). Kshitij ke bahut paas ye sapaat-parat wala hisaab **thoda galat** ho jaata hai (zameen gol hai), isliye hum sirf moderate kon lenge.

### Example 4: Sooraj kshitij se 30° ooper

Dopahar ko Sooraj sir ke theek upar (90° par). Shaam ko Sooraj kshitij se 30° ooper. Parat 10 km moti maano. Dono ke raaste?

**Given:** moti parat = 10 km; kon 90° aur 30°; sin 90° = 1, sin 30° = 0.5.
**Find:** path length.
**Formula:** path = 10 km ÷ sin(kon).
**Substitution:** 10 km ÷ 1 = 10 km (dopahar). 10 km ÷ 0.5 = 20 km (shaam). Metre mein: 10 km = 10 × 1000 m = 10,000 m, aur 20 km = 20,000 m.
**Answer:** dopahar ko lagbhag **10 km**, 30° par lagbhag **20 km**: raasta **2 guna**.

**Jaanch:** 20 km ÷ 10 km = 2 ✅. Unit km ÷ km, bina unit ka 2 ✅. Kon chhota hone par raasta bada hua: sensible ✅

### Example 5: Raasta 4 guna, to neela kitna kata?

Maan lo shaam ko raasta dopahar ke raaste se 4 guna lamba hai. Simple model: bikhri hui roshni ∝ (raaste ki lambai) × (1/λ⁴). Dopahar ke **laal** ko 1.00 maan kar, shaam ke neele (400 nm) aur laal (700 nm) kitne bikharte hain? Aur ye 4 guna lamba raasta kis kon ka hoga?

**Given:** path factor = 4; neela : laal = 9.38 : 1; sin θ = 1 ÷ 4.
**Find:** shaam ka neela aur laal (dopahar ke laal ke mukable) aur kon θ.
**Formula:** bikhra = path factor × relative scattering; sin θ = 1 ÷ factor.
**Substitution:** laal: 4 × 1.00 = 4.00. Neela: 4 × 9.38 = 37.5. sin θ = 0.25, to θ = 14.5° (calculator se).
**Answer:** laal **4 guna**, neela **37.5 guna** (dopahar ke laal ke mukable); Sooraj lagbhag 14.5° ooper.

**Jaanch:** shaam ka neela ÷ shaam ka laal = 37.5 ÷ 4 = 9.38, wahi anupaat jo Example 1 mein tha ✅ (raaste ki lambai badalne se do rangon ka anupaat nahi badalta; dono zyada bikharte hain, par neela bahut zyada).

**Ek imaandar note:** asal mein roshni raaste mein ghatti hai: har chhote tukde mein ek fraction nikal jaata hai, aur neela lagbhag poora bikhar kar bahar ho jaata hai. Upar wala "gunaa" model sirf shuruaati halat ke liye theek hai. Par nateeja sahi dikhata hai: lamba raasta aane par **neela rang beam se lagbhag nikal jaata hai**, aur **laal-narangi** hamari aankh tak pahunchta hai. Isliye Sooraj laal, aur uske aaspaas ka aasmaan narangi-laal.

## Part E: Safed badal, laal signal, aur jahan hawa nahi

### Badal safed, kohra safed, doodh safed

Badal aur kohre ki boondein **bade kan** hain (wavelength se kai guna badi). Bade kan par sab rang lagbhag **barabar** bikharte hain, to bikhri hui roshni mein sab rang aur lagbhag barabar mix hokar **safed** (ya bahut ghane badal mein **grey**) dikhti hai. Doodh bhi isi wajah se safed hai: usmein kaafi **bade** kan hain. Isi liye Part A mein doodh ki sirf **do-teen boond** daalni thi: tab chhote kan wala neela-pan dikhta hai. Doodh zyada hua to bade kan sab rang barabar bikherte hain aur paani safed ho jaata hai.

### Laal danger signal, peeli fog-lamp

Laal rang sabse **kam** bikharta hai (Example 1 ka sirf lagbhag 11 percent). Isliye laal roshni dhool-dhuaan mein **door tak seedhi** pahunchti hai aur door se dikhti hai. Ek badi wajah yahi hai ki **danger signal laal** hota hai: wo door se nazar aa jaaye.

Fog-lamp aksar **peeli** hoti hain. Honest baat: kohre ke boondein bade kan hain, to wahan rang ka bahut farak nahi padta; par dhuaan, dhool aur haze mein chhote kan hote hain jo neele ko zyada bikherte hain, aur peeli roshni kam. Peeli roshni ankhon ko bhi chakachaundh ke mukable aaram deti hai, aisa aksar bataya jaata hai.

### Chaand par aasmaan kaala

Chaand par **hawa nahi hai**, to roshni bikharne wala koi kan nahi. Isliye chaand par dopahar mein bhi aasmaan **kaala** dikhta hai, aur taare saaf dikhte hain. Astronauts bhi Earth ke upar jaakar jab hawa ki parat se bahar nikalte hain, to unhein Sooraj ke saath kaala aasmaan dikhta hai. (Orbiting telescopes ke saath yahi baat Topic 1 mein bhi aayi thi.)

### Do chhoti baatein

- **Mars ka sunset neela:** Mars ki hawa mein bahut bareek dhool hai. Wahan dopahar ka aasmaan lagbhag peela-bhoora hota hai, par Sooraj ke ekdum aaspaas sunset par neela-sa dikhta hai.
- **Lunar eclipse mein chaand laal:** poore grahan ke samay Sooraj ki roshni **Earth ki hawa se guzarkar** Chaand tak pahunchti hai, neela rang raaste mein bikhar jaata hai, aur laal wala hissa mudkar Chaand par girta hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Aasmaan neela isliye hai kyunki wo samundar ka reflection hai."**

Demonstration: teen sawaal poocho. (i) Registan ya pahaad ke beech, samundar se saikdon km door, aasmaan kyun neela dikhta hai? (ii) Raat ko samundar wahin hota hai, par aasmaan kaala kyun? (iii) Badal wale din samundar aur aasmaan dono grey kyun? Agar reflection hota to registan mein neela nahi hota. **Sahi baat:** aasmaan ka neela hawa ke chhote kan ki scattering se aata hai. Ulta bhi sach hai: samundar ka paani **aasmaan ke neele ko apni sateh par reflect** karta hai, aur paani laal ko khud soakhta hai (aur thoda neela khud bikherta hai), isliye samundar neela-sa dikhta hai.

**Galti 2: "Laal rang sabse zyada bikharta hai."**

Demonstration: Figure 2.2 ke bar dekho: 400 nm par 9.38, 500 nm par 3.84, 600 nm par 1.85, 700 nm par 1.00. Laal ka bar **sabse chhota** hai. Agar laal sabse zyada bikharta, to sunset par lambe raaste mein laal hi hat jaata aur Sooraj neela-hara dikhta. Par hum Sooraj ko **laal** dekhte hain: matlab laal **kam** bikharta hai aur seedha hamari aankh tak pahunchta hai, jabki neela bikhar kar nikal jaata hai. **Sahi:** chhoti wavelength zyada bikharti hai, laal sabse kam.

## Quick Recap

- **Scattering** = roshni ka chhote kan se takrakar har dishaa mein phailna.
- **Chhote kan** (hawa ke molecules): scattering ∝ 1/λ⁴. Chhoti wavelength zyada bikharti hai. **Bade kan** (badal, kohra, doodh): sab rang lagbhag barabar bikharte hain, isliye safed.
- Relative scattering = (lambi λ ÷ chhoti λ)⁴, dono nm mein. **Neela 400 nm laal 700 nm se lagbhag 9 guna** (1.75⁴ = 9.38).
- Aasmaan violet nahi, neela: **teen kaaran**: Sooraj mein violet kam, upar ki hawa kuch absorb karti hai, aankh violet ke liye kam sensitive.
- Sunset par roshni ka raasta kai guna lamba, neela bikhar kar nikal jaata hai, laal-narangi pahunchta hai: **Sooraj laal**. Raasta = 10 km ÷ sin(kon) (sapaat-parat model, moderate kon).
- Laal sabse kam bikharta hai: isliye **danger signal laal**. Chaand par hawa nahi, isliye aasmaan **kaala**.
- Mars ka neela sunset (bareek dhool) aur lunar eclipse mein laal Chaand: sirf ek ek line ka mention.
- Doodh wale paani ki beam: side se neeli, aage se narangi (ghar par adult ke saath, torch aankh ki taraf kabhi nahi).

## Practice Questions

1. Green light of wavelength 550 nm and red light of wavelength 650 nm pass through very clean air. By what factor is green scattered more than red? Give the answer to two decimal places.
2. Violet light has a wavelength of 400 nm and blue light 450 nm. How many times more strongly are the violet rays scattered by air molecules?
3. An infrared beam of wavelength 1000 nm and red light of wavelength 700 nm travel through haze made of tiny particles. Which is scattered more, and by what factor? What fraction of the red light's scattering is that of the infrared beam? (Give the answer as a fraction and as a percent.)
4. A bluish-green light of 480 nm and an orange light of 620 nm are compared. Find the ratio of their scattering by air molecules. Express the weaker one as a percent of the stronger one.
5. The Sun is 45° above the horizon on one evening and 20° above on another. Take the air layer as 10 km thick (flat-layer model). Find the path length of sunlight through the air in each case. Which evening will the Sun look redder, and why?
6. At noon the path of sunlight through air is taken as 1 unit and at sunset as 6 units. Using a blue : red scattering ratio of about 9 for equal path, find (a) how many times more blue than red is scattered at sunset, (b) how many times more blue is scattered at sunset than at noon, (c) how many times more blue is scattered at sunset than red at noon.

14. A friend says the Sun is red at sunset "because the evening air is full of red gas". Write a short answer to correct her, naming the effect and the role of path length.

### Answers

1. **Given:** λ_green = 550 nm, λ_red = 650 nm. **Formula:** (λ_red ÷ λ_green)⁴.
   650 ÷ 550 = 1.1818. 1.1818² = 1.3967, 1.3967² = 1.9508.
   **Answer:** green lagbhag **1.95 guna** zyada bikharta hai.
   **Jaanch:** 1.95 ≥ 1 (chhoti λ zyada bikhri) ✅. Pair 400-700 (9.38) se kam, kyunki yahan dono rang paas-paas hain ✅

2. **Given:** 400 nm aur 450 nm. **Formula:** (450 ÷ 400)⁴.
   450 ÷ 400 = 1.125. 1.125² = 1.2656, 1.2656² = 1.6018.
   **Answer:** violet lagbhag **1.60 guna** zyada.
   **Jaanch:** metre mein 450 nm = 4.5 × 10⁻⁷ m aur 400 nm = 4.0 × 10⁻⁷ m: 4.5 ÷ 4.0 = 1.125, same ✅

3. **Given:** 1000 nm (infrared), 700 nm (laal). Chhoti λ zyada bikharti hai, to **laal** zyada bikharta hai.
   Anupaat = (1000 ÷ 700)⁴ = 1.4286⁴. 1.4286² = 2.0408, 2.0408² = 4.165.
   **Answer:** laal lagbhag **4.16 guna** zyada. Infrared ka scattering laal ka 1 ÷ 4.165 = 0.2401, yaani lagbhag **0.24 (24 percent)** hai.
   **Jaanch:** 4.165 × 0.2401 = 1.000 ✅

4. **Given:** 480 nm aur 620 nm. (620 ÷ 480)⁴ = 1.2917⁴. 1.2917² = 1.6684, 1.6684² = 2.7836.
   **Answer:** bluish-green lagbhag **2.78 guna** zyada bikharta hai. Orange ka scattering = 1 ÷ 2.7836 = 0.359, yaani bluish-green ka lagbhag **35.9 percent**.
   **Jaanch:** 2.7836 × 0.3592 = 1.000 ✅

5. **Given:** moti parat = 10 km; sin 45° = 0.7071, sin 20° = 0.3420. Path = 10 km ÷ sin(kon).
   45° par: 10 ÷ 0.7071 = **14.1 km**. 20° par: 10 ÷ 0.3420 = **29.2 km**.
   Jis shaam Sooraj 20° par hai, raasta lamba hai (29.2 km > 14.1 km), neela zyada hat jaata hai, to Sooraj **zyada laal** dikhega.
   **Jaanch:** 14.1 km × 0.7071 = 10.0 km ✅ aur 29.24 km × 0.3420 = 10.0 km ✅

6. **Given:** sunset path = 6, noon path = 1, blue : red = 9 (equal path).
   (a) Ek hi raaste par anupaat nahi badalta: **9 guna**.
   (b) Raasta 6 guna: 6 × 1 = **6 guna**.
   (c) 6 × 9 = **54 guna** (blue sunset ÷ red noon).
   **Jaanch:** 54 ÷ 6 = 9 ✅. Simple model hai; asal mein neela lagbhag poora nikal jaata hai.

7. Scattering ∝ 1/λ⁴. λ 3 guna: 3⁴ = 81, to scattering ghatkar **1/81** reh jaati hai. λ aadhi: (1/(1/2))⁴ = 2⁴ = 16, to scattering **16 guna** badh jaati hai.
   **Jaanch:** 81 × (1/81) = 1 ✅ aur (1/2)⁴ = 1/16 ✅

8. Badal ki boondein (lagbhag kuch µm) bade kan hain, wavelength (400 se 700 nm) se kai guna badi. Bade kan sab rang lagbhag barabar bikherte hain, to sab rang mil kar safed. Aaspaas ki hawa ke chhote kan neela bikherte hain, par wo badal ke safed ke saamne dikhta nahi.

9. Laal rang sabse kam bikharta hai (Example 1: neela ka lagbhag 11 percent), isliye dhool-kohre mein bhi door tak seedha pahunchta hai aur door se dikh jaata hai. Isliye danger signal laal.

10. Chaand par hawa nahi, to roshni ko bikherne wala koi kan nahi. Roshni seedhi aati hai aur aankh tak sirf Sooraj se aati hai, baaki aasmaan **kaala**. Sooraj ke aaspaas bhi neela ya laal ghera nahi hoga, sirf kaala aasmaan, taare tak dikh sakte hain.

11. (i) Sooraj mein violet kam hai. (ii) Upar ki hawa kuch violet (aur UV) absorb kar leti hai. (iii) Aankh violet ke liye kam sensitive hai.

12. Side se: chhote kan neela bikherte hain, to side se neela-sa. Aage se: neela side mein bikhar chuka, to bacha hua narangi-peela. Aur doodh badhane par kan jhund bante hain, wo bade kan jaise sab rang barabar bikherte hain, to paani **safed-doodhiya** dikhta hai aur beam ka rang dikhna band ho jaata hai.

    (b) **True** ✅. Hawa nahi, bikharne ka kaam nahi.
    (c) **False** ❌. Aasmaan ka neela hawa ki scattering se aata hai; samundar ka rang uske baad ki alag baat hai.
    (d) **True** ✅. Bade kan sab rang lagbhag barabar bikherte hain.
