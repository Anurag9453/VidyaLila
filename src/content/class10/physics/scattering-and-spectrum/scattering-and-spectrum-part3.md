# Topic 3: Electromagnetic Waves: Ek Parivaar, Alag Wavelength

Topic 2 mein humne dekha ki roshni ka rang aur uska bikharna **wavelength** par tika hai: chhoti wavelength wala neela zyada bikharta hai, lambi wavelength wala laal kam. ## Part A: EM wave kya hai

### Do field, ek saath

Charge ke aas-paas ek **electric field** hota hai. Charge ko hilao to uske aas-paas ka field bhi badalta hai. Aur hilta hua charge ek chhota current bhi hai, jo **magnetic field** banata hai. Bina kisi sahare ke ye dono field ek-doosre ko banate hue **aage badhte jaate hain**. Ye jodi hai **electromagnetic wave**.

Teen baatein yaad rakhne layak:

1. **Medium nahi chahiye.** Fields khud space mein chalte hain. Isi liye Sun ki roshni vacuum se Earth tak aati hai, jabki sound nahi aa sakti.
2. **Transverse hai.** Electric field upar-neeche, magnetic field usse samkon (page ke andar-bahar), aur wave aage ki disha mein: dono field wave ki disha ke samkon dolte hain (Class 9 ke rope-wave jaisi transverse wave).
3. **Hilta hua (accelerate hota hua) charge hi inhe banata hai.** Antenna mein electrons ko f frequency par aage-peeche hilao, to f frequency ki EM wave nikalti hai.

```
                       <--------- λ --------->
                     ******                ******
                   **      **            **      **
E (upar-neeche)   *          *          *          *
                              **      **            **      **
                                ******                ******

                     ******                ******
                   **      **            **      **
B (andar-bahar)   *          *          *          *
                              **      **            **      **
                                ******                ******

Dono ek saath chalte hain, aage ki disha mein  ===>
```

> **Figure 3.1** – EM wave ka seedha chitra. Upar wala graph electric field (upar-neeche) aur neeche wala magnetic field (page ke andar-bahar) dikhata hai: dono ek saath badhte-ghatte hain, aur ek hi disha mein chalte hain. Do lagataar crests ke beech ki doori wavelength λ hai. Chitra sirf shape dikhane ke liye hai, field ki asli size nahi.

### Wave ka hisaab wahi, bas speed c

Class 9 wala v = f × λ yahan bhi chalta hai. Fark sirf itna: **vacuum mein har EM wave ki speed ek hi hai, c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s** (lagbhag). To

> **c = f × λ**   aur   **f = c ÷ λ**,   **λ = c ÷ f**

Ab ek zaroori baat. Class 9 ke sound mein medium badalne par speed badalti thi, aur frequency (source ne tay ki) wahi rehti thi. EM waves mein bhi aisa hi hai: glass mein roshni ki speed kam ho jaati hai (2 × 10⁸ m/s, Class 9), aur isi se prism mein rang alag-alag mudte hain. Par **vacuum (aur lagbhag hawa) mein sabki speed c**. Isi "ek hi speed" ke kaaran ek wave ki frequency badhe to uski wavelength ghat jaati hai.

## Part B: Wavelength se frequency, frequency se wavelength

Is parivaar mein wavelength metre ke 10¹¹ vein hisse se bhi chhoti se lekar kilometre tak jaati hai. To units sambhalna zaroori hai. **Hamesha pehle metre aur hertz mein badlo.**

| Chhota/bada unit | Metre ya hertz mein |
|---|---|
| 1 nm (nanometre) | 10⁻⁹ m |
| 1 µm (micrometre) | 10⁻⁶ m |
| 1 mm | 10⁻³ m |
| 1 cm | 10⁻² m |
| 1 MHz | 10⁶ Hz |
| 1 GHz | 10⁹ Hz |

### Example 1: FM station, 100 MHz

Ek typical FM station ki frequency lagbhag 100 MHz hai. Uski wavelength kya hai?

**Given:** f = 100 MHz. c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s. **Find:** λ.
**Formula:** λ = c ÷ f
**Substitution:** f = 100 MHz = 100 × 10⁶ Hz = 10⁸ Hz. λ = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ÷ 10⁸ Hz = 3 m.
**Answer:** λ = **3 m** (lagbhag).
**Jaanch:** f × λ = 10⁸ Hz × 3 m = 3 × 10⁸ m/s = c ✅. Unit: m/s ÷ Hz = m ✅

### Example 2: Mobile signal, 900 MHz aur 1800 MHz

Mobile network ke do aam bands lagbhag 900 MHz aur 1800 MHz ke hain. Dono ki wavelength nikalo aur dekho kaun lambi hai.

**Given:** f₁ = 900 MHz, f₂ = 1800 MHz. **Find:** λ₁, λ₂.
**Formula:** λ = c ÷ f
**Substitution:** f₁ = 900 × 10⁶ Hz = 9 × 10⁸ Hz, to λ₁ = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 9 × 10⁸ = 0.333 m. f₂ = 1800 × 10⁶ Hz = 1.8 × 10⁹ Hz, to λ₂ = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 1.8 × 10⁹ = 0.1667 m.
**Answer:** λ₁ lagbhag **0.33 m** (33 cm), λ₂ lagbhag **0.17 m** (17 cm). 900 MHz wali lambi hai.
**Jaanch:** f dugni hui (900 se 1800), to λ aadhi (0.333 se 0.1667) ✅. 9 × 10⁸ × 0.333 = 3 × 10⁸ ✅

### Example 3: Hare rang ki roshni, 500 nm

Hare rang ki roshni ki wavelength lagbhag 500 nm hai. Uski frequency kya hai?

**Given:** λ = 500 nm. **Find:** f. **Formula:** f = c ÷ λ
**Substitution:** λ = 500 × 10⁻⁹ m = 5 × 10⁻⁷ m. f = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ÷ 5 × 10⁻⁷ m = 6 × 10¹⁴ Hz.
**Answer:** f = **6 × 10¹⁴ Hz**. Yaani ek second mein lagbhag 600 million million (6 × 10¹⁴) wave-chakkar.
**Jaanch:** f × λ = 6 × 10¹⁴ × 5 × 10⁻⁷ = 30 × 10⁷ = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ✅. Sound ke 500 Hz se ye lagbhag 10¹² guna zyada hai: roshni ki frequency itni badi isliye ki wavelength itni chhoti hai.

### Parivaar ki line: kaun kahan

Ab saari waves ko wavelength ke kram mein rakhte hain. Yaad karne ke liye ek khaane wali line: **R**ahul **M**ixes **I**dli, **V**ada, **U**ttapam, **X**-tra **G**aram, yaani Radio, Microwave, Infrared, Visible, Ultraviolet, X-ray, Gamma.

```
lambi wavelength  ===============================>  chhoti wavelength
kam frequency     ===============================>  zyada frequency

   Radio   Microwave Infrared   Visible     UV       X-ray     Gamma
+---------+---------+---------+---------+---------+---------+---------+
        30 cm     1 mm     700 nm    400 nm     10 nm    0.01 nm
        1 GHz    300 GHz  4.3×10¹⁴  7.5×10¹⁴   3×10¹⁶    3×10¹⁹
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Electromagnetic spectrum (UV = ultraviolet): lambi wavelength (kam frequency) ki taraf radio, chhoti wavelength (zyada frequency) ki taraf gamma. Neeche ki pehli line wavelength ki seema, doosri frequency (Hz) ki seema dikhati hai; sab lagbhag hain. Har dibba equal chauda sirf dikhane ke liye hai: asal mein wavelength ki scale bahut bade-bade chhalaang leti hai (radio kilometre mein, gamma metre ke 10¹¹ vein hisse se bhi chhoti).

| Region | Wavelength (lagbhag) | Frequency (lagbhag) | Ek jaana-pehchana naam |
|---|---|---|---|
| Radio waves | lagbhag 30 cm se lambi (AM lagbhag 200 se 600 m, FM lagbhag 3 m, TV lagbhag 1 se 5 m) | lagbhag 1 GHz se kam | FM 100 MHz |
| Microwaves | 1 mm se lagbhag 30 cm | 1 GHz se 300 GHz | Wi-Fi 2.4 aur 5 GHz, oven 2.45 GHz |
| Infrared | lagbhag 700 nm se 1 mm | lagbhag 3 × 10¹¹ se 4.3 × 10¹⁴ Hz | garam cheez ki garmi |
| Visible light | 400 nm (violet) se 700 nm (red) | lagbhag 4.3 × 10¹⁴ se 7.5 × 10¹⁴ Hz | VIBGYOR |
| Ultraviolet | lagbhag 10 nm se 400 nm | lagbhag 7.5 × 10¹⁴ se 3 × 10¹⁶ Hz | Sun ki tanning wali kirnein |
| X-rays | lagbhag 0.01 nm se 10 nm | lagbhag 3 × 10¹⁶ se 3 × 10¹⁹ Hz | hospital ki X-ray |
| Gamma rays | lagbhag 0.01 nm se chhoti | lagbhag 3 × 10¹⁹ Hz se zyada | nuclei se |

Table ki frequencies c ÷ λ se nikli hain, jaise 3 × 10⁸ ÷ (1 × 10⁻³) = 3 × 10¹¹ Hz = 300 GHz (microwave ka upar ka kinara). Do cheezein dhyan mein: pehli, **seemayein dhundhli hain**: kitabon mein radio aur microwave ki seema thodi alag di jaati hai, aur mobile 900 MHz radio ke kinare par, 1800 MHz microwave ke kinare par baithta hai. Doosri, **visible light ek bahut patli patti hai**: 400 se 700 nm, yaani poore parivaar ka sirf ek chhota sa hissa, jisse hamari aankh dekh sakti hai.

## Part C: Waves kahan se aati hain, aur kaun pakadta hai

**Sources (kaise banti hain):**

| Source | Kaise | Aam taur par kaun si wave |
|---|---|---|
| Antenna mein aage-peeche hilte electrons | Hilta charge wave banata hai | Radio, microwave |
| Garam cheez | Uske atoms garmi se hilte hain, to wave nikalti hai (Class 9 radiation) | Infrared, aur bahut garam ho to roshni |
| Atoms ke andar ke electrons | Atom ke electrons ki halchal | Visible, ultraviolet |
| Atom ke nucleus | Nucleus ka andar se badalna (Chapter 11 Radioactivity mein) | Gamma |

**Detectors (kaun pakadta hai):** radio aur microwave ko **antenna** (radio, mobile, dish). Infrared ko garmi se (thermal camera, hamari twacha ki garmi). Visible ko **hamari aankh**, camera ka sensor, photographic film. Ultraviolet aur X-ray ko film ya sensor jaisi chaadar (kuch cheezein UV mein chamakti bhi hain). Gamma ko **Geiger counter** (Chapter 11 Radioactivity mein). Alag-alag wave ke liye alag-alag detector chahiye, kyunki koi ek cheez poore parivaar ko nahi pakad sakti: hamari aankh sirf visible wali patti dekhti hai (Topic 5 mein visible light ka vistaar).

### Example 4: Insaani shareer ki infrared

Insaani shareer ki thermal radiation lagbhag 10 µm ki wavelength par sabse zyada hoti hai. Ye kaun si region hai, aur uski frequency kya hai?

**Given:** λ = 10 µm. **Find:** region aur f. **Formula:** f = c ÷ λ
**Substitution:** λ = 10 × 10⁻⁶ m = 10⁻⁵ m. f = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 10⁻⁵ = 3 × 10¹³ Hz.
**Answer:** f = **3 × 10¹³ Hz**. Infrared ki seema 700 nm se 1 mm hai, aur 10 µm = 10,000 nm isme aata hai, to ye **infrared** hai.
**Jaanch:** 3 × 10¹³ Hz table ki infrared seema (3 × 10¹¹ se 4.3 × 10¹⁴ Hz) ke andar hai ✅. Hamari aankh ise nahi dekhti kyunki 10 µm, 700 nm se kaafi lambi hai: isliye andhere mein bhi insaan "garam" hote hain par dikhte nahi.

**Ghar par adult ke saath (radio se wave sunna):** ek chhota battery wala AM radio lo, aur use kisi station se thoda hata kar (jahan sirf "ssh" aaye) rakho. Ab adult kamre ki light ka switch ya fan ka regulator on-off kare (**sirf switch, koi taar ya plug nahi chhona**). Radio mein aksar "tak-tak" ki aawaz sunai deti hai: switch ki chhoti chingari mein charge achanak hilte hain, aur wo ek EM wave bhejte hain jise radio ka antenna pakad leta hai. Bina taar ke sandesh aa gaya: **hilta charge = EM wave**.

## Part D: Raftaar c ko number mein

c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s bahut badi raftaar hai (ek second mein 3 lakh km). Par space aur bhi bada hai, isliye roshni ko bhi samay lagta hai.

### Example 5: Sun aur Moon se roshni kitni der mein aati hai

Earth se Sun ki doori lagbhag 1.5 × 10¹¹ m hai, aur Earth se Moon ki lagbhag 3.84 × 10⁸ m ([gravitation-and-space-missions-part4.md](../gravitation-and-space-missions/gravitation-and-space-missions-part4.md) ki table). Roshni dono se kitni der mein aati hai?

**Given:** d(Sun) = 1.5 × 10¹¹ m, d(Moon) = 3.84 × 10⁸ m, c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s. **Find:** t.
**Formula:** t = d ÷ c (seedhi speed = doori ÷ samay)
**Substitution:** t(Sun) = 1.5 × 10¹¹ ÷ 3 × 10⁸ = 500 s = 500 ÷ 60 = 8.33 minute. t(Moon) = 3.84 × 10⁸ ÷ 3 × 10⁸ = 1.28 s.
**Answer:** Sun ki roshni lagbhag **500 s (8.3 minute)** mein, Moon se lagbhag **1.3 s** mein.
**Jaanch:** c × t = 3 × 10⁸ × 500 = 1.5 × 10¹¹ m ✅; 3 × 10⁸ × 1.28 = 3.84 × 10⁸ m ✅. Matlab hum Sun ko jaisa wo **lagbhag 8 minute pehle** tha waisa dekhte hain, aur Moon ko lagbhag 1.3 second pehle waisa.

**Dhyan:** Sun ki taraf kabhi seedha mat dekho; ye sirf hisaab hai.

## Part E: EM wave aur sound wave: jodi nahi, alag khaandaan

| Baat | EM wave | Sound wave |
|---|---|---|
| Kya hilta hai | Electric aur magnetic field | Medium ke kan (hawa, paani) |
| Medium chahiye? | **Nahi** | **Haan** |
| Type | Transverse | Hawa mein longitudinal |
| Vacuum mein speed | c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s | Chal hi nahi sakti |
| Hawa mein speed | lagbhag 3 × 10⁸ m/s | lagbhag 340 m/s |
| Speed kise par tika | Medium par thodi (vacuum mein sabki ek) | Medium aur temperature par |

### Example 6: Wahi frequency, par kitna alag wavelength

Ek hi frequency 1700 Hz ki sound wave hawa mein (340 m/s) aur ek EM wave (vacuum mein) ki wavelength nikalo. EM wave kitni guna lambi hai?

**Given:** f = 1700 Hz, v(sound) = 340 m/s, c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s. **Find:** λ aur unka ratio. **Formula:** λ = speed ÷ f
**Substitution:** λ(sound) = 340 ÷ 1700 = 0.2 m. λ(EM) = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 1700 = 1.76 × 10⁵ m. Ratio = 1.76 × 10⁵ ÷ 0.2 = 8.8 × 10⁵.
**Answer:** sound 0.2 m (20 cm), EM lagbhag 1.76 × 10⁵ m (lagbhag 176 km). EM wave lagbhag **8.8 lakh guna** lambi.
**Jaanch:** ratio = c ÷ v = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 340 = 8.8 × 10⁵ ✅ (frequency same hone par ratio sirf speeds ka ratio hota hai).

## Wapas shaadi hall mein

Mr. Lonkar ne Dnyanada ko wahi parivaar wali baat tod kar samjhayi.

**Surname (jo sab mein same hai):** sab EM waves hain: electric aur magnetic field ki jodi, **bina medium ke chalti** hain, **transverse** hain, aur vacuum mein sabki raftaar **c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s**.

**Chehra (jo sab mein alag hai):** **wavelength (aur isliye frequency)**. Hall ke FM radio ki wave lagbhag 3 m (100 MHz). Mobile ka signal lagbhag 0.17 se 0.33 m (1800 aur 900 MHz, Example 2). Rasoi ke oven ki wave lagbhag 12 cm (2.45 GHz: 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 2.45 × 10⁹ = 0.122 m). Tube light ki roshni 400 se 700 nm. Sab kuch ek hi parivaar ki, bas chehre (wavelength) alag. Isi liye tumhara phone, TV ka antenna aur oven alag kaam karte hain, par kisi ko medium nahi chahiye.

"Aur aankh sirf roshni hi kyun dekhti hai?" Dnyanada ne poochha. "Kyunki aankh ka detector sirf 400 se 700 nm tak ki patti ke liye bana hai," Mr. Lonkar bole. "Baaki ke chehre dikhte nahi, par maujood hain." Microwave oven doodh kaise garam karta hai aur radio ya mobile ka antenna kitna bada hona chahiye, ye Topic 4 mein dekhenge.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Sound bhi ek electromagnetic wave hai, kyunki wo bhi wave hai."**

Demonstration (cricket ground): Mr. Lonkar ke ghar ke saamne ki ground mein koi 170 m door se batsman ko shot lagate dekho. Pehle **shot dikhta hai**, aawaz **lagbhag 0.5 second baad** sunai deti hai (170 m ÷ 340 m/s = 0.5 s). Roshni (EM wave) ko 170 m jaane mein 170 ÷ 3 × 10⁸ = 5.7 × 10⁻⁷ s (lagbhag 0.57 µs) lagta hai: sound se lagbhag **8.8 lakh guna tez**. Aur Example 6 mein dekha: wahi 1700 Hz par wavelength 0.2 m (sound) bhi aur 176 km (EM) bhi: dono alag cheezein. Sound ko **medium chahiye** (Class 9 ka bell jar), EM wave ko nahi. Aap Moon ya space mein sound nahi sun sakte (astronaut radio, yani EM wave, se baat karte hain).

**Sahi:** sound ek **yantrik (mechanical) wave** hai, EM wave nahi. "Wave hai" bas itna hi common hai.

**Galti 2: "X-rays aur radio waves vacuum mein alag-alag speed se chalti hain."**

Demonstration: teen waves ke f aur λ ko multiply karke dekho:

| Wave | f | λ | f × λ |
|---|---|---|---|
| FM radio | 10⁸ Hz | 3 m | 3 × 10⁸ m/s |
| Hari roshni | 6 × 10¹⁴ Hz | 5 × 10⁻⁷ m | 3 × 10⁸ m/s |
| Ek X-ray | 1.5 × 10¹⁸ Hz | 0.2 × 10⁻⁹ m = 2 × 10⁻¹⁰ m | 3 × 10⁸ m/s |

Teeno ka f × λ ek hi aaya: **c**. Frequency lagbhag 10¹⁰ guna badi, wavelength utni hi guna chhoti, par **raftaar wahi**. X-ray ki frequency zyada hai (zyada oscillation per second), raftaar nahi. Haan, glass ya paani jaise medium mein alag waves ki speed thodi alag ho sakti hai (isi se prism mein rang alag mudte hain), par **vacuum mein sabki same**.

## Quick Recap

- **EM wave** = electric aur magnetic field ka ek saath chalna. **Medium nahi chahiye.** Transverse.
- Vacuum mein har EM wave ki speed **c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s** (lagbhag). **c = f × λ**, f = c ÷ λ, λ = c ÷ f.
- Hamesha pehle metre aur hertz: 1 nm = 10⁻⁹ m, 1 µm = 10⁻⁶ m, 1 MHz = 10⁶ Hz, 1 GHz = 10⁹ Hz.
- **Spectrum** (lambi se chhoti wavelength): Radio, Microwave, Infrared, Visible (400 se 700 nm), Ultraviolet, X-ray, Gamma. Yaad: Rahul Mixes Idli, Vada, Uttapam, X-tra Garam.
- **Hilta hua charge** EM wave banata hai (antenna); garam cheez infrared deti hai; nucleus gamma deta hai. Alag wave, alag detector. Aankh sirf visible dekhti hai.
- Roshni Sun se lagbhag 8.3 minute (500 s) aur Moon se lagbhag 1.3 s mein aati hai.
- **Sound EM wave nahi hai**: medium chahiye, hawa mein lagbhag 340 m/s. EM waves ki vacuum mein speed sabki ek.

## Practice Questions

Take c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s. In air take the speed of sound as 340 m/s.

1. A radio station broadcasts at 91.1 MHz. Find the wavelength of its waves.
2. A mobile network uses a signal of frequency 2100 MHz. Find its wavelength in cm. In which region of the spectrum does the wave lie?
3. A microwave link between two towers uses a signal of frequency 7.5 GHz. Find its wavelength in cm.
4. A red laser pointer emits light of wavelength 650 nm. Find its frequency. (A laser must never be pointed at anyone's eyes.)
5. An ultraviolet lamp emits waves of wavelength 250 nm. Find the frequency. Can the human eye see this wave? Give a reason.
6. Light takes how long to travel from the Sun to Jupiter, whose average distance from the Sun is about 7.8 × 10¹¹ m? Give the answer in seconds and in minutes.
7. A communication satellite is about 36,000 km above a point on the equator. Find the time a radio signal takes to go from that point straight up to the satellite and back down to the same point.
8. A wave has a frequency of 6 × 10¹⁶ Hz. Find its wavelength in nm and name its region.
9. A wave has a frequency of 2 × 10¹⁰ Hz. Find its wavelength in cm and name its region.
10. An AM radio station broadcasts waves of wavelength 480 m. Find its frequency in MHz.
11. A tuning fork produces a sound of frequency 272 Hz. Find the wavelength in air. Then find the wavelength of an EM wave of the same frequency in vacuum. Which is longer, and by how many times?
12. A mobile tower is 4.5 km from a phone. How long does a signal take to travel from the tower to the phone? Give the answer in microseconds.
13. Say true or false and give a one-line reason.
    (a) Radio waves need air to travel.
    (b) In vacuum, microwaves and gamma rays travel at the same speed.
    (c) A wave of 100 nm is visible light.

14. A friend says, "Sound and light are both waves, so sound is also an electromagnetic wave." Give two differences that show she is wrong.

### Answers

1. **Given:** f = 91.1 MHz. **Find:** λ. **Formula:** λ = c ÷ f.
   **Substitution:** f = 91.1 × 10⁶ Hz = 9.11 × 10⁷ Hz. λ = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 9.11 × 10⁷ = 3.29 m.
   **Answer:** λ = **3.29 m** (lagbhag). **Jaanch:** 9.11 × 10⁷ × 3.29 = 3.0 × 10⁸ ✅; FM ke lagbhag 3 m ke paas hai ✅

2. **Given:** f = 2100 MHz. **Formula:** λ = c ÷ f.
   **Substitution:** f = 2100 × 10⁶ = 2.1 × 10⁹ Hz. λ = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 2.1 × 10⁹ = 0.1429 m = 0.1429 × 100 = 14.3 cm.
   **Answer:** λ = **14.3 cm**. 2.1 GHz 1 se 300 GHz ke beech hai, to **microwave** region. **Jaanch:** 2.1 × 10⁹ × 0.1429 = 3.0 × 10⁸ ✅

3. **Given:** f = 7.5 GHz. **Formula:** λ = c ÷ f.
   **Substitution:** f = 7.5 × 10⁹ Hz. λ = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 7.5 × 10⁹ = 0.04 m = 4 cm.
   **Answer:** λ = **4 cm**. **Jaanch:** 7.5 × 10⁹ × 0.04 = 3 × 10⁸ ✅ (aur Example 2 ke mobile se chhoti, kyunki f zyada)

4. **Given:** λ = 650 nm. **Formula:** f = c ÷ λ.
   **Substitution:** λ = 650 × 10⁻⁹ = 6.5 × 10⁻⁷ m. f = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 6.5 × 10⁻⁷ = 4.62 × 10¹⁴ Hz.
   **Answer:** f = **4.62 × 10¹⁴ Hz**. **Jaanch:** 4.62 × 10¹⁴ × 6.5 × 10⁻⁷ = 3.0 × 10⁸ ✅; 4.3 × 10¹⁴ (red ka kinara) ke paas, to red hi hai ✅

5. **Given:** λ = 250 nm. **Formula:** f = c ÷ λ.
   **Substitution:** λ = 250 × 10⁻⁹ = 2.5 × 10⁻⁷ m. f = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 2.5 × 10⁻⁷ = 1.2 × 10¹⁵ Hz.
   **Answer:** f = **1.2 × 10¹⁵ Hz**. Aankh sirf 400 se 700 nm dekhti hai, aur 250 nm uski seema se chhoti hai (ultraviolet), to **nahi dekh sakti**. **Jaanch:** 1.2 × 10¹⁵ × 2.5 × 10⁻⁷ = 3 × 10⁸ ✅; 1.2 × 10¹⁵ ≥ 7.5 × 10¹⁴ ✅

6. **Given:** d = 7.8 × 10¹¹ m. **Formula:** t = d ÷ c.
   **Substitution:** t = 7.8 × 10¹¹ ÷ 3 × 10⁸ = 2600 s. Minute mein: 2600 ÷ 60 = 43.3.
   **Answer:** **2600 s** = lagbhag **43.3 minute**. **Jaanch:** 3 × 10⁸ × 2600 = 7.8 × 10¹¹ ✅; Sun-Earth (500 s) se lagbhag 5.2 guna, jaise ki Jupiter lagbhag 5.2 guna door hai ✅

7. **Given:** h = 36,000 km. Aana-jaana = 2 × 36,000 = 72,000 km. **Formula:** t = d ÷ c.
   **Substitution:** d = 72,000 km = 72,000 × 1000 m = 7.2 × 10⁷ m. t = 7.2 × 10⁷ ÷ 3 × 10⁸ = 0.24 s.
   **Answer:** t = **0.24 s** (lagbhag). Isliye satellite ke raaste ke sandesh mein ek chhoti si deri aati hai. **Jaanch:** 3 × 10⁸ × 0.24 = 7.2 × 10⁷ m ✅

8. **Given:** f = 6 × 10¹⁶ Hz. **Formula:** λ = c ÷ f.
   **Substitution:** λ = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 6 × 10¹⁶ = 5 × 10⁻⁹ m. nm mein: 5 × 10⁻⁹ ÷ 10⁻⁹ = 5 nm.
   **Answer:** λ = **5 nm**. X-ray ki seema 0.01 se 10 nm hai, to **X-ray**. **Jaanch:** 6 × 10¹⁶ × 5 × 10⁻⁹ = 3 × 10⁸ ✅; f 3 × 10¹⁶ aur 3 × 10¹⁹ ke beech ✅

9. **Given:** f = 2 × 10¹⁰ Hz. **Formula:** λ = c ÷ f.
   **Substitution:** λ = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 2 × 10¹⁰ = 0.015 m = 1.5 cm.
   **Answer:** λ = **1.5 cm**. Ye 1 mm se 30 cm ke beech hai, to **microwave**. **Jaanch:** 2 × 10¹⁰ × 0.015 = 3 × 10⁸ ✅; 20 GHz, 1 se 300 GHz ke andar ✅

10. **Given:** λ = 480 m. **Formula:** f = c ÷ λ.
    **Substitution:** f = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 480 = 6.25 × 10⁵ Hz. MHz mein: 6.25 × 10⁵ ÷ 10⁶ = 0.625 MHz.
    **Answer:** f = **0.625 MHz**. **Jaanch:** 6.25 × 10⁵ × 480 = 3 × 10⁸ ✅; 480 m AM ki 200 se 600 m ki patti ke andar ✅

11. **Given:** f = 272 Hz, v = 340 m/s, c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s. **Formula:** λ = speed ÷ f.
    **Substitution:** λ(sound) = 340 ÷ 272 = 1.25 m. λ(EM) = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 272 = 1.10 × 10⁶ m. Ratio = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 340 = 8.8 × 10⁵.
    **Answer:** sound 1.25 m, EM lagbhag 1.10 × 10⁶ m (lagbhag 1100 km). **EM wave lambi**, lagbhag **8.8 lakh guna**. **Jaanch:** 1.10 × 10⁶ ÷ 1.25 = 8.8 × 10⁵ ✅

12. **Given:** d = 4.5 km = 4.5 × 1000 = 4500 m. **Formula:** t = d ÷ c.
    **Substitution:** t = 4500 ÷ 3 × 10⁸ = 1.5 × 10⁻⁵ s. Microsecond mein: 1.5 × 10⁻⁵ ÷ 10⁻⁶ = 15 µs.
    **Answer:** t = **15 µs**. **Jaanch:** 3 × 10⁸ × 1.5 × 10⁻⁵ = 4500 m ✅. Itna kam samay hai ki hamein koi deri mehsoos nahi hoti.

13. (a) **False** ❌. EM waves ko medium nahi chahiye, Sun ki roshni vacuum se aati hai.
    (b) **True** ✅. Vacuum mein har EM wave ki speed c hai.
    (c) **False** ❌. 100 nm, 400 nm se chhoti hai: ultraviolet, dikhti nahi.
    (d) **True** ✅. λ = c ÷ f, c fixed, to f dugni hone par λ aadhi.
    (e) **False** ❌. Sound EM wave nahi, mechanical wave hai (hawa mein longitudinal).

14. Do farak: (i) EM wave ko **medium nahi chahiye** (vacuum mein chalti hai), sound ko **chahiye** (vacuum mein nahi chalti). (ii) Speed: EM wave hawa mein lagbhag 3 × 10⁸ m/s, sound lagbhag 340 m/s: lagbhag 8.8 lakh guna kam. (Teesra: sound hawa mein longitudinal hai, EM wave transverse.) "Wave hona" sab waves mein common hai, isse sound EM wave nahi ban jaati.
