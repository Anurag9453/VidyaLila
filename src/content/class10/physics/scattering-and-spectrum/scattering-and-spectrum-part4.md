## Part A: Radio waves: antenna aur broadcast

### Radio waves kahan rehti hain

Radio waves EM parivaar ki **sabse lambi wavelength** wali sadasya hain: lagbhag 30 cm se lekar kilometre tak (microwave aur radio ki seema thodi dhundhli hai, kuch bands dono mein ginti hain). Hamare gharon ke aas-paas ki ye kuch jaani-pehchani bands hain (sab values lagbhag hain):

| Kaam | Wave ka kshetra | Frequency ya wavelength |
|---|---|---|
| AM radio | Radio | wavelength lagbhag 200 se 600 m |
| FM radio | Radio | lagbhag 100 MHz, wavelength lagbhag 3 m |
| TV (antenna wala) | Radio | wavelength lagbhag 1 se 5 m |
| Mobile phone | Radio ke kinare (900 MHz) aur microwave ke kinare (1800 MHz) | lagbhag 900 MHz (λ lagbhag 0.33 m) aur 1800 MHz (λ lagbhag 0.17 m) |

AM ki wavelength 200 m se 600 m ho to frequency f = c ÷ λ se lagbhag 1.5 MHz se 0.5 MHz nikalti hai (3 × 10⁸ ÷ 200 = 1.5 × 10⁶ Hz, 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 600 = 0.5 × 10⁶ Hz). TV ki 1 m se 5 m wavelength ka matlab lagbhag 300 MHz se 60 MHz hai. Radio waves ko hum na dekh sakte hain na mehsoos kar sakte hain, kyunki hamari aankh sirf 400 se 700 nm dekhti hai.

### Antenna kaise bhejta aur sunta hai

Radio station mein ek antenna ki dhaatu ki rod ke andar electrons **baar-baar tezi se aage-peeche** hilaye jaate hain (alternating current). Hilte electrons electric aur magnetic fields ko hilate hain, aur ye hilti field **EM wave ban kar** bahar nikal jaati hai (Topic 3: accelerate hote charge EM wave bhejte hain). Ek second mein 100 million baar hilne par 100 MHz ki FM wave banti hai.

Sunne wale antenna mein ulta hota hai. Aati hui wave ki electric field apni rod ke electrons ko dhakelti hai, aur rod mein **bahut chhoti si alternating current** ban jaati hai. Radio ka tuner usi ek frequency ki current ko chhaant kar baaki ko chhod deta hai. Ye "tuning" ek aur idea se judi hai, jise Chapter 6 (Vibrations and Resonance) mein samjhenge.

### Antenna ki lambai: λ ÷ 2

Antenna sabse achha tab kaam karta hai jab uski lambai wave ki **wavelength ki aadhi (λ ÷ 2)** ke aas-paas ho (ya chauthai, λ ÷ 4, jo car ki rod wale antenna mein hota hai). Wajah itni simple hai: rod ke electrons ko wave ke saath taal milakar hilna hai, aur ye taal sabse achhi tab milti hai jab rod wave ka theek aadha hissa ho.

```
Wave ki ek poori wavelength (100 MHz FM, λ = 3 m)
       ************
   ****            ****
***                    *****                    **
                            ****            ****
                                ************
+-----------------------++-----------------------+
  ½ λ = 1.5 m              ½ λ = 1.5 m
=========================  <- half-wave rod: lambai 1.5 m
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Half-wave antenna: 100 MHz ki wave ki ek wavelength 3 m hai; uska pehla aadha hissa (½ λ = 1.5 m) rod ki lambai ke barabar dikhaya gaya hai. Ye ek schematic hai (wave ko rod ke upar kheencha gaya hai), antenna ki asli taal nahi.

### Example 1: FM antenna ki lambai

Ek FM station 100 MHz par aati hai. Wavelength aur half-wave antenna ki lambai nikalo. Car ki rod (λ ÷ 4) kitni lambi hogi?

**Given:** f = 100 MHz = 100 × 10⁶ Hz = 10⁸ Hz, c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s.
**Find:** λ, λ ÷ 2, λ ÷ 4.
**Formula:** λ = c ÷ f.
**Substitution:** λ = (3 × 10⁸ m/s) ÷ (10⁸ Hz) = **3 m**. λ ÷ 2 = 3 ÷ 2 = **1.5 m**. λ ÷ 4 = 3 ÷ 4 = **0.75 m**.
**Answer:** half-wave antenna lagbhag 1.5 m, car wali rod lagbhag 0.75 m.
**Jaanch:** f × λ = 10⁸ Hz × 3 m = 3 × 10⁸ m/s = c ✅. Units: m/s ÷ Hz = m ✅. Aur 1.5 m "lagbhag ek metre ke aas-paas" wali baat se milta hai ✅

### Example 2: AM ke liye antenna itna bada kyun?

Ek AM station 1 MHz par aati hai. Uski wavelength aur half-wave antenna ki lambai nikalo, aur Example 1 ke FM antenna se compare karo.

**Given:** f = 1 MHz = 10⁶ Hz.
**Find:** λ, λ ÷ 2, aur FM antenna se anupaat.
**Formula:** λ = c ÷ f.
**Substitution:** λ = (3 × 10⁸ m/s) ÷ (10⁶ Hz) = **300 m** (jo AM ki 200 se 600 m wali range ke andar hai). λ ÷ 2 = **150 m**. Anupaat = 150 m ÷ 1.5 m = **100**.
**Answer:** AM ka half-wave antenna lagbhag 150 m, FM ke antenna se lagbhag 100 guna lamba.
**Jaanch:** 10⁶ Hz × 300 m = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ✅. Isi liye AM station ke paas **bahut oonchi mast** (tower) hota hai, jabki FM radio ki chhoti si rod kaafi hoti hai ✅

### Radio wave deewar se kyun nikal jaati hai

Lambi wavelength ki radio waves brick ya concrete ki deewar se aam taur par kaafi had tak guzar jaati hain, isliye ghar ke andar radio, TV aur mobile chalte hain. Par **dhaatu ki parat** (lift ka dibba, bandh almirah) waves ko rok ya laut deti hai, isliye lift mein mobile ka signal akshar gir jaata hai.

**Ghar par adult ke saath (battery wala chhota radio):** radio ko FM par ek station par lagao aur uski rod antenna ko poora chhota rakho, phir dheere-dheere khinch kar lamba karo. Signal ka saaf hona dekho. Kisi dhaatu ke dibbe (jaise steel ki tiffin) ke andar rakhne par aawaz kaise badalti hai, ye bhi dekho. Radio ko bijli ke plug se na chalao.

### Mobile network, TV dish aur satellite

Mobile phone apne paas ke **tower** se radio waves bhejta aur leta hai (900 aur 1800 MHz). Tower ka ilaaka kai "cells" mein bata hota hai, isliye kai phone ek saath baat kar paate hain. TV ki dish (Class 9 mein concave dish: [light-reflection-part6.md](../../../class9/physics/light-reflection/light-reflection-part6.md)) microwave-radio waves ko ek chhote receiver par ikattha karti hai. Ye waves aksar **space ke satellite** se aati hain, jo bhoo-sthir (geostationary) orbit mein lagbhag 36,000 km upar hote hain (Chapter 1, [gravitation-and-space-missions-part4.md](../gravitation-and-space-missions/gravitation-and-space-missions-part4.md)).

## Part B: Microwaves: oven, Wi-Fi aur radar

### Microwaves: chhoti wavelength, bada kaam

Microwaves ki wavelength lagbhag 1 mm se lagbhag 30 cm tak hai, yaani frequency lagbhag 1 se 300 GHz. (1 GHz = 10⁹ Hz.) Ye radio se chhoti hain, isliye antenna aur dish chhote ho jaate hain. Inke teen aam kaam: **microwave oven (lagbhag 2.45 GHz)**, **Wi-Fi aur Bluetooth (lagbhag 2.4 GHz aur 5 GHz)** aur **radar**.

### Example 3: Microwave oven ki wavelength

Oven ki frequency lagbhag 2.45 GHz hai. Wavelength kya hai?

**Given:** f = 2.45 GHz = 2.45 × 10⁹ Hz.
**Find:** λ.
**Formula:** λ = c ÷ f.
**Substitution:** λ = (3 × 10⁸ m/s) ÷ (2.45 × 10⁹ Hz) = 0.1224 m = **12.2 cm** (lagbhag).
**Answer:** oven ki wave ki wavelength lagbhag 12 cm hai, microwave ke 1 mm se 30 cm wale daayre ke andar.
**Jaanch:** f × λ = 2.45 × 10⁹ × 0.1224 = 2.999 × 10⁸ m/s ≈ c ✅. 12 cm 1 mm aur 30 cm ke beech mein hai ✅

### Oven khaana kaise garam karta hai

Paani ke har molecule ka ek sira thoda **positive** aur doosra thoda **negative** hota hai: ek chhote magnet ki tarah. Oven ki microwave ki electric field **ek second mein lagbhag 2.45 arab baar** aage-peeche (poora chakkar) dolti hai, to paani (aur kuch aur aise molecules, jaise chikni ya meethi cheezon ke) har baar ghoomne ki koshish karte hain, aur aas-paas ke molecules se takrate-ragadte hain. Isse unki halchal, yaani **garmi**, badh jaati hai. Khaane mein paani har jagah hai, isliye garmi sirf sateh par nahi, **khaane ke andar (kuch cm tak)** bhi shuru hoti hai; baaki garmi conduction se failti hai.

```
+------------------------------------------------------------+
: Source: 2.45 GHz ki waves                            ####  :
: ~~~> ~~~> ~~~> ~~~> ~~~>                             ####  :
:                                                      ####  :
:           \_______ katori mein paani _______/        ####  :
:               (+-) (-+) (+-) (-+) (+-)               ####  :
:            paani ke kan ghoom-te hain                ####  :
:                                                      ####  :
: Andar ki deewar dhaatu ki: waves laut-ti hain        ####  :
:                                                     jaali  :
+------------------------------------------------------------+
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Microwave oven ka schematic: baayein source se waves aati hain, katori ke paani ke molecules (+-) ko baar-baar ghumati hain. Andar ki dhaatu ki deewar waves ko laut deti hai. Daayein taraf door ki dhaatu ki jaali (####) hai. Ye figure sirf idea dikhati hai, oven ki asli banaavat nahi.

**Dhaatu ko oven se bahar kyun rakhein?** Dhaatu ke andar electrons aasani se hilte hain. Aati wave ki field unhein itna dhakelti hai ki **chingaariyan (sparks)** nikalne lagti hain, khaas kar crumpled foil ya nukile kinaaron par, aur oven kharab ho sakta hai ya aag lag sakti hai. Isliye steel ki katori, foil ya dhaatu ki sajavat wale bartan oven mein kabhi nahi daalte.

**Door ki jaali kyun surakshit hai?** Door mein dhaatu ki jaali ke chhed lagbhag kuch mm ke hote hain, jabki oven ki wave ki wavelength lagbhag 12 cm (Example 3) hai. Itni badi wave itne chhote chhedon se bahar nahi nikal paati, par visible roshni ki wavelength 400 se 700 nm bahut chhoti hai, wo chhedon se guzar jaati hai, isliye hum andar khaana dekh pate hain.

**Ghar par adult ke saath (sirf dekhne ke liye):** adult microwave-safe bartan mein thoda paani garam karte hain. Aap dekhte ho ki **paani bahut garam** ho gaya hai, par bartan ka upar ka kinara ya haath ka handle kam garam hai. Bartan ko kabhi sealed (band) mat rakhna, kabhi khaali oven mat chalana, aur paani ko nikalne se pehle thodi der rukna: kabhi-kabhi paani zyada garam ho kar achanak ubal padta hai. Garam bartan sirf adult nikaale.

### Wi-Fi, Bluetooth aur oven ka rishta

Wi-Fi aur Bluetooth (earphone, speaker) ki frequency lagbhag 2.4 GHz hai, oven (lagbhag 2.45 GHz) ke bahut paas. Bas fark power ka hai: oven mein waves ko **band dabbe ke andar** bahut zyada power se bheja jaata hai (saikdon se hazaaron guna), router ki waves bahut halki hoti hain. Isliye technician ne kaha tha, "Router ko oven se door rakhna": oven ki bachi-khuchi waves kabhi-kabhi Wi-Fi ke signal ko dhundhla kar sakti hain. Aur 5 GHz ka band aam taur par tez internet deta hai, par deewar ke aar-paar kam door tak jaata hai.

### Radar: echo se doori

Radar apne antenna se microwave ya radio ki ek bahut chhoti **pulse** bhejta hai. Pulse jahaaz jaisi cheez se takrakar **echo** ban kar wapas aati hai, aur radar wahi antenna se use pakadta hai. Pulse c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s se jaati hai, aur Class 9 ke SONAR wale rule jaisa hi: **d = c × t ÷ 2**, kyunki t mein jaana aur aana dono hai.

```
Radar                                                             Jahaaz
[R] pulse -------------------------------------------------------->  [A]
    <-------------------------------------------------------- echo

 +-------------------------------- d --------------------------------+

Pulse jaata bhi hai aur aata bhi hai: safar 2d, speed c, to t = 2d ÷ c

```

> **Figure 4.3** – Radar: pulse radar se jahaaz tak jaati hai aur echo wapas aati hai. Doori d ek taraf ki hai, pulse ka safar 2d hai.

### Example 4: Jahaaz kitni door?

Ek airport radar ki pulse ka echo bhejne ke 120 µs baad wapas milta hai. Jahaaz kitni door hai?

**Given:** t = 120 µs = 120 × 10⁻⁶ s = 1.2 × 10⁻⁴ s, c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s.
**Find:** d.
**Formula:** d = c × t ÷ 2.
**Substitution:** d = (3 × 10⁸ m/s × 1.2 × 10⁻⁴ s) ÷ 2 = (3.6 × 10⁴ m) ÷ 2 = 1.8 × 10⁴ m = **18 km**.
**Answer:** jahaaz lagbhag 18 km door hai.
**Jaanch:** back-substitution: t = 2d ÷ c = (2 × 18000 m) ÷ (3 × 10⁸ m/s) = 1.2 × 10⁻⁴ s = 120 µs ✅. Units: m/s × s = m ✅

Mausam ke radar bhi isi tarah kaam karte hain: unki microwaves baarish ki boondon se laut-ti hain, aur radar dekhta hai ki baadal kahan hain.

## Part C: Infrared: garmi ki roshni

### Har garam cheez infrared deti hai

Class 9 mein dekha tha ki garam cheez infrared radiation deti hai. Ab number lagate hain: infrared ki wavelength lagbhag 700 nm se 1 mm hai, yaani visible lal roshni ke **thik baad**. Hamara shareer bhi infrared deta hai, aur uski shikhar (peak) wavelength lagbhag 10 µm hai. Cheez jitni garam, utni zyada infrared; ek chamakdaar bulb ya garam toast sekne wali coil to **laal chamakne** lagti hai, kyunki uski infrared ke saath thodi visible roshni bhi aa jaati hai. Sooraj ki roshni ka lagbhag 45 percent hissa infrared hai, isliye dhoop mein garmi lagti hai.

**Ghar par adult ke saath:** apni hatheli ko apne gaal se 2 se 3 cm door rakho (chhuo mat). Aap hatheli ki garmi mehsoos karenge: ye shareer ki infrared hai jo seedhi gaal tak pahunch rahi hai, bina chhue.

### Example 5: TV remote ki frequency

TV remote ka infrared LED lagbhag 940 nm ki wavelength deta hai. Uski frequency nikalo, aur batao ki ye visible lal roshni se kam hai ya zyada.

**Given:** λ = 940 nm = 940 × 10⁻⁹ m = 9.4 × 10⁻⁷ m.
**Find:** f, aur lal (700 nm) se tulna.
**Formula:** f = c ÷ λ.
**Substitution:** f = (3 × 10⁸ m/s) ÷ (9.4 × 10⁻⁷ m) = **3.19 × 10¹⁴ Hz**. Lal roshni ke liye: f = (3 × 10⁸) ÷ (7 × 10⁻⁷) = 4.29 × 10¹⁴ Hz.
**Answer:** remote ki infrared lagbhag 3.2 × 10¹⁴ Hz hai, jo lal roshni ki 4.3 × 10¹⁴ Hz se **kam** hai.
**Jaanch:** f × λ = 3.19 × 10¹⁴ × 9.4 × 10⁻⁷ = 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s ✅. Lambi wavelength, kam frequency: 940 nm > 700 nm, to f kam ✅

### Example 6: Shareer ki infrared

Shareer ki infrared ki shikhar wavelength lagbhag 10 µm hai. Frequency aur remote ki frequency se anupaat nikalo.

**Given:** λ = 10 µm = 10 × 10⁻⁶ m = 10⁻⁵ m.
**Find:** f, aur (remote ki f) ÷ (shareer ki f).
**Formula:** f = c ÷ λ.
**Substitution:** f = (3 × 10⁸) ÷ (10⁻⁵) = **3 × 10¹³ Hz**. Anupaat = (3.19 × 10¹⁴) ÷ (3 × 10¹³) = **10.6**.
**Answer:** shareer ki infrared ki frequency lagbhag 3 × 10¹³ Hz hai; remote ki frequency isse lagbhag 10.6 guna zyada hai.
**Jaanch:** wavelength ka anupaat 10000 nm ÷ 940 nm = 10.6, wahi anupaat ✅ (f aur λ ulte badalte hain).

### Remote control: roshni ka jhilmil

Remote ke andar infrared LED har button dabane par ek **khaas pattern mein tez jhilmil (on-off pulses)** bhejta hai; TV ka chhota receiver us pattern ko code ki tarah padhta hai. LED ki 940 nm wali roshni hamari aankh ke liye (jo 700 nm tak hi dekhti hai) dikhti nahi. Phone camera ka sensor 700 nm se thoda aage tak sensitive hota hai, isliye wo jhilmil dikhta hai (kuch camera mein ye kam dikhta hai; tab front camera try karo).

**Ghar par adult ke saath:** mobile ka camera remote ke aage ke bindu ki taraf rakho, remote ke button dabao: screen par halki baingani-safed jhilmil dikhti hai. Agar remote kharab ho, to nahi dikhegi. Remote ko apni ya kisi ki aankh ke bilkul paas mat laana.

**Line of sight kyun?** 940 nm ki infrared lagbhag roshni jaisi hai: deewar ya kisi insaan se nahi guzarti, isliye remote ko TV ki taraf point karna padta hai (ya deewar se laut kar kabhi-kabhi pahunch jaati hai). Wi-Fi ki kai cm lambi waves brick ki deewar se kaafi had tak aar-paar chali jaati hain, isliye usme point nahi karna padta.

### Thermal camera, night-vision aur fever scanner

Thermal camera cheez ki apni infrared (lagbhag 10 µm ke aas-paas) ko rangon mein badalta hai: garam jagah chamakdaar, thandi jagah gehri. Isliye wo **bina roshni ke andhere mein** bhi insaan aur jaanwar dikha deta hai. Aam kaanch aksar is infrared ko rok deta hai, isliye thermal camera kaanch ke aar-paar aksar nahi dekh paata. Kuch "night-vision" CCTV alag hote hain: wo apni infrared LED (jaise 940 nm wale) se jagah ko roshan karte hain aur infrared-sensitive camera se dekhte hain.

Maathe par lagaya jaane wala **fever scanner** bhi isi tarah kaam karta hai: wo ek chhote hisse ki skin ki infrared napta hai. Par ye sirf **skin ki upari garmi** deta hai, bahar ki hawa, paseena aur doori se badal sakti hai. Isliye bukhar ki pakki jaanch doctor ya thermometer se karte hain.

### Infrared heater aur toaster

Heater aur toaster ki laal tapti coil seedhi infrared bhejti hai, jo paas ki cheez ya roti ki sateh sokh leti hai: hawa ko garam kiye bina! Isliye heater ke saamne baithne par seedhi garmi milti hai, aur peeche baithne par kam.

### Greenhouse effect: infrared ka khel

Class 9 ([heat-and-temperature-part6.md](../../../class9/physics/heat-and-temperature/heat-and-temperature-part6.md) mein greenhouse ka naam, aur [atmospheric-pollution-part3.md](../../../class9/chemistry/atmospheric-pollution/atmospheric-pollution-part3.md) mein global warming) ek baat adhuri chhod gayi: **garmi ki wo kaunsi radiation hai jo gas rok leti hai?** Jawab: infrared. Sooraj ki roshni mein zyadatar visible hota hai, aur wo CO₂ jaisi gaison ki parat se guzar kar zameen ko garam karti hai. Garam zameen (shareer ki tarah) khud **infrared** bhejti hai, lagbhag 10 µm ke aas-paas. Hawa mein CO₂ aur paani ki bhaap jaisi gaison ki parat **us infrared ko sokh leti hai** aur use phir har disha mein bhejti hai: kuch hissa wapas neeche. Isse dharti garam rehti hai. Ye parat na ho to dharti bahut thandi hoti; dikkat tab hoti hai jab gaison ki matra bahut badh jaati hai.

```
Dhoop (visible)
   ↓     ↓     ↓
~~ gas ki parat (CO₂ jaisi) ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
   ↓     ↓     ↓                 ↑     ↑     ↑           ↓
   ↓     ↓     ↓                 ↑     ↑     ↑           ↓
==================================================================
Dharti ki surface

dhoop andar aati            surface se infrared     parat se kuch
hai, surface                (lagbhag 10 µm) upar    wapas neeche
garam hoti hai              jaati hai

```

> **Figure 4.4** – Greenhouse effect (schematic): dhoop (visible) gas ki parat se guzar kar surface ko garam karti hai; surface se infrared upar jaati hai, parat use sokhti hai aur kuch wapas neeche bhejti hai. Teer ki ginti sirf pehchaan ke liye hai, asli anupaat nahi.

### Optical fibre mein infrared

Class 9 mein hamne dekha tha ki optical fibre mein light total internal reflection se chalti hai ([light-refraction-part4.md](../../../class9/physics/light-refraction/light-refraction-part4.md)). Internet ke fibre mein jo light chalti hai, wo aksar infrared hoti hai (lagbhag 1.5 µm ke aas-paas ki ek wavelength), kyunki fibre ka kaanch is infrared ko bahut kam sokhta hai.

---

## Part D: Safety aur myths: mobile aur Wi-Fi ki sachchai

Mobile, Wi-Fi aur tower ki radiation ke baare mein bahut daraane wali baatein ghoomti rehti hain. Ek **sachchi aur seedhi** tasveer:

1. Ye waves EM parivaar ke **lambi wavelength** wale hisse (radio aur microwave) mein hain. Inme X-ray jaisi taaqat nahi hoti ki wo atoms se electrons ko nikaal kar cells ko tod saken. Is baat ko "ionising aur non-ionising" naam se Topic 5 mein theek se dekhenge.
2. Inka **power bahut kam** hota hai: ek phone ya router ki power aam taur par ek watt ke aas-paas ya usse kam (lagbhag), jo oven jaise band dabbe ke saikdon guna kam hai.
3. Duniya bhar ki vaigyanik sansthayein **safe limit ke guidelines** banati hain, aur phone aur tower unke andar rehne chahiye. Zyadatar adhyayan mein guidelines ke andar koi pakka nuksaan nahi mila, par lambi avadhi par research chalta rehta hai.
4. Phir bhi **samajhdaari wali savdhaani** theek hai: kisi call ko chhota rakho, lambi baat mein speaker ya earphone use karo, aur signal kamzor ho to phone zyada power lagata hai, to wahan lambi call se bachho. Sote waqt phone **takiye ke neeche** nahi: wahan garam ho sakta hai aur neend bhi kharab hoti hai. Ye sab **ghar par adult se baat** karke ghar ka niyam banao.

Is paragraph ka matlab ye nahi ki "kuch fark nahi padta," ye hai ki **darne ki jagah samajh se kaam lo.**

## Wapas ghar par

**Wi-Fi ka jawab:** router ki waves lagbhag 2.4 GHz aur 5 GHz hain, yaani microwaves (kai cm lambi). Ye radio ki tarah deewar ke aar-paar jaa sakti hain, isliye band darwaze ke peeche bhi signal aaya. Router ko almirah (dhaatu) mein nahi, khuli shelf par aur oven se door rakhna isi liye kaha tha.

**Remote ka jawab:** remote ki 940 nm ki infrared hamari aankh ke liye dikhti nahi (aankh sirf 400 se 700 nm dekhti hai), par camera ka sensor usse pakad leta hai. Aur kyunki wo roshni ke jaisi hai, deewar ya log use rok dete hain: isliye seedha point karna padta hai.

**Oven ka jawab:** oven aur Wi-Fi bhai-behen hain (dono lagbhag 2.4 GHz), bas power aur band dabbe ka fark hai. Technician ne isliye router ko oven se door rakhne ko kaha.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Microwave oven pehle bartan ko garam karta hai, phir khaana garam hota hai"**

Demonstration (ghar par adult ke saath, sirf dekhne ke liye): adult ek microwave-safe katori mein dal ya paani garam karte hain. Oven band hone ke baad dekho: **dal ya paani bahut garam hai, par katori ka upar ka kinara aur handle kam garam** hain. Agar oven pehle katori ko garam karta, to katori sabse garam hoti aur khaana baad mein garam hota. Aisa nahi hota, kyunki microwave-safe glass ya ceramic waves ko bahut kam sokhta hai. Paani wala khaana waves ko sokhta hai, aur khaane ki garmi **conduction se** katori tak aati hai (Class 9), isliye katori garam bhi hoti hai, par khaane ke baad. Sahi: oven **khaane ke andar ke paani** ko seedha garam karta hai. (Khaali oven kabhi mat chalana: waves sokhne ko kuch na ho to oven kharab ho sakta hai.)

**Galti 2: "Mobile aur Wi-Fi bhi X-ray jaisi nuksaan wali radiation hain"**

Demonstration: Wi-Fi ki frequency 2.4 GHz = 2.4 × 10⁹ Hz. X-ray ki range ka sabse lamba end lagbhag 10 nm hai: uski frequency f = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ (10 × 10⁻⁹) = 3 × 10¹⁶ Hz. Anupaat: 3 × 10¹⁶ ÷ 2.4 × 10⁹ = **1.25 × 10⁷**, yaani lagbhag 1.25 crore guna. Wi-Fi ki frequency sabse lambi wavelength wale X-ray se bhi lagbhag 1.25 crore guna kam hai. X-ray ki wave itni tez hoti hai ki wo atoms se electrons ko nikaal sakti hai; Wi-Fi mein ye taaqat nahi. Ye "sab kuch ek hi radiation" ka bhram todta hai. Sahi: EM parivaar ki har sadasya alag hai; "radiation" shabd sunkar darna nahi, **kaun si wavelength aur kitna power** dekhna hai. (Par microwave aur infrared garmi paida kar sakte hain: oven aur heater!)

## Quick Recap

- **Radio waves** (sabse lambi wavelength): AM lagbhag 200 se 600 m, FM lagbhag 100 MHz (λ lagbhag 3 m), TV lagbhag 1 se 5 m, mobile lagbhag 900 aur 1800 MHz.
- **Antenna** bhejta hai (electrons hilte hain, wave banti hai) aur sunta hai (wave electrons ko hilati hai). Best lambai lagbhag **λ ÷ 2** (ya λ ÷ 4). Formula: λ = c ÷ f.
- **Microwaves** (lagbhag 1 mm se 30 cm): oven lagbhag 2.45 GHz (λ lagbhag 12 cm), Wi-Fi aur Bluetooth lagbhag 2.4 GHz aur 5 GHz, radar, satellite links.
- Oven khaane ke **andar ke paani** ko garam karta hai. Dhaatu andar nahi, door ki jaali wave ko rokti hai.
- **Radar:** d = c × t ÷ 2.
- **Infrared** (lagbhag 700 nm se 1 mm): har garam cheez deti hai (shareer lagbhag 10 µm). Remote (lagbhag 940 nm, camera dekh leta hai), thermal camera, heater, optical fibre. Greenhouse effect mein CO₂ jaisi gaison ki parat dharti ki infrared sokhti hai.
- Mobile aur Wi-Fi: kam power aur lambi wavelength, X-ray jaise nahi, par samajhdaari wali savdhaani theek hai.

## Practice Questions

Use c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s. Convert every unit to metres and hertz before you calculate.

1. An FM station broadcasts at 98.3 MHz. Find the wavelength and the length of a half-wave antenna for it.
2. A second FM station broadcasts at 104.6 MHz. Find its half-wave antenna length. By how much (in cm) is it shorter than the antenna for the 98.3 MHz station?
3. A mobile network uses 1800 MHz. Find the wavelength and the length of a quarter-wave antenna. Why can a phone hide such an antenna inside its body, while an FM radio needs a long rod?
4. A radar sends a pulse towards a target 15 km away. How long after sending does the echo return? Give the answer in microseconds.
5. A radar echo returns 80 µs after the pulse is sent. How far is the target?
6. A thermal camera forms an image using infrared of wavelength 9.5 µm. Find the frequency. In which part of the spectrum does it lie? How many times is it longer than red light of 700 nm?
7. A router works on two bands, 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz. Find the wavelength of each and the ratio of the longer wavelength to the shorter one. If one band has to pass through two walls, which would you expect to do better, using the fact that longer waves usually pass through walls more easily?
8. An infrared LED of a CCTV camera has a wavelength of 850 nm. Find its frequency. Can the human eye see it? Give a reason using the range of visible light.
9. A telescopic radio rod is extended to 1.45 m and works best as a half-wave antenna. For which frequency (in MHz) is it best? Does this lie in the FM band, whose wavelength is about 3 m?
10. An AM station broadcasts at 0.75 MHz. Find the wavelength and the half-wave antenna length. Why do AM stations use tall towers and not a short rod?
11. Say true or false and give a one-line reason.
    (a) A microwave oven first heats the bowl and then the food.
    (b) A phone camera can sometimes show the flashing of a TV remote's infrared LED.
    (c) Infrared has a shorter wavelength than microwaves.
    (d) Radar uses sound waves.
    (e) Wi-Fi waves can knock electrons out of atoms, just like X-rays.
12. Pranit says, "The TV remote must be pointed at the TV, but my Wi-Fi works in the next room, so the remote must be using a weaker kind of wave." Use the facts that the remote uses infrared of about 940 nm (which does not pass through walls) and Wi-Fi uses waves of several centimetres (which partly pass through walls) to correct him.
13. A friend says, "I sleep with my phone under my pillow because radiation from Wi-Fi and phones is harmless." Give an honest, balanced reply of three or four lines.

### Answers

1. **Given:** f = 98.3 MHz = 98.3 × 10⁶ Hz.
   **Formula:** λ = c ÷ f, antenna = λ ÷ 2.
   **Substitution:** λ = (3 × 10⁸) ÷ (98.3 × 10⁶) = **3.05 m**. Antenna = 3.05 ÷ 2 = **1.53 m** (lagbhag).
   Jaanch: 98.3 × 10⁶ × 3.05 = 3.0 × 10⁸ ✅

2. f = 104.6 × 10⁶ Hz. λ = (3 × 10⁸) ÷ (104.6 × 10⁶) = 2.868 m. Antenna = 2.868 ÷ 2 = **1.434 m**.
   Farak = 1.526 − 1.434 = 0.092 m = **9.2 cm** chhota.
   Jaanch: zyada frequency, chhoti wavelength, to chhota antenna ✅ (104.6 > 98.3)

3. f = 1800 MHz = 1.8 × 10⁹ Hz. λ = (3 × 10⁸) ÷ (1.8 × 10⁹) = 0.1667 m = **16.7 cm**. Quarter-wave = 0.1667 ÷ 4 = 0.0417 m = **4.17 cm**.
   Itna chhota antenna phone ke andar aasaani se chhup jaata hai, jabki FM ke 3 m ki wavelength ke liye rod 1.5 m ki chahiye. Chhoti wavelength, chhota antenna.
   Jaanch: 1.8 × 10⁹ × 0.1667 = 3.0 × 10⁸ ✅

4. d = 15 km = 15000 m. t = 2d ÷ c = (2 × 15000) ÷ (3 × 10⁸) = 10⁻⁴ s = **100 µs**.
   Jaanch: d = c t ÷ 2 = (3 × 10⁸ × 10⁻⁴) ÷ 2 = 15000 m = 15 km ✅

5. t = 80 µs = 80 × 10⁻⁶ s. d = c t ÷ 2 = (3 × 10⁸ × 80 × 10⁻⁶) ÷ 2 = 24000 ÷ 2 = 12000 m = **12 km**.
   Jaanch: t = 2 × 12000 ÷ (3 × 10⁸) = 8 × 10⁻⁵ s = 80 µs ✅

6. λ = 9.5 µm = 9.5 × 10⁻⁶ m. f = (3 × 10⁸) ÷ (9.5 × 10⁻⁶) = **3.16 × 10¹³ Hz**. Ye **infrared** hai (700 nm se 1 mm ke beech). Anupaat = 9.5 µm ÷ 0.7 µm = **13.6**.
   Jaanch: 3.16 × 10¹³ × 9.5 × 10⁻⁶ = 3.0 × 10⁸ ✅. 9.5 µm = 9500 nm, 9500 ÷ 700 = 13.6 ✅

7. 2.4 GHz = 2.4 × 10⁹ Hz: λ = (3 × 10⁸) ÷ (2.4 × 10⁹) = **0.125 m = 12.5 cm**. 5 GHz = 5 × 10⁹ Hz: λ = **0.06 m = 6 cm**. Anupaat = 0.125 ÷ 0.06 = **2.08**.
   Lambi wavelength wala 2.4 GHz band deewaron se behtar guzarega.
   Jaanch: 5 ÷ 2.4 = 2.08 (frequency ka ulta anupaat) ✅

8. λ = 850 nm = 850 × 10⁻⁹ m = 8.5 × 10⁻⁷ m. f = (3 × 10⁸) ÷ (8.5 × 10⁻⁷) = **3.53 × 10¹⁴ Hz**. Aankh ise **nahi dekh sakti**: 850 nm visible range (400 se 700 nm) se bahar, lal ke aage hai.
   Jaanch: 3.53 × 10¹⁴ × 8.5 × 10⁻⁷ = 3.0 × 10⁸ ✅

9. Half-wave rod hai, to λ = 2 × 1.45 = 2.9 m. f = c ÷ λ = (3 × 10⁸) ÷ 2.9 = 1.034 × 10⁸ Hz = **103 MHz** (lagbhag).
   FM ki λ lagbhag 3 m, to ye **FM band** ke andar hai.
   Jaanch: 103.4 × 10⁶ × 2.9 = 3.0 × 10⁸ ✅

10. f = 0.75 MHz = 7.5 × 10⁵ Hz. λ = (3 × 10⁸) ÷ (7.5 × 10⁵) = **400 m** (AM ki 200 se 600 m range ke andar). Half-wave = **200 m**.
    Itni lambi rod ke liye hi oonchi mast chahiye; chhoti rod waves se taal nahi mila paati.
    Jaanch: 7.5 × 10⁵ × 400 = 3 × 10⁸ ✅

11. (a) **False** ❌. Bartan waves ko bahut kam sokhta hai; khaane ka paani sokhta hai, aur bartan khaane ki garmi se garam hota hai.
    (b) **True** ✅. Camera ka sensor 940 nm ki infrared ko pakad leta hai.
    (c) **True** ✅. Infrared (700 nm se 1 mm) microwave (1 mm se 30 cm) se chhoti wavelength ki hai.
    (d) **False** ❌. Radar radio ya microwaves use karta hai.
    (e) **False** ❌. Wi-Fi ki waves mein itni taaqat nahi.

12. Remote "kamzor wave" nahi hai, sirf bahut alag wavelength ki hai. Remote ki 940 nm ki infrared roshni ki tarah deewar ya insaan se nahi guzarti, isliye point karna padta hai. Wi-Fi ki kai cm ki waves deewar se kaafi had tak guzar jaati hain. Fark wave ke prakar ka hai, "kamzori" ka nahi. (Dono EM waves hain aur c par chalti hain.)

13. Wi-Fi aur phone ki waves non-ionising hoti hain aur power bahut kam, to X-ray jaisa nuksaan nahi. Par research chalta rehta hai aur phone garam bhi ho sakta hai. Isliye sensible: **phone takiye ke neeche nahi**, lambi call mein earphone ya speaker, aur kamzor signal mein phone ko kam use karo. "Harmless" ya "bahut khatarnak": dono se bacho. Aur **ghar par adult se baat karke** niyam banao.
