# Topic 5: Visible, UV, X-Ray Aur Gamma: Tez Aur Khatarnak Wale

Topic 4 mein humne parivaar ke **lambi wavelength** wale sadasya dekhe: radio, microwave aur infrared. Ab hum doosre sire par chalte hain: pehle wo patli patti jo hamari aankh dekh sakti hai (**visible light**), aur phir uske aage ki **chhoti wavelength, zyada frequency** wali teen waves: **ultraviolet (UV), X-ray aur gamma ray**. Inhein "tez" kehte hain kyunki inki frequency bahut zyada hai, aur kuch "khatarnak" kyunki ye jeevit cells ko nuksaan pahuncha sakti hain. Dhyan rahe: har wave khatarnak nahi, aur har khatarnak wave bekaar nahi: sab **kitni, kitni der, aur kaun si** par tika hai.

## Dentist ka clinic: apron aur band darwaza

Mr. Sohoni ki bhatiji **Juilee** ke daant mein kai din se dard tha. Ek shaam wo Chacha ke saath dentist ke clinic gayi. Dentist ne daant dekha aur boli, "Andar ki jad dekhni hai, ek chhota X-ray lenge." Ek nurse ne Juilee ke seene par ek bhari, dhatu jaisi **apron** daal di, aur munh mein ek chhoti si plate rakh di.

Dentist ne machine Juilee ke gaal ke paas ghumayi, aur phir **kamre se bahar** nikal gayi. Darwaze ke peeche se usne button dabaya: ek halki si "beep", ek-do second. Juilee ko kuch mehsoos nahi hua: na garmi, na roshni, na koi chhua. Dentist wapas aayi aur computer par Juilee ke daant ki **safed-kaali tasveer** dikhayi: daant ki jad aur uske neeche ek kaala dhabba.

"Chacha," Juilee boli, "mujhe kuch dikha hi nahi, par tasveer ban gayi! Apron kyun pehnayi, aur doctor bahar kyun chali gayi? Agar ye itni khatarnak hai to mujhe kyun diya?" Mr. Sohoni ne kaha, "Teeno sawaal ek hi jagah se jude hain: kuch waves hamari aankh ko dikhti nahi, par shareer ke andar tak jaa sakti hain. Chalo Part A se E tak dekhte hain, aur 'Wapas dentist ke yahan' mein teeno jawab milenge."

## Part A: Visible light: patli patti, saat rang

Topic 3 mein dekha tha ki visible light **400 nm (violet) se 700 nm (red)** tak hai: poore parivaar ki sirf ek patli patti. Is patti ko aankh ne apna "kaam ka hissa" bana liya. Sooraj ki roshni ka lagbhag aadha hissa isi patti mein aata hai (baaki lagbhag 45 percent infrared aur lagbhag 5 percent UV).

Saat rangon ki wavelength aur frequency (f = c ÷ λ, c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s):

| Rang | Wavelength (nm, lagbhag) | Frequency (× 10¹⁴ Hz, lagbhag) |
|---|---|---|
| Violet | 400 se 440 | 7.5 se 6.8 |
| Indigo | 440 se 460 | 6.8 se 6.5 |
| Blue | 460 se 490 | 6.5 se 6.1 |
| Green | 490 se 570 | 6.1 se 5.3 |
| Yellow | 570 se 590 | 5.3 se 5.1 |
| Orange | 590 se 620 | 5.1 se 4.8 |
| Red | 620 se 700 | 4.8 se 4.3 |

Ye seemayein **lagbhag** hain: alag kitabein thodi alag seema deti hain, aur indigo aksar dhundhla rang maana jaata hai. Ek baat saaf dikhti hai: **violet ki wavelength sabse chhoti aur frequency sabse zyada, red ki wavelength sabse lambi aur frequency sabse kam.** Isliye red ki frequency sabse kam hai.

### Example 1: Peeli roshni ki frequency

Ek peele rang ki roshni ki wavelength lagbhag 580 nm hai. Uski frequency kya hogi, aur kya wo table ke peele rang ki seema mein aati hai?

**Given:** λ = 580 nm = 580 × 10⁻⁹ m = 5.8 × 10⁻⁷ m, c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s.
**Find:** f.
**Formula:** f = c ÷ λ.
**Substitution:** f = (3 × 10⁸) ÷ (5.8 × 10⁻⁷) = 5.17 × 10¹⁴ Hz.
**Answer:** f = **5.17 × 10¹⁴ Hz** (lagbhag).
**Jaanch:** 5.17 × 10¹⁴ × 5.8 × 10⁻⁷ = 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s ✅. Table ki peeli seema 5.3 se 5.1 (× 10¹⁴ Hz) hai, 5.17 isi ke andar ✅. Unit: Hz = 1 ÷ s, (m/s) ÷ m = 1/s ✅.

Ek baat yaad rakhna: jab sunlight kisi cheez par girti hai to wo kuch rang reflect karti hai aur kuch soakh leti hai; isi se hari patti hari, aur safed kameez safed dikhti hai.

## Part B: Ultraviolet (UV): violet ke us paar

**Ultra-violet** matlab "violet ke paar": wo waves jinki wavelength violet (400 nm) se bhi chhoti hai. Aankh inhein nahi dekhti, kyunki aankh ka dekhne wala hissa 400 nm par khatam ho jaata hai. UV ko teen hisson mein baanta jaata hai (Figure 5.1 mein dikha raha hai ki kaun si zameen tak pahunchti hai):

| Hissa | Wavelength (lagbhag) | Zameen tak kitni pahunchti hai |
|---|---|---|
| UV-A | 315 se 400 nm | Zyadatar pahunchti hai |
| UV-B | 280 se 315 nm | Ozone ne bahut hissa roka, kuch pahunchti hai |
| UV-C | 100 se 280 nm | Ozone aur hawa ne lagbhag poori roki, zameen tak lagbhag nahi |

### Ozone layer: dhoop ki chhatri

```
  oonchai    UV-C    UV-B    UV-A
  (km)        v       v       v
              v       v       v
  30  ----------------------------------------
      ########x#######:#######|###############    ozone layer: lagbhag 20 se 30 km
  20  ----------------------------------------
                      :       |                   UV-C: lagbhag poori roki gayi (x)
                      :       |                   UV-B: bahut hissa roka, kuch bachi (:)
                      :       |                   UV-A: zyadatar guzarti hai (|)
  10  - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -     weather wali hawa
                      :       |
                      :       |
   0  ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~    zameen

```

> **Figure 5.1** – Sooraj ki UV oopar se neeche aati hai. Ozone layer (lagbhag 20 se 30 km, Class 9 mein 15 se 35 km ki patti padhi thi, sabse ghana hissa beech mein) UV-C ko lagbhag poori rok deti hai (x), UV-B ka bahut hissa rok deti hai (:), aur UV-A zyadatar nikal jaati hai (|). Zameen ke paas ka weather wala hissa lagbhag 10 km tak hai. Sketch hai, scale par nahi.

Is figure se ek seedha nateeja: ozone layer na hoti to UV-C aur bahut zyada UV-B zameen tak aati. **CFC** gaisein ozone ko todti hain, isliye 1987 ka **Montreal Protocol** CFC ko dheere-dheere band karne ke liye hua, aur ab layer sudhar rahi hai (Class 9 mein poori kahani). Aur Topic 2 mein jo bola tha ki upar ki hawa chhoti wavelength ka kuch hissa soakh leti hai: UV wale hisse ke liye ye wahi parat hai.

### UV kahan se aati hai aur kya kaam aati hai

- **Sources:** Sooraj (lagbhag 5 percent output), **UV lamps**, aur **welding arc** (bijli ki chingaari, jise kabhi nange aankh se nahi dekhna: welder mask lagata hai).
- **Sterilisation:** UV-C lamps hawa, paani aur surfaces ke kitanu maarti hain (ek band dabbe ke andar, jahan koi insaan na ho: UV-C aankh aur twacha ke liye bhi nuksaandayi hai).
- **Fluorescence:** kuch cheezein UV soakh kar chamakti dikhne wali roshni deti hain, isliye **nakli note** pakadne wale UV torch ke neeche asli note ke kuch nishaan chamakte hain (kaun se nishaan, note ke hisaab se alag).
- **Vitamin D:** hamari twacha dhoop ki UV-B se vitamin D banati hai: kuch minute ki dhoop kaafi hoti hai (kitni, ye doctor se poochho).

### UV ka nuksaan

Zyada UV se **sunburn**, jaldi budhapa dikhne wali twacha, aur lambi avadhi mein **skin cancer** ka khatra badhta hai. Aankh mein zyada UV se **cataract** (lens ka dhundhla hona, [human-eye-part3.md](../human-eye/human-eye-part3.md) mein dekha tha) ka khatra badhta hai. Isliye: dopahar ki tez dhoop mein topi, poore kapde, **UV protection wala chashma** (sirf gehra rang kaafi nahi, [human-eye-part6.md](../human-eye/human-eye-part6.md)), aur **sunscreen**.

**Sunscreen aur SPF:** label par likha SPF jitna bada, UV-B se utna zyada bachav. Kitaabi thumb-rule: SPF × (jitni der mein bina sunscreen skin jalti hai) = lagbhag bachav ka samay.

### Example 2: UV-B ki frequency

Ek UV wave ki wavelength lagbhag 300 nm hai. Uski frequency nikaalo aur batao wo UV ka kaun sa hissa hai.

**Given:** λ = 300 nm = 300 × 10⁻⁹ m = 3 × 10⁻⁷ m.
**Find:** f aur hissa.
**Formula:** f = c ÷ λ.
**Substitution:** f = (3 × 10⁸) ÷ (3 × 10⁻⁷) = 1.0 × 10¹⁵ Hz.
**Answer:** f = **1.0 × 10¹⁵ Hz**. 300 nm, 280 se 315 nm ke beech hai, to **UV-B**.
**Jaanch:** 1.0 × 10¹⁵ × 3 × 10⁻⁷ = 3 × 10⁸ ✅. Frequency 7.5 × 10¹⁴ (violet) se zyada hai, jaise UV ki honi chahiye ✅.

### Example 3: Sunscreen ka kitaabi hisaab

Ek bachche ki twacha bina sunscreen ke tez dhoop mein lagbhag 10 minute mein jalne lagti hai. Wo SPF 30 sunscreen theek se lagata hai. Kitaabi thumb-rule se kitni der tak bachav milega?

**Given:** jalne ka samay = 10 min, SPF = 30.
**Find:** bachav ka samay.
**Formula:** samay = SPF × 10 min.
**Substitution:** 30 × 10 min = 300 min. Ghante mein: 300 ÷ 60 = 5 h.
**Answer:** lagbhag **300 minute = 5 ghante** (kitaabi hisaab).
**Jaanch:** 300 ÷ 30 = 10 min wapas milta hai ✅. **Dhyan:** asli zindagi mein sunscreen pasine, paani aur kam lagane se jaldi utarta hai, isliye label par aksar "har do ghante mein dobara lagao" likha hota hai; 5 ghante bina dobara lagaye bharosa mat karo.

**Ghar par adult ke saath (sunscreen label padho):** ghar mein kisi sunscreen ki bottle ka label dhyan se padho: SPF kitna hai, "broad spectrum" likha hai ya nahi (matlab UV-A aur UV-B dono se bachav), kitni der baad dobara lagana hai, aur expiry kab hai. Teen baatein copy mein likho. Sunscreen khaane ya aankh mein jaane ki cheez nahi.

**Ghar par adult ke saath (sirf dekhne ke liye, UV torch aur note):** agar ghar mein "money detector" jaisi chhoti UV torch ho, to adult usse **kuch second ke liye** ek note ki taraf kare (note ko mez par rakhkar, kamre ko thoda andhera karke). Kuch nishaan chamakte dikhte hain. **UV torch kabhi kisi ki aankh ya twacha ki taraf nahi**, band karke hi rakho, aur bachche akele na chalayein. Nakli note pehchaan ka ye ek tareeka hai, par asli jaanch bank hi karta hai. Aise hi **UV beads** dhoop mein rang badalte hain; unhein bag ke andar rakho to nahi badalte: isse dikhta hai ki UV ko kapda rok leta hai.

## Part C: X-ray: seene ke andar ki jhalak

**X-ray** ki wavelength lagbhag 0.01 nm se 10 nm hai: visible light se hazaaron guna chhoti. Isse wo atoms ke beech se nikal jaati hai. Ye banti kaise hai? Ek **X-ray tube** ke andar bahut tez gati se chalte **electrons** ek dhatu ke **target** se takrate hain. Takkar mein unki gati achanak ghatti hai aur energy X-ray ban kar nikalti hai (lagbhag 1895 mein pehli baar dekhi gayi thi).

```
X-ray tube                    muh ka hissa              sensor


              ##            +---------------------+       +--------+
              ##----------------------------------------> # kaala  #
              ##             gaal                         #        #
e- e- e- ->   ##--------------------#####                 # safed  #
              ##                    #####                 #        #
              ##----------------------------------------> # kaala  #
              ##            +---------------------+       +--------+
             metal target           daant


```

> **Figure 5.2** – Dental X-ray ka schematic. Tez electrons dhatu ke target (##) par girte hain; wahan se X-ray ki kirnein muh ke hisse ki taraf jaati hain. Gaal ke komal hisse se kirnein aar-paar nikal kar sensor par kaala nishaan banati hain; daant (hard, #####) beech ki kiran ko rok leta hai, to sensor par wahan **safed chhaya** bani. (Sketch, scale par nahi: asli tube bahut chhota hota hai.)

**Aar-paar kyun nikalti hai?** Komal hisse (maans, gaal) mein halke atoms hote hain jo X-ray ko kam rokte hain; haddi aur daant mein calcium jaise zyada rokne wale atoms hote hain. Isliye haddi **safed chhaya** dikhti hai. Dhatu (lead) usse bhi zyada rokti hai: isiliye lead apron.

**Kaam:** toote haddi aur daant ki tasveer; **airport baggage scanner** (bag ke andar ka saaman); **CT scan** (alag-alag kone se kai X-ray tasveerein, jinhein computer jodkar andar ka cross-section banata hai: sirf naam yaad rakho).

**Khatra:** X-ray **ionising** hai: wo atom se electron nikaal sakti hai, jisse cell ke andar ke DNA jaise hisse toot sakte hain. Ek baar ki chhoti si dose bahut kam hoti hai (aksar bataya jaata hai ki ek dental X-ray ki dose ek din ya usse kam ki kudrati background radiation ke aas-paas hoti hai: lagbhag), par **dose jodti jaati hai**. Isliye X-ray tabhi jab doctor zaroori samjhe, aur **garbhvati mahila** ka X-ray sirf tabhi jab bahut zaroori ho, kyunki bachche ke cells tezi se badhte hain aur zyada sensitive hote hain (doctor aksar pehle ultrasound jaise sound-based tareeke chunte hain: [sound-part6.md](../../../class9/physics/sound/sound-part6.md), sound koi radiation nahi).

### Example 4: Dental X-ray ki frequency

Ek dental X-ray ki wavelength lagbhag 0.05 nm maan lo (ye maan hedged hai, asli machine par thoda alag ho sakta hai). Frequency nikaalo, aur ye bhi batao ki wo example 1 ki peeli roshni se kitni guna zyada hai.

**Given:** λ = 0.05 nm = 0.05 × 10⁻⁹ m = 5 × 10⁻¹¹ m. Peeli: f_y = 5.17 × 10¹⁴ Hz (Example 1).
**Find:** f, aur f ÷ f_y.
**Formula:** f = c ÷ λ.
**Substitution:** f = (3 × 10⁸) ÷ (5 × 10⁻¹¹) = 6 × 10¹⁸ Hz. Ratio = (6 × 10¹⁸) ÷ (5.17 × 10¹⁴) = 1.16 × 10⁴.
**Answer:** f = **6 × 10¹⁸ Hz**; X-ray ki frequency peeli roshni se lagbhag **11,600 guna** zyada.
**Jaanch:** 6 × 10¹⁸ × 5 × 10⁻¹¹ = 3 × 10⁸ ✅. Wavelength se ratio: 580 nm ÷ 0.05 nm = 11,600 ✅. f, 3 × 10¹⁶ se 3 × 10¹⁹ Hz (X-ray ki seema) ke andar ✅.

## Part D: Gamma ray: nucleus se, sabse bhedne wali

**Gamma ray** lagbhag 0.01 nm se bhi chhoti wavelength ki EM wave hai. Isse **nucleus** (atom ke beech ka kendra) banata hai: kuch nuclei khud ba khud badalte hain aur gamma ray chhodte hain (isko Chapter 11 Radioactivity mein theek se dekhenge; abhi sirf itna: "nucleus se"). X-ray aur gamma ki seema thodi dhundhli hai: dono ki wavelength ek-doosre ke upar thodi chadh sakti hai; mukhya farak ye hai ki X-ray electrons se banti hai aur gamma nucleus se.

```

UV      ~~~~~~> ###   kapde ya twacha ki upari parat

X-ray   ~~~~~~> ########   patli lead ki chaadar

Gamma   ~~~~~~> ####################   moti lead ya moti concrete

jitna mota barrier chahiye, wave utni zyada bhedne wali
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Kaun si wave ko rokne ke liye kitna mota barrier chahiye (schematic): UV ko kapda ya twacha ki upari parat bhi bahut rok deti hai, X-ray ke liye patli lead chaadar, aur gamma ke liye moti lead ya moti concrete. Chauda sirf tulna ke liye hai, scale par nahi aur wave ki asli energy par bhi tika hai.

**Gamma ke kaam:**
- **Cancer radiotherapy:** gamma ya bahut tez X-ray ki kiranein cancer ke cells par seedhi daalte hain, aur cells ko maar dete hain (doctor ki dekh-rekh mein).
- **Medical equipment ki sterilisation:** sui-syringe aur patties ko packet mein band karke gamma se kitanu-rahit karte hain.
- **Welds ki jaanch:** bade pul aur pipeline ke jodon mein andar ki daraar gamma ya X-ray ki tasveer se dikhti hai.

**Khatra:** ionising bhi aur sabse zyada bhedne wali. Isliye sab kaam bhari lead aur concrete ke peeche, door se aur kam samay mein hote hain.

### Example 5: Gamma ki frequency

Ek gamma ray ki wavelength lagbhag 0.005 nm hai. Frequency nikaalo aur Example 4 ki X-ray se tulna karo.

**Given:** λ = 0.005 nm = 5 × 10⁻³ × 10⁻⁹ m = 5 × 10⁻¹² m. X-ray (Example 4): 6 × 10¹⁸ Hz, 5 × 10⁻¹¹ m.
**Find:** f aur ratio.
**Formula:** f = c ÷ λ.
**Substitution:** f = (3 × 10⁸) ÷ (5 × 10⁻¹²) = 6 × 10¹⁹ Hz. Ratio = (6 × 10¹⁹) ÷ (6 × 10¹⁸) = 10.
**Answer:** f = **6 × 10¹⁹ Hz**, X-ray se **10 guna** zyada.
**Jaanch:** 6 × 10¹⁹ × 5 × 10⁻¹² = 3 × 10⁸ ✅. Wavelength 0.01 nm se chhoti hai, aur f 3 × 10¹⁹ Hz se bada hai: gamma ki seema ✅. Ratio wavelength se bhi: 0.05 ÷ 0.005 = 10 ✅.

## Part E: Ionising ya non-ionising: seema kahan?

Poore parivaar mein ek seema hai: **kya wave atom se electron nikaal sakti hai?** Jo nikaal sakti hai wo **ionising**, jo nahi wo **non-ionising**. Ek baat bina formula ke yaad rakho: **wavelength jitni chhoti (frequency jitni badi), wave ke ek "packet" mein utni zyada energy.** Isliye X-ray aur gamma mein itni taaqat aati hai ki wo electron nikaal dein, jabki radio aur microwave mein nahi. (Ye energy kaise naapte hain, wo aage ki class mein.)

| Wave | Ionising? | Asar | Kaise bachein |
|---|---|---|---|
| Radio, microwave | Non-ionising | Microwave oven jaisi jagah par garmi (Topic 4) | Dhatu ki jaali, guidelines |
| Infrared | Non-ionising | Garmi, jalan | Garam cheez se doori |
| Visible | Non-ionising | Bahut tez roshni (jaise Sooraj ya laser) aankh ko nuksaan | Sooraj ya laser ko kabhi aankh se nahi dekhna |
| UV | Aam taur par non-ionising  | Sunburn, cataract, skin cancer, kyunki cell ke hisse badal jaate hain | Topi, chashma, sunscreen |
| X-ray | **Ionising** | Cells ke DNA ko nuksaan | Lead apron, kam dose, door |
| Gamma | **Ionising** | Cells ke DNA ko nuksaan, sabse bhedne wali | Bhari lead aur concrete, door |

Is table se ek seedha nateeja: **non-ionising ka matlab "bina asar" nahi**: oven ki garmi aur UV ka sunburn dono non-ionising wave ke hain. Aur **ionising ka matlab "hamesha khatarnak" nahi**: dose aur samay hi sab kuch hain, jaise Part B aur C mein dekha.

## Wapas dentist ke yahan

**Apron ka jawab:** X-ray komal hisse se guzar jaati hai, par lead se nahi. Apron seene aur pet jaise hisson ko un kirnon se bachati hai jo tasveer ke liye zaroori nahi, kyunki ionising waves ki dose jodti jaati hai.

**Dentist bahar kyun gayi?** Juilee ke liye ek X-ray ek baar ki chhoti dose hai. Par dentist din mein kai marizon ke X-ray leti hai; agar har baar wahi kamre mein rahe to uski dose roz jodti jaayegi. Isliye wo door ya deewar ke peeche se button dabati hai.

**Phir mujhe kyun diya?** Kyunki daant ki jad ka dard dekhne ka seedha tareeka yahi hai, aur ek chhoti si dose se jo faayda milta hai (jad ki sahi jaankari, sahi ilaaj), wo risk se kaafi zyada hai. Doctor tabhi leta hai jab zaroori ho. Juilee ne apron ko haath se thapthapaya aur boli, "Chacha, ab samajh aaya: dar ki jagah hisaab."

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Har radiation khatarnak hoti hai."**

Demonstration (ek din ki chhoti kahani): subah Juilee ne 15 minute subah ki dhoop mein pani paudhon ko diya (UV kam, vitamin D ke liye achha), phir **dopahar 1 baje** bina topi 3 ghante cricket khela (UV tez, sunburn ka khatra), phir shaam ko dentist ka X-ray (lead apron ke saath, ek-do second). **Teeno radiation hain, par asar bahut alag.** Aur raat ko bulb ki visible light mein padhna bhi radiation hai: koi nuksaan nahi. Matlab "radiation" shabd se dar nahi, **kaun si wave, kitni power, kitni der, kya bachav** poochho. Sahi: ionising (X-ray aur gamma) mein savdhaani zyada, par chhoti dose aur lead se kaam chal jaata hai.

**Galti 2: "UV matlab baingani (violet) roshni; dikhti hai, isliye torch ki taraf dekh sakte hain."**

Demonstration: aankh ka kaam 400 nm tak hi hai (Part A), aur UV-A bhi 400 nm se chhoti hai (315 se 400 nm). Ek UV torch ko adult ke saath safe jagah (kamre ke andar, **aankh ki taraf nahi**) safed deewar ki taraf karo: deewar par halka baingani sa daag dikh sakta hai. Wo daag UV nahi hai: torch ki LED thodi violet visible roshni bhi deti hai (isliye hum use baingani dekhte hain), aur asli UV to dikhti hi nahi. Note par jo **chamak** dikhi wo fluorescence thi: note ne UV soakha aur visible roshni di. **UV khud kabhi nahi dikhti**, aur isliye wo aankh ko chupchaap nuksaan pahuncha sakti hai (koi dard ya chhuan nahi, nuksaan baad mein dikhta hai). Sahi: UV violet se aage ki, aankh ke liye **na dikhne wali** roshni hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Visible** lagbhag 400 nm (violet) se 700 nm (red): VIBGYOR. Violet ki frequency lagbhag 7.5 × 10¹⁴ Hz sabse zyada, red ki lagbhag 4.3 × 10¹⁴ Hz sabse kam.
- **UV** lagbhag 10 nm se 400 nm: UV-A (315 se 400), UV-B (280 se 315), UV-C (100 se 280 nm). Ozone layer (lagbhag 20 se 30 km) UV-C lagbhag poori aur UV-B ka bahut hissa rokti hai.
- UV ke kaam: sterilisation, nakli note ki jaanch (fluorescence), vitamin D. Nuksaan: sunburn, cataract, skin cancer. Bachav: topi, UV chashma, sunscreen (SPF).
- **X-ray** lagbhag 0.01 nm se 10 nm: tez electrons dhatu ke target par takrate hain. Komal hisse se guzarti hai, haddi se kam. Dental, fracture, baggage scanner, CT. Ionising: lead apron, kam dose.
- **Gamma** lagbhag 0.01 nm se chhoti: nucleus se (Chapter 11). Radiotherapy, sterilisation, welds ki jaanch. Ionising aur sabse bhedne wali.
- **Ionising** (X-ray, gamma): atom se electron nikaal sakti hai. Radio, microwave, infrared, visible non-ionising hain, par garmi ya tez roshni ka asar phir bhi ho sakta hai.
- Wavelength jitni chhoti, wave utni zyada energy wali (aage ki class mein naap). f = c ÷ λ hamesha.
- Radiation ka asar: **kaun si wave, kitni power, kitni der, kya bachav**.

## Practice Questions

Use c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s. Convert every unit to metres and hertz first. Where a question gives made-up numbers, treat them as given data.

1. A germicidal UV lamp emits UV-C of wavelength 254 nm. Find its frequency. Check that the wavelength lies in the UV-C range.
2. An X-ray has a wavelength of 0.1 nm. Find its frequency. Is it in the X-ray range of lagbhag 0.01 nm to 10 nm?
3. A gamma ray has a wavelength of 0.002 nm. Find its frequency. How many times is it higher than the frequency of the X-ray in Question 2?
4. Violet light has a wavelength of 400 nm and red light 700 nm. Find the frequency of each, and say how many times the violet frequency is larger than the red one. By how much (in Hz) does the frequency of violet exceed that of red?
5. A lamp that checks banknotes emits UV of wavelength 365 nm. Find its frequency. Which UV part (A, B or C) is it? Can the human eye see this wave?
6. A girl's skin burns in 8 minutes without sunscreen in strong sun. Using the thumb-rule (time = SPF × burn time), find the protection time for SPF 15 and for SPF 50. Why should she still reapply sunscreen sooner than the answer says?
7. In a hospital (made-up numbers, not real safety limits), a staff member may receive at most 18 units of dose in a year. Standing behind a lead screen she receives 0.06 unit per working day, and without the screen she would receive 0.45 unit per day. She works 220 days a year. Is she within the limit with the screen? Without the screen, how many times over the limit would she be?
8. Name the region of each wave and say whether it is ionising: (a) 5 µm, (b) 0.004 nm, (c) 200 nm, (d) 0.5 nm, (e) 530 nm.
9. Out of every 600 units of energy arriving from the Sun (lagbhag: about half visible, about 45 percent infrared, about 5 percent UV), how many units are visible, infrared and UV? UV is the smallest share: why do we still need sunscreen?
10. A dentist leaves the room, a patient wears a lead apron. In three or four lines, explain both, and say why the patient is not afraid of the single X-ray.
11. Say true or false and give a one-line reason.
    (a) Most UV-C from the Sun reaches the ground.
    (b) X-rays pass through bone more easily than through soft tissue.
    (c) Gamma rays come from the nucleus.
    (d) Visible light is ionising.
    (e) The ozone layer lies above the region of weather.
    (f) UV-A has a longer wavelength than UV-B.
12. Shravani says, "A UV torch just shows violet light, so I can look into it to see what it is." Correct her with two facts and tell what a safe use is.
13. A friend says, "X-rays are radiation and radiation is dangerous, so I will refuse even the X-ray my doctor advised for a suspected fracture." Give an honest reply of three or four lines.
14. Arrange in order of increasing frequency: red light, UV-B, X-ray, infrared, gamma ray, microwave. Give one line of reason.

### Answers

1. **Given:** λ = 254 nm = 254 × 10⁻⁹ m = 2.54 × 10⁻⁷ m.
   **Formula:** f = c ÷ λ.
   **Substitution:** f = (3 × 10⁸) ÷ (2.54 × 10⁻⁷) = 1.18 × 10¹⁵ Hz.
   **Answer:** f = **1.18 × 10¹⁵ Hz** (lagbhag). 254 nm, UV-C ki seema 100 se 280 nm ke andar hai.
   **Jaanch:** 1.18 × 10¹⁵ × 2.54 × 10⁻⁷ = 3.0 × 10⁸ ✅

2. **Given:** λ = 0.1 nm = 0.1 × 10⁻⁹ = 1 × 10⁻¹⁰ m.
   **Substitution:** f = (3 × 10⁸) ÷ (1 × 10⁻¹⁰) = 3 × 10¹⁸ Hz.
   **Answer:** f = **3 × 10¹⁸ Hz**. 0.1 nm, 0.01 se 10 nm ke beech hai, to **X-ray**.
   **Jaanch:** 3 × 10¹⁸ × 1 × 10⁻¹⁰ = 3 × 10⁸ ✅; 3 × 10¹⁸, 3 × 10¹⁶ aur 3 × 10¹⁹ ke beech ✅

3. **Given:** λ = 0.002 nm = 2 × 10⁻³ × 10⁻⁹ = 2 × 10⁻¹² m.
   **Substitution:** f = (3 × 10⁸) ÷ (2 × 10⁻¹²) = 1.5 × 10²⁰ Hz. Ratio = (1.5 × 10²⁰) ÷ (3 × 10¹⁸) = 50.
   **Answer:** f = **1.5 × 10²⁰ Hz**, X-ray se **50 guna** zyada.
   **Jaanch:** wavelength ratio 0.1 ÷ 0.002 = 50 ✅; 0.002 nm, 0.01 nm se chhoti, to gamma ✅

4. **Given:** λ_v = 400 nm = 4 × 10⁻⁷ m, λ_r = 700 nm = 7 × 10⁻⁷ m.
   **Substitution:** f_v = (3 × 10⁸) ÷ (4 × 10⁻⁷) = 7.5 × 10¹⁴ Hz. f_r = (3 × 10⁸) ÷ (7 × 10⁻⁷) = 4.29 × 10¹⁴ Hz. Ratio = 7.5 ÷ 4.29 = 1.75. Antar = (7.5 − 4.29) × 10¹⁴ = 3.2 × 10¹⁴ Hz.
   **Answer:** violet **7.5 × 10¹⁴ Hz**, red **4.3 × 10¹⁴ Hz** (lagbhag); violet ki f red se **1.75 guna** zyada, antar lagbhag **3.2 × 10¹⁴ Hz**.
   **Jaanch:** wavelength ratio 700 ÷ 400 = 1.75 ✅; 7.5 × 10¹⁴ × 4 × 10⁻⁷ = 3 × 10⁸ ✅

5. **Given:** λ = 365 nm = 3.65 × 10⁻⁷ m.
   **Substitution:** f = (3 × 10⁸) ÷ (3.65 × 10⁻⁷) = 8.22 × 10¹⁴ Hz.
   **Answer:** f = **8.22 × 10¹⁴ Hz**. 365 nm, 315 se 400 nm ke beech hai, to **UV-A**. Aankh sirf 400 se 700 nm dekhti hai, to ye wave **nahi dikhti** (torch ki visible baingani jhalak alag cheez hai).
   **Jaanch:** 8.22 × 10¹⁴ × 3.65 × 10⁻⁷ = 3.0 × 10⁸ ✅; f, 7.5 × 10¹⁴ se zyada hai ✅

6. **Given:** burn time = 8 min. **Formula:** samay = SPF × 8 min.
   **Substitution:** SPF 15: 15 × 8 = 120 min = 2 h. SPF 50: 50 × 8 = 400 min = 400 ÷ 60 = 6.67 h.
   **Answer:** **120 minute (2 ghante)** aur **400 minute (lagbhag 6.7 ghante)**. Jaanch: 120 ÷ 15 = 8 ✅.
   Phir bhi jaldi dobara lagao: kitaabi hisaab hai, pasine, paani aur kam lagane se sunscreen utar jaata hai.

7. **Given:** limit = 18 units/year; screen ke saath 0.06 unit/din; bina screen 0.45 unit/din; 220 din.
   **Substitution:** screen ke saath = 0.06 × 220 = 13.2 units. Bina screen = 0.45 × 220 = 99 units. Ratio = 99 ÷ 18 = 5.5.
   **Answer:** screen ke saath 13.2 < 18: **limit ke andar**. Bina screen 99 units, limit se lagbhag **5.5 guna** zyada.
   **Jaanch:** 13.2 ÷ 0.06 = 220 din ✅; 99 > 18 ✅. (Units made-up hain.)

8. (a) 5 µm = 5000 nm: 700 nm se 1 mm ke beech, **infrared**, non-ionising.
   (b) 0.004 nm, 0.01 nm se chhoti: **gamma**, ionising.
   (c) 200 nm: UV ki seema mein, 100 se 280 nm ke beech, **UV-C**, aam taur par non-ionising (par bahut nuksaandayi, sterilisation mein kaam aati hai).
   (d) 0.5 nm: 0.01 se 10 nm, **X-ray**, ionising.
   (e) 530 nm: 400 se 700 nm, **visible (hara)**, non-ionising.
   **Jaanch:** 5 µm × 1000 = 5000 nm ✅

9. **Given:** total = 600 units.
   **Substitution:** visible = 600 × 0.50 = 300. Infrared = 600 × 0.45 = 270. UV = 600 × 0.05 = 30. Jaanch: 300 + 270 + 30 = 600 ✅.
   **Answer:** **300, 270, 30 units**. UV sabse kam hai, par UV ki wavelength chhoti hone se uske ek "packet" mein zyada energy hoti hai aur wo twacha ke cells ke hisse badal sakti hai, isliye thodi si UV bhi lambe samay mein sunburn karti hai: sunscreen zaroori.

10. Dentist ek din mein kai X-ray leti hai, to bahar jaane se uski dose roz-roz nahi jodti. Patient ke seene par lead apron X-ray ko rokti hai, kyunki tasveer sirf daant ki chahiye. Ek hi X-ray ki dose chhoti hoti hai aur faayda (sahi ilaaj) zyada, isliye patient ko darne ki zaroorat nahi.

11. (a) **False** ❌. Ozone aur hawa UV-C ko lagbhag poori rok deti hain.
    (b) **False** ❌. Haddi zyada rokti hai, komal hissa kam: isliye haddi safed chhaya banati hai.
    (c) **True** ✅. Gamma nucleus se aati hai.
    (d) **False** ❌. Visible light non-ionising hai.
    (e) **True** ✅. Weather lagbhag 10 km tak, ozone lagbhag 20 se 30 km.
    (f) **True** ✅. UV-A (315 se 400 nm) UV-B (280 se 315 nm) se lambi hai.

12. Shravani galat hai. (i) UV ki wavelength 400 nm se chhoti hai (UV-A 315 se 400 nm), aankh use nahi dekhti, aur torch ka baingani rang uski LED ki thodi visible roshni hai. (ii) UV aankh ko bina dard ke nuksaan pahuncha sakti hai. Safe use: adult ke saath, torch kabhi aankh ya twacha ki taraf nahi, kuch second ke liye note ya deewar par.

13. X-ray ionising hai, par ek zaroori X-ray ki dose chhoti hoti hai aur doctor tabhi likhta hai jab faayda risk se zyada ho. Fracture ka sahi pata na chale to ilaaj galat ho sakta hai, jo kahin zyada nuksaan karega. Lead apron aur kam dose se risk aur ghatta hai. Sahi tareeka: doctor se poochho "kya iske bina kaam chal sakta hai?", par bina soche mana mat karo.

14. Microwave < infrared < red light < UV-B < X-ray < gamma ray. **Reason:** wavelength jitni chhoti, f = c ÷ λ utna bada; parivaar ki line lambi se chhoti wavelength tak isi kram mein hai.
