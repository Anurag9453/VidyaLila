## Part A: Kab kaun sa tool

Is chapter ke sabhi numbers ke sawal paanch-chhe tool se hal ho jaate hain. Sawal padhte hi pehle ye dekho ki **diya kya hai**.

```
Diya kya hai              Kaun sa tool                 Pehle ya baad mein yaad rakho
------------------------  ---------------------------  ---------------------------------------
wavelength ya frequency   c = f × λ                    nm, µm, MHz, GHz ko pehle m aur Hz mein
do rangon ki scattering   (λ lambi ÷ λ chhoti)⁴        anupaat hamesha 1 se bada aata hai
taare ya Sooraj ka shift  15° per ghanta (1° = 4 min)  0.5° lagbhag 2 minute
roshni ki doori ya samay  t = d ÷ c                    km ko pehle m mein
radar ka echo             d = c × t ÷ 2                aana aur jaana, isliye 2 se bhaag
antenna ki lambai         λ ÷ 2, pehle λ nikalo        λ = c ÷ f
```

> **Figure 6.1** – "Kab kaun sa tool" chart: sawal mein jo diya ho, usse seedha tool tak jao. Teen tool (c = f × λ, t = d ÷ c, antenna λ ÷ 2) Topic 3 aur 4 ke hain (radar ka d = c × t ÷ 2 bhi Topic 4 ka), scattering ka anupaat Topic 2 ka, aur 15° per ghanta Topic 1 ka.

Unit ki galti sabse badi galti hai, isliye ek chhoti si seedhi bhi yaad rakho.

```
Naap   Metre ya hertz mein  Kahan milta hai
-----  -------------------  --------------------------------
1 nm   10⁻⁹ m               visible, UV, X-ray
1 µm   10⁻⁶ m               infrared
1 mm   10⁻³ m               infrared aur microwave ka sangam
1 cm   10⁻² m               microwave
1 MHz  10⁶ Hz               radio
1 GHz  10⁹ Hz               mobile, Wi-Fi, oven
```

> **Figure 6.2** – Unit ki seedhi: har naap ke saamne uska metre ya hertz wala roop aur wo kis region mein aam taur par aata hai. Hamesha pehle yahan se m ya Hz mein badlo, phir c = f × λ lagao.

**Pakki aadat:** jawab aane ke baad "andaaza jaanch" karo. Jaise 450 nm ki frequency 6.7 × 10⁶ Hz aaye to ye radio ki frequency hai, jabki 450 nm to visible hai: matlab nm ko metre mein badalna bhool gaye.

## Part B: Master table: saaton regions ek jagah

Neeche ki table Topic 3, 4 aur 5 ki baaton se bani hai. Saari seemayein **lagbhag** hain aur kinare dhundhle hain: radio waves **lagbhag 30 cm aur usse lambi** (frequency lagbhag 1 GHz se kam), microwaves **lagbhag 1 mm se 30 cm** (1 se 300 GHz). Isliye mobile 900 MHz radio ke kinare par aur 1800 MHz microwave ke kinare par baithta hai, aur Wi-Fi 2.4 aur 5 GHz microwave hain.

| Region | Wavelength (lagbhag) | Frequency (lagbhag) | Source | Ek use | Hazard |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Radio waves | lagbhag 30 cm se lambi (AM lagbhag 200 se 600 m, FM lagbhag 3 m, TV lagbhag 1 se 5 m) | lagbhag 1 GHz se kam | antenna mein hilte charge | radio, TV, mobile (900 MHz) | kam power par non-ionising, aam taur par saaf nuksaan nahi |
| Microwaves | lagbhag 1 mm se 30 cm | 1 se 300 GHz | microwave oven ka source, router, radar | oven (2.45 GHz), Wi-Fi, radar | non-ionising par bahut tez power se garam karti hain |
| Infrared | lagbhag 700 nm se 1 mm | lagbhag 3 × 10¹¹ se 4.3 × 10¹⁴ Hz | har garam cheez (shareer lagbhag 10 µm par) | remote, thermal camera | zyada tez par jalan |
| Visible | 400 nm se 700 nm | lagbhag 4.3 × 10¹⁴ se 7.5 × 10¹⁴ Hz | Sooraj, bulb | dekhna, photosynthesis | bahut tez roshni ya laser se aankh ko chot |
| Ultraviolet | lagbhag 10 nm se 400 nm | lagbhag 7.5 × 10¹⁴ se 3 × 10¹⁶ Hz | Sooraj, UV lamp | sterilisation, vitamin D | sunburn, skin cancer, cataract |
| X-rays | lagbhag 0.01 nm se 10 nm | lagbhag 3 × 10¹⁶ se 3 × 10¹⁹ Hz | tez electron dhaatu se takrane par | hospital aur dant ki X-ray | ionising, lead apron zaroori |
| Gamma rays | lagbhag 0.01 nm se chhoti | lagbhag 3 × 10¹⁹ Hz se zyada | nuclei (Chapter 11 mein) | radiotherapy, medical items ka sterilisation | ionising, sabse zyada penetrating |

Do baatein dhyan se: Sooraj ki output mein lagbhag aadhi visible, lagbhag 45 percent infrared aur lagbhag 5 percent UV hai. Aur **frequency ke saath energy badhti hai**: isliye X-ray aur gamma khatarnak, radio aur microwave nahi. (Ye kyun hota hai, ye "aage ki class mein".)

## Part C: Mile-jule sawal

### Example 1: Satellite TV dish ki wave (Topic 3 aur 4)

Ek satellite TV link 12 GHz par chalta hai. Uski wavelength nikalo aur region batao.

**Given:** f = 12 GHz. c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s. **Find:** λ aur region.
**Formula:** λ = c ÷ f
**Substitution:** f = 12 × 10⁹ Hz. λ = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ (12 × 10⁹) = 0.025 m = 2.5 cm.
**Answer:** λ = **2.5 cm** (lagbhag). 12 GHz, 1 se 300 GHz ke beech hai, to **microwave**.
**Jaanch:** 12 × 10⁹ × 0.025 = 3 × 10⁸ ✅; 2.5 cm, 1 mm se 30 cm ke andar ✅

### Example 2: Do rang, ek shaam (Topic 2)

Neela 450 nm aur laal-narangi 630 nm ki scattering ka anupaat nikalo. Phir maan lo ki shaam ko roshni ka raasta dopahar se 5 guna hai (given factor, flat-layer model). Shaam ko kaun sa rang zyada bacha rahega?

**Given:** λ₁ = 450 nm, λ₂ = 630 nm, path factor = 5. **Find:** anupaat aur shaam ka rang.
**Formula:** anupaat = (λ lambi ÷ λ chhoti)⁴
**Substitution:** 630 ÷ 450 = 1.4. 1.4² = 1.96, 1.96² = 3.8416.
**Answer:** neela, narangi se lagbhag **3.84 guna** zyada bikharta hai. Raasta 5 guna ho to dopahar ke mukable shaam ko neele ka kaatna 5 guna hota hai, aur dopahar ke narangi ke mukable shaam ke neele ka kaatna 5 × 3.84 = **19.2 guna**. To shaam ko **laal-narangi** bacha rehta hai, Sooraj laal dikhta hai.
**Jaanch:** 3.84 > 1 ✅ (chhoti λ zyada bikhri); 19.2 ÷ 5 = 3.84 ✅. (Ye sirf ek simple model hai; asal mein neela lagbhag poora nikal jaata hai.)

### Example 3: Lambe raaste mein roshni ko kitna waqt lagta hai (Topic 1, 2 aur 3)

Hawa ki parat lagbhag 10 km moti maano. Dopahar ko roshni seedhi neeche 10 km tay karti hai; sunrise par raasta 8 guna, yaani 80 km maano (given). Extra raaste ka extra waqt kitna hai? Aur kya isse "Sooraj 2 minute pehle dikhta hai" samjha ja sakta hai?

**Given:** extra raasta = 80 − 10 = 70 km. c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s. **Find:** extra waqt.
**Formula:** t = d ÷ c
**Substitution:** d = 70 km = 70 × 1000 = 7 × 10⁴ m. t = 7 × 10⁴ ÷ 3 × 10⁸ = 2.33 × 10⁻⁴ s = 233 µs.
**Answer:** extra waqt lagbhag **233 µs**. Ye 2 minute (120 s) ke saamne bahut chhota hai: 120 ÷ (2.33 × 10⁻⁴) = lagbhag 5 × 10⁵ guna. Isliye 2 minute ka fark waqt ki deri se nahi, hawa ke **refraction** se hai (Topic 1: dikhne wali jagah thodi oopar).
**Jaanch:** 3 × 10⁸ × 2.33 × 10⁻⁴ = 7 × 10⁴ m ✅; 2.33 × 10⁻⁴ s = 233 × 10⁻⁶ s ✅

### Example 4: Earth ghoomi, roshni kitni gayi (Topic 1 aur 3)

Kisi din Earth ko 0.85° ghoomne mein kitna waqt lagta hai? Us waqt mein roshni kitni door jaati hai?

**Given:** angle = 0.85°, rate = 15° per ghanta, c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s. **Find:** t aur d.
**Formula:** t = angle ÷ rate, d = c × t
**Substitution:** t = 0.85 ÷ 15 = 0.05667 h = 0.05667 × 3600 = 204 s (3.4 min). d = 3 × 10⁸ × 204 = 6.12 × 10¹⁰ m.
**Answer:** t = **204 s** (3.4 minute), roshni lagbhag **6.12 × 10¹⁰ m** (lagbhag 6.1 crore km) jaati hai.
**Jaanch:** 1° = 4 min, 0.85 × 4 = 3.4 min = 204 s ✅; 6.12 × 10¹⁰ ÷ 3 × 10⁸ = 204 s ✅

### Example 5: Teen anjaan waves ko pehchaano (Topic 3 aur 5)

Teen waves ki frequency: (a) 4 × 10⁹ Hz, (b) 9 × 10¹⁵ Hz, (c) 5 × 10²⁰ Hz. Har ki wavelength nikalo aur region batao.

**Given:** teen f. **Formula:** λ = c ÷ f.
**Substitution:** (a) 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 4 × 10⁹ = 0.075 m = 7.5 cm. (b) 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 9 × 10¹⁵ = 3.33 × 10⁻⁸ m = 33.3 nm. (c) 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 5 × 10²⁰ = 6 × 10⁻¹³ m = 6 × 10⁻⁴ nm.
**Answer:** (a) **7.5 cm: microwave**. (b) **33.3 nm: ultraviolet** (10 se 400 nm). (c) **0.0006 nm: gamma** (0.01 nm se chhoti), ionising.
**Jaanch:** 4 × 10⁹ × 0.075 = 3 × 10⁸ ✅; 9 × 10¹⁵ × 3.33 × 10⁻⁸ = 3 × 10⁸ ✅; 5 × 10²⁰ × 6 × 10⁻¹³ = 3 × 10⁸ ✅

### Band edge par ek wave

1 GHz ki wave ki wavelength 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 10⁹ = 0.3 m = 30 cm aati hai: wahi **kinara**, jahan radio aur microwave milte hain. Isi tarah 300 GHz par λ = 1 mm: microwave aur infrared ka kinara. Aise kinare par kitab aur bhaasha dono alag-alag naam de sakti hain, isliye "lagbhag" likhna zaroori hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Wavelength aur frequency dono saath-saath badhte hain"**

Demonstration: teen waves ka f × λ dekho, vacuum mein har baar c:

| Wave | f | λ | f × λ |
|---|---|---|---|
| AM radio | 5 × 10⁵ Hz | 600 m | 3 × 10⁸ m/s |
| Satellite link | 12 × 10⁹ Hz | 0.025 m | 3 × 10⁸ m/s |
| Narangi roshni | 5 × 10¹⁴ Hz | 6 × 10⁻⁷ m | 3 × 10⁸ m/s |

f badhti gayi aur λ ghatti gayi. Kyunki c fixed hai, ek upar to doosri neeche.

**Galti 2: "nm ya GHz ko metre aur hertz mein badalna zaroori nahi"**

Demonstration: 450 nm ko seedha 450 maan ke f = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 450 = 6.7 × 10⁵ Hz aata hai: ye to radio hai, jabki 450 nm neela hai. Sahi: 450 × 10⁻⁹ = 4.5 × 10⁻⁷ m, f = 6.67 × 10¹⁴ Hz (visible). Aur 12 GHz ko 12 Hz maan lo to λ = 2.5 × 10⁷ m (25,000 km), jo bilkul bemel hai: koi microwave itni lambi nahi hoti. Sahi 2.5 cm.

**Galti 3: "Laal rang ki frequency sabse zyada hoti hai"**

Demonstration: laal 700 nm: f = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 7 × 10⁻⁷ = 4.3 × 10¹⁴ Hz. Violet 400 nm: f = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 4 × 10⁻⁷ = 7.5 × 10¹⁴ Hz. Laal ki frequency **kam**, violet ki zyada. Laal "tez" lagta hai kyunki danger signal hai (Topic 2), par wo sabse kam bikharta hai, aur uski wavelength sabse **lambi**.

**Galti 4: "Atmosphere Sooraj ya Chaand ko bada kar deta hai"**

Demonstration (raat ko, ghar par adult ke saath, sirf Chaand): jab Chaand kshitij ke paas ho, haath ko poora failakar ek matar ka dana uske saamne rakho; phir jab Chaand oopar ho, wahi dana dobara. Dana dono baar lagbhag barabar dikhta hai: Chaand ka kon-size lagbhag aadha degree hi rehta hai. Kshitij par bada lagna zyadatar **dimaag ka bhram** hai. Aur Topic 1 ne dikhaya ki hawa ka asar Sooraj ko vertical mein **chhota** (chapta) karta hai, bada nahi. Sooraj ko kabhi seedha mat dekho.

## Quick Recap

- **Tool chart:** c = f × λ, anupaat (λ lambi ÷ λ chhoti)⁴, 15° per ghanta, t = d ÷ c, d = c × t ÷ 2, antenna λ ÷ 2.
- Pehle metre aur hertz: 1 nm = 10⁻⁹ m, 1 µm = 10⁻⁶ m, 1 MHz = 10⁶ Hz, 1 GHz = 10⁹ Hz.
- Spectrum (lambi se chhoti λ): Radio, Microwave, Infrared, Visible, Ultraviolet, X-ray, Gamma. Kinare dhundhle: radio lagbhag 30 cm se lambi, microwave 1 mm se 30 cm.
- Wavelength badhe to frequency ghatti hai. Laal ki frequency sabse kam, violet ki sabse zyada.
- Sirf X-ray aur gamma ionising hain (UV aam taur par nahi); visible, IR, microwave aur radio atom se electron nahi nikaalte.
- Atmosphere Sooraj ko bada nahi karta; 2 minute ka fark refraction se hai, deri se nahi.

## Practice Questions

Take c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s. Convert every unit to metres and hertz before you calculate.

1. A TV tower broadcasts at 250 MHz. Find the wavelength, name the region and find the length of a half-wave antenna.
2. A mobile network uses a signal of 1950 MHz. Find its wavelength in cm and name the region. How many times shorter is it than the wave in Question 1?
3. A thermal sensor works on infrared of wavelength 3 µm. Find its frequency. How many times shorter is it than the 10 µm radiation of a human body?
4. An ultraviolet lamp emits waves of 320 nm. Find the frequency and say which UV band (UVA, UVB or UVC) it belongs to.
5. A medical X-ray has a wavelength of 1 nm. Find its frequency. Is it ionising? How many times higher is its frequency than that of the infrared in Question 3?
6. Arrange the waves of Questions 1 to 5 in order of increasing frequency.
7. Compare the scattering of the 320 nm ultraviolet and violet light of 400 nm by tiny particles. Which is scattered more and by what factor?
8. In a flat-layer model, the air is 10 km thick. At sunset the sunlight's path is 12 times its noon path. Find the extra path and the extra time light takes for it.
9. Using the factor of 12 in Question 8 and the blue : red scattering ratio of about 9 for the same path, find how many times more blue is removed at sunset than red is removed at noon, in the model.
10. A star near the horizon appears shifted by 0.7°. Convert this to a time in seconds using the Earth's rate of 15° per hour.
11. A signal from the TV tower of Question 1 reaches a house 27 km away. How long does it take, in microseconds?
12. For each situation name the tool you would use (no numbers needed): (a) finding f from λ; (b) finding which colour is scattered more; (c) finding the time of an extra sunrise shift; (d) finding the distance from a radar echo.
13. Say true or false and give a one-line reason.
    (a) The wave of Question 4 is visible to the human eye.
    (b) Doubling the frequency doubles the wavelength.
    (c) A 3 µm wave has a longer wavelength than a 1 nm wave.
    (d) Red light has the highest frequency in VIBGYOR.
14. A friend says, "1 nm is a smaller number than 3 µm, so the 1 nm wave must have the longer wavelength." Find his unit mistake and correct him.

### Answers

1. **Given:** f = 250 MHz = 250 × 10⁶ Hz = 2.5 × 10⁸ Hz. **Formula:** λ = c ÷ f, antenna = λ ÷ 2.
   **Substitution:** λ = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 2.5 × 10⁸ = 1.2 m. Antenna = 1.2 ÷ 2 = 0.6 m.
   **Answer:** λ = **1.2 m** (lagbhag, TV ki 1 se 5 m ki range). 30 cm se lamba, to **radio**. Antenna **0.6 m**. **Jaanch:** 2.5 × 10⁸ × 1.2 = 3 × 10⁸ ✅

2. **Given:** f = 1950 MHz = 1.95 × 10⁹ Hz. **Formula:** λ = c ÷ f.
   **Substitution:** λ = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 1.95 × 10⁹ = 0.1538 m = 15.4 cm. Anupaat = 1.2 ÷ 0.1538 = 7.8.
   **Answer:** λ = **15.4 cm**, 30 cm se chhota, 1.95 GHz 1 se 300 GHz ke beech: **microwave**. Question 1 se lagbhag **7.8 guna** chhoti. **Jaanch:** 1950 ÷ 250 = 7.8 ✅

3. **Given:** λ = 3 µm = 3 × 10⁻⁶ m. **Formula:** f = c ÷ λ.
   **Substitution:** f = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 3 × 10⁻⁶ = 10¹⁴ Hz. 10 ÷ 3 = 3.33.
   **Answer:** f = **10¹⁴ Hz**, infrared. Body ki 10 µm se lagbhag **3.33 guna** chhoti. **Jaanch:** 10¹⁴ × 3 × 10⁻⁶ = 3 × 10⁸ ✅

4. **Given:** λ = 320 nm = 320 × 10⁻⁹ = 3.2 × 10⁻⁷ m. **Formula:** f = c ÷ λ.
   **Substitution:** f = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 3.2 × 10⁻⁷ = 9.375 × 10¹⁴ Hz.
   **Answer:** f = **9.4 × 10¹⁴ Hz** (lagbhag). 315 se 400 nm: **UVA**. **Jaanch:** 9.375 × 10¹⁴ × 3.2 × 10⁻⁷ = 3 × 10⁸ ✅

5. **Given:** λ = 1 nm = 10⁻⁹ m. **Formula:** f = c ÷ λ.
   **Substitution:** f = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 10⁻⁹ = 3 × 10¹⁷ Hz. Anupaat = 3 × 10¹⁷ ÷ 10¹⁴ = 3000.
   **Answer:** f = **3 × 10¹⁷ Hz**. **Ionising** hai (isliye lead apron). Question 3 ke infrared se **3000 guna** zyada. **Jaanch:** 3 × 10¹⁷ × 10⁻⁹ = 3 × 10⁸ ✅; 3 µm ÷ 1 nm = 3000 ✅

6. f: Q1 = 2.5 × 10⁸ Hz, Q2 = 1.95 × 10⁹ Hz, Q3 = 10¹⁴ Hz, Q4 = 9.4 × 10¹⁴ Hz, Q5 = 3 × 10¹⁷ Hz.
   **Answer:** **Q1 (radio), Q2 (microwave), Q3 (infrared), Q4 (UV), Q5 (X-ray)**. **Jaanch:** chhoti wavelength, zyada frequency: 1.2 m > 15.4 cm > 3 µm > 320 nm > 1 nm ✅

7. **Given:** 320 nm aur 400 nm. **Formula:** (λ lambi ÷ λ chhoti)⁴.
   **Substitution:** 400 ÷ 320 = 1.25. 1.25² = 1.5625, 1.5625² = 2.441.
   **Answer:** 320 nm wali UV lagbhag **2.44 guna** zyada bikharti hai. **Jaanch:** 2.44 > 1 ✅ (chhoti λ zyada bikhri)

8. **Given:** moti parat = 10 km, sunset path = 12 × 10 = 120 km. Extra = 120 − 10 = 110 km = 1.1 × 10⁵ m.
   **Formula:** t = d ÷ c.
   **Substitution:** t = 1.1 × 10⁵ ÷ 3 × 10⁸ = 3.67 × 10⁻⁴ s = 367 µs.
   **Answer:** extra raasta **110 km**, extra waqt lagbhag **367 µs**. **Jaanch:** 3 × 10⁸ × 3.67 × 10⁻⁴ = 1.1 × 10⁵ m ✅

9. **Given:** path factor 12, blue : red = 9. **Formula:** 12 × 9.
   **Answer:** simple model mein lagbhag **108 guna**. **Jaanch:** 108 ÷ 12 = 9 ✅. (Model hai; asal mein neela lagbhag poora nikal jaata hai.)

10. **Given:** 0.7°, rate 15° per ghanta. **Formula:** t = angle ÷ rate.
    **Substitution:** t = 0.7 ÷ 15 = 0.04667 h = 0.04667 × 3600 = 168 s (2.8 min).
    **Answer:** **168 s**. **Jaanch:** 1° = 240 s, 0.7 × 240 = 168 s ✅

11. **Given:** d = 27 km = 27 × 1000 = 27000 m. **Formula:** t = d ÷ c.
    **Substitution:** t = 27000 ÷ 3 × 10⁸ = 9 × 10⁻⁵ s = 90 µs.
    **Answer:** **90 µs**. **Jaanch:** 3 × 10⁸ × 9 × 10⁻⁵ = 27000 m ✅

12. (a) **c = f × λ**, f = c ÷ λ. (b) **(λ lambi ÷ λ chhoti)⁴** ka anupaat. (c) **15° per ghanta** (1° = 4 min). (d) **d = c × t ÷ 2**.

13. (a) **False** ❌. 320 nm, 400 nm se chhoti hai: UV, dikhti nahi.
    (b) **False** ❌. λ = c ÷ f: f doguni, to λ aadhi.
    (c) **True** ✅. 3 µm = 3000 nm, 1 nm se bahut lambi.
    (d) **False** ❌. Laal ki frequency sabse kam (lagbhag 4.3 × 10¹⁴ Hz) aur violet ki sabse zyada.

14. Unit alag hain. 3 µm = 3 × 10⁻⁶ m aur 1 nm = 10⁻⁹ m. 3 µm = 3000 nm, to 3 µm wali wave **3000 guna lambi** hai, 1 nm wali chhoti. 1 aur 3 compare karna tab sahi hota jab dono ka unit ek ho.
