Chapter 4 mein humne **aankh** ko ek lens system ki tarah dekha, aur telescope ko door ki cheez paas laate hue. Ab aankh se bahar nikalte hain, us hawa mein jo hamare aur taaron ke beech hai. ## Terrace par ek raat

Garmi ki chhutti thi. Mr. Dhavale apni bhatiji Shalmali ke saath society ki terrace par chatai bichhakar leta hua tha. Bijli gayi hui thi, to aasmaan ek dum saaf tha. Shalmali ne dekha ki zyaadatar taare **jhilmil-jhilmil** kar rahe hain: kabhi tez, kabhi dheeme, kabhi lagta hai jaise kaanp rahe hon.

"Chacha, ye taare itna hilte kyun hain? Kya wahan koi baar-baar light on-off kar raha hai?" Mr. Dhavale hans pade, "Pehle ek kaam karo. Ye dekho." Unhone ek bahut tez chamakta bindu dikhaya, jo seedha jala hua tha, zara bhi nahi jhilmila raha tha. "Ye bhi to taare jaisa dikhta hai, par hilta nahi!" Phone ke sky app ne bataya ki wo ek **grah (planet)** hai.

"Dono chhote bindu jaise dikhte hain, phir ek jhilmilata hai aur doosra nahi?" Shalmali ne soch mein sir khujaya. Phir usne apna doosra sawaal poocha: "Aur kal subah mummy ne kaha ki Sooraj ka time table mein 6:12 likha hai, par maine to 6:10 par hi dekh liya!" Mr. Dhavale ne muskura kar kaha, "Teeno sawaalon ka jawab ek hi hai: **hawa**. Chalo dhire dhire dekhte hain."

Teen sawaal: **taara jhilmilata kyun hai, grah kyun nahi, aur sooraj time se pehle kaise dikh gaya?** Aakhir mein hum "Wapas terrace par" section mein teeno solve karenge.

## Part A: Hawa ki parton se mudti roshni

### Hawa ek jaisi nahi, parton mein hai

Zameen ke paas hawa sabse ghani hai aur oopar jaate hue patli hoti jaati hai. Hawa jitni ghani, uska refractive index utna thoda zyada (Class 9 mein n = c ÷ v dekha tha). Sooraj ya taare se aati roshni pehle khali ant-riksh (space) se aati hai, jahan hawa nahi, phir oopar ki bahut patli hawa mein, phir dheere dheere ghani parton mein. Ye ek **slab ki tarah ek baar nahi, balki bahut si patli parton mein** hota hai, isliye bending bhi har parat par thodi thodi hoti hai.

Har parat par rarer se denser mein jaate hue ray **normal ki taraf** mudti hai (normal = seema par seedhi khadi line). Matlab har parat ke baad ray thodi aur **seedhi (zyada khadi)** ho jaati hai.

```

                          \            ·
                            \           ·                      ant-riksh (khali)
                               \        ·
─────────────────────────────────\──────────────────────────
                                  \       ·
                                    \     ·                    parat 1 (patli)
                                      \    ·
────────────────────────────────────────\───────────────────
                                         \  ·
                                          \  ·                 parat 2
                                           \ ·
─────────────────────────────────────────────\──────────────
                                              \

                                               \·
────────────────────────────────────────────────\───────────

                                                  O
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Hawa ki parton se aati taare ki roshni (schematic, bending badha kar dikhayi gayi). Asli ray S se chalkar har parat ki seema par normal (seedhi khadi line) ki taraf thodi mudti hai, aur observer O tak aate aate zyada khadi ho jaati hai. O ko aakhri ray ki disha dikhti hai, isliye taara peeche badhai hui (dotted) line par, S' par, dikhta hai: asli jagah S se **thoda oopar**.

### Apparent position: taara thoda oopar

Hamari aankh sirf aakhri disha jaanti hai: roshni jis disha se aankh tak aayi, wahi se aati hui lagti hai. Figure 1.1 mein aakhri ray zyada khadi hai, to taara hamein S ki jagah S' par, yaani **zyada oonchai par** dikhta hai. Kshitij ke paas ray hawa mein sabse lambi raah tay karti hai, isliye wahan ye shift sabse zyada, lagbhag **0.5°** hota hai. Taara seedha sir ke upar (zenith par) ho to uski roshni parton ke seema ko seedhi (normal ke saath) kaatti hai, to mudti nahi: **zenith par shift 0**.

> **Dhyaan rahe:** kshitij par shift lagbhag 0.5° hai, aur 0.5° wahi hai jo sooraj ke apne disc ka kon hai. Isliye jab sooraj kshitij par "chhoo raha" dikhta hai, wo asal mein (geometry se) kshitij ke **neeche** pahunch chuka hota hai.

### Ghar par adult ke saath: katori mein sikka

Ek katori ke andar neeche ek sikka rakho. Katori ko itni door se aur is kon se dekho ki katori ke kinare ki wajah se sikka bas chhup jaye. Ab kisi **adult ke saath**, ek jug se dheere dheere paani katori mein daalo (sikka mat hilne do). Sikka phir dikhne lagega, jaise upar utha ho. Ye apparent position hai: paani se nikalti ray mud kar aankh tak aati hai. Ye activity **kamre ki roshni mein** karo, dhoop mein nahi (paani bhara kaanch dhoop ko ikattha kar sakta hai).

### Teen line mein: mirage aur looming

Class 9 ka mirage garam sateh ke paas hawa ke refraction ka kamaal tha. Ulte tarah ka maamla bhi hota hai: kabhi thande samudra ya barf par thandi hawa neeche aur garam hawa upar hoti hai, to door ka jahaz kshitij ke upar uthi hui ya lambi dikh sakti hai. Isko **looming** kehte hain. Dono mein baat wahi hai: hawa ki parton ka refractive index badalta hai, ray mudti hai, aur cheez apni asli jagah se alag **dikhti** hai.

## Part B: Taare jhilmilate kyun hain, grah kyun nahi

### Parton ko hilta dekho

Figure 1.1 mein hawa ki parte jam kar baithi nahi hain. Garam hawa upar uthti hai, thandi neeche aati hai, hawa ke jhonke chalte hain: har second parton ki density thodi badalti rehti hai. To ray ka raasta har pal **thoda alag** hota hai. Taare ki roshni ka raasta kabhi thoda zyada mudta hai, kabhi kam. Ab do asar milte hain:

- **Dikhne wali jagah** thodi thodi hilti hai (S' ka kaampna).
- Kabhi kabhi zyada roshni aankh mein aati hai (taara chamak uthta hai), kabhi kam (taara dheema dikhta hai).

Dono milkar **jhilmilana (twinkling)** kehlate hain. Taara ek **point source** hai: itna door hai ki uski roshni ek hi chhote raaste se aati hai. Raasta hila to poori chamak hil gayi.

### Grah kyun nahi jhilmilate

Grah (jaise Venus ya Jupiter) hamse taaron se bahut paas hain, to aasmaan mein ek chhoti si **disc** jaise dikhte hain (extended source). Us disc ke har bindu se alag alag raaston ki roshni aati hai. Kuch raaste mud kar thodi chamak badhate hain, kuch ghatate hain, par sabka jod lagbhag **sthir** rehta hai. Isliye grah aam taur par jhilmilate nahi. (Bahut kharaab ya jhonke wali hawa mein, ya kshitij ke bahut paas, grah bhi thoda jhilmila sakte hain.)

```
    Taara: ek bindu                     Grah: chhoti si disc (kai bindu)
           *                                  *     *     *
           ↓                                  ↓     ↓     ↓
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~    ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
           ↓                                  ↘     ↓     ↙
           ↘
            ↓
          Aankh                                  Aankh
Ek hi raasta hilta hai:               Raaste alag alag hilte hain:
poori chamak ek saath hilti           kuch tez, kuch dheemi: kul lagbhag sthir

```

> **Figure 1.2** – Taara (ek bindu) aur grah (kai bindu ki disc) ki roshni hawa ki hilti parat (wavy line) se guzarte hue. Ek raasta hila to taare ki chamak hili; grah ke kai raaste ek doosre ki kami poori kar dete hain, isliye grah lagbhag sthir dikhta hai. Teeron ka matlab sirf roshni ki disha hai.

### Aur teen baatein jo "twinkling" ko samjhati hain

1. **Kshitij ke paas taare zyada jhilmilate hain.** Wahan roshni ko hawa mein **lambi raah** tay karni padti hai, to zyada parton aur zyada jhonkon se guzarti hai. Seedha sir ke upar ka taara kam jhilmilata hai, kyunki raah chhoti hai.
2. **Bina hawa ke koi twinkling nahi.** Ant-riksh mein, hawa ke oopar, taare jhilmilate nahi, steady chamakte hain. Isliye Hubble jaise telescope ([human-eye-part5.md](../human-eye/human-eye-part5.md), Topic 5) Zameen ke oopar orbit mein bheje jaate hain (orbit ke baare mein Chapter 1: [gravitation-and-space-missions.md](../gravitation-and-space-missions/gravitation-and-space-missions.md)): wahan hawa ka jhilmilana nahi, to tasveer aur saaf.
3. **Garam chai ya garam chhat ke upar bhi wahi.** Garam hawa upar uthti hai aur uska density badalta rehta hai, to uske peeche ki seedhi cheez (jaise deewar ya ped) kaanpti si dikhti hai. Wahan koi cheez "flicker" nahi kar rahi, bas roshni ki raah hil rahi hai.

**Ghar par adult ke saath (sirf dekhne ke liye):** garmi ki dopahar mein dhoop se tapi chhat ya sadak ke upar door ki deewar ya ped ko dekho: kinare halke se hilte dikhenge. Ya rasoi mein adult ke garam chai ke cup ke bagal se door ki light ko dekho. Cup ya garam cheez ko haath mat lagao.

## Part C: Sooraj jaldi dikhta hai aur der se doobta hai

### Zameen ghoomti hai, aur 0.5° kitna waqt hai

Zameen 24 ghante mein 360° ghoomti hai. Isliye har ghante lagbhag **15°** (360° ÷ 24 h = 15° per hour), yaani har **4 minute mein 1°**. Ye ek sidha tarika hai ki ek kon ko samay mein badal do.

**Example 1: 0.5° ka shift kitna time hai?**

Given: Zameen 360° ghoomti hai 24 h mein. Kshitij par atmospheric refraction ka shift = 0.5°.
Find: sooraj kitna pehle dikhta hai (time t).
Formula: rate = 360° ÷ 24 h = 15° per hour; t = angle ÷ rate.
Substitution: t = 0.5° ÷ (15° per hour) = 0.0333 h. Ghante ko minute mein badlo: 0.0333 h × 60 min per h = 2 min.
Answer: t = **2 minute**. Sunrise par 2 minute pehle, sunset par 2 minute baad: total din lagbhag **4 minute** lamba.
Jaanch: 15° per hour = 15° per 60 min = 0.25° per min, yaani 1° per 4 min. 0.5° × 4 min per ° = 2 min ✅. Dono ends: 2 min + 2 min = 4 min, jo Class 9 ki line se milta hai ✅

**Example 2: Almanac aur asli dikhna**

Mr. Dhavale ke almanac mein ek din ka geometric sunrise 6:14 aur geometric sunset 18:41 hai. Atmospheric refraction se sooraj dono ends par 2 minute ka fayda lega. Shalmali ko dikhne wala sunrise, sunset aur din ki lambai nikalni hai.

Given: sunrise (geometric) = 6:14, sunset (geometric) = 18:41, fayda 2 min har ends par.
Find: dikhne wala sunrise, sunset aur din ki lambai.
Formula: dikhne wala sunrise = geometric − 2 min; dikhne wala sunset = geometric + 2 min.
Substitution: sunrise = 6:14 − 0:02 = 6:12; sunset = 18:41 + 0:02 = 18:43. Din ki lambai = 18:43 − 6:12 = 12 h 31 min.
Answer: dikhne wala sunrise **6:12**, sunset **18:43**, din **12 h 31 min**. Geometric din = 18:41 − 6:14 = 12 h 27 min.
Jaanch: 12 h 31 min − 12 h 27 min = 4 min, yaani dono ends ka 2 + 2 min ✅

**Example 3: Time se shift ka kon aur kam-zyada**

Kisi jagah ek din sunrise lagbhag 2.4 min pehle aur sunset lagbhag 2.6 min baad tak dikha (ye us din ka diya hua data hai). (a) Har ek ends ka shift kitne degree ka hua? (b) Kul faayda? (c) Agar geometric din 12 h 10 min ka tha, to dikhne wala din kitna?

(a) Given: 4 min = 1°, to 1 min = 0.25°. Sunrise shift = 2.4 min × 0.25° per min = **0.6°**. Sunset shift = 2.6 min × 0.25° per min = **0.65°**.
(b) Kul faayda = 2.4 min + 2.6 min = **5.0 min**.
(c) Dikhne wala din = 12 h 10 min + 5.0 min = **12 h 15 min**.
Jaanch: 0.6° ÷ (15° per h) = 0.04 h = 2.4 min ✅ aur 0.65° ÷ (15° per h) = 0.0433 h × 60 = 2.6 min ✅

> **Ek imaandar baat:** ye seedha hisaab equator ke paas theek baithta hai, jahan sooraj seedha oopar uthta hai. Door ki jagahon par sooraj tirchha uthta hai, isliye wahi 0.5° mein zyada waqt lagta hai. 2 minute ek **lagbhag** figure hai, jagah, mausam aur din ke saath badalta hai.

### Sooraj kshitij par chapta kyun dikhta hai

Doobte ya ugte sooraj ko dhyan se dekho: wo gol nahi, **thoda dabaa hua** (oval) dikhta hai. Wajah: sooraj ke **neeche ke kinare** ki roshni kshitij ke sabse paas ki hawa se aati hai, jahan bending sabse zyada hai, to wo kinara zyada oopar uthta dikhta hai. **Oopar ke kinare** ki roshni thodi oonchai se aati hai, jahan bending thodi kam hai, to wo kam uthta dikhta hai. Neeche wala kinara zyada uthta hai, upar wala kam: dono ke beech ki **oonchai ghat** jaati hai. Chaudai (left se right) mein bending nahi badalti, isliye wo wahi rehti hai.

```
        .-----.                             .-----------.
       (       )                           (_____________)
        '-----'
                                    ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Oonchai par (dopahar):            Kshitij par (sunset):
  gol, width = height               chauda, par patla


```

> **Figure 1.3** – Sooraj ki dikhne wali shakal. Oonchai par gol; kshitij par neeche ka kinara zyada oopar uthne se oonchai ghat jaati hai, chaudai nahi (schematic, ghatav badha kar dikhaya gaya).

### Ek aur dhyan dene wali line

Ye sab sirf **dikhne** ka maamla hai: sooraj, taare ya grah ki asli jagah nahi badalti. Hawa sirf roshni ki aakhri disha badal deti hai.

## Wapas terrace par

Teeno sawaal ab saaf hain.

1. **Taara jhilmilata kyun?** Kyunki wo point source hai aur hawa ki hilti parton se uski roshni ka raasta har pal badalta hai, to chamak aur jagah dono thoda hilte hain.
2. **Grah kyun nahi?** Kyunki wo ek chhoti disc hai: kai raaste ek doosre ki kami poori kar dete hain.
3. **Sooraj 6:10 par kaise dikha jabki almanac mein 6:12?** Kshitij par refraction ne use lagbhag 2 minute pehle utha diya: Shalmali ne Sooraj ko tab dekha jab geometry ke hisaab se wo kshitij ke neeche tha.

Mr. Dhavale ne kaha, "Agle saal tum kisi taare ko seedha sir ke upar dekhna: wo kam jhilmilayega, kyunki raah chhoti hai." Shalmali ne sky app mein dekha: Sirius jaisa chamakdaar taara kshitij ke paas bahut tezi se rang badal-badal kar jhilmila raha tha, jabki upar ka taara aaram se chamak raha tha.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Taare isliye jhilmilate hain kyunki wo khud flicker karte hain"**

Demonstration 1 (grah se): Shalmali ki terrace par wahi sky app. Ek bahut tez chamakdaar taara aur ek grah (jaise Jupiter) dono saath dikhte hain. Taara jhilmilata hai, grah sthir hai. Dono ek hi hawa se hokar aate hain, aur dono taaron jaise chhote bindu dikhte hain. Fark sirf is baat ka hai ki taara ek bindu hai aur grah ek chhoti disc. Agar jhilmilana taare ka apna hota, to grah ki roshni ko bhi jhilmilana chahiye tha. Matlab asar hawa ka hai, srot ka nahi.

Demonstration 2 (ant-riksh se): Hubble ki ant-riksh se li gayi tasveeron mein taare **sthir** chamakte hain, hilte nahi. Wahi taare, wahi taara jo zameen se jhilmilata hai. Matlab flicker taare mein nahi, **hamari hawa mein** hai.

Demonstration 3 (chhat se): garam chhat ke upar door ki seedhi, sthir deewar bhi kaanpti dikhti hai. Wo deewar to flicker nahi kar rahi: bas beech ki hawa hil rahi hai.

**Sahi:** taare ki roshni **hamari hawa ki hilti parton** se hokar aati hai, isliye jhilmilati hai.

**Galti 2: "Hawa na hoti to sunrise ke time mein koi fark nahi padta"**

Demonstration: ek din dikhne wala sunrise 6:27 aur dikhne wala sunset 18:48 hai, yaani dikhne wala din 12 h 21 min. Refraction har ends par 2 minute ka faayda deta hai, to bina hawa ke sunrise 6:27 + 0:02 = **6:29** aur sunset 18:48 − 0:02 = **18:46**: din 18:46 − 6:29 = **12 h 17 min**. Yaani bina hawa ke din lagbhag **4 minute chhota** hota (12 h 21 min − 12 h 17 min = 4 min ✅). Hawa na ho to sooraj kshitij par tabhi dikhta jab geometry mein wo vahan hota: na jaldi, na der.

**Sahi:** hawa ki wajah se din lagbhag 4 minute lamba hota hai (har ends par lagbhag 2 minute). Bina hawa ke ye extra waqt nahi milta.

## Quick Recap

- **Atmospheric refraction:** hawa ghani aur parton mein hai. Har parat par ray normal ki taraf mudti hai, to aakhri ray zyada khadi aati hai aur taara asli jagah se **thoda oopar** dikhta hai (apparent position).
- Kshitij par shift lagbhag **0.5°** (sooraj ke disc ke barabar), seedha sir ke upar (zenith par) shift **0**.
- **Twinkling:** hawa ki hilti parton se taare ki chamak aur jagah hilti hai. Taara point source hai, isliye jhilmilata hai; grah chhoti disc hai (kai raaste), isliye aam taur par sthir. Kshitij ke paas aur kharaab hawa mein zyada jhilmilana; ant-riksh mein nahi.
- Zameen **15° per hour** ghoomti hai, yaani **1° per 4 min**. 0.5° ka shift lagbhag **2 minute**: sooraj sunrise par lagbhag 2 min pehle, sunset par lagbhag 2 min baad dikhta hai (din lagbhag 4 min lamba). Ye lagbhag figure hai, jagah ke saath badalti hai.
- Kshitij par sooraj chapta (oonchai ghati, chaudai wahi) dikhta hai. Mirage aur looming bhi hawa ki parton ke refraction se hain.
- **Galtiyan:** taare khud flicker nahi karte; hawa na ho to din lagbhag 4 min chhota hota.

## Practice Questions

1. The Earth rotates through how many degrees in 20 minutes? Use the rate 15° per hour.

2. In an imaginary planet's sky the atmospheric shift near the horizon is 2.25° (much bigger than the real Earth's). If this planet turns at the same rate as the Earth, how many minutes does it take to turn through this angle?

3. The atmospheric shift near the horizon for a star is lagbhag 0.45° at a certain night. Convert this to a time in seconds.

4. At one place, the Sun was seen 2.7 minutes before the geometric sunrise and 3.1 minutes after the geometric sunset (given data). Find (a) the total gain in the length of the day, (b) the angle corresponding to the sunrise shift.

5. For one day, the geometric sunrise is 5:52 and the geometric sunset is 18:27. Refraction gives 2.5 minutes of gain at each end (given data). Find the seen sunrise time, the seen sunset time, and the seen length of the day.

6. With the atmosphere present, the Sun is seen to rise at 6:30:00. Refraction advances the sunrise by 1.9 minutes (given data). At what time would the Sun rise geometrically?

7. Why does a star at the zenith show no atmospheric shift, even though a star at the horizon shows lagbhag 0.5°?

8. Sirius twinkles much more when it is only a few degrees above the horizon than when it is high in the sky. Explain with the path of light through the air.

9. Venus and Jupiter usually shine steadily, while Sirius twinkles. Explain using the ideas of a point source and an extended source.

10. A photograph of the setting Sun has a width of 9.0 cm and a height of 7.5 cm. By what percentage is the height smaller than the width? Which effect makes the Sun look like this?

11. State true or false and give a reason: (a) A star twinkles because its own brightness changes rapidly. (b) The Hubble Space Telescope sees steady, non-twinkling stars partly because it is above the air. (c) The refraction of light by the atmosphere changes the actual position of a star in space.

12. Through how many hours does the Earth turn while it rotates through 135°? Use the rate 15° per hour.

13. Why does a distant tree seen across a hot road or a hot roof appear to shake, although the tree is still?

### Answers

1. Given: rate = 15° per hour, t = 20 min. Find: angle. Formula: angle = rate × time. 20 min ÷ 60 min per h = 0.333 h. angle = 15° per h × 0.333 h = **5°**.
   Jaanch: 15° per h = 0.25° per min, 0.25° per min × 20 min = 5° ✅

2. Given: angle = 2.25°. Rate 15° per h, to t = 2.25° ÷ 15° per h = 0.15 h. 0.15 h × 60 min per h = **9 min**.
   Jaanch: 1° = 4 min, to 2.25° × 4 min per ° = 9 min ✅

3. Given: 0.45°. 1° = 4 min = 240 s. t = 0.45° × 240 s per ° = **108 s** (= 1.8 min, kyunki 0.45 × 4 = 1.8).
   Jaanch: 1.8 min × 60 s per min = 108 s ✅

4. (a) Total gain = 2.7 min + 3.1 min = **5.8 min**.
   (b) 1 min = 0.25°, to sunrise shift = 2.7 min × 0.25° per min = **0.675°** (lagbhag 0.68°).
   Jaanch: 0.675° ÷ (15° per h) = 0.045 h = 2.7 min ✅

5. Given: geometric sunrise 5:52, sunset 18:27, gain 2.5 min = 2 min 30 s. Seen sunrise = 5:52:00 − 0:02:30 = **5:49:30**. Seen sunset = 18:27:00 + 0:02:30 = **18:29:30**. Seen day = 18:29:30 − 5:49:30 = **12 h 40 min**. Geometric day = 18:27 − 5:52 = 12 h 35 min.
   Jaanch: 12 h 40 min − 12 h 35 min = 5 min = 2.5 min + 2.5 min ✅

6. Given: seen sunrise 6:30:00, advance 1.9 min = 1.9 × 60 s = 114 s = 1 min 54 s. Geometric sunrise = seen + advance = 6:30:00 + 0:01:54 = **6:31:54**.
   Jaanch: 6:31:54 − 0:01:54 = 6:30:00 ✅

7. Zenith par taare ki roshni parton ki seema ko seedhi (normal ke saath, incidence angle 0°) kaatti hai, to ray mudti nahi. Mudti nahi, to disha nahi badalti: shift **0**. Kshitij par ray bahut tirchhi aati hai, har parat par mudti hai aur lambi raah tay karti hai, to shift lagbhag 0.5°.

8. Kshitij ke paas taare ki roshni ko hawa mein bahut **lambi** raah tay karni padti hai: zyada parte, zyada garam-thandi hawa ke jhonke. Raasta zyada baar aur zyada hilta hai, to chamak aur jagah zyada hilti hain. Oonchai par raah chhoti hai, to kam hilta hai.

9. Taara bahut door hai: ek **point source**. Uski roshni ek hi chhote raaste se aati hai, raasta hila to poori chamak hil jaati hai. Venus aur Jupiter paas hain: chhoti **disc** (extended source), kai bindu, kai raaste, har raaste ki kami doosre poori kar dete hain, to chamak lagbhag sthir.

10. Given: width = 9.0 cm, height = 7.5 cm. Fark = 9.0 − 7.5 = 1.5 cm. Percentage = (1.5 ÷ 9.0) × 100 = **16.7%** (lagbhag). Asar: kshitij par neeche ka kinara zyada uthta dikhta hai, oonchai ghat jaati hai, chaudai wahi.
    Jaanch: 7.5 ÷ 9.0 = 0.833, 1 − 0.833 = 0.167 = 16.7% ✅

11. (a) **False** ❌. Taare ki chamak sthir hai; hawa ki hilti parton se raasta badalta hai.
    (b) **True** ✅. Hawa ke oopar hone se parton ka hilna nahi hota.
    (c) **False** ❌. Taara apni jagah par hi hai; sirf dikhne wali (apparent) disha badalti hai.

12. Given: 135°, rate 15° per hour. t = 135° ÷ 15° per h = **9 h**.
    Jaanch: 15° per h × 9 h = 135° ✅

13. Garam sateh ke paas hawa ke jhonke uthte hain, to density aur refractive index tezi se badalte hain. Ped se aati roshni ka raasta har pal badalta hai, to uski dikhne wali jagah hilti hai. Ped hil nahi raha: hawa hil rahi hai.
