# Topic 2: Fossil Fuels Aur Thermal Power: Koyle Se Bijli Tak

Topic 1 mein humne seekha ki accha fuel kaise parakhte hain: calorific value (kJ/kg), store aur transport mein aasaani, daam, safety aur pollution; aur strot ko renewable ya non-renewable mein kaise baantte hain. Ab chapter ka sabse bada non-renewable strot andar se dekhte hain: **fossil fuels**. Class 9 mein ([work-energy-and-power-part3.md](../../../class9/physics/work-energy-and-power/work-energy-and-power-part3.md)) humne itna jaana tha ki koyla, petrol aur diesel purane paudhon-janwaron se bane hain, aur wahan likha tha ki inki poori kahani Class 10 mein hai. Wahi kahani ab shuru hoti hai: ye kaise bane, inse bijli kaise banti hai, kitni energy raaste mein kho jaati hai, aur iski keemat hawa aur zameen kaise chukati hain.

## Bus ki khidki se dikhe cooling tower

School ki trip ki bus ek lambi seedhi sadak par chal rahi thi. Shantanu khidki wali seat par tha, aur uske Chacha, Mr. Padhye, jo trip mein volunteer banke saath aaye the, uske paas baithe the. Achanak door khet ke upar teen-chaar bahut bade, kamar se patle aur upar se chaude gol tower dikhe, aur unke upar safed baadal ki tarah kuch uth raha tha. Unke peeche ek bahut ooncha, patla sa chimney bhi tha.

"Chacha, ye bade bade chimney dhuan chhod rahe hain! Kitna dhuan hai!" Shantanu ne kaha. Phir usne sadak ke saath saath chalti ek maal-gaadi dekhi: kaale koyle se bhare wagon, ek ke peeche ek. Usne ginna shuru kiya: ek, do, teen... **58 wagon**. Aur jaise hi wo gaadi bus se aage nikli, uske peeche ek aur lambi gaadi aati dikhi.

Chacha muskuraye. "Ginti rakho, Shantanu, aaj kaam aayegi. Par pehle ek baat theek kar lo: wo moti, gol cheezein **cooling tower** hain, aur unse jo safed cheez nikal rahi hai wo dhuan nahi, bhaap aur paani ki bahut baareek boondein hain. Asli dhuan wala chimney wo patla, ooncha wala hai." Shantanu ne poocha, "To ye sab hai kya? Aur itna koyla ye roz kyun maangta hai? Chacha, plant bijli **banata** hai na?"

Chacha ne khidki ke bahar dekhte hue kaha, "Ye hai ek **thermal power plant**. Aur tumhare aakhri sawaal ka jawab hum aaj dhoondhenge, kyunki wo word 'banata' sunne mein sahi lagta hai par physics mein thoda gadbad hai. Pehle ye bata: ek wagon mein lagbhag 60 tonne coal aata hai, aur aise rake roz aate hain. Itna koyla jalkar aakhir milta kya hai, aur baaki sab kahan jaata hai?"

Chalo, koyle ki shuruaat se shuru karte hain: wo kahan se aaya, phir plant mein kya hota hai.

## Part A: Fossil fuels kaise bane

"Fossil" ka matlab hai zameen se khod kar nikala gaya, purana jama hua. Teeno fossil fuels ki kahani ek jaisi hai: **Suraj ki energy se jeevon ne apne andar carbon wali energy banayi, wo jeev mar kar dab gaye, aur karodon saal ki garmi aur dabav ne unhe fuel bana diya.** Isiliye Class 9 mein kaha tha ki sabki jad Suraj hai.

**Coal (koyla).** Lagbhag 30 crore saal pehle dharti par dalaldali jungle the. Paudhe marte rahe, geele keechad mein girte rahe, aur wahan oxygen kam hone ki wajah se wo poori tarah galte nahi the. Upar keechad aur mitti ki parat par parat jamti gayi. Dabav aur garmi se pehle spongy **peat** bana, phir dheere dheere, kai chaaron (peat, lignite, bituminous, anthracite) mein, kadak aur zyaada carbon wala coal. Isliye coal ki ek parat ke peeche karodon saal ki mehnat hai.

**Petroleum aur natural gas.** Purane samundar mein bahut sare chhote jeev (plankton, algae) rehte the. Ye marke samundar ke tale mein keechad aur reth ke neeche dabte gaye. Karodon saal ki garmi aur dabav se unse **crude oil** bana, aur uske saath gas, jo mukhya roop se **methane** hai. Tel aur gas halke hain, isliye wo spongy (porous) chattan ke chhote chhote khaali hisson mein oopar uthte gaye, jab tak koi thos, tel na jaane dene wali chattan (**cap rock**) ne unhe rok nahi liya. Wahin ye jama ho jaate hain: sabse oopar gas, uske neeche tel, uske neeche khaara paani. Hum us jagah tak kuan khod kar (well) unhe bahar laate hain. Crude oil ko refinery mein saaf karke LPG, petrol, kerosene aur diesel milte hain (LPG ke source ki baat [carbon-and-its-compounds-part4.md](../../chemistry/carbon-and-its-compounds/carbon-and-its-compounds-part4.md) mein hai).

```
 [ Ped-paudhe, jungle ]             [ Samundar ke chhote jeev ]
            v mitti mein dab gaye                v reth ke neeche dab gaye
 [ Peat ]                           [ Keechad ki parat ]
            v dabav, garmi, crore saal           v dabav, garmi, crore saal
 [ COAL (koyla) ]                   [ Tel aur Natural gas ]
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Fossil fuels ki do kahaniyan: coal paudhon se, tel aur gas samundar ke chhote jeevon se. Dono mein jeev dabte hain, aur dabav, garmi aur karodon saal baaki kaam karte hain.

```
Zameen ki sataha ========================[ rig ]=====
                                            :
Cap rock: tel-gas rokne wali thos chattan   :
------------------------------------------  :
Natural gas : sabse halki, sabse oopar      :
Crude oil   : beech mein                    :
Khaara paani: sabse neeche
------------------------------------------
Porous chattan: spongy, khaali jagah wali
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Tel aur gas ka jamaav (side view, scale ke hisaab se nahi). Cap rock ke neeche halki gas oopar, uske neeche crude oil, aur sabse neeche khaara paani. Kuan (well) cap rock ko paar karke tel tak jaata hai.

Ye kahani ek zaroori sabak deti hai: **fossil fuel banne mein karodon saal lagte hain, par hum use kuch sau saal mein jala rahe hain** (Part G mein hum ise number mein dekhenge). Isiliye ye non-renewable hain.

## Part B: Thermal power plant: koyle se bijli tak

Topic 1 mein humne koyle ki calorific value dekhi thi: good coal lagbhag 30,000 kJ/kg. Ye chemical energy hai. Plant ka kaam hai us chemical energy ko **bijli (electrical energy)** mein badalna. Raaste mein energy kai roop badalti hai (Class 9 ke energy conversion ki hi tarah, bas ek lambi chain):

**chemical energy (coal)  →  heat (boiler)  →  kinetic energy (ghoomti turbine)  →  electrical energy (generator)**

Ab ek ek hissa dekhte hain, Figure 2.3 saath rakhna.

```
[ Coal store ]
       v coal, powder bana kar
[ Boiler ] --- hot gas, fly ash --->        [ Chimney ]
       v high-pressure steam
[ Turbine ] --- used steam --->             [ Condenser ]
       v shaft ghoomta hai                (thanda wapas, garam aage)
[ Generator ]                               [ Cooling tower ]
       v bijli (electricity)                (pump: paani wapas boiler ko)
[ Transformer ] --- grid ke taar --->
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Thermal power plant ka block diagram. Coal powder banakar boiler mein jalta hai; garam gas aur fly ash chimney ki taraf jaati hain (zyaadatar fly ash filter mein pehle hi pakad li jaati hai). Bhaap turbine ko ghumati hai, turbine generator ko. Istemaal hui bhaap condenser mein wapas paani banti hai; condenser ka garam paani cooling tower mein thanda hota hai, aur pump se condensed paani wapas boiler mein jaata hai.

1. **Coal handling.** Wagon se koyla belt par jaata hai, mills mein baareek powder banta hai. Powder ke baareek kan hawa ko zyaada surface par chhoote hain, isliye wo tezi se aur poora jalte hain.
2. **Boiler.** Koyla bahut garam jalta hai. Garam gas pipes mein bhare paani ko garam karti hai, jisse paani **bahut zyaada dabav wali bhaap** ban jaata hai (lagbhag 500 °C se bhi zyaada garam). Bachi hui garam gas aur fly ash chimney ki taraf jaate hain.
3. **Turbine.** Bhaap blades par zor se takrati hai, jaise hawa chakri ko ghumati hai. Turbine ki shaft tez ghoomti hai. Yahan **heat se kinetic energy** banti hai.
4. **Generator.** Shaft se juda generator ghoomti shaft ki energy ko bijli mein badalta hai. Kaise, ye Chapter 9 (Electromagnetism) mein dekhenge; abhi bas itna ki ye machine **kinetic se electrical** badalti hai.
5. **Condenser aur cooling tower.** Turbine se nikli bhaap ko wapas paani banana padta hai. Isse sirf paani nahi bachta: turbine ke peeche dabav bahut kam rehta hai, to bhaap andar se bahar "bhaagti" rehti hai aur turbine ko dhakka milta rehta hai. Bhaap ki bachi garmi condenser ke thande paani ko garam kar deti hai. Wo garam paani cooling tower mein neeche girte hue hawa se thanda hota hai: kuch paani **vaashpit** hota hai aur baaki ko thanda karta hai. Wahi vaashp tower ke upar safed baadal jaisi dikhti hai.
6. **Transformer aur grid.** Bijli ko transformer se transmission lines mein bheja jaata hai, jo ghar, factory aur rail tak jaati hain.

**Ek zaroori sawaal:** bhaap ki bachi hui garmi ko bhi kaam mein kyun nahi laa lete? Wo garmi bahut kam temperature par hoti hai (condenser ke paani se thodi hi zyada), isliye usse bahut kam kaam nikalta hai. Is par poori baat Class 11 mein aayegi. Abhi yaad rakho: **is garmi ka bada hissa bahar phenkna hi padta hai**, aur wo cooling tower se jaata hai.

## Part C: Energy ki chain aur efficiency

Class 9 ([work-energy-and-power-part7.md](../../../class9/physics/work-energy-and-power/work-energy-and-power-part7.md)) mein efficiency = (useful output ÷ input) × 100 percent thi. Plant ki chain mein har step ki apni efficiency hoti hai, aur **poori chain ki efficiency in sab ka guna** hoti hai. Ek typical thermal plant ke liye (sab lagbhag, samjhane ke liye):

| Step | Kya hota hai | Efficiency (lagbhag) |
|---|---|---|
| Boiler | Coal ki chemical energy se bhaap ki heat | 88 percent (12 percent chimney ki gas aur raakh mein) |
| Turbine | Bhaap ki heat se shaft ki kinetic energy | 41 percent (baaki condenser aur cooling tower mein) |
| Generator | Shaft ki energy se bijli | 97 percent |

### Example 1: Poori chain ki efficiency

Coal mein 100 unit energy hai. Upar ki teen efficiency lekar bijli kitni milti hai, aur har step par kitna jata hai?

**Given:** boiler 88 percent, turbine 41 percent, generator 97 percent; coal mein energy 100 unit
**Find:** bijli ki energy, aur har jagah ka nuksaan
**Formula:** overall efficiency = boiler × turbine × generator
**Substitution:** bhaap ki heat = 100 × 0.88 = 88 unit; shaft ki energy = 88 × 0.41 = 36.08 unit; bijli = 36.08 × 0.97 = 35.0 unit
**Answer:** overall efficiency lagbhag **35 percent**, yaani 100 unit mein se 35 unit bijli. Nuksaan: chimney aur raakh 12, condenser aur cooling tower lagbhag 52, generator lagbhag 1.
**Jaanch:** 0.88 × 0.41 × 0.97 = 0.35 (lagbhag) ✅ aur 35 + 12 + 52 + 1 = 100 unit ✅ (energy conserved).

```
Coal mein energy = 100 unit (ek # = 1 unit)
Bijli (kaam ki)           ###################################  35
Condenser, cooling tower  ####################################################  52
Chimney, raakh (boiler)   ############  12
Generator ki garmi        #  1
```

> **Figure 2.4** – Coal ki 100 unit energy ka batwara. Bijli sirf 35 unit hai. Sabse bada hissa (52 unit) condenser aur cooling tower se vaayu mein bahar chala jaata hai. Ek # barabar 1 unit.

### Example 2: Ek unit bijli ke liye coal

1 unit (1 kWh) bijli banane ke liye kitna coal lagega? Plant ki efficiency lagbhag 35 percent, coal ki calorific value 30,000 kJ/kg (30 MJ/kg) lo.

**Given:** bijli = 1 kWh = 3.6 MJ; efficiency = 35 percent = 0.35; calorific value = 30 MJ/kg
**Find:** coal ka mass
**Formula:** coal ki zaroori energy = bijli ÷ efficiency; coal ka mass = energy ÷ calorific value
**Substitution:** energy = 3.6 MJ ÷ 0.35 = 10.29 MJ; mass = 10.29 MJ ÷ 30 MJ/kg = 0.343 kg
**Answer:** lagbhag **0.34 kg coal** (340 g) per unit. Ulta dekho to 1 tonne coal lagbhag 1000 ÷ 0.343 = **2917 unit** bijli deta hai.
**Jaanch:** 0.343 kg × 30 MJ/kg = 10.29 MJ, aur 10.29 MJ × 0.35 = 3.6 MJ ✅. Units: MJ ÷ (MJ/kg) = kg ✅.

**Ek imaandaar baat:** Bharat mein jo coal asal mein jalta hai wo aksar yahan ke "good coal" (30,000 kJ/kg) se kamzor hota hai, uski raakh bhi zyaada hoti hai (lagbhag 30 se 40 percent). Isiliye asli plants lagbhag 0.6 kg ya usse zyaada coal per unit jalate hain. Is topic ke sab hisaab mein hum hamesha 30,000 kJ/kg aur 35 percent hi lenge, taaki tulna saaf rahe.

## Part D: Plant ke pamaane par hisaab

Plant ki "capacity" **power** hoti hai (MW mein), energy nahi (Class 9 ke power wale topic mein: 1 MW = 10⁶ W). Power ko time se guna karo to energy milti hai: 1 kW ki power 1 ghante mein 1 kWh deti hai, to 1 MW ki power 1 ghante mein 1000 kWh (1 MWh).

### Example 3: 100 MW ka plant aur wagon

Ek 100 MW ka plant poori power se 1 din (24 ghante) chalta hai. Efficiency 35 percent, coal 30 MJ/kg. Coal per ghanta, per din, kitni units bijli, aur kitne 60 tonne ke wagon?

**Given:** P = 100 MW = 10⁸ W; t = 1 h = 3600 s (aur 24 h); efficiency 0.35; calorific value 30 MJ/kg; wagon = 60 tonne
**Find:** coal per ghanta, coal per din, units per din, wagon ki ginti
**Formula:** bijli E = P × t; coal ka mass = E ÷ (efficiency × calorific value)
**Substitution:** ek ghante mein E = 10⁸ W × 3600 s = 3.6 × 10¹¹ J = 360,000 MJ; coal = 360,000 MJ ÷ (0.35 × 30 MJ/kg) = 34,286 kg lagbhag **34.3 tonne per ghanta**. Din bhar mein 34.29 × 24 = **822.9 tonne**; units = 100,000 kW × 24 h = **2.4 × 10⁶ kWh**; wagon = 822.9 ÷ 60 = **13.7**, yaani lagbhag 14 wagon roz.
**Answer:** lagbhag 34.3 t per ghanta, 823 t per din, 2.4 × 10⁶ unit per din, aur roz lagbhag 14 wagon.
**Jaanch:** 823 t ÷ 2.4 × 10⁶ unit = 0.343 kg per unit, wahi jo Example 2 mein aaya ✅. Story ka 58 wagon ka rake (58 × 60 = 3480 t) is plant ko 3480 ÷ 822.9 = lagbhag 4.2 din chalayega ✅.

Isi wajah se coal plants ke paas koyle ke rake roz aate rehte hain: 100 MW jaise chhote plant ko bhi roz lagbhag 14 wagon chahiye.

## Part E: Petrol engine aur gaadi

Gaadi ka engine bhi ek chhota "plant" hai: fuel jal kar garm gas banti hai, wo piston ko dhakelti hai, aur piston se pahiye ghoomte hain. Par yahan **petrol engine ki efficiency lagbhag 25 percent** hoti hai: yaani petrol ki chemical energy ka sirf lagbhag chauthayi hissa pahiyon tak pahunchta hai.

```
Petrol mein energy = 100 unit (ek # = 2.5 unit)
Pahiyon tak (kaam ki)     ##########  25
Engine ki garmi + dhuan   ##############################  75
```

> **Figure 2.5** – Petrol engine mein 100 unit energy ka batwara: lagbhag 25 unit kaam ki, baaki 75 unit exhaust ki garam gas aur engine ki garmi (radiator, hawa) ke roop mein bahar. Ek # barabar 2.5 unit.

### Example 4: Engine ko petrol kitna chahiye?

Ek petrol engine ko 3 kg petrol milta hai (petrol 45 MJ/kg, efficiency 25 percent). Kaam ki energy aur bekaar gayi garmi kitni?

**Given:** m = 3 kg; calorific value = 45 MJ/kg; efficiency = 0.25
**Find:** useful energy aur waste energy
**Formula:** input = m × calorific value; useful = efficiency × input
**Substitution:** input = 3 × 45 = 135 MJ; useful = 0.25 × 135 = 33.75 MJ; waste = 135 − 33.75 = 101.25 MJ
**Answer:** useful **33.75 MJ**; waste lagbhag **101 MJ**.
**Jaanch:** 33.75 + 101.25 = 135 MJ ✅. Ulta: 9 MJ ki useful energy ke liye petrol 9 ÷ (0.25 × 45) = 0.8 kg ✅ (kam efficiency, to zyaada petrol).

## Part F: LPG aur CNG: saaf, par fossil

LPG petroleum se aur CNG natural gas se milti hai ([carbon-and-its-compounds-part4.md](../../chemistry/carbon-and-its-compounds/carbon-and-its-compounds-part4.md)). **Ye bhi fossil fuels hain**, bas ye zyaada saafi se jalti hain: dhuan aur soot kam, sulphur lagbhag nahi. Class 9 ([atmospheric-pollution-part6.md](../../../class9/chemistry/atmospheric-pollution/atmospheric-pollution-part6.md)) mein ek **kilo** fuel ki CO₂ tulna thi: 1 kg methane (CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O) se 44 ÷ 16 = 2.75 kg CO₂. Wahan likha tha ki **barabar energy** ki tulna thodi aur behtar hogi. Ab wo karte hain.

### Example 5: Barabar energy par CO₂

Coal ko lagbhag 75 percent carbon wala maan lo (ye is example ki maani hui value hai; asli coal mein ye alag-alag hota hai). Atomic masses: C = 12, O = 16, H = 1. Coal (30 MJ/kg) aur CNG ki methane (50 MJ/kg) mein 1 MJ energy par kitni CO₂ nikalti hai?

**Given:** coal mein carbon 75 percent; C + O₂ → CO₂ (12 g C se 44 g CO₂); CH₄ se 44 ÷ 16 = 2.75 kg CO₂ per kg
**Find:** CO₂ ka mass per MJ dono ke liye, aur unka anupaat
**Formula:** CO₂ per MJ = (CO₂ per kg fuel) ÷ (MJ per kg fuel)
**Substitution:** coal: 1 kg mein 0.75 kg carbon, CO₂ = 0.75 × 44 ÷ 12 = 2.75 kg; per MJ = 2.75 ÷ 30 = 0.0917 kg. Methane: 2.75 ÷ 50 = 0.055 kg per MJ. Anupaat = 0.055 ÷ 0.0917 = 0.6.
**Answer:** coal lagbhag 0.092 kg CO₂ per MJ, methane lagbhag 0.055 kg per MJ: barabar energy par CNG lagbhag **40 percent kam** CO₂ deti hai.
**Jaanch:** 0.75 × 44 = 33; 33 ÷ 12 = 2.75 ✅; 0.6 × 0.0917 = 0.055 ✅. Dono ek kilo mein lagbhag 2.75 kg CO₂ dete hain, par methane ek kilo mein zyaada energy deti hai, isliye per MJ kam. Phir bhi 0.055 kg CO₂ **shoonya nahi** hai.

## Part G: Keemat: pradushan aur kam hote bhandar

### Pollution ka hisaab

| Kya nikalta hai | Kahan se aata hai | Kya asar |
|---|---|---|
| CO₂ | Fuel ka carbon jal kar | Greenhouse effect, global warming ([atmospheric-pollution-part3.md](../../../class9/chemistry/atmospheric-pollution/atmospheric-pollution-part3.md)) |
| SO₂ | Fuel mein maujood sulphur (S + O₂ → SO₂) | Acid rain ([atmospheric-pollution-part2.md](../../../class9/chemistry/atmospheric-pollution/atmospheric-pollution-part2.md)), saans ki takleef; scrubber isse roke |
| NOx | Bahut garam hawa mein nitrogen aur oxygen ke milne se | Smog aur acid rain |
| Fly ash | Coal ki nahi jalne wali mitti-patthar | Phephdon ke liye nuksaan; filter aur ash pond; kuch cement-eent mein kaam |
| Garam paani aur bhaap | Cooling system se | Nadi ya jheel ka paani garam, hawa mein bhaap |

Tel ke saath ek aur khatra hai: **oil spill**. Samundar mein jahaaz ya well se tel phail jaye to machhli, samundari panchhi aur tat bahut nuksaan uthate hain. Aur koyla khodne (mining) se zameen ko bhi chot lagti hai.

### Bhandar kitne?

Zameen ke neeche kitna bacha hai, iska anuman badalta rehta hai. Aksar bataya jaata hai ki aaj ki raftaar se tel aur natural gas lagbhag 50 saal aur coal lagbhag 130 saal ke liye bacha hai (naye kuan aur khadaan milne se ye badal sakta hai). Ek aur dikkat: ye bhandar kuch hi deshon mein zyaada hain, isliye kai desh doosre desh ke tel par depend hain. Bharat apne kachche tel ki zaroorat ka bada hissa (lagbhag 85 se 90 percent) bahar se mangata hai.

### Example 6: Ek coalfield kitne saal chalega?

Ek coalfield mein lagbhag 600 million tonne coal hai. Example 3 ka 100 MW plant roz 822.9 tonne jalata hai. Ek plant, aur phir aise 100 plant, field ko kitne saal mein khatam karenge? (1 saal = 365 din)

**Given:** bhandar = 600 million tonne = 6 × 10⁸ t; ek plant ka coal = 822.9 t per din
**Find:** saal, ek plant aur 100 plant ke liye
**Formula:** saal = bhandar ÷ (coal per din × 365)
**Substitution:** ek saal mein ek plant = 822.9 × 365 = 300,343 t lagbhag 3.0 × 10⁵ t; saal = 6 × 10⁸ ÷ 3.0 × 10⁵ = 1998 saal. 100 plant ke liye coal 100 guna, to 1998 ÷ 100 = 19.98 saal
**Answer:** ek plant ko lagbhag **2000 saal**, 100 plant ko lagbhag **20 saal**.
**Jaanch:** 3.0 × 10⁵ t × 1998 = 6.0 × 10⁸ t ✅. Plant badhne se saal ghate: units sahi ✅.

### Example 7: Banne ki raftaar vs jalne ki raftaar

Maan lo ek fuel jamaav banne mein lagbhag 30 crore (3 × 10⁸) saal laga aur hum use lagbhag 300 saal mein jala dete hain. Jalna banne se kitne guna tez hai?

**Given:** banne ka time = 3 × 10⁸ saal; jalne ka time = 300 saal
**Find:** raftaar ka anupaat
**Formula:** anupaat = banne ka time ÷ jalne ka time
**Substitution:** 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 300 = 10⁶
**Answer:** hum use lagbhag **10 lakh guna tez** jala rahe hain.
**Jaanch:** 10⁶ × 300 = 3 × 10⁸ ✅. Isliye "non-renewable": banne ka intezaar insaani zindagi mein sambhav nahi.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti: "Power plant bijli ya energy banata hai"**

Demonstration 1: Example 1 dekho. Coal mein 100 unit energy thi aur bijli sirf 35 unit mili. Baaki 65 unit kahin khatam nahi hui: 12 chimney aur raakh mein, 52 cooling tower ke raaste mein hawa aur paani ki garmi bankar gayi, 1 generator ki garmi. 35 + 65 = 100. Plant ne kuch **banaya** nahi: usne ek roop (chemical) ko doosre roop (electrical) mein **badla** aur saath mein bahut sa hissa garmi mein phailaya. Class 9 ke conservation of energy ka yahi rule: energy na banti hai na khatam hoti hai.

Demonstration 2: Dono haath 30 second zor se aapas mein ragdo (ghar par adult ke saath). Haath garam ho jaate hain. Tumne garmi "banayi"? Nahi: tumhari maspeshiyon ki chemical energy (khane se) pehle kaam ki energy bani, aur ragad se garmi mein badal gayi.

Demonstration 3: Ek 660 MW ke plant ke baare mein "660 MW bijli banata hai" kehna bhi sahi shabd nahi. 660 MW uski bijli dene ki **dar** (power) hai. Din bhar mein 660,000 kW × 24 h = 15,840,000 kWh energy milti hai, aur wo koyle ki energy se aati hai (Example 3 jaisa hisaab: coal roz hazaaron tonne).

**Sahi:** plant energy ko **badalta** hai; bijli ki har unit ke peeche coal ki lagbhag 2.9 guna energy lagti hai, jisme se lagbhag 65 percent garmi ban kar bahar jaati hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Fossil fuel** (coal, petroleum, natural gas) karodon saal pehle ke jeevon se bana; dabav, garmi aur lamba time zaroori. Non-renewable hai.
- **Thermal plant** chain: chemical (coal) → heat (boiler) → kinetic (turbine) → electrical (generator). Bhaap condenser mein wapas paani banti hai; cooling tower garam paani ko thanda karta hai.
- **Poori efficiency = steps ka guna:** 0.88 × 0.41 × 0.97 ≈ 0.35. Lagbhag 35 percent bijli, 65 percent garmi.
- **Coal per unit:** 3.6 MJ ÷ (0.35 × 30 MJ/kg) ≈ 0.34 kg; asli coal kamzor, to lagbhag 0.6 kg ya zyaada.
- Plant ki **capacity power** (MW) hai; energy = P × t.
- **Petrol engine** lagbhag 25 percent efficient: baaki garmi aur exhaust.
- **LPG aur CNG** saaf hain par fossil hain; barabar energy par CNG lagbhag 40 percent kam CO₂ deti hai (shoonya nahi).
- **Pradushan:** CO₂, SO₂ (acid rain), NOx, fly ash, oil spill, garam paani.
- Bharat ki bijli ka lagbhag **70 percent** coal se.
- Plant energy **badalta** hai, **banata** nahi.

## Practice Questions

1. A 500 MW thermal power plant runs at full power for one day. Its efficiency is 35 percent and the coal has a calorific value of 30,000 kJ/kg. Find the coal burnt in tonnes and the electrical energy produced in kWh.

2. A 210 MW unit runs at full power. Taking the same efficiency and coal as in Question 1, find the coal burnt per hour and per day (in tonnes), and the electricity produced per day in kWh.

3. A diesel generator of 5 kW runs for 6 hours. It converts 30 percent of the diesel's energy into electricity, and diesel has a calorific value of 45 MJ/kg. Find the mass of diesel burnt and the energy lost as heat.

4. A car runs 15 km per litre of petrol, and one litre of petrol has about 32 MJ of energy. For a trip of 90 km find the petrol used, the energy in that petrol, and the useful energy reaching the wheels if the engine is 25 percent efficient (also in kWh).

5. A power plant burns 1800 tonnes of coal per day, and the coal has 0.6 percent sulphur by mass. Find the mass of sulphur and the mass of SO₂ formed per day if all the sulphur burns (S = 32, O = 16).

6. In a plant the boiler is 80 percent efficient, the turbine stage 38 percent and the generator 95 percent. Find the overall efficiency and the coal needed per unit (kWh) of electricity at 30,000 kJ/kg.

7. A household uses 240 units of electricity in a month. How much coal does a plant of 35 percent efficiency (30,000 kJ/kg coal) burn to supply it?

8. A 400 MW power plant has an efficiency of 35 percent. Find the rate at which fuel energy enters (in MW) and the rate at which energy is lost as heat. Where does most of it go?

9. A coal sample of 40 kg contains 60 percent carbon by mass. Find the mass of CO₂ formed when all the carbon burns (C = 12, O = 16). Is the CO₂ mass more or less than the coal mass?

10. A fossil fuel deposit took about 60 million years to form and a country burns it in 120 years. How many times faster is the burning than the forming?

11. A petrol engine gives 15 kW of useful power and is 25 percent efficient. Find the power of the fuel burning, the power lost as heat, and the petrol (45 MJ/kg) used per hour.

12. State true or false, with a reason:
    (a) A power plant creates energy.
    (b) The white cloud above a cooling tower is smoke from burning coal.
    (c) CNG is a fossil fuel.
    (d) A 500 MW plant running for 2 hours gives 1000 MWh.

13. Why must a thermal power plant have a condenser and a cooling tower even though they "waste" heat? Answer in three or four sentences.

14. Which of these are fossil fuels: coal, wood, petrol, biogas, diesel, LPG, cow-dung cake, CNG?

### Answers

1. **Given:** P = 500 MW = 5 × 10⁸ W; t = 24 h; efficiency 0.35; calorific value 30 MJ/kg. **Find:** coal mass, electrical energy. **Formula:** E = P × t; coal = E ÷ (efficiency × calorific value). **Substitution:** E = 5 × 10⁸ W × 86,400 s = 4.32 × 10¹³ J = 4.32 × 10⁷ MJ; coal = 4.32 × 10⁷ ÷ (0.35 × 30) = 4.114 × 10⁶ kg. Units: 500,000 kW × 24 h = 1.2 × 10⁷ kWh. **Answer:** lagbhag **4114 tonne coal** aur **1.2 × 10⁷ kWh**. **Jaanch:** 4114 t = 4.114 × 10⁶ kg, aur 4.114 × 10⁶ kg ÷ 1.2 × 10⁷ unit = 0.343 kg per unit ✅.

2. **Given:** P = 210 MW; efficiency 0.35; 30 MJ/kg. **Formula:** coal = P × t ÷ (efficiency × calorific value). **Substitution:** ek ghante mein E = 2.1 × 10⁸ W × 3600 s = 7.56 × 10¹¹ J = 7.56 × 10⁵ MJ; coal = 7.56 × 10⁵ ÷ 10.5 = 72,000 kg. Din mein 72 × 24 = 1728 t. Units: 210,000 kW × 24 h = 5.04 × 10⁶ kWh. **Answer:** **72 tonne per ghanta**, **1728 tonne per din**, **5.04 × 10⁶ kWh per din**. **Jaanch:** 1728 ÷ 4114 = 0.42 = 210 ÷ 500 ✅ (coal power ke anupaat mein).

3. **Given:** P = 5 kW; t = 6 h; efficiency 0.30; 45 MJ/kg. **Formula:** electricity = P × t; fuel energy = electricity ÷ efficiency; mass = fuel energy ÷ 45. **Substitution:** E = 5 × 6 = 30 kWh = 30 × 3.6 = 108 MJ; fuel energy = 108 ÷ 0.30 = 360 MJ; mass = 360 ÷ 45 = 8 kg; heat = 360 − 108 = 252 MJ. **Answer:** **8 kg diesel**, **252 MJ** garmi mein gayi. **Jaanch:** 8 × 45 × 0.30 = 108 MJ ✅.

4. **Given:** 15 km/L; 32 MJ/L; 90 km; efficiency 0.25. **Substitution:** petrol = 90 ÷ 15 = 6 L; energy = 6 × 32 = 192 MJ; useful = 0.25 × 192 = 48 MJ = 48 ÷ 3.6 = 13.3 kWh. **Answer:** **6 L**, **192 MJ**, useful **48 MJ** (lagbhag **13.3 kWh**). **Jaanch:** energy per km = 32 ÷ 15 = 2.13 MJ, 2.13 × 90 = 192 ✅.

5. **Given:** coal = 1800 t; sulphur 0.6 percent; S + O₂ → SO₂. **Substitution:** S = 1800 × 0.006 = 10.8 t; 32 g S se 64 g SO₂, to SO₂ = 10.8 × 64 ÷ 32 = 21.6 t. **Answer:** **10.8 tonne sulphur**, **21.6 tonne SO₂** per din. **Jaanch:** SO₂ ka mass sulphur ka doguna (64 ÷ 32 = 2) ✅.

6. **Formula:** overall = 0.80 × 0.38 × 0.95 = 0.2888. **Answer:** overall lagbhag **28.9 percent**. Coal per unit = 3.6 ÷ (0.2888 × 30) = 0.4155 kg lagbhag **0.42 kg**. **Jaanch:** 0.2888 × 30 × 0.4155 = 3.6 MJ ✅ (35 percent wale 0.34 kg se zyaada, kyunki efficiency kam).

7. 240 unit × 0.343 kg per unit = **82.3 kg** coal lagbhag. **Jaanch:** 82.3 kg × 30 MJ/kg × 0.35 = 864 MJ aur 240 × 3.6 = 864 MJ ✅.

8. **Formula:** input power = output ÷ efficiency. **Substitution:** 400 ÷ 0.35 = 1142.9 MW; loss = 1142.9 − 400 = 742.9 MW. **Answer:** lagbhag **1143 MW** andar, lagbhag **743 MW** garmi. Zyaadatar condenser aur cooling tower (aur kuch chimney) ke raaste vaayu mein jaati hai. **Jaanch:** 400 + 743 = 1143 MW ✅.

9. **Given:** coal 40 kg; carbon 60 percent. **Substitution:** C = 24 kg; CO₂ = 24 × 44 ÷ 12 = 88 kg. **Answer:** **88 kg CO₂**, jo coal ke 40 kg se **zyaada** hai (oxygen hawa se judti hai). **Jaanch:** 88 − 24 = 64 kg oxygen = 24 × 32 ÷ 12 ✅.

10. **Formula:** anupaat = 60,000,000 ÷ 120 = **5 × 10⁵**. Jalna banne se lagbhag **5 lakh guna** tez hai. **Jaanch:** 5 × 10⁵ × 120 = 6 × 10⁷ ✅.

11. **Formula:** input = output ÷ efficiency. **Substitution:** 15 ÷ 0.25 = 60 kW; heat = 60 − 15 = 45 kW; ek ghante mein 60,000 W × 3600 s = 2.16 × 10⁸ J = 216 MJ; petrol = 216 ÷ 45 = 4.8 kg. **Answer:** **60 kW**, **45 kW**, **4.8 kg per ghanta**. **Jaanch:** 4.8 kg × 45 MJ/kg × 0.25 = 54 MJ = 15 kW × 3600 s ✅.

12. (a) **False** ❌: energy banti nahi, badalti hai; coal ki energy bijli aur zyaadatar garmi mein jaati hai. (b) **False** ❌: wo mostly paani ki baareek boondein aur bhaap hai; dhuan chimney se nikalta hai. (c) **True** ✅: CNG natural gas se aati hai, jo fossil fuel hai. (d) **True** ✅: 500 MW × 2 h = 1000 MWh.

13. Turbine ko bhaap se dhakka tabhi milta hai jab bhaap turbine ke aage kam dabav wali jagah ki taraf nikal sake, isliye bhaap ko condenser mein wapas paani banana padta hai. Is process mein bhaap ki garmi condenser ke paani mein chali jaati hai, aur wo garam paani cooling tower mein hawa se thanda hota hai, taaki wahi paani dobara kaam aa sake. Isliye ye heat sirf "waste" nahi, balki plant ke chalne ka zaroori hissa hai. Aur kam temperature ki wajah se is garmi se aur bijli nahi nikaali ja sakti.

14. **Fossil fuels:** coal, petrol, diesel, LPG, CNG. **Fossil nahi:** wood, biogas, cow-dung cake (ye jeevon ke taaza hisse ya unse bane hain, karodon saal purane nahi). **Jaanch:** 5 + 3 = 8 ✅.
