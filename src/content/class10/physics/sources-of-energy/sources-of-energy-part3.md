# Topic 3: Hydro, Wind Aur Tidal: Pani Aur Hawa Ki Taqat

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki thermal power plant mein koyla jalta hai, paani bhaap banta hai, bhaap **turbine** ghumati hai aur turbine se jude **generator** se bijli milti hai. Is poori kahani mein sabse bhaari cheez thi **jalta hua eendhan**: wo mehenga bhi hai aur dhuan bhi chhodta hai. Ab ek seedha sawaal: turbine ko ghumane ke liye bhaap hi kyun? Agar koi cheez pehle se hi **chal rahi** ho, jaise girta hua paani, behti hawa ya samundar ki tide, to usse turbine seedha ghumaya ja sakta hai. Na koyla, na boiler. Is topic mein hum ye teen strot dekhenge, aur har ek ka **hisaab** bhi lagayenge: kitni power milegi, aur kis cheez par wo nirbhar karti hai.

## Monsoon mein dam ki sair

Mr. Sardesai apni bhatiji Shrinidhi ko monsoon ki ek subah pahaadi ghati ke ek dam par le gaye. Raste mein siren ki tarah ek chetavani baj chuki thi: "Dam ke gate khole ja rahe hain, nadi ke kinare na jaayein." Dam ke upar se unhone dekha: gate ke neeche se paani **garajta hua** bahar nikal raha tha, dhund jaisi phuhaar upar tak aa rahi thi, aur nadi neeche ki taraf bhoori aur tez ho gayi thi.

Par Shrinidhi ka dhyan kisi aur cheez par tha. Dam ke bilkul neeche ek lambi imaarat se ek **gehri gungunahat** (hum) aa rahi thi, jaise koi bahut bada fan chal raha ho. Guard ne bataya, "Wahi powerhouse hai. Andar turbine chal rahi hain."

"Chacha," Shrinidhi boli, "ek baat samajh nahi aayi. Itna paani to gate se **bekaar** bahi ja raha hai. Aur powerhouse mein bijli bhi ban rahi hai. To kya dam andar bijli **bharkar** rakhta hai, jaise mere phone ka power bank? Aur agar paani hi bijli banata hai, to ye saara paani bijli kyun nahi bana raha?"

Mr. Sardesai hans pade. "Do sawaal, aur dono mein ek-ek galatfehmi chhipi hai. Dam bijli **nahi** bharta, wo **paani** bharta hai, aur paani ki bijli banane ki ek seema hoti hai. Chalo, upar se neeche tak ek boond ka safar karte hain, phir hawa aur samundar ki taraf chalenge."

## Part A: Hydroelectric plant: girte paani se bijli

Class 9 mein dekha tha ki oonchai par rakha paani **potential energy (PE)** rakhta hai, aur girte hue wo **kinetic energy (KE)** ban jaati hai. Hydro plant isi ko ek mashin mein bandh deta hai.

```
 [ RESERVOIR (paani ki PE) ]
                  :   intake gate khulta hai, paani neeche girta hai
                  v
 [ PENSTOCK (PE se KE) ]
                :   tez dhaar blades par takrati hai
                v
 [ TURBINE (rotational KE) ]
                 :   shaft se jude hue
                 v
 [ GENERATOR (electrical energy) ]
                   :   transformer se voltage badhta hai
                   v
 [ TAAR AUR GRID (sheher tak) ]
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Hydroelectric plant ka block diagram: reservoir mein PE, penstock mein girte hue PE kam aur KE zyada, turbine mein rotational KE, generator mein bijli. Har arrow ke saath energy ka roop likha hai: kahin bhi energy "banti" nahi, sirf badalti hai.

Har hissa kya karta hai:

- **Dam aur reservoir:** nadi ko rokkar paani ko oonchai par jama karte hain. Jitna oonchai par paani, utni PE.
- **Penstock:** paani ko tez dhaar mein neeche turbine tak laati hai. Jitni zyada oonchai se girega, utni raftaar.
- **Turbine:** blades par takrakar paani unhe ghumata hai. Paani ki KE ka bada hissa turbine ki rotation ban jaata hai.
- **Generator:** turbine ke shaft se juda hai, aur ghumte hue bijli banata hai (Chapter 9 mein generator ki poori kahani aayegi).
- **Turbine ke baad:** paani nadi mein neeche chala jaata hai. Paani **khatam nahi hota**, sirf uski PE kharch hui. Barish use phir reservoir tak laati hai: isliye hydro **renewable** hai (Suraj ki garmi samundar se paani uthati hai).

Ab Shrinidhi ke dono sawaalon ka pehla jawab. Gate se bahta paani (**spillway**) wo **extra** paani hai jo reservoir mein samaa nahi sakta. Turbine ko ek hi samay mein **kitna paani** chahiye, wo uske size par tay hai. Isse zyada paani sirf nadi mein jaata hai.

## Part B: Hydro ka hisaab: P = (m ÷ t) × g × h × efficiency

Ek second mein jitna paani turbine se guzre, uski PE kitni hai? Class 9 ka formula: **PE = m g h**. Agar ek second mein m kilogram paani h oonchai se gire, to **ek second mein** (yaani power ke roop mein):

**Power (paani se) = (m ÷ t) × g × h**

Yahan m ÷ t "ek second mein kitne kg paani" hai (kg/s). Paani ka density 1000 kg/m³ hai, isliye agar flow rate Q m³/s hai, to **m ÷ t = Q × 1000 kg/s**. Hum g = 9.8 m/s² lenge.

Generator se jo asli bijli milti hai wo kuch kam hoti hai, kyunki turbine aur generator mein friction aur heat ka nuksaan hota hai. Turbine-generator ki efficiency lagbhag **80 percent** hoti hai:

**P(bijli) = 0.80 × (m ÷ t) × g × h**

```
 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~##
 ~~~~~~ RESERVOIR ~~~~~~~~##     paani ka upari level
 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~##
 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~##
 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~##
 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~##
 ====== penstock ======[ TURBINE ]====> nadi mein neeche
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Head h wo seedhi (vertical) oonchai hai jo reservoir ke upari paani level se turbine tak naapi jaati hai. Penstock kitni bhi lambi ho ya tedhi, sirf is seedhi oonchai h se PE ka hisaab lagta hai (Class 9 mein ramp ke saath bhi yahi dekha tha).

**Example 1: Ek hydro plant ki power**

Ek plant mein head 45 m hai aur turbine se 30 m³/s paani guzarta hai. Efficiency 80 percent hai. Bijli ki power nikaalo.

**Given:** h = 45 m, Q = 30 m³/s, efficiency = 80 percent, g = 9.8 m/s², density = 1000 kg/m³.
**Find:** bijli ki power P.
**Formula:** m ÷ t = Q × 1000; P(paani) = (m ÷ t) × g × h; P(bijli) = 0.80 × P(paani).
**Substitution:** m ÷ t = 30 × 1000 = 30,000 kg/s. P(paani) = 30,000 × 9.8 × 45 = 13,230,000 W = 13.23 MW. P(bijli) = 0.80 × 13.23 = 10.584 MW.
**Answer:** **P ≈ 10.6 MW** (lagbhag).
**Jaanch:** unit: kg/s × m/s² × m = (kg m/s²) × m / s = N m / s = J/s = W ✅. Head ko double (90 m) karo to wahi flow 21.168 MW deta hai, yaani power head ke **barabar** badhti hai ✅.

**Example 2: Diya hua power, flow kitna chahiye?**

Ek plant se 40 MW bijli chahiye, head 120 m aur efficiency 80 percent hai. Paani ka flow rate kitna hona chahiye?

**Given:** P(bijli) = 40 MW = 4 × 10⁷ W, h = 120 m, efficiency = 80 percent.
**Find:** Q.
**Formula:** P(paani) = P(bijli) ÷ 0.80; Q = P(paani) ÷ (1000 × g × h).
**Substitution:** P(paani) = 4 × 10⁷ ÷ 0.80 = 5 × 10⁷ W. Q = 5 × 10⁷ ÷ (1000 × 9.8 × 120) = 5 × 10⁷ ÷ 1,176,000 = 42.5 m³/s.
**Answer:** **Q ≈ 42.5 m³/s** (lagbhag).
**Jaanch:** peeche se: 42.5 × 1000 × 9.8 × 120 × 0.80 = 3.998 × 10⁷ W ≈ 40 MW ✅.

**Example 3: Ek din ki energy aur bill wale "units"**

Example 1 wala plant (10.584 MW) poora din (24 ghante) isi power par chale to kitni energy (kWh mein) milegi?

**Given:** P = 10.584 MW = 10,584 kW, t = 24 h.
**Find:** energy E (kWh).
**Formula:** E = P × t (Class 9 part 7: kW × ghanta = kWh).
**Substitution:** E = 10,584 × 24 = 254,016 kWh.
**Answer:** **E ≈ 2.54 × 10⁵ kWh** yaani lagbhag 2.54 lakh "units". Joule mein: 254,016 × 3.6 × 10⁶ = 9.14 × 10¹¹ J.
**Jaanch:** 10.584 MW × 24 h = 254.0 MWh = 254,016 kWh ✅.

## Part C: Hydro ke prakaar, fayde aur dikkatein

**Teen tarah ke hydro plant:**

- **Dam wala plant:** bada reservoir, bada head, bada power. Monsoon ka paani jama hota hai aur saal bhar dheere dheere chhoda jaata hai. Himalayan states (Himachal, Uttarakhand, Sikkim, Arunachal) mein kai hydro plant hain, kyunki wahan nadiyan oonchai se tezi se girti hain.
- **Run-of-river plant:** bada dam nahi, bas nadi ki dhaar ka thoda paani mod kar turbine chalaya jaata hai. Sasta aur gaon-basti ko kam asar, par nadi mein paani kam ho (sookhe mausam mein) to power ghat jaati hai, kyunki paani jama kiya hi nahi hota.
- **Pumped storage:** ek oonchai par aur ek neeche, do jheelein. Raat ko jab bijli bachti hai, us bijli se paani **neeche se oopar pump** kiya jaata hai. Din ki maang badhne par wahi paani wapas girta hai aur turbine chalata hai. Yahan bhi **paani** hi store hua, bijli nahi.

**Fayde:**

- Eendhan nahi jalta, to plant par **CO₂ nahi**, dhuan nahi.
- Paani **renewable** hai.
- Turbine kuch minuton mein start ya band ho jaati hai: maang ke saath bijli ghatane badhane ke liye achhi.
- Dam se **sinchai**, baadh par kaabu aur peene ka paani bhi milta hai.

**Dikkatein:**

- **Visthaapan:** reservoir mein gaon, kheti aur jungle doob jaate hain. Hazaaron logon ko doosri jagah basaana padta hai.
- **Silting:** nadi ke saath aayi mitti aur ret reservoir ke tale mein jam jaati hai. Saalon mein reservoir mein paani ki jagah kam hoti jaati hai.
- **Baadh aur ecology:** gate achanak khulne par neeche ki nadi mein baadh aa sakti hai. Bandh machhliyon ka raasta rok deta hai aur nadi ka swabhaav badal deta hai.
- **Methane:** reservoir mein doobe paudhe sadte hain aur methane (ek greenhouse gas) banate hain: ye matra chhoti hai par ginti mein aati hai.
- **Barish par nirbhar:** sookhe saal mein reservoir nahi bharta, to bijli kam.
- **Kharcha aur samay:** dam banane mein saal lagte hain aur bahut paisa.

## Part D: Wind energy: hawa se bijli

Hawa chalti hai, yaani **KE** rakhti hai. Wind turbine us KE ka kuch hissa pakadkar bijli banati hai. (Pawan chakki ki chain Class 9 ke part 6 mein bhi thi.)

```
 [ CHALTI HAWA (KE) ]
              :   blades par takrati hai, blades ghoomti hain
              v
 [ ROTOR AUR BLADES ]
             :   dheeme shaft se tez shaft tak
             v
 [ GEARBOX ]
         :   tez ghumta shaft
         v
 [ GENERATOR (electrical energy) ]
                :   transformer se grid mein
                v
 [ TAAR AUR GRID ]
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Wind turbine ki chain: hawa ki KE se blades ghoomti hain, gearbox shaft ko tez karta hai, generator bijli banata hai. Yahan bhi koi eendhan nahi jalta, sirf KE badalti hai.

**Blades ka design:** blades hawai jahaaz ke pankh ki tarah banti hain: hawa unhe ek taraf se dhakelti hai aur wo ghoomti hain. Turbine ko hawa ki taraf ghumane ke liye upar ek chhota motor aur sensor hota hai.

### Hawa ki raftaar aur P ∝ v³

Wind turbine ki power hawa ki raftaar v par bahut tezi se nirbhar karti hai: **P ∝ v³** (power raftaar ke **cube** ke barabar). Kyun? Do cheezein ek saath badhti hain:

1. Raftaar double hone par ek second mein blades se **double hawa** guzarti hai.
2. Hawa ke har kg ki KE ½ m v² hoti hai, jo raftaar double hone par **4 guna** ho jaati hai.

Dono milakar: 2 × 4 = **8 guna**. Ratio ke tareeke se (koi formula yaad rakhne ki zaroorat nahi):

**P₂ ÷ P₁ = (v₂ ÷ v₁)³**

```
 Hawa ki raftaar    Power (relative)

 v        :#                                   1
 2 v      :########                            8
 3 v      :###########################       27
```

> **Figure 3.4** – Raftaar v, 2v aur 3v par power 1, 8 aur 27 guna. Har # ek unit power hai: 1, 8 aur 27 #. Hawa ki raftaar thodi badhne par bhi power bahut badhti hai.

**Example 4: Raftaar badhi, power kitni badhi?**

Ek turbine 8 m/s ki hawa mein 120 kW deti hai. Hawa 12 m/s ho jaaye (turbine abhi cut-out ke andar hai) to power kitni?

**Given:** v₁ = 8 m/s, P₁ = 120 kW, v₂ = 12 m/s.
**Find:** P₂.
**Formula:** P₂ ÷ P₁ = (v₂ ÷ v₁)³.
**Substitution:** (12 ÷ 8)³ = 1.5³ = 3.375. P₂ = 120 × 3.375 = 405 kW.
**Answer:** **P₂ = 405 kW** (raftaar sirf 1.5 guna, power 3.375 guna).
**Jaanch:** raftaar badhi to power badhni chahiye: 405 > 120 ✅. Raftaar barabar karne par ratio 1 aata hai ✅.

**Example 5: Raftaar ghati, power ghati**

Hawa 20 percent dheemi ho gayi (v₂ = 0.8 v₁). Power kitni reh jaati hai?

**Given:** v₂ ÷ v₁ = 0.8.
**Find:** P₂ ÷ P₁.
**Formula:** P₂ ÷ P₁ = (v₂ ÷ v₁)³.
**Substitution:** 0.8³ = 0.8 × 0.8 × 0.8 = 0.512.
**Answer:** **Power lagbhag aadhi (51.2 percent)** reh jaati hai: raftaar mein 20 percent ki kami, power mein lagbhag 49 percent ki kami.
**Jaanch:** 0.512 < 0.8, yaani power raftaar se zyada tezi se giri ✅.

### Cut-in, cut-out aur wind farm

- **Cut-in:** bahut halki hawa (lagbhag 15 km/h se kam) mein blades ghoom bhi jayein to bijli banane layak nahi. Lagbhag 15 km/h par turbine shuru hoti hai.
- **Cut-out:** bahut tez hawa (lagbhag 90 km/h se zyada) mein blades aur tower ko khatra hota hai. Tab turbine ke blades ko ghumakar hawa se hata diya jaata hai aur brake laga diye jaate hain.

km/h ko m/s mein badalne ke liye 3.6 se bhaag karo: 15 km/h = 15 ÷ 3.6 = 4.17 m/s aur 90 km/h = 90 ÷ 3.6 = 25 m/s. Yaani turbine lagbhag **4 m/s se 25 m/s** ke beech kaam karti hai.

Bahut saari turbines ek jagah lagakar **wind farm** banta hai, jahan hawa tez aur lagbhag har din chalti ho. India mein Rajasthan ke **Jaisalmer** ke wind farms aur **Tamil Nadu** ke wind farms jaane-maane hain. Turbines ke beech ki zameen par kheti bhi ho sakti hai.

**Dikkatein:** badi zameen, blades ki aawaz (noise), **panchhiyon** ka blades se takrana, aur sabse badi baat: hawa **steady nahi** hoti, kabhi tez kabhi bilkul nahi. Hawa na ho to turbine ruki rehti hai.

## Part E: Capacity factor: "average" mein kitni bijli?

Ek wind turbine par likha hota hai "2 MW". Ye uski **zyada se zyada (rated) power** hai, jo wo tabhi deti hai jab hawa ek achhi raftaar par ho. Par hawa har time waisi nahi hoti. Isliye saal bhar mein turbine **average** roop mein apni rated power ka sirf ek hissa deti hai. Wo hissa **capacity factor** kehlata hai. 30 percent ka matlab: average power = 0.30 × rated power.

**Example 6: Chhoti turbine ka saal bhar ka hisaab**

Ek turbine rated 1.5 MW ki hai aur 25 percent capacity factor par chalti hai. Uski average power aur ek saal (8760 ghante) mein energy kitni?

**Given:** rated power = 1.5 MW, capacity factor = 25 percent = 0.25, t = 8760 h.
**Find:** average power aur saal bhar ki energy.
**Formula:** average P = capacity factor × rated P; E = average P × t.
**Substitution:** average P = 0.25 × 1.5 = 0.375 MW = 375 kW. E = 375 × 8760 = 3,285,000 kWh.
**Answer:** **Average power = 375 kW** aur **E ≈ 3.29 × 10⁶ kWh** (lagbhag 33 lakh units) saal mein.
**Jaanch:** agar 1.5 MW poore saal chalti to 1500 × 8760 = 13,140,000 kWh; uska 25 percent = 3,285,000 kWh ✅.

Isi liye 1.5 MW likhi turbine ek 1.5 MW ke coal unit ke barabar bijli nahi deti: coal unit eendhan hone par din-raat rated power de sakta hai, hawa nahi.

## Part F: Tidal energy: samundar ka upar-neeche

Class 9 mein dekha tha ki Chaand ki gravity se samundar ka paani din mein **do baar upar chadhta** (high tide) aur do baar utarta (low tide) hai. Lagbhag har 12 ghante 25 minute mein ek high tide aati hai. Ye upar-neeche hona bhi ek tarah ki "girte paani" ki energy hai.

**Tidal barrage:** kisi khaadi (bay) ya nadi ke muhane par bandh banate hain jisme turbine lagi hoti hain.

```
 HIGH TIDE ke time:
   [ SAMUNDAR (level ooncha) ]
                  :   gate khulta hai, paani turbine se andar jaata hai
                  v
   [ TURBINE aur GENERATOR (bijli) ]
                  :   paani andar basin mein bharta hai
                  v
   [ BASIN (level neecha, bharta hua) ]

 LOW TIDE ke time:
   [ BASIN (level ooncha, khaali hota hua) ]
                  :   paani turbine se wapas samundar mein jaata hai
                  v
   [ TURBINE aur GENERATOR (bijli) ]
                  :   paani bahar nikalta hai
                  v
   [ SAMUNDAR (level neecha) ]
```

> **Figure 3.5** – Tidal barrage ke do daur: high tide par samundar se basin ki taraf, low tide par basin se samundar ki taraf paani turbine se guzarta hai. Dono baar level ka antar (head) hi paani ko dhakelta hai, aur dono baar bijli banti hai. Is head ko banane wali tide Chaand (aur Suraj) ki gravity se aati hai.

Hisaab wahi hai jo hydro ka tha: **P(bijli) = 0.80 × (m ÷ t) × g × h**, bas yahan h (tidal range ya level ka antar) kuch metre hi hota hai, isliye **flow bahut bada** chahiye. Aur h tide ke saath badalta rehta hai.

**Example 7: Tidal plant**

Ek tidal barrage ke turbines se 400 m³/s paani guzarta hai aur level ka antar (head) 3 m hai. Efficiency 80 percent. Bijli?

**Given:** Q = 400 m³/s, h = 3 m, efficiency = 80 percent.
**Find:** P(bijli).
**Formula:** P = 0.80 × (Q × 1000) × g × h.
**Substitution:** m ÷ t = 400 × 1000 = 400,000 kg/s. P(paani) = 400,000 × 9.8 × 3 = 11,760,000 W. P(bijli) = 0.80 × 11.76 = 9.408 MW.
**Answer:** **P ≈ 9.4 MW** (lagbhag).
**Jaanch:** peeche se: 9.408 MW ÷ 0.80 = 11.76 MW; 11.76 × 10⁶ ÷ (1000 × 9.8 × 3) = 400 m³/s ✅. Ye Example 1 jaisi power hai (lagbhag 10 MW), par head 45 m ki jagah sirf 3 m, isliye flow 30 m³/s ki jagah 400 m³/s chahiye: P ∝ Q × h ✅.

**Tidal energy ki sachchai:**

- Bijli **saaf** hai (koi eendhan nahi) aur uska **time-table pehle se pata** hota hai, kyunki tide ka samay pehle se nikaala ja sakta hai.
- Par bijli **kuch hi ghanton** ke liye milti hai (tide ke beech mein level ka antar kam hota hai), aur poore din nahi.
- Achhi jagah chahiye jahan tidal range bada ho, isliye **duniya mein bahut kam** tidal plant hain (jaise France ka La Rance aur South Korea ka Sihwa). India mein **Kutch** ki khadi aur **Sundarbans** ko sambhavit (potential) jagah mana jaata hai, par abhi bade pamaane par bijli nahi ban rahi.
- Bandh samudri jeevon aur paani ke behaav ko badalta hai, aur banana mehenga hai.

**Do aur naam** (sirf naam): **wave energy** (samundar ki lehron ka oopar-neeche hona turbine ya piston chalata hai) aur **ocean thermal energy** (samundar ki garam upari satah aur thandi gehri satah ke temperature ke antar se bijli). Dono abhi chhote prayog ke star par hain.

### Teen strot ek nazar mein

| Strot | Kya chalta hai | Bijli kab milti hai | Badi dikkat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hydro (dam) | Girta paani | Jab tak reservoir mein paani ho | Visthaapan, silting |
| Wind | Chalti hawa | Lagbhag 15 km/h se 90 km/h ki hawa mein | Hawa steady nahi |
| Tidal | Tide ka upar-neeche | Tide ke kuch ghanton mein, din mein do baar | Kam jagah, mehenga |

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Dam bijli store karta hai"**

Dam **paani** store karta hai, bijli nahi. Bijli ko aam taur par utni hi der mein use karna padta hai jitni der mein wo banti hai.

Demonstration: maan lo ek reservoir mein 3 × 10⁶ m³ paani hai aur wo turbine se average 40 m upar hai. Paani ka mass = 3 × 10⁶ × 1000 = 3 × 10⁹ kg. PE = m g h = 3 × 10⁹ × 9.8 × 40 = 1.176 × 10¹² J. kWh mein: 1.176 × 10¹² ÷ 3.6 × 10⁶ = 326,667 kWh (lagbhag). Efficiency 80 percent lein to bijli 261,333 kWh. Ye energy dam mein **paani ki PE** ke roop mein pad hai. Ek 10 MW (10,000 kW) ka plant ise lagbhag 261,333 ÷ 10,000 = 26 ghante mein bijli bana dega, tabhi jab **gate khule aur turbine chale**. Pumped storage mein bhi bijli se paani oopar uthaya jaata hai, aur wahi paani baad mein bijli banata hai: store paani ki PE hui, bijli nahi.
Jaanch: units: kg × m/s² × m = J; 1.176 × 10¹² J ÷ 3.6 × 10⁶ J/kWh = 326,667 kWh ✅.

**Galti 2: "Hawa ki raftaar double hui to power bhi double hui"**

Sahi: raftaar double hone par power **8 guna** hoti hai (P ∝ v³).

Demonstration: ek turbine 25 km/h ki hawa mein 5 kW deti hai. Hawa 50 km/h ho (turbine ke cut-out se kam hai). Ratio = (50 ÷ 25)³ = 2³ = 8, to power = 5 × 8 = **40 kW**, 10 kW nahi. Aur raftaar teen guna (25 se 75 km/h) hoti to power 3³ = 27 guna: 5 × 27 = 135 kW. Galat sochne wale "10 kW" par ruk jaate: asli power uski chaar guna hai.
Jaanch: 5 × 8 = 40 aur 5 × 27 = 135 ✅.

## Quick Recap

- Hydro plant: reservoir → penstock → turbine → generator. Paani ki PE → KE → rotation → bijli. Paani **khatam nahi hota**.
- P(bijli) = 0.80 × (Q × 1000) × g × h, flow Q m³/s mein, g = 9.8 m/s². Power head ke barabar badhti hai.
- Dam **paani** store karta hai, bijli nahi. Pumped storage mein bhi paani ki PE store hoti hai.
- Hydro ke fayde: koi eendhan nahi, renewable, baadh par kaabu. Dikkatein: visthaapan, silting, ecology, methane, barish par nirbhar.
- Wind turbine: hawa ki KE → blades → gearbox → generator. Power ∝ v³: raftaar double to power 8 guna. Cut-in lagbhag 15 km/h, cut-out lagbhag 90 km/h.
- Capacity factor: turbine average mein rated power ka ek hissa deti hai (jaise 30 percent).
- Tidal: din mein do high tide, barrage ke turbines dono taraf paani se chalti hain. Head chhota, flow bada, kam jagah, kuch hi ghante.
- Wave energy aur ocean thermal energy: sirf naam.

## Practice Questions

1. A dam has a head of 80 m and 50 m³/s of water passes through its turbines. The turbine-generator efficiency is 80 percent. Find the electrical power and the energy produced in one full day in kWh.
2. A small hydro plant has a head of 12 m. (a) Find its electrical power if 3.5 m³/s of water flows through the turbine at 80 percent efficiency. (b) What flow rate is needed for 500 kW?
3. A hill village has a run-of-river plant and a town has a dam-based plant. In the dry months the river flow drops to a trickle. Explain which plant is affected more, and why.
4. A wind turbine is in a place where the wind speed is 6 m/s and then 12 m/s. (a) Find the ratio of the power in the two cases. (b) If the turbine gives 25 kW at 6 m/s, what does it give at 12 m/s?
5. (a) The wind speed rises from 5 m/s to 15 m/s. By what factor does the power rise? If the turbine gave 4 kW at 5 m/s, what does it give at 15 m/s? (b) The wind speed falls from 10 m/s to 7 m/s. Find the fraction of the earlier power that remains.
6. A 2 MW turbine runs at a capacity factor of 30 percent. (a) Find its average power. (b) Find the energy (kWh) in one year of 8760 hours. (c) How many such turbines are needed to give an average of 90 MW?
7. A tidal barrage has a head of 5 m and 250 m³/s flows through its turbines at 80 percent efficiency. (a) Find the electrical power. (b) If it generates at this power for 10 hours in a day in total, find the energy in kWh.
8. The cut-in and cut-out speeds of a turbine are about 15 km/h and 90 km/h. Which of these wind speeds make the turbine run: 10 km/h, 54 km/h, 108 km/h? Give the speeds in m/s as well.
9. A hydro plant gets 20 m³/s of water. Compare the electrical power if the head is 75 m and if it is 15 m. By what factor is the power larger at 75 m?
10. A turbine set can use at most 40 m³/s. In the peak monsoon the river brings 120 m³/s to a dam with 50 m head. (a) How much water goes over the spillway? (b) What is the maximum electrical power at 80 percent efficiency?
11. (a) A hydro plant receives 2.5 MW of power from the water and gives 1.9 MW of electricity. Find its efficiency. (b) A turbine of efficiency 88 percent drives a generator of efficiency 92 percent. Find the efficiency of the pair.
12. Water of 15 m³/s falls through a head of 60 m. Find the electrical energy produced in one hour at 80 percent efficiency, in kWh.
13. State True or False and give a reason: (a) A hydroelectric dam stores electricity. (b) When the wind speed doubles, the power doubles. (c) A wind turbine keeps running at full speed in a 108 km/h storm wind. (d) A tidal plant gives steady power all day. (e) Pumped storage uses spare electricity to lift water.

### Answers

1. **Given:** h = 80 m, Q = 50 m³/s, efficiency = 80 percent. **Find:** P(bijli) aur ek din ki energy. **Formula:** P(paani) = Q × 1000 × g × h; P(bijli) = 0.80 × P(paani); E = P × t. **Substitution:** m ÷ t = 50,000 kg/s; P(paani) = 50,000 × 9.8 × 80 = 3.92 × 10⁷ W = 39.2 MW; P(bijli) = 0.80 × 39.2 = 31.36 MW; E = 31.36 MW × 24 h = 752.64 MWh = 752,640 kWh. **Answer:** **P ≈ 31.4 MW** aur **E ≈ 7.5 × 10⁵ kWh** lagbhag (7.5 lakh units). **Jaanch:** 31,360 kW × 24 = 752,640 kWh ✅.

2. (a) **Given:** h = 12 m, Q = 3.5 m³/s. m ÷ t = 3500 kg/s; P(paani) = 3500 × 9.8 × 12 = 411,600 W; P(bijli) = 0.80 × 411,600 = 329,280 W = **329 kW** lagbhag. (b) P(paani) = 500,000 ÷ 0.80 = 625,000 W; Q = 625,000 ÷ (1000 × 9.8 × 12) = 625,000 ÷ 117,600 = **5.31 m³/s** lagbhag. **Jaanch:** 5.31 × 1000 × 9.8 × 12 × 0.80 = 499,600 W ≈ 500 kW ✅.

3. **Run-of-river** plant zyada prabhavit hoga. Usme paani jama nahi hota, to nadi ki dhaar kam hui to flow Q kam, aur P ∝ Q hone se bijli bhi kam. Dam wala plant monsoon ka paani reservoir mein rakhta hai aur sookhe mahino mein chhodta hai, to uski power zyada tikti hai (jab tak reservoir mein paani hai). **Jaanch:** P ∝ Q: flow aadha to power aadhi ✅.

4. (a) **Formula:** P₂ ÷ P₁ = (v₂ ÷ v₁)³ = (12 ÷ 6)³ = 2³ = **8**. (b) P₂ = 25 × 8 = **200 kW**. **Jaanch:** 12 m/s, 6 m/s ka double hai aur double raftaar par power 8 guna hoti hai, 2 guna nahi ✅.

5. (a) (15 ÷ 5)³ = 3³ = **27** guna. P₂ = 4 × 27 = **108 kW**. (b) (7 ÷ 10)³ = 0.7 × 0.7 × 0.7 = **0.343**: lagbhag **34.3 percent** power bachti hai (65.7 percent ki kami, jabki raftaar sirf 30 percent ghati). **Jaanch:** 0.343 < 0.7 ✅.

6. (a) **Given:** rated = 2 MW, capacity factor = 0.30. Average P = 0.30 × 2 = **0.6 MW** (600 kW). (b) E = 600 kW × 8760 h = 5,256,000 kWh = **5.26 × 10⁶ kWh** lagbhag. (c) Turbines = 90 ÷ 0.6 = **150**. **Jaanch:** poore saal rated power par 2000 × 8760 = 17,520,000 kWh; uska 30 percent = 5,256,000 kWh ✅.

7. (a) **Given:** h = 5 m, Q = 250 m³/s, efficiency = 80 percent. m ÷ t = 250,000 kg/s; P(paani) = 250,000 × 9.8 × 5 = 12,250,000 W; P(bijli) = 0.80 × 12.25 = **9.8 MW**. (b) E = 9,800 kW × 10 h = **98,000 kWh** (lagbhag 98 hazaar units). **Jaanch:** 9.8 MW × 10 h = 98 MWh = 98,000 kWh ✅.

8. Cut-in 15 km/h = 4.17 m/s aur cut-out 90 km/h = 25 m/s. 10 km/h = 2.78 m/s: cut-in se kam, to turbine **nahi chalegi**. 54 km/h = 54 ÷ 3.6 = 15 m/s: 4.17 aur 25 ke beech, to **chalegi**. 108 km/h = 108 ÷ 3.6 = 30 m/s: cut-out se zyada, to turbine surakshit roki jaayegi aur **nahi chalegi**. **Jaanch:** 15 m/s × 3.6 = 54 km/h ✅.

9. Head 75 m: P(bijli) = 0.80 × 20,000 × 9.8 × 75 = 11,760,000 W = **11.76 MW**. Head 15 m: P = 0.80 × 20,000 × 9.8 × 15 = 2,352,000 W = **2.35 MW**. Ratio = 11.76 ÷ 2.352 = **5**, jo 75 ÷ 15 = 5 ke barabar hai (P ∝ h). **Jaanch:** ratio = head ka ratio ✅.

10. (a) Spillway se = 120 − 40 = **80 m³/s**. (b) m ÷ t = 40,000 kg/s; P = 0.80 × 40,000 × 9.8 × 50 = 15,680,000 W = **15.68 MW** lagbhag. Aur turbine badhana mehenga hai aur baaki mahino mein flow kam hota hai, isliye extra turbines akaar rehti. **Jaanch:** 40 + 80 = 120 ✅.

11. (a) Efficiency = 1.9 ÷ 2.5 × 100 = **76 percent**. (b) 0.88 × 0.92 = 0.8096, yaani **80.96 percent**, lagbhag 81 percent, jo "lagbhag 80 percent" wali value ke paas hai. **Jaanch:** 2.5 × 0.76 = 1.9 ✅.

12. **Given:** Q = 15 m³/s, h = 60 m. m ÷ t = 15,000 kg/s; P(paani) = 15,000 × 9.8 × 60 = 8,820,000 W = 8.82 MW; P(bijli) = 0.80 × 8.82 = 7.056 MW = 7056 kW. E = 7056 kW × 1 h = **7056 kWh** (lagbhag 7056 units). **Jaanch:** 7056 × 3.6 = 25,401.6 MJ = 2.54 × 10¹⁰ J ✅ (7.056 × 10⁶ W × 3600 s = 2.54 × 10¹⁰ J).

13. (a) **False** ❌: dam paani ki PE store karta hai. (b) **False** ❌: power 2³ = 8 guna hoti hai. (c) **False** ❌: 108 km/h cut-out (lagbhag 90 km/h) se zyada hai, turbine roki jaati hai. (d) **False** ❌: tide ke kuch ghanton mein hi achhi power milti hai. (e) **True** ✅: raat ki bachi bijli se paani oopar uthaya jaata hai.
