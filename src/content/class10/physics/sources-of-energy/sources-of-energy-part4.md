## Part A: Suraj, sabki jad

### Ek hi shrot, kai raaste

Class 9 mein dekha tha ki zyaadatar energy ki jad Suraj hai ([work-energy-and-power-part3.md](../../../class9/physics/work-energy-and-power/work-energy-and-power-part3.md)). Ab raaste ek ek karke dekhte hain.

```
[ Suraj ]-->[ purane paudhe     ]-->[ koyla, petrol ]-->[ thermal plant  ]-->[ bijli ]
[ Suraj ]-->[ asamaan ki garmi  ]-->[ hawa          ]-->[ wind turbine   ]-->[ bijli ]
[ Suraj ]-->[ samundar se bhaap ]-->[ baarish, nadi ]-->[ dam, turbine   ]-->[ bijli ]
[ Suraj ]-->[ paudhe            ]-->[ lakdi, gobar  ]-->[ chulha, biogas ]-->[ garmi ]
[ Suraj ]-->[ solar cell        ]-->[ bijli         ]
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Paanch raaste jo Suraj se hamare ghar tak aate hain. Pehli teen chain mein dhoop ne pehle koi aur roop liya (purane paudhe, hawa, baarish) aur uske baad bijli bani. Chautha raasta paudhon se garmi tak jaata hai. Paanchva solar cell Suraj se **seedha** bijli banata hai: beech mein kuch jalaana nahi padta.

- **Koyla, petrol:** karodon saal pehle ke paudhon aur jeevon mein Suraj ki energy jama thi.
- **Hawa:** Suraj dharti ke alag hisson ko alag tarah garam karta hai, garam hawa upar uthti hai, aur hawa chalti hai.
- **Behta paani:** Suraj samundar aur nadiyon ka paani bhaap banata hai, baarish se wo paani pahaad par girta hai, aur dam ke paani ko oonchai milti hai.
- **Lakdi, gobar, anaaj:** paudhe dhoop se apna khana banate hain (photosynthesis).

Imaandari ki baat: **sab kuch** Suraj se nahi aata. Zameen ke andar ki garmi aur uranium ki energy ka Suraj se koi rishta nahi (Topic 5 mein aayenge), aur tide mukhya roop se Chaand ki kheench se bante hain.

### Dhoop mein kitna dum hai?

Suraj ki energy Earth tak kirnon ke roop mein aati hai. Atmosphere ke **bahar**, Suraj ki taraf seedhi ek m² par ek second mein lagbhag **1.4 kW** girta hai (solar constant: lagbhag 1.4 kW/m²). Zameen tak aate aate hawa, dhool aur badal kuch roshni rok lete hain, isliye saaf dopahar mein bhi ek m² par lagbhag **1 kW** (1000 W/m²) pahunchta hai.

Jaanch ke liye ratio: 1 ÷ 1.4 ≈ 0.71, yaani zameen tak lagbhag **71 percent** dhoop pahunchti hai.

### Example 1. Meez par kitni dhoop?

Saaf dopahar mein 0.5 m² ki ek meez dhoop mein rakhi hai. Wo 10 minute mein kitni energy leti hai? (Insolation lagbhag 1 kW/m²)

**Given:** A = 0.5 m², insolation = 1000 W/m² (lagbhag), t = 10 min = 600 s
**Find:** Dhoop ki power P aur energy E
**Formula:** P = A × insolation; E = P × t
**Substitution:** P = 0.5 × 1000 = 500 W; E = 500 × 600 = **300,000 J = 0.3 MJ**
**Answer:** 500 W ki dhoop, 10 minute mein lagbhag 0.3 MJ.
**Jaanch:** 300,000 ÷ 3,600,000 = 0.083 kWh, aur 0.5 kW × (1/6) h = 0.083 kWh ✅. Unit: W × s = J ✅.

Dekho, 0.5 m² ki meez 10 minute mein itni energy leti hai ki 100 W ka bulb lagbhag 50 minute jal jaaye. Par ye sab dhoop hai, bijli nahi: dhoop ko kaam ki cheez mein badalna hi asli sawal hai.

## Part B: Dhoop se garmi: cooker aur water heater

### Solar cooker: kaala, kaanch aur mirror

**Box type solar cooker** ek hi seedhi si baat par chalta hai: dhoop ko andar aane do, par garmi ko bahar mat jaane do. Teen cheezein ye karti hain.

1. **Andar ki kaali sateh aur kaala bartan.** Kaala rang dhoop ko **sokhta** hai aur garmi mein badalta hai. Safed ya chamkeeli sateh dhoop ko wapas phenk deti hai.
2. **Shaffaf dhakkan (kaanch).** Roshni andar aa jaati hai, par garam kaali sateh se nikli **infrared** garmi kaanch se bahar nahi jaa paati, aur andar ki garam hawa bhi bahar nahi nikalti. Ye wahi **greenhouse** wala idea hai jo [atmospheric-pollution-part3.md](../../../class9/chemistry/atmospheric-pollution/atmospheric-pollution-part3.md) mein Earth ke liye dekha tha: roshni andar, garmi andar hi.
3. **Mirror reflector.** Dhakkan ke saath khulne wala sheesha ya foil ki patti ek aur hissa dhoop dabbe mein mod deti hai.

Dabbe ke dewaron aur tale mein thermocol ya gadda lagta hai, taaki garmi bahar na bhaage.

```
  mirror
      \       dhoop ke kiran
       \     v     v     v     v     v
        \=====================================   <- shaffaf dhakkan
        #  garam hawa andar hi band          #
        #      .---------------.             #
        #      :  kaala bartan :             #   <- paani/khana
        #      '---------------'             #
        #kaali sateh andar (garmi sokhti hai)#
        ######################################   <- neeche aur dewaron mein thermocol
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Box type solar cooker (side view, scale ke hisaab se nahi). Mirror dhoop ko dhakkan se andar mod deta hai; kaali sateh garmi sokhti hai; kaanch garmi ko bahar nahi jaane deta; thermocol bahar nikalne wali garmi rokta hai.

Asli cooker andar lagbhag **100 se 140 °C** tak pahunchta hai, yaani dal-chawal ya sabzi dheere dheere pak sakti hai. Par uski seemayein hain: pakne mein aksar **ek ghante se zyada** lagta hai, badal ho to bahut kamzor, aur har aadhe-ek ghante mein use Suraj ki taraf ghumana padta hai. Raat mein to kaam hi nahi karta.

### Example 2. Cooker mein paani

Ek cooker ka khula mooh 0.36 m² hai. Saaf dopahar mein jo dhoop us par girti hai, uska lagbhag 40 percent hissa bartan ke paani tak garmi ban kar pahunchta hai (baaki dabbe, kaanch aur bahar ki hawa mein chala jaata hai). 1.5 kg paani 25 °C se 95 °C tak garam karna hai. Kitna time lagega? (Paani ka c = 4200 J/kg °C)

**Given:** A = 0.36 m², insolation = 1000 W/m² (lagbhag), useful = 40 percent, m = 1.5 kg, ΔT = 95 − 25 = 70 °C, c = 4200 J/kg °C
**Find:** Garmi Q, paani tak pahunchti power P, time t
**Formula:** Q = m c ΔT; P = A × insolation × 0.40; t = Q ÷ P
**Substitution:** Q = 1.5 × 4200 × 70 = 441,000 J; P = 0.36 × 1000 × 0.40 = 144 W; t = 441,000 ÷ 144 = **3062.5 s ≈ 51 min**
**Answer:** Lagbhag 51 minute.
**Jaanch:** 144 W × 3062.5 s = 441,000 J ✅. Limiting case: mirror ya badal se P aadhi ho jaye to time doguna (lagbhag 102 min), jo cooker ke "dhoop badle to time badle" wale swabhaav se milta hai ✅.

### Solar water heater (flat plate)

Chhaton par jo kaala chapta dabba aur uske saath ek tanki dikhti hai, wo **solar water heater** hai. Dabbe ke andar kaale rang ki tanbe ki naliyan hoti hain, upar kaanch, neeche thermocol. Naliyon ka paani dhoop se garam hota hai, halka hokar upar tanki ki taraf uthta hai (**convection**, [heat-and-temperature-part6.md](../../../class9/physics/heat-and-temperature/heat-and-temperature-part6.md)), aur thanda paani neeche se aakar uski jagah leta hai. Is tarah bina pump ke chakkar chalta rehta hai (kuch systems mein chhota pump bhi lagta hai).

### Example 3. 100 litre paani ka solar heater

Ek flat plate heater ka collector 2 m² ka hai aur wo girti dhoop ka lagbhag 50 percent garmi mein badalta hai (maana hua; ye cell ke 18 percent se kahin zyada hai, kyunki yahan sirf garmi chahiye, bijli nahi). 100 kg paani ko 30 °C se 60 °C tak garam karna hai. Kitna time lagega, aur 2 kW ke electric geyser ke muqable kitni bijli bachi? (Saaf dopahar, ₹6 per unit maana hua)

**Given:** A = 2 m², insolation = 1000 W/m² (lagbhag), efficiency = 50 percent, m = 100 kg, ΔT = 30 °C, c = 4200 J/kg °C
**Find:** Q, time t, bachi energy (kWh)
**Formula:** Q = m c ΔT; P = A × insolation × 0.50; t = Q ÷ P
**Substitution:** Q = 100 × 4200 × 30 = 12,600,000 J = 12.6 MJ; P = 2 × 1000 × 0.50 = 1000 W; t = 12,600,000 ÷ 1000 = **12,600 s = 3.5 h**
**Answer:** 3.5 ghante; geyser (maana 100 percent efficient) chalta to 12.6 MJ ÷ 3.6 MJ per kWh = **3.5 kWh** (3.5 unit) lagta, yaani ₹21 maanke.
**Jaanch:** 3.5 kWh ÷ 2 kW = 1.75 h, yaani geyser ko aadha time lagta par bijli (energy) wahi 3.5 kWh jaati ✅. Unit: kg × J/kg °C × °C = J ✅.

Ek teesri katori ko kaale kaagaz mein lapet kar dhoop mein rakho, chauthi ko chamkeeli foil mein. Apna table banao: kaun sabse tez garam hui? Isse kaale rang ka sokhna dikhta hai. ## Part C: Solar cell: dhoop se seedha bijli

### Photovoltaic: roshni ko bijli mein badalna

**Photo** ka matlab roshni, **voltaic** ka matlab bijli ka dabaav (voltage). **Solar cell** silicon ke patle tukde se bana hota hai. Is tukde ki do parat hoti hain. Jab dhoop upar ki parat par padti hai, wo parat ke electrons ko dhakka deti hai aur wo ek taraf se doosri taraf behne lagte hain: bijli ka current. Cell mein na koi hissa ghoomta hai, na koi cheez jalti hai, na dhuan nikalta hai. Ye bahut simple hai, aur isliye saalon chalta hai. (Ye kyun hota hai, uski poori kahani aage ki classes mein.)

Ek cell lagbhag **0.5 V** hi deta hai. Torch ke cells ki tarah, **series** mein jodne par voltage jud jaata hai ([electricity-part3.md](../../../class9/physics/electricity/electricity-part3.md)). Isliye kai cells ek panel mein series mein jode jaate hain.

```
                    jodo                       frame mein                kai panel
[ 1 cell 0.5 V ]----------->[ 36 cell series ]----------->[ Panel 18 V ]----------->[ Array ]

           DC bijli                AC bijli
[ Array ]----------->[ Inverter ]----------->[ Ghar ka board ]
```

> **Figure 4.3** – Ek cell (lagbhag 0.5 V) se bada system: 36 cells series mein jodne par lagbhag 36 × 0.5 = 18 V ka panel banta hai, aur kai panels milkar array. Solar cell **DC** deta hai; ghar ke board ke liye inverter use AC mein badalta hai.

- **Efficiency:** aaj ke solar panel girti dhoop ka lagbhag **15 se 20 percent** bijli mein badalte hain, baaki garmi ban jaati hai. Hum numericals mein, jab tak sawaal kuch aur na de, **18 percent** lenge.
- **Raat aur badal:** raat mein cell kuch nahi deta. Isliye jahan grid nahi hai, wahan panel ke saath ek **battery** aur ek **charge controller** (battery ko zyada bharne se bachane wala chhota dabba) lagta hai.
- **Kharch:** panel ki keemat pichhle saalon mein kaafi giri hai, isliye chhat par panel lagana ab aam hota ja raha hai.

**Kahan kahan:** calculator ke chhote panel mein, **street lights** mein, **rooftop** system mein, aur **satellites** mein. Satellite atmosphere ke bahar hota hai, wahan dhoop zyada tez (lagbhag 1.4 kW/m²) milti hai, isliye uske lambe "paankh" jaise panel behtareen kaam dete hain (orbit ki baatein [gravitation-and-space-missions-part4.md](../gravitation-and-space-missions/gravitation-and-space-missions-part4.md) mein). Rajasthan ka **Bhadla Solar Park** duniya ke sabse bade solar parks mein se ek hai: registan ki khuli zameen aur tez dhoop wahan panels ki lambi qataarein sajati hain.

### Solar cooker aur solar cell: farak ek nazar mein

| Baat | Solar cooker | Solar cell (panel) |
|---|---|---|
| Dhoop se kya banta hai | Garmi | Bijli |
| Kaise | Kaali sateh dhoop sokhti hai, kaanch garmi rokta hai | Silicon mein electrons behte hain |
| Kya chahiye | Kaala bartan, kaanch, mirror, thermocol | Silicon cells, taar, inverter |
| Efficiency (garmi ya bijli ki) | Lagbhag 40 percent garmi (hamare Example 2 mein maana hua) | Lagbhag 15 se 20 percent bijli |
| Kaam | Khana pakana | Bulb, pankha, mobile charge karna |

## Part D: Panel ka hisaab

### Peak sun hours: lambe din ko ek chhote ghante-hisaab mein

Dhoop poore din ek jaisi tez nahi hoti: subah-shaam kamzor, dopahar mein tez. Hisaab asaan karne ke liye poore din ki dhoop ko jodkar maante hain ki "din mein itne ghante poori (1 kW/m² wali) dhoop mili". Ise **peak sun hours** kehte hain. Hum **5 ghante** lenge.

```
   7-8 baje : ##
   8-9 baje : ######
  9-10 baje : ###########
 10-11 baje : ###############
 11-12 baje : ################
 12-13 baje : ################
 13-14 baje : ###############
 14-15 baje : ###########
 15-16 baje : ######
 16-17 baje : ##
              +------------------+
              0%              100%
```

> **Figure 4.4** – Ek saaf din ka maana hua namoona: bar ki lambai us ghante mein panel ki rated power ka kitna hissa mila (20 '#' = poori rated power). Dason ghanton ka jod 0.1 + 0.3 + 0.55 + 0.75 + 0.8 aur phir ulta = 5.0, yaani "5 ghante poori power" ke barabar.

**Formula:** din ki bijli E = (panel ka area A) × (insolation) × (efficiency) × (peak sun hours)

### Example 4. Mr. Ghanekar ki chhat

Chhat par 8 panels hain, har panel 1.6 m² ka. Efficiency 18 percent, insolation lagbhag 1 kW/m², peak sun hours 5. (a) Saaf dopahar mein power? (b) Din ki bijli? (c) 30 din ki? (d) Ghar mahine mein 280 kWh use kare to kitni bijli bachi?

**Given:** n = 8, har panel 1.6 m², efficiency = 0.18, insolation = 1 kW/m², t = 5 h
**Find:** P, E per day, E per month, bachat
**Formula:** A = n × 1.6; P = A × insolation × efficiency; E = P × t
**Substitution:** A = 8 × 1.6 = 12.8 m²; P = 12.8 × 1 × 0.18 = **2.304 kW** (lagbhag 2.3 kW); E = 2.304 × 5 = **11.52 kWh** per din; 30 din mein 11.52 × 30 = **345.6 kWh**; bachat = 345.6 − 280 = **65.6 kWh**
**Answer:** Lagbhag 2.3 kW; 11.5 unit per din; 345.6 unit per mahina; 65.6 unit grid ko.
**Jaanch:** 345.6 ÷ (30 × 5) = 2.304 kW ✅, 2.304 ÷ (1 × 0.18) = 12.8, yaani 12.8 m² ✅. Units: m² × kW/m² = kW; kW × h = kWh ✅.

Mr. Ghanekar ne Kanishk ko bataya ki 11.5 unit roz ka matlab ek achhe-khaase ghar ki bijli ka bada hissa, aur ₹6 per unit (maana hua) par ₹69 roz ke barabar.

### Example 5. 3 kW ke system ke liye kitni jagah

3 kW ka system banana hai, efficiency 18 percent, insolation lagbhag 1 kW/m². Chhat par kitna area chahiye?

**Given:** P = 3 kW, efficiency = 0.18, insolation = 1 kW/m²
**Find:** A
**Formula:** P = A × insolation × efficiency, to A = P ÷ (insolation × efficiency)
**Substitution:** A = 3 ÷ (1 × 0.18) = 16.67 m², yaani **lagbhag 16.7 m²**
**Answer:** Lagbhag 16.7 m² (chhote kamre ka size nahi, bada kamra).
**Jaanch:** 16.67 × 1 × 0.18 = 3.0 kW ✅.

### Example 6. Solar lantern ki battery: V × Ah

Ek solar lantern mein 6 V, 10 Ah ki battery hai. **Ah** (ampere-hour) ka matlab hai itne ampere ka current itne ghante tak. Battery mein kitni energy jama hai, aur 3 W ki LED kitni der chalegi?

**Given:** V = 6 V, capacity = 10 Ah, LED power = 3 W
**Find:** Energy E aur time t
**Formula:** E = V × Ah (Wh mein); t = E ÷ P
**Substitution:** E = 6 × 10 = **60 Wh** = 0.06 kWh = 60 × 3600 = 216,000 J; t = 60 Wh ÷ 3 W = **20 h**
**Answer:** 60 Wh; LED lagbhag 20 ghante chalegi (asal mein thoda kam, kyunki battery poori khaali nahi karte).
**Jaanch:** 3 W × 20 h = 60 Wh ✅. 1 Wh = 3600 J, to 60 Wh = 216,000 J ✅.

## Part E: Chhat se grid tak, aur fayde-nuksaan

### Net metering: bachi bijli ka hisaab

```
               DC                     AC                      bachi bijli                 export
[ Panels ]---------->[ Inverter ]---------->[ Ghar ka board ]------------>[ Net meter ]---------->[ Grid ]

           import                  bijli
[ Grid ]---------->[ Net meter ]---------->[ Ghar ka board ]
```

> **Figure 4.5** – Upar: din mein panels ki bijli pehle ghar ke board mein jaati hai, jo bachti hai wo net meter se hokar grid mein jaati hai (export). Neeche: raat mein panels kuch nahi dete, grid se bijli net meter se hokar ghar mein aati hai (import).

Mahine ke ant mein import aur export ka antar bill banata hai. Agar export zyada hai to bachi bijli ka credit mil jaata hai, aur import zyada hai to wahi antar ka bill dena padta hai. Niyam aur dar jagah ke hisaab se badalte hain, par idea yahi hai. Isse **rooftop solar** ghar ka bijli bill kaafi kam kar deta hai.

### Fayde aur seemayein

| Fayde | Seemayein |
|---|---|
| Dhoop muft hai aur khatam nahi hoti (renewable) | Raat mein kuch nahi, badal mein kam |
| Chalte waqt na dhuan, na CO₂ | Battery ya grid ke bina raat ke liye bijli nahi |
| Koi hissa ghoomta nahi, rakh-rakhaav kam | Bade projects ke liye bahut zameen chahiye |
| Chhat par lag sakta hai, grid se door jagah par bhi | Panel banane mein bhi energy aur material lagta hai (manufacturing footprint) |
| Keemat dheere dheere ghati hai | Panel ki puri keemat shuru mein ek saath deni padti hai |

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Badal wale din solar panel kuch bhi bijli nahi dete"**

Demonstration: Mr. Ghanekar ka system saaf dopahar mein 2.304 kW deta hai. Maan lo ghane badal mein sirf 20 percent roshni panel tak pahunchi. To power = 0.20 × 2.304 = **0.4608 kW**, yaani lagbhag **461 W**. Isse ek ceiling fan (60 W) aur ek fridge (200 W) abhi bhi chal sakte hain. Panel **kam** deta hai, **zero nahi**. Raat ko, jab roshni sach mein zero hoti hai, tab hi output zero hota hai.

**Sahi:** badal mein output ghatta hai, aur raat mein hi zero hota hai. Ghane badal mein bhi bachi roshni bijli banati hai.

**Galti 2: "Solar cooker aur solar cell ek hi cheez hain"**

Demonstration: ek hi 1 m² dhoop-wali jagah lo, jis par saaf dopahar mein 1000 W dhoop girti hai.
- Solar **cell**: 18 percent = **180 W bijli**. Ye 180 W ek bulb, ek pankha ya mobile charger chala sakti hai. Cell se sirf bijli milti hai; khana pakane ke liye us bijli ko alag heater mein dena padega.
- Solar **cooker** (hamare maane hue 40 percent par): **400 W garmi**. Ye paani garam kar degi, par isse mobile charge nahi hoga aur bulb nahi jalega.

Dono "solar" hain, par ek dhoop ko **garmi** mein badalta hai (kaala rang, kaanch, mirror), doosra **bijli** mein (silicon). Dono ke output alag kism ke hain, isliye 400 W garmi aur 180 W bijli ko seedha tulna karna theek nahi.

**Sahi:** cooker = dhoop → garmi. Cell = dhoop → bijli. Dono ko alag naam aur alag kaam ke liye use karte hain.

## Quick Recap

- Koyla, hawa, behta paani, lakdi aur anaaj, sab ki jad lagbhag Suraj hai. Solar cell Suraj se **seedha** bijli banata hai.
- Solar constant lagbhag 1.4 kW/m² atmosphere ke bahar; zameen par saaf dopahar mein lagbhag 1 kW/m².
- **Solar cooker:** kaali sateh (sokhti hai), kaanch (greenhouse: garmi andar), mirror (zyada dhoop), thermocol. Andar lagbhag 100 se 140 °C.
- **Solar cell:** silicon, lagbhag 0.5 V per cell, series mein jodkar panel, kai panel ka array; DC bijli, inverter se AC.
- Efficiency lagbhag 15 se 20 percent (hum 18 percent lete hain).
- Din ki bijli: **E = A × insolation × efficiency × peak sun hours** (5 h). Battery energy = V × Ah (Wh).
- **Net metering:** bachi bijli grid ko, raat mein grid se lena; mahine ka antar bill banata hai.
- Fayde: dhoop muft, dhuan nahi. Seemayein: raat, badal, zameen, battery, manufacturing.
- Badal mein panel **kam** deta hai, zero nahi. Cooker aur cell **alag** hain.

## Practice Questions

1. A solar panel of area 2 m² and efficiency 18 percent faces the Sun at a clear noon (about 1 kW/m²). Find (a) its power output, (b) the energy it gives in a day of 5 peak sun hours, in kWh, and (c) for how long that energy can run a 45 W fan.

2. A 5 kW rooftop system (rated at 1 kW/m² with 18 percent efficiency) gets 5 peak sun hours every day. Find (a) the energy per day, (b) the energy in 30 days, (c) the panel area needed, and (d) the worth of the 30-day energy at ₹6 per unit (assumed).

3. A school roof has an area of 120 m². Panels cover 60 percent of it. Efficiency is 18 percent. Find (a) the power at clear noon, (b) the energy in a day of 5 peak sun hours, and (c) the fraction of the school's use of 150 kWh per day (assumed) that this supplies.

4. A solar lantern has a 12 V, 7 Ah battery. Find (a) the energy stored in Wh, kWh and joules, (b) how long it can run a 6 W LED, and (c) how long a 20 W panel in full sun would need to refill it, ignoring losses.

5. A box cooker has an aperture of 0.5 m². Assume 35 percent of the sunlight energy reaches 2 L (2 kg) of water, in full clear noon sun. Find the heat needed to warm it from 28 °C to 98 °C and the time taken. (c of water = 4200 J/kg °C)

6. Each solar cell gives 0.5 V and a current of 3 A in full sun. How many cells in series make a 24 V panel? What current and power does that panel give?

7. A 4 kW rooftop system is under a thick cloud that lets through only 20 percent of the clear-sky light (assumed). What is its output? What is the output at 8 pm in the dark?

8. A house with rooftop solar and a net meter exports 210 kWh and imports 170 kWh in a month. What is the net export, and what happens to the bill? (Assume a net import is billed and a net export is carried forward as credit.)

9. Panel A has an area of 1.7 m² and gives 340 W at 1 kW/m². Panel B has an area of 1.9 m² and gives 285 W at 1 kW/m². Find each efficiency and the area each needs for a 1 kW array.

10. In a box solar cooker, state the job of (a) the black inner surface, (b) the glass lid, (c) the mirror reflector. What would happen to the temperature if the lid were removed, and why is a solar cooker not a solar cell?

11. Trace each of these back to the Sun in a short chain: (a) a tube light run by a hydro plant, (b) a stove burning cow-dung cake, (c) a diesel generator. Name one energy source that does not come from the Sun.

12. A satellite has 6 m² of panel at 18 percent efficiency in sunlight at about 1.4 kW/m². Find its output. What would the same panel give on the ground at a clear noon (about 1 kW/m²)? Compare the two.

13. A solar street light has a 14 W LED that stays on for 11 hours every night. Find the energy it needs in Wh, and the smallest panel (in W, at 5 peak sun hours, ignoring losses) that can refill it each day.

14. True or false, with a one-line reason: (a) a solar cell gives AC directly, (b) ten identical cells in series give ten times the voltage of one, (c) a solar cooker works as well in thick cloud as in a clear sky, (d) 18 percent efficiency means 18 J of electricity from every 100 J of sunlight on the panel, (e) a solar panel produces nothing at all on a cloudy day, (f) a solar cooker produces electricity.

### Answers

1. **Given:** A = 2 m², efficiency 0.18, insolation 1 kW/m², t = 5 h, fan 45 W. **Formula:** P = A × insolation × efficiency; E = P × t; t = E ÷ P_fan. (a) P = 2 × 1000 × 0.18 = **360 W**. (b) E = 0.36 kW × 5 h = **1.8 kWh**. (c) 1800 Wh ÷ 45 W = **40 h**.
   Jaanch: 45 W × 40 h = 1800 Wh ✅.

2. **Given:** P = 5 kW, t = 5 h, 30 din. (a) E = 5 × 5 = **25 kWh**. (b) 25 × 30 = **750 kWh**. (c) A = 5000 ÷ (1000 × 0.18) = 27.78, yaani **lagbhag 27.8 m²**. (d) 750 × 6 = **₹4500**.
   Jaanch: 27.78 × 1 × 0.18 = 5.0 kW ✅; 25 × 30 = 750 ✅.

3. (a) A = 0.60 × 120 = 72 m²; P = 72 × 1 × 0.18 = **12.96 kW** (lagbhag 13 kW). (b) E = 12.96 × 5 = **64.8 kWh**. (c) 64.8 ÷ 150 = 0.432, yaani **43.2 percent**.
   Jaanch: 0.432 × 150 = 64.8 ✅.

4. **Given:** V = 12 V, 7 Ah. (a) E = 12 × 7 = **84 Wh** = **0.084 kWh** = 84 × 3600 = **302,400 J**. (b) t = 84 ÷ 6 = **14 h**. (c) t = 84 ÷ 20 = **4.2 h**.
   Jaanch: 6 × 14 = 84 Wh ✅; 20 × 4.2 = 84 Wh ✅.

5. **Given:** A = 0.5 m², 35 percent, m = 2 kg, ΔT = 98 − 28 = 70 °C. **Formula:** Q = m c ΔT; P = A × 1000 × 0.35; t = Q ÷ P. Q = 2 × 4200 × 70 = **588,000 J**. P = 0.5 × 1000 × 0.35 = 175 W. t = 588,000 ÷ 175 = **3360 s = 56 min**.
   Jaanch: 175 × 3360 = 588,000 J ✅.

6. Series mein voltage jud jaata hai: n = 24 ÷ 0.5 = **48 cells**. Series mein current wahi rehta hai: **3 A**. P = V × I = 24 × 3 = **72 W**.
   Jaanch: 48 × 0.5 = 24 V ✅; ek cell ki power 0.5 × 3 = 1.5 W, 48 × 1.5 = 72 W ✅.

7. Cloud mein: 0.20 × 4 = **0.8 kW** (800 W), zero nahi. Raat 8 baje: roshni zero, to output **0 W**.
   Jaanch: 0.8 ÷ 4 = 0.2 ✅.

8. Net = 210 − 170 = **40 kWh export**. Import se zyada export hai, to bill mein koi net units nahi bante, aur 40 kWh ka credit aage jaata hai.
   Jaanch: 170 + 40 = 210 ✅.

9. Panel A: efficiency = 340 ÷ (1.7 × 1000) = 0.20 = **20 percent**. Panel B: 285 ÷ (1.9 × 1000) = 0.15 = **15 percent**. 1 kW ke liye: A mein 1000 ÷ (1000 × 0.20) = **5.0 m²**, B mein 1000 ÷ (1000 × 0.15) = **6.67 m²**. Zyaada efficient panel kam jagah leta hai.
   Jaanch: 5.0 × 200 W/m² = 1000 W ✅, 6.67 × 150 = 1000 W ✅.

10. (a) Kaali sateh dhoop ko sokhkar garmi banati hai. (b) Kaanch roshni ko andar aane deta hai par garmi (infrared aur garam hawa) ko bahar nahi jaane deta: greenhouse idea. (c) Mirror zyada dhoop andar mod deta hai. Lid hatane par garm hawa aur garmi bahar nikal jaati hai, to temperature **kaafi kam** rahega. Cooker dhoop ko **garmi** mein badalta hai, cell **bijli** mein.
    Jaanch: teeno hisse alag kaam karte hain ✅.

11. (a) Suraj → samundar se bhaap → baarish → dam ka paani → turbine → generator → tube light. (b) Suraj → ghaas → gaay ka khana → gobar → cake → jalna → garmi. (c) Suraj → purane jeev → petroleum → diesel → engine → generator → bijli. Jo Suraj se nahi aata: **zameen ke andar ki garmi (geothermal)** ya **uranium (nuclear)**; tide mukhya roop se Chaand ki kheench se bante hain.
    Jaanch: har chain mein Suraj pehle aaya ✅.

12. Satellite: P = 6 × 1.4 × 0.18 = **1.512 kW**, yaani lagbhag **1.5 kW**. Zameen par: 6 × 1 × 0.18 = **1.08 kW**. Anupaat 1.512 ÷ 1.08 = **1.4**, yaani satellite par 1.4 guna zyada (kyunki 1.4 ÷ 1 = 1.4).
    Jaanch: ratio insolation ke ratio se match karta hai ✅.

13. E = 14 × 11 = **154 Wh**. Panel: 154 ÷ 5 = **30.8 W** (lagbhag 31 W; asal mein losses ke liye thoda bada lete hain).
    Jaanch: 30.8 W × 5 h = 154 Wh ✅.

14. (a) **False** ❌: solar cell DC deta hai; AC ke liye inverter. (b) **True** ✅: series mein voltage jud jaata hai (10 × 0.5 V = 5 V). (c) **False** ❌: ghane badal mein dhoop kam, cooker bahut slow. (d) **True** ✅: efficiency = useful ÷ input × 100. (e) **False** ❌: panel kam deta hai, zero nahi. (f) **False** ❌: cooker garmi deta hai, bijli nahi.
