## Pahaad ka kund, gobar-gas aur akhbaar ki line

Mr. Bhalerao ek pahaadi mandir-shehar ke guesthouse mein Pavani ko ghumane laye the. Pavani unki bhanji thi, aur pehli baar pahaad par aayi thi. Subah sabse pehle wo **garam paani ka kund** dekhne gayi: pathar ke gadde se bhaap uth rahi thi aur railing par likha tha "paani bahut garam hai, andar mat jao". "Mama, yahan koi chulha to dikhta nahi. Paani ko garam kisne kiya?"

Mr. Bhalerao ne kaha, "Zameen ne. Ruko, pehle nashta."

Guesthouse ki rasoi mein ek moti nali aangan ke gol dhakkan se aati thi, aur gas ka neeli lau wala chulha jal raha tha. "Mama, ye cylinder wali gas nahi hai, na?" Mr. Bhalerao hanse. "Ye **gobar-gas** hai. Guesthouse ki gaayon ka gobar aangan ke gaddhe mein jaata hai aur wahin se gas banti hai. Dekho, na dhuan, na kaalikh."

Chai ki dukaan par akhbaar padha hua tha. Pavani ne pehle panne ki ek line uthayi: "Parmanu bijlighar ki nayi unit ne bijli dena shuru kiya." Wo ghabra gayi. "Mama, nuclear matlab atom bomb? Wo to fat jaata hai na? Phir bijlighar mein kyun?"

"Teen sawaal ho gaye," Mr. Bhalerao ne kaha, "kund, gobar-gas aur nuclear. Teeno 'zameen ke andar ki' ya 'jeevon ke bache hue' energy ki kahani hain. Chalo, ek ek kar ke."

## Part A: Biomass aur biogas

### Jeevon se bana fuel

**Biomass** wo sab hai jo kabhi jeevit tha ya jeevon se bana: lakdi, gobar ka upla, fasal ka bhusa, ganne ki khoi. Topic 1 mein hum inka calorific value dekh chuke hain (shared table): lakdi lagbhag 17,000 kJ/kg aur gobar ka upla lagbhag 7,000 kJ/kg. Ek aur roop hai **charcoal**: lakdi ko bahut kam hawa mein garam karne par bani kaali cheez, jo lakdi se zyada garmi deti hai aur kam dhuan karti hai.

Biomass tabhi renewable hai jab hum jitna kaatein, utna ugayein. Nahi to jungle ghatta hai. Aur jalane par dhuan aur kan nikalte hain, isliye bandh rasoi mein dung cake ya lakdi ka dhuan phephdon ke liye bura hai (Topic 1 mein yahi galti dekhi thi ki renewable ka matlab pollution-free nahi).

### Biogas plant: dhuan ke bina gobar ki energy

Chemistry mein humne biogas plant ka kaam dekha tha ([carbon-and-its-compounds-part4.md](../../chemistry/carbon-and-its-compounds/carbon-and-its-compounds-part4.md)): gobar aur paani ko band gaddhe mein bina hawa ke bacteria sadate hain, aur banne wali gas mein lagbhag 55 se 65 percent methane hota hai. Yahan hum use **energy ke nazariye** se dekhte hain. **Fixed-dome plant** mein gaddha aur uska dhakkan (dome) zameen mein pakka bana hota hai. Gas banti hai to oopar jama hoti hai aur slurry ko neeche se outlet tank ki taraf dhakel deti hai. Chulha jalane par gas nikalti hai aur slurry wapas neeche aati hai.

```
                  milao                 neeche nali                                  gas jama               nali
[ Gobar + paani ]------->[ Inlet tank ]------------->[ Digester (band, hawa nahi) ]---------->[ Gas dome ]------>[ Chulha ]

              slurry nikli                  hataao
[ Digester ]-------------->[ Outlet tank ]-------->[ Khaad ka gaddha ]
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Fixed-dome biogas plant ka block diagram. Upar: gobar aur paani milkar inlet tank se digester mein jaate hain; band digester mein bacteria gas banate hain jo dome mein jama hokar nali se chulhe tak jaati hai. Neeche: digester se nikli slurry outlet tank mein aati hai aur wahan se khaad ke gaddhe mein jaati hai. Plant ke paas aag nahi, aur gaddhe mein kabhi jhaankna ya utarna nahi.

**Biogas dung cake se behtar kyun?**

- **Dhuan nahi:** biogas saaf jalti hai, rasoi mein kaalikh aur aankhon ki jalan nahi.
- **Zyada garmi:** 1 m³ biogas lagbhag 20 MJ deti hai; utni hi energy ke liye dung cake (7 MJ/kg) lagbhag 20 ÷ 7 = **2.9 kg** jalana padta hai. (Biogas gas hai, isliye hum use kg mein nahi badalte.)
- **Khaad bachti hai:** cake jalane se gobar ke poshak tatva raakh aur hawa mein chale jaate hain; plant mein slurry khaad ban kar khet mein wapas jaati hai.
- **Chulha asaani se control hota hai:** gas ko ek knob se kam ya zyada kar sakte hain.

### Example 1. 4 m³ biogas ka roz ka hisaab

Ek gaon ke plant se roz 4 m³ biogas milti hai. Is gas mein lagbhag 20 MJ per m³ energy hai. Roz ki energy kitni hai, aur utni energy LPG (50 MJ/kg) ke kitne kg mein hoti?

**Given:** V = 4 m³ per din, 20 MJ per m³, LPG = 50 MJ/kg
**Find:** E per din, LPG ka mass
**Formula:** E = V × (energy per m³); mass = E ÷ (calorific value)
**Substitution:** E = 4 × 20 = **80 MJ**; mass = 80 ÷ 50 = **1.6 kg**
**Answer:** Roz lagbhag 80 MJ, jo lagbhag 1.6 kg LPG ke barabar hai.
**Jaanch:** 1.6 kg × 50 MJ/kg = 80 MJ ✅. Unit: m³ × MJ/m³ = MJ ✅.

### Example 2. Ek parivaar ka dung cake

Ek parivaar roz lagbhag 3 kg dung cake jalata hai (maana hua). 30 din mein kitni energy nikalti hai, aur utni energy kitni m³ biogas (20 MJ/m³) mein hoti? Kya Example 1 ka plant kaafi hai?

**Given:** 3 kg per din, 30 din, cake = 7 MJ/kg, biogas = 20 MJ/m³
**Find:** E aur V
**Formula:** E = mass × calorific value; V = E ÷ 20
**Substitution:** mass = 3 × 30 = 90 kg; E = 90 × 7 = **630 MJ**; V = 630 ÷ 20 = **31.5 m³**
**Answer:** 630 MJ, yaani 31.5 m³ biogas. Example 1 ka plant 30 din mein 4 × 30 = **120 m³** deta hai, to kaafi se zyada hai.
**Jaanch:** 31.5 × 20 = 630 MJ ✅. 120 > 31.5 ✅.

(Dhyan: chulhe ki apni efficiency alag hoti hai, aur biogas ka burner dung cake ke chulhe se kaafi achha hota hai. Isliye asli bachat is hisaab se aur bhi zyada hoti hai.)

### Activity 1: Apne mohalle ka biomass nakshaa (ghar par adult ke saath)

Ek kagaz par teen khane banao: lakdi, gobar ka upla, fasal ka kachra. Apne ghar, dukaan ya mohalle mein dekho kaun inhe kahan use karta hai (dhaba, bhatti, gaon ka ghar, mandir ka bhandara) aur likho. Kuch jalana ya chhoona nahi hai. Sirf dekhna, poochna aur likhna.

## Part B: Geothermal: zameen ki apni bhatti

Zameen jitni gehri, utni garam. Kuan khodne par bhi temperature badhta jaata hai, aur kai kilometre neeche chattanein bahut garam hain. Is garmi ka ek hissa Earth ke bante samay ka bacha hua hai, aur ek hissa chattanon ke andar ke radioactive tatvon se aata hai (Chapter 11 Radioactivity mein aur). **Isliye geothermal ka Suraj se koi rishta nahi.**

Kai jagah baarish ka paani dararon se neeche jaata hai, garam chattanon se tapkar bhaap ya bahut garam paani banta hai aur oopar aa jaata hai. Wahi hai kund, **hot spring** aur geyser (zameen se phoot kar nikalta fawwara). Isi garm bhaap se turbine ghumayi ja sakti hai, bilkul thermal plant ki tarah (Topic 2), bas yahan **boiler aur coal ki zaroorat nahi**.

```
                 thanda paani neeche                     bhaap upar             ghumaye                bijli
[ Injection well ]--------------------->[ Garam chattan ]------------>[ Turbine ]--------->[ Generator ]------->[ Grid ]

            bhaap bachi                wapas paani
[ Turbine ]------------->[ Condenser ]------------->[ Injection well ]
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Geothermal plant ka block diagram. Ek well se thanda paani garam chattan tak jaata hai, wahan garam hokar bhaap ban kar doosre (production) well se oopar aati hai aur turbine ko ghumati hai. Turbine generator ko ghumata hai. Bachi bhaap condenser mein paani ban kar wapas injection well mein bheji jaati hai, taaki paani bhi bache aur zameen ke andar dabav bana rahe.

**Kahan?** Iceland, New Zealand aur USA mein geothermal plants chalte hain, kyunki wahan garam chattanein zameen ke paas hain. Bharat mein **Puga valley (Ladakh)** aur **Manikaran (Himachal)** ke garam jharne is liye jaane jaate hain ki wahan geothermal energy ki **potential** hai: kaam abhi shuruaati hai, bade plants nahi.

**Fayde:** steady (din raat, mausam se bina farak), chalte waqt CO₂ bahut kam, fuel muft. **Seemayein:** sirf kuch khaas jagahon par; zameen se kabhi kabhi sulphur wali gas aur khanij nikalte hain; temperature kam hone par efficiency kam rehti hai.

### Example 3. Ek geothermal plant

Ek plant ko zameen se 120 MW garmi milti hai aur wo 12 percent efficiency se bijli banata hai. Wo din bhar chalta hai. Bijli kitni, din mein kitne kWh, aur Topic 2 ke 0.34 kg coal per kWh se kitna coal bachta? (Asli coal ke 0.6 kg per kWh par bhi nikalo.)

**Given:** garmi 120 MW, efficiency 0.12, t = 24 h, coal 0.34 kg per kWh aur 0.6 kg per kWh
**Find:** P, E, coal
**Formula:** P = input × efficiency; E = P × t; coal = E × (kg per kWh)
**Substitution:** P = 120 × 0.12 = **14.4 MW**; E = 14.4 × 24 = 345.6 MWh = **345,600 kWh**; coal = 345,600 × 0.34 = 117,504 kg = **lagbhag 117.5 tonne**; 345,600 × 0.6 = 207,360 kg = **lagbhag 207 tonne**
**Answer:** 14.4 MW, 345,600 kWh per din; roz lagbhag 117 se 207 tonne coal bachta.
**Jaanch:** 14.4 ÷ 120 = 0.12 ✅; 345,600 kWh × 0.34 kg = 117,504 kg ✅. Unit: MW × h = MWh ✅.

## Part C: Nuclear fission: parmanu ko todna

### Ek neutron, ek dhamaka, aur teen neutron

Class 9 mein humne **isotopes** dekhe the: ek hi tatva ke atom jinme neutron alag. Uranium ke do isotope zaroori hain: **U-238** (kudrati uranium ka lagbhag 99.3 percent) aur **U-235** (lagbhag 0.7 percent). Jab ek dheema neutron U-235 ke nucleus se takrata hai, nucleus doladne lagta hai aur **do medium tukdon mein toot jaata hai**, saath mein **2 ya 3 neutron** aur bahut saari energy nikalti hai. Ek tareeka:

U-235 + n → Ba-141 + Kr-92 + 3 n + energy

Jaanch: upar 235 + 1 = 236 aur neeche 141 + 92 + 3 = 236 ✅; protons: 92 = 56 + 36 ✅.

Nikle hue neutron agle U-235 nuclei ko todte hain, wo aur neutron chhodte hain: yahi **chain reaction** hai.

```
Bina control: har fission 2 naye fission karata hai (# = ek fission)
 Pidhi 0 : #
 Pidhi 1 : ##
 Pidhi 2 : ####
 Pidhi 3 : ########
 Pidhi 4 : ################

Control rods ke saath: har fission ke baad sirf ek naya fission
 Pidhi 0 : #
 Pidhi 1 : #
 Pidhi 2 : #
 Pidhi 3 : #
 Pidhi 4 : #
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Chain reaction ki pidhiyan. Upar: har fission do aur karaye to 1, 2, 4, 8, 16: 10 pidhi mein 2¹⁰ = 1024, 20 pidhi mein 1,048,576. Neeche: reactor mein control rods itne neutron sokh lete hain ki ek fission ke baad ausat ek hi naya fission hota hai, to reaction steady rehti hai, na badhti na bujhti.

### Example 4. Ek fission aur ek kilo

Ek fission lagbhag 200 MeV deta hai. (a) Ye joule mein kitna? (b) 1 kg U-235 mein sab nuclei toot jayein to kitne fission chahiye, agar kul energy lagbhag 8 × 10¹³ J ho?

**Given:** 200 MeV per fission, 1 MeV = 1.6 × 10⁻¹³ J, 1 kg se 8 × 10¹³ J
**Find:** (a) E₁ per fission, (b) fission ki ginti
**Formula:** E₁ = 200 × 1.6 × 10⁻¹³; ginti = kul energy ÷ E₁
**Substitution:** E₁ = **3.2 × 10⁻¹¹ J**; ginti = 8 × 10¹³ ÷ 3.2 × 10⁻¹¹ = **2.5 × 10²⁴**
**Answer:** Ek fission bahut chhota (3.2 × 10⁻¹¹ J), par 1 kg mein lagbhag 2.5 × 10²⁴ nuclei hote hain.
**Jaanch:** 1000 g ÷ 235 g per mole × 6 × 10²³ ≈ 2.6 × 10²⁴ ✅ (lagbhag wahi). Chhote dhamake ki badi ginti hi badi energy hai.

### Activity 2: Domino chain (ghar par adult ke saath)

20 dominoes ya kitaabein ek ke baad ek khadi karo, aur ek jagah se Y-aakar mein do shaakhein banao. Pehle domino ko dhakka do: poori chain girti hai. Ab beech ke kuch dominoes hata do (ye hain **control rods**): chain wahin ruk jaati hai. Soch ke likho: reactor mein rods kab andar aate hain aur kab bahar?

## Part D: Reactor: control mein chalti chain

Nuclear power plant ko samajhna asaan hai agar thermal plant yaad ho (Topic 2): wahan coal boiler mein jalta tha, yahan **fission reactor** mein hoti hai. Bhaap banana, turbine ghumana, generator, condenser: **sab wahi**.

```
##############################################
# REACTOR CORE                               #
# fuel rods    : uranium, yahin fission      #
# control rods : neutron sokhte hain         #
# moderator    : neutron ko dheema karta hai #
##############################################

                 garam coolant                      bhaap              ghumaye                bijli
[ Reactor core ]--------------->[ Steam generator ]------->[ Turbine ]--------->[ Generator ]------->[ Grid ]

                    thanda coolant (pump)
[ Steam generator ]----------------------->[ Reactor core ]
```

> **Figure 5.4** – Nuclear power plant ka block diagram. Core mein fuel rods (U-235 wale) fission se garam hote hain, control rods neutron sokhkar chain ko kaabu mein rakhti hain, moderator neutron ko dheema karta hai. Coolant core ki garmi steam generator tak le jaata hai, wahan paani se bhaap banti hai, bhaap turbine ko ghumati hai aur turbine generator ko. Thanda hua coolant pump se wapas core mein jaata hai. (Bhaap ko condenser mein wapas paani banana thermal plant jaisa hai: yahan nahi dikhaya.)

- **Fuel rods:** enriched uranium ki goliyan patli naliyon mein. Reactor ka fuel lagbhag 3 se 5 percent U-235 wala hota hai.
- **Control rods:** cadmium ya boron ki. Zyada andar utaro to zyada neutron sokhenge aur reaction dheeme ya band; bahar nikalo to reaction badhegi.
- **Moderator:** dheema neutron U-235 ko zyada asaani se todta hai. Paani ya graphite kaam aata hai; Class 9 ka **heavy water** bhi kuch reactors mein moderator hai.
- **Coolant:** agar garmi na nikle to core bahut garam ho jaayega. Band karne ke baad bhi fuel kuch der garmi deta rehta hai, isliye thandak chalti rehni chahiye.

Bharat mein **Tarapur** (Maharashtra) aur **Kudankulam** (Tamil Nadu) parmanu bijlighar hain.

## Part E: E = mc² aur itni energy kahan se?

Fission ke tukdon aur neutron ka kul mass shuru ke U-235 aur neutron ke mass se **thoda kam** hota hai. Ye bacha hua mass gaayab nahi hota, energy ban jaata hai: **E = mc²**, jahan c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s. c² = 9 × 10¹⁶ m²/s² itna bada hai ki thoda sa mass bhi bahut energy ban jaata hai.

### Example 5. 1 milligram mass ka energy

Kisi nuclear reaction mein 1 mg (1 × 10⁻⁶ kg) mass energy ban jaata hai. Kitni energy, kitne kWh aur coal (30 MJ/kg) ke kitne kg ke barabar?

**Given:** m = 1 × 10⁻⁶ kg, c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s
**Find:** E, kWh, coal
**Formula:** E = mc²; kWh = E ÷ 3.6 × 10⁶; coal = E ÷ (30 × 10⁶ J/kg)
**Substitution:** E = 1 × 10⁻⁶ × 9 × 10¹⁶ = **9 × 10¹⁰ J**; kWh = 9 × 10¹⁰ ÷ 3.6 × 10⁶ = **25,000 kWh**; coal = 9 × 10¹⁰ ÷ 3 × 10⁷ = **3000 kg = 3 tonne**
**Answer:** 9 × 10¹⁰ J, yaani 25,000 unit bijli, jo 3 tonne coal jalane ke barabar hai.
**Jaanch:** Unit: kg × (m/s)² = J ✅. Dekho, 1 g U-235 se lagbhag 8 × 10¹⁰ J milte hain: mass ka sirf lagbhag 0.09 percent (8 × 10¹⁰ ÷ 9 × 10¹⁶ = 0.9 mg per g) energy bana ✅.

### Ek kilo uranium, kitna coal?

1 kg U-235 se lagbhag 8 × 10¹³ J milta hai (lagbhag 22 million kWh). Utni hi energy dene wale doosre fuel ka mass (shared calorific values se):

| Fuel | Energy per kg | Utni (8 × 10¹³ J) energy ke liye mass |
|---|---|---|
| Uranium-235 | lagbhag 8 × 10¹³ J | 1 kg |
| Hydrogen | 150,000 kJ/kg | lagbhag 533 tonne |
| LPG | 50,000 kJ/kg | lagbhag 1600 tonne |
| Petrol | 45,000 kJ/kg | lagbhag 1778 tonne |
| Coal | 30,000 kJ/kg | lagbhag 2667 tonne |
| Lakdi (dry) | 17,000 kJ/kg | lagbhag 4706 tonne |

Coal se tulna: 8 × 10¹³ ÷ 3 × 10⁷ = 2.67 × 10⁶ kg, yaani **1 kg uranium lagbhag 2700 tonne coal ke barabar** (kai kitaabon mein lagbhag 2500 tonne bhi likha milta hai). Isliye ek reactor ko saal bhar mein tonnon uranium chahiye, coal plant ko lakhon tonne.

### Example 6. 640 MW ka reactor roz kitna uranium todta hai?

Ek nuclear plant 640 MW bijli deta hai aur uski overall efficiency lagbhag 32 percent hai. Din mein kitna U-235 fission hota hai (1 kg = 8 × 10¹³ J)? Utni garmi ke liye coal kitna?

**Given:** P = 640 MW, efficiency 0.32, t = 24 h = 86,400 s
**Find:** fuel ki garmi, uranium aur coal
**Formula:** input power = output ÷ efficiency; E = power × t; uranium = E ÷ (8 × 10¹³ J/kg)
**Substitution:** input = 640 ÷ 0.32 = 2000 MW = 2 × 10⁹ W; E = 2 × 10⁹ × 86,400 = **1.728 × 10¹⁴ J**; uranium = 1.728 × 10¹⁴ ÷ 8 × 10¹³ = **2.16 kg**; coal = 1.728 × 10¹⁴ ÷ 3 × 10⁷ = 5.76 × 10⁶ kg = **5760 tonne**
**Answer:** Roz lagbhag 2.16 kg uranium (coal se lagbhag 5760 tonne).
**Jaanch:** 2.16 kg mein se energy bana mass = 1.728 × 10¹⁴ ÷ 9 × 10¹⁶ = 1.92 × 10⁻³ kg (lagbhag 1.9 g), yaani 2.16 kg ka 0.09 percent ✅, jo Example 5 ke 0.09 percent se milta hai.

## Part F: Fayde, khatre aur bhavishya

| Fayde | Khatre aur seemayein |
|---|---|
| Thodi si fuel mein bahut energy | Radioactive waste: kuch hissa hazaaron saal khatarnaak rehta hai, isliye ise shield karke surakshit rakha jaata hai |
| Din raat steady bijli, mausam se bebas | Bahut mehnga banana aur band karna |
| Chalte waqt CO₂ lagbhag nahi | Sabse bada darr: accident |
| Chhoti jagah mein bada plant | Uranium khaan se aata hai, limited hai; kai deshon ko bahar se lena padta hai |

**Do accident:** **Chernobyl (1986)** mein reactor ek jaanch ke dauran kaabu se bahar hokar bahut garam ho gaya aur bhaap ka dhamaka hua. **Fukushima (2011)** mein bhookamp aur tsunami ne cooling pumps ki bijli kaat di, to fuel bahut garam ho gaya. Dono mein radioactive padarth bahar nikla. Isliye reactor mein cooling aur kai suraksha tareeke zaroori hain.

Uranium khaan se aata hai aur ek baar kharch hone par dobara nahi banta, isliye ise aam taur par non-renewable gina jaata hai.

### Suraj ki energy: fusion

Fission mein bhaari nucleus tootta hai. **Fusion** mein halke nuclei jud jaate hain: Suraj ke andar hydrogen ke nuclei jud kar helium banate hain, aur yahan bhi mass thoda kam hota hai jo E = mc² se energy ban jaata hai. Iske liye **kai million degrees** aur bahut dabav chahiye, jo Suraj ke andar milta hai. Hydrogen ke isotopes (deuterium aur tritium, Class 9) ka ek jodna: ²H + ³H → ⁴He + n (2 + 3 = 4 + 1 ✅). Dharti par fusion reactor **abhi ban raha hai**: koi fusion plant abhi grid ko bijli nahi deta.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Nuclear power plant bomb ki tarah phat sakta hai"**

Demonstration: bomb mein bahut zyada enriched uranium (lagbhag 90 percent U-235) chahiye aur chain ko achanak bahut tezi se badhne diya jaata hai. 1000 kg fuel mein 90 percent matlab 900 kg U-235; reactor ke fuel mein lagbhag 4 percent, yaani 1000 kg mein sirf **40 kg**. Upar se control rods chain ko 1, 1, 1, 1 par rakhti hain, jabki bina control 1, 2, 4, 8 (Figure 5.3). Reactor ka khatra bomb jaisa dhamaka nahi: asli khatra hai **garmi ka control se bahar hona** (cooling fail) aur radioactive padarth ka bahar nikalna, jaisa Chernobyl aur Fukushima mein dekha.

**Sahi:** reactor ka fuel bomb-grade nahi hota, to wo parmanu bomb ki tarah nahi phat sakta. Par reactor mein bhaap ya hydrogen gas ke dhamake ho sakte hain (Chernobyl mein bhaap ka, Fukushima mein hydrogen ke dhamake hue), isliye khatre alag aur sachche hain: cooling, accident aur waste.

**Galti 2: "Biogas aur natural gas alag kism ki gas hain"**

Demonstration: dono mein mukhya gas **methane (CH₄)** hai. Natural gas lagbhag poori methane hoti hai (zyaadatar 90 percent se oopar); biogas mein lagbhag 55 se 65 percent, baaki mukhya roop se CO₂. Maan lo biogas mein 60 percent methane aur natural gas mein 90 percent: 1 m³ natural gas mein biogas se 90 ÷ 60 = **1.5 guna** methane hota hai, to utni hi matra ki gas se lagbhag 1.5 guna garmi milti hai. Farak methane ki alag gas hone ka nahi, **na jalne wali CO₂ ke hisse ka** hai. Doosra farak **kahan se**: natural gas karodon saal purane jeevon se (fossil), biogas aaj ke gobar se.

**Sahi:** dono mainly methane. Biogas taaza biomass se, natural gas fossil se.

## Quick Recap

- **Biomass:** lakdi, gobar, fasal ka kachra. **Biogas:** lagbhag 55 se 65 percent methane; fixed-dome plant; slurry khaad. Biogas lagbhag 20 MJ per m³ (kg mein nahi badalte), dung cake lagbhag 7,000 kJ/kg, biogas smokeless.
- **Geothermal:** zameen ki garam chattanon ki garmi, Suraj se nahi. Iceland, New Zealand, USA. Puga (Ladakh) aur Manikaran potential.
- **Fission:** U-235 + neutron → 2 tukde + 2 ya 3 neutron + energy; chain reaction. Control rods (cadmium, boron) sokhte hain; moderator dheema karta hai; coolant garmi le jaata hai.
- **E = mc²** (c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s); 1 kg U-235 lagbhag 8 × 10¹³ J, lagbhag 2700 tonne coal ke barabar. 1 MeV = 1.6 × 10⁻¹³ J.
- **Khatre:** waste, accident (Chernobyl 1986, Fukushima 2011), cost. Plants: Tarapur, Kudankulam.
- **Fusion:** hydrogen se helium, Suraj mein; dharti par reactor abhi ban raha hai.
- Reactor bomb nahi; biogas aur natural gas dono mainly methane.

## Practice Questions

1. A biogas plant gives 2 m³ of gas per day at 20 MJ/m³. Find the energy per day, the energy in 30 days, the LPG (50 MJ/kg) needed for the same 30-day energy, and the dung cake (7 MJ/kg) that would give it.

2. A family burns 150 kg of dung cake in a month (7 MJ/kg). Find the energy released, the volume of biogas (20 MJ/m³) with the same energy and the mass of dry wood (17 MJ/kg). Is a 2 m³ per day plant enough for 30 days?

3. A geothermal plant receives 200 MW of heat and gives 30 MW of electricity. Find its efficiency, its electrical energy in 24 hours in kWh, and the coal saved per day at 0.34 kg per kWh and at 0.6 kg per kWh.

4. A mass of 2 g is completely converted into energy. Find the energy in joules and kWh, and the mass of coal (30 MJ/kg) that gives the same energy.

5. A 1000 MW nuclear plant has an efficiency of 33 percent. Find how many kg of U-235 are fissioned per day, and per year (365 days), taking 1 kg = 8 × 10¹³ J. Also find the coal (30 MJ/kg) needed per day for the same heat.

6. A reactor produces 800 MW of heat. If each fission gives 200 MeV (1 MeV = 1.6 × 10⁻¹³ J), how many fissions per second take place?

7. In a chain reaction each fission causes 3 new fissions. Starting with 1 fission, how many fissions are there in the 8th generation? What number results if control rods keep it at an average of 1 per fission?

8. Name the part of a reactor that does each job: (a) soaks up neutrons, (b) slows neutrons, (c) carries heat out of the core. What happens if the control rods are pushed fully into the core?

9. For 360 MJ of heat, find the volume of biogas (20 MJ/m³) and, separately, the mass of CNG (50 MJ/kg). Why are both called mainly methane, and in what way do they truly differ?

10. Reactor fuel is lagbhag 4 percent U-235 and bomb material lagbhag 90 percent. Find the U-235 in 500 kg of each. Why does this matter for the statement "a power plant can explode like a bomb"?

11. Why is geothermal energy not counted as coming from the Sun? Why is used water injected back into the ground? Name two countries with geothermal plants and two Indian sites with potential.

12. A thermal plant and a nuclear plant both have turbine, generator and condenser. Which single part of the thermal plant does the reactor replace, and what is the energy difference in one line?

13. 5 kg of U-235 undergoes fission (8 × 10¹³ J per kg). Find the total energy, the equivalent kWh, and the coal (30 MJ/kg) in tonnes.

14. True or false with a one-line reason: (a) fission splits a heavy nucleus, (b) pulling out the control rods slows the chain, (c) fusion plants supply the grid today, (d) biogas leaves useful manure, (e) geothermal energy comes from the Sun, (f) 1 kg of U-235 releases about a million times more energy than 1 kg of coal.

### Answers

1. **Given:** 2 m³ per day, 20 MJ per m³. (a) E = 2 × 20 = **40 MJ per din**. (b) 40 × 30 = **1200 MJ**. (c) LPG = 1200 ÷ 50 = **24 kg**. (d) Cake = 1200 ÷ 7 = **171.4 kg**.
   Jaanch: 24 × 50 = 1200 ✅; 171.4 × 7 = 1200 ✅.

2. E = 150 × 7 = **1050 MJ**. Biogas = 1050 ÷ 20 = **52.5 m³**. Wood = 1050 ÷ 17 = **61.8 kg**. Plant 30 din mein 2 × 30 = 60 m³ deta hai, 60 > 52.5, to **haan, kaafi** (7.5 m³ bachti).
   Jaanch: 52.5 × 20 = 1050 ✅; 61.8 × 17 = 1050 ✅.

3. Efficiency = 30 ÷ 200 = 0.15 = **15 percent**. E = 30 MW × 24 h = 720 MWh = **720,000 kWh**. Coal: 720,000 × 0.34 = 244,800 kg = **244.8 tonne**; 720,000 × 0.6 = 432,000 kg = **432 tonne**.
   Jaanch: 200 × 0.15 = 30 MW ✅; 244.8 ÷ 432 = 0.34 ÷ 0.6 = 0.567 ✅.

4. E = mc² = 2 × 10⁻³ × 9 × 10¹⁶ = **1.8 × 10¹⁴ J**. kWh = 1.8 × 10¹⁴ ÷ 3.6 × 10⁶ = **5 × 10⁷ kWh**. Coal = 1.8 × 10¹⁴ ÷ 3 × 10⁷ = 6 × 10⁶ kg = **6000 tonne**.
   Jaanch: 5 × 10⁷ × 3.6 × 10⁶ = 1.8 × 10¹⁴ ✅. Unit kg × (m/s)² = J ✅.

5. Input = 1000 ÷ 0.33 = 3030.3 MW. E per day = 3.0303 × 10⁹ × 86,400 = 2.618 × 10¹⁴ J. Uranium per day = 2.618 × 10¹⁴ ÷ 8 × 10¹³ = **3.27 kg**. Per year 3.27 × 365 = **lagbhag 1195 kg (1.2 tonne)**. Coal per day = 2.618 × 10¹⁴ ÷ 3 × 10⁷ = 8.73 × 10⁶ kg = **lagbhag 8730 tonne**.
   Jaanch: 3.27 × 8 × 10¹³ = 2.62 × 10¹⁴ ✅.

6. 800 MW = 8 × 10⁸ J/s. E₁ = 200 × 1.6 × 10⁻¹³ = 3.2 × 10⁻¹¹ J. Fissions per second = 8 × 10⁸ ÷ 3.2 × 10⁻¹¹ = **2.5 × 10¹⁹**.
   Jaanch: 2.5 × 10¹⁹ × 3.2 × 10⁻¹¹ = 8 × 10⁸ ✅.

7. Pidhi n mein 3ⁿ fission: 1, 3, 9, 27, 81, 243, 729, 2187, **6561** (8th generation par). Control rods ke saath ausat 1, to har pidhi mein **1** hi rehta hai.
   Jaanch: 3⁸ = 6561 ✅; 1 × 1 × ... = 1 ✅.

8. (a) **Control rods** (cadmium ya boron). (b) **Moderator** (paani ya graphite). (c) **Coolant**. Rods poori utar jaayein to zyaadatar neutron sokh liye jaate hain, chain ruk jaati hai: reactor band hota hai. Fuel mein thodi der garmi phir bhi aati rehti hai, isliye cooling chalegi.
   Jaanch: teeno kaam alag hain ✅.

9. Biogas = 360 ÷ 20 = **18 m³**; CNG = 360 ÷ 50 = **7.2 kg**. Dono mein mukhya gas methane hai; farak biogas mein CO₂ ke hisse ka hai (isliye garmi kam) aur **source ka**: biogas taaze gobar se (renewable), natural gas aur CNG fossil se.
   Jaanch: 18 × 20 = 360 ✅; 7.2 × 50 = 360 ✅. (Dono ko kg ya m³ mein ek doosre se nahi badla.)

10. Reactor fuel: 0.04 × 500 = **20 kg**; bomb material: 0.90 × 500 = **450 kg**. Reactor fuel mein U-235 bahut kam hai aur control rods chain ko 1 par rakhti hain, to bomb jaisa dhamaka nahi ban sakta. Khatre alag hain: cooling fail hona, accident aur radioactive waste.
    Jaanch: 450 ÷ 20 = 22.5 guna ✅.

11. Geothermal garmi zameen ke andar ke garam chattanon se aati hai (Earth ke bante samay ki garmi aur chattanon ke andar ka radioactive tatva), Suraj se nahi. Paani wapas isliye bheja jaata hai ki paani na khatam ho aur andar dabav bana rahe. Desh: Iceland, New Zealand (ya USA). Bharat: Puga valley (Ladakh) aur Manikaran (Himachal).
    Jaanch: do desh aur do jagah likhe ✅.

12. Reactor **boiler** ki jagah leta hai. Thermal plant mein coal ki rasayanik energy se garmi, nuclear plant mein uranium ke nucleus ki fission se garmi; baaki turbine, generator aur condenser wahi.
    Jaanch: chain mein baaki hissa wahi ✅.

13. E = 5 × 8 × 10¹³ = **4 × 10¹⁴ J**. kWh = 4 × 10¹⁴ ÷ 3.6 × 10⁶ = **1.11 × 10⁸ kWh**. Coal = 4 × 10¹⁴ ÷ 3 × 10⁷ = 1.333 × 10⁷ kg = **lagbhag 13,333 tonne**.
    Jaanch: 1.333 × 10⁷ × 3 × 10⁷ = 4 × 10¹⁴ ✅.

14. (a) **True** ✅. (b) **False** ❌: rods nikalne se reaction badhti hai; andar daalne se ghatti hai. (c) **False** ❌: fusion reactor abhi ban raha hai. (d) **True** ✅: slurry khaad. (e) **False** ❌: zameen ki garmi. (f) **True** ✅: 8 × 10¹³ ÷ 3 × 10⁷ = 2.67 × 10⁶, yaani lagbhag 2.7 million guna.
