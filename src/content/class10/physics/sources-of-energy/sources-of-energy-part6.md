Avantika ne pocket se phone nikala. Uski battery 3.8 V aur 4.0 Ah ki thi. Ab dekhte hain wo energy kahan se aayi.

### Example 1. Ek charge ke peeche kitna coal?

Phone ki battery 3.8 V, 4.0 Ah hai. Charger aur taar milkar 70 percent energy battery tak pahunchate hain (sawaal mein maana hua). Bijli coal plant se aati hai: 35 percent efficiency, 30,000 kJ/kg. Ek poore charge ke liye (a) deewar se kitni energy, (b) coal kitna, (c) CO₂ kitni (coal: lagbhag 0.9 kg per kWh)?

**Given:** V = 3.8 V, 4.0 Ah, charger-taar efficiency 0.70; ek kWh ke peeche coal 0.343 kg (Topic 2); CO₂ 0.9 kg per kWh
**Find:** deewar ki energy, coal, CO₂
**Formula:** battery ki energy = V × Ah (Wh); deewar ki energy = battery ÷ 0.70; coal = deewar ki kWh × 0.343
**Substitution:** battery = 3.8 × 4.0 = 15.2 Wh; deewar = 15.2 ÷ 0.70 = 21.7 Wh = 0.0217 kWh; coal = 0.0217 × 0.343 = 0.00745 kg; CO₂ = 0.0217 × 0.9 = 0.0195 kg
**Answer:** Lagbhag 21.7 Wh, coal lagbhag 7.4 g (asli Indian coal 0.6 kg per kWh ke hisaab se lagbhag 13 g), CO₂ lagbhag 19.5 g.
**Jaanch:** 7.4 g × 30 MJ/kg × 0.35 = 0.0074 × 30 × 0.35 = 0.0777 MJ = 21.6 Wh ✅. Battery 15.2 Wh se deewar ki energy zyaada ✅ (efficiency 100 percent se kam).

### Example 2. Hospital ka backup: generator ya solar plus battery

Ek hospital ke zaroori wardon ko bijli jaane par 14 kW chahiye, 6 ghante tak. (a) Diesel generator (28 percent efficiency, sawaal mein diya, 45 MJ/kg): diesel kitna? (b) Solar plus battery: itni energy ek din mein dobara bharne ke liye panel kitni kW ka aur kitne m² ka (5 peak sun hours, 1 kW/m², 18 percent, nuksaan ignore)?

**Given:** P = 14 kW, t = 6 h, generator 0.28, diesel 45 MJ/kg
**Find:** diesel, panel ki kW aur area
**Formula:** E = P × t; diesel = E ÷ (0.28 × 45); panel = E ÷ 5 h; area = panel kW ÷ (1 × 0.18)
**Substitution:** E = 14 × 6 = 84 kWh = 84 × 3.6 = 302.4 MJ; diesel = 302.4 ÷ (0.28 × 45) = 302.4 ÷ 12.6 = 24 kg. Panel = 84 ÷ 5 = 16.8 kW; area = 16.8 ÷ 0.18 = 93.3 m²
**Answer:** Generator ko 24 kg diesel; solar ke liye lagbhag 16.8 kW ke panel, lagbhag 93.3 m².
**Jaanch:** 24 × 45 × 0.28 = 302.4 MJ = 84 kWh ✅. 16.8 kW × 5 h = 84 kWh ✅.
**Faisla:** generator turant chalta hai, jagah kam, shuruaat sasta, par eendhan, shor aur dhuan. Solar-battery chupchaap, par badi jagah aur battery ki keemat. Isliye aksar dono: kuch hissa solar-battery se, aakhri bharosa generator.

### Example 3. 60 gharon ka gaon, grid se door

Gaon ke har ghar ko roz lagbhag 1.6 kWh chahiye. Nadi ki dhaar ek jagah 15 m ooncha giri hai. (a) Gaon ki roz ki energy aur average power? (b) Solar ka area? (c) Diesel generator (28 percent, 45 MJ/kg) se roz kitna diesel? (d) Chhote hydro (efficiency 80 percent) ke liye kitna flow?

**Given:** 60 ghar, 1.6 kWh per din; h = 15 m; solar 5 peak sun hours, 18 percent, 1 kW/m²; generator 0.28; g = 9.8 m/s²
**Find:** E, P, area, diesel, Q
**Formula:** E = 60 × 1.6; P = E ÷ 24 h; area = E ÷ (5 × 0.18); diesel = E ÷ (0.28 × 45 ÷ 3.6); Q = P ÷ (0.80 × 1000 × g × h)
**Substitution:** E = 96 kWh; P = 96 ÷ 24 = 4 kW. Area = 96 ÷ 0.9 = 106.7 m². Diesel = 96 × 3.6 ÷ 12.6 = 27.4 kg. Q = 4000 ÷ (0.80 × 1000 × 9.8 × 15) = 4000 ÷ 117,600 = 0.034 m³/s
**Answer:** 96 kWh per din, 4 kW average. Solar lagbhag 107 m². Diesel lagbhag 27.4 kg roz (lagbhag 10 tonne saal mein: 27.4 × 365 = 10,011 kg). Hydro ke liye sirf lagbhag 34 litre per second kaafi.
**Jaanch:** 4 kW × 24 h = 96 kWh ✅. Hydro: 0.034 × 1000 × 9.8 × 15 × 0.80 = 3998 W ≈ 4 kW ✅. Head bada ho to bahut kam paani se kaam chalta hai (Topic 3 ka P ∝ Q × h).
**Faisla:** agar 15 m ki dhaar saal bhar rahe to chhota hydro sabse achha (steady, saaf). Warna solar plus battery, aur diesel sirf aakhri backup.

## Part D: Climate ka hisaab

Class 9 ke greenhouse effect se hum jaante hain ki CO₂ garmi rokti hai. Ab ek kWh bijli ke peeche CO₂ (lagbhag; plant ke chalte waqt):

```
  Coal              :##################   0.9
  Gas (natural gas) :########   0.4
  Hydro             :   lagbhag 0
  Wind              :   lagbhag 0
  Solar             :   lagbhag 0
  Nuclear           :   lagbhag 0
```

> **Figure 6.4** – Bijli ki CO₂ per kWh (plant ke chalte waqt, lagbhag). Har # = 0.05 kg. Coal 18 # (0.9 kg), gas 8 # (0.4 kg). Hydro, wind, solar aur nuclear lagbhag zero: unke banane, khodne aur banane ke kharch ki CO₂ alag aati hai (ye chalte waqt ka hisaab hai).

Coal ka 0.9 kg dobara jaanchein: Topic 2 mein 1 kWh ke peeche 0.343 kg coal. Maan lo coal 75 percent carbon hai (Topic 2 ki maani hui value); 12 g C se 44 g CO₂. To 0.343 × 0.75 × 44 ÷ 12 = lagbhag 0.94 kg, yaani lagbhag 0.9 kg ✅. Gas ka 0.4: Topic 2 ka 0.055 kg CO₂ per MJ × 3.6 MJ ÷ 0.5 (gas plant lagbhag 50 percent efficient) = lagbhag 0.4 kg ✅. Asli Indian coal (0.6 kg per kWh) mein CO₂ aur zyaada ho sakti hai: yahan hum 0.9 hi lenge.

### Example 4. Ek state ka energy mix

Ek state ki bijli ek saal mein 2.5 × 10¹⁰ kWh hai (25,000 million). Mix: coal 60 percent, gas 5, hydro 15, wind 8, solar 7, nuclear 5. (a) Average CO₂ per kWh aur saal ki kul CO₂? (b) Agar coal ka 20 percent point solar le le (coal 40, solar 27), to kitni kami?

**Given:** total 2.5 × 10¹⁰ kWh; CO₂: coal 0.9, gas 0.4, baaki 0 kg per kWh
**Find:** average, total, kami
**Formula:** average = Σ (share × CO₂ per kWh); total = average × kWh
**Substitution:** (a) average = 0.60 × 0.9 + 0.05 × 0.4 = 0.54 + 0.02 = 0.56 kg/kWh; total = 0.56 × 2.5 × 10¹⁰ = 1.4 × 10¹⁰ kg = 1.4 crore tonne. (b) naya average = 0.40 × 0.9 + 0.02 = 0.38 kg/kWh; total = 9.5 × 10⁹ kg; kami = (0.56 − 0.38) ÷ 0.56 = 0.32
**Answer:** 0.56 kg/kWh aur lagbhag 1.4 crore tonne; badlaav ke baad 0.38 kg/kWh, yaani lagbhag 32 percent kam (45 lakh tonne bachi).
**Jaanch:** percent: 60 + 5 + 15 + 8 + 7 + 5 = 100 ✅; naya: 40 + 5 + 15 + 8 + 27 + 5 = 100 ✅. 20 percent point coal hataya, par CO₂ 32 percent ghati, kyunki coal hi akela bada hissedaar tha ✅.

## Part E: Ghar aur school mein energy ki bachat

Ab laal batti ka raaz. Plug mein laga band TV ya computer **standby load** leta hai. Avantika ki team ne dekha ki lab mein 5 aise saaman har ek lagbhag 4 W (maana hua) 20 ghante roz chalu rehte hain.

### Example 5. LED ya purana bulb: ek saal ka hisaab

Ek 60 W bulb ko 9 W LED se badla. Dono roz 5 ghante, saal ke 365 din chalte hain. (a) Dono ki saal bhar ki energy? (b) Bachat kWh aur ₹ (₹6 per unit maana hua)? (c) Bachat ke peeche coal (0.343 kg per kWh) aur CO₂ (0.9 kg per kWh)? (d) Agar LED ₹90 (maana hua) ka ho, kitne din mein paise wapas?

**Given:** 60 W, 9 W, 5 h, 365 din, ₹6 per unit
**Find:** E, bachat, coal, CO₂, payback
**Formula:** E = P × t; bachat = purani − nayi
**Substitution:** (a) bulb = 0.060 × 5 × 365 = 109.5 kWh; LED = 0.009 × 5 × 365 = 16.4 kWh (16.425). (b) bachat = 109.5 − 16.425 = 93.075 kWh; ₹ = 93.075 × 6 = ₹558. (c) coal = 93.075 × 0.343 = 31.9 kg; CO₂ = 93.075 × 0.9 = 83.8 kg. (d) roz ki bachat = 558.45 ÷ 365 = ₹1.53; payback = 90 ÷ 1.53 = 58.8 din
**Answer:** 109.5 kWh aur 16.4 kWh; bachat lagbhag 93.1 kWh (lagbhag ₹558); coal lagbhag 31.9 kg (asli Indian coal par lagbhag 55.8 kg), CO₂ lagbhag 83.8 kg; paise lagbhag 59 din mein wapas.
**Jaanch:** 9 ÷ 60 = 0.15, 109.5 × 0.15 = 16.4 ✅. Bachat ka ratio 1 − 0.15 = 0.85 aur 109.5 × 0.85 = 93.1 ✅.

### Example 6. Standby: chhoti batti, bada bill

Lab ke 5 saaman, har ek 4 W, roz 20 ghante standby par. Saal ki energy, ₹ aur CO₂?

**Given:** 5 × 4 W, 20 h per din, 365 din, ₹6 per unit, 0.9 kg CO₂ per kWh
**Find:** E, ₹, CO₂
**Formula:** E = P × t
**Substitution:** P = 5 × 4 = 20 W = 0.020 kW; roz = 0.020 × 20 = 0.4 kWh; saal = 0.4 × 365 = 146 kWh; ₹ = 146 × 6 = ₹876; CO₂ = 146 × 0.9 = 131.4 kg
**Answer:** 146 kWh, lagbhag ₹876 aur lagbhag 131 kg CO₂ saal mein: bina kuch kiye.
**Jaanch:** 146 kWh = 146 × 3.6 = 525.6 MJ; 20 W × (20 × 365 × 3600 s) = 20 × 26,280,000 = 525.6 MJ ✅.

Baaki upaay, jinhe Avantika ne list mein likha:
- **Insulation aur parde:** kamre ko garmi ya sardi se bachane se AC aur heater kam chalte hain, yaani kam bijli.
- **Public transport:** Topic 1 aur 2 ke numbers se: CNG bus 5 km per kg, 50 MJ/kg, yaani 10 MJ per km; 40 sawaari ho to 10 ÷ 40 = 0.25 MJ per sawaari-km. Car 15 km per litre, 32 MJ per litre, yaani 32 ÷ 15 = 2.13 MJ per km; 2 sawaari ho to lagbhag 1.07 MJ per sawaari-km, bus se lagbhag 4.3 guna.
- **Star-rated saaman, solar water heater, LED:** wahi kaam, kam energy.

## Part F: Mixed hisaab: coal ya uranium?

### Example 7. Ek 540 MW ka unit: roz kitna fuel?

540 MW ka ek nuclear unit (efficiency 30 percent, sawaal mein diya) aur 540 MW ka coal unit (35 percent, 30 MJ/kg) ek din poori power par chalte hain. (a) Roz ki bijli kWh mein? (b) Uranium-235 kitna fission hota hai (1 kg ≈ 8 × 10¹³ J)? (c) Coal kitna? (d) Fission mein kitna mass energy ban jaata hai (E = mc², c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s)?

**Given:** P = 540 MW, t = 24 h = 86,400 s; 8 × 10¹³ J per kg uranium-235; coal 3 × 10⁷ J/kg
**Find:** E, mass of uranium, mass of coal, mass converted
**Formula:** E = P × t; fuel energy = E ÷ efficiency; mass = fuel energy ÷ energy per kg; m = E ÷ c²
**Substitution:** (a) E = 5.4 × 10⁸ × 86,400 = 4.6656 × 10¹³ J; kWh = 4.6656 × 10¹³ ÷ 3.6 × 10⁶ = 1.296 × 10⁷ kWh. (b) fuel energy = 4.6656 × 10¹³ ÷ 0.30 = 1.5552 × 10¹⁴ J; uranium = 1.5552 × 10¹⁴ ÷ 8 × 10¹³ = 1.944 kg. (c) coal = 4.6656 × 10¹³ ÷ (0.35 × 3 × 10⁷) = 4.443 × 10⁶ kg. (d) m = 1.5552 × 10¹⁴ ÷ (9 × 10¹⁶) = 1.728 × 10⁻³ kg
**Answer:** lagbhag 1.3 crore kWh roz; lagbhag 1.94 kg uranium-235, ya lagbhag 4443 tonne coal (lagbhag 2300 guna zyaada); fission mein sirf lagbhag 1.7 g mass energy ban gaya.
**Jaanch:** 1.944 × 8 × 10¹³ × 0.30 = 4.67 × 10¹³ J ✅. Coal ÷ uranium = 4.443 × 10⁶ ÷ 1.944 = 2285 ✅. m c² = 1.728 × 10⁻³ × 9 × 10¹⁶ = 1.5552 × 10¹⁴ J ✅.

Ye hisaab bataata hai ki nuclear fuel ka mass kitna kam hai, par ye bhi: kam fuel ka matlab kam khatra nahi. Waste aur surakshit chalana (Chernobyl 1986, Fukushima 2011, Topic 5) alag, bade sawal hain.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "kWh power ki unit hai"**

Demonstration: ek 1.5 kW ka geyser 40 minute chalta hai. Uski **power** 1.5 kW hai (kitni tezi se energy leta hai). Usne **energy** li = 1.5 × (40 ÷ 60) = **1.0 kWh** = 3.6 MJ. Check: power = energy ÷ time = 3.6 × 10⁶ ÷ 2400 s = 1500 W ✅. Bill mein "units" yaani kWh energy ke hote hain; plant ya turbine par likhe MW power ke. **Sahi:** power = kW, energy = kWh = kW × h.

**Galti 2: "Jo machine achhi ho, uski efficiency 100 percent se zyaada ho sakti hai"**

Demonstration: ek motor 800 J bijli leti hai aur 740 J kaam ki energy deti hai, baaki 60 J garmi aur awaaz. Efficiency = 740 ÷ 800 = 92.5 percent. Agar kisi ne 880 J ka dava kiya, to 80 J "kahin se" aaye, jo energy conservation todta hai. Chain mein bhi har kadi 1 se chhoti hai, to guna aur chhota: 0.88 × 0.41 × 0.97 = 0.35, jo har akeli kadi se kam hai. **Sahi:** efficiency hamesha 100 percent se kam.

**Galti 3: "Energy kharch hokar khatam ho jaati hai"**

Demonstration: 60 W ka bulb 5 ghante jalta hai: 0.060 × 5 = 0.3 kWh = 1.08 MJ. Wo energy gayab nahi hui: lagbhag poori garmi banke kamre mein phail gayi. Itni garmi 10 kg paani ko lagbhag 1.08 × 10⁶ ÷ (10 × 4200) = 25.7 °C garam kar sakti thi. Bill us energy ka hai jo humne **kaam ke roop mein** li; wo phailkar aisi garmi ban gayi jise hum dobara bijli nahi bana sakte. Topic 2 mein bhi 35 + 65 = 100 unit tha. **Sahi:** energy badalti aur phailti hai, khatam nahi hoti.

**Galti 4: "Nuclear energy renewable hai"**

Demonstration: Example 7 ka unit saal mein 1.944 × 365 = **709.6 kg** uranium-235 jalata hai; wo wapas nahi banta. Jab tak khadaan se ore nikalta hai, hum use kharch hi kar rahe hain: Topic 1 ki kasauti (kharch ki raftaar aur banne ki raftaar) par nuclear **non-renewable** hai. Wo "kam CO₂" hai, par kam CO₂ aur renewable alag sawal hain.

| Strot | Renewable? | Chalte waqt CO₂ |
|---|---|---|
| Coal | Nahi | Zyaada |
| Solar | Haan | Lagbhag zero |
| Nuclear | Nahi | Lagbhag zero |
| Lakdi | Haan | Zyaada |

**Sahi:** nuclear non-renewable aur kam-carbon hai; lakdi renewable aur zyaada-CO₂ hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Master table:** koi strot sab kasautiyon par jeet nahi paata; steady aur saaf aksar saath nahi milte; mix chahiye.
- **Energy chain:** gadget ki energy peeche jaakar Suraj (coal, hydro, wind, solar, biomass) ya parmanu/zameen ki garmi tak jaati hai; har kadi par nuksaan.
- **Choice:** zaroorat likho, saat kasautiyan lagao, shortlist, numbers, backup. Gaon, shahar, satellite aur hospital ke alag jawab.
- **CO₂ per kWh:** coal lagbhag 0.9 kg, gas lagbhag 0.4 kg, hydro, wind, solar aur nuclear chalte waqt lagbhag zero. Mix ka average = share × CO₂ ka jod.
- **Bachat:** LED, standby band, insulation, public transport. LED ka payback lagbhag 59 din tha (Example 5).
- **Galtiyan:** kWh energy hai, power nahi; efficiency 100 percent se kam; energy badalti hai, khatam nahi hoti; nuclear non-renewable hai.

## Practice Questions

1. A hospital ward needs 3.5 kW for 8 hours during a power cut. (a) A diesel generator of 25 percent efficiency (45 MJ/kg) supplies it: find the diesel mass. (b) Find the panel power and area (18 percent, 1 kW/m², 5 peak sun hours, no losses) to refill that energy in one day.

2. A hamlet of 45 homes needs 1.2 kWh per home per day. A stream falls through a head of 9 m with a flow of 0.05 m³/s, and the turbine-generator efficiency is 80 percent. Find the electrical power, the energy per day, and whether it meets the need.

3. A school replaces 150 bulbs of 75 W with LEDs of 12 W. They burn 6 hours a day for 220 school days. Find the energy saved in a year, the CO₂ avoided (0.9 kg per kWh) and the money saved at ₹6 per unit.

4. Six devices each draw 3 W on standby for 18 hours a day. Find the energy wasted in a year, its cost at ₹6 per unit and the CO₂ at 0.9 kg per kWh.

5. A state makes 8 × 10⁹ kWh a year: coal 55 percent, gas 10, hydro 20, wind 9, solar 4, nuclear 2. Find the average CO₂ per kWh (coal 0.9, gas 0.4, others about zero) and the total CO₂. What happens to the total if the gas share is taken over by solar?

(b) A hilltop mobile tower that needs power day and night, next to a fast hill stream that never dries, with no fuel supply. (c) A street light on an open road with no river and no fuel supply, needed only at night, with a battery. (d) A weather instrument on a sunny desert plateau with no fuel supply, used only in daytime.

7. A laptop battery is 11.4 V and 5.0 Ah. Charger and wires pass 75 percent of the energy. The power plant is 35 percent efficient with 30,000 kJ/kg coal. Find the energy from the wall in Wh, the coal needed per full charge in grams (using 0.343 kg per kWh), and the CO₂ at 0.9 kg per kWh.

8. A hydro chain has turbine 90 percent, generator 94 percent and transmission 96 percent efficient. Find the overall efficiency. If the falling water gives 1200 kW, what power reaches the town?

9. A panel of 4.5 m² (18 percent, 1 kW/m², 5 peak sun hours) feeds a house through an inverter of 95 percent efficiency. Find the daily energy used directly, and the energy available at night if it first goes through a battery of 85 percent efficiency.

10. A 250 MW nuclear unit with efficiency 32 percent runs for a day (1 kg of uranium-235 gives 8 × 10¹³ J). Find the uranium used, the mass that is converted to energy, and the coal (30 MJ/kg) a 35 percent coal plant would need for the same electricity.

11. State True or False with a reason: (a) A 3 kW heater uses 3 kWh of energy every hour. (b) A machine takes 500 J and gives 520 J of useful work. (c) The energy of a bulb is used up once it is switched off. (d) Nuclear energy is renewable because it keeps producing power. (e) Solar panels release no CO₂ while working. (f) A 40 W lamp running 25 hours uses 1 kWh.

12. An iron of 1.8 kW is used for 20 minutes. Find its power, the energy in kWh and in MJ, and the cost at ₹6 per unit.

13. A family uses 180 units a month. Find the yearly energy, the CO₂ if all of it came from coal (0.9 kg per kWh), and the coal mass at 0.343 kg per kWh and at 0.6 kg per kWh.

14. A bus uses 22 kg of CNG (50 MJ/kg) for 100 km with 55 passengers. A car with 3 persons uses 2.5 MJ per km. Compare the energy per passenger-km.
