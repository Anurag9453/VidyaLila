# Topic 1: Accha Fuel Kaun Sa? Energy Ke Strot Ki Kasauti

Chapter 2 mein humne cheezon ko tikte, girte aur ghoomte dekha. Har baar ek sawaal chhupa tha: ye sab **chalta kisse hai**? Crane ko, bus ko, satellite ko, aur tumhe bhi, **energy** chahiye. Class 9 mein hum jaan chuke hain ki energy ke kai roop hote hain, wo ek roop se doosre mein badalti hai, aur banti ya khatam nahi hoti. Is chapter mein hum us energy ke **strot (sources)** ki poori duniya dekhenge: koyla, petrol, hawa, paani, Suraj, uranium. Har strot ek **plant** ki tarah kaam karta hai, aur hum numbers se dekhenge ki kaun kitna deta hai, kitna bekaar jaata hai, aur kis ki kya keemat hai.

Pehle topic mein sabse badi kasauti: **ek achha strot (aur khaaskar ek achha fuel) kise kahte hain?** Iska jawab ek rasoi se shuru hota hai.

Usne socha ki lakdi gaon mein mufta mil jaati hai, LPG ka cylinder khareedna padta hai, aur induction ko to bijli chahiye, aur bijli ka bill alag. "Mama, har ek mein kuch achha hai, kuch bura. "Bilkul yahi ulajhan aaj suljhayenge," Mr. Kaikini bole. "Aur ek raaz batata hoon: ye sawaal sirf rasoi ka nahi. Bijli-ghar, bus, rocket aur tumhare phone tak ka sawaal hai: **kaun sa energy ka strot kab achha hai?** Chalo, ek kasauti banate hain, aur phir teeno chulhon ko numbers se tolte hain."

## Part A: Hamein "strot" kyun chahiye?

Class 9 ka sabse bada sach ye hai ki energy **banti nahi, khatam nahi hoti**, sirf roop badalti hai ([conservation of energy](../../../class9/physics/work-energy-and-power/work-energy-and-power-part6.md)). Phir ye kyun kehte hain ki "energy khatam ho rahi hai" ya "energy bachao"? Isliye ki jab hum chai ubaalte hain, rasoi ki chemical energy garmi ban kar **hawa mein phail jaati hai**. Wo hai to, par ab itni bikhri hui ki hum use dobara kaam mein nahi laa sakte. Hamein **kaam ki energy** chahiye, aur wo kisi **strot** se milti rehni chahiye. Strot wo bhandaar hai jahan se hum **kaam ki energy** nikal sakte hain.

Ek insaan ko khud rozana lagbhag **10 MJ** (lagbhag 2400 kcal) ki energy khane se chahiye ([energy ke roop, part 3](../../../class9/physics/work-energy-and-power/work-energy-and-power-part3.md): 1 kcal lagbhag 4.2 kJ). Ye bhi ek strot ki kahani hai: khana paudhon se, aur paudhe Suraj se.

Class 9 mein humne dekha tha ki zyaadatar strot ki jad **Suraj** hai. Ek jhalak ye rahi (poora chain, bijli tak, Topic 4 mein banayenge).

```
SURAJ  --> paudhe (photosynthesis)               --> lakdi, gobar, biogas
SURAJ  --> purane paudhe, janwar (karodon saal)  --> koyla, petrol, LPG
SURAJ  --> zameen-samundar ki asamaan garmi      --> hawa (wind)
SURAJ  --> samundar ka bhaap, phir baarish       --> behta paani (hydro)
SURAJ  --> seedhi dhoop                          --> solar cell, solar cooker
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Zyaadatar strot ki jad Suraj mein: paudhon se lakdi aur biogas, karodon saal purane paudhon aur janwaron se koyla, petrol aur LPG, Suraj ki asamaan garmi se hawa, aur samundar ke bhaap banne se baarish aur behta paani. Solar cell Suraj ki roshni se seedhe bijli banate hain.

### Example 1. Ek insaan ka khana aur bijli ka heater

Ek insaan ko ek din mein lagbhag 10 MJ ki energy chahiye. Ye energy kitne kWh hai? Aur itni energy ek 1 kW ke heater ko kitne ghante chalaa sakti hai?

**Given:** E = 10 MJ = 10 × 10⁶ J, 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J, P = 1 kW = 1000 W
**Find:** E kWh mein, aur time t
**Formula:** kWh = J ÷ (3.6 × 10⁶); t = E ÷ P
**Substitution:** E = (10 × 10⁶) ÷ (3.6 × 10⁶) = **2.78 kWh** lagbhag. t = 10 × 10⁶ J ÷ 1000 W = 10,000 s = 10,000 ÷ 3600 = **2.78 ghante** lagbhag
**Answer:** Ek insaan ka ek din ka khana lagbhag 2.78 kWh ke barabar hai: yaani 1 kW ka heater lagbhag 2.78 ghante.
**Jaanch:** 2.78 kWh ÷ 1 kW = 2.78 h ✅. Units: J ÷ W = s ✅.

## Part C: Calorific value: kitni garmi, kitne fuel se?

**Calorific value** wo energy hai jo **1 kg fuel ke poore jalne** par garmi ke roop mein milti hai. Ise **kJ/kg** ya **MJ/kg** mein likhte hain (1 MJ = 1000 kJ). Agar fuel gas hai to kabhi kabhi **MJ per m³** bhi likhte hain, par hum aksar **per kg** par rehenge.

**E = m × calorific value**

Isse teen kaam nikalte hain: (1) fuel ki di hui mass se energy, (2) chahiye hui energy se mass, (3) do fuels ki tulna. Is chapter mein hum sab jagah ye **ek hi table** lenge (sab lagbhag):

| Fuel | Calorific value (kJ/kg) |
|---|---|
| Hydrogen | 150,000 |
| LPG | 50,000 |
| CNG / methane | 50,000 |
| Petrol, diesel, kerosene | 45,000 |
| Biogas | gas hai, isliye sirf lagbhag 20 MJ per m³ (kg mein nahi badalte) |
| Coal (achha) | 30,000 |
| Lakdi (sookhi) | 17,000 |
| Gobar ka upla | 7,000 |

```
Hydrogen                 ############################################################ 150 MJ/kg
LPG, CNG                 #################### 50 MJ/kg
Petrol, diesel, kerosene ################## 45 MJ/kg
Coal (achha)             ############ 30 MJ/kg
Lakdi (sookhi)           ####### 17 MJ/kg
Gobar ka upla            ### 7 MJ/kg
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Fuels ki calorific value MJ/kg mein: har # lagbhag 2.5 MJ/kg dikhata hai (chhote bars ko nazdeeki gol kiya gaya hai). Hydrogen sabse oopar, gobar ka upla sabse neeche. (Biogas gas hai, uski energy m³ mein hai, isliye is chart mein nahi.) LPG aur CNG petrol ke thode oopar, aur lakdi coal se aadhe se thodi zyaada.

### Example 2. Coal se kitni garmi?

2.5 kg achhe coal ke poore jalne par kitni energy milti hai? Isse bijli ke bill ke "unit" mein kitni energy hai?

**Given:** m = 2.5 kg, calorific value = 30,000 kJ/kg
**Find:** E (kJ, MJ aur kWh)
**Formula:** E = m × calorific value; 1 kWh = 3.6 MJ
**Substitution:** E = 2.5 × 30,000 = 75,000 kJ = **75 MJ**. kWh mein: 75 ÷ 3.6 = **20.8 kWh** lagbhag
**Answer:** 2.5 kg coal se 75 MJ, yaani bill ke lagbhag 20.8 unit ki garmi (sirf garmi; bijli nahi).
**Jaanch:** 75,000 kJ ÷ 2.5 kg = 30,000 kJ/kg ✅. Units: kg × kJ/kg = kJ ✅.

### Example 3. Insaan ke din bhar ke khane jitni energy kitne fuel mein?

Ek insaan ko ek din mein lagbhag 10 MJ chahiye. Itni energy (a) coal ke kitne kg mein, (b) petrol ke kitne kg mein hoti hai?

**Given:** E = 10 MJ = 10,000 kJ; coal 30,000 kJ/kg; petrol 45,000 kJ/kg
**Find:** m_coal aur m_petrol
**Formula:** m = E ÷ calorific value
**Substitution:** m_coal = 10,000 ÷ 30,000 = **0.333 kg** (333 g) lagbhag; m_petrol = 10,000 ÷ 45,000 = **0.222 kg** (222 g) lagbhag
**Answer:** Ek din ke khane ki energy lagbhag 333 g coal ya 222 g petrol ki garmi ke barabar hai.
**Jaanch:** 0.333 × 30,000 = 9,999 ≈ 10,000 ✅; 0.222 × 45,000 = 9,990 ≈ 10,000 ✅. (Dhyan do: hamara sharir fuel ki tarah jalta nahi, ye sirf energy ki **matra** ki tulna hai.)

### Example 4. Ek hi energy, teen fuels: kaun kitna?

Ek chhoti factory ko ek din mein 900 MJ ki garmi chahiye. Hydrogen, diesel aur coal mein se har ek ki kitni mass lagegi?

**Given:** E = 900 MJ = 900,000 kJ; hydrogen 150,000, diesel 45,000, coal 30,000 kJ/kg
**Find:** m for each
**Formula:** m = E ÷ calorific value
**Substitution:** hydrogen: 900,000 ÷ 150,000 = **6 kg**; diesel: 900,000 ÷ 45,000 = **20 kg**; coal: 900,000 ÷ 30,000 = **30 kg**
**Answer:** 6 kg hydrogen, 20 kg diesel ya 30 kg coal.
**Jaanch:** 6 × 150,000 = 900,000 ✅; 20 × 45,000 = 900,000 ✅; 30 × 30,000 = 900,000 ✅. Ratio: hydrogen ki calorific value diesel se 150,000 ÷ 45,000 = 3.33 guna, isliye mass 20 ÷ 6 = 3.33 guni kam ✅.

Isse ek sawaal paida hota hai: **agar hydrogen 6 kg mein kaam kar deta hai, to sab log hydrogen kyun nahi jalate?** Is ka jawab "Aam galtiyan" mein hai.

## Part D: Strot ke do tarah ke baante

Class 9 mein hamne strot ko do hisson mein baanta tha: renewable aur non-renewable ([energy ke roop, part 3](../../../class9/physics/work-energy-and-power/work-energy-and-power-part3.md)). Ab hum ek **doosra** baanta bhi jodte hain: **conventional** (purane, bade pamaane par wale) aur **non-conventional** (naye). Ye do alag sawal hain, isliye ek **do-tarfa table** banta hai:

| | Conventional (purane, bade pamaane par) | Non-conventional (naye) |
|---|---|---|
| **Non-renewable** | Coal, petroleum, natural gas | Nuclear (uranium): lists ise alag-alag tarah se rakhti hain, Topic 5 mein dekhenge |
| **Renewable** | Lakdi (jab naye ped lagte rahein), bade dam ka paani | Solar, wind, tidal, geothermal, biogas |

Ek strot **renewable** hai ya nahi, ye is par depend karta hai ki hum use **kitni raftaar se** kharch karte hain aur wo kitni raftaar se wapas banta hai. Ek jungle jo saal mein jitna ugta hai, hum usse zyaada kaatenge to lakdi "renewable" hone par bhi khatam ho jaayegi.

## Part E: Teeno chulhon ka asli muqabla

Wapas Mr. Kaikini ki rasoi. Mrudula ke saamne ek **numerical sawaal** rakha gaya: 2 kg paani ko 25 °C se 100 °C tak garam karna hai. Teeno chulhe wahi kaam karenge, par har ek **apni efficiency** se. Class 9 ke hisaab se paani ki specific heat **4200 J/kg °C** hai ([heat-and-temperature-part3.md](../../../class9/physics/heat-and-temperature/heat-and-temperature-part3.md)). Aur teeno ki efficiency (lagbhag): **lakdi ka chulha 10 percent, LPG stove 60 percent, induction plate 85 percent.** Yaani chulhe mein 100 units lakdi ki garmi mein se sirf 10 units paani tak pahunchte hain, baaki 90 units aas-paas ki hawa mein.

```
Lakdi ka chulha  [UU..................]  10 percent
LPG stove        [UUUUUUUUUUUU........]  60 percent
Induction plate  [UUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUU...]  85 percent
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Teeno chulhon ki efficiency: har U (useful) lagbhag 5 percent hai, aur har . (lost) bhi 5 percent. Chulhe mein 20 mein se sirf 2 hisse paani ko, induction mein 17 hisse.

### Example 5. Paani ko ubalne tak: teeno chulhon ka fuel

2 kg paani ko 25 °C se 100 °C tak garam karna hai. Wood chulha, LPG stove aur induction plate mein se har ek ke liye fuel ya bijli ki matra nikalo.

**Given:** m = 2 kg, c = 4200 J/kg °C, temperature badlaav = 100 − 25 = 75 °C. Efficiency: wood chulha 10 percent, LPG stove 60 percent, induction 85 percent. Lakdi 17,000 kJ/kg, LPG 50,000 kJ/kg.
**Find:** useful heat Q, phir har chulhe ka input
**Formula:** Q = m c ΔT; input = Q ÷ efficiency
**Substitution:**
- Useful heat Q = 2 × 4200 × 75 = 630,000 J = **630 kJ**
- Wood chulha: input = 630 ÷ 0.10 = 6,300 kJ; lakdi = 6,300 ÷ 17,000 = **0.371 kg** (371 g) lagbhag
- LPG stove: input = 630 ÷ 0.60 = 1,050 kJ; LPG = 1,050 ÷ 50,000 = **0.021 kg** (21 g)
- Induction plate: input = 630 ÷ 0.85 = 741 kJ lagbhag = 741 ÷ 3600 = **0.206 kWh** lagbhag (yaani lagbhag 0.2 unit)
**Answer:** Wahi 630 kJ paani tak pahunchane ke liye 371 g lakdi, 21 g LPG ya lagbhag 0.2 unit bijli.
**Jaanch:** Back-substitution: 371 g × 17,000 kJ/kg × 0.10 = 0.371 × 17,000 × 0.10 = 630.7 ≈ 630 kJ ✅. 0.021 × 50,000 × 0.60 = 630 kJ ✅. 741 × 0.85 = 630 kJ ✅. Efficiency 100 percent se kam, isliye input har baar Q se zyaada ✅.

**Dhyan do:** induction plate tak bijli ko aane mein bhi energy jaati hai: agar wo bijli coal ke power plant se aati hai jiski efficiency lagbhag 35 percent hai, to koyle ki energy se lekar paani tak lagbhag 0.85 × 0.35 = 0.30, yaani **lagbhag 30 percent** hi pahunchti hai. Bijli kaise banti hai aur kitna jaata hai, ye hum Topic 2 mein dekhenge. Isliye "induction sabse achha" kehna bhi poora sach nahi: kaun sa chulha achha hai, ye **bijli ke strot** par bhi tikta hai.

Mrudula ne jaldi se jod liya: "Mama, to lakdi ka chulha sabse **bekaar** hai? 90 percent garmi bekaar!" Mr. Kaikini bole: "Efficiency ek kasauti hai, aur dhuan doosri. Par gaon mein lakdi mufta hai, aur LPG ke liye door shahar jaana padta hai. Isliye 'achha' ek **tarazu** hai, ek akela number nahi."

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Jis fuel ki calorific value sabse zyaada hai, wahi sabse achha hai"**

Demonstration: Hydrogen ki calorific value lagbhag 150,000 kJ/kg hai, bus ke diesel (45,000) se 3.33 guna. Phir bhi aaj ki zyaadatar bus diesel ya CNG par kyun chalti hain? Teen wajah:
1. **Store karna mushkil.** Hydrogen bahut halki gas hai: ek kilo hydrogen bharne ke liye bahut bada tank ya bahut zyaada dabaav chahiye. Diesel ek aam tank mein bhar jaata hai.
2. **Banana mehnga.** Class 10 Chemistry mein dekha tha ki paani ke electrolysis se 1 kg hydrogen banane mein lagbhag **50 se 55 kWh** bijli lagti hai ([electrolysis-part5.md](../../chemistry/electrolysis/electrolysis-part5.md)). Par 1 kg hydrogen ki calorific energy 150 MJ ÷ 3.6 = **lagbhag 41.7 kWh** hi hai. Yaani jitni bijli lagi, usse **kam** energy wapas aayi. Isliye green hydrogen ko bijli **store** karne ka ek tareeka kaha jaata hai, jismein kuch energy hamesha kho jaati hai.
3. **Sasta aur surakshit.** Calorific value sirf pehli kasauti hai; baaki chhe kasautiyan (Part B) bhi utni hi zaroori hain.

**Sahi:** Sabse achha fuel wo hai jo saat kasautiyon ka **santulan** banaye, na ki sirf ek number mein sabse bada ho.

**Galti 2: "Renewable ka matlab hai pollution-free"**

Demonstration: ek gaon ko lagbhag 100 MJ ki **useful** garmi chahiye. Lakdi ka chulha (efficiency 10 percent, lakdi 17,000 kJ/kg) lagbhag 1000 MJ ki lakdi jalayega: 1000 ÷ 17 = **58.8 kg** lakdi. Gobar ka upla (7,000 kJ/kg) par: 1000 ÷ 7 = **142.9 kg**. Dono renewable hain, par itni lakdi ya upla jalane se bahut dhuan aur PM2.5 banta hai ([atmospheric-pollution-part5.md](../../../class9/chemistry/atmospheric-pollution/atmospheric-pollution-part5.md)): ghar ke andar, jahan khana banane wale aur bachche saans lete hain. Wahi 100 MJ LPG stove (60 percent) par: 100 ÷ 0.60 = 166.7 MJ ki garmi, yaani 166.7 ÷ 50 = **3.33 kg LPG**: bahut kam dhuan.

Dusri misal: bade dam ka paani renewable hai, par dam ke peeche gaon doob sakte hain (Topic 3 mein vistar se). Lakdi tab hi sachchi renewable hai jab ugne ki raftaar kaatne se zyaada ho.

**Sahi:** Renewable ka matlab hai ki strot **wapas banta hai**; "saaf hai" ya nahi, ye alag sawal hai, jo dhuan, raakh aur zameen ke asar se tay hota hai.

## Quick Recap

- Energy banti ya khatam nahi hoti, par kaam ki energy bikharti jaati hai: isliye hamein lagatar **strot** chahiye.
- Zyaadatar strot ki jad **Suraj** hai (lakdi, koyla, petrol, hawa, behta paani, solar).
- **Achha fuel**: zyaada calorific value, store aur transport aasaan, sasta, surakshit, kam pollution, aasaani se mile, steady jale.
- **Calorific value** = 1 kg fuel ke poore jalne par mili garmi (kJ/kg); **E = m × calorific value**, m = E ÷ calorific value.
- Is chapter ka table: hydrogen 150,000; LPG aur CNG 50,000; petrol, diesel, kerosene 45,000; coal 30,000; lakdi 17,000; gobar ka upla 7,000 kJ/kg; biogas lagbhag 20 MJ per m³ (lagbhag).
- Strot **do tarah** se baante jaate hain: renewable ya non-renewable, aur conventional ya non-conventional. Ye do alag sawal hain.
- Chulhon ki efficiency (lagbhag): lakdi 10 percent, LPG stove 60 percent, induction plate 85 percent.
- Sabse badi calorific value wala fuel hamesha achha nahi, aur renewable ka matlab pollution-free nahi.

## Practice Questions

1. Find the energy released by completely burning 7.5 kg of dry wood (calorific value 17,000 kJ/kg). Give the answer in MJ.

2. A family burns cow-dung cakes to get 31.5 MJ of heat for a day. How many kg of dung cakes (7,000 kJ/kg) is that?

3. An LPG cylinder holds 14.2 kg of LPG (50 MJ/kg). A family uses it on an LPG stove of efficiency 60 percent. (a) Find the total energy in the cylinder. (b) Find the useful heat it delivers. (c) If the family needs 14.2 MJ of useful heat each day, for how many days does the cylinder last?

4. A city bus runs 5 km for each kg of CNG. It runs 120 km on one trip. (a) How many kg of CNG does it use? (b) How much energy is that (CNG 50 MJ/kg)? (c) What mass of petrol (45 MJ/kg) contains the same energy?

5. A family burns 12 kg of cow-dung cakes (7,000 kJ/kg). What mass of dry wood (17,000 kJ/kg) gives the same energy?

6. A wood chulha of efficiency 10 percent is used to heat 3 kg of water from 20 °C to 100 °C. Taking the specific heat of water as 4200 J/kg °C, find (a) the useful heat, (b) the mass of dry wood needed (17,000 kJ/kg), and (c) the mass of cow-dung cakes (7,000 kJ/kg) if used on the same chulha.

7. The same 3 kg of water is heated from 20 °C to 100 °C on (a) an LPG stove of efficiency 60 percent and (b) an induction plate of efficiency 85 percent. Find the mass of LPG used (50,000 kJ/kg) in grams, and the electrical energy used in kWh.

8. A kerosene stove burns 1.8 kg of kerosene (45,000 kJ/kg). (a) Find the heat released in MJ. (b) What mass of dry wood (17,000 kJ/kg) would release the same heat?

9. A 0.16 kg sample of a fuel releases 1,120 kJ on complete burning. Find its calorific value in kJ/kg and say which fuel of this chapter's table it is.

10. A family of 4 needs about 10 MJ of food energy each per day. What mass of kerosene (45,000 kJ/kg) has the same energy as the daily food of the whole family? (Compare quantities of energy only.)

11. Calculate how much dry wood is needed in a wood chulha (efficiency 10 percent) to give the same useful heat as one full 14.2 kg LPG cylinder (50 MJ/kg) on an LPG stove (efficiency 60 percent).

12. Kerosene, wood, cow-dung cake, LPG and CNG: classify each as renewable or non-renewable (wood only if new trees keep being planted). Which of the renewable ones are still polluting when burnt in a chulha?

13. State True or False with a reason.
    (a) A fuel with the highest calorific value is always the best fuel.
    (b) Renewable means free of pollution.
    (c) Calorific value is measured in kJ/kg.
    (d) A stove of efficiency 10 percent gives out 10 percent of the fuel energy as useful heat.

14. A village is deciding between LPG (50 MJ/kg) and cow-dung cakes (7 MJ/kg) for school meals. Give two reasons why LPG is better and one reason why the village might still prefer dung cakes. Which of the seven criteria of Part B is each reason about?

### Answers

1. **Given:** m = 7.5 kg, calorific value = 17,000 kJ/kg. **Find:** E. **Formula:** E = m × calorific value. **Substitution:** E = 7.5 × 17,000 = 127,500 kJ = **127.5 MJ**.
   Jaanch: 127,500 ÷ 7.5 = 17,000 ✅. Units: kg × kJ/kg = kJ ✅.

2. **Given:** E = 31.5 MJ = 31,500 kJ, calorific value = 7,000 kJ/kg. **Formula:** m = E ÷ calorific value. **Substitution:** m = 31,500 ÷ 7,000 = **4.5 kg**.
   Jaanch: 4.5 × 7,000 = 31,500 ✅.

3. (a) Total energy = 14.2 × 50 = **710 MJ**. (b) Useful heat = 710 × 0.60 = **426 MJ**. (c) Din = 426 ÷ 14.2 = **30 din**.
   Jaanch: 14.2 MJ × 30 = 426 MJ ✅; 426 ÷ 710 = 0.60 ✅.

4. (a) Fuel = 120 km ÷ 5 km/kg = **24 kg**. (b) E = 24 × 50 = **1200 MJ**. (c) Petrol = 1200 ÷ 45 = **26.7 kg** lagbhag.
   Jaanch: 26.7 × 45 = 1201.5 ≈ 1200 ✅; petrol ki calorific value CNG se kam, isliye mass zyaada ✅.

5. Energy = 12 × 7,000 = 84,000 kJ = 84 MJ. Lakdi = 84,000 ÷ 17,000 = **4.94 kg** lagbhag.
   Jaanch: 4.94 × 17,000 = 83,980 ≈ 84,000 ✅. Wood ki calorific value zyaada, isliye mass kam ✅.

6. (a) Q = m c ΔT = 3 × 4200 × 80 = 1,008,000 J = **1008 kJ**. (b) Input = 1008 ÷ 0.10 = 10,080 kJ; lakdi = 10,080 ÷ 17,000 = **0.593 kg** lagbhag. (c) Dung = 10,080 ÷ 7,000 = **1.44 kg**.
   Jaanch: 0.593 × 17,000 × 0.10 = 1008 ✅; 1.44 × 7,000 × 0.10 = 1008 ✅.

7. Useful heat wahi 1008 kJ. (a) Input = 1008 ÷ 0.60 = 1680 kJ; LPG = 1680 ÷ 50,000 = 0.0336 kg = **33.6 g**. (b) Input = 1008 ÷ 0.85 = 1185.9 kJ lagbhag = 1185.9 ÷ 3600 = **0.329 kWh** lagbhag.
   Jaanch: 0.0336 × 50,000 × 0.60 = 1008 ✅; 1185.9 × 0.85 = 1008 ✅.

8. (a) E = 1.8 × 45,000 = 81,000 kJ = **81 MJ**. (b) m = 81,000 ÷ 17,000 = **4.76 kg** lagbhag.
   Jaanch: 4.76 × 17,000 = 80,920 ≈ 81,000 ✅.

9. **Formula:** calorific value = E ÷ m = 1,120 ÷ 0.16 = **7,000 kJ/kg**. Ye table mein **gobar ka upla** hai.
   Jaanch: 0.16 × 7,000 = 1,120 ✅.

10. Family ki energy = 4 × 10 MJ = 40 MJ = 40,000 kJ. Kerosene = 40,000 ÷ 45,000 = **0.889 kg** lagbhag (889 g).
    Jaanch: 0.889 × 45,000 = 40,005 ≈ 40,000 ✅.

11. LPG cylinder ki useful heat = 14.2 × 50 × 0.60 = 426 MJ. Chulhe ki efficiency 10 percent hai, to input = 426 ÷ 0.10 = 4260 MJ. Lakdi = 4260 ÷ 17 = **250.6 kg** lagbhag.
    Jaanch: 250.6 × 17 × 0.10 = 426 MJ ✅. Wood chulhe ki efficiency LPG stove se 6 guna kam aur lakdi ki calorific value LPG se kam hai, isliye lakdi ka kg LPG ke kg se bahut zyaada ✅.

12. Kerosene: **non-renewable**. Wood: **renewable** (agar naye ped lagte rahein). Cow-dung cake: **renewable** (gobar roz banta hai). LPG: **non-renewable**. CNG: **non-renewable**. Renewable mein se **lakdi aur gobar ka upla** chulhe mein dhuan dete hain.
    Jaanch: Part D ke table se mila kar; teen fossil fuels non-renewable, do biomass renewable ✅.

13. (a) **False** ❌: hydrogen ki calorific value sabse zyaada hai par store karna aur banana mushkil hai. (b) **False** ❌: lakdi renewable hai par bahut dhuan deti hai. (c) **True** ✅: kJ/kg ya MJ/kg. (d) **False** ❌: 10 percent hi useful heat hai; baaki 90 percent aas-paas mein chali jaati hai.

14. LPG ke do reason: (1) calorific value 50 ÷ 7 = lagbhag 7 guna zyaada, to kam mass (criterion 1), (2) kam dhuan, bachchon aur rasoiyon ki sehat (criterion 5). Dung cakes ka reason: gaon mein mufta mil jaate hain aur cylinder ki delivery door se aati hai (criteria 3 aur 6). Isliye faisla kai kasautiyon ka santulan hai.
    Jaanch: 50 ÷ 7 = 7.14 ✅.
