# Topic 4: Resonance: Jab Frequency Mil Jaaye

Topic 3 mein humne dekha ki jab koi periodic force (driver) kisi object ko hilata hai, to object driver ki frequency f_d par hilta hai, apni natural frequency f_n par nahi. Aur amplitude tab bahut chhota rehta hai jab f_d, f_n se door ho, aur f_d, f_n ke paas aate hi curve par ek ooncha peak banta hai. Wahan humne likha tha ki "bahut zyada, Topic 4 mein". Aaj us "bahut zyada" ka naam bhi seekhenge aur raaz bhi: **itna bada amplitude chhote se push se kaise ban jaata hai?** Sympathetic vibration ko bhi ab uska asli naam milega.

Shaam ko society garden mein Mr. Sahasrabudhe apni bhanji Sanjana ke saath aaye. Garden ke kone mein ek jhoola tha, jiski chain lagbhag 3 m lambi thi. Dheere-dheere jhoola oopar-oopar uthne laga!

Sanjana hairaan thi. "Mama, pehle aap zor se dhakka dete the aur kuch nahi hua. Ab bas halka tap de rahe ho aur main itni oopar ja rahi hoon! Ye jaadu hai kya?" ## Part A: Jhoole ka raaz: push taal mein

Jhoola ek pendulum hai, to uski apni natural frequency f_n hai. Mama ke 10 jhoole 35 s mein: T = 3.5 s, to f_n = 1 ÷ 3.5 lagbhag 0.29 Hz. Mama driver hain, aur unke taps ki frequency f_d hai.

**Mama ne tap kab diya?** Har baar us pal, jab jhoola peeche ke point se aage jaana shuru karta hai. Us pal push **jhoole ki chaal ke saath** hota hai, to jhoole ko thodi aur energy mil jaati hai. Agla push theek ek poore jhoole (3.5 s) baad, phir wahi pal, phir wahi madad. Ye **taal mein** push hai: f_d = f_n. Har push ki madad pichhli madad ke upar jud jaati hai, isliye energy dheere-dheere jama hoti jaati hai.

Ab mana lo Mama har 2.0 s mein tap dete. Figure 4.1 dono ko saath dikhati hai.

```
swing:         FFFFFFFbbbbbbbFFFFFFFbbbbbbbFFFFFFFbbbbbbbFFFFFFFbbbbbbbF
Mama, 3.5 s:   P             P             P             P             P
Mama, 2.0 s:   P       P       P       P       P       P       P       P
t (s)          0   .   2   .   4   .   6   .   8   .   10  .   12  .   14
               F = swing aage ja rahi hai, b = swing peeche aa rahi hai
```

> **Figure 4.1** – 14 s ka samay. Upar ki patti mein jhoole ka aage (F) aur peeche (b) jaana, T = 3.5 s ke hisaab se. Beech ki line mein har 3.5 s par push (P): har push F ke bilkul shuru mein hai. Neeche ki line mein har 2.0 s par push: kuch push F mein, kuch b mein.

### Example 1: Jhoole ke push ka rhythm

Jhoole ka T = 3.5 s hai. Uski f_n, ek minute mein push ki sankhya, aur chain ki lambai nikalo.

**Given:** T = 3.5 s, g = 9.8 m/s², π = 3.14.
**Find:** f_n, push per minute, l.
**Formula:** f = 1 ÷ T, push = 60 s ÷ T, l = g T² ÷ (4π²) (T = 2π√(l ÷ g) se).
**Substitution:** f_n = 1 ÷ 3.5 s = 0.286 Hz. Push = 60 ÷ 3.5 = 17.1, yaani lagbhag **17**. l = (9.8 × 3.5²) ÷ (4 × 3.14²) = 120.05 ÷ 39.44 = **3.04 m**.
**Answer:** f_n lagbhag 0.29 Hz, 17 push per minute, chain lagbhag 3 m.
**Jaanch:** 17 × 3.5 = 59.5 s, lagbhag 60 s ✅. T = 2π√(3.04 ÷ 9.8) = 6.28 × 0.557 = 3.50 s ✅. Chain lagbhag 3 m wahi hai jo kahani mein thi ✅

### Example 2: Galat rhythm kya karta hai?

Wahi jhoola (T = 3.5 s), par driver har 2.0 s par tap deta hai, pehla tap t = 0 par jab jhoola peeche ke point se aage jaana shuru kar raha hai. Pehle 7 taps (t = 0 se 12 s) mein kitne madad karte hain aur kitne ulta?

Jhoole ka aadha T = 1.75 s aage jaata hai, phir 1.75 s peeche. Push tab madad karta hai jab jhoola aage ja raha ho (aage ka push). Har tap ka pal T ka kitna hissa hai, wo (t mod 3.5) ÷ 3.5 se nikalte hain:

| t (s) | 0 | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 | 12 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T ka hissa | 0 | 0.57 | 0.14 | 0.71 | 0.29 | 0.86 | 0.43 |
| Jhoola kidhar | aage | peeche | aage | peeche | aage | peeche | aage |
| Asar | madad | ulta | madad | ulta | madad | ulta | madad |

**Answer:** 7 mein se 4 madad, 3 ulta. Hissa 0.5 se kam ho to jhoola aage ja raha hai.
**Jaanch:** 4 + 3 = 7 taps ✅. Resonance wale push mein 7 mein se 7 madad karte hain. Yahan ulta push madad ko kaat deta hai, isliye net badhat bahut kam hai ✅ (taps ki taakat ek jaisi maani gayi, ye ek simple model hai).

**Ghar par adult ke saath (jhoole ki jagah haath ka pendulum):** dhaage ke ek sire par chhota halka bob (ek button ya mitti ki chhoti goli, koi bhaari cheez nahi) bandho aur dhaage ka doosra sira ungli aur angoothe se pakdo, jaise bob aapki ungli ke neeche latak raha ho. Pehle bob ko ek baar hilne do aur 10 jhoole ka time naap kar T nikalo. Ab haath ko bahut chhote, sirf 1 cm ke, left-right jhatke do, kabhi tez, kabhi dheere. Jab jhatkon ka rhythm bob ke apne rhythm ke barabar ho jaata hai, tab bob achanak bahut zyada hilne lagta hai. Haath ka itna chhota hilna dekh kar hairaan raho! Koi kursi ya table par khade hokar ye mat karo.

## Part B: Energy ka jodna, aur peak

### Itna bada amplitude kyun, aur kab tak?

Har push jhoole ko thodi energy deta hai (Class 9: zyada energy matlab zyada oonchai). Par Topic 2 ke damping ke hisaab se jhoola har jhoole mein kuch energy hawa aur chain ke ghisaav mein kho bhi deta hai. To kya amplitude hamesha badhta rahega?

**Example 3: Energy ka lena-dena (kalpanik numbers)**

Maan lo (sirf samajhne ke liye, asli maap nahi) har push jhoole ko 6 units energy deta hai aur jhoola har jhoole mein apni energy ka 4 percent kho deta hai. Pehle push se pehle energy 0 hai. Push n ke baad energy: E_naya = 0.96 × E_purana + 6.

**Given:** push = 6 units, loss = 4 percent per swing.
**Find:** E pehle 5 push ke baad aur 10 push ke baad, aur sabse bada sambhav E.
**Formula:** E_naya = 0.96 × E_purana + 6. Sthir sthiti mein E_naya = E_purana = E, to 0.04 × E = 6.
**Substitution:** n = 1: 6. n = 2: 0.96 × 6 + 6 = 11.76. n = 3: 17.29. n = 4: 22.60. n = 5: 27.69. n = 10: **50.28**. Sthir E = 6 ÷ 0.04 = **150 units**.
**Answer:** energy lagataar badhti hai (10 push ke baad lagbhag 50 units) aur 150 units par ruk jaati hai.
**Jaanch:** E = 150 par 0.96 × 150 + 6 = 144 + 6 = 150 ✅. Jitni energy push se aati hai, utni hi har jhoole mein jaati hai, isliye amplitude hamesha nahi badhta ✅

Yaani resonance mein amplitude tab tak badhta hai jab tak **har jhoole mein andar aane wali energy = bahar jaane wali energy**. Energy banti nahi, Mama ke push se aati hai.

(Ek imaandari ki baat: jab Sanjana bahut oopar jaati hai, to bade angle par jhoole ka T thoda badal sakta hai. Topic 1 ka T = 2π√(l ÷ g) chhote jhoolon ke liye hai. Ye ek line ki baat hai, humare numbers par asar nahi.)

### Response curve aur uska peak

Topic 3 ka response curve (f_d ke saath amplitude ka graph) ab hum zyada dhyan se dekhte hain. Figure 4.2 mein x-axis par f_d ÷ f_n hai: 1.0 ka matlab f_d = f_n.

```
 6.0 I                                o
     I
 5.0 I
     I
 4.0 I                             o
     I                                   o
 3.0 I
     I                          o
 2.0 I                       o  +  +  +     o
     I                 *  *  +           +  +  o
 1.0 I  *  *  *  *  *                          +  *  o
     I                                               +  *  *  *  *  *
     +------------------------------------------------------------------> f_d ÷ f_n
       0.0            0.5            1.0            1.5            2.0
   (y: amplitude, 1 = jab driver bahut dheere ho; 1 row = 0.5 unit)
   o = halka damping (patla, ooncha peak), + = bhaari damping (chauda, neecha peak), * = dono curve ek hi jagah
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Response curve (schematic, numbers samjhane ke liye). Halke damping wale (o) curve ka peak f_d = f_n par bahut ooncha aur patla hai: amplitude 6 units tak, jabki f_d bahut dheere ho (shuru mein) to 1 unit. Bhaari damping wale (+) curve ka peak sirf 2 units hai aur chauda hai.

Dekho: peak sirf bahut paas aane par aata hai. f_d ÷ f_n = 0.5 par amplitude lagbhag 1.3 unit hai, 1.0 par 6 unit, aur 1.5 par phir lagbhag 0.8 unit. Isiliye jhoole ko 3.5 s se bahut alag rhythm par tap karne se kuch nahi hota.

**Damping aur resonance saath:** damping jitni bhaari, peak utna neecha aur chauda. Jhoola agar shahad mein hota, to resonance par bhi bada amplitude kabhi nahi banta. Isi liye shock absorber wali car (Topic 2) ki body sadak ke gaddhon par resonance se nahi hilti: damping peak ko dabaa deta hai.

### Example 4: Kaun sa pendulum resonate karega?

Figure 4.3 ke D ki lambai 60 cm hai. Paanchon halke pendulums ka T nikal kar batao kaun sa D ke saath resonate karega.

**Given:** l = 0.30, 0.45, 0.60, 0.75, 0.90 m, g = 9.8 m/s², π = 3.14.
**Find:** T aur f har ek ka, aur resonate karne wala.
**Formula:** T = 2π√(l ÷ g), f = 1 ÷ T.
**Substitution:** T = 6.28 × √(l ÷ 9.8).

| l (m) | T (s) | f (Hz) |
|---|---|---|
| 0.30 | 1.10 | 0.91 |
| 0.45 | 1.35 | 0.74 |
| 0.60 | 1.55 | 0.64 |
| 0.75 | 1.74 | 0.58 |
| 0.90 | 1.90 | 0.53 |

**Answer:** D ka T = 1.55 s, to driver frequency f_d = 0.64 Hz. Sirf 0.60 m wala pendulum (C) isse milta hai, to **C resonate karega**.
**Jaanch:** T ∝ √l: 0.90 ÷ 0.60 = 1.5, √1.5 = 1.22, to T = 1.55 × 1.22 = 1.90 s ✅ (table se milta). Units: m ÷ (m/s²) = s², √ se s ✅

### Example 5: Jhoole ke liye "seconds pendulum"

Ek driver har 0.5 Hz par push deta hai (ek push har 2 s mein). Kitni lambai ka pendulum resonate karega?

**Given:** f_d = 0.5 Hz, g = 9.8 m/s², π = 3.14.
**Find:** l jab f_n = f_d.
**Formula:** T = 1 ÷ f, l = g T² ÷ (4π²).
**Substitution:** T = 1 ÷ 0.5 = 2 s. l = (9.8 × 2²) ÷ (4 × 3.14²) = 39.2 ÷ 39.44 = **0.994 m**.
**Answer:** lagbhag **0.99 m** (lagbhag 1 m).
**Jaanch:** T = 2π√(0.99 ÷ 9.8) = 6.28 × 0.3178 = 1.996 s, lagbhag 2.0 s ✅. Isi ko "seconds pendulum" kehte hain ✅

## Part D: Resonance aur kahan?

### Sympathetic vibration ka asli naam

Topic 3 mein ek tuning fork ko maarne par usi frequency ka doosra fork bhi bajne lagta tha. Ab hum ise jaante hain: doosre fork ki f_n pehle ki f_d ke barabar thi, to **resonance** hua. Guitar ki do taarein bhi aisi: ek taar chhedo to usi f_n wali doosri taar bhi hilti hai.

### Example 6: String aur fork

Ek guitar string ki natural frequency 262 Hz (middle C ke lagbhag) hai. Hawa mein v = 340 m/s. Kis frequency ka fork usse resonate karega? Us fork ka T aur hawa mein λ nikalo.

**Given:** f_n = 262 Hz, v = 340 m/s.
**Find:** f_d, T, λ.
**Formula:** f_d = f_n, T = 1 ÷ f, λ = v ÷ f.
**Substitution:** f_d = **262 Hz**. T = 1 ÷ 262 = 0.00382 s = **3.82 ms**. λ = 340 ÷ 262 = **1.30 m**.
**Answer:** 262 Hz ka fork, T = 3.82 ms, λ lagbhag 1.30 m.
**Jaanch:** 262 Hz × 0.00382 s = 1.00 ✅, 262 × 1.30 = 340.6 m/s, lagbhag 340 ✅

### Radio ka dial

Chapter 5 ke Topic 4 mein ([scattering-and-spectrum-part4.md](../scattering-and-spectrum/scattering-and-spectrum-part4.md)) likha tha ki radio ka tuner ek hi frequency ki current ko chhaant leta hai aur "tuning" Chapter 6 mein samjhenge. Wo ye hai: radio ke andar ek circuit ki apni natural frequency hoti hai, jise dial ghuma kar badalte hain. Har station ki wave us circuit ko drive karti hai, par bada response sirf us station ka aata hai jiski frequency circuit ki f_n ke barabar ho gayi. Baaki stations Barton ke A, B, E, F ki tarah bahut chhota response dete hain. Dial ghumana = apni f_n badalna = **tuning**. (Radio waves EM waves hain, sound nahi. Circuit ke formula aage ki class mein.)

### Bottle ka air column (ghar par adult ke saath)

Ek khaali plastic bottle (kaanch ki nahi) ke munh ke upar se phoonk maaro. Ek sur nikalta hai. Ab adult bottle mein thoda paani daale, aur phir phoonko: sur **ooncha** ho gaya. Paani se air column chhota hua, aur **chhote air column ki f_n zyada hoti hai**. Phoonk ki hawa ke jhonke jis frequency par air column ko hilate hain, wahi sur bada ban jaata hai. Flute mein ungli ke holes air column ko chhota-bada karte hain. (Formula aage ki class mein.)

### Gaane se glass toota? Imaandaar jawab

Haan, ye sach ho sakta hai: glass ki apni f_n hoti hai (wine glass lagbhag 500 se 1000 Hz, glass par depend karta hai). Agar bahut tez, **steady** aur bilkul usi frequency ki aawaz glass ko kuch der tak mile, aur glass patla aur nazuk ho, to uska amplitude itna bada ho sakta hai ki wo toot jaaye. Par ye asaani se nahi hota: sahi frequency, bahut loud aawaz aur sahi glass sab ek saath chahiye. **Ghar par glass tod kar kabhi mat try karo**: toota glass chot deta hai. Ye sirf padhne ke liye.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **"Resonance ke liye bada force chahiye."** ❌ Resonance mein **chhota force bhi sahi samay par baar-baar** lage to bada amplitude banata hai. Demonstration (Example 3 ke kalpanik numbers): ek bada dhakka 20 units ka, aur uske 10 jhoole baad bachi energy 20 × 0.96¹⁰ = 13.3 units. Doosri taraf 6 units ke 10 chhote taps (taal mein) 50 units tak pahunch gaye. Chhote taps ka ekatth hona bade ek dhakke par bhaari pada. Aur ghar par haath wale pendulum mein bhi 1 cm ka jhatka kaafi tha.
2. **"Resonance hamesha cheezein tod deta hai."** ❌ Resonance sirf bada amplitude hai, aur wo kaafi baar kaam ki cheez hai. Demonstration: Sanjana ka jhoola (maza aata hai), radio ka tuning (bina resonance ke koi station nahi), guitar ki body, bottle ki sur. Nuksaan tab hota hai jab koi imaarat ya pul bahut kam damping ke saath apni f_n par push paata hai, aur wo Topic 5 mein dekhenge.

## Quick Recap

- **Resonance:** f_d = f_n par amplitude sabse bada.
- Har push **taal mein** (har jhoole mein ek hi pal par) aata hai, to madad jud jaati hai; bina taal ke push ek doosre ko kaat dete hain.
- Energy push se aati hai. Amplitude tab ruk jaata hai jab andar aane wali energy = damping se jaane wali energy.
- Response curve ka **resonance peak** f_d = f_n par: halka damping to ooncha aur patla, bhaari damping to neecha aur chauda.
- **Barton's pendulums:** driver ki lambai wala pendulum sabse bada hilta hai, baaki chhote. Resonance ke liye pendulum ki lambai l = g T² ÷ (4π²), T = 1 ÷ f_d.
- Sympathetic vibration, radio ki tuning, bottle ka air column (chhota column, ooncha sur) sab resonance ke udaharan.
- Resonance bada force nahi mangta aur hamesha todta nahi.

5. A driver pendulum is 0.20 m long. A second pendulum is 0.80 m long. Find both time periods and their ratio. Will the second one resonate with the first? What length would?
6. A student moves a thread side to side 9 times in 18.9 s. She has two pendulums on it, of lengths 1.10 m and 0.70 m. Which one will swing widely, and why?
7. A guitar string has a natural frequency of 247 Hz. Three tuning forks of 235 Hz, 247 Hz and 259 Hz are struck near it one by one. Which fork makes the string sound strongly? Find the string's time period in ms.
8. A string has a natural frequency of 294 Hz. Find the time period and the wavelength in air of the sound that will drive it to resonance.
9. A playground swing has T = 4.0 s. Find its natural frequency, the number of in-step pushes needed in 2 minutes, and the chain length.
10. A swing has T = 2.5 s. Pusher A pushes every 2.5 s, pusher B every 1.8 s. Both start at t = 0 when the swing is at the back point starting forward. For B's first six pushes (t = 0, 1.8, 3.6, 5.4, 7.2, 9.0 s), count how many help (swing moving forward) and how many oppose. Which pusher builds a larger swing?
11. The same driver is used on a pendulum swinging in air and on the same pendulum with a large cardboard sheet attached. Which shows the taller peak and which responds over a wider range of driver frequencies? Why?
12. Two plastic bottles blow notes when you blow across their mouths. The air column in bottle P is 15 cm and in bottle Q is 24 cm. Which gives the higher note? What happens to Q's note if water is added to shorten its air column?
13. State true or false and give a one-line reason: (a) A small push can build a large swing. (b) At resonance, amplitude grows forever. (c) Resonance occurs when f_d is much larger than f_n. (d) In Barton's set, only the pendulum equal in length to the driver moves.
14. A student says, "I will break a wine glass with a 100 Hz loudspeaker at home." Is the plan sensible? Give two reasons it is unlikely to work, and one reason it is unsafe.

### Answers

1. **Given:** T = 3.0 s. **Find:** f_d, l. **Formula:** f = 1 ÷ T, l = g T² ÷ (4π²). **Substitution:** f_d = 1 ÷ 3.0 = **0.33 Hz**. l = (9.8 × 3.0²) ÷ 39.44 = 88.2 ÷ 39.44 = **2.24 m**.
   Jaanch: T = 2π√(2.24 ÷ 9.8) = 6.28 × 0.478 = 3.00 s ✅

2. T = 2.4 s. l = (9.8 × 2.4²) ÷ 39.44 = 56.45 ÷ 39.44 = **1.43 m**.
   Jaanch: 6.28 × √(1.43 ÷ 9.8) = 6.28 × 0.382 = 2.40 s ✅

3. f_d = 1 ÷ 1.3 = **0.77 Hz**. l = (9.8 × 1.3²) ÷ 39.44 = 16.56 ÷ 39.44 = **0.42 m**.
   Jaanch: 6.28 × √(0.42 ÷ 9.8) = 6.28 × 0.207 = 1.30 s ✅

4. T = 6.28 × √(l ÷ 9.8). l = 0.25 m: 6.28 × 0.1597 = **1.00 s**. l = 0.49 m: 6.28 × 0.2236 = **1.40 s**. l = 1.00 m: 6.28 × 0.3194 = **2.01 s**. Driver T = 1.4 s, to **0.49 m wala resonate karega**.
   Jaanch: l ko 4 guna karne (0.25 se 1.00) par T do guna: 1.00 s se 2.01 s ✅

5. T₁ = 6.28 × √(0.20 ÷ 9.8) = 6.28 × 0.1429 = **0.90 s**. T₂ = 6.28 × √(0.80 ÷ 9.8) = 6.28 × 0.2857 = **1.79 s**. Anupaat = 1.79 ÷ 0.90 = **2.0**. Nahi, T₂ ≠ T₁, to resonance nahi. Resonance ke liye **0.20 m** chahiye.
   Jaanch: l 4 guna to T 2 guna (T ∝ √l, √4 = 2) ✅

6. T_d = 18.9 ÷ 9 = 2.1 s. 1.10 m wale ka T = 6.28 × √(1.10 ÷ 9.8) = 6.28 × 0.335 = **2.10 s**. 0.70 m wale ka T = 6.28 × √(0.70 ÷ 9.8) = 6.28 × 0.267 = **1.68 s**. Isliye **1.10 m wala** bada hilega, kyunki uska T = T_d (f_d = f_n).
   Jaanch: f_d = 1 ÷ 2.1 = 0.476 Hz aur 1.10 m wale ki f_n = 1 ÷ 2.10 = 0.476 Hz, barabar ✅

7. 247 Hz wala fork, kyunki f_d = f_n = 247 Hz. T = 1 ÷ 247 = 0.00405 s = **4.05 ms**. 235 aur 259 Hz wale fork se string bahut halka hilegi.
   Jaanch: 247 Hz × 0.00405 s = 1.00 ✅

8. f_d = 294 Hz. T = 1 ÷ 294 = 0.0034 s = **3.40 ms**. λ = 340 ÷ 294 = **1.16 m**.
   Jaanch: 294 × 1.16 = 341 m/s, lagbhag 340 ✅

9. f_n = 1 ÷ 4.0 = **0.25 Hz**. 2 minutes = 120 s, pushes = 120 ÷ 4.0 = **30**. l = (9.8 × 16) ÷ 39.44 = 156.8 ÷ 39.44 = **3.98 m**, lagbhag 4 m.
   Jaanch: 30 × 4.0 = 120 s ✅. T = 6.28 × √(3.98 ÷ 9.8) = 6.28 × 0.637 = 4.00 s ✅

10. B ke taps ka hissa = (t mod 2.5) ÷ 2.5: t = 0: 0 (madad), 1.8: 0.72 (ulta), 3.6: 0.44 (madad), 5.4: 0.16 (madad), 7.2: 0.88 (ulta), 9.0: 0.60 (ulta). **3 madad, 3 ulta**, to kuch khaas badhat nahi. A ka f_d = f_n, har tap pehle jaisa madad. **A bada jhoola banayega.**
    Jaanch: 3 + 3 = 6 pushes ✅

11. Cardboard wale mein zyada air resistance, yaani bhaari damping. Isliye **bina cardboard wale** (air mein) ka peak ooncha, aur **cardboard wale** ka peak neecha par chauda, yaani zyada f_d par response (Figure 4.2).

12. **Bottle P** (15 cm) ka note ooncha hai, kyunki chhota air column, ooncha sur. Q mein paani daal kar column chhota karne par Q ka sur **ooncha** hoga.

13. (a) **True** ✅, sahi samay par baar-baar push jud jaate hain. (b) **False** ❌, damping se jaati energy = push se aati energy par ruk jaata hai. (c) **False** ❌, f_d lagbhag f_n hona chahiye. (d) **False** ❌, saare pendulums hilte hain, par amplitude sirf barabar lambai wale ka sabse bada hota hai.

14. Plan sensible nahi. Wajah 1: 100 Hz, glass ki f_n (lagbhag 500 se 1000 Hz) se bahut door hai, to resonance nahi. Wajah 2: glass tootne ke liye bahut loud, steady aur patle glass ki zaroorat hai. Unsafe: toota glass chot deta hai. Kabhi try mat karo.
