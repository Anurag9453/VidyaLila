# Topic 1: Hilna-Dolna: Natural Vibrations Aur Pendulum

Pichhle chapter mein humne light aur EM waves ka lamba safar kiya. Ab hum ek bilkul paas ki, ghar-aangan wali cheez par aate hain: **jhoola, ghadi ka pendulum, guitar ki taar**. Ye sab baar-baar idhar-udhar hilte hain. Class 9 mein hum ise "vibration" aur "oscillation" kehkar naap chuke hain (amplitude, f aur T). Is chapter mein hum ye samjhenge ki **kaun si cheez kitni tezi se hilegi, ye kaun tay karta hai**, aur aage jaakar ki jab koi bahar se hilata hai to kya hota hai. Is pehle topic mein sirf ek sawal: jab hum kisi cheez ko ek baar hila kar **chhod dete hain**, to wo apni marzi se kitni tezi se hilti hai?

## Park ke jhoole aur lamba jhoola

Itwaar ki subah Mr. Gurjar apni bhanji Bhagyashree ko mohalle ke park mein le gaye. Park mein teen jhoole the. Chhote bachhon ka jhoola chhoti chain ka tha, beech wala jhoola thoda lamba tha, aur sabse kone wala bada jhoola bahut lambi chains par latka tha. Bhagyashree bench par baithi dekh rahi thi. Achanak wo boli, "Mama, ek ajeeb baat hai. Chhote jhoole ka bachha jaldi-jaldi aage-peechhe ho raha hai. Bade jhoole wala to ek chakkar poora karne mein jaise **ages** laga raha hai. Dono ko ek jaisa dhakka mila hai, phir bhi!"

Mr. Gurjar muskuraye. "Aur ek baat dekho. Abhi jo bachha bada jhoola jhool raha hai, uski jagah uska bada bhai baith jaaye to?" Bhagyashree soch mein padh gayi. "Shayad jhoola dheema ho jaayega, kyunki wo bhaari hai?" "Ye tumhara guess hai," Mr. Gurjar bole. "Aaj hum ise guess nahi, **pakki baat** bana kar chhodenge. Aur hum ye bhi dekhenge ki har cheez ki apni ek 'dhun' hoti hai."

## Part A: Free vibration aur "apni dhun"

Jab hum jhoole ko ek baar dhakka dekar chhod dete hain, to wo apne aap aage-peechhe hota rehta hai. Koi lagataar dhakka nahi de raha. Is tarah ke hilne ko hum **free vibration** kehte hain. Free vibration mein cheez kitni baar prati second hilti hai, wo uski **natural frequency f_n** hai.

Bhagyashree ne poochha, "Mama, f_n kaun tay karta hai? Dhakka kitna zor se diya?" Mr. Gurjar ne jawab diya, **"Nahi. Dhakka sirf ye tay karta hai ki jhoola kitna oonche tak jaayega (amplitude). Kitni jaldi-jaldi hilega, ye cheez khud tay karti hai."** Aisa kyun?

Socho: jhoola jab ek taraf pahunchta hai to gravity use wapas beech ki taraf kheenchti hai. Wapas laut aane ki ye taakat aur use palatne mein lagne wala samay cheez ki **banaavat** par depend karte hain: kitni lambi chain hai, kitna bhaari hai, kitni kadak hai. Isliye **har cheez ki apni ek dhun (apna f_n) hoti hai**, jaise har insaan ka apna chalne ka dhang.

### Teen cheezein jo f_n tay karti hain

| Cheez | Kya badalne par f_n kaise badlega |
|---|---|
| Lambai | Pendulum ya jhoole ki chain lambi to f_n kam (dheere hilega) |
| Mass (spring par latka) | Zyada mass to f_n kam (dheere hilega) |
| Kadakpan | Zyada kadak (stiff) spring ya patti to f_n zyada (jaldi hilegi) |

Mass-spring wali baat sirf gunaatmak (qualitative) hai: formula aage ki class mein aata hai. Par pendulum ka formula hum is topic mein paayenge.

### Safe activity 1: ruler ka "tuk-tuk" (school mein teacher ke saath ya ghar par adult ke saath)

Ek plastic ya lakdi ki 30 cm wali ruler lo. Use mez par rakh kar ek moti kitaab se ek chhor ke paas dabao taaki baaki ka hissa mez ke kinare se bahar nikla rahe. Bahar nikle hisse ko **overhang** kehte hain. Overhang ko haath se thoda neeche daba kar chhodo (chehre se door rakh kar): ruler "tuk-tuk-tuk" karti hai.

```
 chhota overhang :  [kitaab]+----------           (10 cm)   tuk-tuk-tuk jaldi-jaldi
 lamba overhang  :  [kitaab]+--------------------  (20 cm)   tuk.. tuk.. dheere
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Mez par kitaab se dabi ruler. Har "-" lagbhag 1 cm overhang hai. Chhota overhang kadak banta hai aur jaldi-jaldi hilta hai, lamba overhang dheere hilta hai. Dono mein dhakka halka ya zor ka, farak sirf kitne oonche tak hilne ka hoga, tuk-tuk ki raftaar ka nahi.

Overhang jitna lamba, ruler utni dheere hilti hai (f_n kam). Dhakka zor se do ya halke se, "tuk-tuk" ki raftaar lagbhag wahi rehti hai. Bas hilne ki oonchai badalti hai.

### Safe activity 2: spring aur halka sikka (ghar par adult ke saath)

Ek chhoti spring ya moti rubber band ek taraf haath se pakdo aur doosre sire se ek **halka** sikka latkao (bhaari cheez nahi, chehre se door). Halka sa neeche kheench kar chhodo. Ab sikka do-teen sikke tak badhao: spring ab pehle se **dheere** uchhalti hai. Spring ki jagah zyada kadak (moti) rubber band lo to wo **jaldi** uchhalti hai.

## Part B: Simple pendulum aur uska formula

Pendulum jhoole ka chhota, saaf-suthra model hai, isliye isse naap kar baat pakki karna aasaan hai.

```
        =============================
                      O  <- O: latkane ka point (support)
                     /I\
                    / I \
                   /  I  \
                  /   I   \
                 /    I    \
                /     I     \
               /      I      \
               o      I      o
               A      o      B
                      C
        A, B: extreme positions (bob ek pal ruka)
        C: beech ka point, sabse neeche
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Simple pendulum. O se bob tak ki lambai l hai. A aur B extreme positions hain (yahan bob ek pal ke liye ruka hai, saari energy PE), C beech ka point hai (saari energy KE). A se C se B aur wapas A tak ek oscillation hai.

Ab hum pendulum ka time period T ek **formula** se paate hain. Ye formula hum yahan sidhe maan lete hain (iski banaavat aage ki class mein hoti hai):

> **T = 2π √(l ÷ g)**
> T (s) = 2 × 3.14 × √ [ l (m) ÷ g (m/s²) ], g = 9.8 m/s²

Is formula se teen khaas baatein nikalti hain:

1. **Bob ka mass formula mein hai hi nahi.** Bhaari bob ho ya halka, T wahi.
2. **Chhote swing mein amplitude bhi formula mein nahi hai.** Thoda kheench kar chhodo ya zyada (par bahut bada swing nahi), T lagbhag wahi. (Bahut bade swing mein T thoda sa badhta hai: ye aage ki class mein.)
3. **l jitni badi, T utna bada** (√l ke saath), aur **g jitna bada, T utna chhota**.

Units ka check: l ÷ g = m ÷ (m/s²) = s². √(s²) = s. 2π ek shuddh sankhya hai. To T ka unit second hai ✅.

### Example 1: l = 1.00 m ka pendulum

Ek pendulum ki lambai l = 1.00 m hai. T aur f nikalo. (g = 9.8 m/s², π = 3.14.)

**Given:** l = 1.00 m, g = 9.8 m/s².
**Find:** T aur f.
**Formula:** T = 2π √(l ÷ g), f = 1 ÷ T.
**Substitution:** l ÷ g = 1.00 ÷ 9.8 = 0.102 s². √0.102 = 0.319 s. T = 6.28 × 0.319 = 2.006 s. f = 1 ÷ 2.006 = 0.498 Hz.
**Answer:** T lagbhag **2.0 s** aur f lagbhag **0.50 Hz**.
**Jaanch:** f × T = 0.498 × 2.006 = 1.0 ✅. Unit s ✅. Ye wahi hai jo "lagbhag 1 m lamba pendulum 2 second mein ek oscillation karta hai" wali baat kehti hai ✅

### Example 2: Seconds pendulum ki lambai

Wo pendulum jiska T = 2 s ho, uski lambai kitni hogi?

**Given:** T = 2 s, g = 9.8 m/s².
**Find:** l.
**Formula:** T = 2π √(l ÷ g). Dono taraf square karo: T² = 4π² × l ÷ g, to **l = g T² ÷ (4π²)**.
**Substitution:** 4π² = 4 × 3.14² = 4 × 9.8596 = 39.44. T² = 4 s². l = 9.8 × 4 ÷ 39.44 = 39.2 ÷ 39.44 = 0.994 m.
**Answer:** l lagbhag **0.99 m**.
**Jaanch:** Wapas daalo: 6.28 × √(0.994 ÷ 9.8) = 6.28 × √0.1014 = 6.28 × 0.3185 = 2.0 s ✅. Units: (m/s²) × s² = m ✅

### Safe activity 3: 20 swings ginna (ghar par adult ke saath)

Dhaage ke ek sire par chhota, halka bob (sikka ya goli, bhaari patthar nahi) baandho aur doosra sira darwaze ki chaukhat par ya kisi stand se baandho. Kursi, mez ya jhoole par khade hokar kabhi nahi: zameen par khade rahkar kaam karo, bob ko chehre se door rakho. Dhaage ki lambai metre-scale se naapo. Bob ko thoda sa kheench kar chhodo aur stopwatch se **20 oscillations** ka total samay naapo, phir 20 se bhaag do (Class 9 ki trick).

### Example 3: Stone-on-thread ka result

Kisi ne dhaage par 20 oscillations 38.4 s mein poore kiye. T, f aur dhaage ki lambai nikalo.

**Given:** 20 oscillations mein 38.4 s, g = 9.8 m/s².
**Find:** T, f aur l.
**Formula:** T = total samay ÷ oscillations; f = 1 ÷ T; l = g T² ÷ (4π²).
**Substitution:** T = 38.4 ÷ 20 = 1.92 s. f = 1 ÷ 1.92 = 0.52 Hz. l = 9.8 × 1.92² ÷ 39.44 = 9.8 × 3.686 ÷ 39.44 = 36.13 ÷ 39.44 = 0.916 m.
**Answer:** T = **1.92 s**, f lagbhag **0.52 Hz**, l lagbhag **0.92 m**.
**Jaanch:** Wapas: 6.28 × √(0.916 ÷ 9.8) = 6.28 × √0.0935 = 6.28 × 0.3058 = 1.92 s ✅. Aur f × T = 0.52 × 1.92 = 1.0 ✅

## Part C: Lambai ka asar: ratio method

Hum har baar poora formula nahi lagate. Formula kehta hai **T ∝ √l**, yaani T lambai ke square root ke saath badhta hai. Isse ek asaan **ratio method** nikalta hai:

> **T₂ ÷ T₁ = √(l₂ ÷ l₁)**   (g wahi rehne par)

Matlab agar lambai **4 guna** ho jaaye to T **√4 = 2 guna** hota hai. Lambai 9 guna to T 3 guna. Lambai ek-chauthai to T aadha.

```
 Har # = 0.25 m (lambai)             Har # = 0.25 s (time period)

 l = 0.25 m : #                      T = 1.00 s : ####
 l = 1.00 m : ####                   T = 2.01 s : ########
 l = 4.00 m : ################       T = 4.01 s : ################
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Lambai ke saath T. l 0.25 m se 1.00 m aur phir 4.00 m: har baar 4 guna. T 1.00 s se 2.01 s aur phir 4.01 s: har baar sirf 2 guna.

### Example 4: Lambai 4 guna

Ek pendulum ki lambai 0.30 m hai. Use 1.20 m kar diya gaya. T pehle aur baad mein kya hai, aur unka ratio?

**Given:** l₁ = 0.30 m, l₂ = 1.20 m, g = 9.8 m/s².
**Find:** T₁, T₂ aur T₂ ÷ T₁.
**Formula:** T = 2π √(l ÷ g); ratio = √(l₂ ÷ l₁).
**Substitution:** T₁ = 6.28 × √(0.30 ÷ 9.8) = 6.28 × 0.175 = 1.10 s. T₂ = 6.28 × √(1.20 ÷ 9.8) = 6.28 × 0.350 = 2.20 s. l₂ ÷ l₁ = 1.20 ÷ 0.30 = 4, √4 = 2.
**Answer:** T₁ lagbhag **1.10 s**, T₂ lagbhag **2.20 s**, ratio **2**.
**Jaanch:** 2.20 ÷ 1.10 = 2 ✅, jo ratio method se aaya ✅

### g ka asar (sirf gunaatmak)

T ∝ 1 ÷ √g: jahan g kam ho wahan pendulum dheere jhoolta hai. Ghadi ka pendulum (jo ginti karke samay batata hai) isiliye kisi **oonchi pahaadi par ya Chaand par** dheema chalega: g chhota hai, har swing lamba lagta hai, aur ghadi ek din mein kam swing ginti hai. (Chaand par g lagbhag 1.6 m/s² hai: [gravitation-and-space-missions-part2.md](../gravitation-and-space-missions/gravitation-and-space-missions-part2.md).)

### Example 5: Ek soch ka sawaal (sirf ratio)

Maan lo (sirf soch ke liye) kisi jagah g ghat kar **ek-chauthai** reh jaata hai. Wahi pendulum wahan T kitna guna ho jaata hai? Aur wahan ki pendulum-ghadi ek asli din (24 ghante) mein kitna samay dikhaayegi?

**Given:** g_naya = g ÷ 4, l wahi.
**Find:** T_naya ÷ T.
**Formula:** T = 2π √(l ÷ g).
**Substitution:** T_naya = 2π √(l ÷ (g ÷ 4)) = 2π √(4l ÷ g) = 2 × 2π √(l ÷ g) = 2T.
**Answer:** T **2 guna** ho jaata hai. Ghadi har swing ko 2 guna der se ginti hai, isliye 24 ghante mein wo sirf **12 ghante** dikhayegi.
**Jaanch:** g aadha nahi, ek-chauthai hai, isliye ratio √4 = 2 ✅ (g ghatne par T badha, sahi disha ✅)

### Example 6: Park ke dono jhoole

Park ke chhote jhoole ki chain lagbhag 2.5 m aur bade jhoole ki chain lagbhag 4.0 m hai. Dono ke T aur f kya honge? (Bachhe ko pendulum ka bob maano, l = chain ki lambai.)

**Given:** l₁ = 2.5 m, l₂ = 4.0 m, g = 9.8 m/s².
**Find:** T₁, T₂, f₁, f₂.
**Formula:** T = 2π √(l ÷ g), f = 1 ÷ T.
**Substitution:** T₁ = 6.28 × √(2.5 ÷ 9.8) = 6.28 × 0.505 = 3.17 s. T₂ = 6.28 × √(4.0 ÷ 9.8) = 6.28 × 0.639 = 4.01 s. f₁ = 1 ÷ 3.17 = 0.32 Hz. f₂ = 1 ÷ 4.01 = 0.25 Hz.
**Answer:** chhota jhoola T lagbhag **3.2 s** (f lagbhag 0.32 Hz), bada jhoola T lagbhag **4.0 s** (f lagbhag 0.25 Hz).
**Jaanch:** Ratio: √(4.0 ÷ 2.5) = √1.6 = 1.265, aur 4.01 ÷ 3.17 = 1.265 ✅. Dono "park ke jhoole lagbhag 3 se 4 s" ki range mein ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Bhaari bob dheere jhoolta hai"**

Demonstration (ghar par adult ke saath): do alag dhaage, dono ki l = 1.00 m. Ek par halka sikka, doosre par goli (dono halke, bhaari patthar nahi). Dono ko **ek saath**, ek jaise thoda kheench kar chhodo. Dono bob lagbhag saath-saath hi aage-peechhe hote dikhenge. 20 oscillations ginne par dono ko lagbhag 40 s lagte hain: kyunki T = 2π √(1.00 ÷ 9.8) = 2.0 s, aur 20 × 2.0 = 40 s. Formula mein mass ka naam tak nahi. Bhaari cheez ko neeche kheenchne wali gravity bhi zyada hoti hai, aur use hilana bhi utna hi mushkil: dono ka asar kat jaata hai. Isiliye **T bob ke mass par depend nahi karta**.

**Galti 2: "Zor se dhakka do to f_n badh jaati hai"**

Demonstration: wahi l = 1.00 m ka pendulum. Pehle bob ko 5 cm kheench kar chhodo, phir 10 cm kheench kar. Dono baar 20 oscillations lagbhag 40 s mein poori hongi, yaani T lagbhag 2.0 s (stopwatch ki chhoti galti se thoda alag aa sakta hai). Ruler wale "tuk-tuk" mein bhi yahi dikha: halka ya zor ka dhakka, raftaar wahi. Dhakka **amplitude** badalta hai (kitna door tak), **f_n nahi**. f_n tay karne wali cheezein hain: **lambai, mass (spring mein) aur kadakpan**, yaani cheez ki apni banaavat.

## Quick Recap

- **Free vibration:** cheez ko ek baar hila kar chhod dena. **Natural frequency f_n:** free vibration mein 1 second ki vibrations (Hz).
- f_n cheez ki apni banaavat tay karti hai (lambai, mass, kadakpan), **dhakke ki taakat nahi**. Dhakka sirf amplitude tay karta hai.
- Spring par bhaari mass dheere hilta hai, kadak spring ya patti jaldi hilti hai (gunaatmak).
- **Simple pendulum:** T = 2π √(l ÷ g), l latkane ke point se bob ke centre tak, g = 9.8 m/s², π = 3.14.
- T bob ke mass par nahi, chhote swing mein amplitude par bhi nahi. T lambai ke saath √l ke roop mein badhta hai, aur g ke saath ghatta hai.
- Ratio method: T₂ ÷ T₁ = √(l₂ ÷ l₁). Lambai 4 guna to T 2 guna.
- l = 1.00 m par T lagbhag 2.0 s. **Seconds pendulum** (T = 2 s) ki l lagbhag 0.99 m.
- Chaand ya pahaadi par g kam hone se pendulum-ghadi dheemi chalti hai.
- Har cheez ki apni "dhun" hai: jhoola lagbhag 0.25 se 0.33 Hz, tuning fork lagbhag 440 Hz, wine glass lagbhag 500 se 1000 Hz.

## Practice Questions

1. A pendulum has a length of 0.40 m. Find its time period and its natural frequency.

3. A student times 15 swings of a pendulum in 27 s. Find T, f and the length of the pendulum.
4. A pendulum of length 0.72 m is taken to the Moon, where g = 1.6 m/s². Find its time period there and the period on Earth. By what factor does the period change? A pendulum clock built with this pendulum shows how many minutes when 1 hour passes on the Moon?
5. The length of a pendulum is increased from 0.50 m to 4.50 m. By what factor does T change? Find T before and after.
6. What length must a pendulum have so that its time period is 1.2 s?
7. Two pendulums have the same length. One has a bob of 30 g and the other a bob of 120 g. Which one has the larger time period? Give a reason.
8. A playground swing has a natural frequency of 0.25 Hz. Find its time period and the approximate length of its chain.
9. Two rulers of the same kind, one with 12 cm overhang and another with 24 cm overhang, are flicked and released. Which one vibrates faster? What happens to the rate of vibration if the same ruler is flicked harder?
10. A student says, "When I pulled the bob farther, its natural frequency increased, because it swung faster in the middle." Correct her.
11. State true or false, with one line of reason: (a) A heavier bob gives a larger T. (b) If the length is made 4 times, T becomes 4 times. (c) A stiffer spring gives a higher natural frequency for the same mass. (d) A pendulum clock runs slower where g is slightly smaller. (e) Pulling a pendulum farther sideways (small swing) changes T a lot.
12. Two guitar strings have the same length and are equally tight, but one is thick and the other is thin. Which has the higher natural frequency? Explain in one or two lines.
13. A pendulum clock is accurate in a city on the plain. It is carried to a hill station. Will it show more or less time than the real time? Explain without calculation.

### Answers

1. **Given:** l = 0.40 m, g = 9.8 m/s². **Find:** T, f. **Formula:** T = 2π √(l ÷ g), f = 1 ÷ T.
   **Substitution:** l ÷ g = 0.40 ÷ 9.8 = 0.0408 s², √ = 0.202 s. T = 6.28 × 0.202 = 1.27 s. f = 1 ÷ 1.27 = 0.79 Hz.
   **Answer:** T = **1.27 s**, f = **0.79 Hz**. **Jaanch:** f × T = 0.79 × 1.27 = 1.0 ✅

2. **Given:** l₁ = 2.25 m, l₂ = 6.25 m. **Find:** T₁, T₂, ratio.
   **Substitution:** √(2.25 ÷ 9.8) = √0.2296 = 0.479, T₁ = 6.28 × 0.479 = 3.01 s. √(6.25 ÷ 9.8) = √0.6378 = 0.799, T₂ = 6.28 × 0.799 = 5.02 s. Ratio = 5.02 ÷ 3.01 = 1.67.
   **Ratio method:** √(6.25 ÷ 2.25) = 2.5 ÷ 1.5 = 5 ÷ 3 = 1.67.
   **Answer:** T₁ = **3.01 s**, T₂ = **5.02 s**, ratio = **1.67**. **Jaanch:** dono tareekon se 1.67 ✅

3. **Given:** 15 swings, 27 s. **Find:** T, f, l.
   **Substitution:** T = 27 ÷ 15 = 1.8 s. f = 1 ÷ 1.8 = 0.56 Hz. l = g T² ÷ (4π²) = 9.8 × 3.24 ÷ 39.44 = 31.75 ÷ 39.44 = 0.805 m.
   **Answer:** T = **1.8 s**, f = **0.56 Hz**, l lagbhag **0.81 m**.
   **Jaanch:** Wapas: 6.28 × √(0.805 ÷ 9.8) = 6.28 × 0.2866 = 1.80 s ✅

4. **Given:** l = 0.72 m, g_Moon = 1.6 m/s², g = 9.8 m/s² (Earth). **Find:** T_Moon, T_Earth, factor, clock reading.
   **Substitution:** T_Moon = 6.28 × √(0.72 ÷ 1.6) = 6.28 × √0.45 = 6.28 × 0.671 = 4.21 s. T_Earth = 6.28 × √(0.72 ÷ 9.8) = 6.28 × 0.271 = 1.70 s. Factor = √(9.8 ÷ 1.6) = √6.125 = 2.47. Ghadi: 60 min ÷ 2.47 = 24.3 min.
   **Answer:** T_Moon = **4.21 s**, T_Earth = **1.70 s**, T lagbhag **2.5 guna** ho gaya. Chaand par 1 ghante mein ghadi lagbhag **24 minute** dikhayegi.
   **Jaanch:** 1.70 × 2.47 = 4.20 s ≈ 4.21 s (rounding) ✅. Ghadi: 24.3 × 2.47 = 60 min ✅

5. **Given:** l₁ = 0.50 m, l₂ = 4.50 m. **Substitution:** l₂ ÷ l₁ = 9, √9 = 3. T₁ = 6.28 × √(0.50 ÷ 9.8) = 6.28 × 0.226 = 1.42 s. T₂ = 6.28 × √(4.50 ÷ 9.8) = 6.28 × 0.678 = 4.26 s.
   **Answer:** T **3 guna** ho jaata hai: 1.42 s se **4.26 s**. **Jaanch:** 1.42 × 3 = 4.26 ✅

6. **Given:** T = 1.2 s. **Formula:** l = g T² ÷ (4π²). **Substitution:** l = 9.8 × 1.44 ÷ 39.44 = 14.11 ÷ 39.44 = 0.358 m.
   **Answer:** l lagbhag **0.36 m**. **Jaanch:** 6.28 × √(0.358 ÷ 9.8) = 6.28 × 0.1911 = 1.20 s ✅

7. **Dono ka T barabar** hai. T = 2π √(l ÷ g) mein mass nahi hota, l aur g dono ke liye same hain. (30 g ho ya 120 g, farak nahi.) **Jaanch:** koi bhi mass daalo, formula wahi rehta hai ✅

8. **Given:** f_n = 0.25 Hz. **Substitution:** T = 1 ÷ 0.25 = 4 s. l = 9.8 × 16 ÷ 39.44 = 156.8 ÷ 39.44 = 3.98 m.
   **Answer:** T = **4 s**, chain lagbhag **3.98 m**, yaani lagbhag 4 m. **Jaanch:** Ye "park ki chain lagbhag 2 se 4 m" wali range ke andar hai ✅

9. **12 cm wala** jaldi vibrate karega (chhota overhang, zyada kadak). Ruler ko zor se flick karne par rate (f_n) **wahi** rehta hai, sirf amplitude badhta hai. **Jaanch:** Figure 1.1 mein chhota overhang jaldi hila tha ✅
