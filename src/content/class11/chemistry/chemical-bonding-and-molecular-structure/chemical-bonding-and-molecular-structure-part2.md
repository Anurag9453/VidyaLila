# Topic 2: VSEPR Theory, Molecules ke 3D Shapes, Bond Parameters, Dipole Moment aur Fajan's Rules

Topic 1 ke Lewis structures ne humein bataya ki kaun-sa atom kisse juda hai, par kaagaz par bane H−O−H ya N−H₃ ke chitra unka asli **3D aakaar (geometry)** nahi dikhate! 1940 mein Sidgwick-Powell aur 1957 mein Nyholm-Gillespie ne ek behad aasaan aur kamaal ka siddhant diya — **VSEPR Theory (Valence Shell Electron Pair Repulsion Theory)** — jisse bina kisi bade computer ke tum kisi bhi molecule ka 3D shape aur bond angle turant bata sakte ho! Iske saath hi hum **Bond Parameters**, **Dipole Moment (μ)** aur **Fajan's Rules** seekhenge.

## Part A: VSEPR Theory — Repulsion Order aur 3D Shapes Master Table

**Golden Repulsion Order (Sabse Zaroori Niyam!):**
> **Lone pair – Lone pair (lp–lp)  >  Lone pair – Bond pair (lp–bp)  >  Bond pair – Bond pair (bp–bp)**

*Kyun?* Bond pair do nuclei ke beech khincha rehta hai (patla hota hai), jabki **lone pair** sirf ek hi central nucleus ke paas phaila (mota!) rehta hai — isliye lone pair baaki bonds ko zor se dabaa kar **bond angle chhota** kar deta hai!

| Total Pairs (bp + lp) | Electron Geometry | Bond Pairs + Lone Pairs | **Asli Molecular Shape** | Bond Angle | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **2** | Linear | 2 bp + 0 lp | **Linear** | 180° | BeCl₂, CO₂ |
| **3** | Trigonal planar | 3 bp + 0 lp | **Trigonal planar** | 120° | BF₃, BCl₃ |
| **3** | Trigonal planar | 2 bp + 1 lp | **Bent (V-shaped)** | ~119° | SO₂, O₃ |
| **4** | Tetrahedral | 4 bp + 0 lp | **Tetrahedral** | **109.5°** | CH₄, CCl₄ |
| **4** | Tetrahedral | 3 bp + 1 lp | **Trigonal pyramidal** | **107°** | NH₃ |
| **4** | Tetrahedral | 2 bp + 2 lp | **Bent (V-shaped)** | **104.5°** | H₂O |
| **5** | Trigonal bipyramidal | 5 bp + 0 lp | **Trigonal bipyramidal** | 90°, 120° | PCl₅ |
| **5** | Trigonal bipyramidal | 4 bp + 1 lp | **See-saw** *(lp equatorial!)* | < 90°, < 120° | SF₄ |
| **5** | Trigonal bipyramidal | 3 bp + 2 lp | **T-shaped** *(dono lp equatorial!)* | < 90° | ClF₃ |
| **5** | Trigonal bipyramidal | 2 bp + 3 lp | **Linear** *(teeno lp equatorial!)* | 180° | XeF₂, I₃⁻ |
| **6** | Octahedral | 6 bp + 0 lp | **Octahedral** | 90° | SF₆ |
| **6** | Octahedral | 5 bp + 1 lp | **Square pyramidal** | < 90° | BrF₅ |
| **6** | Octahedral | 4 bp + 2 lp | **Square planar** *(lp 180° opposite!)* | 90° | XeF₄ |

### Example 1. CH₄ (109.5°) → NH₃ (107°) → H₂O (104.5°) aur SF₄ (See-Saw) ka Raaz

- **CH₄, NH₃ aur H₂O** teeno mein kul **4 electron pairs** (tetrahedral electron geometry) hain:
  - **CH₄ (4 bp + 0 lp):** Sirf bp–bp repulsion → exact **109.5°** (Tetrahedral).
  - **NH₃ (3 bp + 1 lp):** 1 mota lone pair teeno N−H bonds ko neeche dabata hai → angle ghat kar **107°** (Trigonal pyramidal).
  - **H₂O (2 bp + 2 lp):** Do lone pairs ke beech sabse tez **lp–lp repulsion** dono O−H bonds ko aur zyada dabata hai → angle ghat kar **104.5°** (Bent)!
- **SF₄ (4 bp + 1 lp = 5 pairs):** Trigonal bipyramid mein 3 **equatorial** (120° par) aur 2 **axial** (90° par) jagah hoti hain. Lone pair hamesha **equatorial** jagah par baithta hai kyunki wahan use sirf **do 90° repulsions** jhelne padte hain (axial par teen 90° repulsions milte!). Isse shape **See-saw** banta hai!

**Jaanch:** 109.5° (0 lp) > 107° (1 lp) > 104.5° (2 lp) — har lone pair angle ko ~2.5° kam karta hai ✅.

## Part B: Bond Parameters, Dipole Moment (NH₃ vs NF₃!) aur Fajan's Rules

### 1. Bond Parameters ka Golden Rishta
- **Bond Order (BO):** Do atoms ke beech bonds ki ginti (single = 1, double = 2, triple = 3).
- **Golden Rule:** **Bond Order jitna ZYADA → Bond Enthalpy (taakat) utni ZYADA → Bond Length utni CHHOTI!**
  - Example: C−C (154 pm, 348 kJ/mol) → C=C (134 pm, 614 kJ/mol) → C≡C (120 pm, 839 kJ/mol).

### 2. Dipole Moment (μ = q × d) aur Mashhoor NH₃ banaam NF₃ Paheli!
Dipole moment ek **vector** hai (disha +ve se −ve end ki taraf, cross-arrow (+--->) se dikhate hain). Symmetrical molecules jaise **CO₂ (linear), BF₃ (trigonal planar), CH₄ / CCl₄ (tetrahedral)** mein saare bond dipoles ek-doosre ko cancel karke **net μ = 0 (non-polar)** kar dete hain!

**Class 11 ka Sabse Famous Sawaal: F, H se zyada electronegative hai, phir bhi NH₃ (1.47 D) ka dipole moment NF₃ (0.23 D) se 6 guna ZYADA kyun hai?**

```
       NH₃ (μ = 1.47 D)                      NF₃ (μ = 0.23 D)
            ^                                       ^
            :  (Lone pair dipole UPAR)              :  (Lone pair dipole UPAR)
           :N:                                     :N:
         /  |  \                                 /  |  \
        H   H   H                               F   F   F
       (^) (^) (^)                             (v) (v) (v)
   (N-H dipoles bhi UPAR!)                 (N-F dipoles NEECHE!)
   Dono JUD JAATE hain --> BADA μ!         Dono TAKRA kar GHAT jaate hain --> CHHOTA μ!
```

> **Figure 2.1** – NH₃ mein lone-pair dipole aur teeno N−H dipoles ek hi disha (upar) mein jud jaate hain (1.47 D); NF₃ mein N−F dipoles neeche ki taraf hokar lone-pair dipole ka virodh karte hain (0.23 D).

### 3. Fajan's Rules (Ionic Bond mein Covalent Character!)
Koi bhi bond 100% ionic nahi hota! Jab ek **chhota, zyada positive charge wala cation** (jaise Li⁺, Be²⁺, Al³⁺) kisi **bade anion** (jaise I⁻, Cl⁻) ke electron cloud ko apni taraf khinch kar teda (polarise) kar de, to ionic bond mein **covalent character** aa jaata hai:
- **Cation:** Jitna **chhota size** aur **zyada charge** → zyada polarising power (e.g. **AlCl₃ > MgCl₂ > NaCl** covalent character!).
- **Anion:** Jitna **bada size** aur **zyada charge** → zyada polarisability (e.g. **LiI > LiBr > LiCl > LiF** covalent character!).

### Example 2. Dipole Moment se % Ionic Character aur Group Hydrides ke Bond Angles

1. **% Ionic Character Formula:** **% Ionic Character = (μ_observed ÷ μ_calculated) × 100** *(jahan μ_calculated = 100% ionic maankar e × d = (1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C) × d(m), aur 1 D = 3.336 × 10⁻³⁰ C·m)*.
   - **HCl (d = 127 pm = 1.27 × 10⁻¹⁰ m, μ_obs = 1.03 D):**
     - μ_calc = (1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ × 1.27 × 10⁻¹⁰) ÷ (3.336 × 10⁻³⁰) = **6.10 D**.
     - **% Ionic Character** = (1.03 ÷ 6.10) × 100 = **16.9%**!
2. **Group mein Neeche Jaane par Bond Angle Ghat-ta Kyun Hai?**
   - **NH₃ (107°) > PH₃ (93.6°) > AsH₃ (91.8°)** aur **H₂O (104.5°) > H₂S (92.1°)**:
   - Central atom ki electronegativity ghatne par shared bond pairs central atom se **door** khisak jaate hain → bond pairs ke beech **bp–bp repulsion kam** ho jaata hai → lone pair unhein aur zyada dabaa deta hai → angle ghat-ta hai!

**Jaanch:** 1.03 ÷ 6.10 = 0.1689 ≈ 16.9% ionic character in HCl ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: SF₄ ya ClF₃ mein lone pairs ko axial position par rakh dena.**
   *Sahi baat:* Trigonal bipyramidal (5 pairs) mein lone pairs **HAMESHA equatorial positions** par baithte hain taaki 90° repulsions kam se kam hon!

2. **Galti 2: "F zyada electronegative hai, isliye NF₃ > NH₃ dipole moment" likh dena.**
   Hamesha **lone-pair orbital dipole** ki disha bhi jodo! NH₃ mein sab ek disha mein (1.47 D), NF₃ mein ulti disha mein (0.23 D)!

3. **Galti 3: Electron-pair geometry aur Molecular shape ko ek hi bol dena.**
   NH₃ aur H₂O dono ki **electron geometry tetrahedral** hai, par lone pairs dikhai nahi dete isliye unka **molecular shape** kramashah **trigonal pyramidal** aur **bent** hai!

## Quick Recap

- **VSEPR Repulsion:** lp–lp > lp–bp > bp–bp; angle ghatta hai: CH₄ (109.5°) > NH₃ (107°) > H₂O (104.5°).
- **5-pair lone pairs:** Hamesha **equatorial** (SF₄ see-saw, ClF₃ T-shaped, XeF₂ linear); **6-pair 2 lp:** 180° opposite (XeF₄ square planar).
- **Higher Bond Order** → Higher Bond Enthalpy → Shorter Bond Length.
- **μ(NH₃) = 1.47 D > μ(NF₃) = 0.23 D** (vector addition vs opposition).
- **Fajan's Rules:** Chhota high-charge cation + bada anion → zyada covalent character.

3. Why do the two lone pairs in **ClF₃** occupy **equatorial** positions rather than axial positions of the trigonal bipyramid, making the molecule **T-shaped**?
4. Predict whether each of the following molecules has a **zero (μ = 0)** or **non-zero (μ ≠ 0)** net dipole moment, and give a one-line reason: (a) **BF₃**, (b) **CCl₄**, (c) **CHCl₃**, (d) **H₂S**, (e) **XeF₄**.
5. Explain clearly with vector arrows why **NH₃ (μ = 1.47 D)** has a much larger dipole moment than **NF₃ (μ = 0.23 D)**, even though the N−F bond is more polar than the N−H bond.
6. Using **Fajan's Rules**, arrange each set in **increasing order of covalent character**:
   (a) **NaCl, MgCl₂, AlCl₃**
   (b) **LiF, LiCl, LiBr, LiI**
7. Compare **C−C, C=C and C≡C** in terms of (a) bond order, (b) bond length, and (c) bond enthalpy.
8. Both **CO₂** and **SO₂** have the formula AB₂, yet CO₂ is **non-polar (μ = 0)** while SO₂ is **polar (μ = 1.62 D)**. Explain why using VSEPR theory.
9. In **XeF₄** (4 bond pairs + 2 lone pairs), why do the two lone pairs sit **180° opposite** to each other, giving a **square planar** shape instead of a distorted shape?
10. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) In VSEPR theory, bp–bp repulsion is stronger than lp–lp repulsion.
    (b) The I₃⁻ ion (2 bp + 3 lp) has a linear molecular shape.
    (c) According to Fajan's rules, a small cation with a high positive charge has high polarising power.
    (d) Higher bond order always corresponds to a longer and weaker bond.
11. The observed dipole moment of **HF** is **1.91 D** and its bond length is **92 pm** (9.2 × 10⁻¹¹ m). Calculate its **percentage ionic character** (e = 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C, 1 D = 3.336 × 10⁻³⁰ C·m).
12. Explain why the bond angle in **H₂O (104.5°)** is significantly larger than the bond angle in **H₂S (92.1°)**, even though both have 2 bond pairs and 2 lone pairs.

### Answers

1. (a) **BeCl₂ (2 bp):** Linear / **Linear** (180°).
   (b) **BCl₃ (3 bp):** Trigonal planar / **Trigonal planar** (120°).
   (c) **ClF₃ (3 bp + 2 lp):** Trigonal bipyramidal / **T-shaped**.
   (d) **BrF₅ (5 bp + 1 lp):** Octahedral / **Square pyramidal**.
   (e) **XeF₂ (2 bp + 3 lp):** Trigonal bipyramidal / **Linear** (180°).
   Jaanch: Har molecule mein bp + lp ka jod electron geometry se match karta hai ✅.

2. Teeno mein 4 electron pairs (tetrahedral) hain, par CH₄ mein 0 lp, NH₃ mein 1 lp aur H₂O mein 2 lp hain. Kyunki **lp–lp > lp–bp > bp–bp**, lone pairs badhne par bond pairs zyada dab jaate hain aur angle 109.5° → 107° → 104.5° ghat jaata hai.

3. Equatorial position par lone pairs ko 120° ki khuli jagah milti hai aur minimum 90° repulsions jhelne padte hain (axial par teen 90° repulsions aate). Isliye dono lone pairs equatorial par baithte hain aur bache 3 F atoms **T-shape** banate hain.

4. (a) **BF₃:** μ = **0** (symmetrical trigonal planar, dipoles cancel).
   (b) **CCl₄:** μ = **0** (symmetrical tetrahedral, dipoles cancel).
   (c) **CHCl₃:** μ **≠ 0** (C−H aur C−Cl bonds alag hain, cancel nahi hote).
   (d) **H₂S:** μ **≠ 0** (bent shape, 2 lone pairs).
   (e) **XeF₄:** μ = **0** (symmetrical square planar, opposite Xe−F dipoles cancel).

5. NH₃ mein N, H se zyada electronegative hai, isliye teeno N−H bond dipoles aur N ka lone-pair dipole **ek hi disha (upar)** mein jud kar bada μ (1.47 D) banate hain. NF₃ mein F, N se zyada electronegative hai, isliye teeno N−F dipoles **neeche** jaate hain aur upar wale lone-pair dipole ko kaafi had tak cancel kar dete hain (0.23 D).

6. (a) **NaCl < MgCl₂ < AlCl₃** (cation charge +1 < +2 < +3 badhta hai aur size ghatta hai → polarising power badhti hai).
   (b) **LiF < LiCl < LiBr < LiI** (anion size F⁻ < Cl⁻ < Br⁻ < I⁻ badhta hai → polarisability badhti hai).
   Jaanch: Chhota high-charge cation + bada anion = sabse zyada covalent ✅.

7. Bond order: C−C (1) < C=C (2) < C≡C (3). Bond enthalpy: C−C < C=C < C≡C (badhti hai). Bond length: **C−C (154 pm) > C=C (134 pm) > C≡C (120 pm)** (ghat-ti hai).

8. **CO₂** mein C par koi lone pair nahi (linear, 180°), isliye dono C=O dipoles ek-doosre ko cancel karke μ = 0 dete hain. **SO₂** mein S par **1 lone pair** hai, isliye shape **bent (~119°)** hota hai aur dipoles cancel nahi hote (polar!).

9. Octahedral geometry mein 180° opposite baithne par dono lone pairs ke beech **lp–lp repulsion sabse kam** hota hai, aur chaaron Xe−F bonds ek hi plane mein 90° par **square planar** shape banate hain.

    (b) **True** ✅. Teeno lone pairs equatorial par baithte hain aur dono terminal I atoms 180° par linear hote hain.
    (c) **True** ✅. High charge/radius ratio electron cloud ko zor se polarise karta hai.

    % Ionic Character = (1.91 ÷ 4.42) × 100 = **43.2%**.
