## Part A: Valence Bond Theory — Sigma (σ) banaam Pi (π) Bonds

VBT ke mutabik covalent bond tab banta hai jab do atoms ke **half-filled orbitals** (opposite spin wale electrons ke saath) aapas mein overlap karte hain. **Overlap jitna zyada → bond utna mazboot!**

```
  1. SIGMA (σ) BOND (Head-on / Axial overlap — MAZBOOT!)
     (  ) + (  )  --->  (  ::  )        (s-s overlap)
     (  ) + ><    --->  ( :: ><         (s-p overlap)
     ><  +  ><    --->  > :: <          (p-p head-on overlap)

  2. PI (π) BOND (Sidewise / Lateral overlap — KAMZOR!)
      /\      /\          /::::\
      \/  +   \/   --->   (σ axis)      (upar aur neeche sidewise overlap)
      /\      /\          \::::/
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Sigma (σ) bond mein orbitals seedhe internuclear axis par zor se overlap karte hain (mazboot bond); Pi (π) bond mein do parallel p-orbitals axis ke upar aur neeche bagal se chhute hain (kam overlap → kamzor bond).

**σ aur π Bond ke 3 Golden Rules:**
1. **Har single bond** = **1 σ** bond.
2. **Har double bond** = **1 σ + 1 π** bond (dono π nahi hote!).
3. **Har triple bond** = **1 σ + 2 π** bonds.
4. **σ bond hamesha π bond se zyada mazboot** hota hai kyunki head-on overlap ka kshetrafal sidewise overlap se kaafi bada hota hai. Free rotation σ bond ke chaaron taraf aasaan hai, par π bond ghoomne par toot jaata hai!

## Part B: Hybridisation — Master Table aur Steric Number Trick

**Yaad rakhne ka sabse tez formula (Steric Number = H):**
> **H = (Central atom par σ bonds ki ginti) + (Central atom par lone pairs ki ginti)**
> *(π bonds ko hybridisation mein NAHI ginte — we unhybridised p-orbitals se bante hain!)*

| Steric Number (H) | Hybridisation | Orbitals Mixed | Electron Geometry | Bond Angle | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **2** | **sp** | 1 s + 1 p | Linear | 180° | BeCl₂, C₂H₂, CO₂ |
| **3** | **sp²** | 1 s + 2 p | Trigonal planar | 120° | BF₃, C₂H₄, SO₂ |
| **4** | **sp³** | 1 s + 3 p | Tetrahedral | 109.5° *(ya 107°, 104.5°)* | CH₄, C₂H₆, NH₃, H₂O |
| **5** | **sp³d** | 1 s + 3 p + 1 d | Trigonal bipyramidal | 90°, 120° | PCl₅, SF₄, ClF₃, XeF₂ |
| **6** | **sp³d²** | 1 s + 3 p + 2 d | Octahedral | 90° | SF₆, BrF₅, XeF₄ |
| **7** | **sp³d³** | 1 s + 3 p + 3 d | Pentagonal bipyramidal | 72°, 90° | IF₇ |

### Example 1. Carbon ki Teeno Hybridisations — CH₄ (sp³), Ethene C₂H₄ (sp²) aur Ethyne C₂H₂ (sp)

- **1. Methane (CH₄) — sp³:** C ke paas 4 σ bonds + 0 lp → H = 4 → **sp³** (tetrahedral, 109.5°). Kul bonds: **4 σ, 0 π**.
- **2. Ethene (CH₂=CH₂) — sp²:** Har C atom 2 H aur 1 doosre C ke saath **3 σ bonds** + 0 lp banata hai → H = 3 → **sp²** (planar, 120°). Har C par **1 unhybridised p-orbital** bachta hai, jo sidewise overlap karke **1 π bond** banata hai!
  - Kul bonds in C₂H₄ = (4 C−H σ) + (1 C−C σ) + (1 C−C π) = **5 σ + 1 π**.
- **3. Ethyne (H−C≡C−H) — sp:** Har C atom 1 H aur 1 doosre C ke saath **2 σ bonds** + 0 lp banata hai → H = 2 → **sp** (linear, 180°). Har C par **2 unhybridised p-orbitals** bachte hain, jo sidewise overlap karke **2 π bonds** banate hain!
  - Kul bonds in C₂H₂ = (2 C−H σ) + (1 C−C σ) + (2 C−C π) = **3 σ + 2 π**.

**Jaanch:** s-character badhne par (sp³ 25% → sp² 33.3% → sp 50%) orbital nucleus ke paas aata hai, isliye C−C bond lambaai ghatti hai: C₂H₆ (154 pm) > C₂H₄ (134 pm) > C₂H₂ (120 pm) ✅.

### Example 2. PCl₅ (sp³d) mein Axial Bonds Lambe Kyun Hote Hain, aur SF₆ (sp³d²)

*(Chetavani: PCl₅ aur BeCl₂/BF₃ corrosive ya zehreele hain — sirf padhne ke liye, lab mein bina teacher ke mat chhuna!)*

- **PCl₅ (sp³d):** Phosphorus (3s² 3p³ 3d⁰) excited state mein 5 unpaired electrons rakhta hai aur 1 s + 3 p + 1 d mila kar **5 sp³d hybrid orbitals** banata hai (trigonal bipyramidal):
  - **3 Equatorial P−Cl bonds** (ek hi plane mein 120° par): bond length = **204 pm**.
  - **2 Axial P−Cl bonds** (plane ke 90° upar-neeche): bond length = **219 pm**!
  - **Aisa kyun?** Har **axial** bond pair ko plane mein baithe **3 equatorial bond pairs se 90° par tez repulsion** jhelna padta hai (jabki equatorial bond ko sirf 2 axial bonds se 90° repulsion milta hai). Zyada repulsion ki wajah se axial bonds thode **lambe aur kamzor** ho jaate hain — isi wajah se PCl₅ garm karne par aasani se toot kar PCl₃ + Cl₂ de deta hai!
- **SF₆ (sp³d²):** Sulphur excited state mein 6 unpaired electrons ke saath 1 s + 3 p + 2 d mila kar **6 bilkul barabar sp³d² orbitals** banata hai — regular **octahedral** (saare angles 90°, saari 6 S−F bond lengths bilkul barabar!).

**Jaanch:** PCl₅ mein axial length 219 pm > equatorial length 204 pm (fark = 15 pm) ✅.

### VBT Potential Energy Curve (H₂ Banna) aur Positive / Negative / Zero Overlap

1. **H₂ ka Potential Energy Curve:** Jab do H atoms (opposite spin e⁻) door se paas aate hain, to attractive forces repulsive forces se bhaari padte hain aur energy ghat-ti jaati hai — **r = 74 pm (bond length)** par energy minimum (**−435.8 kJ/mol**) par pahunchti hai (stable H₂!). Agar aur paas laao to nuclei ke repulsion se energy tezi se upar chadh jaati hai! *(Parallel spin wale H atoms ya do He atoms mein repulsive forces hamesha bhaari rehte hain, koi minimum nahi banta → He₂ nahi banta!)*
2. **Positive, Negative & Zero Overlap:**
   - **Positive (in-phase) overlap:** Same sign (+ with +) ke lobes milte hain → **bond banta hai**!
   - **Negative (out-of-phase) overlap:** Opposite sign (+ with −) ke lobes milte hain → **bond nahi banta**.
   - **Zero overlap:** Jab orbitals ka orientation match na kare (jaise **s-orbital + pₓ-orbital** jab internuclear axis **z** ho!) → + aur − overlap bilkul cancel → **zero overlap (koi bond nahi!)**.

### Example 3. Acrylonitrile (CH₂=CH−C≡N) mein Hybridisation aur σ/π Count

**Sawaal:** CH₂=CH−C≡N (C1=C2−C3≡N) mein har Carbon atom ki hybridisation aur kul σ tatha π bonds gino.

**Hal:**
- **C1 (CH₂=):** 3 σ + 1 π → **sp²**; **C2 (=CH−):** 3 σ + 1 π → **sp²**; **C3 (−C≡N):** 2 σ + 2 π → **sp**.
- **σ bonds:** 3 (C−H) + 2 (C−C) + 1 (C−N) = **6 σ bonds** *(7 atoms open chain → 7 − 1 = 6 σ!)*.
- **π bonds:** 1 (C=C) + 2 (C≡N) = **3 π bonds**.

**Jaanch:** 7 atoms open chain → 6 σ + 3 π bonds ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: Double bond ko "2 π bonds" ya triple bond ko "3 π bonds" gin lena.**
   *Sahi baat:* Do atoms ke beech pehla bond **hamesha 1 σ** hota hai! Baaki extra bonds hi π hote hain: double = **1 σ + 1 π**, triple = **1 σ + 2 π**!

2. **Galti 2: Ye sochna ki hybridisation do alag-alag atoms ke orbitals ke beech hoti hai.**
   Hybridisation hamesha **EK HI central atom** ke orbitals (jaise Carbon ke apne 2s aur 2p) ke beech hoti hai; do alag atoms ke beech to **overlapping** (bond formation) hoti hai!

3. **Galti 3: Hybridisation nikaalte waqt lone pairs ko bhool jaana.**
   H₂O mein Oxygen par sirf 2 O−H bonds hain, par us par **2 lone pairs** bhi hain! Isliye H = 2 bp + 2 lp = **4 → sp³** (sp nahi!).

---

## Quick Recap

- **σ bond:** Head-on overlap (mazboot); **π bond:** Sidewise overlap of unhybridised p-orbitals (kamzor, sirf σ ke saath banta hai).
- **Double bond** = 1 σ + 1 π; **Triple bond** = 1 σ + 2 π.
- **Steric Number (H) = σ bonds + lone pairs:** 2 → sp (180°), 3 → sp² (120°), 4 → sp³ (109.5°), 5 → sp³d (90°/120°), 6 → sp³d² (90°), 7 → sp³d³ (72°/90°).
- **C₂H₆** (sp³, 7 σ), **C₂H₄** (sp², 5 σ + 1 π), **C₂H₂** (sp, 3 σ + 2 π).
- **PCl₅ (sp³d):** Axial bonds (219 pm) > Equatorial bonds (204 pm) due to greater 90° repulsions.

## Practice Questions

1. Explain why a **sigma (σ) bond** is stronger than a **pi (π) bond** formed between the same two atoms.
2. Predict the **hybridisation of the central atom** and the **electron-pair geometry** of: (a) **BeCl₂**, (b) **BF₃**, (c) **NH₃**, (d) **H₂O**, (e) **BrF₅**, (f) **XeF₄**, (g) **IF₇**. *(Corrosive/toxic reagents: paper problem only.)*
3. Count the exact total number of **σ bonds** and **π bonds** in:
   (a) **Ethane (C₂H₆)**
   (b) **Propene (CH₃−CH=CH₂)**
   (c) **Propyne (CH≡C−CH₃)**
4. Identify the **hybridisation of every carbon atom** (labelled C1, C2, C3 from left to right) in:
   (a) **Propene (CH₃−CH=CH₂)**
   (b) **Propyne (CH≡C−CH₃)**
5. Why are the **two axial P−Cl bonds (219 pm)** in **PCl₅** longer and weaker than the **three equatorial P−Cl bonds (204 pm)**, whereas all six S−F bonds in **SF₆** are completely equal?
6. Explain why the central oxygen atom in **H₂O** is **sp³ hybridised** even though it forms only two covalent bonds with hydrogen.
7. Arrange the hybrid orbitals **sp, sp² and sp³** in **increasing order of percentage s-character** and explain how this affects the **C−H bond length** in C₂H₆, C₂H₄ and C₂H₂.
8. Even though **NH₃** and **CH₄** both have **sp³ hybridised** central atoms, why is the H−N−H bond angle **107°** instead of **109.5°**?
9. Why can a molecule rotate freely around a **C−C single bond** (as in C₂H₆) but NOT around a **C=C double bond** (as in C₂H₄) at room temperature?
10. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) A triple bond consists of 1 σ bond and 2 π bonds.
    (b) Hybridisation takes place between orbitals belonging to two different atoms.
    (c) A π bond is formed by the head-on overlap of two hybrid orbitals.
    (d) In SF₆, sulphur uses one 3s, three 3p and two 3d orbitals to form six sp³d² hybrid orbitals.
11. What is meant by **zero overlap** of atomic orbitals? Give one example of an orbital pair along the z-axis that produces zero overlap.
12. Count the total number of **σ and π bonds** and state the **hybridisation of each carbon atom** in **1,3-butadiene (CH₂=CH−CH=CH₂)**.

### Answers

1. σ bond **head-on (axial) overlap** se banta hai jismein orbitals ka overlap area bahut zyada hota hai; π bond **sidewise overlap** se banta hai jismein overlap kam hota hai — isliye σ bond zyada mazboot hota hai.

2. (a) **BeCl₂** (2 σ + 0 lp = 2): **sp** / Linear.
   (b) **BF₃** (3 σ + 0 lp = 3): **sp²** / Trigonal planar.
   (c) **NH₃** (3 σ + 1 lp = 4): **sp³** / Tetrahedral (shape: trigonal pyramidal).
   (d) **H₂O** (2 σ + 2 lp = 4): **sp³** / Tetrahedral (shape: bent).
   (e) **BrF₅** (5 σ + 1 lp = 6): **sp³d²** / Octahedral (shape: square pyramidal).
   (f) **XeF₄** (4 σ + 2 lp = 6): **sp³d²** / Octahedral (shape: square planar).
   (g) **IF₇** (7 σ + 0 lp = 7): **sp³d³** / Pentagonal bipyramidal.
   Jaanch: Har molecule mein H = σ + lp hybridisation ke total orbitals ke barabar hai ✅.

3. (a) **C₂H₆:** 6 C−H + 1 C−C = **7 σ, 0 π**.
   (b) **Propene (CH₃−CH=CH₂):** 6 C−H + 2 C−C σ + 1 C=C π = **8 σ, 1 π**.
   (c) **Propyne (CH≡C−CH₃):** 4 C−H + 2 C−C σ + 2 C≡C π = **6 σ, 2 π**.
   Jaanch: Propene mein 9 atoms hain (open chain → 9 − 1 = 8 σ bonds + 1 π bond) ✅; Propyne mein 7 atoms hain (7 − 1 = 6 σ bonds + 2 π bonds) ✅.

4. (a) **Propene (C1H₃−C2H=C3H₂):** C1 = **sp³** (4 σ), C2 = **sp²** (3 σ + 1 π), C3 = **sp²** (3 σ + 1 π).
   (b) **Propyne (C1H≡C2−C3H₃):** C1 = **sp** (2 σ + 2 π), C2 = **sp** (2 σ + 2 π), C3 = **sp³** (4 σ).

5. **PCl₅ (sp³d)** mein har axial bond ko **3 equatorial bonds se 90° repulsion** milta hai (jabki equatorial ko sirf 2 axial bonds se 90° repulsion milta hai), isliye axial bonds khinch kar lambe (219 pm) aur kamzor ho jaate hain. **SF₆ (sp³d²)** poori tarah symmetrical octahedron hai jismein har S−F bond par bilkul barabar repulsions lagte hain, isliye sabhi 6 bonds barabar hote hain.

6. H₂O mein Oxygen par **2 bond pairs + 2 lone pairs = 4** electron domains hain. Chaar domains ko rakhne ke liye 1 s + 3 p milkar **4 sp³ hybrid orbitals** banate hain (2 mein lone pairs, 2 mein O−H bonds).

7. Increasing s-character: **sp³ (25%) < sp² (33.3%) < sp (50%)**. Zyada s-character ka matlab hybrid orbital nucleus ke zyada paas aur chhota hota hai, isliye C−H bond length ghatti hai: **C₂H₆ (sp³) > C₂H₄ (sp²) > C₂H₂ (sp)**.

8. Dono sp³ hain, par NH₃ mein **1 lone pair** hai. Kyunki **lp–bp repulsion > bp–bp repulsion**, lone pair teeno N−H bonds ko dabaa kar angle 109.5° se **107°** kar deta hai.

9. C−C **σ bond** internuclear axis ke chaaron taraf bilkul gol (cylindrically symmetrical) hota hai, isliye ghoomne par overlap nahi tootta. C=C mein **π bond** ke parallel p-orbitals ghoomte hi apna sidewise overlap kho dete hain (π bond toot jaayega!), isliye free rotation ruka rehta hai.

10. (a) **True** ✅.
    (b) **False** ❌. Hybridisation hamesha **ek hi atom** ke atomic orbitals ke beech hoti hai.
    (c) **False** ❌. π bond do **unhybridised p-orbitals ke sidewise overlap** se banta hai.
    (d) **True** ✅.

11. Jab do orbitals ke orientation ki wajah se unka positive (+ +) aur negative (+ −) overlap barabar hokar ek-doosre ko cancel kar de, to use **zero overlap** kehte hain (koi bond nahi banta!). Example: z-axis par **s-orbital aur pₓ-orbital** (ya pₓ aur pᵧ) ka overlap.

12. **CH₂=CH−CH=CH₂** mein chaaron Carbon atoms 3 σ + 1 π banate hain → **chaaron C atoms sp² hybridised** hain!
    σ bonds = 6 (C−H) + 3 (C−C) = **9 σ bonds** (10 atoms open chain → 10 − 1 = 9 σ); π bonds = **2 π bonds** (do C=C).
    Jaanch: 9 σ + 2 π ✅.
