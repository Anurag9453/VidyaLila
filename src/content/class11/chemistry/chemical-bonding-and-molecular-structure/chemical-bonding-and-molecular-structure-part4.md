# Topic 4: Molecular Orbital Theory (MOT), Bond Order aur O₂ ka Paramagnetism

Lewis structure mein O₂ ko **:Ö=Ö:** likhte hain — saare 16 electrons jode mein (paired) dikhte hain! Is hisaab se O₂ ko **diamagnetic** (chumbak se door bhagne wala) hona chahiye tha. Par jab scientists ne thandi liquid Oxygen ko ek strong magnet ke beech dala, to wo **magnet se chipak kar (paramagnetic)** ruk gayi! Is paheli ko 1932 mein Hund aur Mulliken ne **Molecular Orbital Theory (MOT)** se suljhaya — aur saath hi ye bhi bataya ki **He₂ aur Ne₂ kyun nahi bante** aur **O₂⁺, O₂ se zyada mazboot kyun hai!**

---

## Firoz aur talaab mein takrati do lehrein

Firoz shaam ko talaab (pond) ke kinare baithkar do chhote patthar ek saath paani mein phenk raha tha. Do gol lehrein (ripples) phailti hui aapas mein takrayin! Jahan ek lehar ka **uncha hissa (crest)** doosri lehar ke **unche hisse (crest)** se mila, wahan paani aur **zyada uncha** uth gaya! Aur jahan ek ka **uncha hissa (crest)** doosre ke **gaddhe (trough)** se mila, wahan paani bilkul **chupchaap chapti (flat cancel)** ho gaya!

Teacher ma'am ne muskurate hue kaha, "Firoz, quantum mechanics mein electron bhi ek **wave (lehar)** hai! Jab do atoms ke atomic orbitals milte hain (LCAO — Linear Combination of Atomic Orbitals):
- **Constructive overlap (+ +)** se kam energy wala **Bonding Molecular Orbital (σ, π)** banta hai — jo atoms ko jodta hai!
- **Destructive overlap (+ −)** se zyada energy wala **Antibonding Molecular Orbital (σ*, π*)** banta hai — jo atoms ko alag dhakelta hai!"

Chalo, MOT ke dono energy orders, Bond Order formula aur O₂ ke paramagnetism ka poora khel seekhte hain!

---

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **LCAO** | Linear Combination of Atomic Orbitals — do atomic orbitals (ψ_A ± ψ_B) milkar do naye Molecular Orbitals banate hain | 1s + 1s → σ1s (bonding) aur σ*1s (antibonding) |
| **Bonding MO (BMO)** | Constructive overlap se bana **kam energy** wala orbital (electron density dono nuclei ke beech hoti hai) | σ1s, σ2s, σ2p_z, π2p_x, π2p_y |
| **Antibonding MO (ABMO)** | Destructive overlap se bana **zyada energy** wala orbital (star * se dikhate hain; beech mein node hota hai) | σ*1s, σ*2s, π*2p_x, π*2p_y, σ*2p_z |
| **Bond Order (BO)** | Bonding aur antibonding electrons ke antar ka aadha: **BO = ½(N_b − N_a)** | N₂ ka BO = 3, O₂ ka BO = 2, He₂ ka BO = 0 |
| **Paramagnetic vs Diamagnetic** | Agar MO diagram mein **1 ya zyada unpaired e⁻** hon → **Paramagnetic** (magnet se khinchega); sab paired hon → **Diamagnetic** | O₂ paramagnetic (2 unpaired e⁻); N₂ diamagnetic |

## Part A: MOT ke Do Energy Orders (Sabse Zaroori Niyam!)

Molecular orbitals mein electrons bilkul atomic orbitals ki tarah **Aufbau, Pauli (max 2 e⁻ per MO) aur Hund's rule (barabar energy wale π2p_x, π2p_y mein pehle 1-1 electron!)** se bhare jaate hain.

**Khaas Baat (14 Electrons ki Seema!):**
2s aur 2p orbitals ke aapas mein mixing hone ke kaaran **Li₂ se N₂ (≤ 14 electrons)** aur **O₂ se Ne₂ (> 14 electrons)** ke energy order mein ek chhota par behad zaroori badlaav aata hai:

1. **Order 1 — Kul ≤ 14 electrons ke liye (H₂, He₂, Li₂, Be₂, B₂, C₂, N₂):**
   > **σ1s < σ*1s < σ2s < σ*2s < (π2p_x = π2p_y) < σ2p_z < (π*2p_x = π*2p_y) < σ*2p_z**
   *(Yaad rakho: 14 tak **π2p neeche** aur **σ2p_z upar** hota hai!)*

2. **Order 2 — Kul > 14 electrons ke liye (O₂, F₂, Ne₂ aur unke ions jaise O₂⁺, O₂⁻, O₂²⁻):**
   > **σ1s < σ*1s < σ2s < σ*2s < σ2p_z < (π2p_x = π2p_y) < (π*2p_x = π*2p_y) < σ*2p_z**
   *(Yaad rakho: O₂ se aage **σ2p_z neeche** aur **π2p upar** aa jaata hai!)*

```
     Order 1 (≤ 14 e⁻: B₂, C₂, N₂)           Order 2 (> 14 e⁻: O₂, F₂, Ne₂)
              [  σ*2p_z  ]                            [  σ*2p_z  ]
          [ π*2p_x ] [ π*2p_y ]                   [ π*2p_x ] [ π*2p_y ]
              [  σ2p_z   ]                        [ π2p_x  ] [ π2p_y  ]
          [ π2p_x  ] [ π2p_y  ]                       [  σ2p_z   ]
              [  σ*2s    ]                            [  σ*2s    ]
              [  σ2s     ]                            [  σ2s     ]
```

> **Figure 4.1** – MOT energy order ka antar: ≤ 14 electrons (B₂, C₂, N₂) mein (π2p_x = π2p_y) ki energy σ2p_z se kam hoti hai; > 14 electrons (O₂, F₂, Ne₂) mein σ2p_z neeche aa jaata hai.

## Part B: Bond Order, Stability aur Homonuclear Diatomics

**Teen Golden Niyam:**
1. **Bond Order (BO) = ½(N_b − N_a)** (jahan N_b = bonding e⁻, N_a = antibonding e⁻).
2. **BO > 0** → molecule **stable** hai (ban sakta hai); **BO ≤ 0** → molecule **exist nahi karta** (jaise He₂, Be₂, Ne₂).
3. **BO jitna ZYADA → Bond utna MAZBOOT (high dissociation energy) → Bond Length utni CHHOTI!**

### Example 1. He₂ (Exist Nahi Karta!), N₂ (BO = 3) aur O₂ (Paramagnetic!)

- **1. He₂ (2 + 2 = 4 e⁻):**
  Configuration: **(σ1s)² (σ*1s)²** → N_b = 2, N_a = 2 → **BO = ½(2 − 2) = 0**!
  *Natija:* **He₂ exist nahi karta** — Helium hamesha akela (monoatomic gas) rehta hai!

- **2. N₂ (7 + 7 = 14 e⁻ — Order 1 lagao!):**
  Configuration: **(σ1s)² (σ*1s)² (σ2s)² (σ*2s)² (π2p_x² = π2p_y²) (σ2p_z)²**
  - N_b = 2 + 2 + 4 + 2 = 10; N_a = 2 + 2 = 4.
  - **BO = ½(10 − 4) = 3** (Triple bond N≡N! Bahut mazboot, 946 kJ/mol).
  - Unpaired e⁻ = **0** → **Diamagnetic**!

- **3. O₂ (8 + 8 = 16 e⁻ — Order 2 lagao!):**
  Configuration: **(σ1s)² (σ*1s)² (σ2s)² (σ*2s)² (σ2p_z)² (π2p_x² = π2p_y²) (π*2p_x¹ = π*2p_y¹)**
  - N_b = 10; N_a = 2 + 2 + 2 = 6.
  - **BO = ½(10 − 6) = 2** (Double bond O=O).
  - **Rahasya Suljha!** Aakhiri 2 electrons Hund's rule ke mutabik **π*2p_x¹** aur **π*2p_y¹** mein **alag-alag (unpaired)** baithte hain! Isliye **O₂ PARAMAGNETIC hai!**

**Jaanch:** N₂ (14 e⁻, 0 unpaired → diamagnetic) aur O₂ (16 e⁻, 2 unpaired in π*2p → paramagnetic) experiment se 100% match karte hain ✅.

### Example 2. Super-Important Comparison: O₂⁺, O₂, O₂⁻ aur O₂²⁻

*(O₂ family mein saare electrons ka badlaav aakhiri **antibonding π*2p** orbitals mein hota hai!)*

| Species | Total e⁻ | Last Orbital (π*2p) | N_b | N_a | **Bond Order = ½(N_b − N_a)** | Magnetism |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **O₂⁺** | 15 | (π*2p_x¹) | 10 | 5 | **2.5** | Paramagnetic (1 unpaired) |
| **O₂** | 16 | (π*2p_x¹ = π*2p_y¹) | 10 | 6 | **2.0** | Paramagnetic (2 unpaired) |
| **O₂⁻** (superoxide) | 17 | (π*2p_x² = π*2p_y¹) | 10 | 7 | **1.5** | Paramagnetic (1 unpaired) |
| **O₂²⁻** (peroxide) | 18 | (π*2p_x² = π*2p_y²) | 10 | 8 | **1.0** | **Diamagnetic (0 unpaired!)** |

- **Stability / Bond Strength Order (BO ke hisaab se):** **O₂⁺ (2.5) > O₂ (2.0) > O₂⁻ (1.5) > O₂²⁻ (1.0)**.
- **Bond Length Order (bilkul ulta!):** **O₂⁺ (112 pm) < O₂ (121 pm) < O₂⁻ (126 pm) < O₂²⁻ (149 pm)**.

**Jaanch:** Antibonding π*2p se 1 electron nikaalne par (O₂ → O₂⁺) bond order 0.5 badh kar 2.5 ho gaya aur bond chhota (112 pm) ho gaya ✅.

### LCAO ki 3 Shartein aur H₂ se Ne₂ Tak Complete MOT Master Table

**Effective LCAO ke liye 3 Shartein:** (1) Combine hone wale atomic orbitals ki **energy lagbhag barabar** ho (1s + 1s ✓, 1s + 2s ✗); (2) Internuclear axis ke chaaron taraf **symmetry same** ho (p_z + p_z ✓, p_z + p_x ✗); (3) **Maximum overlap** ho!

| Molecule | Total e⁻ | Bond Order | Unpaired e⁻ | Magnetism | Exist Karta Hai? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **H₂** | 2 | **1** | 0 | Diamagnetic | Haan |
| **He₂** | 4 | **0** | 0 | — | **Nahi!** |
| **Li₂** | 6 | **1** | 0 | Diamagnetic | Haan (vapour) |
| **Be₂** | 8 | **0** | 0 | — | **Nahi!** |
| **B₂** | 10 | **1** | **2** (π2p) | **Paramagnetic** | Haan |
| **C₂** | 12 | **2** (dono π!) | 0 | Diamagnetic | Haan |
| **N₂** | 14 | **3** | 0 | Diamagnetic | Haan (sabse mazboot!) |
| **O₂** | 16 | **2** | **2** (π*2p) | **Paramagnetic** | Haan |
| **F₂** | 18 | **1** | 0 | Diamagnetic | Haan |
| **Ne₂** | 20 | **0** | 0 | — | **Nahi!** |

### Example 3. Isoelectronic Heteronuclear Diatomics — CO (14 e⁻) aur NO (15 e⁻)

- **CO (6 + 8 = 14 e⁻, N₂ ke isoelectronic!):** N_b = 10, N_a = 4 → **BO = 3**, **Diamagnetic**!
- **NO (7 + 8 = 15 e⁻):** 15vaan electron π*2p mein jaata hai → N_b = 10, N_a = 5 → **BO = 2.5**, **Paramagnetic (1 unpaired e⁻)**! *(NO⁺ mein 14 e⁻ → BO = 3, diamagnetic!)*

**Jaanch:** N₂, CO aur NO⁺ teeno 14-electron isoelectronic species hain → teeno ka BO = 3 aur diamagnetic ✅.

1. **Galti 1: B₂, C₂ aur N₂ ke liye O₂ wala energy order (σ2p_z < π2p) laga dena.**
   *Sahi baat:* Agar B₂ (10 e⁻) mein σ2p_z neeche rakh doge to B₂ diamagnetic dikhega — jabki asli **B₂ paramagnetic (2 unpaired e⁻ in π2p_x¹, π2p_y¹)** hai! Hamesha ≤ 14 e⁻ ke liye **π2p < σ2p_z** lagao!

2. **Galti 2: Lewis structure O=O dekhkar O₂ ko diamagnetic likh dena.**
   Lewis structure aur VBT O₂ ke paramagnetism ko nahi samjha sakte — sirf **MOT** batati hai ki π*2p mein 2 unpaired electrons hain!

3. **Galti 3: Zyada Bond Order ka matlab "zyada bond length" samajh lena.**
   Bilkul ulta hota hai! **Bond Order jitna zyada → atoms utne kas kar paas aate hain → Bond Length utni CHHOTI!**

## Quick Recap

- **LCAO:** Atomic orbitals milkar **Bonding MO (kam energy, σ/π)** aur **Antibonding MO (zyada energy, σ*/π*)** banate hain.
- **Energy Order ≤ 14 e⁻ (B₂, C₂, N₂):** (π2p_x = π2p_y) < σ2p_z; **> 14 e⁻ (O₂, F₂, Ne₂):** σ2p_z < (π2p_x = π2p_y).
- **Bond Order = ½(N_b − N_a):** BO > 0 stable; BO = 0 does not exist (He₂, Ne₂).
- **O₂ (16 e⁻):** BO = 2, **paramagnetic** (2 unpaired e⁻ in π*2p).
- **O₂⁺ (2.5) > O₂ (2.0) > O₂⁻ (1.5) > O₂²⁻ (1.0)** in stability; **exact opposite** in bond length!

## Practice Questions

1. Write the full Molecular Orbital electronic configuration, calculate the **Bond Order**, and predict the **magnetic nature** (paramagnetic or diamagnetic) of:
   (a) **H₂** (2 e⁻)
   (b) **H₂⁺** (1 e⁻)
   (c) **Li₂** (6 e⁻)
   (d) **F₂** (18 e⁻) *(Toxic/corrosive gas: paper problem only.)*
2. Using MOT, prove mathematically why **Ne₂** (20 e⁻) does NOT exist under normal conditions.
3. Write the MO electronic configuration of **B₂** (10 e⁻) and **C₂** (12 e⁻) using the ≤ 14 e⁻ energy order. Predict their bond orders and magnetic behaviour. *(Bonus: What is unique about the double bond in C₂?)*
4. Explain with an MO configuration why **O₂** is **paramagnetic**, whereas its Lewis structure O=O suggests it should be diamagnetic.
5. Compare **N₂** (14 e⁻), **N₂⁺** (13 e⁻) and **N₂⁻** (15 e⁻) by calculating their **Bond Orders** and stating which one is the **most stable** and which two have **unpaired electrons**.
6. Between **N₂⁺** (BO = 2.5) and **N₂⁻** (BO = 2.5), both have the same bond order — which one is slightly **more stable** and why? *(Hint: check the number of antibonding electrons!)*
7. Arrange **O₂, O₂⁺, O₂⁻ and O₂²⁻** in **increasing order of O−O bond length**, and identify the **only diamagnetic** species in this group.
8. Why does the MO energy order switch from **(π2p_x = π2p_y) < σ2p_z** (up to N₂) to **σ2p_z < (π2p_x = π2p_y)** (from O₂ onwards)? Give a one-line conceptual reason.
9. Compare the **bond order and stability** of **He₂⁺** (3 e⁻) and **He₂** (4 e⁻). Which one can exist transiently in gas-discharge tubes?
10. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) An antibonding molecular orbital (σ*) has lower energy than its corresponding bonding molecular orbital (σ).
    (b) In the C₂ molecule, both bonds of the double bond are π bonds.
    (c) Removing one electron from O₂ to form O₂⁺ increases the bond order from 2.0 to 2.5.
    (d) A species with Bond Order = 0 is extremely stable.
11. State the **three essential conditions** for the effective Linear Combination of Atomic Orbitals (LCAO) to form molecular orbitals.
12. Compare the **bond order and magnetic nature** of **NO (15 e⁻)** and **NO⁺ (14 e⁻)**. Why is the N−O bond shorter in NO⁺ than in NO?

### Answers

1. (a) **H₂ (2 e⁻):** (σ1s)² → BO = ½(2 − 0) = **1**, **Diamagnetic**.
   (b) **H₂⁺ (1 e⁻):** (σ1s)¹ → BO = ½(1 − 0) = **0.5**, **Paramagnetic** (1 unpaired e⁻).
   (c) **Li₂ (6 e⁻):** (σ1s)² (σ*1s)² (σ2s)² → BO = ½(4 − 2) = **1**, **Diamagnetic**.
   (d) **F₂ (18 e⁻, Order 2):** (σ1s)² (σ*1s)² (σ2s)² (σ*2s)² (σ2p_z)² (π2p_x² = π2p_y²) (π*2p_x² = π*2p_y²) → N_b = 10, N_a = 8 → BO = ½(10 − 8) = **1**, **Diamagnetic**.
   Jaanch: H₂, Li₂ aur F₂ teeno mein BO = 1 aur 0 unpaired electrons hain ✅.

2. **Ne₂ (20 e⁻, Order 2):** (σ1s)² (σ*1s)² (σ2s)² (σ*2s)² (σ2p_z)² (π2p_x² = π2p_y²) (π*2p_x² = π*2p_y²) (σ*2p_z)².
   Yahan N_b = 10 aur N_a = 10 → **BO = ½(10 − 10) = 0**. Zero bond order ka matlab koi net attraction nahi — isliye **Ne₂ exist nahi karta**.
   Jaanch: 10 + 10 = 20 electrons, BO = 0 ✅.

3. - **B₂ (10 e⁻):** (σ1s)² (σ*1s)² (σ2s)² (σ*2s)² (π2p_x¹ = π2p_y¹) → N_b = 6, N_a = 4 → **BO = 1**, **Paramagnetic** (2 unpaired e⁻)!
   - **C₂ (12 e⁻):** (σ1s)² (σ*1s)² (σ2s)² (σ*2s)² (π2p_x² = π2p_y²) → N_b = 8, N_a = 4 → **BO = 2**, **Diamagnetic** (0 unpaired e⁻)!
   - *Khaas baat:* C₂ ke chaaron valence bonding electrons **π2p_x aur π2p_y** mein hain — isliye C₂ ka double bond **dono π bonds** se bana hai (koi net σ2p bond nahi)!

4. O₂ (16 e⁻) ka MO config hai: (σ1s)² (σ*1s)² (σ2s)² (σ*2s)² (σ2p_z)² (π2p_x² = π2p_y²) (π*2p_x¹ = π*2p_y¹). Hund's rule ke kaaran π*2p_x aur π*2p_y mein **2 unpaired electrons** rehte hain, jisse O₂ **paramagnetic** hota hai.

5. - **N₂ (14 e⁻):** N_b = 10, N_a = 4 → **BO = 3.0** (Diamagnetic — **sabse zyada stable!**).
   - **N₂⁺ (13 e⁻):** σ2p_z se 1 e⁻ gaya → N_b = 9, N_a = 4 → **BO = 2.5** (Paramagnetic, 1 unpaired e⁻).
   - **N₂⁻ (15 e⁻):** π*2p mein 1 e⁻ aaya → N_b = 10, N_a = 5 → **BO = 2.5** (Paramagnetic, 1 unpaired e⁻).
   Jaanch: N₂ (3.0) > N₂⁺ (2.5) = N₂⁻ (2.5) ✅.

6. **N₂⁺**, N₂⁻ se thoda **zyada stable** hota hai kyunki barabar Bond Order (2.5) hone ke baavajood **N₂⁺ mein kam antibonding electrons (N_a = 4)** hain, jabki **N₂⁻ mein ek extra antibonding electron (N_a = 5)** destabilising repulsion badhata hai!

7. Increasing bond length (highest BO to lowest BO): **O₂⁺ (2.5) < O₂ (2.0) < O₂⁻ (1.5) < O₂²⁻ (1.0)**.
   Is group mein sirf **O₂²⁻ (18 e⁻)** hi **diamagnetic** hai (saare π*2p electrons paired hain).

8. B₂, C₂ aur N₂ mein **2s aur 2p orbitals ke beech energy gap chhota** hota hai, jisse 2s–2p mixing hoti hai aur σ2p_z upar dhakel diya jaata hai; O₂ se aage nuclear charge badhne par 2s–2p gap bada ho jaata hai aur mixing ruk jaati hai.

9. **He₂⁺ (3 e⁻):** (σ1s)² (σ*1s)¹ → **BO = ½(2 − 1) = 0.5** (positive BO → discharge tube mein thodi der ke liye exist kar sakta hai!). **He₂ (4 e⁻):** BO = **0** (exist nahi karta).

10. (a) **False** ❌. Antibonding MO (σ*) ki energy bonding MO (σ) se **zyada** hoti hai.
    (b) **True** ✅. C₂ mein 4 valence bonding electrons π2p_x² aur π2p_y² mein hote hain.
    (c) **True** ✅. Antibonding π*2p se electron nikalne par N_a 6 se 5 ho jaata hai aur BO 2.0 se 2.5 badh jaata hai.
    (d) **False** ❌. BO = 0 ka matlab molecule **unstable hai aur exist nahi karta**.

11. Teen shartein: (1) Atomic orbitals ki **energy lagbhag barabar** ho, (2) Internuclear axis par **symmetry same** ho, (3) Dono orbitals ke beech **maximum overlap** ho.

12. **NO (15 e⁻):** BO = **2.5**, **Paramagnetic** (1 unpaired e⁻ in π*2p).
    **NO⁺ (14 e⁻, N₂ isoelectronic):** π*2p antibonding electron nikal gaya → BO = **3.0**, **Diamagnetic**!
    Zyada bond order (3.0 > 2.5) hone ke kaaran NO⁺ mein N−O bond chhota aur mazboot hota hai!
    Jaanch: NO (2.5) → NO⁺ (3.0) ✅.
