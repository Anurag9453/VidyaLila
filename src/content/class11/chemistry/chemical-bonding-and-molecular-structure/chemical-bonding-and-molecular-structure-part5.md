# Topic 5: Hydrogen Bonding (Intermolecular aur Intramolecular) aur Uske Anokhe Asar

Ab tak humne dekha ki ek hi molecule ke andar atoms **covalent ya ionic bond** se kaise judte hain. Par ab ek anokha sawaal socho: Oxygen aur Sulphur ek hi Group 16 ke bhai hain. **H₂S (molar mass 34 u)** lagbhag **H₂O (molar mass 18 u)** se dugna bhaari hai — phir bhi H₂S kamre ke taapmaan par ek **badbudaar gas (bp −60 °C)** hai, jabki halka-phulka **H₂O liquid (bp 100 °C)** hai! Agar ye jaadu na hota, to Dharti par saara paani bhaap bankar ud chuka hota aur jeevan hi na hota! Is jaadu ka naam hai — **Hydrogen Bonding**!

---

## Umang aur nimbu-paani par tairti barf

Garmi ki dopahar mein Umang ne glass mein thanda nimbu-paani banaya aur usmein **barf (ice) ke tukde** daale — saare barf ke tukde paani ke **upar tairne** lage! Par kal hi jab usne katori mein **pighle hue mom (melted wax)** mein thos mom ka tukda daala tha, to thos mom **neeche doob** gaya tha!

Umang ne hairaan hokar Mummy se poocha, "Aam taur par har cheez ka solid uske liquid se bhaari (dense) hota hai aur doob jaata hai — phir **barf paani par kyun tairti hai?** Aur itna halka H₂O gas kyun nahi hai?"

Mummy ne muskurate hue kaha, "Umang, iska raaz hai **Hydrogen Bonding**! Barf jamte waqt H₂O ke molecules H-bonds ki madad se ek **khula hua pinjra (open hexagonal cage)** bana lete hain jismein beech mein khaali jagah (empty space) bach jaati hai — isliye barf ka volume badh jaata hai aur density liquid paani se kam ho jaati hai!" Chalo, Hydrogen Bonding ki shartein, uske do prakar aur duniya par uske kamaal ke asar padhte hain.

## Part A: Hydrogen Bonding ki Do Pakki Shartein (FON Rule!)

Har Hydrogen wale molecule (jaise CH₄ ya H₂S) mein H-bond **NAHI** banta! H-bond banne ke liye **dono shartein** poori honi zaroori hain:
1. **High Electronegativity:** Hydrogen seedhe kisi behad electronegative atom se covalently juda ho — poori periodic table mein sirf teen aise atoms hain: **F, O, ya N** *(yaad rakho: **FON** — phone!)*.
2. **Small Atomic Size:** Us electronegative atom ka **size chhota** hona chahiye taaki uspe charge density bahut zyada ho!
   - *Khaas paheli:* **Chlorine (Cl)** ki electronegativity (3.0) lagbhag **Nitrogen (N = 3.0)** ke barabar hai — phir bhi **NH₃** mein mazboot H-bonding hoti hai, jabki **HCl** mein nahi! **Kyun?** Kyunki **Cl ka atomic size (Period 3) N (Period 2) se kaafi bada hai**, isliye Cl par negative charge phail jaata hai (kam charge density)!

**Strength of H-bond:** H-bond normal van der Waals force se bahut **mazboot** (10–40 kJ/mol) hota hai, par asli covalent bond (200–450 kJ/mol) se **kamzor** hota hai!
- Order of H-bond strength: **H−F ··· H  >  H−O ··· H  >  H−N ··· H** *(kyunki electronegativity F > O > N)*.

## Part B: Intermolecular vs Intramolecular H-Bonding

### 1. Intermolecular H-Bonding (Alag Molecules ke Beech — Boiling Point UPAR!)

*(Chetavani: HF ek behad corrosive aur zehreela acid hai — sirf padhne ke liye, lab mein kabhi use nahi karna!)*

```
  1. INTERMOLECULAR (HF chain)            2. ICE ka OPEN CAGE (har O par 4 bonds)
                                                H         H
   δ+  δ-     δ+  δ-     δ+  δ-                  \       /
   H - F ··· H - F ··· H - F                      O ··· H - O
         H-bond      H-bond                      /           \
                                                H             H
   (Alag molecules judte hain -->               ·             ·
    High bp, zyada viscosity!)                  (Beech mein KHAALI JAGAH -->
                                                 Ice ki density KAM --> tairti hai!)
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Intermolecular H-bonding: HF molecules lambe zig-zag chain mein jud jaate hain, aur barf (ice) mein har H₂O molecule chaar doosre H₂O se judkar ek khula pinjra banata hai jismein khaali jagah rehti hai.

### Example 1. Anomalous Boiling Points (H₂O vs H₂S aur HF vs HCl) aur Ice ka Tairna

- **H₂O (100 °C) banaam H₂S (−60 °C):** H₂O mein ek molecule up to **4 H-bonds** (2 apni H + 2 apne O ke lone pairs se) bana sakta hai → poora 3D network ban jaata hai, jise todne mein bahut zyada heat lagti hai (bp = **100 °C**). H₂S mein S ka size bada aur EN kam hai, isliye H-bonding nahi hoti (bp = **−60 °C**).
- **HF (19.5 °C) banaam HCl (−85 °C):** HF mein mazboot intermolecular H-bonding zig-zag chains banati hai (bp **+19.5 °C**, liquid near room temp!), jabki HCl mein H-bonding nahi hoti (bp **−85 °C**, gas).
  - *Ek aur twist:* HF ka single H-bond H₂O ke single H-bond se zyada mazboot hai, phir bhi **H₂O (100 °C) ka bp HF (19.5 °C) se zyada kyun hai?** Kyunki har H₂O **4 H-bonds** (3D network) banata hai, jabki HF sirf **2 H-bonds** (chain) bana sakta hai!
- **Ice floats & Maximum Density of Water at 4 °C:** Barf (0 °C) mein open hexagonal cage ke kaaran density sirf **0.917 g/cm³** hoti hai. Jab barf pighalkar paani banti hai aur 0 °C se **4 °C** tak garm hoti hai, to kuch H-bonds tootne se molecules khaali jagahon mein bharne lagte hain aur density badhkar **4 °C par maximum (1.00 g/cm³)** ho jaati hai! (4 °C ke upar normal thermal expansion se density phir ghatti hai.)

**Jaanch:** H₂O (100 °C) > HF (19.5 °C) > NH₃ (−33 °C) — teeno apne-apne group ke bhaari hydrides (H₂S −60 °C, HCl −85 °C, PH₃ −88 °C) se kahin zyada bp rakhte hain ✅.

### 2. Intramolecular H-Bonding (Ek Hi Molecule ke Andar — Boiling Point NEECHE!)

### Example 2. *o*-Nitrophenol banaam *p*-Nitrophenol (Class 11 ka Classic Sawaal!)

*(Chetavani: Nitrophenols toxic chemicals hain — ye antar sirf padhne aur teacher demonstration ke liye hai.)*

- ***o*-Nitrophenol (ortho):** Ismein −OH aur −NO₂ groups **bilkul bagal-bagal** hain, isliye **ek hi molecule ke andar** H-bond (6-membered ring — **intramolecular H-bonding**) ban jaata hai!
  - Ab ye molecule doosre *o*-nitrophenol molecules se nahi judta (akela rehta hai) → **kam boiling point** aur **steam-volatile** (steam distillation se aasani se alag ho jaata hai)!
- ***p*-Nitrophenol (para):** Ismein −OH aur −NO₂ groups **180° opposite** hain, isliye andar H-bond nahi ban sakta — we **padosi molecules** ke saath **intermolecular H-bonding** banate hain!
  - Molecules aapas mein chipak jaate hain → **zyada boiling point** aur **less steam-volatile**!

**Jaanch:** Intramolecular (*o*-nitrophenol) = low bp, steam-volatile; Intermolecular (*p*-nitrophenol) = high bp, less volatile ✅.

---

### Example 3. KHF₂ Kyun Banta Hai (Par KHCl₂ Nahi!) aur Jeevan mein H-Bonding ka Kamaal

1. **KHF₂ banaam KHCl₂:** HF mein F ke chhote size aur high EN (4.0) ke kaaran mazboot H-bond ban kar **bifluoride ion [F−H···F]⁻** ban jaata hai → **KHF₂** stable solid hai! Cl, Br aur I bade atoms hain (H-bond nahi banta), isliye **KHCl₂, KHBr₂ aur KHI₂ exist nahi karte!**
2. **Jeevan (Biology) mein H-Bonding:** Hamare sharir ke **DNA double helix** ki dono chains **A=T** (2 H-bonds) aur **G≡C** (3 H-bonds) ke zariye judi rehti hain! Proteins ka α-helix aakaar bhi H-bonds se hi tika rehta hai!

**Jaanch:** [F−H···F]⁻ sirf F ke saath banta hai (FON rule) ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: Har Hydrogen wale compound (CH₄, H₂S, PH₃) mein Hydrogen bonding maan lena.**
   *Sahi baat:* H-bonding sirf tab hoti hai jab H seedhe **F, O, ya N** (chhote, high-EN atoms) se juda ho!

2. **Galti 2: "Cl ki EN (3.0) N (3.0) ke barabar hai, isliye HCl mein bhi mazboot H-bonding hoti hai" likh dena.**
   Nahi! Cl ka **atomic size bahut bada** hai (3rd period), isliye charge density kam hoti hai aur HCl mein mazboot H-bonding nahi banti.

3. **Galti 3: Intermolecular aur Intramolecular H-bonding ka boiling point par asar ulta likh dena.**
   - **Inter**molecular (alag molecules ke beech) → molecules chipakte hain → **bp BADHTA hai** (*p*-nitrophenol, H₂O).
   - **Intra**molecular (apne hi andar) → padosiyon se rishta nahi banta → **bp GHATTA hai** (*o*-nitrophenol).

---

## Quick Recap

- **FON Rule:** Mazboot H-bonding ke liye H ka **F, O, ya N** (high EN + small size) se juda hona zaroori hai.
- **Intermolecular H-bonding** raises boiling point, viscosity and water solubility: **H₂O (100 °C) > H₂S (−60 °C)**, **HF (19.5 °C) > HCl (−85 °C)**, **NH₃ (−33 °C) > PH₃ (−88 °C)**.
- **Ice** has an open cage structure (4 H-bonds per H₂O) → lower density than liquid water → **ice floats**, and water has **max density at 4 °C**.
- **Intramolecular H-bonding** (*o*-nitrophenol) lowers boiling point and makes the compound **steam-volatile** compared to *p*-nitrophenol (intermolecular).

## Practice Questions

1. State the **two essential conditions** required for the formation of a strong **Hydrogen bond**. Which three elements in the periodic table satisfy both conditions?
2. Explain why **NH₃ (bp −33 °C)** has a much higher boiling point than **PH₃ (bp −88 °C)**, even though PH₃ has a higher molar mass (34 u vs 17 u).
3. **Ethanol (CH₃CH₂OH)** and **dimethyl ether (CH₃OCH₃)** have the exact same molecular formula (**C₂H₆O**, molar mass **46 u**). Yet ethanol boils at **78 °C** and mixes completely with water, while dimethyl ether is a gas at room temperature (bp **−24 °C**). Explain why!
4. Both **Nitrogen (N)** and **Chlorine (Cl)** have almost the same electronegativity (**3.0**). Why does **NH₃** show strong hydrogen bonding while **HCl** does not?
5. Even though an individual **H−F ··· H** hydrogen bond is stronger than an individual **H−O ··· H** hydrogen bond, why does **H₂O (bp 100 °C)** have a much higher boiling point than **HF (bp 19.5 °C)**?
6. Explain clearly why **ice floats on liquid water** at 0 °C, and why liquid water reaches its **maximum density at 4 °C**.
7. How can a mixture of ***o*-nitrophenol** and ***p*-nitrophenol** be separated by **steam distillation**? Explain using the two types of hydrogen bonding. *(Toxic chemicals: paper problem only.)*
8. Why does **ethanoic acid / acetic acid (CH₃COOH)** exist as a **dimer** (apparent molar mass = 120 u instead of 60 u) when dissolved in a non-polar solvent like benzene?
9. Why is **H₂O** a liquid at room temperature while **H₂S** is a gas, and why is **sugar (sucrose)** — a covalent compound — highly soluble in water?
10. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) A hydrogen bond is stronger than a normal covalent bond.
    (b) CH₄ shows strong intermolecular hydrogen bonding because it has four H atoms.
    (c) Intramolecular hydrogen bonding generally lowers the boiling point compared to intermolecular hydrogen bonding.
    (d) Each H₂O molecule in ice is tetrahedrally surrounded by four other H₂O molecules through hydrogen bonds.
11. Explain why **KHF₂** is a well-known stable compound, whereas **KHCl₂** and **KHBr₂** do **not** exist. *(HF is corrosive/toxic: paper problem only.)*
12. Give two examples of how **hydrogen bonding** plays an essential role in living organisms (biology).

### Answers

1. Do shartein: (1) Hydrogen kisi **behad electronegative** atom se covalently juda ho, aur (2) us electronegative atom ka **atomic size chhota** ho. Sirf teen elements — **Fluorine (F), Oxygen (O) aur Nitrogen (N)** — dono shartein poori karte hain.

2. **NH₃** mein H chhote aur electronegative N se juda hai, isliye molecules ke beech **intermolecular H-bonding** hoti hai jise todne mein zyada energy lagti hai (bp −33 °C). **PH₃** mein Phosphorus ka size bada aur EN kam (2.1) hai, isliye H-bonding nahi hoti (bp −88 °C).

3. **Ethanol (CH₃CH₂−O−H)** mein H seedhe **O** se juda hai, isliye ethanol ke molecules aapas mein aur paani ke saath mazboot **intermolecular H-bonds** banate hain → high bp (78 °C) aur water miscible! **Dimethyl ether (CH₃−O−CH₃)** mein koi H seedhe O se nahi juda (saare H Carbon par hain), isliye aapas mein H-bonding nahi hoti → bahut kam bp (−24 °C).
   Jaanch: Same molar mass 46 u, par H-bonding se bp mein 102 °C ka bada antar ✅.

4. Electronegativity barabar (3.0) hone ke baavajood **Cl (Period 3) ka atomic size N (Period 2) se kaafi bada** hai. Bade size par negative charge phail jaata hai (kam charge density), isliye Cl mazboot H-bond nahi bana paata.

5. Har **H₂O** molecule apne 2 H atoms aur O ke 2 lone pairs se **4 H-bonds** (3D network) bana sakta hai, jabki **HF** mein sirf 1 H hone ke kaaran har molecule aam taur par **2 H-bonds** (chain) banata hai. Zyada H-bonds todne ke liye H₂O ko zyada energy chahiye (100 °C > 19.5 °C).

6. Barf (0 °C) mein H-bonds ki wajah se ek **khula hexagonal cage** banta hai jismein kaafi khaali jagah hoti hai → density (0.917 g/cm³) paani (1.00 g/cm³) se kam ho jaati hai, isliye barf tairti hai! 0 °C se 4 °C tak garm karne par kuch H-bonds toot-te hain aur molecules khaali jagahon mein samate hain, jisse **4 °C par density maximum** ho jaati hai.

7. ***o*-Nitrophenol** mein **intramolecular H-bonding** hoti hai → molecules aapas mein nahi chipakte → low bp aur **steam-volatile** (steam ke saath pehle bahar aa jaata hai!). ***p*-Nitrophenol** mein **intermolecular H-bonding** hoti hai → high bp aur **less volatile** (flask mein peeche reh jaata hai!).

8. Non-polar benzene mein paani jaisa koi polar partner nahi hota, isliye **do CH₃COOH molecules** aapas mein **do intermolecular H-bonds** banakar ek stable **cyclic dimer (8-membered ring)** bana lete hain — isliye apparent molar mass 2 × 60 = **120 u** milta hai!
   Jaanch: 2 × 60 u = 120 u ✅.

9. H₂O mein mazboot intermolecular H-bonding hai par H₂S mein nahi (isliye H₂O liquid, H₂S gas). Sugar (sucrose) mein kai **−OH groups** hote hain jo paani ke molecules ke saath mazboot **intermolecular H-bonds** bana lete hain — isliye covalent hone ke baavajood sugar paani mein aasani se ghul jaati hai!

10. (a) **False** ❌. Hydrogen bond (10–40 kJ/mol) normal covalent bond (200–450 kJ/mol) se **kamzor** hota hai.
    (b) **False** ❌. CH₄ mein H Carbon se juda hai (F, O ya N se nahi), isliye CH₄ mein **H-bonding nahi hoti**.
    (c) **True** ✅. Intramolecular H-bonding molecules ko aapas mein chipakne se rokti hai.
    (d) **True** ✅. Har H₂O chaar padosi H₂O se tetrahedrally H-bonded hota hai.

11. Fluorine (chhota size + EN 4.0) mazboot H-bond banakar stable **[F−H···F]⁻** ion bana leta hai, jisse **KHF₂** banta hai. Cl aur Br ke bade size ke kaaran [Cl−H···Cl]⁻ nahi banta, isliye KHCl₂ aur KHBr₂ exist nahi karte!

12. (1) **DNA double helix** ke dono strands base pairs (A=T, G≡C) ke beech H-bonds se jude rehte hain; (2) **Proteins** ka α-helix aur β-sheet 3D structure H-bonds se stable rehta hai!
