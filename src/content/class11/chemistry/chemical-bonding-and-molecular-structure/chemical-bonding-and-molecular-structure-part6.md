# Topic 6: Sab Kuch Mila Kar: Mixed Bonding Puzzles aur Poora Chapter Wrap-Up

Asli Chemistry mein koi molecule kabhi ye likhkar nahi aata ki "mujhpar sirf VSEPR lagao" ya "mujhpar sirf MOT lagao"! Ek hi molecule — jaise **SO₂** ya **CH₃COOH** — tumse ek saath uska **Lewis/Resonance structure, Formal Charge, Hybridisation, 3D VSEPR shape, Dipole Moment aur Hydrogen Bonding** sab pooch leta hai! Is aakhiri topic mein hum poore chapter ke paanchon tools ko ek saath jodkar bade puzzles hal karenge, aur ant mein poore chapter ka **Quick Wrap-Up Table** banayenge.

## Mixed Worked Examples

### Mixed Example 1. Sulphur Dioxide (SO₂) ka Poora X-Ray (Resonance + FC + Hybridisation + VSEPR + Dipole!)

*(Chetavani: SO₂ ek teekhi aur zehreeli gas hai — sirf padhne aur paper calculation ke liye!)*

**Sawaal:** SO₂ ke liye (a) kul valence electrons aur resonance structures (formal charge ke saath), (b) S ki hybridisation, (c) VSEPR shape & bond angle, aur (d) net dipole moment (polar ya non-polar) batao.

**Hal:**
1. **Valence e⁻ & Resonance (Topic 1):** S (6) + 2 × O (6) = **18 valence e⁻**.
   - Do equivalent octet resonance structures banti hain: **O=S−O** ↔ **O−S=O** (beech mein S par 1 lone pair!).
   - Formal charges (octet form mein): double-bonded O = **0**, central S = 6 − 2 − ½(6) = **+1**, single-bonded O = 6 − 6 − ½(2) = **−1**.
   - Dono S−O bonds **resonance hybrid** ke kaaran bilkul **barabar length (143 pm)** ke hote hain (bond order = 1.5)!
2. **Hybridisation (Topic 3):** Central S par **2 σ bonds + 1 lone pair** → Steric Number H = **3** → **sp² hybridised**!
3. **VSEPR Shape & Angle (Topic 2):** 3 electron pairs (trigonal planar electron geometry) mein se 1 lone pair hai → asli shape **Bent (V-shaped)**, aur lp–bp repulsion se angle 120° se thoda ghatkar **~119°**!
4. **Dipole Moment (Topic 2):** Bent shape hone ke kaaran dono S=O bond dipoles cancel nahi hote → **μ ≠ 0 (Polar molecule, 1.62 D)**!

**Jaanch:** Formal charges ka jod = 0 + (+1) + (−1) = 0 (neutral SO₂); H = 2 σ + 1 lp = 3 (sp², bent, polar) ✅.

### Mixed Example 2. MOT ka Master Twist — N₂⁺ (BO 2.5) banaam O₂⁺ (BO 2.5)!

**Sawaal:** N₂ se 1 electron nikaalne par **N₂⁺** banta hai aur O₂ se 1 electron nikaalne par **O₂⁺** banta hai. Dono ka Bond Order **2.5** ho jaata hai — phir bhi **N₂ → N₂⁺** mein bond **KAMZOR** hota hai, jabki **O₂ → O₂⁺** mein bond **MAZBOOT** hota hai! Kyun?

**Hal:**
1. **N₂ (14 e⁻, Order 1) → N₂⁺ (13 e⁻):**
   - N₂ ka aakhiri electron **bonding orbital (σ2p_z)** mein tha → BO = ½(10 − 4) = **3.0**.
   - 1 electron nikaalne par **bonding electron** kam hua → N_b = 9, N_a = 4 → **BO = ½(9 − 4) = 2.5**!
   - BO **3.0 se ghatkar 2.5** hua → bond **kamzor aur lamba** hua!
2. **O₂ (16 e⁻, Order 2) → O₂⁺ (15 e⁻):**
   - O₂ ke aakhiri electrons **antibonding orbital (π*2p)** mein the → BO = ½(10 − 6) = **2.0**.
   - 1 electron nikaalne par **antibonding electron** kam hua → N_b = 10, N_a = 5 → **BO = ½(10 − 5) = 2.5**!
   - BO **2.0 se badhkar 2.5** hua → bond **mazboot aur chhota** hua!
3. **Magnetism:** Dono (N₂⁺ aur O₂⁺) mein **1 unpaired electron** hai → dono **paramagnetic** hain!

**Jaanch:** Bonding se e⁻ nikla → BO ghatta (3.0 → 2.5); Antibonding se e⁻ nikla → BO badhta (2.0 → 2.5) ✅.

### Mixed Example 3. Ethanoic Acid (CH₃COOH) — Hybridisation + σ/π Count + H-Bonded Dimer!

**Sawaal:** Vinegar (sirka) mein paaye jaane wale **ethanoic acid (CH₃−C(=O)−O−H)** mein: (a) dono Carbon atoms ki hybridisation batao, (b) kul σ aur π bonds gino, aur (c) batao ye benzene mein dimer kyun banata hai.

**Hal:**
1. **Hybridisation (Topic 3):**
   - **C1 (CH₃ wala):** 3 C−H + 1 C−C = **4 σ bonds, 0 lp** → **sp³** (tetrahedral, ~109.5°).
   - **C2 (−COOH wala):** 1 C−C + 1 C−O + 1 C=O (σ) = **3 σ bonds, 0 lp** (+ 1 π bond) → **sp²** (trigonal planar, ~120°).
2. **σ aur π Bonds Count (Topic 3):**
   - Single bonds (sab σ): 3 (C−H) + 1 (C−C) + 1 (C−O) + 1 (O−H) = **6 σ**.
   - Double bond (C=O): **1 σ + 1 π**.
   - **Total = 7 σ bonds + 1 π bond!** *(Shortcut check: open-chain molecule mein 8 atoms hain → 8 − 1 = 7 σ bonds!)*
3. **Hydrogen Bonding (Topic 5):** −COOH group mein H seedhe **O** se juda hai (FON rule!), aur bagal mein C=O ka Oxygen bhi hai. Non-polar benzene mein do CH₃COOH molecules aapas mein **do intermolecular H-bonds** banakar ek **cyclic dimer** bana lete hain!

**Jaanch:** 8 atoms open chain → 7 σ bonds + 1 π bond; C1 = sp³, C2 = sp² ✅.

### Mixed Example 4. Resonance Bond Order + % Ionic Character + MOT (NO vs NO⁺)

**Sawaal:**
(a) **CO₃²⁻** aur **ClO₄⁻** mein kiska X−O resonance bond order zyada hai?
(b) **NO** aur **NO⁺** mein kaun paramagnetic hai aur kiska bond zyada mazboot hai?
(c) **H₂O (104.5°)** aur **H₂S (92.1°)** mein H₂O ka bond angle aur boiling point dono zyada kyun hain?

**Hal:**
- (a) **CO₃²⁻** = 4 ÷ 3 = **1.33**; **ClO₄⁻** = 7 ÷ 4 = **1.75** → **ClO₄⁻** ka bond order zyada!
- (b) **NO (15 e⁻):** BO = 2.5, **paramagnetic** (1 unpaired e⁻ in π*2p); **NO⁺ (14 e⁻):** BO = **3.0**, **diamagnetic** (zyada mazboot!).
- (c) **Bond angle:** O ki EN (3.5) > S (2.5) → H₂O mein bp O ke paas rehkar zyada bp–bp repulsion dete hain (104.5° > 92.1°). **Boiling point:** H₂O mein mazboot intermolecular **H-bonding** hai, H₂S mein nahi!

**Jaanch:** ClO₄⁻ (1.75 > 1.33) aur NO⁺ (BO 3.0 > 2.5) ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (mixed questions mein bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: Polar bonds dekhkar seedhe poore molecule ko polar (μ ≠ 0) bol dena.**
   *Sahi baat:* Hamesha VSEPR shape aur **symmetry** check karo! **BF₃, CCl₄, SF₆ aur XeF₄** mein har bond polar hai, par poori symmetry ke kaaran saare vectors cancel hokar **net μ = 0 (non-polar)** ho jaate hain!

2. **Galti 2: Electron nikaalne par hamesha Bond Order ghatne ka andaza lagana.**
   *Sahi baat:* Ye is baat par nirbhar karta hai ki electron **bonding MO** se nikla (jaise N₂ → N₂⁺, BO ghatta hai) ya **antibonding MO** se nikla (jaise O₂ → O₂⁺, BO **badhta** hai)!

## Practice Questions (Sab Kuch Mila Kar — 12 Fresh Questions)

1. For the **nitrite ion (NO₂⁻)**:
   (a) Draw its two resonance structures and find the **formal charge** on each atom in one structure.
   (b) Predict the **hybridisation** of the central N atom and the **molecular shape** of NO₂⁻.
2. For **XeF₂**:
   (a) Determine the **hybridisation** of Xe.
   (b) State its **electron-pair geometry** and **actual molecular shape**, explaining where the 3 lone pairs sit.
   (c) Predict whether its **net dipole moment (μ)** is zero or non-zero.
3. Compare **C₂H₆, C₂H₄ and C₂H₂** in a compact summary on: (a) Carbon hybridisation, (b) total σ and π bonds, and (c) order of C−C bond length.
4. Both **O₂** (16 e⁻) and **O₂²⁻** (18 e⁻) contain only oxygen atoms, yet **O₂ is paramagnetic** while **O₂²⁻ is diamagnetic**. Prove this using their MOT configurations and compare their bond orders.
5. **Ammonia (NH₃)** shows two famous chemistry surprises: (a) it has a much **larger dipole moment** than NF₃ (1.47 D vs 0.23 D), and (b) it has a much **higher boiling point** than PH₃ (−33 °C vs −88 °C). Explain **both** facts concisely using two different bonding concepts from this chapter.
6. Using **Fajan's Rules**, explain why **BeCl₂** is predominantly **covalent** (and forms a linear sp-hybridised monomer in vapour phase), whereas **BaCl₂** is predominantly **ionic**. *(BeCl₂ is toxic: paper problem only.)*
7. Compare the **formal charges** on the central atom in **O₃** (ozone) and on both atoms in **CO** (carbon monoxide, :C≡O:). Why does CO have a very small dipole moment despite O being much more electronegative than C?
8. **Benzene (C₆H₆)** is a flat hexagonal ring of 6 carbon atoms with alternating single and double bonds (each C also holds 1 H atom). Find:
   (a) the **hybridisation** of every carbon atom, and
   (b) the exact total number of **σ bonds** and **π bonds** in one benzene molecule.
9. Arrange the following four species in **increasing order of N−O / O−O or bond strength**:
   (a) **O₂²⁻, O₂⁻, O₂, O₂⁺** (increasing O−O bond strength)
   (b) **H₂S, H₂O, HF** (increasing boiling point)
10. **SF₄** and **XeF₄** both have four fluorine atoms around a central atom. Compare them on: (a) number of lone pairs on the central atom, (b) hybridisation, (c) molecular shape, and (d) net polarity (polar or non-polar).
11. Why does **B₂ (10 e⁻)** turn out to be **paramagnetic**, whereas **C₂ (12 e⁻)** and **N₂ (14 e⁻)** are **diamagnetic**? Show the last filled MO for each using the ≤ 14 e⁻ energy order.
12. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) In PCl₅, all five P−Cl bonds have the exact same bond length.
    (b) Removing one electron from N₂ decreases its bond order, while removing one electron from O₂ increases its bond order.
    (c) *o*-Nitrophenol has a higher boiling point than *p*-nitrophenol due to intramolecular H-bonding.
    (d) Every molecule with polar covalent bonds must have a non-zero net dipole moment.

### Answers

1. (a) **NO₂⁻ (5 + 12 + 1 = 18 valence e⁻):** O=N−O ↔ O−N=O (N par 1 lp). Formal charges: double-bonded O = **0**, N = 5 − 2 − ½(6) = **0**, single-bonded O = 6 − 6 − ½(2) = **−1** (sum = −1).
   (b) N par 2 σ + 1 lp → H = 3 → **sp²**, shape = **Bent (~115°)**.
   Jaanch: 0 + 0 + (−1) = −1 (ion charge match) ✅.

2. (a) Xe (8 valence e⁻) mein 2 Xe−F σ bonds + 3 lone pairs → H = 5 → **sp³d**.
   (b) Electron geometry = **Trigonal bipyramidal**; teeno lone pairs **equatorial** positions (120°) par baithte hain aur dono F axial (180°) par → molecular shape = **Linear (180°)**.
   (c) **μ = 0 (non-polar)** kyunki dono opposite Xe−F dipoles 180° par ek-doosre ko cancel kar dete hain.

3. - **C₂H₆:** **sp³**, **7 σ + 0 π**, C−C = 154 pm (sabse lamba).
   - **C₂H₄:** **sp²**, **5 σ + 1 π**, C=C = 134 pm.
   - **C₂H₂:** **sp**, **3 σ + 2 π**, C≡C = 120 pm (sabse chhota).
   Order of C−C bond length: **C₂H₆ > C₂H₄ > C₂H₂**.

4. - **O₂ (16 e⁻):** ... (π*2p_x¹ = π*2p_y¹) → **2 unpaired e⁻** → **Paramagnetic**, **BO = 2.0**.
   - **O₂²⁻ (18 e⁻):** ... (π*2p_x² = π*2p_y²) → **0 unpaired e⁻** → **Diamagnetic**, **BO = 1.0**.
   Jaanch: BO(O₂) = 2.0 > BO(O₂²⁻) = 1.0 ✅.

5. (a) **Dipole moment (VSEPR/Vectors):** NH₃ mein N−H dipoles aur lone-pair dipole ek hi disha mein jud jaate hain (1.47 D), jabki NF₃ mein N−F dipoles lone-pair dipole ka virodh karte hain (0.23 D).
   (b) **Boiling point (H-Bonding):** NH₃ mein N chhota aur high-EN hai → mazboot **intermolecular H-bonding** (bp −33 °C); PH₃ mein P bada aur kam EN hai → no H-bonding (bp −88 °C).

6. Fajan's rules ke mutabik **Be²⁺** ka size **Ba²⁺** se bahut chhota hai → Be²⁺ ki **polarising power bahut zyada** hoti hai, jo Cl⁻ ke electron cloud ko kheenchkar **BeCl₂ ko covalent** bana deti hai (vapour mein linear, sp). **Ba²⁺** bada cation hai → kam polarising power → **BaCl₂ ionic** rehta hai.

7. - **O₃** (O=O−O): Central O par FC = 6 − 2 − ½(6) = **+1**.
   - **CO** (:C≡O:): C par FC = 4 − 2 − ½(6) = **−1**; O par FC = 6 − 2 − ½(6) = **+1**.
   - CO mein Oxygen zyada electronegative hai (electron apni taraf khinchta hai), par formal charge C(−1) aur O(+1) iska ulta asar daalta hai — dono aapas mein kaafi had tak cancel hokar **bahut chhota dipole moment (0.11 D)** dete hain!
   Jaanch: CO mein (−1) + (+1) = 0 ✅.

8. (a) Benzene mein har Carbon atom **3 σ bonds (2 C−C + 1 C−H) + 1 π bond** banata hai → har C **sp² hybridised** hai!
   (b) Ring mein 6 C−C σ + 6 C−H σ = **12 σ bonds**, aur 3 alternating double bonds se **3 π bonds**!
   Jaanch: Total bonds in C₆H₆ = **12 σ + 3 π** ✅.

9. (a) Increasing O−O bond strength (lowest to highest BO): **O₂²⁻ (1.0) < O₂⁻ (1.5) < O₂ (2.0) < O₂⁺ (2.5)**.
   (b) Increasing boiling point: **H₂S (−60 °C) < HF (19.5 °C) < H₂O (100 °C)**.

10. - **SF₄:** (a) **1 lp**, (b) **sp³d** (4 σ + 1 lp = 5), (c) **See-saw**, (d) **Polar (μ ≠ 0)**.
    - **XeF₄:** (a) **2 lp**, (b) **sp³d²** (4 σ + 2 lp = 6), (c) **Square planar**, (d) **Non-polar (μ = 0, symmetrical!)**.

11. Order 1 (≤ 14 e⁻) mein **(π2p_x = π2p_y) < σ2p_z**:
    - **B₂ (10 e⁻):** ... (π2p_x¹ = π2p_y¹) → **2 unpaired e⁻ → Paramagnetic** (BO = 1).
    - **C₂ (12 e⁻):** ... (π2p_x² = π2p_y²) → **0 unpaired e⁻ → Diamagnetic** (BO = 2).
    - **N₂ (14 e⁻):** ... (π2p_x² = π2p_y²) (σ2p_z)² → **0 unpaired e⁻ → Diamagnetic** (BO = 3).

12. (a) **False** ❌. PCl₅ mein 2 axial bonds (**219 pm**) 3 equatorial bonds (**204 pm**) se lambe aur kamzor hote hain.
    (b) **True** ✅. N₂ mein e⁻ bonding σ2p_z se nikalta hai (3.0 → 2.5); O₂ mein antibonding π*2p se nikalta hai (2.0 → 2.5).
    (c) **False** ❌. *o*-Nitrophenol mein intramolecular H-bonding hone se uska boiling point *p*-nitrophenol se **KAM** hota hai.
    (d) **False** ❌. Symmetrical molecules jaise CO₂, BF₃, CCl₄, SF₆ aur XeF₄ mein polar bonds hone ke baavajood dipoles cancel hokar **μ = 0** hota hai.

## Chapter Ka Saar: Chemical Bonding and Molecular Structure

| Topic | Mukhya Concept | Golden Formula / Niyam | Star Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Lewis, FC & Resonance** | Lattice enthalpy, Formal charge, Resonance hybrid, Octet limitations | **FC = V − L − ½B**; real molecule = static hybrid | O₃ (128 pm), CO₃²⁻, NO, SF₆ |
| **2. VSEPR, Dipole & Fajan** | 3D shapes, Bond parameters, Vector dipole moment, Ionic → covalent character | **lp–lp > lp–bp > bp–bp**; **μ = q × d**; small high-charge cation + large anion | NH₃ (107°, 1.47 D) vs NF₃ (0.23 D); SF₄ see-saw; XeF₄ sq planar |
| **3. VBT & Hybridisation** | σ (head-on) vs π (sidewise), Steric number hybridisation, Axial vs Equatorial | **H = σ bonds + lp** (2→sp, 3→sp², 4→sp³, 5→sp³d, 6→sp³d²) | C₂H₄ (5σ+1π), C₂H₂ (3σ+2π), PCl₅ (219 vs 204 pm) |
| **4. MOT & Magnetism** | LCAO bonding/antibonding MOs, 2 energy orders (≤14 vs >14 e⁻), Magnetism | **BO = ½(N_b − N_a)**; higher BO = shorter & stronger bond | O₂ paramagnetic (BO 2); O₂⁺ (2.5) > O₂ (2) > O₂⁻ (1.5) > O₂²⁻ (1); He₂ = 0 |
| **5. Hydrogen Bonding** | Intermolecular (raises bp) vs Intramolecular (lowers bp) H-bonding | **FON Rule** (H on F, O, N — high EN + small size) | H₂O (100 °C) > H₂S (−60 °C); Ice floats (4 °C max density); *o*- vs *p*-nitrophenol |
