# Topic 1: Kossel-Lewis Approach, Lattice Enthalpy, Formal Charge, Resonance aur Octet Rule Ki Seemayein

Class 9 ([chemical-bonding.md](../../../class9/chemistry/chemical-bonding/chemical-bonding.md) se [chemical-bonding-part3.md](../../../class9/chemistry/chemical-bonding/chemical-bonding-part3.md)) aur Class 10 ([chemical-bonding-ii-part3.md](../../../class10/chemistry/chemical-bonding-ii/chemical-bonding-ii-part3.md)) mein tumne octet rule, ionic bond, covalent bond aur coordinate bond ki buniyaad seekhi thi. Class 11 mein hum teen bade naye sawaalon ka jawab dete hain: (1) ionic crystal (jaise NaCl) itna stable kyun hota hai (**Lattice Enthalpy**), (2) jab ek hi polyatomic ion ke kai Lewis structures ban sakein to sabse achha structure kaise chunein (**Formal Charge**), aur (3) jab ek akela Lewis structure molecule ki barabar bond lengths ko samjha na paaye (jaise Ozone O₃ aur Carbonate CO₃²⁻), to **Resonance Hybrid** kaise kaam karta hai!

## Malhar aur biology corner par khachchar (mule) ka poster

Malhar apne school ke biology corner mein ek chart dekh raha tha: ek taraf ghoda (horse), doosri taraf gadha (donkey), aur beech mein **khachchar (mule)**. Malhar ne apni teacher ma'am se haskar poochha, "Ma'am, kya khachchar aadhe samay ghoda aur aadhe samay gadha bana rehta hai?"

Ma'am muskurayin: "Bilkul nahi Malhar! Khachchar ek hi **asli, sthir (blended) jaanwar** hai jismein dono ke gun mile hue hain — wo kabhi ghode se gadhe mein flip nahi hota! Chemistry mein **Ozone (O₃)** aur **Carbonate (CO₃²⁻)** bhi bilkul aise hi hain: kaagaz par hum unke do ya teen alag Lewis structures banate hain, par asli molecule un sabka ek **sthir mila-jula roop (Resonance Hybrid)** hota hai!" Chalo, Lattice Enthalpy, Formal Charge, Resonance aur Octet Rule ki seemaon ko samajhte hain.

## Part A: Ionic Bond ki Asli Taakat — Lattice Enthalpy

Na(g) se Na⁺(g) banane mein energy lagti hai (ΔᵢH = +496 kJ/mol), aur Cl(g) se Cl⁻(g) banne mein energy nikalti hai (ΔₑgH = −349 kJ/mol). Dekho: +496 − 349 = **+147 kJ/mol** — yaani sirf gaseous ions banana to ghaate ka sauda hai! Phir NaCl itna mazboot aur stable kyun banta hai?

**Jawab hai Lattice Enthalpy!** Jab arbon-kharbon gaseous Na⁺(g) aur Cl⁻(g) ions aapas mein khinch kar 3D crystal lattice (NaCl solid) banate hain, to **788 kJ/mol** ki bhari energy bahar nikalti hai — jo +147 kJ/mol ke kharche ko aaraam se dhak kar poore crystal ko behad stable bana deti hai!
- **Lattice enthalpy kab zyada hoti hai?** (1) Ions par **charge zyada** ho (Mg²⁺O²⁻ > Na⁺Cl⁻), aur (2) Ions ka **size chhota** ho (LiF > NaCl).

## Part B: Formal Charge (FC) aur Polyatomic Ions ke Lewis Structures

Kisi bhi Lewis structure mein har atom ka **Formal Charge** is chhote formula se nikalta hai:

**FC = V − L − (B ÷ 2)**

*(Jahan V = free neutral atom ke valence electrons; L = us atom par bache lone-pair (non-bonding) electrons; B = us atom ke saath jude bonds ke kul shared electrons.)*

- **Check (Jaanch):** Sabhi atoms ke formal charges ka jod neutral molecule mein **0** aur polyatomic ion mein uske **kul ion charge** ke barabar aana chahiye!
- **Best Lewis structure kaun-sa?** Jismein formal charges **0 ke sabse paas** hon, aur agar negative FC aaye to wo **zyada electronegative atom** par baitha ho!

### Example 1. Ozone (O₃) ke Teeno Oxygen Atoms par Formal Charge *(O₃ tez oxidiser hai: sirf padhne ke liye)*

Ozone (O₃) mein kul valence electrons = 3 × 6 = **18 e⁻**. Ek Lewis structure aisa dikhta hai: O(1) = O(2) − O(3), jahan O(1) par 2 lone pairs (4 e⁻), central O(2) par 1 lone pair (2 e⁻), aur O(3) par 3 lone pairs (6 e⁻) hain.

**Hal:** Har O atom ke liye V = 6:
- **O(1) (double bonded):** L = 4, B = 4 → FC = 6 − 4 − (4 ÷ 2) = **0**.
- **O(2) (central):** L = 2, B = 6 → FC = 6 − 2 − (6 ÷ 2) = **+1**.
- **O(3) (single bonded):** L = 6, B = 2 → FC = 6 − 6 − (2 ÷ 2) = **−1**.

**Jaanch:** Kul FC = 0 + (+1) + (−1) = 0 (O₃ neutral hai) ✅; kul electrons = 4 + 2 + 6 (lone) + 6 (3 bonds) = 18 e⁻ ✅.

## Part C: Resonance aur Resonance Hybrid

Example 1 ke Lewis structure ke mutabik O₃ mein ek **chhota O=O double bond (121 pm)** aur ek **lamba O−O single bond (148 pm)** hona chahiye tha. Par prayog (experiment) mein dono O−O bonds **bilkul barabar (128 pm)** nikle — 121 aur 148 ke theek beech!

Iska matlab akela Lewis structure poori sachai nahi dikha paata. Hum do **canonical (resonating) structures** banate hain aur unke beech **↔** teer lagate hain:

```
   Canonical Structure I            Canonical Structure II         Asli Resonance Hybrid

          (+)                              (+)                            (+)
          :O:                              :O:                             O
         // \                             / \\                           /   \
       :O:   :O:⁻        <------>       ⁻:O:  :O:           ===>       O - - - O
       (121) (148)                      (148) (121)                  (Dono 128 pm barabar!)
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Ozone (O₃) ke do canonical structures aur unka asli Resonance Hybrid, jismein dono O−O bonds bilkul barabar (128 pm) hain aur Bond Order = 3 ÷ 2 = 1.5 hai.

### Example 2. Carbonate Ion (CO₃²⁻) ka Formal Charge aur Resonance Bond Order

**Sawaal:** CO₃²⁻ (kul valence e⁻ = 4 + 18 + 2 = 24 e⁻) mein ek C=O aur do C−O bonds wale canonical structure par har atom ka Formal Charge nikaalo, aur Resonance Hybrid mein C−O bond order batao.

**Hal:**
- **C (central):** V = 4, L = 0, B = 8 → FC = 4 − 0 − 4 = **0**.
- **Double-bonded O:** V = 6, L = 4, B = 4 → FC = 6 − 4 − 2 = **0**.
- **Har Single-bonded O (dono):** V = 6, L = 6, B = 2 → FC = 6 − 6 − 1 = **−1**.
- Kyunki double bond teeno oxygens par barabar ghoom kar **3 canonical structures** banata hai, isliye asli hybrid mein teeno C−O bonds bilkul barabar hain:
  **Bond Order = (Kul bonds ÷ Resonance structures) = 4 ÷ 3 = 1.33**.

**Jaanch:** Kul FC = 0 + 0 + (−1) + (−1) = −2 (CO₃²⁻ ka charge!) ✅.

### Ionic Bond Banne ki 3 Shartein aur Resonance Bond Order Formula
1. **Ionic Bond ki 3 Anukul Shartein:** (i) Metal ki **kam Ionisation Enthalpy (ΔᵢH)**, (ii) Non-metal ki **zyada negative Electron Gain Enthalpy (ΔₑgH)**, aur (iii) Crystal ki **bahut zyada Lattice Enthalpy (U ∝ q₁q₂ ÷ (r₊ + r₋))**.
2. **Resonance Hybrid mein Average Bond Order:** **BO = (Kisi ek canonical structure mein kul bonds) ÷ (Resonance mein hissa lene wale bond positions)**.
   - Example 3: **PO₄³⁻** (1 P=O + 3 P−O → 5 bonds ÷ 4 positions = **1.25**); **ClO₄⁻** (3 Cl=O + 1 Cl−O → 7 bonds ÷ 4 positions = **1.75**); **SO₄²⁻** (6 ÷ 4 = **1.50**).

**Jaanch:** Bond order badhne par (PO₄³⁻ 1.25 < SO₄²⁻ 1.50 < ClO₄⁻ 1.75) X−O bond strength badhti hai ✅.

## Part D: Octet Rule ki Chaar Badi Seemayein

1. **Incomplete Octet (8 se kam electrons):** BeCl₂ (Be par 4 e⁻), BF₃ / BCl₃ (B par 6 e⁻), LiCl (Li par 2 e⁻).
2. **Odd-Electron Molecules:** NO (11 valence e⁻) aur NO₂ (17 valence e⁻) — inmein sabhi atoms ka octet poora nahi ho sakta *(dono toxic gases: sirf padhne ke liye)*.
3. **Expanded Octet (8 se zyada electrons — Period 3 aur neeche):** PCl₅ (P par 10 e⁻), SF₆ (S par 12 e⁻), H₂SO₄ (S par 12 e⁻) — 3d orbitals ke upyog se.
4. **Noble-Gas Compounds:** Octet rule ne maana tha ki noble gases bilkul react nahi kartin, par Xenon aur Krypton **XeF₂, XeF₄, XeOF₂, KrF₂** jaise compounds banate hain! Saath hi Octet rule molecules ke **3D shapes** aur **relative stability** nahi bata paata.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: Ye soch lena ki Resonance mein molecule I se II mein baar-baar palat-ta (oscillate karta) rehta hai.**
   *Sahi baat:* Malhar ke khachchar ki tarah, canonical structures sirf hamare kaagaz ki kalpana hain; asli molecule hamesha **ek hi sthir Resonance Hybrid** ke roop mein rehta hai (aur uski energy kisi bhi canonical structure se kam yaani zyada stable hoti hai!).

2. **Galti 2: Formal Charge ke formula mein B ko 2 se bhaag dena bhool jaana.**
   FC = V − L − B lagaane par har bond double count ho jaata hai! Sahi formula **FC = V − L − (B ÷ 2)** hai (ya seedha **V − L − Bonds ki ginti**).

3. **Galti 3: NO₃⁻ (nitrate) mein Nitrogen par 5 bonds bana dena.**
   N Period 2 ka tatva hai (max covalency 4)! Sahi NO₃⁻ Lewis structure mein N par **4 bonds** (1 N=O aur 2 N−O, N par FC = +1) hote hain, 5 kabhi nahi!

## Quick Recap

- **Lattice Enthalpy:** Ionic crystal ki asli stability ka kaaran (NaCl = 788 kJ/mol; zyada charge, chhota size → zyada lattice enthalpy).
- **Formal Charge:** FC = V − L − (B ÷ 2); sabhi FC ka jod molecule/ion ke kul charge ke barabar hota hai.
- **Resonance Hybrid:** O₃ (BO = 1.5, 128 pm) aur CO₃²⁻ (BO = 1.33) mein sabhi bonds barabar aur extra stable hote hain.
- **Octet Limitations:** Incomplete (BF₃, BeCl₂), Odd-e⁻ (NO, NO₂), Expanded (PCl₅, SF₆), Noble-gas compounds (XeF₂).

### Answers

1. NO₃⁻ (24 valence e⁻):
   - **N (central):** V = 5, L = 0, B = 8 → FC = 5 − 0 − 4 = **+1**.
   - **Double-bonded O:** V = 6, L = 4, B = 4 → FC = 6 − 4 − 2 = **0**.
   - **Har Single-bonded O (dono):** V = 6, L = 6, B = 2 → FC = 6 − 6 − 1 = **−1**.
   Jaanch: (+1) + 0 + (−1) + (−1) = −1 (NO₃⁻ ka charge) ✅.

2. :C≡O: (10 valence e⁻):
   - **C:** V = 4, L = 2, B = 6 → FC = 4 − 2 − 3 = **−1**.
   - **O:** V = 6, L = 2, B = 6 → FC = 6 − 2 − 3 = **+1**.
   Kul jod = (−1) + (+1) = 0, isliye poora CO molecule neutral hai (dono ka octet poora hai).
   Jaanch: 2 + 2 + 6 = 10 valence e⁻ ✅.

3. NO₂⁻ ke do canonical structures: **[O=N−O]⁻ ↔ [O−N=O]⁻** (N par 1 lone pair, double-bonded O par 2 lp, single-bonded O par 3 lp).
   Asli hybrid mein dono N−O bonds barabar hain → **Bond Order = 3 ÷ 2 = 1.5**.
   Jaanch: Dono structures mein kul 18 valence e⁻ ✅.

4. (a) **AlCl₃** & (e) **BeH₂:** Incomplete octet (Al par 6 e⁻, Be par 4 e⁻).

5. **MgO** ki lattice enthalpy NaCl se kahin zyada hai (lagbhag 3800 vs 788 kJ/mol), kyunki Mg²⁺ aur O²⁻ par **dugna charge (+2, −2)** aur chhota ionic size hai → electrostatic aakarshan bahut zyada!

6. Expanded SO₄²⁻ (2 S=O, 2 S−O):
   - **S:** V = 6, L = 0, B = 12 → FC = 6 − 0 − 6 = **0**.
   - **Double-bonded O (dono):** FC = 6 − 4 − 2 = **0**.
   - **Single-bonded O (dono):** FC = 6 − 6 − 1 = **−1**.
   Ye structure behtar hai kyunki ismein S aur do O par FC = **0** aa jaata hai (minimal formal charge).
   Jaanch: 0 + 0 + 0 + (−1) + (−1) = −2 ✅.

7. Ozone dono canonical structures [O=O−O ↔ O−O=O] ka **Resonance Hybrid** hai; π-electrons teeno oxygens par barabar fail (delocalise) jaate hain, isliye dono bonds ek jaise (BO = 1.5, length = 128 pm) hote hain.

8. **Structure A [S=C=N]⁻:** S (6 − 4 − 2 = **0**), C (4 − 0 − 4 = **0**), N (5 − 4 − 2 = **−1**).

   *(Structure A mein −1 zyada electronegative N par hai, isliye dono ka yogdaan mahatvapoorna hai.)*
   Jaanch: Dono mein kul FC = −1 ✅.

9. Resonance hybrid ki actual energy sabse stable canonical structure se bhi **kam** hoti hai; isi energy ke antar ko **Resonance Energy** kehte hain — kam energy matlab zyada stability!

10. (a) **False** ❌. Molecule kabhi flip nahi karta; wo hamesha ek hi sthir Resonance Hybrid ke roop mein rehta hai.
    (b) **True** ✅. Atoms ki jagah wahi rehti hai, sirf π aur lone-pair electrons ki position badalti hai.
    (c) **True** ✅. Neutral molecule mein sabhi FC ka jod 0 hota hai.
    (d) **False** ❌. N Period 2 ka hai (no d-orbitals), isliye max 4 bonds hi bana sakta hai.

11. (a) **PO₄³⁻:** BO = 5 ÷ 4 = **1.25**.
    (b) **ClO₄⁻:** BO = 7 ÷ 4 = **1.75**.
