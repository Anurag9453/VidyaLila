# Topic 2: Electronic Configuration aur Periodic Table Ke Chaar Blocks (s, p, d, f)

Topic 1 mein humne dekha ki Modern Periodic Table atomic number Z par chalta hai. Par sabse mazedaar baat ye hai ki is table ka har period (2, 8, 8, 18, 18, 32, 32) aur har group seedha Chapter 2 ke **s, p, d, f electronic configurations** ([structure-of-atom-part5.md](../structure-of-atom/structure-of-atom-part5.md)) ka naqsha hai! Jis subshell (s, p, d ya f) mein tatva ka **aakhri (differentiating) electron** jaata hai, wahi us tatva ka **Block** tay karta hai. Aaj hum chaaron blocks ki pehchaan, unke general outer configurations, aur sirf atomic number Z dekh kar kisi bhi tatva ka **Period, Block aur Group** nikaalne ka pakka formula seekhenge!

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Block (s, p, d, f)** | Wo subshell jismein kisi tatva ka aakhri (differentiating) electron bharta hai | Na (3s¹) → s-block; Fe (3d⁶4s²) → d-block |
| **Representative (Main-group) elements** | s-block aur p-block ke sabhi tatva (Groups 1, 2 aur 13–18) | Na, Mg, C, N, O, Cl |

## Part A: Chaar Blocks Ek Nazar Mein

```
   s-block (G 1-2)       d-block (G 3-12)            p-block (G 13-18)
   [2 columns: ns¹⁻²]    [10 columns: (n-1)d¹⁻¹⁰]    [6 columns: np¹⁻⁶]
                                                                 (He yahan G18!)
   +-----+               +---------------------+     +---------------+
   :     :               :                     :     :               :
   :  s  :               :   d (Transition)    :     :       p       :
   :     :               :                     :     :               :
   +-----+               +---------------------+     +---------------+

                 f-block (Group 3 ke andar, neeche alag 2 rows):
                 [14 columns: 4f Lanthanoids (58-71) & 5f Actinoids (90-103)]
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Modern Periodic Table ka 4-block layout: s (2 columns), d (10 columns), p (6 columns) aur neeche f (14 columns).

| Block | Groups | General Outer Configuration | Khas Gun (Properties) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **s-block** | 1 (Alkali metals) & 2 (Alkaline earth) | **ns¹⁻²** | Mulayam, reactive metals; kam ionisation enthalpy; hamesha +1 ya +2 ions; zyadaatar ionic compounds (Li, Be ko chhodkar) |
| **p-block** | 13 se 18 (He ko milakar) | **ns² np¹⁻⁶** | Metals, metalloids aur sabhi non-metals (Halogens G17, Chalcogens G16, Noble gases G18) |
| **d-block** | 3 se 12 | **(n−1)d¹⁻¹⁰ ns⁰⁻²** | Sabhi metals; coloured ions, variable oxidation states, paramagnetic, achhe catalysts! |
| **f-block** | Group 3 (Periods 6 & 7) | **(n−2)f¹⁻¹⁴ (n−1)d⁰⁻¹ ns²** | Sabhi metals; Lanthanoids (4f) aur Actinoids (5f — sabhi radioactive; Z > 92 transuranium) |

### Do Zaroori Exceptions (Exam Favorites!):
1. **Helium (1s²):** Aakhri electron s mein jaata hai, par uska poora shell bhara (1s²) aur noble-gas vyavhaar hone ke kaaran use **Group 18 (p-block)** mein rakha gaya hai!
2. **Zn (30), Cd (48) aur Hg (80) d-block mein hain, par "true transition elements" NAHI hain!** Kyun? Kyunki unke neutral atom (d¹⁰ s²) aur +2 ion (d¹⁰) dono mein d-orbital **poora bhara (d¹⁰)** hota hai, adhoora nahi!

### Metals, Non-Metals aur Metalloids ka Naksha
- Poore Periodic Table mein **78% se zyada tatva Metals** hain (poora s-block, poora d-block, poora f-block aur p-block ka nichla-baayan hissa).
- **Non-metals** sirf upar-daayin taraf (p-block, ~20 tatva) mein hain.
- Dono ke beech ek **zig-zag seedhi (staircase line)** par **Metalloids (Semi-metals)** baithe hain: **Si, Ge, As, Sb, Te** (aur kuch kitabon mein B, Po) — jo metals aur non-metals dono ke gun dikhate hain!

## Part B: Sirf Atomic Number (Z) Se Period, Block aur Group Kaise Nikaalein?

Noble gases ke atomic numbers yaad rakho — yahi tumhare **milestones** hain:
**He (2), Ne (10), Ar (18), Kr (36), Xe (54), Rn (86), Og (118)**.

1. **Period Number** = Electronic configuration mein **sabse bada principal quantum number (n_max)**!
2. **Block** = Jis subshell (s, p, d ya f) mein **aakhri electron** bhara!
3. **Group Number:**
   - **s-block:** Group = **ns electrons ki ginti** (1 ya 2).
   - **p-block:** Group = **10 + (ns + np electrons)** = **12 + np electrons** (13 se 18).
   - **d-block:** Group = **(n−1)d electrons + ns electrons** (3 se 12).
   - **f-block:** Hamesha **Group 3**! (Period 6 → 4f Lanthanoids Z = 58–71; Period 7 → 5f Actinoids Z = 90–103).

### Example 1. Z = 17, 26, 38 aur 114 ka Period, Block aur Group Nikaalna

**Sawaal:** In chaar tatvon ka electronic configuration likh kar Period, Block aur Group batao:
(a) Z = 17   (b) Z = 26   (c) Z = 38   (d) Z = 114

**Hal:**
- **(a) Z = 17:** [Ne] 3s² 3p⁵ (Ne = 10 + 7 = 17).
  - n_max = 3 → **Period 3**; aakhri electron p mein → **p-block**; Group = 10 + (2 + 5) = **Group 17** (Halogen, Cl).
- **(b) Z = 26:** [Ar] 3d⁶ 4s² (Ar = 18 + 8 = 26).
  - n_max = 4 → **Period 4**; aakhri electron d mein → **d-block**; Group = 6 + 2 = **Group 8** (Fe).
- **(c) Z = 38:** [Kr] 5s² (Kr = 36 + 2 = 38).
  - n_max = 5 → **Period 5**; aakhri electron s mein → **s-block**; Group = **Group 2** (Sr).
- **(d) Z = 114:** Rn (86) ke baad Period 7 chalta hai (7s² 5f¹⁴ 6d¹⁰ 7p² → 86 + 2 + 14 + 10 + 2 = 114).
  - n_max = 7 → **Period 7**; aakhri electron p mein → **p-block**; Group = 10 + (2 + 2) = **Group 14** (Carbon family, Flerovium Fl!).

**Jaanch:** Z = 114 mein 118 (Group 18) se 4 kadam peeche gino: 18 − 4 = Group 14 ✅! Z = 38 mein Kr (36) ke 2 kadam baad → Period 5, Group 2 ✅!

### Example 2. Period aur Group se Atomic Number (Z) Ulta Nikaalna

**Sawaal:** Us tatva ka electronic configuration aur atomic number **Z** batao jo:
(a) **Period 4, Group 16** mein ho;
(b) **Period 5, Group 11** mein ho (Silver).

**Hal:**
- (a) Period 4 + Group 16 → p-block, np⁴ → [Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s² 4p⁴ → Z = 18 + 10 + 2 + 4 = **34 (Selenium, Se)**!
- (b) Period 5 + Group 11 → d-block, (n−1)d + ns = 11 → [Kr] 4d¹⁰ 5s¹ → Z = 36 + 10 + 1 = **47 (Silver, Ag)**!

**Jaanch:** Se (34) Kr (36) se 2 peeche → Group 18 − 2 = 16 ✅; Ag (47) Group 11 (coinage metal, Cu ke neeche!) ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: Helium (1s²) ko s-block Group 2 mein rakh dena.**
   *Sahi baat:* Helium ka 1s² shell poora bhara hai aur wo ek inert gas hai, isliye use **Group 18 (p-block)** mein rakha jaata hai (Be, Mg wale Group 2 mein nahi!).

2. **Galti 2: p-block ka Group nikaalte waqt 10 jodna bhool jaana.**
   Phosphorus ([Ne] 3s² 3p³) mein 5 valence electrons dekh kar ek student ne "Group 5" likh diya!
   *Sahi baat:* Group 3 se 12 beech mein d-block ke liye hain! Isliye p-block ka Group hamesha **10 + valence electrons** = 10 + 5 = **Group 15** hota hai!

3. **Galti 3: Lanthanoids ya Actinoids ko Group 6 ya Group 7 mein likh dena.**
   *Sahi baat:* Sabhi 14 Lanthanoids (58–71, Period 6) aur sabhi 14 Actinoids (90–103, Period 7) **Group 3** mein aate hain!

## Quick Recap

- **4 Blocks:** s (G1–2, ns¹⁻²), p (G13–18, ns²np¹⁻⁶), d (G3–12, (n−1)d¹⁻¹⁰ns⁰⁻²), f (G3, (n−2)f¹⁻¹⁴(n−1)d⁰⁻¹ns²).
- **Period = n_max**; **Group:** s → ns; p → 10 + ns + np; d → (n−1)d + ns; f → Group 3.
- **Zn, Cd, Hg** d-block ke hain par partially filled d-orbital na hone se true transition elements nahi hain.

## Practice Questions

1. Predict the **Period, Block and Group** of each element from its atomic number Z: (a) **Z = 15**, (b) **Z = 23**, (c) **Z = 35**, (d) **Z = 37**.
2. Predict the **Period, Block and Group** for: (a) **Z = 47** (Ag), (b) **Z = 56** (Ba), (c) **Z = 64** (Gd), (d) **Z = 117** (Ts).
3. If an undiscovered element with **Z = 120** is synthesised in the future, predict its (a) IUPAC systematic name and symbol, (b) Period, Block and Group, and (c) expected general outer electronic configuration.
4. Why are **Zinc (Z = 30)**, **Cadmium (Z = 48)** and **Mercury (Z = 80)** placed in the d-block, yet not considered typical **transition elements**?
5. Identify the element (give Z and Block/Group) whose ground-state outer electronic configuration is:
   (a) [Ar] 3d³ 4s²   (b) [Ne] 3s² 3p⁴   (c) [Kr] 4d¹⁰ 5s² 5p⁵   (d) [Xe] 6s¹
6. Why does Period 6 contain **32 elements**, whereas Period 4 contains **18 elements**? Answer directly in terms of the subshells filled in each period.
7. List three characteristic properties that distinguish **d-block transition metals** from **s-block alkali metals**.
8. What are **representative (main-group) elements** and **transuranium elements**? Give two examples of each.
9. Why is **Hydrogen (1s¹)** placed separately at the top of the periodic table, and why is **Helium (1s²)** placed in Group 18 instead of Group 2?
10. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) An element with configuration [Ar] 3d⁷ 4s² belongs to Period 4, d-block, Group 9.
    (b) All 14 Lanthanoids belong to Period 6 and Group 3.
    (c) Every element in the p-block is a non-metal.
    (d) The general outer configuration of Group 16 (chalcogens) is ns² np⁴.
11. Name five common **metalloids (semi-metals)** that lie along the zig-zag boundary between metals and non-metals in the p-block.
12. Find the **atomic number Z** and outer electronic configuration of the element located in (a) **Period 4, Group 15**, and (b) **Period 6, Group 2**.

### Answers

1. (a) **Z = 15:** [Ne] 3s² 3p³ → **Period 3, p-block, Group 15** (10 + 5).
   (b) **Z = 23:** [Ar] 3d³ 4s² → **Period 4, d-block, Group 5** (3 + 2).
   (c) **Z = 35:** [Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s² 4p⁵ → **Period 4, p-block, Group 17** (10 + 7).
   (d) **Z = 37:** [Kr] 5s¹ → **Period 5, s-block, Group 1**.
   Jaanch: Z = 35, Kr (36) se 1 peeche → Period 4, Group 17 ✅; Z = 37, Kr (36) se 1 aage → Period 5, Group 1 ✅.

2. (a) **Z = 47 (Ag):** [Kr] 4d¹⁰ 5s¹ → **Period 5, d-block, Group 11** (10 + 1).
   (b) **Z = 56 (Ba):** [Xe] 6s² → **Period 6, s-block, Group 2**.
   (c) **Z = 64 (Gd):** 58 se 71 ke beech (4f Lanthanoid) → **Period 6, f-block, Group 3**.
   (d) **Z = 117 (Ts):** [Rn] 5f¹⁴ 6d¹⁰ 7s² 7p⁵ → **Period 7, p-block, Group 17** (Og 118 se 1 peeche!).
   Jaanch: Z = 117 → 18 − 1 = Group 17 ✅.

3. (a) **Unbinilium (Ubn)**.
   (b) Og (118) par Period 7 khatam → 119 (P8, G1), **120 → Period 8, s-block, Group 2** (alkaline earth family).
   (c) Outer configuration: **[Og] 8s²**.
   Jaanch: 118 + 2 = 120 ✅.

4. Transition element ki shart hai ki atom ya uske aam ion mein **adhoora bhara d-orbital (d¹–d⁹)** hona chahiye. Zn, Cd aur Hg ke atom (d¹⁰ s²) aur +2 ions (d¹⁰) dono mein d-subshell poora bhara hota hai, isliye we d-block ke hain par true transition elements nahi maane jaate.

5. (a) Z = 18 + 5 = **23 (V)**, d-block, Group 5.
   (b) Z = 10 + 6 = **16 (S)**, p-block, Group 16.
   (c) Z = 36 + 17 = **53 (I)**, p-block, Group 17.
   (d) Z = 54 + 1 = **55 (Cs)**, s-block, Group 1.
   Jaanch: 53, Xe (54) se 1 peeche → Group 17 ✅.

6. Period 4 mein sirf **4s (2) + 3d (10) + 4p (6) = 18** electrons bharte hain → 18 elements. Period 6 mein **6s (2) + 4f (14) + 5d (10) + 6p (6) = 32** electrons bharte hain → 32 elements.
   Jaanch: 2 + 14 + 10 + 6 = 32 ✅.

7. d-block transition metals: (1) variable oxidation states dikhate hain (jaise Fe²⁺, Fe³⁺), (2) coloured ions/compounds banate hain, (3) aksar catalysts ka kaam karte hain (jabki s-block alkali metals sirf +1 dikhate hain aur colourless ions banate hain).

8. **Representative elements:** s-block aur p-block ke tatva (jaise Na, Cl). **Transuranium elements:** Uranium (Z = 92) ke baad ke manav-nirmit radioactive tatva (jaise Np Z = 93, Pu Z = 94).

9. Hydrogen (1s¹) ek electron dekar Group 1 (H⁺) jaisa aur ek electron lekar Group 17 (H⁻) jaisa dono vyavhaar karta hai, isliye use upar alag rakha jaata hai. Helium (1s²) ka pehla shell poora bhara hai aur ye chemically inert noble gas hai, isliye use Group 18 mein rakha gaya hai.

10. (a) **True** ✅. n_max = 4, d-block, Group = 7 + 2 = 9 (Cobalt).
    (b) **True** ✅. Z = 58–71 sabhi Period 6, Group 3 mein hain.
    (c) **False** ❌. p-block mein non-metals ke saath metalloids (Si, Ge) aur metals (Al, Sn, Pb, Bi) bhi aate hain.
    (d) **True** ✅. Group 16 (O, S, Se...) mein 6 valence electrons: ns² np⁴.

11. Paanch mashhoor metalloids: **Silicon (Si), Germanium (Ge), Arsenic (As), Antimony (Sb) aur Tellurium (Te)**.

12. (a) Period 4, Group 15 → [Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s² 4p³ → Z = 18 + 10 + 2 + 3 = **33 (Arsenic, As)**.
    (b) Period 6, Group 2 → [Xe] 6s² → Z = 54 + 2 = **56 (Barium, Ba)**.
    Jaanch: 33, Kr (36) se 3 peeche → Group 15 ✅; 56, Xe (54) se 2 aage → Group 2 ✅.
