Class 10 ([periodic-properties.md](../../../class10/chemistry/periodic-properties/periodic-properties.md), [periodic-properties-part2.md](../../../class10/chemistry/periodic-properties/periodic-properties-part2.md) aur [periodic-properties-part3.md](../../../class10/chemistry/periodic-properties/periodic-properties-part3.md)) mein tumne teen aasaan trends yaad kiye the: period mein baayein se daayein size ghat-ta hai, ionisation energy badhti hai aur electron attraction badhta hai. ## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Covalent radius (r_cov)** | Ek hi tatva ke single-bonded do atoms ke nuclei ki doori ka aadha | Cl₂ mein bond length 198 pm → r_cov(Cl) = 99 pm |
| **Metallic radius (r_met)** | Metal crystal mein do paas-paas atoms ke nuclei ki doori ka aadha | Cu mein 256 pm ÷ 2 = 128 pm |
| **van der Waals radius (r_vdW)** | Do alag-alag non-bonded paas-paas molecules ke atoms ke nuclei ki doori ka aadha (sabse bada!) | Cl ke liye r_vdW = 180 pm > r_cov = 99 pm |
| **Effective nuclear charge (Z_eff)** | Andar ke electrons ki shielding ghatane ke baad bahari electron par lagne wala asli nuclear khinchaav | Period mein baayein se daayein Z_eff badhta hai |
| **Ionisation enthalpy (ΔᵢH)** | Isolated gaseous atom se sabse dheela electron nikaalne ke liye lagne wali energy (hamesha positive, endothermic; kJ/mol) | X(g) → X⁺(g) + e⁻, ΔᵢH₁ > 0 |
| **Electron gain enthalpy (ΔₑgH)** | Isolated gaseous atom mein ek electron jodne par hone wala energy badlaav (energy nikle to **negative**, lage to **positive**; kJ/mol) | Cl(g) + e⁻ → Cl⁻(g), ΔₑgH = −349 kJ/mol |

## Part A: Teen Prakaar ke Atomic Radii aur Isoelectronic Ions

Kyunki electron ek 3D probability cloud hai (Chapter 2), kisi akele atom ki pakki boundary nahi hoti. Isliye hum do paas-paas atoms ke nuclei ki doori ka aadha naapte hain:

1. **Covalent Radius (r_cov):** Bond mein electron clouds ek-doosre ke andar **overlap** karte hain → doori sabse kam!
2. **Metallic Radius (r_met):** Metal atoms touch karte hain par covalent jitna overlap nahi → beech ka size.
3. **van der Waals Radius (r_vdW):** Do alag molecules sirf door se touch karte hain (koi bond nahi; noble gases ke liye yahi naapa jaata hai!) → **sabse bada radius**!

> **Hamesha yaad rakho:** **r_vdW > r_metallic > r_covalent** *(Noble gas ka radius period mein achanak bada kyun dikhta hai? Kyunki uske liye covalent nahi, balki van der Waals radius naapa jaata hai!)*

### Isoelectronic Species ka Size Rule
Jab alag ions mein **electrons ki ginti barabar** ho (jaise 10 e⁻: N³⁻, O²⁻, F⁻, Na⁺, Mg²⁺, Al³⁺):
- **Jitna zyada nuclear charge (Z / protons), utna tez andar ka khinchaav → utna CHHOTA size!**
- Kram: **Al³⁺ (Z=13) < Mg²⁺ (12) < Na⁺ (11) < F⁻ (9) < O²⁻ (8) < N³⁻ (7)**.

## Part B: Ionisation Enthalpy (ΔᵢH) aur Period 2 ke Do Mashhoor Exceptions!

Aam trend: Period mein baayein se daayein Z_eff badhne aur size ghatne se **ΔᵢH₁ badhti hai**, aur group mein neeche jaane par size badhne se **ΔᵢH₁ ghat-ti hai**.

**Par Period 2 mein do jagah graph neeche girta hai (Class 11 ka sabse zaroori sawaal!):**

```
   ΔᵢH₁ (kJ/mol) — Period 2 (Li se Ne)

   2100 :                                           Ne (2080)
   1700 :                                      F (1681)
   1400 :                         N (1402)
   1300 :                                O (1314) <-- N > O !
   1100 :                   C (1086)
    900 :        Be (899)
    800 :              B (801) <-- Be > B !
    500 :  Li (520)
        +-----------------------------------------------------> Z
           Li    Be    B    C     N      O     F    Ne
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Period 2 ki first ionisation enthalpy (ΔᵢH₁) mein do pakke ultaav: Be (899) > B (801) aur N (1402) > O (1314).

### In Do Exceptions ka Asli Quantum Kaaran:
1. **ΔᵢH₁ of Be (899 kJ/mol) > B (801 kJ/mol) kyun?**
   - **Be:** 1s² **2s²** (electron **2s** se nikaalna hai, jo nucleus ke zyada paas **penetrate** karta hai aur poora bhara hai).
   - **B:** 1s² 2s² **2p¹** (electron **2p** se nikaalna hai, jo 2s ke muqable nucleus se door aur 2s electrons dwara shield kiya hua hai!).
   - Isliye B se electron nikaalna Be se aasaan hai → **Be > B**! *(Bilkul yahi Period 3 mein **Mg > Al** mein hota hai!)*
2. **ΔᵢH₁ of N (1402 kJ/mol) > O (1314 kJ/mol) kyun?**
   - **N:** 1s² 2s² **2p³** ([↑][↑][↑] — **exactly half-filled**, extra stable!).
   - **O:** 1s² 2s² **2p⁴** ([↑↓][↑][↑] — ek box mein **do electrons paired** hain, jinmein aapas ka repulsion hai; ek electron nikaalte hi O⁺ half-filled 2p³ ban jaata hai!).
   - Isliye O se pehla electron nikaalna N se aasaan hai → **N > O**! *(Bilkul yahi Period 3 mein **P > S** mein hota hai!)*

### Successive Ionisation Enthalpies (ΔᵢH₁ < ΔᵢH₂ < ΔᵢH₃)
Har agla electron nikaalna pichhle se zyada mushkil hota hai kyunki positive ion mein bache electrons par khinchaav badh jaata hai. Jab **noble-gas core** se electron nikaalna pade, to ΔᵢH mein ek **bahut badi chhalaang (huge jump)** dikhti hai — isi se tatva ka Group pata chal jaata hai!

## Part C: Electron Gain Enthalpy (ΔₑgH) — Sign Convention aur Cl > F ka Raaz

- Jab gaseous atom electron lekar **energy bahar chhodta hai (exothermic)**, to **ΔₑgH NEGATIVE** hoti hai (jitni zyada negative, electron lene ka utna zyada josh!).
- Jab electron zabardasti daalne par **energy deni pade (endothermic)**, to **ΔₑgH POSITIVE** hoti hai (jaise Noble gases: Ne = **+116 kJ/mol**, kyunki naya electron agle bade shell mein jaata hai!).

### Do Sabse Zaroori Class 11 Facts on ΔₑgH:
1. **Chlorine (−349 kJ/mol) ki ΔₑgH Fluorine (−328 kJ/mol) se ZYADA NEGATIVE kyun hai? (Aur S −200 > O −141 kyun?)**
   - Fluorine (2p⁵) aur Oxygen (2p⁴) ke 2p orbitals bahut **chhote aur ghane (compact)** hain. Jab naya electron andar aata hai, to wahan pehle se maujood electrons use tez **electron-electron repulsion** dete hain, jisse kuch energy kharch ho jaati hai.
   - Chlorine (3p⁵) aur Sulphur (3p⁴) ke **3p orbitals bade aur khule** hain — repulsion bahut kam hota hai! Isliye **ΔₑgH (zyada negative): Cl > F > Br > I** aur **S > O**!
2. **Doosra Electron Gain Enthalpy (ΔₑgH₂) HAMESHA POSITIVE (Endothermic) kyun hota hai?**
   - O(g) + e⁻ → O⁻(g): ΔₑgH₁ = **−141 kJ/mol** (exothermic — neutral atom ne electron khincha).
   - O⁻(g) + e⁻ → O²⁻(g): ΔₑgH₂ = **+780 kJ/mol** (endothermic! Kyunki negative O⁻ ion naye aane wale negative electron ko **tez repel** karta hai; energy zabardasti deni padti hai!).

### Example 1. Period 2 ka Sahi ΔᵢH₁ Kram aur Isoelectronic Size

**Sawaal:**
(a) Period 2 ke elements (Li, Be, B, C, N, O, F, Ne) ko **badhti hui ΔᵢH₁** ke sahi kram mein lagao.
(b) Isoelectronic ions **F⁻, Na⁺, O²⁻, Al³⁺, Mg²⁺** ko **badhte hue ionic radius** ke kram mein lagao.

**Hal:**
- (a) Be > B aur N > O ke exceptions yaad rakho:
  **Li (520) < B (801) < Be (899) < C (1086) < O (1314) < N (1402) < F (1681) < Ne (2080)** kJ/mol.
- (b) Sabhi mein 10 electrons hain; zyada Z → chhota radius:
  **Al³⁺ (Z=13) < Mg²⁺ (12) < Na⁺ (11) < F⁻ (9) < O²⁻ (8)**.

**Jaanch:** Kram mein Z ghat raha hai (13 → 12 → 11 → 9 → 8) → radius lagatar badh raha hai ✅.

### Shielding Effect, Poor d/f Shielding aur ΔᵢH₂ ka Twist (Na vs Mg!)

1. **Shielding Order:** **s > p > d > f** (d aur f orbitals phailay hue hote hain, isliye we bahar ke electrons ko nucleus ke khinchaav se **achhe se nahi bacha paate!**). Isi poor f-shielding ke kaaran Lanthanoids (4f) mein aane wale 5d elements (jaise **Hf**) ka size 4d (**Zr**) ke lagbhag barabar reh jaata hai — ise **Lanthanoid Contraction** kehte hain!
2. **Example 2. ΔᵢH₁ banaam ΔᵢH₂ of Na (11) aur Mg (12) + ΔₑgH of Be, Mg, N**
   - **ΔᵢH₁:** Mg (3s², 738 kJ/mol) > Na (3s¹, 496 kJ/mol).
   - **ΔᵢH₂:** Ek electron nikalne ke baad **Na⁺ = 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ (stable Neon core!)** ban jaata hai, jabki **Mg⁺ = [Ne] 3s¹** rehta hai! Isliye doosra electron nikaalna Na⁺ se behad mushkil hai: **ΔᵢH₂(Na = 4562) >> ΔᵢH₂(Mg = 1451) kJ/mol**!
   - **Positive / Near-Zero ΔₑgH (Be, Mg, N):** Be (2s²), Mg (3s²) ke s-orbitals poore bhare hain aur N (2p³) exactly half-filled hai — inmein naya electron daalne par stability toot-ti hai, isliye inki **ΔₑgH lagbhag zero ya positive** hoti hai!

**Jaanch:** Na⁺ mein stable 2p⁶ core tootne se ΔᵢH₂(Na) > 3 × ΔᵢH₂(Mg) ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: "Fluorine sabse zyada electronegative hai, isliye F ki ΔₑgH sabse zyada negative hogi" likh dena.**
   *Sahi baat:* Electronegativity (bond mein khinchna) mein F sabse aage hai (4.0), par **Electron Gain Enthalpy** (isolated gaseous atom mein electron jodna) mein **Chlorine (−349 kJ/mol)**, Fluorine (−328 kJ/mol) se aage hai — 2p compact repulsion ke kaaran!

2. **Galti 2: Period 2 ya Period 3 ki ΔᵢH₁ mein Be/B (ya Mg/Al) aur N/O (ya P/S) ka flip bhool jaana.**
   Hamesha check karo: **Group 2 > Group 13** (s² penetration) aur **Group 15 > Group 16** (half-filled p³ stability)!

3. **Galti 3: Positive ΔₑgH (+116 kJ/mol) ko "bahut zyada electron attraction" samajh lena.**
   *Sahi baat:* ΔₑgH mein **MINUS (−)** sign energy nikalna (zyada attraction) dikhata hai; **PLUS (+)** sign ka matlab hai ki atom electron lena **nahi chahta** (noble gases, N, Be, Mg).

## Quick Recap

- **Radius order:** r_vdW > r_metallic > r_covalent; Isoelectronic mein zyada Z → chhota radius.
- **ΔᵢH₁ Exceptions:** Period 2 mein **Be > B** aur **N > O**; Period 3 mein **Mg > Al** aur **P > S**.
- **ΔₑgH Exceptions:** **Cl (−349) > F (−328)** aur **S (−200) > O (−141)** (compact 2p repulsion); 2nd ΔₑgH hamesha **positive (endothermic)**.

## Practice Questions

1. Arrange the Period 3 elements (**Na, Mg, Al, Si, P, S, Cl, Ar**) in **increasing order of first ionisation enthalpy (ΔᵢH₁)**, clearly explaining the two inversions in the sequence.
2. An element M has the following successive ionisation enthalpies (in kJ/mol): **ΔᵢH₁ = 738**, **ΔᵢH₂ = 1451**, **ΔᵢH₃ = 7733**, **ΔᵢH₄ = 10540**.
   (a) How many valence electrons does M have?
   (b) Which group of the periodic table does M belong to, and what is the formula of its chloride?
3. Arrange the 18-electron isoelectronic species **Ca²⁺ (Z=20), K⁺ (Z=19), Cl⁻ (Z=17), S²⁻ (Z=16)** in **decreasing order of ionic radius**. Explain your order using nuclear charge.
4. Why is the **electron gain enthalpy of Sulphur (−200 kJ/mol)** more negative than that of **Oxygen (−141 kJ/mol)**, even though Oxygen is smaller and higher in Group 16?
5. Explain why the first electron gain enthalpy of Oxygen (O → O⁻, −141 kJ/mol) is **negative**, whereas the second electron gain enthalpy (O⁻ → O²⁻, +780 kJ/mol) is **positive**.
6. Why do **noble gases (He, Ne, Ar)** have large **positive** electron gain enthalpies and unusually large reported atomic radii compared to the halogens right next to them?
7. Compare **covalent radius**, **metallic radius** and **van der Waals radius** in order of increasing size, and explain why the covalent radius of an element is always smaller than its van der Waals radius.
8. Between **N** and **O**, which has the higher **first** ionisation enthalpy (ΔᵢH₁), and which has the higher **second** ionisation enthalpy (ΔᵢH₂)? Explain using their electronic configurations.
9. Arrange the halogens **F, Cl, Br, I** in order of **increasingly negative** (least negative to most negative) electron gain enthalpy.
10. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) Across a period from left to right, effective nuclear charge (Z_eff) increases and atomic radius generally decreases.
    (b) ΔᵢH₁ of Aluminium (3s²3p¹) is greater than ΔᵢH₁ of Magnesium (3s²).
    (c) A cation is always smaller than its parent neutral atom, whereas an anion is always larger.
    (d) Adding an electron to an isolated gaseous Ne atom releases a large amount of energy.
11. Explain why **ΔᵢH₂ of Sodium (4562 kJ/mol)** is much higher than **ΔᵢH₂ of Magnesium (1451 kJ/mol)**, even though ΔᵢH₁ of Mg is higher than ΔᵢH₁ of Na.
12. Why do **Be (2s²)** and **N (2p³)** have nearly zero or positive electron gain enthalpies, unlike their neighbouring elements B and C?

### Answers

1. Sahi badhta kram: **Na < Al < Mg < Si < S < P < Cl < Ar**.
   Do ultaav: (i) **Mg (3s²) > Al (3s²3p¹)** kyunki 3s electron zyada penetrate karta hai aur poora bhara hai; (ii) **P (3p³) > S (3p⁴)** kyunki P ka 3p³ exactly half-filled aur extra stable hai, jabki S ke 3p⁴ mein paired repulsion hai.
   Jaanch: Group 2 > Group 13 aur Group 15 > Group 16 ✅.

2. (a) ΔᵢH₂ (1451) se ΔᵢH₃ (7733) ke beech lagbhag **5.3 guna ki badi chhalaang** hai → matlab 2 electrons nikalne ke baad noble-gas core aa gaya! Isliye M mein **2 valence electrons** hain.
   (b) Ye **Group 2** (alkaline earth metal, asal mein Mg!) ka tatva hai; iske chloride ka formula **MCl₂** hoga.
   Jaanch: 7733 ÷ 1451 = 5.33 (huge jump after 2nd e⁻) ✅.

3. Ghatta hua ionic radius (sabse bada pehle, sabse chhota Z pehle):
   **S²⁻ (Z=16) > Cl⁻ (Z=17) > K⁺ (Z=19) > Ca²⁺ (Z=20)**.
   Sabhi mein 18 electrons hain, par Ca²⁺ mein 20 protons un 18 electrons ko sabse zyada kas kar andar kheench rahe hain (sabse chhota size).
   Jaanch: Z badh raha hai (16 → 17 → 19 → 20) → size lagatar ghat raha hai ✅.

4. Oxygen ka 2p subshell bahut chhota aur compact hai, isliye naye aane wale electron ko pehle se maujood 2p electrons se tez repulsion milta hai. Sulphur ka 3p subshell bada aur khula hai, jahan repulsion kam hota hai — isliye S zyada energy chhodta hai (−200 vs −141 kJ/mol).

5. Neutral O atom mein pehla electron jodna nucleus ke aakarshan se exothermic (−141 kJ/mol) hota hai. Par ek baar **O⁻** ban jaane ke baad, doosra negative electron jodne par **negative O⁻ ion aur negative electron ke beech tez electrostatic repulsion** hota hai — is repulsion ko todne ke liye energy deni padti hai (+780 kJ/mol, endothermic).

6. Noble gases ke valence shells poore bhare (ns²np⁶) hote hain; naya electron agle oonche energy shell mein jaata hai, jiske liye energy deni padti hai (positive ΔₑgH). Unka atomic radius covalent bond se nahi balki **van der Waals radius** se naapa jaata hai, jo hamesha covalent radius se kaafi bada hota hai.

7. Badhta kram: **r_covalent < r_metallic < r_van der Waals**. Covalent bond mein dono atoms ke electron clouds ek doosre ke andar **overlap** karte hain (doori kam), jabki van der Waals mein do alag molecules bina overlap ke sirf door se touch karte hain.

8. **ΔᵢH₁:** **N (2p³) > O (2p⁴)** (N half-filled hai).
   **ΔᵢH₂:** Ek electron nikalne ke baad **O⁺ = 2p³ (half-filled!)** ban jaata hai jabki **N⁺ = 2p²** hota hai — isliye doosra electron nikaalna O⁺ se zyada mushkil hai: **ΔᵢH₂(O) > ΔᵢH₂(N)**!
   Jaanch: O⁺ (7 e⁻) = 1s²2s²2p³ half-filled ✅.

9. Kam negative se zyada negative: **I (−295) < Br (−325) < F (−328) < Cl (−349)** kJ/mol.
   Jaanch: Cl sabse zyada negative (−349) ✅.

10. (a) **True** ✅. Z_eff badhne se electron cloud andar khinchta hai.
    (b) **False** ❌. ΔᵢH₁ of **Mg (3s²) > Al (3s²3p¹)** (s-orbital penetration).
    (c) **True** ✅. Cation mein Z/e ratio badhta hai (sikudta hai); anion mein Z/e ghatta aur repulsion badhta hai (failta hai).
    (d) **False** ❌. Ne mein electron jodne par energy nikalne ke bajay **lagti hai** (ΔₑgH = +116 kJ/mol, endothermic).

11. Pehla electron nikalne ke baad **Na⁺** ka configuration **[Ne] (1s² 2s² 2p⁶)** — ek behad stable noble-gas core — ban jaata hai, jisse doosra electron nikaalna bahut mushkil hota hai (4562 kJ/mol). **Mg⁺** mein [Ne] 3s¹ bachta hai, jisse 3s¹ electron aasani se nikal jaata hai (1451 kJ/mol).

12. **Be (2s²)** ka s-subshell poora bhara hai (naya electron oonche 2p mein jaayega) aur **N (2p³)** ka p-subshell exactly half-filled aur extra stable hai — isliye dono naya electron lene ka virodh karte hain aur unki ΔₑgH positive/near-zero hoti hai.
