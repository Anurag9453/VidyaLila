# Topic 4: Electronegativity, Valence aur Oxidation States, Second Period ke Anomalous Properties aur Diagonal Relationship

Topic 3 mein humne isolated gaseous atoms ke gun (size, ΔᵢH, ΔₑgH) dekhe. Ab hum un tatvon ko **chemical compounds** ke andar dekhte hain: bond mein electrons kheenchne ki taakat (**Electronegativity**), **Valence banaam Oxidation State**, har group ke pehle sadasya (**Period 2: Li, Be, B, C, N, O, F**) ka apne hi group se bilkul alag vyavhaar (**Anomalous Properties**), aur ek mazedaar **Diagonal Relationship** (tircha rishta) jismein Lithium Magnesium jaisa aur Beryllium Aluminium jaisa bartaav karta hai!

## Elina aur joint family ke bade bhaiya ka tircha rishta

Elina ne apne joint family mein ek dilchasp baat notice ki: unke chacha ka sabse bada beta (Group 1 ka pehla sadasya — Lithium jaisa) apne chhote bhaiyon (Na, K) se zyada alag swabhav ka hai, par uski aadatein bagal wale parivaar ke **ek saal chhote cousin** (Group 2, Period 3 — Magnesium jaisa) se bilkul milti hain!

Elina ki Didi (jo chemistry padhti hain) ne hans kar kaha, "Elina, periodic table mein bhi theek yahi hota hai! Har group ka pehla element (**Period 2: Li, Be, B, C, N, O, F**) chhote size aur **d-orbitals na hone** ke kaaran apne group ke baaki sadasyon se alag vyavhaar karta hai — aur neeche daayin taraf baithe **diagonal partner (Li–Mg, Be–Al, B–Si)** se dosti kar leta hai!" Chalo, is poori chemical periodicity ko aasaani se samajhte hain.

## Part A: Electronegativity aur Valence banaam Oxidation State

Class 10 ([periodic-properties-part4.md](../../../class10/chemistry/periodic-properties/periodic-properties-part4.md)) mein tumne Pauling scale (F = 4.0, O = 3.5, N = 3.0, Cl = 3.0, C = 2.5, H = 2.1, Na = 0.9) aur uska trend (period mein badhti hai, group mein ghat-ti hai) seekha tha. Class 11 mein do nayi baatein jodo:
1. **Electronegativity koi constant number nahi hai:** Ye atom ke chemical environment (jaise uski oxidation state) ke saath thodi badal sakti hai (jaise Fe³⁺ ki EN, Fe²⁺ se zyada hoti hai). *(Ek aur scale — **Mulliken scale** — EN ko IE aur EA ka average maanta hai: χ_Pauling ≈ (IE + EA) ÷ 540 jab IE, EA kJ/mol mein hon.)*
2. **Valence banaam Oxidation State:**
   - Representative elements (s aur p-block) ke liye aam **valence** = valence electrons ki ginti (n) ya **(8 − n)**.
   - Par **Oxidation State** mein **sign (+ ya −)** bhi hota hai aur ye electronegativity par nirbhar karta hai!

### Example 1. Oxygen ka Oxidation State: OF₂ banaam Na₂O aur H₂O₂ *(OF₂ aur Na₂O₂ tez oxidisers hain: sirf padhne ke liye)*

**Sawaal:** OF₂, Na₂O aur H₂O₂ mein Oxygen (O) ki oxidation state nikaalo aur batao ki OF₂ mein Oxygen positive kyun hai!

**Hal:**
- **Na₂O:** O (3.5) > Na (0.9) → O zyada electronegative hai, har Na se 1 e⁻ kheenchta hai → **Na = +1, O = −2**.
- **OF₂:** **Fluorine (4.0) > Oxygen (3.5)**! Fluorine poore periodic table mein sabse zyada electronegative hai, isliye dono F atoms Oxygen se 1-1 electron apni taraf kheench lete hain → **F = −1** (har ek), aur **O = +2**!
- **H₂O₂ (Hydrogen peroxide):** Do O atoms aapas mein jude hain (H−O−O−H); har O sirf ek H se electron kheenchta hai → **H = +1, O = −1**.

**Jaanch (Neutral compound mein kul charge = 0):** OF₂: (+2) + 2(−1) = 0 ✅; Na₂O: 2(+1) + (−2) = 0 ✅; H₂O₂: 2(+1) + 2(−1) = 0 ✅.

## Part B: Period 2 ke Anomalous Properties aur Diagonal Relationship

Har s-block aur p-block group ka **pehla sadasya (Li, Be, B, C, N, O, F)** apne neeche wale bhaaiyon (Na, Mg, Al, Si, P, S, Cl) se kaafi alag bartaav karta hai. Iske **chaar pakke kaaran** hain:
1. **Bahut chhota atomic/ionic size**
2. **Zyada charge-to-radius ratio (high polarising power)**
3. **Zyada Electronegativity**
4. **Valence shell mein d-orbitals ka NA hona!**

### d-Orbitals Na Hone ka Sabse Bada Nateeja: Maximum Covalency = 4!
- **Period 2 (n = 2):** Valence shell mein sirf **2s (1 orbital) + 2p (3 orbitals) = kul 4 orbitals** hain! Isliye Period 2 ka koi bhi tatva (B, C, N, O, F) **4 se zyada covalent bonds nahi bana sakta!**
  - Isliye **[BF₄]⁻** banta hai, par **[BF₆]³⁻ kabhi nahi banta**!
  - Isi tarah **NCl₃** banta hai, par **NCl₅ nahi banta**!
- **Period 3 (n = 3):** Valence shell mein **3s + 3p + khaali 3d orbitals** maujood hain! Isliye Period 3 ke tatva apni covalency 4 se badhakar **5 ya 6** kar sakte hain → **[AlF₆]³⁻**, **PCl₅**, **SF₆** sab aasani se bante hain!
- **pπ–pπ Multiple Bonding:** Chhote size ke kaaran Period 2 ke C, N aur O aapas mein majboot double/triple bonds (**C=C, C≡C, N≡N, O=O**) bana lete hain, jabki bade Period 3 ke Si, P aur S aasani se aise multiple bonds nahi banate.

### Diagonal Relationship (Tircha Rishta)
Period mein baayein se daayein jaane par size ghatta hai (charge/radius badhta hai); group mein neeche jaane par size badhta hai (charge/radius ghatta hai). Jab tum **ek kadam daayein aur ek kadam neeche (diagonally)** chalte ho, to dono asar ek-doosre ko lagbhag **cancel** kar dete hain!

```
   Period 2:    Li         Be          B          C
                 \          \           \
                  \          \           \
   Period 3:    Na  -->   Mg   -->    Al   -->   Si
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Periodic table ke teen mashhoor diagonal jode: Li ↔ Mg, Be ↔ Al aur B ↔ Si — lagbhag barabar charge/radius ratio aur electronegativity ke kaaran inke gun milte-julte hain.

- **Li ↔ Mg:** Dono sakht hain, covalent compounds banate hain, hawa mein N₂ se seedha nitride (Li₃N, Mg₃N₂) banate hain, aur dono ke carbonates garam karne par toot kar oxide + CO₂ dete hain (jabki Na₂CO₃ nahi toot-ta!).
- **Be ↔ Al:** Dono ki Pauling EN lagbhag barabar (~1.5) hai, dono ke oxides (**BeO aur Al₂O₃**) **amphoteric** hain (acid aur base dono se react karte hain!), aur dono covalent chlorides (BeCl₂, AlCl₃) banate hain.
- **B ↔ Si:** Dono metalloids/semiconductors hain aur dono acidic oxides (B₂O₃, SiO₂) banate hain.

## Part C: Oxides ki Prakriti (Class 10 Bridge + Amphoteric & Neutral Oxides)

Class 10 ([periodic-properties-part5.md](../../../class10/chemistry/periodic-properties/periodic-properties-part5.md)) mein humne Period 3 ke oxides ka safar dekha tha: **Na₂O (strongly basic) → MgO (basic) → Al₂O₃ (amphoteric) → SiO₂, P₄O₁₀, SO₃ → Cl₂O₇ (strongly acidic)**.
Class 11 mein do pakke points yaad rakho:
- **Amphoteric oxide (Al₂O₃)** acid aur base dono se react karta hai *(lab teacher demo)*:
  - Al₂O₃ + 6HCl → 2AlCl₃ + 3H₂O
  - Al₂O₃ + 2NaOH → 2NaAlO₂ + H₂O
- **Neutral oxides:** Kuch non-metal oxides na acidic hote hain na basic — in teeno ke naam hamesha yaad rakho: **CO, NO aur N₂O**!

### Periodic Trends in Chemical Reactivity (Ek Nazar Mein)
1. **Metallic Character & Reactivity of Metals (Group 1, 2):** Group mein neeche jaane par size badhta hai aur ΔᵢH ghat-ti hai → electron dena aasaan hota jaata hai → **Group 1 reactivity badhti hai (Li < Na < K < Rb < Cs)**!
2. **Non-Metallic Character & Reactivity of Non-Metals (Group 17):** Period mein baayein se daayein badhti hai; Group 17 mein neeche jaane par electron lene ki taakat ghat-ti hai → **reactivity ghat-ti hai (F₂ > Cl₂ > Br₂ > I₂)**!
3. **Oxidation State & Electronegativity of Hybridised Carbon:** Jitna zyada s-character (sp 50% > sp² 33.3% > sp³ 25%), utni zyada Carbon ki electronegativity: **C(sp) > C(sp²) > C(sp³)**!

### Example 2. Metallic Character, Reactivity aur Hybridised Carbon ki Electronegativity

**Sawaal:**
(a) **Na, Mg, Si, P, Cl** ko **ghat-te hue metallic character** ke kram mein lagao.
(b) Group 1 mein **Cs** sabse reactive metal hai jabki Group 17 mein **F₂** sabse reactive non-metal — dono ka trend ek-doosre ka ulta kyun hai?

**Hal:**
- (a) Period 3 mein baayein se daayein metallic character ghat-ta hai: **Na > Mg > Si > P > Cl**.
- (b) Metals electron **dekar** react karte hain (kam ΔᵢH → zyada reactive → Group 1 mein sabse neeche **Cs**!), jabki non-metals electron **lekar** react karte hain (high EN + chhota size → Group 17 mein sabse upar **F₂**!).

**Jaanch:** Left-bottom (Cs) = sabse strong metal; Top-right (F) = sabse strong non-metal ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: "Oxygen ka oxidation state hamesha −2 hota hai" maan lena.**
   *Sahi baat:* Aam oxides mein −2 hota hai, par **H₂O₂ / Na₂O₂** (peroxides) mein **−1** aur **OF₂** mein **+2** hota hai (kyunki F, O se zyada electronegative hai)!

2. **Galti 2: [BF₆]³⁻ ya NCl₅ ko valid compounds maan lena.**
   B aur N Period 2 ke tatva hain — unke paas koi 2d orbital nahi hai, isliye unki **maximum covalency 4** hai!

3. **Galti 3: CO aur NO ko "non-metal oxide = acidic oxide" bol dena.**
   CO₂ aur NO₂ acidic hain, par **CO, NO aur N₂O neutral oxides** hain!

## Quick Recap

- **Electronegativity:** Period mein badhti hai, group mein ghat-ti hai (F = 4.0 max); oxidation state ke saath badal sakti hai.
- **Oxidation state of O:** Aam taur par −2, peroxides (H₂O₂) mein −1, OF₂ mein **+2**.
- **Anomalous Period 2 (Li–F):** Chhota size, high charge/radius, high EN, aur **no d-orbitals → max covalency = 4** ([BF₄]⁻ ✓, [BF₆]³⁻ nahi; [AlF₆]³⁻ ✓).
- **Diagonal pairs:** **Li ↔ Mg**, **Be ↔ Al**, **B ↔ Si**.
- **Oxides:** Left basic (Na₂O), middle amphoteric (Al₂O₃, ZnO, BeO), right acidic (SO₃, Cl₂O₇); **neutral oxides = CO, NO, N₂O**.

## Practice Questions

1. Predict the formulas of the stable **hydride** and **highest oxide** of (a) **Germanium (Ge, Group 14)** and (b) **Arsenic (As, Group 15)** based on their group valence.
2. Explain clearly why **[AlF₆]³⁻** and **PCl₅** exist, whereas **[BF₆]³⁻** and **NCl₅** do **not** exist.
3. Find the oxidation state of Oxygen in: (a) **H₂O**, (b) **H₂O₂**, and (c) **OF₂**. Explain why Oxygen shows a positive oxidation state in OF₂.
4. Classify each of the following oxides as **basic, acidic, amphoteric, or neutral**: **Na₂O, Cl₂O₇, Al₂O₃, NO, MgO, SO₃, CO, BeO**.
5. Write two balanced chemical equations to prove that **Al₂O₃** is an amphoteric oxide. Verify the atom counts of both equations.
6. What is the **diagonal relationship** in the periodic table? Why do diagonally adjacent elements such as **Li and Mg** or **Be and Al** show similar chemical properties?
7. Give two specific chemical similarities between (a) **Li and Mg**, and (b) **Be and Al**.
8. Why does **Carbon (Period 2)** readily form strong multiple bonds (C=C, C≡C) with itself, whereas **Silicon (Period 3)** in the very same group rarely forms Si=Si double bonds?
9. Why is **Electronegativity** not a fixed constant for an element (unlike atomic number), and how does the electronegativity of Iron compare in **Fe²⁺** versus **Fe³⁺**?
10. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) The maximum covalency of any Period 2 element is 4 because its valence shell has only one 2s and three 2p orbitals.
    (b) Carbon monoxide (CO) and Nitric oxide (NO) are strongly acidic oxides.
    (c) Across Period 3 from Na₂O to Cl₂O₇, the basic character of oxides decreases and the acidic character increases.
    (d) Boron shows a diagonal relationship with Aluminium.
11. Explain why chemical reactivity **increases** down Group 1 (Li → Cs), whereas it **decreases** down Group 17 (F₂ → I₂).
12. Arrange **Na, K, Mg, Al** in **increasing order of metallic character**, and identify which of their oxides is amphoteric.

### Answers

1. (a) **Ge (Group 14, valence 4):** Hydride = **GeH₄**, Highest oxide = **GeO₂**.
   (b) **As (Group 15, valence 3 aur 5):** Hydride = **AsH₃**, Highest oxide = **As₂O₅**.
   Jaanch: Ge (+4) + 2 O (−2) = 0 ✅; 2 As (+5) + 5 O (−2) = 0 ✅.

2. B aur N **Period 2** ke tatva hain jinke valence shell (n = 2) mein sirf **4 orbitals (2s + teen 2p)** hain aur koi d-orbital nahi — isliye unki **max covalency 4** hai ([BF₆]³⁻ aur NCl₅ nahi ban sakte). Al aur P **Period 3** ke hain jinke paas **khaali 3d orbitals** hain — isliye we 5 ya 6 bonds bana kar [AlF₆]³⁻ aur PCl₅ bana lete hain.

3. (a) **H₂O:** O = **−2**.
   (b) **H₂O₂:** O = **−1** (peroxide bond).
   (c) **OF₂:** O = **+2** (kyunki F ki EN 4.0 > O ki EN 3.5, isliye dono F electron kheench lete hain).
   Jaanch: OF₂ mein (+2) + 2(−1) = 0 ✅.

4. **Basic:** Na₂O, MgO. **Acidic:** Cl₂O₇, SO₃. **Amphoteric:** Al₂O₃, BeO. **Neutral:** NO, CO.

5. Acid ke saath: **Al₂O₃ + 6HCl → 2AlCl₃ + 3H₂O** (Jaanch: Al 2=2, O 3=3, H 6=6, Cl 6=6 ✅).
   Base ke saath: **Al₂O₃ + 2NaOH → 2NaAlO₂ + H₂O** (Jaanch: Al 2=2, O 3+2=5=4+1, Na 2=2, H 2=2 ✅).

6. Period 2 ka tatva Period 3 mein apne neeche-daayein (diagonal) tatva se milte-julte gun dikhata hai. Baayein se daayein size ghatta hai aur upar se neeche badhta hai — isliye diagonal chalne par dono ka **charge-to-radius ratio (polarising power)** aur electronegativity lagbhag barabar ho jaati hai.

7. (a) **Li aur Mg:** Dono hawa ki N₂ se seedha nitride (Li₃N, Mg₃N₂) banate hain; dono ke carbonates garam karne par oxide + CO₂ mein toot jaate hain.
   (b) **Be aur Al:** Dono ke oxides (BeO, Al₂O₃) amphoteric hain; dono covalent chlorides (BeCl₂, AlCl₃) banate hain.

8. Carbon ka atomic size bahut chhota hai, isliye do Carbon atoms ke 2p orbitals side-by-side achhe se overlap karke majboot **pπ–pπ** double/triple bonds bana lete hain. Silicon bada atom hai jiske 3p orbitals door hone se sidewise overlap kamzor pad jaata hai.

9. Electronegativity kisi atom ke chemical environment (oxidation state, bonding) par nirbhar karti hai. **Fe³⁺** par zyada positive charge aur chhota size hone se wo electrons ko zyada zor se kheenchta hai, isliye **EN(Fe³⁺) > EN(Fe²⁺)**.

10. (a) **True** ✅. 2s (1) + 2p (3) = 4 orbitals → max covalency 4.
    (b) **False** ❌. CO aur NO **neutral oxides** hain.
    (c) **True** ✅. Na₂O (basic) → Al₂O₃ (amphoteric) → Cl₂O₇ (strongly acidic).
    (d) **False** ❌. Boron ka diagonal partner **Silicon (Si)** hai (Aluminium, Beryllium ka diagonal partner hai!).

11. Group 1 (alkali metals) electron **khokar** react karte hain — neeche jaane par size badhne se ΔᵢH ghat-ti hai, isliye reactivity **badhti** hai (Li < Na < K < Rb < Cs). Group 17 (halogens) electron **lekar** react karte hain — neeche jaane par size badhne se electron kheenchne ki taakat (EN) ghat-ti hai, isliye reactivity **ghat-ti** hai (F₂ > Cl₂ > Br₂ > I₂).

12. Increasing metallic character (right to left, top to bottom): **Al < Mg < Na < K**. Inmein **Al₂O₃** amphoteric oxide hai!
    Jaanch: K (Period 4, G1) sabse zyada metallic aur Al (Period 3, G13) sabse kam ✅.
