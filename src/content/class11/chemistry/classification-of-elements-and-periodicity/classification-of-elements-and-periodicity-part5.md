## Part B: Jasmeet Ke Teen Olympiad Cards (Solved Examples)

### Example 1 (Card 1). Z = 115 ka Poora Periodic Profile

**Sawaal:** Element **Z = 115** ke liye batao: (a) IUPAC systematic name aur symbol, (b) Period, Block aur Group, (c) outer electronic configuration, aur (d) expected valence aur oxide formulas.

**Hal:**
- (a) 1-1-5 → un + un + pent + ium = **Ununpentium (Uup)** *(official name: Moscovium, Mc)*.
- (b) Og (118) Period 7, Group 18 par hai; 115 usse 3 kadam peeche hai → **Period 7, p-block, Group 15** (Nitrogen family!).
- (c) Outer configuration: **[Rn] 5f¹⁴ 6d¹⁰ 7s² 7p³**.
- (d) Group 15 ki general valence **3 aur 5** hoti hai → expected oxides **X₂O₃** aur **X₂O₅** (ya halides XCl₃).

**Jaanch:** 86 (Rn) + 14 + 10 + 2 + 3 = 115 ✅; Group = 10 + (2 + 3) = 15 ✅.

---

### Example 2 (Card 2). Successive IE Jump se Tatva ki Pehchaan aur Diagonal Partner

**Sawaal:** Period 3 ke ek tatva **M** ki successive ionisation enthalpies hain: **ΔᵢH₁ = 578**, **ΔᵢH₂ = 1817**, **ΔᵢH₃ = 2745**, **ΔᵢH₄ = 11577 kJ/mol**.
(a) M ka Group aur naam pehchano.
(b) M ke oxide ka formula aur prakriti (acidic/basic/amphoteric) batao.
(c) Kya M **[MF₆]³⁻** bana sakta hai? Iska diagonal partner kaun hai?

**Hal:**
- (a) ΔᵢH₃ (2745) se ΔᵢH₄ (11577) par **4.2 guna ki badi chhalaang** hai → 3 valence electrons nikalne ke baad [Ne] core aa gaya! Period 3 + Group 13 = **Aluminium (Al, Z = 13)**.
- (b) Oxide = **Al₂O₃**, jo prakriti mein **amphoteric** hai.
- (c) Haan! Al Period 3 ka hai aur iske paas khaali **3d orbitals** hain, isliye **[AlF₆]³⁻** banta hai! Iska diagonal partner **Beryllium (Be)** hai.

**Jaanch:** 11577 ÷ 2745 = 4.22 (sabse bada jump 3rd ke baad) → 3 valence electrons → Group 13 ✅.

---

### Example 3 (Card 3). Chaar Non-Metals (O, S, F, Cl) — Chaar Alag Trends Ek Table Mein!

**Sawaal:** **O, S, F aur Cl** ko in chaar gunon ke **badhte hue kram (increasing order)** mein lagao:
(a) Atomic radius   (b) First ionisation enthalpy (ΔᵢH₁)   (c) Negative electron gain enthalpy (kam se zyada negative)   (d) Pauling Electronegativity

**Hal:**
- **(a) Atomic radius:** Period 2 chhota, Period 3 bada; daayein chhota → **F < O < Cl < S**.
- **(b) ΔᵢH₁:** Chhota size + high Z_eff → **S < Cl < O < F** *(S 1000 < Cl 1251 < O 1314 < F 1681 kJ/mol)*.
- **(c) ΔₑgH (kam negative se sabse zyada negative):** 2p repulsion ke kaaran Period 3 aage nikal jaata hai → **O (−141) < S (−200) < F (−328) < Cl (−349)** kJ/mol!
- **(d) Electronegativity:** **S (2.5) < Cl (3.0) < O (3.5) < F (4.0)**.

**Jaanch:** Dhyan do — Electronegativity mein **F (4.0)** sabse upar hai, jabki Electron Gain Enthalpy mein **Cl (−349)** sabse zyada negative hai ✅!

### Example 4 (Card 4). Isoelectronic Radius + ΔᵢH₂ Flip + Amphoteric/Neutral Oxide Combo

**Sawaal:**
(a) **O²⁻, F⁻, Na⁺, Mg²⁺** ko ghat-te ionic radius mein lagao.
(b) **Na aur Mg** mein kiski **ΔᵢH₂** zyada hai, aur **N aur O** mein kiski **ΔᵢH₂** zyada hai?
(c) **CO, BeO, SO₃, K₂O** mein se har ek oxide ki prakriti batao.

**Hal:**
- (a) 10 e⁻ isoelectronic; kam Z → bada radius: **O²⁻ (8) > F⁻ (9) > Na⁺ (11) > Mg²⁺ (12)**.
- (b) **ΔᵢH₂(Na) >> ΔᵢH₂(Mg)** (kyunki Na⁺ = stable [Ne] core!); **ΔᵢH₂(O) > ΔᵢH₂(N)** (kyunki O⁺ = half-filled 2p³!).
- (c) **CO** = Neutral; **BeO** = Amphoteric; **SO₃** = Acidic; **K₂O** = Strongly basic.

**Jaanch:** Dono ΔᵢH₂ flips stable noble-gas/half-filled core se aate hain ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: Ek hi tatva ke alag cations (jaise Fe²⁺ aur Fe³⁺) ka radius barabar maan lena.**
   *Sahi baat:* Dono mein protons same (Z = 26) hain, par **Fe³⁺** mein ek electron kam (23 e⁻ vs 24 e⁻) hone se bache electrons par khinchaav zyada hota hai → **r(Fe³⁺) < r(Fe²⁺) < r(Fe)**!

2. **Galti 2: Group number bhool kar Period number se chemical formula predict kar dena.**
   *Sahi baat:* Chemical formulas (oxide, hydride, chloride) **Group (valence electrons)** se tay hote hain, Period se nahi — Period sirf shell size aur d-orbital availability (covalency 4 vs 6) tay karta hai!

---

## Quick Recap

- **Z se poora profile:** Noble-gas milestones (2, 10, 18, 36, 54, 86, 118) se turant Period, Block, Group aur oxide/halide formula milta hai.
- **Successive IE jump** seedha valence electrons aur Group number batata hai.
- **Same element ions:** Positive charge badhne par ionic radius lagatar ghat-ta hai (r(M³⁺) < r(M²⁺) < r(M⁺) < r(M)).

## Practice Questions

1. For the superheavy element **Z = 116** (Livermorium):
   (a) Write its IUPAC systematic name and 3-letter symbol.
   (b) Predict its Period, Block, Group and outer electronic configuration.
   (c) Predict the formula of its simple hydride (like H₂O / H₂S).
2. If element **Z = 119** is discovered, predict its IUPAC name, Period, Block, Group, the formula of its oxide, and whether that oxide will be strongly basic or acidic.
3. A Period 3 element **X** shows successive ionisation enthalpies: **ΔᵢH₁ = 496**, **ΔᵢH₂ = 4562**, **ΔᵢH₃ = 6912 kJ/mol**. Identify X, write its electronic configuration, and state the nature of its oxide (X₂O).
4. Explain why **[SiF₆]²⁻** is a well-known stable ion, whereas **[CF₆]²⁻** does **not** exist, even though both Carbon and Silicon belong to Group 14.
5. Arrange the following in **decreasing order of radius**: (a) **Fe, Fe²⁺, Fe³⁺**; (b) **N³⁻, F⁻, Mg²⁺, Al³⁺**.
6. Compare the four Period 3 elements **P, S, Cl, Ar** in **increasing order** of (a) atomic radius (covalent for P, S, Cl; note Ar uses van der Waals radius), and (b) first ionisation enthalpy ΔᵢH₁.
7. An element **E** has outer configuration **[Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s² 4p⁵**.
   (a) Find its atomic number Z, Period, Block and Group.
   (b) Predict the formula of the compound formed between **E** and **Aluminium (Al, Group 13)**.
8. Which element of **Period 2** shows a diagonal relationship with (a) **Mg**, (b) **Al**, and (c) **Si**? State one reason common to all three diagonal pairs.
9. Between **Cl⁻** and **Ar**, both have 18 electrons. Which one has a **higher first ionisation enthalpy** (harder to remove an electron from), and why?
10. Write the oxidation state of Chlorine in **Cl₂O₇** and of Sulphur in **SO₃**, and state whether these two oxides are acidic, basic or amphoteric.
11. Identify the element from these three clues: (i) It belongs to Period 2 and Group 16; (ii) It has a less negative electron gain enthalpy than the element directly below it; (iii) It shows oxidation state +2 when combined with Fluorine.
12. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) The ionic radius of Fe³⁺ is smaller than the ionic radius of Fe²⁺.
    (b) Element Z = 114 belongs to the Carbon family (Group 14).
    (c) Between N and O, Oxygen has the higher first ionisation enthalpy (ΔᵢH₁).
    (d) All f-block elements (Lanthanoids and Actinoids) are placed in Group 3.

### Answers

1. (a) 1-1-6 → **Ununhexium (Uuh)**.
   (b) Og (118) se 2 peeche → **Period 7, p-block, Group 16**; outer configuration **[Rn] 5f¹⁴ 6d¹⁰ 7s² 7p⁴**.
   (c) Group 16 hydride formula: **H₂X** (H₂Lv).
   Jaanch: 86 + 14 + 10 + 2 + 4 = 116 ✅.

2. Z = 119 → **Ununennium (Uue)**; Og (118) ke 1 aage → **Period 8, s-block, Group 1** (alkali metal); oxide = **X₂O**, jo **strongly basic** hoga.
   Jaanch: 118 + 1 = 119 (Group 1, valence 1 → X₂O) ✅.

3. ΔᵢH₁ (496) se ΔᵢH₂ (4562) par **9.2 guna ki badi chhalaang** hai → sirf **1 valence electron** → Period 3, Group 1 = **Sodium (Na, Z = 11)**, configuration **1s² 2s² 2p⁶ 3s¹**. Iska oxide **Na₂O** strongly **basic** hota hai.
   Jaanch: 4562 ÷ 496 = 9.20 ✅.

4. Carbon Period 2 ka tatva hai jiske valence shell mein koi d-orbital nahi (max covalency = **4**, isliye [CF₆]²⁻ asambhav). Silicon Period 3 ka hai aur iske paas khaali **3d orbitals** hain, isliye ye 6 covalent bonds banakar **[SiF₆]²⁻** bana leta hai.

5. (a) **Fe > Fe²⁺ > Fe³⁺** (positive charge badhne par effective nuclear pull badhta hai aur radius ghat-ta hai).
   (b) Sabhi 10-electron isoelectronic hain; kam Z → bada radius: **N³⁻ (7) > F⁻ (9) > Mg²⁺ (12) > Al³⁺ (13)**.
   Jaanch: Z kram 7 < 9 < 12 < 13 → radius lagatar ghata ✅.

6. (a) Covalent radius: **Cl < S < P** (baayein se daayein ghatta hai; Ar ka van der Waals radius teeno se bada dikhta hai).
   (b) ΔᵢH₁: **S < P < Cl < Ar** (P ka half-filled 3p³ S se upar!).
   Jaanch: Group 15 (P) > Group 16 (S) ✅.

7. (a) Z = 18 + 10 + 2 + 5 = **35 (Bromine, Br)** → **Period 4, p-block, Group 17**.
   (b) Al (valence 3) aur Br (valence 1) → **AlBr₃** (AlE₃).
   Jaanch: 35, Kr (36) se 1 peeche → Group 17 ✅.

8. (a) **Li** (Mg ke saath), (b) **Be** (Al ke saath), (c) **B** (Si ke saath).
   Common kaaran: diagonal chalne par dono ka **charge-to-radius ratio (polarising power)** aur electronegativity lagbhag barabar ho jaati hai.

9. **Ar (Z = 18)** ki ionisation enthalpy **Cl⁻ (Z = 17)** se kaafi zyada hogi! Dono mein 18 electrons hain, par Ar ke nucleus mein 18 protons (Cl⁻ ke 17 se zyada) un electrons ko zyada kas kar pakde hue hain.

10. **Cl₂O₇** mein 2x + 7(−2) = 0 → **Cl = +7**; **SO₃** mein x + 3(−2) = 0 → **S = +6**. Dono non-metal ke high-oxidation-state oxides hain, isliye dono **strongly acidic** hain.
    Jaanch: 2(+7) − 14 = 0 ✅; (+6) − 6 = 0 ✅.

11. **Oxygen (O, Z = 8)** — Period 2 Group 16; ΔₑgH (−141) Sulphur (−200) se kam negative; aur OF₂ mein +2 oxidation state dikhata hai!

12. (a) **True** ✅. Zyada positive charge → chhota ionic radius.
    (b) **True** ✅. 118 − 4 = 114 → Group 14.
    (c) **False** ❌. Half-filled 2p³ stability ke kaaran **N (1402)** ki ΔᵢH₁, **O (1314)** se zyada hai.
    (d) **True** ✅. Lanthanoids (Period 6) aur Actinoids (Period 7) dono Group 3 mein aate hain.
