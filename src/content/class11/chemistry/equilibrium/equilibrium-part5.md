# Topic 5: Sparingly Soluble Salts, Solubility Product (K_sp), Common Ion Effect on Solubility aur Precipitation

Ab tak humne aise salts dekhe jo paani mein aasani se ghul jaate hain (NaCl, KNO₃). Par kuch salts — jaise **AgCl, BaSO₄, PbCl₂, CaF₂, Mg(OH)₂** — paani mein behad kam ghulte hain (**sparingly soluble**, solubility < 0.01 M)! Inka ek chhota sa hissa ghulkar ions banata hai aur bacha hua solid tal mein baithkar ek **heterogeneous ionic equilibrium** bana leta hai — jise **Solubility Product (K_sp)** se naapa jaata hai! Aaj hum K_sp aur molar solubility **S** ka pakka rishta, **Q_sp vs K_sp** se precipitation check, common ion ka solubility par asar aur salt-analysis mein selective precipitation seekhenge.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Sparingly soluble salt** | Aisa ionic solid jo paani mein bahut hi kam matra (< 0.01 M) mein ghulta hai | AgCl, BaSO₄, PbCl₂, CaF₂, Ag₂CrO₄ |
| **Molar solubility (S)** | Constant T par 1 litre saturated solution mein ghulne wale salt ke **moles** (unit: **mol/L**) | AgCl ke liye S ≈ 1.26 × 10⁻⁵ mol/L |
| **Solubility Product (K_sp)** | Saturated solution mein sparingly soluble salt ke ions ki concentrations (stoichiometric powers par) ka product (sirf **T** par nirbhar!) | AgCl(s) ⇌ Ag⁺ + Cl⁻ → K_sp = [Ag⁺][Cl⁻] |
| **Ionic Product (Q_sp)** | Kisi bhi haalat mein (chahe saturated ho ya na ho!) ions ki concentrations ka product | **Q_sp > K_sp → Precipitate banta hai!** |

## Part A: K_sp aur Molar Solubility (S) ka General Formula (Master Table!)

Kisi bhi sparingly soluble salt **A_x B_y(s) ⇌ x A^(y+)(aq) + y B^(x−)(aq)** ke liye (pure solid ka active mass = 1!):
- Agar molar solubility = **S** mol/L ho, to equilibrium par **[A^(y+)] = xS** aur **[B^(x−)] = yS**.
- Isliye: **K_sp = (xS)^x × (yS)^y = x^x · y^y · S^(x+y)**!

| Salt Type | Dissociation Equilibrium | Ion Concentrations | **K_sp Formula (S ke roop mein)** | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **AB (1:1)** | AgCl(s) ⇌ Ag⁺ + Cl⁻ | S, S | **K_sp = S²** | AgCl, BaSO₄, CuS, ZnS |
| **AB₂ ya A₂B (1:2 / 2:1)** | PbCl₂(s) ⇌ Pb²⁺ + 2Cl⁻ | S, 2S | **K_sp = (S)(2S)² = 4S³** | PbCl₂, CaF₂, Mg(OH)₂, Ag₂CrO₄ |
| **AB₃ ya A₃B (1:3)** | Al(OH)₃(s) ⇌ Al³⁺ + 3OH⁻ | S, 3S | **K_sp = (S)(3S)³ = 27S⁴** | Al(OH)₃, Fe(OH)₃ |
| **A₂B₃ ya A₃B₂ (2:3)** | Ca₃(PO₄)₂(s) ⇌ 3Ca²⁺ + 2PO₄³⁻ | 3S, 2S | **K_sp = (3S)³(2S)² = 108S⁵** | Ca₃(PO₄)₂, Bi₂S₃ |

### Example 1. AgCl (AB) aur PbCl₂ (AB₂) ki Molar Solubility (S) Nikaalna

*(Chetavani: Silver aur Lead salts toxic hain — sirf paper calculation!)*

**Sawaal:** 298 K par pure water mein molar solubility **S** (mol/L mein) nikaalo:
(a) **AgCl** (K_sp = **1.6 × 10⁻¹⁰**)
(b) **PbCl₂** (K_sp = **3.2 × 10⁻⁵**)

**Hal:**
- (a) **AgCl (AB type → K_sp = S²):**
  - S² = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁰ → **S = √(1.6 × 10⁻¹⁰) = 1.26 × 10⁻⁵ mol/L**!
- (b) **PbCl₂ (AB₂ type → K_sp = 4S³):**
  - 4S³ = 3.2 × 10⁻⁵ → S³ = 0.8 × 10⁻⁵ = **8.0 × 10⁻⁶**
  - **S = (8.0 × 10⁻⁶)^(1/3) = 2.0 × 10⁻² mol/L = 0.020 M**!

**Jaanch:** AgCl: (1.265 × 10⁻⁵)² = 1.60 × 10⁻¹⁰ ✅; PbCl₂: 4 × (0.020)³ = 4 × 8 × 10⁻⁶ = 3.2 × 10⁻⁵ ✅.

## Part B: Common Ion Effect on Solubility aur Precipitation Criterion (Q_sp vs K_sp)

### 1. Common Ion Effect: Solubility Tezi se Ghat Jaati Hai!
Agar tum **AgCl** ko pure water ke bajay **0.10 M NaCl** (common ion **Cl⁻ = 0.10 M**) mein gholo, to Le Chatelier se **AgCl(s) ⇌ Ag⁺ + Cl⁻** peeche (reverse) khisak jaata hai aur AgCl ki solubility hazaaron guna kam ho jaati hai! *(Yaad rakho: **K_sp** wahi rehta hai, sirf **S** ghat-ta hai!)*

### Example 2. 0.10 M NaCl mein AgCl ki Solubility (Common Ion Effect!)

**Sawaal:** AgCl (K_sp = **1.6 × 10⁻¹⁰**) ki molar solubility **S'** **0.10 M NaCl** solution mein nikaalo aur pure water (1.26 × 10⁻⁵ M) se tulna karo!

**Hal:**
- Maan lo 0.10 M NaCl mein AgCl ki solubility = **S'** mol/L hai.
- Equilibrium par: **[Ag⁺] = S'**, **[Cl⁻] = 0.10 + S' ≈ 0.10 M** *(kyunki S' << 0.10)*.
- K_sp = [Ag⁺][Cl⁻] → 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁰ = S' × 0.10
- **S' = 1.6 × 10⁻⁹ mol/L**! *(Pure water ke muqable solubility lagbhag **7900 guna ghat** gayi!)*

**Jaanch:** (1.6 × 10⁻⁹) × 0.10 = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁰ = K_sp ✅.

---

### 2. Precipitation Check: Q_sp banaam K_sp (3 Pakke Cases!)
- **Q_sp < K_sp:** Solution **unsaturated** hai → **koi precipitate nahi** banega (aur salt ghul sakta hai).
- **Q_sp = K_sp:** Solution **exact saturated** hai (equilibrium — precipitation ki boundary).
- **Q_sp > K_sp:** Solution **supersaturated** hai → **PRECIPITATE BANEGA!** *(Jab tak Q_sp ghatkar K_sp ke barabar na ho jaaye!)*

### Example 3. Do Equal Volumes Milane par Precipitation Check (Dilution Trap!)

**Sawaal:** **100 mL of 2.0 × 10⁻⁴ M BaCl₂** aur **100 mL of 2.0 × 10⁻⁴ M Na₂SO₄** ko milaya gaya *(Barium salts toxic: sirf paper calculation!)*. Kya **BaSO₄** (K_sp = **1.1 × 10⁻¹⁰**) ka safed precipitate banega?

**Hal:**
- **STEP 1 (SABSE ZAROORI — Volume Dugna hua = Concentrations AADHI hongi!):**
  - Total volume = 100 + 100 = **200 mL** (2× dilution!).
  - Mixing ke baad: **[Ba²⁺] = (2.0 × 10⁻⁴) ÷ 2 = 1.0 × 10⁻⁴ M**; **[SO₄²⁻] = (2.0 × 10⁻⁴) ÷ 2 = 1.0 × 10⁻⁴ M**.
- **STEP 2 (Q_sp nikalo):**
  - Q_sp = [Ba²⁺][SO₄²⁻] = (1.0 × 10⁻⁴) × (1.0 × 10⁻⁴) = **1.0 × 10⁻⁸**.
- **STEP 3 (K_sp se tulna):**
  - **Q_sp (1.0 × 10⁻⁸) > K_sp (1.1 × 10⁻¹⁰)** (lagbhag 91 guna bada!) → **HAAN, BaSO₄ ka safed precipitate pakka banega!**

**Jaanch:** 1.0 × 10⁻⁸ > 1.1 × 10⁻¹⁰ → precipitate forms ✅.

### Selective Precipitation (Qualitative Salt Analysis mein Upyog!)
Lab ke Group II cations (**Cu²⁺, Pb²⁺**, K_sp of CuS bahut chhota ~10⁻³⁶) aur Group IV cations (**Zn²⁺, Ni²⁺**, K_sp of ZnS bada ~10⁻²¹) ko alag karne ke liye **H₂S gas** *(zehreeli gas: sirf teacher demo/paper!)* pass karte waqt Group II mein pehle **dilute HCl** milaya jaata hai! **Kyun?** HCl ke common ion **H⁺** se H₂S (H₂S ⇌ 2H⁺ + S²⁻) ka dissociation dab jaata hai → **[S²⁻] bahut kam** rehta hai — itna kam ki sirf **CuS** (bahut chhota K_sp) ka Q_sp > K_sp hokar precipitate banta hai, jabki **ZnS** nahi girta!

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: Har salt ke liye K_sp = S² laga dena!**
   *Sahi baat:* K_sp = S² sirf **AB (1:1)** salts (AgCl, BaSO₄) ke liye hai! **PbCl₂, CaF₂, Mg(OH)₂, Ag₂CrO₄** (AB₂ ya A₂B) ke liye **K_sp = 4S³**, aur **Al(OH)₃** ke liye **27S⁴** hota hai!

2. **Galti 2: Do barabar volumes milate waqt concentrations ko AADHA (÷ 2) karna bhool jaana!**
   100 mL + 100 mL = 200 mL hone par har ion ki concentration **aadhi** ho jaati hai — bina aadha kiye Q_sp **4 guna galat** aata hai!

3. **Galti 3: Alag stoichiometry wale salts (AB vs A₂B) mein seedha bada K_sp dekh kar "zyada soluble" bol dena!**
   Misal: AgCl (AB, K_sp = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁰) ka S = **1.26 × 10⁻⁵ M**, jabki Ag₂CrO₄ (A₂B, chhota K_sp = 4.0 × 10⁻¹²) ka S = **1.0 × 10⁻⁴ M** (AgCl se **8 guna zyada soluble!**)! Hamesha **S** nikalkar tulna karo, sirf K_sp dekhkar nahi!

---

## Quick Recap

- **A_x B_y:** **K_sp = x^x y^y S^(x+y)** → AB: **S²**; AB₂/A₂B: **4S³**; AB₃: **27S⁴**; A₂B₃: **108S⁵**.
- **Common Ion Effect:** Solubility **S ghat-ti hai**, par **K_sp nahi badalta** (K_sp sirf T par nirbhar!).
- **Precipitation:** **Q_sp > K_sp → Precipitate**; equal volumes milane par concentrations **aadhi** karo!
- **Group II Analysis:** HCl ka common ion H⁺ → [S²⁻] kam → sirf low-K_sp sulphides (CuS) precipitate hote hain!

## Practice Questions

1. Write the **K_sp expression** and the **relationship between K_sp and molar solubility (S)** for: (a) **BaSO₄**, (b) **CaF₂**, (c) **Ag₂CrO₄**, (d) **Al(OH)₃**, (e) **Ca₃(PO₄)₂**.
2. The solubility product of **BaSO₄** at 298 K is **1.0 × 10⁻¹⁰**. Calculate its molar solubility **S** in pure water (in mol/L and g/L; molar mass of BaSO₄ = 233 g/mol). *(Barium salts: paper problem only.)*
3. Calculate the molar solubility **S** of **CaF₂** in pure water at 298 K, given **K_sp(CaF₂) = 4.0 × 10⁻¹²**.
4. The molar solubility of **Ag₂CrO₄** in pure water at 298 K is **1.0 × 10⁻⁴ mol/L**. Calculate its **K_sp**. *(Toxic chromate: paper problem only.)*
5. Calculate the molar solubility of **BaSO₄** (K_sp = **1.0 × 10⁻¹⁰**) in a **0.010 M Na₂SO₄** solution. By what factor does the solubility decrease compared to pure water?
6. Equal volumes of **2.0 × 10⁻⁵ M AgNO₃** and **2.0 × 10⁻⁵ M NaCl** are mixed at 298 K. Will a precipitate of **AgCl** (**K_sp = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁰**) form? Show your dilution step clearly!
7. Equal volumes of **2.0 × 10⁻⁶ M AgNO₃** and **2.0 × 10⁻⁶ M NaCl** are mixed at 298 K. Will **AgCl** (**K_sp = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁰**) precipitate in this case?
8. Compare **AgCl** (K_sp = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁰) and **Ag₂CrO₄** (K_sp = 4.0 × 10⁻¹²). Even though AgCl has a **40 times larger K_sp**, prove mathematically that **Ag₂CrO₄ has a higher molar solubility (S)** in pure water!
9. In qualitative inorganic analysis, why is **dilute HCl** added before passing **H₂S gas** to precipitate Group II cations (like Cu²⁺ as CuS) without precipitating Group IV cations (like Zn²⁺ as ZnS)? *(H₂S is toxic: paper problem only.)*
10. Why does pure **NaCl** precipitate out when **HCl gas** is bubbled through a saturated aqueous solution of impure common salt (brine)? *(Teacher demo / industrial purification only.)*
11. If the solubility of **Mg(OH)₂** in pure water is **1.0 × 10⁻⁴ mol/L**, calculate (a) its **K_sp**, and (b) the **pH** of its saturated aqueous solution at 298 K. *(log 2 = 0.3010.)*
12. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) Adding a common ion decreases both the molar solubility S and the solubility product K_sp of a sparingly soluble salt.
    (b) Precipitation occurs only when the ionic product Q_sp exceeds K_sp.
    (c) For Fe(OH)₃, K_sp = 27 S⁴.
    (d) When two equal volumes of solutions are mixed, the concentration of every ion becomes double.

### Answers

1. (a) **BaSO₄:** K_sp = [Ba²⁺][SO₄²⁻] = **S²**.
   (b) **CaF₂:** K_sp = [Ca²⁺][F⁻]² = **4S³**.
   (c) **Ag₂CrO₄:** K_sp = [Ag⁺]²[CrO₄²⁻] = **4S³**.
   (d) **Al(OH)₃:** K_sp = [Al³⁺][OH⁻]³ = **27S⁴**.
   (e) **Ca₃(PO₄)₂:** K_sp = [Ca²⁺]³[PO₄³⁻]² = **108S⁵**.

2. BaSO₄ (AB): S² = 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁰ → **S = 1.0 × 10⁻⁵ mol/L**.
   g/L mein: 1.0 × 10⁻⁵ × 233 = **2.33 × 10⁻³ g/L**.
   Jaanch: (10⁻⁵)² = 10⁻¹⁰ = K_sp ✅.

3. CaF₂ (AB₂): 4S³ = 4.0 × 10⁻¹² → S³ = 1.0 × 10⁻¹² → **S = 1.0 × 10⁻⁴ mol/L**.
   Jaanch: 4 × (10⁻⁴)³ = 4.0 × 10⁻¹² ✅.

4. Ag₂CrO₄ (A₂B): K_sp = 4S³ = 4 × (1.0 × 10⁻⁴)³ = **4.0 × 10⁻¹²**.
   Jaanch: 4 × 10⁻¹² = 4.0 × 10⁻¹² ✅.

5. 0.010 M Na₂SO₄ mein [SO₄²⁻] ≈ 0.010 M:
   S' × 0.010 = 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁰ → **S' = 1.0 × 10⁻⁸ mol/L**.
   Pure water (10⁻⁵ M) ke muqable solubility **1000 guna (10³ times) ghat** gayi!
   Jaanch: 10⁻⁸ × 10⁻² = 10⁻¹⁰ ✅.

6. Equal volumes milane par concentrations aadhi: [Ag⁺] = **1.0 × 10⁻⁵ M**, [Cl⁻] = **1.0 × 10⁻⁵ M**.
   Q_sp = (1.0 × 10⁻⁵)(1.0 × 10⁻⁵) = **1.0 × 10⁻¹⁰**.
   Yahan **Q_sp (1.0 × 10⁻¹⁰) < K_sp (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁰)** → **NAHI, koi precipitate nahi banega!**
   Jaanch: 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁰ < 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁰ → no precipitate ✅.

7. Concentrations aadhi: [Ag⁺] = [Cl⁻] = 1.0 × 10⁻⁶ M → Q_sp = **1.0 × 10⁻¹²** << K_sp (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁰) → **NAHI, koi precipitate nahi banega!**

8. **AgCl:** S = √(1.6 × 10⁻¹⁰) = **1.26 × 10⁻⁵ M**.
   **Ag₂CrO₄:** 4S³ = 4.0 × 10⁻¹² → S = **1.0 × 10⁻⁴ M** (= 10 × 10⁻⁵ M!).
   Isliye chhota K_sp hone ke baavajood **Ag₂CrO₄ lagbhag 8 guna zyada soluble** hai!
   Jaanch: 10 × 10⁻⁵ > 1.26 × 10⁻⁵ ✅.

9. HCl strong acid hai jo common ion **H⁺** dekar H₂S (H₂S ⇌ 2H⁺ + S²⁻) ke dissociation ko dabaa deta hai → **[S²⁻] bahut kam** ho jaata hai. Itne kam [S²⁻] par sirf **CuS** (behad chhota K_sp) ka Q_sp > K_sp hota hai aur wo precipitate hota hai, jabki ZnS ghol mein hi rehta hai!

10. Saturated brine mein **NaCl(s) ⇌ Na⁺ + Cl⁻** equilibrium par hota hai. HCl gas pass karne par common ion **[Cl⁻]** tezi se badhta hai → Q_sp > K_sp → **shuddh NaCl precipitate** hokar neeche baith jaata hai (impurities ghol mein reh jaati hain)!

11. (a) Mg(OH)₂ (AB₂): K_sp = 4S³ = 4 × (1.0 × 10⁻⁴)³ = **4.0 × 10⁻¹²**.
    (b) [OH⁻] = 2S = **2.0 × 10⁻⁴ M** → pOH = 4 − log 2 = 4 − 0.301 = **3.70** → **pH = 14.00 − 3.70 = 10.30**!
    Jaanch: 3.70 + 10.30 = 14.00 ✅.

12. (a) **False** ❌. Common ion sirf **solubility S** ko ghatata hai; **K_sp constant** rehta hai (sirf T par nirbhar!).
    (b) **True** ✅.
    (c) **True** ✅. (S)(3S)³ = 27S⁴.
    (d) **False** ❌. Equal volumes milane par har ion ki concentration **aadhi (half)** ho jaati hai!
