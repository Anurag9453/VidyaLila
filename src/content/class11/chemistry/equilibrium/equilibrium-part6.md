## Mixed Worked Examples

### Mixed Example 1 (Station 1). ICE Table + K_c + K_p + Le Chatelier (PCl₅ Dissociation)

*(Chetavani: PCl₅, PCl₃ aur Cl₂ corrosive/toxic gases hain — sirf paper calculation!)*

**Sawaal:** **500 K** par **1.00 M PCl₅(g)** ko ek band vessel mein rakha gaya: **PCl₅(g) ⇌ PCl₃(g) + Cl₂(g)** (ΔH = +92.5 kJ). Equilibrium par PCl₅ ka **20% (α = 0.20)** dissociate hota hai.
(a) **K_c** aur **K_p** (bar mein, R = 0.0831 L·bar K⁻¹ mol⁻¹) nikaalo.
(b) Pressure badhane aur Temperature badhane ka asar batao.

**Hal:**
- **(a) ICE Table:** [PCl₅] = 1.00 − 0.20 = **0.80 M**; [PCl₃] = **0.20 M**; [Cl₂] = **0.20 M**.
  - **K_c** = (0.20 × 0.20) ÷ 0.80 = 0.04 ÷ 0.80 = **0.050 mol L⁻¹**.
  - Δn_g = (1 + 1) − 1 = **+1** → **K_p** = K_c (RT)¹ = 0.050 × (0.0831 × 500) = 0.050 × 41.55 = **2.08 bar**!
- **(b) Le Chatelier:**
  - **Pressure badhane par:** Kam gas moles (1 mol PCl₅) → **REVERSE** shift!
  - **Temperature badhane par:** Endothermic (ΔH > 0) → **FORWARD** shift (aur K_c, K_p badhenge!).

**Jaanch:** 0.04 ÷ 0.80 = 0.05; 0.05 × 41.55 = 2.0775 ≈ 2.08 bar ✅.

### Mixed Example 2 (Station 2). Ek Hi Acid (HCOOH) ke 3 Solutions — Weak Acid, Salt aur Buffer!

**Sawaal:** Formic acid (**HCOOH**) ka **pK_a = 3.74** (K_a = **1.8 × 10⁻⁴**) hai. 298 K par teeno solutions ka pH nikaalo:
(a) **0.10 M HCOOH** (Weak acid alone) *(log 4.24 = 0.627)*
(b) **0.10 M HCOONa** (Salt of WA + SB)
(c) **Buffer:** **0.10 M HCOOH + 0.10 M HCOONa**

**Hal:**
- **(a) Weak Acid (Ostwald Law):** [H⁺] = √(K_a × c) = √(1.8 × 10⁻⁴ × 0.10) = √(1.8 × 10⁻⁵) = **4.24 × 10⁻³ M**.
  - **pH = −log(4.24 × 10⁻³) = 3 − 0.627 = 2.37** (acidic!).
- **(b) Salt Hydrolysis (WA + SB):** log(0.10) = −1.
  - **pH = 7 + ½(3.74) + ½(−1) = 7 + 1.87 − 0.50 = 8.37** (basic!).
- **(c) Acidic Buffer (Henderson–Hasselbalch):** [Salt] = [Acid] = 0.10 M → log(1) = 0!
  - **pH = pK_a + 0 = 3.74** (maximum buffer capacity!).

**Jaanch:** Pure acid (2.37) < Buffer (3.74) < Salt (8.37) — kram 100% logical hai ✅!

### Mixed Example 3 (Station 3). K_sp + pH + Common Ion (Mg(OH)₂ in Pure Water vs pH = 12 Buffer!)

**Sawaal:** **Mg(OH)₂** ka **K_sp = 4.0 × 10⁻¹²** hai.
(a) Pure water mein iski molar solubility **S** aur saturated solution ka **pH** nikaalo. *(log 2 = 0.3010.)*
(b) Ek **pH = 12.0** wale buffer solution mein Mg(OH)₂ ki nayi solubility **S'** nikaalo!

**Hal:**
- **(a) Pure Water:** Mg(OH)₂ ⇌ Mg²⁺ + 2OH⁻ → 4S³ = 4.0 × 10⁻¹² → **S = 1.0 × 10⁻⁴ M**.
  - [OH⁻] = 2S = 2.0 × 10⁻⁴ M → pOH = 4 − 0.301 = 3.70 → **pH = 14 − 3.70 = 10.30**.
- **(b) pH = 12.0 Buffer (Common Ion OH⁻!):**
  - pOH = 14 − 12 = 2.0 → **[OH⁻] = 1.0 × 10⁻² M** (buffer dwara fixed!).
  - K_sp = [Mg²⁺][OH⁻]² → 4.0 × 10⁻¹² = S' × (1.0 × 10⁻²)² = S' × 10⁻⁴
  - **S' = 4.0 × 10⁻⁸ mol/L** *(solubility 2500 guna kam ho gayi!)*

**Jaanch:** (4.0 × 10⁻⁸) × (10⁻²)² = 4.0 × 10⁻¹² = K_sp ✅.

---

### Mixed Example 4 (Station 4). Thermodynamics (Δ_rG°) → K → Reaction Quotient (Q)

**Sawaal:** 298 K par kisi reaction ka **Δ_rG° = −11.41 kJ mol⁻¹** hai (2.303 RT = 5.706 kJ/mol).
(a) Equilibrium constant **K** nikaalo.
(b) Agar kisi pal reaction quotient **Q = 25** hai, to reaction kis disha mein chalega?

**Hal:**
- (a) Δ_rG° = −2.303 RT log K → −11.41 = −5.706 × log K → log K = **2.00** → **K = 100**.
- (b) **Q (25) < K (100)** → reaction **FORWARD (products ki taraf)** chalega!

**Jaanch:** −5.706 × log(100) = −11.412 kJ/mol; Q < K → forward ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (mixed problems mein bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: Buffer solution mein paani milane (dilution) par uska pH badal jaana maan lena.**
   *Sahi baat:* Paani milane par [Salt] aur [Acid] **dono ek hi factor se aadhe/kam** hote hain, isliye unka ratio **[Salt] / [Acid] wahi rehta hai** — aur Henderson–Hasselbalch (pH = pK_a + log([Salt]/[Acid])) se **buffer ka pH lagbhag nahi badalta!**

2. **Galti 2: Ek hi sawaal mein K_a, K_b, K_h aur K_sp ko aapas mein mila dena.**
   Hamesha pehle compound pehchano: **Weak acid** → K_a; **Weak base** → K_b; **Salt alone** → K_h (hydrolysis); **Weak acid/base + uska salt** → Henderson–Hasselbalch (buffer); **Sparingly soluble solid** → K_sp!

## Practice Questions (Sab Kuch Mila Kar — 12 Fresh Questions)

1. For **2NH₃(g) ⇌ N₂(g) + 3H₂(g)** at **500 K**, **K_c = 1.0 × 10⁻⁴ (mol/L)²**. Calculate **K_p** in bar². *(R = 0.0831 L·bar K⁻¹ mol⁻¹.)*
2. For the gas-phase equilibrium **A(g) ⇌ B(g) + C(g)**, starting with **0.50 M A** in a closed flask, **40%** of A dissociates at equilibrium. Calculate **K_c**.
3. Consider the endothermic heterogeneous equilibrium: **CaCO₃(s) ⇌ CaO(s) + CO₂(g)** (ΔH = +178 kJ). State the effect on equilibrium of: (a) increasing temperature, (b) decreasing the volume of the container, (c) adding more solid CaO.
4. Calculate the **pH** of **1.0 × 10⁻⁸ M NaOH** at 298 K. *(Hint: include 10⁻⁷ M OH⁻ from water! log 1.1 = 0.04; exact pH ≈ 7.02–7.04.)*
5. Identify the **Lewis acid**, **Lewis base**, and **Brønsted–Lowry conjugate acid of NH₃** in the two reactions:
   (i) **Ag⁺ + 2:NH₃ → [Ag(NH₃)₂]⁺**
   (ii) **NH₃ + H₂O ⇌ NH₄⁺ + OH⁻**
6. A buffer is made by mixing **0.25 M CH₃COOH** and **0.25 M CH₃COONa** (**pK_a = 4.76**).
   (a) What is its pH?
   (b) If this buffer is diluted with an equal volume of pure water, what happens to its pH? Explain!
7. Calculate the molar solubility **S'** of **SrF₂** (**K_sp = 2.5 × 10⁻⁹**) in a **0.10 M NaF** solution.
8. Calculate the **pH** of an aqueous solution of **ammonium cyanide (NH₄CN)** at 298 K, given **pK_a(HCN) = 9.30** and **pK_b(NH₃) = 4.74**. Is the solution acidic or basic? *(Toxic salt: paper problem only.)*
9. At 298 K, a reaction has **Δ_rG° = +5.71 kJ mol⁻¹** (2.303 RT = 5.706 kJ/mol). Calculate **K**, and predict the reaction direction if at some instant **Q = 0.50**.
10. A **0.10 M** solution of a weak monobasic acid HA is **1.0% ionised** (α = 0.010). Calculate (a) **[H₃O⁺]**, (b) **pH**, (c) **K_a**, and (d) **K_b** of its conjugate base **A⁻** at 298 K.
11. Equal volumes of **4.0 × 10⁻⁴ M CaCl₂** and **4.0 × 10⁻⁴ M Na₂SO₄** are mixed at 298 K. Will **CaSO₄** (**K_sp = 2.4 × 10⁻⁵**) precipitate? Show your Q_sp calculation after dilution.
12. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) Diluting a buffer solution with an equal volume of water cuts its pH in half.
    (b) For a reaction with Δn_g = 0, K_p is always equal to K_c.
    (c) An aqueous solution of FeCl₃ is acidic due to cation hydrolysis.
    (d) Adding a catalyst increases the equilibrium yield of ammonia in the Haber process.

### Answers

1. Δn_g = (1 + 3) − 2 = **+2**.
   RT = 0.0831 × 500 = **41.55** → (RT)² = **1726.4**.
   K_p = K_c (RT)² = (1.0 × 10⁻⁴) × 1726.4 = **0.173 bar²**.
   Jaanch: 41.55² = 1726.4; × 10⁻⁴ = 0.1726 bar² ✅.

2. 40% of 0.50 M = **0.20 M** dissociates:
   [A] = 0.50 − 0.20 = **0.30 M**; [B] = **0.20 M**; [C] = **0.20 M**.
   K_c = (0.20 × 0.20) ÷ 0.30 = 0.04 ÷ 0.30 = **0.133 mol L⁻¹** (exact 2/15).
   Jaanch: 4/30 = 2/15 = 0.1333 ✅.

3. (a) **T badhane par:** Endothermic → **Forward** shift (zyada CO₂).
   (b) **Volume ghatane (Pressure badhane) par:** Kam gas moles (0 mol gas left) → **Reverse** shift!
   (c) **Solid CaO milane par:** Pure solid ka active mass 1 → **Koi shift nahi**!

4. 10⁻⁸ M NaOH bahut dilute hai → paani ka 10⁻⁷ M OH⁻ jodo:
   [OH⁻]_total ≈ 10⁻⁷ + 10⁻⁸ = 1.1 × 10⁻⁷ M → pOH ≈ 7 − 0.04 = **6.96** → **pH ≈ 14 − 6.96 = 7.04** *(exact quadratic se **7.02** — 7 se thoda upar, halka basic!)*
   Jaanch: Base ka pH 7 se thoda zyada (7.02–7.04) ✅.

5. (i) **Ag⁺** = **Lewis acid** (e⁻-pair acceptor); **:NH₃** = **Lewis base** (e⁻-pair donor).
   (ii) NH₃ ka Brønsted–Lowry conjugate acid = **NH₄⁺**.

6. (a) [Salt] = [Acid] = 0.25 M → **pH = pK_a + log(1) = 4.76**.
   (b) Equal volume paani milane par dono 0.125 M ho jaate hain → ratio 0.125 / 0.125 = **1** hi rehta hai → **pH 4.76 par hi tika rehta hai (no change!)**.

7. 0.10 M NaF se [F⁻] ≈ 0.10 M.
   K_sp = [Sr²⁺][F⁻]² → 2.5 × 10⁻⁹ = S' × (0.10)² = S' × 0.01 → **S' = 2.5 × 10⁻⁷ mol/L**.
   Jaanch: 2.5 × 10⁻⁷ × 10⁻² = 2.5 × 10⁻⁹ = K_sp ✅.

8. NH₄CN (WA + WB):
   pH = 7 + ½pK_a − ½pK_b = 7 + ½(9.30) − ½(4.74) = 7 + 4.65 − 2.37 = **9.28** (> 7, **basic** kyunki HCN, NH₃ se zyada weak hai!).
   Jaanch: 7 + 2.28 = 9.28 ✅.

9. +5.71 = −5.706 × log K → log K = **−1.00** → **K = 10⁻¹ = 0.10**.
   Yahan **Q (0.50) > K (0.10)** → reaction **REVERSE (reactants ki taraf)** chalega!
   Jaanch: K = 0.10 < Q = 0.50 → reverse ✅.

10. (a) [H₃O⁺] = cα = 0.10 × 0.010 = **1.0 × 10⁻³ M**.
    (b) **pH = −log(10⁻³) = 3.0**.
    (c) **K_a = cα² = 0.10 × (0.010)² = 1.0 × 10⁻⁵**.
    (d) **K_b(A⁻) = K_w ÷ K_a = 10⁻¹⁴ ÷ 10⁻⁵ = 1.0 × 10⁻⁹**.
    Jaanch: K_a × K_b = 10⁻⁵ × 10⁻⁹ = 10⁻¹⁴ = K_w ✅.

11. Equal volumes milane par concentrations aadhi: [Ca²⁺] = **2.0 × 10⁻⁴ M**, [SO₄²⁻] = **2.0 × 10⁻⁴ M**.
    Q_sp = (2.0 × 10⁻⁴)(2.0 × 10⁻⁴) = **4.0 × 10⁻⁸**.
    Kyunki **Q_sp (4.0 × 10⁻⁸) << K_sp (2.4 × 10⁻⁵)**, **NAHI, CaSO₄ precipitate nahi hoga!**
    Jaanch: 4.0 × 10⁻⁸ < 2.4 × 10⁻⁵ ✅.

12. (a) **False** ❌. Dilution par ratio [Salt]/[Acid] nahi badalta, isliye buffer ka pH lagbhag **constant** rehta hai!
    (b) **True** ✅. (RT)⁰ = 1 → K_p = K_c.
    (c) **True** ✅. Fe³⁺ (weak base se aaya cation) paani se hydrolyse hokar H⁺ deta hai.
    (d) **False** ❌. Catalyst yield ya K nahi badalta, sirf equilibrium **jaldi** laata hai.

| **1. Physical/Chemical Eq, K_c & K_p** | Dynamic nature (r_f = r_b), Henry's Law, Homogeneous vs Heterogeneous, K algebra | **K_p = K_c (RT)^(Δn_g)**; Reverse → 1/K; ×n → Kⁿ; Add → K₁K₂ | Pure solids/liquids = 1 (omit!); R = 0.0831 L·bar K⁻¹ mol⁻¹ |
| **2. Q, ICE Table & Le Chatelier** | Direction via Q vs K, ICE table solving, 5 factors (Conc, P, T, Catalyst, Inert gas) | Q < K forward, Q > K reverse; **ONLY T changes K!** | High P → kam gas moles; Catalyst = no shift; Inert gas: const V (no shift) vs const P (more gas moles) |

| **4. Common Ion, Hydrolysis & Buffers** | Suppression of weak electrolyte, 4 salt types, Henderson–Hasselbalch equations | **pH = pK_a + log([Salt]/[Acid])**; WA+SB: 7+½pK_a+½log c; SA+WB: 7−½pK_b−½log c | Blood buffer: H₂CO₃/HCO₃⁻ (pH 7.4); dilution doesn't change buffer pH! |
| **5. Solubility Product (K_sp)** | Sparingly soluble salts, Common ion lowers S (not K_sp!), Precipitation & salt analysis | **A_x B_y: K_sp = x^x y^y S^(x+y)** (S², 4S³, 27S⁴, 108S⁵); **Q_sp > K_sp → ppt!** | Mixing equal volumes → **HALVE** concentrations before Q_sp! |
