# Topic 1: Physical aur Chemical Equilibrium, Dynamic Nature, Law of Mass Action aur Equilibrium Constants (K_c, K_p)

Ab tak humne zyadaatar aise reactions dekhe the jo ek hi disha mein poori tarah khatam ho jaate hain (A → B, **irreversible**). Par asli chemistry aur hamare sharir mein zyadaatar reactions **reversible (dono dishaon mein chalne wale: A ⇌ B)** hote hain! Jab aage jaane ki raftaar (**forward rate, r_f**) aur peeche aane ki raftaar (**backward rate, r_b**) bilkul **barabar** ho jaati hain, to system ek khaas santulan mein pahunch jaata hai — jise **Equilibrium** kehte hain! Aaj hum physical equilibria (Henry's Law sahit), chemical equilibrium ki dynamic prakriti, **K_c**, **K_p**, **K_p = K_c (RT)^(Δn_g)** aur K ke algebraic rules seekhenge.

## Lakshmi, shopping mall ka escalator aur thandi soda bottle

Lakshmi apni Mummy ke saath mall mein thi. Usne dekha ki ground floor se 1st floor par jaane wale escalator par har minute **15 log upar** ja rahe the, aur theek usi samay 1st floor se neeche aane wale escalator par har minute **15 log neeche** aa rahe the!

Lakshmi ne hairani se kaha, "Mummy, 1st floor par har waqt lagbhag **200 log** hi dikh rahe hain — ginti bilkul badal nahi rahi, jabki ek bhi insaan ruka hua nahi hai!"

Phir food court mein Mummy ne ek **band chilled soda bottle** ka dhakkan khola — turant *fizzzzzz* ki aawaz ke saath CO₂ ke bulbule bahar bhaagne lage! Mummy muskurayin: "Lakshmi, band bottle mein CO₂ gas aur liquid soda ke beech, aur mall ke escalator par — dono jagah **Dynamic Equilibrium** chal raha tha! Dono taraf ki gati chalu rehti hai, par **aage aur peeche ki raftaar barabar** hone se bahar se sab sthir (constant) dikhta hai!" Chalo, physical aur chemical equilibrium ke saare kanoon seekhte hain.

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |

| **Dynamic equilibrium** | Wo haalat jab **forward rate (r_f) = backward rate (r_b)** ho; macroscopically sab properties constant dikhti hain par microscopic level par dono reactions lagatar chalti rehti hain | Band bottle mein H₂O(l) ⇌ H₂O(g) |

## Part A: Physical Equilibria aur Henry's Law

Band (closed) bartan mein constant temperature par 5 mukhya physical equilibria milte hain:
1. **Solid ⇌ Liquid (Melting/Freezing):** 0 °C aur 1 atm par barf aur paani ek saath: rate of melting = rate of freezing (normal melting point).
2. **Liquid ⇌ Vapour (Evaporation/Condensation):** Band bartan mein rate of evaporation = rate of condensation → constant **equilibrium vapour pressure** (sirf T par nirbhar!).
3. **Solid ⇌ Vapour (Sublimation):** Band flask mein I₂(s) ⇌ I₂(g) (baingani bhaap ka rang constant ho jaata hai; *sirf teacher demo*).
4. **Solid ⇌ Solution (Dissolution):** Saturated sugar solution mein rate of dissolution = rate of crystallisation (radioactive sugar daalkar dynamic nature prove kiya jaata hai!).
5. **Gas ⇌ Solution (Henry's Law):** Band soda bottle mein **CO₂(g) ⇌ CO₂(aq)**. **Henry's Law** kehta hai ki constant T par liquid mein ghuli gas ka mass (m) gas ke pressure (p) ke proportional hota hai (**m ∝ p**). Bottle kholte hi upar ka pressure achanak ghat jaata hai, isliye ghuli CO₂ fizz bankar bahar nikal aati hai! *(Aur garam karne par gas ki solubility ghatti hai — isliye garm soda jaldi flat ho jaata hai!)*

## Part B: Chemical Equilibrium, K_c, K_p aur K_p = K_c (RT)^(Δn_g)

```
   Rate                                 Concentration
    ^                                     ^
    : \  r_f (ghat-ti hai)                : \  [Reactants]
    :  \                                  :  \________________  (Constant!)
    :   \_______________ r_f = r_b        :   /
    :   /                (Equilibrium!)   :  /----------------  [Products] (Constant!)
    :  /  r_b (badhti hai)                : /
    +---------------------> Time          +---------------------> Time
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Equilibrium par forward rate (r_f) aur backward rate (r_b) **barabar** ho jaati hain (baayan graph), jabki Reactants aur Products ki concentrations **constant** ho jaati hain (zaroori nahi ki aapas mein barabar hon — daayan graph!).

### 1. Law of Chemical Equilibrium (K_c aur K_p)
Kisi bhi reversible reaction **aA + bB ⇌ cC + dD** ke liye:
- **Concentration ke roop mein (K_c):** **K_c = ([C]^c [D]^d) ÷ ([A]^a [B]^b)** *(molarity mol/L mein)*
- **Partial pressure ke roop mein (K_p, sirf gases ke liye):** **K_p = (p_C^c × p_D^d) ÷ (p_A^a × p_B^b)** *(bar ya atm mein)*
- **Heterogeneous Equilibrium ka Golden Rule:** **Pure solid (s)** aur **pure liquid (l)** ki molar concentration (density ÷ molar mass) hamesha constant rehti hai — isliye unka **active mass = 1** liya jaata hai aur unhein K_c ya K_p ke expression mein **NAHI likha jaata!**

### 2. K_p aur K_c ka Derivation aur Rishta
Ideal gas equation se p_i = (n_i / V) RT = [i] RT. K_p mein rakhne par:
- K_p = ([C]RT)^c ([D]RT)^d ÷ (([A]RT)^a ([B]RT)^b) = K_c × (RT)^((c+d) − (a+b))
> **K_p = K_c × (RT)^(Δn_g)**
- Jahan **Δn_g = (Gaseous products ke moles) − (Gaseous reactants ke moles)**!
- **R ki unit ka khaas dhyan:** Pressure **bar** mein ho to **R = 0.0831 L·bar K⁻¹ mol⁻¹**; **atm** mein ho to **R = 0.0821 L·atm K⁻¹ mol⁻¹**! *(Kabhi 8.314 mat rakhna!)*
- Agar **Δn_g = 0** (jaise H₂(g) + I₂(g) ⇌ 2HI(g)), to **K_p = K_c**!

### 3. Equilibrium Constant (K) ke 3 Algebraic Rules + Extent of Reaction
1. **Equation reverse (ulta) karne par:** **K_rev = 1 / K**
2. **Equation ko 'n' se guna karne par:** **K_new = (K)^n** *(half karne par K_new = √K!)*
3. **Do equations ko jodne par:** **K_net = K₁ × K₂** *(Hess's Law mein ΔH judte the, yahan K **guna** hote hain!)*
4. **Reaction ki Hadd (Extent):**
   - **K > 10³:** Products bahut zyada bante hain (reaction lagbhag poora!).
   - **K < 10⁻³:** Reactants hi zyada bachte hain (reaction mushkil se chalta hai!).
   - **10⁻³ < K < 10³:** Dono reactants aur products achhi matra mein maujood!

## Solved Examples

### Example 1. Homogeneous aur Heterogeneous Reactions ke K_c aur K_p Likhna

**Sawaal:** K_c aur K_p ke expressions likho:
(a) **N₂(g) + 3H₂(g) ⇌ 2NH₃(g)** (Homogeneous)
(b) **CaCO₃(s) ⇌ CaO(s) + CO₂(g)** (Heterogeneous)

**Hal:**
- (a) Sab gas hain: **K_c = [NH₃]² ÷ ([N₂][H₂]³)**; **K_p = (p_NH₃)² ÷ (p_N₂ × (p_H₂)³)**; Δn_g = 2 − 4 = **−2**.
- (b) CaCO₃(s) aur CaO(s) pure solids hain (active mass = 1 → hata do!):
  - **K_c = [CO₂]**; **K_p = p_CO₂**; Δn_g = 1 − 0 = **+1**.

**Jaanch:** Heterogeneous reaction mein sirf gaseous CO₂ hi K_c aur K_p mein aaya ✅.

---

### Example 2. Equilibrium Concentrations se K_c Nikaalna

**Sawaal:** 700 K par reaction **H₂(g) + I₂(g) ⇌ 2HI(g)** mein equilibrium concentrations hain: **[H₂] = 0.20 M**, **[I₂] = 0.20 M**, **[HI] = 1.20 M**. **K_c** aur **K_p** nikaalo.

**Hal:**
- K_c = [HI]² ÷ ([H₂][I₂]) = (1.20)² ÷ (0.20 × 0.20) = 1.44 ÷ 0.04 = **36**!
- Δn_g = 2 − (1 + 1) = **0** → **K_p = K_c (RT)⁰ = 36**!

**Jaanch:** 1.44 ÷ 0.04 = 144 ÷ 4 = 36; Δn_g = 0 → K_p = K_c ✅.

---

### Example 3. K_c se K_p Nikaalna (2NOCl ⇌ 2NO + Cl₂) *(toxic gases: sirf paper calculation)*

**Sawaal:** **500 K** par **2NOCl(g) ⇌ 2NO(g) + Cl₂(g)** ka **K_c = 2.0 × 10⁻³ mol L⁻¹** hai. **K_p** (bar mein) nikaalo. *(R = 0.0831 L·bar K⁻¹ mol⁻¹.)*

**Hal:**
- Δn_g = (2 + 1) − 2 = **+1**.
- K_p = K_c × (RT)¹ = (2.0 × 10⁻³) × (0.0831 × 500) = (2.0 × 10⁻³) × 41.55 = **0.0831 bar** (ya **8.31 × 10⁻² bar**)!

**Jaanch:** 0.0831 × 500 = 41.55; 41.55 × 0.002 = 0.0831 bar ✅.

---

### Example 4. K ke Algebraic Rules (Reverse aur Half!)

**Sawaal:** Agar **N₂(g) + 3H₂(g) ⇌ 2NH₃(g)** ka K_c = **64** hai, to **NH₃(g) ⇌ ½N₂(g) + (3/2)H₂(g)** ka K_c' kya hoga?

**Hal:**
- Step 1 (Reverse): 2NH₃ ⇌ N₂ + 3H₂ → K₁ = 1 / 64.
- Step 2 (Half / multiply by ½): NH₃ ⇌ ½N₂ + (3/2)H₂ → **K_c' = (1 / 64)^(1/2) = 1 / 8 = 0.125**!

**Jaanch:** (0.125)² = 1/64, aur uska reciprocal = 64 ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: Heterogeneous equilibrium mein pure solids (s) ya pure liquids (l) ko K_c / K_p mein likh dena.**
   CaCO₃(s) ⇌ CaO(s) + CO₂(g) mein agar koi student K_c = [CaO][CO₂] ÷ [CaCO₃] likh de, to galat hai! Pure solids aur pure liquids ka active mass **1** hota hai → sahi roop **K_c = [CO₂]** hai!

2. **Galti 2: Equilibrium ka matlab "[Reactants] = [Products]" samajh lena.**
   Example 2 mein [H₂] = 0.20 M aur [HI] = 1.20 M barabar nahi the — phir bhi system equilibrium par tha! Equilibrium par **concentrations CONSTANT** hoti hain, **barabar sirf RATES (r_f = r_b)** hoti hain!

3. **Galti 3: K_p = K_c (RT)^(Δn_g) mein R = 8.314 rakh dena.**
   8.314 J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹ energy ke liye hai! K_p aur K_c mein pressure (bar/atm) aur volume (L) hote hain, isliye hamesha **R = 0.0831 L·bar K⁻¹ mol⁻¹** (ya 0.0821 L·atm K⁻¹ mol⁻¹) use karo!

## Quick Recap

- **Dynamic Equilibrium:** r_f = r_b; concentrations constant; sirf **closed system** mein aur **constant T** par milta hai; **K sirf Temperature par nirbhar** karta hai!
- **Henry's Law:** Constant T par ghuli gas ka mass m ∝ p.
- **K_p = K_c (RT)^(Δn_g)** (sirf gases ke moles, R = 0.0831 L·bar K⁻¹ mol⁻¹); pure solids/liquids = 1 (omit!).
- **Algebraic Rules:** Reverse → 1/K; Multiply by n → Kⁿ; Add equations → K₁ × K₂.

## Practice Questions

1. Explain why chemical equilibrium is called **dynamic** rather than static, and state two key characteristics of a system at chemical equilibrium.
2. State **Henry's Law**. Using Henry's Law, explain why a chilled bottle of soda fizzes vigorously the moment its cap is opened.
3. Write the **K_c** and **K_p** expressions for each of the following equilibria:
   (a) **2SO₂(g) + O₂(g) ⇌ 2SO₃(g)**
   (b) **C(s) + CO₂(g) ⇌ 2CO(g)** *(Toxic gas CO: paper problem only.)*
   (c) **NH₄HS(s) ⇌ NH₃(g) + H₂S(g)** *(Toxic gases: paper problem only.)*
4. At 500 K, the equilibrium concentrations for **PCl₅(g) ⇌ PCl₃(g) + Cl₂(g)** are: **[PCl₅] = 0.040 M**, **[PCl₃] = 0.20 M**, and **[Cl₂] = 0.20 M**. Calculate **K_c**. *(Corrosive/toxic gases: paper problem only.)*
5. For the reaction **N₂O₄(g) ⇌ 2NO₂(g)** at **300 K**, **K_c = 4.60 × 10⁻³ mol L⁻¹**. Calculate **K_p** in bar. *(R = 0.0831 L·bar K⁻¹ mol⁻¹.)*
6. For which of the following reactions is **K_p = K_c**? Give a one-line reason using Δn_g:
   (a) **CO(g) + H₂O(g) ⇌ CO₂(g) + H₂(g)**
   (b) **N₂(g) + O₂(g) ⇌ 2NO(g)**
   (c) **2NH₃(g) ⇌ N₂(g) + 3H₂(g)**
   (d) **CaCO₃(s) ⇌ CaO(s) + CO₂(g)**
7. The equilibrium constant for **H₂(g) + I₂(g) ⇌ 2HI(g)** at a certain temperature is **K_c = 49**. Calculate the equilibrium constant for:
   (a) **2HI(g) ⇌ H₂(g) + I₂(g)**
   (b) **½H₂(g) + ½I₂(g) ⇌ HI(g)**
   (c) **HI(g) ⇌ ½H₂(g) + ½I₂(g)**
8. Given two equilibria at the same temperature:
   - A(g) ⇌ B(g), **K₁ = 4.0**
   - B(g) ⇌ C(g), **K₂ = 5.0**
   Find the equilibrium constant **K_net** for the overall reaction **A(g) ⇌ C(g)**.
9. At 1000 K, for **CaCO₃(s) ⇌ CaO(s) + CO₂(g)**, **K_p = 0.04155 bar**. Calculate **K_c** (in mol L⁻¹). *(R = 0.0831 L·bar K⁻¹ mol⁻¹.)*
10. Predict the **extent of reaction** (mostly products, mostly reactants, or significant amounts of both) for reactions with: (a) **K_c = 2.0 × 10⁸**, (b) **K_c = 5.0 × 10⁻⁹**, (c) **K_c = 3.5**.
11. Why are the concentrations of **pure solids** and **pure liquids** omitted from the equilibrium constant expression?
12. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) At equilibrium, the concentration of reactants is always equal to the concentration of products.
    (b) Chemical equilibrium can be attained from either the reactants' side or the products' side.
    (c) If a chemical equation is doubled, its equilibrium constant K is multiplied by 2.
    (d) Equilibrium constant K depends only on temperature for a given balanced equation.

### Answers

1. Equilibrium par forward aur reverse reactions ruktii nahi hain, balki **barabar raftaar (r_f = r_b)** par lagatar chalti rehti hain — isliye ise **dynamic** kehte hain. Do gun: (1) Sabhi macroscopic properties (concentration, pressure, colour) constant rehti hain; (2) Equilibrium kisi bhi taraf (reactants ya products) se haasil kiya ja sakta hai.

2. **Henry's Law:** Constant T par liquid mein ghuli gas ka mass uske pressure ke directly proportional (m ∝ p) hota hai. Band soda bottle mein CO₂ high pressure par ghuli rehti hai; dhakkan kholte hi pressure achanak ghat jaata hai, isliye extra CO₂ fizz bankar bahar nikal aati hai!

3. (a) **K_c = [SO₃]² ÷ ([SO₂]²[O₂])**; **K_p = (p_SO₃)² ÷ ((p_SO₂)² p_O₂)**.
   (b) C(s) solid hatao → **K_c = [CO]² ÷ [CO₂]**; **K_p = (p_CO)² ÷ p_CO₂**.
   (c) NH₄HS(s) solid hatao → **K_c = [NH₃][H₂S]**; **K_p = p_NH₃ × p_H₂S**.

4. K_c = ([PCl₃][Cl₂]) ÷ [PCl₅] = (0.20 × 0.20) ÷ 0.040 = 0.040 ÷ 0.040 = **1.0 mol L⁻¹**.
   Jaanch: 0.040 ÷ 0.040 = 1.0 ✅.

5. Δn_g = 2 − 1 = **+1**.
   K_p = K_c (RT)¹ = (4.60 × 10⁻³) × (0.0831 × 300) = (4.60 × 10⁻³) × 24.93 = **0.1147 bar ≈ 0.115 bar**.
   Jaanch: 0.0831 × 300 = 24.93; 24.93 × 0.0046 = 0.1147 bar ✅.

6. **(a) aur (b)** mein Δn_g = 0 hai (2 − 2 = 0), isliye **K_p = K_c**! (c) mein Δn_g = +2 aur (d) mein Δn_g = +1 hai, isliye wahan K_p ≠ K_c.

7. (a) Reverse → **1 / 49** (≈ 0.0204).
   (b) Half (× ½) → **√49 = 7**.
   (c) Reverse + Half → **1 / √49 = 1 / 7** (≈ 0.143).
   Jaanch: 7 × (1/7) = 1 ✅.

8. Equations jodkar B kat jaata hai → **K_net = K₁ × K₂ = 4.0 × 5.0 = 20.0**.
   Jaanch: 4 × 5 = 20 ✅.

9. Δn_g = 1 − 0 = +1 → K_c = K_p ÷ (RT) = 0.04155 ÷ (0.0831 × 1000) = 0.04155 ÷ 83.1 = **5.0 × 10⁻⁴ mol L⁻¹**.
   Jaanch: 5.0 × 10⁻⁴ × 83.1 = 0.04155 bar ✅.

10. (a) **Mostly products** (K > 10³ — reaction lagbhag poora!).
    (b) **Mostly reactants** (K < 10⁻³ — reaction bahut kam chala!).
    (c) **Significant amounts of both** reactants aur products (10⁻³ < K < 10³).

11. Pure solid ya pure liquid ki molar concentration (Density ÷ Molar Mass) constant rehti hai (matra badhane ya ghatane par bhi nahi badalti!), isliye unka active mass **1** maankar unhein K expression mein shamil nahi kiya jaata.

12. (a) **False** ❌. Equilibrium par concentrations **constant** hoti hain (zaroori nahi barabar hon); barabar sirf **rates (r_f = r_b)** hoti hain.
    (b) **True** ✅.
    (c) **False** ❌. Equation double karne par K **square (K²)** ho jaata hai, 2K nahi!
    (d) **True** ✅.
