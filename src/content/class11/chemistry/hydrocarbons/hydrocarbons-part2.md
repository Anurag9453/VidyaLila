# Topic 2: Alkenes: Preparation (Saytzeff Rule), Markovnikov vs Peroxide Effect aur Ozonolysis

Alkanes (sirf σ bonds) shaant the, par **Alkenes (CₙH₂ₙ, Olefins)** mein ek **C=C double bond (1 σ + 1 π)** hota hai! π-bond ke electrons loose aur exposed hote hain — isliye alkenes turant **Electrophilic Addition Reactions** dikhate hain! Aaj hum alkenes banane ke 4 tarike (**Lindlar vs Na/liq. NH₃** aur **Saytzeff's Rule**), **Markovnikov's Rule** banaam **Kharash Peroxide Effect**, **Br₂/CCl₄ & Baeyer's Unsaturation Tests**, aur **Ozonolysis** (forward + reverse!) seekhenge!

## Part A: Alkenes ki 4 Preparation Methods + Saytzeff's Rule

1. **Alkynes ka Partial Reduction (Stereoselective!):**
   - **Lindlar's catalyst (Pd/C + quinoline):** Alkyne + H₂ → ***cis*-Alkene**!
   - **Birch-type reduction (Na / liquid NH₃):** Alkyne + 2[H] → ***trans*-Alkene**!
2. **Dehydrohalogenation of Alkyl Halides (β-Elimination with alc. KOH):**
   - R−CH₂−CH₂−X + alc. KOH —(Δ)→ R−CH=CH₂ + KX + H₂O.
   - **Saytzeff's Rule:** Jab 2 alag alkenes ban sakte hon, to **zyada substituted (zyada α-H wala)** alkene **major** hota hai!
3. **Dehalogenation of Vicinal Dihalides:** CH₂Br−CH₂Br + **Zn** → CH₂=CH₂ + ZnBr₂.
4. **Acidic Dehydration of Alcohols:** CH₃−CH₂−OH —(conc. H₂SO₄, 443 K)→ CH₂=CH₂ + H₂O *(also obeys Saytzeff's rule!)*.

## Part B: Addition Reactions (Markovnikov vs Peroxide), Unsaturation Tests aur Ozonolysis

1. **Unsaturation ke Do Pakke Lab Tests *(Br₂/CCl₄ aur KMnO₄: sirf teacher demo!)*:**
   - **Bromine Water / Br₂ in CCl₄ Test:** Alkenes **orange-red Br₂** ka rang turant **colourless** kar dete hain (dibromide banta hai!).
   - **Baeyer's Test (Cold, dilute, alkaline KMnO₄):** Alkenes **purple KMnO₄** ko **colourless** karke *vicinal* diol (e.g. ethane-1,2-diol) banate hain! *(Hot acidic KMnO₄ C=C ko poora tod kar carboxylic acid/ketone/CO₂ bana deta hai!)*

2. **Markovnikov vs Anti-Markovnikov (Peroxide Effect) — Mechanism ka Raaz!**
   - **Markovnikov (Ionic / Carbocation Mechanism):** Propene (**CH₃−CH=CH₂**) par pehle **H⁺** judta hai → 2° carbocation (**CH₃−CH⁺−CH₃**, zyada stable!) banta hai → phir **Br⁻** beech wale C par lagkar **2-bromopropane (major)** banata hai!
   - **Anti-Markovnikov (Free-Radical Mechanism — SIRF HBr + Peroxide!):** Peroxide se pehle **Br• radical** banta hai → wo kinare wale C par judkar zyada stable **2° free radical (CH₃−CH•−CH₂Br)** banata hai → phir H judkar **1-bromopropane (major)** deta hai!
   - **Sirf HBr ke saath hi kyun? (HCl ya HI ke saath NAHI!):** H−Cl bond (431 kJ/mol) bahut mazboot hai (homolytic cleavage mushkil), aur H−I mein bana I• radical C=C par judne ke bajay aapas mein judkar **I₂** bana leta hai!

3. **Ozonolysis (Reductive: O₃, phir Zn/H₂O) — 2-Second Shortcut!**
   - **Forward:** C=C double bond ko beech se **kaat do** aur dono kate hue carbons par **=O** chipka do!
   - **Reverse:** Do carbonyl products ke **=O** hatao aur dono carbons ko **C=C** se wapas jod do — asli alkene mil jaayega!

## Solved Examples

### Example 1 (Saytzeff's Rule). 2-Bromobutane + alc. KOH

**Sawaal:** **CH₃−CH(Br)−CH₂−CH₃** ko alcoholic KOH ke saath garam karne par kaun-sa major aur kaun-sa minor alkene banega?

**Hal:**
- **Major (80%):** **CH₃−CH=CH−CH₃ (but-2-ene)** — ismein **6 α-H** hain (zyada stable, Saytzeff product!).
- **Minor (20%):** **CH₂=CH−CH₂−CH₃ (but-1-ene)** — ismein sirf **2 α-H** hain.

**Jaanch:** 6 α-H > 2 α-H → but-2-ene major ✅.

---

### Example 2 (Markovnikov vs Peroxide Effect). Propene + HBr

**Sawaal:** **CH₃−CH=CH₂ + HBr** ka major product likho: (a) bina peroxide ke, (b) benzoyl peroxide ke saath.

**Hal:**
- (a) Bina peroxide (Markovnikov via 2° carbocation): **CH₃−CH(Br)−CH₃ (2-bromopropane)**.
- (b) Peroxide ke saath (Anti-Markovnikov via 2° free radical): **CH₃−CH₂−CH₂Br (1-bromopropane)**.

**Jaanch:** Dono cases mein zyada stable 2° intermediate (carbocation vs free radical) hi major product tay karta hai ✅!

---

### Example 3 (Forward & Reverse Ozonolysis).

**Sawaal:**
(a) **2-Methylbut-2-ene ((CH₃)₂C=CH−CH₃)** ke reductive ozonolysis (O₃, Zn/H₂O) ke products likho.
(b) Ek alkene ozonolysis par **methanal (HCHO)** aur **propanone ((CH₃)₂C=O)** deta hai. Asli alkene pehchano!

**Hal:**
- (a) C=C ko kaat kar dono taraf =O lagao → **(CH₃)₂C=O (Propanone)** + **O=CH−CH₃ (Ethanal)**!
- (b) H₂C=**O** + **O**=C(CH₃)₂ ke dono O hatakar jodo → **CH₂=C(CH₃)₂ (2-Methylpropene)**!

**Jaanch:** (a) 5 C = 3 C + 2 C ✅; (b) 1 C + 3 C = 4 C (2-methylpropene) ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: HCl + Peroxide ya HI + Peroxide mein bhi Anti-Markovnikov product likh dena!**
   *Sahi baat:* Peroxide effect **SIRF HBr** ke saath chalta hai! HCl ya HI mein peroxide hone par bhi **Markovnikov** product hi banta hai!

2. **Galti 2: 2-Bromobutane + alc. KOH mein but-1-ene ko major product likh dena.**
   Saytzeff's rule se zyada substituted alkene (**but-2-ene**, 6 α-H) hamesha major hota hai!

3. **Galti 3: Lindlar's catalyst aur Na/liq. NH₃ ke *cis/trans* products ko ulta kar dena.**
   Yaad rakhne ki trick: **Lindlar → *cis***; **Na / liq. NH₃ → *trans***!

## Quick Recap

- **Prep:** Alkyne + H₂ (Lindlar → *cis*; Na/liq. NH₃ → *trans*); Alkyl halide + alc. KOH (**Saytzeff**); Alcohol + conc. H₂SO₄.
- **Addition:** **Markovnikov** (carbocation) vs **Peroxide Effect** (ONLY **HBr**, free radical!).
- **Tests:** **Br₂/CCl₄** (orange-red → colourless) & **Baeyer's cold dil. KMnO₄** (purple → colourless, *vic*-diol).
- **Ozonolysis (O₃, Zn/H₂O):** C=C kaato aur dono carbons par =O lagao!

## Practice Questions

1. Write the major product when **but-2-yne (CH₃−C≡C−CH₃)** is reduced with:
   (a) **H₂ / Lindlar's catalyst (Pd/C)**
   (b) **Na / liquid NH₃**
2. Write the major product of each addition reaction:
   (a) **CH₃−CH₂−CH=CH₂ (but-1-ene) + HBr** (no peroxide)
   (b) **CH₃−CH₂−CH=CH₂ (but-1-ene) + HBr** (in presence of peroxide)
   (c) **CH₃−CH₂−CH=CH₂ + HCl** (in presence of peroxide — careful!)
   (d) **(CH₃)₂C=CH₂ + H₂O / H⁺**
3. Explain clearly why the **Peroxide (Anti-Markovnikov) Effect** is observed ONLY with **HBr**, and NOT with HCl or HI.
4. Predict the **reductive ozonolysis (O₃, Zn/H₂O)** products of:
   (a) **Pent-2-ene (CH₃−CH=CH−CH₂−CH₃)**
   (b) **2,3-Dimethylbut-2-ene ((CH₃)₂C=C(CH₃)₂)**
   (c) **Cyclohexene**
5. An alkene **X** on reductive ozonolysis gives **only ethanal (2 moles of CH₃CHO)**. Identify **X** and write its IUPAC name.
6. Write the major alkene formed when **butan-2-ol (CH₃−CH(OH)−CH₂−CH₃)** is heated with conc. H₂SO₄ at 443 K. State the rule applied.
7. Describe **two simple chemical tests** (with colour changes) used to detect the presence of a C=C double bond (unsaturation) in an organic liquid. *(Teacher demo / paper question.)*
8. Compare the **boiling point** and **melting point** of ***cis*-but-2-ene** and ***trans*-but-2-ene**, explaining why *cis* has a higher boiling point whereas *trans* has a higher melting point.
9. What happens when **propene (CH₃−CH=CH₂)** is oxidised with:
   (a) **Cold, dilute alkaline KMnO₄ (Baeyer's reagent)**, and
   (b) **Hot, acidic KMnO₄**?
10. Using carbocation stability, explain step by step why **propene + HBr** gives **2-bromopropane** as the major product rather than 1-bromopropane.
11. Write the chemical equation for the **polymerisation** of (a) ethene to polythene, and (b) propene to polypropene.
12. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) In the presence of peroxide, propene + HCl gives 1-chloropropane as the major product.
    (b) Baeyer's reagent is cold, dilute, alkaline KMnO₄ solution.
    (c) According to Saytzeff's rule, the less substituted alkene is the major elimination product.
    (d) Reductive ozonolysis of ethene gives two molecules of methanal (HCHO).

### Answers

1. (a) ***cis*-but-2-ene**; (b) ***trans*-but-2-ene**.

2. (a) **2-bromobutane** (Markovnikov); (b) **1-bromobutane** (Peroxide effect!); (c) **2-chlorobutane** (Peroxide effect HCl par kaam NAHI karta → Markovnikov!); (d) **2-methylpropan-2-ol ((CH₃)₃C−OH)** (Markovnikov via 3° carbocation!).

3. H−Cl bond (431 kJ/mol) bahut mazboot hone se peroxide radical use homolytically nahi tod paata; aur H−I mein bana I• radical C=C par judne ke bajay aapas mein judkar I₂ bana leta hai (endothermic addition). Sirf **HBr** mein dono propagation steps exothermic aur tez hote hain!

4. (a) **Ethanal (CH₃CHO) + Propanal (CH₃CH₂CHO)**.
   (b) **2 moles of Propanone ((CH₃)₂C=O)**.
   (c) **Hexanedial (OHC−(CH₂)₄−CHO)** (ring khulkar 6-carbon dialdehyde!).
   Jaanch: (a) 2 + 3 = 5 C ✅; (b) 3 + 3 = 6 C ✅; (c) 6 C ✅.

5. Do CH₃CH=**O** ke O hatakar jodo → **CH₃−CH=CH−CH₃ (But-2-ene)**!
   Jaanch: But-2-ene → 2 CH₃CHO ✅.

6. **But-2-ene (CH₃−CH=CH−CH₃)** (major product) — **Saytzeff's Rule** (zyada substituted, 6 α-H).

7. (1) **Br₂ in CCl₄ test:** Orange-red colour gayab (colourless) ho jaata hai!
   (2) **Baeyer's test (cold dil. alk. KMnO₄):** Purple/pink colour gayab (colourless) hokar *vic*-diol banta hai!

8. ***cis*-but-2-ene** polar (μ ≠ 0) hai → dipole–dipole attraction → **zyada boiling point**. ***trans*-but-2-ene** zyada symmetrical hai → solid crystal lattice mein zyada kas kar pack hota hai → **zyada melting point**!

9. (a) Cold dil. KMnO₄: **Propane-1,2-diol (CH₃−CH(OH)−CH₂OH)**.
   (b) Hot acidic KMnO₄: C=C poora toot jaata hai → **Ethanoic acid (CH₃COOH) + CO₂ + H₂O**.

10. Step 1: H⁺ judne par do carbocations ban sakte hain — **2° (CH₃−CH⁺−CH₃, 6 α-H, zyada stable!)** aur 1° (CH₃−CH₂−CH₂⁺, 2 α-H, kam stable). Step 2: Br⁻ tezi se zyada stable 2° carbocation par attack karke **2-bromopropane** (major) banata hai!

11. (a) **n CH₂=CH₂ —(high T, P, catalyst)→ −(CH₂−CH₂)ₙ− (Polythene)**.
    (b) **n CH₃−CH=CH₂ → −(CH(CH₃)−CH₂)ₙ− (Polypropene)**.

12. (a) **False** ❌. Peroxide effect **sirf HBr** par chalta hai → HCl se **2-chloropropane** hi banega!
    (b) **True** ✅.
    (c) **False** ❌. Saytzeff's rule mein **more substituted** (zyada α-H) alkene major hota hai!
    (d) **True** ✅.
