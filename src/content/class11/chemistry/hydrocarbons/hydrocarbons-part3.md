# Topic 3: Alkynes: Structure, Preparation, Acidity of Terminal Alkynes aur Reactions

**Alkynes (CₙH₂ₙ₋₂)** mein ek **C≡C triple bond (1 σ + 2 π, bond length 120 pm, linear 180°)** hota hai. Sabse chhota alkyne **Ethyne (Acetylene, H−C≡C−H)** hai — jo oxy-acetylene welding flame (3000 °C+) mein kaam aata hai! Alkynes ki sabse anokhi khoobi hai unke **terminal sp-C−H hydrogen ki Acidity**, aur unke do jaadui reactions: **Hydration** (jisse seedha aldehyde/ketone banta hai!) aur **Cyclic Polymerisation** (jisse 3 ethyne judkar **Benzene** banate hain!).

## Part A: Alkynes ki Preparation aur Terminal Alkyne ki Acidity

### 1. Alkynes Banane ke 2 Mukhya Tarike
1. **Industrial Method (Calcium Carbide + Water):**
   - CaCO₃ —(Δ)→ CaO + CO₂;  CaO + 3C —(2000 °C)→ CaC₂ + CO
   - **CaC₂(s) + 2H₂O(l) → HC≡CH(g) + Ca(OH)₂(aq)**
2. **From Vicinal Dihalides (2-Step Dehydrohalogenation):**
   - Step 1: CH₂Br−CH₂Br + **alc. KOH** → CH₂=CHBr (vinyl bromide) + KBr + H₂O
   - Step 2: CH₂=CHBr + **NaNH₂ (Sodamide — zyada strong base!)** → **HC≡CH** + NaBr + NH₃! *(Vinyl halide ka C−Br bond resonance se mazboot hota hai, isliye 2nd step mein alc. KOH ke bajay strong base **NaNH₂** lagta hai!)*

### 2. Acidity of Alkynes (50% s-Character ka Kamaal!)
- Hybridisation s-character: **sp (50%) > sp² (33.3%) > sp³ (25%)** → Electronegativity: **C(sp) > C(sp²) > C(sp³)**!
- **Acidity Order:** **HC≡CH > CH₂=CH₂ > CH₃−CH₃** *(aur alkynes mein: **HC≡CH > CH₃−C≡CH > CH₃−C≡C−CH₃**, kyunki +I CH₃ group acidity ghatata hai!)*
- **Terminal Alkyne ke Acidic Reactions *(Na, NaNH₂: sirf padhne ke liye)*:**
  - HC≡CH + Na → **HC≡C⁻Na⁺** (monosodium ethynide) + ½H₂↑
  - HC≡CH + 2Na → **Na⁺⁻C≡C⁻Na⁺** (disodium ethynide) + H₂↑
  - **Distinction Test (But-1-yne vs But-2-yne!):** Terminal **but-1-yne** NaNH₂ / ammoniacal AgNO₃ (Tollens' reagent) ke saath react karke **white ppt** deta hai; internal **but-2-yne** mein koi ≡C−H nahi → **NO reaction**!

---

## Part B: Addition Reactions, Hydration aur Cyclic Polymerisation

1. **Addition of H₂:** HC≡CH + H₂ —(Pt/Pd/Ni)→ CH₂=CH₂ —(+H₂)→ CH₃−CH₃.
2. **Addition of X₂ (Br₂/CCl₄):** Orange-red colour gayab → pehle dibromoethene, phir **1,1,2,2-tetrabromoethane** (CHBr₂−CHBr₂)!
3. **Addition of HX (2 steps — Markovnikov!):**
   - HC≡CH + HBr → CH₂=CHBr —(+HBr, Markovnikov!)→ **CH₃−CHBr₂ (1,1-dibromoethane — geminal dihalide!)**.
4. **Hydration (Addition of Water — HgSO₄ / dil. H₂SO₄ at 333 K):**
   - **Ethyne + H₂O** → [CH₂=CH−OH] *(vinyl alcohol, unstable enol)* —(Tautomerism)→ **CH₃−CHO (Ethanal)**! *(Sirf ethyne aldehyde deta hai!)*
   - **Propyne + H₂O** (Markovnikov!) → [CH₃−C(OH)=CH₂] —(Tautomerism)→ **CH₃−CO−CH₃ (Propanone / Acetone)**! *(Baaki sabhi alkynes ketones dete hain!)*
5. **Cyclic Polymerisation (Benzene Banana — Super-Important Conversion!):**
   - **3 HC≡CH —(Red-hot Iron tube, 873 K)→ Benzene (C₆H₆)**!

## Solved Examples

### Example 1. But-1-yne aur But-2-yne ko Kaise Alag Pehchanein?

**Sawaal:** Tumhare paas do rangheen liquids hain: **But-1-yne (CH₃CH₂C≡CH)** aur **But-2-yne (CH₃C≡CCH₃)**. Ek simple chemical test se dono mein antar batao.

**Hal:**
- Dono mein **NaNH₂** (ya ammoniacal AgNO₃) milao:
  - **But-1-yne** (terminal alkyne, acidic ≡C−H hai!) → react karke **H₂/NH₃ gas** (ya AgNO₃ ke saath **white ppt**) deta hai!
  - **But-2-yne** (internal alkyne, koi ≡C−H nahi!) → **KOI reaction nahi** deta!

**Jaanch:** Sirf terminal alkynes hi acidic reactions dikhate hain ✅.

---

### Example 2. Hydration of Ethyne banaam Propyne

**Sawaal:** Hg²⁺ / H⁺ (333 K) ke saath hydration ke final products likho: (a) **Ethyne**, (b) **Propyne**.

**Hal:**
- (a) **HC≡CH + H₂O** → [CH₂=CHOH] → **CH₃CHO (Ethanal)**.
- (b) **CH₃−C≡CH + H₂O** (Markovnikov: −OH beech wale C par!) → [CH₃−C(OH)=CH₂] → **CH₃−CO−CH₃ (Propanone)**.

**Jaanch:** Ethyne = 2 C aldehyde; Propyne = 3 C ketone ✅.

---

### Example 3. Ethyne se 1,1-Dibromoethane aur Benzene Banana

**Sawaal:** Ethyne se (a) **1,1-dibromoethane**, aur (b) **Benzene** kaise banaoge?

**Hal:**
- (a) **HC≡CH + 2HBr → CH₃−CHBr₂** (2-step Markovnikov addition!).
- (b) **3 HC≡CH —(Red-hot Fe tube, 873 K)→ C₆H₆ (Benzene)**!

**Jaanch:** 3 × C₂H₂ = C₆H₆ ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: But-2-yne ko bhi Ethyne ki tarah acidic maan lena!**
   *Sahi baat:* Acidity sirf **terminal alkynes (R−C≡C−H)** mein hoti hai! But-2-yne mein C≡C par koi H nahi hai → **non-acidic!**

2. **Galti 2: Ethyne + H₂O (Hg²⁺/H⁺) ka final product "ethenol (CH₂=CHOH)" likh kar chhod dena.**
   Ethenol unstable enol hai jo turant tautomerise hokar **Ethanal (CH₃CHO)** ban jaata hai!

3. **Galti 3: Ethyne + 2HBr se 1,2-dibromoethane bana dena.**
   Doosra HBr Markovnikov rule se usi C par Br lagata hai → **1,1-dibromoethane (geminal dihalide)** banta hai!

## Quick Recap

- **Ethyne:** sp linear (180°), C≡C = 120 pm (1 σ + 2 π); Prep: **CaC₂ + 2H₂O** ya vicinal dihalide (**alc. KOH + NaNH₂**).
- **Acidity:** **HC≡CH > CH₂=CH₂ > CH₃−CH₃** (50% s-character); sirf **terminal alkynes** Na/NaNH₂ se react karte hain!
- **Reactions:** + 2HBr → geminal dihalide; **+ H₂O (Hg²⁺/H⁺, 333 K)** → Ethyne se **Ethanal**, higher alkynes se **Ketones**; **3 C₂H₂ (Red-hot Fe, 873 K) → Benzene**!

## Practice Questions

6. Explain why the hydration of **ethyne** gives an **aldehyde (ethanal)**, whereas the hydration of **propyne** gives a **ketone (propanone)**.
7. Show how you can convert **ethyne** into **benzene** in one step, specifying the exact reagent/conditions (temperature and tube material).
8. Compare **ethane (C₂H₆), ethene (C₂H₄) and ethyne (C₂H₂)** in a compact summary on: (a) C hybridisation, (b) C−C bond length (154, 134, 120 pm), and (c) σ and π bond counts.
9. How would you chemically distinguish between **ethene** and **ethyne**? *(Hint: both decolorise Br₂/CCl₄, so use terminal alkyne acidity!)*
10. Write the balanced reactions of **propyne (CH₃−C≡CH)** with: (a) **Na metal**, and (b) **excess H₂ / Ni**.
11. Convert **CaC₂** into **ethanal (CH₃CHO)** in two clean steps!
12. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) But-2-yne reacts with NaNH₂ to liberate ammonia gas.
    (b) Addition of 2 moles of HBr to ethyne gives 1,1-dibromoethane.
    (c) Ethyne is a linear molecule with a bond angle of 180°.
    (d) Passing ethyne through a red-hot iron tube at 873 K yields cyclohexane.

### Answers

1. (a) **CaC₂ + 2H₂O → HC≡CH + Ca(OH)₂**.
   (b) **CH₂Br−CH₂Br + alc. KOH → CH₂=CHBr —(NaNH₂)→ HC≡CH**.

2. Pehle step mein bana **vinyl bromide (CH₂=CHBr)** mein Br ka lone pair C=C ke saath resonance karke partial double-bond character bana leta hai → C−Br bond mazboot ho jaata hai → use todne ke liye alc. KOH se zyada strong base **NaNH₂** chahiye!

3. Ethyne mein Carbon **sp (50% s)** hai, ethene mein **sp² (33.3% s)**, aur ethane mein **sp³ (25% s)**. Zyada s-character ka matlab zyada electronegativity → sp Carbon C−H electrons ko sabse zor se kheenchta hai aur **H⁺** aasani se chhod deta hai!

4. Decreasing acidity: **HC≡CH > CH₃C≡CH > CH₃C≡CCH₃ (non-acidic!)**. CH₃ ka +I effect acetylide anion ko thoda destabilise karta hai, aur but-2-yne mein koi terminal ≡C−H hai hi nahi!

5. (a) **1,1,2,2-Tetrabromoethane (CHBr₂−CHBr₂)**.
   (b) **1,1-Dibromoethane (CH₃−CHBr₂)**.
   (c) **Butan-2-one (CH₃−CH₂−CO−CH₃)** (Markovnikov hydration + tautomerism!).

6. Ethyne symmetrical hai → enol CH₂=CHOH tautomerise hokar **ethanal (CH₃CHO)** banata hai. Propyne unsymmetrical hai → Markovnikov rule se −OH beech wale C2 par lagta hai → enol CH₃−C(OH)=CH₂ tautomerise hokar **propanone (CH₃COCH₃)** banata hai!

7. **3 HC≡CH —(Red-hot Iron tube, 873 K)→ C₆H₆ (Benzene)** (cyclic polymerisation!).

8. **C₂H₆:** sp³, 154 pm, 7σ + 0π; **C₂H₄:** sp², 134 pm, 5σ + 1π; **C₂H₂:** sp, 120 pm, 3σ + 2π.

9. **Ammoniacal AgNO₃** (ya NaNH₂) se test karo: **Ethyne** (terminal alkyne) react karke **white ppt** (silver acetylide) deta hai, jabki **Ethene** koi reaction nahi deta!

10. (a) **CH₃−C≡CH + Na → CH₃−C≡C⁻Na⁺ + ½H₂↑**.
    (b) **CH₃−C≡CH + 2H₂ —(Ni, Δ)→ CH₃−CH₂−CH₃ (Propane)**.

11. Step 1: **CaC₂ + 2H₂O → HC≡CH + Ca(OH)₂**.
    Step 2: **HC≡CH + H₂O —(HgSO₄ / dil. H₂SO₄, 333 K)→ CH₃CHO (Ethanal)**.

12. (a) **False** ❌. But-2-yne internal alkyne hai (koi terminal H nahi) → NaNH₂ se react nahi karta!
    (b) **True** ✅.
    (c) **True** ✅.
    (d) **False** ❌. Red-hot iron tube mein ethyne se **Benzene (C₆H₆)** banta hai (cyclohexane nahi!).
