# Topic 4: Aromatic Hydrocarbons: Benzene, Hückel's Rule, Electrophilic Substitution aur Directive Influence

1825 mein Michael Faraday ne **Benzene (C₆H₆)** khoja. Formula dekhkar lagta tha ki ismein 3 C=C double bonds hain, isliye ye alkenes ki tarah turant **Br₂/CCl₄** aur **KMnO₄** ko decolorise kar dega! Par hairani ki baat: Benzene normal haalat mein **addition reactions ka virodh** karta hai, aur uske **chhahon C−C bonds bilkul barabar (139 pm)** hain! Aaj hum **Benzene ki Resonance Stability (~150 kJ/mol)**, **Hückel's (4n + 2)π Rule**, **5 Electrophilic Aromatic Substitution (EAS) reactions + 3-step mechanism**, aur **Directive Influence** (ortho/para vs meta) seekhenge! *(Suraksha: Benzene toxic & carcinogenic hai — saare reactions sirf padhne ke liye hain!)*

## Part A: Benzene ka Structure, Hückel's Rule aur Preparation

1. **Benzene (C₆H₆) ki 3 Khaas Baatein:**
   - Sabhi 6 C atoms **sp² hybridised** (120°, flat hexagon); kul **12 σ + 3 π** bonds.
   - Sabhi 6 C−C bond lengths bilkul barabar (**139 pm**, jo C−C 154 pm aur C=C 134 pm ke theek beech hain!).
   - **Resonance Energy ≈ 150 kJ/mol** — isi bhari stability ko bachane ke liye benzene **addition** ke bajay **Electrophilic Substitution** pasand karta hai!
2. **Benzene Banane ke 3 Mukhya Tarike:**
   - (a) **Cyclic polymerisation of Ethyne:** 3 HC≡CH —(Red-hot Fe tube, 873 K)→ C₆H₆.
   - (b) **Decarboxylation of Sodium Benzoate:** C₆H₅COONa + NaOH —(CaO, Δ)→ C₆H₆ + Na₂CO₃.
   - (c) **Reduction of Phenol:** C₆H₅OH + **Zn dust** —(Δ)→ C₆H₆ + ZnO.

## Part B: 5 Electrophilic Aromatic Substitutions (EAS) + 3-Step Mechanism + Directive Influence

### 3-Step Universal EAS Mechanism (Har Reaction Mein Wahi 3 Kadam!):
1. **Step 1 (Generation of Electrophile E⁺):** Catalyst ki madad se strong electrophile banta hai (e.g. HNO₃ + 2H₂SO₄ → **NO₂⁺** + H₃O⁺ + 2HSO₄⁻).
2. **Step 2 (Formation of Carbocation / Arenium Ion):** Benzene ka π-cloud E⁺ par attack karta hai → resonance-stabilised **σ-complex (arenium ion)** banta hai *(slow, rate-determining step!)*.
3. **Step 3 (Loss of Proton H⁺):** Base (HSO₄⁻ ya AlCl₄⁻) proton H⁺ lekar aromaticity wapas laata hai → **C₆H₅−E**!

| Reaction | Reagent + Catalyst | **Asli Electrophile (E⁺)** | Product |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Nitration** | Conc. HNO₃ + Conc. H₂SO₄ (323–333 K) | **NO₂⁺** (Nitronium ion) | **Nitrobenzene** (C₆H₅NO₂) |
| **2. Halogenation** | Cl₂ / Br₂ + **anhyd. FeCl₃ / AlCl₃** (dark, cold) | **Cl⁺ / Br⁺** | **Chlorobenzene / Bromobenzene** |
| **3. Sulphonation** | Fuming H₂SO₄ (Oleum: H₂SO₄ + SO₃) | **SO₃** (neutral electrophile!) | **Benzenesulphonic acid** (C₆H₅SO₃H) |
| **4. Friedel–Crafts Alkylation** | R−Cl + **anhyd. AlCl₃** | **R⁺** (e.g. CH₃⁺) | **Alkylbenzene** (e.g. Toluene) |
| **5. Friedel–Crafts Acylation** | RCOCl (ya (RCO)₂O) + **anhyd. AlCl₃** | **R−C⁺=O** (Acylium ion) | **Acylbenzene** (e.g. Acetophenone C₆H₅COCH₃) |

*(Drastic conditions par Addition Reactions: Benzene + 3H₂ —(Ni, high T, P)→ **Cyclohexane (C₆H₁₂)**; Benzene + 3Cl₂ —(UV light, 500 K)→ **BHC / Gammaxene / Lindane (C₆H₆Cl₆)**!)*

### Directive Influence — 3 Groups ka Golden Rule!
1. **Activating & *ortho, para*-directing (+R / +I):** **−NH₂, −OH, −OCH₃, −CH₃, −C₂H₅** → ring ko electron-rich aur tez banate hain!
2. **Deactivating & *meta*-directing (−R, −I):** **−NO₂, −CN, −CHO, −COOH, −SO₃H** → ring ko electron-poor banate hain, *o, p* par δ⁺ laate hain → agla E⁺ *meta* par lagta hai!
3. **SPECIAL CASE — Halogens (−F, −Cl, −Br, −I):** Ye **−I effect se ring ko DEACTIVATE** (dheema) karte hain, par **+R (lone pair) se *ortho, para*-DIRECTING** hote hain!

## Solved Examples

### Example 1 (Hückel's Rule Test). Kaun Aromatic Hai?

**Sawaal:** Hückel's rule se check karo: (a) **Benzene (6π)**, (b) **Naphthalene (10π)**, (c) **Cyclobutadiene (4π)**, (d) **Cyclopentadienyl anion (C₅H₅⁻, 6π)**.

**Hal:**
- (a) **Benzene:** Cyclic, planar, conjugated, 6π (4×1 + 2) → **Aromatic!**
- (b) **Naphthalene:** Cyclic, planar, conjugated, 10π (4×2 + 2) → **Aromatic!**
- (c) **Cyclobutadiene:** 4π (4n, n = 1) → **NOT aromatic (Anti-aromatic!)**.
- (d) **Cyclopentadienyl anion:** 2 double bonds (4π) + 1 lone pair (2π) = **6π** → **Aromatic!**

**Jaanch:** 4n + 2 = 2, 6, 10, 14 → (a), (b), (d) aromatic; (c) 4π anti-aromatic ✅.

---

### Example 2. Directive Influence — Toluene, Nitrobenzene aur Chlorobenzene ka Nitration

**Sawaal:** Conc. HNO₃ + H₂SO₄ ke saath major nitration products likho: (a) **Toluene**, (b) **Nitrobenzene**, (c) **Chlorobenzene**.

**Hal:**
- (a) Toluene (−CH₃, +I/hyperconjugation = *o, p*): ***o*-Nitrotoluene + *p*-Nitrotoluene**!
- (b) Nitrobenzene (−NO₂, −R = *meta*): ***m*-Dinitrobenzene**!
- (c) Chlorobenzene (−Cl, deactivating par +R = *o, p*!): ***o*-Chloronitrobenzene + *p*-Chloronitrobenzene**!

**Jaanch:** −CH₃ aur −Cl dono *o, p*-directing; −NO₂ *meta*-directing ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: "−Cl deactivating hai, isliye wo *meta*-directing hoga" likh dena!**
   *Sahi baat:* Halogens (−Cl, −Br) **deactivating (−I > +R)** hote hue bhi ***ortho, para*-directing (+R)** hote hain!

2. **Galti 2: Nitration ka electrophile "NO₂" ya Sulphonation ka "HSO₄⁻" likh dena!**
   Sahi electrophiles: Nitration = **NO₂⁺ (nitronium ion)**; Sulphonation = **SO₃ (neutral sulphur trioxide)**!

3. **Galti 3: Cyclobutadiene (4π) ya Cyclooctatetraene (8π) ko aromatic bol dena.**
   Dono 4n π-electrons wale hain (4n + 2 nahi!) → **NOT aromatic!**

## Quick Recap

- **Benzene:** 6 sp² C, 139 pm all C−C equal, Resonance energy ~150 kJ/mol → **EAS** pasand karta hai!
- **Hückel's Rule:** Cyclic + Planar + Conjugated + **(4n + 2)π electrons** (2, 6, 10, 14).
- **5 EAS Electrophiles:** Nitration (**NO₂⁺**), Halogenation (**Cl⁺/Br⁺**), Sulphonation (**SO₃**), FC Alkylation (**R⁺**), FC Acylation (**RCO⁺**).
- **Directive Influence:** +R/+I → *o, p* activating; −R → *meta* deactivating; **Halogens → *o, p* deactivating**!

## Practice Questions

1. State the **four conditions** of **Hückel's Rule** for aromaticity, and classify each of the following as **aromatic** or **non-aromatic / anti-aromatic**:
   (a) **Anthracene (3 fused rings, 14π)**
   (b) **Cyclopropenyl cation (C₃H₃⁺, 2π)**
   (c) **Cyclooctatetraene (C₈H₈, 8π, tub-shaped)**
   (d) **Pyridine (C₅H₅N, 6π)**
2. Write the **balanced equations** for preparing **benzene** from: (a) **phenol**, (b) **sodium benzoate**, and (c) **ethyne**.
3. Name the **exact electrophile (E⁺)** generated in each of the five EAS reactions of benzene: (a) Nitration, (b) Chlorination, (c) Sulphonation, (d) Friedel–Crafts Alkylation, (e) Friedel–Crafts Acylation.
4. Write the **three-step mechanism** for the **nitration of benzene** using conc. HNO₃ and conc. H₂SO₄. *(Corrosive/toxic: paper problem only.)*
5. Why does **benzene** resist addition reactions (such as decolorising Br₂/CCl₄) under normal conditions and readily undergo **electrophilic substitution** instead?
6. Explain the dual behaviour of **chlorine (−Cl)** in **chlorobenzene**: why is it **ring-deactivating** yet ***ortho, para*-directing**?
7. Write the major product of each reaction:
   (a) **Benzene + CH₃COCl —(anhyd. AlCl₃)→ ?**
   (b) **Benzene + 3Cl₂ —(UV light, 500 K)→ ?**
   (c) **Benzene + 3H₂ —(Ni, high T, P)→ ?**
8. Show how you would convert **phenol** into **acetophenone (C₆H₅COCH₃)** in two clean steps!
9. Why does **nitrobenzene** NOT undergo **Friedel–Crafts alkylation or acylation**? *(Hint: −NO₂ is strongly deactivating AND complexes with AlCl₃!)*
10. Classify the following substituents on benzene as **activating *o, p***, **deactivating *o, p***, or **deactivating *meta***: **−NH₂, −CHO, −Br, −CH₃, −CN, −OH, −COOH**.
11. Why are all six C−C bond lengths in benzene equal to **139 pm** instead of alternating 154 pm and 134 pm?
12. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) In sulphonation of benzene, the active electrophile is neutral SO₃.
    (b) Cyclobutadiene (4π electrons) obeys Hückel's rule and is aromatic.
    (c) The product of benzene + 3Cl₂ in UV light is benzene hexachloride (BHC, C₆H₆Cl₆).
    (d) Nitration of toluene gives mainly *m*-nitrotoluene.

### Answers

1. 4 Shartein: Cyclic, Planar, Complete conjugation, (4n + 2)π electrons.
   (a) **Anthracene (14π, n = 3):** **Aromatic**.
   (b) **Cyclopropenyl cation (2π, n = 0):** **Aromatic**.
   (c) **Cyclooctatetraene (8π, non-planar tub):** **Non-aromatic**.
   (d) **Pyridine (6π, n = 1):** **Aromatic**.

2. (a) **C₆H₅OH + Zn dust —(Δ)→ C₆H₆ + ZnO**.
   (b) **C₆H₅COONa + NaOH —(CaO, Δ)→ C₆H₆ + Na₂CO₃**.
   (c) **3 HC≡CH —(Red-hot Fe tube, 873 K)→ C₆H₆**.

3. (a) **NO₂⁺**, (b) **Cl⁺**, (c) **SO₃**, (d) **R⁺**, (e) **R−C⁺=O**.

4. Step 1: HNO₃ + 2H₂SO₄ → **NO₂⁺** + H₃O⁺ + 2HSO₄⁻.
   Step 2: C₆H₆ + NO₂⁺ → **[C₆H₆NO₂]⁺** (arenium σ-complex, slow step).
   Step 3: [C₆H₆NO₂]⁺ + HSO₄⁻ → **C₆H₅NO₂** + H₂SO₄ (aromaticity restored!).

5. Benzene mein 6π electrons ki delocalisation se **~150 kJ/mol resonance energy** ki bhari stability milti hai. Addition karne par aromaticity toot jaati hai, jabki **electrophilic substitution** mein Step 3 ke baad aromatic ring bilkul salamat wapas mil jaati hai!

6. Chlorine ka **−I effect** (high EN) ring se σ-electrons kheenchkar electron density kam karta hai (**deactivating**); par uske lone pairs ka **+R effect** *ortho* aur *para* par electron density badhata hai (***o, p*-directing**)!

7. (a) **Acetophenone (C₆H₅COCH₃) + HCl** (Friedel–Crafts acylation!).
   (b) **Benzene hexachloride / BHC (C₆H₆Cl₆)** (Gammaxene!).
   (c) **Cyclohexane (C₆H₁₂)**.

8. Step 1: **Phenol + Zn dust —(Δ)→ Benzene + ZnO**.
   Step 2: **Benzene + CH₃COCl —(anhyd. AlCl₃)→ Acetophenone + HCl**.

9. **−NO₂** bahut strong electron-withdrawing (−R, −I) group hai jo benzene ring ko buri tarah deactivate kar deta hai, aur Lewis acid catalyst **AlCl₃** ke saath khud coordinate karke use bekaar kar deta hai!

10. **Activating *o, p*:** −NH₂, −CH₃, −OH. **Deactivating *o, p*:** −Br. **Deactivating *meta*:** −CHO, −CN, −COOH.

11. Benzene dono Kekulé structures ka **Resonance Hybrid** hai — 6π electrons sabhi 6 C−C bonds par barabar phailte hain (bond order = 1.5), isliye sabhi 6 bonds barabar (**139 pm**) hote hain!

12. (a) **True** ✅.
    (b) **False** ❌. 4π electrons = 4n (Hückel 4n + 2 nahi!) → cyclobutadiene **anti-aromatic** hai.
    (c) **True** ✅.
    (d) **False** ❌. −CH₃ *o, p*-directing hai → toluene ka nitration ***o*- aur *p*-nitrotoluene** deta hai!
