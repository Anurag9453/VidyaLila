# Topic 5: Carcinogenicity aur Toxicity of Benzene aur Polynuclear Aromatic Hydrocarbons (PAHs)

Itihas mein benzene aur uske parivaar ko unki khaas gandh ke kaaran **"Aromatic"** naam de diya gaya tha — par is shabd se kabhi dhokha mat khaana! Aadhunik medical science ne sabit kar diya hai ki **Benzene** aur **do se zyada judi hui benzene rings** wale **Polynuclear Aromatic Hydrocarbons (PAHs)** behad **toxic aur carcinogenic (cancer paida karne wale)** hote hain! Aaj hum samjhenge ki ye khatarnak compounds kahan se aate hain, sharir ke andar **DNA** ko kaise nuksaan pahunchate hain, aur hum khud ko aur paryavaran ko inse kaise bacha sakte hain.

---

## Dinesh, petrol pump ka warning sticker aur sadak kinare kaala dhuaan

Dinesh apne Papa ke saath petrol bharwane gaya tha. Nozzle ke upar ek bada peela warning sticker laga tha: **"CAUTION: Petrol contains Benzene — Do NOT inhale fuel vapours!"** Ghar laut-te waqt sadak kinare ke paudhon ke patton par diesel gaadiyon ke **kaale dhuen ki kaalikh (soot)** jami hui thi.

Dinesh ne poochha, "Papa, Chemistry mein to benzene ko 'aromatic' kehte hain — phir iski bhaap itni khatarnak kyun hai?" Papa ne samjhaya, "Dinesh, petrol, koyla ya tambaku jab **adhoori aanch (incomplete combustion)** mein jalte hain, to kaale dhuen mein **Polynuclear Aromatic Hydrocarbons (PAHs)** bante hain — jo saans ke zariye sharir mein jaakar DNA ko bigaad kar **cancer** ka khatra badha dete hain!" Chalo, iski poori chemistry aur safety rules samajhte hain.

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Carcinogen** | Aisa chemical ya agent jo sharir ki koshikaon (cells) ke DNA ko nuksaan pahunchakar **cancer** paida kar sakta hai | Benzene, 1,2-Benzpyrene |
| **Polynuclear Aromatic Hydrocarbons (PAHs)** | **Do se zyada benzene rings** aapas mein judkar (fused hokar) banne wale aromatic hydrocarbons | 1,2-Benzanthracene, 3-Methylcholanthrene, **1,2-Benzpyrene**, 1,2,5,6-Dibenzanthracene |

| **Mutation** | Cell ke **DNA** ke genetic code mein hone wala permanent bigaad, jo aniyantrit cell division (tumour/cancer) shuru kar sakta hai | PAH epoxides ka DNA se chipakna |

## Part A: Famous Carcinogenic PAHs aur Unke Rozmarra ke Srot (Sources)

### 4 Mashhoor Carcinogenic Polynuclear Hydrocarbons (Class 11 Key Names!):
1. **1,2-Benzanthracene** (4 fused benzene rings)
2. **3-Methylcholanthrene** (5-ring system — bahut tez carcinogen!)
3. **1,2-Benzpyrene** *(Benzo[a]pyrene — 5 fused rings, cigarette smoke aur vehicle soot mein sabse aam!)*
4. **1,2,5,6-Dibenzanthracene** (5 fused rings)

### Ye Kahan Se Aate Hain? (Incomplete Combustion ke 4 Bade Srot)
1. **Tobacco / Cigarette Smoke:** Tambaku ke adhoori tarah jalne se **1,2-benzpyrene** aur kai PAHs seedhe phephdon (lungs) mein jaate hain (lung cancer ka bada kaaran!).
2. **Vehicle Exhaust & Coal Burning:** Purane petrol/diesel engines aur koyle ke bhatton mein kam O₂ par hydrocarbons poori tarah CO₂ + H₂O nahi ban paate → soot aur PAHs nikalte hain!
3. **Over-charred / Burnt Food:** Khana ya barbecue jab bahut zyada kaala jal jaata hai, to jale hue hisse mein bhi trace PAHs ban jaate hain.
4. **Benzene Vapours:** Benzene bone marrow ko nuksaan pahunchata hai aur **leukaemia (blood cancer)** ka khatra badhata hai — isliye ab school labs mein benzene ki jagah surakshit solvents use kiye jaate hain!

## Part B: PAHs Sharir ke Andar Cancer Kaise Paida Karte Hain? (3-Step Pathway)

```
  Incomplete Combustion          Liver Enzymes            Reactive Epoxides
  (Smoke / Soot / Fuel)   --->  (Biochemical      --->   bind covalently to DNA
      PAHs inhaled               Oxidation)                     :
                                                                v
                                                    DNA Damage & Mutation
                                                                :
                                                                v
                                                 Uncontrolled Cell Growth (Cancer!)
```

> **Figure 5.1** – PAHs ki carcinogenicity ka 3-step biochemical rasta: sharir mein pahunch kar PAHs reactive oxides/epoxides mein badalte hain, jo DNA se chipak kar genetic code (mutation) bigaad dete hain.

### Bachav aur Suraksha ke 4 Pakke Upaay:
1. **Catalytic Converters in Vehicles:** Gaadiyon ke silencer mein lage catalytic converters unburnt hydrocarbons aur CO ko **CO₂ aur H₂O** mein badalkar PAH pollution ko bahut kam kar dete hain!
2. **Low-Benzene / Unleaded Fuels & Clean Energy (CNG / EV):** Benzene ki matra petrol mein sakhti se seemit (< 1%) ki jaati hai.
3. **Tobacco-Free Life:** Cigarette/bidi ke dhuen (aur secondhand smoke) se poori tarah door rehna!
4. **Lab Safety Rules:** Organic solvents (benzene, chloroform, toluene) ko kabhi **soonghna (inhale) nahi** chahiye, hamesha **fume hood** (teacher supervision) mein hi kaam hona chahiye!

## Solved Examples

### Example 1. Complete banaam Incomplete Combustion ka Antar

**Sawaal:** Octane (petrol ka hissa, C₈H₁₈) ke **complete** aur **incomplete** combustion mein kya antar hai, aur kaunsa PAHs banata hai?

**Hal:**
- **Complete combustion (paryapt O₂):** 2C₈H₁₈ + 25O₂ → **16CO₂ + 18H₂O** (saaf neeli lauh, koi soot ya PAH nahi!).
- **Incomplete combustion (kam O₂):** **CO (zehreeli gas) + C (kaala soot) + toxic PAHs (1,2-benzpyrene)** bante hain — yahi swasthya ke liye behad khatarnak hai!

**Jaanch:** Complete combustion = CO₂ + H₂O; Incomplete = CO + soot + PAHs ✅.

---

### Example 2. Benzene banaam 1,2-Benzpyrene aur Bachav ke Tarike

**Sawaal:** (a) Benzene aur 1,2-Benzpyrene mein structural antar kya hai? (b) Gaadiyon se PAHs kam karne ka ek engineering upaay batao.

**Hal:**
- (a) **Benzene** mein sirf **1 aromatic ring** (C₆H₆) hai; **1,2-Benzpyrene** ek PAH hai jismein **5 fused benzene rings** hoti hain — dono carcinogenic hain!
- (b) Gaadiyon mein **Catalytic Converter** lagana aur engine ki regular tuning (paryapt O₂ supply) rakhna!

**Jaanch:** PAH = more than two fused benzene rings ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: "Aromatic" shabd dekh kar benzene ya toluene ko surakshit/khushboodar maan kar lab mein soonghna!**
   *Sahi baat:* Benzene aur kai aromatic solvents **toxic aur carcinogenic** hain — lab mein kisi bhi chemical ko kabhi seedhe naak ke paas le jaakar mat soongho!

2. **Galti 2: Ye sochna ki PAHs complete combustion mein bante hain.**
   PAHs hamesha **INCOMPLETE combustion** (kam oxygen par koyla, petrol, tambaku jalne) mein bante hain!

## Quick Recap

- **Benzene & PAHs** (> 2 fused benzene rings) **toxic aur carcinogenic** hote hain.
- **4 Famous PAHs:** 1,2-Benzanthracene, 3-Methylcholanthrene, **1,2-Benzpyrene**, 1,2,5,6-Dibenzanthracene.
- **Sources:** Tobacco smoke, incomplete combustion of coal/petroleum, charred food.
- **Mechanism:** PAHs → biochemical oxidation → reactive intermediates → **DNA damage / mutation → cancer**.
- **Prevention:** Catalytic converters, clean fuels, no tobacco, lab fume hoods.

## Practice Questions

1. What are **Polynuclear Aromatic Hydrocarbons (PAHs)**? Name any **three** well-known carcinogenic PAHs.
2. Explain clearly how **PAHs** formed during incomplete combustion cause **cancer** inside the human body (trace the biochemical steps).
3. List **three major everyday sources** of carcinogenic polynuclear hydrocarbons in our environment.
4. Why is **benzene** no longer used as a routine cleaning or extraction solvent in school chemistry laboratories? Which blood disorder is linked to long-term benzene exposure?
5. Contrast the products of **complete combustion** and **incomplete combustion** of hydrocarbons. Which one produces PAHs and carbon monoxide?
6. How do **catalytic converters** fitted in modern car exhaust systems reduce the emission of toxic hydrocarbons and PAHs?
7. Why is the historical name **"aromatic"** misleading when it comes to laboratory safety?
8. Explain why **avoiding tobacco smoke** (both active and passive/secondhand smoking) dramatically lowers a person's exposure to **1,2-benzpyrene**.
9. State **three essential safety precautions** that must always be followed whenever volatile aromatic liquids are handled by trained chemists or teachers.
10. Why does over-charring (burning black) meat or food over an open coal fire increase the risk of ingesting trace PAHs?
11. How does switching city buses from old diesel engines to **CNG (methane)** or **electric vehicles** help lower urban PAH levels?
12. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) Benzene and polynuclear hydrocarbons with more than two fused rings are generally toxic and carcinogenic.
    (b) PAHs are produced mainly during complete combustion of hydrocarbons in excess oxygen.
    (c) Inside the body, PAHs undergo biochemical reactions that ultimately damage DNA.
    (d) 1,2-Benzpyrene is a harmless flavouring compound found in fruit juices.

### Answers

1. **PAHs** wo aromatic hydrocarbons hain jinmein **do se zyada benzene rings** aapas mein judi (fused) hoti hain. Teen mashhoor carcinogenic PAHs: **1,2-Benzanthracene**, **1,2-Benzpyrene** aur **1,2,5,6-Dibenzanthracene** (ya 3-Methylcholanthrene).

2. Saans ya bhojan se sharir mein pahunch kar PAHs enzymes dwara **reactive oxidised intermediates (epoxides)** mein badal jaate hain → ye **DNA** ke saath covalently jud kar uska genetic code bigaad (**mutation**) dete hain → isse cells aniyantrit roop se badhne lagti hain (**cancer**)!

3. Teen bade srot: (1) **Tobacco/cigarette smoke**, (2) **Vehicles aur coal plants ka incomplete combustion exhaust**, (3) **Bahut zyada jala hua (charred) khana**.

4. Benzene ki bhaap behad toxic aur carcinogenic hai — lambe samay tak sampark mein rehne se bone marrow ko nuksaan aur **leukaemia (blood cancer)** ka khatra hota hai! Isliye school labs mein ise aam solvent ke roop mein use nahi karte.

5. **Complete combustion (excess O₂):** Sirf CO₂ + H₂O. **Incomplete combustion (kam O₂):** Zehreeli CO gas, kaala soot aur carcinogenic **PAHs**!

6. Catalytic converters mein Pt/Pd/Rh catalyst unburnt hydrocarbons aur CO ko excess hawa ke saath poori tarah **CO₂ aur H₂O** mein oxidise kar dete hain, jisse PAH emissions bahut kam ho jaate hain.

7. "Aromatic" shabd purane zamane mein khushboo (*aroma*) se aaya tha, par iska matlab "surakshit" bilkul nahi — benzene aur PAHs jaanleva carcinogens hain!

8. Tambaku ke adhoori tarah jalne se dhuen mein **1,2-benzpyrene** aur kai doosre PAHs seedhe phephdon mein jaate hain; tambaku aur secondhand smoke se door rehne par is exposure se poori tarah bacha ja sakta hai.

9. (1) Hamesha **fume hood** (achhi ventilation) mein kaam karna; (2) Kabhi organic vapours ko **inhale/soonghna nahi**; (3) Gloves aur safety goggles pehnna aur aag/spark se door rakhna (kyunki ye flammable hain!).

10. Koyle ki aanch par khane ke tel/fat ke adhoori tarah jalne (incomplete combustion) se kaali jali hui parat mein trace **PAHs** ban jaate hain.

11. CNG (methane, CH₄) saaf aur lagbhag complete combustion karta hai (soot aur PAHs na ke barabar), aur electric vehicles mein exhaust dhuaan bilkul **zero** hota hai!

12. (a) **True** ✅.
    (b) **False** ❌. PAHs **incomplete combustion** (kam O₂) mein bante hain!
    (c) **True** ✅.
    (d) **False** ❌. 1,2-Benzpyrene ek khatarnak **carcinogenic PAH** hai!
