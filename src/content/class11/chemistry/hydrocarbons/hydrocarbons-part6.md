## Mixed Worked Examples

### Mixed Example 1 (Roadmap 1). CaC₂ → Ethyne → Benzene → Toluene → *o/p*-Nitrotoluene!

**Hal:**
- **Step 1 (Topic 3):** CaC₂ + 2H₂O → **HC≡CH (Ethyne)** + Ca(OH)₂
- **Step 2 (Topic 3):** 3 HC≡CH —(Red-hot Fe tube, 873 K)→ **C₆H₆ (Benzene)**
- **Step 3 (Topic 4 — Friedel–Crafts Alkylation):** C₆H₆ + CH₃Cl —(anhyd. AlCl₃)→ **C₆H₅CH₃ (Toluene)** + HCl
- **Step 4 (Topic 4 — Nitration, −CH₃ is *o, p*-directing!):** C₆H₅CH₃ + conc. HNO₃/H₂SO₄ → ***o*-Nitrotoluene + *p*-Nitrotoluene**!

**Jaanch:** Har step mein exact reagent aur directive influence match karta hai ✅!

---

### Mixed Example 2 (Roadmap 2). Ozonolysis Deduction + Markovnikov vs Peroxide Effect

**Sawaal:** Ek alkene **A (C₅H₁₀)** reductive ozonolysis (O₃, Zn/H₂O) par **ethanal (CH₃CHO)** aur **propanone ((CH₃)₂CO)** deta hai. (a) **A** pehchano, aur (b) **A + HBr** (bina peroxide) aur **A + HBr (peroxide ke saath)** ke major products likho!

**Hal:**
- **(a) Reverse Ozonolysis:** CH₃CH=**O** + **O**=C(CH₃)₂ → **A = CH₃−CH=C(CH₃)₂ (2-Methylbut-2-ene)**!
- **(b) HBr Additions:**
  - **Bina peroxide (Markovnikov — Br kam H wale C2 par, 3° carbocation!):** **2-Bromo-2-methylbutane**!
  - **Peroxide ke saath (Anti-Markovnikov — Br zyada H wale C3 par!):** **2-Bromo-3-methylbutane**!

**Jaanch:** 2-Methylbut-2-ene = C₅H₁₀; dono HBr products alag positions (C2 vs C3) par hain ✅!

### Mixed Example 3 (Roadmap 3). Ethane, Ethene, Ethyne aur Benzene ki Pehchaan!

| Test | **Ethane** (Alkane) | **Ethene** (Alkene) | **Ethyne** (Terminal Alkyne) | **Benzene** (Aromatic) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Br₂ / CCl₄ (Orange-red)** | No change | **Decolorises!** | **Decolorises!** | **No change!** (EAS needs FeBr₃) |
| **2. Baeyer's cold dil. KMnO₄** | No change | **Decolorises!** | **Decolorises!** | **No change!** |
| **3. Ammoniacal AgNO₃** | No change | No change | **White ppt!** | No change |
| **4. Conc. HNO₃ + H₂SO₄** | No change | — | — | **Nitrobenzene (oily yellow)** |

**Jaanch:** Sirf Ethyne white ppt deta hai; sirf Ethene & Ethyne Br₂/KMnO₄ decolorise karte hain; Benzene EAS deta hai par Br₂/CCl₄ nahi ✅!

## Aam galtiyan (mixed hydrocarbon problems mein bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: Benzene se *p*-Bromonitrobenzene banane ke liye pehle Nitration kar dena!**
   *Dekho kya hota hai:* Agar pehle Nitration kiya to **Nitrobenzene (−NO₂ = *meta*-directing!)** ban jaayega, aur phir Bromination karne par ***m*-bromonitrobenzene** milega!
   *Sahi Order:* ***p*-Bromonitrobenzene** ke liye pehle **Bromination** karo (**−Br = *o, p*-directing!**), phir **Nitration** karo!

2. **Galti 2: Benzene ko bhi Ethene ki tarah Br₂/CCl₄ decolorise karwa dena.**
   Resonance stability (~150 kJ/mol) ke kaaran benzene normal Br₂/CCl₄ ya Baeyer's KMnO₄ ke saath **bilkul react nahi** karta!

## Practice Questions (Sab Kuch Mila Kar — 12 Fresh Questions)

1. Carry out the following **two-step conversions** clearly showing reagents and conditions:
   (a) **Benzene → *p*-Bromonitrobenzene** *(Choose the order of Nitration vs Bromination carefully!)*
   (b) **Benzene → *m*-Bromonitrobenzene**
2. Carry out these conversions:
   (a) **Ethyne → Ethanal**
   (b) **Propene → 1-Bromopropane**
   (c) **Propene → 2-Bromopropane**
   (d) **Sodium benzoate → Benzene → Acetophenone**
3. An alkene **X (C₆H₁₂)** on reductive ozonolysis gives **only propanone (2 moles of CH₃COCH₃)**. Identify **X**, write its IUPAC name, and predict its major product with **HBr** (no peroxide).
4. Compare **Wurtz reaction** and **Soda-lime decarboxylation** for preparing alkanes: which one **increases** the carbon chain length (doubles it), and which one **decreases** it by one carbon? Give one equation for each.
5. Using **Hückel's Rule**, identify which of the following are **aromatic**: (a) **Benzene**, (b) **Cyclopentadienyl anion (6π)**, (c) **Cyclobutadiene (4π)**, (d) **Naphthalene (10π)**.
6. Why is the **staggered conformation** of ethane more stable than the **eclipsed conformation** by **12.5 kJ/mol**?
7. Write the major product of **2-bromopentane + alc. KOH (Δ)** and name the rule that predicts it.
8. Arrange **ethyne, ethene and ethane** in **decreasing order of acidity**, and give a one-line chemical test to distinguish **propene** from **propyne**.
9. What happens when **benzene** is treated with:
   (a) **Cl₂ + anhyd. FeCl₃ (dark, cold)**
   (b) **3Cl₂ + UV light (500 K)**
   State the type of reaction (substitution vs addition) in each case!
10. Convert **n-hexane** into **nitrobenzene** in two clean steps!
11. Why does incomplete combustion of petrol or tobacco pose a serious **carcinogenic hazard**? Name any two carcinogenic **PAHs** formed.
12. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) To convert benzene into *p*-bromonitrobenzene, nitration must be carried out before bromination.
    (b) Benzene readily decolorises cold, dilute alkaline KMnO₄ (Baeyer's reagent).
    (c) Reductive ozonolysis of cyclohexene gives hexanedial.
    (d) Peroxide effect works with HBr, HCl and HI equally well.

### Answers

1. (a) **Benzene → *p*-Bromonitrobenzene:** Pehle **Bromination** (Br₂ / FeBr₃ → Bromobenzene, −Br is *o, p*-directing!) → phir **Nitration** (conc. HNO₃ + H₂SO₄ → ***p*-bromonitrobenzene** major + *o*-isomer)!
   (b) **Benzene → *m*-Bromonitrobenzene:** Pehle **Nitration** (conc. HNO₃ + H₂SO₄ → Nitrobenzene, −NO₂ is *meta*-directing!) → phir **Bromination** (Br₂ / FeBr₃ → ***m*-bromonitrobenzene**)!

2. (a) **HC≡CH + H₂O —(HgSO₄ / dil. H₂SO₄, 333 K)→ CH₃CHO**.
   (b) **CH₃−CH=CH₂ + HBr —(Peroxide)→ CH₃−CH₂−CH₂Br**.
   (c) **CH₃−CH=CH₂ + HBr (no peroxide) → CH₃−CH(Br)−CH₃**.
   (d) **C₆H₅COONa + NaOH —(CaO, Δ)→ C₆H₆ —(CH₃COCl, anhyd. AlCl₃)→ C₆H₅COCH₃**.

3. Do (CH₃)₂C=**O** ke O hatakar jodo → **X = (CH₃)₂C=C(CH₃)₂ (2,3-Dimethylbut-2-ene)**!
   Symmetrical alkene hai → + HBr gives **2-bromo-2,3-dimethylbutane**!
   Jaanch: C₆H₁₂ → 2 × C₃H₆O ✅.

4. **Wurtz reaction** carbon chain ko **double** karta hai (2CH₃Br + 2Na → C₂H₆ + 2NaBr); **Soda-lime decarboxylation** carbon chain ko **1 Carbon kam** karta hai (CH₃CH₂COONa + NaOH —CaO, Δ→ C₂H₆ + Na₂CO₃).

5. **Aromatic:** (a) **Benzene (6π)**, (b) **Cyclopentadienyl anion (6π)**, (d) **Naphthalene (10π)**. **Anti-aromatic:** (c) Cyclobutadiene (4π).

6. Staggered form (60°) mein C−H bonds sabse door hote hain → **minimum torsional strain**; Eclipsed form (0°) mein C−H bonds aamne-saamne aakar electron-cloud repulsion (12.5 kJ/mol) paida karte hain.

7. **Pent-2-ene** (CH₃−CH=CH−CH₂−CH₃, 5 α-H, major!) — **Saytzeff's Rule** (zyada substituted alkene major!).

8. Decreasing acidity: **Ethyne (sp, 50% s) > Ethene (sp², 33.3% s) > Ethane (sp³, 25% s)**.
   **Test:** Ammoniacal AgNO₃ milao — **Propyne** (terminal alkyne) **white ppt** deta hai, **Propene** koi reaction nahi deta!

9. (a) **Chlorobenzene (C₆H₅Cl) + HCl** (**Electrophilic Substitution**).
   (b) **Benzene hexachloride / BHC (C₆H₆Cl₆)** (**Free-Radical Addition**).

10. Step 1 (Aromatisation): **n-Hexane —(Cr₂O₃/Al₂O₃, 773 K)→ Benzene + 4H₂**.
    Step 2 (Nitration): **Benzene + conc. HNO₃ / H₂SO₄ (330 K)→ Nitrobenzene + H₂O**.

11. Incomplete combustion se **PAHs** bante hain jo sharir mein oxidise hokar **DNA** ko damage (mutation) karte hain aur **cancer** paida karte hain. Do PAHs: **1,2-Benzpyrene** aur **1,2-Benzanthracene**.

12. (a) **False** ❌. *p*-Bromonitrobenzene ke liye **Bromination pehle** karna zaroori hai!
    (b) **False** ❌. Benzene resonance stability ke kaaran Baeyer's KMnO₄ ko decolorise **nahi** karta.
    (c) **True** ✅.
    (d) **False** ❌. Peroxide effect **SIRF HBr** ke saath kaam karta hai!
