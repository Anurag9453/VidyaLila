# Topic 1: Classification of Hydrocarbons aur Alkanes (Preparation, Properties aur Conformations)

LPG (propane + butane), CNG (methane), petrol, diesel aur kerosene — hamari rozmarra ki energy ke sabse bade srot sirf **Carbon aur Hydrogen** se bane compounds hain, jinhein **Hydrocarbons** kehte hain! Inmein sabse shaant parivaar hai **Alkanes** (CₙH₂ₙ₊₂, sirf C−C aur C−H σ bonds → kam reactive, isliye inhein **Paraffins** = *parum affinis* = kam aakarshan kehte hain!). Aaj hum alkanes banane ke sabhi 5 mukhya tarike, branching ka boiling point par asar, free-radical halogenation, aromatisation, aur **Ethane ke Conformations (Staggered vs Eclipsed)** seekhenge.

## Santosh aur ek hi straw par ghoomti do phirkiyan

Santosh ne ek straw (axle) ke dono siron par **do teen-pankhudi wali kagaz ki phirkiyan (pinwheels)** lagayin. Jab usne aage wali phirki ko ghumaya, to do khaas haalatein banin:
1. **Eclipsed haalat:** Aage wale teen pankh theek peeche wale teen pankhon ke **seedhe saamne (0° par)** aa gaye — sab ek-doosre ke bahut paas aur bheed mein!
2. **Staggered haalat:** Aage wale pankh peeche wale pankhon ke **theek beech ki khaali jagah (60° par)** aa gaye — sabse zyada khuli jagah aur sabse kam takraav!

Teacher ma'am ne muskurate hue kaha, "Santosh, **Ethane (CH₃−CH₃)** ke C−C single bond par free rotation hone se bilkul yahi **infinite Conformations** bante hain — jinmein **Staggered sabse zyada stable** aur **Eclipsed sabse kam stable** hota hai!" Chalo, alkanes ki poori chemistry seekhte hain.

| **Wurtz reaction** | Dry ether mein 2 alkyl halides (RX) ka **Na metal** se react karke **dugne (even-C) symmetrical alkane** banana | 2CH₃Br + 2Na —(dry ether)→ CH₃−CH₃ + 2NaBr |
| **Decarboxylation** | Carboxylic acid ke sodium salt ko **soda lime (NaOH + CaO)** ke saath garam karke **CO₂ (as Na₂CO₃)** hatana → **1 Carbon KAM** wala alkane! | CH₃COONa + NaOH —(CaO, Δ)→ CH₄ + Na₂CO₃ |

## Part A: Classification of Hydrocarbons aur Alkanes ki 5 Preparation Methods

**Classification:** (1) **Saturated** (Alkanes, C−C single bond), (2) **Unsaturated** (Alkenes C=C, Alkynes C≡C), (3) **Aromatic** (Benzene aur uske derivatives).

### Alkanes Banane ke 5 Mukhya Tarike *(Na/dry ether aur flammable gases: sirf padhne ke liye!)*
1. **Catalytic Hydrogenation (Sabatier–Senderens):** Alkene/Alkyne + H₂ —(Pt/Pd at room T, ya Ni at 523–573 K)→ Alkane.
   - CH₂=CH₂ + H₂ —(Ni, Δ)→ CH₃−CH₃.
2. **Reduction of Alkyl Halides:** R−X + H₂ —(Zn, dil. HCl)→ R−H + HX *(R−F nahi hota!)*.
3. **Wurtz Reaction (Symmetrical Even-C Alkanes):**
   - **2 R−X + 2 Na —(dry ether)→ R−R + 2 NaX**
   - *Seema:* Isse **Methane (CH₄)** nahi ban sakta (minimum 2 C = ethane!), aur do alag halides (R−X + R'−X) lene par 3 alkanes (R−R, R'−R', R−R') ka mushkil mixture banta hai!
4. **Soda-Lime Decarboxylation (1 Carbon GHATANA!):**
   - **R−COONa + NaOH —(CaO, Δ)→ R−H + Na₂CO₃**
   - *(Yahan 1 C kam hota hai — isliye sodium ethanoate (2 C) se **Methane (1 C)** banta hai!)*
5. **Kolbe's Electrolytic Method:**
   - **2 R−COONa + 2 H₂O —(Electrolysis)→ R−R + 2 CO₂ (Anode) + H₂ (Cathode) + 2 NaOH**
   - *(Isse bhi **Methane nahi** ban sakta — minimum Ethane banta hai!)*

## Part B: Physical Properties, Chemical Reactions aur Ethane Conformations

1. **Boiling Point Rule:**
   - Alkanes non-polar hain (weak van der Waals forces). Carbon chain badhne par boiling point badhta hai (C₁–C₄ gases, C₅–C₁₇ liquids, C₁₈+ waxy solids).
   - **Branching Rule (Exam Favorite!):** Same formula wale isomers mein **branching badhne par boiling point GHAT-TA hai!** Kyun? Branching se molecule gol (spherical) ho jaata hai → **surface area kam** → weak van der Waals forces!
   - Example: ***n*-Pentane (309 K) > Isopentane (301 K) > Neopentane (282.5 K)**!

2. **5 Mukhya Chemical Reactions of Alkanes:**
   - **Free-Radical Halogenation (Substitution):** CH₄ + Cl₂ —(hν)→ CH₃Cl → CH₂Cl₂ → CHCl₃ → CCl₄.
     - *3 Steps:* (i) **Initiation** (Cl₂ —hν→ 2Cl•), (ii) **Propagation** (CH₄ + Cl• → CH₃• + HCl; CH₃• + Cl₂ → CH₃Cl + Cl•), (iii) **Termination** (radicals combine).
     - *Reactivity:* Halogens **F₂ > Cl₂ > Br₂ > I₂** (iodination reversible → HIO₃/HNO₃ lagate hain!); H-abstraction **3° H > 2° H > 1° H**!
   - **Complete Combustion:** CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ + ((3n+1)/2)O₂ → nCO₂ + (n+1)H₂O + Heat *(Incomplete combustion se carbon black banta hai!)*.
   - **Controlled Oxidation:** 2CH₄ + O₂ —(Cu, 523 K, 100 atm)→ 2CH₃OH; CH₄ + O₂ —(Mo₂O₃, Δ)→ HCHO + H₂O.
   - **Isomerisation:** *n*-Hexane —(anhyd. AlCl₃ + HCl)→ branched hexanes (petrol octane number badhane mein!).
   - **Aromatisation (Reforming):** ***n*-Hexane —(Cr₂O₃ / V₂O₅ / Mo₂O₃, 773 K, 10–20 atm)→ Benzene (C₆H₆) + 4H₂**! *(n-Heptane se Toluene banta hai!)*
   - **Pyrolysis (Cracking):** Bhaari alkanes ko high T par chhote alkanes/alkenes mein todna.

3. **Conformations of Ethane (Sawhorse & Newman Projections):**
   - **Staggered (dihedral angle 60°):** C−H bonds sabse door → minimum torsional strain → **sabse stable**!
   - **Eclipsed (dihedral angle 0°):** C−H bonds aamne-saamne → maximum torsional strain → **12.5 kJ/mol** zyada energy (kam stable)!
   - Kyunki 12.5 kJ/mol barrier bahut chhota hai, kamre ke taapmaan par ethane ke dono forms tezi se ek-doosre mein ghoomte rehte hain aur alag nahi kiye ja sakte!

```
   NEWMAN PROJECTIONS OF ETHANE (C1-C2 bond ke seedhe saamne se dekhne par)

            H (front)                         H / H (0° aamne-saamne!)
            :                                   :
       H    :    H                           H--O--H  (ECLIPSED — 0°,
         \  O  /                                :      zyada torsional strain!)
          / : \                              H / \ H
         H  :  H
       (STAGGERED — 60° gap,
        SABSE ZYADA STABLE!)
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Ethane ke Newman projections: Staggered form (60°) mein C−H bonds sabse door aur stable hain; Eclipsed form (0°) mein bonds aamne-saamne aakar 12.5 kJ/mol torsional strain paida karte hain.

## Solved Examples

### Example 1. Ethane (C₂H₆) Banane ke Teen Alag Tarike

**Sawaal:** Ethane banao: (a) Wurtz reaction se, (b) Soda-lime decarboxylation se, aur (c) Kolbe's electrolysis se.

**Hal:**
- (a) **Wurtz:** 2CH₃Br + 2Na —(dry ether)→ **CH₃−CH₃** + 2NaBr.
- (b) **Decarboxylation (1 C kam hota hai → 3 C salt lo!):** CH₃CH₂COONa + NaOH —(CaO, Δ)→ **CH₃−CH₃** + Na₂CO₃.
- (c) **Kolbe (dugna hota hai → 2 C salt lo!):** 2CH₃COONa + 2H₂O —(Electrolysis)→ **CH₃−CH₃** + 2CO₂ + H₂ + 2NaOH.

**Jaanch:** Teeno mein exact C₂H₆ (2 C) bana ✅.

---

### Example 2. C₅H₁₂ Isomers ka Boiling Point Order

**Sawaal:** ***n*-Pentane, Isopentane aur Neopentane** ko ghat-te boiling point mein lagao aur kaaran batao.

**Hal:**
- ***n*-Pentane (309 K) > Isopentane (301 K) > Neopentane (282.5 K)**.
- Branching badhne par molecule gol (compact) hota jaata hai → **surface area ghat-ta hai** → intermolecular van der Waals forces kamzor hoti hain → bp ghat-ta hai!

**Jaanch:** 0 branch (309 K) > 1 branch (301 K) > 2 branches (282.5 K) ✅.

---

### Example 3. Propane ka Complete Combustion Stoichiometry

**Sawaal:** **C₃H₈** ke 1 mol ke complete combustion ki balanced equation likho aur batao kitne moles O₂ lagenge.

**Hal:**
- Formula: CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ + ((3n+1)/2)O₂ → nCO₂ + (n+1)H₂O; n = 3 → (3×3+1)/2 = **5 O₂**!
- **C₃H₈(g) + 5O₂(g) → 3CO₂(g) + 4H₂O(l)**.

**Jaanch:** C (3=3), H (8=8), O (10 = 6+4) ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: Wurtz reaction ya Kolbe's electrolysis se Methane (CH₄) bana dena!**
   Dono reactions mein do alkyl groups judkar **R−R** bante hain → minimum 2 C (**Ethane**) banta hai! Methane banane ke liye **Soda-lime decarboxylation** (CH₃COONa → CH₄) use karo!

2. **Galti 2: Branching badhne par boiling point badhta hai bol dena!**
   Bilkul ulta! Branching surface area kam karti hai → **boiling point GHAT-TA hai**!

3. **Galti 3: Staggered aur Eclipsed conformations ko "structural isomers" keh dena.**
   Inmein connectivity bilkul same hai, sirf C−C single bond par rotation alag hai → ye **Conformational Isomers (Rotamers)** hain!

## Quick Recap

- **Alkanes Prep:** Hydrogenation, Alkyl halide reduction, **Wurtz** (even-C R−R), **Decarboxylation** (1 C less!), **Kolbe** (R−R + CO₂ anode, H₂ cathode).
- **BP Trend:** Branching ↑ → Surface area ↓ → Boiling point ↓.
- **Reactions:** Free-radical halogenation (3° > 2° > 1°), Combustion, Aromatisation (*n*-hexane → benzene).
- **Ethane Conformations:** **Staggered (60°)** > Eclipsed (0°) by **12.5 kJ/mol** torsional strain.

## Practice Questions

1. Write the balanced chemical equation for preparing **n-butane** using:
   (a) **Wurtz reaction** (from bromoethane)
   (b) **Kolbe's electrolysis** (from potassium propanoate)
   (c) **Soda-lime decarboxylation** (from sodium pentanoate)
2. Why is the **Wurtz reaction** NOT suitable for preparing **unsymmetrical odd-carbon alkanes** such as propane (CH₃CH₂CH₃)? Name the three products formed if CH₃Br and C₂H₅Br are heated together with Na in dry ether.
3. Why can **methane (CH₄)** NOT be prepared by either Wurtz reaction or Kolbe's electrolytic method?
4. Arrange the three isomers of hexane — **n-hexane, 2-methylpentane, and 2,2-dimethylbutane** — in **decreasing order of boiling point**, giving the clear physical reason.
5. Write the three steps (**Initiation, Propagation and Termination**) of the free-radical chlorination of methane (CH₄ + Cl₂ —hν→ CH₃Cl + HCl). *(Flammable/toxic gases: paper problem only.)*
6. Why is direct **iodination of methane** (CH₄ + I₂ ⇌ CH₃I + HI) carried out in the presence of an oxidising agent like **HIO₃** or **HNO₃**?
7. Write the balanced equation for the **aromatisation** of (a) **n-hexane**, and (b) **n-heptane** at 773 K over Cr₂O₃/Al₂O₃ catalyst.
8. Draw/describe the **Newman projections** of the **staggered** and **eclipsed** conformations of ethane. What is the energy difference between them, and why can they NOT be isolated separately at room temperature?
9. Write the balanced equation for the complete combustion of **butane (C₄H₁₀)** and calculate the volume of O₂ (at STP, 22.4 L/mol) required to burn **0.50 mol of butane** completely.
10. What is **torsional strain** in ethane, and at what dihedral angle is it (a) maximum, and (b) minimum?
11. Which sodium salt of a carboxylic acid will yield **propane (C₃H₈)** upon heating with soda lime (NaOH + CaO)? Write the equation.
12. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) In Kolbe's electrolysis of sodium acetate, ethane and CO₂ are liberated at the anode.
    (b) Neopentane has a higher boiling point than n-pentane because it is more compact.
    (c) During free-radical halogenation of alkanes, a 3° hydrogen is abstracted faster than a 1° hydrogen.
    (d) Alkanes are called paraffins because of their low chemical reactivity.

### Answers

1. (a) **2C₂H₅Br + 2Na —(dry ether)→ C₄H₁₀ + 2NaBr**.
   (b) **2C₂H₅COOK + 2H₂O —(Electrolysis)→ C₄H₁₀ + 2CO₂ + H₂ + 2KOH**.
   (c) **C₄H₉COONa + NaOH —(CaO, Δ)→ C₄H₁₀ + Na₂CO₃**.

2. Do alag halides (CH₃Br + C₂H₅Br) lene par 3 alag alkanes ka mixture banta hai: **ethane (C₂H₆)**, **propane (C₃H₈)** aur **butane (C₄H₁₀)** — jinke boiling points paas hone se unhein alag karna bahut mushkil hota hai!

3. Dono methods mein 2 alkyl radicals (R• + R•) judkar **R−R** banate hain → minimum 2 Carbons (**ethane**) banta hai, 1 Carbon (methane) kabhi nahi!

4. Decreasing bp: **n-hexane > 2-methylpentane > 2,2-dimethylbutane**. Branching badhne par surface area ghat-ta hai aur van der Waals forces kamzor hoti hain!

5. (1) **Initiation:** Cl₂ —(hν)→ 2Cl•
   (2) **Propagation:** CH₄ + Cl• → CH₃• + HCl; CH₃• + Cl₂ → CH₃Cl + Cl•
   (3) **Termination:** Cl• + Cl• → Cl₂; CH₃• + CH₃• → C₂H₆; CH₃• + Cl• → CH₃Cl.

6. Iodination reversible hai kyunki bana hua **HI** ek strong reducing agent hai jo CH₃I ko wapas CH₄ mein badal deta hai! **HIO₃** ya **HNO₃** is HI ko I₂ mein oxidise karke hata deta hai → reaction forward chalta hai!

7. (a) **n-Hexane (C₆H₁₄) —(Cr₂O₃/Al₂O₃, 773 K)→ Benzene (C₆H₆) + 4H₂**.
   (b) **n-Heptane (C₇H₁₆) —(Cr₂O₃/Al₂O₃, 773 K)→ Toluene (C₆H₅CH₃) + 4H₂**.
   Jaanch: C₆H₁₄ → C₆H₆ + 8 H (4H₂) ✅.

8. **Staggered:** Dihedral angle **60°** (minimum repulsion); **Eclipsed:** Dihedral angle **0°** (maximum repulsion). Energy difference = **12.5 kJ/mol**. Ye barrier itna chhota hai ki room-temperature collisions se dono lagatar interconvert hote rehte hain, isliye alag nahi kiye ja sakte!

9. **C₄H₁₀ + (13/2)O₂ → 4CO₂ + 5H₂O** (ya 2C₄H₁₀ + 13O₂ → 8CO₂ + 10H₂O).
   0.50 mol butane ke liye O₂ = 0.50 × 6.5 = **3.25 mol** → STP Volume = 3.25 × 22.4 = **72.8 L**!
   Jaanch: 3.25 × 22.4 = 72.8 L ✅.

10. C−H bond ke electron clouds ke aapas ke repulsion ko **torsional strain** kehte hain: (a) Maximum at **0° (eclipsed)**, (b) Minimum at **60° (staggered)**.

11. **Sodium butanoate (CH₃CH₂CH₂COONa)** (4 C salt → 3 C alkane!):
    **CH₃CH₂CH₂COONa + NaOH —(CaO, Δ)→ CH₃CH₂CH₃ + Na₂CO₃**.

12. (a) **True** ✅.
    (b) **False** ❌. Neopentane ka boiling point (282.5 K) n-pentane (309 K) se **KAM** hota hai.
    (c) **True** ✅. 3° radical sabse zyada stable hota hai.
    (d) **True** ✅.
