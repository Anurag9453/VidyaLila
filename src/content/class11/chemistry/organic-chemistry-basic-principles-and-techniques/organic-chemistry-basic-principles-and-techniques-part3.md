# Topic 3: Isomerism: Structural Isomerism (5 Prakar) aur Stereoisomerism ka Parichay

Organic Chemistry mein ek hi molecular formula (jaise **C₄H₁₀O**) se 7 alag-alag compounds ban sakte hain — kuch liquid alcohol, kuch gas-like ether, aur sabke boiling point aur chemical reactions alag! Aise compounds jinka **molecular formula same** ho par **structure ya 3D arrangement alag** ho, **Isomers** kehlate hain. Aaj hum **Structural Isomerism ke sabhi 5 prakar** (khaas kar **Metamerism** aur **Tautomerism**) aur **Stereoisomerism** (**Geometrical *cis–trans*** aur **Optical chirality**) seekhenge!

---

## Sudhir, magnetic building blocks aur baayein-daayein haath ke dastane

Sudhir ke paas theek **6 magnetic building blocks** the. Unhi 6 blocks se usne pehle ek **seedha pul** banaya, phir ek **branched tower** (**Chain Isomerism**), aur phir ek laal block ko kone se uthakar beech mein laga diya (**Position Isomerism**)!

Phir usne sardi ke **baayein aur daayein haath ke dastane (left & right gloves)** dekhe: dono mein barabar 5 ungliyan thin, dono ek-doosre ki **mirror image** the — par baayan dastana kabhi daayein haath par **theek upar fit (superimpose)** nahi hota tha! Teacher ma'am ne muskurate hue kaha, "Sudhir, yahi **Optical Isomerism (Chirality)** hai!" Chalo, isomerism ka poora ped (tree) khol kar dekhte hain.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Structural (Constitutional) isomers** | Same molecular formula, par atoms ke judne ka **kram (connectivity)** alag | Ethanol (CH₃CH₂OH) & Dimethyl ether (CH₃OCH₃) |
| **Metamerism** | **Ek hi polyvalent functional group** (−O−, −CO−, −NH−) ke dono taraf alag-alag size ki alkyl chains hona | Diethyl ether (C₂H₅−O−C₂H₅) & Methyl propyl ether (CH₃−O−C₃H₇) |
| **Tautomerism** | Do structural isomers ka ek-doosre mein **dynamic equilibrium** mein badalte rehna (aksar ek H atom aur π-bond ka shift: **keto ⇌ enol**) | CH₃−CO−CH₃ (keto) ⇌ CH₂=C(OH)−CH₃ (enol) |
| **Geometrical (*cis–trans*) isomerism** | C=C double bond par **restricted rotation** ke kaaran 3D mein groups ka ek hi taraf (*cis*) ya opposite (*trans*) hona | *cis*-but-2-ene vs *trans*-but-2-ene |
| **Chiral carbon (Asymmetric C)** | Wo sp³ Carbon jis par **chaaron groups bilkul ALAG** (a ≠ b ≠ c ≠ d) jude hon — non-superimposable mirror images (**Enantiomers**) banata hai! | Lactic acid CH₃−CH*(OH)−COOH; 2-chlorobutane |

## Part A: Structural Isomerism — Sabhi 5 Prakar Ek Table Mein

| Type | Kya Badalta Hai? | Classic Examples (Same Formula!) |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Chain Isomerism** | Main carbon chain ki **lambai / branching** | C₅H₁₂: *n*-pentane (5 C), isopentane (4 C), neopentane (3 C) |
| **2. Position Isomerism** | Same chain, par **FG / double bond / substituent ki position** | Propan-1-ol vs Propan-2-ol; But-1-ene vs But-2-ene |
| **3. Functional Group Isomerism** | **Functional group** hi alag ho jaata hai! | C₂H₆O: Alcohol (CH₃CH₂OH) vs Ether (CH₃OCH₃); C₃H₆O: Propanal vs Propanone; C₃H₆O₂: Propanoic acid vs Methyl ethanoate |
| **4. Metamerism** | Same polyvalent FG (−O−, −S−, −CO−, −NH−), par **dono taraf alkyl chains ka size alag** | C₄H₁₀O: CH₃CH₂−O−CH₂CH₃ vs CH₃−O−CH₂CH₂CH₃; C₅H₁₀O: Pentan-2-one vs Pentan-3-one |
| **5. Tautomerism (Keto–Enol)** | Dynamic equilibrium mein **α-H aur C=O / C=C ka shift** | Acetone (keto > 99%) ⇌ Prop-1-en-2-ol (enol) |

*(Chhota bonus type — **Ring-Chain Isomerism**: ek open-chain alkene aur ek cyclic alkane, jaise Propene CH₃−CH=CH₂ aur Cyclopropane, dono C₃H₆!)*

## Part B: Stereoisomerism (Introduction — 3D Space ka Khel!)

Stereoisomers mein atoms ke judne ka kram (connectivity) **bilkul same** hota hai, par **3D space mein unki disha (arrangement)** alag hoti hai!
1. **Geometrical (*cis–trans*) Isomerism:**
   - **2 Pakki Shartein:** (i) **Restricted rotation** hona chahiye (jaise **C=C** double bond ya ring), aur (ii) Double bond ke **DONO carbons par lage dono groups alag (a ≠ b aur c ≠ d)** hone chahiye!
   - ***cis*-isomer:** Same groups **ek hi taraf** (e.g. *cis*-but-2-ene → polar, zyada bp).
   - ***trans*-isomer:** Same groups **180° opposite** (e.g. *trans*-but-2-ene → non-polar μ = 0, zyada symmetrical → higher melting point!).
2. **Optical Isomerism (Chirality):**
   - Agar kisi molecule mein **Chiral Carbon (C*)** ho (chaar alag groups), to uski mirror image us par superimpose nahi hoti — aise jode ko **Enantiomers** kehte hain! Ye plane-polarised light ko daayein (*d* / +) ya baayein (*l* / −) ghumate hain.

```
     cis-but-2-ene (same side)         trans-but-2-ene (opposite side)

        CH₃        CH₃                    CH₃         H
          \        /                        \        /
            C == C                            C == C
          /        \                        /        \
         H          H                      H          CH₃
```

> **Figure 3.1** – But-2-ene ke Geometrical Isomers: *cis* mein dono CH₃ ek taraf, *trans* mein opposite taraf.

## Solved Examples

### Example 1. Hexane (C₆H₁₄) ke Sabhi 5 Structural Isomers!

**Sawaal:** C₆H₁₄ ke sabhi **5 structural isomers** likho aur unke IUPAC naam batao.

**Hal:**
1. **Hexane** (6 C straight): CH₃(CH₂)₄CH₃
2. **2-Methylpentane** (5 C chain): CH₃CH(CH₃)CH₂CH₂CH₃
3. **3-Methylpentane** (5 C chain): CH₃CH₂CH(CH₃)CH₂CH₃
4. **2,2-Dimethylbutane** (4 C chain): (CH₃)₃C−CH₂CH₃
5. **2,3-Dimethylbutane** (4 C chain): CH₃CH(CH₃)CH(CH₃)CH₃

**Jaanch:** Sabhi 5 mein kul 6 C aur 14 H (C₆H₁₄) hain aur sabke IUPAC naam alag hain ✅!

---

### Example 2. Chaar Pairs mein Isomerism ka Prakar Pehchanna

**Sawaal:** In chaar jodon mein kaun-sa isomerism hai?
(a) **CH₃CH₂OCH₂CH₃** aur **CH₃OCH₂CH₂CH₃**
(b) **CH₃CH₂CHO** aur **CH₃COCH₃**
(c) **CH₃−CH₂−CH₂−OH** aur **CH₃−CH(OH)−CH₃**
(d) **CH₂=CH−CH₂−CH₃** aur **Cyclobutane**

**Hal:**
- (a) Same −O− group, alag alkyl chains (2+2 vs 1+3) → **Metamerism**!
- (b) Aldehyde vs Ketone (C₃H₆O) → **Functional Group Isomerism**!
- (c) −OH at C1 vs C2 → **Position Isomerism**!
- (d) Open-chain alkene vs Ring (C₄H₈) → **Ring-Chain (Functional) Isomerism**!

**Jaanch:** Har pair ka molecular formula bilkul barabar hai ✅.

---

### Example 3. Kaun *cis–trans* Isomerism Dikhayega?

**Sawaal:** Check karo: (a) **Propene (CH₃CH=CH₂)**, (b) **But-2-ene (CH₃CH=CHCH₃)**, (c) **2-Methylpropene ((CH₃)₂C=CH₂)**, (d) **1,2-Dichloroethene (ClCH=CHCl)**.

**Hal:**
- (a) Propene: right C par 2 same H (=CH₂) → **NAHI**!
- (b) But-2-ene: dono C par (CH₃, H) alag → **HAAN**!
- (c) 2-Methylpropene: dono C par same groups → **NAHI**!
- (d) 1,2-Dichloroethene: dono C par (Cl, H) alag → **HAAN**!

**Jaanch:** Dono C=C carbons par a ≠ b shart sirf (b) aur (d) mein poori hui ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: *n*-Pentane aur Neopentane ko "position isomers" keh dena!**
   Parent chain ki lambai 5 se 3 ho gayi hai → ye **Chain Isomers** hain!

2. **Galti 2: Propene ya But-1-ene ke *cis–trans* isomers bana dena!**
   Terminal **=CH₂** par dono H atoms bilkul ek jaise hain — ghumane par wahi molecule milta hai → **NO geometrical isomerism!**

3. **Galti 3: Metamerism ko Functional Isomerism bol dena!**
   Metamers mein **functional group SAME** rehta hai (dono ether, ya dono ketone, ya dono 2° amine), sirf dono taraf ke alkyl groups ki lambai badalti hai!

---

## Quick Recap

- **5 Structural Isomerisms:** Chain, Position, Functional Group, Metamerism (polyvalent FG), Tautomerism (keto ⇌ enol).
- **Geometrical (*cis–trans*):** Restricted C=C rotation + **a ≠ b on BOTH carbons**!
- **Optical:** Chiral carbon (4 alag groups) → non-superimposable mirror images (**Enantiomers**).
