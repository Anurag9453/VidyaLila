# Topic 4: Electronic Displacements: Inductive, Resonance, Electromeric aur Hyperconjugation

Organic reactions kyun aur kahan hote hain? **Cl₃CCOOH** (trichloroacetic acid) aam sirke (**CH₃COOH**) se hazaaron guna zyada acidic kyun hai? Phenol par naya group **ortho aur para** par hi kyun lagta hai jabki Nitrobenzene par **meta** par? Aur **3° carbocation (CH₃)₃C⁺** sabse zyada stable kyun hota hai? In saare rahasyon ki chaabi hai — **Chaar Electronic Displacement Effects**: **Inductive (I), Resonance (R), Electromeric (E) aur Hyperconjugation (H)**!

---

## Archana, rassa-kashi ka khel, baalti ki chain aur referee ki seeti

Archana school ke sports day mein teen alag nazare dekh rahi thi:
1. **Rassa-kashi (Tug-of-war):** Ek takatwar khiladi ne rassi ko apni taraf zor se kheencha — uska khinchaav poori rassi par aage tak gaya, par 3–4 khiladiyon ke baad kamzor pad gaya (**Inductive Effect**: permanent, σ-bond se chalta hai, doori ke saath ghat-ta hai!).
2. **Paani ki baalti ki chain:** Sabhi bachhe ek line mein baalti ek haath se doosre haath mein barabar aage bhej rahe the (**Resonance Effect**: permanent, π-electrons poori conjugated chain par ghoomte hain!).
3. **Referee ki seeti:** Sabhi players chupchaap khade the, par **sirf referee ki seeti bajte hi** turant ek taraf daud pade — seeti rukte hi wapas apni jagah aa gaye (**Electromeric Effect**: 100% **temporary**, sirf attacking reagent ke aane par hota hai!).

Chalo, in teeno effects aur chauthe super-effect **Hyperconjugation** ko poori gehraai se seekhte hain!

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Inductive effect (I)** | Electronegativity ke antar se **σ-electrons** ka permanent khisakna (3–4 carbons ke baad lagbhag khatam!) | −I (kheenchne wale): −NO₂, −CN, −F, −Cl; +I (dene wale): −C(CH₃)₃ > −CH(CH₃)₂ > −C₂H₅ > −CH₃ |
| **Resonance / Mesomeric effect (R / M)** | Conjugated **π-electrons** ya lone pair ka permanent delocalisation (doori se kamzor NAHI hota!) | +R: −OH, −NH₂, −OCH₃; −R: −NO₂, −CHO, −COOH, −CN |
| **Electromeric effect (E)** | Attacking reagent ki maang par π-bond ke electrons ka **poora, temporary transfer** (+E: reagent ki taraf; −E: reagent se door!) | H⁺ aane par C=C ke π-electrons ek C par shift hona |
| **Hyperconjugation (H)** | **No-bond resonance:** Adjacent sp³ C−H ke **σ-electrons** ka khaali p-orbital (carbocation) ya π* orbital (alkene) ke saath overlap! | Jitne zyada **α-Hydrogens**, utni zyada stability! |

## Part A: Chaar Electronic Effects ki Master Tulna Table

| Effect | Permanent ya Temporary? | Kaunse Electrons? | Distance par Nirbhar? | Mukhya Asar / Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Inductive (I)** | **Permanent** | **σ-electrons** | **Haan** (3–4 C ke baad gayab!) | Acidity: **Cl₃CCOOH > Cl₂CHCOOH > ClCH₂COOH > CH₃COOH** |
| **2. Resonance (R/M)** | **Permanent** | **π-electrons & lone pairs** | **Nahi** (poori conjugated chain mein!) | Phenol (+R → *o, p* electron-rich); Nitrobenzene (−R → *o, p* electron-poor!) |
| **3. Electromeric (E)** | **TEMPORARY!** | **π-electrons** | Sirf reagent aane par! | Alkene + H⁺ (+E); Carbonyl C=O + CN⁻ (−E) |
| **4. Hyperconjugation (H)** | **Permanent** | **σ(C−H) → p / π*** | Sirf **α-C** par lage H! | Carbocation **3° (9 α-H) > 2° (6) > 1° (3) > CH₃⁺ (0)**; Alkene stability! |

### +I vs −I aur +R vs −R ke Pakke Orders
- **−I (Electron-withdrawing) order:** **−NF₃⁺ > −NR₃⁺ > −NO₂ > −CN > −COOH > −F > −Cl > −Br > −I > −OR > −OH**
- **+I (Electron-donating) order:** **−O⁻ > −COO⁻ > −C(CH₃)₃ (3°) > −CH(CH₃)₂ (2°) > −CH₂CH₃ (1°) > −CH₃**
- **+R (Ring mein electron DENE wale — *o, p* directing):** **−NH₂, −NHR, −OH, −OR, −NHCOCH₃, −X** *(ring se jude atom par **lone pair** hota hai!)*
- **−R (Ring se electron KHEENCHNE wale — *meta* directing):** **−NO₂, −CN, −CHO, −COR, −COOH, −COOR** *(ring se jude atom par **double/triple bond** with electronegative O/N hota hai!)*

## Solved Examples

### Example 1. Inductive Effect se Carboxylic Acids ki Acidity Rank Karna

**Sawaal:** In paanch acids ko **badhti hui acidity** ke kram mein lagao: **CH₃COOH, FCH₂COOH, ClCH₂COOH, BrCH₂COOH, Cl₃CCOOH**.

**Hal:**
- Carboxylic acid jitni aasani se H⁺ de (yaani uska conjugate base **R−COO⁻** jitna zyada **−I effect** se stabilise ho!), wo utna **strong acid** hota hai!
- **−I order:** F > Cl > Br, aur 3 Cl atoms ka −I ek F atom se bhi bada hota hai!
- Badhta acidity kram: **CH₃COOH (+I, weakest) < BrCH₂COOH < ClCH₂COOH < FCH₂COOH < Cl₃CCOOH (3 −I groups, strongest!)**.

**Jaanch:** Zyada −I → carboxylate anion zyada stable → zyada acidic ✅.

---

### Example 2. Resonance Effect — Phenol (+R) banaam Nitrobenzene (−R)

- **Phenol (C₆H₅−OH):** Oxygen par **lone pair** hai → ye ring mein electron density deta hai (**+R effect**). Resonance structures mein **negative charge (δ⁻) ortho aur para positions** par aata hai → isliye naya electrophile hamesha **ortho ya para** par lagta hai!
- **Nitrobenzene (C₆H₅−NO₂):** −NO₂ ring se π-electrons bahar kheenchta hai (**−R effect**). Resonance structures mein **positive charge (δ⁺) ortho aur para positions** par aata hai (unhein electron-poor bana deta hai!) → isliye electrophile sirf **meta** position par lagta hai!

**Jaanch:** +R → *o, p* par electron density badhti hai; −R → *o, p* par ghat-ti hai ✅.

---

### Example 3. Hyperconjugation — α-Hydrogens Ginkar Carbocation aur Alkene Stability!

**Sawaal:** α-Hydrogens ginkar stability order batao:
(a) Carbocations: **(CH₃)₃C⁺, (CH₃)₂CH⁺, CH₃CH₂⁺, CH₃⁺**
(b) Alkenes: **(CH₃)₂C=CHCH₃ (2-methylbut-2-ene), CH₃CH=CHCH₃ (but-2-ene), CH₃CH=CH₂ (propene), CH₂=CH₂ (ethene)**

**Hal:**
- **(a) Carbocations (C⁺ ke bagal wale sp³ carbon par lage H = α-H!):**
  - **(CH₃)₃C⁺ (3°):** **9 α-H** (10 hyperconjugative structures!)
  - **(CH₃)₂CH⁺ (2°):** **6 α-H**
  - **CH₃CH₂⁺ (1°):** **3 α-H**
  - **CH₃⁺:** **0 α-H**
  - Stability order: **(CH₃)₃C⁺ > (CH₃)₂CH⁺ > CH₃CH₂⁺ > CH₃⁺**!
- **(b) Alkenes (C=C ke bagal wale sp³ carbons par lage H = α-H!):**
  - **2-Methylbut-2-ene:** 3 CH₃ groups = **9 α-H** (sabse stable!)
  - **But-2-ene:** 2 CH₃ groups = **6 α-H**
  - **Propene:** 1 CH₃ group = **3 α-H**
  - **Ethene:** **0 α-H**
  - Stability order: **2-methylbut-2-ene > but-2-ene > propene > ethene**!

**Jaanch:** Zyada α-H → zyada hyperconjugation structures → zyada stability ✅!

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: Electromeric effect ko permanent effect maan lena.**
   *Sahi baat:* Inductive (I), Resonance (R) aur Hyperconjugation (H) **permanent** hain; **Electromeric (E) 100% TEMPORARY** hai — reagent hat-te hi gayab ho jaata hai!

2. **Galti 2: C=C carbon par seedhe lage H ko α-hydrogen gin lena!**
   Ethene (**CH₂=CH₂**) mein 4 H dikhte hain, par we sp² double-bonded carbons par hain — usmein **ZERO (0) α-hydrogens** hain! α-H sirf **adjacent sp³ carbon** par hota hai!

3. **Galti 3: Inductive effect ki distance-dependence bhool jaana.**
   2-chlorobutanoic acid, 3-chlorobutanoic acid se zyada acidic hai aur 4-chlorobutanoic acid se kahin zyada — kyunki −I effect har C−C bond ke saath kamzor padta jaata hai!

---

## Quick Recap

- **Inductive (I):** Permanent, σ-electrons, distance-dependent (−I acidity badhata hai, +I ghatata hai).
- **Resonance (R/M):** Permanent, π/lone-pair delocalisation (+R → *o, p* electron-rich; −R → *o, p* electron-poor).
- **Electromeric (E):** Temporary, π-electrons, sirf reagent ke aane par.
- **Hyperconjugation (H):** Permanent, σ(C−H) overlap; **zyada α-H → zyada stable carbocation/alkene**!

## Practice Questions

1. Classify each of the following groups as **+I or −I** and as **+R or −R** (when attached to a benzene ring):
   **−NO₂, −OH, −CN, −NH₂, −COOH, −CH₃, −OCH₃, −C(CH₃)₃**.
2. Arrange **HCOOH, CH₃COOH and CH₃CH₂COOH** in **decreasing order of acidic strength**, explaining clearly using the **+I effect**.
3. Arrange **2-chlorobutanoic acid, 3-chlorobutanoic acid and 4-chlorobutanoic acid** in **decreasing order of acidity** and state which property of the inductive effect this demonstrates.
4. Count the exact number of **α-hydrogens** in each alkene and arrange them in **increasing order of stability**:
   (a) **CH₂=CH₂**, (b) **CH₃−CH₂−CH=CH₂ (but-1-ene)**, (c) **CH₃−CH=CH−CH₃ (but-2-ene)**, (d) **(CH₃)₂C=C(CH₃)₂ (2,3-dimethylbut-2-ene)**.
5. Explain clearly why **−OH** in phenol shows a **−I effect** (O is more electronegative than C), yet overall **increases electron density** in the benzene ring at the *ortho* and *para* positions!
6. Distinguish between **Inductive effect** and **Electromeric effect** on any three clear points.
7. Why is **Hyperconjugation** often called **"no-bond resonance"**? Illustrate with the ethyl carbocation (**CH₃−CH₂⁺**).
8. Predict whether **Aniline (C₆H₅−NH₂)** and **Benzaldehyde (C₆H₅−CHO)** show **+R** or **−R** effect, and state where electron density is highest on the benzene ring in each case.
9. Compare the **+E** and **−E** electromeric effects with one clear example of each.
10. Why does the **tert-butyl carbocation (CH₃)₃C⁺** have greater stability than the **isopropyl carbocation (CH₃)₂CH⁺**? Cite BOTH electronic effects responsible!
11. Arrange **CF₃COOH, CCl₃COOH and CH₃COOH** in **increasing order of acidic strength**.
12. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) Inductive effect operates through π-bonds and remains constant over long carbon chains.
    (b) Electromeric effect is a temporary effect that occurs only in the presence of an attacking reagent.
    (c) Ethene (CH₂=CH₂) has 4 α-hydrogens available for hyperconjugation.
    (d) In nitrobenzene, the −NO₂ group withdraws electron density from the benzene ring via −R and −I effects.

### Answers

1. **−NO₂, −CN, −COOH:** −I aur −R.
   **−OH, −NH₂, −OCH₃:** −I par **+R** (lone pair donate karte hain!).
   **−CH₃, −C(CH₃)₃:** **+I** (alkyl groups).

2. Decreasing acidity: **HCOOH > CH₃COOH > CH₃CH₂COOH**.
   Alkyl group ka **+I effect** (−CH₂CH₃ > −CH₃ > −H) carboxylate anion (RCOO⁻) par negative charge badha kar use destabilise karta hai → acidity ghat-ti hai!

3. Decreasing acidity: **2-chlorobutanoic acid > 3-chlorobutanoic acid > 4-chlorobutanoic acid**.
   Ye dikhata hai ki **Inductive effect doori (distance) badhne ke saath tezi se kamzor** ho jaata hai!

4. α-H count: (a) **Ethene = 0**; (b) **But-1-ene = 2** (sirf C3 ke CH₂ par!); (c) **But-2-ene = 6**; (d) **2,3-Dimethylbut-2-ene = 12**!
   Increasing stability: **Ethene (0) < But-1-ene (2) < But-2-ene (6) < 2,3-Dimethylbut-2-ene (12)**.
   Jaanch: 0 < 2 < 6 < 12 ✅.

5. Phenol mein Oxygen par lone pairs ke kaaran **+R (resonance donation)**, uske **−I (inductive withdrawal)** se kahin zyada mazboot (**+R > −I**) hota hai → isliye net electron density *ortho* aur *para* par badh jaati hai!

6. (1) Inductive **permanent** hai, Electromeric **temporary** (sirf reagent aane par); (2) Inductive **σ-electrons** mein partial shift hai, Electromeric **π-electrons** ka complete shift hai; (3) Inductive mein reagent ki zaroorat nahi, Electromeric bina reagent ke nahi hota.

7. Ethyl carbocation (CH₃−CH₂⁺) mein α C−H bond ke σ-electrons khaali p-orbital mein delocalise hokar **H⁺  CH₂=CH₂** jaisi structure banate hain jismein us H⁺ aur C ke beech koi seedha bond nahi dikhta — isliye ise **no-bond resonance** kehte hain!

8. **Aniline (−NH₂):** **+R effect** → *ortho* aur *para* par electron density sabse zyada!
   **Benzaldehyde (−CHO):** **−R effect** → *o, p* par δ⁺ aata hai → *meta* par relative electron density zyada!

9. **+E effect:** π-electrons us atom par jaayein jis par attacking reagent (electrophile H⁺) jud raha hai (jaise CH₂=CH₂ + H⁺ → CH₃−CH₂⁺).
   **−E effect:** π-electrons attacking reagent (nucleophile CN⁻) se door wale atom par jaayein (jaise >C=O + CN⁻ → >C(CN)−O⁻).

10. **(CH₃)₃C⁺** mein **(1) zyada α-H (9 vs 6)** → zyada hyperconjugation structures, aur **(2) teen +I CH₃ groups (vs do)** → positive charge zyada neutralise hota hai!

11. Increasing acidity: **CH₃COOH < CCl₃COOH < CF₃COOH** (kyunki −I of F > Cl!).

12. (a) **False** ❌. Inductive effect **σ-bonds** se chalta hai aur 3–4 carbons ke baad lagbhag khatam ho jaata hai.
    (b) **True** ✅.
    (c) **False** ❌. Ethene mein **0 α-hydrogens** hain (saare H sp² carbons par hain!).
    (d) **True** ✅.
