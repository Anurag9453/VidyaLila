# Topic 5: Bond Fission, Reaction Intermediates, Electrophiles/Nucleophiles aur 4 Organic Reaction Types

Har organic reaction mein purane covalent bonds toot-te hain aur naye bante hain! Par ek covalent bond (2 shared electrons) **do bilkul alag tarikon** se toot sakta hai — aur usi par nirbhar karta hai ki beech mein kaunsa **Reaction Intermediate** (Carbocation, Carbanion ya Free Radical) banega! Aaj hum **Homolytic vs Heterolytic Fission**, teeno intermediates ki **geometry aur stability order**, **Electrophiles vs Nucleophiles**, aur organic reactions ke **chaar mukhya prakar** seekhenge.

---

## Jitendra aur ₹100 ke note ke do tarah ke batware

Jitendra aur uske chhote bhai ko Papa ne milkar kharch karne ke liye ek **₹100 ka note** (2 electrons ka shared bond!) diya. Batwara do tarah se ho sakta tha:
1. **Barabar Batwara (₹50–₹50):** Dono ko 1-1 electron barabar mila → **Homolytic Fission** (bante hain neutral **Free Radicals**!).
2. **Ek-tarfa Batwara (₹100 ek ko, ₹0 doosre ko):** Zyada takatwar (electronegative) atom dono electrons le uda → ek par **−ve charge (Carbanion)** aur doosre par **+ve charge (Carbocation)** → **Heterolytic Fission**!

Teacher ma'am ne muskurate hue kaha, "Jitendra, isi tarah reaction mein hissa lene wale players bhi do team ke hote hain: **electron-bhookhe (Electrophiles)** aur **electron-ameer (Nucleophiles)**!" Chalo, poora mechanism toolkit seekhte hain.

## Part A: Teeno Reaction Intermediates — Master Comparison Table!

| Intermediate | Kaise Banta Hai? | Valence e⁻ on C | Hybridisation & Shape | **Stability Order (Sabse Zaroori!)** | Kaaran |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Carbocation (R⁺)** | Heterolytic fission | **6 e⁻** (sextet, empty p!) | **sp² — Trigonal Planar (120°)** | **Benzyl/Allyl > 3° > 2° > 1° > CH₃⁺** | +I aur Hyperconjugation (α-H) +ve charge kam karte hain! |

| **3. Free Radical (R•)** | Homolytic fission | **7 e⁻** (odd electron!) | **sp² — Planar** (ya shallow pyramid) | **Benzyl/Allyl > 3° > 2° > 1° > CH₃•** | Hyperconjugation (α-H) odd electron ko stabilise karta hai! |

## Part B: Chaar Mukhya Organic Reaction Types

1. **Substitution Reaction:** Ek atom/group hatkar doosra lag jaata hai (saturation nahi badalta):
   - Free radical: **CH₄ + Cl₂ —(hν)→ CH₃Cl + HCl**; Nucleophilic: **CH₃Br + OH⁻ → CH₃OH + Br⁻**.
2. **Addition Reaction:** Unsaturated (C=C ya C≡C) ka π-bond tootkar do naye σ-bonds bante hain:
   - **CH₂=CH₂ + Br₂ → BrCH₂−CH₂Br**; **CH₂=CH₂ + HBr → CH₃−CH₂Br**.
3. **Elimination Reaction:** Addition ka theek ulta — ek molecule se 2 chhote groups (jaise H aur Br) nikalkar **double/triple bond** banta hai:
   - **CH₃−CH₂Br + alc. KOH —(Heat)→ CH₂=CH₂ + KBr + H₂O**.
4. **Rearrangement Reaction:** Molecule ke andar atoms/groups apni jagah badalkar zyada stable isomer ya intermediate banate hain:
   - 1° carbocation (**CH₃−CH₂−CH₂⁺**) mein 1,2-hydride shift hokar zyada stable 2° carbocation (**CH₃−CH⁺−CH₃**) banna!

## Solved Examples

### Example 1. Electrophiles aur Nucleophiles ki Pehchaan

**Sawaal:** Classify karo: **BF₃, :NH₃, NO₂⁺, CN⁻, H₂O, AlCl₃, CH₃⁺, OH⁻**.

**Hal:**
- **Electrophiles (electron-deficient / Lewis acids):** **BF₃, AlCl₃** (neutral, incomplete octet!), **NO₂⁺, CH₃⁺** (+ve charged!).
- **Nucleophiles (electron-rich / Lewis bases):** **CN⁻, OH⁻** (−ve charged!), **:NH₃, H₂O** (neutral, lone pair!).

**Jaanch:** Har electrophile electron acceptor hai aur har nucleophile electron donor ✅.

---

### Example 2. Carbocation banaam Carbanion ka Ulta Stability Order!

**Sawaal:** **(CH₃)₃C⁺, (CH₃)₂CH⁺, CH₃CH₂⁺, CH₃⁺** aur **(CH₃)₃C⁻, (CH₃)₂CH⁻, CH₃CH₂⁻, CH₃⁻** ko ghat-ti stability mein lagao aur antar samjhao.

**Hal:**
- **Carbocations:** **(CH₃)₃C⁺ > (CH₃)₂CH⁺ > CH₃CH₂⁺ > CH₃⁺** *(CH₃ groups +I aur hyperconjugation se +ve charge ko shaant/disperse karte hain!)*.
- **Carbanions:** **CH₃⁻ > CH₃CH₂⁻ > (CH₃)₂CH⁻ > (CH₃)₃C⁻** *(CH₃ groups ka +I effect pehle se maujood −ve charge ko aur badha deta hai → destabilisation!)*

**Jaanch:** Dono ke stability orders ek-doosre ke theek ulte hain ✅.

---

### Example 3. Chaar Reactions ka Classification

**Sawaal:** Classify karo:
(a) **CH₄ + Cl₂ —(hν)→ CH₃Cl + HCl**
(b) **CH₃−CH=CH₂ + HBr → CH₃−CH(Br)−CH₃**
(c) **CH₃−CH₂−OH —(conc. H₂SO₄, 443 K)→ CH₂=CH₂ + H₂O**
(d) **CH₃−CH₂−CH₂⁺ → CH₃−CH⁺−CH₃**

**Hal:**
- (a) **Substitution** (H ki jagah Cl aaya).
- (b) **Addition** (π-bond tootkar H aur Br jude).
- (c) **Elimination** (H₂O nikalkar C=C bana).
- (d) **Rearrangement** (1° → 2° carbocation hydride shift).

**Jaanch:** Sabhi 4 mukhya organic reaction types match hue ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: NH₄⁺ ya Na⁺ par "+" dekh kar unhein Electrophile bol dena!**
   *Sahi baat:* **NH₄⁺** mein Nitrogen ka octet (8 e⁻) poora bhara hai aur koi khaali orbital nahi — isliye **NH₄⁺ electrophile NAHI hai**! Electrophile hone ke liye **khaali orbital / electron kami** zaroori hai (jaise NO₂⁺, CH₃⁺, BF₃)!

2. **Galti 2: Carbanion ka stability order bhi 3° > 2° > 1° likh dena!**
   Carbanion mein −ve charge hota hai — electron-donating (+I) alkyl groups use **destabilise** karte hain → **CH₃⁻ > 1° > 2° > 3°**!

3. **Galti 3: Homolytic fission mein full double-headed arrow laga dena.**
   Full arrow **2 electrons** (heterolytic) dikhata hai; **1 electron** (homolytic) ke liye hamesha **half-headed (fish-hook) arrow** use hota hai!

---

## Quick Recap

- **Homolytic** (1–1 e⁻, UV/heat) → **Free Radicals** (7 e⁻, 3° > 2° > 1° > CH₃•).
- **Heterolytic** (2–0 e⁻, polar solvent) → **Carbocation** (6 e⁻, sp² planar, **3° > 2° > 1° > CH₃⁺**) & **Carbanion** (8 e⁻, sp³ pyramidal, **CH₃⁻ > 1° > 2° > 3°**).
- **Electrophile** = e⁻-deficient (NO₂⁺, BF₃, AlCl₃); **Nucleophile** = e⁻-rich (OH⁻, CN⁻, NH₃, H₂O).
- **4 Reaction Types:** Substitution, Addition, Elimination, Rearrangement.

## Practice Questions

1. Distinguish clearly between **homolytic fission** and **heterolytic fission** on: (a) electron distribution, (b) favouring conditions, and (c) intermediates formed.
2. State the **number of valence electrons on the central carbon**, the **hybridisation**, and the **3D molecular geometry** of: (a) **CH₃⁺** (methyl carbocation), and (b) **CH₃⁻** (methyl carbanion).
3. Arrange the following **carbocations** in **decreasing order of stability**, giving a brief reason: **C₆H₅CH₂⁺ (benzyl), (CH₃)₃C⁺ (3°), CH₂=CH−CH₂⁺ (allyl), CH₃CH₂⁺ (1°), CH₃⁺**.
4. Arrange the following **free radicals** in **increasing order of stability**: **CH₃•, (CH₃)₃C•, CH₃CH₂•, (CH₃)₂CH•**.
5. Explain why **NH₄⁺** is NOT an electrophile even though it carries a positive charge, whereas neutral **BF₃** and **SO₃** ARE electrophiles.
6. Classify each of the following as an **electrophile** or a **nucleophile**: **Cl⁺, RO⁻, FeCl₃, R−NH₂, Br⁻, CO₂, H₃O⁺ (check carefully!)**.
7. Why is the **stability order of carbanions (CH₃⁻ > 1° > 2° > 3°)** exactly opposite to that of **carbocations (3° > 2° > 1° > CH₃⁺)**?
8. Identify the type of organic reaction (**substitution, addition, elimination, or rearrangement**) in each case:
   (a) **CH₃−CH₂−I + KOH(aq) → CH₃−CH₂−OH + KI**
   (b) **CH₃−CH₂−I + KOH(alc.) → CH₂=CH₂ + KI + H₂O**
   (c) **CH≡CH + 2H₂ —(Ni)→ CH₃−CH₃**
   (d) **C₆H₆ + Cl₂ —(FeCl₃)→ C₆H₅Cl + HCl**
9. Explain why **CH₂=CH−CH₂⁺ (allyl carbocation)** and **C₆H₅−CH₂⁺ (benzyl carbocation)** are much more stable than a simple primary carbocation like CH₃−CH₂⁺.
10. Why does the **1° carbocation (CH₃)₂CH−CH₂⁺** rapidly rearrange via a 1,2-hydride shift to **(CH₃)₃C⁺**?
11. Identify the **nucleophilic centre** (electron-rich atom) and **electrophilic centre** (electron-poor atom) in **CH₃−C(=O)−H** (ethanal).
12. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) A carbocation has 6 valence electrons on the positively charged carbon and is sp² hybridised.
    (b) Homolytic fission of a C−C bond produces one carbocation and one carbanion.
    (c) Neutral molecules with lone pairs such as H₂O and NH₃ act as nucleophiles.
    (d) Elimination reactions convert saturated compounds into unsaturated compounds.

### Answers

1. **Homolytic:** 1–1 e⁻ barabar batwara; UV light / high T / peroxides; **Free radicals** bante hain.
   **Heterolytic:** 2–0 e⁻ asamaan batwara; polar solvents / EN difference; **Carbocation + Carbanion** bante hain.

2. (a) **CH₃⁺:** **6 valence e⁻**, **sp²**, **Trigonal planar (120°)**.
   (b) **CH₃⁻:** **8 valence e⁻** (3 bp + 1 lp), **sp³**, **Trigonal pyramidal (~107°)**.

3. Decreasing stability: **C₆H₅CH₂⁺ (4 resonance forms) > (CH₃)₃C⁺ (9 α-H) ≈ CH₂=CH−CH₂⁺ (2 resonance forms) > CH₃CH₂⁺ (3 α-H) > CH₃⁺ (0 α-H)**. *(Benzyl aur allyl resonance se extra stable hote hain!)*

4. Increasing stability: **CH₃• (0 α-H) < CH₃CH₂• (3 α-H) < (CH₃)₂CH• (6 α-H) < (CH₃)₃C• (9 α-H)**.

5. **NH₄⁺** mein N ka octet poora (8 e⁻) hai aur koi khaali orbital nahi → naya electron pair nahi le sakta! **BF₃** (6 e⁻, empty 2p orbital) aur **SO₃** (polar S=O bonds) electron-deficient hain aur lone pair accept kar sakte hain → electrophiles!

6. **Electrophiles:** **Cl⁺, FeCl₃, CO₂** (C par δ⁺).
   **Nucleophiles:** **RO⁻, R−NH₂, Br⁻**.
   *(H₃O⁺ mein O ka octet poora hai, isliye wo direct e⁻-pair acceptor nahi, par proton H⁺ donor hai!)*

7. Alkyl groups (+I) electrons donate karte hain: carbocation mein ye +ve charge ko kam karke **stabilise** karte hain (3° best!), jabki carbanion mein ye −ve charge ko aur badha kar **destabilise** karte hain (CH₃⁻ best!).

8. (a) **Substitution** (nucleophilic); (b) **Elimination** (alc. KOH se C=C bana!); (c) **Addition** (hydrogenation); (d) **Substitution** (electrophilic aromatic).

9. Allyl aur benzyl carbocations mein **π-bonds ke saath resonance (delocalisation)** hoti hai jisse +ve charge kai carbons par phail jaata hai — resonance, simple inductive/hyperconjugation se zyada powerful stabilisation deta hai!

10. (CH₃)₂CH−CH₂⁺ ek **1° carbocation** (1 α-H) hai; 1,2-hydride shift ke baad ye **3° carbocation (CH₃)₃C⁺** (9 α-H) ban jaata hai jo bahut zyada stable hai!

11. **CH₃−C(δ⁺)=O(δ⁻)−H:** Oxygen par lone pairs aur δ⁻ charge hai → **Nucleophilic centre**; Carbonyl Carbon par δ⁺ charge hai → **Electrophilic centre**!

12. (a) **True** ✅.
    (b) **False** ❌. Homolytic fission se **do Free Radicals** bante hain (ions heterolytic se bante hain).
    (c) **True** ✅.
    (d) **True** ✅.
