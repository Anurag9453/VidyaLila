# Topic 6: Purification Techniques, Qualitative Analysis (Lassaigne's Test) aur Quantitative Analysis

Lab mein jab koi organic compound banaya jaata hai ya paudhon se nikala jaata hai, to wo shuruaat mein ashuddh (impure) hota hai! Pehle use **shuddh (purify)** karna padta hai (sharp melting/boiling point = pure!), phir **Qualitative Analysis** se pata lagate hain ki usmein C, H ke alawa **N, S ya Halogens (Cl, Br, I)** hain ya nahi, aur phir **Quantitative Analysis** se unka **exact percentage** nikaalte hain! Aaj hum saari lab techniques aur numerical formulas seekhenge. *(Suraksha: Sodium fusion, fuming HNO₃ aur conc. H₂SO₄ behad khatarnak hain — ye sab sirf padhne aur teacher demo ke liye hain!)*

---

## Vandana, Dadi ka kapoor, chini ke crystals aur chalk par failti syahi

Vandana ne ghar par teen kamaal ke purification tarike dekhe:
1. Dadi ne namak mein mile hue **kapoor (camphor)** ko dheemi aanch par garam kiya — kapoor bina pighle seedhe bhaap bankar upar thande bartan par shuddh jam gaya (**Sublimation**)!
2. Garam chashni (sugar syrup) thandi hone par usmein chamakdar shuddh **chini ke crystals** nikal aaye (**Crystallisation**)!
3. School lab mein Vandana ne filter paper par kaali syahi ki ek boond lagayi aur use paani mein thoda duboya — paani upar chadhte hi kaali syahi **neele, peele aur laal** alag rangon ki pattiyon mein bant gayi (**Chromatography**)!

Teacher ma'am ne muskurate hue kaha, "Vandana, yahi teeno — aur inke saath 4 tarah ki Distillation — Organic Chemistry ke sabse shaktishaali purification tools hain!" Chalo, purification, Lassaigne's Test aur quantitative formulas seekhte hain.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Distillation under reduced pressure** | Pressure kam karke liquid ko uske normal boiling point se **kam temperature** par ubaalna (taaki wo decompose na ho!) | **Glycerol** ko spent-lye (sabun factory) se alag karna! |
| **Steam distillation** | Aise compounds ko bhaap ke saath alag karna jo **steam-volatile** hon aur **paani mein aghulansheel (immiscible)** hon | **Aniline + water**, ya ***o*-nitrophenol + *p*-nitrophenol** |
| **Retardation factor (R_f)** | Chromatography (TLC / Paper) mein solute aur solvent ki doori ka ratio: **R_f = (Distance moved by spot) ÷ (Distance moved by solvent front)** | Hamesha **0 < R_f < 1**! |
| **Lassaigne's test** | Organic compound ko **sodium metal** ke saath pighla kar covalent N, S, X ko **ionic NaCN, Na₂S, NaX** mein badalna taaki aasani se test ho sake! | N → **Prussian blue**; N + S → **Blood-red**! |

## Part A: Sabhi 6 Purification Methods — Master Selection Table!

| Method | Kab Use Karte Hain? | Classic Example |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Sublimation** | Solid bina pighle seedhe gas ban jaaye (impurities non-sublimable) | Camphor + NaCl; Naphthalene; Iodine |
| **2. Crystallisation** | Compound aur impurity ki **solubility** garam/thande solvent mein alag ho | Sugar + NaCl; Benzoic acid |
| **3a. Simple Distillation** | Do miscible liquids jinke boiling points mein **bada antar (> 40 K)** ho | **Chloroform (334 K) + Aniline (457 K)** |
| **3b. Fractional Distillation** | Do miscible liquids jinke boiling points **bahut paas (< 30 K)** hon (fractionating column!) | **Crude oil (petroleum)** fractions; Acetone + Methanol |
| **3c. Reduced Pressure Distillation** | High-bp liquid jo apne normal bp par **decompose (toot)** jaata ho! | **Glycerol** (normal bp 563 K par toot-ta hai → 12 mm Hg par 453 K par shuddh!) |
| **3d. Steam Distillation** | **Steam-volatile** aur **water-immiscible** organic compound | **Aniline–water** mixture; ***o*-nitrophenol** (intramolecular H-bond!) |
| **4. Differential Extraction** | Aqueous solution se organic compound ko **organic solvent** (ether/CHCl₃) mein hila kar nikaalna | Benzoic acid ko paani se ether mein nikaalna |
| **5. Chromatography** | Rang/mixtures ko **Stationary phase** aur **Mobile phase** par alag affinity (adsorption ya partition) se alag karna | Column, **TLC (Adsorption)**, **Paper Chromatography (Partition!)** |

## Part B: Qualitative Analysis — Lassaigne's Sodium Fusion Test *(Sirf Padhne Ke Liye!)*

Organic compounds mein N, S aur Halogens **covalent bonds** mein bandhe hote hain, isliye seedhe AgNO₃ ya FeSO₄ se react nahi karte! Sodium metal ke saath fuse karne par **Lassaigne's Extract (Sodium Fusion Extract, SFE)** banta hai:
- Na + C + N → **NaCN**;  2Na + S → **Na₂S**;  Na + X → **NaX** (X = Cl, Br, I);  Na + C + N + S → **NaSCN**!

| Element | Lassaigne's Extract Test | **Pehchaan ka Rang / Precipitate** | Formula of Coloured Species |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nitrogen (N)** | SFE + FeSO₄ (boil) + dil. H₂SO₄ (+ FeCl₃) | **Prussian Blue** colour! | **Fe₄[Fe(CN)₆]₃** |
| **Sulphur (S)** | (a) SFE + Sodium nitroprusside; (b) SFE + Acetic acid + Lead acetate | (a) **Violet** colour; (b) **Black ppt** | (a) [Fe(CN)₅NOS]⁴⁻; (b) **PbS** |
| **N aur S DONO!** | SFE + FeCl₃ (acidified) | **Blood-Red** colour! | **[Fe(SCN)]²⁺** |
| **Halogens (Cl, Br, I)** | SFE + dil. HNO₃ (boil to remove HCN/H₂S!) + AgNO₃ | **Cl:** White ppt (NH₄OH mein ghul); **Br:** Pale yellow (halka ghul); **I:** Yellow (aghulansheel!) | **AgCl, AgBr, AgI** |

## Part C: Quantitative Analysis — Sabhi 5 Exact Formulas!

1. **Liebig's Method (C aur H):**
   - **%C = (12 ÷ 44) × (m_CO₂ ÷ m_org) × 100**;  **%H = (2 ÷ 18) × (m_H₂O ÷ m_org) × 100**
2. **Dumas Method (Nitrogen as N₂ gas):**
   - **%N = (28 ÷ 22400) × (V_N₂ at STP in mL ÷ m_org) × 100**
3. **Kjeldahl's Method (Nitrogen as NH₃ absorbed in standard acid):**
   - **%N = (1.4 × M_acid × V_acid_used × basicity) ÷ m_org** *(H₂SO₄ ki basicity = **2**, HCl ki = **1**!)*
   - **Khaas Seema (Exam Favorite!):** Kjeldahl method **Nitro (−NO₂)**, **Azo (−N=N−)** aur **ring mein N (jaise Pyridine)** wale compounds par **FAIL** ho jaata hai kyunki unka N ammonium sulphate mein nahi badalta!
4. **Carius Method (Halogens as AgX aur Sulphur as BaSO₄):**
   - **%Cl = (35.5 ÷ 143.5) × (m_AgCl ÷ m_org) × 100** *(Br: 80/188; I: 127/235)*
   - **%S = (32 ÷ 233) × (m_BaSO₄ ÷ m_org) × 100**

## Solved Examples

### Example 1. Thin Layer Chromatography (TLC) mein R_f Value

**Sawaal:** TLC plate par solvent front **8.0 cm** upar chadha. Ink spot A **3.6 cm** aur spot B **6.0 cm** upar chadha. Dono ke **R_f values** nikalo aur batao kaunsa spot stationary phase se zyada chipka (strongly adsorbed) tha!

**Hal:**
- **R_f(A)** = 3.6 ÷ 8.0 = **0.45**; **R_f(B)** = 6.0 ÷ 8.0 = **0.75**.
- Spot A kam chala (chhota R_f = 0.45) → **Spot A stationary phase par zyada mazbooti se adsorbed tha!**

**Jaanch:** Dono 0 < R_f < 1 ke beech hain ✅.

---

### Example 2. Kjeldahl's Method se % Nitrogen Nikaalna

**Sawaal:** **0.50 g** organic compound ka Kjeldahl analysis kiya gaya. Nikli hui NH₃ ko **20.0 mL of 0.25 M H₂SO₄** mein absorb kiya gaya. Bache hue extra acid ko neutralise karne mein **10.0 mL of 0.50 M NaOH** laga. Compound mein **%N** nikaalo!

**Hal:**
- Total millimoles of H⁺ from H₂SO₄ = 2 × M × V = 2 × 0.25 × 20.0 = **10.0 mmol H⁺**.
- Excess H⁺ neutralised by NaOH = 1 × 0.50 × 10.0 = **5.0 mmol H⁺**.
- NH₃ dwara use hue H⁺ = 10.0 − 5.0 = **5.0 mmol** (= **5.0 mmol NH₃** = **5.0 mmol N**!).
- Shortcut formula: **%N = (1.4 × mmol of H⁺ used by NH₃) ÷ m_org = (1.4 × 5.0) ÷ 0.50 = 7.0 ÷ 0.50 = 14.0%**!

**Jaanch:** 5.0 mmol N = 0.005 mol × 14 g/mol = 0.070 g N; (0.070 ÷ 0.50) × 100 = 14.0% ✅!

---

### Example 3. Carius Method se % Chlorine aur % Sulphur

**Sawaal:**
(a) Carius estimation mein **0.250 g** organic compound se **0.287 g AgCl** bana. **%Cl** nikaalo. *(Ag = 108, Cl = 35.5 → AgCl = 143.5.)*
(b) **0.466 g** organic compound se **0.466 g BaSO₄** bana. **%S** nikaalo. *(BaSO₄ = 233, S = 32.)*

**Hal:**
- (a) %Cl = (35.5 ÷ 143.5) × (0.287 ÷ 0.250) × 100 = 35.5 × 0.002 ÷ 0.250 × 100 = **28.4%**! *(0.287 ÷ 143.5 = 0.0020 exact!)*
- (b) %S = (32 ÷ 233) × (0.466 ÷ 0.466) × 100 = 3200 ÷ 233 = **13.73%**!

**Jaanch:** (a) 0.287 g AgCl = 0.002 mol Cl = 0.071 g Cl → 0.071/0.250 = 28.4% ✅; (b) 32/233 = 13.73% ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: Glycerol ko Simple Distillation se purify karne ko keh dena.**
   Glycerol apne normal bp (563 K) par **decompose** ho jaata hai → hamesha **Distillation under Reduced Pressure** chuno!

2. **Galti 2: Kjeldahl's method mein H₂SO₄ ki basicity (2) bhool jaana!**
   1 mol H₂SO₄ **2 mol H⁺** deta hai (1 mol NH₃ sirf 1 H⁺ leta hai!) → isliye H₂SO₄ ke liye **2 × M × V** lagao!

3. **Galti 3: R_f value ko 1 se bada likh dena.**
   Solute spot kabhi solvent front se aage nahi bhaag sakta → isliye **R_f hamesha 0 aur 1 ke beech** hota hai!

## Quick Recap

- **Purification:** Sublimation (camphor), Simple dist (Δbp > 40 K, CHCl₃+aniline), Fractional (crude oil), Reduced pressure (**glycerol**), Steam (**aniline+water, *o*-nitrophenol**), Chromatography (TLC adsorption, Paper partition; **0 < R_f < 1**).
- **Lassaigne's Test:** N = **Prussian blue** (Fe₄[Fe(CN)₆]₃); S = violet / black PbS; N + S = **Blood-red** ([Fe(SCN)]²⁺); Cl/Br/I = AgCl (white)/AgBr (pale yellow)/AgI (yellow).
- **Quantitative:** Liebig (C, H), Dumas (N₂ gas), Kjeldahl (NH₃ — fails for nitro, azo, pyridine!), Carius (AgX, BaSO₄).

## Practice Questions

1. Name the **most suitable purification technique** for each of the following mixtures, giving a one-line reason:
   (a) **Glycerol** from spent-lye in soap industry
   (b) ***o*-Nitrophenol** and ***p*-nitrophenol**
   (c) **Chloroform (bp 334 K)** and **Aniline (bp 457 K)**
   (d) **Camphor** mixed with common salt (NaCl)
   (e) Coloured plant pigments (chlorophyll, carotene)
2. Why is **Paper Chromatography** classified as **Partition Chromatography** rather than Adsorption Chromatography?
3. Why is the organic compound fused with **sodium metal** in **Lassaigne's test** before testing for Nitrogen, Sulphur or Halogens? *(Paper question only.)*
4. In Lassaigne's test for **halogens**, why is the sodium fusion extract first boiled with **concentrated HNO₃** before adding AgNO₃?
5. What colour is observed in Lassaigne's test when (a) **only Nitrogen** is present, (b) **both Nitrogen and Sulphur** are present, and (c) **only Sulphur** is tested with sodium nitroprusside? Write the formula of the coloured species for (a) and (b).
6. In **Dumas' method**, **0.300 g** of an organic compound gave **56.0 mL of dry N₂ gas** at STP (molar volume 22400 mL). Calculate the **percentage of Nitrogen** in the compound.
7. In **Kjeldahl's method**, ammonia evolved from **0.700 g** of an organic compound was completely neutralised by **25.0 mL of 0.20 M H₂SO₄**. Calculate the **percentage of Nitrogen** in the compound.
8. Name **three classes of nitrogen-containing organic compounds** to which **Kjeldahl's method CANNOT be applied**, and explain why!
9. In **Carius estimation**, **0.200 g** of an organic bromo-compound yielded **0.188 g of AgBr**. Calculate the **percentage of Bromine** in the compound. *(Ag = 108, Br = 80 → AgBr = 188.)*
10. In **Carius estimation of Sulphur**, **0.320 g** of an organic compound produced **0.233 g of BaSO₄**. Calculate the **percentage of Sulphur**. *(BaSO₄ = 233, S = 32.)*
11. On a TLC plate, the solvent front moved **10.0 cm** while a dye spot moved **6.5 cm**. Calculate its **R_f value**. Can R_f ever be greater than 1?
12. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) Steam distillation is used for compounds that are steam-volatile and miscible with water.
    (b) In Lassaigne's test, AgI is a yellow precipitate insoluble in NH₄OH.
    (c) Kjeldahl's method works accurately for nitrobenzene and pyridine.
    (d) Fractional distillation separates miscible liquids whose boiling points lie very close to each other.

### Answers

1. (a) **Distillation under reduced pressure** (glycerol decomposes at normal bp 563 K).
   (b) **Steam distillation** (*o*-nitrophenol steam-volatile due to intramolecular H-bond).
   (c) **Simple distillation** (bp difference = 123 K > 40 K).
   (d) **Sublimation** (camphor sublimes directly).
   (e) **Chromatography** (differential adsorption/partition of pigments).

2. Paper chromatography mein filter paper ke andar trapped **paani (water)** ek liquid **stationary phase** ka kaam karta hai aur upar chadhne wala solvent liquid **mobile phase** — dono liquids ke beech solute ka batwara (**partition**) hota hai!

3. Organic compounds mein N, S, X **covalent** roop mein jude hote hain (ionise nahi hote). Sodium ke saath fuse karne par we **ionic, water-soluble NaCN, Na₂S, NaX** mein badal jaate hain jinke ions aasani se test diye ja sakte hain.

4. Agar compound mein N ya S ho, to SFE mein **NaCN aur Na₂S** honge jo AgNO₃ ke saath AgCN (white) ya Ag₂S (black) bana kar halogen test bigaad denge! Conc. HNO₃ ke saath ubaalne par NaCN (as HCN) aur Na₂S (as H₂S) gas bankar udd jaate hain!

5. (a) **Only N:** **Prussian Blue** — **Fe₄[Fe(CN)₆]₃**.
   (b) **Both N & S:** **Blood-Red** — **[Fe(SCN)]²⁺**.
   (c) **Only S (nitroprusside):** **Violet** colour ([Fe(CN)₅NOS]⁴⁻).

6. %N = (28 ÷ 22400) × (56.0 ÷ 0.300) × 100 = (28 ÷ 400) × (100 ÷ 0.300) = 0.07 ÷ 0.300 × 100 = **23.33%**.
   Jaanch: 56 / 22400 = 0.0025 mol N₂ = 0.070 g N; 0.070 / 0.300 = 23.33% ✅.

7. H₂SO₄ ki basicity = 2 → mmol of H⁺ used = 2 × 0.20 × 25.0 = **10.0 mmol**.
   %N = (1.4 × 10.0) ÷ 0.700 = 14.0 ÷ 0.700 = **20.0%**!
   Jaanch: 10 mmol N = 0.140 g N; (0.140 ÷ 0.700) × 100 = 20.0% ✅.

8. **Nitro compounds** (nitrobenzene), **Azo compounds** (−N=N−), aur **ring mein N** wale heterocyclic compounds (**pyridine**). Inka Nitrogen Kjeldahl digestion mein ammonium sulphate ((NH₄)₂SO₄) mein nahi badal paata!

9. %Br = (80 ÷ 188) × (0.188 ÷ 0.200) × 100 = (80 × 0.001 ÷ 0.200) × 100 = **40.0%**!
   Jaanch: 0.188 g AgBr = 0.001 mol Br = 0.080 g Br; 0.080/0.200 = 40.0% ✅.

10. %S = (32 ÷ 233) × (0.233 ÷ 0.320) × 100 = (32 × 0.001 ÷ 0.320) × 100 = **10.0%**!
    Jaanch: 0.233 g BaSO₄ = 0.001 mol S = 0.032 g S; 0.032/0.320 = 10.0% ✅.

11. **R_f** = 6.5 ÷ 10.0 = **0.65**. Nahi, R_f kabhi 1 se bada nahi ho sakta kyunki solute kabhi solvent front se aage nahi nikal sakta!

12. (a) **False** ❌. Steam distillation **water-immiscible** (paani mein aghulansheel) steam-volatile compounds ke liye hota hai!
    (b) **True** ✅.
    (c) **False** ❌. Kjeldahl method nitrobenzene aur pyridine par **fail** ho jaata hai.
    (d) **True** ✅.
