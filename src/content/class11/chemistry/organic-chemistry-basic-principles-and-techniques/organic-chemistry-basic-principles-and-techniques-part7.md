## Mixed Worked Examples

### Mixed Example 1 (Folder 1). CH₃−CH(OH)−CH₂−COOH ka Poora Audit!

**Sawaal:** **CH₃−CH(OH)−CH₂−COOH** ke liye batao: (a) IUPAC naam, (b) har C ki hybridisation, (c) kul σ aur π bonds, aur (d) kya ismein koi **chiral carbon** hai?

**Hal:**
- **(a) IUPAC Naam (Topic 2):** Principal FG = **−COOH** (C1!), C3 par −OH (prefix *hydroxy-*) → **3-hydroxybutanoic acid**!
- **(b) Hybridisation (Topic 1):** C1 (−COOH) = **sp²**; C2, C3, C4 = **sp³**.
- **(c) σ aur π Bonds (Topic 1):** Formula = C₄H₈O₃ (kul **15 atoms** open chain!) → **14 σ bonds** + **1 π bond** (C=O)!
- **(d) Chirality (Topic 3):** **C3** par chaar bilkul alag groups jude hain: **−H, −OH, −CH₃, −CH₂COOH** → **HAAN, C3 ek chiral carbon hai** (optical isomerism dikhayega!).

**Jaanch:** 15 atoms open chain → 15 − 1 = 14 σ + 1 π; C3 par 4 alag groups ✅.

### Mixed Example 2 (Folder 2). Carbocation Stability + Carboxylic Acid Acidity Combo

**Sawaal:**
(a) Decreasing stability mein lagao: **C₆H₅CH₂⁺, (CH₃)₃C⁺, CH₃CH₂⁺, CH₃⁺**.
(b) Decreasing acidity mein lagao: **CF₃COOH, CCl₃COOH, CH₃COOH**.

**Hal:**
- (a) **C₆H₅CH₂⁺** (resonance!) > **(CH₃)₃C⁺** (9 α-H + 3 +I) > **CH₃CH₂⁺** (3 α-H + 1 +I) > **CH₃⁺** (0 α-H).
- (b) **CF₃COOH** (3 strong −I F atoms) > **CCl₃COOH** (3 −I Cl atoms) > **CH₃COOH** (+I CH₃ group!).

**Jaanch:** Dono orders Resonance, Hyperconjugation aur Inductive effect se 100% match karte hain ✅.

### Mixed Example 3 (Folder 3). Forensic Lassaigne's Test + Carius Sulphur Estimation

**Sawaal:** Ek unknown organic compound (0.320 g) Lassaigne's test mein FeCl₃ ke saath **blood-red** rang deta hai, aur Carius method mein **0.466 g BaSO₄** banata hai.
(a) Compound mein kaunse do extra elements pakke maujood hain?
(b) Compound mein **% Sulphur** nikaalo! *(BaSO₄ = 233, S = 32.)*

**Hal:**
- (a) Blood-red colour (**[Fe(SCN)]²⁺**) tabhi aata hai jab **Nitrogen (N) aur Sulphur (S) DONO** ek saath maujood hon!
- (b) **%S** = (32 ÷ 233) × (0.466 ÷ 0.320) × 100 = (32 × 0.002 ÷ 0.320) × 100 = (0.064 ÷ 0.320) × 100 = **20.0%**!

**Jaanch:** 0.466 g BaSO₄ = 0.002 mol S = 0.064 g S; 0.064 ÷ 0.320 = 20.0% ✅!

## Aam galtiyan (mixed organic questions mein bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: Lassaigne's test mein Blood-Red ([Fe(SCN)]²⁺) aur Prussian Blue (Fe₄[Fe(CN)₆]₃) ko aapas mein badal dena!**
   - Sirf **N** → **Prussian Blue**!
   - **N aur S DONO** → **Blood-Red**!

2. **Galti 2: Carbocation stability order (3° > 2° > 1°) ko Carbanion par bhi laga dena.**
   Carbanions mein +I groups −ve charge ko destabilise karte hain → order bilkul **ulta (CH₃⁻ > 1° > 2° > 3°)** hota hai!

## Practice Questions (Sab Kuch Mila Kar — 12 Fresh Questions)

1. For the compound **CH₃−CO−CH=CH−COOH**:
   (a) Write its correct **IUPAC name**.
   (b) State the **hybridisation** of C1, C2, C3, C4 and C5.
   (c) Count its total **σ and π bonds**.
2. Compare **but-2-ene (CH₃−CH=CH−CH₃)** and **2-methylpropene ((CH₃)₂C=CH₂)** on:
   (a) type of structural isomerism between them,
   (b) which one shows **geometrical (*cis–trans*) isomerism**, and
   (c) number of **α-hydrogens** in each.
3. Classify each of the following as an **electrophile** or **nucleophile**, and state whether **homolytic** or **heterolytic** fission produces carbocations: **AlCl₃, CN⁻, NO₂⁺, H₂O**.
4. In **Dumas' method**, **0.250 g** of an organic compound gave **22.4 mL of dry N₂ gas** at STP (molar volume = 22400 mL). Calculate the **percentage of Nitrogen**.
5. In **Carius estimation**, **0.500 g** of an organic iodo-compound gave **0.235 g of AgI** (molar mass AgI = 235, I = 127). Calculate the **percentage of Iodine**.
6. Choose the best **purification method** for: (a) separating **Aniline from water**, and (b) separating **Chloroform (bp 334 K) from Aniline (bp 457 K)**.
7. Explain why **Phenol (C₆H₅OH)** directs incoming electrophiles to the *ortho* and *para* positions, whereas **Benzoic acid (C₆H₅COOH)** directs them to the *meta* position.
8. Arrange the following **carbanions** in **decreasing order of stability**: **(CH₃)₃C⁻, CH₃⁻, CH₃CH₂⁻, (CH₃)₂CH⁻**, and give the one-line electronic reason.
9. A student tests an organic compound with Lassaigne's sodium fusion extract and gets a **Prussian blue** colour with FeSO₄/FeCl₃. Which element is confirmed? Will **Kjeldahl's method** work if this compound turns out to be **nitrobenzene**?
10. Draw all **3 metamer/ether isomers** of **C₄H₁₀O** and write their IUPAC names.
11. Identify the **chiral carbon** in **2-bromopropanoic acid (CH₃−CH(Br)−COOH)** and count the number of **1° and 2° carbons** in **2-methylpropane**.
12. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) In Lassaigne's test, a blood-red colour with FeCl₃ indicates the presence of both Nitrogen and Sulphur.
    (b) Hyperconjugation is a temporary effect that disappears when the attacking reagent is removed.
    (c) Tropolone is an example of a non-benzenoid aromatic compound.
    (d) In IUPAC naming of polyfunctional compounds, −OH has higher priority than −COOH.

### Answers

1. (a) **4-oxopent-2-enoic acid** (−COOH = C1, C=C at C2, ketone at C4!).
   (b) C1, C2, C3, C4 = **sp²**; C5 (CH₃) = **sp³**.
   (c) Formula C₅H₆O₃ (14 atoms open chain) → **13 σ bonds** + **3 π bonds** (two C=O + one C=C).
   Jaanch: 14 − 1 = 13 σ + 3 π ✅.

2. (a) **Chain isomers** (4 C chain vs 3 C branched chain, dono C₄H₈).
   (b) **But-2-ene** *cis–trans* dikhata hai (2-methylpropene mein =CH₂ par 2 same H hain!).
   (c) Dono mein **6 α-hydrogens** hain!

3. **Electrophiles:** AlCl₃, NO₂⁺; **Nucleophiles:** CN⁻, H₂O. Carbocations **heterolytic fission** se bante hain!

4. %N = (28 ÷ 22400) × (22.4 ÷ 0.250) × 100 = (28 × 0.001 ÷ 0.250) × 100 = **11.2%**!
   Jaanch: 22.4/22400 = 0.001 mol N₂ = 0.028 g N; 0.028/0.250 = 11.2% ✅.

5. %I = (127 ÷ 235) × (0.235 ÷ 0.500) × 100 = (127 × 0.001 ÷ 0.500) × 100 = **25.4%**!
   Jaanch: 0.235 g AgI = 0.001 mol I = 0.127 g I; 0.127/0.500 = 25.4% ✅.

6. (a) **Steam distillation** (aniline steam-volatile aur water-immiscible hai); (b) **Simple distillation** (bp difference = 123 K > 40 K).

7. Phenol ka **−OH** group **+R effect** se *o, p* par electron density badhata hai (*o, p* directing); Benzoic acid ka **−COOH** group **−R effect** se *o, p* par δ⁺ laata hai, isliye electrophile *meta* par lagta hai!

8. Decreasing stability: **CH₃⁻ > CH₃CH₂⁻ > (CH₃)₂CH⁻ > (CH₃)₃C⁻** (alkyl groups ka +I effect negative charge ko badha kar destabilise karta hai!).

9. Prussian blue → **Nitrogen (N)** confirmed! **NAHI**, Kjeldahl's method **nitrobenzene** par kaam nahi karta kyunki −NO₂ group NH₄⁺ mein nahi badalta!

10. (1) **Ethoxyethane** (C₂H₅−O−C₂H₅), (2) **1-Methoxypropane** (CH₃−O−CH₂CH₂CH₃), (3) **2-Methoxypropane** (CH₃−O−CH(CH₃)₂).

11. **2-Bromopropanoic acid** mein **C2** chiral hai (−H, −Br, −CH₃, −COOH). **2-Methylpropane ((CH₃)₃CH)** mein **3 (1° C)** aur **0 (2° C)** (ek 3° C!) hain.

12. (a) **True** ✅.
    (b) **False** ❌. Hyperconjugation **permanent** hai; **Electromeric** effect temporary hota hai!
    (c) **True** ✅.
    (d) **False** ❌. **−COOH** priority list ka sabse bada boss hai (−COOH > −OH)!
