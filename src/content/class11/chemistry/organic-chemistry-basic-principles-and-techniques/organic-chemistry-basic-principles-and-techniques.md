# Topic 1: Carbon ki Tetravalence, Structural Representations aur Organic Compounds ka Classification

1828 se pehle scientists maante the ki organic compounds (jaise urea, sugar, DNA) sirf jeevit sharir ki kisi jaadui **"Vital Force"** se hi ban sakte hain — lab mein nahi! Par 1828 mein **Friedrich Wöhler** ne inorganic salt **ammonium cyanate (NH₄CNO)** ko garam karke lab mein pehla organic compound **Urea (NH₂CONH₂)** bana diya aur Vital Force Theory ko hamesha ke liye khatam kar diya! Aaj hum Carbon ki hybridisation, **1°/2°/3°/4° carbons**, **Bond-Line aur 3D Wedge-Dash** formulas, aur organic compounds ka poora **Classification Tree** (benzenoid, non-benzenoid aur heterocyclic sahit!) seekhenge.

## Part A: Hybridisation, Degree of Carbon aur 4 Structural Representations

1. **Carbon ki Teeno Hybridisations (Chapter 4 ka pul!):**
   - **sp³** (4 single bonds, tetrahedral, 109.5°, 25% s-character) → alkanes (CH₄, C₂H₆).
   - **sp²** (1 double + 2 single, trigonal planar, 120°, 33.3% s-character) → alkenes (C₂H₄).
   - **sp** (1 triple + 1 single ya 2 double, linear, 180°, 50% s-character) → alkynes (C₂H₂).
   - **s-character jitna ZYADA → Carbon utna zyada electronegative aur C−H / C−C bond utna CHHOTA aur MAZBOOT!**

2. **Degree of Carbon aur Hydrogen:**
   - **Primary (1°) Carbon:** Sirf **1** (ya 0, jaise CH₄) doosre C se juda; us par lage H = **1° H**.
   - **Secondary (2°) Carbon:** **2** doosre C se juda; us par lage H = **2° H**.
   - **Tertiary (3°) Carbon:** **3** doosre C se juda; us par lage H = **3° H**.
   - **Quaternary (4°) Carbon:** **4** doosre C se juda — **is par koi H nahi hota (0 H)!**

3. **4 Structural Representations (Isobutane / 2-Methylpropane, C₄H₁₀):**
   - **Complete:** Saare C−C aur C−H dashes dikhaye jaate hain.
   - **Condensed:** **CH₃CH(CH₃)CH₃** ya **(CH₃)₃CH**.
   - **Bond-Line:** Ek ulta 'Y' jismein beech ke vertex par 1 C (3° C, 1 implicit H) aur teeno siron par 3 CH₃ groups!
   - **3D Wedge-Dash:** Solid wedge = observer ki taraf, dashed wedge = observer se door, normal lines = plane of paper!

## Solved Examples

### Example 1. Condensed, Bond-Line aur Degree of Carbon/Hydrogen

**Sawaal:** **2-Methylbutane (CH₃−CH(CH₃)−CH₂−CH₃)** mein kul kitne **1°, 2°, 3° aur 4° Carbon atoms** aur kitne **1°, 2°, 3° Hydrogen atoms** hain?

**Hal:**
- Teen kinare wale **−CH₃** groups: har ek sirf 1 C se juda → **3 (1° C)** → 3 × 3 = **9 (1° H)**.
- Ek **−CH₂−** group: 2 C se juda → **1 (2° C)** → **2 (2° H)**.
- Ek **>CH−** group: 3 C se juda → **1 (3° C)** → **1 (3° H)**; **0 (4° C)**.

**Jaanch:** Kul C = 3 + 1 + 1 = **5**; Kul H = 9 + 2 + 1 = **12** → **C₅H₁₂** ✅!

---

### Example 2. CH₃−CH=CH−C≡CH mein Hybridisation aur σ/π Count

**Sawaal:** **C1H₃−C2H=C3H−C4≡C5H** mein har Carbon ki hybridisation aur poore molecule ke kul σ aur π bonds gino.

**Hal:**
- **C1:** **sp³** (4 σ); **C2 & C3:** **sp²** (3 σ + 1 π); **C4 & C5:** **sp** (2 σ + 2 π).
- **σ bonds:** 6 (C−H) + 4 (C−C) = **10 σ bonds** *(11 atoms open chain → 11 − 1 = 10 σ!)*.
- **π bonds:** 1 (C=C) + 2 (C≡C) = **3 π bonds**.

**Jaanch:** 11 − 1 = 10 σ bonds + 3 π bonds ✅.

---

### Example 3. 7 Organic Compounds ka Exact Classification

**Sawaal:** Classify karo: **Isobutane, Cyclohexane, Benzene, Naphthalene, Tropolone, Furan, Pyridine**.

**Hal:**
- **Isobutane:** Acyclic (branched open-chain).
- **Cyclohexane:** Homocyclic, alicyclic.
- **Benzene & Naphthalene:** Homocyclic, benzenoid aromatic.
- **Tropolone:** Homocyclic, **non-benzenoid aromatic** (7-membered ring!).
- **Furan (O) & Pyridine (N):** **Heterocyclic aromatic**!

**Jaanch:** Har ek compound Figure 1.1 ke exact branch mein fit hota hai ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: Bond-line formula ke har kone (vertex) par H atoms ginna bhool jaana.**
   *Sahi baat:* Carbon hamesha **4 bonds** banata hai! Har vertex par dikhne wale lines gino aur **4 − (lines)** = wahan chhupe hue H atoms!

2. **Galti 2: CH₃− group ko "3° Carbon" bol dena kyunki uspe 3 H lage hain!**
   *Sahi baat:* Degree of Carbon **doosre CARBON atoms** ki ginti se tay hoti hai, H se nahi! CH₃− sirf **1** Carbon se juda hai, isliye wo **1° (primary)** Carbon hai!

3. **Galti 3: Cyclohexene ko aromatic maan lena.**
   Sirf double bond hone se ring aromatic nahi ban jaata — cyclohexene **alicyclic** hai!

## Quick Recap

- **Wöhler (1828):** NH₄CNO (heat) → NH₂CONH₂ (Urea) — Vital Force Theory khatam!
- **Degree of C:** 1° (CH₃), 2° (CH₂), 3° (CH), 4° (C, zero H).
- **Representations:** Complete, Condensed, Bond-Line (vertices = C, implicit H), 3D Wedge-Dash.
- **Classification:** Acyclic vs Cyclic → Homocyclic (Alicyclic, Benzenoid/Non-benzenoid Aromatic) & Heterocyclic (THF, Furan, Thiophene, Pyridine).

## Practice Questions

1. Write the chemical equation for **Friedrich Wöhler's 1828 synthesis of urea** and explain its historical significance in organic chemistry.
2. Expand the following condensed formulas into complete structural formulas and state their molecular formulas: (a) **CH₃CH₂COCH₂CH₃**, (b) **CH₃CH=CH(CH₂)₃CH₃**, (c) **(CH₃)₃CCH₂OH**.
3. Count the exact number of **1°, 2°, 3° and 4° carbon atoms** and **1°, 2° and 3° hydrogen atoms** in:
   (a) **Neopentane: C(CH₃)₄**
   (b) **2,2,4-Trimethylpentane (isooctane): (CH₃)₃C−CH₂−CH(CH₃)₂**
4. Convert the following bond-line descriptions into molecular formulas:
   (a) A regular **hexagon** with no double bonds (cyclohexane)
   (b) A **pentagon** with one =O attached to a ring carbon (cyclopentanone)
   (c) A **triangle** with one methyl group (−CH₃) attached to one corner
5. Identify the **hybridisation of every carbon atom** and count the total **σ and π bonds** in:
   (a) **CH₂=C=O** (ketene)
   (b) **CH₃−CH₂−C≡N** (propanenitrile)
6. Classify each of the following organic compounds as **acyclic, alicyclic, benzenoid aromatic, non-benzenoid aromatic, or heterocyclic**:
   **Tetrahydrofuran (THF), Thiophene, Aniline, Cyclobutene, Propene, Tropolone**.
7. Explain the exact meaning of a **solid wedge**, a **dashed wedge**, and a **plain line** in a 3D wedge-dash representation of methane (CH₄).
8. Arrange **sp³, sp² and sp** hybridised carbon atoms in **increasing order of electronegativity** and explain how this affects the **C−H bond length** in alkanes, alkenes and alkynes.
9. Why does a **quaternary (4°) carbon atom** carry **zero hydrogen atoms**? Give an example of a molecule containing a 4° carbon.
10. What is a **heteroatom** in a cyclic organic compound? Name the heteroatom present in each of: (a) **Furan**, (b) **Thiophene**, (c) **Pyridine**.
11. How many σ bonds and π bonds are present in **CH₃−C(=O)−CH=CH₂**?
12. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) In a bond-line formula, carbon and hydrogen atoms bonded to carbon are not written explicitly.
    (b) Cyclohexene is a benzenoid aromatic compound.
    (c) Every sp-hybridised carbon atom has 50% s-character.
    (d) A CH₃− group attached to a carbon chain is a tertiary (3°) carbon.

### Answers

1. **NH₄CNO (Ammonium cyanate) —(Heat)→ NH₂CONH₂ (Urea)**. Isne sabit kiya ki organic compounds bina kisi "Vital Force" ke lab mein inorganic compounds se banaye ja sakte hain!

2. (a) **C₅H₁₀O** (pentan-3-one); (b) **C₇H₁₄** (hept-2-ene); (c) **C₅H₁₂O** (2,2-dimethylpropan-1-ol).
   Jaanch: (c) mein 3(CH₃) + C + CH₂ + OH = C₅H₁₂O ✅.

3. (a) **Neopentane C(CH₃)₄:** **4 (1° C)**, **0 (2° C)**, **0 (3° C)**, **1 (4° C)**; **12 (1° H)**, 0 (2° H), 0 (3° H).
   (b) **2,2,4-Trimethylpentane (C₈H₁₈):** **5 (1° C)**, **1 (2° C)**, **1 (3° C)**, **1 (4° C)**; **15 (1° H)**, **2 (2° H)**, **1 (3° H)**.
   Jaanch: 5+1+1+1 = 8 C; 15+2+1 = 18 H (C₈H₁₈) ✅.

4. (a) **C₆H₁₂** (6 CH₂); (b) **C₅H₈O** (4 CH₂ + 1 C=O); (c) **C₄H₈** (methylcyclopropane: 2 CH₂ + 1 CH + 1 CH₃).
   Jaanch: (c) mein 4 + 1 + 3 = 8 H ✅.

5. (a) **CH₂=C=O:** C1 = **sp²**, C2 = **sp**; **4 σ + 2 π** (5 atoms open chain → 4 σ).
   (b) **CH₃−CH₂−C≡N:** C1 = **sp³**, C2 = **sp³**, C3 = **sp**; **8 σ + 2 π** (9 atoms open chain → 8 σ).
   Jaanch: Dono mein σ = atoms − 1 ✅.

6. **THF:** Heterocyclic (alicyclic); **Thiophene:** Heterocyclic (aromatic); **Aniline:** Benzenoid aromatic; **Cyclobutene:** Alicyclic; **Propene:** Acyclic; **Tropolone:** Non-benzenoid aromatic.

7. **Solid wedge:** bond kaagaz se bahar **tumhari taraf** aa raha hai; **Dashed wedge:** bond kaagaz ke **peeche (door)** ja raha hai; **Plain line:** bond **kaagaz ke hi plane** mein hai.

8. Increasing EN: **sp³ (25% s) < sp² (33.3% s) < sp (50% s)**. Zyada s-character se orbital nucleus ke paas rehta hai → C−H bond chhota aur mazboot hota jaata hai (**alkane > alkene > alkyne** in C−H length).

9. Carbon ki tetravalence sirf **4 bonds** ki hoti hai. Quaternary (4°) carbon apni chaaron valencies **4 doosre Carbon atoms** se bhar leta hai, isliye H ke liye 0 bonds bachte hain! Example: **Neopentane, C(CH₃)₄**.

10. Ring mein Carbon ke alawa maujood koi bhi atom (O, N, S) **heteroatom** kehlata hai: (a) Furan = **O**, (b) Thiophene = **S**, (c) Pyridine = **N**.

11. **CH₃−C(=O)−CH=CH₂** (C₄H₆O = 11 atoms open chain): **10 σ bonds** + **2 π bonds** (1 C=O + 1 C=C).
    Jaanch: 11 − 1 = 10 σ + 2 π ✅.

12. (a) **True** ✅.
    (b) **False** ❌. Cyclohexene **alicyclic** hai, aromatic nahi.
    (c) **True** ✅.
    (d) **False** ❌. CH₃− sirf 1 Carbon se juda hota hai, isliye wo **primary (1°)** carbon hai!
