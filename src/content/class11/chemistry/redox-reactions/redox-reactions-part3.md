# Topic 3: Balancing Redox Reactions: Oxidation Number Method aur Half-Reaction Method

Chhoti chemical equations ko tum Class 9–10 mein hit-and-trial se balance kar lete the — par jab **Cr₂O₇²⁻ + Fe²⁺ → Cr³⁺ + Fe³⁺** (acidic medium) ya **MnO₄⁻ + I⁻ → MnO₂ + I₂** (basic medium) jaisi ionic redox equations aati hain, to hit-and-trial se ghanton lag jaate hain aur charge galat reh jaata hai! Class 11 mein hum **do pakke, 100% fail-proof scientific methods** seekhenge: (1) **Oxidation Number Method** aur (2) **Half-Reaction (Ion-Electron) Method** — jinse duniya ki koi bhi redox equation 2 minute mein balance ho jaati hai!

---

## Roshni aur do-palde wale taraazu ka dohri-jaanch niyam

Roshni school lab mein purane do-palde wale taraazu (two-pan balance) ko dekh rahi thi. Teacher ma'am ne kaha, "Roshni, redox equation balance karna bilkul is taraazu ko barabar karne jaisa hai — par yahan **EK nahi, DO cheezein** dono paldon par bilkul barabar honi chahiye:
1. **Har tatva ke ATOMS ki ginti** (Mass Balance), aur
2. **Dono taraf ka KUL ELECTRIC CHARGE** (Charge & Electron Balance)!"

Agar left taraf kul charge **+24** hai aur right taraf bhi **+24** aa gaya (saath mein saare atoms barabar!), to samajh jaao tumhara jawab 100% sahi hai! Chalo, dono methods ko kadam-dar-kadam seekhte hain.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Half-reaction** | Poore redox reaction ko do alag tukdon mein baantna: **Oxidation half** (e⁻ loss) aur **Reduction half** (e⁻ gain) | Fe²⁺ → Fe³⁺ + e⁻ (Oxidation half) |
| **Acidic medium** | Jismein O ko balance karne ke liye **H₂O** aur H ko balance karne ke liye **H⁺** joda jaata hai | Cr₂O₇²⁻ + 14H⁺ + 6e⁻ → 2Cr³⁺ + 7H₂O |
| **Basic (Alkaline) medium** | Jismein final balanced equation mein **H⁺ bilkul NAHI** rehta — har H⁺ ko dono taraf barabar **OH⁻** jodkar H₂O mein badal diya jaata hai! | MnO₄⁻ + 2H₂O + 3e⁻ → MnO₂ + 4OH⁻ |
| **Net charge balance** | Left side ke sabhi ions ke charges ka jod = Right side ke sabhi ions ke charges ka jod | Left (+12) = Right (+12) |

## Part A: Half-Reaction (Ion-Electron) Method — 6-Step Fail-Proof Recipe!

1. **Step 1 (Split):** Equation ko **Oxidation half** aur **Reduction half** mein baanto.
2. **Step 2 (Main atoms):** O aur H ko chhodkar baaki sabhi mukhya atoms (Cr, Mn, Fe, I, S...) balance karo!
3. **Step 3 (O aur H balance karo — Acidic style pehle!):**
   - Jis taraf jitne **O kam** hain, udhar utne **H₂O** jodo.
   - Jis taraf jitne **H kam** hain, udhar utne **H⁺** jodo.
4. **Step 4 (Charge balance karo):** Jis taraf zyada positive charge ho, udhar utne **electrons (e⁻)** jodo taaki dono taraf charge barabar ho jaaye!
5. **Step 5 (Equalise e⁻ & Add):** Dono half-reactions ko chhote integers se guna karo taaki **electrons lost = electrons gained** ho jaayein, phir dono ko jodkar e⁻ cancel kar do!
6. **Step 6 (Sirf BASIC medium ke liye extra 10-second step!):** Final equation mein jitne **H⁺** dikhein, **dono taraf utne hi OH⁻** jodo! (Jis taraf H⁺ + OH⁻ milenge, wo **H₂O** ban jaayega — phir dono taraf ke extra H₂O cancel kar do!)

## Solved Examples (Sabhi 4 Cases Cover!)

### Example 1 (Acidic Medium, Half-Reaction Method). Cr₂O₇²⁻ + Fe²⁺ → Cr³⁺ + Fe³⁺

*(Chetavani: Dichromate toxic/carcinogenic hai — sirf paper calculation!)*

**Hal:**
- **Oxidation half:** Fe²⁺ → Fe³⁺ + **e⁻**   *(Charge: +2 = +3 − 1)*
- **Reduction half:**
  - Main atom Cr (2 hain!): Cr₂O₇²⁻ → **2**Cr³⁺
  - 7 O kam right mein → + **7H₂O**: Cr₂O₇²⁻ → 2Cr³⁺ + 7H₂O
  - 14 H kam left mein → + **14H⁺**: Cr₂O₇²⁻ + 14H⁺ → 2Cr³⁺ + 7H₂O
  - Charge: Left = −2 + 14 = **+12**; Right = 2(+3) = **+6** → Left mein **+ 6e⁻** jodo:
    **Cr₂O₇²⁻ + 14H⁺ + 6e⁻ → 2Cr³⁺ + 7H₂O**
- **Equalise e⁻:** Oxidation half ko **6** se guna karo (6Fe²⁺ → 6Fe³⁺ + 6e⁻) aur dono jod do!
- **Final Balanced Equation:**
  > **Cr₂O₇²⁻ + 6Fe²⁺ + 14H⁺ → 2Cr³⁺ + 6Fe³⁺ + 7H₂O**

**Jaanch:** Atoms: Cr (2=2), O (7=7), Fe (6=6), H (14=14); Charge: Left (−2 + 12 + 14 = **+24**) = Right (6 + 18 = **+24**) ✅!

---

### Example 2 (Acidic Medium, Oxidation Number Method). MnO₄⁻ + C₂O₄²⁻ → Mn²⁺ + CO₂

**Hal:**
1. **ON changes per formula unit (DHYAN DO!):**
   - **Mn** in MnO₄⁻ (+7) → Mn²⁺ (+2): **Decrease = 5** per MnO₄⁻.
   - **C** in **C₂**O₄²⁻ (+3) → CO₂ (+4): 1 C mein +1 badha, par C₂O₄²⁻ mein **2 C atoms** hain → **Total Increase = 2 × 1 = 2** per C₂O₄²⁻!
2. **Cross-multiply (5 aur 2 ko barabar karo → LCM = 10):** MnO₄⁻ ko **2** se aur C₂O₄²⁻ ko **5** se guna karo:
   - **2MnO₄⁻ + 5C₂O₄²⁻ → 2Mn²⁺ + 10CO₂**
3. **Balance O (H₂O se) aur H (H⁺ se):**
   - Left O = 8 + 20 = **28**; Right O = 10 × 2 = **20** → Right mein **8H₂O** jodo!
   - Right H = **16** → Left mein **16H⁺** jodo!
- **Final Balanced Equation:**
  > **2MnO₄⁻ + 5C₂O₄²⁻ + 16H⁺ → 2Mn²⁺ + 10CO₂ + 8H₂O**

**Jaanch:** Atoms: Mn (2=2), C (10=10), O (28=20+8), H (16=16); Charge: Left (−2 − 10 + 16 = **+4**) = Right (2×2 = **+4**) ✅!

---

### Example 3 (Basic Medium, Half-Reaction Method). MnO₄⁻ + I⁻ → MnO₂ + I₂

**Hal:**
- **Oxidation half:** 2I⁻ → I₂ + **2e⁻**
- **Reduction half (pehle acidic style):**
  - MnO₄⁻ + 4H⁺ + **3e⁻** → MnO₂ + 2H₂O
- **Equalise e⁻ (×3 aur ×2 → 6e⁻) aur jodo:**
  - 2MnO₄⁻ + 6I⁻ + **8H⁺** → 2MnO₂ + 3I₂ + 4H₂O
- **Step 6 (BASIC conversion — dono taraf 8OH⁻ jodo!):**
  - Left par 8H⁺ + 8OH⁻ = **8H₂O** → Right ke 4H₂O se cancel hokar Left par **4H₂O** bacha!
- **Final Balanced Equation (Basic):**
  > **2MnO₄⁻ + 6I⁻ + 4H₂O → 2MnO₂ + 3I₂ + 8OH⁻**

**Jaanch:** Atoms: Mn (2=2), I (6=6), O (8+4=12 = 4+8), H (8=8); Charge: Left (−2 − 6 = **−8**) = Right (**−8**) ✅!

---

### Example 4 (Basic Disproportionation). P₄ + OH⁻ + H₂O → PH₃ + H₂PO₂⁻

*(Chetavani: White phosphorus aur PH₃ behad zehreele/pyrophoric hain — sirf paper calculation!)*

**Hal:**
- **Reduction half:** P₄ → 4PH₃ (P: 0 → −3, 4 P ke liye 12e⁻) → P₄ + 12H⁺ + 12e⁻ → 4PH₃.
- **Oxidation half:** P₄ → 4H₂PO₂⁻ (P: 0 → +1, 4 P ke liye 4e⁻) → P₄ + 8H₂O → 4H₂PO₂⁻ + 8H⁺ + 4e⁻.
- **Equalise e⁻ (Oxidation × 3 → 12e⁻) aur jodo:**
  - 4P₄ + 24H₂O + 12H⁺ → 4PH₃ + 12H₂PO₂⁻ + 24H⁺
  - H⁺ cancel aur 4 se divide karo: P₄ + 6H₂O → PH₃ + 3H₂PO₂⁻ + 3H⁺
- **Basic conversion (dono taraf 3OH⁻ jodo, 3H₂O cancel):**
  > **P₄ + 3OH⁻ + 3H₂O → PH₃ + 3H₂PO₂⁻**

**Jaanch:** P (4=1+3), O (3+3=6 = 3×2), H (3+6=9 = 3+6); Charge: Left (−3) = Right (−3) ✅!

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: Sirf atoms ginkar ruk jaana aur NET CHARGE check na karna!**
   Agar koi equation atoms mein sahi dikhe par Left charge +10 aur Right charge +8 ho, to wo **100% galat** hai! Hamesha aakhir mein Left Charge = Right Charge check karo!

2. **Galti 2: Basic medium ke final answer mein H⁺ chhod dena!**
   Basic solution mein free H⁺ nahi hota — hamesha dono taraf barabar **OH⁻** jodkar H⁺ ko H₂O mein badlo!

3. **Galti 3: Cr₂O₇²⁻ ya C₂O₄²⁻ mein 2 atoms ka multiplication bhool jaana!**
   Cr₂O₇²⁻ mein **2 Cr** (+6 → +3) hain → total electron gain = 2 × 3 = **6e⁻** (3e⁻ nahi!); C₂O₄²⁻ mein **2 C** (+3 → +4) hain → total loss = 2 × 1 = **2e⁻**!

---

## Quick Recap

- **Half-Reaction Recipe (Acidic):** Split → Main atoms → O via H₂O → H via H⁺ → Charge via e⁻ → Equalise e⁻ & Add!
- **Basic Medium Twist:** Acidic result mein jitne H⁺ hon, dono taraf utne **OH⁻** jodo (H⁺ + OH⁻ = H₂O) aur extra H₂O cancel karo!
- **Final 2-Second Audit:** Har atom barabar AND **Total Charge Left = Total Charge Right**!

## Practice Questions

Balance each of the following redox reactions using either the **Half-Reaction (Ion-Electron) Method** or the **Oxidation Number Method**, and verify both atom balance and charge balance:

1. *(Acidic medium)* **MnO₄⁻ + Fe²⁺ → Mn²⁺ + Fe³⁺**
2. *(Acidic medium)* **Cr₂O₇²⁻ + SO₃²⁻ → Cr³⁺ + SO₄²⁻**
3. *(Acidic medium)* **Cu(s) + NO₃⁻(aq) → Cu²⁺(aq) + NO(g)** *(NO is toxic: paper problem only.)*
4. *(Acidic medium)* **MnO₄⁻ + H₂O₂ → Mn²⁺ + O₂**
5. *(Acidic medium)* **Cr₂O₇²⁻ + C₂O₄²⁻ → Cr³⁺ + CO₂**
6. *(Acidic medium)* **MnO₄⁻ + I⁻ → Mn²⁺ + I₂**
7. *(Basic medium — Disproportionation!)* **Cl₂(g) → Cl⁻(aq) + ClO₃⁻(aq)**
8. *(Basic medium)* **Cr(OH)₃(s) + IO₃⁻(aq) → CrO₄²⁻(aq) + I⁻(aq)** *(Chromate is toxic: paper problem only.)*
9. *(Basic medium)* **Zn(s) + NO₃⁻(aq) → Zn(OH)₄²⁻(aq) + NH₃(g)**
10. *(Acidic medium)* **IO₃⁻ + I⁻ → I₂** *(Comproportionation!)*
11. Why must the **total number of electrons lost** in the oxidation half-reaction always equal the **total number of electrons gained** in the reduction half-reaction?
12. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) In acidic medium, oxygen atoms are balanced by adding OH⁻ ions.
    (b) In a balanced ionic equation, the net electric charge on the left side must equal the net electric charge on the right side.
    (c) In the reduction half-reaction Cr₂O₇²⁻ → 2Cr³⁺, a total of 6 electrons are gained per Cr₂O₇²⁻ ion.
    (d) In basic medium, H⁺ ions may appear in the final balanced equation.

### Answers

1. **MnO₄⁻ + 5Fe²⁺ + 8H⁺ → Mn²⁺ + 5Fe³⁺ + 4H₂O**
   Jaanch: Mn(1), Fe(5), O(4), H(8); Charge: −1 + 10 + 8 = **+17** = +2 + 15 ✅.

2. **Cr₂O₇²⁻ + 3SO₃²⁻ + 8H⁺ → 2Cr³⁺ + 3SO₄²⁻ + 4H₂O**
   Jaanch: Cr(2), S(3), O(7+9=16 = 12+4), H(8); Charge: −2 − 6 + 8 = **0** = +6 − 6 ✅.

3. **3Cu + 2NO₃⁻ + 8H⁺ → 3Cu²⁺ + 2NO + 4H₂O**
   Jaanch: Cu(3), N(2), O(6 = 2+4), H(8); Charge: −2 + 8 = **+6** = 3(+2) ✅.

4. **2MnO₄⁻ + 5H₂O₂ + 6H⁺ → 2Mn²⁺ + 5O₂ + 8H₂O**
   Jaanch: Mn(2), O(8+10=18 = 10+8), H(10+6=16); Charge: −2 + 6 = **+4** = 2(+2) ✅.

5. **Cr₂O₇²⁻ + 3C₂O₄²⁻ + 14H⁺ → 2Cr³⁺ + 6CO₂ + 7H₂O**
   Jaanch: Cr(2), C(6), O(7+12=19 = 12+7), H(14); Charge: −2 − 6 + 14 = **+6** = 2(+3) ✅.

6. **2MnO₄⁻ + 10I⁻ + 16H⁺ → 2Mn²⁺ + 5I₂ + 8H₂O**
   Jaanch: Mn(2), I(10), O(8), H(16); Charge: −2 − 10 + 16 = **+4** = 2(+2) ✅.

7. **3Cl₂ + 6OH⁻ → 5Cl⁻ + ClO₃⁻ + 3H₂O**
   Jaanch: Cl(6=5+1), O(6=3+3), H(6); Charge: **−6** = −5 − 1 ✅.

8. **2Cr(OH)₃ + IO₃⁻ + 4OH⁻ → 2CrO₄²⁻ + I⁻ + 5H₂O**
   Jaanch: Cr(2), I(1), O(6+3+4=13 = 8+5), H(6+4=10); Charge: −1 − 4 = **−5** = −4 − 1 ✅.

9. **4Zn + NO₃⁻ + 7OH⁻ + 6H₂O → 4Zn(OH)₄²⁻ + NH₃**
   Jaanch: Zn(4), N(1), O(3+7+6=16 = 16), H(7+12=19 = 16+3); Charge: −1 − 7 = **−8** = 4(−2) ✅.

10. **IO₃⁻ + 5I⁻ + 6H⁺ → 3I₂ + 3H₂O**
    Jaanch: I(1+5=6), O(3), H(6); Charge: −1 − 5 + 6 = **0** = 0 ✅.

11. Free electrons solution mein jama nahi reh sakte (Law of Conservation of Charge) — jitne electrons reductant chhodta hai, theek utne hi electrons oxidant ko lene padte hain!

12. (a) **False** ❌. Acidic medium mein O atoms **H₂O** jodkar balance kiye jaate hain (aur H ko H⁺ se).
    (b) **True** ✅.
    (c) **True** ✅. 2 Cr atoms × (6 − 3) = 6e⁻.
    (d) **False** ❌. Basic medium mein sabhi H⁺ ko OH⁻ se neutralise karke hata diya jaata hai.
