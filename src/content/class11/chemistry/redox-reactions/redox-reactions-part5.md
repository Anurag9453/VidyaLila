## Mixed Worked Examples

### Mixed Example 1 (Case 1). Bleaching Powder (CaOCl₂) ka Structural ON + Hot Alkali Disproportionation

**Sawaal:**
(a) Bleaching powder (**CaOCl₂**, structural formula **Ca(OCl)Cl**) mein dono Chlorine atoms ke alag-alag oxidation numbers batao!
(b) Hot concentrated NaOH mein Chlorine ke disproportionation **Cl₂ + OH⁻ → Cl⁻ + ClO₃⁻ + H₂O** ko balance karo aur verify karo.

**Hal:**
- **(a) Ca(OCl)Cl:** Ye do alag anions se bana hai — **OCl⁻** (hypochlorite) aur **Cl⁻** (chloride)!
  - **Cl⁻** mein Cl = **−1**.
  - **OCl⁻** mein O (−2) + x = −1 → **Cl = +1**!
  - *(Average ON = (−1 + 1) ÷ 2 = 0, par asli dono Cl atoms **+1 aur −1** par hain!)*
- **(b) Balancing (Basic Disproportionation):**
  - Reduction: Cl₂ + 2e⁻ → 2Cl⁻ (× 5 → 5Cl₂ + 10e⁻ → 10Cl⁻)
  - Oxidation: Cl₂ + 12OH⁻ → 2ClO₃⁻ + 6H₂O + 10e⁻
  - Jodo aur 2 se divide karo: **3Cl₂ + 6OH⁻ → 5Cl⁻ + ClO₃⁻ + 3H₂O**!

**Jaanch:** CaOCl₂: (+2) + (−2) + (+1) + (−1) = 0 ✅; Balanced eq: Cl (6=5+1), O (6=3+3), H (6=6), Charge (−6 = −6) ✅.

### Mixed Example 2 (Case 2). Redox Titration Stoichiometry (KMnO₄ + FeSO₄ in Acidic Medium!)

*(Chetavani: KMnO₄ aur H₂SO₄ corrosive/oxidisers hain — sirf paper calculation / teacher demo!)*

**Sawaal:** Acidic medium mein **20.0 mL of 0.10 M KMnO₄** solution **25.0 mL** unknown **FeSO₄** solution ko poori tarah oxidise kar deta hai. FeSO₄ solution ki **Molarity (M)** nikaalo!

**Hal:**
1. **Balanced Ionic Equation (Topic 3):**
   **MnO₄⁻ + 5Fe²⁺ + 8H⁺ → Mn²⁺ + 5Fe³⁺ + 4H₂O**
   *(Mole ratio: **1 mol MnO₄⁻** reacts with **5 mol Fe²⁺**!)*
2. **Moles of MnO₄⁻ (Chapter 1 ka pul!):**
   - n(MnO₄⁻) = M × V(L) = 0.10 mol/L × 0.020 L = **0.0020 mol** (ya 2.0 mmol).
3. **Moles aur Molarity of Fe²⁺:**
   - n(Fe²⁺) = 5 × n(MnO₄⁻) = 5 × 0.0020 = **0.0100 mol** (10.0 mmol).
   - **M(FeSO₄) = n ÷ V(L) = 0.0100 ÷ 0.025 = 0.40 M**!

**Jaanch:** Shortcut (M₁V₁ / n₁ = M₂V₂ / n₂): (0.10 × 20) / 1 = (0.40 × 25) / 5 → 2.0 = 2.0 ✅!

### Mixed Example 3 (Case 3). Maximum Voltage Galvanic Cell Design

**Sawaal:** Diye gaye E° values hain: **E°(Ag⁺/Ag) = +0.80 V**, **E°(Ni²⁺/Ni) = −0.25 V**, **E°(Cr³⁺/Cr) = −0.74 V**.
(a) Inmein se kin do electrodes ko jodkar **sabse zyada voltage (E°_cell)** milega?
(b) Us cell ka IUPAC notation, balanced overall equation aur exact **E°_cell** likho!

**Hal:**
- (a) Sabse bada E° (**Ag⁺/Ag = +0.80 V** → Cathode) aur sabse chhota E° (**Cr³⁺/Cr = −0.74 V** → Anode) chuno!
- (b) **Cell Notation:** **Cr(s) | Cr³⁺(1 M) || Ag⁺(1 M) | Ag(s)**.
  - **Balanced Equation:** **Cr(s) + 3Ag⁺(aq) → Cr³⁺(aq) + 3Ag(s)**.
  - **E°_cell** = (+0.80) − (−0.74) = **+1.54 V**! *(Dhyan do: Ag⁺ ko 3 se guna kiya, par +0.80 V ko 3 se guna NAHI kiya!)*

**Jaanch:** 0.80 + 0.74 = +1.54 V > 0; Charge left (+3) = right (+3) ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (mixed problems mein bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: Redox titration mein seedha M₁V₁ = M₂V₂ (1:1 ratio) laga dena!**
   Example 2 mein agar tum 0.10 × 20 = M₂ × 25 kar doge to M₂ = 0.08 M (5 guna galat!) aayega! Hamesha balanced equation ka stoichiometric ratio lagao: **(M₁V₁) ÷ n₁ = (M₂V₂) ÷ n₂** (jahan MnO₄⁻ : Fe²⁺ = 1 : 5!).

2. **Galti 2: Highest ON wali species (jaise HNO₃, KMnO₄, H₂SO₄) ko reducing agent maan lena!**
   KMnO₄ mein Mn (+7) aur H₂SO₄ mein S (+6) apne **maximum ON** par hain — we aur oxidise nahi ho sakte, isliye we **sirf OXIDISING AGENTS** ka kaam karte hain!

## Practice Questions (Sab Kuch Mila Kar — 12 Fresh Questions)

1. Compare the oxidation number of Chromium in **K₂CrO₄** and in **CrO₅**, and explain why the second one requires knowing its butterfly peroxide structure.
2. Balance the following redox reaction in **basic medium** and verify both atom balance and net charge balance:
   **MnO₄⁻(aq) + Br⁻(aq) → MnO₂(s) + BrO₃⁻(aq)**
3. *(Acidic Redox Titration)* **25.0 mL** of **0.050 M K₂Cr₂O₇** solution reacts completely with **30.0 mL** of an unknown **FeSO₄** solution in acidic medium:
   **Cr₂O₇²⁻ + 6Fe²⁺ + 14H⁺ → 2Cr³⁺ + 6Fe³⁺ + 7H₂O**
   Calculate the **Molarity** of the FeSO₄ solution. *(Dichromate is toxic: paper problem only.)*
4. Explain using oxidation numbers why **SO₂** can act as **both** an oxidising agent and a reducing agent, whereas **SO₃** can act **only** as an oxidising agent and **H₂S** can act **only** as a reducing agent.
5. Predict whether **Sn²⁺** can reduce **Fe³⁺** to **Fe²⁺** under standard conditions, given **E°(Fe³⁺/Fe²⁺) = +0.77 V** and **E°(Sn⁴⁺/Sn²⁺) = +0.15 V**. Write the balanced ionic equation and calculate **E°_cell**.
6. Balance the iodine–thiosulphate titration reaction: **S₂O₃²⁻(aq) + I₂(aq) → S₄O₆²⁻(aq) + I⁻(aq)**, and find the **average oxidation number of Sulphur** before and after the reaction.
7. Write the **Stock Notation** for **Fe₃O₄** (represented as FeO·Fe₂O₃), **Mn₃O₄** (MnO·Mn₂O₃), and **Cu₂Cl₂**.
8. Classify each of the following reactions and state whether it is a redox reaction:
   (a) **2NO₂(g) + 2OH⁻(aq) → NO₂⁻(aq) + NO₃⁻(aq) + H₂O(l)**
   (b) **AgNO₃(aq) + KCl(aq) → AgCl(s) + KNO₃(aq)**
   (c) **V₂O₅(s) + 5Ca(s) → 2V(s) + 5CaO(s)**
9. *(Acidic Redox Titration)* What volume (in mL) of **0.020 M KMnO₄** is required to completely oxidise **25.0 mL of 0.050 M oxalic acid (H₂C₂O₄)** in acidic medium?
   *(Balanced equation: **2MnO₄⁻ + 5C₂O₄²⁻ + 16H⁺ → 2Mn²⁺ + 10CO₂ + 8H₂O**.)*
10. Given **E°(Zn²⁺/Zn) = −0.76 V**, **E°(Fe²⁺/Fe) = −0.44 V**, and **E°(Cu²⁺/Cu) = +0.34 V**:
    (a) Which pair gives the **lowest positive voltage** galvanic cell, and what is its E°_cell?
    (b) Write the IUPAC cell notation for that cell.
11. Balance the disproportionation of **Bromine (Br₂)** in hot aqueous NaOH: **Br₂ + OH⁻ → Br⁻ + BrO₃⁻ + H₂O**, and verify atom and charge counts.
12. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) In Ca(OCl)Cl (bleaching powder), both Chlorine atoms have an oxidation number of 0.
    (b) In acidic titration, 1 mole of KMnO₄ oxidises 5 moles of Fe²⁺ ions.
    (c) If E°_cell for a redox reaction is positive, then Δ_rG° for that reaction is negative (spontaneous).
    (d) H₂S (S = −2) can act as a strong oxidising agent.

### Answers

1. **K₂CrO₄:** 2(+1) + x + 4(−2) = 0 → **Cr = +6**.
   **CrO₅:** Seedha formula +10 deta hai jo asambhav hai (Cr ka max ON +6); butterfly structure mein 2 peroxide linkages (4 O at −1) aur 1 normal O (−2) hone se asli ON = **+6**!
   Jaanch: Dono mein Cr = +6 ✅.

2. **2MnO₄⁻ + Br⁻ + H₂O → 2MnO₂ + BrO₃⁻ + 2OH⁻**
   *(Mn: +7→+4 = 3e⁻ × 2 = 6e⁻; Br: −1→+5 = 6e⁻ × 1 = 6e⁻!)*
   Jaanch: Mn(2), Br(1), O(8+1=9 = 4+3+2), H(2); Charge: −2 − 1 = **−3** = −1 − 2 ✅.

3. Mole ratio Cr₂O₇²⁻ : Fe²⁺ = **1 : 6**.
   n(Cr₂O₇²⁻) = 0.050 × 0.025 = **0.00125 mol** → n(Fe²⁺) = 6 × 0.00125 = **0.00750 mol**.
   **M(FeSO₄)** = 0.00750 ÷ 0.030 = **0.25 M**!
   Jaanch: (0.050 × 25) / 1 = 1.25 = (0.25 × 30) / 6 ✅.

4. Sulphur ka ON range **−2 se +6** hai: **H₂S (−2, min)** sirf badh sakta hai → **only reducing agent**; **SO₃ (+6, max)** sirf ghat sakta hai → **only oxidising agent**; **SO₂ (+4, beech mein)** upar (+6) aur neeche (0/−2) dono ja sakta hai → **both oxidant & reductant**!

5. **Balanced Equation:** **2Fe³⁺ + Sn²⁺ → 2Fe²⁺ + Sn⁴⁺**.
   Cathode (Fe³⁺/Fe²⁺ = +0.77 V), Anode (Sn⁴⁺/Sn²⁺ = +0.15 V) → **E°_cell = 0.77 − 0.15 = +0.62 V > 0** → **HAAN, Sn²⁺ aasani se Fe³⁺ ko reduce kar sakta hai!**
   Jaanch: 0.77 − 0.15 = +0.62 V > 0 ✅.

6. **Balanced Equation:** **2S₂O₃²⁻ + I₂ → S₄O₆²⁻ + 2I⁻**.
   S₂O₃²⁻ mein average ON of S = **+2**; S₄O₆²⁻ mein average ON of S = **+2.5** (oxidised!).
   Jaanch: S(4), O(6), I(2); Charge: −4 = −2 − 2 ✅.

7. **Fe₃O₄:** Fe(II)O·Fe₂(III)O₃; **Mn₃O₄:** Mn(II)O·Mn₂(III)O₃; **Cu₂Cl₂:** Cu₂(I)Cl₂.

8. (a) **Disproportionation redox** (N: +4 → +3 aur +5!).
   (b) **Non-redox** (double displacement / precipitation — koi ON change nahi!).
   (c) **Metal displacement redox** (Ca: 0 → +2, V: +5 → 0!).

9. Mole ratio MnO₄⁻ : C₂O₄²⁻ = **2 : 5**: (M₁V₁) / 2 = (M₂V₂) / 5
   → (0.020 × V₁) / 2 = (0.050 × 25.0) / 5 = 0.25
   → 0.010 × V₁ = 0.25 → **V₁ = 25.0 mL**!
   Jaanch: n(MnO₄⁻) = 0.50 mmol, n(C₂O₄²⁻) = 1.25 mmol → ratio 0.50 : 1.25 = 2 : 5 ✅.

10. (a) Sabse kam positive antar **Zn (−0.76 V) aur Fe (−0.44 V)** ke beech hai:
       **E°_cell = (−0.44) − (−0.76) = +0.32 V**!
    (b) Notation: **Zn(s) | Zn²⁺(1 M) || Fe²⁺(1 M) | Fe(s)**.
    Jaanch: −0.44 + 0.76 = +0.32 V ✅.

11. **3Br₂ + 6OH⁻ → 5Br⁻ + BrO₃⁻ + 3H₂O**.
    Jaanch: Br(6=5+1), O(6=3+3), H(6); Charge: −6 = −5 − 1 ✅.

12. (a) **False** ❌. Ca(OCl)Cl mein ek Cl **+1** aur doosra Cl **−1** oxidation state mein hai (sirf average 0 hai!).
    (b) **True** ✅. MnO₄⁻ + 5Fe²⁺ + 8H⁺ → Mn²⁺ + 5Fe³⁺ + 4H₂O.
    (c) **True** ✅. (Δ_rG° = −n F E°_cell < 0).
    (d) **False** ❌. H₂S mein S apne minimum ON (−2) par hai, isliye ye sirf **reducing agent** ka kaam karta hai!
