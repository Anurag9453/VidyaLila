# Topic 1: Oxidation aur Reduction (Electronic Concept), Oxidation Number, Stock Notation aur Structural ON

Lohe mein jung lagna, mobile ki battery se bijli banna, gadiyon mein fuel jalna aur hamare sharir mein glucose se energy milna — in sab mein ek hi reaction chalti hai: **Redox Reaction (Reduction + Oxidation)**! ## Part A: Oxidation Number (ON) Nikaalne ke 7 Pakke Niyam

1. **Free / Uncombined Element:** Har akele tatva (Na, Fe, H₂, O₂, O₃, P₄, S₈, Cl₂) ka ON hamesha **0** hota hai.
2. **Monoatomic Ion:** Kisi akele ion ka ON uske **charge** ke barabar hota hai (Na⁺ = +1, Mg²⁺ = +2, Al³⁺ = +3, Cl⁻ = −1, O²⁻ = −2).
3. **Alkali Metals (Group 1) aur Alkaline Earth Metals (Group 2):** Compounds mein Group 1 (Li, Na, K) hamesha **+1**, aur Group 2 (Be, Mg, Ca) hamesha **+2**!
4. **Fluorine (F):** Poori periodic table mein sabse zyada electronegative — isliye sabhi compounds mein F ka ON hamesha **−1** hota hai (kabhi positive nahi!).
5. **Hydrogen (H):** Aam taur par **+1** (HCl, H₂O, NH₃), par **metal hydrides** (NaH, CaH₂, LiAlH₄) mein **−1**!
6. **Oxygen (O):** Aam oxides mein **−2** (H₂O, CO₂, MnO₄⁻), par 3 mashhoor exceptions:
   - **Peroxides (O−O single bond):** H₂O₂, Na₂O₂, BaO₂ mein **−1**!
   - **Superoxides:** KO₂, RbO₂ mein **−½**!
   - **Oxygen Fluorides:** **OF₂** mein **+2** aur **O₂F₂** mein **+1**!
7. **Sum Rule:** Neutral molecule mein sabhi atoms ke ON ka jod = **0**; Polyatomic ion mein sabhi atoms ke ON ka jod = **ion ka net charge**!

### Example 1. Aam aur Tricky Compounds mein Oxidation Number Nikaalna

**Sawaal:** Underlined/central atom ka ON nikalo: (a) **Mn** in KMnO₄, (b) **Cr** in Cr₂O₇²⁻, (c) **S** in H₂SO₄, (d) **O** in KO₂, (e) **H** in NaH.

**Hal:**
- (a) **KMnO₄:** (+1) + x + 4(−2) = 0 → x − 7 = 0 → **Mn = +7**.
- (b) **Cr₂O₇²⁻:** 2x + 7(−2) = −2 → 2x = +12 → **Cr = +6**.
- (c) **H₂SO₄:** 2(+1) + x + 4(−2) = 0 → x = **+6**.
- (d) **KO₂ (Superoxide):** K hamesha +1 → (+1) + 2x = 0 → **O = −½**.
- (e) **NaH (Metal hydride):** Na hamesha +1 → (+1) + x = 0 → **H = −1**.

**Jaanch:** Har molecule/ion mein ON ka jod net charge se match karta hai ✅.

## Part B: Paradoxical / Fractional Oxidation Numbers (Structure Zaroor Dekho!)

Kabhi-kabhi seedha formula lagane par ON ya to **element ke maximum group ON se zyada** aa jaata hai (jo asambhav hai!) ya **fraction (jaise +2.5, +8/3)** mein aata hai! Tab **asli structure** dekhna zaroori hota hai:

### Example 2. CrO₅ (Butterfly Peroxide) aur Tetrathionate Ion (S₄O₆²⁻) ka Asli ON!

1. **CrO₅ (Chromium pentoxide):**
   - *Galat formula check:* x + 5(−2) = 0 → x = +10 (**Asambhav!** Cr Group 6 ka hai, uske paas sirf 6 valence electrons hain → max ON = **+6**!).
   - *Asli Structure (Butterfly):* CrO₅ mein **1 normal O (=O, ON = −2)** aur **2 peroxide linkages (O−O, chaar O atoms, har ek ka ON = −1!)** hote hain:
     - x + 1(−2) + 4(−1) = 0 → x − 6 = 0 → **Cr = +6**!
2. **S₄O₆²⁻ (Tetrathionate ion):**
   - *Average ON:* 4x + 6(−2) = −2 → 4x = +10 → **x = +2.5** (fraction!).
   - *Asli Structure:* **⁻O−S(=O)₂ − S − S − S(=O)₂−O⁻**
     - Beech wale **do S atoms** sirf S se jude hain → unka ON = **0**!
     - Kinare wale **do S atoms** oxygens se jude hain → unka ON = **+5**!
     - Average = (5 + 0 + 0 + 5) ÷ 4 = 10 ÷ 4 = **+2.5**!

**Jaanch:** CrO₅ mein (+6) + (−2) + 4(−1) = 0 ✅; S₄O₆²⁻ mein 2(+5) + 2(0) + 6(−2) = −2 ✅.

---

### Example 3. Oxidant, Reductant aur Stock Notation ki Pehchaan

**Sawaal:**
(a) Reaction **Zn(s) + Cu²⁺(aq) → Zn²⁺(aq) + Cu(s)** mein batao kaun oxidise hua, kaun reduce hua, kaun oxidant hai aur kaun reductant.
(b) **Fe₂O₃, Cu₂O aur MnO₂** ko Stock Notation mein likho.

**Hal:**
- (a) **Zn (0 → +2):** ON badha → **Oxidised** → isliye **Zn = Reducing agent (Reductant)**.
  - **Cu²⁺ (+2 → 0):** ON ghata → **Reduced** → isliye **Cu²⁺ = Oxidising agent (Oxidant)**.
- (b) **Fe₂O₃** (Fe = +3) → **Fe₂(III)O₃**; **Cu₂O** (Cu = +1) → **Cu₂(I)O**; **MnO₂** (Mn = +4) → **Mn(IV)O₂**.

**Jaanch:** Zn ne 2e⁻ diye (OIL), Cu²⁺ ne 2e⁻ liye (RIG) ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: CrO₅ mein Cr = +10 ya H₂SO₅ (Caro's acid) mein S = +8 likh dena!**
   *Sahi baat:* Kisi bhi element ka ON uske **valence electrons (Group max)** se bada nahi ho sakta! Jab bhi calculated ON max se bada aaye, samajh jaao usmein **peroxide bond (O−O, ON = −1)** chhupa hai! (CrO₅ mein Cr = **+6**, H₂SO₅ mein S = **+6**!)

2. **Galti 2: "Jo oxidise hota hai, wahi Oxidising Agent hai" soch lena!**
   *Bilkul ulta!* **Oxidising agent (Oxidant)** doosre ko oxidise karta hai aur **khud REDUCED** hota hai; **Reducing agent (Reductant)** doosre ko reduce karta hai aur **khud OXIDISED** hota hai!

3. **Galti 3: Fractional ON (+2.5 ya +8/3) ka matlab "aadha electron nikla" samajh lena!**
   Electrons hamesha poora-ank mein transfer hote hain! Fractional ON sirf ek hi element ke **alag-alag atoms ke whole-number ONs ka average** hota hai!

## Quick Recap

- **OIL RIG:** Oxidation = e⁻ loss (ON ↑); Reduction = e⁻ gain (ON ↓).
- **Oxidant** khud reduce hota hai; **Reductant** khud oxidise hota hai.
- **Exceptions:** F hamesha −1; H metal hydrides mein −1; O peroxide mein −1, superoxide mein −½, OF₂ mein +2.
- **Max ON se upar aaye** → Peroxide bond check karo (**CrO₅: +6**, **H₂SO₅: +6**)!
- **Fractional ON = Average** of distinct whole-number states (**S₄O₆²⁻**: +5, 0, 0, +5; **Fe₃O₄**: +2, +3, +3).

## Practice Questions

1. Calculate the **oxidation number** of the central/indicated atom in each species:
   (a) **N** in NH₄⁺, NO₃⁻, N₂O and HN₃ (hydrazoic acid)
   (b) **P** in H₃PO₂, H₃PO₃ and H₃PO₄
   (c) **Cl** in HClO, HClO₃ and HClO₄
2. Why does the standard formula give S = +8 for **H₂SO₅** (peroxymonosulphuric acid / Caro's acid), which is impossible? Use its peroxide-bond structure **H−O−S(=O)₂−O−O−H** to find the true oxidation number of Sulphur.
3. Calculate the average oxidation number of Iron in **Fe₃O₄** (magnetite) and Carbon in **C₃O₂** (carbon suboxide, O=C=C=C=O), and then state the **individual whole-number oxidation numbers** of each Fe and C atom!
4. Write the following compounds using **Stock Notation**: (a) **SnCl₄**, (b) **HgCl₂**, (c) **AuCl₃**, (d) **V₂O₅**, (e) **FeO**, (f) **CuO**.
5. In each of the following reactions, identify (i) the substance oxidised, (ii) the substance reduced, (iii) the oxidising agent, and (iv) the reducing agent:
   (a) **Fe₂O₃(s) + 3CO(g) → 2Fe(s) + 3CO₂(g)** *(CO is toxic: paper problem only.)*
   (b) **H₂S(g) + Cl₂(g) → 2HCl(g) + S(s)** *(Toxic gases: paper problem only.)*
6. Calculate the oxidation number of **Oxygen** in: (a) **BaO₂**, (b) **RbO₂**, (c) **O₂F₂**, (d) **H₂O**.
7. Why can **Fluorine (F)** never show a positive oxidation number in any of its compounds, whereas **Chlorine (Cl)** shows oxidation numbers from −1 up to +7?
8. Find the oxidation number of **Carbon** in: (a) **CH₄**, (b) **CH₃OH**, (c) **HCHO**, (d) **HCOOH**, (e) **CO₂**. What trend do you notice as oxygen replaces hydrogen?
9. Explain why **HNO₃** (N = +5) can act ONLY as an oxidising agent, **NH₃** (N = −3) acts ONLY as a reducing agent, while **HNO₂** (N = +3) can act as **both** an oxidising and a reducing agent!
10. Find the oxidation number of **S** in **S₂O₈²⁻** (peroxydisulphate ion, which contains one −O−O− peroxide bridge between the two sulphur groups).
11. Distinguish clearly between **valency** and **oxidation number** using Carbon in **CH₄** and **CH₂Cl₂** as examples.
12. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) In a redox reaction, the oxidising agent loses electrons and gets oxidised.
    (b) The oxidation number of Hydrogen in CaH₂ is −1.
    (c) The oxidation number of Sulphur in S₈ is zero.
    (d) In CrO₅, Chromium has an oxidation number of +10.

### Answers

1. (a) **NH₄⁺:** x + 4 = +1 → **−3**; **NO₃⁻:** x − 6 = −1 → **+5**; **N₂O:** 2x − 2 = 0 → **+1**; **HN₃:** 1 + 3x = 0 → **−1/3**.
   (b) **H₃PO₂:** 3 + x − 4 = 0 → **+1**; **H₃PO₃:** 3 + x − 6 = 0 → **+3**; **H₃PO₄:** 3 + x − 8 = 0 → **+5**.
   (c) **HClO:** **+1**; **HClO₃:** **+5**; **HClO₄:** **+7**.
   Jaanch: H₃PO₂ (+1) < H₃PO₃ (+3) < H₃PO₄ (+5) ✅.

2. Sulphur Group 16 ka hai → max ON = **+6** (+8 asambhav!). H₂SO₅ mein **1 peroxide bond (−O−O−, 2 O at −1)** aur **3 normal O (at −2)** hain:
   2(+1) + x + 3(−2) + 2(−1) = 0 → 2 + x − 6 − 2 = 0 → **S = +6**!
   Jaanch: +2 + 6 − 6 − 2 = 0 ✅.

3. - **Fe₃O₄:** Average ON = **+8/3** (+2.67). Asal mein Fe₃O₄ = FeO · Fe₂O₃ hai → **1 Fe at +2** aur **2 Fe at +3**! (Average = (2 + 3 + 3)/3 = 8/3.)
   - **C₃O₂ (O=C=C=C=O):** Average ON = **+4/3**. Individual: dono kinare wale C = **+2**, beech wala C = **0**! (Average = (2 + 0 + 2)/3 = 4/3.)
   Jaanch: 2 + 3 + 3 = 8 ✅; 2 + 0 + 2 = 4 ✅.

4. (a) **Sn(IV)Cl₄**, (b) **Hg(II)Cl₂**, (c) **Au(III)Cl₃**, (d) **V₂(V)O₅**, (e) **Fe(II)O**, (f) **Cu(II)O**.

5. (a) **Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂:** CO (C: +2 → +4) **oxidised / reducing agent**; Fe₂O₃ (Fe: +3 → 0) **reduced / oxidising agent**.
   (b) **H₂S + Cl₂ → 2HCl + S:** H₂S (S: −2 → 0) **oxidised / reducing agent**; Cl₂ (Cl: 0 → −1) **reduced / oxidising agent**.

6. (a) **BaO₂** (peroxide): **−1**; (b) **RbO₂** (superoxide): **−½**; (c) **O₂F₂:** 2x − 2 = 0 → **+1**; (d) **H₂O:** **−2**.

7. Fluorine sabse zyada electronegative (4.0) hai aur koi d-orbital nahi rakhta → har bond mein sirf electron kheenchta hai (ON = −1). Chlorine ki EN O aur F se kam hai aur uske paas 3d orbitals hain → O/F ke saath +1, +3, +5, +7 tak positive ON dikha sakta hai!

8. (a) **CH₄:** −4; (b) **CH₃OH:** −2; (c) **HCHO:** 0; (d) **HCOOH:** +2; (e) **CO₂:** +4.
   Jaise-jaise H hatkar zyada electronegative O judta hai, Carbon ka ON −4 se +4 tak lagatar badhta (oxidise hota) jaata hai!

9. Nitrogen ka ON range **−3 (min) se +5 (max)** hai. **HNO₃ (+5)** aur upar nahi ja sakta → sirf ghat sakta (reduced) → **only oxidant**! **NH₃ (−3)** aur neeche nahi ja sakta → sirf badh sakta (oxidised) → **only reductant**! **HNO₂ (+3)** beech mein hai → upar (+5) aur neeche (+2/0) dono ja sakta hai → **both oxidant & reductant**!

10. **S₂O₈²⁻** mein **2 peroxide O (−1)** aur **6 normal O (−2)** hain:
    2x + 6(−2) + 2(−1) = −2 → 2x − 14 = −2 → 2x = +12 → **S = +6**!
    Jaanch: 2(+6) − 12 − 2 = −2 ✅.

11. **Valency** sirf bonds ki ginti hai (bina +/− sign ke, hamesha whole number — CH₄ aur CH₂Cl₂ dono mein C ki valency **4** hai!). **Oxidation number** mein +/− sign hota hai (CH₄ mein C = **−4**, jabki CH₂Cl₂ mein C = **0**!).

12. (a) **False** ❌. Oxidising agent electrons **gain** karta hai aur khud **reduced** hota hai.
    (b) **True** ✅. CaH₂ metal hydride hai (Ca = +2, H = −1).
    (c) **True** ✅. Free uncombined element ka ON hamesha 0 hota hai.
    (d) **False** ❌. CrO₅ mein 2 peroxide linkages hone se Cr ka asli ON **+6** hai.
