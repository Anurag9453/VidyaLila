## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Limiting reagent** | Wo reactant jo reaction mein pehle poori tarah khatam ho jaaye aur product ki adhiktam matra tay kare | 18 bread + 7 cheese mein cheese slice |
| **Excess reagent** | Wo reactant jo limiting reagent ke khatam hone ke baad bhi bina reaction kiye bacha reh jaaye | 4 bread slices bachi rehna |
| **Percentage yield** | Asli (actual) product aur hisaab se bane (theoretical) product ka pratishat: (Actual ÷ Theoretical) × 100 | 40.8 g mila jabki 51.0 g banna tha → 80% yield |
| **Mole fraction (χ)** | Kisi ek component ke moles aur solution ke sabhi components ke kul moles ka ratio (unitless) | χ_A = n_A ÷ (n_A + n_B), aur χ_A + χ_B = 1 |
| **Molarity (M)** | 1 litre (1000 mL) **solution** mein ghule hue solute ke moles ki sankhya (unit: mol/L ya M) | 0.20 mol NaCl in 0.25 L solution = 0.80 M |
| **Molality (m)** | 1 kilogram (1000 g) **solvent** mein ghule hue solute ke moles ki sankhya (unit: mol/kg ya m) | 0.10 mol glucose in 0.20 kg water = 0.50 m |

## Part A: Limiting Reagent, Excess Reagent aur Percentage Yield

Jab kisi reaction mein **do reactants ke masses (ya moles) dono diye hon**, to seedha kisi ek se product mat nikaalo! Pehle 3-step method se **Limiting Reagent** pakdo:

1. Balanced equation likho aur dono reactants ke **moles** nikaalo.
2. Har reactant ke liye **Ratio = (Diye gaye moles) ÷ (Balanced equation ka coefficient)** nikaalo.
3. **Jiska Ratio SABSE CHHOTA hoga, wahi Limiting Reagent (LR) hai!** Poora product aur doosre reactant ki khapat sirf LR se hi calculate hogi.

```
   Balanced Equation:   a A   +   b B   --->   c C

   Step 1: n(A) aur n(B) nikaalo.
   Step 2: Tulna karo  -->  [ n(A) / a ]   vs   [ n(B) / b ]
   Step 3: Jo CHHOTA hai = LIMITING REAGENT (LR) !
           Product C ke moles  = (LR ke moles / LR ka coeff) × c
           Excess bacha hua    = Shuruati moles − Kharch hue moles
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Limiting reagent pakadne ka 3-step shortcut: moles ko unke stoichiometric coefficient se bhaag do; jo number chhota aaye wahi reaction ki limit tay karega.

### Example 1. Ammonia banana (Haber reaction): Limiting Reagent aur Bacha Hua Excess *(sirf padhne ke liye; high-pressure industrial gas reaction)*

**Sawaal:** N₂(g) + 3H₂(g) → 2NH₃(g). Agar **28.0 g N₂** aur **10.0 g H₂** ko milakar reaction karaya jaaye, to:
(a) Kaun limiting reagent hai?
(b) Kitne gram NH₃ banega?
(c) Excess reagent kitne gram bina reaction kiye bachega?

**Hal:**
- Balanced equation: N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃ (atom check: N 2 = 2, H 6 = 6).
- Molar masses: N₂ = 28 g/mol, H₂ = 2 g/mol, NH₃ = 17 g/mol.
- **Step 1 (Moles):**
  - n(N₂) = 28.0 ÷ 28 = **1.00 mol**
  - n(H₂) = 10.0 ÷ 2 = **5.00 mol**
- **Step 2 (Ratio = Moles ÷ Coefficient):**
  - N₂ ke liye: 1.00 ÷ 1 = **1.00** (CHHOTA!)
  - H₂ ke liye: 5.00 ÷ 3 = **1.67**
- (a) **Limiting Reagent = N₂** (kyunki 1.00 < 1.67).
- (b) **NH₃ ka mass:** 1 mol N₂ se 2 mol NH₃ banta hai → 1.00 × 2 = 2.00 mol NH₃ → 2.00 × 17 = **34.0 g NH₃**.
- (c) **Bacha hua H₂ (Excess):** 1.00 mol N₂ ko sirf 1.00 × 3 = 3.00 mol H₂ chahiye.
  - Bacha hua H₂ = 5.00 − 3.00 = 2.00 mol = 2.00 × 2 = **4.0 g H₂**.

**Jaanch (Mass Conservation):** Shuruati kul mass = 28.0 + 10.0 = 38.0 g. Ant mein = 34.0 g (NH₃) + 4.0 g (bacha H₂) = 38.0 g ✅.

### Example 2. Aluminium ka Oxidation aur Percentage Yield

**Sawaal:** 4Al(s) + 3O₂(g) → 2Al₂O₃(s). Agar **27.0 g Al** ko **32.0 g O₂** ke saath reaction karaya jaaye:
(a) Limiting reagent aur theoretical yield of Al₂O₃ (gram mein) nikaalo.
(b) Agar experiment mein asli mein sirf **40.8 g Al₂O₃** mila, to **Percentage Yield** kitna hua? *(Al = 27, O = 16)*

**Hal:**
- Molar masses: Al = 27, O₂ = 32, Al₂O₃ = 2(27) + 3(16) = **102 g/mol**.
- **Step 1 (Moles):** n(Al) = 27.0 ÷ 27 = **1.00 mol**; n(O₂) = 32.0 ÷ 32 = **1.00 mol**.
- **Step 2 (Moles ÷ Coeff):**
  - Al: 1.00 ÷ 4 = **0.25** (CHHOTA!)
  - O₂: 1.00 ÷ 3 = **0.333**
- (a) **Limiting Reagent = Al**.
  - Al₂O₃ ke moles = 0.25 × 2 = **0.50 mol** → Theoretical yield = 0.50 × 102 = **51.0 g Al₂O₃**.
  - *(Extra check: O₂ kharch = 0.25 × 3 = 0.75 mol = 24.0 g; bacha O₂ = 32.0 − 24.0 = 8.0 g.)*
- (b) **Percentage Yield** = (Actual Yield ÷ Theoretical Yield) × 100
  = (40.8 ÷ 51.0) × 100 = **80.0%**.

**Jaanch:** 27.0 g Al + 24.0 g O₂ = 51.0 g Al₂O₃ ✅. 80% of 51.0 g = 0.80 × 51.0 = 40.8 g ✅.

## Part B: Concentration of Solutions — Mass %, Mole Fraction, Molarity aur Molality

Class 9 ([is-matter-around-us-pure-part2.md](../../../class9/chemistry/is-matter-around-us-pure/is-matter-around-us-pure-part2.md)) mein tumne **Mass Percent (% w/w)** = (Mass of solute ÷ Mass of solution) × 100 padha tha. Class 11 mein hum teen aur shaktishaali concentration units seekhte hain:

| Unit | Symbol | Formula | SI / Lab Unit | Taapman (T) se Badalta Hai? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mole Fraction** | χ_A | n_A ÷ (n_A + n_B) | Unitless (koi unit nahi) | **Nahi** (mass/mole based) |
| **Molarity** | **M** | n_solute ÷ V_solution (L mein) | mol/L (ya M) | **Haan** (garam hone par volume failta hai, M ghatta hai) |
| **Molality** | **m** | n_solute ÷ W_solvent (kg mein) | mol/kg (ya m) | **Nahi** (sirf mass par nirbhar!) |

**Yaad rakhne ka golden difference (Agastya ka lab sawaal!):**
- **Molarity (M)** ke neeche **SOLUTION ka Volume (L mein)** hota hai → garam karne par liquid ka volume thoda badh jaata hai, isliye **Molarity taapman ke saath badalti hai**.
- **Molality (m)** ke neeche **SOLVENT ka Mass (kg mein)** hota hai → mass taapman se nahi badalta, isliye **Molality aur Mole Fraction taapman se aazaad (temperature-independent)** hain!

### Dilution aur Mixing ke Do Super-Shortcuts (Molarity ke liye)
1. **Dilution (paani milakar halka karna):** Paani milane se solute ke moles nahi badalte →
   **M₁ V₁ = M₂ V₂**
   *(Dhyan do: V₂ **kul final volume** hai; kitna paani milaya = V₂ − V₁!)*
2. **Ek hi solute ke do solutions milana:** Kul moles jud jaate hain →
   **M_mix = (M₁ V₁ + M₂ V₂) ÷ (V₁ + V₂)**

### Example 3. Molarity, Molality aur Mole Fraction nikaalna

**Sawaal:**
(a) **11.7 g NaCl** (molar mass 58.5 g/mol) ko paani mein gholkar **250 mL solution** banaya gaya. Molarity (M) nikaalo.
(b) **18.0 g glucose** (C₆H₁₂O₆, molar mass 180 g/mol) ko **200 g paani** mein ghola gaya. Molality (m) aur glucose ka Mole Fraction (χ_glucose) nikaalo.

**Hal:**
- (a) n(NaCl) = 11.7 ÷ 58.5 = **0.20 mol**.
  - Volume V = 250 mL = 250 ÷ 1000 = **0.250 L**.
  - Molarity M = 0.20 ÷ 0.250 = **0.80 mol/L (0.80 M)**.
- (b) n(glucose) = 18.0 ÷ 180 = **0.10 mol**.
  - Mass of solvent (paani) = 200 g = **0.200 kg**.
  - Molality m = 0.10 ÷ 0.200 = **0.50 mol/kg (0.50 m)**.
  - Paani ke moles n(H₂O) = 200 ÷ 18 = **11.11 mol**.
  - χ_glucose = 0.10 ÷ (0.10 + 11.11) = 0.10 ÷ 11.21 = **0.0089** (aur χ_water = 1 − 0.0089 = **0.9911**).

**Jaanch:** 0.80 mol/L × 0.250 L = 0.20 mol = 11.7 g NaCl ✅. 0.50 mol/kg × 0.200 kg = 0.10 mol = 18.0 g glucose ✅. χ_glucose + χ_water = 0.0089 + 0.9911 = 1.0000 ✅.

### Example 4. Dilution aur Molarity-se-Molality Interconversion (Density ke saath) *(HCl dilution sirf teacher demo)*

**Sawaal:**
(a) Teacher demo mein **50 mL of 4.0 M HCl** ko paani mein dheere-dheere milakar (hamesha acid ko paani mein!) **250 mL** final solution banaya gaya. Nayi molarity M₂ kya hogi?
(b) Ek **2.0 M NaCl** aqueous solution ki density **1.08 g/mL** hai. Is solution ki **Molality (m)** nikaalo. *(NaCl = 58.5 g/mol)*

**Hal:**
- (a) M₁ V₁ = M₂ V₂ → 4.0 × 50 = M₂ × 250 → M₂ = 200 ÷ 250 = **0.80 M**.
- (b) **M se m nikaalne ka 3-line method (1 L = 1000 mL solution maano!):**
  - 1 L (1000 mL) solution mein solute (NaCl) ke moles = **2.0 mol** → Solute ka mass = 2.0 × 58.5 = **117 g**.
  - 1000 mL solution ka kul mass = Volume × Density = 1000 mL × 1.08 g/mL = **1080 g**.
  - Solvent (paani) ka mass = Kul solution − Solute = 1080 − 117 = **963 g = 0.963 kg**.
  - Molality m = Solute ke moles ÷ Solvent ka mass (kg) = 2.0 ÷ 0.963 = **2.08 mol/kg (2.08 m)**.

**Jaanch:** 2.08 × 0.963 = 2.00 mol NaCl ✅; 117 g + 963 g = 1080 g = 1000 mL × 1.08 g/mL ✅.

### Parts Per Million (ppm) aur Molality–Mole Fraction Pul

1. **Parts Per Million (ppm):** Jab solute bahut hi kam matra (trace amounts — jaise peene ke paani mein fluoride ya hawa mein pollutant) mein ho:
   > **ppm = (Mass of solute ÷ Mass of solution) × 10⁶** *(dilute aqueous solution mein 1 ppm ≈ 1 mg/L!)*
2. **Molality (m) aur Mole Fraction (χ_B) ka seedha pul:**
   > **m = (χ_B × 1000) ÷ (χ_A × M_A)** *(jahan χ_B = solute ka mole fraction, χ_A = 1 − χ_B = solvent ka mole fraction, M_A = solvent ka molar mass; paani ke liye M_A = 18 g/mol!)*

### Example 5. Drinking Water mein ppm aur Mole Fraction se Molality

**Sawaal:**
(a) **500 g** drinking water sample mein **0.0020 g (2.0 mg)** dissolved CaCO₃ mila. Concentration **ppm** mein nikaalo.
(b) Ek aqueous solution mein solute ka mole fraction **χ_B = 0.05** hai. Iski **Molality (m)** nikaalo.

**Hal:**
- (a) ppm = (0.0020 ÷ 500) × 10⁶ = 0.000004 × 10⁶ = **4.0 ppm**.
- (b) χ_B = 0.05 → χ_A (paani) = 1 − 0.05 = **0.95**, M_A = **18 g/mol**.
  - m = (0.05 × 1000) ÷ (0.95 × 18) = 50 ÷ 17.1 = **2.92 mol/kg (2.92 m)**.

**Jaanch:** 4.0 ppm × 500 g ÷ 10⁶ = 0.0020 g ✅; 2.92 mol solute + 55.56 mol H₂O (1 kg) → χ_B = 2.92 ÷ 58.48 = 0.050 ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: Jiska gram mass kam dikhe, usi ko Limiting Reagent maan lena.**
   Example 1 mein 28 g N₂ aur 10 g H₂ diya tha. Ek student ne "10 g chhota hai" soch kar H₂ ko limiting reagent maan liya!
   *Sahi baat:* H₂ bahut halka hai (2 g/mol), isliye 10 g H₂ mein **5 mol** hain, jabki 28 g N₂ mein sirf **1 mol** hai! Hamesha **moles ÷ coefficient** ki tulna karo, gram ki nahi!

2. **Galti 2: Molality ke denominator mein solvent ke bajay poore solution ka mass rakh dena.**
   Example 4(b) mein 1080 g (kul solution) se bhaag dene par 2.0 ÷ 1.080 = 1.85 m galat jawab aata hai.
   *Sahi baat:* Molality hamesha **per kg of SOLVENT** hoti hai, isliye kul solution (1080 g) mein se solute (117 g) ghatakar **963 g (0.963 kg)** se bhaag do!

3. **Galti 3: Dilution mein V₂ ko "milaye gaye paani ka volume" samajh lena.**
   "100 mL of 3.0 M solution ko 0.50 M banane ke liye kitna paani milayein?" — M₁V₁ = M₂V₂ se V₂ = 600 mL aata hai. Student 600 mL paani milane ko keh deta hai!
   *Sahi baat:* V₂ = 600 mL **final total volume** hai. 100 mL pehle se hai, isliye extra paani = 600 − 100 = **500 mL** milana hoga!

4. **Galti 4: Molarity aur Molality mein taapman ka asar ulta kar dena.**
   Yaad rakho: **Molarity = Volume** (V garam hone par badhta hai → M badalta hai); **Molality = Mass** (Mass taapman se nahi badalta → m sthir rehta hai).

## Quick Recap

- **Limiting Reagent:** Sabse chhota (moles ÷ coefficient) wala reactant product ki limit tay karta hai; doosra excess bacha rehta hai.
- **Percentage Yield** = (Actual Yield ÷ Theoretical Yield) × 100.
- **Mole Fraction:** χ_A = n_A ÷ (n_A + n_B); sabhi mole fractions ka jod hamesha **1** hota hai.
- **Molarity (M)** = n_solute ÷ V_solution (L); **M₁V₁ = M₂V₂** (dilution); **M_mix = (M₁V₁ + M₂V₂) ÷ (V₁ + V₂)**; taapman par nirbhar.
- **Molality (m)** = n_solute ÷ W_solvent (kg); taapman se bilkul aazaad!

### Answers

1. Balanced: 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO (Mg = 24, O₂ = 32, MgO = 40).
   n(Mg) = 12.0 ÷ 24 = **0.50 mol**; n(O₂) = 16.0 ÷ 32 = **0.50 mol**.
   Ratio: Mg = 0.50 ÷ 2 = **0.25** (chhota!); O₂ = 0.50 ÷ 1 = 0.50.
   (a) **Limiting reagent = Mg**.
   (b) n(MgO) = 0.50 mol → Mass = 0.50 × 40 = **20.0 g MgO**.
   (c) O₂ kharch = 0.25 mol = 8.0 g → Bacha O₂ = 16.0 − 8.0 = **8.0 g**.
   Jaanch: 12.0 + 16.0 = 28.0 g; ant mein 20.0 + 8.0 = 28.0 g ✅.

2. Balanced: CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O (CH₄ = 16, O₂ = 32, CO₂ = 44).
   n(CH₄) = 32.0 ÷ 16 = **2.00 mol**; n(O₂) = 96.0 ÷ 32 = **3.00 mol**.
   Ratio: CH₄ = 2.00 ÷ 1 = 2.00; O₂ = 3.00 ÷ 2 = **1.50** (chhota!).
   **Limiting reagent = O₂**.
   n(CO₂) = 1.50 mol → Mass = 1.50 × 44 = **66.0 g CO₂**.
   CH₄ kharch = 1.50 mol = 24.0 g → Bacha CH₄ = 32.0 − 24.0 = **8.0 g CH₄**.
   Jaanch: 24.0 g CH₄ + 96.0 g O₂ = 120.0 g; products 66.0 g CO₂ + 3.00 × 18 (54.0 g H₂O) = 120.0 g ✅.

3. Molar masses: P₄ = 4 × 31 = **124 g/mol**, O₂ = 32, P₄O₁₀ = 124 + 160 = **284 g/mol**.
   n(P₄) = 62.0 ÷ 124 = **0.50 mol**; n(O₂) = 64.0 ÷ 32 = **2.00 mol**.
   Ratio: P₄ = 0.50 ÷ 1 = 0.50; O₂ = 2.00 ÷ 5 = **0.40** (chhota!).
   **Limiting reagent = O₂**.
   n(P₄O₁₀) = 0.40 mol → Mass = 0.40 × 284 = **113.6 g P₄O₁₀**.
   P₄ kharch = 0.40 mol = 49.6 g → Bacha P₄ = 62.0 − 49.6 = **12.4 g P₄**.
   Jaanch: 49.6 + 64.0 = 113.6 g ✅.

4. CaCO₃ (100) → CaO (56) + CO₂ (44).
   n(CaCO₃) = 50.0 ÷ 100 = 0.50 mol → Theoretical yield of CaO = 0.50 × 56 = **28.0 g**.
   Percentage yield = (21.0 ÷ 28.0) × 100 = **75.0%**.
   Jaanch: 0.75 × 28.0 = 21.0 g ✅.

5. Molar mass NaOH = 23 + 16 + 1 = **40 g/mol**.
   n(NaOH) = 4.00 ÷ 40 = **0.10 mol**; V = 500 mL = **0.500 L**.
   Molarity M = 0.10 ÷ 0.500 = **0.20 M**.
   Jaanch: 0.20 mol/L × 0.500 L × 40 g/mol = 4.00 g ✅.

6. Molar mass C₂H₅OH = 24 + 6 + 16 = **46 g/mol**; H₂O = **18 g/mol**.
   n(ethanol) = 46.0 ÷ 46 = **1.00 mol**; n(water) = 72.0 ÷ 18 = **4.00 mol**.
   Kul moles = 1.00 + 4.00 = 5.00 mol.
   χ_ethanol = 1.00 ÷ 5.00 = **0.20**; χ_water = 4.00 ÷ 5.00 = **0.80**.
   Jaanch: 0.20 + 0.80 = 1.00 ✅.

7. Molar mass KNO₃ = 39 + 14 + 48 = **101 g/mol**.
   n(KNO₃) = 20.2 ÷ 101 = **0.20 mol**; Solvent (paani) = 400 g = **0.400 kg**.
   Molality m = 0.20 ÷ 0.400 = **0.50 mol/kg (0.50 m)**.
   Jaanch: 0.50 × 0.400 × 101 = 20.2 g ✅.

8. M₁ V₁ = M₂ V₂ → 3.0 × 100 = 0.50 × V₂ → V₂ = 300 ÷ 0.50 = **600 mL** (kul final volume).
   Milaya jaane wala paani = 600 − 100 = **500 mL**.
   Jaanch: 0.50 M × 600 mL = 300 mmol = 3.0 M × 100 mL ✅.

9. M_mix = (200 × 1.5 + 300 × 2.5) ÷ (200 + 300) = (300 + 750) ÷ 500 = 1050 ÷ 500 = **2.1 M**.
   Jaanch: 2.1 M, 1.5 M aur 2.5 M ke beech hai aur 2.5 M ke zyada paas kyunki uska volume (300 mL) zyada tha ✅.

10. 1 L (1000 mL) solution maano:
    - Solute (NaOH) = 3.0 mol = 3.0 × 40 = **120 g**.
    - Solution ka kul mass = 1000 mL × 1.12 g/mL = **1120 g**.
    - Solvent (paani) ka mass = 1120 − 120 = **1000 g = 1.000 kg**.
    - Molality m = 3.0 ÷ 1.000 = **3.00 mol/kg (3.00 m)**.
    Molality taapman badalne par bhi nahi badalti kyunki ye sirf mass par nirbhar karti hai (volume par nahi).
    Jaanch: 120 g + 1000 g = 1120 g = 1000 mL × 1.12 g/mL ✅.

11. ppm = (0.0015 ÷ 250) × 10⁶ = 0.000006 × 10⁶ = **6.0 ppm**.
    Jaanch: 6.0 × 250 ÷ 10⁶ = 0.0015 g ✅.

12. χ_ethanol = 0.10 → χ_water = 1 − 0.10 = 0.90; M_water = 18 g/mol.
    m = (0.10 × 1000) ÷ (0.90 × 18) = 100 ÷ 16.2 = **6.17 mol/kg (6.17 m)**.
    Jaanch: 6.17 ÷ (6.17 + 55.56) = 6.17 ÷ 61.73 = 0.100 ✅.
