# Topic 1: Chemistry Ka Mahatva, Matter Ki Prakriti, Maapan aur Uncertainty

Class 9 aur 10 mein tumne matter ki states, pure substances, mixtures, SI units aur basic significant figures ka parichay liya tha. Class 11 mein chemistry sirf "kya bana" tak seemit nahi rehti — yahan har experiment mein **kitna exact mass, kitna exact temperature aur kitne bharosemand digits** hain, ye sabse bada sawaal ban jaata hai. Aaj hum dekhenge ki chemistry hamare aas-paas ki duniya mein kahan-kahan kaam karti hai, intensive aur extensive properties mein kya farq hai, dimensional analysis se bade-se-bade unit conversion bina galti kaise karte hain, aur significant figures ke jod-ghata aur guna-bhaag ke pakke niyam kya hain.

## Subodh aur dawai ki dukaan ka chaar-decimal wala taraazu

Subodh apni Mummy ke saath mohalle ki purani compounding dispensary gaya tha, jahan pharmacist didi ek khaas marham (ointment) ke liye safed powder tol rahi thin. Glass ke band box ke andar rakhe digital analytical balance ki screen par likha aaya: **0.2500 g**.

Subodh ne hairani se poochha, "Didi, aapke taraazu mein aakhir mein do zero kyun dikh rahe hain? Ghar ke rasoi wale scale par to 0.2500 aur 0.25 ek hi baat hoti, balki wo to seedha 0.3 g dikha deta!"

Pharmacist didi muskurayin aur bottle par chipka ek chhota label dikhaya: **"Store between 15 °C and 25 °C (59 °F to 77 °F). Dose: 250.0 mg."** Unhone kaha, "Subodh, maths mein 0.25 aur 0.2500 barabar ho sakte hain, par chemistry lab aur dawai ki duniya mein un do aakhri zero ka bahut bada matlab hai! 0.25 g ka matlab hai ki galti lagbhag 0.01 g tak ho sakti hai, jabki 0.2500 g batata hai ki humne **das-hazaarvein gram (0.0001 g)** tak pakka tola hai. Dawai mein itna sa farq bhi asar badal sakta hai."

Subodh ko ek nayi baat samajh aayi: chemistry mein har naap ke saath uski **unit** aur uski **pakki hadd (uncertainty)** dono jude hote hain. Chalo, chemistry ki is buniyaad ko shuru se samajhte hain.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Intensive property** | Aisa gun jo padarth ki matra (size ya mass) par nirbhar nahi karta | Temperature, density, boiling point, rang |
| **Extensive property** | Aisa gun jo padarth ki matra badhne par seedha badhta hai | Mass, volume, total energy |

## Part A: Chemistry Ka Mahatva aur Matter Ki Prakriti (Tez Bridge + Nayi Baat)

Chemistry wo vigyan hai jo padarth (matter) ki **banawat (composition), sanrachna (structure), gunon (properties)** aur unmein hone wale **badlaavon (transformations)** ka adhyayan karta hai.

- **Dawaiyan aur Swasthya:** Jaan bachane wali dawaiyan chemistry ki den hain — jaise cancer ke ilaj mein upyog hone wale **cisplatin** aur **taxol**, aur AIDS ke marijon ke liye **AZT (Azidothymidine)**. *(Sirf jaankari ke liye; koi bhi dawai sirf doctor ki salah se li jaati hai.)*
- **Naye Padarth aur Takneek:** Superconducting ceramics, optical fibres, solar cells ke silicon chips, aur halka-mazboot polymer — sab chemistry ki samajh se bane hain.
- **Paryavaran aur Green Chemistry:** Ozone parat ko nuksaan pahunchane wale CFCs (chlorofluorocarbons) ki jagah surakshit vikalp banana, aur aise tareeke dhoondhna jisse factories kam se kam zehreela kachra (waste) banayein — ise **Green Chemistry** kehte hain.

### Matter ka Bantwara (Class 9 se Bridge)

Class 9 ([is-matter-around-us-pure.md](../../../class9/chemistry/is-matter-around-us-pure/is-matter-around-us-pure.md) aur [is-matter-around-us-pure-part6.md](../../../class9/chemistry/is-matter-around-us-pure/is-matter-around-us-pure-part6.md)) mein humne dekha tha ki matter do bade hisson mein bant-ta hai: **Pure Substances** (Elements aur Compounds) aur **Mixtures** (Homogeneous aur Heterogeneous).

Class 11 mein hum padarth ke **gunon (properties)** ko do aur kaam ke hisson mein baant-te hain:

1. **Physical vs Chemical Properties:**
   - **Physical properties** (rang, gandh, melting point, boiling point, density) bina padarth ki pahchan badle naapi ja sakti hain.
   - **Chemical properties** (acidity, combustibility, rusting, reaction with acids) tabhi dikhti hain jab padarth chemical change se guzre.
2. **Intensive vs Extensive Properties (Naya Class 11 Idea):**
   - Maano tumhare paas ek jug mein **500 mL paani** hai jiska taapman **25 °C** aur density **1.00 g/cm³** hai. Agar tum use do barabar glasson mein baant do:
     - Har glass ka **mass** aur **volume** aadha (250 mL) ho gaya → ye **Extensive properties** hain (matra par nirbhar).
     - Par dono glasson ka **temperature (25 °C)** aur **density (1.00 g/cm³)** wahi raha! → ye **Intensive properties** hain (matra se aazaad).
   - **Kaam ka raaz:** Do extensive properties ka ratio aksar ek intensive property hota hai! Jaise **Density = Mass (extensive) ÷ Volume (extensive)** → Intensive!

```
   500 mL Paani (25 °C, d = 1.00 g/cm³)
              :
       Do glasson mein baanta
              :
      +-------+-------+
      :               :
      v               v
   Glass 1         Glass 2
   250 mL          250 mL       <--- Volume aadha (EXTENSIVE)
   25 °C           25 °C        <--- Taapman wahi (INTENSIVE)
   1.00 g/cm³      1.00 g/cm³   <--- Density wahi (INTENSIVE)
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Paani ko do glasson mein baantne par volume aur mass aadhe ho jaate hain (extensive), par temperature aur density bilkul nahi badalte (intensive).

## Part B: Maapan, Temperature Scales aur Dimensional Analysis

7 SI base units (metre m, kilogram kg, second s, ampere A, kelvin K, mole mol, candela cd), prefixes (milli, micro, nano, kilo, mega) aur scientific notation tum Class 9 Physics ([measurement-and-units-part2.md](../../../class9/physics/measurement-and-units/measurement-and-units-part2.md)) mein padh chuke ho. Yahan hum chemistry lab ke **teen sabse kaam ke tools** par dhyan denge:

### 1. Mass vs Weight aur Volume ki Units
- **Mass** padarth mein maujood matter ki matra hai (har jagah sthir); **Weight** us par lagne wala gravity ka bal hai (jagah ke saath badal sakta hai). Lab mein hum analytical balance se mass naapte hain.
- **Volume:** SI unit **m³** hai, par lab ke liye ye bahut badi hai. Lab mein hum **L (litre)**, **mL**, **cm³** aur **dm³** chalate hain:
  - **1 L = 1000 mL = 1000 cm³ = 1 dm³**
  - **1 m³ = 1000 L = 10⁶ cm³**

### 2. Teen Temperature Scales (°C, K aur °F)

Class 9 mein humne K = °C + 273 ka aasaan roop dekha tha. Class 11 mein exact offset **273.15** aur **Fahrenheit (°F)** bhi jodte hain:

- **Celsius se Kelvin:** K = °C + 273.15
- **Celsius se Fahrenheit:** °F = (9/5) × (°C) + 32
- **Fahrenheit se Celsius:** °C = (5/9) × (°F − 32)

```
   Scale          Paani ka Jamna      Paani ka Ubalna     Beech ke Kadam
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------
   Celsius (°C)       0.00 °C            100.00 °C          100 kadam
   Kelvin (K)       273.15 K             373.15 K           100 kadam
   Fahrenheit (°F)   32.0  °F            212.0  °F          180 kadam
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Teeno temperature scales par paani ke freezing aur boiling points. Celsius aur Kelvin mein kadam ka size barabar (100) hai, jabki Fahrenheit mein 180 kadam hain — isliye ratio 180/100 = 9/5 aata hai.

*(Ek mazedaar tathya: Kelvin scale par negative temperature nahi hota, jabki °C aur °F negative ho sakte hain.)*

### Example 1. Subodh ke dispensary label ka temperature conversion

**Sawaal:** Insaan ke sharir ka samanya taapman **37.0 °C** hota hai. Ise (a) Fahrenheit (°F) aur (b) Kelvin (K) mein badlo. Saath hi dikhao ki **−40 °C** Fahrenheit scale par kitna hota hai.

**Hal:**
- (a) °F = (9/5) × 37.0 + 32 = (333 / 5) + 32 = 66.6 + 32 = **98.6 °F**.
- (b) K = 37.0 + 273.15 = **310.15 K** (37.0 mein 1 decimal place hai, isliye 1 decimal tak likhein to **310.2 K**, exact offset par 310.15 K).
- (c) −40 °C ke liye: °F = (9/5) × (−40) + 32 = −72 + 32 = **−40 °F**! (Yahi wo akela taapman hai jahan Celsius aur Fahrenheit dono ek hi number dikhate hain.)

**Jaanch:** Ulta check: °C = (5/9) × (98.6 − 32) = (5/9) × 66.6 = 5 × 7.4 = 37.0 °C ✅. Aur (5/9) × (−40 − 32) = (5/9) × (−72) = −40 °C ✅.

### 3. Dimensional Analysis (Factor-Label / Unit Factor Method)

Jab ek saath 2 ya 3 units badalni hon (jaise g/cm³ ko kg/m³ mein), to "multiply karoon ya divide?" ka confusion aksar galti karwata hai. **Unit Factor Method** is confusion ko jad se khatam kar deta hai:
- Har barabari (jaise 1 kg = 1000 g) se **1 ke barabar** ek fraction banao: (1 kg / 1000 g) ya (1000 g / 1 kg).
- Fraction ko is tarah lagao ki **purani unit upar-neeche kat jaaye** aur **nayi unit bach jaaye**!

### Example 2. Concentrated H₂SO₄ ki density ko SI unit mein badalna *(sirf padhne ke liye; H₂SO₄ tez corrosive acid hai)*

**Sawaal:** Lab ki bottle par concentrated sulphuric acid ki density **1.84 g/cm³** likhi hai. Dimensional analysis se ise **kg/m³** mein badlo. Saath hi **2.50 L** ko **m³** mein badlo.

**Hal:**
1. Unit factors: 1 kg = 1000 g → (1 kg / 1000 g); aur 1 m = 100 cm → (100 cm / 1 m)³ = (10⁶ cm³ / 1 m³).
   - Density = 1.84 (g / cm³) × (1 kg / 1000 g) × (10⁶ cm³ / 1 m³)
   - g se g kata, cm³ se cm³ kata:
   - Density = 1.84 × (10⁶ / 10³) kg/m³ = 1.84 × 1000 = **1840 kg/m³** (ya **1.84 × 10³ kg/m³**).
2. 2.50 L ko m³ mein: 1 m³ = 1000 L → unit factor (1 m³ / 1000 L).
   - Volume = 2.50 L × (1 m³ / 1000 L) = **0.00250 m³** (ya **2.50 × 10⁻³ m³**).

**Jaanch:** Paani ki density 1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³ hoti hai; 1.84 g/cm³ uska 1.84 guna hai → 1840 kg/m³ ✅. Aur 0.00250 m³ × 1000 L/m³ = 2.50 L ✅.

## Part C: Uncertainty, Significant Figures ke Arithmetic Rules aur Rounding Off

Class 9 Physics ([measurement-and-units-part4.md](../../../class9/physics/measurement-and-units/measurement-and-units-part4.md)) mein tumne **accuracy** (asli value ke kitna paas), **precision** (kai readings aapas mein kitni paas) aur **significant figures ginne ke 5 niyam** seekhe the. Ek tez yaad:
- Sabhi non-zero digits significant hote hain (285 → 3).
- Non-zero digits ke beech ke zero significant hote hain (2.005 → 4).
- Shuruaat ke (leading) zero kabhi significant nahi hote (0.0034 → 2).
- Decimal point ke baad aakhir ke (trailing) zero significant hote hain (0.2500 → 4, jaise Subodh ke balance mein!).
- **Exact numbers** (ginti ki sankhyayein jaise 20 students, ya paribhasha jaise 1 min = 60 s) mein **infinite (anant) significant figures** hote hain.

Ab Class 11 ke **do naye bade niyam** — jab do measured numbers ko joda, ghataya, guna ya bhaag kiya jaaye:

### 1. Addition aur Subtraction (Jod-Ghata) ka Niyam
> **Result mein decimal point ke baad utne hi digits (decimal places) rakho, jitne sabse kam decimal places wale number mein hain.** *(Yahan total sig figs nahi, sirf decimal places dekhte hain!)*

### 2. Multiplication aur Division (Guna-Bhaag) ka Niyam
> **Result mein kul utne hi significant figures rakho, jitne sabse kam significant figures wale number mein hain.**

### 3. Rounding Off ka Pakka Niyam (Even-Digit Rule)
Jab aakhri digit hatana ho:
1. Hatane wala digit **5 se bada** ho → pichhle digit mein **+1** karo (1.386 → 1.39).
2. Hatane wala digit **5 se chhota** ho → pichhla digit **wahi** rahega (4.334 → 4.33).
3. Hatane wala digit **theek 5** ho (aur uske baad kuch na ho ya sirf 0 ho):
   - Pichhla digit **even (0, 2, 4, 6, 8)** hai → **wahi rahega** (6.45 → 6.4).
   - Pichhla digit **odd (1, 3, 5, 7, 9)** hai → **+1 karke even** bana do (6.35 → 6.4).
   *(Kyu? Agar har 5 ko hamesha upar round karoge to hazaaron readings ka average thoda zyada ki taraf jhuk jaayega. Even-digit rule aadhe 5 upar aur aadhe neeche bhej kar balance rakhta hai!)*

### Example 3. Jod-Ghata, Guna-Bhaag aur Even-Digit Rounding

**Sawaal:** Sahi significant figures tak hal karo:
(a) 14.32 + 2.1 + 0.456
(b) 3.42 × 1.8
(c) 6.35 aur 6.45 dono ko 2 significant figures tak round karo.

**Hal:**
- (a) Seedha jod = 14.32 + 2.1 + 0.456 = 16.876.
  - Decimal places: 14.32 (2), **2.1 (1 — sabse kam)**, 0.456 (3).
  - Result mein sirf **1 decimal place** rahega. 16.876 mein 8 ke baad 7 (> 5) hai, isliye **16.9**.
- (b) Seedha guna = 3.42 × 1.8 = 6.156.
  - Significant figures: 3.42 (3), **1.8 (2 — sabse kam)**.
  - Result mein sirf **2 significant figures** rahenge. 6.156 mein 1 ke baad 56 hai (5 ke baad non-zero 6 hai, matlab 5 se bada), isliye **6.2**.
- (c) **6.35** (2 sig figs): hatane wala digit theek 5, pichhla digit **3 (odd)** → +1 karke **6.4**.
  - **6.45** (2 sig figs): hatane wala digit theek 5, pichhla digit **4 (even)** → wahi rahega, **6.4**.

**Jaanch:** 14.32 + 2.1 + 0.456 = 16.876 → 1 decimal place = 16.9 ✅. 3.42 × 1.8 = 6.156 → 2 sig figs = 6.2 ✅. Dono 6.35 aur 6.45 even digit 4 par ruke → 6.4 ✅.

### 4. Scientific Notation (N × 10ⁿ) mein Jod-Ghata aur Guna-Bhaag

Chemistry mein hum bahut chhote (electron ka mass 9.11 × 10⁻³¹ kg) aur bahut bade (6.022 × 10²³) numbers ke saath kaam karte hain. Inke arithmetic ke do pakke niyam yaad rakho:
- **Multiplication aur Division:** Numbers ko aapas mein guna/bhaag karo, aur **10 ki powers (exponents)** ko guna mein **jod** do, bhaag mein **ghata** do!
  - (N₁ × 10ᵃ) × (N₂ × 10ᵇ) = (N₁ × N₂) × 10ᵃ⁺ᵇ
  - (N₁ × 10ᵃ) ÷ (N₂ × 10ᵇ) = (N₁ ÷ N₂) × 10ᵃ⁻ᵇ
- **Addition aur Subtraction:** Seedhe nahi jod sakte! Pehle dono numbers ki **10 ki power (exponent) ko BARABAR** banao, phir aage ke numbers ko jodo ya ghatao!

### Example 4. Scientific Notation ka Jod-Ghata aur Guna-Bhaag

**Sawaal:** Sahi scientific notation aur significant figures mein hal karo:
(a) (6.65 × 10⁴) + (8.95 × 10³)
(b) (5.6 × 10⁵) × (6.9 × 10⁸)
(c) (2.7 × 10⁻³) ÷ (5.5 × 10⁴)

**Hal:**
- (a) Pehle dono ki power **10⁴** banao: 8.95 × 10³ = **0.895 × 10⁴**.
  - Jod = (6.65 + 0.895) × 10⁴ = 7.545 × 10⁴.
  - 6.65 mein 2 decimal places hain → even-digit rule se 4 (even) ke baad exact 5 hai, isliye **7.54 × 10⁴**!
- (b) Guna = (5.6 × 6.9) × 10⁵⁺⁸ = 38.64 × 10¹³ = 3.864 × 10¹⁴ → 2 sig figs mein **3.9 × 10¹⁴**.
- (c) Bhaag = (2.7 ÷ 5.5) × 10⁻³⁻⁴ = 0.4909 × 10⁻⁷ = 4.909 × 10⁻⁸ → 2 sig figs mein **4.9 × 10⁻⁸**.

**Jaanch:** 66500 + 8950 = 75450 = 7.545 × 10⁴ → 7.54 × 10⁴ ✅; 5.6 × 6.9 = 38.64 → 3.9 × 10¹⁴ ✅; 2.7 ÷ 5.5 = 0.4909 → 4.9 × 10⁻⁸ ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: Intensive property ko extensive ki tarah jod dena.**
   Ek student ne 25 °C wale do glass paani ko ek bade jug mein milaya aur likh diya: "ab paani ka taapman 25 + 25 = 50 °C ho gaya!"
   *Sahi baat:* Volume (250 + 250 = 500 mL) judta hai kyunki wo extensive hai, par **temperature intensive hai** — dono glass milane ke baad bhi jug ka paani **25 °C** hi rahega!

2. **Galti 2: Addition/Subtraction mein Multiplication wala sig-fig niyam laga dena.**
   14.32 + 2.1 = 16.42 ko ek student ne "2.1 mein 2 sig figs hain" soch kar 16 likh diya!
   *Sahi baat:* Jod-ghata mein **total sig figs nahi**, balki **decimal places** dekhte hain. 2.1 mein 1 decimal place hai, isliye sahi jawab **16.4** (3 sig figs) hai, na ki 16!

3. **Galti 3: Decimal ke baad ke trailing zeros ko bekaar samajh kar hata dena.**
   Subodh ke analytical balance ne 0.2500 g dikhaya; agar student notebook mein 0.25 g likh de, to wo balance ki 4-sig-fig precision ko 2-sig-fig mein gira deta hai.
   *Sahi baat:* Measured value ke decimal ke baad wale trailing zeros precision batate hain — unhe kabhi apni marzi se mat hatao!

4. **Galti 4: Exact 5 aane par hamesha upar round kar dena.**
   6.45 ko 2 sig figs mein 6.5 likh dena aam galti hai.
   *Sahi baat:* Jab hatane wale 5 ke baad kuch na ho, to pichhle digit ko dekho — 4 pehle se **even** hai, isliye **6.4** hi sahi hai.

## Quick Recap

- Chemistry matter ki banawat, gunon aur badlaavon ka vigyan hai (dawaiyan, naye padarth, green chemistry).
- **Extensive properties** (mass, volume) matra par nirbhar karti hain; **Intensive properties** (temperature, density, boiling point) matra se aazaad hoti hain.
- **Volume:** 1 L = 1000 mL = 1000 cm³ = 1 dm³; 1 m³ = 1000 L.
- **Temperature:** K = °C + 273.15; °F = (9/5)(°C) + 32.
- **Dimensional Analysis:** Unit factors (ratio = 1) lagao taaki purani unit kat jaaye aur nayi unit bach jaaye.
- **Sig figs:** Jod-ghata mein **sabse kam decimal places**; guna-bhaag mein **sabse kam significant figures**; exact 5 par **even-digit rule**.

### Answers

1. **Intensive:** (a) boiling point, (c) density, (e) temperature (matra par nirbhar nahi).
   **Extensive:** (b) volume, (d) mass, (f) total heat content (matra ke saath badhte hain).

2. (a) **25.0 °C:** °F = (9/5)(25.0) + 32 = 45.0 + 32 = **77.0 °F**; K = 25.0 + 273.15 = **298.15 K** (1 decimal place par 298.2 K).

   (d) **350.15 K:** °C = 350.15 − 273.15 = **77.00 °C**; °F = (9/5)(77.00) + 32 = 138.6 + 32 = **170.6 °F**.
   Jaanch: (5/9)(170.6 − 32) = (5/9)(138.6) = 77.0 °C ✅.

3. 13.6 (g / cm³) × (1 kg / 1000 g) × (10⁶ cm³ / 1 m³) = 13.6 × 1000 = **13600 kg/m³** (3 sig figs mein **1.36 × 10⁴ kg/m³**).
   Jaanch: 13600 ÷ 1000 = 13.6 g/cm³ ✅.

4. (a) 4.80 dm³ × (1000 cm³ / 1 dm³) = **4800 cm³** (ya **4.80 × 10³ cm³**).
   (b) 1.25 (g / L) × (1000 mg / 1 g) × (1 L / 1000 mL) = **1.25 mg/mL** (1000 upar-neeche kat gaya!).
   (c) 3.60 h × (60 min / 1 h) × (60 s / 1 min) = 3.60 × 3600 = **12960 s** (3 sig figs mein **1.30 × 10⁴ s**).
   Jaanch: 12960 ÷ 3600 = 3.60 h ✅.

5. (a) **4** significant figures (shuruaat ke 0.00 nahi gine jaate; 4, 0, 8 aur aakhri trailing 0 chaaron significant hain).
   (b) **3** significant figures (5, 2, 0).
   (c) **5** significant figures (beech ke aur decimal ke baad aakhri zero dono significant).
   (d) **Infinite** significant figures (exact ginti ki sankhya).

6. (a) 28.456 − 4.2 = 24.256 → 4.2 mein 1 decimal place → **24.3** (2 ke baad 56 hai, jo 5 se bada hai).
   (b) 6.25 ÷ 2.5 = 2.5 → 2.5 mein 2 sig figs → **2.5**.
   (c) 1.05 + 0.032 + 12.4 = 13.482 → 12.4 mein 1 decimal place → **13.5**.
   (d) 450 × 0.0020 = 0.90 → 2.0 × 10⁻³ mein 2 sig figs → **0.90**.
   Jaanch: 2.5 × 2.5 = 6.25 ✅; 13.482 ko 1 decimal par 13.5 ✅.

7. (a) 4.75 → 7 odd hai → **4.8**.
   (b) 4.65 → 6 even hai → **4.6**.
   (c) 8.25 → 2 even hai → **8.2**.
   (d) 8.350 → 5 ke baad sirf 0 hai, 3 odd hai → **8.4**.
   (e) 3.451 → 5 ke baad non-zero 1 hai (matlab 5 se bada), isliye even-digit rule nahi lagega, seedha upar round hoga → **3.5**.

8. **Student A** ka average = (4.98 + 5.01 + 5.00)/3 = **4.997 g** (asli 5.00 g ke bahut paas) aur readings aapas mein bhi kareeb hain → **Accurate bhi hai aur Precise bhi**.
   **Student B** ka average = (4.72 + 4.71 + 4.72)/3 = **4.717 g** (asli 5.00 g se kaafi door, isliye **accurate nahi**), par teeno readings aapas mein bahut kareeb hain → **Precise hai** (shayad uske balance mein zero error tha).

9. Jab padarth ki matra dugni ki jaati hai, to mass aur volume dono ek hi factor se badhte hain, aur ratio lete waqt wo factor kat jaata hai. Example: 20 g paani ka volume 20 cm³ → d = 20/20 = 1.0 g/cm³; 60 g paani ka volume 60 cm³ → d = 60/60 = 1.0 g/cm³. Isliye density matra se aazaad (intensive) hai.

10. (a) **True** ✅. 5, 6 aur trailing 0 teeno significant hain.
    (b) **False** ❌. Kelvin scale par 0 K (absolute zero) sabse nichli seema hai; negative Kelvin temperature nahi hota.
    (c) **False** ❌. Exact counted numbers mein **infinite** significant figures hote hain, zero nahi.
    (d) **True** ✅. Volume extensive hai (100 + 100 = 200 mL), par temperature intensive hai (20 °C hi rahega).

11. (a) Power 10⁻³ barabar karo: 1.5 × 10⁻⁴ = 0.15 × 10⁻³.
       Jod = (4.20 + 0.15) × 10⁻³ = **4.35 × 10⁻³** (2 decimal places).
    (b) Bhaag = (8.4 ÷ 2.10) × 10⁶⁻⁽⁻²⁾ = 4.0 × 10⁸ → 2 sig figs mein **4.0 × 10⁸**.
    Jaanch: 0.00420 + 0.00015 = 0.00435 = 4.35 × 10⁻³ ✅; 8.4 ÷ 2.10 = 4.0 ✅.

12. 72.0 (km/h) × (1000 m / 1 km) × (1 h / 3600 s) = **20.0 m/s**.
    cm/min mein: 20.0 (m/s) × (100 cm / 1 m) × (60 s / 1 min) = **1.20 × 10⁵ cm/min**.
    Jaanch: 20.0 × 3.6 = 72.0 km/h ✅.
