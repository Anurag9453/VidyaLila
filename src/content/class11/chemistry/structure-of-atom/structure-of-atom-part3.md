# Topic 3: Dual Behaviour of Matter (de Broglie) aur Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle

Topic 2 mein humne dekha ki radiation (roshni) kabhi wave jaisi aur kabhi particle (photon) jaisi bartaav karti hai. 1924 mein Louis de Broglie ne is baat ko ulta ghumaya: *"Agar roshni ke paas dual nature hai, to chalti hui har cheez — electron, proton, yahan tak ki cricket ball — ke paas bhi **wave nature** hona chahiye!"* Aur teen saal baad 1927 mein Werner Heisenberg ne ek aisa niyam diya jisne Bohr ke "pakke gol track (orbit)" wale khayal ko poori tarah badal diya. Aaj hum in dono krantikari siddhanton ka ganit aur asli matlab samajhte hain.

## Nabhya aur Papa ke camera par tez ghoomte pankhe ki tasveer

Nabhya Papa ke DSLR camera se chhat par tez ghoomte hue pankhe (ceiling fan) ki photo le rahi thi. Pehle usne **bahut tez shutter speed** rakhi: photo mein pankhe ka har patta bilkul **jama hua (sharp)** dikha — yaani uski exact jagah (position) saaf pata chal gayi, par photo dekh kar bilkul andaza nahi lag raha tha ki pankha tez ghoom raha hai ya ruka hua hai (speed gayab)!

Phir Nabhya ne **dheemi shutter speed** rakhi: ab photo mein pankhe ke patte ki lambi **motion-blur lakeer** dikhi — usse speed aur disha ka andaza to mil gaya, par patta theek kis jagah par tha, wo dhundhla ho gaya!

Papa muskuraye: "Nabhya, electron ki duniya mein isse bhi gehra kanoon chalta hai — jise **Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle** kehte hain! Camera mein to tum behtar machine la sakti ho, par prakriti mein ek chhote electron ki **exact position** aur **exact velocity (momentum)** ek hi pal mein pakki naapna **kudratan asambhav** hai!" Chalo, de Broglie waves aur Heisenberg ke is niyam ko aasaan ganit se dekhte hain.

## Part A: de Broglie ka Matter-Wave Equation (1924)

Louis de Broglie ne Einstein ke energy-mass rishte (E = mc²) aur Planck ke quantum rishte (E = hc ÷ λ) ko jodkar chalte hue kan (mass m, speed v) ke liye ek chhota, sundar formula diya:

**λ = h ÷ (m × v) = h ÷ p**

*(Jahan h = 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s, mass m **kilogram (kg)** mein, speed v **m/s** mein, aur λ **metre (m)** mein aati hai!)*

### Do Badi Baatein:
1. **Cricket ball mein wave kyun nahi dikhti, par electron mein dikhti hai?**
   Kyunki h = 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ bahut chhota hai! Bhaari cricket ball (m = 0.15 kg) ke liye λ lagbhag **10⁻³⁴ m** aata hai — jo nucleus se bhi karodon-arabon guna chhota hai, isliye hum use kabhi nahi dekh paate. Par **electron** bahut halka hai (m = 9.11 × 10⁻³¹ kg), isliye uska λ lagbhag **10⁻¹⁰ m (atom ke size ke barabar!)** aata hai — aur electron beam asli mein waves ki tarah **diffraction** dikhati hai!
2. **de Broglie ne Bohr ke mvr = nh/(2π) ka "kyun" bata diya!**
   Agar electron ek wave hai jo gol orbit (paridhi 2πr) par ghoom rahi hai, to wave ko khud se takrakar mitne se bachne ke liye orbit ki poori lambai mein **poora-ank (n) waves** fit honi chahiye:
   **2πr = n × λ**
   Ab λ = h ÷ (mv) rakho → 2πr = n × h ÷ (mv) → **m v r = n × (h / 2π)**!
   Bohr ne jo rule sirf "maan liya" tha, de Broglie ne uska asli wave-kaaran de diya!

```
   2πr = n λ  (Standing wave fit hui -> STABLE orbit!)

          . ~ ~ .
       ~           ~
      :    ( + )    :    <-- Wave ke poore n chakkar orbit par fit!
       ~           ~
          ' ~ ~ '
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Jab electron ki de Broglie wave ke poore n chakkar orbit ki paridhi (2πr = nλ) mein fit ho jaate hain, tabhi wave sthir rehti hai — yahi Bohr ke angular momentum quantisation ka asli kaaran hai.

### Example 1. Cricket Ball banaam Electron ki de Broglie Wavelength

**Sawaal:**
(a) **0.150 kg** ki cricket ball **30.0 m/s** ki speed se phenki gayi. Uski de Broglie wavelength nikaalo.
(b) Ek **electron** (m_e = 9.109 × 10⁻³¹ kg) **2.00 × 10⁶ m/s** ki speed se chal raha hai. Uski de Broglie wavelength metre aur picometre (pm) mein nikaalo.

**Hal:**
- (a) Cricket ball:
  - λ = h ÷ (m × v) = (6.626 × 10⁻³⁴) ÷ (0.150 × 30.0) = (6.626 × 10⁻³⁴) ÷ 4.50 = **1.47 × 10⁻³⁴ m** (naapna asambhav!).
- (b) Electron:
  - λ = (6.626 × 10⁻³⁴) ÷ (9.109 × 10⁻³¹ × 2.00 × 10⁶) = (6.626 × 10⁻³⁴) ÷ (1.8218 × 10⁻²⁴) = **3.64 × 10⁻¹⁰ m** = **364 pm** (atom ke size ke bilkul barabar — aasani se diffraction dikhata hai!).

**Jaanch:** m × v × λ = (9.109 × 10⁻³¹) × (2.00 × 10⁶) × (3.637 × 10⁻¹⁰) = 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s = h ✅.

## Part B: Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle (1927)

Werner Heisenberg ne dikhaya ki kisi bhi microscopic particle ki **position (x)** aur **momentum (p = mv)** ko ek hi pal mein exact jaanna asambhav hai:

**Δx · Δp ≥ h ÷ (4π)**   ya   **Δx · Δv ≥ h ÷ (4π × m)**

*(Yaad rakhne ke liye useful constant: **h ÷ (4π) = 5.27 × 10⁻³⁵ J·s** = 5.27 × 10⁻³⁵ kg·m²/s.)*

- **Asli matlab:** Ye hamare microscope ya instrument ki kami nahi hai — ye **prakriti ka mool kanoon** hai! Electron ko dekhne ke liye kam se kam ek photon us par daalna padega; chhote λ wala high-energy photon electron ko dhakka maar kar uski velocity (Δv) badal dega!
- **Bohr Model par asar:** Agar tum electron ki position atom ke andar (Δx ≈ 10⁻¹⁰ m) pakki kar do, to niche Example 2 mein dekho ki uski velocity mein uncertainty **~5.8 × 10⁵ m/s** aa jaati hai — yaani uski exact path (circular orbit) ki baat karna hi be-matlab ho jaata hai! Yahi humein **Quantum Mechanical Model (probability cloud / orbitals)** ki taraf le jaata hai.

### Example 2. Electron banaam Tennis Ball par Heisenberg Uncertainty

**Sawaal:**
(a) Ek electron (m_e = 9.109 × 10⁻³¹ kg) ki position **Δx = 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁰ m** (1 Å) ki accuracy ke saath naapi gayi. Uski velocity mein kam-se-kam uncertainty (Δv) kitni hogi?
(b) Ek **0.050 kg** ki tennis ball ki position **Δx = 1.0 × 10⁻⁶ m** tak naapi gayi. Uski velocity mein minimum uncertainty (Δv) kitni hogi?

**Hal:**
- (a) Electron ke liye:
  - Δv ≥ h ÷ (4π × m_e × Δx) = (5.273 × 10⁻³⁵) ÷ (9.109 × 10⁻³¹ × 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁰)
  - Δv ≥ (5.273 × 10⁻³⁵) ÷ (9.109 × 10⁻⁴¹) = **5.79 × 10⁵ m/s**! (Lagbhag 5.8 lakh metre/second ki bhari uncertainty!)
- (b) Tennis ball ke liye:
  - Δv ≥ (5.273 × 10⁻³⁵) ÷ (0.050 × 1.0 × 10⁻⁶) = (5.273 × 10⁻³⁵) ÷ (5.0 × 10⁻⁸) = **1.05 × 10⁻²⁷ m/s**! (Bilkul na ke barabar — aam zindagi mein dikhta hi nahi!)

**Jaanch:** Electron ke liye m_e × Δx × Δv = (9.109 × 10⁻³¹) × (10⁻¹⁰) × (5.789 × 10⁵) = 5.27 × 10⁻³⁵ J·s = h/(4π) ✅.

## Part C: Do Super-Important Extensions (Voltage se λ aur Electron Nucleus mein Kyun Nahi Reh Sakta!)

1. **Kinetic Energy (KE) aur Accelerating Potential (V) se de Broglie Wavelength:**
   Kyunki KE = ½mv² = p² ÷ (2m), isliye **p = √(2m · KE)**, aur jab charge q (ya electron e) ko voltage **V** se accelerate kiya jaaye to KE = eV:
   > **λ = h ÷ √(2 m · KE) = h ÷ √(2 m e V)**
   > *(Electron ke liye super-fast shortcut: **λ ≈ 12.27 ÷ √V Å** = **1227 ÷ √V pm**!)*
2. **Energy–Time Uncertainty Form:** **ΔE · Δt ≥ h ÷ (4π)** *(bahut chhote samay Δt tak rehne wali excited states ki energy bilkul sharp nahi hoti!)*

### Example 3. Electron Nucleus ke Andar Kyun Nahi Reh Sakta? + Voltage Shortcut

**Sawaal:**
(a) Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle se siddh karo ki ek electron **nucleus (radius ~1.0 × 10⁻¹⁵ m)** ke andar nahi reh sakta!
(b) Ek electron ko **100 V** potential difference se accelerate kiya gaya. Uski de Broglie wavelength Å aur pm mein nikaalo.

**Hal:**
- (a) Agar electron nucleus mein band hota, to uski position uncertainty adhiktam **Δx ≈ 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁵ m** hoti.
  - Δv ≥ (5.273 × 10⁻³⁵) ÷ (9.109 × 10⁻³¹ × 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁵) = **5.79 × 10¹⁰ m/s**!
  - Par poori prakriti mein koi bhi cheez light ki speed (**c = 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s**) se tez nahi chal sakti! Yahan Δv light ki speed se lagbhag **193 guna bada** aa raha hai — isliye **electron nucleus ke andar nahi reh sakta!**
- (b) V = 100 V → √V = 10:
  - λ = 12.27 ÷ √100 = 12.27 ÷ 10 = **1.227 Å = 122.7 pm**!

**Jaanch:** 5.79 × 10¹⁰ >> 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s (impossible!) ✅; 12.27 ÷ 10 = 1.227 Å ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: de Broglie aur Heisenberg ke formulas mein mass ko gram (g) mein hi rakh dena.**
   Ek student ne 50 g ki ball ko m = 50 rakh kar λ nikaal diya — jawab 1000 guna chhota aa gaya!
   *Sahi baat:* h = 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ **J·s = kg·m²/s** mein **kg** chhupa hai! Isliye mass hamesha **kg** mein (g ÷ 1000) rakho!

2. **Galti 2: Heisenberg Uncertainty ko "machine ki galti" samajh lena.**
   Bahut students sochte hain ki bhavishya mein behtar laser banne par Δx aur Δv dono zero ho jaayenge.
   *Sahi baat:* Ye machine ki kami nahi, balki matter ke **wave-particle dual nature** se nikla prakriti ka pakka niyam hai — koi bhi machine Δx · Δp ko h/(4π) se chhota nahi kar sakti.

3. **Galti 3: Uncertainty formula mein 4π ke bajay 2π ya sirf h rakh dena.**
   Bohr ke angular momentum mein **h / (2π)** hota hai, par Heisenberg Uncertainty mein **h / (4π)** hota hai — dono ko mix mat karo!

## Quick Recap

- **de Broglie (1924):** λ = h ÷ (mv) = h ÷ p. Macroscopic cheezon ke liye λ bahut chhota (unobservable); microscopic electrons ke liye λ ≈ atomic size (electron diffraction dwara proved).
- **2πr = nλ** se Bohr ka mvr = nh/(2π) seedha nikal aata hai.
- **Heisenberg (1927):** Δx · Δp ≥ h/(4π) ya Δx · Δv ≥ h/(4πm), jahan h/(4π) = 5.27 × 10⁻³⁵ J·s.
- Heisenberg principle ne Bohr ke fixed circular orbit ko khatam karke **probability-based orbitals** ka darwaza khola.

## Practice Questions

1. A golf ball of mass **0.045 kg** moves with a speed of **40.0 m/s**. Calculate its de Broglie wavelength in metres. Why is its wave nature not observed in daily life?
2. Calculate the de Broglie wavelength (in metres and picometres) of an electron (m_e = 9.109 × 10⁻³¹ kg) travelling at a speed of **1.00 × 10⁶ m/s**.
3. A proton (mass m_p = 1.673 × 10⁻²⁷ kg) moves at **1.00 × 10⁵ m/s**. Calculate its de Broglie wavelength in picometres.
4. At what speed must an electron travel to have a de Broglie wavelength of **500 pm** (5.00 × 10⁻¹⁰ m)?
5. If an electron and a proton are travelling at the **same speed**, which one has the **longer de Broglie wavelength**, and roughly by how many times?
6. The uncertainty in the velocity of a moving electron is **Δv = 1.0 × 10⁴ m/s**. Using Heisenberg's Uncertainty Principle, calculate the minimum uncertainty in its position (Δx) in metres.
7. A tiny dust grain of mass **1.0 mg** (1.0 × 10⁻⁶ kg) has its position measured within **Δx = 1.0 × 10⁻⁶ m**. Calculate the minimum uncertainty in its velocity (Δv). Is this uncertainty noticeable?
8. Show in two lines how de Broglie's standing-wave condition **2πr = nλ** leads directly to Bohr's angular momentum quantisation rule **mvr = nh / (2π)**.
9. For an electron in the **n = 3** Bohr orbit of hydrogen (radius r₃ = 52.9 × 9 = 476.1 pm), how many complete de Broglie wavelengths fit around the circumference of the orbit, and what is the value of λ in pm?
10. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) According to de Broglie, a heavier particle moving at the same speed as a lighter particle has a shorter wavelength.
    (b) Heisenberg's Uncertainty Principle can be overcome by building a more sensitive optical microscope.
    (c) If the uncertainty in position (Δx) of an electron is made smaller, the minimum uncertainty in its momentum (Δp) increases.
    (d) Electron diffraction experiments confirmed the wave nature of electrons.
11. An electron is accelerated from rest through a potential difference of **400 V**. Using **λ = 12.27 / √V Å**, calculate its de Broglie wavelength in Å and pm.
12. Two particles A (mass m) and B (mass 4m) have the **same kinetic energy**. What is the ratio of their de Broglie wavelengths **λ_A : λ_B**?

### Answers

1. λ = (6.626 × 10⁻³⁴) ÷ (0.045 × 40.0) = (6.626 × 10⁻³⁴) ÷ 1.80 = **3.68 × 10⁻³⁴ m**.
   Ye wavelength itni chhoti hai ki koi bhi slit ya instrument iska wave behaviour nahi naap sakta.
   Jaanch: 1.80 × 3.681 × 10⁻³⁴ = 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ ✅.

2. λ = (6.626 × 10⁻³⁴) ÷ (9.109 × 10⁻³¹ × 1.00 × 10⁶) = **7.27 × 10⁻¹⁰ m** = **727 pm**.
   Jaanch: Example 1(b) mein speed 2.00 × 10⁶ m/s par λ = 364 pm tha; speed aadhi hone par λ dugna (~727 pm) ho gaya ✅.

3. λ = (6.626 × 10⁻³⁴) ÷ (1.673 × 10⁻²⁷ × 1.00 × 10⁵) = (6.626 × 10⁻³⁴) ÷ (1.673 × 10⁻²²) = **3.96 × 10⁻¹² m** = **3.96 pm**.
   Jaanch: 1.673 × 10⁻²² × 3.96 × 10⁻¹² = 6.625 × 10⁻³⁴ ✅.

4. v = h ÷ (m_e × λ) = (6.626 × 10⁻³⁴) ÷ (9.109 × 10⁻³¹ × 5.00 × 10⁻¹⁰) = (6.626 × 10⁻³⁴) ÷ (4.5545 × 10⁻⁴⁰) = **1.45 × 10⁶ m/s**.
   Jaanch: 9.109 × 10⁻³¹ × 1.455 × 10⁶ × 5.00 × 10⁻¹⁰ = 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ ✅.

5. **Electron** ki wavelength lambi hogi! Kyunki λ = h ÷ (mv) mein mass neeche hai, aur electron proton se lagbhag **1836 guna halka** hai — isliye same speed par electron ki λ proton se **lagbhag 1836 guna lambi** hogi.

6. Δx ≥ h ÷ (4π × m_e × Δv) = (5.273 × 10⁻³⁵) ÷ (9.109 × 10⁻³¹ × 1.0 × 10⁴) = (5.273 × 10⁻³⁵) ÷ (9.109 × 10⁻²⁷) = **5.79 × 10⁻⁹ m** (5.79 nm).
   Jaanch: 9.109 × 10⁻³¹ × 10⁴ × 5.789 × 10⁻⁹ = 5.273 × 10⁻³⁵ = h/(4π) ✅.

7. m = 1.0 mg = 1.0 × 10⁻⁶ kg.
   Δv ≥ (5.273 × 10⁻³⁵) ÷ (1.0 × 10⁻⁶ × 1.0 × 10⁻⁶) = **5.27 × 10⁻²³ m/s**.
   Ye sankhya bilkul na ke barabar hai, isliye dhool ke kan par bhi ye uncertainty dikhayi nahi deti.
   Jaanch: 10⁻⁶ × 10⁻⁶ × 5.273 × 10⁻²³ = 5.273 × 10⁻³⁵ ✅.

8. Standing wave condition: 2πr = nλ.
   de Broglie se λ = h ÷ (mv) rakhne par: 2πr = n × h ÷ (mv) → **mvr = n × (h / 2π)** (jo Bohr ka quantisation postulate hai).
   Jaanch: Dono taraf units kg·m²/s (J·s) match karti hain ✅.

9. n = 3 ke liye theek **3 poori wavelengths** fit hongi (3λ = 2πr₃).
   λ = (2 × 3.1416 × 476.1 pm) ÷ 3 = 2991.4 ÷ 3 = **997 pm** (lagbhag 1.00 nm).
   Jaanch: 3 × 997.1 = 2991 pm ≈ 2π(476.1) ✅.

10. (a) **True** ✅. λ = h/(mv) mass ke vyutkramanupati (inversely proportional) hai.
    (b) **False** ❌. Ye prakriti ka mool niyam hai, koi bhi behtar microscope ise paar nahi kar sakta.
    (c) **True** ✅. Δx · Δp ≥ h/(4π) mein ek ghatega to doosra badhega.
    (d) **True** ✅. Crystal se guzarne par electrons waves ki tarah diffraction pattern banate hain.

11. V = 400 V → √400 = 20.
    λ = 12.27 ÷ 20 = **0.6135 Å = 61.35 pm**.
    Jaanch: V 4 guna badhne par (100 V → 400 V) λ aadha (1.227 → 0.6135 Å) ho gaya ✅.

12. λ = h ÷ √(2m · KE). Same KE par λ ∝ 1 / √m.
    λ_A ÷ λ_B = √(m_B / m_A) = √(4m / m) = √4 = **2 : 1**!
    Jaanch: Halka particle A zyada lambi wavelength rakhta hai (2 : 1) ✅.
