## Kapil aur double-decker sleeper bus ke teen bharne ke niyam

Kapil apni Mummy ke saath raat ki double-decker sleeper bus mein chadha. Bus mein neeche aur upar alag-alag berths the, aur har berth mein adhiktam do log baith sakte the. Kapil ne dhyan se dekha ki musafir teen ajeeb niyamon se baith rahe the:
1. Sabse pehle sabse **aasaan aur nichle level** wali seats bharti thin — koi bhi bina zaroorat upar ki oonchi seat par nahi chadhta tha.
2. Jab ek hi row mein teen khaali double-berths hote, to naye musafir pehle **ek-ek karke teeno khaali berths mein akele** baithte the; pairing (doosra saathi bithana) tabhi shuru hoti jab koi khaali berth na bache!
3. Aur jab do log ek hi double-berth share karte, to takraane se bachne ke liye dono **ulte siron (opposite directions)** ki taraf muh karke baithte the!

Kapil muskuraya: "Mummy, ye teeno aadat to bilkul atom ke electrons jaisi hain! Pehla niyam **Aufbau**, doosra **Hund's Rule**, aur teesra **Pauli Exclusion Principle**!" Chalo, in teeno niyamon aur Cr/Cu ke khas jaadu ko dekhte hain.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Aufbau principle** | Ground state mein electrons hamesha sabse kam energy wale orbital se badhti energy ke kram mein bharte hain | 1s → 2s → 2p → 3s → 3p → 4s → 3d |
| **(n + l) rule (Bohr-Bury)** | Multi-electron atom mein jiska (n + l) chhota, uski energy kam; (n + l) barabar ho to chhote n wale ki energy kam | 4s (4+0 = 4) < 3d (3+2 = 5) |
| **Pauli Exclusion Principle** | Ek hi atom ke koi bhi do electrons ke chaaron quantum numbers (n, l, mₗ, mₛ) ek jaise nahi ho sakte | Ek orbital mein max 2 electrons: ek ↑ (+½) aur ek ↓ (−½) |
| **Hund's Rule of Maximum Multiplicity** | Barabar energy (degenerate) orbitals mein pairing tab tak nahi hoti jab tak har orbital mein 1-1 electron parallel spin ke saath na bhar jaaye | Nitrogen 2p³: [↑][↑][↑] (3 unpaired) |
| **Exchange energy** | Ek hi subshell mein parallel spin wale electrons ke aapas mein jagah badalne (exchange) se milne wali extra stability | 3d⁵ mein 10 exchange pairs (3d⁴ ke 6 pairs se zyada!) |

## Part A: Orbitals ki Energy aur Teen Bharne ke Niyam

### 1. Hydrogen Atom banaam Multi-Electron Atom
- **Hydrogen (1 electron):** Koi doosra electron repulsion karne ke liye nahi hai, isliye energy **sirf n** par nirbhar karti hai:
  **1s < 2s = 2p < 3s = 3p = 3d < 4s = 4p = 4d = 4f** (yaani H mein 2s aur 2p degenerate hain!).
- **Multi-Electron Atoms (Z ≥ 2):** Andar wale electrons bahar wale electrons ko nucleus ke aakarshan se thoda rok dete hain (**shielding / screening**). Isse s < p < d < f ka kram banta hai, aur energy **(n + l) Rule** se tay hoti hai:
  1. Jiska **(n + l)** chhota, uski energy kam (pehle bharega).
  2. Agar do orbitals ka **(n + l) barabar** ho, to jiska **n chhota** hai, uski energy kam!

**Class 9 ka adhoora sawaal suljha!**
- **4s** ke liye: n + l = 4 + 0 = **4**.
- **3d** ke liye: n + l = 3 + 2 = **5**.
Kyunki 4 < 5, isliye **4s ki energy 3d se kam** hai — yahi kaaran hai ki Potassium (19) aur Calcium (20) mein electron pehle **4s** mein jaata hai, aur Scandium (21) se **3d** bharna shuru hota hai!

**Bharne ka Pakka Kram (Aufbau Order):**
**1s < 2s < 2p < 3s < 3p < 4s < 3d < 4p < 5s < 4d < 5p < 6s ...**

### 2. Pauli Exclusion Principle (Wolfgang Pauli, 1926)
- Ek atom mein do electrons ka chaar-number pata (n, l, mₗ, mₛ) kabhi bilkul same nahi ho sakta.
- Nateeja: Ek single orbital (jismein n, l, mₗ pehle se fixed hain) mein adhiktam sirf **2 electrons** aa sakte hain, aur unke spins **ulte (↑ aur ↓)** hone chahiye!

### 3. Hund's Rule of Maximum Multiplicity
- Jab p (3 boxes), d (5 boxes) ya f (7 boxes) ke barabar energy wale orbitals bharte hain, to pehle har box mein **1-1 electron ek hi spin (↑ ↑ ↑)** ke saath daalo — kyunki alag-alag kamron mein rehne se electrons ke beech repulsion sabse kam hota hai! Pairing uske baad shuru hoti hai.

```
   Nitrogen (Z = 7): 1s² 2s² 2p³          Oxygen (Z = 8): 1s² 2s² 2p⁴

   2p:  [ ↑ ] [ ↑ ] [ ↑ ]  (3 unpaired)   2p:  [ ↑↓ ] [ ↑ ] [ ↑ ]  (2 unpaired)
         2pₓ   2pᵧ   2p_z                        2pₓ    2pᵧ   2p_z
   (Hund's Rule: pehle 1-1 har box mein!)  (4th electron ab pair banata hai!)
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Nitrogen (2p³) aur Oxygen (2p⁴) ke 2p orbitals ka bharna. Hund's rule ke anusar Nitrogen ke teeno 2p electrons alag-alag boxes mein parallel spin ke saath rehte hain; Oxygen ka chautha electron tab pairing shuru karta hai.

## Part B: Z = 19 se Z = 30, Chromium (24) / Copper (29) ka Apvaad aur Cations!

Argon (Z = 18) = **1s² 2s² 2p⁶ 3s² 3p⁶** ko hum chhote roop mein **[Ar]** likhte hain (isi tarah Neon Z = 10 ko **[Ne]**). Ab Z = 19 se Z = 30 dekho:

| Tatva (Z) | Expected / Normal Rule | **Asli Ground-State Configuration** | Unpaired e⁻ |
|---|---|---|---|
| K (19) | [Ar] 4s¹ | [Ar] 4s¹ | 1 |
| Ca (20) | [Ar] 4s² | [Ar] 4s² | 0 |
| Sc (21) | [Ar] 3d¹ 4s² | [Ar] 3d¹ 4s² | 1 |
| Ti (22) | [Ar] 3d² 4s² | [Ar] 3d² 4s² | 2 |
| V (23) | [Ar] 3d³ 4s² | [Ar] 3d³ 4s² | 3 |
| **Cr (24)** | [Ar] 3d⁴ 4s² | **[Ar] 3d⁵ 4s¹** *(Half-filled 3d⁵!)* | **6** |
| Mn (25) | [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s² | [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s² | 5 |
| Fe (26) | [Ar] 3d⁶ 4s² | [Ar] 3d⁶ 4s² | 4 |
| Co (27) | [Ar] 3d⁷ 4s² | [Ar] 3d⁷ 4s² | 3 |
| Ni (28) | [Ar] 3d⁸ 4s² | [Ar] 3d⁸ 4s² | 2 |
| **Cu (29)** | [Ar] 3d⁹ 4s² | **[Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s¹** *(Full-filled 3d¹⁰!)* | **1** |
| Zn (30) | [Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s² | [Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s² | 0 |

### Chromium (24) aur Copper (29) Extra Stable Kyun Hain? (Do Asli Kaaran!)
3d aur 4s ki energies ek doosre ke bahut paas hoti hain. Ek electron 4s se 3d mein koodne par **exactly half-filled (3d⁵)** ya **completely filled (3d¹⁰)** ban jaata hai, jisse do bade fayde milte hain:
1. **Symmetrical Distribution:** Paanchon d-orbitals mein bilkul barabar (1-1 ya 2-2) electrons hone se shielding kam hoti hai aur nucleus electrons ko zyada kas kar pakadta hai.
2. **Zyada Exchange Energy:** Parallel-spin electrons aapas mein jagah badal (exchange kar) sakte hain; har pair of exchanges energy kam karta hai (stability badhata hai!).
   - Exchange pairs ka formula: **k(k − 1) ÷ 2** (jahan k = ek hi spin wale electrons).
   - 3d⁴ mein 4 parallel electrons → 4 × 3 ÷ 2 = **6 exchange pairs**.
   - **3d⁵** mein 5 parallel electrons → 5 × 4 ÷ 2 = **10 exchange pairs**! (4 extra exchanges → bahut zyada stability!)

### Example 1. Transition Metal Ions (Fe²⁺ aur Fe³⁺) ka Configuration — Sabse Zaroori Niyam!

**Sawaal:** Iron (Fe, Z = 26), Fe²⁺ aur Fe³⁺ ke electronic configurations likho aur har ek mein unpaired electrons gino.

**Hal:**
- **Fe (26):** [Ar] 3d⁶ 4s² (3d⁶ = [↑↓][↑][↑][↑][↑] → **4 unpaired electrons**).
- **Golden Rule for Cations:** Jab transition metal electron khota hai, to electrons pehle **sabse bahar wale shell (sabse bade n, yaani 4s!)** se nikalte hain, **3d se nahi**!
- **Fe²⁺ (24 e⁻):** 4s ke dono electrons gaye → **[Ar] 3d⁶** (**4 unpaired electrons**).
- **Fe³⁺ (23 e⁻):** 4s ke 2 + 3d ka 1 paired electron gaya → **[Ar] 3d⁵** (3d⁵ = [↑][↑][↑][↑][↑] → **5 unpaired electrons**, half-filled stability!).

**Jaanch:** Electron ginti: Fe (18 + 6 + 2 = 26), Fe²⁺ (18 + 6 = 24), Fe³⁺ (18 + 5 = 23) ✅.

### Effective Nuclear Charge (Z_eff) aur Spin-Only Magnetic Moment (μ)

1. **Shielding / Penetration Order:** Ek hi shell n mein nucleus ke sabse paas rehne (penetrate karne) ki kshamta ka kram hai: **s > p > d > f**. Isliye s-electrons nucleus ka sabse zyada **effective nuclear charge (Z_eff = Z − σ)** mehsoos karte hain aur unki energy sabse kam (s < p < d < f) hoti hai!
2. **Spin-Only Magnetic Moment (μ):** Kisi paramagnetic atom ya ion ki chumbakiya taakat uske **unpaired electrons (n)** se seedha nikalti hai:
   > **μ = √(n(n + 2)) BM** *(BM = Bohr Magneton)*

### Example 2. Transition-Metal Ions ka Spin-Only Magnetic Moment (μ)

**Sawaal:** **Fe²⁺, Fe³⁺, Mn²⁺ aur Zn²⁺** ke spin-only magnetic moments (BM mein) nikaalo.

**Hal:**
- **Fe²⁺ ([Ar] 3d⁶, n = 4):** μ = √(4 × 6) = √24 = **4.90 BM**.
- **Fe³⁺ ([Ar] 3d⁵, n = 5):** μ = √(5 × 7) = √35 = **5.92 BM**.
- **Mn²⁺ ([Ar] 3d⁵, n = 5):** μ = √(5 × 7) = √35 = **5.92 BM**.
- **Zn²⁺ ([Ar] 3d¹⁰, n = 0):** μ = √(0 × 2) = **0 BM** (diamagnetic!).

**Jaanch:** Unpaired electrons badhne par (0 → 4 → 5) magnetic moment lagatar badhta hai (0 → 4.90 → 5.92 BM) ✅.

2. **Galti 2: Cr (24) ko [Ar] 3d⁴ 4s² aur Cu (29) ko [Ar] 3d⁹ 4s² likh dena.**
   Hamesha yaad rakho: Cr = **[Ar] 3d⁵ 4s¹** (6 unpaired) aur Cu = **[Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s¹** (1 unpaired)!

3. **Galti 3: Hund's rule tod kar pehle box mein pairing kar dena.**
   Nitrogen (2p³) ko [↑↓][↑][ ] likhna galat hai — isse repulsion badhta hai. Sahi roop [↑][↑][↑] hai (teeno parallel spin, 3 unpaired).

4. **Galti 4: Hydrogen atom mein bhi 2s < 2p maan lena.**
   2s < 2p sirf multi-electron atoms mein shielding ke kaaran hota hai. **Hydrogen (1 electron)** mein 2s aur 2p ki energy **bilkul barabar** hoti hai!

## Quick Recap

- **Aufbau & (n + l) Rule:** Kam (n + l) → pehle bharega; barabar (n + l) → chhota n pehle bharega (isliye 4s < 3d < 4p).
- **Pauli Exclusion Principle:** Ek orbital mein max 2 electrons, ulte spin (↑↓) ke saath.
- **Hund's Rule:** Degenerate orbitals mein pehle 1-1 parallel-spin electron, phir pairing.
- **Cr (24) = [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s¹** aur **Cu (29) = [Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s¹** (symmetry + zyada exchange energy).
- **Cations:** Transition metals mein electrons pehle **4s** se nikalte hain, phir 3d se.

## Practice Questions

1. Using the **(n + l) rule**, arrange the following subshells in **increasing order of energy** in a multi-electron atom: **4p, 5s, 4d, 3d**. Show the (n + l) value of each.
2. Write the complete and noble-gas-core electronic configurations, and state the number of **unpaired electrons**, for: (a) Phosphorus (Z = 15), (b) Sulphur (Z = 16), (c) Titanium (Z = 22), (d) Manganese (Z = 25), (e) Nickel (Z = 28), (f) Zinc (Z = 30).
3. Write the electronic configurations (using [Ar] core) and count the unpaired electrons in these transition-metal ions: (a) **Mn²⁺ (Z = 25)**, (b) **Cu⁺ (Z = 29)**, (c) **Cu²⁺ (Z = 29)**, (d) **Cr³⁺ (Z = 24)**, (e) **Co²⁺ (Z = 27)**.
4. Explain with **exchange-pair calculations** why Chromium (Z = 24) prefers [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s¹ over [Ar] 3d⁴ 4s².
5. Why is the energy of the **2s** orbital equal to the **2p** orbital in a hydrogen atom, whereas **2s < 2p** in a lithium or carbon atom?
6. Identify which rule (**Aufbau**, **Pauli**, or **Hund**) is violated in each of the following 2p / valence diagrams:
   (a) 1s² 2s² 2p² drawn as 2p: [↑↓] [ ] [ ]
   (b) 1s² drawn as 1s: [↑↑]
   (c) 1s² 2s¹ 2p³ for a ground-state carbon atom
7. Calculate the total number of **s-electrons**, **p-electrons**, and **d-electrons** in a neutral **Copper (Z = 29)** atom.
8. Between **Fe²⁺** and **Fe³⁺**, which ion has greater electronic stability and more unpaired electrons? Give a brief reason.
9. Write the four quantum numbers (n, l, mₗ, mₛ) for the **19th (outermost) electron** of Potassium (Z = 19).
10. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) In a neutral Zinc (Z = 30) atom, there are zero unpaired electrons.
    (b) When forming Ti²⁺ from Ti ([Ar] 3d² 4s²), two electrons are removed from the 3d subshell.
    (c) A 3d⁵ configuration has 10 exchange pairs among its five parallel-spin electrons.
    (d) According to Pauli's Exclusion Principle, two electrons in the same orbital must have opposite spins.
11. Calculate the **spin-only magnetic moment (μ = √(n(n + 2)) BM)** for: (a) **Cr³⁺ (Z = 24)**, (b) **Ni²⁺ (Z = 28)**, and (c) **Cu⁺ (Z = 29)**.
12. A dipositive metal ion **M²⁺** of the 3d series has a spin-only magnetic moment of **3.87 BM**. Find the number of unpaired electrons **n** in M²⁺, and suggest a possible 3d configuration for M²⁺.

### Answers

1. (n + l) values:
   - **3d:** 3 + 2 = **5** (n = 3)
   - **4p:** 4 + 1 = **5** (n = 4)
   - **5s:** 5 + 0 = **5** (n = 5)
   - **4d:** 4 + 2 = **6**
   Badhta kram: **3d < 4p < 5s < 4d** (kyunki 3d, 4p, 5s teeno ka jod 5 hai, isliye chhota n pehle aaya!).
   Jaanch: n ka kram 3 < 4 < 5, aur 4d ka jod 6 sabse bada ✅.

2. (a) **P (15):** [Ne] 3s² 3p³ → **3 unpaired**.
   (b) **S (16):** [Ne] 3s² 3p⁴ → **2 unpaired**.
   (c) **Ti (22):** [Ar] 3d² 4s² → **2 unpaired**.
   (d) **Mn (25):** [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s² → **5 unpaired**.
   (e) **Ni (28):** [Ar] 3d⁸ 4s² → **2 unpaired**.
   (f) **Zn (30):** [Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s² → **0 unpaired**.
   Jaanch: Z = 28 (Ni): 18 + 8 + 2 = 28; 3d⁸ mein 3 pairs + 2 unpaired = 8 ✅.

3. (4s se pehle electrons nikaalo!):
   (a) **Mn²⁺:** [Ar] 3d⁵ → **5 unpaired**.
   (b) **Cu⁺:** [Ar] 3d¹⁰ → **0 unpaired** (4s¹ gaya).
   (c) **Cu²⁺:** [Ar] 3d⁹ → **1 unpaired** (4s¹ + 3d ka 1 gaya).
   (d) **Cr³⁺:** [Ar] 3d³ → **3 unpaired** (4s¹ + 3d ke 2 gaye).
   (e) **Co²⁺:** [Ar] 3d⁷ → **3 unpaired** (4s² gaya).
   Jaanch: Cr³⁺ = 18 + 3 = 21 = 24 − 3 ✅; Cu⁺ = 18 + 10 = 28 = 29 − 1 ✅.

4. 3d⁴ mein 4 parallel-spin electrons hote hain → exchange pairs = (4 × 3) ÷ 2 = **6**. 3d⁵ mein 5 parallel-spin electrons hote hain → exchange pairs = (5 × 4) ÷ 2 = **10**. Saath hi 3d⁵ poori tarah symmetrical (har box mein 1 electron) hai. Is extra symmetry aur 4 extra exchange pairs ki stability ke kaaran Cr [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s¹ chunta hai.

5. Hydrogen mein sirf 1 electron hai, isliye koi electron-electron repulsion ya shielding nahi hoti — energy sirf n par nirbhar karti hai (2s = 2p). Lithium ya Carbon mein 1s ke electrons bahar wale 2s aur 2p ko shield karte hain; 2s nucleus ke zyada paas penetrate karta hai, isliye uski energy 2p se kam (2s < 2p) ho jaati hai.

6. (a) **Hund's Rule** violated (doosra electron khaali 2pᵧ box mein jaana chahiye tha).
   (b) **Pauli Exclusion Principle** violated (ek hi orbital mein dono electrons ka spin same ↑↑ nahi ho sakta).
   (c) **Aufbau Principle** violated (2s poora bhare bina 2p mein electrons chala gaya).

7. Cu (Z = 29) = **1s² 2s² 2p⁶ 3s² 3p⁶ 3d¹⁰ 4s¹**.
   - **s-electrons:** 2 + 2 + 2 + 1 = **7**.
   - **p-electrons:** 6 + 6 = **12**.
   - **d-electrons:** **10**.
   Jaanch: 7 + 12 + 10 = 29 ✅.

8. **Fe³⁺ ([Ar] 3d⁵)** zyada stable hai (exactly half-filled 3d⁵ symmetry aur 10 exchange pairs ke kaaran) aur ismein **5 unpaired electrons** hain (Fe²⁺ ke 4 se zyada).

9. K (19) = [Ar] 4s¹ → 19vaan electron **4s** orbital mein hai: **n = 4, l = 0, mₗ = 0, mₛ = +½** (ya −½).
   Jaanch: 4s ke liye n = 4, l = 0, mₗ = 0 ✅.

10. (a) **True** ✅. Zn = [Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s² mein sabhi orbitals poore bhare (paired) hain.
    (b) **False** ❌. Electrons pehle bahari **4s** subshell se nikalte hain → Ti²⁺ = [Ar] 3d².
    (c) **True** ✅. (5 × 4) ÷ 2 = 10 exchange pairs.
    (d) **True** ✅. Tabhi unke chaaron quantum numbers alag-alag honge.

11. (a) **Cr³⁺ ([Ar] 3d³, n = 3):** μ = √(3 × 5) = √15 = **3.87 BM**.
    (b) **Ni²⁺ ([Ar] 3d⁸, n = 2):** μ = √(2 × 4) = √8 = **2.83 BM**.
    (c) **Cu⁺ ([Ar] 3d¹⁰, n = 0):** μ = **0 BM** (diamagnetic).
    Jaanch: 3.87² ≈ 15 ✅; 2.83² ≈ 8 ✅.

12. √(n(n + 2)) = 3.87 → n(n + 2) ≈ 15 → **n = 3 unpaired electrons**!
    Possible 3d configs for M²⁺ with 3 unpaired electrons: **[Ar] 3d³ (V²⁺)** ya **[Ar] 3d⁷ (Co²⁺)**!
    Jaanch: 3 × (3 + 2) = 15 → √15 = 3.87 BM ✅.
