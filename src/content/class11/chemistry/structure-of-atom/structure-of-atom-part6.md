## Part A: Poore Chapter Ka Quantum Master Map

```
   [ Bohr Transition ]  -->  ΔE = 13.6 Z² (1/n₁² − 1/n₂²) eV
           :
      (Photon gira)
           v
   [ Photoelectric ]    -->  KE_max = ΔE − W₀  (eV ko × 1.602×10⁻¹⁹ se Joules banao!)
           :
      v = √(2·KE / m_e)
           v
   [ de Broglie Wave ]  -->  λ = h ÷ (m_e × v)    (Heisenberg: Δx·Δv ≥ h / 4πm_e)
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Chapter 2 ke quantitative formulas ki master kadi: Bohr transition ki energy se photoelectric kinetic energy, usse speed v, aur usse electron ki de Broglie wavelength seedha judti hai.

## Part B: Anjor Ke Teen Multi-Concept Relay Desks (Solved Examples)

### Example 1 (Desk 1). Bohr Photon → Photoelectric Effect → de Broglie Wavelength

**Sawaal:** Hydrogen atom (Z = 1) mein ek electron **n = 3 se n = 1** par koodta hai. Isse nikla photon ek metal surface par girta hai jiska work function **W₀ = 4.09 eV** hai.
(a) Nikalne wale photoelectron ki maximum kinetic energy (eV aur Joules mein) nikaalo.
(b) Us tez electron ki speed v aur uski **de Broglie wavelength (λ)** pm mein nikaalo.

**Hal:**
- **Step 1 (Bohr ΔE):** E₃ = −13.6 ÷ 9 = −1.51 eV; E₁ = −13.60 eV.
  - ΔE = (−1.51) − (−13.60) = **12.09 eV**.
- **Step 2 (Photoelectric KE_max):**
  - KE_max = ΔE − W₀ = 12.09 − 4.09 = **8.00 eV**.
  - Joules mein: KE_max = 8.00 × 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ = **1.28 × 10⁻¹⁸ J**.
- **Step 3 (Speed v aur de Broglie λ):**
  - ½ m_e v² = KE_max → v = √(2 × 1.2816 × 10⁻¹⁸ ÷ 9.109 × 10⁻³¹) = √(2.814 × 10¹²) = **1.68 × 10⁶ m/s**.
  - λ = h ÷ (m_e × v) = (6.626 × 10⁻³⁴) ÷ (9.109 × 10⁻³¹ × 1.677 × 10⁶) = 4.34 × 10⁻¹⁰ m = **434 pm**.

**Jaanch:** ½ × (9.109 × 10⁻³¹) × (1.677 × 10⁶)² = 1.28 × 10⁻¹⁸ J = 8.00 eV ✅; λ = 434 pm atomic scale par hai ✅.

### Example 2 (Desk 2). Configuration + Unpaired Electrons + Paramagnetism + Nodes

**Sawaal:** Ek neutral element X ka configuration **[Ar] 3d⁶ 4s²** hai.
(a) X ka atomic number Z aur naam batao.
(b) X³⁺ ion ka configuration likho aur batao ye paramagnetic hai ya diamagnetic.
(c) Iske 3d orbital mein kitne radial aur kitne angular nodes hain?

**Hal:**
- (a) Kul electrons = 18 + 6 + 2 = **26** → **Iron (Fe)**.
- (b) **Fe³⁺** (4s se 2 aur 3d se 1 electron gaya): **[Ar] 3d⁵**. Ismein **5 unpaired electrons** hain, isliye ye strongly **paramagnetic** hai.
- (c) **3d orbital (n = 3, l = 2):** Radial nodes = n − l − 1 = 3 − 2 − 1 = **0**; Angular nodes = l = **2**; Total nodes = n − 1 = **2**.

**Jaanch:** 0 + 2 = 2 = 3 − 1 ✅; Fe³⁺ mein 18 + 5 = 23 electrons ✅.

---

### Example 3 (Desk 3). Li²⁺ Ion (Z = 3) ki Transition Energy aur Radius Ratio

**Sawaal:** Li²⁺ (Z = 3) ke liye:
(a) **n = 2 → n = 1** transition se nikalne wale photon ki energy (eV mein) nikaalo.
(b) Li²⁺ ke **n = 2** orbit ke radius aur Hydrogen ke **n = 1** orbit ke radius ka ratio r₂(Li²⁺) ÷ r₁(H) nikaalo.

**Hal:**
- (a) Li²⁺ ke liye Eₙ = −13.6 × (3² / n²) = −122.4 ÷ n² eV.
  - E₂ = −122.4 ÷ 4 = −30.60 eV; E₁ = −122.4 eV.
  - ΔE = (−30.60) − (−122.40) = **91.80 eV** (bahut tez UV/soft X-ray photon!).
- (b) rₙ = 52.9 × (n² / Z) pm.
  - r₂(Li²⁺) ÷ r₁(H) = (2² / 3) ÷ (1² / 1) = **4 / 3** (≈ 1.33).

**Jaanch:** H mein 2 → 1 ka ΔE = 10.20 eV hota hai; Li²⁺ (Z = 3) mein Z² = 9 guna → 9 × 10.20 = 91.80 eV ✅.

### Example 4 (Desk 4). Magnetic Moment + Orbital Angular Momentum + Accelerating Voltage

**Sawaal:**
(a) **Co²⁺ (Z = 27)** ka electronic configuration likho, uska **spin-only magnetic moment (μ)** BM mein nikaalo, aur uske 3d electron ka **orbital angular momentum (L)** h/(2π) mein batao.
(b) Ek electron ko **25 V** se accelerate kiya gaya — uski de Broglie wavelength Å mein nikaalo.

**Hal:**
- (a) Co (27) = [Ar] 3d⁷ 4s² → **Co²⁺ = [Ar] 3d⁷** (4s ke dono e⁻ gaye!).
  - 3d⁷ = [↑↓][↑↓][↑][↑][↑] → **n = 3 unpaired electrons** → **μ = √(3 × 5) = √15 = 3.87 BM**.
  - 3d orbital ke liye l = 2 → **L = √(2 × 3) × h/(2π) = √6 × h/(2π)**.
- (b) V = 25 V → √25 = 5 → **λ = 12.27 ÷ 5 = 2.454 Å (245.4 pm)**!

**Jaanch:** Co²⁺ mein 18 + 7 = 25 = 27 − 2 e⁻ ✅; 5 × 2.454 = 12.27 ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: v = √(2·KE ÷ m_e) mein KE ko eV mein hi rakh dena!**
   Example 1 mein agar tum 8.00 eV ko bina 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ se guna kiye seedha formula mein rakh do, to speed 10⁹ guna galat aa jaayegi!
   *Sahi baat:* SI unit formula (m_e kg mein, h J·s mein) mein energy hamesha **Joules (J)** mein honi chahiye: **1 eV = 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ J**.

2. **Galti 2: Multi-part sawaalon mein angular nodes (l) aur total nodes (n − 1) ko aapas mein badal dena.**
   4d orbital (n = 4, l = 2) mein total nodes = 3 hain, par angular nodes sirf **2** aur radial nodes **1** hain — hamesha pehle (n, l) side mein likho, phir teeno formula lagao!

## Quick Recap

- Multi-step atomic numericals mein hamesha SI units pakdo: **m (kg)**, **λ (m)**, **E (J = eV × 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹)**, **v (m/s)**.
- **Paramagnetic:** ≥ 1 unpaired electron; **Diamagnetic:** 0 unpaired electrons.
- **Bohr scaling (1-electron species):** ΔE ∝ Z² aur rₙ ∝ n² / Z.

## Practice Questions

1. A photon emitted during the **n = 2 → n = 1** transition of a hydrogen atom (ΔE = 10.20 eV) strikes a metal surface whose work function is **W₀ = 4.20 eV**. Calculate (a) the maximum kinetic energy of the ejected photoelectron in eV and Joules, (b) its speed v, and (c) its de Broglie wavelength in pm.
2. An electron has a kinetic energy of **1.50 eV**. Calculate its speed v (in m/s) and its de Broglie wavelength λ (in pm).
3. Calculate the photon energy (in eV) emitted when an electron in a **He⁺ ion (Z = 2)** jumps from **n = 4 to n = 2**. Compare this energy with the **n = 2 → n = 1** transition in a hydrogen atom.
4. If the position of an electron in the first Bohr orbit of hydrogen is known with an uncertainty equal to the Bohr radius itself (**Δx = 5.29 × 10⁻¹¹ m**), calculate the minimum uncertainty in its velocity (**Δv**) using Heisenberg's Uncertainty Principle.
5. For **Vanadium (V, Z = 23)**:
   (a) Write the electronic configurations of neutral **V** and the **V³⁺** ion using the [Ar] core.
   (b) State whether **V³⁺** is paramagnetic or diamagnetic, and give the number of unpaired electrons.
   (c) How many radial and angular nodes are present in one of its **3d** orbitals?
6. Write the complete set of four quantum numbers (n, l, mₗ, mₛ) possible for the **last (17th) electron** entering a neutral **Chlorine (Z = 17)** atom.
7. Show that **³⁹₁₉K** and **⁴⁰₂₀Ca** are **isotones**, and state whether **K⁺** and **Ca²⁺** are **isoelectronic** with each other.
8. Arrange the following species in **increasing number of unpaired electrons**: **Zn²⁺ (Z = 30), Cu²⁺ (Z = 29), Ni²⁺ (Z = 28), Co²⁺ (Z = 27), Mn²⁺ (Z = 25)**.
9. An orbital has **n = 4** and **2 angular nodes**. Identify the orbital subshell (such as 4s, 4p, 4d or 4f), calculate its **radial nodes**, and state the maximum number of electrons this subshell can hold.
10. A beam of light has wavenumber **ν̄ = 2.00 × 10⁶ m⁻¹**. Calculate its wavelength λ (in nm), frequency ν (in Hz), and the energy of **1 mole of photons** (in kJ/mol). *(Use Nₐ = 6.022 × 10²³ mol⁻¹)*
11. Which of the following quantum-number sets is **illegal**, and why?
    (i) (3, 2, +2, −½)   (ii) (4, 0, 0, +½)   (iii) (2, 1, −2, +½)   (iv) (5, 3, −1, −½)
12. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) Cu⁺ ([Ar] 3d¹⁰) is diamagnetic because it has zero unpaired electrons.
    (b) In a hydrogen atom, the 3s, 3p and 3d orbitals all have the same energy.
    (c) For the He⁺ ion, the radius of the n = 2 orbit is equal to the radius of the n = 1 orbit of hydrogen.
    (d) An electron with larger kinetic energy has a longer de Broglie wavelength.

### Answers

1. (a) KE_max = 10.20 − 4.20 = **6.00 eV** = 6.00 × 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ = **9.61 × 10⁻¹⁹ J**.
   (b) v = √(2 × 9.612 × 10⁻¹⁹ ÷ 9.109 × 10⁻³¹) = √(2.110 × 10¹²) = **1.45 × 10⁶ m/s**.
   (c) λ = (6.626 × 10⁻³⁴) ÷ (9.109 × 10⁻³¹ × 1.453 × 10⁶) = 5.01 × 10⁻¹⁰ m = **501 pm**.
   Jaanch: ½ × 9.109 × 10⁻³¹ × (1.453 × 10⁶)² = 9.61 × 10⁻¹⁹ J = 6.00 eV ✅.

2. KE = 1.50 × 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ = **2.40 × 10⁻¹⁹ J**.
   v = √(2 × 2.403 × 10⁻¹⁹ ÷ 9.109 × 10⁻³¹) = √(5.276 × 10¹¹) = **7.26 × 10⁵ m/s**.
   λ = (6.626 × 10⁻³⁴) ÷ (9.109 × 10⁻³¹ × 7.264 × 10⁵) = 1.001 × 10⁻⁹ m = **1001 pm** (1.00 nm).
   Jaanch: Q1 mein KE 6.00 eV (4×) par λ = 501 pm tha; KE 1/4 hone par v aadha aur λ dugna (~1001 pm) hua ✅.

3. He⁺ (Z = 2): ΔE = 13.6 × 2² × (1/2² − 1/4²) = 54.4 × (1/4 − 1/16) = 54.4 × (3/16) = **10.20 eV**.
   Ye Hydrogen ke n = 2 → n = 1 transition (10.20 eV) ke **bilkul barabar** hai!
   Jaanch: 2² × (3/16) = 3/4 = (1/1² − 1/2²) ✅.

4. Δv ≥ (5.273 × 10⁻³⁵) ÷ (9.109 × 10⁻³¹ × 5.29 × 10⁻¹¹) = (5.273 × 10⁻³⁵) ÷ (4.819 × 10⁻⁴¹) = **1.09 × 10⁶ m/s**.
   Jaanch: 1.094 × 10⁶ × 4.819 × 10⁻⁴¹ = 5.27 × 10⁻³⁵ = h/(4π) ✅.

5. (a) **V (23):** [Ar] 3d³ 4s²; **V³⁺:** [Ar] 3d² (4s se 2 aur 3d se 1 electron gaya).
   (b) V³⁺ mein **2 unpaired electrons** hain → **Paramagnetic**.
   (c) 3d (n = 3, l = 2): Radial nodes = 3 − 2 − 1 = **0**; Angular nodes = **2**.
   Jaanch: V³⁺ electrons = 18 + 2 = 20 = 23 − 3 ✅.

6. Cl (Z = 17) = [Ne] 3s² 3p⁵ → 17vaan electron **3p** orbital mein hai:
   **n = 3, l = 1**, mₗ = **−1, 0 ya +1** (koi ek), mₛ = **−½** (ya +½).
   Jaanch: 3p ke liye n = 3, l = 1 ✅.

7. Neutrons: ³⁹₁₉K = 39 − 19 = **20**; ⁴⁰₂₀Ca = 40 − 20 = **20** → dono **isotones** hain.
   Electrons: K⁺ = 19 − 1 = **18**; Ca²⁺ = 20 − 2 = **18** → haan, dono **isoelectronic** hain!
   Jaanch: Dono mein 20 neutrons aur dono ions mein 18 electrons ✅.

8. Unpaired electrons: **Zn²⁺ ([Ar] 3d¹⁰: 0) < Cu²⁺ ([Ar] 3d⁹: 1) < Ni²⁺ ([Ar] 3d⁸: 2) < Co²⁺ ([Ar] 3d⁷: 3) < Mn²⁺ ([Ar] 3d⁵: 5)**.
   Jaanch: 0 < 1 < 2 < 3 < 5 ✅.

9. Angular nodes = l = **2** aur n = **4** → Subshell = **4d**.
   Radial nodes = n − l − 1 = 4 − 2 − 1 = **1** (total nodes = 3).
   4d subshell mein 5 orbitals × 2 = **10 electrons** max aa sakte hain.
   Jaanch: 1 + 2 = 3 = 4 − 1 ✅.

10. λ = 1 ÷ ν̄ = 1 ÷ (2.00 × 10⁶) = 5.00 × 10⁻⁷ m = **500 nm**.
    ν = c × ν̄ = (3.00 × 10⁸) × (2.00 × 10⁶) = **6.00 × 10¹⁴ Hz**.
    1 photon ki energy = hν = 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ × 6.00 × 10¹⁴ = 3.976 × 10⁻¹⁹ J.
    1 mole photons ki energy = (3.976 × 10⁻¹⁹) × (6.022 × 10²³) = 2.394 × 10⁵ J/mol = **239 kJ/mol**.
    Jaanch: 500 nm × 6.00 × 10¹⁴ Hz = 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s ✅.

11. **(iii) (2, 1, −2, +½) illegal hai!** Kyunki l = 1 (p-orbital) ke liye mₗ sirf −1, 0, +1 ho sakta hai; −2 allowed range (−l se +l) se bahar hai.

12. (a) **True** ✅. 3d¹⁰ mein sabhi 10 electrons paired hain.
    (b) **True** ✅. 1-electron H atom mein energy sirf n par nirbhar karti hai.
    (c) **False** ❌. r₂(He⁺) = 52.9 × (2² / 2) = 2 × 52.9 pm = **2 r₁(H)** (barabar nahi, dugna!).
    (d) **False** ❌. Zyada KE → zyada speed v → λ = h/(mv) **chhota (shorter)** hoga.
