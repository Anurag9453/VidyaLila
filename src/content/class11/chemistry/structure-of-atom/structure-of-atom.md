## Part A: Electron, Proton aur Neutron — Class 11 Ke Exact Numbers

### 1. Thomson ka e/m Prayog (1897) aur Millikan ka Oil-Drop Prayog (1909) *(dono high-voltage lab prayog: sirf padhne ke liye)*
- **Thomson ne kya naapa:** Cathode ray tube mein ek saath electric field aur magnetic field lagakar unhone dekha ki deflection teen cheezon par nirbhar karta hai: particle ka charge (zyada charge → zyada mudna), mass (halka particle → zyada mudna), aur fields ki taakat. Dono fields ko balance karke unhone naapa:
  **e / m_e = 1.7588 × 10¹¹ C/kg**
  Sabse hairan karne wali baat: tube mein chahe koi bhi gas bharo ya kisi bhi metal ka electrode lagao, **cathode rays ka e/m hamesha wahi aata tha!** Iska saboot tha ki **electrons har tarah ke matter ka common hissa hain**.
- **Millikan ka Oil-Drop Prayog:** R. A. Millikan ne tel ki nanhi boondon par X-rays se charge daala aur electric field se unhein hawa mein rok kar har boond par kul charge q naapa. Har baar q ek hi chhoti sankhya ka poora-ank gunaj (q = n × e) nikla:
  **e = 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C** *(electron par −1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C)*
- **Electron ka mass (do numbers ko bhaag dekar!):**
  m_e = e ÷ (e / m_e) = (1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C) ÷ (1.7588 × 10¹¹ C/kg) = **9.109 × 10⁻³¹ kg** (lagbhag 9.11 × 10⁻³¹ kg).

### 2. Proton (Canal Rays) aur Neutron (Chadwick, 1932)
- **Canal rays (positive rays) ka khas farq:** Cathode rays ke ulta, **canal rays ka e/m ratio tube mein bhari gas ke saath BADAL jaata hai!** Kyun? Kyunki positive ions us bhari hui gas ke atoms se bante hain (jaise He⁺, O⁺, H⁺). Sabse zyada e/m (aur sabse halka positive ion) **hydrogen gas** se mila — yahi **proton** hai (m_p = 1.673 × 10⁻²⁷ kg, lagbhag 1836 guna electron se bhaari).
- **Chadwick ka Neutron (1932):** Beryllium ki patli sheet par radioactive source se alpha particles (⁴He²⁺) maarne par ek bina-charge wala bhaari kan nikla *(sirf padhne ke liye; alpha aur neutron radiation khatarnak hain)*:
  **⁹₄Be + ⁴₂He → ¹²₆C + ¹₀n**
  *(Mass number check: 9 + 4 = 12 + 1 = 13; Charge check: 4 + 2 = 6 + 0 = 6.)*
  Neutron ka mass m_n = **1.675 × 10⁻²⁷ kg** (proton se mamuli sa bhaari, charge = 0).

### Cathode Rays banaam Anode (Canal) Rays — Ek Nazar Mein

| Gun (Property) | Cathode Rays (Electrons) | Anode / Canal Rays (Positive Ions) |
|---|---|---|
| **Utpatti (Origin)** | Cathode se nikalkar anode ki taraf chalti hain | Discharge tube mein bhari **gas ke atoms ke ionisation** se banti hain |
| **Charge** | Negative (−e) | Positive (+q, gas ke ion par nirbhar) |
| **e/m Ratio** | Hamesha **constant** (1.7588 × 10¹¹ C/kg) — gas par nirbhar NAHI | **Gas badalne par BADAL jaata hai** (H₂ ke liye sabse zyada!) |
| **Magnetic/Electric Field** | Positive plate ki taraf mudti hain | Negative plate ki taraf mudti hain |

### Nuclear Radius ka Empirical Formula (Class 11 Extra Insight)
Nucleus ka size uske mass number **A** (kul protons + neutrons) par nirbhar karta hai:
> **R = R₀ × A^(1/3)** *(jahan R₀ ≈ **1.2 × 10⁻¹⁵ m** = 1.2 fm)*
*(Matlab: nucleus ka volume ∝ R³ ∝ A — yaani sabhi nuclei ki **density lagbhag ek hi** hoti hai, ~2 × 10¹⁷ kg/m³!)*

## Part B: Atomic Models (Thomson & Rutherford) Ki Class 11 Seemayein

Thomson ke plum-pudding model aur Rutherford ke gold-foil prayog ka basic roop tum Class 9 ([structure-of-the-atom-part2.md](../../../class9/chemistry/structure-of-the-atom/structure-of-the-atom-part2.md)) mein padh chuke ho. Yahan hum **exact scale** aur **fail hone ke asli physics kaaran** dekhte hain:

1. **Atom banaam Nucleus ka Scale:**
   - Atom ka radius ≈ **10⁻¹⁰ m** (1 Å = 100 pm).
   - Nucleus ka radius ≈ **10⁻¹⁵ m** (1 fm).
   - Radius ka ratio = 10⁻¹⁰ ÷ 10⁻¹⁵ = **10⁵** (ek laakh guna!).
   - **Volume ka ratio** = (10⁵)³ = **10¹⁵**! Yaani atom ka 99.9999999999999% hissa khaali jagah hai!

2. **Rutherford ke Model ki Do Badi Seemayein (Class 11 Core):**
   - **Seema 1 (Atom ki Stability — Maxwell ka Electromagnetic Niyam):** James Clerk Maxwell ke niyam ke mutabik jab bhi koi **charged particle (jaise electron) accelerate hota hai** (gol ghoomne mein disha har pal badalti hai, isliye centripetal acceleration hota hai!), to use lagatar **electromagnetic radiation (energy) chhodni chahiye**. Energy khote-khote electron ka ghera chhota hota jaayega aur calculation batati hai ki electron sirf **lagbhag 10⁻⁸ second** mein spiral banakar nucleus mein gir jaayega! Par asli atom to bilkul stable hote hain — Rutherford iska jawab nahi de paaye.
   - **Seema 2 (Electronic Structure aur Line Spectra):** Rutherford ye nahi bata paaye ki electrons nucleus ke chaaron taraf kaise bate hain, aur garam hydrogen gas sirf kuch khaas rangon ki patli lakeerein (**line spectrum**) kyun chhodti hai, lagatar indradhanush kyun nahi!

```
   Rutherford ke model par Maxwell ka niyam lagane par:

              . - - .
           .'   e⁻    '.
          :    /   \    :      (Accelerating e⁻ energy khota jaata hai)
          :   : (+) :   :  --> Spiral banakar ~10⁻⁸ s mein nucleus mein girna chahiye!
          :    \   /    :      Par asli atom STABLE hai --> Naya model chahiye!
           '.  - -   .'
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Classical electromagnetic theory ke anusar gol ghoomta hua electron lagatar energy khokar ~10⁻⁸ second mein nucleus mein gir jaana chahiye tha — yahi Rutherford ke model ki sabse badi kami thi.

## Part C: Isotones aur Isoelectronic Species + Solved Examples

Class 9 ([structure-of-the-atom-part4.md](../../../class9/chemistry/structure-of-the-atom/structure-of-the-atom-part4.md) aur [structure-of-the-atom-part6.md](../../../class9/chemistry/structure-of-the-atom/structure-of-the-atom-part6.md)) mein tumne Z, A, isotopes (same Z) aur isobars (same A) seekhe the. Class 11 mein do naye parivaar judte hain:
- **Isotones:** Alag tatva, par **same number of neutrons (N = A − Z)**.
- **Isoelectronic species:** Alag atoms/ions, par **same total electrons**.

### Example 1. Millikan ke Oil-Drop par Electrons ki Ginti

**Sawaal:** Millikan ke prayog mein ek tel ki boond par kul charge **q = −4.806 × 10⁻¹⁹ C** naapa gaya. Is boond par kitne extra electrons baithe hain?

**Hal:**
- Quantisation of charge: q = n × (−e), jahan e = 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C.
- n = (−4.806 × 10⁻¹⁹) ÷ (−1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹) = **3**.
- Boond par theek **3 electrons** hain.

**Jaanch:** 3 × (−1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C) = −4.806 × 10⁻¹⁹ C ✅.

---

### Example 2. Isotones aur Isoelectronic Species ki Pehchaan

**Sawaal:**
(a) Dikhao ki **¹⁴₆C, ¹⁵₇N aur ¹⁶₈O** isotones hain.
(b) Dikhao ki **N³⁻, O²⁻, F⁻, Na⁺ aur Mg²⁺** ek isoelectronic series banate hain.

**Hal:**
- (a) Neutrons N = A − Z:
  - ¹⁴₆C: 14 − 6 = **8**; ¹⁵₇N: 15 − 7 = **8**; ¹⁶₈O: 16 − 8 = **8**. Teeno mein 8 neutrons hain → **Isotones**!
- (b) Electrons (negative charge jodo, positive charge ghatao):
  - N³⁻ (Z = 7): 7 + 3 = **10**; O²⁻ (Z = 8): 8 + 2 = **10**; F⁻ (Z = 9): 9 + 1 = **10**; Na⁺ (Z = 11): 11 − 1 = **10**; Mg²⁺ (Z = 12): 12 − 2 = **10**. Sabmein 10 electrons → **Isoelectronic**!

**Jaanch:** Har species mein protons (Z) alag hain (7, 8, 9, 11, 12), par kul electrons sabmein 10 hain ✅.

---

### Example 3. 1 Mole Electrons ka Mass aur Charge + Nuclear Radius Ratio

**Sawaal:**
(a) **1 mole electrons** (6.022 × 10²³ electrons) ka kul mass (mg mein) aur kul charge (Coulomb mein — jise **1 Faraday** kehte hain!) nikaalo.
(b) **²⁷₁₃Al** aur **⁶⁴₂₉Cu** ke nuclei ke radii ka ratio (R_Al ÷ R_Cu) nikaalo.

**Hal:**
- (a) **Mass of 1 mol e⁻** = 6.022 × 10²³ × 9.109 × 10⁻³¹ kg = **5.485 × 10⁻⁷ kg = 0.549 mg** (ek chawal ke daane se bhi halka!).
  - **Charge of 1 mol e⁻** = 6.022 × 10²³ × 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C = **96472 C ≈ 96500 C (1 Faraday, F)**!
- (b) R ∝ A^(1/3) → R_Al ÷ R_Cu = (27)^(1/3) ÷ (64)^(1/3) = **3 ÷ 4 = 0.75**!

**Jaanch:** 3³ = 27 aur 4³ = 64 → ratio exact 3 : 4 ✅; 96472 C ≈ 96500 C (1 F) ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: Ye soch lena ki canal rays (anode rays) ka e/m bhi cathode rays ki tarah har gas ke liye constant hota hai.**
   Cathode rays sirf electrons se bani hain, isliye unka e/m (1.7588 × 10¹¹ C/kg) har gas mein same hai. Par **canal rays** gas ke bache hue positive ions (H⁺, He⁺, Ne⁺) se banti hain jinka mass alag-alag hota hai — isliye canal rays ka e/m gas badalne par badal jaata hai!

2. **Galti 2: Isotopes, Isobars aur Isotones ke aksharon mein gadbadi.**
   Aasaan yaad karne ki trick: Iso**p**es → same **p**rotons (Z); Iso**b**ars → same **b**haar / mass number (A); Iso**n**es → same **n**eutrons (A − Z).

3. **Galti 3: Positive ion (cation) mein electrons jodne ki galti.**
   Mg²⁺ (Z = 12) mein ek student ne "+2 dekh kar 12 + 2 = 14 electrons" likh diya!
   *Sahi baat:* Positive charge (+) tab aata hai jab negative electrons **bahar nikal jaayein**. Isliye Mg²⁺ mein 12 − 2 = **10 electrons** hain!

## Quick Recap

- **Thomson (1897):** e / m_e = 1.7588 × 10¹¹ C/kg (gas aur electrode se aazaad).
- **Millikan (1909):** e = 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C (q = n × e) → m_e = 9.109 × 10⁻³¹ kg.
- **Canal rays:** e/m gas par nirbhar; sabse halka positive ion = proton (H⁺, 1.673 × 10⁻²⁷ kg).
- **Chadwick (1932):** ⁹₄Be + ⁴₂He → ¹²₆C + ¹₀n (neutron, 1.675 × 10⁻²⁷ kg).
- **Rutherford ki seemayein:** Maxwell ke niyam se electron ko ~10⁻⁸ s mein nucleus mein girna chahiye tha; line spectra aur electronic arrangement nahi samjha paaye.
- **Isotones:** same neutrons (A − Z). **Isoelectronic:** same total electrons.

## Practice Questions

1. In Millikan's oil-drop experiment, a drop carries a total charge of **−8.01 × 10⁻¹⁹ C**. How many electrons are attached to this drop? Could a drop ever carry a charge of **−2.403 × 10⁻¹⁹ C**? Explain why or why not.
2. Using Millikan's value e = 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C and Thomson's value e/m_e = 1.7588 × 10¹¹ C/kg, show step by step how the mass of an electron (m_e) is calculated.
3. Why does the charge-to-mass ratio (e/m) of **cathode rays** remain identical regardless of the gas in the discharge tube, whereas the e/m of **anode (canal) rays** changes when the gas is changed?
4. Arrange the following particles in **increasing order of their e/m (magnitude of charge-to-mass ratio)**: neutron (n), alpha particle (⁴He²⁺), proton (H⁺), and electron (e⁻). Give a brief reason.
5. Calculate the number of protons, neutrons and electrons in each of the following species: (a) **⁵⁶₂₆Fe³⁺**, (b) **³¹₁₅P³⁻**, (c) **⁸⁰₃₅Br⁻**.
6. Show that **³⁰₁₄Si, ³¹₁₅P and ³²₁₆S** form a set of **isotones**.
7. Identify which of the following species are **isoelectronic** with Argon (₁₈Ar): **S²⁻ (Z = 16), Cl⁻ (Z = 17), K⁺ (Z = 19), Ca²⁺ (Z = 20), Sc³⁺ (Z = 21), Mg²⁺ (Z = 12)**.
8. Write the balanced nuclear equation for James Chadwick's discovery of the neutron (1932) and verify that both the total mass number and the total atomic number are conserved.
9. Explain in your own words why **Maxwell's electromagnetic theory** posed a fatal problem for Rutherford's nuclear model of the atom.
10. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) The volume of an atom is roughly 10¹⁵ times larger than the volume of its nucleus.
    (b) Two species with the same mass number A are called isotones.
    (c) A neutral atom and its cation always have the same number of protons.
    (d) Thomson's plum-pudding model could explain the 180° back-scattering of alpha particles observed by Rutherford.
11. Calculate the number of **electrons** that together weigh exactly **1.00 gram** (m_e = 9.109 × 10⁻³¹ kg).
12. Using **R = R₀ A^(1/3)** (R₀ = 1.2 × 10⁻¹⁵ m), calculate the nuclear radius of **¹²⁵₅₂Te** in femtometres (1 fm = 10⁻¹⁵ m) and metres.

### Answers

1. n = (−8.01 × 10⁻¹⁹) ÷ (−1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹) = **5 electrons**.
   −2.403 × 10⁻¹⁹ C ke liye n = 2.403 ÷ 1.602 = **1.5** — ye **asambhav** hai kyunki charge quantised hota hai (sirf poora-ank n = 1, 2, 3... ho sakta hai, aadha electron nahi!).
   Jaanch: 5 × 1.602 = 8.01 ✅; 1.5 × 1.602 = 2.403 ✅.

2. m_e = e ÷ (e / m_e) = (1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C) ÷ (1.7588 × 10¹¹ C/kg) = (1.602 ÷ 1.7588) × 10⁻³⁰ kg = 0.91085 × 10⁻³⁰ kg = **9.109 × 10⁻³¹ kg**.
   Jaanch: 9.109 × 10⁻³¹ × 1.7588 × 10¹¹ = 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ C ✅.

3. Cathode rays sabhi gases ke atoms se nikale gaye **electrons** se bani hoti hain, aur har tatva ke electron ka charge aur mass bilkul same hota hai. Anode (canal) rays gas ke bache hue **positive ions** se banti hain — alag gases ke ions ka mass alag hota hai, isliye unka e/m gas ke saath badal jaata hai.

4. Badhta kram (increasing e/m): **n (0) < ⁴He²⁺ (2/4 = 0.5 unit) < H⁺ (1/1 = 1 unit) < e⁻ (sabse halka mass, isliye e/m sabse bada, ~1836× proton se)**.
   Jaanch: Neutron ka charge 0 hai to e/m = 0; electron ka mass sabse chhota hai to e/m sabse bada ✅.

5. (a) **⁵⁶₂₆Fe³⁺:** protons = 26, neutrons = 56 − 26 = 30, electrons = 26 − 3 = **23**.
   (b) **³¹₁₅P³⁻:** protons = 15, neutrons = 31 − 15 = 16, electrons = 15 + 3 = **18**.
   (c) **⁸⁰₃₅Br⁻:** protons = 35, neutrons = 80 − 35 = 45, electrons = 35 + 1 = **36**.
   Jaanch: Charge = protons − electrons → Fe³⁺ (26 − 23 = +3), P³⁻ (15 − 18 = −3), Br⁻ (35 − 36 = −1) ✅.

6. Neutrons (A − Z): ³⁰₁₄Si = 30 − 14 = **16**; ³¹₁₅P = 31 − 15 = **16**; ³²₁₆S = 32 − 16 = **16**. Teeno mein 16 neutrons hain, isliye ye **isotones** hain.
   Jaanch: 14 + 16 = 30, 15 + 16 = 31, 16 + 16 = 32 ✅.

7. Ar mein **18 electrons** hain. S²⁻ (16 + 2 = 18), Cl⁻ (17 + 1 = 18), K⁺ (19 − 1 = 18), Ca²⁺ (20 − 2 = 18), Sc³⁺ (21 − 3 = 18) — ye paanchon Ar ke saath **isoelectronic** hain. Mg²⁺ mein sirf 10 electrons hain, isliye ye isoelectronic nahi hai.

8. **⁹₄Be + ⁴₂He → ¹²₆C + ¹₀n**.
   Jaanch: Mass number (upar): 9 + 4 = 13 = 12 + 1 ✅. Atomic number (neeche): 4 + 2 = 6 = 6 + 0 ✅.

9. Maxwell ke niyam ke anusar koi bhi accelerated charged particle (jaise nucleus ke chaaron taraf gol ghoomta electron) lagatar electromagnetic radiation ke roop mein energy khota hai. Aisa hone par electron ka orbit sikud kar ~10⁻⁸ second mein nucleus mein gir jaana chahiye tha — par asli atoms sthir (stable) hote hain.

10. (a) **True** ✅. Radius ratio 10⁵ hai, isliye volume ratio (10⁵)³ = 10¹⁵.
    (b) **False** ❌. Same mass number A wale species **isobars** kehlate hain (isotones mein neutrons A − Z same hote hain).
    (c) **True** ✅. Cation banne mein sirf electrons nikalte hain; nucleus ke protons (Z) nahi badalte.
    (d) **False** ❌. Thomson ke model mein positive charge poore atom mein faila tha, isliye heavy alpha particles 180° wapas nahi mud sakte the.

11. 1.00 g = 1.00 × 10⁻³ kg.
    Electrons ki ginti = (1.00 × 10⁻³ kg) ÷ (9.109 × 10⁻³¹ kg) = **1.098 × 10²⁷ electrons**!
    Jaanch: 1.098 × 10²⁷ × 9.109 × 10⁻³¹ = 1.00 × 10⁻³ kg = 1.00 g ✅.

12. A = 125 → (125)^(1/3) = **5**.
    R = 1.2 × 5 = **6.0 fm = 6.0 × 10⁻¹⁵ m**.
    Jaanch: 5³ = 125; 1.2 × 5 = 6.0 fm ✅.
