# Topic 4: Spontaneity, Entropy (S), Second Law aur Third Law of Thermodynamics

First Law kehta hai ki energy conserved rehti hai — par First Law is baat par koi rok nahi lagaata ki ek thandi chai ka cup table ki hawa se heat lekar khud-ba-khud ubalne lage! Phir bhi aisa kabhi nahi hota! Aur pehle scientists sochte the ki sirf **exothermic (ΔH < 0)** processes hi apne aap (**spontaneous**) hote hain — par **barf ka pighalna (ΔH > 0)** aur **NH₄NO₃ ka paani mein ghulna (ΔH > 0, thanda lagta hai!)** to endothermic hokar bhi **apne aap** ho jaate hain! Aakhir prakriti mein kisi badlaav ki **asli disha** kaun tay karta hai? Jawab hai — **Entropy (S) aur Second Law**!

| **Spontaneous process** | Wo badlaav jo **apne aap** (ya chhoti shuruaati chingari ke baad lagatar bina bahari zor ke) ek khaas disha mein chale; ye **irreversible** hota hai | Paani ka upar se neeche girna, ink ka phailna, lohe mein jung lagna |

| **Third Law** | **Absolute zero (0 K)** par kisi bhi **perfectly crystalline pure solid** ki entropy bilkul **ZERO (S = 0)** hoti hai | 0 K par sabhi particles bilkul jam kar ek perfect order mein rehte hain |

## Part A: Entropy (ΔS) ka Sign Aankh Dekhkar Kaise Pehchanein?

Bina kisi formula ke tum kisi bhi process mein **ΔS positive (+)** hoga ya **negative (−)**, in **4 golden clues** se turant bata sakte ho:
1. **State badalna:** Solid → Liquid → Gas mein randomness zabardast badhti hai (**ΔS > 0**); Gas → Liquid → Solid mein ghatti hai (**ΔS < 0**).
2. **Gas ke moles (Δn_g):** Agar reaction mein **gaseous products > gaseous reactants (Δn_g > 0)** → **ΔS > 0**! Agar **Δn_g < 0** → **ΔS < 0**!
3. **Temperature ya Volume badhna:** Garam karne par particles tez daudte hain → **ΔS > 0**; gas phailne par zyada jagah milti hai → **ΔS > 0**.
4. **Dissolving / Mixing:** Solid (jaise namak/chini) paani mein ghulne par azaad ions/molecules bante hain → **ΔS > 0**!

```
   SOLID (kam S)          LIQUID (beech ka S)         GAS (bahut ZYADA S!)
   +-----------+            +-----------+             +-----------+
   : o o o o o :  Melting   :  o   o  o : Vaporisation: o       o :
   : o o o o o : ---------> : o  o   o  : ----------> :    o      :
   : o o o o o :  (ΔS > 0)  :   o  o  o :   (ΔS > 0)  :  o     o  :
   +-----------+            +-----------+             +-----------+
   Perfect order            Thoda azaad               Poori azaadi!
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Solid se Liquid aur Liquid se Gas banne par particles ki azaadi aur randomness (Entropy S) lagatar badhti jaati hai: S(solid) < S(liquid) << S(gas).

### Example 1. Teen Reactions mein ΔS ka Sign Pehchanna

1. **H₂O(l) → H₂O(g):** Liquid se gas ban rahi hai → randomness bahut badhi → **ΔS > 0 (+)**.
2. **CaCO₃(s) → CaO(s) + CO₂(g):** Left mein 0 mol gas, right mein **1 mol CO₂ gas** (Δn_g = +1) → **ΔS > 0 (+)**.
3. **N₂(g) + 3H₂(g) → 2NH₃(g):** 4 mol gas ghatkar sirf **2 mol gas** rah gayi (Δn_g = −2) → **ΔS < 0 (−)**.

**Jaanch:** Jahan gas bani ya badhi wahan ΔS > 0; jahan gas ke moles ghate wahan ΔS < 0 ✅.

## Part B: Entropy Formula (ΔS = q_rev / T) aur Second Law

1. **Isothermal reversible process:** **ΔS = q_rev / T** *(T hamesha **Kelvin** mein!)*
2. **Phase transitions (constant T aur P par):**
   - Melting (fusion): **Δ_fusS = Δ_fusH / T_m**
   - Boiling (vaporisation): **Δ_vapS = Δ_vapH / T_b**
3. **Second Law of Thermodynamics (Universe ka Asli Niyam!):**
   > **ΔS_total = ΔS_system + ΔS_surroundings**
   - **ΔS_total > 0** → Process **Spontaneous** hai!
   - **ΔS_total = 0** → System **Equilibrium** par hai!
   - **ΔS_total < 0** → Process **Non-spontaneous** hai (ulta process spontaneous hoga!).
   - *Surroundings ki entropy:* **ΔS_surr = −ΔH_sys / T** *(agar system exothermic hai, ΔH_sys < 0, to heat bahar jaakar surroundings ki randomness badhati hai → ΔS_surr > 0!)*.

### Example 2. Paani ke Vaporisation ki Entropy (Δ_vapS) Nikaalna

**Sawaal:** Paani ka boiling point **373 K (100 °C)** hai aur uski enthalpy of vaporisation **Δ_vapH = 40.66 kJ mol⁻¹** hai. **Δ_vapS** (J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹ mein) nikalo.

**Hal:**
- Pehle kJ ko J mein badlo: Δ_vapH = 40.66 × 1000 = **40660 J mol⁻¹**.
- T_b = **373 K**.
- **Δ_vapS = Δ_vapH / T_b = 40660 / 373 = +109.01 J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹**!

**Jaanch:** 109.01 × 373 = 40660.7 J ≈ 40.66 kJ/mol, aur liquid → gas mein ΔS positive (+) aaya ✅.

### Ideal Gas ka Isothermal Entropy Change aur Trouton's Rule

1. **Ideal Gas Expansion/Compression (Constant T):** **ΔS = 2.303 n R log(V₂ / V₁) = 2.303 n R log(P₁ / P₂)**. *(Volume badhne par molecules ko zyada jagah milti hai → ΔS > 0!)*
2. **Trouton's Rule:** Aam non-H-bonded liquids (jaise acetone, benzene) ke liye boiling point par **Δ_vapS ≈ 88 J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹** hota hai! Paani (109 J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹) isse zyada kyun hai? Kyunki liquid paani H-bonding ke kaaran zyada ordered hota hai, isliye bhaap banne par disorder ka jump bada hota hai!

### Example 3. Ideal Gas ke Isothermal Expansion mein ΔS

**Sawaal:** **1.0 mol** ideal gas **300 K** par **10 L se 100 L** tak phailti hai. **ΔS** (J K⁻¹ mein) nikaalo. *(R = 8.314 J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹, log 10 = 1.)*

**Hal:**
- ΔS = 2.303 × 1 × 8.314 × log(100 / 10) = 2.303 × 8.314 × 1 = **+19.15 J K⁻¹**!

**Jaanch:** Volume 10 guna badha → ΔS positive (+19.15 J/K) ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galti 1: Ye maan lena ki sirf Exothermic (ΔH < 0) processes hi spontaneous hote hain.**
   *Sahi baat:* Barf ka 25 °C par pighalna aur paani ka bhaap banna **endothermic (ΔH > 0)** hain, phir bhi spontaneous hain kyunki unmein **entropy (ΔS > 0)** bahut badhti hai!

2. **Galti 2: Ye sochna ki spontaneous hone ke liye ΔS_system ka hamesha positive hona zaroori hai.**
   Nahi! Asli shart **ΔS_total = ΔS_sys + ΔS_surr > 0** hai! Misal ke liye, −10 °C par paani ka jamkar barf banna (ΔS_sys < 0) spontaneous hai kyunki jamne se jo heat bahar nikalti hai, wo surroundings ki entropy itni zyada badha deti hai ki **ΔS_total > 0** ho jaata hai!

3. **Galti 3: ΔS = q_rev / T mein temperature ko °C mein rakh dena.**
   Hamesha **Kelvin (K = °C + 273)** use karo! (Agar 0 °C rakh doge to 0 se divide karne par infinity aa jaayegi!)

## Quick Recap

- **Spontaneous process:** Bina lagatar bahari zor ke apne aap ek disha mein chalta hai (irreversible).
- **Entropy (S):** Randomness ka naap (**S_gas >> S_liquid > S_solid**); state function; unit **J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹**.
- **ΔS = q_rev / T** (T in Kelvin); **Δ_vapS = Δ_vapH / T_b**; **ΔS_surr = −ΔH_sys / T**.
- **Second Law:** Spontaneous hone ke liye **ΔS_total = ΔS_sys + ΔS_surr > 0**.
- **Third Law:** 0 K par perfect crystal ki entropy **S = 0**.

## Practice Questions

1. Predict whether **ΔS** is **positive (+)** or **negative (−)** for each of the following processes, giving a one-line reason:
   (a) Sublimation of solid iodine: **I₂(s) → I₂(g)**
   (b) Freezing of liquid water into ice: **H₂O(l) → H₂O(s)**
   (c) **2SO₂(g) + O₂(g) → 2SO₃(g)**
   (d) Dissolving common salt **NaCl(s)** in water
   (e) Cooling a fixed volume of nitrogen gas from 400 K to 250 K
   (f) Dissociation of hydrogen gas into atoms: **H₂(g) → 2H(g)**
2. Why can an **endothermic process** (like the melting of ice at room temperature or dissolving NH₄NO₃ in water) still be **spontaneous**?
3. Calculate the **entropy of fusion (Δ_fusS)** of ice at its melting point **0 °C (273 K)**, given **Δ_fusH = 6.00 kJ mol⁻¹**. Express your answer in **J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹**.
4. An exothermic chemical reaction releases **59.6 kJ** of heat to the surroundings at a constant temperature of **298 K**. Calculate **ΔS_surroundings** in **J K⁻¹**.
5. For a certain reaction at 300 K, **ΔS_system = −80 J K⁻¹** and **ΔS_surroundings = +120 J K⁻¹**. Calculate **ΔS_total** and state whether the reaction is **spontaneous** or **non-spontaneous**.
6. Explain why **2 mol of H(g) atoms** have a higher entropy than **1 mol of H₂(g) molecules** at the same temperature and pressure.
7. State the **Third Law of Thermodynamics**. Why is this law useful for calculating the **absolute entropy (S°)** of a substance, whereas we can only measure *changes* (ΔU, ΔH) in internal energy and enthalpy?
8. At **−10 °C**, liquid water freezes spontaneously into ice, even though the entropy of the water (system) **decreases** (ΔS_sys < 0). Does this violate the Second Law of Thermodynamics? Explain!
9. Liquid acetone boils at **56 °C (329 K)** with **Δ_vapH = 29.1 kJ mol⁻¹**. Calculate **Δ_vapS** in **J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹**. *(Flammable liquid: paper problem only.)*
10. State whether each statement is True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) For every spontaneous process, ΔS_system must always be greater than zero.
    (b) Entropy is a state function and an extensive property.
    (c) For an isolated system, any spontaneous process increases the entropy of the system (ΔS_sys > 0).
    (d) A spontaneous reaction always occurs very rapidly (in seconds).
11. Calculate the entropy change **ΔS** (in J K⁻¹) when **2.0 mol** of an ideal gas expands isothermally from **5.0 L to 50.0 L** (R = 8.314 J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹, log 10 = 1).
12. What is **Trouton's Rule**, and why is the entropy of vaporisation of water (**109 J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹**) noticeably higher than Trouton's value of ~88 J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹?

### Answers

1. (a) **ΔS > 0 (+)** (solid → gas, randomness badhi).
   (b) **ΔS < 0 (−)** (liquid → solid, particles jam gaye).
   (c) **ΔS < 0 (−)** (Δn_g = 2 − 3 = −1, gas ke moles ghate).
   (d) **ΔS > 0 (+)** (solid crystal tootkar azaad ions Na⁺, Cl⁻ bane).
   (e) **ΔS < 0 (−)** (thanda hone par molecules ki raftaar aur randomness ghati).
   (f) **ΔS > 0 (+)** (1 mol gas se 2 mol gas atoms bane, Δn_g = +1).

2. Spontaneity sirf ΔH par nahi, balki **entropy (disorder)** par bhi nirbhar karti hai! Ice pighalne ya NH₄NO₃ ghulne par **ΔS bahut zyada positive** hota hai, jisse poore universe ki kul entropy (ΔS_total) badh jaati hai (> 0)!

3. Δ_fusH = 6.00 × 1000 = 6000 J mol⁻¹; T_m = 273 K.
   Δ_fusS = 6000 / 273 = **+21.98 J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹**.
   Jaanch: 21.98 × 273 = 6000.5 J ≈ 6.00 kJ/mol ✅.

4. System ne heat chhodi → ΔH_sys = −59.6 kJ = −59600 J (surroundings ne +59600 J li!).
   ΔS_surr = −ΔH_sys / T = +59600 / 298 = **+200.0 J K⁻¹**.
   Jaanch: 200 × 298 = 59600 J = 59.6 kJ ✅.

5. ΔS_total = ΔS_sys + ΔS_surr = (−80) + (+120) = **+40 J K⁻¹**.
   Kyunki **ΔS_total > 0**, ye reaction **spontaneous** hai!
   Jaanch: −80 + 120 = +40 > 0 ✅.

6. 1 mol H₂(g) mein dono H atoms ek bond se bandhe (saath ghoomne ko majboor) hain, jabki **2 mol H(g)** mein dugne azaad particles hain jo alag-alag dishaon mein ghoom sakte hain → zyada microstates aur **zyada entropy**!

7. **Third Law:** 0 K par kisi perfect pure crystal ki entropy bilkul **zero (S = 0)** hoti hai. Kyunki humein ek pakka **zero reference point (0 K par S = 0)** mil jaata hai, hum kisi bhi temperature T par **absolute entropy S°** nikaal sakte hain (jabki U aur H ka koi absolute zero point nahi hota!).

8. **Bilkul nahi!** Second Law sirf **ΔS_total = ΔS_sys + ΔS_surr > 0** maangta hai. Freezing exothermic hai — jo heat barf bante waqt bahar nikalti hai, wo −10 °C par surroundings ki entropy ko itna badha deti hai ki **|ΔS_surr| > |ΔS_sys|**, aur net **ΔS_total > 0** rehta hai!

9. Δ_vapH = 29.1 × 1000 = 29100 J mol⁻¹; T_b = 329 K.
   Δ_vapS = 29100 / 329 = **+88.45 J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹**.
   Jaanch: 88.45 × 329 = 29100.05 J ≈ 29.1 kJ/mol ✅.

10. (a) **False** ❌. **ΔS_total** (system + surroundings) ka > 0 hona zaroori hai, ΔS_system negative bhi ho sakta hai.
    (b) **True** ✅.
    (c) **True** ✅. Isolated system mein ΔS_surr = 0, isliye ΔS_total = ΔS_sys > 0.
    (d) **False** ❌. Spontaneity ka matlab **raftaar (speed)** nahi hota — lohe mein jung lagna aur diamond ka graphite banna spontaneous hain, par bahut dheeme hote hain!

11. ΔS = 2.303 n R log(V₂/V₁) = 2.303 × 2.0 × 8.314 × log(50/5) = 2.303 × 2.0 × 8.314 × 1 = **+38.29 J K⁻¹**.
    Jaanch: 2 mol ke liye 1 mol (19.15 J/K) ka dugna = 38.29 J/K ✅.

12. **Trouton's Rule:** Aam non-associated liquids ka Δ_vapS ≈ **88 J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹** hota hai. Liquid H₂O mein mazboot **Hydrogen bonding** hone se wo aam liquids se zyada ordered (kam entropy) hota hai, isliye gas banne par entropy mein zyada bada jump (**109 J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹**) aata hai!
