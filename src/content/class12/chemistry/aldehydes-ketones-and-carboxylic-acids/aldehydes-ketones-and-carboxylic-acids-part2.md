# Preparation of Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids

> **Pichle topic mein:** Humne [Nomenclature and Structure of the Carbonyl and Carboxyl Groups](aldehydes-ketones-and-carboxylic-acids.md) padha. Ab hum aldehydes, ketones aur carboxylic acids banane ke sabhi classic named reactions — **Rosenmund, Stephen, DIBAL-H, Etard, Gattermann–Koch, Friedel–Crafts Acylation, aur Grignard Carbonation** — seekhenge!

---

Basant ne apni study desk ke saamne ek colourful chart chipkaya tha: "Benzaldehyde banane ke 4 raaste!" Acid chloride se **Rosenmund**, cyanide se **Stephen**, toluene se **Etard**, aur seedha benzene se **Gattermann–Koch**! Basant ne dekha ki har reaction mein ek hi smart trick hai — **reaction ko aldehyde stage par rokna**, taki woh aage alcohol ya carboxylic acid na ban jaye!

---

## Naye Shabd (Key Terms)

| Term | Hinglish Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Rosenmund Reduction** | Acid chloride (R–COCl) + H₂ over **Pd–BaSO₄** (poisoned catalyst) → **Aldehyde (R–CHO)** |
| **Stephen Reaction** | Nitrile (R–CN) + **SnCl₂ / HCl** → imine hydrochloride —(H₃O⁺)→ **Aldehyde (R–CHO)** |
| **DIBAL-H** | Diisobutylaluminium hydride [AlH(i-Bu)₂] — selective reducing agent jo nitriles aur esters ko **aldehydes** par rok deta hai (bina C=C tode!) |
| **Etard Reaction** | Toluene + **CrO₂Cl₂ (chromyl chloride) in CS₂**, phir H₃O⁺ → **Benzaldehyde** |
| **Gattermann–Koch Reaction** | Benzene + **CO + HCl** (anhyd. AlCl₃ / CuCl) → **Benzaldehyde** |

---

## 1. Preparation of Aldehydes ONLY (Selective Named Reactions!)

*(General methods for both aldehydes & ketones: Oxidation of 1°/2° alcohols, Dehydrogenation over Cu at 573 K, Ozonolysis of alkenes, Hydration of alkynes with HgSO₄/H₂SO₄ — Class 11/Ch 7 bridges!)*

1. **Rosenmund Reduction (From Acyl Chloride):**
   **R–COCl + H₂ —(Pd / BaSO₄)→ R–CHO + HCl**
   - Example: Benzoyl chloride (C₆H₅COCl) → **Benzaldehyde (C₆H₅CHO)**.
   - **BaSO₄ (or S/quinoline) kyun?** Catalyst Pd ko **poison (mild)** karne ke liye, taki bana hua aldehyde aage reduce hokar 1° alcohol na ban jaye!
   - *(Note: Formaldehyde HCHO is method se nahi ban sakta kyunki HCOCl room temp par unstable hai!)*
2. **Stephen Reaction (From Nitriles):**
   **R–C≡N + SnCl₂ + 2HCl → R–CH=NH·HCl —(H₃O⁺)→ R–CHO**
3. **Reduction with DIBAL-H (Best for Nitriles & Esters, Preserves C=C!):**
   - R–CN —(1. DIBAL-H, 2. H₂O)→ **R–CHO**
   - CH₃–CH=CH–CH₂–CH₂–CN —(1. DIBAL-H, 2. H₂O)→ **CH₃–CH=CH–CH₂–CH₂–CHO** (double bond untouched!).
   - R–COOR' —(1. DIBAL-H, 2. H₂O)→ **R–CHO**!
4. **Etard Reaction (Toluene → Benzaldehyde):**
   **C₆H₅–CH₃ + CrO₂Cl₂ (in CS₂) → [brown chromium complex] —(H₃O⁺)→ C₆H₅–CHO**
   - Alternate (with acetic anhydride): Toluene + CrO₃ / (CH₃CO)₂O (273–283 K) → benzylidene diacetate —(H₃O⁺)→ **Benzaldehyde** (prevents over-oxidation to benzoic acid!).
   - Commercial route (side-chain chlorination): Toluene + Cl₂ (hν) → C₆H₅–CHCl₂ (benzal chloride) —(H₂O, 373 K)→ **Benzaldehyde**.
5. **Gattermann–Koch Reaction (Benzene → Benzaldehyde):**
   **C₆H₆ + CO + HCl —(anhyd. AlCl₃ / CuCl)→ C₆H₅–CHO**

## 2. Preparation of Ketones ONLY

1. **From Acyl Chlorides + Dialkylcadmium (R₂Cd):**
   *(R₂Cd is prepared from 2RMgX + CdCl₂ → R₂Cd + 2MgXCl; less reactive than RMgX so it stops at ketone and does NOT react further to 3° alcohol!)*
   **2R'–COCl + R₂Cd → 2R'–CO–R + CdCl₂**
2. **From Nitriles + Grignard Reagent:**
   **R–C≡N + R'–MgX —(dry ether)→ R–C(=NMgX)–R' —(H₃O⁺)→ R–CO–R'**
   - Example: CH₃CH₂CN + C₆H₅MgBr —(1. ether, 2. H₃O⁺)→ **Propiophenone (C₆H₅–CO–CH₂CH₃)**.
3. **Friedel–Crafts Acylation (Aromatic Ketones):**
   **C₆H₆ + R–COCl (or Ar–COCl) —(anhyd. AlCl₃)→ C₆H₅–CO–R** (e.g., + CH₃COCl → **Acetophenone**; + C₆H₅COCl → **Benzophenone**).

---

## 3. Preparation of Carboxylic Acids

1. **Oxidation of 1° Alcohols & Aldehydes:**
   R–CH₂OH —(alk. KMnO₄ / H₃O⁺ or CrO₃–H₂SO₄ Jones reagent)→ **R–COOH**.
2. **Oxidation of Alkylbenzenes (SUPER IMPORTANT TRICK!):**
   Koi bhi alkyl side-chain (CH₃, C₂H₅, n-propyl, isopropyl — bas kam se kam **1 benzylic H** hona chahiye!) hot **alkaline KMnO₄ + H₃O⁺** se **seedha –COOH (Benzoic acid)** mein badal jati hai!
   - C₆H₅–CH₂CH₂CH₃ —(1. KMnO₄/KOH, Δ; 2. H₃O⁺)→ **C₆H₅–COOH (Benzoic acid)**!
   - *(Exception: **tert-Butylbenzene** C₆H₅–C(CH₃)₃ mein benzylic H nahi hai → **NO oxidation**!)*
3. **Hydrolysis of Nitriles (R–CN) and Amides (R–CONH₂):**
   - R–CN —(H⁺ or OH⁻, H₂O, Δ)→ R–CONH₂ —(H⁺ or OH⁻, H₂O, Δ)→ **R–COOH** (+ NH₃/NH₄⁺).
   - *(Step-up trick: R–X → R–CN → R–COOH increases chain by **+1 carbon**!)*
4. **From Grignard Reagents + Dry Ice (Solid CO₂):**
   **R–Mg–X + O=C=O (dry ice) —(dry ether)→ R–COOMgX —(H₃O⁺)→ R–COOH** (also adds **+1 carbon**!).
5. **Hydrolysis of Acyl Halides, Anhydrides, and Esters:**
   - R–COCl + H₂O → R–COOH + HCl
   - (RCO)₂O + H₂O → 2R–COOH
   - R–COOR' + H₂O —(H⁺)→ R–COOH + R'–OH (acidic hydrolysis is reversible; basic hydrolysis gives R–COO⁻Na⁺ + R'OH, then H⁺ → R–COOH).

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Choosing the Best Reagent for Each Synthesis
Suggest a suitable reagent/named reaction for each conversion:
(a) Benzoyl chloride → Benzaldehyde
(b) Toluene → Benzaldehyde
(c) Ethylbenzene → Benzoic acid
(d) Bromobenzene → Benzoic acid

**Solution:**
(a) **H₂ / Pd–BaSO₄** (Rosenmund reduction).
(b) **1. CrO₂Cl₂ / CS₂, 2. H₃O⁺** (Etard reaction).
(c) **1. KMnO₄ / KOH (Δ), 2. H₃O⁺** (complete side-chain oxidation to –COOH).
(d) **1. Mg / dry ether → C₆H₅MgBr; 2. CO₂ (dry ice); 3. H₃O⁺** → C₆H₅COOH!

Jaanch: Each route is direct, high-yield, and stops cleanly at the target functional group ✅

---

### Example 2: Preparing a Ketone Without Over-Reaction
Why is **dialkylcadmium (R₂Cd)** used instead of a Grignard reagent (RMgX) to prepare ketones from acyl chlorides (R'–COCl)?

**Solution:**
Grignard reagents (RMgX) are so reactive that they react immediately with the ketone formed in the first step to produce a **tertiary alcohol (R'–C(OH)R₂)**. Dialkylcadmium (R₂Cd) is much less reactive — it reacts cleanly with acyl chlorides to give **ketones** and stops there!

Jaanch: R₂Cd stops at ketone; RMgX over-reacts to 3° alcohol ✅

---

### Example 3: Selective Reduction Preserving a Double Bond
Predict the product of: CH₃–CH=CH–CH₂–CN —(1. DIBAL-H, 2. H₂O)→ ?

**Solution:**
**DIBAL-H** selectively reduces the –CN group to –CHO without touching the C=C double bond:
Product = **CH₃–CH=CH–CH₂–CHO (Pent-3-enal)**.

Jaanch: Carbon count stays 5; C=C preserved; –CN → –CHO ✅

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **Ethylbenzene + KMnO₄ se phenylacetic acid (C₆H₅CH₂COOH) bana dena:**
   - *Galat:* C₆H₅–CH₂CH₃ + KMnO₄ → C₆H₅–CH₂–COOH.
   - *Sahi:* Side-chain kitni bhi lambi ho (CH₃, C₂H₅, C₃H₇), KMnO₄/KOH + H₃O⁺ poori side-chain ko kat kar **seedha Benzoic acid (C₆H₅–COOH)** banata hai!

2. **Rosenmund aur Stephen ke reagents mix kar dena:**
   - *Galat:* R–CN + H₂ / Pd–BaSO₄ → R–CHO.
   - *Sahi:* **Rosenmund** = R–COCl + H₂/Pd–BaSO₄; **Stephen** = R–CN + SnCl₂/HCl, then H₃O⁺!

3. **Alkyne hydration (ethyne vs propyne) ke products mix kar dena:**
   - *Galat:* Propyne + H₂O (HgSO₄/H₂SO₄) → Propanal.
   - *Sahi:* Sirf **Ethyne (HC≡CH)** aldehyde (**ethanal**) deta hai! Baaki saare alkynes (jaise propyne) Markovnikov addition se **ketone (propanone)** dete hain!

## Quick Recap

- **Aldehydes ONLY:** Rosenmund (RCOCl + H₂/Pd-BaSO₄), Stephen (RCN + SnCl₂/HCl), DIBAL-H (RCN/ester → RCHO), Etard (Toluene + CrO₂Cl₂/CS₂), Gattermann–Koch (Benzene + CO/HCl/AlCl₃).
- **Ketones ONLY:** R'COCl + R₂Cd; RCN + R'MgX/H₃O⁺; Friedel–Crafts acylation (C₆H₆ + RCOCl/AlCl₃).
- **Carboxylic Acids:** 1° alcohol/aldehyde + KMnO₄; Alkylbenzene + KMnO₄/KOH → Benzoic acid; RCN + H₃O⁺; **RMgX + CO₂ (dry ice) / H₃O⁺** (+1 carbon!).

## Practice Questions

1. Write the chemical equation for Rosenmund reduction. What is the role of BaSO₄ in this reaction?
2. Write the chemical equations for: (a) Stephen reaction, (b) Etard reaction, (c) Gattermann–Koch reaction.
3. How does DIBAL-H convert: (a) CH₃(CH₂)₃CN, (b) CH₃CH₂COOC₂H₅ into aldehydes?
4. Write the preparation of propanone from ethanoyl chloride (CH₃COCl) using dimethylcadmium.
5. How will you prepare acetophenone from: (a) benzene, (b) benzonitrile (C₆H₅CN)?
6. Predict the product when **propylbenzene** is heated with alkaline KMnO₄ followed by acidification. What happens with **tert-butylbenzene** under the same conditions?
7. Write two methods to convert **bromoethane** into **propanoic acid** (adding +1 carbon).
8. Complete the reaction: CH₃–C≡CH + H₂O —(HgSO₄ / dil. H₂SO₄, 333 K)→ ?
9. How is benzaldehyde prepared commercially from toluene via side-chain chlorination?
10. Convert: (a) Cyclohexene to hexanedioic acid (adipic acid), (b) Butan-1-ol to butanoic acid.

---

### Answers

1. **R–COCl + H₂ —(Pd/BaSO₄)→ R–CHO + HCl**. **BaSO₄ poisons the Pd catalyst**, reducing its activity so the aldehyde is NOT further reduced to a 1° alcohol.
2. (a) **Stephen:** R–CN + SnCl₂ + 2HCl → R–CH=NH·HCl —(H₃O⁺)→ **R–CHO**.
   (b) **Etard:** C₆H₅CH₃ + CrO₂Cl₂ (CS₂) → chromium complex —(H₃O⁺)→ **C₆H₅CHO**.
   (c) **Gattermann–Koch:** C₆H₆ + CO + HCl —(anhyd. AlCl₃/CuCl)→ **C₆H₅CHO**.
3. (a) CH₃(CH₂)₃CN —(1. DIBAL-H, 2. H₂O)→ **CH₃(CH₂)₃CHO (pentanal)**.
   (b) CH₃CH₂COOC₂H₅ —(1. DIBAL-H, 2. H₂O)→ **CH₃CH₂CHO (propanal)** (+ C₂H₅OH).
4. **2CH₃COCl + (CH₃)₂Cd → 2CH₃COCH₃ (propanone) + CdCl₂**.
5. (a) **Friedel–Crafts:** C₆H₆ + CH₃COCl —(anhyd. AlCl₃)→ **C₆H₅COCH₃** + HCl.
   (b) **Grignard on nitrile:** C₆H₅CN + CH₃MgBr —(1. dry ether, 2. H₃O⁺)→ **C₆H₅COCH₃**.
6. **Propylbenzene** has benzylic hydrogens → oxidises completely to **Benzoic acid (C₆H₅COOH)**. **tert-Butylbenzene** has NO benzylic hydrogen → **NO reaction**!
7. - **Route 1 (Cyanide hydrolysis):** CH₃CH₂Br + KCN → CH₃CH₂CN —(H₃O⁺, Δ)→ **CH₃CH₂COOH**.
   - **Route 2 (Grignard + CO₂):** CH₃CH₂Br + Mg (dry ether) → CH₃CH₂MgBr —(1. CO₂, 2. H₃O⁺)→ **CH₃CH₂COOH**.
   Jaanch: Both routes cleanly extend C2 → C3 ✅
8. Markovnikov hydration gives enol CH₃–C(OH)=CH₂ which tautomerises to **CH₃–CO–CH₃ (propanone)**.
9. Toluene + 2Cl₂ —(UV light, hν)→ C₆H₅–CHCl₂ (benzal chloride) —(H₂O, 373 K)→ **C₆H₅–CHO (benzaldehyde)** + 2HCl.
10. (a) Cyclohexene + hot acidic/alkaline KMnO₄ (followed by H₃O⁺) → **HOOC–(CH₂)₄–COOH (hexanedioic acid)**.
    (b) Butan-1-ol + Jones reagent (CrO₃–H₂SO₄) or KMnO₄/H⁺ → **CH₃CH₂CH₂COOH (butanoic acid)**.
