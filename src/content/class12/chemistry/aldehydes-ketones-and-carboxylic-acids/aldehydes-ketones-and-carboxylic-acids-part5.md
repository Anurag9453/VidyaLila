## Mixed Solved Examples

### Example 1: Four Classic Multi-Step Conversions
Carry out the following conversions (max 2–3 steps):
(a) Benzoic acid → Benzaldehyde
(b) Ethanal → But-2-enoic acid
(c) Propanone → Propene
(d) Benzaldehyde → 3-Phenylpropan-1-ol

**Solution:**
(a) **Benzoic acid → Benzaldehyde:**
C₆H₅COOH —(SOCl₂)→ C₆H₅COCl —(H₂ / Pd–BaSO₄, Rosenmund)→ **C₆H₅CHO**.

(b) **Ethanal → But-2-enoic acid:**
- Step 1 (Aldol condensation): 2CH₃CHO —(dil. NaOH, Δ)→ CH₃–CH=CH–CHO (but-2-enal)
- Step 2 (Mild selective oxidation — preserves C=C!): CH₃–CH=CH–CHO —(Tollens' reagent [Ag(NH₃)₂]⁺ / OH⁻, then H⁺)→ **CH₃–CH=CH–COOH (but-2-enoic acid)**!

(c) **Propanone → Propene:**
CH₃COCH₃ —(NaBH₄)→ CH₃CH(OH)CH₃ —(conc. H₂SO₄, 443 K)→ **CH₃–CH=CH₂**.

(d) **Benzaldehyde → 3-Phenylpropan-1-ol:**
- Step 1 (Cross-Aldol with ethanal): C₆H₅CHO + CH₃CHO —(dil. NaOH, Δ)→ C₆H₅–CH=CH–CHO (cinnamaldehyde)
- Step 2 (Catalytic hydrogenation reduces BOTH C=C and –CHO!): C₆H₅–CH=CH–CHO + 2H₂ —(Ni/Pd)→ **C₆H₅–CH₂–CH₂–CH₂OH (3-phenylpropan-1-ol)**!

Jaanch: Tollens' preserves C=C in (b); H₂/Ni reduces both C=C and C=O cleanly in (d) ✅

### Example 2: Organic Compound Deduction (Formula + Tests)
An organic compound **A** (C₈H₈O) forms an orange-red precipitate with 2,4-DNP reagent and gives a **yellow precipitate** on heating with iodine in the presence of NaOH. It **neither reduces Tollens' reagent nor Fehling's reagent**. On drastic oxidation with chromic acid, it gives a carboxylic acid **B** (C₇H₆O₂). Identify **A** and **B**.

**Solution:**
- Positive 2,4-DNP + negative Tollens'/Fehling's → **A is a Ketone**!
- Positive Iodoform test (yellow CHI₃ ppt) → A contains **–CO–CH₃ (methyl ketone)**!
- C₈H₈O minus –COCH₃ (C₂H₃O) leaves **C₆H₅– (phenyl ring)** → **A = Acetophenone (C₆H₅–CO–CH₃)**!
- Drastic oxidation of acetophenone gives **B = Benzoic acid (C₆H₅–COOH, C₇H₆O₂)**!

Jaanch: Formula of C₆H₅COCH₃ = C₈H₈O; C₆H₅COOH = C₇H₆O₂; matches all 4 tests ✅

## Aam Galtiyan (Mixed-Conversion Traps)

1. **But-2-enal se But-2-enoic acid banane mein KMnO₄ use kar dena:**
   - *Galat:* CH₃–CH=CH–CHO + KMnO₄ → CH₃–CH=CH–COOH.
   - *Sahi:* KMnO₄ C=C double bond ko bhi tod dega! Sirf –CHO ko –COOH mein badalne ke liye **Tollens' reagent** use karo (C=C safe rehta hai!).

2. **Aldol product ke carbon atoms ghalat ginna:**
   - *Galat:* 2CH₃CHO se 3-carbon product banana.
   - *Sahi:* Self-aldol hamesha **carbons ko DOUBLE** karta hai! Ethanal (2 C) → **But-2-enal (4 C)**; Propanone (3 C) → **6 C**!

## Quick Recap

- **Acid → Aldehyde:** RCOOH —(SOCl₂)→ RCOCl —(H₂/Pd-BaSO₄)→ RCHO!
- **Aldehyde/Ketone → Alkane:** Clemmensen (Zn–Hg/HCl) or Wolff–Kishner (NH₂NH₂/KOH).
- **Doubling carbons (2C → 4C):** Aldol condensation (dil. NaOH, Δ)!
- **Selective oxidation preserving C=C:** Tollens' reagent!

## Practice Questions

1. Convert: (a) Benzene to benzaldehyde, (b) Toluene to benzoic acid, (c) Bromobenzene to 1-phenylethanol.
2. Convert: (a) Propanone to 4-methylpent-3-en-2-one, (b) Benzaldehyde to benzophenone, (c) Benzoic acid to m-nitrobenzyl alcohol.
3. Give ONE simple chemical test to distinguish between:
   (a) Phenol and Benzoic acid
   (b) Benzaldehyde and Acetophenone
   (c) Ethanal and Propanal
4. An organic compound **A** (C₃H₆O) gives a positive 2,4-DNP test, reduces Tollens' reagent, and does NOT give a yellow precipitate with I₂ + NaOH. Identify **A**.
5. Arrange in increasing order of reactivity towards nucleophilic addition:
   Acetophenone, Benzaldehyde, Propanal, Ethanal.
6. Arrange in increasing order of acid strength:
   Benzoic acid, 4-Nitrobenzoic acid, 3,4-Dinitrobenzoic acid, 4-Methoxybenzoic acid.
7. Complete the following sequence:
   CH₃COOH —(SOCl₂)→ **A** —(H₂ / Pd–BaSO₄)→ **B** —(dil. NaOH, Δ)→ **C**.
8. Write the products of treating **benzaldehyde** with: (a) conc. NaOH, (b) NH₂NH₂ followed by KOH/ethylene glycol (Δ).
9. Why does **2,2-dimethylpropanal** give Cannizzaro reaction while **propanal** gives Aldol condensation?
10. Convert **ethanoic acid** into **methane** in two steps.

---

### Answers

1. (a) C₆H₆ + CO + HCl —(anhyd. AlCl₃/CuCl, Gattermann–Koch)→ **C₆H₅CHO**.
   (b) C₆H₅CH₃ —(1. KMnO₄/KOH, Δ; 2. H₃O⁺)→ **C₆H₅COOH**.
   (c) C₆H₅Br + Mg (dry ether) → C₆H₅MgBr —(1. CH₃CHO, 2. H₂O/H⁺)→ **C₆H₅CH(OH)CH₃**.
2. (a) 2CH₃COCH₃ —(Ba(OH)₂, Δ — Aldol)→ **(CH₃)₂C=CH–CO–CH₃**.
   (b) C₆H₅CHO —(1. C₆H₅MgBr/ether, 2. H₃O⁺)→ C₆H₅CH(OH)C₆H₅ —(CrO₃)→ **C₆H₅COC₆H₅**.
   (c) C₆H₅COOH —(conc. HNO₃/H₂SO₄)→ m-O₂N–C₆H₄–COOH —(1. B₂H₆/THF, 2. H₂O)→ **m-O₂N–C₆H₄–CH₂OH** (B₂H₆ preserves –NO₂!).
   Jaanch: Using B₂H₆ in (c) avoids reducing –NO₂ (which LiAlH₄ would reduce!) ✅
3. (a) **NaHCO₃:** Benzoic acid → brisk CO₂↑; Phenol → no reaction.
   (b) **Tollens' test** (or Iodoform test): Benzaldehyde → silver mirror; Acetophenone → yellow CHI₃ ppt with I₂/NaOH.
   (c) **Iodoform test (I₂ + NaOH):** Ethanal → yellow CHI₃ ppt; Propanal → no ppt.
4. C₃H₆O isomers are propanal and propanone. Positive Tollens' + negative iodoform → **Propanal (CH₃CH₂CHO)**.
5. Increasing nucleophilic addition reactivity: **Acetophenone < Benzaldehyde < Propanal < Ethanal**.
6. Increasing acid strength: **4-Methoxybenzoic acid < Benzoic acid < 4-Nitrobenzoic acid < 3,4-Dinitrobenzoic acid**.
7. **A = CH₃COCl (ethanoyl chloride)**; **B = CH₃CHO (ethanal)**; **C = CH₃–CH=CH–CHO (but-2-enal)**.
8. (a) **C₆H₅CH₂OH + C₆H₅COONa** (Cannizzaro reaction).
   (b) **C₆H₅CH₃ (toluene)** + N₂↑ (Wolff–Kishner reduction).
9. 2,2-Dimethylpropanal ((CH₃)₃C–CHO) has **no α-hydrogen**, so it undergoes **Cannizzaro** reaction with conc. NaOH. Propanal (CH₃CH₂CHO) has **2 α-hydrogens**, so it undergoes **Aldol** condensation with dil. NaOH.
10. CH₃COOH + NaOH → CH₃COONa —(NaOH + CaO, Δ — soda lime decarboxylation)→ **CH₄ (methane)** + Na₂CO₃.
