## Naye Shabd (Key Terms)

| Term | Hinglish Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Carbonyl Group (>C=O)** | Carbon–oxygen double bond jo aldehydes, ketones, carboxylic acids, esters, amides sab mein maujood hota hai |
| **Aldehyde (R–CHO)** | Woh carbonyl compound jismein C=O carbon kam se kam ek **hydrogen atom** se juda ho (chain ke end par hota hai) |
| **Ketone (R–CO–R')** | Woh carbonyl compound jismein C=O carbon **do carbon atoms (alkyl/aryl)** ke beech juda ho |
| **Carboxylic Acid (R–COOH)** | Woh compound jismein carbonyl group (>C=O) aur hydroxyl group (–OH) ek hi carbon par jude hon (carboxyl group) |
| **α-Carbon & α-Hydrogen** | Carbonyl carbon (>C=O) ke **theek bagal wala** sp³ carbon atom aur us par jude hydrogens |

## 1. IUPAC and Common Nomenclature

### (a) Aldehydes (Suffix: **-al** ; Ring attached: **-carbaldehyde**)
- Longest chain containing –CHO choose karo; **–CHO carbon ko hamesha C1** milta hai (number 1 likhne ki zaroorat nahi!).
- Common names carboxylic acids ke root (form-, acet-, propion-, butyr-, valer-) + **-aldehyde** se bante hain; substituents ki positions **α, β, γ** se dikhate hain!
- Jab –CHO seedha kisi **ring** par juda ho, toh suffix **-carbaldehyde** use karte hain (e.g., Cyclohexanecarbaldehyde; Benzene + CHO = **Benzaldehyde** / Benzenecarbaldehyde).

| Formula | Common Name | IUPAC Name |
|---|---|---|
| HCHO | Formaldehyde | **Methanal** |
| CH₃CHO | Acetaldehyde | **Ethanal** |
| CH₃CH₂CHO | Propionaldehyde | **Propanal** |
| CH₃CH(Br)CH₂CHO | β-Bromobutyraldehyde | **3-Bromobutanal** |
| CH₂=CH–CHO | Acrolein | **Prop-2-enal** |
| C₆H₅–CH=CH–CHO | Cinnamaldehyde | **3-Phenylprop-2-enal** |
| OHC–CH₂–CHO | — | **Propane-1,3-dial** |

### (b) Ketones (Suffix: **-one** ; Prefix when lower priority: **oxo-**)
- Longest chain containing >C=O choose karo aur numbering udhar se karo jahan >C=O ko **lowest locant** mile.
- Common names: dono alkyl groups alphabetically + **ketone** (e.g., ethyl methyl ketone). Aromatic ketones with –COCH₃ = **phenones** (Acetophenone, Benzophenone).

| Formula | Common Name | IUPAC Name |
|---|---|---|
| CH₃–CO–CH₃ | Acetone (Dimethyl ketone) | **Propanone** |
| CH₃–CO–CH₂CH₃ | Ethyl methyl ketone | **Butan-2-one** |
| C₆H₅–CO–CH₃ | Acetophenone | **1-Phenylethanone** |
| C₆H₅–CO–C₆H₅ | Benzophenone | **Diphenylmethanone** |
| (CH₃)₂C=CH–CO–CH₃ | Mesityl oxide | **4-Methylpent-3-en-2-one** |

### (c) Carboxylic Acids (Suffix: **-oic acid** ; Ring attached: **-carboxylic acid**)
- –COOH ko sabhi common functional groups mein **highest IUPAC priority** milti hai (C1 hamesha –COOH!).
- Important dicarboxylic acids (Mnemonic: **O M S G A P** = Oxalic, Malonic, Succinic, Glutaric, Adipic, Pimelic — 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 carbons!):
  - HOOC–COOH: Oxalic acid → **Ethanedioic acid** (2 C)
  - HOOC–CH₂–COOH: Malonic acid → **Propanedioic acid** (3 C)
  - HOOC–(CH₂)₂–COOH: Succinic acid → **Butanedioic acid** (4 C)
  - HOOC–(CH₂)₄–COOH: Adipic acid → **Hexanedioic acid** (6 C)
  - C₆H₄(COOH)₂ (1,2): Phthalic acid → **Benzene-1,2-dicarboxylic acid**.

## 2. Structure of the Carbonyl Group (>C=O) and Carboxyl Group (–COOH)

### (a) Carbonyl Group (>C=O):
- Carbonyl carbon **sp² hybridised** hai → 3 σ-bonds ek hi plane mein **~120°** angles par (**trigonal planar** geometry).
- 4th unhybridised p-orbital oxygen ke p-orbital ke saath sidewise overlap karke **π-bond** banata hai (π-electron cloud plane ke upar aur neeche!).
- **High Polarity:** Oxygen carbon se kahin zyada electronegative hai, isliye π-electrons oxygen ki taraf khinche rehte hain:
  **>C=O  ↔  >C⁺—O⁻**
- **Consequence:**
  - Carbonyl **carbon** = **electrophilic (Lewis acid, δ⁺)** → nucleophiles (Nu⁻) is par attack karte hain!
  - Carbonyl **oxygen** = **nucleophilic (Lewis base, δ⁻)** → protons (H⁺) is par judte hain!
  - Carbonyl compounds ka dipole moment ethers se kahin zyada hota hai (e.g., propanone μ = 2.88 D vs diethyl ether μ = 1.18 D).

### (b) Carboxyl Group (–COOH):
- Carboxyl carbon bhi **sp² hybridised (planar, ~120°)** hai.
- **Why carboxylic acids do NOT give typical aldehyde/ketone reactions (like 2,4-DNP or NaHSO₃)?**
  Kyunki –OH group ka oxygen apna **lone pair resonance** ke zariye carbonyl carbon ko donate kar deta hai (**R–C(=O)–OH ↔ R–C(O⁻)=O⁺H**), jisse carbonyl carbon ka **electrophilic character (δ⁺) bahut kam** ho jata hai!

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Writing IUPAC Names of Polyfunctional Carbonyls
Write the IUPAC names of:
(a) OHC–CH₂–CH(CHO)–CH₂–CHO
(b) CH₃–CO–CH₂–CH₂–COOH
(c) CH₃–CH=CH–CHO

**Solution:**
(a) Three –CHO groups directly attached to a 3-carbon propane chain → treat all three symmetrically using **-tricarbaldehyde** → **Propane-1,2,3-tricarbaldehyde**.
(b) –COOH has higher priority than ketone >C=O (which becomes prefix **oxo-**): number from –COOH as C1 → **4-Oxopentanoic acid**.
(c) –CHO gets C1; double bond at C2 → **But-2-enal** (crotonaldehyde).

Jaanch: Highest priority group gets C1 in (b) and (c); symmetrical 3-CHO rule avoids leaving one –CHO out in (a) ✅

---

### Example 2: Explaining Why Carboxylic Acids Are Less Electrophilic Than Aldehydes
Why does the carbonyl carbon of ethanoic acid (CH₃COOH) fail to undergo nucleophilic addition with HCN or NaHSO₃, whereas ethanal (CH₃CHO) reacts readily?

**Solution:**
In ethanoic acid, the lone pair on the –OH oxygen delocalises into the C=O group by **resonance**, reducing the partial positive charge (δ⁺) on the carbonyl carbon. Hence it is far less electrophilic than the carbonyl carbon of ethanal (where no –OH resonance donation exists).

Jaanch: Resonance donation from –OH reduces electrophilicity of carboxyl carbon ✅

---

### Example 3: Counting σ and π Bonds & Hybridisation
In propanone (CH₃–CO–CH₃), state the hybridisation of each carbon atom and count the total σ and π bonds.

**Solution:**
- C1 and C3 (–CH₃): **sp³ hybridised**.
- C2 (>C=O): **sp² hybridised** (trigonal planar, ~120°).
- σ-bonds: 6 (C–H) + 2 (C–C) + 1 (C–O) = **9 σ-bonds**.
- π-bonds: **1 π-bond** (in C=O).

Jaanch: Total valence bonds = 9 σ + 1 π = 10 bonds; matches 3(4)/2 + 6(1)/2 + 1(2)/2 = 6 + 3 + 1 = 10 ✅

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **Ketone aur Acid ek saath hone par dono ka suffix likh dena:**
   - *Galat:* CH₃–CO–CH₂–COOH ko 3-butanone-1-oic acid likhna.
   - *Sahi:* Sirf **highest-priority group (–COOH)** suffix banega; ketone prefix **oxo-** banega → **3-Oxobutanoic acid**!

2. **OHC–CH₂–CH(CHO)–CH₂–CHO ko 3-formylpentanedial likh dena:**
   - *Galat:* Ek –CHO ko substituent (formyl) bana dena jab teeno seedhe chain par hon.
   - *Sahi:* Jab 3 ya zyada –CHO (ya –COOH) seedhe ek chain par jude hon, toh unhe exclude karke **-tricarbaldehyde** (ya **-tricarboxylic acid**) lagate hain → **Propane-1,2,3-tricarbaldehyde**!

3. **Carbonyl carbon ko sp³ maan lena:**
   - *Galat:* >C=O tetrahedral hai.
   - *Sahi:* Carbonyl carbon **sp² hybridised aur trigonal planar (~120°)** hota hai!

## Quick Recap

- **Suffixes:** Aldehyde = **-al** (-carbaldehyde on ring); Ketone = **-one** (prefix: oxo-); Acid = **-oic acid** (-carboxylic acid on ring).
- **Dicarboxylic acids (OMSGAP):** Oxalic (2C), Malonic (3C), Succinic (4C), Glutaric (5C), Adipic (6C), Pimelic (7C).
- **Carbonyl structure:** sp² planar (~120°), strongly polar (**C^δ+ = O^δ−**) → C is electrophilic, O is nucleophilic.
- **Carboxyl group:** –OH resonance reduces electrophilicity of C=O.

   (a) CH₃–CH(CH₃)–CHO
   (b) CH₃–CO–CH(CH₃)–CH₃
   (c) HOOC–CH₂–CH₂–COOH

   (a) C₆H₅–CH=CH–CHO
   (b) OHC–C₆H₄–CHO (para)

4. Explain the geometry, hybridisation, and polarity of the carbonyl group (>C=O).
5. Why do aldehydes and ketones have much higher dipole moments than ethers of comparable molar mass?
6. Give the common and IUPAC names of: (a) HCHO, (b) CH₃COCH₃, (c) HCOOH.
7. Why is the carbonyl carbon of a carboxylic acid less electrophilic than that of an aldehyde?
8. Identify the α-carbon(s) and count the total number of α-hydrogens in: (a) ethanal, (b) propanone, (c) benzaldehyde, (d) 2,2-dimethylpropanal.
9. Write the structure and IUPAC name of: (a) Acetophenone, (b) Benzophenone, (c) Phthalic acid.
10. Why does the aldehyde group (–CHO) never require a locant number (like 1) in open-chain monoaldehydes?

---

### Answers

1. (a) **2-Methylpropanal**
   (b) **3-Methylbutan-2-one**
   (c) **Butanedioic acid** (succinic acid)
2. (a) **3-Phenylprop-2-enal** (cinnamaldehyde)
   (b) **Benzene-1,4-dicarbaldehyde**
   (c) **Propane-1,2,3-tricarboxylic acid**
3. (a) (CH₃)₂CH–CH₂–CHO
   (b) CH₃–CO–CH₂–CH(Cl)–CH₃
   (c) p-CH₃–C₆H₄–CHO
4. Carbonyl carbon is **sp² hybridised** with **trigonal planar** geometry (~120° angles) and a π-bond perpendicular to the plane. Due to oxygen's higher electronegativity, the C=O bond is strongly polar (**C^δ+ = O^δ−**), making C electrophilic and O nucleophilic.
5. Because the π-electrons of the C=O double bond are far more easily polarised toward electronegative oxygen (resonance >C=O ↔ >C⁺–O⁻) than the σ-electrons of C–O single bonds in ethers.
6. (a) Formaldehyde / **Methanal**
   (b) Acetone / **Propanone**
   (c) Formic acid / **Methanoic acid**
7. Because lone-pair resonance donation from the adjacent –OH oxygen into the C=O group reduces the partial positive charge on the carboxyl carbon.
8. (a) Ethanal (CH₃CHO): **3** α-H.
   (b) Propanone (CH₃COCH₃): **6** α-H (3 on each α-carbon).
   (c) Benzaldehyde (C₆H₅CHO): **0** α-H (ring C1 has no H!).
   (d) 2,2-Dimethylpropanal ((CH₃)₃C–CHO): **0** α-H!
   Jaanch: Benzaldehyde and (CH₃)₃CCHO lack α-H → key for Cannizzaro reaction later! ✅
9. (a) C₆H₅–CO–CH₃ (**1-Phenylethanone**)
   (b) C₆H₅–CO–C₆H₅ (**Diphenylmethanone**)
   (c) Benzene-1,2-dicarboxylic acid (two –COOH ortho)
10. Because –CHO is a terminal functional group (one valency of C is used by H and two by =O), so it can ONLY sit at the end of a carbon chain and is always assigned **C1** automatically.
