# Classification, Structure and Nomenclature of Amines

> **Pichle chapter mein:** Humne [Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids — Mixed Conversions & Wrap-up](../aldehydes-ketones-and-carboxylic-acids/aldehydes-ketones-and-carboxylic-acids-part5.md) complete kiya. **Class 11 bridge:** Hybridisation aur basic IUPAC rules hum [Organic Chemistry: Basic Principles and Techniques](../../../class11/chemistry/organic-chemistry-basic-principles-and-techniques/organic-chemistry-basic-principles-and-techniques.md) mein padh chuke hain — ab hum nitrogen-containing organic compounds ka sabse vital chapter, **Amines**, shuru kar rahe hain!

---

Dimple ne apni medical dictionary mein padha ki hamare sharir ka stress hormone **adrenaline**, blood-pressure dawai **ephedrine**, dentist ka local anaesthetic **Novocain**, aur allergy ki dawai **Benadryl** — sabhi mein ek common functional group hai: **Amine**! Ammonia (NH₃) ke ek, do, ya teeno hydrogens ko alkyl ya aryl groups se replace karke amines bante hain!

---

## Naye Shabd (Key Terms)

| Term | Hinglish Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Amine** | Ammonia (NH₃) ka organic derivative jismein 1, 2, ya 3 H-atoms alkyl/aryl groups se replace kiye gaye hon |
| **Primary (1°) Amine** | NH₃ ka 1 H replace hua ho (**R–NH₂** ya **Ar–NH₂**) |
| **Secondary (2°) Amine** | NH₃ ke 2 H replace hue hon (**R–NH–R'**) |
| **Tertiary (3°) Amine** | NH₃ ke teeno H replace hue hon (**R₃N**) |
| **Quaternary Ammonium Salt** | Nitrogen par 4 alkyl groups + positive charge (**R₄N⁺ X⁻**) |
| **Pyramidal Geometry** | Amines mein sp³ nitrogen (3 bond pairs + 1 lone pair) ka 3D shape (bond angle ~108° in (CH₃)₃N) |

---

## 1. Classification & Structure of Amines

### (a) Classification (1°, 2°, 3° — CAREFUL: Different from Alcohols!)
- Alcohols mein 1°/2°/3° **carbon** se dekhte hain, par amines mein **nitrogen se directly jude carbon atoms ki sankhya** se dekhte hain!
  - **(CH₃)₃C–NH₂** ek **PRIMARY (1°) amine** hai! (Kyunki N par sirf **1** carbon juda hai aur 2 H bache hain — bhale hi woh carbon 3° ho!)
  - **CH₃–NH–CH₃** = **2° amine** (2 carbons on N).
  - **(CH₃)₃N** = **3° amine** (3 carbons on N).
- **Simple amine:** Sabhi alkyl/aryl groups same (e.g., (C₂H₅)₂NH). **Mixed amine:** Groups different (e.g., CH₃–NH–C₂H₅).

### (b) Structure of Amines:
- Ammonia ki tarah, amines mein nitrogen atom **sp³ hybridised** hota hai (3 σ-bonds + 1 unshared **lone pair**).
- Geometry: **Pyramidal**!
- **Bond Angle:** Ideal tetrahedral angle 109.5° se thoda kam — kyunki **lone pair–bond pair repulsion > bond pair–bond pair repulsion**!
  - NH₃ = 107°; Trimethylamine **(CH₃)₃N = 108°** (bulky –CH₃ groups ke steric repulsion ki wajah se 107° se badh kar 108° ho jata hai!).

---

## 2. IUPAC and Common Nomenclature

- **Aliphatic 1° amines:** Alkane ka final 'e' hata kar **-amine** lagao (e.g., CH₃CH₂NH₂ → **Ethanamine**; CH₃CH(NH₂)CH₃ → **Propan-2-amine**).
- **Diamines (keep 'e'!):** H₂N–(CH₂)₆–NH₂ → **Hexane-1,6-diamine**.
- **2° and 3° Amines:**
  - Sabse **lambi carbon chain ko parent alkanamine** banao!
  - Nitrogen par jude baaki chhote alkyl groups ko **N-alkyl** (ya **N,N-dialkyl**) prefix ke roop mein likho!
  - Example: CH₃–NH–CH₂CH₃ → **N-Methylethanamine**.
  - Example: (CH₃)₂N–CH₂CH₂CH₃ → **N,N-Dimethylpropan-1-amine**.
- **Aryl Amines:**
  - **C₆H₅–NH₂** = **Aniline** (IUPAC accepted! Also **Benzenamine**).
  - o/m/p-Methylaniline = **o/m/p-Toluidine** (2/3/4-methylaniline).
  - C₆H₅–NH–CH₃ = **N-Methylaniline**; C₆H₅–N(CH₃)₂ = **N,N-Dimethylaniline**.

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Classifying Amines Correctly
Classify each amine as 1°, 2°, or 3°:
(a) (CH₃)₃C–NH₂
(b) C₆H₅–NH–CH₃
(c) (C₂H₅)₂N–CH₃
(d) C₆H₅–CH₂–NH₂

**Solution:**
(a) **1° amine** (N bonded to only 1 carbon, retains –NH₂!).
(b) **2° amine** (N bonded to 2 carbons: phenyl and methyl).
(c) **3° amine** (N bonded to 3 carbons: two ethyls + one methyl).
(d) **1° amine** (benzylamine — N bonded to 1 benzylic carbon).

Jaanch: Count carbons directly attached to N: (a) 1, (b) 2, (c) 3, (d) 1 ✅

---

### Example 2: Writing IUPAC Names of 1°, 2°, and 3° Amines
Write the IUPAC names of:
(a) CH₂=CH–CH₂–NH₂
(b) CH₃–CH₂–NH–CH₂–CH₂–CH₃
(c) p-Br–C₆H₄–NH₂

**Solution:**
(a) –NH₂ gets higher priority than C=C → number from –NH₂ carbon: **Prop-2-en-1-amine**.
(b) Longer chain = 3 C (propan-1-amine), N-substituent = ethyl → **N-Ethylpropan-1-amine**.
(c) **4-Bromoaniline** (or 4-bromobenzenamine).

Jaanch: Priority –NH₂ > C=C in (a); 3C parent > 2C substituent in (b) ✅

---

### Example 3: Writing All Isomeric Amines of C₄H₁₁N
How many structural isomers of molecular formula C₄H₁₁N exist as 1°, 2°, and 3° amines?

**Solution:**
- **1° Amines (4):** Butan-1-amine, Butan-2-amine, 2-Methylpropan-1-amine, 2-Methylpropan-2-amine.
- **2° Amines (3):** N-Methylpropan-1-amine, N-Methylpropan-2-amine, N-Ethylethanamine.
- **3° Amine (1):** N,N-Dimethylethanamine.
- **Total = 4 + 3 + 1 = 8 structural isomers!**

Jaanch: 4 + 3 + 1 = 8 distinct isomers, each with formula C₄H₁₁N ✅

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **(CH₃)₃C–NH₂ ko 3° amine bol dena:**
   - *Galat:* tert-Butyl group laga hai toh yeh 3° amine hoga.
   - *Sahi:* Nitrogen par sirf **1 carbon** juda hai (–NH₂ group poora hai) → yeh **1° amine** (2-methylpropan-2-amine) hai!

2. **2° amine mein chhoti chain ko parent bana dena:**
   - *Galat:* CH₃–NH–C₂H₅ ko N-ethylmethanamine likhna.
   - *Sahi:* Hamesha **lambi chain parent** hoti hai → **N-Methylethanamine**!

3. **Hexane-1,6-diamine mein 'e' hata dena:**
   - *Galat:* Hexan-1,6-diamine.
   - *Sahi:* 'di' consonant se shuru hota hai, isliye 'e' rakho → **Hexane-1,6-diamine**!

---

## Quick Recap

- **Classification:** By number of C atoms directly on N: 1° (RNH₂), 2° (R₂NH), 3° (R₃N).
- **Structure:** sp³ N, **pyramidal** (~108° in (CH₃)₃N due to lp–bp repulsion < 109.5°).
- **IUPAC:** Longest chain = parent **alkanamine**; other groups on N = **N-alkyl** prefixes; C₆H₅NH₂ = **Aniline**.

## Practice Questions

1. Classify as 1°, 2°, or 3° amines: (a) (CH₃)₂CH–NH₂, (b) C₆H₅–N(CH₃)₂, (c) (CH₃CH₂)₂NH.
2. Why is (CH₃)₃C–NH₂ classified as a primary amine even though (CH₃)₃C–OH is a tertiary alcohol?
3. State the hybridisation, geometry, and C–N–C bond angle of trimethylamine ((CH₃)₃N).
4. Write the IUPAC names of:
   (a) CH₃–CH(NH₂)–CH₂–CH₃
   (b) (CH₃)₂N–CH₂–CH₃
   (c) H₂N–CH₂–CH₂–NH₂
5. Write the structures of: (a) N-Methylaniline, (b) 2-Methylpropan-2-amine, (c) N,N-Dimethylbutan-1-amine.
6. Write all structural isomers of amines with molecular formula C₃H₉N and classify each as 1°, 2°, or 3°.
7. Why is the C–N–C bond angle in (CH₃)₃N (108°) slightly less than the tetrahedral angle of 109.5°?
8. What is the IUPAC name of: (a) p-Toluidine, (b) Allylamine (CH₂=CH–CH₂NH₂)?
9. Distinguish between simple and mixed amines with one example each.
10. What type of isomerism is exhibited by propan-1-amine, N-methylethanamine, and N,N-dimethylmethanamine?

---

### Answers

1. (a) **1° amine**, (b) **3° amine**, (c) **2° amine**.
2. Alcohols are classified by the degree of the **carbon** attached to –OH (here 3° C → 3° alcohol), whereas amines are classified by the **number of carbon atoms directly bonded to nitrogen** (here only 1 C on N → 1° amine!).
3. **sp³ hybridised**, **pyramidal geometry**, C–N–C bond angle = **108°**.
4. (a) **Butan-2-amine**
   (b) **N,N-Dimethylethanamine**
   (c) **Ethane-1,2-diamine**
5. (a) C₆H₅–NH–CH₃
   (b) (CH₃)₃C–NH₂
   (c) (CH₃)₂N–CH₂CH₂CH₂CH₃
6. Four isomers of C₃H₉N:
   1. CH₃CH₂CH₂NH₂: Propan-1-amine (**1°**)
   2. CH₃CH(NH₂)CH₃: Propan-2-amine (**1°**)
   3. CH₃–NH–CH₂CH₃: N-Methylethanamine (**2°**)
   4. (CH₃)₃N: N,N-Dimethylmethanamine (**3°**)
   Jaanch: 2 (1°) + 1 (2°) + 1 (3°) = 4 isomers of C₃H₉N ✅
7. Because the **lone pair–bond pair repulsion** on the sp³ nitrogen is stronger than the bond pair–bond pair repulsion, compressing the C–N–C angle from 109.5° to 108°.
8. (a) **4-Methylaniline** (4-methylbenzenamine)
   (b) **Prop-2-en-1-amine**
9. **Simple amine** has identical alkyl/aryl groups on N (e.g., (CH₃)₂NH); **mixed amine** has different groups on N (e.g., CH₃–NH–C₂H₅).
10. **Functional group isomerism** (1°, 2°, and 3° amines with the same formula C₃H₉N are treated as functional isomers!).
