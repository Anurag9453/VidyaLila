## Naye Shabd (New Words)

- **Rate of Reaction:** Per unit time kisi reactant ke concentration mein kami (ya product ke concentration mein badhotri). SI/common unit: **mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹** (gases ke liye **atm s⁻¹**).
- **Average Rate (r_avg):** Kisi finite time interval Δt mein rate: **r_avg = −Δ[R]/Δt = +Δ[P]/Δt**.
- **Instantaneous Rate (r_inst):** Kisi exact pal t par rate (concentration-time graph par tangent ka slope!).

---

## 1. Stoichiometric Rate Expression (Sabhi Species ka Ek Hi Rate!)

Maan lo balanced chemical reaction hai:

**a A + b B → c C + d D**

Alag-alag reactants/products ke kharch hone ya banne ki speed unke stoichiometric coefficients ke hisaab se alag hoti hai — par poore reaction ka **single unique Rate** paane ke liye har species ke rate ko uske **stoichiometric coefficient se divide** kar dete hain!

**Rate = − (1 / a) (Δ[A] / Δt) = − (1 / b) (Δ[B] / Δt) = + (1 / c) (Δ[C] / Δt) = + (1 / d) (Δ[D] / Δt)**

- **Reactants** ke aage **minus (−)** sign isliye lagate hain kyunki unka concentration ghatta hai (Δ[R] < 0), aur minus lagane se **Rate hamesha positive** aata hai!
- **Example (Haber's process: N₂ + 3 H₂ → 2 NH₃):**
  **Rate = −Δ[N₂]/Δt = −(1/3) Δ[H₂]/Δt = +(1/2) Δ[NH₃]/Δt**
  *(Iska matlab: NH₃ ke banne ki speed N₂ ke kharch hone se **2 guna**, aur H₂ ke kharch hone ki speed N₂ se **3 guna** hoti hai!)*

## 2. Five Factors Affecting Rate of a Reaction

1. **Concentration of Reactants:** Zyada concentration ⟹ zyada collisions ⟹ tez rate!
2. **Temperature:** Har 10 °C badhne par lagbhag **2 se 3 guna** tez!
3. **Catalyst:** Activation energy kam karke reaction ko tez karta hai (bina khud kharch hue!).
4. **Surface Area (Solids):** Powdered solid (zyada surface area) bade tukde ke muqable kahin tez react karta hai!
5. **Nature of Reactants & Light:** Ionic reactions instant hoti hain; photochemical reactions (jaise H₂ + Cl₂) sunlight mein tez hoti hain.

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Stoichiometric Rates in 2 N₂O₅ → 4 NO₂ + O₂
For the decomposition **2 N₂O₅(g) → 4 NO₂(g) + O₂(g)**, the concentration of **NO₂** increases by **1.6 × 10⁻² M** in **4.0 seconds**. Calculate:
(a) the rate of formation of NO₂,
(b) the overall rate of the reaction, and
(c) the rate of disappearance of N₂O₅.

**Hal (Solution):**
- (a) Rate of formation of NO₂ = +Δ[NO₂]/Δt = (1.6 × 10⁻²) / 4.0 = **4.0 × 10⁻³ mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹**.
- (b) Overall Rate = +(1/4) Δ[NO₂]/Δt = (4.0 × 10⁻³) / 4 = **1.0 × 10⁻³ mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹**.
- (c) −(1/2) Δ[N₂O₅]/Δt = Rate ⟹ **Rate of disappearance of N₂O₅ = 2 × Rate = 2.0 × 10⁻³ mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹**.
- **Jaanch:** NO₂ forms 2× faster than N₂O₅ disappears (4 : 2 = 2 : 1) ✅.

### Example 2: Average Rate from Concentration Drop
The concentration of reactant R drops from **0.03 M** to **0.02 M** in **25 minutes**. Calculate the average rate of reaction in (a) **mol L⁻¹ min⁻¹**, and (b) **mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹**.

**Hal (Solution):**
- Δ[R] = 0.02 − 0.03 = −0.01 M.
- (a) r_avg = −(−0.01) / 25 = **4.0 × 10⁻⁴ mol L⁻¹ min⁻¹**.
- (b) 25 min = 1500 s ⟹ r_avg = 0.01 / 1500 ≈ **6.67 × 10⁻⁶ mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹**.
- **Jaanch:** 4.0 × 10⁻⁴ / 60 = 6.67 × 10⁻⁶ ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **Stoichiometric coefficient se multiply kar dena (divide karne ke bajay!):**
   - *Galat:* 2 A → B ke liye Rate = −2 Δ[A]/Δt likh dena!
   - *Sahi:* Hamesha coefficient se **DIVIDE** karo: **Rate = −(1/2) Δ[A]/Δt = +Δ[B]/Δt**!
2. **"Rate of reaction" aur "Rate of disappearance of A" ko ek hi maan lena:**
   - *Sahi:* Rate of disappearance of A = **−Δ[A]/Δt** (bina 1/a ke!); Overall Rate = **−(1/a) Δ[A]/Δt**!
3. **Rate ko negative value likh kar chhod dena:**
   - *Sahi:* Reaction rate hamesha **POSITIVE** hota hai!

## Practice Questions

1. Define rate of a chemical reaction and write its common unit for solutions and for gases.
2. Distinguish between average rate and instantaneous rate of a reaction.
3. For the reaction **2 A + B → 3 C**, write the rate of reaction in terms of Δ[A]/Δt, Δ[B]/Δt, and Δ[C]/Δt.
4. In the Haber process **N₂ + 3 H₂ → 2 NH₃**, if the rate of formation of NH₃ is 6.0 × 10⁻⁴ mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹, calculate (a) the rate of disappearance of H₂, and (b) the overall rate of reaction.
5. Why does powdered zinc react much faster with dilute acid (teacher demo) than a single large lump of zinc of the same mass?
6. For the reaction **2 HI → H₂ + I₂**, the concentration of HI decreases from 0.50 M to 0.40 M in 10 minutes. Calculate the average rate of the reaction in mol L⁻¹ min⁻¹.
7. List four factors that influence the rate of a chemical reaction.
8. Why is a minus sign placed before Δ[R]/Δt when expressing reaction rate in terms of a reactant?
9. State whether True or False: The rate of a reaction normally stays constant from start to finish.
10. For the reaction **4 NH₃ + 5 O₂ → 4 NO + 6 H₂O**, if the rate of formation of NO is 3.6 × 10⁻³ mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹, find the rate of formation of H₂O.

### Answers

1. Per unit time reactant concentration mein kami ya product concentration mein badhotri. Units: **mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹** (solutions) aur **atm s⁻¹** (gases).

2. **Average rate** ek finite time interval Δt par naapa jaata hai; **Instantaneous rate** kisi exact pal t par (graph ke tangent slope se) naapa jaata hai.

3. **Rate = −(1/2) Δ[A]/Δt = −Δ[B]/Δt = +(1/3) Δ[C]/Δt**.

4. (b) Overall Rate = +(1/2) Δ[NH₃]/Δt = (6.0 × 10⁻⁴)/2 = **3.0 × 10⁻⁴ mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹**.
   (a) Rate of disappearance of H₂ = 3 × Rate = **9.0 × 10⁻⁴ mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹**.
   Jaanch: H₂ : NH₃ = 3 : 2 ⟹ 6 × (3/2) = 9 × 10⁻⁴ ✅.

5. Powdered zinc ka **surface area** bahut zyada hota hai, jisse per second acid ke saath effective collisions kai guna badh jaati hain!

6. Rate = −(1/2) × (0.40 − 0.50) / 10 = (1/2) × (0.10 / 10) = **5.0 × 10⁻³ mol L⁻¹ min⁻¹**.
   Jaanch: Δ[HI]/Δt = 0.01; divide by 2 = 0.005 ✅.

7. Concentration of reactants, Temperature, Presence of a catalyst, aur Surface area of solid reactants.

8. Reactant ka concentration samay ke saath ghatta hai (Δ[R] negative hota hai); minus sign lagane se reaction rate **positive** ban jaata hai!

9. **False ❌.** Reactants kharch hone ke saath concentration ghatta hai, isliye reaction ka rate samay ke saath **dheema (decreases)** hota jaata hai (zero-order exception ko chhod kar!).

10. +(1/6) Δ[H₂O]/Δt = +(1/4) Δ[NO]/Δt ⟹ Δ[H₂O]/Δt = (6/4) × (3.6 × 10⁻³) = 1.5 × 3.6 × 10⁻³ = **5.4 × 10⁻³ mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹**.
    Jaanch: 3.6 × 1.5 = 5.4 ✅.
