## Naye Shabd (Key Terms)

| Term | Hinglish Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Isomers** | Do ya zyada compounds jinka chemical formula same ho par atoms ka linkage ya 3D arrangement alag ho |
| **Structural Isomers** | Woh isomers jinke chemical bonds (connectivity) hi alag hote hain (4 types: ionisation, solvate, linkage, coordination) |
| **Stereoisomers** | Woh isomers jinke bonds same hote hain, par ligands ka **3D spatial arrangement** alag hota hai (Geometrical + Optical) |
| **cis / trans Isomers** | Geometrical isomers: **cis** = identical ligands adjacent (90°); **trans** = identical ligands opposite (180°) |
| **fac / mer Isomers** | Octahedral Ma₃b₃ complexes mein: **facial (fac)** = 3 same ligands ek triangular face par; **meridional (mer)** = 3 same ligands ek meridian (arc) par |
| **Optical Isomers (Enantiomers)** | Non-superimposable mirror images jo plane-polarised light ko opposite directions (d / + aur l / −) mein rotate karti hain |
| **Chiral Complex** | Woh complex jismein koi plane of symmetry (POS) nahi hota aur jiski mirror image us par superimpose nahi hoti |

## 1. Structural Isomerism (4 Types)

### (a) Ionisation Isomerism
Jab counter ion aur coordination sphere ke andar ka anionic ligand aapas mein jagah badal lein → paani mein **alag-alag ions** dete hain!
- **Example:** [Co(NH₃)₅(SO₄)]Br (red-violet; AgNO₃ se pale yellow AgBr ppt deta hai) aur [Co(NH₃)₅Br]SO₄ (red; BaCl₂ se white BaSO₄ ppt deta hai).

### (b) Solvate (Hydrate) Isomerism
Ionisation isomerism ka special case jismein **solvent (paani, H₂O)** andar ligand ke roop mein ya bahar lattice water ke roop mein hota hai.
- **Classic Example (CrCl₃·6H₂O ke 3 isomers):**
  1. [Cr(H₂O)₆]Cl₃ (violet — 3 mol AgCl ppt)
  2. [Cr(H₂O)₅Cl]Cl₂·H₂O (blue-green — 2 mol AgCl ppt)
  3. [Cr(H₂O)₄Cl₂]Cl·2H₂O (dark green — 1 mol AgCl ppt)

### (c) Linkage Isomerism
Sirf un complexes mein hota hai jismein **ambidentate ligand** (jaise NO₂⁻, SCN⁻, CN⁻) ho!
- **Example:** [Co(NH₃)₅(NO₂)]Cl₂ (yellow, N-bonded: nitrito-N) aur [Co(NH₃)₅(ONO)]Cl₂ (red, O-bonded: nitrito-O).

### (d) Coordination Isomerism
Tab hota hai jab **cation aur anion dono hi complex ions** hon aur unke beech ligands ka exchange ho jaye!
- **Example:** [Co(NH₃)₆][Cr(CN)₆] aur [Cr(NH₃)₆][Co(CN)₆].

## 2. Geometrical Isomerism (cis–trans & fac–mer)

Yeh **CN = 4 (Square Planar)** aur **CN = 6 (Octahedral)** complexes mein paya jata hai.
- **IMPORTANT:** **Tetrahedral complexes (CN = 4) geometrical isomerism NAHI dikhate!** Kyun? Kyunki tetrahedron mein sabhi 4 positions ek-doosre se same angle (109.5°) par hoti hain — koi adjacent/opposite farq hi nahi hota!

### (a) Square Planar Complexes (CN = 4):
- **Ma₂b₂** (jaise [Pt(NH₃)₂Cl₂]): **2 geometrical isomers** — **cis** (two Cl at 90°) and **trans** (two Cl at 180°).
- **Ma₂bc:** **2 geometrical isomers** (cis and trans w.r.t. the two 'a' ligands).
- **Mabcd:** **3 geometrical isomers** (fix 'a' and place b, c, or d trans to it!).
- **Ma₄, Ma₃b:** No geometrical isomerism.

```
Square Planar Ma₂b₂:

    H₃N     Cl               H₃N     Cl
       \   /                    \   /
        Pt                       Pt
       /   \                    /   \
    H₃N     Cl               Cl      NH₃

   cis-isomer               trans-isomer
   (cisplatin)              (transplatin)
```

### (b) Octahedral Complexes (CN = 6):
- **Ma₄b₂** (jaise [Co(NH₃)₄Cl₂]⁺) aur **Ma₄bc:** **2 geometrical isomers** — **cis** (two b at 90°) and **trans** (two b at 180°).
- **M(AA)₂b₂** (jaise [Co(en)₂Cl₂]⁺, jahan AA = symmetrical bidentate ligand): **2 geometrical isomers** — **cis** and **trans**.
- **Ma₃b₃** (jaise [Co(NH₃)₃(NO₂)₃]): **2 geometrical isomers** —
  - **fac (facial):** Teeno 'a' ligands ek octahedral face ke 3 corners par (sabhi a–M–a angles = 90°).
  - **mer (meridional):** Teeno 'a' ligands ek plane/meridian par (ek a–M–a angle = 180°, do = 90°).

## 3. Optical Isomerism (Chirality in Octahedral Complexes)

Woh complexes jinki mirror image unke upar superimpose nahi hoti (**chiral** — no plane of symmetry), **dextrorotatory (d / +)** aur **laevorotatory (l / −)** enantiomers banate hain!

- **Square planar complexes** (jaise Ma₂b₂) aam taur par optically **inactive** hote hain kyunki poora molecule ek hi plane mein (molecular plane = plane of symmetry) hota hai.
- **Most common optically active octahedral complexes (with bidentate ligands):**
  1. **[M(AA)₃]** — jaise **[Co(en)₃]³⁺** aur **[Cr(ox)₃]³⁻**: **Optically active** (exists as d and l enantiomer pair; no geometrical isomers).
  2. **cis-[M(AA)₂b₂]** — jaise **cis-[Co(en)₂Cl₂]⁺**: **Optically active** (exists as d and l pair!).
  3. **trans-[M(AA)₂b₂]** — jaise **trans-[Co(en)₂Cl₂]⁺**: **Optically INACTIVE**! Kyunki ismein plane of symmetry hota hai (dono Cl upar-neeche, dono en rings ek hi equatorial plane mein!).
  4. **cis-[M(AA)₂bc]:** **Optically active** (d and l); **trans-[M(AA)₂bc]:** optically inactive.

Total stereoisomers of **[Co(en)₂Cl₂]⁺** = **3** (cis-d, cis-l, and trans)!

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Identifying the Type of Structural Isomerism
Identify the type of isomerism shown by each pair:
(a) [Co(NH₃)₅(SCN)]²⁺ and [Co(NH₃)₅(NCS)]²⁺
(b) [Pt(NH₃)₄Cl₂]Br₂ and [Pt(NH₃)₄Br₂]Cl₂
(c) [Cu(NH₃)₄][PtCl₄] and [Pt(NH₃)₄][CuCl₄]

**Solution:**
(a) **Linkage isomerism** — SCN⁻ is an ambidentate ligand binding via S in one and via N in the other.
(b) **Ionisation isomerism** — Cl⁻ and Br⁻ exchange positions between inside and outside the coordination sphere, giving different ions in water.
(c) **Coordination isomerism** — NH₃ and Cl⁻ ligands exchange between the cationic and anionic complex spheres.

Jaanch: Each pair has identical molecular formula and matches its exact structural class ✅

---

### Example 2: Counting Stereoisomers of [Co(en)₂Cl₂]⁺
Draw/describe all possible stereoisomers of [Co(en)₂Cl₂]⁺ and state which are optically active.

**Solution:**
- **Geometrical isomers:** 2 — **cis** (two Cl⁻ at 90°) and **trans** (two Cl⁻ at 180°).
- **Optical activity:**
  - **trans-[Co(en)₂Cl₂]⁺** has a horizontal plane of symmetry passing through Co and both en rings → **optically inactive** (1 isomer).
  - **cis-[Co(en)₂Cl₂]⁺** lacks any plane of symmetry → **optically active**, forming a non-superimposable mirror-image pair: **d-cis** and **l-cis** (2 isomers).
- **Total stereoisomers = 1 + 2 = 3.**

Jaanch: trans (1, achiral) + cis (2, d & l enantiomers) = 3 stereoisomers ✅

---

### Example 3: Why [Ni(NH₃)₂Cl₂] (Tetrahedral) Has No Geometrical Isomers
Explain why square planar [Pt(NH₃)₂Cl₂] shows cis–trans isomerism, whereas tetrahedral [Ni(NH₃)₂Cl₂] does not.

**Solution:**
In a square planar complex, the four ligand positions lie in one plane with two distinct bond angles (**90° adjacent = cis** and **180° opposite = trans**). In a tetrahedral complex, all four positions are equivalent with every L–M–L angle equal to **109.5°**, so any arrangement of two NH₃ and two Cl⁻ is identical — hence no geometrical isomerism.

Jaanch: Every pair of vertices in a regular tetrahedron is adjacent at 109.5° ✅

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **trans-[Co(en)₂Cl₂]⁺ ko bhi optically active maan lena:**
   - *Galat:* [Co(en)₂Cl₂]⁺ mein cis aur trans dono optical isomers banate hain (4 total).
   - *Sahi:* **Sirf cis-[Co(en)₂Cl₂]⁺ optically active hai!** trans form mein plane of symmetry hota hai, isliye total stereoisomers **3** hote hain (d-cis, l-cis, trans)!

2. **Tetrahedral Ma₂b₂ mein cis/trans draw kar dena:**
   - *Galat:* Tetrahedral [Zn(NH₃)₂Cl₂] ke cis aur trans isomers hote hain.
   - *Sahi:* Tetrahedral complexes mein saare angles 109.5° barabar hote hain → **NO geometrical isomerism!**

3. **fac/mer ko Ma₄b₂ par laga dena:**
   - *Galat:* [Co(NH₃)₄Cl₂]⁺ mein fac aur mer isomers hote hain.
   - *Sahi:* **fac aur mer** sirf **Ma₃b₃** type octahedral complexes mein hote hain; Ma₄b₂ mein **cis aur trans** hote hain!

## Quick Recap

- **4 Structural Isomers:** Ionisation (counter-ion swap), Solvate/Hydrate (H₂O swap), Linkage (ambidentate NO₂⁻/SCN⁻), Coordination (cation/anion complex ligand swap).
- **Geometrical Isomerism:** Square planar Ma₂b₂ (2), Mabcd (3); Octahedral Ma₄b₂ (cis/trans), Ma₃b₃ (fac/mer), M(AA)₂b₂ (cis/trans). **Never in tetrahedral!**
- **Optical Isomerism:** Requires chirality (no POS). Active: **[M(AA)₃]** (d/l) and **cis-[M(AA)₂b₂]** (d/l). Inactive: **trans-[M(AA)₂b₂]**.

## Practice Questions

4. Why do tetrahedral complexes of type Ma₂b₂ not exhibit geometrical isomerism?
5. How many geometrical isomers are possible for a square planar complex of type [Mabcd] (e.g., [Pt(NH₃)(Br)(Cl)(py)])?
6. Explain the difference between facial (fac) and meridional (mer) isomers with an example.
7. Out of cis-[Co(en)₂Cl₂]⁺ and trans-[Co(en)₂Cl₂]⁺, which one is optically active and why?
8. Write the total number of stereoisomers for: (a) [Co(en)₃]³⁺, (b) [Co(NH₃)₄Cl₂]⁺, (c) [Cr(en)₂Cl₂]⁺.
9. Identify the type of isomerism shown by [Cr(H₂O)₆]Cl₃ and [Cr(H₂O)₅Cl]Cl₂·H₂O. How many moles of AgCl does 1 mole of each give with excess AgNO₃?
10. Which isomer of [Pt(NH₃)₂Cl₂] is used in chemotherapy to inhibit tumour growth?

### Answers

1. **Ionisation isomerism**, **Solvate (Hydrate) isomerism**, **Linkage isomerism**, and **Coordination isomerism**.
2. Treat aqueous solutions separately with AgNO₃ and BaCl₂:
   - **[Co(NH₃)₅(SO₄)]Br** gives a pale yellow precipitate of **AgBr** with AgNO₃ (no ppt with BaCl₂).
   - **[Co(NH₃)₅Br]SO₄** gives a white precipitate of **BaSO₄** with BaCl₂ (no ppt with AgNO₃).
3. Linkage isomer: **[Co(NH₃)₅(ONO)]Cl₂**.
   - [Co(NH₃)₅(NO₂)]Cl₂: **pentaamminenitrito-N-cobalt(III) chloride**
   - [Co(NH₃)₅(ONO)]Cl₂: **pentaamminenitrito-O-cobalt(III) chloride**
4. Because all four ligand positions in a regular tetrahedron are equivalent (every bond angle is 109.5°), so no adjacent vs opposite distinction exists.
5. **3 geometrical isomers** (fix one ligand, say NH₃, and place Br, Cl, or py trans to it).
6. In octahedral **Ma₃b₃** (e.g., [Co(NH₃)₃(NO₂)₃]):
   - **fac (facial):** Three identical ligands occupy three adjacent corners forming one triangular face of the octahedron (all angles 90°).
   - **mer (meridional):** Three identical ligands lie around the meridian of the octahedron (two are 180° trans to each other).
7. **cis-[Co(en)₂Cl₂]⁺** is optically active because it lacks a plane of symmetry (chiral, forms non-superimposable d and l mirror images). **trans-[Co(en)₂Cl₂]⁺** has a plane of symmetry and is optically inactive.
8. (a) [Co(en)₃]³⁺: **2** (d and l optical isomers; 0 geometrical).
   (b) [Co(NH₃)₄Cl₂]⁺: **2** (cis and trans; both achiral).
   (c) [Cr(en)₂Cl₂]⁺: **3** (d-cis, l-cis, and trans).
   Jaanch: Counts verified for all three symmetry classes ✅
9. **Hydrate (solvate) isomerism**. [Cr(H₂O)₆]Cl₃ precipitates **3 moles of AgCl**; [Cr(H₂O)₅Cl]Cl₂·H₂O precipitates **2 moles of AgCl**.
10. **cis-[Pt(NH₃)₂Cl₂]** (**cisplatin**).
