## TV Remote Cell, Car Battery, Spacecraft Fuel Cell aur Zang!

**Kanta** ne ghar aur science museum mein chaar alag electrochemical systems dekhe: TV remote ka sasta **Dry Cell** (jo khatam hone par phenkna padta hai), car ki rechargeable **Lead-Storage Battery**, Apollo spacecraft ka pollution-free **H₂–O₂ Fuel Cell** (jiska by-product peene layak **pure paani** tha!), aur baarish mein bheeg kar zang laga lohe ka gate (**Corrosion**)!

Aaj hum in chaaron ke exact anode/cathode reactions aur corrosion se bachav ke tareeke seekhenge!

---

## Naye Shabd (New Words)

- **Primary Battery:** Non-rechargeable cell jismein reaction sirf ek baar hoti hai (e.g., Dry cell, Mercury cell).
- **Secondary Battery:** Rechargeable cell jise bahar se reverse current dekar wapas charge kiya ja sakta hai (e.g., Lead-storage battery, Ni-Cd cell, Li-ion battery).
- **Fuel Cell:** Aisa galvanic cell jo fuel (H₂, CH₄) ke combustion ki energy ko seedha electricity mein badalta hai (~70% efficiency, zero pollution!).
- **Corrosion (Rusting):** Metal ka hawa aur nami (O₂ + H₂O) ke saath dheere-dheere electrochemical oxidation hokar kharaab hona (Iron → Rust **Fe₂O₃·xH₂O**).

---

## 1. Primary Batteries (Use Once!)

1. **Dry Cell (Leclanché Cell — ~1.5 V, clocks & remotes):**
   - **Anode:** Zinc container (**Zn → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻**)
   - **Cathode:** Carbon (graphite) rod surrounded by powdered **MnO₂ + C**
   - **Electrolyte:** Moist paste of **NH₄Cl + ZnCl₂**
   - **Cathode reaction:** **MnO₂ + NH₄⁺ + e⁻ → MnO(OH) + NH₃** (NH₃ forms complex [Zn(NH₃)₄]²⁺ with Zn²⁺!).
2. **Mercury Cell (~1.35 V — watches, hearing aids):**
   - **Anode:** Zn-Hg amalgam; **Cathode:** Paste of HgO + C; **Electrolyte:** KOH + ZnO paste.
   - **Overall reaction:** **Zn(Hg) + HgO(s) → ZnO(s) + Hg(l)**.
   - **Special Advantage:** Overall reaction mein **koi aqueous ion involved nahi hai** jiska concentration badle — isliye iska **voltage poori life exact constant (1.35 V)** rehta hai!

---

## 2. Secondary Batteries (Rechargeable!)

### Lead Storage Battery (Car / Inverter — 6 cells × 2 V = 12 V):
- **Anode:** Lead grid (**Pb**); **Cathode:** Lead grid packed with **PbO₂**; **Electrolyte:** **38% H₂SO₄** solution (acidic — never open a car battery!).
- **Discharging Reactions:**
  - Anode: **Pb(s) + SO₄²⁻(aq) → PbSO₄(s) + 2e⁻**
  - Cathode: **PbO₂(s) + SO₄²⁻(aq) + 4 H⁺(aq) + 2e⁻ → PbSO₄(s) + 2 H₂O(l)**
  - **Overall (Discharging):** **Pb + PbO₂ + 2 H₂SO₄ → 2 PbSO₄ + 2 H₂O**
  *(Discharging mein H₂SO₄ consume hota hai ⟹ electrolyte ki density ghatti hai!)*
- **Charging:** Bahar se reverse voltage lagane par upar wali poori reaction **exact ULTI** chalti hai — **PbSO₄** wapas **Pb** aur **PbO₂** ban jaata hai aur H₂SO₄ regenerate ho jaata hai!

---

## 3. Hydrogen–Oxygen Fuel Cell (Apollo Space Programme!)

Porous carbon electrodes (with Pt/Pd catalyst) aur aqueous **NaOH / KOH** electrolyte mein H₂ aur O₂ gases lagataar bheji jaati hain:
- **Anode:** **2 H₂(g) + 4 OH⁻(aq) → 4 H₂O(l) + 4e⁻**
- **Cathode:** **O₂(g) + 2 H₂O(l) + 4e⁻ → 4 OH⁻(aq)**
- **Overall:** **2 H₂(g) + O₂(g) → 2 H₂O(l)**!
- **3 Big Advantages:** (1) High efficiency (**~70%** vs ~40% thermal power plants), (2) Continuous power jab tak H₂/O₂ supply chal rahi ho, (3) **100% pollution-free** (product sirf pure water!).

## 4. Corrosion (Rusting of Iron as an Electrochemical Cell!)

Lohe par paani ki boond (jismein CO₂ ghul kar H⁺ ions banati hai) ek **mini galvanic cell** bana deti hai:
- **Anode (Iron dissolves):** **2 Fe(s) → 2 Fe²⁺(aq) + 4e⁻** (E° = −0.44 V)
- **Cathode (Oxygen reduced in acidic water):** **O₂(g) + 4 H⁺(aq) + 4e⁻ → 2 H₂O(l)** (E° = +1.23 V)
- **Overall:** **2 Fe + O₂ + 4 H⁺ → 2 Fe²⁺ + 2 H₂O** (E°_cell = **+1.67 V** — highly spontaneous!)
- Baad mein Fe²⁺ hawa ki O₂ se oxidise hokar **Rust (Fe₂O₃·xH₂O)** bana leta hai!

### Prevention of Corrosion:
1. **Barrier Protection:** Paint, oil, grease, ya non-corroding coating (e.g., bisphenol).
2. **Galvanisation:** Iron par **Zinc (Zn)** ki parat chadhana!
3. **Sacrificial Protection (Cathodic Protection):** Iron pipes/ship hulls ko zyada reactive metal (**Mg** ya **Zn**, jinka E° Iron se kam hai!) ke blocks se jodna — Mg/Zn khud oxidise (sacrifice) hokar Iron ko bacha lete hain!

## Solved Conceptual Examples

### Example 1: Why Mercury Cell Maintains Constant Voltage
Why does the cell potential of a **mercury cell** remain constant (1.35 V) throughout its entire lifetime, unlike a dry cell?

**Hal (Solution):**
- Mercury cell ki overall reaction **Zn(Hg) + HgO(s) → ZnO(s) + Hg(l)** mein sirf pure solids aur liquids hain — **koi bhi ion solution mein nahi** jiska concentration samay ke saath badle! Nernst equation mein Q = 1 (constant) ⟹ **E_cell = constant (1.35 V)**!
- **Jaanch:** Dry cell mein NH₄⁺ aur Zn²⁺ ke concentrations badalte hain, isliye uska voltage dheere-dheere girta hai ✅.

### Example 2: Why Galvanised Iron Still Resists Rust Even When Scratched
If the Zinc coating on a galvanised iron sheet gets scratched, exposing the iron underneath to rain, why does the iron **still not rust**?

**Hal (Solution):**
- Zinc ka standard reduction potential (**E° = −0.76 V**) Iron (**E° = −0.44 V**) se **kam (more negative)** hai!
- Isliye scratch hone par bhi **Zinc anode** bankar khud oxidise (sacrifice) hota hai aur exposed Iron ko **cathode** banakar rusting se bachaye rakhta hai!
- **Jaanch:** More reactive metal acts as sacrificial anode ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **Lead-storage battery mein charging aur discharging reactions ko ulta likh dena:**
   - *Sahi:* **Discharging:** Pb + PbO₂ + 2 H₂SO₄ → 2 PbSO₄ + 2 H₂O (H₂SO₄ kharch hota hai!). **Charging:** Exact reverse!
2. **Fuel cell ko rechargeable secondary battery bol dena:**
   - *Sahi:* Fuel cell mein chemicals andar store nahi hote — bahar se **lagataar H₂ aur O₂ fuel supply** kiya jaata hai!
3. **Sacrificial anode ke liye Copper (Cu) chun lena:**
   - *Galat:* Iron ko bachane ke liye Copper block jodna!
   - *Sahi:* Copper (E° = +0.34 V) Iron (−0.44 V) se **kam reactive** hai — Copper jodne par Iron aur **TEZI se rust** karega! Hamesha **Mg (−2.36 V)** ya **Zn (−0.76 V)** chuno!

## Quick Recap

| System | Anode / Cathode | Overall Reaction / Key Fact |
|---|---|---|
| Dry Cell (1.5 V) | Zn / Carbon + MnO₂ | Primary; paste of NH₄Cl + ZnCl₂ |
| Mercury Cell (1.35 V) | Zn-Hg / HgO + C | **Constant voltage** (no ions in overall reaction!) |
| Lead Storage (12 V) | Pb / PbO₂ (in 38% H₂SO₄) | Discharging: Pb + PbO₂ + 2H₂SO₄ → 2PbSO₄ + 2H₂O |
| H₂–O₂ Fuel Cell | Porous C + Pt/Pd (KOH) | 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O; ~70% efficient, zero pollution |
| Rusting of Iron | Fe → Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻ / O₂ + 4H⁺ + 4e⁻ → 2H₂O | Rust = Fe₂O₃·xH₂O; Prevented by **Zn/Mg sacrificial anode** |

## Practice Questions

1. Distinguish between primary batteries and secondary batteries with one example of each.
2. Write the anode and cathode reactions occurring during the **discharging** of a lead storage battery.
3. What happens to the concentration (and density) of H₂SO₄ in a lead storage battery while it is being **discharged**?
4. Why does a mercury cell give a constant voltage of 1.35 V throughout its working life?
5. Write the anode, cathode, and overall reactions of an **H₂–O₂ fuel cell**, and state two of its major advantages over thermal power plants.
6. Write the electrochemical reactions at the anodic and cathodic spots during the **rusting of iron**.
7. Why is the rusting of iron faster in **saline (salty) water** or **acidic rain** than in pure distilled water?
8. Explain how **galvanisation** (coating with Zinc) protects iron from rusting even after the zinc layer is scratched.
9. State whether True or False: Connecting a block of Copper (E° = +0.34 V) to an underground iron pipe (E° = −0.44 V) will protect the iron pipe from corrosion.
10. Suggest two metals other than Zinc that can be used for **cathodic (sacrificial) protection** of iron structures.

### Answers

1. **Primary battery:** Non-rechargeable, ek baar use hoti hai (e.g., Dry cell, Mercury cell). **Secondary battery:** Reverse current se baar-baar recharge ho sakti hai (e.g., Lead storage battery, Ni-Cd).

2. **Anode:** Pb(s) + SO₄²⁻ → PbSO₄(s) + 2e⁻.
   **Cathode:** PbO₂(s) + SO₄²⁻ + 4H⁺ + 2e⁻ → PbSO₄(s) + 2H₂O.

3. Discharging mein H₂SO₄ consume hota hai aur H₂O banta hai ⟹ H₂SO₄ ka concentration aur density **ghat (decrease)** jaati hai!

4. Overall reaction **Zn(Hg) + HgO(s) → ZnO(s) + Hg(l)** mein koi aqueous ion involved nahi hota jiska concentration badle — isliye voltage poori life constant (1.35 V) rehta hai!

5. Anode: 2H₂ + 4OH⁻ → 4H₂O + 4e⁻; Cathode: O₂ + 2H₂O + 4e⁻ → 4OH⁻; Overall: **2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O**. Advantages: (i) High efficiency (~70%), (ii) Pollution-free (only water is produced!).

6. Anode: **2 Fe → 2 Fe²⁺ + 4e⁻**; Cathode: **O₂ + 4 H⁺ + 4e⁻ → 2 H₂O**; then Fe²⁺ oxidises to **Fe₂O₃·xH₂O (rust)**.

7. Salty water mein Na⁺/Cl⁻ ions solution ki **conductivity** badha dete hain, aur acidic rain zyada **H⁺ ions** deti hai jo cathode reaction ko tez kar dete hain!

8. Zinc ka E° (−0.76 V) Iron (−0.44 V) se zyada negative hai ⟹ scratch hone par bhi Zinc **sacrificial anode** bankar khud oxidise hota hai aur Iron ko safe rakhta hai!

9. **False ❌.** Copper ka E° Iron se zyada positive hai — Copper jodne par Iron anode ban jayega aur aur **zyada tezi se rust** hoga!

10. **Magnesium (Mg, E° = −2.36 V)** aur **Aluminium (Al, E° = −1.66 V)** (dono ka E° Iron se zyada negative hai!).
