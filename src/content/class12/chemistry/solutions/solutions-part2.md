## Naye Shabd (New Words)

- **Solubility:** Ek fixed temperature par 100 g solvent mein solute ki ghul sakne wali **maximum maatra** (saturated solution).
- **Dissolution vs Crystallisation:** Solute ka solvent mein ghulna (dissolution) aur wapas solid bankar alag hona (crystallisation) — jab dono rates barabar hon to **Dynamic Equilibrium** (saturated solution) banta hai!
- **Henry's Law:** Constant temperature par kisi liquid mein gas ki solubility (mole fraction **x**), liquid ki satah ke upar us gas ke **partial pressure (p)** ke directly proportional hoti hai: **p = K_H x**.
- **Henry's Law Constant (K_H):** Har gas-solvent pair ke liye constant (unit: **bar** ya **atm**). **Jitna BADA K_H, utni KAM solubility!**

---

## 1. Solubility of Solids in Liquids

1. **Nature ("Like Dissolves Like"):** Polar solutes (NaCl, sugar) polar solvents (water) mein ghulte hain; non-polar solutes (naphthalene, anthracene) non-polar solvents (benzene) mein ghulte hain!
2. **Effect of Temperature (Le Chatelier's Principle):**
   - **Endothermic dissolution (ΔH_sol > 0, e.g., NaNO₃, KCl):** Temperature badhane par solubility **BADHTI hai**!
   - **Exothermic dissolution (ΔH_sol < 0, e.g., NaOH, Ce₂(SO₄)₃):** Temperature badhane par solubility **GHATTI hai**!
3. **Effect of Pressure:** Solids aur liquids lagbhag incompressible hote hain ⟹ pressure ka **koi khaas asar nahi** padta!

---

## 2. Solubility of Gases in Liquids — Henry's Law

- **Effect of Temperature:** Gas ka paani mein ghulna hamesha **Exothermic (ΔH < 0)** hota hai ⟹ Temperature badhane par gas ki solubility **GHATTI hai**! *(Isliye machhliyan garm paani ke muqable **thande paani** mein zyada aaram se saans leti hain — thande paani mein zyada O₂ ghuli hoti hai!)*
- **Effect of Pressure — Henry's Law:**

**p = K_H × x**   ⟹   **x = p / K_H**

- **Key Insight:** Same pressure par **higher K_H ⟹ lower solubility (x)**! Aur temperature badhne par **K_H badhta hai** ⟹ solubility ghatti hai!

### Henry's Law ke 3 Real-World Applications:
1. **Carbonated Soft Drinks:** CO₂ ki solubility badhane ke liye bottles ko **high pressure** par seal kiya jaata hai!
2. **Scuba Diving (Preventing "Bends"):** Samundar ki gahrai mein high pressure se blood mein bahut zyada N₂ ghul jaati hai. Upar aate hi pressure ghatne se blood mein **N₂ ke bubbles** ban jaate hain (**Bends** — bahut dardnak aur khatarnak!). Isse bachne ke liye divers ke tanks mein **Helium (~11.7%)** milaya jaata hai (Helium ka K_H bahut zyada ⟹ blood mein bahut kam ghulti hai!).
3. **High-Altitude Anoxia:** Oonche pahadon par O₂ ka partial pressure kam hota hai ⟹ blood mein kam O₂ ghulti hai ⟹ climbers ko kamzori aur sochne mein dikkat (**Anoxia**) hoti hai!

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Solubility of N₂ Gas in Water using Henry's Law
If N₂ gas is bubbled through water at 293 K, how many **millimoles** of N₂ gas dissolve in **1 litre of water**? Assume N₂ exerts a partial pressure of **0.987 bar** and **K_H for N₂ at 293 K = 76.48 kbar**.

**Hal (Solution):**
- p = 0.987 bar, K_H = 76.48 kbar = **76,480 bar**.
- Henry's law: **x(N₂) = p / K_H = 0.987 / 76480 ≈ 1.29 × 10⁻⁵**.
- 1 litre water = 1000 g ⟹ **n_water = 1000 / 18 = 55.5 mol**.
- Kyunki n(N₂) << 55.5 mol: x(N₂) ≈ n(N₂) / 55.5 ⟹
  **n(N₂) = (1.29 × 10⁻⁵) × 55.5 ≈ 7.16 × 10⁻⁴ mol = 0.716 mmol**!
- **Jaanch:** 0.987 / 76480 ≈ 1.2905 × 10⁻⁵; × 55.5 ≈ 0.716 mmol ✅.

### Example 2: Comparing Solubilities from K_H Values
At 298 K, the Henry's law constants in water for gases A, B, and C are **40 kbar**, **1.5 kbar**, and **80 kbar** respectively. Arrange them in order of **increasing solubility** in water at the same pressure.

**Hal (Solution):**
- x = p / K_H ⟹ **Higher K_H = Lower Solubility**!
- Increasing solubility order: **C (80 kbar) < A (40 kbar) < B (1.5 kbar)**!
- **Jaanch:** Sabse chhota K_H (B = 1.5 kbar) sabse zyada soluble hai ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **Bade K_H ko zyada solubility maan lena:**
   - *Galat:* K_H zyada hai to gas zyada ghulegi!
   - *Sahi:* **x = p / K_H** — K_H denominator mein hai ⟹ **Bada K_H = KAM solubility**!
2. **Temperature badhane par gas ki solubility badha dena:**
   - *Galat:* Cheeni ki tarah gas bhi garam paani mein zyada ghulti hai!
   - *Sahi:* Gas ka ghulna exothermic hota hai ⟹ garmi badhane par gas ki solubility **GHATTI hai**!
3. **1 L water mein n_water = 1000 mol rakh dena:**
   - *Sahi:* 1 L water = 1000 g ⟹ **n_water = 1000 / 18 = 55.55 mol**!

## Quick Recap

| Concept | Rule / Formula | Everyday Link |
|---|---|---|
| Solid in Liquid | "Like dissolves like"; ΔH > 0 → T↑ S↑ | Pressure has almost NO effect |
| Gas in Liquid (Henry's Law) | **p = K_H x** (T↑ → K_H↑ → S↓) | Higher K_H = Lower solubility |
| 3 Applications | Soda fizz, Scuba tanks (He-O₂), Altitude anoxia | Aquatic life prefers **cold** water (more O₂!) |

## Practice Questions

1. State Henry's law and write its mathematical expression. What is the SI/common unit of K_H?
2. Why are aquatic species (fish) more comfortable in cold water than in warm water?
3. Explain why scuba divers' breathing tanks are diluted with Helium gas.
4. What is **anoxia**, and why do mountaineers experience it at high altitudes?
5. At 298 K, K_H for CO₂ in water is 1.67 × 10⁸ Pa (= 1.67 kbar). Calculate the quantity of CO₂ in 500 mL of soda water when packed under 2.5 atm (≈ 2.53 × 10⁵ Pa) CO₂ pressure. (Use n_water = 27.78 mol.)
6. Why does pressure have almost no effect on the solubility of a solid in a liquid?
7. At a given temperature, K_H for O₂ is 34.86 kbar and for He is 144.97 kbar in water. Which gas is more soluble in water?
8. State two conditions under which Henry's law is NOT strictly obeyed (limitations of Henry's law).
9. State whether True or False: Increasing the temperature of water increases the value of Henry's law constant K_H for a dissolved gas.
10. H₂S (a toxic gas — theory only!) has solubility 0.195 m in water at STP (p = 0.987 bar). Calculate Henry's law constant K_H in bar (1 kg water = 55.55 mol).

### Answers

1. **Henry's Law:** Constant temperature par liquid mein gas ka mole fraction (x) uske partial pressure (p) ke directly proportional hota hai: **p = K_H x**. Unit of K_H: **bar, atm, ya Pa** (pressure ki hi unit, kyunki x unitless hai!).

2. Gas ka paani mein ghulna exothermic hai ⟹ thande paani mein **K_H kam** aur dissolved **O₂ ki solubility zyada** hoti hai, isliye machhliyan thande paani mein aaram se saans leti hain!

3. High underwater pressure par zyada N₂ blood mein ghul jaati hai jo upar aane par bubbles (**bends**) banati hai. Helium ka K_H bahut zyada hone se woh blood mein bahut kam ghulti hai aur bends se bachati hai!

4. **Anoxia:** High altitudes par oxygen ka partial pressure kam hone se blood aur tissues mein O₂ ki kami ho jaana — jisse climbers kamzor mehsoos karte hain aur saaf soch nahi paate.

5. x(CO₂) = p / K_H = (2.53 × 10⁵) / (1.67 × 10⁸) ≈ **1.515 × 10⁻³**.
   n(CO₂) ≈ x × n_water = (1.515 × 10⁻³) × 27.78 ≈ **0.0421 mol**.
   Mass of CO₂ = 0.0421 × 44 ≈ **1.85 g**.
   Jaanch: 2.53 / 1.67 ≈ 1.515; 1.515 × 27.78 × 44 × 10⁻³ ≈ 1.85 g ✅.

6. Solids aur liquids lagbhag **incompressible** hote hain (pressure se unke volume par koi farq nahi padta).

7. **O₂** zyada soluble hai — kyunki iska K_H (34.86 kbar) Helium (144.97 kbar) se bahut **kam** hai!

8. Henry's law tab fail hota hai jab: (i) pressure bahut **zyada** ya temperature bahut **kam** ho; (ii) gas solvent ke saath **chemically react** ya dissociate kar le (jaise NH₃ ya HCl paani mein!).

9. **True ✅.** Temperature badhne par solubility x ghatti hai ⟹ **K_H = p / x badhta hai**!

10. 0.195 m = 0.195 mol H₂S in 55.55 mol water ⟹ total moles = 55.745 mol.
    x = 0.195 / 55.745 ≈ 3.498 × 10⁻³.
    **K_H = p / x = 0.987 / (3.498 × 10⁻³) ≈ 282 bar**.
    Jaanch: 282 × 0.0035 ≈ 0.987 bar ✅.
