## Ethanol-Water vs Acetone-Chloroform ka Vapour Pressure Khel!

Chemistry class mein **Vipin** ne teacher ma'am ke board par do ajeeb mixing experiments dekhe:
- Jab **n-hexane** aur **n-heptane** milaye gaye, to na solution garam hua, na thanda (ΔH_mix = 0), aur total volume exact sum ke barabar raha (ΔV_mix = 0)!
- Par jab **Acetone** aur **Chloroform** milaye gaye, to beaker halka **garam** ho gaya (ΔH_mix < 0) aur vapour pressure ummeed se **kam** aaya!

Teacher ma'am boli: "Yeh sab **Raoult's Law** aur **Intermolecular Forces (A–B vs A–A, B–B)** ka khel hai! Aaj hum **Ideal Solutions**, **Non-Ideal Solutions (+ve & −ve deviations)** aur **Azeotropes** seekhenge!"

---

## Naye Shabd (New Words)

- **Vapour Pressure (p):** Bandh bartan mein constant temperature par liquid ke saath equilibrium mein maujood uski vapour dwara lagaya gaya pressure.
- **Raoult's Law (Volatile Liquids):** Solution mein har volatile component ka partial vapour pressure uske pure vapour pressure (p°) aur mole fraction (x) ke product ke barabar hota hai: **p_A = p_A° x_A**.
- **Ideal Solution:** Aisa solution jo har concentration par Raoult's law ko exact follow kare (**ΔH_mix = 0**, **ΔV_mix = 0**, A–B forces ≈ A–A & B–B forces).
- **Azeotrope (Constant Boiling Mixture):** Aisa binary liquid mixture jiski liquid aur vapour phase mein **composition bilkul SAME** hoti hai aur jo ek fixed constant temperature par boil hota hai (fractional distillation se alag **nahi** ho sakta!).

---

## 1. Raoult's Law for Two Volatile Liquids (A and B)

Maan lo do volatile liquids A aur B (pure vapour pressures **p_A°** aur **p_B°**) ke mole fractions **x_A** aur **x_B** (**x_A + x_B = 1**) hain:
- **p_A = p_A° x_A**   aur   **p_B = p_B° x_B**
- Dalton's law of partial pressures se **Total Vapour Pressure**:

**P_total = p_A + p_B = p_A° x_A + p_B° x_B = p_B° + (p_A° − p_B°) x_A**

*(Note: Raoult's law is a special case of Henry's law jahan K_H = p°!)*

---

## 2. Ideal vs Non-Ideal Solutions (+ve & −ve Deviations)

| Feature | Ideal Solution | Positive (+ve) Deviation | Negative (−ve) Deviation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Intermolecular Forces** | A–B ≈ A–A, B–B | **A–B WEAKER** than A–A, B–B (molecules escape easily!) | **A–B STRONGER** than A–A, B–B (molecules held tightly!) |
| **Vapour Pressure** | P = p_A° x_A + p_B° x_B | **P > Raoult's value** | **P < Raoult's value** |
| **ΔH_mix** | **0** | **> 0 (Endothermic — cools!)** | **< 0 (Exothermic — warms!)** |
| **ΔV_mix** | **0** | **> 0 (Expands!)** | **< 0 (Contracts!)** |
| **Azeotrope Type** | None (separable!) | **Minimum-boiling azeotrope** | **Maximum-boiling azeotrope** |
| **Classic Examples** | n-Hexane + n-Heptane; Benzene + Toluene; Bromoethane + Chloroethane | **Ethanol + Acetone**; **Ethanol + Water (95%!)**; CS₂ + Acetone | **Chloroform + Acetone** (new H-bond!); **HNO₃ + Water (68%)**; Phenol + Aniline |

*(Kyun Chloroform + Acetone mein −ve deviation hota hai? Kyunki CHCl₃ ka H aur Acetone ka O milkar naya **Hydrogen Bond** bana lete hain — jisse molecules ka bhaap banna mushkil ho jaata hai aur P ghat jaata hai!)*

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Ideal Solution Total Vapour Pressure
At 300 K, the vapour pressures of pure benzene (**p_A° = 100 mm Hg**) and pure toluene (**p_B° = 40 mm Hg**) form an ideal solution. Calculate the total vapour pressure of a mixture containing **2 mol of benzene** and **3 mol of toluene**.

**Hal (Solution):**
- Total moles = 2 + 3 = 5 mol ⟹ **x_A = 2/5 = 0.4**, **x_B = 3/5 = 0.6**.
- p_A = 100 × 0.4 = **40 mm Hg**; p_B = 40 × 0.6 = **24 mm Hg**.
- **P_total = 40 + 24 = 64 mm Hg**.
- **Jaanch:** Formula check: 40 + (100 − 40)(0.4) = 40 + 24 = 64 mm Hg ✅.

### Example 2: Identifying Deviation Type
When Ethanol and Acetone are mixed, why does the solution show **positive deviation** from Raoult's law?

**Hal (Solution):**
- Pure ethanol molecules ke beech strong **Hydrogen Bonding** hoti hai. Acetone milane par acetone molecules ethanol ke beech ghus kar unke H-bonds tod dete hain ⟹ naye ethanol-acetone interactions **kamzor (weaker)** hote hain ⟹ molecules zyada aasaani se vapour bante hain ⟹ **P > P_ideal (+ve deviation)**!
- **Jaanch:** Weaker A–B forces ⟹ higher P, ΔH_mix > 0, minimum-boiling azeotrope ✅.

---

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **+ve deviation ko Maximum-boiling azeotrope se jod dena:**
   - *Galat:* Positive deviation = maximum-boiling azeotrope!
   - *Sahi:* Bilkul ULTA! +ve deviation mein **vapour pressure ZYADA** hota hai ⟹ liquid jaldi boil hota hai ⟹ **MINIMUM-boiling azeotrope**! (−ve deviation → **MAXIMUM-boiling azeotrope**!)
2. **Chloroform + Acetone ko +ve deviation bol dena:**
   - *Sahi:* Chloroform + Acetone mein naya **H-bond** banta hai ⟹ stronger attraction ⟹ **Negative (−ve) deviation**!
3. **Azeotrope ko fractional distillation se alag karwana:**
   - *Sahi:* Azeotrope ke components ko **fractional distillation se alag NAHI kiya ja sakta**, kyunki liquid aur vapour mein unka ratio bilkul same hota hai!

## Quick Recap

| Concept | Key Formula / Fact |
|---|---|
| Raoult's Law | p_A = p_A° x_A; P_total = p_B° + (p_A° − p_B°) x_A |
| Ideal Solution | ΔH_mix = 0, ΔV_mix = 0 (Benzene + Toluene, Hexane + Heptane) |
| +ve Deviation | A–B weaker; P↑, ΔH > 0, ΔV > 0 → **Min-boiling azeotrope** (Ethanol + Water/Acetone) |
| −ve Deviation | A–B stronger; P↓, ΔH < 0, ΔV < 0 → **Max-boiling azeotrope** (CHCl₃ + Acetone, HNO₃ + H₂O) |

## Practice Questions

1. State Raoult's law for a solution of two volatile liquids.
2. What are the thermodynamic conditions (ΔH_mix, ΔV_mix) and molecular interaction conditions for an **ideal solution**? Give two examples.
3. Why does a mixture of **chloroform and acetone** show negative deviation from Raoult's law?
4. Define an **azeotrope**. Why can an azeotropic mixture not be separated into pure components by fractional distillation?
5. Distinguish between minimum-boiling azeotropes and maximum-boiling azeotropes with one example of each.
6. Pure liquids A (p_A° = 450 mm Hg) and B (p_B° = 700 mm Hg) form an ideal solution at 350 K. Calculate the total vapour pressure when x_A = 0.40.
7. In Question 6, find the composition (x_A and x_B) of the liquid mixture if the total vapour pressure is 600 mm Hg.
8. Vapour pressure of pure chloroform (CHCl₃, 119.5 g/mol) is 200 mm Hg and dichloromethane (CH₂Cl₂, 85 g/mol) is 415 mm Hg at 298 K. Find the total vapour pressure of an ideal mixture of 25.5 g CH₂Cl₂ and 40 g CHCl₃ (use 25.5/85 = 0.30 mol, 40/119.5 ≈ 0.335 mol).
9. State whether True or False: Raoult's law becomes a special case of Henry's law when the Henry's constant K_H equals the vapour pressure of the pure component p°.
10. When 50 mL of liquid X and 50 mL of liquid Y are mixed, the final volume is 98 mL and the mixture becomes warm. What type of deviation from Raoult's law does this mixture show?

### Answers

1. **Raoult's Law:** Volatile liquids ke solution mein har component ka partial vapour pressure uske mole fraction ke directly proportional hota hai: **p_i = p_i° x_i**.

2. **ΔH_mix = 0**, **ΔV_mix = 0**, aur A–B forces ≈ A–A & B–B forces. Examples: **Benzene + Toluene**, **n-Hexane + n-Heptane**.

3. Chloroform (CHCl₃) ka H atom aur Acetone ((CH₃)₂C=O) ka O atom milkar naya **Hydrogen Bond** bana lete hain ⟹ A–B attraction strong ho jaata hai ⟹ vapour pressure ghat jaata hai (−ve deviation)!

4. **Azeotrope:** Aisa constant-boiling liquid mixture jiski liquid aur vapour phase mein composition bilkul same hoti hai. Kyunki bhaap mein bhi wahi ratio nikalta hai jo liquid mein hai, fractional distillation se dono components alag nahi ho sakte!

5. **Minimum-boiling azeotrope** (+ve deviation): **95% Ethanol + 5% Water** (boils below both pure liquids!). **Maximum-boiling azeotrope** (−ve deviation): **68% HNO₃ + 32% Water** (boils above both!).

6. x_A = 0.40 ⟹ x_B = 0.60:
   P_total = 450(0.40) + 700(0.60) = 180 + 420 = **600 mm Hg**.
   Jaanch: 180 + 420 = 600 mm Hg ✅.

7. Question 6 mein dekha ki P_total = 600 mm Hg exact **x_A = 0.40, x_B = 0.60** par aata hai!
   Jaanch: 700 + (450 − 700) x_A = 600 ⟹ 250 x_A = 100 ⟹ x_A = 0.40 ✅.

8. n(CH₂Cl₂) = 0.30 mol, n(CHCl₃) = 0.335 mol, total = 0.635 mol.
   x(CH₂Cl₂) = 0.30 / 0.635 ≈ 0.472; x(CHCl₃) = 1 − 0.472 = 0.528.
   P_total = 415(0.472) + 200(0.528) = 195.9 + 105.6 = **301.5 mm Hg**.
   Jaanch: Lies between 200 and 415 mm Hg ✅.

9. **True ✅.** Henry's law p = K_H x mein K_H = p° rakhne par seedha Raoult's law p = p° x mil jaata hai!

10. Volume ghat gaya (ΔV_mix = −2 mL < 0) aur mixture garam hua (ΔH_mix < 0) ⟹ **Negative (−ve) deviation** from Raoult's law!
