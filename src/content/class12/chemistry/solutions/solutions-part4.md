## Naye Shabd (New Words)

- **Colligative Properties:** Dilute solutions ki woh chaar properties jo sirf **solute particles ki sankhya (moles)** par depend karti hain, unke chemical nature par nahi!
- **Ebullioscopic Constant (K_b):** Molal boiling point elevation constant (water ke liye **K_b = 0.52 K kg/mol**).
- **Cryoscopic Constant (K_f):** Molal freezing point depression constant (water ke liye **K_f = 1.86 K kg/mol**).
- **Osmosis:** Semi-permeable membrane (SPM) ke through **solvent molecules** ka kam concentration (pure solvent) se zyada concentration (solution) ki taraf spontaneous flow!
- **Osmotic Pressure (π):** Osmosis ko bilkul rokne ke liye solution side par lagana padne wala extra hydrostatic pressure: **π = C R T**.
- **Reverse Osmosis (RO):** Jab solution par osmotic pressure se **zyada pressure (P > π)** lagaya jaaye, to pure water solution se bahar nikal aata hai (samundar ka paani meetha karne aur ghar ke RO purifier ka principle!).

## 1. The Four Colligative Properties (Master Formulas!)

Jab kisi pure solvent mein koi **non-volatile solute** (jo bhaap nahi banta, jaise glucose, urea, sucrose) milaya jaata hai, to surface par solvent molecules ki ginti kam ho jaati hai ⟹ **vapour pressure ghat jaata hai**! Isse chaar colligative properties banti hain:

### 1. Relative Lowering of Vapour Pressure (RLVP):
**(p° − p) / p° = x_B ≈ n_B / n_A = (w_B × M_A) / (M_B × w_A)**  *(for dilute solutions!)*

### 2. Elevation of Boiling Point (ΔT_b = T_b − T_b°):
Vapour pressure kam hone se solution ko 1 atm tak pahunchane ke liye zyada garam karna padta hai ⟹ **Boiling point BADH jaata hai**:
**ΔT_b = K_b × m = K_b × (w_B × 1000) / (M_B × W_A)**

### 3. Depression of Freezing Point (ΔT_f = T_f° − T_f):
Vapour pressure kam hone se solution ka freezing point **GHAT jaata hai** (isliye Shimla mein namak daalne se barf pighalti hai, aur car radiator mein **ethylene glycol** antifreeze daalte hain!):
**ΔT_f = K_f × m = K_f × (w_B × 1000) / (M_B × W_A)**

### 4. Osmotic Pressure (π):
**π = C R T = (n_B / V) R T = (w_B R T) / (M_B V)**
*(jahan **R = 0.0821 L atm / (mol K) = 0.08314 L bar / (mol K)**!)*

- **Isotonic Solutions:** Same osmotic pressure (**π₁ = π₂**, e.g., **0.9% w/v NaCl** saline drip human blood cells ke isotonic hoti hai!).
- **Hypertonic:** Zyada π (blood cell ismein **sikud / shrink** jaati hai!).
- **Hypotonic:** Kam π (blood cell ismein **phool / swell** jaati hai!).

**Why is Osmotic Pressure BEST for finding Molar Mass of Proteins / Polymers?**
1. Room temperature par naapa jaata hai (proteins garm karne par **denature** ho jaate hain!).
2. Bahut dilute solutions ke liye bhi **π ki value kaafi badi aur aasaani se measurable** hoti hai (jabki ΔT_b aur ΔT_f 0.0001 K jitne tiny hote hain)!

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Boiling Point Elevation of Glucose Solution
**18 g of glucose** (C₆H₁₂O₆, molar mass **180 g/mol**) is dissolved in **1.0 kg of water**. At what temperature will the water boil at 1.013 bar? (Given **K_b for water = 0.52 K kg/mol**, normal b.p. = **373.15 K**.)

**Hal (Solution):**
- Moles of glucose: n_B = 18 / 180 = **0.10 mol**; W_A = 1.0 kg ⟹ **m = 0.10 mol/kg**.
- **ΔT_b = K_b × m = 0.52 × 0.10 = 0.052 K**.
- New boiling point: **T_b = 373.15 + 0.052 = 373.202 K** (or **100.052 °C**).
- **Jaanch:** 0.52 × 0.10 = 0.052 K; boiling point increases ✅.

### Example 2: Molar Mass of a Polymer from Osmotic Pressure
**200 cm³ (0.20 L)** of an aqueous solution of a protein contains **1.26 g** of the protein. The osmotic pressure at **300 K** is found to be **2.57 × 10⁻³ bar**. Calculate the molar mass of the protein (take **R = 0.083 L bar / (mol K)**).

**Hal (Solution):**
- π = (w_B R T) / (M_B V) ⟹ **M_B = (w_B R T) / (π V)**.
- M_B = (1.26 × 0.083 × 300) / (2.57 × 10⁻³ × 0.20)
  = 31.374 / (5.14 × 10⁻⁴) ≈ **61,039 g/mol** (≈ 61 kg/mol — bada protein molecule!).
- **Jaanch:** 1.26 × 24.9 = 31.374; 31.374 / 0.000514 ≈ 61,039 g/mol ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **Boiling point mein ΔT_b ghatana aur Freezing point mein ΔT_f jod dena:**
   - *Sahi:* Solute milane par **Boiling Point BADHTA hai (T_b = T_b° + ΔT_b)** aur **Freezing Point GHATTA hai (T_f = T_f° − ΔT_f)**!
2. **Osmosis mein solute ke chalne ki baat keh dena:**
   - *Galat:* Osmosis mein salt/sugar SPM ke paar jaata hai!
   - *Sahi:* SPM (semi-permeable membrane) se **SIRF SOLVENT (water) molecules** guzarte hain — dilute se concentrated side ki taraf!
3. **Osmotic pressure π = CRT mein R ki galat unit rakh dena:**
   - *Sahi:* Agar π **atm** mein ho aur V **Litres** mein, to **R = 0.0821 L atm/(mol K)**; agar **bar** mein ho to **R = 0.08314 L bar/(mol K)** (8.314 sirf Pa aur m³ ke liye)!

## Quick Recap

| Colligative Property | Formula | Key Application |
|---|---|---|
| Relative Lowering of VP | (p° − p)/p° = x_B | Depends only on mole fraction of solute |
| Elevation of Boiling Point | ΔT_b = K_b m (T_b = T_b° + ΔT_b) | K_b(water) = 0.52 K kg/mol |
| Depression of Freezing Point | ΔT_f = K_f m (T_f = T_f° − ΔT_f) | K_f(water) = 1.86 K kg/mol; antifreeze & de-icing |
| Osmotic Pressure | **π = C R T** | Best for **protein/polymer molar mass**; **RO** water purifier |

## Practice Questions

1. Define colligative properties and name all four colligative properties of dilute solutions.
2. Why is osmotic pressure measurement preferred over boiling point elevation for determining the molar mass of proteins and polymers?
3. What is **Reverse Osmosis (RO)**? Mention one major practical application.
4. Why is 0.9% (w/v) NaCl solution called **normal saline** and used for intravenous injections? What would happen if pure water were injected instead?
5. The vapour pressure of pure benzene at a certain temperature is 0.850 bar. A non-volatile, non-electrolyte solid weighing 0.50 g when added to 39.0 g of benzene (molar mass 78 g/mol) lowers the vapour pressure to 0.845 bar. Find the molar mass of the solid.
6. Calculate the freezing point of a solution containing 45 g of ethylene glycol (C₂H₆O₂, molar mass 62 g/mol) mixed with 600 g of water (K_f for water = 1.86 K kg/mol, normal f.p. = 273.15 K).
7. A 5% (w/v) aqueous solution of cane sugar (molar mass 342 g/mol) is isotonic with a 0.877% (w/v) solution of an unknown non-electrolyte X. Calculate the molar mass of X.
8. Calculate the osmotic pressure (in atm) of a 0.10 M aqueous urea solution at 300 K (R = 0.0821 L atm / (mol K)).
9. State whether True or False: Relative lowering of vapour pressure depends on the chemical identity of the non-volatile solute.
10. What mass of sucrose (molar mass 342 g/mol) must be dissolved in 500 g of water so that the boiling point of water is raised by 0.26 K? (K_b = 0.52 K kg/mol.)

### Answers

1. **Colligative properties** woh properties hain jo sirf solute particles ki **sankhya (moles)** par depend karti hain, unke nature par nahi. Chaar properties: (1) Relative lowering of VP, (2) Elevation of b.p., (3) Depression of f.p., (4) Osmotic pressure.

2. (i) Osmotic pressure **room temperature** par naapa jaata hai (proteins garmi se kharaab nahi hote); (ii) Dilute protein solutions ke liye bhi π kaafi bada aur measurable hota hai.

3. Jab solution par osmotic pressure se zyada pressure (**P > π**) lagaya jaaye, to solvent solution se pure solvent ki taraf ulta behta hai (**RO**). Application: **Desalination of seawater** aur **domestic RO water purifiers**.

4. 0.9% (w/v) NaCl solution human blood cells ke andar ke fluid ke **isotonic** hota hai (no swelling or shrinking). Pure water (hypotonic) inject karne par osmosis se water blood cells mein ghus kar unhein phula kar phaad (burst) sakta hai!

5. n_benzene = 39.0 / 78 = 0.50 mol.
   (p° − p) / p° = (0.850 − 0.845) / 0.850 = 0.005 / 0.850 = 1 / 170.
   n_B / 0.50 = 1 / 170 ⟹ n_B = 0.50 / 170 ⟹ **M_B = 0.50 / (0.50 / 170) = 170 g/mol**!
   Jaanch: 0.50 kat gaya; M_B = 170 g/mol ✅.

6. n_B = 45 / 62 ≈ 0.7258 mol; W_A = 0.600 kg ⟹ m = 0.7258 / 0.600 ≈ 1.21 mol/kg.
   ΔT_f = 1.86 × 1.21 ≈ **2.25 K**.
   Freezing point = 273.15 − 2.25 = **270.90 K** (or **−2.25 °C**).
   Jaanch: 1.86 × 1.21 = 2.25 K ✅.

7. Isotonic ⟹ equal molar concentrations (C₁ = C₂):
   5 / 342 = 0.877 / M_X ⟹ **M_X = (0.877 × 342) / 5 ≈ 60.0 g/mol** (urea!).
   Jaanch: 0.877 × 342 = 299.9 ≈ 300; 300 / 5 = 60 g/mol ✅.

8. π = C R T = 0.10 × 0.0821 × 300 = **2.463 atm**.
   Jaanch: 0.1 × 300 = 30; 30 × 0.0821 = 2.463 atm ✅.

9. **False ❌.** Yeh ek colligative property hai — sirf solute particles ke mole fraction par depend karti hai, unki chemical identity par nahi!

10. ΔT_b = K_b × m ⟹ 0.26 = 0.52 × m ⟹ m = 0.50 mol/kg.
    500 g (0.50 kg) water mein moles needed: n_B = 0.50 × 0.50 = 0.25 mol.
    Mass of sucrose = 0.25 × 342 = **85.5 g**.
    Jaanch: 0.25 / 0.50 = 0.50 m; 0.52 × 0.50 = 0.26 K ✅.
