## 1 Mole NaCl ne Freezing Point Double Kyun Giraya?

Lab report dekh kar **Vivek** chaunk gaya: 1 kg paani mein **1 mole Glucose** gholne par freezing point **1.86 °C** gira, par 1 kg paani mein **1 mole NaCl** gholne par freezing point lagbhag **3.72 °C (DOUBLE!)** gir gaya! Aur doosri taraf, benzene mein **Acetic acid (CH₃COOH)** gholne par freezing point ummeed se **AADHA** gira!

Teacher ma'am muskurayi: "Colligative properties **particles ki ginti** par depend karti hain! Paani mein 1 NaCl toot kar **2 ions (Na⁺ + Cl⁻)** banata hai (**Dissociation**), jabki benzene mein 2 acetic acid molecules H-bonding se judkar **1 dimer** bana lete hain (**Association**)! Isse molar mass galat (**Abnormal**) aata hai — aur ise theek karne ke liye 1880 mein **van't Hoff** ne **van't Hoff Factor (i)** diya!"

---

## Naye Shabd (New Words)

- **Abnormal Molar Mass:** Dissociation ya association ke karan colligative properties se aane wala molar mass jo normal formula mass se kam (dissociation mein) ya zyada (association mein) hota hai!
- **van't Hoff Factor (i):**
  **i = Normal Molar Mass / Abnormal (Observed) Molar Mass = Observed Colligative Property / Calculated Colligative Property = Total moles of particles AFTER dissociation or association / Moles BEFORE**.
- **Degree of Dissociation (α):** Total moles mein se jitna fraction ions mein toot jaata hai (**0 ≤ α ≤ 1**).
- **Degree of Association (α):** Total moles mein se jitna fraction aapas mein judkar dimers/trimers bana leta hai.

---

## 1. van't Hoff Factor (i) — Teen Cases!

1. **Non-electrolytes (Glucose, Urea, Sucrose):** Na toot'te hain na judte hain ⟹ **i = 1** (Normal molar mass!).
2. **Dissociation (NaCl, KCl, BaCl₂, K₂SO₄ in water):** Particles badh jaate hain ⟹ **i > 1** (Observed molar mass < Normal molar mass!).
   - Complete dissociation (α = 1) par: **NaCl, KCl → i = 2**; **BaCl₂, CaCl₂, K₂SO₄ → i = 3**; **K₄[Fe(CN)₆] → i = 5**!
   - Partial dissociation (1 molecule → **n** ions, degree **α**):
     Total moles = (1 − α) + n α = **1 + (n − 1) α** ⟹
     **i = 1 + (n − 1) α**   ⟹   **α = (i − 1) / (n − 1)**
3. **Association (CH₃COOH ya Benzoic acid in Benzene — dimerisation!):** Particles ghat jaate hain ⟹ **i < 1** (Observed molar mass > Normal molar mass!).
   - **n** molecules judkar 1 cluster banayein (dimer ke liye **n = 2**):
     Total moles = (1 − α) + α / n ⟹
     **i = 1 − α + α / n**   ⟹   **α = (1 − i) / (1 − 1/n)**  *(Dimer n = 2 ke liye: **α = 2 (1 − i)**!)*

---

## 2. Modified Colligative Property Equations (Hamesha 'i' Lagao!)

- **RLVP:** **(p° − p) / p° = i x_B**
- **Boiling Point Elevation:** **ΔT_b = i K_b m**
- **Freezing Point Depression:** **ΔT_f = i K_f m**
- **Osmotic Pressure:** **π = i C R T**

---

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Degree of Association of Benzoic Acid in Benzene
**2.0 g of benzoic acid** (C₆H₅COOH, normal molar mass **122 g/mol**) dissolved in **25 g of benzene** shows a depression in freezing point of **1.62 K** (**K_f for benzene = 4.9 K kg/mol**). Calculate the percentage **association** of benzoic acid if it forms a **dimer** in benzene.

**Hal (Solution):**
- Observed (abnormal) molar mass:
  M_obs = (K_f × w_B × 1000) / (ΔT_f × W_A) = (4.9 × 2.0 × 1000) / (1.62 × 25) = 9800 / 40.5 ≈ **241.98 g/mol**.
- van't Hoff factor: **i = M_normal / M_obs = 122 / 241.98 ≈ 0.504** (< 1 ⟹ association!).
- Dimerisation (n = 2): **α = 2 (1 − i) = 2 (1 − 0.504) = 2 × 0.496 = 0.992 = 99.2%**!
- **Jaanch:** Observed molar mass (~242) is almost double of normal (122) ⟹ nearly 100% dimerisation (99.2%) ✅.

### Example 2: Freezing Point of Dissociating K₂SO₄ Solution
Calculate the depression in freezing point of a **0.10 m** aqueous solution of **K₂SO₄**, assuming **80% dissociation** (**K_f = 1.86 K kg/mol**).

**Hal (Solution):**
- K₂SO₄ → 2 K⁺ + SO₄²⁻ ⟹ **n = 3 ions**, α = **0.80**.
- **i = 1 + (n − 1) α = 1 + (3 − 1)(0.80) = 1 + 1.60 = 2.60**.
- **ΔT_f = i K_f m = 2.60 × 1.86 × 0.10 = 0.4836 K ≈ 0.484 K**.
- **Jaanch:** 1 + 2(0.8) = 2.6; 2.6 × 0.186 = 0.4836 K ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **i = M_abnormal / M_normal ulta kar dena:**
   - *Galat:* Dissociation mein M_obs chhota hota hai to i < 1 nikaal dena!
   - *Sahi:* **i = M_normal / M_abnormal** (kyunki M colligative property ke inversely proportional hai!). Dissociation → **i > 1**; Association → **i < 1**!
2. **Dimerisation (n = 2) mein α = (1 − i) likh kar factor 2 bhool jaana:**
   - *Sahi:* Dimer (n = 2) ke liye **i = 1 − α/2** ⟹ **α = 2 (1 − i)**!
3. **Salts (NaCl, BaCl₂) ke colligative numericals mein 'i' bhool jaana:**
   - *Sahi:* Electrolyte dikhte hi sabse pehle **i** check karo (non-electrolyte → i = 1; strong electrolyte → i = ions count)!

---

## Quick Recap

| Case | van't Hoff Factor (i) | Relation with α | Observed Molar Mass |
|---|---|---|---|
| Non-electrolyte (Glucose, Urea) | i = 1 | — | M_obs = M_normal |
| Dissociation (1 → n ions) | **i > 1** | **i = 1 + (n − 1) α** | M_obs < M_normal |
| Association (n → 1 cluster) | **i < 1** | **i = 1 − α(1 − 1/n)** (Dimer: α = 2(1−i)) | M_obs > M_normal |

## Practice Questions

1. Define van't Hoff factor (i). What is its value for (a) complete dissociation of Al₂(SO₄)₃, and (b) complete dimerisation of acetic acid in benzene?
2. Why does the molar mass of acetic acid determined by freezing-point depression in benzene come out to be around 120 g/mol instead of 60 g/mol?
3. Arrange the following 0.1 M aqueous solutions in order of **increasing boiling point**: Glucose, NaCl, BaCl₂, AlCl₃ (assume complete dissociation of salts).
4. A 0.10 M aqueous solution of KCl is 90% dissociated. Calculate its van't Hoff factor i and its osmotic pressure at 300 K (R = 0.0821 L atm / (mol K)).
5. If 0.10 m aqueous solution of a weak acid HA freezes at −0.2232 °C (K_f = 1.86 K kg/mol), calculate (a) van't Hoff factor i, and (b) percentage dissociation α of the acid.
6. Calculate the osmotic pressure of a 0.05 M aqueous CaCl₂ solution at 27 °C (300 K) assuming complete dissociation (R = 0.0821 L atm / (mol K)).
7. For a solute that **trimerises** (n = 3) with 60% association in a solvent, calculate the van't Hoff factor i.
8. Which of the following 0.1 m aqueous solutions has the **lowest freezing point**: (a) Urea, (b) KCl, (c) K₂SO₄, (d) K₄[Fe(CN)₆]?
9. State whether True or False: When an electrolyte dissociates in water, its observed molar mass is greater than its normal molar mass.
10. Calculate the boiling point of a solution made by dissolving 5.85 g of NaCl (molar mass 58.5 g/mol, assume complete dissociation) in 500 g of water (K_b = 0.52 K kg/mol, normal b.p. = 100 °C).

### Answers

1. **i** = Observed colligative property / Calculated colligative property (= M_normal / M_abnormal).
   (a) Al₂(SO₄)₃ → 2 Al³⁺ + 3 SO₄²⁻ ⟹ **i = 5**.
   (b) Complete dimerisation (α = 1, n = 2) ⟹ **i = 1/2 = 0.5**.

2. Benzene (non-polar solvent) mein 2 acetic acid molecules intermolecular **Hydrogen Bonding** se judkar **dimer** bana lete hain ⟹ particles aadhe ho jaate hain (i = 0.5) ⟹ observed molar mass double (~120 g/mol) aata hai!

3. Increasing i (and hence increasing ΔT_b & boiling point):
   **Glucose (i = 1) < NaCl (i = 2) < BaCl₂ (i = 3) < AlCl₃ (i = 4)**!

4. KCl → K⁺ + Cl⁻ (n = 2), α = 0.90 ⟹ **i = 1 + (2 − 1)(0.90) = 1.90**.
   π = i C R T = 1.90 × 0.10 × 0.0821 × 300 = 1.90 × 2.463 ≈ **4.68 atm**.
   Jaanch: 1.9 × 2.463 = 4.6797 ≈ 4.68 atm ✅.

5. ΔT_f = 0.2232 K:
   (a) i = ΔT_f / (K_f m) = 0.2232 / (1.86 × 0.10) = 0.2232 / 0.186 = **1.20**.
   (b) HA → H⁺ + A⁻ (n = 2): **α = (i − 1)/(2 − 1) = 1.20 − 1 = 0.20 = 20%**.
   Jaanch: 1.2 × 0.186 = 0.2232 K ✅.

6. CaCl₂ → Ca²⁺ + 2 Cl⁻ (**i = 3**):
   π = 3 × 0.05 × 0.0821 × 300 = 0.15 × 24.63 ≈ **3.69 atm**.
   Jaanch: 3 × 0.05 × 300 = 45; 45 × 0.0821 = 3.6945 atm ✅.

7. n = 3, α = 0.60: **i = 1 − α(1 − 1/3) = 1 − 0.60 × (2/3) = 1 − 0.40 = 0.60**.
   Jaanch: 0.40 monomer + 0.60/3 (0.20 trimer) = 0.60 ✅.

8. Lowest freezing point = largest ΔT_f = largest **i**: **K₄[Fe(CN)₆] (i = 5)**!

9. **False ❌.** Dissociation mein particles badhte hain (i > 1), isliye observed molar mass **KAM (smaller)** aata hai (M_obs = M_normal / i)!

10. n(NaCl) = 5.85 / 58.5 = 0.10 mol in 0.50 kg water ⟹ m = 0.10 / 0.50 = 0.20 mol/kg; **i = 2**.
    ΔT_b = 2 × 0.52 × 0.20 = **0.208 K** ⟹ Boiling point = **100.208 °C**.
    Jaanch: 2 × 0.20 = 0.40; 0.40 × 0.52 = 0.208 K ✅.
