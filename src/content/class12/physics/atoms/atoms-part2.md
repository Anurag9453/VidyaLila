## Naye Shabd (New Words)

- **Line Emission Spectrum:** Garam gas ke atoms dwara sirf kuch **khaas discrete wavelengths (bright lines on dark background)** ki light chhodna.
- **Stationary Orbits:** Bohr ke anusaar woh khaas allowed circular orbits jinmein ghoomte hue electron **koi EM radiation nahi chhodta** (energy constant rehti hai!).
- **Bohr Radius (a₀):** Hydrogen atom ki sabse chhoti (n = 1) orbit ka radius: **a₀ = 0.529 Å ≈ 0.53 Å = 0.053 nm**.

---

## 1. Bohr's Three Postulates (Hydrogen Atom)

1. **First Postulate (Stationary Orbits):** Electron nucleus ke chaaron taraf sirf kuch khaas stable circular orbits mein bina koi energy radiate kiye ghoom sakta hai (centripetal force = electrostatic attraction: **m v² / r = k Z e² / r²**).
2. **Second Postulate (Quantisation of Angular Momentum):** Sirf wahi orbits allowed hain jinmein electron ka angular momentum **L = m v r** constant **h / (2π)** ka integer multiple ho:
   **L = m v r = n h / (2π)**   *(n = 1, 2, 3, ... = Principal Quantum Number!)*
3. **Third Postulate (Frequency Condition — Transitions):** Electron jab high energy orbit (**E_i**) se low energy orbit (**E_f**) mein koodta (jump) hai, to dono ke energy difference ke barabar energy ka **ek photon** emit karta hai:
   **h ν = E_i − E_f**

---

## 2. Hydrogen Atom ke 3 Golden Formulas (Derivation Highlights!)

Equation 1 (**m v² r = k Z e²**) aur Equation 2 (**m v r = n h / 2π**) ko solve karne par milte hain:

### (a) Radius of n-th Orbit (r_n):
**r_n = (n² h²) / (4π² m k Z e²) = 0.53 × (n² / Z) Å**
- Hydrogen (Z = 1) ke liye: **r_n = 0.53 n² Å** ⟹ **r_n ∝ n²**!
  (n = 1: **0.53 Å**; n = 2: **2.12 Å**; n = 3: **4.77 Å**!)

### (b) Speed of Electron in n-th Orbit (v_n):
**v_n = (2π k Z e²) / (n h) = (c / 137) × (Z / n) ≈ 2.19 × 10⁶ × (Z / n) m/s**
- Hydrogen (Z = 1) ke liye: **v_n ∝ 1 / n**! (Bahar ki orbits mein electron **dheema** chalta hai!)

### (c) Kinetic, Potential & Total Energy (E_n):
- Kinetic Energy: **K = + ½ (k Z e² / r_n)** *(Positive!)*
- Potential Energy: **U = − (k Z e² / r_n)** *(Negative!)*
- Total Energy: **E_n = K + U = − ½ (k Z e² / r_n)**!

**Super Shortcut Relations (Hamesha Yaad Rakho!):**
**K = − E_n**   aur   **U = 2 E_n**

Numbers daalne par Hydrogen (Z = 1) ke liye:

**E_n = − 13.6 / n² eV**   *(general Hydrogen-like ion: **E_n = −13.6 Z² / n² eV**!)*

- **n = 1 (Ground State):** E₁ = **−13.6 eV**
- **n = 2 (1st Excited State):** E₂ = −13.6 / 4 = **−3.40 eV**
- **n = 3 (2nd Excited State):** E₃ = −13.6 / 9 = **−1.51 eV**
- **n = 4 (3rd Excited State):** E₄ = −13.6 / 16 = **−0.85 eV**
- **n = ∞ (Free Electron):** E_∞ = **0 eV**

*(Minus sign ka matlab hai ki electron nucleus se **bandha (bound)** hua hai — use azaad karne ke liye bahar se energy deni padegi!)*

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Radius, KE, PE & Total Energy in n = 2 of Hydrogen
For an electron in the **n = 2** orbit of a Hydrogen atom, calculate:
(a) the orbit radius **r₂**,
(b) the total energy **E₂**,
(c) the kinetic energy **K₂**, and
(d) the potential energy **U₂**.

**Hal (Solution):**
- (a) r₂ = 0.53 × (2)² = 0.53 × 4 = **2.12 Å** (= 0.212 nm).
- (b) E₂ = −13.6 / (2)² = −13.6 / 4 = **−3.40 eV**.
- (c) K₂ = −E₂ = **+3.40 eV**.
- (d) U₂ = 2 E₂ = 2 × (−3.40) = **−6.80 eV**.
- **Jaanch:** K₂ + U₂ = +3.40 − 6.80 = −3.40 eV = E₂ ✅.

### Example 2: Angular Momentum in n = 3 Orbit
Calculate the angular momentum of an electron in the **3rd orbit (n = 3)** of a Hydrogen atom (take h = 6.63 × 10⁻³⁴ J s, π = 3.14).

**Hal (Solution):**
- L = n h / (2π) = (3 × 6.63 × 10⁻³⁴) / (2 × 3.14)
  = (19.89 × 10⁻³⁴) / 6.28 ≈ **3.17 × 10⁻³⁴ J s**.
- **Jaanch:** 19.89 / 6.28 ≈ 3.167 ⟹ 3.17 × 10⁻³⁴ J s ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **K, U aur E ke signs aur ratios ulta kar dena:**
   - *Galat:* E = −3.4 eV hone par K = −3.4 eV ya U = −1.7 eV bol dena!
   - *Sahi:* Hamesha yaad rakho: **K = +|E| = −E** (positive!), aur **U = 2 E** (negative, magnitude double!)!
2. **Radius ko r_n ∝ n maan lena:**
   - *Galat:* n = 2 ka radius 2 × 0.53 = 1.06 Å likhna!
   - *Sahi:* **r_n ∝ n²** ⟹ n = 2 ka radius **4 × 0.53 = 2.12 Å**!
3. **Sochna ki n badhne par total energy E_n ghatti hai:**
   - *Galat:* 3.4 < 13.6 hai to E₂ < E₁ bolna!
   - *Sahi:* Minus sign dekho! **−3.4 eV > −13.6 eV** ⟹ n badhne par total energy **BADHTI (less negative)** hai!

## Quick Recap

| Quantity (Hydrogen, Z = 1) | Formula | Dependence on n |
|---|---|---|
| Angular Momentum | L = n h / (2π) | L ∝ n |
| Orbit Radius | r_n = 0.53 n² Å | **r_n ∝ n²** |
| Electron Speed | v_n = 2.19 × 10⁶ / n m/s | **v_n ∝ 1/n** |
| Total Energy | E_n = −13.6 / n² eV | Increases towards 0 as n → ∞ |
| KE & PE Relations | **K = −E_n**; **U = 2 E_n** | K > 0, U < 0, E < 0 |

## Practice Questions

1. State Bohr's three postulates for the Hydrogen atom.
2. What is the physical significance of the negative sign in the total energy E_n = −13.6/n² eV of an electron in a Hydrogen atom?
3. Calculate the radius of the (a) 3rd orbit (n = 3), and (b) 4th orbit (n = 4) of a Hydrogen atom (given a₀ = 0.53 Å).
4. The total energy of an electron in the first excited state (n = 2) of Hydrogen is −3.4 eV. What are its kinetic energy and potential energy in this state?
5. Find the ratio of speeds of an electron in the n = 1 and n = 3 orbits of a Hydrogen atom.
6. Calculate the energy (in eV) of the photon emitted when an electron in Hydrogen jumps from n = 3 (−1.51 eV) to n = 2 (−3.40 eV).
7. What is the ratio of the radii of the 1st, 2nd, and 3rd Bohr orbits of Hydrogen?
8. Calculate the ground-state (n = 1) total energy of a singly ionised Helium ion He⁺ (Z = 2) using E_n = −13.6 Z²/n² eV.
9. State whether True or False: According to Bohr's model, an electron continuously radiates electromagnetic energy while revolving in a stationary orbit.
10. If the kinetic energy of an electron in a Bohr orbit is +1.51 eV, find (a) its total energy, (b) its potential energy, and (c) the orbit number n (for Hydrogen).

### Answers

1. (i) Electron stationary circular orbits mein bina radiation chhode ghoomta hai; (ii) Angular momentum quantised hai: **L = n h / (2π)**; (iii) Jump karne par photon emit/absorb hota hai: **h ν = E_i − E_f**.

2. Negative sign batata hai ki electron nucleus ke electrostatic attraction se **bandha (bound)** hua hai — use atom se poori tarah alag karne ke liye bahar se positive energy deni padegi!

3. (a) r₃ = 0.53 × 3² = 0.53 × 9 = **4.77 Å**.
   (b) r₄ = 0.53 × 4² = 0.53 × 16 = **8.48 Å**.
   Jaanch: 0.53 × 9 = 4.77; 0.53 × 16 = 8.48 ✅.

4. **K = −E = +3.4 eV**; **U = 2 E = −6.8 eV**.
   Jaanch: +3.4 − 6.8 = −3.4 eV ✅.

5. v_n ∝ 1 / n ⟹ **v₁ : v₃ = 3 : 1**.

6. ΔE = E₃ − E₂ = −1.51 − (−3.40) = **1.89 eV**.
   Jaanch: 3.40 − 1.51 = 1.89 eV ✅.

7. r_n ∝ n² ⟹ r₁ : r₂ : r₃ = 1² : 2² : 3² = **1 : 4 : 9**.

8. Z = 2, n = 1: E₁ = −13.6 × (2)² / 1² = −13.6 × 4 = **−54.4 eV**.
   Jaanch: 13.6 × 4 = 54.4 eV ✅.

9. **False ❌.** Bohr ke first postulate ke anusaar stationary orbit mein ghoomte waqt electron **koi EM energy radiate NAHI karta**!

10. (a) **E = −K = −1.51 eV**.
    (b) **U = 2 E = −3.02 eV**.
    (c) −13.6 / n² = −1.51 ⟹ n² = 13.6 / 1.51 ≈ 9 ⟹ **n = 3**!
    Jaanch: −13.6 / 9 = −1.511 eV ✅.
