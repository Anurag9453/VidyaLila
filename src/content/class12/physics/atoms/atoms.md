Carrom khelte waqt **Roli** ne notice kiya ki jab woh striker ko tez maarti hai, to agar raasta khali ho to striker seedha paar chala jaata hai; agar kisi coin ke kinare ko chhooye to thode angle par mud jaata hai; aur sirf tabhi **180° seedha wapas laut'ta hai** jab kisi bhaari coin ke bilkul beechon-beech (head-on) takraye!

## Naye Shabd (New Words)

- **Alpha Particle (α):** Helium nucleus (⁴₂He) — charge **+2e** aur mass lagbhag **4 proton masses**!
- **Impact Parameter (b):** Alpha particle ki initial velocity line aur nucleus ke centre ke beech ki **perpendicular doori**. (Head-on collision mein **b = 0 ⟹ θ = 180°**!)
- **Distance of Closest Approach (r₀):** Head-on collision (b = 0) mein alpha particle nucleus ke jitna **sabse paas** pahunch kar pal bhar ke liye rukta hai (aur phir wapas laut'ta hai) — yeh nucleus ke size ki **upper limit** deta hai!

---

## 1. Geiger-Marsden Alpha Scattering ke Quantitative Nateeje

1. **99.86% se zyada α-particles** bina mude (< 1°) seedhe paar nikal gaye ⟹ Atom ka lagbhag poora hissa **khali (empty space)** hai!
2. Sirf **8000 mein se ~1 α-particle** 90° se zyada muda (aur kuch seedhe 180° wapas laute!) ⟹ Atom ka poora positive charge (+Ze) aur lagbhag poora mass ek **bahut nanhe centre (Nucleus, radius ~10⁻¹⁵ m)** mein jama hai (jabki atom ka radius ~10⁻¹⁰ m = **100,000 guna bada** hota hai!).
3. **Impact Parameter (b) aur Scattering Angle (θ):**
   - Bada b (nucleus se door) ⟹ chhota θ (lagbhag seedha).
   - Chhota b (nucleus ke paas) ⟹ bada θ.
   - **b = 0 (head-on)** ⟹ **θ = 180°** (seedha wapas!).

---

## 2. Distance of Closest Approach (r₀) — Energy Conservation se Derivation!

Maan lo kinetic energy **K** wala ek alpha particle (charge **q₁ = +2e**) ek stationary gold nucleus (charge **q₂ = +Ze**) ki taraf seedha (head-on, b = 0) badh raha hai:
- Door (infinity) par: Kinetic Energy = **K**, Potential Energy = **0**.
- Sabse paas (**r = r₀**) par: Alpha particle ek pal ke liye ruk jaata hai ⟹ Kinetic Energy = **0**, saari energy Electrostatic Potential Energy ban jaati hai:
  **U = k (2e)(Ze) / r₀ = (2 k Z e²) / r₀**
- Conservation of Energy (**K = U**) lagao:

**r₀ = (2 k Z e²) / K**   *(jahan k = 9 × 10⁹ N m²/C² aur **1 MeV = 1.6 × 10⁻¹³ J**!)*

---

## 3. Rutherford Model ki 2 Fatal Limitations (Kyun Fail Hua?)

1. **Atomic Instability (Spiral Collapse):** Classical electromagnetism kehti hai ki circle mein ghoomta hua electron **accelerated** hai — isliye use lagataar **EM waves radiate** karni chahiye! Energy ghat'te-ghat'te electron ko sirf **10⁻⁸ second** mein spiral hokar nucleus mein gir jaana chahiye tha! Par asal mein atoms bilkul **stable** hote hain!
2. **Continuous vs Line Spectrum:** Spiral girte waqt electron ki frequency lagataar badalni chahiye thi ⟹ **continuous rainbow spectrum** milna chahiye tha! Par real gases (jaise Hydrogen) sirf **sharp discrete line spectrum** deti hain!

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Distance of Closest Approach for a Gold Nucleus
An alpha particle of kinetic energy **K = 5.0 MeV** makes a head-on collision with a stationary gold nucleus (**Z = 79**). Calculate the distance of closest approach **r₀**.

**Hal (Solution):**
- K = 5.0 MeV = 5.0 × 1.6 × 10⁻¹³ J = **8.0 × 10⁻¹³ J**.
- Numerator: 2 k Z e² = 2 × (9 × 10⁹) × 79 × (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹)²
  = 18 × 79 × 2.56 × 10⁻²⁹ = 1422 × 2.56 × 10⁻²⁹ ≈ **3.64 × 10⁻²⁶ J m**.
- **r₀ = (3.64 × 10⁻²⁶) / (8.0 × 10⁻¹³) = 4.55 × 10⁻¹⁴ m** (= 45.5 fm)!
- **Jaanch:** 1422 × 2.56 = 3640.32; 36.4 / 8 = 4.55; 10⁻²⁷/10⁻¹³ = 10⁻¹⁴ m ✅.

### Example 2: Effect of Doubling Kinetic Energy on r₀
If the kinetic energy of the alpha particle in Example 1 is **doubled** to 10 MeV, what happens to the distance of closest approach r₀?

**Hal (Solution):**
- Formula **r₀ = (2 k Z e²) / K** ⟹ **r₀ ∝ 1 / K**!
- K double hone par r₀ **aadhi (halved)** ho jaati hai: **r'₀ = 4.55 / 2 = 2.275 × 10⁻¹⁴ m**!
- **Jaanch:** Zyada energy wala alpha particle nucleus ke aur zyada paas pahunch sakta hai ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **Alpha particle ka charge +e rakh dena (+2e bhool jaana!):**
   - *Galat:* r₀ = k Z e² / K likhna!
   - *Sahi:* Alpha particle (⁴₂He) mein **2 protons (+2e)** hote hain ⟹ **r₀ = (2 k Z e²) / K**!
2. **MeV ko Joules mein convert karna bhool jaana:**
   - *Galat:* K = 5 MeV ko seedha 5 ya 5 × 10⁶ rakh dena!
   - *Sahi:* **1 eV = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ J** ⟹ **1 MeV = 10⁶ eV = 1.6 × 10⁻¹³ J**!
3. **Impact parameter b = 0 par θ = 0° bol dena:**
   - *Galat:* b = 0 matlab koi scattering nahi!
   - *Sahi:* b = 0 ka matlab **seedhi head-on takkar** ⟹ **θ = 180°** (maximum deflection, seedha wapas)!

## Quick Recap

| Concept | Formula / Value | Key Fact |
|---|---|---|
| Atomic vs Nuclear Size | Atom: ~10⁻¹⁰ m; Nucleus: ~10⁻¹⁵ m | Atom is mostly empty space! |
| Impact Parameter (b) | b = 0 ⟹ θ = 180°; large b ⟹ θ ≈ 0° | Perpendicular distance of velocity line from nucleus |
| Distance of Closest Approach | **r₀ = (2 k Z e²) / K** (for α-particle) | Upper limit on nuclear radius; r₀ ∝ 1/K |
| Rutherford's 2 Failures | (1) Predicts spiral collapse; (2) Predicts continuous spectrum | Fixed by **Bohr's Quantum Model**! |

## Practice Questions

1. Define impact parameter (b) and distance of closest approach (r₀) in Rutherford's alpha-scattering experiment.
2. What is the scattering angle θ when the impact parameter is (a) b = 0, and (b) very large?
3. Why was a very thin gold foil (~10⁻⁷ m) used in the Geiger-Marsden experiment?
4. State the two major limitations of Rutherford's nuclear model of the atom.
5. An alpha particle of kinetic energy 7.7 MeV is directed head-on toward a gold nucleus (Z = 79). Calculate the distance of closest approach r₀.
6. What will be the distance of closest approach if a **proton** (charge +e) of kinetic energy 5.0 MeV approaches a gold nucleus (Z = 79) head-on? (Compare with Example 1!)
7. If the speed **v** of an alpha particle is **doubled**, by what factor does its distance of closest approach r₀ change?
8. What is the approximate ratio of the radius of an atom to the radius of its nucleus?
9. State whether True or False: In Rutherford's scattering experiment, electrons in the gold atom cause significant deflection of the heavy alpha particles.
10. Two alpha particles of kinetic energies 4 MeV and 8 MeV approach the same gold nucleus head-on. Find the ratio of their distances of closest approach r₁ : r₂.

### Answers

1. **Impact parameter (b):** Alpha particle ki initial velocity line se nucleus ke centre ki perpendicular doori. **Distance of closest approach (r₀):** Head-on collision mein alpha particle ki nucleus se minimum doori jahan uski saari KE electrostatic PE ban jaati hai.

2. (a) b = 0 (head-on): **θ = 180°**.
   (b) Large b: **θ ≈ 0°** (almost undeflected).

3. Thin gold foil isliye use ki gayi taaki alpha particle sirf **ek hi (single) scattering** kare (baar-baar takkar na ho), aur gold ka nucleus bhaari (Z = 79) hone ke karan takkar par khud hilta nahi!

4. (i) **Stability of atom** nahi samjha saka (accelerated electron ko EM radiation chhod kar 10⁻⁸ s mein nucleus mein gir jaana chahiye tha); (ii) **Discrete line spectra** nahi samjha saka (continuous spectrum predict kiya).

5. K = 7.7 × 1.6 × 10⁻¹³ = 1.232 × 10⁻¹² J.
   Numerator (from Ex 1) = 3.64 × 10⁻²⁶ J m.
   r₀ = (3.64 × 10⁻²⁶) / (1.232 × 10⁻¹²) ≈ **2.95 × 10⁻¹⁴ m** (≈ 30 fm).
   Jaanch: 3.64 / 1.232 ≈ 2.954 ⟹ 2.95 × 10⁻¹⁴ m ✅.

6. Proton ka charge (+e) alpha particle (+2e) ka **aadha** hai ⟹ same K = 5.0 MeV par r₀_proton = r₀_alpha / 2 = 4.55 / 2 = **2.275 × 10⁻¹⁴ m**!
   Jaanch: r₀ ∝ q₁ ⟹ half charge = half r₀ ✅.

7. Speed v double ⟹ Kinetic energy K = ½mv² **4 guna (4×)** ho jaati hai ⟹ r₀ ∝ 1/K **1/4 (one-fourth)** ho jaayegi!

8. R_atom / R_nucleus ≈ 10⁻¹⁰ / 10⁻¹⁵ = **10⁵ : 1** (lagbhag 1 lakh guna bada!).

9. **False ❌.** Electron alpha particle se ~7300 guna halka hota hai — isliye electrons alpha particles ko bilkul deflect nahi kar paate (saara deflection bhaari positive nucleus se hota hai!).

10. r₀ ∝ 1 / K ⟹ r₁ : r₂ = K₂ : K₁ = 8 : 4 = **2 : 1**.
    Jaanch: Kam energy wala particle zyada door rukta hai ✅.
