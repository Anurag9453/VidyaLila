Physics club ke quiz mein **Reema** ko ek ajeeb paheli mili: "Ek sodium metal plate par 1000-watt ki super-bright **red** spotlight daali gayi — ek bhi electron nahi nikla! Phir ek chhoti si 1-watt ki dim **violet** torch daali gayi — aur turant electrons nikalne lage! Aisa kaise hua?"

Teacher ma'am muskurayi: "1905 mein **Albert Einstein** ne iska jawaab diya — jis par unhe **Nobel Prize** mila! Einstein ne kaha ki light continuous wave ki tarah nahi, balki chhote-chhote energy packets (**Photons / Quanta**) ke roop mein aati hai! Ek red photon mein electron ko bahar nikaalne layak energy nahi hoti, jabki ek akela violet photon kaafi energy lekar aata hai!"

---

## Naye Shabd (New Words)

- **Photon (Light Quantum):** Electromagnetic radiation ki energy ka sabse chhota packet, jiski energy **E = h ν = h c / λ** aur momentum **p = h / λ = E / c** hota hai!
- **Planck's Constant (h):** Quantum physics ka fundamental constant: **h = 6.63 × 10⁻³⁴ J s**.
- **Threshold Wavelength (λ₀):** Woh **maximum wavelength** jiske upar (λ > λ₀) photoelectric emission nahi hota: **λ₀ = h c / φ₀**.

---

## 1. Photon ki 5 Golden Properties

1. Har photon ki energy: **E = h ν = h c / λ**.
   - **Super Shortcut (eV & nm ke liye!):** Kyunki h c ≈ 1.989 × 10⁻²⁵ J m ≈ **1240 eV nm**:
     **E (in eV) ≈ 1240 / λ (in nm)**  *(calculation 2 second mein!)*
2. Har photon ka momentum: **p = E / c = h / λ**.
3. Photon ka **rest mass ZERO** hota hai, aur yeh vacuum mein hamesha **c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s** se chalta hai.
4. Photon **electrically neutral (uncharged)** hota hai ⟹ electric aur magnetic fields se **kabhi deflect nahi hota**!
5. Photon-electron takkar mein **Total Energy aur Total Momentum conserved** rehte hain (par photons ki ginti conserved hona zaroori nahi — photon absorb ho sakta hai!).

---

## 2. Einstein's Photoelectric Equation (Nobel Prize Formula!)

Jab ek photon (energy **h ν**) metal ke andar ek electron se takrata hai, to poori energy us **ek hi electron** ko de deta hai (all-or-nothing collision!):
- Is energy ka ek hissa (**φ₀**, work function) electron ko metal satah se bahar nikaalne mein kharch hota hai.
- Bachi hui energy electron ki **maximum kinetic energy (K_max)** ban jaati hai!

**h ν = φ₀ + K_max**   ⟹   **K_max = e V₀ = h ν − φ₀**

Kyunki threshold frequency par K_max = 0 hota hai, **φ₀ = h ν₀ = h c / λ₀**! Isliye:

**K_max = e V₀ = h (ν − ν₀) = h c (1/λ − 1/λ₀)**

### V₀ versus ν Graph ka Secret (Straight Line!):
e V₀ = h ν − φ₀ ko **e** se divide karo:

**V₀ = (h / e) ν − (φ₀ / e)**

Ise straight line **y = m x + c** se compare karo:
- **Slope = h / e** (Universal constant! Sabhi metals ke liye lines **parallel** hoti hain!)
- **x-intercept = ν₀** (Threshold frequency!)
- **y-intercept = −φ₀ / e** (Work function batata hai!)

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Photoelectric Effect on Sodium
Light of wavelength **λ = 400 nm** falls on a sodium metal surface whose work function is **φ₀ = 2.30 eV**. Calculate (a) the energy of each incident photon in eV, (b) the maximum kinetic energy **K_max**, and (c) the stopping potential **V₀**.

**Hal (Solution):**
- (a) Shortcut: **E = 1240 / λ(nm) = 1240 / 400 = 3.10 eV**.
- (b) Einstein's equation: **K_max = E − φ₀ = 3.10 − 2.30 = 0.80 eV**.
- (c) K_max = e V₀ ⟹ **V₀ = 0.80 V**.
- **Jaanch:** 2.30 + 0.80 = 3.10 eV ✅; 400 × 3.10 = 1240 ✅.

### Example 2: Number of Photons Emitted per Second by a Laser
A monochromatic light source of power **P = 3.315 mW** emits light of frequency **ν = 5.0 × 10¹⁴ Hz**. Calculate the number of photons emitted per second (**N / t**). (Take h = 6.63 × 10⁻³⁴ J s.)

**Hal (Solution):**
- Ek photon ki energy: **E = h ν = (6.63 × 10⁻³⁴) × (5.0 × 10¹⁴) = 3.315 × 10⁻¹⁹ J**.
- Total power P = 3.315 mW = **3.315 × 10⁻³ J/s**.
- Photons per second: **N / t = P / E = (3.315 × 10⁻³) / (3.315 × 10⁻¹⁹) = 1.0 × 10¹⁶ photons/s**.
- **Jaanch:** (10¹⁶)(3.315 × 10⁻¹⁹) = 3.315 × 10⁻³ W = 3.315 mW ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **Einstein's equation mein eV aur Joules ko bina convert kiye ghatana:**
   - *Galat:* hν Joules mein nikaal kar usmein se seedha φ₀ = 2.3 eV ghata dena!
   - *Sahi:* Dono ko **ek hi unit** (dono eV mein, ya dono Joules mein) rakho! (1 eV = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ J.)
2. **V₀ vs ν graph ka slope alag-alag metals ke liye alag bol dena:**
   - *Galat:* Caesium aur Copper ke V₀ vs ν graphs ka slope alag hota hai.
   - *Sahi:* Har metal ke liye **Slope = h / e** (universal constant) — sirf intercept (ν₀ aur φ₀) badalta hai!
3. **Photon ka momentum zero bol dena kyunki uska rest mass zero hai:**
   - *Galat:* m₀ = 0 ⟹ p = m₀ v = 0!
   - *Sahi:* Photon ke liye relativistic momentum **p = h / λ = E / c ≠ 0** hota hai!

## Quick Recap

| Concept | Formula | Key Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Photon Energy & Momentum | E = hν = hc/λ ≈ 1240/λ(nm) eV; p = h/λ = E/c | Rest mass = 0; uncharged; speed = c |
| Einstein's Equation | K_max = e V₀ = hν − φ₀ = h(ν − ν₀) | Explains all 4 photoelectric laws instantly! |
| V₀ vs ν Graph | V₀ = (h/e) ν − (φ₀/e) | Slope = **h/e** (same for all metals!) |
| Photon Rate from Power P | N / t = P / (hν) = P λ / (h c) | Number of photons emitted per second |

## Practice Questions

1. Write Einstein's photoelectric equation and explain how it accounts for the existence of threshold frequency ν₀.
2. State four properties of a photon.
3. Calculate the energy (in eV) of a photon of wavelength (a) 620 nm, and (b) 310 nm (use hc = 1240 eV nm).
4. The work function of caesium is 2.14 eV. Find (a) its threshold frequency ν₀ (take h = 6.63 × 10⁻³⁴ J s, 1 eV = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ J), and (b) its threshold wavelength λ₀ (use hc = 1240 eV nm).
5. Light of wavelength 248 nm falls on a metal of work function 3.2 eV. Calculate (a) K_max of emitted photoelectrons in eV, and (b) the stopping potential V₀.
6. A 6.63 mW laser emits light of frequency 1.0 × 10¹⁵ Hz. How many photons does it emit per second? (Take h = 6.63 × 10⁻³⁴ J s.)
7. What does the **slope** and **x-intercept** of the stopping potential (V₀) versus frequency (ν) graph represent?
8. Calculate the momentum of a photon of wavelength 663 nm (take h = 6.63 × 10⁻³⁴ J s).
9. State whether True or False: Increasing the frequency of incident light increases the energy of each individual photon.
10. For a certain metal, the threshold wavelength is 620 nm. Will light of wavelength 700 nm eject photoelectrons from this metal? Give the reason.

### Answers

1. **K_max = h ν − φ₀**. Kyunki kinetic energy K_max negative nahi ho sakti (K_max ≥ 0), emission tabhi hoga jab **h ν ≥ φ₀ ⟹ ν ≥ φ₀/h (= ν₀)**! Isliye ν < ν₀ par zero emission!

2. (i) Energy E = hν = hc/λ; (ii) Momentum p = h/λ = E/c; (iii) Rest mass = 0, travels at c in vacuum; (iv) Electrically neutral (no deflection by E or B fields).

3. (a) E = 1240 / 620 = **2.0 eV**.
   (b) E = 1240 / 310 = **4.0 eV**.
   Jaanch: 620 × 2 = 1240; 310 × 4 = 1240 ✅.

4. (a) φ₀ = 2.14 × 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ = 3.424 × 10⁻¹⁹ J.
       ν₀ = φ₀ / h = (3.424 × 10⁻¹⁹) / (6.63 × 10⁻³⁴) ≈ **5.16 × 10¹⁴ Hz**.
   (b) λ₀ = 1240 / 2.14 ≈ **579 nm**.
   Jaanch: 5.16 × 10¹⁴ × 5.79 × 10⁻⁷ ≈ 2.99 × 10⁸ ≈ 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s ✅.

5. Incident photon energy: E = 1240 / 248 = **5.0 eV**.
   (a) K_max = E − φ₀ = 5.0 − 3.2 = **1.8 eV**.
   (b) **V₀ = 1.8 V**.
   Jaanch: 248 × 5 = 1240; 5.0 − 3.2 = 1.8 eV ✅.

6. E = h ν = (6.63 × 10⁻³⁴)(10¹⁵) = 6.63 × 10⁻¹⁹ J.
   N / t = P / E = (6.63 × 10⁻³) / (6.63 × 10⁻¹⁹) = **1.0 × 10¹⁶ photons/s**.
   Jaanch: 6.63 kat gaya; 10⁻³ / 10⁻¹⁹ = 10¹⁶ ✅.

7. **Slope = h / e** (universal constant for all metals); **x-intercept = ν₀** (threshold frequency of that metal).

8. p = h / λ = (6.63 × 10⁻³⁴) / (663 × 10⁻⁹) = (6.63 × 10⁻³⁴) / (6.63 × 10⁻⁷) = **1.0 × 10⁻²⁷ kg m/s**.
   Jaanch: 6.63 kat gaya; 10⁻³⁴ / 10⁻⁷ = 10⁻²⁷ ✅.

9. **True ✅.** Photon energy **E = h ν** frequency ke directly proportional hai!

10. **Nahi!** Photoelectric emission ke liye incident wavelength threshold wavelength se **chhoti ya barabar (λ ≤ λ₀)** honi chahiye! 700 nm > 620 nm (photon ki energy φ₀ se kam hai) ⟹ **NO emission!**
