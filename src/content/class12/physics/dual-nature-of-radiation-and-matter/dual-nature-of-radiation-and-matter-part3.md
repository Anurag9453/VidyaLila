Biology magazine mein **Rina** ne ek nanhe virus ki super-sharp 3D photo dekhi jiske neeche likha tha: *"Captured with an Electron Microscope — 100× sharper than any light microscope!"* Rina ne socha: "Light microscope ki limit light ki wavelength (400 nm) se hoti hai — par Electron Microscope mein to **electrons** (particles!) bheje jaate hain! Electrons ki apni wavelength kaise ho sakti hai?"

Teacher ma'am boli: "1924 mein Louis **de Broglie** ne ek brilliant symmetry sochi: *Nature symmetry pasand karti hai! Agar Light (wave) kabhi Particle (photon) ban sakti hai, to har chalta hua Particle (electron, proton, even cricket ball!) bhi ek **Wave (Matter Wave)** ki tarah behave karta hai!*"

---

## Naye Shabd (New Words)

- **Matter Waves (de Broglie Waves):** Har chalte hue material particle (mass m, velocity v) ke saath judi hui wave, jiski wavelength **λ = h / p = h / (m v)** hoti hai! *(Yeh EM waves nahi hoti — yeh neutral neutrons ke saath bhi hoti hain!)*
- **Davisson-Germer Experiment:** 1927 ka woh famous experiment jismein **54 V** par accelerated electrons ne Nickel crystal se **diffraction** dikhakar de Broglie ki matter-wave theory ko 100% sahi sabit kiya!

---

## 1. de Broglie Wavelength ke Chaar Super-Useful Forms

Photon ka momentum **p = h / λ** ⟹ **λ = h / p**. de Broglie ne yahi formula har particle (mass **m**, speed **v**, kinetic energy **K**) par lagaya:

1. **In terms of momentum (p) & velocity (v):**
   **λ = h / p = h / (m v)**
2. **In terms of Kinetic Energy (K):** (kyunki p = √(2 m K)):
   **λ = h / √(2 m K)**
3. **Charged particle (q, m) accelerated from rest through Potential Difference V:** (K = q V):
   **λ = h / √(2 m q V)**
4. **Electron Shortcut (SIRF Electron ke liye!):**
   h = 6.63 × 10⁻³⁴, m_e = 9.11 × 10⁻³¹, e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ rakhne par:

**λ_electron = 1.227 / √V nm = 12.27 / √V Å**

### Cricket Ball Wave Kyun Nahi Dikhati?
150 g (0.15 kg) ki cricket ball 20 m/s se jaaye to uska momentum p = 3 kg m/s ⟹ **λ = (6.63 × 10⁻³⁴) / 3 = 2.21 × 10⁻³⁴ m**! Yeh ek atomic nucleus (10⁻¹⁵ m) se bhi 10¹⁹ guna chhoti hai — isliye rozmarra ki badi cheezon ka wave nature kabhi dikhai nahi deta! Par **electron** ka mass bahut chhota (9.1 × 10⁻³¹ kg) hai, isliye uski wavelength (~0.1 nm = crystal atoms ke gap ke barabar!) aasaani se diffraction dikhati hai!

## 2. Davisson-Germer Experiment (Qualitative Confirmation)

1927 mein C.J. Davisson aur L.H. Germer ne low-voltage electron beam ko ek **Nickel crystal** par daala aur scattered electrons ki intensity alag-alag angles par naapi:
- **V = 54 V** par aur scattering angle **θ = 50°** par ek **sharp intensity peak (constructive diffraction maximum!)** mila!
- Crystal diffraction (Bragg's law) se naapi gayi electron wavelength aayi: **λ_exp = 0.165 nm**.
- de Broglie formula se: **λ_theory = 1.227 / √54 ≈ 0.167 nm**!
- Dono values ka exact match **Electron ke Wave Nature ka sabse pakka experimental saboot** bana!

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Electron Accelerated Through 100 V
An electron is accelerated from rest through a potential difference of **V = 100 V**. Calculate its de Broglie wavelength in nm and Angstroms (Å).

**Hal (Solution):**
- Electron shortcut: **λ = 1.227 / √V nm** = 1.227 / √100 = 1.227 / 10 = **0.1227 nm = 1.227 Å**.
- **Jaanch:** √100 = 10; 1.227 / 10 = 0.1227 nm (X-ray wavelength ke order ki — isliye Electron Microscope itna sharp hota hai!) ✅.

### Example 2: Electron vs Proton with Same Kinetic Energy
An electron and a proton have the **same kinetic energy K**. Which one has the **longer** de Broglie wavelength? (Given m_p ≈ 1836 m_e.)

**Hal (Solution):**
- Formula: **λ = h / √(2 m K)**.
- Same K ⟹ **λ ∝ 1 / √m**!
- Kyunki electron ka mass proton se bahut chhota hai (m_e << m_p), isliye **electron ki de Broglie wavelength zyada lambi (longer)** hogi — lagbhag **√1836 ≈ 42.8 guna lambi**!
- **Jaanch:** Smaller mass in denominator ⟹ larger λ ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **Proton ya Alpha particle par bhi 1.227 / √V nm laga dena:**
   - *Galat:* 100 V par proton ki λ = 0.1227 nm likh dena!
   - *Sahi:* **1.227 / √V nm SIRF ELECTRON ke liye hai!** Proton ya alpha particle ke liye hamesha general formula **λ = h / √(2 m q V)** use karo!
2. **Matter waves ko Electromagnetic waves bol dena:**
   - *Galat:* de Broglie waves EM waves ka hi ek type hain.
   - *Sahi:* Matter waves **EM waves NAHI hain** (uncharged neutron ki bhi de Broglie wavelength hoti hai, jabki EM waves sirf accelerating charges se banti hain)!
3. **V double ya 4× karne par λ ko V ke inverse maan lena (√ bhool jaana!):**
   - *Galat:* V ko 4 guna karne par λ 1/4 ho jayegi!
   - *Sahi:* **λ ∝ 1 / √V** ⟹ V ko **4 guna** karne par λ **aadhi (1/2)** hoti hai!

## Quick Recap

| Formula | Expression | Applicability |
|---|---|---|
| de Broglie Relation | λ = h / p = h / (m v) | Universal for ALL moving particles |
| In terms of KE or V | λ = h / √(2mK) = h / √(2mqV) | Any particle of mass m, charge q |
| Electron Shortcut | **λ_e = 1.227 / √V nm = 12.27 / √V Å** | **ONLY for electrons!** |
| Davisson-Germer | 54 V electrons on Ni crystal → peak at 50° | λ ≈ 0.167 nm — proved wave nature of electrons! |

## Practice Questions

1. Write the de Broglie relation for the wavelength of a moving particle in terms of (a) momentum p, and (b) kinetic energy K.
2. Why do macroscopic objects like a moving car or a football not show noticeable wave-like (diffraction) properties?
3. Calculate the de Broglie wavelength of an electron accelerated from rest through a potential difference of (a) 400 V, and (b) 10,000 V (10 kV).
4. By what factor does the de Broglie wavelength of an electron change if its accelerating potential V is increased to **9 times** its original value?
5. Calculate the de Broglie wavelength of a bullet of mass 0.04 kg moving at a speed of 1000 m/s (take h = 6.63 × 10⁻³⁴ J s).
6. A proton and an alpha particle (mass 4m_p, charge 2e) are accelerated from rest through the **same potential difference V**. Find the ratio of their de Broglie wavelengths **λ_p : λ_α**.
7. A proton and an electron have the **same de Broglie wavelength**. Which one has the greater momentum? Which one has the greater kinetic energy?
8. What was the aim and main conclusion of the Davisson-Germer experiment?
9. State whether True or False: An uncharged neutron moving at velocity v has NO de Broglie wavelength because it has zero charge.
10. Find the accelerating potential V needed to give an electron a de Broglie wavelength of 0.10 nm (= 1.0 Å).

### Answers

1. (a) **λ = h / p**; (b) **λ = h / √(2 m K)**.

2. Macroscopic objects ka mass m bahut zyada hota hai, isliye unki de Broglie wavelength **λ = h / (mv) ≈ 10⁻³⁴ m** itni zyada chhoti hoti hai ki kisi bhi slit ya obstacle se diffraction observe karna namumkin hai!

3. Electron shortcut λ = 1.227 / √V nm:
   (a) V = 400 V (√400 = 20): λ = 1.227 / 20 = **0.06135 nm ≈ 0.614 Å**.
   (b) V = 10,000 V (√10000 = 100): λ = 1.227 / 100 = **0.01227 nm ≈ 0.123 Å**.
   Jaanch: 1.227 / 20 = 0.06135; 1.227 / 100 = 0.01227 ✅.

4. λ ∝ 1 / √V ⟹ V ko 9 guna karne par √9 = 3 ⟹ **λ ghat kar 1/3 (one-third)** ho jayegi!

5. p = m v = 0.04 × 1000 = 40 kg m/s.
   λ = h / p = (6.63 × 10⁻³⁴) / 40 ≈ **1.66 × 10⁻³⁵ m**.
   Jaanch: 6.63 / 40 = 0.16575 ⟹ 1.66 × 10⁻³⁵ m ✅.

6. λ = h / √(2 m q V). Same V ⟹ **λ ∝ 1 / √(m q)**.
   Proton: m_p × e; Alpha: (4 m_p)(2 e) = 8 m_p e.
   λ_p / λ_α = √(8 / 1) = **2√2 : 1** (≈ 2.83 : 1).
   Jaanch: √(4 × 2) = √8 = 2√2 ✅.

7. Same λ ⟹ **p = h / λ dono ka BILKUL BARABAR (1:1)** hai!
   Kinetic energy **K = p² / (2 m)** ⟹ same p ke liye chhote mass wale **Electron ki kinetic energy zyada** hogi!

8. **Aim:** Electron ke wave nature (de Broglie hypothesis) ko experimentally check karna. **Conclusion:** 54 V par Nickel crystal se electron diffraction peak (λ ≈ 0.167 nm) mila, jisne **electrons ke wave nature** ko sabit kar diya!

9. **False ❌.** de Broglie wavelength **λ = h / (m v)** charge par nahi, mass aur velocity par depend karti hai — isliye chalte hue **neutrons ki bhi matter waves** hoti hain!

10. λ = 1.227 / √V nm ⟹ 0.10 = 1.227 / √V ⟹ √V = 12.27 ⟹ **V = (12.27)² ≈ 150.6 V**.
    Jaanch: √150.6 ≈ 12.27; 1.227 / 12.27 = 0.10 nm ✅.
