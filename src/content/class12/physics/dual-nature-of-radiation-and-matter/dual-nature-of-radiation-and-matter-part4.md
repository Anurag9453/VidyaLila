## Mixed Solved Examples (Sab Kuch Mila Kar!)

### Example 1: Photoelectric Effect + de Broglie Wavelength of Fastest Photoelectron!
Light of wavelength **λ = 310 nm** falls on a metal of work function **φ₀ = 2.40 eV**.
(a) Find the stopping potential **V₀** of the emitted photoelectrons.
(b) Find the **minimum de Broglie wavelength** of the emitted photoelectrons!

**Hal (Solution):**
- (a) Incident photon energy: **E = 1240 / 310 = 4.0 eV**.
  K_max = E − φ₀ = 4.0 − 2.40 = **1.60 eV** ⟹ **V₀ = 1.60 V**!
- (b) Sabse chhoti (minimum) de Broglie wavelength sabse tez (K_max = e V₀) electrons ki hogi!
  Electron shortcut seedha **V = V₀ = 1.60 V** ke saath lagao (√1.60 ≈ 1.265):
  **λ_min = 1.227 / √1.60 ≈ 1.227 / 1.265 ≈ 0.97 nm**!
- **Jaanch:** 310 × 4 = 1240; 4.0 − 2.4 = 1.6 eV ✅; 1.227 / 1.265 ≈ 0.970 nm ✅.

### Example 2: Same Momentum vs Same Energy (Photon vs Electron)
An electron and a photon both have the same wavelength **λ = 1.0 nm**. Compare (a) their momenta, and (b) their energies!

**Hal (Solution):**
- (a) Dono ke liye **p = h / λ** ⟹ same λ hone par dono ka **momentum BILKUL BARABAR (1 : 1)** hai!
  p = (6.63 × 10⁻³⁴) / (1.0 × 10⁻⁹) = **6.63 × 10⁻²⁵ kg m/s**.
- (b) **Photon energy:** E_photon = 1240 / 1.0 = **1240 eV**!
  **Electron kinetic energy:** λ_e = 1.227 / √V = 1.0 nm ⟹ √V = 1.227 ⟹ **K_electron = (1.227)² ≈ 1.51 eV**!
  *(Dekha? Same wavelength par photon ki energy electron se lagbhag **820 guna zyada** hoti hai!)*
- **Jaanch:** p same (h/λ); E_photon = 1240 eV >> 1.51 eV ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Synthesis Mistakes)

1. **Photoelectron ki max speed nikaalte waqt K_max ko eV mein hi rakh dena:**
   - *Galat:* v_max = √(2 × 1.6 / m_e) likhna jab K_max = 1.6 eV ho!
   - *Sahi:* Pehle **K_max (J) = 1.6 × 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ J** mein convert karo, tab √(2 K_max / m_e) lagao!
2. **Threshold wavelength λ₀ ke liye λ ≥ λ₀ samajh lena:**
   - *Galat:* Emission ke liye λ > λ₀ chahiye!
   - *Sahi:* Frequency ke liye **ν ≥ ν₀**, par wavelength inverse hai ⟹ emission ke liye **λ ≤ λ₀** (chhoti wavelength = zyada energy)!
3. **Bade work function wale metal ki threshold wavelength badi bol dena:**
   - *Galat:* φ₀ bada hai to λ₀ bhi bada hoga!
   - *Sahi:* **λ₀ = h c / φ₀** ⟹ bada work function = **chhoti threshold wavelength λ₀**!

## Practice Questions (Mixed Chapter Challenge)

1. Light of wavelength 200 nm (E = 6.2 eV) falls on a metal of work function 4.2 eV. Calculate (a) K_max in eV and Joules, and (b) stopping potential V₀.
2. What is the minimum de Broglie wavelength of the emitted photoelectrons in Question 1? (Take √2 = 1.414.)
3. The threshold wavelength of a metal is 400 nm. Calculate its work function in eV (use hc = 1240 eV nm).
4. A 10 mW monochromatic source emits photons of energy 2.5 eV each. Calculate the number of photons emitted per second (1 eV = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ J).
5. An alpha particle and a proton are accelerated through the **same potential difference V**. If the de Broglie wavelength of the proton is 0.04 nm, find the de Broglie wavelength of the alpha particle (take √2 = 1.414).
6. An electron is accelerated through 64 V. Calculate its de Broglie wavelength in nm and Å.
7. Two metals X and Y have work functions 2.0 eV and 5.0 eV. Light of wavelength 413 nm (E = 3.0 eV) shines on both. From which metal(s) will photoelectrons be emitted?
8. What is the ratio of the de Broglie wavelength of a photon to that of an electron if both have the **same momentum p**?
9. State whether True or False: In a photoelectric cell, if both the intensity and frequency of incident light are doubled (with ν > ν₀), the stopping potential V₀ becomes more than double.
10. Find the kinetic energy (in eV) of an electron whose de Broglie wavelength is 0.2454 nm.

### Answers

1. (a) K_max = 6.2 − 4.2 = **2.0 eV** = 2.0 × 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ = **3.2 × 10⁻¹⁹ J**.
   (b) **V₀ = 2.0 V**.
   Jaanch: 6.2 − 4.2 = 2.0 eV ✅.

2. λ_min = 1.227 / √V₀ = 1.227 / √2 = 1.227 / 1.414 ≈ **0.868 nm**.
   Jaanch: 0.868 × 1.414 ≈ 1.227 ✅.

3. φ₀ = 1240 / 400 = **3.10 eV**.
   Jaanch: 3.1 × 400 = 1240 ✅.

4. Ek photon ki energy: E = 2.5 × 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ = 4.0 × 10⁻¹⁹ J.
   N / t = P / E = (10 × 10⁻³) / (4.0 × 10⁻¹⁹) = **2.5 × 10¹⁶ photons/s**.
   Jaanch: 2.5 × 4 = 10; 10¹⁶ × 10⁻¹⁹ = 10⁻³ W = 10 mW ✅.

5. λ_p / λ_α = 2√2 ≈ 2.828 ⟹ λ_α = 0.04 / 2.828 ≈ **0.0141 nm**.
   Jaanch: 0.01414 × 2.828 ≈ 0.040 nm ✅.

6. √64 = 8 ⟹ λ_e = 1.227 / 8 ≈ **0.153 nm = 1.53 Å**.
   Jaanch: 0.1534 × 8 = 1.227 ✅.

7. Incident photon energy = 3.0 eV. Metal X (2.0 eV < 3.0 eV): **Haan, emission hoga!** (K_max = 1.0 eV). Metal Y (5.0 eV > 3.0 eV): **Nahi, zero emission!**

8. **1 : 1** — kyunki dono ke liye λ = h / p!

9. **True ✅.** Pehle e V₀ = h ν − φ₀; ν double karne par e V'₀ = 2 h ν − φ₀ = 2(h ν − φ₀) + φ₀ = 2 e V₀ + φ₀ > 2 e V₀! (Kyunki φ₀ positive hai, V'₀ hamesha 2 V₀ se **zyada** hota hai!)

10. λ_e = 1.227 / √V = 0.2454 nm ⟹ √V = 1.227 / 0.2454 = **5.0** ⟹ V = 25 V ⟹ **KE = 25 eV**.
    Jaanch: 0.2454 × 5 = 1.227; 5² = 25 eV ✅.
