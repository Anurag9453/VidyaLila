## Naye Shabd (New Words)

- **Work Function (φ₀):** Kisi metal ki satah se ek free electron ko theek bahar nikaalne ke liye zaroori **minimum energy**. Unit **electron-volt (eV)** (1 eV = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ J). (Caesium ka sabse kam ~2.14 eV; Platinum ka zyada ~5.65 eV.)
- **Photoelectric Effect:** Kisi metal satah par suitable high-frequency light (UV ya visible) girne par usse electrons (**photoelectrons**) ka turant bahar nikalna!
- **Threshold Frequency (ν₀):** Woh **minimum frequency** jiske neeche (ν < ν₀) kitni bhi tez light daalo, metal se ek bhi photoelectron bahar nahi nikalta!
- **Stopping Potential (V₀):** Collector plate par lagaya gaya woh minimum **negative** voltage jo sabse tez bhagne wale photoelectrons ko bhi rok kar photocurrent ko **zero** kar de: **K_max = e V₀**.

---

## 1. Electron Emission ke Teen Tareeke

Metal ke andar free electrons hote hain, par positive ions ke attraction ke karan woh satah se bahar nahi nikal paate jab tak unhein kam se kam **φ₀ (Work Function)** jitni energy na mile:
1. **Thermionic Emission:** Metal ko bahut garam karke (heat energy dekar).
2. **Field Emission:** Bahut tez electric field (~10⁸ V/m) lagakar.
3. **Photoelectric Emission:** Suitable frequency ki **light** daalkar!

---

## 2. Hertz-Lenard Observations — Photoelectric Effect ke 4 Pakke Niyam!

1. **Threshold Frequency (ν₀) ka Niyam:** Har metal ke liye ek khaas minimum frequency **ν₀** hoti hai. Agar incident light ki frequency **ν < ν₀** ho, to chahe light kitni bhi **bright (high intensity)** ho, **ZERO photoelectrons** nikalenge!
2. **Intensity vs Photocurrent:** Agar ν > ν₀ ho, to **photocurrent (per second nikalne wale electrons ki ginti)** light ki **intensity ke directly proportional** hoti hai! Par intensity badhane se electrons ki **maximum kinetic energy (K_max) ya stopping potential (V₀) BILKUL NAHI badalta**!
3. **Frequency vs Stopping Potential (V₀):** Light ki **frequency ν** badhane par (ν > ν₀) photoelectrons ki **K_max aur stopping potential V₀ linearly badhte hain**!
4. **Instantaneous Process:** Light padne aur electron nikalne ke beech **koi time lag nahi** hota (~10⁻⁹ s mein turant nikal jaata hai — chahe light kitni hi dim kyun na ho!).

```
   Photocurrent (I)                    Stopping Potential (V0)
   ^                                   ^
   :      ________ I2 (high intensity) :        / Metal A
   :     /________ I1 (low intensity)  :       /  / Metal B
   :    /                              :      /  /
   +---*-----------> V                 +-----*--*-------> frequency (nu)
      -V0 (SAME!)                           nu0A nu0B
```

### Classical Wave Theory Yahan Kyun Fail Ho Gayi?
1. Wave theory kehti thi ki zyada intensity (zyada amplitude) wali light se **K_max badhni chahiye** — par experiment mein K_max intensity par depend hi nahi karti!
2. Wave theory kehti thi ki kisi bhi frequency (chahe red) ki tez light dheere-dheere energy jama karke electron nikaal degi — par **ν < ν₀** par kabhi ek electron nahi nikla!
3. Wave theory ke hisaab se dim light ko energy jama karne mein ghanton lagne chahiye the — par emission **instantaneous** (< 10⁻⁹ s) hota hai!

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Stopping Potential & Maximum Kinetic Energy
In a photoelectric experiment, the stopping potential is found to be **V₀ = 1.5 V**. Calculate the maximum kinetic energy **K_max** of the emitted photoelectrons in (a) **eV**, and (b) **Joules**.

**Hal (Solution):**
- (a) **K_max = e V₀ = 1.5 eV**.
- (b) Joules mein: **K_max = 1.5 × 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ = 2.4 × 10⁻¹⁹ J**.
- **Jaanch:** 1.5 × 1.6 = 2.4 ⟹ 2.4 × 10⁻¹⁹ J ✅.

### Example 2: Effect of Doubling Intensity vs Increasing Frequency
Monochromatic light of frequency ν (> ν₀) falls on a photocell. What happens to (a) saturation photocurrent, and (b) stopping potential V₀ if:
(i) the **intensity** of light is doubled (keeping frequency constant)?
(ii) the **frequency** of light is increased (keeping intensity constant)?

**Hal (Solution):**
- (i) **Intensity doubled:** Saturation photocurrent **doubles** (2× electrons per second!); Stopping potential V₀ stays **UNCHANGED**!
- (ii) **Frequency increased:** Stopping potential V₀ **increases** (faster electrons!); Saturation photocurrent depends on photon count (not higher K_max).
- **Jaanch:** Intensity controls **count (I)**; Frequency controls **energy (K_max, V₀)** ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **Intensity badhane par K_max ya Stopping Potential V₀ badha dena:**
   - *Galat:* Bulb ko 2 guna bright karne par photoelectrons ki speed badh jayegi!
   - *Sahi:* Intensity badhane se sirf **electrons ki ginti (photocurrent)** badhti hai — unki **K_max aur V₀ bilkul nahi badalte**!
2. **Threshold frequency se kam frequency par bright light se emission kara dena:**
   - *Galat:* Red light ko 1000× tez kar dein to zinc se electrons nikal aayenge!
   - *Sahi:* Agar **ν < ν₀**, to chahe intensity infinite kar do, **ZERO electrons** nikalenge!
3. **Saturation current aur Stopping potential ko confuse karna:**
   - *Galat:* V₀ ko maximum current bol dena.
   - *Sahi:* **V₀** collector ka woh **negative voltage** hai jahan current **zero** ho jaata hai; **Saturation current** positive voltage par milne wala **maximum steady current** hai!

## Quick Recap

| Term / Observation | Meaning / Relation |
|---|---|
| Work Function (φ₀) | Minimum energy to eject an electron from metal (unit: eV) |
| Threshold Frequency (ν₀) | Minimum frequency for emission (ν < ν₀ ⟹ NO emission!) |
| Effect of Intensity (ν > ν₀) | Photocurrent **∝ Intensity**; **K_max & V₀ UNCHANGED** |
| Effect of Frequency (ν > ν₀) | **K_max = e V₀** increases linearly with ν |
| Time Lag | **Zero** (instantaneous, < 10⁻⁹ s) — Wave theory fails! |

## Practice Questions

1. Define (a) work function of a metal, (b) threshold frequency, and (c) stopping potential.
2. Why are alkali metals like Caesium and Potassium commonly used as photosensitive materials in photocells?
3. State three experimental observations of the photoelectric effect that cannot be explained by classical wave theory of light.
4. The maximum kinetic energy of photoelectrons emitted from a metal surface is 3.2 × 10⁻¹⁹ J. Calculate the stopping potential V₀ in Volts.
5. If the stopping potential in a photoelectric experiment is 2.5 V, what is the maximum kinetic energy of the photoelectrons in eV and in Joules?
6. How does the saturation photocurrent change when the distance between a point light source and the photocell is halved (keeping frequency ν > ν₀ constant)? (Hint: intensity I ∝ 1/r²!)
7. What happens to the stopping potential in Question 6 when the point light source is brought closer?
8. Name the three main ways to emit free electrons from a metal surface.
9. State whether True or False: If light of frequency less than the threshold frequency ν₀ shines on a metal for a very long time, photoelectrons will eventually be emitted.
10. Two metals A and B have work functions 2.2 eV and 4.5 eV respectively. Which metal has the higher threshold frequency ν₀?

### Answers

1. (a) **Work function (φ₀):** Metal satah se ek electron bahar nikaalne ki minimum energy.
   (b) **Threshold frequency (ν₀):** Minimum frequency jiske bina photoelectric emission nahi hota.
   (c) **Stopping potential (V₀):** Minimum negative collector voltage jo photocurrent ko zero kar de (K_max = eV₀).

2. Caesium aur Potassium ka **work function φ₀ bahut kam** (~2.1–2.3 eV) hota hai, isliye woh aam **visible light** se bhi aasaani se photoelectrons emit kar dete hain!

3. (i) K_max intensity par depend nahi karti (wave theory kehti thi depend karni chahiye); (ii) Threshold frequency ν₀ ka hona (ν < ν₀ par zero emission); (iii) Emission ka instantaneous (< 10⁻⁹ s) hona (koi time lag nahi!).

4. K_max = e V₀ ⟹ V₀ = (3.2 × 10⁻¹⁹) / (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹) = **2.0 V**.
   Jaanch: 2.0 × 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ = 3.2 × 10⁻¹⁹ J ✅.

5. **K_max = 2.5 eV** = 2.5 × 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ = **4.0 × 10⁻¹⁹ J**.
   Jaanch: 2.5 × 1.6 = 4.0 ✅.

6. Doori r aadhi hone par intensity **I ∝ 1/r²** **4 guna (4 times)** badh jaati hai ⟹ **saturation photocurrent 4 guna** badh jayega!

7. Stopping potential V₀ **bilkul nahi badlega (unchanged)**, kyunki V₀ sirf frequency par depend karta hai, intensity par nahi!

8. (i) **Thermionic emission** (heating), (ii) **Field emission** (strong electric field), (iii) **Photoelectric emission** (light).

9. **False ❌.** Agar ν < ν₀ ho, to chahe kitni hi der ya kitni hi bright light daalo, **kabhi koi electron nahi nikalta**!

10. **Metal B (4.5 eV)** ki threshold frequency ν₀ zyada hogi (kyunki φ₀ = h ν₀ ⟹ zyada work function = zyada threshold frequency)!
