# Topic 2: Electric Field, Field Lines Aur Electric Dipole in an Electric Field

Topic 1 mein humne dekha ki do charges bina ek-doosre ko chhue door se force lagate hain. Par Michael Faraday ne ek gehra sawaal poocha: *"Charge q₁ ko kaise pata chalta hai ki wahan q₂ baitha hai?"* Unka jawab tha **Electric Field**: har charge apne charon taraf space mein ek adrishya (invisible) prabhav-kshetra bana deta hai, aur doosra charge usi field ko mehsoos karke bal khata hai! Is topic mein hum **Electric Field (E)**, **Electric Field Lines**, aur **Electric Dipole** (axial/equatorial field aur torque) seekhenge.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Electric field (E)** | Kisi point par unit positive test charge (+1 C) par lagne wala force: **E = F / q₀**; SI unit: **N/C** (ya V/m) | Point charge: E = kq / r² |

## Part A: Electric Field (E) Aur Field Lines

Kisi source charge Q se r doori par ek bahut chhota positive test charge q₀ rakho (itna chhota ki Q ki position na hile!). Tab us point par **Electric Field**:

> **E = F / q₀ = k Q / r²**   *(vector: +Q se **radially outward**, −Q ki taraf **radially inward**!)*

- Agar us point par koi bhi charge q rakha jaaye, to us par force: **F = qE** (+q par E ki disha mein, −q par E ke ulte!).
- **Superposition of Electric Fields:** Kai charges ke karan kisi point par net field unke alag-alag fields ka **vector sum** hota hai: **E_net = E₁ + E₂ + ...**.

### Electric Field Lines ke 5 Pakke Niyam

1. Field lines **positive (+)** charge se shuru hoti hain aur **negative (−)** charge par khatam hoti hain (akela charge ho to ∞ tak jaati/aati hain).
2. Kisi bhi point par **tangent** us point par net E ki disha batata hai.
3. **Do field lines KABHI ek-doosre ko kaat (intersect) nahi saktin!** *Kyun?* Agar kaatein, to intersection point par do tangents hongi — yaani ek hi jagah electric field ki **do alag dishaayein**, jo namumkin hai!
4. Electrostatic field lines **closed loops nahi banatin** (magnetic field lines ke ulta!).
5. Jahan lines **paas-paas (crowded)** hon wahan E **strong** hai; jahan **door-door** hon wahan E **weak** hai. Uniform field mein lines **parallel aur equidistant** hoti hain.

### Example 1: Point Charge ka Electric Field

**q = +5 μC** charge se **0.5 m** door electric field E nikalo, aur batao us point par **−2 nC** charge rakhne par kitna force lagega?

**Hal:**
1. E = kq / r² = (9 × 10⁹ × 5 × 10⁻⁶) / (0.5)² = (45 × 10³) / 0.25 = **1.8 × 10⁵ N/C** (charge se bahar ki taraf).
2. Force F = |q₀| E = (2 × 10⁻⁹) × (1.8 × 10⁵) = **3.6 × 10⁻⁴ N** (kyunki q₀ negative hai, force E ke **ulte**, yaani +5 μC ki taraf lagega!).

**Jaanch:** 45 / 0.25 = 180, 180 × 10³ = 1.8 × 10⁵ N/C ✅; 2 × 1.8 × 10⁻⁴ = 3.6 × 10⁻⁴ N ✅.

## Part B: Electric Dipole — Axial Field, Equatorial Field Aur Torque

Do barabar aur ulte charges **−q** aur **+q** ek chhoti doori **2a** par rakhe hon, to unka **Dipole Moment**:
> **p = q × 2a**   *(direction: **−q se +q** ki taraf!)*

```
                   Equatorial point (E_eq <--, opposite to p!)
                         :
                         : r
                         :
       −q  o------a------+------a------o  +q -------->  Axial point (E_ax -->, along p!)
                   (centre O)
                p: ---------->  (−q se +q)
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Electric dipole: p ki disha −q se +q hai. Axial point par net field **p ki disha mein** hota hai, aur equatorial point par **p ke ulte** hota hai!

### 1. Axial Line Par Electric Field (Centre O se r doori par)
- +q se doori (r − a) → E₊ = kq / (r − a)² (p ki disha mein)
- −q se doori (r + a) → E₋ = kq / (r + a)² (p ke ulte)
- Net E_axial = kq [1/(r − a)² − 1/(r + a)²] = kq [4ar / (r² − a²)²] = **2kpr / (r² − a²)²** (along p!).
- **Short dipole (r ≫ a):** a² ko chhod do:
  > **E_axial = 2kp / r³ = [1 / (4πε₀)] · (2p / r³)**   *(direction: along p)*

### 2. Equatorial Line Par Electric Field (Centre O se r doori par)
- Dono charges se doori √(r² + a²) barabar hai, to E₊ = E₋ = kq / (r² + a²).
- Vertical (sin θ) components aapas mein kat jaate hain; horizontal (cos θ = a / √(r² + a²)) components **−p ki disha mein** jud jaate hain!
- E_eq = 2 E₊ cos θ = **kp / (r² + a²)^(3/2)** (opposite to p!).
- **Short dipole (r ≫ a):**
  > **E_eq = kp / r³ = [1 / (4πε₀)] · (p / r³)**   *(direction: opposite to p)*

**Super-Important Relation:** Barabar doori r par short dipole ke liye: **E_axial = 2 E_eq**!

### 3. Uniform Electric Field Mein Dipole Par Force Aur Torque
- +q par force **+qE** aur −q par force **−qE** → **Net Force = +qE − qE = 0** (uniform field mein dipole aage-peeche nahi bhagta!).
- Par dono forces alag lines par lagte hain (couple!), jinke beech perpendicular doori **2a sin θ** hai!
- Isliye **Torque:**
  > **τ = (qE) × (2a sin θ) = pE sin θ**   *(vector form: **τ = p × E**)*
- **θ = 0°:** τ = 0 (**Stable equilibrium** — p aur E parallel).
- **θ = 180°:** τ = 0 (**Unstable equilibrium** — p aur E anti-parallel).
- **θ = 90°:** τ_max = **pE**!

### Example 2: Short Dipole ka Axial aur Equatorial Field

Ek electric dipole mein **±4 μC** charges **2 mm** door rakhe hain.
(i) Dipole moment p nikalo.
(ii) Centre se **20 cm** door Axial aur Equatorial points par electric field nikalo.

**Hal:**
- (i) q = 4 × 10⁻⁶ C, 2a = 2 mm = 2 × 10⁻³ m:
  **p = q × 2a** = (4 × 10⁻⁶)(2 × 10⁻³) = **8 × 10⁻⁹ C m**.
- (ii) r = 20 cm = 0.2 m (≫ a = 1 mm, short dipole valid!); r³ = (0.2)³ = 8 × 10⁻³ m³:
  - **E_axial** = 2kp / r³ = (2 × 9 × 10⁹ × 8 × 10⁻⁹) / (8 × 10⁻³) = 18 / (10⁻³) = **1.8 × 10⁴ N/C** (along p).
  - **E_eq** = E_axial / 2 = **9.0 × 10³ N/C** (opposite to p).

**Jaanch:** 2 × 9 × 8 / 8 = 18, 10⁰ / 10⁻³ = 10³ → 1.8 × 10⁴ N/C ✅; E_axial / E_eq = 2 ✅!

### Example 3: Uniform Field Mein Dipole Par Torque

Dipole moment **p = 4 × 10⁻⁹ C m** wala ek dipole **E = 5 × 10⁴ N/C** ke uniform field ke saath **30°** ke angle par rakha hai. Torque nikalo!

**Hal:**
τ = pE sin θ = (4 × 10⁻⁹)(5 × 10⁴) sin 30° = (20 × 10⁻⁵)(0.5) = **1.0 × 10⁻⁴ N m**.

**Jaanch:** 4 × 5 × 0.5 = 10; 10 × 10⁻⁵ = 1.0 × 10⁻⁴ N m ✅.

## Aam galtiyan

1. **Dipole length 2a ko galti se 'a' maan kar p = q × 2 × (2a) kar dena:**
   - *Sahi soch:* Dono charges ke beech ki **poori doori hi 2a** hoti hai! Agar doori 2 mm di hai, to 2a = 2 mm (p = q × 2 mm, dobara 2 se multiply mat karo!).
2. **Short dipole ke field mein 1/r² laga dena (1/r³ ke bajay):**
   - *Yaad rakho:* Point charge → **E ∝ 1/r²**; Short dipole → **E ∝ 1/r³** (bahut tezi se ghatta hai!).
3. **Uniform field mein dipole par net force non-zero samajhna:**
   - *Sahi soch:* Uniform field mein **F_net = 0** hamesha, sirf torque lagta hai! (Net force sirf **non-uniform** field mein lagta hai.)

---

## Quick Recap

- **E = F/q₀ = kQ/r²** (+Q se bahar, −Q ki taraf); F = qE.
- Field lines: + se −, kabhi intersect nahi kartin, closed loops nahi banatin.
- **Dipole:** p = q(2a) (−q se +q); **E_axial = 2kp/r³** (along p); **E_eq = kp/r³** (opposite to p); **E_axial = 2 E_eq**.
- **Uniform field:** F_net = 0, **τ = pE sin θ**.

## Practice Questions

1. Find the magnitude and direction of the electric field at a distance of **30 cm** from a point charge of **−6 μC**.

2. An electron falls through a distance of **1.5 cm** in a uniform electric field of magnitude **2.0 × 10⁴ N/C** (directed upward, so the force on the electron is downward). Find the acceleration of the electron and the time of fall. (m_e = 9.1 × 10⁻³¹ kg, e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C; neglect gravity.)

3. Two point charges **q₁ = +3 μC** and **q₂ = −3 μC** are located **20 cm** apart in vacuum.
   (a) What is the electric field at the midpoint O of the line AB joining the two charges?
   (b) If a negative test charge of magnitude **1.5 × 10⁻⁹ C** is placed at O, what is the force experienced by it?

4. A system has two charges **q_A = +2.5 × 10⁻⁷ C** and **q_B = −2.5 × 10⁻⁷ C** located at points A(0, 0, −15 cm) and B(0, 0, +15 cm) respectively. What are the total charge and electric dipole moment of the system?

5. An electric dipole of dipole moment **5 × 10⁻⁹ C m** is placed in a uniform electric field of **4 × 10⁴ N/C** at an angle of **60°** to the field. Calculate the magnitude of the torque acting on it.

6. Calculate the electric field at a distance of **10 cm** from the centre of a short dipole of dipole moment **2 × 10⁻⁹ C m** on:
   (a) its axial line, and
   (b) its equatorial line.

7. Why can two electric field lines never cross each other? Explain in two lines.

8. An electric dipole of length **4 cm**, when placed with its axis making an angle of **30°** with a uniform electric field of **10⁵ N/C**, experiences a torque of **4 N m**. Calculate the magnitude of charge on each end of the dipole.

9. At what orientation θ in a uniform electric field E is an electric dipole in (a) stable equilibrium, (b) unstable equilibrium? What is the torque at both angles?

10. Two point charges **+8 μC** and **+2 μC** are placed **30 cm** apart. Find the point on the line joining them where the net electric field is **zero**.

### Answers

1. E = (9 × 10⁹ × 6 × 10⁻⁶) / (0.3)² = (54 × 10³) / 0.09 = **6 × 10⁵ N/C**, directed **towards** the −6 μC charge. Jaanch: 54/0.09 = 600 ✅.

2. a = eE / m_e = (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ × 2.0 × 10⁴) / (9.1 × 10⁻³¹) = **3.52 × 10¹⁵ m/s²**.
   t = √(2h / a) = √(2 × 0.015 / 3.52 × 10¹⁵) = √(8.52 × 10⁻¹⁸) ≈ **2.92 × 10⁻⁹ s** (2.92 ns!).

3. (a) Midpoint O par dono charges (r = 0.1 m) ka field **B (−3 μC) ki taraf** judta hai!
   E₁ = E₂ = (9 × 10⁹ × 3 × 10⁻⁶) / 0.01 = 2.7 × 10⁶ N/C.
   E_net = 2 × 2.7 × 10⁶ = **5.4 × 10⁶ N/C** (along OB, towards −3 μC).
   (b) F = |q₀| E = (1.5 × 10⁻⁹)(5.4 × 10⁶) = **8.1 × 10⁻³ N** (along OA, towards +3 μC, kyunki q₀ negative hai!).
   Jaanch: 1.5 × 5.4 = 8.1 ✅.

4. Total charge = +2.5 × 10⁻⁷ − 2.5 × 10⁻⁷ = **0**.
   Separation 2a = 15 − (−15) = **30 cm = 0.3 m**.
   p = q(2a) = (2.5 × 10⁻⁷)(0.3) = **7.5 × 10⁻⁸ C m** (from B to A, i.e., along negative z-axis!).
   Jaanch: 2.5 × 0.3 = 0.75 ✅.

5. τ = pE sin 60° = (5 × 10⁻⁹)(4 × 10⁴)(√3/2) = 10⁻⁴ √3 ≈ **1.73 × 10⁻⁴ N m**.

6. r = 0.1 m ⟹ r³ = 10⁻³ m³:
   (a) E_axial = (2 × 9 × 10⁹ × 2 × 10⁻⁹) / 10⁻³ = **3.6 × 10⁴ N/C**.
   (b) E_eq = E_axial / 2 = **1.8 × 10⁴ N/C**. Jaanch: 36/10⁻³ = 3.6 × 10⁴ ✅.

7. Agar do field lines kisi point par kaatein, to us ek hi point par do tangents banengi, jiska matlab wahan net electric field ki **do alag dishaayein** hongi — jo physical roop se namumkin hai.

8. τ = q(2a)E sin 30° ⟹ 4 = q(0.04)(10⁵)(0.5) = 2000 q ⟹ **q = 4/2000 = 2 × 10⁻³ C = 2 mC**.
   Jaanch: (2 × 10⁻³)(0.04)(10⁵)(0.5) = 4 N m ✅.

9. (a) **θ = 0°** (stable equilibrium), (b) **θ = 180°** (unstable equilibrium). Dono angles par **τ = 0**.

10. +2 μC se x cm door (beech mein): 8/(30 − x)² = 2/x² ⟹ 4/(30 − x)² = 1/x² ⟹ 2/(30 − x) = 1/x ⟹ 3x = 30 ⟹ **x = 10 cm from +2 μC** (20 cm from +8 μC).
    Jaanch: 8/400 = 2/100 ✅.
