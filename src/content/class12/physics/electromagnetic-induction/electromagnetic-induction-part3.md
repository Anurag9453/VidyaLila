## Magnet ke Beech Bina Chhooye Ruki Copper Plate!

Physics lab mein teacher ma'am ne ek strong horseshoe magnet ke dono poles ke beech ek solid copper ki plate ko pendulum ki tarah latka diya (safe classroom demo). **Leena** ne dekha ki jab ma'am ne plate ko chhoda, to woh hawa mein hi bina magnet ko chhooye achanak **dheemi hokar turant ruk gayi**! Phir ma'am ne waisi hi doosri copper plate lagayi jismein kanghi (comb) jaise **patle slots (cuts)** kate the — aur woh plate aaram se kaafi der tak jhoolti rahi!

Leena ne poocha: "Ma'am, copper to chumbak se chipakta bhi nahi hai, phir solid copper plate bina chhooye brake kaise lag gayi — aur slots kaatne par brake gayab kyun ho gayi?"

Teacher ma'am boli: "Solid metal plate ke andar badalte magnetic flux se paani ke bhanwar (whirlpools) jaise gol-gol induced currents ban jaate hain — inhein **Eddy Currents** kehte hain!"

---

## Naye Shabd (New Words)

- **Eddy Currents (Foucault Currents):** Kisi solid bulk conductor (jaise copper, aluminium, ya iron ki moti plate/block) ko badalte magnetic field mein rakhne ya usmein hilane par uske andar banne wale **gol-gol bandh (looping) induced currents**.
- **Laminated Core:** Ek solid metal block ki jagah bahut saari patli insulated metal sheets (varnish/lacquer se alag ki hui) ko chipka kar banaya gaya core, jo eddy currents ko bahut kam kar deta hai.
- **Electromagnetic Damping:** Eddy currents ke Lenz-law virodh ka upyog karke kisi ghoomti ya jhoolti coil (jaise galvanometer) ko bina jhatke ke turant shant (rest par) laana.

## 1. Eddy Currents Kaise Bante Hain aur Unka Nuksaan Kya Hai?

Jab koi moti conducting plate magnetic field ke andar jaati hai ya bahar nikalti hai, to usse guzarne wala magnetic flux badalta hai. Kyunki poori plate conductor hai, isliye electrons chhote-chhote **bhanwar (eddies)** ke roop mein poore metal ke andar ghoomne lagte hain!

- Lenz's law ke anusaar yeh eddy currents **plate ki gati ka virodh (oppose)** karte hain — isliye Leena ki solid copper plate turant ruk gayi!
- **Undesirable Effect (Nuksaan):** Transformers, electric motors aur dynamos ke iron cores mein jab AC (badalta magnetic field) guzarta hai, to eddy currents bahut zyada **Joule heat (I²R)** paida karte hain — jisse energy waste hoti hai aur machine garam hokar kharaab ho sakti hai!

### Eddy Currents ko Kam Kaise Karein?
1. **Slots (cuts) kaat kar:** Plate mein patle cuts laga dene se bade gol loops toot jaate hain ⟹ eddy current ka raasta lamba aur resistance bahut zyada ho jaata hai ⟹ eddy currents kam ho jaate hain!
2. **Laminated Core banakar:** Transformer ya motor ke soft iron core ko ek solid block ki jagah **patli-patli insulated (lacquer-coated) pattiyon (laminations)** ko jorkar banate hain, jinka plane magnetic field ke parallel rakha jaata hai!

```
   Solid Copper Plate          Slotted Copper Plate
   (Big eddy loops!)           (Loops broken -> weak!)
   +---------------+           +---+ +---+ +---+
   :   (  -->  )   :           :   : :   : :   :
   :   (  <--  )   :           :   : :   : :   :
   +---------------+           +---+ +---+ +---+
```

## 2. Eddy Currents ke 4 Zabardast Uses (Faayde!)

Nuksaan ke saath-saath engineers ne eddy currents ko 4 kamaal ke kaamon mein lagaya hai:

1. **Magnetic Braking in Trains:** High-speed electric trains mein rails ke upar strong electromagnets lage hote hain. Switch on karte hi rails mein eddy currents bante hain jo train ki gati ka virodh karte hain — **bina kisi mechanical friction ya jhatke ke smooth brake!**
2. **Electromagnetic Damping (Dead-Beat Galvanometer):** Moving coil galvanometer ki coil ko non-magnetic metallic (aluminium) frame par lapeta jaata hai. Jab coil ghoomti hai, frame mein bane eddy currents use extra hilne-dulne nahi dete aur sui seedha exact reading par aakar ruk jaati hai!
3. **Induction Furnace:** High-frequency AC coil ke andar rakhe metal (jaise steel/alloys) mein itne tez eddy currents bante hain ki metal bina aag lagaye pighal (melt) jaata hai! (Ghar ka **induction cooktop** bhi bilkul isi principle par steel ke bartan ko garam karta hai!)
4. **Electric Power Meters (Analog Energy Meters):** Ghar ke purane bijli meter mein ghoomne wali chamakdaar **aluminium disc** mein AC coil se eddy currents bante hain jo use ghumaate hain aur bijli ki khapat record karte hain!

## Solved Conceptual Examples

### Example 1: Aluminium Ring Dropping Through a Magnet
A solid aluminium ring and a cut (broken) aluminium ring of the same size are dropped simultaneously from the same height into a strong vertical magnetic field. Which ring hits the ground first?

**Hal (Solution):**
- **Cut (broken) ring** pehle zameen par giregi!
- *Kyun?* Solid ring closed loop hai ⟹ neeche girte waqt usmein eddy/induced currents bante hain jo Lenz's law se girne ka virodh (upward braking force) karte hain, isliye woh dheere girti hai. Cut ring mein closed path nahi hai ⟹ koi induced current nahi ⟹ sirf gravity (g) se tezi se girti hai!
- **Jaanch:** Open loop mein I = 0 ⟹ braking force F = 0 ⟹ acceleration = full g ✅.

### Example 2: Why Transformer Cores are Laminated
Explain why the core of a transformer is made of thin insulated laminations of soft iron instead of a single solid iron block.

**Hal (Solution):**
- Transformer mein AC lagataar magnetic flux badalta hai. Agar core solid block ho to bade-bade eddy currents bankar bahut zyada **heat (I²R)** waste karenge.
- Patli **insulated laminations** eddy current ke raaste ko tod deti hain aur resistance badha deti hain, jisse **eddy current loss bahut kam** ho jaata hai!
- **Jaanch:** Thin sheets + insulating varnish ⟹ higher resistance to eddy paths ⟹ minimal heat loss ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **Sochna ki eddy currents sirf iron/steel jaise magnetic metals mein bante hain:**
   - *Galat:* Copper non-magnetic hai, isliye copper mein eddy currents nahi ban sakte.
   - *Sahi:* Eddy currents **kisi bhi good conductor** (Copper, Aluminium, Silver, Iron) mein bante hain — chahe woh magnetic ho ya non-magnetic!
2. **Slots kaatne se eddy currents 100% zero maan lena:**
   - *Galat:* Slots kaatne par eddy currents bilkul khatam ho jaate hain.
   - *Sahi:* Slots bade loops ko tod kar unhe **bahut kam (minimise)** kar dete hain, poori tarah zero nahi (chhote-chhote kamzor loops ab bhi rehte hain).
3. **Eddy current loss aur Hysteresis loss ko ek hi samajhna:**
   - *Galat:* Core laminate karne se hysteresis loss kam hota hai.
   - *Sahi:* **Lamination** se **Eddy current loss** kam hota hai; **Soft iron** (narrow hysteresis loop) chunne se **Hysteresis loss** kam hota hai!

## Practice Questions

1. What are eddy currents? How are they produced?
2. Why does a solid copper plate oscillating between the poles of a strong magnet come to rest quickly, whereas a slotted copper plate keeps swinging much longer?
3. How are eddy currents minimized in the core of a transformer?
4. Name any four practical applications of eddy currents.
5. Explain how electromagnetic damping works in a moving coil galvanometer.
6. Why is magnetic braking in high-speed trains smoother and more durable than conventional mechanical friction brakes?
7. What happens to the temperature of a solid metal block placed inside a coil carrying high-frequency alternating current? Name the industrial device that uses this effect.
8. Distinguish between eddy current loss and hysteresis loss in a transformer core, and state how each is minimized.
9. State whether True or False: Eddy currents cannot be produced in a non-magnetic conductor like aluminium.
10. A magnet is dropped down two identical vertical pipes of the same length — one made of plastic and the other made of thick copper. Which pipe does the magnet take longer to fall through, and why?

### Answers

1. **Eddy currents** kisi bulk conductor ke andar badalte magnetic flux ke karan banne wale gol-gol (whirlpool-like) induced currents hain. Yeh Faraday's law of EMI se paida hote hain jab koi solid metal badalte magnetic field mein rakha ya hilaya jaaye.

2. Solid copper plate mein bade-bade eddy currents bante hain jo Lenz's law ke anusaar uski gati ka tez virodh (damping) karte hain, isliye woh jaldi ruk jaati hai. Slotted plate mein cuts eddy loops ko tod dete hain ⟹ resistance badhta hai ⟹ eddy currents bahut kamzor ho jaate hain aur plate der tak jhoolti rehti hai!

3. Transformer core ko ek solid block ki jagah **patli insulated (varnish-coated) soft iron laminations** se banakar — jisse eddy currents ka area aur loop size bahut chhota ho jaata hai.

4. (i) **Magnetic braking in trains**, (ii) **Electromagnetic damping** (galvanometers), (iii) **Induction furnace** (melting metals), (iv) **Analog electric power meters**.

5. Galvanometer ki coil ek non-magnetic metallic (aluminium) frame par lipti hoti hai. Jab coil deflect hokar hilne lagti hai, frame mein eddy currents bante hain jo extra oscillation ko turant rok dete hain aur pointer steady reading par ruk jaata hai!

6. Magnetic braking mein brake pads aur wheel/rail ke beech koi **physical contact (friction)** nahi hota — isliye koi ghisaav (wear & tear) ya jhatka (jerk) nahi hota!

7. High-frequency AC se metal block mein bahut tez eddy currents bante hain, jisse uska temperature tezi se badh kar woh **pighal (melt)** sakta hai. Is device ko **Induction Furnace** kehte hain.

8. **Eddy current loss:** Bulk core mein swirling currents se heat loss — **laminated core** se kam hota hai. **Hysteresis loss:** Baar-baar magnetisation/demagnetisation se heat loss — **soft iron** (narrow hysteresis loop) chunne se kam hota hai.

9. **False ❌.** Aluminium non-magnetic zaroor hai, par ek achha **conductor** hai — isliye usmein aasaani se eddy currents bante hain!

10. **Copper pipe** mein magnet ko neeche girne mein **zyada samay** lagega! Girte magnet ke badalte flux se copper pipe ki deewaron mein eddy currents bante hain jo Lenz's law se magnet ko upar ki taraf oppose (brake) karte hain. Plastic insulator hai, isliye usmein koi eddy current nahi banta aur magnet free-fall mein tezi se gir jaata hai!
