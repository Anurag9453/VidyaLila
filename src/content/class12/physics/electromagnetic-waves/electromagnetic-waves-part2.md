## Naye Shabd (New Words)

- **Electromagnetic Wave (EM Wave):** Ek **transverse** wave jismein electric field **E→** aur magnetic field **B→** ek-doosre ke aur wave chalne ki direction ke **perpendicular (90°)** oscillate karte hain, aur bina kisi material medium ke vacuum mein chal sakti hai.
- **Wave Number (k):** **k = 2π / λ** (unit: rad/m).
- **Radiation Pressure:** EM wave dwara kisi surface par takrane par lagaya gaya halka sa pressure (kyunki EM waves **energy** ke saath-saath **momentum p = U / c** bhi le kar chalti hain!).

---

## 1. Source of EM Waves (EM Wave Kaun Paida Karta Hai?)

- **Ruka hua charge (v = 0):** Sirf static Electric Field banata hai (koi wave nahi!).
- **Constant velocity se chalta charge:** Steady Electric + steady Magnetic field banata hai (koi wave nahi!).
- **Accelerating / Oscillating Charge:** Jab koi charge baar-baar aage-peeche oscillate (accelerate) karta hai (jaise antenna ya LC circuit mein), to uske badalte E aur B fields space mein **Electromagnetic Waves** ke roop mein radiate ho jaate hain — aur wave ki frequency oscillating charge ki frequency ke **barabar** hoti hai!

---

## 2. Plane EM Wave ki Equations aur 6 Golden Properties

Maan lo ek plane EM wave **+x direction** mein chal rahi hai, uska Electric Field **y-axis** ke along aur Magnetic Field **z-axis** ke along oscillate kar raha hai:

**E_y = E₀ sin(k x − ω t)**   aur   **B_z = B₀ sin(k x − ω t)**

jahan **k = 2π / λ** aur **ω = 2π f**!

### 6 Must-Know Properties of EM Waves:
1. **Strictly Transverse:** **E→ ⊥ B→ ⊥ Wave propagation direction**! Direction of propagation hamesha **E→ × B→** ki direction mein hoti hai!
2. **Same Phase:** E aur B dono ek saath maximum aur ek saath zero hote hain (in phase!).
3. **Speed in Vacuum (Maxwell's Miracle Formula):**
   **c = 1 / √(μ₀ ε₀) = ω / k = f λ ≈ 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s**
   *(Kisi dielectric medium (permittivity ε, permeability μ) mein speed: **v = 1 / √(μ ε) = c / n**, jahan n = √(μ_r ε_r) refractive index hai!)*
4. **Ratio of Amplitudes (E₀ / B₀ = c):**
   Har pal **E / B = E₀ / B₀ = c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s**! (Iska matlab SI units mein B₀ ki numeric value E₀ se bahut chhoti hoti hai!)
5. **Equal Energy Sharing:** Electric field ki average energy density (**u_E = ¼ ε₀ E₀²**) aur Magnetic field ki average energy density (**u_B = B₀² / (4 μ₀)**) **bilkul barabar** hoti hain (**u_E = u_B**)! Total average energy density: **u_avg = ½ ε₀ E₀² = B₀² / (2 μ₀)**.
6. **Carry Momentum & Exert Radiation Pressure:**
   - Agar koi surface EM wave ki total energy **U** ko **poori tarah absorb** kar le, to transferred momentum: **p = U / c**.
   - Agar **poori tarah reflect** kar de, to transferred momentum double: **p = 2 U / c**!

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Finding B₀ from E₀
In a plane electromagnetic wave travelling in vacuum along the +x direction, the maximum electric field is **E₀ = 120 N/C** along the +y direction. Find the magnitude and direction of the maximum magnetic field **B₀**.

**Hal (Solution):**
- Magnitude: **B₀ = E₀ / c** = 120 / (3.0 × 10⁸) = **4.0 × 10⁻⁷ T**.
- Direction: Propagation (+x) = E (+y) × B ⟹ **î = ĵ × k̂** ⟹ **B₀ points along the +z direction**!
- **Jaanch:** B₀ × c = (4.0 × 10⁻⁷)(3.0 × 10⁸) = 120 N/C ✅.

### Example 2: Reading Wave Parameters from Equation
The electric field of a plane EM wave in vacuum is given by:
**E_y = 60 sin(500 x − 1.5 × 10¹¹ t) N/C** (where x is in metres and t in seconds).
Find (a) wave number k and wavelength λ, (b) angular frequency ω and speed c, and (c) write the equation for **B_z**.

**Hal (Solution):**
- Compare with E_y = E₀ sin(k x − ω t):
  - (a) **k = 500 rad/m** ⟹ **λ = 2π / k = 6.28 / 500 = 1.256 × 10⁻² m = 1.256 cm**.
  - (b) **ω = 1.5 × 10¹¹ rad/s** ⟹ **c = ω / k = (1.5 × 10¹¹) / 500 = 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s**.
  - (c) **B₀ = E₀ / c = 60 / (3.0 × 10⁸) = 2.0 × 10⁻⁷ T** (along +z, same phase!):
    **B_z = 2.0 × 10⁻⁷ sin(500 x − 1.5 × 10¹¹ t) T**.
- **Jaanch:** 1.5 × 10¹¹ / 500 = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ✅; 60 / (3 × 10⁸) = 2 × 10⁻⁷ T ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **B₀ / E₀ = c likh kar B₀ ko bahut bada nikaal dena:**
   - *Galat:* B₀ = E₀ × c = 120 × 3 × 10⁸ T likhna!
   - *Sahi:* **E₀ / B₀ = c** ⟹ **B₀ = E₀ / c** (bahut chhota number in Tesla!).
2. **EM waves ko longitudinal bol dena:**
   - *Galat:* Sound ki tarah EM waves bhi longitudinal hoti hain.
   - *Sahi:* EM waves hamesha **Transverse** hoti hain (E→ ⊥ B→ ⊥ propagation direction)!
3. **Constant speed se chalte charge ko EM wave ka source bol dena:**
   - *Galat:* DC wire mein chalte electrons EM waves radiate karte hain.
   - *Sahi:* **SIRF Accelerating / Oscillating charges** EM waves paida karte hain!

## Quick Recap

| Property | Formula / Fact | Key Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Source of EM Waves | **Accelerating / Oscillating charges** | Wave frequency = oscillation frequency |
| Speed in Vacuum | c = 1/√(μ₀ε₀) = ω/k = fλ = 3 × 10⁸ m/s | In medium: v = c / n |
| Field Relation & Direction | E₀ / B₀ = c; direction = **E→ × B→** | E and B are in phase and mutually perpendicular |
| Energy & Momentum | u_E = u_B; p = U/c (absorbed), 2U/c (reflected) | EM waves exert radiation pressure |

## Practice Questions

1. What is the fundamental source of electromagnetic waves? Can a charge moving with uniform velocity radiate EM waves?
2. Write the formula for the speed of electromagnetic waves in vacuum in terms of μ₀ and ε₀.
3. In a plane EM wave, the magnetic field amplitude is B₀ = 5.0 × 10⁻⁸ T. Calculate the electric field amplitude E₀.
4. A plane EM wave propagates along the **+z direction**. If its electric field oscillates along the **+x direction**, along which axis does its magnetic field oscillate?
5. The magnetic field in a plane EM wave is given by B_y = 3.0 × 10⁻⁷ sin(1000 x + 3.0 × 10¹¹ t) T. Find (a) the direction of wave propagation, (b) the wavelength λ (take π = 3.14), and (c) the electric field amplitude E₀.
6. What is the ratio of average electric energy density (u_E) to average magnetic energy density (u_B) in a plane EM wave?
7. A laser pulse delivers 6.0 J of electromagnetic energy onto a perfectly **absorbing** black target. Calculate the momentum transferred to the target.
8. What would be the momentum transferred in Question 7 if the target were a perfectly **reflecting** mirror?
9. State whether True or False: Electromagnetic waves are deflected by strong electric and magnetic fields.
10. An EM wave of frequency 6.0 × 10¹⁴ Hz enters glass of refractive index n = 1.5 from vacuum. Find its (a) speed in glass, and (b) wavelength in glass (frequency does not change!).

### Answers

1. **Accelerating (or oscillating) electric charges** EM waves ka source hain. **Nahi**, uniform velocity se chalta charge EM waves radiate nahi karta!

2. **c = 1 / √(μ₀ ε₀)** ≈ 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s.

3. E₀ = c B₀ = (3.0 × 10⁸) × (5.0 × 10⁻⁸) = **15 N/C**.
   Jaanch: 3 × 5 = 15; 10⁸ × 10⁻⁸ = 1 ✅.

4. Propagation (+z = k̂) = E (+x = î) × B ⟹ **î × ĵ = k̂** ⟹ Magnetic field oscillates along the **+y direction**!

5. (a) Phase mein **(kx + ωt)** plus sign hai ⟹ wave **−x direction** mein chal rahi hai!
   (b) k = 1000 m⁻¹ ⟹ **λ = 2π / k = 6.28 / 1000 = 6.28 × 10⁻³ m = 6.28 mm**.
   (c) **E₀ = c B₀ = (3.0 × 10⁸) × (3.0 × 10⁻⁷) = 90 N/C**.
   Jaanch: 3 × 3 × 10 = 90 N/C ✅.

6. **1 : 1** (u_E = u_B — electric aur magnetic fields mein energy bilkul barabar bant'ti hai!).

7. Complete absorption: **p = U / c = 6.0 / (3.0 × 10⁸) = 2.0 × 10⁻⁸ kg m/s** (or N s).
   Jaanch: 6 / 3 = 2 ⟹ 2.0 × 10⁻⁸ kg m/s ✅.

8. Complete reflection: **p = 2 U / c = 2 × (2.0 × 10⁻⁸) = 4.0 × 10⁻⁸ kg m/s**.

9. **False ❌.** EM waves neutral (uncharged) photons/fields hain — isliye woh electric ya magnetic fields se **kabhi deflect nahi hoti**!

10. (a) **v = c / n = (3.0 × 10⁸) / 1.5 = 2.0 × 10⁸ m/s**.
    (b) **λ_glass = v / f = (2.0 × 10⁸) / (6.0 × 10¹⁴) = (1/3) × 10⁻⁶ m ≈ 333 nm**.
    Jaanch: 3.0 / 1.5 = 2.0; 2/6 = 1/3 ≈ 0.333 μm = 333 nm ✅.
