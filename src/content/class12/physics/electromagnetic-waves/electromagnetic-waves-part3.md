## Naye Shabd (New Words)

- **Electromagnetic Spectrum:** Sabhi electromagnetic waves ka unki **wavelength (λ)** ya **frequency (f)** ke badhte/ghat'te order mein poora parivaar.
- **Greenhouse Effect (Infrared):** Earth se nikalti infrared (heat) waves ko atmosphere ki gases (CO₂, water vapour) dwara trap kar lena, jisse Prithvi garam rehti hai.
- **Ozone Layer (UV Shield):** Stratosphere mein O₃ ki parat jo Sooraj se aane wali khatarnak ultraviolet rays ko absorb kar leti hai.

## 1. The Complete EM Spectrum (Yaad Rakhne ka Trick: R M I V U X G!)

**Mnemonic (Increasing Frequency / Decreasing Wavelength):**
**R**ich **M**en **I**n **V**egas **U**se **X**-ray **G**oggles!
*(Radio → Microwave → Infrared → Visible → Ultraviolet → X-rays → Gamma rays)*

- Left se Right jaane par: **Wavelength (λ) GHATTI hai**, aur **Frequency (f) & Energy BADHTI hai**!
- Sabhi ke liye vacuum mein: **c = f λ = 3 × 10⁸ m/s**!

| EM Band | Typical Wavelength (λ) | Production / Source | Key Uses & Facts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Radio Waves** | > 0.1 m (km to cm) | Oscillating charges in LC circuits / antennas | AM/FM radio, TV broadcasting, cellular communication |
| **2. Microwaves** | 0.1 m to 1 mm | Special vacuum tubes (Klystron, Magnetron, Gunn diode) | **RADAR** (aircraft navigation), satellite links, microwave ovens (water molecules resonate!) |
| **3. Infrared (IR)** | 1 mm to 700 nm | Vibrating atoms & hot bodies ("Heat waves"!) | TV remote controls, night-vision goggles, physiotherapy, Greenhouse effect |
| **4. Visible Light** | **700 nm (Red) to 400 nm (Violet)** | Electron transitions in atoms | Human vision, photography, optical fibres (VIBGYOR) |
| **5. Ultraviolet (UV)** | 400 nm to 1 nm | Inner-shell electron transitions, Sun, UV lamps | Water purifiers (kills germs), LASIK eye surgery, welding arcs (blocked by Ozone layer!) |
| **6. X-Rays** | 1 nm to 10⁻³ nm | Fast electrons hitting heavy metal target (X-ray tube) | Medical imaging (bone fractures), airport baggage scanners, crystal structure study |
| **7. Gamma (γ) Rays** | < 10⁻³ nm | **Radioactive nuclear decay** | Cancer radiotherapy (destroying tumour cells), sterilising surgical tools |

*(Safety Note: UV, X-rays aur Gamma rays high-energy **ionising radiations** hain — inka upyog sirf trained professionals aur medical supervision mein hi hota hai!)*

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Wavelength of an FM Radio Station
An FM radio station broadcasts at a frequency of **f = 100 MHz**. Calculate the wavelength **λ** of its radio waves in vacuum.

**Hal (Solution):**
- f = 100 MHz = 100 × 10⁶ Hz = **1.0 × 10⁸ Hz**, c = 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s.
- **λ = c / f** = (3.0 × 10⁸) / (1.0 × 10⁸) = **3.0 m**.
- **Jaanch:** f λ = (10⁸)(3.0) = 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s ✅.

### Example 2: Frequency of Yellow-Orange Visible Light
Calculate the frequency of visible light having wavelength **λ = 600 nm** in vacuum.

**Hal (Solution):**
- λ = 600 nm = 600 × 10⁻⁹ m = **6.0 × 10⁻⁷ m**.
- **f = c / λ** = (3.0 × 10⁸) / (6.0 × 10⁻⁷) = **0.5 × 10¹⁵ = 5.0 × 10¹⁴ Hz**.
- **Jaanch:** (5.0 × 10¹⁴) × (6.0 × 10⁻⁷) = 30 × 10⁷ = 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **Wavelength aur Frequency ka order ulta kar dena:**
   - *Galat:* Gamma rays ki wavelength sabse badi hoti hai bolna!
   - *Sahi:* **Radio waves = Longest λ, Lowest f**; **Gamma rays = Shortest λ, Highest f**!
2. **Sochna ki Gamma rays vacuum mein Radio waves se tez chalti hain:**
   - *Galat:* Gamma rays ki energy zyada hai isliye unki speed c se zyada hogi.
   - *Sahi:* Vacuum mein **SAARI** EM waves exact **c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s** ki speed se chalti hain!
3. **Infrared aur Ultraviolet ke kaam mix kar dena:**
   - *Galat:* Greenhouse effect UV se hota hai ya water purifier mein IR use hota hai bolna.
   - *Sahi:* **Infrared = Heat waves (Greenhouse effect, TV remote)**; **Ultraviolet = Germ killing (water purifier) & blocked by Ozone layer**!

---

## Quick Recap

- **Order (λ decreasing, f increasing):** **Radio → Microwave → Infrared → Visible (700–400 nm) → UV → X-rays → Gamma**.
- **Universal Relation (vacuum):** **c = f λ = 3 × 10⁸ m/s** for ALL bands!
- **Key Uses:** Radio (broadcasting), Microwave (RADAR, ovens), IR (remotes, greenhouse), UV (water purification, ozone absorbs it), X-rays (medical bone imaging), Gamma (cancer therapy, nuclear origin).

## Practice Questions

1. Arrange the following EM waves in order of **increasing frequency**: X-rays, Radio waves, Ultraviolet, Infrared, Microwaves, Gamma rays, Visible light.
2. Which part of the electromagnetic spectrum is used in (a) RADAR systems for aircraft navigation, (b) TV remote controls, (c) killing germs in water purifiers, and (d) treating cancer tumours?
3. Why are infrared waves often called "heat waves"?
4. Which layer in the upper atmosphere protects life on Earth from harmful ultraviolet radiation?
5. Calculate the wavelength of an AM radio wave of frequency 1500 kHz.
6. An X-ray has a wavelength of 0.10 nm (1.0 × 10⁻¹⁰ m). Calculate its frequency in Hz.
7. What is the ratio of the speed of gamma rays to the speed of radio waves in vacuum?
8. How are microwaves and X-rays produced? (State one source for each.)
9. State whether True or False: Visible red light (~700 nm) has a higher frequency than visible violet light (~400 nm).
10. Identify the EM spectrum band corresponding to a wavelength of 3.0 cm and state one major application of it.

### Answers

1. Increasing frequency (R M I V U X G): **Radio waves < Microwaves < Infrared < Visible light < Ultraviolet < X-rays < Gamma rays**.

2. (a) **Microwaves**, (b) **Infrared**, (c) **Ultraviolet (UV)**, (d) **Gamma rays**.

3. Kyunki water, CO₂ aur NH₃ jaise molecules infrared waves ko aasaani se absorb kar lete hain, jisse unka thermal motion (temperature) badh jaata hai aur aas-paas garmi mehsoos hoti hai!

4. **Ozone Layer (O₃)** (stratosphere mein).

5. f = 1500 kHz = 1.5 × 10⁶ Hz:
   λ = c / f = (3.0 × 10⁸) / (1.5 × 10⁶) = **200 m**.
   Jaanch: 1.5 × 10⁶ × 200 = 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s ✅.

6. f = c / λ = (3.0 × 10⁸) / (1.0 × 10⁻¹⁰) = **3.0 × 10¹⁸ Hz**.
   Jaanch: 3 × 10¹⁸ × 10⁻¹⁰ = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ✅.

7. **1 : 1** — vacuum mein sabhi EM waves exact same speed **c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s** se chalti hain!

8. **Microwaves:** Special vacuum tubes (Klystron / Magnetron / Gunn diode). **X-rays:** High-speed electrons ko heavy metal target par takrane (X-ray tube) se.

9. **False ❌.** Red light ki wavelength badi (700 nm) hai ⟹ iski **frequency kam** hai! Violet light (400 nm) ki frequency zyada hoti hai!

10. λ = 3.0 cm = 0.03 m (1 mm se 10 cm range) ⟹ **Microwaves**! Application: **RADAR** (aircraft navigation / speed guns) ya satellite communication.
