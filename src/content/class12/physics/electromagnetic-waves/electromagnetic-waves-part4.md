## Mixed Solved Examples (Sab Kuch Mila Kar!)

### Example 1: Complete EM Wave Equation + Band Identification
The electric field of a plane EM wave in vacuum is:
**E_z = 90 sin(2.0π × 10⁶ y − 6.0π × 10¹⁴ t) N/C**.
Find (a) the direction of propagation, (b) wavelength λ and frequency f, (c) which EM band it belongs to, and (d) the equation for the magnetic field **B**.

**Hal (Solution):**
- (a) Argument **(k y − ω t)** mein minus sign aur y hai ⟹ wave **+y direction** mein chal rahi hai!
- (b) k = 2.0π × 10⁶ m⁻¹ ⟹ **λ = 2π / k = 1.0 × 10⁻⁶ m = 1000 nm** (= 1 μm).
  ω = 6.0π × 10¹⁴ rad/s ⟹ **f = ω / (2π) = 3.0 × 10¹⁴ Hz**.
- (c) λ = 1000 nm (> 700 nm, < 1 mm) ⟹ **Infrared (IR) band**!
- (d) B₀ = E₀ / c = 90 / (3.0 × 10⁸) = **3.0 × 10⁻⁷ T**.
  Direction check: E (+z = k̂) × B = propagation (+y = ĵ). Kyunki **k̂ × î = ĵ**, isliye **B points along +x**!
  Equation: **B_x = 3.0 × 10⁻⁷ sin(2.0π × 10⁶ y − 6.0π × 10¹⁴ t) T**.
- **Jaanch:** f λ = (3 × 10¹⁴)(10⁻⁶) = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ✅; k̂ × î = ĵ ✅.

### Example 2: Radiation Momentum & Force on a Solar Sail
A sunlight beam with average power **P = 600 W** falls perpendicularly on a perfectly **reflecting** space solar sail for **10 seconds**. Calculate (a) the total energy U hitting the sail, (b) the total momentum transferred, and (c) the average radiation force on the sail.

**Hal (Solution):**
- (a) U = P × t = 600 × 10 = **6000 J**.
- (b) Complete reflection ⟹ **Δp = 2 U / c = (2 × 6000) / (3.0 × 10⁸) = 4.0 × 10⁻⁵ kg m/s**.
- (c) Average force **F = Δp / t = (4.0 × 10⁻⁵) / 10 = 4.0 × 10⁻⁶ N** (direct: F = 2 P / c = 1200 / 3×10⁸ = 4.0 × 10⁻⁶ N!).
- **Jaanch:** 2 × 600 / (3 × 10⁸) = 4.0 × 10⁻⁶ N ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Synthesis Mistakes)

1. **Perfect reflection mein momentum transfer ka factor 2 bhool jaana:**
   - *Galat:* Mirror par bhi Δp = U / c likh dena!
   - *Sahi:* Absorption mein **Δp = U / c**; **Perfect reflection** mein wave wapas laut'ti hai ⟹ **Δp = 2 U / c**!
2. **E→ × B→ direction rule ulta laga dena:**
   - *Galat:* B→ × E→ se propagation direction nikaalna.
   - *Sahi:* Hamesha **E→ × B→** wave propagation ki direction deta hai (î × ĵ = k̂, ĵ × k̂ = î, k̂ × î = ĵ)!
3. **k = 2π / λ ki jagah k = 1 / λ rakh dena:**
   - *Galat:* sin(2π × 10⁶ y) mein λ = 1/(2π × 10⁶) likhna!
   - *Sahi:* **λ = 2π / k** — isliye 2π upar-neeche kat jaata hai!

## Practice Questions (Mixed Chapter Challenge)

1. A parallel plate capacitor of capacitance 4.0 μF is charged so that the voltage increases at 50 V/s. Find the displacement current inside the capacitor.
2. In a plane EM wave in vacuum, E_x = 150 sin(1000 z − 3.0 × 10¹¹ t) N/C. Find (a) the direction of propagation, (b) the amplitude and direction of B, and (c) the wavelength λ (take π = 3.14).
3. A radio wave has a frequency of 30 MHz. Calculate its wavelength in vacuum and identify its EM spectrum band.
4. Calculate the speed of an EM wave in a non-magnetic medium (μ_r = 1) whose dielectric constant (relative permittivity) is ε_r = 4.
5. An EM beam of power 300 W falls normally on a perfectly **absorbing** black surface for 20 seconds. Find (a) the total momentum delivered, and (b) the average radiation force.
6. What is the average total energy density of an EM wave in vacuum whose peak electric field is E₀ = 200 N/C? (Take ε₀ = 8.85 × 10⁻¹² F/m.)
7. Name the EM waves used for: (a) LASIK eye surgery, (b) night-vision cameras, (c) detecting bone fractures, and (d) cellular mobile phones.
8. A parallel plate capacitor has circular plates of radius 10 cm and carries a charging current of 0.15 A. What is the displacement current between the plates?
9. State whether True or False: In an electromagnetic wave travelling in vacuum, the electric field and magnetic field oscillate with a phase difference of 90°.
10. An EM wave has wavelength λ = 500 nm in vacuum. Find its frequency and identify the colour/band it belongs to.

### Answers

1. I_d = C (ΔV / Δt) = (4.0 × 10⁻⁶) × 50 = **2.0 × 10⁻⁴ A = 0.20 mA**.
   Jaanch: 4 × 50 = 200 ⟹ 200 × 10⁻⁶ = 2.0 × 10⁻⁴ A ✅.

2. (a) **(kz − ωt)** ⟹ **+z direction**!
   (b) B₀ = E₀ / c = 150 / (3.0 × 10⁸) = **5.0 × 10⁻⁷ T**. Direction: î × ĵ = k̂ ⟹ **+y direction**!
   (c) λ = 2π / k = 6.28 / 1000 = **6.28 × 10⁻³ m = 6.28 mm**.
   Jaanch: 150 / 3 = 50 ⟹ 5.0 × 10⁻⁷ T ✅.

3. λ = c / f = (3.0 × 10⁸) / (30 × 10⁶) = **10 m**. Band: **Radio waves**.
   Jaanch: 30 × 10⁶ × 10 = 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s ✅.

4. n = √(μ_r ε_r) = √(1 × 4) = 2.
   **v = c / n = (3.0 × 10⁸) / 2 = 1.5 × 10⁸ m/s**.
   Jaanch: 3 / 2 = 1.5 ✅.

5. (a) U = P t = 300 × 20 = 6000 J. Absorption ⟹ **p = U / c = 6000 / (3.0 × 10⁸) = 2.0 × 10⁻⁵ kg m/s**.
   (b) **F = p / t = (2.0 × 10⁻⁵) / 20 = 1.0 × 10⁻⁶ N** (= P/c).
   Jaanch: 300 / (3 × 10⁸) = 1.0 × 10⁻⁶ N ✅.

6. u_avg = ½ ε₀ E₀² = 0.5 × (8.85 × 10⁻¹²) × (200)² = 0.5 × 8.85 × 10⁻¹² × 4 × 10⁴
   = 2 × 8.85 × 10⁻⁸ = **1.77 × 10⁻⁷ J/m³**.
   Jaanch: 0.5 × 4 = 2; 2 × 8.85 = 17.7 ⟹ 1.77 × 10⁻⁷ J/m³ ✅.

7. (a) **Ultraviolet (UV)**, (b) **Infrared (IR)**, (c) **X-rays**, (d) **Radio waves / Microwaves**.

8. Plates ke beech displacement current wire ke conduction current ke bilkul barabar hota hai: **I_d = 0.15 A**.

9. **False ❌.** E aur B ek-doosre ke 90° par **space** mein hote hain, par **phase** mein bilkul **in phase (phase difference = 0°)** hote hain (ek saath max, ek saath zero)!

10. f = c / λ = (3.0 × 10⁸) / (500 × 10⁻⁹) = (3.0 × 10⁸) / (5.0 × 10⁻⁷) = **6.0 × 10¹⁴ Hz**.
    500 nm visible range (400–700 nm) mein hai ⟹ **Visible Light (Blue-Green)**.
    Jaanch: 6 × 5 = 30 ⟹ 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s ✅.

## Chapter Ka Saar: Electromagnetic Waves

| Topic | Mukhya Formula / Niyam | Yaad Rakhne Ki Khaas Baat |
|---|---|---|
| Displacement Current | I_d = ε₀ (ΔΦ_E / Δt) = C (ΔV / Δt) | Completes Ampere-Maxwell law: Σ B·Δl = μ₀(I_c + I_d) |
| Source & Nature of EM Waves | Accelerating/oscillating charges; **Transverse** | E ⊥ B ⊥ propagation (**E→ × B→**); E and B are in phase |
| Speed & Amplitude Ratio | c = 1/√(μ₀ε₀) = ω/k = fλ = 3 × 10⁸ m/s; E₀/B₀ = c | In medium: v = c/n = c/√(μ_r ε_r) |
| Energy & Momentum | u_E = u_B; u_avg = ½ε₀E₀²; p = U/c (abs), 2U/c (refl) | EM waves exert radiation pressure |
| EM Spectrum (R M I V U X G) | Radio → Micro → IR → Visible (700–400 nm) → UV → X → Gamma | λ decreases, f & energy increase; all move at c in vacuum |
