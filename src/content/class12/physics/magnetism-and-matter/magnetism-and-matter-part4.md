## Naye Shabd (New Words)

- **Diamagnetic Material:** Jo external magnetic field mein halka sa **repel** ho aur strong field se weak field ki taraf jaaye (χ chhota aur **negative**).
- **Paramagnetic Material:** Jo external magnetic field mein halka sa **attract** ho aur weak se strong field ki taraf jaaye (χ chhota aur **positive**).
- **Ferromagnetic Material:** Jo external magnetic field mein **bahut zor se attract** ho (χ bahut bada aur positive, ~1000+); ismein atomic dipoles ke chhote-chhote aligned groups (**Domains**) hote hain!
- **Curie's Law:** Paramagnetic material ki susceptibility absolute temperature T ke inversely proportional hoti hai: **χ = C / T**.
- **Curie Temperature (T_C):** Woh temperature jiske upar garm karne par koi ferromagnetic material apna domain alignment khokar **paramagnetic** ban jaata hai (Iron ke liye ~1043 K).
- **Retentivity:** External field H = 0 karne ke baad material mein bacha rehne wala magnetism (B).
- **Coercivity:** Us bache hue magnetism ko poori tarah zero karne ke liye lagana padne wala ulta magnetic intensity (−H).

## 1. Diamagnetic, Paramagnetic aur Ferromagnetic — Complete Comparison

| Property | Diamagnetic | Paramagnetic | Ferromagnetic |
|---|---|---|---|
| External field mein vyavahaar | Weakly **repelled** (strong → weak field) | Weakly **attracted** (weak → strong field) | **Strongly attracted** (weak → strong field) |
| Susceptibility (χ) | Small & **Negative** (−10⁻⁵ to −10⁻⁹) | Small & **Positive** (+10⁻⁵ to +10⁻³) | Very **Large Positive** (≫ 1, ~10³ to 10⁵) |
| Relative Permeability (μ_r) | **0 ≤ μ_r < 1** | **Slightly > 1** | **Very Large (≫ 1)** |
| Field lines ka vyavahaar | Lines ko bahar **dhakelta (expels)** hai | Lines thoda andar **jama** hoti hain | Lines bahut zyada andar **khinch** jaati hain |
| Temperature dependence | **Independent** of T | **Curie's Law:** χ ∝ 1/T | Above Curie temp **T_C**, becomes paramagnetic! |
| Examples | **Bismuth, Copper, Gold, Water, Silicon** | **Aluminium, Sodium, Oxygen (O₂), Platinum** | **Iron (Fe), Cobalt (Co), Nickel (Ni), Alnico** |

*(Special Diamagnet: **Superconductors** bahut kam temperature par **perfect diamagnets** hote hain — χ = −1, μ_r = 0! Yeh magnetic field lines ko apne andar se 100% bahar nikaal dete hain — isse **Meissner Effect** kehte hain!)*

## 2. Hysteresis Loop, Electromagnets aur Permanent Magnets

Jab kisi ferromagnetic material ko pehle magnetise kiya jaata hai (H badhao) aur phir demagnetise karke ulta magnetise kiya jaata hai (H ghatao), to **B aur H ka graph ek bandh loop** banata hai — isse **Hysteresis Loop** kehte hain (kyunki B, H se peeche reh jaata hai — "lagging behind")!

- **Hysteresis Loop ka Area:** Ek poori AC magnetisation cycle mein **per unit volume heat ke roop mein waste hone wali energy** ke barabar hota hai!

### Sahi Material Kaise Chunein?
1. **Permanent Magnets (jaise fridge magnet, loudspeaker, compass):**
   - Humein chahiye ki magnet banne ke baad magnetism pakka tika rahe aur aasaani se na mite!
   - Chahiye: **High Retentivity** + **High Coercivity** + High permeability.
   - Materials: **Steel, Alnico, Ticonal**.
2. **Electromagnets aur Transformer Cores (jaise Karuna ki scrapyard crane!):**
   - Humein chahiye ki switch on par turant strong magnet bane, switch off par turant demagnetise ho, aur AC mein heat loss kam ho!
   - Chahiye: **High Permeability** + **Low Coercivity** + **Narrow (patla) Hysteresis Loop** (kam energy loss!).
   - Material: **Soft Iron**!

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Curie's Law Calculation
A paramagnetic sample has magnetic susceptibility **χ₁ = 2.0 × 10⁻⁴** at temperature **T₁ = 300 K**. What will be its susceptibility at **T₂ = 150 K**?

**Hal (Solution):**
- Curie's law: **χ ∝ 1 / T** ⟹ χ₂ / χ₁ = T₁ / T₂.
- χ₂ = χ₁ × (T₁ / T₂) = (2.0 × 10⁻⁴) × (300 / 150) = (2.0 × 10⁻⁴) × 2 = **4.0 × 10⁻⁴**.
- **Jaanch:** Temperature aadha hua ⟹ thermal disturbance kam hua ⟹ susceptibility double (4.0 × 10⁻⁴) ✅.

### Example 2: Identifying Materials from χ
Classify the following three materials A, B, and C based on their magnetic susceptibility values:
- Material A: χ = −2.5 × 10⁻⁵
- Material B: χ = +3.0 × 10⁻⁴
- Material C: χ = +2500

**Hal (Solution):**
- **Material A** (chhota negative χ): **Diamagnetic**.
- **Material B** (chhota positive χ): **Paramagnetic**.
- **Material C** (bahut bada positive χ): **Ferromagnetic**.
- **Jaanch:** Negative = Dia; small positive = Para; huge positive = Ferro ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **Diamagnetic material ke liye χ > 0 ya μ_r > 1 bol dena:**
   - *Galat:* Sabhi materials ke liye χ positive hota hai.
   - *Sahi:* Diamagnetic materials magnetic field ka **virodh** karte hain ⟹ **χ < 0 (negative)** aur **μ_r = 1 + χ < 1**!
2. **Electromagnet ya Transformer Core ke liye High Coercivity chun lena:**
   - *Galat:* Electromagnet mein magnetism tikna chahiye isliye high coercivity chahiye.
   - *Sahi:* Electromagnet ko switch off par turant zero hona chahiye aur AC mein energy loss kam rakhna hai ⟹ **Low Coercivity** aur **narrow hysteresis loop (Soft Iron)**!
3. **Diamagnetic material par Curie's law laga dena:**
   - *Galat:* Temperature badhane par diamagnetic material ki χ ghatti hai.
   - *Sahi:* **Diamagnetism temperature par depend nahi karta**! Curie's law (χ ∝ 1/T) **paramagnetic** materials ke liye hota hai!

## Quick Recap

| Type / Application | Key Properties | Classic Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Diamagnetic | χ < 0, μ_r < 1, repelled, T-independent | Bi, Cu, Au, H₂O, Superconductors (χ = −1) |
| Paramagnetic | χ > 0 (small), μ_r > 1, attracted, χ ∝ 1/T | Al, Na, O₂, Pt |
| Ferromagnetic | χ ≫ 1, domains, becomes para above T_C | Fe, Co, Ni |
| Permanent Magnet | High retentivity + **High coercivity** | Steel, Alnico |
| Electromagnet / Transformer Core | High permeability + **Low coercivity** + narrow loop | **Soft Iron** |

## Practice Questions

1. Give two examples each of diamagnetic, paramagnetic, and ferromagnetic substances.
2. What is the value of magnetic susceptibility χ and relative permeability μ_r for a superconductor (perfect diamagnet)?
3. How does a diamagnetic rod align itself when freely suspended in a uniform magnetic field? What about a paramagnetic rod?
4. State Curie's law for paramagnetic materials.
5. A paramagnetic gas has susceptibility χ = 1.2 × 10⁻⁴ at 27 °C (300 K). Find its susceptibility at 327 °C (600 K).
6. What is Curie temperature (T_C)? What happens to a piece of iron when heated above its Curie temperature?
7. Define retentivity and coercivity of a ferromagnetic material.
8. Why is **soft iron** preferred over **steel** for making the core of an electromagnet or a transformer?
9. State whether True or False: The area enclosed by a hysteresis loop represents the energy lost as heat per unit volume in one complete cycle of magnetisation.
10. Which magnetic material property makes Steel or Alnico ideal for making permanent magnets?

### Answers

1. **Diamagnetic:** Bismuth, Copper (ya Water). **Paramagnetic:** Aluminium, Oxygen (ya Sodium). **Ferromagnetic:** Iron, Nickel (ya Cobalt).

2. Superconductor (perfect diamagnet) ke liye: **χ = −1** aur **μ_r = 1 + (−1) = 0** (Meissner effect!).

3. **Diamagnetic rod** magnetic field ke **perpendicular (90°)** align hoti hai; **Paramagnetic rod** magnetic field ke **parallel (along B)** align hoti hai.

4. **Curie's Law:** Kisi paramagnetic material ki magnetic susceptibility χ uske absolute (Kelvin) temperature T ke inversely proportional hoti hai: **χ = C / T**.

5. T₁ = 300 K, T₂ = 600 K (double ho gaya!):
   χ₂ = χ₁ × (T₁ / T₂) = (1.2 × 10⁻⁴) × (300 / 600) = **6.0 × 10⁻⁵**.
   Jaanch: Kelvin temperature double ⟹ χ aadhi = 0.6 × 10⁻⁴ = 6.0 × 10⁻⁵ ✅.

6. **Curie temperature (T_C)** woh khaas temperature hai jiske upar koi ferromagnetic material apni domain structure khokar **paramagnetic** ban jaata hai. Iron ko 1043 K se upar garam karne par woh ferromagnetic se paramagnetic ban jaata hai.

7. **Retentivity:** External magnetising field H ko zero karne par material mein bacha rehne wala residual magnetism B. **Coercivity:** Us residual magnetism ko zero karne ke liye lagani padne wali ulta magnetising intensity (−H).

8. Soft iron ki **permeability high**, **coercivity low**, aur **hysteresis loop narrow (patla)** hota hai — jisse yeh turant magnetise/demagnetise ho jaata hai aur AC cycle mein heat energy loss bahut kam hota hai!

9. **True ✅.** Hysteresis loop ka area ek complete cycle mein per unit volume heat loss ke barabar hota hai.

10. **High retentivity** (achha strong magnetism bacha rehta hai) aur **High coercivity** (bahari jhatke ya stray fields se aasaani se demagnetise nahi hota!).
