## E = m c² aur "Gayab Hua Mass" Kahan Gaya?

Science museum ki diwaar par bade aksharon mein **E = m c²** likha tha. Uske neeche ek weighing scale ka model dikhaya gaya tha: left pan par **2 protons + 2 neutrons** alag-alag rakhe the, aur right pan par unhi chaaron se bana ek **Helium (⁴₂He) nucleus**! **Sajid** hairaan reh gaya — Helium nucleus un chaaron nucleons ke total wazan se **thoda halka** tha!

Guide ne bataya: "Jab protons aur neutrons judkar nucleus banate hain, to unka thoda sa mass (**Mass Defect, Δm**) gayab hokar **E = Δm c²** ke roop mein zabardast **Binding Energy** bankar bahar nikal jaata hai!"

---

## Naye Shabd (New Words)

- **Mass-Energy Equivalence (E = m c²):** Einstein ka niyam ki mass (m) aur energy (E) ek hi cheez ke do roop hain! **1 u mass = 931.5 MeV energy**!
- **Mass Defect (Δm):** Kisi nucleus ke sabhi protons aur neutrons ke alag-alag masses ke sum aur actual bound nucleus ke mass (M) ka antar: **Δm = [ Z m_p + (A − Z) m_n ] − M**.
- **Binding Energy (E_b):** Nucleus ko tod kar uske saare nucleons ko ek-doosre se poori tarah alag karne ke liye zaroori energy: **E_b = Δm c² = Δm (in u) × 931.5 MeV**.
- **Binding Energy per Nucleon (E_bn = E_b / A):** Ek nucleon ko bahar nikaalne ki average energy — yahi **nuclear stability ka asli naap** hai!

---

## 1. Mass-Energy Equivalence (1 u = 931.5 MeV)

1 atomic mass unit (**1 u = 1.6605 × 10⁻²⁷ kg**) ko poori tarah energy mein badlein:
- E = m c² = (1.6605 × 10⁻²⁷) × (3.0 × 10⁸)² ≈ 1.4924 × 10⁻¹⁰ J.
- MeV mein badalne ke liye **1.602 × 10⁻¹³ J/MeV** se divide karo:
  **E ≈ 931.5 MeV**!
*(Isliye agar Mass Defect Δm atomic mass units **u** mein diya ho, to seedha **931.5** se multiply karo — answer seedha **MeV** mein!)*

---

## 2. Binding Energy per Nucleon Curve (Physics ka Sabse Zaroori Graph!)

Jab hum **E_bn = E_b / A** ko **Mass Number A** ke khilaaf plot karte hain, to ek super-important curve milta hai:

```
   E_bn (MeV)
   ^
 9 :           * 56Fe (8.75 MeV - PEAK!)
 8 :       * *   * * * * * *
 7 :     * 4He                * * * 238U (7.6 MeV)
 5 :    *
 2 :  * 2H
   +------------------------------------> Mass Number A
   0    20    56          150       238
```

### Graph ke 4 Mahatvapurna Nateeje:
1. **Peak at Iron (⁵⁶₂₆Fe):** **E_bn** Iron-56 par maximum (**~8.75 MeV/nucleon**) hota hai — yaani **⁵⁶Fe sabse zyada stable nuclei mein se ek hai**!
2. **Middle Region (30 < A < 170) lagbhag Flat (~8.0–8.75 MeV):** Yeh sabit karta hai ki **nuclear force short-range aur saturated** hota hai (har nucleon sirf apne paas wale padosiyon se hi bandhta hai!).
3. **Very Light Nuclei (A < 20) — FUSION!** Light nuclei (jaise ²H) ka E_bn kam hota hai. Jab do halke nuclei judkar bhaari nucleus (jaise ⁴He, 7.07 MeV/nucleon) banate hain, to E_bn **badhta** hai aur zabardast energy nikalti hai (**Nuclear Fusion**)!
4. **Very Heavy Nuclei (A > 170) — FISSION!** Heavy nuclei (jaise ²³⁵U, ~7.6 MeV/nucleon) jab toot kar do medium-size nuclei (~8.5 MeV/nucleon) banate hain, to E_bn **badhta** hai (~0.9 MeV per nucleon × 235 ≈ **200 MeV** energy nikalti hai — **Nuclear Fission**)!

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Complete Conversion of 1 Gram of Matter
Calculate the energy released in Joules if **1 gram (10⁻³ kg)** of matter is completely converted into energy.

**Hal (Solution):**
- E = m c² = (1.0 × 10⁻³) × (3.0 × 10⁸)² = 10⁻³ × 9.0 × 10¹⁶ = **9.0 × 10¹³ J** (= 25 million kWh — ek poore shehar ki din bhar ki bijli!).
- **Jaanch:** 3² = 9; 10⁻³ × 10¹⁶ = 10¹³ J ✅.

### Example 2: Binding Energy & E_bn of Oxygen-16 (¹⁶₈O)
The mass defect of an Oxygen-16 (**¹⁶₈O**) nucleus is **Δm = 0.1369 u**. Calculate (a) its total binding energy **E_b** in MeV, and (b) its binding energy per nucleon **E_bn**.

**Hal (Solution):**
- (a) **E_b = Δm × 931.5** = 0.1369 × 931.5 ≈ **127.5 MeV**.
- (b) A = 16 ⟹ **E_bn = E_b / 16** = 127.5 / 16 ≈ **7.97 MeV/nucleon**.
- **Jaanch:** 16 × 7.97 ≈ 127.5 MeV ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **Bade total Binding Energy (E_b) ko zyada stability maan lena:**
   - *Galat:* Uranium-238 ka total E_b (~1800 MeV) Iron-56 (~490 MeV) se bada hai, isliye Uranium zyada stable hai!
   - *Sahi:* Stability **Binding Energy PER NUCLEON (E_b / A)** se tay hoti hai! Iron-56 (**8.75 MeV**) Uranium-238 (**7.6 MeV**) se zyada stable hai!
2. **Mass defect Δm mein nucleus ka mass nucleons se zyada likh dena:**
   - *Galat:* Bound nucleus ka mass = protons + neutrons + Δm!
   - *Sahi:* Bound nucleus hamesha **HALKA** hota hai: **M_nucleus = Z m_p + (A − Z) m_n − Δm**!
3. **1 u = 931.5 eV likh dena (Mega bhool jaana!):**
   - *Galat:* 931.5 eV likhna!
   - *Sahi:* **1 u = 931.5 MeV** (Mega electron-volt = 10⁶ eV)!

## Quick Recap

| Concept | Formula / Fact | Key Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Mass-Energy | E = m c²; **1 u = 931.5 MeV** | 1 g matter = 9 × 10¹³ J |
| Mass Defect | Δm = [Z m_p + (A − Z) m_n] − M_nucleus | Bound nucleus is lighter than separate nucleons |
| Binding Energy & E_bn | E_b = Δm × 931.5 MeV; **E_bn = E_b / A** | E_bn measures stability; peaks at **⁵⁶Fe (~8.75 MeV)** |
| Energy Release Rule | Moving UP the E_bn curve releases energy! | Light nuclei → **Fusion**; Heavy nuclei → **Fission** |

## Practice Questions

1. Define mass defect and binding energy of a nucleus.
2. What is the energy equivalent of 1 atomic mass unit (1 u) in MeV?
3. Why is the binding energy per nucleon (E_b / A), rather than total binding energy (E_b), a true measure of nuclear stability?
4. Calculate the energy released in Joules if 2 mg (2 × 10⁻⁶ kg) of matter is completely converted into energy.
5. The mass defect of a Deuteron (²₁H) nucleus is 0.00239 u. Calculate its (a) binding energy in MeV, and (b) binding energy per nucleon.
6. A Nitrogen-14 (¹⁴₇N) nucleus has a total binding energy of 104.7 MeV. Find its binding energy per nucleon and its mass defect in atomic mass units (u).
7. Using the binding energy per nucleon curve, explain why energy is released during **both** nuclear fission of heavy nuclei and nuclear fusion of light nuclei.
8. Which nucleus lies near the peak of the binding energy per nucleon curve, and what is its approximate E_bn value?
9. State whether True or False: The mass of a stable nucleus is always slightly greater than the sum of the masses of its individual constituent protons and neutrons.
10. Calculate the binding energy (in MeV) of a Helium-4 (⁴₂He) nucleus given: m_p = 1.00728 u, m_n = 1.00866 u, and actual mass of ⁴₂He nucleus M = 4.00150 u.

### Answers

1. **Mass defect (Δm):** Free nucleons ke total mass aur bound nucleus ke actual mass ka antar. **Binding energy (E_b = Δm c²):** Nucleus ko tod kar saare nucleons ko alag karne ke liye zaroori energy.

2. **1 u ≈ 931.5 MeV**.

3. Bada nucleus (jaise ²³⁸U) mein zyada nucleons hone ke karan total E_b to bada hota hai, par har ek nucleon kitni mazbooti se bandha hai yeh sirf **average energy per nucleon (E_b / A)** batata hai!

4. E = m c² = (2 × 10⁻⁶) × (9 × 10¹⁶) = **1.8 × 10¹¹ J**.
   Jaanch: 2 × 9 = 18; 10⁻⁶ × 10¹⁶ = 10¹⁰ ⟹ 1.8 × 10¹¹ J ✅.

5. (a) E_b = 0.00239 × 931.5 ≈ **2.23 MeV**.
   (b) A = 2 ⟹ E_bn = 2.23 / 2 ≈ **1.11 MeV/nucleon**.
   Jaanch: 0.00239 × 931.5 = 2.226 MeV ✅.

6. E_bn = 104.7 / 14 ≈ **7.48 MeV/nucleon**.
   Δm = 104.7 / 931.5 ≈ **0.1124 u**.
   Jaanch: 14 × 7.48 ≈ 104.7; 0.1124 × 931.5 ≈ 104.7 ✅.

7. Fission mein bhaari nucleus (E_bn ~7.6 MeV) toot kar zyada stable medium nuclei (~8.5 MeV) banata hai; Fusion mein halke nuclei (E_bn ~1–2 MeV) judkar zyada stable nucleus (~7 MeV) banate hain. Dono mein **E_bn badhta** hai (product zyada tightly bound + halka hota hai), aur mass difference energy bankar nikalta hai!

8. **Iron-56 (⁵⁶₂₆Fe)**, jiska E_bn lagbhag **8.75 MeV/nucleon** hota hai.

9. **False ❌.** Stable nucleus ka mass hamesha uske alag-alag protons aur neutrons ke mass ke sum se **thoda KAM (lighter)** hota hai (yahi mass defect Δm hai!).

10. 2 m_p + 2 m_n = 2(1.00728) + 2(1.00866) = 2.01456 + 2.01732 = 4.03188 u.
    Δm = 4.03188 − 4.00150 = **0.03038 u**.
    E_b = 0.03038 × 931.5 ≈ **28.3 MeV** (E_bn ≈ 7.07 MeV/nucleon!).
    Jaanch: 0.03038 × 931.5 = 28.299 ≈ 28.3 MeV ✅.
