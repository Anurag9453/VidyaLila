## Naye Shabd (New Words)

- **Q-Value of a Nuclear Reaction:** Reaction mein release (ya absorb) hui net energy: **Q = (m_reactants − m_products) × 931.5 MeV**. (Q > 0: Exothermic / energy released; Q < 0: Endothermic / energy absorbed!)
- **Nuclear Fission:** Ek bhaari nucleus (jaise ²³⁵₉₂U) par slow (thermal) neutron takrane par uska do medium-size nuclei mein tootna + 2–3 neutrons + **~200 MeV** energy!
- **Nuclear Fusion:** Do ya zyada bahut halke nuclei (jaise Hydrogen isotopes) ka behad high temperature (~10⁷ K) par judkar ek bhaari nucleus (jaise Helium) banana aur zabardast energy chhodna!
- **Moderator:** Nuclear reactor mein fast neutrons ko **dheema (thermal neutrons)** karne wala material (e.g., Heavy water D₂O, Graphite, Ordinary water).
- **Control Rods:** Reactor mein extra neutrons ko **absorb** karke chain reaction ko control (ya shut down) karne wali rods (e.g., **Cadmium, Boron**).

## 1. Nuclear Fission aur Nuclear Reactor (Descriptive Only!)

Jab ek slow (thermal) neutron **²³⁵₉₂U** se takrata hai:

**¹₀n + ²³⁵₉₂U → ²³⁶₉₂U* → ¹⁴¹₅₆Ba + ⁹²₃₆Kr + 3 ¹₀n + ~200 MeV**

- Har fission mein lagbhag **200 MeV (= 3.2 × 10⁻¹¹ J)** energy aur 2–3 naye neutrons nikalte hain — jo aage aur Uranium nuclei ko tod kar **Chain Reaction** chala sakte hain!
- **Multiplication Factor (k):** Agli generation ke neutrons / pichhli generation ke neutrons:
  - **k = 1 (Critical):** Steady controlled power generation (Nuclear Power Plant!).
  - **k > 1 (Supercritical):** Reaction tezi se badhti hai (uncontrolled!).
  - **k < 1 (Subcritical):** Reaction dheemi hokar ruk jaati hai.

### Nuclear Reactor ke 5 Mukhya Hisse:
1. **Nuclear Fuel:** Enriched Uranium (²³⁵U) ya Plutonium (²³⁹Pu) rods.
2. **Moderator:** Fast neutrons ko slow (thermal) banata hai taaki ²³⁵U unhe aasaani se pakad sake (**Heavy water D₂O, Graphite**).
3. **Control Rods:** Extra neutrons ko absorb karke **k = 1** banaye rakhti hain (**Cadmium, Boron**).
4. **Coolant:** Reactor core ki heat bahar le jaakar paani se steam banata hai (jo turbine ghuma kar bijli banati hai!) — e.g., pressurised water, liquid sodium.
5. **Protective Shielding:** Moti concrete aur lead ki deewarein jo harmful radiations ko bahar aane se rokti hain.

## 2. Nuclear Fusion — Sooraj aur Taaron ki Shakti!

Sooraj ke core mein lagbhag **1.5 × 10⁷ K** temperature par chaar protons (Hydrogen nuclei) proton-proton cycle se judkar ek Helium nucleus banate hain:

**4 ¹₁H → ⁴₂He + 2 e⁺ + 2 ν + 26.7 MeV**

- **Itna High Temperature (~10⁷ K) Kyun Chahiye?**
  Dono protons positive (+e) hain — paas aane par unke beech zabardast **Coulomb repulsion (barrier)** lagta hai! Is repulsion ko paar karke 1–2 fm (nuclear force range!) tak pahunchne ke liye nuclei ko behad high thermal kinetic energy chahiye — isliye ise **Thermonuclear Fusion** kehte hain!
- **Fission vs Fusion (Per Nucleon Energy):**
  Ek fission reaction mein total energy zyada (~200 MeV) hai, par 235 nucleons hain ⟹ **~0.85 MeV/nucleon**.
  Fusion (4 protons → ⁴He) mein 26.7 MeV / 4 ⟹ **~6.7 MeV/nucleon**! Yaani **per kg fuel, Fusion Fission se lagbhag 7–8 guna zyada energy** deta hai aur radioactive waste bhi nahi chhodta!

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Number of Fissions per Second in a Power Plant
A nuclear power reactor produces **3.2 MW** of power. If each fission of **²³⁵U** releases **200 MeV** of energy, calculate the number of fissions occurring per second!

**Hal (Solution):**
- Ek fission ki energy: **E₁ = 200 MeV = 200 × 1.6 × 10⁻¹³ J = 3.2 × 10⁻¹¹ J**.
- Total power P = 3.2 MW = **3.2 × 10⁶ J/s**.
- Fissions per second: **N / t = P / E₁ = (3.2 × 10⁶) / (3.2 × 10⁻¹¹) = 1.0 × 10¹⁷ fissions/s**!
- **Jaanch:** 200 × 1.6 × 10⁻¹³ = 3.2 × 10⁻¹¹ J; 3.2×10⁶ / 3.2×10⁻¹¹ = 10¹⁷ ✅.

### Example 2: Q-Value of a Deuterium-Tritium Fusion Reaction
In the fusion reaction **²₁H + ³₁H → ⁴₂He + ¹₀n**, the masses are: m(²H) = 2.0141 u, m(³H) = 3.0160 u, m(⁴He) = 4.0026 u, m(n) = 1.0086 u. Calculate the Q-value (energy released) in MeV.

**Hal (Solution):**
- Reactants mass: 2.0141 + 3.0160 = **5.0301 u**.
- Products mass: 4.0026 + 1.0086 = **5.0112 u**.
- Mass defect: **Δm = 5.0301 − 5.0112 = 0.0189 u**.
- **Q = 0.0189 × 931.5 ≈ 17.6 MeV** (Exothermic — huge energy released!).
- **Jaanch:** 0.0189 × 931.5 = 17.605 ≈ 17.6 MeV ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **Moderator aur Control Rods ke kaam aapas mein badal dena:**
   - *Galat:* Cadmium rods neutrons ko slow karti hain aur heavy water neutrons ko absorb karta hai!
   - *Sahi:* **Moderator (Heavy water / Graphite) = SLOWS DOWN neutrons**; **Control Rods (Cadmium / Boron) = ABSORBS excess neutrons**!
2. **Sochna ki Fission per kg fuel zyada energy deta hai kyunki 200 MeV > 26.7 MeV:**
   - *Galat:* 200 MeV > 26.7 MeV isliye fission zyada powerful hai!
   - *Sahi:* Per reaction fission bada hai, par **per nucleon / per kg fuel, FUSION (~6.7 MeV/nucleon) Fission (~0.85 MeV/nucleon) se ~8 guna zyada energy** deta hai!
3. **Q-value mein Products − Reactants likh dena:**
   - *Galat:* Q = (m_products − m_reactants) c²!
   - *Sahi:* Exothermic energy release ke liye reactants bhaari aur products halke hote hain ⟹ **Q = (m_reactants − m_products) × 931.5 MeV**!

## Quick Recap

| Process / Device | Key Reaction / Role | Energy / Materials |
|---|---|---|
| Nuclear Fission | ¹n + ²³⁵U → Ba + Kr + 3n + ~200 MeV | ~0.85 MeV/nucleon; k = 1 for steady reactor |
| Reactor Controls | Moderator slows neutrons; Control rods absorb neutrons | Moderator: D₂O / Graphite; Control: Cd / B |
| Nuclear Fusion | 4 ¹H → ⁴He + 2e⁺ + 2ν + 26.7 MeV | ~6.7 MeV/nucleon; needs ~10⁷ K (Coulomb barrier!) |
| Q-Value | Q = (m_reactants − m_products) × 931.5 MeV | Q > 0 exothermic; Q < 0 endothermic |

## Practice Questions

1. Distinguish between nuclear fission and nuclear fusion in three clear points.
2. What is the function of (a) a moderator, and (b) control rods in a nuclear reactor? Give one example material for each.
3. Why is an extremely high temperature (~10⁷ K) required to initiate nuclear fusion?
4. What should be the value of the neutron multiplication factor **k** for steady, controlled power generation in a nuclear reactor?
5. If each fission of ²³⁵U releases 200 MeV of energy, how many fissions per second are needed to produce a power output of 64 MW?
6. Calculate the energy released in a nuclear reaction where the total mass of reactants exceeds the total mass of products by 0.020 u.
7. Why is energy released per unit mass much higher in nuclear fusion than in nuclear fission?
8. Write the overall net nuclear fusion equation that powers the Sun.
9. State whether True or False: In an exothermic nuclear reaction (Q > 0), the total mass of the products is slightly **less** than the total mass of the reactants.
10. Find the missing mass number A and atomic number Z in the fission reaction: ¹₀n + ²³⁵₉₂U → ¹⁴⁴₅₆Ba + ᴬ_Z Kr + 3 ¹₀n.

### Answers

1. (i) **Fission:** Bhaari nucleus ka tootna; **Fusion:** Halke nuclei ka judna.
   (ii) **Fission** room temperature par thermal neutrons se hota hai; **Fusion** ke liye ~10⁷ K temperature chahiye.
   (iii) **Fusion** mein per nucleon (~6.7 MeV) energy Fission (~0.85 MeV) se kahin zyada hoti hai aur kam radioactive waste hota hai.

2. (a) **Moderator:** Fast neutrons ko slow (thermal) karta hai — e.g., **Heavy water (D₂O) ya Graphite**.
   (b) **Control rods:** Extra neutrons ko absorb karke chain reaction control karti hain — e.g., **Cadmium ya Boron**.

3. Do positively charged light nuclei ke beech strong **electrostatic (Coulomb) repulsion** hota hai. Is barrier ko paar karke 1–2 fm (nuclear force range) tak laane ke liye unhein bahut high thermal kinetic energy (~10⁷ K) chahiye!

4. Steady controlled power generation ke liye **k = 1** (critical state) hona chahiye!

5. E₁ = 200 MeV = 3.2 × 10⁻¹¹ J; P = 64 × 10⁶ J/s:
   N / t = (64 × 10⁶) / (3.2 × 10⁻¹¹) = **2.0 × 10¹⁸ fissions/s**.
   Jaanch: 64 / 3.2 = 20; 20 × 10¹⁷ = 2.0 × 10¹⁸ ✅.

6. Q = Δm × 931.5 = 0.020 × 931.5 = **18.63 MeV**.
   Jaanch: 2 × 9.315 = 18.63 MeV ✅.

7. Light nuclei (A < 4) ka binding energy per nucleon curve bahut **steep (tezi se chadhne wala)** hai (~1 MeV se ~7 MeV = ~6 MeV gain per nucleon!), jabki heavy nuclei ke fission mein sirf ~0.85 MeV/nucleon ka gain hota hai!

8. **4 ¹₁H → ⁴₂He + 2 e⁺ + 2 ν + 26.7 MeV**.

9. **True ✅.** Gayab hua mass (m_reactants − m_products > 0) hi Q = Δm c² energy bankar bahar nikalta hai!

10. Conservation of Mass Number (A): 1 + 235 = 144 + A + 3(1) ⟹ 236 = 147 + A ⟹ **A = 89**.
    Conservation of Atomic Number (Z): 0 + 92 = 56 + Z + 0 ⟹ **Z = 36** (⁸⁹₃₆Kr!).
    Jaanch: 144 + 89 + 3 = 236; 56 + 36 = 92 ✅.
