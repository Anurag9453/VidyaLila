## Decision Map: Kaunsa Formula Kab Lagayein?

| Sawaal mein kya dikhe? | Kaunsa tool chuno? | Key Formula |
|---|---|---|
| Nuclear radius & density | Size formula | R = R₀ A^(1/3) (R₀ = 1.2 fm); ρ = constant (1 : 1!) |
| Mass defect & Binding Energy | Einstein's equivalence | E_b = Δm × 931.5 MeV; E_bn = E_b / A |
| Q-value from masses vs Binding Energies | Watch the order! | Masses: **(m_R − m_P) × 931.5**; Binding Energies: **E_b(Products) − E_b(Reactants)**! |
| Radioactive decay & half-life | Exponential / half-life shortcut | N/N₀ = (1/2)^n (n = t/T_½); R = λN; T_½ = 0.693/λ = 0.693 τ |
| Nuclear power plant fission rate | Convert MeV → Joules! | P = (N/t) × E_fission (1 MeV = 1.6 × 10⁻¹³ J) |

## Mixed Solved Examples (Sab Kuch Mila Kar!)

### Example 1: Finding Q-Value Directly from Binding Energies per Nucleon!
A heavy nucleus of mass number **A = 240** with binding energy per nucleon **7.6 MeV** splits into two identical fragments of mass number **A = 120** each, having binding energy per nucleon **8.5 MeV**. Calculate the total energy **Q** released in this fission!

**Hal (Solution):**
- Reactant ka total binding energy: **E_b(reactant) = 240 × 7.6 = 1824 MeV**.
- Dono products ka total binding energy: **E_b(products) = 2 × (120 × 8.5) = 240 × 8.5 = 2040 MeV**.
- Energy released: **Q = E_b(products) − E_b(reactant) = 2040 − 1824 = +216 MeV**!
  *(Shortcut: Q = 240 × (8.5 − 7.6) = 240 × 0.9 = **216 MeV**!)*
- **Jaanch:** 240 × 0.9 = 216 MeV ✅.

### Example 2: Half-Life + Activity Reduction
A radioactive isotope has half-life **T_½ = 4 hours**. Its initial activity is **R₀ = 3200 Bq**.
(a) What will its activity be after **12 hours**?
(b) How much time will it take for the activity to drop to **100 Bq**?

**Hal (Solution):**
- (a) 12 hours mein half-lives: **n = 12 / 4 = 3**.
  R = R₀ × (1/2)³ = 3200 / 8 = **400 Bq**.
- (b) R / R₀ = 100 / 3200 = **1 / 32 = (1/2)⁵** ⟹ **n = 5 half-lives**!
  Time **t = 5 × 4 = 20 hours**!
- **Jaanch:** 3200 → 1600 → 800 → 400 (12 h) → 200 → 100 (20 h) ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Synthesis Mistakes)

1. **Binding Energies se Q-value nikaalte waqt Reactants − Products kar dena:**
   - *Galat:* Q = E_b(reactants) − E_b(products) = −216 MeV!
   - *Sahi:* **Masses** ke liye **m_reactants − m_products** hota hai, par **Binding Energies** ke liye ulta hota hai: **Q = E_b(products) − E_b(reactants)** (kyunki products zyada tightly bound hote hain)!
2. **Power (Watts) nikaalte waqt MeV ko Joules mein convert karna bhool jaana:**
   - *Galat:* P = (N/t) × 200 likh kar Watts bol dena!
   - *Sahi:* 1 Watt = 1 J/s ⟹ hamesha **1 MeV = 1.6 × 10⁻¹³ J** se multiply karo!
3. **T_½ aur Mean life τ ka relation ulta kar dena:**
   - *Galat:* τ = 0.693 T_½ likhna!
   - *Sahi:* **T_½ = 0.693 τ** (Mean life τ hamesha Half-life T_½ se **badi** hoti hai: τ ≈ 1.44 T_½)!

## Practice Questions (Mixed Chapter Challenge)

1. Two nuclei have mass numbers 8 and 27. Find the ratio of (a) their nuclear radii, and (b) their nuclear densities.
2. Calculate the binding energy per nucleon of ¹²₆C if its mass defect is 0.0989 u (take 1 u = 931.5 MeV).
3. Two Deuterons (²₁H, E_bn = 1.1 MeV each) fuse to form a Helium nucleus (⁴₂He, E_bn = 7.0 MeV). Calculate the energy Q released in MeV.
4. A radioactive element has a mean life τ = 1000 days. Calculate its half-life T_½ in days.
5. After 15 minutes, 7/8 of a radioactive sample has decayed. Find the half-life of the sample.
6. A nucleus ²³⁸₉₂U undergoes 1 alpha decay followed by 2 beta-minus decays. Find the atomic number Z and mass number A of the final daughter nucleus.
7. A nuclear reactor generates 16 MW of thermal power using ²³⁵U fission (200 MeV per fission). How many fissions occur per second?
8. Explain briefly why nuclear density is constant for all nuclei, and why the binding energy per nucleon curve is nearly flat for 30 < A < 170.
9. State whether True or False: In a nuclear reaction, if the total binding energy of the products is greater than the total binding energy of the reactants, energy is released (exothermic).
10. If 1.0 mg (10⁻⁶ kg) of mass is converted into energy during nuclear fusion, how many Joules of energy are released?

### Answers

1. (a) R₁ / R₂ = (8 / 27)^(1/3) = **2 : 3**.
   (b) Nuclear density ratio = **1 : 1** (independent of A!).
   Jaanch: 2³ = 8, 3³ = 27 ✅.

2. E_b = 0.0989 × 931.5 ≈ 92.13 MeV.
   E_bn = 92.13 / 12 ≈ **7.68 MeV/nucleon**.
   Jaanch: 12 × 7.68 ≈ 92.16 MeV ✅.

3. E_b(reactants) = 2 × (2 × 1.1) = 4.4 MeV; E_b(product) = 4 × 7.0 = 28.0 MeV.
   **Q = E_b(product) − E_b(reactants) = 28.0 − 4.4 = 23.6 MeV**.
   Jaanch: 28.0 − 4.4 = 23.6 MeV ✅.

4. **T_½ = 0.693 τ = 0.693 × 1000 = 693 days**.
   Jaanch: T_½ < τ ✅.

5. 7/8 decayed ⟹ **remaining fraction = 1 − 7/8 = 1/8 = (1/2)³** ⟹ **n = 3 half-lives** in 15 minutes!
   **T_½ = 15 / 3 = 5 minutes**.
   Jaanch: 3 × 5 = 15 min ✅.

6. 1 α-decay: A → 238 − 4 = 234, Z → 92 − 2 = 90.
   2 β⁻-decays: A stays **234**, Z → 90 + 2 = **92**!
   Final nucleus: **Z = 92, A = 234** (²³⁴₉₂U — isotope of original Uranium!).
   Jaanch: Z = 92 − 2 + 2 = 92; A = 238 − 4 = 234 ✅.

7. E₁ = 200 MeV = 3.2 × 10⁻¹¹ J; P = 16 × 10⁶ J/s:
   N / t = (16 × 10⁶) / (3.2 × 10⁻¹¹) = **5.0 × 10¹⁷ fissions/s**.
   Jaanch: 16 / 3.2 = 5.0 ⟹ 5.0 × 10¹⁷ ✅.

8. Nuclear volume V ∝ A aur mass M ∝ A hone se density ρ = M/V se A kat jaata hai (constant!). Nuclear force short-range aur saturated hone ke karan har nucleon sirf apne padosiyon se bandhta hai — isliye middle nuclei (30 < A < 170) mein E_bn lagbhag flat (~8.5 MeV) rehta hai!

9. **True ✅.** Q = E_b(products) − E_b(reactants) > 0 ⟹ energy is released!

10. E = m c² = (1.0 × 10⁻⁶) × (9.0 × 10¹⁶) = **9.0 × 10¹⁰ J**.
    Jaanch: 10⁻⁶ × 9 × 10¹⁶ = 9 × 10¹⁰ J ✅.
