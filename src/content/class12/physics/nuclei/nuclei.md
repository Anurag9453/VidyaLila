## Naye Shabd (New Words)

- **Atomic Mass Unit (1 u):** Carbon-12 (¹²₆C) atom ke mass ka theek **1/12th** hissa: **1 u = 1.66 × 10⁻²⁷ kg**.
- **Nucleons:** Nucleus ke andar rehne wale **Protons (Z)** aur **Neutrons (N)** ko milakar nucleons kehte hain. Total nucleons = **Mass Number (A = Z + N)**. (Notation: **ᴬ_Z X**.)
- **Isotopes:** Same **Z** (protons), alag **A** (neutrons) — e.g., ¹₁H, ²₁H, ³₁H.
- **Isobars:** Same **A** (total nucleons), alag **Z** — e.g., ³₁H aur ³₂He (ya ¹⁴₆C aur ¹⁴₇N).
- **Isotones:** Same **N = A − Z** (neutrons), alag **Z** — e.g., ¹⁹⁸₈₀Hg (N=118) aur ¹⁹⁷₇₉Au (N=118) (ya ³₁H aur ⁴₂He, dono mein N = 2!).
- **Fermi (fm):** Nuclear dooriyan naapne ki unit: **1 fm = 10⁻¹⁵ m**.

## 1. Size of the Nucleus (R = R₀ A^(1/3))

Scattering experiments se pata chala ki kisi bhi nucleus ka volume **V** usmein maujood nucleons ki ginti **A** ke directly proportional hota hai (**V ∝ A**):
- (4/3) π R³ ∝ A  ⟹  **R³ ∝ A**  ⟹

**R = R₀ A^(1/3)**

jahan **R₀ ≈ 1.2 × 10⁻¹⁵ m = 1.2 fm** (empirical constant)!

### Do Nuclei ke Radii ka Ratio:
**R₁ / R₂ = (A₁ / A₂)^(1/3)**

---

## 2. Proof: Nuclear Density is Independent of Mass Number A!

Maan lo ek nucleon ka average mass **m** (≈ 1.67 × 10⁻²⁷ kg) hai:
- Nucleus ka mass: **M ≈ A × m**
- Nucleus ka volume: **V = (4/3) π R³ = (4/3) π (R₀ A^(1/3))³ = (4/3) π R₀³ A**
- **Nuclear Density (ρ = M / V):**
  ρ = (A m) / [ (4/3) π R₀³ A ]
- **Dekho! Upar-neeche A kat gaya!**

**ρ = 3 m / (4 π R₀³) ≈ 2.3 × 10¹⁷ kg/m³**  *(CONSTANT for ALL nuclei!)*

Chahe chhota Helium (A = 4) ho ya bhaari Gold (A = 197) ya Uranium (A = 238) — **sabki nuclear density ka ratio hamesha 1 : 1 hota hai!**

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Ratio of Nuclear Radii & Densities
Calculate (a) the ratio of the nuclear radii of Aluminium (**²⁷₁₃Al**) and Tellurium (**¹²⁵₅₂Te**), and (b) the ratio of their nuclear densities.

**Hal (Solution):**
- A₁ = 27, A₂ = 125.
- (a) **R₁ / R₂ = (27 / 125)^(1/3) = 3 / 5** (ratio **3 : 5**!).
- (b) Nuclear density mass number A par depend nahi karti ⟹ **ρ₁ : ρ₂ = 1 : 1**!
- **Jaanch:** 3³ = 27, 5³ = 125 ⟹ (27/125)^(1/3) = 3/5 ✅.

### Example 2: Nuclear Radius of Copper (A = 64)
Calculate the approximate radius of a Copper nucleus (**⁶⁴₂₉Cu**), given **R₀ = 1.2 fm**.

**Hal (Solution):**
- A = 64 ⟹ A^(1/3) = (64)^(1/3) = **4**.
- **R = R₀ A^(1/3) = 1.2 × 4 = 4.8 fm = 4.8 × 10⁻¹⁵ m**.
- **Jaanch:** 4³ = 64; 1.2 × 4 = 4.8 fm ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **Bhaari nucleus (Uranium) ki density halke nucleus (Carbon) se zyada bol dena:**
   - *Galat:* Uranium mein 238 nucleons hain isliye uski nuclear density Carbon se 20 guna zyada hai!
   - *Sahi:* Volume bhi A ke saath badhta hai ⟹ A kat jaata hai ⟹ **Nuclear density sabhi nuclei ke liye CONSTANT (1 : 1)** hoti hai!
2. **R ∝ A likh dena (cube root bhool jaana!):**
   - *Galat:* A = 64 ka radius A = 8 ke radius se 8 guna bada hoga!
   - *Sahi:* **R ∝ A^(1/3)** ⟹ (64/8)^(1/3) = 8^(1/3) = **2 guna**!
3. **Isotopes, Isobars aur Isotones ko aapas mein badal dena:**
   - *Sahi:* Iso**p**es (same **p**rotons Z); Iso**b**ars (same mass number **A**); Iso**n**es (same **n**eutrons N = A − Z)!

|---|---|---|
| Atomic Mass Unit (1 u) | 1.66 × 10⁻²⁷ kg | 1/12th mass of ¹²₆C atom |
| Nucleons | A = Z + N (Z protons, N neutrons) | Isotopes (same Z), Isobars (same A), Isotones (same N) |

## Practice Questions

1. Define 1 atomic mass unit (1 u) and write its value in kilograms.
2. Distinguish between isotopes, isobars, and isotones with one example pair for each.
3. Find the number of protons, neutrons, and nucleons in (a) ¹⁶₈O, and (b) ²³⁵₉₂U.
4. Show mathematically that the nuclear density of any nucleus is independent of its mass number A.
5. Two nuclei have mass numbers in the ratio 1 : 27. Find (a) the ratio of their nuclear radii, and (b) the ratio of their nuclear densities.
6. Given R₀ = 1.2 fm, calculate the nuclear radius of (a) ⁸₄Be, and (b) ²⁷₁₃Al.
7. What is the ratio of the nuclear **volumes** of two nuclei having mass numbers 8 and 64?
8. Identify whether the pair ¹⁴₆C and ¹⁴₇N are isotopes, isobars, or isotones.
9. State whether True or False: The nuclear density of a Gold nucleus (A = 197) is much greater than that of an Oxygen nucleus (A = 16).
10. If the radius of a nucleus with A = 27 is 3.6 fm, what will be the radius of a nucleus with A = 216?

### Answers

1. **1 u** = Carbon-12 (¹²₆C) atom ke mass ka theek 1/12th hissa = **1.66 × 10⁻²⁷ kg**.

2. **Isotopes** (same Z): ¹²₆C & ¹⁴₆C; **Isobars** (same A): ¹⁴₆C & ¹⁴₇N; **Isotones** (same N): ³₁H (N=2) & ⁴₂He (N=2).

3. (a) ¹⁶₈O: Z = **8 protons**, N = 16 − 8 = **8 neutrons**, A = **16 nucleons**.
   (b) ²³⁵₉₂U: Z = **92 protons**, N = 235 − 92 = **143 neutrons**, A = **235 nucleons**.
   Jaanch: 92 + 143 = 235 ✅.

4. Mass M = A m; Volume V = (4/3)πR³ = (4/3)πR₀³ A.
   Density **ρ = M / V = (A m) / [(4/3)πR₀³ A] = 3 m / (4π R₀³)** — A kat gaya, isliye ρ independent of A hai!

5. (a) R₁ / R₂ = (1 / 27)^(1/3) = **1 : 3**.
   (b) Nuclear density ratio = **1 : 1** (constant!).
   Jaanch: 3³ = 27 ⟹ 1:3 ✅.

6. (a) A = 8 ⟹ 8^(1/3) = 2 ⟹ R = 1.2 × 2 = **2.4 fm**.
   (b) A = 27 ⟹ 27^(1/3) = 3 ⟹ R = 1.2 × 3 = **3.6 fm**.
   Jaanch: 1.2 × 2 = 2.4; 1.2 × 3 = 3.6 ✅.

7. Volume V ∝ A ⟹ V₁ : V₂ = 8 : 64 = **1 : 8**.
   Jaanch: R₁:R₂ = 1:2 ⟹ V₁:V₂ = 1³:2³ = 1:8 ✅.

8. Dono ka mass number **A = 14** same hai (par Z = 6 aur 7 alag hain) ⟹ **Isobars**!

9. **False ❌.** Sabhi nuclei ki nuclear density lagbhag **barabar (~2.3 × 10¹⁷ kg/m³)** hoti hai!

10. R₂ / R₁ = (216 / 27)^(1/3) = 8^(1/3) = **2** ⟹ **R₂ = 2 × 3.6 = 7.2 fm**.
    Jaanch: 6³ = 216, 3³ = 27 ⟹ 6/3 = 2 ⟹ 7.2 fm ✅.
