## Naye Shabd (New Words)

- **Refractive Index (n = c / v):** Vacuum mein light ki speed (c) aur medium mein speed (v) ka ratio. (Snell's Law: **n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r**.)
- **Apparent Depth (d_app):** Rarer medium (air) se denser medium (water/glass) ke andar dekhne par kisi cheez ki jo kam gahrai dikhti hai: **d_app = d_real / n**.
- **Critical Angle (i_c):** Denser medium mein woh khaas angle of incidence jis par rarer medium mein angle of refraction theek **90°** ho jaaye: **sin i_c = 1 / n** (rarer medium = air).
- **Total Internal Reflection (TIR):** Jab denser medium se rarer medium ki taraf jaati light ka angle of incidence **i > i_c** ho, to 100% light bina refract hue usi denser medium mein reflect ho jaati hai!

---

## 1. Real Depth, Apparent Depth aur Normal Shift

Jab tum hawa (n = 1) se paani (n = 4/3) ya glass (n = 1.5) ke andar rakhe sikke ko seedha upar se dekhte ho:
- **Apparent Depth:** **d_app = d_real / n**  *(hamesha d_real se kam!)*
- **Normal Shift (kitna upar utha dikha):** **Δd = d_real − d_app = d_real (1 − 1 / n)**!

---

## 2. Total Internal Reflection (TIR) — Do Pakki Shartein!

TIR tabhi hota hai jab yeh **DONO** shartein poori hon:
1. Light **Optically Denser se Optically Rarer** medium mein ja rahi ho (jaise Glass → Air ya Water → Air).
2. Angle of incidence **Critical Angle se zyada (i > i_c)** ho!

### Critical Angle (i_c) ka Formula:
Snell's law lagao (denser medium n → air n₂ = 1, r = 90°):
n sin i_c = 1 × sin 90° = 1 ⟹

**sin i_c = 1 / n**  *(general two media n_denser → n_rarer: **sin i_c = n_rarer / n_denser**!)*

```
     Air (rarer, n = 1)        r = 90 deg
   -------------------------*------------>
             ^            /   \
             :         i_c     \  i > i_c (TIR!)
     Water / :        /         v
     Glass   :       /   (100% reflected!)
```

### TIR ke 4 Zabardast Applications:
1. **Optical Fibres (High-Speed Internet & Medical Endoscopy):** Hair-thin glass fibre mein andar **Core** (higher refractive index n₁) aur bahar **Cladding** (lower refractive index n₂ < n₁) hota hai! Light core ke andar baar-baar **TIR** hokar bina energy loss ke hazaaron km (mod par bhi!) daud jaati hai!
2. **Brilliance of Diamond:** Diamond ka refractive index bahut zyada (**n ≈ 2.42**) hai ⟹ iska critical angle bahut chhota (**i_c ≈ 24.4°**) hota hai! Isliye andar ghusi light baar-baar TIR hokar chamak uthti hai!
3. **Mirage (Garmi mein Sadak par Paani ka Bhram):** Garam zameen ke paas hawa halki (kam n) aur upar thandi hawa ghani (zyada n) hoti hai ⟹ aasman se aati rays neeche jaate-jaate TIR hokar upar mud jaati hain aur paani jaisa reflection dikhta hai!
4. **Totally Reflecting Prisms (45°–90°–45° Glass Prisms):** Glass ka i_c ≈ 42° (< 45°) hota hai ⟹ 45° par takrati light bina loss ke **90° ya 180°** mud jaati hai (binoculars aur periscopes mein mirrors se bhi behtar!).

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Apparent Depth & Normal Shift of a Tank
A water tank of refractive index **n = 4/3** is filled to a real depth of **12 cm**. Find (a) the apparent depth of a coin at its bottom, and (b) how much the coin appears raised (normal shift).

**Hal (Solution):**
- (a) d_app = d_real / n = 12 / (4/3) = 12 × (3/4) = **9.0 cm**.
- (b) Normal shift = d_real − d_app = 12 − 9 = **3.0 cm** (coin 3 cm upar utha dikhega!).
- **Jaanch:** 12 × (1 − 3/4) = 12 × 1/4 = 3.0 cm ✅.

### Example 2: Critical Angle of Glass & Diamond
Calculate the critical angle for (a) glass-air interface (**n = 1.5**), and (b) a medium with refractive index **n = √2** (in air).

**Hal (Solution):**
- (a) sin i_c = 1 / 1.5 = 2/3 ≈ 0.667 ⟹ **i_c ≈ 41.8°** (≈ 42°).
- (b) sin i_c = 1 / √2 ⟹ **i_c = 45°**!
- **Jaanch:** sin 45° = 1/√2 ✅; sin 41.8° ≈ 0.667 ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **Rarer se Denser medium mein TIR karwa dena:**
   - *Galat:* Air se glass mein 60° par light jaane par TIR hoga!
   - *Sahi:* Rarer → Denser mein light normal ki taraf mudti hai — **TIR KABHI nahi hota**! TIR **SIRF Denser → Rarer** mein hota hai!
2. **sin i_c = n likh dena (1/n ulta kar dena!):**
   - *Galat:* sin i_c = 1.5 likhna!
   - *Sahi:* sin θ kabhi 1 se bada nahi ho sakta! Hamesha **sin i_c = 1 / n** (ya n_rarer / n_denser < 1)!
3. **Optical fibre mein core ka n cladding se kam maan lena:**
   - *Galat:* Cladding ka refractive index core se zyada hota hai.
   - *Sahi:* TIR core ke andar hona hai (Denser → Rarer) ⟹ **n_core > n_cladding** zaroori hai!

## Quick Recap

| Concept | Formula / Condition | Key Application |
|---|---|---|
| Snell's Law | n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r; n = c / v | Light bends towards normal in denser medium |
| Apparent Depth & Shift | d_app = d_real / n; Shift = d_real(1 − 1/n) | Bottom of pool/tank looks shallower |
| Critical Angle & TIR | Denser → Rarer AND i > i_c; **sin i_c = 1/n** | Optical fibres (n_core > n_cladding), diamond, mirage, prisms |

## Practice Questions

1. State the two essential conditions for Total Internal Reflection (TIR) to occur.
2. What is the refractive index of a medium whose critical angle with air is 30°?
3. A swimming pool is 2.0 m deep and filled with water (n = 4/3). What is its apparent depth when viewed vertically from above?
4. A glass slab (n = 1.5) of thickness 9 cm is placed over a printed page. By how much do the letters appear raised when viewed from directly above?
5. Why must the refractive index of the **core** be greater than that of the **cladding** in an optical fibre?
6. Light travels from glass (n = 1.5) to water (n = 4/3). Find sin i_c for this glass-water interface.
7. Explain why a diamond sparkles much more brilliantly than a piece of glass cut in the exact same shape.
8. How can a right-angled isosceles glass prism (45°–90°–45°, i_c ≈ 42°) turn a light ray by (a) 90°, and (b) 180° without using any silvered mirror?
9. State whether True or False: When light enters water from air, its speed and wavelength decrease, while its frequency remains unchanged.
10. A small bulb is placed at the bottom of a tank containing water (n = 4/3) to a depth of 80 cm. Find the radius of the circular area on the water surface through which light from the bulb can emerge into air? (Hint: at the edge of the circle, i = i_c, so tan i_c = R/h; use √7 ≈ 2.65.)

### Answers

1. (i) Light ray **optically denser se optically rarer** medium mein jaani chahiye; (ii) Angle of incidence **critical angle se zyada (i > i_c)** hona chahiye.

2. sin i_c = 1 / n ⟹ sin 30° = 0.5 = 1 / n ⟹ **n = 2.0**.
   Jaanch: 1 / 2.0 = 0.5 = sin 30° ✅.

3. d_app = d_real / n = 2.0 / (4/3) = 6 / 4 = **1.5 m**.
   Jaanch: 1.5 × (4/3) = 2.0 m ✅.

4. Shift = d_real (1 − 1/n) = 9 × (1 − 2/3) = 9 × (1/3) = **3.0 cm**.
   Jaanch: d_app = 9/1.5 = 6 cm; 9 − 6 = 3.0 cm ✅.

5. Kyunki optical fibre mein light ko **core ke andar TIR** karwana hota hai — aur TIR sirf **denser se rarer** boundary par hota hai, isliye **n_core > n_cladding** zaroori hai!

6. sin i_c = n_rarer / n_denser = (4/3) / (3/2) = (4/3) × (2/3) = **8 / 9 ≈ 0.889**.
   Jaanch: 4/3 ÷ 1.5 = 8/9 < 1 ✅.

7. Diamond ka refractive index (2.42) glass (1.5) se bahut zyada hai, isliye uska **critical angle bahut chhota (24.4°)** hota hai. Andar ghusi light kai baar TIR hokar alag-alag facets se chamak kar bahar nikalti hai!

8. Kyunki glass ka i_c ≈ 42° < 45°, 45° par hypotenuse face par girne wali ray 100% TIR hokar **90°** mud jaati hai; aur agar hypotenuse se enter kare to dono perpendicular faces par do baar 45° TIR hokar **180°** ulat kar wapas aa jaati hai!

9. **True ✅.** Medium badalne par **frequency (source property) kabhi nahi badalti**; denser medium mein v = c/n aur λ = λ₀/n dono ghat jaate hain!

10. sin i_c = 1/n = 3/4 ⟹ cos i_c = √(1 − 9/16) = √7 / 4 ⟹ tan i_c = 3 / √7.
    R = h tan i_c = 80 × (3 / 2.65) = 240 / 2.65 ≈ **90.6 cm** (≈ 0.91 m).
    Jaanch: sin i_c = 0.75, cos i_c ≈ 0.661, tan i_c ≈ 1.134; 80 × 1.134 ≈ 90.7 cm ✅.
