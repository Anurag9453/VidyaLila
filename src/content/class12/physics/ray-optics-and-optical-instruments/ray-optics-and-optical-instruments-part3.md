## Optician ki Dukaan aur Chashme ke Lens ka Formula!

**Pragya** apni Mummy ka naya chashma (+2.0 D power ka) banwane optician ki dukaan par gayi thi. Wahan usne dekha ki optician ke paas alag-alag curve (radius of curvature R₁ aur R₂) wale glass blanks rakhe the. Pragya ne poocha: "Uncle, aapko kaise pata chalta hai ki +2.0 D power ke liye glass ke dono faces ko kitna gol (curved) ghisna hai?"

Optician muskuraye: "Iske liye physics ka ek zabardast formula hai — **Lens Maker's Formula**! Glass ka refractive index n aur dono surfaces ke radii R₁, R₂ daalo, aur exact focal length **f** aur power **P** mil jaati hai!"

---

## Naye Shabd (New Words)

- **Spherical Refracting Surface:** Ek curved boundary (radius R) jo refractive index n₁ aur n₂ wale do media ko alag karti hai.
- **Lens Maker's Formula:** Thin lens ki focal length ko uske material (n) aur dono curved surfaces ke radii (R₁, R₂) se jodne wala formula: **1/f = (n − 1) (1/R₁ − 1/R₂)**.
- **Power of a Lens (P):** Lens ki light rays ko converge ya diverge karne ki taqat: **P = 1 / f (in metres)**. SI unit **Dioptre (D)** = m⁻¹. (Convex: +P; Concave: −P.)

---

## 1. Refraction at a Single Spherical Surface

Jab light refractive index **n₁** wale medium se **n₂** wale medium mein radius of curvature **R** ki curved surface se guzarti hai (paraxial rays), to object distance **u** aur image distance **v** ka rishta hota hai:

**n₂ / v − n₁ / u = (n₂ − n₁) / R**

*(Yaad rakhne ka trick: Jahan ray ja rahi hai us medium ka **n₂** hamesha **v** ke upar aur **n₂ − n₁** mein pehle aata hai!)*

---

## 2. Lens Maker's Formula aur Liquid mein Lens ka Badlaav!

Ek thin lens (refractive index **n₂**) do spherical surfaces (radii **R₁** aur **R₂**) se milkar bana hota hai, jo surrounding medium **n₁** (usually air, n₁ = 1) mein rakha hai. Pehli aur doosri surface par upar wala formula lagakar jodne par milta hai **Lens Maker's Formula**:

**1 / f = (n₂/n₁ − 1) × (1 / R₁ − 1 / R₂)**

Air mein (n₁ = 1, n₂ = n):

**1 / f = (n − 1) × (1 / R₁ − 1 / R₂)**

### Sign Convention for R₁ and R₂ (Super Important!):
- **Double Convex (Biconvex) Lens:** Pehli surface ka centre right mein (**R₁ > 0**), doosri ka left mein (**R₂ < 0**)! (Equiconvex ke liye R₁ = +R, R₂ = −R ⟹ **1/f = 2(n − 1) / R**!)
- **Double Concave (Biconcave) Lens:** **R₁ < 0**, **R₂ > 0**!
- **Plano-convex Lens:** Ek surface flat (**R = ∞ ⟹ 1/∞ = 0**)!

### Jab Lens ko Liquid (n_l) mein Duboya Jaaye:
1. **n_l < n_glass** (jaise glass 1.5 ko water 1.33 mein): (n_g/n_l − 1) chhota ho jaata hai ⟹ **Focal length f BADH jaati hai** (glass in water: lagbhag **4 guna**!), par nature (converging/diverging) wahi rehta hai!
2. **n_l = n_glass:** (1 − 1) = 0 ⟹ **f = ∞**! Lens **gayab (invisible)** ho jaata hai — simple flat glass plate ban jaata hai!
3. **n_l > n_glass** (jaise glass 1.5 ko CS₂ 1.63 mein): (n_g/n_l − 1) **negative** ho jaata hai ⟹ **Convex lens diverging (concave) ki tarah behave karne lagta hai!**

---

## 3. Thin Lens Formula, Power aur Combination of Lenses

- **Thin Lens Formula:** **1 / v − 1 / u = 1 / f**
- **Magnification:** **m = h' / h = + v / u** *(Mirror mein −v/u tha, Lens mein **+v/u** hai!)*
- **Power:** **P = 1 / f(m) = 100 / f(cm)** (unit **Dioptre, D**).
- **Thin Lenses in Contact:** Agar do (ya zyada) thin lenses (focal lengths f₁, f₂; powers P₁, P₂) ek-doosre se chipka kar rakhe jaayein:
  - **1 / f_eq = 1 / f₁ + 1 / f₂ + ...**
  - **P_eq = P₁ + P₂ + ...**
  - **Total Magnification:** **m = m₁ × m₂ × ...** (multiply hoti hain!)

---

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Lens Maker's Formula (Air vs Water)
A double convex glass lens (**n = 1.5**) has radii of curvature **R₁ = +20 cm** and **R₂ = −20 cm**.
(a) Calculate its focal length and power in air.
(b) What is its new focal length when completely immersed in water (**n_w = 4/3**)?

**Hal (Solution):**
- (a) Air mein: 1/f_air = (1.5 − 1) × [ 1/20 − 1/(−20) ] = 0.5 × (2/20) = 0.5 × (1/10) = +1/20 ⟹ **f_air = +20 cm**.
  Power: **P = 100 / 20 = +5.0 D**.
- (b) Water mein: n_rel = n_g / n_w = (3/2) / (4/3) = **9/8 = 1.125**.
  1/f_water = (9/8 − 1) × (2/20) = (1/8) × (1/10) = +1/80 ⟹ **f_water = +80 cm** (4 guna badh gayi!).
- **Jaanch:** (1.5 − 1) / (1.125 − 1) = 0.5 / 0.125 = 4 ⟹ 4 × 20 = 80 cm ✅.

### Example 2: Combination of Thin Lenses in Contact
A convex lens of focal length **20 cm** (**+5 D**) is placed in contact with a concave lens of focal length **−33.33 cm** (**−3 D**). Find the combined power, focal length, and nature of the combination.

**Hal (Solution):**
- **P_eq = P₁ + P₂ = +5 D + (−3 D) = +2.0 D** (Positive ⟹ **Converging / Convex**!).
- **f_eq = 100 / P_eq = 100 / 2.0 = +50 cm**.
- **Jaanch:** 1/20 − 3/100 = 5/100 − 3/100 = 2/100 = 1/50 ⟹ +50 cm ✅.

---

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **Double convex lens ke liye R₁ = +20 aur R₂ = +20 rakh dena:**
   - *Galat:* Dono positive rakhne par 1/20 − 1/20 = 0 ⟹ f = ∞ aa jaata hai!
   - *Sahi:* Double convex mein **R₁ > 0** aur **R₂ < 0** hota hai (dono curved faces opposite directions mein khulte hain)!
2. **Power P = 1 / f mein f ko centimetres mein rakh dena:**
   - *Galat:* f = 20 cm ke liye P = 1/20 = 0.05 D likhna!
   - *Sahi:* f metres mein rakho (**P = 1 / 0.20 = +5 D**) ya **P = 100 / f(cm)** use karo!
3. **Lens formula mein 1/v + 1/u laga dena:**
   - *Galat:* Mirror wala plus sign lens mein laga dena.
   - *Sahi:* **Lens formula: 1/v − 1/u = 1/f** (MINUS!) aur **Magnification: m = +v/u** (PLUS!).

---

## Quick Recap

| Formula | Expression | Key Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Single Spherical Surface | n₂/v − n₁/u = (n₂ − n₁)/R | Light goes from medium n₁ to n₂ |
| Lens Maker's Formula | 1/f = (n₂/n₁ − 1)(1/R₁ − 1/R₂) | Biconvex: R₁ > 0, R₂ < 0; Plano: R = ∞ |
| Thin Lens & Magnification | 1/v − 1/u = 1/f; m = +v/u | Convex: f > 0; Concave: f < 0 |
| Power & Contact Combination | P = 1/f(m) = 100/f(cm); P_eq = P₁ + P₂ | m_total = m₁ × m₂ |

## Practice Questions

1. Write the Lens Maker's formula and explain what each symbol represents.
2. A plano-convex lens of glass (n = 1.5) has radius of curvature of its curved surface equal to 15 cm. Calculate its focal length and power in air.
3. What happens to a convex glass lens (n = 1.5) when immersed in a liquid of refractive index (a) 1.5, and (b) 1.65?
4. An object is placed 30 cm in front of a convex lens of focal length 20 cm. Find the position, magnification, and nature of the image.
5. A concave lens of focal length 15 cm forms an image 10 cm from the lens. How far is the object placed from the lens?
6. Two thin lenses of focal lengths +25 cm and −50 cm are kept in contact. Find the equivalent focal length and power of the combination.
7. A point object is placed in air (n₁ = 1) at a distance of 40 cm in front of a convex spherical glass surface (n₂ = 1.5) of radius of curvature R = +20 cm. Find the position of the image.
8. If an equiconvex lens of focal length f is cut into two equal halves **perpendicular to the principal axis** (forming two plano-convex lenses), what is the focal length of each half?
9. State whether True or False: The power of a diverging (concave) lens is always negative.
10. A convex lens of power +4 D is placed in contact with another lens such that the combination behaves like a diverging lens of focal length 50 cm. Find the power of the second lens.

### Answers

1. **1/f = (n₂/n₁ − 1)(1/R₁ − 1/R₂)**, jahan f = focal length, n₂ = lens material ka refractive index, n₁ = surrounding medium ka refractive index, aur R₁, R₂ = dono surfaces ke radii of curvature hain.

2. Plano-convex: R₁ = +15 cm, R₂ = ∞ (1/R₂ = 0):
   1/f = (1.5 − 1)(1/15 − 0) = 0.5 / 15 = 1 / 30 ⟹ **f = +30 cm**.
   Power **P = 100 / 30 ≈ +3.33 D**.
   Jaanch: 0.5 / 15 = 1/30 ⟹ f = 30 cm ✅.

3. (a) **n_l = 1.5 = n_g:** f = ∞ — lens **invisible** ho jaata hai (bina mode light seedhi nikal jaati hai!).
   (b) **n_l = 1.65 > n_g:** f negative ho jaati hai — convex lens **diverging (concave) lens** ban jaata hai!

4. u = −30 cm, f = +20 cm:
   1/v = 1/f + 1/u = 1/20 + 1/(−30) = (3 − 2)/60 = +1/60 ⟹ **v = +60 cm** (Real & inverted).
   m = v/u = (+60)/(−30) = **−2** (2× magnified, inverted).
   Jaanch: 1/60 − 1/(−30) = 3/60 = 1/20 ✅.

5. Concave lens hamesha virtual image usi side banata hai ⟹ f = −15 cm, v = −10 cm:
   1/u = 1/v − 1/f = 1/(−10) − 1/(−15) = −1/10 + 1/15 = (−3 + 2)/30 = −1/30 ⟹ **u = −30 cm** (30 cm in front of lens).
   Jaanch: 1/(−10) − 1/(−30) = −2/30 = −1/15 ✅.

6. 1/f_eq = 1/25 + 1/(−50) = (2 − 1)/50 = +1/50 ⟹ **f_eq = +50 cm**.
   **P_eq = 100 / 50 = +2.0 D** (converging).
   Jaanch: P₁ = +4 D, P₂ = −2 D ⟹ +4 − 2 = +2 D ✅.

7. n₂/v − n₁/u = (n₂ − n₁)/R ⟹ 1.5/v − 1/(−40) = (1.5 − 1)/20 = 0.5/20 = 1/40.
   1.5/v + 1/40 = 1/40 ⟹ 1.5/v = 0 ⟹ **v = ∞** (rays glass ke andar principal axis ke parallel ho jaati hain!).
   Jaanch: 1/40 − 1/40 = 0 ⟹ v = ∞ ✅.

8. Original equiconvex: 1/f = (n − 1)(2/R). Har plano-convex half: 1/f' = (n − 1)(1/R − 0) = ½ (1/f) ⟹ **f' = 2 f** (focal length double ho jaati hai!).

9. **True ✅.** Concave lens ki focal length f negative hoti hai ⟹ P = 1/f hamesha **negative**!

10. Combined diverging focal length = −50 cm ⟹ P_eq = 100 / (−50) = −2 D.
    P_eq = P₁ + P₂ ⟹ −2 = +4 + P₂ ⟹ **P₂ = −6.0 D**.
    Jaanch: +4 D + (−6 D) = −2 D ✅.
