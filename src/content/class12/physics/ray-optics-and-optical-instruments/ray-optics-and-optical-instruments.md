## Dentist ka Mirror aur Car ka Rear-View Mirror!

Dental clinic mein **Payal** ne dekha ki dentist ek chhote se **concave mirror** ko daant ke bahut paas le jaakar dekhta hai to daant ka bada aur seedha (magnified erect) image dikhta hai! Clinic se bahar aakar jab woh Papa ki car mein baithi, to side wale **convex mirror** par likha tha: *"Objects in mirror are closer than they appear"* — kyunki usmein peeche ki poori sadak ki chhoti aur seedhi image dikh rahi thi!

Aaj Class 12 mein hum **Cartesian Sign Convention**, **f = R / 2** ka geometry proof, aur **Mirror Equation** ke exact numericals master karenge!

## Naye Shabd (New Words)

- **Paraxial Rays:** Aisi light rays jo principal axis ke bahut paas aur uske saath bahut chhote angles par chalti hain (mirror equation aur f = R/2 inhi ke liye exact hote hain).
- **New Cartesian Sign Convention:**
  1. Saari dooriyan mirror ke **Pole (P)** se naapi jaati hain.
  2. Incident light ki direction mein (+x) naapi dooriyan **Positive (+)**; uske opposite (−x) **Negative (−)**.
  3. Principal axis ke upar (+y) **Positive (+)**; neeche (−y) **Negative (−)**.
- **Linear Magnification (m):** Image ki height (h') aur object ki height (h) ka ratio: **m = h' / h = −v / u**.

---

## 1. Relation Between Focal Length and Radius of Curvature (f = R / 2)

Maan lo ek concave mirror (centre of curvature **C**, focus **F**, pole **P**) par principal axis ke parallel ek **paraxial ray** point M par takrati hai aur reflect hokar focus **F** se guzarti hai:
- Normal CM mirror ke perpendicular hai ⟹ angle of incidence **θ** = angle of reflection **θ**.
- Alternate interior angles se **∠MCF = θ**, aur exterior angle se **∠MFP = 2θ**.
- Paraxial rays (chhote angle θ) ke liye: **tan θ ≈ θ = MP / R** aur **tan 2θ ≈ 2θ = MP / f**.
- Divide karo: **2 = (MP / f) / (MP / R) = R / f** ⟹

**f = R / 2**

*(Sign Convention yaad rakho: **Concave mirror** ke liye **f < 0, R < 0**; **Convex mirror** ke liye **f > 0, R > 0**; aur real object ke liye **u < 0** hamesha!)*

---

## 2. Mirror Equation aur Magnification

Similar triangles (object AB aur image A'B' se pole P aur focus F par bane triangles) ko compare karne par milta hai **Mirror Equation**:

**1 / v + 1 / u = 1 / f**

Aur **Linear Magnification (m)**:

**m = h' / h = − v / u = f / (f − u) = (f − v) / f**

### Sign of Magnification (m) — Instant Nature Check!
- **m < 0 (Negative):** **Real & Inverted** image!
- **m > 0 (Positive):** **Virtual & Erect** image!
- **|m| > 1:** Magnified (badi); **|m| < 1:** Diminished (chhoti); **|m| = 1:** Same size!

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Concave Mirror (Real vs Virtual Image)
A concave mirror has focal length **15 cm** (**f = −15 cm**). Find the position, nature, and magnification of the image when an object is placed at:
(a) **u = −30 cm**, and
(b) **u = −10 cm**.

**Hal (Solution):**
- **(a) u = −30 cm:**
  1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/(−15) − 1/(−30) = −1/15 + 1/30 = (−2 + 1)/30 = −1/30 ⟹ **v = −30 cm** (in front of mirror).
  m = −v/u = −(−30)/(−30) = **−1** (Real, inverted, same size — object C par hai!).
- **(b) u = −10 cm:**
  1/v = 1/(−15) − 1/(−10) = −1/15 + 1/10 = (−2 + 3)/30 = +1/30 ⟹ **v = +30 cm** (behind the mirror!).
  m = −v/u = −(+30)/(−10) = **+3** (Virtual, erect, 3× magnified — bilkul Payal ke dentist mirror jaisa!).
- **Jaanch:** (a) 1/(−30) + 1/(−30) = −2/30 = −1/15 ✅. (b) 1/30 + 1/(−10) = −2/30 = −1/15 ✅.

### Example 2: Convex Rear-View Mirror
A convex mirror of radius of curvature **R = +40 cm** is used as a rear-view mirror. A car is **20 cm** in front of it (**u = −20 cm**). Find the image position and magnification.

**Hal (Solution):**
- f = R / 2 = +40 / 2 = **+20 cm**; u = −20 cm.
- 1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/20 − 1/(−20) = 1/20 + 1/20 = 2/20 = +1/10 ⟹ **v = +10 cm** (behind the mirror).
- m = −v/u = −(+10)/(−20) = **+0.5** (Virtual, erect, half the size!).
- **Jaanch:** 1/10 + 1/(−20) = (2 − 1)/20 = +1/20 = 1/f ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **u aur concave mirror ke f ka minus sign chhod dena:**
   - *Galat:* Concave mirror f = 15 cm, u = 10 cm ko seedha +15, +10 rakh dena!
   - *Sahi:* Real object ke liye **u hamesha negative (−)**, aur concave mirror ke liye **f hamesha negative (−)** hota hai!
2. **Mirror formula mein plus ki jagah minus laga dena:**
   - *Galat:* 1/v − 1/u = 1/f likhna (yeh lens formula hai!).
   - *Sahi:* **Mirror formula = 1/v + 1/u = 1/f** (PLUS sign!); **Magnification = −v/u** (MINUS sign!).
3. **Convex mirror se real image bana dena:**
   - *Galat:* Real object ke liye convex mirror mein v negative nikaalna!
   - *Sahi:* Real object ke liye convex mirror **HAMESHA virtual, erect aur diminished** image (v > 0, 0 < m < 1) banata hai!

---

## Quick Recap

| Mirror Type | Sign of f & R | Mirror Formula & Magnification | Image Nature (Real Object) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concave Mirror | **f < 0, R < 0** | 1/v + 1/u = 1/f; m = −v/u | Real & inverted (u > f); Virtual & magnified (u < f) |
| Convex Mirror | **f > 0, R > 0** | f = R / 2 (paraxial rays) | Always **Virtual, Erect & Diminished** |

## Practice Questions

1. State the New Cartesian Sign Convention for spherical mirrors.
2. What are paraxial rays? Why is f = R/2 valid only for paraxial rays?
3. An object of height 3 cm is placed 24 cm in front of a concave mirror of focal length 16 cm. Find the position, nature, and height of the image.
4. Where should an object be placed in front of a concave mirror of focal length 20 cm to obtain a **real** image that is **2 times** the size of the object?
5. Where should the object in Question 4 be placed to obtain a **virtual** image that is **2 times** the size of the object?
6. A convex mirror used on a bus has radius of curvature 3.0 m. If a scooter is located 6.0 m behind the mirror, find the position and magnification of its image.
7. Why is a convex mirror preferred as a rear-view mirror in vehicles instead of a plane mirror?
8. A concave mirror produces a real image of the same size as the object when the object is placed 40 cm from the mirror. Find its focal length and radius of curvature.
9. State whether True or False: The focal length of a spherical mirror changes if the mirror is completely immersed in water.
10. An object is placed 12 cm in front of a concave mirror of radius of curvature 36 cm. Find the image distance v and magnification m.

### Answers

1. (i) Saari dooriyan Pole (P) se naapi jaati hain; (ii) Incident light ki direction mein **+** aur opposite **−**; (iii) Axis ke upar **+** aur neeche **−**.

2. **Paraxial rays** principal axis ke bahut paas aur chhote angle par chalti hain. Chhote angles par hi tan θ ≈ θ aur tan 2θ ≈ 2θ approximations sahi hoti hain, jisse **f = R/2** milta hai!

3. u = −24 cm, f = −16 cm:
   1/v = 1/(−16) − 1/(−24) = −1/16 + 1/24 = (−3 + 2)/48 = −1/48 ⟹ **v = −48 cm** (Real & inverted).
   m = −v/u = −(−48)/(−24) = −2 ⟹ **h' = m h = (−2)(3) = −6 cm** (6 cm tall, inverted).
   Jaanch: 1/(−48) + 1/(−24) = −3/48 = −1/16 ✅.

4. Real image ⟹ m = −2 = −v/u ⟹ v = 2u. f = −20 cm:
   1/(2u) + 1/u = 1/(−20) ⟹ 3/(2u) = −1/20 ⟹ **u = −30 cm** (30 cm in front of mirror).
   Jaanch: u = −30, v = −60 ⟹ −1/60 − 1/30 = −3/60 = −1/20 ✅.

5. Virtual image ⟹ m = +2 = −v/u ⟹ v = −2u. f = −20 cm:
   1/(−2u) + 1/u = 1/(−20) ⟹ 1/(2u) = −1/20 ⟹ **u = −10 cm** (10 cm in front of mirror).
   Jaanch: u = −10, v = +20 ⟹ 1/20 − 1/10 = −1/20 ✅.

6. R = +3.0 m ⟹ f = +1.5 m, u = −6.0 m:
   1/v = 1/1.5 − 1/(−6.0) = 2/3 + 1/6 = 5/6 ⟹ **v = +6/5 = +1.2 m** (behind mirror).
   m = −v/u = −(1.2)/(−6.0) = **+0.2** (virtual, erect, diminished).
   Jaanch: 1/1.2 − 1/6 = 5/6 − 1/6 = 4/6 = 2/3 = 1/1.5 ✅.

7. Convex mirror hamesha **erect aur diminished** image banata hai, jisse uska **field of view bahut wide (chauda)** ho jaata hai aur driver peeche ki kaafi badi sadak ek saath dekh sakta hai!

8. Real & same size ⟹ object Centre of Curvature C par hai ⟹ **R = −40 cm** (magnitude 40 cm), aur **f = R/2 = −20 cm** (magnitude 20 cm)!

9. **False ❌.** Reflection sirf mirror ki geometry (R) par depend karta hai, surrounding medium ke refractive index par nahi — isliye **mirror ki focal length water mein bilkul nahi badalti**!

10. R = −36 cm ⟹ f = −18 cm, u = −12 cm:
    1/v = 1/(−18) − 1/(−12) = −1/18 + 1/12 = (−2 + 3)/36 = +1/36 ⟹ **v = +36 cm** (virtual & erect).
    m = −(+36)/(−12) = **+3**.
    Jaanch: 1/36 − 1/12 = −2/36 = −1/18 ✅.
