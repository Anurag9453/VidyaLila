## Do Ungliyon ke Beech Patli Daraar se Dikhi Pattiyan!

Shaam ko **Pritam** apni do ungliyon ko aankh ke paas laakar unke beech baal jitni patli daraar (slit) banakar door jalte street lamp ko dekh raha tha (kabhi Sooraj ya laser ko seedha mat dekhna — sirf door ka halka street lamp!). Usne hairani se dekha ki daraar ke beech mein ek chaudi chamakdaar patti aur uske dono taraf patli-patli kaali aur halki chamakdaar lines dikh rahi thin!

Teacher ma'am ne bataya: "Yahan do slits nahi, sirf **ek hi patli slit** hai! Jab light kisi bahut chhote obstacle ya slit (jiska size light ki wavelength λ ke kareeb ho) ke kinaron se guzarti hai, to woh seedhi chalne ke bajay **kinaron par mud jaati hai** — isse **Diffraction of Light** kehte hain!"

---

## Naye Shabd (New Words)

- **Diffraction:** Kisi chhote obstacle ya narrow slit ke kinaron par light waves ka geometric shadow ke andar **mud jaana (bending)**.
- **Central Maximum:** Single-slit diffraction pattern ke bilkul beech mein banne wala sabse **chauda (twice as wide!)** aur **sabse chamakdaar** bright band.
- **Secondary Maxima:** Central maximum ke dono taraf banne wale chhote aur lagataar **dhundhle (decreasing intensity)** bright bands.

---

## 1. Single-Slit Diffraction (Minima aur Maxima Conditions)

Maan lo width **a** ki ek single narrow slit par wavelength **λ** ki plane light wave padti hai, aur doori **D** par screen rakhi hai. Slit ke alag-alag points se nikalne wale Huygens' secondary wavelets screen par interfere karte hain!

### Dhyaan Se Dekho (YDSE ka Bilkul Ulta Formula!):
1. **Condition for DARK Fringes (Minima — n = 1, 2, 3, ...):**
   Slit ko 2n barabar hisson mein baantne par har adjacent pair ka path difference λ/2 hokar cancel ho jaata hai:
   **a sin θ = n λ**   *(chhote angle θ ke liye: **θ_n = n λ / a**, aur screen par doori **y_n = n λ D / a**!)*
2. **Condition for Secondary BRIGHT Fringes (Maxima — n = 1, 2, 3, ...):**
   **a sin θ = (n + ½) λ = (2n + 1) λ / 2**
   *(Intensity bahut tezi se ghatti jaati hai — 1st secondary maximum central max ka sirf ~4.5% bright hota hai!)*

### Width of Central Maximum (Sabse Famous Formula!):
Central maximum dono taraf ke **pehle dark fringes** (**y₁ = +λD/a** aur **y₋₁ = −λD/a**) ke beech faila hota hai! Isliye:

- **Linear Width of Central Maximum:** **W_central = 2 y₁ = (2 λ D) / a**
- **Angular Width of Central Maximum:** **2 θ₁ = (2 λ) / a**  *(radians mein!)*

*(Jabki har secondary maximum ki width sirf **λ D / a** hoti hai — yaani **Central Maximum baaki maxima se DOUBLE chauda** hota hai!)*

---

## 2. Interference (YDSE) vs Diffraction (Single Slit) — 4 Pakke Farq!

| Feature | Interference (YDSE — 2 Slits) | Diffraction (Single Slit) |
|---|---|---|
| Cause | Do alag coherent slits ki waves ka superposition | **Ek hi slit** ke alag-alag points ke secondary wavelets ka superposition |
| Width of fringes | Sabhi fringes **barabar chaudai (β = λD/d)** ki hoti hain | **Central maximum double chauda (2λD/a)** hota hai; baaki λD/a |
| Intensity of bright fringes | Sabhi bright fringes **barabar chamakdaar (4I₀)** hoti hain | Central max sabse bright; secondary maxima **tezi se dhundhle** hote jaate hain |
| Dark fringe condition | d sin θ = (n − ½) λ | **a sin θ = n λ** |

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Single-Slit Central Maximum Width
Light of wavelength **λ = 500 nm** falls on a single slit of width **a = 0.50 mm**, forming a diffraction pattern on a screen **D = 2.0 m** away. Calculate:
(a) the distance of the **1st dark fringe** from the centre, and
(b) the total linear width of the **central maximum**.

**Hal (Solution):**
- λ = 5.0 × 10⁻⁷ m, a = 5.0 × 10⁻⁴ m, D = 2.0 m.
- (a) First minimum (n = 1): **y₁ = λ D / a** = (5.0 × 10⁻⁷ × 2.0) / (5.0 × 10⁻⁴) = **2.0 × 10⁻³ m = 2.0 mm**.
- (b) Central maximum width: **W_central = 2 y₁ = 2 × 2.0 = 4.0 mm**.
- **Jaanch:** 5/5 = 1; 2.0 × 10⁻³ = 2.0 mm; double = 4.0 mm ✅.

### Example 2: Angular Width of Central Maximum
A single slit of width **a = 0.20 mm** is illuminated by light of wavelength **λ = 600 nm**. Find the angular width of the central maximum in radians.

**Hal (Solution):**
- **Angular width = 2 λ / a** = (2 × 6.0 × 10⁻⁷) / (2.0 × 10⁻⁴) = (12 × 10⁻⁷) / (2.0 × 10⁻⁴) = **6.0 × 10⁻³ rad**.
- **Jaanch:** 2 × 6 / 2 = 6; 10⁻⁷ / 10⁻⁴ = 10⁻³ rad ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **Single-slit mein a sin θ = nλ ko BRIGHT fringe maan lena:**
   - *Galat:* YDSE ki tarah nλ par bright maximum bol dena!
   - *Sahi:* Single slit mein **a sin θ = nλ** (n = 1, 2, ...) **DARK (Minimum)** deta hai! (Sirf n = 0 par Central Maximum hota hai!)
2. **Central maximum ki width ko λD/a likh dena (2 bhool jaana!):**
   - *Galat:* W_central = λ D / a likhna!
   - *Sahi:* Central max −y₁ se +y₁ tak faila hota hai ⟹ **W_central = 2 λ D / a**!
3. **Diffraction ke sabhi bright fringes ko barabar chamakdaar bolna:**
   - *Galat:* YDSE ki tarah sabhi maxima equal brightness ke hote hain.
   - *Sahi:* Single-slit diffraction mein **intensity central max se door jaane par tezi se ghatti** hai!

---

## Quick Recap

| Quantity | Single-Slit Formula | Key Fact |
|---|---|---|
| Dark Fringes (Minima) | a sin θ = n λ; y_n = n λ D / a (n = 1, 2, ...) | Opposite of YDSE bright condition! |
| Secondary Maxima | a sin θ = (n + ½) λ | Intensity drops sharply as n increases |
| Central Maximum Width | Linear: **2 λ D / a**; Angular: **2 λ / a** | **Twice** as wide as any secondary maximum |

## Practice Questions

1. What is diffraction of light? What condition must the size of the slit/obstacle satisfy for diffraction to be clearly observable?
2. Write three key differences between an interference pattern (YDSE) and a single-slit diffraction pattern.
3. A parallel beam of light of wavelength 600 nm falls on a single slit of width 0.30 mm. Find the distance of the first minimum from the centre on a screen placed 1.5 m away.
4. What is the linear width of the central maximum in Question 3?
5. If the slit width **a** in a single-slit diffraction experiment is **doubled**, what happens to (a) the width of the central maximum, and (b) its brightness?
6. Light of wavelength 500 nm produces a diffraction pattern through a single slit where the first minimum is at 2.5 mm from the centre on a screen 1.0 m away. Calculate the width **a** of the slit.
7. Find the angular position (in radians) of the **2nd minimum** for a single slit of width 0.40 mm illuminated by light of wavelength 600 nm.
8. Why is diffraction of sound waves around a door easily noticed in daily life, whereas diffraction of light around a door is not?
9. State whether True or False: In single-slit diffraction, the condition a sin θ = 2λ gives the position of the second bright fringe.
10. If red light (λ = 700 nm) is replaced by violet light (λ = 400 nm) in a single-slit diffraction experiment, how does the width of the central maximum change?

### Answers

1. **Diffraction:** Kisi chhote obstacle ya slit ke kinaron par light ka geometric shadow mein mud jaana. Clear diffraction ke liye slit/obstacle ka size light ki **wavelength λ ke order (comparable)** ka hona chahiye!

2. (i) Interference mein sabhi fringes **equal width** ki hoti hain; diffraction mein **central max double wide** hota hai.
   (ii) Interference mein sabhi bright fringes **equal intensity** ki hoti hain; diffraction mein intensity tezi se **ghatti** hai.
   (iii) Interference do coherent sources se hota hai; diffraction **ek hi slit** ke secondary wavelets se hota hai.

3. y₁ = λ D / a = (6.0 × 10⁻⁷ × 1.5) / (3.0 × 10⁻⁴) = (9.0 × 10⁻⁷) / (3.0 × 10⁻⁴) = **3.0 × 10⁻³ m = 3.0 mm**.
   Jaanch: 6 × 1.5 / 3 = 3; 10⁻³ m = 3.0 mm ✅.

4. W_central = 2 y₁ = 2 × 3.0 = **6.0 mm**.
   Jaanch: 2 × 3.0 = 6.0 mm ✅.

5. (a) W_central = 2λD/a ⟹ slit width **a double** karne par central maximum ki width **aadhi (halved)** ho jaati hai!
   (b) Slit chaudi hone se zyada light aati hai aur chhote area mein focus hoti hai ⟹ **brightness badh (increases) jaati hai**!

6. y₁ = λ D / a ⟹ a = λ D / y₁ = (5.0 × 10⁻⁷ × 1.0) / (2.5 × 10⁻³) = **2.0 × 10⁻⁴ m = 0.20 mm**.
   Jaanch: 5.0 / 2.5 = 2.0; 10⁻⁷ / 10⁻³ = 10⁻⁴ m = 0.20 mm ✅.

7. 2nd minimum (n = 2): θ₂ = 2 λ / a = (2 × 6.0 × 10⁻⁷) / (4.0 × 10⁻⁴) = (12 × 10⁻⁷) / (4.0 × 10⁻⁴) = **3.0 × 10⁻³ rad**.
   Jaanch: 12 / 4 = 3; 10⁻³ rad ✅.

8. Sound waves ki wavelength (~0.5 m to 1 m) darwaze ke size ke comparable hoti hai, isliye sound aasaani se mud jaati hai; jabki light ki wavelength (~5 × 10⁻⁷ m) darwaze ke muqable lakhon guna chhoti hai, isliye light ka diffraction aam aankh se nahi dikhta!

9. **False ❌.** Single-slit diffraction mein a sin θ = 2λ **second DARK fringe (minimum)** ki position deta hai!

10. W_central = 2 λ D / a ⟹ W ∝ λ. Violet ki λ (400 nm) red (700 nm) se kam hai ⟹ central maximum **sikud kar patla (narrower — 4/7 guna)** ho jayega!
