## Talaab mein Kankar aur Phailti Gol Lehrein!

Park ke shant talaab mein **Praveen** ne ek chhota sa kankar daala. Turant paani ki satah par ek ke baad ek **gol-gol lehrein (concentric ripples)** bahar ki taraf failti gayin — aur har gol ring par paani ke sabhi particles ek hi samay par upar uth rahe the (same phase mein)!

Teacher ma'am ne bataya: "1678 mein Christiaan Huygens ne kaha tha ki light bhi bilkul aisi hi **tarangon (waves)** ke roop mein chalti hai! Ek hi phase mein vibrate karne wale sabhi points ko milane wali satah ko **Wavefront** kehte hain — aur isse reflection aur refraction ke dono niyam bina kisi ray assumption ke seedha prove ho jaate hain!"

---

## Naye Shabd (New Words)

- **Wavefront:** Kisi medium mein un sabhi points ka locus (satah) jo ek hi pal par **same phase** mein oscillate kar rahe hon.
- **Ray:** Energy ke chalne ki direction dikhane wali line — jo hamesha **Wavefront ke perpendicular (90°)** hoti hai!
- **Huygens' Principle:**
  1. Kisi wavefront ka **har ek point** naye disturbance (chhote **secondary wavelets**) ke source ki tarah kaam karta hai jo medium mein wave ki speed **v** se failte hain.
  2. Kisi baad ke samay t par un sabhi forward secondary wavelets ko chhoone wali common tangent surface (**forward envelope**) naya wavefront banati hai!

---

## 1. Wavefronts ke Teen Types

1. **Spherical Wavefront:** Chhote **point source** (jaise door ka bulb) se nikalne wala wavefront (gol spheres!).
2. **Cylindrical Wavefront:** **Linear source** (jaise lambi patli slit ya tubelight) se nikalne wala wavefront.
3. **Plane Wavefront:** Bahut **door (infinity)** par rakhe source (jaise Sooraj!) se aane wala wavefront — bade sphere ka chhota hissa lagbhag **flat plane** hota hai!

```
   Point Source:             Plane Wavefront (rays ⊥ wavefront):
       . - ~ - .                :    :    :
     '   . - .   '              :    :    :   ---> rays
    (   (  *  )   )             :    :    :   --->
     .   ' - '   .              :    :    :
       ' - _ - '              (parallel flat planes)
```

## 2. Huygens' Principle se Reflection aur Refraction ka Proof

### (a) Laws of Reflection (i = r):
- Ek **plane wavefront AB** reflecting mirror MN par angle of incidence **i** par girti hai.
- Jab point A mirror ko touch karta hai, tab point B mirror se doori **BC = v t** door hai.
- Jitne samay **t** mein B chal kar C par pahunchta hai (v t), utne hi samay mein A se nikla secondary wavelet radius **AD = v t** ka sphere bana leta hai!
- C se is sphere par tangent CD kheenchne par **reflected wavefront CD** milta hai!
- Right triangles **ΔABC** aur **ΔADC** mein: AC common, ∠B = ∠D = 90°, aur BC = AD = v t ⟹ dono triangles congruent!
- Isliye **∠i = ∠r** (Law of Reflection proved!)!

### (b) Laws of Refraction — Snell's Law:
- Ab maan lo plane wavefront AB medium 1 (speed **v₁**) se denser medium 2 (speed **v₂ < v₁**) ki boundary MN par angle **i** par girti hai:
- Samay **t** mein B medium 1 mein doori **BC = v₁ t** chal kar C par pahunchta hai, jabki A se nikla secondary wavelet medium 2 mein chhoti doori **AE = v₂ t** chalta hai!
- Right triangle ΔABC se: **sin i = BC / AC = (v₁ t) / AC**.
- Right triangle ΔAEC se: **sin r = AE / AC = (v₂ t) / AC**.
- Divide karo (AC aur t kat gaye!):

**sin i / sin r = v₁ / v₂ = n₂ / n₁**  *(Snell's Law proved!)*

### Refraction mein Frequency Constant, Wavelength Ghatti Hai!
Jab light rarer (n = 1) se denser medium (refractive index **n**) mein jaati hai:
- **Frequency (f):** Source ki property hai ⟹ **UNCHANGED**!
- **Speed:** **v = c / n** (ghat jaati hai — Newton ke corpuscular model ke bilkul ulta, aur experiments ne Huygens ko 100% sahi sabit kiya!).
- **Wavelength:** **λ_medium = λ_air / n** (ghat jaati hai!).

## Solved Examples

### Example 1: Light Entering Water from Air
Monochromatic light of wavelength **λ = 600 nm** in air enters water of refractive index **n = 4/3**. Calculate its (a) frequency in air and in water, (b) speed in water, and (c) wavelength in water.

**Hal (Solution):**
- c = 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s, λ_air = 600 × 10⁻⁹ = 6.0 × 10⁻⁷ m, n = 4/3.
- (a) **f = c / λ_air** = (3.0 × 10⁸) / (6.0 × 10⁻⁷) = **5.0 × 10¹⁴ Hz** (water mein bhi bilkul **5.0 × 10¹⁴ Hz** hi rahegi!).
- (b) **v_water = c / n** = (3.0 × 10⁸) / (4/3) = (9/4) × 10⁸ = **2.25 × 10⁸ m/s**.
- (c) **λ_water = λ_air / n** = 600 / (4/3) = 600 × (3/4) = **450 nm**.
- **Jaanch:** f × λ_water = (5.0 × 10¹⁴) × (4.5 × 10⁻⁷) = 2.25 × 10⁸ m/s = v_water ✅.

### Example 2: Wavefront Shape After a Convex Lens
What is the shape of the emergent wavefront when:
(a) a plane wavefront passes through a thin convex lens?
(b) a spherical wavefront originates from a point source placed at the focus of a convex lens?

**Hal (Solution):**
- (a) Convex lens parallel rays ko focus par converge karta hai ⟹ emergent wavefront ek **converging spherical wavefront** (focus ki taraf sikudta hua) ban jaata hai!
- (b) Focus se nikli rays lens ke baad parallel ho jaati hain ⟹ emergent wavefront ek **plane wavefront** ban jaata hai!
- **Jaanch:** Rays hamesha wavefront ke 90° par hoti hain ⟹ parallel rays = plane wavefront, converging rays = converging spherical wavefront ✅.

## Aam Galtiyan (Common Mistakes)

1. **Refraction mein light ki frequency badal dena:**
   - *Galat:* Water mein speed kam hui to frequency bhi kam ho gayi!
   - *Sahi:* **Frequency f kabhi nahi badalti** (yeh source par depend karti hai)! Sirf **speed v** aur **wavelength λ** 1/n guna ghat jaati hain!
2. **Wavefront aur Ray ko parallel maan lena:**
   - *Galat:* Ray wavefront ke along chalti hai.
   - *Sahi:* **Ray hamesha Wavefront ke perpendicular (90°)** hoti hai!
3. **Huygens ki theory ko denser medium mein zyada speed se jodna:**
   - *Galat:* Huygens ne kaha tha ki glass mein light tez chalti hai.
   - *Sahi:* Woh galti Newton ki corpuscular theory ki thi! **Huygens' wave theory** ne sahi predict kiya ki denser medium mein **v₂ < v₁** hota hai!

## Quick Recap

| Concept | Key Fact / Formula |
|---|---|
| Wavefront & Ray | Constant-phase surface; Rays are ALWAYS **⊥** to wavefronts |
| Shapes | Point source → Spherical; Line source → Cylindrical; Distant source → Plane |
| Huygens' Principle | Every point on wavefront emits secondary wavelets; forward envelope = new wavefront |
| Refraction by Wavefronts | sin i / sin r = v₁ / v₂ = n₂ / n₁; **f constant**, v = c/n, λ' = λ/n |

## Practice Questions

1. Define a wavefront. What is the angle between a light ray and its corresponding wavefront?
2. State the two postulates of Huygens' principle.
3. What is the shape of the wavefront emerging from (a) a small point bulb, (b) a very distant star, and (c) a plane wavefront passing through a glass prism?
4. Monochromatic light of wavelength 589 nm in air is incident on a water surface (n = 1.33). Find the wavelength, frequency, and speed of the (a) reflected light, and (b) refracted light.
5. Why does the frequency of light remain unchanged when it travels from air into glass?
6. Light of wavelength 500 nm in vacuum enters a glass slab of refractive index 1.5. Calculate its speed and wavelength inside the glass.
7. A plane wavefront is incident on a concave mirror. What is the shape of the reflected wavefront?
8. In Huygens' wave theory, how is the refractive index n of a medium related to the speed of light c in vacuum and v in the medium?
9. State whether True or False: According to Huygens' wave theory, light travels faster in glass than in air.
10. If the speed of light in a liquid is 2.0 × 10⁸ m/s, find (a) its refractive index, and (b) the wavelength in that liquid of light whose wavelength in air is 630 nm.

### Answers

1. **Wavefront:** Medium mein ek hi phase mein oscillate karne wale sabhi points ki satah. Light ray aur wavefront ke beech ka angle hamesha **90° (perpendicular)** hota hai.

2. (i) Wavefront ka har point naye **secondary wavelets** ka source banta hai jo medium mein speed v se failte hain; (ii) Un sabhi forward wavelets ka common tangent (**forward envelope**) naya wavefront deta hai.

3. (a) **Spherical wavefront**, (b) **Plane wavefront**, (c) **Tilted plane wavefront** (prism ke base ki taraf jhuka hua!).

4. (a) **Reflected light** usi air medium mein rehti hai: λ = **589 nm**, v = **3.0 × 10⁸ m/s**, f = (3.0 × 10⁸)/(5.89 × 10⁻⁷) ≈ **5.09 × 10¹⁴ Hz**.
   (b) **Refracted light** (water, n = 1.33): f = **5.09 × 10¹⁴ Hz** (unchanged!); v = 3.0 × 10⁸ / 1.33 ≈ **2.26 × 10⁸ m/s**; λ = 589 / 1.33 ≈ **443 nm**.
   Jaanch: 5.09 × 10¹⁴ × 4.43 × 10⁻⁷ ≈ 2.255 × 10⁸ m/s ✅.

5. Frequency light ke **source** (atoms ke oscillation) se tay hoti hai; boundary par har second jitne wave crests enter karte hain utne hi aage badhte hain, isliye frequency kabhi nahi badalti!

6. v = c / n = (3.0 × 10⁸) / 1.5 = **2.0 × 10⁸ m/s**.
   λ_glass = 500 / 1.5 ≈ **333.3 nm**.
   Jaanch: 500 / 1.5 = 1000/3 ≈ 333.3 nm ✅.

7. Concave mirror parallel rays ko focus par milata hai ⟹ reflected wavefront ek **converging spherical wavefront** hoga!

8. **n = c / v** (aur Snell's law: sin i / sin r = v₁ / v₂ = n₂ / n₁).

9. **False ❌.** Huygens' wave theory ke anusaar light denser medium (glass) mein **dheemi (slower)** chalti hai (v_glass < c)!

10. (a) n = c / v = (3.0 × 10⁸) / (2.0 × 10⁸) = **1.5**.
    (b) λ_liq = λ_air / n = 630 / 1.5 = **420 nm**.
    Jaanch: 420 × 1.5 = 630 nm ✅.
