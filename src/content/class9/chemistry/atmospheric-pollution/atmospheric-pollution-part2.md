# Topic 2: Acid Rain

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki hawa mein SO₂, NO, CO aur soot jaise **primary pollutants** seedhe nikalte hain, aur kuch **secondary pollutants** hawa ke andar hi naye bante hain. Ek secondary pollutant ka zikr hua tha: **baarish ka khatta ho jaana**. Aaj usi ki poori kahani: gas hawa mein kaise khatti hoti hai, wo baarish ke saath neeche kaise aati hai, aur patthar ki imaarat, jheel aur mitti par kya karti hai. Aakhir mein ek chhoti activity aur ek mass ka hisaab.

---

## Agra ka peela-sa safed patthar

Mr. Walawalkar apni class ko Agra ki trip par le gaye. Subah-subah Taj Mahal ke saamne khade hokar sab chup ho gaye. Safed sangmarmar (marble) dhoop mein chamak raha tha. Hardik ne ek minar ke paas jaakar dhyan se dekha. "Sir, door se ye doodh jaisa safed lagta hai, par paas se kuch jagah **peela aur dhundhla** hai. Aur ye naqqashi ke kinare thode ghise hue lagte hain. Itna majboot patthar aur kharab kaise ho gaya?"

Mr. Walawalkar muskuraye. "Bahut accha dekha, Hardik. Marble waqai bahut majboot hai, par ek cheez uska dushman hai: **khatti baarish**. Aur ye khatti baarish aasman se ek ghol ki tarah aati hai, jo chup-chaap patthar ko dheere-dheere ghulati hai."

"Baarish khatti? Baarish to saaf paani hai na?" Hardik ne poocha.

"Yehi to aaj ka sawaal hai. Sabse pehle hum dekhenge ki baarish mein khattapan aata kahan se hai. Phir dekhenge ki marble par uska kya asar hota hai, aur hum ise rokne ke liye kya kar sakte hain. Ghar jaakar ek chhoti si activity bhi karenge, jisme koi aag ya khatra nahi."

Ye bhi sach hai ki Taj Mahal ke aas-paas factories aur dhuein wali gaadiyon par kai saal se rok lagayi gayi hai, taaki imaarat bachi rahe. Aaj hum is samasya ki chemistry samjhenge.

## Part A: Baarish normal bhi thodi khatti hoti hai

Pehli surprise: **bilkul saaf, saaf hawa mein girne wali baarish bhi thodi khatti hoti hai.** Kyun?

Hawa mein lagbhag 0.04% (lagbhag 420 ppm) **carbon dioxide** hai (Topic 1). Baarish ki boondein girte waqt is CO₂ ka thoda hissa ghol leti hain, aur bahut halka acid, **carbonic acid**, ban jaata hai:

CO₂ + H₂O → H₂CO₃

Atom count: baayein 1 C, 2 O + 2 H, 1 O = 1 C, 2 H, 3 O; daayein 2 H, 1 C, 3 O ✅.

Isliye normal baarish ka khattapan lagbhag **5.6** (pH) hota hai. Ye hamare liye khatarnak nahi hai: ye prakriti ka hissa hai. Aur ye aashcharya bhi hai ki hamari soda ki bottle mein bhi yehi halka carbonic acid hota hai.

**Acid rain** wo baarish hai jo is 5.6 se kaafi zyada khatti ho, aam taur par lagbhag **4 se 4.5** ke aas-paas (lagbhag, jagah aur samay ke hisaab se badalta hai). Is zyada khattepan ke peeche do tez acid hain: **sulphuric acid** aur **nitric acid**.

## Part B: Sulphur wali gas, SO₂, acid kaise banti hai

Koyla aur kuch petroleum fuel mein **sulphur (S)** ki thodi matra hoti hai. Jab ye fuel jalta hai (thermal power plant, factory, bhatti), sulphur oxygen ke saath jal kar **sulphur dioxide** banata hai (Topic 1 mein ye primary pollutant tha):

S + O₂ → SO₂

Atom count: 1 S, 2 O dono taraf ✅.

Ab ye SO₂ hawa mein udta hai. Hawa mein oxygen aur dhoop ki madad se (aur hawa mein maujood chhote kano ki sataha par) ye **dheere-dheere** ek aur oxide **SO₃** mein badalta hai:

2SO₂ + O₂ → 2SO₃

Atom count: baayein 2 S, 4 O + 2 O = 6 O; daayein 2 S, 6 O ✅.

Phir SO₃ baadal ya baarish ki boondon ke paani se turant mil kar **sulphuric acid** banata hai:

SO₃ + H₂O → H₂SO₄

Atom count: baayein 1 S, 3 O + 1 O = 4 O, 2 H; daayein 2 H, 1 S, 4 O ✅.

Dhyan do: **SO₂ primary pollutant** hai (seedhe nikli), aur **H₂SO₄ secondary pollutant** hai (hawa ke andar bana). Ye wahi fark hai jo Topic 1 mein dekha tha.

## Part C: Nitrogen wali gas, NOx, acid kaise banti hai

Hawa ka 78% nitrogen (N₂) hai, par wo bahut kam reactive hai (Chapter 7 mein chips ke packet mein nitrogen yaad karo). Normal temperature par wo oxygen ke saath nahi judti. Par **bahut garmi** par judti hai: **bijli ki kadak** ke aas-paas aur **gaadi ke engine** ke andar, jahan temperature bahut zyada hota hai:

N₂ + O₂ → 2NO

Atom count: 2 N, 2 O dono taraf ✅.

Bijli ki kadak ye prakritik tareeke se karti hai, aur gaadiyan aur power plant insaan ke tareeke se. Ab NO hawa mein aate hi oxygen se aur judta hai:

2NO + O₂ → 2NO₂

Atom count: baayein 2 N, 2 O + 2 O = 4 O; daayein 2 N, 4 O ✅.

Aur NO₂ baarish ke paani aur oxygen ke saath **nitric acid** banata hai:

4NO₂ + O₂ + 2H₂O → 4HNO₃

Atom count: baayein 4 N, 8 + 2 + 2 = 12 O, 4 H; daayein 4 H, 4 N, 12 O ✅.

```
   FUEL JALNA / ENGINE / BIJLI                  HAWA MEIN BADLAAV                BAARISH MEIN

   Koyla, petrol mein sulphur (S)
        :  jalne par
        v
   SO₂ (sulphur dioxide)  ----- O₂ -----> SO₃ ----- H₂O -----> H₂SO₄ (sulphuric acid)
                                                                      \
                                                                       \   baadal ki boondon
                                                                        >  mein ghulkar
   Engine ya bijli: bahut garmi                                        /   ACID RAIN
        :  N₂ + O₂                                                    /
        v                                                            /
   NO ----- O₂ -----> NO₂ ----- H₂O aur O₂ -----> HNO₃ (nitric acid)
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Acid rain kaise banti hai: sulphur wale fuel se SO₂ banti hai, jo hawa mein SO₃ aur phir paani ke saath H₂SO₄ banati hai. Garam engine aur bijli ki kadak se N₂ aur O₂ milkar NO, phir NO₂ aur HNO₃ banate hain. Dono acid baarish ki boondon mein ghul kar neeche aate hain.

Ek chhoti baat: ye acid sirf baarish ke roop mein nahi girte. **Kohra, barf, aur sukhi dhool aur gas** ke roop mein bhi girte hain. Isliye ise kabhi-kabhi **acid deposition** kehte hain. Aur ye gas hawa ke saath **kai sau kilometre** door tak jaa sakti hain: jahan factory hai, wahan se door ke jungle ya jheel par bhi acid rain ho sakti hai.

> **Jaanch:** Ek bachhe ne kaha: "Acid rain sirf factory ke paas hi girti hai." ❌ Nahi. Gas hawa mein kaafi door tak jaa sakti hain, isliye acid rain factory se door bhi ho sakti hai.

## Part D: Normal baarish aur acid rain: number line par

Khattepan ke number (pH) ko aage Class 10 mein vistaar se padhoge. Abhi sirf itna: **number jitna chhota, utni zyada khatti.** Pure paani ka number 7 hota hai. (Is number ka poora scale Class 10 mein.)

   3         4         5         6         7
             [====]          ^             ^
             acid rain       :             pure paani: 7
             (lagbhag        :
             4 se 4.5)       normal baarish: lagbhag 5.6
                             (CO₂ ki wajah se halki khatti)

   Rule: 5.6 se KAM  --->  baarish "khatti baarish" (acid rain) kehlati hai

Ye ek dilchasp baat hai: normal baarish ko bhi "thodi khatti" kehna kuch ajeeb lagta hai, par yehi baat acid rain ko samajhne ka sahi paimana hai.

## Part E: Marble par acid rain ka asar

Marble aur **limestone** (chuna patthar) dono ka mukhya hissa hai **calcium carbonate, CaCO₃** (Chapter 7 mein CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂ wala decomposition yaad karo). CaCO₃ khatte padarth se reaction karta hai. Sulphuric acid ke saath:

CaCO₃ + H₂SO₄ → CaSO₄ + H₂O + CO₂

Atom count: baayein 1 Ca, 1 C, 3 O + 2 H, 1 S, 4 O = 7 O; daayein 1 Ca, 1 S, 4 O + 2 H, 1 O + 1 C, 2 O = 7 O ✅.

Yaani patthar ka CaCO₃ ek **naye namak, CaSO₄ (calcium sulphate)**, mein badal jaata hai, aur CO₂ gas bahar uth jaati hai. CaSO₄ marble jaisa majboot nahi hota: wo thoda sa paani mein ghul jaata hai aur baarish ke saath beh jaata hai, ya dhool ki tarah jhad jaata hai. Isliye marble ki upri sataha **dhundhli, kharaab aur ghisi hui** ho jaati hai. (Ye reaction Chapter 7 ke double displacement jaisa lagta hai: ek acid aur ek carbonate, jisme CO₂ gas nikalti hai.) Nitric acid se bhi aisa hi hota hai:

CaCO₃ + 2HNO₃ → Ca(NO₃)₂ + H₂O + CO₂

Atom count: baayein 1 Ca, 1 C, 3 O, 2 H, 2 N, 6 O = 9 O; daayein 1 Ca, 2 N, 6 O, 2 H, 1 O, 1 C, 2 O = 9 O ✅.

```
   PEHLE: naya marble                       BAAD MEIN: kai saal ki acid rain ke baad

      ___________                               ___________
     /           \                             /  .  _  .  \
    I   ( o  o )  I    patthar ki             I   ( .  - )  I    sataha khurdari,
    I      ^      I    murti ya               I    . ^ .    I    naqqashi dhundhli,
    I    \___/    I    minar                  I  . \_~_/ .  I    chehra ghisa hua
    I_____________I                           I_____________I    thoda peela-dhundhla
      CaCO₃ ki                                  upri parat CaSO₄ mein
      chamakdar sataha                          badal kar beh gayi
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Marble ki murti: naye marble ki sataha chamakdar aur naqqashi saaf (CaCO₃). Kai saal ki acid rain ke baad upri parat CaSO₄ mein badalkar beh jaati hai, sataha khurdari aur dhundhli ho jaati hai.

Taj Mahal ka peela-sa rang sirf acid rain ki wajah se nahi hota. Dhool, soot (kaala kan) aur dhuein ke kan bhi patthar ki sataha par chipakte hain (Topic 1 ka particulate matter). Dono milkar asar daalte hain. Isiliye Taj Mahal ke aas-paas dhuein wali gaadiyon par rok lagayi gayi, aur uski safai ke liye khas mitti ka lep (multani-mitti jaisa) bhi use hua, jo dhool aur grime ko kheench leta hai.

### Jaanch ke liye ek soch

Marble par acid rain ka asar **dheera** hota hai: saalon mein. Ek baarish se kuch nahi dikhta. Par saalon ki baarish, har saal, **jodkar** bada fark daalti hai. Ye wahi baat hai jo zang ke saath thi (Chapter 7): dheere-dheere ho kar bhi saalon mein bahut nuksan.

## Part F: Dusre asar

**1. Jheel aur nadiyaan.** Acid rain jheel mein girti hai, to paani ka khattapan badhta hai. Machhli ke ande aur chhote jeev (jo food chain ki shuruaat hain) kam khatte paani mein hi jee paate hain. Zyada khatte paani mein machhliyaan aur doosre jalchar mar sakte hain.

**2. Mitti.** Acid mitti ke zaroori poshak tatvon (jaise calcium, magnesium aur potassium ke salt) ko ghol kar baarish ke paani ke saath neeche baha le jaata hai, jisse paudhon ko kam poshan milta hai. Saath hi mitti mein kuch aise tatva ghulne lagte hain jo paudhon ke liye theek nahi.

**3. Jungle.** Patte kamzor aur peele ho jaate hain, aur ped dheere-dheere kamzor hote hain.

**4. Imaarat aur metal.** Patthar ke alawa **loha** bhi acid se tez zang khata hai. Zinc ki chaadar, pul, railing, gaadi ka paint aur bijli ke khambhe bhi kharab hote hain. Kuch metals acid ke saath hydrogen gas bhi dete hain:

Fe + H₂SO₄ → FeSO₄ + H₂

Atom count: 1 Fe, 2 H, 1 S, 4 O dono taraf ✅. (Yahan jo H₂ gas nikli, wahi Chapter 11 wali hydrogen hai.)

**5. Sehat.** Acid rain ki boondein seedhe haath par girne se insaan ko koi bada khatra nahi hota. Par SO₂ aur NOx ki gas saans ke zariye phephdon ko takleef deti hain, aur ye dono gas chhote kano (PM2.5) ban kar hawa ko aur kharab karti hain (aage Topic 5 mein).

## Part G: Safe activity: marble ka tukda sirka mein (ghar par adult ke saath)

Kya hum acid rain ka chhota model ghar par dekh sakte hain? Haan, par ye **sirf ek model** hai: sirka (vinegar) acid rain se kai guna zyada khatta hai, par reaction ka tarika bilkul wahi hai: **khatta padarth + CaCO₃ = CO₂ ke bulbule**.

**Zaroorat:** do saaf kaanch ke glass, thoda sirka, ek chhota marble chip ya chaak (chalk) ka tukda, thoda saada paani, ek tray ya plate. **Ghar par adult ke saath karo.**

**Steps:**
1. Do glass lo: Glass A mein thoda **saada paani**, Glass B mein utna hi **sirka**.
2. Dono mein ek-ek chhota marble chip (ya chaak ka tukda) dheere se daalo. Chehra glass se kuch door rakho.
3. 5 se 10 minute dhyan se dekho aur likho.

**Kya dikhega:** Glass A mein kuch nahi (ya bahut kam). Glass B mein **bulbule uthte hain**: ye CO₂ gas hai. Chaak ya marble ka tukda dheere-dheere chhota hota jaata hai.

Reaction (sirka ka acid, acetic acid, CH₃COOH):

CaCO₃ + 2CH₃COOH → Ca(CH₃COO)₂ + H₂O + CO₂

Atom count: baayein CaCO₃ (1 Ca, 1 C, 3 O) + 2 × C₂H₄O₂ (4 C, 8 H, 4 O) = 1 Ca, 5 C, 8 H, 7 O; daayein Ca(C₂H₃O₂)₂ (1 Ca, 4 C, 6 H, 4 O) + H₂O (2 H, 1 O) + CO₂ (1 C, 2 O) = 1 Ca, 5 C, 8 H, 7 O ✅.

**Safety:**
- **Kuch bhi chakhna nahi hai.** Sirka aur chaak khane ki cheezein nahi hain is activity mein; glass ke andar ka ghol kabhi mat piyo.
- Sirka aankh mein na jaaye. Kaam ke baad haath dho lo.
- Ye ghol aakhir mein sink mein bahaya ja sakta hai (adult ke saath).
- Is activity mein koi aag, garmi ya band dibba nahi hai.

**Jaanch:** Agar Glass A (saada paani) mein bulbule nahi aaye, to ye ek **control** hai: ye batata hai ki bulbule paani se nahi, **acid** se aaye. ✅

## Part H: Mass ka hisaab (Chapter 4 ke masses ke saath)

Chapter 4 ke molar masses: Ca = 40, C = 12, O = 16, H = 1, S = 32 (aur N = 14). To:
CaCO₃ = 40 + 12 + 48 = **100 g/mol**; H₂SO₄ = 2 + 32 + 64 = **98 g/mol**; CaSO₄ = 40 + 32 + 64 = **136 g/mol**; CO₂ = **44 g/mol**; H₂O = **18 g/mol**; SO₂ = 32 + 32 = **64 g/mol**.

**Solved Example 1: acid se kitna marble gala?**
Mana ki marble par **4.9 g H₂SO₄** girta hai. Kitna CaCO₃ ghulega, aur kitna CaSO₄ aur CO₂ banega?

Equation: CaCO₃ + H₂SO₄ → CaSO₄ + H₂O + CO₂ (sab ka ratio 1 : 1 : 1 : 1 : 1).

- H₂SO₄ ke moles = 4.9 ÷ 98 = **0.05 mol**.
- Ratio 1 : 1 hai, to CaCO₃ bhi **0.05 mol** = 0.05 × 100 = **5 g**.
- CaSO₄ = 0.05 × 136 = **6.8 g**; H₂O = 0.05 × 18 = **0.9 g**; CO₂ = 0.05 × 44 = **2.2 g**.

**Jaanch (mass conserved):** pehle: 4.9 + 5 = **9.9 g**. Baad mein: 6.8 + 0.9 + 2.2 = **9.9 g** ✅.

**Solved Example 2: koyle ke sulphur se kitna sulphuric acid?**
Kisi power plant mein koyle ke jalne se **10 kg sulphur** SO₂ banta hai. Kitna SO₂ banega, aur agar wo poora H₂SO₄ ban jaaye to kitna?

Chain: S → SO₂ → SO₃ → H₂SO₄. Har step mein 1 S ka 1 H₂SO₄ banta hai.
- S ke moles = 10 kg ÷ 32 g/mol = 10000 ÷ 32 = **312.5 mol**.
- SO₂ = 312.5 × 64 = 20000 g = **20 kg** (sulphur ka bhaar sirf do guna: oxygen ki wajah se).
- H₂SO₄ = 312.5 × 98 = 30625 g ≈ **30.6 kg** (lagbhag).

Ye dikhata hai ki thode se sulphur se bhi kitna zyada acid ban sakta hai.

**Solved Example 3: scrubber mein chuna patthar**
Power plant ki chimney se **640 kg SO₂** nikalni hai. Scrubber mein limestone (CaCO₃) aur oxygen ke saath:

2CaCO₃ + 2SO₂ + O₂ → 2CaSO₄ + 2CO₂

Atom count: baayein 2 Ca, 2 C, 6 O + 2 S, 4 O + 2 O = 2 Ca, 2 C, 2 S, 12 O; daayein 2 Ca, 2 S, 8 O + 2 C, 4 O = 2 Ca, 2 S, 2 C, 12 O ✅.

- SO₂ ke moles = 640 ÷ 64 = **10 kmol** (1 kmol = 1000 mol).
- CaCO₃ ratio 2 : 2 = 1 : 1, to 10 kmol × 100 = **1000 kg CaCO₃** (1 tonne).
- CaSO₄ = 10 × 136 = **1360 kg**. (Ye CaSO₄, yaani gypsum, aage plaster aur cement mein kaam aa sakta hai.)

> **Jaanch:** Mass check: 1000 + 640 + 160 (O₂: 5 kmol × 32) = 1800 kg. Baad mein: 1360 + 440 (CO₂: 10 × 44) = 1800 kg ✅.

## Part I: Acid rain ko kaise rokein?

Hamari saari strategy ek hi baat par tiki hai: **SO₂ aur NOx hawa mein jaane se pehle hi kam karo.**

| Tareeka | Kaise kaam karta hai | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Fuel mein sulphur kam karna** | Sulphur kam, to SO₂ kam | Kam-sulphur diesel aur petrol (BS-VI fuel, aage Topic 6 mein) |
| **Scrubber** | Chimney ki gas ko chuna ya limestone se milakar SO₂ ko CaSO₄ jaise thos mein badalna | Thermal power plant ka flue-gas desulphurisation |
| **Catalytic converter** | Gaadi ke exhaust mein platinum jaise metals CO aur NO jaisi gas ko CO₂ aur N₂ mein badalte hain | Petrol car: 2CO + O₂ → 2CO₂ aur 2NO + 2CO → N₂ + 2CO₂ |
| **Saaf fuel** | Jalne par kam SO₂ aur NOx dene wala fuel | CNG, LPG; bijli (electric vehicle) |
| **Renewable energy** | Koyla jalna kam: solar, wind, hydro | Surya urja ke plant |
| **Jheel mein chuna (liming)** | Khatti jheel mein chuna daal kar uska khattapan kam karna | CaO + H₂SO₄ → CaSO₄ + H₂O |

Atom count jaanch: 2CO + O₂ → 2CO₂ (2 C, 4 O dono taraf ✅); 2NO + 2CO → N₂ + 2CO₂ (2 N, 4 O, 2 C: baayein 2 N, 2 O + 2 C, 2 O = 2 N, 4 O, 2 C; daayein 2 N, 2 C, 4 O ✅); CaO + H₂SO₄ → CaSO₄ + H₂O (1 Ca, 1 S, 5 O, 2 H dono taraf ✅).

Ye saari cheezein **hum aur sarkar milkar** karte hain. Ek student ke roop mein hum bijli bachakar, public transport use karke, aur gaadi ki time par service karwakar apna hissa daal sakte hain.

Acid rain ka ek hopeful sach: kai deshon mein SO₂ ke niyam (sulphur kam, scrubber) ke baad acid rain **kam hui hai**. Yaani ye samasya **suljhayi ja sakti hai**.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Normal baarish bilkul neutral (pH 7) hoti hai"**
Nahi. CO₂ ghulne se normal baarish bhi lagbhag 5.6 par thodi khatti hoti hai. Acid rain us se bhi zyada khatti hoti hai.

**Galti 2: "Acid rain ka matlab hai ki boond haath par girte hi jala degi"**
Nahi. Acid rain bahut halki hoti hai. Asar **dheere-dheere, saalon mein** imaarat, jheel aur ped-paudhon par dikhta hai.

**Galti 3: "Acid rain aur greenhouse effect (Topic 3) ek hi cheez hain"**
Nahi. Acid rain SO₂ aur NOx se khatte-pan ki samasya hai. Greenhouse effect ka sambandh CO₂ jaisi gas se garmi ke rukne se hai.

**Galti 4: "Sirf SO₂ se acid rain hoti hai"**
SO₂ (se H₂SO₄) aur NOx (se HNO₃) dono zimmedar hain.

**Galti 5: "N₂ aur O₂ hamesha milkar NO banati hain"**
Nahi. Normal temperature par nahi. Sirf bahut garmi (bijli ki kadak ya engine) par.

**Galti 6: "Marble ka patthar acid mein ghul kar gayab ho jaata hai, kuch nahi bachta"**
Marble ka CaCO₃ naye padarth CaSO₄ (ya Ca(NO₃)₂), paani aur CO₂ gas mein badalta hai. Kuch gayab nahi hota: sirf badalta hai (mass conserved).

## Quick Recap

- Normal baarish bhi **thodi khatti** (pH lagbhag 5.6) hoti hai, kyunki CO₂ ghulkar carbonic acid banta hai: CO₂ + H₂O → H₂CO₃.
- **Acid rain** wo baarish hai jo 5.6 se kaafi zyada khatti ho (lagbhag 4 se 4.5).
- **Sulphur ka raasta:** S + O₂ → SO₂; 2SO₂ + O₂ → 2SO₃; SO₃ + H₂O → H₂SO₄.
- **Nitrogen ka raasta:** bahut garmi (engine, bijli) par N₂ + O₂ → 2NO; 2NO + O₂ → 2NO₂; 4NO₂ + O₂ + 2H₂O → 4HNO₃.
- **Marble aur limestone (CaCO₃)** acid se ghulte hain: CaCO₃ + H₂SO₄ → CaSO₄ + H₂O + CO₂. Isliye monument dhundhle aur ghise hue ho jaate hain.
- Asar: monument, jheel aur machhli, mitti, jungle, loha aur imaarat; gas door tak jaa sakti hain.
- **Upay:** kam-sulphur fuel, scrubber, catalytic converter, CNG aur renewable energy, jheel mein chuna.
- **Activity:** marble chip ya chaak sirka mein (adult ke saath, kuch chakhna nahi): bulbule = CO₂.

## Practice Questions

1. Why is ordinary clean rain slightly acidic? Write the balanced equation and name the acid formed.

2. Write the three balanced equations that explain how burning sulphur-containing coal can finally lead to sulphuric acid in rain. Which of the substances is a primary pollutant and which a secondary pollutant?

3. Nitrogen is very unreactive, yet nitrogen oxides form in a car engine and during lightning. Explain why. Then write the equations leading to nitric acid.

4. A marble statue in a city is losing about 250 g of its CaCO₃ over many years due to sulphuric acid in the rain. Using the equation CaCO₃ + H₂SO₄ → CaSO₄ + H₂O + CO₂ and the masses Ca = 40, C = 12, O = 16, H = 1, S = 32, calculate how much H₂SO₄ was needed.

5. Rain with nitric acid falls on limestone. Using CaCO₃ + 2HNO₃ → Ca(NO₃)₂ + H₂O + CO₂ (N = 14), how much CaCO₃ is dissolved by 31.5 g of HNO₃? How much CO₂ is released?

6. A student drops 20 g of chalk (assume it is pure CaCO₃) into an excess of vinegar in an open container. Using CaCO₃ + 2CH₃COOH → Ca(CH₃COO)₂ + H₂O + CO₂, calculate the mass of CO₂ that escapes as bubbles. What will the student observe?

7. A small plant burns 400 kg of coal that has 3% sulphur by mass. How much SO₂ would be produced if all the sulphur burns? If all of that SO₂ turned into H₂SO₄, how much H₂SO₄ is that?

8. A scrubber uses limestone to remove 160 kg of SO₂ from the flue gas, with 2CaCO₃ + 2SO₂ + O₂ → 2CaSO₄ + 2CO₂. Calculate the mass of CaCO₃ required and of CaSO₄ produced. Check mass conservation.

9. Explain the marble-chip-in-vinegar activity: what do you add to the control glass, what is observed, which gas is formed, and why is the activity only a model of acid rain? List two safety points.

10. Two samples of rainwater are tested: sample P has a pH of 5.6 and sample Q has a pH of 4.2. Which one would you call acid rain? Which is more acidic? Why does sample P not count as acid rain even though it is not neutral?

11. Give two reasons why acid rain can fall far from the factory that caused it.

12. Explain how each of these helps to reduce acid rain: (a) a scrubber, (b) a catalytic converter, (c) switching a power plant to solar energy, (d) adding lime to an acidic lake.

13. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) Acid rain falls only as rain, never as snow or fog.
    (b) CaSO₄ is more soluble in water than marble and so washes away.
    (c) Lightning is a natural source of nitrogen oxides.
    (d) The mass of reactants is more than the mass of products when marble dissolves in acid.

### Answers

1. Hawa ka CO₂ baarish ki boondon mein ghulta hai: **CO₂ + H₂O → H₂CO₃.** Check: baayein 1 C, 2 H, 3 O; daayein 2 H, 1 C, 3 O ✅. Bana acid **carbonic acid** hai, jo halka hai, isliye normal baarish lagbhag 5.6 par thodi khatti hoti hai.

2. **S + O₂ → SO₂** (1 S, 2 O dono taraf ✅); **2SO₂ + O₂ → 2SO₃** (2 S, 6 O dono taraf ✅); **SO₃ + H₂O → H₂SO₄** (1 S, 4 O, 2 H dono taraf ✅). **SO₂ primary pollutant** hai (seedhe nikli), **H₂SO₄ secondary pollutant** hai (hawa mein bana). SO₃ beech ka padarth hai.

3. N₂ ke atoms aapas mein bahut mazbooti se jude hote hain, isliye normal temperature par wo oxygen se nahi judta. Engine ke andar aur bijli ki kadak par **bahut zyada temperature** hota hai, jo itni energy deta hai. **N₂ + O₂ → 2NO** (2 N, 2 O dono taraf ✅); **2NO + O₂ → 2NO₂** (2 N, 4 O dono taraf ✅); **4NO₂ + O₂ + 2H₂O → 4HNO₃** (4 N, 12 O, 4 H dono taraf ✅).

4. CaCO₃ = 100 g/mol, H₂SO₄ = 98 g/mol. 250 g ÷ 100 = **2.5 mol CaCO₃**. Ratio 1 : 1, to 2.5 mol H₂SO₄ = 2.5 × 98 = **245 g H₂SO₄**.

5. HNO₃ = 1 + 14 + 48 = **63 g/mol**. 31.5 ÷ 63 = **0.5 mol HNO₃**. Ratio 2 HNO₃ : 1 CaCO₃, to CaCO₃ = 0.25 mol = 0.25 × 100 = **25 g**. CO₂ bhi 0.25 mol = 0.25 × 44 = **11 g**. Jaanch: Ca(NO₃)₂ = 0.25 × 164 = 41 g; H₂O = 0.25 × 18 = 4.5 g. Pehle: 31.5 + 25 = 56.5 g. Baad mein: 41 + 4.5 + 11 = 56.5 g ✅.

6. 20 ÷ 100 = **0.2 mol CaCO₃**. Ratio 1 : 1 (CaCO₃ : CO₂), to CO₂ = 0.2 mol = 0.2 × 44 = **8.8 g**. Student ko **bulbule** uthte dikhenge (CO₂), aur chaak ka tukda dheere-dheere chhota hota jaayega. (Vinegar ke excess mein hone se saara chaak ghul jaata hai.)

7. Sulphur = 3% of 400 kg = 12 kg. Moles = 12000 ÷ 32 = **375 mol**. SO₂ = 375 × 64 = 24000 g = **24 kg**. H₂SO₄ (1 S se 1 H₂SO₄) = 375 × 98 = 36750 g = **36.75 kg** (lagbhag 36.8 kg).

8. SO₂ = 160 ÷ 64 = **2.5 kmol**. CaCO₃ : SO₂ = 2 : 2 = 1 : 1, to CaCO₃ = 2.5 kmol × 100 = **250 kg**; CaSO₄ = 2.5 × 136 = **340 kg**. O₂ chahiye: 1.25 kmol × 32 = 40 kg; CO₂ banega: 2.5 × 44 = 110 kg. Pehle: 250 + 160 + 40 = 450 kg. Baad mein: 340 + 110 = 450 kg ✅.

9. Control glass mein **saada paani** (aur ek hi jaisa marble chip) daalte hain. Sirka wale glass mein **bulbule** dikhte hain aur chip dheere-dheere chhota hota hai. Gas **CO₂** hai. Ye sirf ek model hai kyunki sirka acid rain se kai guna zyada khatta hai, par reaction ka tarika (acid + CaCO₃ → CO₂) wahi hai. Safety: kuch chakhna nahi; adult ke saath karo, aankh mein sirka na jaaye aur haath dho lo.

10. Sample **Q (4.2)** acid rain hai, kyunki wo 5.6 se kam hai. Q zyada khatta hai (chhota number = zyada khatta). Sample P (5.6) normal baarish hai: wo neutral (7) se thoda khatta hai (CO₂ ki wajah se), par ye prakritik hai, isliye acid rain nahi kehte.

11. (i) SO₂ aur NOx hawa mein kai sau kilometre door tak baha kar jaati hain. (ii) Gas dheere-dheere acid mein badalti hain, isliye jab tak wo acid ban kar girti hain, tab tak wo factory se door pahunch chuki hoti hain.

12. (a) Scrubber chimney ki SO₂ ko chuna ya limestone se CaSO₄ jaise thos mein badal kar hawa mein jaane se rokta hai. (b) Catalytic converter CO aur NO ko CO₂ aur N₂ mein badalta hai, to NOx kam nikalti hai. (c) Solar energy mein koyla nahi jalta, to SO₂ aur NOx banti hi nahi. (d) Chuna khatte paani ke acid ko neutralise karke jheel ka khattapan kam karta hai.

13. (a) **False** ❌. Acid kohra, barf aur sukhi dhool ke roop mein bhi girta hai. (b) **True** ✅. CaSO₄ marble ke CaCO₃ se kamzor hai aur paani ke saath beh sakta hai. (c) **True** ✅. Bijli ki kadak ke bahut garam hisse mein N₂ aur O₂ NO banate hain. (d) **False** ❌. Mass conserved hota hai: reactants aur products ka mass barabar hota hai (Example 1: 9.9 g dono taraf).
