# Topic 3: Greenhouse Effect aur Global Warming

Pichhle topic mein hum ne dekha ki kuch gaisein baarish ko khatta bana deti hain, yaani hawa mein jaane wali cheez zameen par wapas aati hai. Ab ek aur sawaal: kuch gaisein hawa mein rehkar **Earth ko garam** rakhti hain. Maze ki baat ye hai ki thodi si garmi to **zaroori** hai, uske bina hum jee hi nahi sakte. Dikkat tab shuru hoti hai jab ye "thodi" garmi **badhne** lage. Is topic mein hum **greenhouse effect** ko samjhenge, dekhenge ki ye achha kab hai aur kab dikkat, aur ek **ginti** karenge: ek litre petrol jalane par kitni CO₂ banti hai. ## Polyhouse mein sardiyon ke tamatar

December ka mahina tha. Sanika apne school ke science teacher Mr. Kasbekar ke saath ek nursery dekhne aayi thi. Bahar halki thandi hawa chal rahi thi, par nursery ke ek lambe, plastic ki chaadar se dhake dhaanche ke andar **laal-laal tamatar** latak rahe the.

"Sir, bahar to sardi hai, aur yahan tamatar ugg rahe hain!" Sanika ne hairaani se kaha.

Nursery ke maali ne muskurakar kaha: "Beti, ye **polyhouse** hai. Dhoop andar aa jaati hai, par andar ki garmi bahar nahi nikal paati. Isliye andar bahar se kuch degree zyada garam rehta hai."

Sanika ne haath andar badhaya: "Sach mein, yahan to garam hai! Par sir, ye plastic garmi paida to nahi karta?"

Mr. Kasbekar hanse. "Bahut achha sawaal. Plastic garmi **banata nahi**, wo bas use **rok kar rakhta hai**. Garmi to Suraj se aati hai. Ab socho, kya Earth ke saath bhi kuch aisa hota hoga? Kya hamari hawa bhi ek bahut badi polyhouse hai?"

Sanika ne copy kholi aur likha: **"Earth ke upar ki hawa ek kambal hai ya khidki?"**

## Part A: Garmi ka safar: Suraj se Earth tak aur wapas

Sabse pehle Earth ke **garam** hone ki kahani. Teen kadam hain.

1. **Suraj ki roshni andar aati hai.** Roshni (dikhne wali kirnein) hawa se aaram se guzar kar zameen aur samundar tak pahunchti hai. Zameen aur samundar ise le kar **garam** ho jaate hain.
2. **Garam Earth garmi bhejti hai.** Har garam cheez garmi nikalti hai. Earth bhi nikalti hai, par ye **infrared** kirnein hain (jaise garam tawe ke paas mehsoos hone wali garmi).
3. **Kuch gaisein is garmi ko rok leti hain.** Hawa ki bahut chhoti si hissedaari wali kuch gaisein (greenhouse gases) infrared ko **sokh** leti hain aur phir use **sab taraf** (kuch wapas neeche bhi) chhodti hain. Isse garmi ka ek hissa Earth ke paas hi ruka rehta hai.

```
        SURAJ KI ROSHNI (andar aati hai)
          \    \    \    \    \
           v    v    v    v    v
   ============================================
     HAWA  (greenhouse gases: CO₂, CH₄, H₂O ...)
   ============================================
           :                    ^   ^
           :   garmi (IR)       :   :  garmi ka kuch hissa
           :   bahar jaati      :   :  gaison se takra kar
           :   hai: kuch        :   :  WAPAS neeche aata hai
           :   seedhi nikal     :   :  (trapped heat)
           :   jaati hai        :   :
           v                    :   :
      ~~~ ~~~ ~~~ ~~~ ~~~ ~~~ ~~~ ~~~ ~~~ ~~~
         ZAMEEN AUR SAMUNDAR (garam hote hain)
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Greenhouse effect: Suraj ki roshni hawa se guzar kar zameen aur samundar ko garam karti hai; garam Earth infrared garmi bhejti hai, jiska kuch hissa seedha bahar jaata hai aur kuch hissa greenhouse gaison se takra kar wapas neeche aata hai. (Sketch simple hai: asli tasveer mein garmi sab dishaon mein jaati hai.)

Isse yaad rakhne ka asaan tareeka: roshni ko **andar aane ki aasaani** hai, par garmi ko **bahar jaane mein thodi rukavat** hai. Jaise sardi mein tum kambal odhte ho: kambal garmi paida nahi karta, wo tumhari apni garmi ko bahar jaane se **dheema** kar deta hai.

> **Jaanch:** "Greenhouse gaisein Earth mein garmi paida karti hain." ❌ Nahi. Garmi ka source Suraj hai. Gaisein bas Earth se bahar jaati garmi ka ek hissa rok leti hain.

## Part B: Natural greenhouse effect: zaroori kambal

Ab ek sawaal: agar hawa mein ye gaisein hoti hi nahi to?

Vaigyanik hisaab ke anusaar, tab Earth ka average temperature lagbhag **−18 °C** hota. (Ye approximate aankda hai.) Hakeekat mein lagbhag **15 °C** hai. Dono ka antar:

15 − (−18) = **33 °C**.

Yaani natural greenhouse effect Earth ko lagbhag **33 °C** zyada garam rakhta hai. Kelvin mein (Physics ka K = °C + 273): −18 °C = 255 K aur 15 °C = 288 K, aur 288 − 255 = 33 K ✅ (antar dono scales mein barabar hai).

−18 °C ka matlab hota: samundar lagbhag sab jam jaate, aur jeevan bahut alag hota. Isliye **natural greenhouse effect hamara dost hai**.

Ek aur example: **Chandrama**. Uski sathah par hawa nahi hai, isliye wahan din mein bahut tez garmi aur raat mein bahut tez sardi hoti hai. Hawa ka kambal na ho to raat mein garmi tezi se bahar chali jaati hai. (Ye bhi approximate, saral samajh hai.)

Ek ullta example: **Venus (Shukra)**. Wahan hawa bahut ghani hai aur lagbhag poori **CO₂** hai, isliye wahan sathah ka temperature lagbhag **460 °C** hai. Ye dikhata hai ki greenhouse gas ki **matra** bahut maayne rakhti hai. (Aankde lagbhag.)

Ab ye bhi samajh lo ki polyhouse sirf ek **upma** hai. Polyhouse mein plastic garam hawa ko **bahar jaane se rokta hai** (convection roka jaata hai, [Physics ka Heat and Temperature Topic 6](../../physics/heat-and-temperature/heat-and-temperature-part6.md)). Earth ki hawa mein, kuch gaisein garmi ki **infrared kirnein** sokhti hain. Dono ka **natija milta-julta** hai (garmi ruk-ruk kar nikalti hai), par tareeka alag hai. Isliye "greenhouse" ek naam hai, poora sach nahi.

## Part C: Polyhouse ka nakasha

Sanika ne maali se poochha ki andar ka temperature kyun alag hai. Maali ne bataya ki andar thoda garam hota hai aur **hawa ki aawajaahi** (vents) dhyaan se kholi aur band ki jaati hai, warna andar bahut garam ho kar paudhe jhulas jaate hain. Ye ek **balance** hai.

```
        Suraj ki roshni
          \    \    \
           v    v    v
       ___________________________
      /   plastic ki chhat        \
     /                             \
    /   andar: garam, hawa band     \
   :   tamatar ke paudhe  Y  Y  Y    :
   :   mitti                         :
   +---------------------------------+
        ^ garam hawa bahar nahi
        jaa paati (vent band)
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Polyhouse: plastic ki chhat dhoop ko andar aane deti hai, par andar ki garam hawa ko bahar jaane se rokti hai, isliye andar ka temperature bahar se zyada rehta hai. (Vents kholne par garam hawa nikal jaati hai aur temperature ghat jaata hai.)

Polyhouse ke faayde: sardiyon mein bhi sabzi ugana, kam paani aur kam keede. Yaani **greenhouse effect ka upyog** insaan ne kheti mein bhi kiya hai. Par jab pure Earth ki hawa mein ye kambal **moti** hone lage, tab dikkat hoti hai.

## Part D: Kaun si gaisein aur wo kahan se aati hain?

Pehle ye note kar lo: hawa mein inki matra bahut kam hoti hai, par **asar bada**.

| Greenhouse gas | Formula | Mukhya sources (lagbhag) |
|---|---|---|
| **Water vapour (bhaap)** | H₂O | Samundar, jheelein aur nadiyon se evaporation (water cycle, [water-part5.md](../water/water-part5.md)); garam hawa mein zyada hoti hai |
| **Carbon dioxide** | CO₂ | Coal, petrol, diesel, natural gas jalana; jungal kaatna; saans, sadna aur jwalamukhi bhi thodi |
| **Methane** | CH₄ | Dhaan ke khet (paddy), gaay-bhains jaise jaanwaron ki digestion, kachre ke dher (landfill), natural gas ka rissav |
| **Nitrous oxide** | N₂O | Khet mein khaad (fertiliser) ka upyog, kuch factories |
| **CFC** | jaise CCl₂F₂ | Purane fridge aur AC ke refrigerant (ab bahut kam; Topic 4) |

Ek dilchasp baat: **water vapour** natural greenhouse effect mein bahut bada hissa rakhti hai, par hum use seedha "badha" nahi sakte. Hawa jitni garam hoti hai, usme utni zyada bhaap rehti hai, isliye wo bas **garmi ka jawab dene** wali gas hai. Insaan ke kaaran jo **badlaav** aa raha hai, wo mukhya roop se CO₂, CH₄ aur N₂O ki badhti matra se hai.

**CO₂ kaise banti hai?** Yaad karo Chapter 7: koi bhi carbon wala eendhan poori tarah jalta hai to **CO₂ aur paani** banta hai. Udaharan:

- Charcoal ya coal (carbon): C + O₂ → CO₂
- CNG (methane): CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O
- Petrol: 2C₈H₁₈ + 25O₂ → 16CO₂ + 18H₂O

Dhyaan do: eendhan mein **carbon** hai, to jalne par **CO₂** banegi hi. Ye koi "galti" nahi, bas rasayan ka niyam hai ([Chapter 7, Topic 6](../chemical-changes-and-reactions/chemical-changes-and-reactions-part6.md)). Dikkat tab hai jab **bahut saare** log aur gaadiyan roz bahut saara eendhan jalayein.

> **Jaanch:** Teeno equations mein atoms ginte hain. Charcoal: C: 1 = 1; O: 2 = 2 ✅. Methane: C: 1 = 1; H: 4 = 4; O: 4 = 2 + 2 ✅. Petrol: C: 16 = 16; H: 36 = 36; O: 50 = 32 + 18 ✅.

## Part E: Zyada ho gaya: enhanced greenhouse effect

**Carbon cycle** ek saral baat se samjho. Paudhe photosynthesis mein CO₂ lete hain (6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂), aur jaanwar aur paudhe saans mein ise wapas karte hain. Hazaaron saal tak hawa mein CO₂ lagbhag **280 ppm** rahi.

Fir aaya **industry ka daur** (lagbhag 1800s se). Coal, petroleum aur gas bahut tezi se jalne lage, jungal kate gaye, aur CO₂ badhne lagi. Aaj wo lagbhag **420 ppm** hai. (Ye approximate aankde hain.)

```
   CO₂ (ppm), lagbhag aankde.  1 block = 20 ppm

   1800s se pehle  ##############        280
   1958 ke aas-paas ################      315
   2000 ke aas-paas ##################    370
   Aaj             #####################  420
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Hawa mein CO₂ ka lagbhag badhna: pehle ke samay mein lagbhag 280 ppm se aaj lagbhag 420 ppm tak (1 block = 20 ppm; 280 ppm = 14 blocks, 420 ppm = 21 blocks). Sab aankde approximate hain.

**Global warming** ka matlab: Earth ke average temperature ka dheere-dheere badhna. Vaigyanik measurements ke anusaar (lagbhag aankde):

- Earth ka average temperature 1800s se **1 °C se zyada** badha hai.
- Samundar ka star 1900 se lagbhag **20 cm** upar aaya hai.

20 cm kam lag sakta hai, par jo jagah samundar ke bilkul kinare hain wahan ye maayne rakhta hai. (Ye wo vishay hai jis par duniya ke bahut saare vaigyanik saath milkar kaam kar rahe hain.)

Ye bhi dhyaan do: "average" ka matlab ye nahi ki har jagah har din ek jaisi garmi badhi. Mausam din-ba-din badalta hai, par **lambi avadhi** ka average (climate) badal raha hai.

> **Jaanch:** "CO₂ 280 se 420 ppm hui hai, to wo 420 ppm hi saari hawa hai." ❌ Nahi. 420 ppm = 420 ÷ 1000000 × 100 = 0.042% hi hai: bahut chhota hissa, par asar bada.

## Part F: Asar kya ho sakte hain?

Jab Earth zyada garam hoti hai, to ye cheezein dekhi ja rahi hain (approximate aur lambe samay ke trends):

| Asar | Kya hota hai |
|---|---|
| **Glaciers aur barf ka pighalna** | Pahaadon ke glaciers aur dhruvon ki barf pighalti hai |
| **Samundar ka star badhna** | Garam pani thoda phailta hai (Physics: thermal expansion) aur barf ka pani samundar mein aata hai |
| **Garam hawayein (heat waves)** | Garmi ke din zyada aur zyada tez |
| **Barish ka badalna** | Kahin bahut tez barish aur baadh, kahin sookha |
| **Coral bleaching** | Samundar zyada garam hone par coral safed padkar bimaar ho jaate hain |
| **Kheti par asar** | Fasal ka samay aur paidavaar badal sakti hai |

Ek baat: kuch asar kaafi bade ho sakte hain, isliye vaigyanik kehte hain ki abhi **kadam uthana** sabse achha hai. Asar ko aur badhne se rokne mein har ek chhota badlaav madad karta hai.

## Part G: Ek litre petrol aur uski CO₂ (ginti)

Ab wo ginti jo Sanika ne baad mein maali ko karke dikhayi. Petrol mein kai chemicals hote hain, hum ise **octane, C₈H₁₈** maan lete hain (saral model). Petrol ki density lagbhag **0.74 kg per L** hai (approximate). Ek litre petrol poora jalane par kitni CO₂ banti hai?

**Balanced equation:** 2C₈H₁₈ + 25O₂ → 16CO₂ + 18H₂O

**Step 1: Octane ka molar mass.** C = 12, H = 1:
M(C₈H₁₈) = 8 × 12 + 18 × 1 = 96 + 18 = **114 g per mol**.

**Step 2: 1 L petrol ka mass.** 0.74 kg = **740 g**.

**Step 3: Octane ke moles.** n = m ÷ M = 740 ÷ 114 = **6.49 mol**.

**Step 4: Mole ratio.** Equation mein 2 mol octane se 16 mol CO₂. Yaani 1 mol octane se 8 mol CO₂.
CO₂ ke moles = 6.49 × 8 = **51.9 mol**.

**Step 5: CO₂ ka molar mass.** M(CO₂) = 12 + 2 × 16 = **44 g per mol**.

**Step 6: CO₂ ka mass.** 51.9 × 44 = **2284 g, yaani lagbhag 2.28 kg**.

Yaani **1 litre petrol jalane par lagbhag 2.3 kg CO₂ banti hai**. Ye petrol ke **apne mass (0.74 kg) se bhi lagbhag teen guna** zyada hai! Ye kaise? Kyunki hawa ki oxygen bhi saath judti hai: octane ke 228 g (2 mol) ke saath 800 g oxygen judti hai, aur nateeje mein 704 g CO₂ (aur 324 g paani) banta hai.

> **Jaanch:** 2 mol octane = 228 g aur 16 mol CO₂ = 16 × 44 = 704 g. 704 ÷ 228 = 3.09, aur 740 × 3.09 = 2285 g ✅ (wahi jawab). Mass bhi bacha: 228 g + 25 × 32 g (800 g) = 1028 g, aur 704 g + 18 × 18 g (324 g) = 1028 g ✅.

**Example: Ek gaadi ek saal mein.** Maan lo ek car saal bhar mein 1000 L petrol jalati hai (jaise 12,000 km par 12 km per L). Tab CO₂ = 1000 × 2.28 = **2280 kg, yaani lagbhag 2.3 tonne**. Ek ped saal mein lagbhag 22 kg CO₂ sokh leta hai (approximate aankda, ped ki jaati aur umar par nirbhar). To ped chahiye lagbhag 2280 ÷ 22 = **104 ped**, sirf ek car ke ek saal ke liye. Isse samjho ki ped lagana achha hai, par eendhan bachana bhi zaroori hai.

**Example: Paani bhi banta hai.** Wahi equation 18H₂O deti hai: 6.49 mol octane × 9 = 58.4 mol H₂O, jiska mass 58.4 × 18 = **1051 g, lagbhag 1.05 kg paani** (bhaap ke roop mein). Isliye sardi ki subah gaadi ke silencer se safed bhaap jaisa nikalta dikhta hai: wo paani ki bhaap hai jo thandi hawa mein chhoti boondon mein badal jaati hai.

## Part H: Greenhouse effect aur ozone hole ek cheez nahi hain

Ye ek **bahut aam** confusion hai, isliye saaf kar lete hain.

| Baat | Greenhouse effect / global warming | Ozone layer ka kam hona |
|---|---|---|
| **Mukhya samasya** | Earth ke paas garmi ka zyada rukna | Suraj ki khatarnak UV kirnein zyada neeche aana |
| **Zimmedaar gaisein** | CO₂, CH₄, N₂O (aur kuch CFCs bhi) | Mukhyatah CFC (chlorine wali gaisein) |
| **Kahan** | Poori hawa (troposphere) | Stratosphere ki ozone parat |
| **Nateeja** | Temperature badhna | Skin cancer, aankhon ki bimari ka khatra |

CFC dono mein aate hain, isliye confusion hota hai, par **problem alag hain**. Ozone layer ke baare mein hum agle topic mein poori tarah padhenge.

## Part I: Hum kya kar sakte hain?

Achhi khabar: har pariwar aur har student kuch kar sakta hai.

1. **Bijli bachao:** kamre se nikalte waqt light aur pankha band karna; LED bulb; 5-star rated appliance. (Bijli ka bada hissa coal se banta hai, to bijli bachana CO₂ bachana hai.)
2. **Paidal, cycle, bus, train ya car-pool:** akeli car ki jagah. Ek bus se bahut log ek saath chalte hain.
3. **Ped lagao aur bachao:** ped CO₂ lete hain.
4. **Renewable energy:** solar, wind aur hydro bijli, jo jalane se CO₂ nahi banti. Solar heater se paani garam karna.
5. **Kachra kam karo:** reduce, reuse, recycle. Khana barbaad na karna (landfill mein sadne se methane banti hai).
6. **Samajhdaari se khana aur kheti:** zaroorat se zyada khaad na daalna.
7. **Dusron ko samjhao.**

Ek chhoti si safe activity.

**Activity: Mini "greenhouse" (ghar par adult ke saath)**
Do ek jaisi paani ki chhoti bottle lo. Dono mein barabar paani bharo (thermometer ho to usse naapo). Ek bottle ko saaf, transparent plastic ki thaili mein rakho aur muh baandh do; doosri khuli rakho. Dono ko **dhoop mein, aaram se** 30 minute ke liye rakho. Phir dono ka temperature naapo ya haath se mehsoos karo. Thaili wali bottle zyada garam hone ki ummeed hai. (Kuch nahi jalana, kuch nahi chakhna; sirf dhoop. Thaili baccho se door rakho aur adult ke saath hi karo.) Ye **model** hai: polyhouse jaisa; asli aasmaan mein kaam alag tareeke se hota hai (Part B).

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Greenhouse effect poori tarah bura hai"**
Natural greenhouse effect ke bina Earth lagbhag −18 °C hoti. Dikkat **extra** greenhouse effect hai.

**Galti 2: "Greenhouse gaisein garmi paida karti hain"**
Garmi Suraj se aati hai; gaisein bas bahar jaati garmi ka ek hissa rokti hain.

**Galti 3: "Greenhouse effect aur ozone hole ek hi cheez hain"**
Nahi. Pehla garmi ke rukne ki baat hai, doosra UV kirnein ke zyada neeche aane ki (Part H).

**Galti 4: "Ek sardi hui to global warming jhoothi hai"**
Mausam (weather) din-ba-din badalta hai. Global warming **lambe samay ke average** (climate) ki baat hai.

**Galti 5: "CO₂ sirf gaadiyon se banti hai"**
Gaadiyan, bijli ke power plant, industry, jungal kaatna, sab milakar. Gaadiyan ek hissa hain.

**Galti 6: "1 litre petrol (740 g) se 740 g hi CO₂ banegi"**
Nahi: hawa ki oxygen judti hai, isliye CO₂ ka mass petrol ke mass se **zyada** hota hai (lagbhag 2.28 kg).

**Galti 7: "420 ppm yaani 42%"**
420 ppm = 0.042%. 1 ppm = 0.0001%.

## Quick Recap

- Greenhouse effect: Suraj ki roshni andar aati hai, garam Earth infrared garmi bhejti hai, aur kuch gaisein (H₂O, CO₂, CH₄, N₂O, CFC) us garmi ka ek hissa rok leti hain.
- Natural greenhouse effect Earth ko lagbhag −18 °C ki jagah lagbhag 15 °C par rakhta hai (antar 33 °C): ye zaroori hai.
- Dikkat **extra** effect hai: fossil fuel jalana, jungal kaatna, paddy aur cattle aur kachre se methane, khaad se N₂O.
- CO₂ lagbhag 280 ppm se lagbhag 420 ppm hui hai (lagbhag 50% ka badhna); average temperature 1800s se 1 °C se zyada, samundar ka star 1900 se lagbhag 20 cm (sab approximate).
- Asar: glaciers ka pighalna, samundar ka star, heat waves, badalti barish, coral bleaching.
- 1 L petrol (octane, 740 g, M = 114 g per mol) ka jalna: 6.49 mol octane, 51.9 mol CO₂, lagbhag **2.28 kg CO₂**.
- Greenhouse effect aur ozone hole alag samasyaein hain.
- Hum kar sakte hain: bijli aur eendhan bachana, public transport, ped, renewable energy, kachra kam karna.

## Practice Questions

1. A household uses a 14.2 kg LPG cylinder in a month. Treat LPG as pure propane, C₃H₈ (molar mass 44 g per mol), burning as C₃H₈ + 5O₂ → 3CO₂ + 4H₂O. How many kg of CO₂ are produced when the whole cylinder is burnt?

2. A city bus runs on CNG, treated as methane, CH₄ (molar mass 16 g per mol). Burning 2.5 kg of CH₄ follows CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O. How many kg of CO₂ are produced?

3. A scooter travels 9000 km in a year at 15 km per litre. Using the result that 1 L of petrol gives about 2.28 kg of CO₂, find the litres of petrol used and the CO₂ produced in a year. How many trees (22 kg of CO₂ per tree per year, approximate) would absorb it?

4. Methane in air is about 1.9 ppm (approximate). (a) Convert this to percent. (b) How many litres of methane are there in 1 million litres of air?

5. A small power plant burns 100 kg of coal, treated as 80% carbon (C + O₂ → CO₂). Find the mass of carbon and the mass of CO₂ produced.

6. A moon-like planet has no atmosphere, and its average temperature is −20 °C. Another planet with the same distance from its star but with a good amount of greenhouse gases has an average of 14 °C. What is the difference in degrees Celsius and in kelvin, and what does the difference tell you?

10. A student says, "It snowed heavily last week, so global warming is false." In 3 to 4 lines, explain the difference between weather and climate and answer the student.

### Answers

1. 14.2 kg = 14200 g. n(C₃H₈) = 14200 ÷ 44 = **322.7 mol**. Equation: 1 mol C₃H₈ se 3 mol CO₂, to n(CO₂) = 322.7 × 3 = **968.2 mol**. Mass = 968.2 × 44 = 42,600 g = **42.6 kg CO₂** ✅. (Shortcut: 44 g propane se 3 × 44 = 132 g CO₂, yaani 3 guna: 14.2 × 3 = 42.6 kg ✅.)

2. 2.5 kg = 2500 g. n(CH₄) = 2500 ÷ 16 = **156.25 mol**. 1 mol CH₄ se 1 mol CO₂, to n(CO₂) = 156.25 mol. Mass = 156.25 × 44 = 6875 g = **6.875 kg**, lagbhag 6.9 kg ✅. (Shortcut: 16 g CH₄ se 44 g CO₂, ratio 2.75, aur 2.5 × 2.75 = 6.875 kg ✅.)

3. Petrol = 9000 ÷ 15 = **600 L**. CO₂ = 600 × 2.28 = 1368 kg, lagbhag **1.37 tonne**. Ped = 1368 ÷ 22 = 62.2, yaani lagbhag **62 ped**. (Aankde approximate.)

4. (a) 1.9 ppm = 1.9 ÷ 1,000,000 × 100 = **0.00019%**. (b) 1 ppm ka matlab 10 lakh mein 1 hissa, to 1,000,000 L mein 1.9 L, yaani **1.9 L methane** ✅.

5. Carbon = 100 × 80 ÷ 100 = **80 kg** = 80,000 g. n(C) = 80000 ÷ 12 = 6666.7 mol. 1 mol C se 1 mol CO₂, to mass = 6666.7 × 44 = 293,333 g = **293.3 kg CO₂** ✅. (Shortcut: 12 g C se 44 g CO₂, 80 × 44 ÷ 12 = 293.3 kg ✅.) CO₂ ka mass carbon ke mass se lagbhag 3.67 guna hai kyunki oxygen judti hai.

6. Antar = 14 − (−20) = **34 °C**. Kelvin mein: −20 °C = 253 K aur 14 °C = 287 K, to antar 287 − 253 = **34 K**. Isse pata chalta hai ki greenhouse gaisein planet ko kaafi garam rakh sakti hain, aur celsius aur kelvin mein antar ek jaisa hota hai ✅.

7. 1 L ethanol = 0.79 kg = 790 g. n(C₂H₅OH) = 790 ÷ 46 = **17.17 mol**. 1 mol se 2 mol CO₂, to n(CO₂) = 34.35 mol. Mass = 34.35 × 44 = 1511 g, yaani **lagbhag 1.51 kg CO₂** ✅. (Atom count: C 2 = 2; H 6 = 6; O: 1 + 6 = 7 aur 4 + 3 = 7 ✅.)

(c) **True** ✅. (d) **False** ❌. Ye alag samasyaein hain: ek garmi ke rukne ki, doosri UV kirnein ke zyada neeche aane ki.
