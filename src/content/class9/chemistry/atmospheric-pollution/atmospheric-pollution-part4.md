# Topic 4: Ozone Layer aur Uska Kam Hona

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki kuch gases dharti ki garmi ko rok leti hain, aur isse temperature badh raha hai. Wahan ek baat kahi thi: **ozone hole alag samasya hai**, greenhouse effect nahi. Aaj hum wahi samasya samjhenge. Ye Sooraj ki **UV (ultraviolet) kiranon** ki kahani hai, jo hamare sir ke upar ki ek patli, anjaani dhaal rokti hai.

---

## Pahad par sunscreen, topi aur chashma

Mr. Paranjape apne bhaanje Yatharth ko ek hill station ghumane laye the. Subah ka sooraj chamak raha tha, hawa thandi thi, aur Yatharth ko laga ki sunscreen ki koi zaroorat hi nahi: "Mama, yahan to thand hai, dhoop kahan chubh rahi hai?"

Mr. Paranjape ne bag se sunscreen, topi aur dhoop ka chashma nikala. "Pahad par dhoop ki UV kiranein aur tez hoti hain, aur thandi hawa mein wo mehsoos nahi hoti. Chalo, lagao."

Raste mein ek guide mila jo ek view-point par sabko samjha raha tha. Usne kaha ki upar aasmaan mein ek patli parat hai, jise **ozone layer** kehte hain, jo Sooraj ki harmful UV kiranon ka bahut bada hissa rok leti hai. "Pehle us parat ko CFC naam ki gas se nuksaan pahuncha tha, par ab duniya ne milkar kaam kiya hai aur wo dheere-dheere theek ho rahi hai," usne kaha.

Yatharth ne topi theek ki aur poochha, "Mama, ye ozone layer hai kya, aur gas se kaise toot jaati hai?" Mr. Paranjape hanse: "Pehle ye samajhte hain ki ozone **hai** kya. Phir dekhenge ki wo tootti kaise hai, aur theek kaise ho rahi hai."

## Part A: Ozone hai kya?

Chapter 3 mein hum O₂ (do atom ka oxygen) aur O₃ ke baare mein padh chuke hain. **Ozone O₃** hai: oxygen ke **teen** atoms ka ek molecule, yaani ek **triatomic** molecule. Jo oxygen hum saans mein lete hain wo O₂ hai; ozone ek alag gas hai, jiski ek halki, tej si gandh hoti hai.

Ek zaroori baat: ozone dharti par kahin bhi ho, wahi O₃ hai. Par wo **kahan** hai, isse tay hota hai ki wo hamare liye **achha** hai ya **bura**.

- **Stratosphere mein (lagbhag 15 se 35 km oopar):** ozone ki layer hai, jo hamari rakhwali karti hai. Ye **achha ozone** hai.
- **Zameen ke paas, jahan hum saans lete hain:** yahan ozone ek pradushak hai (Topic 1 ka secondary pollutant: vehicle ke dhuen se dhoop mein banta hai). Ye **bura ozone** hai: saans ki takleef, khansi aur aankhon mein jalan de sakta hai.

Achhe ozone ko bachana hai, bure ko kam karna hai. Ye dono alag kaam hain, aur dono mein "ozone" naam hone se confusion hota hai, isliye Part G mein hum ise saaf karenge.

Ye bhi dhyan rahe ki ozone layer hawa mein bahut **patli** hai: hawa ke baaki gases ke muqable ozone ki matra bahut kam hoti hai (aam taur par lagbhag kuch hisse prati million, approximate). Par itni kam matra ke bawajood ye UV ko rokne ka bahut bada kaam karti hai.

## Part B: Ozone layer kaise banti aur kaam karti hai

Stratosphere mein Sooraj ki UV kiranein oxygen par padti hain. UV mein itni energy hoti hai ki wo O₂ molecule ko do oxygen **atoms** mein tod deti hai:

O₂ → O + O (UV ki energy se)

Akela O atom bahut sakriya hota hai, aur wo turant kisi doosre O₂ se jud jaata hai:

O + O₂ → O₃

In dono ka milaa-jula roop (net) aise likhte hain:

3O₂ → 2O₃

(Jaanch neeche Part F mein hai.) Yaani stratosphere mein UV ki energy se oxygen ka kuch hissa ozone banta rehta hai. Is tarah UV ki energy ka kuch hissa **kharch** ho jaata hai.

Ab ozone ko UV khud bhi tod deti hai:

O₃ → O₂ + O (UV ki energy se)

Aur ye akela O atom phir kisi O₂ se milkar O₃ bana sakta hai. Ye **banna aur tootna** ka chakkar chalta rehta hai. Har baar jab O₂ ya O₃ ko UV todti hai, UV ki harmful energy use ho jaati hai (garmi mein badal jaati hai). Isiliye stratosphere ki upar ki parat thodi garam hoti hai, aur neeche tak harmful UV bahut kam pahunchti hai.

Natija: **ozone layer sooraj ki harmful UV kiranon ka bahut bada hissa sokh leti hai**, aur zameen par jeevan ke liye ek dhaal ban jaati hai. Jab tak banne aur tootne ki ye ginti santulit rehti hai, layer sthir rehti hai. Dikkat tab shuru hoti hai jab koi bahari cheez is santulan mein ghus jaati hai.

```
        Sooraj ki UV kiranein
           \     \     \
            \     \     \
   ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~   50 km (stratosphere ka upari sira)
            .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .
   ########################################   OZONE LAYER (O₃)
   ########################################   lagbhag 15 se 35 km
   ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~   15 km (ozone layer ka neeche ka sira)
            :           :
        bahut kam UV  pahunchti hai
            v           v
   ========================================   troposphere (badal, mausam)
   ----------------------------------------   zameen: hum, ped, samudra
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Ozone layer stratosphere mein (lagbhag 15 se 35 km). Sooraj ki UV kiranein oopar se aati hain; ozone layer unka bahut bada hissa sokh leti hai, isliye zameen tak bahut kam UV pahunchti hai. (Heights approximate hain aur sketch scale par nahi hai.)

## Part C: CFC aur chlorine atom ka chakkar

Beesvi sadi ke beech mein ek naya padarth bahut pasand kiya gaya: **CFC (chlorofluorocarbon)**. Ye gases jalti nahi, zehreeli nahi lagti, aur thanda karne ke liye bahut achhi thi. Isliye ye fridge, air conditioner mein coolant ke roop mein, aur aerosol sprays (deodorant, kuch paints) mein propellant ke roop mein bahut use hui. Ek example CFC-12 hai, jiska formula CCl₂F₂ hai.

Problem ye thi: CFC hawa mein **bahut der tak tikti** hai, tootti nahi. Wo hawa ke saath dheere-dheere oopar uthti hai aur stratosphere tak pahunch jaati hai. Wahan tez UV use todti hai aur ek **chlorine atom (Cl)** azaad ho jaata hai:

CCl₂F₂ → CClF₂ + Cl (UV ki energy se)

Ye akela Cl atom bahut sakriya hota hai. Ab dekho wo ozone ke saath kya karta hai:

**Step 1:** Cl + O₃ → ClO + O₂ (chlorine ne ozone ka ek atom le liya)

**Step 2:** ClO + O → Cl + O₂ (ClO ne ek aur O atom se milkar Cl ko wapas azaad kar diya)

Dhyan se dekho: Step 2 ke baad **Cl wapas aa gaya**. Chemical ke kaam mein ye wahi **catalyst** wali baat hai jo humne Chapter 7 mein padhi thi: catalyst reaction ko tez karta hai par **khud khatam nahi hota**. Cl khatam nahi hua, to wo agle ozone par kaam shuru kar deta hai.

Dono steps ko jodo (jo cheez dono taraf aati hai, wo kat jaati hai): Cl aur ClO dono taraf barabar hain, to bacha:

O₃ + O → 2O₂

Yaani net result: ozone **kam** hua aur aam oxygen O₂ ban gaya. Ek **akela chlorine atom lagbhag 1,00,000 (ek lakh) ozone molecules** tak tod sakta hai, isse pehle ki wo kisi aur tarah se hata liya jaye (ye ek **approximate** sankhya hai). Isiliye CFC ki thodi si matra bhi ozone layer ke liye bahut bhaari padi.

```
                 +------------------------------+
                 I   Cl atom (azaad)            I
                 +--------------+---------------+
                                :  + O₃
                                v
                 Cl + O₃ --> ClO + O₂   (ozone toota)
                                :
                                :  + O
                                v
                 ClO + O --> Cl + O₂    (Cl wapas mila)
                                :
                                +----------> phir se Cl, phir se naya O₃
                                             (chakkar baar-baar ghoomta hai)
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Chlorine atom ka chakkar. Cl ek ozone ko todta hai (ClO banta hai), phir ClO ek O atom se milkar Cl ko wapas azaad kar deta hai. Isliye ek Cl atom baar-baar ozone todta hai (lagbhag ek lakh baar, approximate). Cl catalyst ki tarah kaam karta hai: khud khatam nahi hota.

(Ye sirf ek saral sketch hai. Asli stratosphere mein aur bhi steps hote hain, jinki detail aage ki class mein.)

## Part D: Antarctica ka ozone hole

1985 mein ek report aayi: Antarctica ke upar, har saal ek khaas mausam mein, ozone layer ka kuch hissa bahut patla ho jaata hai. Ise **ozone hole** kaha gaya. Dhyan se: "hole" ka matlab layer mein sachmuch ka chhed nahi, balki layer ka **bahut patla** ho jaana hai.

Ye Antarctica ke upar zyada isliye dikha kyunki wahan sardi ke mausam mein stratosphere mein bahut thande badal bante hain, jo chlorine ke ozone todne ke kaam ko aur tez kar dete hain. (Ye detail aage ki class mein; abhi bas itna ki wahan ye zyada hota hai.)

Wahan se hawa dusre hisson mein bhi phailti hai, isliye ozone ka kam hona poori duniya ke liye chinta ki baat bani. Par achhi khabar aage hai.

---

## Part E: Montreal Protocol aur layer ka theek hona

Report aane ke sirf do saal baad, **1987** mein, duniya ke deshon ne milkar **Montreal Protocol** par sahi ki. Iska matlab tha: ozone ko todne wale padarth (jaise CFC) ko dheere-dheere **band** kiya jaye. Duniya ke lagbhag sabhi deshon ne ise apnaya, aur ek-ek karke ye padarth hataye gaye. Fridge aur AC mein CFC ki jagah doosre, ozone-surakshit refrigerants aaye.

Parinaam: ozone layer ab **dheere-dheere theek ho rahi hai**. Ye prakriya lambi hai kyunki CFC hawa mein bahut der tak rehti hai, par disha sahi hai. Ye ek ummeed dene wali kahani hai: jab duniya milkar ek vaigyanik samasya par kaam karti hai, to uska asar dikhta hai.

Har equation mein har atom dono taraf barabar hona chahiye. Neeche har equation ke atom dono taraf gine gaye hain.

## Part H: Zyada UV se nuksaan

Jab ozone layer patli hoti hai, to zameen par zyada UV pahunch sakti hai. Iska asar:

- **Skin:** sunburn (jalan, laali), jaldi bujurgi ke nishaan aur lambe samay mein **skin cancer** ka khatra.
- **Aankhein:** **cataract** (aankh ke lens ka dhundhla ho jaana) aur aankhon mein jalan.
- **Fasalein aur ped:** kuch fasalon ki paidawar ghat sakti hai.
- **Samudra ka plankton:** samudra ke chhote-chhote jeev (plankton) UV se nuksaan paate hain. Plankton machhliyon aur doosre jeevon ka bhojan hai, isliye poora food chain asar mein aata hai.

Ye sab baatein dar ke liye nahi, samajhdari ke liye hain: UV se bachna aasan hai.

## Part I: Hum kya kar sakte hain

- **Safe refrigerants:** naye fridge aur AC ozone-surakshit refrigerants use karte hain. Naya kharidte waqt ye check karna.
- **Purane fridge aur AC ko sahi tarah se nikalna:** jab purana fridge ya AC phenkna ho, to use kabadi mein ya kachre mein tod ke na phenko. Authorised recycler ya service centre ko do, taaki andar ki gas surakshit tarike se nikali jaye.
- **Sunscreen:** dhoop mein nikalne se pehle lagao (khaaskar pahad par ya garmi mein).
- **Topi aur chashma:** UV se bachane wale dhoop ke chashme (UV protection likha ho) aur chaude kinare ki topi pehno.
- **Dopahar ki tez dhoop se bachna:** zyada UV ke ghanton mein kuch der chhaanv mein raho.
- **Jaankari failao:** ghar mein bataao ki ozone layer theek ho rahi hai, par hamari madad se aur tezi se.

## Safe activity: ozone ka paper model (ghar par adult ke saath, ya school lab mein teacher ke saath)

Isme kuch jalana, gas banana ya garm karna nahi hai. Sirf kagaz aur pen.

1. Kagaz ke chhote tukdon par ye likho: **O₃ ke 5 card**, **O ke 5 card**, aur ek card par **Cl**.
2. Ek bada kagaz lo aur use "stratosphere" naam do. Cl card ko beech mein rakho.
3. Ek O₃ card Cl ke paas laao. Equation Cl + O₃ → ClO + O₂ ke hisaab se: O₃ card ko hata kar do card rakho: ek **ClO** (Cl ke upar ek O) aur ek **O₂**.
4. Ab ClO card ke paas ek **O** card laao. ClO + O → Cl + O₂ ke hisaab se: **Cl** wapas alag karo aur ek naya **O₂** card banao.
5. Gino: Cl card ab bhi ek hai (khatam nahi hua), aur ab tak kitne ozone toote? Phir se Cl ko agle O₃ card ke paas le jao aur chakkar dohrao.

Ye model dikhata hai ki chlorine "catalyst" hai: wo baar-baar kaam karta hai.

## Part J: Solved examples

### Example 1. Ek chhoti equation jaanch

3O₂ → 2O₃ balanced hai ya nahi?

- Left: O = 3 × 2 = 6.
- Right: O = 2 × 3 = 6.

**Jawab:** Balanced hai.

Jaanch: Dono taraf O = 6 ✅

### Example 2. Chlorine cycle ka net nikalo

Cl + O₃ → ClO + O₂ aur ClO + O → Cl + O₂ ko jodkar net equation likho.

- Left: Cl + O₃ + ClO + O. Right: ClO + O₂ + Cl + O₂.
- Cl dono taraf hai, to kat gaya. ClO dono taraf hai, to kat gaya.
- Bacha: O₃ + O → 2O₂.

**Jawab:** Net: O₃ + O → 2O₂. Chlorine net equation mein nahi aata, kyunki wo catalyst hai.

Jaanch: Left O = 3 + 1 = 4, right O = 2 × 2 = 4 ✅

### Example 3. Ozone banne mein mass

9 mol oxygen (O₂) se 3O₂ → 2O₃ ke hisaab se kitna ozone banega? Dono ka mass kitna hoga? (O = 16.)

- 3 mol O₂ se 2 mol O₃ banta hai. 9 mol O₂ = 3 guna, to O₃ = 2 × 3 = 6 mol.
- O₂ ka molar mass = 2 × 16 = 32 g/mol, to 9 mol = 9 × 32 = 288 g.
- O₃ ka molar mass = 3 × 16 = 48 g/mol, to 6 mol = 6 × 48 = 288 g.

**Jawab:** 6 mol O₃ banega. Dono ka mass 288 g hai: mass conserve hua.

Jaanch: 288 g = 288 g ✅

### Example 4. CFC-12 mein chlorine ka percent

CCl₂F₂ mein chlorine ka mass percent nikalo. (C = 12, Cl = 35.5, F = 19.)

- Molar mass = 12 + 2 × 35.5 + 2 × 19 = 12 + 71 + 38 = 121 g/mol.
- Cl ka mass = 71 g.
- Percent = 71 ÷ 121 × 100 = 58.7 (lagbhag).

**Jawab:** Lagbhag 58.7% chlorine.

Jaanch: 12 + 71 + 38 = 121 ✅ aur 121 × 0.587 = 71.0 ✅

### Example 5. Ek lakh guna asar

Maan lo 50 chlorine atoms mein se har ek lagbhag 1,00,000 ozone molecules todta hai. Kul kitne ozone molecules toote?

- 50 × 100000 = 5000000.

**Jawab:** Lagbhag 50,00,000 (pachaas lakh) ozone molecules. (Ye sirf ek sankhya wala udaharan hai; 1,00,000 khud approximate hai.)

Jaanch: 5000000 ÷ 100000 = 50 ✅

### Example 6. Oonchai aur saal ka gunaa-bhaag

(a) Ozone layer lagbhag 15 se 35 km tak hai. Uski motai (oonchai ka phailaav) kitni hui? (b) Ozone hole ki report 1985 mein aur Montreal Protocol 1987 mein. Kitne saal ka gap?

- (a) 35 − 15 = 20 km.
- (b) 1987 − 1985 = 2 saal.

**Jawab:** (a) lagbhag 20 km (approximate). (b) 2 saal.

Jaanch: 15 + 20 = 35 ✅ aur 1985 + 2 = 1987 ✅

### Example 7. Achha ya bura?

Neeche diye ozone ko achha ya bura batao: (a) 25 km ki oonchai par ozone, (b) ek shehar mein dhoop mein vehicle ke dhuen se bana ozone.

- (a) Stratosphere (15 se 35 km) ke andar, UV rokta hai.
- (b) Zameen ke paas, secondary pollutant.

**Jawab:** (a) achha ozone, (b) bura ozone.

Jaanch: Figure 4.3 ke hisaab se (a) upar layer mein, (b) neeche zameen ke paas ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Ozone hole aur global warming ek hi samasya hai"**

Nahi. Global warming (Topic 3) greenhouse gases ke garmi rokne se hoti hai. Ozone hole stratosphere mein UV rokne wali layer ke patla hone se hota hai. CFC ka dono se thoda sambandh hai (CFC ek greenhouse gas bhi hai), par ye do alag samasyaein hain.

**Galti 2: "Ozone layer mein sachmuch ka chhed hota hai"**

Nahi. "Hole" ka matlab ozone ka bahut kam (patla) ho jaana hai, na ki aasmaan mein chhed.

**Galti 3: "Ozone sab jagah achha hai"**

Nahi. Stratosphere mein achha, zameen ke paas bura (pradushak).

**Galti 4: "Chlorine atom ek ozone todkar khatam ho jaata hai"**

Nahi. Wo catalyst ki tarah kaam karta hai: Step 2 mein wapas mil jaata hai, aur lagbhag ek lakh ozone tak tod sakta hai (approximate).

**Galti 5: "Ozone layer ab hamesha ke liye toot gayi hai"**

Nahi. Montreal Protocol ke baad ye dheere-dheere theek ho rahi hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Ozone O₃** oxygen ke teen atoms ka molecule hai. Stratosphere mein (lagbhag 15 se 35 km) ozone layer Sooraj ki harmful **UV** kiranon ka bahut bada hissa rokti hai.
- UV O₂ ko todti hai (O₂ → O + O), O + O₂ → O₃ banta hai: net 3O₂ → 2O₃. UV O₃ ko bhi todti hai: O₃ → O₂ + O. Ye chakkar UV ki energy kharch karta hai.
- **CFC** (jaise CCl₂F₂) stratosphere tak pahunchti hai, UV se Cl atom azaad karti hai. Cl + O₃ → ClO + O₂ aur ClO + O → Cl + O₂: Cl wapas aa jaata hai, isliye **ek chlorine atom lagbhag ek lakh ozone** tak tod sakta hai (approximate). Net: O₃ + O → 2O₂.
- **Ozone hole** ki report 1985 mein Antarctica ke upar aayi. **Montreal Protocol (1987)** ne CFC dheere-dheere band kiye aur layer ab dheere-dheere theek ho rahi hai.
- Zyada UV se sunburn, skin cancer, cataract, fasal aur plankton ko nuksaan.
- **Achha ozone** stratosphere mein; **bura ozone** zameen ke paas ek pradushak.
- Hum kya karein: safe refrigerants, purane fridge/AC ko sahi tarah se dena, sunscreen, topi aur UV-protection chashma.
- Ozone hole aur global warming **alag** samasyaein hain.

    (a) The ozone layer is in the troposphere.
    (b) The ozone hole and global warming are the same problem.
    (c) A chlorine atom is used up after destroying one ozone molecule.
    (d) The ozone layer is slowly recovering after the Montreal Protocol.

### Answers

1. Ozone ka formula **O₃** hai: ek molecule mein **3 atoms** (triatomic). Jo oxygen hum saans mein lete hain wo **O₂** hai (2 atoms). Ozone ek alag gas hai jiski tej gandh hoti hai, aur zameen ke paas wo pradushak hai.

2. Ozone layer **stratosphere** mein hai, lagbhag **15 se 35 km** oopar (approximate). Wo Sooraj ki harmful UV kiranon ka bahut bada hissa sokh leti hai, isliye zameen par jeevan (skin, aankh, fasal, plankton) surakshit rehta hai.

3. UV O₂ ko todti hai: O₂ → O + O. Phir O + O₂ → O₃. Net: 3O₂ → 2O₃. Aur UV ozone ko bhi todti hai: O₃ → O₂ + O. Dono mein UV ki energy kharch hoti hai, isliye harmful UV neeche kam pahunchti hai.

4. **CFC** (chlorofluorocarbon) carbon, chlorine aur fluorine wali gases hain (jaise CCl₂F₂). Ye fridge aur AC mein coolant ke roop mein aur aerosol sprays mein use hoti thi. Ye hawa mein bahut der tak nahi tootti, isliye hawa ke saath dheere-dheere oopar stratosphere tak pahunch jaati hain.

5. Step 1: Cl + O₃ → ClO + O₂. Step 2: ClO + O → Cl + O₂. Cl **wapas mil jaata hai**, khatam nahi hota, isliye wo catalyst ki tarah baar-baar ozone todta hai (lagbhag ek lakh baar, approximate).

6. Dono ko jodne par Cl aur ClO dono taraf aate hain, to kat jaate hain. Net: **O₃ + O → 2O₂**. Left O = 3 + 1 = 4, right O = 2 × 2 = 4, balanced. Cl net equation mein nahi hai kyunki wo catalyst hai: shuru mein bhi tha, ant mein bhi bacha.

7. **Achha ozone** stratosphere mein hai aur UV rokta hai. **Bura ozone** zameen ke paas vehicle ke dhuen aur dhoop se banta hai aur saans, aankh aur fasal ko nuksaan deta hai. Zameen ke paas ozone chhodne se wo upar stratosphere tak layer bharne nahi jaata, aur neeche phephdon ko nuksaan deta hai, isliye ye theek upay nahi hai.

8. 3 mol O₂ → 2 mol O₃, to 12 mol O₂ = 4 guna, O₃ = 2 × 4 = **8 mol**. Mass = 8 × 48 = **384 g**. Jaanch: 12 × 32 = 384 g, dono mass barabar ✅.

9. CCl₃F ka molar mass = 12 + 3 × 35.5 + 19 = 12 + 106.5 + 19 = 137.5 g/mol. Cl ka mass = 106.5 g. Percent = 106.5 ÷ 137.5 × 100 = **lagbhag 77.5%**. Jaanch: 137.5 × 0.775 = 106.6 (lagbhag 106.5) ✅.

10. 30 × 100000 = **3000000** (tees lakh) ozone molecules, lagbhag (kyunki ek lakh khud approximate hai).

11. **Ozone hole** matlab Antarctica ke upar ek mausam mein ozone ka bahut kam (patla) ho jaana; ye **1985** mein report hua. **Montreal Protocol (1987)** ne CFC jaise ozone todne wale padarth dheere-dheere band kiye, aur uske baad layer dheere-dheere theek ho rahi hai.

12. Humans par: sunburn, skin cancer ka khatra, aankh ka cataract. Doosre jeevon par: fasalon ki paidawar ghat sakti hai, aur samudra ke plankton ko nuksaan hota hai (jisse food chain asar mein aati hai).

13. Pahad par UV zyada tez ho sakti hai aur thandi hawa mein dhoop ki jalan mehsoos nahi hoti, isliye nuksaan ka pata der se chalta hai. Do kaam: sunscreen lagana, aur topi aur UV-protection chashma pehenna (dopahar mein kuch der chhaanv mein rehna bhi).

14. (a) **False** ❌. Ozone layer **stratosphere** mein hai. (b) **False** ❌. Ye do alag samasyaein hain: ozone hole UV-dhaal ke patla hone se, global warming greenhouse gases ki wajah se garmi rukne se. (c) **False** ❌. Cl wapas mil jaata hai aur lagbhag ek lakh ozone tak tod sakta hai (approximate). (d) **True** ✅. Montreal Protocol ke baad layer dheere-dheere theek ho rahi hai.
