## Part A: Smog kya hai? Do kism ke smog

**Smog** shabd bana hai **sm**oke + f**og** se. Pehle isse us dhundh ko kehte the jisme kohra aur dhuan mile hote the. Aaj ye uss har tarah ki dhundh ko kehte hain jisme hawa dhuan, dhool aur gaason se bhari ho.

Chapter 2 mein hum ne dekha tha ki dhuan aur kohra **colloid** hain: unke kan itne chhote hain ki gir nahi paate, par itne bade ki roshni ko bikher dete hain (Tyndall effect). Isliye hawa dhundhli dikhti hai aur sooraj ka gola phika padh jaata hai.

Smog do mukhya kism ka hota hai.

**1. Dhuan wala (sardiyon ka) smog.** Isme kohra, dhuan, soot (kaalikh), dhool aur SO₂ jaisi gaasen hoti hain. Ye thandi, shaant, geeli sardi ki subah mein bahut banta hai. Hamare uttar Bharat ke sardiyon ke smog mein yahi mukhya hai (Part B).

**2. Photochemical smog (dhoop se bana smog).** Gaadiyon ke engine mein bahut ucche temperature par nitrogen aur oxygen milkar NO banate hain (Topic 2 yaad karo), jo hawa mein NO₂ ban jaati hai. Jab tez dhoop is NO₂ par padti hai, to wo toot kar NO aur ek oxygen atom deti hai. Ye atom hawa ki oxygen se mil kar **ozone** banata hai:

NO₂ → NO + O (dhoop ki roshni se)
O + O₂ → O₃

Ye ozone zameen ke paas ban raha hai, isliye ye wahi **"bura ozone" (ground-level ozone)** hai jiska zikr Topic 4 mein tha. Ye aankhon mein jalan aur khaansi karta hai, aur ek **secondary pollutant** hai: ye gaadi se seedha nahi nikalta, hawa mein dhoop ke saath banta hai. Garam, dhoop wale, traffic-bhare shehron mein ye jyada banta hai.

> **Jaanch:** "Dhoop wala smog aur dhundh wala smog ek hi tareeke se banta hai." ❌ Nahi. Sardiyon ka smog thandi, shaant hawa mein dhuan ke fas jaane se banta hai; photochemical smog dhoop mein NO₂ ke tootne se ozone banne se.

## Part B: Sardiyon mein uttar Bharat ka smog

Har saal October-November se January tak uttar Bharat ke kai shehron mein hawa kharab hoti hai. Iske kaaran ek nahi, **kai** hain, jo ek saath aa jaate hain:

| Source | Kya hota hai |
|---|---|
| **Fasal ke avshesh (parali) ka jalna** | Dhaan ki katai ke baad agli fasal se pehle thode hi din bachte hain, aur kuch khet mein dandi jalai jaati hai |
| **Gaadiyaan** | Lakhon gaadiyon ka dhuan (NO₂, CO, soot, PM2.5) |
| **Udyog aur thermal power plant** | Coal jalane se SO₂, soot aur raakh |
| **Construction aur sadak ki dhool** | PM10 ka bada hissa |
| **Kooda aur patte jalana, chulha, angeethi** | Dhuan aur PM2.5 |
| **Mausam** | Kam hawa, thandi raat, aur sabse zaroori: temperature inversion |

Ye saare source saal bhar kuch na kuch hote hain. To **sardiyon mein hi smog itna kyun badh jaata hai?** Isliye ki sardiyon mein **mausam hawa ko saaf hone se rokta hai**. Isi ko samjhte hain.

### Temperature inversion: hawa ka "dhakkan"

Troposphere mein (Topic 1) aam taur par **jaise jaise oonchai badhti hai, temperature girta hai**. Yaani zameen ke paas hawa garam aur upar thandi. Garam hawa halki hoti hai, isliye wo **upar uthti hai** aur apne saath dhuan aur dhool bhi le jaati hai, jahan wo phail kar kam ho jaate hain. Ye wahi **convection** hai jo Physics ke chapter "Heat and Temperature" mein dekha tha: garam fluid upar uthta hai, thanda neeche aata hai.

Sardiyon ki lambi, saaf aur shaant raat mein zameen apni garmi raat bhar aasman ki taraf chhodti jaati hai (radiation) aur bahut thandi ho jaati hai. Zameen ke paas ki hawa bhi thandi ho jaati hai, jabki kuch oonchai par ki hawa abhi utni thandi nahi hui hoti. Ab ek ulti sthiti ban jaati hai: **neeche thandi hawa, uske upar garam hawa ki parat.** Isi ko **temperature inversion** kehte hain ("inversion" yaani ulta).

Ab dekho kya hota hai. Neeche ki thandi hawa **zyada ghani (dense) aur bhaari** hai. Wo garam, halki hawa ke upar nahi uth sakti. Convection ruk gaya. Dhuan, dhool aur gaasen neeche hi fas jaati hain, jaise kisi bartan par dhakkan lag gaya ho. Upar se hawa bhi shaant ho to unhe udane wala bhi koi nahi.

```
   oonchai
     ^
     :   upar ki thandi hawa   (T kam)
     :   ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
     :   GARAM hawa ki parat   (T zyada)   <- "dhakkan"
     :   ====================
     :   ye parat upar jaane se rokti hai
     :   thandi hawa  (zameen ke paas)
     :   . : . : . : . : . : . : .   dhuan, dhool, gaasen fasi hain
     :   gaadi  chulha  parali  factory
     +---------------------------------------->
                  zameen
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Temperature inversion: zameen ke paas thandi (bhaari) hawa ke upar garam hawa ki parat hoti hai. Neeche ki hawa upar nahi uth paati, isliye dhuan aur dhool zameen ke paas fas kar smog bana dete hain.

Din chadhne par dhoop zameen ko garam karti hai, neeche ki hawa bhi garam hoti hai, aur "dhakkan" kamzor padne lagta hai. Tez hawa ya barish aa jaye to bhi smog chhat jaata hai. Isiliye subah aur raat ka smog doopahar ke baad kuch halka ho jaata hai. (Ye inversion prakriti ka mausam hai: pradushan ka source insaan ke kaam hain, par dhakkan mausam ka hai.)

> **Jaanch:** "Temperature inversion mein zameen ke paas ki hawa garam aur upar ki thandi hoti hai." ❌ Nahi. Aam din mein aisa hota hai. Inversion mein **thandi hawa neeche** aur uske upar garam parat hoti hai, isliye hawa upar nahi uth paati.

## Part C: Particulate Matter sharir mein kahan tak jaata hai?

Chapter ke Topic 1 mein dekha tha: PM10 mein 10 micrometre tak ke kan aur PM2.5 mein 2.5 micrometre tak ke kan. Baal ki motai lagbhag 70 micrometre hoti hai. Ab ye dekhte hain ki ye kan hamare andar kahan tak pahunchte hain.

```
   Hawa mein kan (PM10 aur PM2.5)
            :
            v
      +-----------+
      I   NAAK    I  baal aur chipchipi parat bade kan (dhool) pakad leti hai
      +-----+-----+
            :
            v
      +-----------+
      I  GALA     I  kuch aur kan yahan atak kar khaansi se bahar
      +-----+-----+
            :
            v
      +-----------+
      I SAANS KI  I  bahut baareek PM2.5 yahan se bhi aage nikal jaate hain
      I   NALI    I
      +-----+-----+
            :
            v
      +-----------+
      I PHEPHDE   I  angoor ke gucche jaisi alveoli
      I (alveoli) I  PM2.5 gehre tak pahunchta hai
      +-----+-----+
            :
            v
      +-----------+
      I  KHOON    I  sabse chhote kan (aur unse judi cheezein) khoon mein
      +-----------+  mil kar sharir ke aur ang tak
```

> **Figure 5.2** – PM ka safar saral roop mein: naak aur gala bade kanon (PM10) ka bada hissa rok lete hain, jabki PM2.5 phephdon ki alveoli tak pahunch jaata hai, aur sabse baareek kan khoon tak jaa sakte hain.

Naak mein baal aur chipchipi parat hoti hai, gale mein khaansi ka system hai: ye sab bade kanon ko rokne ke liye hain. Par PM2.5 itna baareek hai ki wo in "darbaanon" se aksar bach kar nikalta hai aur phephdon ki **alveoli** tak jaa sakta hai. Wahan se bahut chhote kan aur unke saath chipki cheezein khoon mein mil jaati hain.

**Example 1: Baal ke muqable PM2.5 kitna patla?**
Baal 70 micrometre, PM2.5 ka kan 2.5 micrometre. 70 ÷ 2.5 = **28**. Yaani PM2.5 baal se lagbhag **28 guna** (lagbhag 30 guna) patla hai. Ek baal ki chaudai mein lagbhag 28 aise kan line mein aa sakte hain.

**Example 2: 2.5 micrometre ko nanometre aur metre mein badlo.**
1 micrometre = 1000 nm, to 2.5 × 1000 = **2500 nm**. Aur 1 micrometre = 10⁻⁶ m, to 2.5 × 10⁻⁶ m = **2.5 × 10⁻⁶ m**.

### Hawa mein kitna PM hai: µg/m³

AQI ke peeche mein PM ki **matra** naapi jaati hai: 1 ghan metre (m³) hawa mein kitne **microgram (µg)** kan hain. 1 µg = 10⁻⁶ g, yaani ek gram ka dus lakhvaan hissa. (Yahan ke saare aankde sochne ke liye example hain, asli reading shehar aur din par nirbhar hoti hai.)

**Example 3: Ek kamre mein PM2.5 ka dravyaman.**
Ek kamra 4 m × 5 m × 3 m hai, to aayatan = 4 × 5 × 3 = **60 m³**. Maan lo PM2.5 ka star **150 µg/m³** hai. Kamre ki hawa mein PM2.5 ka kul dravyaman = 60 × 150 = **9000 µg = 9 mg** (1 mg = 1000 µg). Ye bahut kam dikhta hai, par ye ek-ek kan sharir ke liye hanikarak hai.
Agar wahi kamra 15 µg/m³ wali saaf hawa se bhara hota (lagbhag wo level jo WHO ki 2021 ki 24 ghante ki salaah ke aas-paas hai), to dravyaman 60 × 15 = **900 µg** hota: 9000 ÷ 900 = **10 guna kam**.

**Example 4: Ek din mein sharir mein kitna PM2.5 jaata hai?**
Maan lo ek vayask lagbhag **7.5 L hawa har minute** andar leta hai (aaram ki avastha ka lagbhag aankda). Ek din = 24 × 60 = 1440 minute, to hawa = 7.5 × 1440 = 10800 L = **10.8 m³**. 150 µg/m³ par andar gayi PM2.5 = 10.8 × 150 = **1620 µg = 1.62 mg**. Saaf hawa mein (15 µg/m³) ye sirf 10.8 × 15 = **162 µg** hoti. (Ye matra hawa mein se andar aayi hai, jo sab sharir mein nahi rukti, par ye dikhata hai ki kharab din par humne kitna zyada saans mein liya.)

> **Jaanch:** 28 × 2.5 = 70 ✅. 60 × 150 = 9000 ✅. 7.5 × 1440 = 10800 ✅.

## Part D: Carbon monoxide: chup-chaap khatara

Chapter 7 (Topic 6) mein dekha tha ki jab jalne ke liye oxygen kam pad jaye to carbon poori tarah CO₂ nahi banta, balki **carbon monoxide (CO)** banta hai: **incomplete combustion**.

2C + O₂ → 2CO
C + O₂ → CO₂ (poori oxygen hone par)

CO **rangheen aur gandhheen** gas hai. Is liye ye na dikhti hai, na sunghi jaati hai. Ye khoon ke **haemoglobin** se, jo oxygen ko sharir mein le jaata hai, oxygen ke muqable **bahut zyada mazbooti se** chipak jaati hai. Jab CO haemoglobin par kabza kar leti hai, to oxygen ke liye jagah nahi bachti, aur dimaag aur dil tak kam oxygen pahunchti hai. Sir dard, chakkar, ghabrahat, aur zyada matra mein behoshi bhi ho sakti hai.

**Example 5: Carbon ke 12 g ke incomplete combustion se kitni CO?**
2C + O₂ → 2CO. Molar mass: C = 12, CO = 12 + 16 = 28 g/mol. 2 mol C (24 g) se 2 mol CO (56 g). Yaani **12 g C se 28 g CO** banti hai. Check: 1 mol C (12 g) gives 1 mol CO (28 g) ✅.
Wahi 12 g C poori oxygen mein jale to C + O₂ → CO₂ se 44 g CO₂ banti hai (CO₂ = 12 + 32 = 44).

**Ghar ke andar ye kyun khatarnak hai?** Band kamre mein angeethi, koyle, lakdi ya gas ka burner jalao to oxygen kam hoti jaati hai, aur CO jama hoti jaati hai. Sardiyon mein log khidki-darwaze band karke angeethi andar rakh lete hain: ye sabse khatarnak aadat hai.

**Safe tareeke (sirf padhne ke liye, koi bhi cheez jalakar test nahi karna):**
- Angeethi, koyla ya gas ka heater kabhi band kamre mein mat jalao. Hawa ka raasta rakho.
- Gas stove ki laal-peeli lau ho to burner saaf karwao (peeli lau incomplete combustion ka sanket hai).
- Garage mein band jagah par gaadi ka engine chalu mat chhodo.
- Gas geyser bathroom ke andar ho to ventilator zaroor ho.

## Part E: Ghar ke andar ka pradushan

Hum sochte hain ki pradushan sirf bahar hai, par ghar ke andar ki hawa bhi bigad sakti hai.
- **Chulha (lakdi, upla, coal)**: bahut dhuan aur PM2.5, aur CO. Jin gharon mein chulhe par khaana pakta hai wahan aurton aur bachchon ki aankhein aur phephde zyada prabhavit hote hain. **LPG ya electric/induction jaise saaf eendhan** kaafi behtar hain (Topic 6 mein dekhenge).
- **Angeethi/koyla band kamre mein**: CO ka khatara.
- **Agarbatti, mosquito coil ka dhuan** band kamre mein: PM2.5 badhta hai.
- **Rasoi mein tel ka dhuan**: chimney ya exhaust fan chalao, khidki kholo.
- **Dhool, seelan, fungus**: saaf-safai rakho.

Ghar ke andar ke liye sabse asaan upay: **hawa ka aana-jaana (ventilation)**, saaf eendhan aur chulhe ka dhuan baahar nikalne ki chimney. (Par bahar ka AQI bahut kharab ho to khidki thodi der kholne ka sahi samay chunna: Part F.)

## Part F: Sehat par asar aur kaise bachein

PM2.5 aur gaasen (CO, SO₂, NO₂, ground-level ozone) milkar ye asar kar sakte hain:
- **Turant**: aankhon mein jalan, gale mein kharash, khaansi, naak behna, sir dard.
- **Dama (asthma) aur saans ki bimariyan**: dauron ka badhna, bronchitis.
- **Lambe samay mein**: phephdon ki kamzori, dil ki bimariyan, aur gambhir rog.
- **Sabse zyada khatra**: bachche (unke phephde abhi bade ho rahe hain), buzurg, dil ya phephde ke marij, garbhvati mahilayein, aur bahar kaam karne wale log (traffic police, delivery, kisan).

Ye sab aise bataya gaya hai ki ek din ki kharab hawa har kisi ko bimar nahi karti: asar matra aur samay par nirbhar karta hai, aur har insaan par alag hota hai. Par jahan bacha ja sakta hai, wahan bachna samajhdaari hai.

**Kya karein?**
1. **AQI dekhne ki aadat daalo.** Phone app ya shehar ki website par roz ka AQI padho.
2. **AQI "Poor" ya usse upar ho to** bahar ka khel-kood aur subah ki daud ghar ke andar karo ya rok do. Bachche aur buzurg khaas dhyan rakhein.
3. **Khidki-darwaze** bure din par kuch der ke liye band rakho (aur chulha/angeethi bilkul band kamre mein na jalao: Part D). Hawa saaf ho jaye to ventilation karo.
4. **Mask:** **N95 (ya FFP2)** type ke mask, jo sahi fit ho aur chehre se chipka ho, **0.3 micrometre ke kanon ka lagbhag 95%** rok dete hain. Kapde ka dheela mask ya bina fit wala mask kam kaam karta hai.
5. **Air purifier** kuch jagahon (jaise kamre mein) madad kar sakta hai: andar ke PM ko filter se kam karta hai. Ye bahar ki hawa ka upay nahi hai, aur band kamre mein hi theek kaam karta hai.
6. **Paani piyo, aankhein dhoyo**, aur sehat mein bahut takleef ho to doctor ko dikhao.
7. **Lambe samay ke upay**: Topic 6 mein dekhenge ki asli samadhaan source par kaam karna hai.

**Example 6: N95 mask kitna rokta hai?**
Maan lo kisi jagah 1000 aise kan hain jinka naap 0.3 micrometre hai (sabse mushkil size). 95% rukte hain: 1000 × 95/100 = **950** ruke, to 1000 − 950 = **50** andar jaate hain. Bina mask ke saare 1000 jaate. (Mask ke kinare se hawa nikal jaye to ye aankda kam ho jaata hai: isliye fit zaroori hai.)

> **Jaanch:** 950 + 50 = 1000 ✅. Aur 50 ÷ 1000 × 100 = 5% ✅.

## Part G: Ghar par test: greased-card dust test

Hawa mein kitni dhool aur kan hain, ye dekhne ka ek aasaan tareeka. **Isme kuch jalana nahi hai.** Ye test sirf **bade, dikhne wale kan** (dhool, soot ke bade tukde, parag) dikhata hai. PM2.5 aankh se dikhta hi nahi: iska matlab ye nahi ki wo nahi hai.

**Kaise karein (ghar par adult ke saath):**
1. Safed card (jaise 10 cm × 10 cm ka chart paper) lo. Us par pencil se **1 cm × 1 cm ke khaane** banao.
2. Card par **vaseline ki bahut patli parat** lagao (ungli se, phir saaf kapde se barabar karo). Hath baad mein sabun se dho lo.
3. Card ko **baahar, kisi surakshit, chhaon wali jagah par** (balcony ki railing ya chhat ka kinara, jahan gire nahi) **kisi adult ke saath** rakho. Aise 2 card lo: ek **sadak ke paas** aur ek **park ya kam traffic wali jagah** par.
4. **24 ghante** baad card ko adult ke saath utha lo (dhyan se, taaki dhool jhade nahi). Dekhne ke liye **magnifying glass** (ya phone ka zoom) lo.
5. Kuch khaane chuno (jaise 4 khaane) aur har khaane mein kan ginkar likho. **Average** nikalo. Ye aankde note karo.

```
   Greased card (10 cm x 10 cm), 24 ghante baad

   +----+----+----+----+----+
   I .. I .  I :  I .: I .  I   har chhote khaane (1 cm x 1 cm)
   +----+----+----+----+----+   mein dikhne wale kan ginte hain
   I :. I .  I .. I .  I :  I
   +----+----+----+----+----+   vaseline ki parat par chipke
   I .  I :: I .  I .: I .. I   dhool aur soot ke kan
   +----+----+----+----+----+
   magnifying glass se dekho    sirf bade kan dikhte hain,
                                PM2.5 nahi
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Greased-card test: vaseline lage card par 24 ghante mein baithe dhool aur soot ke kan, magnifying glass se 1 cm × 1 cm khaano mein ginte jaate hain. Sadak ke paas aur park ke card ki tulna karke hawa ki dhool ka andaza milta hai.

**Example 7: Card ka hisaab.**
Sadak ke paas wale card ke 3 khaano mein kan: 12, 15, 9. Average = (12 + 15 + 9) ÷ 3 = 36 ÷ 3 = **12 kan per cm²**. Card ka kshetrafal 10 × 10 = 100 cm², to poore card par lagbhag **12 × 100 = 1200 kan** (lagbhag andaza).

**Test ki seema:** ye test **keval tulna** deta hai (kahan zyada, kahan kam), saaf AQI nahi batata. Ye sirf bade kan dikhata hai. Mausam, hawa ki disha, card kitni der rakha aur vaseline ki parat par bhi nirbhar hai. Ek hi din ka card pakka nishkarsh nahi deta: kayi din dohrao.

**Safety:** kuch bhi jalana nahi. Card ko adult ke saath hi baahar rakho aur uthao. Vaseline munh ya aankhon mein na jaye. Kaam ke baad hath dho lo.

## Part H: AQI padhne ki practice (reasoning)

Yaad karo AQI ka table: **Good 0-50, Satisfactory 51-100, Moderate 101-200, Poor 201-300, Very Poor 301-400, Severe 401-500.**

**Example 8: Aarvi ke shahar ke din.**
- Pehla din AQI **462**: 401 se 500 ke beech hai, to **Severe**.
- Doosra din AQI **187**: 101 se 200, to **Moderate**.
- Teesra din AQI **52**: 51 se 100, to **Satisfactory**.
- Chautha din AQI **300**: 201 se 300, to **Poor**; agar **301** hota to **Very Poor**. Ek hi ank ka fark category badal deta hai.

**Example 9: Kya school band hona chahiye?**
AQI 462 (Severe): bahar khelne ya assembly karne ki salah nahi di jaati, khaaskar bachchon ko. School ya shehar ka prashasan band karne jaise kadam utha sakta hai. Par AQI **187 (Moderate)** par aam taur par school chal sakta hai, bas sanvedansheel bachche (jaise dama wale) dhyan rakhein aur bahar ke khel kam karein. Ye faisle prashasan karta hai, par samajh ye hai ki **category ka matlab sehat par sambhavit asar hai.**

> **Jaanch:** 462 ki category Severe, kyunki 462 ≥ 401 ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Smog matlab sirf kohra"**
Smog = smoke + fog. Isme dhuan, dhool aur gaasen bhi hoti hain, isliye ye sirf paani ki boondon ka kohra nahi hai.

**Galti 2: "Smog sirf parali jalane se hota hai"**
Parali ek source hai, par gaadiyaan, udyog, construction ki dhool, kooda jalana aur sardiyon ka mausam bhi bade kaaran hain.

**Galti 3: "Thandi hawa upar uthti hai"**
Garam hawa halki hokar upar uthti hai. Thandi hawa bhaari hoti hai: inversion mein wo neeche rehti hai aur dhuan ko neeche fasati hai.

**Galti 4: "PM2.5 aankh se dikhta hai"**
Nahi: wo baal se lagbhag 28 guna patla hai. Hum jo dhundh dekhte hain wo bahut saare kanon ka mil kar asar hai.

**Galti 5: "CO ki gandh aati hai, to pata chal jayega"**
CO rangheen aur gandhheen hai, isliye ye chup-chaap khatarnak hai. Band kamre mein angeethi kabhi mat jalao.

**Galti 6: "Kapde ka koi bhi mask N95 jaisa kaam karta hai"**
N95 sahi fit hone par 0.3 micrometre ke lagbhag 95% kan rokta hai. Dheela kapda kam rokta hai.

**Galti 7: "Greased-card test PM2.5 naapta hai"**
Ye sirf bade, dikhne wale kan dikhata hai aur tulna ke liye hai.

**Galti 8: "Photochemical smog aur ozone hole ek hi hain"**
Nahi. Photochemical smog mein zameen ke paas ka ground-level ozone bana hai (jo harmful hai), jabki ozone hole stratosphere ke achhe ozone ka kam hona hai (Topic 4).

## Quick Recap

- **Smog** = smoke + fog: dhundh jisme dhuan, dhool aur gaasen mili hain. Do kism: sardiyon ka dhuan wala smog aur dhoop se bana **photochemical smog** (NO₂ → NO + O, O + O₂ → O₃).
- Sardiyon mein **temperature inversion**: neeche thandi, upar garam parat, convection ruk jaata hai, dhuan aur dhool neeche fas jaate hain.
- Uttar Bharat ke smog ke kaaran kai hain: parali jalana, gaadiyaan, udyog, construction dhool, kooda jalana, mausam.
- **PM2.5** baal se lagbhag 28 guna patla (70 ÷ 2.5 = 28), phephdon ki alveoli tak aur khoon tak pahunch sakta hai.
- µg/m³ ka matlab: 1 m³ hawa mein kitne microgram kan. 1 µg = 10⁻⁶ g.
- **CO** (2C + O₂ → 2CO): rangheen, gandhheen, haemoglobin se bahut zyada mazbooti se judti hai. Band kamre mein angeethi kabhi nahi.
- Ghar ke andar ke pradushan: chulha, angeethi, agarbatti, tel ka dhuan. Ventilation aur saaf eendhan se kam hota hai.
- Bachne ke tareeke: AQI dekhna, bure din ghar ke andar khelna, sahi fit wala N95, air purifier (andar), khidki-darwaze ka sahi samay.
- N95: 0.3 micrometre ke lagbhag 95% kan rokta hai.
- Greased-card test sirf bade kanon ki tulna karta hai: kuch jalana nahi, adult ke saath.
- AQI: 0-50 Good, 51-100 Satisfactory, 101-200 Moderate, 201-300 Poor, 301-400 Very Poor, 401-500 Severe.

## Practice Questions

1. Give the AQI category for each reading: (a) 38, (b) 164, (c) 256, (d) 349, (e) 478, (f) 400, (g) 401.

2. A school checks the AQI at three times: 410 in the morning, 295 at noon, 180 the next day. Name the category of each. On which of these would outdoor games be most strongly discouraged, and why?

3. (a) How many times thinner than a human hair (70 micrometres) is a PM10 particle of 10 micrometres? (b) How many times larger is a 10 micrometre particle than a 2.5 micrometre particle? (c) Express 0.3 micrometre in nanometres and in metres. (d) About how many 0.3 micrometre particles would fit side by side across the width of one hair (70 micrometres)?

4. A classroom is 8 m long, 6 m wide and 3 m high. The PM2.5 level is 80 µg/m³. (a) Find the volume of the room. (b) Find the total mass of PM2.5 in the room in µg and in mg. (c) A purifier brings the level down to 20 µg/m³. How much PM2.5 mass has been removed?

5. A child breathes in about 6 L of air per minute. (a) How many cubic metres of air does the child breathe in 24 hours? (b) How many µg of PM2.5 are breathed in a day if the level is 250 µg/m³, and how many if it is 15 µg/m³? (c) How many times larger is the first amount?

6. In a made-up test, 20000 particles of size 0.3 micrometre approach the face. An N95 mask stops 95% of them. A loose cloth mask stops only 40%. How many particles get through in each case?

7. Methane in a poorly ventilated gas burner may burn incompletely: 2CH₄ + 3O₂ → 2CO + 4H₂O. (a) Check by counting atoms that the equation is balanced. (b) How many grams of CO form from 8 g of methane (molar mass CH₄ = 16 g, CO = 28 g)? (c) With enough oxygen, CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O. How many grams of CO₂ form from the same 8 g (CO₂ = 44 g)?

8. On a clear, calm winter night, smoke from a village stays low near the ground instead of rising. Explain using temperature inversion and convection. Why does it often clear up after the Sun has been up for several hours?

9. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) Photochemical smog needs sunlight. (b) Carbon monoxide has a strong smell, so we can easily notice it. (c) PM2.5 is stopped completely by the nose. (d) A greased-card test can tell us the exact PM2.5 value. (e) Burning charcoal in a closed room is safe if the fire is small.

10. In a greased-card test, four 1 cm² squares on Card A (near a busy road) had 6, 8, 10 and 12 specks, and on Card B (in a park) 2, 1, 3 and 2. (a) Find the average count per cm² for each card. (b) How many times more specks per cm² does A have than B? (c) Estimate the specks on the whole 10 cm × 10 cm card A and card B. (d) Why can this test not tell the PM2.5 level?

### Answers

1. Table se: (a) 38: 0 se 50 ke beech, **Good**. (b) 164: 101 se 200, **Moderate**. (c) 256: 201 se 300, **Poor**. (d) 349: 301 se 400, **Very Poor**. (e) 478: 401 se 500, **Severe**. (f) 400: 301 se 400, **Very Poor** (400 abhi bhi usi band mein hai). (g) 401, **Severe**.

2. 410: 401 se 500 mein, **Severe**. 295: 201 se 300 mein, **Poor**. 180: 101 se 200 mein, **Moderate**. Sabse zyada rok **subah ke 410 (Severe)** par, kyunki us din hawa mein pradushan sabse zyada hai aur bahar khelne se bachche gehri saans mein zyada pradushak andar lete hain. 295 (Poor) mein bhi bahar ke khel kam karne chahiye.

3. (a) 70 ÷ 10 = **7 guna** patla (PM10 baal se 7 guna patla). (b) 10 ÷ 2.5 = **4 guna** bada. (c) 0.3 × 1000 = **300 nm**, aur 0.3 × 10⁻⁶ = **3 × 10⁻⁷ m**. (d) 70 ÷ 0.3 = 233.3, yaani lagbhag **233** kan ek baal ki chaudai mein aa sakte hain ✅.

4. (a) 8 × 6 × 3 = **144 m³**. (b) 144 × 80 = **11520 µg** = 11520 ÷ 1000 = **11.52 mg**. (c) 20 µg/m³ par dravyaman = 144 × 20 = 2880 µg. Hata hua = 11520 − 2880 = **8640 µg** (= 8.64 mg) ✅. (Ye sirf soch ka hisaab hai: asli purifier kamre ki size aur samay par nirbhar karta hai.)

5. (a) 6 L × 60 × 24 = 8640 L = **8.64 m³**. (b) 250 µg/m³ par 8.64 × 250 = **2160 µg**. 15 µg/m³ par 8.64 × 15 = **129.6 µg**. (c) 2160 ÷ 129.6 = **lagbhag 16.7 guna** (250 ÷ 15 = 16.67 ✅).

6. N95: 95% ruke, to 5% jaate hain: 20000 × 0.05 = **1000 kan** andar. Cloth mask: 40% ruke, to 60% jaate hain: 20000 × 0.6 = **12000 kan** andar. N95 mein 12 guna kam kan jaate hain (12000 ÷ 1000 = 12). (Aankde made-up hain.)

7. (a) Baayein: C = 2, H = 2 × 4 = 8, O = 3 × 2 = 6. Daayein: CO se C = 2, O = 2; 4H₂O se H = 8, O = 4, kul O = 2 + 4 = 6. Dono taraf C = 2, H = 8, O = 6: **balanced** ✅. (b) 8 g ÷ 16 = 0.5 mol CH₄. Equation mein 2 mol CH₄ se 2 mol CO, to 0.5 mol CH₄ se 0.5 mol CO = 0.5 × 28 = **14 g CO**. (c) 1 mol CH₄ se 1 mol CO₂, to 0.5 mol CO₂ = 0.5 × 44 = **22 g CO₂**. Dono mein carbon ke mol barabar (0.5) hain: farak sirf ye hai ki incomplete mein CO (zehreeli) aur poori mein CO₂.

8. Saaf, shaant raat mein zameen aasman ki taraf garmi chhodkar thandi ho jaati hai, aur uske paas ki hawa bhi thandi aur bhaari ho jaati hai. Upar ki hawa kuch garam rehti hai: **temperature inversion**. Garam hawa upar uthti hai (convection), par yahan upar wali parat garam aur halki hai, neeche ki thandi hawa upar nahi uth sakti, to convection ruk jaata hai aur dhuan neeche fasa rehta hai. **Din chadhne par** dhoop zameen ko garam karti hai, neeche ki hawa bhi garam hoti hai, "dhakkan" kamzor hota hai, convection shuru hota hai aur dhuan upar tak failta hai (aur hawa chalne se bhi chhat jaata hai).

9. (a) **True** ✅. Dhoop mein NO₂ tootkar NO aur O banata hai, jisse ozone bante hain. (b) **False** ❌. CO **rangheen aur gandhheen** hai, isliye pata nahi chalta. (c) **False** ❌. Naak bade kanon ko rokta hai, par PM2.5 phephdon tak pahunch sakta hai. (d) **False** ❌. Ye sirf bade, dikhne wale kan dikhata hai aur tulna deta hai, exact PM2.5 nahi. (e) **False** ❌. Band kamre mein chhoti aag se bhi CO jama ho sakti hai: kabhi nahi.

10. (a) Card A: (6 + 8 + 10 + 12) ÷ 4 = 36 ÷ 4 = **9 per cm²**. Card B: (2 + 1 + 3 + 2) ÷ 4 = 8 ÷ 4 = **2 per cm²**. (b) 9 ÷ 2 = **4.5 guna**. (c) Card ka kshetrafal 100 cm², to A: 9 × 100 = **900**; B: 2 × 100 = **200** kan (lagbhag). (d) Kyunki PM2.5 itna baareek hai ki aankh ya magnifying glass se saaf nahi ginta, aur card par mostly bade kan jamte hain. Ye test sirf dhool ki tulna deta hai.
