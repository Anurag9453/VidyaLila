## Part A: Pehle chulha, fuel aur gaadi (source par roko)

Sabse achha upay hai ki gandagi **bane hi nahi**. Isliye pehle fuel aur vahano ki baat.

**1. BS-VI fuel aur gaadiyan.** Pichhle topics mein humne dekha ki sulphur waala fuel jalne par SO₂ deta hai (Topic 2), aur gaadi ke engine se nitrogen oxides aur dhuan nikalte hain. BS-VI niyam (April 2020 se poore Bharat mein) mein petrol aur diesel mein sulphur lagbhag **10 ppm** tak rakha gaya hai. Purane BS-IV mein ye lagbhag **50 ppm** tha (approximate).

**Example 1: Sulphur kitna ghata?**
1000 kg petrol mein 50 ppm (mass ke hisaab se) sulphur ka matlab 50 mg har kg, yaani 1000 × 50 = 50000 mg = **50 g** sulphur.
10 ppm par: 1000 × 10 = 10000 mg = **10 g** sulphur.
Kami: 50 − 10 = 40 g, yaani 40 ÷ 50 = **80%** kam sulphur.
Ye sulphur jalkar SO₂ banata hai (S + O₂ → SO₂, 32 g S se 64 g SO₂), to 50 g S se 100 g SO₂ aur 10 g S se 20 g SO₂: **80 g SO₂ ka fark** har 1000 kg fuel par.

> **Jaanch:** 100 − 20 = 80 g SO₂ ✅ aur 80 ÷ 100 = 80% ✅ (wahi pratishat jo sulphur ka tha).

**2. Saaf fuel: CNG aur LPG.** CNG mein mukhya gas **methane (CH₄)** hoti hai aur LPG mein **propane (C₃H₈)** aur butane jaisi gaasein. Ye gaasein petrol-diesel se zyada saafi se jalti hain: dhuan aur soot (PM) bahut kam aur sulphur lagbhag nahi. Ek tulna dekho.

**Example 2: CO₂, ek kilo CNG ya ek kilo petrol?**
CNG (methane): CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O. 1 mol CH₄ = 16 g deta hai 1 mol CO₂ = 44 g. To 1 kg CH₄ se 44 ÷ 16 = **2.75 kg CO₂**.
Petrol (octane maanein, Topic 3): 2C₈H₁₈ + 25O₂ → 16CO₂ + 18H₂O. 2 mol octane = 2 × 114 = 228 g deta hai 16 × 44 = 704 g CO₂. To 1 kg octane se 704 ÷ 228 = **3.09 kg CO₂**.
Yaani CNG thodi kam CO₂ deti hai (lagbhag 11% kam, per kilo). Dhyan do: ye tulna **ek kilo fuel** ki hai, barabar urja (energy) ki nahi; ek kilo methane jalne par ek kilo petrol se thodi zyada urja bhi deti hai, isliye barabar kaam ke hisaab se CNG ki CO₂ aur bhi thodi kam padti hai (vistaar aage ki class mein). Bada fayda saaf jalne, kam soot aur kam sulphur mein hai.

> **Jaanch:** 2.75 × 16 = 44 ✅ aur 3.09 × 228 ≈ 704 ✅.

**3. Electric vehicles (EV).** Bijli se chalne wali gaadi mein **exhaust pipe se koi dhuan nahi** nikalta, isliye shehar ki hawa mein sudhaar hota hai. Ek imandaar baat: asal fayda tab poora milta hai jab bijli khud **renewable** source se bane (solar, wind). Agar bijli koyle ke power plant se aati hai, to pradushan chimney tak chala jaata hai, isliye power plant ke upay (Part C) bhi utne hi zaroori hain.

**4. Behtar chulha.** Gaon ke ghar mein lakdi ya gobar ke chulhe ka dhuan andar ki hawa ko kharab karta hai (Topic 5). LPG ya biogas jaise saaf fuel aur **behtar chimney wale stove** isse bahut bachate hain.

## Part B: Catalytic converter (gaadi ka chhota safai kendra)

Petrol ya diesel engine ke exhaust mein teen cheezein khatarnak hoti hain: **carbon monoxide (CO)**, **nitrogen oxides (NO, NO₂)** aur **na jala hua fuel** (jaise octane ke bachey hue bhaag). Catalytic converter exhaust pipe ke beech mein lagta hai. Uske andar ek **honeycomb** (chhatte jaisi) ceramic ki sanrachna hoti hai, jis par **platinum, palladium aur rhodium** jaise dhaatuon ki bahut patli parat chadhi hoti hai. Ye dhaatuen **catalyst** hain (Chapter 7 mein dekha tha ki catalyst reaction tez karta hai aur khud khatam nahi hota). Honeycomb isliye taaki gas ko dhaatu ki bahut badi sataha mil sake.

```
   engine se garam exhaust
   (CO, NO, NO₂, na jala fuel)
              :
              v
   +--------------------------+
   I  # # # # # # # # # # #   I   honeycomb par platinum,
   I  # # # # # # # # # # #   I   palladium, rhodium ki
   I  # # # # # # # # # # #   I   patli parat (catalyst)
   +--------------------------+
              :
              v
   saafi ke baad nikalne wali gas
   (CO₂, N₂, H₂O ki bhaap)
   silencer ---> tail pipe ---> bahar
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Catalytic converter ki seedhi sketch: exhaust honeycomb se guzarta hai jahan catalyst par CO, nitrogen oxides aur na jala fuel kam zehreeli gaason (CO₂, N₂ aur paani ki bhaap) mein badal jaate hain. (Seedhi sketch, anupaat sahi nahi; asli converter ka bhaag kai parton ka hota hai.)

**Chemistry kya hoti hai.** Teen mukhya reaction (sab balanced):

| Samasya | Reaction | Natija |
|---|---|---|
| CO (zehreeli) | 2CO + O₂ → 2CO₂ | CO, CO₂ mein badli |
| NO (nitrogen oxide) | 2NO + 2CO → N₂ + 2CO₂ | NO se hawa ki hi gas N₂ bani, aur CO bhi hat gayi |
| NO₂ (nitrogen oxide) | 2NO₂ + 4CO → N₂ + 4CO₂ | NO₂ se N₂ bani |
| Na jala fuel (octane) | 2C₈H₁₈ + 25O₂ → 16CO₂ + 18H₂O | Poora jalkar CO₂ aur paani |

Ek khaas baat: nitrogen oxides ko **N₂** mein badalna bahut achha hai, kyunki N₂ wahi gas hai jo hawa ka lagbhag 78% hai (Chapter 2) aur bilkul harmless hai. Yahan ek tarah ki "reduction" bhi hoti hai (Chapter 7 Topic 5): nitrogen oxides apna oxygen kho dete hain.

**Example 3: CO ka CO₂ mein badalna.**
Maan lo ek gaadi ke exhaust mein kisi din 2.8 g CO aayi. 2CO + O₂ → 2CO₂.
CO ki moles = 2.8 ÷ 28 = **0.1 mol**. Utni hi mol CO₂ banegi: 0.1 mol, yaani 0.1 × 44 = **4.4 g CO₂**. Oxygen chahiye: 0.05 mol, yaani 0.05 × 32 = **1.6 g**.
Ek imandaar baat: CO ki jagah ab **CO₂** nikli, jo ek greenhouse gas hai (Topic 3). Converter **zehreeli** gaasein hatata hai, par **CO₂ kam nahi karta**. Isliye CO₂ ghatane ke liye fuel kam jalana aur renewable urja zaroori hai.

> **Jaanch:** Mass: 2.8 g CO + 1.6 g O₂ = 4.4 g aur 4.4 g CO₂ ✅ (dravyaman ka niyam, Chapter 4).

**Example 4: NO se N₂.**
Ek engine ne 60 g NO nikali. 2NO + 2CO → N₂ + 2CO₂. NO ki molar mass = 14 + 16 = 30 g.
Moles NO = 60 ÷ 30 = **2 mol**. Reaction se N₂ banegi 2 ÷ 2 = **1 mol**, yaani 1 × 28 = **28 g N₂**. CO ki zaroorat 2 mol, yaani 2 × 28 = **56 g**. CO₂ banegi 2 mol, yaani 2 × 44 = **88 g**.

> **Jaanch:** Pehle: 60 g NO + 56 g CO = 116 g. Baad mein: 28 g N₂ + 88 g CO₂ = 116 g ✅.

Do zaroori batein, ek surakshit chetavani: (1) converter tabhi achha kaam karta hai jab wo **kaafi garam** ho, isliye pehle kuch minute kaam kam hota hai. (2) Ye **sirf unleaded** (lead rahit) petrol ke saath chalta hai; lead catalyst ko "zeher" de deta hai, isliye Bharat mein lead wala petrol band kar diya gaya.

## Part C: Factory aur power plant (chimney ki safai)

Thermal power plant aur factory ki badi chimneys se SO₂ aur dhuan (dhool, rakh) nikalte hain. Inke liye do bade upay hote hain: **scrubber** aur **electrostatic precipitator**.

### Scrubber: lime ya limestone se SO₂ ko pakadna

SO₂ ek **acidic** gas hai (Topic 2: ye acid rain banati hai). Acid ko pakadne ke liye hum use ek **base jaisi** cheez se milate hain. Power plant mein chimney ki gas ko **chuna ya chuna-patthar (limestone, CaCO₃)** ke ghol ya paste se guzara jaata hai. Reaction (balanced):

CaCO₃ + SO₂ → CaSO₃ + CO₂

Hawa ke oxygen se CaSO₃ aage badal kar **CaSO₄** (calcium sulphate) ban jaata hai, jo **gypsum** jaisa safed, kam hanikarak solid hai (jise plaster aur cement mein kaam mein bhi liya jaata hai):

2CaSO₃ + O₂ → 2CaSO₄

Dono ko jodkar: 2CaCO₃ + 2SO₂ + O₂ → 2CaSO₄ + 2CO₂.

Ye reaction Topic 2 ke marble wale reaction jaisa hai (wahan bhi CaCO₃ sulphur wale acid se CaSO₄ banata tha, aur Topic 2 ke scrubber example mein yahi poori equation thi), bas yahan ye **chimney ke andar** jaan boojhkar hota hai, taaki SO₂ aasman tak jaaye hi nahi. Chuna (Ca(OH)₂) se bhi ye kaam hota hai: Ca(OH)₂ + SO₂ → CaSO₃ + H₂O.

**Example 5: Ek power plant ka hisaab.**
Ek thermal power plant roz **1000 tonne coal** jalata hai, jisme **1%** sulphur hai (illustrative).
Sulphur = 1000 × 0.01 = **10 tonne**. S + O₂ → SO₂ mein 32 g S se 64 g SO₂ banti hai, to SO₂ = 10 × 64 ÷ 32 = **20 tonne**.
Limestone ki zaroorat: CaCO₃ + SO₂ → CaSO₃ + CO₂ mein 64 g SO₂ ko 100 g CaCO₃ chahiye (molar masses: CaCO₃ = 40 + 12 + 48 = 100, SO₂ = 32 + 32 = 64). To 20 × 100 ÷ 64 = **31.25 tonne** limestone (maante hue ki limestone shuddh hai).
Poori reaction (2CaCO₃ + 2SO₂ + O₂ → 2CaSO₄ + 2CO₂) ke hisaab se CaSO₄ banegi 20 × 136 ÷ 64 = **42.5 tonne** (CaSO₄ ki molar mass 40 + 32 + 64 = 136).

> **Jaanch:** SO₂ ke 20 tonne = 20 ÷ 64 = 0.3125 (tonne-mol), aur limestone 0.3125 × 100 = 31.25 ✅, CaSO₄ 0.3125 × 136 = 42.5 ✅.

### Electrostatic precipitator (ESP): static bijli se dhool pakadna

Dhuein ke bahut chhote kan (PM) scrubber se nahi pakde jaate. ESP mein chimney ke andar ek **pura tar-jaal** laga hota hai jo kanon ko **bijli ka charge** deta hai (Physics ka static bijli ka vichar: ek jaise charge ek doosre ko door dhakelte hain, ulte charge khinchte hain). Charge wale kan, **ulte charge wali plates** ki taraf khinchkar chipak jaate hain. Kuch der baad plates ko hilakar rakh (fly ash) neeche ek hopper mein gira di jaati hai.

**Example 6: ESP ki kshamta.**
Ek ESP 99% kan pakadta hai. Agar chimney mein har ghante **100 kg** dhool aati hai, to nikalne wali dhool = 100 × (1 − 0.99) = **1 kg** har ghante. Pakdi gayi = **99 kg**.

> **Jaanch:** 99 + 1 = 100 ✅. (Bina ESP ke wahi 100 kg bahar jaati.)

Ek nazar mein: **scrubber gas ko pakadta hai** (SO₂), **ESP thos kanon ko pakadta hai** (dhool aur rakh). Dono saath lagte hain.

## Part D: Saaf urja, hydrogen aur ped

**Renewable energy.** Solar panel dhoop se bijli banata hai, wind turbine hawa se, hydro plant behte paani se. In mein **jalne ki koi reaction nahi** hoti: na SO₂, na soot, na (chalte waqt) CO₂. Isliye ye acid rain, smog aur global warming, **teeno** ki jad ko ek saath kam karte hain. (Panel aur turbine banane mein bhi urja lagti hai, par poori umar ke hisaab se wo fossil fuel se kaafi kam pradushan dete hain.)

**Green hydrogen.** Chapter 11 mein dekha tha ki hydrogen jalne par sirf paani deta hai: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O. Agar hydrogen **solar ya wind ki bijli** se paani todkar (electrolysis) banayi jaaye, to use **green hydrogen** kehte hain. Ye bhaari trucks, factories aur steel jaisi jagahon ke liye ek saaf fuel ki ummeed hai. Abhi ye mehngi hai aur technology badh rahi hai (approximate sthiti).

**Ped aur green belt.** Ped CO₂ lete hain, chhaya dete hain aur patton par dhool jamne se kuch dhool hawa se hat jaati hai. Lekin ek imandaar baat: ped **madad** karte hain, par jalaye gaye fuel ki CO₂ ko poori tarah akele sambhal nahi sakte. Shehar ya factory ke chaaron taraf **green belt** aur sadakon ke kinare ped lagana ek achha **sahaayak** upay hai, jo source par pradushan rokne ke saath-saath chalta hai.

**Kachra aur open burning.** Khule mein kachra ya fasal ka avshesh jalane se dhuan, PM2.5 aur CO nikalti hai (Topic 5). **Segregation** (geela, sookha, khatarnak alag), compost (geele kachre se khaad) aur recycling se burning ki zaroorat ghatati hai. Fasal ke avshesh ke liye machine se zameen mein milana, khaad banana ya bio-fuel mein badalna jaise vikalp hain.

**Public transport aur car-pooling.** Ek bus mein 40 log ek saath chalte hain, 40 alag cars mein nahi. Kam gaadiyan matlab kam exhaust, kam traffic jam aur kam fuel.

> **Figure 6.2** – "My pollution diary": roz ka safar, bijli ke ghante, kachra alag kiya ya nahi aur ek achha kaam likhne ki seedhi table (aankde sirf udaharan hain; apni asli entry likho).

**Diary se hisaab.** Shreyas ne apni diary mein dekha ki Shanivar ko usne car se **12 km** safar kiya. Car ka mileage lagbhag **15 km har litre** hai, to petrol = 12 ÷ 15 = **0.8 L**. Topic 3 se: 1 L petrol lagbhag 2.3 kg CO₂ deta hai (approximate), to CO₂ = 0.8 × 2.3 = **1.84 kg** (lagbhag). Agar wahi 12 km ke liye wo cycle ya bus lega to uski apni CO₂ lagbhag zero (cycle) ya bahut kam (bus mein baant jaati hai).

**Example 7: Bijli ki bachat.**
Shreyas ke ghar mein ek **60 W** ka purana bulb roz **5 ghante** jalta hai. Use **10 W** ke LED se badalne par bachat = (60 − 10) = 50 W = 0.05 kW, 0.05 × 5 = **0.25 kWh roz**. Ek saal (365 din) mein 0.25 × 365 = **91.25 kWh**.
Agar bijli koyle se bane aur har kWh par lagbhag **0.8 kg CO₂** nikle (ek maani hui, approximate sankhya), to bachat = 91.25 × 0.8 = **73 kg CO₂ saal mein**, sirf ek bulb badalne se.

> **Jaanch:** 50 W × 5 h = 250 Wh = 0.25 kWh ✅; 0.25 × 365 = 91.25 ✅; 91.25 × 0.8 = 73 ✅.

**Roz ki aadatein (ek nazar mein).**
- Paas ki doori paidal ya cycle se; door ke liye bus ya metro; car-pooling.
- Bijli: zaroorat na ho to light, fan, AC band; LED; energy-saving AC ka sahi temperature.
- Kachra: segregation, compost, kuch bhi khule mein nahi jalana.
- Gaadi ki time par service; lambi red light ya der tak rukna ho to engine band karo.
- Ped lagao aur unki dekhbhal karo.
- Bure AQI wale din (Topic 5) bahar kam jao, mask lagao.
- Doosron ko jaankari do: tumhari jaankari sabse badi taaqat hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Catalytic converter CO₂ ko khatam kar deta hai."**
Nahi. Ye CO, NO, NO₂ aur na jale fuel ko badalta hai, par kaam mein **CO₂ ban bhi jaati hai**. CO₂ kam karne ke liye fuel ki khapat ghatani padti hai.

**Galti 2: "EV matlab bilkul pradushan-mukt."**
Gaadi ke pipe se nahi, par bijli kaise bani ye bhi dekhna padta hai. Renewable bijli se chalne par fayda sabse zyada.

**Galti 3: "Ek insaan kuch nahi kar sakta."**
Example 7 mein ek bulb se saal ke 73 kg CO₂ ki bachat dikhi (approximate). Chhoti aadatein jodti hain, aur doosron ko bhi badalne ko prerit karti hain.

**Galti 4: "Ped lagana akela kaafi hai."**
Ped madad karte hain par pradushan ke **source** ko kam karna pehle aata hai.

**Galti 5: Scrubber aur ESP ko ek hi samajhna.**
Scrubber **gas** (SO₂) ko chemical se pakadta hai, ESP **thos kanon** ko static charge se.

**Galti 6: "Kachra jalana sabse aasan hai."**
Open burning dhuan aur PM2.5 deta hai. Segregation aur compost behtar.

**Galti 7: Prayog mein dhuan banana.**
Hum **diary** se, kagaz par seekhte hain: kabhi kuch jalakar pradushan "dikhane" ki koshish mat karo.

## Quick Recap

- Sabse achha upay: pradushan banne hi na do (saaf fuel, BS-VI, CNG, LPG, EV, saaf stove, kam fuel).
- **Catalytic converter:** CO → CO₂ (2CO + O₂ → 2CO₂), NO → N₂ (2NO + 2CO → N₂ + 2CO₂), na jala fuel → CO₂ aur paani. Platinum, palladium, rhodium catalyst hain. CO₂ ko ye kam nahi karta. Sirf unleaded petrol.
- **Scrubber:** limestone ya chuna se SO₂ pakadna: CaCO₃ + SO₂ → CaSO₃ + CO₂, phir 2CaSO₃ + O₂ → 2CaSO₄. **ESP:** static charge se dhool pakadta hai.
- **Renewable energy** aur **green hydrogen** (2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O) jalne se hone wale pradushan ko hi hata dete hain.
- Ped, green belt, segregation, compost, public transport: sab madad karte hain.
- **My pollution diary:** apni aadatein dekhna aur badalna; 1 L petrol lagbhag 2.3 kg CO₂ (approximate).
- Samasya bhi chemistry hai, hal bhi chemistry hai.

## Practice Questions

1. In a catalytic converter 5.6 g of carbon monoxide is converted by 2CO + O₂ → 2CO₂. Calculate the mass of CO₂ formed and the mass of oxygen used. (Molar masses: CO = 28, CO₂ = 44, O₂ = 32.)

2. A faulty engine releases 150 g of nitric oxide, NO. Using 2NO + 2CO → N₂ + 2CO₂, find the mass of nitrogen formed, the mass of CO that reacts and the mass of CO₂ formed. Show that the total mass is conserved.

3. In a converter, nitrogen dioxide is removed by 2NO₂ + 4CO → N₂ + 4CO₂. If 9.2 g of NO₂ (molar mass 46 g) reacts, find the masses of N₂ and CO₂ formed and of CO used.

4. A power plant burns 600 tonnes of coal per day containing 2.5% sulphur. (a) How many tonnes of SO₂ are formed? (b) How many tonnes of pure limestone are needed to capture it as CaSO₃ using CaCO₃ + SO₂ → CaSO₃ + CO₂? (c) How many tonnes of CaSO₄ would form if all the SO₂ is turned into CaSO₄? (Use S = 32, O = 16, Ca = 40, C = 12.)

5. Older diesel had about 350 ppm sulphur by mass; BS-VI diesel has about 10 ppm. For 500 kg of diesel, find the mass of sulphur in each case, the mass of SO₂ each would form on burning, and the percentage reduction in sulphur. (Treat the ppm values as approximate.)

6. An electrostatic precipitator removes 99.5% of dust. If 240 kg of dust per hour enters it, how much escapes through the chimney and how much is collected? Which principle from Physics does it use?

7. LPG is mainly propane, C₃H₈ (molar mass 44). Using C₃H₈ + 5O₂ → 3CO₂ + 4H₂O, how many kg of CO₂ does 1 kg of propane give? How does this compare with 2.75 kg for methane and 3.09 kg for octane per kg of fuel? Does a lower value always mean cleaner burning? Explain.

8. A hydrogen fuel cell bus burns 10 g of green hydrogen in oxygen (2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O). Find the mass of oxygen used and of water produced. Why is this fuel "clean" at the point of use, and what must be true for it to be "green"?

9. A family replaces a 75 W bulb by a 15 W LED. Each is used 4 hours a day for 30 days. Calculate the energy saved in kWh. Assuming about 0.8 kg of CO₂ per kWh from coal-based electricity (an assumed approximate value), find the CO₂ avoided.

10. A group of friends from the same colony travels 20 km to a match. Two cars each with a mileage of 16 km per litre would carry them in two separate cars; instead they go in one car of the same mileage. Using 2.3 kg of CO₂ per litre of petrol (approximate), calculate the petrol and CO₂ saved by the one-car trip.

11. A newspaper says "Catalytic converters have made cars harmless." Write two reasons why this statement is too strong. Also explain why a car using leaded petrol would damage its converter.

12. A tree absorbs about 22 kg of CO₂ in a year (a rough value; it varies a lot with type and age). Using 2.3 kg of CO₂ per litre of petrol, how many litres of petrol does that match? What other benefits do trees give besides absorbing CO₂, and why are they not a substitute for reducing emissions?

13. Match each problem with the most suitable solution and give a one-line reason: (a) SO₂ from a power plant; (b) fine dust in chimney smoke; (c) CO and nitrogen oxides in car exhaust; (d) PM2.5 from burning crop residue; (e) CO₂ from electricity generation.
    Solutions: renewable energy; electrostatic precipitator; scrubber with limestone; catalytic converter; collecting and using the residue instead of burning.

14. True or false (give a reason): (a) A scrubber and an electrostatic precipitator do the same job. (b) An electric car has no connection with air pollution. (c) Segregation of waste helps reduce open burning. (d) A catalyst is used up in a converter and must be refilled every week.

### Answers

1. 5.6 g CO = 5.6 ÷ 28 = **0.2 mol**. 2CO + O₂ → 2CO₂: utni hi mol CO₂ banegi, 0.2 × 44 = **8.8 g CO₂**. O₂ = 0.1 mol, 0.1 × 32 = **3.2 g**. Jaanch: 5.6 + 3.2 = 8.8 ✅.

2. NO ki molar mass 30 g, to 150 ÷ 30 = **5 mol**. Reaction mein 2 mol NO se 1 mol N₂ banti hai, to N₂ = 2.5 mol = 2.5 × 28 = **70 g**. CO = 5 mol = 5 × 28 = **140 g**. CO₂ = 5 mol = 5 × 44 = **220 g**. Jaanch: 150 + 140 = 290 aur 70 + 220 = 290 ✅.

3. 9.2 ÷ 46 = **0.2 mol** NO₂. 2NO₂ : 1N₂ : 4CO : 4CO₂. N₂ = 0.1 mol = 0.1 × 28 = **2.8 g**. CO = 0.4 mol = 0.4 × 28 = **11.2 g**. CO₂ = 0.4 mol = 0.4 × 44 = **17.6 g**. Jaanch: 9.2 + 11.2 = 20.4 aur 2.8 + 17.6 = 20.4 ✅.

4. (a) Sulphur = 600 × 0.025 = 15 tonne, SO₂ = 15 × 64 ÷ 32 = **30 tonne**. (b) Limestone = 30 × 100 ÷ 64 = **46.875 tonne**, lagbhag 46.9 tonne. (c) CaSO₄ = 30 × 136 ÷ 64 = **63.75 tonne**. Jaanch: 30 ÷ 64 = 0.46875 aur 0.46875 × 100 = 46.875 ✅; 0.46875 × 136 = 63.75 ✅.

5. Purani diesel: 500 kg × 350 ppm = 500 × 350 mg = 175000 mg = **175 g** sulphur, to SO₂ = 175 × 2 = **350 g**. BS-VI: 500 × 10 = 5000 mg = **5 g** sulphur, SO₂ = **10 g**. Kami = (350 − 10) ÷ 350 = (175 − 5) ÷ 175 = 170 ÷ 175 = **97.1%** (lagbhag, kyunki ppm approximate hain). Jaanch: 350 × 0.0286 ≈ 10 ✅.

6. Escape = 240 × (1 − 0.995) = 240 × 0.005 = **1.2 kg** har ghante; collected = 240 − 1.2 = **238.8 kg**. Principle: static bijli ke charge (ulte charge khinchte hain, ek jaise dhakelte hain). Jaanch: 1.2 + 238.8 = 240 ✅.

7. C₃H₈ (44 g) se 3 × 44 = 132 g CO₂ banti hai. 1 kg propane se 132 ÷ 44 = **3.0 kg CO₂**. Methane 2.75 < propane 3.0 < octane 3.09 (per kg). Nahi, kam CO₂ ka matlab hamesha saaf jalna nahi: saaf burning ka matlab dhuan aur soot ka kam hona bhi dekhna padta hai (aur CO, PM). Fuel ka asli fayda tab hai jab CO₂, PM, SO₂ aur CO sab kam hon. Jaanch: 3.0 × 44 = 132 ✅.

8. 10 g H₂ = 10 ÷ 2 = **5 mol**. 2H₂ : 1O₂ : 2H₂O to O₂ = 2.5 mol = 2.5 × 32 = **80 g**, H₂O = 5 mol = 5 × 18 = **90 g**. Jaanch: 10 + 80 = 90 ✅. Jalne ke samay sirf paani nikalta hai, CO₂, CO, SO₂ aur soot nahi, isliye "saaf". "Green" tabhi jab hydrogen renewable bijli (solar ya wind) se paani todkar bani ho (electrolysis).

9. Bachat = (75 − 15) = 60 W = 0.06 kW. Ghante = 4 × 30 = 120 h. Energy = 0.06 × 120 = **7.2 kWh**. CO₂ = 7.2 × 0.8 = **5.76 kg** (lagbhag, assumed). Jaanch: 60 × 120 = 7200 Wh = 7.2 kWh ✅.

10. Ek car 20 km: petrol = 20 ÷ 16 = **1.25 L**. Do alag cars: 2 × 1.25 = 2.5 L. Ek car: 1.25 L. Bachat = **1.25 L** petrol. CO₂ bachi = 1.25 × 2.3 = **2.875 kg** (lagbhag 2.9 kg, approximate). Jaanch: 1.25 × 2.3 = 2.875 ✅.

11. Kaaran: (1) converter CO₂ kam nahi karta, balki CO se CO₂ banti hai (greenhouse gas). (2) Wo sabhi zehreeli gaason ko poori tarah nahi hatata, aur kaafi garam hone par hi achha chalta hai. (3) Dhuan ke kan (PM) aur gaadi banane/chalane mein lagne wali urja ki baat alag hai. Leaded petrol mein lead ke kan catalyst ki sataha par jam jaate hain aur use "zeher" de dete hain (poison), isliye converter kaam karna band kar deta hai.

12. 22 ÷ 2.3 = 9.57, yaani lagbhag **9.6 L** petrol (approximate; Topic 3 wala 22 kg ka aankda). Ped chhaya dete hain, kuch dhool rokte hain, pakshiyon aur jeevon ko ghar dete hain, mitti bachate hain aur kai jagah barish mein madad karte hain. Par ek car ek saal mein saikdon litre petrol jala deti hai (jaise 15 km per L par 12000 km ke liye 800 L, yaani lagbhag 800 × 2.3 = 1840 kg CO₂, jiske liye lagbhag 84 ped chahiye), to ped akele sab CO₂ nahi sambhal sakte: pehle emissions kam karo, ped sahayak hain. Jaanch: 12000 ÷ 15 = 800 L ✅.

13. (a) SO₂: **scrubber with limestone**, kyunki CaCO₃ acidic SO₂ ko CaSO₃ aur phir CaSO₄ mein badal deta hai. (b) Fine dust: **electrostatic precipitator**, kyunki charged kan ulte charge wali plates par chipak jaate hain. (c) CO aur nitrogen oxides: **catalytic converter**, kyunki catalyst CO → CO₂ aur NO → N₂ badal deta hai. (d) PM2.5 from crop residue: **collecting and using the residue instead of burning** (khaad, bio-fuel, zameen mein milana). (e) CO₂ from electricity: **renewable energy**, kyunki solar, wind aur hydro mein jalne ki reaction nahi hoti.

14. (a) **False** ❌: scrubber gas (SO₂) ko chemical reaction se pakadta hai, ESP thos kanon ko static charge se. (b) **False** ❌: gaadi ke pipe se dhuan nahi nikalta, par bijli kaise bani ye bhi pradushan se judta hai. (c) **True** ✅: alag-alag kachre se compost aur recycling ho paati hai aur jalane ki zaroorat ghatati hai. (d) **False** ❌: catalyst reaction mein khatam nahi hota; bas lead jaise "zeher" se kharab ho sakta hai.
