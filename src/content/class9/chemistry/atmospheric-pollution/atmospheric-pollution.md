## Part A: Saaf hawa mein kya hota hai?

Chapter 2 mein humne dekha tha ki hawa ek **mixture** hai. Ab hum use thoda aur dhyan se, **volume ke hisaab se** dekhte hain. Neeche ke aankde **lagbhag (approximate)** hain, kyunki asli hawa mein jagah aur samay ke saath thoda fark hota hai.

| Gas | Lagbhag matra (volume ke hisaab se) |
|---|---|
| Nitrogen (N₂) | 78% |
| Oxygen (O₂) | 21% |
| Argon (Ar) | 0.93% |
| Carbon dioxide (CO₂) | lagbhag 0.04% (lagbhag 420 ppm) |
| Neon, helium, methane aur doosri gasein | bahut kam (traces) |
| Paani ki bhaap | badalti rehti hai: registan mein bahut kam, barsaat ke mausam mein zyada |

Ye table 100 hisson ki banti hai. Dekho, 100 chhote dibbe maan lo, har dibba hawa ka 1%.

```
   N N N N N N N N N N
   N N N N N N N N N N
   N N N N N N N N N N
   N N N N N N N N N N
   N N N N N N N N N N
   N N N N N N N N N N
   N N N N N N N N N N
   N N N N N N N N O O
   O O O O O O O O O O
   O O O O O O O O O A

   N = nitrogen (78 dibbe)    O = oxygen (21 dibbe)
   A = argon, CO₂ aur baaki sab (lagbhag 1 dibba)
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Hawa ka 100 dibbon ka model (volume ke hisaab se, lagbhag): 78 dibbe nitrogen, 21 dibbe oxygen aur lagbhag 1 dibba argon, carbon dioxide aur baaki gason ka. Paani ki bhaap yahan alag se nahi dikhayi gayi, kyunki wo badalti rehti hai.

Dhyan do: carbon dioxide sirf **0.04%** hai, yaani 100 dibbon mein ek dibbe ka bhi bahut chhota sa hissa. Phir bhi iska asar bahut bada hota hai (Topic 3 mein dekhenge). Isse ek zaroori baat samajh aati hai: **bahut thodi matra bhi hawa ke liye bahut maayne rakh sakti hai**. Isliye bahut chhoti matra ko naapne ke liye ek naya unit chahiye: **ppm**.

### ppm kya hai?

**1 ppm = 10 lakh (10,00,000) hisson mein 1 hissa.** Ye wahi hai jaise **percent** ka matlab 100 hisson mein kitne. Dono ka rishta:

- 1% = 1 ÷ 100 = 10,000 ÷ 10,00,000, yaani **1% = 10,000 ppm**.
- Isliye ppm ko percent mein badalne ke liye **10,000 se bhaag** do, aur percent ko ppm mein badalne ke liye **10,000 se gunna** karo.

**Example 1: CO₂ ke 420 ppm ko percent mein badlo.**
420 ÷ 10,000 = **0.042%**. Ye lagbhag 0.04% hi hai, jaisa table mein tha ✅.

**Example 2: Argon ke 0.93% ko ppm mein badlo.**
0.93 × 10,000 = **9300 ppm**.

**Example 3: "Baaki sab" ko ppm mein samjho.**
Nitrogen aur oxygen ke baad bacha: 100 − 78 − 21 = **1%**. Ye 1 × 10,000 = **10,000 ppm** hua. Isme argon 9300 ppm, aur CO₂ lagbhag 420 ppm. To neon, helium, methane aur baaki sab milkar: 10,000 − 9300 − 420 = **lagbhag 280 ppm**.

> **Jaanch:** 78 + 21 + 0.93 + 0.04 = 99.97, yaani lagbhag 100 aur bacha 0.03% (lagbhag 300 ppm) ✅. 280 ppm ki baat usi ke kareeb hai (aankde lagbhag).

**Example 4: Kamre mein kitni oxygen?**
Ek kamra 3 m × 4 m × 2.5 m ka hai. Aayatan = 3 × 4 × 2.5 = **30 m³**. 1 m³ = 1000 L (Water chapter ki tarah), to 30 m³ = 30,000 L. Oxygen 21% hai, to 30,000 × 21 ÷ 100 = **6300 L**. Aur nitrogen 78% = 30,000 × 0.78 = **23,400 L**.

> **Jaanch:** 6300 + 23,400 = 29,700 L, aur bacha 300 L (1%) ✅ (6300 + 23,400 + 300 = 30,000).

## Part B: Atmosphere ki parte

Hawa Earth ke chaaron taraf ek **moti parat** hai, par wo ek jaisi nahi hai. Oonchai badalne par uska pressure, temperature aur gun badalte hain. Hum atmosphere ko **paanch parton** mein baantte hain. Neeche ki oonchaiyaan **lagbhag (approximate)** hain.

```
   oonchai
   (lagbhag)

   600 km ke upar
   +----------------------------------+
   I  EXOSPHERE: bahut hi patli hawa, I
   I  space mein milti hui            I
   +----------------------------------+
   85 km se upar, lagbhag 600 km tak
   +----------------------------------+
   I  THERMOSPHERE: bahut patli hawa  I
   I  (satellite yahan ghoomte hain)  I
   +----------------------------------+
   50 km se 85 km
   +----------------------------------+
   I  MESOSPHERE: bahut thandi,       I
   I  ulkaayein yahan jalti dikhti    I
   +----------------------------------+
   12 km se 50 km
   +----------------------------------+
   I  STRATOSPHERE: OZONE LAYER       I
   I  (zyadatar 15 se 35 km ke beech) I
   +----------------------------------+
   0 se 12 km
   +----------------------------------+
   I  TROPOSPHERE: hum yahin rehte    I
   I  hain; baadal, baarish, mausam   I
   +----------------------------------+
   ======== Earth ki sathah ==========
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Atmosphere ki paanch parte (lagbhag oonchaiyaan, anupaat sahi nahi): troposphere (0 se 12 km), stratosphere (12 se 50 km, jisme ozone layer hai), mesosphere (50 se 85 km), thermosphere (85 km se lagbhag 600 km) aur sabse upar exosphere.

Kuch seedhi baatein:

- **Troposphere** mein hawa sabse ghani hai: poori hawa ka bahut bada hissa isi mein hai. Yahan oonchai badhne par **temperature girta hai** (isi liye pahaad thande hote hain). Mausam, baadal aur baarish yahin hote hain. Hamara jeevan aur lagbhag saara pradushan yahin hota hai. Hawai jahaaz lagbhag 10 se 12 km par chalte hain, yaani troposphere ke upari hisse mein.
- **Stratosphere** mein **ozone layer** hai (zyadatar lagbhag 15 se 35 km par). Wo Sun ki harmful UV kirnon ko rokti hai (Topic 4 mein).
- **Mesosphere** mein bahut thand hoti hai, aur aasmaan se girte patthar (ulkaayein) yahin jalte dikhte hain.
- **Thermosphere** aur **exosphere** mein hawa bahut patli ho jaati hai aur space mein dheere dheere ghul jaati hai.

Ye baat zaroori hai ki hum jis hawa mein saans lete hain aur jise ganda karte hain, wo **troposphere** hai. Hawa ka "garam hokar upar uthna" (convection) isi parat mein hota hai: [heat-and-temperature-part6.md](../../physics/heat-and-temperature/heat-and-temperature-part6.md). Is wajah se sardi ki dhundh ka raaz bhi isi se judta hai (Topic 5 mein).

> **Jaanch:** "Ozone layer troposphere mein hai." ❌ Nahi. Ozone layer **stratosphere** mein hai (zyadatar lagbhag 15 se 35 km par).

## Part C: Pradushan kya hai? Primary aur secondary

Hawa mein aisi kai cheezein hain jo **hamesha thodi matra mein hoti hain**, jaise CO₂. To pradushan ki sahi pehchan kya hai?

> **Pollutant** wo cheez hai jo hawa mein **galat jagah ya galat matra mein** aa gayi ho aur jeevon ya cheezon ko **nuksaan** pahunchaye.

Dekho, CO₂ saaf hawa ka hissa hai, par agar wo bahut badh jaye to dikkat. Aur CO (carbon monoxide) to saaf hawa mein hona hi nahi chahiye. Isliye pollutant ka matlab "hamesha zehar" nahi hai, balki **"nuksaan karne layak matra mein"**.

### Primary aur secondary pollutants

| Prakaar | Kaise banta hai | Udaharan |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary pollutant** | Seedhe kisi source se hawa mein aata hai | Carbon monoxide (CO), sulphur dioxide (SO₂), nitric oxide (NO), soot (dhuen ke kala kan) |
| **Secondary pollutant** | Hawa mein primary pollutants ke aapas mein ya sunlight se milne par banta hai | Zameen ke paas ka ozone, baarish mein ghula acid |

Ek seedhi tulna: **primary** ka matlab aapne kheer ke saath chini daali, to meetha **seedha** mila. **Secondary** ka matlab doodh, chini aur chawal pakane par bani kheer: kuch **naya** bana. Secondary pollutant hawa mein ban jaata hai, isliye uska source dhundhna kathin hota hai.

Dhyan do: **zameen ke paas ka (ground-level) ozone** ek secondary pollutant hai aur saans ke liye nuksaandeh hai. Ye wo achha ozone **nahi** hai jo stratosphere mein hamein UV se bachata hai. Dono ka formula O₃ hi hai (Chapter 3), par jagah alag hai, to asar bhi alag. Isse Topic 4 mein dekhenge.

## Part D: Pradushan kahan se aata hai?

Source do tarah ke hote hain: **prakritik (natural)** aur **insaani (human)**.

| Prakaar | Udaharan |
|---|---|
| **Prakritik** | Jwalamukhi ka dhuan aur raakh, dhool bhari aandhi, jungle ki aag |
| **Insaani** | Gaadiyon ka dhuan, thermal power plants (koyle se bijli), factories, parali jalana (fasal ka bacha hissa), nirman ki dhool, chulhe par khana, kachra jalana |

Prakritik sources hamesha se hain, par **insaani sources se aaj bahut zyada pradushan** hota hai, aur use ghataya ja sakta hai. Ye baat hamein aashavaadi rakhti hai: jo hamne badhaya, use hum kam bhi kar sakte hain.

Ek dhyan dene wali baat: kai sources ek hi cheez ko alag tarah se nikalte hain. Jaise **koyla ya petrol-diesel jalane par** CO₂, CO, SO₂, nitrogen ke oxides aur soot nikalte hain. Chapter 7 mein humne dekha tha ki **poora jalne** par CO₂ banti hai aur **adhoora jalne** par CO aur soot bhi banta hai: [chemical-changes-and-reactions-part6.md](../chemical-changes-and-reactions/chemical-changes-and-reactions-part6.md).

> **Jaanch:** "Pradushan sirf insaan ki wajah se hota hai." ❌ Nahi. Jwalamukhi, dhool bhari aandhi aur jungle ki aag jaise prakritik sources bhi hain. Par aaj ka zyada pradushan insaani sources se hai.

## Part E: Particulate matter: PM10 aur PM2.5

Dhundh mein jo cheez hum **dekh** sakte hain, uska bada hissa **particulate matter (PM)** hota hai: hawa mein tairte dhool, soot aur boondon ke bahut chhote kan. Inhe unki **chaudai (diameter)** se naapte hain, **micrometre (µm)** mein.

**1 micrometre = 1000 nanometre (nm) = 10⁻⁶ m.** Yaani 1 µm, 1 mm ka ek hazaarvan hissa hai.

- **PM10:** wo kan jinki chaudai **10 µm tak** hai.
- **PM2.5:** wo kan jinki chaudai **2.5 µm tak** hai. Ye PM10 ka hi ek **chhota** hissa hai.

Insaan ke baal ki chaudai lagbhag **70 µm** hoti hai (lagbhag, baal-baal mein fark hota hai). Dekho PM2.5 us se kitna chhota hai. Figure mein 1 chinha (#) = 2.5 µm maana gaya hai.

```
   Baal (70 µm):
   [############################]   28 chinha

   PM10 (10 µm):
   [####]                           4 chinha

   PM2.5 (2.5 µm):
   [#]                              1 chinha
```

> **Figure 1.3** – PM2.5 aur PM10 ki tulna insaan ke baal se (lagbhag): jahan baal ki chaudai 28 chinha hai, wahan PM10 sirf 4 chinha aur PM2.5 sirf 1 chinha hai. Yaani 28 PM2.5 kan ek ke baad ek rakhein to lagbhag ek baal ki chaudai banti hai. (Scale: 1 chinha = 2.5 µm.)

**Example 5: Baal PM2.5 se kitna chauda?**
70 ÷ 2.5 = **28 guna**. Yaani PM2.5 baal se lagbhag **28 guna** patla hai (lagbhag 30 guna kehna bhi theek hai).

**Example 6: Baal PM10 se kitna chauda?**
70 ÷ 10 = **7 guna**.

**Example 7: PM2.5 ki chaudai nanometre mein?**
2.5 µm × 1000 = **2500 nm**. Aur meter mein: 2.5 × 10⁻⁶ m.

> **Jaanch:** 28 × 2.5 = 70 ✅ aur 7 × 10 = 70 ✅.

Itne patle kan **tairte rehte hain**, bahut der tak hawa mein rehte hain aur saans ke saath **gehraai tak** phephdon mein pahunch sakte hain. Aise kan sirf aankh se alag-alag nahi dikhte, bas dhundh ki tarah dikhte hain. Kaise hote hain asar, wo Topic 5 mein dekhenge.

## Part F: AQI padhna

Ab Kshitij ke gate ke board par lautte hain. **AQI (Air Quality Index)** ek **number** hai jo hawa ki halat batata hai. Kai pradooshakon (PM2.5, PM10, ozone, SO₂, CO, aadi) ko naap kar sabse kharab wale se AQI banta hai. **Zyada number, zyada kharab hawa.** India ki categories neeche hain.

| AQI ki range | Category | Matlab (aam bhasha mein) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 se 50 | Good (achhi) | Saaf hawa, koi dikkat nahi |
| 51 se 100 | Satisfactory (santoshjanak) | Zyadatar log theek, bahut sensitive logon ko halki takleef |
| 101 se 200 | Moderate (madhyam) | Phephdon ya dil ke rogiyon, bachchon aur buzurgon ko takleef |
| 201 se 300 | Poor (kharab) | Zyada der bahar rehne par aam logon ko bhi takleef |
| 301 se 400 | Very Poor (bahut kharab) | Lambe samay tak bahar rehne par beemari ka khatra |
| 401 se 500 | Severe (gambhir) | Swasth logon par bhi asar; bahar ki gatividhiyan kam rakho |

**Example 8: Kshitij ke gate ka AQI 312 hai. Category?**
312, **301 se 400** ke beech hai, to category **Very Poor**.

**Example 9: Alag-alag readings.**
- AQI 56: 51 se 100 ke beech, **Satisfactory**.
- AQI 187: 101 se 200 ke beech, **Moderate**.
- AQI 265: 201 se 300 ke beech, **Poor**.
- AQI 412: 401 se 500 ke beech, **Severe**.

**Example 10: Kal 156 tha, aaj 312.**
312 ÷ 156 = **2 guna**. Hawa ka AQI **double** ho gaya, aur category **Moderate se Very Poor** ho gayi (156 Moderate mein hai, kyunki 101 se 200 ke beech hai). Dhyan do: AQI ek **index** hai, isliye "doguna AQI" ka matlab ye zaroori nahi ki pradooshan ki asli matra theek doguna hai. Bas itna ki halat kaafi bigdi.

> **Jaanch:** 156 × 2 = 312 ✅. Chhe categories ki range: 50 + 50 + 100 + 100 + 100 + 100 = 500 ✅ (0 se 500).

**Dhyan rakho:** categories ke beech ki seema par dhyan do. 200 **Moderate** hai, par 201 **Poor** ho jaata hai. 50 **Good** hai, 51 **Satisfactory**.

Ab Kshitij ko samajh aaya ki subah ki dhundh ka board kya kehta hai. Wadekar sir ne kaha: "Is board ko roz dekhne ki aadat daalo. AQI ke saath ye bhi dekho ki aaj bahar kitna rukna theek hai."

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Hawa mein sabse zyada oxygen hoti hai"**
Nahi. Sabse zyada **nitrogen** (78%) hai, oxygen sirf 21% hai.

**Galti 2: "CO₂ pradooshak hai, isliye saaf hawa mein bilkul nahi hoti"**
Nahi. Saaf hawa mein bhi CO₂ lagbhag 0.04% hoti hai. Dikkat tab hai jab matra aur badhti jaye (Topic 3).

**Galti 3: "ppm aur percent ek hi cheez hain"**
Dono ek hi cheez naapte hain (hissa), par 1% = 10,000 ppm.

**Galti 4: "PM2.5 baal se thoda chhota hota hai"**
PM2.5 baal se lagbhag 28 guna patla hota hai.

**Galti 5: "Ozone hamesha harmful hai"**
Stratosphere ka ozone hamein bachata hai. Zameen ke paas ka ozone nuksaandeh pollutant hai.

**Galti 6: "AQI ka bada number matlab achhi hawa"**
Ulta. Number jitna bada, hawa utni kharab.

**Galti 7: "Pradushan sirf factories se hota hai"**
Kai sources hain: gaadiyaan, power plants, parali, dhool, chulha, kachra jalana, aur prakritik sources bhi.

## Quick Recap

- Saaf hawa (volume): lagbhag 78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen, 0.93% argon, lagbhag 0.04% (420 ppm) CO₂, thode traces, aur badalti paani ki bhaap.
- 1% = 10,000 ppm. ppm se percent: 10,000 se bhaag; percent se ppm: 10,000 se gunna.
- Atmosphere ki parte: troposphere (lagbhag 12 km tak), stratosphere (lagbhag 50 km tak, ozone layer isme), mesosphere (lagbhag 85 km tak), thermosphere, exosphere.
- Pollutant: galat jagah ya galat matra mein aayi cheez jo nuksaan kare. Primary seedhe nikalta hai (CO, SO₂, NO, soot); secondary hawa mein banta hai (zameen ke paas ozone, acid).
- Sources: prakritik (jwalamukhi, dhool bhari aandhi, jungle ki aag) aur insaani (gaadiyaan, power plants, factories, parali, nirman dhool, chulha, kachra jalana).
- PM10 (10 µm tak) aur PM2.5 (2.5 µm tak). PM2.5 baal (lagbhag 70 µm) se lagbhag 28 guna patla. 1 µm = 1000 nm = 10⁻⁶ m.
- AQI: 0 se 50 Good, 51 se 100 Satisfactory, 101 se 200 Moderate, 201 se 300 Poor, 301 se 400 Very Poor, 401 se 500 Severe.

## Practice Questions

1. Helium makes up about 5 ppm of air and methane about 1.9 ppm (approximate values). Express each as a percentage.

2. A classroom measures 10 m × 6 m × 3 m. Using the air composition of this topic, find (a) the volume of the room in m³, (b) the volume of oxygen in it in m³ and in litres, and (c) the volume of nitrogen in m³.

3. A dust particle is 5 µm across and another is 0.5 µm across. How many times thinner than a 70 µm hair is each? Is each particle counted as PM10, PM2.5, or both?

4. Name the AQI category for each reading: 45, 95, 150, 200, 201, 320, 455.

5. Say whether each pollutant is primary or secondary and give a reason: (a) carbon monoxide from a scooter, (b) soot from a diesel truck, (c) ground-level ozone formed in sunlight from vehicle exhaust, (d) sulphur dioxide from a power plant chimney.

6. In which layer of the atmosphere would you find: (a) a passenger plane at 10 km, (b) the top of Mount Everest at about 8.8 km, (c) the densest part of the ozone layer at about 25 km, (d) a shooting star burning at about 70 km?

7. Classify each as a natural or a human source of air pollution: (a) a forest fire, (b) a brick kiln, (c) a volcano, (d) a coal-based power plant, (e) a dust storm, (f) a burning garbage heap.

8. State True or False and correct the false ones:
    (a) Air is about 21% nitrogen.
    (b) 1% equals 10,000 ppm.
    (c) An AQI of 120 is in the Poor category.
    (d) Weather such as rain happens mainly in the troposphere.

9. In a city, the AQI rises from 180 on Monday to 360 on Wednesday. By what factor did it change, and in which categories were the two days? What simple precaution would you take on Wednesday?

10. Convert: (a) 1 µm to nm, (b) 0.1 µm to nm, (c) 4 µm to m (in powers of ten), (d) 25 µm to mm.

11. In a closed, crowded classroom the CO₂ level rises to about 1200 ppm. How many litres of carbon dioxide are there in 1 m³ of this air? Give the answer in litres and in millilitres, and as a percentage.

### Answers

1. Percent = ppm ÷ 10,000. Helium: 5 ÷ 10,000 = **0.0005%**. Methane: 1.9 ÷ 10,000 = **0.00019%**. Jaanch: 0.0005 × 10,000 = 5 ✅ aur 0.00019 × 10,000 = 1.9 ✅.

2. (a) Volume = 10 × 6 × 3 = **180 m³**. (b) Oxygen 21%: 180 × 21 ÷ 100 = **37.8 m³**. Litre mein: 37.8 × 1000 = **37,800 L**. (c) Nitrogen 78%: 180 × 78 ÷ 100 = **140.4 m³**. Jaanch: 37.8 + 140.4 = 178.2 m³ aur bacha 1.8 m³ (1% of 180) ✅.

3. 70 ÷ 5 = **14 guna** patla, aur 70 ÷ 0.5 = **140 guna** patla. 5 µm wala kan 10 µm se chhota hai, par 2.5 µm se bada, to wo sirf **PM10** mein gina jaata hai. 0.5 µm wala kan 2.5 µm se bhi chhota hai, to wo **PM2.5 aur PM10 dono** mein aata hai. Jaanch: 14 × 5 = 70 ✅ aur 140 × 0.5 = 70 ✅.

4. 45: **Good** (0 se 50). 95: **Satisfactory** (51 se 100). 150: **Moderate** (101 se 200). 200: **Moderate** (upari seema). 201: **Poor** (201 se 300). 320: **Very Poor** (301 se 400). 455: **Severe** (401 se 500).
