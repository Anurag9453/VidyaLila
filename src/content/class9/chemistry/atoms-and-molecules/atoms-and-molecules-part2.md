# Topic 2: Fixed Hisaab: Law of Constant Proportions

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki reaction mein **kul mass wahi rehta hai**: kuch gayab nahi hota. Ab ek naya sawaal. Maan lo kisi compound mein do tatva hain. Kya wo **kisi bhi matra mein** mil sakte hain, ya unka koi **fixed hisaab** hota hai? Chapter 2 mein humne bataya tha ki paani mein hydrogen aur oxygen mass mein **1 : 8** ke ratio mein hote hain, aur us waqt ye bhi kaha tha ki iska poora niyam is chapter mein aayega. Aaj wahi niyam hai: **Law of Constant Proportions**.

---

## Pahaad ki dhaara aur ghar ka nal

Mr. Bakshi apne bhanje Dhairya ke saath Himalaya ke ek chhote trek par gaye the. Subah ek thandi, saaf dhaara ke paas unhone apni bottle bhari. Dhairya ne ek ghoont piya aur bola, "Mamu, ye paani to ghar ke nal wale paani se bilkul alag lagta hai. Kitna meetha aur thanda! Kya iska hydrogen aur oxygen ka hisaab bhi alag hoga? Shayad pahaad ke paani mein oxygen zyada ho!"

Mr. Bakshi muskuraye. "Swaad, thandak aur saaf ya ghula hua paani alag ho sakta hai, kyunki dhaara ke paani mein kuch ghule hue khanij ya mitti ke kan ho sakte hain. Par agar hum **sirf paani** lein, yaani H₂O, to wo kahin ka bhi ho, uska hisaab ek hi hota hai. Kareeb 1799 ke aas-paas ek vaigyanik ne is par bahut lagan se kaam kiya tha."

"Wo kaun?" Dhairya ne poocha.

"Joseph Proust, ek French chemist. Wo kai saal tak alag-alag jagah ke compounds ko tolta raha, aur usne dekha ki ek hi compound chahe kahin se aaye, kaise bhi bane, **uske tatvon ka mass ratio hamesha wahi** nikalta hai. Chal, hum bhi gin kar dekhte hain."

## Part A: Proust ka niyam

**Law of Constant Proportions** kehta hai:

> Kisi bhi **shuddh compound** mein uske tatva hamesha ek **fixed mass ratio** mein hote hain, chahe compound kahin se liya gaya ho ya kisi bhi tareeke se banaya gaya ho.

Ek tarah se har compound ka ek apna **nuskha** hota hai, bilkul kheer ki recipe jaisa (Chapter 3 mein dekha tha), bas yahan nuskha **mass** mein hai. Aao teen alag sources ka paani lekar jaanchein.

**Teen alag source, ek hi ratio.** Maan lo teen jagah ke paani ko hydrogen aur oxygen mein todkar alag-alag tola gaya (ye ek soch wala prayog hai, ghar ka kaam nahi):

| Source | Paani ka mass | Hydrogen | Oxygen | H : O |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ghar ka nal | 36 g | 4 g | 32 g | 4 : 32 = 1 : 8 |
| Himalaya ki dhaara | 27 g | 3 g | 24 g | 3 : 24 = 1 : 8 |
| Hydrogen jala kar banaya | 45 g | 5 g | 40 g | 5 : 40 = 1 : 8 |

Teeno mein ek hi ratio nikla: **1 : 8**. Dhairya ka dar (ki pahaad ke paani mein oxygen zyada hoga) galat tha.

```
   Ghar ka nal       Himalaya ki dhaara     Hydrogen jala kar
   36 g paani        27 g paani             45 g paani

   H = 4 g           H = 3 g                H = 5 g
   O = 32 g          O = 24 g               O = 40 g

   H : O = 1 : 8     H : O = 1 : 8          H : O = 1 : 8
        \                 |                  /
         \                |                 /
          +------>   ek hi ratio: 1 : 8   <------+
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Teen alag source (nal, himalayan dhaara, hydrogen jala kar banaya paani) ka mass alag hai, par hydrogen aur oxygen ka ratio teeno mein ek hi hai: 1 : 8.

Jaanch: 4 + 32 = 36 ✅. 3 + 24 = 27 ✅. 5 + 40 = 45 ✅. Aur teeno mein O ÷ H = 8 ✅

Dhyan do: **mass alag-alag ho sakta hai** (36 g, 27 g, 45 g), par **ratio nahi badalta**. Paani ki matra badhane se H aur O ek hi guna badhte hain, jaise 5 guna kheer mein chawal, doodh, cheeni teeno 5 guna.

## Part B: Ratio kaise nikalte hain

**Steps:**

1. Dono tatvon ke mass likho (grams mein).
2. Chhote mass se dono ko baant do (ya dono ko common factor se baant do) taaki ek sankhya 1 ho jaaye ya sabse saral whole numbers aa jaayein.
3. Ratio ko "A : B" likho.
4. Jaanch: dono mass ka jod kul mass ke barabar ho (Topic 1 ka niyam!).

### Example 1. Carbon dioxide

12 g carbon ko oxygen mein poora jala kar 44 g carbon dioxide banti hai. Carbon aur oxygen ka ratio kya hai?

- Carbon = 12 g. Oxygen = 44 − 12 = 32 g (Topic 1 ki tarah, kul mass se minus karke).
- C : O = 12 : 32. Dono ko 4 se baanto: **3 : 8**.

Jaanch: 12 + 32 = 44 ✅. 12 ÷ 4 = 3 aur 32 ÷ 4 = 8 ✅

Chapter 2 mein jo "carbon dioxide mein C : O = 3 : 8" bataya tha, wo yahi hai.

### Example 2. Ammonia

Ammonia gas ke 17 g mein 14 g nitrogen hai. Hydrogen kitna hai, aur N : H ka ratio kya hai?

- Hydrogen = 17 − 14 = 3 g.
- N : H = 14 : 3. Aur kuch baanta nahi (14 aur 3 mein koi common factor nahi).

Jaanch: 14 + 3 = 17 ✅. **N : H = 14 : 3**

### Example 3. Namak (NaCl)

Namak ke 11.7 g mein 4.6 g sodium aur 7.1 g chlorine hai. Ratio nikalo.

- Jaanch pehle: 4.6 + 7.1 = 11.7 ✅
- Na : Cl = 4.6 : 7.1. Dono ko 10 se guna karein to 46 : 71 (aur ye aage saral nahi hota).

Ab isse bhi mass percent nikal sakte hain: Na = 4.6 ÷ 11.7 × 100 ≈ **39.3%**. Cl = 7.1 ÷ 11.7 × 100 ≈ **60.7%**. Ye wahi 39.3% hai jo Chapter 2 mein namak ke sodium ke liye bataya tha. Percentage ka poora kaam Topic 7 mein hoga.

(Is chapter mein hum atoms ka mass in numbers mein rounded lete hain: Na = 23, Cl = 35.5. Dekho 23 : 35.5 bhi 46 : 71 ke barabar hai ✅. Ye kyun hota hai, wo Topic 4 mein.)

## Part C: Ratio se gum hua mass nikalna

Jab ek baar ratio pata chal gaya, to wo **kisi bhi matra** ke liye kaam karta hai. Tarika wahi: **ratio ke parts jodo, phir kul mass ko usi hisaab se baanto.**

### Example 4. Paani ke 9 g mein kitna H aur O?

H : O = 1 : 8, to total parts = 1 + 8 = 9.

- Hydrogen = 9 × (1/9) = **1 g**
- Oxygen = 9 × (8/9) = **8 g**

Jaanch: 1 + 8 = 9 ✅. 8 ÷ 1 = 8 ✅

### Example 5. Paani ke 72 g mein?

Parts 9 wahi hain. Ek part = 72 ÷ 9 = 8 g.

- Hydrogen = 1 × 8 = **8 g**
- Oxygen = 8 × 8 = **64 g**

Jaanch: 8 + 64 = 72 ✅. 64 ÷ 8 = 8 ✅

### Example 6. 40 g carbon dioxide mein carbon aur oxygen?

C : O = 3 : 8, parts = 3 + 8 = 11. Ek part = 40 ÷ 11 ≈ 3.636 g.

- Carbon = 3 × 40/11 ≈ **10.9 g**
- Oxygen = 8 × 40/11 ≈ **29.1 g**

Jaanch: 10.9 + 29.1 = 40 ✅. 29.1 ÷ 10.9 ≈ 2.67 ≈ 8/3 ✅

### Example 7. Ammonia 3.4 g mein?

N : H = 14 : 3, parts = 17. Ek part = 3.4 ÷ 17 = 0.2 g.

- Nitrogen = 14 × 0.2 = **2.8 g**
- Hydrogen = 3 × 0.2 = **0.6 g**

Jaanch: 2.8 + 0.6 = 3.4 ✅. 2.8 ÷ 0.6 ≈ 4.67 = 14/3 ✅

## Part D: Ek tatva diya ho, doosra kitna lagega?

### Example 8. 6 g carbon ko poori tarah jalane ke liye kitna oxygen aur kitni CO₂?

C : O = 3 : 8. Carbon 6 g = 3 ke 2 guna (6 = 3 × 2). Oxygen bhi 2 guna: 8 × 2 = **16 g**. Carbon dioxide = 6 + 16 = **22 g**.

Jaanch: 6 : 16 = 3 : 8 ✅. 6 + 16 = 22 ✅

### Example 9. 5 g hydrogen jalane ke liye kitna oxygen, aur kitna paani banega?

H : O = 1 : 8. 5 g hydrogen ke liye oxygen = 5 × 8 = **40 g**. Paani = 5 + 40 = **45 g**.

Jaanch: 5 : 40 = 1 : 8 ✅. 5 + 40 = 45 ✅ (yahi Himalaya wale table mein teesri row thi).

### Example 10. Agar ek tatva zyada de diya jaaye?

Maan lo 4 g hydrogen aur 16 g oxygen milaye. Bana hua paani phir bhi 1 : 8 ka hi hoga (2 g hydrogen + 16 g oxygen = 18 g paani); jo hydrogen zyada tha (2 g), wo bas bina react kiye pada reh jaata hai. Ratio **zabardasti nahi badalta**.

### Example 11. Kya ye paani ka sahi sample hai?

Ek student kehta hai ki usne 27 g ka ek shuddh paani ka sample tola, usme 3 g hydrogen aur 24 g oxygen mile. Kya ye paani ho sakta hai?

- H : O = 3 : 24 = 1 : 8 ✅. Haan, ye shuddh paani ka ratio hai.

Ab doosra student: "mere 23 g ke sample mein 3 g hydrogen aur 20 g oxygen hai."

- H : O = 3 : 20, jo 1 : 8 nahi hai (3 g hydrogen ke saath 24 g oxygen chahiye thi).
- Matlab ya to wo **shuddh paani nahi**, ya fir kisi doosre compound ka sample hai. (Hydrogen aur oxygen se ek aur compound bhi banta hai, hydrogen peroxide H₂O₂, jisme ratio 1 : 16 hota hai. Wo paani se bilkul alag cheez hai.)

Jaanch: 3 + 20 = 23 ✅ (mass to mil gaya, par ratio nahi mila).

## Part E: Compound versus mixture

Constant proportions sirf **compound** ke liye sahi hai, **mixture** ke liye nahi. Chapter 2 mein humne dekha tha ki mixture mein cheezein **kisi bhi ratio** mein mil sakti hain.

| | Compound (paani, CO₂, NaCl) | Mixture (cheeni ka paani, namak-ret) |
|---|---|---|
| Ratio | Fixed hota hai | Kuch bhi ho sakta hai |
| Example | H : O hamesha 1 : 8 | 5 g ya 50 g cheeni ek glass paani mein |
| Alag karna | Rasayanik tareeke se | Aasaan bhautik tareeke se |
| Gun | Naye gun, tatvon se alag | Dono ke gun mile-jule |

Dekho:

```
   COMPOUND: paani                      MIXTURE: cheeni ka paani

   H : O = 1 : 8                        1 glass paani + 1 chammach cheeni
   H : O = 1 : 8                        1 glass paani + 3 chammach cheeni
   H : O = 1 : 8                        1 glass paani + 5 chammach cheeni

   Ratio har baar wahi.                 Ratio har baar alag, phir bhi
                                        sab "cheeni ka paani" hain.
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Left: paani ek compound hai, to H : O ka ratio hamesha ek jaisa hai. Right: cheeni ka paani ek mixture hai, to cheeni aur paani ka ratio jitna chaho utna badla ja sakta hai.

Iska matlab: **agar koi cheez kisi bhi ratio mein ban sakti hai, to wo mixture hai.** Aur agar uska ek fixed ratio hai (kisi bhi source se), to wo shuddh compound hai.

Ek safe activity ghar par (adult ke saath, saaf rasoi ki cheeni aur saaf paani): ek glass paani mein 1 chammach cheeni ghol kar chakho, phir doosre glass mein 3 chammach. Dono meethe lagenge, par alag-alag meethe. Cheeni ka paani kisi bhi ratio mein ban sakta hai, isliye wo mixture hai. Par kisi bhi tareeke se banaye gaye paani mein hydrogen aur oxygen ka ratio nahi badalta.

## Part F: Ratio aata kahan se hai?

Itna fixed ratio kyun? Iska jawab **atoms** mein hai. Chapter 3 mein dekha tha ki paani ka formula H₂O hai: har molecule mein **2 hydrogen atom aur 1 oxygen atom**. Aur har tatva ke atom ka apna ek fixed bhaar hota hai. Atoms ki ginti fixed hai, har atom ka bhaar fixed hai, to total ka ratio bhi fixed hoga.

Ek tarah ki jhalak (rounded atomic masses: H = 1, O = 16):

- 2 hydrogen atoms ka bhaar = 2 × 1 = 2 units
- 1 oxygen atom ka bhaar = 1 × 16 = 16 units
- Ratio = 2 : 16 = **1 : 8** ✅

Yahi wo 1 : 8 hai jo humne Part A mein tola tha! Isi tarah carbon dioxide CO₂ mein: C = 12 aur O₂ = 2 × 16 = 32, to 12 : 32 = **3 : 8** ✅. Ammonia NH₃ mein: N = 14 aur H₃ = 3 × 1 = 3, to **14 : 3** ✅.

```
   H₂O ka ek molecule:

        O (16 units)
       / \
      H   H   (1 unit + 1 unit = 2 units)

   H : O = 2 : 16 = 1 : 8   (mass mein)
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Paani ke ek molecule mein 2 H aur 1 O hain. Mass mein ratio 2 : 16 = 1 : 8 aata hai, aur har molecule ka yahi hisaab hai, isliye bahut saare molecules ka bhi yahi ratio rahega.

(Atoms ke bhaar ko theek se kaise napte aur tulna karte hain, ye Topic 4 mein aayega. Abhi bas samjho ki fixed formula + fixed bhaar = fixed mass ratio.)

## Part G: Ek aur niyam, sirf naam

Paani (H₂O) mein H : O = 1 : 8 hai, par hydrogen aur oxygen ka ek aur compound hydrogen peroxide (H₂O₂) hai, jisme 1 : 16 hota hai. Yaani **wahi do tatva alag-alag compounds bana sakte hain, aur har compound ka apna fixed ratio hota hai**. Ye sambandh **Law of Multiple Proportions** kehlata hai. Iska naam yaad rakho, iski poori kahani **aage ki class mein** aayegi.

---

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Mass ko ratio samajhna**

"4 g hydrogen" ek mass hai, "1 : 8" ek ratio hai. Dono alag baatein hain. 36 g paani mein hydrogen 4 g hai, par ratio 4 : 32 nahi, saral karke **1 : 8** likhte hain.

**Galti 2: Ratio ko saral karna bhool jaana**

12 : 32 ko aise hi chhod dena theek nahi, ise 3 : 8 likhna chahiye (dono ko 4 se bhaag karke).

**Galti 3: Parts ka jod bhool jaana**

9 g paani mein hydrogen nikalte waqt 9 × (1/8) likhna galat hai, kyunki 8 to sirf oxygen ka hissa hai. Sahi: parts jodo (1 + 8 = 9) aur 9 se bhaag karo.

**Galti 4: Mixture par bhi niyam laga dena**

Cheeni ke paani mein koi fixed ratio nahi. Constant proportions sirf shuddh compounds ke liye hai.

**Galti 5: Ek tatva zyada dene par ratio badal jaana samajhna**

Agar 4 g hydrogen aur 16 g oxygen mile, to paani ka ratio phir bhi 1 : 8 rahega; extra hydrogen alag bacha rahega (Example 10).

**Galti 6: Do alag compounds ko ek samajh lena**

H₂O (1 : 8) aur H₂O₂ (1 : 16) mein tatva wahi hain par compounds alag hain, isliye ratio bhi alag.

## Quick Recap

- **Law of Constant Proportions** (Proust): kisi bhi shuddh compound mein tatva hamesha ek fixed mass ratio mein hote hain, chahe wo kahin se aaya ho ya kaise bhi bana ho.
- Paani: **H : O = 1 : 8**. Carbon dioxide: **C : O = 3 : 8**. Ammonia: **N : H = 14 : 3**. Namak: **Na lagbhag 39.3%**, Cl lagbhag 60.7%.
- Ratio nikalne ke liye: mass likho, saral karo, aur check karo ki dono ka jod kul mass ke barabar hai.
- Missing mass nikalne ke liye: ratio ke parts jodo, kul mass ko parts ke jod se baanto, phir har tatva ke parts se guna karo.
- Mixture mein koi fixed ratio nahi hota; compound mein hota hai.
- Fixed ratio ka kaaran: formula mein atoms ki fixed ginti aur har atom ka fixed bhaar (Topic 4 mein poora).
- **Law of Multiple Proportions**: sirf naam, aage ki class mein.

## Practice Questions

1. State the Law of Constant Proportions in your own words. Who proposed it?

2. A sample of pure water from a river has 6 g of hydrogen and 48 g of oxygen. Find the mass ratio H : O and the mass of the sample. Is this consistent with the law?

3. 24 g of carbon is completely burnt in oxygen to give 88 g of carbon dioxide. Find the mass of oxygen used and the C : O ratio.

4. How much hydrogen and oxygen are present in 54 g of water?

5. How much carbon and oxygen are present in 66 g of carbon dioxide?

6. In 34 g of ammonia, find the mass of nitrogen and hydrogen (N : H = 14 : 3).

7. How much oxygen is needed to burn 10 g of hydrogen completely, and how much water forms?

8. 8 g of hydrogen is mixed with 16 g of oxygen. The water formed still has H : O = 1 : 8 by mass. How much water forms, and how much hydrogen simply stays unreacted?

9. Two samples of a "pure compound of hydrogen and oxygen" give H : O = 1 : 8 and 1 : 16. Can both be water? Explain.

10. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) The ratio of hydrogen to oxygen in water depends on where the water comes from.
    (b) Sugar solution always has the same sugar to water ratio.
    (c) The Law of Constant Proportions holds for pure compounds.
    (d) The mass of carbon in 44 g of CO₂ is 32 g.

### Answers

1. **Law of Constant Proportions:** kisi bhi shuddh compound mein tatva hamesha ek fixed mass ratio mein hote hain, chahe wo kahin se liya gaya ho ya kisi bhi tareeke se bana ho. Ise **Joseph Proust** ne diya tha.

2. H : O = 6 : 48 = **1 : 8**. Sample ka mass = 6 + 48 = **54 g**. Jaanch: 6 + 48 = 54 ✅. Ratio 1 : 8 hai, to ye niyam ke anusaar sahi hai ✅.

3. Oxygen = 88 − 24 = **64 g**. C : O = 24 : 64, dono ko 8 se baanto: **3 : 8**. Jaanch: 24 + 64 = 88 ✅. 24 ÷ 8 = 3, 64 ÷ 8 = 8 ✅

4. Parts = 1 + 8 = 9. Ek part = 54 ÷ 9 = 6 g. Hydrogen = **6 g**, Oxygen = 8 × 6 = **48 g**. Jaanch: 6 + 48 = 54 ✅

5. C : O = 3 : 8, parts = 11, ek part = 66 ÷ 11 = 6 g. Carbon = 3 × 6 = **18 g**, Oxygen = 8 × 6 = **48 g**. Jaanch: 18 + 48 = 66 ✅. 18 : 48 = 3 : 8 ✅

6. Parts = 14 + 3 = 17, ek part = 34 ÷ 17 = 2 g. Nitrogen = 14 × 2 = **28 g**, Hydrogen = 3 × 2 = **6 g**. Jaanch: 28 + 6 = 34 ✅. 28 : 6 = 14 : 3 ✅

7. Oxygen = 10 × 8 = **80 g**. Paani = 10 + 80 = **90 g**. Jaanch: 80 ÷ 10 = 8 ✅. 10 + 80 = 90 ✅

8. 16 g oxygen ko 16 ÷ 8 = 2 g hydrogen chahiye. To paani = 2 + 16 = **18 g**. Hydrogen bacha = 8 − 2 = **6 g**. Jaanch: 18 g paani mein H : O = 2 : 16 = 1 : 8 ✅. Pehle total = 8 + 16 = 24 g; baad mein 18 + 6 = 24 ✅.

9. Nahi, dono paani nahi ho sakte. Paani mein H : O hamesha 1 : 8 hota hai. Jis sample mein 1 : 16 hai wo ek alag compound hai (jaise hydrogen peroxide H₂O₂), kyunki ek compound ka ratio fixed hota hai. Ye Law of Constant Proportions ka seedha use hai.

10. (a) **False** ❌. Paani ka ratio hamesha 1 : 8 hota hai, kahin se bhi aaye. (b) **False** ❌. Cheeni ka paani mixture hai, usme ratio kuch bhi ho sakta hai. (c) **True** ✅. (d) **False** ❌. CO₂ ke 44 g mein carbon 12 g hai aur oxygen 32 g hai (3 : 8 ratio, 44 ÷ 11 = 4, to C = 12, O = 32).
