# Topic 3: Dalton Ka Parmanu Siddhant

Pichhle do topics mein humne do pakke niyam dekhe. Pehla: **chemical reaction mein kul dravyaman nahi badalta** (Conservation of Mass). Doosra: **ek shuddh yaugik mein tatva hamesha ek fixed dravyaman ratio mein hote hain**, chahe wo kahin se aaya ho (Constant Proportions). Dono niyam bilkul sach nikle, lekin ek sawaal abhi adhoora hai: **aisa hota kyun hai?** Kya koi ek simple tasveer hai jo dono niyamon ko ek saath samjha de? Is topic mein hum wahi tasveer dekhenge: **parmanu (atom) ka siddhant**.

## Barfi ki thali aur "kahan ruke?" ka sawaal

Mr. Sengupta apni bhanji Kimaya ko lekar mohalle ki mithai ki dukaan par gaye. Halwai ne abhi-abhi kaaju barfi ki bhari-poori thali jamayi thi. Kimaya ne dekha ki halwai chaaku se pehle thali ko aadha kaat raha hai, phir har aadhe ko aur aadha, phir aur aadha.

"Mama," Kimaya ne poochha, "agar ye aise hi kaat-ta rahe, to kya kabhi rukega?"

Mr. Sengupta muskuraye. "Socho. Thali ke tukde bane, phir bade tukde, phir chhote tukde. Ab maan lo ek bahut tez aur bahut patla chaaku hai. Barfi ke tukde ko hum itna chhota kar dein ki ek **kan** reh jaaye. Do raaste hain: ya to tukde hamesha aur chhote hote jaayenge, kabhi ruk nahi payenge, ya phir kahin ek **sabse chhota tukda** aayega jise aur kaata hi nahi ja sakta."

Kimaya ne aankhein chhoti ki. "Ye to sirf sochne ki baat hai, mama. Hum itna chhota kaat to sakte nahi."

"Bilkul," Mr. Sengupta ne kaha, "aur isi sawaal par hazaaron saal pehle insaan sochne lage the. Unke paas aaj jaise microscope nahi the, par unke paas **dimaag** tha. Aaj hum wahi kahani sunenge."

```
   Poori thali     Aadha           Chauthayi         Chhote tukde      Sabse chhota
   +---------+     +----+----+     +----+----+       +-+-+-+-+-+
   :         :     :    :    :     :    :    :       +-+-+-+-+-+       +-+
   :         : --> :    :    : --> +----+----+  -->  +-+-+-+-+-+  -->  +-+
   :         :     :    :    :     :    :    :       +-+-+-+-+-+   (aur nahi katta)
   +---------+     +----+----+     +----+----+       +-+-+-+-+-+
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Barfi ko kaat-te jaane ka vichar: thali, aadha, chauthayi, aur chhote tukde. Sawaal ye tha ki kya ye kaat-na kabhi rukta hai, ya ek "sabse chhota tukda" aata hai jise aur nahi kaata ja sakta. Wahi sabse chhota tukda parmanu ka vichar hai.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Parmanu / Atom** | Tatva ka sabse chhota kan jo us tatva ke gun rakhta hai | Hydrogen ka ek atom |
| **Anu (Kanada ka shabd)** | Prachin Bharat mein sochi gayi "bahut chhote, aur kaate na ja sakne wale kan" ki dhaarna | Kanada ka vichar |
| **Atomos (Democritus ka shabd)** | Greek shabd jiska matlab hai "jise kaata na ja sake" | Atom shabd isi se aaya |
| **Siddhant (Theory)** | Ek aisi soch jo bahut saari baaton ko ek saath samjhaye aur jise parkha ja sake | Dalton ka parmanu siddhant |
| **Postulate** | Siddhant ki ek buniyadi baat jo hum maan kar chalte hain | "Parmanu naye nahi bante" |
| **Aviyajya (indivisible)** | Jo tukdon mein toda na ja sake | Dalton ke hisaab se parmanu |
| **Punarvyavastha (rearrange)** | Cheezein wahi rehti hain, bas naye tareeke se jud jaati hain | Reaction mein atoms ka naya jodna |

## Part A: Insaan ki lambi soch: Kanada, Democritus aur phir Dalton

Ye sawaal ki "cheezein kis se bani hain?" bahut purana hai.

**Kanada (prachin Bharat).** Vaisheshika naam ke bhartiya darshan ki parampara mein Kanada ko is vichar ke saath jodha jaata hai ki padaarth **bahut chhote, aur aage na kaate ja sakne wale kanon** se bane hain. In kanon ke liye **anu** aur **parmanu** jaise shabd istemaal hue. Dhyan do: ye vichar **soch aur tark** se aaya tha. Us samay lab mein tola-maapi karke prayog nahi hote the.

**Democritus (prachin Greece).** Greece mein bhi ek vichar tha ki matter ko baar-baar todte jaao to ek aisi seema aayegi jahan tukda aur nahi tootega. Unhone is sabse chhote tukde ke liye **atomos** shabd istemaal kiya. Hamara shabd "atom" yahin se nikla hai.

Dono ke paas kamaal ka vichar tha, par ek kami thi: ye vichar **pareeksha** ya **tola-maapi** se jaanche nahi gaye the. Sawaal ye tha: kya koi vichar hai jo Lavoisier aur Proust ke niyamon ko bhi explain kare?

**John Dalton (England, 1808).** Dalton ne un niyamon ko dhyan se dekha jo tum Topic 1 aur Topic 2 mein seekh chuke ho. Phir unhone 1808 mein ek **parmanu siddhant** prakashit kiya, jisne parmanu ke purane vichar ko naye roop mein rakha: ab ye sirf soch nahi thi, balki **aise postulates** the jinse dravyaman ke niyam seedhe samjhe ja sakte the.

Yaad rakho: Dalton kisi ek raat mein nahi jaage. Unse pehle ka sochna (Kanada, Democritus) aur unke samay ke niyam (Lavoisier, Proust) sab ne milkar raasta banaya.

## Part B: Dalton ke 5 postulates, simple bhasha mein

Hum Dalton ke vicharon ko 5 baaton mein rakhenge. Inhe **P1 se P5** kahenge, taaki aage hum bata sakein ki "kaun sa postulate kaam aaya."

| No. | Postulate | Seedhi bhasha mein |
|---|---|---|
| **P1** | Matter bahut chhote kanon se bana hai jinhe **parmanu (atoms)** kehte hain, aur parmanu ko tod nahi sakte | Barfi ka "sabse chhota tukda" |
| **P2** | Ek hi tatva ke saare parmanu **ek jaise** hote hain: dravyaman bhi wahi, gun bhi wahi | Saare hydrogen atoms bilkul ek jaise |
| **P3** | Alag-alag tatvon ke parmanu **alag** hote hain: dravyaman bhi alag, gun bhi alag | Hydrogen ka atom aur oxygen ka atom alag |
| **P4** | Yaugik tab banta hai jab alag tatvon ke parmanu **chhote, poore sankhya (whole-number) ke ratio** mein judte hain | 1 carbon + 2 oxygen = CO₂. Aadha atom kabhi nahi |
| **P5** | Chemical reaction mein parmanu **na naye bante hain, na khatam hote hain**: sirf unki jodiyan badalti hain (punarvyavastha) | Atoms ka naya grouping |

Ab dekhte hain ye 5 baatein kaise kaam karti hain.

## Part C: P5 aur Conservation of Mass (Topic 1 ka "kyun")

Topic 1 mein hum ne dekha tha: reaction ke pehle aur baad mein kul dravyaman barabar rehta hai. **Kyun?**

P5 kehta hai ki atoms na naye bante hain na khatam hote hain, sirf apne saathi badalte hain. Agar atoms wahi hain aur har atom ka dravyaman bhi wahi hai (P2 aur P3), to atoms ka **kul** dravyaman badal hi nahi sakta. Ye Lego ki tarah hai: ek ghar ke Lego block se doosra ghar bana lo, blocks ka total weight wahi rahega.

**Example 1.** 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O (Chapter 3 ka balanced equation). Abhi ke liye bas maan lo (ye sankhyayein kahan se aati hain, wo Topic 4 mein dekhenge) ki ek H atom ka dravyaman 1 unit aur ek O atom ka 16 units hai.

- Pehle (reactants): H atoms = 2 × 2 = 4, to 4 × 1 = 4 units. O atoms = 2, to 2 × 16 = 32 units. Kul = 36 units.
- Baad (products): 2 paani ke molecules mein H = 4 (4 units) aur O = 2 (32 units). Kul = 36 units.

Jaanch: 36 = 36 ✅

```
   Pehle:                          Baad:

   (H)(H)   (H)(H)   (O)(O)        (H)(O)(H)    (H)(O)(H)
    H₂       H₂       O₂              H₂O          H₂O

   4 H atoms + 2 O atoms           4 H atoms + 2 O atoms
          wahi 6 atoms, bas naye jode mein
```

> **Figure 3.2** – P5 ka chitra: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O. Baayein taraf 4 H atoms aur 2 O atoms hain, daayein taraf bhi wahi 4 H aur 2 O. Kuch bana nahi, kuch gaya nahi; sirf jodiyan badli hain. Isi liye kul dravyaman nahi badalta.

**Example 2.** Topic 1 ka C + O₂ → CO₂. Ek C atom aur ek O₂ molecule (2 O atoms) mil kar ek CO₂ molecule banate hain. Atoms: C = 1 aur O = 2, dono taraf wahi. Kul atoms wahi, har atom ka dravyaman wahi, to 12 g C + 32 g O₂ = 44 g CO₂ hona hi tha.

Dhyan do: Topic 1 mein khuli bottle wali tablet aur jalte kaagaz mein jo dravyaman "ghata hua" dikha tha, wo bhi P5 ke khilaf nahi tha. Atoms gas ban kar hawa mein chale gaye the, gayab nahi hue.

## Part D: P2, P3, P4 aur Constant Proportions (Topic 2 ka "kyun")

Topic 2 mein dekha tha: paani mein H : O hamesha 1 : 8 (dravyaman ke hisaab se), chahe Himalaya ki dhaara ka ho ya nal ka. **Kyun?**

Chain ko aise padho:

- Paani ke har molecule mein 2 H atoms aur 1 O atom hote hain. Ye formula H₂O hamesha wahi rehta hai (P4: whole-number ratio, aur har paani ka molecule wahi).
- Saare H atoms ek jaise hain (P2), saare O atoms ek jaise hain (P2). Isliye har paani ke molecule ka dravyaman bhi ek jaisa hai.
- To chahe paani ek boond ho ya ek tanker, H : O ka dravyaman ratio ek hi rahega.

**Example 3 (CO₂).** Maan lo C atom = 12 units, O atom = 16 units. Ek CO₂ molecule: C = 12, O = 2 × 16 = 32. Ratio C : O = 12 : 32 = **3 : 8**.

Ab 5 molecules lo: C = 5 × 12 = 60, O = 10 × 16 = 160. Ratio = 60 : 160 = 3 : 8. 100 molecules lo: C = 1200, O = 3200, ratio = 1200 : 3200 = 3 : 8. Jitne bhi molecules lo, ratio wahi: Topic 2 ka 3 : 8 wapas mil gaya ✅

**Example 4 (paani).** 1000 paani ke molecules: H = 2000 × 1 = 2000, O = 1000 × 16 = 16000. Ratio = 2000 : 16000 = **1 : 8** ✅

**Example 5 (ammonia).** NH₃ mein 1 N aur 3 H. Maan lo N = 14, H = 1. Ek molecule: N = 14, H = 3, ratio = 14 : 3. 100 molecules: N = 1400, H = 300, ratio = 1400 : 300 = 14 : 3 ✅ (Topic 2 ka N : H = 14 : 3).

**Mixture ka fark.** Namak ghol ya cheeni ghol mein koi bhi ratio ho sakta hai, kyunki mixture mein alag-alag cheezein sirf mili hoti hain, atoms ke fixed groups nahi bante. Isliye Constant Proportions sirf **yaugik** ka niyam hai, mixture ka nahi.

**Ek naam aur sunte jao.** Agar do tatva milkar ek se zyada yaugik banayein (jaise carbon aur oxygen se CO aur CO₂), to unke dravyaman ke ratio ek khaas pattern mein aate hain. Is niyam ko **Law of Multiple Proportions** kehte hain. Ye **aage ki class** mein seekhenge; yahan sirf naam jaan lo.

## Part E: Dalton ke symbols aur aaj ke letters

Dalton ne har tatva ke liye ek chhota sa **chitra** (circle wala) banaya tha. Aaj hum **Berzelius ke letters** (H, O, C, N, S...) istemaal karte hain, jaisa Chapter 3 mein dekha tha: ek ya do letters, aur wo kisi bhi bhasha mein padhe ja sakte hain.

```
   Tatva        Dalton ka chitra        Aaj ka symbol

   Hydrogen        (.)                      H
   Oxygen          ( )                      O
   Carbon          (●)                      C
   Nitrogen        (|)                      N
   Sulphur         (+)                      S
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Dalton ke circle-chitra aur aaj ke letter-symbols ek saath (saral sketch: asli chitra thode alag chhapte hain). Chitra banana kathin tha, isliye aaj ke letters zyada kaam ke hain: likhne mein asaan, aur formula jaise H₂O, CO₂ likhna aasan.

## Part F: Aaj hum kya jaante hain: Dalton kahan sahi, kahan adhoora

Dalton ka siddhant itna kaamyaab tha ki aaj bhi hum chemistry usi par khadi rakhte hain. Lekin vigyan aage badha, aur ye batana imaandari ki baat hai ki kaun si baatein badal gayi hain.

| Postulate | Aaj ki tasveer |
|---|---|
| **P1:** Atoms tod nahi sakte | **Adhoora.** Atom ke andar aur bhi chhote kan hote hain. Ye **Chapter 5 (Structure of the Atom)** mein dekhenge |
| **P2:** Ek tatva ke saare atoms ek jaise (dravyaman bhi) | **Lagbhag sahi, par poora nahi.** Ek hi tatva ke kuch atoms ka dravyaman thoda alag ho sakta hai (isotopes). Ye bhi Chapter 5 mein |
| **P3:** Alag tatvon ke atoms alag | **Sahi** |
| **P4:** Chhote whole-number ratio mein judte hain | **Sahi** (chemistry ke yaugik mein) |
| **P5:** Chemical reaction mein atoms na bante na khatam | **Sahi** chemical reaction ke liye. (Nuclear reaction alag hoti hai, ye aage ki class mein) |

Isse Dalton chhote nahi ho jaate. Ek achchha siddhant wo hota hai jo apne samay ke niyamon ko sahi samjhaye, aur jab naye saboot aayein to usme sudhaar ho sake. Aaj ke khaas microscopes se atoms ki tasveer bhi li ja chuki hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Dalton ne atom ka vichar diya" samajhna**

Atom ka vichar Dalton se bahut pehle se tha (Kanada ke anu aur Democritus ka atomos). Dalton ki khaasiyat ye thi ki unhone 1808 mein usse **dravyaman ke niyamon** se jod kar ek siddhant bana diya.

**Galti 2: "Atom kabhi toot hi nahi sakta" ko aaj ka sach maan lena**

Ye Dalton ki baat thi (P1). Aaj hum jaante hain ki atom ke andar bhi kan hain. Par ek **chemical reaction** mein atom ke andar kuch nahi badalta, wo sirf saathi badalta hai.

**Galti 3: P5 ko "kuch gayab nahi hota" ka matlab "dravyaman kabhi kam dikhta hi nahi" samajhna**

Khuli bottle mein gas bahar nikalne se dravyaman kam dikh sakta hai (Topic 1). Atoms gayab nahi hue, gas ban kar bahar chale gaye.

**Galti 4: Constant Proportions ko mixture par lagana**

Namak ka ghol kisi bhi ratio mein ban sakta hai. Fixed ratio sirf shuddh **yaugik** ka hota hai.

**Galti 5: "Aadha atom" ka vichar**

P4 kehta hai ki atoms poori sankhya mein judte hain. "1.5 atoms" ya "aadha oxygen atom" chemical reaction mein nahi hota.

## Quick Recap

- Prachin Bharat mein **Kanada** (Vaisheshika) ne **anu / parmanu** ka vichar rakha aur Greece mein **Democritus** ne **atomos** shabd diya: ye soch aur tark se aaye vichar the.
- **John Dalton** ne **1808** mein parmanu siddhant ko 5 postulates mein rakha.
- **P1:** matter parmanuon se bana hai, aviyajya. **P2:** ek tatva ke atoms ek jaise. **P3:** alag tatvon ke atoms alag. **P4:** yaugik mein atoms chhote whole-number ratio mein. **P5:** reaction mein atoms na bante na khatam, sirf regroup.
- **P5 (aur P2, P3)** Conservation of Mass ko samjhate hain: wahi atoms, wahi dravyaman. Jaise 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O mein 36 units = 36 units (maan liye hue 1 aur 16 units ke saath).
- **P2, P3, P4** Constant Proportions ko samjhate hain: har molecule ek jaisa, to ratio hamesha ek jaisa (CO₂: 3 : 8, H₂O: 1 : 8, NH₃: 14 : 3).
- **Law of Multiple Proportions** sirf naam ke roop mein: aage ki class mein.
- Dalton ke **circle-chitra** ki jagah aaj Berzelius ke **letters** hain.
- Aaj hum jaante hain ki atom ke andar bhi kan hote hain (P1 adhoora) aur ek tatva ke kuch atoms ka dravyaman thoda alag ho sakta hai (P2 adhoora): Chapter 5 mein. P3, P4 aur chemical reaction ke liye P5 sahi hain.

10. Match each Dalton postulate to what we know today: (a) P1, (b) P2, (c) P3, (d) P5, with one of "needs correction (Chapter 5)", "still true", "true for chemical reactions". Also write: why did we replace Dalton's circle-drawings with letters?

### Answers

3. **P1:** matter parmanuon se bana hai jinhe tod nahi sakte. **P2:** ek tatva ke saare parmanu ek jaise (dravyaman aur gun). **P3:** alag tatvon ke parmanu alag. **P4:** yaugik mein parmanu chhote whole-number ratio mein judte hain. **P5:** reaction mein parmanu na bante na khatam, sirf regroup hote hain.

4. (a) **P2, P3 aur P4**: har molecule H₂O hai aur atoms ek jaise hain, to ratio fixed. (b) **P5**: atoms wahi, bas regroup. (c) **P4**: whole-number ratio, aadha atom nahi. (d) **P2**: ek tatva ke saare atoms ek jaise.

5. P5 ke hisaab se carbon aur oxygen ke wahi atoms CO₂ mein regroup hue hain, koi naya nahi bana aur koi gaya nahi. Har atom ka dravyaman wahi hai, to kul dravyaman wahi: 12 + 32 = **44 g**, na 45 g na 43 g.

6. Pehle: 4 H atoms = 4 units, 2 O atoms = 32 units, kul = 36. Baad: 2 H₂O mein 4 H (4 units) aur 2 O (32 units), kul = 36. Jaanch: 36 = 36 ✅

7. (a) 12 : (2 × 16) = 12 : 32 = **3 : 8**. (b) C = 50 × 12 = 600, O = 100 × 16 = 1600, ratio = 600 : 1600 = **3 : 8**. Ratio wahi hai chahe molecules kitne bhi hon. P2 (ek jaise atoms) aur P4 (har molecule mein 1 C : 2 O) ye samjhate hain.

8. 10 molecules mein N = 10 × 14 = 140, H = 30 × 1 = 30. Ratio = 140 : 30 = **14 : 3**, Topic 2 wala ratio ✅

9. Salt solution ek **mixture** hai, yaugik nahi. Constant Proportions sirf shuddh yaugik par lagta hai. Mixture mein ratio kuch bhi ho sakta hai.

10. (a) P1: **needs correction (Chapter 5)**, kyunki atom ke andar bhi kan hote hain. (b) P2: **needs correction (Chapter 5)**, kyunki ek tatva ke kuch atoms ka dravyaman thoda alag ho sakta hai (isotopes). (c) P3: **still true**. (d) P5: **true for chemical reactions** (nuclear reaction alag hoti hai, aage ki class mein). Circle-chitra banana kathin tha aur har desh mein samjhana mushkil; letters likhne mein asaan hain aur formula mein seedhe aa jaate hain (H₂O, CO₂).
