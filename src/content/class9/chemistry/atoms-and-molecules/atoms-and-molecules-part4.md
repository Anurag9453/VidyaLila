# Topic 4: Parmanu Dravyaman, Anu Dravyaman aur Formula Unit Mass

Pichhle topic mein Dalton ne bataya ki har tatva ke atoms ka apna ek **fixed dravyaman (mass)** hota hai, aur isi se Topic 2 ka "fixed hisaab" samajh aaya. Par ab ek seedha sawaal: ek atom ka dravyaman **kitna** hota hai? Aur itne chhote atom ko hum tolenge kaise? Is topic mein hum atoms aur molecules ke dravyaman ko ek chhoti si **tulna ki ginti** se likhna seekhenge, aur dekhenge ki formula dekh kar hi paani ka 1 : 8 wala hisaab kaise nikal aata hai.

---

## Kata aur standard baat

Mr. Nayak apni bhatiji Misha ko lekar gaon ki anaj mandi gaye. Wahan ek bada **kata** (taraazu) laga tha. Ek taraf anaj ki bori, doosri taraf lohe ke **standard baat** (weights) rakhe the: 1 kg, 5 kg, 10 kg ke.

Misha ne dekha ki har kisan ki bori usi kata aur usi baat se tolte hain. Usne poocha, "Chacha, baat ka apna vazan kaun jaanta hai? Aur agar har dukaan ke baat alag hote to?"

Mr. Nayak hanse. "Isi liye mandi mein **standard baat** hote hain, sabke liye ek hi reference. Bori ka vazan hum seedha nahi 'jaante', hum use **baat se tulna** karke batate hain: 'is bori ka vazan 10 kg ke baat jitna hai'. Ab ek sawaal soch: ek **atom** ko kisi kate par kaise rakhoge?"

Misha ne socha: ek chawal ka dana bhi kate par dikhta nahi, aur atom to chawal ke dane se bhi **karodon karod guna** chhota hai. Mr. Nayak ne kaha, "Bilkul. Atom ko kisi kate par nahi tol sakte. Isliye chemist bhi wahi karte hain jo mandi karti hai: ek **standard** chunte hain, aur har atom ko us standard se **tulna** karke bataate hain ki wo kitna guna bhaari hai. Chal, dekhte hain ye standard kya hai."

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Atomic mass unit (u)** | Atoms ka dravyaman naapne ki chhoti ikai; carbon-12 atom ke dravyaman ka 1/12 hissa | 1 u |
| **Reference atom** | Wo standard atom jisse baaki sab ki tulna hoti hai | Carbon-12 |
| **Relative atomic mass** | Kisi tatva ka atom carbon-12 ke 1/12 hisse se kitna guna bhaari hai | O = 16, Na = 23 |
| **Molecular mass** | Ek molecule mein saare atoms ke atomic mass ka jod (u mein) | H₂O = 18 u |
| **Formula unit** | Ionic yaugik ke formula mein likha sabse chhota ginti-ka-set | NaCl, CaCO₃ |
| **Formula unit mass** | Ek formula unit ke saare atoms ke atomic mass ka jod (u mein) | NaCl = 58.5 u |

## Part A: Atom ko tolna mushkil kyun hai

Ek hydrogen atom ka asli dravyaman lagbhag 0.000 000 000 000 000 000 000 001 67 gram hai. Itni chhoti sankhya likhna aur padhna dono mushkil hain, aur koi bhi kata itna halka vazan nahi pakad sakta. Isliye atoms ka dravyaman gram mein likhna kaam ki baat nahi.

Doosra tareeka hai **tulna**. Mandi ki tarah hum bolte hain: "oxygen atom, hydrogen atom se kitna guna bhaari hai?" Is tulna mein gram ki zaroorat hi nahi padti, bas ek **ginti** aati hai.

---

## Part B: Carbon ka standard aur atomic mass unit

Sau saal se zyada chemists ne alag-alag standard try kiye (pehle hydrogen, phir oxygen). Aaj ka standard hai **carbon-12 atom**. Isse chun-ne ka faayda ye hai ki carbon bahut saare yaugik mein milta hai aur uska ek sthir roop sab tarah se sahi tulna deta hai. (Carbon ke alag-alag roop aur "-12" ka matlab kya hai, ye Chapter 5 "Structure of the Atom" mein samjhenge.)

Naap ki ikai ban gayi:

**1 atomic mass unit (1 u) = carbon-12 atom ke dravyaman ka 1/12 hissa**

Is hisaab se **ek carbon atom ka dravyaman 12 u** hai. Ab kisi bhi doosre atom ke liye ek hi sawaal poochte hain: "wo is 1 u se kitna guna bhaari hai?" Jo ginti aati hai wahi uska **relative atomic mass** hai. Hydrogen ka 1, oxygen ka 16, matlab oxygen atom hydrogen atom se **16 guna** bhaari hai.

```
   Relative atomic mass (har . = 1 u)

   H  1     .
   C  12    ............  <- reference (carbon-12)
   N  14    ..............
   O  16    ................
   Na 23    .......................
   Cl 35.5  ...................................
   Fe 56    ........................................................
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Kuch atoms ka relative atomic mass, carbon-12 (12 u) ko reference maan kar; har bindu 1 u hai (Cl ka aadha u bindu mein nahi dikhaya). Ye alag-alag atoms ke dravyaman ki **tulna** hai (jaise kate par alag-alag bori), kisi atom ko tolna nahi.

Ek zaroori baat: relative atomic mass ek **tulna ki ginti** hai, isliye uski koi ikai nahi hoti. Jab hum use ikai ke saath likhte hain to wo "u" hota hai (jaise oxygen 16 u).

## Part D: Molecular mass: molecule ka dravyaman

**Molecular mass** = ek molecule mein jitne atoms hain, un sab ke atomic mass ka **jod**.

Tareeka bahut seedha hai:

1. Formula likho.
2. Har tatva ke atoms ki ginti subscript se dekho.
3. Ginti × atomic mass karo.
4. Sab ko jodo.

### Example 1. Paani, H₂O

H₂O mein 2 H aur 1 O hain.

- H: 2 × 1 = 2
- O: 1 × 16 = 16
- Jod: 2 + 16 = **18 u**

Jaanch: 2×1 + 1×16 = 18 ✅

### Example 2. Carbon dioxide, CO₂

- C: 1 × 12 = 12
- O: 2 × 16 = 32
- Jod: 12 + 32 = **44 u**

Jaanch: 12 + 32 = 44 ✅

### Example 3. Ammonia, NH₃

- N: 1 × 14 = 14
- H: 3 × 1 = 3
- Jod: 14 + 3 = **17 u**

Jaanch: 14 + 3 = 17 ✅

### Example 4. Methane, CH₄

- C: 1 × 12 = 12
- H: 4 × 1 = 4
- Jod: **16 u**

Jaanch: 12 + 4 = 16 ✅

### Example 5. Sulphuric acid, H₂SO₄

- H: 2 × 1 = 2
- S: 1 × 32 = 32
- O: 4 × 16 = 64
- Jod: 2 + 32 + 64 = **98 u**

Jaanch: 2 + 32 + 64 = 98 ✅

### Example 6. Glucose, C₆H₁₂O₆

- C: 6 × 12 = 72
- H: 12 × 1 = 12
- O: 6 × 16 = 96
- Jod: 72 + 12 + 96 = **180 u**

Jaanch: 72 + 12 + 96 = 180 ✅

## Part E: Tatva ke molecules bhi

Chapter 3 se yaad karo (atomicity): kuch tatva atoms ke **groups** ya molecules ke roop mein milte hain. Unka molecular mass bhi usi tareeke se nikalta hai.

### Example 7. Oxygen gas O₂ aur ozone O₃

- O₂: 2 × 16 = **32 u**
- O₃: 3 × 16 = **48 u**

Dono sirf oxygen se bane hain, par molecules alag hain, isliye dravyaman bhi alag (atomicity 2 aur 3).

Jaanch: 2×16 = 32 ✅, 3×16 = 48 ✅

### Example 8. Phosphorus P₄ aur sulphur S₈

- P₄: 4 × 31 = **124 u**
- S₈: 8 × 32 = **256 u**

Jaanch: 4×31 = 124 ✅, 8×32 = 256 ✅

Dhyan do: **atomic mass** ek atom ka hota hai (O = 16 u), aur **molecular mass** ek molecule ka (O₂ = 32 u). "Oxygen ka mass" bolte waqt dhyan se batao ki atom ki baat ho rahi hai ya molecule ki.

## Part F: Formula unit mass: ionic yaugik ke liye

NaCl (namak) ya CaCO₃ (chuna-patthar) jaise **ionic yaugik** mein koi alag, akela **molecule** hota hi nahi. Unme positive aur negative ions ek **bade, lagataar jaal (lattice)** mein ek ke baad ek jude hote hain, aur formula bas ye batata hai ki ions kis **ratio** mein hain. (Ye jaal kaise bante hain, ye Chapter 6 mein, "Chemical Bonding" mein.)

```
   Namak ka crystal (ek patla hissa)

   Na⁺  Cl⁻  Na⁺  Cl⁻  Na⁺
   Cl⁻  Na⁺  Cl⁻  Na⁺  Cl⁻
   Na⁺  Cl⁻  Na⁺  Cl⁻  Na⁺

   Formula NaCl = sirf ratio 1 : 1
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Namak ke crystal ka ek patla hissa (saral sketch): Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ ions baari-baari se lagataar jaal mein jude hain. Koi alag "NaCl molecule" nahi dikhta; formula sirf 1 : 1 ratio batata hai.

Is wajah se hum "NaCl ka molecule" nahi kehte. Hum kehte hain **formula unit**: formula mein likha sabse chhota set. NaCl ka formula unit = 1 Na⁺ + 1 Cl⁻.

**Formula unit mass** = ek formula unit mein saare atoms ke atomic mass ka jod. Tareeka bilkul molecular mass jaisa hai, bas naam alag.

### Example 9. Namak, NaCl

- Na: 1 × 23 = 23
- Cl: 1 × 35.5 = 35.5
- Jod: **58.5 u**

Jaanch: 23 + 35.5 = 58.5 ✅

### Example 10. Chuna-patthar, CaCO₃

- Ca: 1 × 40 = 40
- C: 1 × 12 = 12
- O: 3 × 16 = 48
- Jod: **100 u**

Jaanch: 40 + 12 + 48 = 100 ✅

### Example 11. Potassium carbonate, K₂CO₃

- K: 2 × 39 = 78
- C: 1 × 12 = 12
- O: 3 × 16 = 48
- Jod: **138 u**

Jaanch: 78 + 12 + 48 = 138 ✅

## Part G: Brackets aur subscripts ka hisaab

Jab formula mein **bracket** hota hai, to bracket ke bahar likha subscript **bracket ke andar ke har atom** ko guna karta hai. Pehle bracket kholo, phir ginti karo.

### Example 12. Calcium hydroxide, Ca(OH)₂

OH ka group 2 baar hai, to: 1 Ca, 2 O, 2 H.

- Ca: 1 × 40 = 40
- O: 2 × 16 = 32
- H: 2 × 1 = 2
- Jod: **74 u**

Jaanch: 40 + 32 + 2 = 74 ✅

### Example 13. Ammonium sulphate, (NH₄)₂SO₄

(NH₄) 2 baar hai: 2 N aur 2 × 4 = 8 H. Phir 1 S aur 4 O.

- N: 2 × 14 = 28
- H: 8 × 1 = 8
- S: 1 × 32 = 32
- O: 4 × 16 = 64
- Jod: 28 + 8 + 32 + 64 = **132 u**

Jaanch: 28 + 8 + 32 + 64 = 132 ✅

### Example 14. Aluminium sulphate, Al₂(SO₄)₃

Bracket (SO₄) 3 baar hai: 3 S aur 3 × 4 = 12 O. Aluminium 2.

- Al: 2 × 27 = 54
- S: 3 × 32 = 96
- O: 12 × 16 = 192
- Jod: 54 + 96 + 192 = **342 u**

Jaanch: 54 + 96 + 192 = 342 ✅

```
   Al₂(SO₄)₃ ka mass, kadam ba kadam

   Kadam 1: bracket kholo
            Al₂ (SO₄)₃  -->  Al = 2,  S = 3,  O = 4 × 3 = 12

   Kadam 2: har tatva ka mass
            Al: 2  × 27  =  54
            S : 3  × 32  =  96
            O : 12 × 16  = 192
                           ----
   Kadam 3: jodo
            54 + 96 + 192 = 342 u

            +-------+------------+------------------------+
            : Al 54 :    S 96    :         O 192          :
            +-------+------------+------------------------+
```

> **Figure 4.3** – Al₂(SO₄)₃ ka formula unit mass kadam ba kadam: pehle bracket kholkar atoms ginte hain (Al 2, S 3, O 12), phir har tatva ka mass nikalte hain, phir jod karke 342 u milta hai. Neeche ki patti mein Al, S aur O ke hisse lagbhag unke mass ke anupaat mein dikhaye hain (har "-" lagbhag 8 u).

## Part H: Formula se mass ratio, Topic 2 se jod

Topic 2 mein humne dekha tha ki paani mein hydrogen aur oxygen ka mass ratio hamesha 1 : 8 hota hai, chahe paani Himalaya ki dhaara ka ho ya nal ka. Tab humne ye ratio **naap kar** paaya tha. Ab hum use **formula se seedha nikaal sakte hain**, bina kuch tole.

Tareeka: har tatva ka **kul mass (ginti × atomic mass)** likho, phir unka ratio lo.

### Example 15. Paani, H₂O

- H ka kul mass: 2 × 1 = 2
- O ka kul mass: 1 × 16 = 16
- Ratio H : O = 2 : 16 = **1 : 8**

Jaanch: 2/16 = 1/8 ✅. Ye bilkul Topic 2 wala hisaab hai.

### Example 16. Carbon dioxide, CO₂

- C: 1 × 12 = 12
- O: 2 × 16 = 32
- Ratio C : O = 12 : 32 = **3 : 8**

Jaanch: 12 ÷ 4 = 3 aur 32 ÷ 4 = 8 ✅

### Example 17. Ammonia, NH₃

- N: 1 × 14 = 14
- H: 3 × 1 = 3
- Ratio N : H = **14 : 3**

Jaanch: Topic 2 ka ammonia ka ratio 14 : 3 hi tha ✅

### Example 18. Glucose, C₆H₁₂O₆

- C: 72, H: 12, O: 96
- Ratio C : H : O = 72 : 12 : 96 = **6 : 1 : 8** (teeno ko 12 se bhaag diya)

Jaanch: 72 ÷ 12 = 6, 12 ÷ 12 = 1, 96 ÷ 12 = 8 ✅

### Example 19. Ulta kaam: mass dekar missing mass

Ek sample mein 36 g paani hai. Usme kitna hydrogen aur kitna oxygen hoga?

- Paani ka molecular mass = 18, jisme H = 2 hissa aur O = 16 hissa.
- H ka hissa: 36 × (2/18) = **4 g**
- O ka hissa: 36 × (16/18) = **32 g**

Jaanch: 4 + 32 = 36 ✅. Ratio 4 : 32 = 1 : 8 ✅

Ab "fixed hisaab" ka asli raaz samajh aaya: ratio isliye fixed hai kyunki **formula fixed hai** (2 H aur 1 O) aur **atomic mass fixed hain** (1 aur 16). Isliye har kisi ki dhaara ka paani wahi ratio dega.

## Part I: Ek aur galat-fehmi: bhaar aur ginti

Dhyan do: H₂O ka molecular mass 18 u hai. Iska matlab **ye nahi** ki paani mein 18 atoms hain. Paani ke ek molecule mein sirf **3 atoms** hain. 18 u uska **dravyaman** hai, ginti nahi. Dravyaman aur ginti alag baatein hain.

Chhoti si example: 1 kg shakkar aur 1 kg namak ka vazan barabar hai, par unke dano ki ginti bilkul alag hai. Isi tarah, ek bhaari atom aur ek halka atom ka dravyaman alag hota hai, par har ek ek hi atom hai.

Ab ek sawaal khada hota hai: agar hum 12 g carbon aur 16 g oxygen lein, to kya dono mein atoms ki ginti barabar hogi? Ye bahut dilchasp baat hai, aur iska jawab aap agle topic mein paayenge.

---

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Bracket ke andar ka subscript na guna karna**

Al₂(SO₄)₃ mein O ki ginti 4 nahi, 4 × 3 = **12** hai. Pehle bracket kholo.

**Galti 2: u ko gram samajhna**

1 u gram ka bahut chhota hissa hai. H₂O ka 18 u ek molecule ka dravyaman hai, 18 gram nahi.

**Galti 3: Atomic mass aur molecular mass mein gadbad**

Oxygen atom 16 u hai, par oxygen gas ka molecule O₂ **32 u** hai. Ye sabse aam galti hai.

**Galti 4: Ionic yaugik ko "molecule" bolna**

NaCl ke liye "molecule" nahi, **formula unit** kehte hain. Mass nikalne ka tareeka same hai, par naam sahi rakho.

**Galti 5: Dravyaman ko ginti samajhna**

H₂O = 18 u ka matlab 18 atoms nahi. Molecule mein sirf 3 atoms hain.

**Galti 6: Subscript ko guna karna bhool jaana**

CO₂ ka mass 12 + 16 = 28 nahi, 12 + 2 × 16 = **44** hai.

## Quick Recap

- Atoms ko kate par nahi tol sakte; isliye hum **tulna** karte hain, bilkul mandi ke standard baat ki tarah.
- Standard hai **carbon-12 atom**: uska 1/12 hissa = **1 atomic mass unit (1 u)**. Carbon atom = 12 u.
- **Relative atomic mass** batata hai ki ek atom 1 u se kitna guna bhaari hai. Is chapter ki rounded table: H 1, C 12, N 14, O 16, Na 23, Mg 24, Al 27, S 32, Cl 35.5, K 39, Ca 40, Fe 56, Cu 63.5, Zn 65, Ag 108.
- **Molecular mass** = molecule ke saare atoms ke atomic mass ka jod: H₂O 18, CO₂ 44, NH₃ 17, CH₄ 16, H₂SO₄ 98, C₆H₁₂O₆ 180, O₂ 32, O₃ 48, P₄ 124, S₈ 256.
- **Formula unit mass** ionic yaugik ke liye (jinme akela molecule nahi hota): NaCl 58.5, CaCO₃ 100, Ca(OH)₂ 74, Al₂(SO₄)₃ 342, (NH₄)₂SO₄ 132, K₂CO₃ 138.
- Bracket ke bahar ka subscript andar ke har atom ko guna karta hai.
- Formula se mass ratio nikalta hai: H₂O mein H : O = 2 : 16 = 1 : 8, CO₂ mein C : O = 3 : 8, NH₃ mein N : H = 14 : 3.
- Dravyaman (u) aur atoms ki **ginti** alag baatein hain.

## Practice Questions

1. Why can we not weigh a single atom on a weighing scale? What do chemists do instead?

2. What is 1 atomic mass unit (1 u)? What is the mass of one carbon-12 atom in u?

3. What does it mean when we say the relative atomic mass of oxygen is 16? How many times heavier than a hydrogen atom is an oxygen atom?

4. Calculate the molecular mass of: (a) CH₄, (b) NH₃, (c) H₂SO₄, (d) O₃, (e) P₄. Show the atom count and the arithmetic in each case.

5. Calculate the formula unit mass of: (a) NaCl, (b) K₂CO₃, (c) Ca(OH)₂, (d) (NH₄)₂SO₄.

6. Calculate the formula unit mass of Al₂(SO₄)₃. A student wrote the oxygen count as 4. What went wrong, and what is the right count?

7. Why do we say "formula unit" for NaCl and not "molecule"?

8. Find the mass ratio of the elements in (a) CO₂ (carbon : oxygen) and (b) NH₃ (nitrogen : hydrogen). How does this connect to the Law of Constant Proportions?

9. A sample has 90 g of water. How many grams of hydrogen and oxygen does it contain in all?

10. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) The molecular mass of O₂ is 16 u.
    (b) H₂O = 18 u means a water molecule has 18 atoms.
    (c) Relative atomic mass has no unit as it is a comparison number, but we write masses with "u".
    (d) 1 u is 1/12 of the mass of a carbon-12 atom.

### Answers

1. Atom itna chhota hai (lagbhag 10⁻²⁴ gram ke aas-paas) ki koi kata use nahi pakad sakta. Isliye chemist **tulna** karte hain: ek standard atom (carbon-12) chunte hain aur har atom batata hai ki wo us standard ke 1/12 hisse se kitna guna bhaari hai, bilkul mandi ke standard baat ki tarah.

2. 1 u = carbon-12 atom ke dravyaman ka 1/12 hissa. Isliye ek carbon-12 atom ka dravyaman **12 u** hai.

3. Iska matlab oxygen atom 1 u se 16 guna bhaari hai. Hydrogen ka mass 1 hai, to oxygen atom hydrogen atom se **16 guna** bhaari hai.

4. (a) CH₄: C 1×12 = 12, H 4×1 = 4, jod **16 u** ✅. (b) NH₃: N 1×14 = 14, H 3×1 = 3, jod **17 u** ✅. (c) H₂SO₄: H 2×1 = 2, S 1×32 = 32, O 4×16 = 64, jod **98 u** ✅. (d) O₃: 3×16 = **48 u** ✅. (e) P₄: 4×31 = **124 u** ✅.

5. (a) NaCl: 23 + 35.5 = **58.5 u** ✅. (b) K₂CO₃: 2×39 + 12 + 3×16 = 78 + 12 + 48 = **138 u** ✅. (c) Ca(OH)₂: 40 + 2×16 + 2×1 = 40 + 32 + 2 = **74 u** ✅. (d) (NH₄)₂SO₄: N 2×14 = 28, H 8×1 = 8, S 32, O 4×16 = 64, jod **132 u** ✅.

6. Al₂(SO₄)₃: Al 2×27 = 54, S 3×32 = 96, O 12×16 = 192, jod 54 + 96 + 192 = **342 u** ✅. Student ne bracket ke bahar ka subscript 3 O par nahi lagaya. Sahi ginti O = 4 × 3 = **12**.

7. NaCl mein akela molecule nahi hota; Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ ions ek bade jaal mein 1 : 1 ratio mein jude hote hain. Isliye "molecule" nahi, **formula unit** kehte hain (iske bonding ki baat Chapter 6 mein).

8. (a) CO₂: C 12, O 2×16 = 32, ratio **3 : 8** ✅. (b) NH₃: N 14, H 3×1 = 3, ratio **14 : 3** ✅. Formula fixed hai aur atomic mass fixed hain, isliye ratio hamesha wahi aata hai. Yahi Law of Constant Proportions hai.

9. Water ka mass 18 u mein H = 2 aur O = 16. H: 90 × 2/18 = **10 g**. O: 90 × 16/18 = **80 g**. Jaanch: 10 + 80 = 90 ✅, ratio 10 : 80 = 1 : 8 ✅.

10. (a) **False** ❌. O atom 16 u hai, par molecule O₂ 2×16 = **32 u** hai. (b) **False** ❌. 18 u dravyaman hai; paani ke ek molecule mein 3 atoms hain. (c) **True** ✅. (d) **True** ✅.

**Aage kya?** Ek dilchasp sawaal baaki hai. 18 u ka paani, 44 u ki carbon dioxide aur 12 u ka carbon dikhne mein "u" mein to hain, par humare haath mein to **gram** hote hain. Agar hum 18 gram paani lein, to usme kitne molecule honge? Agle topic **"Mole: Kan Ginne Ka Naya Tareeka"** mein hum is badi ginti ka ek naya, dilchasp tareeka seekhenge.
