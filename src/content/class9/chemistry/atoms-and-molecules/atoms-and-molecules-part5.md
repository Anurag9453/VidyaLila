# Topic 5: Mole: Kan Ginne Ka Naya Tareeka

Pichhle topic mein humne seekha ki ek atom ya molecule ka dravyaman kaise likhte hain: C ka 12 u, H₂O ka 18 u, NaCl ka 58.5 u. (Hum rounded atomic masses hi use kar rahe hain, jaise H = 1, C = 12, O = 16, Na = 23, Cl = 35.5.) Par ek bada sawaal baaki hai. Lab mein ya factory mein hum **ek-ek atom** thodi lete hain. Hum to gram ya kilo mein cheez tolte hain. To gram aur atoms ki ginti ke beech ka **pul** kya hai? Topic 4 ke aakhir mein yahi sawaal chhoda tha: 18 g paani mein kitne molecules, aur kya 12 g carbon aur 16 g oxygen mein atoms barabar honge? Is pul ka naam hai **mole**, aur iske jawab Part H mein milenge.

---

## Button wali dukaan

Mr. Ahuja apni bhanji Aadhya ko bazaar ki ek purani button wali dukaan par le gaye. Dukaan mein dher saare buttons dibbon mein bhare the. Ek grahak ne kaha, "Bhaiya, mujhe 2500 buttons chahiye, apne karkhane ke liye."

Aadhya ne socha, ab dukaandaar baithkar 2500 buttons ginega, aur wo bhi ek-ek karke! Par dukaandaar ne bas ek chhoti si **taraazu** nikali. Usne ginkar sirf **50 buttons** taraazu par rakhe aur tola: **100 g**. Phir bola, "Ek button 2 g ka hua. 2500 buttons ka vazan 2500 × 2 = 5000 g, yaani **5 kg**." Usne ek bori mein 5 kg button tole aur grahak ko de diye. Ginti ek baar bhi nahi karni padi.

Aadhya hairaan: "Mamaji, isne ginte bina 2500 buttons de diye!" Mr. Ahuja muskuraye: "Yahi **ginti ko taul se badalna** hai. Jab har button ka vazan ek jaisa ho, to vazan se ginti nikal sakte hain. Chemistry mein atoms bahut-bahut chhote hain, aur hum unhe isi tareeke se ginte hain: **taulkar**. Is trick ka sabse bada hathiyar hai mole."

## Part A: Ginti ko taul se badalna

Button ke jaise, ek simple recipe hai: **ek kan ka vazan pata ho aur kul vazan pata ho, to ginti nikal jaati hai.** Par ek dikkat hai. Ek carbon atom ka vazan sirf lagbhag 2 × 10⁻²³ g hai. Ek-ek atom ko taraazu par rakhna to naamumkin hai. Isliye hum atoms ka ek **bahut bada, tay sankhya wala jattha** banate hain, aur us poore jatthe ko taulte hain.

```
   Button ki dukaan                    Chemistry ki dukaan

   50 button   =  100 g                6.022 × 10²³ atom C  =  12 g
   1 button    =  2 g                  (ek "jattha", yaani 1 mole)
   5000 g      =  2500 button

   taraazu  ──►  ginti                 taraazu  ──►  kano ki ginti
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Counting by weighing: button ki dukaan mein 50 button ka vazan 100 g se ek button 2 g nikalta hai, aur 5 kg mein 2500 button hote hain. Chemistry mein 12 g carbon mein 6.022 × 10²³ atoms hote hain, yaani vazan se hi kano ki ginti pata chalti hai.

Ab sawaal ye hai: wo jattha kitna bada ho? Aur kya ye number har padaarth ke liye ek hi ho sakta hai? Pehle ek jaan-pehchaan ki baat dekhte hain.

## Part B: Darjan, gross aur mole

Rozmarra ki zindagi mein hum alag-alag cheezon ke liye **ginti ki ikai** use karte hain:

- **1 jodi** = 2 cheezein (juute, mozay)
- **1 darjan** = 12 cheezein (kele, ande)
- **1 gross** = 144 cheezein, yaani 12 darjan (pencil, buttons)

Dhyan do ki darjan ek **number** hai, vazan nahi. 1 darjan ande aur 1 darjan haathi dono "12" hain, par unka vazan bahut alag hai. 12 haathi ka vazan 12 ande se kai guna zyada hoga.

**Mole bhi bilkul aisi hi ginti ki ikai hai.** Farak bas ye hai ki ye bahut, bahut bada hai:

**1 mole = 6.022 × 10²³ kan**

Ye kan atoms ho sakte hain, molecules, ya formula units (ionic yaugik ke liye, jo Topic 4 mein padha tha). Jaise "ek darjan" kehne se pata nahi chalta ki ande hain ya kele, waise hi "ek mole" kehne par batana padta hai ki **kis cheez ka**: "1 mole carbon atoms", "1 mole paani ke molecules", "1 mole NaCl formula units".

Ye number **Avogadro ka number** kehlata hai (Italy ke vaigyanik Amedeo Avogadro ke sammaan mein rakha gaya naam). Hum ise 6.022 × 10²³ likhenge. Poora likho to: **602,200,000,000,000,000,000,000**.

Aisa bada number kyun chuna gaya? Kyunki ise jaan-boojh kar aisa chuna gaya hai ki gram aur u ka ek bahut accha jod ban jaaye, jo hum Part D mein dekhenge.

---

## Part C: Itna bada number kitna bada hai?

6.022 × 10²³ ko mehsoos karne ke liye kuch **andaze wali (approximate)** tulnayein dekho. Ye sab gol-matol hisaab hain, bas bada-pan samajhne ke liye.

**1. Ek-ek karke ginna.** Maan lo tum ek second mein ek kan ginte ho, bina ruke, din-raat. Ek saal mein lagbhag 3.15 × 10⁷ second hote hain. To 6.022 × 10²³ kan ginne mein lagbhag **1.9 × 10¹⁶ saal** lagenge. Brahmaand ki umra lagbhag 1.4 × 10¹⁰ saal maani jaati hai. Yaani tum brahmaand ki umra se **10 lakh guna se bhi zyada** samay tak ginte rehte.

**2. Duniya ki aabadi se tulna.** Duniya mein lagbhag 8 × 10⁹ log hain. Agar 6.022 × 10²³ kano ko saare logon mein barabar baanta jaye, to har insaan ke hisse mein lagbhag **7.5 × 10¹³** kan aayenge, yaani har insaan ek mole ka sirf ek chhota sa tukda sambhale, phir bhi ginti 75 lakh crore ke aaspaas.

**3. Paani ki ek boond.** Ek aam boond lagbhag 0.05 g paani ki hoti hai (andaza). Isme H₂O ke lagbhag **1.7 × 10²¹** molecules hote hain, yaani ek mole ke 0.28% se bhi kam. Ek boond mein hi itne molecules! Ek poora mole paani to bas lagbhag **18 g, yaani ek bade ghoont jitna** hota hai.

Ye sab tulnayein ek hi baat batati hain: **atoms itne chhote hain ki ek mole jaisi bahut badi ginti ek hathelli mein samaa jaati hai.**

## Part D: Ek mole ka vazan: molar mass

Ab asli chamatkaar. Kisi bhi padaarth ke ek mole ka **vazan gram mein** wahi number hota hai jo uska **dravyaman (u mein)** hota hai.

- Carbon ka atomic mass 12 u hai, to **1 mole carbon = 12 g**.
- H₂O ka molecular mass 18 u hai, to **1 mole paani = 18 g**.
- NaCl ka formula unit mass 58.5 u hai, to **1 mole NaCl = 58.5 g**.

Ek mole ke vazan (gram mein) ko **molar mass** kehte hain aur ise M se likhte hain. Iski ikai **g/mol** hoti hai (gram per mole). To C ka M = 12 g/mol, H₂O ka M = 18 g/mol, NaCl ka M = 58.5 g/mol.

```
   1 mole ka vazan (molar mass)

   Carbon, C        Paani, H₂O       Namak, NaCl      Oxygen, O₂
   +-------+        +-------+        +-------+        +-------+
   │ 12 g  │        │ 18 g  │        │ 58.5 g│        │ 32 g  │
   +-------+        +-------+        +-------+        +-------+
   6.022×10²³       6.022×10²³       6.022×10²³       6.022×10²³
   atoms C          molecules        formula units    molecules
                    H₂O              NaCl             O₂
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Chaar padaarthon ke ek mole: vazan sabka alag (12 g, 18 g, 58.5 g, 32 g), par kano ki ginti sabki ek hi: 6.022 × 10²³. Bilkul jaise 1 darjan ande aur 1 darjan haathi: ginti barabar, vazan alag.

Ye connection kyun kaam karta hai? Socho: ek carbon atom ka vazan 12 u hai aur ek oxygen atom ka 16 u. To oxygen ka ek atom carbon se `16 / 12` guna bhaari hai. Ab agar dono ke 6.022 × 10²³ atoms liye jaayein, to unke vazan ka anupaat bhi wahi `16 : 12` rahega. Avogadro ka number aise tay kiya gaya hai ki **12 g carbon mein bilkul itne atoms** aayein. Isse har padaarth ke ek mole ka vazan gram mein usi number ke barabar aa jaata hai jo uska dravyaman u mein hai.

> **Dhyan do:** ye "gram wala number" tab sahi hai jab u wale number ke saath hum **sahi kan** ko pakadte hain. O ka atomic mass 16 u hai, to 1 mole O **atoms** = 16 g. Par oxygen gas O₂ ke **molecules** hote hain, aur O₂ ka molecular mass 32 u hai, to 1 mole O₂ **molecules** = 32 g. Aur isme atoms honge 2 × 6.022 × 10²³ ≈ 1.204 × 10²⁴.

## Part E: Ek kan ka vazan gram mein

Molar mass aur Avogadro ke number se ek akela atom ya molecule ka vazan bhi nikal sakte hain: **ek mole ka vazan ÷ ek mole mein kan.**

- Ek carbon atom: 12 g ÷ 6.022 × 10²³ = lagbhag **2.0 × 10⁻²³ g**.
- Ek H₂O molecule: 18 g ÷ 6.022 × 10²³ = lagbhag **3.0 × 10⁻²³ g**.
- Ek NaCl formula unit: 58.5 g ÷ 6.022 × 10²³ = lagbhag **9.7 × 10⁻²³ g**.

Ye number kitne chhote hain, 0.00000000000000000000002 g jaise. Taraazu par to ek atom kabhi nahi dikhega, par ek mole mein gina hua jattha 12 g ka ek chhota sa piece ban jaata hai.

## Part F: Mole ke teen rishte

Ab hum kisi bhi sawaal mein teen cheezon ko jodte hain: **vazan (m)**, **moles (n)** aur **kano ki ginti (N)**.

**Rishta 1: vazan se moles**

```
                n  =  m / M
```

Yaani **moles = diya gaya vazan ÷ molar mass**. Button ki dukaan jaisa: kul vazan ÷ ek button ka vazan = ginti. Fark bas itna ki yahan "ek button" ki jagah "ek mole" ka vazan hai.

**Rishta 2: moles se kano ki ginti**

```
                N  =  n × 6.022 × 10²³
```

Yaani **kano ki ginti = moles × 6.022 × 10²³**. Jaise "3 darjan = 3 × 12 = 36".

Dono rishton ko ek hi triangle mein samet sakte hain. Jis cheez ko nikalna hai use dhak lo, baaki dono ka rishta dikh jaata hai:

```
                 +-------+
                 │   m   │
                 +-------+
                /    ×    \
               /           \
        +-------+       +-------+
        │   n   │───────│   M   │
        +-------+       +-------+

   m = n × M        n = m / M        M = m / n
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Mole triangle (vazan wala): upar m, neeche n aur M. Beech ka "×" batata hai ki n aur M ko guna karne se m milta hai. Nikalna jo ho use dhak do: n = m / M, M = m / n.

Aur moles se kano tak ka pul alag se:

```
        vazan m (g)
            │
            ▼   ÷ M   (n = m / M)
        moles n (mol)
            │
            ▼   × 6.022 × 10²³   (N = n × 6.022 × 10²³)
        kano ki ginti N
```

> **Figure 5.4** – Mole beech ka pul hai: vazan ko M se bhaag kar moles, moles ko 6.022 × 10²³ se guna karke kano ki ginti. Ulti taraf chalne par guna aur bhaag badal jaate hain.

**Ikai ka dhyan:** m gram mein, M gram per mole mein, to n = m / M ki ikai g ÷ (g/mol) = mol aati hai.

## Part G: Chhote solved examples

Ab kuch chhote examples. (Bade aur zyada problems agle topic mein aayenge.) Rounded atomic masses use karenge: H = 1, C = 12, O = 16, Na = 23, Cl = 35.5. Kano ki badi ginti ko hum 6.022 ki tarah **4 significant figures** tak likhenge, jaise 2 × 6.022 × 10²³ = 12.044 × 10²³ ≈ 1.204 × 10²⁴.

### Example 1. 36 g paani mein kitne mole hain? Kitne molecules?

H₂O ka M = 2×1 + 16 = 18 g/mol.

- n = m / M = 36 / 18 = **2 mol**.
- N = n × 6.022 × 10²³ = 2 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **1.204 × 10²⁴ molecules**.

Jaanch: 36 g, 18 g (1 mole) se do guna hai, to 2 mole ✅. Kan bhi do guna: 2 × 6.022 × 10²³ ✅

### Example 2. 6 g carbon mein kitne mole hain? Kitne atoms?

C ka M = 12 g/mol.

- n = 6 / 12 = **0.5 mol**.
- N = 0.5 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **3.011 × 10²³ atoms**.

Jaanch: 6 g, 12 g ka aadha hai, to atoms bhi 6.022 × 10²³ ke aadhe ✅

### Example 3. 0.25 mole NaCl ka vazan kitna hoga?

NaCl ka M = 23 + 35.5 = 58.5 g/mol.

- m = n × M = 0.25 × 58.5 = **14.625 g**, yaani lagbhag **14.6 g**.

Jaanch: 1 mole = 58.5 g, to chauthai mole = 58.5 ÷ 4 = 14.625 g ✅

### Example 4. 117 g NaCl mein kitne mole formula units hain?

- n = 117 / 58.5 = **2 mol**.
- N = 2 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **1.204 × 10²⁴ formula units**.

Jaanch: 2 mol × 58.5 g/mol = 117 g ✅

## Part H: Teen alag cheezein, ek hi number

Ek sawaal aksar poochha jaata hai: **18 g paani, 12 g carbon aur 32 g oxygen gas mein se kisme zyada kan hain?** Jawab: **teeno mein barabar**, har ek mein 1 mole kan:

- 18 g H₂O = 18 / 18 = 1 mol molecules.
- 12 g C = 12 / 12 = 1 mol atoms.
- 32 g O₂ = 32 / 32 = 1 mol molecules.

Sabme 6.022 × 10²³ kan (par unka vazan alag, kyunki har kan ka apna vazan hai). Yahi mole ka asli faayda hai: ginti ko vazan se jodna.

Ab Topic 4 wala sawaal: 12 g carbon aur 16 g oxygen **atoms** mein? 12 / 12 = 1 mol C atoms aur 16 / 16 = 1 mol O atoms, to **haan, dono mein atoms barabar** hain: 6.022 × 10²³. (Par dhyan do, 16 g oxygen **gas** O₂ ho to wo 16 / 32 = 0.5 mol molecules hai, jisme atoms phir bhi 1 mol hi hain.)

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Mole ko vazan samajhna**

Mole ginti ki ikai hai, vazan ki nahi. 1 mole carbon ka vazan 12 g hai, par 1 mole paani ka vazan 18 g hai. Mole barabar, vazan alag.

**Galti 2: Atoms aur molecules mein gadbad**

1 mole O₂ matlab 6.022 × 10²³ **molecules**, aur atoms usse do guna (1.204 × 10²⁴), kyunki har molecule mein 2 atoms hain. Hamesha poochho: kaun sa kan?

**Galti 3: Molar mass mein u aur g ki ikai bhoolna**

Ek atom ka mass u mein hota hai, ek mole ka mass **g/mol** mein. Number wahi, ikai alag.

**Galti 4: Rishta ulta lagana**

n = m / M hai, M / m nahi. Agar vazan 36 g aur M = 18 g/mol ho, to 36 / 18 = 2 mol, par 18 / 36 = 0.5 galat jawab dega. Ikai se jaanch lo: g ÷ (g/mol) = mol.

**Galti 5: "Mole" ko sirf gas ke liye samajhna**

Mole har cheez ke liye hai: thos, tarl, gas, atom, molecule, ion. (Gas ke ek mole ka size kitna hota hai, ye aage ke chapter "Gas Laws" mein aayega.)

## Quick Recap

- Atoms bahut chhote hain, isliye ginti ko **taul se badalte hain**, jaise button ki dukaan mein 50 button ka vazan dekh kar 2500 button ginte hain.
- **1 mole = 6.022 × 10²³ kan** (Avogadro ka number), jaise 1 darjan = 12 aur 1 gross = 144. Kan atom, molecule ya formula unit ho sakte hain.
- 6.022 × 10²³ itna bada hai ki ek-ek second mein ginte to lagbhag 1.9 × 10¹⁶ saal lagte (andaza); ek boond paani mein hi lagbhag 1.7 × 10²¹ molecules hote hain (andaza).
- **Molar mass M** (g/mol) = atomic ya molecular (ya formula unit) mass ka number: C 12, H₂O 18, NaCl 58.5, O₂ 32.
- Ek carbon atom ka vazan lagbhag 2.0 × 10⁻²³ g, ek H₂O molecule ka lagbhag 3.0 × 10⁻²³ g.
- **n = m / M** aur **N = n × 6.022 × 10²³**: vazan se moles, moles se kano ki ginti.
- 18 g H₂O, 12 g C aur 32 g O₂ mein kan ki ginti barabar (1 mol), par vazan alag.

## Practice Questions

1. A shopkeeper weighs 40 buttons and finds a mass of 80 g. How many buttons are there in a 3 kg bag?

2. What is a mole? Explain with the help of the dozen and the gross.

3. Find the molar mass of (a) O₂, (b) CO₂, (c) NH₃, (d) CaCO₃, using H = 1, C = 12, N = 14, O = 16, Ca = 40.

4. How many moles are present in 36 g of water? How many molecules?

5. How many atoms are there in 6 g of carbon?

6. Find the mass of 0.25 mole of NaCl (Na = 23, Cl = 35.5).

7. Which has more particles: 18 g of water or 32 g of oxygen gas? Explain by finding moles.

8. Calculate the mass of one atom of carbon in grams (C = 12, Avogadro number 6.022 × 10²³).

9. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
   (a) 1 mole of carbon and 1 mole of water have the same mass.
   (b) 1 mole of O₂ molecules contains 6.022 × 10²³ molecules.
   (c) 1 mole of O₂ molecules contains 6.022 × 10²³ atoms.
   (d) n = M / m.

10. A student counts one particle per second. Roughly how many years would it take to count 6.022 × 10²³ particles (take 1 year as 3.15 × 10⁷ seconds)?

### Answers

1. 80 g ÷ 40 = 2 g ek button. 3 kg = 3000 g, to 3000 ÷ 2 = **1500 buttons**. Jaanch: 1500 × 2 = 3000 g ✅

2. Mole ginti ki ikai hai: 1 mole = 6.022 × 10²³ kan. Jaise 1 darjan = 12 aur 1 gross = 144 (kisi bhi cheez ke liye), waise hi 1 mole kisi bhi kan ke 6.022 × 10²³ ke liye. Ye number hai, vazan nahi.

3. (a) O₂ = 2×16 = **32 g/mol**. (b) CO₂ = 12 + 2×16 = **44 g/mol**. (c) NH₃ = 14 + 3×1 = **17 g/mol**. (d) CaCO₃ = 40 + 12 + 3×16 = **100 g/mol**.

4. H₂O ka M = 18 g/mol. n = 36 / 18 = **2 mol**. N = 2 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **1.204 × 10²⁴ molecules**.

5. n = 6 / 12 = 0.5 mol. N = 0.5 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **3.011 × 10²³ atoms**.

6. M = 23 + 35.5 = 58.5 g/mol. m = 0.25 × 58.5 = **14.625 g** (lagbhag 14.6 g).

7. Paani: 18 / 18 = 1 mol. Oxygen: 32 / 32 = 1 mol. **Dono mein kan ki ginti barabar hai**, 6.022 × 10²³ molecules.

8. 12 ÷ 6.022 × 10²³ = lagbhag **2.0 × 10⁻²³ g**.

9. (a) **False** ❌. Mole barabar hain par vazan alag: C 12 g, H₂O 18 g. (b) **True** ✅. (c) **False** ❌. Molecules 6.022 × 10²³, par atoms 2 × 6.022 × 10²³ ≈ 1.204 × 10²⁴. (d) **False** ❌. Sahi hai n = m / M.

10. 6.022 × 10²³ ÷ 3.15 × 10⁷ = lagbhag **1.9 × 10¹⁶ saal**, brahmaand ki umra se bhi lakhon guna zyada (andaza).
