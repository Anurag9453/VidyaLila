# Topic 6: Mole Ke Sawaal: Mass, Moles aur Kan

Pichhle topic mein humne mole ko ek **ginti ki ikai** ki tarah seekha: 1 mole = 6.022 × 10²³ kan, aur 1 mole ka vazan (grams mein) bilkul wahi sankhya hota hai jo atomic ya molecular mass hai. Ab aayi **practice** ki baari. Is topic mein hum ek saaf-suthra step method banayenge aur uske saath kai sawaal hal karenge, taaki mass, moles aur kan ke beech aana-jaana bilkul aasaan ho jaaye.

**Atomic masses (rounded, poore topic mein yahi use honge):** H 1, C 12, N 14, O 16, Na 23, Mg 24, S 32, Cl 35.5, Ca 40, Fe 56, Cu 63.5. Avogadro ki sankhya 6.022 × 10²³ per mole.

---

## Caterer ki shaadi

Mr. Bhide ke bhai ki shaadi thi, aur caterer Pappu bhaiya ke paas 350 mehmaanon ka order tha. Zoravar, Mr. Bhide ka bhatija, unke saath menu dekh raha tha. "Har mehmaan ko 2 rasgulle," Pappu bhaiya ne kaha. Zoravar ne jod diya: 350 × 2 = **700 rasgulle**. Phir usne poocha, "Par bhaiya, mithai to aap kilo mein mangate ho. Ye 700 kilo mein kitne hue?"

Pappu bhaiya ne ek rasgulla tarazu par rakha: 40 g. "Bas, ab simple hai. 700 × 40 g = 28000 g = **28 kg**. Dukaan wale ko bolunga, 28 kilo bhejo."

Zoravar ne socha: "Bhaiya, aapne to **ginti (pieces) se vazan (kilo)** nikaal liya, ek hi 'ek piece ka vazan' ke sahare!" Mr. Bhide muskuraye, "Bilkul. Chemistry mein bhi yahi hota hai. Wahan 'piece' ek atom ya molecule hai, aur 'kilo' ki jagah grams hain. Bas ginti itni badi hoti hai ki hum use **mole** mein gin lete hain, aur dono ke beech **molar mass** ek pul ka kaam karta hai."

Aaj ke saare sawaal isi caterer wale tareeke ke hain: kabhi vazan se ginti, kabhi ginti se vazan.

## Part A: Teen raaste, ek hi pul

Pichhle topic ka mole triangle (m, n, M) aur moles se kano tak wala pul yaad karo. Ab dono ko ek hi naqshe mein jodte hain. Mass, moles aur kan teen alag-alag cheezein hain, aur **mole beech ka pul** hai. Seedha mass se kan, ya kan se mass nahi jaate: pehle **mole** tak aate hain, phir doosri taraf jaate hain.

```
               MASS (m, g)
                │      ▲
          ÷ M   │      │   × M
                ▼      │
               MOLES (n)
                │      ▲
   × 6.022×10²³ │      │   ÷ 6.022×10²³
                ▼      │
             KAN (N, ginti)
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Conversion road map: mass aur moles ke beech M (molar mass) se, moles aur kan ke beech 6.022 × 10²³ se. Mass se kan tak ya kan se mass tak hamesha moles se hokar jaate hain.

**Chaar formule (sab Topic 5 ke do rishton se hi nikle hain):**

- n = m ÷ M  (mass se moles)
- m = n × M  (moles se mass)
- N = n × 6.022 × 10²³  (moles se kan)
- n = N ÷ 6.022 × 10²³  (kan se moles)

---

## Part B: Hal karne ka step method

Har sawaal ke liye yahi chaar kadam:

1. **Likho kya diya hai** aur kya poocha hai (mass? moles? kan?).
2. **M nikalo.** Formula se molar mass banao (subscript aur bracket dhyaan se).
3. **Moles tak aao.** Agar diya hua mass hai to m ÷ M; agar kan hai to N ÷ 6.022 × 10²³.
4. **Jo poocha hai wahan jao**, aur jawab ko **Jaanch** karo (ulta chal kar wapas wahi mila ya nahi, ya sankhya ka size theek lagta hai ya nahi).

**Scientific notation ki seedhi baatein:**

- 2 × 6.022 × 10²³ = 12.044 × 10²³ = 1.2044 × 10²⁴ ≈ **1.204 × 10²⁴** (10 ke power ko ek badhao jab pehli sankhya 10 se badi ho jaaye).
- Chhoti sankhya ko badi se bhaag: 3.011 × 10²² ÷ 6.022 × 10²³ = 0.5 × 10⁻¹ = 0.05.
- **Significant figures:** hum **4 significant figures** rakhenge (kyunki 6.022 mein 4 hain). Jahan jawab bilkul saaf hota hai (jaise exact 2 mol) wahan wahi likhenge.

## Part C: Solved problems

### Problem 1. Mass se moles: 36 g paani

36 g paani (H₂O) mein kitne mole hain?

- M(H₂O) = 2 × 1 + 16 = 18 g per mol.
- n = 36 ÷ 18 = **2 mol**.

Jaanch: 2 mol × 18 g per mol = 36 g ✅

### Problem 2. Mass se moles: 11 g CO₂

11 g carbon dioxide mein kitne mole?

- M(CO₂) = 12 + 2 × 16 = 44 g per mol.
- n = 11 ÷ 44 = **0.25 mol**.

Jaanch: 0.25 × 44 = 11 g ✅

### Problem 3. Moles se mass: 0.5 mol NaCl

0.5 mol namak (NaCl) ka vazan kitna?

- M(NaCl) = 23 + 35.5 = 58.5 g per mol.
- m = 0.5 × 58.5 = **29.25 g**.

Jaanch: 29.25 ÷ 58.5 = 0.5 mol ✅

### Problem 4. Moles se kan: 2 mol paani

2 mol paani mein kitne molecules?

- N = 2 × 6.022 × 10²³ = 12.044 × 10²³ = **1.204 × 10²⁴ molecules** (4 sig. figs.).

Jaanch: 1.204 × 10²⁴ ÷ 6.022 × 10²³ ≈ 2 mol ✅ (aakhri ank ka chhota fark sirf rounding ka hai)

### Problem 5. Kan se moles: 3.011 × 10²² molecules

Kisi sample mein 3.011 × 10²² molecules hain. Kitne mole?

- n = 3.011 × 10²² ÷ 6.022 × 10²³ = 0.5 × 10⁻¹ = **0.05 mol**.

Jaanch: 0.05 × 6.022 × 10²³ = 3.011 × 10²² ✅

### Problem 6. Mass se kan (do kadam): 9 g paani

9 g paani mein kitne molecules?

- Kadam 1 (mass se moles): n = 9 ÷ 18 = 0.5 mol.
- Kadam 2 (moles se kan): N = 0.5 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **3.011 × 10²³ molecules**.

Jaanch: Ulta chalo: 3.011 × 10²³ ÷ 6.022 × 10²³ = 0.5 mol, aur 0.5 × 18 = 9 g ✅

### Problem 7. Kan se mass (do kadam): 3.011 × 10²³ molecules CO₂

Itne molecules CO₂ ka vazan?

- Kadam 1: n = 3.011 × 10²³ ÷ 6.022 × 10²³ = 0.5 mol.
- Kadam 2: m = 0.5 × 44 = **22 g**.

Jaanch: 22 ÷ 44 = 0.5 mol, 0.5 × 6.022 × 10²³ = 3.011 × 10²³ ✅

### Problem 8. Molecule ke andar ke atoms: 36 g paani

36 g paani mein hydrogen aur oxygen ke kitne **atoms**?

Pehle ek mole ka naksha dekho:

```
   1 mole H₂O  =  6.022 × 10²³ molecules

        H₂O                          H    H
       ┌────┐                         \  /
       │ 1  │   ek molecule mein        O
       └────┘   2 H aur 1 O

   1 mol H₂O  ──►  2 mol H atoms
                   1 mol O atoms
                   -------------
                   3 mol atoms kul
```

> **Figure 6.2** – 1 mole H₂O ke andar: 2 mole H atoms, 1 mole O atoms, kul 3 mole atoms. Molecules ki ginti 6.022 × 10²³, par atoms ki ginti usse teen guna.

- n(H₂O) = 36 ÷ 18 = 2 mol.
- H atoms: 2 mol × 2 = **4 mol**, yaani 4 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **2.409 × 10²⁴ H atoms**.
- O atoms: 2 mol × 1 = **2 mol**, yaani 2 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **1.204 × 10²⁴ O atoms**.
- Kul atoms: 6 mol = 6 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **3.613 × 10²⁴ atoms**.

Jaanch: 4 + 2 = 6 mol ✅. Aur 2.409 × 10²⁴ + 1.204 × 10²⁴ = 3.613 × 10²⁴ ✅

### Problem 9. Kaun zyada molecules wala: 18 g paani, 32 g oxygen, 2 g hydrogen

- n(H₂O) = 18 ÷ 18 = 1 mol.
- n(O₂) = 32 ÷ 32 = 1 mol. (M(O₂) = 2 × 16 = 32)
- n(H₂) = 2 ÷ 2 = 1 mol. (M(H₂) = 2 × 1 = 2)

Teeno mein **barabar molecules** hain: har ek mein 6.022 × 10²³. Vazan 18 g, 32 g aur 2 g alag hain, par ginti ek jaisi! Aur atoms? H₂O mein 3 mol atoms, O₂ mein 2 mol, H₂ mein 2 mol. To **atoms sabse zyada paani mein** hain.

Jaanch: 1 mol × 18, 32 aur 2 g per mol wapas ye teeno mass deta hai ✅

### Problem 9b. Barabar vazan, kya ginti bhi barabar? (10 g har ek)

10 g H₂, 10 g O₂ aur 10 g H₂O mein kaun zyada molecules wala?

- n(H₂) = 10 ÷ 2 = **5 mol**.
- n(O₂) = 10 ÷ 32 = **0.3125 mol**.
- n(H₂O) = 10 ÷ 18 = **0.5556 mol**.

Barabar vazan lene par **sabse halka molecule (H₂) sabse zyada** ginti mein aata hai: 5 mol = 5 × 6.022 × 10²³ = 3.011 × 10²⁴ molecules. O₂ ke 0.3125 mol = 1.882 × 10²³ molecules.

Jaanch: 5 × 2 = 10 g, 0.3125 × 32 = 10 g, 0.5556 × 18 ≈ 10 g ✅

Caterer jaisi soch: 10 kg ke dher mein halke rasgulle zyada aate hain, bhaari gulab jamun kam. (Ye sirf ek moti tulna hai; asli niyam ye hai ki har padaarth ke **1 mole mein kan barabar** hote hain, bas 1 mole ka vazan alag hota hai.)

### Problem 10. Caterer ka order, aur uska chemistry jodidaar

**Pehla bhaag (shaadi):** 350 mehmaan, har ek ko 2 rasgulle, ek rasgulla 40 g. Kul order?

- Pieces: 350 × 2 = 700. Vazan: 700 × 40 = 28000 g = **28 kg**.

**Doosra bhaag (chemistry):** 3.011 × 10²⁴ molecules paani ka vazan?

- n = 3.011 × 10²⁴ ÷ 6.022 × 10²³ = 5 mol.
- m = 5 × 18 = **90 g**.

Jaanch: Dono mein aakar wahi pul: ginti → (ek ka vazan) → kul vazan. 90 ÷ 18 = 5 mol, 5 × 6.022 × 10²³ = 3.011 × 10²⁴ ✅

### Problem 11. Ek molecule ya ek atom ka vazan

(a) Ek molecule paani ka vazan grams mein?

- 1 mol (18 g) mein 6.022 × 10²³ molecules.
- Ek molecule = 18 ÷ 6.022 × 10²³ = **2.989 × 10⁻²³ g**.

(b) Ek copper atom ka vazan?

- Ek Cu atom = 63.5 ÷ 6.022 × 10²³ = **1.054 × 10⁻²² g**.

Jaanch: (a) 2.989 × 10⁻²³ × 6.022 × 10²³ = 18.0 g ✅. (b) 1.054 × 10⁻²² × 6.022 × 10²³ = 63.5 g ✅

### Problem 12. Barabar vazan, atoms ki ginti: 12 g carbon aur 12 g magnesium

- n(C) = 12 ÷ 12 = 1 mol, yaani **6.022 × 10²³ atoms**.
- n(Mg) = 12 ÷ 24 = 0.5 mol, yaani **3.011 × 10²³ atoms**.

Carbon ke atoms Mg se **do guna** hain, kyunki carbon ka ek atom Mg se aadha bhaari hai.

Jaanch: 1 mol × 12 = 12 g aur 0.5 mol × 24 = 12 g ✅

### Problem 13. Molecule ke andar ka sirf ek tatva: 22 g CO₂ mein O atoms

- n(CO₂) = 22 ÷ 44 = 0.5 mol.
- Har CO₂ mein 2 O atoms: n(O atoms) = 0.5 × 2 = 1 mol.
- Atoms ki ginti = 1 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **6.022 × 10²³ O atoms**.

Jaanch: 6.022 × 10²³ O atoms = 1 mol O = 16 g O. 22 g CO₂ mein oxygen ka mass (32 ÷ 44 ka hissa) = 22 × 32 ÷ 44 = 16 g ✅

### Problem 14. Brackets: 37 g Ca(OH)₂ mein H aur O atoms

- M[Ca(OH)₂] = 40 + 2 × (16 + 1) = 40 + 34 = 74 g per mol.
- n = 37 ÷ 74 = 0.5 mol.
- Har formula mein 2 O aur 2 H (bracket ke bahar ka 2 dono par lagta hai): O = 0.5 × 2 = 1 mol, H = 0.5 × 2 = 1 mol.
- O atoms = H atoms = **6.022 × 10²³** har ek.

Jaanch: Ca atoms 0.5 mol = 3.011 × 10²³. Kul atoms 0.5 × 5 = 2.5 mol ✅

### Problem 15. Ek gram paani mein bhi bahut saare molecules

1 g paani mein kitne molecules?

- n = 1 ÷ 18 = 0.0556 mol.
- N = (1 ÷ 18) × 6.022 × 10²³ = **3.346 × 10²² molecules**.

1 g paani to bas lagbhag 20 boond jitna hai, par 3.346 × 10²² ko poora likho to 23 ankon ki sankhya banti hai. Mole ki zaroorat isi liye padi.

Jaanch: 3.346 × 10²² ÷ 6.022 × 10²³ = 0.0556 mol, × 18 = 1.00 g ✅

### Problem 16. Glucose: 90 g mein

90 g glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆, M = 180 g per mol) mein molecules aur carbon atoms?

- n = 90 ÷ 180 = 0.5 mol.
- Molecules = 0.5 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **3.011 × 10²³**.
- Har molecule mein 6 C: n(C) = 0.5 × 6 = 3 mol, yaani 3 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **1.807 × 10²⁴ C atoms**.

Jaanch: 3 mol C × 12 = 36 g carbon; 90 g glucose mein carbon = 90 × 72 ÷ 180 = 36 g ✅

## Part D: Rounding aur sig. figs. kaise rakhein

- Atomic masses humne **rounded** liye hain (H 1, O 16 ...). Isliye jawab bhi usi tarah rounded hote hain.
- Scientific notation mein **pehli sankhya 1 se 10 ke beech** rakhte hain: 12.044 × 10²³ ko 1.204 × 10²⁴ likho.
- Hum **4 significant figures** rakhte hain, kyunki 6.022 chaar ankon ka hai. Agar jawab bilkul pakka hai (jaise 2 mol) to wahi likho.
- Bhaag dene par 10 ke powers **ghatate** hain (10²² ÷ 10²³ = 10⁻¹), aur guna karne par **jodte** hain (10²³ × 10¹ = 10²⁴).

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Atoms aur molecules ko ek samajh lena**

1 mol H₂O = 6.022 × 10²³ **molecules**, par atoms ke liye 3 se guna karna hota hai (2 H + 1 O). Sawaal padho: "molecules" ya "atoms"?

**Galti 2: Subscript bhool jaana**

O₂ ka M = 2 × 16 = **32**, 16 nahi. CO₂ mein O ke 2 atoms hain. Bracket mein Ca(OH)₂ mein OH do baar aata hai, 1 baar nahi.

**Galti 3: Seedha mass ko 6.022 × 10²³ se guna karna**

Kan tak hamesha **moles** se hokar jaate hain. 18 g × 6.022 × 10²³ galat hai; pehle 18 ÷ 18 = 1 mol, phir kan.

**Galti 4: Unit mistakes**

Molar mass **g per mol** hai, kg per mol nahi. Agar mass kg mein diya ho to pehle grams mein badlo (28 kg = 28000 g).

**Galti 5: Barabar vazan matlab barabar kan samajhna**

10 g H₂ aur 10 g O₂ mein kan barabar nahi hain (5 mol aur 0.3125 mol). Barabar **moles** matlab barabar kan.

**Galti 6: Scientific notation mein power ki galti**

12.044 × 10²³ ko 1.2044 × 10²³ likhna galat hai; sahi 1.2044 × 10²⁴ hai (4 significant figures mein 1.204 × 10²⁴). Pehli sankhya ko 1 aur 10 ke beech laate samay power ek badhana (ya ghatana) mat bhoolo.

## Quick Recap

- Mass, moles aur kan ke beech **mole pul** hai: mass ↔ moles M se, moles ↔ kan 6.022 × 10²³ se. Seedha mass se kan nahi.
- Step method: kya diya/poocha, M nikalo, moles tak aao, phir jahan jaana ho wahan jao, aur **Jaanch** karo.
- n = m ÷ M, m = n × M, N = n × 6.022 × 10²³, n = N ÷ 6.022 × 10²³.
- 1 mol H₂O = 6.022 × 10²³ molecules = 2 mol H atoms + 1 mol O atoms = 3 mol atoms.
- 18 g H₂O, 32 g O₂ aur 2 g H₂ mein **molecules barabar** (1 mol har ek), par atoms alag (3, 2, 2 mol).
- Barabar vazan lene par halka kan (H₂) sabse zyada ginti mein aata hai.
- Ek molecule ya atom ka vazan = M ÷ 6.022 × 10²³ (H₂O: 2.989 × 10⁻²³ g; Cu atom: 1.054 × 10⁻²² g).
- Jawab 4 significant figures mein rakhte hain; atomic masses rounded hain.

## Practice Questions

1. How many moles are present in 54 g of water? How many molecules is that?

2. What is the mass of 0.2 mol of carbon dioxide?

3. A sample has 6.022 × 10²² molecules of ammonia (NH₃, M = 17 g per mol). Find its mass.

4. Find the number of copper atoms in 127 g of copper.

5. How many moles of atoms of each element are in 49 g of sulphuric acid (H₂SO₄, M = 98 g per mol)? How many hydrogen atoms is that?

6. Which has the most molecules: 8 g of methane (CH₄), 8 g of oxygen (O₂) or 8 g of hydrogen (H₂)? Give the moles of each.

7. Find the mass of one molecule of carbon dioxide in grams.

8. A caterer needs 3 sweets per guest for 240 guests, each sweet weighing 25 g. How many kilograms should he order? Which step of the mole method does this match?

9. A student says, "18 g of water has 6.022 × 10²³ atoms." Find the mistake and give the correct number of atoms.

10. How many carbon atoms and total atoms are in 18 g of glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆, M = 180 g per mol)?

### Answers

1. n = 54 ÷ 18 = **3 mol**. Molecules = 3 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **1.807 × 10²⁴**. Jaanch: 3 × 6.022 = 18.066, yaani 1.807 × 10²⁴ ✅; 3 × 18 = 54 g ✅

2. M(CO₂) = 44. m = 0.2 × 44 = **8.8 g**. Jaanch: 8.8 ÷ 44 = 0.2 mol ✅

3. n = 6.022 × 10²² ÷ 6.022 × 10²³ = 0.1 mol. m = 0.1 × 17 = **1.7 g**. Jaanch: 1.7 ÷ 17 = 0.1 mol, 0.1 × 6.022 × 10²³ = 6.022 × 10²² ✅

4. n = 127 ÷ 63.5 = 2 mol. Atoms = 2 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **1.204 × 10²⁴ Cu atoms**. Jaanch: 2 × 63.5 = 127 g ✅

5. n(H₂SO₄) = 49 ÷ 98 = 0.5 mol. H = 0.5 × 2 = **1 mol**, S = 0.5 × 1 = **0.5 mol**, O = 0.5 × 4 = **2 mol**; kul 3.5 mol atoms. H atoms = 1 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **6.022 × 10²³**. Jaanch: 1 + 0.5 + 2 = 3.5 mol ✅

6. n(CH₄) = 8 ÷ 16 = 0.5 mol; n(O₂) = 8 ÷ 32 = 0.25 mol; n(H₂) = 8 ÷ 2 = 4 mol. **Hydrogen mein sabse zyada molecules** hain (4 mol = 2.409 × 10²⁴). Jaanch: 0.5 × 16 = 8, 0.25 × 32 = 8, 4 × 2 = 8 g ✅

7. M(CO₂) = 44. Ek molecule = 44 ÷ 6.022 × 10²³ = **7.307 × 10⁻²³ g**. Jaanch: 7.307 × 10⁻²³ × 6.022 × 10²³ = 44.0 g ✅

8. Sweets = 240 × 3 = 720. Vazan = 720 × 25 = 18000 g = **18 kg**. Ye "kan se mass" jaisa hai: ginti × ek ka vazan. Jaanch: 18000 ÷ 25 = 720 ✅

9. 18 g paani = 1 mol = 6.022 × 10²³ **molecules**, atoms nahi. Har molecule mein 3 atoms hain, isliye atoms = 3 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **1.807 × 10²⁴**. Jaanch: 1 mol × 3 = 3 mol atoms ✅

10. n = 18 ÷ 180 = 0.1 mol. C = 0.1 × 6 = 0.6 mol, yaani 0.6 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **3.613 × 10²³ C atoms**. Kul atoms per molecule = 6 + 12 + 6 = 24, to 0.1 × 24 = 2.4 mol = 2.4 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **1.445 × 10²⁴ atoms**. Jaanch: 0.6 + 1.2 + 0.6 = 2.4 mol ✅
