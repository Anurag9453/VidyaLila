# Topic 7: Percentage Composition aur Chapter Ka Saar

Pichhle topic mein humne mole ke sawaal hal kiye: kisi cheez ka mass dekh kar moles aur kanon ki ginti nikaalna, aur ginti se wapas mass tak aana. Us khel mein humne har baar molar mass use kiya. Ab usi molar mass ko ek aur kaam mein lagate hain: kisi yaugik mein **har tatva ka kitna hissa (kitne percent) hai**, ye formula se seedha nikaalna. Aur aakhir mein chapter ke saare 7 topics ko ek table mein samet lenge.

Ek chhoti baat pehle: is poore topic mein hum Topic 4 wale **rounded atomic masses** hi use karenge: H 1, C 12, N 14, O 16, Na 23, S 32, Cl 35.5, Ca 40. Isliye jawab kabhi-kabhi last decimal mein thoda alag aa sakte hain; har jagah rounding likhi gayi hai.

## Part A: Percentage composition ka formula

Topic 4 mein humne dekha tha ki formula se molecular ya formula unit mass nikalta hai, aur Topic 2 mein ki yaugik mein tatvon ka mass-ratio fixed hota hai. Ab dono ko jod dete hain.

**Mass percent of an element** = (formula mein us tatva ka kul mass) ÷ (yaugik ka molecular ya formula unit mass) × 100

Steps:

1. Formula likho aur har tatva ke atoms gino (subscript aur bracket dhyan se).
2. Har tatva ka kul mass nikalo: atoms ki ginti × atomic mass.
3. Saare tatvon ke mass jodo: ye yaugik ka molecular ya formula unit mass hoga.
4. Har tatva ka mass ÷ kul mass × 100.
5. **Jaanch:** saare percent jodo, 100 (ya rounding ke karan 99.9 ya 100.1) aana chahiye.

### Example 1. Paani, H₂O

- H: 2 × 1 = 2. O: 1 × 16 = 16. Kul = 18.
- H % = 2 ÷ 18 × 100 = 11.11%. O % = 16 ÷ 18 × 100 = 88.89%.

Jaanch: 11.11 + 88.89 = 100 ✅

Dhyan do: Topic 2 mein paani ka ratio H : O = 1 : 8 (mass mein) tha. Yahan 11.11 : 88.89 = 1 : 8, wahi baat, bas percent ki bhasha mein. (11.11 ka 8 guna = 88.88, rounding ki wajah se 88.89 se thoda alag dikhta hai.)

### Example 2. Calcium carbonate, CaCO₃ (chalk, sangmarmar)

- Ca: 1 × 40 = 40. C: 1 × 12 = 12. O: 3 × 16 = 48. Kul = 100.
- Ca = 40 ÷ 100 × 100 = 40%. C = 12%. O = 48%.

Jaanch: 40 + 12 + 48 = 100 ✅

```
   CaCO₃ ke 100 hisse (1 block = 4%)

   Ca: ##########   (10 blocks = 40%)
   C : ###          (3 blocks  = 12%)
   O : ############ (12 blocks = 48%)
```

> **Figure 7.1** – CaCO₃ ka percentage bar: 1 block = 4%. Ca ke 10 blocks (40%), C ke 3 blocks (12%) aur O ke 12 blocks (48%): kul 25 blocks = 100%.

### Example 3. Table salt, NaCl

- Na = 23, Cl = 35.5. Kul = 58.5.
- Na = 23 ÷ 58.5 × 100 = 39.32 ≈ **39.3%**. Cl = 35.5 ÷ 58.5 × 100 = 60.68 ≈ **60.7%**.

Jaanch: 39.3 + 60.7 = 100.0 ✅. Chapter 2 mein humne bataya tha ki namak mein lagbhag 39.3% sodium hota hai. Wahi sankhya aaj formula se hamare haath aa gayi.

### Example 4. Carbon dioxide, CO₂

- C = 12, O = 2 × 16 = 32. Kul = 44.
- C = 12 ÷ 44 × 100 = 27.27 ≈ **27.3%**. O = 32 ÷ 44 × 100 = 72.73 ≈ **72.7%**.

Jaanch: 27.3 + 72.7 = 100.0 ✅. Topic 2 ka ratio C : O = 3 : 8 yahan bhi dikhta hai (27.3 : 72.7 ≈ 3 : 8).

(Rounding: percent do decimal tak nikaal kar, jahan zaroorat hui wahan ek decimal tak round kiye.)

### Example 5. Glucose, C₆H₁₂O₆ (kuch dhyan se)

- C: 6 × 12 = 72. H: 12 × 1 = 12. O: 6 × 16 = 96. Kul = 180.
- C = 72 ÷ 180 × 100 = **40.0%**. H = 12 ÷ 180 × 100 = 6.67 ≈ **6.7%**. O = 96 ÷ 180 × 100 = 53.33 ≈ **53.3%**.

Jaanch: 40.0 + 6.7 + 53.3 = 100.0 ✅

## Part B: Ulta use: diye gaye mass mein tatva ka mass

Percentage composition sirf dekhne ke liye nahi, kaam ki cheez hai. Agar kisi yaugik ke diye gaye mass mein tatva ka mass chahiye:

**tatva ka mass = (tatva ka percent ÷ 100) × yaugik ka mass**

### Example 6. 250 g CaCO₃ mein kitna calcium?

Ca = 40%, to 40 ÷ 100 × 250 = **100 g**.

Jaanch: 100 g CaCO₃ mein 40 g Ca hai, to 250 g (2.5 guna) mein 40 × 2.5 = 100 g ✅

### Example 7. 36 g paani mein kitna oxygen aur kitna hydrogen?

O = 88.89% ya seedha 16/18 hissa. 36 × 16 ÷ 18 = **32 g oxygen**. H = 36 − 32 = **4 g hydrogen**.

Jaanch: 4 : 32 = 1 : 8 ✅ (Topic 2 ka ratio).

### Example 8. 117 g namak (NaCl) mein kitna sodium?

Na ka hissa = 23 ÷ 58.5. 117 × 23 ÷ 58.5 = **46 g**. (Kyunki 117 = 2 × 58.5, yaani 2 mole namak mein 2 × 23 = 46 g Na.)

Jaanch: 46 ÷ 117 × 100 = 39.3% ✅

### Example 9. 200 g CO₂ mein kitna carbon?

C ka hissa = 12 ÷ 44 = 3 ÷ 11. 200 × 3 ÷ 11 = 54.545... ≈ **54.5 g** (ek decimal tak round).

Jaanch: 54.5 ÷ 200 × 100 = 27.3% ✅

## Part C: Percentage hamesha fixed kyun?

Kisi bhi paani ka namoona lo: Himalayan jharne ka, ghar ke nal ka, ya hydrogen jalakar banaya hua. Har namoone mein H = 11.11% aur O = 88.89% hi milega. Topic 2 ka **Law of Constant Proportions** yahi kehta hai: shuddh yaugik mein tatvon ka mass-ratio fixed hota hai, isliye percent bhi fixed hota hai.

**Example 10.** 900 g paani mein hydrogen aur oxygen kitne?

- H = 11.11% ka 900 = 100 g (ya 900 × 2 ÷ 18 = 100 g). O = 900 − 100 = 800 g.
- Ratio 100 : 800 = 1 : 8 ✅.

Ab ek sawaal: 5 g paani lo, ya 5 tanker. Percent badlega? **Nahi.** Matra kam-zyada hone se hisse ka percent nahi badalta; ye sirf formula aur atomic masses se tay hota hai. Isiliye percentage composition ek **pehchaan** ki tarah bhi kaam aati hai: agar kisi "paani" mein H 11.11% nahi mila, to wo shuddh paani nahi hai (usme kuch mila hua hai).

## Part D: Khaad ki dukaan wapas: kaun sa fertiliser zyada nitrogen deta hai?

Hetvi ne teen khaad ke formula likhe aur har ek mein nitrogen ka percent nikaala.

### Urea, CO(NH₂)₂

Bracket ke andar NH₂ hai aur uska 2 se guna hai:

- C: 1 × 12 = 12. O: 1 × 16 = 16. N: 2 × 14 = 28. H: 2 × 2 × 1 = 4. Kul = 12 + 16 + 28 + 4 = **60**.
- N = 28 ÷ 60 × 100 = 46.67 ≈ **46.7%**.

### Ammonium nitrate, NH₄NO₃

- N: 2 × 14 = 28 (ek NH₄⁺ se, ek NO₃⁻ se). H: 4 × 1 = 4. O: 3 × 16 = 48. Kul = 28 + 4 + 48 = **80**.
- N = 28 ÷ 80 × 100 = **35.0%**.

### Ammonium sulphate, (NH₄)₂SO₄

- N: 2 × 14 = 28. H: 8 × 1 = 8. S: 32. O: 4 × 16 = 64. Kul = 28 + 8 + 32 + 64 = **132**.
- N = 28 ÷ 132 × 100 = 21.21 ≈ **21.2%**.

Teeno mein N ka kul mass 28 hi hai, par kul formula mass alag-alag, isliye percent alag-alag.

```
   Nitrogen ki hissedaari (100 kg khaad mein, 1 block = 2 kg)

   Urea             : #######################  46.7 kg
   Ammonium nitrate : ##################       35.0 kg
   Ammonium sulphate: ###########              21.2 kg
```

> **Figure 7.2** – Teen khaad ke 100 kg mein nitrogen: urea 46.7 kg, ammonium nitrate 35.0 kg, ammonium sulphate 21.2 kg (1 block = 2 kg, nazdeeki poore block tak round kiya: 23, 18 aur 11 blocks). Urea mein sabse zyada, ammonium sulphate mein sabse kam.

**Jaanch:** urea 46.7 ÷ 35.0 ≈ 1.33. Yaani urea mein ammonium nitrate se lagbhag 1.33 guna nitrogen. Seedha 28/60 aur 28/80 se: 80 ÷ 60 = 1.33 ✅

### Ek bori mein kitna nitrogen? (50 kg ki bori)

- Urea: 50 × 28 ÷ 60 = **23.33 kg** nitrogen.
- Ammonium nitrate: 50 × 28 ÷ 80 = **17.5 kg**.
- Ammonium sulphate: 50 × 28 ÷ 132 = **10.61 kg**.

### Kharch ka hisaab: paisa kis par vasool?

**Dhyan se: neeche ke daam sirf padhne ke liye bane hue (made-up) udaaharan hain, asli bazaar bhav nahi hain.**

Maan lo ek 50 kg ki bori ka daam:

| Khaad | Maan liya daam (50 kg bori) | Nitrogen (kg) | Daam per kg nitrogen |
|---|---|---|---|
| Urea | ₹300 | 23.33 | 300 ÷ 23.33 = ₹12.86 |
| Ammonium nitrate | ₹250 | 17.5 | 250 ÷ 17.5 = ₹14.29 |
| Ammonium sulphate | ₹180 | 10.61 | 180 ÷ 10.61 = ₹16.97 |

(Rounding: daam do decimal tak.)

Dekho kya hua! **Ammonium sulphate ki bori sabse sasti (₹180) hai, par uska nitrogen sabse mehnga (lagbhag ₹17 per kg).** Urea ki bori sabse mehngi hai, par nitrogen sabse sasta (lagbhag ₹12.9 per kg). Isiliye Hetvi ne kaka se kaha: "Bori ka daam mat dekhiye, **per kg nitrogen ka daam** dekhiye."

**Example 11.** Dharam kaka ko 14 kg nitrogen chahiye. Teeno khaadon ki kitni-kitni matra lagegi, aur (made-up daam par, ₹6, ₹5 aur ₹3.6 per kg) kharch kitna?

- Urea: 14 ÷ (28 ÷ 60) = 14 × 60 ÷ 28 = **30 kg**. Kharch 30 × 6 = **₹180**.
- Ammonium nitrate: 14 × 80 ÷ 28 = **40 kg**. Kharch 40 × 5 = **₹200**.
- Ammonium sulphate: 14 × 132 ÷ 28 = **66 kg**. Kharch 66 × 3.6 = **₹237.60**.

Jaanch: 30 kg urea mein nitrogen = 30 × 28 ÷ 60 = 14 kg ✅; 40 kg AN mein 40 × 28 ÷ 80 = 14 kg ✅; 66 kg AS mein 66 × 28 ÷ 132 = 14 kg ✅.

Is made-up daam par urea sabse sasti rahi, ammonium nitrate beech mein, aur ammonium sulphate sabse mehngi, jabki bori ka daam ulta dikhata tha.

> **Zaroori baat:** asli zindagi mein kisan sirf nitrogen nahi dekhta. Mitti kaisi hai, fasal ko sulphur ya kuch aur chahiye ya nahi (jaise ammonium sulphate mein sulphur bhi hota hai), ye sab bhi dekha jaata hai, aur khaad ki sahi matra krishi sahayak se poochni chahiye. Hum yahan sirf **hisaab** seekh rahe hain; ye asli salah nahi hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Bracket ko ignore karna**

(NH₄)₂SO₄ mein N ke 2 atoms hain aur H ke 8. Agar H = 4 gin liya to kul 128 aa jaayega, jabki sahi 132 hai. CO(NH₂)₂ mein bhi NH₂ do baar hai (N = 2, H = 4).

**Galti 2: Tatva ke ek atom ka mass le lena, atoms ki ginti bhool jaana**

Paani mein H ka mass 2 hai, 1 nahi. 1 ÷ 18 × 100 = 5.56% galat hai; sahi 2 ÷ 18 × 100 = 11.11%.

**Galti 3: Sab percent jodna bhool jaana**

Hamesha jaanch lo ki total 100 (ya rounding ke karan 99.9 ya 100.1) aaye. Agar total 87 ya 130 aa raha hai, to kahin kul mass galat jodha hai.

**Galti 4: Percent aur mass ko mila dena**

40% Ca ka matlab "40 g Ca" nahi; ye tabhi 40 g hai jab yaugik **100 g** ho. 250 g mein to 100 g Ca hota hai.

**Galti 5: "Mehngi bori matlab zyada nitrogen"**

Nitrogen ka hisaab bori ke daam se nahi, **per kg nitrogen** ke daam se lagta hai. Sasti bori bhi zyada mehngi nitrogen de sakti hai.

**Galti 6: Rounding ke chakkar mein samajh lena ki ratio badal gayi**

11.11 aur 88.89 ka 1 : 8 hona rounding ki wajah se thoda hilta hai (11.11 × 8 = 88.88). Asli ratio 2 : 16 = 1 : 8 hi hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Mass percent** = (tatva ka kul mass formula mein ÷ yaugik ka molecular ya formula unit mass) × 100. Saare percent ka jod 100 hona chahiye.
- H₂O: H 11.11%, O 88.89%. CaCO₃: Ca 40%, C 12%, O 48%. NaCl: Na 39.3%, Cl 60.7%. CO₂: C 27.3%, O 72.7%. Glucose: C 40.0%, H 6.7%, O 53.3%.
- **Ulta use:** tatva ka mass = (percent ÷ 100) × yaugik ka mass. 250 g CaCO₃ mein 100 g Ca; 36 g paani mein 32 g O.
- Percent **fixed** hai, kyunki Law of Constant Proportions kehta hai ki shuddh yaugik mein tatvon ka ratio fixed hota hai. Matra kam-zyada karne se percent nahi badalta.
- **Khaad:** nitrogen % urea 46.7, ammonium nitrate 35.0, ammonium sulphate 21.2. Bori ke daam se nahi, **per kg nitrogen ke daam** se tulna karo. (Daam sirf made-up udaaharan the.)
- Brackets aur subscripts ka dhyan rakho, aur hamesha jaanch lo.
- Rounded atomic masses use hue hain; isliye jawab last decimal tak thoda alag ho sakte hain.

## Practice Questions

1. Find the percentage composition by mass of NaCl and show that the two percentages add up to 100. State the rounding you used.

2. Calculate the mass percent of each element in CaCO₃ and verify that the total is 100.

3. Calculate the mass percent of nitrogen and hydrogen in ammonia, NH₃. Give each to one decimal place and check that they add up to 100.0.

4. How much oxygen and how much hydrogen is present in 54 g of water? Check that your masses are in the ratio 1 : 8 for hydrogen to oxygen.

5. How much carbon is present in 90 g of glucose, C₆H₁₂O₆?

6. How many grams of CaCO₃ contain 20 g of calcium?

7. Which compound has a higher percentage of oxygen: H₂O or CO₂? Show your calculation.

8. A sample of water from a mountain stream and a sample of tap water are analysed. Both show H : O = 1 : 8 by mass. Which law explains this? What would you conclude if a "water" sample showed H : O = 1 : 7?

9. (Made-up prices, for practice only.) A farmer needs 28 kg of nitrogen. Urea costs ₹6 per kg, ammonium nitrate ₹5 per kg and ammonium sulphate ₹3.6 per kg (these are invented numbers). Find the mass of each fertiliser needed and the total cost for each. Which is cheapest for the nitrogen?

10. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) The mass percent of hydrogen in water changes if we take a bigger sample.
    (b) The mass percent of nitrogen in urea is higher than in ammonium nitrate.
    (c) The mass percents of all elements in a compound always add up to 100 (apart from small rounding).
    (d) The cheapest bag always gives the cheapest nitrogen.

### Answers

1. NaCl ka formula unit mass = 23 + 35.5 = 58.5. Na = 23 ÷ 58.5 × 100 = 39.32 ≈ **39.3%**. Cl = 35.5 ÷ 58.5 × 100 = 60.68 ≈ **60.7%**. Jaanch: 39.3 + 60.7 = 100.0 ✅ (ek decimal tak round kiya).

2. CaCO₃: Ca 40, C 12, O 3 × 16 = 48; kul = 100. **Ca = 40%, C = 12%, O = 48%.** Jaanch: 40 + 12 + 48 = 100 ✅

3. NH₃ ka molecular mass = 14 + 3 × 1 = 17. N = 14 ÷ 17 × 100 = 82.35 ≈ **82.4%**. H = 3 ÷ 17 × 100 = 17.65 ≈ **17.6%**. Jaanch: 82.4 + 17.6 = 100.0 ✅ (82.353 aur 17.647 ko ek decimal tak round kiya).

4. H₂O mein H ka hissa 2 ÷ 18 = 1 ÷ 9. 54 ÷ 9 = **6 g hydrogen**, aur O = 54 − 6 = **48 g oxygen** (ya 54 × 16 ÷ 18 = 48). Jaanch: 6 : 48 = 1 : 8 ✅

5. Glucose mein C = 72 ÷ 180 = 40%. 40 ÷ 100 × 90 = **36 g** carbon. Jaanch: 36 ÷ 90 × 100 = 40% ✅

6. Ca = 40%, to 20 g Ca ke liye CaCO₃ = 20 ÷ 0.40 = **50 g**. Jaanch: 50 g ka 40% = 20 g ✅

7. H₂O mein O = 16 ÷ 18 × 100 = 88.89%. CO₂ mein O = 32 ÷ 44 × 100 = 72.73%. **H₂O mein oxygen ka percent zyada hai.**

8. **Law of Constant Proportions** (Topic 2): shuddh yaugik mein tatvon ka mass-ratio fixed hota hai, chahe source kuch bhi ho. Agar kisi namoone mein H : O = 1 : 7 aaye, to ya to wo shuddh paani nahi hai (kuch mila hua hai) ya nap mein galti hai.

9. Urea mein N = 28 ÷ 60, to urea = 28 × 60 ÷ 28 = **60 kg**, kharch 60 × 6 = **₹360**. Ammonium nitrate = 28 × 80 ÷ 28 = **80 kg**, kharch 80 × 5 = **₹400**. Ammonium sulphate = 28 × 132 ÷ 28 = **132 kg**, kharch 132 × 3.6 = **₹475.20**. Made-up daam par **urea** sabse sasta nitrogen deta hai, bhale hi ammonium sulphate ka per kg daam sabse kam tha.

10. (a) **False** ❌. Percent fixed hai, matra badalne se nahi badalta. (b) **True** ✅. Urea 46.7%, ammonium nitrate 35.0%. (c) **True** ✅. Saare hisse milkar poora yaugik banate hain. (d) **False** ❌. Sasti bori mein nitrogen kam ho sakta hai; per kg nitrogen ka daam dekhna chahiye.
