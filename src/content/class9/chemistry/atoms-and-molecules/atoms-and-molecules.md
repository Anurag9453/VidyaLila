# Topic 1: Dravyaman Ka Niyam: Law of Conservation of Mass

Pichhle chapter ke aakhir mein humne reactions ko **balanced equations** mein likha, jaise 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O, aur ek rule ki tarah maana ki **har tatva ke atoms arrow ke dono taraf barabar** hone chahiye. Humne ye bhi kaha tha ki is rule ka asli naam aur kisne ise seedha dikhaya, wo is chapter mein aayega. Aur ek sawaal adhoora chhoda tha: atom kitna bhaari hota hai, aur itne chhote atoms ko gin kaise sakte hain? Is chapter mein wahi sab hai. Shuruaat us rule se karte hain jis par poori chemistry ka hisaab tika hai: **reaction mein kul dravyaman (mass) kabhi nahi badalta.**

---

## Bottle, tablet aur rasoi ka kata

Ms. Rajput ki bhatiji Aanya chhutti mein unke ghar aayi thi. Ms. Rajput ke paas ek antacid ki effervescent tablet thi, wahi jo paani mein daalte hi fizz karti hai. Aanya ne poochha, "Bua, ye tablet gayab kaise ho jaati hai? Kya iska vazan bhi khatam ho jaata hai?"

Ms. Rajput muskurayi. "Chal, rasoi ke digital kata (kitchen scale) par dekhte hain. Bas ek baat: ye sab tum bade ki nigrani mein hi karogi, aur tablet ko kabhi khana nahi hai."

Unhone ek plastic ki bottle li, usme thoda paani bhara, aur tablet ko cap ke andar ek tissue mein alag rakha taaki wo abhi paani mein na gire. Poori bottle (paani, tablet aur cap ke saath) kata par rakhi: **250.0 g**. Phir bottle ko palat kar tablet paani mein giraayi aur cap kas kar band rakha. Bottle mein fizz hui, bubbles uthe, bottle thodi tight ho gayi. Aanya ne kata par dekha: **250.0 g**, bilkul wahi.

"Ab kya hua?" Aanya ne soch kar kaha, "Gas to bani, par vazan wahi!"

Ab Ms. Rajput ne wahi experiment **khuli bottle** (cap hataye, upar se khuli) mein dohraya. Tablet girne se pehle kata ne 250.0 g dikhaya. Fizz khatam hone par kata ne dikhaya **249.6 g**. Aanya chaunk gayi. "Ab 0.4 g kam ho gaya! Kya vazan sach mein khatam ho gaya?"

"Nahi," Ms. Rajput boli. "Jo gas bani, wo bottle se bahar nikal gayi. Wo gas hawa mein chali gayi aur hum use kata par tol nahi paye. Cap lagate hi gas bottle mein rukti hai, aur vazan wahi rehta hai. Dhyan se dekho: **gayab kuch nahi hua**, bas hum gas ko tolna bhool gaye."

(Ye numbers ek udaharan hain, sahi tablet aur bottle ke hisaab se thode alag aa sakte hain. Par pattern wahi rahega.)

Isi sawaal ko ek insaan ne bahut pehle bahut dhyan se parkha tha. Aao uski kahani aur uska niyam dekhte hain.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Dravyaman (mass)** | Kisi cheez mein kitna padaarth hai, ye mapne wali raashi; kata se gram ya kilogram mein tolte hain | 12 g carbon |
| **Reactant** | Reaction shuru karne wali cheez | Carbon aur oxygen |
| **Product** | Reaction se bani nayi cheez | Carbon dioxide |
| **Closed system** | Aisa set-up jisme se na kuch bahar ja sake na andar aa sake | Cap kasi hui bottle |
| **Open system** | Aisa set-up jisme gas aur dhuaan bahar ya andar aa-ja sakte hain | Khuli bottle, jalta kagaz |
| **Law of Conservation of Mass** | Chemical reaction mein kul mass na banta hai, na khatam hota hai | Reactants ka total = products ka total |
| **Conservation** | Surakshit rehna, yaani matra ka wahi rehna | Pehle 250.0 g, baad mein bhi 250.0 g |

## Part A: Lavoisier aur uska kata

Aaj se kareeb 250 saal pehle, France mein **Antoine Lavoisier** naam ke vaigyanik the. Us zamane mein log jalne, zang lagne aur garam karne par cheezon ka vazan badalte dekh kar uljhan mein rehte the. Kuch cheezein jalne par halki ho jaati thi, aur kuch metal garam karne par **bhaari** ho jaate the. Log kehte the ki jalne par koi rahasyamayi cheez nikalti ya judti hai.

Lavoisier ki khaasiyat thi unki **sahi tulaa (balance)** aur unka ek rule: **har cheez ko pehle aur baad mein tolo, aur jo gas bane use bhi pakdo.** Unhone cheezein **band bartanon** mein garam kin, taaki gas bahar na ja sake, aur bartan ko pehle-baad tola. Unhe baar-baar ek hi nateeja mila: **bartan ka kul vazan nahi badalta.**

Sabko ye bhi samajh aaya ki jo vazan "badhta" ya "ghat-ta" dikhta tha, wo hawa ki oxygen ke judne ya gas ke nikal jaane ki wajah se tha. Pehle log in gason ko tolna bhool jaate the.

Yahin se ek niyam bana, jise hum ye kehte hain:

> **Law of Conservation of Mass:** Kisi bhi chemical reaction mein mass na banaya ja sakta hai, na nasht kiya ja sakta hai. **Sabhi reactants ka kul mass = sabhi products ka kul mass.**

Ek line mein: *total mass before = total mass after* (jab kuch bhi bahar na jaaye).

## Part B: Band bottle aur khuli bottle

Aanya ke experiment ka rahasya yahi hai. Dono bottle mein ek hi reaction hui, par ek mein gas ko bahar jaane ka raasta mila, doosri mein nahi.

```
   Closed bottle (cap band)              Open bottle (cap khula)

        +-----+                              +-----+
        │ cap │                              │     │   CO₂ bahar
        +-----+                              │     │  ─────────►
        │ o o │                              │ o o │
        │ o   │  gas andar hi                │ o   │
        │~~~~~│  rukti hai                   │~~~~~│
        │paani│                              │paani│
        +-----+                              +-----+
       ┌───────┐                            ┌───────┐
       │ kata  │                            │ kata  │
       │250.0 g│                            │249.6 g│
       └───────┘                            └───────┘
       pehle = baad mein                    gas bahar gayi,
       mass wahi                            kata ne kam dikhaya
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Band bottle mein sab kuch andar rehta hai, isliye kata par mass wahi dikhta hai (250.0 g). Khuli bottle mein bani CO₂ gas bahar nikal jaati hai, isliye kata kam mass dikhata hai (249.6 g), par mass sach mein nasht nahi hua, bas hawa mein chala gaya.

**Jaanch:** khuli bottle mein 250.0 − 249.6 = 0.4 g mass kam hua; ye 0.4 g gas ka mass hai jo bahar nikla ✅. Agar hum us gas ko kisi tarah pakad kar bottle mein hi rakhte, to total wahi 250.0 g rehta ✅.

Isse hume ye samajh aata hai: **law ko parakhne ke liye system closed hona chahiye**, ya fir hume jo gas bahar jaa rahi ho use bhi hisaab mein lena chahiye.

## Part C: Jo "ghat-ta" ya "badhta" dikhta hai, uska raaz

Rozmarra ki kuch cheezein jo law ko todti dikhti hain, par todti nahi:

1. **Kagaz ya lakdi jalana (khuli jagah mein).** Jalne ke baad rakh (ash) kagaz se bahut halki hoti hai. Jaise 5 g kagaz jala kar maan lo 1 g rakh bachi. Baaki 4 g gaya kahan? Wo **carbon dioxide aur paani ki bhaap** ban kar hawa mein ud gaya. Saath mein hawa ki oxygen bhi judi thi. Agar hum sab kuch ek band, bhaari dabbe mein jalate (kisi adult ki nigrani mein lab mein), to dabbe ka kul vazan wahi rehta. Ghar par ye mat karna.

2. **Fizzing tablet ya soda.** Jaisa Aanya ne dekha: khuli bottle mein gas nikli to vazan kam hua.

3. **Zang lagna (rusting).** Loha jab hawa aur nami ke saath reaction karta hai to **zang** banti hai. Lohe ki ek keel jo kai hafton mein zang pakad leti hai, wo thodi **bhaari** ho jaati hai, kyunki hawa ki **oxygen** (aur paani) loha se jud gayi. Isme kuch bhi bana nahi, loha + oxygen ka kul mass zang ke mass ke barabar hai. (Ghar par ise naapne ki zaroorat nahi: ek keel par kuch din mein badlav bahut kam hota hai. Ye sirf soch ka hisaab hai.)

**Jaanch (sochne wali):** maan lo 5.6 g ki ek keel ka saara loha zang ban gaya, aur zang ka mass **8.0 g** nikla. To loha se judi oxygen = 8.0 − 5.6 = **2.4 g**. Ulta jodo: 5.6 + 2.4 = 8.0 ✅. Yaani zang bhaari hai, par extra mass hawa ki oxygen ka hai, kahin se "bana" nahi. (Asli zang mein paani bhi judta hai; yahan saral hisaab ke liye sirf oxygen li hai.)

## Part D: Balanced equation hi is niyam ki kahani hai

Chapter 3 mein hum bolte the, "atoms ka hisaab". Ab hum jaante hain ki isi ko **Law of Conservation of Mass** kehte hain. Kyunki:

- Reaction mein atoms na bante hain, na khatam hote hain, sirf **apne saathi badalte hain** (regroup hote hain).
- Har atom ka apna mass hota hai.
- Isliye dono taraf **wahi atoms** hain, to dono taraf **wahi kul mass** hoga.

```
   Pehle (reactants)                  Baad mein (products)

   H  H      O  O      H  H           H  O  H      H  O  H
   (H₂)      (O₂)      (H₂)           (H₂O)        (H₂O)

   4 H atoms + 2 O atoms      ──►     4 H atoms + 2 O atoms
   Atom wahi, jodiyan nayi: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Reaction mein atoms regroup hote hain: 4 hydrogen aur 2 oxygen atom pehle bhi the aur baad mein bhi hain, bas H-H aur O-O ki jodiyan tootkar H-O-H ke naye jode ban gaye. Atoms wahi, to mass wahi.

**Numerical se jaanch.** 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O mein H = 1 aur O = 16 maan kar (ye sankhyein "atomic mass" kehlati hain, aage Topic 4 mein samjhenge):

- Reactants: 2 × H₂ = 2 × 2 = 4, aur O₂ = 32. Kul = 4 + 32 = **36**.
- Products: 2 × H₂O = 2 × (2 + 16) = 2 × 18 = **36**.
- 36 = 36 ✅. Mass conserved!

Aur kuch aur reactions ko dekhein (har ek ka hisaab ek hi tarah ka):

| Reaction | Reactants ka mass | Products ka mass |
|---|---|---|
| 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO | 2×24 + 32 = 80 | 2×(24+16) = 80 |
| CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O | 16 + 2×32 = 80 | 44 + 2×18 = 80 |
| Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂ | 65 + 2×36.5 = 138 | (65+71) + 2 = 138 |
| 4Fe + 3O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃ | 4×56 + 3×32 = 320 | 2×160 = 320 |

(Is table ke masses rounded atomic masses se liye gaye hain: H 1, C 12, O 16, Mg 24, Cl 35.5, Fe 56, Zn 65.)

Isse ye bhi dikhta hai ki balancing sirf "sundar likhna" nahi hai. **Balance karna = conservation of mass ka niyam lagana.** Agar equation balanced nahi hai, to wo is niyam ko tod rahi hai.

## Part E: Solved numericals

### Example 1. Carbon jalakar CO₂ banana

12 g carbon ko 32 g oxygen ke saath jalaya gaya (C + O₂ → CO₂). Kitni CO₂ banegi?

- Reactants ka total = 12 + 32 = 44 g.
- Law ke hisaab se products ka total bhi 44 g hoga.
- Yahan ek hi product hai, to **CO₂ ka mass = 44 g**.

Jaanch: C = 12, O₂ = 32, CO₂ = 12 + 32 = 44 ✅

### Example 2. Magnesium ribbon jalana

24 g magnesium jalane par 40 g magnesium oxide mili. Kitni oxygen judi?

- Reactants: Mg (24 g) + O₂ (x g). Product: MgO (40 g).
- 24 + x = 40, to **x = 16 g**.

Jaanch: 24 + 16 = 40 ✅. Dekho, jalne ke baad ash **bhaari** hai (40 g > 24 g). Law toota nahi, 16 g oxygen hawa se judi thi.

### Example 3. Decomposition: ek product ka mass "difference" se

50 g calcium carbonate (CaCO₃, chuna-patthar) ko garam karne par 28 g calcium oxide (CaO) bacha. CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂. Kitni CO₂ gas nikli?

- Reactant: 50 g. Products: CaO (28 g) + CO₂ (x g).
- 28 + x = 50, to **x = 22 g**.

Jaanch: 28 + 22 = 50 ✅. (Dhyan do: ye wohi "kam hua mass" hai jo khuli jagah mein tol kar dikhta, kyunki CO₂ hawa mein chali gayi.)

### Example 4. Hydrogen aur oxygen se paani

4 g hydrogen aur 32 g oxygen poori tarah react hote hain (2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O). Kitna paani bana?

- Reactants: 4 + 32 = 36 g.
- Products ka mass bhi 36 g, to **paani = 36 g**.

Jaanch: 2 × 18 = 36 ✅

### Example 5. Dono taraf ka mass milao

Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂ mein, 65 g zinc aur 73 g HCl se ZnCl₂ aur H₂ bante hain. Agar 136 g ZnCl₂ bana, to H₂ kitni bani?

- Reactants: 65 + 73 = 138 g.
- Products: 136 + x = 138, to **x = 2 g**.

Jaanch: 136 + 2 = 138 ✅

## Part F: Ye niyam kya nahi kehta

1. **Atoms ka kul number conserve hota hai, par molecules ka nahi.** Jaise 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O mein baayein 3 molecules hain aur daayein 2. Mass wahi, par molecules ki ginti badal gayi. Sahi baat: *atoms* ginte hain (aur unka mass), molecules nahi.
2. **Ye sirf chemical change ke liye hai.** Physical change (barf pighalna) mein to waise bhi koi naya padaarth nahi banta. Mass wahan bhi wahi rehta hai.
3. **Ye mass ke baare mein hai, energy ke baare mein nahi.** Reaction mein garmi nikalti ya jaati hai, par ye alag baat hai.
4. **Ye niyam sirf aam chemical reactions par pakka hai.** Parmanu (nuclear) badlav (jaise suraj ya parmanu bijli mein) mein thoda mass energy mein badalta hai. Ye sab aage ki class ki baat hai, abhi ke liye hamara niyam bilkul theek hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Khuli bottle ka vazan kam hua, to kaha "mass nasht ho gaya"**

Nahi. Gas hawa mein gayi. System open tha. Band karke ya gas ko hisaab mein lekar tolo, to mass wahi milega.

**Galti 2: Jalne par ash halki hai, to law toot gayi**

Ash ke saath gas (CO₂, bhaap) bhi to bani. Sab products tolo (ash + gas), to total reactants ke barabar milega.

**Galti 3: Zang bhaari hai, to "mass bana"**

Zang ka extra mass hawa ki oxygen (aur paani) se aaya. Loha + oxygen = zang. Kuch bana nahi, kuch judaa.

**Galti 4: Molecules ki ginti barabar honi chahiye**

Nahi. Barabar hona chahiye **har tatva ke atoms** ka aur **kul mass** ka. 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O mein 3 molecules se 2 molecules ho gaye, par atoms wahi hain.

**Galti 5: Sirf ek taraf ka mass dekhna**

Law mein **dono taraf** ka total tulna hota hai: sabhi reactants ka mass aur sabhi products ka mass. Ek hi product ya ek hi reactant ko dekhne se sahi nateeja nahi aata.

## Quick Recap

- **Law of Conservation of Mass** (Lavoisier): chemical reaction mein mass na banta hai, na khatam hota hai. **Kul mass reactants = kul mass products.**
- Lavoisier ne **band bartan** aur sahi tulaa se ye dikhaya. Pehle jo mass "gayab" ya "paida" hota dikhta tha, wo gas ke nikalne ya judne ki wajah se tha.
- **Closed system** mein mass wahi dikhta hai; **open system** mein gas bahar jaane par kata kam mass dikhata hai, par mass nasht nahi hota.
- Jalna, fizzing tablet aur zang: teeno mein mass conserved hai, bas hawa ki gas ya oxygen kahin se nikli ya judi hoti hai.
- **Balanced equation** = is niyam ka rupa. Dono taraf har tatva ke atoms barabar, isliye dono taraf kul mass barabar. Jaise 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O mein 36 = 36.
- Numericals: kul reactants ka mass = kul products ka mass; ek anjaan mass ko **difference** se nikalte hain.
- Ye niyam mass ke liye hai (energy ke liye nahi), aur atoms conserve hote hain, molecules zaroori nahi. Parmanu badlav aage ki class mein.

## Practice Questions

1. State the Law of Conservation of Mass in your own words. Who demonstrated it, and what did he do differently from the people before him?

2. Aanya's tablet-and-bottle activity shows 250.0 g in the closed bottle after the reaction and 249.6 g in the open bottle. Explain both readings. How much gas escaped from the open bottle?

3. 12 g of carbon burns completely in 32 g of oxygen. How much carbon dioxide is formed? State the law you used.

4. When 24 g of magnesium burns in air, 40 g of magnesium oxide is formed. How much oxygen from the air joined the magnesium? Why is the ash heavier than the ribbon?

5. When 50 g of calcium carbonate is heated, 28 g of calcium oxide is left behind. Find the mass of carbon dioxide released. If this were done in an open dish, what would the balance show before and after?

6. Zinc (65 g) reacts with hydrochloric acid (73 g) to give zinc chloride (136 g) and hydrogen. How much hydrogen is produced? Write the balanced equation.

7. Show that 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO obeys the Law of Conservation of Mass using H = 1, O = 16, Mg = 24.

8. A piece of paper burns in an open dish and the ash is much lighter than the paper. Does this break the law? Explain where the rest of the mass went.

9. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
   (a) In an open system, a chemical reaction can destroy mass.
   (b) A balanced chemical equation has the same number of atoms of each element on both sides.
   (c) The number of molecules is always the same on both sides of a balanced equation.
   (d) Rust is heavier than the iron nail because oxygen from the air joins it.

10. 4 g of hydrogen reacts completely with 32 g of oxygen. How much water is formed? Check using 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O with H₂O = 18.

### Answers

1. **Law of Conservation of Mass:** chemical reaction mein mass na banta hai na khatam hota hai; reactants ka kul mass = products ka kul mass. **Lavoisier** ne ye dikhaya. Unse pehle log gas ko nahi tolte the, isliye mass kabhi ghat-ta aur kabhi badhta dikhta tha. Lavoisier ne **band bartan** aur sahi tulaa istemaal ki, aur pehle-baad ka kul vazan tola, to wahi mila.

2. Closed bottle mein bani gas andar hi rahi, isliye total mass wahi: 250.0 g. Open bottle mein CO₂ bahar nikal gayi, isliye kata ne kam dikhaya. Gas ka mass = 250.0 − 249.6 = **0.4 g** ✅. Mass nasht nahi hua, hawa mein gaya.

3. 12 + 32 = 44 g reactants, to **CO₂ = 44 g** (Law of Conservation of Mass). Jaanch: C + O₂ → CO₂, 12 + 32 = 44 ✅.

4. 24 + x = 40, to **x = 16 g** oxygen. Ash isliye bhaari hai kyunki hawa ki oxygen ribbon se jud gayi: Mg (24 g) + O (16 g) = MgO (40 g) ✅.

5. 28 + x = 50, to **CO₂ = 22 g**. Open dish mein CO₂ hawa mein jaati hai, to balance par dish ka vazan 50 g se ghat kar sirf 28 g (CaO) dikhta, yani 22 g "kam" lagta, par asal mein mass nasht nahi hua ✅.

6. Reactants: 65 + 73 = 138 g. Products: 136 + x = 138, to **H₂ = 2 g**. Balanced equation: **Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂** ✅.

7. Reactants: 2 × 24 + 32 = 80. Products: 2 × (24 + 16) = 80. 80 = 80 ✅. Mass conserved.

8. Nahi, law nahi tootta. Kagaz jalne par carbon dioxide aur paani ki bhaap bani jo hawa mein chali gayi, aur hawa ki oxygen bhi judi thi. Ash + gas + oxygen ka hisaab karein to dono taraf mass barabar hai. Ash halki isliye dikhti hai kyunki gas hum tol nahi paye (open system).

9. (a) **False** ❌. Open ho ya closed, mass nasht nahi hota; open mein gas bahar jaane se kata kam dikhata hai. (b) **True** ✅. Yahi atoms ka hisaab hai. (c) **False** ❌. Molecules ki ginti badal sakti hai (2H₂ + O₂ mein 3, 2H₂O mein 2); atoms aur mass barabar rehte hain. (d) **True** ✅. Loha + oxygen = zang, to zang bhaari.

10. Reactants = 4 + 32 = 36 g, to **paani = 36 g**. Jaanch: 2 × 18 = 36 ✅.
