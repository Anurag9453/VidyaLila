# Topic 2: Ionic Bond: Electron Dene-Lene Ka Rishta

Topic 1 mein humne seekha ki atom stable baahari shell (duplet ya octet) chahta hai, aur uske paas teen raaste hain: electron **dena**, **lena** ya **share** karna. Humne ye bhi dekha ki metals (1 se 3 baahari electron) dena pasand karte hain, aur non-metals (5 se 7 baahari electron) lena. Ab sawaal ye hai: agar ek metal aur ek non-metal **mil jaayein**, to kya hota hai? Aaj "dena" aur "lena" milkar ek bond banate hain, jise **ionic bond** kehte hain. Chapter 4 mein humne kaha tha ki NaCl jaise compounds mein ek akela "molecule" nahi hota aur ye baat is chapter mein samjhayenge. Wo vaada aaj poora hoga.

## Part A: Ionic bond kaise banta hai: Na aur Cl

Topic 1 ke electron-dot symbols yaad karo. Hum **metal ke electrons ko x (cross)** se aur **non-metal ke electrons ko . (dot)** se likhenge, taaki dikhe ki kaun sa electron kahan se aaya. Electrons sab ek jaise hote hain; ye alag nishaan sirf humari ginti ke liye hain. Topic 1 ki tarah yaad rakho: dots kis taraf rakhe hain, ye sirf picture hai; **ginti** hi maayne rakhti hai. Text ke beech hum chhota roop bhi likhenge, jaise [ : Cl : ]⁻, jiska matlab hai poore 8 baahari electrons wala Cl⁻.

**Sodium (Na):** Z = 11, configuration 2, 8, **1**. Baahari 1 electron hai. Wo de de to bachta hai 2, 8: neon jaisa octet.

**Chlorine (Cl):** Z = 17, configuration 2, 8, **7**. Baahari 7 hain, octet mein 1 kam. 1 electron mil jaaye to 2, 8, 8: argon jaisa octet.

Step-by-step:

1. **Dena:** Na apna 1 baahari electron Cl ko de deta hai. Na ke 11 protons (+11) aur ab 10 electrons (−10) bache: charge **+1**. Ye **Na⁺** hai (2, 8).
2. **Lena:** Cl ko wo 1 electron mil jaata hai. Cl ke 17 protons (+17) aur ab 18 electrons (−18): charge **−1**. Ye **Cl⁻** hai (2, 8, 8).
3. **Aakarshan:** Na⁺ (positive) aur Cl⁻ (negative) ek doosre ko kheenchte hain, jaise magnet ke ulte pole. Ye aakarshan hi **ionic bond** hai.

```
   Pehle: alag atoms                       Baad mein: ions

                         ..                                    ..
   Na x  ---------->   : Cl .          [Na]⁺               [ : Cl .x ]⁻
                         ..                                    ..

   Na: 2, 8, 1          Cl: 2, 8, 7       Na⁺: 2, 8          Cl⁻: 2, 8, 8
   baahari: 1           baahari: 7        baahari: 8         baahari: 8
   charge: 0            charge: 0         charge: +1         charge: -1
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Na aur Cl: sodium ka akela baahari electron (x) chlorine ke paas chala jaata hai. Cl ke 7 dots ke saath ye x milkar 8 ban jaate hain (right side mein ".x"). Na⁺ ke baahari shell mein ab L shell ke 8 hain, aur Cl⁻ ke 8 hain. Dono ke upar bracket mein charge likhte hain.

Dhyan do: ek electron kaise Na se Cl tak "gaya", ye ek dhyan se dekhne wali baat hai. Dono ke dil mein protons wahi hain (11 aur 17). Sirf electrons ki ginti badli, isliye **tatva wahi** hai. Chemistry mein hum is poori kahani ko ek hi line mein likhte hain: Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ milkar **NaCl** (sodium chloride, yaani namak) dete hain.

## Part B: Mg aur O: do-do electron ka len-den

Is baar dono ko **2** ka len-den karna hai.

**Magnesium (Mg):** Z = 12, configuration 2, 8, **2**. 2 electron dene par 2, 8 bachta hai.

**Oxygen (O):** Z = 8, configuration 2, **6**. 2 electron lene par 2, 8 ho jaata hai.

Mg apne **dono** baahari electron O ko de deta hai. Mg ke 12 protons aur 10 electrons: charge **+2**, ion **Mg²⁺**. O ke 8 protons aur 10 electrons: charge **−2**, ion **O²⁻**.

```
        Mg                         O                       Mg²⁺               O²⁻

                                  ..                                          ..
     x Mg x   ------------>      : O                     [Mg]²⁺           [ : O xx ]²⁻
                                  ..                                          ..

   Mg: 2, 8, 2               O: 2, 6                   Mg²⁺: 2, 8         O²⁻: 2, 8
   baahari: 2                baahari: 6                baahari: 8         baahari: 8
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Magnesium ke dono baahari electrons (xx) oxygen ke paas jaate hain. Oxygen ke 6 dots ke saath ye 2 x milkar 8 banate hain (right side ka pair). Mg²⁺ aur O²⁻ dono ki configuration 2, 8 (neon jaisi) hai. Ek Mg aur ek O, to formula **MgO** (magnesium oxide).

Yahan 1 Mg ne 2 diye aur 1 O ne 2 liye: **diye hue = liye hue = 2**. Isliye 1 Mg aur 1 O kaafi hain.

## Part C: Mg aur 2 Cl: jab ginti barabar na ho

Ab Mg ko Cl ke saath milao. Mg **2** electron dena chahta hai, lekin ek Cl sirf **1** le sakta hai (Cl ko octet ke liye bas 1 chahiye). Ek Cl ke baad bhi Mg ka ek electron bach jaata hai. To Mg ko **do Cl atoms** chahiye: ek electron ek Cl ko, doosra doosre ko.

```
       ..                               ..                 ..                     ..
     : Cl .  <------- x Mg x -------> . Cl :           [ : Cl .x ]⁻  [Mg]²⁺  [ x. Cl : ]⁻
       ..                               ..                 ..                     ..

   Cl: 2, 8, 7              Mg: 2, 8, 2                Cl⁻: 2, 8, 8   Mg²⁺: 2, 8   Cl⁻: 2, 8, 8
   baahari 7                baahari 2                  baahari 8      baahari 8     baahari 8
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Mg ke 2 baahari electrons (x) alag-alag Cl atoms ko jaate hain. Har Cl ke 7 dots ko 1 x milta hai aur wo 8 ho jaate hain. Beech ka Mg²⁺ ab 2, 8 hai. Total: 1 Mg²⁺ aur 2 Cl⁻, to formula **MgCl₂** (magnesium chloride).

Ginti ka hisaab: Mg ne diye 2, 2 Cl ne liye 1 + 1 = 2. **Diye hue = liye hue.**

## Part D: Criss-cross kyun kaam karta hai?

Chapter 3 mein humne formula likhne ke liye **criss-cross** seekha tha: ek tatva ki valency doosre ke subscript ban jaati hai. Tab bas ek trick thi. Ab uska asli reason mil gaya.

**Niyam:** Ionic compound mein **diye gaye electrons ki kul ginti = liye gaye electrons ki kul ginti**. Isi se kul charge **zero** hota hai. Isliye atoms ki wo ginti chuno jisse dono taraf ka total barabar ho.

| Metal | Non-metal | Metal ne diye | Non-metal ne liye (har ek) | Atoms ki ginti | Diye = liye | Formula |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Na (1) | Cl (1) | 1 | 1 | 1 Na, 1 Cl | 1 = 1 | NaCl |
| Mg (2) | O (2) | 2 | 2 | 1 Mg, 1 O | 2 = 2 | MgO |
| Mg (2) | Cl (1) | 2 | 1 | 1 Mg, 2 Cl | 2 = 1 + 1 | MgCl₂ |
| Na (1) | O (2) | 1 | 2 | 2 Na, 1 O | 1 + 1 = 2 | Na₂O |
| Ca (2) | F (1) | 2 | 1 | 1 Ca, 2 F | 2 = 1 + 1 | CaF₂ |
| Al (3) | O (2) | 3 | 2 | 2 Al, 3 O | 6 = 6 | Al₂O₃ |

Dekho: Al₂O₃ mein criss-cross se "2" Al par aur "3" O par aata hai. Iska matlab: 2 Al atoms **6** electron dete hain (2 × 3), aur 3 O atoms **6** electron lete hain (3 × 2). Criss-cross wahi **LCM** hai jo dono ko 6 par milata hai. Isliye criss-cross sirf ratta nahi tha; ye electron ki ginti ka hisaab tha.

Ek aur baat: jab dono subscript mein koi common factor ho (jaise Mg₂O₂), to usse chhota karke likhte hain (MgO). Ye Chapter 3 ka wahi simplest ratio ka niyam hai.

## Part E: Crystal lattice aur formula unit

Ab tak humne ek Na aur ek Cl ki baat ki, jaise bas ek jodi ho. Lekin asli namak ke ek chhote se dane mein **arabon-kharabon** ions hote hain. Na⁺ sirf ek Cl⁻ ko nahi kheenchta; wo apne **aas-paas ke sabhi Cl⁻ ko** kheenchta hai. Aur har Cl⁻ aas-paas ke sabhi Na⁺ ko. Isliye ions ek **regular, baar-baar dohrane wala jaal** banate hain, jise **crystal lattice** kehte hain.

```
   Na⁺   Cl⁻   Na⁺   Cl⁻   Na⁺
   Cl⁻   Na⁺   Cl⁻   Na⁺   Cl⁻
   Na⁺   Cl⁻   Na⁺   Cl⁻   Na⁺
   Cl⁻   Na⁺   Cl⁻   Na⁺   Cl⁻

   Beech ke Na⁺ ke 4 sabse paas ke padosi (upar, neeche, left, right): sab Cl⁻.
```

> **Figure 2.4** – NaCl ke lattice ka ek chhota sa **do-dimensional** tukda. Har Na⁺ ke chaaron taraf Cl⁻ hai aur har Cl⁻ ke chaaron taraf Na⁺. Asli crystal teen dimension mein hota hai: wahan har ion ke ek aage aur ek peeche, do aur padosi judte hain (4 + 2 = 6), to **har Na⁺ ko 6 Cl⁻ aur har Cl⁻ ko 6 Na⁺** ghere rehte hain. Ginti 1 : 1 rehti hai, isliye formula NaCl.

**Formula unit kyun, molecule kyun nahi?** Is jaal mein koi ek Na⁺ ek hi Cl⁻ ka "partner" nahi hai. Wo apne 6 Cl⁻ padosiyon se ek barabar juda hai. Isliye kisi ek jodi ko alag karke "ye ek molecule hai" nahi kah sakte. Hum bas **ions ki ginti ka sabse chhota ratio** likhte hain: NaCl mein 1 : 1, MgCl₂ mein 1 : 2. Ye ratio hi **formula unit** hai.

Chapter 4 mein jo "formula unit mass" nikalte the, wo bhi isi se jud gaya:

- NaCl: 23 + 35.5 = **58.5 u**
- MgO: 24 + 16 = **40 u**
- MgCl₂: 24 + 2 × 35.5 = **95 u**

Ionic compounds ke naam hamesha aise: **metal ka naam pehle, non-metal ka naam baad mein, aur non-metal ke naam ke ant mein "-ide"**: sodium chloride, magnesium oxide, calcium fluoride, aluminium oxide.

## Part F: Solved examples

### Example 1. Na aur Cl se NaCl

- Na: 2, 8, 1 (Z = 11) deta hai 1. Cl: 2, 8, 7 (Z = 17) leta hai 1.
- Na⁺: 11 protons, 10 electrons, 2, 8. Cl⁻: 17 protons, 18 electrons, 2, 8, 8.
- Dot diagram: Na x → Na⁺ aur : Cl : ke 7 dots + 1 x → [ : Cl : ]⁻.
- Formula: 1 Na⁺ + 1 Cl⁻ = **NaCl**.

Jaanch: diye 1, liye 1 ✅. Charge: +1 − 1 = 0 ✅. Na⁺ ke 10 electrons ✅, Cl⁻ ke 18 ✅.

### Example 2. Mg aur O se MgO

- Mg: 2, 8, 2 (Z = 12) deta hai 2. O: 2, 6 (Z = 8) leta hai 2.
- Mg²⁺: 12 protons, 10 electrons, 2, 8. O²⁻: 8 protons, 10 electrons, 2, 8.
- Formula: **MgO**.

```
                     ..
      [Mg]²⁺      [ : O : ]²⁻
                     ..
```

Jaanch: diye 2, liye 2 ✅. Charge +2 − 2 = 0 ✅. Dono ke 10 electrons, neon jaise ✅.

### Example 3. Mg aur Cl se MgCl₂

- Mg 2 deta hai. Ek Cl sirf 1 leta hai, to 2 Cl chahiye.
- Mg²⁺: 10 electrons. Har Cl⁻: 18 electrons.
- Formula: **MgCl₂**.

```
       ..                   ..
   [ : Cl : ]⁻  [Mg]²⁺  [ : Cl : ]⁻
       ..                   ..
```

Jaanch: diye 2, liye 1 + 1 = 2 ✅. Charge +2 + (−1) + (−1) = 0 ✅. Criss-cross se bhi MgCl₂ ✅.

### Example 4. Al aur O se Al₂O₃

- Al: 2, 8, 3 (Z = 13) deta hai 3. O: 2, 6 leta hai 2.
- 3 aur 2 ka LCM = 6. Isliye 6 ÷ 3 = **2 Al** aur 6 ÷ 2 = **3 O**.
- Al³⁺: 13 protons, 10 electrons, 2, 8. O²⁻: 8 protons, 10 electrons, 2, 8.
- 2 Al³⁺ ka charge +6, 3 O²⁻ ka charge −6.

Jaanch: diye 2 × 3 = 6, liye 3 × 2 = 6 ✅. Charge +6 − 6 = 0 ✅. Criss-cross se Al₂O₃ ✅.

### Example 5. Ca aur F se CaF₂

- Ca: 2, 8, 8, 2 (Z = 20) deta hai 2. F: 2, 7 (Z = 9) leta hai 1.
- Ca²⁺: 20 protons, 18 electrons, 2, 8, 8 (argon jaisa). Har F⁻: 9 protons, 10 electrons, 2, 8 (neon jaisa).
- 1 Ca ko 2 F chahiye. Formula: **CaF₂** (calcium fluoride).

Jaanch: diye 2, liye 1 + 1 = 2 ✅. Charge +2 − 2 = 0 ✅.

### Example 6. Na aur O se Na₂O

- Na deta hai 1, O leta hai 2. Ek O ko 2 electrons chahiye, to **2 Na** dete hain 1 + 1.
- 2 Na⁺ (har ek 10 electrons) aur 1 O²⁻ (10 electrons). Formula: **Na₂O** (sodium oxide).

Jaanch: diye 2 × 1 = 2, liye 1 × 2 = 2 ✅. Charge +2 − 2 = 0 ✅.

### Example 7. Li aur F se LiF: helium jaisa ion

- Li: 2, 1 (Z = 3) deta hai 1. Bacha 2: K shell, **duplet**.
- F: 2, 7 leta hai 1: 2, 8.
- Li⁺: 3 protons, 2 electrons, 2 (helium jaisa). F⁻: 9 protons, 10 electrons, 2, 8 (neon jaisa).
- Formula: **LiF** (lithium fluoride).

Jaanch: diye 1, liye 1 ✅. Charge +1 − 1 = 0 ✅. Li⁺ ke 2 electrons ✅ (octet nahi, duplet; Topic 1 ki baat).

### Example 8. Ca aur O se CaO

- Ca deta hai 2, O leta hai 2. 1 Ca aur 1 O.
- Ca²⁺: 18 electrons, 2, 8, 8. O²⁻: 10 electrons, 2, 8.
- Formula: **CaO** (calcium oxide, jise chuna ya quicklime bhi kehte hain).

Jaanch: diye 2, liye 2 ✅. Charge +2 − 2 = 0 ✅.

### Example 9. Configuration se pehchano: kaun si jodi?

Ek metal ki configuration 2, 8, 8, 1 hai aur ek non-metal ki 2, 8, 6. Dono milke kya banayenge?

- Metal: kul electrons 19, to Z = 19: potassium (K). Baahari 1, deta hai 1.
- Non-metal: kul electrons 16, to Z = 16: sulphur (S). Baahari 6, leta hai 2.
- Ek S ko 2 chahiye, to 2 K chahiye. K⁺: 18 electrons. S²⁻: 18 electrons. Dono 2, 8, 8.
- Formula: **K₂S** (potassium sulphide).

Jaanch: diye 2 × 1 = 2, liye 1 × 2 = 2 ✅. Charge +2 − 2 = 0 ✅.

### Example 10. Ion mein protons aur electrons gino

Mg²⁺ aur Cl⁻ mein kul electrons kitne? Kya dono alag tatva hain?

- Mg²⁺: Z = 12, electrons 12 − 2 = **10**.
- Cl⁻: Z = 17, electrons 17 + 1 = **18**.
- Dono alag tatva hain (protons 12 aur 17).

Jaanch: Mg²⁺ ka charge +12 − 10 = +2 ✅. Cl⁻ ka charge +17 − 18 = −1 ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Compound mein atoms ko phir se neutral samajhna**

NaCl mein Na aur Cl **ions** hain (Na⁺ aur Cl⁻), neutral atoms nahi. Isliye NaCl ke gun sodium metal aur chlorine gas jaise nahi hote. Sodium metal bahut reactive hota hai, magar namak hum khaate hain.

**Galti 2: Ionic compound ka "molecule" likhna**

NaCl ka ek "molecule" nahi hota. Sahi shabd **formula unit** hai.

**Galti 3: Mg²⁺ ko Mg²⁻ likh dena**

Jo electron **deta** hai wo positive hota hai. Mg ne 2 electron diye, to Mg²⁺. O ne 2 liye, to O²⁻.

**Galti 4: Diye aur liye hue electrons ki ginti na milana**

Hamesha check karo: diye hue total = liye hue total. Nahi mile to formula galat hai.

**Galti 5: Ek hi atom ke ion ko doosre ki configuration ka samajhna**

Na⁺ ki configuration neon jaisi hai, par Na⁺ neon nahi hai (protons 11 vs 10).

**Galti 6: Formula mein charge likhna**

Compound ke formula mein ions ke charge nahi likhte: NaCl likhte hain, Na⁺Cl⁻ nahi. Charge sirf ions alag dikhate waqt likhte hain.

**Galti 7: Metal ko "lena" aur non-metal ko "dena"**

Ionic bond mein metal electron deta hai, non-metal leta hai. Ulta nahi.

## Quick Recap

- **Ionic bond**: metal ne electron **diye**, non-metal ne **liye**, aur ulte charge wale ions ka **aakarshan** unhe jodta hai.
- Metal **cation** (positive) banta hai, non-metal **anion** (negative). Dono ki baahari shell stable ho jaati hai: octet ya Li⁺ jaisa duplet.
- Na (2, 8, 1) + Cl (2, 8, 7) → Na⁺ (2, 8) + Cl⁻ (2, 8, 8) → **NaCl**.
- Mg (2, 8, 2) + O (2, 6) → Mg²⁺ + O²⁻ → **MgO**. Mg + 2 Cl → **MgCl₂**. 2 Al + 3 O → **Al₂O₃** (6 electron diye = 6 liye). Ca + 2 F → **CaF₂**.
- **Criss-cross ka asli reason**: diye hue electrons = liye hue electrons, isliye kul charge zero.
- Ionic compound ek **crystal lattice** banata hai. NaCl mein har Na⁺ ko 6 Cl⁻ aur har Cl⁻ ko 6 Na⁺ ghere rehte hain.
- Isliye ionic compound ke liye **formula unit** kehte hain, molecule nahi. NaCl ka formula unit mass 23 + 35.5 = 58.5 u.
- Naam: pehle metal, phir non-metal ke ant mein **-ide** (sodium chloride, magnesium oxide).

    (a) In an ionic bond, the non-metal gives electrons to the metal.
    (b) The ions Na⁺ and Mg²⁺ both have 10 electrons.

### Answers

1. Na (2, 8, 1) deta hai 1 electron: **Na⁺** (11 protons, 10 electrons, 2, 8, charge +1). Cl (2, 8, 7) leta hai 1: **Cl⁻** (17 protons, 18 electrons, 2, 8, 8, charge −1). Diagram: Na x → [Na]⁺ aur : Cl : (7 dots) + x → [ : Cl : ]⁻ (8 electrons). Dono ke beech aakarshan se **NaCl**.

2. Mg 2 electron deta hai (Mg²⁺: 10 electrons, 2, 8), O 2 electron leta hai (O²⁻: 10 electrons, 2, 8). Diye 2 = liye 2. Formula ka ratio hamesha **sabse chhota** likhte hain; 1 : 1 ko Mg₂O₂ nahi, **MgO** likhte hain.

3. Mg 2 electron dena chahta hai, ek Cl sirf 1 le sakta hai. Isliye **2 Cl** chahiye. Diagram: [ : Cl : ]⁻  [Mg]²⁺  [ : Cl : ]⁻. Diye = 2, liye = 1 + 1 = 2 ✅. Formula **MgCl₂**.

4. Har Al deta hai 3 electron, har S leta hai 2 (2, 8, 6 se 2, 8, 8). LCM 6: **2 Al** (2 × 3 = 6 diye) aur **3 S** (3 × 2 = 6 liye). Charge: 2 Al³⁺ = +6, 3 S²⁻ = −6, total 0 ✅. Formula **Al₂S₃** (aluminium sulphide).

5. (a) K deta 1, F leta 1: **KF**. (b) Ca deta 2, Cl leta 1: 1 Ca, 2 Cl: **CaCl₂**. (c) Li deta 1, O leta 2: 2 Li, 1 O: **Li₂O**. (d) Al deta 3, F leta 1: 1 Al, 3 F: **AlF₃**. Har ek mein diye = liye ✅.

6. (a) Ca²⁺: **20 protons, 18 electrons**, argon jaisa (2, 8, 8). (b) F⁻: **9 protons, 10 electrons**, neon jaisa (2, 8). (c) Li⁺: **3 protons, 2 electrons**, helium jaisa (2). (d) S²⁻: **16 protons, 18 electrons**, argon jaisa (2, 8, 8).

7. Criss-cross mein Al ki valency 3 O ke subscript par aur O ki valency 2 Al ke subscript par jaati hai: **Al₂O₃**. Iska matlab 2 Al atoms 2 × 3 = 6 electron dete hain aur 3 O atoms 3 × 2 = 6 electron lete hain. Criss-cross asal mein "diye hue = liye hue" ka shortcut hai.

8. Har Na⁺ ko **6 Cl⁻** ghere rehte hain (aur har Cl⁻ ko 6 Na⁺). Koi Na⁺ ek hi Cl⁻ ka partner nahi hai, to ek "molecule" alag nahi kiya ja sakta. Isliye ratio dikhane wala **formula unit** (NaCl, 1 : 1) kehte hain.

9. Na₂O = 2 × 23 + 16 = 46 + 16 = **62 u**. CaO = 40 + 16 = **56 u**. CaF₂ = 40 + 2 × 19 = 40 + 38 = **78 u**.

10. NaCl mein Na aur Cl **neutral atoms nahi, ions** hain (Na⁺ aur Cl⁻). Unki baahari shell poori hai aur unke gun alag hote hain. Isliye NaCl ke gun sodium metal ya chlorine gas jaise nahi hote.

11. Configuration 2, 8, 2 (12 electrons) = **magnesium**. 2, 7 (9 electrons) = **fluorine**. Mg deta hai 2, F leta hai 1, to 1 Mg aur 2 F: [ : F : ]⁻  [Mg]²⁺  [ : F : ]⁻. Formula **MgF₂** (magnesium fluoride). Diye 2 = liye 1 + 1 ✅.

12. (a) **False** ❌. Metal electron deta hai aur non-metal leta hai. (b) **True** ✅ (Na⁺: 11 − 1 = 10; Mg²⁺: 12 − 2 = 10). (c) **True** ✅. (d) **False** ❌. Formula mein charge nahi likhte: **MgCl₂**.

Agle topic **"Covalent Bond: Electron Baantne Ka Rishta"** mein dekhenge ki wo electrons ko ek doosre ke saath **share** karte hain, aur isi se paani (H₂O) jaise molecules bante hain.
