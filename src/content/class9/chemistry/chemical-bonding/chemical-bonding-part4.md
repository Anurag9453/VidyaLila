# Topic 4: Ionic aur Covalent Compounds Ke Gun

Ab tak hum bond **banana** seekh chuke hain: ionic bond mein electron **dena-lena**, aur covalent bond mein electron **baantna**. Ek sawaal bacha hai: isse hume kya farak padta hai? Jawab: bond ka type tay kar deta hai ki padarth **kaisa dikhega, kaisa chalega**. Wo kitna kadak hoga, kab pighlega, paani mein ghulega ya nahi, aur bijli chalne dega ya nahi. Aaj hum ye sab **reason ke saath** dekhenge, aur ek chhota, bilkul safe experiment bhi karenge.

---

## Do ek jaise dabbe

Mr. Khosla ki rasoi ki shelf par do steel ke dabbe rakhe the, bilkul ek jaise. Dono mein safed, chhote-chhote dane. Ek mein **namak**, ek mein **cheeni**. Par label chipka hua tha, wo kal paani se gal gaya. Unki bhatiji Eshita ne dabba kholkar kaha, "Chacha, ab dono ek jaise dikhte hain. Chakh ke dekhun?"

"Bilkul nahi," Mr. Khosla ne turant kaha. "Science ka pehla niyam: **kabhi kuch chakhna nahi**, chahe wo rasoi ka hi kyun na ho aur lab ka to bilkul nahi. Dabbe par label na ho, to dabba hi khatre ki ghanti hai." Eshita hans padi. "To phir kaise pehchanen?"

Mr. Khosla ne ek 2 AA cells ka chhota holder, ek chhota LED bulb aur do taar nikaale. "Ek tester banate hain. Dono ko paani mein ghol ke dekhte hain. Jis ghol se bulb jalega, wo **namak** hoga. Jis se nahi jalega, wo **cheeni**." Eshita chaunki, "Par kyun? Dono to paani mein ghul jaate hain!" "Isi 'kyun' ka jawab aaj ka topic hai," Mr. Khosla muskuraye. "Namak ionic compound hai aur cheeni covalent. Aur dono ke **gun** (properties) unke bond se aate hain."

Chalo, pehle zaroori shabd, phir tester, phir saare gun ek ek reason ke saath.

## Part A: Pehle ek bahut zaroori tasveer

Is poore topic ki chaabi ek hi hai. Do tarah ke attraction (aakarshan) hote hain, aur bahut alag-alag taakat ke:

- **Ionic compound:** Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ jaise ions ke beech attraction hota hai. Ye attraction **positive aur negative charge** ka hai, bahut **strong**, aur ye poore crystal lattice mein **har ion ko chaaron taraf** se pakde rehta hai (Topic 2 ka lattice yaad karo: har Na⁺ ko 6 Cl⁻ ghere hue the).
- **Covalent compound:** Ek molecule ke **andar** ka bond (shared electrons) to strong hota hai. Lekin ek molecule aur doosre molecule ke **beech** ka attraction (Chapter 1 wala aakarshan) bahut **kamzor** hota hai.

Yaad rakho, covalent compound ko pighlane ya ubalne mein molecule ke andar ka bond **nahi todna** padta. Bas ek molecule ko doosre se alag karna padta hai, jo aasan hai.

Ek ghar ki upma: ionic compound ek **kasi hui deewar** jaisa hai jisme har eent seedhe cement se jodi gayi hai. Covalent compound **gattha (cartons) ke dher** jaisa hai: har gattha khud mazboot hai, par gatthe ek doosre se sirf halke se rakhe hain.

Aage har gun mein hum isi tasveer ko lagayenge.

## Part B: Safe activity: conductivity tester

**Pehle safety (zaroor padho):**

- Tester mein **sirf 2 AA cells (kul 3 volt)** aur ek **chhota LED ya torch ka bulb** use karna hai. **Kabhi ghar ki bijli (mains), socket ya plug nahi.** Ghar ki bijli se paani mein taar dalna jaanlewa ho sakta hai.
- Experiment ke waqt **ek bada (adult) paas** mein hona chahiye.
- **Kuch bhi chakhna nahi.** Cheeni aur namak bhi nahi; dabbon ko label lagao.
- **Koi bhi jalne wala (flammable) tel ya liquid nahi:** kerosene, petrol, spirit wagairah bilkul mat lo. Hum sirf **cooking oil** lenge.
- Taar ke dono sire paani mein dalte waqt aapas mein **chhune nahi chahiye**, aur kaam ke baad haath dho lo.
- LED ke saath **hamesha ek chhota resistor (100 ohm se 330 ohm) series mein** lagao, warna 3 V par LED kharab ho sakti hai. Ya resistor ki jagah **torch ka bulb** (lagbhag 2.5 V) use karo, jo seedha chal jaata hai. LED ki do taangein hoti hain aur wo ek hi disha mein jalti hai: agar taar seedha jodne par bhi na jale, to cell ki disha ulti kar ke dekho.

**Samaan:** 2 AA cells (holder ke saath), ek chhota LED aur ek 100 se 330 ohm ka resistor (ya sirf ek torch bulb), do taar, 4 kaanch ke glass ya katori, saaf (distilled ya tap) paani, namak, cheeni, cooking oil, ek chammach.

**Tareeka:**

1. Cells, LED (resistor ke saath) ya bulb, aur do taar jodke ek **adhoori circuit** banao: bijli chalne ka raasta beech mein khula rahe, jahan taar ke do sire **khule** hain.
2. Ek glass mein saaf paani lo. Taar ke dono khule sire paani mein dalo (par aapas mein na chhoone do). LED dekho.
3. Sire nikalo, **paani se dho ke poonchh lo**, phir har baar yahi karo: (a) namak ghola hua paani, (b) cheeni ghola hua paani, (c) cooking oil.
4. Har baar likho: LED jali ya nahi.

```
      +-------[ 2 AA cells, 3 V ]-------+
      :                                 :
  [LED + resistor]                      :  taar
  (ya torch bulb)                       :
      :                                 :
      :  taar                           :
     (A)                               (B)
      v                                 v
    ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
    ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~  ghol  ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
    ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Conductivity tester: cells, LED aur resistor (ya torch bulb) aur taar ki ek series wali adhoori circuit, jiske do khule sire A aur B ghol mein dube hain. Circuit ka bacha hua hissa ghol khud hai. Agar ghol mein **free ions** hain to ghol circuit ko poora kar deta hai, current chalta hai aur LED jalti hai. Agar ghol mein ions nahi hain, to circuit khula rehta hai aur LED nahi jalti. Sirf 2 AA cells use karo, ghar ki bijli kabhi nahi.

**Typical (expected) result:**

| Ghol | LED | Matlab |
|---|---|---|
| Saaf (distilled) paani | Nahi jalti | Lagbhag koi ion nahi |
| Namak ka ghol | **Jalti hai** | Free Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ hain |
| Cheeni ka ghol | Nahi jalti | Cheeni ke molecule hain, ion nahi |
| Cooking oil | Nahi jalti | Molecule hain, ion nahi |

Dhyan do: ye **typical** natije hain. **Tap water** mein bahut thode ghule hue namak (minerals) hote hain, isliye kabhi-kabhi LED bahut halki si jal sakti hai, ya bilkul nahi. Agar tumhare cells purane hain ya LED bahut chhoti hai, to namak ke ghol mein bhi roshni dheemi dikhegi; taar paas karne ya namak badhane se roshni badhegi.

Eshita ke dono dabbon ka sawaal ab hal ho gaya. Jis dabbe ke ghol se LED jali, wo **namak**; jis se nahi jali, wo **cheeni**. Par **kyun**?

## Part C: Bijli kyun chalti hai, kyun nahi?

Physics ke Electricity chapter mein humne dekha tha ki bijli ka matlab **charge ka behna** hai. Dhaatu ke taar mein electron behte hain, aur namak ke paani jaise ghol mein ions ([electricity-part7.md](../../physics/electricity/electricity-part7.md)). Ghol mein bhi bijli behne ke liye **charge wale kan** chahiye jo **hil-dul sakein**.

**Solid ionic compound (jaise namak ka crystal):** Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ maujood hain, lekin lattice mein **apni apni jagah par jakde** hue hain. Charge hain, par **behne ke liye azaad nahi**. Isliye solid namak bijli **nahi** chalata.

**Pighla hua (molten) ionic compound:** Garam karne par lattice toot jaata hai. Ions ab aapas mein phisalte hain: **free ions**. Free ions bijli chalate hain. (Namak ko pighlane ke liye lagbhag 801 °C chahiye, isliye ye ghar par bilkul nahi karte; ye sirf lab ya factory ki baat hai.)

**Paani mein ghola hua ionic compound:** Paani ke molecule lattice se ions ko alag kar dete hain aur ions ghol mein **free** ho jaate hain. Bas, wahi kaaran hai ki namak ka ghol LED jalata hai.

**Covalent compound (cheeni, oil, paani ke molecule):** Isme **molecule** hain, jo khud neutral hain. Na koi + ion, na koi − ion, to charge ka behna hi nahi. Isliye cheeni ka ghol aur cooking oil bijli **nahi** chalate.

```
 Solid (fixed)       Pighla (molten)       Paani mein ghola

  +  -  +  -  +        +     -   +          +  (H₂O)  -
  -  +  -  +  -          -  +        -     (H₂O)  -   +
  +  -  +  -  +        -    +    +             -  (H₂O) +
  -  +  -  +  -           +     -   -       +   -   (H₂O)

 ions jakde hue      ions free, ghoomte     ions free, paani ke
 (bijli nahi)        (bijli chalti hai)     molecule ghere hue
                                            (bijli chalti hai)
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Ionic compound ke ions teen roop mein. **Solid:** + aur − ions regular lattice mein fixed, hil nahi sakte. **Pighla:** lattice toot gaya, ions free hain aur idhar-udhar ghoom sakte hain. **Paani mein ghola:** (H₂O) paani ke molecule hain jo ions ko alag kar dete hain aur ions ghol mein free ho jaate hain. Dono free-ion wali halat mein bijli chalti hai. (Sketch hai; asli ions bahut zyada aur paani ke molecule bahut chhote hote hain.)

Ek baat saaf rakho: ionic compound ke ions **pehle se maujood** hain, bas fixed hain. Paani ya garmi unhe **banati nahi**, sirf **azaad** karti hai.

## Part D: Covalent liquid ke molecules

Ab covalent compound ki tasveer dekhte hain. Maan lo wo ek liquid hai (jaise paani ya cooking oil). Uske molecule ek doosre ke paas hain, par bas **halka sa** attraction hai.

```
     H-O-H          H-O-H
              H-O-H           H-O-H
   H-O-H                H-O-H
          H-O-H                  H-O-H
     H-O-H        H-O-H
```

> **Figure 4.3** – Covalent liquid ke molecules (yahan paani ke H-O-H molecule). Har molecule ke **andar** ke bond (H-O) strong hain, par molecule **aapas mein** sirf halke attraction se jude hain: paas-paas hain, par kisi tay jagah par nahi, isliye wo sarakte aur phisalte hain. Isme koi + ya − ion nahi, bas neutral molecule hain. Isi liye ye liquid bijli nahi chalata aur ise ubalne ke liye sirf molecules ko alag karna padta hai, bond todna nahi.

(Dhyan: saaf paani mein bahut kam miqdaar mein ions hote hain, isliye distilled paani lagbhag non-conductor mana jaata hai. Ye detail aage ki class mein.)

## Part E: Comparison table: har gun ke saath uska reason

Ye topic ki sabse zaroori table hai. Har row mein "gun" ke saath **kaaran** bhi hai.

| Gun | Ionic compound | Covalent compound | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Physical state** | Kadak solid | Gas, liquid ya naram solid | Ionic: ion-ion attraction bahut strong, ion jakde rehte hain. Covalent: molecules ke beech attraction kamzor, aasani se phisalte ya door ho jaate hain |
| **Kadakpan / hardness** | Kadak, crystal | Naram (jaise wax) ya gas/liquid | Lattice mein har ion chaaron taraf se jakda hai |
| **Brittle ya nahi** | Brittle: zor lagane par tukde | Aam taur par naram, brittle nahi | Layer khisakne par same charge aamne-saamne aate hain, wo ek doosre ko door dhakelte hain aur crystal chatak jaata hai |
| **Melting point** | Bahut ooncha (NaCl lagbhag 801 °C) | Neecha (wax kam garmi par pighalta hai) | Ion-ion ke strong attraction ko todne ko bahut energy (garmi) chahiye. Molecules ke kamzor attraction ko thodi garmi kaafi |
| **Boiling point** | Bahut ooncha (NaCl lagbhag 1413 °C) | Neecha (paani 100 °C, methane lagbhag −162 °C) | Wahi: strong ya kamzor attraction todne ki energy |
| **Paani mein ghulna** | Aam taur par ghulta hai | Aam taur par nahi ghulta (par cheeni aur alcohol ghulte hain) | Paani ke molecule ions ko lattice se kheench ke alag kar dete hain. Covalent molecules ke aise charge nahi hote |
| **Tel/petrol jaise mein ghulna** | Aam taur par nahi ghulta | Aam taur par ghulta hai | Tel ke molecule lattice ke ions ko alag nahi kar paate; covalent molecule tel ke molecules ke saath aasani se mil jaate hain |
| **Bijli (solid)** | Nahi chalta | Nahi chalta | Solid ionic mein ions fixed; covalent mein ions hi nahi |
| **Bijli (pighla ya ghola)** | Chalata hai | Nahi chalata (ghola bhi) | Free ions hain / ions nahi hain |

**Sabse chhota yaad rakhne ka tareeka:** ionic = **strong ion-ion attraction** = kadak, ooncha melting/boiling point, brittle, free ions mil jaayein to bijli. Covalent = **molecules ke beech kamzor attraction** = naram ya gas/liquid, neecha melting/boiling point, bijli nahi.

## Part F: Do imandaar apwaad (exceptions)

Class 9 ka niyam hai: "aam taur par". Kuch cheezein is niyam se hat ke chalti hain:

1. **HCl gas:** HCl covalent hai (H aur Cl electron baantte hain), phir bhi **paani mein ghulne par ions banate hain** (H⁺ aur Cl⁻), isliye uska ghol bijli chalata hai. Ye kaise hota hai, aage ki class mein.
2. **Diamond:** Diamond bhi covalent hai (sirf carbon atoms, har ek 4 se jude), par wo **bahut kadak** aur uska **melting point bahut ooncha** hai. Ye isliye ki wahan alag-alag molecule nahi, balki poora crystal ek hi bade jaal ki tarah baandha hua hai. Aage ki class mein.

Ye apwaad niyam ko galat nahi karte; ye batate hain ki "aam taur par" ka matlab kya hai.

## Part G: Solved examples

### Example 1. Eshita ke dabbon ka faisla

Ek dabbe ke ghol se LED jali, doosre ke ghol se nahi. Kaun sa namak hai?

- LED jalne ka matlab: ghol mein **free ions**. Namak (NaCl) ionic hai, paani mein Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ free ho jaate hain.
- Cheeni covalent hai, uska ghol sirf cheeni ke molecule rakhta hai, ions nahi.

**Jawab:** LED jalne wala dabba namak ka; na jalne wala cheeni ka.

Jaanch: kuch chakhne ki zaroorat nahi padi ✅

### Example 2. Solid namak se LED kyun nahi jalti?

- Solid NaCl mein Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ lattice mein **fixed** hain.
- Free ions nahi, to circuit poora nahi hota.

**Jawab:** Ions maujood hain par hil nahi sakte.

Jaanch: Taar ko sookhe namak par rakhne par LED nahi jalti; paani daalne par jal sakti hai ✅

### Example 3. Namak ka melting point ooncha kyun?

NaCl ka melting point lagbhag 801 °C hai.

- NaCl mein har Na⁺ ko 6 Cl⁻ aur har Cl⁻ ko 6 Na⁺ pakde hue hain.
- Is strong ion-ion attraction ko tod ke ions ko aapas mein phisalne dene ke liye bahut zyada energy chahiye.

**Jawab:** Strong attraction, isliye ooncha melting point.

Jaanch: Paani 0 °C par pighalta hai (kamzor attraction) ✅

### Example 4. Methane ka boiling point −162 °C kyun?

- CH₄ ke molecule alag-alag hain, aur ek molecule ke dusre molecule se attraction bahut kamzor hai.
- Ubalne par sirf molecules ko door karna hai, CH₄ ke andar ke C-H bond nahi todne.
- Isliye bahut kam garmi (balki bahut thanda temperature) par hi gas ban jaata hai. Room temperature par methane gas hai.

**Jawab:** Kamzor attraction, isliye bahut neecha boiling point.

Jaanch: Chapter 1 mein bhi aakarshan kamzor ho to gas ✅. Aur boiling point −162 °C ka matlab ki −162 °C se upar methane gas hai ✅

### Example 5. Namak ka crystal brittle kyun?

Namak ka crystal hathode se maaro to chatak jaata hai.

- Maan lo ek layer thodi khisak jaati hai. Ab + ion + ion ke saamne aa jaata hai, aur − ion − ion ke saamne.
- Same charge ek doosre ko **door dhakelte** hain, to crystal us jagah se **chatak** jaata hai.

**Jawab:** Layer khisakne par same charges aamne-saamne aate hain aur repel karte hain.

Jaanch: dhaatu (jaise loha) mudti hai, chatakti nahi; uska bond alag hai, aage ki class mein ✅

### Example 6. Namak paani mein kyun ghulta hai?

- Paani ke molecules ke sire par thoda + aur thoda − hota hai (aage ki class mein detail). Ye molecule lattice ke Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ ko kheench ke alag kar dete hain.
- Ions ghol mein free ho jaate hain.

**Jawab:** Paani ions ko lattice se alag kar deta hai.

Jaanch: Ghol mein free ions the, isliye LED jali ✅

### Example 7. Cooking oil mein namak kyun nahi ghulta?

- Oil ke molecules covalent hain aur namak ke strong ion-ion attraction ko tod nahi paate.
- Isliye namak oil mein neeche baith jaata hai.

**Jawab:** Oil ke molecules ions ko alag nahi kar paate.

Jaanch: Isi tarah tester oil mein LED nahi jalayega (koi ion nahi) ✅

### Example 8. Predict karo: MgO ya CH₄?

MgO aur CH₄ mein se kiska melting point zyada hoga?

- MgO ionic hai (Mg²⁺ aur O²⁻), ions ke strong attraction ke kaaran **bahut ooncha**: lagbhag 2850 °C.
- CH₄ covalent hai, kamzor attraction, **neecha**.

**Jawab:** MgO ka melting point bahut zyada.

Jaanch: MgO ke ions par 2+ aur 2− charge hain, to NaCl (1+, 1−) se bhi zyada attraction hai, isliye melting point (2850 °C) NaCl (801 °C) se bhi ooncha hai ✅

### Example 9. Ek anjaan safed solid

Ek safed solid kadak hai, hathode se chatak jaata hai, paani mein ghulta hai aur uska ghol LED jalata hai. Ionic ya covalent?

- Kadak, brittle, ghol bijli chalata hai: ye sab ionic ke gun hain.

**Jawab:** Ionic compound.

Jaanch: solid hone par ye bijli nahi chalayega (ek aur check) ✅

### Example 10. Ek anjaan liquid

Ek saaf liquid paani mein nahi milta (upar tairta hai), uska boiling point 69 °C ke paas hai aur wo tester par LED nahi jalata. Ionic ya covalent?

- Liquid, neecha boiling point, bijli nahi: ye covalent ke gun hain.

**Jawab:** Covalent compound.

Jaanch: ionic compound ka boiling point bahut ooncha hota, 69 °C jaisa neecha nahi ✅

### Example 11. Cheeni paani mein ghulti hai phir bhi bijli kyun nahi?

- Ghulne ka matlab ye nahi ki ions bane. Cheeni covalent hai aur ghol mein cheeni ke **poore molecule** alag-alag ghoomte hain, ye neutral hain.
- Charge wale kan nahi, to bijli nahi.

**Jawab:** Ghulna aur bijli chalana alag baatein hain; ions chahiye.

Jaanch: Tester mein cheeni ke ghol se LED nahi jali ✅

### Example 12. Dhaatu aur namak ka ghol

Tester ko taar se seedha jodne par LED jalegi, namak ke ghol se bhi. Dono kaise alag?

- Taar mein **electron** behte hain; ghol mein **ions** behte hain.
- Dono mein charge ka behna hi bijli hai.

**Jawab:** Dhaatu mein electrons, ionic ghol mein ions bijli le jaate hain.

Jaanch: Dono ko hi bijli chalane ke liye charge wale kan azaad chahiye ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Solid namak mein ions nahi hote"**

Hote hain, bas jakde hue hain. Isiliye wo bijli nahi chalata, na ki ions na hone ki wajah se.

**Galti 2: "Jo paani mein ghulta hai, wo bijli chalata hai"**

Cheeni paani mein ghulti hai par bijli nahi chalati. Bijli ke liye **ions** chahiye, ghulna alag baat hai.

**Galti 3: "Covalent bond kamzor hota hai, isliye melting point neecha"**

Nahi. Molecule ke **andar** ka bond strong hota hai. Melting point isliye neecha hota hai ki **molecules ke beech** ka attraction kamzor hai, aur wahi todna padta hai.

**Galti 4: "Paani garam karne se ionic compound mein ions bante hain"**

Ions pehle se hote hain. Garmi ya paani unhe free karta hai.

**Galti 5: "Saare covalent compounds paani mein nahi ghulte"**

Cheeni aur alcohol ghulte hain. Isiliye hum kehte hain "aam taur par".

**Galti 6: Safety: "tel ke liye petrol ya kerosene le lo"**

Nahi. Jalne wale liquid kabhi nahi. Sirf cooking oil. Aur tester ke liye ghar ki bijli kabhi nahi, sirf 2 AA cells.

## Quick Recap

- Ionic compound mein ions ke beech **strong** attraction; covalent compound mein molecules ke beech **kamzor** attraction (molecule ke andar bond strong).
- Ionic: kadak crystalline solid, **brittle**, **ooncha melting aur boiling point** (NaCl lagbhag 801 °C aur 1413 °C; MgO lagbhag 2850 °C), aam taur par **paani mein ghulta** hai, tel mein nahi.
- Covalent: gas, liquid ya naram solid, **neecha melting aur boiling point** (paani 0 aur 100 °C; methane boiling lagbhag −162 °C), aam taur par paani mein nahi (par cheeni, alcohol ghulte hain), aam taur par tel mein ghulta hai.
- **Bijli:** ionic compound solid mein nahi chalata (ions fixed), par **pighla hone par ya paani mein ghola jaane par chalata hai** (free ions). Covalent compound kisi bhi roop mein bijli nahi chalata (ions nahi).
- Tester: sirf **2 AA cells** aur LED/torch bulb, adult paas mein, kuch chakhna nahi, tel mein sirf cooking oil.
- Apwaad: **HCl gas** paani mein ions banata hai aur bijli chalata hai; **diamond** covalent hai par bahut kadak aur ooncha melting point (aage ki class mein).
- Kisi anjaan padarth ke gun dekh ke bond ka type predict kar sakte hain.

10. A white powder melts only above 700 °C, does not dissolve in petrol, and conducts electricity when melted but not as a solid. Is it ionic or covalent? Give two reasons.

11. A soft white solid melts at about 60 °C, does not dissolve in water but dissolves in cooking oil, and does not conduct electricity even when melted. Is it ionic or covalent? Explain.

12. Give one exception for ionic/covalent properties for each of HCl and diamond (one line each). Why are statements like "covalent compounds do not dissolve in water" written with the word "usually"?

13. State True or False and correct the false ones:
    (a) Solid NaCl has no ions in it.
    (b) Covalent compounds have low melting points because the bonds inside the molecule are weak.
    (c) Anything that dissolves in water conducts electricity.
    (d) Free ions are needed for a liquid to conduct electricity.

### Answers

1. Do gun: **melting point** aur **conductivity**. Ionic compound ka melting point bahut ooncha hota hai kyunki ions ke beech strong attraction todne ko bahut energy chahiye. Covalent compound ka melting point neecha hota hai kyunki molecules ke beech attraction kamzor hai. Bijli: ionic compound pighla ya ghola hone par free ions ke kaaran chalata hai; covalent compound mein ions nahi, isliye nahi chalata.

2. Solid NaCl mein Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ lattice mein **fixed** hain, hil nahi sakte, isliye bijli nahi chalti. Pighlane par lattice toot jaata hai aur ions **free** ho jaate hain, jo charge le jaate hain. Paani mein ghulne par paani ke molecule ions ko alag kar dete hain aur ions ghol mein free ho jaate hain, isliye ghol bijli chalata hai.

3. Sugar covalent hai. Paani mein ghulne par uske **poore molecules** alag ho jaate hain, jo neutral hain, koi + ya − ion nahi banta. Charge wale kan nahi, to bijli nahi chalti. Ghulna aur bijli chalana alag baatein hain.

4. Crystal mein + aur − ions ki layers ek ke baad ek hain. Hathode se maarne par ek layer thodi khisak jaati hai, to **same charge wale ions aamne-saamne** aa jaate hain aur ek doosre ko door dhakelte hain. Isse crystal ki us layer mein darar padti hai aur wo chatak jaata hai.

5. NaCl ionic hai: Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ ke beech **strong** attraction, jise todne ko bahut energy (801 °C) chahiye. Paani ke molecules ke beech attraction **kamzor** hai, isliye sirf 0 °C par hi molecules phisalne lagte hain.

6. **MgO** ka attraction zyada strong hai. Mg²⁺ aur O²⁻ par 2+ aur 2− charge hain, jabki Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ par sirf 1+ aur 1−. Jitna zyada charge, utna strong attraction, isliye melting point (2850 °C) bhi zyada. (Isse bada detail aage ki class mein.)

7. CH₄ covalent hai aur uske molecules ke beech attraction bahut kamzor hai, isliye −162 °C se upar hi wo gas ban jaata hai (room temperature par gas). Ubalne se **C-H bond nahi tootte**, sirf molecules ek doosre se door ho jaate hain.

8. (a) Distilled water: LED **nahi** jalti (lagbhag koi ion nahi). (b) Namak ka ghol: **jalti** hai (free Na⁺ aur Cl⁻). (c) Cheeni ka ghol: **nahi** (neutral molecule). (d) Cooking oil: **nahi** (covalent molecules, ions nahi).

9. Safety: (i) sirf 2 AA cells (3 V), (ii) adult paas mein, (iii) kuch chakhna nahi, (iv) jalne wala liquid nahi, sirf cooking oil, (v) taar ke sire aapas mein na chhooen, aur haath dho lo. Mains bijli kabhi nahi, kyunki ghar ki bijli se paani mein taar dalna jaanlewa ho sakta hai. Kerosene aag pakad sakta hai, isliye uski jagah cooking oil.

10. **Ionic.** Kaaran: (i) melting point bahut ooncha (700 °C se upar), jo strong ion-ion attraction ka nishaan hai; (ii) solid mein bijli nahi par pighalne par bijli, kyunki pighalne par ions free ho jaate hain. Petrol mein na ghulna bhi ionic ka gun hai.

11. **Covalent.** Naram hai aur melting point neecha (60 °C), paani mein nahi par tel mein ghulta hai, aur pighalne par bhi bijli nahi chalata (koi ion nahi). Ye sab molecules ke beech kamzor attraction aur ions ke na hone ke gun hain (mom/wax jaisa padarth).

12. HCl covalent hai, par paani mein ghulne par ions banata hai, isliye uska ghol bijli chalata hai. Diamond covalent hai, par bahut kadak aur uska melting point bahut ooncha hai (aage ki class mein kyun). Isliye "usually" (aam taur par) likhte hain, kyunki kuch compounds niyam ke apwaad hain.

13. (a) **False** ❌. Solid NaCl mein ions hain, bas fixed hain, free nahi. (b) **False** ❌. Molecule ke andar ka bond strong hota hai; melting point isliye neecha ki **molecules ke beech** attraction kamzor hai. (c) **False** ❌. Cheeni paani mein ghulti hai par bijli nahi chalati; bijli ke liye ions chahiye. (d) **True** ✅. Free ions (ya electrons) ke bina charge nahi behta.
