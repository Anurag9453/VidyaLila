# Topic 5: Kaun Sa Bond? Practice aur Chapter Ka Saar

Pichhle topics mein humne dono bond alag-alag samjhe: **ionic** (metal electron deta hai, non-metal leta hai, ions ek-dusre ko kheenchte hain) aur **covalent** (do non-metals electron ki pairs baantte hain). Topic 4 mein dono ke gun bhi dekhe: ionic compound kathor, high melting point, aur pighla ya ghola hua ho to bijli chalane wala; covalent compound zyadatar gas, liquid ya naram solid, low melting point, aur bijli nahi chalane wala. Aaj ka kaam practice ka hai: koi bhi simple compound dikhao, hum **bina rate-rataye** bata denge ki uska bond kaun sa hai, aur dot diagram bhi bana denge. Aakhir mein poore chapter ka saar ek table mein.

## Part A: Bond ka type pehchanne ka 3-step tareeka

**Step 1: Dono tatvon ko metal ya non-metal mein baanto.** Chapter 3 ki symbol aur valency table yaad karo: Li, Na, K, Mg, Ca, Al metals hain. H, C, N, O, F, P, S, Cl non-metals hain. (Hydrogen ek non-metal hai, chahe uska 1 baahari electron Li aur Na jaisa dikhta ho.)

**Step 2: Type tay karo.**

- Metal + non-metal: **ionic.**
- Non-metal + non-metal (ya same non-metal ke atoms): **covalent.**
- Metal + metal: ye ionic ya covalent nahi hota; ye **metallic** hota hai (aage ki class mein).

**Step 3: Electron-count se check karo.** Chapter 5 ki configuration likho (K 2, L 8, M 8):

- Ionic: metal ke outer electrons kitne hain (wahi wo dega) aur non-metal ko octet ke liye kitne chahiye (8 − outer electrons). **Diye gaye electrons = liye gaye electrons** hone chahiye. Isi se formula banta hai.
- Covalent: har atom ko duplet (H ke liye 2) ya octet (baaki ke liye 8) chahiye. Jitne electron kam hain utni **pairs share** hongi.

```
              Do tatva diye hain
                     |
                     v
        Dono ko metal / non-metal mein baanto
                     |
        +------------+--------------+
        |                           |
        v                           v
  1 metal + 1 non-metal      dono non-metal
        |                           |
        v                           v
   IONIC BOND                  COVALENT BOND
   (metal deta hai,            (dono electron
    non-metal leta hai)         pairs baantte hain)
        |                           |
        v                           v
  diye = liye electron        har atom: duplet / octet
  Na₂O: 2 x 1 = 1 x 2         H₂S: S ko 2, H ko 1 chahiye
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Bond ka type tay karne ka flow: pehle metal aur non-metal pehchano, phir ionic (diye = liye electron) ya covalent (duplet ya octet tak share) ka electron-hisaab check karo.

Ek sacchi baat: ye rule Class 9 ke simple compounds ke liye bilkul theek chalta hai. Aage ki class mein dekhenge ki kuch compounds beech ki halat mein bhi hote hain (jisme electron poori tarah diye-liye nahi jaate, par baante bhi nahi jaate). Abhi hum simple rule hi lagayenge.

## Part B: Ek hi non-metal, do alag partner

Sulphur (S) ka configuration 2, 8, 6 hai, yaani isko octet ke liye **2 electron** chahiye. Dekho ye kaise alag partner ke saath alag tareeke se kaam karta hai.

```
 Mg ke saath (metal)           H ke saath (non-metal)

 Mg: 2, 8, 2   S: 2, 8, 6     H: 1 (duplet ko 1 aur chahiye)

 Mg 2 electron deta hai        S aur H electron baantte hain
 S 2 electron leta hai

                ..                    ..
 [Mg]²⁺      [ :S: ]²⁻           H x. S .x H
                ..                    ..
 Mg²⁺ : 2, 8      S²⁻ : 2, 8, 8        S ke paas: 6 apne + 2 H ke
 (8 outer)        (8 outer)            = 8 (octet);  har H ke paas
                                       1 apna + 1 S ka = 2 (duplet)

 Formula : MgS                  Formula : H₂S
 IONIC (2 diye = 2 liye)        COVALENT (2 pairs share)
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Mixed example: sulphur Mg ke saath ionic MgS banata hai (electron leta hai), aur H ke saath covalent H₂S banata hai (electron baantta hai). Dono mein sulphur ka octet poora hota hai, tareeka alag hai. (S ke around ".." wali jodi lone pair hai: wo bond mein hissa nahi leti.)

Ye diagram ek zaroori baat batata hai: **octet poora karne ka goal wahi hai; raasta partner par depend karta hai.** Metal partner ho to non-metal electron *leta* hai; non-metal partner ho to electron *baante* jaate hain.

## Part C: 12 solved examples

Har example mein: elements, configuration, type, aur electron-hisaab. Mr. Trivedi ke store room ke bottles ek-ek karke.

### Group 1: Metal + non-metal (ionic)

**Example 1: KCl (potassium chloride).**
K (19): 2, 8, 8, 1, metal, 1 outer electron. Cl (17): 2, 8, 7, non-metal, 1 electron chahiye. K ek electron deta hai, Cl leta hai: 1 diya = 1 liya ✅.
K → K⁺ (2, 8, 8) aur Cl → Cl⁻ (2, 8, 8). Dot diagram: [K]⁺ [ :Cl: ]⁻ (Cl ke 8 dots). Formula **KCl**: **ionic**, shelf "IONIC".

**Example 2: CaCl₂ (calcium chloride).**
Ca (20): 2, 8, 8, 2, 2 electron dega. Har Cl ko 1 chahiye, to 2 Cl chahiye: 2 diye = 2 × 1 liye ✅.
Ca²⁺ (2, 8, 8) aur 2 Cl⁻ (2, 8, 8 each). Formula **CaCl₂**: **ionic.** (Chapter 3 ke criss-cross se bhi wahi: Ca valency 2, Cl valency 1.)

**Example 3: Na₂O (sodium oxide).**
Na (11): 2, 8, 1, 1 deta hai. O (8): 2, 6, 2 chahiye. 2 Na ke 2 electron ek O ko mil gaye: 2 × 1 = 1 × 2 ✅.
2 Na⁺ (2, 8) aur O²⁻ (2, 8). Formula **Na₂O**: **ionic.**

**Example 4: MgS (magnesium sulphide).**
Mg (12): 2, 8, 2, 2 deta hai. S (16): 2, 8, 6, 2 leta hai: 2 = 2 ✅.
Mg²⁺ (2, 8) aur S²⁻ (2, 8, 8). Formula **MgS** (1 : 1): **ionic.**

**Example 5: LiF (lithium fluoride).**
Li (3): 2, 1, 1 deta hai. F (9): 2, 7, 1 leta hai: 1 = 1 ✅.
Li⁺ (2, duplet, jaise helium) aur F⁻ (2, 8, octet, jaise neon). Formula **LiF**: **ionic.**

**Example 6: CaO (calcium oxide).**
Ca 2 deta hai (2, 8, 8, 2), O 2 leta hai (2, 6): 2 = 2 ✅. Ca²⁺ (2, 8, 8) aur O²⁻ (2, 8). Formula **CaO**: **ionic.**

**Example 7: K₂S (potassium sulphide).**
K 1 deta hai, S ko 2 chahiye, to 2 K lagenge: 2 × 1 = 1 × 2 ✅. 2 K⁺ aur S²⁻. Formula **K₂S**: **ionic.**

### Group 2: Non-metal + non-metal (covalent)

**Example 8: HF (hydrogen fluoride).**
H: 1 electron, duplet ke liye 1 aur chahiye. F (2, 7): octet ke liye 1 chahiye. Ek pair share: H x. F (F ke baaki 3 pairs lone pairs), dash mein H - F. H ke paas 1 + 1 = 2 (duplet) ✅, F ke paas 7 + 1 = 8 (octet) ✅. Ek **single bond**, **covalent.**

**Example 9: CCl₄ (carbon tetrachloride).**
C (6): 2, 4, 4 chahiye. Cl: 1 chahiye. Isliye C ke saath 4 Cl, har ek se 1 pair share. Check: C ke paas 4 apne + 4 share = 8 ✅; har Cl ke paas 7 apne + 1 = 8 ✅. 4 single bonds, **covalent.** Ye ek liquid hai jo paani mein nahi ghulta aur bijli nahi chalata.

**Example 10: H₂S (hydrogen sulphide).**
S (2, 8, 6) ko 2 chahiye, har H ko 1. To 2 H, 2 pairs (Figure 5.2 mein dikha). S: 6 + 2 = 8 ✅; har H: 1 + 1 = 2 ✅. **Covalent**, ek gas.

**Example 11: PH₃ (phosphine).**
P (15): 2, 8, 5, 3 chahiye. H: 1 chahiye. To 3 H, 3 pairs. P: 5 + 3 = 8 ✅; H: 1 + 1 = 2 ✅. **Covalent**, ek gas. (Formula mein 3 isliye: P ki valency 3.)

**Example 12: Cl₂ (chlorine gas).**
Dono atoms same, dono ko 1 electron chahiye. Koi bhi nahi dega, to share. Cl: 7 + 1 = 8 ✅ (dono). **Covalent**, single bond: Cl - Cl.

## Part E: Mixed reasoning, sab chapters ek saath

**Reasoning 1 (Chapter 3 formula + bond).** Magnesium aur chlorine se bane compound ka formula aur bond batao.
Mg valency 2, Cl valency 1: criss-cross se MgCl₂. Mg ne 2 electron diye, do Cl ne ek-ek liya. Metal + non-metal: **ionic.**

**Reasoning 2 (Chapter 5 configuration).** Kisi element X ka configuration 2, 8, 8, 2 hai aur Y ka 2, 8, 7. X Y se kaun sa bond banayega aur formula kya?
X = Ca (metal, 2 outer electrons). Y = Cl (7 outer, 1 chahiye). Ca 2 deta hai, har Cl 1 leta hai: **CaCl₂, ionic.**

**Reasoning 3 (Properties se bond).** Ek safed solid ka melting point bahut high hai, solid mein bijli nahi chalata, par paani mein ghulne par bulb jalata hai. Bond ka type?
Ghole hue mein bijli chalana = free ions. High melting point = ions ka strong attraction. **Ionic.** (Topic 4 ki tarah.)

**Reasoning 4 (Bond se properties).** CCl₄ ke baare mein 3 baatein predict karo.
Non-metals ka covalent compound: (1) low melting-boiling point, kamre ke taapman par liquid; (2) bijli nahi chalata (koi ions nahi); (3) paani mein nahi ghulta. Sahi: ye ek liquid hai jo paani se alag rehta hai.

**Reasoning 5 (Electron count se formula).** Aluminium (2, 8, 3) aur oxygen (2, 6) ka compound: formula aur check.
Al 3 deta hai, O 2 leta hai. LCM 6: 2 Al × 3 = 6 diye, 3 O × 2 = 6 liye ✅. Formula **Al₂O₃**, ionic. (Criss-cross: Al 3 aur O 2 ko ulta likha, wahi.)

**Reasoning 6 (Gadbad pakadna).** Ek student kehta hai "NaCl ka ek molecule" aur "H₂O ka ek formula unit". Kya sahi hai?
Dono galat. NaCl ionic hai: **formula unit**. H₂O covalent hai: **molecule.**

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "H ka 1 baahari electron hai, to wo Na jaisa metal hoga"**
H non-metal hai. Isliye HF, H₂S, PH₃ covalent hain, ionic nahi.

**Galti 2: Formula mein sirf aakhri tatva dekhna**
Bond tay karne ke liye **dono** tatva dekho: metal hai ya nahi.

**Galti 3: Ionic compound mein bhi "molecule" bolna**
Ionic mein formula unit, covalent mein molecule.

**Galti 4: Ionic mein diye aur liye electron barabar na hona**
Na₂O mein 2 Na ke 2 electron O ko milte hain. Agar numbers match nahi karte to formula galat likha hai.

**Galti 5: Covalent mein saare outer electrons share maan lena**
Sirf utne hi share hote hain jitne octet (ya duplet) poora karne ke liye chahiye. Baaki lone pairs hain (jaise S ya Cl ke).

**Galti 6: Ye sochna ki "covalent compound paani mein ghule to bijli chalega"**
Aam taur par nahi chalata. (Ek exception: HCl gas paani mein ions banata hai, aur kuch aur bhi; aage ki class mein.)

## Quick Recap

- Bond ka type tay karne ke liye: **dono tatva metal ya non-metal?** Metal + non-metal = ionic. Non-metal + non-metal = covalent.
- Ionic: **diye hue electron = liye hue electron**; isi se formula banta hai (Na₂O, CaCl₂, MgS, Al₂O₃).
- Covalent: har atom duplet (H) ya octet tak share karta hai; jitne electron kam, utni pairs (CCl₄ mein 4, PH₃ mein 3, H₂S mein 2, HF mein 1).
- Same non-metal ke atom (Cl₂, O₂, N₂) hamesha covalent.
- Sulphur Mg ke saath ionic (MgS) aur H ke saath covalent (H₂S): partner raasta tay karta hai, octet ka goal wahi.
- Ionic: formula unit, kathor, high melting point, ghole ya pighle mein bijli. Covalent: molecule, low melting point, bijli nahi.

## Practice Questions

1. State the three-step method to decide whether the bond in a compound is ionic or covalent.

2. Classify as ionic or covalent and justify with the types of elements: (a) KF, (b) CS₂, (c) CaS, (d) CF₄, (e) Li₂O, (f) PCl₃.

3. Show with electron counts (configurations) that the formula of potassium oxide is K₂O. Draw the dot diagram.

4. Draw the dot-and-cross diagram of NCl₃ (N: 2, 5; Cl: 2, 8, 7) and show that every atom gets an octet. How many lone pairs does N have?

5. Lithium (2, 1) and sulphur (2, 8, 6) form a compound. Find its formula using the electrons given and taken.

6. Oxygen (2, 6) and fluorine (2, 7) are both non-metals. Using the electrons each one needs, find the formula of their compound, the number of shared pairs, and the bond type.

7. Sulphur forms MgS with magnesium but H₂S with hydrogen. Explain in two or three lines why the bond type differs.

8. A white solid dissolves in water and the solution lights an LED tester, but the solid itself does not. Its melting point is very high. Name the bond type and give a reason. Suggest one possible formula from this lesson.

9. Which of the following would you expect to have a lower boiling point, CCl₄ or CaO? Give a reason.

10. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) Cl₂ is an ionic compound because it contains chlorine, a reactive element.
    (b) Li and F form LiF by the transfer of one electron.
    (c) CCl₄ has four single covalent bonds.
    (d) The correct word for one unit of CaCl₂ is "molecule".

### Answers

1. **Step 1:** dono tatvon ko metal ya non-metal mein baanto. **Step 2:** metal + non-metal = ionic; non-metal + non-metal = covalent. **Step 3:** electron-count se check karo: ionic mein diye = liye electron, covalent mein har atom duplet ya octet tak share karta hai.

2. (a) KF: K metal, F non-metal, **ionic.** (b) CS₂: C aur S dono non-metal, **covalent.** (c) CaS: Ca metal, S non-metal, **ionic.** (d) CF₄: dono non-metal, **covalent.** (e) Li₂O: Li metal, O non-metal, **ionic.** (f) PCl₃: dono non-metal, **covalent.**

3. K (2, 8, 8, 1) deta hai 1 electron. O (2, 6) ko 2 chahiye. To 2 K: 2 × 1 diye = 2 liye ✅. 2 K⁺ (2, 8, 8) aur O²⁻ (2, 8). Dot diagram: [K]⁺ [ :O: ]²⁻ [K]⁺, O²⁻ ke 8 dots (6 apne + 2 K ke). Charge +1 + 1 − 2 = 0 ✅. Formula **K₂O.**

4. N (2, 5) ko 3 chahiye, har Cl (2, 8, 7) ko 1. To N 3 Cl ke saath ek-ek pair share karta hai: Cl - N - Cl, aur teesra Cl N ke neeche. N: 3 shared pairs (6) + **1 lone pair** (2) = 8 ✅. Har Cl: 3 lone pairs (6) + 1 shared pair (2) = 8 ✅. Kul baahari electrons 5 + 3 × 7 = 26 = bond 6 + lone pairs 2 + 18 ✅. Diagram mein N ke electron dot (.) aur Cl ke cross (x).

5. Li 1 deta hai (2, 1 se 2), S 2 leta hai (2, 8, 6 se 2, 8, 8). 2 Li × 1 = 2 diye, 1 S × 2 = 2 liye ✅. Formula **Li₂S** (lithium sulphide), ionic.

6. O ko octet ke liye 2 chahiye, har F ko 1. Isliye ek O do F ke saath ek-ek pair share karta hai: formula **OF₂**, F - O - F, **2 shared pairs**. O: 2 shared pairs (4) + 2 lone pairs (4) = 8 ✅; har F: 1 shared pair (2) + 3 lone pairs (6) = 8 ✅. Dono non-metal, to **covalent.**

7. Mg metal hai, wo S ko 2 electron *de* deta hai (ionic MgS). H non-metal hai, wo electron *dene* wala nahi, to S aur H electron **baante** hain (covalent H₂S). Dono mein S ka octet poora hota hai.

8. **Ionic.** Ghole hue mein free ions bijli le jaate hain, solid mein ions bandhe hain, aur high melting point ions ke strong attraction se aata hai. Ek possible formula: NaCl ya KCl.

9. **CCl₄** ka boiling point kam hoga. Wo covalent molecules ka liquid hai, jinke beech kamzor attraction hai. CaO ionic hai, uske ions ke beech bahut strong attraction hai, isliye bahut high melting aur boiling point.

10. (a) **False** ❌. Cl₂ mein dono atom same non-metal hain, wo electron baantte hain: covalent, single bond. (b) **True** ✅. Li ek electron deta hai, F leta hai: Li⁺ aur F⁻. (c) **True** ✅. C ke 4 electron 4 Cl ke saath share hote hain. (d) **False** ❌. CaCl₂ ionic hai, uski ek ikai **formula unit** hai.
