# Topic 1: Chemical Bond Kyun Banta Hai: Stability aur Octet

Pichhle chapter ke Topic 5 mein humne dekha tha ki har atom ki baahari shell mein kuch electrons hote hain, aur **poori bhari baahari shell** (duplet ya octet) stable hoti hai. Wahan hum ek jagah ruk gaye the: Na ne 1 electron diya, Cl ne 1 electron liya, aur kahani adhoori chhod di gayi. Ab sawaal ye hai ki **atoms aapas mein judte kyun hain?** Paani ki ek boond, namak ka ek dana, hawa mein oxygen, ye sab judey hue atoms hi to hain. Aaj hum sirf **"kyun"** ka jawab dhoondenge, aur kuch electron-dot ka kaam seekhenge jo aane wale saare topics mein kaam aayega. **"Kaise"** ki poori kahani agle topics mein aayegi.

## Taash ka ghar

Mr. Batra ke bete Saksham ko taash ke patton ka ghar banane ki dhun chadhi thi. Ek ghanta laga kar usne teen manzil ka ghar khada kiya. Patte ek doosre se tike hue the, bilkul santulan mein. Usne saans rok rakhi thi.

Tabhi Mr. Batra chai lekar kamre mein aaye. Unke kadmon se zameen thodi si hili, aur **phat se** poora ghar dhah gaya. Saksham chillaya, "Papa!" Mr. Batra ne maafi maangi, par ek baat poochhi: "Beta, ek kaam kar. Ab jo patte zameen par bikhre hain, unhe kya kisi ne gira diya?" Saksham ne kaha, "Nahi, wo to khud hi gire." "Aur ab ye patte bikhre hue hain, ab inhe koi chhoo de to wo dobara gir jaayenge?" Saksham hans pada, "Nahi, ab to ye zameen par pade hain, ab kahan girenge?"

Mr. Batra ne samjhaya, "Yahi hai asli baat. Khada hua ghar **mehnat** se tika tha: har patta thoda sa santulan sambhal raha tha, aur zara si hulchal par sab gir gaya. Zameen par pada hua dher **aaraam se** pada hai, usse neeche jaane ki jagah hi nahi. Prakriti ki zyadatar cheezein aisi hi hoti hain: wo us arrangement ki taraf jaati hain jahan wo **zyada sthir** (stable) hon, jahan unhe kam 'mehnat' karni pade."

Saksham ne socha, "To kya atoms bhi aisa karte hain?" Mr. Batra muskuraye. "Bilkul. Bahut se atoms ki baahari shell adhoori hoti hai, aur wo adhoori shell taash ke khade ghar jaisi 'khichi hui' arrangement hai. Atoms **judkar** ek zyada sthir arrangement mein pahunchna chahte hain. Is judne ka naam hi **chemical bond** hai."

## Part A: Stable matlab kya?

Taash ka ghar aur zameen ka dher ek baat sikhate hain: ek hi cheez (wahi 52 patte) do arrangement mein ho sakti hai, ek **khichi hui** aur ek **aaraam wali**. Pehla arrangement apne aap tikta nahi; doosra tika rehta hai.

```
    Khada ghar (mehnat se)              Dhah gaya                Dher (stable)

            /\
           /  \                         \  /  /                 ___ ___ ___
          /\  /\                         /  \ /                 \_\_/_/_/_/
         /  \/  \                       /  /\ \                  patte zameen par
        /\  /\  /\                                               pade hain
       /  \/  \/  \

     patte santulan mein,           zara si hulchal               ab aur neeche
     par kisi bhi pal gir           se saare gire                 jaane ki jagah
     sakte hain                                                   nahi
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Taash ka ghar (khada, khichi hui arrangement) zara si hulchal se dhah jaata hai aur patte zameen par bikhre dher (aaraam wali, stable arrangement) mein pahunch jaate hain. Atoms bhi aisi arrangement ki taraf jaate hain jo zyada sthir ho: kam khichav, zyada aaraam. Ye bas ek upma (analogy) hai, atom taash ke patte nahi hote.

Chemistry ki bhasha mein hum kehte hain ki jo arrangement zyada stable hota hai uski **energy kam** hoti hai. Abhi itna samajh lo: **stable = sthir, jise badalne ka dil na kare.** Aur kisi bhi atom ke liye sabse jaana-pehchana stable arrangement kya hai? Wohi jo humne pichhle chapter mein dekha tha.

## Part B: Noble gas: sabse aaraam mein baithe atoms

Pichhle chapter mein humne dekha ki **He (2), Ne (2, 8) aur Ar (2, 8, 8)** ki baahari shell poori bhari hai: **duplet** ya **octet**. Ye teeno gas **lagbhag kisi se nahi judti**; unhe judne ki zaroorat hi nahi, wo pehle se "zameen par pade taash ke dher" jaisi hain. Ye hi hamare **reference (aadarsh)** hain.

Baaki atom (jaise Na, Cl, O, Mg) ki baahari shell adhoori hai. Unke paas teen raaste hain apni shell ko noble gas jaisi poori karne ke liye:

1. **Electron de dena (lose):** baahari ke kuch electron kisi aur ko de do. Neeche wali bhari hui shell baahari ban jaati hai.
2. **Electron le lena (gain):** kisi doosre se kuch electron le lo taaki baahari shell poori ho jaaye.
3. **Electron share karna:** kisi doosre atom ke saath electron saajha karo, taaki dono ki shell poori ginti mein aaye.

```
    1. DENA (lose)            2. LENA (gain)            3. SHARE KARNA

    Na  ----->  Na⁺           Cl  <-----  e⁻            Cl  ::  Cl
    (2, 8, 1)   (2, 8)        (2, 8, 7)                 dono ke beech
     1 e⁻ gaya   neon jaisa    1 e⁻ aaya                 ek jodi electron
                              Cl⁻ (2, 8, 8)             dono ki ginti mein
                              argon jaisa               aati hai
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Shell poori karne ke teen raaste. (1) Na apna 1 baahari electron de deta hai, Na⁺ ki configuration 2, 8 (neon jaisi). (2) Cl 1 electron le leta hai, Cl⁻ ki configuration 2, 8, 8 (argon jaisi). (3) Do Cl atom ek jodi electron saajha karte hain, aur har Cl ko apni octet poori milti hai. Teesre raaste ki poori kahani Topic 3 mein.

Ek chhota preview (taki aage ka rasta pata rahe): jab ek atom electron **deta** hai aur doosra **leta** hai, to ek **ionic bond** banta hai (Topic 2 ka vishay). Jab do atom electron **share** karte hain, to ek **covalent bond** banta hai (Topic 3 ka vishay). Abhi in dono ko na seekhna hai na yaad rakhna hai; bas itna dhyan rakho ki **bond banne ka kaaran ek hi hai: stable, noble-gas jaisi arrangement.**

## Part C: Bond asal mein hai kya?

Bond koi dhaaga ya takwa nahi hai jo atoms ko baandh de. **Bond ek aakarshan (kheench) hai.** Chapter 1 mein humne dekha tha ki particles ke beech aakarshan hota hai, aur wahi tay karta tha ki cheez thos hai, tarl hai ya gas. Yahan bhi wahi baat hai, bas ye aakarshan **bahut mazboot** hai aur electrons ki lena-dena ya sajhedari se paida hota hai. Is topic mein itna kaafi hai: **chemical bond = atoms (ya ions) ko jodkar rakhne wala aakarshan, jo electrons ke karan banta hai, aur jiska maqsad stable arrangement paana hai.**

## Part D: Electron-dot symbols (Lewis symbols)

Bonding ki saari kahaniyon mein bas **baahari electrons** kaam karte hain. Andar ke shells ko kuch nahi karna. To har baar poora atom diagram banane ki jagah hum ek **shorthand** use karte hain: tatva ka **symbol** likho aur uske chaaron taraf **valence electrons** ko **dots (•)** se dikhao. Ise **electron-dot symbol** ya **Lewis symbol** kehte hain.

**Kaise banayein? 4 qadam:**

1. Tatva ka symbol likho (Na, Cl, O ...). Symbol atom ka nucleus aur andar ke saare shells maana jaata hai.
2. Configuration se **baahari (valence) electrons ki ginti** nikalo. (Pichhle chapter ki table se: K 2, L 8, M 8, phir N.)
3. Symbol ke **chaar taraf** (upar, daayen, neeche, baayen) dots rakho. Pehle **har taraf ek-ek** dot rakho.
4. Agar 4 se zyada dots hain, to 5vein dot se shuru karke dots ko **jodi mein** karo: pehle upar wali taraf, phir daayen, phir neeche, phir baayen.

Helium ek khaas case hai: uski sirf ek shell (K) hai jo 2 se poori hoti hai, isliye uske 2 dots ek jodi mein dikhate hain (duplet).

Neeche pehle 20 tatvon ki poori table hai. Har valence electron ginti configuration se li gayi hai (K 2, L 8, M 8, phir N), aur har tatva ki dots ki jodi aur akele dots ginkar jaanch ki gayi hai: **jodi x 2 + akele = valence electrons.**

- Dots ki **jagah** (kaun sa dot kis taraf) sirf ek picture hai; usse atom ka swabhav nahi badalta. Aage ke topics ke bond diagrams mein dots kabhi doosri taraf bhi dikhenge. Zaroori sirf **ginti** hai: kitne baahari electrons hain.

## Part E: Kaun dega, kaun lega, kaun share karega?

Ab asli sawaal: kisi atom ke baahari electrons dekh kar kaise pata chale ki wo kaun sa raasta chunega? Yaad rakho, atom ko apni baahari shell **octet (ya duplet)** tak pahunchani hai, aur wo **kam se kam mehnat** wala raasta chunega.

- **1 se 3 baahari electrons wale** (Li, Na, K: 1; Be, Mg, Ca: 2; Al: 3): electron **dena** aasan hai. 1 se 3 electron de do to neeche wali bhari hui shell baahari ban jaati hai. Lena to 5 se 7 electrons lene padte, jo bahut mushkil. Ye **metals** (dhatu) hain, aur ye electron **dene** ka raasta pasand karte hain.
- **5 se 7 baahari electrons wale** (N, P: 5; O, S: 6; F, Cl: 7): octet mein sirf 3, 2 ya 1 electron kam hain. **Lena** (ya share karna) aasan hai. Ye **non-metals** (adhatu) hain.
- **4 baahari electrons wale** (C, Si): 4 dena ya 4 lena dono barabar mushkil hain, isliye ye aksar **share** karte hain. (Carbon ki is baat ka bahut bada kaam aage dikhega.)
- **8 (ya He ke 2) wale** (He, Ne, Ar): kuch karne ki zaroorat nahi: noble gas.
- **Hydrogen** thoda alag hai: uska 1 electron hai aur duplet ke liye 1 aur chahiye. Wo aksar share karta hai.

| Baahari electrons | Misaal | Octet ke liye kitna chahiye (8 − n) | Aam raasta |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Na, K | 7 lena ya 1 dena | **Dena** |
| 2 | Mg, Ca | 6 lena ya 2 dena | **Dena** |
| 3 | Al | 5 lena ya 3 dena | **Dena** |
| 4 | C, Si | 4 lena ya 4 dena | **Share karna** |
| 5 | N, P | 3 lena | **Lena ya share karna** |
| 6 | O, S | 2 lena | **Lena ya share karna** |
| 7 | F, Cl | 1 lena | **Lena ya share karna** |
| 8 | Ne, Ar | kuch nahi | Judte nahi |

(Be aur Al ke kuch compounds share karne wale bhi hote hain; Si aur B ko dhatu aur adhatu ke beech ka tatva maana jaata hai. Ye sab aage ki class mein.)

Ek aam pattern jo is table se dikhta hai: **dhatu ka valence electron kam (1 se 3)** aur **adhatu ka valence electron zyada (4 se 7).** Aur bond banta kaise hai? **Dhatu + adhatu** ke beech lena-dena hota hai (ionic bond, Topic 2). **Adhatu + adhatu** ke beech sharing hoti hai (covalent bond, Topic 3). Dono ka maqsad ek hi: sabke paas octet (ya duplet).

## Part F: Solved examples

### Example 1. Nitrogen ka dot symbol banao

- Nitrogen: Z = 7, configuration 2, 5. Valence electrons = 5.
- Pehle 4 taraf 4 dots (upar, daayen, neeche, baayen). 5vaan dot upar wale ke saath jodi banata hai.
- Natija: upar **jodi** (2 dots) aur baaki teen taraf **1-1 dot**.

Jaanch: 2 (jodi) + 1 + 1 + 1 = 5 = valence electrons ✅

### Example 2. Sulphur ka dot symbol

- Sulphur: Z = 16, 2, 8, 6. Valence electrons = 6.
- 4 taraf 1-1, phir 5vaan upar (jodi), 6th daayen (jodi).
- Natija: upar aur daayen **jodi**, neeche aur baayen **akela dot**.

Jaanch: 2 + 2 + 1 + 1 = 6 ✅ Oxygen (2, 6) ka dot symbol bhi bilkul yahi hoga, kyunki dono ke valence electrons 6 hain ✅

### Example 3. Dot symbol dekh kar tatva ka valence electron batao

Ek tatva ke symbol ke aas-paas **teen jodi aur ek akela dot** hain. Valence electrons kitne?

- 3 jodi = 6 dots. 1 akela dot = 1. Kul = 6 + 1 = **7**.
- 7 valence electrons wale tatva: F (2, 7) ya Cl (2, 8, 7).

Jaanch: 3 × 2 + 1 = 7 ✅ Dono ko 1 electron chahiye octet ke liye (8 − 7 = 1) ✅

### Example 4. Chlorine ko kitne electron chahiye, sodium ko kitne dene hain?

- Cl: 2, 8, 7. Octet ke liye 8 − 7 = **1 electron lena** hai.
- Na: 2, 8, 1. 1 dene se L shell (8) baahari ban jaati hai, to **1 electron dena** aasan hai. (Lena hota to 7 lene padte.)

Jaanch: Na ko dene wala 1 electron, aur Cl ko lene wala 1 electron: dono ki ginti **barabar** hai ✅ Is wajah se ek Na aur ek Cl ki jodi banegi, aur ye baat Topic 2 mein poori dikhayenge.

### Example 5. Magnesium aur calcium ke dot symbols ek jaise kyun?

- Mg: 2, 8, 2 aur Ca: 2, 8, 8, 2. Dono ke valence electrons **2**.
- Dono ke dot symbol: do akele dots (upar aur daayen).

Jaanch: Dono ke baahari 2 ✅ Shell alag hain par dots wahi hain, isliye dono ka swabhav ek jaisa: 2 electron **dene** wale metal.

### Example 6. Helium aur beryllium: dono ke 2 valence electrons, phir bhi alag kyun?

- He: configuration 2. Uski ek hi shell K hai, aur wo 2 se **poori bhari** hai (duplet). Dots ki **jodi**.
- Be: configuration 2, 2. Baahari L shell mein sirf 2 hain, jabki L ko 8 chahiye. Shell **adhoori** hai. Dots **akele-akele**.

Jaanch: He ki baahari shell poori hai, isliye wo stable aur nishkriya ✅. Be ki L shell adhoori hai, isliye wo bond banata hai ✅. Dots ki ginti ek jaisi hone par bhi pata chalta hai ki shell ki **kitni jagah** hai.

### Example 7. Octet ke liye kitni kami?

Neeche diye atoms ko octet (H ke liye duplet) ke liye kitne electron lene padenge: (a) O, (b) P, (c) H.

- (a) O: 2, 6. 8 − 6 = **2**.
- (b) P: 2, 8, 5. 8 − 5 = **3**.
- (c) H: 1. Duplet 2 ka hai, 2 − 1 = **1**.

Jaanch: Pichhle chapter ki valency table se: O 2, P 3, H 1 ✅

### Example 8. Kaun sa atom sabse zyada "judne ko betab"?

Li (1 baahari), Ne (8 baahari), O (6 baahari): kaun judne ko sabse kam betab hai?

- Ne ki octet poori hai, wo sabse **kam** betab (lagbhag koi bond nahi).
- Li aur O dono ki shell adhoori hai, dono bond banate hain.

Jaanch: Ne ka dot symbol chaar jodi hai, akela dot koi nahi, matlab koi electron nahi chahiye ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Bond ko dhaaga samajhna**

Bond ek **aakarshan** hai, jo electrons ke karan paida hota hai. Atoms ke beech koi asli dhaaga nahi bandha hota.

**Galti 2: Ye maanna ki atom "chahta" hai, yaani wo soch kar kaam karta hai**

"Atom ko octet chahiye" sirf samajhne ka aasaan tareeka hai. Atom soch nahi sakta. Sach ye hai ki octet wali arrangement zyada stable hoti hai (kam energy), isliye wahi arrangement banti hai.

**Galti 3: Dot symbol mein andar ke electrons bhi dikhana**

Na ke dot symbol mein 11 dots nahi, sirf **1** dot hota hai (valence). Andar ke 10 electrons symbol mein hi samaaye hain.

**Galti 4: Dots ki ginti galat karna**

Cl ke 7 dots hone chahiye, 17 nahi. Valence electrons configuration ki **aakhri** ginti hain: Cl 2, 8, **7**.

**Galti 5: Sab atoms ko dhatu ya sab ko adhatu samajhna**

1 se 3 baahari electrons wale (dhatu) **dene** ko aasan samajhte hain. 4 se 7 wale (adhatu) **lene** ya **share** karne ko. Ek hi raasta sab par laagu nahi hota.

**Galti 6: He ko Be jaisa samajhna**

Dono ke dots 2 hain, par He ki shell poori hai aur Be ki adhoori. Dots ke saath hamesha **shell ki jagah** bhi dekho.

## Quick Recap

- Atoms **zyada stable arrangement** paane ke liye judte hain (taash ka ghar dhah kar dher ban jaata hai, jo zyada sthir hai).
- Stable arrangement ka reference **noble gas** hai: He (2, duplet), Ne (2, 8) aur Ar (2, 8, 8) (octet).
- Teen raaste: electron **dena**, **lena**, ya **share karna**. Dena + lena se **ionic bond** (Topic 2), share karne se **covalent bond** (Topic 3).
- **Chemical bond** = atoms (ya ions) ko jodkar rakhne wala aakarshan, jo electrons ke karan banta hai.
- **Electron-dot symbol** = symbol ke chaaron taraf valence electrons ke dots: pehle 1-1 dot chaaron taraf, phir jodi banao. He mein 2 dots ki jodi.
- **Dhatu** (baahari 1 se 3): dene wale. **Adhatu** (baahari 4 se 7): lene ya share karne wale. C, Si (4): aksar share. **Noble gas**: judte nahi.
- Octet ke liye kami = 8 − (valence electrons) (H ke liye 2 − 1 = 1).

### Answers

1. Kyunki bahut se atoms ki baahari shell adhoori hoti hai, aur adhoori shell kam stable hai. Atoms electron dekar, lekar ya share karke noble gas jaisi poori shell (duplet ya octet) pa lete hain, jo zyada stable (kam energy wali) arrangement hai. Bond isi se banta hai.

2. Chemical bond atoms (ya ions) ko jodkar rakhne wala **aakarshan** hai, jo electrons ke karan banta hai. Ye koi asli dhaaga nahi hai.

3. Li: 2, 1 (1 valence). C: 2, 4 (4). O: 2, 6 (6). Cl: 2, 8, 7 (7). Ca: 2, 8, 8, 2 (2). Dot symbols: Li ke 1 dot; C ke chaaron taraf 1-1 (4 akele); O ke 2 jodi + 2 akele; Cl ke 3 jodi + 1 akela; Ca ke 2 akele dots.

4. H: 1 dot. He: 1 jodi (2 dots). N: 1 jodi + 3 akele (5). Ne: 4 jodi (8). Al: 3 akele dots (3). S: 2 jodi + 2 akele (6). Har ek mein ginti valence electrons ke barabar hai (1, 2, 5, 8, 3, 6).

5. 2 jodi = 4 dots, 2 akele dots = 2 dots, kul **6** valence electrons. Pehle 20 tatvon mein O (2, 6) aur S (2, 8, 6).

6. Li (2, 1), Na (2, 8, 1) aur K (2, 8, 8, 1) mein valence electrons 1 hain, isliye dot symbol mein 1 dot. F (2, 7) aur Cl (2, 8, 7) mein 7 hain, isliye 3 jodi + 1 akela. Dot symbol sirf valence electrons ginti par tikta hai, andar ke shell par nahi.

7. Electron **dena**, **lena**, ya **share karna**. Na (2, 8, 1): **dena**, kyunki 1 dene se 2, 8 bachta hai (neon jaisa), jabki lena ho to 7 lene padte. Cl (2, 8, 7): **lena** (ya share karna), kyunki sirf 1 kam hai octet ke liye (8 − 7 = 1).

8. He (2) ki ek hi shell K hai aur wo 2 se poori bhari hai (duplet). Be (2, 2) ki baahari L shell mein sirf 2 hain jabki 8 chahiye, shell adhoori hai.

9. (a) F (2, 7): **1**. (b) S (2, 8, 6): **2**. (c) N (2, 5): **3**. (d) H (1): duplet ke liye **1**.

10. K: 1 valence, **dene wala**. O: 6, **lene ya share karne wala**. Mg: 2, **dene wala**. P: 5, **lene ya share karne wala**. Al: 3, **dene wala**. F: 7, **lene ya share karne wala**.

11. (a) Na (2, 8, 1) electron **deta** hai. (b) Cl (2, 8, 7) electron **leta** hai. (c) Cl₂ mein dono Cl atom ek jodi electron **share** karte hain.

12. (a) **False** ❌. Ne ke 8 dots (octet) hain, par wo stable hai aur lagbhag kisi se nahi judta. (b) **False** ❌. Na ke sirf **1** dot hote hain (valence electron). (c) **True** ✅. Dono ke 4 valence electrons hain, 4 akele dots. (d) **True** ✅. Unki shell poori hai.

Shaabash! Ab tumhe pata hai ki atoms **judte kyun hain**: kyunki jud kar wo taash ke dhahe hue dher ki tarah zyada stable arrangement paa lete hain. Tum kisi bhi pehle-20 tatva ka electron-dot symbol bana sakte ho, aur dekh kar bata sakte ho ki wo dene, lene ya share karne wala hai. Saksham ne us din ek aur ghar nahi banaya, lekin patton ka dher dekh kar bola, "Papa, ye dher bhi kamaal ka hai."

**Aage kya?** Agle topic **"Ionic Bond: Electron Dene-Lene Ka Rishta"** mein dekhenge ki jab ek dene wala dhatu aur ek lene wala adhatu milte hain, to Na aur Cl ka 1-1 electron ka lena-dena kaise unhe ek mazboot rishte mein baandh deta hai, aur isi se namak (NaCl), MgO aur kai compounds ke formula kaise nikalte hain.
