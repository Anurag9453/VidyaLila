# Topic 2: Combination aur Decomposition: Jodna aur Todna

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki har reaction mein purane bond **tootte** hain, atoms nayi tarah se **arrange** hote hain, aur naye bond **bante** hain. Hamne ye bhi dekha ki itne saare reactions ko samajhne ke liye unhe **types** mein baant dete hain, jaise phal, sabzi aur anaaj ko alag-alag group mein rakhte hain. Aaj hum do sabse seedhe group dekhte hain: ek reaction jisme cheezein **mil kar ek** ban jaati hain, aur doosra jisme **ek toot kar kai** ban jaati hai.

---

## Bakery ki kahani

Mr. Pandit ke saath Rutvik subah-subah mohalle ki bakery ke paas se guzar raha tha. Bakery ki deewar par chuna (whitewash) lagne wala tha, aur darwaze ke bahar safed ghol ki ek balti rakhi thi. Andar bhatti jaisa oven chalu tha, aur bakery wale kaka ek cake ka ghol tray mein daal kar oven mein rakh rahe the.

Rutvik ne dekha: ghol tray mein bas aadha tha. Kuch der baad kaka ne tray bahar nikala to cake **phoola hua, sponge jaisa, poore tray tak** aa gaya tha. "Kaka ne to kuch aur daala hi nahi, phir ye phoola kaise?" Rutvik ne poochha.

Mr. Pandit ne kaha, "Ghol mein ek chutki **baking soda** thi. Oven ki garmi se wo **toot gaya**, aur usse gas nikli, jo ghol ke andar chhote-chhote bubbles ban gayi. Wahi bubbles cake ko phoola dete hain." Phir unhone bahar ki balti ki taraf ishara kiya: "Aur ye safed ghol **chuna** hai. Is balti mein kaka ke mazdoor ne ek patthar jaisi cheez, jise **quicklime** kehte hain, paani mein daali thi. Tab paani sachmuch garam ho gaya tha aur bhap uthi thi. Wahan do cheezein **mil kar ek nayi cheez** ban gayi."

"To ek jagah cheez **tooti**, aur doosri jagah cheezein **judi**?" Rutvik ne poochha.

"Bilkul," Mr. Pandit ne muskura kar kaha. "Isi ko hum kehte hain **decomposition** (todna) aur **combination** (jodna). Ye ek hi sikke ke do pehlu hain. Aaj dono ko samjhte hain."

## Part A: Combination reaction: do ya zyada se ek

Naam hi sab kuch bata deta hai: **combination** yaani **milana**. Do ya zyada reactants (tatva ya compounds) milte hain aur **sirf ek hi product** banta hai. Ek chhota sa pattern yaad rakho: **A + B → AB**.

Pichhle topic mein humne bonds ki baat ki thi. Combination mein reactants ke purane bond tootte hain aur atoms nayi tarah se jud kar **ek bada, naya** compound bana dete hain.

```
   Combination:      A    +    B    ───►    A B

                  (alag)    (alag)         (ek hi jura hua)


   Ek asli udaharan: 2H₂ + O₂ ───► 2H₂O

     H─H   H─H          O═O                H─O─H     H─O─H

    baayein: H = 4, O = 2                daayein: H = 4, O = 2
    (hydrogen ke 2 molecule, oxygen ka 1)   (paani ke 2 molecule)
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Combination: A aur B mil kar ek AB banate hain. Neeche asli udaharan: hydrogen ke 2 aur oxygen ka 1 molecule mil kar paani ke 2 molecule banate hain. Atoms gine to dono taraf H = 4 aur O = 2: koi atom na naya bana, na gaya, bas bond badal gaye.

### Combination ke kuch jaane-pehchane udaharan

| Reaction | Kaun mila, kya bana | Kahan dikhta hai |
|---|---|---|
| 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO | Magnesium + oxygen → magnesium oxide | Mg ribbon jalne par tez safed roshni |
| C + O₂ → CO₂ | Carbon + oxygen → carbon dioxide | Koyla ya charcoal ka jalna |
| 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O | Hydrogen + oxygen → paani | Hydrogen ka jalna |
| N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃ | Nitrogen + hydrogen → ammonia | Khaad (fertiliser) ke factory mein |
| CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂ | Quicklime + paani → slaked lime | Bakery ki balti ka safed ghol |

Dhyan do: pehle chaar mein **do tatva** mil rahe hain, jabki aakhri mein **do compounds** (CaO aur H₂O) mil kar ek naya compound bana rahe hain. Dono combination hain, kyunki dono mein **product ek hi hai**. (CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O mein product do hain, isliye wo combination nahi hai; usse hum Topic 6 mein "combustion" ke roop mein dekhenge.)

### Ek jhalak: magnesium ka jalna

**Sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo).** School lab mein teacher ek patli, saaf kiye hue Mg ribbon ko chimte se pakad kar jalate hain. Ribbon **bahut tez safed roshni** deta hai aur piche **safed powder** (MgO) bacha rehta hai. Ye roshni itni tez hoti hai ki **seedha dekhna nahi chahiye**, aankhon ko nuksan ho sakta hai. Student ise khud nahi karte; door se, teacher ke bataye anusaar dekhte hain.

Reaction: **2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO**. Jaanch: Mg: 2 = 2 ✅, O: 2 = 2 ✅.

### Quicklime ka slaking: sabse garam combination

Bakery ki balti wala reaction: **CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂**. Quicklime (CaO) ek safed, dhela-sa patthar hota hai. Jab usme paani milta hai to wo bahut zor se tootta-sa hai, hissing hoti hai, **bahut garmi nikalti hai** aur kabhi-kabhi paani ubalne lagta hai. Isliye iska naam **slaking** (bujhana) hai: chuna "bujh" gaya aur slaked lime ban gaya. Ye reaction **exothermic** hai: garmi bahar deta hai.

> **Safety:** Quicklime **kostik** hota hai. Ye aankhon aur skin ko jalata hai. Isse sirf **adult**, dastaane (gloves) aur chashme pahan kar sambhalte hain. **Students ise kabhi nahi chhuenge.** Slaked lime (Ca(OH)₂) kam tej hai, par wo bhi aankh, skin aur munh ke liye theek nahi, isliye whitewash ko bhi haath se chhoote ya chaatte nahi.

## Part B: Solved examples: combination

### Example 1. Mg aur O₂ ke masses se MgO ka mass

Mg ka mass 24 u, O ka 16 u (Chapter 4 se). 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO mein: reactants ka mass = 2 × 24 + 2 × 16 = 48 + 32 = **80**. Product: 2 × (24 + 16) = 2 × 40 = **80**.

Jaanch: dono taraf 80 ✅. To 48 g Mg poori tarah 32 g oxygen ke saath mil kar **80 g** MgO deta hai. Jo cheez jalne ke baad dikhti hai wo halki nahi, balki **zyada bhaari** hai, kyunki hawa ka oxygen usme judh gaya.

### Example 2. Ammonia banane ka hisaab

N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃. Kya ye balanced hai? Baayein N = 2, H = 3 × 2 = 6. Daayein N = 2 × 1 = 2, H = 2 × 3 = 6.

Jaanch: N: 2 = 2 ✅, H: 6 = 6 ✅. Ek N₂ ko 3 H₂ chahiye: jaisa dekha, 1 : 3 ka fixed hisaab (kheer ke nuskhe jaisa).

### Example 3. Ek reaction combination hai ya nahi?

Teen reactions dekho:
- (i) C + O₂ → CO₂: reactants 2, product **1** ✅ combination.
- (ii) CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂: reactants 2, product **1** ✅ combination.
- (iii) CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O: reactants 2, product **2** ❌ combination nahi.

Pehchaan ka seedha tareeka: **arrow ke daayein sirf ek formula**.

## Part C: Decomposition reaction: ek se do ya zyada

Ab ulta socho. Combination mein cheezein judti thi. **Decomposition** (de-composition: "composition" ka ulta) mein **ek hi reactant** hota hai, aur wo toot kar **do ya zyada, zyada simple** products banata hai. Pattern: **AB → A + B**.

Ab ek sawaal: ek compound khud se kyun tootega? Uske bond **mazboot** hote hain. To use toodne ke liye **energy dena** padti hai. Ye energy teen tareeke se di jaati hai: **garmi se, bijli se, roshni se**. Isliye decomposition ke teen mukhya flavours hain.

```
                                      thermal decomposition
                   ┌── garmi (heat) ──►  CaCO₃ ──► CaO + CO₂
                   │
                   │                   electrolytic decomposition
   AB ─────────────┼── bijli ─────────►  2H₂O ──► 2H₂ + O₂
   (ek reactant)   │   (electricity)
                   │
                   │                   photolytic decomposition
                   └── roshni (light) ─►  2AgBr ──► 2Ag + Br₂

   AB ──► A + B     (ek toot kar do ya zyada ban gaye)
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Decomposition ke teen flavour: garmi se (thermal), bijli se (electrolytic) aur roshni se (photolytic). Teeno mein ek hi reactant tootkar do products deta hai. Kisi bhi flavour mein atoms ki ginti dono taraf barabar rehti hai.

### 1. Thermal decomposition: garmi se tootna

**(a) Baking soda aur cake.** Baking soda ka naam **sodium hydrogencarbonate**, formula NaHCO₃ hai. Garam oven mein ye toot jaata hai:

**2NaHCO₃ → Na₂CO₃ + H₂O + CO₂**

Jaanch: Na: 2 = 2 ✅, H: 2 = 2 ✅, C: 2 = 1 + 1 = 2 ✅, O: 6 = 3 + 1 + 2 = 6 ✅.

Yahi **CO₂ gas** ghol mein chhote-chhote bubbles banati hai aur cake ko phoolati hai. (Bakers aksar baking soda ke saath thoda dahi, nimbu ya baking powder bhi daalte hain. Iska "kyun" aage ki class mein, taaki cake ka swaad kadwa na ho.) Ye cake ke liye **bilkul safe aur aam** reaction hai.

**(b) Limestone aur chuna bhatti (lime kiln).** Limestone ya chuna-patthar ka formula CaCO₃ hai. Bahut tez garmi (lagbhag 900 °C) par ye toot jaata hai:

**CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂**

Jaanch: Ca: 1 = 1 ✅, C: 1 = 1 ✅, O: 3 = 1 + 2 = 3 ✅.

Aisi garmi ke liye bade **lime kiln** (chuna bhatti) bante hain. Kiln mein limestone ki kai tonne daali jaati hai, neeche se garmi di jaati hai, aur upar se CO₂ gas nikal jaati hai. Neeche bachta hai **quicklime (CaO)**. Ye ek **bahut bada, factory-level** kaam hai: student ke liye ye ghar ya lab ka experiment **nahi** hai.

### 2. Electrolytic decomposition: bijli se tootna

Paani (H₂O) ke bond itne mazboot hain ki sirf thodi si garmi usse nahi todti. Par agar usme **bijli** (electric current) guzaren, to wo toot jaata hai:

**2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂**

Jaanch: H: 4 = 4 ✅, O: 2 = 2 ✅.

**Sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo).** Teacher ek chhoti cell ya battery se do electrode paani mein dubate hain (shuddh paani mein bijli mushkil se chalti hai, isliye teacher usme ek suitable electrolyte milate hain). Dono electrodes par bubbles aate hain. Ek taraf **hydrogen**, aur doosri taraf **oxygen**, aur **hydrogen ka volume oxygen se do guna** hota hai, kyunki equation mein 2H₂ aur sirf 1 O₂ hai. (Ek hi garmi aur dabav par gas ka volume uske molecules ki ginti ke saath chalta hai; iska kaaran aage ki class mein.) Ye dikhata hai ki paani koi "tatva" nahi, ek compound hai (Chapter 2). Bijli kya hoti hai, uski kahani Physics ke chapter [Electricity](../../physics/electricity/electricity.md) mein hai. **Students ghar par ya mains se (wall socket) ye kabhi na karein**: sirf teacher ki battery wali setup.

### 3. Photolytic decomposition: roshni se tootna

Kuch compounds roshni se toot jaate hain. Sabse purana udaharan hai **silver bromide (AgBr)**, jo purani **black-and-white photographic film** mein roshni-sensitive parat hoti thi:

**2AgBr → 2Ag + Br₂**

Jaanch: Ag: 2 = 2 ✅, Br: 2 = 2 ✅.

Jab camera ka shutter khulta, to film par roshni padti, AgBr toot kar **bareek kaali silver ki kanikayein (Ag)** bana deta. Jahan roshni zyada thi wahan zyada kaala dag, jahan kam wahan kam. Isi se tasveer bani. **Ye sirf padhne ke liye**: silver ke compounds aur bromine students ke liye nahi hain.

### Chauthi cheez: catalyst se tootna (jhalak)

Kabhi-kabhi ek chhoti-si madadgaar cheez compound ko **tez** tod deti hai, par khud kharch nahi hoti. Isse **catalyst** kehte hain. Udaharan: hydrogen peroxide (H₂O₂, dawai ki dukaan ka 3% rangheen liquid):

**2H₂O₂ → 2H₂O + O₂**

Jaanch: H: 4 = 4 ✅, O: 4 = 2 + 2 = 4 ✅.

Thoda H₂O₂ dheere-dheere khud toot-ta rehta hai, aur roshni mein aur tez (isliye bottle bhoore rang ki hoti hai). Par thoda **yeast** ya **kachche aloo ka tukda** dalne par bubbles bahut tez uthte hain. Catalyst kaise kaam karte hain, ye hum Topic 4 mein dekhenge.

### Ghar par kar sakte ho: yeast ya aloo ke saath H₂O₂ (ghar par adult ke saath)

- **Kya chahiye:** ek chhota steel ya plastic ka katora, 3% hydrogen peroxide (dawai ki dukaan par milta hai), ek chutki yeast ya kachche aloo ka patla tukda, ek chammach.
- **Kaise:** **Adult** katore mein thoda sa H₂O₂ daalein. Aankhon ke liye **chashma** pahne. Ab usme yeast ya aloo ka tukda daalein. **Dekhna:** jhaag jaisa bubbles nikalte hain.
- **Kya nahi karna:** isse **piyo ya chakho nahi**, aankh ya muh ke paas na laao, band bottle mein na rakho (gas bhar jaati hai, bottle fat sakti hai). Kaam ke baad haath dho lo.

### Cake ka phoolna dekho (ghar par adult ke saath)

Ek adult ke saath, cake ke ghol ke do chhote katore banao: ek mein **ek chutki baking soda**, doosre mein **nahi**. Dono ko oven mein pakao (oven adult chalayega). **Dekhna:** kaun sa phoola hua, sponge jaisa; aur kaun sa **chipta** aur ghana. Ye dikhata hai ki baking soda ki gas ne kya kiya. **Kaccha ghol ya baking soda khud chakho nahi.**

### Thermal decomposition ki dikhne wali pehchaan (school lab mein teacher ke saath)

**School lab mein teacher ke saath.** Teacher ek sookhi, majboot test tube mein thoda baking soda lekar use halke se garam karte hain. Tube ke munh se ek patli nali (delivery tube) gas ko chuna-paani (lime water) tak le jaati hai, to gas ka raasta hamesha **khula** rehta hai. **Dhyan:** tube kabhi **band** nahi kiya jaata, kyunki band tube mein gas bharne se wo fat sakta hai. **Dekhna:** chuna-paani **doodhiya** ho jaata hai, jo batata hai ki gas **CO₂** hai. (Ye doodhiyapan CaCO₃ ke bareek kan hain: wahi reaction jo Part F mein whitewash ki deewar par hota hai.)

## Part D: Solved examples: decomposition

### Example 4. Limestone se kitna quicklime milta hai?

Masses: Ca = 40, C = 12, O = 16. CaCO₃ ka mass = 40 + 12 + 16 × 3 = **100**. CaO ka mass = 40 + 16 = **56**. CO₂ ka mass = 12 + 32 = **44**.

Jaanch: 100 = 56 + 44 ✅. To **100 kg** shuddh limestone se **56 kg** quicklime aur **44 kg** CO₂ gas. To **1 tonne** (1000 kg) shuddh limestone se **560 kg** quicklime banta hai. Jo mass "gayab" hua wo CO₂ ke roop mein hawa mein chala gaya, jaisa Chapter 4 mein dekha tha.

### Example 5. Baking soda ka hisaab

2NaHCO₃ → Na₂CO₃ + H₂O + CO₂. Masses: Na = 23, H = 1, C = 12, O = 16. NaHCO₃ = 23 + 1 + 12 + 48 = **84**. 2 × 84 = **168**.

Daayein: Na₂CO₃ = 46 + 12 + 48 = **106**. H₂O = **18**. CO₂ = **44**. Total = 106 + 18 + 44 = **168**.

Jaanch: 168 = 168 ✅. Ye mass bachne (Chapter 4) ka ek aur saboot hai.

### Example 6. Kaun sa flavour hai?

Neeche diye reactions mein flavour pehchano:
- (i) Oven mein 2NaHCO₃ → Na₂CO₃ + H₂O + CO₂: **garmi** se, thermal.
- (ii) Battery se paani ke electrode par 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂: **bijli** se, electrolytic.
- (iii) Film par 2AgBr → 2Ag + Br₂: **roshni** se, photolytic.
- (iv) Yeast ke saath 2H₂O₂ → 2H₂O + O₂: **catalyst** ki madad se.

Sab mein **ek hi reactant**, to sab decomposition hain. Sawal poochho: arrow ke baayein kitne formula? Agar **ek** hai, to decomposition ki sambhavna.

## Part E: Ek sikke ke do pehlu

Combination aur decomposition ek doosre ke **ulte** lagte hain. Aap ek hi reaction ko do dishaon mein dekho:

| Jodna (combination) | Todna (decomposition) |
|---|---|
| 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O (hydrogen jalna) | 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂ (bijli se) |
| CaO + CO₂ → CaCO₃ (nayi chattan ban-ti hai) | CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂ (kiln mein garmi se) |

Ek baat dhyan se: dono dishaon mein **atoms wahi hain**, bas unke saathi badal gaye. Iska matlab ye nahi ki ulta reaction **aise hi** shuru ho jaata hai. Har dishaa ke liye alag **shart** chahiye: ek ke liye 900 °C garmi, doosre ke liye hawa mein dheere-dheere. Aur energy ke hisaab se bhi dono ulte hain: jo reaction garmi bahar deta hai, uska ulta garmi leta hai. Ye hum Topic 4 mein dekhenge.

Pehchaan ka niyam yaad rakho:

- **A + B → AB**: arrow ke daayein **ek** formula = combination.
- **AB → A + B**: arrow ke baayein **ek** formula = decomposition.

## Part F: Lime ki poori kahani: bhatti se deewar tak

Ab bakery wale mazdoor ki balti ko poori kahani ke saath jodte hain. Chuna ek **ghoomta chakra** hai, jisme **decomposition aur combination dono** aate hain.

```
   LIMESTONE (CaCO₃)            "chuna-patthar", khan se
        │
        │  lime kiln mein garmi ~900 °C         (decomposition)
        │  CaCO₃ ──► CaO + CO₂↑
        ▼
   QUICKLIME (CaO)              safed patthar, kostik: sirf adult ke saath
        │
        │  paani milao, bahut garmi nikalti hai (combination)
        │  CaO + H₂O ──► Ca(OH)₂
        ▼
   SLAKED LIME (Ca(OH)₂)        whitewash ka ghol: deewar par lagaya jaata hai
        │
        │  deewar par sookhne ke baad dheere-dheere hawa ki CO₂ se
        │  Ca(OH)₂ + CO₂ ──► CaCO₃ + H₂O        (teesra reaction: do products, combination nahi)
        ▼
   CaCO₃ ki patli parat         deewar par safed chamak, wapas limestone jaisi
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Lime ki kahani: limestone (CaCO₃) garmi se toot kar quicklime (CaO) aur CO₂ deta hai. Quicklime paani se mil kar slaked lime (Ca(OH)₂) banata hai. Whitewash sookhne par hawa ki CO₂ lekar wapas CaCO₃ ki parat ban jaati hai. Is chakra mein ek decomposition, ek combination aur ek teesra reaction aaya.

Ye Diwali se pehle ghar ki deewarein safed karne ki kahani bhi hai: chuna lagao, sookhne do, aur kuch dinon mein deewar par ek safed, thodi chamakti parat ban jaati hai. Uski wajah **hawa ki CO₂** hai.

Last wale reaction ki jaanch: **Ca(OH)₂ + CO₂ → CaCO₃ + H₂O**. Ca: 1 = 1 ✅, C: 1 = 1 ✅, O: 2 + 2 = 4 aur 3 + 1 = 4 ✅, H: 2 = 2 ✅. (Is reaction mein product do hain, to ye strictly combination nahi hai. Isse bas "ek aur reaction" samjho; iske jaise reactions ka type tum aage seekhoge.)

## Part G: Balancing practice

Chapter 3 ke Topic 6 wala hit-and-trial yaad rakho: pehle **formula**, phir **coefficient**, aur aakhir mein har atom ki ginti.

### Example 7. Mg + O₂ → MgO

Baayein Mg = 1, O = 2. Daayein Mg = 1, O = 1. O barabar karne ke liye 2MgO: ab daayein Mg = 2, O = 2. Baayein Mg ko 2 karo: **2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO**.

Jaanch: Mg: 2 = 2 ✅, O: 2 = 2 ✅.

### Example 8. NaHCO₃ → Na₂CO₃ + H₂O + CO₂

Daayein Na = 2, to baayein 2NaHCO₃. Ab baayein Na = 2, H = 2, C = 2, O = 6. Daayein: Na = 2, H = 2, C = 1 + 1 = 2, O = 3 + 1 + 2 = 6. **2NaHCO₃ → Na₂CO₃ + H₂O + CO₂**.

Jaanch: Na: 2 = 2 ✅, H: 2 = 2 ✅, C: 2 = 2 ✅, O: 6 = 6 ✅.

### Example 9. H₂O₂ → H₂O + O₂

Baayein H = 2, O = 2. Daayein H = 2, O = 1 + 2 = 3. O ka hisaab bigda. O₂ aur H₂O₂ mein O ki ginti 2 ke gunak mein aati hai, to 2H₂O₂ lo: baayein H = 4, O = 4. Daayein 2H₂O (H = 4, O = 2) aur O₂ (O = 2): **2H₂O₂ → 2H₂O + O₂**.

Jaanch: H: 4 = 4 ✅, O: 4 = 2 + 2 = 4 ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Combination ko "do cheezein mili" tak hi samajhna**

C + O₂ → CO₂ aur CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O, dono mein do reactant hain. Par pehla combination hai (ek product), doosra nahi (do products). Reactants nahi, **product ki ginti** dekho.

**Galti 2: Decomposition ko "sirf garmi se tootna" samajhna**

Teen flavour hain: garmi, bijli, roshni. Baking soda aur limestone garmi se, paani bijli se, AgBr roshni se. Catalyst wala bhi hota hai.

**Galti 3: Chuna haath se chhoona**

Quicklime aur slaked lime kostik hote hain. Sirf adult dastaane aur chashme ke saath sambhalte hain. Tum bas door se samjho.

**Galti 4: Band tube ya bottle mein gas banne wala reaction karna**

CO₂ ya O₂ banne wale reaction band bottle ya tube mein **kabhi** na karo: andar dabav badhta hai aur wo fat sakta hai. Hamesha khula rakho.

**Galti 5: Mass "gayab" ho jaata hai**

Limestone garam karne par jo patthar bacha wo halka hai (100 se 56). Par CO₂ gas bahar chali gayi (44). Total hamesha same: 56 + 44 = 100.

---

## Quick Recap

- **Combination:** do ya zyada reactants mil kar **ek hi product**. A + B → AB. Udaharan: 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO, C + O₂ → CO₂, 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O, N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃, CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂.
- **Decomposition:** **ek reactant** toot kar do ya zyada simple products. AB → A + B.
- Decomposition ke flavour: **garmi** (CaCO₃, NaHCO₃), **bijli** (2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂), **roshni** (2AgBr → 2Ag + Br₂), aur **catalyst** (2H₂O₂ → 2H₂O + O₂).
- Dono ek doosre ke ulte hain, par ulta reaction apni alag shart (garmi, bijli) maangta hai.
- Lime ki kahani: CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂ (kiln), CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂ (slaking), phir deewar par CO₂ se wapas CaCO₃.
- Quicklime kostik hai: sirf adult ke saath; students nahi chhuenge. Gas banne wale reaction **band** container mein kabhi nahi.
- Teeno mein mass dono taraf barabar: 100 = 56 + 44, 168 = 106 + 18 + 44.

## Practice Questions

1. Define a combination reaction and a decomposition reaction in your own words. Give one example of each with a balanced equation.

2. Look at the equation CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O. Is it a combination reaction? Give a reason using the number of products.

3. Balance each equation and verify by counting atoms. Say whether each is combination or decomposition: (a) Ca + O₂ → CaO (b) KClO₃ → KCl + O₂ (c) Na + Cl₂ → NaCl (d) N₂ + H₂ → NH₃.

4. Why does a cake rise when baking soda is added to the batter and baked? Write the equation of the reaction and name the gas.

5. Name the three main ways to decompose a compound. Give one example for each, with its balanced equation, and say which one is used in old black-and-white photographic film.

6. Calculate how much quicklime and how much carbon dioxide you get by heating 500 kg of pure limestone (Ca = 40, C = 12, O = 16). Check that the masses add up.

7. When water is decomposed by electric current, 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂, how do the volumes of hydrogen and oxygen compare? Explain using the equation.

8. Write the story of lime in order: limestone, quicklime, slaked lime, and back to limestone. Which steps are decomposition and which are combination?

9. State True or False and correct the false ones:
   (a) In a combination reaction, the product is always one substance.
   (b) A decomposition reaction needs two reactants.
   (c) Quicklime is safe to touch with bare hands.
   (d) It is safe to heat baking soda in a tightly closed test tube.

10. Classify each of the following as combination or decomposition, and explain: (a) 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O (b) CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂ (c) CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂ (d) 2AgBr → 2Ag + Br₂ (e) C + O₂ → CO₂.

11. Two reactions look like opposites: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O and 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂. In what two ways are they the same, and in what way are they different?

### Answers

1. **Combination:** do ya zyada reactants mil kar **ek hi product** banate hain, jaise 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO (Mg: 2 = 2 ✅, O: 2 = 2 ✅). **Decomposition:** **ek reactant** toot kar do ya zyada products banata hai, jaise CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂ (Ca 1 = 1 ✅, C 1 = 1 ✅, O 3 = 3 ✅).

2. **Nahi.** Isme do products hain (CO₂ aur H₂O). Combination mein arrow ke daayein sirf **ek** formula hota hai. Ye combustion hai, jo Topic 6 mein aayega.

3. (a) **2Ca + O₂ → 2CaO** (combination): Ca 2 = 2 ✅, O 2 = 2 ✅. (b) **2KClO₃ → 2KCl + 3O₂** (decomposition): K 2 = 2 ✅, Cl 2 = 2 ✅, O 6 = 3 × 2 = 6 ✅. Yahan O pehle 3 aur 2 ke beech atka tha, isliye 6 tak le gaye. (c) **2Na + Cl₂ → 2NaCl** (combination): Na 2 = 2 ✅, Cl 2 = 2 ✅. (d) **N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃** (combination): N 2 = 2 ✅, H 6 = 6 ✅.

4. Baking soda garam oven mein toot-ta hai: **2NaHCO₃ → Na₂CO₃ + H₂O + CO₂**. **CO₂** gas ke bubbles ghol mein bante hain aur cake ko phoola dete hain. Ye thermal decomposition hai.

5. Garmi (thermal): **CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂**. Bijli (electrolytic): **2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂**. Roshni (photolytic): **2AgBr → 2Ag + Br₂**; ye black-and-white film mein hota tha. Teeno equations balanced hain: Ca/C/O 1,1,3 = 1,1,3 ✅; H/O 4,2 = 4,2 ✅; Ag/Br 2,2 = 2,2 ✅.

6. CaCO₃ = 100, CaO = 56, CO₂ = 44, to 100 : 56 : 44. 500 kg ke liye 5 guna: **quicklime = 5 × 56 = 280 kg**, **CO₂ = 5 × 44 = 220 kg**. Jaanch: 280 + 220 = 500 ✅.

7. **Hydrogen ka volume oxygen se do guna** hota hai, kyunki equation mein 2 molecule H₂ aur sirf 1 molecule O₂ hai (ek hi garmi aur dabav par gas ka volume molecules ki ginti ke saath chalta hai; iska kaaran aage ki class mein). Jaanch: H: 4 = 4 ✅, O: 2 = 2 ✅.

8. Limestone (CaCO₃) → garmi se quicklime (CaO) + CO₂: **decomposition**. Quicklime + paani → slaked lime (Ca(OH)₂): **combination**. Whitewash sookhne par hawa ki CO₂ se wapas CaCO₃: **Ca(OH)₂ + CO₂ → CaCO₃ + H₂O** (Ca 1 = 1 ✅, C 1 = 1 ✅, O 4 = 4 ✅, H 2 = 2 ✅). Ye do products wala reaction hai, to strictly combination nahi.

9. (a) **True** ✅. (b) **False** ❌. Decomposition mein **ek** reactant hota hai. (c) **False** ❌. Quicklime kostik hai, sirf adult dastaane aur chashme ke saath. (d) **False** ❌. Band tube mein gas bharne se wo fat sakta hai; tube khula rehna chahiye.

10. (a) **Combination** (do reactants, ek product). (b) **Decomposition** (ek reactant, do products). (c) **Combination** (do reactants, ek product). (d) **Decomposition** (ek reactant, do products). (e) **Combination** (do reactants, ek product).

11. Dono mein **wahi atoms** hain (H = 4, O = 2 dono taraf) aur dono mein bond todke naye jodte hain. Farq ye hai ki pehla **combination** hai (hydrogen jalna, garmi bahar nikalti hai), aur doosra **decomposition** hai jisme **bijli** deni padti hai.
