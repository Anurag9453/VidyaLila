# Topic 3: Displacement aur Double Displacement

Pichhle topic mein hamne reactions ke do type dekhe: **combination** (kai cheezein judkar ek) aur **decomposition** (ek cheez tootkar kai). Dono mein "jodna" ya "todna" hi tha. Aaj ke do type thode alag hain: yahan ek cheez doosri ki **jagah le leti hai**, ya do compounds apne **saathi badal lete hain**. Pehle ek lal-si kahani suno.

## Angoor ke bagh ka lal tank

Nashik ke paas Mr. Bhosale ka angoor ka bagh hai. Unki bhanji Aarya chhutti mein mama ke yahan aayi thi aur subah-subah bagh mein ghoom rahi thi. Mr. Bhosale ek tank wale sprayer se angoor ki bel par **neela-sa ghol** chhidak rahe the. Ye **tutiya** (blue vitriol, yaani **copper sulphate**, CuSO₄) ka ghol tha, jo bel ko phafoondi (fungus) ki bimari se bachata hai. Mr. Bhosale ne dastane (gloves) pehne the, aur unhone Aarya ko door rehne ko kaha: "Ye zeher hai, haath mat lagana, chakhna to bilkul nahi."

Aarya ne dekha ki sprayer ka loha ka tank andar se thoda **lal-bhoora** ho gaya hai, jabki tank pehle chamakdar grey tha. "Mama, ye zang hai kya?" Mr. Bhosale hanse: "Zang jaisa dikhta hai, par ye **tamba (copper)** hai! Is tank mein loha hai aur ghol mein tutiya hai. Loha tutiya ke ghol se **tamba nikaal deta hai** aur apni jagah wahan baith jaata hai. Isliye ab hum kai baar plastic ke tank use karte hain."

Aarya ko vishwas nahi hua. "Loha tamba kaise nikaal sakta hai? Dono dhatu hi to hain." Mr. Bhosale ne kaha, "Wahi to sawaal hai. Ghar chal, sab dikhata hoon." Aur sach mein, ghar ke angan mein unhone ek **chhoti si ladai** dikhayi: **kaun si dhatu kisko uski jagah se hata sakti hai.** Aaj hum yahi ladai samjhenge.

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Displacement reaction** | Zyada reactive dhatu, ek salt ke ghol mein se kam reactive dhatu ko hata kar uski jagah le leti hai | Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu |

## Part A: Displacement ka matlab: jagah le lena

Pehle ek rozmarra ki tulna. Class mein ek bench par Rahul baitha hai. Ek **bada aur taakatwar** bachcha aata hai aur bolta hai, "Utho, ye meri jagah hai." Rahul hat jaata hai. Ab bench par bada bachcha baitha hai aur Rahul alag. Lekin ek **chhota** bachcha aakar kisi bade bachche ko utha nahi sakta. Jagah wahi le sakta hai jo **zyada taakatwar** ho.

Dhatuon mein bhi yahi hota hai. Ek compound mein ek dhatu, ek non-metal ke saath judi hoti hai (jaise tutiya mein Cu, SO₄ ke saath). Agar koi **zyada reactive** dhatu aa jaye, to wo us jagah par baith jaati hai aur pehli dhatu akeli bachti hai. Is ko kehte hain **displacement**.

Aam shakal:

```
   A  +  B C   -->   A C  +  B
   (zyada reactive   (A ne B ki jagah
    dhatu)            le li, B bahar)
```

Dhyan do: reaction mein **ek hi cheez displace hoti hai**, aur ek element (A) ek compound (BC) se B ko bahar nikaal deta hai. Isliye isey **single displacement** bhi kehte hain.

## Part B: Loha aur tutiya: nail ki kahani

Mr. Bhosale ne ek chhoti si activity ki, aur tum bhi kar sakte ho (sirf adult ke saath, neeche ke safety rules ke saath).

> **Activity 3.1 (ghar par adult ke saath, ya school lab mein teacher ke saath).** Ek saaf **loha ki keel (nail)** ko halke sandpaper se ragad kar chamkao (adult ya teacher ki madad se). Adult ek kaanch ke glass mein thoda paani le kar usme **tutiya (copper sulphate) ke kuch kan** ghole, jisse neela ghol bane. Keel ko glass mein latka do (ya rakh do) aur **20 se 30 minute** dekho. Phir keel nikaal kar tissue par rakho.
>
> **Safety:** copper sulphate **sirf adult sambhale**. Kabhi chakhna nahi, muh ya aankh ke paas nahi le jaana. Kaam ke baad **haath sabun se dho lo**. Glass bachcho aur pet ki pahunch se door rakho; ghol ko baad mein adult hi theek se fenke. Jo cheezein is kaam mein lagi, unhe rasoi ke kaam mein dobara use mat karo.

Tum dekhoge:

1. Keel par **lal-bhoora parat** chadh jaati hai. Ye **tamba (Cu)** hai.
2. Ghol ka **gehra neela rang halka hota jaata hai**, aur der tak chhodne par **halka hara** (pale green) dikhta hai.

Ye dono baatein ek hi reaction se aati hain:

> **Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu**

Is equation ko Chapter 3 ke tareeke se parkho: Fe: 1 aur 1. Cu: 1 aur 1. S: 1 aur 1. O: 4 aur 4. Balance hai.

Matlab: **neela CuSO₄** ghol mein tha. Loha ne Cu ko hataya, aur wo keel par tamba banke baith gaya. Loha khud **FeSO₄** ban kar ghol mein chala gaya. **FeSO₄ ka ghol halka hara hota hai**, isliye neela rang ghat gaya aur hara rang aa gaya. Aarya ke mama ke sprayer ke tank mein bhi yahi hua, bas dheere-dheere kai mahino mein.

Ab particle-level par dekhte hain (Chapter 6 yaad karo ki ghol mein ions hote hain). Ghol mein tutiya Cu²⁺ aur SO₄²⁻ ke roop mein tairta hai. Keel ke andar Fe ke atom hain. Do copies lekar picture banate hain:

```
      PEHLE                                   BAAD MEIN

     keel (grey Fe)                       keel par lal-bhoora Cu ki parat
          ||                                   ||
     ___________                          ___________
    /   ||      \                        /   ##      \
   /    ||       \                      /    ##       \
  /  neela ghol   \                    /  halka hara   \
  \_______________/                    \_______________/
   Cu²⁺ ke karan neela                  Fe²⁺ ke karan halka hara

   Kan ginti (2 copies ka hisaab):

   PEHLE
     keel mein:  Fe  Fe
     ghol mein:  Cu²⁺  SO₄²⁻  Cu²⁺  SO₄²⁻

   BAAD
     keel par:   Cu  Cu
     ghol mein:  Fe²⁺  SO₄²⁻  Fe²⁺  SO₄²⁻
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Loha ki keel tutiya ke ghol mein, pehle aur baad. Do Fe atoms ne do Cu²⁺ ko hataya: keel par 2 Cu aa gaye, ghol mein 2 Fe²⁺ chale gaye. SO₄²⁻ ko koi farq nahi pada, wo bas "darshak" ki tarah raha. Dono taraf 2 Fe, 2 Cu aur 2 SO₄²⁻ hain, yaani kuch banta ya mitta nahi, sirf jagah badli. (Asal mein Fe aur Cu ke beech electrons ka lena-dena hota hai; uski jhalak Topic 5 mein.)

### Ek chhota mass ka hisaab

Atomic mass lo: Fe = 56 u, Cu = 63.5 u, S = 32 u, O = 16 u. Formula unit mass:

- CuSO₄ = 63.5 + 32 + 4 × 16 = **159.5 u**
- FeSO₄ = 56 + 32 + 4 × 16 = **152 u**

Reactants ka kul: 56 + 159.5 = **215.5**. Products ka kul: 152 + 63.5 = **215.5**. Barabar: Chapter 4 ka niyam (mass sthir rehta hai) yahan bhi sahi baitha.

Ek mazedaar baat: 56 g Fe ke jaane par 63.5 g Cu keel par chadhta hai. Yaani jitna loha nikla, usse **thoda zyada tamba** chadha. Par asli keel par parat dhili aur halki hoti hai aur ghol mein bhi bahut kuch rehta hai, isliye ghar par tarazu se ye farq bahut chhota aur nikalna mushkil hai.

**Aarya ka sawaal: ye hota kyun hai?** Kyunki **loha, tamba se zyada reactive hai.** Is "zyada reactive" ko samajhne ke liye ek ladder chahiye. Chaliye banate hain.

## Part C: Reactivity series: kaun kisko nikalta hai

Alag-alag dhatuon ko tutiya, namak, acid jaise cheezon ke saath milakar dekha gaya, aur dekha gaya ki kaun kisko hata sakti hai. In sab ke nateejon ko ek list mein likha gaya, jo **reactivity series** hai. Ise ek **seedhi (ladder)** ki tarah socho: upar wale taakatwar, neeche wale kamzor.

```
      SABSE ZYADA REACTIVE
              ^
         1.   K    Potassium    bahut tezi se reaction karta hai
         2.   Na   Sodium
         3.   Ca   Calcium
         4.   Mg   Magnesium
         5.   Al   Aluminium
         6.   Zn   Zinc
         7.   Fe   Iron (loha)
         8.   Pb   Lead
         9.   H    Hydrogen     (dhatu nahi, par tulna ke liye list mein)
        10.   Cu   Copper (tamba)
        11.   Hg   Mercury
        12.   Ag   Silver
        13.   Au   Gold         bahut kam reaction, isliye chamakta rehta hai
              v
      SABSE KAM REACTIVE
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Reactivity ladder (13 jagah): K se Au tak, zyada reactive se kam reactive ki taraf. Ladder mein jo upar hai wo neeche wale ko uske salt ke ghol mein se hata sakta hai. Hydrogen dhatu nahi hai, par acid ki baat karne ke liye ise ladder mein rakha jaata hai.

**Niyam ek hi line mein:**

> **Ladder mein upar wali dhatu, neeche wali dhatu ko uske salt ke ghol se bahar nikaal sakti hai. Neeche wali upar wali ko nahi nikaal sakti.**

Isi liye Fe (7) tutiya mein se Cu (10) ko nikaal deta hai, kyunki Fe, Cu se upar hai. Aur isi liye **Cu, ZnSO₄ ke ghol mein se Zn ko nahi nikaal sakta**, kyunki Cu, Zn se neeche hai. Ye ladder aise bhi dikhati hai ki sone ke gehne purane hone par bhi chamakte kyun hain: Au sabse neeche hai, hawa-paani se bhi kam hi reaction karta hai.

**Safety line:** ladder mein K, Na jaisi dhatuein paani ya hawa se bhi **bahut tezi se** reaction karti hain (aag tak lag sakti hai). Ye **sirf teacher ke liye** hain, aur wo bhi special tareeke se. Lead (Pb) aur mercury (Hg) **zeherile** hain, aur silver nitrate (AgNO₃) daag chhodta hai aur chamdi ko nuksan pahunchata hai. **In sab ke saath student koi activity nahi karte**; hum inka naam sirf padhne ke liye le rahe hain.

## Part D: Prediction: reaction hoga ya nahi?

Ladder ka sabse bada fayda: kagaz par hi pata lag jaata hai ki reaction hoga ya nahi. Bas dekho: **akeli dhatu ladder mein upar hai ya neeche, us dhatu se jo salt mein baithi hai.**

### Example 1. Zn + CuSO₄: hoga?

- Zn ladder mein **6**, Cu **10**. Zn upar hai.
- To Zn, Cu ko hata dega. **Haan, reaction hoga.**

```
   Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu
```

Jaanch: Zn 1=1, Cu 1=1, S 1=1, O 4=4 ✅. Zinc ki keel dalo to neela ghol halka hota jaata hai aur lal-bhoora tamba chadhta hai. **ZnSO₄ ka ghol rangheen** (colourless) hota hai.

### Example 2. Cu + ZnSO₄: hoga?

- Cu **10**, Zn **6**. Cu neeche hai.
- Neeche wali dhatu upar wali ko nahi hata sakti. **Nahi, koi reaction nahi.**

```
   Cu + ZnSO₄ → koi reaction nahi
```

Taambe ki taar ko ZnSO₄ ke ghol mein dalo to kuch nahi hoga. Yaani ye cheez ek taraf hi chalti hai: **Zn, Cu ko hataye, par Cu, Zn ko nahi.**

### Example 3. Fe + CuSO₄: hoga?

- Fe **7**, Cu **10**. Fe upar hai. **Haan, reaction hoga.** Ye wahi Part B wali keel hai.

### Example 4. Mg + FeSO₄: hoga?

- Mg **4**, Fe **7**. Mg upar. **Haan.**

```
   Mg + FeSO₄ → MgSO₄ + Fe
```

Jaanch: Mg 1=1, Fe 1=1, S 1=1, O 4=4 ✅.

### Example 5. Fe + ZnSO₄: hoga?

- Fe **7**, Zn **6**. Fe **neeche** hai, Zn upar. **Nahi.** Loha, zinc ko nahi hata sakta.

### Example 6. Ag + CuSO₄: hoga?

- Ag **12**, Cu **10**. Ag neeche. **Nahi.** (Isliye chandi ke chamche tutiya ke ghol se kharab nahi hote, par tutiya ko phir bhi bachcho se door rakho.)

### Example 7. Al + CuSO₄: hoga?

- Al **5**, Cu **10**. Al upar. **Haan, reaction ho sakta hai.**

```
   2Al + 3CuSO₄ → Al₂(SO₄)₃ + 3Cu
```

Jaanch: Al 2=2, Cu 3=3, S 3=3, O 12=12 ✅. (Aluminium par hawa mein ek patli oxide ki parat chadhi hoti hai, jo use shuru mein reaction se roak sakti hai; ye baat Topic 6 mein.)

Ek table mein sab ek jagah:

| Dhatu | Salt ka ghol | Dhatu ladder mein upar hai? | Reaction? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zn | CuSO₄ | Haan (6 versus 10) | Haan |
| Cu | ZnSO₄ | Nahi (10 versus 6) | Nahi |
| Fe | CuSO₄ | Haan (7 versus 10) | Haan |
| Mg | FeSO₄ | Haan (4 versus 7) | Haan |
| Fe | ZnSO₄ | Nahi (7 versus 6) | Nahi |
| Ag | CuSO₄ | Nahi (12 versus 10) | Nahi |
| Al | CuSO₄ | Haan (5 versus 10) | Haan |

## Part E: Displacement acid ke saath: hydrogen nikalna

Ladder mein **H** bhi hai. Acid (jaise HCl) mein hydrogen ek "partner" hai, aur H se upar wali dhatuein (jaise Zn, Fe, Mg) us hydrogen ko **hata sakti hain**: hydrogen gas ke bulbule nikalte hain.

```
   Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂
```

Jaanch: Zn 1=1, H 2=2, Cl 2=2 ✅.

> **Activity 3.2 (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo).** Teacher ek test tube mein bahut **patla (dilute) HCl** leta hai aur usme zinc ke chhote tukde daalta hai. Bulbule uthte dikhte hain. Ye hydrogen gas hai. Bulbule dekho, test tube ko aag se door rakho aur kabhi hydrogen ko jalane ki koshish mat karo. **Student ye khud nahi karte**: acid nuksan karta hai aur hydrogen aag pakad sakti hai.

Ab Cu ke saath: Cu ladder mein H se **neeche** hai. To Cu + HCl mein **koi reaction nahi** hota.

## Part F: Double displacement: saathi badalna

Ab dusra type. Displacement mein **ek akeli dhatu** ek compound mein se kisi ko hataati hai. Par kabhi **do compounds** milte hain, dono ke ions ghol mein tairte hain, aur wo **apne-apne saathi badal lete hain**. Ye hai **double displacement**: yahan **do** displacement ek saath ho jaate hain.

Topic 1 wali dance party yaad karo. Do jodiyan naach rahi hain: Jatin-Komal aur Sushant-Bhavya. Gaane ke beech dono jodiyan **saathi badal leti hain**: Jatin-Bhavya aur Sushant-Komal. Log wahi hain, bas jodiyan badal gayin.

Aam shakal:

```
   AB  +  CD   -->   AD  +  CB
   (A aur C ke saathi B aur D, ab swap ho gaye)
```

Pehli misaal: **calcium chloride aur washing soda**. Calcium chloride (CaCl₂) ek namak hai (jisse kuch jagah nami sokhne ke liye use karte hain), aur washing soda (Na₂CO₃) kapde dhone ka soda hai. Dono ko paani mein alag-alag ghola jaata hai, phir milaya jaata hai.

```
   CaCl₂(aq) + Na₂CO₃(aq) → CaCO₃(s)↓ + 2NaCl(aq)
```

Jaanch: Ca 1=1, Cl 2=2, Na 2=2, C 1=1, O 3=3 ✅.

```
   PEHLE:  Ca²⁺  Cl⁻  Cl⁻   +   Na⁺  Na⁺  CO₃²⁻
              \_______ saathi badle _______/
   BAAD:   Ca²⁺  CO₃²⁻  (CaCO₃, thos)   +   Na⁺  Cl⁻   +   Na⁺  Cl⁻  (2 NaCl, ghol mein)
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Double displacement mein partner swapping. Pehle Ca²⁺ ka saathi Cl⁻ tha, aur Na⁺ ka saathi CO₃²⁻ tha. Reaction ke baad Ca²⁺ ka saathi CO₃²⁻ ban gaya, aur Na⁺ ka saathi Cl⁻. Dono taraf 1 Ca²⁺, 2 Cl⁻, 2 Na⁺ aur 1 CO₃²⁻ hain: kuch gaya nahi, sab ne sirf jodi badli. Naya bana CaCO₃ paani mein nahi ghulta, wahi **precipitate** hai.

### Precipitate: jab ghol mein "dudhiya dhool" ban jaaye

Jab ye dono ghol mile, **dudhiya safed dhool** achanak ban gayi, jo ghol ko gadla kar deti hai aur kuch der baad neeche baith jaati hai. Ye **CaCO₃** hai (wahi jo chuna-patthar aur sangmarmar mein hota hai). Kyunki ye **paani mein nahi ghulta**, ghol se alag hokar thos ban jaata hai. Isi thos ko **precipitate** kehte hain, aur ise equation mein **(s)** ke saath aur kai baar neeche ki taraf tir **↓** se dikhaate hain.

Dusri taraf NaCl, paani mein ghula rehta hai: usko **(aq)** likhte hain.

**Dhyan do:** kaun sa compound ghulta hai aur kaun nahi, iska koi chhota sa niyam yahan nahi sikhayenge. Ye bas unhi examples se jaan lo jo diye gaye hain; aage ki class mein is ke niyam aayenge.

### Example 8. AgNO₃ aur NaCl: safed curdy precipitate

Silver nitrate (AgNO₃) aur namak (NaCl) ke ghol milane par **safed, dahi jaisa (curdy) precipitate** banta hai:

```
   AgNO₃(aq) + NaCl(aq) → AgCl(s)↓ + NaNO₃(aq)
```

Jaanch: Ag 1=1, N 1=1, O 3=3, Na 1=1, Cl 1=1 ✅. Saathi badle: Ag ka saathi NO₃ se Cl ban gaya; Na ka saathi Cl se NO₃ ban gaya. AgCl paani mein nahi ghulta, isliye precipitate.

> **Activity 3.3 (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo).** Teacher bahut patla silver nitrate ka ghol aur namak ka ghol milata hai. Student sirf door se dekhte hain. **Student ye khud nahi karte**: silver nitrate chamdi par kaale daag chhodta hai aur aankhon ke liye nuksandeh hai. Teacher dastane aur chashma pehente hain, aur bacha hua ghol sambhalkar fenka jaata hai.

Isi tarah ke ek aur mashhoor demo mein lead ka salt aur potassium iodide milate hain aur sone jaisa precipitate banta hai ("golden rain"). **Lead zeher hai**, isliye wo sirf teacher aur sirf lab mein; student nahi karte. Hum use sirf dekhne ke liye naam le rahe hain.

## Part G: Neutralisation: ye bhi double displacement hai

Ek aur wahi reaction, jo tumne rozmarra mein suna hoga: **acid aur base** ka milkar **salt aur paani** banana. Ise **neutralisation** kehte hain (acid aur base ki detail aage ki class mein; yahan bas itna jaano ki HCl ek acid hai aur NaOH ek base).

```
   HCl(aq) + NaOH(aq) → NaCl(aq) + H₂O(l)
```

Jaanch: H 2=2 (HCl ka 1 + NaOH ka 1 = 2; H₂O mein 2), Cl 1=1, Na 1=1, O 1=1 ✅.

Dekho ye bhi double displacement hai: **H** (jo HCl ka saathi tha) ne **OH** ko pakda, aur **Na** (jo OH ka saathi tha) ne **Cl** ko pakda. Saathi badle: HOH = H₂O aur NaCl. Is mein koi precipitate nahi banta, kyunki dono products ghol mein rehte hain, par **paani** ban jaata hai, isliye reaction "hota" hai. Isko ek baar yaad rakho:

> Double displacement tabhi sachmuch hota hai jab **kuch alag ban jaaye**: ek precipitate ya paani (jaise neutralisation mein). Agar sab ions ghol mein pehle jaise hi tairte rahein, to bas milawat hui, reaction nahi.

Isliye kagaz par saathi badalna to hamesha ho sakta hai, par asli duniya mein reaction tabhi dikhta hai jab ek naya thos ya paani ban jaye. (Gas bhi ban sakti hai, ye aage ki class mein.)

**Safety line:** ghar par acid aur base (jaise safai ke cleaners) ko **kabhi milana nahi**. Ye teacher ke saath lab ka kaam hai, aur uski detail aage ki class mein aayegi.

## Part H: Chaaron type: kaise pehchanein?

Ab hamare paas chaar type hain. Sirf equation dekh kar type pehchanne ka tareeka:

| Type | Aam shakal | Pehchaan ka sawaal | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Combination | A + B → AB | Kya **kai cheezein judkar ek** ban rahi hain? | 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O |
| Decomposition | AB → A + B | Kya **ek cheez tootkar kai** ban rahi hai? | CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂ |
| Displacement | A + BC → AC + B | Kya **ek akeli dhatu ne compound mein se doosri ko hataya**? | Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu |
| Double displacement | AB + CD → AD + CB | Kya **do compounds ne saathi badle**? | CaCl₂ + Na₂CO₃ → CaCO₃ + 2NaCl |

Tareeke ke teen sawaal:

1. **Left side par kitni cheezein hain, right par kitni?** 1 → 2 ya zyada: decomposition. 2 ya zyada → 1: combination.
2. Agar left par **ek element + ek compound** hai: displacement.
3. Agar left par **do compounds** hain aur right par bhi do: double displacement.

## Part I: Solved examples: type pehchano

### Example 9. Neeche ke reactions ka type batao

Pehle har equation ko balance dekh lo (dono taraf har atom gino), phir type pehchano.

| Reaction | Type | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO | Combination | 2 cheezein judkar 1 |
| CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂ | Decomposition | 1 cheez tootkar 2 |
| Zn + H₂SO₄ → ZnSO₄ + H₂ | Displacement | Zn ne H ko hataya |
| KOH + HNO₃ → KNO₃ + H₂O | Double displacement | K aur H ne saathi badle (neutralisation) |
| Mg + CuSO₄ → MgSO₄ + Cu | Displacement | Mg ne Cu ko hataya |
| 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂ | Decomposition | Bijli se paani tootta hai |
| N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃ | Combination | 2 cheezein judkar 1 |
| Fe + 2HCl → FeCl₂ + H₂ | Displacement | Fe ne H ko hataya |
| H₂SO₄ + 2NaOH → Na₂SO₄ + 2H₂O | Double displacement | H aur Na ne saathi badle (neutralisation) |
| 2NaHCO₃ → Na₂CO₃ + H₂O + CO₂ | Decomposition | 1 cheez tootkar 3 |
| Pb(NO₃)₂ + 2KI → PbI₂ + 2KNO₃ | Double displacement | Pb aur K ne saathi badle (golden rain wala, sirf teacher demo) |
| C + O₂ → CO₂ | Combination | 2 cheezein judkar 1 |

Jaanch: har equation mein dono taraf har atom ki ginti barabar hai, sab balance hain ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Reactivity ladder ko ulta padhna**

Upar wali dhatu **zyada** reactive hai. Isliye Zn (6) Cu (10) ko hata sakta hai, Cu Zn ko nahi. Ulta socha to Cu + ZnSO₄ ka reaction maan loge, jo hota hi nahi.

**Galti 2: Keel "tamba ban gayi" kehna**

Loha **tamba nahi ban jaata**. Loha ghol mein FeSO₄ ban kar chala jaata hai, aur **ghol ke Cu** keel par jamta hai. Dono alag dhatuein hain: koi dhatu doosri dhatu mein nahi badli, bas jagah badli.

**Galti 3: Neela ghol "kharab ho gaya" ya "pighal gaya" kehna**

Rang isliye gaya kyunki neela Cu²⁺ **kam** hua aur hara Fe²⁺ **aaya**. Kuch pighla nahi, sirf ions badle.

**Galti 4: Double displacement mein ions ke charge ya number ignore karna**

CaCl₂ + Na₂CO₃ mein **Ca²⁺ ke do Cl⁻** the, isliye right side par **2 NaCl** aata hai. Agar sirf NaCl likhoge to Na aur Cl ki ginti galat ho jaayegi.

**Galti 5: Har "saathi badalna" ko reaction maan lena**

Kagaz par saathi hamesha badle ja sakte hain, par reaction tabhi hota hai jab kuch **naya** bane: precipitate ya paani.

**Galti 6: Precipitate ko "dhuaan" ya "gas" kehna**

Precipitate **thos** hota hai, jo ghol mein tairta ya neeche baithta hai. Gas bulbule banata hai. Dono alag cheezein hain.

## Quick Recap

- **Displacement:** ek element ek compound ke ghol mein se doosre element ko hata kar uski jagah le leta hai. Zyada reactive dhatu kam reactive ko nikaalti hai.
- **Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu:** keel par lal-bhoora Cu aur neela ghol halka hara (pale green) ho jaata hai. Isi liye loha ke tank mein tutiya ka spray rakhne par tank par lal parat chadh jaati hai.
- **Reactivity series:** K, Na, Ca, Mg, Al, Zn, Fe, Pb, H, Cu, Hg, Ag, Au (zyada se kam). Upar wali neeche wali ko nikaalti hai, ulta nahi: Zn, Cu ko nikaalta hai; Cu, Zn ko nahi.
- H se upar wali dhatuein acid se **H₂ gas** nikaalti hain (Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂). Ye teacher ka kaam hai.
- **Double displacement:** do compounds ke ions saathi badalte hain: AB + CD → AD + CB.
- **Precipitate:** na ghulne wala thos, jise (s) aur ↓ se dikhate hain. CaCl₂ + Na₂CO₃ mein CaCO₃(s) banta hai, AgNO₃ + NaCl mein AgCl(s).
- **Neutralisation** (HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O) bhi double displacement hai.
- Chaar type: combination (kai → ek), decomposition (ek → kai), displacement (element + compound), double displacement (do compounds ke saathi badalte hain).
- Silver nitrate, lead, barium aur mercury ke saath student koi activity nahi karte; copper sulphate bhi sirf adult sambhalta hai.

### Answers

1. **Zyada reactive** dhatu, **kam reactive** dhatu ko uske salt ke ghol se hata kar uski jagah le leti hai. Ladder mein jo upar hai wo neeche wali ko nikaalti hai, ulta nahi.

2. **Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu.** Ginti: Fe 1=1, Cu 1=1, S 1=1, O 4=4 ✅. Do badlav: (i) keel par **lal-bhoora Cu** ki parat, kyunki Fe ne Cu ko hataya aur wo keel par jam gaya; (ii) neela ghol **halka hara** ho jaata hai, kyunki Cu²⁺ (neela) ghat gaya aur FeSO₄ (hara) ban gaya.

3. Tank loha ka hai aur ghol mein **tutiya (CuSO₄)** hai. Loha tamba se zyada reactive hai, to **Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu**. Tamba tank ki deewar par lal-bhoori parat ki tarah jam jaata hai. Ye zang nahi, tamba hai.

4. (a) **Haan.** Zn (6) Fe (7) se upar. **Zn + FeSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Fe** (Zn 1=1, Fe 1=1, S 1=1, O 4=4 ✅).
   (b) **Nahi.** Cu (10) Fe (7) se neeche.
   (c) **Haan.** Mg (4) Zn (6) se upar. **Mg + ZnSO₄ → MgSO₄ + Zn** (Mg 1=1, Zn 1=1, S 1=1, O 4=4 ✅).
   (d) **Nahi.** Fe (7) Mg (4) se neeche.
   (e) **Haan.** Mg (4) H (9) se upar, to hydrogen nikalta hai. **Mg + 2HCl → MgCl₂ + H₂** (Mg 1=1, H 2=2, Cl 2=2 ✅).
   (f) **Nahi.** Ag (12) H (9) se neeche.

5. Double displacement mein do compounds ke ions apne **saathi badal lete hain**. CaCl₂ + Na₂CO₃: Ca²⁺ ka saathi Cl⁻ tha, Na⁺ ka saathi CO₃²⁻; baad mein Ca²⁺ ne CO₃²⁻ ko pakda (CaCO₃) aur Na⁺ ne Cl⁻ ko (NaCl). **CaCl₂(aq) + Na₂CO₃(aq) → CaCO₃(s)↓ + 2NaCl(aq).** Ginti: Ca 1=1, Cl 2=2, Na 2=2, C 1=1, O 3=3 ✅.

6. **Precipitate** reaction mein bana wo **thos** padarth hai jo paani mein nahi ghulta aur ghol mein dhool ki tarah baithta hai. (a) **CaCO₃**: dudhiya **safed**. (b) **AgCl**: safed, dahi jaisa (**curdy**).

7. HCl mein partners H aur Cl, NaOH mein Na aur OH. Reaction ke baad **H ne OH ko pakda (H₂O)** aur **Na ne Cl ko pakda (NaCl)**. Saathi badle, to double displacement. **Precipitate nahi** banta: NaCl ghol mein rehta hai aur H₂O (liquid) banta hai.

8. Reactants: Fe + CuSO₄ = 56 + 159.5 = **215.5 u**. Products: FeSO₄ + Cu = 152 + 63.5 = **215.5 u**. Barabar ✅, mass sthir hai.

9. (a) Combination: 2 se 1; S 1=1, O 2=2 ✅. (b) Displacement: Mg ne acid ke H ko hataya; Mg 1=1, H 2=2, S 1=1, O 4=4 ✅. (c) Double displacement (neutralisation): Na 1=1, O 4=4, H 2=2, N 1=1 ✅. (d) Decomposition: 1 se 2; Cu 1=1, C 1=1, O 3=3 ✅. (e) Displacement: Al (5) ne Fe (7) ko hataya; Al 2=2, Fe 3=3, S 3=3, O 12=12 ✅. (f) Decomposition: Ag 4=4, O 2=2 ✅. (g) Combination: 2 se 1; S 2=2, O 6=6 ✅. (h) Double displacement: Cu ka saathi SO₄ se OH bana, Na ka saathi OH se SO₄; Cu 1=1, S 1=1, O 6=6, Na 2=2, H 2=2 ✅. (i) Displacement: Fe ne H ko hataya; Fe 1=1, H 2=2, S 1=1, O 4=4 ✅. (j) Double displacement (neutralisation): Ca 1=1, O 2=2, H 4=4, Cl 2=2 ✅.

10. (a) **False** ❌. Cu, Zn se neeche hai, isliye wo Zn ko nahi hata sakta. (b) **True** ✅. (c) **False** ❌. Precipitate **thos** hai, gas nahi. (d) **False** ❌. Neutralisation (HCl + NaOH) mein koi precipitate nahi banta, paani banta hai.
