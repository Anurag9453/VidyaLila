# Topic 4: Energy aur Raftaar: Exothermic, Endothermic aur Catalyst

Pichle topics mein humne dekha ki reaction kitne **type** ke hote hain: combination, decomposition, displacement aur double displacement. Ye sab sawaal ka jawab dete hain "**kya** bana?". Aaj do naye sawaal: "reaction ke dauran **garmi** kahan jaati ya aati hai?" aur "reaction **kitni jaldi** hota hai, aur use tez ya dheema kaise karein?" Ek thande pahad par hum iska jawab ek chhote se pouch se dhoondhenge.

## Pahad par garam pouch

December ki subah thi. Mr. Salvi apne bhanje Shlok ko Kalsubai ki chadhai par le gaye the. Hawa itni thandi thi ki Shlok ke dastane ke andar bhi ungliyan sunn ho rahi thi. "Mama, haath jam gaye!" wo bola.

Mr. Salvi ne bag se ek chhota, chapta plastic ka packet nikala, jis par likha tha "hand warmer: kholne ke baad hawa lagne dein". Unhone seal faadi, packet ko haath mein halke se masla aur Shlok ko pakda diya. Kuch hi minute mein packet **garam** hone laga, itna ki dastane ke andar haath ko aaram aa gaya. "Isme bijli to hai nahi, na aag, phir garmi kahan se aayi?" Shlok chaunka.

"Kholne se pehle ye pouch thanda tha, seal faadte hi garam hone laga," Mr. Salvi ne kaha. "Matlab seal ke andar jo kuch bhi tha, wo hawa lagte hi **reaction** karne laga, aur us reaction ne **garmi bahar nikali**. Aaj ka topic isi baat ka hai: kuch reactions garmi **dete** hain, kuch garmi **lete** hain. Aur ek aur sawaal: yahi reaction tez ya dheema kaise hota hai? Chalo shuru karte hain."

Pahle naye shabd, phir energy ki tasveer, phir pouch ka raaz, aur phir reaction ki **raftaar**.

## Part A: Reaction mein energy: aa rahi hai ya ja rahi hai?

Topic 1 mein humne dekha tha ki reaction mein purane bond **tootte** hain aur naye bond **bante** hain. Ek baat qualitative (bina number ke) yaad rakho:

- **Bond todne mein energy lagti hai** (jaise rassi ki ganth kholne mein mehnat).
- **Naya bond banne par energy wapas milti hai** (jaise do magnet jodne par "chat" ki awaaz aur halka sa jhatka).

Ab do hi cases ho sakte hain:

1. **Naye bond banne par zyada energy nikli, bond todne mein kam lagi.** Bachi hui energy garmi (aur kabhi roshni) ke roop mein bahar aati hai. Aas-paas **garam** ho jaata hai. Ye hai **exothermic reaction** ("exo" = bahar, "thermic" = garmi).
2. **Bond todne mein zyada energy lagi, naye bond ne kam wapas di.** Kami aas-paas se leni padti hai. Aas-paas **thanda** ho jaata hai, ya hume garmi lagatar deni padti hai. Ye hai **endothermic reaction** ("endo" = andar).

(Garmi aur temperature ka farak aur garmi ka behna Physics ke Heat and Temperature chapter mein tha: yahan bas itna lena hai ki garmi energy ka ek roop hai jo garam se thande ki taraf behti hai.)

Ek aasan upma: ek bhaari bag ko seedhi se neeche utaarna aasan hai, energy **nikalti** hai; neeche se upar le jaane mein mehnat (energy) **deni** padti hai. Exothermic "neeche utarna" hai, endothermic "upar chadhna".

### Energy-level sketch

Reactants aur products ke "energy level" ko ek ooncha-neecha star maan lo. Neeche exothermic ka sketch dekho.

```
Energy
  ^
  :   Reactants  ________
  :                      \  garmi bahar
  :                       \ nikli
  :                        v
  :                 Products  ________
  :
  +----------------------------------> reaction ka safar
```

Ab ulta: endothermic reaction mein products ka level reactants se **ooncha** hota hai. Dono sketch ek hi figure ke do hisse hain.

```
Energy
  ^
  :                 Products  ________
  :                         /
  :                        /  garmi andar
  :                       /   leni padi
  :   Reactants  ________/
  :
  +----------------------------------> reaction ka safar
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Energy-level sketches. **Pehla (exothermic):** reactants ka level **ooncha**, products **neecha**; farak ki energy garmi (kabhi roshni) ban ke bahar nikli, aas-paas garam hota hai. **Doosra (endothermic):** reactants **neeche**, products **ooncha**; ooncha chadhne ke liye energy bahar se leni padi (garmi, roshni ya bijli), isliye aas-paas thanda hota hai ya hume garmi dete rehna padta hai. Ye sirf sketch hain, isme koi number nahi.

## Part B: Exothermic reactions: garmi dene wale

Ye roz ki zindagi mein bahut hain. Har ek ke liye hum dekhenge ki garmi kahan mehsoos hoti hai.

| Reaction | Equation | Garmi kahan dikhti hai |
|---|---|---|
| Fuel ka jalna (methane / cooking gas) | CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O + garmi | Chulhe ki jwala aur garmi |
| Respiration (hamari cells mein) | C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + energy | Hamare sharir ki garmi (37 °C) aur kaam karne ki taakat |
| Chuna bujhana (slaking of lime) | CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂ + garmi | Paani garam ho jaata hai: **sirf bade (adult) hi karen** |
| Neutralisation | HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O + garmi | Dilute ghol thoda garam hota hai |
| Hand warmer mein loha | 4Fe + 3O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃ (saral roop) + garmi | Pouch garam hota hai |
| Khaad (compost) banna | Sade patte aur kachra, microbes ke kaaran | Compost ka dher andar se garam hota hai |

**Safety ke saath ek baat:** chuna bujhane wali reaction (CaO + H₂O) mein quicklime bahut **kostik (caustic)** hota hai aur paani garam ho jaata hai. Ye sirf teacher ya bade logon ka kaam hai. Student quicklime ko haath nahi lagate.

Respiration ke baare mein ek line: hamari cells mein glucose dheere-dheere oxygen ke saath jalta hai aur energy nikalti hai. Isse hamara sharir garam rehta hai. Isse "slow combustion" bhi kehte hain; Topic 6 mein detail hogi.

## Part C: Endothermic reactions: garmi lene wale

| Reaction | Equation | Energy kahan se aati hai |
|---|---|---|
| Photosynthesis | 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ | Sooraj ki roshni se |
| Chuna-patthar ko garam karna | CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂ | Bhatti (lime kiln) ki garmi se |
| Baking soda aur nimbu ka ras | 3NaHCO₃ + C₆H₈O₇ → Na₃C₆H₅O₇ + 3H₂O + 3CO₂ | Aas-paas ke paani aur ghol ki garmi se; ghol **thanda** lagta hai |
| Paani ko bijli se todna | 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂ | Bijli se (teacher demo) |

Photosynthesis ke bina ped glucose nahi bana sakte, aur wo bina roshni ke nahi hota: roshni ki energy glucose ke bond mein **jama** ho jaati hai. Jab hum respiration mein glucose ko jalate hain, to wahi energy wapas milti hai. Ye dono reactions ek doosre ke ulte hain: photosynthesis endothermic, respiration exothermic.

Is equation mein C₆H₈O₇ **citric acid** hai, jo nimbu mein hota hai. Ye wahi glass hai jo Topic 1 ki activity mein thoda thanda laga tha: CO₂ ke bubble bante hain aur ghol aas-paas se garmi leta hai. Cake mein baking soda alag tarah se (garmi se) CO₂ deta hai, jo Topic 2 mein tha.

### Ek imandaar baat: instant cold pack

Khilaadiyon ke liye jo "instant cold pack" hota hai (moch lagne par daba dete ho aur wo thanda ho jaata hai), usme aam taur par ammonium nitrate (NH₄NO₃) aur paani ek andar-andar ki thaili mein alag-alag hote hain. Dabane par thaili phat ti hai, ammonium nitrate paani mein ghulta hai, aur pack thanda ho jaata hai.

**Honest note:** NH₄NO₃ ka paani mein ghulna **chemical reaction nahi, ghulna (dissolving)** hai. Naya padarth nahi banta; sirf NH₄NO₃ ke ions paani mein alag ho jaate hain:

NH₄NO₃(s) → NH₄⁺(aq) + NO₃⁻(aq)

Par **garmi ka idea wahi hai**: ghulne ke dauran aas-paas se garmi **li** jaati hai, isliye pack endothermic ki tarah thanda hota hai. Isiliye cold pack ko aksar endothermic example ke saath gina jaata hai, magar hum ye farak yaad rakhte hain. (Aur cold pack ka andar ka powder kabhi nahi khaana, aur pack phat jaye to haath dho lo.)

## Part D: Reaction shuru karne ke liye "dhakka"

Ek sawaal: agar chulhe ki gas aur hawa ka oxygen kitchen mein pehle se mile hue hain, aur reaction exothermic hai, to gas apne aap kyun nahi jal jaati? Ek maachis ki teeli kyun lagti hai?

Kyunki zyaadatar reactions ko **shuru** hone ke liye ek chhote se "dhakke" ki zaroorat hoti hai: pehle kuch purane bond todne hi padte hain, aur uske liye thodi energy chahiye. Maachis ki teeli ek chhoti si chingari deti hai. Ek baar reaction chal padi, to jo garmi nikalti hai wo agle hisse ko shuru kar deti hai, aur jwala chalti rehti hai. (Isko "activation energy" kehte hain; uski calculation aage ki class mein.)

Dekho: exothermic reaction ka matlab ye nahi ki wo apne aap chalegi. Shuruaat ke dhakke ke baad hi wo chal padti hai.

## Part E: Pouch ka raaz

Ab Shlok ke hand warmer par wapas aate hain. Pouch ke andar **loha ka bahut baareek churan (iron powder)** hota hai, thoda namak, paani, aur kuch aisa padarth jo paani ko roke rakhta hai (jaise carbon ya vermiculite). Packet seal band hota hai, isliye hawa (oxygen) andar nahi jaati, aur loha **ruka rehta hai**.

Seal kholte hi hawa andar aati hai. Loha oxygen se milke iron oxide banata hai, yani **zang (rust)** lagne wali reaction, par bahut **tezi se**. Saral roop mein:

4Fe + 3O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃ + garmi

(Ye saral roop hai: asli zang mein paani bhi juda hota hai. Poori baat Topic 6 mein.) Loha ka churan baareek hai, isliye zyada surface hawa ko milta hai, aur namak aur paani reaction ko tez kar dete hain. Normal loha ka tukda mahino mein zang pakadta hai, par ye churan kuch hi ghanton mein. Garmi lagatar nikalti rehti hai, isliye pouch kai ghante (aksar 6 se 10) garam rehta hai.

```
   Seal band: Fe churan ruka hai
   +------------------------------+
   :  Fe  Fe  Fe  Fe  Fe  Fe      :   pouch thanda
   :  Fe  Fe  Fe  Fe  Fe  Fe      :
   +------------------------------+
                 :
            seal faadi
                 v
   Hawa (O₂) andar aayi
   +------------------------------+
   : 4Fe + 3O₂ --> 2Fe₂O₃ + garmi :   pouch garam
   :  O₂ -> Fe  Fe  Fe  Fe  Fe    :
   +------------------------------+
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Hand-warmer pouch. **Upar:** seal band hone par andar sirf loha ka churan (Fe) hai, oxygen nahi, isliye reaction ruka hai aur pouch thanda hai. **Neeche:** seal faadne par hawa ka oxygen andar aaya, loha ke churan se milke Fe₂O₃ (zang) banata hai aur garmi nikalti hai: ye exothermic reaction hai. (Sketch hai; asli pouch ke andar aur cheezein hoti hain.)

**Safety (hand warmer):**
- Ye **ek baar** ke liye hota hai. Loha khatam hone par ye thanda ho jaata hai, isko phir se garam nahi kar sakte.
- Seedhe skin par der tak mat rakho (jal sakta hai), kapde ke upar rakho. Pouch ko katna, phadna ya andar ka churan khaana mana hai.
- Pouch ko microwave ya aag par mat rakho.
- Use kachre mein fenk do, nadi ya naali mein nahi.

## Part F: Reaction ki raftaar (rate of reaction)

Ab doosra sawaal: reaction **kitni jaldi** hota hai. Kuch reactions bahut tez hain (patakhe ka phatna), kuch bahut dheeme (loha ka zang lagna, doodh ka dahi banna). Hum chaar cheezein badal ke raftaar badal sakte hain.

### 1. Temperature (garmi)

Garam karne par particles zyada tezi se chalte hain, aapas mein zyada baar aur zyada zor se takrate hain, isliye reaction **tez** hoti hai. Thanda karne par dheema. Thumb rule (sirf idea): temperature kuch badhane par reaction kaafi tez ho jaata hai.

### 2. Concentration (ghol kitna gaadha)

Zyada gaadha ghol ka matlab ek jagah mein zyada particles, to takkar ke mauke zyada. Zyada concentration, **tez** reaction.

### 3. Surface area (tukde kitne chhote)

Jab ek thos ka bada tukda doosre padarth se react karta hai, to sirf **bahar ki satah** reaction mein hissa leti hai, andar ka hissa nahi. Jab hum tukde ko **kuchal ke** ya **pees ke** chhote tukde kar dete hain, to zyada satah khul jaati hai. Isliye **chhote tukde, tez reaction**. Ek bada barf ka tukda der se pighalta hai, par barf ka churan jaldi (pighalna physical change hai, par satah wala idea wahi hai).

### 4. Catalyst

Catalyst wo padarth hai jo reaction ko **tez** karta hai par **khud khatam nahi** hota: reaction ke baad wo waisa ka waisa bacha rehta hai. Reaction ke liye ek "aasan raasta" bana deta hai.

Hydrogen peroxide (H₂O₂) ka udaharan: ye apne aap bahut dheere-dheere ghat-ta hai:

2H₂O₂ → 2H₂O + O₂

Par jab isme **MnO₂** (lab mein), ya thodi **yeast**, ya kaccha **aloo** ka tukda milate hain, to turant oxygen ke bubble ban jaate hain. Catalyst reaction ke baad bhi bacha rehta hai.

**Ghar par adult ke saath** (Topic 2 wali activity, ab raftaar ki nazar se): 3% hydrogen peroxide (chemist ki dukaan ka antiseptic), ek chhoti si katori mein thodi si yeast ya aloo ka patla tukda dalo. Aankhon ko bachao (chashma ya dhyan se door raho), kuch chakhna nahi, aur **band bottle mein nahi** karna (gas dabaav bana sakti hai). Bubble dekho: ye oxygen hai.

**Enzymes:** hamare sharir mein aur har sajeev cheez mein catalysts hote hain jinhe enzymes kehte hain. Jaise muh ka amylase jo roti ke starch ko meethi cheez mein badalta hai (roti ko der chabane par meethi lagne lagti hai). Enzymes ko "prakriti ke catalyst" kaho.

### Safe activity: effervescent tablet (ghar par adult ke saath)

Ye activity dekhne ke liye ki surface area aur temperature raftaar kaise badalte hain.

**Pehle safety (zaroor padho):**

- **Adult paas mein** rahe. Garam paani adult hi daale.
- **Kuch bhi chakhna ya peena nahi.** Tablet ka ghol bhi nahi peena.
- **Dono glass mein paani ki quantity bilkul barabar** (jaise 100 mL) honi chahiye, warna test sahi nahi hoga.
- Sirf khula glass lo, **band bottle nahi** (gas dabaav bana sakti hai).
- Tablet adult hi kuchle (saaf zip pouch mein chammach se); kuchla powder suunghna ya muh ke paas lana nahi.
- Kaam ke baad haath dho lo, bacha hua ghol sink mein bahao.

**Samaan:** 3 ya 4 saaf kaanch ke glass, ek barabar naap wala katori ya naapne ka cup, ek ghadi ya phone ka timer, effervescent tablets (jaise vitamin C ya antacid tablet, adult ki dekhrekh mein), ek chammach.

**Activity 1: poora tablet versus kuchla hua tablet (surface area):**

1. Dono glass mein **barabar** kamre ke temperature wala paani lo (jaise 100 mL).
2. Ek mein **poora tablet** daalo, doosre mein **kuchla hua (pissa hua) tablet** (do ek jaise tablet lo; ek ko adult ne zip pouch mein chammach se kuchal ke pees diya). Dono ek saath daalo.
3. Timer chalu karo aur dekho ki bubble kab tak chalte hain aur tablet kab poori tarah gayab hota hai.

**Typical (expected) result:** kuchla hua tablet **jaldi** khatam hota hai; poora tablet der se. (Natije tablet par nirbhar karte hain; samay alag aa sakta hai par dishaa wahi rahegi.)

**Activity 2: thanda versus garam paani (temperature):**

1. Ek glass mein **thanda** paani (frij wala), doosre mein **halka garam** paani (adult ne daala, bilkul ubalta hua nahi, sirf itna ki haath se chhoo sako). Dono mein **paani ki matra barabar**.
2. Dono mein **ek jaise poore tablet** ek saath daalo, aur timer se dekho.

**Typical result:** garam paani mein bubble tezi se aate hain aur tablet jaldi khatam hota hai.

```
 Poora tablet:
  samay -->  0s      20s     40s     60s     80s
            [#####][#####][#####][####]    khatam
            bubble dheere dheere, der se khatam

 Kuchla hua tablet:
  samay -->  0s      20s     40s
            [#####][#####]  khatam
            bubble bahut tez, jaldi khatam
```

> **Figure 4.3** – Poora tablet versus kuchla hua tablet (ek sketch, asli samay tablet ke hisaab se alag ho sakta hai). Dono glass mein paani ki matra aur temperature barabar. Kuchle hue tablet ki surface area zyada hai, isliye wo jaldi khatam hota hai. Dono mein ek hi reaction hai, sirf raftaar alag.

Is tablet mein bhi wahi baat hoti hai jo baking soda aur citric acid mein: ek acid aur ek carbonate paani mein milte hain aur CO₂ ke bubble nikalte hain. Tablet jab tak sookha hota hai, dono padarth paas-paas hote hue bhi reaction nahi karte; paani mein ghulte hi particles mil jaate hain aur fizz shuru ho jaata hai.

## Part G: Raftaar ke reasoning examples

**Sabzi chhote tukdon mein kyun kaatte hain?**
Chhote tukde ki surface area zyada hoti hai, garmi aur masale ke paani sabzi ke andar tak jaldi pahunchte hain, aur sabzi jaldi pakti hai. (Ye wahi surface area wala idea hai.)

**Doodh ko frij mein kyun rakhte hain?**
Doodh mein bacteria aur reactions chalte rehte hain jo use khatta (kharab) karte hain. Thanda temperature in reactions ko **dheema** kar deta hai, isliye doodh der tak chalta hai. Garmi mein doodh jaldi phatta hai.

**Yeast aate ko kyun phulata hai?**
Yeast ke andar enzymes (catalysts) hote hain jo aate ki cheeni ko tod ke CO₂ gas banate hain. Ye gas aate mein phans ke bubble banati hai, aur aata phoolta hai. Garam jagah par ye reaction tez hota hai, isliye aata garam kamre mein jaldi phoolta hai, thande mein der se.

**Pressure cooker mein daal jaldi kyun galti hai?**
Andar ka temperature 100 °C se zyada ho jaata hai, aur zyada temperature se pakne ki reactions tez ho jaati hain. (Pressure ka detail aage ki class mein.)

## Part H: Solved examples

### Example 1. Exothermic ya endothermic?

Neeche ke reactions ko do group mein baanto: (a) cooking gas ka jalna, (b) photosynthesis, (c) CaCO₃ ko garam karna, (d) hand warmer, (e) respiration, (f) CaO + H₂O.

- Garmi dene wale: (a), (d), (e), (f).
- Garmi (ya roshni) lene wale: (b), (c).

**Jawab:** Exothermic: a, d, e, f. Endothermic: b, c.

Jaanch: Photosynthesis mein roshni ki energy chahiye, CaCO₃ ko bhatti ki garmi chahiye: dono lene wale ✅

### Example 2. Energy-level se pehchano

Ek reaction mein products ka level reactants se **neecha** hai. Ye kaun sa type hai? Aas-paas ka temperature kya hoga?

**Jawab:** Exothermic. Energy bahar nikli, isliye aas-paas garam hoga.

Jaanch: Figure 4.1 mein exothermic sketch mein reactants ooncha, products neecha tha ✅

### Example 3. Atoms gino: hand warmer ki equation

4Fe + 3O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃ balanced hai ya nahi?

- Left: Fe = 4, O = 3 × 2 = 6.
- Right: Fe = 2 × 2 = 4, O = 2 × 3 = 6.

**Jawab:** Balanced hai.

Jaanch: Dono taraf Fe = 4 aur O = 6 ✅

### Example 4. Respiration ki equation balanced hai?

C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O

- Left: C = 6, H = 12, O = 6 + 12 = 18.
- Right: C = 6, H = 6 × 2 = 12, O = 6 × 2 + 6 = 18.

**Jawab:** Balanced hai.

Jaanch: C 6 = 6, H 12 = 12, O 18 = 18 ✅

### Example 5. Photosynthesis respiration ka ulta?

6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

- Left: C = 6, H = 12, O = 12 + 6 = 18.
- Right: C = 6, H = 12, O = 6 + 12 = 18.

**Jawab:** Balanced hai, aur ye respiration ka ulta hai: ek mein energy nikalti hai (exothermic), doosre mein roshni se energy andar jaati hai (endothermic).

Jaanch: Dono equations mein sirf taraf badli hai ✅

### Example 6. Poora tablet versus kuchla hua

Maan lo poora tablet ghulne mein 90 second leta hai aur kuchla hua tablet 30 second. Kuchla hua tablet kitne guna tez hai?

- 90 ÷ 30 = 3.

**Jawab:** Lagbhag 3 guna tez (samay jitna kam, raftaar utni zyada).

Jaanch: 30 × 3 = 90 ✅

### Example 7. Garam versus thanda paani

Maan lo thande paani mein tablet 80 second mein ghula aur garam paani mein 20 second mein. Raftaar mein kitna farak hai?

- 80 ÷ 20 = 4.

**Jawab:** Garam paani mein lagbhag 4 guna tez.

Jaanch: 20 × 4 = 80 ✅ (Ye sirf ek udaharan ke number hain; asli samay tablet par nirbhar karta hai.)

### Example 8. Catalyst ki pehchaan

H₂O₂ mein yeast daalne se oxygen ke bubble bane. Reaction ke baad yeast ka kya hua?

**Jawab:** Yeast (catalyst) khatam nahi hui; wo bachi rahti hai. Usne sirf reaction tez ki.

Jaanch: Equation 2H₂O₂ → 2H₂O + O₂ mein yeast likha hi nahi, kyunki wo na reactant hai na product ✅

### Example 9. Equation verify: H₂O₂

2H₂O₂ → 2H₂O + O₂

- Left: H = 4, O = 4.
- Right: H = 4, O = 2 + 2 = 4.

**Jawab:** Balanced hai.

Jaanch: H 4 = 4, O 4 = 4 ✅

### Example 10. Kaun tez: chhota tukda ya bada?

Chuna-patthar (CaCO₃) ka ek bada tukda aur utna hi wazan ka chuna-patthar ka powder, dono ko ek hi acid mein daalo. Kaun tez react karega aur kyun?

**Jawab:** Powder. Uski surface area zyada hai, isliye acid ko zyada jagah milti hai.

Jaanch: Chhote tukde, zyada satah, tez reaction ✅

### Example 11. Bhatti ko garmi kyun deni padti hai?

CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂ ke liye bhatti ko lagatar garam kyun rakhte hain?

- Ye endothermic reaction hai: bond todne mein zyada energy lagti hai aur wapas kam milti hai.
- Garmi band hui to reaction ruk jayegi.

**Jawab:** Kyunki reaction garmi **leti** hai, use garmi lagatar milni chahiye.

Jaanch: CaCO₃ ko sirf haath mein rakhne se kuch nahi hota ✅

### Example 12. Cold pack versus reaction

Cold pack ke thanda hone ko kya hum chemical reaction keh sakte hain?

**Jawab:** Nahi. NH₄NO₃ ka paani mein ghulna dissolving hai, naya padarth nahi banta. Par garmi lene ka idea endothermic jaisa hi hai.

Jaanch: NH₄NO₃(s) → NH₄⁺(aq) + NO₃⁻(aq) mein sirf ion alag hue, koi naya padarth nahi ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Exothermic reaction apne aap shuru ho jaati hai"**

Nahi. Zyaadatar ko shuru karne ke liye ek chhote dhakke (chingari, garmi) ki zaroorat hoti hai. Shuru hone ke baad wo khud garmi deti rehti hai.

**Galti 2: "Endothermic ka matlab hamesha thanda hona"**

Aas-paas se garmi lene par thanda lagta hai, par kai endothermic reactions (jaise CaCO₃ ka bhatti mein) ko hum **garmi dete** hain, wahan kuch bhi thanda nahi lagta. Matlab hai energy lene wali.

**Galti 3: "Catalyst reaction mein khatam ho jaata hai"**

Nahi. Catalyst reaction ko tez karta hai par khud bacha rehta hai.

**Galti 4: "Cold pack ek chemical reaction hai"**

Ghulna chemical reaction nahi hai (naya padarth nahi banta). Garmi ka idea wahi hai, par dono alag hain.

**Galti 5: "Zyada garm karne se hamesha reaction tez hoti hai, to ubalte paani mein tablet daalo"**

Safety ka sawaal. Activity mein sirf halka garam paani, jise adult ne diya, aur sirf khule glass mein.

**Galti 6: "Jo reaction tez hota hai, wo zyada garmi bhi deta hai"**

Nahi. Raftaar aur garmi do alag baatein hain. Loha ka zang dheere lagta hai par garmi deta hai; maachis ki jwala tez hai. Ek reaction tez ho kar bhi kam garmi de sakta hai.

## Quick Recap

- Reaction mein bond todne mein energy lagti hai aur naye bond banne par energy wapas milti hai. Jo zyada, uske hisaab se **exothermic** ya **endothermic**.
- **Exothermic:** garmi bahar (fuel ka jalna, respiration, CaO + H₂O, neutralisation, hand warmer mein loha, compost). Energy-level sketch mein products neeche.
- **Endothermic:** garmi andar (photosynthesis, CaCO₃ ko garam karna, baking soda aur citric acid, paani ka electrolysis). Sketch mein products ooncha.
- Cold pack ka NH₄NO₃ paani mein ghulna chemical reaction nahi, dissolving hai; par garmi lene ka idea wahi hai.
- Zyaadatar reactions ko shuru karne ke liye ek chhote **dhakke** ki zaroorat hoti hai (maachis ki teeli).
- **Hand warmer:** baareek loha ka churan + hawa (oxygen) → zang jaisi tez reaction, garmi nikalti hai: 4Fe + 3O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃. Ek baar ke liye, skin par der tak nahi.
- **Raftaar badhti hai:** zyada temperature, zyada concentration, chhote tukde (zyada surface area), aur catalyst se.
- **Catalyst** reaction tez karta hai par khud khatam nahi hota (H₂O₂ mein MnO₂, yeast, aloo). **Enzymes** sajeev cheezon ke catalyst hain.
- Activities: effervescent tablet, adult paas mein, kuch chakhna nahi, dono glass mein paani ki matra aur (agar surface area dekh rahe ho) temperature barabar.

## Practice Questions

14. State True or False and correct the false ones:
    (a) A catalyst is used up in the reaction.
    (b) Photosynthesis is an endothermic reaction.

### Answers

1. **Exothermic:** wo reaction jisme garmi bahar nikalti hai; udaharan: fuel ka jalna, respiration, CaO + H₂O (chuna bujhana), hand warmer. **Endothermic:** wo reaction jisme garmi (energy) andar leni padti hai; udaharan: photosynthesis, CaCO₃ ko garam karna, paani ka electrolysis.

2. Reaction mein purane bond todne ke liye energy lagti hai aur naye bond banne par energy wapas milti hai. Agar naye bond ne zyada energy wapas di, to bachi hui energy garmi ban ke bahar aati hai: exothermic. Agar bond todne mein zyada lagi aur wapas kam mili, to kami bahar se leni padti hai: endothermic.

3. **Exothermic:** reactants ka level **ooncha**, products ka level **neecha**, farak garmi ban ke bahar. **Endothermic:** reactants **neeche**, products **ooncha**, farak ki energy bahar se leni padi (Figure 4.1).

4. Seal band hone par pouch mein oxygen nahi jaata, isliye loha ka baareek churan react nahi karta. Seal kholne par hawa ka oxygen andar aata hai aur loha ke saath zang jaisi tez reaction hoti hai, jo garmi deti hai. Andar **iron (loha) ka churan** hota hai. Saral equation: 4Fe + 3O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃. Ise "saral" isliye kehte hain ki asli rust mein paani bhi judta hai.

5. Ammonium nitrate ke paani mein ghulne par koi naya padarth nahi banta; wahi ion sirf paani mein alag ho jaate hain, isliye ye dissolving hai, reaction nahi. Par ghulte waqt wo aas-paas se garmi leta hai, isliye pack thanda hota hai: garmi lene ka idea endothermic jaisa hi hai.

6. Zyaadatar reactions ko shuru karne ke liye ek chhote dhakke (energy) ki zaroorat hoti hai, taaki pehle kuch purane bond tootein. Maachis ki chingari wo dhakka deti hai. Ek baar jalne par nikli hui garmi reaction ko aage chalati rehti hai.

7. (i) Temperature badhao: garam paani mein tablet jaldi ghulta hai. (ii) Concentration badhao: gaadha ghol zyada tez react karta hai. (iii) Chhote tukde (zyada surface area): chhoti kaati hui sabzi jaldi pakti hai. (iv) Catalyst: H₂O₂ mein yeast ya aloo se oxygen jaldi nikalti hai.

8. Kuchla hua (crushed) tablet jaldi ghulta hai, kyunki uski surface area zyada hai. Paani ki matra barabar isliye chahiye ki agar paani alag hota, to farak surface area se aaya ya paani ki matra se, ye pata nahi chalta. Ek hi cheez badalni chahiye, baaki sab barabar.

9. Safety: (i) adult paas mein ho, (ii) kuch chakhna ya peena nahi, (iii) dono glass mein paani ki matra barabar, (iv) garam paani sirf adult daale aur ubalta hua nahi, (v) kaam ke baad haath dho lo. Band bottle isliye nahi kyunki CO₂ gas ka dabaav bottle ko phaad ya uda sakta hai.

10. 150 ÷ 30 = 5, yani crushed tablet **5 guna** tez hai. Jaanch: 30 × 5 = 150 ✅.

11. Catalyst wo padarth hai jo reaction ko tez karta hai par khud khatam nahi hota. H₂O₂ ke liye MnO₂, yeast ya kaccha aloo catalyst ban sakte hain. Catalyst used up nahi hota, reaction ke baad bacha rehta hai.

12. (a) Chhote tukde ki surface area zyada hoti hai, garmi aur paani andar tak jaldi pahunchte hain, to jaldi pakti hai. (b) Frij ka thanda temperature kharab karne wale reactions ko dheema kar deta hai. (c) Garm jagah par yeast ke enzymes tez kaam karte hain aur CO₂ jaldi banti hai, isliye aata jaldi phoolta hai.

13. Left: C = 6, H = 12, O = 6 + 12 = 18. Right: C = 6, H = 12, O = 12 + 6 = 18. Dono taraf barabar, to balanced hai. Respiration **exothermic** hai: isme energy nikalti hai jisse hamara sharir garam rehta hai.

14. (a) **False** ❌. Catalyst khatam nahi hota; wo reaction ke baad bacha rehta hai. (b) **True** ✅. Photosynthesis roshni ki energy leti hai. (c) **False** ❌. Zyaadatar ko shuru karne ke liye ek chhote dhakke (jaise chingari) ki zaroorat hoti hai. (d) **False** ❌. Hand warmer ek baar ke liye hota hai; loha khatam hone par dobara garam nahi hota.
