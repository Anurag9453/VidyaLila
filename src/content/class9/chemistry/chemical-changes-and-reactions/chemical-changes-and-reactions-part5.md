# Topic 5: Oxidation aur Reduction

Pichhle topic mein hum ne dekha ki reactions **garmi** dete ya lete hain, aur kuch reactions **tez** hote hain, kuch **dheere**. Hand-warmer ke pouch mein iron hawa ke oxygen se mil raha tha. Ab ek sawaal: **oxygen** to bahut reactions mein aata hai (jalna, zang, saans), aur kabhi kabhi **hydrogen** bhi aata ya jaata hai. Kya in sab reactions mein ek **common kahani** hai? Haan: kisi ko oxygen **milta** hai aur kisi se oxygen **chhin jaata** hai. Aaj usi lena-dena ka naam seekhenge: **oxidation** aur **reduction**.

---

## Kati hui seb ki kahani

Mr. Purohit ne apni student Vedika ko do plate di. Dono mein ek-ek seb ki kati hui phaank thi. Ek phaank par unhone thoda **nimbu ka ras** laga diya tha, doosri par kuch nahi. Phir bole, "Das minute baad dekhna."

Das minute baad: bina ras wali phaank **bhuri (brown)** ho chuki thi, aur nimbu wali phaank **halki aur safed** hi thi.

Vedika: "Sir, seb ko hua kya? Kati hui jagah par hi kyun bhura hua?"

Mr. Purohit: "Kyunki wahan seb ka andar ka hissa pehli baar **hawa ke oxygen** ke saamne aaya. Seb ke andar ke kuch padarth oxygen ke saath dheere dheere react karte hain aur bhure rang ke naye padarth bante hain. Chhilke wali jagah par oxygen pahunch nahi pata tha."

Vedika: "Aur nimbu?"

Mr. Purohit: "Nimbu mein **vitamin C** hota hai. Wo seb ke padarth se **pehle khud oxygen le leta hai**, jaise line mein aage khada bachcha jo pehle samosa utha le. Jab tak vitamin C bacha hai, seb ke asli padarth ko oxygen nahi milta aur phaank halki-safed rehti hai."

Ye hai **oxidation** ki ek rozmarra misaal: **kisi cheez ko oxygen milna**. **Activity (ghar par adult ke saath, kaatne ka kaam adult karega):** seb ki do phaank lo, ek par nimbu ka ras lagao, doosri ko aise hi rehne do, aur das minute baad dekho kaun bhura hua.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Oxidation** | Kisi padarth ko oxygen milna, ya uska hydrogen nikal jaana | Mg ka MgO banna |
| **Reduction** | Kisi padarth ka oxygen nikal jaana, ya use hydrogen milna | CuO ka Cu banna |
| **Redox reaction** | Wo reaction jisme oxidation aur reduction **saath saath** hote hain | CuO + H₂ → Cu + H₂O |
| **Oxidising agent** | Wo padarth jo doosre ko oxidise kare (oxygen de ya hydrogen le le) | CuO |
| **Reducing agent** | Wo padarth jo doosre ko reduce kare (oxygen le le ya hydrogen de) | H₂ |
| **Oxide** | Kisi element ka oxygen ke saath bana compound | CuO, MgO, Fe₂O₃ |

## Part A: Do paribhashayein (oxygen aur hydrogen)

Is class mein hum **do hi paribhashayein** kaam mein lenge. Ye aasaan hain, bas **lena aur dena** ginna hai.

| | Oxygen ke hisaab se | Hydrogen ke hisaab se |
|---|---|---|
| **Oxidation** | oxygen **milna** | hydrogen **nikalna** |
| **Reduction** | oxygen **nikalna** | hydrogen **milna** |

Yaad rakhne ka tareeka: **"Oxygen mila = Oxidation. Oxygen gaya = Reduction. Hydrogen ka matlab ulta."**

```
   OXYGEN
            +O                          -O
   padarth ---> oxide            oxide ---> padarth
   (oxidation)                   (reduction)

   HYDROGEN
            -H                            +H
   padarth ---> (hydrogen kam)   padarth ---> (hydrogen zyada)
   (oxidation)                   (reduction)
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Oxygen ya hydrogen ke lene-dene ka nakshaa. Oxygen milna aur hydrogen khona **dono oxidation** hain; oxygen khona aur hydrogen milna **dono reduction** hain.

---

## Part B: Ek reaction, do kirdaar

Sabse pehle ek saaf-suthra reaction: **kaala copper(II) oxide** garam karke **hydrogen gas** ke saath. Kaala powder laal-bhure copper mein badal jaata hai aur paani ki boondein bhi banti hain. (**Sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo).** Hydrogen jalne wali gas hai, to students ise nahi karte.)

CuO + H₂ → Cu + H₂O

Ye equation pehle se balanced hai (Chapter 3 ki tarah check karo: 1 Cu, 1 O, 2 H dono taraf). Ab dekho **kaun kiska kya le raha hai**:

- **CuO** ne apna **oxygen khoya** (CuO se Cu bana). Oxygen gaya = **reduction**. CuO **reduce** hua.
- **H₂** ne **oxygen paya** (H₂ se H₂O bana). Oxygen mila = **oxidation**. H₂ **oxidise** hua.

Dono kaam **ek hi reaction mein, ek hi samay par** ho gaye. Jo oxygen CuO ne chhoda, wahi H₂ ne le liya. Oxygen kahin gayab nahi hua; wo sirf **hath badal gaya**, jaise ek ladke ne gend phenki aur doosre ne pakad li.

```
          oxygen CuO se nikal kar H₂O mein gaya
       +-----------------------------------+
       |                                   v
      CuO   +    H₂    --->    Cu    +    H₂O
      ----                     --         ---
      REDUCE hua              (bana)      (bana)
      (oxygen khoya)

                   H₂ : oxygen mila  --->  OXIDISE hua
                   CuO: oxygen gaya  --->  REDUCE hua

      Oxidising agent : CuO  (H₂ ko oxygen diya)
      Reducing agent  : H₂   (CuO ka oxygen le liya)
```

> **Figure 5.2** – CuO + H₂ → Cu + H₂O. Oxygen CuO se H₂ ki taraf gaya. CuO reduce hua (oxidising agent), H₂ oxidise hua (reducing agent). Dono kirdaar saath hi aate hain.

**Ek zaroori niyam:** oxidation aur reduction **kabhi akele nahi hote**. Agar koi cheez oxygen le rahi hai, to koi doosri cheez oxygen de rahi hai. Isi liye poore reaction ko **redox** kehte hain (**red**uction + **ox**idation).

## Part C: Oxidising agent aur reducing agent

Seb ki kahani yaad karo: vitamin C ne seb ki jagah khud oxygen le liya. Wo ek **reducing agent** ki tarah kaam kar raha hai: khud oxidise hota hai taaki seb bache.

Seedha niyam:

- **Oxidising agent** wo hai jo **doosre ko oxidise kare**. Wo khud **reduce** hota hai.
- **Reducing agent** wo hai jo **doosre ko reduce kare**. Wo khud **oxidise** hota hai.

CuO + H₂ ke liye: CuO oxidising agent (H₂ ko oxygen diya), H₂ reducing agent (CuO ka oxygen le liya).

Ek baat jo ulti lagti hai par sahi hai: **jo oxidise hota hai wo reducing agent hai**, aur **jo reduce hota hai wo oxidising agent hai**. Agent ka matlab "kaam karwane wala", to naam doosre ke upar kiye gaye asar se hai.

## Part D: Ek ek reaction ko dekho: 12 equations

Neeche har equation balanced hai (dono taraf har element ke atom barabar). Ab oxygen aur hydrogen ke lene-dene se role pehchano.

| No. | Equation | Oxidise hua (oxygen mila ya hydrogen gaya) | Reduce hua (oxygen gaya ya hydrogen mila) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CuO + H₂ → Cu + H₂O | H₂ (oxygen mila) | CuO (oxygen gaya) |
| 2 | 2Cu + O₂ → 2CuO | Cu (oxygen mila) | Sirf oxygen ke hisaab se nahi dikhta, note dekho |
| 3 | 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO | Mg (oxygen mila) | Sirf oxygen ke hisaab se nahi dikhta, note dekho |
| 4 | ZnO + C → Zn + CO | C (oxygen mila) | ZnO (oxygen gaya) |
| 5 | Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂ | CO (oxygen mila) | Fe₂O₃ (oxygen gaya) |
| 6 | CuO + CO → Cu + CO₂ | CO (oxygen mila) | CuO (oxygen gaya) |
| 7 | 2H₂S + O₂ → 2S + 2H₂O | H₂S (hydrogen gaya) | O₂ (hydrogen mila) |
| 8 | CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O | CH₄ (oxygen mila, hydrogen bhi gaya) | O₂ (hydrogen mila) |
| 9 | 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O | H₂ (oxygen mila) | O₂ (hydrogen mila) |
| 10 | C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O | C₆H₁₂O₆ (oxygen mila, hydrogen gaya) | O₂ (hydrogen mila) |
| 11 | 4Fe + 3O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃ | Fe (oxygen mila) | Sirf oxygen ke hisaab se nahi dikhta, note dekho |
| 12 | Fe₂O₃ + 2Al → Al₂O₃ + 2Fe | Al (oxygen mila) | Fe₂O₃ (oxygen gaya) |

**Note (equations 2, 3, 11):** In mein ek padarth ko oxygen mil raha hai, par kisi ka oxygen nikal nahi raha, na hydrogen ka lena-dena hai. To sirf oxygen ya hydrogen ki paribhasha se hum **sirf oxidise hone wala** pehchan paate hain; saath mein O₂ **oxidising agent** hai (wahi oxygen deta hai). Dusra hissa (O₂ ka reduce hona) electron ki nazar se saaf dikhta hai, jo ek chhote paragraph mein aage aayega.

Kuch equations ko khol ke dekhte hain.

### Example 1. 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO

Magnesium ki patti hawa mein jalti hai to chamakdaar safed roshni deti hai (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo; seedha roshni ko mat dekho). Jalne ke baad safed powder MgO bachta hai.

- Mg ko oxygen mila, to **Mg oxidise hua**.
- O₂ oxygen dene wala hai, to **O₂ oxidising agent** hai, aur Mg reducing agent.
- O₂ khud reduce hua, par ye oxygen-hydrogen wali paribhasha se nahi dikhta; Part G ke electron wale paragraph mein dikhega.

Atom count: Mg 2 = 2, O 2 = 2 ✅.

### Example 2. ZnO + C → Zn + CO

Zinc oxide (jo safed powder hai) ko carbon (koyle) ke saath bahut garam karne par zinc dhatu milti hai. (Ye industry ki bhatti ki baat hai, sirf padhne ke liye.)

- ZnO ne oxygen **khoya** (Zn bana): **reduction**, ZnO reduce hua, oxidising agent.
- C ne oxygen **paya** (CO bana): **oxidation**, C oxidise hua, reducing agent.

Atom count: Zn 1 = 1, O 1 = 1, C 1 = 1 ✅.

### Example 3. Fe₂O₃ + 3CO → 2Fe + 3CO₂

Lohe ki kaan (iron ore) mein Fe₂O₃ hota hai. Blast furnace mein carbon monoxide gas us se oxygen le jaati hai aur lohaa alag ho jaata hai. (Sirf padhne ke liye; ye bahut garam bhatti mein hota hai.)

- Fe₂O₃ ne oxygen khoya: **reduce hua**, oxidising agent.
- CO ne oxygen paya (CO₂ bana): **oxidise hua**, reducing agent.

Atom count: Fe 2 = 2, C 3 = 3, O: 3 + 3 = 6 aur 3 × 2 = 6 ✅.

### Example 4. 2H₂S + O₂ → 2S + 2H₂O

Ye ek **hydrogen wala** misaal hai. H₂S ek badbudaar gas hai (sade hue ande jaisi). Ye kam oxygen mein jalti hai to sulphur banta hai. (Sirf kagaz par; H₂S zehreeli gas hai.)

- H₂S ne **hydrogen khoya** (hydrogen paani mein chala gaya): **oxidation**. H₂S reducing agent.
- O₂ ne **hydrogen paya** (H₂O bana): **reduction**. O₂ oxidising agent.

Atom count: H 4 = 4, S 2 = 2, O 2 = 2 ✅.

### Example 5. CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O

Gas stove ki gas (methane) jalti hai. Aage Topic 6 mein poori tarah dekhenge, abhi sirf roles:

- CH₄ ko oxygen mila (CO₂ bana) aur hydrogen bhi gaya (H₂O mein): **oxidise hua**, reducing agent.
- O₂ ko hydrogen mila: **reduce hua**, oxidising agent.

Atom count: C 1 = 1, H 4 = 4, O 2 × 2 = 4 aur right side 2 (CO₂ mein) + 2 (2H₂O mein) = 4 ✅.

## Part E: Oxygen ka "haath badalna"

```
   Pehle (reactants)                     Baad (products)

   CuO      H₂                           Cu       H₂O
   [Cu][O]  [H][H]    ------>            [Cu]    [H][O][H]

   O ka saathi pehle Cu tha              O ka naya saathi H hai
   (Cu se O gaya: reduction)             (H₂ ko O mila: oxidation)
```

> **Figure 5.3** – CuO + H₂ → Cu + H₂O mein oxygen ne saathi badla: pehle Cu ke saath tha, ab H ke saath hai. Cu se oxygen gaya (reduction), H₂ ko oxygen mila (oxidation).

## Part F: Rozmarra ki redox kahaaniyan

1. **Jalna (burning):** lakdi, gas, mombatti sab oxygen ke saath jalte hain. Fuel ko oxygen milta hai, to fuel **oxidise** hota hai (CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O). Poora kahani Topic 6 mein.
2. **Zang lagna:** lohe ko hawa aur nami ke saath oxygen milta hai, to Fe **oxidise** hota hai (4Fe + 3O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃, Topic 4 wala saral roop). Hand-warmer ke pouch mein bhi yahi tez hota hai (Topic 4).
3. **Saans (respiration):** hamare cells mein glucose ko oxygen milta hai: C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + energy. Glucose **oxidise** hota hai, ye ek dheeme oxidation ki tarah chalta hai.
4. **Seb ka bhura hona:** upar ki kahani. Oxygen ke saath dheeme reaction, aur vitamin C jaise "pehle oxygen lene wale" use rokte hain.
5. **Bleaching:** safed kapde ya purane daag ko **bleach** (jaise ghar mein kapde ke liye bikne wala safedi karne wala ghol) oxidise karta hai, jisse rangeen padarth bina rang ke ban jaata hai. Bleach ek **oxidising agent** hai.

> **Safety (ek baar, saaf-saaf):** **Bleach ko kabhi kisi doosre cleaner ke saath mat milao.** Toilet cleaner, acid, ammonia wale cleaner ya kisi bhi aur cleaning product ke saath milane se zehreeli gas ban sakti hai. Bleach ko akela, bataye gaye tareeke se, hawadaar jagah mein, adult ke saath hi istemaal karo.

## Part G: Aage ki class mein (electron wali nazar)

Oxygen aur hydrogen ke naam wali paribhasha kuch reactions par hi fit baithti hai, jaise hum ne equations 2, 3 aur 11 mein dekha. **Aage ki class mein** Chapter 5 (structure of the atom) aur Chapter 6 (chemical bonding) ki jaankari se ek zyada gehri nazar milti hai: **oxidation matlab electron khona, reduction matlab electron paana.** Jaise Mg → Mg²⁺ + 2e⁻ (Mg ne 2 electron diye, wo oxidise hua) aur O + 2e⁻ → O²⁻ (O ne 2 electron liye, wo reduce hua). Is nazar se 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO mein dono kirdaar saaf dikhte hain. Isi ke saath **oxidation number** aur redox equations ko balance karne ke naye tareeke bhi aate hain, jo abhi hamare level se bahar hain.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Sochna ki oxidation aur reduction alag-alag hote hain**

Dono **hamesha saath** hote hain. Kisi ne oxygen liya to kisi ne diya. Isiliye naam **redox**.

**Galti 2: "Reduce" ka matlab "kam hona" samajhna**

Yahan reduce ka matlab **oxygen ka kam hona** (ya hydrogen ka badhna) hai. CuO se Cu bana, oxide ka hissa kam hua, aur wahi reduction hai. Ye "poora padarth kam ho gaya" nahi hai.

**Galti 3: Agent aur role ko ulta bolna**

Jo oxidise hota hai wo **reducing agent** hai. Jo reduce hota hai wo **oxidising agent** hai. Naam doosre par asar ke hisaab se hai.

**Galti 4: Har oxygen wala reaction redox samajhna**

CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂ mein oxygen ka lena-dena nahi dikhta; wo sirf combination reaction hai. Equation mein ye dekho ki kis ko oxygen mila ya gaya, ya hydrogen mila ya gaya.

**Galti 5: Bleach ko doosre cleaner ke saath milana**

Bilkul nahi. Upar wali safety line dobara yaad rakho.

## Quick Recap

- **Oxidation** = oxygen milna ya hydrogen nikalna. **Reduction** = oxygen nikalna ya hydrogen milna.
- Dono **saath saath** hote hain, is liye reaction ko **redox** kehte hain.
- CuO + H₂ → Cu + H₂O: CuO **reduce** hua (oxidising agent), H₂ **oxidise** hua (reducing agent).
- **Oxidising agent** doosre ko oxidise karta hai aur khud reduce hota hai; **reducing agent** doosre ko reduce karta hai aur khud oxidise hota hai.
- Redox ke rozmarra misaal: jalna, zang, saans (respiration), seb ka bhura hona, bleaching.
- **Aage ki class mein:** oxidation = electron khona, reduction = electron paana (Mg → Mg²⁺ + 2e⁻).

### Answers

1. **Oxidation:** oxygen milna, ya hydrogen nikalna. **Reduction:** oxygen nikalna, ya hydrogen milna.

2. **H₂** oxidise hua (use oxygen mila, H₂O bana). **CuO** reduce hua (oxygen gaya, Cu bana). **CuO oxidising agent** hai (H₂ ko oxygen diya). **H₂ reducing agent** hai (CuO ka oxygen le liya).

3. (a) **C** oxidise hua (oxygen mila, CO bana), **CuO** reduce hua (oxygen gaya, Cu bana). Ginti: Cu 1 = 1, O 1 = 1, C 1 = 1 ✅. (b) **Al** oxidise hua (oxygen mila, Al₂O₃ bana), **Cr₂O₃** reduce hua (oxygen gaya, Cr bana). Ginti: Cr 2 = 2, O 3 = 3, Al 2 = 2 ✅. (c) **H₂S** oxidise hua (hydrogen gaya, S bana), **SO₂** reduce hua (oxygen gaya, S bana). Ginti: H 4 = 4, S 2 + 1 = 3, O 2 = 2 ✅. Mazedaar baat: dono taraf se sulphur hi banta hai.

4. Oxygen kabhi nasht nahi hota, wo sirf ek padarth se doosre ke paas jaata hai. Jo oxygen le raha hai wo oxidise hota hai, aur jis se oxygen ja raha hai wo reduce hota hai. To dono kaam saath hote hain, isliye **redox**.

5. **Mg** oxidise hua (oxygen mila). **O₂** oxidising agent hai (oxygen deta hai). Yahan kisi ka oxygen nikal nahi raha, na hydrogen ka lena-dena hai, to sirf is paribhasha se reduce hone wala padarth saaf nahi dikhta. Electron ki nazar (aage ki class mein) se dikhta hai ki O₂ ne electron liye.

6. Left: Fe 4, O 3 × 2 = 6. Right: Fe 2 × 2 = 4, O 2 × 3 = 6 ✅. **Fe** oxidise hua (oxygen mila).

7. Seb ke andar ke padarth oxygen ke saath react karke bhure ho jaate hain (oxidation). Nimbu mein vitamin C hota hai, jo pehle khud oxygen le leta hai, to seb ka padarth bacha rehta hai. Vitamin C **reducing agent** jaisa kaam karta hai, kyunki khud oxidise hota hai aur seb ko bachata hai.

8. **Tez:** gas stove par LPG ka jalna. **Dheema:** lohe ka zang lagna, ya seb ka bhura hona.

9. (a) **False** ❌. Reduction mein oxygen **nikalta** hai (ya hydrogen milta hai); oxygen milna oxidation hai. (b) **True** ✅. H₂ ne CuO ka oxygen le liya. (c) **False** ❌. Oxidation aur reduction hamesha saath hote hain. (d) **True** ✅. CH₄ ko oxygen milta hai (CO₂ bana).

10. Bleach ko doosre cleaners (toilet cleaner, acid, ammonia wale) ke saath milane se zehreeli gas ban sakti hai. Isliye bleach ko akela, hawadaar jagah mein aur adult ke saath hi istemaal karte hain.

11. Electron ki nazar se Mg **oxidise** hua, kyunki usne 2 electron **khoye** (oxidation = electron khona).
