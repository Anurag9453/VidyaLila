## Part A: Jalna (Combustion)

Combustion matlab koi padarth (**fuel**) **oxygen** mein jal kar **garmi aur roshni** deta hai. Ye ek exothermic reaction hai (Topic 4) aur ek oxidation bhi (Topic 5): fuel oxygen se judta hai.

Chulhe mein jalne wali gas ka sabse simple example methane hai:

CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O  (garmi aur roshni ke saath)

Ghar ka LPG ek **mixture** hai (kai gas ka), uska formula yaad rakhne ki zaroorat nahi. Bas itna samjho: jo fuel mein carbon aur hydrogen hain, wo jal kar **CO₂** aur **paani (bhaap)** dete hain. Atom count: baayein taraf 1 C, 4 H, 4 O; daayein taraf 1 C, 4 H, 2 + 2 = 4 O ✅.

Seedha carbon ka jalna bhi dekho: C + O₂ → CO₂. Koyla jalta hai to yehi hota hai.

### Aag ke liye teen cheezein: fire triangle

Aag lagne ke liye **teeno** chahiye: **fuel**, **oxygen** aur **garmi** (itni garmi ki fuel jalna shuru kar de). Ek bhi hataya to aag nahi jalegi, ya bujh jayegi.

```
                    GARMI
                      /\
                     /  \
                    /    \
                   /  AAG \
                  /        \
                 /__________\
              FUEL          OXYGEN

   Aag bujhane ke teen tareeke: teeno mein se ek kona hatao
     fuel hatao   : gas ka valve band karo, jalti lakdi alag karo
     oxygen hatao : kambal ya gili bori se dhako, CO₂ wala extinguisher
     garmi hatao  : paani daalo (thanda karne ke liye)
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Fire triangle: aag ke liye fuel, oxygen aur garmi teeno chahiye. Inme se ek bhi hata do to aag bujh jaati hai.

Kuch real example, sirf samajhne ke liye:

- **Gas ka chulha band karna:** valve band karne par fuel milna band, aag bujh gayi. (Fuel hataya.)
- **Kadhai mein tel ki aag par gili bori ya dhakkan:** oxygen ruk gaya. (Oxygen hataya.) Yahan **paani kabhi nahi** daalna: jalta tel chheente ki tarah bikhar kar aur phail jaata hai.
- **Lakdi ki aag par paani:** garmi kam hui. (Garmi hatayi.)
- **CO₂ wala extinguisher:** CO₂ bhaari gas hai, aag par kambal ki tarah failkar oxygen ko door karti hai.

Aag ka koi bhi prayog bachche **sirf bade ya teacher ke saath** dekhein, khud kabhi nahi.

### Poora jalna aur adhura jalna: carbon monoxide ka khatra

Jab oxygen **kaafi** hota hai, carbon poora jal kar CO₂ banata hai. Jab oxygen **kam** hota hai (band kamra, dhakka hua chulha), carbon poora nahi jalta aur **carbon monoxide (CO)** banta hai:

2C + O₂ → 2CO  (adhura jalna)

Methane ke liye bhi: 2CH₄ + 3O₂ → 2CO + 4H₂O. Atom count: baayein 2 C, 8 H, 6 O; daayein 2 C, 8 H, 2 + 4 = 6 O ✅.

**CO ek zehreeli gas hai.** Ye **bina rang aur bina gandh** ki hai, to dikhti bhi nahi aur pata bhi nahi chalta. Ye khoon mein oxygen ki jagah le leti hai, isliye chakkar, sar dard aur behoshi ho sakti hai, aur band jagah mein jaan ka khatra hai. Isi liye:

- **Band kamre mein angithi ya koyle ki sigri jala kar kabhi mat sona.** Har saal sardiyon mein aisi durghatnayein hoti hain.
- **Gas geyser** bathroom ke andar band jagah mein ho to ventilation (hawa ka raasta) zaroor ho, aur geyser chalate waqt dwar aur khidki ke paas hawa aane do.
- Rasoi mein chulhe ki lau **neeli** honi chahiye. Peeli, dhuaan wali lau ka matlab oxygen kam hai aur adhura jalna ho raha hai: burner saaf karwao.
- Kisi ko CO ki zehar lage to turant use **khuli hawa mein** le jao aur doctor ko bulao.

Bachche ye saari cheezein sirf jaan lein aur bado ko bataayein; khud koi aag ya geyser nahi chalayein.

Ek aur baat: CO khud bhi jal sakti hai aur zyada oxygen mile to CO₂ ban jaati hai: 2CO + O₂ → 2CO₂ ✅ (2 C, 4 O dono taraf).

> **Jaanch:** Gas ki lau peeli aur dhuaan wali hai. Kya ye poora jalna hai? ❌ Nahi, ye adhura jalna hai (oxygen kam), aur isme CO bhi ban sakti hai.

## Part B: Zang (Rusting)

**Loha (iron)** agar **hawa (oxygen)** aur **nami (paani)** dono ke sampark mein rahe, to dheere-dheere uspar **bhoora-laal bhurbhura coat** ban jaata hai. Ise **zang** kehte hain. Zang loha nahi hai: ye ek naya padarth hai, jo kamzor aur jhadne wala hai.

Simplified equation:

4Fe + 3O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃

Atom count: baayein 4 Fe, 6 O; daayein 2 × 2 = 4 Fe, 2 × 3 = 6 O ✅.

**Sacchi baat:** asli zang mein paani ke kuch kan bhi judte hain (hydrated), isliye ye equation sirf **simplified** hai. Par ye mool baat dikhati hai: loha oxygen se judta hai, yaani **oxidise** hota hai (Topic 5).

Zang tab hi lagti hai jab **dono** hon. Sukhe mein ya bina hawa ke loha jaldi zang nahi khata. Samundar ke paas, jahan hawa mein nami aur namak hai, zang aur tez lagti hai.

```
        HAWA (oxygen)          PAANI / NAMI
              \                     /
               v                   v
          ,~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~,
          |  paani ki patli parat        |
          '~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~'
          ################################   <- loha (Fe)
                  ||        ||
            zang: bhoora-laal, bhurbhura Fe₂O₃ ban raha hai
          ##########xxxxxx################
                   (zang jhadkar neeche ka loha
                    phir hawa-paani ke saamne laati hai)
```

> **Figure 6.2** – Loha, hawa ki oxygen aur paani saath mein aate hain to loha oxidise hokar zang (Fe₂O₃) banata hai. Zang bhurbhuri hai, isliye jhad jaati hai aur neeche ka loha dobara khulta jaata hai, aur zang aage badhti hai.

### Safe jaanch: zang ke liye kya zaroori hai (ghar par adult ke saath)

Teen saaf glass ya jar lo, har ek mein ek **naya loha ka keel**:

1. Jar A: keel ko **paani** mein aadha doobo (hawa aur paani dono).
2. Jar B: keel ko **ubale hue, thanda kiye hue paani** mein dalo aur upar thoda tel ki parat daal do (paani hai, par hawa nahi).
3. Jar C: keel ko **sukhe** jar mein rakho (hawa hai, nami nahi).

Garam ubla paani adult hi handle kare. Dhakkan dheela rakho aur 4 se 7 din dekho. **Dekhoge:** keel A par zang, B aur C mein bahut kam ya nahi. Natija: zang ke liye hawa **aur** nami dono chahiye. Keel haath se chhoo kar haath dho lo.

### Zang se bachne ke tareeke

Zang rokne ka mool tareeka hai: loha ko hawa aur paani se **door** rakho.

| Tareeka | Kaise kaam karta hai | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Paint** | Loha ke upar parat, hawa-paani nahi pahunchte | Gate, railing |
| **Tel ya grease** | Patli chikni parat | Cycle ki chain, tools |
| **Galvanising (zinc ki parat)** | Zinc ki parat chadhao; zinc loha se zyada reactive hai, wahi pehle oxidise hota hai aur loha bachta hai | Loha ki chaadar, paani ki tanki |
| **Tin ya chromium plating** | Ek patli parat chadhao | Dabbe, bumper |
| **Alloy (stainless steel)** | Loha mein chromium, nickel milakar mishran, jisme zang nahi lagti | Rasoi ke bartan, chamach |

> **Jaanch:** Paint chhil jaaye to loha zang khayega. ✅ Kyunki loha dobara hawa aur nami ke sampark mein aa jaata hai.

## Part C: Dusre metals ka corrosion

Zang sirf loha ke liye kehte hain. Dusre metals bhi dheere-dheere kharab hote hain, ise **corrosion** kehte hain.

- **Taambe (copper) par hara coat:** purane taambe ke bartan ya mandir ki moorti par hawa ki oxygen, CO₂ aur nami se hara parat jam jaati hai. Nimbu-imli se ragadne par chamak wapas aati hai (ghar par adult ke saath).
- **Chaandi (silver) ka kaala hona:** hawa mein bahut kam sulphur wali gas (jaise H₂S) hoti hai. Isse chaandi par kaala parat Ag₂S ka banta hai: 4Ag + 2H₂S + O₂ → 2Ag₂S + 2H₂O. Atom count: baayein 4 Ag, 4 H, 2 S, 2 O; daayein 4 Ag, 2 S, 4 H, 2 O ✅. (Ande ya pyaaz ke paas rakhi chaandi isi liye kaali padti hai.)
- **Aluminium:** hawa mein turant oxygen se judkar bahut patli, kasi hui **Al₂O₃ ki parat** banata hai: 4Al + 3O₂ → 2Al₂O₃ ✅ (4 Al, 6 O dono taraf). Ye parat paani aur hawa ko andar nahi jaane deti, isliye aluminium ka bartan aur foil ko aage corrosion nahi hota. Yahan oxide **madad karta hai**, loha ke zang se ulta.

## Part D: Rancidity (tel-ghee ka baasi hona)

Mr. Kelkar ne namkeen ke dabbe ki taraf ishara kiya. "Is namkeen mein tel hai. Tel ya ghee jaise **fats** hawa ki oxygen se dheere-dheere **oxidise** hote hain. Isse naye chhote padarth bante hain jinki gandh aur swaad kharab hota hai. Ise **rancidity** kehte hain."

Dhyan do: ye wahi oxidation hai jo Topic 5 mein tha. Garmi, roshni aur nami isko tez kar dete hain.

**Rancidity se bachne ke tareeke:**

- **Airtight dabba:** hawa (oxygen) andar nahi jaati. Isliye dhakkan kas kar band karo.
- **Fridge mein rakhna:** thandak se reaction dheema hota hai (Topic 4 ka rate wala niyam).
- **Antioxidants:** khane mein milaye jaane wale chhote padarth, jo oxidation ko dheema karte hain.
- **Nitrogen gas bharna:** chips ke packet mein hawa ki jagah nitrogen bhari jaati hai. Nitrogen bahut kam reactive hai, to oxidation nahi hota. Gas phoolkar packet ka kushan bhi banati hai, isliye chips toot nahi paate.

```
    Dheela dabba                  Nitrogen wala packet
    (hawa andar aati hai)         (hawa ki jagah nitrogen)

    +----------------+            ,-~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~-.
    |  O₂   O₂   O₂  |           (  N₂   N₂   N₂   N₂  )
    |   chips / tel  |           (   N₂  chips  N₂     )
    |  O₂  se oxidise|            ( N₂   N₂   N₂   N₂ )
    +----------------+            '-~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~-'

    oxygen se tel ka               oxygen nahi, to
    oxidation: baasi gandh         oxidation nahi: chips taaze
```

> **Figure 6.3** – Dheele dabbe mein hawa ki oxygen tel ko oxidise karti hai (rancidity). Chips ke packet mein oxygen ki jagah nitrogen bhari hoti hai, isliye chips taaze rehte hain aur packet phoola dikhta hai.

> **Jaanch:** Ghee ke dabbe ko dhoop mein khula chhod diya. Ghee jaldi baasi hoga. ✅ Hawa, roshni aur garmi teeno oxidation tez karte hain.

## Part E: Saans: sharir ke andar dheema jalna

Hum jo khana khate hain (jaise glucose), hamari **cells** mein oxygen ke saath dheere-dheere "jalta" hai aur sharir ko **energy** deta hai:

C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + energy

Atom count: baayein 6 C, 12 H, 6 + 12 = 18 O; daayein 6 C, 12 H, 12 + 6 = 18 O ✅.

Ye **respiration** hai, aur ise **slow combustion** (dheema jalna) bhi kaha ja sakta hai: fuel (glucose) oxygen ke saath CO₂, paani aur energy deta hai, bas **aag aur lau ke bina**, sharir ke taapman par, aur aaram se. Isliye saans lene par hum garam rehte hain aur CO₂ bahar chhodte hain.

## Part F: Packaging ke reasoning examples

**Example 1: Chips ke packet mein nitrogen kyun?**
Chips mein tel hai, jo oxygen se rancid hota hai. Nitrogen kam reactive hai, to oxygen hat gayi, oxidation ruka, chips taaze. ✅

**Example 2: Ghee ka jar kaanch ka, airtight aur andhere shelf par kyun?**
Airtight: oxygen nahi. Andhera aur thanda: roshni aur garmi oxidation tez karte hain. Isliye ghee der tak chalta hai.

**Example 3: Rasoi ke bartan stainless steel ke kyun?**
Stainless steel ek alloy hai, jisme zang nahi lagti, aur roz paani, namak aur nami ke baad bhi sahi rehta hai. Aam loha ka tawa ya kadhai mein tel ki patli parat se zang rukti hai.

**Example 4: Loha ki chaadar par zinc ki parat kyun?**
Zinc loha se zyada reactive hai. Parat chhil bhi jaaye to pehle zinc oxidise hota hai aur loha bachta hai. (Topic 3 ke reactivity series se milti baat.)

**Example 5: Ek bacche ne kaha, "Aluminium ka oxide bura hota hai, jaise loha ka zang."** Sahi ya galat?
Galat. Zang corrosion hai, par aluminium ka oxide ek patli surakshit parat banata hai jo metal ko bachati hai.

**Example 6: Band bathroom mein gas geyser ka CO ka khatra.**
Geyser mein oxygen kam pade to gas adhuri jalti hai aur CO banti hai. CO bina rang-gandh ki zehreeli gas hai, to dikhti nahi. Isliye ventilation ya bahar ki taraf nikasi zaroori hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Aag ke liye bas fuel aur garmi chahiye"**
Teen cheezein chahiye: fuel, oxygen **aur** garmi. Oxygen na ho to aag nahi jalegi.

**Galti 2: "Tel ki aag par paani daalna chahiye"**
Nahi. Paani jalte tel ko bikher kar aag ko phailata hai. Dhakkan ya gili bori se oxygen roko, ya gas band karo.

**Galti 3: "CO ki gandh se pata chal jayega"**
CO ki koi gandh aur rang nahi hota. Ventilation hi bachaav hai.

**Galti 4: "Zang aur loha ek hi cheez hain"**
Zang (Fe₂O₃) naya, kamzor padarth hai, loha ko ghata kar banta hai.

**Galti 5: "Zang sirf paani se lagti hai"**
Paani **aur** oxygen dono chahiye.

**Galti 6: "Rancidity matlab ghee ka kharab hona, kisi reaction ki wajah se nahi"**
Ye oxygen se hone wala oxidation hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Combustion** mein fuel oxygen mein jal kar garmi aur roshni deta hai: CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O.
- **Fire triangle:** fuel, oxygen, garmi. Inme se ek hatao to aag bujhti hai.
- Oxygen kam ho to **adhura jalna** aur **CO** (zehreeli, rang-gandh rahit): band jagah mein aag ya geyser se hamesha ventilation rakho.
- **Zang:** loha + hawa + nami → bhoora Fe₂O₃ (simplified: 4Fe + 3O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃). Bachaav: paint, tel, galvanising, plating, stainless steel.
- Taambe par hara coat, chaandi ka kaala hona, aluminium ki surakshit oxide parat: ye corrosion ke alag-alag roop hain.
- **Rancidity:** tel-ghee ka hawa se oxidise hona. Bachaav: airtight dabba, fridge, antioxidants, chips mein nitrogen.
- **Respiration:** sharir mein dheema jalna: C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + energy.

## Chapter Ka Saar: Chemical Changes and Reactions

Ek nazar mein poore chapter ka safar: reaction kya hai, uske prakaar, energy aur raftaar, oxidation-reduction, aur aaj roz ki zindagi mein inka asar.

| Topic | Kya seekha | Ek line |
|---|---|---|
| Topic 1 | Chemical reaction kya hai | Reaction mein purane bond tootte hain aur naye bante hain, atoms sirf naye tareeke se jud jaate hain (na bante, na khatam hote), isliye mass wahi rehta hai; reactants aur products equation mein dikhte hain. |
| Topic 2 | Combination aur decomposition | Combination mein do ya zyada padarth milkar ek banate hain (2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O); decomposition mein ek padarth tootkar kai banta hai, garmi, bijli ya roshni se (CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂). |
| Topic 3 | Displacement aur double displacement | Zyada reactive metal kam reactive ko uske salt ke ghol se nikalta hai (Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu), reactivity series se pehle hi andaaza; double displacement mein ions saathi badalte hain, aur precipitate ya paani banta hai. |
| Topic 4 | Energy aur raftaar | Exothermic reaction garmi deta hai, endothermic leta hai; raftaar badhti hai garmi, concentration, chhote tukde aur catalyst se (catalyst khud khatam nahi hota). |
| Topic 5 | Oxidation aur reduction | Oxidation matlab oxygen judna ya hydrogen nikalna, reduction ulta; dono saath hote hain (redox), aur oxidising aur reducing agent ye kaam karwate hain. |
| Topic 6 | Roz ki chemistry | Jalna (fire triangle, CO ka khatra), zang aur uski roktham, dusre metals ka corrosion, rancidity aur respiration, sab oxidation ke hi roop hain. |

Ashwin ne chips ka packet khola aur ek chips khaya. "Mama, ab main samajh gaya. Nitrogen ka phoola packet koi thagi nahi, chemistry ka kamaal hai. Aur ye namkeen? Ye ab bahar jayegi, aur agli baar dabba airtight." Mr. Kelkar muskuraye. "Shabaash! Ab tumhe har jagah reaction dikhegi."
