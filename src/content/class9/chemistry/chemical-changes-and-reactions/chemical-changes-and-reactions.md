# Topic 1: Chemical Reaction Kya Hai: Todna aur Jodna

Pichhle chapter ke end mein humne ek sawaal chhoda tha: **jab purane bond tootte hain aur naye bond bante hain, tab kya hota hai?** Chapter "Chemical Bonding" mein humne dekha ki atoms electron dene-lene ya baantne se kaise jude rehte hain. Chapter "Language of Chemistry" mein humne reaction ko equation mein likhna aur balance karna seekha. Aaj in dono ko jodte hain: equation ke arrow ke peeche asal mein **bond todne aur bond banane ka khel** chal raha hota hai.

---

## Phuljhari ki kahani

Diwali ki shaam thi. Mr. Gaikwad ne apni beti Swara ko ek phuljhari (sparkler) pakadayi aur kaha, "Haath door rakh, aur sirf mere saamne jala." Adult ke saath Swara ne phuljhari jalayi. Chhan-chhan karke chingaariyan udne lagi, tez roshni hui, aur haath tak garmi mehsoos hui.

Do minute mein phuljhari bujh gayi. Swara ne dekha ki ek lambi tez cheez ab **patla kaala-sa taar** reh gayi hai. Usne puchha, "Papa, baaki phuljhari kahan gayi? Gayab ho gayi?"

Mr. Gaikwad ne kaha, "Gayab nahi hui, beta. Phuljhari ke masale mein jo metal ka powder aur doosri cheezein thi, wo **hawa ki oxygen ke saath mil gayi** aur **nayi cheezein** ban gayi: dhuaan, rakh jaisa powder, aur bahut saari garmi aur roshni. Ye taar to bas sahara tha. Jo kuch hua, wo **chemical reaction** thi."

Swara ne bujha hua taar dekha. "To ye taar aur wo chingaariyan, sab same atoms se bane hain jo pehle the?" "Bilkul," Mr. Gaikwad muskuraye. "Atoms wahi hain. Bas unke **jodidaar badal gaye**."

(Phuljhari sirf bade ke saath, khule mein, haath door rakh ke jalayi jaati hai. Ye humne sirf **dekhne** ke liye li hai, aap khud iska reaction try nahi karna.)

## Part A: Reaction matlab atoms ki nayi jodiyan

Pichhle chapter mein humne dekha tha ki molecule mein atoms **bond** se bandhe hote hain. Ab ek sawaal: agar bond itna pakka hai, to reaction mein kuch naya kaise banta hai?

Jawab: **bond ko todna padta hai, aur phir naya bond banana padta hai.** Ek shaadi ka example lo. Ek party mein jodiyan (couples) naach rahi hain. Gaane ke beech mein jodiyan **ek doosre ka haath chhodti** hain (purana bond tootta hai) aur phir **alag-alag partner ka haath pakadti** hain (naya bond banta hai). Party mein log wahi hain, bas partner badal gaye.

Chemical reaction mein bhi 3 baatein hamesha hoti hain:

1. **Reactants ke andar ke kuch purane bond toot jaate hain.** Isme energy lagti hai (jaise haath chhudane mein thodi mehnat).
2. **Atoms nayi tarah se arrange hote hain.** Koi atom naya nahi banta, koi khatam nahi hota (Chapter 4 mein conservation of mass).
3. **Naye bond ban jaate hain**, aur isse energy wapas milti hai (qualitative baat: kabhi zyada milti hai, kabhi kam; iska poora hisaab aage ki class mein).

> **Dhyan do:** Hum yahan energy ki sirf baat kar rahe hain, koi sankhya nahi. Kaun si reaction garmi deti hai aur kaun si garmi leti hai, ye Topic 4 mein dekhenge.

Isliye **tatva kabhi nahi badalte**, sirf **padarth** badalte hain. Jab hum Chapter 2 mein "chemical change" bolte the, to wahi baat thi: naya padarth ban gaya. Ab hum jaante hain ki **kyun** ban gaya: bond badal gaye.

## Part B: H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl: todna aur jodna gin ke dekho

Chalo ek chhoti reaction ko pura dekhte hain. Hydrogen gas aur chlorine gas mil kar hydrogen chloride (HCl) gas banate hain. (Ye reaction sirf kagaz par hai; chlorine zehreeli gas hai, isliye ye teacher bhi bahut dhyan se hi dikhate hain, hum nahi karenge.)

**Balanced equation:** H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl

Isse hum ye padhte hain: "**Ek** molecule hydrogen aur **ek** molecule chlorine mil kar HCl ke **do** molecule banate hain." Ab gino ki bond kitne tootte hain aur kitne bante hain.

```
   Pehle (reactants)             Beech (bond toot gaye)         Baad mein (products)

   H - H       Cl - Cl           H    H    Cl    Cl             H - Cl        H - Cl
   ek bond     ek bond           (4 akele atoms)               ek bond       ek bond

   Todne wale bond: 2  ----------------------------------------->  Bante bond: 2
   Atoms: H = 2, Cl = 2           H = 2, Cl = 2                   H = 2, Cl = 2
```

> **Figure 1.1** – H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl ka bond picture. Baayein: 2 bond (H–H aur Cl–Cl). Beech mein bond tootne ke baad 4 akele atoms (2 H, 2 Cl). Daayein: 2 naye bond (H–Cl aur H–Cl). Dono taraf H = 2 aur Cl = 2: koi atom na badha, na ghata, sirf jodidaar badle. (Beech ka "akela atom" sirf samajhne ke liye ek sochne wali stage hai, asli reaction mein ye ek hi pal mein hota hai.)

### Ginti ki jaanch (electrons ke saath)

Pichhle chapter ki tarah ab electrons bhi ginte hain. Sirf baahari (outer) electrons lo: H ke 1, Cl ke 7.

- **Pehle:** H₂ mein 1 + 1 = 2 electron (1 shared pair). Cl₂ mein 7 + 7 = 14 (1 shared pair aur 6 lone pairs ke 12 electron). Kul = 2 + 14 = **16**.
- **Baad mein:** har HCl mein H aur Cl ke 1 + 7 = 8 electron, yaani 1 shared pair aur 3 lone pairs. 2 HCl mein 2 × 8 = **16**.

16 = 16 ✅. Shared pairs: pehle 2 (H₂ ka 1 + Cl₂ ka 1), baad mein 2 (har HCl ka 1). Lone pairs: pehle 6 (Cl₂ ke), baad mein 2 × 3 = 6. **Sirf bond ke saathi badle, electrons ki kul ginti wahi rahi.**

Ek aur ginti, bhaar ki: H = 1, Cl = 35.5 lene par pehle H₂ = 2 aur Cl₂ = 71, kul **73**. Baad mein 2 × HCl = 2 × (1 + 35.5) = 2 × 36.5 = **73** ✅. Yahi Chapter 4 ki conservation of mass hai: reaction se pehle aur baad ka vazan barabar.

## Part C: Ek aur reaction: atoms ki nayi tolian

Ab ek aur reaction dekhte hain, jisme paani banta hai: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O. Isme O₂ mein double bond hai, isliye picture mein O=O likha hai.

```
   Pehle: 2H₂ + O₂                      Baad mein: 2H₂O

   H - H     H - H     O = O            H - O - H        H - O - H

   Atoms: H = 4, O = 2                  Atoms: H = 4, O = 2
   Bond: do H–H aur ek O=O              Bond: chaar O–H (har H₂O mein 2)
```

> **Figure 1.2** – 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O atoms ki nayi jodiyon ki tarah. Pehle 4 H aur 2 O alag-alag molecules mein the. Baad mein wahi 4 H aur 2 O paani ke 2 molecules mein hain. Atoms wahi, saathi naye.

Jaanch (Chapter 3 wali): H: baayein 2 × 2 = 4, daayein 2 × 2 = 4 ✅. O: baayein 2, daayein 2 × 1 = 2 ✅. Bhaar: 2 × 2 + 32 = 36 u (H = 1, O = 16) aur 2 × 18 = 36 u ✅.

## Part D: Equation padhna aur reaction ke clues

### Equation ko zor se padhna

Chhoti equations aise bolke padho:

| Equation | Bolke padho |
|---|---|
| H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl | Hydrogen aur chlorine mil kar HCl ke do molecule banate hain |
| 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O | Hydrogen ke do molecule aur oxygen ka ek molecule mil kar paani ke do molecule banate hain |
| CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂ | Calcium carbonate garam hone par calcium oxide aur carbon dioxide mein toot jaata hai |
| Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂ | Zinc aur do HCl se zinc chloride aur hydrogen gas milti hai |

Arrow ko "**ban jaata hai**" ya "**banata hai**" padhte hain. Coefficient batata hai ki kitne molecule ya atom shamil hain (Chapter 3).

### Reaction ke clues (Chapter 2 se yaad karo)

Chapter 2 mein humne dekha tha ki chemical change ke kuch **clues** hote hain. Ab hum jaante hain ki ye kyun aate hain: kyunki naye padarth ban rahe hain.

| Clue | Aam example | Dhyan rakho |
|---|---|---|
| Gas ke bubbles | Baking soda ko nimbu ke ras mein daalna | Ubalte paani ke bubbles bhi aate hain, par wo sirf paani ki bhaap hai (physical change). Naya padarth hua ya nahi, ye asli sawaal hai |
| Rang badalna | Lohe ki keel par laal-bhoora jaisa coat | Rang kabhi sirf ghulne se bhi badalta hai |
| Precipitate (thos neeche baithna) | Do safed ghol milane par doodhiya ho jaana | Aage Topic 3 mein |
| Garmi ya thandak (temperature badalna) | Hand-warmer garam hona | Barf ka pighalna bhi thandak deta hai, par naya padarth nahi banta |
| Roshni ya chingaariyan | Phuljhari | Bulb bhi roshni deta hai, par wahan koi reaction nahi |
| Naya gandh | Dahi ya doodh ka phat jaana, ya jala hua khaana | Gandh bhi kabhi sirf bhaap se aati hai |

Isliye **ek clue se pakka nahi hota**; sabse bharosemand tarika hai dekhna ki **naya padarth** bana ya nahi.

### Conditions aur state symbols

Language of Chemistry mein humne seekha: arrow ke upar **condition** (heat, catalyst) aur formula ke baad **state symbols**: (s), (l), (g), (aq). Yahan bas yaad dilana hai:

CaCO₃(s) ──heat──► CaO(s) + CO₂(g)

Isse pata chalta hai ki reaction ko **garmi chahiye**, chuna-patthar thos hai, aur CO₂ gas ban kar nikalti hai. Kuch reactions bina condition ke apne aap ho jaati hain, kuch ko **push** (garmi, roshni ya catalyst) chahiye. Ek match ki teeli se gas ka chulha jalana yaad karo: pehle thodi si chingaari (push), phir aag khud chalti rehti hai.

## Part E: Sab reactions ek jaisi nahi: types ka idea

Ab tak humne 3 reactions dekhi: H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl, 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O aur CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂. Kuch dikh raha hai? Pehli do mein **do cheezein mil kar ek** ban rahi hain. Teesri mein **ek cheez tootkar do** ban rahi hai. Kuch aur reactions mein ek atom doosre ko **jagah se hata** deta hai.

Bilkul waise jaise hum aam cheezon ko group karte hain: phal, sabzi, anaaj. Group se hum **pehle se andaaza** laga sakte hain ki naya aane wala kaisa hoga. Reactions ke bhi 4 main group hain:

```
   Jodna            A + B  ----->  AB          (combination)
   Todna            AB     ----->  A + B       (decomposition)
   Jagah badalna    A + BC ----->  AC + B      (displacement)
   Saathi badalna   AB + CD ---->  AD + CB     (double displacement)
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Reactions ke 4 main prakaar ka preview. Har baar bond tootte aur bante hain, bas kaise, ye alag hai. Har prakaar ko Topic 2 aur Topic 3 mein aaram se dekhenge.

Isi ke saath reactions ko 2 aur nazar se bhi dekhte hain:

| Nazariya | Sawaal | Kahan seekhenge |
|---|---|---|
| Energy | Garmi nikalti hai (exothermic) ya jaati hai (endothermic)? Reaction kitni tez? | Topic 4 |
| Oxygen aur hydrogen | Oxygen jud raha hai ya ja raha hai (oxidation aur reduction)? | Topic 5 |
| Roz ki zindagi | Jalna, zang, rancidity | Topic 6 |

Abhi in sab ke naam ratne nahi hain: bas samajhna hai ki **ek hi reaction ko kai nazron se dekha ja sakta hai**.

## Part F: Ghar par ek chhoti activity: **ghar par adult ke saath**

Ek steel ya kaanch ke glass mein thoda nimbu ka ras lo. Adult ke saath ek chutki baking soda daalo. Dhyan se dekho.

- Jhaag (bubbles) uthenge: gas ka clue.
- Haath se glass ko chhuo: thoda thanda lag sakta hai: temperature ka clue.
- Kabhi chakhna nahi, aur khula glass hi use karo, band dibba nahi.

Kya naya padarth bana? Haan, kyunki gas ban kar nikal rahi hai, jo pehle glass mein nahi thi. Isko hum Topic 4 mein energy ke saath phir dekhenge. (Sirf nimbu ke ras aur baking soda ke saath; kisi aur cheez ko mila kar mat dekhna.)

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Reaction mein atoms naye ban jaate hain"**

Nahi. Atoms wahi rehte hain; H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl mein 2 H aur 2 Cl pehle bhi the, baad mein bhi. Sirf bond badle.

**Galti 2: "Jahan bubbles hain wahan reaction hai"**

Ubalta paani bhi bubbles deta hai, par wo sirf paani ki bhaap hai (physical change). Naya padarth banne ki jaanch karo.

**Galti 3: Bond banana aur todna ek jaisa samajhna**

Bond todne mein energy **lagti** hai, bond banne mein energy **milti** hai. Dono alag baatein hain.

**Galti 4: "Balanced equation matlab reaction ho jaayegi"**

Balanced hone ka matlab sirf atoms ka hisaab sahi hai. Reaction hogi ya nahi aur kitni tezi se, wo alag baat hai (Topic 4).

**Galti 5: "Phuljhari jal gayi to uska vazan gayab ho gaya"**

Vazan kahin nahi gaya. Jo dhuaan aur gas hawa mein gayi, aur jo rakh bachi, sab milakar vazan wahi hai jo phuljhari aur hawa ki oxygen ka tha.

## Quick Recap

- **Chemical reaction** mein reactants ke purane bond tootte hain, atoms nayi tarah arrange hote hain, aur products ke naye bond bante hain.
- Atoms na bante hain na khatam hote hain; isliye mass aur har tatva ke atoms barabar rehte hain.
- H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl mein 2 bond tootte hain (H–H, Cl–Cl) aur 2 bante hain (2 H–Cl). Electrons: 16 pehle, 16 baad mein; mass: 73 u dono taraf.
- 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O mein 4 H aur 2 O dono taraf, sirf jodidaar badle.
- Clues (gas, rang, precipitate, garmi ya thandak, roshni, gandh) se shak hota hai, par pakka pata naya padarth banne se chalta hai.
- Arrow ke upar conditions aur state symbols reaction ki aur jaankari dete hain; kuch reactions ko shuru hone ke liye push chahiye.
- Reactions ke 4 main prakaar: combination, decomposition, displacement, double displacement; aur unhe energy, oxygen aur roz ki zindagi ki nazar se bhi dekhte hain.

## Practice Questions

1. In your own words, explain what happens to the bonds of the reactants and the products during a chemical reaction. Use the dance-party idea or your own example.

2. For H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl, count how many bonds are broken and how many are formed. Also count the atoms of H and Cl on both sides.

3. In the same reaction, count the outer electrons (H has 1, Cl has 7) before and after, and show that the number is the same.

4. The mass of H₂ is 2 u, of Cl₂ is 71 u and of HCl is 36.5 u. Show that the total mass is the same before and after for H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl.

5. Read aloud, in words, the equation 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O. How many atoms of H and O are there on each side?

6. A student says: "Bubbles appeared when I boiled water, so a chemical reaction happened." Is she right? Give a reason.

7. Why is looking for only one clue not enough to say a chemical reaction has happened? Give one example of a clue that can also happen in a physical change.

8. Write CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂ with state symbols and the heat condition on the arrow. What does the (g) tell you?

9. Look at the three reactions in this topic: H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl, 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O and CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂. In which of them do two or more reactants make one product, and in which does one reactant break into two products?

10. State True or False and correct the false ones:
    (a) In a reaction, new atoms are created.
    (b) Breaking bonds takes energy.
    (c) Forming bonds gives energy back.
    (d) A balanced equation always means the reaction will happen.

### Answers

1. Reactants ke kuch purane bond **tootte** hain, atoms nayi jodiyan banate hain, aur products ke **naye bond** bante hain. Party mein jodiyan haath chhodti hain aur naye partner ke saath haath pakadti hain, log wahi rehte hain. Atoms na naye bante hain na khatam hote hain.

2. Bond tootte hain: **2** (ek H–H aur ek Cl–Cl). Bond bante hain: **2** (do H–Cl). Atoms: baayein H = 2, Cl = 2; daayein H = 2 × 1 = 2, Cl = 2 × 1 = 2 ✅.

3. Pehle: H₂ ke 1 + 1 = 2, Cl₂ ke 7 + 7 = 14, kul **16**. Baad mein: har HCl mein 1 + 7 = 8, do HCl mein 2 × 8 = **16**. Dono barabar ✅.

4. Pehle: 2 + 71 = **73** u. Baad mein: 2 × 36.5 = **73** u. Barabar ✅.

5. "Hydrogen ke do molecule aur oxygen ka ek molecule mil kar paani ke do molecule banate hain." Baayein: H = 2 × 2 = 4, O = 2. Daayein: H = 2 × 2 = 4, O = 2 × 1 = 2 ✅.

6. Nahi. Ubalte paani ke bubbles sirf paani ki bhaap hain, aur paani wahi (H₂O) rehta hai. Koi naya padarth nahi bana, isliye ye physical change hai.

7. Kuch clues physical change mein bhi aate hain, jaise ubalte paani ke bubbles ya barf ke pighalne par thandak. Pakka sabut tab milta hai jab **naya padarth** ban gaya ho.

8. **CaCO₃(s) ──heat──► CaO(s) + CO₂(g).** (g) ka matlab CO₂ gas hai aur wo gas ban kar nikalti hai. Check: Ca 1 = 1, C 1 = 1, O 3 = 1 + 2 = 3 ✅.

9. Pehli do (H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl aur 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O) mein do reactants mil kar ek product banate hain. Teesri (CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂) mein ek reactant tootkar do products banata hai. Inke naam aur detail Topic 2 mein.

10. (a) **False** ❌. Atoms naye nahi bante, wahi atoms nayi tarah judte hain. (b) **True** ✅. (c) **True** ✅. (d) **False** ❌. Balanced hone ka matlab sirf atoms ka hisaab sahi hai; reaction hogi ya nahi, ye alag baat hai.
