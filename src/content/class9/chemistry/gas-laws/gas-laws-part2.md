# Topic 2: Boyle Ka Niyam: Dabao Badhao, Aayatan Ghatao

Topic 1 mein humne seekha ki gas ka pressure kanon ke deewar se takrane se banta hai, aur gas ko jaanne ke chaar variables hain: pressure (P), volume (V), temperature (T) aur matra (n). Humne ye bhi tay kiya tha ki ek samay mein **sirf ek cheez badlenge**, baaki sab constant rakhenge. Aaj pehla asli experiment: temperature aur matra ko pakka rakho, aur dekho ki **volume badalne par pressure ka kya hota hai**. Is ka jawab ek bahut mashhoor niyam hai, jo 1662 mein Robert Boyle ne diya.

## Angootha, syringe aur ek chhota sa sawaal

Science lab mein Mr. Ketkar ne ek plastic ki medical syringe uthayi. **Usme koi needle nahi thi**, bas khaali, saaf barrel, ek plunger aur aage ek patli si nozzle. Unhone plunger ko 20 mL ke nishaan tak khinch kar rakha, taaki andar 20 mL hawa bhar jaaye. Phir apne angoothe se nozzle ko kas kar band kar diya.

"Yashvi, ab plunger ko dhakelo," unhone kaha.

Aur 5 mL par to plunger itna ada ki Yashvi ka haath thak gaya. Jaise hi usne haath hataya, plunger **uchhal kar waapas peeche aa gaya**!

"Sir, ye to jaise andar koi spring hai!" Yashvi hairan thi.

"Spring hi samjho," Mr. Ketkar muskuraye. "Par ye spring **hawa ka pressure** hai. Aaj hum is spring ka poora hisaab nikalenge: jitna volume ghatao, pressure kitna badhta hai, aur kyun." (Ye syringe sirf lab mein teacher ke saath chalane ke liye thi. Needle waali syringe ko bachche kabhi haath nahi lagate.)

## Part A: Syringe mein kya ho raha hai?

Chalo syringe ko ek kan-wali tasveer mein dekhte hain. Topic 1 se yaad karo: gas ke kan tez bhaagte hain, aur har baar jab ek kan deewar se takrata hai, deewar par ek chhota dhakka lagta hai. Bahut saare kanon ke bahut saare dhakke milkar **pressure** bante hain.

```
   Pehle: 20 mL hawa, pressure 1 atm

                 +----------------------------------+
   plunger ======|  .  o    .     o   .      o      |==== nozzle
                 |    .   o     .      o   .    .   |     (angootha se band)
                 +----------------------------------+
                 <------------- 20 mL -------------->

   Baad mein: plunger dabaya, 10 mL hawa, pressure 2 atm

                                   +----------------+
   plunger ========================|  .  o   .   o  |==== nozzle
                                   |  o   .   o  .  |     (angootha se band)
                                   +----------------+
                                   <---- 10 mL ----->

   o aur . = hawa ke kan (kan utne hi hain, bas jagah aadhi hai)
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Syringe ke andar hawa ke kan: pehle 20 mL mein kan door-door the; plunger dabane par wahi kan sirf 10 mL mein aa gaye. (Kanon ki ginti dono mein barabar rakhi gayi hai aur doori bahut badi dikhai gayi hai. Asli syringe mein kan itne zyada hote hain ki ginna mumkin nahi.)

Ab teen baatein dhyan se dekho.

**1. Kanon ki ginti badli nahi.** Angootha nozzle par hai, to koi kan bahar nahi gaya, aur koi andar nahi aaya. Matra (n) constant hai.

**2. Kanon ki speed nahi badli** (agar plunger dheere dabaya jaaye aur temperature wahi rahe). Temperature constant hai, isliye kan utne hi tez bhaag rahe hain.

**3. Jagah aadhi ho gayi.** Wahi kan ab aadhe barrel mein hain. Ab har kan ko deewar tak pahunchne ke liye aadhi doori tay karni hai, to wo **pehle se zyada baar** deewar se takrayega. Kanon ki ginti wahi, jagah aadhi: isliye deewar ki har chhoti si patti par per second takkaron ki ginti lagbhag **double** ho jaati hai.

Isliye: **volume aadha, to pressure double.** Volume ek-chauthai, to pressure chaar guna. Ye wahi "spring" hai jo Yashvi ke haath ko wapas dhakel raha tha: andar ke zyada pressure ne plunger ko bahar dhakela.

Ek zaroori baat: jab plunger ko bahut tezi se dabate hain to hawa thodi garam ho jaati hai (jaise pump ka barrel dabaane par garam lagta hai), aur tab ye simple niyam thoda sa galat baithta hai. Isliye niyam ke liye hum maante hain ki plunger **dheere dheere** dabaya gaya, taaki temperature wahi rahe.

## Part B: Boyle ka niyam

Boyle ne bahut saare aise measurements liye aur dekha ki pressure aur volume ka **guna-fal (product)** har baar lagbhag ek hi aata hai.

> **Boyle ka niyam:** Constant temperature par, kisi nishchit matra ki gas ke liye **pressure (P) uske volume (V) ke ulte anupaat mein hota hai.**
> Yaani **P × V = constant**, ya

> **P₁V₁ = P₂V₂**

Yahan P₁ aur V₁ **pehle** ki sthiti ka pressure aur volume hain, P₂ aur V₂ **baad** ki sthiti ke. Syringe ke liye: P₁ = 1 atm, V₁ = 20 mL, V₂ = 10 mL, to P₂ = (1 × 20) ÷ 10 = **2 atm**.

Ye niyam tabhi chalta hai jab:

- **temperature constant** ho,
- **gas ki matra constant** ho (koi gas bahar na nikle, na andar aaye).

**Units ka niyam:** P₁ aur P₂ **same unit** mein hone chahiye (dono atm, ya dono mm Hg). Aur V₁ aur V₂ bhi same unit mein (dono mL, ya dono litre). Kyunki hum sirf ratio use kar rahe hain, unit koi bhi chalega, bas **dono taraf ek hi**. Temperature kelvin mein nahi badalni, kyunki wo to badal hi nahi raha.

## Part C: Graph se samjho

Boyle ke jaise readings lete hain. Ek hawa ka sample, constant temperature par, aur ye table (illustrative readings):

| Volume V (L) | Pressure P (atm) | P × V |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 1 | 10 |
| 5 | 2 | 10 |
| 4 | 2.5 | 10 |
| 2.5 | 4 | 10 |
| 2 | 5 | 10 |

Dekho: volume **aadha** (10 se 5) to pressure **double** (1 se 2). Volume 4 guna kam (10 se 2.5) to pressure 4 guna zyada (1 se 4). Aur **P × V har jagah 10** aata hai.

Seedhi line ka graph aam taur par **pehchanna aasaan** hota hai, isliye vaigyanik aisa graph pasand karte hain.

## Part D: Roz ki zindagi mein Boyle ka niyam

- **Syringe:** plunger khinchne par barrel ka volume badhta hai, andar ka pressure bahar ke pressure se kam ho jaata hai, aur bahar ka pressure dawa (ya hawa) ko andar dhakel deta hai. (Syringe kaise kaam karti hai ye ek sahi udaharan hai, par asli injection sirf doctor ya nurse lagate hain.)
- **Cycle ya football ka pump:** pump ke barrel mein hawa dabane par uska pressure ball ya tyre ke pressure se zyada ho jaata hai, aur wo ball mein ghus jaati hai. Pump ka barrel garam bhi hota hai, kyunki hawa tezi se dabti hai.
- **Paani mein bubble:** paani ke andar pressure har 10 m gehrayi par lagbhag 1 atm badhta hai (Physics ka idea, lagbhag value). Gehrayi par choti bubble upar aate samay badi hoti jaati hai, kyunki ooper pressure kam hai. Isliye scuba divers kabhi saans rok kar upar nahi aate: phephdon ki hawa ka volume bahut phail kar phephdon ko nuksaan pahuncha sakta hai.
- **Saans lena:** diaphragm neeche jaata hai to phephdon ka volume badhta hai, andar ka pressure bahar se thoda kam ho jaata hai, aur bahar ki hawa andar aa jaati hai. Saans chhodte samay ulta hota hai: volume ghatta hai, pressure badhta hai, aur hawa bahar nikalti hai.
- **Band bottle pahaad se utarte samay:** pahaad ki chhoti par bahar ka pressure kam hota hai. Wahan band ki hui khaali bottle neeche laane par kuchle ya pichak jaati hai, kyunki neeche bahar ka pressure zyada hai aur andar ka kam (ye kahani aage Topic 6 mein bhi aayegi).

**Activity 1 (school lab mein teacher ke saath ya ghar par adult ke saath):** bina needle ki plastic syringe lo (jo doctor ya chemist ke paas se adult ne laayi ho). Plunger ko lagbhag aadhe tak khincho, phir angoothe se nozzle kas kar band karo. Ab plunger ko dheere dabao aur mehsoos karo ki kitna zor lag raha hai. Phir plunger ko khinchne ki koshish karo: wo waapas apni jagah ki taraf jaana chahega. **Syringe kisi ki taraf nahi karni, plunger ko poora bahar nahi nikalna aur isme koi dawa, paani ya chemical nahi bharna.** Kuch bhi garam nahi karna.

## Part E: Solved examples

(Sab hisaab constant temperature aur constant matra par maane gaye hain. Jahan "illustrative" likha ho, wahan ye kahani wali sankhya hai, asli measurement nahi.)

**Example 1: syringe ka pressure.** Syringe mein 20 mL hawa 1 atm par hai. Plunger se dabaakar 10 mL aur phir 5 mL ki gayi. Dono sthitiyon mein pressure kya hoga?

- P₁V₁ = P₂V₂ se P₂ = P₁V₁ ÷ V₂.
- 10 mL par: (1 × 20) ÷ 10 = **2 atm**
- 5 mL par: (1 × 20) ÷ 5 = **4 atm**

**Jaanch:** P × V: 1 × 20 = 20, 2 × 10 = 20, 4 × 5 = 20 ✅ (har baar 20).

**Example 2: volume nikalo.** 600 mL gas 1 atm par hai. Use dabaakar 3 atm pressure tak laaya gaya. Naya volume kya hai?

- V₂ = P₁V₁ ÷ P₂ = (1 × 600) ÷ 3 = **200 mL**

**Jaanch:** 3 × 200 = 600 = 1 × 600 ✅. Pressure 3 guna, to volume 3 guna kam.

**Example 3: gas ko phailne do.** Ek lambe piston-cylinder mein 2 L gas 4 atm par hai. Piston peeche khincha gaya jab tak pressure 1 atm na ho jaaye. Volume kitna hua?

- V₂ = (4 × 2) ÷ 1 = **8 L**

**Jaanch:** 1 × 8 = 8 = 4 × 2 ✅. Pressure 4 guna kam, volume 4 guna zyada.

**Example 4: mm Hg mein.** 500 mL gas 760 mm Hg par hai. Pressure ghatakar 380 mm Hg kiya gaya. Naya volume?

- V₂ = (760 × 500) ÷ 380 = **1000 mL** (= 1 L)

**Jaanch:** 380 × 1000 = 380 000 = 760 × 500 ✅. Yahan atm mein badalne ki zaroorat nahi padi, kyunki dono pressure ek hi unit mein the.

**Example 5: bicycle pump.** Pump ke barrel mein 1000 cm³ hawa 1 atm par hai. Valve band hai aur pump dheere dabaakar barrel ka volume 250 cm³ kar diya gaya. Barrel mein hawa ka pressure kitna hua?

- P₂ = (1 × 1000) ÷ 250 = **4 atm**

**Jaanch:** 4 × 250 = 1000 ✅. (Tyre ka pressure isse alag hota hai aur zyada hawa jaati rehti hai; ye bas ek barrel ka hisaab hai. Asli pump garam bhi hota hai.)

**Example 6: kya ye readings Boyle ke niyam se milti hain?** Do students ne apne measurements likhe:

| Student A: P (atm) | V (mL) | Student B: P (atm) | V (mL) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 48 | 1 | 30 |
| 1 | 24 | 2 | 15 |
| 2 | 12 | 3 | 10 |
| 2.5 | 9.6 | 4 | 8 |
| 4 | 6 | 5 | 6 |

- Student A: P × V = 24, 24, 24, 24, 24. **Sab barabar:** Boyle ka niyam theek baithta hai.
- Student B: P × V = 30, 30, 30, 32, 30. **P = 4 atm wali reading gadbad hai.** Constant 30 hai, to P = 4 par V = 30 ÷ 4 = **7.5 mL** hona chahiye tha, 8 nahi.

**Jaanch:** 4 × 7.5 = 30 ✅. Student B ne shayad volume padhne mein thodi galti ki, ya plunger thoda phisal gaya.

**Example 7: paani ke andar ki bubble.** Talab mein 20 m gehrayi par ek bubble ka volume 1 mL hai. Wahan kul pressure lagbhag 1 atm (hawa) + 20 m ÷ 10 m per atm = 3 atm hai. Bubble surface par (1 atm) pahunchegi to uska volume kitna hoga? (Temperature wahi maano; ye lagbhag hisaab hai.)

- V₂ = (3 × 1) ÷ 1 = **3 mL**

**Jaanch:** 1 × 3 = 3 ✅. Bubble 3 guna badi ho gayi. Isiliye bubbles upar aate aate badi hoti dikhti hain.

**Example 8: saans mein pressure ka farak.** Saans lene se pehle phephdon mein 2.00 L hawa 760 mm Hg par hai. Diaphragm ke neeche jaane se volume 2.01 L ho gaya. Phephdon ke andar ka pressure kitna ghata? (Illustrative: asli farak bhi sirf kuch mm Hg ka hi hota hai.)

- P₂ = (760 × 2.00) ÷ 2.01 = **756.2 mm Hg** (1 decimal tak)
- Kami = 760 − 756.2 = **3.8 mm Hg**

**Jaanch:** 756.2 × 2.01 = 1520 ≈ 760 × 2.00 = 1520 ✅. Itne chhote pressure ke farak se bahar ki 760 mm Hg wali hawa phephdon mein dhakel di jaati hai.

**Example 9: pahaad se utarti bottle.** Pahaad par ek patli plastic ki bottle mein 1.0 L hawa 0.90 atm par band ki gayi. Neeche laane par bahar ka pressure 1.00 atm hai aur bottle itni lachili hai ki andar-bahar ka pressure barabar ho jaata hai. Bottle ka naya volume kya hoga? (Lagbhag; temperature wahi maano.)

- V₂ = (0.90 × 1.0) ÷ 1.00 = **0.90 L**

**Jaanch:** 1.00 × 0.90 = 0.90 = 0.90 × 1.0 ✅. Bottle 10% pichak gayi.

**Example 10: "badhaya gaya" ka matlab.** 3 L gas 2 atm par hai. Pressure **2 atm aur badhaya** gaya (kul pressure 4 atm). Naya volume?

- P₂ = 2 + 2 = 4 atm (kul pressure), **kul** pressure hi formula mein jaata hai.
- V₂ = (2 × 3) ÷ 4 = **1.5 L**

**Jaanch:** 4 × 1.5 = 6 = 2 × 3 ✅. (Agar koi galti se P₂ = 2 le leta to V₂ = 3 L aata, yaani kuch badla hi nahi.)

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Pressure double to volume bhi double."**
Nahi. Boyle ka niyam ulta hai: pressure double to volume **aadha**. Dono ka **guna-fal** constant rehta hai.

**Galti 2: P₁ aur P₂ ko alag units mein lena.**
Ek atm mein aur ek mm Hg mein likha ho to pehle sab ek unit mein badlo. Jaise Example 4 mein dono mm Hg mein the.

**Galti 3: Boyle ka niyam har halat mein laga dena.**
Ye tabhi chalta hai jab temperature aur matra constant ho. Agar gas bahar nikal rahi hai ya garam ho rahi hai, to ye niyam seedha nahi lagega.

**Galti 4: "Pressure 2 atm badhaya" ko P₂ = 2 maan lena.**
"Badhaya" ka matlab hai purane mein jodna. P₂ **kul** pressure hai (Example 10).

**Galti 5: P + V constant samajhna.**
Constant to **P × V** hai, P + V nahi. Example 1 mein 1 + 20 = 21 aur 2 + 10 = 12; ye barabar nahi hain, par 1 × 20 = 2 × 10.

**Galti 6: "Boyle ka niyam real gas ke liye hamesha poora sahi hai."**
Bahut zyada pressure aur bahut kam temperature par asli gas is niyam se thoda hat jaati hai (aage ki class mein). Class 9 ke hisaab mein hum har gas ko aisi gas maante hain jo niyam ko poori tarah maanti hai.

**Galti 7: Paani ke andar ka pressure sirf paani ka maan lena.**
Paani mein gehrayi par kul pressure = bahar ka 1 atm **plus** paani ka dabav (Example 7).

## Quick Recap

- Constant temperature aur matra par: **P × V = constant**, yaani **P₁V₁ = P₂V₂**. (Boyle, 1662)
- Volume aadha, to pressure double. Volume 3 guna kam, to pressure 3 guna zyada.
- **Kan-wali wajah:** kan wahi hain, jagah kam, isliye deewar par per second zyada takkar, yaani zyada pressure.
- **P-V graph** jhuka hua curve (hyperbola) hai; **P aur 1/V graph** origin se guzarti seedhi line hai.
- P₁, P₂ ek unit mein aur V₁, V₂ ek unit mein hone chahiye; kul (total) pressure use karo.
- Uses: syringe, pump, paani mein bubble, saans lena, pahaad se laayi band bottle.
- Safety: syringe bina needle ki, teacher ya adult ke saath; kisi band ya garam cheez ko dabaana ya garam karna nahi.

## Practice Questions

1. Explain, using the idea of gas particles hitting the walls, why the pressure of a gas goes up when its volume is made smaller at constant temperature.

2. A needle-less syringe holds 50 mL of air at 1 atm with the nozzle sealed. The plunger is pushed slowly until the volume is 20 mL. Find the new pressure.

3. A gas occupies 5 L at 3 atm. What volume will it occupy at 1.5 atm, the temperature being constant?

4. A sample of gas occupies 250 mL at 380 mm Hg. The pressure is increased to 950 mm Hg at constant temperature. Find the new volume in mL.

5. A student records these readings for a fixed sample of gas at constant temperature:

   | P (atm) | 1 | 2 | 4 | 5 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | V (mL) | 20 | 10 | 5 | 3 |

   One reading is wrong. Find it and give the correct value.

6. A small bubble of volume 2 mL is at a depth of 10 m in a lake, where the total pressure is about 2 atm. What will its volume be at the surface, where the pressure is 1 atm?

7. A football of fixed volume 2.0 L already contains air at 1 atm. A hand pump delivers 0.25 L of air at 1 atm per stroke. If all the air is pushed into the ball at constant temperature, how many strokes are needed to raise the pressure inside the ball to 1.5 atm?

8. A fixed amount of gas has P × V = 12 atm L. Find (i) its pressure when V = 3 L, (ii) its pressure at 1/V = 0.5 per litre. What is the slope of the line in a P against 1/V graph?

9. A sealed 1.5 L bottle of air is closed on a hill where the pressure is 0.92 atm. Assuming it can shrink freely and the temperature does not change, what volume will it have when brought down to a place where the pressure is 1.00 atm?

10. A student says: "When I pushed the plunger of a sealed syringe very quickly, P × V did not stay exactly constant." Give one reason and say what condition of Boyle's law was not followed.

11. State True or False and correct the false ones:
    (a) When the volume of a gas becomes half, its pressure becomes half.
    (b) At constant temperature, P × V of a fixed gas sample does not change.
    (c) The graph of P against 1/V for a gas at constant temperature is a straight line through the origin.
    (d) Boyle's law can be applied to a gas leaking out of a cylinder.

### Answers

1. Gas ke kan deewar se takrate hain, aur wahi takkarein pressure banati hain. Volume ghatne par wahi kan chhoti jagah mein aa jaate hain, to ek kan ko deewar tak ki doori kam tay karni padti hai aur wo **per second zyada baar** takrata hai. Temperature wahi hai, isliye kanon ki speed wahi hai, aur zyada takkaron ki wajah se pressure badh jaata hai.

2. P₂ = (1 × 50) ÷ 20 = **2.5 atm**.
   Jaanch: 2.5 × 20 = 50 = 1 × 50 ✅.

3. V₂ = (3 × 5) ÷ 1.5 = **10 L**.
   Jaanch: 1.5 × 10 = 15 = 3 × 5 ✅.

4. V₂ = (380 × 250) ÷ 950 = **100 mL**.
   Jaanch: 950 × 100 = 95 000 = 380 × 250 ✅. Pressure 2.5 guna hua, volume 2.5 guna kam.

5. P × V: 1 × 20 = 20, 2 × 10 = 20, 4 × 5 = 20, 5 × 3 = 15. **Aakhri reading (V = 3 mL) galat hai**; 5 atm par V = 20 ÷ 5 = **4 mL** hona chahiye.
   Jaanch: 5 × 4 = 20 ✅.

6. V₂ = (2 × 2) ÷ 1 = **4 mL**.
   Jaanch: 1 × 4 = 4 = 2 × 2 ✅. Bubble double ho gayi.

7. Ball ka volume fixed hai (2.0 L). 1.5 atm par 2.0 L hawa ke liye 1 atm par kul hawa chahiye: V = (1.5 × 2.0) ÷ 1 = 3.0 L. Ball mein pehle se 2.0 L (1 atm par) hai, to aur 3.0 − 2.0 = 1.0 L chahiye. Strokes = 1.0 ÷ 0.25 = **4**.
   Jaanch: 4 × 0.25 = 1.0 L, aur 2.0 + 1.0 = 3.0 L at 1 atm, jo 2.0 L mein dabane par 3.0 ÷ 2.0 = 1.5 atm ✅.

8. (i) P = 12 ÷ 3 = **4 atm**. (ii) 1/V = 0.5 per litre ka matlab V = 2 L, P = 12 ÷ 2 = **6 atm**. Line ki slope = P ÷ (1/V) = P × V = **12 atm L**.
   Jaanch: 4 × 3 = 12 ✅, 6 × 2 = 12 ✅.
