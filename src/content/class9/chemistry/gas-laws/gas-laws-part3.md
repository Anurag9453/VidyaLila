# Topic 3: Charles Ka Niyam: Garam Karo, Gas Phailti Hai

Topic 2 mein humne Boyle ka niyam dekha: **temperature aur gas ki matra ko pakka rakh kar** dabav badhao to aayatan ghatta hai, aur P × V ek constant rehta hai. Wahan hum ek cheez badalte the aur baaki ko sthir rakhte the. Aaj hum doosra jodi chunte hain: **dabav ko pakka rakho (aur gas ki matra bhi), aur temperature badlo.** Sawal: garam karne par gas ka aayatan kitna badhta hai? Aur kya iska koi seedha, gin sakne wala rishta hai? Chalo ek mele ke maidan mein chalte hain.

---

## Utsav ke maidan mein ek bada gubbara

Shaam ke saat baje the. Shehar ke utsav ke maidan mein ek rang-birangi, dus-manzila ooncha **hot-air balloon** zameen par pada tha, aur uski tokri mein ek mota-sa burner laga tha. Mr. Raut apne bhanje Ruhan ko lekar wahan pahunche the. Pilot ne burner khola, ek tez "fuuuush" ki awaaz hui, aur lau balloon ke neeche ke khule munh ke andar gai. Dekhte hi dekhte dheele-dheele kapde ne saans bhari, phoola, seedha khada hua, aur dheere-dheere zameen chhod kar upar uthne laga.

Ruhan ki aankhein phail gayi. "Mama, ye to hawa se bhara hai na? Hawa to hamare chaaron taraf bhi hai. Phir ye upar kyun chala gaya? Aur burner ka kaam kya hai, hawa ko garam karne se kya hota hai?"

Mr. Raut ne muskuraake kaha, "Bilkul sahi sawal. Is balloon mein **koi khaas halki gas nahi hai**, sirf wahi hawa jo hamare chaaron taraf hai. Bas wo **garam** hai. Aur garam hone par gas ke saath ek seedhi si baat hoti hai: **wo phailti hai**. Is phailne ka rishta bahut saaf hai, itna saaf ki 1787 mein ek French vaigyanik, **Jacques Charles**, ne use naap kar ek niyam bana diya. Wo khud bhi gubbare udaane wale logon mein se the. Aaj hum bhi wahi naapenge: temperature kitna badhe to aayatan kitna badhta hai."

## Part A: Charles ka niyam: garam karo, gas phailti hai

Pehle ek chhoti si soch. Suraj mein rakhe gubbare ko dekho: gas garam hone par phailti hai. Aur jaise Physics ke chapter mein dekha tha (thermal expansion), garam karne par cheezein phailti hain, par **gas mein ye phailna solid ya liquid se bahut zyada hota hai**, isliye gas par ye bahut saaf dikhta hai.

**Charles ka niyam (words mein):** gas ki ek matra par **dabav constant** rakhen, to uska **aayatan (V) uske Kelvin temperature (T) ke seedhe anupaat mein** badalta hai.

Ganit mein:

**V ÷ T = constant**  (T kelvin mein)

Do avasthaon ke liye likhein to:

**V₁ ÷ T₁ = V₂ ÷ T₂**

Is formula ko aise padho: "Pehle aayatan V₁ tha aur temperature T₁; garam ya thanda karne ke baad aayatan V₂ aur temperature T₂ hai, **par dabav aur gas ki matra wahi hain**." Agar temperature double hoga (Kelvin mein), to aayatan double hoga. Agar temperature aadha hoga, to aayatan aadha.

### Pehli numerical jhalak: Jacques Charles ke tareeke se

Prayogon mein dekha gaya ki gas 0 °C se jab 1 °C garam hoti hai to uska aayatan 0 °C wale aayatan ka lagbhag **1/273** hissa badhta hai. Ye 273 wahi number hai jo K = °C + 273 mein aata hai. Isi se seedha rishta niklta hai: 273 mL gas 0 °C par ho, to 1 °C par 274 mL, 27 °C par 300 mL, aur 100 °C par 373 mL (neeche Example 6).

### Ek zaroori shart: temperature sirf Kelvin mein

Charles ke niyam mein **T hamesha Kelvin mein** likhna hai, Celsius mein nahi. Kyun? Kyunki "double" aur "aadha" jaisi baatein tab hi sahi baithti hain jab temperature ka **zero asli zero** ho (kuch bhi garmi nahi). Celsius ka 0 °C to sirf barf ke pighalne ka point hai, jo apni suvidha ke liye chuna gaya tha. 0 °C par bhi gas ke kan bahut tezi se bhaag rahe hain. Isiliye Celsius se hisaab lagaoge to galat jawab aayega (Example 2 mein ye saaf dikhega). Kelvin ki baat Chapter 1 mein ho chuki hai: **K = °C + 273**.

Ek chhota sa hisaab dekho: pehle V₁ = 2.0 L aur T₁ = 300 K (27 °C). Baad mein T₂ = 360 K (87 °C). Tab V₂ = V₁ × T₂ ÷ T₁ = 2.0 × 360 ÷ 300 = 2.4 L. Temperature 1.2 guna hua, to aayatan bhi 1.2 guna.

## Part B: Hot-air balloon upar kyun uthta hai?

Ab Ruhan ke sawaal par wapas. Balloon ke neeche ka munh **khula** hai. Isliye andar ki hawa ka dabav bahar ke dabav ke barabar hi rehta hai: constant dabav ki shart apne aap poori hai. Burner chalu hone par andar ki hawa garam hoti hai, aur Charles ke niyam se wo **phailna chahti hai**. Balloon ka kapda to ek fixed size ka thaila hai, to phailti hawa ka kuch hissa **neeche ke khule munh se bahar nikal jaata hai**.

           /  o   o   o   o   \

                 /\     /\

> **Figure 3.1** – Hot-air balloon: neeche ka munh khula hai, isliye andar ka dabav bahar ke barabar rehta hai. Burner andar ki hawa ko garam karta hai; garam hawa phailti hai aur uska kuch hissa munh se bahar nikal jaata hai. Balloon ke andar ki hawa ke kan zyada tez aur door-door hain. (Drawing sirf bhaav dikhane ke liye hai.)

Ab dekho kya hua. Balloon ka size wahi hai, par andar hawa **kam** reh gayi (kuch bahar nikal gayi), yaani andar ki hawa **halki** ho gayi (density kam). Aur jab balloon + tokri + andar ki hawa ka kul vazan ek hi size ki **bahar ki thandi hawa** ke vazan se kam ho jaata hai, to balloon ko upar ki taraf dhakka milta hai (Physics ke chapter "Pressure and Fluids" ka tairne ka idea). Bas, wahi utthan hai.

Isse ek aur baat nikalti hai: balloon ko neeche lana ho to pilot burner band kar deta hai, andar ki hawa thandi hoti hai aur wapas ghani ho jaati hai.

**Zaroori safety baat:** hot-air balloon ek khule munh waala balloon hai, aur uska burner training aur poori safety wale pilots hi chalate hain. Ghar ya school mein hum kabhi **lau, burner ya band bottle ko garam karne ka experiment nahi karte**. LPG cylinder, aerosol (spray) can aur tyre ko kabhi garam nahi karna, aur kabhi dhoop mein ya garam jagah par nahi rakhna. (Iska karan Topic 4 mein pata chalega.)

### Absolute zero: line kahan khatam hoti hai?

Graph ki sabse chaunkane wali baat ye hai. Hum jis bhi gas par ye experiment karein (alag dabav, alag matra), har line peeche kheenchne par **ek hi point par** aakar aayatan zero tak pahunchti dikhti hai: **−273 °C par.** Alag gas ho, alag mool aayatan ho, par ye point wahi rehta hai. Isi point ko **absolute zero** kehte hain, aur Kelvin scale ka 0 K yahi hai (thoda sa aur sahi: −273.15 °C; hum 273 se kaam chalate hain).

Isliye Kelvin scale itna asaan hai: 0 K = −273 °C, aur uske neeche koi temperature nahi hota. Isliye Kelvin mein negative number kabhi nahi milta.

Is line ke baare mein do imaandar baatein:

1. **Ye graph ek extrapolation hai.** Humne temperature 0 °C se 200 °C tak naapa, aur aage ki line ko peeche kheench kar dekha. Jaise kisi seedhi sadak ko dekhkar andaaza lagana ki wo aage kahan jaati hai.
2. **Asli gas −273 °C tak pahunch hi nahi paati.** Pehle hi wo **liquid ban jaati hai** (nitrogen −196 °C par, oxygen −183 °C par, aur sabse mushkil helium bhi lagbhag −269 °C par), aur liquid par Charles ka niyam lagu nahi hota. Isiliye "aayatan zero ho jaata hai" ka matlab ye **nahi** ki gas ka koi aayatan nahi bachta. Matlab sirf ye hai ki **agar** gas gas hi bani rehti (jo hota nahi), to niyam kehta ki aayatan zero ho jaata. Absolute zero ek lowest limit hai: usse neeche koi temperature nahi, aur us tak kabhi pahunchna bhi nahi ho paata. (Asli gas bahut zyada dabav ya bahut kam temperature par niyam se hat jaati hai: ye aage ki class mein.)

## Part D: Particles ke hisaab se: gas phailti kyun hai?

Chapter 1 ka particle picture yaad karo: gas ke kan door-door, random aur tez bhaagte hain, aur deewar se takrakar dabav banate hain (Topic 1 mein wo dabav hum dekh chuke hain). Ab garam karne par kya badalta hai?

- Temperature badhne ka matlab kan **zyada tez** bhaagte hain.
- Tez kan deewar se **zyada zor se** aur **zyada baar** takrate hain. Agar jagah wahi rehti, to dabav badh jaata.
- Par humne dabav **constant** rakha hai (kyunki deewar chhoot se hil sakti hai: gubbare ki tvacha, ya syringe ka halka piston). Isliye deewar **bahar ki taraf hat jaati hai**, aayatan badhta hai, kan ab zyada jagah mein ghoomte hain, aur deewar par har second takraav ghat kar wapas pehle jaisa dabav ho jaata hai.

Socho ki thande gas ke kan dheere bhaag rahe hain aur chhoti jagah mein hain; garam gas ke kan tez bhaagte hain aur deewar ko door dhakel kar badi jagah bana lete hain, taaki dabav wahi rahe.

Mazedaar baat: garam karne par kanon ki **sankhya** nahi badalti. Sirf unki **raftaar** aur unhe milne wali **jagah** badalti hai. Isliye V ÷ T constant rehta hai: temperature double to kanon ki raftaar ka asar double, aur dabav wahi rakhne ke liye jagah bhi double.

## Part E: Roz ki zindagi mein Charles ka niyam

- **Hot-air balloon:** garam hawa phailti hai, kuch bahar nikalti hai, balloon halka hokar uthta hai (Example 7 aur 8).
- **Gubbara garmi mein phoolta, sardi mein pichakta hai:** garam jagah par andar ki hawa phailti hai, thandi jagah par sikudti hai (Activity 1 mein khud dekho).
- **Garam hawa upar uthti hai:** heater ke upar ki hawa phailkar halki hoti hai aur upar chadhti hai, balloon wali hi baat.
- **Band, sakht container mein ye niyam nahi lagta:** cylinder ya aerosol can ka aayatan badal nahi sakta, to garam hone par **dabav** badhta hai, jo khatarnak hai (Topic 4).

**Activity 1 (ghar par adult ke saath): gubbara garam aur thande paani ke upar.**

Saamaan: ek khaali **plastic** ki chhoti bottle, ek gubbara, do bade katore ya bartan, thanda paani (aur chahein to barf), garam paani (adult taiyaar karega), ek dhaaga aur scale.

1. Adult gubbare ko pehle ek-do baar phoola kar dheela kar deta hai, phir hawa nikaal kar uska munh bottle ke munh par khinch kar chadha deta hai (gubbara bottle ki hawa ko chhoot se phailne ya sikudne deta hai, isliye dabav lagbhag constant rehta hai).
2. **Garam paani sirf adult** katore mein daalega, itna garam ki haath se chhoone par aaram se sahan ho jaye (nahane ke garam paani jitna, ubalta paani nahi). Bottle ko us katore mein khada karo, paani ka level bottle ki aadhi oonchai tak. **Koi lau ya stove paas mein nahi.**
3. Kuch minute baad gubbare ko dekho: wo thoda **phool gaya** hoga. Dhaaga gubbare ke beech mein lapetkar nap lo aur likh lo.
4. Ab usi bottle ko thande paani (ya barf wale paani) ke katore mein rakho. Gubbara **pichakne** lagega. Dhaage se nap lo.

Dhyan do: ye chhota farak hai (kuch percent), isliye dhaage se dhyan se nap kar dekhna. **Kabhi bottle ko lau, stove ya ubalte paani ke paas na le jao, aur sakht band bottle ko garam mat karo: wo phat sakti hai.**

## Part F: Solved examples

(Sab hisaab ke liye: dabav aur gas ki matra constant. T hamesha Kelvin mein: K = °C + 273. Jawab sensible rounding ke saath diye gaye hain.)

**Example 1: garam karne par aayatan.** 2.0 L gas 27 °C par hai. Constant dabav par ise 87 °C tak garam karte hain. Naya aayatan kya hoga?

- T₁ = 27 + 273 = 300 K, T₂ = 87 + 273 = 360 K
- V₂ = V₁ × T₂ ÷ T₁ = 2.0 × 360 ÷ 300 = **2.4 L**

**Jaanch:** V₁ ÷ T₁ = 2.0 ÷ 300 = 0.00667 aur V₂ ÷ T₂ = 2.4 ÷ 360 = 0.00667 ✅. Temperature 1.2 guna hua, aayatan bhi 1.2 guna ✅.

**Example 2: Celsius mein galat jawab.** Wahi sawal Celsius se hal karein to kya jawab aayega?

- Galat tareeka: V₂ = 2.0 × 87 ÷ 27 = **6.44 L** (galat!)
- Sahi jawab 2.4 L tha (Example 1).

**Jaanch:** 27 °C se 87 °C tak temperature sirf 60 degree badha, jo mool 300 K ka sirf 20% hai, to aayatan lagbhag 20% (2.0 se 2.4) hi badhna chahiye, na ki teen guna ✅. Celsius se jawab 3.2 guna aa gaya, jo bilkul bekaar hai.

**Example 3: thanda karne par aayatan.** 600 mL gas 47 °C par hai. Constant dabav par ise −33 °C tak thanda karte hain. Naya aayatan?

- T₁ = 47 + 273 = 320 K, T₂ = −33 + 273 = 240 K
- V₂ = 600 × 240 ÷ 320 = **450 mL**

**Jaanch:** 450 ÷ 240 = 1.875 aur 600 ÷ 320 = 1.875 ✅. (Temperature ka anupaat 240/320 = 3/4, to aayatan bhi 3/4: 600 × 3/4 = 450 ✅.)

**Example 4: kitne temperature tak garam karein?** 300 mL gas 27 °C par hai. Kis temperature (°C mein) par iska aayatan 450 mL ho jaayega? (dabav constant)

- T₁ = 300 K. V₂ ÷ V₁ = 450 ÷ 300 = 1.5, to T₂ = 300 × 1.5 = 450 K
- Celsius mein: 450 − 273 = **177 °C**

**Jaanch:** 450 ÷ 450 = 1.0 aur 300 ÷ 300 = 1.0 ✅. Ye bhi dekho: "aayatan 1.5 guna" ke liye Celsius 27 se 177 hua, yaani lagbhag 6.6 guna: isliye Celsius se andaaza lagana galat hai.

**Example 5: readings ka table: kya V ÷ T constant hai?** Ek student ne ek gas ki readings li:

| T (K) | 250 | 300 | 350 | 400 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V (L) | 5.0 | 6.0 | 7.0 | 8.0 |

- V ÷ T: 5.0 ÷ 250 = 0.020, 6.0 ÷ 300 = 0.020, 7.0 ÷ 350 = 0.020, 8.0 ÷ 400 = 0.020
- Har reading mein **0.020 L per K** milta hai, to Charles ka niyam sahi baithta hai.

**Jaanch:** 0.020 × 400 = 8.0 ✅. Is gas ka aayatan **har 1 K par 0.020 L (20 mL)** badhta hai.

**Example 6: 273 mL ka Charles wala nazaara.** 0 °C par 273 mL gas hai, dabav constant. 1 °C, 27 °C aur 100 °C par aayatan?

- 1 °C = 274 K: V = 273 × 274 ÷ 273 = **274 mL**
- 27 °C = 300 K: V = 273 × 300 ÷ 273 = **300 mL**
- 100 °C = 373 K: V = 273 × 373 ÷ 273 = **373 mL**

**Jaanch:** Har 1 °C par bilkul 1 mL badhta hai (273 mL ka 1/273 hissa) ✅. Aur 273 + 100 = 373 ✅. Ye Figure 3.2 wale graph ki line hai.

**Example 7: hot-air balloon ki hawa.** Balloon ke andar 1000 m³ hawa 27 °C par hai. Burner chalane par hawa 127 °C tak garam ho jaati hai (dabav constant, kyunki neeche munh khula hai). (a) Agar hawa bahar na nikalti to uska aayatan kitna hota? (b) Balloon ka size 1000 m³ hi rahta hai: kitni hawa bahar nikal jaayegi (fraction)?

- T₁ = 300 K, T₂ = 127 + 273 = 400 K
- (a) V₂ = 1000 × 400 ÷ 300 = **1333 m³** (lagbhag, yaani 1333.3)
- (b) Balloon mein sirf 1000 m³ jagah hai. Bacha hua hissa = 1000 ÷ 1333.3 = **0.75** (3/4). Bahar gayi hawa = 1 − 0.75 = **0.25**, yaani **25%** hawa.

**Jaanch:** 300 ÷ 400 = 0.75 ✅ (hissa seedha T₁ ÷ T₂). Balloon ke andar ki hawa 25% kam ho gayi, isliye wo halki ho gayi.

**Example 8: balloon ka utthan (lagbhag).** Hawa ki density 0 °C (273 K) par lagbhag 1.29 kg per m³ hoti hai (is value ko Topic 5 mein samjhenge). Constant dabav par density T ke ulte anupaat mein badalti hai. 27 °C aur 127 °C par hawa ki density aur 1000 m³ ke liye utthan kya hoga?

- 27 °C (300 K): 1.29 × 273 ÷ 300 = **1.17 kg per m³** (lagbhag)
- 127 °C (400 K): 1.29 × 273 ÷ 400 = **0.88 kg per m³** (lagbhag)
- 1000 m³ ke liye farak: (1.1739 − 0.8804) × 1000 = **293 kg** (lagbhag)

**Jaanch:** 1.17 × 300 = 351 aur 0.88 × 400 = 352: dono lagbhag barabar ✅ (density × T constant). Yaani 1000 m³ hawa ko 127 °C tak garam karne par lagbhag 290 kg ka utthan milta hai. (Ye mota andaaza hai; asli balloon ka design aur hisaab zyada jatil hai.)

**Example 9: absolute zero data se nikalna.** Ek nali mein band gas ka aayatan 27 °C par 300 mL aur 127 °C par 400 mL mila (dabav constant). Dono readings se line kis temperature par aayatan zero tak jaati dikhti hai?

- Slope = (400 − 300) ÷ (127 − 27) = 100 ÷ 100 = **1 mL per °C**
- Aayatan 300 mL se zero tak jaane ke liye 300 mL ghatana hoga, yaani 300 °C peeche: 27 − 300 = **−273 °C**

**Jaanch:** Kelvin mein: V ÷ T = 300 ÷ 300 = 1.0 aur 400 ÷ 400 = 1.0 ✅ (V = T mL, to V = 0 par T = 0 K = −273 °C ✅).

**Example 10: Celsius double hone ka bharam.** Ek 5 L gas 20 °C par hai. Use 40 °C tak garam karte hain (temperature "double"). Kya aayatan double hoga?

- T₁ = 293 K, T₂ = 313 K
- V₂ = 5 × 313 ÷ 293 = **5.34 L** (lagbhag; 2 decimal place par 5.34)

**Jaanch:** Celsius double hone ke baad bhi Kelvin mein sirf 313 ÷ 293 = 1.068 guna hua, isliye aayatan 5 × 1.068 = 5.34 L ✅. Aayatan double nahi hua (double hota to 10 L). Aayatan tab double hota jab **Kelvin** temperature double hota: 293 K se 586 K (yaani 313 °C).

**Example 11: freezer ka trap.** 1.0 L gas 27 °C par hai. Use freezer mein −23 °C tak thanda karte hain (dabav constant). Sahi aayatan? Celsius se karein to kya ajeeb baat dikhegi?

- Sahi: T₁ = 300 K, T₂ = 250 K, V₂ = 1.0 × 250 ÷ 300 = **0.83 L**
- Celsius se: V₂ = 1.0 × (−23) ÷ 27 = **−0.85 L**: negative aayatan sambhav hi nahi!

**Jaanch:** 0.83 ÷ 250 = 0.00333 aur 1.0 ÷ 300 = 0.00333 ✅. Negative aayatan ye saaf bata deta hai ki Celsius mein hisaab karna galat hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Temperature Celsius mein daalna.**
Charles ke niyam mein T Kelvin mein hi hota hai: Example 2 mein 6.44 L aaya jo galat tha, sahi 2.4 L tha. Pehle hamesha K = °C + 273.

**Galti 2: "Celsius double to aayatan double."**
Example 10 mein 20 °C se 40 °C par aayatan sirf 5 L se 5.34 L hua, 10 L nahi. Double sirf Kelvin mein hota hai.

**Galti 3: Dabav ko badalne dena.**
Charles ka niyam tabhi lagta hai jab dabav constant ho. Band sakht dabbe mein aayatan badalta hi nahi, to wahan ye niyam lagta hi nahi (wahan dabav badhta hai: Topic 4).

**Galti 4: "Absolute zero par gas ka aayatan zero hota hai."**
Asli gas −273 °C tak pahunchne se pehle liquid ban jaati hai, aur absolute zero tak koi pahunch hi nahi sakta. Graph ka −273 °C par pahunchna ek extrapolation hai.

**Galti 5: "Garam karne par gas ke kan zyada ho jaate hain."**
Nahi. Kan utne hi rehte hain (matra constant), bas tez bhaagte hain aur zyada jagah mein phail jaate hain.

**Galti 6: Boyle aur Charles ka formula ulta karna.**
Boyle: P₁V₁ = P₂V₂ (T constant), jisme V ulte anupaat mein hai. Charles: V₁ ÷ T₁ = V₂ ÷ T₂ (P constant), jisme V seedhe anupaat mein hai. Charles mein temperature badhe to aayatan **badhta** hai, Boyle mein dabav badhe to aayatan **ghatta** hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Charles ka niyam:** dabav aur matra constant ho to V ÷ T = constant, yaani V₁ ÷ T₁ = V₂ ÷ T₂, **T Kelvin mein**.
- K = °C + 273. Celsius mein hisaab karoge to jawab galat aayega (Example 2).
- Temperature (K) double to aayatan double; Celsius double hone se aayatan double nahi hota.
- V-T line ko −273 °C tak peeche kheenchne par aayatan zero tak pahunchta dikhta hai: ye **absolute zero** (0 K) hai.
- Asli gas −273 °C se pehle liquid ban jaati hai, aur absolute zero tak koi nahi pahunch sakta: graph ek extrapolation hai.
- Particles: garam karne par kan tez hote hain; dabav constant rakhne ke liye gas phailti hai. Kanon ki sankhya nahi badalti.
- Hot-air balloon: garam hawa phailti hai, kuch bahar nikalti hai, andar ki hawa halki, balloon upar.
- Safety: LPG cylinder, aerosol can aur tyre kabhi garam na karo ya dhoop mein na rakho; garam paani sirf adult; band sakht dabbe ko kabhi garam nahi karna.

## Practice Questions

1. A syringe-like cylinder with a movable piston holds 800 mL of air at 17 °C. The pressure stays constant while the air is warmed to 77 °C. Find the new volume (round to the nearest mL).

2. A 3.0 L balloon is at 37 °C. It is cooled to 2 °C at constant pressure. Find its new volume (round to two decimal places) and state by what percent it shrinks (one decimal place).

3. A gas sample of 250 cm³ at 20 °C is heated to 100 °C at constant pressure. Find its new volume (one decimal place). Then say whether 100 °C is "5 times" 20 °C and whether the volume became 5 times larger; explain.

4. A 5 L sample at 7 °C is allowed to expand at constant pressure to 7 L. At what temperature (in °C) does it reach 7 L?

5. 120 mL of gas at 127 °C is cooled to 27 °C at constant pressure. Find the new volume, and state what fraction of the original volume remains.

6. A student records these values at constant pressure: 27 °C with 15.0 mL, 77 °C with 17.5 mL, 127 °C with 20.0 mL and 177 °C with 24.0 mL. Convert the temperatures to kelvin and check V ÷ T for each reading. Which reading does not obey Charles' law?

7. An empty plastic bottle has a balloon stretched over its mouth (volume of trapped air about 1.5 L at 25 °C). An adult places the bottle in a bowl of warm water at 45 °C. Estimate the new volume and the percent increase. Why does the effect look small?

8. A hot-air balloon envelope holds 2000 m³. The air inside is heated from 27 °C to 77 °C at constant pressure, and the open mouth lets air escape so that the envelope stays at 2000 m³. What fraction of the air (by mass) is left inside after heating, and what percent has left? (Give the percent to one decimal place.)

9. A student says: "100 °C is twice as hot as 50 °C, so a gas at 100 °C should have double the volume of the same gas at 50 °C (constant pressure)." Show by calculation why the statement is wrong, and give the correct volume ratio (two decimal places).

10. A gas sample has volume 232 mL at 17 °C and 272 mL at 67 °C (constant pressure). Use the two readings to find the temperature at which the straight V-T line would reach zero volume. What name is given to that temperature, and why can a real gas not actually reach it?

11. A steel LPG cylinder must not be kept in the sun. A friend says: "Charles' law says the gas will just expand, so the cylinder is safe." Explain, in 3 to 4 lines, why this reasoning does not apply to a cylinder. (Hint: think about the constant-pressure condition.)

12. State True or False and correct the false ones:
    (a) Charles' law can be used with temperatures in degree Celsius directly.
    (b) At constant pressure, doubling the kelvin temperature doubles the volume.
    (c) When a gas is heated at constant pressure, the number of particles increases.
    (d) A real gas can be cooled to exactly 0 K while remaining a gas.

### Answers

1. T₁ = 17 + 273 = 290 K, T₂ = 77 + 273 = 350 K. V₂ = 800 × 350 ÷ 290 = 965.5 ≈ **966 mL**.
   Jaanch: 800 ÷ 290 = 2.759 aur 965.5 ÷ 350 = 2.759 ✅.

2. T₁ = 310 K, T₂ = 2 + 273 = 275 K. V₂ = 3.0 × 275 ÷ 310 = 2.661 ≈ **2.66 L**. Kami = 3.0 − 2.661 = 0.339 L, percent = 0.339 ÷ 3.0 × 100 = 11.3%, yaani gubbara lagbhag **11.3%** sikud gaya (ek aur tareeka: 1 − 275/310 = 0.113).
   Jaanch: 2.661 ÷ 275 = 0.009677 aur 3.0 ÷ 310 = 0.009677 ✅.

3. T₁ = 293 K, T₂ = 373 K. V₂ = 250 × 373 ÷ 293 = 318.26 ≈ **318.3 cm³**. 100 °C, 20 °C ka 5 guna hai, par aayatan sirf 318.3 ÷ 250 = **1.27 guna** hua, 5 guna nahi. Kyunki Kelvin mein temperature sirf 373 ÷ 293 = 1.27 guna hua; Celsius ke 5 guna ka matlab kuch nahi hai.
   Jaanch: 250 × 1.273 = 318.3 ✅.

4. T₁ = 280 K. T₂ = T₁ × V₂ ÷ V₁ = 280 × 7 ÷ 5 = 392 K. Celsius mein: 392 − 273 = **119 °C**.
   Jaanch: 5 ÷ 280 = 0.017857 aur 7 ÷ 392 = 0.017857 ✅.

5. T₁ = 400 K, T₂ = 300 K. V₂ = 120 × 300 ÷ 400 = **90 mL**. Bacha hua hissa = 90 ÷ 120 = **3/4** (yaani 300 ÷ 400).
   Jaanch: 90 ÷ 300 = 0.30 aur 120 ÷ 400 = 0.30 ✅.

6. Kelvin: 300, 350, 400, 450. V ÷ T: 15.0 ÷ 300 = 0.0500, 17.5 ÷ 350 = 0.0500, 20.0 ÷ 400 = 0.0500, 24.0 ÷ 450 = 0.0533. Pehli teen readings mein V ÷ T = 0.0500 hai, **aakhri reading (177 °C, 24.0 mL)** niyam ke hisaab se nahi baithti. Wahan aayatan 0.0500 × 450 = 22.5 mL hona chahiye tha, shayad naapne mein galti hui.
   Jaanch: 0.0500 × 450 = 22.5 ✅.

7. T₁ = 25 + 273 = 298 K, T₂ = 45 + 273 = 318 K. V₂ = 1.5 × 318 ÷ 298 = 1.60 L. Badhotri = 1.60 − 1.5 = 0.10 L, percent = (318 ÷ 298 − 1) × 100 = **6.7%**. Asar chhota isliye lagta hai kyunki Kelvin mein temperature sirf 298 se 318 hua (sirf 20 K ka badlaav); bade farak ke liye bahut zyada temperature badalna padta, aur wo is activity mein safe nahi hai.
   Jaanch: 1.5 × 1.0671 = 1.6007 ✅.

8. T₁ = 300 K, T₂ = 350 K. Garam hone ke baad bina bahar nikle hawa 2000 × 350 ÷ 300 = 2333 m³ chahti, par jagah 2000 m³ hai, to bacha hua hissa = 2000 ÷ 2333.3 = 300 ÷ 350 = 0.857, yaani **0.857 (6/7)**. Bahar gayi hawa = 1 − 0.857 = 0.143, yaani **14.3%**.
   Jaanch: 0.857 × 350 = 300 ✅.

9. 100 °C = 373 K, 50 °C = 323 K. Aayatan ka anupaat = 373 ÷ 323 = 1.15 (2 decimal) : yaani sirf lagbhag **1.15 guna**, double (2.00) nahi. Galti ye hai ki Celsius ka zero asli zero nahi hai; double sirf Kelvin ke hisaab se hota hai.
   Jaanch: 323 × 2 = 646 K tab double hota (373 °C), jo 100 °C nahi hai ✅.

10. Slope = (272 − 232) ÷ (67 − 17) = 40 ÷ 50 = 0.8 mL per °C. 232 mL ko zero tak laane ke liye 232 ÷ 0.8 = 290 °C peeche jaana hoga: 17 − 290 = **−273 °C**. Is temperature ka naam **absolute zero (0 K)** hai. Asli gas ismein se pehle hi liquid ban jaati hai (aur Charles ka niyam liquid par lagu nahi hota); aur absolute zero tak koi nahi pahunch sakta.
    Jaanch: Kelvin mein V ÷ T = 232 ÷ 290 = 0.8 aur 272 ÷ 340 = 0.8 ✅.

11. Charles ka niyam tab lagta hai jab **dabav constant** ho, yaani gas ko phailne ki chhoot ho (jaise khula gubbara ya halka piston). LPG cylinder **sakht aur band** hai, uska aayatan nahi badal sakta, isliye garam hone par gas phail nahi sakti; usi jagah mein tez kan zyada zor se takrate hain, aur **dabav** khatarnak roop se badhta hai. Isliye cylinder ko dhoop ya garmi mein nahi rakhna chahiye. (Topic 4 mein iska hisaab seekhenge.)

12. (a) **False** ❌. Charles ke niyam mein T Kelvin mein hi hona chahiye, Celsius se jawab galat aata hai (Example 2). (b) **True** ✅. V ÷ T constant hai, T double to V double. (c) **False** ❌. Kanon ki sankhya wahi rehti hai (matra constant); kan sirf tez bhaagte hain aur gas zyada jagah mein phail jaati hai. (d) **False** ❌. Asli gas 0 K se bahut pehle liquid (ya solid) ban jaati hai, aur 0 K tak koi pahunch hi nahi paaya.
