# Topic 4: Dabao Aur Taapman: Pressure-Temperature Niyam aur Combined Gas Equation

Pichhle topics mein humne do niyam seekhe. Boyle: taapman constant ho to pressure aur volume ulte chalte hain (P₁V₁ = P₂V₂). Charles: pressure constant ho to volume aur kelvin taapman saath chalte hain (V₁/T₁ = V₂/T₂). Ek jodi abhi baaki hai: agar **volume ko hi pakad ke rakha jaaye** (gas ek sakht, tight container mein ho), aur **taapman badhe**, to kya badlega? Gas phail nahi sakti, to wo kuch to karegi. Wo kuch hai **pressure**. Aaj ka topic ek chhote se warning label se shuru hota hai, aur phir teeno niyam ek hi equation mein jud jaate hain.

---

## Deodorant ke can ka warning

Mr. Dalvi ki class ke bahar ek din Taarini apne bag se deodorant ka can nikaal rahi thi. Can ke peeche chhote akshar mein likha tha: **"Pressurised container. Garmi aur dhoop se door rakhein. 50 °C se upar na rakhein. Aag ke paas na chhidkein. Khaali hone par bhi na jalayein."** Taarini ne hans ke poochha, "Sir, ek chhoti si deodorant ke can ko itna darane wala warning kyun? Isme to bas gas hai, jo spray karne par nikalti hai."

Mr. Dalvi ruk gaye. "Bahut accha sawaal. Ye warning sirf deodorant par nahi, LPG cylinder, mosquito spray, paint spray, har aerosol can par hota hai. Soch kar batao: can band hai, uski deewarein sakht hain. Andar ki gas ko phailne ki jagah nahi hai. Ab can ko dhoop mein ya garam gaadi ki dickey mein rakh do to gas ke kan **zyada tez** daudne lagte hain. Tez kan deewar se kitni baar aur kitne zor se takrayenge?"

Taarini ne socha, "Zyada baar aur zyada zor se. To pressure badhega?" "Bilkul," Mr. Dalvi bole. "Pressure badhta jaata hai, aur kaafi garam karne par can ki deewar usse sambhal nahi paati, aur **can phat sakta hai**. Aaj hum dekhenge ki ye pressure kitna badhta hai, aur uska ek bahut saaf niyam hai."

**Pehle ek zaroori safety baat, poore chapter ke liye:** LPG cylinder, aerosol can, gas lighter, aur gaadi ka tyre ko **kabhi garam nahi karna**, **dhoop mein ya garam gaadi mein nahi chhodna**, aur **aag ke paas nahi rakhna**. Is topic mein koi bhi experiment band container ko garam karne ka nahi hai. Neeche jo kuch hai, wo sirf **soch aur hisaab (calculation)** hai, kisi ko try nahi karna.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |

## Part A: Pressure-Temperature niyam

Do baatein hamesha yaad rakhni hain: gas ki **matra (n)** constant hai (kuch bahar nahi nikla, kuch andar nahi gaya) aur **volume (V)** constant hai (sakht container).

Taapman badhane par kan tez daudte hain (Chapter 1 ki particle picture aur is chapter ka Topic 1 yaad karo). Pressure deewar par kan ke takrane se aata hai (Physics: force per area). Tez kan, zyada takkar, zyada pressure. Aur Charles ki tarah yahan bhi **kelvin** wali pakki dosti chalti hai:

> **Pressure aur kelvin taapman seedhe anupaat mein hain:** P ∝ T, yaani **P/T = constant**.

Samikaran roop mein:

**P₁/T₁ = P₂/T₂** (T hamesha **kelvin** mein, K = °C + 273)

Pressure ki koi bhi ikai chalti hai (atm, mm Hg, Pa), bas dono taraf wahi ho. Taapman ke liye kelvin zaroori hai, jaise Charles ke niyam mein.

Shabdon mein: kelvin taapman do guna to pressure do guna, taapman aadha to pressure aadha. Ye Charles ka hi saathi hai: Charles mein volume taapman ke saath badhta tha kyunki gas ko jagah mili thi; yahan jagah nahi hai, isliye wahi "phailne ki koshish" pressure ban jaati hai.

**Dhyan do:** ye niyam gas ko garam karne ka bahana nahi hai. Ye sirf samjhata hai ki **taapman kam-zyada ho jaye to** (jaise dhoop, garmi ki raat, ya sardi) band container ka pressure kitna badalta hai.

### Roz ki jagah jahan ye dikhta hai

- **Aerosol can aur LPG cylinder par warning:** Garmi se pressure badhta hai, isliye dhoop aur aag se door.
- **Gaadi ka tyre garmi mein:** tyre ke andar hawa ka volume lagbhag same, taapman badhne par pressure badhta hai. Isliye tyre ka pressure **subah thande tyre mein** (gaadi chalane se pehle) check karna chahiye, aur garmi ke mausam mein tyre ko jyada na bharwaayen.
- **Basketball ya football sardi mein pichak jaati hai:** thandi hawa mein andar ka pressure kam ho jaata hai, ball pichki lagti hai.
- **Pressure cooker:** isme ye niyam kaam karta hai, par uska design (safety valve, whistle) alag baat hai, aur bachche use khud kabhi nahi chalate.

## Part B: "Kaun sa niyam?" table

Teeno niyam ek hi parivaar ke hain. Fark sirf ye hai ki **kya constant hai**. Neeche ka table dekhne se har sawaal mein pehle ye pehchaan sakoge ki kaun sa niyam lagana hai.

```
   Niyam            Constant kya hai    Kaun sa badla         Samikaran
   --------------   ------------------  --------------------  ----------------
   Boyle            T aur n             P aur V (ulte)        P₁ V₁ = P₂ V₂
   Charles          P aur n             V aur T (seedhe)      V₁/T₁ = V₂/T₂
   P-T (Gay-Lussac) V aur n             P aur T (seedhe)      P₁/T₁ = P₂/T₂
   Combined         sirf n              P, V, T teeno         P₁V₁/T₁ = P₂V₂/T₂
```

> **Figure 4.2** – "Kaun sa niyam?" table: har niyam mein gas ki matra (n) constant rehti hai, aur ek aur cheez bhi constant hoti hai. T hamesha kelvin mein. Combined equation tab lagti hai jab teeno P, V, T badal jaayein.

Sawaal padhte hi apne se poochho: **kya constant hai?** Agar sawaal mein likha ho "rigid container", "cylinder", "sealed can" (volume constant), to P-T niyam. "Syringe, taapman same" to Boyle. "Balloon, pressure vahi (khuli hawa)" to Charles. Teeno badal rahe hon (jaise upar jaata weather balloon) to combined.

## Part C: Combined gas equation

Boyle: P₁V₁ = P₂V₂ (T constant). Charles: V₁/T₁ = V₂/T₂ (P constant). P-T: P₁/T₁ = P₂/T₂ (V constant). Ek hi gas ke saath teeno kabhi-kabhi ek saath badalte hain. Tab teeno niyam ek mein mil jaate hain:

**P₁V₁ / T₁ = P₂V₂ / T₂**  (n constant, T kelvin mein)

Ise **combined gas equation** kehte hain. Ye teeno niyam ka "bada bhai" hai, aur teeno uske **special case** hain:

- **T₁ = T₂ ho** (taapman constant): T dono taraf kat jaata hai, bachta hai **P₁V₁ = P₂V₂**: Boyle.
- **P₁ = P₂ ho** (pressure constant): P kat jaata hai, bachta hai **V₁/T₁ = V₂/T₂**: Charles.
- **V₁ = V₂ ho** (volume constant): V kat jaata hai, bachta hai **P₁/T₁ = P₂/T₂**: P-T niyam.

```
                  P₁ V₁       P₂ V₂
                  -----   =   -----          (combined, n constant)
                   T₁          T₂

        T constant            P constant            V constant
        (T₁ = T₂)             (P₁ = P₂)             (V₁ = V₂)
            |                      |                     |
            v                      v                     v
      P₁ V₁ = P₂ V₂          V₁/T₁ = V₂/T₂        P₁/T₁ = P₂/T₂
         BOYLE                 CHARLES              P-T (GAY-LUSSAC)
```

> **Figure 4.3** – Combined gas equation aur teeno niyam: jab ek rashi constant ho jaati hai to wo dono taraf kat jaati hai aur ek purana niyam bach jaata hai. Isse teeno niyam alag-alag yaad karne ki zaroorat nahi: combined likho aur jo constant ho use kaat do.

**Ek zaroori note:** ye sab niyam **ideal** (aadarsh) gas ke liye hain, jo ki **aam pressure aur aam taapman** par asli gas ke liye bahut achha kaam karte hain. Bahut zyada pressure aur bahut kam taapman par asli gas in niyam se thoda hat jaati hai. Ye baat aur PV = nRT jaisi poori equation **aage ki class mein**.

## Part D: Solved examples

(Sab numbers udaharan ke liye hain. Rounding: do decimal places ya jaisa likha ho. T hamesha kelvin mein: K = °C + 273.)

**Example 1: dhoop mein aerosol can (soch ka hisaab).** Ek aerosol can ka pressure 27 °C par 4.0 atm hai. Agar kabhi use galti se 57 °C tak garam gaadi mein chhod diya jaaye (aisa kabhi nahi karna), to pressure kitna ho jaayega?

- T₁ = 27 + 273 = 300 K, T₂ = 57 + 273 = 330 K, P₁ = 4.0 atm
- P₂ = P₁ × T₂ / T₁ = 4.0 × 330 / 300 = **4.4 atm**

**Jaanch:** P₂/T₂ = 4.4/330 = 0.01333 aur P₁/T₁ = 4.0/300 = 0.01333 ✅. Pressure 4.0 se 4.4 atm, yaani 10% zyada, sirf 30 degree ki garmi se. Can ki deewar ke liye ye bhi zyada ho sakta hai.

**Example 2: cylinder ka pressure.** Ek rigid cylinder mein gas 2.0 atm par 300 K (27 °C) par hai. Taapman 330 K ho jaata hai (garam din, bahar ki garmi se). Naya pressure?

- P₂ = 2.0 × 330 / 300 = **2.2 atm**

**Jaanch:** 330/300 = 1.1 aur 2.2/2.0 = 1.1 ✅ (dono taraf wahi guna).

**Example 3: kis taapman par?** Ek rigid tank mein gas 280 K par 1.6 atm hai. Kis taapman par uska pressure 2.0 atm ho jaayega?

- T₂ = T₁ × P₂ / P₁ = 280 × 2.0 / 1.6 = **350 K**
- Celsius mein: 350 − 273 = **77 °C**

**Jaanch:** 1.6/280 = 0.005714 aur 2.0/350 = 0.005714 ✅.

**Example 4: tyre garmi mein.** Subah ek car ke tyre ka (absolute) pressure 2.4 atm hai jab hawa 17 °C par hai. Chalne ke baad tyre ka andar ka taapman 47 °C ho jaata hai. Maan lo volume same rahe. Pressure?

- T₁ = 17 + 273 = 290 K, T₂ = 47 + 273 = 320 K
- P₂ = 2.4 × 320 / 290 = **2.65 atm** (do decimal tak)

**Jaanch:** 2.65 × 290 / 320 = 2.40 ✅. Isliye tyre ka pressure hamesha **thanda tyre** par check karte hain: garam tyre mein reading zyada aati hai. (Asli tyre ka volume thoda badal bhi sakta hai, isliye ye ek andaaza hai.)

**Example 5: sardi mein ball.** Ek football (maan lo volume same) mein 1.5 atm (absolute) par gas 27 °C par hai. Sardi mein 270 K (−3 °C) ho jaye to pressure?

- P₂ = 1.5 × 270 / 300 = **1.35 atm**

**Jaanch:** 1.35 < 1.5, taapman kam hua to pressure kam ✅. Pressure 10% kam, isliye ball pichki lagti hai.

**Example 6: Celsius ka jaal (galat vs sahi).** Ek rigid container mein gas ka pressure 20 °C par 2 atm hai. 40 °C par pressure kitna? Ek dost ne Celsius mein hi hisaab kiya: 40/20 = 2 guna, 4 atm. Sahi kya hai?

- Galat: P₂ = 2 × 40/20 = 4 atm (Celsius mein shunya ka matlab "kuch nahi" nahi hai)
- Sahi: T₁ = 293 K, T₂ = 313 K, P₂ = 2 × 313 / 293 = **2.14 atm** (do decimal tak)

**Jaanch:** 20 °C se 40 °C sirf 6.8% zyada garmi (313/293 = 1.068), to pressure bhi sirf lagbhag 6.8% badha ✅. Doguna garmi nahi hui, 4 atm bilkul galat hai.

**Example 7: readings ka table.** Ek rigid container ka pressure alag-alag taapman par naapa gaya. Kya P-T niyam sahi hai?

| T (K) | 250 | 300 | 350 | 400 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P (atm) | 1.0 | 1.2 | 1.4 | 1.6 |

- P/T: 1.0/250 = 0.004, 1.2/300 = 0.004, 1.4/350 = 0.004, 1.6/400 = 0.004

**Jaanch:** chaaron mein P/T = 0.004 atm per K barabar hai ✅. Niyam sahi hai. Iska matlab 500 K par pressure 0.004 × 500 = **2.0 atm** hoga.

**Example 8: warning kyun?** Ek can 27 °C par 5.0 atm par bhara hai. Maan lo uski deewar adhiktam 8.0 atm sah sakti hai. Soch ka sawaal: kis taapman par pressure 8.0 atm hoga, aur wo kya hai?

- T₁ = 300 K. T₂ = 300 × 8.0 / 5.0 = **480 K**
- 480 − 273 = **207 °C**

**Jaanch:** 5.0/300 = 0.016667 aur 8.0/480 = 0.016667 ✅. 207 °C sirf aag ke paas ya bahut tez garmi mein milta hai. Yahi wajah hai ki **aag ke paas nahi** likha hota hai. Asli can ki deewar bahut pehle kamzor hone lagti hai, isliye label ek kam taapman (50 °C) ki had deta hai, 207 °C ki nahi. Ye kabhi experiment nahi hai, sirf kagaz ka hisaab.

**Example 9: combined, simple.** 1.0 L gas 1.0 atm aur 273 K par hai. Ise 2.0 atm aur 546 K par laya gaya. Naya volume?

- V₂ = P₁V₁T₂ / (T₁P₂) = 1.0 × 1.0 × 546 / (273 × 2.0) = **1.0 L**

**Jaanch:** pressure doguna (volume aadha karne wala asar) aur taapman doguna (volume doguna karne wala asar): dono ek dusre ko kaat dete hain, volume 1.0 L hi ✅.

**Example 10: combined, weather balloon.** Ek weather balloon zameen par 5.0 L hai, jahan pressure 1.0 atm aur taapman 300 K hai. Upar jaakar pressure 0.5 atm aur taapman 250 K ho jaata hai. Naya volume?

- V₂ = 5.0 × 1.0 × 250 / (300 × 0.5) = 1250 / 150 = **8.33 L** (do decimal tak)

**Jaanch:** P₁V₁/T₁ = 5.0 × 1.0/300 = 0.016667 aur P₂V₂/T₂ = 0.5 × 8.33/250 = 0.016660 ≈ 0.016667 ✅. Pressure aadha hone ka asar doguna, taapman kam hone ka asar thoda kam: volume 5.0 ke dugne se thoda kam, 8.33 L.

**Example 11: combined se purane niyam banana.** 3.0 L gas 1.0 atm par 300 K par hai. Taapman constant rakh kar pressure 2.0 atm kiya (Boyle). Phir pressure constant rakh kar taapman 300 K se 360 K kiya (Charles).

- Boyle waala step: V₂ = 3.0 × 1.0 / 2.0 = **1.5 L** (T₁ = T₂ hai, to combined se P₁V₁ = P₂V₂)
- Charles waala step: V₃ = 1.5 × 360 / 300 = **1.8 L**
- Seedha combined se: V₃ = 3.0 × 1.0 × 360 / (300 × 2.0) = 1080 / 600 = **1.8 L**

**Jaanch:** dono raaste se 1.8 L ✅. Matlab combined equation ek hi baar mein wahi deti hai jo teeno niyam alag-alag steps mein dete.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Celsius mein hi P-T niyam laga do."**
Nahi. T hamesha kelvin mein: K = °C + 273. Example 6 mein 4 atm galat tha, 2.14 atm sahi.

**Galti 2: "Pressure-temperature niyam mein volume badalta hai."**
Nahi. Is niyam mein volume constant hai (sakht container). Agar volume badalta ho to combined ya Charles.

**Galti 3: "Gas ki matra badalne par bhi ye niyam chalte hain."**
Nahi. Teeno niyam mein n constant hai. Agar can se gas nikal jaaye (spray karne par), to ye niyam seedha nahi lagte.

**Galti 4: "Combined equation mein sab kuch ek taraf ek saath daal do."**
P₁V₁/T₁ = P₂V₂/T₂ ka matlab: **ek taraf poora "pehle" ke liye, doosri taraf poora "baad ke" liye**. P₁ ko V₂ ke saath mat milana.

**Galti 5: "Pressure double hone par taapman bhi Celsius mein double hoga."**
Nahi. Kelvin mein double: 300 K se 600 K (27 °C se 327 °C), 54 °C nahi.

**Galti 6: "Garmi mein can phat sakta hai, isliye hum use thoda sa garam karke dekhenge."**
**Bilkul nahi.** Aerosol can, LPG cylinder, lighter ya band bottle ko kabhi garam nahi karna aur dhoop mein nahi chhodna. Ye niyam sirf kagaz par hisaab ke liye hai.

## Quick Recap

- Rigid (sakht) container, gas ki matra constant: **P/T = constant**, yaani **P₁/T₁ = P₂/T₂**, T kelvin mein.
- Taapman badha to kan tez, deewar par zyada takkar, pressure zyada. Taapman ghata to pressure ghata.
- Isi wajah se LPG cylinder aur aerosol can par "garmi aur aag se door" likha hota hai.
- Combined gas equation: **P₁V₁/T₁ = P₂V₂/T₂** (n constant, T kelvin).
- T constant to Boyle, P constant to Charles, V constant to P-T niyam: teeno combined ke special case.
- Sawaal mein pehle dekho: **kya constant hai?** Phir sahi niyam chuno.
- Celsius mein kabhi nahi, hamesha K = °C + 273.
- Safety: aerosol can, LPG cylinder, tyre aur band container ko kabhi garam nahi karna, dhoop mein nahi chhodna, aag ke paas nahi rakhna. Koi bhi experiment band container ko garam karne wala nahi hai.

## Practice Questions

(Har sawaal mein naye numbers hain. T kelvin mein. Do decimal places tak round karo.)

1. A deodorant can has a pressure of 3.0 atm at 27 °C. If it were (wrongly) left in a hot car where the temperature reaches 47 °C, what would the pressure become? Why must it never be left there?

2. A bicycle tyre has an absolute pressure of 1.8 atm at 7 °C on a winter morning. By the afternoon, in the sun, the air inside is at 37 °C. Assuming the volume does not change, find the new pressure (to two decimal places).

3. A rigid gas container has gas at 5.0 atm and 300 K. The temperature falls to 250 K on a winter night. Find the new pressure (to two decimal places).

4. The gas in a steel cylinder is at 1.5 atm and 290 K. At what temperature (in K and in °C) will its pressure be 2.1 atm?

5. A scientist measures a gas in a rigid vessel: 1.5 atm at 300 K, 1.75 atm at 350 K, 2.0 atm at 400 K. Does it obey the pressure-temperature law? What would the pressure be at 500 K?

6. 2.0 L of gas at 2.0 atm and 400 K is changed to 1.0 atm and 300 K. Find the new volume.

7. 10.0 L of gas at 1.0 atm and 27 °C is compressed and warmed to 4.0 atm and 87 °C. Find the new volume.

8. For each case, name the law (Boyle, Charles, pressure-temperature, or combined) to use, and say what is constant: (a) a gas in a syringe pushed slowly at room temperature; (b) the air in a rigid steel tank on a hot day; (c) a balloon, open to the air, warmed over a bowl of warm water held by an adult; (d) a weather balloon rising, where P, V and T all change.

9. A can is filled to 4.0 atm at 27 °C. If its wall can bear at most 6.0 atm, at what temperature (in °C) does the pressure reach this limit? Explain how this connects with the warning on the label.

10. A student says: "A rigid tank is at 1.0 atm at 0 °C. At 100 °C the pressure will be 100 times more, as 100 ÷ 1 = 100." Correct the student and find the right pressure (calculation only, never to be tried).

11. State True or False and correct the false ones:
    (a) The pressure-temperature law needs the volume to be constant.
    (b) If the temperature in kelvin doubles at constant volume, the pressure doubles.
    (c) The combined gas equation reduces to Boyle's law when the pressure is constant.
    (d) A closed aerosol can may be safely kept near a flame if it is almost empty.

### Answers

1. T₁ = 27 + 273 = 300 K, T₂ = 47 + 273 = 320 K. P₂ = 3.0 × 320 / 300 = **3.2 atm**. Hot car mein pressure badhta hai, aur zyada garmi par aur badhega, isliye can ki deewar kamzor ho sakti hai aur can phat sakta hai. Isliye aerosol can ko kabhi garam jagah ya dhoop mein nahi chhodte.
   Jaanch: 3.2/320 = 0.01 aur 3.0/300 = 0.01 ✅.

2. T₁ = 7 + 273 = 280 K, T₂ = 37 + 273 = 310 K. P₂ = 1.8 × 310 / 280 = **1.99 atm** (lagbhag 1.9929, do decimal tak).
   Jaanch: 1.99 × 280 / 310 = 1.80 ✅.

3. P₂ = 5.0 × 250 / 300 = **4.17 atm** (lagbhag 4.1667).
   Jaanch: 4.17 < 5.0, taapman kam hua, to pressure kam ✅; 4.1667/250 = 0.016667 aur 5.0/300 = 0.016667 ✅.

4. T₂ = 290 × 2.1 / 1.5 = **406 K**. Celsius mein: 406 − 273 = **133 °C**.
   Jaanch: 1.5/290 = 0.005172 aur 2.1/406 = 0.005172 ✅.

5. P/T: 1.5/300 = 0.005, 1.75/350 = 0.005, 2.0/400 = 0.005. Teeno barabar, to **haan, niyam sahi hai** (P/T = 0.005 atm per K). 500 K par P = 0.005 × 500 = **2.5 atm**.
   Jaanch: 2.5/500 = 0.005 ✅.

6. V₂ = P₁V₁T₂ / (T₁P₂) = 2.0 × 2.0 × 300 / (400 × 1.0) = 1200 / 400 = **3.0 L**.
   Jaanch: P₁V₁/T₁ = 4.0/400 = 0.01 aur P₂V₂/T₂ = 1.0 × 3.0/300 = 0.01 ✅.

7. T₁ = 300 K, T₂ = 87 + 273 = 360 K. V₂ = 1.0 × 10.0 × 360 / (300 × 4.0) = 3600 / 1200 = **3.0 L**.
   Jaanch: 1.0 × 10.0/300 = 0.03333 aur 4.0 × 3.0/360 = 0.03333 ✅.

8. (a) **Boyle**: taapman aur gas ki matra constant. (b) **Pressure-temperature niyam**: volume (sakht tank) aur matra constant. (c) **Charles**: pressure (khuli hawa) aur matra constant. (d) **Combined equation**: sirf matra constant, P, V, T teeno badal rahe hain.

9. T₂ = 300 × 6.0 / 4.0 = 450 K. Celsius mein: 450 − 273 = **177 °C**. 177 °C aag ya bahut tez garmi mein hi milta hai. Label mein 50 °C ke aas-paas ki kam had isliye hoti hai ki deewar bahut pehle hi kamzor na pade, aur isliye "aag ke paas nahi, garmi se door" likha rehta hai.
   Jaanch: 4.0/300 = 0.013333 aur 6.0/450 = 0.013333 ✅.

10. Galat, kyunki 0 °C ka matlab 0 K nahi hai, aur Celsius ke ratio ka koi arth nahi. Sahi: T₁ = 273 K, T₂ = 373 K. P₂ = 1.0 × 373 / 273 = **1.37 atm** (lagbhag 1.366), 100 guna nahi.
    Jaanch: 1.366/373 = 0.003663 aur 1.0/273 = 0.003663 ✅.

11. (a) **True** ✅. (b) **True** ✅. (c) **False** ❌. Pressure constant hone par combined equation **Charles** ka niyam ban jaati hai; Boyle tab banta hai jab **taapman** constant ho. (d) **False** ❌. Aerosol can kabhi bhi aag ke paas nahi rakhna, bhale hi lagbhag khaali ho: andar abhi bhi gas aur jalne wala propellant bacha rehta hai.
