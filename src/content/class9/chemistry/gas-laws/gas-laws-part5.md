# Topic 5: STP, Avogadro Ka Niyam aur Molar Volume (thoda extra, Class 10 overlap)

Pichhle teen topics mein humne ek gas ke **P, V aur T** ke beech ke rishte dekhe, aur combined equation se teeno ko ek saath badalna seekha. Par ek cheez abhi tak chhupi rahi: **gas ki matra (kitne kan)**. Humne har baar maan liya tha ki gas utni hi rahe, na kam na zyada. Is topic mein hum us matra ko bhi jodte hain. Sawaal seedha hai: **ek mole gas** (Chapter 4 ka mole, [atoms-and-molecules-part5.md](../atoms-and-molecules/atoms-and-molecules-part5.md)) kitni jagah gheregi? Chapter 4 ne yahi wada kiya tha ki molar volume "Gas Laws" mein milega. Saath hi, Chapter 7 aur Chapter 9 ne paani ke electrolysis mein hydrogen aur oxygen ka **2 : 1** volume ratio dikhaya tha, par uska kaaran "aage ki class mein" par chhoda tha. Aaj wo udhaar bhi chukta hota hai.

(Ye topic **thoda extra** hai: iska kuch hissa Class 10 se milta hai. Samajh na aaye to bhi chapter ka baaki hissa theek chalega.)

**Atomic masses (poore topic mein yahi use honge):** H 1, He 4, C 12, N 14, O 16, Cl 35.5. Molar volume 22.4 L per mol (STP par). Avogadro ki sankhya 6.022 × 10²³.

## Birthday party aur do gubbare

Mr. Navale ke bhatije Hiren ka birthday tha. Ghar ke hall mein chhoti si party hui. Ek gubbara **helium** se bhara tha (dukaan se bharwa kar laaya gaya): Hiren ne uska dhaaga chhoda, aur wo seedha upar tairta hua chhat tak chala gaya. Hiren hairaan. "Dono gubbare hain, dono gas se bhare, par ek upar aur ek neeche? Aur chacha, mole wale chapter mein padha tha ki 1 mole mein chahe jo bhi kan ho, unki ginti barabar hoti hai. To ek mole gas kitni jagah ghergi, aur kya ye bhi sabke liye ek hi hogi?"

Mr. Navale muskuraye. "Do sawaal, aur dono ka jawab ek hi baat mein chhupa hai. ## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **STP** | Standard Temperature and Pressure: tay "standard" halat jisme gas ke volume ki tulna hoti hai; is course mein 273 K (0 °C) aur 1 atm (760 mm Hg) | 0 °C aur 1 atm |
| **Avogadro ka niyam** | Same T aur P par barabar volume ki sabhi gas mein kanon (molecules) ki sankhya barabar | 1 L H₂ aur 1 L CO₂ mein kan barabar |
| **Molar volume** | 1 mole gas ka volume | STP par 22.4 L per mol |
| **Gas ki density** | 1 litre gas ka vazan, g per L mein | N₂ ki 1.25 g per L |
| **Molar mass (M)** | 1 mole ka vazan, g per mol mein | CO₂ ka 44 g per mol |

## Part A: STP kyun chahiye?

Pichhle topics ne dikhaya: gas ka volume P aur T badalne par badalta hai. To "5 litre gas" kehna adhoora hai: kis dabao aur taapman par? Do alag laboratories ki report tabhi mil sakti hai jab sab ek **tay (standard)** halat par volume naapein. Is course mein hum ye halat lenge:

- **Taapman:** 273 K (yaani 0 °C)
- **Dabao:** 1 atm (yaani 760 mm Hg, Topic 1 ki unit table dekho)

Is halat ko **STP** kehte hain. (Physics ka atmospheric pressure ka vichar [pressure-and-fluids-part4.md](../../physics/pressure-and-fluids/pressure-and-fluids-part4.md) mein tha: 1 atm samudra tal par hawa ka lagbhag dabao hai.) Dhyan do: STP ka matlab "roz ka kamra" nahi; 0 °C thanda taapman hai. Kamre ka taapman (lagbhag 27 °C, yaani 300 K) STP se alag hai, aur ye fark aage ek solved example mein dikhega.

## Part B: Avogadro ka niyam

Ab sabse dilchasp vichar. Maan lo tumhare paas teen ek jaise dibbe hain, har ek **1 litre** ka, STP par: ek mein hydrogen, ek mein oxygen, ek mein carbon dioxide. Hydrogen ke kan bahut halke aur chhote, CO₂ ke bhaari aur bade. Phir bhi 1 litre ki jagah mein kitne kan honge?

Italy ke Amedeo Avogadro (1811) ne yahi kaha: **same taapman aur same dabao par, barabar volume ki sabhi gas mein kanon (molecules) ki sankhya barabar hoti hai.** Ye **Avogadro ka niyam** hai.

```
   Teen dibbe, har ek 1 litre, sab STP par

   ┌────────────┐   ┌────────────┐   ┌────────────┐
   │ o   o    o │   │ O   O    O │   │ @@  @@   @@│
   │    o   o   │   │   O    O   │   │   @@   @@  │
   │ o     o    │   │ O     O    │   │ @@    @@   │
   └────────────┘   └────────────┘   └────────────┘
     hydrogen          oxygen         carbon dioxide
   chhote, halke    madhyam, bhaari   bade, sabse bhaari

   Har dibbe mein 7 molecule dikhaye (ginti barabar).
   Vazan alag hai, ginti ek jaisi.
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Barabar volume, barabar molecules: teeno dibbon mein molecules ki ginti ek jaisi hai (yahan 7 dikhaye). Kan ka aakar aur vazan alag hai, par gas kan itne door-door hote hain ki ginti hi volume tay karti hai (Chapter 1 ka particle picture). Drawing sirf nidarshan hai.

**Ye kyun sahi lagta hai?** Gas mein kan ek-doosre se bahut door hote hain, aur unka apna aakar volume ke mukable bahut chhota hota hai (Chapter 1 se yaad karo). Isliye volume ka bada hissa **khaali jagah** hai. Jagah ghirne ka kaam kan ke aakar se nahi, **kitne kan hain** isse tay hota hai. Isi liye hydrogen ho ya CO₂, barabar volume mein ginti barabar.

(Ek imaandar baat: ye niyam **aadarsh** (ideal) halat mein theek baithta hai. Bahut zyada dabao ya bahut kam taapman par asli gas thodi alag chalti hain; wo baat aage ki class mein.)

## Part C: Molar volume: 1 mole, 22.4 litre

Avogadro ka niyam aur mole ko jodo: 1 mole mein 6.022 × 10²³ kan hote hain, chahe kisi bhi gas ke ho. Barabar kan matlab barabar volume. To 1 mole gas ka volume **sabhi gaso ke liye ek hi** hona chahiye. Prayog se naapne par STP par ye volume nikla:

**1 mole kisi bhi gas = 22.4 L (STP par).** Ise **molar volume** kehte hain. (Poori shuddhta se 22.414 L, hum 22.4 L rakhenge.)

22.4 litre kitna hota hai? Lagbhag 22 bottle (1 L wali) ke barabar, yaani ek chhote carton jitni jagah. Ek mole paani sirf 18 mL hota hai (kuch chamach jitna), par ek mole gas itni badi jagah leti hai kyunki gas mein kan door-door hain.

```
   1 mole gas, STP par: 22.4 L

        ┌──────────────────────────┐
        │  o      o     o       o  │
        │     o        o     o     │     Andar 6.022 × 10²³
        │  o      o         o      │     molecules
        │      o      o        o   │
        │  o         o      o      │     Vazan:
        └──────────────────────────┘     H₂ ka 2 g
          22.4 L (har kinara             He ka 4 g
          lagbhag 28 cm)                 O₂ ka 32 g
                                         CO₂ ka 44 g
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Ek mole kisi bhi gas ka STP par 22.4 L jagah leta hai. Dibbe ka naap lagbhag 28 cm × 28 cm × 28 cm (28³ = 21952 cm³, lagbhag 22 L), ye sirf andaaz hai. Andar molecules ki ginti 6.022 × 10²³ hai, par vazan gas ke hisaab se alag hai.

**Teen kaam ke rishte (Chapter 4 ka mole + molar volume):**

- n = m ÷ M  (mass se moles)
- V = n × 22.4  (moles se volume, STP par, litre mein)
- N = n × 6.022 × 10²³  (moles se molecules)

Mass, volume aur kan, teeno ke beech mole ka pul hai. Pehle **moles** nikalo, phir jahan jaana ho wahan jao.

**Dhyan:** 22.4 L sirf **gas** ke liye hai aur sirf **STP par**. Paani, namak ya lohe jaise liquid-solid ke liye ye lagu nahi hota.

## Part D: Solved problems (STP par)

### Problem 1. Volume se moles: 44.8 L nitrogen

STP par 44.8 L nitrogen (N₂) mein kitne mole?

- n = V ÷ 22.4 = 44.8 ÷ 22.4 = **2 mol**.

Jaanch: 2 × 22.4 = 44.8 L ✅

### Problem 2. Moles se volume: 0.25 mol oxygen

STP par 0.25 mol oxygen ka volume?

- V = 0.25 × 22.4 = **5.6 L**.

Jaanch: 5.6 ÷ 22.4 = 0.25 mol ✅

### Problem 3. Volume se mass: 5.6 L oxygen

STP par 5.6 L oxygen (O₂) ka vazan?

- n = 5.6 ÷ 22.4 = 0.25 mol.
- M(O₂) = 2 × 16 = 32 g per mol.
- m = 0.25 × 32 = **8 g**.

Jaanch: 8 ÷ 32 = 0.25 mol, 0.25 × 22.4 = 5.6 L ✅

### Problem 4. Mass se volume: 4.4 g carbon dioxide

STP par 4.4 g CO₂ kitni jagah gheregi?

- M(CO₂) = 12 + 2 × 16 = 44 g per mol.
- n = 4.4 ÷ 44 = 0.1 mol.
- V = 0.1 × 22.4 = **2.24 L**.

Jaanch: 2.24 ÷ 22.4 = 0.1 mol, 0.1 × 44 = 4.4 g ✅

### Problem 5. Volume se molecules: 2.24 L koi bhi gas

STP par 2.24 L ki **koi bhi** gas mein kitne molecules?

- n = 2.24 ÷ 22.4 = 0.1 mol.
- N = 0.1 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **6.022 × 10²² molecules**.

Gas ka naam poochha hi nahi gaya, kyunki Avogadro ke niyam se naam se fark nahi padta.

Jaanch: 6.022 × 10²² ÷ 6.022 × 10²³ = 0.1 mol, 0.1 × 22.4 = 2.24 L ✅

### Problem 6. Gas kam hai ya zyada: 1 g hydrogen

STP par 1 g hydrogen (H₂) kitni jagah gheregi?

- M(H₂) = 2 g per mol. n = 1 ÷ 2 = 0.5 mol.
- V = 0.5 × 22.4 = **11.2 L**.

Sirf 1 g gas ek bade dibbe jitni jagah leti hai: 11.2 L, yaani 11 bottle (1 L wali) ke barabar!

Jaanch: 11.2 ÷ 22.4 = 0.5 mol, 0.5 × 2 = 1 g ✅

### Problem 7. Kaun si jagah zyada gheregi: 28 g N₂ ya 28 g CO₂?

Ek aam soch: "barabar vazan to barabar volume." Dekho:

- n(N₂) = 28 ÷ 28 = 1 mol, V = **22.4 L**.
- n(CO₂) = 28 ÷ 44 = 0.636 mol, V = 0.636 × 22.4 = **14.25 L**.

Barabar **vazan** par barabar **volume** nahi aata. Barabar **moles** (yaani barabar ginti) par aata hai.

Jaanch: 28 ÷ 44 = 0.6364 (4 ank) aur 0.6364 × 22.4 = 14.25 L ✅

### Problem 8. STP nahi, kamre ka taapman (Topic 3 ka Charles niyam)

Agar taapman 27 °C (300 K) ho aur dabao 1 atm hi rahe, to 1 mole gas ka volume kitna?

- Dabao same hai, to Charles ka niyam: V₁ ÷ T₁ = V₂ ÷ T₂.
- V₂ = 22.4 × 300 ÷ 273 = **24.6 L**.

STP par 22.4 L, kamre ke taapman par 24.6 L: 22.4 aise taapman par seedha nahi lagta. Kisi bhi aur halat ke liye pehle combined gas equation se volume badlo.

Jaanch: Wapas: 24.6 × 273 ÷ 300 = 22.4 L ✅ (rounding ke andar)

## Part E: Gas ki density: gubbare upar kyun, neeche kyun

**Density = vazan ÷ volume.** STP par 1 mole ka vazan M hai aur volume 22.4 L hai, to:

**Gas ki density (g per L) = M ÷ 22.4**

Isse table banti hai (gas ka 1 litre, STP par):

| Gas | Molar mass M (g per mol) | Density = M ÷ 22.4 (g per L) |
|---|---|---|
| Hydrogen H₂ | 2 | 0.09 |
| Helium He | 4 | 0.18 |
| Nitrogen N₂ | 28 | 1.25 |
| Hawa (air), ausat | lagbhag 29 | 1.29 |
| Oxygen O₂ | 32 | 1.43 |
| Carbon dioxide CO₂ | 44 | 1.96 |

(Hawa ek mixture hai, isliye uska M ausat hai: lagbhag 78 percent nitrogen aur 21 percent oxygen ka mel, jisse lagbhag 29 g per mol aata hai. Table ke ank rounded hain.)

**Seedha nateeja:** jis gas ka M hawa ke ausat (29) se **kam** ho, wo hawa mein **upar** uthti hai; jiska M **zyada** ho, wo **neeche** baithti hai.

- **Helium (M = 4):** density 0.18 g per L, hawa (1.29) se bahut halki. Helium ka gubbara upar jaata hai.
- **CO₂ (M = 44):** density 1.96 g per L, hawa se bhaari. CO₂ ka soda wala bubble neeche utarta hai.
- **N₂ (M = 28)** hawa se bas thoda halka hai: ek nitrogen ka gubbara zyada nahi uthega.

### Problem 9. Helium ke gubbare ko kitna uthaav milta hai

STP jaisi halat mein 10 L ka helium ka gubbara (gubbare ki rubber ka vazan chhod do): hawa ka vazan aur helium ka vazan kitna hai?

- Helium: 10 × (4 ÷ 22.4) = 10 × 0.1786 = **1.79 g**.
- Wahi 10 L hawa: 10 × (29 ÷ 22.4) = 10 × 1.2946 = **12.95 g**.
- Fark: 12.95 − 1.79 = 11.16 ≈ **11.2 g** (uthaav, lagbhag).

Gubbara tabhi tairta hai jab rubber aur dhaage ka vazan is uthaav se kam ho. 10 L ke gubbare ko lagbhag 11 g utha lene ki shakti milti hai: ek haath mein pakadne laayak halka gubbara aaram se tair jaata hai.

Jaanch: bina rounded ankon ke 10 × (29 − 4) ÷ 22.4 = 11.16 g, yaani lagbhag 11 g ✅

(Hydrogen bhi bahut halki hai, par wo aag pakadne wali gas hai, isliye party ke gubbaron mein helium hi bhari jaati hai, aur wo bhi dukaan par trained log bharte hain.)

### Problem 10. Anjaan gas ki pehchaan: density se M

Ek gas ki density STP par 1.25 g per L nikli. Uska molar mass kya hai?

- M = density × 22.4 = 1.25 × 22.4 = **28 g per mol**. Ye N₂ (ya CO) ke barabar hai.

Jaanch: 28 ÷ 22.4 = 1.25 g per L ✅

## Part F: Ek purana udhaar chukta hota hai: paani ka electrolysis

Chapter 7 ([chemical-changes-and-reactions-part2.md](../chemical-changes-and-reactions/chemical-changes-and-reactions-part2.md)) mein humne dekha tha ki paani mein bijli bhejne par wo toot kar **hydrogen** aur **oxygen** deta hai: 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂, aur **hydrogen ka volume oxygen se do guna** nikalta hai. Par iska kaaran "aage ki class mein" kehkar chhoda gaya tha. Ye 2 : 1 hamesha kyun aata hai?

Ab hum ye jaante hain. Equation 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂ batati hai ki 2 molecule paani se **2 molecule H₂** aur **1 molecule O₂** bante hain. Avogadro ke niyam se, same T aur P par **molecules ki ginti volume ke barabar** hoti hai. 2 molecule H₂ aur 1 molecule O₂ matlab **2 volume H₂ aur 1 volume O₂**.

```
   Paani ka electrolysis: 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂

     Hydrogen (−)         Oxygen (+)
       tube                 tube
     ┌──┐                 ┌──┐
     │  │                 │  │
     │H₂│                 │  │
     │H₂│                 │O₂│
     │H₂│                 │O₂│
     │H₂│                 │O₂│
     └──┘                 └──┘
    gas ka volume        gas ka volume
    2 ikai               1 ikai

        Volume ka anupaat  H₂ : O₂ = 2 : 1
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Electrolysis mein hydrogen wale tube mein gas ka volume oxygen wale tube se do guna hota hai. Wajah: 2 molecule H₂ ke saath 1 molecule O₂ banta hai, aur same T, P par barabar volume mein barabar molecules hote hain. Tubes ki chaudaai barabar maani hai. Ye drawing sirf anupaat dikhati hai.

### Problem 11. 36 g paani ka electrolysis

36 g paani poora toot jaaye to STP par kitna H₂ aur O₂ banega?

- n(H₂O) = 36 ÷ 18 = 2 mol.
- 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂: 2 mol H₂O se **2 mol H₂** aur **1 mol O₂**.
- V(H₂) = 2 × 22.4 = **44.8 L**; V(O₂) = 1 × 22.4 = **22.4 L**.
- Anupaat 44.8 : 22.4 = **2 : 1** ✅

Jaanch (vazan bachta hai?): m(H₂) = 2 × 2 = 4 g; m(O₂) = 1 × 32 = 32 g; 4 + 32 = 36 g = shuru ka paani ✅

(Chapter 9 ([water.md](../water/water.md)) mein bhi yahi 2 : 1 volume aur 1 : 8 mass dekha tha. Ab volume wale 2 : 1 ka kaaran bhi saaf hai: ye paani ke formula H₂O ka ek aur saboot hai.)

### Problem 12. Ek aur volume udaharan: ammonia

Haber ki vidhi mein nitrogen aur hydrogen milkar ammonia deti hain: **N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃**. Same T aur P par ye **volume** mein kya batati hai?

- 1 molecule N₂ + 3 molecule H₂ → 2 molecule NH₃.
- Avogadro ke niyam se: **1 volume N₂ + 3 volume H₂ → 2 volume NH₃**.
- Udaharan: 10 L N₂ + 30 L H₂ → **20 L NH₃** (T aur P same).

Dhyan do ki volume 10 + 30 = 40 se ghatkar 20 ho gaya, kyunki molecules ki ginti 4 se ghatkar 2 ho gayi. Volume ginti ke saath chalta hai, vazan ka is par seedha asar nahi.

Jaanch: molecules 1 + 3 = 4 se 2 hue, yaani aadhe; volume bhi 40 L se 20 L, yaani aadha ✅. Vazan bhi bachta hai (STP maanein): 10 L N₂ = 12.5 g; 30 L H₂ = 2.68 g; kul 15.18 g; 20 L NH₃ = 0.8929 mol × 17 = 15.18 g ✅

**Aage ki class mein:** equation dekhkar volume ke poore numerical sawaal (jaise "itni gas banane ke liye kitni gas chahiye" ke saare roop) baad ki class ka vishay hain. Yahan humne sirf do udaharan se vichar dekha.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: 22.4 L ko har halat par maanna**

22.4 L sirf **STP** (273 K, 1 atm) par hai. Kamre ke taapman par 1 mole gas 24.6 L leti hai (Problem 8).

**Galti 2: 22.4 L ko liquid ya solid par lagana**

22.4 L sirf **gas** ke liye hai. 1 mole paani 18 g ya lagbhag 18 mL hai, 22.4 L nahi.

**Galti 3: Barabar vazan ko barabar volume samajhna**

28 g N₂ aur 28 g CO₂ ka volume alag hai (Problem 7). Barabar **moles** barabar volume dete hain.

**Galti 4: Volume mein litre aur mL ko mila dena**

22.4 L = 22400 mL. Agar volume 224 mL diya hai to wo 0.224 L hai, aur moles = 0.224 ÷ 22.4 = 0.01 mol (224 ÷ 22.4 = 10 nahi). Pehle ikai ek karo.

**Galti 5: Avogadro ke niyam mein "barabar volume" ko "barabar vazan" padh lena**

Avogadro ke niyam mein **ginti** barabar hoti hai, vazan nahi, aur ye sirf **same T aur P** par sach hai.

**Galti 6: M ko bhool kar density nikalna**

Density = M ÷ 22.4 mein M **g per mol** mein hona chahiye. O₂ ke liye 32 (16 nahi, kyunki O₂ do atoms ka molecule hai).

## Quick Recap

- **STP:** 273 K (0 °C) aur 1 atm (760 mm Hg): gas ki volume ki tulna ki tay halat.
- **Avogadro ka niyam:** same T, P par barabar volume ki sabhi gas mein molecules ki ginti barabar.
- **Molar volume:** 1 mole kisi bhi gas = **22.4 L (STP par)**; ye sirf gas aur sirf STP ke liye.
- n = m ÷ M, V = n × 22.4 (L), N = n × 6.022 × 10²³; hamesha **moles** ke raste se jao.
- **Gas ki density = M ÷ 22.4** (g per L): H₂ 0.09, He 0.18, N₂ 1.25, hawa 1.29, O₂ 1.43, CO₂ 1.96.
- Hawa (M ≈ 29) se halki gas (He, H₂) upar uthti hai, bhaari gas (CO₂) neeche baithti hai.
- Electrolysis mein H₂ : O₂ = **2 : 1** (volume mein) kyunki 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂ mein 2 aur 1 molecule bante hain.
- N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃: 1 volume + 3 volume → 2 volume.
- Equations se volume ke poore numerical baad ki class mein; asli gas bahut zyada dabao aur bahut kam taapman par aadarsh niyam se thoda hat jaati hain.
- Safety: cylinder, aerosol can, tyre ko kabhi garam na karo, aur gubbare sirf adult ya pump se phulayein, gas cylinder se kabhi nahi.

### Answers

1. n = 33.6 ÷ 22.4 = **1.5 mol**. m = 1.5 × 17 = **25.5 g**. Jaanch: 25.5 ÷ 17 = 1.5 mol, 1.5 × 22.4 = 33.6 L ✅

2. M(N₂) = 28. n = 14 ÷ 28 = 0.5 mol. V = 0.5 × 22.4 = **11.2 L**. Jaanch: 11.2 ÷ 22.4 = 0.5 mol, 0.5 × 28 = 14 g ✅

3. M(He) = 4 g per mol. n = 10 ÷ 4 = **2.5 mol**. V = 2.5 × 22.4 = **56 L**. Jaanch: 56 ÷ 22.4 = 2.5 mol, 2.5 × 4 = 10 g ✅

4. n = 67.2 ÷ 22.4 = 3 mol. Molecules = 3 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **1.807 × 10²⁴**. M(Cl₂) = 2 × 35.5 = 71, m = 3 × 71 = **213 g**. Jaanch: 213 ÷ 71 = 3 mol ✅; 3 × 6.022 = 18.066, yaani 1.807 × 10²⁴ ✅

5. M(Cl₂) = 71. Density = 71 ÷ 22.4 = **3.17 g per L**. Ye hawa ki 1.29 se bahut zyada hai, to gubbara **neeche baithega (sink)**. Jaanch: 3.17 × 22.4 = 71 ✅ (Chlorine ek zehrili gas hai; ye sirf kitaab ka hisaab hai, kisi ko isse gubbara nahi bharna.)

6. 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂ ke hisaab se H₂ : O₂ = 2 : 1 volume mein, kyunki 2 molecule H₂ ke saath 1 molecule O₂ banta hai aur same T, P par barabar volume mein barabar molecules hote hain. H₂ = 2 × 30 = **60 mL**. Jaanch: 60 : 30 = 2 : 1 ✅

7. N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃ mein volume anupaat 1 : 3 : 2. 45 L H₂ matlab 45 ÷ 3 = 15 "hissa". N₂ = 1 × 15 = **15 L**, NH₃ = 2 × 15 = **30 L**. Jaanch: 15 : 45 : 30 = 1 : 3 : 2 ✅ (Ye Problem 12 ka hi vichar hai; equation-based poore numerical baad ki class mein.)

8. Paani ek **liquid** hai, 22.4 L sirf **gas** ke liye hota hai. 1 mol paani = 18 g, aur density 1 g per mL maanein to volume = 18 mL = **0.018 L**. Gas ke 22.4 L se lagbhag 1200 guna chhota, kyunki liquid mein kan paas-paas hote hain. Jaanch: 22.4 ÷ 0.018 = 1244, yaani lagbhag 1200 guna ✅

9. M = 1.78 × 22.4 = 39.9, yaani **lagbhag 40 g per mol**. Ye **Ar (argon)** ho sakti hai. Jaanch: 40 ÷ 22.4 = 1.79 g per L ✅ (H₂ ki 0.09, N₂ ki 1.25, O₂ ki 1.43 se alag).

10. n(CH₄) = 8 ÷ 16 = **0.5 mol** (3.011 × 10²³ molecules). n(N₂) = 8.96 ÷ 22.4 = **0.4 mol** (2.409 × 10²³ molecules). **Methane (8 g) mein zyada molecules** hain. Jaanch: 0.5 × 16 = 8 g ✅, 0.4 × 22.4 = 8.96 L ✅

11. 546 K = 2 × 273 K, dabao same. V₂ = 22.4 × 546 ÷ 273 = **44.8 L**. Taapman do guna to volume do guna. Jaanch: 44.8 ÷ 22.4 = 2 ✅ (K mein taapman, Topic 3 ki tarah)
