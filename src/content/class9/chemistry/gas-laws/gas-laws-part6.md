# Topic 6: Gas Laws Roz Ki Zindagi Mein + Chapter Ka Saar

Pichhle topics mein humne gaason ke chaar variables (P, V, T, matra) aur teen niyam seekhe, phir STP, Avogadro ka niyam aur molar volume. Ab sabse zaroori kaam: **kaun sa niyam kab lagana hai** ye pehchanna. Is topic mein ek ghat road ki bus yatra se shuru karke ear popping, tyre, saans lena, balloon, gotakhor ke bubbles aur scuba tank jaise **10 se zyada mixed problems** hal karenge, ek decision flow banayenge, safety yaad karenge, aur aakhir mein poore chapter ka saar dekhenge.

## Ghat road par pichki hui bottle

School trip par Nirvi apni class aur Mr. Sapre (Sapre sir) ke saath bus se ek pahadi sthaan gayi thi. Wapsi mein bus ghat road se neeche utar rahi thi, mod par mod.

"Sir, mere kaan band se ho gaye hain!" Nirvi ne kaan pakde.

Mr. Sapre muskuraye. "Nigal lo ya jamhai lo, khul jayenge." Nirvi ne dhere se nigla, aur kaan mein halka sa **pop** hua. "Ho gaya!"

Phir Nirvi ko yaad aaya. Upar pahaad par usne ek **khaali paani ki bottle** ka dhakkan kas kar band kiya tha. Ab usne bag se wo bottle nikali. Wo **pichki hui** thi, jaise kisi ne dabaaya ho. Bottle ko kisi ne chhua bhi nahi tha!

"Sir, ye kaise hua?" Nirvi chaunki.

"Ye wahi sawaal hai jo aaj poore chapter ko jodta hai," Sapre sir bole. "Bottle ke andar upar ki halki hawa band thi. Neeche aaye to bahar ki hawa ka pressure badh gaya, par andar wali hawa wahi rahi. **Bahar ka pressure zyada, andar kam**, isliye bottle pichak gayi. Aur kaan ka pop bhi wahi kahani hai."

Nirvi ne copy kholi aur likha: **"Pressure, volume aur temperature mein se kaun sa niyam kab chalega?"**

## Part A: Kaun sa niyam? (decision flow)

Har problem mein pehle ye likho: **kya constant hai, kya badal raha hai?** Phir niyam chuno.

```
          Gas ki matra (n) badli?
          (leak hui, gas daali ya nikali)
                      |
           +----------+----------+
           |                     |
          haan                  nahi
           |                     |
           v                     v
   Is chapter ka niyam     Teen mein se kaun constant hai?
   seedha nahi lagta
   (aage ki class)         T constant ---> Boyle: P₁V₁ = P₂V₂
                           P constant ---> Charles: V₁/T₁ = V₂/T₂
                           V constant ---> P-T niyam: P₁/T₁ = P₂/T₂
                           teeno badle --> Combined: P₁V₁/T₁ = P₂V₂/T₂

   Dhyan rakho: T hamesha kelvin mein (K = °C + 273)
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Decision flow: pehle dekho ki gas ki matra badli ya nahi; phir dekho kaun sa variable constant hai (T, P ya V) aur usi ka niyam lagao; agar teen badle to combined equation. Temperature har jagah kelvin mein.

Ek chhoti table mein bhi yaad karo (Topic 4 ki hi baat):

| Kya constant hai | Kya badal raha hai | Niyam |
|---|---|---|
| T aur matra | P aur V | Boyle: P₁V₁ = P₂V₂ |
| P aur matra | V aur T | Charles: V₁/T₁ = V₂/T₂ |
| V aur matra | P aur T | Pressure-temperature: P₁/T₁ = P₂/T₂ |
| Sirf matra | P, V aur T teeno | Combined: P₁V₁/T₁ = P₂V₂/T₂ |

Pehchaan ke liye: "volume badha, pressure ghata" jaisa sawaal Boyle hota hai; "gubbara garam karne par phoola" Charles; "band cylinder garam hua" pressure-temperature.

> **Jaanch:** "Band steel ka cylinder (kadak) garam karne par uska volume badhega, to Charles lagega." ❌ Nahi. Cylinder kadak hai, volume constant hai, isliye pressure-temperature niyam lagega (aur wahan pressure khatarnak tarah se badhta hai).

## Part B: Ghat road wali bottle (Boyle ke andar)

```
   PAHAAD KE UPAR              GHAT KE NEECHE
   (hawa halki, P = 0.90 atm)  (hawa bhaari, P = 1.00 atm)

   bahar:  0.90 atm            bahar:  1.00 atm
   andar:  0.90 atm            andar:  0.90 atm  (band hai)

     +--------+                   +------+
     |        |  dhakkan band     | pich |   bahar ka pressure
     |  hawa  |      ===>         | ki   |   zyada, andar kam
     |        |                   | hui  |   to bottle dab gayi
     +--------+                   +------+
```

> **Figure 6.2** – Band bottle upar aur neeche: upar andar aur bahar dono ka pressure lagbhag barabar (0.90 atm), neeche bahar ka pressure 1.00 atm hai par andar wahi hawa rehti hai, isliye patli bottle pichak jaati hai. (Aankde lagbhag aur illustrative.)

Particle ke roop mein: neeche ki hawa ghani hai, to bahar ke kan bottle ki deewar se zyada baar takraate hain (tezi wahi, kyunki temperature wahi hai). Andar ke kan pehle jitni hi baar takraate hain. Jab bahar wale **zyada dhakka** dete hain to bottle sikud jaati hai, aur andar ke kan ab chhoti jagah mein aa gaye hain. (Patli plastic ke saath volume badal sakta hai, isliye wo flexible container hai.)

**Example 1: Bottle kitni sikudi?**
Maan lo bottle mein 1.00 L hawa upar band hui, jahan pressure lagbhag **0.90 atm** tha (lagbhag 1000 m ki oonchai par, approximate). Neeche pressure **1.00 atm** hai. Temperature barabar maano. Boyle: P₁V₁ = P₂V₂ (Topic 2 ke Example 9 wala hi hisaab).
V₂ = P₁V₁ ÷ P₂ = 0.90 × 1.00 ÷ 1.00 = **0.90 L**.
Yaani bottle ka volume **10%** ghat gaya.

**Example 2: Bahar ka dhakka kitna hai?**
Pressure ka fark = 1.00 − 0.90 = **0.10 atm**. 1 atm ≈ 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa (Physics: [pressure-and-fluids-part4.md](../../physics/pressure-and-fluids/pressure-and-fluids-part4.md)), to 0.10 atm = 0.10 × 1.013 × 10⁵ = **10130 Pa** (lagbhag 1.0 × 10⁴ Pa).
Maan lo bottle ki ek chaudi deewar ka kshetrafal 400 cm² = 0.04 m² hai. Force = P × A = 10130 × 0.04 = **405.2 N** (lagbhag 405 N, yaani kareeb 40 kg ke vazan ke barabar dhakka).
Itne bade dhakke se patli bottle pich jaati hai.

> **Jaanch:** 0.90 × 1.00 = 0.90 aur 1.00 × 0.90 = 0.90, dono PV barabar ✅. 10130 Pa × 0.04 m² = 405.2 N ✅ (1 N = 1 Pa × 1 m²).

**Ulti kahani (surakshit rehna).** Agar bottle neeche band karke upar le jayein, to andar ka pressure bahar se zyada ho jaata hai aur bottle phool jaati hai (jaise chips ke packet pahaad par phool jaate hain). Isliye **neeche ya samundar ke paas band kiya hua bottle ya jar upar le jaakar kholte waqt dhakkan dhere kholo aur munh apni aur doosron ki taraf na rakho**.

**Example 3: Kaan ka "pop".**
Kaan ke parde ke peeche hawa ki ek chhoti jagah hai jo ek patli nali se gale se judi hoti hai. Maan lo upar uska pressure 0.90 atm tha aur volume 2.0 mL. Neeche bahar ka pressure 1.00 atm aur nali band hai: ab kaan ka parda andar ki taraf dabta hai aur "band" sa lagta hai. Agar nali khul jaaye to hawa ghus kar andar ka pressure barabar kar deti hai. Agar volume badal sakta to V₂ = 0.90 × 2.0 ÷ 1.00 = **1.8 mL** hota. Nigalne ya jamhai se nali khulti hai: **pop**.

> **Jaanch:** 0.90 × 2.0 = 1.8 aur 1.00 × 1.8 = 1.8 ✅.

## Part C: Saans lena (Boyle ka sabse paas ka udaharan)

```
       saans andar                       saans bahar

       hawa andar                        hawa bahar
           |                                 ^
           v                                 |
        +-----+                           +-----+
        |     |   phephde bade            |     |   phephde chhote
        |     |   V badha, P ghata        |     |   V ghata, P badha
        |     |                           +-----+
        |     |                      ======diaphragm (upar)======
        +-----+
   ======diaphragm (neeche)======
```

> **Figure 6.3** – Phephde aur diaphragm (seedhi sketch): saans andar lete waqt diaphragm neeche jaata hai, phephdon ka volume badhta hai, andar ka pressure bahar se kam ho jaata hai aur hawa andar aati hai; saans chhodte waqt ulta hota hai. (Seedhi sketch, anupaat sahi nahi.)

Kuch lines mein: saans andar lene mein hum **hawa ko khinchte nahi**. Diaphragm neeche jaata hai, phephdon ka **volume badhta hai**, Boyle ke hisaab se andar ka **pressure ghat jaata hai**, aur bahar ka zyada pressure hawa ko andar dhakel deta hai.

**Example 4: Phephde ka pressure.**
Maan lo phephde mein 2.4 L hawa hai aur pressure 1.00 atm (shaant avastha, bahar ke barabar). Diaphragm se volume 2.6 L ho jaata hai (temperature constant).
P₂ = P₁V₁ ÷ V₂ = 1.00 × 2.4 ÷ 2.6 = **0.923 atm** (3 decimal tak).
Yaani pressure bahar ke 1.00 atm se **0.077 atm kam** ho gaya, to hawa andar aayi.

> **Jaanch:** 1.00 × 2.4 = 2.4 aur 0.923 × 2.6 = 2.40 ✅.

**Ghar par adult ke saath / school lab mein teacher ke saath (phephde ka model).** Ek plastic bottle ko adult neeche se kaate (kaatne ka kaam adult hi karega). Bottle ke munh par ek gubbara andar ki taraf latkao, neeche ki khuli taraf par ek aur gubbara kas kar baandho. Neeche ke gubbare ko dhere se neeche kheencho: andar wala gubbara phoolta hai. **Koi aag, garam paani ya pump nahi.**

## Part D: Gotakhor ke bubbles aur scuba tank

Paani ke andar pressure har lagbhag **10 m ki gehraai par 1 atm** badhta hai (Physics ka approximate niyam). Isliye samundar mein 30 m neeche kul pressure = surface ka 1 atm + 3 atm = **4 atm**.

**Example 5: Bubble upar aata hai.**
30 m neeche ek bubble ka volume 2.0 mL hai. Surface par pressure 1 atm hai. Temperature barabar maanein (approximate).
V₂ = P₁V₁ ÷ P₂ = 4 × 2.0 ÷ 1 = **8.0 mL**.
Bubble ka volume **4 guna** ho gaya.

> **Jaanch:** 4 × 2.0 = 8 aur 1 × 8.0 = 8 ✅.

**Safety yaad:** Isi wajah se gotakhor **upar aate waqt saans nahi rokte**. Agar gehraai par saans bhar kar bina chhode upar aaye to phephdon ki hawa Boyle ke niyam se phailegi aur phephde ko chot pahunch sakti hai. Scuba diving sirf training aur instructor ke saath hoti hai.

**Example 6: Scuba tank (shabdon mein).** Ek scuba tank mein 10 L ka kadak cylinder hota hai jisme hawa 150 atm par bhari hai (illustrative). Cylinder ka volume badalta nahi, par agar hum us hawa ko 1 atm par bahar nikalein (temperature barabar) to:
V₂ = P₁V₁ ÷ P₂ = 150 × 10 ÷ 1 = **1500 L** hawa 1 atm par.
Gotakhor 20 m gehraai par (kul pressure = 1 + 2 = 3 atm) har saans mein surface ke hisaab se zyada hawa kharch karta hai: surface par maan lo 15 L har minute, to 3 atm par **15 × 3 = 45 L** (surface ke barabar) har minute. Tank kitna chalega?
Samay = 1500 ÷ 45 = **33.3 minute** (lagbhag, aur asli diving mein surakshit reserve alag rakhte hain).

> **Jaanch:** 150 atm × 10 L = 1500 atm·L aur 1 atm × 1500 L = 1500 atm·L ✅. 45 × 33.3 ≈ 1500 ✅.

Dhyan rakho: **scuba tank bhi cylinder hai.** Use kabhi garam na karo, dhoop mein na rakho, girao nahi.

## Part E: Tyre aur gubbara (mixed)

**Example 7: Garmi mein car ka tyre.**
Kadak tyre mein 2.0 atm (absolute pressure) hawa hai jab temperature **17 °C** hai. Dhoop wali sadak par tyre **47 °C** tak garam ho jaata hai. Volume lagbhag constant maano.
T₁ = 17 + 273 = **290 K**, T₂ = 47 + 273 = **320 K**.
P₂ = P₁T₂ ÷ T₁ = 2.0 × 320 ÷ 290 = **2.21 atm** (2 decimal).
Pressure lagbhag **0.21 atm** badha. Isliye garmi mein tyre pressure thanda hone par hi naapo, aur kabhi tyre ko dhoop mein zyada na bharo.

> **Jaanch:** 2.0 ÷ 290 = 0.0069 aur 2.21 ÷ 320 = 0.0069 ✅ (P/T barabar, rounding ke andar).

**Example 8: Helium ka gubbara upar jata hai (combined).**
Ek helium gubbara 27 °C aur 1.00 atm par 5.0 L ka hai. Wo oonchai par jaata hai jahan pressure **0.50 atm** aur temperature **−3 °C** hai. Naya volume?
T₁ = 27 + 273 = **300 K**, T₂ = −3 + 273 = **270 K**.
V₂ = P₁V₁T₂ ÷ (T₁P₂) = 1.00 × 5.0 × 270 ÷ (300 × 0.50) = 1350 ÷ 150 = **9.0 L**.
Pressure ghata (volume badha), temperature ghata (volume ghata), par pressure ka asar zyada raha. Isliye weather balloons oonchai par bahut bade ho jaate hain aur ek had par phat jaate hain.

> **Jaanch:** P₁V₁/T₁ = 1.00 × 5.0 ÷ 300 = 0.01667 aur P₂V₂/T₂ = 0.50 × 9.0 ÷ 270 = 0.01667 ✅.

## Part F: Combined problem aur moles

**Example 9: Kamre ka sample STP par.**
Ek gas ka 1.5 L sample **27 °C** aur **2.0 atm** par hai. STP (273 K, 1 atm) par uska volume aur moles?
T₁ = 300 K, T₂ = 273 K.
V₂ = P₁V₁T₂ ÷ (T₁P₂) = 2.0 × 1.5 × 273 ÷ (300 × 1) = 819 ÷ 300 = **2.73 L**.
Moles = V ÷ 22.4 = 2.73 ÷ 22.4 = **0.122 mol** (3 decimal tak). (Topic 5 ka molar volume; thoda extra, Class 10 overlap.)

> **Jaanch:** 2.0 × 1.5 ÷ 300 = 0.0100 aur 1 × 2.73 ÷ 273 = 0.0100 ✅.

## Part G: Seedhe "kaun sa niyam?" sawaal

**Example 10:** Neeche ke sawaalon mein niyam aur variable pehchano.
- Football dhoop mein rakhne par kadak ho gaya: football kadak (V constant) hai, T badha, to P badha. **P/T niyam**.
- Syringe ka nozzle band karke plunger dabaaya: T aur matra constant, **Boyle**.
- Garam paani ke upar gubbara phoola (adult paani sambhaale): P constant, T badha, **Charles**.
- Pahaad se utarte waqt band bottle pichki: bahar ka P badha, bottle flexible: **Boyle** (aur andar ka P nahi badla).
- Samundar mein bubble ka upar aana: P ghata, V badha, **Boyle**.
- Weather balloon: P, V, T teeno badle, **combined**.

**Example 11: Ek sawaal jisme niyam lagana galat hai.**
Ek gubbara (jisme se gas dheere dheere leak ho rahi hai) sikud raha hai. Yahan **matra badal rahi hai**, isliye teen niyam seedhe nahi lagte. Gas ki matra badalne ka hisaab aage ki class mein aata hai (Topic 5 mein sirf Avogadro ka niyam dekha).

## Real gases aur aage ki class

Humne ye saare niyam maan kar chale ki gas bilkul "saaf" hai (ideal). Asli gaas **bahut zyada pressure par aur bahut kam temperature par** in niyamon se thoda hat jaati hai (kan thode paas aa jaate hain aur ek doosre ko khinchte hain; aur gaas liquid bhi ban jaati hai). Kamre ke temperature aur halke pressure par ye niyam bahut achhe chalte hain. **Aage ki class mein** in teeno niyamon aur Avogadro ko ek hi equation PV = nRT mein jodenge, aur alag-alag gaason ke mixture, diffusion aur real gases ko ache se padhenge. Abhi ye sirf ek jhalak hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Celsius mein T daalna**
Charles aur P/T mein hamesha **kelvin**. 27 °C ko 27 nahi, **300 K** likho.

**Galti 2: Gauge pressure aur absolute pressure milana**
Tyre pe likha "32" (psi mein) gauge pressure (bahar se zyada) hota hai. Gaas ke niyam mein poora (absolute) pressure lagta hai: gauge + lagbhag atmospheric pressure.

**Galti 3: Band cylinder mein volume badalna**
Kadak cylinder ka V constant hai. Wahan P/T niyam lagta hai.

**Galti 4: Gas ki matra badalne par bhi niyam lagana**
Agar gas leak ho rahi hai ya nayi daali ja rahi hai, to teen niyam seedhe nahi lagte.

**Galti 5: Gotakhor ka saans rokna**
Upar aate waqt saans na roko: volume badhta hai (Boyle).

**Galti 6: "Garam karke dekh lo"**
Band cylinder, can ya bottle ko garam karna prayog nahi, khatra hai.

**Galti 7: Bahar ka pressure ignore karna**
Bottle pichkane wala asli kaaran "bahar ka zyada pressure" hai, andar ka pressure badla nahi.

## Quick Recap

- Pehle sawaal: kya constant hai? T constant: Boyle; P constant: Charles; V constant: P/T niyam; teeno badle: combined. Temperature hamesha kelvin.
- Ghat road ki bottle: bahar ka pressure badha, andar wahi raha, bottle pichki. Kaan ka pop bhi isi wajah se.
- Saans: diaphragm neeche, volume badha, pressure ghata, hawa andar.
- Gotakhor: har 10 m par lagbhag +1 atm; bubble upar aate waqt uska volume badhta hai; saans nahi rokte.
- Garmi mein tyre aur band cylinder ka pressure badhta hai: ye khatarnak ho sakta hai.
- Real gases bahut zyada P aur bahut kam T par niyam se hat jaati hain. PV = nRT aage ki class mein.
- Cylinder, aerosol can, band bottle, tyre: **kabhi garam na karo, dhoop mein na chhodo.**

## Practice Questions

6. A gas of 4.0 L at 2.0 atm and 300 K is changed to 1.0 atm and 450 K. What is the new volume?

(b) A diver breathes 20 L per minute at the surface; at 30 m (4 atm) how many litres of surface-equivalent air per minute does he use, and for how many minutes does the full tank last (ignoring any reserve)?

9. A sealed packet of chips of volume 200 mL is closed at sea level (1.00 atm) and taken to a hill where the pressure is 0.80 atm (same temperature). Why does the packet puff up, and what is its new volume?

10. Choose the correct law (Boyle, Charles, pressure-temperature, or combined) and give the constant(s) for each: (a) a steel gas cylinder left in sunlight; (b) a bicycle pump with its outlet blocked, pushed in slowly; (c) a party balloon carried from an air-conditioned room into a warm room; (d) a thin sealed plastic bottle carried down a hill on a day when the plain is also warmer than the hilltop.

11. A sealed empty plastic bottle is carried from a hill where the air pressure is 0.85 atm down to a plain at 1.00 atm. (a) What is the extra pressure difference in pascals (1 atm = 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa)? (b) If one flat face has an area of 300 cm², what force acts on it? (c) Why is it unsafe to heat a sealed bottle to "reverse" the dent?

12. A helium balloon of 2.0 L at 27 °C and 1.0 atm rises to a height where the pressure is 0.40 atm and the temperature is −33 °C. Find the new volume.

### Answers

1. T constant, to Boyle: P₂ = P₁V₁ ÷ V₂ = 1.0 × 600 ÷ 150 = **4.0 atm**. Jaanch: 1.0 × 600 = 600 aur 4.0 × 150 = 600 ✅.

2. (a) T₁ = 300 K, T₂ = 87 + 273 = 360 K. Can ka volume kadak hai, to P₂ = 4.0 × 360 ÷ 300 = **4.8 atm**. (b) T₂ = T₁ × P₂ ÷ P₁ = 300 × 7.0 ÷ 4.0 = **525 K**, aur 525 − 273 = **252 °C**. (c) Garmi se andar ka pressure badhta hai (kan tezi se aur zyada takraate hain), aur band can phat sakta hai: ye khatarnak hai. Jalane par yahi hota hai, isliye aerosol can ko kabhi garam mat karo ya aag mein mat daalo. Jaanch: 4.0 ÷ 300 = 0.01333 aur 4.8 ÷ 360 = 0.01333 ✅; 7.0 ÷ 525 = 0.01333 ✅.

3. T₁ = 27 + 273 = 300 K, T₂ = −23 + 273 = 250 K. V₂ = V₁T₂ ÷ T₁ = 3.0 × 250 ÷ 300 = **2.5 L**. Kelvin isliye kyunki gaas ka volume absolute temperature ke seedhe anupaat mein hota hai; Celsius ka 0 asli zero nahi hai. Jaanch: 3.0 ÷ 300 = 0.0100 aur 2.5 ÷ 250 = 0.0100 ✅.

4. 40 m par kul pressure = 1 + 4 = 5 atm. V₂ = 5 × 1.5 ÷ 1 = **7.5 mL**. Upar aate waqt saans rok kar rakhne par phephdon ki hawa isi tarah phailegi (Boyle) aur phephde ko chot pahunch sakti hai, isliye gotakhor saans dhere dhere chhodte hue upar aata hai. Jaanch: 5 × 1.5 = 7.5 aur 1 × 7.5 = 7.5 ✅.

5. P₂ = 1.0 × 3.0 ÷ 3.3 = **0.909 atm** (3 decimal). Ye 1.0 atm se kam hai, to bahar ka zyada pressure hawa ko andar dhakelta hai: **hawa andar aayegi**. Jaanch: 3.0 × 1.0 = 3.0 aur 0.909 × 3.3 = 3.00 ✅.

6. V₂ = P₁V₁T₂ ÷ (T₁P₂) = 2.0 × 4.0 × 450 ÷ (300 × 1.0) = 3600 ÷ 300 = **12 L**. Jaanch: 2.0 × 4.0 ÷ 300 = 0.02667 aur 1.0 × 12 ÷ 450 = 0.02667 ✅.

7. T₁ = 127 + 273 = 400 K. V₂ = 1.5 × 6.0 × 273 ÷ (400 × 1) = 2457 ÷ 400 = **6.14 L** (2 decimal, lagbhag 6.1425). Moles = 6.14 ÷ 22.4 = **0.274 mol** (3 decimal). Jaanch: 1.5 × 6.0 ÷ 400 = 0.0225 aur 1 × 6.1425 ÷ 273 = 0.0225 ✅.

8. (a) V = 200 × 15 ÷ 1 = **3000 L**. (b) 4 atm par kharch = 20 × 4 = **80 L har minute** (surface ke barabar). Samay = 3000 ÷ 80 = **37.5 minute**. Jaanch: 80 × 37.5 = 3000 ✅. Dhyan rakho: tank cylinder hai, garam ya dhoop mein nahi rakhte.

9. Bahar ka pressure kam ho gaya aur packet ke andar ka pressure pehle jaisa tha, to andar ka zyada pressure packet ko phoola deta hai. T constant, Boyle: V₂ = P₁V₁ ÷ P₂ = 1.00 × 200 ÷ 0.80 = **250 mL**. Jaanch: 1.00 × 200 = 200 aur 0.80 × 250 = 200 ✅.

10. (a) Cylinder kadak, V constant: **pressure-temperature niyam** (V aur matra constant). (b) Dheere dabaya, to T aur matra constant: **Boyle**. (c) Gubbare ke andar ka pressure lagbhag bahar ke 1 atm jitna hi rehta hai, sirf T badla: **Charles** (P aur matra constant). (d) Bahar ka P badha (bottle pichki, V ghata) aur T bhi badha: P, V, T teeno badle, to **combined** (sirf matra constant).

11. (a) Pressure ka fark = 1.00 − 0.85 = 0.15 atm; 0.15 × 1.013 × 10⁵ = **15195 Pa** (lagbhag 1.5 × 10⁴ Pa). (b) 300 cm² = 0.03 m² (1 m² = 10000 cm²). Force = 15195 × 0.03 = **455.85 N**, yaani lagbhag **456 N**. (c) Band bottle ko garam karne se uske andar ka pressure badhta hai (P/T niyam) aur wo phat sakti hai, isliye band bottle ko kabhi garam mat karo. Dent khulwane ke liye dhakkan dhere kholo ya adult ko dikhao. Jaanch: 15195 Pa × 0.03 m² = 455.85 N ✅.

12. T₁ = 300 K, T₂ = −33 + 273 = 240 K. V₂ = 1.0 × 2.0 × 240 ÷ (300 × 0.40) = 480 ÷ 120 = **4.0 L**. Jaanch: 1.0 × 2.0 ÷ 300 = 0.006667 aur 0.40 × 4.0 ÷ 240 = 0.006667 ✅.

## Chapter Ka Saar: Gas Laws

Ek nazar mein poore chapter ka safar: gas ke chaar variables, teen niyam, STP aur molar volume, aur roz ke jeevan mein kaun sa niyam kab lagta hai.
