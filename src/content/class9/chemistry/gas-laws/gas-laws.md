## PT period mein volleyball

Mr. Marathe PT period mein bachchon ko volleyball ki practice karwa rahe the. Bhavik ek pichki hui, thodi dheeli volleyball le kar aaya. "Sir, ye to dab rahi hai. Isse kaise khelenge?"

Bhavik ne socha. "Sir... pump se **zyada hawa** andar gayi?"

"Bilkul. Aur uska asar kya hua? Wahi pressure hai jise tum mehsoos kar rahe ho. Chalo, ab ye samjhein ki ye pressure kya cheez hai, aur ise naapte kaise hain."

## Part A: Gas ke kan aur deewar (recall)

Chapter 1 mein humne dekha tha ki gas ke kan **bahut door-door** hote hain, **random** dishaon mein, bahut **tez** daudte hain, aur jagah ghere rakhne ke liye har taraf phail jaate hain. Chalte-chalte wo ek-doosre se aur container ki deewar se **takrate** hain.

Ab ek baat jodte hain. Har takkar mein kan deewar ko chhota sa **dhakka** deta hai. Ek kan ka dhakka bahut chhota hota hai, par ek container mein **arabon-kharabon** kan hote hain aur wo har second mein bahut baar takrate hain. Itne dhakke milkar ek **lagaataar, samaan bal** jaisa mehsoos hote hain. Ye hi **gas ka pressure** hai.

```
   +-----------------------------------+
   ! o->        <-o            o       !
   !        o                   |      !
   !  o         ->o        <-o  v      !
   !       o->           o             !
   !  <-o          o->           o->   !
   +-----------------------------------+
        ^ ^ ^ ^ ^   (deewar par dhakke)
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Gas ke kan container mein har dishaa mein tez daudte hain aur deewar se takrate hain. Har takkar deewar par ek chhota dhakka hai, aur bahut saari takkaron ka milkar asar **gas ka pressure** hai. (Chitra mein sirf kuch kan dikhaaye hain; asal mein arabon-kharabon hote hain.)

Physics ke chapter mein hum ne pressure ko **bal ÷ kshetrafal** (force per area) ke roop mein padha tha, aur dekha tha ki hawa ka apna bhi pressure hota hai (atmospheric pressure, barometer). Wahi baat yahan gas ke kan ki takkaron se samjhi jaa rahi hai: Physics ne bataya ki pressure **kya** hai, Chemistry batati hai ki wo **kyun** hota hai.

Isse ek seedha nateeja nikalta hai: **zyada takkar ya zor se takkar, to zyada pressure.** Volleyball mein pump se zyada kan (zyada hawa) gaye, to deewar par takkarein bahut badh gayi, aur ball kadak ho gayi.

> **Jaanch:** "Gas ka pressure isliye hota hai kyunki gas ke kan upar se neeche girte hain, gurutvaakarshan ki wajah se." ❌ Nahi. Gas ke kan har dishaa mein tez daudte hue deewar se takrate hain; isi liye pressure **har dishaa mein** barabar hota hai, sirf neeche nahi.

## Part B: Gas ke chaar naap

Kisi bhi gas ko poori tarah bataane ke liye chaar cheezein chaahiye:

1. **Pressure (P):** gas deewar par kitna dhakka deti hai.
2. **Volume (V):** gas kitni jagah ghere hai.
3. **Temperature (T):** gas kitni garam hai.
4. **Matra (n):** gas kitni hai (kitne kan).

```
          +-----------------------------+
          !   GAS KA CONTAINER          !
          !                             !
          !   P : dhakka (deewar par)   !
          !   V : jagah (container ka)  !
          !   T : garam ya thanda       !
          !   n : kitni gas (mole)      !
          !                             !
          +-----------------------------+
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Gas ke chaar naap: pressure P, volume V, temperature T aur matra n. Aage ke topics mein ek-ek karke dekhenge ki inme se do ke beech kya rishta hai, jabki baaki do sthir rakhe jaate hain.

Ye chaaron **ek-doosre se jude** hain. Volleyball mein humne n (hawa ki matra) badhai, to P badha, jabki V lagbhag wahi raha aur T bhi wahi raha. Aisa kyun hota hai, ye is chapter ke aage ke topics mein dekhenge. Abhi sirf ye jaan lo ki ek badalne par baaki badal sakte hain.

## Part C: Ikaaiyan (units) aur unka badalna

### Pressure ki ikaaiyan

Alag-alag jagah par pressure alag ikaai mein likha jaata hai. Teen ikaaiyan jaan lo:

- **atm** (atmosphere): 1 atm = samudra ke tal par hawa ka pressure.
- **mm Hg** (millimetre of mercury): barometer mein paare ke stambh ki oonchai. **1 atm = 760 mm Hg** (yaani 76 cm Hg).
- **Pascal (Pa)**: SI ikaai. **1 atm ≈ 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa** (ek lakh teras hazaar Pa ke lagbhag; sahi maan 101325 Pa).

Isse tum kisi bhi pressure ko ek ikaai se doosri mein badal sakte ho:

- atm se mm Hg: **760 se guna karo.**
- mm Hg se atm: **760 se bhaag do.**
- atm se Pa: 101325 se guna karo (lagbhag 1.013 × 10⁵).

### Volume ki ikaaiyan

- **1 L (litre) = 1000 mL = 1000 cm³ = 1 dm³.**
- **1 m³ = 1000 L** (Chapter Water mein bhi yahi dekha tha).
- mL aur cm³ **ek hi** cheez hain: 1 mL = 1 cm³.

### Temperature: Kelvin zaroori kyun

Chapter 1 mein tumne Kelvin scale dekhi thi: **K = °C + 273** (Topic 3, [yahan](../matter-in-our-surroundings/matter-in-our-surroundings-part3.md)). Is chapter ke hisaab mein **hamesha kelvin hi vaparna** padega. Isliye aage ke topics mein ye galti na ho, aaj hi yaad kar lo: °C se K banane ke liye **273 jodo**, K se °C banane ke liye **273 ghatao**.

### Matra

Gas ki matra ko **gram** mein bhi naap sakte hain aur **mole** mein bhi. Chapter 4 mein hum ne mole dekha tha: **n = m ÷ M** (m = gram mein dravyamaan, M = molar mass g/mol), aur 1 mole mein **6.022 × 10²³** kan hote hain.

```
   PRESSURE                      VOLUME                  TEMPERATURE
   -----------------------       --------------------    ------------------
   x 760  : atm  -> mm Hg        x 1000 : L  -> mL       + 273 : °C -> K
   / 760  : mm Hg -> atm         / 1000 : mL -> L        - 273 : K  -> °C
   x 101325 : atm -> Pa          x 1000 : m³ -> L
   / 101325 : Pa  -> atm         / 1000 : L  -> m³       (mL = cm³ hi hota hai)
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Ikaai badalne ka chart: pressure (atm, mm Hg, Pa), volume (L, mL, m³) aur temperature (°C, K). Chhote se bade ikaai mein jaate waqt guna ya bhaag ka chunav dhyan se karo.

## Part D: Ikaaiyan badalne ke solved examples

Ab Bhavik ne Mr. Marathe ke kehne par kuch ikaaiyan badal kar dekhi. (Saare maan illustrative hain.)

**Example 1: atm se mm Hg.** Ek gas ka pressure **3 atm** hai. mm Hg mein?
3 × 760 = **2280 mm Hg**.

**Example 2: chhota atm.** Pressure **0.25 atm** hai. mm Hg mein?
0.25 × 760 = **190 mm Hg**.

**Example 3: atm se Pa.** Pressure **1.5 atm** hai. Pa mein?
1.5 × 101325 = 151987.5 ≈ **1.52 × 10⁵ Pa** (3 significant figures tak round kiya).

**Example 4: L se mL, mL se L.** Ek ball ka aayatan **3.2 L** hai. mL mein 3.2 × 1000 = **3200 mL (= 3200 cm³)**. Ek syringe mein **450 mL** hawa hai. Litre mein 450 ÷ 1000 = **0.45 L**.

**Example 5: °C se K.** Kamre ka temperature **27 °C** hai: 27 + 273 = **300 K**. Ek thandi raat **−73 °C**: −73 + 273 = **200 K**. Aur **400 K** ko °C mein: 400 − 273 = **127 °C**.

**Example 6: sab ek saath.** Ek ghubbare mein **1.2 L** gas **37 °C** par aur **760 mm Hg** pressure par hai. Chaaron naap sahi ikaai mein likho.
V = 1.2 × 1000 = **1200 mL**. T = 37 + 273 = **310 K**. P = 760 ÷ 760 = **1 atm**. (Matra abhi nahi di gayi.)

**Example 7: gas ki matra (Chapter 4 se).** **4 g helium** kitne mole hai? He ka molar mass 4 g/mol hai.
n = 4 ÷ 4 = **1 mole**. Isme 6.022 × 10²³ kan hain.

> **Jaanch:** 3 atm × 760 = 2280 mm Hg, aur wapas 2280 ÷ 760 = 3 atm ✅. Aur 310 − 273 = 37 °C ✅.

## Part E: Ek-ek badlaav kyun? (kyun hum ek cheez sthir rakhte hain)

Mr. Marathe ne Bhavik se poochha: "Agar mai tumse pooch lu: ball kadak kyun hui, to tum kaun si cheez badalne ko kahoge?"

"Sir... hawa ki matra."

"Ab socho agar ball garam dhoop mein bhi rakhi ho, aur pump bhi chal raha ho, aur ball ki deewar bhi thodi phool rahi ho. Tab kaise pata chalega ki kadakpan kis wajah se aayi? Ye alag-alag karne ka **vaigyanik tareeka** hai: **ek samay mein sirf ek cheez badlo, baaki sthir (constant) rakho.**"

Isi tareeke se purane vaigyanikon ne gas ke **niyam** khoje. Chaar naap hain: P, V, T, n. Agar **n aur T** sthir hon aur hum sirf **P aur V** ko dekhein, to ek rishta milta hai (agle topic mein). Agar **n aur P** sthir hon aur **V aur T** dekhein, to doosra rishta (Topic 3). Agar **n aur V** sthir hon aur **P aur T**, to teesra (Topic 4). Ye sab hum ek-ek karke sikhenge.

Abhi ek **jhalak** hi lo. Neeche ki table mein shabdon mein likha hai ki jab ek cheez badalti hai aur baaki sthir rahte hain, to dusra kis disha mein jaata hai. (Ye abhi sirf **pahchaan** ke liye hai; koi niyam yaad nahi karna, aur kaaran aage ke topics mein milega.)

| Kya sthir rahe | Kya badla | Kya hota dikhta hai |
|---|---|---|
| T aur n | V ghatao | P badhta hai (syringe ko dabane par) |
| P aur n | T badhao | V badhta hai (garam hawa phailti hai) |
| V aur n | T badhao | P badhta hai (band cylinder garam ho to) |
| T aur V | n badhao | P badhta hai (volleyball mein pump) |

Table ki aakhri line volleyball ki hai: pump se n badha, to P badha. **Ye sab abhi pehchaan hai, hisaab nahi.**

## Part F: Ghar par karne wale safe prayog

> **Safety pehle:** Ye sab prayog **sirf ghar par adult ke saath** ya **school lab mein teacher ke saath** hi karo. Gas ke cylinder (LPG), aerosol/deodorant can aur gaadi ke tyre **kabhi garam mat karo aur dhoop mein mat chhodo**, kyunki andar ka pressure badhne se wo phat sakte hain. Band bottle ya can ko kabhi garam mat karo. Ghubbara kisi **adult ya pump** se phulwao, kisi gas cylinder se kabhi nahi, aur aag ke paas kuch bhi mat karo.

**Prayog 1: Ghubbara dabake dekho (ghar par adult ke saath).**
Ek adult ek ghubbara thoda phulaye aur munh baandh de. Use dono haathon se halke se dabao. Mehsoos karo: ghubbara **dabta hai** par andar ki hawa wapas **dhakel bhi rehti hai**, jaise koi chhota sa spring ho. Ye wahi pressure hai jo andar ke kan ki takkaron se bana hai. Zyada zor se mat dabao, ghubbara phat sakta hai.

**Prayog 2: Syringe se hawa dabaana (ghar par adult ke saath).**
Bina needle wali plastic syringe lo (medical dukaan ya science lab se, adult ke saath). Plunger ko beech tak kheencho taaki andar hawa bhar jaaye, phir nozzle par apna **angootha** rakh kar use band kar do. Ab plunger ko dheere se dabao. Mehsoos karo: plunger **kuch door tak dabta hai, phir kadak** ho jaata hai. Angoothha hata do to plunger wapas pichhe aa jaata hai. Kya hua? Andar ke kan jagah kam paa kar deewar par zyada takrate hain: pressure badha (aur ye tumhara agle topic ka sawaal hai!).

**Prayog 3: Ball ka kadakpan (ghar par adult ke saath).**
Do ball lo, ek dheeli aur ek kadak (volleyball ya football), aur ungli se dabake farq mehsoos karo. Dheeli ball mein kam hawa, kadak mein zyada hawa. Ye **n** badalne ka seedha asar hai. (Ball par kudna ya use bahut garam jagah mein rakhna nahi hai.)

**Dhyan rakho:** Syringe mein sirf hawa; koi liquid, gas ya chemical mat bharo. Needle bilkul mat lagana.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Pressure sirf neeche ki taraf hota hai"**
Gas ka pressure **har dishaa** mein deewar par hota hai, kyunki kan har dishaa mein daudte hain.

**Galti 2: "1 atm = 100 mm Hg"**
1 atm = **760 mm Hg** (76 cm Hg). Cm Hg aur mm Hg ke beech 10 ka farq yaad rakho.

**Galti 3: "1 m³ = 100 L"**
1 m³ = **1000 L**. (Pichhle chapter mein bhi yahi dekha.)

**Galti 4: "mL aur cm³ alag hain"**
Dono **barabar** hain: 1 mL = 1 cm³. Aur 1 L = 1000 cm³.

**Galti 5: "Temperature hamesha °C mein"**
Gas ke hisaab mein hum **kelvin** vaparte hain: K = °C + 273. °C mein hisaab karne se aage galat jawab aayenge (Topic 3 mein dekhenge).

**Galti 6: "Gas ka volume uske kan ka size hai"**
Gas ka volume **container ki jagah** hai, kan ke apne size nahi. Gas mein kan door-door hote hain, isliye volume bahut had tak khaali jagah hai.

**Galti 7: "Dheeli ball mein hawa nahi hoti"**
Dheeli ball mein bhi hawa hoti hai, bas kam. Pressure kam hone ke karan wo dabti hai.

**Galti 8: "Band container ko garam karna ek achha prayog hai"**
Bilkul nahi. Band cylinder, can ya bottle ko kabhi garam mat karo. Pressure badhne se wo phat sakta hai (ye Topic 4 mein samjhenge).

## Quick Recap

- Gas ke kan tez, random aur door-door hote hain, aur deewar se takrate hain. In takkaron ka milkar asar **gas ka pressure** hai.
- Gas ke **chaar naap** hain: **P** (pressure), **V** (volume), **T** (temperature) aur **n** (matra).
- Pressure: 1 atm = 760 mm Hg = 76 cm Hg ≈ 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa (101325 Pa).
- Volume: 1 L = 1000 mL = 1000 cm³ = 1 dm³; 1 m³ = 1000 L.
- Temperature: K = °C + 273 (gas ke hisaab mein kelvin zaroori).
- Matra: n = m ÷ M (mole), aur 1 mole mein 6.022 × 10²³ kan.
- Ek samay mein sirf ek cheez badlo, baaki sthir rakho: yahi gas ke niyamon ka tareeka hai.
- Cylinder, aerosol can aur tyre ko kabhi garam ya dhoop mein mat rakho.

## Practice Questions

1. Convert a pressure of 4 atm into mm Hg, and a pressure of 1520 mm Hg into atm.

2. A gas has a pressure of 38 cm Hg. Express it in (a) mm Hg and (b) atm.

3. Convert: (a) 0.6 L to mL, (b) 2500 cm³ to L, (c) 1.2 m³ to L.

4. Convert to kelvin: (a) 45 °C, (b) 25 °C. Convert to °C: (c) 500 K, (d) 100 K.

5. A pressure of 202650 Pa is given. Express it in atm. Is it more or less than the normal atmospheric pressure?

6. A sample of oxygen gas has a mass of 16 g. Using the molar mass of O₂ as 32 g/mol, find the number of moles and the number of molecules in it (use 6.022 × 10²³ per mole).

7. A cylinder contains 22 g of carbon dioxide (molar mass 44 g/mol). How many moles is that, and how does the amount compare with 1 mole?

8. Write down all four measurements of a gas sample in suitable units: volume 750 cm³, pressure 380 mm Hg, temperature 25 °C, mass 2 g of hydrogen (molar mass of H₂ is 2 g/mol). Give V in L, P in atm, T in K, n in mol.

9. State True or False and correct the false ones:
    (a) The pressure of a gas is caused by its particles colliding with the walls.
    (b) 1 atm equals 100 mm Hg.
    (c) 1 mL is equal to 1 cm³.
    (d) The temperature in gas calculations is taken in °C.

10. Why should an LPG cylinder never be kept in the sun or heated? Answer in one or two sentences, using the idea of gas pressure from this topic.

### Answers

1. 4 atm × 760 = **3040 mm Hg**. Wapas: 1520 ÷ 760 = **2 atm** ✅ (2 × 760 = 1520).

2. (a) 38 cm Hg × 10 = **380 mm Hg** (1 cm = 10 mm). (b) 380 ÷ 760 = **0.5 atm** ✅ (adha atm).

3. (a) 0.6 × 1000 = **600 mL**. (b) 2500 cm³ = 2500 mL, 2500 ÷ 1000 = **2.5 L**. (c) 1.2 × 1000 = **1200 L**.

4. (a) 45 + 273 = **318 K**. (b) 25 + 273 = **298 K**. (c) 500 − 273 = **227 °C**. (d) 100 − 273 = **−173 °C**.

5. 202650 ÷ 101325 = **2 atm** ✅. Ye normal hawa ke pressure (1 atm) se **zyada** hai, aur ek atm ka theek double hai.

6. n = m ÷ M = 16 ÷ 32 = **0.5 mol**. Molecules = 0.5 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **3.011 × 10²³** molecules ✅.

7. n = 22 ÷ 44 = **0.5 mol**. Ye 1 mole ka **aadha** hai, yaani 3.011 × 10²³ molecules.

8. V = 750 ÷ 1000 = **0.75 L**. P = 380 ÷ 760 = **0.5 atm**. T = 25 + 273 = **298 K**. n = 2 ÷ 2 = **1 mol** ✅.

9. (a) **True** ✅. Kan deewar se takrate hain, aur milkar ye pressure banta hai. (b) **False** ❌. 1 atm = **760 mm Hg**. (c) **True** ✅. (d) **False** ❌. Temperature **kelvin** mein liya jaata hai (K = °C + 273).

10. Cylinder band hota hai, aur garam karne par andar ki gas ke kan tez daudte hain aur deewar par zor se takrate hain, to **pressure badhta hai**. Agar pressure cylinder ki sahan-shakti se zyada ho gaya to cylinder phat sakta hai, isliye use sada thanda aur chaanv mein rakho. (Iski poori ginti Topic 4 mein.)
