# Topic 2: Hydrogen Ki Lab Mein Taiyari

Pichhle topic mein humne jaana ki hydrogen sabse halka aur brahmand mein sabse zyada milne wala element hai, par **dharti par ye lagbhag akela nahi milta**: ye paani, hydrocarbon aur jeevit cheezon ke andar **juda hua** hai. To agar hame hydrogen **gas** chahiye, to use kisi compound se **nikaalna** padega. Aaj hum seekhenge ki school ki lab mein ye kaise nikala jaata hai: ek dhatu aur paani mein ghola hua halka acid (**dilute acid**) ka milan. Dhyan rahe: ye poora topic **dekhne aur samajhne** ka hai, karne ka nahi. Hydrogen bahut jwalansheel gas hai, isliye isse jude saare prayog **sirf teacher** karte hain.

## Mr. Mirajkar ki yaad aur ek "pop"

Tanisha school se laut kar apne chacha Mr. Mirajkar ko apni science copy dikha rahi thi. Copy ke ek panne par teacher ne likha tha: "Agle hafte lab mein **hydrogen ka teacher demo** hoga. Hamare teacher ne ek chhote se flask mein **zinc ke tukde** daale, upar se **halka acid** daala, aur flask mein **bulbule** uthne lage. Phir unhone gas ko ek chhoti test tube mein jama kiya, tube ko ulta pakda, aur door se uske munh par **jalti hui patli teeli** (splinter) laayi. Bas ek **'pop!'** ki chhoti, **cheekhti hui awaaz** aayi. Poori class chaunk gayi. Teacher ne kaha tha ki ye awaaz hi hydrogen ki pehchaan hai."

Tanisha ne poochha, "To bulbule sirf zinc aur acid se kaise aa gaye?" Mr. Mirajkar muskuraye. Isliye **student kabhi hydrogen banate, jama karte, jalate ya test nahi karte.** Ye sab **sirf teacher** karte hain, **bahut chhoti matra** mein, **koi aag ya jalti cheez apparatus ke paas nahi**, hawadaar kamre mein aur **safety screen** ke peeche. Sirf dekho aur samjho. Is topic mein jahan bhi prayog hai, uska label hai: **"sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo)"**.

## Part A: Kaun si dhatu hydrogen deti hai, aur kyun?

Chapter 7 mein humne **reactivity series** dekhi thi: dhatuon ki wo list jo zyada reactive se kam reactive ki taraf chalti hai ([chemical-changes-and-reactions-part3.md](../chemical-changes-and-reactions/chemical-changes-and-reactions-part3.md)):

**K, Na, Ca, Mg, Al, Zn, Fe, Pb, H, Cu, Hg, Ag, Au**

Is list mein **hydrogen (H) bhi shamil hai**, jaise wo bhi koi dhatu ho: ye uska ek upyogi trick hai, ki hum dhatu aur hydrogen ki **taakat ki tulna** kar sakein. Dilute acid mein hydrogen pehle se juda hota hai. Ab niyam seedha hai:

> **Jo dhatu series mein hydrogen se UPAR hai, wo dilute acid se hydrogen ko bahar nikaal sakti hai** (displace kar sakti hai). **Jo dhatu hydrogen se NEECHE hai, wo nahi kar sakti.**

Bilkul Chapter 7 ke bench wale bade bachche ki tarah: zyada taakatwar dhatu kamzor ki jagah le leti hai. Yahan "kamzor" hydrogen hai.

Series ko hydrogen ki line ke saath aise padho:

- **Line ke upar, bahut tez ya khatarnak:** K, Na, Ca (lab mein is prayog ke liye use nahi hote).
- **Line ke upar, kaam ki:** Mg (tez), Al (oxide ki parat ki wajah se shuru mein dheere), Zn (madhyam, lab ke liye sabse achhi), Fe (dheere), Pb (bahut dheere, lab mein kaam ka nahi).
- **Hydrogen (H): yahi "hydrogen ki line" hai.**
- **Line ke neeche, dilute acid se hydrogen nahi milti:** Cu, Hg, Ag, Au.

### Lab mein zinc hi kyun?

- **Mg** bahut tezi se reaction karti hai: bulbule itni jaldi aate hain ki gas ko sambhalna mushkil, aur garmi bhi badh jaati hai.
- **Fe** bahut dheere reaction karti hai: gas banne mein der lagti hai.
- **Zn** beech ki dhatu hai: bulbule **saaf aur dheere-dheere**, nikalne ki raftaar teacher ke qaabu mein. Isliye **zinc ke granules (dane)** lab ki pasandida dhatu hain.
- **Na aur K** (aur Ca) ka to paani ke saath hi bahut tez aur khatarnak reaction hota hai (Chapter 8 mein inka paani ke saath vyavhaar sirf bataaya gaya tha). Inhe **kabhi acid ke saath nahi** milaate: ye lab ke is prayog ke liye bilkul theek nahi.

**Tanisha ka sawaal: kya zinc ke bajaye tamba (copper) kaam karega?** Nahi. Copper hydrogen se **neeche** hai, to wo dilute acid mein se hydrogen ko **nahi hata sakta**: na bulbule, na gas.

### Do aam acid: sulphuric aur hydrochloric

Hum do dilute acid dekhenge (acid kya hote hain, ye Class 10 mein poori tarah padhoge; abhi bas itna jaano ki inke ghol mein hydrogen juda hota hai):

- **Dilute sulphuric acid**, H₂SO₄: zinc, iron ya magnesium ke saath chalta hai.
- **Dilute hydrochloric acid**, HCl: zinc, magnesium ke saath chalta hai.

**Nitric acid (HNO₃) alag hai.** Ye bhi ek acid hai, par ye dhatuon ke saath **alag tarah se** vyavhaar karta hai aur aam taur par **hydrogen nahi deta**. Isliye lab ke hydrogen prayog mein nitric acid **nahi** use hoti. Iski wajah Class 10 mein padhoge; abhi bas ye yaad rakho ki **har acid "dhatu + acid = hydrogen" wala niyam nahi maanta.**

## Part B: Apparatus: flask, thistle funnel aur delivery tube

**Sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo).** Teacher ek **conical flask** leta hai. Flask ke munh par ek **cork** (airtight, yaani hawa na jaane de) lagta hai. Cork ke do chhed hote hain: ek mein **thistle funnel**, doosre mein **delivery tube**. Funnel ki naali itni lambi hoti hai ki uska neeche ka sira **acid ke andar dooba** rahe (ye bahut zaroori hai, aage dekhenge). Flask mein pehle **zinc ke granules** rakhe jaate hain, phir funnel se **dilute acid** daala jaata hai.

```
                \         /
                 \       /      <- thistle funnel
                  \     /          (isse acid daalte hain)
                   \   /
                    │ │       ┌──────────────> jama karne wali jar ki taraf
                    │ │       │   <- delivery tube (gas isi se bahar jaati hai)
         cork -> [==│ │=======│==]
                 │  │ │       ╵  │       <- tube ka sira cork ke thoda neeche,
                /   │ │           \         acid se kaafi upar
               /    │ │            \
              /     │ │             \
             │      │ │              │
             │~~~~~~│~│~~~~~~~~~~~~~~│   <- dilute acid ka star
             │      │ │              │   <- funnel ki nok acid ke andar
             │   Zn   Zn   Zn   Zn   │   <- zinc ke granules
             └───────────────────────┘
                   conical flask
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Hydrogen banane ka apparatus (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo): conical flask, cork, thistle funnel aur delivery tube. Funnel ki naali ka neeche ka sira acid ke star ke **neeche** hai, taaki bani hui gas funnel se bahar na nikal jaaye. Delivery tube ka doosra sira gas ko jama karne wali jar tak jaata hai. Chitra sirf nidarshan hai, naap sahi nahi.

**Funnel ki nok acid ke andar kyun?** Reaction mein jaise hi hydrogen banti hai, wo gas hai aur upar uthna chahti hai. Agar funnel ki nok acid ke **upar** ho, to gas funnel ke raaste hi **bahar nikal jaayegi** aur delivery tube tak kam pahunchegi (aur kamre mein bhi gas phail sakti hai, jo khatarnak hai). Nok acid mein doobi ho to acid khud **dhakkan ki tarah** funnel ka raasta band kar deta hai, aur gas ko delivery tube se hi jaana padta hai.

**Cork airtight kyun?** Agar flask se gas idhar-udhar leak ho jaaye, to gas jama nahi hogi, aur kamre mein phailegi, jahan hawa ke saath mil kar ye **dhamake wala mel** bana sakti hai. Isliye teacher pehle apparatus ko leak ke liye jaanchta hai, aur sab kuch hawadaar jagah par, aag se door, safety screen ke peeche karta hai.

## Part C: Hydrogen kaise jama karte hain

Hydrogen ko do tareeke se jama kiya ja sakta hai. Dono ke peeche ek-ek gun hai: **ek, paani mein bahut kam ghulna; doosra, hawa se bahut halka hona.**

### Tareeka 1: Paani ke upar (collection over water)

Ek paani se bhari trough (chauda bartan) mein ek **paani se pooree bhari jar** ulti ki jaati hai, munh neeche, paani ke andar. Delivery tube ka sira jar ke munh ke neeche pahunchta hai. Gas ke bulbule jar ke andar jaate hain, paani ko **neeche dhakelte** hain, aur jar mein hydrogen bhar jaati hai.

```
          ulti jar (pehle poori paani se bhari)
              ┌───────────┐
              │           │
              │  H₂ gas   │   <- gas upar jama hoti hai
              │           │
              │- - - - - -│   <- jar ke andar paani ka star neeche utar gaya
              │~~~~~~~~~~~│
   ~~~~~~~~~~~│~~~~~~~~~~~│~~~~~~~~~~~   <- trough ke paani ka star
   ~~~~~~~~~~~│~~~ o ~~~~~│~~~~~~~~~~~   <- jar ka munh paani ke andar
   ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ o ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
        delivery tube ke bulbule jar ke
        munh ke neeche se upar uthte hain
   ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Hydrogen paani ke upar jama karna (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo). Delivery tube se aati gas ulti jar mein upar uthti hai aur andar ka paani neeche dhakel kar bahar nikaal deti hai. Jar ka paani ka star neeche jitna gaya, utni gas jama hui. Chitra sirf nidarshan hai.

**Ye kyun chalta hai?** Hydrogen paani mein **bahut kam ghulti hai** (sparingly soluble). Isliye jama hone par wo paani mein ghul kar kho nahi jaati. Is tareeke ka ek aur fayda: jar mein paani ka star dikhta hai, to pata chalta hai ki jar poori bhar gayi ya nahi, aur bhari hui jar ke bahar paani ka **bandh (seal)** gas ko rokta hai. Nuksaan ye ki is tareeke se mili gas mein **thodi nami (paani ki bhaap)** hoti hai.

### Tareeka 2: Hawa ko neeche dhakel kar (downward displacement of air)

Hydrogen hawa se **bahut halki** hai (Chapter 10: hydrogen ki density lagbhag 0.09 g per L, hawa ki 1.29 g per L, [gas-laws-part5.md](../gas-laws/gas-laws-part5.md)). To jab ek khaali jar ko **ulta (munh neeche)** pakda jaaye aur delivery tube ka sira jar ke andar, upar tak le jaaya jaaye, to hydrogen **jar ke upar** jama hoti hai aur **bhaari hawa ko neeche se, munh ki taraf se bahar dhakelti** hai.

```
            ┌───────────┐   <- jar ulti rakhi hai (band hissa upar)
            │ H₂  ╷  H₂ │   <- halki hydrogen upar jama hoti hai
            │  H₂ │ H₂  │
            │     │     │
            │  ↓  │  ↓  │   <- bhaari hawa neeche dhakeli jaati hai
            │ hawa│ hawa│
            │     │     │
            ↓     │     ↓   <- munh neeche, khula: hawa yahin se bahar
                  │
     ─────────────┘
     delivery tube (flask se aati hai, jar ke andar upar tak jaati hai)
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Hydrogen hawa ko neeche dhakel kar jama karna (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo). Jar ulti hai, munh neeche khula. Hydrogen halki hai, to upar jama hoti hai aur bhaari hawa ko neeche se bahar dhakel deti hai. Jar ko ulta hi rakhna hota hai, warna gas upar se nikal jaayegi. Chitra sirf nidarshan hai.

**Dhyan dena:** ye tareeka kisi **aisi gas ke liye** hai jo **hawa se halki** ho. Bhaari gas (jaise CO₂, Chapter 10) ke liye jar seedhi rakhi jaati hai, ulti nahi. Hydrogen ke liye ulti jar isliye ki wo upar uthti hai. Is tareeke ka fayda: gas **sookhi** jama hoti hai (paani se sampark nahi). Nuksaan: gas bahut halki hai, to jar ko **jaldi dhak dena** padta hai, warna gas upar se nikal jaati hai, aur hawa ke saath thodi milawat ka khatra rehta hai, jo khatarnak dhamake wala mel ban sakti hai. Isi liye **test sirf teacher** karta hai.

### Do tareeke ek nazar mein

| Tareeka | Kyun chalta hai | Gas ki halat | Dhyan |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paani ke upar | Hydrogen paani mein bahut kam ghulti hai | Thodi nami wali | Paani ka star dekh kar jar bharna pata chalta hai |
| Hawa ko neeche dhakel kar | Hydrogen hawa se bahut halki hai | Sookhi | Jar ulti rakhni hoti hai; jaldi dhakna padta hai |

## Part D: Gas ko sukhana (drying)

Jo gas paani ke upar jama hui, usme thodi **nami (paani ki bhaap)** rehti hai. Agar gas bilkul **sookhi** chahiye, to use **drying agent** se guzaara jaata hai. Chapter 9 mein humne drying agent padhe the ([water-part4.md](../water/water-part4.md)): wo cheezein jo hawa ya gas ki nami kheench leti hain.

Hydrogen ke liye do aam drying agent hain:

- **Concentrated sulphuric acid**, ek chhoti si bottle (wash bottle) mein bharkar, jisme se gas bulbule ki tarah guzarti hai. Acid nami kheench leta hai. **Ye acid bahut khatarnak hai** (jalata hai), isliye ise **sirf teacher** sambhalta hai.
- **Anhydrous calcium chloride (CaCl₂)**, thos dano ke roop mein ek U-shape tube mein bhara hua; gas dano ke beech se guzarti hai aur nami dano mein reh jaati hai.

**Sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo):** gas ko sukhane ka kaam bhi sirf teacher karta hai, chhoti matra mein, aag se door aur safety screen ke peeche.

Dhyan do: **sukhai gayi hydrogen ko paani ke upar jama nahi karte** (nami wapas aa jaayegi). Sookhi hydrogen ke liye **hawa ko neeche dhakelne wala tareeka** hi theek rehta hai.

## Part E: Pehchaan: "pop" wali test

Mr. Mirajkar ke teacher ne jo "pop" suna tha, uski kahani ab samjho.

> **Sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo).** Teacher gas ki **bahut chhoti** matra ek chhoti test tube mein jama karta hai, tube ko ulta pakadta hai (hydrogen halki hai, to upar rehti hai). Phir wo tube ko **flask aur apparatus se kaafi door** le jaakar uske munh ke paas ek **jalti hui patli teeli (splinter)** laata hai. **Student door baithe rehte hain.** Agar gas hydrogen hai, to ek **chhoti, tez "pop" awaaz** aati hai.

**Pop kyun?** Tube mein hydrogen thodi hawa ke saath milti hai. Jalti teeli ki garmi se hydrogen **hawa ke oxygen ke saath ek hi pal mein** jalti hai, aur is jalne se **paani ki bhaap** banti hai: **2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O** (Chapter 7 ka burning). Garm bhaap achanak phailti hai, aur hawa ki ek lahar si banti hai, jo "pop" ki awaaz hai. Chhoti matra ki wajah se ye sirf ek **chhoti awaaz** hai, par **badi matra mein ye bada, khatarnak dhamaka ban sakti hai.** Isliye hi ye kaam sirf teacher karta hai, thodi si gas se, aur dur se.

Tube ke andar baad mein **paani ki bahut halki bund** bhi dikh sakti hai: jalne ka ek aur saboot.

**Kya "pop" akela kaafi saboot hai?** Pop test hydrogen ki pehchaan ki **mukhya test** hai: koi aur aam gas aisi "pop" ke saath nahi jalti. Isliye ye sabse mashhoor test hai.

## Part F: Reaction ka equation, aur atom ki jaanch

Ab hum is prayog ki equation likhte hain (Chapter 3 ka tareeka: har taraf har atom ginte hain).

### Zinc + dilute sulphuric acid

> **Zn + H₂SO₄ → ZnSO₄ + H₂**

- Zn: 1 aur 1
- H: 2 aur 2
- S: 1 aur 1
- O: 4 aur 4

Balance hai ✅. **ZnSO₄ (zinc sulphate)** ek salt hai jo ghol mein rehta hai; **H₂ gas** ban kar bulbule deti hai. Dhyan do: ek zinc ne ek **H₂SO₄** ke 2 hydrogen ko hata diya (Chapter 7 ka displacement).

### Zinc + dilute hydrochloric acid

> **Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂**

- Zn: 1 aur 1
- H: 2 aur 2
- Cl: 2 aur 2

Balance hai ✅. Yahan **2HCl** isliye likha ki ek ZnCl₂ mein 2 Cl chahiye aur ek H₂ mein 2 H chahiye.

### Magnesium + dilute hydrochloric acid

> **Mg + 2HCl → MgCl₂ + H₂**

- Mg: 1 aur 1; H: 2 aur 2; Cl: 2 aur 2. Balance hai ✅.

### Iron + dilute sulphuric acid

> **Fe + H₂SO₄ → FeSO₄ + H₂**

- Fe: 1 aur 1; H: 2 aur 2; S: 1 aur 1; O: 4 aur 4. Balance hai ✅.

Ye sab ek hi pattern dikhate hain:

> **Dhatu (hydrogen se upar) + dilute acid → salt + hydrogen gas**

### Mass ki jaanch (Chapter 4 ka niyam: mass bachta hai)

Atomic mass: H 1, O 16, Mg 24, S 32, Cl 35.5, Zn 65.

**Zn + H₂SO₄ → ZnSO₄ + H₂**

- H₂SO₄ = 2 × 1 + 32 + 4 × 16 = **98**
- ZnSO₄ = 65 + 32 + 64 = **161**
- Reactants: 65 + 98 = **163**. Products: 161 + 2 = **163** ✅

**Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂**

- 2HCl = 2 × (1 + 35.5) = **73**; ZnCl₂ = 65 + 2 × 35.5 = **136**
- Reactants: 65 + 73 = **138**. Products: 136 + 2 = **138** ✅

Dono mein ek baat samaan: **65 g zinc** se **2 g hydrogen** hi nikalti hai (1 mole Zn se 1 mole H₂, aur 1 mole H₂ ka vazan 2 g). Zinc ka vazan to bahut hai, par hydrogen itni halki hai ki uska vazan bahut kam aata hai.

## Part G: Solved examples

### Example 1. Kaun si dhatu hydrogen degi?

Dilute hydrochloric acid ke saath in dhatuon mein kaun si hydrogen degi: **Mg, Cu, Fe, Ag, Zn**?

Reactivity series: K, Na, Ca, Mg, Al, Zn, Fe, Pb, **H**, Cu, Hg, Ag, Au.

- Mg: H se upar, to **degi**
- Zn: H se upar, to **degi**
- Fe: H se upar, to **degi** (dheere)
- Cu: H se neeche, to **nahi degi**
- Ag: H se neeche, to **nahi degi**

**Jaanch:** upar se teen (Mg, Zn, Fe) hydrogen ki line ke upar hain, aur Cu, Ag line ke neeche ✅

### Example 2. Ek dhatu ka vyavhaar

Ek anjaan dhatu **X** dilute sulphuric acid mein **tez bulbule** deti hai, aur ek aur dhatu **Y** koi bulbule nahi deti. Dono mein se kaun reactivity series mein hydrogen ke upar hai?

- **X** hydrogen ke **upar** hai (usne acid se hydrogen nikaali).
- **Y** hydrogen ke **neeche** hai (acid se kuch nahi nikla).

Aur agar dono aur ek line mein rakhne hon: X zyada reactive, Y kam reactive.

**Jaanch:** bulbule = hydrogen nikli = H se upar. Koi bulbule nahi = H se neeche ✅

### Example 3. Funnel ka role

Ek student (kagaz par) kehta hai: "Thistle funnel ki nok acid ke upar rakhne se kaam aasan hoga." Galti kya hai?

- Funnel ki nok acid ke **upar** ho, to bani hui hydrogen funnel ke raaste hi bahar niklegi.
- Gas delivery tube tak kam pahunchegi, aur kamre mein jama ho sakti hai: **khatarnak**.
- Isliye nok **acid ke andar** dooba honi chahiye.

**Jaanch:** funnel mein acid ki parat gas ko rokti hai, delivery tube ka raasta hi khula rehta hai ✅

### Example 4. Jama karne ka tareeka chunna

Teacher ko **sookhi** hydrogen ki ek jar chahiye. Kaun sa tareeka theek rahega: paani ke upar ya hawa ko neeche dhakel kar? Kyun?

- Paani ke upar jama karne se gas mein **nami** aa jaati hai.
- Sookhi gas ke liye pehle gas ko **drying agent** (jaise conc. H₂SO₄ ya CaCl₂) se guzaarte hain, phir **hawa ko neeche dhakel kar** jama karte hain (hydrogen halki hai, ulti jar).

**Jaanch:** paani se dur rehne par nami nahi judti ✅

### Example 5. Mass ka hisaab

Zinc aur dilute hydrochloric acid ki is equation **Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂** mein, agar **130 g** zinc poori tarah reaction kare, to kitni hydrogen (g mein) banegi?

- 65 g Zn se 2 g H₂ (equation 1 : 1 ke mole ratio mein).
- 130 g Zn = 130 ÷ 65 = **2 mol Zn**.
- 2 mol Zn se 2 mol H₂, yaani 2 × 2 = **4 g H₂**.

**Jaanch:** 130 ÷ 65 = 2 mol ✅; 2 mol H₂ × 2 g = 4 g ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Koi bhi dhatu aur koi bhi acid se hydrogen milegi."**

Nahi. Dhatu hydrogen se **upar** honi chahiye (Cu, Ag, Au nahi degi), aur nitric acid jaise acid dhatuon ke saath alag vyavhaar karte hain, aam taur par hydrogen nahi dete.

**Galti 2: "Sodium ya potassium zyada reactive hain, to sabse achhe rahenge."**

Zyada reactive ka matlab zyada khatarnak. Sodium aur potassium bahut tezi se aur khatarnak tareeke se reaction karte hain; lab mein ye is kaam ke liye nahi liye jaate. Zinc **kaabu** mein chalta hai.

**Galti 3: "Hydrogen ko hawa ki jar mein seedhi khadi jar mein jama karenge."**

Hydrogen **halki** hai. Seedhi jar mein wo upar se nikal jaayegi. **Ulti jar** (hawa ko neeche dhakelna) ya paani ke upar jama karna.

**Galti 4: "Sukhai hui hydrogen ko paani ke upar jama karein."**

Paani ke upar jama karne se nami wapas aa jaati hai. Sookhi gas hawa ko neeche dhakel kar jama karte hain.

**Galti 5: "Pop test ghar par kar sakte hain."**

Bilkul nahi. Hydrogen jwalansheel hai aur hawa ke saath dhamaka kar sakti hai. **Pop test, aur poora hydrogen ka prayog, sirf teacher ka kaam hai.**

## Quick Recap

- Lab mein hydrogen **dhatu + dilute acid** se banti hai: **Zn + H₂SO₄ → ZnSO₄ + H₂** aur **Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂**; saath mein **Mg + 2HCl → MgCl₂ + H₂** aur **Fe + H₂SO₄ → FeSO₄ + H₂**.
- **Hydrogen se upar** wali dhatuein (Mg, Zn, Fe...) dilute acid se hydrogen deti hain; **neeche** wali (Cu, Hg, Ag, Au) nahi deti.
- Zinc lab ke liye sabse achha: **na bahut tez, na bahut dheere**. Na aur K bahut khatarnak; **nitric acid alag tarah se vyavhaar karta hai**, hydrogen nahi deta (aage ki class mein).
- Apparatus: **flask, cork, thistle funnel (nok acid ke andar), delivery tube**.
- Jama karna: **paani ke upar** (hydrogen paani mein kam ghulti hai) ya **hawa ko neeche dhakel kar** (hydrogen hawa se halki hai, ulti jar).
- Sukhana: **concentrated H₂SO₄** ya **anhydrous CaCl₂** (drying agent, Chapter 9); sookhi gas ko paani ke upar jama nahi karte.
- Pehchaan: **jalti teeli se chhoti "pop"**.
- Mass bachta hai: 65 + 98 = 161 + 2; 65 g Zn se 2 g H₂.
- **Safety:** hydrogen jwalansheel hai; **sab kuch sirf teacher demo**: chhoti matra, aag door, hawadaar jagah, safety screen. Student kabhi nahi.

## Practice Questions

1. Using the reactivity series, say which of these metals will give hydrogen with dilute sulphuric acid: magnesium, silver, iron, gold, zinc. Give the equation for magnesium with dilute sulphuric acid.

2. Show that the equation **Mg + H₂SO₄ → MgSO₄ + H₂** is balanced by counting atoms, and check the mass balance using Mg 24, H 1, S 32, O 16.

3. A metal Z gives no bubbles with dilute hydrochloric acid, but a student wants hydrogen from it. What do you tell the student, and why?

4. A teacher collects hydrogen in a gas jar that is held upside down. Why is the jar held upside down, and what would happen if it were held upright?

5. A teacher wants dry hydrogen. Name one drying agent he can use, and explain why he should not collect the dried gas over water.

6. Why must the lower end of the thistle funnel dip below the surface of the acid?

7. The equation **Fe + 2HCl → FeCl₂ + H₂** is balanced. Check it by counting atoms. How many grams of hydrogen would 28 g of iron (Fe 56) give, assuming it all reacts?

8. Six grams of magnesium react completely with dilute hydrochloric acid (**Mg + 2HCl → MgCl₂ + H₂**, Mg 24, H 1). Find the mass of hydrogen produced and check by the mass of the products.

9. A friend says, "Sodium is more reactive than zinc, so sodium with dilute acid is a better way to make hydrogen in the lab." Is he right? Explain.

10. Which activity is allowed for a student: (a) preparing hydrogen at home with zinc and acid, (b) drawing the hydrogen apparatus on paper and labelling it, (c) testing hydrogen with a burning match? Explain your choice.

### Answers

1. Reactivity series mein hydrogen se **upar**: Mg, Zn, Fe. **Neeche**: Ag, Au. To **magnesium, zinc aur iron** hydrogen denge; silver aur gold **nahi**. Magnesium ka equation: **Mg + H₂SO₄ → MgSO₄ + H₂**. Jaanch: Mg 1-1, H 2-2, S 1-1, O 4-4 ✅

2. Atom ginti: left mein Mg 1, H 2, S 1, O 4; right mein Mg 1, S 1, O 4, H 2 ✅ balance. Mass: H₂SO₄ = 2 + 32 + 64 = 98; reactants 24 + 98 = **122**. MgSO₄ = 24 + 32 + 64 = 120; H₂ = 2; products 120 + 2 = **122** ✅

3. Dhatu Z hydrogen se **neeche** hogi (jaise copper), isliye wo dilute acid se hydrogen **nahi** nikaal sakti. Student ko koi aisi dhatu leni chahiye jo hydrogen se upar ho (jaise zinc), aur wo bhi sirf teacher ki dekhrekh mein. Jaanch: koi bulbule nahi = H se neeche ✅

4. Hydrogen hawa se **halki** hai, to wo upar ki taraf uthti hai. Ulti jar mein wo upar jama hoti hai aur bhaari hawa ko **neeche, munh ki taraf se bahar dhakel** deti hai. Seedhi jar mein hydrogen **upar se nikal jaati** aur jama nahi hoti. Jaanch: density 0.09 < 1.29 ✅

5. Drying agent: **concentrated sulphuric acid** ya **anhydrous calcium chloride**. Sukhai hui gas ko paani ke upar jama karne se paani ki bhaap **wapas** mil jaayegi, to sukhana bekaar ho jaayega. Isliye sookhi gas **hawa ko neeche dhakel kar** jama karte hain. Jaanch: paani se sampark nahi, to nami nahi ✅

6. Agar nok acid ke upar ho, to banti hui hydrogen funnel ke raaste **bahar nikal jaayegi** aur delivery tube tak kam pahunchegi, aur kamre mein phail kar khatarnak ho sakti hai. Nok acid mein dooba ho to acid ki parat gas ko delivery tube ki taraf hi bhejti hai. Jaanch: funnel ka raasta band, delivery tube ka khula ✅

7. Atom ginti: Fe 1-1, H 2-2, Cl 2-2 ✅ balance. 28 g Fe = 28 ÷ 56 = **0.5 mol**. Equation 1 : 1 ke mole ratio mein hai, to 0.5 mol H₂ banegi: 0.5 × 2 = **1 g hydrogen**. Jaanch: 0.5 mol Fe × 56 = 28 g ✅ aur 0.5 mol H₂ × 2 = 1 g ✅

8. Mg ke moles = 6 ÷ 24 = **0.25 mol**. 1 : 1 ratio, to 0.25 mol H₂, yaani 0.25 × 2 = **0.5 g hydrogen**. Jaanch (mass): 0.25 mol MgCl₂ = 0.25 × 95 = 23.75 g; 2HCl ke 0.5 mol = 0.5 × 36.5 = 18.25 g; reactants 6 + 18.25 = **24.25 g**; products 23.75 + 0.5 = **24.25 g** ✅

9. Nahi, wo galat hai. Sodium zyada reactive hai, par isi wajah se bahut **tezi se aur khatarnak** tareeke se reaction karta hai, to lab mein acid ke saath use nahi hota. Lab ke liye **zinc** jaisi madhyam reactivity wali dhatu achhi hai, jiska reaction kaabu mein rehta hai. Jaanch: "zyada reactive" matlab "zyada khatarnak" ✅
