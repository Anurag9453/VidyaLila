# Topic 3: Hydrogen Banane Ke Aur Tareeke

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki lab mein hydrogen **zinc aur dilute acid** se banta hai, aur teacher use thodi si matra mein, bahut savdhaani se, sirf demo ke liye banate hain. Par dunia ko hydrogen **tonnon** mein chahiye: khaad ke liye, refinery ke liye, aur ab bhavishya ke fuel ke liye. Itne bade scale par zinc aur acid se kaam nahi chalega. To sawal ye hai: hydrogen **aur kin tareeqon se** banta hai, kaun sa tareeka lab ka hai, kaun sa factory ka, aur kaun sa **saaf** hai? Aaj hum paani se, natural gas se, coke se, namak ke ghol se, aur sooraj ki roshni se hydrogen banane ki kahani dekhenge.

## Chhat par lagi solar panel

School ki chhat par ek nayi solar panel lagi thi. Mr. Vartak, jo Chemistry padhate the, Rishabh ke saath chhat par gaye. Panel ke neeche ek chhota sa charge controller aur ek rechargeable cell ka dabba laga tha, jisme ek hari light jal rahi thi. "Sir, ye light to sirf dhoop mein jalti hai," Rishabh ne kaha. "Dhoop gayi, to light bhi gayi. Cell mein bijli to jama ho jaati hai, par kal jab Physics wale sir ne bataya ki battery bhi ek din khatam ho jaati hai..."

Mr. Vartak muskuraye. "Sahi pakda. Bijli ko jama karna mushkil hai. Ab ek dilchasp sawal sochte hain: **kya sooraj ki roshni se koi aisi cheez banayi ja sakti hai jise hum dibbe mein bhar kar kal, ya agle mahine, ya doosre sheher mein bhi jala sakein, ek fuel?**" Rishabh ne socha. "Sir, panel se bijli milti hai. Aur bijli se paani ke do tukde ho jaate hain, Chemistry class mein wo electrolysis wala demo... hydrogen aur oxygen!" Mr. Vartak ne taali bajayi. "Bas, tumne abhi **green hydrogen** ka idea khud nikal liya. Sooraj ki bijli, paani, aur nateeja: ek aisi gas jo jalne par sirf paani deti hai."

Par phir Rishabh ne ek aur sawal poochha: "Sir, ye hi to sabse saaf tareeka hua. Fir duniya mein sab log isi se hydrogen kyun nahi banate?" Is topic mein hum **saare tareeke** dekhenge, aur ye bhi ki saaf wala tareeka abhi sabse zyada kyun nahi chalta.

## Part A: Paani se hydrogen

### 1. Electrolysis (Chapter 7 ka recall)

Chapter 7 mein humne dekha tha ki paani ko bijli se todne par **do gas** milti hain:

2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂

**Sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo).** Teacher ek chhoti battery se do electrodes paani mein dubate hain (shuddh paani mein bijli bahut kam chalti hai, isliye usme ek suitable electrolyte mila diya jaata hai). Ek electrode par **hydrogen** ke bubbles, doosre par **oxygen** ke. Hydrogen ka volume oxygen se **do guna** hota hai, kyunki equation mein 2H₂ aur 1 O₂ hai (Chapter 10 ka Avogadro ka niyam: ek hi taapman aur dabav par gas ka volume molecules ki ginti ke saath chalta hai, [gas-laws-part5.md](../gas-laws/gas-laws-part5.md)). Teacher ye demo chhoti matra mein, aag se door, hawadaar kamre mein aur safety screen ke peeche karte hain. **Students ye ghar par kabhi nahi karenge, aur wall socket ki bijli se to bilkul nahi**: hydrogen aur oxygen ka mix ek chingaari se phat sakta hai.

```
       Hydrogen (−)         Oxygen (+)
           tube                tube
           ┌──┐                ┌──┐
           │H₂│                │O₂│
           │H₂│                │O₂│
           │H₂│                │~~│
           │H₂│                │~~│
       ┌───│~~│────────────────│~~│───┐
       │~~~│~~│~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~│~~│~~~│  <- paani + thoda electrolyte
       │~~~│[]│~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~│[]│~~~│  <- electrode ([ ])
       │~~~~│~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~│~~~~~│
       └────┼───────────────────┼─────┘
            │                   │
            └─(−)  battery  (+)─┘

       Hydrogen ka volume : Oxygen ka volume = 2 : 1
       (H₂ wali tube mein gas ka stambh doguna ooncha)
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Paani ka electrolysis (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo): negative (−) electrode ki ulti tube mein hydrogen, positive (+) wali mein oxygen jama hoti hai aur tube ka paani neeche dhakelti hai. Hydrogen ka volume oxygen se do guna hota hai (2 : 1), kyunki 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂.

Ye tareeka **bahut shuddh hydrogen** deta hai (dono gas alag nali mein aati hain), par **bijli bahut kharch hoti hai**, isliye sirf tab sasta hai jab bijli sasti ya saaf ho. Is wajah se ye lab ya chhote scale par aur ab solar-wind ke saath bade scale par dekha jaata hai.

### 2. Active metals aur thanda paani (sirf describe)

Reactivity series (Chapter 7) mein jo metals bahut upar hain, wo **thande paani** se bhi hydrogen nikaal dete hain:

2Na + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + H₂

2K + 2H₂O → 2KOH + H₂

Ca + 2H₂O → Ca(OH)₂ + H₂

Sodium aur potassium **bahut tezi se** react karte hain: dhaatu paani par daudti hai, garmi itni nikalti hai ki hydrogen aksar khud jal uthti hai, aur kabhi-kabhi dhamaka bhi hota hai (Chapter 8 mein alkali metals ki kahani yaad karo). Calcium dheere react karta hai: paani mein chhote bubbles aate hain aur paani doodhiya ho jaata hai (kyunki bana hua Ca(OH)₂ paani mein kam ghulta hai). **Ye reactions hum sirf padh rahe hain. Students inhe kabhi nahi karte**: sodium aur potassium ko kisi bhi jagah, chhoti matra mein bhi, student ke haath mein dena allowed nahi hai. Ye metals mehnge aur khatarnak hote hain, isliye hydrogen banane ka ye factory tareeka nahi hai.

### 3. Metals aur bhaap (steam) (ek line mein)

Kam active metal, jaise magnesium (aur garm iron), **thande paani se nahi, par garm bhaap se** react karte hain:

Mg + H₂O → MgO + H₂

Yaani metal ka oxide banta hai aur hydrogen milti hai. Ye bhi sirf **padhne ke liye** hai: garm metal ke paas hydrogen banna khatarnak hai, isliye ye kaam sirf trained log karte hain.

### Jaanch (Part A)

Kya "copper thande paani se hydrogen deta hai"? ❌ Nahi: copper reactivity series mein hydrogen ke **neeche** hai, wo paani se hydrogen nahi nikaal paata (Chapter 7 aur is chapter ka Topic 2).

## Part B: Factory ke tareeke: natural gas aur coke se

Duniya mein **zyadatar hydrogen** aaj bhi seedhe paani se nahi, balki **fossil fuel** se banta hai. Wajah seedhi hai: ye tareeke **sasta aur bade scale** par chalte hain. Hydrogen khud kai fossil fuel ke andar bhi hai (methane CH₄ mein 4 hydrogen atoms hain), to socho: **kyun na use bhaap ki madad se bahar nikaal lein?**

### Step 1: Steam reforming (natural gas se)

Natural gas mein zyadatar **methane (CH₄)** hoti hai. Isko bahut oonche taapman par bhaap ke saath garm kiya jaata hai, nickel jaise catalyst ki madad se:

CH₄ + H₂O → CO + 3H₂

Ek methane aur ek paani ke molecule se **3 hydrogen molecules** aur **1 carbon monoxide** milte hain. Is mix (CO + H₂) ko **synthesis gas** bhi kehte hain.

### Step 2: Coke aur bhaap (water gas)

Agar natural gas na ho, to **coke** (coal se bana kaala, lagbhag carbon) ko bahut garm karke uske upar bhaap daali jaati hai:

C + H₂O → CO + H₂

Isse **water gas** milti hai: CO aur H₂ ka mix. Ye tareeka coal waale deshon mein purana aur pehchana hai (Bharat jaise coal-rich desh mein bhi iski jaankari zaroori hai).

### Step 3: Bosch step (CO ko bhi hydrogen mein badalna)

Dono tareeqon mein carbon monoxide (CO) ek baaki bachi hui gas hai. Use fenkne ki jagah, **bhaap ke saath** aur react kiya jaata hai, **iron oxide catalyst** ke saath:

CO + H₂O → CO₂ + H₂

Isse **aur hydrogen** milti hai, aur CO badal kar **carbon dioxide (CO₂)** ban jaati hai.

### Step 4: CO₂ ko hataana

Ab hydrogen ke saath CO₂ mili hui hai. Ise **dabav ke neeche paani mein ghula** kar alag kiya jaata hai (CO₂ paani mein achhi tarah ghulti hai, hydrogen lagbhag nahi, Topic 2 mein dekha tha). Bachi hui gas **lagbhag shuddh hydrogen** hoti hai.

```
   NATURAL GAS (methane)          COKE (kaala carbon)
         │                               │
         v                               v
   +------------------+          +------------------+
   │ 1. Steam         │          │ 2. Water gas     │
   │    reforming     │          │    C + H₂O       │
   │  CH₄ + H₂O       │          │    -> CO + H₂    │
   │  -> CO + 3H₂     │          │                  │
   +------------------+          +------------------+
              \                       /
               v                     v
            +---------------------------+
            │  CO + H₂ ka mix           │
            │  (synthesis gas)          │
            +---------------------------+
                         │
                         v
            +---------------------------+
            │ 3. Bosch step             │
            │  CO + H₂O -> CO₂ + H₂     │
            │  (iron oxide catalyst)    │
            +---------------------------+
                         │
                         v
            +---------------------------+
            │ 4. CO₂ hataana            │
            │  (dabav mein paani mein   │
            │   ghulana)                │
            +---------------------------+
                         │
                         v
                 SHUDDH HYDROGEN
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Factory ka raasta: methane ya coke se bhaap ke saath CO aur H₂ ka mix, phir Bosch step se aur hydrogen, aur aakhir mein CO₂ ko dabav mein paani mein ghula kar hataana.

**Dhyan do:** ye tareeke **saaf nahi hain**, kyunki banne wali CO₂ agar hawa mein chhod di jaaye, to wo atmosphere mein jaati hai (iski kahani agle chapter "Atmospheric Pollution" mein). Isi wajah se hydrogen ke **colour names** bane hain (Part D mein).

### Natural gas ka ek poora hisaab

Dono step milakar likho:

- Step 1: CH₄ + H₂O → CO + 3H₂
- Step 3: CO + H₂O → CO₂ + H₂

Jodne par (CO dono taraf se kat jaati hai): **CH₄ + 2H₂O → CO₂ + 4H₂**

Yaani **1 methane** se aakhir mein **4 hydrogen molecules** (8 H atoms) milte hain: 4 H atoms methane ke aur 4 H atoms 2 paani ke molecules ke. Yaani hydrogen ka **aadha** hissa methane se, **aadha** paani se aata hai.

## Part C: Brine ka by-product, aur green hydrogen

### Brine ke electrolysis mein hydrogen (by-product)

Factory mein **namak (NaCl) ke gaadhe ghol (brine)** mein bijli chalayi jaati hai, mukhya taur par **chlorine** aur **sodium hydroxide** (saabun aur kaagaz ke kaam ka) banane ke liye. Isi mein **hydrogen apne aap** bhi milti hai:

2NaCl + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + Cl₂ + H₂

Yahan hydrogen **asli lakshya nahi** hai, ye **by-product** hai: factory ne chlorine aur NaOH ke liye ye process chalayi, aur hydrogen saath mein mil gayi, jise bechne ya jalane ke liye use kar liya jaata hai. (Is process ki zyada kahani **aage ki class mein**.)

### Green hydrogen: sooraj aur hawa se

Ab wapas Rishabh ke sawal par. Paani ka electrolysis **saaf** tab hota hai jab uske liye bijli bhi **saaf** ho. Agar bijli **solar panel** ya **wind turbine** se aaye, to:

2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂   (bijli solar ya wind se)

Is tarah bana hydrogen **green hydrogen** kehlata hai: ise banate waqt koi CO₂ nahi nikalti (saath mein sirf oxygen milti hai), aur jab isse jalaya jaata hai, to wapas **sirf paani** banta hai.

```
     SOORAJ
       \│/
     --- O ---
       /│\
        │
        v
   +-----------+     bijli      +------------------+
   │  SOLAR    │ -------------> │  ELECTROLYSER    │
   │  PANEL    │                │  (paani ko tode) │
   +-----------+                +------------------+
                                  │             │
                         paani ---+             │
                         andar                  │
                                                v
                            +----------+   +----------+
                            │ HYDROGEN │   │ OXYGEN   │
                            │ (H₂)     │   │ (O₂)     │
                            +----------+   +----------+
                                 │
                                 v
                    STORE KARO: tank mein ya pipe se,
                    aur baad mein use karo: fuel ke roop mein
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Green hydrogen: solar panel ki bijli electrolyser mein paani ko H₂ aur O₂ mein todti hai. Hydrogen ko store karke baad mein fuel ke roop mein use kiya ja sakta hai.

**Bharat ki baat:** Bharat ne 2023 mein **National Green Hydrogen Mission** shuru kiya. Iska maksad hai ki desh mein **green hydrogen** banaya jaaye (lagbhag 2030 tak **bahut badi matra** ka lakshya rakha gaya hai: lagbhag 5 million tonne har saal, ye ek mota, approximate lakshya hai), taaki refinery, khaad aur steel jaise udyogon mein fossil fuel ki jagah saaf hydrogen use ho. Hamare yahan dhoop aur hawa dono bahut hain, isliye ye idea desh ke liye khaas hai.

**Par green hydrogen abhi sasta kyun nahi?** Kyunki electrolysis mein **bahut bijli** lagti hai. Neeche ke example mein hisaab dekho.

## Part D: Hydrogen ke "colour names" (ek chhota note)

Duniya mein log hydrogen ko uske **banane ke tareeke** ke hisaab se rang ke naam dete hain. Ye sirf **label** hain, hydrogen ka asli rang to koi nahi hota (hydrogen **colourless** gas hai):

- **Grey hydrogen:** natural gas se (steam reforming), aur CO₂ hawa mein chhod di jaati hai.
- **Blue hydrogen:** grey jaisa hi, par CO₂ ko **pakad kar zameen ke neeche store** kar diya jaata hai, taaki hawa mein kam jaaye.
- **Green hydrogen:** solar ya wind ki bijli se paani ke electrolysis se.

(Coal se bane hydrogen ko bhi log aksar alag rang (jaise kaala ya bhura) bol dete hain; naam alag-alag jagah thoda alag ho sakte hain, isliye sirf yaad rakho: **rang = kaise bana**.)

## Part E: Tareeqon ki tulna

| Tareeka | Raw material | Mukhya product | Saaf ya nahi |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lab: metal + dilute acid | Zinc ya magnesium, dilute acid | Hydrogen (aur ek salt) | Bahut chhoti matra, sirf teacher demo |
| Paani ka electrolysis (bijli grid se) | Paani, bijli | Hydrogen aur oxygen | Bijli kaise bani, us par nirbhar |
| Green hydrogen | Paani, solar ya wind ki bijli | Hydrogen aur oxygen | Saaf: koi CO₂ nahi |
| Steam reforming (natural gas) | Methane, bhaap | Hydrogen aur CO₂ | Saaf nahi: CO₂ banti hai (grey), blue agar CO₂ pakdi jaaye |
| Water gas (coke) | Coke, bhaap | Hydrogen, CO, phir CO₂ | Saaf nahi: bahut CO₂ banti hai |
| Brine ka electrolysis | Namak ka ghol, bijli | Chlorine, NaOH, aur hydrogen by-product | Hydrogen by-product hai, asli lakshya nahi |
| Sodium ya potassium + paani | Active metal, paani | Hydrogen aur ek hydroxide | Sirf describe: khatarnak, factory ke liye nahi |

**Sabse bada sabak:** koi bhi tareeka **sab mein best** nahi hai. Sasta tareeka (fossil fuel) saaf nahi, saaf tareeka (green) abhi mehnga hai. Isliye duniya dono ke beech sambhal rahi hai.

## Part F: Solved examples

**Example 1 (electrolysis ke volume):** Ek teacher ke demo mein paani ke electrolysis se **24 mL** hydrogen mili. Oxygen kitni mili hogi, aur kul gas kitni?

- Hydrogen : oxygen = 2 : 1
- Oxygen = 24 ÷ 2 = **12 mL**
- Kul gas = 24 + 12 = **36 mL**

**Jaanch:** 24 ÷ 12 = 2 ✅ (2 : 1). Aur equation 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂ mein bhi 2 hydrogen par 1 oxygen hai: H = 4 = 4, O = 2 = 2 ✅.

**Example 2 (natural gas ka hisaab, ginti se):** Ek reactor mein **100 methane molecules** steam reforming se (CH₄ + H₂O → CO + 3H₂) react kiye. Kitne hydrogen molecules bane? Phir Bosch step (CO + H₂O → CO₂ + H₂) mein aur kitne? Kul kitne?

- Step 1: 100 × 3 = **300** H₂ (aur 100 CO)
- Step 3: 100 CO → 100 × 1 = **100** H₂ (aur 100 CO₂)
- Kul = 300 + 100 = **400** H₂

**Jaanch:** poora equation CH₄ + 2H₂O → CO₂ + 4H₂ se 100 methane par 100 × 4 = 400 H₂ ✅. Aur 100 CO₂ bhi banti hai: ye wohi CO₂ hai jise Step 4 mein hataana padta hai.

**Example 3 (mass ka hisaab: water gas):** Ek hi reaction C + H₂O → CO + H₂ ke liye relative masses lo (C = 12, H = 1, O = 16): kya dono taraf mass barabar hai?

- Left: C = 12, H₂O = 2 × 1 + 16 = 18, kul = 12 + 18 = **30**
- Right: CO = 12 + 16 = 28, H₂ = 2, kul = 28 + 2 = **30**

**Jaanch:** 30 = 30 ✅ (mass conservation, Chapter 4). Yaani 12 g coke aur 18 g paani se 28 g CO aur 2 g hydrogen milti hai.

**Example 4 (paani se kitna hydrogen?):** Electrolysis mein **9 kg paani** se kitna hydrogen milega? (2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂, H = 1, O = 16.)

- 2H₂O ki mass = 2 × 18 = 36 u; 2H₂ ki mass = 2 × 2 = 4 u
- Mass ratio paani : hydrogen = 36 : 4 = 9 : 1
- 9 kg paani se **1 kg hydrogen** (aur baaki 8 kg oxygen)

**Jaanch:** 1 + 8 = 9 ✅. Aur 9 kg = 9000 g = 500 × 18 g, to 500 mol paani se 500 mol H₂ = 500 × 2 g = 1000 g = 1 kg ✅.

**Example 5 (green hydrogen: bijli ka hisaab):** Ek solar panel plant **4 kW** par **5 ghante** chalta hai. Maan lo ki 1 kg hydrogen banane mein lagbhag **50 kWh** bijli lagti hai (lagbhag aur rounded value, sirf practice ke liye). Kitna hydrogen banega?

- Bijli = 4 kW × 5 h = **20 kWh**
- Hydrogen = 20 ÷ 50 = **0.4 kg = 400 g**

**Jaanch:** 0.4 kg × 50 kWh/kg = 20 kWh ✅.

**Example 6 (energy ki tulna):** 1 kg hydrogen jalne par lagbhag **120 MJ** energy deti hai (lagbhag). 1 kWh = 3.6 MJ. Ye kitne kWh hua? Agar banane mein 50 kWh lage, to kitna percent wapas mila?

- 120 ÷ 3.6 = **33.3 kWh** (lagbhag)
- Wapas mila = 33.3 ÷ 50 × 100 ≈ **67%** (lagbhag)

**Jaanch:** 33.3 × 3.6 = 119.88 ≈ 120 ✅. Matlab itni bijli lagakar utni energy nahi milti: kuch hissa electrolysis mein garmi ban kar kho jaata hai. Isliye green hydrogen abhi mehnga hai.

## Part G: Reasoning: "Kaun sa tareeka kab?"

| Situation | Sabse sahi tareeka | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| Ek school lab mein thoda hydrogen dikhana (teacher demo) | Zinc + dilute acid | Chhoti matra, aasaan apparatus |
| Ek bada plant jise saste mein bahut hydrogen chahiye aur natural gas paas hai | Steam reforming | Sasta aur bade scale par chalta hai |
| Ek desh jo carbon-free hydrogen chahta hai aur jahan dhoop bahut hai | Solar bijli se electrolysis (green) | Koi CO₂ nahi banti |
| Ek chlorine-NaOH factory | Brine electrolysis | Hydrogen by-product ke roop mein mil jaati hai |

**Jaanch (galat soch):** "Sodium ko paani mein daalna hydrogen banane ka achha factory tareeka hai." ❌ Nahi: bahut tez, khatarnak, mehnga. Factory mein ye use nahi hota.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Hydrogen ka rang hota hai (green, grey, blue)"**

Galat. Hydrogen **colourless** gas hai. Rang sirf ye batate hain ki wo **kaise bana**.

**Galti 2: "Green hydrogen mein bijli kahin se bhi chalegi"**

Galat. Green tab hai jab bijli **solar ya wind** jaisi saaf source se aaye. Agar bijli koyle se bani ho, to hydrogen green nahi.

**Galti 3: "Brine electrolysis ka asli maksad hydrogen banana hai"**

Galat. Mukhya maksad chlorine aur NaOH hai. Hydrogen **by-product** hai.

**Galti 4: "Students sodium ya potassium se hydrogen bana kar dekh sakte hain"**

Bilkul nahi. Ye reactions sirf describe hain. Student ke liye ye bahut khatarnak hain.

**Galti 5: "Steam reforming saaf tareeka hai kyunki isme paani use hota hai"**

Galat. Isme **CO₂** banti hai (aur methane khud ek fossil fuel hai), isliye ye saaf nahi.

**Galti 6: "Paani jaisa saaf kuch hai to hydrogen khud-ba-khud saaf hogi"**

Galat. Saaf hone ke liye poora raasta dekho: raw material, bijli, aur banne wali CO₂.

## Quick Recap

- **Paani se:** electrolysis 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂ (volume 2 : 1, teacher demo). Active metals + thanda paani (Na, K, Ca) aur Mg + bhaap: **sirf describe**.
- **Steam reforming:** CH₄ + H₂O → CO + 3H₂. **Water gas:** C + H₂O → CO + H₂. **Bosch step:** CO + H₂O → CO₂ + H₂ (iron oxide catalyst). CO₂ ko dabav ke neeche paani mein ghula kar hataate hain. Poora: CH₄ + 2H₂O → CO₂ + 4H₂.
- **Brine electrolysis:** 2NaCl + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + Cl₂ + H₂: hydrogen by-product.
- **Green hydrogen:** solar ya wind ki bijli se electrolysis. Bharat ka National Green Hydrogen Mission 2023 mein shuru hua.
- **Colour names:** grey (natural gas, CO₂ chhodi), blue (CO₂ pakdi gayi), green (saaf bijli). Hydrogen khud colourless hai.
- Saaf tareeka mehnga, sasta tareeka saaf nahi.
- Students hydrogen **kabhi nahi** banate, na sodium ya potassium paani mein daalte hain; sab kuch teacher demo ya sirf padhne ke liye.
