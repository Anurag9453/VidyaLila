# Topic 4: Hydrogen Ke Gun: Bhautik aur Rasayanik

Pichhle topics mein humne dekha ki hydrogen kya hai aur use **kaise banate hain**: lab mein metal aur acid se, industry mein natural gas aur steam se, aur saaf tareeke se paani se. Ab ek seedha sawaal: jab hydrogen mil gayi, to **wo kaisi hai aur kya karti hai?** Kya wo dikhti hai? Kya uska koi swaad ya gandh hai? Wo gubbare ko upar kyun uthati hai, aur aag ke paas jaate hi kyun fatt se bol padti hai? Aaj hum hydrogen ke **bhautik gun** (dekh kar, naap kar pata chalne wale) aur **rasayanik gun** (doosre padarthon ke saath reaction) dono dekhenge.

## Rocket ki aag jo "garam paani" nikli

Mr. Waghmare ke ghar TV par ISRO ke ek rocket ka live launch chal raha tha. Unki bhanji Aashna sofe par aalthi-paalthi maar kar baithi thi. Commentator bola, "Ab rocket ka **cryogenic stage** chalu ho gaya hai." Screen par rocket ke peeche se tez, lagbhag peele-safed dhuen ka gubbar dikha, par aag ka rang halka sa tha, itna halka ki bas hawa kaanpti si dikhti thi.

Aashna: "Mama, ye kaisi aag hai? Aag to laal-peeli hoti hai. Yahan to bas halka sa dhuaan hai."

Mr. Waghmare: "Ye dhuaan nahi hai, ye **bhaap (water vapour)** hai. Is stage mein **liquid hydrogen** jalti hai **liquid oxygen** ke saath. Hydrogen ke jalne se kya banta hai, batao."

Aashna ne socha. "Paani?" 

"Bilkul. 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O. Rocket ke engine se garam paani ki bhaap nikalti hai. Ye gas itni halki hai ki rocket ke ek kilo fuel se bahut zyada dhakka milta hai, par isse thanda rakhna padta hai: liquid hydrogen ka taapman **−253 °C** hota hai."

"−253!? Itni thandi cheez ko jalaya jaata hai?"

"Haan. Aur yahi hydrogen ki do alag-alag shakliyat hain: bahut thandi aur bahut jalne wali. Chalo, iske **gun** ek ek karke dekhte hain."

> **Safety:** Hydrogen aag pakadne wali aur kuch mixtures mein **dhamake wali** gas hai. Is chapter mein koi student hydrogen nahi banata, nahi bharta, nahi jalata. Jahan jalane ya test ka zikr hai, wo **sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo)** hai: chhoti matra, aag door, hawadaar jagah aur safety screen ke saath. Gubbaron mein hydrogen kabhi nahi bharte; helium dukaan se bharwayi jaati hai.

## Part A: Bhautik gun: dekho, naapo, tulna karo

| Gun | Hydrogen (H₂) |
|---|---|
| Rang, gandh, swaad | Rangheen, gandhheen, swaadheen (swaad koi kabhi chakhta nahi, ye sirf jaankari hai) |
| Density (STP par) | lagbhag 0.09 g per L (hawa 1.29 g per L) |
| Boiling point | lagbhag −253 °C, yaani 20 K |
| Melting point | lagbhag −259 °C, yaani 14 K |
| Paani mein ghulna | bahut kam (isi liye lab mein paani ke upar ikattha kar sakte hain, Topic 2) |
| Litmus par asar | koi nahi: **neutral** |
| Aag | Khud jalti hai, par doosre ko jalne mein madad nahi karti (wo to khud fuel hai) |

Taapman ko K mein badalne ka tareeka Chapter 1 se yaad hai: K = °C + 273. Check: −253 + 273 = 20 K aur −259 + 273 = 14 K ✅. Boiling aur melting point mein sirf 6 degree ka fark hai: hydrogen sirf 14 K se 20 K ke chhote se "darwaze" mein liquid rehti hai. Isi liye use liquid rakhna bahut mushkil hai (Topic 5 mein dekhenge).

**Itne kam boiling point ka kaaran** (ek line mein): H₂ ke kan chhote aur halke hain, aur kanon ke beech aakarshan bahut kam hai, to bahut thande hone par hi ye gas se liquid banti hai. (Aage ki class mein kaaran aur gehra ho jaayega.)

## Part B: Sabse halki gas: density ka tulna

Gas Laws chapter ([gas-laws-part5.md](../gas-laws/gas-laws-part5.md)) ka niyam: **gas ki density = M ÷ 22.4 g per L** (STP par). Hydrogen ke liye: 2 ÷ 22.4 = **0.0893**, yaani lagbhag **0.09 g per L**. Helium ke liye 4 ÷ 22.4 = **0.1786**, lagbhag **0.18**. Hawa ke liye lagbhag 29 ÷ 22.4 = **1.29 g per L**. Aaj hum nap-tol ke liye **kg per m³** mein sochenge (1 m³ = 1000 L, to 0.09 g per L = 0.09 kg per m³).

```
        1 m³ gas ka vazan (STP jaisi halat)

                                 +---------------+
                                 │               │
                                 │   AIR (hawa)  │
                                 │   N₂, O₂ ...  │
                  +-------+      │               │
                  │  He   │      │               │
   +-------+      │       │      │               │
   │  H₂   │      │       │      │               │
   +-------+      +-------+      +---------------+
   0.09 kg        0.18 kg        1.29 kg
   (sabse halki)  (2 guna        (H₂ se 14 guna zyada)
                   H₂ se)

   Hawa/H₂ = 1.29 / 0.09 = 14.3     Hawa/He = 1.29 / 0.18 = 7.2
```

> **Figure 4.1** – 1 m³ jagah mein teen gas: hydrogen 0.09 kg, helium 0.18 kg, hawa 1.29 kg. Blocks ki oonchai sirf vazan ka mota nidarshan hai (He ka block H₂ se doguna, hawa ka sabse ooncha; paimane par nahi), asli volume teeno ka barabar (1 m³) hai.

Isiliye hydrogen **sabse halki gas** hai: hawa se lagbhag **14 guna halki** (1.29 ÷ 0.09 = 14.3), helium se aadhi.

### Example 1. Hydrogen ke balloon ka uthaav

Ek bade gubbare ka volume 100 m³ hai (sirf kaagaz ka hisaab, kisi ko ye gubbara bharna nahi hai) aur wo hydrogen se bhara hai. Gubbare ko kitna uthaav milega? (Gubbare ke kapde ka vazan chhod do.)

- Hawa jo 100 m³ ne hataayi: 100 × 1.29 = **129 kg**.
- Andar ki hydrogen ka vazan: 100 × 0.09 = **9 kg**.
- Uthaav = 129 − 9 = **120 kg**.

Ya seedha: har 1 m³ par 1.29 − 0.09 = **1.20 kg**, to 100 m³ par 100 × 1.20 = 120 kg.

Jaanch: 129 − 9 = 120 aur 100 × 1.20 = 120 ✅

**Ye uthaav kaam kaise karta hai?** Gubbare ke hataaye hue hawa ke vazan se andar ki gas ka vazan ghata do, bacha hua dhakka upar jaata hai (jaise paani mein lakdi ka tukda upar tairta hai). Kapde aur dhaage ka vazan is dhakke se kam ho tabhi gubbara upar jaata hai.

### Example 2. Hydrogen versus helium: kitna fark?

Wahi 100 m³ ka gubbara agar helium se bhara ho:

- Helium ka uthaav per m³: 1.29 − 0.18 = **1.11 kg**.
- 100 m³ par: 100 × 1.11 = **111 kg**.
- Hydrogen ke 120 kg se tulna: 111 ÷ 120 = **0.925**, yaani helium hydrogen ka lagbhag **92.5 percent** uthaav deti hai.

Sirf 7.5 percent kam uthaav, par helium **jalti nahi**. Isiliye gubbaron mein helium hi bhari jaati hai, hydrogen nahi.

Jaanch: 1.29 − 0.09 = 1.20 aur 1.29 − 0.18 = 1.11 ✅; fark 120 − 111 = 9 kg ✅

(Dhyan do: ye sab hisaab STP jaisi halat par hai. Taapman aur dabao badalne par density thodi badalti hai, to asli uthaav thoda alag ho sakta hai.)

## Part C: Rasayanik gun 1: hydrogen jalti hai (par oxygen ke saath hi)

Hydrogen jalne wali gas hai, par uske **jalne ke liye oxygen (ya hawa)** chahiye. Wahi reaction jo rocket mein chal raha tha:

**2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O** (garmi nikalti hai: exothermic, Chapter 7 Topic 4)

- H: 4 = 4, O: 2 = 2 ✅
- Chapter 9 ka mass anupaat yaad karo: 4 g H₂ + 32 g O₂ → 36 g paani, yaani H : O = 1 : 8 (mass mein).

**Sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo):** teacher hydrogen ki chhoti si dhaar ko jalate hain to **halki neeli, lagbhag na dikhne wali** lau banti hai (chhoti matra, aag ka koi aur source paas nahi, safety screen ke peeche). Is lau ka rang itna halka hota hai ki din ki roshni mein ye dikhti hi nahi. **Ye bahut khatarnak hai** (kyunki kisi ko pata hi nahi chalta ki aag lagi hai), aur isiliye hydrogen wale kaam ki jagah par khaas detector lagaye jaate hain.

Jaanch (vazan bachta hai): 4 + 32 = 36 ✅

### Example 3. Mass ka hisaab

Agar 4 g hydrogen poori jal jaye, to kitna oxygen lagega aur kitna paani banega?

- 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O mein hydrogen aur oxygen ka mass anupaat 1 : 8 hai.
- Oxygen = 4 × 8 = **32 g**.
- Paani = 4 + 32 = **36 g**.

Jaanch: 36 ÷ 9 = 4 g hydrogen (paani mein H ka hissa 1/9) ✅

## Part D: Rasayanik gun 2: hydrogen dusre ke saath milti hai

Hydrogen sirf oxygen ke saath nahi, aur bhi kai padarthon ke saath milti hai (har ek teacher demo ya factory ka hissa hai, students ke liye nahi):

| Reaction | Equation | Kab hota hai | Banne wala padarth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oxygen ke saath | 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O | chinagari ya garmi se | paani (aur garmi) |
| Chlorine ke saath | H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl | tez roshni ya garmi se | hydrogen chloride gas (paani mein ghul kar hydrochloric acid) |
| Nitrogen ke saath | N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃ | Haber vidhi: iron catalyst, bahut dabao, garmi; reaction ulta bhi ho sakta hai | ammonia (khaad ka kaccha maal) |

Teeno equations ka atom check: 2H₂ + O₂: H 4 = 4, O 2 = 2 ✅. H₂ + Cl₂: H 2 = 2, Cl 2 = 2 ✅. N₂ + 3H₂: N 2 = 2, H 6 = 6 ✅.

Ek baat: ammonia wala reaction **dono taraf chalta hai** (ulta bhi), aur uski poori sthitiyan (conditions) Class 10 ka vishay hain. Yahan sirf naam aur iska itna kaam yaad rakho: ye ammonia banati hai jo khaad ke liye zaroori hai (Topic 5 mein aur).

## Part E: Rasayanik gun 3: hydrogen ek reducing agent

Chapter 7 ke **Oxidation aur Reduction** ([chemical-changes-and-reactions-part5.md](../chemical-changes-and-reactions/chemical-changes-and-reactions-part5.md)) mein hum ne yahi reaction dekha tha, ab hydrogen ki nazar se:

**CuO + H₂ → Cu + H₂O**

**Sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo):** garam kiye hue kaale CuO ke upar se hydrogen ki dhaar guzaari jaati hai. Kaala powder laal-bhure copper mein badal jaata hai aur tube ke thande hisse par paani ki boondein dikhti hain.

```
        hydrogen ----->  [ garam CuO (kaala) ]  -----> Cu (laal-bhura) + H₂O
                               ^
                         halki garmi

   CuO + H₂  ---->  Cu  +  H₂O

   CuO : oxygen gaya  -> REDUCE hua    -> OXIDISING AGENT
   H₂  : oxygen mila  -> OXIDISE hua   -> REDUCING AGENT
```

> **Figure 4.2** – CuO + H₂ → Cu + H₂O. CuO ka oxygen H₂ ke paas gaya. Is reaction mein **CuO** reduce hua (oxidising agent) aur **H₂** oxidise hua (reducing agent): hydrogen yahan reducing agent ka kaam kar rahi hai.

Ek aur misaal: **Fe₂O₃ + 3H₂ → 2Fe + 3H₂O**.

- Atom check: Fe 2 = 2, O 3 = 3, H 6 = 6 ✅
- Fe₂O₃ ne oxygen khoya: **reduce**. H₂ ne oxygen paya: **oxidise**, reducing agent.

**Hydrogen kin oxides ko reduce karti hai?** Un metals ke oxide jo reactivity series mein bahut upar nahi hain (Chapter 7 Topic 3), jaise copper, lead, tungsten, aur garam karne par iron. Bahut reactive metals (sodium, calcium, magnesium, aluminium) ka oxygen se rishta itna majboot hai ki hydrogen unse oxygen nahi chheen paati. Aisa kyun, iska poora jawab Class 10 mein.

### Example 4. Roles pehchano: WO₃ + 3H₂ → W + 3H₂O

Tungsten (bulb ke filament ka metal) kaccha maal WO₃ se hydrogen ki madad se banaya jaata hai (factory mein).

- Atom check: W 1 = 1, O 3 = 3, H 6 = 6 ✅
- WO₃ ne oxygen khoya (W bana): **reduce hua**, oxidising agent.
- H₂ ne oxygen paya (H₂O bana): **oxidise hua**, reducing agent.

Jaanch: dono taraf O = 3 ✅, aur oxygen H₂ ke paas chala gaya ✅

## Part F: Hydrogen kamre ke taapman par shaant kyun hai?

Ek achambhe wali baat: hydrogen aur oxygen ko ek jagah milakar rakho to **kuch nahi hota**, saalon tak bhi nahi. Par ek chinagari se achanak bahut tez reaction. Kyun?

1. H₂ mein **H–H bond** hota hai (Chapter 6: ek single covalent bond). Reaction ke liye pehle ye bond **tootna** zaroori hai, aur bond todne mein **energy** lagti hai (Chapter 7 Topic 4: shuruaati energy ki rukawat).
2. Kamre ke taapman par kanon ke paas itni energy nahi hoti ki bond toote.
3. Chinagari ya garmi shuru mein kuch kanon ke bond tod deti hai. Toote hue atom naye bond banate hain (H–O) aur **garmi nikalti hai**, aur ye garmi paas ke aur kanon ke bond tod deti hai. Ye silsila apne aap tez hota jaata hai.

```
   Kamre ke taapman par:     H–H   O=O      (bond sahi salamat, reaction nahi)
                                │
                          chinagari / garmi
                                │
                                v
   Kuch kan ke bond toote ----> naye H–O bond bane ----> garmi nikli
                                                              │
                                  ye garmi aur kanon ke bond todti hai
```

> **Figure 4.3** – Hydrogen kamre ke taapman par shaant kyun hai: pehle H–H aur O=O bond todne ke liye chinagari ya garmi chahiye; naye H–O bond bante hi garmi nikalti hai, jo aur bond todti hai, aur reaction apne aap tez ho jaata hai.

Ye wahi vichar hai jo kaagaz ya LPG ke saath dikha: kisi cheez ko jalane ke liye pehle **chinagari** chahiye, phir wo apni garmi se aage jalti rehti hai. Ek aur dilchasp baat: platinum ya palladium jaise **catalyst** ki madad se hydrogen aur oxygen kamre ke taapman par bhi dheere dheere mil sakte hain (Chapter 7 Topic 4 ka catalyst). Isi vichar par fuel cell chalti hai, jiski baat Topic 5 mein hogi.

## Part G: Dhamaka kab hota hai: hydrogen ka mixture

Hydrogen sirf apne aap mein khatarnak nahi, **hawa ke saath ek khaas mixture mein** khatarnak hoti hai. Agar hawa mein hydrogen bahut kam hai, to jalne ke liye kaafi nahi. Agar bahut zyada hai, to oxygen kam pad jaata hai. Lagbhag **4% se 75%** (volume ke hisaab se, ye ank approx hain) ke beech mixture bahut tezi se jalta hai, yaani **dhamake ki seema (explosive range)**. Ye seema bahut chaudi hai: LPG ya petrol ki bhaap ki seema bahut chhoti hoti hai.

```
   Hawa mein hydrogen ka percent (volume ke hisaab se, lagbhag)

    4%                                 75%
     ↓                                   ↓
   +-+===================================+-----------+
   0%                                             100%

   0% se 4%   : bahut kam hydrogen, mixture nahi jalta
   4% se 75%  : DHAMAKE KI SEEMA (===), chinagari se tezi se jalta hai
   75% se 100%: bahut zyada hydrogen, oxygen kam (par hawa milte hi khatra)
```

> **Figure 4.4** – Hydrogen ka explosive range lagbhag 4% se 75% (approx). 4% se kam mein mixture jal nahi paata; 75% se zyada mein hawa ka oxygen kam pad jaata hai. Beech ki chaudi patti mein ek chinagari bhi dhamaka kara sakti hai.

### Example 5. Ek m³ hawa-hydrogen mixture

1 m³ (1000 L) ke mixture mein hydrogen ki matra kab khatarnak hai?

- 4% par: 1000 × 0.04 = **40 L** hydrogen (seema ka kinara).
- 75% par: 1000 × 0.75 = **750 L** hydrogen.
- To 40 L se 750 L hydrogen wale 1000 L mixture mein jalne ka khatra hai.

Sirf 40 L hydrogen (1 litre wali lagbhag 40 bottle jitni) se hi 1 m³ ka mixture dhamake ki seema mein aa jaata hai. Isi liye hydrogen ki leak bahut serious maani jaati hai.

Jaanch: 40 ÷ 1000 = 4% ✅ aur 750 ÷ 1000 = 75% ✅

**Ye bhi yaad rakho:** hydrogen bahut halki hai, to leak ho to wo **upar chhat ki taraf** jaati hai aur wahan jama ho sakti hai. Isiliye jin jagah par hydrogen ka kaam hota hai, wahan chhat ke paas hawa nikalne ki jagah aur sensor rakhe jaate hain.

## Part H: "Pop" test ka matlab

Topic 2 ke lab prep mein hydrogen ki pehchaan thi: **jalti hui teeli** ko test tube ke munh ke paas laane par **"pop"** ki aawaaz. (**Sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo).** Ye test sirf teacher karta hai, bahut chhoti matra mein.)

Ab hum samajh sakte hain ki pop kyun aata hai:

1. Chhoti test tube mein hydrogen ke saath thodi hawa mili hoti hai, yaani mixture **dhamake ki seema** (4% se 75%) mein aa chuka hota hai.
2. Teeli ki chinagari mixture ko jala deti hai, aur **poora mixture ek hi pal mein** jal jaata hai.
3. Reaction se bani garmi paani ko bhaap mein badalti hai, aur bhaap achanak phailti hai: isi se **chhoti si pop** sunayi deti hai.

Agar sirf hydrogen ho (bina hawa ke mile), wo munh par shaanti se jalti hai, pop nahi hota. Aur kabhi bhi **bade ya band bartan mein** hydrogen aur hawa ka mixture nahi jalaya jaata, kyunki wahan pop ki jagah asli dhamaka hoga.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Sochna ki hydrogen doosre ko jalne mein madad karti hai**

Nahi. Hydrogen **khud jalti hai** (fuel hai). Jalne mein madad **oxygen** karti hai.

**Galti 2: Hydrogen aur helium ko ek jaisa samajhna**

Dono halki hain, par helium **nahi jalti**, hydrogen jalti hai. Helium ki density 0.18, hydrogen ki 0.09 g per L. Isiliye gubbaron mein helium bharte hain.

**Galti 3: Mixture kisi bhi percent par dhamake wala maan lena**

Dhamake ki seema lagbhag 4% se 75% hai. 2% ya 90% par chinagari se dhamaka nahi hoga (par 90% bhi surakshit nahi, hawa se mix hone par seema mein aa jaata hai).

**Galti 4: "Reduce" ko "kam hona" samajhna**

CuO + H₂ mein CuO **reduce** hua: iska matlab oxygen nikla (Chapter 7), poora CuO kam nahi hua, wo Cu mein badla.

**Galti 5: Sochna ki hydrogen kamre ke taapman par reaction karti hi nahi**

Bina chinagari, garmi ya catalyst ke reaction lagbhag shuru hi nahi hota (bond nahi toot-te). Chinagari, garmi ya catalyst mile to reaction shuru ho jaata hai.

**Galti 6: Lift ka hisaab karte waqt hydrogen ka vazan jodna**

Lift = hawa ka vazan **minus** gas ka vazan. 1.29 − 0.09 = 1.20 (1.29 + 0.09 nahi).

## Quick Recap

- Hydrogen **rangheen, gandhheen, swaadheen**, paani mein bahut kam ghulti, litmus par **neutral**.
- Density lagbhag **0.09 g per L**: hawa (1.29) se lagbhag **14 guna halki**; helium (0.18) se aadhi.
- Boiling point lagbhag **−253 °C (20 K)**, melting point **−259 °C (14 K)**.
- **Uthaav** = hawa ka vazan − gas ka vazan: hydrogen 1.20 kg per m³, helium 1.11 kg per m³ (helium lagbhag 92.5% uthaav deti hai par jalti nahi).
- **2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O**: garmi deti hai; mass mein H : O = 1 : 8.
- H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl; N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃ (Haber, iron catalyst, ulta bhi hota hai).
- Hydrogen **reducing agent** hai: CuO + H₂ → Cu + H₂O (CuO reduce, H₂ oxidise).
- Kamre ke taapman par shaant: H–H bond todne ke liye chinagari ya garmi chahiye.
- **Dhamake ki seema** lagbhag 4% se 75% (approx); pop test chhoti matra ka mixture hai (sirf teacher demo).

## Practice Questions

1. A weather-balloon manufacturer wants to know the lift of a hydrogen-filled balloon of volume 250 m³ (ignore the weight of the cover). Use air 1.29 kg per m³ and hydrogen 0.09 kg per m³.

2. A balloon must carry a camera of mass 8 kg. Using helium (0.18 kg per m³) and air (1.29 kg per m³), find the minimum volume of helium needed to lift only this camera (ignore the balloon cover).

3. Oxygen melts at −218 °C and boils at −183 °C. Convert both into kelvin. Hydrogen is a liquid only between 14 K and 20 K. Which stays a liquid over a wider range of temperature, hydrogen or oxygen?

4. Name the oxidising agent and the reducing agent in PbO + H₂ → Pb + H₂O. Check that the equation is balanced. (Lead and its compounds are poisonous: this is an equation-only question, never an activity.)

5. 27 g of water is formed when hydrogen burns in oxygen. How much hydrogen and how much oxygen (mass) were used?

6. A mixture contains 2% hydrogen in air, another 30%, and another 90%. Which one(s) can explode on meeting a spark? Give a reason using the explosive range.

7. A storeroom of 60 m³ has a hydrogen leak. How much hydrogen (in m³) makes the air in the room reach the lower end of the explosive range (4%)?

8. Hydrogen and oxygen can stay mixed for years but explode with a spark. Explain using bonds.

9. Why is the pop test done only in a small test tube and only by a teacher?

10. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) Hydrogen is slightly soluble in water. (b) Hydrogen turns blue litmus red. (c) Helium is safer than hydrogen for balloons because it does not burn. (d) In Fe₂O₃ + 3H₂ → 2Fe + 3H₂O, hydrogen is oxidised.

11. A colourless gas is lighter than air, is neutral to litmus, and burns with a pop in a small test tube with a spark (teacher demo). It forms water on burning. Name the gas.

12. Will Al₂O₃ be reduced by hydrogen at the same conditions as CuO? Give a reason using the reactivity series.

### Answers

1. Har 1 m³ par uthaav = 1.29 − 0.09 = 1.20 kg. 250 m³ par = 250 × 1.20 = **300 kg**. Jaanch: 250 × 1.29 = 322.5 kg, 250 × 0.09 = 22.5 kg, 322.5 − 22.5 = 300 ✅

2. Helium ka uthaav per m³ = 1.29 − 0.18 = 1.11 kg. Volume = 8 ÷ 1.11 = **7.2 m³** (lagbhag). Jaanch: 7.2 × 1.11 = 7.99 kg ≈ 8 kg ✅ (asli balloon mein cover ka vazan bhi hota hai, to zyada volume chahiye.)

3. Oxygen: −218 + 273 = **55 K** (melting) aur −183 + 273 = **90 K** (boiling). Oxygen ki liquid range: 90 − 55 = **35 K**; hydrogen ki 20 − 14 = sirf **6 K**. To **oxygen** chaudi range mein liquid rehti hai. Jaanch: −183 − (−218) = 35 ✅; 20 − 14 = 6 ✅

4. **PbO** ka oxygen gaya (reduce hua), wo **oxidising agent** hai. **H₂** ko oxygen mila (oxidise hua), wo **reducing agent** hai. Atom check: Pb 1 = 1, O 1 = 1, H 2 = 2 ✅

5. Paani mein mass anupaat H : O = 1 : 8, yaani hydrogen ka hissa 1/9. H₂ = 27 ÷ 9 = **3 g**; O₂ = 27 − 3 = **24 g**. Jaanch: 24 ÷ 3 = 8 ✅ aur 3 + 24 = 27 ✅

6. Sirf **30%** (4% se 75% ke beech) dhamake wala hai. 2% seema se kam hai (jalne ke liye hydrogen kam), 90% seema se zyada hai (oxygen kam). Jaanch: 4 < 30 < 75 ✅ (90% mixture bhi bahar nikal kar hawa se mil jaaye to seema mein aa jaata hai.)

7. 4% of 60 = 60 × 0.04 = **2.4 m³** hydrogen. Jaanch: 2.4 ÷ 60 = 0.04 ✅

8. Kamre ke taapman par H–H aur O=O bond tootne ke liye kaafi energy nahi hoti, to reaction shuru nahi hota. Chinagari kuch bond tod deti hai, naye H–O bond banne se garmi nikalti hai, wo aur bond todti hai, aur reaction tezi se phail jaata hai.

9. Test tube mein hydrogen ke saath thodi hawa mili hoti hai, to mixture dhamake ki seema mein aata hai aur chhoti si "pop" se jal jaata hai. Bade bartan mein wahi mixture asli dhamaka karta. Isiliye ye sirf teacher karta hai, bahut chhoti matra mein, safety screen ke saath.

10. (a) **True** ✅. Bahut kam ghulti hai. (b) **False** ❌. Hydrogen litmus par **koi asar nahi** karti (neutral). (c) **True** ✅. Helium jalti nahi. (d) **True** ✅. H₂ ko oxygen mila (H₂O bana).

11. **Hydrogen**. Hawa se halki, litmus par neutral, spark se pop, aur jalne par paani: ye sab hydrogen ke gun hain. (Ye test sirf teacher karta hai.)

12. **Nahi.** Aluminium reactivity series mein bahut upar hai aur oxygen ko bahut majbooti se pakadta hai, isliye hydrogen Al₂O₃ se oxygen nahi chheen paati. Copper kam reactive hai, to CuO ka oxygen H₂ le leti hai.

Shabaash! Ab tum hydrogen ke **gun** jaante ho: sabse halki gas, hawa se 14 guna halki, jis par gubbaron ka uthaav (1.20 kg per m³) bhi tum ginti se nikaal sakte ho; jo jalti hai aur sirf paani banati hai; jo CuO jaise oxides ka oxygen le leti hai; aur jo dhamake ki seema (4% se 75%) mein khatarnak hai. Rocket ki "garam paani wali aag" ka raaz bhi ab saaf hai.

**Aage kya?** In sabhi gunon se hydrogen ke **upyog** nikalte hain: ammonia se khaad, tel se vanaspati, rocket aur bijli bananewali fuel cell. Lekin utna hi zaroori hai ye jaanna ki ise **surakshit** kaise rakhein aur Hindenburg jaise haadse se kya seekhein. Agle topic **"Hydrogen Ke Upyog, Safety aur Bhavishya"** mein ye sab, aur hydrogen kyun "kal ka fuel" kehlaati hai.
