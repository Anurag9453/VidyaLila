# Topic 5: Hydrogen Ke Upyog, Safety aur Bhavishya

Pichhle topic mein humne hydrogen ke gun jaane: sabse halki gas, hawa mein 4% se 75% ke beech explosive, aur ek achha reducing agent. Ab sawaal ye hai ki **in gunon ka fayda kahan milta hai?** Is topic mein dekhenge ki hydrogen ammonia, vanaspati, rocket aur **fuel cell** mein kaise kaam aata hai, uski **energy petrol se kitni zyada** hai, usse **store karna itna mushkil kyun** hai, **Hindenburg** ki ghatna se kya seekha, helium aur hydrogen ke balloon mein kaun surakshit hai, aur kya kabhi hum **hydrogen economy** mein rahenge.

## Auto expo ki khamosh bus

Mr. Sabnis (Sabnis sir) apne shagird Mrinal ko ek **auto expo** mein le gaye. Hall mein tez engine ki awaazein, chamakti gaadiyan, aur har taraf petrol aur diesel ki halki si gandh.

Achanak ek bus dheere se gujri. **Na engine ki garjan, na dhuan.** Bus ke peeche exhaust pipe se sirf **paani ki boondein** tapak rahi thin, jaise AC se tapakti hain.

"Sir, ye bus kaise chal rahi hai?" Mrinal ne aankhein faad kar poocha. "Isme to dhuan hi nahi hai!"

Mr. Sabnis ne board padha: **"Hydrogen Fuel-Cell Bus."** "Isme hydrogen ek tank mein bhari hui hai. Hydrogen aur hawa ki oxygen ek **fuel cell** mein milti hain, bijli banti hai, aur bijli motor chalati hai. Bachta kya hai?"

"Paani!" Mrinal bola. "To wahi boondein!"

"Bilkul. Par ek baat dhyan se sunna," Sabnis sir ne tank ke paas laga **pila warning sticker** dikhaya: *Flammable gas. No open flame.* "Ye wahi gas hai jo hawa mein jalkar dhamaka kar sakti hai. Isliye is bus ko banane mein utni mehnat tank, valve aur sensor par lagti hai jitni engine par. Chalo, seekhte hain ki hydrogen ke itne upyog kyun hain aur dikkat kahan hai."

Mrinal ne copy mein likha: **"Hydrogen: kaam bhi bahut, savdhaani bhi bahut."**

## Part B: Ammonia aur fertiliser

Hydrogen ka **sabse bada upyog** ammonia banane mein hai. Ye Topic 4 wala hi niyam hai: nitrogen aur hydrogen mil kar ammonia dete hain.

N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃

Is vidhi ko **Haber process** kehte hain: iron catalyst, bahut zyada pressure aur garmi. Ye reaction **reversible** hai (aage-peeche dono chalti hai); iski poori conditions aur equilibrium aage ki class mein aayenge. Ammonia se phir **fertiliser** (jaise urea) bante hain, jo khet ki fasal ko nitrogen dete hain. Isliye hum jo roti khaate hain, uske peeche bhi hydrogen ka haath hai.

**Example 1: Ammonia ka mass hisaab.**
Atomic masses (lagbhag): H = 1, N = 14.
- 3H₂ ka mass = 3 × 2 = **6 g**
- N₂ ka mass = 2 × 14 = **28 g**
- 2NH₃ ka mass = 2 × (14 + 3) = 2 × 17 = **34 g**

Yaani **6 g hydrogen + 28 g nitrogen → 34 g ammonia**.
Kul mass pehle: 6 + 28 = 34 g aur baad mein: 34 g. Mass bacha ✅ (Chapter 4 ka niyam).

> **Jaanch:** Atom count: baayen taraf N = 2, H = 6; daayen taraf 2NH₃ mein N = 2, H = 6 ✅. 6 + 28 = 34 ✅.

## Part C: Hydrogenation: tel se vanaspati

Vegetable tel liquid hota hai, par kuch **vanaspati** jama hua (solid) hota hai. Dono ke andar ke particles ki lambi chain mein kuch jagah **double bond** hota hai. Factory mein tel ko garam karke, **nickel catalyst** ke saath usme hydrogen gas guzaarne par hydrogen un double bondon par jud jaata hai aur chain "bhar" jaati hai. Jis chain mein double bond hota hai wo tel hai (liquid), jo "bhari hui" ho wo zyada solid (vanaspati) ban jaata hai.

Isko seedhe **"tel mein hydrogen jodna"** kehte hain, yaani **hydrogenation**. Ek bahut chhote model se dekhein (ye Class 10 ki ek jhalak hai; carbon compounds tab padhenge, abhi sirf andaaza dene ke liye ethene C₂H₄ ka model):

C₂H₄ + H₂ → C₂H₆

Ek double bond wala C₂H₄ (jaise tel ki chain mein) nickel catalyst ke saath H–H se milta hai, double bond khulta hai aur har carbon par ek H judta hai: bhara hua C₂H₆ (jaise vanaspati ki chain).

> **Jaanch:** Atom count: baayen taraf C = 2, H = 4 + 2 = 6; daayen taraf C = 2, H = 6 ✅.

**Dhyan:** Ye kaam factory mein special tank aur catalyst ke saath hota hai. **Ghar par ya school mein koi nahi karta**, hum sirf padh rahe hain.

## Part D: Rocket fuel (cryogenic stage)

Rocket ko bahut zyada energy chahiye, par bahut halka bhi rehna hai. Hydrogen yahan chamakta hai: **liquid hydrogen (LH₂)** ko **liquid oxygen (LOX)** ke saath jalate hain. ISRO ke **GSLV Mk III** rocket ke **cryogenic stage** mein yahi joda use hota hai.

2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O

- Liquid hydrogen lagbhag **−253 °C** par banta hai. Kelvin mein: −253 + 273 = **20 K** (Topic 4 ka boiling point).
- Liquid oxygen lagbhag **−183 °C** par (approximate).
- Jalne ke baad exhaust mein mukhya roop se **paani ki bhaap** nikalti hai. Isi wajah se Topic 4 wali rocket-flame bhi lagbhag saaf dikhti thi.

> **Jaanch:** Atom count: baayen taraf H = 4, O = 2; daayen taraf 2H₂O mein H = 4, O = 2 ✅. −253 + 273 = 20 ✅.

Cryogenic tank kaafi **mazboot aur ghani insulation wala** hota hai taaki liquid hydrogen garam hokar bhaap na ban jaaye. Ye kaam sirf **trained engineers** bade safety niyamon ke saath karte hain.

## Part E: Fuel cell: bijli seedhi, paani ka bonus

Ab bus wali baat. **Fuel cell** ek aisa "cell" hai jise Physics ke battery cell ki tarah samajh sakte ho, par isme chemical **bahar se lagatar daalte rehte** hain: hydrogen ek taraf, oxygen (hawa se) doosri taraf. Dono ke milne se paani banta hai, aur **electrons ek taar se guzar kar bijli (current)** ban jaate hain.

Kul reaction wahi hai jo jalne mein hota hai, par **dhamaake aur aag ke bina**:

2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O (+ bijli)

```
        H₂ (hydrogen) andar                 O₂ (hawa se oxygen) andar
              │                                       │
              v                                       v
     +-----------------+   electrolyte    +--------------------+
     │   H₂ wali       │  (ek khaas       │   O₂ wali          │
     │   plate (−)     │   parda)         │   plate (+)        │
     +-----------------+                  +--------------------+
              │                                       │
              +------- taar -----[ bulb / motor ]-----+
                       electrons ka bahav (current)

                       ... dono taraf milkar ...
                              │
                              v
                       PAANI (H₂O) bahar
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Fuel cell ka saral roop: ek taraf H₂, doosri taraf O₂ andar jaati hai; beech mein ek khaas parda (electrolyte) hota hai (uske andar kya hota hai, ye aage ki class mein), aur electrons bahar ke taar se ghoom kar current dete hain, bulb ya motor chalta hai; antim product sirf paani. (Seedhi sketch, asli cell mein kai parat aur catalyst hote hain.)

Ye jalne se alag kaise? **Jalne** mein heat ek jhatke mein nikalti hai. **Fuel cell** mein hum wahi reaction dheere, niyantrit tareeke se karwate hain aur energy ka bada hissa **seedhe bijli** mein badal jaata hai. Isliye fuel-cell bus engine se zyada shaant aur saaf chalti hai.

**Example 2: Fuel cell se kitna paani banta hai?**
2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O mein:
- 2H₂ ka mass = 2 × 2 = **4 g**
- 2H₂O ka mass = 2 × 18 = **36 g**

Yaani 4 g hydrogen se 36 g paani, to **1 kg hydrogen se 9 kg paani** banta hai (36 ÷ 4 = 9). Isliye bus ke peeche se paani ki boondein tapakti hain.

> **Jaanch:** Atom count ✅ (H = 4, O = 2 dono taraf). 36 ÷ 4 = 9 ✅. Oxygen jo hawa se li gayi: 36 − 4 = 32 g ✅ (O₂ ka mass 32).

**Paper activity (sirf kaagaz par, koi chemical nahi):** Ek A4 kaagaz par fuel cell ka figure khud banao. Dono plates, H₂ aur O₂ ke arrows, electrolyte, taar aur bulb, aur paani ki nikaasi label karo.

## Part G: Energy per kilogram: hydrogen kitna "taakatwar" hai?

Fuel ko naapne ka ek seedha tareeka: **1 kg jalne par kitni energy milti hai?**

- **Hydrogen:** lagbhag **120 MJ per kg** (approximate)
- **Petrol:** lagbhag **45 MJ per kg** (approximate)

```
   Energy per kilogram (lagbhag, MJ per kg)
   har  █  =  5 MJ

   Hydrogen  ████████████████████████  120 MJ
   Petrol    █████████                  45 MJ

   Hydrogen petrol se lagbhag 2.7 guna
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Energy per kilogram: hydrogen ke 1 kg se lagbhag 120 MJ aur petrol ke 1 kg se lagbhag 45 MJ; hydrogen lagbhag 2.7 guna. (Approximate, alag-alag source ki value thodi alag ho sakti hai.)

**Example 3: Ek jaisa mass, kitni energy?**
2 kg fuel jalne par:
- Hydrogen: 2 × 120 = **240 MJ**
- Petrol: 2 × 45 = **90 MJ**
- Anupaat: 120 ÷ 45 = **2.67** (lagbhag 2.7 guna)

Isliye rocket ko, jahan **har kilogram bhaar** mehnga hai, hydrogen pasand hai.

> **Jaanch:** 2 × 120 = 240 ✅, 2 × 45 = 90 ✅, 120 ÷ 45 = 2.667 ✅.

**Par ek problem:** energy **per kilogram** ek baat hai, energy **per litre (volume)** doosri. Hydrogen itni halki hai ki 1 kg hydrogen bahut bahut bada volume leta hai. Dekhte hain.

## Part H: Storage ki samasya

Topic 4 ka density yaad karo: hydrogen lagbhag **0.09 kg per m³** (STP par, approximate). Petrol liquid hai, lagbhag **740 kg per m³** (approximate).

**Example 4: Ek ghan meter (1 m³) mein kitni energy?**
- **Hydrogen gas (STP par):** 0.09 kg × 120 MJ per kg = **10.8 MJ**
- **Liquid hydrogen** (density lagbhag 71 kg per m³, approximate): 71 × 120 = **8,520 MJ**
- **Petrol:** 740 × 45 = **33,300 MJ**

Taalika ke roop mein:

| Cheez (1 m³) | Energy (lagbhag) | Petrol ke muqable |
|---|---|---|
| Hydrogen gas, STP | 10.8 MJ | lagbhag 3,000 guna kam |
| Liquid hydrogen | 8,520 MJ | lagbhag 3.9 guna kam |
| Petrol | 33,300 MJ | 1 (maanak) |

Yaani **kilogram ke hisaab se hydrogen jeet jaata hai, par jagah ke hisaab se petrol**. Gaadi mein tank ke liye jagah kam hoti hai, isliye hydrogen ko bahut dabaana ya thanda karke liquid banana padta hai.

> **Jaanch:** 0.09 × 120 = 10.8 ✅. 71 × 120 = 8520 ✅. 740 × 45 = 33300 ✅. 33300 ÷ 10.8 = 3083 (lagbhag 3,000) ✅. 33300 ÷ 8520 = 3.91 ✅.

**Hydrogen ko store karne ke do mukhya tareeke:**
1. **Compressed gas:** bahut mazboot cylinder mein bahut zyada pressure par (Chapter 10 ki cylinder safety lagti hai: kabhi garam nahi, dhoop mein nahi, girao nahi, regular check).
2. **Liquid hydrogen:** −253 °C tak thanda karke, ghani insulation wale tank mein. Bahut zyada energy lagti hai thanda karne mein, aur thodi hydrogen dheere dheere "boil off" hoti rehti hai.

**Doosri dikkatein:**
- Hydrogen ke **kan bahut chhote** hain, to wo chhote se chhote **leak** se bhi nikal jaati hai.
- Ye **rangeen nahi, gandh nahi** hai, to leak dikhta ya sunghta nahi (isliye alag sensor lagte hain).
- **Explosive range 4% se 75%** (Topic 4) bahut chauda hai, to band jagah mein leak khatarnak hai.

## Part I: Hindenburg ki seekh

**1937** mein ek bahut bada hawai jahaz (airship) **Hindenburg** hydrogen se bhara tha. Amerika mein utarte waqt usme aag lag gayi aur wo kuch hi der mein jal gaya. **Kul 36 logon ki maut hui.** Uska volume lagbhag **2 × 10⁵ m³** tha (approximate); jab uski hydrogen ne hawa se mil kar aag pakdi to bachna mushkil tha.

**Example 5: Hydrogen ya helium se kitna lift?**
Topic 4 ki tarah lift = hawa ki density − gas ki density (per m³). Density: hawa 1.29, hydrogen 0.09, helium 0.18 (kg per m³ ya g per L, approximate).
- Hydrogen ka lift = 1.29 − 0.09 = **1.20 kg per m³**
- Helium ka lift = 1.29 − 0.18 = **1.11 kg per m³**
- Antar = 1.20 − 1.11 = 0.09 kg per m³, yaani hydrogen ka lift sirf **7.5% zyada** (helium ka 92.5%).

Yaani helium **sirf thoda** kam lift deta hai par **aag nahi pakadti**. Hindenburg jaise bade volume mein: 1.20 × 2 × 10⁵ = **2.4 × 10⁵ kg** (lagbhag 240 tonne) hydrogen se lift, helium se 1.11 × 2 × 10⁵ = 2.22 × 10⁵ kg (lagbhag 222 tonne).

> **Jaanch:** 1.29 − 0.09 = 1.20 ✅, 1.29 − 0.18 = 1.11 ✅, 1.11 ÷ 1.20 = 0.925 ✅. 1.20 × 200000 = 240000 ✅.

**Seekh (strict safety):**
- Aaj **gubbare aur airship helium** se bharte hain, hydrogen se nahi.
- **Bachchon ke liye hydrogen ke gubbare kabhi nahi.** Hydrogen ka gubbara ek chingari se (static bijli ya dia se bhi) phat kar jal sakta hai.
- Gubbare **shop se helium** ke hi lo, kisi cylinder se khud kabhi na bharo.
- **Kabhi bhi hydrogen ke aas-paas aag, chingari, dia, agarbatti ya mobile jaise cheezein na lao.**

## Part J: Hydrogen economy: sapna aur sach

**Hydrogen economy** ka sapna ye hai: **suraj aur hawa ki bijli** (solar, wind) se paani ka electrolysis karke **green hydrogen** banao (Topic 3), use store karo, aur gaadi, factory, jahaz aur bijli ke liye use karo, aur bachao sirf **paani**.

Chakra aise chalta hai: suraj ya hawa se bijli, bijli se paani ka electrolysis, green H₂, tank mein store, fuel cell ya factory mein use, aur bachta hai paani, jo phir electrolysis mein kaam aa sakta hai.

Bharat mein **National Green Hydrogen Mission** (2023 mein shuru) is disha mein kadam hai.

Par abhi raste mein rukawatein hain:
- **Aaj ki zyadatar hydrogen "grey"** hai (natural gas se, Topic 3), jo saaf nahi hai.
- **Green hydrogen mehngi** hai.
- **Storage aur transport** mushkil hai (Part H).
- **Safety ke liye naya infrastructure** (pump, sensor, training) chahiye.

Isliye hydrogen economy **ek badi koshish hai**, aaj ki sachchai nahi. Par seedha sawaal ye hai: **agar hydrogen jalne par sirf paani deti hai, to dusre fuel kya chhodte hain?** Ye hamein agle chapter **Atmospheric Pollution** tak le jaata hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Hydrogen ka gubbara phula lete hain."**
Nahi. Bachchon ke gubbare helium se hote hain. Hydrogen aag pakad sakti hai.

**Galti 2: "Fuel cell mein jalne wali aag hoti hai."**
Fuel cell mein aag nahi, ek niyantrit chemical reaction se seedhe bijli banti hai.

**Galti 3: "Hydrogen petrol se har tarah se behtar hai."**
Sirf **per kilogram** energy mein. **Per litre** mein petrol aage hai.

**Galti 4: "Hydrogen ek natural fuel hai, kuan khod lo."**
Dharti par hydrogen almost hamesha combined hai; use banana padta hai (Topic 3), isliye ye "fuel" se zyada **energy carrier** hai.

**Galti 5: "Fuel cell ka matlab sirf bus."**
Fuel cell chhote bijli generator se lekar gaadi aur ghar tak ke liye ban sakta hai.

**Galti 6: "Leak sunghkar pakad lenge."**
Hydrogen bina gandh ki hai. Sensor aur ventilation zaroori.

**Galti 7: "Cylinder ko dhoop mein ya aag ke paas rakh sakte hain."**
Nahi. Band cylinder garam hone par phat sakta hai (Chapter 10).

## Quick Recap

- Hydrogen ke mukhya upyog: **ammonia** (N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃, fertiliser), **vanaspati** (tel + H₂, nickel catalyst), **rocket fuel** (LH₂ + LOX, ISRO GSLV Mk III), **fuel cell** (H₂ + O₂ → paani + bijli), **oxy-hydrogen welding**, HCl, methanol aur dhatu-nikaasi.
- Fuel cell ek aisa cell hai jisme H₂ aur O₂ andar jaati hain, bijli bahar aur paani hi bachta hai; 1 kg H₂ se 9 kg paani.
- Energy per kilogram: hydrogen lagbhag 120 MJ, petrol lagbhag 45 MJ, anupaat lagbhag 2.7 (approximate).
- Per litre mein hydrogen peeche: gas lagbhag 3,000 guna, liquid lagbhag 3.9 guna kam energy (approximate). Isliye store karna mushkil: compressed ya liquid (−253 °C).
- Hindenburg (1937, 36 maut): hydrogen ka airship jal gaya. Aaj gubbare aur airship **helium** se; **hydrogen ke gubbare kabhi nahi**.
- Helium ka lift sirf lagbhag 7.5% kam, par aag nahi pakadti.
- Hydrogen economy: green hydrogen, store, fuel cell, bachta sirf paani. Rukawat: laagat, storage, safety, aur aaj ki zyadatar hydrogen grey.
- Hydrogen jalne par paani deti hai; dusre fuel kya dete hain? Agla chapter.

## Practice Questions

1. In the methanol reaction CO + 2H₂ → CH₃OH, how many grams of methanol are made from 10 g of hydrogen (enough CO available)? Use C = 12, O = 16, H = 1. First check that the equation is balanced.

5. A weather balloon of 20 m³ is to be filled with helium (0.18 kg per m³) in air of density 1.29 kg per m³. Find its lift. How much more lift would the same volume of hydrogen give, and why is helium still the choice?

6. Hydrogen can reduce iron(III) oxide: Fe₂O₃ + 3H₂ → 2Fe + 3H₂O. (a) Verify the atom balance. (b) How many grams of iron come from 12 g of hydrogen? (Fe = 56, O = 16.) (c) Which substance is oxidised and which is reduced?

7. Write true or false and correct the false ones: (a) A fuel cell produces electricity by burning hydrogen with a flame. (b) Hydrogen has more energy per kilogram than petrol. (c) A child can safely fill a balloon with hydrogen from a school cylinder if no one is smoking nearby. (d) Liquid hydrogen is stored at about −253 °C.

8. A student says, "Propene (C₃H₆) takes up hydrogen just like the oil in vanaspati-making: C₃H₆ + H₂ → C₃H₈." Check that this equation is balanced and say what the hydrogen did to the double bond.

9. A hydrogen filling station is being planned. List four safety features that you would insist on, with a one-line reason for each (paper task).

10. Match the property of hydrogen with the use it explains: (a) lightest gas; (b) burns to give only water; (c) reducing agent; (d) very hot flame with oxygen; (e) combines with nitrogen. Uses: welding, ammonia, weather balloon (helium is safer in practice), fuel cell, extracting a metal from its oxide.

### Answers

1. Atom check: baayen taraf C = 1, O = 1, H = 4; daayen taraf CH₃OH mein C = 1, O = 1, H = 4 ✅. 2H₂ ka mass = 4 g aur CH₃OH ka mass = 12 + 4 + 16 = 32 g, to 4 g H₂ se 32 g methanol. 10 g H₂ ke liye: 10 ÷ 4 = 2.5 baar, to 2.5 × 32 = **80 g methanol**. Jaanch: 80 ÷ 32 = 2.5 aur 10 ÷ 4 = 2.5 ✅.

2. 1 kg H₂ se 9 kg paani (Example 2), to 12 kg H₂ se 12 × 9 = **108 kg paani**. Tailpipe par sirf paani nikalta hai: CO₂ ya dhuan nahi (par hydrogen kaise bani, wo alag sawaal hai). Jaanch: 12 × 9 = 108 ✅ (12 kg H₂ + 96 kg O₂ = 108 kg).

3. Hydrogen: 5 × 120 = **600 MJ**; petrol: 5 × 45 = **225 MJ**. Sirf mass dekhein to **hydrogen behtar**: 600 ÷ 225 = 2.67 guna. Jaanch: 5 × 120 = 600 ✅, 5 × 45 = 225 ✅, 600 ÷ 225 = 2.667 ✅.

4. 40 × 120 = **4,800 MJ** per m³. Petrol 33,300 MJ per m³, to 33,300 ÷ 4,800 = **lagbhag 6.9 guna** (petrol zyada). Hydrogen ko bahut mazboot, bhaari cylinder, ghani safety, aur khas filling pumps ki zaroorat hoti hai; wahan bhi usi jagah mein kam energy aati hai. Jaanch: 40 × 120 = 4800 ✅, 33300 ÷ 4800 = 6.94 ✅.

5. Lift (helium) = 1.29 − 0.18 = 1.11 kg per m³, to 20 m³ ka lift = 1.11 × 20 = **22.2 kg**. Hydrogen ka lift = (1.29 − 0.09) × 20 = 1.20 × 20 = **24.0 kg**. Antar = 24.0 − 22.2 = **1.8 kg** (lagbhag 7.5% zyada). Helium phir bhi isliye, kyunki wo **aag nahi pakadti**; thoda sa lift badhaane ke liye khatra lena sahi nahi. Jaanch: 1.11 × 20 = 22.2 ✅, 1.20 × 20 = 24.0 ✅.

6. (a) Baayen taraf: Fe = 2, O = 3, H = 6; daayen taraf: 2Fe aur 3H₂O mein Fe = 2, O = 3, H = 6 ✅. (b) 3H₂ ka mass = 6 g aur 2Fe ka mass = 2 × 56 = 112 g, to 6 g H₂ se 112 g Fe. 12 g H₂ = 2 baar, to **224 g iron**. (c) **Hydrogen oxidise** hota hai (oxygen leta hai, H₂ → H₂O); **Fe₂O₃ reduce** hota hai (oxygen khota hai, Fe banta hai). Hydrogen yahan reducing agent hai. Jaanch: 12 ÷ 6 = 2 aur 2 × 112 = 224 ✅ (Fe₂O₃ ka 2 × 160 = 320 g lagta hai: 320 − 96 = 224 ✅).

7. (a) **False** ❌. Fuel cell mein aag nahi hoti; hydrogen aur oxygen ke niyantrit reaction se seedhe bijli banti hai. (b) **True** ✅ (lagbhag 120 MJ per kg ke saamne 45 MJ per kg, **per kilogram**). (c) **False** ❌. Hydrogen ke gubbare kabhi nahi; ek chingari bhi aag laga sakti hai, aur gubbare sirf shop se helium ke hi lo. (d) **True** ✅ (lagbhag −253 °C, yaani 20 K).

8. Atom check: baayen taraf C = 3, H = 6 + 2 = 8; daayen taraf C₃H₈ mein C = 3, H = 8 ✅. Hydrogen ne **double bond ko tod kar** donon carbon par ek-ek H jod diya, double bond ab single bond ban gaya: chain "bhar" gayi (hydrogenation).

9. Ek achha jawab: (i) **Hydrogen sensor aur alarm**, kyunki leak dikhta ya sunghta nahi. (ii) **Khuli, hawadaar jagah**, taaki leak jama na ho aur 4% se 75% range tak na pahunche. (iii) **No flame, no smoking, no mobile zone**, kyunki ek chingari bhi aag laga sakti hai. (iv) **Mazboot, rakhrakhav wale cylinder aur valve** jo dhoop aur garmi se door hon, kyunki band cylinder garam hone par phat sakta hai. (Aur bhi sahi jawab ho sakte hain: trained staff, aag bujhane ka intezaam.)

10. (a) Lightest gas, **weather balloon** (par helium zyada surakshit). (b) Burns to give only water, **fuel cell** (aur rocket). (c) Reducing agent, **extracting a metal from its oxide**. (d) Very hot flame with oxygen, **welding**. (e) Combines with nitrogen, **ammonia**.
