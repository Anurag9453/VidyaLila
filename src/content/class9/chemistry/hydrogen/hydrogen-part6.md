## Part A: Kaun sa tareeka? (decision flow)

Hydrogen banane ke kai tareeke hain (Topic 2 aur Topic 3). Sawaal hai: **kab kaun sa?** Isme teen cheezein dekhi jaati hain: **kitni** chahiye, **kaccha maal** kya hai, aur **saaf** hona zaroori hai ya nahi.

```
   +---------------------------------------------------+
   SAWAAL A: Bahut thodi hydrogen, sirf dekhne ke liye
             (school lab, teacher demo)?
   +---------------------------------------------------+
        haan --> metal + dilute acid (Zn, Mg ya Fe)
                 [Topic 2, thodi matra, teacher ke saath]
        nahi
         v
   +---------------------------------------------------+
   SAWAAL B: Factory ke pamaane par, saste mein, aur
             natural gas ya coke maujood hai?
   +---------------------------------------------------+
        haan --> steam reforming ya water gas, phir Bosch step
                 [Topic 3, "grey" (CO₂ nikalti hai)]
        nahi
         v
   +---------------------------------------------------+
   SAWAAL C: Saaf hydrogen chahiye aur solar ya wind ki
             sasti bijli mil rahi hai?
   +---------------------------------------------------+
        haan --> paani ka electrolysis
                 [Topic 3, "green"]
        nahi
         v
   +---------------------------------------------------+
   SAWAAL D: Namak ke ghol (brine) ka electrolysis pehle se
             kisi kaam ke liye chal raha hai?
   +---------------------------------------------------+
        haan --> hydrogen by-product ke roop mein mil jaati hai
        nahi --> sawaal A se phir dekho: kaun si zaroorat badli?
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Decision flow: hydrogen banane ka tareeka chunne ke liye pehle matra aur kaccha maal dekho. Thodi matra (sirf teacher demo): metal aur dilute acid. Bahut zyada aur saste mein: natural gas ya coke se. Saaf aur sasti bijli ke saath: paani ka electrolysis (green). Brine ke electrolysis mein hydrogen apne aap by-product mil jaati hai.

**Example 1: Kaun sa tareeka?**
Teen situations: (a) Class ke saamne teacher ek chhota demo dikhana chahte hain. (b) Ek khaad ka bada plant roz hazaron kilo hydrogen chahta hai aur paas mein natural gas ki pipeline hai. (c) Ek registani ilaka jahan dhoop bahut hai aur solar bijli bahut sasti hai, aur sarkar saaf hydrogen chahti hai.
(a) Sawaal A: haan, to **zinc granules aur dilute acid** (Topic 2: zinc ka reaction kaabu mein rehta hai), sirf teacher ke saath, thodi matra mein, aag door, hawa ki aavjaahi, safety screen ke saath (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo).
(b) Sawaal A nahi (matra bahut badi). Sawaal B: haan, to **steam reforming**: CH₄ + H₂O → CO + 3H₂, phir Bosch step: CO + H₂O → CO₂ + H₂. Ye "grey" hydrogen hai kyunki CO₂ nikalti hai.
(c) Sawaal C: haan, to **solar bijli se paani ka electrolysis**: 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂. Ye **green hydrogen** hai, aur isi ke liye India ne 2023 mein **National Green Hydrogen Mission** shuru kiya.

> **Jaanch:** Teeno jawab alag-alag sawaal par ruke. Agar (c) mein bijli coal se aati to hydrogen "green" nahi kehlati, kyunki asli saaf-pan bijli ke source par bhi tika hai. ✅

**Example 2: Natural gas se kitni hydrogen?**
Steam reforming aur Bosch step ke do equations ko jodo:
CH₄ + H₂O → CO + 3H₂
CO + H₂O → CO₂ + H₂
Dono ka jod: **CH₄ + 2H₂O → CO₂ + 4H₂** (beech ka CO dono taraf kat gaya).
Yaani 1 CH₄ se **4 H₂** milte hain. 10 CH₄ kan se **10 × 4 = 40 H₂** kan. In 4 hydrogen molecules mein 8 hydrogen atoms hain: 4 CH₄ ke kan se aate hain, aur 4 do paani ke kan se (2 × 2).

> **Jaanch:** Atom count. Baayein: C 1, H 4 + 4 = 8, O 2. Daayein: C 1, O 2, H 8. ✅

Dhyan do: iska CO₂ hawa mein chhodna hi "grey" hone ki wajah hai. Agar CO₂ ko pakad kar zameen mein rok diya jaye, to wo "blue" kehlati hai (Topic 3).

**Example 3: Paani ka electrolysis, mass aur volume.**
Maan lo 90 g paani ka electrolysis hota hai: 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂. Paani ka molar mass 2 × 1 + 16 = 18 g, to 90 g = 90 ÷ 18 = 5 mol paani. 2 mol paani se 2 mol H₂ milta hai, to 5 mol paani se **5 mol H₂**, yaani 5 × 2 = **10 g H₂**. Oxygen: 2 mol paani se 1 mol O₂, to 5 mol se 2.5 mol O₂ = 2.5 × 32 = **80 g O₂**. Kul 10 + 80 = 90 g: bilkul paani ka mass (mass conserved).
Volume ke liye Chapter 10: 2 volume hydrogen aur 1 volume oxygen. Agar negative electrode par 40 mL hydrogen ikattha hui, to positive electrode par **20 mL O₂** hogi.

> **Jaanch:** 10 + 80 = 90 ✅. Hydrogen aur oxygen ka mass ratio 10 : 80 = 1 : 8, jo paani ka fixed composition hai (Chapter 9). Volume 40 : 20 = 2 : 1 ✅.

## Part B: Kaun sa metal kaam karega?

Topic 2 mein humne dekha tha ki sirf wahi metals hydrogen dete hain jo reactivity series mein **hydrogen se upar** hain. Series (upar se neeche): K, Na, Ca, Mg, Al, Zn, Fe, Pb, **(H)**, Cu, Hg, Ag, Au.

**Example 4: Kaun hydrogen dega?**
Dilute hydrochloric acid ke saath in metals ko dekhte hain (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo): Mg, Zn, Fe, Cu, Ag, Na.
- **Mg, Zn, Fe**: hydrogen se upar, to bubbles dete hain. Mg + 2HCl → MgCl₂ + H₂ (tez), Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂ (darmiyani), Fe + 2HCl → FeCl₂ + H₂ (dheere).
- **Cu, Ag**: hydrogen se neeche, to **kuch nahi hota**.
- **Na**: bahut upar hai, acid ya paani ke saath itna tez aur khatarnak react karta hai ki lab mein use is kaam ke liye nahi lete.

> **Jaanch:** Atom count: Mg + 2HCl → MgCl₂ + H₂: Mg 1, H 2, Cl 2 dono taraf ✅. Zn + 2HCl: Zn 1, H 2, Cl 2 ✅. Fe + 2HCl: Fe 1, H 2, Cl 2 ✅.

**Example 5: Anjaan metal X.**
Ek anjaan metal X dilute HCl ke saath bubbles deta hai. X zinc sulphate ke ghol se zinc ko **nahi hataata**, par copper sulphate ke ghol se copper ko hata deta hai. Options: Mg, Fe, Cu, Ag. Kaun hai X?
Bubbles diye: to X hydrogen se upar: Cu aur Ag **kat gaye**. X, zinc ko nahi hata paaya: to X zinc se **kam reactive** hai. Mg zinc se zyada reactive hai aur zinc ko hata deta, to Mg **kat gaya**. X ne copper ko hataya: to X copper se zyada reactive. Bacha **Fe**: Zn ke neeche aur H aur Cu ke upar. Jawab: **Fe** (iron), aur sirf ek hi option sab clues par khara utarta hai.

> **Jaanch:** Fe, Zn se neeche ✅ (Zn ko nahi hataata), Fe, H se upar ✅ (bubbles), Fe, Cu se upar ✅ (Cu ko hataata). Teen clue, ek jawab.

## Part C: Riddle card: "Main kaun hoon?"

Ab Riddhi ke card ko ek ek clue se jaanchte hain. Har clue ek gas hata sakta hai, aur aakhir mein **sirf ek** bachni chahiye.

```
   *====================================================*
   *            RIDDLE CARD No. 1  (Main kaun hoon?)    *
   *----------------------------------------------------*
   *  1. Main sabse halki gas hoon.                     *
   *  2. Mera koi rang, gandh ya swad nahi.             *
   *  3. Paani mein main lagbhag nahi ghulti.           *
   *  4. Oxygen ke saath jalne par mujhse sirf paani    *
   *     banta hai (aur kuch nahi).                     *
   *  5. Meri thodi matra jalte splinter ke paas aaye   *
   *     to "pop" (sirf teacher demo).                  *
   *                                                    *
   *  MAIN KAUN HOON?                                   *
   *====================================================*
```

> **Figure 6.2** – Riddle card: ek gas ke paanch clue. Har clue ek ya zyada gaason ko hataata hai; jo gas saare clue par khari utarti hai wahi jawab hai. (Ye sirf kagaz ka khel hai, koi gas banayi ya jalayi nahi jaati.)

**Example 6: Card ka hal.**
- Clue 1 ("sabse halki gas"): helium dusri sabse halki hai, hydrogen pehli. Sirf **hydrogen** bachti hai.
- Clue 2: hydrogen colourless, odourless, tasteless ✅ (Topic 4).
- Clue 3: hydrogen paani mein lagbhag nahi ghulti ✅ (isi liye paani ke upar ikattha ki jaati hai, Topic 2).
- Clue 4: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O, sirf paani ✅ (CO₂ ya N₂ jaisi gas ye nahi karti).
- Clue 5: jalte hue splinter se **pop** ki aavaaz (sirf teacher demo, Topic 2 aur Topic 4) ✅.
Saare clue hydrogen par khare utarte hain, aur clue 1 akela hi doosri gaason (He, N₂, O₂, CO₂) ko hata deta hai. Jawab: **hydrogen**, sirf ek.

**Example 7: Card No. 2, naya riddle.**
"Main ek gas hoon jo **Bosch step** mein banti hoon (CO + H₂O → CO₂ + H₂). Mujhe dabav (pressure) mein paani mein ghola ja sakta hai taaki hydrogen alag ho jaye. Main chuna-paani ko doodhiya kar deti hoon. Main hawa se bhaari hoon." Kaun?
Bosch step mein do gas milti hain: H₂ aur **CO₂**. "Paani mein ghola jaata hai" wali gas CO₂ hai (hydrogen to lagbhag nahi ghulti). "Lime water doodhiya" CO₂ ka test hai. "Hawa se bhaari": CO₂ ka molar mass 12 + 32 = 44 g, hawa ka lagbhag 29 g ✅ (Chapter 10). Jawab: **CO₂**.

> **Jaanch:** Bosch step ke baad hydrogen aur CO₂ ke mishran ko paani se guzarne par CO₂ ghul jaati hai aur hydrogen bach jaati hai: kyunki hydrogen lagbhag nahi ghulti ✅. 44 > 29 ✅.

## Part D: Equation aur reducing agent

**Example 8: Hydrogen ek reducing agent.**
Fe₂O₃ + 3H₂ → 2Fe + 3H₂O
Atom count: baayein: Fe 2, O 3, H 6. Daayein: Fe 2, H 6, O 3 ✅.
Chapter 7 ke hisaab se: **Fe₂O₃ ne oxygen khoya** to wo **reduce** hua (aur Fe milta hai). **H₂ ne oxygen paya** (H₂O bana) to wo **oxidise** hua. Isliye hydrogen yahan **reducing agent** hai: wo doosre se oxygen le leta hai.
Ye tabhi hota hai jab metal hydrogen se neeche ya thoda upar ho (jaise Cu aur Fe). Bahut reactive metals (jaise Mg) ke oxide ko hydrogen reduce **nahi** kar paati.

> **Jaanch:** CuO + H₂ → Cu + H₂O: Cu 1, O 1, H 2 dono taraf ✅. Paani bana, to hydrogen ne CuO ka oxygen liya ✅.

**Example 9: Ammonia banane mein hydrogen ke kan.**
N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃ (Haber process, iron catalyst, bahut pressure, ulti ho sakne wali reaction: aage ki class mein).
Agar 5 N₂ kan hain to **5 × 3 = 15 H₂** kan lagenge, aur **5 × 2 = 10 NH₃** kan bante hain (ideal roop mein). Atom count: baayein: N 5 × 2 = 10, H 15 × 2 = 30. Daayein: N 10, H 10 × 3 = 30 ✅.
NH₃ ka molar mass 14 + 3 × 1 = **17 g**. Ye ammonia fertiliser banane mein jaati hai (Topic 5).

> **Jaanch:** Ek NH₃ mein 3 H hain aur ek N: valency 3 aur 1 ka sauda, N ki valency 3 ✅.

**Example 10: Hydrogen kitni "bhaari" hai ammonia, paani aur methane mein?**
Mass percent of hydrogen (H = 1, C = 12, N = 14, O = 16, Cl = 35.5):
- CH₄: 4 ÷ 16 × 100 = **25%**
- NH₃: 3 ÷ 17 × 100 = **17.6%**
- H₂O: 2 ÷ 18 × 100 = **11.1%**
- HCl: 1 ÷ 36.5 × 100 = **2.7%**
Sabse zyada hydrogen mass ke hisaab se **methane (CH₄)** mein hai, isi liye natural gas hydrogen banane ka achha source hai.

> **Jaanch:** Percentages ke formula mein H ka mass ÷ kul mass lena tha; CH₄ mein 4 hydrogen = 4 g, kul 16 g: 4 ÷ 16 = 0.25 ✅.

## Part E: Balloon, energy aur bulk ke hisaab

**Example 11: Balloon ka lift.**
Chapter 10 se: gas ki density = molar mass ÷ 22.4 L (STP). Hydrogen: 2 ÷ 22.4 = 0.0893 g per L ≈ **0.09 kg per m³**. Hawa: 1.29 kg per m³, to hawa hydrogen se 1.29 ÷ 0.09 ≈ **14 guna** bhaari. Helium: 4 ÷ 22.4 = 0.1786 ≈ **0.18 kg per m³**.
Lift per m³: hydrogen = 1.29 − 0.09 = **1.20 kg**, helium = 1.29 − 0.18 = **1.11 kg**.
Agar 10 m³ ka ek balloon ho: hydrogen ka lift 10 × 1.20 = 12.0 kg, helium ka 10 × 1.11 = **11.1 kg**. Helium ka lift hydrogen ka 11.1 ÷ 12.0 = **92.5%** hai: sirf 7.5% kam. Isliye weather balloon mein **helium** dalte hain: lift lagbhag utna hi, aur wo jalti nahi. (Balloon mein kabhi hydrogen mat bharo: shop se sirf helium lo, Topic 5.)

> **Jaanch:** 1.20 − 1.11 = 0.09 ✅. 12.0 − 11.1 = 0.9 kg fark, 0.9 ÷ 12.0 = 7.5% ✅.

**Example 12: Energy aur jagah.**
Hydrogen: lagbhag **120 MJ per kg**; petrol: lagbhag **45 MJ per kg** (approximate). Hydrogen kareeb 120 ÷ 45 = **2.7 guna** zyada energy per kg deti hai.
2.5 kg hydrogen ki energy = 2.5 × 120 = **300 MJ**. Itni energy ke liye petrol: 300 ÷ 45 = **6.67 kg**. Yaani mass mein hydrogen bahut halki padti hai.
Par jagah ka kya? 2.5 kg hydrogen = 2.5 × 1000 ÷ 2 = 1250 mol. STP par 1250 × 22.4 = 28000 L = **28 m³** (ek chhote kamre ki jagah!). Isi wajah se hydrogen ko bahut dabaa kar (compress) ya thanda karke (liquid) rakhna padta hai, aur yahin se **storage ki samasya** aati hai (Topic 5).

> **Jaanch:** 28 m³ ya 28000 L: 1250 × 22.4 = 28000 ✅. 6.67 × 45 = 300 ✅.

**Example 13: Kya jal sakti hai?**
Hydrogen hawa mein lagbhag **4% se 75%** (approximate) ke beech jal ya phat sakti hai. Teen mishran: (a) 3% hydrogen, (b) 30%, (c) 80%.
(a) 3% < 4%: jalne ke liye bahut kam (lean). (b) 30%: **range ke andar, khatarnak**. (c) 80% > 75%: bahut zyada (rich), par **surakshit nahi**: jaise hi ye hawa mein milti hai, mishran 75% se 4% ki range se hokar guzarta hai.
Isliye leak hone par bhi sabse zaroori baat: **aag door, hawa ki aavjaahi, band jagah mein kabhi jama na hone do.**

> **Jaanch:** 3 < 4 ✅, 4 ≤ 30 ≤ 75 ✅, 80 > 75 ✅.

## Part F: Chapters ka jod

**Example 14: Isotopes aur heavy water.**
Chapter 5 se: protium (1 proton, 0 neutron), deuterium (1 proton, 1 neutron), tritium (1 proton, 2 neutrons, radioactive, rare). Protium lagbhag 99.98% hai: 50000 hydrogen atoms mein protium **50000 × 0.9998 = 49990**, baaki 10 (deuterium aur tritium).
Heavy water D₂O: molar mass 2 × 2 + 16 = **20 g**, jabki H₂O ka 18 g. 20 ÷ 18 = **1.11 guna**: lagbhag 11% zyada bhaari. D₂O ke ek kan mein neutrons: 2 deuterium ke 1 + 1 = 2, aur oxygen-16 ke 8: kul **10 neutrons** (H₂O mein sirf 8). Heavy water India ke heavy-water reactors mein **moderator** ke roop mein kaam aati hai.

> **Jaanch:** D₂O mein protons: 1 + 1 + 8 = 10 aur neutrons: 1 + 1 + 8 = 10, kul 20 ✅.

## Surakshit rehne ke niyam (hydrogen)

- Hydrogen **bahut jwalansheel** hai aur hawa ya oxygen ke saath mishran **phat** sakta hai. Ise banana, bharna, jalana ya test karna sirf **teacher ke saath school lab mein (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo)** hota hai, thodi matra mein, aag door, hawa ki aavjaahi aur safety screen ke saath.
- **Kabhi bhi ghar par hydrogen mat banao ya jalao.** Ghar par sirf kagaz ke kaam (riddle, sorting, diagram) karo.
- **Balloon mein hydrogen kabhi nahi:** shop se sirf helium. 1937 ka Hindenburg airship hadsa (36 mauten) yaad rakho.
- **Band dibba kabhi garam mat karo.** Cylinder dhoop ya aag se door.
- Sodium, potassium, calcium jaise metals ko paani ya acid ke saath mat milao: ye sirf padhne ke liye hain.
- Koi bhi gas kabhi **chakh kar** ya **sungh kar** mat dekho.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **"Hydrogen bina hawa ya oxygen ke bhi jal jaati hai."** Nahi. Hydrogen **jalti** hai, par jalne ke liye **oxygen** chahiye. Bina oxygen ke wo bas rakhi rehti hai.
2. **"Hydrogen sab metals se banti hai."** Nahi. Sirf wahi metals jo reactivity series mein hydrogen se **upar** hain (Cu, Ag, Au nahi). Aur Na, K jaise bahut tez metals lab mein nahi lete.
3. **"Nitric acid ke saath bhi hydrogen milti hai."** Nahi. Nitric acid alag tarah se kaam karta hai; iska hisaab Class 10 mein hai.
4. **"Hydrogen ka pop test ghar par bhi kar sakte hain."** Nahi. Hydrogen ki taiyari, test aur jalana sirf **teacher demo** hai.
5. **"Hydrogen fuel hai to dhuan nahi chhodti."** Hydrogen jalane par sirf paani deti hai, par agar wo natural gas se bani hai ("grey"), to **banate waqt CO₂ nikalti hai.** Saaf-pan poore safar ka hai.
6. **"Hydrogen ki energy kam hai kyunki wo halki hai."** Per **kg** energy zyada hai (120 vs 45 MJ), par per **m³** bahut kam, isliye storage mushkil hai.
7. **"Hydrogen ek metal hai kyunki group 1 ke upar hai."** Nahi. Wo ek **non-metal gas** hai; sirf suvidha ke liye group 1 ke upar rakhi jaati hai (Chapter 8).
8. **"Zyada hydrogen mishran ho to surakshit."** Nahi. Hawa mein milte hi wo jalne ki range se guzarti hai (Example 13).

## Quick Recap

- Hydrogen: sabse halki, brahmand mein sabse zyada, symbol H, valency 1, H₂ (ek covalent bond). Isotopes: protium, deuterium, tritium. Heavy water D₂O (20 g).
- Lab (teacher demo): Zn, Mg ya Fe + dilute acid; copper nahi deta. Paani ke upar ya hawa ko neeche dhakel kar (ulti jar) ikattha; pop test.
- Aur tareeke: paani ka electrolysis (2 : 1 volume), active metals aur paani (sirf padhna), steam, natural gas aur coke se factory mein, brine ka by-product, green hydrogen.
- Gun: colourless, odourless, density lagbhag 0.09 g per L, boiling point lagbhag −253 °C (20 K), neutral. Jalti hai (2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O), Cl₂ aur N₂ ke saath milti hai, reducing agent hai.
- Upyog: ammonia (fertiliser), vanaspati, rocket fuel, fuel cell, welding. Energy 120 MJ per kg, par bahut bulky.
- Safety: jwalansheel, 4% se 75% hawa mein phat sakti hai, balloon mein helium, band dibba garam nahi.
- Hydrogen jalane par sirf paani; baaki fuels kya chhodte hain, agla chapter batayega.

## Practice Questions

1. Choose the best method of making hydrogen for each situation and state one reason: (a) a steel plant that already has coke and steam; (b) a chlor-alkali plant that splits brine to make chlorine and sodium hydroxide; (c) a school lab that has only copper turnings and dilute hydrochloric acid.

2. A teacher plans a demonstration with dilute sulphuric acid. Which of these metals will give hydrogen: magnesium, gold, potassium, iron? For each one that does not, give the reason.

3. Balance and check atoms: Fe + H₂O (steam) → Fe₃O₄ + H₂. Is iron oxidised or reduced here? Why?

4. Combine C + H₂O → CO + H₂ and CO + H₂O → CO₂ + H₂ into one equation. How many H₂ molecules come from 7 carbon atoms by this two-step route?

5. Riddle: "I am a colourless, odourless gas. I am made at the positive electrode when water is electrolysed. The volume I make is half the volume of the gas at the other electrode. I relight a glowing splinter." Who am I? Explain why only one gas fits.

6. Riddle: "I am a greenish-yellow gas. I am made together with hydrogen and sodium hydroxide when brine is electrolysed. Hydrogen joins with me in sunlight or on heating to give hydrogen chloride. I am heavier than air." Who am I? Write the two equations and give the molar mass check for "heavier than air" (Cl = 35.5).

7. A rocket stage carries 4 kg of hydrogen. (a) How much energy is that (120 MJ per kg)? (b) What mass of petrol would give the same energy (45 MJ per kg)? (c) What volume would 4 kg of hydrogen occupy at STP, in m³?

8. A weather balloon of 25 m³ is filled with helium (density 0.18 kg per m³, air 1.29 kg per m³). Find the lift. What lift would hydrogen give, and why is helium still chosen?

9. In the Haber process N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃, how many H₂ and how many NH₃ particles correspond to 12 N₂ particles (ideal case)? Name the catalyst and state whether the reaction can go both ways.

10. "Semi-heavy" water is HDO (one protium, one deuterium, one oxygen-16). Find its molar mass and the number of neutrons in one molecule. In 20000 hydrogen atoms of ordinary hydrogen, how many are protium (99.98%)?

11. Find the mass percent of hydrogen in methanol, CH₃OH (C = 12, O = 16, H = 1). Is it more or less than in water?

12. Classify each mixture as below, inside or above the flammable range (about 4% to 75% hydrogen in air) and say which is safe to leave near a spark: (a) 6%; (b) 50%; (c) 90%; (d) 0.5%.

13. Liquid oxygen boils at −183 °C and hydrogen at −253 °C. Give both in kelvin, the difference, and say which gas needs more cooling to liquefy.

14. True or false, with a reason: (a) Copper gives hydrogen with dilute hydrochloric acid. (b) In Fe₃O₄ + 4H₂ → 3Fe + 4H₂O hydrogen is oxidised. (c) Ordinary hydrogen (protium) has no neutron. (d) Students may test hydrogen with a lighted splinter at home.

---

### Answers

1. (a) **Water gas**: C + H₂O → CO + H₂, phir Bosch step; coke aur steam pehle se maujood hain. (b) **By-product**: brine ke electrolysis mein hydrogen apne aap mil jaati hai, alag se banane ki zaroorat nahi. (c) Copper hydrogen se neeche hai, dilute HCl se **hydrogen nahi** dega: ye lab is kaam ke liye galat samagri rakhti hai (Zn, Mg ya Fe chahiye). Jaanch: teeno mein alag-alag sawaal (A, B, D) ka jawab alag. ✅

2. **Magnesium** (Mg + H₂SO₄ → MgSO₄ + H₂) aur **iron** (Fe + H₂SO₄ → FeSO₄ + H₂) dete hain. **Gold** hydrogen se bahut neeche hai: koi reaction nahi. **Potassium** bahut zyada reactive hai: acid ya paani ke saath bahut tez aur khatarnak, lab mein is kaam ke liye nahi liya jaata. Jaanch: Mg 1, S 1, O 4, H 2 dono taraf ✅.

3. **3Fe + 4H₂O → Fe₃O₄ + 4H₂**. Atom count: baayein Fe 3, H 8, O 4. Daayein Fe 3, O 4, H 8 ✅. Iron ne oxygen paya, to **oxidise** hua (aur steam ke hydrogen ne oxygen khoya, wo H₂ bana). Jaanch: Chapter 7: oxygen milna oxidation ✅.

4. C + H₂O → CO + H₂ aur CO + H₂O → CO₂ + H₂ ko jodo: **C + 2H₂O → CO₂ + 2H₂** (CO dono taraf kat gaya). 1 C se 2 H₂, to 7 C se 7 × 2 = **14 H₂**. Jaanch: baayein C 1, H 4, O 2; daayein C 1, O 2, H 4 ✅.

5. **Oxygen (O₂)**. Electrolysis mein positive electrode par oxygen aur negative par hydrogen banti hai; volume ka ratio 2 : 1, to oxygen ka volume hydrogen ka aadha. Glowing splinter ko relight karna oxygen ka test hai. Sirf ek gas fit hoti hai kyunki "positive electrode" aur "aadha volume" milkar sirf O₂ ko chunte hain. Jaanch: 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂: 2 volume H₂ ke saath 1 volume O₂ ✅.

6. **Chlorine (Cl₂)**. Brine ka electrolysis: 2NaCl + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + Cl₂ + H₂ (Topic 3). Hydrogen ke saath: H₂ + Cl₂ → 2HCl (Topic 4). Cl₂ ka molar mass 2 × 35.5 = 71 g, hawa ka lagbhag 29 g, to 71 > 29: hawa se bhaari ✅. Hara-peela rang aur brine wala clue milkar sirf chlorine ko chunte hain (hydrogen aur NaOH ke saath wahi banti hai). Jaanch: 2NaCl + 2H₂O mein Na 2, Cl 2, H 4, O 2 dono taraf ✅; H₂ + Cl₂ mein H 2, Cl 2 dono taraf ✅. (Chlorine zehreeli gas hai: ye sirf kaagaz ka riddle hai.)

7. (a) 4 × 120 = **480 MJ**. (b) 480 ÷ 45 = **10.67 kg** petrol (lagbhag 10.7 kg). (c) 4 kg = 4000 ÷ 2 = 2000 mol, 2000 × 22.4 = 44800 L = **44.8 m³**. Isliye rocket mein hydrogen ko liquid bana kar rakha jaata hai. Jaanch: 10.67 × 45 = 480 ✅.

8. Helium ka lift per m³ = 1.29 − 0.18 = 1.11 kg, to 25 × 1.11 = **27.75 kg**. Hydrogen ka lift per m³ = 1.29 − 0.09 = 1.20, to 25 × 1.20 = **30.0 kg**. Fark 30.0 − 27.75 = 2.25 kg (lagbhag 7.5%). Helium isliye chuni jaati hai kyunki wo **jalti nahi**, hydrogen jwalansheel hai. Jaanch: 27.75 ÷ 30.0 = 0.925 ✅.

9. 12 N₂ ke liye 12 × 3 = **36 H₂** kan, aur 12 × 2 = **24 NH₃** kan. Catalyst: **iron**. Reaction **ulti ho sakti hai** (reversible), aage ki class mein. Jaanch: baayein N 24, H 72; daayein N 24, H 24 × 3 = 72 ✅.

10. HDO ka molar mass = 1 + 2 + 16 = **19 g**. Neutrons: protium 0 + deuterium 1 + oxygen-16 8 = **9**. 20000 × 0.9998 = **19996** protium. Jaanch: 19996 + 4 = 20000 ✅.

11. CH₃OH: C 12 + 4 × 1 + O 16 = **32 g**, hydrogen 4. 4 ÷ 32 × 100 = **12.5%**. Paani mein 11.1%, to methanol mein **zyada**. Jaanch: 12 + 4 + 16 = 32 ✅.

12. (a) 6%: **range ke andar** (khatarnak). (b) 50%: **andar** (khatarnak). (c) 90%: **upar**, par leak hote hi hawa mein milkar range se guzarta hai, isliye surakshit nahi. (d) 0.5%: **neeche**, jalne ke liye kam. Spark ke paas rakhna kisi bhi mein surakshit nahi (hydrogen ka leak kabhi band jagah mein jama na ho); sabse kam khatarnak (d), par asli niyam hai aag door aur hawa ki aavjaahi. Jaanch: 4 ≤ 6 ≤ 75, 4 ≤ 50 ≤ 75, 90 > 75, 0.5 < 4 ✅.

13. Oxygen: −183 + 273 = **90 K**. Hydrogen: −253 + 273 = **20 K**. Fark: 90 − 20 = **70 K** (ya 70 °C, kyunki fark dono scale par barabar). Hydrogen ko zyada thanda karna padta hai (20 K tak), isliye uska liquefaction mushkil hai. Jaanch: −183 − (−253) = 70 ✅.

14. (a) **False** ❌. Copper hydrogen se neeche hai, dilute HCl se hydrogen nahi deta. (b) **True** ✅. Hydrogen ne oxygen liya aur H₂O bani, to oxidise hui (Fe₃O₄ reduce hua). Atom count: Fe 3, O 4, H 8 dono taraf. (c) **True** ✅. Protium: 1 proton, 0 neutron. (d) **False** ❌. Hydrogen ka test sirf teacher ke saath, school lab mein (sirf dekhne ke liye, teacher demo).
