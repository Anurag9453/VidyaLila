# Topic 1: Hydrogen: Position, Parichay aur Isotopes

Pichhle chapter "Gas Laws" mein humne gubbaron ki kahani se seekha ki kisi gas ki halkapan uske **molar mass** se tay hota hai: jis gas ka M hawa ke ausat (lagbhag 29) se kam, wo upar uthti hai. ## Part A: Hydrogen ka parichay: atom aur molecule

### Atom: sabse sada

Chapter 5 se yaad karo: atom ki pehchaan uska **atomic number Z** hai, yaani protons ki ginti. Hydrogen ka **Z = 1**: uske atom mein **1 proton** aur **1 electron** hai. Sabse ajeeb baat: aam hydrogen ke nucleus mein **neutron hai hi nahi** (0 neutron). Isliye ye sabse chhota aur sabse halka atom hai: mass number A = 1 + 0 = 1, atomic mass 1 u (Chapter 4).

- **Symbol:** H
- **Electronic configuration:** 1 (sirf K shell mein 1 electron)
- **Valency:** 1. Hydrogen ek electron **kho** sakta hai, ya ek electron **aur le** kar (ya share karke) apni K shell ko duplet (2 electron) se bhar sakta hai. Dono tarah se uski valency 1 hi aati hai.

### Molecule: H₂

Prakriti mein hydrogen akela atom bahut kam tikta hai. Do hydrogen atom apna-apna ek electron **share** karte hain (Chapter 6 ka covalent bond) aur **H₂** banate hain. Har atom ko apni K shell mein **2 electron** dikhte hain (duplet), isliye dono sthir ho jaate hain. Do atoms ke beech **ek shared pair**, yaani **ek single covalent bond** (H–H) hota hai. Isliye hydrogen gas ko "H₂" likhte hain, "H" nahi: wo **diatomic** hai.

```
   Hydrogen ka ek atom (H)            Hydrogen ka molecule (H₂)

         1 electron                       shared pair
            o                              (e e)
        .   .   .                      .--------------.
      .           .                  (  H     :    H  )
     .     (+)     .                  '--------------'
      .   1 proton .
        .   .   .                    H : H   yaani   H–H
                                     (ek single bond)

   Nucleus mein sirf 1 proton,       Do nucleus, beech mein 2 electron
   neutron 0. K shell mein           saajha. Har atom ki K shell
   1 electron (aadhi bhari).         "duplet" se poori.
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Hydrogen ka atom (1 proton, 1 electron, 0 neutron) aur H₂ molecule (do atoms ke beech ek shared pair, yaani ek single covalent bond). Drawing sirf nidarshan hai; asal mein electron nucleus se bahut door ghoomta hai aur nucleus bahut chhota hota hai.

Jaanch: H₂ ka molar mass = 2 × 1 = 2 g per mol, jo Gas Laws wali table (hydrogen ka M = 2) se milta hai ✅

### Hydrogen kitna halka hai?

Hydrogen ka 1 mole (H₂) sirf **2 g** ka hota hai. Ye har gas mein sabse kam hai: helium ka 4 g, nitrogen ka 28 g, hawa ka lagbhag 29 g (Gas Laws, Topic 5). Isi liye **hydrogen sabse halki gas** hai, aur hawa mein sabse tezi se upar uthti hai.

> **Jaanch:** Ek atom mein 1 proton aur 0 neutron ho to mass number 1 hota hai: 1 + 0 = 1 ✅

## Part B: Brahmand aur dharti par hydrogen

Ab Sarthak ke sawaal par aate hain: "Sooraj mein kitni hydrogen hai?" Vaigyanik Sooraj ki roshni ko alag-alag rangon mein todkar (ek tarah ka "spectrum") dekhte hain aur usse pata lagate hain ki wahan kaun sa tatva kitna hai. Mote taur par Sooraj ke **vazan** ka lagbhag **73 percent hydrogen**, lagbhag **25 percent helium** aur baaki lagbhag **2 percent** doosre tatva (oxygen, carbon, iron wagairah) hain. (Ye ankde **lagbhag** hain; alag-alag source mein thode alag mil sakte hain.)

```
   Sooraj ke 100 "hisse" (vazan ke hisaab se, lagbhag)

   H H H H H H H H H H
   H H H H H H H H H H
   H H H H H H H H H H
   H H H H H H H H H H
   H H H H H H H H H H
   H H H H H H H H H H
   H H H H H H H H H H
   H H H E E E E E E E
   E E E E E E E E E E
   E E E E E E E E x x

   H = hydrogen (73 hisse)    E = helium (25 hisse)
   x = baaki sab tatva (2 hisse)
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Sooraj ke vazan ka lagbhag batwara, 100 chhote dibbon mein: hydrogen 73, helium 25, baaki 2 (lagbhag ankde). Sooraj aur doosre sitaron ka bada hissa hydrogen hi hai.

Poore **brahmand** ke liye bhi lagbhag yahi tasveer hai: vazan ke hisaab se lagbhag 75 percent hydrogen, lagbhag 24 percent helium, aur sab kuch milakar 1 percent. Isliye hydrogen **brahmand ka sabse zyada milne wala tatva** kahlata hai, aur sitaron ka mukhya "eendhan" aur "kachcha maal" yehi hai.

### Par dharti par ulta hai

Dharti par hydrogen **akeli (free)** lagbhag nahi milti. Hawa mein H₂ ka hissa bahut kam hai: lagbhag 0.00005 percent (lagbhag ankda). Wajah: H₂ itni halki hai ki wo hawa mein upar uthte-uthte dharti ke bahar antariksh mein nikal jaati hai (Gas Laws ki halki gas wali baat). Isliye dharti par hydrogen zyadatar **doosre tatvon se jud kar (compound mein)** milti hai:

- **Paani (H₂O):** har paani ke anu mein 2 hydrogen atoms. Samudra, nadi, baarish, sab.
- **Jeevit cheezein:** pedon, jaanwaron aur hamare sharir mein (kai compounds mein).
- **Hydrocarbon (fuels):** petrol, diesel, LPG, natural gas, mom (hydrogen + carbon).
- **Acid:** jaise hamare pet ka acid (hydrochloric acid, HCl). Acid ki poori kahani Class 10 mein.

Paani mein hydrogen kitni hai, ek chhota hisaab:

### Problem 1. Paani mein hydrogen ka vazan

180 g paani mein kitne gram hydrogen hota hai? (H = 1, O = 16.)

- M(H₂O) = 2 × 1 + 16 = 18 g per mol.
- Hydrogen ka hissa = 2 ÷ 18 = 0.1111, yaani lagbhag **11.1 percent**.
- 180 g paani mein hydrogen = 180 × 2 ÷ 18 = **20 g**.

Jaanch: 20 g hydrogen + 160 g oxygen = 180 g ✅ (oxygen: 180 × 16 ÷ 18 = 160 g)

Ye wahi 1 : 8 vazan ka anupaat hai jo Chapter 9 mein mila tha: 20 : 160 = 1 : 8 ✅

## Part C: Periodic table mein hydrogen ka ajeeb ghar

Chapter 8 mein dekha tha ki hydrogen table mein **group 1 ke upar, period 1** mein baitha hai, Li, Na, K ke upar. Par kya wo unka "saathi" hai? Chalo dono taraf ki sachchai dekhte hain.

| Baat | Group 1 ke metals (Li, Na, K) jaisa | Hydrogen asal mein |
|---|---|---|
| Baahari electron | 1 | 1 (isliye upar rakha gaya) |
| Valency | 1 | 1 |
| Roop | Chamakdar, thos dhatu | Rangheen, gandhheen gas |
| Type | Metal | **Non-metal** |
| Molecule | Dhatu ka jaal (metallic) | H₂, do atom ka (jaise Cl₂) |

Hydrogen ka 1 baahari electron use group 1 jaisa dikhata hai, par uske gun (gas, non-metal, covalent H₂ banana) halogens (F, Cl) jaise hain, jo ek electron **lekar** duplet ya octet banate hain. Isliye hydrogen "do group ke beech" ka hai. Use group 1 ke upar sirf **suvidha** ke liye rakha gaya hai, aur ye yaad rakhna zaroori hai ki wo **metal nahi** hai (periodic-table-part3.md ka note).

> **Jaanch:** Hydrogen ko group 1 mein dekhkar use metal samajhna galat hai. ❌ Hydrogen ek non-metal gas hai; group 1 mein uski jagah sirf 1 baahari electron ki wajah se hai. ✅

## Part D: Hydrogen ke teen isotopes

Chapter 5 ke Topic 6 mein dekha tha ki ek hi tatva ke atoms mein protons same aur neutrons alag ho sakte hain, ye **isotopes** hain. Wahan hydrogen ke teen isotopes ka chhota table tha. Ab unhe is chapter ke andaaz se dubara dekhte hain:

| Naam | Symbol | Protons | Neutrons | Electrons | Mass number A |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Protium | ¹₁H | 1 | 0 | 1 | 1 |
| Deuterium | ²₁H | 1 | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| Tritium | ³₁H | 1 | 2 | 1 | 3 |

Neutrons = A − Z: 1 − 1 = 0, 2 − 1 = 1, 3 − 1 = 2 ✅

```
   Protium          Deuterium        Tritium
   (A = 1)          (A = 2)          (A = 3)

     (+)            (+)(n)           (+)(n)(n)
   nucleus          nucleus          nucleus
      o                o                o
   1 electron       1 electron       1 electron

   (+) = proton   (n) = neutron   o = electron

   Aam hydrogen     Bhaari           Radioactive,
   (lagbhag         hydrogen         bahut rare
   99.98 percent)
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Hydrogen ke teen isotopes: teeno mein 1 proton aur 1 electron, neutron 0, 1 aur 2. Isliye mass number 1, 2, 3. (Sketch sirf ginti dikhane ke liye hai.)

- **Protium** hi hamari "aam" hydrogen hai: lagbhag **99.98 percent** hydrogen atoms protium hain. 10000 hydrogen atoms mein se lagbhag 9998 protium. Baaki bahut kam (lagbhag 0.02 percent) mein zyadatar deuterium hai.
- **Deuterium** ko kai baar "heavy hydrogen" kehte hain (symbol D bhi).
- **Tritium** **radioactive** hai (kuch tarah ki kirnein chhodta hai) aur prakriti mein bahut hi kam hota hai. Uske upyog Chapter 5 mein aaye the; yahan bas pehchaan.

Teeno ka **chemical behaviour lagbhag same** hai, kyunki chemistry electrons par chalti hai aur teeno mein electron 1 hi hai (Chapter 5 Part C). Neutron sirf **mass** badalta hai.

### Problem 2. Deuterium atom mein kya kya hai

Deuterium (²₁H) ke ek neutral atom mein kitne proton, neutron aur electron hote hain?

- Z = 1, to protons = **1**.
- Neutral atom mein electrons = protons = **1**.
- Neutrons = A − Z = 2 − 1 = **1**.

Jaanch: protons + neutrons = 1 + 1 = 2 = A ✅

### Problem 3. Hydrogen gas ke teen roop (molar mass)

Normal hydrogen H₂ ka molar mass 2 g per mol hai. Agar dono atom deuterium (D, mass 2) hon, to D₂ ka molar mass? Aur ek protium + ek deuterium wale HD ka?

- D₂: 2 × 2 = **4 g per mol**. (Helium ke barabar! Par D₂ hydrogen hi hai, helium nahi; mass ka barabar hona sirf ek sanyog hai.)
- HD: 1 + 2 = **3 g per mol**.

Jaanch: H₂ 2 < HD 3 < D₂ 4 ✅ (har deuterium ek extra neutron jodta hai, yaani 1 u zyada.)

## Part E: Heavy water

Paani H₂O mein agar normal hydrogen (protium) ki jagah **deuterium** ho, to jo paani banta hai use **heavy water**, **D₂O** kehte hain. Dekhne mein wo bilkul normal paani jaisa lagta hai, par thoda bhaari hota hai, kyunki har D ek extra neutron lekar aata hai. (Chemistry same, mass alag, bilkul isotopes ki tarah.)

### Problem 4. H₂O aur D₂O ka molar mass

Dono ka molar mass nikaalo aur tulna karo. (H = 1, D = 2, O = 16.)

- H₂O: 2 × 1 + 16 = **18 g per mol**.
- D₂O: 2 × 2 + 16 = **20 g per mol**.
- Fark = 20 − 18 = **2 g per mol**.
- D₂O zyada bhaari hai: 20 ÷ 18 = 1.111, yaani lagbhag **11 percent** zyada.

Jaanch: 18 × 1.111 = 20.0 ✅

Ye "bahut thoda sa" bhaari fark nuclear reactors mein kaam aata hai: **heavy water** kuch tarah ke nuclear reactors (jaise Bharat ke heavy-water reactors) mein **moderator** ki tarah use hota hai, yaani wo reactor ke andar tezi se chalte kanon (neutrons) ki raftaar ko dheema karne mein madad karta hai. Ye bhi Class 9 ke liye bas ek jhalak hai; reactor ki poori kahani aage ki class mein.

> **Safety:** Heavy water ek nuclear technology ka hissa hai; ise sirf prashikshit log sambhalte hain. Ghar ya school mein isse koi prayog nahi karna. Roz ka paani peene ke liye normal paani (H₂O) hi hai.

## Part F: Chhote hisaab: hydrogen ke atoms ginte hain

### Problem 5. 1 g hydrogen mein atoms

1 g hydrogen (H₂) mein kitne atoms hain? (Avogadro ki sankhya 6.022 × 10²³.)

- M(H₂) = 2 g per mol, to 1 g = 1 ÷ 2 = 0.5 mol **molecules**.
- Har molecule mein 2 atoms: moles of atoms = 0.5 × 2 = **1 mol**.
- Atoms = 1 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **6.022 × 10²³ atoms**.

Jaanch: Seedha: 1 mol H atoms ka vazan 1 g (atomic mass 1), to 1 g mein 1 mol atoms ✅

### Problem 6. 1000 kg Sooraj ke padarth mein

Agar Sooraj ke 1000 kg padarth ko (vazan ke hisaab se 73 : 25 : 2) baanta jaaye, to kitna hydrogen, helium aur baaki?

- Hydrogen = 1000 × 0.73 = **730 kg**.
- Helium = 1000 × 0.25 = **250 kg**.
- Baaki = 1000 × 0.02 = **20 kg**.

Jaanch: 730 + 250 + 20 = 1000 kg ✅

### Problem 7. 10000 hydrogen atoms mein isotopes

10000 hydrogen atoms mein lagbhag kitne protium aur kitne "baaki" (deuterium + tritium) hain?

- Protium = 10000 × 0.9998 = **9998**.
- Baaki = 10000 − 9998 = **2**.

Jaanch: 2 ÷ 10000 = 0.0002 = 0.02 percent ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Hydrogen gas ko H₂ ki jagah sirf "H" likhna**

Gas hydrogen **H₂** hai (do atoms ka molecule). Akela H ek atom hai. Reaction likhte waqt H₂ hi likho.

**Galti 2: Group 1 mein dekhkar hydrogen ko metal maan lena**

Hydrogen ek **non-metal gas** hai. Wo group 1 mein sirf 1 baahari electron ki wajah se suvidha ke liye rakha gaya hai.

**Galti 3: Sochna ki hydrogen dharti par bahut milti hai**

Brahmand mein sabse zyada, par dharti par **akeli (free)** bahut kam hai; zyadatar paani, jeevit cheezon aur fuels mein **jud kar** milti hai.

**Galti 4: Isotopes ko alag tatva samajhna**

Protium, deuterium aur tritium **teeno hydrogen** hi hain, kyunki protons teeno mein 1 hain. Fark sirf neutrons ka hai.

**Galti 5: Heavy water ko "bhaari bojh wala" khatarnak paani samajhna**

Heavy water D₂O normal paani ka hi roop hai jisme deuterium hai, aur wo sirf lagbhag 11 percent bhaari (molar mass 20 against 18). Wo ek khaas technology mein kaam aata hai; use sambhalna prashikshit logon ka kaam hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Hydrogen:** symbol H, Z = 1, 1 proton + 1 electron, aam hydrogen mein 0 neutron; valency 1; sabse halka tatva.
- Gas ka roop **H₂**: do atoms ke beech **ek single covalent bond** (H–H); molar mass 2 g per mol, sabse halki gas.
- **Brahmand ka sabse zyada milne wala tatva:** Sooraj mein vazan ke hisaab se lagbhag 73 percent H, 25 percent He, 2 percent baaki.
- Dharti par hydrogen zyadatar **compounds** mein: paani (H₂O mein lagbhag 11.1 percent vazan), jeevit cheezein, hydrocarbon fuels, acid. Akeli H₂ bahut kam (lagbhag 0.00005 percent hawa mein).
- Periodic table mein hydrogen group 1 ke upar sirf suvidha ke liye hai; wo **non-metal gas** hai, metal nahi.
- **Isotopes:** protium (1p, 0n, lagbhag 99.98 percent), deuterium (1p, 1n), tritium (1p, 2n, radioactive, rare); teeno ki chemistry lagbhag same.
- **Heavy water D₂O:** molar mass 20 against H₂O ka 18; kuch nuclear reactors mein moderator.
- Safety: hydrogen ek aag pakadne wali gas hai; iske sabhi prayog sirf teacher ke saath hote hain, bachche akele kabhi nahi.

## Practice Questions

1. How many protons, neutrons and electrons are there in a neutral tritium atom (³₁H)? What is its mass number?

2. Compare the percentage by mass of hydrogen in ammonia (NH₃) and in methane (CH₄). Which has the larger share? (N = 14, C = 12, H = 1.)

3. Find the molar mass of tritium gas T₂ (each T has mass 3) and of the mixed molecule HT.

4. How many moles of molecules and how many hydrogen atoms are there in 4 g of hydrogen gas (H₂)? (Avogadro number = 6.022 × 10²³.)

5. In 5,000,000 hydrogen atoms, about how many are protium and how many are not (use 99.98 percent for protium)?

6. A sample of 400 kg of cosmic gas is about 75 percent hydrogen, 24 percent helium and 1 percent other elements by mass. Find the mass of each.

7. Compare the mass of 5 mol of D₂O with the mass of 5 mol of H₂O. Which is heavier, and by how much?

8. Hydrogen is placed above group 1 in the periodic table. Give one reason it fits there and two reasons it is not a group 1 metal.

9. Why is hydrogen mostly found in compounds on Earth, although it is the most abundant element in the universe? Name three places where you will find combined hydrogen.

10. Protium, deuterium and tritium behave almost the same in chemical reactions but have different masses. Explain why.

### Answers

1. Tritium: Z = 1, so **1 proton**; a neutral atom has **1 electron**; neutrons = A − Z = 3 − 1 = **2 neutrons**; mass number **A = 3**. Jaanch: 1 + 2 = 3 ✅

2. NH₃: M = 14 + 3 = 17; hydrogen = 3 ÷ 17 = 0.1765, yaani **lagbhag 17.6 percent**. CH₄: M = 12 + 4 = 16; hydrogen = 4 ÷ 16 = 0.25, yaani **25 percent**. **Methane** mein hydrogen ka hissa zyada hai. Jaanch: NH₃ mein nitrogen 14 ÷ 17 = 82.4 percent, 17.6 + 82.4 = 100 ✅; CH₄ mein carbon 12 ÷ 16 = 75 percent, 25 + 75 = 100 ✅

3. T₂ = 2 × 3 = **6 g per mol**; HT = 1 + 3 = **4 g per mol**. Jaanch: har tritium aam hydrogen se 2 u bhaari hai, to H₂ (2) se T₂ 2 + 2 × 2 = 6 ✅

4. M(H₂) = 2, to moles of molecules = 4 ÷ 2 = **2 mol**. Atoms ke moles = 2 × 2 = 4 mol. Atoms = 4 × 6.022 × 10²³ = **2.409 × 10²⁴ atoms** (lagbhag). Jaanch: 4 g hydrogen atoms = 4 mol (atomic mass 1), 4 × 6.022 × 10²³ = 2.409 × 10²⁴ ✅

5. Protium = 5,000,000 × 0.9998 = **4,999,000**. Baaki = 5,000,000 − 4,999,000 = **1000**. Jaanch: 1000 ÷ 5,000,000 = 0.0002 = 0.02 percent ✅

6. Hydrogen = 400 × 0.75 = **300 kg**; helium = 400 × 0.24 = **96 kg**; baaki = 400 × 0.01 = **4 kg**. Jaanch: 300 + 96 + 4 = 400 kg ✅

7. M(H₂O) = 18, M(D₂O) = 20. 5 mol H₂O = 5 × 18 = **90 g**; 5 mol D₂O = 5 × 20 = **100 g**. **D₂O zyada bhaari hai, 10 g zyada** (lagbhag 11 percent zyada). Jaanch: 100 ÷ 90 = 1.111 ✅

8. **Reason it fits:** uske **1 baahari electron** aur **valency 1** group 1 ke metals jaise hain. **Do reasons it is not a metal:** (i) wo **rangheen gas** hai, chamakdar thos dhatu nahi; (ii) wo **non-metal** hai aur Cl₂ ki tarah do atoms ka **covalent H₂ molecule** banata hai (halogens jaisa). Isliye uski jagah sirf **suvidha** ke liye hai.

9. Brahmand mein sitaron ka bada hissa hydrogen hai, par dharti par akeli H₂ itni **halki** hai ki wo hawa mein upar uthkar antariksh mein nikal jaati hai, isliye hawa mein bahut kam bachti hai. Dharti par wo zyadatar **jud kar** milti hai: **paani** (H₂O), **fuels** (petrol, LPG: hydrocarbon) aur **jeevit cheezein** (sharir, ped). Acid mein bhi.

10. Chemical reactions **electrons** par chalti hain, aur teeno isotopes mein **1 proton aur 1 electron** hai, isliye unka chemical behaviour lagbhag same. Alag hai sirf **neutrons ki ginti** (0, 1, 2), jo **mass** badalti hai (mass number 1, 2, 3).
