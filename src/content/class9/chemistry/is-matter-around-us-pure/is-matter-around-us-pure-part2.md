# Topic 2: Solutions: Ghol Aur Uski Taakat

Topic 1 mein hamne dekha ki zyaadatar cheezein **mixture** hoti hain, aur mixture do tarah ke hote hain: **heterogeneous** (jisme alag-alag hisse dikh jaate hain, jaise salad) aur **homogeneous** (jisme har jagah ek jaisa, hisse alag dikhte hi nahi, jaise sea water ya brass). Ab hum homogeneous mixtures ke sabse jaane-pehchaane parivaar se milenge: **solutions**. Aur sirf milenge nahi, yeh bhi naapenge ki kisi solution mein "kitna" ghula hai.

## School ke sick room mein ORS

Lunch ke baad Ritisha ka dost Pranay football khelte-khelte chakkar khaane laga. Uska chehra utar gaya tha. Ritisha use sahara dekar sick room le gayi, jahan school ke Dr. Deol baithte the.

Dr. Deol ne ek ORS ka packet phaada, steel ke glass mein saaf paani liya, aur chammach se hilaane lage. Ritisha ne dekha: safed powder paani mein daalte hi **gayab** ho gaya. Na koi kan dikha, na paani gadla hua. Paani bas pehle jaisa saaf raha.

Ritisha ne poochha, "Sir, powder gaya kahan? Kya wo khatam ho gaya?"

Dr. Deol ne muskuraake kaha, "Khatam nahi hua, bas itna chhota-chhota bikhar gaya ki aankhein use alag dekh nahi paati. Chakhne ki zaroorat nahi, yakeen karo, wo glass mein hi hai. Ek tareeka hai pakdne ka: agar paani sukha do, to powder waapas mil jaayega."

Phir unhone ek zaroori baat batayi: "ORS ka bhi ek **hisaab** hota hai. Powder kam daalo to asar kam. Zyada daal do to bhi theek nahi, kyunki paani mein ek had ke baad powder ghulta hi nahi. Isliye packet par likha hota hai ki **kitne paani mein** kitna powder." Ritisha ne sochha: "Ghulna" kya hota hai, aur "kitna ghula" ye bataane ka koi seedha tareeka hai? Aaj yahi samjhenge.

## Part A: Solution kya hota hai?

Ek glass paani mein ek chammach cheeni daalo aur hilao. Pehle cheeni ke daane dikhte hain, thodi der mein gayab. Paani saaf hai, par chakho to meetha (ghar ki saaf cheeni aur saaf glass ho tabhi chakhna, lab mein ya anjaan cheez ko kabhi nahi). Ye hai **solution**.

Solution ke do hisse hote hain:

- **Solute:** jo ghulta hai (yahan cheeni).
- **Solvent:** jisme ghulta hai (yahan paani).

Aam niyam: jo hissa **zyada** matra mein ho, use solvent maante hain. Chai mein paani, doodh, cheeni, patti sab hain, par hum yahan sirf "cheeni paani mein" jaisi simple jodi se shuru kar rahe hain.

Topic 1 se yaad karo: homogeneous mixture mein har jagah ek jaisa hota hai. Solution isiliye homogeneous hai: glass ke upar se, neeche se, beech se chammach bharke chakho, har jagah **barabar meetha**.

### Ghulna andar se kaisa dikhta hai?

Chapter 1 mein dekha tha ki sab cheezein kano (particles) se bani hain, aur ye kan hamesha hilte-dulte rehte hain. Cheeni ka daana bhi kanon ka ek jattha hai. Paani ke kan chalte-phirte hain, cheeni ke kanon se takrate hain, unhein jatthe se **alag kar dete hain**, aur phir cheeni ke har kan ko apne beech bikher dete hain.

```
   Pehle:                       Baad mein (hilaane ke baad):

   paani ke kan:  o             o   o  S   o   o
   cheeni ka daana: SSS         S  o   o   S   o
                                o   S   o  o  S
        o  o  SSS  o            o  o   S  o   o
        o   SSS  o  o           S   o  o   S  o
        o  o  o  o              o  o   S   o  o
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Dissolving: pehle cheeni ke kan (S) ek jagah jame hue hain, aur paani ke kan (o) unke aas-paas. Baad mein cheeni ke kan paani ke kanon ke beech **har jagah** baraabar phail gaye hain.

Kyunki ye kan bahut hi chhote hote hain, hum unhein nahi dekh paate. Isiliye ghol saaf dikhta hai. (Ye kan "atom" ya "molecule" ho sakte hain, jo hum chapter "Atoms and Molecules" mein seekhenge.)

## Part B: Paani hi solvent nahi hota

Hum "ghol" ka naam sunte hi paani sochte hain, kyunki paani bahut cheezein ghula leta hai, isliye use **common solvent** kehte hain. Par har solution paani ka nahi hota. Aur har solution ka solute solid bhi nahi hota.

| Solution | Solute | Solvent | Dhyan do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Namak ka paani | Namak (solid) | Paani | Sabse aam |
| Iodine tincture (chot par lagane wali dawai) | Iodine (solid) | Alcohol | Iodine paani mein bahut kam ghulta hai, alcohol mein theek se |
| Nail-polish remover | Nail polish ki parat ghulti hai | Acetone jaisa solvent | Paani nail polish nahi hatata |
| Soda | Carbon dioxide (gas) | Paani | Gas bhi ghul sakti hai |
| Hawa | Oxygen aadi (gas) | Nitrogen (gas) | Gas mein gas |
| Brass (peetal) | Zinc | Copper | Topic 1 wala alloy: solid mein solid |

Do sabak milte hain. Pehla: **solute aur solvent solid, liquid ya gas kuch bhi ho sakte hain**. Doosra: koi ek solvent sab kuch nahi ghulata. Cheeni paani mein ghulti hai, par nail polish paani mein nahi. Iodine alcohol mein ghulta hai, par paani mein bahut kam. Isiliye har kaam ke liye sahi solvent chunna padta hai.

Paani ka solvent hona itna khaas kyun hai, ye ek alag, bada sawaal hai. Uski poori kahani aage ki class mein aayegi. Abhi bas itna: "Paani sabse aam solvent hai, par akela nahi."

**Safety:** Nail-polish remover aur iodine tincture ko chakhna ya soongna nahi hai, aur inhein sirf bade ki madad se hi use karo. Ghar mein sirf cheeni ya namak jaise khaane ke solute se activity karo.

## Part C: Solution ke gun

Topic 1 mein dekha tha ki mixture ke hisse apne gun nahi chhodte. Ab ek **true solution** ke ye gun yaad rakho:

1. **Homogeneous:** har jagah ek jaisa.
2. **Kan bahut chhote:** aankh se to kya, aam microscope se bhi alag nahi dikhte.
3. **Stable:** ghol ko chhod do, to solute neeche baith nahi jaata. Ghante, din guzar jaayein, cheeni paani mein hi rehti hai.
4. **Filter paper se paar:** solute ke kan itne chhote hain ki filter paper ke chhedon se paani ke saath nikal jaate hain. Filter karke solute alag nahi hota.
5. **Saaf (transparent):** ghol par rang ho sakta hai (jaise copper sulphate ka neela ghol), par wo saaf hota hai, gadla nahi.

Ek **chhoti activity** (ghar par): ek glass mein namak ghol lo. Chai-chhanni ya kapde se chhaan do. Chhanne ke baad bhi paani namkeen hai. Namak chhanni mein nahi ruka. Ab ek doosre glass mein mitti ghol ke chhaan lo: mitti chhanni par ruk jaayegi. Ye farak aage Topic 3 mein kaam aayega.

## Part D: Ghol ki taakat: Concentration

ORS packet mein bhi hisaab hota hai, aur chai mein bhi. Ek chai "kam meethi" hai, ek "zyada meethi". Ye wahi baat hai: **solute kitna hai, solution ke mukabale**. Is hisaab ko concentration kehte hain. Isse naapne ke teen aam tareeke hain.

### 1. Mass percent (Mass by mass percent)

100 g **solution** mein kitne gram solute hai:

> **Mass percent = (solute ka mass ÷ solution ka mass) × 100**
> **Solution ka mass = solute ka mass + solvent ka mass**

Yahan sabse zaroori baat: neeche **solution** ka mass aata hai, solvent ka nahi.

**Example 1:** 20 g cheeni ko 80 g paani mein ghola gaya. Mass percent kya hai?

- Solution ka mass = 20 g + 80 g = **100 g**
- Mass percent = (20 ÷ 100) × 100 = **20%**

**Jaanch:** 100 g solution mein 20 g cheeni, matlab 80 g paani: 20 + 80 = 100 ✅. Aur 20% ka matlab 100 g mein 20 g, yahi diya hai ✅

**Example 2:** 5 g namak ko 95 g paani mein ghola gaya. Mass percent kya hai?

- Solution = 5 + 95 = **100 g**
- Mass percent = (5 ÷ 100) × 100 = **5%**

**Jaanch:** 5% matlab 100 g mein 5 g, aur 100 − 5 = 95 g paani ✅

**Example 3 (ulta sawaal):** 250 g ke 8% namak ke ghol mein kitna namak aur kitna paani hai?

- Namak = (8 ÷ 100) × 250 = 0.08 × 250 = **20 g**
- Paani = 250 − 20 = **230 g**

**Jaanch:** (20 ÷ 250) × 100 = 0.08 × 100 = 8% ✅. Aur 20 + 230 = 250 ✅

**Example 4 (solvent ka mass galat lene ki galti):** 15 g cheeni 135 g paani mein. Mass percent?

- Solution = 15 + 135 = 150 g
- Mass percent = (15 ÷ 150) × 100 = **10%**

**Jaanch:** 10% of 150 = 0.1 × 150 = 15 ✅. (Agar 135 se divide karte to 11.1% aata, jo galat hai.)

### 2. Volume percent (Volume by volume percent)

Jab solute bhi **liquid** ho (jaise alcohol paani mein), to hum mass ki jagah **volume** (mL) se naapte hain:

> **Volume percent = (solute ka volume ÷ solution ka volume) × 100**

**Example 5:** 40 mL alcohol ko paani mein milakar kul 200 mL ghol banaya gaya. Volume percent?

- (40 ÷ 200) × 100 = 0.2 × 100 = **20%**

**Jaanch:** 20% of 200 mL = 0.2 × 200 = 40 mL ✅

**Example 6:** Ek 500 mL mouthwash bottle par likha hai "15% volume mein alcohol". Bottle mein kitna alcohol hai?

- Alcohol = (15 ÷ 100) × 500 = 0.15 × 500 = **75 mL**

**Jaanch:** (75 ÷ 500) × 100 = 0.15 × 100 = 15% ✅

### 3. Mass-by-volume percent

Ye sabse kaam ka hai jab **solid** solute ko **liquid ke ghol** mein naapna ho, kyunki gram naapna aur mL naapna dono aasaan hain (bhaar tolo, aur ghol ko measuring jar mein naapo):

> **Mass-by-volume percent = (solute ka mass, g mein ÷ solution ka volume, mL mein) × 100**

Matlab 100 mL solution mein kitne gram solute.

**Example 7 (ORS ki kahani):** Maan lo ek ORS packet ka powder 20 g hai, aur use paani mein ghol kar kul 1 litre (1000 mL) ghol banaya gaya. Mass-by-volume percent?

(Ye safe, simple number hain; asli packets par jo likha ho, wahi follow karo.)

- (20 ÷ 1000) × 100 = 0.02 × 100 = **2%**

**Jaanch:** 2% matlab 100 mL mein 2 g. 1000 mL mein 10 guna: 2 × 10 = 20 g ✅

**Example 8:** Ritisha ne usi 2% ghol ka ek 200 mL glass bhara. Us glass mein kitna powder hai?

- (2 ÷ 100) × 200 = 0.02 × 200 = **4 g**

**Jaanch:** (4 ÷ 200) × 100 = 0.02 × 100 = 2% ✅

**Example 9:** Drip mein 0.9% mass-by-volume namak ka ghol (saline) hota hai. 500 mL saline mein kitna namak hai?

- (0.9 ÷ 100) × 500 = 0.009 × 500 = **4.5 g**

**Jaanch:** (4.5 ÷ 500) × 100 = 0.009 × 100 = 0.9% ✅

### Teeno tareeke ek nazar mein

| Tareeka | Kisse kisse ka hisaab | Kab aata hai |
|---|---|---|
| Mass percent | solute ka mass, solution ke mass mein | Sab solid ya mass se naapna ho |
| Volume percent | solute ka volume, solution ke volume mein | Liquid mein liquid |
| Mass-by-volume percent | solute ka mass, solution ke volume mein | Solid solute, liquid ghol |

Teeno mein ek cheez common hai: **neeche hamesha poora solution** (solvent akela nahi).

## Part E: Unsaturated aur saturated solution

Ab ek glass paani lo aur usme cheeni ek-ek chammach daalte jao, har baar hilao.

Pehle kuch chammach to aaram se ghul jaate hain. Phir ek stage aati hai jab hilaane ke baad bhi cheeni **neeche bachne lagti hai**, ghulti hi nahi. Us stage tak:

- Jab tak aur ghul sakta hai, solution **unsaturated** hai.
- Jab us temperature par aur nahi ghulta, solution **saturated** hai.

```
   UNSATURATED                   SATURATED

   o  S   o   o  S              o  S   o  S   o
   S   o  o  S   o              S   o  S  o   S
   o   o  S   o  o              o  S   o   S  o
   S  o   o  o  S               S   o  S   o  S
                                o  S   o  S   o
   aur cheeni ghul sakti hai     ------------------
                                 SSS  SS  SSS  (neeche bacha)
                                 ab aur nahi ghulegi
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Unsaturated solution mein abhi ghulne ki jagah hai. Saturated solution mein paani utna solute le chuka hai jitna wo us temperature par le sakta hai, isliye extra solute (SSS) neeche bacha reh jaata hai. (Particles ko bahut zyada bada banaya gaya hai, taaki dikh sakein.)

Ek baat dhyan se: saturated ka matlab ye nahi ki ghol meetha ya namkeen "bahut zyada" hai. Iska matlab sirf itna ki **us temperature par ye ghol aur nahi le sakta**.

**Activity (ghar par):** ek glass thande paani mein namak ek-ek chammach daalo aur har baar hilao. Gino ki kitne chammach ke baad namak neeche bachne lagta hai. Wahi saturated stage hai.

## Part F: Solubility: kitna ghul sakta hai?

Ek temperature par **100 g solvent** (yahan paani) mein jitne gram solute ghul sakta hai, use us solute ki us temperature par **solubility** kehte hain. Alag-alag cheezon ki solubility alag hoti hai. Lagbhag values, 20 °C par:

| Solute | 100 g paani mein lagbhag kitna ghulta hai (20 °C par) |
|---|---|
| Namak (table salt) | lagbhag 36 g |
| Potassium nitrate (ek safed namak) | lagbhag 32 g |
| Cheeni | lagbhag 200 g |

Dekha? Cheeni namak se bahut zyada ghulti hai. Isiliye "meetha sharbat" ek chhote glass mein bhi bahut meetha ban sakta hai, par namak ke liye wahi mushkil hai.

### Garmi ka asar

**Solid solute:** zyaadatar solids ke liye **paani garam karne par zyada ghulta hai.** Isiliye garam chai mein cheeni thande paani se jaldi aur zyada ghulti hai. Potassium nitrate ka udaharan (lagbhag values, 100 g paani mein):

| Temperature | Potassium nitrate ki solubility (lagbhag) |
|---|---|
| 20 °C | 32 g |
| 40 °C | 64 g |
| 60 °C | 110 g |

Jab temperature badhta hai to paani ke kan zyada tezi se chalte hain aur zyada solute ko apne beech jagah de paate hain.

**Gas solute:** yahan ulta hota hai: **garmi se gas ki solubility ghat jaati hai.** Thanda soda khol ke dekho: bulbule dheere nikalte hain. Wahi soda garam ho jaaye to khulte hi turant fiz-fiz karke gas bahar nikalti hai, aur wo jaldi "flat" ho jaata hai. Garmi ke mausam mein garam talab ke paani mein ghuli oxygen kam hoti hai, isliye machhliyon ko saans lene mein dikkat hoti hai.

Yaani solid ke liye garam paani zyada ghulata hai (solubility badhti hai), aur gas ke liye garam paani kam ghulata hai (solubility ghatti hai). Poora graph aage ki class mein seekhoge.

**Example 10 (solubility se mass percent):** 20 °C par saturated namak ka ghol: 100 g paani mein 36 g namak. Iska mass percent kya hai?

- Solution = 36 + 100 = **136 g**
- Mass percent = (36 ÷ 136) × 100 = 0.2647 × 100 ≈ **26.5%**

**Jaanch:** 26.5% of 136 = 0.265 × 136 = 36.04 ≈ 36 ✅. (Dhyan do: 36% nahi, kyunki solution ka mass 100 g nahi, 136 g hai.)

**Example 11 (aadhe paani mein):** 40 °C par 100 g paani mein 64 g potassium nitrate ghulta hai. 50 g paani mein kitna ghulega?

- 50 g paani, 100 g ka aadha. To solute bhi aadha: 64 ÷ 2 = **32 g**

**Jaanch:** 32 ÷ 50 = 0.64 g per g paani, aur 64 ÷ 100 = 0.64 ✅

**Example 12 (thanda karne par kristal):** 60 °C par 100 g paani mein saturated potassium nitrate ka ghol banaya gaya. Use 20 °C tak thanda kiya gaya. Kitna solute wapas ghul nahi paata aur neeche baith jaata hai?

- 60 °C par ghula hua = 110 g
- 20 °C par ghul sakta hai = 32 g
- Neeche baithne wala = 110 − 32 = **78 g**

**Jaanch:** 32 + 78 = 110 ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Mass percent mein solvent ke mass se divide karte hain"**

Nahi. Neeche **solution** ka mass aata hai (solute + solvent). Example 4 mein 135 se divide karne par 11.1% aata, sahi 10% hai.

**Galti 2: "Saturated ka matlab bahut zyada concentrated"**

Nahi. Saturated ka matlab hai ki **us temperature par** aur solute nahi ghulega. Cheeni ka saturated ghol bahut meetha hoga, par kisi aur cheez ka saturated ghol halka bhi ho sakta hai.

**Galti 3: "Garmi se sab kuch zyada ghulta hai"**

Zyaadatar **solids** ke liye sahi hai, par **gas** ke liye ulta. Garam soda jaldi flat hota hai.

**Galti 4: "Gayab ho gaya, matlab khatam ho gaya"**

Ghulne par solute khatam nahi hota, sirf bikhar jaata hai. Ghol ka paani sukha do, solute waapas mil jaata hai.

**Galti 5: "Ghol hamesha paani ka hota hai aur liquid hota hai"**

Nahi. Iodine tincture mein alcohol solvent hai. Hawa gas mein gas ka ghol hai, aur brass solid mein solid.

**Galti 6: mass percent aur mass-by-volume ko mila dena**

20 g cheeni 80 g paani mein: mass percent 20%. Wahi 20 g cheeni ko 100 mL ghol mein banao to mass-by-volume 20%. Dono ke "100" alag cheezein hain: ek 100 g solution, doosra 100 mL solution. Sawal mein dekho ki g di hain ya mL.

## Quick Recap

- **Solution** do ya zyada cheezon ka homogeneous mixture hai. **Solute** ghulta hai, **solvent** mein.
- Ghulne par solute ke kan solvent ke kanon ke beech bikhar jaate hain, khatam nahi hote.
- Paani sabse aam solvent hai, par akela nahi: iodine tincture (alcohol), nail-polish remover (acetone jaisa), soda (gas paani mein), hawa, brass.
- **True solution ke gun:** homogeneous, kan bahut chhote, stable (neeche nahi baithta), filter paper se paar, saaf.
- **Mass percent** = (solute ka mass ÷ solution ka mass) × 100. **Volume percent** = (solute ka volume ÷ solution ka volume) × 100. **Mass-by-volume percent** = (solute ka mass g mein ÷ solution ka volume mL mein) × 100. Neeche hamesha poora solution.
- **Unsaturated:** aur ghul sakta hai. **Saturated:** us temperature par aur nahi ghulta.
- **Solubility:** 100 g solvent mein ek temperature par adhiktam ghulne wala solute (g mein). Namak lagbhag 36 g, potassium nitrate lagbhag 32 g (20 °C), cheeni lagbhag 200 g.
- Garmi se **solid** ki solubility badhti hai, **gas** ki ghatti hai.
- Ritisha ka ORS: 20 g in 1000 mL = 2% mass-by-volume, 200 mL glass mein 4 g.

## Practice Questions

1. In your own words, explain what is meant by solute, solvent and solution. In a cup of sweet tea, which one is the solute and which is the solvent?

2. Name one solution in which the solvent is not water. Name another in which the solute is a gas.

3. A student stirs 15 g of sugar into 135 g of water. Calculate the mass percent of sugar in the solution.

4. How much salt is present in 400 g of a 6% (by mass) salt solution? How much water?

5. A solution is prepared by mixing 45 mL of alcohol with water to make 300 mL of solution. Find its volume percent.

6. A solution has 8 g of salt dissolved to make 200 mL of solution. Find the mass-by-volume percent.

7. A saline drip is 0.9% mass-by-volume. How much salt is there in 200 mL of it?

8. At 20 °C, 25 g of a salt just dissolves in 50 g of water to make a saturated solution. What is the solubility of this salt per 100 g of water? How much would dissolve in 200 g of water at the same temperature?

9. At 40 °C, 100 g of water can dissolve 64 g of potassium nitrate. A student adds 80 g of it to 100 g of water at 40 °C and stirs well. Is the solution saturated or unsaturated? How much solid remains undissolved?

10. At 20 °C, 100 g of water dissolves about 32 g of potassium nitrate. Calculate the mass percent of this saturated solution (round to one decimal place).

11. A cold soft drink stays fizzy longer than the same drink at room temperature after opening. Explain why.

12. State True or False and correct the false ones:
    (a) A true solution settles down if left undisturbed for a day.
    (b) The particles of a solution can be separated by ordinary filter paper.
    (c) When a solid dissolves in water, it disappears and its mass is lost.
    (d) The solubility of most solids increases when the water is heated.

### Answers

1. **Solution** wo homogeneous mixture hai jo do ya zyada cheezon se banta hai. **Solute** wo hai jo ghulta hai (kam matra mein aam taur par), **solvent** wo hai jisme ghulta hai (zyada matra). Meethi chai mein cheeni solute hai aur paani solvent (chai mein doosri cheezein bhi hain, par cheeni-paani wali jodi ke hisaab se).

2. Solvent paani nahi: **iodine tincture** (solvent alcohol) ya **nail-polish remover** ke saath nail polish. Gas solute: **soda** (carbon dioxide paani mein) ya hawa mein oxygen.

3. Solution = 15 + 135 = 150 g. Mass percent = (15 ÷ 150) × 100 = **10%**.
   Jaanch: 10% of 150 = 15 ✅

4. Namak = (6 ÷ 100) × 400 = **24 g**. Paani = 400 − 24 = **376 g**.
   Jaanch: (24 ÷ 400) × 100 = 6% ✅, aur 24 + 376 = 400 ✅

5. Volume percent = (45 ÷ 300) × 100 = **15%**.
   Jaanch: 15% of 300 = 0.15 × 300 = 45 ✅

6. Mass-by-volume percent = (8 ÷ 200) × 100 = **4%**.
   Jaanch: 4% of 200 mL = 0.04 × 200 = 8 g ✅

7. Namak = (0.9 ÷ 100) × 200 = **1.8 g**.
   Jaanch: (1.8 ÷ 200) × 100 = 0.9% ✅

8. 50 g paani mein 25 g, to 100 g paani mein dugna = **50 g per 100 g paani**. 200 g paani mein 50 × 2 = **100 g**.
   Jaanch: 25 ÷ 50 = 0.5 aur 100 ÷ 200 = 0.5 ✅

9. 40 °C par 100 g paani mein adhiktam 64 g ghulta hai. 80 g daale to ghol **saturated** hai aur 80 − 64 = **16 g** neeche bacha rahega.
   Jaanch: 64 + 16 = 80 ✅

10. Solution = 32 + 100 = 132 g. Mass percent = (32 ÷ 132) × 100 = 24.24... ≈ **24.2%**.
    Jaanch: 24.2% of 132 = 0.242 × 132 = 31.9 ≈ 32 ✅
