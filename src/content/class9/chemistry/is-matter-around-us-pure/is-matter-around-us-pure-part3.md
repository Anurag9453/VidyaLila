# Topic 3: Suspension aur Colloid: Beech Ka Raasta

Topic 2 mein humne **solution** dekha: namak ya cheeni paani mein aise ghul gayi ki dikhna hi band ho gayi. Particles itne chhote ki na filter paper rok paya, na wo neeche baithe, aur ghol saaf ka saaf raha. Par kitchen mein har mixture itna "imaandaar" nahi hota. Mitti ko paani mein daalo to wo thodi der mein neeche baith jaati hai. Doodh ko chhodo to wo na saaf hota hai, na neeche kuch baithta hai. To kya "bilkul ghul gaya" aur "bilkul nahi ghula" ke **beech** bhi koi raasta hai? Chaliye ek subah ki highway se pata karte hain.

## Kohre mein chamakti headlight

Ms. Menezes apne parivaar ke saath subah-subah highway par trip par nikli thi. Peeche ki seat par unka bhanja Atharv baitha tha, jo school mein Class 9 mein tha. Bahar **ghana kohra (fog)** tha. Papa ne gaadi ki headlight on ki, aur Atharv ne ek ajeeb cheez dekhi: headlight ki roshni hawa mein ek **moti, saaf, chamakti hui lakeer** ki tarah dikh rahi thi, jaise koi thos chhad ho. Jabki raat ko saaf mausam mein beam itni nahi dikhti.

"Mausi, ye roshni hawa mein itni saaf kyun dikh rahi hai? Hawa to transparent hoti hai!" Atharv ne poochha.

Ms. Menezes muskuraayi, "Kyunki aaj hawa akeli nahi hai Atharv. Hawa mein **paani ki bahut-bahut chhoti boondein** tair rahi hain. Ye boondein itni chhoti hain ki gir nahi rahi, par itni badi hain ki roshni ko idhar-udhar bikher deti hain. Isi se beam ka raasta hamari aankhon ko dikhta hai." Phir usne hansi mein kaha, "Ghar pahunch kar tum ye khud karke dekhna. Bas ek torch, ek glass paani aur thoda doodh chahiye."

Atharv ko lagne laga ki kohra, doodh aur mitti wala paani, teeno ek hi parivaar ke alag-alag sadasya hain. Aaj hum isi parivaar se milenge.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Suspension** | Aisa heterogeneous mixture jisme bade, aankh se dikhne wale kan paani (ya kisi liquid) mein bas "latke" hote hain aur chhodne par neeche baith jaate hain | Mitti ka paani, paani mein chalk powder |
| **Colloid** | Aisa mixture jisme kan itne chhote ki dikhte nahi aur neeche nahi baithte, par itne bade ki roshni ko bikher dete hain | Doodh, kohra, makkhan, jelly |
| **Dispersed phase** | Colloid mein bikhre hue chhote kan (jo "bikhre" hain) | Doodh mein chhoti chikni boondein |
| **Dispersing medium** | Wo cheez jisme wo kan bikhre hain | Doodh mein paani, kohre mein hawa |
| **Tyndall effect** | Roshni ke raste mein aaye chhote kano se roshni ka bikharna, jisse beam ka raasta dikhne lagta hai | Kohre mein headlight ki beam |
| **Zigzag gati (Brownian motion)** | Colloid ke kanon ka lagatar tedhi-medhi, idhar-udhar ghoomna | Dhoop mein dikhte dhool ke kan |
| **Nanometre (nm)** | Bahut chhoti lambai: 1 nm = 10⁻⁹ m (ek meter ka ek arabwaan hissa) | Kano ka size batane ke liye |

## Part A: Suspension: jab kan bade hon

Ek glass saaf paani lo aur usme ek chamach **chalk powder** (ya aangan ki mitti) daalo, phir hila do. Paani dudhiya ya mailaa ho jaata hai. Ab glass ko ek jagah chhod do. Kuch minute baad dekhoge ki neeche ek **parat (layer)** jam gayi hai aur upar ka paani kaafi saaf ho gaya hai.

Ye hai **suspension**. Is mein chalk ya mitti ke kan itne bade hote hain ki:

- **Aankh se dikh jaate hain** (ya hilaane par lahrate hue dikhte hain).
- **Chhodne par neeche baith jaate hain** (gravity unhe kheench leti hai). Is liye suspension **stable nahi** hota.
- **Filter paper par ruk jaate hain**: filter karne par kan kaagaz par bache rehte hain aur neeche saaf paani niklta hai.
- **Roshni ko rokte hain**: suspension aam taur par **dhundhla ya opaque** hota hai.
- **Heterogeneous** hota hai: upar-neeche ek jaisa nahi, kyunki kan baith gaye.

Rozmarra ke example: barsaat ka mailaa paani, nadi ka paani jo mitti le kar aata hai, paani mein ghula-mila **geru ya chalk**, aur wo "hilao phir piyo" waali **dawai** (syrup) jo bottle ke neeche baith jaati hai.

> **Yaad rakhna:** suspension ka matlab "latka hua". Kan paani mein sirf *latke* hain, ghule nahi hain. Isliye der tak chhodne par wo neeche aa jaate hain.

## Part B: Colloid: kan jo na dikhte hain, na baithte hain

Ab ek glass mein **doodh** lo. Doodh ek saaf paani jaisa nahi lagta, safed aur dhundhla lagta hai. Par chhodne par uske kan neeche nahi baithte. Raat bhar rakh do to bhi nahi (bas upar malai aa sakti hai, ye alag baat hai). Doodh ko filter paper se chhano to wo poora niche chhan jaata hai, kaagaz par kuch nahi bachta. Aankh ya aam magnifying glass se dekho to ek jaisa dikhta hai. Fir wo "dudhiya" kyun hai?

Kyunki doodh mein chikni (fat) ki bahut hi chhoti-chhoti **boondein** paani mein bikhri hain. Ye boondein itni chhoti hain ki:

- **Aankh se alag-alag nahi dikhti** (isliye mixture ek jaisa, yaani homogeneous, lagta hai, par asal mein ye **heterogeneous** mixture hai).
- **Neeche nahi baithti** (itni chhoti ki chhodne par bhi gravity unhe nahi baitha pati, ye aage dekhenge ki zigzag gati unhe tairata rakhti hai).
- **Filter paper se nikal jaati hain.**
- Par itni **badi** hain ki **roshni ko bikher deti hain**. Isi wajah se doodh dhundhla/dudhiya dikhta hai.

Ye hain **colloid**. Colloid solution aur suspension ke **beech ka raasta** hai: solution jitna saaf nahi, suspension jitna kachra bhi nahi.

Colloid ke aur examples: **kohra aur dhuaan** (hawa mein chhote kan), **makkhan**, **mayonnaise**, **jelly**, **syahi (ink)**, **shaving cream ka jhaag**, **khoon (blood)**, aur **dahi**. (Khoon mein badi cells bhi hoti hain, to wo thoda complicated mixture hai. Abhi bas itna: usme colloid jaise hisse hote hain.)

### Kano ka size: teeno mixtures mein

Kan kitne chhote? Iske liye **nanometre (nm)** naam ki ikai chahiye: 1 nm = 10⁻⁹ m. Taaki andaza lage: 1 mm mein **10,00,000 nm** (10⁶ nm) aate hain. Bahut mota-mota size:

| Mixture | Kano ka lagbhag size | Dikhte hain? |
|---|---|---|
| Solution (true solution) | 1 nm ya usse bhi chhota | Nahi |
| Colloid | 1 nm se 1000 nm tak | Nahi (aam aankh se) |
| Suspension | 1000 nm se bade | Haan (ya hilaane par) |

Dhyan do ki ye seema (boundary) kuch had tak "mota-mota" hai: 1000 nm ke aas-paas ke kano ke liye kabhi-kabhi kehna mushkil hota hai ki mixture colloid hai ya suspension. Par seedhi samajh yahi hai ki **size jitna bada, utna suspension ke kareeb**.

```
 TRUE SOLUTION           COLLOID                   SUSPENSION

   .  .   .             o   o      o                OOOO
  .   .  .              o       o                  OOOOOO
   .  .    .               o   o     o              OOOO
  .    .  .             o      o                    OOOOO
                                                   OOOOOO
 bahut chhote            beech ke kan:               bade kan:
 (1 nm ya kam)           1 nm se 1000 nm             1000 nm se bade
 dikhte nahi             dikhte nahi                 dikhte hain,
 baithte nahi            baithte nahi                neeche baith jaate hain
```

## Part C: Tyndall effect: safe activity

Ab Atharv ke ghar ki activity. **Ye ek aasan aur safe activity hai**, bas ek chhota sa niyam yaad rakhna: torch ki roshni kabhi kisi ki aankh mein mat daalna. Ek bade bachche ya bade ki madad lo, aur sirf ek chhoti **LED torch** ka use karo (koi laser nahi).

**Kya chahiye:**
- Teen saaf glass (transparent), har ek mein lagbhag 250 mL paani
- Ek chhoti LED torch
- Thoda doodh (ek chamach), thodi si mitti (ek chutki)
- Ek kamra jise thoda andhera kiya ja sake (parde band kar lo, bijli band karne ki zaroorat nahi, bas kam roshni ho)

**Kaise karein:**
1. Glass 1: sirf **saaf paani** (ya usme ek chutki cheeni ghol do, ye true solution hai).
2. Glass 2: saaf paani mein sirf **3 se 5 boond doodh** daalo aur halka hilao. Itna kam doodh ki paani lagbhag saaf hi lage (bas halka sa dhundhla).
3. Glass 3: paani mein **ek chutki mitti** daal kar hilao (muddy water).
4. Kamre ko halka andhera karo. Torch ko glass ki ek **side** se glass ke andar chamkao, aur beam ko **side se** (torch ki seedh mein nahi) dekho. Teeno glass ke liye yahi karo.

**Kya dikhega:**
- **Glass 1 (saaf paani ya cheeni ka ghol):** beam glass ke andar **dikhai nahi deti**. Roshni seedhi nikal jaati hai. Ye true solution hai.
- **Glass 2 (doodh ke chand boond):** beam glass ke andar ek **saaf chamakti lakeer** ki tarah dikhti hai. Ye **Tyndall effect** hai: colloid ke kan roshni ko bikhera rahe hain.
- **Glass 3 (mitti):** beam bhi dikhti hai (bade kan roshni ko aur bhi bikherte hain, balki zyada), par paani **mailaa aur dhundhla** hai, aur thodi der chhodne par **mitti neeche baith jaati hai**. Isse tum ise colloid se alag pehchan sakte ho.

```
  GLASS 1               GLASS 2                GLASS 3
  saaf paani            paani + 3-5            mitti ka paani
  ya cheeni ghol        boond doodh

  +---------+           +---------+            +---------+
  |         |           |         |            | * *  *  |
  | torch   |           | torch   |            | torch * |
  | ==>     |           | ==>=====|            | ==>==*==|
  |  (beam  |           |  (beam  |            |  (beam  |
  |  nahi   |           |  saaf   |            | dhundhli|
  |  dikhti)|           |  dikhti)|            |  dikhti)|
  +---------+           +---------+            +---------+
  koi lakeer nahi       chamakti lakeer        mailaa paani,
                        (Tyndall effect)       kan baithte hain
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Torch ki beam teen glass mein. Saaf paani/cheeni ke ghol mein beam nahi dikhti, doodh ke chand boond wale paani mein saaf chamakti lakeer dikhti hai (Tyndall effect), aur mitti wale paani mein dhundhli beam dikhti hai jabki kan baithte bhi hain.

**Safety yaad rakhna:** bas torch ka use karo, laser pointer nahi. Torch ko kabhi kisi ke chehre ya aankh par mat daalo. Kuch bhi muh mein mat daalo (activity ke paani ko piyo nahi, glass alag rakho). Mitti wala paani nali mein hi bahao.

### Aisa kyun hota hai?

Roshni jab kisi chhote kan se takrati hai to wo **sab taraf bikhar jaati hai** (scatter hoti hai). Agar kan itne chhote hon ki roshni se takrate hi nahi (jaise true solution ke kan, jinme se roshni seedhi nikal jaati hai, isiliye Topic 2 ka ghol saaf dikhta tha), to beam dikhai nahi deti. Agar kan itne bade hon (colloid ya suspension) ki roshni ko bikher dein, to beam ka raasta aankh ko dikh jaata hai. Isiliye:

- Kohre ya dhuein mein headlight/torch ki beam dikhti hai.
- **Dhool bhare kamre** mein jab dhoop kisi roshandaan se aati hai, to wo ek **chamakti patti** ki tarah dikhti hai (dhool ke kan roshni ko bikherte hain).
- **Ped-patton ke beech** se subah ki dhoop ya mist mein se chhan kar aati kirnein dikhti hain.
- **Cinema hall** mein projector ki beam peeche se aate hue dikhti hai.

> **Tyndall effect = colloid pehchanne ka ek aasan tareeka.** True solution mein ye nahi dikhta, colloid mein dikhta hai. (Suspension mein bhi dikhta hai, par wo to aankh se hi dhundhla aur gadla pata chal jaata hai.)

## Part D: Zigzag gati: colloid baithta kyun nahi?

Agar colloid ke kan paani se bhaari hain, to gravity unhe neeche kyun nahi baitha deti? Ek wajah to ye hai ki kan itne bahut chhote hain. Dusri wajah ye ki wo **chain se baithe hi nahi rehte**. Ek colloid ko ultramicroscope jaise tez zoom wale microscope se dekho to uske kan **lagatar tedhe-medhe, zigzag raste** par idhar-udhar bhaagte dikhte hain.

Ye isiliye hota hai ki medium ke (jaise paani ke) bahut chhote, tezi se ghoomte particles har pal colloid ke kan ko **har taraf se dhakka** dete rehte hain (Chapter 1 mein humne dekha tha ki particles hamesha harkat mein rehte hain). Kabhi ek taraf se zyada dhakka lagta hai, kabhi dusri taraf se, aur kan zigzag chalta hai. Ye gati kano ko neeche baithne se rokti hai. Ise **Brownian motion** (zigzag gati) kehte hain.

```
        start
          o
           \    /\
            \  /  \
             \/    \      /
                     \    /
                      \  /
                       \/
                        \
                         o end
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Ek colloid kan ka tedha-medha zigzag raasta (idea dikhane ke liye). Kan lagatar idhar-udhar dhakke khata hai, isiliye wo neeche nahi baithta.

## Part E: Colloid ke prakaar (sirf examples se)

Colloid mein **bikhre hue kan** (dispersed phase) aur **jisme bikhre hain** (dispersing medium), dono solid, liquid ya gas ho sakte hain. Isi se alag-alag naam bante hain. Naam yaad karne ki zaroorat nahi, bas example se pehchano:

| Bikhre kan (dispersed phase) | Jisme bikhre hain (medium) | Naam | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Liquid ya solid kan | Gas (hawa) | **Aerosol** | Kohra, badal (liquid kan), dhuaan (solid kan), spray |
| Liquid | Liquid | **Emulsion** | Doodh, makkhan, mayonnaise, cream |
| Gas | Liquid ya solid | **Foam (jhaag)** | Shaving cream, whipped cream, sponge |
| Solid | Liquid | **Sol** | Syahi (ink), paint, kuch dawaiyan |
| Liquid | Solid | **Gel** | Jelly, paneer, hair gel |

(Kuch colloids jaise doodh mein "paani aur fat" dono liquid hain, par wo mile nahi, bas ek ke chhote kan doosre mein bikhre hain.)

Ye sirf parichay hai. Naam yaad karne ki jagah is ek baat par dhyan do: **dono "phase" mil kar ek colloid banate hain, aur dono mein kuch na kuch ho sakta hai (solid, liquid ya gas)**.

## Part F: Teeno mixtures ek saath

| Gun (property) | True solution | Colloid | Suspension |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kano ka size | 1 nm ya chhota | 1 nm se 1000 nm | 1000 nm se bada |
| Aankh se dikhte hain? | Nahi | Nahi | Haan (ya hilaane par) |
| Homogeneous ya heterogeneous? | Homogeneous | Lagbhag homogeneous (kan dikhte nahi), par asal mein heterogeneous | Heterogeneous |
| Chhodne par baithte hain? | Nahi | Nahi | Haan |
| Filter paper se nikal jaate hain? | Haan | Haan | Nahi (kaagaz par ruk jaate hain) |
| Tyndall effect | Nahi dikhta | Dikhta hai | Dikhta hai (par mixture dhundhla hota hai) |
| Dikhne mein | Saaf (transparent) | Dudhiya ya thoda dhundhla | Mailaa, dhundhla |
| Example | Namak ka paani, cheeni ka sharbat | Doodh, kohra, makkhan, ink | Mitti ka paani, chalk ka paani |

> **Chhoti chaabi:** teen sawal poocho. (1) Kan dikhte hain? (2) Chhodne par baithte hain? (3) Torch ki beam dikhti hai? Teeno **nahi** to **true solution**. Pehla nahi, dusra nahi, teesra haan to **colloid**. Dikhte hain aur baithte hain to **suspension**.

## Part G: Solved examples

**Example 1 (units badalna):** Ret ke ek kan ka size 0.2 mm hai. Use nm mein badlo.

- 1 mm = 10⁶ nm
- 0.2 mm = 0.2 × 10⁶ nm = **2,00,000 nm** (2 × 10⁵ nm)

**Jaanch:** 2 × 10⁵ nm = 2 × 10⁵ × 10⁻⁹ m = 2 × 10⁻⁴ m = 0.2 mm ✅. 2,00,000 nm 1000 nm se kaafi bada hai, to ye suspension ka kan hai.

**Example 2 (kis mixture ka kan?):** Neeche ke kano ko solution, colloid ya suspension mein baanto: (a) 0.5 nm, (b) 200 nm, (c) 20,000 nm, (d) 1 mm.

- (a) 0.5 nm: 1 nm se chhota, **true solution**.
- (b) 200 nm: 1 nm aur 1000 nm ke beech, **colloid**.
- (c) 20,000 nm: 1000 nm se bahut bada, **suspension**.
- (d) 1 mm = 10⁶ nm: 1000 nm se bahut bada, **suspension**.

**Jaanch:** (b) ke liye 1 < 200 < 1000 ✅. (c) ke liye 20,000 > 1000 ✅.

**Example 3 (size ki tulna):** Ek colloid ka kan 100 nm ka hai aur ek solution ke kan 0.5 nm ke hain. Colloid ka kan kitna guna bada hai? Aur 20,000 nm wale suspension ke kan se colloid kan kitna chhota hai?

- Colloid ÷ solution = 100 ÷ 0.5 = **200 guna** bada.
- Suspension ÷ colloid = 20,000 ÷ 100 = **200 guna** bada (yaani colloid ka kan us suspension ke kan se 200 guna chhota).

**Jaanch:** 0.5 × 200 = 100 ✅. 100 × 200 = 20,000 ✅. Colloid ke kan "beech mein" hain: solution se 200 guna bade aur is suspension se 200 guna chhote.

**Example 4 (doodh kitna pata chalta hai?):** Atharv ne 250 mL paani mein doodh ki 5 boondein daali. Maan lo 1 boond lagbhag 0.05 mL ki hai. Doodh ki volume percent (volume by volume) kitni hui? (Topic 2 ka concentration yaad karo.)

- Doodh ki volume = 5 × 0.05 mL = **0.25 mL**
- Percent = (0.25 ÷ 250) × 100 = **0.1 %** (volume by volume, lagbhag; paani aur doodh ki volume milaane ka farak itna chhota hai ki hum 250 mL hi le rahe hain)

**Jaanch:** 0.1 % of 250 mL = 0.001 × 250 = 0.25 mL ✅. Yaani sirf **0.1 %** doodh hone par bhi beam saaf dikhne lagi: colloid ka Tyndall effect bahut sensitive hota hai.

**Example 5 (kya karein?):** Ek bottle mein safed-sa liquid hai. Torch jalane par beam dikhti hai, neeche kuch nahi baitha hai, aur filter paper par kuch nahi bacha. Ye kaun sa mixture hai?

- Beam dikhi: Tyndall effect, to **solution nahi**.
- Neeche nahi baitha: **suspension nahi**.
- Filter paper se poora nikal gaya: kan chhote hain.
- Jawab: **colloid**.

**Example 6 (kohra aur kis ka mixture?):** Kohre mein dispersed phase aur dispersing medium kya hain, aur ye kis type ka colloid hai?

- Dispersed phase: paani ki chhoti boondein (liquid).
- Dispersing medium: hawa (gas).
- Liquid kan, gas medium: **aerosol**.

**Jaanch:** Part E ki table mein "Liquid ya solid kan, gas medium" aerosol hi hai ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Doodh ek solution hai, kyunki ek jaisa dikhta hai"**

Nahi. Dikhne mein ek jaisa hone se kuch nahi hota. Doodh mein Tyndall effect dikhta hai aur kan roshni ko bikherte hain. Isliye wo **colloid** hai, solution nahi.

**Galti 2: "Colloid ka kan baithta nahi, to wo filter ho jaayega"**

Ulta hai. Colloid filter paper se **nikal jaata hai** (kaagaz par kuch nahi bachta). Filter se to suspension ke kan roke jaate hain. Colloid ke kan alag karne ke liye alag tareeka chahiye (aage Topic 4 mein dekhenge).

**Galti 3: "Tyndall effect sirf colloid mein hota hai"**

Suspension mein bhi roshni bikharti hai. Par suspension aankh se hi mailaa dikhta hai aur kan baithte hain, isliye pehchaan mein wo gadbad nahi karta. Asli baat ye hai ki **true solution mein ye nahi hota**.

**Galti 4: "Colloid aur suspension ek hi cheez hain"**

Nahi. Suspension ke kan bade, dikhte hain aur baithte hain. Colloid ke kan chhote, dikhte nahi aur baithte nahi.

**Galti 5: Kohre ko sirf "hawa" samajhna**

Kohra sirf hawa nahi, hawa mein bikhri **paani ki chhoti boondein** hain. Isiliye wo ek mixture (colloid) hai, aur isiliye beam dikhti hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Suspension:** bade kan (1000 nm se bade), dikhte hain, **neeche baith jaate hain**, **filter paper par ruk jaate hain**, dhundhla, heterogeneous. Example: mitti ka paani, chalk ka paani.
- **Colloid:** beech ke kan (1 nm se 1000 nm), dikhte nahi, **baithte nahi**, **filter paper se nikal jaate hain**, par **roshni ko bikherte hain**. Example: doodh, kohra, makkhan, ink, jelly.
- **True solution:** sabse chhote kan (1 nm ya kam), saaf, stable, roshni nahi bikherti.
- **Tyndall effect:** roshni ka bikharna, jisse beam ka raasta dikhta hai. Safe tareeka: andhere kamre mein LED torch + saaf paani, doodh ke chand boond, aur mitti wala paani. Colloid pehchanne ka aasan tareeka.
- **Zigzag gati (Brownian motion):** medium ke particles ke dhakke se colloid kan lagatar tedhe-medhe chalte hain, isiliye wo baithte nahi.
- Colloid ke prakaar (example se): aerosol (kohra, dhuaan), emulsion (doodh, makkhan), foam (shaving cream), sol (ink, paint), gel (jelly).
- Unit: 1 mm = 10⁶ nm, 1 nm = 10⁻⁹ m.

## Practice Questions

1. Name the three kinds of mixtures you have studied in the last two topics (solution, colloid, suspension) and say which one is stable and which one settles on standing.

2. A student mixes a spoonful of chalk powder in water and another spoonful of sugar in water. (a) Which one will show the beam of a torch more clearly and look cloudy? (b) Which one will leave a residue on filter paper?

3. Why does a torch beam look like a bright streak in a foggy morning but not in a clear evening?

4. Classify each of the following as solution, colloid or suspension, giving one reason: (a) salt dissolved in water, (b) milk, (c) muddy river water, (d) smoke, (e) jelly, (f) sugar syrup.

5. Convert 0.05 mm into nanometres. Is a particle of this size likely to be in a true solution, a colloid or a suspension? (1 mm = 10⁶ nm.)

6. A colloid particle is 50 nm wide and a particle in a true solution is 0.25 nm wide. How many times larger is the colloid particle?

7. A student adds 4 drops of milk (1 drop = 0.05 mL) to 200 mL of water. Find the volume percent of milk in the mixture (approximately).

8. Explain in two or three lines why colloid particles do not settle at the bottom.

9. Give the dispersed phase, the dispersing medium and the type (aerosol, emulsion, foam, sol or gel) for: (a) fog, (b) shaving cream, (c) butter.

10. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) A colloid can be separated by an ordinary filter paper.
    (b) A true solution scatters light strongly.
    (c) A suspension settles on standing.
    (d) The particles of a colloid are bigger than those of a suspension.

### Answers

1. Solution, colloid aur suspension. **Solution aur colloid stable** hain (kan chhodne par neeche nahi baithte). **Suspension** chhodne par **baith jaata hai**.

2. (a) **Chalk powder wala paani**: usme kan bade hain, wo dhundhla dikhta hai aur beam bikherta hai. (b) **Chalk powder** filter paper par bacha rahega, kyunki wo suspension hai. Cheeni ka paani true solution hai, uska kuch residue nahi bachta.

3. Kohre mein hawa mein **paani ki bahut chhoti boondein** bikhri hain. Ye roshni ko bikherti hain (Tyndall effect), isliye beam ka raasta dikhta hai. Saaf shaam ko hawa mein aise kan nahi hote, to roshni seedhi nikal jaati hai aur beam dikhai nahi deti.

4. (a) **Solution** (kan bahut chhote, saaf, beam nahi dikhti). (b) **Colloid** (kan chhote par roshni bikherte hain, baithte nahi). (c) **Suspension** (mitti ke kan bade, baithte hain, mailaa paani). (d) **Colloid** (hawa mein chhote solid kan, ek aerosol). (e) **Colloid** (ek gel). (f) **Solution** (cheeni ghul gayi hai, saaf).

5. 0.05 mm = 0.05 × 10⁶ nm = **50,000 nm** (5 × 10⁴ nm).
   Jaanch: 50,000 nm = 50,000 × 10⁻⁹ m = 5 × 10⁻⁵ m = 0.05 mm ✅
   50,000 nm, 1000 nm se bahut bada hai, to ye **suspension** ka kan hone ki sambhavna zyada hai.

6. 50 ÷ 0.25 = **200 guna** bada.
   Jaanch: 0.25 × 200 = 50 ✅

7. 4 boondein = 4 × 0.05 mL = 0.2 mL. Percent = (0.2 ÷ 200) × 100 = **0.1 %** (volume by volume, lagbhag).
   Jaanch: 0.1 % of 200 mL = 0.001 × 200 = 0.2 mL ✅

8. Colloid ke kan bahut chhote hote hain, aur medium ke particles unhe har taraf se lagatar dhakka dete rehte hain (zigzag gati, Brownian motion). Isliye gravity unhe neeche nahi baitha paati.

9. (a) Fog: dispersed phase **liquid (paani ki boondein)**, medium **gas (hawa)**, type **aerosol**. (b) Shaving cream: dispersed phase **gas**, medium **liquid**, type **foam**. (c) Butter: dispersed phase **liquid (paani ki boondein)**, medium **fat (chikni)**, type **emulsion** (kuch log ise gel bhi kehte hain; emulsion likhna theek hai).

10. (a) **False** ❌. Colloid ke kan filter paper se nikal jaate hain, isliye ordinary filter paper unhe alag nahi kar sakta. (b) **False** ❌. True solution ke kan bahut chhote hain, wo roshni ko nahi bikherti, beam nahi dikhti. (c) **True** ✅. Suspension ke bade kan neeche baith jaate hain. (d) **False** ❌. Suspension ke kan colloid ke kano se bade hote hain.
