# Topic 4: Mixtures Ko Alag Karna 1: Aasaan Tareeke

Pichhle topics mein humne dekha ki hamare aas-paas ki zyaadatar cheezein **mixture** hain: solution, suspension aur colloid. Topic 3 ke aakhir mein ek baat nikli thi: suspension ke particles bade hote hain, isliye wo **neeche baith jaate hain** aur **filter paper mein ruk jaate hain**, jabki solution ke particles filter paper se nikal jaate hain. Yaani mixture ke hisson mein koi na koi **fark** hota hai. Ab sawal ye hai: agar hume mixture ke hisse **alag-alag** chahiye hon, to us fark ka faayda kaise uthayein? Aaj ke topic mein hum sabse aasaan tareeke seekhenge.

---

## Khet mein gehun ki safai

Holi ke baad Ishani apne Chopra mama (Mr. Chopra) ke gaon gayi thi. Unke khet mein gehun ki kataai ho chuki thi. Ek badi si bori mein gehun ke daane, thodi si bhusi (husk), mitti ke dhele aur kuch chhote kankad mile hue the. Ishani ne bori mein haath daala aur boli, "Mama, itna sab kuch to ek saath mila hua hai! Is gehun ka aata kaise banega? Kya aap ek-ek kankad haath se chunenge?"

Mr. Chopra hanse. Unhone ek mazdoor ko bulaya. Wo soop (chhaaj) mein gehun lekar kuch oonchaai se dheere-dheere girane laga. Hawa chal rahi thi. Bhusi halki thi, to wo hawa mein udkar thodi door ja giri. Gehun ke daane bhaari the, to seedhe neeche dher mein gire. Kankad aur bhi bhaari the, to wo bhi neeche gire, par daano se alag jagah par.

"Dekha Ishani? Humne ek bhi cheez ko chhua nahi, par bhusi, gehun aur kankad teeno alag ho gaye. Sab ka raaz ek hi hai: **inke beech koi na koi property alag hai**. Yahan wo property thi **vajan (halka ya bhaari)**. Mixture ko alag karne ka sabse pehla niyam hai: pehle dhundho ki hisson mein **kya alag hai**, phir uske hisaab se tareeka chuno."

Ishani ne poochha, "To kya har mixture ke liye ek hi tareeka kaam karta hai?" "Nahi," Mr. Chopra bole, "isiliye aaj hum kai tareeke dekhenge: aasaan se lekar thoda dimaag lagane wale tak."

## Part A: Pehle sochna: "kaun si property alag hai?"

Koi bhi mixture alag karne se pehle ek sawal poochho: **mixture ke hisson mein kaun si property alag hai?** Property ho sakti hai: **aakaar (size)**, **vajan**, **magnet se lagaav**, **paani mein ghulna ya na ghulna**, **garmi se seedha gas ban jaana**, aur bhi. Jis property mein fark milega, wahi tareeka hoga.

Samajhne ke liye ek kaam ka example: **aloo ke chips ke packet** mein chips ke saath chhote tukde (churra) bhi hote hain. Aap unhe alag karna chahte ho. Fark kya hai? **Aakaar.** Bade chips ko pakdo, hila kar chhote tukde jhad jaate hain. Yaani aakaar ka fark, aur tareeka: chhanna.

Ab ek ek karke tareeke dekhte hain.

---

## Part B: Haath se aur hawa se (Handpicking aur Winnowing)

### 1. Handpicking

Jab mixture mein alag karne wale tukde **bade aur saaf dikhte** hon aur matra **kam** ho, to haath se chun lena sabse aasaan tareeka hai. Daal ya chawal saaf karte waqt kankad, chhote patthar ya kaale daane **haath se chun** lete hain. Property: **dikhne wala fark** (rang, aakaar).

Par jab matra bahut zyada ho (jaise poori bori ya poora khet), to haath se chunna thakaane wala aur dheema hai. Wahan hum doosre tareeke ka sahara lete hain.

### 2. Winnowing (ooraana)

Mr. Chopra ke khet mein jo hua, wahi **winnowing** hai. Gehun, bhusi aur kankad mein **vajan ka fark** hai: bhusi sabse halki, daana bhaari, kankad aur bhi bhaari. Jab ye mixture hawa mein girta hai, to **halki cheez zyada door udti hai** aur **bhaari cheez seedhe neeche girti hai**. Isliye ye alag-alag dher mein gir jaate hain.

Isi tareeke se dhan (paddy) ya gehun ki phasal kaatne ke baad **bhusi** daane se alag ki jaati hai. Aajkal ye kaam bijli ke pankhe ya machine se bhi hota hai, par idea wahi hai: **hawa halke ko door le jaati hai**.

**Ghar par try karo (safe):** ek katori mein thode **chawal** aur thode **sookhe patton ke chure ya chhote kagaz ke tukde** milao. Ek bade bartan ke upar khade hokar (khule mein ya khidki ke paas, jahan halki hawa ho) dheere-dheere girao. Kagaz ke tukde idhar-udhar ud jaayenge, chawal seedhe girenge.

## Part C: Chhalni se (Sieving)

**Sieving** mein jaali (chhalni) ke chhote chhedon se **chhote kan niklte hain aur bade kan upar rukte hain**. Property: **aakaar ka fark**.

- Ghar par **aata chhanna**: aate se bhusi ke bade tukde ya gaanth ke tukde chhanni par rukte hain, mahin aata neeche gir jaata hai.
- **Mason ki ret ki chhalni**: bade kankad upar ruk jaate hain, mahin ret neeche. Isse makaan banane ke liye mahin ret milti hai.
- **Chai chhanni**: chai ki patti chhedon se nahi nikalti, chai nikal jaati hai (ye sieving se milta-julta, par asal mein filtration ka example hai).

Dhyan do: chhalni ka chhed **kitna bada hai** ye tay karta hai ki kaun nikalega. Isi wajah se alag-alag kaam ke liye alag chhalni hoti hai (mota aur mahin).

---

## Part D: Magnet se (Magnetic separation)

Agar mixture ka ek hissa **magnet se chipakta hai** aur doosra nahi, to sab kuch bahut aasaan ho jaata hai. **Loha, steel aur nickel** magnet se chipakte hain; **ret, namak, plastic** nahi chipakte.

**Ghar par try karo:** ek plate par thodi si ret ya aata lo, usme kuch **chhote loha ke keel ya paper-clip** (steel ki) milao. Ab ek magnet ko kagaz ya polythene ke upar rakhkar ghumao. Loha chipak kar upar aa jaayega, ret pichhe rah jaayegi. (Magnet ko seedhe mixture mein na daalo, taaki saaf rahe.)

**Kahan use hota hai?** Kabaadi ke yard mein bade **electromagnet** wali crane loha aur steel alag karti hai (aap Physics ke chapter mein dekh chuke ho). Khaan se nikle **loha ke ore** ko bekaar mitti se alag karne mein bhi kuch jagah magnet use hota hai.

## Part E: Neeche baithna aur chhanna (Sedimentation, Decantation, Filtration)

Ab wo mixtures jinme **liquid** hai. Topic 3 mein dekha tha ki suspension mein bade kan hote hain jo kuch der baad **neeche baith jaate hain**. Yahin se do tareeke nikalte hain.

### 3. Sedimentation aur Decantation

**Activity (safe):** ek glass mein paani lo aur usme ek chammach **mitti** daal kar hila do. Paani muddy ho jaayega. Ab glass ko hilaye bina **kuch der** (ek-do ghante) rakh do. Dekhoge: **bhaari kan neeche baith gaye**, upar ka paani lagbhag saaf ho gaya. Ye **sedimentation** hai. Neeche jami mitti ko **sediment** kehte hain.

Ab dheere se glass ko tirchha karke upar ka saaf paani **doosre bartan mein ulat do**, bina neeche ki mitti ko hilaye. Ye **decantation** hai. Wohi tareeka gaon mein bhi dikhta hai: **dhobi ya kisaan** paani ko kuch der rukne dete hain, phir upar ka saaf paani nikaal lete hain. Daal pakate waqt bhi kabhi-kabhi daal ke dhone ke baad upar ka paani ulat dete hain.

Par sedimentation dheema hai. Isliye ek tez tareeka chahiye: filtration. (Aur **bahut mahin kan**, jaise Topic 3 ke colloid ke, na neeche baithte hain na filter paper par rukte hain. Unke liye Part F ka centrifugation kaam aata hai.)

### 4. Filtration

**Filtration** mein mixture ko **filter paper** (ya mahin kapde, jaali) se guzaara jaata hai. Paper mein bahut chhote chhed hote hain. **Liquid aur uske ghule hue chhote kan** chhedon se nikal jaate hain, par **bade kan upar ruk jaate hain**. Property: **kanon ka aakaar**.

- Jo cheez filter par rukti hai, use **residue** kehte hain.
- Jo liquid neeche aata hai, use **filtrate** kehte hain.

```
          mitti wala paani
              (mixture)
             \           /
              \  ~~~~~  /      <- funnel
               \ ~~~~~ /
          ──────\─────/──────
                 \ v /         <- filter paper (cone)
                  \ /          <- residue (mitti) paper par
                   V
                   │
                   │
             ┌─────┴─────┐
             │           │
             │  . . . .  │     <- filtrate (saaf paani)
             └───────────┘
              beaker / glass
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Filtration: funnel mein rakhe filter paper par mitti (residue) ruk jaati hai, aur saaf paani (filtrate) neeche glass mein jama hota hai.

**Ghar par try karo:** ek plastic ki chhoti bottle ko neeche se kaato (bade bachhe ya bade log ki madad se), ulta karke funnel banao, usme **coffee filter paper ya saaf cotton ka kapda** laga do. Ab muddy paani dheere se daalo. Neeche ke glass mein kaafi saaf paani aayega. Par dhyan: ye paani **peene ke liye** safe nahi hai, kyunki isme dikhne wali nahi, par ghuli hui ya jeevanu wali ashuddhiyan reh sakti hain. Peene ka paani saaf karne ke aur tareeke aage ki class mein aate hain.

**Dhyan rakho:** filtration sirf wahi alag karta hai jo **filter paper ke chhedon se bada** ho. Namak ya cheeni paani mein ghul jaate hain (solution, Topic 2). Unke particles chhedon se nikal jaate hain, isliye **namak ke paani ko filter karne se namak alag nahi hota**. Wahan hume doosra tareeka chahiye, jo hum Part H mein dekhenge.

## Part F: Tez ghumana (Centrifugation)

**Dahi ya doodh se makkhan** nikalna ek purana desi tareeka hai. Jab hum **mathani** se dahi ko tez-tez ghumate hain, to **halka makkhan upar aa jaata hai** aur chhaachh neeche reh jaati hai. Aaj ke dairy mein ye kaam **centrifuge** (cream separator) machine karti hai.

**Centrifugation** ka idea: jab koi bartan **bahut tezi se ghoomta hai**, to uske andar rakhe liquid ke **bhaari hisse bartan ki deewar ki taraf (bahar ki or) phenke jaate hain** aur **halke hisse beech mein ya upar reh jaate hain**. Dhyan do: Sedimentation mein bhaari kan dheere-dheere neeche baithte the. Centrifuge mein wahi kaam **bahut tezi se** hota hai.

```
      Ghoomne se pehle         Tezi se ghoomne ke baad

       ┌───────────┐              ┌───────────┐
       │ o . o .   │              │ halka     │
       │ . o  . o  │              │ liquid    │
       │ o . o .   │     ───▶     │───────────│
       │ . o  . o  │              │ o o o o   │
       │ (mixed)   │              │ o o o o   │
       └───────────┘              └───────────┘
        tube (mixed)                bhaari kan neeche
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Centrifugation: tez ghumaane par bhaari kan tube ke tale (bahar ki taraf) jama ho jaate hain aur halka liquid upar rehta hai. Mixture ke ghoomne se hi alag ho jaata hai.

**Kahan use hota hai?**
- **Dairy** mein doodh se **malai (cream)** alag karne ke liye.
- **Washing machine ke spinner** mein: kapde ke geele hone par drum tezi se ghoomta hai, kapde ke andar ka **paani chhedon se bahar phenk diya jaata hai** aur kapde kaafi sookhe nikalte hain.
- **Hospital ya pathology lab** mein khoon ki jaanch ke liye: khoon ko centrifuge mein ghumane par **khoon ke bhaari kan (cells) neeche** aur **halka peela liquid (plasma) upar** aa jaata hai.

Matlab: jab sedimentation bahut dheema ho ya kan bahut chhote hon, to **tez ghumana** madad karta hai.

## Part G: Do liquid jo aapas mein nahi milte (Separating funnel)

Agar tel aur paani ko ek bottle mein milakar hilao, to thodi der ke liye dono mil jaate hain, par kuch der baad **tel upar aur paani neeche** alag ho jaate hain. Kyunki dono **aapas mein nahi ghulte (immiscible)** aur **tel halka hai** (paani se kam ghana). Is baat se ek tareeka nikalta hai.

**Separating funnel** ek naashpati ke aakaar ka glass funnel hai jisme **neeche ek tap (stopcock)** laga hota hai. Mixture ko funnel mein daalkar rakh dete hain. Thodi der mein do alag layer ban jaati hain: **neeche bhaari liquid (paani)** aur **upar halka liquid (tel)**. Ab tap ko dheere se kholte hain: **neeche wali layer nikalti hai**. Jaise hi layer ke beech ki line tap tak pahunche, tap band kar dete hain. Baaki liquid funnel mein rah jaata hai, jise doosre bartan mein nikaal sakte hain.

```
          stopper
            ┌──┐
         ┌──┘  └──┐
        ╱          ╲
       │ halka tel  │   <- upar ki layer
       │ ~~~~~~~~~~ │   <- beech ki line
       │   paani    │   <- neeche ki layer
       │  (bhaari)  │
        ╲          ╱
         ╲        ╱
          ╲      ╱
           ╲    ╱
            │▒▒│        <- stopcock (tap)
            │  │
             ▼
          neeche wala bartan
```

> **Figure 4.3** – Separating funnel: tel (halka) upar aur paani (bhaari) neeche. Tap kholne par neeche ki layer pehle nikalti hai; layer ki line tap tak aate hi tap band kar dete hain.

Ye tareeka sirf tab chalta hai jab dono liquid **aapas mein na milte hon** aur unka ghanapan alag ho. Jaise: **tel aur paani**, **mitti ka tel aur paani**. Jo liquid aapas mein mil jaate hain (jaise paani aur alcohol), unke liye separating funnel kaam nahi karta, unke liye aage ek aur tareeka aayega.

**Ghar par try karo (bina funnel ke):** ek chhoti bottle mein paani aur thoda sa tel daalo, thodi der rakho. Dono layer saaf dikhengi. Upar ke tel ko **chamach ya dropper** se dheere se nikaal sakte ho. Separating funnel isi kaam ko saaf aur zyada sahi tareeke se karta hai.

## Part H: Ghule hue namak ko paani se alag karna (Evaporation)

Ab wapas wo sawal: namak ka paani filter karne se namak alag nahi hota. To kya karein? Yahan **evaporation** kaam aata hai. Chapter 1 ke Topic 4 mein dekha tha ki **paani surface se, har temperature par, dheere-dheere bhaap ban kar ud jaata hai.** Par namak (ek solid) bhaap nahi banta. Isliye jab paani ud jaata hai, **namak plate par reh jaata hai**.

**Ghar par try karo (safe, aag ke bina):** ek plate mein thoda namak ghule hue paani ka solution lo (ek chammach namak, ek glass paani). Plate ko dhoop mein ek-do din rakh do. Paani dheere-dheere ud jaayega aur plate par **safed namak ki parat** bachegi. (Namak chakhna nahi, ye bas observation ke liye hai.)

Yahi tareeka **namak banane ke tatton (salt pans)** mein bhi hota hai: samudr ka paani bade tatton mein bhar diya jaata hai, dhoop se paani ud jaata hai aur namak bachta hai.

**Par evaporation ki kamiyaan:**
- Isme **paani ud kar hawa mein chala jaata hai**, hum use wapas nahi pa sakte. Yaani agar hume paani bhi chahiye, to ye tareeka kaam ka nahi.
- Jo cheez **garmi se kharab ho jaati hai** (jaise kuch cheeni ke solution), unke liye ye tareeka theek nahi.
- Namak ke **saaf, bade, sundar daane** (crystal) is tareeke se nahi milte.

In kamiyon ka ilaaj ek aur tareeka hai, jo agle topic mein milega.

## Part I: Seedha gas ban jaane wala hissa (Sublimation)

Chapter 1 ke Topic 3 mein humne dekha tha ki kuch solid, jaise **kapoor, naphthalene aur ammonium chloride**, garmi milne par **bina pighle seedha gas** ban jaate hain. Ise **sublimation** kehte hain. Thanda hone par wo gas wapas seedha solid ban jaati hai.

Agar mixture mein ek cheez **sublimate** hoti ho aur doosri **nahi**, to is property se unhe alag kar sakte hain. Jaise: **ammonium chloride + namak** ya **kapoor + ret**.

**Ye ek school lab mein teacher ki nigrani mein dikhaya jaane wala demonstration hai. Ghar par kabhi mat karo, kyunki isme garmi (heating) lagti hai aur ammonium chloride ka dhuaan saans mein nahi jaana chahiye; teacher ise hawadaar jagah mein karte hain.**

Tareeka (sirf samajhne ke liye): ek **china dish** mein mixture lo, uske upar ek **ulta funnel** rakho (funnel ki nali ka muh cotton ki goli se band ho), aur dish ko **dheere se** garm karo. Ammonium chloride **gas** ban kar upar uthta hai aur thande funnel ki deewar par lagkar wapas **safed solid** ban jaata hai. Namak (ya ret) dish mein hi rah jaata hai.

## Part K: Solved examples

**Example 1 (kisan ki bori):** Mr. Chopra ki bori mein kul 40 kg maal hai. Winnowing aur chhanne ke baad pata chala ki usme 2 kg kankad aur 1 kg bhusi thi. Saaf gehun kitna mila, aur ashuddhiyaan kitne percent thi?

- Ashuddhiyaan kul = 2 kg + 1 kg = **3 kg**
- Saaf gehun = 40 kg − 3 kg = **37 kg**
- Ashuddhiyon ka percent = (3 ÷ 40) × 100 = **7.5%**

**Jaanch:** 37 + 3 = 40 ✅; 3 ÷ 40 = 0.075, yaani 7.5% ✅

**Example 2 (namak ka solution):** Ek solution 500 g hai aur usme **20% namak (mass ke hisaab se)** hai. Poora paani evaporate karne par kitna namak bachega, aur kitna paani ud jaayega?

- Namak = 20% of 500 g = (20 ÷ 100) × 500 = **100 g**
- Paani = 500 g − 100 g = **400 g** (ye ud jaayega)

**Jaanch:** 100 + 400 = 500 ✅. Mass percent: 100 ÷ 500 × 100 = 20% ✅ (Topic 2 ka concentration wala formula)

**Example 3 (centrifuge se cream):** Ek dairy mein 5 litre doodh mein lagbhag 6% (volume ke hisaab se) cream hai. Centrifuge se kitni cream alag hogi?

- Cream = 6% of 5 L = (6 ÷ 100) × 5 L = **0.3 L** (yaani 300 mL)

**Jaanch:** 0.3 L ÷ 5 L = 0.06, yaani 6% ✅

**Example 4 (separating funnel):** 300 mL mixture mein tel aur paani hain. Tel 20% (volume ke hisaab se) hai. Funnel mein alag karne par neeche kitna paani aur upar kitna tel milega?

- Tel = 20% of 300 mL = **60 mL** (upar ki layer)
- Paani = 300 mL − 60 mL = **240 mL** (neeche ki layer, pehle nikalegi)

**Jaanch:** 60 + 240 = 300 ✅. Tap kholne par pehle **paani** nikalta hai kyunki wo neeche hai.

**Example 5 (sublimation):** 50 g ke mixture mein ammonium chloride aur namak hain. Dheere se garm karke sublimation karne par dish mein 18 g namak bacha. Mixture mein kitna ammonium chloride tha, aur kitne percent?

- Ammonium chloride = 50 g − 18 g = **32 g**
- Percent = (32 ÷ 50) × 100 = **64%**

**Jaanch:** 32 + 18 = 50 ✅; 32 ÷ 50 = 0.64 ✅

**Example 6 (kaun sa tareeka? reasoning):** Ek mixture mein **ret, loha ke keel aur namak** hain. Aap use kaise alag karoge, kis order mein?

- **Step 1: Magnet** se loha ke keel nikaalo (ye sabse pehle, kyunki ye bina paani ke ho jaata hai).
- **Step 2: Paani daalo** aur hilao. Namak ghul jaayega, ret nahi ghulegi.
- **Step 3: Filtration.** Ret filter paper par rukegi (residue), namak ka paani neeche aayega (filtrate).
- **Step 4: Evaporation.** Filtrate ko dhoop mein rakho. Paani ud jaayega, namak bachega.

Ye wahi idea hai jo Mr. Chopra ne bataya tha: pehle **property ka fark** dhundho, fir tareeka chuno. Aage Topic 5 mein ek aur aise hi bade "method chooser" example par kaam karenge.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Filtration se namak paani se alag ho jaata hai"**

Nahi. Namak paani mein **ghul chuka** hai (solution), uske particles itne chhote hain ki filter paper se paani ke saath nikal jaate hain. Filtration sirf **bade, na ghule hue kanon** ko alag karta hai (jaise mitti, ret). Namak ke liye evaporation chahiye.

**Galti 2: "Winnowing har jagah chalta hai"**

Nahi. Winnowing tab chalta hai jab hisson ka **vajan** kaafi alag ho (jaise bhusi aur gehun). Jin cheezon ka vajan lagbhag barabar ho (jaise ret aur namak ke daane), unke liye winnowing kaam nahi karti.

**Galti 3: "Sedimentation aur decantation ek hi hain"**

Nahi. Sedimentation hai **neeche baith jaana**. Decantation hai baithe hue ko chhode bina **upar ka liquid ulat kar alag karna**. Dono saath-saath use hote hain, par alag step hain.

**Galti 4: "Separating funnel kisi bhi do liquid ko alag kar sakta hai"**

Nahi. Sirf wo do liquid jo aapas mein **nahi milte** (jaise tel aur paani). Paani aur alcohol jaise liquid aapas mein mil jaate hain, unke liye funnel bekaar hai.

**Galti 5: "Magnet se har dhaatu alag ho jaati hai"**

Nahi. Magnet sirf **loha, steel, nickel** jaise kuch cheezein kheenchta hai. Aluminium, tamba, peetal aur sone ko aam magnet nahi kheenchta.

**Galti 6: "Evaporation karne se paani aur namak dono mil jaate hain"**

Nahi. Evaporation mein **paani hawa mein ud jaata hai**, hum use wapas nahi pa sakte. Sirf namak bachta hai.

## Quick Recap

- Mixture alag karne se pehle poochho: **hisson mein kaun si property alag hai?** Usi se tareeka milta hai.
- **Handpicking:** haath se chunna (bade, dikhne wale tukde). **Winnowing:** hawa se halka aur bhaari alag (bhusi aur gehun). **Sieving:** chhalni se bade aur chhote kan alag.
- **Magnetic separation:** magnet loha ya steel ko kheench leta hai, ret ya namak ko nahi.
- **Sedimentation:** bhaari kan neeche baithte hain. **Decantation:** upar ka saaf liquid ulat kar alag karna. **Filtration:** filter paper par bade kan (residue) rukte hain, saaf liquid (filtrate) nikalta hai.
- **Centrifugation:** tez ghumaane se bhaari hissa bahar ya neeche chala jaata hai (dairy mein cream, washing machine spinner, khoon ki jaanch).
- **Separating funnel:** do liquid jo aapas mein nahi milte (tel aur paani); bhaari neeche, halka upar; tap se neeche wala pehle nikalta hai.
- **Evaporation:** ghule hue solid (namak) ko paani se alag karne ke liye; paani ud jaata hai. **Sublimation:** jo solid seedha gas banta hai (ammonium chloride, kapoor) use doosre solid se alag karta hai; sirf teacher ki nigrani mein.
- Filtration se namak ka paani alag nahi hota, kyunki namak ghul chuka hai.
- Mixture ke hisse chahe jo ho, **ek property ka fark** dhundhna sabse zaroori kadam hai.

## Practice Questions

1. In your own words, why must we first look for a property that differs before choosing a method to separate a mixture?

2. Name the best method to separate each of these mixtures: (a) husk from wheat grains, (b) iron nails from sand, (c) cream from milk, (d) oil from water, (e) muddy water into clear water, (f) stones from flour.

3. Explain the difference between sedimentation and decantation. Why do we often use them together?

4. Why does filtering salt solution not remove the salt? What method should be used instead?

5. A bag of 60 kg grain contains 3 kg of stones and 1.5 kg of husk. Find the mass of clean grain and the percentage of impurities.

6. A 400 g salt solution contains 25% salt by mass. If all the water is evaporated, how much salt remains and how much water is lost?

7. A dairy has 8 litres of milk that contains 5% cream by volume. How much cream can be separated?

8. A mixture of 250 mL has oil and water. Oil makes up 30% by volume. When a separating funnel is used, (a) which liquid comes out first, and (b) what are the volumes of the two liquids?

9. A 60 g mixture of ammonium chloride and sand is gently heated by a teacher in a lab. The sand left behind weighs 21 g. What is the mass and the percentage of ammonium chloride?

10. A mixture contains sand, iron filings, sugar (soluble in water) and water-insoluble sawdust that floats. Write a sensible order of steps to separate all four, naming the method used in each step.

11. State True or False, and correct the false ones: (a) Winnowing uses the difference in size. (b) A magnet can separate copper from sand. (c) In filtration, the liquid that passes through is the filtrate. (d) A separating funnel can separate water and alcohol.

### Answers

1. Har method kisi na kisi **property ke fark** par kaam karti hai (size, weight, magnet se lagaav, solubility, sublimation). Agar fark pata hi nahi hoga, to galat method chun lenge aur mixture alag nahi hoga. Jaise namak ke solution par filtration lagana bekaar hai.

2. (a) **Winnowing** (vajan ka fark). (b) **Magnetic separation** (loha magnet se chipakta hai). (c) **Centrifugation** (cream halki hai). (d) **Separating funnel** (aapas mein nahi milte). (e) **Sedimentation, decantation aur filtration** (bade kan neeche baithte hain, filter paper par rukte hain). (f) **Sieving** (patthar ke tukde aate se bade hain, chhalni par ruk jaate hain).

3. Sedimentation: bhaari kan dheere-dheere **neeche baith jaate hain**. Decantation: baithe kanon ko hilaye bina **upar ka saaf liquid ulat kar alag karna**. Pehle sedimentation hota hai, tabhi decantation ho sakta hai, isliye dono saath use hote hain.

4. Namak paani mein **ghul chuka** hai, uske particles filter paper ke chhedon se chhote hain, isliye wo paani ke saath nikal jaate hain. Namak alag karne ke liye **evaporation** (paani ud jaane do, namak bach jaayega) use karte hain.

5. Impurities = 3 + 1.5 = 4.5 kg. Saaf grain = 60 − 4.5 = **55.5 kg**. Percentage = (4.5 ÷ 60) × 100 = **7.5%**.
   Jaanch: 55.5 + 4.5 = 60 ✅; 4.5 ÷ 60 = 0.075 ✅

6. Namak = 25% of 400 g = 0.25 × 400 = **100 g**. Paani = 400 − 100 = **300 g** (ud jaayega).
   Jaanch: 100 + 300 = 400 ✅; 100 ÷ 400 = 25% ✅

7. Cream = 5% of 8 L = 0.05 × 8 = **0.4 L** (400 mL).
   Jaanch: 0.4 ÷ 8 = 0.05 ✅

8. Oil = 30% of 250 mL = 0.3 × 250 = **75 mL**; paani = 250 − 75 = **175 mL**. (a) **Paani** pehle nikalta hai (neeche ki layer, bhaari). (b) Paani 175 mL, tel 75 mL.
   Jaanch: 75 + 175 = 250 ✅

9. Ammonium chloride = 60 − 21 = **39 g**. Percentage = (39 ÷ 60) × 100 = **65%**.
   Jaanch: 39 + 21 = 60 ✅; 39 ÷ 60 = 0.65 ✅

10. Ek sahi order: **(1) Magnet** se iron filings nikalo. **(2) Paani daalo** aur hilao: sugar ghul jaayegi, sawdust upar tairegi, sand neeche baithegi. **(3) Sawdust ko** upar se chhanni ya haath se nikalo. **(4) Decantation / filtration** se sand ko sugar ke paani se alag karo (sand residue, sugar ka paani filtrate). **(5) Evaporation** se sugar ke paani se paani udaao, sugar bachegi (garmi se jale nahi, isliye halki dhoop mein).
    Dhyan do: order me koi aur tarkeeb bhi sahi ho sakti hai, bas har step mein property ka fark clear ho.

11. (a) **False** ❌. Winnowing mein **vajan ka fark** use hota hai; size ke fark par sieving kaam karti hai. (b) **False** ❌. Copper ko aam magnet nahi kheenchta; iron ko kheenchta hai. (c) **True** ✅. (d) **False** ❌. Paani aur alcohol aapas mein mil jaate hain, to separating funnel kaam nahi karta.
