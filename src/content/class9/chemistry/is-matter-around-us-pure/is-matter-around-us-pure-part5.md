# Topic 5: Mixtures Ko Alag Karna 2: Distillation, Crystallisation aur Chromatography

Topic 4 mein humne aasaan tareeke seekhe: kisi mixture ke hisson mein **size, weight, magnetism, ghulna ya na ghulna** jaisi koi property alag thi, to usi se unhe alag kar liya. Par kuch mixtures zidd ke pakke hote hain. **Namak ghula hua paani** filter paper se chhan jaata hai (Topic 2 mein dekha tha, true solution ke kan bahut chhote hote hain), magnet use khinchta nahi, aur plain evaporation se namak to mil jaata hai par **paani ud kar kho jaata hai**. Aaj hum teen aise tareeke dekhenge jo in "zidd" mixtures ko bhi alag kar dete hain: **crystallisation**, **distillation** aur **chromatography**.

## Pressure cooker ka dhakkan aur kaali pen ki lakeer

Ms. Awasthi apni bhanji Saanvi ko lekar sunday ki dopahar kitchen mein thi. Chawal ke liye pressure cooker chadha tha. Seeti ke baad Ms. Awasthi ne cooker ko chulhe se utaara, aur Saanvi ne dekha ki **dhakkan ke andar ki taraf paani ki boondein** latak rahi hain, aur kuch boondein tapak kar plate mein gir bhi gayi.

"Mausi, ye paani kahan se aaya? Cooker ke andar to bas chawal aur namak-paani tha, aur ye boondein to bilkul saaf lag rahi hain."

"Wo hi to jaadu hai," Ms. Awasthi ne kaha. "Garam paani ki **bhaap** bani, wo thande dhakkan se takrayi aur wapas **paani ki boondein** ban gayi. Ek bhi dana namak is boondon mein nahi gaya, kyunki namak bhaap ke saath nahi udta. Isi ko to Chapter 1 mein condensation kaha tha."

Usi din shaam ko Saanvi homework kar rahi thi ki uske haath se paani ka glass ek geele tissue par ulat gaya. Tissue par ek **kaali sketch pen ki lakeer** thi, aur Saanvi ne dekha ki paani ke saath wo lakeer phailne lagi, aur kaali ke bajaye usme **neela, lal aur peela** rang alag-alag ugne lage. "Ye kaali pen to **ek hi rang** ki thi na? Ye rang kahan se aa gaye?"

Ms. Awasthi muskuraayi. "Saanvi, aaj tumne ek hi din mein do alag kamaal dekh liye. Pehla: paani ko bhaap banakar aur wapas paani banakar **namak se alag kar liya**. Doosra: ek **kaali pen ka raaz** khol diya, jo asal mein kai rangon ka mixture thi. Ye dono hi aaj ke topic ke tareeke hain: **distillation** aur **chromatography**. Pehle ek ek karke seekhte hain, aur phir tumhari ek mixed sample ka poora plan banayenge."

> **Suraksha ka niyam, pehle hi:** is topic mein jahan bhi **garmi, gas ya ubalta paani** hai, wo kaam **sirf bade ya teacher ke saath** karna hai. Bachche akele stove ya heater ke paas nahi jaate. Aur **koi bhi cheez, chahe paani jaisi dikhe, kabhi chakhni nahi hai**: lab ki ya ghar ki kisi bhi chemical, ya alag kiya hua paani, ya ink ko mooh mein nahi daalna.

## Part A: Crystallisation: plain evaporation se behtar kyun?

Topic 4 mein evaporation se namak nikala tha: namak-paani ko dhoop mein rakha, paani ud gaya, namak bacha. (Jaldi karna ho to log ghol ko garam karke bhi paani udaate hain.) Ye kaam chal jaata hai, par kuch mushkilein hain:

- **Kuch padaarth garmi se kharab ho jaate hain.** Chini ko jyada tez garam karo to wo kaali-bhoori ho jaati hai (caramel), aur sookhne ke baad kaarmel jaisi chipchipi cheez bachti hai, shuddh chini nahi.
- **Evaporation se saari ashuddhiyan bhi saath mein sookh jaati hain.** Agar ghol mein thodi mitti ya doosri chhoti-moti cheezein bhi ghuli hon, to paani udne ke baad wo sab bhi **usi namak ke saath bachti hain**. Matlab namak ka saaf-suthra dher nahi, ek ganda dher milta hai.
- **Crystals ki shakl nahi banti.** Jaldi sookhne par barik chura bachta hai, jabki dheere sookhne par nukile, saaf crystals bante hain.

**Crystallisation** in teeno kamiyon ko door karti hai. Idea ye hai ki hum paani ko **poora ud jaane nahi dete**. Hum ghol ko garam karke itna gaadha bana lete hain ki wo **saturated** ban jaaye (Topic 2 mein dekha tha: saturated ghol mein jyada solute ghul nahi sakta). Phir ghol ko **dheere-dheere thanda hone dete hain**. Thanda hone par paani utna solute **sambhal nahi paata** (Topic 2 ka solubility waala niyam: thande paani mein solid kam ghulta hai), to bacha hua solute **crystals** ke roop mein bahar nikalne lagta hai. Ashuddhiyan kam maatra mein hoti hain aur **mother liquor mein hi ghuli reh jaati hain**, isliye crystals saaf nikalte hain.

> **Dhyan do:** crystallisation aur evaporation mein farak ye hai ki evaporation mein **saara paani udaya jaata hai**, jabki crystallisation mein **paani ko poora udaye bina** ghol ko thanda hone diya jaata hai, aur **crystal nikalne ke baad bacha hua ghol alag kar liya jaata hai**.

**Kab kaam aata hai?** Fitkari (alum) ke saaf crystal banana, **chini** ke dane banana (sugar mills mein), aur samudri paani se **namak** banana (jahan dheemi dhoop mein paani ud kar namak ke crystal chhodta hai, aise kheton ko salt pans kehte hain). Lab mein **copper sulphate** (neela thootha) ke chamakdaar, saaf-neele crystal is tarah banaye jaate hain.

> **Safe home activity (bade ki nigrani mein):** garam paani mein (jo bade ne bana kar diya ho) chamach-chamach karke **fitkari ya chini** tab tak ghulao jab tak aur na ghule. Is gaadhe ghol ko ek saaf katori mein daalo aur **hilaye bina, kai din** chhod do. Katori ke kinaron par chhote crystals ubharte dikhenge. Dhyan: **kabhi chakhna nahi**, chahe chini ho. Experiment ke crystals khaane ke liye nahi hain.

### Solved example 1: crystallisation mein kitne crystal nikle?

Maan lo ek ghol **80 °C par saturated** hai. Is temperature par 100 g paani mein maan lo **50 g** solid ghulta hai. (Ye numbers sirf sawal ke liye maane gaye hain, kisi asli padaarth ke nahi.) Ghol ko **20 °C** tak thanda kiya gaya, jahan 100 g paani mein sirf **20 g** hi ghul sakta hai. 100 g paani wale ghol se kitne gram crystal nikle?

- 80 °C par ghula hua solid: **50 g**
- 20 °C par ghula reh sakne wala solid: **20 g**
- Bahar nikla = 50 − 20 = **30 g crystal**

**Jaanch:** 20 + 30 = 50 g, jitna shuru mein ghula tha ✅. Agar 300 g paani ka ghol hota to 3 guna, yaani 90 g crystal nikalte ✅ (30 × 3 = 90).

## Part B: Simple distillation: namak-paani se saaf paani

Ab wapas Saanvi ke cooker par aate hain. Kitchen mein ye sab apne aap ho gaya tha: **paani ubla → bhaap bani → thande dhakkan par boondein bani**. Namak peeche cooker mein hi reh gaya, kyunki wo bhaap ke saath nahi udta. Yehi pura idea **distillation** hai.

**Idea:** jab liquid ko garam karte hain aur wo ubalne lagta hai, to Chapter 1 ke hisaab se uske kan (particles) **gas ban kar upar jaate hain**. Par ghule hue namak ke kan **bhaap nahi bante**, wo bartan mein hi reh jaate hain (namak ka boiling point paani se bahut zyada hota hai). Agar hum bhaap ko ek **thandi nali (condenser)** mein bhej dein, to wo thandi hoke wapas **paani** ban jaati hai, aur wo paani **saaf** hota hai.

```
              thermometer
                   │
              ┌────┴────┐     condenser (thandi nali)
              │  bhaap  ├════════════════════════╗
              │    ▲    │   bhaap ──▶ paani ban  ║
         ┌────┘    │    └────┐  jaati hai        ║
         │  ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~  │                   ▼
         │    namak-paani    │                ┌─────┐
         │      (flask)      │                │ ~~~ │ ← saaf paani (distillate)
         └───────────────────┘                └─────┘
                   ▲                     ikattha karne wala bartan
                   │ garmi (sirf bade ya teacher ke liye)
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Simple distillation ka set-up: flask mein namak-paani ubalta hai, bhaap thandi nali (condenser) mein jaakar wapas paani ban jaati hai aur doosre bartan mein ikattha hoti hai. Namak flask mein hi reh jaata hai. (Heating hamesha bade ya teacher kare.)

**Step by step:**

1. Namak-paani ko flask mein daalkar halka garam karo (adult/teacher). Thermometer ko flask ke muh ke paas lagao.
2. Paani ubalta hai (lagbhag **100 °C**), bhaap bani aur nali ki taraf gayi. Namak flask mein hi raha.
3. Nali ke bahar thanda paani chalta hai, isliye bhaap thandi hoti hai aur **condense** hoke boondein ban jaati hain.
4. Boondein bartan mein ikatthi hoti hain: ye **distillate** (saaf paani) hai.
5. Jab flask mein thoda hi paani bache aur namak ka gadha ghol ya namak bachi dikhe, garmi band kar do. Flask mein jo bacha, wo **residue** hai.

> **Aise saaf paani ko bhi kabhi chakhna nahi hai**, aur bottle par "distilled water" ka label lagao. Ye peene ke liye sahi nahi samjha jaata kyunki usme zaroori khanij (minerals) bhi nahi hote. Distilled water ka kaam: batteries, lab, aur iron (press) mein.

**Kab kaam aata hai?** Jab **ghula hua solid** aur **liquid** alag karne hon, aur hume **liquid** chahiye ho (paani ka bachna zaroori ho). Samudri jahaazon par aur registani ilaakon mein khaare paani ko meetha banane ka ek tareeka ye hi hai.

**Simple distillation kab kaam nahi karti?** Agar do liquids **dono** ubalte hon aur unke boiling point paas-paas hon (jaise alcohol aur paani), to bhaap mein dono aa jaate hain. Tab hume agla tareeka chahiye.

### Solved example 2: kitna saaf paani mila?

500 g samudri jaisa paani lo, jisme **3.5%** namak (mass ke hisaab se) ghula hai. Poora paani distil kar liya jaaye to kitna distillate (saaf paani) aur kitna namak (residue) milega?

- Namak = 3.5% of 500 g = 0.035 × 500 = **17.5 g**
- Paani = 500 − 17.5 = **482.5 g**
- Distil karne par saaf paani lagbhag **482.5 g**, aur residue **17.5 g** namak

**Jaanch:** 482.5 + 17.5 = 500 g ✅ (mass bachi). Asal mein thodi bhaap kho bhi sakti hai, isliye real experiment mein distillate thoda kam mil sakta hai.

## Part C: Fractional distillation: jab dono liquid ubalte hon

Ab ek mushkil mixture: **alcohol (ethanol) aur paani**. Dono ek doosre mein **poori tarah mil jaate hain** (isliye separating funnel Topic 4 mein yahan kaam nahi aata, wo to un liquids ke liye tha jo mix hi nahi hote jaise tel aur paani). Filter bhi nahi, kyunki dono liquid hain aur chhote kanon ke hain.

Par ek property alag hai: **boiling point**.

| Liquid | Boiling point (lagbhag) |
|---|---|
| Alcohol (ethanol) | **78 °C** |
| Paani | **100 °C** |

Ab socho: jab ye mixture garam hota hai to **pehle 78 °C ke aas-paas** alcohol zyada tezi se bhaap banta hai, aur paani **baad mein**. Par ek pareshani hai: 78 °C par bhi thoda paani bhaap banta hai, aur bhaap mein dono mil jaate hain. Isliye ek aur cheez lagti hai: ek **lambi (fractionating) column**. Ye column bhaap ka raasta lamba aur thanda-sa kar deti hai, jisse **paani ki bhaap (jiska boiling point zyada hai) neeche hi condense hoke wapas flask mein tapak jaati hai**, aur sirf **alcohol ki bhaap upar tak pahunchti hai**. Upar thermometer **78 °C ke aas-paas** hi theher jaata hai, jab tak alcohol nikalta rehta hai.

```
         thermometer
              │
              ▼
          ┌───┴───┐
          │ ○ ○ ○ ├────────╲      thandi nali (condenser)
          │ ○ ○ ○ │         ╲
   upar:  │ ○ ○ ○ │          ╲
 zyada    │ ○ ○ ○ │         ╭─┴─╮
 thanda   │ ○ ○ ○ │         │   │ ← pehle yahan alcohol (78 °C par)
          │ ○ ○ ○ │         ╰───╯
          │ ○ ○ ○ │   fractionating column
   neeche:│ ○ ○ ○ │   (beads ya glass ke ring)
  zyada   └───┬───┘
  garam       │
          ┌───┴───┐
          │ ~~~~~ │
          │alcohol│
          │+ paani│
          └───────┘
              ▲
              │ garmi (sirf bade ya teacher)
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Fractional distillation ka idea: lambi column mein neeche garam aur upar thanda hota hai. Zyada boiling point wali cheez (paani) neeche hi condense hoke wapas gir jaati hai, kam boiling point wali (alcohol) upar tak pahunchti hai aur nikalti hai.

**Kaise karte hain, short mein:**

1. Mixture ko dheere garam karo (adult/teacher).
2. Thermometer dekhte raho: jab wo **78 °C** ke aas-paas theher jaaye, to jo liquid nikal raha hai wo mostly alcohol hai. Use ek bartan mein ikattha karo.
3. Jab thermometer **78 °C se upar chadhne lage**, to alcohol lagbhag khatam ho raha hai. Bartan badal do. Ab agla hissa (**fraction**) aayega, jisme paani zyada hoga.
4. Ye ek tarah ka **"hissa-hissa karke" nikalna** hai, isiliye iska naam fractional distillation hai.

**Ek baat dhyan se:** jab do mile hue liquids ke boiling points mein **bahut farak** ho (mota-mota **25 °C se zyada**), to simple distillation se bhi kaafi had tak kaam chal jaata hai. Jab farak **kam** ho (**25 °C se kam**), tab lambi column wali fractional distillation zaroori hai. Alcohol (78 °C) aur paani (100 °C) mein sirf 22 °C ka farak hai, isliye inke liye fractional distillation chahiye. Farak jitna kam, kaam utna dheema aur mushkil.

### Hawa ko alag karna (sirf idea)

Topic 1 mein dekha tha ki **hawa ek mixture hai** (nitrogen, oxygen, argon aur thodi aur gaison ka). Ise alag karne ke liye bhi fractional distillation hi lagta hai, bas pehle hawa ko **bahut thanda karke liquid bana liya jaata hai** (liquefaction). Phir us **liquid hawa ko dheere dheere garam** karte hain:

| Gas | Boiling point (lagbhag) |
|---|---|
| Nitrogen | **−196 °C** |
| Argon | **−186 °C** |
| Oxygen | **−183 °C** |

Sabse kam boiling point wala **nitrogen pehle** ubal kar gas ban jaata hai aur nikalta hai, uske baad argon, aur phir oxygen. Isi tarah **oxygen cylinders** (hospital ke liye) aur nitrogen industry mein banti hai. (Oxygen aur argon ke boiling points paas hain, isliye unhe alag karna thoda mushkil kaam hai. Ye aage ki class ka vishay hai.)

### Crude oil: 2-3 line mein

**Crude oil** (kachcha tel) zameen se nikalne wala ek kaala, chipchipa **mixture** hai, jisme sainkdon alag liquids hote hain. Use ek bahut bade **fractionating tower** mein garam kiya jaata hai. Tower mein neeche garam aur upar thanda hota hai, isliye alag-alag **boiling point** wale hisse **alag-alag unchai par** nikalte hain: sabse upar **LPG** aur petrol jaise halke fractions, beech mein **kerosene** aur **diesel**, aur sabse neeche **bhaari tel aur tar (bitumen)** jisse sadak banti hai. Poori detail aage ki classes mein seekhoge.

### Solved example 3: kaun pehle nikla?

Ek mixture mein **A (boiling point 65 °C)** aur **B (boiling point 110 °C)** hain, jo aapas mein mile hue hain. Fractional distillation karte samay (a) pehle kaun nikalega? (b) thermometer pehle kis temperature ke aas-paas rukega?

- (a) Kam boiling point wala, yaani **A**, pehle bhaap banta aur column se upar tak pahunchta hai.
- (b) Thermometer **65 °C** ke aas-paas rukega.
- Phir temperature 65 °C se upar chadhne lagega. Uske baad **B** ka hissa aana shuru hoga, aur B ke liye thermometer **110 °C** ke aas-paas rukega.

**Jaanch:** 110 − 65 = 45 °C ka farak, jo 25 °C se kaafi zyada hai. Isliye yahan simple distillation se bhi mota-mota alag ho jaata, aur column ke saath to aur saaf alag hoga; dono haalat mein A pehle nikalega ✅.

## Part D: Paper chromatography: kaali pen ka raaz

Ab Saanvi ki kaali pen par aate hain. Rang ya dye ke mixtures ko alag karne ke liye **distillation kaam nahi karta** (rang ubalte nahi, ya garmi se kharab ho jaate hain). Yahan ek chhoti magar zabardast trick kaam karti hai: **chromatography** (chroma = rang). Isme alag-alag rang kaagaz par chadhte hue **alag raftaar** se chalte hain.

**Kaise?** Do cheezein hoti hain:

- **Kaagaz** (filter paper ya tissue), jis par rang ki bindi lagayi jaati hai. Kaagaz ke andar kuch rang **zyada chipakte** hain.
- **Solvent** (yahan paani), jo kaagaz par **upar chadhta** hai (jaise blotting paper paani sokhta hai) aur apne saath rang bhi kheenchta hai.

Jo rang **paani mein zyada ghulta hai aur kaagaz se kam chipakta hai**, wo **tez aur door tak** chalta hai. Jo rang kaagaz se **zyada chipakta hai** ya paani mein kam ghulta hai, wo **dheema aur kam doori** tak. Isliye ek rang ki bindi mein agar teen rang mile hain, to wo kaagaz par **teen alag jagah** pahunch kar alag ho jaate hain.

```
        ┌───────────┐
        │- - - - - -│ ← solvent front (paani yahan tak pahuncha)
        │           │
        │     ●     │ ← peela (sabse door, sabse tez)
        │           │
        │     ●     │ ← lal
        │           │
        │     ●     │ ← neela (sabse kam, sabse dheema)
        │           │
        │─────○─────│ ← pencil ki base line; kaali bindi yahan lagi thi
        │           │
     ~~~│~~~~~~~~~~~│~~~ ← paani (base line se neeche)
        └───────────┘
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Chromatography strip ka idea: kaali bindi ke rang alag raftaar se chalte hain. Peela sabse door, neela sabse kam. Bindi paani ke **upar** rakhi jaati hai, paani mein dooba kar nahi.

**Safe home activity:**

1. Ek **white coffee filter**, tissue ya filter paper ki ek lambi patti kaato (lagbhag 2 cm chaudi aur 10-12 cm lambi).
2. Neeche se lagbhag **2 cm upar** ek **pencil se halki si lakeer** kheencho (ye **base line**: pencil hi kyun, kyunki pencil ka graphite paani mein nahi ghulta, pen ki ink ghul jaati).
3. Us lakeer ke beech mein **kaali sketch pen** (water-based, washable wali) se ek **chhoti si bindi** lagao aur sukhao.
4. Ek glass mein **thoda sa paani** daalo (lagbhag 1 cm), itna ki **bindi paani ke upar rahe**.
5. Patti ko pencil se latka do taaki uska neeche ka sira bas paani ko chhuye.
6. **Hilaye bina** dekho: paani upar chadhega aur bindi ke rang alag hote jaayenge. Jab paani upar ke kareeb pahunch jaaye, patti nikaal kar **pencil se solvent front ki jagah** nishaan lagao aur sukha do.

**Kuch sawal jo dimag mein aate hain:**

- **Permanent marker** par try karo to rang shayad na chale, kyunki uski ink paani mein nahi ghulti. Uske liye paani ki jagah doosra solvent (jaise rubbing alcohol) lagta hai, aur wo kaam **bade** karein.
- Ek hi company ki alag kaali pens ke patterns alag aa sakte hain. Isi wajah se chromatography ka use jaanch mein bhi hota hai: **forensic** mein dhamki bhare note ya cheque par ki ink kaun si pen ki hai, ye pata karne mein, aur **khaane ke rang** mein milawat jaanchne mein.

> **Aage ki class mein:** doosre prakar ki chromatography (gas, column) aur ek number jise **Rf value** kehte hain, wo bhi seekhoge. Abhi ye hi samajhna kaafi hai ki alag cheezein alag raftaar se chalti hain.

### Solved example 4: kaun sa rang tez chala?

Chromatography ke baad ek patti par kaali pen ke teen rang mile. Bindi (base line) se solvent front tak ki doori **8 cm** thi. Rang A bindi se **6 cm**, rang B **4 cm** aur rang C **2 cm** door pahunche. (a) Kaun sa rang sabse tez chala? (b) Har rang ki doori solvent ki doori ke kitne hisse ke barabar hai? (Ise ek tarah ka "kitna hissa chala" number maan lo.)

- (a) Sabse zyada doori tay karne wala: **A (6 cm)**.
- (b) A: 6 ÷ 8 = **0.75**. B: 4 ÷ 8 = **0.5**. C: 2 ÷ 8 = **0.25**.

**Jaanch:** A (0.75) > B (0.5) > C (0.25) aur teeno 1 se chhote hain, jaisa hona chahiye kyunki koi rang solvent se aage nahi ja sakta ✅. Dhyan do: ek hi rang ke liye ye number lagbhag same aata hai agar paani, kaagaz aur paristhitiyan same ho, isliye isse rang pehchaan sakte hain (aage ki class mein ise Rf kehte hain).

## Part E: Ek mixed sample ka poora plan (Method chooser)

Ab sab seekhe hue tareeke ek saath. Ms. Awasthi ne Saanvi ko ek challenge diya: **"Ek bartan mein ret (sand), namak, lohe ka burada (iron filings) aur paani mile hue hain. In chaaron ko alag-alag kaise nikaaloge? Step order batao."**

Saanvi ne pehle **sochne ka tareeka** apnaya: har cheez ki **alag property** dhoondho.

| Cheez | Alag property |
|---|---|
| Lohe ka burada (iron filings) | Magnet se chipakta hai, baaki kuch nahi chipakta |
| Ret (sand) | Paani mein nahi ghulti, mote kan, filter paper par ruk jaati hai |
| Namak | Paani mein ghul jaata hai, filter paper se nikal jaata hai |
| Paani | Ubalta hai (100 °C), bhaap ban sakta hai, namak nahi udta |

**Step order (kyun is order mein):**

1. **Magnet se lohe ka burada nikalo.** Ye sabse pehle kyun? Kyunki iske liye mixture ke **geela hone ki zarurat nahi**, aur agar baad mein chhodein to filtration ya doosre steps ke beech mein ye bura-bura phaila ya kharab ho sakta hai. (Agar mixture geela hai, to magnet ko **plastic ki thaili mein rakhkar** ghumao, taaki burada saaf nikalke thaili se hat sake.) Ek baar iska kaam ho gaya, ab baaki teen cheezein bachi.
2. **Filtration (chhaanna) se ret alag karo.** Mixture ko filter paper se chhano. **Ret** filter paper par reh jaati hai (**residue**), aur **namak-paani wala ghol** neeche aa jaata hai (**filtrate**). (Topic 4 ka filtration.) Ret ko paani se dho kar sukha lo.
3. **Ab namak-paani ka ghol bacha.** Do option hain, apni zarurat ke hisaab se:
   - **Agar namak chahiye (paani ki parwah nahi):** **evaporation** ya, behtar, **crystallisation**. Ghol ko dheere garam karo (adult/teacher), thoda gaadha karke dheere thanda ya sukhne do, namak ke crystal bante hain.
   - **Agar paani bhi chahiye:** **simple distillation**. Paani bhaap bankar condenser mein saaf paani ban jaata hai, aur namak flask mein reh jaata hai.

**Final summary:**

| Step | Tareeka | Kya nikalta hai |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Magnet | Lohe ka burada |
| 2 | Filtration | Ret (residue) |
| 3a | Crystallisation ya evaporation | Namak |
| 3b | Distillation | Saaf paani (aur residue mein namak) |

**Kya order badal sakte hain?** Agar pehle filtration karte, to filter paper par **ret aur lohe ka burada dono** aa jaate, aur unko alag karne ke liye baad mein magnet lagana padta. Wo bhi chalta, par pehle magnet lagane se kaam **seedha aur saaf** rehta hai. Yaad rakho: **order wo chuno jisme har step mixture ko saral banaye**.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Evaporation aur crystallisation ek hi hain."**
Nahi. Evaporation mein **saara paani ud jaata hai** aur ashuddhiyan namak ke saath reh jaati hain. Crystallisation mein ghol ko **saturated karke dheere thanda** karte hain, shuddh crystal bante hain aur ashuddhiyan mother liquor mein reh jaati hain.

**Galti 2: "Distillation mein namak bhaap ke saath chala jaata hai."**
Nahi. Namak ke kan bhaap nahi bante (uska boiling point bahut zyada hai). Namak flask mein reh jaata hai. Isliye distillate saaf paani hota hai.

**Galti 3: "Simple distillation se alcohol aur paani alag ho jaate hain."**
Poori tarah nahi. Dono ke boiling point paas hain aur dono bhaap banate hain. Unke liye **fractional distillation** (column ke saath) chahiye.

**Galti 4: "Pen ki bindi paani mein dooba do, to jaldi alag hoga."**
Nahi. Bindi paani ke **upar** rehni chahiye. Agar paani mein dooba di, to ink paani mein ghul jaati hai aur kaagaz par chadhne ki jagah glass mein phail jaati hai. Aur base line **pencil** se kheenchte hain, pen se nahi.

**Galti 5: "Chromatography mein rang kaagaz mein 'badalte' hain."**
Nahi. Rang **pehle se pen ki ink mein mile hue the**. Chromatography unhe sirf **alag raftaar se chalakar alag** dikha deti hai. Naya rang nahi banta.

**Galti 6: "Distilled paani peene ke liye sabse achha hai."**
Nahi. Wo sirf saaf paani hai aur usme zaroori khanij nahi hote. Wo lab, battery aur iron ke liye hai. Aur lab ka koi bhi liquid kabhi chakhna nahi hai.

**Galti 7: "Garmi wale kaam bachche kar sakte hain, bas dhyan se."**
Nahi. Distillation aur crystallisation mein **garam paani ya bhaap** hoti hai. Ye kaam **sirf bade ya teacher** karenge.

## Quick Recap

- Kuch mixtures aasaan tareeke se alag nahi hote (jaise namak-paani, ya do mile hue liquids). Unke liye **crystallisation, distillation, chromatography** hain.
- **Crystallisation:** saturated garam ghol ko dheere thanda karke **shuddh crystals** nikalna. Ashuddhiyan **mother liquor** mein rehti hain. Ye plain evaporation se behtar hai, kyunki garmi se kharab hone wale padaarth bachte hain aur crystal saaf milte hain. Fitkari, copper sulphate, chini aur namak iske examples hain.
- **Simple distillation:** liquid ko ubalo, bhaap ko condenser mein thanda karo, **distillate** milta hai. Ghule hue solid aur liquid ko alag karne ke liye (namak-paani se saaf paani). Solid **residue** mein reh jaata hai.
- **Fractional distillation:** do mile hue liquids jinke **boiling points alag** hain (alcohol 78 °C aur paani 100 °C). Lambi column lagti hai, kam boiling point wala pehle nikalta hai. Hawa ko pehle liquid banakar alag karte hain (nitrogen −196 °C pehle nikalta hai). **Crude oil** ko fractionating tower mein LPG, petrol, kerosene, diesel aur bitumen jaise fractions mein baanta jaata hai.
- **Paper chromatography:** rangon ko alag raftaar se chalakar alag karna. Kaali pen ki ink asal mein kai rangon ka mixture hai. **Pencil ki base line**, bindi paani ke **upar**.
- **Method chooser:** ret + namak + lohe ka burada + paani: pehle **magnet**, phir **filtration**, phir **crystallisation ya distillation**.
- **Safety:** garmi wala koi bhi kaam **sirf bade ya teacher** ke saath. **Kabhi kuch chakhna nahi.**

## Practice Questions

1. Name two reasons why crystallisation is better than plain evaporation for getting pure crystals of a solid from its solution.

2. A saturated solution at 90 °C contains 60 g of a solid in 100 g of water. When the solution is cooled to 30 °C, only 25 g of the solid can stay dissolved in 100 g of water. How many grams of crystals form from 100 g of water? How many would form from 250 g of water?

3. A student distils 200 g of salt water that contains 5% salt by mass. How much salt and how much pure water (distillate) can she expect, ignoring any loss?

4. What is the role of the condenser in simple distillation? Which part of the set-up collects the pure water, and what is left behind in the flask?

5. Why do we not need fractional distillation to separate oil and water, while we do need it for alcohol and water? Name the better method for oil and water.

6. A mixture has liquid X (boiling point 56 °C) and liquid Y (boiling point 118 °C), and the two are fully mixed. Which one comes out first in fractional distillation, and at about what temperature does the thermometer stay while it is being collected? The rough guideline is that a difference of more than 25 °C can be separated fairly well even by simple distillation. Is that true here?

7. In paper chromatography, the solvent front moved 10 cm from the base line. Three colours moved 8 cm, 5 cm and 2 cm. Find the fraction of the solvent distance moved by each colour, and say which colour sticks most to the paper.

8. Why is the starting spot in a chromatography experiment drawn above the water level and why is the base line drawn with a pencil and not a pen?

9. A sample contains chalk powder, iron filings, sugar and water. Write the steps (in order) to get all four separately. Name the method used at each step. (Hint: chalk does not dissolve in water, sugar does.)

10. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) In simple distillation, the dissolved salt travels with the steam into the condenser.
    (b) A permanent marker may not move with plain water, because its ink is not dissolved by water.
    (c) Distillation of salt water is a safe activity for children to do alone with a stove.
    (d) In fractional distillation of liquid air, nitrogen comes out first because it has the lowest boiling point of the three gases named in this lesson.

### Answers

1. Pehli wajah: evaporation mein saara paani garam karke udaya jaata hai, isliye kuch padaarth (jaise chini) **garmi se kharab** (caramel) ho sakte hain, jabki crystallisation mein dheere thanda karte hain. Doosri wajah: evaporation mein ashuddhiyan bhi solid ke saath **sookh kar reh jaati hain**, jabki crystallisation mein wo **mother liquor** mein ghuli reh jaati hain, isliye crystal zyada shuddh aur saaf milte hain.

2. 100 g paani se: 60 − 25 = **35 g** crystal. 250 g paani se: 250 ÷ 100 = 2.5 guna, to 35 × 2.5 = **87.5 g** crystal.
   Jaanch: 100 g paani mein 25 g ghula raha, 35 g nikla, 25 + 35 = 60 g ✅. 250 g paani mein 60 × 2.5 = 150 g tha, 25 × 2.5 = 62.5 g ghula reh gaya, 150 − 62.5 = 87.5 g ✅.

3. Namak = 5% of 200 = 0.05 × 200 = **10 g**. Paani = 200 − 10 = **190 g**.
   Jaanch: 190 + 10 = 200 g ✅.

4. Condenser bhaap ko **thanda** karti hai, jisse wo wapas **liquid (paani)** ban jaati hai. Saaf paani **ikattha karne wale bartan (receiving vessel)** mein jama hota hai. Flask mein **namak (residue)** reh jaata hai.

5. Tel aur paani **aapas mein mix nahi hote**, wo alag-alag parat banate hain, isliye unhe fractionating column ki zarurat nahi, bas **separating funnel** se alag kar sakte hain. Alcohol aur paani poori tarah mix ho jaate hain, isliye funnel kaam nahi karta. Unke boiling points (78 °C aur 100 °C) alag hain, isliye fractional distillation chalti hai.

6. Kam boiling point wala **X** pehle nikalega, aur thermometer **56 °C** ke aas-paas rukega. Farak 118 − 56 = **62 °C** hai, jo 25 °C se bahut zyada hai, isliye **haan**, yahan simple distillation se bhi kaafi had tak alag ho jaayenge; column ke saath aur bhi saaf.
   Jaanch: 118 − 56 = 62 ✅.

7. Doori ka hissa: 8 ÷ 10 = **0.8**, 5 ÷ 10 = **0.5**, 2 ÷ 10 = **0.2**. Sabse kam chalne wala rang (2 cm, 0.2) kaagaz se **sabse zyada chipka** hua hai.
   Jaanch: teeno 1 se chhote hain ✅.

8. Bindi paani ke **upar** rakhte hain, nahi to ink paani mein ghul kar glass mein phail jaati aur kaagaz par rang alag nahi hote. Base line **pencil** se isliye, kyunki pencil ka graphite paani mein nahi ghulta aur wo rang ke saath chal nahi deta; pen ki ink ghul jaati aur chal padti, jisse base line bhi ek rang ban jaati.

9. Steps: (1) **Magnet** se iron filings nikalo (sookhe mixture par bhi chalta hai). (2) Baaki mixture mein **paani daalke hilao**: sugar ghul jaati hai, chalk nahi. (3) **Filtration** se chalk powder (residue) alag karo. (4) Filtrate (sugar-paani) ko **crystallisation** se sugar ke crystal ke liye (chini zyada garmi se kharab hoti hai), ya **distillation** se saaf paani ke liye alag karo.

10. (a) **False** ❌. Namak flask mein reh jaata hai, bhaap ke saath nahi jaata. (b) **True** ✅. Permanent ink paani mein nahi ghulti, isliye doosra solvent lagta hai. (c) **False** ❌. Garmi aur ubalte paani wale kaam sirf bade ya teacher karte hain. (d) **True** ✅. Nitrogen (−196 °C) argon (−186 °C) aur oxygen (−183 °C) se pehle nikalta hai.
