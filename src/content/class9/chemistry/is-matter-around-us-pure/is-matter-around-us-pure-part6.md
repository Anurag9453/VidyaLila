# Topic 6: Tatva (Element) aur Yaugik (Compound): Kaun Kya Hai

Topic 4 aur 5 mein humne mixtures ko alag karne ke kaafi tareeke seekhe: chhanna, filter karna, ubaal kar bhaap banana, crystal jamana, chromatography. Har baar kisi na kisi **physical property** (size, magnet ka asar, boiling point, ghulne ki kshamta) ka fark kaam aaya. Ab ek bada sawal: **pure substance** ko in tareekon se todne ki koshish karo, to kya hoga? Pure paani ko ubaalo to bhaap banti hai, par wo bhi paani hi hai. Pure namak ko chhaano ya ghol kar sukhao, to namak hi wapas milta hai. To kya pure substance ke andar kuch aur chhupa nahi hota? Aaj is sawal ka jawab milega, aur ek chaunkane wali baat bhi.

## Dining table ka namak-daan

Ravivaar ki dopahar thi. Mr. Lahiri apne bhatije Shaurya ke saath khaane baithe the. Mez par dal, sabzi, chawal aur beech mein steel ka chhota sa namak-daan (salt shaker). Shaurya ne daal mein thoda namak chhidka aur achanak ruk gaya.

"Lahiri uncle, hum Topic 1 mein padh chuke hain ki pure substance ek hi tarah ka hota hai. Ye namak bhi pure hai na? To ye **kis cheez ka bana hai**? Paani mein se dikhta nahi, chhanne par ek hi tarah ke safed dane. Isme aakhir hai kya?"

Mr. Lahiri muskuraaye aur dheere se bole, "Tumhein pata hai Shaurya, is namak-daan ke andar ek **khatarnak dhaatu (metal)** aur ek **zehrili gas** chhupi hui hai. Aur jab wo dono milti hain, to **khaane ka namak** ban jaata hai."

Shaurya ne apni chammach neeche rakh di. "Kya? Zehrili gas? Aur main wahi daal mein daal kar kha raha hoon?"

"Haan, aur tum bilkul theek ho," Mr. Lahiri hanse. "Kyun? Ye aaj ke topic ka raaz hai. Pehle ye samjhte hain ki kuch cheezein aisi hoti hain jo kisi aur cheez se bani hi nahi hoti (tatva), aur kuch aisi jo kai tatvon ke jud jaane se bani hoti hain (yaugik). Aakhir mein hum is namak ka hisaab bhi nikalenge."

## Part A: Tatva (Element): ek hi tarah ke kan

Chapter 1 mein humne dekha tha ki har cheez **kanon (particles)** ki bani hai. Ab ek nayi baat: kisi substance mein ye kan **bilkul ek hi tarah ke** ho sakte hain, ya **alag-alag tarah ke jude hue**. Jis substance mein sab kan ek hi tarah ke hon, use **tatva (element)** kehte hain. (Ye kan **atom** bhi ho sakte hain aur **molecule** bhi. Dono ko hum aage "Atoms and Molecules" chapter mein theek se seekhenge, abhi bas "ek hi tarah ka kan" yaad rakho.)

Tatva ki pehchan ek line mein:

> **Tatva wo pure substance hai jise kisi bhi physical ya chemical tareeke se aur saral (simpler) substances mein nahi toda ja sakta.**

Soch kar dekho: tumhare paas ek loha ki keel hai. Use kaato, pighlao, pissho, ya kisi bhi tareeke se chemical lagao, to bhi usme se kuch "aur saral cheez" nikalti nahi. Loha loha hi rehta hai. Isliye **loha ek tatva** hai. Isi tarah tamba, aluminium, sona, oxygen, hydrogen aur sulphur sab tatva hain.

Abhi tak vaigyanik **lagbhag 118 tatva** pehchan chuke hain. Sochne wali baat: duniya ki lakhon alag-alag cheezein (pathar, paani, plastic, kapde, khana, hamara sharir) sirf in lagbhag 118 tatvon ke alag-alag mel se bani hain. Jaise sirf 26 akshar milke hazaron shabd bana dete hain!

**Tatva roz ki zindagi mein:**
- **Aluminium foil** roti lapetne wali: aluminium tatva.
- **Tamba ka taar** (copper wire) ya paani ka lota: copper tatva.
- **Pencil ki "lead"**: asli mein graphite hai, jo **carbon** tatva ka ek roop hai.
- **Sona (gold)** ki angoothi: gold tatva (par saaf 24 carat sona; 22 carat sona mixture hai, ye aage dekhenge).
- Jo **oxygen** hum saans mein lete hain: ek gas tatva.
- **Sulphur** ka peela powder: ek tatva (pehle ke zamane mein ise gandhak kehte the).

Isi wajah se tatva ko **sabse saral pure substance** maana jaata hai.

## Part B: Tatvon ke teen parivaar: dhaatu, adhaatu, upadhaatu

118 tatvon ko yaad rakhna mushkil hai, isliye vaigyanikon ne unhein **teen group** mein baant diya. Bas apne gun (properties) dekh kar:

| Gun | Dhaatu (Metal) | Adhaatu (Non-metal) |
|---|---|---|
| Chamak | Chamakdaar (lustrous) | Aksar dull (chamak nahi) |
| Kadapan | Kadak (Zyada tar) | Solid ho to aksar bhurbhura aur tootne wala |
| Hathode se peetne par | Chadar ban jaati hai (malleable) | Toot kar bikhar jaata hai |
| Taar banana | Khinch kar taar banta hai (ductile) | Taar nahi bante |
| Garmi aur bijli | Achha conductor | Aam taur par kharab conductor |
| Thokne par awaaz | Ghanti jaisi awaaz (sonorous) | Dhimi ya koi awaaz nahi |
| Roz ki misaal | Loha, tamba, aluminium, sona, chandi | Sulphur, carbon (koyla), oxygen, nitrogen, chlorine |

(Ek apwaad: **graphite**, jo carbon ka ek roop hai, adhaatu hone ke bawajood bijli ka achha conductor hai. Ye aage ki classes mein samjhenge.)

**Upadhaatu (Metalloids)** beech wale tatva hain. Inme kuch gun dhaatu jaise aur kuch adhaatu jaise hote hain. Silicon (computer chip aur mobile mein) aur germanium aur arsenic isi parivaar ke hain. Silicon dekhne mein chamakdaar lagta hai, par chhoone par tootne wala hota hai, aur bijli ko na poori tarah chalata hai na poori tarah rokta hai.

### Do tatva jo thoda alag hain

Chapter 1 mein humne teen states padhi thi: solid, liquid, gas. Kamre ke normal temperature par zyada tar dhaatu **solid** hote hain, aur zyada tar adhaatu **solid ya gas**. Par do tatva liquid bhi hain:

- **Mercury (paara):** ek **dhaatu** jo kamre ke temperature par **liquid** hai. Purane thermometer mein chamakdaar chaandi jaisa liquid yahi tha. (Mercury ki bhaap zehrili hoti hai, isliye toote thermometer ko kabhi haath mat lagao, bade se pucho.)
- **Bromine:** ek **adhaatu** jo kamre ke temperature par **liquid** hai: gahra laal-bhura, aur uski bhaap zehrili aur aankhon ko jalane wali hoti hai.

Yaani "dhaatu = hamesha solid" aur "adhaatu = hamesha gas" jaise rule sahi nahi hain.

## Part C: Yaugik (Compound): jud kar nayi cheez

Ab Mr. Lahiri ke namak-daan par wapas aate hain. Namak (table salt) ek **pure substance** hai. Par wo tatva nahi hai, kyunki use chemical tareeke se todne par do alag tatva milte hain:

- **Sodium:** ek **dhaatu**. Itni naram ki chaaku se kat jaati hai, aur itni khatarnak ki paani se milte hi bhadak kar jalne lagti hai. Isliye use mitti ke tel (kerosene) mein rakhte hain.
- **Chlorine:** ek **hari-peeli, zehrili gas**. Ye gas bahut tez smell wali hoti hai aur zyada saans mein jaaye to phephdon ko nuksaan karti hai.

Ye dono, jo akele bahut khatarnak hain, jab chemical tareeke se jud jaate hain, to **khaane ka safed, namkeen namak** ban jaate hain. Yahi hai Mr. Lahiri ka raaz. Namak ka na wo gun hai jo sodium ka hai, na wo jo chlorine ka. **Bilkul naya substance, naye gun.**

Isi ko **yaugik (compound)** kehte hain:

> **Compound wo pure substance hai jo do ya zyada tatvon ke chemical roop se, ek fixed ratio mein, jud jaane se bana hota hai. Uske gun uske tatvon ke gunon se bilkul alag hote hain.**

**Teen jaane-pehchane compound:**

| Compound | Kin tatvon se bana | Gun |
|---|---|---|
| **Paani** (aage seekhenge ki ise H₂O likhte hain) | Hydrogen (aag pakadne wali gas) aur oxygen (jalne mein madad karne wali gas) | Paani na aag pakadta hai, na jalne mein madad karta hai, balki aag bujhata hai |
| **Carbon dioxide** | Carbon (kaala, solid) aur oxygen (gas) | Soda ke bulbule wali gas, aag bujhane mein bhi kaam aati hai |
| **Namak (table salt)** | Sodium (khatarnak dhaatu) aur chlorine (zehrili gas) | Khaane mein swaad, sehat ke liye zaroori (thodi matra mein) |

Dhyan do: paani ka ek bahut bada sabut. Hydrogen khud aag pakadti hai, oxygen khud jalne mein madad karti hai, par jab dono ek compound mein jude hain, to aag bujhane wala paani ban jaata hai. Yaani **compound ke gun tatvon se bilkul nahi milte**.

### Compound ko kaise todte hain?

Compound ko **physical tareeke** (chhanna, filter, ubalna, magnet) se nahi toda ja sakta, ye humne Topic 4 aur 5 mein dekha: pure paani ko ubaal kar bhaap banane par bhi paani hi milta hai, hydrogen aur oxygen nahi. Compound ko todne ke liye **chemical tareeke** chahiye, jaise **bijli ka karant** (electricity). Paani mein bijli guzaarne par wo do gas mein toot jaata hai: hydrogen aur oxygen. (Ye prayog school lab mein teacher ki nigrani mein kiya jaata hai, aage ke chapters mein aayega.)

### Fixed ratio ka matlab

Compound mein tatva **hamesha ek hi anupaat (ratio) mein** jude hote hain, chahe namak samundar se aaya ho ya khaan se, chahe tumhari rasoi ka ho ya Shaurya ki. Kuch jaane-pehchane anupaat, bhaar (mass) ke hisaab se:

- **Paani:** hydrogen aur oxygen **1 : 8** ke ratio mein. Yaani 9 g paani mein 1 g hydrogen aur 8 g oxygen.
- **Carbon dioxide:** carbon aur oxygen **3 : 8** ke ratio mein. Yaani 11 g carbon dioxide mein 3 g carbon aur 8 g oxygen.
- **Namak:** lagbhag 100 g namak mein 39 g sodium aur 61 g chlorine.

Ye anupaat kabhi nahi badalte. Ek bahut bada sabut hai ki compound sirf "mix" nahi, balki **chemical roop se jud** gaye hain. (Ye niyam aage "Atoms and Molecules" chapter mein "Law of Constant Proportions" ke naam se padhoge.)

## Part D: Ratio wale sawal

**Example 1 (paani mein tatvon ka bhaar):** 27 g paani mein kitna hydrogen aur kitna oxygen hota hai? (Paani mein hydrogen : oxygen = 1 : 8, bhaar ke hisaab se)

- Ratio ke kul hisse = 1 + 8 = **9**
- Hydrogen = 27 × (1 ÷ 9) = **3 g**
- Oxygen = 27 × (8 ÷ 9) = **24 g**

**Jaanch:** 3 g + 24 g = 27 g ✅. Aur 3 : 24 = 1 : 8 ✅.

**Example 2 (kitna paani banega?):** 4 g hydrogen poori tarah oxygen se jud kar paani banata hai. (a) Kitni oxygen chahiye? (b) Kitna paani banega?

- (a) 1 g hydrogen ke liye 8 g oxygen, to 4 g hydrogen ke liye 4 × 8 = **32 g oxygen**
- (b) Paani = 4 g + 32 g = **36 g**

**Jaanch:** 4 : 32 = 1 : 8 ✅. Aur 36 g paani mein hydrogen 36 × (1 ÷ 9) = 4 g ✅.

**Example 3 (carbon dioxide):** 22 g carbon dioxide mein kitna carbon aur kitna oxygen hai? (Carbon : oxygen = 3 : 8)

- Kul hisse = 3 + 8 = **11**
- Carbon = 22 × (3 ÷ 11) = **6 g**
- Oxygen = 22 × (8 ÷ 11) = **16 g**

**Jaanch:** 6 + 16 = 22 g ✅. 6 : 16 = 3 : 8 ✅.

**Example 4 (namak-daan ka hisaab, Shaurya ke sawal ka jawab):** Poore parivaar ki daal ke bhagone mein lagbhag **5.85 g** namak pada tha (Shaurya ki plate wala chutki bhar namak to isse bahut kam hai). Us 5.85 g namak ke andar lagbhag kitna sodium aur kitna chlorine tha? (Namak ka lagbhag 39.3% sodium aur 60.7% chlorine hai, bhaar ke hisaab se. Ye mass percent Topic 2 jaisa hi hai: hissa ÷ kul × 100.)

- Sodium = 5.85 × 39.3 ÷ 100 ≈ **2.3 g**
- Chlorine = 5.85 × 60.7 ÷ 100 ≈ **3.55 g**

**Jaanch:** 2.3 + 3.55 = 5.85 g ✅. 39.3 + 60.7 = 100 ✅. Dhyan do, agar ye 2.3 g sodium akela hota to khatarnak hota, par namak ke roop mein bilkul surakshit hai, kyunki ek naya substance ban chuka hai.

**Example 5 (alag-alag sample, ek ratio):** Ek student ne **9 g** paani, aur doosre ne **90 g** paani ko todkar hydrogen nikali. Kya dono mein hydrogen ka ratio same rahega? Kitni-kitni hydrogen milegi?

- 9 g paani mein hydrogen = 9 × (1 ÷ 9) = **1 g**
- 90 g paani mein hydrogen = 90 × (1 ÷ 9) = **10 g**

**Jaanch:** 1 ÷ 9 aur 10 ÷ 90 dono = 1 ÷ 9 ✅. Compound ki shuddhta ka sabut: **sample chhota ho ya bada, ratio wahi**.

## Part E: Mixture aur Compound: ek jaisa dikhne wale, par alag

Mixture (Topic 1) aur compound dono mein "kai tatva saath" lagte hain, isliye galtfehmi hoti hai. Par dono bilkul alag hain.

```
  TATVA               MIXTURE                 COMPOUND
 (ek hi tarah        (alag-alag kan          (alag-alag tatvon ke kan
  ke kan)             bas saath-saath,        chemical roop se jude hain,
                      alag-alag rehte)        har ek jodi ek jaisi)

  o  o  o  o         o  x  o  o  x            ox   ox   ox
  o  o  o  o         x  o  x  o  o            ox   ox   ox
  o  o  o  o         o  o  x  x  o            ox   ox   ox
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Tatva mein sab kan ek jaise (o). Mixture mein o aur x ke kan bas saath-saath hain aur kisi bhi anupaat mein ho sakte hain. Compound mein o aur x jud kar ek nayi jodi (ox) banate hain aur har jodi ek jaisi hoti hai, isliye anupaat hamesha fixed rehta hai.

Ab dono ka poora tulna:

| Baat | Mixture | Compound |
|---|---|---|
| Bana kaise | Cheezein bas **mili hui** hain, koi chemical joda nahi | Tatva **chemical roop se jude** hain |
| Naya substance | Nahi bana | **Naya substance** bana, naye gun |
| Gun | Hissoon ke **gun bane rehte hain** | Hissoon se **bilkul alag** gun |
| Composition (anupaat) | **Koi bhi**, badal sakta hai (chai mein chini kam ya zyada) | **Fixed**, hamesha ek hi |
| Alag karna | **Physical tareeke** se (chhanna, ubalna, magnet, filter) | Sirf **chemical tareeke** se (physical se nahi) |
| Garmi ya roshni nikalna | Aam taur par nahi | Banate waqt aksar garmi ya roshni nikalti hai (ya leti hai) |
| Pure ya impure | **Impure** (kai substance milke) | **Pure substance** (ek hi tarah ka) |
| Misaal | Hawa, samundar ka paani, chai, mitti | Paani, namak, carbon dioxide, chini |

Ek zaroori baat, jo log aksar bhool jaate hain: **compound pure substance hota hai**, mixture nahi. Paani H₂O mein do tatva hain, phir bhi ye pure substance hai, kyunki uske sabhi kan ek jaise hain (ek hi jodi). Isliye "do tatva = mixture" kehna galat hai.

## Part F: Loha aur sulphur: teacher ka prayog (sirf dekhne ke liye)

Ab ek bahut mashhoor prayog, jo aksar school lab mein **teacher khud karte hain**. Ye mixture aur compound ka fark aankhon ke saamne dikha deta hai. **Ye prayog tum khud ghar par ya akele kabhi mat karna.** Isme teacher test tube ko tez garmi dete hain aur dhuan nikalta hai, jisko **achhi hawadaar jagah ya fume cupboard** mein hi karna zaroori hai. Students door se sirf dekhte hain.

**Pehla step: mixture banana.** Teacher loha ke baarik chhure (iron filings) aur peele sulphur powder ko mila dete hain. Lagbhag 7 g loha ke chhure aur 4 g sulphur le liye jaate hain. Kya dikhta hai?

- Magnifying glass se dekho to **sleti (grey), chamakdaar loha ke dane** aur **peele sulphur ke dane** alag-alag dikhte hain.
- Ek magnet is mixture par ghumao to **loha magnet se chipak jaata hai** aur sulphur peeche reh jaata hai. Yaani **physical tareeke se alag ho gaye**.
- Is mixture mein loha aur sulphur dono ke **apne gun** bane hue hain.

Ye ek **mixture** hai, aur isme loha aur sulphur kisi bhi anupaat mein mile ho sakte the (zyada loha, kam sulphur, kuch bhi).

**Doosra step: garm karna (compound banana).** Ab teacher is mixture ko ek test tube mein daal kar tez garmi dete hain. Thodi der mein mixture **laal tapta** hai aur chamakne lagta hai. Test tube ke andar ek **kaala, thos substance** ban jaata hai. Garmi hatane par bhi wo kaala hi rehta hai. Hawa mein halki tez gandh aati hai (ye dhuan ya fumes hi wo wajah hain ki ye prayog hawadaar jagah par karte hain).

**Teesra step: jaanch.** Is kaale substance par magnet laate hain. Ab **magnet use nahi kheenchta**. Magnifying glass se dekhne par na loha dikhta hai, na sulphur: sirf ek kaala substance. Ye **iron sulphide** naam ka **naya compound** hai.

| Sawal | Mixture (loha + sulphur, bina garm kiye) | Compound (garm karne ke baad) |
|---|---|---|
| Rang | Sleti aur peela alag-alag dikhte hain | Ek jaisa kaala |
| Magnet ka asar | Loha chipakta hai | Koi asar nahi |
| Loha ke gun | Maujood | Gayab |
| Sulphur ke gun | Maujood | Gayab |
| Alag karna | Magnet se aasaan | Magnet se nahi ho sakta |

Ye **7 : 4** ka ratio bhi koi sanyog nahi hai. Loha (7 hisse) aur sulphur (4 hisse) ke bhaar ke hisaab se hi wo poori tarah jud kar iron sulphide banate hain. Isi prayog ke aankdon par ek sawal:

**Example 6 (zyada sulphur le liya to?):** Teacher ne 7 g loha aur 6 g sulphur ko garm kiya. (a) Kitna iron sulphide bana? (b) Kitna sulphur bach gaya? (Iron sulphide mein loha : sulphur = 7 : 4)

- 7 g loha ko poori tarah jud-ne ke liye 4 g sulphur chahiye (ratio 7 : 4).
- (a) Iron sulphide = 7 g + 4 g = **11 g**
- (b) Bacha hua sulphur = 6 g − 4 g = **2 g** (ye bina jude bacha, aur ise alag se pehchana ja sakta hai)

**Jaanch:** Kul bhaar pehle = 7 + 6 = 13 g. Baad mein = 11 + 2 = 13 g ✅. Aur 7 : 4 ratio ✅. Yahi hai fixed ratio ka sabut: hum mix kitna bhi kar lein, jud kar bana compound **hamesha 7 : 4 ratio mein** hi banega.

## Part G: Alloy kya hai? Mixture hi hai

Topic 1 mein humne alloys (jaise brass, steel) dekhe the. Ab ye sawal uthta hai: alloy mein dhaatu **milaayi** jaati hai, to kya wo compound hai? **Nahi.**

- **Brass (peetal)** copper aur zinc ko pighla kar milane se banta hai. Brass ke alag-alag sample mein copper aur zinc ka **anupaat alag-alag** ho sakta hai (jaise 60 : 40 ya 70 : 30). Compound mein anupaat fixed hota, par yahan nahi.
- Brass mein copper aur zinc ke **apne gun bane rehte hain**, bas mazboot aur sunehri-peele rang ka naya "mel" banta hai.
- Isliye alloys **mixtures** hain (solid solution jaisi cheezein), **compound nahi**.

Isi wajah se **22 carat sona** bhi ek alloy yaani mixture hai (sona mein tamba ya chandi mili hoti hai taaki gehna mazboot bane), jabki 24 carat saaf sona ek tatva hai.

## Part H: Poori family tree: matter ka bantwara

Topic 1 se Topic 6 tak humne matter ko alag-alag tarah se baanta. Ab sab ek jagah dekho:

```
                          MATTER
                             :
             +---------------+---------------+
             :                               :
       PURE SUBSTANCE                     MIXTURE
    (ek hi tarah ka padaarth)        (kai substance milke)
             :                               :
      +------+------+                 +------+------+
      :             :                 :             :
    TATVA         YAUGIK         HOMOGENEOUS   HETEROGENEOUS
   (element)     (compound)      (ek jaisa)    (alag-alag dikhta)
      :             :                 :             :
  loha, sona,   paani, namak,     solution,     mitti, salad,
  oxygen,       CO2, chini        alloy, hawa   chalk-paani
  sulphur
```

> **Figure 6.2** – Matter ki family tree. Matter ya to pure substance (tatva ya yaugik) hota hai, ya mixture (homogeneous ya heterogeneous). Mixture ko physical tareeke se todte hain, compound ko sirf chemical tareeke se, aur tatva ko aur nahi toda ja sakta.

Ek dum chhoti si "kaun si category" chaabi:

1. Kya physical tareeke se (chhanna, ubalna, magnet) alag ho jaata hai? **Haan** → **Mixture**.
2. **Nahi** (sab ek hi tarah ka hai, ek pure substance) → ab dekho kya chemical tareeke se aur saral substances mein toot sakta hai?
   - **Haan** → **Yaugik (compound)**.
   - **Nahi** → **Tatva (element)**.

## Part I: Kaun kya hai? Solved example

**Example 7:** Neeche likhi cheezon ko element, compound ya mixture mein baanto: hawa, brass, loha, saaf paani (distilled), sona (24 carat), chini, samundar ka paani, oxygen, carbon dioxide, steel.

| Cheez | Category | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| Hawa | Mixture | Nitrogen, oxygen aadi kai gas ka mel, anupaat badal sakta hai |
| Brass | Mixture (alloy) | Copper aur zinc ka mel, anupaat badal sakta hai |
| Loha | Tatva | Ek hi tarah ka kan, aur saral cheez mein nahi toot-ta |
| Saaf paani (distilled) | Compound | Hydrogen aur oxygen chemical roop se jude, fixed ratio 1 : 8 |
| 24 carat sona | Tatva | Ek hi tarah ka kan |
| Chini | Compound | Carbon, hydrogen, oxygen jude hain, fixed ratio (formula aage seekhenge) |
| Samundar ka paani | Mixture | Paani, namak aur anya cheezein ghuli hui, anupaat badalta hai |
| Oxygen | Tatva | Ek hi tarah ka kan |
| Carbon dioxide | Compound | Carbon aur oxygen, 3 : 8 ratio mein |
| Steel | Mixture (alloy) | Loha mein thoda carbon, anupaat badal sakta hai |

**Jaanch:** Gino to 10 cheezein hain: 3 tatva (loha, sona, oxygen), 3 compound (paani, chini, carbon dioxide), 4 mixture (hawa, brass, samundar ka paani, steel). 3 + 3 + 4 = 10 ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Compound aur mixture ek hi cheez hain, dono mein kai cheezein hoti hain"**

Nahi. Mixture mein cheezein bas **saath** hoti hain, apne gun banaye rakhti hain, aur kisi bhi anupaat mein ho sakti hain. Compound mein tatva **chemical roop se jude** hote hain, naye gun lete hain aur **fixed ratio** mein hote hain. Loha + sulphur (bina garm kiye) mixture, garm karne ke baad compound.

**Galti 2: "Namak mein sodium aur chlorine ke gun milte hain"**

Nahi. Sodium khatarnak dhaatu, chlorine zehrili gas, par namak surakshit aur khaane ke kaam ka. Compound ke gun tatvon se **bilkul alag** hote hain.

**Galti 3: "Paani ek mixture hai, kyunki isme do gas (hydrogen aur oxygen) hain"**

Nahi. Paani mein hydrogen aur oxygen **gas ki tarah alag-alag** nahi hain, wo **chemical roop se jud kar** paani banate hain, aur hamesha 1 : 8 ratio mein. Paani ek **compound**, yaani pure substance.

**Galti 4: "Har dhaatu solid aur har adhaatu gas hota hai"**

Nahi. **Mercury** (dhaatu) liquid hai aur **bromine** (adhaatu) bhi liquid hai. Aur bahut se adhaatu (sulphur, carbon) solid hain.

**Galti 5: "Alloy ek compound hai, kyunki isme dhaatu pighla kar milayi jaati hai"**

Nahi. Alloy ek mixture hai: uske hisse apne gun rakhte hain aur anupaat badal sakta hai (brass 60 : 40 ya 70 : 30).

**Galti 6: "Agar cheez ko physical tareeke se nahi toda ja sakta to wo tatva hai"**

Zaroori nahi. Compound ko bhi physical tareeke se nahi toda ja sakta (paani ubalne par paani hi bhaap banta hai). Tatva wo hai jo **chemical tareeke se bhi** saral cheez mein na tute. Compound chemical tareeke (jaise bijli) se tut jaata hai.

**Galti 7: "Magnet se loha alag hota hai to iron sulphide bhi alag hoga"**

Nahi. Garm karne ke baad bana **iron sulphide** ek naya compound hai, aur magnet use nahi kheenchta. Magnet sirf loha aur sulphur ke **mixture** se loha khinch paata hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Tatva (element):** ek hi tarah ke kan ka pure substance; chemical tareeke se bhi aur saral cheez mein nahi tootta. Lagbhag 118 tatva pehchane gaye hain.
- Tatva ke teen group: **dhaatu** (chamakdaar, kadak, garmi-bijli ke achhe conductor), **adhaatu** (dull, tootne wale, kharab conductor), **upadhaatu** (beech ke gun: silicon, germanium, arsenic).
- **Mercury** (dhaatu) aur **bromine** (adhaatu) kamre ke temperature par liquid hain.
- **Yaugik (compound):** do ya zyada tatva chemical roop se **fixed ratio** mein jude, **naye gun** (paani 1 : 8, carbon dioxide 3 : 8, namak lagbhag 39% sodium aur 61% chlorine).
- Namak ke andar sodium (khatarnak dhaatu) aur chlorine (zehrili gas) chhupi hain, par namak surakshit hai: compound ke gun tatvon se alag hote hain.
- Compound pure substance hai. Mixture ko physical tareeke se alag karte hain, compound ko sirf chemical tareeke se.
- Loha + sulphur: bina garm kiye **mixture** (magnet se loha alag), garm karne ke baad **iron sulphide** compound (magnet ka asar nahi). Ye prayog sirf teacher karte hain, hawadaar jagah mein.
- **Alloys mixtures hain**, compound nahi (brass, steel, 22 carat sona).
- Family tree: Matter, pure substance (tatva, yaugik) ya mixture (homogeneous, heterogeneous).

## Practice Questions

1. Define an element and a compound in one line each. Give two examples of each.

2. Name two metals, two non-metals and two metalloids. Which one element is a metal that is liquid at room temperature, and which non-metal is also a liquid?

3. A student says, "Water has two elements, hydrogen and oxygen, so water is a mixture." Explain why this is wrong, using at least two points.

4. In water, hydrogen and oxygen are present in a mass ratio of 1 : 8. How much hydrogen and how much oxygen are present in 45 g of water?

5. In carbon dioxide, carbon and oxygen are present in a mass ratio of 3 : 8. How much oxygen is needed to completely combine with 9 g of carbon? How much carbon dioxide forms?

6. Table salt is about 39.3% sodium and 60.7% chlorine by mass. How much sodium and chlorine are there in 20 g of table salt (round to 1 decimal place)?

7. Make a table comparing a mixture and a compound on at least five points.

8. Iron and sulphur combine in a mass ratio of 7 : 4 to form iron sulphide. If 14 g of iron is heated with 12 g of sulphur, how much iron sulphide forms and how much of which substance is left over?

9. Classify each as element, compound or mixture and give a reason: (a) steel, (b) copper, (c) sugar, (d) tea with sugar, (e) air, (f) carbon dioxide.

10. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) Brass is a compound of copper and zinc.
    (b) A magnet can separate iron from a heated mixture of iron and sulphur.
    (c) All metals are solid at room temperature.
    (d) Compounds have the same properties as the elements they are made of.
    (e) A pure substance can be an element or a compound.

### Answers

1. **Element:** ek hi tarah ke kan ka pure substance, jo chemical tareeke se bhi aur saral cheez mein nahi tootta. Examples: loha, oxygen. **Compound:** do ya zyada tatva chemical roop se fixed ratio mein jude hue pure substance. Examples: paani, namak.

2. Metals: iron, copper (koi bhi do). Non-metals: sulphur, oxygen (koi bhi do). Metalloids: silicon, germanium (ya arsenic). Liquid metal: **mercury**. Liquid non-metal: **bromine**.

3. Galat, kyunki: (i) paani mein hydrogen aur oxygen **chemical roop se jude** hain, alag gas ki tarah nahi, aur paani ke gun (aag bujhana) dono gas se bilkul alag hain. (ii) Unka ratio **fixed** (1 : 8) hai, mixture ki tarah badalta nahi. (iii) Paani ko physical tareeke se (ubalne, chhanne se) hydrogen aur oxygen mein nahi toda ja sakta. Isliye paani ek **compound** hai.

4. Kul hisse = 1 + 8 = 9. Hydrogen = 45 × (1 ÷ 9) = **5 g**. Oxygen = 45 × (8 ÷ 9) = **40 g**.
   Jaanch: 5 + 40 = 45 ✅, 5 : 40 = 1 : 8 ✅

5. 3 g carbon ke liye 8 g oxygen. 9 g carbon = 3 × 3, to oxygen = 3 × 8 = **24 g**. Carbon dioxide = 9 + 24 = **33 g**.
   Jaanch: 9 : 24 = 3 : 8 ✅. 33 × (3 ÷ 11) = 9 g carbon ✅

6. Sodium = 20 × 39.3 ÷ 100 = 7.86 ≈ **7.9 g**. Chlorine = 20 × 60.7 ÷ 100 = 12.14 ≈ **12.1 g**.
   Jaanch: 7.9 + 12.1 = 20.0 g ✅

7. Koi paanch points: (1) Bana kaise: mixture mein bas mela hua, compound mein chemical roop se juda. (2) Naya substance: mixture mein nahi, compound mein haan. (3) Gun: mixture mein hisson ke gun bane rehte hain, compound mein naye gun. (4) Anupaat: mixture mein koi bhi, compound mein fixed. (5) Alag karna: mixture physical tareeke se, compound sirf chemical tareeke se. (6) Misaal: hawa/chai aur paani/namak.

8. 7 g loha ke liye 4 g sulphur chahiye. 14 g loha = 2 × 7, to sulphur = 2 × 4 = 8 g. Iron sulphide = 14 + 8 = **22 g**. Sulphur bacha = 12 − 8 = **4 g sulphur** (loha poora khatam).
   Jaanch: pehle kul 14 + 12 = 26 g; baad mein 22 + 4 = 26 g ✅

9. (a) Steel: **mixture (alloy)**, loha mein thoda carbon, anupaat badal sakta hai. (b) Copper: **element**, ek hi tarah ka kan. (c) Sugar: **compound**, carbon, hydrogen aur oxygen chemical roop se jude. (d) Tea with sugar: **mixture**, anupaat badal sakta hai. (e) Air: **mixture**, kai gas ka mel. (f) Carbon dioxide: **compound**, carbon aur oxygen 3 : 8 mein.

10. (a) **False** ❌. Brass ek **alloy yaani mixture** hai, aur copper-zinc ka anupaat badal sakta hai. (b) **False** ❌. Heat karne ke baad iron sulphide naya compound banta hai, jise magnet nahi kheenchta. (c) **False** ❌. Mercury ek dhaatu hai jo kamre ke temperature par liquid hai. (d) **False** ❌. Compound ke gun tatvon se **bilkul alag** hote hain (jaise namak aur sodium-chlorine). (e) **True** ✅. Pure substance tatva ya compound dono ho sakta hai.
