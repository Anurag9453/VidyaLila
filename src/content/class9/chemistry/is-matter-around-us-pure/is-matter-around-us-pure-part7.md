# Topic 7: Physical aur Chemical Change + Chapter Ka Saar

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki jab iron filings aur sulphur ko sirf mila diya jaaye to mixture banta hai, jisme dono ke gun bache rehte hain aur magnet se loha alag ho jaata hai. Par jab wahi mixture garam kiya gaya to ek **bilkul nayi kaali cheez** (ek yaugik) ban gayi, jise magnet ne nahi khincha. Wahan humne dekha ki kabhi cheez bas **mil** jaati hai aur kabhi **nayi cheez ban** jaati hai. Ab is farq ko poori tarah samjhte hain: kaun se badlaav mein kuch naya nahi banta (**physical change**) aur kaun se mein naya padaarth ban jaata hai (**chemical change**). Phir chapter ke saare 7 topics ka hisaab ek table mein rakhenge.

## Zang lagi gate aur pighalti kulfi

Monsoon ke baad ek dopahar Mr. Shetty, jo mohalle ke house painter hain, Eshan ke ghar ka purana loha ka gate scrape kar rahe the. Gate par bhoore-narangi rang ki parat jami thi: **zang (rust)**. Jab Mr. Shetty ne wire brush ghisa, to zang bhurbhura kar jhad gayi, par neeche ka loha jagah-jagah patla aur gadhedaar ho chuka tha.

Eshan haath mein kulfi liye khada tha. Garmi itni thi ki kulfi pighal kar uski ungliyon par tapakne lagi. Usne jaldi se kulfi ko chaata, phir gate ki taraf dekha. "Uncle, kulfi bhi badal rahi hai aur gate bhi badal gaya. Dono mein kya fark hai?"

Mr. Shetty hanse, "Dhyan se dekh. Kulfi pighli to wo abhi bhi kulfi hi hai: wahi doodh, wahi cheeni, bas thandi aur thos se narm aur tarl ho gayi. Dobara freezer mein rakh de, phir jam jaayegi. Par ye zang? Ye loha **thi hi nahi**. Ye ek nayi cheez hai jo loha, hawa aur paani ke milne se bani. Is zang ko dobara loha banane ka koi aasaan tareeka mere paas nahi hai. Isiliye main isse scrape karke hata deta hoon aur upar se paint kar deta hoon, taaki naya zang na bane."

Eshan ne socha: "To ek badlaav mein cheez wahi rehti hai, aur doosre mein nayi cheez ban jaati hai?" Mr. Shetty ne sir hilaya: "Bilkul. Chal, aaj isi ko poori tarah samajhte hain."

## Part A: Physical change: cheez wahi, roop alag

Physical change mein padaarth ka **naam, pehchan aur kaam wahi rehta hai**, bas uska bahari roop badalta hai: aakaar, size, state ya mixture ka hona. Particles ki jodi nahi badalti, sirf unka arrangement ya ek jagah se doosri jagah jaana badalta hai.

Kuch aam physical changes:

- **State badalna:** barf ka paani, paani ki bhaap, kapoor ka seedha gas ban jaana (sublimation). Ye sab Chapter 1 mein dekhe the. Kulfi ka pighalna bhi yahi hai.
- **Shape ya size badalna:** kaagaz ko phaadna, lakdi ko kaatna, chalk ko pees kar powder banana, dhaatu ko hathode se peet kar patra banana, taar kheench kar lamba karna.
- **Dissolving:** namak ya cheeni ko paani mein ghol dena. Ye Topic 2 mein dekha tha. Ghol mein namak ya cheeni ke particles bas paani ke beech faile hain, naye padaarth nahi bane.
- **Mixture banana aur alag karna:** Topic 4 aur 5 ke saare tareeke (chhaanna, filtration, distillation, crystallisation, magnet) **physical** tareeke hain, kyunki unse bas cheezein alag hoti hain, koi nayi cheez nahi banti.

**Dissolving physical kyun hai?** Ek chhota numerical dekhte hain.

**Example 1.** 20 g cheeni ko 100 g paani mein ghola. Ghol ka kul vazan kitna hoga? Phir sara paani evaporate kar do to kya bachega?

- Ghol ka vazan = 20 g + 100 g = **120 g**.
- Paani evaporate hone par (Chapter 1 wala evaporation) wapas bachegi **wahi 20 g cheeni** (crystals ke roop mein).

Cheeni wapas aa gayi, matlab cheeni kisi nayi cheez mein badli hi nahi thi: sirf paani mein chhup gayi thi. Isliye dissolving **physical change** hai. Jaanch: 20 + 100 = 120 ✅, aur sookhne par 20 g cheeni wahi wapas ✅

Physical change ki pehchan: aksar **reversible** hota hai (sab kuch palat sakta hai, jaise barf-paani-barf), aur naya padaarth nahi banta. Par ye "aksar" hai, "hamesha" nahi: kaagaz ke tukde karna palatna mushkil hai, phir bhi wo physical change hai, kyunki har tukda abhi bhi kaagaz hi hai.

## Part B: Chemical change: nayi cheez ka janm

Chemical change mein purani cheezein khatam hokar **nayi cheezein** banti hain, jinka **rang, gun, ya swaad** purani cheezon se alag hota hai.

Kuch chemical changes jo tum roz dekhte ho:

- **Rusting (zang lagna):** loha + hawa + paani se bhoora, bhurbhura zang. Zang mein loha ke gun nahi hain: wo na chamakta hai, na kheencha jaata hai, na mudta hai, bas jhad jaata hai.
- **Jalna (burning):** lakdi, kaagaz, LPG, mombatti jalte hain to raakh, dhuan aur gases ban jaate hain. Raakh ko wapas lakdi mein nahi badla ja sakta.
- **Doodh ka phatna / dahi jamna:** doodh khatta hokar paneer jaisa ban jaata hai.
- **Khaana pakana:** roti sekne par aate ka rang, swaad aur sakhti badal jaati hai; wo wapas aata nahi banti. Pakke chawal wapas kachche nahi ho sakte.
- **Digestion (khaana hazam hona):** khaane ke bade kan pet mein chhote, naye padaarthon mein tootte hain jinhe sharir use kar sake.
- **Fal ka pakna aur sadna:** kela peela aur narm hota hai, phir kaala; pakaa fal aur sadi hui sabzi ka swaad aur smell alag ho jaata hai.
- **Iron + sulphur ko garam karna:** Topic 6 ki kaali, non-magnetic cheez, ek naya yaugik.

Chemical change ki pehchan: **naya padaarth** banta hai, aur aksar **irreversible** hota hai (palatna bahut mushkil ya bina khaas tareeke ke naamumkin).

> **Chemical reaction ki ek jhalak:** jab bhi chemical change hota hai, use chemistry mein **chemical reaction** kehte hain. Purani cheezein (jaise loha, hawa, paani) **reactants** kehlaati hain aur nayi cheezein (zang) **products**. Reactions ko likhne ka tareeka, aur unke prakaar, tum Chapter 7 "Chemical Changes and Reactions" mein detail se seekhoge. Abhi bas ye pehchaanna kaafi hai ki kab nayi cheez bani.

## Part C: Chemical change ke sanket (clues)

Naya padaarth banta hai ye aankh se seedha nahi dikhta, par kuch **sanket (clues)** hote hain jo humein shak karne ki wajah dete hain:

```
   CHEMICAL CHANGE ke clues

        naya rang              gas / bubbles
        (zang: bhoora)         (nimbu + baking soda)
                 \                 /
                  \               /
                  +---------------+
     nayi gandh --+ NAYA PADAARTH +-- garmi ya roshni
  (doodh phatna)  +---------------+   (jalna)
                          │
                          v
                thos nayi cheez banna
               (doodh se paneer, zang)
```

> **Figure 7.1** – Chemical change ke paanch aam clues: naya rang, gas ya bubbles, nayi gandh, garmi ya roshni nikalna, aur koi nayi thos cheez banna.

Dhyan do: ye clues sirf **shak** dete hain, pakka saboot nahi. Kuch physical changes mein bhi inme se ek clue aa sakta hai:

- **Paani ubalne par bubbles** aate hain, par wo bas paani ki bhaap hai: physical change.
- **Bijli ke bulb ka filament** garam hokar chamakta hai aur garmi deta hai, par bulb band karne par wahi filament wapas aa jaata hai: physical change.
- **Paani mein rang ghol dena:** rang naya dikhta hai, par wo bas dissolving hai.

Isliye pakka sawaal ye hai: **"Kya naya padaarth bana, jiska gun purane se alag ho?"** Clues sirf shuruaati ishaara hain.

## Part D: Tricky cases: dono badlaav ek saath

**1. Jalti hui mombatti: dono!** Mombatti ka wax batti ke aas-paas pighalta hai (physical change, jam kar phir wax ban jaata hai). Par jo wax-bhaap batti ke dhaage ke paas lau mein jalti hai, wo naye padaarth (gases, thodi kaalikh) mein badal jaati hai: **chemical change**. Ek hi jagah par dono hota hai.

**2. Anda ubalna:** Anda ubalne par andar ka paardarshi, behta hua hissa sakht aur safed ho jaata hai, aur peela hissa bhi jam jaata hai. Ab usse wapas kaccha anda nahi banaya ja sakta. Ye **chemical change** hai. (Ubalne ke liye paani ka garam hona physical hai, par anda khud badal gaya.)

**3. Namak ko paani mein daalna:** Physical. Par agar kisi cheez ko paani mein daalne par bubbles, garmi ya naya rang aaye, to ye sirf dissolving nahi, ek reaction ho sakta hai. Isliye clues dekhna zaroori hai.

**4. Sublimation (kapoor, naphthalene, dry ice):** Solid seedha gas ban gaya, par cheez wahi hai. **Physical.** Kapoor ko jalaya jaaye to wo chemical change hai, par wo alag baat hai.

**5. Loha ko magnet banana:** Loha ke kan ek hi taraf sajte hain, par loha loha hi rehta hai: **physical.**

**6. Mixture banana versus compound banana:** Topic 6 yaad karo. Iron + sulphur ko bas milana = physical (mixture). Garam karna = chemical (compound). Wahi do cheezein, par badlaav ka prakaar alag.

## Part E: Ghar par safe activities

### Activity 1: Baking soda aur nimbu

**Kya chahiye:** ek steel ya plastic ka glass, ek chammach baking soda, nimbu ka ras, thoda paani.

**Kaise karein:**
1. Glass mein ek chhoti chammach baking soda daalo.
2. Dheere dheere nimbu ka ras daalo.
3. Dekho: kya hua?

**Kya dikhega:** turant **fizzing** hogi: bahut saare bubbles uthenge, aur kabhi glass thoda thanda mehsoos ho sakta hai. Ye bubbles ek **nayi gas** ke hain (carbon dioxide, naam yaad rakhna, formula aage seekhoge).

**Kyun chemical change hai?** Baking soda aur nimbu ke khatte padaarth (acid) mil kar naye padaarth banate hain, jisme gas bhi hai. Gas ka banna naye padaarth ka sanket hai. Dobara se wahi baking soda aur nimbu ras wapas nahi mil sakte.

**Safety:** Ye bilkul safe hai, par glass ko kisi **band bottle mein na karo** (gas bharne se dabav badh sakta hai). Khuli jagah, bade ki nigrani mein karo, aur aankhon mein ras na jaane do. Kuch chakhna nahi hai.

**Control (tulna) ke liye:** Alag glass mein baking soda ko sirf paani mein dalo. Thoda sa ghulega, koi fizzing nahi hogi. Ye dissolving (physical) hai. Dono glass tulna karne par farak saaf dikhta hai.

### Activity 2: Loha ki keel aur zang (kuch din ka prayog)

**Kya chahiye:** teen saaf loha ki keelein (nail), teen shishe ke glass ya jar, paani, thoda sa tel ya cooking oil.

**Kaise karein:**
1. Glass A mein sookhi keel rakho (bina paani).
2. Glass B mein keel ko aadhe se zyada paani mein dubo do.
3. Glass C mein paani daalo, keel dubao, aur upar se paani ki satah par tel ki patli parat daal do (taaki paani mein hawa na ghule).
4. Teeno glass ko 5 se 7 din tak chhod do, roz dekho.

**Kya dikhega:** Glass B ki keel par, khaaskar paani ki satah ke paas, **bhoora zang** dikhega. A (sookhi) aur C (tel wali) mein zang bahut kam ya nahi hoga.

**Kya seekha:** zang lagne ke liye **hawa (oxygen) aur paani** dono chahiye. Jahan inme se ek bhi nahi hai, wahan zang nahi laga. Isiliye gate par paint ya tel lagate hain: wo loha ko hawa aur paani se door rakhte hain. Samudra ke paas (namkeen hawa) zang aur tezi se lagta hai, isliye wahan gate jaldi kharab hote hain.

**Safety:** zang lagi keelein haath mein sambhal ke pakdo, kati-chheel se bachao, aur kaam ke baad haath dho lo. **Kuch jalana nahi hai**; jalne wale kisi bhi prayog ko hamesha bade ke saath hi karo.

## Part G: Solved reasoning examples

**Example 2. Neeche diye badlaav physical hain ya chemical?** (a) Chalk ko pees kar powder banana, (b) paneer ke liye doodh phatna, (c) sooraj ki garmi se samudra ka paani sookhna, (d) loha ke gate par zang lagna, (e) cheeni ko tawe par garam karte hue kaala hona.

- (a) **Physical.** Chalk ke tukde chote hue par chalk wahi hai.
- (b) **Chemical.** Doodh khatta ho kar paneer aur paani mein badla; naya padaarth bana, wapas doodh nahi banta.
- (c) **Physical.** Paani bhaap ban gaya (evaporation), namak peeche bacha; ye wapas paani mein badla ja sakta hai.
- (d) **Chemical.** Zang ek nayi cheez hai, loha nahi.
- (e) **Chemical.** Cheeni jal kar kaali ho gayi: naya padaarth bana, wapas cheeni nahi mil sakti. (Garam karne par cheeni pehle pighalti bhi hai, wo hissa physical hai.)

**Example 3. Raunak kehta hai: "Bubbles uthe, isliye ye pakka chemical change hai." Kya Raunak theek hai?**

Nahi. Ubalte paani mein bhi bubbles uthte hain, par wo bas paani ki bhaap hai, naya padaarth nahi. Pakka sawal ye hai ki **naya padaarth bana ya nahi.** Nimbu aur baking soda mein bubbles naye gas ke the, isliye wahan chemical change tha; ubalte paani mein wahi paani gas ban gaya, isliye wahan physical.

**Example 4. Ek mombatti ka wax pighal kar neeche jam gaya. Phir batti ke upar ka wax jal gaya. Kaun sa physical, kaun sa chemical?**

Pighal kar jamna: **physical**, kyunki wax wahi hai, ye palat sakta hai (dobara pighla sakte ho). Lau mein jalna: **chemical**, kyunki wax ki bhaap naye padaarthon mein badal gayi (gases aur kaalikh) jo wapas wax nahi bante.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Jo badlaav palat jaaye wahi physical hai aur jo na palte wahi chemical"**

Ye lagbhag sahi hai, par pakka rule nahi. Kaagaz ko phaadna palatna mushkil hai, par wo physical hai. Isliye asli sawal hai: **"Naya padaarth bana?"**

**Galti 2: "Gas ke bubbles dikhe, matlab chemical change"**

Ubalte paani ke bubbles bhaap ke hain, naya padaarth nahi. Clues sirf shak dete hain.

**Galti 3: "Dissolving ek chemical change hai kyunki cheeni gayab ho gayi"**

Cheeni gayab nahi hui, paani mein faili hui hai. Paani sookhne par cheeni wapas milti hai. Ye physical change hai.

**Galti 4: "Garmi ya roshni nikle to chemical change hi hoga"**

Bijli ke bulb ka filament garam hokar chamakta hai, par wahi filament bana rehta hai. Physical.

**Galti 5: "Mombatti sirf chemical change hai"**

Wax ka pighalna physical hai aur uska jalna chemical hai. Ek hi cheez mein dono ho sakte hain.

**Galti 6: "Chemical change ka matlab hamesha khatarnak ya zehrili cheez"**

Nahi. Roti sekna, khaana hazam hona aur dahi jamna, sab chemical change hain aur roz ki zindagi ka hissa hain.

## Quick Recap

- **Physical change:** naya padaarth nahi banta; cheez wahi rehti hai, bas aakaar, size, state ya mixture badalta hai. Aksar reversible. Misaal: pighalna, dissolving, kaagaz phadna, sublimation.
- **Chemical change:** naya padaarth banta hai jiske gun alag hote hain. Aksar irreversible. Misaal: zang, jalna, doodh phatna, khaana pakna, digestion. Ise chemical reaction bhi kehte hain (Chapter 7 mein poori tarah).
- **Clues:** naya rang, gas ya bubbles, nayi gandh, garmi ya roshni, nayi thos cheez. Ye sirf shak dete hain: ubalte paani ke bubbles aur bulb ke filament ki roshni physical change hain.
- **Tricky cases:** mombatti mein dono badlaav; anda ubalna chemical; dissolving aur sublimation physical.
- **Zang** ke liye hawa aur paani dono chahiye; paint ya tel unhe loha se door rakhte hain.
- **Chapter ka saar:** matter ya to pure substance (element, compound) hai ya mixture (homogeneous, heterogeneous). Mixtures ko unke alag gun ke aadhaar par physical tareeke se alag karte hain; compound ko alag karne ke liye chemical change chahiye.

6. A burning candle shows both a physical and a chemical change. Identify each.

8. Is separating a mixture of iron filings and sulphur with a magnet a physical or a chemical change? What about heating the same mixture strongly?

9. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
   (a) In a physical change a new substance is always formed.
   (b) Rusting is a chemical change.
   (c) Every change that is hard to reverse is a chemical change.
   (d) A glowing bulb filament shows a chemical change because it gives off light.

10. Make a tree diagram showing how all matter is classified in this chapter, from matter down to element, compound, homogeneous mixture and heterogeneous mixture. Give one example at each end.

### Answers

1. **Physical change:** wo badlaav jisme koi naya padaarth nahi banta (barf pighalna, kaagaz phadna). **Chemical change:** wo badlaav jisme naya padaarth banta hai jiske gun alag hote hain (zang lagna, doodh phatna).

2. (a) **Physical**: ice cream wahi hai, bas thos se tarl. (b) **Chemical**: nayi gandh, naya swaad, naye padaarth. (c) **Physical**: kaagaz abhi bhi kaagaz hai. (d) **Chemical**: chawal ka swaad aur gun badal jaate hain. (e) **Physical**: namak paani sookhne par wapas milta hai. (f) **Physical** (sublimation): solid seedha gas bana, wahi camphor.

3. Clues: naya rang, gas ya bubbles, nayi gandh, garmi ya roshni, nayi thos cheez banna (koi bhi chaar). Inhe "clues" isliye kehte hain kyunki physical change mein bhi inme se kuch aa sakte hain (ubalte paani ke bubbles, bulb ki roshni). Pakka saboot hai **naya padaarth banna**.

4. Baking soda aur nimbu ke ras mein bubbles ek **naye gas** ke hain, isliye chemical change. Ubalte paani mein bubbles wahi paani ki bhaap hain: sirf state badli, naya padaarth nahi, isliye physical.

5. (a) 30 + 120 = **150 g**. (b) Evaporate hone par wahi **30 g namak** bachega. (c) **Physical change**: namak badla nahi, wapas mil gaya. Jaanch: 30 + 120 = 150 ✅ aur 30 g wapas = shuruat jaisa ✅

6. Wax ka pighalna aur dobara jamna = **physical**. Batti ke paas wax ki bhaap ka lau mein jalna (gases aur kaalikh ban-na) = **chemical**.

7. Zang ke liye **hawa aur paani (nami)** dono chahiye. Sookhi hawa mein nami kam hoti hai, isliye zang kam lagta hai. Bachaane ke tareeke: **paint karna**, **tel ya grease lagana** (kisi bhi do).

8. Magnet se alag karna **physical** hai (dono cheezein wahi hain, bas alag hui). Strong garam karna **chemical** hai (nayi kaali cheez banti hai).

9. (a) **False** ❌. Physical change mein naya padaarth nahi banta; chemical change mein banta hai. (b) **True** ✅. Zang naya padaarth hai. (c) **False** ❌. Kaagaz phadna palatna mushkil hai par physical hai. (d) **False** ❌. Filament garam hokar roshni deta hai par wahi filament rehta hai: physical.

10. Matter se do shaakhaein: **Pure substance** aur **Mixture**. Pure substance se **Element** (jaise loha, sona) aur **Compound** (jaise paani, namak). Mixture se **Homogeneous** (jaise namak ka ghol, brass) aur **Heterogeneous** (jaise mitti-paani, salad).
