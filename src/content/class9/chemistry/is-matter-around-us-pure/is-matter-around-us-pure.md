# Topic 1: Pure aur Mixture: Shuddh Ka Matlab Kya Hai

Chapter 1 mein humne seekha ki har cheez **matter** hai, matter **particles (kan)** se bani hai, aur wahi particles kaise baithe hain us se solid, liquid ya gas ban jaata hai. Wahan hum zyadatar "ek hi cheez" ki baat kar rahe the: barf, paani, bhaap. Par ghar ke aas-paas dekho: doodh, hawa, samundar ka paani, mitti, sabzi ka salad. Kya ye sab bhi "ek hi cheez" hain? Ya inme kai cheezein mili hui hain? Is chapter ka sawaal yahi hai: **jo matter humein ghere hue hai, kya wo shuddh (pure) hai?** Aur sabse pehle ye samajhna hai ki science "shuddh" ka matlab kya leta hai.

## "100% shuddh shahad" ka board

Mr. Gokhale apne bhanje Advait ko saath leke bazaar gaye the. Ek dukaan ke bahar bada sa board laga tha: **"100% shuddh shahad"**. Advait ne board padha aur turant bola, "Mama, ye to bilkul pure hoga na? 100% likha hai!"

Mr. Gokhale muskuraaye. "Chalo, dukaandar se pooch ke dekhte hain ki isme kya-kya hai." Dukaandar ne bataya, "Saahab, isme sirf madhumakkhi ka shahad hai. Na cheeni, na paani upar se milaya." Advait ne khush hokar kaha, "Dekha mama!"

Par Mr. Gokhale ne ek aur sawaal kiya, "Advait, tumne kabhi ek glass paani mein do chammach cheeni ghol kar piya hai? Use tum 'shuddh paani' kahoge ya 'meetha paani'?" Advait soch mein pad gaya. "Meetha paani... lekin us mein bhi to paani hi paani dikhta hai!"

"Bas yahin se sawaal shuru hota hai," bole Mr. Gokhale. "Dukaan ke board par **'shuddh'** ka matlab hai 'milawat nahi hai'. Par science jab 'pure' kehta hai, to uska matlab thoda alag aur bahut sakht hota hai. Aao dekhein kaise."

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Pure substance (shuddh padaarth)** | Aisa matter jisme **sirf ek hi tarah ke particles** hon | Distilled paani, cheeni ka dana, sone ka tukda |
| **Mixture (mishran)** | Do ya zyada alag-alag cheezein, jo bina naya padaarth banaye mil gayi hon | Nimbu-paani, mitti, salad |

## Part A: Daily life ka "shuddh" aur science ka "pure"

**Daily life mein** hum "shuddh" ka matlab kuch aisa lete hain:

- "Isme milawat nahi hai": shuddh doodh (paani nahi mila), shuddh ghee (dalda nahi mila).
- "Ye saaf aur safe hai": shuddh paani (jo peene layak ho, bacteria-free ho).
- "Asli hai, nakli nahi": 24 carat ka shuddh sona.

**Science mein** "pure" ka matlab ek sakht aur seedhi shart hai:

> **Pure substance wo hai jiske saare particles ek hi tarah ke hon.** Usme kuch aur mila hi nahi hai, chahe thoda sa bhi.

Is shart ko pakad lo to ek dilchasp baat nikalti hai: daily life ka "shuddh" aur science ka "pure" aksar **alag** nikalte hain.

- **Dukaan ka shuddh shahad:** sirf madhumakkhi ka shahad, upar se kuch nahi mila, to daily life mein shuddh. Par science ki nazar mein shahad mein **cheeni ki kai kisme, paani aur aur bhi kuch cheezein** milti hain. Matlab shahad ek **mixture** hai, pure substance nahi. Dukaandar jhooth nahi bol raha: uske liye "shuddh" ka matlab "milawat rahit" hai.
- **Bottle band "mineral water":** peene ke liye saaf hai, daily life mein shuddh. Par usme thode se namak jaise **minerals ghule hain**, aur yahi "mineral" naam ki wajah hai. Science ke liye ye mixture hai.
- **Distilled paani** (jo aksar battery mein ya prayogshala mein use hota hai): isme sirf paani ke particles hote hain, to ye **science ka pure substance** hai. Par ise peene ke liye kehna achha nahi, kyunki isme wo minerals nahi hote jo body ko roz chahiye. Yaani jo **science ka pure hai, wo peene ke liye "behtar" ho, ye zaroori nahi**.

Isliye dhyan rakho:

| Soch | Daily life ka "shuddh" | Science ka "pure" |
|---|---|---|
| Matlab | Milawat nahi, saaf, safe, asli | Sirf ek hi tarah ke particles |
| Shahad | Sirf madhumakkhi ka hai, to shuddh | Kai cheezein mili hain, to mixture |
| Mineral water | Peene ke liye shuddh | Namak vagairah ghule hain, to mixture |
| Distilled paani | Shayad "bekaar" (swaad nahi) | Pure substance |

Yaani yahan koi jhagda nahi hai: dukaandar ki baat sahi hai aur science ki baat bhi sahi hai, bas dono ke "pure" ka matlab alag hai. **Is chapter mein jahan bhi "pure" likha hoga, wo science wala matlab hoga.**

## Part B: Pure substance aur Mixture: dono ki pehchaan

Matter ko do bade dibbon mein baant sakte hain:

1. **Pure substance:** sirf ek hi tarah ke particles. (Ek hi cheez.)
2. **Mixture:** do ya zyada alag-alag pure cheezein mili hui.

Particles ki tasveer mein ye fark saaf dikhta hai. Maan lo ek hi tarah ke particles **o** se dikhaye gaye hain, aur doosri tarah ke **x** se.

```
   Pure substance (sirf ek tarah ke particles)

      o  o  o  o  o  o
      o  o  o  o  o  o
      o  o  o  o  o  o
      o  o  o  o  o  o
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Pure substance: saare particles ek hi tarah ke (sab "o"). Ye particles atoms ho sakte hain ya molecules; ye hum chapter "Atoms and Molecules" mein seekhenge. Abhi bas "ek hi tarah ke kan" yaad rakho.

Ab mixture ko dekho. Usme ek se zyada tarah ke particles hain.

```
   Mixture (do tarah ke particles, bilkul mile hue)

      o  x  o  o  x  o
      x  o  o  x  o  x
      o  o  x  o  o  o
      x  o  o  x  o  x
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Homogeneous mixture: "o" aur "x" dono tarah ke particles poori tarah mile hue. Kahin se bhi ek chhota sa hissa le lo, to usme dono ka wahi anupaat milega.

Aur ek tarah ka mixture aisa bhi hota hai jisme alag-alag cheezein **alag-alag ilaakon mein** simti rahti hain.

```
   Heterogeneous mixture (alag-alag patch)

      o  o  o  o     x  x  x
      o  o  o  o     x  x  x
      o  o  o  o  o  x  x  x
      o  o  o  o  o  x  x  x
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Heterogeneous mixture: "o" ek taraf, "x" doosri taraf, beech mein saaf sima. Kahin "o" zyada, kahin "x" zyada.

(Upar ki tasveeron mein sirf mixture ka **idea** dikhaya gaya hai. Asli mixture mein bahut hi zyada particles hote hain, aur haan: gas ya liquid mixture mein particles aise sajaye hue nahi, balki chalte-phirte hote hain, jaisa tumne Chapter 1 mein padha.)

### Pure substance ki kuch pehchaan

- Usme **sirf ek hi tarah ka padaarth** hota hai. Jaise paani (distilled), cheeni, namak, sona, loha.
- Pure substance ka **fixed melting point aur boiling point** hota hai. Chapter 1 mein tumne dekha tha ki barf 0 °C par pighalti hai. Ye pure barf (pure paani) ki baat thi.
- Pure substance ko hum **seedhe** alag-alag cheezon mein nahi tod sakte, kam se kam physical tareekon se (chhaanna, ubaalna waghairah).

### Mixture ki kuch pehchaan

Mixture ki teen badi baatein hain:

1. **Hissa apni pehchaan nahi khota (properties retained).** Mixture mein har ghatak (constituent) apni asli properties rakhta hai. Cheeni ghole paani mein cheeni ka meethapan abhi bhi hai, paani ki taral prakruti bhi abhi hai.
2. **Koi fixed composition nahi.** Tum chaaho to chai mein aadha chammach cheeni daalo, ya do chammach. Dono chai hi hain. Mixture mein ghatak **kisi bhi anupaat mein** mil sakte hain.
3. **Mixture ko aasaan tareekon se alag kiya ja sakta hai.** Chhaanna, thehrana, ubaalna, chumbak jaise physical tareeke kaam aate hain. Ye hum Topic 4 aur 5 mein dekhenge.

Ek chhoti si jaanch karo: **Kya chai mixture hai?** Usme paani hai, doodh hai, cheeni hai, chai ki patti ka ras hai, aur kai ingredients ka anupaat tumhari pasand par depend karta hai. Haan, chai ek mixture hai.

## Part C: Homogeneous aur Heterogeneous mixture

Sabhi mixtures ek jaise nahi dikhte. Isiliye unhe do tarah mein baanta jaata hai.

### Homogeneous mixture (ek jaisa mixture)

- **Poore mixture mein har jagah ek jaisa**: ek jaisa rang, ek jaisa swaad, ek jaisi banaavat.
- Ghatak aise mil jaate hain ki **aankh se alag nahi dikhte**, microscope se bhi nahi (aam microscope se).
- Isko ek bartan mein se kahin se bhi thoda sa le lo to **usme wahi anupaat** milega.

Examples:

- **Cheeni ya namak ka paani**
- **Hawa** (jab saaf ho, bina dhuen ke)
- **Peetal (brass)** aur **steel**
- **Sirka (vinegar)**, **nimbu-paani** (jab achhi tarah ghola ho aur beej/gudde na ho)

Homogeneous mixture ko **solution (ghol)** bhi kehte hain, aur uske baare mein hum agle topic mein padhenge.

### Heterogeneous mixture (alag-alag dikhne wala mixture)

- Poore mixture mein **alag-alag jagah par alag-alag cheezein** dikhti hain.
- Ghatak ke beech **saaf sima** hoti hai, aur aksar hum unhe **aankh se hi pehchaan** lete hain.
- Do jagah se sample lo, to hosakta hai dono mein **anupaat alag** ho.

Examples:

- **Salad** (kheera, tamatar, pyaaz alag-alag dikhte hain)
- **Mitti** (ret, kankar, sadi hui patte, aur bahut kuch)
- **Paani mein ret ya kankar**
- **Dal-chawal ki khichdi** jab usme sabzi ke tukde dikhein
- **Rai aur namak ka mixture**, **masoor dal mein kankar**

### Fark ek jhalak mein

| Baat | Homogeneous | Heterogeneous |
|---|---|---|
| Dikhta kaisa hai | Har jagah ek jaisa | Alag-alag hisse alag dikhte hain |
| Ghatak aankh se alag dikhte hain? | Nahi | Haan, aksar |
| Kahin se bhi sample lo | Wahi anupaat | Anupaat badal sakta hai |
| Example | Cheeni ka paani, hawa, peetal | Salad, mitti, paani mein ret |

**Ek chhota sa trick:** agar sochna pad raha ho ki kaun sa type hai, to apne aap se poochho: *"Kya mixture ke andar ghatak alag-alag dikhte hain?"* Haan to heterogeneous, nahi to homogeneous. (Ek chhoti si chhoot: doodh, kohra, dhuaan aur syahi jaise kuch mixtures dikhne mein ek jaise lagte hain, par asal mein heterogeneous hote hain, kyunki unke kan solution ke kanon se kaafi bade hote hain. Inki kahani Topic 3 mein aayegi. Isliye in cheezon ko abhi homogeneous ki list mein nahi rakha hai.)

## Part D: Teen dilchasp cases: hawa, samundar ka paani aur alloys

### 1. Hawa: ek mixture, ek hi cheez nahi

Hum hawa ko roz "ek cheez" ki tarah bolte hain, jaise "hawa chal rahi hai". Par hawa mein kai gas mili hui hain. **Saaf, sookhi hawa** ke 100 hisse lo to lagbhag:

- **Nitrogen:** lagbhag 78 hisse
- **Oxygen:** lagbhag 21 hisse
- **Baaki gases** (argon, carbon dioxide aur kai aur): lagbhag 1 hissa

**Jaanch:** 78 + 21 + 1 = 100 ✅

Hawa **homogeneous mixture** hai kyunki gases aapas mein poori tarah mil jaati hain aur har jagah ek jaisa mixture milta hai. Hawa **mixture** isliye hai kyunki:

- usme alag-alag gases apni **asli properties** rakhti hain (oxygen jalne mein madad karti hai, nitrogen nahi);
- wo **kisi bhi anupaat** mein mil sakti hain (band kamre mein carbon dioxide zyada, aur nami har din alag);
- unhe aasaan tareeke se alag kiya ja sakta hai (hum ye Topic 5 mein dekhenge ki hawa ko pehle thanda karke liquid banate hain aur phir alag karte hain).

(Hawa mein pani ki bhaap bhi hoti hai, jo jagah aur mausam ke hisaab se badalti rehti hai. Isliye hawa mein hisse **fixed nahi** hote, yahi mixture ki pehchaan hai.)

### 2. Samundar ka paani

Samundar ke paani mein **namak ghula hua** hai, aur bhi bahut kuch. Ek lagbhag aankda: **1 kg samundar ke paani mein lagbhag 35 g** mineral salts hote hain, jisme zyadatar wahi namak hai jo hum khaate hain. Matlab 1000 g paani mein 35 g, yaani **3.5%**.

**Jaanch:** 35 ÷ 1000 × 100 = 3.5% ✅

Par ye anupaat **har samundar mein ek jaisa nahi** hota. Kuch jagah par kam, kuch band samundar mein (jaise Dead Sea) bahut zyada hota hai. Is baat se pata chalta hai ki samundar ka paani **mixture** hai, kyunki uska anupaat fixed nahi. Aur wo ek **homogeneous mixture** hai, kyunki ek baar ghul jaane par har jagah se ek jaisa dikhta hai.

### 3. Alloys: dhaatu bhi mixture ho sakti hai

Ye tumhe chauka sakta hai: **thos cheezein bhi mixture ho sakti hain.** Jab do ya zyada dhaatuon ko pighla kar mila diya jaata hai aur phir thanda kiya jaata hai, to ek **alloy** banti hai.

- **Peetal (brass):** taamba (copper) aur jasta (zinc) ka mixture. Aam taur par lagbhag 60 se 70% taamba aur 30 se 40% jasta.
- **Steel:** loha (iron) mein bahut thoda sa carbon (aam steel mein lagbhag 0.2% se 1% ke beech). Kuch steel mein chromium, nickel jaise aur ghatak bhi hote hain (jaise stainless steel).

**Alloy ko mixture kyun kehte hain, naya padaarth kyun nahi?**

- Peetal ka anupaat **badal sakta hai**: ek tarah ka peetal 70% taamba wala, doosra 60% wala, dono peetal hi hain. Pure substance mein aisa nahi hota.
- Peetal mein taambe aur jaste ki **kuch properties** abhi bhi hain, bas mil jaane par sakht ya chamakdaar ban gaye.
- Haan, alloy ke dhaatuon ko aasaan physical tareekon se alag karna mushkil hota hai. Phir bhi upar ki do baaton (anupaat badal sakta hai, ghatak ki properties bachi rehti hain) ki wajah se alloy ko mixture hi maana jaata hai.

**Alloy kyun banate hain?** Alloy aksar apne ghatak dhaatuon se **zyada majboot, zyada sakht, ya zang (rust) se bachne wali** hoti hai. Steel loha se zyada kaam ki cheez hai aur stainless steel mein zang nahi lagta, isiliye hum bartan aur chammach stainless steel ke banate hain.

Alloy **homogeneous mixture** mana jaata hai, kyunki pighle hue mixture mein dhaatu poori tarah mil jaati hain aur thanda hone ke baad bhi uski banaavat ek jaisi dikhti hai.

## Part E: Solved examples

**Example 1 (pure ya mixture?)**
Neeche di gayi cheezon mein se pure substance aur mixture alag karo: (a) distilled paani, (b) doodh, (c) cheeni ka dana, (d) hawa, (e) sone ka bina milawat wala tukda, (f) mitti.

- **Pure substance** (sirf ek tarah ke particles): (a) distilled paani, (c) cheeni, (e) sona.
- **Mixture** (kai tarah ke particles): (b) doodh (paani, chikna, protein, cheeni ka mixture), (d) hawa (kai gases), (f) mitti (ret, kankar, sadi hui cheezein).

**Jaanch:** teen pure aur teen mixture, kul 6 cheezein ✅

**Example 2 (homogeneous ya heterogeneous?)**
Har ek ko batao: (a) namak ka paani, (b) khichdi jisme sabzi ke tukde dikhte hain, (c) peetal, (d) sirka (vinegar), (e) paani mein kankar.

- (a) **Homogeneous**: namak aisa ghul jaata hai ki alag dikhta hi nahi.
- (b) **Heterogeneous**: sabzi ke tukde alag dikhte hain.
- (c) **Homogeneous**: alloy poori tarah ek jaisi dikhti hai.
- (d) **Homogeneous**: sirke mein uska khatta padaarth paani mein poori tarah ghula hai, har jagah ek jaisa.
- (e) **Heterogeneous**: kankar aankh se alag dikhte hain.

**Jaanch:** (b) aur (e) mein ghatak alag dikhte hain, baaki teen mein nahi ✅

**Example 3 (salad ki composition)**
Ek plate mein 200 g salad hai: 80 g kheera, 70 g tamatar aur 50 g pyaaz. Kheera kitne percent hai? Agar doosri plate mein 200 g salad mein 50 g kheera, 100 g tamatar aur 50 g pyaaz ho, to kya dono salad ek jaise hain?

- Plate 1: kheera = 80 ÷ 200 × 100 = **40%**, tamatar = 70 ÷ 200 × 100 = **35%**, pyaaz = 50 ÷ 200 × 100 = **25%**.
- Plate 2: kheera = 50 ÷ 200 × 100 = **25%**, tamatar = 100 ÷ 200 × 100 = **50%**, pyaaz = 50 ÷ 200 × 100 = **25%**.
- Dono plate mein anupaat **alag** hai, phir bhi dono salad hi hain. Yaani **mixture ki composition fixed nahi hoti**.

**Jaanch:** Plate 1: 80 + 70 + 50 = 200 aur 40 + 35 + 25 = 100% ✅. Plate 2: 50 + 100 + 50 = 200 aur 25 + 50 + 25 = 100% ✅

**Example 4 (hawa mein oxygen kitni?)**
Ek band kamre mein 500 litre saaf, sookhi hawa hai. Agar usme oxygen ka hissa lagbhag 21% hai, to kamre mein lagbhag kitni oxygen hai?

- Oxygen = 21% of 500 L = 21 ÷ 100 × 500 = **105 litre** (lagbhag).

**Jaanch:** 105 ÷ 500 × 100 = 21% ✅. (Ye sirf ek lagbhag aankda hai, kyunki asli hawa mein anupaat jagah ke hisaab se thoda badalta hai.)

**Example 5 (samundar ke paani se namak)**
Agar 1 kg samundar ke paani mein lagbhag 35 g namak (mineral salts) hai, to 20 kg samundar ke paani mein lagbhag kitna namak hoga?

- 1 kg mein 35 g, to 20 kg mein = 35 × 20 = **700 g**.

**Jaanch:** 700 ÷ 20,000 g × 100 = 3.5% ✅. (Ye maan kar chal rahe hain ki is samundar ka anupaat 3.5% hi hai, jo har jagah ek jaisa nahi hota.)

**Example 6 (peetal ka hisaab)**
Ek peetal ki chhadi 200 g ki hai aur usme 70% taamba hai. Usme taamba aur jasta kitna-kitna hai?

- Taamba = 70% of 200 g = 70 ÷ 100 × 200 = **140 g**.
- Jasta = 200 − 140 = **60 g** (yaani 30%).

**Jaanch:** 140 + 60 = 200 ✅, aur 60 ÷ 200 × 100 = 30% ✅

**Example 7 (kya ye mixture hai?)**
Ek student ne kaha: "Cheeni ka dana pure hai, to cheeni ka paani bhi pure hoga, kyunki usme cheeni hi to hai." Kya wo sahi hai?

- Nahi. Cheeni ka dana pure substance hai (sirf cheeni ke particles). Par cheeni ka paani mein **paani ke aur cheeni ke, dono tarah ke particles** hain.
- Do tarah ke particles hain, to ye **mixture** hai. Wo bhi homogeneous mixture.

**Jaanch:** Particles ki tasveer: "o" (cheeni) aur "x" (paani) dono hain, jaise Figure 1.2 ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Shuddh ka matlab hamesha pure substance hota hai"**

Nahi. Dukaan ka "shuddh shahad" daily life mein shuddh hai (milawat nahi), par science mein ek mixture hai. Daily life ka shuddh aur science ka pure, dono alag cheezein hain.

**Galti 2: "Jo ek jaisa dikhta hai, wo pure hi hoga"**

Nahi. Cheeni ka paani, hawa, peetal sab ek jaise dikhte hain, par wo mixture hain (homogeneous). Pure hone ka matlab hai ki **sirf ek hi tarah ke particles** hon, na ki "dikhne mein ek jaisa".

**Galti 3: "Mixture ka matlab hamesha alag-alag dikhna"**

Nahi. Wo sirf heterogeneous mixture mein hota hai. Homogeneous mixture mein ghatak alag nahi dikhte, jaise namak ka paani.

**Galti 4: "Thos cheez mixture nahi ho sakti"**

Alloy thos hote hain, par wo mixture hain. Peetal aur steel isi ke examples hain.

**Galti 5: "Mixture ke ghatak apni pehchaan kho dete hain"**

Mixture mein har ghatak apni **asli properties** rakhta hai. Cheeni ke paani mein paani ab bhi paani hai, cheeni ab bhi meethi hai. (Jab ghatak mil kar bilkul **naya padaarth** banate hain, tab ye mixture nahi, kuch aur hai. Ye hum Topic 6 mein dekhenge.)

## Quick Recap

- **Daily life ka shuddh** = milawat nahi, saaf, safe, asli. **Science ka pure** = sirf ek hi tarah ke particles.
- **Pure substance:** ek hi tarah ke particles (distilled paani, cheeni, sona).
- **Mixture:** do ya zyada pure cheezein mili hui. Har ghatak apni properties rakhta hai aur composition fixed nahi hoti.
- **Homogeneous mixture:** har jagah ek jaisa (namak ka paani, hawa, peetal, steel).
- **Heterogeneous mixture:** alag-alag hisse alag dikhte hain (salad, mitti, paani mein ret).
- **Hawa** ek mixture hai: lagbhag 78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen, 1% baaki.
- **Samundar ka paani** ek mixture hai: lagbhag 3.5% mineral salts, par anupaat har jagah ek jaisa nahi.
- **Alloy** (peetal, steel) bhi mixture hai, kyunki uska anupaat badal sakta hai aur ghatak ki kuch properties bachi rehti hain.
- Shahad aur mineral water daily life mein "shuddh" ho sakte hain, par science mein ye mixture hain.

## Practice Questions

1. In your own words, explain the difference between "pure" as we use it in daily life and "pure" as used in science. Give one example where the two meanings disagree.

2. Classify each as a pure substance or a mixture: distilled water, sea water, brass, sugar, soil, air, gold, milk.

3. Classify each mixture below as homogeneous or heterogeneous: sugar solution, salad, soil, brass, vinegar, sand in water.

4. What are the three important properties of a mixture? Explain each with an everyday example.

5. Why is brass called a mixture and not a pure substance, even though it looks like a single solid piece?

6. A 400 g fruit salad has 120 g apple, 160 g banana and the rest papaya. (a) How much papaya is there? (b) Find the percentage of each fruit. (c) A friend's 400 g fruit salad has 200 g apple, 100 g banana and 100 g papaya. Are the two salads the same mixture? Explain.

7. A room contains 2000 litres of dry air. If about 21% of it is oxygen and about 78% is nitrogen, find the approximate volume of (a) oxygen, (b) nitrogen, (c) all the other gases together.

8. A sample of sea water has 3.5 g of salt in every 100 g. How much salt is present in 4 kg of this sea water? Give your answer in grams.

9. A brass rod of mass 500 g contains 65% copper. Find the mass of copper and zinc in it.

10. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) Every homogeneous mixture is a pure substance.
    (b) The components of a mixture keep their own properties.
    (c) The composition of a pure substance can vary from sample to sample.
    (d) Steel is a mixture.

### Answers

1. Daily life mein "shuddh" ka matlab hai milawat nahi, saaf aur safe, jaise "shuddh doodh". Science mein "pure" ka matlab hai ki sirf ek hi tarah ke particles hon. Example jahan dono alag hote hain: shuddh shahad (milawat nahi, to daily life mein shuddh) mein kai cheezein (cheeni ki kisme, paani aur aur kuch) mili hoti hain, to science mein wo mixture hai.

2. **Pure substance:** distilled water, sugar, gold. **Mixture:** sea water, brass, soil, air, milk.
   Jaanch: 3 pure + 5 mixture = 8 cheezein ✅

3. **Homogeneous:** sugar solution, brass, vinegar. **Heterogeneous:** salad, soil, sand in water.
   Jaanch: teen-teen cheezein, kul 6 ✅

4. (i) **Har ghatak apni properties rakhta hai**: cheeni ke paani mein cheeni ab bhi meethi hai. (ii) **Fixed composition nahi hoti**: chai mein cheeni aadha chammach bhi ho sakti hai, do bhi, dono chai hi hain. (iii) **Aasaan physical tareekon se alag ho sakte hain**: namak ke paani ko ubaal kar paani ko bhaap bana kar alag kar sakte hain aur namak bach jaata hai.

5. Peetal taambe aur jaste ka mixture hai. Uska anupaat alag-alag peetal mein alag ho sakta hai (jaise 70:30 ya 60:40), aur wo dono ki kuch properties rakhta hai. Ek hi tukde jaisa dikhna isliye hai ki do dhaatuein poori tarah mil gayi hain, ye homogeneous mixture hai.

6. (a) Papaya = 400 − 120 − 160 = **120 g**. (b) Apple = 120 ÷ 400 × 100 = **30%**, banana = 160 ÷ 400 × 100 = **40%**, papaya = 120 ÷ 400 × 100 = **30%**. (c) Doosra salad: apple 50%, banana 25%, papaya 25%. Anupaat alag hai, phir bhi dono fruit salad hain, isliye mixture ki composition fixed nahi hoti.
   Jaanch: 30 + 40 + 30 = 100% ✅

7. (a) Oxygen = 21% of 2000 = 0.21 × 2000 = **420 L**. (b) Nitrogen = 0.78 × 2000 = **1560 L**. (c) Baaki = 2000 − 420 − 1560 = **20 L** (yaani 1%).
   Jaanch: 420 + 1560 + 20 = 2000 ✅

8. 100 g mein 3.5 g, to 4 kg = 4000 g mein = 3.5 × 40 = **140 g** namak.
   Jaanch: 140 ÷ 4000 × 100 = 3.5% ✅

9. Taamba = 65% of 500 = 0.65 × 500 = **325 g**. Jasta = 500 − 325 = **175 g**.
   Jaanch: 325 + 175 = 500 ✅, aur 175 ÷ 500 = 35% (65 + 35 = 100) ✅

10. (a) **False** ❌. Homogeneous mixture phir bhi mixture hota hai (cheeni ka paani); pure substance mein sirf ek hi tarah ke particles hote hain. (b) **True** ✅. (c) **False** ❌. Pure substance ki banaavat fixed hoti hai, anupaat badalna mixture ki pehchaan hai. (d) **True** ✅. Steel loha aur carbon ka mixture (alloy) hai.

---

Advait ka sawaal ab kaafi saaf ho gaya hai. Dukaan ka "100% shuddh shahad" daily life mein shuddh ho sakta hai, par science ki nazar mein wo ek mixture hai, aur isme koi jhooth nahi hai, bas "pure" ke do matlab hain. Agli baar kisi packet par "shuddh" padho, to ek sawaal poochh lena: *kya ye ek hi tarah ke particles hain, ya kai cheezein mili hui?*

**Aage kya?** Homogeneous mixtures mein sabse zyada kaam aane wali kisme **solution (ghol)** hai, aur usme ek cheez "ghulti" hai doosri mein. Kaun sa hissa kya kehlata hai, kitni cheeni ghole to paani "meetha" aur kitni ghole to cheeni neeche beth jaaye, aur garam paani mein zyada kyun ghulti hai: ye sab hum **Topic 2: Solutions** mein dekhenge.
