# Topic 2: Atom, Molecule aur Ion: Kan Ke Teen Roop

Pichhle topic mein humne tatvon ko chhote symbol se likhna seekha: H, O, C, Na, Fe. Aur ek line yaad hai: **symbol ka matlab us tatva ka ek atom, yaani us tatva ka sabse chhota kan.** Ab ek natural sawal uthta hai. Hum paani ko "H₂O" likhte hain, hawa mein maujood oxygen ko "O₂", aur carbon dioxide ko "CO₂". Ye chhote-chhote number symbol ke neeche kyun laga diye gaye? Aur kya H, H₂ aur 2H teeno ek hi cheez hain? Aaj hum kanon ke teen roop, **atom, molecule aur ion**, samjhenge, aur phir ye likhawat padhna seekhenge, jaise koi chhoti si bhasha ka grammar.

## Moti-mala ka stall

Shaam ko Ms. Zaveri apni bhanji Ojasvi ko mela dikhane le gayi thi. Ek kone mein moti-mala ka stall tha. Aadhe stall par **khule moti** (single beads) dibbon mein bhare the: laal ke dibbe mein sirf laal moti, neele mein sirf neele. Baaki aadha stall **taiyar malaon** se saja tha: kuch malayein ek hi rang ke motiyon ki, aur kuch laal-safed motiyon ka mel.

Ojasvi ne dukaan wali didi se poocha, "Didi, ek khula moti kitne ka hai aur ek mala kitne ki?"

Didi ne kaha, "Khula moti do rupaye ka, aur ye mala (jisme 2 laal aur 1 safed moti hain) pachaas ka. Mala mein dhaaga, design aur mehnat bhi to judi hai."

Ojasvi ne dheere se Ms. Zaveri se kaha, "Mausi, mala mein bhi to wahi moti hain jo dibbe mein khule pade hain. To mala alag cheez kaise ho gayi?"

Ms. Zaveri muskuraayi. "Bas yahi aaj ka chemistry hai, Ojasvi. Dhyan se dekho:

- **Khula moti** = ek **atom**. Tatva ka sabse chhota kan.
- **Do ya zyada motiyon ki jodi hui mala** = ek **molecule**. Atoms jud kar ek naya, saath chalne wala kan.
- Ek hi rang ke do moti jud jaayein to **tatva ka molecule** (jaise H₂). Alag-alag rang ke moti jud jaayein to **compound ka molecule** (jaise H₂O).

Aur ye bhi dekho: khule moti ko hum seedha bech sakte hain, par taiyar mala ka apna alag roop aur kaam hai. Chemistry mein bhi molecule ke gun aksar uske akele atoms se alag hote hain."

Ojasvi ne apne dono haathon mein ek-ek moti utha kar kaha, "To mujhe bas ye yaad rakhna hai ki moti kitne hain, aur kaun se rang ke, aur kaise jude hain?" Ms. Zaveri ne taali bajayi. "Bilkul. Aur chemistry ki bhasha mein ye sab ek chhote se likhe hue 'naam' mein aa jaata hai."

## Part A: Atom: tatva ka sabse chhota kan

Topic 1 mein humne dekha tha ki symbol "ek atom" dikhata hai. Ab is baat ko thoda gehra karte hain.

> **Atom kisi tatva ka wo sabse chhota kan hai jo us tatva ki pehchan banaye rakhta hai.**

Lohe ka koi bhi tukda lo aur use baar-baar chhota karte jao. Ek had ke baad tumhare paas loha ke **ek atom** bachega, jo "loha" ka sabse chhota roop hai. Sone ke tukde ko is tarah tode to akhir mein sone ka ek atom aayega. Yaani **har tatva ke apne atoms hote hain**, aur ek tatva ke sab atom ek jaise, doosre tatvon ke atoms se alag hote hain.

Ek bahut zaroori baat: atom itne chhote hote hain ki hum unhein aankh se to kya, school ke microscope se bhi nahi dekh sakte. Ek chhote se full stop (.) ke andar karodon-karodon atoms aa jaate hain. Atom ke andar kya hai (ye "Structure of the Atom" wale chapter mein aayega), aur ek atom ka bhaar kitna hota hai (ye "Atoms and Molecules" chapter mein). Abhi hamare liye bas itna kaafi hai: **atom = tatva ka sabse chhota kan**, aur uska symbol (H, O, Na, Fe...).

Aksar atom akele rehna pasand nahi karte. Zyada tar atoms ya to **aapas mein jud jaate hain**, ya doosre tatvon ke atoms se. Isi se molecule banta hai.

## Part B: Molecule: jude hue atoms ki mala

> **Molecule do ya zyada atoms ka aisa samooh hai jo aapas mein jud kar ek saath rehta hai aur ek kan ki tarah vyavhaar karta hai.**

Ojasvi ki mala jaisa: moti alag-alag nahi gaye, ek unit ban gaye.

### Tatva ke molecule: ek hi rang ke moti

Jab ek hi tatva ke atom aapas mein jude hon, to us tatva ka molecule banta hai.

- **Hydrogen:** 2 hydrogen atom jud kar ek hydrogen molecule banate hain. Likhte hain **H₂**.
- **Oxygen:** 2 oxygen atom jud kar **O₂**. Ye wahi gas hai jo hum saans mein lete hain.
- **Nitrogen:** **N₂**. Hawa ka lagbhag 78% hissa yahi hai.
- **Chlorine:** **Cl₂**.
- **Ozone:** 3 oxygen atom jud kar **O₃**. Ye alag gas hai (oxygen O₂ nahi).
- **Phosphorus:** 4 atom jud kar **P₄**.
- **Sulphur:** 8 atom jud kar **S₈**, ek chhalle jaisi (ring ke aakar ki) banawat.

Dhyan do: **O₂ aur O₃ dono sirf oxygen ke atoms se bane hain**, par dono alag gas hain, kyunki unme jude atoms ki ginti alag hai. Ye bahut bada sabut hai ki molecule mein sirf atom ka naam nahi, **atoms ki ginti** bhi mayne rakhti hai.

Kuch tatvon ke **molecule nahi** hote ya hum unhe simple symbol se likhte hain:

- **Helium (He), Neon (Ne), Argon (Ar)** jaisi gas bilkul akele atoms ki tarah rehti hain.
- **Dhaatuyein** (loha, tamba, sona, sodium) aur **carbon** (C) ko hum aam taur par seedhe symbol se likhte hain: Fe, Cu, Au, Na, C. Inme bahut saare atom ek bade jaal mein jude hote hain, isliye inhe ek chhote "H₂ jaise" molecule ke roop mein nahi likhte.

### Yaugik ke molecule: alag-alag rang ke moti

Jab **alag-alag tatvon** ke atom jud kar ek molecule banate hain, to wo us **yaugik (compound) ka molecule** hota hai.

- **Paani H₂O:** 2 hydrogen atom + 1 oxygen atom.
- **Carbon dioxide CO₂:** 1 carbon atom + 2 oxygen atom.
- **Ammonia NH₃:** 1 nitrogen atom + 3 hydrogen atom (kheton mein khaad aur safai ke cleaner mein tez gandh wali gas).
- **Hydrogen chloride HCl:** 1 hydrogen atom + 1 chlorine atom.

Compound ka molecule hamesha **fixed ratio** mein atoms rakhta hai (ye humne Chapter 2 mein "fixed ratio" ke roop mein dekha tha). H₂O mein hamesha 2 : 1, kabhi 3 : 1 nahi.

Ab teen figure dekho. Pehle atom versus molecule:

```
   ATOM (khule moti)             MOLECULE (jude hue moti)

       (H)    (H)    (H)             (H)-(H)     (H)-(H)

   3 alag hydrogen atom           2 hydrogen molecule (H₂)
   (har ek akela)                 (har molecule mein 2 atom)
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Khule atoms (H) akele-akele hain. Molecule (H₂) mein do atom ek saath jude hain aur ek kan ki tarah rehte hain. Dono baar hydrogen hi hai, par ginti aur jod ka tareeka alag hai.

Ab tatva ke molecule: ek-atom wala aur do-atom wala:

```
   HELIUM (He)                    HYDROGEN (H₂)             OXYGEN (O₂)

   (He)   (He)   (He)             (H)-(H)  (H)-(H)          (O)=(O)  (O)=(O)

   har kan 1 atom ka              har kan 2 atom ka          har kan 2 atom ka
   (monoatomic)                   (diatomic)                 (diatomic)
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Helium ke kan akele atom hain (monoatomic). Hydrogen aur oxygen ke kan do-do atom ke molecule hain (diatomic). Oxygen mein do atom ke beech double line sirf yaad dilane ke liye hai ki ye jod hydrogen ke jod se alag hai; iska kaaran aage ki class mein.

Aur ab yaugik ke molecule: har molecule mein alag-alag tatvon ke atom, ek jaisi jodi mein:

```
   PAANI (H₂O)         CARBON DIOXIDE (CO₂)       AMMONIA (NH₃)

      (O)                                              (N)
     /   \             (O)=(C)=(O)                   /  |  \
   (H)   (H)                                       (H) (H) (H)

   1 O + 2 H           1 C + 2 O                   1 N + 3 H
   (kul 3 atom)        (kul 3 atom)                (kul 4 atom)
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Compound ke molecule mein alag-alag tatvon ke atom jude hote hain. Paani mein 2 hydrogen aur 1 oxygen, carbon dioxide mein 1 carbon aur 2 oxygen, ammonia mein 1 nitrogen aur 3 hydrogen. Har molecule mein ratio fixed rehta hai. (Molecule ki asli aakriti is simple sketch se thodi alag ho sakti hai; hum yahan sirf "kaun, kitne" dikha rahe hain.)

## Part C: Atomicity: molecule mein kitne atom?

Ek molecule mein jitne atom hote hain, us ginti ko **atomicity** kehte hain. Number sidha **symbol ke neeche likhe subscript** se padha jaata hai. Agar symbol ke neeche koi number nahi likha, to uska matlab **1**.

| Naam | Formula | Atoms ki ginti (atomicity) | Naam (Hinglish) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Helium | He | 1 | Monoatomic |
| Neon | Ne | 1 | Monoatomic |
| Argon | Ar | 1 | Monoatomic |
| Hydrogen | H₂ | 2 | Diatomic |
| Oxygen | O₂ | 2 | Diatomic |
| Nitrogen | N₂ | 2 | Diatomic |
| Chlorine | Cl₂ | 2 | Diatomic |
| Hydrogen chloride | HCl | 2 | Diatomic |
| Ozone | O₃ | 3 | Triatomic |
| Paani | H₂O | 3 | Triatomic |
| Carbon dioxide | CO₂ | 3 | Triatomic |
| Phosphorus | P₄ | 4 | Tetraatomic |
| Ammonia | NH₃ | 4 | Tetraatomic |
| Methane | CH₄ | 5 | Pentaatomic |
| Sulphur | S₈ | 8 | Polyatomic (bahut atom) |

Ye naam yaad rakhne ka trick: **mono = 1, di = 2, tri = 3, tetra = 4, penta = 5**. Jahan atoms bahut zyada hon, use **polyatomic** (poly = bahut) kehte hain.

**Dhyan do:** HCl diatomic hai kyunki isme 1 + 1 = 2 atom hain, par ye **compound** hai (alag-alag tatva). H₂ bhi diatomic hai, par ye **tatva** ka molecule hai. Atomicity sirf **kul atoms** ginti hai, ye nahi batati ki molecule tatva ka hai ya compound ka.

### Solved examples: atomicity

**Example 1:** Sulphuric acid ka formula H₂SO₄ hai. Is molecule mein kul kitne atom hain, aur kaun se tatva ke kitne?

- H ke neeche 2, to hydrogen ke **2** atom.
- S ke neeche koi number nahi, to sulphur ka **1** atom.
- O ke neeche 4, to oxygen ke **4** atom.
- Kul atoms = 2 + 1 + 4 = **7** atom.

**Jaanch:** 2 + 1 + 4 = 7 ✅. Ye tri, tetra se bahut bada hai, isliye ise polyatomic bolenge.

**Example 2:** Glucose (jo hum khaane se paate hain) ka formula C₆H₁₂O₆ hai. Kul kitne atom hain?

- C = 6, H = 12, O = 6
- Kul = 6 + 12 + 6 = **24** atom.

**Jaanch:** 6 + 12 = 18, 18 + 6 = 24 ✅.

**Example 3:** Ozone (O₃) aur oxygen (O₂) dono sirf oxygen atoms ke bane hain. Inme fark kya hai?

- O₂: ek molecule mein **2** oxygen atom. Diatomic.
- O₃: ek molecule mein **3** oxygen atom. Triatomic.
- Dono ke atom ek jaise (oxygen ke), par jod ki ginti alag hai, isliye dono ke gun alag hain.

**Jaanch:** 2 ≠ 3, isliye molecule alag ✅.

## Part D: Kya likha hai? H, H₂, 2H, 2H₂, 3H₂O

Ab Ms. Zaveri ka asli grammar. Symbol ke **neeche** chhota number aur symbol ke **aage** bada number, in dono ke mayne bilkul alag hain.

> **Symbol ke neeche chhota number (subscript)** batata hai ek molecule ke **andar** us tatva ke kitne atom hain.
> **Formula ke aage bada number (coefficient)** batata hai **kitne molecule (ya atom)** hain.

Moti-mala ki bhasha mein: neeche ka number batata hai **ek mala mein** kitne moti hain. Aage ka number batata hai **kitni malayein** hain.

Hydrogen ke saath padhte hain:

| Likha hai | Kya hai | Kitne atom |
|---|---|---|
| H | Hydrogen ka **1 atom** | 1 |
| H₂ | Hydrogen ka **1 molecule** (usme 2 atom jude hain) | 2 |
| 2H | Hydrogen ke **2 alag atom** (jude nahi hain) | 2 |
| 2H₂ | Hydrogen ke **2 molecule** (har ek mein 2 atom) | 4 |
| 3H₂O | Paani ke **3 molecule** (har ek mein 2 H aur 1 O) | 9 (6 H + 3 O) |

```
   H          H₂          2H            2H₂

  (H)       (H)-(H)     (H)   (H)    (H)-(H)   (H)-(H)

  1 atom    1 molecule  2 alag atom   2 molecule
            (2 atom)    (kul 2)       (kul 4 atom)
```

> **Figure 2.4** – H ek atom. H₂ ek molecule jisme 2 jude atom hain. 2H ka matlab 2 alag, khule atom. 2H₂ ka matlab 2 molecule, yaani kul 4 atom. Aage ka bada number molecule (ya atom) ki ginti hai, neeche ka chhota number molecule ke andar atoms ki ginti.

**Sabse zaroori niyam:** neeche ka subscript **kabhi nahi badalna chahiye**. H₂ ko "H₃" ya "H" kar dene se ek alag cheez ban jaati hai. Agar aur molecule chahiye to **aage ka number** badalo (2H₂, 3H₂).

### Solved examples: kya likha hai

**Example 4:** 3H₂O mein kitne molecule, kitne hydrogen atom, kitne oxygen atom, aur kul kitne atom?

- Aage ka 3 = **3 molecule** paani ke.
- Har molecule mein 2 H aur 1 O.
- Hydrogen atoms = 3 × 2 = **6**
- Oxygen atoms = 3 × 1 = **3**
- Kul atoms = 6 + 3 = **9**

**Jaanch:** 3 molecule × 3 atom har ek (H₂O triatomic hai) = 9 ✅.

**Example 5:** 2H₂ aur 2H mein kya fark hai? Dono mein kul atom kitne hain?

- 2H₂ = 2 molecule × 2 atom = **4 atom** (jude hue, 2 jodiyan).
- 2H = **2 atom** (khule, alag).
- Dono ek jaise nahi, kyunki jud-ne ka fark hai, aur kul atom bhi alag hain (4 aur 2).

**Jaanch:** 2 × 2 = 4 ✅. 2H sirf 2 ✅.

**Example 6:** 5O₂ ko shabdon mein padho aur kul atom batao.

- 5 molecule oxygen ke, har molecule diatomic (O₂).
- Kul oxygen atom = 5 × 2 = **10**.

**Jaanch:** 5 molecule × 2 atom = 10 ✅.

**Example 7:** 4NH₃ mein kitne nitrogen atom, kitne hydrogen atom, kul kitne atom?

- 4 molecule ammonia ke. Har molecule mein 1 N aur 3 H.
- Nitrogen = 4 × 1 = **4**
- Hydrogen = 4 × 3 = **12**
- Kul = 4 + 12 = **16**

**Jaanch:** NH₃ tetraatomic hai (4 atom), 4 × 4 = 16 ✅.

**Example 8:** 2CO₂ + 3H₂ mein kul kitne atom hain? (Ye sirf ginti ka abhyas hai, koi reaction nahi.)

- 2CO₂ = 2 molecule × 3 atom = **6 atom** (2 C aur 4 O).
- 3H₂ = 3 molecule × 2 atom = **6 atom** (6 H).
- Kul = 6 + 6 = **12** atom.

**Jaanch:** 2 C + 4 O + 6 H = 12 ✅.

**Example 9 (likhna):** "Chlorine ke paanch molecule" ko chemical bhasha mein likho aur uske kul atom batao.

- Chlorine ka molecule Cl₂. Paanch molecule = **5Cl₂**.
- Kul atom = 5 × 2 = **10 Cl atom**.

**Jaanch:** 5 × 2 = 10 ✅.

## Part E: Ion: jab kan par charge aa jaaye

Atom aur molecule dono **neutral** hote hain, yaani unpar koi bijli ka charge nahi hota. Par kabhi-kabhi atom (ya atoms ka samooh) **electron** naam ke bahut chhote kan **le leta hai ya de deta hai**. (Electron kya hain aur kyun lete-dete hain, ye "Structure of the Atom" chapter mein aayega. Abhi bas itna samjho: **electron lena ya dena**.)

Jab aisa hota hai, to us kan par **charge** aa jaata hai. Charge wala kan **ion** kehlata hai.

- **Positive charge wala ion = cation.** Ye tab banta hai jab atom electron **de** deta hai. Jaise **Na⁺** (sodium ion), **Ca²⁺** (calcium ion).
- **Negative charge wala ion = anion.** Ye tab banta hai jab atom electron **le** leta hai. Jaise **Cl⁻** (chloride ion), **O²⁻** (oxide ion).

Charge **upar, symbol ke daayein taraf** likhte hain. Subscript neeche hota hai, charge upar. Dono ka kaam alag hai:

| Likha hai | Kya hai |
|---|---|
| Na | Sodium ka **atom** (neutral) |
| Na⁺ | Sodium ka **ion** (1 positive charge, electron de diya) |
| Cl | Chlorine ka atom (neutral) |
| Cl⁻ | Chlorine ka **ion** (1 negative charge, electron le liya) |
| Ca²⁺ | Calcium ion, **2** positive charge |
| O²⁻ | Oxide ion, **2** negative charge |

Ek aur baat: ion **sirf ek atom ka** nahi, **atoms ke samooh ka** bhi ho sakta hai.

- **Simple ion:** ek atom par charge: Na⁺, Cl⁻, Mg²⁺, Al³⁺.
- **Polyatomic ion:** kai atoms ka samooh jis par ek poora charge hai aur jo ek saath chalta hai: **NH₄⁺** (ammonium), **OH⁻** (hydroxide), **SO₄²⁻** (sulphate), **CO₃²⁻** (carbonate).

Polyatomic ion ko poori table ke saath **Topic 4** mein seekhenge. Abhi bas naam pehchano.

Table salt ke andar **Na⁺ aur Cl⁻** ions hote hain. Alag-alag ek positive, ek negative: aur opposite charge ek doosre ko khinchte hain, isliye ye jude rehte hain. (Ye "aakarshan" ki baat aage ke chapters mein.)

```
   NEUTRAL ATOM                    ION

   (Na)                        (Na⁺)  = electron de diya, positive
   (Cl)                        (Cl⁻)  = electron le liya, negative

   Na  +  Cl   --->   Na⁺  ..  Cl⁻
   (dono neutral)    (ek positive, ek negative, ek doosre ko kheenchte hain)
```

> **Figure 2.5** – Neutral atom aur ion: sodium electron de kar Na⁺ banta hai aur chlorine electron le kar Cl⁻. Opposite charge wale ye do ion ek doosre ko kheenchte hain.

### Solved example: ion

**Example 10:** Neeche likhe kanon ko atom, molecule ya ion mein baanto: Na, Cl₂, Mg²⁺, H₂O, F⁻, He, NH₄⁺.

| Kan | Category | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| Na | Atom | Ek symbol, koi charge nahi |
| Cl₂ | Molecule (tatva ka) | 2 same atom jude, koi charge nahi |
| Mg²⁺ | Ion (cation) | Positive charge (electron diye) |
| H₂O | Molecule (yaugik ka) | Alag atom jude, koi charge nahi |
| F⁻ | Ion (anion) | Negative charge (electron liya) |
| He | Atom | Helium akele atom ki tarah rehta hai, koi charge nahi |
| NH₄⁺ | Polyatomic ion (cation) | 5 atom ka samooh jis par 1 positive charge hai |

**Jaanch:** Gino to 7 kan hain: atom 2 (Na, He), molecule 2 (Cl₂, H₂O), ion 3 (Mg²⁺, F⁻, NH₄⁺). 2 + 2 + 3 = 7 ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "H₂ aur 2H ek hi cheez hain, dono mein 2 hydrogen atom hain"**

Nahi. H₂ ek molecule hai jisme 2 atom **jude** hain. 2H mein 2 atom **alag-alag** hain. Dono ke gun alag hote hain (jaise khula moti aur mala).

**Galti 2: "2H₂O ka matlab hai 2 hydrogen aur 1 oxygen"**

Nahi. Aage ka 2 poore formula par lagta hai. 2H₂O ka matlab **2 molecule paani**, yaani 4 hydrogen atom aur 2 oxygen atom. (2 × H₂O = 2 × (2 H + 1 O).)

**Galti 3: "Oxygen ko O likhna sahi hai, hawa ki oxygen O hai"**

Hawa mein oxygen gas **O₂** ke roop mein hoti hai (2 atom ka molecule). Sirf O ek akela atom hai.

**Galti 4: "O₂ aur O₃ ek hi gas hain, kyunki dono oxygen hain"**

Nahi. Dono sirf oxygen ke atoms se bane hain, par molecule mein ginti alag hai (2 aur 3), isliye dono alag gas hain.

**Galti 5: "Co aur CO ek hi hain"**

Co (Topic 1) cobalt tatva ka symbol hai. CO carbon aur oxygen ka compound (carbon monoxide) hai. Capital aur small letter ka dhyan rakho.

**Galti 6: "Paani ko H₃O ya HO₂ bhi likh sakte hain"**

Nahi. Formula ke subscript **fixed** hote hain. Paani sirf **H₂O** hai. Subscript badalne se doosra substance ban jaata hai.

**Galti 7: "Compound ka molecule sirf ek tatva ka hota hai"**

Nahi. Compound ka molecule **do ya zyada alag tatvon** ke atoms ka hota hai. Ek hi tatva ke atoms ka molecule **tatva** ka molecule hota hai.

**Galti 8: "Ion matlab koi nayi tarah ka tatva"**

Nahi. Na aur Na⁺ ek hi tatva (sodium) hain; bas Na⁺ ne ek electron de diya hai, isliye uspar charge hai. Dono ke gun bahut alag hote hain.

## Quick Recap

- **Atom:** tatva ka sabse chhota kan; symbol se likha jaata hai (H, O, Na).
- **Molecule:** do ya zyada atoms jo jud kar ek saath rehte hain. **Tatva ka molecule** (ek hi tatva ke atom): H₂, O₂, N₂, Cl₂, O₃, P₄, S₈. **Yaugik ka molecule** (alag tatvon ke atom): H₂O, CO₂, NH₃, HCl.
- **Atomicity:** ek molecule mein atoms ki ginti. Mono (1), di (2), tri (3), tetra (4), penta (5), poly (bahut). Noble gases (He, Ne, Ar) monoatomic hain.
- Symbol ke **neeche** ka chhota number: molecule ke **andar** atoms ki ginti. Formula ke **aage** ka bada number: **kitne molecule (ya atom)**.
- H = 1 atom, H₂ = 1 molecule (2 atom), 2H = 2 alag atom, 2H₂ = 2 molecule (4 atom), 3H₂O = 3 molecule paani (9 atom: 6 H + 3 O).
- Subscript kabhi nahi badalte, ginti badhani ho to aage ka number badlo.
- **Ion:** electron lene ya dene se charge wala kan. **Cation** (positive, electron diya): Na⁺, Ca²⁺. **Anion** (negative, electron liya): Cl⁻, O²⁻. Polyatomic ion (NH₄⁺, SO₄²⁻) atoms ka samooh hai jo ek charge ke saath saath chalta hai.
- Charge symbol ke **upar-daayein** likhte hain, subscript **neeche**.

2. What is atomicity? Write the atomicity of each: He, N₂, O₃, P₄, NH₃, CH₄.

3. Explain the difference between H₂ and 2H, using a picture or the bead-necklace idea.

4. Read in words and count the total atoms: (a) 3O₂, (b) 2H₂O, (c) 4HCl, (d) 2P₄.

5. In 5H₂O, how many molecules are there, how many hydrogen atoms, how many oxygen atoms and how many atoms in all?

6. The formula of glucose is C₆H₁₂O₆. How many atoms of each element are there in 2 molecules of glucose, and how many atoms in all?

7. Write in chemical language: (a) four molecules of chlorine, (b) three molecules of carbon dioxide, (c) five separate sodium atoms, (d) two molecules of ammonia.

8. Why are O₂ and O₃ different gases although both contain only oxygen atoms?

9. Classify as atom, molecule or ion: K, N₂, K⁺, CO₂, S²⁻, Ar, SO₄²⁻. Also name which are cations and which are anions.

10. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) H₂ and 2H mean the same thing.
    (b) A molecule of a compound contains atoms of at least two different elements.
    (c) Na⁺ has gained an electron.
    (d) Helium exists as single atoms.
    (e) In 2H₂O the number 2 tells that each molecule has 2 hydrogen atoms.

### Answers

1. **Atom:** tatva ka sabse chhota kan. **Molecule:** do ya zyada atoms ka jude hue samooh jo ek kan ki tarah rehta hai. Tatva ke molecule: H₂, O₂ (ya N₂, Cl₂). Yaugik ke molecule: H₂O, CO₂ (ya NH₃, HCl).

2. **Atomicity** = ek molecule mein atoms ki ginti. He = 1 (mono), N₂ = 2 (di), O₃ = 3 (tri), P₄ = 4 (tetra), NH₃ = 1 + 3 = 4 (tetra), CH₄ = 1 + 4 = 5 (penta).

3. H₂ ek **molecule** hai jisme 2 hydrogen atom **jude** hain (jaise do motiyon ki mala). 2H mein 2 hydrogen atom **alag-alag** hain (do khule moti). Picture: H₂ = (H)-(H), 2H = (H)   (H). Dono mein kul 2 atom, par jud-ne ka fark hai aur gun alag hain.

4. (a) 3O₂ = 3 molecule oxygen, 3 × 2 = **6** atom. (b) 2H₂O = 2 molecule paani, 2 × 3 = **6** atom (4 H + 2 O). (c) 4HCl = 4 molecule hydrogen chloride, 4 × 2 = **8** atom (4 H + 4 Cl). (d) 2P₄ = 2 molecule phosphorus, 2 × 4 = **8** atom.
   Jaanch: (b) 4 + 2 = 6 ✅, (c) 4 + 4 = 8 ✅

5. 5H₂O = **5 molecule**. Hydrogen = 5 × 2 = **10**. Oxygen = 5 × 1 = **5**. Kul = 10 + 5 = **15** atom.
   Jaanch: 5 molecule × 3 atom = 15 ✅

6. Ek molecule mein C = 6, H = 12, O = 6. 2 molecule mein: C = 12, H = 24, O = 12. Kul = 12 + 24 + 12 = **48** atom.
   Jaanch: ek molecule mein 24 atom, 2 × 24 = 48 ✅

7. (a) **4Cl₂**. (b) **3CO₂**. (c) **5Na**. (d) **2NH₃**.

8. O₂ mein ek molecule mein 2 oxygen atom hain aur O₃ mein 3. Atoms ki ginti alag hai, isliye molecule alag aur unke gun alag. O₂ saans ke kaam ki gas hai, O₃ ozone alag gas hai.

9. K = **atom**. N₂ = **molecule** (tatva ka). K⁺ = **ion, cation** (positive). CO₂ = **molecule** (yaugik ka). S²⁻ = **ion, anion** (negative). Ar = **atom** (noble gas, akele atom). SO₄²⁻ = **polyatomic ion, anion**. Cation: K⁺. Anion: S²⁻, SO₄²⁻.

10. (a) **False** ❌. H₂ ek molecule hai (2 jude atom), 2H do alag atom hain. (b) **True** ✅. (c) **False** ❌. Na⁺ ne ek electron **de** diya hai, isliye positive hai. (d) **True** ✅. (e) **False** ❌. Aage ka 2 batata hai ki **2 molecule** paani hain; har molecule mein 2 hydrogen atom neeche ke subscript se pata chalte hain.
