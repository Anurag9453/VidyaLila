# Topic 3: Valency: Jodne Ki Kshamta

Topic 1 mein humne tatvon ke naam (symbols) seekhe, aur Topic 2 mein dekha ki atom akele bhi rehte hain aur aapas mein jud kar molecule bhi banate hain, jaise H₂O, NH₃, CO₂. Ab ek bada sawal: **ek atom doosre ke saath kitni jodi bana sakta hai?** Paani mein oxygen ke saath 2 hydrogen kyun jude hain, 1 ya 3 kyun nahi? Is sawal ka jawab hai ek chhota sa number, jise **valency** kehte hain. Isi number se hum aage poore formulae likhna seekhenge.

---

## Gaon ki bus aur uski benches

Shaam ki aakhri bus gaon ki sadak par hichkole khati chali ja rahi thi. Mr. Chawla apni bhatiji Dhruvi ke saath khidki wali seat par baithe the. Dhruvi ne dekha ki bus mein teen tarah ki benches hain: aage kuch **2-seater**, beech mein kuch **3-seater**, aur sabse peeche ek lambi **back bench**.

Conductor ne ek parivaar ko bulaya: "Teen log hain? Beech wali 3-seater par baith jaiye." Phir do dost aaye: "Aap dono us 2-seater par." Ek aunty akeli aayi, aur wo ek **single seat** par baith gayi.

Dhruvi hansi: "Uncle, har bench ki apni ginti hai. Kisi par 1 insaan, kisi par 2, kisi par 3. Aur koi bench kisi doosre ki seat nahi le sakti."

Mr. Chawla ne sir hilaya: "Bilkul. Aur ek aur baat dekho. Bus tabhi theek se bhari mani jaati hai jab **har insaan ko seat mil jaye aur koi seat khaali na bache**. Chemistry mein bhi kuch aisa hi chalta hai. Har tatva ke atom ki ek **fixed seating capacity** hoti hai: wo kitne doosre atoms ko apne saath jod sakta hai. Isi ko hum **valency** kehte hain."

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Valency** | Ek atom ki **jodne ki kshamta** (combining capacity): wo doosre atoms ke saath kitni "jodiyan" bana sakta hai | Hydrogen ki valency 1, oxygen ki 2 |
| **Combining capacity** | Valency ka hi doosra naam | Bench ki seats ki ginti jaisa |
| **Variable valency** | Jab ek hi tatva ki ek se zyada valency ho sakti hai | Iron: 2 aur 3 |
| **Noble gases** | Helium, neon, argon jaisi gas jo kisi se judti hi nahi, yaani valency **0** | He, Ne, Ar |
| **Valency line** | Figure mein dikhaya jaane wala chhota sa dash (-) jo ek jodi ko dikhata hai | H-Cl mein ek line |

## Part A: Valency kya hai?

Hydrogen sabse simple example hai. Hydrogen ka ek atom **sirf ek** doosre atom se jud sakta hai. Isliye uski valency **1** hai. Ise bench ki bhasha mein kaho to hydrogen ek **single seat** hai.

> **Valency wo number hai jo bataata hai ki ek atom kitne hydrogen atoms ke barabar (ya kitni jodiyan) doosre atoms ke saath bana sakta hai.**

Kuch seedhe example dekho:

- **HCl** (hydrogen chloride): 1 hydrogen aur 1 chlorine. Dono ek-ek jodi banate hain. Hydrogen ki valency 1, chlorine ki valency 1.
- **H₂O** (paani): oxygen ke saath **2 hydrogen** jude hain. Oxygen ne 2 jodiyan banayi, isliye oxygen ki valency **2**.
- **NH₃** (ammonia): nitrogen ke saath **3 hydrogen**. Nitrogen ki valency **3**.
- **CH₄** (methane): carbon ke saath **4 hydrogen**. Carbon ki valency **4**.

Dekha? Hydrogen ko "paimana" maan lo (valency 1), aur baaki tatva ko usi paimane se naapo: kitne hydrogen ke barabar jodiyan banti hain?

> **Ek zaroori baat (kyun ka jawab):** Kisi tatva ki valency wahi kyun hai, ye is chapter mein **nahi** samjhenge. Iska raaz atom ke andar chhupa hai, jo **Chapter 5 (Structure of the Atom)** mein khulega. Abhi hum valency ko ek **seekhne wali table** ki tarah lenge aur uska istemaal karna seekhenge, bilkul jaise bus ki seats ki ginti pehle yaad karte hain, phir ye nahi poochte ki bus ke designer ne 3 seat kyun rakhi. Aur yaad rakho: tatva ki **position dekh kar valency nikaalne ka shortcut** bhi abhi nahi seekhenge, wo bhi Chapter 5 ke baad.

```
   BUS KI BENCHES                 TATVON KI "SEATING CAPACITY"

   [ 1 ]  single seat        ->    H, Na, Cl      (valency 1)
   [ 2 ][ 2 ]  2-seater      ->    O, Mg, Ca      (valency 2)
   [ 3 ][ 3 ][ 3 ]  3-seater ->    N, Al          (valency 3)
   [ 4 ][ 4 ][ 4 ][ 4 ]      ->    C, Si          (valency 4)
   [ 0 ]  band bench         ->    He, Ne, Ar     (valency 0)
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Bench analogy: jaise har bench par baithne wale logon ki ginti fixed hai, waise hi har tatva ke atom ki jodne ki kshamta fixed hai. Noble gases ki "bench" par koi baithta hi nahi (valency 0).

## Part C: Kuch aur zaroori tatva (variable valency)

Pehle 20 tatvon ke alawa kuch tatva roz ki zindagi mein bahut milte hain. Unki valency bhi jaan lo:

| Tatva | Symbol | Valency |
|---|---|---|
| Zinc | Zn | 2 |
| Silver | Ag | 1 |
| Lead | Pb | 2 |
| Iron | Fe | 2 aur 3 |
| Copper | Cu | 1 aur 2 |
| Mercury | Hg | 1 aur 2 |
| Tin | Sn | 2 aur 4 |

Dhyan do: kuch tatva ki **ek se zyada valency** hoti hai. Ise **variable valency** kehte hain. Jaise bus mein ek hi insaan kabhi 2-seater par baith jaye aur kabhi 3-seater par.

**Variable valency ka matlab kya hai?** Wo tatva **alag-alag compounds** mein alag-alag valency dikha sakta hai, aur har baar **naya compound** banta hai. Do example:

- **Iron (Fe):** FeCl₂ mein iron ki valency 2, aur FeCl₃ mein iron ki valency 3. Dono alag compounds hain: pehle ka naam iron(II) chloride, doosre ka iron(III) chloride. (Roman number valency batata hai.)
- **Copper (Cu):** Cu₂O mein copper ki valency 1, aur CuO mein valency 2.

Phosphorus mein bhi aisa hi hai: PH₃ mein valency 3, PCl₅ mein valency 5.

Is chapter mein hum bas yaad rakhte hain ki **kaun sa tatva kaun si valency dikha sakta hai**. Kisi formula mein kaun si valency lagi hai, ye aksar compound ke naam ya diye hue data se pata chalta hai. Ye bhi **kyun hota hai**, uski kahani Chapter 5 se jud kar aayegi.

## Part D: Valency se compound ka jod-tod

Ab asli kaam. **Compound mein jodiyan poori ho jaani chahiye: koi seat khaali na rahe, koi insaan khada na rahe.** Matlab dono taraf ki "kul valency" barabar honi chahiye.

> **Kul valency = (atoms ki ginti) × (ek atom ki valency)**
> Compound tab sahi hai jab dono tatvon ki kul valency **barabar** ho.

Dekhte hain H₂O mein: hydrogen ke 2 atom, har ek ki valency 1, to kul = 2 × 1 = **2**. Oxygen ka 1 atom, valency 2, to kul = 1 × 2 = **2**. Dono barabar. Jodiyan poori!

Ab figure dekho, jahan har line ek jodi hai:

```
       H₂O              NH₃                 CH₄

     H     H          H     H                H
      \   /            \   /                 :
        O                N               H - C - H
                         :                   :
                         H                   H

   O ke paas         N ke paas             C ke paas
   2 lines           3 lines               4 lines
   (valency 2)       (valency 3)           (valency 4)
   H ke paas         H ke paas             H ke paas
   1 line            1 line                1 line
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Atoms ko valency lines se joda gaya hai. Oxygen se 2 lines (H₂O), nitrogen se 3 lines (NH₃), carbon se 4 lines (CH₄) niklti hain. Har hydrogen se sirf 1 line niklti hai, kyunki uski valency 1 hai. (Ye sirf sketch hai; asli molecule ka aakaar ye nahi dikhata.)

Ek aur figure dekho, jisme bench ki bhasha mein MgO aur HCl dikh rahe hain:

```
   HCl:   H - Cl                1 line, dono taraf 1 ka 1: jodi poori

   MgO:   Mg = O                2 lines, Mg ki valency 2 aur O ki 2

   AlCl₃:      Cl
               :
         Cl - Al                Al ki 3 lines, har Cl ki 1 line
               :
               Cl
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Valency lines ka hisaab: har line dono taraf se ginti jaati hai, isliye dono atoms ki lines ki kul ginti barabar rehti hai.

## Part E: Solved examples

**Example 1 (HCl):** Hydrogen aur chlorine kitne-kitne atoms mein judenge?

- H ki valency = 1, Cl ki valency = 1.
- 1 H (kul 1) aur 1 Cl (kul 1) barabar hain.
- Formula: **HCl**. Ek H aur ek Cl.

**Jaanch:** 1 × 1 = 1 aur 1 × 1 = 1 ✅

**Example 2 (H₂O):** Hydrogen aur oxygen kitne-kitne atoms mein judenge?

- H ki valency = 1, O ki valency = 2.
- Oxygen ka 1 atom do jodiyan banata hai, to usse do hydrogen jud sakte hain (har ek ki ek jodi).
- Formula: **H₂O**. 2 H aur 1 O.

**Jaanch:** Hydrogen ki kul valency 2 × 1 = 2. Oxygen ki kul valency 1 × 2 = 2 ✅

**Example 3 (NH₃):** Nitrogen ke ek atom ke saath kitne hydrogen judenge?

- N ki valency = 3, H ki valency = 1.
- Nitrogen ki 3 jodiyan, har hydrogen ek jodi leta hai, to **3 hydrogen**.
- Formula: **NH₃**.

**Jaanch:** 3 × 1 = 3 aur 1 × 3 = 3 ✅

**Example 4 (CH₄):** Carbon ke ek atom ke saath kitne hydrogen judenge?

- C ki valency = 4, H ki valency = 1.
- 4 jodiyan, to **4 hydrogen**.
- Formula: **CH₄**.

**Jaanch:** 4 × 1 = 4 aur 1 × 4 = 4 ✅

**Example 5 (MgO):** Magnesium aur oxygen kitne-kitne atoms mein judenge?

- Mg ki valency = 2, O ki valency = 2.
- 1 Mg (kul 2) aur 1 O (kul 2) barabar. Koi aur atom lagane ki zaroorat nahi.
- Formula: **MgO**. Ek Mg aur ek O.

**Jaanch:** 1 × 2 = 2 aur 1 × 2 = 2 ✅

**Example 6 (CaCl₂):** Calcium aur chlorine kitne-kitne atoms mein judenge?

- Ca ki valency = 2, Cl ki valency = 1.
- Calcium ki 2 jodiyan hain, har chlorine ek hi jodi leta hai, to **2 chlorine** chahiye.
- Formula: **CaCl₂**.

**Jaanch:** Ca: 1 × 2 = 2. Cl: 2 × 1 = 2 ✅

**Example 7 (Al₂O₃, thoda naya):** Aluminium (valency 3) aur oxygen (valency 2) kitne-kitne atoms mein judenge?

- Ek Al ki 3, ek O ki 2. Seedha 1 : 1 mein barabar nahi hota.
- Hum aisi kul valency dhundhte hain jo 3 aur 2 dono ka multiple ho: 6. 6 ÷ 3 = **2 atom Al** (2 × 3 = 6). 6 ÷ 2 = **3 atom O** (3 × 2 = 6).
- Formula: **Al₂O₃**.

**Jaanch:** Al: 2 × 3 = 6. O: 3 × 2 = 6 ✅

**Example 8 (variable valency, FeCl₂ aur FeCl₃):** Iron ki valency 2 ho to chlorine ke kitne atom jude honge? Valency 3 ho to?

- Valency 2 ho to 2 jodiyan, har Cl 1 jodi leta hai, to **2 Cl**: **FeCl₂**.
- Valency 3 ho to 3 jodiyan, to **3 Cl**: **FeCl₃**.

**Jaanch:** FeCl₂: 1 × 2 = 2 aur 2 × 1 = 2 ✅. FeCl₃: 1 × 3 = 3 aur 3 × 1 = 3 ✅

> Ye method (jodiyan barabar karna) hi aage **criss-cross method** ban jaayega, jise Topic 5 mein poori tarah seekhenge. Abhi chhote examples par ginti se kaam chalao.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Valency ka matlab atoms ki ginti hai"**

Nahi. Valency ek atom ki **jodne ki kshamta** hai. Atoms ki ginti formula mein neeche likhe subscripts se dikhti hai (jaise H₂O mein 2). Dono alag baatein hain.

**Galti 2: "Har tatva ki valency ek hi hoti hai"**

Nahi. Fe, Cu, Hg, Sn, P jaise tatva ki **do ya zyada** valency ho sakti hain (variable valency).

**Galti 3: "Noble gases bahut zyada judti hain"**

Ulta. Helium, neon, argon ki valency **0** hai: ye kisi se judti hi nahi.

**Galti 4: "Valency kyun hoti hai, ye yahin samjha do"**

Is chapter mein nahi. Iska reason **Chapter 5** mein milega. Abhi valency ko table ki tarah seekhna aur uska istemaal karna hai.

**Galti 5: "Valency ka number position dekh kar bhi nikaal lenge"**

Ye shortcut abhi allowed nahi hai. Wo bhi Chapter 5 ke baad hi bharosemand tareeke se seekha jaata hai.

**Galti 6: "Oxygen ki valency 1 hai, kyunki O mein ek O hai"**

Nahi. Oxygen ki valency **2** hai (paani mein 2 hydrogen se judta hai). Symbol ke number se valency nahi nikalti.

## Quick Recap

- **Valency** = ek atom ki jodne ki kshamta (combining capacity), jaise bus ki bench ki seats.
- Hydrogen ki valency 1 hai, aur baaki tatvon ko hydrogen ke hisaab se naapte hain.
- Pehle 20 tatvon ki valency: H, Li, Na, K, F, Cl = 1; Be, Mg, Ca, O, S = 2; B, Al, N = 3; C, Si = 4; P = 3 aur 5; He, Ne, Ar = 0.
- Extra tatva: Zn 2, Ag 1, Pb 2, Fe 2 aur 3, Cu 1 aur 2, Hg 1 aur 2, Sn 2 aur 4.
- **Variable valency:** ek tatva ki ek se zyada valency, har baar alag compound (FeCl₂ aur FeCl₃).
- Compound mein dono taraf ki **kul valency barabar** hoti hai: ginti × valency.
- Examples: HCl, H₂O, NH₃, CH₄, MgO, CaCl₂, Al₂O₃.
- Valency kyun hoti hai, ye **Chapter 5** ki kahani hai.

## Practice Questions

1. What is valency? Why do we use hydrogen as the standard to measure it?

2. Write the valency of each: Na, Mg, Al, C, Cl, O, N, Ca, F, Ne.

3. How many hydrogen atoms combine with one atom of (a) chlorine, (b) oxygen, (c) nitrogen, (d) carbon?

4. How many atoms of chlorine combine with one atom of (a) sodium, (b) magnesium, (c) aluminium?

5. How many atoms of oxygen combine with one atom of (a) magnesium, (b) calcium, (c) sodium? (Na has valency 1, so think about how many Na atoms fit.)

6. How many atoms of aluminium join with how many atoms of oxygen? Write the formula.

7. Iron shows valency 2 and 3. Write how many chlorine atoms join with one atom of iron in each case.

8. How many hydrogen atoms combine with one atom of sulphur (valency 2)? Write the formula.

9. State True or False and correct the false ones: (a) Helium has valency 2. (b) Carbon has valency 4. (c) A compound can have different total valency on its two sides. (d) The reason for valency is explained in this chapter. (e) Copper has only one valency.

### Answers

1. **Valency** ek atom ki jodne ki kshamta hai: wo doosre atoms ke saath kitni jodiyan bana sakta hai. Hydrogen ko standard isliye maante hain kyunki wo sabse chhota aur simple hai, aur uski valency sirf 1 hai, to baaki tatvon ko "kitne hydrogen ke barabar" keh kar naapna aasaan ho jaata hai.

2. Na = **1**, Mg = **2**, Al = **3**, C = **4**, Cl = **1**, O = **2**, N = **3**, Ca = **2**, F = **1**, Ne = **0**.

3. (a) Cl ki valency 1, to **1** hydrogen (HCl). (b) O ki valency 2, to **2** hydrogen (H₂O). (c) N ki valency 3, to **3** hydrogen (NH₃). (d) C ki valency 4, to **4** hydrogen (CH₄).

4. (a) Na valency 1, to **1** chlorine (NaCl). (b) Mg valency 2, to **2** chlorine (MgCl₂). (c) Al valency 3, to **3** chlorine (AlCl₃).
   Jaanch: 1 × 1 = 1; 2 × 1 = 2; 3 × 1 = 3 ✅

5. (a) Mg valency 2, O valency 2: **1** oxygen (MgO). (b) Ca valency 2: **1** oxygen (CaO). (c) Na valency 1, O valency 2: ek O ko 2 jodiyan chahiye, to **2 Na atoms** judenge ek oxygen ke saath (Na₂O). Yaani 2 Na : 1 O.
   Jaanch: Na₂O: 2 × 1 = 2 aur 1 × 2 = 2 ✅

6. Al ki valency 3, O ki 2. Kul valency 6 mein dono barabar: Al = 6 ÷ 3 = 2 atoms, O = 6 ÷ 2 = 3 atoms. Formula **Al₂O₃**.
   Jaanch: 2 × 3 = 6 aur 3 × 2 = 6 ✅

7. Valency 2 mein **2** chlorine (FeCl₂). Valency 3 mein **3** chlorine (FeCl₃).

8. Sulphur ki valency 2 hai, to **2 hydrogen** judenge: **H₂S**.
   Jaanch: 2 × 1 = 2 aur 1 × 2 = 2 ✅

9. (a) **False** ❌. Helium ki valency **0** hai. (b) **True** ✅. (c) **False** ❌. Dono taraf ki kul valency barabar honi chahiye. (d) **False** ❌. Reason Chapter 5 mein aata hai. (e) **False** ❌. Copper ki valency 1 aur 2 dono hain.
