# Topic 4: Radical: Atoms Ka Group Jo Ek Saath Chalta Hai

Topic 3 mein humne dekha ki har tatva ke paas jodne ki ek **kshamta (valency)** hoti hai, aur usi se pata chalta hai ki kitne atoms aapas mein judenge. Ab ek nayi baat: kuch jagah atoms akele nahi, **ek tolee (group) banakar** chalte hain. Wo tolee ek hi **unit** ki tarah kaam karti hai aur uske upar ek **charge** bhi hota hai. Is tolee ko chemistry mein **radical** kehte hain. Aaj hum radicals ki poori list seekhenge, unke naam rakhne ke niyam jaanenge, aur yaad rakhne ke tricks bhi banayenge.

---

## Tempo traveller ka ek ticket

Shanivaar ki subah thi. Mr. Srinivasan apne bhatije Nirav ke saath bus-stand par khade the. Pass mein paanch dost hansi-mazaak kar rahe the: Mehak, Nakul, Jeevan, Pallavi aur Abhay. Wo sab Lonavala ghoomne ja rahe the, aur unhone **ek hi tempo traveller** book kiya tha.

Booking counter par clerk ne poochha, "Kitne ticket?" Un paanchon ne kaha, "Ek hi ticket. Hum paanch ek saath hain, ek group."

Nirav ne dheere se pucha, "Uncle, ye log paanch hain, phir bhi ek ticket? Aur dekho, driver ne bas ek naam likha: 'Mehak Group, 5 log'."

Mr. Srinivasan muskuraaye. "Yahi to dekho Nirav. Booking ke liye ye paanchon **ek unit** hain. Koi chadhega to paanchon saath, utrega to paanchon saath. Koi ek akela baith kar bas mein nahi jaayega. Ab ek baat yaad rakho: chemistry mein bhi kuch atoms aise hi **ek group bana kar, ek ticket par** chalte hain. Wo kabhi akele nahi bikhrate, aur doosre se jodne ke liye poora group ek saath jaata hai."

"Aur uska kya naam hai?" Nirav ne pucha.

"**Radical**," Mr. Srinivasan bole. "Aao, chaay peete hue is tempo traveller wale group ke chemistry wale bhai-behnon se milte hain."

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Radical** | Charge wala ek atom ya atoms ka group, jo reaction mein ek unit ki tarah chalta hai | Na⁺, Cl⁻, SO₄²⁻, NH₄⁺ |
| **Simple radical** | Sirf ek atom ka bana radical (charge ke saath) | Na⁺, Mg²⁺, Cl⁻, O²⁻ |
| **Compound (polyatomic) radical** | Do ya zyada atoms ka bana radical, jo poora ek unit hai | OH⁻, NO₃⁻, SO₄²⁻, NH₄⁺ |
| **Positive radical (basic radical)** | Jis radical par **plus (+)** charge hota hai; ise cation bhi kehte hain | Na⁺, Ca²⁺, Al³⁺ |
| **Negative radical (acid radical)** | Jis radical par **minus (−)** charge hota hai; ise anion bhi kehte hain | Cl⁻, OH⁻, CO₃²⁻ |
| **Charge** | Radical par likha plus ya minus ka nishaan aur uski ginti | Ca²⁺ mein 2 plus, PO₄³⁻ mein 3 minus |

## Part A: Radical kya hota hai?

Topic 2 mein humne **ion** ka naam suna tha: charge wala kan. Radical aur ion lagbhag ek hi cheez hain, bas radical naam mostly tab bolte hain jab hum **formula banane** ki baat kar rahe hote hain. Do tarah ke radical hote hain:

1. **Simple radical:** sirf ek atom, uske upar charge. Jaise Na⁺, Cl⁻, Mg²⁺, O²⁻.
2. **Compound radical (polyatomic):** kai atoms ek saath jude hue, aur **poore group ke upar ek hi charge**. Jaise SO₄²⁻, NO₃⁻, NH₄⁺, OH⁻.

Charge kaise aata hai? Ek line mein: jab koi atom ya atoms ka group **electron le leta hai ya de deta hai**, to wo charge wala ban jaata hai. (Electron kya hai aur kyun lete ya dete hain, ye **Chapter 5 "Structure of the Atom"** mein samjhenge. Abhi bas charge ko ek tag ki tarah yaad rakho.)

- Electron **de** diye to **plus (+)** charge, jaise Na⁺.
- Electron **le** liye to **minus (−)** charge, jaise Cl⁻.

### Ek unit, ek charge

Compound radical ki sabse zaroori baat: **charge poore group par hai, kisi ek atom par nahi.** Sulphate SO₄²⁻ mein ek S aur chaar O hain, par 2⁻ ka charge pure group ka hai. Isliye us group ko bina tode, ek saath, ek ticket ki tarah chalna padta hai.

```
        SO₄ radical (sulphate)

        +-----------------+
        |   O      O      |
        |     \  /        |
        |      S          |   ===>  SO₄²⁻
        |     /  \        |
        |   O      O      |
        +-----------------+

   1 S + 4 O  = 5 atoms, par ek hi unit
   poore group par charge: 2 minus
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Sulphate radical SO₄²⁻: ek sulphur aur chaar oxygen atoms ek saath ek unit banate hain, aur poore group par 2 minus ka charge hai. Likhte waqt charge ko poore formula ke baad, upar kone par likhte hain: SO₄²⁻.

Notation dhyan se padho:
- Symbol ke **neeche chhota number** (subscript): us atom ki ginti, jaise SO₄ mein O ke 4 atoms.
- Poore group ke **upar chhota number aur nishaan** (superscript): charge, jaise ²⁻.
- Agar charge ek hi hai to sirf nishaan likhte hain: Na⁺, Cl⁻ (1 nahi likhte).

## Part B: Positive radicals (basic radicals)

Positive radical wo hain jin par **plus** charge hai. Zyada tar dhaatu (metal) ke atoms positive radical banate hain. Ek ajeeb apwaad: **NH₄⁺** (ammonium) ek compound radical hai jo do adhaatu (N aur H) se bana hai, par positive hai. Aur H⁺ bhi positive radical hai.

| Naam | Formula | Charge |
|---|---|---|
| Hydrogen | H⁺ | 1 plus |
| Sodium | Na⁺ | 1 plus |
| Potassium | K⁺ | 1 plus |
| Silver | Ag⁺ | 1 plus |
| Ammonium | NH₄⁺ | 1 plus |
| Magnesium | Mg²⁺ | 2 plus |
| Calcium | Ca²⁺ | 2 plus |
| Zinc | Zn²⁺ | 2 plus |
| Copper(I) (cuprous) | Cu⁺ | 1 plus |
| Copper(II) (cupric) | Cu²⁺ | 2 plus |
| Iron(II) (ferrous) | Fe²⁺ | 2 plus |
| Lead | Pb²⁺ | 2 plus |
| Aluminium | Al³⁺ | 3 plus |
| Iron(III) (ferric) | Fe³⁺ | 3 plus |

(Copper aur iron do-do roop mein mil sakte hain, ye hum Topic 3 ki **variable valency** mein dekh chuke hain. Isliye unke naam ke saath bracket mein Roman number likhte hain, jo charge ka number hai: Fe²⁺ iron(II), Fe³⁺ iron(III), Cu⁺ copper(I), Cu²⁺ copper(II). Ferrous, ferric, cuprous, cupric inke purane naam hain; is chapter mein aage hum Roman number wale naam hi use karenge.)

Charge ke hisaab se teen group yaad rakhna aasaan hai:

| Charge | Radicals |
|---|---|
| 1 plus | H⁺, Na⁺, K⁺, Ag⁺, NH₄⁺, Cu⁺ |
| 2 plus | Mg²⁺, Ca²⁺, Zn²⁺, Cu²⁺, Fe²⁺, Pb²⁺ |
| 3 plus | Al³⁺, Fe³⁺ |

## Part C: Negative radicals (acid radicals)

Negative radical wo hain jin par **minus** charge hai. Zyada tar adhaatu ke atoms ya oxygen wale groups yahan aate hain.

| Naam | Formula | Charge |
|---|---|---|
| Chloride | Cl⁻ | 1 minus |
| Bromide | Br⁻ | 1 minus |
| Iodide | I⁻ | 1 minus |
| Hydroxide | OH⁻ | 1 minus |
| Nitrate | NO₃⁻ | 1 minus |
| Hydrogencarbonate (bicarbonate) | HCO₃⁻ | 1 minus |
| Oxide | O²⁻ | 2 minus |
| Sulphide | S²⁻ | 2 minus |
| Sulphate | SO₄²⁻ | 2 minus |
| Sulphite | SO₃²⁻ | 2 minus |
| Carbonate | CO₃²⁻ | 2 minus |
| Phosphate | PO₄³⁻ | 3 minus |

Yahan bhi charge ke hisaab se group:

| Charge | Radicals |
|---|---|
| 1 minus | Cl⁻, Br⁻, I⁻, OH⁻, NO₃⁻, HCO₃⁻ |
| 2 minus | O²⁻, S²⁻, SO₄²⁻, SO₃²⁻, CO₃²⁻ |
| 3 minus | PO₄³⁻ |

Is list mein **simple** radical hain Cl⁻, Br⁻, I⁻, O²⁻, S²⁻ (ek atom). Baaki **compound** radical hain: OH⁻, NO₃⁻, HCO₃⁻, SO₄²⁻, SO₃²⁻, CO₃²⁻, PO₄³⁻.

## Part D: Charge aur valency: ek hi number

Topic 3 mein valency ek number thi: Na ki 1, Mg ki 2, Al ki 3, O ki 2, Cl ki 1. Ab dekho radical ka charge:

| Tatva ya radical | Valency (Topic 3) | Charge |
|---|---|---|
| Na | 1 | Na⁺ (1 plus) |
| Mg | 2 | Mg²⁺ (2 plus) |
| Al | 3 | Al³⁺ (3 plus) |
| Cl | 1 | Cl⁻ (1 minus) |
| O | 2 | O²⁻ (2 minus) |

**Number wahi hai.** Valency batati hai "kitna jodne ki kshamta", aur charge bas us kshamta ka **tag** hai: plus ya minus ke saath. Isliye:

> **Charge ka number = valency**, aur nishaan (+ ya −) batata hai ki radical positive hai ya negative.

Compound radical ki valency bhi isi tarah hoti hai: SO₄²⁻ ki valency 2, PO₄³⁻ ki valency 3, NO₃⁻ ki valency 1. Ye number agle topic mein formula banane ke kaam aayega.

## Part E: Naam rakhne ke niyam

Radicals ke naam rakhne ke kuch seedhe niyam hain.

**Niyam 1: Positive radical ka naam, tatva ka naam hi hota hai.** Na⁺ ko sodium, Ca²⁺ ko calcium, Al³⁺ ko aluminium. Sirf NH₄⁺ ka alag naam hai: ammonium.

**Niyam 2: Ek atom wale negative radical ka naam "-ide" par khatam hota hai.** Chlorine se chloride (Cl⁻), bromine se bromide (Br⁻), iodine se iodide (I⁻), sulphur se sulphide (S²⁻), oxygen se oxide (O²⁻).

**Niyam 3: Oxygen wale negative radicals ka naam "-ate" ya "-ite" par khatam hota hai.**
- Jis radical mein oxygen **zyada** hai, usse **-ate**: SO₄²⁻ sulphate, NO₃⁻ nitrate, CO₃²⁻ carbonate, PO₄³⁻ phosphate.
- Jis radical mein usi tatva ke saath oxygen **ek kam** hai, usse **-ite**: SO₃²⁻ sulphite.

**Niyam 4: OH⁻ ek apwaad hai.** Isme oxygen aur hydrogen dono hain, phir bhi iska naam **hydroxide** hai ("-ide" par khatam). Aur HCO₃⁻ ka naam hydrogencarbonate (ya bicarbonate), kyunki isme carbonate ke saath ek H laga hai.

Is tarah naam ka ek chhota chart:

```
   Ek atom wala radical -> "-ide"
   Cl⁻   chloride
   S²⁻   sulphide
   O²⁻   oxide

   Oxygen wala group
   SO₄²⁻   sulphate    (zyada O)   -> "-ate"
   SO₃²⁻   sulphite    (ek kam O)  -> "-ite"
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Naam ka niyam: ek atom wale negative radical ka naam "-ide" par khatam hota hai, aur oxygen wale group ka "-ate" (zyada oxygen) ya "-ite" (ek kam oxygen).

## Part F: Solved examples

**Example 1 (radical ka naam batao):** Naam likho: (a) SO₄²⁻ (b) NO₃⁻ (c) Ca²⁺ (d) OH⁻ (e) Br⁻.

- (a) SO₄²⁻: oxygen wala group, 4 oxygen, zyada, to **sulphate**
- (b) NO₃⁻: **nitrate**
- (c) Ca²⁺: positive radical, tatva ka naam hi, **calcium**
- (d) OH⁻: apwaad wala naam, **hydroxide**
- (e) Br⁻: ek atom wala negative radical, **bromide**

**Jaanch:** Table se milaya, sab naam sahi ✅.

**Example 2 (radical mein atoms ginna):** Neeche diye radicals mein kitne atoms hain aur kaun-kaun se? (a) SO₄²⁻ (b) PO₄³⁻ (c) NH₄⁺ (d) HCO₃⁻ (e) CO₃²⁻

- (a) SO₄²⁻: S ke 1 + O ke 4 = **5 atoms**
- (b) PO₄³⁻: P ke 1 + O ke 4 = **5 atoms**
- (c) NH₄⁺: N ke 1 + H ke 4 = **5 atoms**
- (d) HCO₃⁻: H ke 1 + C ke 1 + O ke 3 = **5 atoms** (teen alag tatva)
- (e) CO₃²⁻: C ke 1 + O ke 3 = **4 atoms**

**Jaanch:** Har symbol ke baad likhe number joda, jahan koi number nahi hai wahan 1 liya ✅. Dhyan do, upar likha charge (jaise ²⁻) atom ki ginti nahi hai.

**Example 3 (mixed list ko baanto):** Is list ko positive aur negative radicals mein baanto: Na⁺, Cl⁻, Ca²⁺, OH⁻, NH₄⁺, CO₃²⁻, Al³⁺, NO₃⁻, S²⁻, Fe³⁺.

| Positive radicals | Negative radicals |
|---|---|
| Na⁺, Ca²⁺, NH₄⁺, Al³⁺, Fe³⁺ | Cl⁻, OH⁻, CO₃²⁻, NO₃⁻, S²⁻ |

**Jaanch:** Positive 5, negative 5, kul 5 + 5 = 10, jo list mein diye gaye radicals ki ginti hai ✅.

**Example 4 (charge se valency):** Neeche diye radicals ki valency batao: (a) Mg²⁺ (b) Cl⁻ (c) PO₄³⁻ (d) Fe³⁺ (e) OH⁻.

- (a) Mg²⁺: charge ka number 2, valency **2**
- (b) Cl⁻: number 1, valency **1**
- (c) PO₄³⁻: number 3, valency **3**
- (d) Fe³⁺: number 3, valency **3**
- (e) OH⁻: number 1, valency **1**

**Jaanch:** Topic 3 mein Mg ki valency 2, Cl ki 1 aur Al ki 3 thi, aur ye isse match karti hai ✅. Fe ki 3 wali valency Topic 3 ke variable valency wale hisse mein bhi thi ✅.

**Example 5 (sulphate ya sulphite?):** Ek radical SO₃²⁻ hai aur doosra SO₄²⁻. Kaun sa sulphate hai, kaun sa sulphite? Dono mein kitne atoms hain?

- SO₄²⁻ mein oxygen zyada (4), to **sulphate (-ate)**; atoms: 1 + 4 = 5.
- SO₃²⁻ mein oxygen ek kam (3), to **sulphite (-ite)**; atoms: 1 + 3 = 4.

**Jaanch:** Dono par charge 2 minus hai, valency dono ki 2 ✅. Faasla sirf ek oxygen ka hai: 5 − 4 = 1 ✅.

**Example 6 (radical ko ek unit ki tarah ginna):** Agar hum NO₃⁻ ke **do** poore radicals saath le len, to kul kitne N atoms aur kitne O atoms honge? Kul charge kitna hoga?

- Ek NO₃⁻ mein: 1 N aur 3 O.
- Do radicals mein: N = 2 × 1 = **2** aur O = 2 × 3 = **6**.
- Kul charge = 2 × (1 minus) = **2 minus**.

**Jaanch:** Atoms kul 2 + 6 = 8, aur ek radical ke 4 atoms ka double bhi 8 ✅. Dhyan do: poora radical ek ticket ki tarah double hua, bich se nahi toota.

## Part G: Yaad rakhne ke tricks

1. **Ticket wali baat:** radical ek tempo traveller ka ek ticket hai, kabhi tootta nahi. Jab ginti karni ho to poora group ginte hain.
2. **Positive wale dhaatu:** Na, K, Ag, Mg, Ca, Zn, Cu, Fe, Pb, Al sab dhaatu hain, aur positive hain. Exceptions yaad rakho: **H⁺ aur NH₄⁺** bhi positive hain.
3. **Teen charge wale kam hain:** Positive mein sirf Al³⁺ aur Fe³⁺, negative mein sirf PO₄³⁻. Inhe alag se yaad rakho, baaki sab 1 ya 2.
4. **"-ide" matlab ek atom, "-ate" matlab zyada oxygen, "-ite" matlab ek kam.** Yaad rakhne ke liye: "-ate" wale ne oxygen zyada "ate" kiya (khaaya), "-ite" wale ne ek kam.
5. **Oxygen wale 1 minus group:** NO₃⁻ (nitrate), HCO₃⁻ (hydrogencarbonate) aur OH⁻ (hydroxide), teeno par sirf 1 minus.
6. **Sulphate, sulphite, carbonate teeno 2 minus**, aur phosphate akela 3 minus: "Phosphate, Paanch atoms, Teen minus" (P se shuru, PO₄³⁻).
7. **Chhoti tukbandi:** "Na K Ag, H aur NH₄: ek-ek plus ke dost" (1 plus wale). "Mg Ca Zn Cu Fe Pb: do-do plus ke dost" (2 plus wale).

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Charge 2⁻ sirf O par hai, S par nahi" (SO₄²⁻ mein)**

Nahi. SO₄²⁻ mein 2 minus ka charge **poore group** par hai, kisi ek atom par nahi. Group ko tod kar ek-ek atom ka charge nahi nikalte.

**Galti 2: "Subscript aur charge ek hi cheez hain"**

Nahi. Subscript (neeche ka chhota number) atoms ki ginti hai, jaise SO₄ mein O ke 4 atoms. Charge (upar ka chhota number aur nishaan) alag cheez hai, jaise ²⁻.

**Galti 3: "OH⁻ ka naam oxide hai"**

Nahi. O²⁻ oxide hai (ek atom, 2 minus). OH⁻ hydroxide hai (do atoms, 1 minus). Dono alag radical hain.

**Galti 4: "Sulphate aur sulphite ek hi hain"**

Nahi. SO₄²⁻ sulphate (4 oxygen), SO₃²⁻ sulphite (3 oxygen). Naam aur oxygen ki ginti dono alag hain.

**Galti 5: "NH₄⁺ positive nahi ho sakta kyunki isme adhaatu hain"**

Nahi. Ammonium NH₄⁺ ek positive radical hai, bhale hi N aur H dono adhaatu hain.

**Galti 6: "Charge likhte waqt plus ya minus ka nishaan chhod do"**

Nahi. Nishaan bahut zaroori hai. Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ ka number dono 1 hai, par plus aur minus se hi pata chalta hai ki kaun kya hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Radical:** charge wala ek atom (simple) ya atoms ka group (compound radical) jo ek unit ki tarah chalta hai. Charge poore group par hota hai.
- **Positive radicals (basic):** H⁺, Na⁺, K⁺, Ag⁺, NH₄⁺, Cu⁺ (1 plus); Mg²⁺, Ca²⁺, Zn²⁺, Cu²⁺, Fe²⁺, Pb²⁺ (2 plus); Al³⁺, Fe³⁺ (3 plus).
- **Negative radicals (acid):** Cl⁻, Br⁻, I⁻, OH⁻, NO₃⁻, HCO₃⁻ (1 minus); O²⁻, S²⁻, SO₄²⁻, SO₃²⁻, CO₃²⁻ (2 minus); PO₄³⁻ (3 minus).
- **Charge ka number valency ke barabar hota hai**, nishaan (+ ya −) positive ya negative batata hai.
- **Naming:** positive ka naam tatva ka naam (NH₄⁺ ammonium); ek atom wale negative ka "-ide"; oxygen wale group ka "-ate" (zyada O) ya "-ite" (ek kam O); OH⁻ hydroxide.
- Radical ko ginte waqt poora group ek unit hai; subscript atoms ki ginti, superscript charge.

## Practice Questions

1. Define a radical. Give two examples of a simple radical and two of a compound radical.

2. Write the name and the charge of each: Mg²⁺, K⁺, Zn²⁺, Br⁻, S²⁻, HCO₃⁻.

3. Write the formula (with charge) of each radical: (a) calcium, (b) ammonium, (c) carbonate, (d) phosphate, (e) hydroxide, (f) iron(III).

4. How many atoms are there in each of these radicals, and which elements do they contain? (a) NO₃⁻ (b) HCO₃⁻ (c) NH₄⁺ (d) OH⁻

5. Sort into positive and negative radicals: Ag⁺, I⁻, SO₄²⁻, Pb²⁺, Fe²⁺, O²⁻, K⁺, PO₄³⁻.

6. State the valency of each: Na⁺, Ca²⁺, Al³⁺, Cl⁻, SO₄²⁻, PO₄³⁻.

7. What is the difference between sulphate and sulphite? Write the formula and charge of each.

8. If you take 3 sulphate radicals (SO₄²⁻) together, how many S atoms and how many O atoms are there in all, and what is the total charge?

9. Hydroxide (OH⁻) contains oxygen, yet its name ends in "-ide". Explain this exception. Also say how HCO₃⁻ differs from CO₃²⁻ in name, formula and charge.

10. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) Cl⁻ is a positive radical.
    (b) NH₄⁺ is a positive radical.
    (c) The charge on SO₄²⁻ is on the sulphur atom only.
    (d) The number in the charge of a radical is equal to its valency.
    (e) OH⁻ is called oxide.

### Answers

1. **Radical:** charge wala ek atom ya atoms ka group, jo ek unit ki tarah kaam karta hai. Simple radical examples: Na⁺, Cl⁻ (aur Mg²⁺, O²⁻). Compound radical examples: SO₄²⁻, NO₃⁻ (aur NH₄⁺, OH⁻).

2. Mg²⁺: magnesium, 2 plus. K⁺: potassium, 1 plus. Zn²⁺: zinc, 2 plus. Br⁻: bromide, 1 minus. S²⁻: sulphide, 2 minus. HCO₃⁻: hydrogencarbonate (bicarbonate), 1 minus.

3. (a) Ca²⁺ (b) NH₄⁺ (c) CO₃²⁻ (d) PO₄³⁻ (e) OH⁻ (f) Fe³⁺

4. (a) NO₃⁻: 1 N + 3 O = **4 atoms**. (b) HCO₃⁻: 1 H + 1 C + 3 O = **5 atoms**. (c) NH₄⁺: 1 N + 4 H = **5 atoms**. (d) OH⁻: 1 O + 1 H = **2 atoms**.

5. Positive: Ag⁺, Pb²⁺, Fe²⁺, K⁺. Negative: I⁻, SO₄²⁻, O²⁻, PO₄³⁻. Jaanch: 4 + 4 = 8 ✅.

6. Na⁺: 1. Ca²⁺: 2. Al³⁺: 3. Cl⁻: 1. SO₄²⁻: 2. PO₄³⁻: 3.

7. Sulphate **SO₄²⁻** (4 oxygen, "-ate"), sulphite **SO₃²⁻** (3 oxygen, ek kam, "-ite"). Dono par 2 minus ka charge hai.

8. 3 radicals mein S = 3 × 1 = **3**, O = 3 × 4 = **12**, kul charge = 3 × 2 minus = **6 minus**. Jaanch: ek radical ke 5 atoms, 3 radical ke 15 atoms, aur 3 + 12 = 15 ✅.

9. **OH⁻** (hydroxide) mein oxygen hai par naam "-ide" hai, "-ate" nahi: ye ek apwaad hai jise seedha yaad karna hota hai. **HCO₃⁻** hydrogencarbonate hai (ek H ke saath, charge 1 minus), jabki **CO₃²⁻** carbonate hai (H nahi, charge 2 minus).

10. (a) **False** ❌. Cl⁻ negative radical hai (chloride). (b) **True** ✅. (c) **False** ❌. 2 minus ka charge poore SO₄ group par hai. (d) **True** ✅. (e) **False** ❌. OH⁻ hydroxide hai; oxide O²⁻ hai.
