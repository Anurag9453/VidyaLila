# Topic 5: Chemical Formula: Criss-Cross Se Banao Aur Naam Likho

Pichhle topic mein humne radicals ki do tables yaad ki: positive charge wale (Na⁺, Ca²⁺, Al³⁺, NH₄⁺ ...) aur negative charge wale (Cl⁻, O²⁻, SO₄²⁻, PO₄³⁻ ...). Ek baat bhi pakki hui thi: **charge aur valency ek hi number hain**. Ab sawal ye hai ki in tukdon ko jod kar kisi compound ka **poora formula** kaise likhein? Kitne Na⁺ ek SO₄²⁻ ke saath aayenge? Aaj ek chhoti si chaal (criss-cross) seekhenge jisse koi bhi formula 30 second mein ban jaata hai, aur phir ulta kaam bhi: formula dekh kar naam likhna.

---

## Gadget Gali ki dukaan

Shanivaar ko Ms. Ojha apne bhatije Naksh ko apni mobile accessories ki dukaan "Gadget Gali" mein le gayi. Counter par chargers, cables aur adapters ke dibbe the. Naksh ne ek 3-pin wala charger uthaya aur ek 2-hole wale socket mein lagane ki koshish ki. Nahi laga.

"Aunty, ye to fit hi nahi ho raha!" usne kaha.

Ms. Ojha hansi. "Har plug ke paas **kuch pins** hote hain aur har socket ke paas **kuch holes**. Jodi tabhi poori hoti hai jab **pins aur holes barabar** ho jaayein. 3-pin wale plug ko 3-hole wala socket chahiye. Agar tumhare paas 3-pin wale plug hain aur sirf 2-hole wale socket, to kya karoge?"

Naksh ne socha. "Plug aur socket **ginti mein badha lunga**. 2 plug se 6 pins, aur 3 socket se 6 holes. Ab 6 aur 6 barabar!"

"Bilkul. Yehi chemistry ka formula banane ka raaz hai," Ms. Ojha ne kaha. "Positive radical **plug** hai, jiske charge ke number jitne pins hain. Negative radical **socket** hai, jiske charge ke number jitne holes hain. Formula wahi, jisme total pins aur total holes barabar hon. Aaj ye 'matching' hum seekhenge."

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Chemical formula** | Kisi compound ko symbols aur chhote numbers se likhne ka tareeka, jo batata hai ki kaun se tatva ya radical, kitni ginti mein hain | NaCl, CaCO₃ |
| **Subscript** | Symbol ya bracket ke **neeche** likha chhota number: batata hai kitne atom ya radical | H₂O mein 2, Al₂O₃ mein 2 aur 3 |
| **Coefficient** | Poore formula ke **aage** likha bada number: batata hai poori jodi kitni baar | 2NaCl (NaCl ki do jodi) |
| **Criss-cross** | Positive aur negative ke charge ke numbers ko **aar-paar badal kar** subscript banana | Al³⁺ aur O²⁻ se Al₂O₃ |
| **Bracket** | Radical ko ek hi tukda dikhane ke liye lagaya jaane wala ( ) | Ca(OH)₂ |
| **Lowest ratio** | Subscripts ka sabse chhota poora-number anupaat, jisme koi aur number dono ko bhaag na de | Mg₂O₂ ki jagah MgO |

## Part A: Chemical formula kya batata hai?

Formula ek chhoti si **recipe card** hai. Isme se kuch padh sakte hain:

- Compound mein **kaun se tatva ya radical** hain.
- Har ek ki **kitni ginti** hai (neeche wale chhote numbers se).
- Jahan subscript nahi likha, wahan matlab **1**.

Dekho **Ca(OH)₂** ko:

- Ca: 1 (kuch likha nahi, matlab 1)
- (OH): bracket ke baad 2 likha hai, yaani OH radical **2** baar
- Bracket ke andar O aur H dono ek-ek hain, to 2 radical mein **2 O aur 2 H**
- Kul atom = 1 + 2 + 2 = **5**

**Jaanch:** Ca ka charge 2⁺ aur 2 OH ka charge 2 × 1⁻ = 2⁻. Positive 2 = negative 2 ✅.

Ek baat yaad rakho: **formula mein charge (⁺ ya ⁻) nahi likhte.** Wo sirf banate waqt kaam aate hain. Final formula mein sirf symbols aur subscripts hote hain. Aur compound **bina kisi kul charge** ka hota hai, yaani poora neutral.

## Part B: Criss-cross method, step by step

Ye wahi plug-socket ki ginti hai, bas ek tarteeb mein likhi hui.

**Step 1: Dono ke symbols likho, charge ke saath.** Pehle positive radical (basic radical), phir negative (acid radical).

**Step 2: Sirf charge ka number dekho.** Plus ya minus ka chinh abhi hata do.

**Step 3: Criss-cross karo.** Positive ka number negative ke **neeche** subscript ban jaata hai. Negative ka number positive ke **neeche** subscript ban jaata hai. Aar-paar!

**Step 4: Lowest ratio mein laao.** Agar dono subscripts ko koi ek number bhaag deta hai (2 aur 2, 4 aur 2 ...), to bhaag do.

**Step 5: Bracket lagao.** Agar koi **radical** (do ya zyada atom wala group) ek se zyada baar aaya, to use **bracket mein** rakh kar subscript bahar likho. Subscript 1 ho to na likho, na bracket lagao.

**Step 6: Jaanch.** Kul positive charge = kul negative charge? Matlab (positive ka number × uski ginti) = (negative ka number × uski ginti).

```
      Al  3+              2-  O
            \            /
             \          /
              \        /
               \      /
                \    /
                 \  /
                  \/
                  /\
                 /  \
                /    \
               /      \
              v        v
          Al   2      3   O

           ==>   Al₂O₃
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Criss-cross: Al³⁺ ka 3 neeche jaakar O ka subscript ban gaya, aur O²⁻ ka 2 neeche jaakar Al ka subscript. Natija Al₂O₃. Jaanch: 2 × 3 = 6 positive, 3 × 2 = 6 negative, barabar.

## Part C: Solved formulae

Har example mein positive radical pehle aur negative baad mein.

**Example 1: Sodium chloride (Na⁺ aur Cl⁻)**

- Charge numbers: 1 aur 1. Criss-cross: Na₁Cl₁, subscript 1 likhte nahi.
- Formula: **NaCl**

**Jaanch:** Positive 1 × 1 = 1, negative 1 × 1 = 1, barabar ✅.

**Example 2: Magnesium chloride (Mg²⁺ aur Cl⁻)**

- Charge numbers: 2 aur 1. Criss-cross: Mg ke neeche 1, Cl ke neeche 2.
- Formula: **MgCl₂**

**Jaanch:** Positive 1 × 2 = 2, negative 2 × 1 = 2, barabar ✅.

**Example 3: Aluminium oxide (Al³⁺ aur O²⁻)**

- Charge numbers: 3 aur 2. Criss-cross: Al₂O₃.
- Formula: **Al₂O₃**

**Jaanch:** Positive 2 × 3 = 6, negative 3 × 2 = 6, barabar ✅.

**Example 4: Magnesium oxide (Mg²⁺ aur O²⁻), anupaat chhota karna**

- Charge numbers: 2 aur 2. Criss-cross: Mg₂O₂.
- Dono subscripts ko 2 se bhaag do: Mg₁O₁.
- Formula: **MgO** (Mg₂O₂ nahi, kyunki lowest ratio chahiye).

**Jaanch:** Positive 1 × 2 = 2, negative 1 × 2 = 2, barabar ✅.

**Example 5: Calcium carbonate (Ca²⁺ aur CO₃²⁻)**

- Charge numbers: 2 aur 2. Criss-cross: Ca₂(CO₃)₂. Bhaag do 2 se: Ca₁(CO₃)₁.
- Ab CO₃ sirf ek baar hai, to bracket ki zarurat nahi.
- Formula: **CaCO₃**

**Jaanch:** Positive 1 × 2 = 2, negative 1 × 2 = 2, barabar ✅.

**Example 6: Calcium hydroxide (Ca²⁺ aur OH⁻)**

- Charge numbers: 2 aur 1. Criss-cross: Ca ke neeche 1, OH ke neeche 2.
- OH radical **2** baar aaya, to bracket: Ca(OH)₂.
- Formula: **Ca(OH)₂**

**Jaanch:** Positive 1 × 2 = 2, negative 2 × 1 = 2, barabar ✅.

**Example 7: Potassium carbonate (K⁺ aur CO₃²⁻)**

- Charge numbers: 1 aur 2. Criss-cross: K ke neeche 2, CO₃ ke neeche 1.
- CO₃ sirf ek baar, to bracket nahi.
- Formula: **K₂CO₃**

**Jaanch:** Positive 2 × 1 = 2, negative 1 × 2 = 2, barabar ✅.

**Example 8: Ammonium sulphate (NH₄⁺ aur SO₄²⁻)**

- Charge numbers: 1 aur 2. Criss-cross: NH₄ ke neeche 2, SO₄ ke neeche 1.
- NH₄ radical 2 baar aaya, to bracket: (NH₄)₂. SO₄ ek baar.
- Formula: **(NH₄)₂SO₄**

**Jaanch:** Positive 2 × 1 = 2, negative 1 × 2 = 2, barabar ✅. (Atom ginti: N 2, H 8, S 1, O 4, kul 15.)

**Example 9: Iron(III) oxide (Fe³⁺ aur O²⁻)**

- Charge numbers: 3 aur 2. Criss-cross: Fe₂O₃. Aur kuch bhaagne ko nahi (2 aur 3 mein koi common factor nahi).
- Formula: **Fe₂O₃**

**Jaanch:** Positive 2 × 3 = 6, negative 3 × 2 = 6, barabar ✅. (Fe ki valency 3 chuni, kyunki naam mein iron(III) hai. Fe²⁺ chunte to FeO banta, wo alag compound hai.)

**Example 10: Aluminium sulphate (Al³⁺ aur SO₄²⁻)**

- Charge numbers: 3 aur 2. Criss-cross: Al ke neeche 2, SO₄ ke neeche 3.
- SO₄ radical 3 baar aaya, to bracket: (SO₄)₃.
- Formula: **Al₂(SO₄)₃**

**Jaanch:** Positive 2 × 3 = 6, negative 3 × 2 = 6, barabar ✅. (Atom ginti: Al 2, S 3, O 12, kul 17.)

**Example 11: Calcium phosphate (Ca²⁺ aur PO₄³⁻)**

- Charge numbers: 2 aur 3. Criss-cross: Ca ke neeche 3, PO₄ ke neeche 2.
- Ca aur PO₄ dono ek se zyada baar aaye. Ca akela atom hai, isliye bracket nahi: Ca₃. PO₄ ek radical hai, isliye bracket chahiye: (PO₄)₂.
- Formula: **Ca₃(PO₄)₂**

**Jaanch:** Positive 3 × 2 = 6, negative 2 × 3 = 6, barabar ✅. (Atom ginti: Ca 3, P 2, O 8, kul 13.)

**Example 12: Aluminium hydroxide (Al³⁺ aur OH⁻)**

- Charge numbers: 3 aur 1. Criss-cross: Al ke neeche 1, OH ke neeche 3.
- Formula: **Al(OH)₃**

**Jaanch:** Positive 1 × 3 = 3, negative 3 × 1 = 3, barabar ✅.

Ek jagah dekho sab:

| Positive | Negative | Criss-cross se | Final formula | Positive kul | Negative kul |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Na⁺ | Cl⁻ | Na₁Cl₁ | NaCl | 1 | 1 |
| Mg²⁺ | Cl⁻ | Mg₁Cl₂ | MgCl₂ | 2 | 2 |
| Al³⁺ | O²⁻ | Al₂O₃ | Al₂O₃ | 6 | 6 |
| Mg²⁺ | O²⁻ | Mg₂O₂, bhaag do | MgO | 2 | 2 |
| Ca²⁺ | CO₃²⁻ | Ca₂(CO₃)₂, bhaag do | CaCO₃ | 2 | 2 |
| Ca²⁺ | OH⁻ | Ca₁(OH)₂ | Ca(OH)₂ | 2 | 2 |
| K⁺ | CO₃²⁻ | K₂(CO₃)₁ | K₂CO₃ | 2 | 2 |
| NH₄⁺ | SO₄²⁻ | (NH₄)₂SO₄ | (NH₄)₂SO₄ | 2 | 2 |
| Fe³⁺ | O²⁻ | Fe₂O₃ | Fe₂O₃ | 6 | 6 |
| Al³⁺ | SO₄²⁻ | Al₂(SO₄)₃ | Al₂(SO₄)₃ | 6 | 6 |
| Ca²⁺ | PO₄³⁻ | Ca₃(PO₄)₂ | Ca₃(PO₄)₂ | 6 | 6 |
| Al³⁺ | OH⁻ | Al₁(OH)₃ | Al(OH)₃ | 3 | 3 |

## Part D: Charge-balance ki tasveer

Criss-cross ka asli matlab "pins = holes" hai. Do example dekho:

```
   Ca(OH)₂ :   Ca²⁺     OH⁻     OH⁻
                (+ +)    (-)     (-)
               2 plus   1 minus  1 minus
               ----------------------
               +2   =   -1 + -1 = -2  (kul 0)

   Al₂O₃  :   Al³⁺ Al³⁺          O²⁻ O²⁻ O²⁻
              (+++)(+++)        (--)(--)(--)
              6 plus     =       6 minus   (kul 0)
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Charge-balance: Ca(OH)₂ mein ek Ca²⁺ ke 2 plus ko do OH⁻ ke 2 minus rok dete hain. Al₂O₃ mein do Al³⁺ ke 6 plus ko teen O²⁻ ke 6 minus rok dete hain. Jab plus aur minus barabar hon, compound neutral ban jaata hai.

Isliye Part B ka Step 6 hamesha lagao. Agar plus aur minus barabar nahi hue, to kahin galti hai.

## Part E: Ulta kaam, formula se naam likhna

Naam likhna aur bhi aasaan hai.

**Niyam:**

1. **Pehle positive** radical ka naam, **phir negative** ka naam.
2. Subscripts naam mein **nahi** aate. (NaCl ho ya MgCl₂, naam mein "ek" ya "do" nahi bolte: sodium chloride, magnesium chloride.)
3. Simple negative atom ke liye **-ide** (chloride, oxide, sulphide). Oxygen wale radical ke liye **-ate** (zyada oxygen) ya **-ite** (kam oxygen): sulphate SO₄²⁻, sulphite SO₃²⁻.
4. Jin metals ke do charge hote hain (Fe, Cu), unka charge **bracket mein roman number** se likhte hain: iron(II), iron(III). Charge nikalne ke liye negative ka kul charge dekho aur positive ki ginti se bhaag do.

| Formula | Positive kul = Negative kul | Naam |
|---|---|---|
| NaCl | 1 = 1 | sodium chloride |
| K₂S | 2 = 2 | potassium sulphide |
| Na₂SO₄ | 2 = 2 | sodium sulphate |
| Na₂SO₃ | 2 = 2 | sodium sulphite |
| KNO₃ | 1 = 1 | potassium nitrate |
| NaHCO₃ | 1 = 1 | sodium hydrogencarbonate |
| Mg(OH)₂ | 2 = 2 | magnesium hydroxide |
| AgNO₃ | 1 = 1 | silver nitrate |
| ZnSO₄ | 2 = 2 | zinc sulphate |
| CuSO₄ | 2 = 2 | copper(II) sulphate |
| Fe(OH)₃ | 3 = 3 | iron(III) hydroxide |

Aakhri do mein charge kaise nikala? **CuSO₄:** SO₄ ka charge 2⁻ aur Cu ek hai, to Cu ka charge 2⁺, yaani copper(II). **Fe(OH)₃:** 3 OH ka kul charge 3⁻ aur Fe ek hai, to Fe ka charge 3⁺, yaani iron(III). Dhyan do, **NaHCO₃** mein HCO₃⁻ ek hi radical hai (hydrogencarbonate), isiliye uska charge 1⁻ hai aur Na⁺ ke saath 1 : 1.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Bracket bhoolna**

Ca(OH)₂ ki jagah **CaOH₂** likhna galat hai. CaOH₂ ka matlab hota 1 Ca, 1 O aur 2 H, yaani OH radical tuta hua. Bracket batata hai ki **poora OH** do baar hai. Isi tarah (NH₄)₂SO₄ ki jagah NH₄₂SO₄ nahi.

**Galti 2: Formula theek karne ke liye subscript badal dena**

Maan lo koi NaCl ke bajay "NaCl₂" likh de ki chalo 2 chahiye. Subscript **banta hai charge se**; use apni marzi se nahi badal sakte. Subscript badalne par **compound hi badal jaata hai**. Aur agar poori jodi **ginti mein** dohrani ho (jaise do NaCl), to wo **coefficient** hai jo formula ke **aage** likhte hain: **2NaCl**. Ye Topic 6 mein kaam aayega. Subscript compound ki pehchan hai, coefficient ginti.

**Galti 3: Anupaat chhota nahi karna**

Mg²⁺ aur O²⁻ se Mg₂O₂ likhna galat hai, sahi **MgO**. Hamesha lowest ratio.

**Galti 4: Radical ke andar ka number bhi chhota kar dena**

(NH₄)₂SO₄ ko "NH₄SO₄" ya "(NH₂)₂SO₂" kar dena galat hai. **Radical ke andar** ke numbers (NH₄ ka 4, SO₄ ka 4) radical ki pehchan hain, unhe kabhi nahi chhedte. Ratio chhota karna sirf **bahar wale** subscripts par hota hai (ya jab pura radical ek baar bachta hai, jaise Ca₂(CO₃)₂ se CaCO₃).

**Galti 5: Final formula mein charge likhna**

Na⁺Cl⁻ ya Mg²⁺Cl₂ likhna galat. Final formula mein ⁺ ya ⁻ nahi hote.

**Galti 6: Symbol ki capital aur small galat likhna**

Topic 1 yaad hai? **CO** (carbon aur oxygen) aur **Co** (cobalt) alag hain. Formula mein har symbol ka case dhyan se.

## Quick Recap

- **Chemical formula** compound ki recipe hai: kaun se tatva ya radical, aur kitni ginti mein (neeche ke subscripts se). Subscript 1 likhte nahi.
- **Criss-cross:** symbols aur charge likho, charge ke numbers aar-paar badal kar subscript banao, lowest ratio mein laao, radical ek se zyada baar ho to bracket lagao.
- **Jaanch:** kul positive charge = kul negative charge. Formula mein charge likhte nahi.
- Pakke formulae: NaCl, MgCl₂, MgO, Al₂O₃, CaCO₃, Ca(OH)₂, K₂CO₃, (NH₄)₂SO₄, Fe₂O₃, Al₂(SO₄)₃, Ca₃(PO₄)₂, Al(OH)₃.
- **Naam:** pehle positive, phir negative. Subscripts naam mein nahi aate. -ide, -ate, -ite. Fe aur Cu ke liye roman number: iron(III), copper(II).
- Galtiyan: bracket bhoolna, subscript badalna, ratio chhota na karna, radical ke andar ka number chhedna.
- Subscript compound ki pehchan hai, coefficient (formula ke aage) ginti hai.
