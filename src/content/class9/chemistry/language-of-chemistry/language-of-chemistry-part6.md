# Topic 6: Chemical Equation: Reaction Ki Recipe

Pichhle topic mein humne seekha ki symbols aur valency/charge ki madad se kisi bhi yaugik ka **formula** kaise likhte hain (NaCl, MgCl₂, Al₂O₃, CaCO₃...). Ab tak humare paas chemistry ke **shabd** hain (symbols aur formulae). Ab inse **poore vaakya** banane ka samay hai. Chemistry mein ek poora vaakya wo hota hai jo batata hai ki **kaun si cheezein mil kar kaun si nayi cheezein bana rahi hain**. Isi vaakya ko **chemical equation** kehte hain. Aur sabse zaroori baat: is vaakya ka "hisaab" bilkul sahi hona chahiye.

---

## Kheer ka recipe card

Mr. Mistry ke ghar mein family function tha, aur Yuvika, unki beti, rasoi mein dadi ke saath recipe card dekh rahi thi. Card par kheer ka nuskha 4 logon ke liye likha tha:

**50 g chawal + 1 litre doodh + 100 g cheeni  →  kheer (4 logon ke liye)**

Function mein 20 log aane the. Yuvika ne socha: "Dadi, bas doodh badha dein to?" Dadi hans padi, "Agar sirf doodh 5 guna kar diya aur chawal wahi 50 g rakha, to kheer patli, feeki aur bekaar banegi. Poora nuskha **ek saath, ek hi guna** badhana padta hai. 20 log matlab 5 guna: 250 g chawal, 5 litre doodh aur 500 g cheeni."

Mr. Mistry ne dekha aur kaha, "Yuvika, chemistry ki reaction bhi ek recipe hai. Usme bhi cheezein ek **fixed hisaab** mein judti hain. Aur jaise tum kheer ke saamaan ko gin kar ek hi guna badhati ho, waise hi chemistry mein hum **atoms ko gin kar** recipe ko balance karte hain. Chal, aaj isi ko seekhte hain."

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Reactant** | Jo cheezein reaction shuru karti hain (arrow ke baayein) | Hydrogen aur oxygen |
| **Product** | Jo nayi cheezein ban kar aati hain (arrow ke daayein) | Paani |
| **Word equation** | Reaction ko sirf naamon se likhna | hydrogen + oxygen → water |
| **Skeleton equation** | Naamon ki jagah formulae likhna, par abhi hisaab theek nahi | H₂ + O₂ → H₂O |
| **Balanced equation** | Wo equation jisme har tatva ke atoms arrow ke dono taraf barabar hon | 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O |
| **Coefficient** | Formula ke aage likhi badi sankhya; batati hai kitne "set" liye | 2 in 2H₂O |
| **State symbols** | Bracket mein likhe letters: (s) thos, (l) tarl, (g) gas, (aq) paani mein ghula | H₂O(l) |
| **Hit and trial** | Coefficients ko ek-ek kar ke try karke hisaab milana | Is topic ka tareeka |

## Part A: Word equation se skeleton equation tak

Sabse pehle reaction ko **shabdon mein** likhte hain. Ek taraf reactants, doosri taraf products, beech mein arrow (→), jo "banata hai" ya "ban jaata hai" padha jaata hai.

**Word equation:**

hydrogen + oxygen → water

Ab har naam ki jagah uska formula likhte hain (Topic 2 se yaad karo ki hydrogen aur oxygen gas ke roop mein H₂ aur O₂ hote hain, aur paani H₂O hai):

**Skeleton equation:**

H₂ + O₂ → H₂O

Is "skeleton" (dhaanche) mein formulae to sahi hain, par ek dikkat hai. Gino to: baayein taraf **2 hydrogen** aur **2 oxygen** hain, par daayein taraf **2 hydrogen** aur sirf **1 oxygen**. Ek oxygen atom kahin gayab ho gaya! Ye ho nahi sakta.

---

## Part B: Atoms ka hisaab: kuch gayab nahi hota

Reaction mein atoms **na naye bante hain, na khatam hote hain**. Wo sirf apne saathi **badal lete hain**, jaise naach mein log jodiyan badalte hain par naachne wale wahi rehte hain. Isliye **har tatva ke atoms ki ginti arrow ke dono taraf barabar** honi chahiye. Hum ise "**atoms ka hisaab**" kahenge. (Isko formal roop se kya kehte hain aur kisne seedha dikhaya tha, ye Chapter 4 mein aayega.)

Dekho H₂ + O₂ → H₂O ka hisaab:

```
   H₂  +  O₂   ──►   H₂O

   Baayein side           Daayein side
   +---------------+      +---------------+
   │ H H    O O    │      │ H O H         │
   +---------------+      +---------------+
   H = 2, O = 2           H = 2, O = 1
                 ^
            O barabar nahi!
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Unbalanced equation H₂ + O₂ → H₂O ke atom-counting boxes: baayein O = 2, daayein O = 1. Ek O atom gayab dikh raha hai, isliye equation galat hai.

Ise sahi karne ke liye hum **formulae ko badalte nahi**, sirf unke **aage coefficient** lagate hain. Aao dekhte hain kaise.

## Part C: Hit and trial se balance karna

**Pehli sunehri baat:** coefficient lagana kheer ke nuskhe ko guna karna jaisa hai: "2H₂O" ka matlab hai paani ke **2 poore set**, har set mein H₂O. Hum **subscript kabhi nahi badalte** (H₂O ko H₂O₂ bana diya to wo paani nahi, kuch aur ho gaya, jaise Topic 5 mein dekha tha).

**Balancing ke steps:**

1. Skeleton equation likho, formulae pehle se sahi hone chahiye.
2. Har tatva ke atoms dono taraf gino (ek chhoti table banao).
3. Jo tatva sabse zyada formulae mein aata hai, use **aakhir** mein chhodo. Pehle woh tatva lo jo ek hi formula mein aata hai.
4. Coefficient lagao, phir saare tatva dobara gino (kyunki ek coefficient doosre tatva ka hisaab bigaad sakta hai).
5. Jab sab barabar ho jaaye, coefficients ko dekho: agar sab mein koi common guna hai to use kam karo (jaise 4, 2, 6 ko 2, 1, 3).
6. **Aakhri jaanch:** har tatva ko dobara gin lo.

### Example 1. H₂ + O₂ → H₂O

Hisaab: baayein H = 2, O = 2. Daayein H = 2, O = 1.

- O barabar karne ke liye daayein 2 paani lo: H₂ + O₂ → **2**H₂O. Ab daayein O = 2, par H = 4.
- H barabar karne ke liye baayein 2 hydrogen lo: **2**H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O.

**Balanced:** 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O

Jaanch: H: baayein 2×2 = 4, daayein 2×2 = 4 ✅. O: baayein 2, daayein 2×1 = 2 ✅

```
   2H₂  +  O₂   ──►   2H₂O

   Baayein side                 Daayein side
   +-------------------+        +-------------------+
   │ H H   H H   O O   │        │ H O H     H O H   │
   +-------------------+        +-------------------+
   H = 4, O = 2                 H = 4, O = 2
                    barabar: dono side sahi
```

> **Figure 6.2** – Balanced equation 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O ke atom-counting boxes: dono taraf H = 4 aur O = 2. Atoms wahi hain, bas jodiyan badli hain.

### Example 2. Mg + O₂ → MgO

Hisaab: baayein Mg = 1, O = 2. Daayein Mg = 1, O = 1.

- O barabar karo: daayein **2**MgO. Ab daayein O = 2, par Mg = 2.
- Mg barabar karo: baayein **2**Mg.

**Balanced:** 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO

Jaanch: Mg: 2 = 2 ✅. O: 2 = 2 ✅

### Example 3. Fe + O₂ → Fe₂O₃

Hisaab: baayein Fe = 1, O = 2. Daayein Fe = 2, O = 3.

Yahan O ki ginti **2** (baayein) aur **3** (daayein) hai, jo alag-alag hai. Aise mein dono ka **sabse chhota common multiple (LCM)** lete hain: 2 aur 3 ka LCM = 6.

- Baayein O = 6 karne ke liye **3**O₂ (3 × 2 = 6).
- Daayein O = 6 karne ke liye **2**Fe₂O₃ (2 × 3 = 6).
- Ab daayein Fe = 2 × 2 = 4, to baayein **4**Fe.

**Balanced:** 4Fe + 3O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃

Jaanch: Fe: 4 = 2×2 = 4 ✅. O: 3×2 = 6 = 2×3 = 6 ✅

### Example 4. N₂ + H₂ → NH₃

Hisaab: baayein N = 2, H = 2. Daayein N = 1, H = 3.

- N barabar karo: daayein **2**NH₃. Ab daayein N = 2, H = 6.
- H barabar karo: baayein **3**H₂ (3 × 2 = 6).

**Balanced:** N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃

Jaanch: N: 2 = 2×1 = 2 ✅. H: 3×2 = 6 = 2×3 = 6 ✅

### Example 5. CH₄ + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O

Hisaab: baayein C = 1, H = 4, O = 2. Daayein C = 1, H = 2, O = 3 (CO₂ ke 2 aur H₂O ka 1).

Yahan ek tip: **O sabse aakhir mein**, kyunki wo do formulae mein aata hai.

- C pehle se barabar (1 = 1).
- H barabar karo: daayein **2**H₂O (H = 4). Ab daayein O = 2 (CO₂ se) + 2 (2H₂O se) = 4.
- O barabar karo: baayein **2**O₂ (O = 4).

**Balanced:** CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O

Jaanch: C: 1 = 1 ✅. H: 4 = 2×2 = 4 ✅. O: 2×2 = 4 = 2 + 2 = 4 ✅

### Example 6. Zn + HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂

Hisaab: baayein Zn = 1, H = 1, Cl = 1. Daayein Zn = 1, Cl = 2, H = 2.

- Cl barabar karo: baayein **2**HCl. Ab baayein Cl = 2, H = 2.
- H daayein bhi 2 hai: barabar.

**Balanced:** Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂

Jaanch: Zn: 1 = 1 ✅. H: 2 = 2 ✅. Cl: 2 = 2 ✅

### Example 7. CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂ (garam karne par)

Hisaab: baayein Ca = 1, C = 1, O = 3. Daayein Ca = 1, C = 1, O = 1 + 2 = 3.

Ye pehle se balanced hai! Har baar coefficient lagana zaroori nahi: kabhi-kabhi skeleton hi sahi nikalta hai. Isse bhi **jaanch zaroor karo**.

**Balanced:** CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂

Jaanch: Ca: 1 = 1 ✅. C: 1 = 1 ✅. O: 3 = 1 + 2 = 3 ✅

### Example 8. Al + O₂ → Al₂O₃

Hisaab: baayein Al = 1, O = 2. Daayein Al = 2, O = 3.

Ye bilkul Fe + O₂ jaisa hai. O ke liye LCM(2, 3) = 6.

- Baayein **3**O₂ (O = 6), daayein **2**Al₂O₃ (O = 6).
- Daayein Al = 2 × 2 = 4, to baayein **4**Al.

**Balanced:** 4Al + 3O₂ → 2Al₂O₃

Jaanch: Al: 4 = 2×2 = 4 ✅. O: 3×2 = 6 = 2×3 = 6 ✅

> **Dhyan do:** agar koi kehta hai "2Al + 3O₂ → 2Al₂O₃", to Al ka hisaab galat hai (baayein 2, daayein 4). Aise galat jawab ko pakadne ka ek hi tareeka hai: **har tatva ko dono taraf gino**.

### Example 9 (sabse mushkil). C₃H₈ + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O

Ye propane hai, jo kai gas cylinders mein hota hai. Chaar tatvon wali ek lambi equation lag sakti hai, par ek-ek kar ke sulajhti hai.

Hisaab: baayein C = 3, H = 8, O = 2. Daayein C = 1, H = 2, O = 3.

- **C pehle:** baayein 3 hai, to daayein **3**CO₂. Ab daayein C = 3, O = 6.
- **H doosre:** baayein 8 hai, to daayein **4**H₂O (4 × 2 = 8). Ab daayein O = 6 (CO₂ se) + 4 (H₂O se) = 10.
- **O aakhir mein:** daayein O = 10, to baayein **5**O₂ (5 × 2 = 10).

**Balanced:** C₃H₈ + 5O₂ → 3CO₂ + 4H₂O

Jaanch: C: 3 = 3 ✅. H: 8 = 4×2 = 8 ✅. O: 5×2 = 10 = 3×2 + 4×1 = 10 ✅

Dekho, tareeka wahi raha: **jo tatva sirf ek jagah aata hai usse pehle, O jaisa tatva jo kai jagah hai use aakhir mein.**

## Part D: Kya badlein, kya nahi

```
   2H₂  +  O₂   ──►   2H₂O

   Coefficient (2, 1, 2): badal sakte hain
   Subscript (₂ in H₂, ₂ in O₂, ₂ in H₂O): kabhi nahi badalte
```

> **Figure 6.3** – Balancing mein sirf coefficient badalte hain; subscript formula ka hissa hai aur use badalne se padaarth hi badal jaata hai.

Agar H₂ + O₂ → H₂O mein tum O ko barabar karne ke liye daayein H₂O₂ likh do, to hisaab milta dikhega, par H₂O₂ paani nahi hai, wo ek alag padaarth hai. Isliye **subscript ko haath mat lagao**. Kheer ke recipe mein bhi tum "100 g cheeni" ko "100 g namak" nahi karte, bas ek hi guna badhate ho.

## Part E: State symbols aur conditions

Equation ko aur zyada batane waali bana sakte hain:

- **State symbols**, har formula ke baad bracket mein:
  - **(s)** = solid (thos)
  - **(l)** = liquid (tarl)
  - **(g)** = gas
  - **(aq)** = aqueous, matlab paani mein ghula hua

Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂ ko state symbols ke saath likhein to:

**Zn(s) + 2HCl(aq) → ZnCl₂(aq) + H₂(g)**

Isse pata chala ki zinc thos hai, HCl paani mein ghula hai, ZnCl₂ bhi paani mein ghula rahega, aur hydrogen gas ban kar nikalta hai.

Isi tarah: **2H₂(g) + O₂(g) → 2H₂O(l)** aur **CaCO₃(s) → CaO(s) + CO₂(g)**.

- **Conditions arrow ke upar ya neeche** likhte hain: garmi ho to "heat" ya Δ, aur agar koi khaas cheez (catalyst) reaction ko madad deti hai to uska naam:

```
   CaCO₃(s)   ──heat──►   CaO(s) + CO₂(g)

   N₂(g) + 3H₂(g)   ──catalyst──►   2NH₃(g)
```

> **Figure 6.4** – Arrow ke upar condition likhna: pehli equation mein garam karna, doosri mein catalyst (ek madadgaar jo khud nahi badalta). Dono equations balanced hain: Ca, C, O (1, 1, 3 = 1, 1, 3) aur N, H (2, 6 = 2, 6).

Ye sab sirf **kagaz par equation** likhne ke tareeke hain. Ghar par ya school mein ye reactions bina teacher aur safety ke nahi karne chahiye. Yahan hum sirf **hisaab** seekh rahe hain.

---

## Part F: Equation kya batati hai, aur kya nahi

**Equation ye batati hai:**

1. **Kaun reactants hain aur kaun products.** (Zn aur HCl se ZnCl₂ aur H₂.)
2. **Kitne molecule mil kar kitne molecule banate hain.** 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O ka matlab: hydrogen ke 2 molecule aur oxygen ka 1 molecule mil kar paani ke 2 molecule banate hain.
3. **Atoms ka hisaab sahi hai**, yaani kuch gayab nahi hua.
4. **Kaun si cheez gas, tarl ya thos hai** (agar state symbols likhe hon).

**Equation ye nahi batati:**

1. **Reaction kitni tezi se hogi.** Ek reaction second mein ho sakti hai, doosri saalon mein (jaise zang).
2. **Kya reaction sach mein hogi bhi ya nahi.** Sirf equation likh dene se reaction nahi ho jaati. Kuch equations balanced hokar bhi tabhi hoti hain jab condition milein.
3. **Garmi nikalegi ya jaayegi**, aur kitni.
4. **Reactants ka vazan aur ginti ka seedha hisaab** (ye Chapter 4 mein aayega, jab hum atoms ka bhaar aur unki ginti seekhenge).

Reactions ke **alag-alag prakaar** (jaise jodna, todna, badalna) aur garmi ke hisaab se unka batwara, ye sab **Chapter 7 "Chemical Changes and Reactions"** mein seekhenge.

## Part G: Recipe ki tarah scale karna

Kheer wali recipe ki tarah, balanced equation bhi **kisi bhi guna par** sahi rehti hai, bas **poori equation ko ek hi guna** se badhao.

**Example 10.** 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O ko 5 guna badhao. Kya ab bhi balanced hai?

- Naya rup: **10**H₂ + **5**O₂ → **10**H₂O.
- Jaanch: H: 10×2 = 20 baayein, 10×2 = 20 daayein ✅. O: 5×2 = 10 baayein, 10×1 = 10 daayein ✅

Molecules ka ratio wahi raha: H₂ : O₂ : H₂O = 2 : 1 : 2 = 10 : 5 : 10. Bilkul waise hi jaise 4 logon wali kheer ka ratio 20 logon ke liye bhi wahi rehta hai (har cheez 5 guna).

**Example 11.** Kheer ka nuskha: 50 g chawal + 1 L doodh + 100 g cheeni = 4 logon ki kheer. 12 logon ke liye kitna lagega?

- 12 ÷ 4 = **3 guna**.
- Chawal: 50 × 3 = **150 g**. Doodh: 1 × 3 = **3 L**. Cheeni: 100 × 3 = **300 g**.
- Jaanch: ratio chawal : doodh : cheeni pehle 50 : 1000 : 100 = 1 : 20 : 2 (g aur mL mein); ab 150 : 3000 : 300 = 1 : 20 : 2 ✅

Balanced equation ka bhi yahi kaam hai: ek **fixed ratio** jo sahi rehta hai chahe tum kam saamaan lo ya zyada.

## Part H: Chapter Ka Saar: poore chapter ka hisaab

Is chapter mein humne chemistry ki poori "bhasha" banayi: akshar (symbols), shabd (formulae), aur vaakya (equations). Ek nazar mein:

```
   SYMBOL           FORMULA            EQUATION
   (akshar)         (shabd)            (vaakya)

     H, O           H₂O, CO₂         2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O
      │                │                    │
      +-> valency ---> radical ---> criss-cross ---> balance
```

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Hisaab milane ke liye subscript badal dena**

H₂ + O₂ → H₂O mein O barabar karne ke liye H₂O₂ likhna galat hai. H₂O₂ alag padaarth hai. Sirf **coefficient** badlo.

**Galti 2: Ek tatva barabar kiya, baaki dobara nahi ginte**

Jaise O barabar karne ke liye Mg + O₂ → 2MgO likh diya, par Mg dobara nahi gina (baayein 1, daayein 2). Sahi: 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO. Har coefficient lagane ke baad **sab tatva dobara gino**.

**Galti 3: Coefficient ko subscript samajhna**

2H₂O ka matlab hai paani ke 2 set (kul 4 H aur 2 O). Ye H₂O₂ nahi hai, jo ek bilkul alag padaarth hai.

**Galti 4: Coefficients ko kam na karna**

Agar 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O ko 4H₂ + 2O₂ → 4H₂O likho to wo bhi sahi hai, par ye ek hi cheez ka **bada roop** hai. Hum sabse chhota whole-number ratio likhte hain: 2 : 1 : 2.

**Galti 5: Soch lena ki balanced equation matlab reaction ho jaayegi**

Balance karna sirf hisaab sahi karna hai. Reaction hoti hai ya nahi, aur kitni tezi se, ye equation nahi batati.

**Galti 6: Pehle se sahi equation mein bhi coefficient thok dena**

CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂ pehle se balanced hai. Bina ginti kiye "2" lagane se ye bigad jaayegi. Hamesha pehle **gino**.

## Quick Recap

- **Word equation** reaction ko naamon mein likhti hai; **skeleton equation** mein formulae aate hain par hisaab abhi theek nahi hota.
- Reaction mein atoms na bante hain na khatam hote hain, isliye **har tatva ke atoms arrow ke dono taraf barabar** hone chahiye (atoms ka hisaab; formal niyam Chapter 4 mein).
- Balancing **hit and trial** se: tatvon ko ginte hain, sirf **coefficient** lagate hain (subscript kabhi nahi), aur aakhir mein sab dobara gin kar jaanch karte hain. Jo tatva ek se zyada jagah hai (jaise O) use aakhir mein lo; LCM (jaise Fe aur Al mein 2 aur 3 ka 6) bhi kaam aata hai.
- Balanced equations jo humne banayi: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O, 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO, 4Fe + 3O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃, N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃, CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O, Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂, CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂, 4Al + 3O₂ → 2Al₂O₃, C₃H₈ + 5O₂ → 3CO₂ + 4H₂O.
- **State symbols:** (s), (l), (g), (aq). **Conditions** (heat, catalyst) arrow ke upar likhte hain.
- Equation batati hai kaun reactants, kaun products, aur kitne molecule (ya atom); ye **nahi** batati ki reaction kitni tezi se hogi ya hogi bhi ya nahi. Reaction ke prakaar Chapter 7 mein.
- Balanced equation ko poora ek hi guna karke bada ya chhota kar sakte hain, bilkul kheer ki recipe ki tarah.

2. Balance each equation and verify by counting atoms on both sides: (a) Mg + O₂ → MgO, (b) N₂ + H₂ → NH₃, (c) Zn + HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂.

3. Balance: (a) Fe + O₂ → Fe₂O₃, (b) Al + O₂ → Al₂O₃. Explain why you used the LCM of 2 and 3.

4. Balance CH₄ + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O. Why is it a good idea to balance oxygen last?

5. Balance the hardest one: C₃H₈ + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O. Show the count of C, H and O before and after your coefficients.

6. A student balances H₂ + O₂ → H₂O by writing H₂ + O₂ → H₂O₂. What is wrong with this?

7. Is CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂ balanced already? Count atoms to prove it. Write it with state symbols and with the condition on the arrow.

8. Write Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂ with state symbols and explain what each symbol tells you.

9. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
   (a) In a balanced equation, the number of atoms of each element is the same on both sides.
   (b) We can change the subscript in a formula to balance an equation.
   (c) A balanced equation tells how fast a reaction will happen.
   (d) 2H₂O means two molecules of water, with 4 hydrogen atoms and 2 oxygen atoms in all.

10. Scale 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O by 3 and check that it is still balanced. Also, a kheer recipe for 4 people needs 50 g rice, 1 L milk and 100 g sugar. How much of each is needed for 16 people?

### Answers

1. **Word equation:** hydrogen + oxygen → water. **Skeleton equation:** H₂ + O₂ → H₂O. Ye abhi galat hai kyunki baayein O = 2 aur daayein O = 1: ek atom gayab dikh raha hai.

2. (a) **2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO.** Jaanch: Mg 2 = 2 ✅, O 2 = 2 ✅. (b) **N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃.** Jaanch: N 2 = 2 ✅, H 6 = 6 ✅. (c) **Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂.** Jaanch: Zn 1 = 1 ✅, H 2 = 2 ✅, Cl 2 = 2 ✅.

3. (a) **4Fe + 3O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃.** Jaanch: Fe 4 = 4 ✅, O 6 = 6 ✅. (b) **4Al + 3O₂ → 2Al₂O₃.** Jaanch: Al 4 = 4 ✅, O 6 = 6 ✅. Baayein O ki ginti 2 ke multiples mein aati hai (O₂ se) aur daayein 3 ke multiples mein (Fe₂O₃ aur Al₂O₃ mein O₃ se). Dono ko barabar karne ke liye sabse chhota common multiple 6 chahiye.

4. **CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O.** Jaanch: C 1 = 1 ✅, H 4 = 4 ✅, O 4 = 4 ✅. O do jagah (CO₂ aur H₂O) aata hai, isliye jab C aur H pehle barabar ho jaate hain tab O ka total ek baar mein pata chal jaata hai.

5. **C₃H₈ + 5O₂ → 3CO₂ + 4H₂O.** Coefficients se pehle (skeleton): baayein C = 3, H = 8, O = 2; daayein C = 1, H = 2, O = 3. Coefficients ke baad: baayein C = 3, H = 8, O = 5×2 = 10; daayein C = 3, H = 4×2 = 8, O = 3×2 + 4 = 10 ✅. Sab dono taraf barabar hain.

6. H₂O₂ paani nahi hai, wo ek alag padaarth hai. Subscript badalne se formula ka padaarth hi badal jaata hai. Hisaab milane ke liye sirf coefficient lagate hain: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O.

7. Ca: 1 = 1, C: 1 = 1, O: 3 = 1 + 2 = 3, **to ye pehle se balanced hai** ✅. State symbols aur condition ke saath: **CaCO₃(s) ──heat──► CaO(s) + CO₂(g)**.

8. **Zn(s) + 2HCl(aq) → ZnCl₂(aq) + H₂(g).** (s) = zinc thos hai, (aq) = HCl aur ZnCl₂ paani mein ghule hain, (g) = hydrogen gas ban kar nikalti hai.

9. (a) **True** ✅. Yahi atoms ka hisaab hai. (b) **False** ❌. Sirf coefficient badal sakte hain, subscript nahi, kyunki subscript badalne se padaarth badal jaata hai. (c) **False** ❌. Equation reaction ki speed nahi batati. (d) **True** ✅. 2 × H₂O mein H = 2×2 = 4 aur O = 2×1 = 2.

10. 3 guna karne par: **6H₂ + 3O₂ → 6H₂O.** Jaanch: H 12 = 12 ✅, O 6 = 6 ✅. Kheer: 16 ÷ 4 = 4 guna, to chawal 50×4 = **200 g**, doodh 1×4 = **4 L**, cheeni 100×4 = **400 g**.
