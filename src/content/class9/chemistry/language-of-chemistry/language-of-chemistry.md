# Topic 1: Symbols: Tatvon Ke Chhote Naam

Pichhle chapter mein hum "loha", "sulphur", "oxygen", "sodium", "chlorine" jaise tatvon ke naam poore-poore shabdon mein likhte rahe. Ek-do baar tak theek hai. Par socho, agar hum kisi badlaav ko likhna chahein, jaise "magnesium aur oxygen jud kar magnesium oxide bana" ya "hydrogen aur oxygen jud kar paani bana", to har baar itne lambe naam likhna kitna thaka dene wala hoga! Aur duniya ke vaigyanik alag-alag bhashaon mein baat karte hain. Is chapter mein hum chemistry ki apni **chhoti, saaf aur sabke liye ek jaisi bhasha** seekhenge. Is bhasha ka pehla kadam hai: **tatvon ke chhote naam, yaani symbols.**

## Airport ke baggage tag

Mr. Mallick apne bhatije Taksh ko saath lekar Mumbai se Delhi jaa rahe the. Taksh ki ye pehli hawai yatra thi. Check-in counter par Mr. Mallick ne bag diya aur counter wale ne bag par ek lambi si paper-tag chipka di. Taksh ne ghoom kar tag padhne ki koshish ki: usme bada-bada likha tha **DEL**.

"Mallick uncle, hum to **Delhi** jaa rahe hain. Ye tag par 'Delhi' poora kyun nahi likha? Sirf teen akshar?"

Mr. Mallick hanse. "Aage dekho." Conveyor belt par aate hue doosre bagon par bhi tag the: **BOM**, **BLR**, **MAA**. Taksh ne ginti ki.

"BLR to Bengaluru hoga. Par **BOM**? Mumbai ka B to hai, par O aur M kahan se aaye? Aur **MAA**? Ye to Chennai hai, isme C, H, E kuch bhi nahi hai!"

"Wahi to maza hai," Mr. Mallick ne kaha. "BOM isliye kyunki Mumbai ka purana naam **Bombay** tha. MAA isliye kyunki Chennai ka purana naam **Madras** tha. Airport ke code purane naam se bane, par ab poori duniya mein ek hi code chalta hai. Ek bag Japan mein ho ya Germany mein, 'DEL' padhte hi har kisi ko pata chal jaata hai ki bag Delhi jaayega. Naam lamba nahi, bhasha ka jhanjhat nahi, galti ka darr kam."

Taksh ne thodi der socha. "Matlab chhota naam, par poori duniya ke liye ek jaisa."

"Bilkul. Chemistry mein bhi bilkul yahi hua hai. Har tatva ka ek chhota 'tag' hai, jise **symbol** kehte hain. Aur kuch tatvon ka symbol bhi bilkul **MAA** jaisa hai: naam kuch aur, par symbol purane naam se bana. Chalo, aaj wahi sab samajhte hain."

> **Figure 1.1** – Airport tag aur tatva ka tag, dono ek hi sochi-samjhi tarkeeb hai. Dono chhote hain, poori duniya mein ek jaise hain, aur kabhi-kabhi purane naam se bane hain (MAA se Madras, Na se Natrium).

```
  Shehar                        Airport tag
  ------------------------------------------
  Mumbai (pehle Bombay)    ->   [ BOM ]
  Delhi                    ->   [ DEL ]
  Bengaluru                ->   [ BLR ]
  Chennai (pehle Madras)   ->   [ MAA ]

  Tatva                         Chemistry tag (symbol)
  ------------------------------------------
  Hydrogen                 ->   [ H  ]
  Magnesium                ->   [ Mg ]
  Sodium (Latin: Natrium)  ->   [ Na ]
  Iron (Latin: Ferrum)     ->   [ Fe ]
```

## Part A: Symbols ki zaroorat kyun padi?

Abhi tak jaane gaye tatvon ki ginti sau se bhi upar hai (lagbhag 118, ye humne pichhle chapter mein dekha tha). Agar har baar naam poora likhna pade to teen dikkatein aati hain:

1. **Samay aur jagah:** "Magnesium" likhna "Mg" se kaafi lamba hai. Ek lambi reaction mein ye naam sau baar aa sakte hain.
2. **Bhasha ka fark:** Sodium ko Hindi mein, Tamil mein, Japanese mein alag naam se bula sakte hain. Par agar sab "Na" likhein, to koi bhi padh sakta hai.
3. **Aage ka kaam:** Aage is chapter mein hum "formula" aur "equation" likhenge, jahan ek line mein kai tatva aayenge. Wahan chhote symbols ke bina kaam chalega hi nahi.

Isliye vaigyanikon ne ek **common, chhoti bhasha** banayi. Jaise ganit mein +, −, = poori duniya ke liye ek hi hain, waise hi tatvon ke symbols bhi.

## Part B: Symbols ki chhoti si kahani

**Dalton ki tasveerein.** Lagbhag 200 saal pehle John Dalton (wahi jinhone aage ke chapter mein aane wali atomic theory di) ne har tatva ke liye ek chhoti **tasveer** (gol, kuch andar ke nishaan wale chhote circle) banayi thi. Hydrogen ke liye ek dot wala circle, sulphur ke liye ek aur nishaan, aur isi tarah baaki. Dikkat ye thi ki ye tasveerein yaad rakhna, copy karna aur chhapna mushkil tha, aur do log ek hi tasveer ko alag banate the.

**Berzelius ke akshar.** Phir Swedish vaigyanik **Jöns Jacob Berzelius** ne ek bahut aasaan idea diya: **tatva ke naam ke pehle akshar (ya pehle do akshar) ko hi uska symbol bana do.** Akshar sabko aate hain, alag-alag keyboard par type ho jaate hain, aur copy karna bhi aasaan. Aaj poori duniya yahi tarika use karti hai. Naye tatvon ke symbol aaj bhi ek antarrashtriya sanstha (international group of chemists) tay karti hai, taaki poori duniya mein ek hi symbol rahe.

## Part C: Symbol likhne ke niyam

### Niyam 1: Pehla akshar hamesha CAPITAL

Symbol mein pehla akshar hamesha **bada (capital)** hota hai.

- Hydrogen: **H**
- Carbon: **C**
- Oxygen: **O**

### Niyam 2: Doosra akshar (agar ho) hamesha small

Kai tatvon ke naam ka pehla akshar doosre tatva ke saath takra jaata hai (jaise Carbon, Calcium, Chlorine, Copper, sabka pehla akshar C hai). Isliye doosre tatvon ke liye **do akshar** liye jaate hain, aur doosra akshar **hamesha chhota (small)** likhte hain.

- Calcium: **Ca** (C capital, a small)
- Chlorine: **Cl** (C capital, l small)
- Magnesium: **Mg**

### Niyam 3: Kabhi do capital nahi

**CA**, **MG**, **NA** likhna galat hai. Ye symbols hain hi nahi ya kuch aur matlab de dete hain (aage dekhenge).

> **Figure 1.2** – Symbol ko padhne aur likhne ka tareeka. Pehla akshar capital, doosra (agar ho) small. Do capital letter ek saath likhne ka matlab alag-alag tatva ho jaata hai.

```
   Ca                              CA  (galat)
   ^^                              ^^
   Capital + small                 Do capital: Ca ki jagah
   = ek tatva (calcium)            ye Calcium ka symbol hi
                                   nahi hai

   Cl   (chlorine)                 CL, cl, cL  (sab galat)
   C aur l: ek jodi                Capital aur small ka
   milkar ek tatva                 niyam toot gaya
```

### Niyam 4: Symbol ke baad full stop nahi lagta

"H." ya "Na." nahi likhte. Bas **H**, **Na**.

## Part D: Kuch symbols "MAA" jaise hain: Latin naam

Zyada tar symbols angrezi naam ke pehle akshar se bane hain (H se Hydrogen, C se Carbon, O se Oxygen). Par kuch tatvon ka symbol **angrezi naam se nahi milta**, bilkul Chennai ke MAA ki tarah. Wajah: ye tatva insaan ko bahut purane zamane se pata the, aur unke naam purane **Latin** shabdon se aaye the. Berzelius ne wahi Latin naam se symbol liya.

| Tatva (angrezi naam) | Latin naam | Symbol | Kuch yaad rakhne wali baat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sodium | Natrium | **Na** | Natrium ka Na |
| Potassium | Kalium | **K** | Kalium ka K (alkali shabd bhi isi se juda hai) |
| Iron (loha) | Ferrum | **Fe** | Ferrum ke pehle do akshar |
| Copper (tamba) | Cuprum | **Cu** | Cuprum; Cyprus naam ke dweep se aaya tamba |
| Silver (chandi) | Argentum | **Ag** | Argentina desh ka naam isi se aaya hai |
| Gold (sona) | Aurum | **Au** | Aurum ka Au |
| Mercury (paara) | Hydrargyrum | **Hg** | Matlab "paani jaisi chandi" (liquid chandi jaisi) |
| Lead (seesa) | Plumbum | **Pb** | Isi se English ka "plumber" shabd aaya (pehle paani ke pipe seese ke bante the) |
| Tin (kalai/tin) | Stannum | **Sn** | Stannum ke S aur n |

Is table ke 9 tatva **zaroor yaad kar lo**: inke symbol angrezi naam se guess nahi ho sakte.

**Jaanch:** Table mein 9 tatva hain: Na, K, Fe, Cu, Ag, Au, Hg, Pb, Sn. Gino: Na (1), K (2), Fe (3), Cu (4), Ag (5), Au (6), Hg (7), Pb (8), Sn (9) ✅.

## Part F: Ek symbol ka matlab kya hota hai?

Symbol sirf ek naam ka short-cut nahi hai. Isme ek aur matlab chhupa hai. Jab hum **Na** likhte hain, to uska matlab hai:

1. **Tatva ka naam:** sodium (poora tatva).
2. **Us tatva ka ek atom:** yaani us tatva ka **sabse chhota kan**. (Atom kya hai, ye Topic 2 mein theek se seekhenge. Abhi bas itna: jaise ek moti-mala ka ek moti, waise hi tatva ka ek atom.)

Yaani **O** ka matlab hai "oxygen", aur wahi bhi "oxygen ka ek atom". **Fe** ka matlab hai "loha" aur "loha ka ek atom".

Symbol ka ye "ek atom" wala matlab hi aage formulae aur equations ko hisaab se likhne mein kaam aayega.

## Part G: Padhne aur likhne ka riyaaz (solved examples)

**Example 1 (symbol se naam):** In symbols ko tatva ke naam mein badlo: **K, Ne, Cu, Cl, Ag, Si**.

- K: **Potassium** (Latin: Kalium)
- Ne: **Neon**
- Cu: **Copper** (Latin: Cuprum)
- Cl: **Chlorine**
- Ag: **Silver** (Latin: Argentum)
- Si: **Silicon**

**Jaanch:** Chhah symbols, chhah naam. Har ek ko table mein dobara dekha: K (19), Ne (10), Cu (extra table), Cl (17), Ag (extra table), Si (14) ✅.

**Example 2 (naam se symbol):** In tatvon ke symbol likho: **calcium, carbon, chlorine, copper, phosphorus, potassium**.

Ye sab "C" ya "P" se shuru hote hain, isliye dhyan se:

- Calcium: **Ca**
- Carbon: **C**
- Chlorine: **Cl**
- Copper: **Cu** (Latin Cuprum)
- Phosphorus: **P**
- Potassium: **K** (Latin Kalium; "P" nahi, kyunki P phosphorus ke paas hai)

**Jaanch:** Sabhi 6 symbols alag-alag hain (Ca, C, Cl, Cu, P, K), koi bhi do ek jaise nahi, aur har ek ka pehla akshar capital hai ✅.

**Example 3 (match the following):** Neeche Column A ke tatvon ko Column B ke symbols se milao.

| Column A (tatva) | Column B (symbol) |
|---|---|
| 1. Sodium | (a) S |
| 2. Potassium | (b) Si |
| 3. Magnesium | (c) P |
| 4. Phosphorus | (d) Mg |
| 5. Silicon | (e) K |
| 6. Sulphur | (f) Na |

**Hal:** 1 - (f) Na (Natrium). 2 - (e) K (Kalium). 3 - (d) Mg. 4 - (c) P. 5 - (b) Si. 6 - (a) S.

**Jaanch:** Chhah mein se har symbol (a) se (f) tak ek hi baar use hua, koi bacha nahi aur koi do baar nahi aaya ✅.

**Example 4 (kaun sa symbol galat hai, aur kyun?):** Neeche diye gaye daawon mein galti dhoondho.

| Daawa | Sahi ya galat | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| Chlorine ka symbol CL hai | Galat | Doosra akshar small hona chahiye: **Cl** |
| Sodium ka symbol So hai | Galat | Latin naam Natrium se bana hai: **Na** |
| Potassium ka symbol P hai | Galat | P phosphorus ka hai. Potassium ka **K** (Kalium) |
| Silver ka symbol Si hai | Galat | Si silicon hai. Silver ka **Ag** (Argentum) |
| Magnesium ka symbol MG hai | Galat | Capital aur small ka niyam: **Mg** |
| Iron ka symbol I hai | Galat | I iodine hai. Iron ka **Fe** (Ferrum) |
| Calcium ka symbol Ca hai | Sahi | Capital C, small a |
| Gold ka symbol Au hai | Sahi | Aurum ka Au |

**Example 5 (symbol se shabd banane ka khel):** Symbols ko jod kar angrezi shabd bhi ban jaate hain! Ye bas ek khel hai (alag likhe symbols ke beech space isliye hai ki dikhe kahan ek symbol khatam hua aur doosra shuru). Dekho:

- **Ca Fe** = "CaFe" (cafe): calcium aur iron ke symbols
- **Ne O N** = "NeON" (neon): neon, oxygen, nitrogen
- **Fe Ar** = "FeAr" (fear): iron aur argon
- **Ca N** = "CaN" (can): calcium aur nitrogen

**Jaanch:** "NeON" mein tatvon ko alag karke dekho: Ne + O + N, teen alag symbols, sab pehle-20 wali table mein hain ✅. Dhyan do, aise banaaye shabd mein bhi capital letter yahi batata hai ki naya symbol kahan shuru hua.

## Part H: Co aur CO: ek chhoti si galti, bada fark

Symbols likhte waqt sabse zyada galti **capital aur small ke chakkar** mein hoti hai, kyunki ek akshar ka chhota-bada hona poora matlab badal deta hai.

- **Co** = ek akshar capital C, ek small o = **cobalt**, ek tatva (ye ek dhaatu hai).
- **CO** = **do capital letter** = ek C aur ek O = **carbon aur oxygen, do alag tatva**. Ye ek yaugik (compound) hai, jise carbon monoxide kehte hain. (Band kamre mein koyla ya gas jalane se banne wali zehrili gas. Compound ke formulae Topic 5 mein aayenge.)

Isi tarah:

- **Si** = silicon (ek tatva), lekin **SI** = S aur I, yaani sulphur aur iodine (do tatva).
- **Hg** = mercury, lekin **HG** = H aur G (G naam ka koi tatva hi nahi hai!).
- **Cu** = copper, lekin **CU** = C aur U, yaani carbon aur uranium, bilkul alag do tatva.

**Seekh:** Ek chhota akshar bhi dhyan se likho. Capital se naya tatva shuru hota hai.

## Part I: Itne symbols yaad kaise rakhein?

Ek saath 30 symbols dekh kar ghabrana mat. Teen cheezein bahut madad karti hain.

**Tarika 1: Tatvon ko teen group mein baanto.**

| Group | Tatva | Kitne |
|---|---|---|
| Angrezi naam ka pehla akshar hi symbol | H, B, C, N, O, F, P, S, I | 9 |
| Angrezi naam ka pehla akshar + naam ka ek aur akshar | He, Li, Be, Ne, Mg, Al, Si, Cl, Ar, Ca, Zn | 11 |
| Latin naam se (angrezi se alag) | Na, K, Fe, Cu, Ag, Au, Hg, Pb, Sn | 9 |

**Jaanch:** Teeno group milake 9 + 11 + 9 = 29 tatva. Ye wahi hain jo humne tables mein padhe: pehle 20 tatva aur 9 extra (Fe, Cu, Zn, Ag, Au, Hg, Pb, Sn, I), yaani 20 + 9 = 29 ✅.

Sabse zaroori group teesra hai: Latin-wale **9 tatva** ratt lo (Part D ki table), kyunki unke symbol guess nahi ho sakte.

**Tarika 2: Chhoti kahani ya tukbandi (mnemonic).** Pehle 20 tatvon ko order mein yaad karne ke liye ek mazedaar line, jahan har shabd ka pehla akshar tatva ka pehla akshar hai:

- H, He, Li, Be, B, C, N, O, F, Ne: **"Happy Hens Like Beans But Can Not Offer Food, Naturally"**
- Na, Mg, Al, Si, P, S, Cl, Ar, K, Ca: **"Naughty Magicians Always Sing Pretty Songs, Clap And Keep Calm"**

(Apni khud ki Hinglish line banao to aur bhi aasaani se yaad rahegi!)

**Tarika 3: Roz ki cheezon se jodo.**

- **Fe**: tumhare ghar ka loha ka gate
- **Cu**: bijli ka taar
- **Au**, **Ag**: gehne ki dukaan
- **Hg**: purana thermometer (dekho, haath mat lagana)
- **Pb**: "plumber" shabd
- **Sn**: dibbe ki kalai
- **Na**, **K**: namak aur kele (potassium kele mein bhi paya jaata hai)
- **Ca**: doodh aur haddi
- **Cl**: swimming pool ki bleaching ki gandh

**Roz 5 symbols** likho, ek hafte mein 30-35 ho jaayenge. Flashcard banao: ek taraf naam, doosri taraf symbol.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Sodium ka symbol So ya S hai"**

Nahi. Sodium ka symbol **Na** hai (Latin Natrium). **S** sulphur ka hai.

**Galti 2: "Potassium ka symbol P hai"**

Nahi. **P** phosphorus ka hai. Potassium ka **K** (Kalium).

**Galti 3: "Symbol ka pehla akshar chhota bhi likh sakte hain, jaise cl"**

Nahi. Pehla akshar **hamesha capital**, doosra **hamesha small**: **Cl**.

**Galti 4: "Co aur CO ek hi cheez hain"**

Nahi. **Co** cobalt (ek tatva) hai, **CO** carbon aur oxygen (do tatva, ek compound).

**Galti 5: "Har symbol do akshar ka hota hai"**

Nahi. H, B, C, N, O, F, P, S, K, I sab **ek akshar** ke hain.

**Galti 6: "Silver ka symbol Si hai"**

Nahi. **Si** silicon hai. Silver ka symbol **Ag** (Argentum).

**Galti 7: "Symbol ka matlab sirf naam ka short-cut hai"**

Adhoora hai. Symbol ka matlab **tatva ka naam** bhi hai aur **us tatva ka ek atom** (sabse chhota kan) bhi. Ye "ek atom" wali baat aage formulae mein kaam aayegi.

## Quick Recap

- Chemistry mein tatvon ke naam **symbols** (chhote, sabke liye ek jaise) mein likhe jaate hain, jaise airport ke tag DEL, BOM, BLR, MAA.
- Dalton ne pehle tasveerein banayi, phir **Berzelius** ne akshar wala tarika diya, jo aaj tak chalta hai.
- **Niyam:** pehla akshar **capital**, doosra (agar ho) **small**. Do capital letter ka matlab do alag tatva.
- Kuch symbols **Latin naam** se bane hain: Na (Natrium), K (Kalium), Fe (Ferrum), Cu (Cuprum), Ag (Argentum), Au (Aurum), Hg (Hydrargyrum), Pb (Plumbum), Sn (Stannum).
- Pehle 20 tatvon ke symbols: H, He, Li, Be, B, C, N, O, F, Ne, Na, Mg, Al, Si, P, S, Cl, Ar, K, Ca. Saath mein Zn aur I bhi yaad karo.
- Ek symbol ka matlab: tatva ka naam, aur us tatva ka **ek atom** (sabse chhota kan).
- Capital aur small ka fark badi cheez hai: **Co** (cobalt, ek tatva) aur **CO** (carbon aur oxygen) alag hain.
- Yaad rakhne ke tareeke: Latin 9 ratt lo, tukbandi banao, roz ki cheezon se jodo.

10. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) The symbol for sodium is S.
    (b) Cu comes from the Latin name Cuprum.
    (c) CO and Co mean the same thing.
    (d) Every symbol has exactly two letters.
    (e) The symbol for mercury is Hg.

### Answers

1. (i) Symbols **chhote** hain, isliye likhne mein samay aur jagah bachti hai. (ii) Symbols poori duniya mein **ek jaise** hain, isliye bhasha ka jhanjhat nahi hota. (Teesra reason bhi ho sakta hai: aage formulae aur equations ek line mein likhne ke liye.)

2. Carbon **C**, calcium **Ca**, chlorine **Cl**, copper **Cu**, phosphorus **P**, potassium **K**, sulphur **S**, silicon **Si**.
   Jaanch: 8 tatva, 8 alag symbols, sabka pehla akshar capital ✅

3. Ne: neon. Ar: argon. Al: aluminium. Sn: tin. Hg: mercury. Ag: silver. Au: gold. Pb: lead.

4. 1-(e) Ag, 2-(f) Au, 3-(a) Pb, 4-(c) Sn, 5-(b) Hg, 6-(d) Zn.
   Jaanch: (a) se (f) tak chhah options ek-ek baar use hue ✅

5. (a) Sahi: **Cl**. Doosra akshar capital nahi hota. (b) Sahi: **Na**. Sodium ka symbol Latin naam Natrium se bana hai, "So" jaisa koi symbol nahi hai. (c) Sahi: **Mg**. Doosra akshar small hota hai. (d) Sahi: **K**. P phosphorus ka symbol hai; potassium Latin Kalium se K leta hai. (e) Sahi: **Fe**. I iodine ka symbol hai; iron Latin Ferrum se Fe leta hai.

6. Natrium: **Na**, sodium. Kalium: **K**, potassium. Ferrum: **Fe**, iron. Cuprum: **Cu**, copper. Argentum: **Ag**, silver. Aurum: **Au**, gold. Hydrargyrum: **Hg**, mercury. Plumbum: **Pb**, lead. Stannum: **Sn**, tin.
   Jaanch: 9 Latin naam, 9 symbols ✅

7. **Co** (C capital, o small) ek tatva hai: cobalt. **CO** (do capital letter) mein do alag tatva hain: carbon (C) aur oxygen (O), yaani carbon monoxide naam ka compound. Capital letter naya symbol shuru karta hai, isliye ek akshar ka chhota-bada hona matlab badal deta hai.

8. Single-letter symbol wale: hydrogen (**H**), boron (**B**), carbon (**C**), fluorine (**F**), sulphur (**S**), potassium (**K**). Do-letter wale: helium (He), neon (Ne), silicon (Si).
   Jaanch: 6 + 3 = 9 tatva ✅

9. (i) Fe ka matlab **iron (loha)** tatva. (ii) Fe ka matlab **loha ka ek atom**, yaani loha ka sabse chhota kan. Symbol Latin naam **Ferrum** se bana hai.

10. (a) **False** ❌. Sodium ka symbol **Na** hai; S sulphur ka hai. (b) **True** ✅. (c) **False** ❌. Co cobalt hai, CO carbon aur oxygen ka compound hai. (d) **False** ❌. H, C, O, N jaise symbols ek akshar ke hain. (e) **True** ✅. Latin naam Hydrargyrum se.
