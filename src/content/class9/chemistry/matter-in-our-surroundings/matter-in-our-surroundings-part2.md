# Topic 2: Solid, Liquid aur Gas: Teen States ki Pehchaan

Topic 1 mein hum is nateeje par pahunche the ki har cheez bahut chhote **kanon (particles)** se bani hai. Kan chhote hain, unke beech khaali jagah hai, wo hamesha hilte-dolte rehte hain aur ek doosre ko thoda **kheenchte** (aakarshan) hain. Itna dekhne ke baad ek natural sawal uthta hai: agar sab cheezein isi tarah ke kanon ki bani hain, to patthar akdu kyun hai, paani bahta kyun hai, aur hawa dikhti hi kyun nahi? Is topic mein hum wahi **particle picture** lekar teeno states ki pehchaan banayenge.

## Sunday ke mele mein teen cheezein

Ek Sunday Ms. Abraham apne bhanje Harsh ko mele mein le gayi. Harsh ko Class 9 ki science aur Ms. Abraham ki baatein, dono thodi "boring" lagti thi, par mela to mela hota hai.

Pehle ek **gubbare wala** mila. Uske paas ek bada sa cylinder tha aur uske muh se gubbare phoolte ja rahe the. Phir wo **khane ke stall** par ruke, jahan Ms. Abraham ne apna **steel ka tiffin** khola: andar garam dal thi. Aur aakhir mein Harsh ne apni **paani ki bottle** nikaali aur ek ghoont piya.

Ms. Abraham ne kaha, "Harsh, abhi tumhare haath mein teen cheezein hain. Bottle ka paani, tiffin ka steel aur wo gubbara jo tumne abhi liya. Bina kuch padhe batao: teeno mein kya alag hai?"

Harsh ne socha, "Steel to **akdu** hai, tiffin ka dabba apni shape nahi badalta. Paani bottle ki shape le leta hai. Aur gubbare ki hawa... wo to poore gubbare mein phail kar baithi hai."

"Bas, tumne teeno states ki pehchaan bata di," Ms. Abraham hansi. "Ab chalo, dekhte hain ki ye farq **kyun** hai: kaun si cheez kiski shape leti hai, kaun dabti hai, kaun behti hai. Aur har baar hum asli wajah ke taur par kanon ki baithak dekhenge."

## Part A: Teeno states ka kanon wala nakshaa

Topic 1 mein particles ki tasveer banayi thi. Ab dekhte hain ki wahi kan teeno states mein kaise baithe hote hain. Yaad rakhna: kan ke beech do cheezein ladti hain: **aakarshan** (ek doosre ko kheenchna) aur kanon ki **harkat** (apni jagah chhod kar bhagne ki koshish). Kaun kitna haavi hai, usi se state tay hoti hai.

```
   SOLID: tiffin ka steel      LIQUID: bottle ka paani      GAS: gubbare ki hawa

   ●  ●  ●  ●  ●  ●            ●  ● ●   ●  ●               ●             ●
   ●  ●  ●  ●  ●  ●             ● ●  ●●  ●                      ●
   ●  ●  ●  ●  ●  ●            ●  ●  ●  ● ●                          ●
   ●  ●  ●  ●  ●  ●             ●●  ●   ●                ●
                                                                    ●     ●
   line mein, bilkul           chipke hue, par               bahut door-door,
   paas, jagah par             ek doosre ke upar             idhar-udhar tez
   kaampte hain                se phisalte hain              udte hain
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Teeno states ka kan-nakshaa. Solid mein kan regular line mein, ek doosre se chipke hue aur fixed jagah par. Liquid mein kan chipke hue par bina kisi pattern ke, aur phisalte rehte hain. Gas mein kan bahut door-door, kisi pattern ke bina, tezi se udte hue.

Yahan do cheezein dhyan se dekho:

- **Solid aur liquid mein kan lagbhag utne hi paas hain.** Dono mein kanon ke beech khaali jagah kam hai. Isliye solid aur liquid dono ka volume lagbhag fixed rehta hai aur dono dabane se nahi dabte.
- **Gas mein kan bahut door hain.** Kanon ke beech jo khaali jagah hai wo **kanon ke apne size se kai guna** zyada hoti hai. Isiliye gas phail bhi sakti hai aur dab bhi sakti hai.

Physics wale chapter mein tumne temperature badhne par kanon ki harkat badhna dekha tha. Wo baat hum yahan nahi dohrayenge; yahan hum sirf ye dekh rahe hain ki **ek hi temperature par** teeno states kaisi hoti hain aur kyun.

### Har state mein kan kaise hilte hain?

| State | Kanon ki baithak | Kanon ki harkat | Aakarshan |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solid | Regular, bahut paas | Apni jagah par hi kaampte hain, jagah nahi chhodte | Bahut mazboot |
| Liquid | Paas, par bina regular pattern ke | Ek doosre ke upar se phisalte hain | Itna ki kan chipke rahein, par itna nahi ki jagah fix ho jaaye |
| Gas | Bahut door-door, random | Tezi se seedhi line mein udte hain, takrate hain, phir mudte hain | Lagbhag nahi ke barabar |

(Ye sab "kanon" par hai. Kan atoms bhi ho sakte hain aur molecules bhi: ye hum chapter "Atoms and Molecules" mein seekhenge. Abhi sirf "kan" kehna kaafi hai.)

## Part B: Shape aur volume: kaun fixed, kaun nahi

### Solid: shape bhi fixed, volume bhi fixed

Tiffin ka steel kahin bhi rakho, wo apni shape nahi badalta. Wajah: uske kan apni jagah par **bandhe** hain. Ek kan ko doosri jagah jaane ke liye aakarshan ke saamne ladna padta hai, aur wo har baar haar jaata hai. Isiliye solid **rigid** (akda) hota hai.

### Liquid: shape badalti hai, volume nahi

Maan lo tumhare paas **1 litre (1000 mL) paani** hai. Ise alag-alag bartanon mein daalte hain:

| Bartan | Kya hua |
|---|---|
| Lambi patli bottle | Paani lambi patli shape ka ho gaya, ooncha khada |
| Chaudi thali (tray) | Paani chauda aur patla phail gaya |
| 4 chhote glass (250 mL ke) | Paani 4 hisson mein baat gaya |

Teeno mein **shape alag**, par **volume har baar 1000 mL hi**.

**Jaanch:** 4 glass ka hisaab: 4 × 250 mL = **1000 mL** ✅

**Kyun?** Liquid ke kan phisal sakte hain, isliye wo bartan ke aakar mein dhal jaate hain (shape nahi tikti). Par kan ek doosre se chipke hain, door nahi ja sakte (aakarshan hai), isliye bartan mein kitna bhi daalo, kanon ke beech ka fasla nahi badhta, aur volume wahi rehta hai.

### Gas: na shape, na volume

Gas ka sab kuch bartan par depend karta hai. Gubbare mein hawa gubbare ki shape le leti hai, cycle ke tube mein tube ki, kamre mein kamre ki. Aisa kyun? Gas ke kan **bahut tezi se** idhar-udhar udte rehte hain aur ek doosre ko lagbhag nahi kheenchte. Koi bhi unhe rokne wala nahi, to jab tak deewar na mile, wo phailte jaate hain. Deewar milne par wahan se takra kar wapas mudte hain. Isliye gas **jis bartan mein ho, poore bartan ko bhar deti hai**, chahe bartan kaisa bhi ho, aur chahe gas thodi ho ya zyada.

```
   Ek hi paani, 3 bartan                    Ek hi gas, 2 bartan

   ┌──┐     ┌──────────┐                     ┌────────┐     ┌────────────────┐
   │  │     │          │                     │ ●   ●  │     │ ●      ●     ● │
   │~~│     │          │                     │   ●  ● │     │    ●      ●    │
   │~~│     │~~~~~~~~~~│                     │ ●   ●  │     │ ●      ●     ● │
   └──┘     └──────────┘                     └────────┘     └────────────────┘
   bottle   chauda tray                      chhota dabba    bada dabba
   volume =  volume =                        gas dono ko poora bhar deti hai
   1 litre   1 litre
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Left: liquid bartan ki shape leti hai, par volume wahi rehta hai. Right: gas jis bhi bartan mein ho, poora bhar deti hai. (Scale ke hisaab se nahi.)

Ek line mein yaad rakhne ka tareeka:

> **Solid:** shape bhi, volume bhi fixed. **Liquid:** volume fixed, shape bartan ki. **Gas:** dono bartan ke hisaab se.

## Part C: Dabna (compressibility)

### Syringe wala experiment, ab number ke saath

Topic 1 mein syringe (bina sui ki) wala experiment kiya tha: nozzle band karke dabane par hawa dab gayi, paani lagbhag nahi. Ab usi ko teeno states ki nazar se, thode number ke saath dekhte hain.

- **Hawa se bhari syringe:** piston andar tak kaafi dab jaata hai. Maan lo 100 mL hawa se shuru kiya aur piston dabate-dabate andar ki hawa lagbhag 25 mL jagah mein aa gayi. Ab wahi kan, jo pehle 100 mL mein phaile the, sirf 25 mL mein hain: yaani **chaar guna zyada paas-paas**. (Ye sirf ek example hai: asli value tumhari ungli ki taakat par depend karegi.)
- **Paani se bhari syringe** (nozzle band karke): piston dabane ki koshish karo. Ab piston lagbhag hilta hi nahi, bahut zor lagane par bhi nahi.
- **Solid:** lakdi ke tukde ya patthar ko syringe mein dabane ka sawal hi nahi.

```
   Dabane se pehle              Dabane ke baad (gas)

   ┌──────────────────┐         ┌─────────┐
   │ ●    ●   ●    ●  │ ◀── P   │ ●●●● ●  │ ◀── P
   │   ●      ●    ●  │         │ ● ●●  ● │
   │ ●     ●     ●    │         └─────────┘
   └──────────────────┘          kan paas aa gaye,
    kan door-door                 bas gap kam hua
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Syringe mein gas ko dabane par kanon ke beech ka khaali gap kam ho jaata hai. Kan khud nahi dabte, bas paas aa jaate hain. (P = piston par dabaav.)

### Ye kyun hota hai?

- **Gas** ke kanon ke beech **bahut khaali jagah** hai, isliye unhe paas laane ki gunjaish hai: gas bahut dabti hai.
- **Liquid** aur **solid** ke kan pehle se hi lagbhag chhoo rahe hain. Dabane ke liye kaun si jagah bachi hai? Is wajah se liquid **bahut kam** dabta hai, aur solid to lagbhag hi nahi.

**Isliye ek gas ko liquid se zyada dabaya ja sakta hai.** (Sirf shabdon mein: gas mein khaali jagah bahut hai, liquid mein nahi.)

### Roz ki zindagi: LPG aur CNG

Ghar mein jo **LPG cylinder** aata hai, uske andar bahut saari gas ek chhote se cylinder mein bhari hoti hai. Ye isliye mumkin hai ki gas ko **dabaya** jaata hai, aur dabane se uske kan itne paas aa jaate hain ki LPG cylinder ke andar **liquid** ban jaati hai (gas se liquid banne ki baat Topic 3 mein). Agar wahi gas bina dabaye rakhte, to use rakhne ke liye bahut bada bartan chahiye hota. Isi tarah gaadiyon mein chalne wali **CNG** (compressed natural gas) normal hawa ke dabaav se **lagbhag 200 guna** zyada dabaav par cylinder mein bhari jaati hai. Naam mein hi "compressed" likha hai: dabayi hui gas.

Cycle ya car ke tyre mein bhi hawa dabakar bhari jaati hai: isi wajah se tyre ke andar ki hawa itni sakht lagti hai.

Ek chhoti si baat: gas ko cylinder mein bharne par bhi uske kan khud chhote nahi hote, sirf unke beech ki khaali jagah kam hoti hai.

(Liquid ki dabne ki kami ka ek bada upyog hai: hydraulic machines. Wo aage physics mein aayega, yahan hum use chhod rahe hain.)

## Part D: Behna (fluidity) aur akdapan (rigidity)

- **Solid:** kan fixed hain, isliye solid **behta nahi**, rigid hai.
- **Liquid:** kan ek doosre ke upar se phisalte hain, isliye liquid **behta hai**. Dal ko katori se thali mein palto to wo dhar banakar girti hai.
- **Gas:** kan to aur bhi azaad hain, isliye gas bhi **behti** hai. Hawa chalti hai, tabhi to pankhe ki hawa lagti hai.

Liquid aur gas dono behte hain, isliye dono ko **fluid** kehte hain. Ye naam kaafi kaam ka hai: "fluid" yaani "jo baha sake".

---

## Part E: Density (ghanatva): kitna matter kitni jagah mein

Density batati hai ki ek jagah (volume) mein **kitna matter** (mass) hai: **density = mass ÷ volume.** Kan jitne paas-paas hon, density utni zyada.

| Cheez | Lagbhag mass jo 1 litre volume mein aata hai |
|---|---|
| Hawa (gas) | lagbhag 1.2 gram |
| Paani (liquid) | 1 kilogram, yaani 1000 gram |
| Lohe ka solid (steel jaisa) | lagbhag 7.8 kilogram |

(Values lagbhag hain, aur sab "1 litre jagah" ke hisaab se.)

**Ek hi jagah, teen alag bharaav.** Hawa ke kan door-door hain, isliye 1 litre mein kam kan aaye, mass kam. Paani ke kan paas hain, isliye ussi 1 litre mein kaafi zyada kan aaye. Lohe ke kan bhi paas hain aur zyada bhaari hain, isliye aur zyada mass.

**Reasoning example 1:** Paani aur hawa ki density kitni guna alag hai?
Same 1 litre mein paani ka mass lagbhag 1000 g aur hawa ka lagbhag 1.2 g hai.
1000 ÷ 1.2 ≈ **833**, yaani paani hawa se lagbhag **800 guna** zyada ghana hai.
**Jaanch:** 1.2 × 833 ≈ 1000 ✅. Isliye hawa mein kan paani ke mukabale bahut door-door hain.

Generally (normal halat mein) **solid ki density sabse zyada, phir liquid, phir gas ki sabse kam** hoti hai. (Iska ek mashhoor apvaad bhi hai, barf aur paani, jo Topic 3 mein aayega.)

## Part F: Diffusion: kaun kitni tez

Topic 1 mein tumne dekha tha ki diffusion kya hai. Ab dekho ki teeno states mein ye kaisa hota hai:

- **Gas mein tez:** Reshma ke kamre wali agarbatti yaad karo: khushboo thodi hi der mein kamre ke doosre kone tak pahunch gayi. Kan door-door hain aur tez ghoom rahe hain, to unhe phailne mein kam rukawat hai.
- **Liquid mein dheere:** Topic 1 wali paani mein ink ki boond dheere-dheere phailti hai (minuton mein). Kan chipke hain, isliye ek doosre ke beech se nikalna dheere hota hai.
- **Solid mein bahut hi dheere:** kan apni jagah par bandhe hain. Aam zindagi mein diffusion nazar hi nahi aata (do alag solid ek doosre se ghul-mil jaayein, ye dekhne mein mahino ya saalon lagte hain).

**Khushboo ka reasoning:** gas mein kan tez hain aur unke beech khaali jagah bahut hai, to raste mein takraate hue bhi khushboo ke kan jaldi kone-kone tak pahunch jaate hain. Isliye khana pakta hai to khushboo kitchen se bahar bhi aa jaati hai.

## Part H: Aam doubts, short reasoning ke saath

### 1. Sponge ko dabao to dab jaata hai, to kya wo solid nahi?

Sponge **solid** hi hai. Usme bahut saare **chhote-chhote chhed** (holes) hain jinme **hawa bhari** hoti hai. Jab tum sponge ko dabate ho, to wo hawa bahar nikal jaati hai aur chhed pichak jaate hain. Yaani asli solid (sponge ka matter) nahi dabta, bas uske andar ki hawa wali jagah kam hoti hai. Dabane ke baad sponge dobara phool jaata hai, kyunki chhedon mein phir se hawa aa jaati hai.

### 2. Rubber band khinchta hai, to kya wo solid nahi?

Rubber band **solid** hai. Use kheenchne par uski shape badalti hai, par chhodte hi wo apni shape mein wapas aa jaata hai. Yaani rubber band apni shape **khud** nahi badalta; hum use badalte hain, aur jaise hi zor hatate hain, wo wapas apni shape le leta hai. Ye solid ka hi gun hai (fixed shape). Liquid ki tarah wo bartan mein dhal nahi jaata.

### 3. Cheeni aur namak bartan ki shape le lete hain, to kya wo liquid hain?

Nahi, dono **solid** hain. Cheeni ya namak ke **har dane (crystal) ki apni shape** hoti hai. Jab tum dane ko katori mein daalte ho, to dane ki shape nahi badalti. Dhair (pile) ki shape isliye badalti hai kyunki bahut saare chhote-chhote dane ek doosre ke upar se **phisalte** hain, jaise tum chhote kankar katori mein daalo to wo bhi katori ki shape mein bhar jaate hain. Dhair solid ki ek **bheed** hai; us bheed ki shape ko solid ki shape nahi maante. Ek hi dane ko dekho: wo na shape badalta hai, na bahta hai.

### 4. Mom (wax) aur glass kya hain?

Dono ko is class mein **solid** hi maano. Mom ki shape aasaani se badal jaati hai, par normal temperature par wo apni shape banaye rakhti hai aur behti nahi. Glass bhi solid hai.

**Ek bahut chalne wali galat baat:** "Purani building ki khidkiyon ke sheeshe neeche se mote ho jaate hain, kyunki glass bahut dheere behta hai, isliye glass asal mein liquid hai." Ye **sach nahi** hai. Purane zamane mein sheet glass banane ka tareeka aisa tha ki glass ki plate poori tarah barabar nahi banti thi. Mazdoor mote wale hisse ko aksar neeche ki taraf lagate the. Isliye neeche se mota hona glass ke behne ki wajah se nahi, balki **banne ke tareeke** ki wajah se hai. Glass solid hai, wo kamre ke temperature par nahi behta.

### 5. Gas bartan ko poora kyun bharti hai?

Kyunki uske kan bahut tezi se idhar-udhar udte hain aur ek doosre ko kheenchte nahi. Koi bhi cheez unhe ek jagah par rokti nahi, to wo tab tak phailte hain jab tak deewar na mil jaaye. Is wajah se gas ek kone mein ikatthi nahi rehti, poore bartan mein barabar phail jaati hai.

### 6. Liquid ki shape fixed kyun nahi, par volume fixed kyun hai?

Shape isliye fixed nahi kyunki kan ek doosre ke upar se phisal sakte hain. Volume isliye fixed hai kyunki kan ek doosre se chipke hain aur door nahi jaa sakte. Jab tumne 1 litre paani bottle se glass mein daala, kanon ne bas jagah badli; kanon ke beech ka fasla nahi badla.

### 7. Gas ko liquid se zyada kyun dabaya ja sakta hai?

Gas mein kanon ke beech **bahut khaali jagah** hai, to dabane par kan paas aa sakte hain. Liquid mein kan pehle se hi lagbhag chhoo rahe hain, unke beech dabane layak jagah hi nahi bachi.

## Part I: Solved examples (reasoning aur chhote hisaab)

**Example 1 (volume paani ka):** 1.5 litre paani ko 500 mL ki katoriyon mein baatna hai. Kitni katoriyan chahiye? Har katori ke paani ka volume kya hoga?

- 1.5 litre = 1500 mL
- Katoriyan = 1500 ÷ 500 = **3 katoriyan**
- Har katori mein **500 mL** paani

**Jaanch:** 3 × 500 = 1500 ✅. Paani ki shape har katori mein alag ho sakti hai, par kul volume nahi badla: **liquid ka volume fixed hai.**

**Example 2 (syringe mein hawa):** Ek syringe mein 60 mL hawa hai. Nozzle band karke piston ko dabate ho aur hawa 20 mL ho jaati hai. Kanon ka kya hua?

- Pehle 60 mL, ab 20 mL: 60 ÷ 20 = **3 guna** kam jagah
- Same kan ab 3 guna kam jagah mein hain, yaani **3 guna zyada paas-paas**
- Kanon ke beech ka gap kam hua, kan khud nahi dabe

**Jaanch:** 20 × 3 = 60 ✅. Agar wahi syringe 60 mL paani se bhari hoti, to piston itna nahi dabta, kyunki liquid ke kan pehle se paas hain.

**Example 3 (kaun sa state?):** Neeche diye gaye cases mein state batao aur ek line ki wajah do: (a) cycle ka tyre jisme hawa bhari hai, (b) kitaab, (c) shahad (honey) jo dheere behta hai, (d) cheeni ka ek dana.

- (a) **Gas** (hawa): dabayi hui hai aur tyre ko poora bharti hai.
- (b) **Solid**: apni shape aur volume fixed.
- (c) **Liquid**: dheere hi sahi, par behta hai aur bartan ki shape le leta hai. (Dheere behne ka matlab solid nahi.)
- (d) **Solid**: dane ki shape fixed hai.

**Jaanch:** shahad bartan ki shape lekar bahta hai, isliye liquid; kitaab behti nahi, isliye solid ✅

## Part J: Roz ki zindagi mein teeno states

- **Solid:** tiffin, ghar ki imaarat, taale. Kan fixed hain, isliye cheez ki shape fixed rehti hai aur wo apna bojh utha sakti hai.
- **Liquid:** paani, doodh, tel. Bartan ki shape lete hain, isliye bottle, kettle, tanki mein bhar sakte hain. Pipe se behkar ek jagah se doosri jagah jaate hain (nal ka paani).
- **Gas:** hawa (saans ke liye), LPG (khana pakane ke liye), CNG (gaadiyon ke liye). Dabakar cylinder mein bharte hain, isliye bahut saari gas ek chhoti si jagah mein aa jaati hai.
- **Cooking mein:** LPG ka cylinder, tiffin ka steel aur uske andar ki dal: teeno states ek saath rasoi mein hain.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Sponge dabta hai, isliye wo solid nahi hai"**

Sponge solid hi hai. Dabta isliye hai kyunki uske andar hawa wale chhed hain. Dabane par asal mein hawa wali jagah kam hoti hai.

**Galti 2: "Cheeni ya namak bartan ki shape le leta hai, isliye wo liquid hai"**

Har dana solid hai aur apni shape nahi badalta. Dhair ki shape badalti hai kyunki dane ek doosre ke upar se phisalte hain.

**Galti 3: "Purane sheeshe neeche se mote hote hain, isliye glass liquid hai"**

Ye sahi nahi. Mota hona purane tareeke se glass banane ki wajah se hai. Glass solid hai.

**Galti 4: "Gas ka volume fixed hota hai"**

Nahi. Gas ka volume bartan ke hisaab se badalta hai. Jis bartan mein rakho, poora bhar deti hai.

**Galti 5: "Liquid ka volume bartan ke hisaab se badalta hai"**

Nahi. Liquid ki **shape** badalti hai, volume nahi. 1 litre paani kisi bhi bartan mein 1 litre hi rehta hai.

**Galti 6: "Gas ko dabane par uske kan chhote ho jaate hain"**

Nahi. Kan wahi rehte hain; unke beech ki khaali jagah kam hoti hai.

## Quick Recap

- Teen states: **solid, liquid, gas**. Farq kanon ki baithak, harkat aur aakarshan ka hai.
- **Solid:** kan regular, paas-paas, apni jagah par kaampte hain. Shape aur volume fixed. Lagbhag nahi dabta. Behta nahi (rigid).
- **Liquid:** kan paas-paas, par ek doosre ke upar se phisalte hain. Volume fixed, shape bartan ki. Bahut kam dabta hai. Behta hai.
- **Gas:** kan bahut door-door, tezi se udte hain. Na shape fixed, na volume: bartan ko poora bharti hai. Bahut dabti hai. Behti hai.
- Liquid aur gas dono **fluid** hain.
- Density: solid aam taur par sabse zyada, gas sabse kam. 1 litre paani ka mass lagbhag 1000 g, hawa ka lagbhag 1.2 g: paani lagbhag 800 guna ghana.
- Diffusion: gas mein tez, liquid mein dheema, solid mein bahut dheema.
- Sponge (hawa ke chhed), rubber band (shape wapas aa jaati hai), cheeni-namak (har dana solid), mom aur glass (solid; glass behta nahi) sab solid hi hain.
- LPG aur CNG dabakar bhari jaati hain: dabane se kan paas aa jaate hain, isliye cylinder mein bahut gas aa jaati hai. (CNG dabi hui gas hi rehti hai; LPG cylinder ke andar liquid ban jaati hai.)

## Practice Questions

1. Name the state of matter that has (a) a fixed shape and fixed volume, (b) a fixed volume but no fixed shape, (c) neither a fixed shape nor a fixed volume.

2. Why does a gas fill the whole container it is kept in, while a liquid does not?

3. 1 litre of water is poured from a bottle into a wide tray. What happens to its shape and what happens to its volume? Explain in terms of particles.

4. A syringe contains 80 mL of air. The nozzle is blocked and the piston is pushed until the air takes up 20 mL. By what factor did the volume decrease? What happened to the gap between the particles?

5. Why can a gas be compressed much more than a liquid?

6. A sponge can be squeezed, but it is a solid. Explain.

7. A student says, "Sugar takes the shape of the bowl, so sugar is a liquid." Is he right? Give a reason.

8. Is a rubber band a solid or a liquid? Why?

9. 1 litre of water has a mass of about 1000 g and 1 litre of air has a mass of about 1.2 g. Roughly how many times denser is water than air? What does this say about the particles?

10. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) Old window panes are thicker at the bottom because glass is a slowly flowing liquid.
    (b) Liquids and gases are both called fluids.
    (c) The volume of a fixed quantity of liquid changes when it is poured into a bigger container.
    (d) Diffusion is fastest in gases.

### Answers

1. (a) **Solid**. (b) **Liquid**. (c) **Gas**.

2. Gas ke kan bahut door-door hain, tezi se idhar-udhar udte hain aur ek doosre ko lagbhag nahi kheenchte. Unhe rokne wala koi nahi, isliye wo phailte hain jab tak deewar na mile aur poora bartan bhar dete hain. Liquid ke kan ek doosre se chipke hain (aakarshan), isliye wo door nahi jaa sakte aur sirf bartan ke neeche ke hisse mein bhar jaate hain.

3. **Shape badal jaati hai** (bottle ki lambi shape se tray ki chaudi, patli shape). **Volume nahi badalta**: wo 1 litre hi rehta hai. Kan phisal kar nayi shape le lete hain par ek doosre se chipke rehte hain, isliye unke beech ka gap nahi badalta.

4. Volume 80 mL se 20 mL hua: 80 ÷ 20 = **4 guna kam**. Kanon ke beech ka **gap kam hua** (kan paas aa gaye). Kan khud nahi dabe.
   Jaanch: 20 × 4 = 80 ✅

5. Gas ke kanon ke beech bahut khaali jagah hai, isliye dabane par kan paas aa sakte hain. Liquid ke kan pehle se hi lagbhag chhoo rahe hain, isliye unke beech dabane layak jagah nahi bachi.

6. Sponge ke andar hawa bhare chhote-chhote chhed hain. Dabane par hawa nikal jaati hai aur chhed pichak jaate hain. Sponge ka solid matter nahi dabta; hawa wali jagah kam hoti hai.

7. **Galat.** Cheeni ka har dana ek solid hai aur uski shape nahi badalti. Bartan mein dhair ki shape isliye badalti hai kyunki bahut saare chhote dane ek doosre ke upar se phisalte hain. Dhair ko solid ki shape nahi maante.

8. **Solid.** Kheenchne par shape badalti hai, par zor hatate hi wo apni shape mein wapas aa jaata hai. Wo liquid ki tarah bartan mein dhal kar behta nahi.

9. 1000 ÷ 1.2 ≈ **833**, yaani lagbhag **800 guna** ghana. Matlab hawa ke kan paani ke kanon se bahut door-door hain.
   Jaanch: 1.2 × 833 ≈ 1000 ✅

10. (a) **False** ❌. Wajah glass banane ka purana tareeka tha; glass solid hai aur kamre ke temperature par nahi behta. (b) **True** ✅. Dono behte hain. (c) **False** ❌. Liquid ka volume fixed rehta hai; sirf shape badalti hai. (d) **True** ✅. Gas ke kan door-door aur tez hain.
