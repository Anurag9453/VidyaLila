# Topic 3: State Badalna: Interconversion, Temperature aur Pressure ka Asar

Topic 2 mein humne teeno states ko side by side rakh kar dekha: solid ke particles fixed jagah par paas-paas, liquid ke paas-paas par phisalte hue, aur gas ke door-door aur azaad. Topic 2 ke end mein Ms. Abraham ne ice-gole ki barf dikha kar poochha tha ki bina kuch naya jode barf paani kaise ban gayi. Matlab ye teeno **alag cheezein nahi, ek hi padaarth ke teen roop** ho sakte hain (barf, paani, bhaap). Ab sawal ye hai ki ek roop se doosre roop mein **jaate kaise hain**? Kaun sa "button" dabana padta hai? Aur kya kuch cheezein beech ka liquid roop chhod kar seedhe gas ban sakti hain? Chalo ek shaadi ke gate par chalte hain.

---

## Shaadi ke gate par "dhuan" ka bowl

Mr. Mathur apni bhanji Anika ke saath ek shaadi mein pahunche. Gate par swagat ke liye ek bada sa bowl rakha tha, aur usse **safed dhuan** nadi ki tarah neeche zameen par behta ja raha tha. Anika ne bowl ke andar jhaanka: usmein barf jaisi chhoti-chhoti safed tikkiyan thin, aur bowl mein thoda paani bhi daala gaya tha, jisse dhuan aur tez ban raha tha.

Anika ne haath badhaya hi tha ki Mr. Mathur ne use rok diya, "Ruko! Ye **dry ice** hai. Isse **kabhi haath mat lagana aur kabhi muh mein mat daalna**. Ye itni thandi hoti hai ki skin ko jala deti hai. Dekhna door se hi."

Anika ne poochha, "Mamu, barf to pighal kar paani banti hai. Is barf ka paani to bana hi nahi, bowl mein bas dhuan hi dhuan hai. Aur ye tikkiyan kam hoti ja rahi hain! Kahan ja rahi hain?"

Mr. Mathur muskuraaye, "Bahut achha dekha. Isi mein aaj ka poora topic chhupa hai. Is barf ke saath kuch **alag** ho raha hai: ye pighal kar liquid nahi ban rahi, **seedhe gas ban rahi hai**. Aaj hum matter ke ek roop se doosre roop mein jaane ke saare raaste dekhenge, aur is bowl ka raaz bhi kholenge."

## Part A: Chhe raaste: state kaise badalti hai

Teen states ke beech **chhe** raaste ban sakte hain. Har raaste ka apna naam hai:

| Badlaav | Naam | Garmi ka kya hota hai? | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solid se liquid | **Melting** | Garmi di jaati hai | Barf se paani |
| Liquid se solid | **Freezing** | Garmi nikaali jaati hai | Paani se barf |
| Liquid se gas | **Vaporisation** (boiling bhi isi ka ek tareeka hai) | Garmi di jaati hai | Paani se bhaap |
| Gas se liquid | **Condensation** | Garmi nikaali jaati hai | Bhaap se paani ki boondein |
| Solid se gas | **Sublimation** | Garmi di jaati hai | Dry ice se CO₂ gas |
| Gas se solid | **Deposition** | Garmi nikaali jaati hai | Hawa ki nami se frost |

```
                     sublimation (garmi do)
        ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
        │                                                ▼
      SOLID ──melting──▶ LIQUID ──vaporisation──▶       GAS
            ◀─freezing──        ◀─condensation──
        ▲                                                │
        └────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                     deposition (garmi nikaalo)

   (melting, vaporisation: garmi do; freezing, condensation: garmi nikaalo)
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Teen states aur unke beech ke chhe raaste. Upar ke raaste (aage ki taraf) mein garmi **di** jaati hai, neeche ke raaste (peeche ki taraf) mein garmi **nikaali** jaati hai.

Ek chhoti si yaad ka tareeka: **garmi do to particles "khulte" hain** (solid se liquid se gas), **garmi nikaalo to particles "simatte" hain** (gas se liquid se solid).

### Ye sab hota kyun hai? Particles ki nazar se

Physics ke chapter ([heat-and-temperature-part5.md](../../physics/heat-and-temperature/heat-and-temperature-part5.md)) mein ye particle picture aur state badalne ka ye chakkar (kapoor, naphthalene aur dry ice ke saath) ek baar aa chuka hai. Yahan bas chemistry ki nazar se chhota sa recap, sirf utna jitna aage chahiye.

- **Garmi dene par:** particles tez kaampne lagte hain. Jab wo itne tez ho jaate hain ki ek doosre ko pakde rakhne wala **aakarshan (hold)** unhe fixed jagah par nahi rakh paata, to solid ke particles apni jagah chhod kar phisalne lagte hain. Ye hui **melting**. Aur garmi do, to liquid ke particles itne tez ho jaate hain ki ek doosre ka saath chhod kar door-door udne lagte hain. Ye hui **vaporisation**.
- **Garmi nikaalne par:** particles ki raftaar kam hoti hai. Ek point par aakarshan unhe wapas paas kheench leta hai: gas se liquid (**condensation**), aur liquid se solid (**freezing**).
- **Sublimation aur deposition:** kuch padaarthon mein particles ka aakarshan itna kamzor hota hai ki garmi milte hi solid ke particles phisalne ki jagah **seedhe door-door** udne lagte hain. Ye hui sublimation. Deposition iska ulta hai: gas ke particles thande hokar seedhe solid ki fixed jagah par baith jaate hain.

```
   SOLID                 LIQUID                 GAS
  (garmi do ──▶)        (garmi do ──▶)

  o o o o o             o  o   o                o         o
  o o o o o              o   o  o                  o
  o o o o o             o  o  o  o            o          o
                         o  o   o                  o   o

  fixed, paas-paas      paas, phisalte          door-door, azaad
  ◀── (garmi nikaalo)   ◀── (garmi nikaalo)
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Garmi dene par particles khulte jaate hain (solid se liquid se gas). Garmi nikaalne par simatte jaate hain. Particles wahi rehte hain, sirf unki arrangement aur raftaar badalti hai.

## Part B: Melting point aur boiling point

Har pure padaarth ke liye solid se liquid banne ka ek fixed temperature hota hai: **melting point**. Liquid ke ubalne ka bhi ek fixed temperature hota hai: **boiling point**. (Pure padaarth ke liye jo temperature par solid pighalta hai, wahi par liquid jamta bhi hai.)

Paani ke liye (normal hawa ke pressure par):

- **Melting point (jamne ka point bhi):** **0 °C**
- **Boiling point:** **100 °C**

Alag-alag padaarthon ke apne alag fixed values hote hain. Kuch aam values (lagbhag):

| Padaarth | Melting point | Boiling point | Kamre ke temperature (lagbhag 25 °C) par kaisa hai? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paani | 0 °C | 100 °C | Liquid |
| Alcohol (ethanol) | -114 °C | 78 °C | Liquid |
| Mercury (paara) | -39 °C | 357 °C | **Liquid** |
| Mom (wax, candle ki) | lagbhag 55 °C | bahut zyada | Solid |
| Iron (loha) | lagbhag 1538 °C | bahut zyada | Solid |
| Nitrogen | -210 °C | **-196 °C** | Gas |

Is table se kuch dilchasp baatein samajh aati hain:

- **Mercury** ka melting point -39 °C hai, aur kamra usse kaafi garam hai (25 °C). Isiliye mercury **kamre ke temperature par liquid** hai. (Purane thermometer mein yahi chamakta liquid hota tha.) Ye ek **dhaatu (metal) hai jo liquid hai**, jo bahut kam dekhne ko milta hai.
- **Alcohol** 78 °C par hi ubalne lagta hai, paani se pehle. Isiliye ethanol jaisi cheezein bahut jaldi udti hain, aur sanitizer lagane par jaldi sookh jaata hai.
- **Nitrogen** ka boiling point -196 °C hai. Matlab -196 °C se thanda karne par hi wo gas se liquid banta hai. Kamre ke temperature par hawa ka bada hissa nitrogen hai, aur wo gas hi hai. **Liquid nitrogen** bahut hi thandi hoti hai (-196 °C), isliye ise sirf trained log hi sambhalte hain.
- **Iron** ko pighlane ke liye bahut tez garmi chahiye (lagbhag 1538 °C), isiliye loha factory ki bhatti mein pighlaya jaata hai.

**Ek zaroori baat:** state badalte waqt **temperature rukta hai**. Jaise pighalti barf 0 °C par hi rehti hai jab tak poori pighal na jaaye, aur ubalta paani 100 °C par hi rehta hai jab tak poora ubal na jaaye. Garmi lagatar milne par bhi thermometer nahi hilta, kyunki wo garmi particles ke hold ko dheela karne mein kharch ho rahi hoti hai. Isse **latent heat (chhupi hui garmi)** kehte hain, aur ye Physics ke Heat chapter ([Topic 5](../../physics/heat-and-temperature/heat-and-temperature-part5.md)) mein poore hisaab ke saath hai. Yahan hum uska hisaab nahi karenge.

## Part C: Temperature ko naapne ki do scales: Celsius aur Kelvin

Ab tak humne temperature **°C (degree Celsius)** mein liya. Vigyan mein ek aur scale bahut use hoti hai: **Kelvin (K)**. Physics ke chapter mein hum iska rishta pehle hi dekh chuke hain:

> **K = °C + 273**

(Ye lagbhag value hai. Zyada sahi value 273.15 hai, par Class 9 mein 273 hi lete hain.)

Kelvin ki ek khaas baat: Kelvin scale par **negative number nahi hota**. Sabse kam temperature 0 K hai (jo lagbhag -273 °C hota hai). Isiliye vigyan ke bahut se sawalon mein Kelvin lena aasaan padta hai. Ye bhi dhyan rakho: Kelvin mein "degree" nahi bolte. Hum **"273 K"** likhte hain, "273 °K" nahi.

Ek ek karke solved conversions dekhte hain:

**Example 1 (paani ka melting point):** 0 °C ko Kelvin mein badlo.

- K = °C + 273 = 0 + 273 = **273 K**

**Jaanch:** 273 - 273 = 0 ✅

**Example 2 (kamre ka temperature):** Kamre ka temperature 25 °C hai. Kelvin mein kitna hua?

- K = 25 + 273 = **298 K**

**Jaanch:** 298 - 273 = 25 ✅

**Example 3 (paani ka boiling point):** 100 °C ko Kelvin mein badlo.

- K = 100 + 273 = **373 K**

**Jaanch:** 373 - 273 = 100 ✅

**Example 4 (liquid nitrogen):** Nitrogen ka boiling point -196 °C hai. Kelvin mein kitna?

- K = -196 + 273 = **77 K**

Dhyan do: negative °C ko Kelvin mein badalne par jawab positive aata hai (kyunki -196 ke saath 273 jodne par 77 milta hai).

**Jaanch:** 77 - 273 = -196 ✅

**Example 5 (ulta: Kelvin se Celsius):** Ek gas ka temperature 300 K hai. Celsius mein kitna?

- °C = K - 273 = 300 - 273 = **27 °C**

**Jaanch:** 27 + 273 = 300 ✅

Ek chhoti conversion table:

| Cheez | °C mein | K mein |
|---|---|---|
| Paani ka melting point | 0 | 273 |
| Kamre ka temperature | 25 | 298 |
| Paani ka boiling point | 100 | 373 |
| Liquid nitrogen ka boiling point | -196 | 77 |
| Alcohol ka boiling point | 78 | 351 |

(Jaanch: 78 + 273 = 351 ✅.)

## Part D: Sublimation aur deposition: liquid ko "skip" karne wale

Ab Anika ke bowl ka raaz kholte hain. Zyadatar cheezein solid se pehle liquid banti hain, phir gas. Par kuch cheezein **beech ka liquid step skip kar deti hain**.

### Sublimation: solid se seedha gas

Kuch padaarth garmi milne par pighalte nahi, **seedhe gas ban jaate hain**. Roz ki zindagi ke examples:

- **Kapoor (camphor):** puja ke thaal mein rakha kapoor kuch dino mein khule mein chhota hota jaata hai aur gayab ho jaata hai, aur khushboo phailti hai. Wo pighal kar liquid nahi banta.
- **Naphthalene ki goliyan:** almari mein kapdon ke saath rakhi jaati hain, taaki keede door rahein. Ye goliyan bhi dheere-dheere chhoti hoti jaati hain aur gas ban kar udti hain. (Ye goliyan khaane ki cheez nahi hain, bachchon se door rakho.)
- **Dry ice:** ye **jama hua carbon dioxide (CO₂)** hai. Shaadi ke bowl mein jo ho raha tha, wo yahi hai.

### Dry ice ko "dry" kyun kehte hain?

Normal barf pighalti hai to **paani** banta hai, aur haath geela ho jaata hai ("wet"). Par dry ice ka **liquid roop banta hi nahi**: wo seedhe CO₂ **gas** ban jaati hai. Isliye na paani, na geelapan: bas **dry** (sookhi). Isliye naam hai "dry" ice.

Dry ice ka temperature lagbhag **-78 °C** hota hai, jo barf (0 °C) se kaafi zyada thanda hai. Is wajah se:

- **Ye skin ko jala deti hai** (frostbite): isliye hamesha chimte ya mote dastaane se pakadte hain, aur bachche kabhi haath na lagayein.
- **Kabhi bhi muh mein na daalna.** Ye jaan ke liye khatarnak hai.
- Band dibbe ya bottle mein na rakho. Jab wo gas banti hai to bahut zyada gas banti hai, aur band dibba phat sakta hai.
- Hamesha **khuli, hawa wali jagah** par use karte hain.

**Dhuan kahan se aaya?** Ye safed "dhuan" asal mein CO₂ gas nahi hai (wo to dikhti hi nahi). Dry ice itni thandi hoti hai ki apne aas-paas ki hawa ko thanda kar deti hai, aur us hawa ki **nami (paani ki bhaap) thandi hokar chhoti-chhoti paani ki boondon mein badal jaati hai (condensation)**. Wahi boondein dhuan jaise dikhti hain. Bowl mein garm paani daalne se dry ice tezi se gas banti hai aur ye dhuan aur ghana ho jaata hai.

```
   DRY ICE (solid CO2)            Seedha gas (liquid nahi banta)

   o o o o                         o         o
   o o o o   ── garmi milti hai ─▶      o   o
   o o o o       (-78 °C par)        o       o       o
                                          o
   particles fixed, paas           particles door, azaad
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Dry ice ke particles garmi milte hi solid se **seedha gas** ban jaate hain, beech mein liquid stage nahi aata. (Normal hawa ke pressure par.)

### Deposition: gas se seedha solid

Sublimation ka ulta: **gas ka seedha solid ban jaana**, jab usse thanda kiya jaata hai. Sabse aasaan example hai **frost (pala)**. Sardiyon ki bahut thandi subah mein, jab zameen ka temperature bahut gir jaata hai, hawa ki nami **boondon ki jagah seedhe barf ke chhote-chhote kanon** mein badal jaati hai aur ghaas ya gaadi ke sheeshe par safed parat ban jaati hai. Ye bhaap se seedha barf bana: **deposition**.

(Dhyan do: kai jagah frost ko "sublimation ka ulta" bhi bolte hain. Ye wahi cheez hai.)

## Part E: Pressure ka asar: gas ko liquid kaise banayein?

Ab tak humne sirf **temperature** (garmi dena ya nikaalna) badla. Par state badalne ka ek aur tareeka hai: **pressure** (dabaav).

Topic 2 mein dekha tha ki gas ko dabaya ja sakta hai (compressible), kyunki uske particles door-door hote hain. Ab socho: agar gas ko bahut zyada **dabate** jaayein, to particles aur paas aate jaate hain.

> Agar gas ko **dabao (pressure badhao)** aur saath mein **thanda karo**, to uske particles itne paas aa jaate hain ki wo **liquid** ban jaati hai.

```
   normal pressure par gas        bahut zyada dabane aur thanda karne par

   o      o      o                    o o  o o
      o      o                         o  o o  o
   o      o      o      ─────▶        o  o o  o
      o      o                         o o  o o
   particles door-door               particles paas-paas (liquid)
```

> **Figure 3.4** – Gas ko dabane aur thanda karne par particles paas aa jaate hain aur gas liquid mein badal jaati hai. (Liquid mein particles paas-paas par bina pattern ke hote hain aur phisalte rehte hain.)

Roz ki zindagi ke examples:

- **LPG cylinder (rasoi ka gas):** cylinder ke andar LPG (cooking gas) ko dabaakar **liquid** bana kar bhara jaata hai. LPG ke liye bahut zyada pressure nahi chahiye: hawa ke normal pressure ka kuch hi guna kaafi hai, kamre ke temperature par bhi. Isse bahut saari gas ek chhote cylinder mein aa jaati hai. Jab tum regulator khol kar burner jalate ho, to cylinder ke andar ka pressure ghatta hai aur liquid wapas **gas** ban kar bahar aati hai.
- **CNG (gaadiyon ka gas):** CNG ka matlab compressed natural gas. Isko bhi bahut zyada dabaakar cylinder mein bharte hain, jisse gaadi lambi doori tak chal sake. (CNG zyadatar dabi hui **gas** hi rehti hai, LPG ki tarah liquid nahi. Kai gaadiyon ki tanki mein ye bahut zyada pressure par bhari hoti hai.)
- **Fire extinguisher:** kai extinguishers mein CO₂ bahut zyada pressure par cylinder mein bhari hoti hai, aur bahar nikalte hi gas ban kar aag ko dhak deti hai.

**Dry ice phir se:** normal hawa ke pressure par CO₂ ko thanda karoge to wo **seedha solid** (dry ice) ban jaati hai aur garmi milne par **seedha gas**, beech ka liquid CO₂ nahi dikhta. Isi liye dry ice sublime hoti hai. Par agar CO₂ ko bahut zyada pressure par rakho, to wo liquid bhi ban sakti hai (jaise cylinder ke andar). Matlab: **state sirf temperature se nahi, pressure se bhi tay hoti hai.**

Is poori baat ka sar:

| Kya badla | Asar |
|---|---|
| Temperature badhao | Particles tez, state "khulti" hai (solid se liquid se gas) |
| Temperature ghatao | Particles dheere, state "simatti" hai (gas se liquid se solid) |
| Pressure badhao (gas par) | Particles paas aate hain, gas se liquid banne mein madad |
| Pressure ghatao | Particles door ja sakte hain, liquid se gas banna aasaan |

Pressure ke asar ki poori detail aage ki classes mein aayegi. Class 9 ke liye itna yaad rakhna kaafi hai.

## Part F: Ek hi cheez, teen states, ek hi particles

Ab ek mazedaar reasoning. Teen cheezein socho: **barf, paani, bhaap**. Dekhne mein bilkul alag lagti hain: barf kathor aur thandi, paani bahta hua, bhaap gayab (dikhti nahi, bas dhuaan sa). Par wo **alag-alag padaarth nahi** hain. Teeno mein wahi particles hain, bas unka **baithne ka tareeka aur raftaar** alag hai.

| | Barf | Paani | Bhaap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Particles kaun se | Wahi | Wahi | Wahi |
| Particles ki arrangement | Regular, fixed | Paas, phisalte hue | Door-door, azaad |
| Particles ki raftaar | Sabse kam (sirf kaampte hain) | Beech ki | Sabse zyada |
| Shape | Fixed | Bartan jaisi | Poore space mein |

**Ek mazedaar apvaad: barf paani par tairti hai.** Topic 2 mein dekha tha ki aam taur par solid apne liquid se zyada ghana hota hai. Par paani jab jam kar barf banta hai, to uske particles ek khaas **khule jaal (open pattern)** mein baithte hain, jisme beech mein zyada khaali jagah hoti hai. Isliye barf ka volume paani se thoda zyada aur density thodi kam hoti hai, aur barf paani par tairti hai (Physics mein paani ka ye anokha vyavhaar [heat-and-temperature-part2.md](../../physics/heat-and-temperature/heat-and-temperature-part2.md) mein aaya tha).

**Reasoning example A: kya pighalne par naya padaarth bana?**

Barf pighal kar paani bani. Kya paani ek naya padaarth hai? **Nahi.** Particles wahi hain, sirf unki arrangement badli hai. Isiliye jab paani wapas jam jaata hai, wahi barf wapas milti hai. Isse **physical change** (bhautik badlaav) kehte hain. Ismein naya padaarth nahi banta, aur badlaav ulta ho sakta hai. (Aage Chemistry mein "chemical change" aayega, jahan naya padaarth banta hai.)

**Reasoning example B: dry ice ka dhuan kyun dikhta hai par CO₂ gas nahi dikhti?**

CO₂ gas ke particles door-door aur bahut chhote hain, isliye gas khud nahi dikhti. Jo safed dhuan dikhta hai wo **hawa ki nami ke condense hone se bani paani ki chhoti boondein** hain. Matlab dhuan mein dikhne wali cheez CO₂ nahi, **paani** hai. Ye sochne layak hai: dry ice ka "dhuan" asal mein CO₂ ka gas nahi, balki hawa ki nami ke condensation se bana hai.

**Reasoning example C: kya mercury aur alcohol dono kamre mein liquid hain, to dono ek jaise hain?**

Dono kamre ke temperature (25 °C) par liquid hain, par unke melting aur boiling points alag hain: mercury ka -39 °C aur 357 °C, alcohol ka -114 °C aur 78 °C. Matlab ek jaisa state hona ek jaisa padaarth hone ki nishani nahi. Har padaarth ke apne **fixed melting aur boiling points** hote hain, aur ye uski pehchaan ka ek hissa hain.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Dry ice ek tarah ka barf hai jo pighal kar paani banti hai"**

Nahi. Dry ice **jama hua CO₂** hai, paani nahi. Ye seedhe gas banti hai (sublimation), paani nahi banti, aur isliye "dry" hai. Normal barf (paani ka solid) alag cheez hai.

**Galti 2: "Dry ice ko haath lagana ya chakhna safe hai, ye bas thandi cheez hai"**

Nahi. Dry ice lagbhag -78 °C par hoti hai aur skin ko jala sakti hai. **Haath mat lagao, muh mein mat daalo.** Hamesha door se dekho.

**Galti 3: Dry ice ke dhuan ko CO₂ gas samajhna**

CO₂ gas dikhti nahi. Safed dhuan **hawa ki nami ki chhoti boondein** hain jo thandi hokar condense hui hain.

**Galti 4: Kelvin mein "degree" lagana ya negative number likhna**

Kelvin mein "273 K" likhte hain, "273 °K" nahi. Aur Kelvin scale par negative number nahi hota. Sabse thanda temperature 0 K hai.

**Galti 5: Kelvin se Celsius ya Celsius se Kelvin mein galat disha mein jodna**

K = °C + 273. Celsius se Kelvin ke liye **jodo**, Kelvin se Celsius ke liye **ghatao**. Example: 25 °C = 298 K, na ki 25 - 273 = -248 K (jo galat hoga, kyunki Kelvin negative nahi hota).

**Galti 6: "State badalte waqt temperature badhta rehta hai"**

Nahi. State badalte waqt temperature fixed rehta hai (pighalte barf par 0 °C, ubalte paani par 100 °C), bhale hi garmi lagatar mil rahi ho.

**Galti 7: Mercury ko solid maan lena kyunki wo metal hai**

Mercury ek **liquid metal** hai: uska melting point -39 °C hai, aur kamre ka temperature usse zyada hai, isliye wo liquid hai.

## Quick Recap

- State badalne ke **chhe raaste**: melting (solid se liquid), freezing (liquid se solid), vaporisation (liquid se gas), condensation (gas se liquid), sublimation (solid se seedha gas), deposition (gas se seedha solid).
- **Garmi do** to particles khulte hain (solid se liquid se gas). **Garmi nikaalo** to particles simatte hain (gas se liquid se solid).
- **Melting point** aur **boiling point** har padaarth ke apne fixed hote hain. Paani: 0 °C aur 100 °C. Alcohol: boiling lagbhag 78 °C. Mercury: -39 °C par pighalta hai, isliye kamre mein liquid. Liquid nitrogen: -196 °C par ubalta hai.
- State badalte waqt **temperature constant** rehta hai (latent heat, jo Physics ke Heat chapter mein hai).
- **K = °C + 273.** 0 °C = 273 K, 25 °C = 298 K, 100 °C = 373 K, -196 °C = 77 K.
- **Sublimation:** kapoor, naphthalene ki goliyan, dry ice (jama hua CO₂). Dry ice ko "dry" isliye kehte hain kyunki wo liquid nahi banti. **Deposition:** frost.
- Dry ice -78 °C par hoti hai: **kabhi haath mat lagao aur kabhi muh mein mat daalo.** Dry ice ka dhuan CO₂ nahi, paani ki chhoti boondein hain.
- Gas ko **dabane aur thanda karne** par wo liquid ban sakti hai (LPG cylinder). CNG ko bhi dabaakar bharte hain, par wo zyadatar gas hi rehti hai.
- Barf, paani, bhaap: **ek hi padaarth, wahi particles**, sirf arrangement aur raftaar alag. Ye physical change hai.

## Practice Questions

1. Name the change of state in each case: (a) wax melting near a flame, (b) water droplets forming on the outside of a cold glass, (c) a camphor tablet becoming smaller in an open dish, (d) frost forming on grass on a cold winter morning.

2. Convert to Kelvin: (a) 0 °C, (b) 37 °C, (c) 100 °C, (d) -196 °C.

3. Convert to Celsius: (a) 273 K, (b) 310 K, (c) 77 K.

4. Why is dry ice called "dry"? Give one safety rule for handling it.

5. The melting point of mercury is -39 °C and the room temperature is 25 °C. Is mercury solid or liquid at room temperature? Explain.

6. Alcohol boils at about 78 °C and water at 100 °C. If both are heated slowly on the same stove, which will start boiling first, and why?

7. Explain, using particles, why a gas can be turned into a liquid by compressing it and cooling it.

8. Why does a cooking gas (LPG) cylinder contain a liquid, but we get a gas at the burner?

9. The "smoke" from dry ice looks white. Is it carbon dioxide gas? Explain what it really is.

10. State True or False, and correct the false ones: (a) The temperature of a boiling liquid keeps rising while it boils. (b) Naphthalene balls shrink in an almirah because they sublime. (c) Kelvin scale has negative temperatures. (d) Ice, water and steam are made of the same particles.

### Answers

1. (a) **Melting**. (b) **Condensation**. (c) **Sublimation**. (d) **Deposition** (gas, yaani hawa ki nami, seedha barf ke kan bana rahi hai).

2. K = °C + 273.
   (a) 0 + 273 = **273 K**.
   (b) 37 + 273 = **310 K**.
   (c) 100 + 273 = **373 K**.
   (d) -196 + 273 = **77 K**.
   Jaanch: 273 - 273 = 0 ✅; 310 - 273 = 37 ✅; 373 - 273 = 100 ✅; 77 - 273 = -196 ✅

3. °C = K - 273.
   (a) 273 - 273 = **0 °C**.
   (b) 310 - 273 = **37 °C**.
   (c) 77 - 273 = **-196 °C**.

4. Dry ice jama hua carbon dioxide hai. Wo pighal kar liquid nahi banti, seedha gas ban jaati hai (sublimation), isliye geelapan nahi hota, wo "dry" hai. Safety: **kabhi haath mat lagao aur muh mein mat daalo** (ye lagbhag -78 °C par hoti hai aur skin jala deti hai). Band dibbe mein bhi mat rakho aur khuli hawa wali jagah par use karo.

5. Mercury ka melting point -39 °C hai, aur kamre ka temperature 25 °C hai, jo melting point se **zyada** hai. Isliye mercury **liquid** hai. Jab temperature melting point se upar ho, to padaarth liquid hota hai (boiling point tak).

6. **Alcohol pehle ubalega**, kyunki uska boiling point (78 °C) paani ke boiling point (100 °C) se kam hai. Stove ki garmi se alcohol 78 °C par pahunchta hai to ubalne lagta hai, jabki paani abhi bhi 78 °C se aage garm hona baaki hai.

7. Gas mein particles door-door hote hain. Dabane se particles ke beech ki jagah kam hoti hai aur wo paas aate hain. Thanda karne se particles ki raftaar kam hoti hai aur aakarshan unhe paas rakhta hai. Dono saath hone par particles itne paas aa jaate hain ki wo liquid ki tarah paas-paas phisalne lagte hain.

8. Cylinder mein LPG ko dabaakar (thode se pressure par) liquid bana kar bhara jaata hai, taaki bahut saari gas chhote cylinder mein aa jaaye. Burner ke paas regulator kholne par cylinder ke andar ka pressure kam hota hai aur liquid wapas gas ban jaata hai, jo burner tak aati hai.

9. **Nahi.** CO₂ gas dikhti hi nahi. Safed "dhuan" hawa ki nami (paani ki bhaap) hai jo dry ice ke paas thandi hokar paani ki chhoti-chhoti boondon mein condense ho gayi. Matlab dikhne wali cheez **paani ki boondein** hain.

10. (a) **False** ❌. Ubalte waqt temperature fixed rehta hai (paani ke liye 100 °C). (b) **True** ✅. Naphthalene solid se seedha gas banti hai (sublimation). (c) **False** ❌. Kelvin scale par negative temperature nahi hota, sabse kam 0 K hai. (d) **True** ✅. Teeno mein wahi particles hain, sirf unki arrangement aur raftaar alag hai.
