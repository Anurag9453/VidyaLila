# Topic 4: Evaporation: Garmi Mein Gili Cheezein Kyun Sookhti Hain

Topic 3 mein humne dekha ki jab liquid ko garmi milti hai aur wo **boiling point** par pahunchta hai, to wo poora ubalta hai aur gas (bhaap) ban jaata hai. Par ek sawal chhoot gaya tha: balcony mein latka geela kapda to kabhi ubalta nahi, na aag, na 100 °C. Phir bhi wo sookh jaata hai, yaani uska paani **gas ban kar ud jaata hai**. Ye kaise? Aaj isi chhupe hue raaz ko particles ke picture se kholenge.

---

## Balcony ka geela kurta

Mrs. Saxena ne Sunday ko apne bete Rohit ka naya cotton ka kurta dhoya aur balcony ki railing par phaila diya. Din dhoop wala tha aur achhi khaasi hawa chal rahi thi. Rohit padhne baitha tha, par uska dhyaan baar baar kurte par ja raha tha. Ek ghante baad usne kurta chhua: **bilkul sookha**.

Do mahine baad barsaat ka mausam tha. Mrs. Saxena ne wahi kurta, wahi cotton, wahi railing, wahi dhulai. Par is baar bahar badal chhaye the, hawa mein nami thi aur hawa bhi lagbhag ruki hui thi. Shaam ho gayi, kurta ab bhi **geela aur bhaari** tha. Agle din dopahar tak bhi thoda sa nam raha.

Rohit ne hairaan hokar poochha, "Mummy, kurta wahi, paani wahi. Phir us din ek ghante mein sookh gaya, aur aaj poore din mein bhi kyun nahi sookha? Aur paani gaya kahan? Kya wo gayab ho gaya?"

Mrs. Saxena hanskar boli, "Gayab nahi hua beta, wo **hawa mein mil gaya**. Par kyun ek din jaldi aur ek din der se, ye samjhne ke liye tumhe paani ke particles ke andar jhaankna padega."

Chalo, hum bhi jhaankte hain.

## Part A: Evaporation hota kaise hai?

Topic 2 aur 3 ka picture yaad karo: liquid mein **particles** (jo atoms ya molecules ho sakte hain, ye hum chapter "Atoms and Molecules" mein padhenge) ek doosre ke paas hote hain, par phisalte rehte hain aur lagatar kaampte aur ghoomte rehte hain. Ab ek zaroori baat jo ab tak nahi boli:

> **Liquid ke saare particles ek hi raftaar se nahi chalte.**

Kuch particles tez hote hain, kuch dheere, kuch beech ke. Jaise class mein sab bachhe ek hi speed se nahi daudte. Temperature ek tarah se in sab ki **ausat (average) energy** ka naap hai.

Ab sochiye liquid ki **surface** par baithe particles. Unke neeche aur aas-paas ke particles unhe aakarshan se kheenchte hain, par unke **upar** koi liquid particle nahi hai, upar to hawa hai. Agar koi surface particle **itna tez** chal raha ho ki wo is kheenchaav ko todkar nikal sake, to wo liquid chhodkar hawa mein chala jaata hai. Wahi particle ab **gas (vapour)** ban chuka hai.

```
         hawa (door-door particles)
      .       o  <--- tez surface particle nikal gaya      .
                 \
   ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~
     o   o  O  o   o   o  O  o   o      <--- surface
    o  o   o   o  o    o   o   o  o
      o   o    o    o   o    o   o
    o   o  o    o  o   o     o   o
        liquid (neeche ke particles andar se kheench rahe hain)
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Surface ke jo particles (bade O) ke paas aakarshan todne layak tez raftaar hoti hai, wo liquid chhodkar hawa mein ud jaate hain: yahi evaporation hai. Andar ke particles ko chaaron taraf se doosre particles kheenchte hain, isliye wo itni aasani se nahi nikalte.

Is picture se evaporation ki teen khaas baatein nikalti hain:

1. **Evaporation sirf surface se hota hai**, kyunki bahar nikalne ka raasta sirf surface wale particles ke paas hai.
2. **Evaporation kisi bhi temperature par ho sakta hai**, 100 °C tak pahunchne ki zaroorat nahi. Jab tak liquid mein kuch particles itne tez hain ki bahar nikal sakein, evaporation chalta rahega. Barf bhi bahut thande mausam mein dheere dheere chhoti ho jaati hai, kyunki uski surface ke tez particles bhi nikalte rehte hain (ye Topic 3 wali sublimation hai, yaani solid se seedha gas, par wajah wahi hai: surface ke tez particles).
3. **Evaporation dheema** hota hai, kyunki ek waqt par sirf kuch hi particles itne tez hote hain.

Ab Rohit ke sawal ka ek hissa solve ho gaya: kurta kisi ubaal se nahi sookha. Uske geele kapde ki surface par rakhe paani ke tez particles chupchap nikalte rahe, aur dheere dheere sab paani vapour ban kar hawa mein mil gaya.

### Evaporation aur boiling: dono alag hain

Topic 3 mein boiling dekhi thi: liquid ek fixed temperature (boiling point) par **poora** ubalta hai. Evaporation usse kaafi alag hai. (Physics ke Heat chapter ([heat-and-temperature-part5.md](../../physics/heat-and-temperature/heat-and-temperature-part5.md)) mein ye farq aur evaporation ke factors sirf naam se aaye the; yahan hum unhe particles ki nazar se kholenge.) Pehle ye dekho ki dono mein particles ke picture mein fark kya hai.

```
   EVAPORATION                         BOILING

  ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~              ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~
   o   o   o   o   o                  o   O   o   O   o
  o   o   o   o   o                     o   (  )   o
    o   o   o   o                      o  (    )  o  O
  o   o   o   o   o                      o  (  )   o
   sirf upar se                       andar bhi bulbule
   particles nikalte hain             bante hain
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Evaporation mein sirf surface se particles nikalte hain. Boiling mein liquid ke **andar** bhi gas ke bulbule ban jaate hain (bulbule ka andar ka hissa paani ki bhaap hota hai), jo upar uthkar surface par fut jaate hain.

Boiling mein andar bulbula bhi kyun ban paata hai? Bulbula banne ke liye liquid ke andar bhi particles ko gas ki tarah door-door hona padta hai. Ye tab hi possible hai jab liquid ke particles itne tez ho jayein ki andar ke kheenchaav se bhi nikal kar bhaap ki jagah bana sakein. Wo temperature liquid ka **boiling point** hai. Us se kam temperature par andar ke particles itne tez nahi hote, isliye bulbule nahi bante, bas surface se evaporation chalta hai.

Dono ko aamne-saamne rakh kar dekhte hain:

| Baat | Evaporation | Boiling |
|---|---|---|
| Kis temperature par | Boiling point se neeche, har temperature par | Sirf boiling point par |
| Kahan se | Sirf surface se | Poore liquid mein, andar bulbule bante hain |
| Raftaar | Dheema | Tez |
| Bulbule | Nahi bante | Bante hain |
| Liquid ka temperature | Bacha hua liquid thanda hota hai | Boiling point par ruka rehta hai (Topic 3 jaisa) |
| Garmi ka source | Aas-paas (hawa, dhoop, kapde, skin) se leta hai | Neeche se heater ya aag deti hai |
| Example | Geele kapde ka sookhna, pasine ka sookhna | Gas par chadha ubalta paani |

> **Yaad rakho:** dono mein liquid se gas banta hai, isliye dono vaporisation ke roop hain. Par **evaporation boiling nahi hai**. Evaporation chupchap surface par chalta rehta hai, boiling ek fixed temperature par poore liquid mein dhoom macha deti hai.

## Part B: Evaporation ki raftaar kin cheezon par depend karti hai?

Ab Rohit ke kurte ka asli raaz: ek din ek ghante mein sookha, doosre din poora din. Is fark ke peeche chaar cheezein hain. Har ek ko particles ke picture se samjhte hain, aur ghar par karne layak safe activity bhi dekhte hain.

### 1. Surface area (liquid kitna phaila hai)

Evaporation surface se hota hai, isliye surface jitni **badi**, utne zyada particles ko nikalne ka mauka milta hai. Isliye wahi paani **thali mein** jaldi, aur **katori mein** der se sookhta hai.

**Activity 1 (safe):** Do bilkul same size ki katori lo ya ek katori aur ek chauri thali. Dono mein **50 mL paani** daalo (naap ke liye medicine wala cup ya measuring cup use karo). Dono ko ek hi jagah rakh do. Kuch ghanton ya kuch dino baad dekho. Thali ka paani pehle khatam hoga. Paani wahi, jagah wahi, bas sataha alag.

Isi wajah se ek mota kurta jo **gol bundle** bana kar rakha ho, **phaile hue** kapde se der se sookhta hai: bundle ke andar ka paani hawa ke sampark mein nahi hai.

### 2. Temperature (liquid kitna garam hai)

Temperature badhne par particles ki **ausat energy** badhti hai, yaani zyada particles itne tez ho jaate hain ki surface se nikal sakein. Isliye garam liquid mein evaporation **tez** hota hai. Isiliye dhoop mein kapde jaldi sookhte hain, aur garm chai mein se bhaap zyada uthti dikhti hai.

**Activity 2 (safe):** Do bilkul same gile kapde (ek hi size ke rumal) lo, dono ko barabar nichod kar geela karo. Ek ko **dhoop** mein aur doosre ko **chhaanv** mein latka do (dono mein hawa lagbhag barabar ho). Dhoop wala pehle sookhega.

### 3. Humidity (hawa mein pehle se kitni bhaap hai)

Hawa mein pehle se paani ki bhaap hoti hai. Jab hawa mein bhaap **zyada** ho (barsaat), to surface se nikle hue particles ko hawa mein "jagah" kam milti hai, aur hawa mein pehle se ghoom rahe bhaap ke particles **wapas surface par aakar liquid mein** mil bhi jaate hain. Isliye bahar jaane wale aur wapas aane wale particles lagbhag barabar ho jaate hain aur kapda **der se sookhta** hai. Hawa **sookhi** ho to wapas aane wale particles kam hote hain aur evaporation tez hota hai.

Yehi wajah hai ki Rohit ke kurte ne barsaat mein poora din liya, jabki sookhe garam din ek ghanta.

### 4. Wind speed (hawa kitni tez hai)

Surface se nikle hue vapour particles agar wahin surface ke paas ghoomte rahein, to unmein se kuch wapas liquid mein gir jaate hain, aur surface ke paas ki hawa **nami se bhar** jaati hai. Jab hawa **tez** chalti hai, to wo nikle hue vapour particles ko surface se door **udaa le jaati** hai aur sookhi hawa ko jagah de deti hai. Isliye wapas aane ke mauke kam hote hain aur evaporation tez hota hai.

**Activity 3 (safe):** Do same gile rumal lo. Ek ko **pankhe ke neeche** (ya khuli hawa mein) aur doosre ko **band kamre mein bina hawa ke** latka do. Hawa wala pehle sookhega.

### Chaaron ko ek saath dekho

| Factor | Zyada ho to evaporation | Particle picture mein wajah |
|---|---|---|
| Surface area | Tez | Zyada surface particles ko nikalne ka mauka |
| Temperature | Tez | Zyada particles ke paas nikalne layak energy |
| Humidity (nami) | Dheema | Hawa mein bhaap zyada, wapas lautne wale particles zyada |
| Wind speed | Tez | Nikle hue vapour particles door chale jaate hain, wapas kam lautte hain |

### Solved examples

**Example 1 (do jagah, ek hi kapda):** Rohit ke dono kurte ek jaise hain aur har kurte mein **100 g** paani laga hai. Dhoop aur hawa wale din ye 100 g paani **1 ghante** mein evaporate ho gaya. Barsaat wale din 1 ghante mein sirf **25 g** paani evaporate hua. Agar barsaat mein bhi har ghante itna hi paani nikalta rahe, to kurta pura sookhne mein kitna time lega? Dono dinon ki raftaar kitni guna alag thi?

- Pehla din: raftaar = 100 g ÷ 1 ghanta = **100 g/ghanta**
- Barsaat: raftaar = 25 g ÷ 1 ghanta = **25 g/ghanta**
- Barsaat mein sookhne ka time = 100 g ÷ 25 g/ghanta = **4 ghante**
- Raftaar ka fark = 100 ÷ 25 = **4 guna**

**Jaanch:** 25 g/ghanta × 4 ghante = 100 g ✅. (Asli zindagi mein raftaar badalti rehti hai, ye bas ek simple andaza hai, par idea sahi hai: nami zyada, raftaar kam, time zyada.)

**Example 2 (teen kapde, kaun pehle sookhega?):** Teen ek jaise geele rumal (P, Q, R), teeno mein barabar paani hai.
- **P:** poora phailakar **dhoop** mein latkaya.
- **Q:** gol bundle (gola) bana kar **dhoop** mein rakha.
- **R:** poora phailakar **chhaanv** mein latkaya.

Kaun sabse pehle sookhega? Kaun sabse baad mein?

- P mein surface area bada hai **aur** temperature zyada hai: dono factors evaporation tez karte hain, isliye **P sabse pehle** sookhega.
- Q mein temperature zyada hai par surface area chhota hai (bundle ke andar ka paani bahar nahi nikal paata).
- R mein surface area bada hai par temperature kam hai.
- Q aur R mein se **kaun pehle** sookhega, ye is par depend karta hai ki bundle kitna kasa hai aur chhaanv kitni thandi hai. Dono mein se ek-ek factor kamzor hai, isliye dono P se **zaroor der se** sookhenge. Hum sirf itna pakka keh sakte hain: P pehle, aur Q ya R mein se koi ek sabse baad mein.

**Jaanch:** Agar ek factor ko badalte hain (Q: area ghataya, R: temperature ghataya), to evaporation dheema hota hai: P > Q aur P > R, yaani P sabse tez ✅.

## Part C: Evaporation se thandak

Garmi mein pasina nikalta hai aur jab hawa chalti hai to bahut thanda lagta hai. Geele rumal ko matthe par rakhne par bhi thanda lagta hai. Acetone ya nail-polish remover hathelli par lagao to bahut thanda lagta hai. In sab mein ek hi baat chal rahi hai: **evaporation thandak deta hai.** Kyun?

### Kaun se particles nikalte hain?

Part A mein dekha: sirf **tez** (zyada energy wale) particles liquid se nikal paate hain. Matlab wo particles jaate waqt apni **zyada energy saath le jaate hain**. Peeche bache hue particles ki **ausat energy kam** ho jaati hai. Aur kam ausat energy ka matlab hai **kam temperature**: bacha hua liquid thanda ho jaata hai.

### Garmi kahan se aati hai? Aas-paas se

Ek aur tareeke se socho. Liquid se gas banne ke liye particles ko ek doosre ka kheenchaav todna padta hai, aur iske liye **garmi (heat)** chahiye (Topic 3 mein boiling mein bhi ye dekha tha aur Physics ke chapter [heat-and-temperature-part5.md](../../physics/heat-and-temperature/heat-and-temperature-part5.md) mein is "chhupi hui garmi" ka hisaab bhi hai). Geele kapde ka paani ye garmi kahan se laaye? Kapde ke paas koi heater to hai nahi.

Jawab: **aas-paas (surroundings) se.** Kapde ka paani:
- kapde se garmi leta hai,
- kapde ke aas-paas ki hawa se leta hai,
- agar kapda hamare badan par hai to hamari **skin** se leta hai.

Jis cheez se garmi chali jaati hai, wo **thandi** ho jaati hai. Isliye evaporation hone par paani ke saath-saath uske aas-paas ki cheezein (hamari skin, matka, kapde, hawa) thandi hoti jaati hain.

> **Evaporation = surroundings se garmi lena, isliye surroundings thanda hota hai.**

### Roz ke examples

1. **Pasina:** Hamare badan ka temperature zyada hone par pasina nikalta hai. Pasina skin ki surface par evaporate hota hai aur skin se garmi leta hai, isliye badan thanda hota hai. Isi tarah hamara sharir garmi mein khud ko thanda rakhta hai.

2. **Matka (mitti ka ghada):** Matke ki deewaron mein bahut chhote chhote chhed hote hain. Paani in chhedon se thoda thoda bahar aakar deewar ki bahari sataha par evaporate hota hai. Evaporate hone ke liye wo garmi andar ke paani se leta hai, isliye matke ka paani fridge ke bina thanda rehta hai. Dhoop ya hawa wali jagah rakhne par matka aur achha thanda karta hai, kyunki evaporation tez hota hai (Part B).

3. **Garam chhat par paani chhidakna:** Dopahar mein tapi hui chhat ya aangan par paani chhidakne se wo thanda ho jaata hai. Paani evaporate hota hai aur zameen se garmi kheench leta hai.

4. **Garmi mein cotton ke kapde aaramdayak kyun?** Cotton paani ko achhi tarah **soakh** leta hai. Isliye pasina cotton mein phail jaata hai (surface area badi) aur jaldi evaporate hota hai, jisse thandak milti hai. Kuch synthetic kapde pasine ko kam soakhte hain, to pasina skin par hi rukta hai ya bahta hai aur utna evaporate nahi hota, isliye chipchip aur garmi lagti hai.

5. **Pankhe ke neeche thanda kyun lagta hai?** Pankha kamre ka temperature nahi ghatata (balki motor ki garmi thodi badha sakta hai). Par wo hamari skin ke paas ki **nami bhari hawa ko hata kar** sookhi hawa laata hai, aur pasine ka evaporation tez ho jaata hai (wind speed, Part B). Tez evaporation matlab zyada thandak.

6. **Desert cooler sookhe mausam mein achha kyun?** Cooler mein gile pad (ghaas ke pad) hote hain, jinse paani evaporate hota hai aur hawa thandi hoti hai. **Sookhi hawa** (kam humidity) mein evaporation tez hota hai, to cooler zyada thanda karta hai. **Nami wale mausam** mein hawa mein pehle se bhaap bhari hai, to evaporation dheema ho jaata hai aur cooler utna asar nahi dikhata, balki hawa mein chipchipahat aur badh jaati hai.

7. **Acetone hathelli par thanda kyun lagta hai?** Acetone bahut **volatile** hai, yaani bahut tezi se evaporate hota hai (usme particles ke beech kheenchaav kam hota hai, isliye tez surface particles aasani se nikal jaate hain). Itni tezi se evaporate hote hue wo hathelli se kaafi garmi kheench leta hai, isliye jaldi aur zyada thanda lagta hai.

> **Safety:** acetone ya nail-polish remover ki bhaap saans mein na lo, aur ye kaam bachhe bade ki nigrani mein hi karein, khule hawadaar kamre mein. Khaana ya peena to bilkul nahi. Ghar par isse bhi safe test hai: ek haath par **paani** aur doosre haath par **alcohol wale hand sanitizer ki ek boond** lagao (kisi bade ki madad se, aankh se door), aur dekho kaun jaldi thandak deta hai. Jo jaldi evaporate hota hai, wahi zyada thanda lagta hai.

**Activity 4 (safe, thandak ka test):** Do thermometer lo (ya ek hi thermometer baari baari se). Ek ke bulb par geela kapda ya geeli ruyi lapet do aur doosre ko sookha rehne do. Dono ko pankhe ke saamne rakh kar 5 minute baad temperature padho. Geele kapde wala thermometer **kam temperature** dikhayega.

### Solved example: thandak ka hisaab

**Example 3 (matke ka paani zyada se zyada kitna thanda ho sakta hai?):** Ek matke mein **1 kg** paani hai. Agar sirf **20 g** paani evaporate hota hai aur **maan lo** uske liye lagi saari garmi **matke ke andar ke paani se hi** aayi (bahar se koi garmi andar nahi aayi), to andar ka paani **zyada se zyada** kitna thanda ho sakta hai? (Ye ek upper limit wala hisaab hai, asli matke ki prediction nahi.) Maan lo evaporation ke liye **1 g paani ko 2260 J** chahiye (Physics chapter wali value, lagbhag), aur paani ki specific heat capacity **4200 J/kg °C** hai.

- Evaporate hone mein lagi garmi = 20 g × 2260 J/g = **45200 J**
- Ye garmi 1 kg paani se nikli, to temperature ghatega: ΔT = Q ÷ (m c) = 45200 J ÷ (1 kg × 4200 J/kg °C) = 45200 ÷ 4200 ≈ **10.8 °C** (zyada se zyada)

**Jaanch:** 4200 × 10.8 ≈ 45360 J ≈ 45200 J ✅. Ye 10.8 °C sirf **upper limit** hai, asli thandak nahi. Asli matke mein garmi kamre ki hawa aur matke ki deewar se bhi aati rehti hai, isliye paani isse kaafi kam thanda hota hai, bas kuch degree. Idea ye hai: sirf **20 g** paani ke evaporate hone se **1 kg** paani ka temperature kai degree kam ho sakta hai, kyunki evaporation mein garmi kaafi zyada lagti hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Evaporation sirf garam paani mein hota hai"**

Nahi. Evaporation thande paani mein bhi hota hai, bas dheema. Table par rakha glass ka thanda paani bhi kuch dino mein kam ho jaata hai.

**Galti 2: "Evaporation aur boiling ek hi cheez hain"**

Dono mein liquid gas banta hai, par evaporation surface se, kisi bhi temperature par, dheere hota hai. Boiling poore liquid mein, ek fixed temperature par, bulbule banakar hoti hai.

**Galti 3: "Geele kapde ka paani gayab ho jaata hai"**

Paani gayab nahi hota. Wo **vapour (gas)** ban kar hawa mein mil jaata hai. Mass kahin nahi jaata, bas liquid se gas ban jaata hai.

**Galti 4: "Pankhe se kamre ka temperature kam ho jaata hai"**

Pankha hawa ko hilaata hai. Hamein thanda isliye lagta hai ki hawa pasine ka evaporation tez kar deti hai. Agar kamre mein koi na ho to pankha chalane se temperature kam nahi hota.

**Galti 5: "Nami wale din mein evaporation hota hi nahi"**

Hota hai, par dheema, kyunki hawa mein pehle se bhaap hai aur kuch particles wapas liquid mein lautne lagte hain. Isliye barsaat mein kapde der se sookhte hain, na ki bilkul nahi.

## Quick Recap

- **Evaporation** liquid ki surface se, **kisi bhi temperature par** (boiling point se neeche), dheere dheere gas (vapour) banna hai.
- Liquid ke saare particles ek speed se nahi chalte. Jo **surface** ke tez particles aakarshan todne layak energy rakhte hain, wahi nikal jaate hain.
- **Boiling** mein liquid ke **andar** bhi bulbule bante hain aur ye ek fixed temperature (boiling point) par hoti hai. Evaporation boiling nahi hai.
- Evaporation tez hota hai jab: **surface area zyada**, **temperature zyada**, **humidity kam**, **hawa tez**. Har ek ki wajah particle picture se samjhi ja sakti hai.
- Evaporation mein tez particles energy saath le jaate hain, aur garmi **aas-paas se** li jaati hai, isliye **bacha hua liquid aur aas-paas thanda** ho jaata hai.
- Roz ke examples: pasina, matka, chhat par paani chhidakna, cotton ke kapde, pankha, desert cooler (sookhi hawa mein zyada achha), acetone ka thanda lagna.
- Example: wahi kapda, wahi paani, par nami wale din 4 guna dheema sookha (100 g/ghanta ke mukable 25 g/ghanta).

2. Explain, using the particle picture, why evaporation happens only from the surface of a liquid.

3. Give one reason from the particle picture for each: (a) wet clothes dry faster in a wide spread-out form, (b) wet clothes dry faster on a hot sunny day, (c) wet clothes dry slowly in the humid monsoon, (d) wet clothes dry faster on a windy day.

4. Two identical wet towels each hold 200 g of water. Towel A loses all its water in 2 hours. Towel B loses 50 g of water in 1 hour. Assume the loss in each case is steady. (a) How long will Towel B take to dry completely? (b) How many times faster does Towel A dry compared to Towel B?

5. Three identical wet shirts are dried like this: Shirt X is spread out in the sun, Shirt Y is spread out in a dark room with closed windows, Shirt Z is folded tightly and kept in the sun. Which dries first? Give reasons.

6. Why does water kept in an earthen pot (matka) stay cooler than water in a steel bottle kept in the same room?

7. Why do we feel more comfortable in cotton clothes than in many synthetic clothes on a hot day?

8. Why does a desert cooler work better on a dry summer day than on a humid day?

9. Acetone poured on the palm feels much colder than the same amount of water. Explain.

10. A student sprinkles 30 g of water on a hot floor and all of it evaporates. If about 2260 J of heat is needed per gram of water, how much heat does the floor lose? (If this heat were taken from 2 kg of water instead, by about how many degrees Celsius would that water cool? Use c = 4200 J/kg °C.)

11. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) Evaporation takes place only at 100 °C.
    (b) During evaporation, the remaining liquid becomes cooler.
    (c) Bubbles forming inside the liquid is a sign of evaporation.
    (d) Evaporation of a liquid is faster when the air around is already full of water vapour.

### Answers

1. **Evaporation** liquid ki surface se, kisi bhi temperature par, dheere dheere vapour banna hai. Liquid mein kuch particles itne tez hote hain ki wo aakarshan todkar nikal sakein, aur ye har temperature par hota hai (temperature kam ho to bas kam particles tez hote hain). **Boiling** mein andar ke particles bhi itne tez hone chahiye ki bulbule bana sakein, aur ye tab hota hai jab liquid ka temperature boiling point par pahunche.

2. Andar ke particles ko chaaron taraf se doosre liquid particles kheench rahe hain, isliye wo nikal nahi paate. Surface par sirf neeche aur side se kheenchaav hota hai aur upar hawa hai, to wahan ke tez particles aasani se nikal jaate hain.

3. (a) Phaile kapde mein surface area zyada hai, to zyada particles ko nikalne ka mauka milta hai. (b) Dhoop mein temperature zyada hai, to zyada particles ke paas nikalne layak energy hoti hai. (c) Hawa mein pehle se bhaap zyada hai, to nikle hue particles ko jagah kam milti hai aur kuch wapas kapde mein aa jaate hain. (d) Hawa nikle hue vapour particles ko surface se door le jaati hai aur sookhi hawa le aati hai, to wapas lautne wale particles kam hote hain.

4. Towel A ki raftaar = 200 g ÷ 2 ghante = 100 g/ghanta. Towel B ki raftaar = 50 g/ghanta. (a) Towel B ko lagega 200 g ÷ 50 g/ghanta = **4 ghante**. (b) 100 ÷ 50 = **2 guna** tez.
   Jaanch: 100 g/ghanta × 2 ghante = 200 g ✅ aur 50 g/ghanta × 4 ghante = 200 g ✅

5. **Shirt X** pehle sookhegi: usmein surface area bhi zyada hai aur temperature bhi zyada hai (sun mein). Shirt Y mein temperature kam aur hawa nahi hai, Shirt Z mein surface area kam hai. Y ya Z mein se koi bhi sabse baad mein sookh sakti hai, par dono X se der se sookhengi.

6. Matke ki deewaron ke chhote chhedon se paani bahar aakar evaporate hota hai aur andar ke paani se garmi leta hai, isliye andar ka paani thanda rehta hai. Steel ki bottle mein paani bahar nahi nikalta, to ye asar nahi hota.

7. Cotton pasine ko achhi tarah soakh kar phaila deta hai, jisse pasina jaldi evaporate hota hai aur skin ko thandak milti hai. Synthetic kapde zyadatar kam soakhte hain, to pasina skin par rukta hai aur kam evaporate hota hai.

8. Cooler mein gile pad ka paani evaporate hokar hawa thandi karta hai. Sookhi hawa (kam humidity) mein evaporation tez hota hai, isliye thandak zyada. Nami wali hawa mein evaporation dheema hota hai, to cooler kam thanda karta hai.

9. Acetone bahut volatile hai, yaani bahut tezi se evaporate hota hai, to wo hathelli se zyada aur jaldi garmi leta hai. Isliye paani ke mukable zyada thanda lagta hai.

10. Garmi = 30 g × 2260 J/g = **67800 J**. Agar ye garmi 2 kg paani se nikalti, to ΔT = 67800 ÷ (2 × 4200) = 67800 ÷ 8400 ≈ **8.07 °C**, yaani lagbhag **8 °C** thanda.
    Jaanch: 8400 × 8.07 ≈ 67788 J ≈ 67800 J ✅

11. (a) **False** ❌. Evaporation har temperature par hota hai (boiling point se neeche). (b) **True** ✅. Tez particles energy le jaate hain aur garmi aas-paas se li jaati hai, to bacha liquid thanda hota hai. (c) **False** ❌. Andar bulbule banna boiling ki nishani hai, evaporation sirf surface se hota hai. (d) **False** ❌. Hawa pehle se nami se bhari ho to evaporation **dheema** hota hai.
