# Topic 5: Plasma, BEC aur Chapter Ka Saar

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki liquid ke surface se tez particles nikal kar bhaap ban jaate hain aur peeche bacha liquid thanda ho jaata hai. Ab tak hamari poori kahani **teen states** ki thi: solid, liquid aur gas. Par kya matter ki bas yahi teen states hain? Is last topic mein hum do aur "ajeeb" states se milenge, phir kuch doubts saaf karenge, apni samajh ko ek reasoning round mein parkhenge, aur aakhir mein poore chapter ka hisaab ek table mein rakhenge.

## Part A: Plasma: chamakti hui charged gas

Topic 1 se yaad hai ki sab kuch chhote **particles (kan)** se bana hai, aur wo atoms ya molecules ho sakte hain (ye humein "Atoms and Molecules" chapter mein poori tarah milenge). Normal gas mein ye particles **bina charge** ke hote hain: na plus, na minus.

Ab kalpana karo ki gas ko **bahut hi zyada garam** kar diya jaaye, ya usme se bijli guzaari jaaye. Particles itni zor se takrate hain ki unmein se kuch apne "chhote saathi" (electrons) kho dete hain. Jo kan bacha, uspar plus charge aa jaata hai, aur jo chhota saathi bhaaga, uspar minus charge hota hai. Is tarah gas ke andar **plus aur minus charged particles ka jhund** ban jaata hai. Gas ki aisi hi state ko **plasma** kehte hain. Isi ko **ionised gas** bhi kaha jaata hai.

```
      GAS (normal)                  PLASMA

     o         o                  +    -        +
          o                    -       +    -
     o              o               +      -   +
         o      o                -      +      -
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Normal gas ke kan bina charge ke (o), jabki plasma mein plus (+) aur minus (-) charged particles tezi se idhar-udhar ghoomte hain.

Plasma ki kuch khaas baatein:

- Isme particles **bahut zyada energy** ke saath tezi se bhaagte hain, gas se bhi zyada.
- Isme charged particles hain, isliye ye **bijli ko achhe se guzarne** deta hai aur **chamakta** (roshni deta) hai.
- Uska apna fixed aakaar ya size nahi hota, gas ki tarah wo bhi phail jaata hai.

### Plasma kahan milta hai?

- **Sooraj aur dusre tare:** unke andar ka matter bahut garam hai aur plasma state mein hai.
- **Bijli ki lakeer (lightning):** kuch pal ke liye hawa ki gas bahut garam hoke plasma ban jaati hai, isiliye wo chamakti hai.
- **Neon sign:** shishe ki nali mein neon gas hoti hai. Usme bijli guzarne par gas ke kuch hi kan charged hote hain: ye **partly ionised gas** (thoda sa plasma) hai, jo chamakti hai. Neon se laal-narangi roshni nikalti hai; doosri gason se doosre rang milte hain.
- **Tubelight:** uski nali ke andar bhi bahut kam matra mein gas aur thodi mercury ki bhaap hoti hai, jo bijli guzarne par partly ionised hokar roshni deti hai.

Mazedaar baat: poore **universe mein jitna dikhne wala matter** hai, uska zyada hissa **plasma** hi hai, kyunki sooraj jaise lakhon-karodon tare plasma ke gole hain. Hum, dharti par, solid, liquid aur gas ke beech rehte hain, jo universe mein relatively kam common hain.

## Part B: Bose-Einstein Condensate (BEC): "wah, aisa bhi hota hai"

Ab plasma ke bilkul ulta chalte hain: bahut zyada garmi se ab bahut zyada **thand** ki taraf.

Pichhle topics mein dekha tha ki Kelvin scale par sabse kam temperature **0 K** hai, jise **absolute zero** kehte hain, yaani lagbhag **−273 °C**. Sochte hain ki agar ek aisi gas ko, jo bahut hi **kam density** wali ho (yaani uske kan bahut door-door hon), absolute zero ke **bahut hi paas** tak thanda kar diya jaaye, to kya hoga?

Itna thanda karne par uske kan lagbhag **ruk se jaate hain** aur sab ek jaisa vyavhaar karne lagte hain, jaise ek hi bada "super-kan" ho. Matter ki is anokhi state ko **Bose-Einstein Condensate (BEC)** kehte hain.

```
   Normal gas                  BEC (absolute zero ke bahut paas)

    o      o                      (  o o o  )
        o        o                (  o o o  )
     o      o                     (  o o o  )
   alag-alag, tez              sab ek jaisa, bahut dheema
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Normal gas ke kan alag-alag aur tez; BEC mein kan lagbhag ruke hue aur ek jaisa vyavhaar karte hue (sirf ek soch ke liye sketch: BEC ab bhi bahut patli gas hai, solid ki tarah jude kan nahi).

Is state ke baare mein kuch baatein:

- Iska naam bhartiya vaigyanik **Satyendra Nath Bose** aur **Albert Einstein** ke naam par pada hai. Bose ne is ke peeche ka idea diya tha, aur Einstein ne use aage badhaya.
- Ise pehli baar **laboratory** mein **1995** mein banaya gaya, yaani idea aane ke kaafi saal baad.
- Isme gas ko absolute zero se sirf **bahut hi thode** upar tak thanda kiya jaata hai (Kelvin ke bahut chhote hisse jitna). Aisa thanda temperature dharti par apne aap kahin nahi milta, isliye ye state sirf khaas labs mein banti hai.

Isse zyada hum abhi nahi jaayenge: iske peeche ki physics aage ki badi classes ki baat hai. Bas yaad rakho ki matter sirf teen roop tak seemit nahi hai.

## Part C: Paanchon states ek saath

Ab tak jo states dekhi hain unhe ek table mein rakhte hain. Dhyan do ki "particle energy" ka matlab hai ki kan kitni tezi se hilte-dulte hain.

| State | Particles ki energy | Particles ka arrangement | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Solid** | Kam: sirf apni jagah par kaampte hain | Paas-paas, fixed aur regular | Barf, lakdi, loha |
| **Liquid** | Solid se zyada: ek doosre par phisalte hain | Paas-paas, par irregular | Paani, doodh, tel |
| **Gas** | Liquid se bhi zyada: tezi se idhar-udhar | Bahut door-door, random | Hawa, bhaap, LPG |
| **Plasma** | Sabse zyada: bahut tez, charged | Door-door, plus aur minus charged particles | Sooraj, bijli ki lakeer, neon sign |
| **BEC** | Bahut hi kam: lagbhag ruke hue | Bahut kam density, sab ek jaisa vyavhaar | Sirf laboratory mein, absolute zero ke paas |

Kuch cheezein table se nikalti hain: aam zindagi mein humein jo teen states dikhti hain, unke beech **solid se gas ki taraf** jaate hue particle energy badhti hai aur arrangement dheela hota jaata hai. Plasma us line ke bhi aage hai (bahut zyada energy), jabki BEC dusre kinare par (bahut kam energy).

## Part D: Kuch doubts saaf karte hain

### 1. "Kya aag (fire) matter ki koi state hai?"

Nahi. Aag ek **alag state nahi** hai. Jab kuch jalta hai (lakdi, mombatti, gas), to ek **chemical badlaav** hota hai aur bahut garam **chamakti hui gases** banti hain. Jo lau (flame) hum dekhte hain, wo mainly **bahut garam, chamakti gas** hai, aur uske sabse garam hisson mein thoda sa plasma bhi ho sakta hai. Par flame kisi nayi, apni alag state ka naam nahi hai: wo bas garam gases ki ek chamak hai. Aage ki class mein jalne (combustion) ka poora chemistry sikhenge.

### 2. "Kya har cheez teeno states mein mil sakti hai?"

Bahut si cheezein haan: paani solid (barf), liquid aur gas (bhaap) teeno roop leta hai, aur loha, sona bhi, bas unke liye bahut zyada garmi ya thand chahiye (loha lagbhag 1538 °C par pighalta hai). Par **sab** cheezein nahi: lakdi aur kaagaz ko garam karne par wo pighalte nahi, balki **jal jaate hain** ya toot jaate hain, yaani unka chemical badlaav ho jaata hai. Cheeni bhi aksar pighalne ke saath jalne lagti hai. Is liye "state badalna" tab hi hai jab **wahi cheez bani rahe**, sirf uske kan ka arrangement badle.

### 3. "Kya plasma aur BEC hamare ghar mein milte hain?"

Plasma ghar mein tubelight aur neon sign mein dikh jaata hai, aur bahar bijli ya sooraj mein. BEC ghar mein **nahi** milta: use banane ke liye bahut hi thandi, khaas laboratory chahiye.

## Part E: Jaanch: apne aap ko parkho

Chapter ke saare topics ko mila kar 7 chhote sawal. Pehle khud sochna, phir answer dekhna.

**Q1 (diffusion).** Ek garam kamre mein aur ek thande kamre mein dono mein ek ek agarbatti jalayi gayi. Kisme khushboo kamre ke door kone tak pehle pahunchegi, aur kyun?

**Q2 (compressibility).** Ek syringe (bina sui ke) mein hawa bhari hai, doosri mein paani. Dono ke piston ko dabate hain. Kaun zyada dab jaayegi aur kyun?

**Q3 (Kelvin).** (a) 27 °C ko Kelvin mein badlo. (b) 250 K ko °C mein badlo.

**Q4 (sublimation).** Almari mein rakhi naphthalene ki goliyan kuch hafton mein chhoti ho jaati hain, par nichhe koi paani jaisa liquid nahi dikhta. Is badlaav ka naam aur wajah batao.

**Q5 (evaporation).** Garmi mein jab tum paseene se bheege hote ho aur pankhe ke neeche baithte ho, to thanda kyun lagta hai?

**Q6 (particle energy).** Solid, gas, plasma aur BEC ko particle energy ke hisaab se kam se zyada ke order mein likho.

**Q7 (plasma aur BEC).** Neon sign ke andar kaun si state hai, aur BEC banane ke liye kis temperature ke paas jaana padta hai?

### Answers

**A1.** **Garam kamre mein** khushboo pehle pahunchegi. Garmi mein particles tez hilte hain, isliye ek chiz ke particles doosri chiz mein jaldi phaile (diffusion tez hota hai). Jaanch: temperature badhne par diffusion badhta hai, particles tez hilte hain to wo jaldi phailte hain ✅

**A2.** **Hawa wali syringe** zyada dabegi. Gas mein particles bahut door-door hote hain, to unke beech bahut khaali jagah hai jisme particles paas aa sakte hain. Paani mein particles pehle se paas-paas hain, isliye wo lagbhag nahi dabta. Jaanch: gas compressible hoti hai, liquid lagbhag nahi ✅

**A3.** Formula: K = °C + 273.
(a) 27 + 273 = **300 K**.
(b) 250 − 273 = **−23 °C**.
Jaanch: 300 − 273 = 27 ✅ aur −23 + 273 = 250 ✅

**A4.** Is badlaav ka naam **sublimation** hai: solid seedhe gas ban gaya, beech mein liquid nahi bana. Isliye goliyan chhoti hoti jaati hain aur paani jaisa kuch nahi dikhta. Jaanch: solid se gas, bina liquid ke = sublimation ✅ (Kapoor aur dry ice bhi aise hi hain).

**A5.** Paseena evaporate hota hai. Evaporate hone wale tez particles apne saath **garmi** le jaate hain, to sharir ki surface thandi ho jaati hai. Pankhe ki hawa evaporation ko aur tez kar deti hai, isliye aur zyada thanda lagta hai. Jaanch: evaporation = cooling ✅

**A6.** **BEC < solid < gas < plasma.** BEC mein kan lagbhag ruke hue hain (sabse kam energy), solid mein sirf kaampte hain, gas mein tez ghoomte hain, aur plasma mein sabse tez. (Liquid agar jodna ho to wo solid aur gas ke beech aayega.) Jaanch: ye order Part C ke table se match karta hai ✅

**A7.** Neon sign ke andar **plasma** hota hai (bijli se chamakti charged gas). BEC ke liye **absolute zero (0 K, lagbhag −273 °C) ke bahut hi paas** jaana padta hai. ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Plasma matlab khoon ka plasma"**

Yahan plasma ka matlab matter ki state hai: charged particles wali ionised gas. Khoon mein bhi "plasma" naam ka ek hissa hota hai, par wo bilkul alag cheez hai, yahan us se kuch lena-dena nahi.

**Galti 2: "Aag bhi ek state hai"**

Nahi. Flame chamakti hui garam gases hai, jo jalne ke chemical badlaav se bani hain. Ye alag state nahi hai.

**Galti 3: "BEC ka matlab bahut garam"**

Bilkul ulta. BEC **bahut thandi** state hai: absolute zero ke bahut paas. Plasma bahut garam hai.

**Galti 4: "BEC aur plasma roz dikhte hain"**

Plasma dikhta hai (bijli, neon sign), par BEC sirf khaas laboratory mein, bahut kam matra mein hoti hai.

**Galti 5: "Gas aur plasma same hain"**

Dono phailte hain, par plasma mein particles **charged** hote hain aur wo bijli guzaar sakta hai. Normal gas ke particles mein charge nahi hota.

## Quick Recap

- **Plasma:** bahut garam, charged particles wali **ionised gas**. Mile: sooraj aur tare, bijli ki lakeer, neon sign, tubelight. Universe ke dikhne wale matter ka zyada hissa plasma hai.
- **BEC:** bahut kam density wali gas ko **absolute zero (0 K, lagbhag −273 °C)** ke bahut paas thanda karne par bani state. Naam **Satyendra Nath Bose** aur **Albert Einstein** ke naam par. Pehli baar laboratory mein **1995** mein bani.
- Paanch states: solid, liquid, gas, plasma, BEC. Solid se gas tak energy badhti hai, plasma sabse zyada energy, BEC sabse kam.
- **Aag** koi alag state nahi hai: wo chamakti hui garam gases hai.
- Har cheez teeno states mein nahi jaati: lakdi, kaagaz jaise padaarth pighalne se pehle jal jaate hain.
- Chapter ka natija: matter particles ka bana hai, particles ke beech jagah hai, wo hamesha hilte hain, aur garmi ya thand se unka arrangement aur isliye state badalti hai.

## Practice Questions

1. What is plasma? Name three places where plasma is found.

2. Why is the Sun said to be made of plasma and not of ordinary gas?

3. What is a Bose-Einstein Condensate? After whom is it named, and when was it first made in a laboratory?

4. Compare plasma and ordinary gas in two points.

5. Is fire a separate state of matter? Explain in two or three lines.

6. Convert into Kelvin: (a) 0 °C, (b) 100 °C, (c) −40 °C. Then convert into °C: (d) 310 K, (e) 77 K.

7. Arrange solid, liquid, gas, plasma and BEC in increasing order of the energy of their particles.

8. A student says, "Camphor kept in an open dish becomes smaller but no liquid forms, so it must be evaporation." Is the student correct? Name the change and the difference from evaporation.

9. A wet cloth dries faster on a windy day than on a calm day. Give a reason using what you learnt about evaporation.

10. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) Plasma is a state of matter that has charged particles.
    (b) BEC is formed at a very high temperature.
    (c) Every substance can exist as solid, liquid and gas without changing into something else.
    (d) Neon signs and tubelights glow because of plasma-like glowing gas inside them.

### Answers

1. **Plasma** ek bahut garam, ionised gas hai jisme plus aur minus charged particles hote hain. Teen jagah: sooraj (aur tare), bijli ki lakeer, neon sign (ya tubelight).

2. Sooraj itna garam hai ki uske andar ka matter ionised ho chuka hai: particles charged hain. Normal gas mein particles par charge nahi hota. Isliye sooraj ko plasma kehte hain, normal gas nahi.

3. **BEC** matter ki wo state hai jo bahut kam density wali gas ko absolute zero ke bahut paas thanda karne par banti hai. Iska naam **Satyendra Nath Bose** aur **Albert Einstein** par hai. Ye pehli baar laboratory mein **1995** mein bani.

4. Plasma mein particles **charged** hote hain, gas mein nahi. Plasma **bijli guzaarta aur chamakta** hai, normal gas aam taur par nahi chamakti. (Dono mein particles door-door aur tez hote hain.)

5. Nahi. Aag koi alag state nahi hai. Jalne par chemical badlaav hota hai aur bahut garam chamakti hui gases banti hain; flame wahi chamakti garam gas hai (uske sabse garam hisse mein thoda plasma ho sakta hai).

6. K = °C + 273. (a) 0 + 273 = **273 K**. (b) 100 + 273 = **373 K**. (c) −40 + 273 = **233 K**. Ulta: °C = K − 273. (d) 310 − 273 = **37 °C**. (e) 77 − 273 = **−196 °C**.
   Jaanch: 273 − 273 = 0 ✅, 373 − 273 = 100 ✅, 233 − 273 = −40 ✅

7. **BEC < solid < liquid < gas < plasma.**

8. Student **galat** hai. Ye **sublimation** hai (solid seedhe gas). Evaporation sirf liquid ki surface se hota hai, jabki yahan solid hai aur beech mein liquid nahi banta.

9. Hawa tez ho to jo bhaap kapde ki surface ke paas bani hai wo door ud jaati hai, aur nayi bhaap aasani se ban sakti hai. Isliye evaporation tez hota hai aur kapda jaldi sookhta hai (wind ek factor hai).

10. (a) **True** ✅. Plasma mein charged particles (ions aur electrons) hote hain. (b) **False** ❌. BEC absolute zero ke bahut paas, bahut thande temperature par banti hai. (c) **False** ❌. Lakdi aur kaagaz jaise padaarth pighalne se pehle jal jaate hain, yaani chemical badlaav ho jaata hai. (d) **True** ✅. Unke andar bijli guzarne par gas chamakti hai.
