# Topic 1: Matter Kya Hai aur Particles se Kaise Bana Hai

Ye Class 9 Chemistry ka pehla topic hai, to piche koi chemistry topic nahi hai. Chemistry ki shuruaat ek simple sawal se hoti hai: **hamare aas-paas ki cheezein aakhir bani kis se hain?** Pen, paani, hawa, chai, mitti, ye sab kis cheez ke bane hain, aur us "cheez" ke andar jhaank kar dekhein to kya milta hai? ## Part A: Matter kise kehte hain?

Hamare aas-paas har cheez matter nahi hoti. Ek cheez ko **matter** tab kehte hain jab uske **do gun** ho:

1. Uska **mass** ho (usme "maal" ho, tarazu par tol sakein).
2. Wo **jagah (space)** ghere (uska volume ho).

> **Matter = wo har cheez jiska mass hai aur jo jagah gherti hai.**

Kuch examples dekho:

| Matter hai | Matter nahi hai |
|---|---|
| Pen, kitaab, mitti, paani | **Light (roshni)** |
| **Hawa** | **Sound (awaaz)** |
| Doodh, chai, cheeni, namak | **Heat (garmi)** |
| Mobile, cycle, tumhara sharir | **Pyaar, gussa, khushi (feelings)** |

Roshni, awaaz aur garmi **energy ke roop** hain. Unka na mass hai, na wo jagah gherti hain. Isi tarah, khushi ya gussa ko tarazu par tol nahi sakte, to wo bhi matter nahi hain.

### Hawa bhi matter hai: do safe activity

Hawa dikhti nahi, isliye ye maanna thoda mushkil lagta hai ki wo matter hai. Chalo ghar par hi saboot nikalte hain.

**Activity 1: Hawa jagah gherti hai.**
Ek badi bucket mein paani bharo. Ek sookhe tissue paper ko ek glass ki tali (andar ki sabse neeche wali satah) mein thoos do taaki wo gire nahi. Ab glass ko **seedha ulta** (muh neeche) rakhkar bucket ke paani mein dheere se dabao. Glass ko tedha mat karna.

Dekhoge ki **tissue paper geela nahi hua** (ya bahut kam geela hua). Kyun? Kyunki glass ke andar **hawa jagah ghere khadi hai**, aur paani us jagah mein ghus nahi paa raha. Agar tum glass ko dheere se tedha karo, to hawa **bulbule banke bahar** nikalti dikhegi, aur tab paani andar jaata hai.

**Activity 2: Hawa ka mass hota hai.**
Ek lambi scale ya patli chhadi lo, uske beech mein dhaaga baandhkar use latka do taaki wo **balance** mein rahe. Ab do ek jaise **gubbare** lo. Pehle dono khaali gubbare chhadi ke dono siron par baandho: chhadi balance mein rahegi. Phir ek gubbare mein hawa bharo, munh baandho aur use wapas usi jagah latka do. Ab wo taraf **neeche jhuk jaayegi**.

Matlab hawa bhare gubbare ka **mass zyada** hai, kyunki usme hawa ka mass bhi jud gaya hai. To hawa ka mass hai, aur wo jagah bhi gherti hai (gubbara phoolta hai). Isliye **hawa matter hai**.

## Part B: Matter chhote-chhote kanon se bana hai

Ab asli sawal: ek paani ka glass lo. Use aadha karo, phir aadha, phir aadha, bahut baar. Kya hoga? Kya ye bina ruke tootta hi jaayega, ya kahin ek aise "sabse chhote tukde" par rukega jise aur nahi todha ja sakta?

Scientists ne kayi tarah ke saboot dekhkar ye nikaala ki **matter chhote-chhote kanon (particles) se bana hai.** (Ye kan aage ke chapter "Atoms and Molecules" mein jaanenge: kabhi wo **atoms** hote hain, kabhi **molecules**. Abhi hum sirf unhe **particles** ya **kan** kahenge.)

Ye particles kaise hote hain, uske **chaar saboot** hain. Ek-ek karke dekhte hain.

## Part C: Saboot 1: Particles bahut hi chhote hote hain

### Activity: Dilution wali activity

Ye activity ghar par ya school lab mein ki ja sakti hai. Ek **potassium permanganate (KMnO₄)** ka **ek chhota sa kan (crystal)** lo (ye "Condy's crystals" naam se milta hai; ise haath ya muh mein na lagao, aur kisi bade ya teacher ki dekhrekh mein hi karo). Agar na mile, to **ek boond gaadhi ink** bhi chalegi.

**Kaise karna hai:**

1. Ek glass mein lagbhag 100 mL paani lo aur usme crystal ya ink ki boond daalo. Hilao. Paani **gaadhe baingani/gulabi** rang ka ho gaya.
2. Is gaadhe paani mein se **1 mL** (ek chammach se thoda sa, ya dropper se) nikalo aur **9 mL saaf paani** mein milao. Total = 10 mL. Rang **halka** ho gaya, par abhi bhi dikh raha hai.
3. Is halke paani mein se phir **1 mL** nikalo aur phir **9 mL saaf paani** mein milao. Rang aur halka.
4. Aise hi **5 se 8 baar** karo (har baar nayi test tube ya glass mein).

**Dekha kya?** Har baar rang halka hota gaya, par **8 baar ke baad bhi** (bahut halke) rang ka asar dikh raha tha, jabki ek chhote se crystal se shuru kiya tha.

**Kyun?** Iska matlab ek **bahut chhote se crystal mein bhi bahut-bahut zyada particles** hote hain, aur har particle itna chhota hai ki thode se particles bhi paani ke ek poore glass ko rang de dete hain.

### Dilution ka reasoning example

**Example 1 (kitni baar dilute hua?):** Har step mein 1 mL ghol ko 9 mL paani mein milaya jaata hai (total 10 mL). Matlab har step mein ghol **10 guna halka** hota hai. Agar ye activity **n baar** ki jaaye, to ghol kitna guna halka hoga?

Step 1 ke baad: 10 guna halka.
Step 2 ke baad: 10 × 10 = **100** guna halka.
Step 3 ke baad: 10 × 10 × 10 = **1000** guna.
Is tarah **n steps ke baad: 10ⁿ guna halka.**

Maan lo shuru mein ghol ki "gaadhapan" ki **ek value 100 units** thi. To:

| Step | Gaadhapan (units) | Shuruaat se kitna guna halka |
|---|---|---|
| 0 (shuruaat) | 100 | 1 guna |
| 1 | 10 | 10 guna |
| 2 | 1 | 100 guna |
| 3 | 0.1 | 1000 guna |
| 4 | 0.01 | 10000 guna |
| 5 | 0.001 | 100000 guna |

**Jaanch:** Har step mein gaadhapan ko 10 se **divide** kiya. 100 ÷ 10 = 10; 10 ÷ 10 = 1; 1 ÷ 10 = 0.1; 0.1 ÷ 10 = 0.01; 0.01 ÷ 10 = 0.001. Aur 5 steps ke baad guna = 10⁵ = 100000, aur 100 ÷ 100000 = 0.001 ✅

Dhyan do: **5 steps ke baad ghol shuru se 1 lakh guna halka** ho chuka tha, phir bhi hum rang dekh paa rahe the. Iska matlab ek-ek particle itna chhota hai ki bahut kam particles hi paani ko rang de dete hain.

Ek aur dekho: agar ek baar mein **1 mL** ghol ko **99 mL** paani mein milao (total 100 mL), to ek hi step mein **100 guna halka** hota hai. Do aise steps ke baad 100 × 100 = **10000 guna** halka.

### Cheeni, namak bhi saboot hain

Ek chammach **namak ya cheeni** ek glass paani mein ghol do. Namak ke dane **gayab** ho gaye. Paani poora meetha ya khaara ho gaya, aur tum ek bhi dana nahi dekh sakte. Iska matlab namak ke dane tootkar itne chhote kanon mein bat gaye ki aankh se dikhna band ho gaye, par wo **hain wahin**: paani ab meetha ya khaara ho gaya hai. (Cheeni-namak khane ki cheezein hain, isliye ye example safe hai; ghar par kisi chemical ko kabhi chakhna nahi.)

```
  Crystal (ek chhota tukda)        Paani mein ghulne ke baad
  
      +---------+                   o  o   o    o   o
      | o o o o |                  o    o    o  o     o
      | o o o o |    ghulne par      o   o     o   o
      | o o o o |    ----------->  o    o   o      o   o
      +---------+                    o     o   o
   (dikhta hai)                    (kan sab taraf phail gaye,
                                    ab tukda nahi dikhta)
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Ek crystal paani mein ghulta hai to uske particles toot kar poore paani mein phail jaate hain. Particles itne chhote hain ki ab dikhte nahi, par rang ya swaad se unka pata chalta hai.

## Part D: Saboot 2: Particles ke beech mein khaali jagah (space) hoti hai

Agar matter ke kan ek doosre se bilkul chipke hote, to kuch bhi **doosri cheez usme mil nahi sakti** thi. Par hum dekhte hain ki cheezein aapas mein mil jaati hain. Iska matlab kanon ke beech **khaali jagah** hoti hai. Ek-ek saboot dekhte hain.

### Saboot 2a: Cheeni ya namak ghulne par paani ka level

Ek glass mein pehle se 100 mL paani lo aur level par nishaan lagao. Ab usme ek chammach **cheeni** daalo aur hilao, jab tak sab ghul na jaaye. Dekho ki paani ka **level lagbhag waisa hi** raha, ya bahut halka sa badha. Jabki cheeni ka chammach (jo apni jagah gherta tha) paani mein poori tarah gayab ho gaya.

**Kyun?** Kyunki paani ke kanon ke **beech mein khaali jagah** thi. Cheeni ke kan wahin **ghus gaye**, jaise ek bhare hue bartan (jisme bahut kam jagah dikhti hai) mein aur chhoti cheezein ghus jaati hain.

**Ek aam analogy:** Ek dibbe mein **marbles** bhar do. Dibba bhar gaya lagta hai. Ab usme **rait (sand)** daalo to rait marbles ke beech ki **khaali jagah** mein chali jaati hai aur dibba utna hi bhara dikhta hai. Isi tarah, paani ke kanon ke beech ki jagah mein cheeni ke kan chale jaate hain.

### Saboot 2b: Syringe mein hawa dabana

Ek khaali **plastic syringe** lo (bina sui ke; dawai ki dukaan par aasani se milti hai). Piston ko aadha bahar kheencho taaki andar hawa bhar jaaye. Ab nozzle (aage ka chhota chhed) par **ungli rakhkar band** kar do aur piston ko dabao.

Tum dekhoge ki piston **kaafi neeche tak jaata hai**: hawa **dab gayi**. Matlab hawa ke kanon ke beech **bahut jagah thi**, jo dabaane par kam ho gayi.

Ab wahi syringe **paani se bharo** (nozzle band karke) aur piston dabao. Is baar piston **bahut kam** hilta hai, aur dabana mushkil lagta hai. Matlab hawa ki tulna mein paani ke kanon ke beech **kam jagah** hai.

> Is topic mein hum sirf itna dekh rahe hain ki hawa mein jagah zyada hai aur paani mein kam. "Kyun" aur teeno roop (solid, liquid, gas) ki poori tulna agla topic hai.

### Saboot 2c: Do liquids ka mix hona (sirf jaanne ke liye)

Lab mein ye dekha gaya hai ki agar **50 mL paani** aur **50 mL alcohol** milate hain to total volume **100 mL se thoda kam** (lagbhag 97 mL) aata hai. (Ye activity sirf teacher ki dekhrekh mein lab mein hoti hai.) Isse bhi yahi pata chalta hai ki ek liquid ke kanon ke beech jagah hoti hai jisme doosre liquid ke kan **ghus jaate hain**.

```
  Pehle                          Mix karne ke baad

  Paani ke kan      Alcohol           Dono mix
   o  o  o           x   x           o x  o  x o
   o  o  o           x   x           x o  x  o x
   o  o  o           x   x           o x  o  x o

   (beech mein      (beech mein      (ek ke kan doosre ke kanon
    jagah)           jagah)           ke beech ki jagah mein ghus gaye,
                                      total volume thoda kam)
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Paani aur alcohol mix karne par ek ke kan doosre ke kanon ke beech ki khaali jagah mein ghus jaate hain, isliye total volume thoda kam aata hai.

## Part E: Saboot 3: Particles hamesha chalte (harakat karte) rehte hain

Kisi bhi cheez mein particles **kabhi ruk kar baithe nahi hote**; wo hamesha idhar-udhar hilte-dulte rehte hain. Iska pehla saboot hume kahani mein hi mil gaya tha.

### Diffusion: agarbatti ki khushboo

Mr. Siddiqui ne Reshma ko samjhaya, "Jab agarbatti jalti hai, to uske **kuch particles hawa mein mil jaate hain**. Hawa ke kan bhi harkat kar rahe hain, aur agarbatti ke particles bhi. Wo sab idhar-udhar tezi se ghoomte hain, ek doosre se takrate hain, aur dheere-dheere **poore kamre mein phail jaate hain**. Jab wo particles tumhari naak tak pahunchte hain, tumhe khushboo aati hai."

**Reshma:** "Par sir, agar wo tezi se chalte hain to khushboo mujhe turant kyun nahi aayi? Der kyun lagi?"

**Sir:** "Kyunki hawa mein khushboo ke kan ek seedhi line mein nahi jaate. Har kadam par hawa ke **bahut saare doosre kanon se takrate** hain aur idhar-udhar bhatakte hain. Isliye poore kamre tak pahunchne mein der lagti hai."

Is tarah **do alag cheezon ke kanon ka apne aap aapas mein mil jaana** hi **diffusion** kehlata hai.

> **Diffusion = do cheezon ke kan, bina hilaye-dulaye, apne aap harkat karke aapas mein mil jaate hain.**

```
  Pehle (agarbatti ke kan ek kone mein)         Kuch der baad

  +---------------------------+              +---------------------------+
  | *                         |              |  *   .  o  *  .  o   .    |
  | * *                       |              |   . *  o   .  *  .  o  .  |
  | * * *                     |   ------->   |  o  .  *  o  .  * .    o  |
  |                           |              |   .  o   * . o   .  *  .  |
  +---------------------------+              +---------------------------+
   (khushboo ke kan * ek jagah)               (kan poore kamre mein phail gaye;
                                               . aur o hawa ke kan hain)
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Diffusion: agarbatti ke kan (*) hawa ke kanon (. aur o) ke beech harkat karte hue poore kamre mein phail jaate hain.

### Diffusion paani mein bhi hota hai

Ek glass mein **bina hilaye** paani rakho aur use ek jagah par rehne do. Ab ek **boond ink** (ya KMnO₄ ka ek crystal) dheere se paani ke andar chhodo. Hilao mat.

Dekhoge ki ink ya rang **apne aap, dheere-dheere** poore paani mein phail jaata hai, bina kisi ke hilaye. Ye diffusion hi hai, par paani mein, aur ye **hawa se bahut dheema** hota hai.

> Ye dikhata hai ki particles sirf hawa mein nahi, **liquid mein bhi** harkat karte hain, bas wahan unke ghoomne ki raftaar kam hoti hai.

### Garmi badhane par diffusion tez ho jaata hai

Ek hi tarah ke do glass lo. Ek mein **thanda paani** (fridge ke paani ya barf wale paani se) aur doosre mein **garam paani** (bahut garam nahi, jaise chai thodi thandi hone ke baad, jisse haath na jale). Dono mein ek hi saath **ek-ek boond ink** daalo, aur dono mein se kisi ko hilao mat.

**Dekhoge:** **Garam paani mein** ink **jaldi** phail jaata hai, aur thande paani mein **der se**.

**Kyun?** Garam paani ke kan **zyada tezi se** harkat karte hain, isliye ink ke kan bhi jaldi phail jaate hain. Thande paani ke kan dheeme chalte hain, isliye ink der se phailta hai.

> **Jitna garam, utni tez harkat, utna tez diffusion.**

### Diffusion ki raftaar: reasoning example

**Example 2 (garam vs thanda paani):** Ek experiment mein ink ki boond ko **thande paani** mein poora phailne mein **180 second** lage. Usi size ke glass mein, usi ink ki boond ko **garam paani** mein poora phailne mein **60 second** lage. Diffusion garam paani mein kitna guna tez hua? Aur agar thande paani mein 6 cm door tak ink phailne mein 180 s lagta hai, to garam paani mein 60 s mein ink kitni door pahunchega, agar ink usi tarah chal raha ho?

(Ye numbers is example ke liye diye gaye hain; asli reading glass, temperature aur ink ke hisaab se badalti hai.)

- Thande paani mein samay = 180 s, garam paani mein = 60 s
- Kitna guna tez = 180 ÷ 60 = **3 guna tez**

Doosre sawal ke liye: garam paani mein ink ko jitna rasta 60 s mein tay karna hai, wo thande paani mein 180 s mein tay hota hai (6 cm). Dono mein **same duri 6 cm** hai. Matlab garam paani mein 6 cm ke liye **sirf 60 s** lagta hai, jabki thande mein 180 s.

**Jaanch:** Duri/samay se raftaar nikalo. Thanda: 6 cm ÷ 180 s = **0.033 cm/s**. Garam: 6 cm ÷ 60 s = **0.1 cm/s**. 0.1 ÷ 0.033 ≈ **3** guna ✅. Matlab garam paani mein diffusion ki average raftaar thande se 3 guna zyada thi.

Dhyan do: garam paani mein ink ka rasta **kam samay mein** tay hua, isliye samay kam hai par raftaar **zyada** hai.

## Part F: Saboot 4: Particles ek doosre ko aakarshan (attraction) se kheenchte hain

Agar particles harkat karte rehte hain, to wo bikhar kyun nahi jaate? Lohe ki keel ke kan bikhar kyun nahi jaate? Kyunki particles ke beech ek **kheenchav (aakarshan)** hota hai jo unhe ek saath pakde rakhta hai. Ye kheenchav **kuch cheezon mein zyada** aur **kuch mein kam** hota hai.

### Kheenchav ki taakat ek jaisi nahi hoti

Teen cheezein lo: ek **lohe ki keel**, ek **chalk**, aur ek glass **paani**.

| Cheez | Todna kaisa lagta hai | Matlab kheenchav |
|---|---|---|
| **Lohe ki keel** | Haath se todna lagbhag **namumkin** | Kheenchav bahut **zyada** |
| **Chalk** | Haath se aasaani se toot jaata hai | Kheenchav **kam** |
| **Paani** | Ungli se aasaani se alag kar do, bahta hai | Kheenchav aur bhi **kam** (par hai) |

### Activity: Steel ka taar, chalk aur rubber band

1. **Steel ka patla taar** (jaise cycle ka bahut patla ya tool-kit ka taar) lo aur use dono haathon se kheencho ya mod-mod kar todne ki koshish karo. Ye **mushkil se** tootega. (Zyada na kheencho, chot na lage.) Taar mein particles ka kheenchav **bahut zyada** hai.
2. **Chalk** ka tukda lo aur use dono haathon se modkar todo. Wo **aasaani se** toot jaata hai. Kheenchav kam hai.
3. **Rubber band** ko kheencho. Wo lambi hoti hai, aur chhodne par wapas aa jaati hai. Ye bhi kheenchav ki wajah se hota hai: particles ek doosre ko pakde rehte hain.

### Paani ki boondein judti kyun hain?

Ek chikni plate par paani ki **do chhoti boondein** paas-paas rakho aur ek ko dheere se doosri ke paas le jaao. Dono **mil kar ek badi boond** ban jaati hain. Ye isliye hota hai kyunki paani ke particles ek doosre ko **kheenchte** hain.

```
   Pehle                    Judne ke baad

    ( o o )   ( o o )          ( o o o o )
    ( o o )   ( o o )          ( o o o o )

   do alag boondein          ek badi boond
```

> **Figure 1.4** – Paani ki do chhoti boondein paas aane par particles ke aapas ke kheenchav ki wajah se ek badi boond bana leti hain.

Dhyan do: ye kheenchav (aakarshan) hi particles ko ek saath **pakde rakhta hai**, aur ye **cheez se cheez badalta hai**. Ye hi wajah hai ki koi cheez mazboot hai (loha), koi naram (rubber), aur koi bahne wali (paani). Is par aage ki classes mein aur detail aayegi; abhi sirf itna yaad rakho: **kheenchav hai, aur sab mein ek jaisa nahi**.

## Part G: Chaaron saboot ek saath

Ab Mr. Siddiqui ne Reshma se kaha: "Ab tumhe agarbatti ka sawal solve karna hai. Batao, uski khushboo doosre kone tak kaise pahunchi?"

Reshma ne socha aur boli: "Sir, hawa matter hai, uske kan hain. Un kanon ke beech **jagah** hai, to agarbatti ke kan usme ghus sakte hain. Sab kan **harkat** karte rehte hain, isliye agarbatti ke kan phailte hain. Aur wo **bahut chhote** hain, isliye hume dikhte nahi, par naak se pata chal jaata hai."

Sir ne taali bajayi, "Shabaash! Tumne teen saboot to ek hi sawal mein laga diye."

| Saboot | Kya dikhata hai | Humari activity |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Particles **bahut chhote** hain | Dilution: 5 steps ke baad bhi rang |
| 2 | Particles ke beech **jagah** hai | Cheeni ghulna, syringe |
| 3 | Particles **harkat** karte hain | Agarbatti, ink, garam vs thanda |
| 4 | Particles ek doosre ko **kheenchte** hain | Keel, chalk, paani ki boond |

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Hawa matter nahi hai kyunki dikhti nahi."**

Nahi. Dikhna matter hone ki shart nahi hai. Hawa ka mass hai (gubbare wali activity) aur wo jagah gherti hai (ulte glass wali activity). Isliye hawa matter hai.

**Galti 2: "Roshni aur awaaz bhi matter hain."**

Nahi. Light, sound aur heat **energy** ke roop hain. Unka mass nahi hota aur wo jagah nahi gherti. Matter sirf wo hai jiska mass aur volume dono ho.

**Galti 3: "Cheeni paani mein ghulkar gayab ho jaati hai."**

Gayab nahi hoti. Wo chhote kanon mein tootkar paani ke kanon ke beech ki jagah mein chali jaati hai. Isliye paani meetha ho jaata hai (aur agar paani ko sukha lein to cheeni wapas mil jaati hai).

**Galti 4: "Diffusion tab hota hai jab hum hilaye-dulaye."**

Nahi. Diffusion apne aap hota hai, kanon ki apni harkat se. Hilane se mixing aur jaldi ho sakti hai, par diffusion ke liye hilana zaroori nahi hai.

**Galti 5: "Paani 'bhara hua' dikhta hai, isliye uske kanon ke beech koi jagah nahi hoti."**

Cheeni ka paani mein ghul jaana aur level ka lagbhag na badhna dikhata hai ki jagah hoti hai, bas wo bahut chhoti hai.

**Galti 6: "Dilution mein 10 guna ka matlab 10 mL paani milana hai."**

Dhyan do: 1 mL ghol ko 9 mL paani mein milane par **total 10 mL** hota hai, aur ghol **10 guna halka** hota hai. Agar 1 mL ghol mein 10 mL paani milaoge, to total 11 mL hoga aur ghol **11 guna** halka hoga, 10 guna nahi.

## Quick Recap

- **Matter** = jiska **mass** hai aur jo **jagah** gherti hai. Hawa matter hai. Light, sound, heat aur feelings matter nahi hain.
- Ulte glass wali activity se hawa **jagah gherti** hai, gubbare wali activity se hawa ka **mass** hota hai.
- Matter **particles (kan)** se bana hai. Ye kan atoms ya molecules ho sakte hain (aage ke chapter "Atoms and Molecules" mein).
- **Saboot 1:** Particles bahut chhote hain: ek crystal ya ink ki boond 5-8 baar 10 guna dilute karne par bhi rang deti hai. n steps mein ghol 10ⁿ guna halka hota hai.
- **Saboot 2:** Particles ke beech jagah hai: cheeni ghulne par paani ka level lagbhag nahi badhta, syringe mein hawa dab jaati hai, do liquids mix hote hain.
- **Saboot 3:** Particles hamesha harkat karte hain: diffusion (agarbatti, ink). Garmi badhane par harkat tez hoti hai, isliye diffusion tez hota hai (hamare example mein 3 guna).
- **Saboot 4:** Particles ke beech aakarshan hota hai: lohe ki keel, chalk aur paani mein alag-alag taakat. Paani ki boondein judti hain.
- Reshma ki agarbatti ki khushboo ka raaz: hawa ke kanon ke beech jagah + harkat + chhote kan = diffusion.

## Practice Questions

1. Define matter. Which of these are matter and which are not: sound, an iron nail, air, heat, a feeling of joy, milk?

2. Describe a simple activity to show that air takes up space.

3. A solution is diluted by taking 1 mL of it and mixing with 9 mL of water. This is repeated 4 times in total. By what factor is the original solution diluted? If its starting strength was 200 units, what is its strength at the end?

4. 2 mL of a dye solution is mixed with 98 mL of water. By what factor has the solution been diluted?

5. When sugar dissolves in water, the water level hardly rises. What does this tell us about the particles of water? Give one more activity that shows the same idea.

6. In a syringe, air can be compressed easily but water cannot be compressed much. What does this suggest?

7. A drop of ink takes 240 s to spread through a glass of cold water and 80 s through a glass of hot water. How many times faster is diffusion in hot water? What does this tell you about the particles?

8. Explain why you can smell a freshly cooked sabzi from another room.

9. Why does a steel wire not break easily, while a piece of chalk does? What property of particles is being shown?

10. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) Light is matter because it takes space.
    (b) Diffusion happens only in gases.
    (c) Particles of matter are constantly moving.
    (d) Cold water shows faster diffusion than hot water.

### Answers

1. **Matter** wo hai jiska **mass** hota hai aur jo **jagah** gherta hai. **Matter hain:** iron nail, air, milk. **Matter nahi hain:** sound, heat, feeling of joy (ye sab energy ya feelings hain, inka mass aur volume nahi hota).

2. Ek ulta glass lo, uske andar sookha tissue paper thoos do, aur glass ko seedha ulta karke paani se bhari bucket mein dabao. Tissue geela nahi hota, kyunki glass ke andar hawa jagah gherti hai aur paani ko andar nahi aane deti. Isse pata chalta hai ki **hawa jagah gherti hai**.

3. Har step mein 10 guna dilution. 4 steps mein 10⁴ = **10000 guna**. Ant mein strength = 200 ÷ 10000 = **0.02 units**.
   Jaanch: 200 ÷ 10 = 20; 20 ÷ 10 = 2; 2 ÷ 10 = 0.2; 0.2 ÷ 10 = 0.02 ✅

4. Total volume = 2 mL + 98 mL = 100 mL. Dilution = 100 ÷ 2 = **50 guna**.
   Jaanch: 2 mL ka 50 guna = 100 mL ✅

5. Paani ke kanon ke beech **khaali jagah** hoti hai, jisme cheeni ke kan ghus jaate hain, isliye level lagbhag nahi badhta. Doosri activity: syringe mein hawa dabana, ya paani aur alcohol mix karke volume dekhna (volume thoda kam aata hai).

6. Hawa ke kanon ke beech **bahut zyada jagah** hai, isliye dabane par kan paas aa jaate hain. Paani ke kanon ke beech **kam jagah** hai, isliye wo kam dabta hai.

7. Garam paani mein diffusion = 240 ÷ 80 = **3 guna tez**.
   Jaanch: 80 × 3 = 240 ✅. Matlab garam paani mein kan zyada tez harkat karte hain, isliye ink jaldi phailta hai.

8. Sabzi ke kuch particles hawa mein mil jaate hain aur **diffusion** se harkat karte hue doosre kamre tak pahunch jaate hain. Jab wo naak tak aate hain, khushboo aati hai.

9. Steel ke taar mein particles ke beech aakarshan (kheenchav) **bahut zyada** hai, isliye wo aasaani se nahi tootta. Chalk mein kheenchav kam hai, isliye wo toot jaata hai. Ye dikhata hai ki **particles ek doosre ko kheenchte hain, aur ye kheenchav alag cheezon mein alag hota hai.**

10. (a) **False** ❌. Light energy hai, uska mass nahi hota aur wo jagah nahi gherti, isliye wo matter nahi hai. (b) **False** ❌. Diffusion liquids mein bhi hota hai (ink paani mein), bas dheere. (c) **True** ✅. (d) **False** ❌. Garam paani mein diffusion tez hota hai, thande mein dheema.
