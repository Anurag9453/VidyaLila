# Topic 2: Mendeleev Ka Periodic Table

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki tatvon ko sajaane ki pehli koshishein kaise hui. Dobereiner ne teen-teen ke triads banaye (Li, Na, K jaise), par aise triads bahut kam mile. Newlands ne tatvon ko mass ke order mein likha aur dekha ki har aathvan tatva pehle jaisa lagta hai (sangeet ke octave jaisa), par ye sirf calcium tak chala aur phir bemel tatva ek saath thoons diye gaye. Ek baat seekhi thi: **achhi arrangement sirf sajati nahi, aage ki baat bhi bata deti hai (predict karti hai).** Aaj ki kahani ek aise vaigyanik ki hai jiska chart sirf sajaata hi nahi tha, balki **khaali jagah chhod kar aane wale tatvon ke baare mein pehle se bata deta tha**, aur baad mein wo baatein sach nikli.

## Phate hue calendar ka ek missing din

Mr. Tamhane ke bhanje Reyaan ne puraane kagzon ke dher mein se ek **phata hua calendar ka panna** nikaala. Panna November 2026 ka tha, aur uski ek tarikh ka tukda gayab tha: **18 tarikh**. Reyaan ne soch kar kaha, "Mama, mujhe pata hai 18 tarikh ko kaun sa din hoga."

Mr. Tamhane ne bhaunh uthayi. "Kaise? Tarikh to phat gayi hai."

Reyaan ne panna dikhaya. "Dekhiye, 17 tarikh **Tuesday** hai aur 19 tarikh **Thursday**. Beech wala din Tuesday aur Thursday ke beech hi aayega: **Wednesday**. Aur dekho, Wednesday ke column mein 4, 11, 25 tarikh likhi hain, to 18 bhi usi column mein hi baithega." Mr. Tamhane ne hans kar kaha, "Shabaash! Tumne calendar ka **pattern** pakda: har hafte ke din ek hi column mein, aur ek khaali jagah bhi bata deti hai ki wahan kya aana chahiye."

Ab sochiye ki koi tatvon ka aisa calendar bana de, jisme kuch jagah **khaali** ho, aur wo kahe, "Is khaali jagah mein ek abhi tak na mila hua tatva hona chahiye, aur uske gun ye honge". Aur kuch hi saalon mein sach mein wo tatva mil jaaye! Aisa hi hua tha, aur ye idea Russia ke vaigyanik **Dmitri Mendeleev** ka tha. Chalo, dekhte hain unhone kya kiya.

## Part A: Mendeleev ka idea (1869)

**Dmitri Mendeleev** (Russia) ke paas bhi wahi problem thi jo Newlands ke paas thi: 60 se zyada tatva mil chuke the (us samay tak), aur unke gun ratne mushkil the. Unhone **1869** mein ek table banayi. Unke idea ke teen mukhya hisse the:

1. Tatvon ko **atomic mass ke badhte order** mein likho (lagbhag, ye aage dekhenge).
2. Jab bhi koi tatva pichhle tatva jaisa lage, use **neeche ki nayi row** mein, pichhle ke theek neeche rakho. Is tarah **milte-julte gun wale tatva ek hi vertical column (group)** mein aate hain, aur har horizontal row ek **period** hai.
3. **Gun ko mass se zyada important maano.** Agar mass ke order se koi tatva galat jagah pad raha ho, to uske gun dekho aur use wahan rakho jahan gun mel khayein, chahe mass ka order thoda ulta ho jaaye. Aur agar kisi group mein koi tatva fit nahi baithta, to wahan **khaali jagah chhod do**: wo tatva abhi mila nahi hai.

Mendeleev ke time mein tatvon ke gun jaanne ka ek mazboot tareeka **oxide ka formula** tha. Oxide matlab tatva ka oxygen ke saath bana compound (Chapter 3 aur 6 mein dekha tha). Dekho kya pattern mila (ye sab hum jaante hain, valency ka asar):

| Group | I | II | III | IV |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Example tatva | Na | Mg | Al | Si |
| Valency | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| Oxide ka formula | Na₂O | MgO | Al₂O₃ | SiO₂ |

Jaanch: Na₂O mein 2 Na (har ek valency 1: total 2) aur 1 O (valency 2): 2 = 2 ✅. MgO mein 2 = 2 ✅. Al₂O₃ mein 2 × 3 = 6 aur 3 × 2 = 6 ✅. SiO₂ mein 4 aur 2 × 2 = 4 ✅.

Ye dekhne mein aaya ki **group ka number badhta hai to oxide mein metal ki valency badhti jaati hai**: Mendeleev ke chart mein group number lagbhag us tatva ki oxide wali valency ke barabar tha. Isi se naye tatvon ke formula ka bhi andaaza lag sakta tha (aage dekhenge).

**Mendeleev ka periodic law:** *"Tatvon ke gun unke atomic mass ke periodic function hain."* Matlab: jab tatvon ko mass ke order mein rakho, to milte-julte gun **ek tay doori ke baad baar-baar** lautte hain, bilkul calendar ke din jaisa.

### Ek chhota Mendeleev-style table

Neeche Mendeleev ke chart ka ek hissa hai (aaj ke andaaz mein rows mein, har tatva ke saath rounded atomic mass). **?** ka matlab **khaali jagah**, jiske liye Mendeleev ne tatva ka andaaza lagaya.

```
 Group:   I        II       III      IV       V        VI       VII

 Row 3:   Na 23    Mg 24    Al 27    Si 28    P 31     S 32     Cl 35.5

 Row 4:   K 39     Ca 40    ? (44)   Ti 48    V 51     Cr 52    Mn 55

 Row 5:   Cu 63.5  Zn 65    ? (68)   ? (72)   As 75    Se 79    Br 80
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Mendeleev ke table ka chhota hissa (3 rows, groups I se VII). Har column ke tatva milte-julte hain, jaise Na, K, Cu valency 1 wale hain aur Cl, Br halogen hain. **?** wali teen jagahein khaali hain: Ca aur Ti ke beech (mass lagbhag 44), aur Zn ke baad do jagahein (mass lagbhag 68 aur 72). Group III ki do khaali jagahein (44 aur 68) aur group IV ki ek (72): har **?** ka matlab hai "yahan ek abhi tak na mila hua tatva aana chahiye."

Dhyan do: Mendeleev ne gap tab chhoda jab uske aage-peeche ke tatvon ke bich mass ka antar bahut bada tha. Jaise Ca (40) ke baad agla tatva Ti (48) hai, antar 8. Beech mein ek tatva 44 ke aas-paas **hona chahiye** (40 aur 48 ka beech: (40 + 48) ÷ 2 = 44 ✅). Agar saare tatva mil chuke hote to aisa bada antar na hota.

## Part B: Teen bhavishyavaniyan: eka-boron, eka-aluminium, eka-silicon

Mendeleev ne un khaali jagahon ke liye sirf ye nahi kaha ki "yahan kuch aayega". Unhone **likh kar bataya** ki us tatva ka atomic mass, density, aur uske oxide ka formula kya hoga. Tatva ke naam unhone aisa diya: "**eka**" + upar ke tatva ka naam.

Eka-aluminium ke atomic mass ka andaaza kaise lagta hai? Ek saral tareeka (Mendeleev ne isse zyada jaankari bhi use ki): khaali jagah ke **upar aur neeche** ke tatvon ka aur **left aur right** ke tatvon ka average dekho.

- Upar-neeche: Al (27) aur In (indium, lagbhag 114.8): (27 + 114.8) ÷ 2 = **70.9**.
- Left-right: Zn (65) aur As (75): (65 + 75) ÷ 2 = **70**.

Dono average **lagbhag 70 se 71** aate hain, jo Mendeleev ke diye hue andaaze **68** ke paas hai. (Ye simple average ka tareeka hai, isse poori sachchai nahi aati, par dikhata hai ki andaaza hawa mein tir nahi tha, pattern par tika tha.)

### Predicted versus real

| Tatva | Mendeleev ka andaaza (mass) | Baad mein mila | Kab mila | Asli mass | Antar |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Eka-aluminium | lagbhag 68 | Gallium (Ga) | 1875 | 69.7 | 1.7 |
| Eka-boron | lagbhag 44 | Scandium (Sc) | 1879 | 45.0 | 1.0 |
| Eka-silicon | lagbhag 72 | Germanium (Ge) | 1886 | 72.6 | 0.6 |

Jaanch: 69.7 − 68 = 1.7 ✅. 45.0 − 44 = 1.0 ✅. 72.6 − 72 = 0.6 ✅. Yani teeno predictions asli mass se sirf **0.6 se 1.7** door thi. Germanium mein ye antar 72.6 ka sirf lagbhag **0.8%** hai ((0.6 ÷ 72.6) × 100 ≈ 0.83) aur gallium mein lagbhag **2.4%** ((1.7 ÷ 69.7) × 100 ≈ 2.44).

### Eka-aluminium aur gallium: haath mein pighalne wali dhaatu

Eka-aluminium aur gallium ko saath-saath dekho:

```
                   Mendeleev ki bhavishyavani     Baad mein mila gallium (1875)
                   (eka-aluminium, 1871 ke aas-paas)

 Atomic mass       lagbhag 68                     69.7
 Density           lagbhag 5.9 g/cm³              lagbhag 5.9 g/cm³
 Gun               kam temperature par pighlegi   pighalne ka bindu 29.8 °C
 Oxide ka formula  Ea₂O₃                          Ga₂O₃
                   (group III: valency 3)         (valency 3)
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Eka-aluminium (Mendeleev ki prediction) aur gallium (asli tatva) ka aamne-saamne. Mass 68 ke samne 69.7; density lagbhag barabar; aur oxide ka formula same pattern (valency 3, to X₂O₃). Ea Mendeleev ke eka-aluminium ka sirf class mein likha shorthand hai, asli symbol nahi.

Kahani ye hai ki gallium ko **1875 mein France ke vaigyanik Lecoq de Boisbaudran** ne khoja. Ye tatva Mendeleev ki prediction ke kuch hi saal baad mila, aur uske gun (mass, density, oxide ka formula) bataye hue gunon se bahut milte the. Is kisse ke vistaar alag-alag jagah thoda alag sunaye jaate hain, isliye hum sirf pakki baat rakhte hain: **prediction pehle likhi gayi, tatva baad mein mila, aur gun mel khaye.**

**Gallium ki sabse maze ki baat:** iska pighalne ka bindu sirf **29.8 °C** hai. Hamare sharir ka taapman lagbhag **37 °C** hai. 37 − 29.8 = **7.2 °C** zyada. Isliye agar gallium ka chhota tukda haath mein rakho, to wo haath ki garmi se **pighalne lagta hai**, aur garmiyon mein hamare kai shehron ke kamre ka taapman (35 °C ke aas-paas) bhi ise pighla sakta hai. Ye sirf **school lab mein teacher ke dikhane par dekhne wali** cheez hai: ghar par isko khud na khareedo, na chhedo. Ye tatva **aluminium ke theek neeche** wale group mein aata hai, isliye uski valency aur oxide ka formula bhi Al jaisa hai: Ga₂O₃ (Al₂O₃ jaisa).

### Eka-silicon aur germanium

Eka-silicon (group IV, Si ke neeche) ke liye Mendeleev ne mass lagbhag 72, density lagbhag 5.5 g/cm³ aur oxide **EsO₂** (Si ke SiO₂ jaisa) bataya tha. **Germanium** 1886 mein mila: mass **72.6**, density lagbhag 5.3 g/cm³, oxide **GeO₂**. Group IV mein valency 4 hoti hai, to oxide XO₂ jaisa hi banta hai: 4 = 2 × 2 ✅. Prediction kaafi achhi nikli.

### Eka-boron aur scandium

Eka-boron (group III, Ca ke baad wali jagah) ka mass lagbhag 44 bataya gaya tha. **Scandium** 1879 mein mila, mass lagbhag **45** (jo upar ki table mein 45.0 diya hai), aur uska oxide Sc₂O₃ (valency 3) bhi group III ke pattern ke hisaab se hai.

> **Jaanch:** Teeno predictions ke antar 1.7, 1.0 aur 0.6 hain. Kya sabse chhota antar germanium ka hai? ✅ Haan, 0.6.

### Is kahani ka sabak

Mendeleev ke table ki sabse badi baat **bhavishyavani ka sach hona** thi. Jab gallium, scandium aur germanium **jaise bataye gaye the waise hi** mile, to duniya ne maana ki ye table sirf tatvon ko sajaana nahi, balki **kudrat ka ek asli pattern** pakadta hai.

## Part C: Mendeleev ke table ki khoobiyan (merits)

1. **Predict kiya.** Teen khaali jagahon ke tatva gun ke saath pehle se bataye gaye aur baad mein mile (upar ki table).
2. **Pattern saaf dikhaya.** Milte-julte tatva ek column mein aaye, to ek tatva ke gun jaan kar uske group ke baaki tatvon ke gun ka andaaza lag gaya. Oxide ke formula ka pattern iska example hai.
3. **Galat naap sudharne mein madad.** Kuch tatvon ke mass galat naape gaye the; table ke pattern se unhe dobara jaanchne ka ishaara mila.
4. **Noble gases bina garbad ke fit hue.** Mendeleev ke time tak He, Ne, Ar jaisi gas mili hi nahi thi (argon 1894 mein mili). Jab ye mili, to unke liye ek **naya group** table mein bina kuch bigade jod diya gaya, aur kisi purani jagah ko nahi hilana pada. Ye bhi table ki taakat ka sabut tha.

## Part D: Mendeleev ke table ki kamiyan (defects)

Koi bhi pehla bada idea poora nahi hota. Mendeleev ke table mein kuch asli problems thi:

**1. Hydrogen ki koi pakki jagah nahi.** Hydrogen ek taraf Li, Na, K jaisa (valency 1, ek electron kho sakta hai) lagta hai, doosri taraf Cl, F jaisa (valency 1, non-metal gas, aur Cl₂ ki tarah do atom wala H₂ molecule banata hai). Mendeleev hydrogen ko kahin bhi pakka nahi bitha paye.

**2. Isotopes ki jagah nahi.** Chapter 5 (Topic 6) mein hum ne dekha ki ek hi tatva ke atoms alag-alag mass ke ho sakte hain (jaise Cl-35 aur Cl-37). Agar table mass se chalti hai, to har isotope ko **alag jagah** milni chahiye, jabki chemistry mein wo ek hi tatva hain. Mendeleev ke time isotopes ka pata nahi tha.

**3. Kuch jodiyan ulti rakhni padi.** Agar sirf mass dekho to kuch tatva galat group mein pad jaate. Mendeleev ne **gun ko mass se upar** rakha aur unhe ulte order mein rakha:

| Jodi | Mass (pehla, doosra) | Mass ke hisaab se kaun pehle? | Mendeleev ne kaise rakha |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ar aur K | 39.95 aur 39.10 | K (kyunki 39.10 < 39.95) | Ar pehle, K baad mein |
| Co aur Ni | 58.93 aur 58.69 | Ni (58.69 < 58.93) | Co pehle, Ni baad mein |
| Te aur I | 127.6 aur 126.9 | I (126.9 < 127.6) | Te pehle, I baad mein |

Jaanch: 39.10 < 39.95 ✅, 58.69 < 58.93 ✅, 126.9 < 127.6 ✅. Teeno jodiyon mein asli mass ka order aur table ka order **ulta** hai. (Yahan mass thode zyada sahi ankon mein likhe hain; Chapter 4 ki table mein Ar aur K round karke 40 aur 39 the, aur wahan bhi Ar hi bhaari hai.)

Ek jodi detail mein: **Ar aur K.** Chapter 5 mein dekha tha ki Ar ka electronic configuration 2, 8, 8 (octet, noble gas) aur K ka 2, 8, 8, 1 hai.

```
   Agar sirf MASS dekho:              Asli CHEMISTRY ke hisaab se:

   K  (39.10)  pehle                  Ar (2, 8, 8)    noble gas: kisi se nahi judti
   Ar (39.95)  baad mein              K  (2, 8, 8, 1) alkali metal: valency 1

   K, Ar se pehle aa jaata.           Ar noble gas wale group mein,
   To K noble gas ke group mein       K Li, Na ke group mein hi aana chahiye.
   aur Ar Li, Na wale group mein      Isliye order ULTA karna pada
   aata: dono galat!                  (Ar pehle, K baad mein).
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Ar (39.95) aur K (39.10) ki ulti jodi. Mass ke hisaab se K pehle aata, to K galat group mein pad jaata. Gun (valency) ke hisaab se Ar pehle aur K baad mein rakhna pada. Chapter 5 ke Ar aur K wale point ka ye hi chhipa hua pehlu hai.

Ye teeno jodiyon mein Mendeleev ne **gun ko mass par chunna**, jo bilkul sahi faisla tha. Par isse ek bada sawaal khada ho gaya: **agar table ka order atomic mass hai, to ye ulti jodiyan kyun?** Mendeleev ne socha ki shayad Te jaise tatvon ke mass galat naape gaye honge. Par baad mein dobara naapne par pata chala ki mass sahi the, galti naap mein nahi thi. Yani **mass hi sahi cheez nahi thi**, kuch aur hona chahiye.

**4. Pattern ka koi kaaran nahi pata tha.** Mendeleev ke time mein atom ko **tootne wali cheez** nahi maana jaata tha: electron 1897 mein mila aur nucleus 1911 mein. Isliye koi nahi jaanta tha ki tatvon ke gun baar-baar kyun lautte hain. Table pattern **dikhata** tha, par **kyun** nahi bata paata tha.

**5. Kuch milte-julte tatva alag aur kuch alag tatva ek saath.** Jaise group I mein alkali metals (Li, Na, K) ke saath Cu aur Ag bhi aa gaye the, jabki unke gun kaafi alag hain. Ye bhi table ki ek kamzori thi.

## Part E: Reasoning ke solved examples

**Example 1: Khaali jagah ka prediction.**
Group III mein Al (27) hai aur uske neeche ek tatva mil chuka hai (indium, 114.8), beech mein gallium wali jagah thi. Agar Zn (65) aur As (75) ke average se mass nikalein to?
(65 + 75) ÷ 2 = **70**. Asli gallium 69.7. Antar 70 − 69.7 = **0.3**. ✅

**Example 2: Formula ki bhavishyavani.**
Eka-silicon group IV mein hai (Si ke neeche). Uska oxide kaisa hoga?
Si ka oxide SiO₂ hai (valency 4). Isi group mein eka-silicon ki valency bhi 4, to oxide **EsO₂** (asli: GeO₂). Jaanch: 4 × 1 = 4 aur 2 × 2 = 4 ✅.

**Example 3: Ar aur K ko ulta kyun rakha?**
Mass se K (39.10) pehle aata, par K ek alkali metal hai (valency 1, Li, Na ke group jaisa), aur Ar noble gas hai. Agar K pehle rakhte to wo noble gas ke group mein pad jaata. Isliye Ar pehle aur K baad mein rakha, kyunki gun ka mel zyada zaroori tha. ✅

**Example 4: Mass ka order aur table ka order alag kab hua?**
Te (127.6) aur I (126.9). I ke gun Cl, Br jaise (valency 1, halogen). Mass se I pehle aata, par Te se pehle rakhne par I galat group mein pad jaata. Isliye Te pehle, I baad mein. 127.6 − 126.9 = **0.7**, yani antar bahut chhota par order phir bhi ulta. ✅

**Example 5: Ek bacche ne kaha, "Mendeleev ne mass ke order ko galat kiya jab Ar aur K ko ulta rakha."** Sahi ya galat?
Galat. Mendeleev ne **jaanboojh kar** gun ko mass se upar rakha, aur uska faisla baad mein sahi nikla. Asli kaaran mass nahi, atomic number hai, jo hum agle topic mein dekhenge.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Mendeleev ke time saare tatva mil chuke the"**
Nahi. Mendeleev ke time bahut saare tatva abhi tak mile hi nahi the (gallium, scandium, germanium, aur saari noble gases). Isi liye khaali jagahein chhodi gayi thi.

**Galti 2: "Mendeleev ki prediction bilkul barabar sahi thi"**
Mendeleev ne **teen** khaali jagahon ke liye mass ka andaaza lagaya tha, jo asli mass se thoda alag nikla (68 ke samne 69.7, 72 ke samne 72.6, 44 ke samne 45.0). Andaaza **lagbhag** sahi tha, bilkul barabar nahi.

**Galti 3: "Mendeleev ke time electron aur nucleus pata the"**
Nahi. Electron 1897 mein aur nucleus 1911 mein mila, Mendeleev ke table ke kaafi baad.

**Galti 4: "Ar aur K ki jodi ulti hai matlab Mendeleev ki table galat hai"**
Ye ulti jodi kamzori hai par table kharab nahi hai: gun ko mass se upar rakhna sahi tha. Ye sirf ishara tha ki order ka asli aadhar kuch aur hai.

**Galti 5: "Eka-aluminium, eka-silicon sach mein tatvon ke symbol hain"**
Nahi. Ye Mendeleev ke diye hue **kaam-chalau naam** the, un tatvon ke jo abhi mile nahi the. Mil jaane par asli naam (gallium, germanium) aur symbol (Ga, Ge) mile.

## Quick Recap

- **Mendeleev (1869)** ne tatvon ko atomic mass ke order mein rakha aur milte-julte gun wale tatva ek **group** (vertical column) mein; **period** horizontal row hai.
- **Periodic law (Mendeleev):** tatvon ke gun unke atomic mass ke periodic function hain.
- Unhone **khaali jagahein** chhodi aur teen tatvon ke gun pehle se bataye: eka-aluminium (68, mila gallium 69.7, 1875), eka-boron (44, mila scandium 45.0, 1879), eka-silicon (72, mila germanium 72.6, 1886).
- Gallium 29.8 °C par pighal jaata hai, isliye haath ki garmi (37 °C) se pighalne lagta hai.
- **Khoobiyan:** predict kiya, pattern dikhaya, noble gases baad mein bina garbad ke fit hue.
- **Kamiyan:** hydrogen ki pakki jagah nahi, isotopes ki jagah nahi, Ar-K, Co-Ni, Te-I ki jodiyan ulti rakhni padi, aur pattern ka kaaran nahi pata tha.
- Mass hi sahi aadhar nahi tha. Ye ishara agle topic mein **atomic number** ki taraf le jaata hai.

### Answers

1. Mendeleev ne tatvon ko **atomic mass ke badhte order** mein rakha aur milte-julte gun wale tatvon ko ek hi column mein laaye. **Group** vertical column hai (milte-julte tatva), **period** horizontal row hai.

2. **Periodic law:** tatvon ke gun unke atomic mass ke periodic function hain. Calendar mein har 7 din baad wahi din (jaise Wednesday) lautta hai; waise hi tatvon mein ek tay doori ke baad milte-julte gun lautte hain.

3. Kuch jagah par agla tatva mass ke hisaab se thik baith hi nahi raha tha, ya antar bahut bada tha. Mendeleev ne wahan **khaali jagah** chhodi, maana ki tatva abhi mila nahi hai, aur **uske mass, density aur oxide ke formula ka andaaza** likha.

4. Eka-aluminium: andaaza 68, gallium 69.7, antar **1.7**. Eka-boron: andaaza 44, scandium 45.0, antar **1.0**. Eka-silicon: andaaza 72, germanium 72.6, antar **0.6**. Sabse paas germanium (0.6).

5. 37 − 29.8 = **7.2 °C** zyada. Haath mein rakhne par gallium ka tukda sharir ki garmi se **pighalne lagega** (pighalne ka bindu 29.8 °C hai, jo haath ke taapman se kam hai).

6. Eka-aluminium group III mein hai, valency 3, aur O ki valency 2. Criss-cross se oxide **Ea₂O₃** (asli: Ga₂O₃). Jaanch: 2 × 3 = 6 aur 3 × 2 = 6 ✅.

7. (i) Teen naye tatvon ki bhavishyavani sach hui. (ii) Milte-julte tatva ek column mein aaye, to ek tatva ke gun se doosron ka andaaza lagna aasan hua. (iii) Noble gases bina table bigade fit ho gayi. (Ya: galat naape gaye mass ko dobara jaanchne ka ishaara mila.)

8. Mendeleev ke time ye gas mili hi nahi thi. Jab mili, to ek **naya group** jod diya gaya aur kisi purani jagah ko nahi hilana pada. Isse dikha ki table ka pattern sahi tha.

9. Hydrogen valency 1 wale alkali metals (Li, Na, K) jaisa bhi lagta hai aur halogens (F, Cl) jaisa bhi. Dono group ke gun uske paas hain, isliye wo kisi ek hi group mein pakka nahi baithta.

10. Isotopes ke atoms ka mass alag hota hai par chemistry same (jaise Cl-35 aur Cl-37). Mass ke aadhar par to dono ko **alag jagah** milni chahiye, jabki wo ek hi tatva hain. Mendeleev ke time isotopes ka pata hi nahi tha.

11. K (39.10) mass mein halka hai, to mass-order mein pehle aata, par K ka configuration 2, 8, 8, 1 hai: ek valence electron, valency 1, alkali metal (Li, Na ke group jaisa). Ar ka 2, 8, 8 hai: octet, noble gas. Agar K pehle rakhte to wo noble gas ke group mein pad jaata, aur Ar alkali metal wale group mein. Isliye gun ke hisaab se Ar pehle aur K baad mein rakha.

12. **Ar aur K, Co aur Ni, Te aur I.** Teeno mein Mendeleev ne **mass ke badle gun (chemistry) ko** follow kiya, to ye tatva apne milte-julte group mein aaye.

13. Mendeleev ke time atom ko tootne wala nahi maana jaata tha: **electron 1897 mein** aur **nucleus 1911 mein** mila, Mendeleev ke table (1869) ke kaafi baad. Isliye pattern ka kaaran (atom ke andar ki banawat) koi nahi jaanta tha.

14. (a) **False** ❌. Mendeleev ka table atomic mass par tha, atomic number par nahi. (b) **True** ✅. Gallium 1875 mein mila, Mendeleev ki prediction ke baad. (c) **False** ❌. "Eka-aluminium" kaam-chalau naam tha, asli tatva gallium (Ga) hai. (d) **True** ✅. Ar ko K se pehle rakha gaya taaki dono apne sahi group mein aayein.

---

Shabaash! Ab tum Mendeleev ke table ki taakat (khaali jagah chhodna aur tatva predict karna) aur uski kamiyan (hydrogen, isotopes, ulti jodiyan) dono samajh gaye. Ek baat dhyan se socho: Ar aur K, Co aur Ni, Te aur I ki ulti jodiyan teeno mein mass ka order galat tha. Isse pata chalta hai ki tatvon ko tay karne wali asli cheez **mass nahi, kuch aur hai**.

**Aage kya?** Agle topic **"Modern Periodic Table: Atomic Number Ki Baat"** mein 1913 ke vaigyanik **Moseley** ki kahani dekhenge, jisne bataya ki tatvon ka asli order **atomic number** se tay hota hai (wahi Z jo Chapter 5 mein protons ki ginti thi). Isse Ar aur K jaisi saari ulti jodiyan apne aap sahi ho jaati hain, aur aaj ka table **18 groups aur 7 periods** wala banta hai.
