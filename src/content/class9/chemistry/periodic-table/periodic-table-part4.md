# Topic 4: Table Ke Andar Ka Pattern: Periodic Trends

Pichhle topic mein humne tatvon ko unke atomic number ke order mein 7 rows (periods) aur 18 columns (groups) mein baitha diya. Period number = shells ki ginti, aur group = baahari (valence) electrons se tay hua. Par table sirf "address book" nahi hai. Ab sawaal ye hai: **ek row mein baayen se daayen chalte hue kya badalta hai? Aur ek column mein upar se neeche utarte hue kya badalta hai?** In badlaavon ke pattern ko **periodic trends** kehte hain. Aaj hum valency, atom ka size aur "metal-pan" ke trends dekhenge, aur har trend ka reason shells aur nucleus ki kheench mein dhoondhenge.

---

## Sardiyon ka alaav

Mr. Dixit apni bhanji Zeel ko lekar gaon ke mela-maidan gaye. Sardi ki shaam thi aur beech mein bada sa alaav (bonfire) jal raha tha. Log gol ghera banakar baithe the. Zeel ne dekha ki **sabse aage wali row** mein baithe logon ke haath-chehre garam the, par **bahut peechhe** baithe logon ne shawl kaske lapet rakhi thi.

Zeel ne poochha, "Mamu, aag toh ek hi hai, phir garmi sabko alag kyun lag rahi hai?" Mr. Dixit ne kaha, "Aag se jitna **door**, utni kam garmi. Aur ek baat aur dekh: aage wali row ke log kuch garmi **rok bhi lete hain**, to peechhe waalon tak aur kam pahunchti hai." Phir unhone ek chhoti si baat jodi, "Agar kal aag aur badi ho jaaye, to sab log thoda aur paas khiskenge, aur ghera chhota ho jaayega."

Zeel hans padi, "Mamu, ye to bilkul atom jaisa hai! Nucleus alaav hai, electrons log hain, aur shells rows hain." Mr. Dixit ne shabaashi di. "Bas yahi picture yaad rakh. Aaj ka poora topic isi alaav ke aas-paas ghoomega."

Chalo, is alaav ko atom ke andar utaar ke dekhte hain.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Periodic trend** | Table mein ek period ya group mein chalte hue kisi gun ke badalne ka pattern | Period mein valency 1, 2, 3, 4, 3, 2, 1, 0 |

## Part A: Bonfire wala picture

Alaav ko atom se milaao:

- **Aag (nucleus)**: jitne zyada protons, utni badi aag, yaani utni zyada **kheench**.
- **Rows (shells)**: aag se jitna door ki row, utni kam garmi (kam kheench).
- **Beech ki rows (inner shells)**: ye aag aur peechhli rows ke beech mein hoti hain, to thodi garmi rok leti hain. Isse peechhli row tak kheench aur kam pahunchti hai.
- **Aakhri row (valence shell)**: yahi baahari electrons hain, aur yahi tay karte hain ki atom electron dega ya lega.

```
   Li (2 shells):   (+) ) e
                          ^ baahari electron paas: kheench zyada, atom chhota

   K  (4 shells):   (+) ) ) ) e
                              ^ baahari electron door: kheench kam, atom bada

   (+) = nucleus (aag)    ) = andar ki ek bhari shell (beech ki row)    e = baahari electron
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Bonfire analogy. Upar Li: sirf 2 shells, to baahari electron aag (nucleus) ke paas hai aur kheench zyada hai. Neeche K: 4 shells, to baahari electron door hai, beech ki teen bhari shells kheench ka kuch hissa rok leti hain, aur kheench kam lagti hai. Ye sirf sketch hai: asli atom gol hota hai aur electron ek line mein nahi baithte, par "zyada shells = door aur kam kheench" wali baat sahi hai.

Is picture se do niyam nikalte hain, jinke aas-paas poora topic chalega:

1. **Zyada shells = atom bada** (baahari electron door baitha hai).
2. **Shells wahi, par zyada protons = atom chhota** (aag badi hui, to wahi rows paas khinch aayin).

## Part B: Ek period mein baayen se daayen (Period 3 ko example banao)

Period 3 mein Na se Ar tak sab ke **3 shells** hain (K, L aur M). Fark ye hai ki har agle tatva mein **1 proton aur 1 electron zyada** hai. Naya electron baahari shell M mein baithta hai (aag ki rows wahi, par aag thodi badi hui).

```
   Period 3:   Na    Mg    Al    Si    P     S     Cl    Ar

   Protons     11    12    13    14    15    16    17    18
   Config      2,8,1 2,8,2 2,8,3 2,8,4 2,8,5 2,8,6 2,8,7 2,8,8
   Valence e   1     2     3     4     5     6     7     8
   Valency     1     2     3     4     3     2     1     0
   Shells      3     3     3     3     3     3     3     3

   ----->  nuclear pull badhti hai, shells wahi rehte hain  ----->
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Period 3 ke saare tatvon ki protons ki ginti, configuration, valence electrons aur valency. Valence electrons 1 se 8 tak badhte hain, valency 1, 2, 3, 4, 3, 2, 1, 0 (valence electrons 4 tak to wahi ginti, aur 4 ke baad 8 − valence electrons). Shells sab ke 3 hain.

Ye pattern Period 2 (Li se Ne) mein bhi bilkul wahi hai: Li 1, Be 2, B 3, C 4, N 3, O 2, F 1, Ne 0. Chapter 5 mein humne ye valency baahari electrons se nikali thi. Ab table mein ye ek **ghoomta hua cycle** dikhta hai.

**Ek period mein baayen se daayen ye badalta hai:**

| Gun | Baayen (Na, Li ki taraf) | Daayen (Cl, F ki taraf) | Kyun? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Valence electrons | 1 | 7 (phir 8) | Har tatva mein 1 electron zyada |
| Valency | 1 (beech mein 4 tak chadhti hai) | Cl par 1, Ar par 0 | 4 tak "dena", 4 ke baad "lena" aasan |
| Atomic size | Bada | Chhota | Shells wahi, protons zyada: kheench badhti hai |
| Metallic character | Zyada | Kam | Baahari electron dena aasan se mushkil |
| Non-metallic character | Kam | Zyada | Electron lena aasan hota jaata hai |

**Size ke baare mein ek dhyan:** Period mein Na se Cl tak atom **chhote hote jaate hain**. Na (11 protons) ke 3 shells aur Cl (17 protons) ke bhi 3 shells hain. Cl mein 6 protons zyada hain, to nucleus ki kheench zyada hai aur wahi 3 rows paas khinch jaati hain. Ye bilkul alaav ki baat hai: aag badi hui, ghera chhota hua. Neon aur argon jaise noble gases ke size ka maap alag tareeke se hota hai, isliye hum is trend ko Li se F aur Na se Cl tak dekhte hain. Is level par ye kafi hai.

**Metallic character kyun girta hai?** Na ke M shell mein akela 1 electron hai, aur aag se wo door sa hai, to wo aasani se nikal jaata hai. Cl ke M shell mein 7 electron hain aur sabko zyada kheench mil rahi hai. Wahan electron **dena** mushkil hai, aur 1 electron **lena** (octet poora karne ko) aasan. Isi liye baayen taraf ke tatva metal hain (electron dene wale) aur daayen taraf non-metal hain (electron lene ya share karne wale).

## Part C: Ek group mein upar se neeche

Ek group ke tatvon ke **valence electrons same** hote hain. Par neeche utarte hue **ek shell aur judta jaata hai**.

| Tatva | Configuration | Shells | Valence e | Valency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Li | 2, 1 | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| Na | 2, 8, 1 | 3 | 1 | 1 |
| K | 2, 8, 8, 1 | 4 | 1 | 1 |

| Tatva | Configuration | Shells | Valence e | Valency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F | 2, 7 | 2 | 7 | 1 |
| Cl | 2, 8, 7 | 3 | 7 | 1 |

| Tatva | Configuration | Shells | Valence e | Valency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Be | 2, 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Mg | 2, 8, 2 | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| Ca | 2, 8, 8, 2 | 4 | 2 | 2 |

Alaav ka picture phir lagao. Neeche utarte hue **baahari row aag se aur door** hoti jaati hai, aur beech ki rows bhi badh jaati hain (parda). Isliye:

| Gun | Group mein upar se neeche | Kyun? |
|---|---|---|
| Valence electrons | Same rehte hain | Group ki pehchaan hi yahi hai |
| Valency | Same rehti hai | Valency valence electrons se aati hai |
| Shells | Badhte hain (1 har period par) | Period number = shells |
| Atomic size | Badhta hai | Zyada shells, baahari electron door |
| Metallic character | Badhta hai | Baahari electron door hai, dena aasan |

Isliye Group 1 mein K ka baahari electron Li se nikalna zyada aasan hai, aur Group 2 mein Ca ka Be se zyada aasan.

## Part D: Metals, non-metals aur metalloid: table ka naksha

Chapter 2 mein humne metals, non-metals aur kuch beech ke tatva (metalloids) dekhe the. Ab trends se ye saaf ho jaata hai ki table mein ye kahan baithte hain. **Baayen aur neeche**: metallic character zyada, yaani **metals**. **Upar daayen**: electron lene wale, yaani **non-metals**. Dono ke beech ek **zigzag seedhi (staircase)** hai jis par **metalloid** baithe hain: jo thode metal aur thode non-metal jaise vyavhaar karte hain.

Pehle 20 tatvon ke liye table ka chhota naksha (groups 3 se 12 yahan chhode gaye hain, aur seedhi ke baaki tatva aage ki classes mein):

```
   Group      1    2    13   14   15   16   17   18
   Period 1   H                                  He
   Period 2   Li   Be   [B]  C    N    O    F    Ne
   Period 3   Na   Mg   Al   [Si] P    S    Cl   Ar
   Period 4   K    Ca

   Kaun kya hai (M metal, N non-metal, S metalloid):

   Group      1    2    13   14   15   16   17   18
   Period 1   N                                  N
   Period 2   M    M    S    N    N    N    N    N
   Period 3   M    M    M    S    N    N    N    N
   Period 4   M    M

   Seedhi: B se Si ki taraf zigzag nikalti hai.
   Seedhi ke baayen aur neeche: metals.  Seedhi ke daayen aur upar: non-metals.
```

> **Figure 4.3** – Pehle 20 tatvon ka chhota table. Upar wale hisse mein symbols, `[B]` aur `[Si]` metalloid hain. Neeche wale hisse mein har jagah M (metal), N (non-metal) ya S (metalloid). Left aur neeche M, right aur upar N, aur beech mein seedhi (B, Si). Hydrogen group 1 mein baitha hai par wo non-metal gas hai (Topic 3 ka note).

Dhyan do: **pehle 20 mein sirf B aur Si metalloid hain.** Noble gases (He, Ne, Ar) bhi non-metal hain. Poori table mein seedhi aur neeche tak jaati hai (wahan Ge, As, Sb, Te jaise metalloid hain), par wo pehle 20 ke bahar hai, isliye aage ki class mein.

Period 3 ko ek hi line mein padho: Na, Mg, Al **metals**, Si **metalloid**, P, S, Cl **non-metals**, Ar noble gas. Valency ke saath isse jodo: Na 1, Mg 2, Al 3 (metals dete hain), Si 4 (beech), P 3, S 2, Cl 1 (non-metals lete ya share karte hain).

## Part E: Reactivity: alkali metals aur halogens (sirf pehchaan, kaam ki baat nahi)

Ab trends ek rochak jagah kaam aate hain: **Group 1 aur Group 17**. Ye sirf shabdon mein samajhna hai, koi activity nahi karni hai (alkali metals aur halogens ke saath ghar par ya school mein kuch nahi karna).

**Group 1 (Li, Na, K): upar se neeche reactivity badhti hai.**

- Teeno ke baahari 1 electron hain, aur teeno ye electron dena chahte hain.
- Neeche utarte hue baahari electron aag se aur door hota jaata hai, aur beech ki rows ka parda badhta hai. To electron aur aasani se nikalta hai.
- Isliye K, Na se zyada tezi se react karta hai, aur Na, Li se zyada. Ye metals hawa aur paani ke saath bahut jaldi react karte hain, isliye inhe tel ke neeche rakha jaata hai (paani ke saath unka vyavhaar agle topic mein, sirf shabdon mein). Humein abhi sirf itna jaanna hai ki trend **neeche badhta hai**.

**Group 17 (F, Cl, Br, I): upar se neeche reactivity ghat-ti hai.**

- Inke baahari 7 electron hain aur ye 1 electron **lena** chahte hain (octet ke liye).
- Upar wale tatva (F) mein baahari shell nucleus ke paas hai. Naye electron ko kheenchne ki taqat zyada hai.
- Neeche utarte hue shell door ho jaati hai (aur parda badhta hai), to naya electron kheenchna kam aasan hota hai. Isliye F sabse zyada reactive halogen hai aur I sabse kam.

Yaad rakhne ka tareeka: **Group 1 mein neeche jao to reactivity badhti hai (Li < Na < K); Group 17 mein neeche jao to ghat-ti hai (F > Cl > Br > I).** Dono ka reason baahari electron ki aag se doori aur kheench hai.

**Dhyan do:** chlorine zehreeli gas hai aur bromine zehreela liquid (jiski bhaap bhi zehreeli hai); inhe sirf bade log, tayyari ke saath, lab mein sambhalte hain. Hum inka iss file mein sirf zikr kar rahe hain. Group 1 aur 17 ke jeevan, upyog aur kahaniyan agle topic mein.

## Part F: Solved examples

Har example mein bonfire picture lagao: **kitni rows (shells)** aur **kitni badi aag (protons)**?

### Example 1. Na aur Mg: kaun bada?

- Na: 2, 8, 1 (3 shells, 11 protons). Mg: 2, 8, 2 (3 shells, 12 protons).
- Shells same (3). Mg mein 1 proton zyada hai, to kheench zyada aur Mg chhota.
- **Na bada hai.**

Jaanch: Period 3 mein baayen se daayen size ghat-ta hai, Na baayen hai ✅

### Example 2. Na aur Mg: kaun zyada metallic?

- Na ko sirf **1** electron dena hai, wo bhi door se. Mg ko **2** dene hain aur wo zyada kheench ke saath bandhe hain.
- **Na zyada metallic hai.** Chapter 7 ki reactivity series mein bhi Na, Mg se upar tha ✅

### Example 3. Li aur K: kaun bada, kaun zyada reactive?

- Li: 2, 1 (2 shells). K: 2, 8, 8, 1 (4 shells). Baahari valence electron dono mein 1.
- K mein 2 shells zyada, to baahari electron aag se bahut door aur K bada.
- Door wala electron aasani se nikalta hai, to **K zyada reactive** (aur zyada metallic).
- Valency dono ki 1 (valence electrons same).

Jaanch: Group 1 mein neeche size aur reactivity badhte hain, K Li se neeche hai ✅

### Example 4. F aur Cl

- F: 2, 7 (2 shells). Cl: 2, 8, 7 (3 shells). Dono ke valence electrons 7, valency 1 (8 − 7).
- **Cl bada** (3 shells).
- Group 17 mein neeche utarte hue reactivity ghat-ti hai, to **F zyada reactive** hai.

Jaanch: F upar hai, isliye zyada reactive; Cl neeche hai, isliye kam ✅

### Example 5. Be aur Mg

- Be: 2, 2 (Period 2, Group 2). Mg: 2, 8, 2 (Period 3, Group 2). Dono ke valence electrons 2, valency 2.
- Mg ke shells zyada (3 vs 2), to **Mg bada** aur **Mg zyada metallic**.

Jaanch: Group 2 mein neeche utarte hue size aur metallic character badhte hain ✅

### Example 6. Period 3 ke tatvon ko size ke order mein likho

Tatva: Na, Mg, Al, Si, P, S, Cl.

- Sab ke 3 shells. Protons: 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17.
- Jitne zyada protons, utna chhota.
- Order (bade se chhote): **Na > Mg > Al > Si > P > S > Cl.**

Jaanch: Cl mein Na se 17 − 11 = **6** protons zyada hain, aur wo Figure 4.2 ki valence electrons ki 1 se 7 tak ki 6 ki badhat se milta hai ✅

### Example 7. K, Na, Li aur Be: bade se chhote

- K: 4 shells. Na: 3 shells. Li: 2 shells (3 protons). Be: 2 shells (4 protons).
- Pehle shells: K (4) > Na (3) > Li, Be (2). Phir Li aur Be mein shells same, Be mein zyada proton, to Be chhota.
- Order: **K > Na > Li > Be.**

Jaanch: Shells aur protons dono ka kaam dikha: shells zyada hone par bada, shells same hone par protons zyada hone par chhota ✅

### Example 8. Configuration 2, 8, 5 wala tatva

- Electrons = 2 + 8 + 5 = 15, to Z = 15: **phosphorus (P)**.
- Shells 3 to Period 3. Valence electrons 5, to Group 5 + 10 = **15**.
- Valency: 5 > 4, to 8 − 5 = **3**. Seedhi ke daayen: **non-metal**.

Jaanch: Figure 4.3 mein P Period 3, Group 15, N ✅

### Example 9. Ek rahasyamayi tatva

Ek tatva Period 3 mein hai, uski valency 4 hai, aur wo seedhi par baitha hai. Kaun?

- Period 3 mein valency 4 sirf Si ki hai (valence electrons 4, Group 14).
- Seedhi par metalloid hai: Si.

Jaanch: Si: 2, 8, 4, valency 4, metalloid ✅

### Example 10. Mg aur Ca

- Mg: 2, 8, 2 (3 shells). Ca: 2, 8, 8, 2 (4 shells). Dono ke valence electrons 2 aur valency 2.
- Ca bada, aur **Ca zyada metallic**.

Jaanch: Chapter 7 ki reactivity series mein Ca, Mg se upar tha ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Ek period mein atom ka size badhna**

Na se Cl tak shells wahi (3) hain, par protons badhte hain, to atom **chhota** hota hai, bada nahi.

**Galti 2: Group mein neeche valency badhna**

K ki valency Li jaisi hi 1 hai. Valency valence electrons se aati hai aur group mein wo same rehte hain. Neeche shells badhte hain, valency nahi.

**Galti 3: Zyada electrons = bada atom**

Cl (17 electron) Na (11 electron) se chhota hai, kyunki dono ke shells 3 hain aur Cl mein protons zyada hain. Size shells aur nuclear pull se tay hota hai, sirf electron ginti se nahi.

**Galti 4: "Metals sab ek jaise hote hain"**

Group 1 ke metals tez react karte hain, par sab metals nahi. Hum trend ki baat kar rahe hain: baayen aur neeche zyada metallic character.

**Galti 5: Group 17 mein reactivity ko Group 1 jaisa samajhna**

Group 1 mein neeche reactivity **badhti** hai, par Group 17 mein neeche **ghat-ti** hai. Dono ka reason alag hai: Group 1 electron **dena** chahta hai (door electron aasani se jaata hai), Group 17 electron **lena** chahta hai (door shell naye electron ko kam kheenchti hai).

**Galti 6: Metalloid ko seedhi se bahar dhoondhna**

B aur Si seedhi par hain. Hydrogen group 1 mein hai par metal nahi, wo non-metal gas hai.

**Galti 7: Noble gas ko "metal" ya "bahut reactive" samajhna**

He, Ne, Ar non-metals hain, valency 0, aur octet (ya duplet) poora hai, isliye lagbhag koi reaction nahi.

## Quick Recap

- **Periodic trend** = period ya group mein chalte hue gun ka pattern. Bonfire picture: nucleus = aag, shells = rows, baahari electron = aakhri row.
- **Period mein baayen se daayen** (Na se Cl): shells wahi, protons badhte hain. Valence electrons 1 se 8, valency 1, 2, 3, 4, 3, 2, 1, 0. Atom chhota hota hai, metallic character ghat-ta hai.
- **Group mein upar se neeche**: valence electrons aur valency same, shells badhte hain, atom bada hota hai, metallic character badhta hai.
- **Table ka naksha**: baayen aur neeche metals, daayen aur upar non-metals, beech ki seedhi par metalloid. Pehle 20 mein sirf B aur Si metalloid.
- **Group 1** mein neeche utarte hue reactivity badhti hai (sirf pehchaan). **Group 17** mein neeche ghat-ti hai.
- Shells zyada = bada. Shells same to protons zyada = chhota.

### Answers

1. **Periodic trend** wo pattern hai jisme table mein ek period ya group mein chalte hue koi gun regular tareeke se badalta hai. Ek period mein: valency (1, 2, 3, 4, 3, 2, 1, 0), atomic size (ghat-ta hai), metallic character (ghat-ta hai).

2. Na 1 e, valency 1; Mg 2, 2; Al 3, 3; Si 4, 4; P 5, 3; S 6, 2; Cl 7, 1; Ar 8, 0. Valency ka pattern **1, 2, 3, 4, 3, 2, 1, 0** hai: 4 tak badhti hai, phir ghat kar 0 par aati hai.

3. Na mein 11 protons, Cl mein 17 protons, dono ke 3 shells. Aag (nucleus) badi hui par rows wahi rahin, to rows paas khinch gayin aur atom chhota ho gaya. Electrons zyada hain par wo wahi shell mein hain, to size nahi badhta.

4. Li: 2, 1 (2 shells). K: 2, 8, 8, 1 (4 shells). K ka baahari electron aag se bahut door hai, isliye K bada. Valency dono ki valence electrons (1) se aati hai, jo same hain, isliye valency 1 hi rehti hai.

5. Na (3 shells, 11 protons), Mg (3 shells, 12 protons), Cl (3 shells, 17 protons): **Na > Mg > Cl** (shells same, protons zyada to chhota). Be, Mg, Ca, Na: Ca (4 shells) sabse bada, Be (2 shells) sabse chhota. Na aur Mg dono ke 3 shells, par Mg mein 12 protons aur Na mein 11, to Mg chhota: **Ca > Na > Mg > Be**.

6. Nahi, valency same rehti hai. Li (2, 1), Na (2, 8, 1) aur K (2, 8, 8, 1) sab mein 1 valence electron, to valency 1. Sirf shells badhte hain.

7. **Ca zyada metallic** (4 shells vs Be ke 2): baahari 2 electron bahut door hain aur aasani se nikalte hain. **Mg zyada metallic** (Al ke 3 ke mukable sirf 2 electron dene hain, wo bhi kam kheench ke saath, kyunki Mg mein 12 protons aur Al mein 13; shells dono ke 3).

8. Metals table ke **baayen aur neeche** hain, non-metals **daayen aur upar**, aur metalloid dono ke beech **seedhi** par. Pehle 20 mein **B aur Si** metalloid hain.

9. Alkali metals: neeche utarte hue reactivity **badhti** hai (baahari electron door ho jaata hai aur aasani se nikalta hai). Halogens: neeche utarte hue **ghat-ti** hai (baahari shell door hoti hai, to naya electron kheenchna kam aasan). Ye reactive aur khatarnak hain (alkali metals tez react karte hain, chlorine aur bromine zehreeli hain), isliye ghar par kuch nahi karna.

10. Electrons = 2 + 8 + 6 = 16: **sulphur (S)**. 3 shells to Period 3; valence electrons 6, to Group 6 + 10 = **16**. Valency 8 − 6 = **2**. **Non-metal**.

11. Period 2, Group 14, valence electrons 14 − 10 = 4, configuration 2, 4: **carbon (C)**, non-metal, valency **4**.

12. (a) **False** ❌. Left se right size **ghat-ta** hai. (b) **True** ✅. Group 17 mein neeche reactivity ghat-ti hai, F sabse upar. (c) **False** ❌. Valency 1, 2, 3, 4, phir 3, 2, 1, 0. (d) **True** ✅.

13. Z = 3 se 18 par valency: 1, 2, 3, 4, 3, 2, 1, 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 3, 2, 1, 0. Graph **zigzag (pahaad jaisa)** banta hai aur ek hi shape **dohrata** hai. Ye periodic pattern hai: har 8 tatvon ke baad baahari electrons ki ginti wahi cycle dobara chalati hai.
