## Part A: Chaaron parivaar ek nazar mein

Chapter 5 ka kaam yaad karo: **valence electrons** hi valency aur behaviour tay karte hain. Group 1 ke sabhi tatvon mein 1 valence electron, Group 2 mein 2, Group 17 mein 7, aur Group 18 mein 8 (helium mein 2, jo uska poora shell hai).

```
   GROUP 1           GROUP 2           GROUP 17          GROUP 18
   alkali metals     alkaline earth    halogens          noble gases
                     metals
   +---------+       +---------+       +---------+       +---------+
   | (H)     |       |         |       |         |       | He  (2) |
   | Li  (1) |       | Be  (2) |       | F   (7) |       | Ne  (8) |
   | Na  (1) |       | Mg  (2) |       | Cl  (7) |       | Ar  (8) |
   | K   (1) |       | Ca  (2) |       | Br  (7) |       |         |
   |         |       |         |       | I   (7) |       |         |
   +---------+       +---------+       +---------+       +---------+

   valence e: 1      valence e: 2      valence e: 7      valence e: 8
   valency: 1        valency: 2        valency: 1        valency: 0

   neeche jaate hue  neeche jaate hue  neeche jaate hue  inert: lagbhag
   reactivity BADHTI reactivity BADHTI reactivity GHATTI koi reaction
   Li < Na < K       Be < Mg < Ca      F > Cl > Br > I   nahi
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Chaar families: bracket mein valence electrons, aur har column mein ek jaise (He ke 2, jo uski poori shell hai). Group 1 aur 2 mein neeche jaate hue reactivity badhti hai; Group 17 mein ghatti hai; Group 18 inert hai. (H) ko Topic 3 ki tarah Group 1 ke upar rakha jaata hai, par wo alkali metal nahi, non-metal gas hai.

Topic 4 wala valency ka pattern bhi yaad karo: **1, 2, 3, 4, 3, 2, 1, 0**. Group 1 aur 17 ki valency 1 hai, par ek **metal** hai aur doosra **non-metal**: kyun? Group 1 apna 1 electron **dena** chahta hai, Group 17 ko 1 electron **lena** hai (Chapter 6 ka octet yaad karo). Isliye dono milkar ek ionic bond banate hain.

## Part B: Group 1, alkali metals (Li, Na, K)

Ye parivaar table ka sabse "ugra" parivaar hai. Inki pehchaan:

- **Narm aur chamakdaar:** chaaku se kat jaate hain; taaza kate hue hisse par chaandi jaisi chamak hoti hai, jo hawa mein bahut jaldi dhundhli pad jaati hai.
- **Halke:** inka ghanatva (density) bahut kam hai. Lithium sabse halka metal hai.
- **Valency 1:** 1 valence electron dene par ek ion banta hai (Li⁺, Na⁺, K⁺).
- **Bahut reactive:** hawa aur paani ke saath tez reaction.

Isliye sodium aur potassium **mitti ke tel (kerosene) ya tel ke neeche** rakhe jaate hain, taaki hawa aur nami se door rahein.

**Paani ke saath unka vyavhaar (sirf padhne ke liye, kabhi activity nahi):** alkali metal paani par tairta hai, tezi se idhar-udhar bhaagta hai aur **hydrogen gas** nikalta hai, jiske baad ghol **alkaline** ban jaata hai. Lithium dheere, sodium zyada tez, aur **potassium sabse tez**, kai baar chingaari ke saath. Ye vyavhaar bahut khatarnak ho sakta hai, isliye in metals ko **sirf prashikshit teacher** lab mein sambhalte hain. **Bachche inhe kabhi na chhuen aur na koi prayog karein.** (Paani aur hydrogen ke baare mein Chapter 9 aur 11 mein.)

Topic 4 ka reactivity trend yaad karo: group mein neeche shells badhte hain, baahari electron nucleus se door hota hai aur aasani se nikal jaata hai. Isliye **Li < Na < K** reactivity badhti hai.

**Inke compounds:** ye ionic compounds banate hain, jo aksar paani mein aasani se ghul jaate hain:

- NaCl (Na⁺ aur Cl⁻): rasoi ka namak
- KBr, KCl
- LiF

Charge balance check: Na (+1) aur Cl (-1) se NaCl, K (+1) aur Br (-1) se KBr, Li (+1) aur F (-1) se LiF; teeno mein ek-ek ion kaafi hai ✅.

> **Jaanch:** Sodium ko tel ke neeche kyun rakhte hain? ✅ Hawa aur nami se bachane ke liye, kyunki ye bahut reactive hai.

## Part C: Group 2, alkaline earth metals (Mg, Ca)

Group 2 ke tatvon mein 2 valence electrons hain, valency 2. Group 1 ke mukable:

- **Zyada sakht** aur zyada ghanatva wale, par phir bhi kaafi reactive.
- **Group 1 se kam reactive** (apni period mein), par neeche jaate hue yahan bhi reactivity badhti hai.

Magnesium ko yaad karo, jo Chapter 7 mein **tez safed lau** ke saath jalta tha: 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO. Ye **MgO** ek ionic compound hai: Mg²⁺ aur O²⁻, ek-ek ion, charge balance ✅ (2 aur 2).

Calcium ke compounds hamari **haddiyon, daanton aur doodh** mein hote hain. Group 2 ke metals chlorine ke saath compound banate hain: Ca (+2) ko do Cl (-1) chahiye, isliye **CaCl₂**; Mg se **MgCl₂**. (Charge: +2 aur 2 × (-1) = -2, kul 0 ✅.)

Dhyan rahe: calcium aur magnesium ke saath bhi koi prayog ghar par nahi karna.

## Part D: Group 17, halogens (F, Cl, Br, I)

"**Halogen**" ka matlab hai **namak banane wala**: ye metals se milkar namak (salts) banate hain, jaise NaCl, KBr. Inmein 7 valence electrons hain, valency 1 (ek electron chahiye octet poora karne ke liye). Ye sab **non-metal** hain aur do-do ke jode (F₂, Cl₂, Br₂, I₂) mein milte hain.

Sabse khaas baat: **ek hi parivaar, teeno states!**

```
        FLUORINE      CHLORINE      BROMINE        IODINE
        F             Cl            Br             I
        (Z = 9)       (Z = 17)      (Z = 35)       (Z = 53)

        halki         peeli-hari    laal-bhoora    baingani-kaala
        peeli gas     gas           taral          thos (kabhi
                                    (liquid)       chamkeeli parat)

        GAS           GAS           LIQUID         SOLID
        (room temp)   (room temp)   (room temp)    (room temp)

        reactivity:  sabse zyada  ---------------->  sabse kam
        (group mein neeche jaate hue ghatti hai)
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Halogens ka rang aur state: F₂ halki peeli gas, Cl₂ peeli-hari gas, Br₂ laal-bhoora liquid, I₂ baingani-kaala thos. Neeche jaate hue reactivity ghatti hai.

Group mein neeche jaate hue dekho: gas se liquid se solid, rang halka se gehra. Ye bhi ek pattern hai, aur reactivity **ghatti** hai, jiska karan: neeche jaate hue shells badhte hain, nucleus ka khichaav baahari shell par kamzor hota hai, to naya electron **khichna** kam aasaan hota hai. F sabse reactive, I sabse kam.

**Safety, bahut zaroori:**

- **Chlorine aur bromine zehreeli** hain. Inki gas ya vapour saans mein jaye to phephdon ko nuksaan hota hai. Inhe kabhi ghar par ya bina teacher ke nahi sambhalna.
- **Fluorine** to aur bhi zyada zehreeli aur reactive hai.
- Bachche in halogens ka koi bhi prayog nahi karein.

**Upyog (kam matra mein, visheshagyon ke dwara):**

- **Chlorine:** paani ko saaf aur kitanu-mukt karne mein, swimming pool mein, aur **bleaching powder** banane mein (kapde aur kaagaz ko safed karne mein). Sarkaari paani ke plant mein bahut kam matra mein daali jaati hai, isliye wo paani peene layak rehta hai; swimming pool ya bleach ko ghar par kabhi mat chakho ya milao.
- **Iodine:** **iodine tincture** (bhoora ghol) ghaav par antiseptic ke roop mein lagti hai, **sirf bade (adult) ka kaam**. Iodised namak mein bhi bahut kam iodine ke compounds hote hain, jo gale ki thyroid gland ke liye zaroori hain.
- **Bromine:** kuch dawai aur rangon mein, kewal industry mein.

**Ek sawaal:** Chlorine gas, KBr ke ghol se Br₂ ko nikaal sakti hai: Cl₂ + 2KBr → 2KCl + Br₂. Reason? Chlorine, bromine se zyada reactive hai, isliye jagah le leti hai (Chapter 7 ka displacement). Atom count: baayein 2 Cl, 2 K, 2 Br; daayein 2 K, 2 Cl, 2 Br ✅. (Ye sirf padhne ke liye hai, koi activity nahi.)

**Halogens ke compounds:** Na ke saath NaCl, Ca ke saath CaCl₂, K ke saath KBr ya KI. Iodine ke saath: NaI, KI (+1 aur -1 ✅).

> **Jaanch:** Chlorine aur bromine ek hi group mein hain, par ek gas hai aur doosra liquid. ✅ Haan, ek hi parivaar mein bhi states badal sakti hain; chemical behaviour ek jaisa rehta hai.

## Part E: Group 18, noble gases (He, Ne, Ar)

Ye table ka sabse **shaant parivaar** hai. Inka baahari shell **poora bhara** hai: He mein 2 (K shell poora), Ne mein 8 aur Ar mein 8 (octet, Chapter 6). Isliye na inhe electron dena hai, na lena, na baantna: valency 0 aur ye **inert** hain.

```
    HELIUM (He, Z = 2)       K shell: 2            poora bhara
         (2)
    NEON (Ne, Z = 10)        K: 2   L: 8           poora bhara
         (2,8)
    ARGON (Ar, Z = 18)       K: 2   L: 8   M: 8    poora bhara
         (2,8,8)

    Shell-wise ginti:
       He:  [2]
       Ne:  [2] [8]
       Ar:  [2] [8] [8]
    Shell 1 mein 2 aur baaki shells mein 8 hone par shell poora hai.

    Teeno ke baahari shell mein jagah nahi bachi, isliye reaction nahi.
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Noble gases ke poore shells: He (2), Ne (2, 8), Ar (2, 8, 8). Bahari shell bhar chuka hai, isliye ye gas lagbhag kabhi reaction nahi karti.

(Ye wahi K, L, M shell hain jo Chapter 5 mein the; 8 ka poora baahari shell octet kehlata hai, aur helium ka 2 wala poora shell duplet.)

**Upyog:**

- **Helium:** **balloons** mein (hawa se bahut halki aur jalti nahi, isliye hydrogen se zyada surakshit); gotakhoron ke gas mixture mein.
- **Neon:** **neon signs** mein: bijli guzarne par laal-narangi roshni (Chapter 1 ki neon sign yaad karo).
- **Argon:** **bulb** mein, kyunki wo filament ko jalne se bachati hai; hawa mein lagbhag 1 percent argon hai.

Kyunki ye reaction nahi karti, noble gases ko ye naam mila: "noble" jaise ek raja jo kisi se mel-jol nahi karta. (Aage ki class mein inke kuch compounds ban sakne ki baat aati hai, hum yahan nahi jaate.)

> **Jaanch:** Helium ka balloon aag pakad kar nahi jalega. ✅ Helium inert hai aur jalta nahi, isliye hydrogen se zyada surakshit hai.

## Part F: Baaki families ek-ek line mein

Table ke beech wale columns bhi family hi hain, hum unhe sirf pehchan lenge:

## Part H: Solved examples

**Example 1: K, Mg aur Cl mein se kaunsa tatva Na jaisa behave karega?**
K bhi Group 1 mein hai (1 valence electron), isliye K aur Na ek parivaar ke hain aur dono alkali metals jaise behave karte hain. ✅

**Example 2: Calcium aur chlorine milkar kya banayenge?**
Ca ke 2 valence electrons hain (Ca²⁺), Cl ko 1 electron chahiye (Cl⁻). Ek Ca ko do Cl chahiye: **CaCl₂**. Charge: +2 aur 2 × (-1) = -2, total 0 ✅.

**Example 3: Sodium aur bromine ka compound?**
Na (+1), Br (-1): **NaBr**, ek-ek ion ✅.

**Example 4: Kaun zyada reactive, Na ya K? F ya Cl?**
Group 1: neeche K, to **K zyada reactive**. Group 17: upar F, to **F zyada reactive**.

**Example 5: Ek tatva 2, 8, 8 shell wala hai (Z = 18). Kaunsa parivaar?**
Bahari shell poora, to **Argon, Group 18, noble gas** ✅ (valency 0).

**Example 6: Ek tatva 2, 8, 7 wala hai (Z = 17). Group aur family?**
7 valence electrons, 3 shells: **Period 3, Group 17: chlorine, halogen.** Chlorine ek electron lekar Cl⁻ banta hai.

**Example 7: Sodium aur chlorine milein to kaunsa bond banega?**
Na electron deta hai, Cl leta hai: **ionic bond**, NaCl (rasoi ka namak) ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Alkali metals ko haath se chhoone mein koi khatra nahi"**
Nahi. Ye bahut reactive hain aur hawa-paani se tez reaction karte hain; sirf teacher sambhalta hai.

**Galti 2: "Halogens group mein neeche jaate hue zyada reactive hote hain"**
Ulta: halogens neeche jaate hue **kam** reactive hote hain (F sabse zyada). Alkali metals neeche jaate hue **zyada** reactive hote hain.

**Galti 3: "Chlorine peene ke paani mein hai to zehreeli hi nahi hai"**
Chlorine gas zehreeli hai; paani mein bahut kam matra mein daali jaati hai, isliye hum use pee sakte hain.

**Galti 4: "Noble gases ke paas valence electrons hain hi nahi"**
Unke paas hain, 8 (He ke 2), par shell **poora bhara** hai, isliye valency 0.

**Galti 5: "Ek group ke tatvon mein koi farak nahi"**
Behaviour ek jaisa hai, par aakaar, state aur reactivity badalte hain (Cl gas, Br liquid, I solid).

## Quick Recap

- **Family (group):** ek column, valence electrons ek jaise, behaviour ek jaisa.
- **Group 1 (alkali):** narm, halke, valency 1, neeche jaate hue zyada reactive; tel ke neeche rakhe jaate hain; paani ke saath tez vyavhaar (sirf padhna, koi activity nahi).
- **Group 2 (alkaline earth):** Mg, Ca, valency 2; Mg tez safed lau se jalta hai (MgO).
- **Group 17 (halogens):** 7 valence electrons, valency 1; F, Cl gas, Br liquid, I solid; reactivity neeche ghatti hai; Cl aur Br zehreeli.
- **Group 18 (noble gases):** poora bhara baahari shell (He 2; Ne, Ar 8), inert, valency 0; balloons, signs, bulbs.
- Group 1 aur 17 milkar ionic compounds banate hain: NaCl, KBr, LiF; Group 2 aur 17: CaCl₂.

### Answers

1. **Family** matlab table ka ek vertical column (group). Ek group ke tatvon ke **valence electrons ki sankhya ek jaisi** hoti hai, aur chemical behaviour valence electrons se tay hota hai, isliye unka vyavhaar ek jaisa hota hai.

2. (a) Alkali metals: **Li, Na, K.** (b) Alkaline earth metals: **Be, Mg, Ca.** (c) Halogens: **F, Cl, Br, I.** (d) Noble gases: **He, Ne, Ar.**

3. Group 1: **1 valence electron, valency 1.** Group 2: **2, valency 2.** Group 17: **7, valency 1.** Group 18: **8 (He mein 2), valency 0.**

4. Narm (chaaku se kat jaate hain), halke (kam ghanatva), kate hue pe chamak par jaldi dhundhle. Sodium aur potassium bahut reactive hain, isliye hawa aur nami se bachane ke liye unhe **kerosene ya tel** ke neeche rakhte hain.

5. **Potassium** zyada reactive hai. K mein 4 shells hain, Li mein 2; K ka baahari electron nucleus se door hai aur nucleus ka khichaav kamzor hai, isliye wo aasani se nikal jaata hai.

6. Alkali metals hawa aur paani se bahut tez, kabhi chingaari ke saath reaction karte hain aur khatarnak ho sakte hain. Isliye sirf prashikshit teacher inhe lab mein sambhalte hain; bachche kabhi na chhuen.

7. **F₂:** halki peeli gas; **Cl₂:** peeli-hari gas; **Br₂:** laal-bhoora liquid; **I₂:** baingani-kaala solid.

8. **Fluorine** zyada reactive hai. Group 17 mein neeche jaate hue reactivity **ghatti** hai (F > Cl > Br > I), kyunki shells badhne se nucleus ka khichaav baahari shell par kamzor hota hai aur naya electron khichna kam aasaan hota hai.

9. Chlorine: paani ko kitanu-mukt karne mein aur bleaching powder banane mein. Iodine: iodine tincture ghaav par antiseptic ke roop mein (sirf bade). Safety: chlorine aur bromine zehreeli hain, unhe bina teacher ke nahi sambhalna; iodine tincture sirf bade lagayein.

10. (a) **NaCl:** Na (+1), Cl (-1) ✅. (b) **KBr:** K (+1), Br (-1) ✅. (c) **CaCl₂:** Ca (+2), 2 × Cl (-1) ✅. (d) **MgO:** Mg (+2), O (-2) ✅. (e) **LiF:** Li (+1), F (-1) ✅.

11. Noble gases ka baahari shell poora bhara hai, isliye unhe electron dena, lena ya baantna nahi padta, aur ve inert hain. **He:** 2. **Ne:** 2, 8. **Ar:** 2, 8, 8.

12. Helium: balloons mein. Neon: neon signs mein. Argon: bulb mein.

13. Group 13: B, Al (valency 3). Group 14: C, Si (valency 4). Group 15: N, P (valency 3). Group 16: O, S (valency 2).

14. (a) **False** ❌. Halogens neeche jaate hue **kam** reactive hote hain. (b) **True** ✅. Helium ke 2 valence electrons ek poora shell banate hain. (c) **True** ✅. (d) **False** ❌. Alkali metals bahut reactive hain aur safely chhue nahi ja sakte.
