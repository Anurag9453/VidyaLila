# Topic 6: Table Se Bhavishyavani: Practice aur Chapter Ka Saar

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki ek hi group ke elements ek **parivaar** ki tarah hote hain: valence electrons same, vyavhaar milta-julta. Ab sabse maze ka kaam: table ko **padhna**. Kisi element ka sirf **period aur group** pata ho to hum bata sakte hain ki uski valency kya hogi, wo metal hai ya non-metal, kis se kaisa bond banayega, aur uska formula kya hoga. Mendeleev ne yehi kiya tha, aur aaj hum bhi karenge. Aakhir mein poore chapter ka saar.

## Part A: Position se sab kuch padho

Chapter ke sab topics ko ek **recipe** mein jodte hain. Kisi bhi element ke liye (pehle 20 mein se):

1. **Electron configuration** likho (K, L, M, N mein 2, 8, 8, 2 ke hisaab se).
2. **Period** = shells ki ginti.
3. **Group:** group 1 aur 2 mein group number = valence electrons; group 13 se 18 mein group number = valence electrons + 10 (helium 2 electron ke saath group 18 mein).
4. **Valency:** valence electrons 4 tak ho to wahi; 4 se zyada ho to 8 minus valence electrons; noble gas ka 0.
5. **Type:** table mein left side metal, right side non-metal, seedhi staircase par metalloid (B aur Si).
6. **Bond:** metal + non-metal = ionic; non-metal + non-metal = covalent.

Do udaharan dekhte hain.

**Sulphur (S, atomic number 16):** configuration 2, 8, 6. Shells 3, to **period 3**. Valence electrons 6, to **group 16**. Valency 8 − 6 = **2**. Right side hai, to **non-metal**. Hydrogen se bond non-metal-non-metal, yaani **covalent**: H₂S (S ki valency 2, H ka 1, to 2 H chahiye).

**Calcium (Ca, atomic number 20):** configuration 2, 8, 8, 2. Shells 4, **period 4**. Valence electrons 2, **group 2**, valency **2**, alkaline earth **metal**. Chlorine se bond metal-non-metal, yaani **ionic**: CaCl₂.

```
   Group:   1   2   13  14  15  16  17  18
           +-------------------------------+
   P1       H                           He
   P2       Li  Be  B   C   N   O   F   Ne
   P3       Na  Mg  Al  Si  P   S   Cl  Ar
   P4       K   Ca
           +-------------------------------+
   Valency  1   2   3   4   3   2   1   0
   Type     metal (left) ... metalloid ... non-metal (right)
            (B aur Si staircase par, Al metal, H non-metal)

   Position padho:  period = shells,  group = valence electrons
                    valency = group ke hisaab se (1 2 3 4 3 2 1 0)
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Pehle 20 elements ka chhota table aur pattern ka recap: row (period) shells batati hai, column (group) valence electrons aur valency. Isi se type aur bond ka andaaza lagta hai.

Dhyan rakho, **is chapter ka sabse bada fayda** yehi hai: har element ki valency aur swabhav alag-alag ratna nahi padta, kyunki position hi sab kuch bata deti hai.

> **Jaanch:** Magnesium (period 3, group 2) ki valency 1 hogi. ❌ Nahi, group 2 ka matlab 2 valence electrons, to valency **2**.

## Part B: Clue Card Game

Ye khel aap bhi kheliye: paper ke chhote cards par clue likho aur doosre se element pakadne ko kaho. **Ye khel sirf kagaz aur pencil se hai, koi chemical nahi.**

```
   +-------------------------------+
   |   CLUE CARD  No. 1            |
   |                               |
   |   Clue 1: Main period 3 mein  |
   |           hoon.               |
   |   Clue 2: Main group 17 mein  |
   |           hoon.               |
   |                               |
   |   Main kaun hoon?             |
   +-------------------------------+
          |
          v
   Period 3 ki row:  Na Mg Al Si P S Cl Ar
   Group 17 ka column: F Cl
   Dono mein common:  Cl   -> Chlorine
```

> **Figure 6.2** – Ek clue card: pehle clue se row chuno, doosre se column; jahan dono milte hain wahi element hai.

Ab Samiksha ke solved puzzles. **Har puzzle ka sirf ek hi jawab nikalta hai** (hume dono clues se baaki sab hata kar ek bachana hai).

**Puzzle 1.** Period 3 aur group 17.
Period 3 mein group 17 wala sirf ek: **Cl** (chlorine). Ye halogen hai.

**Puzzle 2.** Meri electron configuration 2, 8, 1 hai.
Teen shells to period 3, 1 valence electron to group 1: **Na** (sodium), alkali metal.

**Puzzle 3.** Main alkaline earth metal hoon aur mere paas 3 shells hain.
Group 2 ke elements: Be (2 shells), Mg (3 shells), Ca (4 shells). 3 shells wala: **Mg** (magnesium).

**Puzzle 4.** Main noble gas hoon aur mere paas sirf 2 shells hain.
Noble gas: He (1 shell), Ne (2 shells), Ar (3 shells). Do shells wala: **Ne** (neon). Signboard ki laal roshni wala.

**Puzzle 5.** Main metalloid hoon, valency 4, aur period 3 mein hoon.
Metalloids B aur Si hain. B ki valency 3, period 2; Si ki valency 4, period 3. To **Si** (silicon).

**Puzzle 6.** Main non-metal hoon, valency 2, aur mere paas 2 shells hain.
2 shells (period 2) non-metals: C (4), N (3), O (2), F (1), Ne (0). Valency 2: **O** (oxygen).

**Puzzle 7.** Main alkali metal hoon aur mere paas 4 shells hain.
Alkali metals (pehle 20 mein): Li (2 shells), Na (3), K (4). **K** (potassium).

**Puzzle 8.** Main group 13 ka metal hoon.
Group 13: B (metalloid) aur Al (metal). Metal wala: **Al** (aluminium).

**Puzzle 9.** Mere paas sirf 1 shell hai, 1 valence electron, aur main non-metal hoon.
1 shell: H aur He. 1 valence electron wala: **H** (hydrogen). (He ke 2 electron hain.)

**Puzzle 10.** Main non-metal hoon, valency 3, aur period 3 mein hoon.
Period 3 mein valency 3: Al aur P. Al metal hai, to non-metal: **P** (phosphorus).

**Puzzle 11.** Main valency 2 wala element hoon, period 4 mein.
Period 4 mein sirf K aur Ca hain. K ki valency 1, Ca ka 2: **Ca** (calcium).

**Puzzle 12.** Main non-metal hoon, valency 3, aur mere paas 2 shells hain.
Period 2, valency 3: B (metalloid) aur N. Non-metal: **N** (nitrogen).

**Puzzle 13.** Main period 2 mein hoon aur valency 4 hai.
Period 2 mein valency 4 sirf C ka: **C** (carbon).

**Puzzle 14.** Main noble gas hoon aur mere paas 3 shells hain.
**Ar** (argon). Bulb ke andar bhari gas.

**Puzzle 15 (formula wala).** Main chlorine ke saath XCl₂ jaisa ionic compound banata hoon, aur period 4 mein hoon.
XCl₂ ka matlab X ki valency 2. Period 4 mein valency 2: **Ca**. CaCl₂ ✅.

Samiksha ne har jawab ke saath khud jaanch bhi ki. "Papa, dekho, har puzzle mein pehle clue se kuch elements gire, doosre clue se aur, aur aakhir mein ek bacha. Bilkul Guess Who? jaisa."

> **Jaanch:** "Main alkali metal hoon" sirf is ek clue se element pakda ja sakta hai. ❌ Nahi, Li, Na aur K teeno alkali metals hain. Doosra clue (shells ki ginti) zaroori hai.

## Part C: Position se formula aur bond

Cross valency ka niyam: ek element ki valency doosre ke atoms ki ginti ban jaati hai, phir common factor se chhota karte hain. Bond ka type metal aur non-metal dekh kar.

| Jodi | Valency | Formula | Bond | Kyun |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Na aur Cl | 1 aur 1 | NaCl | ionic | metal + non-metal |
| Mg aur Cl | 2 aur 1 | MgCl₂ | ionic | metal + non-metal |
| Ca aur Cl | 2 aur 1 | CaCl₂ | ionic | metal + non-metal |
| Li aur F | 1 aur 1 | LiF | ionic | metal + non-metal |
| Mg aur O | 2 aur 2 | MgO | ionic | metal + non-metal |
| Al aur O | 3 aur 2 | Al₂O₃ | ionic | metal + non-metal |
| K aur O | 1 aur 2 | K₂O | ionic | metal + non-metal |
| H aur Cl | 1 aur 1 | HCl | covalent | non-metal + non-metal |
| H aur O | 1 aur 2 | H₂O | covalent | non-metal + non-metal |
| N aur H | 3 aur 1 | NH₃ | covalent | non-metal + non-metal |
| C aur H | 4 aur 1 | CH₄ | covalent | non-metal + non-metal |
| H aur S | 1 aur 2 | H₂S | covalent | non-metal + non-metal |
| C aur Cl | 4 aur 1 | CCl₄ | covalent | non-metal + non-metal |

Ek mazedaar baat: **ek hi group ke elements ke formula same dhang ke hote hain.** Mg ke saath Cl: MgCl₂, to Ca ke saath bhi CaCl₂. Na ke saath Cl: NaCl, to K ke saath bhi KCl. O ke saath H: H₂O, to S ke saath H₂S. Yehi parivaar wali baat hai (Topic 5).

Ye formula mass bhi nikal sakte hain (Chapter "Atoms and Molecules" ke atomic masses se: Mg 24, Cl 35.5, Ca 40, Al 27, O 16):

- MgCl₂ = 24 + 2 × 35.5 = 24 + 71 = **95**
- CaCl₂ = 40 + 71 = **111**
- Al₂O₃ = 2 × 27 + 3 × 16 = 54 + 48 = **102**

Ionic bond mein electron kaise jaate hain, ek udaharan: Mg (2, 8, 2) ke 2 valence electron chhodta hai, to Mg ke paas 2, 8 bachte hain (Ne jaisa). Do Cl (2, 8, 7) har ek 1 electron leta hai, to 2, 8, 8 (Ar jaisa). Isliye 1 Mg ke saath 2 Cl: MgCl₂ ✅.

## Part D: Mixed reasoning (sab chapters ek saath)

**Example 1: Atomic number 12 wale element ki poori kundli.**
Configuration 2, 8, 2. Period 3 (3 shells), group 2 (2 valence electrons), valency 2, metal (magnesium). Chlorine ke saath MgCl₂, ionic. Kyunki Mg metal aur Cl non-metal hain. Mass 95 ✅.

**Example 2: Atomic number 16 aur hydrogen.**
Configuration 2, 8, 6, period 3, group 16, valency 2 (8 − 6), non-metal (sulphur). Formula H₂S, bond covalent (dono non-metal), formula mass 2 × 1 + 32 = 34 ✅.

**Example 3: Lithium aur fluorine.**
Li: 2, 1 (period 2, group 1). F: 2, 7 (period 2, group 17). Li ek electron deta hai, F ek leta hai: LiF, ionic, mass 7 + 19 = 26 ✅.

**Example 4: Aluminium aur oxygen.**
Al: 2, 8, 3, valency 3. O: 2, 6, valency 2. Cross valency se Al₂O₃. Check: 2 Al ke 6 electron nikalte hain, 3 O 6 electron lete hain ✅. Metal aur non-metal, to ionic.

**Example 5: Chaar elements mein odd one out.** Ne, Ar, Na, He mein alag kaun?
Ne, Ar, He noble gases (group 18) hain, Na alkali metal (group 1). Odd one: **Na**.

**Example 6: Na aur K mein zyada reactive kaun?**
Dono group 1 mein hain. K ke 4 shells, Na ke 3. K ka baahari electron nucleus se zyada door, to aasani se nikalta hai: **K zyada reactive** (Topic 4 aur 5).

**Example 7: F aur Cl mein zyada reactive halogen kaun?**
Halogen mein reactivity group mein neeche jaate hue ghatti hai, to **F zyada reactive**.

**Example 8: Table ki bhavishyavani: Rb ke baare mein.**
Rubidium (Rb) group 1 mein, K ke theek neeche aata hai (5 shells). Bina padhe hum bata sakte hain: valency 1, soft, chaandi jaisa, paani ke saath K se bhi zyada tez reaction, formula RbCl. Hum ye sirf padhne ke liye keh rahe hain, kisi ko reaction karna nahi hai. Mendeleev ne bhi isi tarah gallium ki bhavishyavani ki thi (Topic 2).

**Example 9: Ek anjaan element X ki valency 3 hai, 3 shells, aur ek metal hai.**
Group 13 (valency 3), period 3: **Al**. Oxygen ke saath Al₂O₃, ionic.

**Example 10: Kya hum bata sakte hain ki H₂O mein bond kaisa hai?**
H aur O dono non-metal, to covalent. Aur formula: H ki valency 1, O ki 2, to 2 H + 1 O = H₂O ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Period 3, group 1 wala element Li hoga"**
Li period 2 mein hai. Period 3 aur group 1: Na.

**Galti 2: "Group 17 ki valency 7 hai"**
Group 17 mein 7 valence electrons hain, par valency 8 − 7 = **1**.

**Galti 3: "Valency hamesha valence electrons ke barabar hota hai"**
Sirf 4 valence electrons tak. 4 se zyada hon to valency = 8 minus valence electrons (aur noble gas ki 0).

**Galti 4: "Ek clue se hi element mil jaata hai"**
Kai elements ek group ya period mein hote hain. Do clue lagte hain.

**Galti 5: "Metal aur non-metal ka bond covalent hota hai"**
Metal + non-metal = ionic. Non-metal + non-metal = covalent.

**Galti 6: "Hydrogen group 1 ka metal hai"**
Hydrogen non-metal gas hai. Convenience ke liye group 1 ke upar rakha gaya hai.

## Quick Recap

- Table padhne ka tarika: configuration, period = shells, group = valence electrons.
- Valency: 1, 2, 3, 4, 3, 2, 1, 0 (group 1, 2, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18).
- Metal left mein, non-metal right mein, B aur Si staircase par.
- Metal + non-metal: ionic; non-metal + non-metal: covalent.
- Cross valency se formula: MgCl₂, Al₂O₃, NH₃, CH₄.
- Ek group ke elements ke formula same dhang ke hote hain (NaCl aur KCl).
- Clue card puzzle mein do clue chahiye, taaki sirf ek jawab bache.

## Practice Questions

1. An element has the electron configuration 2, 5. Find its period, group, valency, whether it is a metal or non-metal, and write its compound with hydrogen.

2. Solve the clue card: "I am in period 3 and in group 2."

3. Solve the clue card: "I am a non-metal gas, I have 2 shells and my valency is 1."

4. Solve the clue card: "I have 3 valence electrons and 3 shells, and I am a metal."

5. Solve the clue card: "I am a noble gas with 1 shell."

6. Solve the clue card: "I am an alkali metal with 2 shells."

7. Solve the clue card: "My valency is 2, I am in period 3, and I am a non-metal."

8. Why does the clue "I am an alkaline earth metal" alone not give a single answer among the first 20 elements? Give one more clue to fix it.

9. Write the formula and bond type of the compound between: (a) Ca and Cl, (b) Al and O, (c) C and H, (d) K and O. Calculate the formula mass of (c) and (d).

10. Using the electron configurations, explain how CaCl₂ is formed.

11. Strontium (Sr) comes just below calcium in Group 2. Predict its valency, how its reactivity compares with calcium and the formula of its chloride.

12. Between Mg and Ca, which one is more reactive, and which one has more shells? Explain in one or two lines.

13. Give the position (period and group) of the element with atomic number 19, and predict the formula of its oxide.

14. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) Group 17 elements have valency 7.
    (b) NH₃ is a covalent compound.
    (c) Na and Cl form a covalent compound.
    (d) The elements of the same group have the same valency.

### Answers

1. Configuration 2, 5: 2 shells, to **period 2**. Valence electrons 5, to group 5 + 10 = **15**. Valency 8 − 5 = **3**. Right side ka, to **non-metal** (nitrogen, Z = 2 + 5 = 7). Hydrogen (valency 1) ke saath criss-cross se **NH₃** (covalent, dono non-metal) ✅.

2. Period 3 ka matlab Na se Ar tak. Group 2 ka matlab 2 valence electron. Dono mila kar: **Mg** (magnesium).

3. 2 shells (period 2), non-metal gas, valency 1: **F** (fluorine) (period 2 mein group 17, valency 8 − 7 = 1).

4. 3 valence electrons aur 3 shells: group 13, period 3. Metal hai, to **Al** (aluminium).

5. 1 shell wale noble gas: **He** (helium). H ek noble gas nahi hai.

6. Alkali metal, 2 shells: **Li** (lithium).

7. Period 3, valency 2: Mg (2, 8, 2) aur S (2, 8, 6, valency 8 − 6 = 2). Mg metal hai, non-metal chahiye, to **S** (sulphur).

8. Alkaline earth metals mein Be, Mg aur Ca teeno aate hain. Ek aur clue: "mere paas 3 shells hain" to **Mg**, ya "mere paas 4 shells hain" to **Ca**.

9. (a) **CaCl₂**, ionic. (b) **Al₂O₃**, ionic. (c) **CH₄**, covalent, mass 12 + 4 × 1 = **16**. (d) **K₂O**, ionic (K valency 1, O valency 2), mass 2 × 39 + 16 = 78 + 16 = **94**.

10. Ca (2, 8, 8, 2) apne 2 valence electrons chhodta hai, to uske paas 2, 8, 8 bachta hai (Ar jaisa). Do Cl (2, 8, 7) mein se har ek 1 electron leta hai, to dono 2, 8, 8 (Ar jaisa) ban jaate hain. Isliye 1 Ca + 2 Cl = **CaCl₂**, aur ye ionic bond hai.

11. Sr group 2 mein hai, to **valency 2**. Group 2 mein bhi neeche jaate hue reactivity badhti hai (shells zyada, baahari electron door), to Sr, Ca se **zyada reactive**. Chloride: Sr (2) aur Cl (1) se **SrCl₂**, bilkul CaCl₂ jaisa.

12. **Ca zyada reactive** hai, aur uske **4 shells** hain (Mg ke 3). Neeche jaate hue shells badhte hain, baahari electron door hota hai, aur aasani se nikalta hai.

13. Atomic number 19 ka matlab K: configuration 2, 8, 8, 1, to **period 4, group 1**, valency 1. Oxide: **K₂O**.

14. (a) **False** ❌. Group 17 mein 7 valence electron hain par valency 1 hai (8 − 7). (b) **True** ✅. N aur H dono non-metal hain. (c) **False** ❌. Na metal aur Cl non-metal hai, to NaCl **ionic** hai. (d) **True** ✅. Ek group ke elements ke valence electrons same hote hain, to valency bhi same.
