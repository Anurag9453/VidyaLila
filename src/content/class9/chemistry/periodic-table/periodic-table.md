# Topic 1: Elements Ko Sajane Ki Koshish: Dobereiner aur Newlands

Chapter 7 ke aakhir mein ek sawaal chhoda tha: kya itne saare tatva (elements), jinke swabhav itne alag hain, ek orderly chart mein aa sakte hain? ## Harmonium ki chaabiyan

Mr. Karnik ke ghar ek purana harmonium rakha tha. Unki bhatiji Mitali ne ek din dhool jhaadkar uski chaabiyan dabayi: **Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni**, aur phir agli chaabi par wahi **Sa** dobara sunai diya, bas thoda unchi awaaz mein. "Chacha, ye kya? Naya swar aaya aur wahi purana sunai de raha hai!"

Mr. Karnik hanse. "Isse **saptak** kehte hain. Saat swar, aur aathvi chaabi par pehle swar jaisa hi swar aata hai, bas unchi pitch par. Isi ko **octave** bhi kehte hain, yaani 'aathva'. Harmonium par kitni bhi chaabiyan hon, har aathvi chaabi par wahi swabhav wapas aa jaata hai."

Mitali ne chaabiyan ginkar dekhi. "Sa se shuru karun to Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni, aur aathvi chaabi phir Sa. Sach mein! To kya chaabiyan ek order mein lagi hain aur har aathvi dohrati hai?" Mr. Karnik ne kaha, "Bilkul. Ab ek sawaal soch: agar tatvon ko bhi ek line mein sahi order mein rakhein, to kya unmein bhi aisa koi dohrana ho sakta hai? Kuch vaigyanikon ne yehi socha tha. Chalo, unki kahani dekhte hain: kahan unhe jeet mili aur kahan wo atak gaye."

## Part A: Tatvon ko order mein kyun rakhein?

Tatva ek hi padarth ki sabse saral kism hain (Chapter 2). Aaj tak insaan ne lagbhag **118** tatva pehchane hain. Itni badi ginti hai ki agar har tatva ke gun alag-alag ratne padein to kaam bahut mushkil ho jaaye.

Ye waisa hai jaise ek badi library mein hazaaron kitaabein **bina shelf aur section ke** pade hon. Kitaab dhoondhna mushkil. Par jab kitaabon ko subject ke hisaab se rakh dein, to ek shelf dekhte hi pata chal jaata hai ki kya milega.

Tatvon ke liye bhi vargikaran (classification) ke teen fayde hain:

1. **Yaad rakhna aasan:** ek samooh ka swabhav pata ho to us samooh ke har tatva ke baare mein andaaza lag jaata hai.
2. **Tulna aasan:** ek jaise tatva paas-paas mil jaate hain.
3. **Andaaza lagana:** kisi naye tatva ki khoj ho to uska samooh dekhkar swabhav ka pata chal sakta hai.

Sabse achhi vargikaran wahi hogi jo teesra kaam kar sake, yaani **bhavishyavani**. Is baat ko yaad rakho, ye aage kaam aayegi.

### Sabse pehli koshish: metals aur non-metals

Sabse puraani aur saral vargikaran Chapter 2 mein hum dekh chuke hain: tatvon ko **metals** aur **non-metals** mein baanta gaya (aur beech ke kuch **metalloids**). Ye baat ki shuruaat Lavoisier jaise vaigyanikon ke samay se maani jaati hai. Par ye vargikaran bahut mota hai: sirf do-teen dibbe. Sodium aur copper dono metal hain, par ek chaaku se kat jaata hai aur doosre ko kaatna bhi mushkil hai. Aur is baat se ye nahi pata chalta ki kaun sa metal kaun se metal jaisa hai. Isliye aur gehri vargikaran ki zaroorat padi.

## Part B: Dobereiner ke triads (1829)

Jab vaigyanikon ne tatvon ke atomic mass naapna shuru kiya, to ek dilchasp baat saamne aayi. **Johann Dobereiner** (1829) ne dekha ki kuch tatva **teen-teen ke gucchhe** mein aise hain ki unke gun milte-julte hain, aur gucchhe ke **beech wale tatva ka atomic mass lagbhag baaki do ke atomic mass ke average (ausat) jaisa** hota hai. Ye teen ka samooh **triad** kehlaya.

Teen triads dekhte hain (atomic mass Chapter 4 ki table aur aam table se):

| Triad | Pehla tatva | Beech ka tatva | Teesra tatva | Pehle aur teesre ka average | Beech ka asli mass |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Triad 1 (Li, Na, K) | Li = 7 | Na = 23 | K = 39 | (7 + 39) / 2 = 23 | 23 |
| Triad 2 (Ca, Sr, Ba) | Ca = 40 | Sr = 87.6 | Ba = 137.3 | (40 + 137.3) / 2 = 88.65 | 87.6 |
| Triad 3 (Cl, Br, I) | Cl = 35.5 | Br = 79.9 | I = 126.9 | (35.5 + 126.9) / 2 = 81.2 | 79.9 |

Dhyan do: Na ke liye average **bilkul** 23 aaya, aur baaki dono mein bhi bahut kareeb (88.65 ke saamne 87.6, aur 81.2 ke saamne 79.9). Ye koi sanyog nahi lagta.

```
   Triad: Li, Na, K  (alkali family jaisa swabhav)

        Li              Na                   K
        7               23                   39
        7 ----- 16 ---> 23 ------ 16 ------> 39

   Average of Li aur K = (7 + 39) / 2 = 23   =  Na ka mass
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Triad: Li (7), Na (23), K (39). Li se Na tak 16 ka fark aur Na se K tak bhi 16 ka fark hai, isliye beech wale Na ka mass baaki do ke average ke barabar hai. Teeno ke gun bhi milte-julte hain: teeno narm, chaandi jaise, aur reaction mein tez.

Is triad ke kuch gun (sirf padhne ke liye, koi activity nahi): Li, Na, K teeno narm metals hain jo chaaku se kat sakte hain, tel ke neeche rakhe jaate hain, aur paani se tez reaction karte hain (iske baare mein aage Topic 5 mein aayega). Cl, Br, I ka triad: teeno non-metals hain, jinke rang gehre hote hain (Cl peeli-hari gas, Br lal-bhoora liquid, I baingani-kaala thos), aur teeno ki valency 1 hai. Cl aur Br zehreeli bhi hain.

### Dobereiner kyun atak gaye?

Dobereiner ka vichaar achha tha, par usme ek bada kamzor pehlu tha: **unhe sirf kuch hi triads mile.** Sabhi tatva teen-teen ke triad mein nahi aate the. Aur jin tatvon ke gun kaafi milte-julte the, unke liye bhi average wala niyam sabhi jagah thik nahi baithta tha. Unka tareeka poore tatvon ke liye kaam nahi kar sakta tha, isliye sirf chhote hisse tak seemit raha.

Phir bhi unka kaam bekaar nahi gaya: unhone ek aham baat dikha di ki **atomic mass aur swabhav ke beech koi rishta hai.**

## Part C: Newlands ka octave ka niyam (1866)

Dobereiner ke kareeb 37 saal baad, **John Newlands** (1866) ne ek aur kadam aage badhaya. Unhone tatvon ko **atomic mass ke badhte order** mein ek line mein likha. Phir dekha ki jab line mein aage badhte hain to **har aathva tatva** pehle tatva jaisa swabhav dikhata hai. Bilkul harmonium ki tarah, jahan aathvi chaabi par wahi swar dobara aata hai! Isi liye is niyam ko **law of octaves** kaha gaya.

Chalo, hum bhi ye pehle-17 tatvon par karke dekhte hain, jo us samay maloom the. Dhyan do: us waqt noble gases (He, Ne, Ar) **abhi khoje nahi gaye the**, isliye is list mein wo nahi hain. Atomic mass Chapter 4 ke hain:

H 1, Li 7, Be 9, B 11, C 12, N 14, O 16, F 19, Na 23, Mg 24, Al 27, Si 28, P 31, S 32, Cl 35.5, K 39, Ca 40

Kul 17 tatva. Ab inhe saat-saat ki rows mein likho, aur har column ko ek "swar" maano:

```
        Sa     Re     Ga     Ma     Pa     Dha    Ni
       ----   ----   ----   ----   ----   ----   ----
        H      Li     Be     B      C      N      O
        F      Na     Mg     Al     Si     P      S
        Cl     K      Ca

   Column dekho (upar se neeche):
     Re:  Li, Na, K       (ek jaise, narm metals)
     Ga:  Be, Mg, Ca      (ek jaise, valency 2)
     Sa:  H, F, Cl        (valency 1 wale)
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Newlands ka octave: 17 tatva atomic mass ke badhte order mein saat-saat ki rows mein, har column ek "swar" ki tarah. Li, Na, K ek column mein aate hain; Be, Mg, Ca bhi ek column mein. Har aathve tatva par "swar" dohrata hai: Li se ginti shuru karo to Li 1, Be 2, B 3, C 4, N 5, O 6, F 7, aur aathva Na hai.

Ye dekhkar kamaal lagta hai: **Li, Na, K** aur **Be, Mg, Ca** sach mein ek jaise gun wale tatva hain (Chapter 5 mein dekha tha ki inke baahari electrons ki ginti same hai, par Newlands ke zamane mein ye baat koi nahi jaanta tha). Ek chhoti si jaanch hum khud bhi kar sakte hain (ye Dobereiner ki 1829 wali list ka triad nahi hai, bas baad mein dekha gaya ek udaharan hai): Be, Mg, Ca par triad wala hisaab lagao to (9 + 40) / 2 = 24.5, aur Mg ka asli mass 24 hai, kaafi kareeb.

### Newlands ka niyam kahan toota?

Ye khoobsurat vichaar bhi zyada door nahi chal saka. Kamzoriyan:

1. **Sirf calcium tak chala.** Ca ke baad naya tatva rakhne par har aathva wala niyam theek nahi baithta tha. Chaabiyan to aur bhi hain, par octave ki dohrana band ho gayi.
2. **Noble gases ka pata chalte hi gadbad.** Jab He, Ne, Ar khoje gaye aur line mein jode gaye, to Li ke baad **aathva** nahi, balki **nauva** tatva Na ban gaya: Li, Be, B, C, N, O, F, **Ne**, Na. To "har aathva" wala simple niyam hi toot gaya. (Hisaab check karo: Li se Na tak ab 9 steps hain, 8 nahi.)
3. **Alag swabhav wale tatva ek hi column mein.** Octave poora karne ke chakkar mein kuch tatva aisi jagah pahunch gaye jahan unka swabhav baaki column se bilkul nahi milta tha. Jaise Newlands ki poori table mein metal cobalt aur nickel ko ek hi khaane mein, F aur Cl jaise non-metals wale column mein rakhna pada.
4. **Khaali jagah nahi chhodi.** Newlands ne har jagah tatva bhar diya. Par kya pata ki kuch tatva abhi khoje hi nahi gaye hon? Unke liye unki table mein koi jagah nahi thi.

Phir bhi Newlands ki ek badi seekh: **tatvon ke gun ek order mein dohrate hain.** Ye wahi **periodic** (baar-baar dohrane wala) pattern hai jo aage ke Periodic Table ka naam banta hai.

### Kaun kitna aage badha? Ek tulna

| Koshish | Kya kiya | Achhi baat | Kamzori |
|---|---|---|---|
| Metals aur non-metals | Tatvon ko do bade samooh | Saral, kaam ki shuruaat | Bahut mota, andar ke farq nahi dikhte |
| Dobereiner (1829) | Teen-teen ke triads, beech ka mass = average | Mass aur swabhav ka rishta dikhaya | Bahut kam triads mile |
| Newlands (1866) | Mass ke order mein, har aathva dohrata | Dohrane (periodicity) ka vichaar | Ca ke baad toota, noble gases se bigda, alag tatva ek column mein |

## Part D: Paper card activity (ghar par, bilkul safe)

Ye activity sirf kagaz aur pen se hai: **koi tatva, koi chemical, koi aag nahi.** Hum is activity ko is chapter ke agle topics mein bhi use karenge, isliye cards sambhaal kar rakhna.

**Saaman:** 20 chhote kagaz ke tukde (visiting-card jitne), pen, ek bada sa table ya farsh.

**Step 1: 20 cards banao.** Pehle 20 tatvon (H se Ca tak) ke liye ek-ek card. Har card par likho:

- upar bada **symbol** (jaise Na)
- neeche chhota **atomic number Z** (jaise 11)
- aur **valence electrons** (baahari electrons, Chapter 5 ki table se; jaise Na ke liye 1)

```
   +---------+   +---------+   +---------+   +---------+
   |   Li    |   |   Na    |   |   K     |   |   Cl    |
   |  Z = 3  |   |  Z = 11 |   |  Z = 19 |   |  Z = 17 |
   |  e = 1  |   |  e = 1  |   |  e = 1  |   |  e = 7  |
   +---------+   +---------+   +---------+   +---------+
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Paper cards ke namune: har card par symbol, atomic number Z aur valence electrons (e). Li, Na, K ke cards par valence electrons ek jaise (1) hain, jabki Cl par 7.

**Step 2: Newlands ki tarah sajao.** Cards ko pehle **mixed** kar do. Phir He, Ne, Ar ke teen cards ek taraf rakh do (jaise ki wo abhi khoje nahi gaye). Baaki 17 cards ko atomic mass ke order mein (H 1, Li 7, Be 9 ... Ca 40) saat-saat ki rows mein sajao, jaise Figure 1.2 mein. Dekho: kaun se column ek jaise lagte hain? Kahan atakna pada?

**Step 3: Ab He, Ne, Ar ke cards bhi jodo.** Inhe Z ke hisaab se apni jagah par ghusao: He ko H ke baad, Ne ko F ke baad, aur Ar ko Cl ke baad. Ab Li se phir ginti karo: aathve number par Ne aata hai, aur Na nauve number par khisak jaata hai (Example 6 mein yahi hisaab hai).

**Step 4: Ek chhota sa khel.** Sabhi 20 cards ko ab **Z ke order mein** (1 se 20) line mein rakho. H aur He ko upar alag rakho, phir Li se shuru karke **aath-aath** ke rows banao: Li se Ne, Na se Ar, aur K, Ca. Har card ke niche likhe **valence electrons** padho. Kuch dikha? (Abhi ise bas **note** kar lo. Iska raaz hum Topic 3 mein kholenge.)

**Dhyan rakhna:** ye bas kagaz ka khel hai. Cards ko kisi chemical ke paas mat le jaao, aur mat sochna ki cards par likhe tatva ghar mein kahin se laane hain. Tatvon ko khud chhoone ya sunghne ki zaroorat nahi.

## Part E: Solved examples

### Example 1. Triad ka beech wala tatva

Li ka mass 7 aur K ka mass 39 hai. Dobereiner ke niyam se Na ka mass kya hona chahiye?

- Average = (7 + 39) / 2 = 46 / 2 = **23**.
- Asli Na ka mass 23 hai ✅. Niyam bilkul sahi baitha.

### Example 2. Ca, Sr, Ba ka triad

Ca = 40, Ba = 137.3. Sr ka andaaza lagao aur asli 87.6 se milao.

- Average = (40 + 137.3) / 2 = 177.3 / 2 = **88.65**.
- Asli Sr = 87.6. Fark = 88.65 − 87.6 = 1.05. Bahut chhota fark, to niyam lagbhag sahi ✅.

### Example 3. Cl, Br, I ka triad

Cl = 35.5, I = 126.9. Br ka andaaza?

- Average = (35.5 + 126.9) / 2 = 162.4 / 2 = **81.2**.
- Asli Br = 79.9. Fark = 81.2 − 79.9 = 1.3. Phir se kareeb ✅. (Cl, Br, I teeno ki valency 1 hai.)

### Example 4. Kya Li, Be, B ek triad ho sakte hain?

Li = 7, B = 11. Average = (7 + 11) / 2 = 9, jo Be ka mass hai. Par kya Li, Be, B ek jaise hain? Nahi: Li valency 1 wala narm metal hai, Be valency 2 aur B valency 3 wala (metalloid hai). Unke gun milte-julte nahi hain. **Sirf mass ka average mil jaana triad banane ke liye kaafi nahi.** Gun bhi milne chahiye. (Isi liye Dobereiner ne gun dekh kar hi triad choose kiye.)

### Example 5. Octave: aathva kaun?

Newlands ki list (noble gases ke bina): H, Li, Be, B, C, N, O, F, Na, Mg, Al, Si, P, S, Cl, K, Ca.

(a) Li se ginti shuru karo: Li 1, Be 2, B 3, C 4, N 5, O 6, F 7, **Na 8**. Aathva tatva **Na** hai, jo Li jaisa hai ✅.

(b) Be se shuru karo: Be 1, B 2, C 3, N 4, O 5, F 6, Na 7, **Mg 8**. Aathva **Mg**, jo Be jaisa hai ✅.

(c) F se shuru karo: F 1, Na 2, Mg 3, Al 4, Si 5, P 6, S 7, **Cl 8**. Aathva **Cl**, jo F jaisa (valency 1) hai ✅.

### Example 6. Noble gas judne se kya bigda?

Newlands ki line mein Ne ko F ke baad (Z ke hisaab se) rakho: Li, Be, B, C, N, O, F, **Ne**, Na.

- Li se ginti: Li 1, Be 2, B 3, C 4, N 5, O 6, F 7, Ne 8, **Na 9**.
- Ab Na **nauva** hai, aathva nahi. Li jaisa Na pehle aathva tha, ab nauva. To simple niyam "har aathva" toot gaya ❌.

### Example 7. Cards se Newlands ki row

Cards (17) saat-saat ki rows mein rakhne par kitni rows aur kitne bache?

- 17 ÷ 7 = 2 rows poori (14 cards), aur 17 − 14 = 3 cards bache (Cl, K, Ca) ✅.
- Isliye teesri row mein sirf 3 tatva hain, jaisa Figure 1.2 mein.

### Example 8. Library vs. periodic

Ek student bolta hai, "Dobereiner aur Newlands dono ne sahi tatva ek saath rakhe, to dono ka kaam barabar hai." Sahi ya galat?

- Galat ❌. Dobereiner ne sirf **kuch** triads dhoonde. Newlands ne **saare** tatvon ko ek line mein rakhne ki koshish ki aur dohrane ka vichaar diya, par wo bhi Ca ke baad toot gaya. Dono ke vichaar aham the, par dono adhoore.

## Part F: Ek zaroori seekh: achha vargikaran bhavishyavani karta hai

Dobereiner aur Newlands, dono ne ek jaisi cheez dhoondhi: **atomic mass ke saath gunon ka pattern.** Dono kuch had tak sahi the, par dono ek sawaal ka jawab nahi de paye: **agar table mein koi jagah khaali ho to?** Kya wo koi aisa tatva hai jo abhi khoja nahi gaya? Newlands ne kabhi khaali jagah chhodi hi nahi, aur jahan fit nahi hua wahan zabardasti fit kiya.

Ek achhi table wo hoti hai jo na sirf jo hai usse sajaye, balki **jo abhi nahi mila uski bhi bhavishyavani** kar sake. Aisi table banane ke liye kisi ko do himmat bhari baatein karni thi: (1) agar pattern ke hisaab se tatva fit nahi baithe to khaali jagah chhod dena, aur (2) us khaali jagah ke tatva ka swabhav pehle se bata dena. Kya ye sambhav hua? Ye kahani agle topic mein.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Triad matlab koi bhi teen tatva"**

Triad mein sirf wahi teen aate hain jinke **gun milte-julte** hon **aur** beech wale ka mass baaki do ke average ke kareeb ho. Example 4 mein dekha ki Li, Be, B ka average mil jaata hai par gun nahi milte.

**Galti 2: "Average bilkul barabar hona chahiye"**

Triad 2 aur 3 mein average asli mass se thoda alag aaya (88.65 vs 87.6; 81.2 vs 79.9). Niyam **lagbhag** sahi hai, bilkul barabar nahi.

**Galti 3: "Newlands ne noble gases ko line mein rakha tha"**

Nahi. Noble gases us samay khoje hi nahi gaye the. Unke judne se hi octave ka niyam bigda.

**Galti 4: "Octave ka matlab aathva tatva alag hota hai"**

Octave mein aathva tatva **pehle jaisa** lagta hai, bilkul harmonium ki aathvi chaabi ki tarah.

**Galti 5: "Dobereiner aur Newlands ne galat kaam kiya, isliye bekaar the"**

Dono ne aham baatein dikhayi (mass aur swabhav ka rishta, aur dohrana). Wahi baatein aage kaam aayi. Ye vigyaan ka tareeka hai: pehle ke vichaar ko sudhaar kar aage badhna.

**Galti 6: "Mass hi sab kuch tay karta hai"**

Aaj hum jaante hain ki sahi basis atomic number hai, mass nahi (Chapter 5, Ar aur K wali baat). Par Dobereiner aur Newlands ke samay ye maloom nahi tha.

## Quick Recap

- Tatva 100 se zyada hain (aaj 118 pehchane gaye hain). Ek vyavasthit vargikaran se yaad rakhna, tulna karna aur bhavishyavani karna aasan hota hai.
- Pehli saral koshish: **metals aur non-metals**. Par ye bahut mota tha.
- **Dobereiner (1829) ke triads:** teen tatva ek jaise gun ke, beech ka mass lagbhag baaki do ka average. Li, Na, K: (7 + 39) / 2 = 23. Ca, Sr, Ba: (40 + 137.3) / 2 = 88.65 (Sr = 87.6). Cl, Br, I: (35.5 + 126.9) / 2 = 81.2 (Br = 79.9). Kamzori: bahut kam triads mile.
- **Newlands (1866) ka law of octaves:** mass ke order mein har aathva tatva pehle jaisa, harmonium ki aathvi chaabi ki tarah. Li, Na, K aur Be, Mg, Ca column mein ek saath aaye.
- Kamzori: Ca ke baad toota, noble gases (jo tab tak khoje nahi the) judne se "aathva" nauva ho gaya, alag tatva ek column mein, aur nayi khoj ke liye jagah nahi.
- Seekh: tatvon ke gun **periodic** (dohrate) hain, aur ek achhi table ko **bhavishyavani** bhi karni chahiye.

### Answers

1. Tatva bahut zyada hain (118). Vargikaran se **yaad rakhna aasan** hota hai, ek jaise tatva paas-paas mil jaate hain (**tulna aasan**), aur ek samooh ka swabhav jaanne par uske baare mein **andaaza** lag sakta hai.

2. **Triad** = teen tatva jinke gun milte-julte hon aur beech wale ka mass baaki do ke average ke kareeb ho. Li = 7, K = 39: average = (7 + 39) / 2 = 46 / 2 = **23**, aur Na = 23 ✅. Teeno narm, chaandi jaise, tez reaction karne wale metals hain, to Li, Na, K triad hain.

3. Ca, Sr, Ba: (40 + 137.3) / 2 = **88.65**, asli Sr = 87.6, fark 1.05, kareeb par bilkul barabar nahi. Cl, Br, I: (35.5 + 126.9) / 2 = **81.2**, asli Br = 79.9, fark 1.3. Dono triads Dobereiner ke vichaar ko lagbhag maante hain, par bilkul barabar nahi.

4. Dobereiner ko **sirf kuch hi triads** mile. Zyadatar tatva kisi triad mein nahi aa paye, to unka tareeka saare tatvon ko vyavasthit nahi kar saka.

5. Newlands ne tatvon ko **atomic mass ke badhte order** mein rakha aur dekha ki **har aathva tatva** pehle jaisa gun dikhata hai. Ye saptak jaisa tha: saat swar ke baad aathva swar pehle jaisa sunai deta hai, isliye ise **law of octaves** kaha gaya.

6. (a) B 1, C 2, N 3, O 4, F 5, Na 6, Mg 7, **Al 8**. Al, B jaisa hai (dono valency 3). (b) N 1, O 2, F 3, Na 4, Mg 5, Al 6, Si 7, **P 8**. P, N jaisa hai (dono valency 3) ✅.

7. (i) Noble gases us samay khoje nahi the, aur jab jode gaye to "har aathva" wala niyam toot gaya. (ii) Alag swabhav wale tatva ek hi column mein aa gaye. (iii) Naye tatvon ke liye khaali jagah nahi thi. (Koi do likho.)

8. Noble gases jodne par Li ke baad ginti hoti hai: Li 1, Be 2, B 3, C 4, N 5, O 6, F 7, Ne 8, **Na 9**. Ab Na **nauva** hai, aathva nahi, to "har aathva" wala niyam ab sahi nahi baithta ❌.

9. Average = (12 + 16) / 2 = 28 / 2 = **14**, jo N ka mass hai. Par C (valency 4), N (valency 3) aur O (valency 2) ke gun milte-julte nahi hain (Example 4 ke Li, Be, B jaisa hi case). Isliye **ye triad nahi hai**: gun milna bhi zaroori hai, sirf average nahi.

10. **Dobereiner:** triads, beech ka mass average, par bahut kam triads. **Newlands:** mass ke order mein, har aathva dohrata, par Ca ke baad toota aur noble gases se bigda. **Dono ne** ye idea diya ki tatvon ke gun atomic mass ke saath **ek pattern** mein badalte ya dohrate hain.

11. Har card par **symbol, atomic number Z aur valence electrons** likho. 17 cards ko mass ke order mein 7-7 ki rows mein rakhne par 2 poori rows (14 cards) aur teesri row mein 3 cards (Cl, K, Ca) aati hain. Columns mein Li, Na, K aur Be, Mg, Ca ek saath aate hain. Ca ke baad aage rakhna mushkil ho jaata hai.

12. (a) **True** ✅. (b) **False** ❌. Mass lagbhag average ke barabar hota hai, bilkul nahi (88.65 vs 87.6). (c) **False** ❌. Noble gases baad mein khoje gaye. (d) **True** ✅. Achhi vargikaran naye tatvon ki bhavishyavani bhi kar sake.

Mr. Karnik muskuraye. "Bilkul. Dobereiner aur Newlands ne pehle swar pakde. Par poora saptak sunane ke liye ek aur dimaag chahiye tha."

**Aage kya?** Newlands ke kuch saal baad (1869) ek vaigyanik ne ek aisi table banayi jisme usne **jaan-boojhkar khaali jagah chhodi**, aur un khaali jagahon ke tatvon ke gun pehle se bata diye. Baad mein jab wo tatva sach mein mile to unke gun usi ke aas-paas nikle! Agle topic **"Mendeleev Ka Periodic Table"** mein is mashhoor table ki kahani, uski khubiyan aur uski kamiyan dekhenge.
