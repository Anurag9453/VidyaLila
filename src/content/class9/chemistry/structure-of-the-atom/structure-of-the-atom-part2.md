# Topic 2: Atom Ke Model: Thomson Se Rutherford Tak

Topic 1 mein humne atom ke andar ke teen kan pehchane: negative **electron**, positive **proton** aur bina charge ka **neutron**. Ye bhi dekha ki atom upar se neutral kyun hota hai. Lekin ek bada sawaal bacha hai: ye teen kan atom ke andar **rakhe kahan hain**? Sab ek gole mein ghul-mil kar? Ya alag-alag jagah par? Atom itna chhota hai ki use seedha dekha nahi ja sakta. To scientists ne kaise pata lagaya? Is topic mein wahi detective-kahani hai: pehle ek **galat-sa model**, phir ek **jaan-bujh kar kiya gaya prayog**, aur phir ek naya model jo prayog ke saboot se nikla.

---

## Chadar ke peeche kya chhupa hai?

Mr. Vora ke society ke aangan mein shaam ko bachchon ka jamghat tha. Unka bhatija Ayaan bhi wahin tha. Mr. Vora ne aangan ke beech ek lambi bedsheet taangi, aur uske peeche **chhupkar** ek cheez rakh di. Kisi ko pata nahi tha ki peeche kya hai: ek bada gola ya ek patla khamba, ya kuch bhi nahi.

"Ab khel ye hai," Mr. Vora ne kaha, "tum chadar ke peeche dekh nahi sakte. Bas kanche (marbles) phenko, aur dekho wo kaise laut-te hain. Phir andaza lagao ki peeche kya hai."

Ayaan ne kai kanche phenke. Zyadatar kanche chadar ke is paar se **seedhe nikal gaye** ya chadar ke peeche se aaram se bahar aa gaye. Kuch kanche **thode tedhe** ho kar nikle. Aur ek kancha, bas ek, **seedha ulta laut aaya**, jaise kisi deewar se takra gaya ho.

Ayaan ne socha. "Chacha, zyadatar kanche seedhe nikle, to peeche zyada jagah khaali hai. Par ek kancha ulta aaya, to kahin **bahut sakht aur bhaari** cheez hai. Aur wo cheez **chhoti** hogi, tabhi to zyadatar kanche use chhoo bhi nahi paaye."

"Shabaash!" Mr. Vora ne chadar hata di. Peeche ek patla, mazboot khamba tha. "Tumne bina dekhe sirf kanchon ke raaste dekh kar andaza laga liya. Ek scientist ne atom ke saath bilkul yahi kiya tha."

## Part A: Thomson ka model: tarbooz aur pudding

Topic 1 mein dekha tha ki J. J. Thomson ne **1897** mein electron khoja. Ab ek naya sawaal aaya: agar atom mein negative electron hain, aur atom neutral hai, to **positive charge kahan hai**? Thomson ne **1904** mein ek model diya.

Unki tasveer ye thi: atom ek **positive charge wala gola** hai, jisme positive charge **poore gole mein phaila** hua hai. Is gole ke andar **electron jade hue** hain, jaise tarbooz mein beej. Ya Christmas pudding mein kishmish. Dono ko milakar atom neutral hai: gole ka positive charge aur electronon ka negative charge barabar.

```
        Thomson ka model

        +  +  +  +  +  +
     +  +  -  +  +  -  +  +
     +  -  +  +  +  +  -  +
     +  +  +  -  +  +  +  +
     +  -  +  +  +  -  +  +
     +  +  -  +  +  +  +  +
        +  +  +  +  +  +

     "+" = positive charge (poore gole mein phaila)
     "-" = electron (beej ki tarah jade hue)
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Thomson ka model: positive charge poore gole mein faila hua, aur uske andar electron jade hue, jaise tarbooz mein beej ya pudding mein kishmish. Is tasveer mein atom ke andar kahin khaali jagah nahi hai: poora gola bhara hua hai.

Ye model us samay ke liye achchha tha, kyunki ye teen baatein samjhata tha: atom mein electron hain, atom neutral hai, aur positive charge bhi hai. Lekin vigyan mein ek achchhe model ki pehchan ye hoti hai ki use **parkha** ja sake. Thomson ka model ek saaf baat kehta tha: **atom andar se ek jaisa bhara hua hai.** Ye baat jaanchi ja sakti thi. Ye jaanch Rutherford ne ki.

## Part B: Rutherford ka gold-foil prayog

**Ernest Rutherford** (aur unke saathiyon) ne **1911** ke aas-paas ek prayog kiya jo aaj bhi vigyan ke sabse mashhoor prayogon mein hai. Idea wahi tha jo Mr. Vora ke aangan ka khel: atom ko seedha dekh nahi sakte, to us par **goliyan** phenko aur dekho wo kaise laut-ti hain.

**Set-up (taiyaari):**

- **Goli:** alpha particles. Ye **positive charge** wale hote hain aur bahut **tez** chalte hain.
- **Target:** sone ki ek **bahut patli parat** (gold foil). Itni patli ki uski motai sirf lagbhag 1000 atoms jitni thi.
- **Pakadne ka tareeka:** parat ke aas-paas ek zinc sulphide ki parat wali screen (jo takrane par chamakti hai) lagayi gayi. Jahan alpha kan takrata, wahan chhoti-si chamak (flash) dikhti. Flash dekh kar pata chalta ki kan kis disha mein gaya.

Rutherford ko ummeed thi ki Thomson ka model sahi hai. Agar atom "bhara hua pudding" hai, to tez alpha kan seedhe chhed kar nikal jayenge, bas thoda-bahut hi mudenge. Par asli nateeje kuch aur nikle.

```
   Alpha kanon ka                 Sone ki patli parat
   source (positive, tez)         (beech mein nucleus N, bahut chhote)

                                          N
   ===(a)=====================================================>  seedhe nikle (zyadatar)

                                          N
   ===(b)==================================\
                                             \___.            thoda mude (kuch)
                                          N

   ===(c)===============================> N
   <===================================                          seedha ulta (bahut kam)

                                          N
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Gold-foil prayog ke teen tarah ke raaste (saral sketch, scale se nahi): (a) zyadatar alpha kan khaali jagah se seedhe nikal gaye, (b) kuch kisi nucleus ke paas se guzre aur chhote kone se mude, (c) bahut hi kam kan lagbhag aamne-saamne takra kar ulte laut aaye. Nucleus asli mein isse lakhon guna chhote hain.

## Part C: Teen observations, teen conclusions

Ab sabse zaroori hissa. Har observation ke peeche ek **soch** hai, aur har soch se ek **nateeja** nikalta hai. Inhe ek-ek karke dekhte hain.

### Observation 1: Zyadatar alpha kan seedhe nikal gaye

Ye bahut baar hua: jab sone ki parat par hazaaron alpha kan phenke gaye, to **zyadatar** bina mude seedhe nikal gaye.

**Soch:** Alpha kan tez aur bhaari hai. Agar atom Thomson ke model jaisa ek **thos bhara hua gola** hota, to har kan ko us gole se guzarte hue rukawat milti, aur wo mudte ya ruk jaate. Seedha nikal jaana tabhi mumkin hai jab raaste mein kuch **rukawat hi na ho**.

**Nateeja 1: Atom ka zyadatar hissa khaali jagah hai.**

Jaise Ayaan ke kanche: zyadatar seedhe nikle, to chadar ke peeche zyadatar jagah khaali thi.

### Observation 2: Kuch alpha kan thode kone se mud gaye

Kuch kan seedhe raaste se hat kar chhote kone se mude (deflect hue).

**Soch:** Alpha kan **positive** hai. Charge ke baare mein ek pakka niyam yaad karo (Physics ke Electricity chapter se): **same charge ek-doosre ko door dhakelte hain.** Alpha kan jab parat se guzar raha hai aur wo **kisi positive charge ke paas** se nikalta hai, to use dhakka lagta hai aur wo mud jaata hai. Kan sirf tab mudega jab raaste mein koi aisi cheez ho jo **positive ho aur bhaari ho**.

**Nateeja 2: Atom ka positive charge poore atom mein phaila nahi hai. Wo kisi ek jagah ikattha hai.**

Agar positive charge poore gole mein halka-halka phaila hota (Thomson ka model), to kisi bhi kan ko kahin bhi koi bada dhakka nahi lagta. Par yahan kuch kan mude: matlab kahin **ikatthe** positive charge ke paas se guzre.

### Observation 3: Bahut hi kam alpha kan seedhe ulte laut aaye

Lagbhag **12000 mein se sirf 1** alpha kan (kaha jaata hai "bahut kam") **seedha ulta** laut aaya. Ye nateeja itna chaunkane wala tha ki scientists ko pehle yakeen hi nahi hua.

**Soch:** Ek tez, bhaari alpha kan ko **ulta** wapas phenkne ke liye bahut bada dhakka chahiye. Ye dhakka tabhi lag sakta hai jab kan **bahut bhaari aur bahut positive** cheez se lagbhag aamne-saamne takraye. Aur dhyan do: sirf **1 kan 12000 mein** aisa takraya. To wo bhaari cheez bahut **chhoti** hogi, tabhi to kanon ko usse takrane ka mauka kam mila.

**Nateeja 3: Atom ke beech mein ek bahut chhota, bahut bhaari (dense) aur positive hissa hai. Isse nucleus kehte hain.**

Ayaan ke kanche mein: zyadatar seedhe nikle (khaali jagah), kuch tedhe hue, aur ek ulta laut aaya, matlab chadar ke peeche ek chhota magar sakht khamba tha.

### Teeno ko ek table mein

| Observation | Soch (kyun hua?) | Nateeja |
|---|---|---|
| Zyadatar kan seedhe nikle | Raaste mein rukawat nahi mili | Atom ka zyadatar hissa khaali jagah hai |
| Kuch kan thode mude | Positive kan ko kisi positive cheez ne dhakela | Positive charge ek jagah ikattha hai |
| Bahut kam kan seedhe ulte aaye | Kisi bhaari, positive aur chhoti cheez se takraye | Ek chhota, bhaari, positive **nucleus** hai |

Ye bhi dekho: electron ke baare mein kya? Electron bahut halka hai (proton se lagbhag 1840 guna halka, Topic 1). Ek tez, bhaari alpha kan ko electron nahi mod sakta, jaise ek chhoti-si makkhi ke takrane se cricket ball ka raasta nahi badalta. Isliye ulta wapas bhejne wala kaam electron ka nahi, **positive nucleus** ka hi ho sakta hai. Aur electrons? Wo nucleus ke **bahar**, baaki khaali jagah mein hain.

## Part D: Kitna chhota hai nucleus? Sizes aur ek ghar ka scale

Ab thodi sankhyaon ki baat. Atom aur nucleus ke aakaar (approximate):

- Atom ka aakaar: lagbhag **10⁻¹⁰ m**
- Nucleus ka aakaar: lagbhag **10⁻¹⁵ m**

**Example 1: Atom nucleus se kitna bada hai?**

Ratio = 10⁻¹⁰ ÷ 10⁻¹⁵ = 10⁻¹⁰⁺¹⁵ = 10⁵ = **1,00,000** guna.

Yaani nucleus atom se lagbhag **ek lakh guna chhota** hai (ek lakh mein ek hissa).

**Example 2: Everyday scale (lagbhag).**

Agar nucleus ko ek **matar ke dane** jitna bada (lagbhag 1 cm) maan lein, to atom kitna bada hoga? Ek lakh guna: 1 cm × 1,00,000 = 1,00,000 cm = 1000 m = **1 km**.

Yaani agar nucleus ek matar ka dana ho, to atom lagbhag **1 kilometre** ke gole jitna hoga. Beech mein ek matar ka dana, aur uske charon taraf 500 m door tak lagbhag **khaali jagah**, jisme electrons ghoom rahe hain. (Ye tulna sirf andaaze ke liye hai: lagbhag, bilkul sahi naap nahi.)

**Example 3: Dravyaman kahan hai?**

Proton aur neutron ka dravyaman lagbhag 1 u har ek, electron ka sirf lagbhag 1/1840 u (Topic 1). Proton aur neutron nucleus mein hain. To atom ka **lagbhag poora dravyaman nucleus mein** hota hai, chahe nucleus ki jagah atom mein bahut kam ho. Matlab nucleus **bahut dense** hai.

Chhota sa dekho: hydrogen mein 1 proton aur 1 electron. Proton ka dravyaman lagbhag 1 u, electron ka 1/1840 u. Kul = 1 + 1/1840 u, aur isme se **99.9%** se zyada dravyaman nucleus (proton) ka hai. Isliye "atom ka dravyaman nucleus mein" kehna sahi hai.

**Example 4: Kitne alpha kan ulte aaye?**

Agar 24000 alpha kan phenke jayein, aur lagbhag 12000 mein 1 ulta laute, to ulte aane wale kan = 24000 ÷ 12000 = lagbhag **2**. Bacche hue lagbhag 23998 kan seedhe ya thode tedhe nikle. Ulte aane wale kan itne kam hain kyunki nucleus atom ke mukable bahut chhota nishana hai.

## Part E: Rutherford ka model

In teen nateejon ko jod kar Rutherford ne atom ka naya model diya:

1. Atom ke beech mein ek bahut chhota, bhaari, positive **nucleus** hai. Isme saare **positive charge (protons)** aur lagbhag **poora dravyaman** hai.
2. **Electron** nucleus ke **bahar** hain, aur nucleus ke chaaron taraf **ghoomte** hain, jaise grah Suraj ke gird.
3. Atom ka zyadatar hissa **khaali jagah** hai.
4. Nucleus ka positive charge aur electrons ka kul negative charge barabar hain, to atom **neutral** hai.
5. Electrons aur nucleus ke beech **aakarshan** (positive-negative ka kheench) electron ko ghoomta rakhta hai.

```
                 Rutherford ka model

                   o   electron
                      .
                .           .
              .                .
             .       +++        .      o
        o   .       +++++        .
             .       +++        .
              .                .
                .           .
                  .   o   .

        Beech mein: nucleus (bahut chhota, bhaari, positive)
        Bahar: electron, nucleus ke gird ghoomte hue
        Beech ki jagah: khaali
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Rutherford ka model: beech mein chhota, bhaari, positive nucleus; uske chaaron taraf electron ghoomte hue; beech ka zyadatar hissa khaali. Ye tasveer Thomson ke bhare hue gole se bilkul alag hai.

Dhyan do: 1911 mein neutron abhi khoja nahi gaya tha (wo 1932 mein Chadwick ne khoja, Topic 1). Isliye Rutherford ke model mein nucleus mein sirf protons ki baat thi. Aaj hum jaante hain ki nucleus mein protons ke saath **neutrons** bhi hote hain (hydrogen ke aam atom ko chhod kar).

**Thomson aur Rutherford, ek jhalak mein**

| Baat | Thomson ka model (1904) | Rutherford ka model (1911) |
|---|---|---|
| Positive charge | Poore gole mein phaila hua | Beech ke chhote nucleus mein ikattha |
| Electron kahan hain | Gole ke andar jade hue | Nucleus ke bahar, uske gird |
| Atom ke andar jagah | Poora bhara hua | Zyadatar khaali |
| Prayog se mel | Nahi (zyadatar seedhe nikalna aur ulta aana samjha nahi sakta) | Haan (teeno observations samjhata hai) |

## Part F: Rutherford ke model ki ek kami

Rutherford ka model prayog ke saath mel khata tha, lekin ek sawaal bahut pareshan karta tha.

Us samay ki physics ke hisaab se **ek charged kan jab ghoomta hai** (yaani lagatar apni disha badalta hai), to wo **urja (energy) ganwata** jaata hai. Agar electron nucleus ke gird ghoomte hue urja ganwayega, to wo dheere-dheere **sikud kar ghoomte-ghoomte nucleus mein gir jaana chahiye** (spiral mein). Aur ye bahut tezi se hona chahiye, ek second ke bahut chhote hisse mein.

Lekin sach kya hai? **Atom stable hain.** Tumhara pen, paani, hawa ke atom, sab saalon-saal se bane hue hain, koi atom apne aap sikud kar khatam nahi hota.

To kuch to adhoora hai. Rutherford ka model **nucleus** wali baat mein bilkul sahi hai, par electron nucleus ke gird **kis tarah** ghoomte hain ki girte nahi, ye abhi tak nahi bataya gaya.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Rutherford ne electron khoja"**

Electron Thomson ne khoja (1897). Rutherford ne **nucleus** ki khoj ka raasta dikhaya (gold-foil prayog) aur uska model diya.

**Galti 2: "Alpha kan negative hota hai"**

Alpha kan **positive** hota hai. Isi liye positive nucleus use dhakelta hai aur wo mud jaata hai ya ulta laut-ta hai.

**Galti 3: "Zyadatar kan seedhe nikle, to atom mein kuch nahi hai"**

Seedhe nikalne se sirf ye pata chala ki **zyadatar** hissa khaali hai. Beech mein ek chhota, bhaari nucleus hai, jisne kuch kanon ko mod diya ya ulta bhej diya.

**Galti 4: "Nucleus bada hota hai kyunki usme saara dravyaman hai"**

Nucleus bahut **chhota** hai (atom ka lagbhag ek lakh mein ek hissa, aakaar mein). Bahut kam jagah mein bahut dravyaman: isliye wo **dense** hai.

**Galti 5: Figure ko asli scale samajhna**

Hamari tasveerein dikhane ke liye hain. Asli atom mein nucleus aur atom ke beech ka fark itna bada hai ki use kagaz par sahi scale mein banana mumkin nahi.

## Quick Recap

- **Thomson ka model (1904):** positive charge wala bhara hua gola, usme electron jade hue (tarbooz-beej, pudding-kishmish).
- **Rutherford ka gold-foil prayog (1911):** tez, positive alpha kan sone ki bahut patli parat par phenke gaye.
- **Observation 1:** zyadatar kan seedhe nikle, to atom ka **zyadatar hissa khaali** hai.
- **Observation 2:** kuch kan thode mude, to positive charge **ek jagah ikattha** hai (same charge dhakelte hain).
- **Observation 3:** bahut kam (lagbhag 12000 mein 1) kan ulte aaye, to beech mein **chhota, bhaari, positive nucleus** hai.
- **Sizes (approximate):** atom lagbhag 10⁻¹⁰ m, nucleus lagbhag 10⁻¹⁵ m: nucleus atom se lagbhag ek lakh guna chhota. Agar nucleus matar ka dana ho to atom lagbhag 1 km ka.
- **Rutherford ka model:** beech mein nucleus (saare positive charge aur lagbhag poora dravyaman), uske bahar electron ghoomte hue, zyadatar khaali jagah.
- **Kami:** ghoomta electron urja ganwaye to nucleus mein gir jaana chahiye, par atom stable hain. Ye sawaal agle topic ka hai.

### Answers

1. Thomson ke model mein atom ek **positive charge wala bhara hua gola** hai, jiske andar **electron jade hue** hain. Tarbooz ka laal hissa positive gola hai aur beej electron. Pudding mein pudding positive aur kishmish electron. Poora atom neutral hai kyunki positive aur negative charge barabar hain.

2. **Goli:** tez, positive alpha kan. **Target:** sone ki bahut patli parat. **Dekhne ka tareeka:** parat ke aas-paas zinc sulphide wali screen, jahan alpha kan takraye wahan chhoti chamak (flash) dikhti, jisse kan ki disha ka pata chalta.

3. (i) Zyadatar kan seedhe nikle: to atom ka zyadatar hissa **khaali** hai. (ii) Kuch kan thode mude: to positive charge **ek jagah ikattha** hai. (iii) Bahut kam (lagbhag 12000 mein 1) ulte aaye: to beech mein ek **chhota, bhaari, positive nucleus** hai.

4. Thomson ke model mein positive charge poore gole mein halka phaila hai, to kisi bhi kan ko bada dhakka nahi lagta, bas thode bend. Ye **seedhe ulta aane** wali observation ko samjha nahi sakta, kyunki wahan koi chhoti, bhaari, positive cheez hi nahi.

5. Alpha kan positive hai, nucleus bhi positive. **Same charge ek-doosre ko dhakelte hain**, to paas se guzarte kan par dhakka lagta hai aur wo mud jaata hai.

6. Electron ka dravyaman proton ka lagbhag 1/1840 hai, bahut halka. Ek tez, bhaari alpha kan ko electron seedha ulta nahi bhej sakta (jaise makkhi cricket ball ko nahi). Ulta bhejne ke liye bhaari, positive nucleus chahiye.

7. Atom = 10⁻¹⁰ ÷ 10⁻¹⁵ = 10⁵ = **1,00,000 guna** bada. Nucleus 2 cm ka ho to atom = 2 cm × 1,00,000 = 2,00,000 cm = 2000 m = lagbhag **2 km**.

8. 36000 ÷ 12000 = lagbhag **3** kan ulte aayenge (approximate).

9. (i) Beech mein chhota, bhaari, positive nucleus (saare positive charge aur lagbhag poora dravyaman). (ii) Electron nucleus ke bahar, uske gird ghoomte hue. (iii) Zyadatar khaali jagah. (iv) Atom neutral. Thomson ke model se fark: wahan positive charge poore gole mein phaila tha aur atom bhara hua tha; yahan positive charge ek chhote nucleus mein hai aur atom zyadatar khaali.

10. Us samay ki physics ke hisaab se ghoomta charged electron urja ganwata hai, to uske chakkar chhote hote jaayenge aur wo spiral mein nucleus mein gir jaana chahiye. Lekin asli atom stable hain, wo aise nahi girte. Rutherford ka model ye nahi samjha paya.
