# Topic 3: Bohr Ka Model: Electrons Ke Shells

Pichhle topic ke end mein ek pareshaan karne wala sawaal chhoda tha. Rutherford ke model mein electron nucleus ke aas-paas ghoomta hai. Lekin ghoomta hua electron dheere-dheere energy kho dega aur ghoomte-ghoomte nucleus mein gir jaayega, yaani atom kabhi stable hona hi nahi chahiye. Phir bhi hamare aas-paas ke atom saalon-saal bane rehte hain. Is topic mein hum dekhenge ki **Niels Bohr** ne is samasya ka kaunsa seedha-sa hal diya, aur uske baad electrons atom mein **kis niyam se baithte hain**.

---

## Lift jo sirf floors par rukti hai

Mr. Iyengar apni bhanji Nayra ko apne office ki building dikhane le gaye. Building 10 manzil ki thi. Dono lift mein chadhe. Nayra ne button dabaya aur lift upar chalne lagi. Beech mein usne dekha ki lift kabhi **do floors ke beech mein** nahi rukti: ya to floor 3 par, ya floor 4 par.

"Mama, ye lift kabhi 3 aur 4 ke beech mein kyon nahi rukti? Jaise ghadi mein koi bhi time ho sakta hai, waise lift kahin bhi kyon nahi ruk sakti?"

Mr. Iyengar hans pade. "Kyunki lift ke liye **sirf kuch tay floors** hain. Tum floor 3 par khadi ho sakti ho, floor 4 par khadi ho sakti ho, par 3.5 floor naam ki koi jagah nahi hai. Aur dekho, upar jaane ke liye lift ko **bijli ki energy** lagti hai, neeche aane par wo wapas mil jaati hai. Ek floor se doosre floor tak jaane ki ek tay 'keemat' hai."

Nayra ne soch kar kaha, "To lift ke floors ek tay seedhi hain. Beech ke raaste mein koi khadi nahi ho sakti."

"Bilkul," Mr. Iyengar ne kaha. "Aur ye hi aadha raaz hai ki Bohr ne atom ke andar electrons ke baare mein kya socha. Bas wahan lift ki jagah **electron** hai aur floors ki jagah **shells**."

```
        Lift ki building                  Atom
        (floors tay hain)                 (shells tay hain)

   Floor 4  -----------------  <-- lift yahan ruk sakti hai      N shell
   Floor 3  -----------------  <-- yahan bhi                     M shell
   Floor 2  -----------------  <-- yahan bhi                     L shell
   Floor 1  -----------------  <-- yahan bhi                     K shell
   Ground   [ lift ]                                             nucleus (+)

   Floor 2 aur 3 ke beech mein ruknaa: nahi ho sakta.
   Shell L aur M ke beech mein electron ka rehna: nahi ho sakta.
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Lift ka analogy. Lift sirf tay floors par rukti hai, beech mein kabhi nahi; usi tarah electron atom mein sirf tay shells mein rehta hai, do shells ke beech mein kabhi nahi. Nucleus se jitna door ka floor (shell), utni zyada energy lagti hai. (Ye ek tulna hai, bilkul wahi cheez nahi.)

## Part A: Bohr ki soch (1913)

Rutherford ke model mein ek kami thi: ghoomta electron energy kho dega. **Niels Bohr** ne 1913 mein ek naya, simple vichar rakha. Isse **Bohr ka model** kehte hain. Uski buniyadi baatein ye hain:

1. Electron nucleus ke chaaron taraf **kuch tay, gol raaston** mein ghoomte hain. In raaston ko **shells** ya **energy levels** kehte hain.
2. Jab tak electron ek shell mein hai, wo **energy nahi khota**. Isliye wo spiral banakar nucleus mein nahi girta. (Yahi Rutherford ki samasya ka hal tha.)
3. Har shell ki ek tay energy hoti hai. Nucleus ke paas wali shell ki energy kam, door wali ki zyada. Electron ek shell se doosri shell mein tabhi jaata hai jab use utni hi energy mile ya de. Beech mein rukna mana hai, bilkul lift ki tarah.

Kyunki ye model Rutherford ke model ka dard door karta hai aur chemistry ke bahut saare nateejon ko saral tareeke se samjha deta hai, isliye hum Class 9 mein isi ko apne kaam ka model banate hain. Dhyan do: bade aur jatil atoms ko poori tarah samjhane ke liye aage ki class mein isme aur sudhaar kiye jaate hain.

Shells ke naam **K, L, M, N** hain. K sabse andar, nucleus ke sabse paas. Baaki isi kram mein bahar ki taraf. Inhe **n = 1, 2, 3, 4** bhi kehte hain.

```
   .-------------------------------------------.
   :  N shell (n = 4)                          :
   :   .-----------------------------------.   :
   :   :  M shell (n = 3)                  :   :
   :   :   .---------------------------.   :   :
   :   :   :  L shell (n = 2)          :   :   :
   :   :   :   .-------------------.   :   :   :
   :   :   :   :  K shell (n = 1)  :   :   :   :
   :   :   :   :         (+)       :   :   :   :
   :   :   :   :       nucleus     :   :   :   :
   :   :   :   '-------------------'   :   :   :
   :   :   '---------------------------'   :   :
   :   '-----------------------------------'   :
   '-------------------------------------------'
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Bohr ke shells ek ke andar ek: sabse andar nucleus (+) aur K shell (n = 1), phir L (n = 2), M (n = 3) aur N (n = 4). Ye tasveer sirf ek yojana hai: asli shells gol hoti hain, yahan dabbon jaisi banayi hain, aur shells ke beech ke fasle bhi asli nahi hain. Electron inhi raaston mein hi rehte hain, beech mein nahi.

## Part B: Ek shell mein kitne electron? Formula 2n²

Har shell ek hall jaisi hai jismein seats ki ek tay sankhya hai. Bohr aur Bury ke kaam se ek formula mila:

> **Shell mein zyada se zyada electrons = 2n²**

Yahan **n** shell ka number hai. Ab hum ye formula n = 1 se n = 4 tak lagate hain. (n² ka matlab n × n.)

**n = 1 (K shell).** n² = 1 × 1 = 1. To 2n² = 2 × 1 = **2** electrons.

**n = 2 (L shell).** n² = 2 × 2 = 4. To 2n² = 2 × 4 = **8** electrons.

**n = 3 (M shell).** n² = 3 × 3 = 9. To 2n² = 2 × 9 = **18** electrons.

**n = 4 (N shell).** n² = 4 × 4 = 16. To 2n² = 2 × 16 = **32** electrons.

| Shell | n | n² | Capacity = 2n² |
|---|---|---|---|
| K | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| L | 2 | 4 | 8 |
| M | 3 | 9 | 18 |
| N | 4 | 16 | 32 |

Jaanch: chaaron shells ka kul = 2 + 8 + 18 + 32 = 60 electrons ✅

Dhyan do: ye **capacity** (zyada se zyada) hai, zaroori nahi ki shell hamesha poori bhari ho. Aage Part C mein dekhenge ki kab kaun si shell bharti hai.

## Part C: Bohr-Bury ke niyam (pehle 20 tatvon ke liye)

Electrons ko shells mein kaise bithayein? Bohr aur **Bury** ne milkar ye niyam diye:

1. **Capacity 2n².** Koi bhi shell iske aage nahi bhar sakti. K mein 2, L mein 8, M mein 18, N mein 32.
2. **Sabse bahari shell mein zyada se zyada 8 electrons** hote hain. (Agar sabse bahari shell K hai, to wahan 2.)
3. **Pehle andar ki shells bharo.** Electron pehle sabse andar wali (kam energy wali) khaali jagah mein jaata hai. K poori bhare, tab L; L poori bhare, tab M, aur aage isi tarah.

Ye niyam pehle **20 tatvon** (hydrogen se calcium tak) par bilkul theek baithte hain. Hum Class 9 mein bas yahi tak jaate hain.

**Ek zaroori baat:** niyam 2 ki wajah se M shell, jiski capacity 18 hai, **8 electrons par rukkar** agli shell shuru karwa deti hai. Isliye argon (2, 8, 8) ke baad agla electron M mein 9wan nahi baithta, balki **N shell** mein jaata hai: potassium 2, 8, 8, 1 aur calcium 2, 8, 8, 2.

**Class 9 ki seema (imaandari ki baat).** Calcium tak ye niyam seedhe kaam karte hain. Uske baad (jaise scandium se aage ke tatvon mein) pattern **zyada ulajha** ho jaata hai: M shell mein 8 se zyada electron bhi aate hain aur sirf "8 ka niyam" kaafi nahi rehta. Wo sab **aage ki class mein** seekhenge. Yahan hum sirf calcium tak ki tasveer ko poori tarah pakka karte hain.

```
   Electron bharne ka kram (pehle 20 tatvon ke liye):

   K (2)  ->  L (8)  ->  M (pehle 8)  ->  N (1, 2)
   pehle      phir         phir          Ar ke baad
   bharo      bharo        8 tak         agla electron
                                         N mein

   Ar : 2, 8, 8       K : 2, 8, 8, 1       Ca : 2, 8, 8, 2
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Bharne ka kram: K, phir L, phir M (8 tak), phir N. Argon ke baad ke do tatvon mein naya electron N shell mein jaata hai, M mein 9wan nahi.

## Part D: Atom ka chitra banana (step by step)

Yahan hum kisi tatva ke electrons ki **kul sankhya** ko "di hui" maan kar chalte hain (ye sankhya kaise tay hoti hai, wo agle topic ka vishay hai). Phir:

**Step 1:** Kul electrons ginte hain.
**Step 2:** K shell mein 2 rakho (ya jitne hon, agar 2 se kam hon).
**Step 3:** Bache hue electrons L shell mein rakho, 8 tak.
**Step 4:** Bache hue M shell mein rakho, 8 tak. Phir N mein.
**Step 5:** Jodkar jaanch karo: shells ka kul = kul electrons.

**Example 1: Hydrogen (1 electron).** K mein 1. Arrangement: **1**. Bas ek shell, usmein ek electron.

**Example 2: Helium (2 electrons).** K mein 2. Arrangement: **2**. K shell poori bhar gayi (capacity 2).

**Example 3: Lithium (3 electrons).** K mein 2, bacha 1 L mein. Arrangement: **2, 1**.

**Example 4: Carbon (6 electrons).** K mein 2, bache 4 L mein. Arrangement: **2, 4**. Jaanch: 2 + 4 = 6 ✅

**Example 5: Oxygen (8 electrons).** K mein 2, bache 6 L mein. Arrangement: **2, 6**. Jaanch: 2 + 6 = 8 ✅

**Example 6: Neon (10 electrons).** K mein 2, bache 8 L mein (L poori bhar gayi). Arrangement: **2, 8**. Jaanch: 2 + 8 = 10 ✅

```
   Lithium (Li): 2, 1          Carbon (C): 2, 4

               e                           e
            .-----.                     .-----.
           /       \                   /       \
          (  e (+) e )               e (  e (+) e )  e
           \       /                   \       /
            '-----'                     '-----'
                                           e

   Neon (Ne): 2, 8

               e
        e   .-----.   e
           /       \
      e   (  e (+) e )   e
           \       /
        e   '-----'   e
               e
```

> **Figure 3.4** – Atom ke chitra. Har chitra mein beech mein nucleus (+), uske chaaron taraf chhota gola K shell (usmein 2 electron "e"), aur uske bahar L shell ke electron. Lithium: 2, 1. Carbon: 2, 4. Neon: 2, 8. Gino aur milao: Li mein 2 + 1 = 3, C mein 2 + 4 = 6, Ne mein 2 + 8 = 10 electrons. (Sketch ek yojana hai: asli shells ka fasla aur electron ki jagah aisi nahi hoti.)

**Example 7: Sodium (11 electrons).** K mein 2 (11 − 2 = 9 bache), L mein 8 (9 − 8 = 1 bacha), M mein 1. Arrangement: **2, 8, 1**. Jaanch: 2 + 8 + 1 = 11 ✅

**Example 8: Chlorine (17 electrons).** K 2, L 8, bache 17 − 10 = 7 M mein. Arrangement: **2, 8, 7**. Jaanch: 2 + 8 + 7 = 17 ✅

**Example 9: Argon (18 electrons).** K 2, L 8, bache 8 M mein. Arrangement: **2, 8, 8**. M mein 8 hi hain, 18 nahi: kyunki sabse bahari shell mein 8 se zyada nahi (niyam 2).

**Example 10: Potassium (19 electrons).** K 2, L 8, M 8, bacha 1 electron. Wo M mein 9wan nahi baith sakta (M sabse bahari shell hai to uska 8 se zyada nahi). Isliye N mein jaata hai. Arrangement: **2, 8, 8, 1**. Jaanch: 2 + 8 + 8 + 1 = 19 ✅

**Example 11: Calcium (20 electrons).** K 2, L 8, M 8, bache 2 N mein. Arrangement: **2, 8, 8, 2**. Jaanch: 2 + 8 + 8 + 2 = 20 ✅

```
   Atom      K        L                   M                  N

   Na (11)   e e      e e e e e e e e     e
   Cl (17)   e e      e e e e e e e e     e e e e e e e
   K  (19)   e e      e e e e e e e e     e e e e e e e e    e
   Ca (20)   e e      e e e e e e e e     e e e e e e e e    e e
```

> **Figure 3.5** – Teen-chaar shell wale atoms ko shell-wise gina: Na 2, 8, 1; Cl 2, 8, 7; K 2, 8, 8, 1; Ca 2, 8, 8, 2. Har column mein "e" ki ginti hi us shell ke electron hain. Dhyan do ki K aur Ca mein M shell mein 8 par ruk kar N shell shuru hui hai.

Ek aur dhyan do: ye saare chitra **ek hi tarah ke electron** se bane hain. Kisi atom ka koi alag "special" electron nahi hota; bas kitne electron hain aur shells mein kaise baithe hain, ye alag hota hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Shell ke K ko potassium ka K samajhna**

**K shell** nucleus ke sabse paas wali shell ka naam hai, aur **K** potassium ka symbol hai. Dono alag cheezein hain. Dhyan se padho ki kahan shell ki baat ho rahi hai aur kahan tatva ki.

**Galti 2: Capacity ko "hamesha poori bhari hoti hai" samajhna**

M shell ki capacity 18 hai, par sodium mein M mein sirf 1 electron hai. Capacity sirf "zyada se zyada" batati hai.

**Galti 3: Potassium ko 2, 8, 9 likhna**

Niyam 2 ke hisaab se sabse bahari shell mein 8 se zyada nahi. Isliye 2, 8, 8, 1 (N shell mein 1).

**Galti 4: Pehle bahar ki shell bharna**

Electron pehle andar ki (kam energy ki) shell bharta hai. Lithium **2, 1** hai, 3 ya 0, 3 nahi.

**Galti 5: 2n² mein n ko double karna**

2n² ka matlab 2 × (n × n) hai. Jaise n = 3 ke liye 2 × 9 = 18, na ki (2 × 3)² = 36.

**Galti 6: Bohr ke model ko "poora aur aakhri" maan lena**

Ye Class 9 ke liye bahut kaam ka model hai, lekin atom ki poori kahani aage ki class mein aur badi hai. Calcium ke baad bhi ye niyam seedhe nahi lagte.

## Quick Recap

- Rutherford ke model ki samasya: ghoomta electron energy kho dega. **Niels Bohr (1913)** ne kaha: electron **tay shells (energy levels)** mein rehte hain aur jab tak ek shell mein hain, **energy nahi khote**.
- Lift ka analogy: lift sirf floors par rukti hai, beech mein nahi; electron sirf shells mein rehta hai, beech mein nahi.
- Shells: **K, L, M, N** (n = 1, 2, 3, 4), nucleus se bahar ki taraf.
- Shell ki capacity = **2n²**: K = 2, L = 8, M = 18, N = 32.
- **Bohr-Bury niyam:** (1) capacity 2n² se zyada nahi, (2) sabse bahari shell mein zyada se zyada 8 (K ho to 2), (3) pehle andar ki shell bharo.
- Pehle 20 tatvon ke arrangement: H 1; He 2; Li 2, 1; C 2, 4; O 2, 6; Ne 2, 8; Na 2, 8, 1; Cl 2, 8, 7; Ar 2, 8, 8; K 2, 8, 8, 1; Ca 2, 8, 8, 2.
- Argon ke baad agla electron M mein 9wan nahi, **N shell** mein jaata hai.
- Class 9 ki seema: calcium ke baad pattern ulajha ho jaata hai: aage ki class mein.

9. The M shell can hold 18 electrons, yet argon has only 8 in it. Why?

### Answers

1. Rutherford ke model mein ghoomte electron ko energy khoni chahiye thi aur use nucleus mein gir jaana chahiye tha. Bohr ne kaha ki electron **tay shells** mein hi rehte hain aur ek shell mein rehte hue **energy nahi khote**, isliye atom stable hai.

2. (i) Electron nucleus ke chaaron taraf tay gol shells (energy levels) mein rehte hain. (ii) Ek shell mein rehte hue electron energy nahi khota. (iii) Electron ek shell se doosri shell mein tabhi jaata hai jab use utni energy mile ya de; beech mein nahi rukta.

3. Lift sirf floors par ruk sakti hai, floor 3 aur 4 ke beech mein koi jagah hi nahi, usi tarah electron ke liye do shells ke beech koi tay jagah nahi hai. Analogy yahan tak kaam karti hai ki dono mein "tay jagahein" hain aur upar jaane ke liye energy lagti hai. Analogy asli nahi hai kyunki lift ek bhaari, dikhne wali cheez hai jabki electron ka vyavhaar kuch alag hai; aur shells ka fasla floors jaisa barabar nahi hota.

4. n = 1: 2 × 1² = **2** (K shell). n = 2: 2 × 4 = **8** (L shell). n = 3: 2 × 9 = **18** (M shell). n = 4: 2 × 16 = **32** (N shell).

5. (1) Shell ki capacity 2n² se zyada nahi. (2) Sabse bahari shell mein zyada se zyada 8 electron (agar sabse bahari shell K ho to 2). (3) Pehle andar ki shells bharo: K, phir L, phir M aur aage. Ye niyam hydrogen se calcium tak ke liye hain.

6. (a) 5 electrons: K 2, L 3, arrangement **2, 3**. (b) 9 electrons: K 2, L 7, **2, 7**. (c) 12 electrons: K 2, L 8, M 2, **2, 8, 2**. (d) 16 electrons: K 2, L 8, M 6, **2, 8, 6**. Har case mein jaanch karo: 2 + 3 = 5, 2 + 7 = 9, 2 + 8 + 2 = 12, 2 + 8 + 6 = 16 ✅. Chitra mein centre mein nucleus (+), K ke gole mein 2 "e", aur baaki "e" aage ki shells mein.

7. Ye 2, 8, 9 galat hai, kyunki sabse bahari shell mein 8 se zyada electron nahi ho sakte. Sahi arrangement **2, 8, 8, 1** (naya electron N shell mein). Jaanch: 2 + 8 + 8 + 1 = 19 ✅

8. (a) 15: K 2, L 8, M 5: **2, 8, 5**; sabse bahari shell M mein 5 electron. (b) 18: **2, 8, 8**; sabse bahari M mein 8. (c) 20: **2, 8, 8, 2**; sabse bahari N mein 2. Jaanch: 2 + 8 + 5 = 15, 2 + 8 + 8 = 18, 2 + 8 + 8 + 2 = 20 ✅

9. Kyunki Bohr-Bury ke niyam 2 ke hisaab se sabse bahari shell mein 8 se zyada electron nahi hote (pehle 20 tatvon ke liye). 18 sirf M shell ki **capacity** (zyada se zyada) hai, har haal mein bharna zaroori nahi. Argon (2, 8, 8) mein M sabse bahari shell hai, to wahan 8 par ruk jaata hai.

10. K shell mein sirf 2 electron aa sakte hain, 8 nahi, aur L shell mein 8 tak: "K mein 8, L mein 3" niyamon ke khilaf hai. Sahi: K 2, L 8, bacha 1 M mein, yaani **2, 8, 1**. Jaanch: 2 + 8 + 1 = 11 ✅

11. Dono baatein galat hain. (a) **False** ❌. "K shell" nucleus ke sabse paas wali shell hai, jabki K potassium ka symbol hai, dono alag cheezein hain. (b) **False** ❌. Shell ko poori bhari hona zaroori nahi; capacity sirf zyada se zyada hai (jaise sodium ke M mein sirf 1 electron).

12. Kyunki calcium (2, 8, 8, 2) tak "sabse bahari shell mein 8" wala niyam seedhe kaam karta hai. Uske baad ke tatvon mein pattern zyada ulajha ho jaata hai aur ye saral niyam kaafi nahi rehte. Wo sab aage ki class mein.
