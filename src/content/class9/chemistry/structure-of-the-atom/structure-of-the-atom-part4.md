# Topic 4: Atomic Number aur Mass Number

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki atom ke andar protons aur neutrons nucleus mein hote hain aur electrons shells mein ghoomte hain. Par ab ek seedha sawaal: kaunsa atom **kaunsa tatva** hai, ye kaun tay karta hai? Sodium aur chlorine ke atoms mein bhi to wahi teen kan (proton, neutron, electron) hain, bas ginti alag hai. Is topic mein hum do chhoti-si sankhyaein seekhenge, **atomic number (Z)** aur **mass number (A)**, jinse hum kisi bhi atom mein protons, neutrons aur electrons ki ginti turant bata sakte hain, aur ye bhi samjhenge ki Chapter 3 ki table mein tatva us "Kram" mein kyun the.

## Part A: Atomic number Z, tatva ki pehchaan

**Atomic number (Z) = atom ke nucleus mein protons ki ginti.**

Yahi sankhya tay karti hai ki wo atom kaunse tatva ka hai:

- Jis atom mein **1 proton** hai, wo hamesha hydrogen hai.
- Jis atom mein **6 protons** hain, wo hamesha carbon hai.
- Jis atom mein **11 protons** hain, wo hamesha sodium hai.

Neutron ya electron badal jaayein to bhi tatva wahi rehta hai jab tak protons wahi hain. Par agar protons ki ginti badal jaaye, to **tatva hi badal jaata hai**. Isiliye Z ko tatva ka "roll number" kehte hain.

Ek zaroori baat: **neutral atom** mein protons positive hain aur electrons negative, aur dono barabar hote hain, isliye kul charge shunya (0) hota hai (Topic 1 ka wahi hisaab, aur charge ke baare mein Physics ke [Electricity](../../physics/electricity/electricity.md) chapter mein padha tha). Isse:

**Neutral atom mein electrons ki ginti = protons ki ginti = Z**

### Example 1. Hydrogen, carbon aur sodium ke Z

Hydrogen: 1 proton, to Z = 1, aur neutral atom mein 1 electron.
Carbon: 6 protons, to Z = 6, aur 6 electrons.
Sodium: 11 protons, to Z = 11, aur 11 electrons.

Jaanch: neutral mein protons = electrons: 1 = 1, 6 = 6, 11 = 11 ✅

## Part B: Mass number A, nucleus ka total

Proton aur neutron dono ka dravyaman lagbhag 1 u hota hai, aur electron to lagbhag 1/1840 hissa, yaani bahut halka (Topic 1 ki table). Isliye atom ka lagbhag poora dravyaman nucleus mein baitha hai, aur wo protons aur neutrons ki **ginti** se tay hota hai.

**Mass number (A) = protons + neutrons**

Ye bhi ek **ginti** hai (poora number), isliye A hamesha ek poori sankhya hota hai. Isse ulta bhi chalta hai:

**Neutrons = A − Z**

Chapter 4 ki table (atomic mass) ko hum nazdeeki poori sankhya mein round karte hain, aur wahi **mass number** hota hai: H 1, C 12, N 14, O 16, F 19, Na 23, Mg 24, Al 27, Si 28, P 31, S 32, Cl 35, K 39, Ca 40. Chlorine ka 35.5 ek **average** hai, kyunki chlorine ke do tarah ke atoms milte hain (ek mein A = 35, doosre mein 37); poori kahani Topic 6 mein.

Dhyan do: **atomic mass** (u mein, jaise 23 u) aur **mass number** (ginti, jaise 23) nazdeek-nazdeek hote hain par ek hi cheez nahi: mass number ginti hai, atomic mass dravyaman ka naap.

## Part C: Z aur A likhne ka tareeka

Chemist symbol ke saath ye do sankhyaein ek fixed jagah par likhte hain: **A upar-baayein (superscript)** aur **Z neeche-baayein (subscript)**.

```
        mass number A  (protons + neutrons)
              :
              v
             23
                 Na   <-- symbol (kaunsa tatva)
             11
              ^
              :
        atomic number Z  (protons)

   Ek line mein likhein to:   ²³₁₁Na
   Matlab:  A = 23,  Z = 11,  tatva = sodium
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Symbol ke baayein taraf A upar aur Z neeche likhne ka tareeka (²³₁₁Na). Upar wali sankhya mass number hai (protons + neutrons), neeche wali atomic number (protons). Symbol hi bata deta hai ki tatva kaunsa hai, isliye Z kabhi-kabhi chhod bhi dete hain.

Isse hum turant padh sakte hain: ²³₁₁Na mein protons = 11, neutrons = 23 − 11 = 12, aur neutral atom mein electrons = 11.

### Example 2. ¹²₆C ko padhna

A = 12, Z = 6.
Protons = 6, neutrons = 12 − 6 = 6, electrons (neutral) = 6.

Jaanch: protons + neutrons = 6 + 6 = 12 = A ✅

## Part D: Ek atom mein ginti, kadam ba kadam

Kisi bhi neutral atom ke liye teen kadam hain:

1. Z dekho. Yahi **protons** hain, aur wahi **electrons** hain.
2. A dekho. **Neutrons = A − Z**.
3. Jaanch karo: **protons + neutrons = A**.

```
   Sodium atom, ²³₁₁Na  (neutral)

            nucleus
        ┌─────────────┐
        │  11 p+      │        shell K : 2 e-
        │  12 n       │        shell L : 8 e-
        └─────────────┘        shell M : 1 e-
                               -----------------
   protons   = Z      = 11     kul e- = 2 + 8 + 1 = 11
   neutrons  = A - Z  = 23 - 11 = 12
   electrons = Z      = 11     (neutral hai, isliye p = e)
   nucleus mein kul = 11 + 12 = 23 = A
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Ek sodium atom (²³₁₁Na) mein teeno kanon ki ginti: nucleus mein 11 protons aur 12 neutrons (kul 23 = A), aur bahar shells mein 2 + 8 + 1 = 11 electrons (Topic 3 ke Bohr-Bury niyam se). Neutral atom hai, isliye protons aur electrons barabar hain.

### Example 3. Sodium, ²³₁₁Na

- Protons = Z = **11**
- Neutrons = 23 − 11 = **12**
- Electrons = **11**

Jaanch: 11 + 12 = 23 ✅, aur 11 p = 11 e ✅

### Example 4. Chlorine, ³⁵₁₇Cl

- Protons = **17**
- Neutrons = 35 − 17 = **18**
- Electrons = **17**

Jaanch: 17 + 18 = 35 ✅

### Example 5. Aluminium, ²⁷₁₃Al

- Protons = **13**
- Neutrons = 27 − 13 = **14**
- Electrons = **13**

Jaanch: 13 + 14 = 27 ✅

### Example 6. Calcium, ⁴⁰₂₀Ca

- Protons = **20**
- Neutrons = 40 − 20 = **20**
- Electrons = **20**

Jaanch: 20 + 20 = 40 ✅

### Example 7. Oxygen, ¹⁶₈O

- Protons = **8**
- Neutrons = 16 − 8 = **8**
- Electrons = **8**

Jaanch: 8 + 8 = 16 ✅

### Example 8. Hydrogen, ¹₁H

- Protons = **1**
- Neutrons = 1 − 1 = **0**
- Electrons = **1**

Jaanch: 1 + 0 = 1 ✅. Haan, sabse halke atom mein neutron hota hi nahi (Topic 1 mein ye baat aayi thi).

## Part E: Ulta kaam: A aur Z se tatva, ya protons se tatva

### Example 9. Kisi tatva mein 6 protons aur 6 neutrons hain. Z, A aur tatva batao.

- Z = protons = **6**
- A = 6 + 6 = **12**
- Z = 6 wala tatva carbon hai, to atom **¹²₆C**.

Jaanch: A − Z = 12 − 6 = 6 neutrons ✅

### Example 10. Ek atom ka A = 31 aur neutrons = 16 hain. Tatva kaunsa hai?

- Z = A − neutrons = 31 − 16 = **15**
- Z = 15 wala tatva phosphorus hai (Chapter 3 ke Kram ke hisaab se 15), to **³¹₁₅P**.

Jaanch: protons 15 + neutrons 16 = 31 ✅, aur electrons 15

### Example 11. Uranium-235, sirf ek diya hua udaharan

Ek bhaari atom ka symbol hai ²³⁵₉₂U. (Uranium ka itihaas ya uske nucleus ke saath kya hota hai, ye yahan nahi sikhna; hum sirf ginti seekh rahe hain.)

- Protons = **92**
- Neutrons = 235 − 92 = **143**
- Electrons (neutral) = **92**

Jaanch: 92 + 143 = 235 ✅. Dekho, yahan A sirf ek ginti hai: mass number poori sankhya, aur protons se kaafi zyaada neutrons.

## Part F: Ions: sirf electrons badalte hain, protons nahi

Topic 3 mein humne dekha tha ki electrons shells mein rehte hain, bahar ki taraf. Isiliye kabhi-kabhi atom ke bahari electrons **nikal jaate hain** ya **aa jaate hain**. Chapter 3 mein humne kaha tha ki charge "electron lena ya dena" se aata hai; ab hum iska ginti wala hisaab dekhte hain.

**Sunehri niyam:** ion banne mein **sirf electrons ki ginti badalti hai. Protons aur neutrons kabhi nahi badalte** (nucleus waise ka waisa rehta hai, isliye tatva wahi rehta hai).

- Electron **diye** (kho diye) to negative kam hua, positive bacha: **positive ion (cation)**.
- Electron **liye** to negative zyaada hua: **negative ion (anion)**.

Charge ki ginti: **charge = protons − electrons**.

Na se Na⁺ aur Cl se Cl⁻ banne ka hisaab:

| | Protons | Electrons | Kul charge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Na (neutral) | 11 | 11 | 0 |
| Na⁺ (1 electron diya) | 11 | 10 | +1 |
| Cl (neutral) | 17 | 17 | 0 |
| Cl⁻ (1 electron liya) | 17 | 18 | −1 |

Dekho, protons dono mein waise ke waise hain (11 aur 17); sirf electrons 1 kam ya 1 zyaada hue. Isiliye ion ka tatva wahi rehta hai.

### Example 12. Na⁺ (A = 23, Z = 11)

- Protons = **11**
- Neutrons = 23 − 11 = **12**
- Electrons = 11 − 1 = **10**

Jaanch: charge = 11 − 10 = **+1** ✅

### Example 13. Cl⁻ (A = 35, Z = 17)

- Protons = **17**
- Neutrons = 35 − 17 = **18**
- Electrons = 17 + 1 = **18**

Jaanch: charge = 17 − 18 = **−1** ✅

### Example 14. Mg²⁺ (A = 24, Z = 12)

- Protons = **12**
- Neutrons = 24 − 12 = **12**
- Electrons = 12 − 2 = **10**

Jaanch: charge = 12 − 10 = **+2** ✅

### Example 15. O²⁻ (A = 16, Z = 8)

- Protons = **8**
- Neutrons = 16 − 8 = **8**
- Electrons = 8 + 2 = **10**

Jaanch: charge = 8 − 10 = **−2** ✅

### Example 16. Al³⁺ (A = 27, Z = 13)

- Protons = **13**
- Neutrons = 27 − 13 = **14**
- Electrons = 13 − 3 = **10**

Jaanch: charge = 13 − 10 = **+3** ✅

### Example 17. Ek tarah ke kai ion: Na⁺, Mg²⁺, O²⁻, Al³⁺

Dhyan se dekho: Na⁺, Mg²⁺, O²⁻ aur Al³⁺, chaaron mein electrons ki ginti **10** hai, par protons 11, 12, 8 aur 13 hain. Matlab electrons wahi, par tatva alag-alag. Ye sirf ek mazedaar sanyog nahi hai; iska reason Topic 5 mein khulega.

Jaanch: Na⁺ 11 − 1 = 10 ✅, Mg²⁺ 12 − 2 = 10 ✅, O²⁻ 8 + 2 = 10 ✅, Al³⁺ 13 − 3 = 10 ✅

### Example 18. Calcium ion Ca²⁺ aur K⁺

- K⁺ (A = 39, Z = 19): protons **19**, neutrons 39 − 19 = **20**, electrons 19 − 1 = **18**.
- Ca²⁺ (A = 40, Z = 20): protons **20**, neutrons 40 − 20 = **20**, electrons 20 − 2 = **18**.

Jaanch: K⁺ charge 19 − 18 = +1 ✅; Ca²⁺ charge 20 − 18 = +2 ✅

## Part G: Tatva kyun Z ke Kram mein sajaaye gaye hain

Chapter 3 ki valency table mein pehle 20 tatva ek **Kram** (serial number) mein the, H = 1, He = 2, ..., Ca = 20. Wahan humne wada kiya tha ki iska reason is chapter mein bataenge. Ab jawab saaf hai: ye "Kram" aur kuch nahi, **atomic number Z** hai. Jaise Ms. Ganguly ki class register mein roll number se bachche sajte hain, waise hi tatva Z se sajte hain.

Aur ek bahut dilchasp cheez: **mass number se sajaane se gadbad ho jaati**. Dekho Ar aur K:

- Argon (Z = 18) ka A = 40
- Potassium (Z = 19) ka A = 39

Argon ka mass number zyaada hai par uska Z kam hai. Agar tatva A ke hisaab se sajte, to potassium argon se pehle aata, jo galat hota. Z ke hisaab se Ar (18) pehle aur K (19) baad mein, aur yahi sahi aur saaf kram hai, kyunki Z hi tatva ki asli pehchaan hai.

### Example 19. Ar aur K ki tulna

| | Argon | Potassium |
|---|---|---|
| Z (protons) | 18 | 19 |
| A | 40 | 39 |
| Neutrons (A − Z) | 22 | 20 |
| Electrons (neutral) | 18 | 19 |

Jaanch: Ar: 40 − 18 = 22 ✅; K: 39 − 19 = 20 ✅. Z se Ar pehle, K baad mein.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Z ko mass number samajhna (ya ulta)**

Z = **protons ki ginti**, A = **protons + neutrons ki ginti**. Symbol ke neeche Z, upar A. Yaad rakho: "A bada, upar; Z chhota, neeche" (A zyaada hota hai kyunki usme neutrons bhi judte hain).

**Galti 2: Neutrons ke liye A + Z ya A × Z karna**

Neutrons = **A − Z**. Jodna nahi, ghatana hai.

**Galti 3: Ion mein protons badal dena**

Na⁺ mein protons 11 hi rehte hain, 10 nahi. Ion banne par sirf **electrons** badalte hain. Protons badalne par to tatva hi badal jaata.

**Galti 4: Positive ion mein electrons badhana**

Mg²⁺ ka matlab electrons **kam** hue (12 se 10), badhe nahi. Positive charge = electrons ka kam hona; negative charge = electrons ka zyaada hona.

**Galti 5: Mass number ko atomic mass samajh kar u mein likhna ya decimal dena**

A ek poori ginti hai (23, 35). Chlorine ka 35.5 atomic mass ka average hai, mass number nahi.

**Galti 6: Electrons ko mass number mein jod dena**

A = protons + neutrons. Electrons ka dravyaman lagbhag 1/1840 hai, isliye A mein electrons nahi ginte.

## Quick Recap

- **Atomic number Z** = nucleus mein protons ki ginti; yahi tatva ki **pehchaan** hai (roll number jaisa).
- **Mass number A** = protons + neutrons; **neutrons = A − Z**.
- Symbol ke saath likhte hain: A upar-baayein, Z neeche-baayein (²³₁₁Na).
- **Neutral atom** mein electrons = protons = Z.
- **Ion** mein sirf electrons badalte hain: positive ion mein electrons kam, negative ion mein zyaada. **Charge = protons − electrons.**
- Na⁺, Mg²⁺, O²⁻, Al³⁺ mein electrons sab mein 10 hain (reason Topic 5 mein).
- Tatva **Z ke Kram** mein sajte hain (Chapter 3 ka "Kram"), kyunki Z asli pehchaan hai: Ar (Z 18, A 40) K (Z 19, A 39) se pehle aata hai.
- Pehle 20 tatvon ki table: H 1, He 4, Li 7, Be 9, B 11, C 12, N 14, O 16, F 19, Ne 20, Na 23, Mg 24, Al 27, Si 28, P 31, S 32, Cl 35, Ar 40, K 39, Ca 40.

## Practice Questions

1. What is the atomic number of an element? What does it tell us about the atom, and why is it called the "identity" of an element?

2. Define mass number. Write the formula for the number of neutrons.

3. Write in the Z and A form (like ²³₁₁Na) and find the protons, neutrons and electrons of a neutral atom of: (a) carbon (Z = 6, A = 12), (b) oxygen (Z = 8, A = 16), (c) aluminium (Z = 13, A = 27), (d) calcium (Z = 20, A = 40).

4. A neutral atom has 17 protons and 18 neutrons. Find its atomic number, mass number and name the element.

5. An atom has mass number 31 and 16 neutrons. Find Z and the element, and the number of electrons in the neutral atom.

6. Find protons, neutrons and electrons in: (a) Na⁺ (A = 23, Z = 11), (b) Cl⁻ (A = 35, Z = 17), (c) Mg²⁺ (A = 24, Z = 12), (d) O²⁻ (A = 16, Z = 8), (e) Al³⁺ (A = 27, Z = 13).

7. A student says "Mg²⁺ has 14 electrons because it gained 2". Find the error and give the correct count.

8. Why do Na⁺, Mg²⁺, O²⁻ and Al³⁺ all have 10 electrons even though they are different elements? What is the difference between them in terms of protons?

9. Argon has mass number 40 and atomic number 18. Potassium has mass number 39 and atomic number 19. In which order should they be placed, and why?

10. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) Atomic number equals the number of neutrons.
    (b) In a neutral atom, the number of electrons equals the number of protons.
    (c) Mass number is always a whole number.
    (d) When an atom forms an ion, its number of protons changes.

### Answers

1. Atomic number Z = nucleus mein **protons ki ginti**. Har tatva ke atom mein protons ki ginti alag aur fixed hoti hai (H mein 1, C mein 6, Na mein 11), isliye Z se hi pata chalta hai ki atom kaunse tatva ka hai. Isiliye ise tatva ki **pehchaan** kehte hain.

2. Mass number A = protons + neutrons (nucleus ke saare kanon ki ginti). Neutrons = **A − Z**.

3. (a) ¹²₆C: protons **6**, neutrons 12 − 6 = **6**, electrons **6**. (b) ¹⁶₈O: protons **8**, neutrons 16 − 8 = **8**, electrons **8**. (c) ²⁷₁₃Al: protons **13**, neutrons 27 − 13 = **14**, electrons **13**. (d) ⁴⁰₂₀Ca: protons **20**, neutrons 40 − 20 = **20**, electrons **20**. Jaanch (protons + neutrons = A): 12, 16, 27, 40 ✅

4. Z = protons = **17**. A = 17 + 18 = **35**. Z = 17 wala tatva **chlorine** hai (³⁵₁₇Cl). Jaanch: 17 + 18 = 35 ✅.

5. Z = A − neutrons = 31 − 16 = **15**. Tatva **phosphorus** hai (³¹₁₅P). Neutral atom mein electrons = **15**. Jaanch: 15 + 16 = 31 ✅.

6. (a) Na⁺: protons **11**, neutrons **12**, electrons 11 − 1 = **10**. (b) Cl⁻: protons **17**, neutrons **18**, electrons 17 + 1 = **18**. (c) Mg²⁺: protons **12**, neutrons **12**, electrons 12 − 2 = **10**. (d) O²⁻: protons **8**, neutrons **8**, electrons 8 + 2 = **10**. (e) Al³⁺: protons **13**, neutrons **14**, electrons 13 − 3 = **10**. Jaanch (charge = protons − electrons): +1, −1, +2, −2, +3 ✅

7. Galti ye hai ki positive charge electrons **khone** se aata hai, paane se nahi. Mg ke neutral atom mein 12 electrons hote hain; Mg²⁺ mein 2 electrons chale gaye, to **12 − 2 = 10** electrons bache. Jaanch: charge = 12 − 10 = +2 ✅.

8. Chaaron ion mein electrons ki ginti 10 hai, par unke nucleus alag hain: Na⁺ mein 11 protons, Mg²⁺ mein 12, O²⁻ mein 8, Al³⁺ mein 13. Tatva protons se tay hota hai, electrons se nahi, isliye ye chaar alag tatva hain. (Electrons ke 10 hone ka asli reason Topic 5 mein aayega.)

9. Order Z se tay hota hai: pehle **argon (Z = 18)**, phir **potassium (Z = 19)**. Mass number se argon (40) potassium (39) ke baad aata, jo galat hota. Z hi tatva ki asli pehchaan hai, isliye Kram usi se banta hai.

10. (a) **False** ❌. Atomic number protons ki ginti hai; neutrons = A − Z. (b) **True** ✅. (c) **True** ✅. (d) **False** ❌. Ion banne par sirf electrons badalte hain, protons nahi.
