# Topic 5: Electronic Configuration aur Valency

Pichhle topic mein humne seekha ki har atom ki pehchaan uska **atomic number Z** hai, aur neutral atom mein electrons ki ginti bhi Z hi hoti hai. Topic 3 mein ye bhi dekha tha ki electrons **shells (K, L, M, N)** mein rehte hain aur har shell ki ek limit hoti hai. Ab do cheezein jodni hain: **kis atom ke electrons kis shell mein baithte hain**, aur isse atom ka **swabhav (valency)** kaise tay hota hai. Chapter 3 mein humne valency ki table ratni padi thi, aur ek "Kram" column bhi tha jiska reason adhoora chhoda tha. Aaj dono ka reason milega.

## Part A: Bharne ke niyam (Bohr-Bury)

Topic 3 ke niyam ek baar phir, chhote mein. In niyamon ko yaad rakhna hi poora kaam hai:

1. Electrons **pehle andar ke shells** mein bharte hain. K bharta hai, phir L, phir M, phir N.
2. Shell ki limit: **K = 2, L = 8, M = 18, N = 32** (2n² ka niyam).
3. **Baahari shell** mein kabhi **8 se zyada** electrons nahi (K shell baahari ho to 2 se zyada nahi).

Pehle 20 tatvon ke liye iska matlab ye hai: K mein 2 bharo, L mein 8, phir M mein sirf **8** tak, aur uske baad agla electron **N shell** mein jaata hai. Isliye M shell abhi 18 tak nahi bharta; wo pehle 8 par ruk jaata hai aur N shell shuru ho jaata hai. Ye cinema hall ka wahi khaas niyam hai.

## Part C: Atom ke diagram (ASCII)

Ab dekhte hain kuch atoms kaise dikhte hain. Beech ka `(Z+)` nucleus hai (Z protons). Har ring ek shell hai aur uspar `e` electron hain.

```
   Lithium (Z = 3)          Nitrogen (Z = 7)          Sodium (Z = 11)

         e                        e                         e
       .-----.              e   .-----.   e           e   .-----.   e
      /       \                /       \                 /       \
     ( e (+) e )            e ( e (+) e )             e ( e (+) e ) e     e  <- M
      \       /                \       /                 \       /
       '-----'                  '-----'               e   '-----'   e
                                  e                         e

   K 2, L 1                 K 2, L 5                  K 2, L 8, M 1
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Lithium (2, 1), nitrogen (2, 5) aur sodium (2, 8, 1) ke saral diagram (Topic 3 wale style mein): beech mein nucleus (+), uske chaaron taraf chhota gola K shell jisme 2 electron, gole ke bahar L shell ke electron, aur sodium mein door ek akela M shell ka electron. Gino: Li 2 + 1 = 3, N 2 + 5 = 7, Na 2 + 8 + 1 = 11. Ye bas sketch hai: asli atom mein electrons gole path par ek jaisi doori par nahi hote. Dhyan do ki sodium ke baahari shell mein sirf **1** electron hai.

Ye diagram bahut jaldi bhar jaate hain, isliye hum aksar sirf **shell-wise ginti** likhte hain: "2, 8, 1". Ye shorthand hai aur wahi kaam karta hai.

```

                                 L: 6           L: 8
                                                M: 7
   baahari: 1     baahari: 2     baahari: 6     baahari: 7
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Diagram ki jagah shell-wise ginti ka chhota roop: H (1), He (2), O (2, 6) aur Cl (2, 8, 7). Sabse neeche har atom ke baahari (valence) electrons.

## Part D: Duplet aur octet: atom kya chahta hai?

Is table mein sabse khaas tatva woh hain jinki baahari shell **poori bhari** hai: **He (2)**, **Ne (2, 8)** aur **Ar (2, 8, 8)**. Ye teeno **noble gases** hain. Inki ek baat bahut khaas hai: ye gases **lagbhag kisi ke saath nahi judti**, na khud mein, na kisi doosre tatva ke saath. Hum inhe "nishkriya" bhi kehte hain.

Isse ek bahut kaam ka vichaar nikalta hai:

> **Poori bhari baahari shell (duplet ya octet) stable hoti hai.**
> Isliye baaki atoms apni baahari shell ko poora karne ki koshish karte hain.

```
   Pehli shell (K): 2 seats               Baahari shell: 8 seats

        e   e                                  e   e
      +-------+                             e         e
      :  He   :                             e    Ne   e
      +-------+                                e   e

       Duplet = 2                            Octet = 8
       (K shell poori)                    (L shell poori)
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Duplet aur octet. Helium mein K shell 2 se poori bhari hai (duplet). Neon mein baahari L shell 8 se poori bhari hai (octet). Argon mein bhi baahari M shell 8 se bhari hai, isliye wo bhi stable hai. Ye teeno noble gases hain.

Kaise poora karte hain? Teen raaste hain: baahari electron **de dena**, kuch electron **le lena**, ya **share** karna. Aakhri raasta (share karna) aur kaun kaun se tatva kaun sa raasta chunte hain, ye sab **Chapter 6 "Chemical Bonding"** mein aayega. Abhi sirf ye jaan lo ki atom ke paas ye teen options hote hain.

## Part E: Valency: baahari electrons se

Chapter 3 mein valency ek "combining power" thi (kitne hooks). Ab hum uska asli reason jaante hain: valency ka matlab hai ki atom ko apni baahari shell **poori karne** ke liye **kitne electron dene, lene ya share karne** padenge.

**Niyam:**

- Agar baahari electrons **4 ya usse kam** hain, to valency = **baahari electrons ki ginti** (chhote number ko *dena* aasan hota hai).
- Agar baahari electrons **4 se zyada** hain, to valency = **8 − baahari electrons** (kuch electron *lena* aasan hota hai).
- Jinki shell poori hai (He, Ne, Ar), unki valency **0** hai.
- Hydrogen ka K shell 2 se poora hota hai: 1 electron hai, to valency 1.

Table dekho aur is niyam se valency nikalo:

| Tatva | Configuration | Baahari electrons | Niyam | Valency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H | 1 | 1 | 1 dena ya 1 lena (duplet) | 1 |
| He | 2 | 2 (poori) | duplet poora | 0 |
| Li | 2, 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Be | 2, 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| B | 2, 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| C | 2, 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| N | 2, 5 | 5 | 8 − 5 | 3 |
| O | 2, 6 | 6 | 8 − 6 | 2 |
| F | 2, 7 | 7 | 8 − 7 | 1 |
| Ne | 2, 8 | 8 | octet poora | 0 |
| Na | 2, 8, 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Mg | 2, 8, 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Al | 2, 8, 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| Si | 2, 8, 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| P | 2, 8, 5 | 5 | 8 − 5 | 3 |
| S | 2, 8, 6 | 6 | 8 − 6 | 2 |
| Cl | 2, 8, 7 | 7 | 8 − 7 | 1 |
| Ar | 2, 8, 8 | 8 | octet poora | 0 |
| K | 2, 8, 8, 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Ca | 2, 8, 8, 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |

Ab isse **Chapter 3 ki valency table** se milao: H 1, Li 1, Na 1, K 1, Be 2, Mg 2, Ca 2, B 3, Al 3, C 4, Si 4, N 3, P 3 (aur 5), O 2, S 2, F 1, Cl 1, aur He, Ne, Ar 0. **Saari 20 valency match karti hain.** Jo table humne ratti thi, uska raaz ye baahari electrons hain.

(Chapter 3 ki table mein P ke saamne 3 aur 5 dono the. Baahari electrons wala niyam P ke liye 3 aur S ke liye 2 deta hai; P ki 5 jaisi doosri valency kyun aati hai, wo aage ki class mein.)

## Part F: Solved examples

### Example 1. Nitrogen (Z = 7) ki configuration aur valency

- Electrons = 7. K mein 2, bache 5 L mein.
- Configuration: **2, 5**. Baahari electrons 5, jo 4 se zyada hain.
- Valency = 8 − 5 = **3**.

Jaanch: 2 + 5 = 7 = Z ✅. Chapter 3 mein N ki valency 3 thi ✅

### Example 2. Aluminium (Z = 13)

- K = 2, L = 8, bache 13 − 10 = 3 M mein.
- Configuration: **2, 8, 3**. Baahari electrons 3, jo 4 se kam hain.
- Valency = **3**.

Jaanch: 2 + 8 + 3 = 13 ✅. Chapter 3 mein Al = 3 ✅

### Example 3. Chlorine (Z = 17)

- K = 2, L = 8, bache 7 M mein.
- Configuration: **2, 8, 7**. Baahari 7 > 4, to valency = 8 − 7 = **1**.

Jaanch: 2 + 8 + 7 = 17 ✅. Chapter 3 mein Cl = 1 ✅

### Example 4. Calcium (Z = 20): N shell kaise?

- K = 2, L = 8. Bache 10. M shell ka niyam hai ki ab baahari shell 8 se zyada nahi, to M ko 8 mein roko: M = 8. Bache 2, wo N shell mein.
- Configuration: **2, 8, 8, 2**. Baahari electrons 2, valency **2**.

Jaanch: 2 + 8 + 8 + 2 = 20 ✅. Chapter 3 mein Ca = 2 ✅

### Example 5. Magnesium aur oxygen mein kaun kya karega?

Mg: 2, 8, 2 (baahari 2). O: 2, 6 (baahari 6).

- Mg ko apni baahari shell poori karni hai. 2 electron **dena** 6 lene se aasan hai; 2 dene ke baad L shell ke 8 electron baahari ban jaate hain (octet). Valency 2.
- O ke paas 6 hain; 2 **lene** se 8 poora ho jaata hai. Valency 8 − 6 = 2.

Jaanch: dono ki valency 2 hai, jo Chapter 3 ki table se match karti hai ✅. (Ye dono kaise jude, Chapter 6 mein.)

### Example 6. Configuration dekh ke tatva batao

Ek atom ki configuration **2, 8, 6** hai. Wo kaun sa tatva hai?

- Kul electrons = 2 + 8 + 6 = 16 = Z.
- Z = 16 wala tatva sulphur (S) hai. Baahari 6, valency 8 − 6 = 2.

Jaanch: S ka Z 16 hai aur valency 2 ✅

### Example 7. Neon ki shell aur uski nishkriyata

Neon (Z = 10): K = 2, L = 8. Configuration **2, 8**.

- Baahari shell L hai aur wo 8 se poori bhari hai. Octet poora.
- Isliye Ne ko electron dene, lene ya share karne ki zaroorat nahi. Valency **0**.

Jaanch: 2 + 8 = 10 = Z ✅

### Example 8. Silicon (Z = 14) aur carbon (Z = 6): ek jaisi valency kyun?

- C: 2, 4 (baahari 4). Si: 2, 8, 4 (baahari 4).
- Dono ke baahari electrons 4 hain, aur 4 ke liye "dena" aur "lena" dono 4 barabar hain. Dono ki valency **4**.

Jaanch: Chapter 3 mein C = 4 aur Si = 4 ✅. Dhyan do ki dono mein baahari electrons ki ginti same hai, shell alag hain. Isi se valency milti hai.

### Example 9. Hydrogen aur lithium ki ek jaisi valency

- H: 1 (K shell mein 1). Li: 2, 1 (baahari 1).
- Dono mein baahari electron 1 hai, to valency **1** dono ki.

Jaanch: Chapter 3 mein H 1 aur Li 1 ✅. Ek aur baat: Na (2, 8, 1) aur K (2, 8, 8, 1) bhi isi group mein hain, kyunki sabke baahari 1 electron hai.

## Part G: Na se Na⁺ aur Cl se Cl⁻: sirf electrons ki kahani

Ab Topic 4 ki baat jodte hain. Wahan humne dekha tha ki ion banne mein **protons nahi badalte, sirf electrons** ghat-badh jaate hain. Ab hum **shell ki nazar** se dekhte hain ki ye kyun hota hai.

**Sodium (Na):** Z = 11, configuration 2, 8, **1**. Baahari shell (M) mein sirf 1 electron hai. Wo akela electron nikal jaaye to? M shell khaali ho jaayegi aur ab L shell (jisme 8 hain) sabse **baahari** shell ban jaayegi: **octet**, neon jaisi arrangement. Is stable hisse ko pane ke liye Na **1 electron de** deta hai.

- Pehle: 11 protons (+11) aur 11 electrons (−11): neutral.
- 1 electron gaya: 11 protons (+11) aur **10** electrons (−10): kul charge **+1**.
- Naya naam: **Na⁺**. Configuration: **2, 8**.

**Chlorine (Cl):** Z = 17, configuration 2, 8, **7**. Baahari shell (M) mein 7 hain, octet mein sirf 1 kam. Isliye Cl **1 electron le leta hai**.

- Pehle: 17 protons (+17), 17 electrons (−17): neutral.
- 1 electron aaya: 17 protons (+17) aur **18** electrons (−18): kul charge **−1**.
- Naya naam: **Cl⁻**. Configuration: **2, 8, 8**.

```
   Na  --(1 electron de diya)-->  Na⁺
   protons  : 11                  protons  : 11
   electrons: 11                  electrons: 10
   shells   : 2, 8, 1             shells   : 2, 8     (neon jaisi)
   charge   : 0                   charge   : +1
   (M shell ka akela e gaya, ab L ke 8 baahari)

   Cl  --(1 electron le liya)-->  Cl⁻
   protons  : 17                  protons  : 17
   electrons: 17                  electrons: 18
   shells   : 2, 8, 7             shells   : 2, 8, 8  (argon jaisi)
   charge   : 0                   charge   : −1
   (M shell mein 1 jagah khaali thi, ek e aa gaya)
```

> **Figure 5.4** – Na ke 1 electron dene se Na⁺ (2, 8, neon jaisi arrangement) aur Cl ke 1 electron lene se Cl⁻ (2, 8, 8, argon jaisi arrangement) banta hai. Dono mein protons ki ginti wahi rehti hai, sirf electrons badalte hain.

Teen zaroori baatein:

1. **Na⁺ neon nahi ban gaya.** Uske paas ab bhi 11 protons hain, to wo ab bhi sodium hi hai. Bas uski **electron arrangement** neon jaisi ho gayi.
2. Isi tarah Cl⁻ argon nahi hai (17 protons).
3. Positive charge **electron ki kami** se aata hai (protons zyada, electrons kam), aur negative charge **electron ki zyadati** se. (Positive aur negative charge ka recall: [electricity.md](../../physics/electricity/electricity.md).)

**Aage kya?** Ye Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ ek doosre ko kheenchkar **namak (NaCl)** kaise banate hain, wo poori kahani Chapter 6 mein. Yahan bas ye jaano ki ion ki charge kyun milti hai.

Chapter 3 mein humne bola tha ki charge "electron lena ya dena" se aata hai. Ab uska reason ye hai ki **atom stable baahari shell chahta hai**.

### Example 10. Magnesium se Mg²⁺ aur oxygen se O²⁻

**Mg:** Z = 12, 2, 8, 2. 2 electron dene par 2, 8 bachta hai.

- Protons 12, electrons 12 − 2 = 10. Charge +12 − 10 = **+2**. Ion: **Mg²⁺**, configuration 2, 8.

**O:** Z = 8, 2, 6. 2 electron lene par 2, 8 ho jaata hai.

- Protons 8, electrons 8 + 2 = 10. Charge +8 − 10 = **−2**. Ion: **O²⁻**, configuration 2, 8.

Jaanch: Mg²⁺ ke 10 electron ✅, O²⁻ ke 10 electron ✅. Dono ki configuration neon jaisi (2, 8) hai ✅. Aur charge ka size valency ke barabar hai: Mg ki valency 2, O ki 2 ✅.

### Example 11. Lithium se Li⁺ aur calcium se Ca²⁺

**Li:** Z = 3, 2, 1. 1 electron gaya to 2 bachte hain: K shell baahari hai aur **duplet** poora, helium jaisa.

- Protons 3, electrons 2. Charge **+1**: **Li⁺**, configuration 2.

**Ca:** Z = 20, 2, 8, 8, 2. 2 electron gaye to 2, 8, 8: argon jaisi.

- Protons 20, electrons 18. Charge **+2**: **Ca²⁺**, configuration 2, 8, 8.

Jaanch: 3 − 1 = 2 electron ✅, 20 − 2 = 18 electron ✅. Ek aur check: Ca²⁺ aur Cl⁻ dono mein 18 electron hain ✅ (isliye dono ki configuration 2, 8, 8 hai).

### Example 12. Aluminium se Al³⁺ aur fluorine se F⁻

**Al:** 2, 8, 3 (Z = 13). 3 electron dene se 2, 8 bachta hai: Al³⁺, protons 13, electrons 10, charge +13 − 10 = **+3**.

**F:** 2, 7 (Z = 9). 1 electron lene par 2, 8: F⁻, protons 9, electrons 10, charge 9 − 10 = **−1**.

Jaanch: Al³⁺ aur F⁻ mein dono ke 10 electron hain, to dono ki configuration 2, 8 ✅. Valency match: Al = 3, F = 1 ✅.

Ye ek dilchasp pattern hai: **Na⁺, Mg²⁺, Al³⁺, O²⁻, F⁻ ye sab 10 electrons wale hain aur Ne jaise (2, 8) hain.** Lekin ye sab alag tatva hain kyunki protons alag hain. Yahi Topic 4 ke Example 17 ka jawab hai: ye ions 10 electrons par isliye rukte hain kyunki 2, 8 ek poora octet hai, neon jaisa stable.

## Part H: Kram aur valency ka pattern

Chapter 3 ki valency table mein ek "Kram" (serial number) column tha. Ab dekho wo Z hi tha. Agar Li se Ne aur Na se Ar tak ke tatvon ko Z ke order mein likho, to ek pattern milta hai:

```
   Valency:   1   2   3   4   3   2   1   0
   Tatva:     Li  Be  B   C   N   O   F   Ne
   Baahari e: 1   2   3   4   5   6   7   8

   Valency:   1   2   3   4   3   2   1   0
   Tatva:     Na  Mg  Al  Si  P   S   Cl  Ar
   Baahari e: 1   2   3   4   5   6   7   8
```

Dono row mein valency ka pattern ek hi hai: **1, 2, 3, 4, 3, 2, 1, 0**. Atomic number badhte jaate hain to baahari electrons 1 se 8 tak jaate hain aur phir wahi cycle dobara shuru ho jaati hai. Isliye valency bhi dohrati hai. Ye periodic pattern hi aage **Periodic Table** ka aadhaar banta hai: ek table jisme tatva Z ke order mein aise rakhe hain ki ek jaise swabhav wale tatva ek hi column mein aa jaayein (jaise Li, Na, K). Wo kahani aage ke chapter "Periodic Table" mein.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: M shell ko turant 18 se bhar dena**

Calcium ko 2, 8, 10 likhna galat hai. Baahari shell mein 8 se zyada nahi, to M mein 8 par ruko aur agla N mein: **2, 8, 8, 2**.

**Galti 2: Valency ko poora electron ginti samajhna**

N ke baahari 5 hain, par valency 5 nahi, **3** hai (8 − 5). Pehle dekho ki baahari electron 4 se zyada to nahi.

**Galti 3: Noble gas ki valency 8 likhna**

Ne ke baahari 8 hain, par octet poora hai, to koi electron dena, lena ya share karna nahi: valency **0**.

**Galti 4: Ion banne mein proton badalna**

Na → Na⁺ mein protons 11 hi rehte hain. Sirf electron 11 se 10 hota hai.

**Galti 5: Na⁺ ko neon samajhna**

Na⁺ ki configuration neon jaisi (2, 8) hai, par nucleus abhi bhi 11 protons ka hai, to wo sodium ka ion hai, neon nahi.

**Galti 6: Total electron na ginna**

Configuration likhne ke baad hamesha jod karke Z se milao. Jaise Cl: 2 + 8 + 7 = 17.

## Quick Recap

- **Electronic configuration** = electrons ka shell-wise batwara. Niyam: andar se bharo, K = 2, L = 8, M = 8 (pehle 20 tatvon ke liye), baahari shell mein 8 se zyada nahi.
- Pehle 20 tatvon ki configuration: H 1, He 2, Li 2,1, Be 2,2, B 2,3, C 2,4, N 2,5, O 2,6, F 2,7, Ne 2,8, Na 2,8,1, Mg 2,8,2, Al 2,8,3, Si 2,8,4, P 2,8,5, S 2,8,6, Cl 2,8,7, Ar 2,8,8, K 2,8,8,1, Ca 2,8,8,2.
- Baahari shell ke electrons = **valence electrons**. Poori bhari baahari shell: **duplet** (2) ya **octet** (8) = stable.
- **Noble gases** (He, Ne, Ar) ki shell poori hai, isliye valency 0 aur ye nishkriya hain.
- **Valency**: baahari electrons 4 tak to wahi ginti; 4 se zyada to 8 − (baahari electrons). Chapter 3 ki saari 20 valency isse match hoti hain.
- **Na → Na⁺** (2, 8, 1 → 2, 8): 1 electron gaya, protons 11, electrons 10, charge +1. **Cl → Cl⁻** (2, 8, 7 → 2, 8, 8): 1 electron aaya, protons 17, electrons 18, charge −1.
- Ion banne mein sirf electrons badalte hain, protons kabhi nahi, to tatva wahi rehta hai.
- Baahari electrons ka dohrana hi valency ka pattern aur aage ke Periodic Table ka raaz hai. Bonding ki poori kahani Chapter 6 mein.

## Practice Questions

1. Write the electronic configuration of: (a) carbon, (b) oxygen, (c) magnesium, (d) sulphur, (e) potassium. Check each by adding the electrons and comparing with Z.

2. What is the maximum number of electrons that the outermost shell of an atom can hold? What is the limit for the first shell? Why does calcium (Z = 20) have configuration 2, 8, 8, 2 and not 2, 8, 10?

3. What are valence electrons? How many valence electrons do each of these have: Li, N, Cl, Ar?

4. State the rule used to find valency from the number of valence electrons. Use it to find the valency of Na, Al, P and F, and compare with the valency table of Chapter 3.

5. Why are helium, neon and argon called noble (inert) gases? Give their configurations.

6. An atom has the configuration 2, 8, 3. Name the element, give its valency, and say what ion it forms.

7. Explain with electron counts how sodium becomes Na⁺. Give protons, electrons and charge before and after. Which noble gas has the same electron arrangement as Na⁺?

8. Explain how chlorine becomes Cl⁻. Give protons, electrons, charge and the configuration of Cl⁻. A student says "Cl⁻ is argon". What is wrong with this?

9. Find the number of electrons and the configuration of: (a) Mg²⁺, (b) O²⁻, (c) Li⁺, (d) Ca²⁺.

10. Silicon (2, 8, 4) and carbon (2, 4) have the same valency. Explain why, using the number of valence electrons.

11. Fill in: Na⁺, Mg²⁺, Al³⁺, F⁻ and O²⁻ all have ___ electrons and the configuration ___. Are they all the same element? Explain.

12. State True or False and correct the false ones:
    (a) Nitrogen has valency 5 because it has 5 valence electrons.
    (b) The valency of argon is 0.
    (c) When Na becomes Na⁺ it gains a proton.
    (d) The configuration 2, 8, 8 is a stable arrangement.

### Answers

1. (a) Carbon Z = 6: K 2, L 4, **2, 4** (2 + 4 = 6 ✅). (b) Oxygen Z = 8: **2, 6** (8 ✅). (c) Magnesium Z = 12: **2, 8, 2** (12 ✅). (d) Sulphur Z = 16: **2, 8, 6** (16 ✅). (e) Potassium Z = 19: K 2, L 8, M 8, N 1: **2, 8, 8, 1** (19 ✅).

2. Baahari shell mein zyada se zyada **8** electron aate hain, aur pehle shell (K) mein **2**. Calcium mein K = 2, L = 8, bache 10. M agar baahari ho to 8 se zyada nahi le sakta, to M = 8 par rukta hai aur bache 2 N shell mein jaate hain: **2, 8, 8, 2**. 2, 8, 10 mein M baahari ban ke 10 rakhta, jo niyam ke khilaaf hota.

3. **Valence electrons** = baahari shell ke electrons. Li (2, 1): **1**. N (2, 5): **5**. Cl (2, 8, 7): **7**. Ar (2, 8, 8): **8**.

4. Baahari electrons 4 ya kam ho to valency = wahi ginti. Zyada ho to valency = 8 − ginti. Poori shell ho to 0. Na (2, 8, 1): **1**. Al (2, 8, 3): **3**. P (2, 8, 5): 8 − 5 = **3**. F (2, 7): 8 − 7 = **1**. Chapter 3 ki table: Na 1, Al 3, P 3, F 1 ✅.

5. Inki baahari shell poori bhari hai (He mein duplet, Ne aur Ar mein octet), isliye ye shayad hi kisi se judti hain. Configuration: He **2**, Ne **2, 8**, Ar **2, 8, 8**.

6. Electrons = 13, to Z = 13: **aluminium**. Baahari 3, valency **3**. Ye 3 electron deta hai to **Al³⁺** (10 electrons, configuration 2, 8) banta hai.

7. Na: 11 protons, 11 electrons, charge 0. Ek electron de ke: 11 protons, **10** electrons, charge +11 − 10 = **+1**, yaani Na⁺ (2, 8). Ye **neon** jaisi arrangement hai.

8. Cl: 17 protons, 17 electrons. 1 electron le ke: 17 protons, **18** electrons, charge 17 − 18 = **−1**, Cl⁻, configuration **2, 8, 8**. "Cl⁻ is argon" galat hai kyunki Cl⁻ ke nucleus mein 17 protons hain jabki argon mein 18; sirf electron arrangement argon jaisi hai.

9. (a) Mg²⁺: 12 − 2 = **10** electrons, **2, 8**. (b) O²⁻: 8 + 2 = **10** electrons, **2, 8**. (c) Li⁺: 3 − 1 = **2** electrons, **2** (K shell). (d) Ca²⁺: 20 − 2 = **18** electrons, **2, 8, 8**.

10. Dono ke valence electrons **4** hain (C: 2, 4 aur Si: 2, 8, 4). 4 ke liye dena ya lena dono 4 hi hota hai, isliye dono ki valency **4**. Shell alag hain, par baahari electrons ki ginti ek jaisi hai.

11. Sab mein **10** electrons aur configuration **2, 8**. Phir bhi ye alag tatva hain, kyunki unke protons alag hain (11, 12, 13, 9, 8).

12. (a) **False** ❌. 5 valence electrons hain, par valency 8 − 5 = **3**. (b) **True** ✅. Octet poora hai. (c) **False** ❌. Na ek **electron** khota hai, proton nahi badalte. (d) **True** ✅ (argon jaisi octet).
