# Topic 6: Isotopes, Isobars aur Unke Upyog + Chapter Ka Saar

Pichhle topics mein humne seekha ki atom ki pehchaan uska **atomic number (Z)** hai, yaani protons ki ginti, aur electrons ki shell-wali seating se uski chemistry tay hoti hai. Ek sawaal abhi baaki hai: kya ek hi tatva ke saare atoms bilkul ek jaise hote hain? Pehle ek baat yaad karo: Chapter 4 mein chlorine ka atomic mass **35.5** tha, aur wahan likha tha ki ye ek *average* hai aur iska raaz is chapter mein khulega. Aaj wo raaz khulta hai, aur hum is chapter ke saare 6 topics ek table mein bhi samet lenge.

## Part A: Isotopes, ek hi tatva ke "bhai"

Ab tak hum maan rahe the ki ek tatva ke saare atoms bilkul ek jaise hote hain (Dalton ka P2, [Chapter 4](../atoms-and-molecules/atoms-and-molecules-part3.md); Topic 1 mein dekha tha ki ye baat adhoori hai). Lekin protons ki ginti (Z) hi tatva ki pehchaan hai, aur **neutrons ki ginti alag ho sakti hai.** Neutrons ki ginti badalne se mass number A = protons + neutrons badalta hai, par tatva wahi rehta hai.

> **Isotopes:** ek hi tatva ke wo atoms jinka **atomic number same** (protons same) hai par **mass number alag** (neutrons alag) hai.

Naam likhne ka tareeka: tatva ka naam, hyphen, mass number. Jaise carbon-12. Ya symbol ke saath: ¹²₆C, yaani upar mass number A = 12 aur neeche atomic number Z = 6 (Topic 4 jaisa).

### Hydrogen ke teen isotopes

| Naam | Symbol | Protons (Z) | Neutrons (A − Z) | Mass number (A) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Protium (ordinary hydrogen) | ¹₁H | 1 | 0 | 1 |
| Deuterium | ²₁H | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| Tritium | ³₁H | 1 | 2 | 3 |

Neutrons = A − Z: 1 − 1 = 0, 2 − 1 = 1, 3 − 1 = 2 ✅

```
   Protium        Deuterium       Tritium
   (A = 1)        (A = 2)         (A = 3)

     (+)           (+)(n)          (+)(n)(n)
     nucleus       nucleus         nucleus
      o             o               o
   1 electron     1 electron      1 electron

   (+) = proton,  (n) = neutron,  o = electron
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Hydrogen ke teen isotopes: sabme 1 proton aur 1 electron, par neutrons 0, 1 aur 2. Isliye mass number 1, 2 aur 3. (Sketch sirf ginti dikhane ke liye hai; asal mein electron nucleus se bahut door hota hai.)

Dhyan do: teeno ke naam alag hain, par teeno **hydrogen** hi hain, kyunki protons teeno mein 1.

### Carbon ke isotopes

| Isotope | Z | A | Neutrons (A − Z) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Carbon-12 | 6 | 12 | 12 − 6 = 6 |
| Carbon-13 | 6 | 13 | 13 − 6 = 7 |
| Carbon-14 | 6 | 14 | 14 − 6 = 8 |

Teeno mein 6 protons aur (neutral atom mein) 6 electrons. Nivaan ke sawaal ka jawab mil gaya: carbon-12, carbon-13 aur carbon-14 alag-alag "type" nahi, ek hi carbon ke atoms hain jinme neutrons 6, 7 aur 8 hain. Prakriti mein carbon-12 sabse zyada milta hai.

### Chlorine ke do isotopes

| Isotope | Z | A | Neutrons (A − Z) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chlorine-35 | 17 | 35 | 35 − 17 = 18 |
| Chlorine-37 | 17 | 37 | 37 − 17 = 20 |

Prakriti mein chlorine ke lagbhag **75%** atoms chlorine-35 ke aur lagbhag **25%** chlorine-37 ke hote hain (yaani ratio lagbhag 3 : 1).

## Part B: Chlorine ka 35.5, raaz khula

Chapter 4 mein hum ne dekha tha ki atomic mass table mein Cl = 35.5 u hai, jabki kisi atom mein protons + neutrons poori ginti hote hain, 35.5 kaise ho sakta hai? Jawab: **koi bhi ek chlorine atom 35.5 u ka nahi hai.** Kuch atoms 35 u ke hain, kuch 37 u ke, aur table mein unka **average** likha hai.

```
   Chlorine ke 100 atoms ka namoona (har [] = 5 atoms)

   Cl-35 :  [] [] [] [] [] [] [] [] [] [] [] [] [] [] []   = 75 atoms
   Cl-37 :  [] [] [] [] []                                 = 25 atoms

   Chhota version (har 4 atoms mein):
        [35] [35] [35] [37]       ->  3 : 1
```

> **Figure 6.2** – Chlorine ka mixture: har 4 atoms mein lagbhag 3 atoms chlorine-35 ke aur 1 chlorine-37 ka (ratio 3 : 1, yaani 75% aur 25%). Mixture ka average mass 35.5 u banta hai.

**Hisaab (weighted average):**

Average = (0.75 × 35) + (0.25 × 37)

- 0.75 × 35 = 26.25
- 0.25 × 37 = 9.25
- Jod: 26.25 + 9.25 = **35.5 u** ✅

**Dusra tareeka (4 atoms lekar):** 3 atoms 35 u ke aur 1 atom 37 u ka. Kul = 3 × 35 + 37 = 105 + 37 = 142. Average = 142 ÷ 4 = **35.5** ✅

Ye ek class ke marks jaisa hai: agar 3 bachchon ke 35 marks aur 1 bachche ke 37 marks hon, to class ka average 35.5 hoga, par kisi ek bachche ke 35.5 marks nahi honge. Isi tarah Chapter 4 ka loose end band hua: Cl = 35.5 ek **average** hai.

(Ye 75% aur 25% "lagbhag" wali ginti hai; asli natural hisse thode alag ho sakte hain, par Class 9 ke liye yahi kaafi hai.)

## Part C: Isotopes ki chemistry same kyun?

Chemistry (kaun se tatva se kaise judna hai) **outer electrons** par chalti hai, jaisa Topic 5 mein dekha. Aur isotopes mein protons same hain, to neutral atom mein **electrons bhi same**, aur unki shell-arrangement bhi same.

| Isotope | Electrons | Electronic configuration | Outer electrons | Valency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chlorine-35 | 17 | 2, 8, 7 | 7 | 1 |
| Chlorine-37 | 17 | 2, 8, 7 | 7 | 1 |
| Carbon-12 | 6 | 2, 4 | 4 | 4 |
| Carbon-14 | 6 | 2, 4 | 4 | 4 |

Isliye ek tatva ke saare isotopes ka **chemical behaviour lagbhag same** hota hai. Neutrons chemistry mein hisse nahi lete; wo sirf **mass** badalte hain. Isliye physical properties (jaise mass, ya density) thodi alag ho sakti hain.

## Part D: Isobars, alag tatva par same mass number

Ab ulta sochte hain. Do alag tatvon ke atoms ka **mass number same** ho sakta hai, par atomic number alag.

> **Isobars:** alag tatvon ke atoms jinka **mass number same** hai par **atomic number alag** hai.

| Atom | Z (protons) | A | Neutrons (A − Z) | Electronic configuration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Argon-40 | 18 | 40 | 40 − 18 = 22 | 2, 8, 8 |
| Calcium-40 | 20 | 40 | 40 − 20 = 20 | 2, 8, 8, 2 |

```
   Argon-40                    Calcium-40
   Z = 18, A = 40              Z = 20, A = 40

   protons  : 18               protons  : 20
   neutrons : 22               neutrons : 20
   ---------------             ---------------
   kul      : 40               kul      : 40   (same A)

   electrons: 18 (2,8,8)       electrons: 20 (2,8,8,2)
```

> **Figure 6.3** – Argon-40 aur calcium-40 isobars hain: dono ka mass number 40, par protons 18 aur 20 alag, isliye dono alag tatva. Argon inert gas hai (outer shell poori), calcium ek dhatu (2 outer electrons).

Dono ka A = 40, lekin chemistry bilkul alag, kyunki electrons alag hain (2, 8, 8 aur 2, 8, 8, 2). Ye bhi dekha ki alag Z wale atoms ki chemistry alag hoti hai.

## Part E: Isotopes ke upyog (sirf jaankari ke liye)

Kuch isotopes **radioactive** hote hain: unke nucleus apne aap se kirnein (radiation) chhodte hain. Class 9 mein hum sirf unke naam aur upyog jaante hain; ye hisaab-kitaab aage ki class ke liye hai.

| Isotope | Upyog |
|---|---|
| Carbon-14 | Purani lakdi, haddiyon aur fossils ki umar pata karna (jaise museum ki naav) |
| Iodine-131 | Thyroid (gale ki gland) ki kuch bimariyon ka ilaaj aur jaanch |
| Cobalt-60 | Cancer ke ilaaj mein aur medical saaman ko germs-free (sterilise) karne mein |
| Uranium-235 | Nuclear reactor mein eendhan (fuel), bijli banane ke liye |
| Deuterium (heavy water mein) | Kuch nuclear reactors mein |

**Carbon dating ka vichaar, bina hisaab ke:** jeevit ped-paudhe aur jaanwar apne andar thoda carbon-14 rakhte hain. Marne ke baad naya carbon-14 aana band ho jaata hai aur purana dheere-dheere kam hota hai. Lakdi ya haddi mein kitna carbon-14 bacha hai, ye naap kar vaigyanik uski umar ka andaaza lagate hain.

> **Suraksha ki baat:** radioactive padarth sirf training-prapt visheshagyon aur kade niyamon ke saath sambhale jaate hain. Inhe kabhi khud nahi chhoona ya prayog karna chahiye. Ye topic sirf padhne aur samajhne ke liye hai.

## Part F: Isotopes aur isobars, ek tulna

| Baat | Isotopes | Isobars |
|---|---|---|
| Tatva | Same tatva | Alag tatva |
| Atomic number (Z) | Same | Alag |
| Mass number (A) | Alag | Same |
| Neutrons | Alag | Alag (aam taur par) |
| Chemical behaviour | Lagbhag same | Alag |
| Example | ¹₁H, ²₁H, ³₁H aur Cl-35, Cl-37 | Ar-40 aur Ca-40 |

Yaad rakhne ka tareeka: **Iso-TOPE: same TOPE (jagah) yaani same position, same tatva.** **Iso-BAR: same BAR (bhaar) yaani same mass number.**

### Solved examples

**Example 1.** Ek atom mein 8 protons aur 9 neutrons hain. Uska A aur naam kya?
Z = 8 yaani oxygen. A = 8 + 9 = 17. Naam oxygen-17, symbol ¹⁷₈O.

**Example 2.** Neon ka Z = 10. Neon-20 aur neon-22 mein neutrons kitne?
Neon-20: 20 − 10 = 10. Neon-22: 22 − 10 = 12. Ye isotopes hain (Z same, A alag).

**Example 3.** Kya Na-24 (Z = 11) aur Mg-24 (Z = 12) isotopes hain ya isobars?
Z alag (11 aur 12), A same (24): **isobars.** Neutrons: Na 24 − 11 = 13, Mg 24 − 12 = 12.

**Example 4.** Ek tatva ke do isotopes ka mass 10 u (20%) aur 11 u (80%) hai. Average?
0.20 × 10 + 0.80 × 11 = 2.0 + 8.8 = **10.8 u.**

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Isotopes ka Z alag hota hai"**
Nahi. Isotopes mein Z (protons) same hota hai; alag neutrons hote hain, isliye A alag.

**Galti 2: "Cl ka 35.5 ek atom ka mass hai"**
Nahi, ye chlorine-35 aur chlorine-37 ke mixture ka average hai.

**Galti 3: Isotopes aur isobars ko ulta kar dena**
Isotopes: same Z. Isobars: same A.

**Galti 4: "Isotopes ki chemistry alag hoti hai"**
Electrons same hain, isliye chemistry lagbhag same hoti hai.

**Galti 5: "Saare isotopes radioactive hote hain"**
Nahi. Carbon-12 aur chlorine-35 radioactive nahi hain; sirf kuch isotopes hote hain.

**Galti 6: Average mein sirf 35 aur 37 ko jod ke 2 se bhaag dena**
Weights (75% aur 25%) lene padte hain: (35 + 37) ÷ 2 = 36 galat hai, sahi 35.5 hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Isotopes:** same Z, alag A (alag neutrons). Hydrogen: protium, deuterium, tritium (0, 1, 2 neutrons). Carbon-12, -13, -14 (6, 7, 8 neutrons).
- **Chlorine:** Cl-35 (18 neutrons, lagbhag 75%) aur Cl-37 (20 neutrons, lagbhag 25%). Average = 0.75 × 35 + 0.25 × 37 = **35.5**.
- Isotopes ke electrons same, isliye chemistry lagbhag same; neutrons sirf mass badalte hain.
- **Isobars:** same A, alag Z. Ar-40 (18 protons, 22 neutrons) aur Ca-40 (20 protons, 20 neutrons).
- **Upyog:** carbon-14 (umar), iodine-131 (thyroid), cobalt-60 (cancer ka ilaaj, sterilise), uranium-235 (reactor fuel), deuterium (heavy water).
- Radioactive padarth sirf visheshagyon ke liye: koi activity nahi.

## Practice Questions

1. Define isotopes and isobars with one example of each.

2. Write the number of protons, neutrons and electrons in a neutral atom of (a) tritium, (b) carbon-14, (c) chlorine-37.

3. Calculate the average atomic mass of chlorine, given that about 75% of its atoms are chlorine-35 and about 25% are chlorine-37.

4. Why do the isotopes of an element have the same chemical behaviour?

5. Argon-40 (Z = 18) and calcium-40 (Z = 20) are isobars. Find the number of neutrons in each and write their electronic configurations.

6. Hydrogen has three isotopes. Name them and give the number of neutrons in each.

7. Two isotopes of an element have masses 10 u and 11 u in the ratio 1 : 4 (20% and 80%). Find the average atomic mass.

8. Name one use each of carbon-14, iodine-131 and cobalt-60.

9. In a sample of 8 chlorine atoms, the atoms are in the ratio 3 : 1 of chlorine-35 to chlorine-37. How many atoms of each type are there? Find the average mass of these 8 atoms.

10. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) Isotopes have the same number of protons.
    (b) Isobars always belong to the same element.
    (c) Chlorine-35 and chlorine-37 have the same electronic configuration.
    (d) Every isotope is radioactive.

### Answers

1. **Isotopes:** ek hi tatva ke atoms jinka atomic number same, mass number alag; jaise carbon-12 aur carbon-14. **Isobars:** alag tatvon ke atoms jinka mass number same, atomic number alag; jaise Ar-40 aur Ca-40.

2. (a) Tritium ³₁H: protons 1, neutrons 3 − 1 = **2**, electrons **1**. (b) Carbon-14: protons 6, neutrons 14 − 6 = **8**, electrons **6**. (c) Chlorine-37: protons 17, neutrons 37 − 17 = **20**, electrons **17**.

3. 0.75 × 35 = 26.25; 0.25 × 37 = 9.25; jod = **35.5 u** ✅

4. Chemistry outer electrons se tay hoti hai. Isotopes mein protons same, to electrons aur unki arrangement same; neutrons sirf mass badalte hain. Isliye chemical behaviour lagbhag same.

5. Ar-40: 40 − 18 = **22 neutrons**, configuration **2, 8, 8**. Ca-40: 40 − 20 = **20 neutrons**, configuration **2, 8, 8, 2**.

6. **Protium** (0 neutrons), **deuterium** (1 neutron), **tritium** (2 neutrons).

7. 0.20 × 10 + 0.80 × 11 = 2.0 + 8.8 = **10.8 u.**

8. Carbon-14: purani lakdi, haddiyon ki umar pata karna. Iodine-131: thyroid ke ilaaj mein. Cobalt-60: cancer ke ilaaj ya sterilise karne mein.

9. 3 : 1 mein 8 atoms: 6 atoms chlorine-35 ke aur 2 atoms chlorine-37 ke. Kul mass = 6 × 35 + 2 × 37 = 210 + 74 = 284. Average = 284 ÷ 8 = **35.5** ✅

10. (a) **True** ✅. Isotopes ka Z same hota hai. (b) **False** ❌. Isobars alag tatvon ke atoms hote hain (Z alag). (c) **True** ✅. Dono 2, 8, 7. (d) **False** ❌. Sirf kuch isotopes radioactive hote hain; carbon-12 nahi hai.
