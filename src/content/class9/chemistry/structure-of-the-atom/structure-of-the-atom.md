# Topic 1: Atom Ke Andar Kya Hai: Electron, Proton aur Neutron

Pichhle chapter mein Dalton ne kaha tha ki atom sabse chhota kan hai jise todna mumkin nahi. Par humne wahi table mein ye bhi likha tha ki ye baat **adhoori** hai, aur ki iska jawab is chapter mein milega. Ab wo waqt aa gaya hai. Is topic mein hum dekhenge ki vaigyanikon ko kaise pata chala ki atom ke andar **teen** tarah ke chhote kan hote hain: **electron, proton aur neutron**. Aur ye bhi ki ek atom bahar se "neutral" kyun dikhta hai.

---

## Cling wrap ka chipakna

Mr. Talwar rasoi mein khade the aur unki bhanji Manya unke saath roti ke dibbe pack kar rahi thi. Cling wrap ka roll kholte hi plastic ki patli parat Manya ke haath se chipak gayi. Usne jhatka diya to wo haath se chhoot kar seedhe steel ke katore par ja chipki.

"Mama, ye to bina glue ke chipak rahi hai!" Manya hairaan thi.

"Haan," Mr. Talwar ne kaha. "Aur dekho, jitna zyada tum ise khinchti ho, utna zyada ye chipakti hai."

Manya ne ek aur parat kheenchi aur is baar usse apni baanh ke baalon ke paas laayi. Baal halke se khade ho gaye.

"Ye wahi cheez hai jo tumne Physics mein padhi thi," Mr. Talwar ne kaha. "Jab do cheezein ragad khaati hain, to un par **charge** aa jaata hai. Cling wrap par charge aa gaya, isliye ye katore aur haath ko kheench rahi hai." (Charge aur static electricity ki poori baat [electricity.md](../../physics/electricity/electricity.md) mein hai.)

Manya ne soch kar poochha, "To ye charge aaya kahan se? Cling wrap to sirf plastic hai."

Mr. Talwar muskuraye. "Bahut achcha sawaal. Iska jawab ye hai ki plastic ke **andar**, aur katore ke andar, aur tumhare haath ke andar, **charge wale kan** pehle se hi bhare hain. Aaj hum wahi kahani sunenge ki unhe kaise dhoondha gaya."

> **Ghar par aajmao (surakshit):** Ek plastic ki kangi (comb) ko sookhe baalon par 20 baar ragdo, aur phir use kaagaz ke chhote tukdon ke paas laao. Tukde kangi se chipak jaayenge. Gila mausam ho to asar kam dikhega. Bas kangi aur kaagaz hi lo, aur kisi bijli ke upkaran ya socket ke paas ye mat karo.

## Part A: Pehla sabut: gas mein bijli, aur ek negative kan

Cling wrap jaisi cheezein dikhati hain ki charge kisi na kisi kan se aata hoga. Par wo kan kaunsa hai? Isse dhoondhne ke liye vaigyanikon ne ek khaas upkaran istemaal kiya: ek **kaanch ki nali** jisme se lagbhag saari hawa nikaal li jaati thi. Nali ke dono sirohn par dhatu ki do plate (electrode) lagi hoti thi aur unke beech **bahut zyada voltage** lagaya jaata tha. Is upkaran ko cathode ray tube kehte hain.

**Dhyan do:** ye prayog ek lab ka khatarnak upkaran hai (bahut zyada voltage aur khaali kaanch ki nali). Ise sirf visheshagya lab mein, suraksha ke saath kiya jaata hai. Ghar par ya school mein bina shikshak ke koi koshish nahi karni hai. Hum yahan sirf iski kahani aur tasveer dekhenge.

Nali mein se jab bijli guzari gayi, to **negative plate (cathode)** se kuch rays nikli aur positive plate (anode) ki taraf chalin. Inhe **cathode rays** kaha gaya. Aur kamaal ye ki **nali mein koi bhi gas ho, ya plate kisi bhi dhatu ki ho, rays ek jaisi hi nikalti thi.**

```
   Cathode (-)                                        Anode (+)
   +-----+                                            +-----+
   :     :  e- e- e- ---- cathode rays ---->          :     :
   +-----+                                            +-----+
      :     kaanch ki nali (hawa bahut kam)              :
      :                                                  :
      +----------(-)  High voltage source  (+)-----------+
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Cathode ray tube ka saral roop (sketch, scale par nahi): kaanch ki nali, jisme se hawa lagbhag nikaal li gayi hai, ek sire par negative plate (cathode) aur doosre par positive plate (anode). Cathode se rays nikal kar anode ki taraf chalti hain. Ye rays **negative charge wale kanon** ki dhaara hain.

**J. J. Thomson (1897)** ne in rays par prayog kiye. Unhone dekha ki agar rays ke raaste ke paas ek positive plate rakho, to rays us plate ki **taraf mud jaati hain**. Aur negative plate ke paas rakho to wo us se door bhaagti hain. Tumne Physics mein dekha tha ki **vipreet charge ek doosre ko kheenchte hain aur sajaatiya charge door dhakelte hain**. Rays positive ki taraf mudi, to matlab rays par **negative charge** hai.

Thomson ne isse bhi aage ek baat dekhi: ye negative kan **har gas aur har dhatu se milte the**. Yaani ye kan kisi ek khaas tatva ka hissa nahi, balki **har atom ka hissa** hain. Aur ye kan itne halke the ki hydrogen ke atom se bhi bahut halke. Inhe naam diya gaya **electron**.

**Electron ke gun:**

- Charge: **negative**, jise hum **−1 ikai** kehte hain
- Dravyaman: ek proton ka lagbhag **1/1840** (sahi mulya 1/1837 ke aas-paas)
- Symbol: **e⁻**

Ek cheez aur dhyan rakho. Hum ye nahi kehte ki Thomson ne hi aakhri baat kah di. Unhone ye dikhaya ki atom ke andar kuch negative kan hain, aur isse aage ki kahani agle topic mein aayegi.

## Part B: Doosra sabut: ulti disha mein chalti rays aur proton

Nali mein negative kan to mil gaye. Par atom to **bahar se charge-rahit** hota hai. Agar atom mein negative kan hain, to unhe santulit karne ke liye kahin na kahin **positive charge** bhi hona chahiye. Ye positive charge kahan hai?

Isi sawaal ka ek hissa **E. Goldstein (1886)** ne dhoondha. Unhone ek aisi hi nali li, par cathode mein **chhote chhote chhed (canals)** banaye. Jab bijli guzari, to cathode rays to anode ki taraf chalin, par **ulti disha mein, cathode ke chhedon se ho kar peeche ki taraf**, kuch aur rays bhi nikli. Chhedon se aane ki wajah se inhe **canal rays** kaha gaya.

```
   Anode (+)                                          Cathode (-), chhed wali
   +-----+                                            +-----+
   :     :  <==== cathode rays (e-) =====             : o   :
   :     :                                            : o   :  <- chhed (canals)
   :     :  ===== canal rays (+) ==========>          : o   :  ===> canal rays (+)
   +-----+                                            +-----+       chhedon se peeche nikli
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Canal rays ka saral chitra (sketch). Cathode rays negative plate se anode ki taraf jaati hain, jabki chhed wali plate ke piche se **positive kan** ulti disha mein, yaani cathode ke piche ki taraf, nikalte hain. Inhe Goldstein ne dekha.

Canal rays ke baare mein teen baatein:

1. Ye rays **positive charge** wali thi (ye electric ya magnet ke asar se electron se ulti taraf mudti thi).
2. Inke kan **bhaari** the, electron se kaafi zyada.
3. Inka dravyaman **nali ke andar ke gas par nirbhar** karta tha: jis gas se nali bhari, usi ke hisaab se kan alag nikle. Sabse halke kan **hydrogen** gas se mile.

Hydrogen se mile sabse halke positive kan ko hi **proton (p⁺)** kaha gaya. Ye naam baad mein **Rutherford** ne diya. Aaj hum jaante hain ki **ordinary hydrogen** ke atom mein **ek proton aur koi neutron nahi** hota (neutron abhi aage hai).

**Proton ke gun:**

- Charge: **positive**, **+1 ikai** (electron ke barabar par ulta)
- Dravyaman: lagbhag **1 u** (Chapter 4 wali atomic mass unit)
- Symbol: **p⁺**

Charge ke baare mein ek baat dhyan se: electron ka charge −1 aur proton ka +1 ikai hai, yaani **dono ka charge ki matra barabar hai, bas chinh ulta hai**. Par dono ka dravyaman bilkul alag hai: ek proton, ek electron se lagbhag 1840 guna bhaari hai.

## Part C: Teesra kan: bina charge wala neutron

Ab maan lo hum helium ka atom lein. Usme 2 proton aur 2 electron hain. Charge ka hisaab theek baithta hai (+2 aur −2). Par helium ka naapa gaya atomic mass (Chapter 4 wali u ikai mein) lagbhag **4 u** hai. Agar sirf 2 proton hote, to wo 2 u hona chahiye tha (electron to bahut halke hain, ginti mein hi nahi aate). Baaki ka **dravyaman kahan se aaya**?

Kuch to aur hona chahiye tha jo **dravyaman rakhta ho par charge na rakhta ho**, kyunki agar charge hota to atom ka santulan bigad jaata.

Is sawaal ka jawab **James Chadwick (1932)** ne diya. Unhone ek aisa kan khoj nikala jiska dravyaman lagbhag proton ke barabar tha, par jis par **koi charge nahi** tha. Isko **neutron (n)** kaha gaya. Isi se helium ke 4 u ka hisaab baitha: 2 proton + 2 neutron = 4 u (electron ka dravyaman na ke barabar).

**Neutron ke gun:**

- Charge: **zero (koi charge nahi)**
- Dravyaman: lagbhag **1 u**
- Symbol: **n**

Dhyan do: ye "bina charge wala" hone ki wajah se neutron ko dhoondhna bahut mushkil tha. Electron aur proton ko to electric aur magnet se mod kar pakda ja sakta tha, par neutron par in cheezon ka asar nahi hota. Isi liye iski khoj **1932** mein hui, baaki dono se kaafi baad mein.

## Part D: Teeno kanon ki table

| Kan | Symbol | Charge | Relative mass (proton ke mukable) | Dravyaman (u mein, lagbhag) | Kahan milta hai |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Electron** | e⁻ | −1 | lagbhag 1/1840 | lagbhag 0 (bahut kam) | Atom ke andar, nucleus ke bahar |
| **Proton** | p⁺ | +1 | 1 | lagbhag 1 | Atom ke centre (nucleus) mein |
| **Neutron** | n | 0 | lagbhag 1 | lagbhag 1 | Atom ke centre (nucleus) mein |

Ek baat dhyan se: table ki aakhri column mein "nucleus" shabd aaya hai. Ye kya hai, aur teeno kan atom mein kahan rehte hain, ye **agle topic** mein Rutherford ke prayog se samjhenge. Abhi sirf itna yaad rakho ki proton aur neutron **bhaari** hain aur electron bahut **halka**; kaun kahan baitha hai, iska saboot Topic 2 mein aayega.

**Zaroori nateeja:** atom ka lagbhag saara dravyaman **proton aur neutron** se aata hai. Electron ka dravyaman itna kam hai ki ginti mein nahi aata.

## Part E: Atom neutral kyun hota hai?

Ab wo sawaal jo Manya ne poochha tha. Cling wrap jaisi cheezein charge ki wajah se chipakti hain, par roz ki zyadatar cheezein (steel ka katora, kursi, hawa) bina ragde chipakti nahi. Aisa kyun?

Kyunki **ek normal (neutral) atom mein protons ki sankhya electrons ki sankhya ke barabar hoti hai.** Har +1 ko ek −1 kaat deta hai, aur kul charge zero ho jaata hai.

**Example 1 (hydrogen).** Hydrogen ke atom mein 1 proton (+1) aur 1 electron (−1). Kul charge = +1 − 1 = **0** ✅

**Example 2 (helium).** 2 protons (+2) aur 2 electrons (−2). Kul charge = +2 − 2 = **0** ✅

**Example 3 (carbon).** 6 protons (+6) aur 6 electrons (−6). Kul charge = +6 − 6 = **0** ✅ (Carbon mein 6 neutron bhi hote hain, par unka charge zero hai, to koi farak nahi padta.)

```
   Ek neutral atom (jaise helium): sketch, scale par nahi

            (e-)
        .-----------.
       /   [p+][n]   \        2 proton (+)   = +2
      (    [n][p+]    )       2 neutron (0)  =  0
       \             /        2 electron (-) = -2
        '-----------'         Kul charge     =  0
            (e-)
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Ek neutral atom (jaise helium) ki saral tasveer. Beech mein bhaari kan (2 proton aur 2 neutron) aur bahar halke electrons. Positive aur negative charge barabar hain, to atom bahar se neutral dikhta hai. (Ye sirf ginti dikhane wali saral tasveer hai. Kaun sa kan atom mein theek kahan baithta hai, ye Topic 2 mein Rutherford ke prayog ki kahani hai.)

**To cling wrap kaise chipakti hai?** Ragadne par kuch **electrons** ek cheez se doosri cheez par chale jaate hain (electron halke hain aur bahar ki taraf hain, proton nahi hilte). Jis cheez ne electron khoye, uske paas ab protons zyada hain, to wo positive ho jaati hai. Jis cheez ko electrons mile, wo negative ho jaati hai. Yahi **electron lena ya dena** hai jiska zikr Chapter 3 mein ions ke saath hua tha.

**Ek zaroori baat.** Ragadne se sirf electron transfer hote hain, proton ya neutron nahi, kyunki wo atom ke andar mazbooti se jame hain.

## Part F: Kya har atom mein kan alag hote hain?

Nahi. Ye ek bahut sundar baat hai: **sab atoms mein wahi teen kisam ke kan hote hain.** Hydrogen ke atom ke electron aur gold ke atom ke electron bilkul ek jaise hain. Ek proton, chahe kahin ka ho, ek hi jaisa hota hai.

Toh tatvon mein farak kahan se aata hai? **Kanon ki ginti se.** Jaise Lego ke wahi blocks se alag-alag cheezein bana sakte ho, waise hi wahi electron, proton aur neutron alag-alag ginti mein milakar alag-alag tatva ban jaate hain.

| Tatva | Proton | Electron (neutral atom mein) |
|---|---|---|
| Hydrogen | 1 | 1 |
| Helium | 2 | 2 |
| Carbon | 6 | 6 |
| Oxygen | 8 | 8 |

Ye ginti ek tatva ko doosre se alag karne wali hai. Isi ginti ko hum aage **atomic number** kahenge (Topic 4).

## Part G: Chapter 4 ka adhoora sawaal: "atom avibhajya hai"?

Chapter 4 mein Dalton ka P1 tha: "atom ko tod nahi sakte." Ab hum jaante hain ki ye adhoora tha. Atom ke andar **teen chhote kan** hain: **electron, proton aur neutron**. Isliye kehte hain ki atom **avibhajya nahi** hai.

Par dhyan do, Dalton ne galat kuch nahi kiya. **Chemical reaction mein atom ke andar ke ye kan nahi tootte.** Wahan sirf kuch **electrons** ka lena-dena ya sajha hota hai (aage ke chapters mein dekhenge). Isliye chemistry ke liye Dalton ka picture aaj bhi kaam aata hai.

Dalton ka P2 bhi adhoora tha: "ek tatva ke saare atom ek jaise hote hain." Ab hum samajh sakte hain ki ek hi tatva ke atoms mein **neutron ki ginti** alag ho sakti hai, to dravyaman bhi thoda alag ho sakta hai (isotopes, Topic 6).

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Neutron ka charge negative hota hai" samajhna**

Neutron ka charge **zero** hota hai. Negative charge sirf electron par hota hai.

**Galti 2: "Proton aur electron ka dravyaman barabar hota hai" samajhna**

Charge ki matra barabar hai, par dravyaman nahi. Ek proton lagbhag 1840 electrons ke barabar bhaari hota hai.

**Galti 3: "Neutral ka matlab atom mein koi charge wala kan hai hi nahi"**

Neutral ka matlab ye hai ki **positive aur negative charge barabar** hain. Kan maujood hain, bas ek-doosre ko santulit karte hain.

**Galti 4: "Alag tatvon ke electrons alag hote hain"**

Electron sab tatvon mein ek jaisa hai. Tatva proton ki ginti se alag hote hain.

**Galti 5: "Cathode ray tube ka prayog ghar par kar sakte hain"**

Nahi. Isme bahut zyada voltage aur khaali kaanch ki nali hoti hai: ye sirf visheshagya lab ka kaam hai. Ghar par kangi aur kaagaz jaise surakshit prayog hi karo.

## Quick Recap

- Ragadne se charge aata hai (cling wrap, kangi): iska matlab hai ki atom ke andar **charge wale kan** hote hain.
- **J. J. Thomson (1897)** ne cathode rays se **electron** dhoondha: negative charge (−1), dravyaman lagbhag proton ka 1/1840, har atom mein milta hai.
- **Goldstein (1886)** ki canal rays se **proton** mila: positive charge (+1), dravyaman lagbhag 1 u. Naam Rutherford ne diya. Ordinary hydrogen mein 1 proton aur koi neutron nahi.
- **Chadwick (1932)** ne **neutron** dhoondha: koi charge nahi, dravyaman lagbhag 1 u.
- Atom ka lagbhag saara dravyaman proton aur neutron se aata hai; electron bahut halka hai.
- Neutral atom mein **protons = electrons**, to kul charge zero.
- Sab atoms mein wahi teen kisam ke kan hote hain; tatva **kanon ki ginti** se alag hote hain.
- Dalton ka P1 adhoora hai (atom ke andar kan hain), P2 bhi (neutron ki ginti alag ho sakti hai).

### Answers

1. Cling wrap ko kheenchne (ragadne) par uspar **charge** aa jaata hai, aur charge wali cheez steel ke katore ko kheenchti hai. Charge ka transfer **electrons** ke karan hota hai (electron halke hain aur bahar ki taraf hain).

2. Ek kaanch ki nali se hawa lagbhag nikaal kar, uske dono sirohn par plate laga kar bahut zyada voltage lagaya gaya. **Cathode (negative plate)** se rays nikli jo **anode (positive plate)** ki taraf chalin. Thomson ne dekha ki ye rays **positive plate ki taraf mudti** hain. Vipreet charge kheenchte hain, to rays par **negative charge** tha.

3. Gas badalne ya plate ki dhatu badalne par bhi rays **ek jaisi** nikli. Matlab ye negative kan kisi ek tatva ka nahi, **har atom ka hissa** hain.

4. Canal rays **positive kanon** ki dhaara hain jo chhed wale cathode ke piche se ulti disha mein nikli. Inka dravyaman nali ke gas par nirbhar karta hai. **Hydrogen** ka atom sabse halka hai, to us se mile positive kan sabse halke hue: ye **proton** hai.

5. (a) **Electron:** J. J. Thomson, 1897, charge **−1**. (b) **Proton:** Goldstein ki canal rays (1886) se, naam Rutherford ne diya, charge **+1**. (c) **Neutron:** Chadwick, 1932, charge **0**.

6. Neutral atom mein protons = electrons, to **9 electrons**. Kul charge = +9 − 9 = **0** ✅

7. Mass = 2 × 1 + 2 × 1 + 2 × 0 = **lagbhag 4 u**. Jaanch: helium ka naapa gaya atomic mass bhi lagbhag 4 u hai ✅

8. Electron: charge −1, relative mass lagbhag 1/1840, atom ke bahar. Proton: +1, 1, nucleus mein. Neutron: 0, lagbhag 1, nucleus mein. Ek proton ke dravyaman ke barabar karne ke liye lagbhag **1840 electrons** chahiye.

9. Neutral ka matlab ye nahi ki koi charge wala kan nahi hai. Matlab hai ki **positive (protons) aur negative (electrons) charge barabar** hain, to kul charge zero hai.

10. Ye baat ki atom ko todna mumkin nahi, **sahi nahi hai**, kyunki atom ke andar electron, proton aur neutron hain. Par **chemical reaction** mein ye kan nahi tootte, sirf kuch electrons ka lena-dena ya sajha hota hai, isliye chemistry mein Dalton ka picture ab bhi kaam karta hai.
