# Topic 2: Paani Ek Solvent: Kya Ghulta Hai Aur Kitna

Topic 1 mein humne dekha ki paani H₂O hai: do hydrogen aur ek oxygen, covalent bond se jude hue, aur mass mein hydrogen : oxygen = 1 : 8. Hum ye bhi jaan chuke hain ki paani hamari duniya mein kitna zyada hai. Ab ek naya sawaal: paani ke saath itni saari cheezein kyun milti hain? Namak, cheeni, ORS, chai ki patti, dawai, sab paani mein ghulti hain. Chapter 2 mein hamne solution, solute, solvent aur solubility ki shuruaat ki thi, aur kaha tha ki **paani ke solvent hone ki poori kahani baad mein**. Wo "baad" aaj hai.

## Dal ki katori mein do cheezein

Sunday ki dopahar thi. Mr. Bapat ne apne bhatije Vivaan ke saamne garam dal ki katori rakhi, jis par tadka ka tel tair raha tha, peele-peele goal ghere banakar. Vivaan ne chammach se dal hilayi aur ruk gaya. "Chacha, ek ajeeb baat hai. Maine dal mein namak daala tha, wo dikhta hi nahi. Par ye tel ka tadka dekho: kitna hilao, wo upar hi tairta hai, alag ka alag!"

Mr. Bapat ne muskuraake kaha, "Sahi pakda. Namak paani mein **ghul gaya**, tel **nahi ghulta**. Dono cheezein dal ke paani se mil rahi hain, par natija bilkul ulta. Aur ek aur baat: agar tum pehle se zyada namak daalte jaao, to ek had ke baad namak ghulna band kar dega."

Vivaan ne poochha, "To kya paani kisi bhi cheez ko ghula sakta hai? Kuch log to paani ko 'universal solvent' bolte hain." "Naam mashhoor hai," Mr. Bapat bole, "par thoda badha-chadha kar diya gaya hai. Aaj dekhte hain ki paani ke andar andar kya hota hai, kis cheez ko wo ghulata hai, kisko nahi, aur ek **thermometer aur graph** ke saath ye bhi, ki garam hone par kitna zyada ghul sakta hai."

## Part A: Paani ke andar ghulna kaise hota hai?

Chapter 6 yaad karo: **ionic compound** (jaise NaCl) mein Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ ions ek regular **lattice** mein jude rehte hain, positive aur negative charge ke aakarshan se. Ye aakarshan bahut strong hota hai, isliye namak ka crystal sakht hai aur aasaani se bikharta nahi.

Phir wo paani mein kaise ghul jaata hai? Raaz paani ke kanon mein hai: paani ke kan ions ko apni taraf khinch lete hain. (Chapter 6 mein ek line aayi thi ki paani ke kan ke sire thode charge wale hote hain; iski poori kahani aage ki class mein.)

Jab namak ka crystal paani mein aata hai, to paani ke kan lattice ki **sateh** par baithe ions ko ghere lete hain aur apni taraf khinchte hain. Ek-ek karke ion lattice se **alag** ho jaata hai aur paani ke kanon se ghira, solution mein tairne lagta hai.

```
   Pehle: crystal paani ke paas       Baad mein: ions paani ke beech

     + - + - + -                       w    w            w
     - + - + - +                         (+)   w   (-)
     + - + - + -                    w          (-)    w
     - + - + - +                         (-)      (+)
                                      w     w   (+)   w
     + = Na+   - = Cl-
     w = paani ka kan                   har ion ko paani ke kan ghere hue hain
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Ionic crystal paani mein: pehle + aur - ions ki regular jaali (lattice). Paani ke kan sateh ke ions ko ghere kar ek-ek karke khinch lete hain, aur ions paani ke beech alag-alag phail jaate hain, har ion ko paani ke kan gherte hue. (Particles aur doori yahan bahut badi dikhai gayi hai.)

**Ek zaroori baat:** ghulne ke baad Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ **kahin ghayab nahi hue**. Wo ions ke roop mein paani mein hain. Isliye ghol sukha do to namak waapas milta hai. Aur kyunki ions charge wale hain, **namak ka ghol bijli chalne deta hai**, jo tumne Chapter 6 ke tester mein dekha tha.

Ek formula ka hisaab: NaCl ka ek formula unit Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ ka 1 + 1 = 2 ions deta hai. CaCl₂ ka ek formula unit Ca²⁺ aur do Cl⁻ deta hai, yaani 1 + 2 = 3 ions. Isliye 5 formula unit CaCl₂ ghulne par 5 Ca²⁺ aur 10 Cl⁻, kul 15 ions.

## Part B: Kya ghulta hai, kya nahi?

Sabhi ionic compounds paani mein nahi ghulte (jaise chaak ya marble, CaCO₃, bahut hi kam ghulte hain), par zyaadatar common namak ghulte hain. Aur sirf ionic hi nahi: kuch covalent padarth bhi ghul jaate hain, jaise cheeni aur alcohol, kyunki paani ke kan unke kanon ko bhi apne beech jagah de dete hain. Neeche ka table ek nazar mein:

| Padarth | Paani mein? | Kaisa padarth |
|---|---|---|
| Rasoi ka namak (NaCl) | Ghulta hai | Ionic |
| Cheeni | Bahut zyada ghulti hai | Covalent |
| Alcohol | Poora mil jaata hai | Covalent |
| Washing soda, copper sulphate | Ghulte hain | Ionic |
| Chaak (CaCO₃) | Lagbhag nahi ghulta | Ionic |
| Rait, mitti | Nahi ghulte, neeche baith jaate hain | Ionic aur anya |
| Khaane ka tel, ghee, mom | Nahi ghulte | Covalent |
| Oxygen, carbon dioxide (gas) | Thodi si ghulti hain | Covalent |

Dekho, pattern ye hai: **"paani ke saath mel khaane wale" padarth ghulte hain**, baaki nahi. Isi liye "universal solvent" naam thoda badha-chadha hai: paani **bahut saari** cheezein ghulata hai, **sab kuch nahi**. Tel, mom aur plastic ke liye alag solvent chahiye (jaise petrol ya acetone, jinhein bachche kabhi nahi sambhalte).

**Activity 1 (ghar par adult ke saath):** teen saaf glass mein thanda paani lo. Ek mein ek chammach namak, doosre mein ek chammach cheeni, teesre mein ek chammach khaane ka tel daalo. Sab ko hilao aur dekho. Namak aur cheeni gayab, tel ki boondein alag tairti hain. **Kuch chakhna nahi hai**, sirf dekhna hai. Tel waale glass ko jyada der hilao to chhoti boondein banti hain, par kuch der baad wo phir upar jama ho jaati hain.

**Jaanch:** Vivaan ke dal mein tel upar kyun tairta hai aur namak kyun gayab? ✅ Namak ke ions paani ke kanon se ghire jaate hain aur ghul jaate hain; tel paani ke saath mel nahi khata, aur halka hone ke kaaran upar tairta hai.

## Part C: Solubility: "kitna" ghulta hai?

Chapter 2 mein padha tha: ek temperature par **100 g paani mein jitne gram solute ghul sakta hai**, wo us solute ki us temperature par **solubility** hai. Ab hum kuch padarthon ke figures ek table mein dekhte hain. (Ye lagbhag (approximate) values hain, alag-alag sroton mein thodi alag bhi mil sakti hain.)

| Temperature | Potassium nitrate, KNO₃ (g per 100 g paani) | Sodium chloride, NaCl (g per 100 g paani) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 °C | 13 | – |
| 20 °C | 32 | 36 |
| 40 °C | 64 | – |
| 60 °C | 110 | – |
| 80 °C | 169 | 38 |

("–" ka matlab: is chapter mein is temperature ki value nahi di gayi.) Cheeni ke liye 20 °C par lagbhag 204 g per 100 g paani.

Do baatein turant dikhti hain:

1. **KNO₃ ki solubility bahut tezi se badhti hai.** 0 °C par 13 g, 80 °C par 169 g: 169 ÷ 13 = 13 guna.
2. **NaCl ki solubility lagbhag same rehti hai.** 20 °C par 36 g, 80 °C par sirf 38 g: 60 °C temperature badhne par sirf 2 g ka farak.

Isiliye KNO₃ ko "temperature ka shaukeen" aur namak ko "temperature se bekhabar" kehte hain. Isi liye garam paani se namak ki taakat lagbhag nahi badalti.

Isliye graph se sirf lagbhag values (nearest 10 g) padh sakte ho; **pakki values hamesha table se lo**. `K` aur `N` data points hain, `k` aur `n` beech ka line hai.

> **Figure 2.2** – Solubility curve (lagbhag): KNO₃ ka curve tezi se oopar chadhta hai (0 °C par 13 g se 80 °C par 169 g), jabki NaCl ki line lagbhag samtal hai (36 g se 38 g). Dono lines lagbhag 22.5 °C par ek doosre ko kaatati hain: us se kam temperature par namak zyada ghulta hai, us se zyada par KNO₃ zyada ghulta hai. (Har row 10 g aur har column 2 °C ka hai, isliye NaCl wali line 40 wali row mein dikh rahi hai, jabki uski asli value 36 se 38 hai.)

Graph padhne ke teen niyam:

- **Curve par ka har point** matlab saturated solution (us temperature par jitna maximum ghul sakta hai).
- **Curve ke neeche** ka point matlab unsaturated solution (aur ghul sakta hai).
- **Curve ke upar** ka point matlab itna ghula hua nahi reh sakta, extra solute neeche baith jaayega.

Do points ke beech ki value ka **andaaza** (interpolation) lagana ho to dono ke beech ki seedhi line maan lo. Jaise 50 °C par KNO₃ ki solubility 40 °C (64 g) aur 60 °C (110 g) ke bilkul beech mein hogi, yaani (64 + 110) ÷ 2 = 87 g. Ye ek andaaza hai, asli value thodi alag ho sakti hai kyunki curve seedhi line nahi, halka sa jhuka hua hai.

## Part D: Solved examples

(Sab data upar ke table ka hai. Jahan temperature table mein nahi hai, wahan andaaza hai.)

**Example 1: table se padhna.** 60 °C par 100 g paani mein KNO₃ aur 20 °C par NaCl kitna ghulta hai?

- KNO₃ at 60 °C = **110 g**
- NaCl at 20 °C = **36 g**

**Jaanch:** table ki 60 °C aur 20 °C wali panktiyan dekhein: 110 aur 36 ✅.

**Example 2: beech ka andaaza (interpolation).** 50 °C par 100 g paani mein KNO₃ ki solubility ka andaaza lagao.

- 40 °C par 64 g, 60 °C par 110 g. Beech ka point: (64 + 110) ÷ 2 = 174 ÷ 2 = **87 g**

**Jaanch:** 64 < 87 < 110 ✅, aur 87 − 64 = 23, 110 − 87 = 23 (dono taraf barabar doori) ✅.

**Example 3: aur kitna ghul sakta hai?** 40 °C par 100 g paani mein 30 g KNO₃ ghola gaya. Ghol saturated hai ya unsaturated? Aur kitna ghul sakta hai?

- 40 °C par adhiktam 64 g.
- 30 < 64, isliye ghol **unsaturated** hai.
- Aur ghul sakta hai: 64 − 30 = **34 g**

**Jaanch:** 30 + 34 = 64 ✅.

**Example 4: zyada paani, zyada solute.** 60 °C par 200 g paani mein saturated ghol banane ke liye kitna KNO₃ chahiye? Us saturated ghol ka kul mass kitna hoga?

- 100 g paani mein 110 g, to 200 g paani (2 guna) mein 110 × 2 = **220 g**
- Ghol ka mass = 220 + 200 = **420 g**

**Jaanch:** 220 ÷ 200 = 1.1 aur 110 ÷ 100 = 1.1 ✅ (per gram paani same).

**Example 5: garam ghol thanda karo, crystal bante hain.** 100 g paani mein 80 °C par saturated KNO₃ ka ghol banaya gaya, phir use 40 °C tak thanda kiya gaya. Kitne gram KNO₃ ke crystal baithenge?

- 80 °C par ghula hua = 169 g
- 40 °C par ghul sakta hai = 64 g
- Crystal = 169 − 64 = **105 g**

**Jaanch:** 64 + 105 = 169 ✅. (Dhyan do: paani utna hi raha, sirf solute ka ghulna kam hua.)

**Example 6: zyada paani ke saath crystal.** 250 g paani mein 60 °C par saturated KNO₃ ka ghol 20 °C tak thanda kiya gaya. Kitna solid niklega?

- 100 g paani ke liye: 110 − 32 = 78 g
- 250 g paani (2.5 guna): 78 × 2.5 = **195 g**

**Jaanch:** 60 °C par 250 g paani mein 110 × 2.5 = 275 g tha, 20 °C par 32 × 2.5 = 80 g ghul sakta hai: 275 − 80 = 195 ✅.

**Example 7: namak crystal se nahi, evaporation se kyun?** 100 g paani mein 80 °C par saturated NaCl ka ghol 20 °C tak thanda karo to kitna namak niklega? Aur KNO₃ ke liye?

- NaCl: 38 − 36 = **2 g** (bahut kam)
- KNO₃ (80 °C se 20 °C): 169 − 32 = **137 g**

**Jaanch:** KNO₃ ka nikalna NaCl se 137 ÷ 2 = 68.5 guna zyada hai ✅. Isliye KNO₃ ko thanda karke aasaani se crystal milte hain, par **namak ko thanda karne se kuch nahi milta**: usse paani ud kar (evaporation) hi nikalte hain, jaise samudri paani se namak banta hai.

**Example 8: saturated ghol ka mass percent.** 80 °C par saturated KNO₃ ghol (100 g paani mein 169 g) ka mass percent kya hai?

- Solution = 169 + 100 = 269 g
- Mass percent = (169 ÷ 269) × 100 = **62.8%** (lagbhag)

**Jaanch:** 62.8% of 269 = 0.628 × 269 = 168.9 ≈ 169 ✅. (Chapter 2 ki tarah solute ko poore solution se divide kiya, 100 g paani se nahi.)

**Example 9: temperature badhane par kya hota hai?** 100 g paani mein 40 °C par 70 g KNO₃ daala gaya. Kya sab ghulega? Phir ghol ko 60 °C tak garam kiya.

- 40 °C par adhiktam 64 g, to 70 − 64 = **6 g neeche bacha** rahega (ghol saturated).
- 60 °C par adhiktam 110 g, 70 < 110, to **sab ghul jaayega** aur ghol unsaturated ho jaayega, jo 110 − 70 = **40 g aur** le sakta hai.

**Jaanch:** 64 + 6 = 70 ✅, 70 + 40 = 110 ✅.

**Example 10: gas aur garmi.** Paani mein oxygen ki ghulne ki matra lagbhag 14.6 mg per litre (0 °C), 9.1 mg per litre (20 °C) aur 7.6 mg per litre (30 °C) hoti hai (lagbhag values). 20 °C se 30 °C tak garam hone par talab ke paani ki oxygen kitni kam hui, aur kitne percent?

- Kami = 9.1 − 7.6 = **1.5 mg per litre**
- Percent = (1.5 ÷ 9.1) × 100 = **16.5%** (lagbhag)

**Jaanch:** 9.1 × 0.165 = 1.50 ✅. Yaani sirf 10 °C garmi se lagbhag ek-chhathai oxygen kam. Garmi mein machhliyon ko isiliye dikkat hoti hai.

## Part E: Gas paani mein: soda aur machhliyan

Solids ke ulta, **gas ki solubility garmi se ghatti hai** (Chapter 2 mein padha tha). Ab iske do roz ke udaharan:

- **Soda ya cold drink:** ek band bottle mein CO₂ gas dabav (pressure) ke saath paani mein ghuli hoti hai. Kholne par gas bulbule banke nikalti hai. Thanda drink der tak fizzy rehta hai, kyunki thande paani mein CO₂ zyada ghuli reh paati hai; garam hone par wo jaldi bahar nikal jaati hai. (Pressure ka gas ki solubility par asar aage ki class mein.)
- **Machhliyan:** machhliyan paani mein ghuli **oxygen** (gills se) leti hain, hawa se seedhi nahi. Garmi mein talab ka paani garam hota hai, oxygen kam ghulti hai (Example 10), aur machhliyan saans ke liye upar aati hain ya mar bhi sakti hain. Factory se nikla garam paani bhi isi tarah nuksaan karta hai (Topic 6 mein).

**Activity 2 (ghar par adult ke saath):** do ek jaise glass mein ghar ke sealed bottle ka thoda sa soda daalo, ek fridge ka thanda aur doosra room-temperature wala (garam nahi). Gaur se dekho ki kahan zyada bulbule nikalte hain. **Chakhna ya peena nahi hai**, sirf dekhna hai. Natija: kyunki garam soda mein CO₂ ki solubility kam hai, wo jaldi bulbule banati hai.

Ye bhi dhyan do ki gas ke sath solubility aur solid ke solubility ka rukh ulta hai: solid ke liye garmi se badhta hai, gas ke liye ghatta hai.

    [  o  o  o ]                [  o  o  o ]             [  o  o  o ]
    [ o  o  o  ]                [ o  o  o  ]             [ o  o  o  ]

## Part F: Paani ke solvent hone ke roz ke kaam

- **Hamare sharir mein:** khaana paach ke chhote kanon mein tootta hai aur paani (khoon) mein ghul kar poore sharir mein pahunchta hai. Paani ka ghol hi wo madhyam hai jisme cells ke reactions hote hain.
- **Paudhe:** jad mitti ke paani mein ghuli khaad aur minerals ko hi khinchti hai (Topic 6 mein khaad ke runoff ki baat).
- **ORS:** powder ko paani mein ghola jaata hai taaki namak aur cheeni sharir mein jaldi pahunche.
- **Safai:** paani se gandagi ghul kar bahti hai (sabun aur detergent ki kahani Topic 3 mein).
- **Crystallisation:** KNO₃ jaisi cheezon ko garam ghol se thanda karke saaf crystal ke roop mein nikalna (Example 5).
- **Samudri namak:** samudri paani ka namak thanda karke nahi, paani ko **dhoop se udake** nikalte hain (Example 7).
- **Nuksan:** wahi ghulne ki taakat paani mein zeher bhi ghula deti hai, jisse paani saaf dikhne par bhi gandaa ho sakta hai. Isse hum Topics 3, 5 aur 6 mein milenge.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Paani sab kuch ghula leta hai."**
Nahi. Tel, mom, plastic aur kaanch lagbhag nahi ghulte. "Universal solvent" ek dhili bhasha ka naam hai.

**Galti 2: "Ghulne ke baad namak khatam ho gaya."**
Nahi. Namak Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ ions ke roop mein ghol mein hai. Paani sukhane par wapas mil jaata hai.

**Galti 3: "Garmi se sab ki solubility badhti hai."**
Zyaadatar solids ke liye sahi, par NaCl ke liye bahut kam (36 se 38 g), aur gas ke liye ulta.

**Galti 4: "Mass percent mein 100 g paani se divide karte hain."**
Nahi. Example 8 mein 269 g (solute + paani) se divide kiya, 100 g se nahi. Solubility 100 g paani ke liye hoti hai, par mass percent poore ghol ke liye.

**Galti 5: "Crystals ka mass paani se nikalta hai."**
Crystal wo solute hai jo ghul nahi paaya; paani ka mass wahi rehta hai. Example 5 mein crystal = 169 − 64 = 105 g, paani wahi 100 g.

**Galti 6: "Curve ke upar ka point bhi saturated hai."**
Nahi. Curve par saturated; curve ke upar solute extra, neeche baith jaata hai; curve ke neeche unsaturated.

## Quick Recap

- Ionic compound paani mein ghulta hai kyunki paani ke kan lattice ke ions ko khinch kar alag kar dete hain; ions ghol mein phail jaate hain, khatam nahi hote.
- Cheeni aur alcohol bhi ghulte hain; tel, mom, chaak lagbhag nahi. "Universal solvent" naam dhila hai.
- **Solubility** = 100 g paani mein ek temperature par ghulne wale solute ke g.
- KNO₃: 13 g (0 °C), 32 g (20 °C), 64 g (40 °C), 110 g (60 °C), 169 g (80 °C): bahut tez badhti. NaCl: lagbhag 36 g (20 °C), 38 g (80 °C): lagbhag samtal.
- Curve par saturated, neeche unsaturated, upar extra solute neeche baithta hai.
- Garam saturated ghol thanda karne par crystal: mass = (ghula hua pehle) − (ghula hua baad mein), paani ke hisaab se scale karo.
- Gas ki solubility garmi se ghatti hai: soda aur garmi mein machhliyan.
- Safety: ghar par sirf namak, cheeni, tel aur soda jaise safe cheezon se activity, boiling paani sirf adult.

## Practice Questions

1. Explain, using the idea of ions and water particles, why common salt dissolves in water but cooking oil does not.

2. When 4 formula units of calcium chloride, CaCl₂, dissolve in water, how many Ca²⁺ ions and how many Cl⁻ ions are released in total?

3. Using the table, how many times larger is the solubility of potassium nitrate at 80 °C than at 0 °C? Is this also true for sodium chloride between 20 °C and 80 °C? Explain.

4. Estimate the solubility of potassium nitrate at 30 °C in 100 g of water (use the 20 °C and 40 °C values). If 40 g is added to 100 g of water at 30 °C, is the solution saturated or unsaturated, and how much more can dissolve?

5. At 20 °C, how much sodium chloride can dissolve in 250 g of water? If a student adds 100 g of salt to that water and stirs well, how much remains undissolved?

6. At 20 °C, the solubility of sugar is about 204 g per 100 g of water. How much sugar can dissolve in 25 g of water at 20 °C? Find the mass percent of a saturated sugar solution at this temperature (round to one decimal place).

7. A saturated solution is made with 150 g of water at 80 °C using potassium nitrate. It is cooled to 0 °C. How many grams of potassium nitrate crystallise out?

8. A solution has 64 g of potassium nitrate dissolved in 100 g of water. At about what temperature does it become saturated when cooled? How much crystallises when it is cooled further to 20 °C?

9. A student cools a saturated sodium chloride solution made with 500 g of water at 80 °C down to 20 °C. How much salt crystallises? Why do salt makers not use cooling to get salt from sea water?

10. Explain why fish may die in a shallow pond in a very hot summer even though the water looks clean. Why does a warm bottle of soda lose its fizz faster than a cold one?

11. State True or False and correct the false ones:
    (a) A point lying above the solubility curve means an unsaturated solution.
    (b) When salt dissolves, the Na⁺ and Cl⁻ ions disappear from the solution.
    (c) The solubility of potassium nitrate increases with temperature much more than that of sodium chloride.
    (d) Water can dissolve everything, so it is truly a universal solvent.

### Answers

1. Namak ionic hai: Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ ek lattice mein jude hain. Paani ke kan in ions ko **apni taraf khinch kar ghere lete hain**, aur ions lattice se alag hokar paani ke beech phail jaate hain. Tel paani ke kanon ke saath aisa mel nahi khata, isliye uske kan paani ke beech nahi bikhrte aur tel alag tairta hai.

2. Har CaCl₂ se 1 Ca²⁺ aur 2 Cl⁻. 4 units: Ca²⁺ = 4 × 1 = **4**, Cl⁻ = 4 × 2 = **8**, kul **12 ions**.
   Jaanch: 4 + 8 = 12 ✅.

3. KNO₃: 169 ÷ 13 = **13 guna**. NaCl: 38 ÷ 36 = 1.06 guna, yaani lagbhag **same**. Isliye nahi: NaCl ki solubility temperature se bahut kam badalti hai.
   Jaanch: 13 × 13 = 169 ✅.

4. 30 °C beech mein hai: (32 + 64) ÷ 2 = **48 g**. 40 g < 48 g, to ghol **unsaturated** hai aur 48 − 40 = **8 g aur** ghul sakta hai.
   Jaanch: 40 + 8 = 48 ✅.

5. 100 g paani mein 36 g, to 250 g mein 36 × 2.5 = **90 g**. 100 g daale to 100 − 90 = **10 g** bacha rahega.
   Jaanch: 90 + 10 = 100 ✅.

6. 100 g mein 204 g, to 25 g (ek-chauthai) mein 204 ÷ 4 = **51 g**. Saturated solution mein 204 g solute, 100 g paani: solution = 304 g, mass percent = (204 ÷ 304) × 100 = 67.1%.
   Jaanch: 0.671 × 304 = 204.0 ✅.

7. 100 g paani ke liye 169 − 13 = 156 g. 150 g paani (1.5 guna): 156 × 1.5 = **234 g**.
   Jaanch: 80 °C par 169 × 1.5 = 253.5 g tha, 0 °C par 13 × 1.5 = 19.5 g ghul sakta hai, 253.5 − 19.5 = 234 ✅.

8. 64 g per 100 g paani 40 °C ki solubility hai, to ghol **40 °C par saturated** hota hai aur crystal shuru ho jaate hain. 20 °C tak thanda karne par 32 g hi ghul sakta hai, to 64 − 32 = **32 g** crystal.
   Jaanch: 32 + 32 = 64 ✅.

9. 100 g paani ke liye 38 − 36 = 2 g. 500 g paani (5 guna): 2 × 5 = **10 g** namak (500 g paani ke liye bahut kam). Salt maker cooling nahi karte kyunki NaCl ki solubility temperature se lagbhag nahi badalti, thanda karne se bahut kam namak nikalta hai; wo samudri paani ko dhoop mein ud kar namak nikalte hain.
   Jaanch: 5 × 2 = 10 ✅.

10. Garmi mein paani mein ghuli oxygen kam ho jaati hai (gas ki solubility garmi se ghatti hai), aur machhliyon ko saans ke liye kam oxygen milti hai, isliye saaf dikhne par bhi wo mar sakti hain. Garam soda mein CO₂ ki solubility kam hoti hai, to gas jaldi bahar nikalti hai aur drink jaldi flat ho jaata hai; thande drink mein gas zyada ghuli rehti hai.

11. (a) **False** ❌. Curve ke **upar** wale point par extra solute neeche baith jaata hai; unsaturated solution curve ke **neeche** hota hai. (b) **False** ❌. Ions ghol mein hi rehte hain, bas lattice se alag ho jaate hain; paani sukhane par namak waapas mil jaata hai. (c) **True** ✅. KNO₃ 13 g se 169 g, NaCl sirf 36 g se 38 g. (d) **False** ❌. Tel, mom aur plastic paani mein nahi ghulte; "universal solvent" bas ek dhila naam hai.

Vivaan ne ab dal ki katori ko nayi nazar se dekha: namak ke Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ ions paani ke kanon se ghire, har jagah baraabar phaile hue, aur tadka ka tel alag, kyunki paani us se mel nahi khata. Mr. Bapat ne hans kar kaha, "Jo ghulta hai, wo bhi hamesha ek had tak hi ghulta hai. Wahi had solubility hai."

**Aage kya?** Paani jo kuch ghulata hai, kabhi-kabhi wahi uski pareshani ban jaata hai. Borewell ka paani jisme calcium aur magnesium ke namak ghule hain, sabun ke saath lather nahi banata, aur kettle ke andar safed parat jama kar deta hai. Aisa paani **kathor (hard)** kyun kehlata hai, ye kaise pehchaane aur kaise sudhaara jaaye: ye hum **Topic 3: Kathor aur Mridu Paani** mein dekhenge.
