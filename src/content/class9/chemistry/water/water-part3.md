# Topic 3: Kathor aur Mridu Paani

Pichhle topic mein hamne dekha ki paani bahut kuch **ghola** leta hai, aur jitna ghola leta hai wo temperature par bhi depend karta hai. Ab ek sawaal: **jo cheezein paani mein ghuli hain, kya wo hamesha bekaar-si chup baithi rehti hain?** Kabhi-kabhi wo kaam bigaad deti hain: sabun ka jhaag rok deti hain, aur kettle ke andar patthar jaisi parat bana deti hain. Aaj usi ki jaanch.

---

## Kettle ki safed parat aur bina jhaag ka sabun

Mr. Phadke ke ghar kai dino se ek chhoti pareshani thi. Unki bhanji Meenal chhutti mein aayi thi, aur subah chai ke liye usne steel ka kettle dekha. Andar **safed, khurdari parat** thi, jaise kisi ne chuna ya chaak ghol kar sukha diya ho. "Mama, kettle saaf kyun nahi hota? Kal hi to ragda tha."

Mr. Phadke hanse. "Ragdo to saaf, par kuch hafte baad phir aa jaati hai. Ye hamare borewell ke paani ki den hai." Phir wo Meenal ko bathroom mein le gaye. Wahan Meenal ki mami **sabun** se haath dho rahi thi, par jhaag nahi ban raha tha. Sabun ghisne par ek **safed, dahi jaisi parat** haath par aur sink ke kinare chipak rahi thi. "Is paani mein sabun ka jhaag hi nahi banta," mami bolin. "Bahut sabun lagta hai."

Meenal ko mazaa aaya. "Mama, borewell ke paani mein aisa kya hai jo kettle mein patthar jamata hai aur sabun ka jhaag bhi khaa jaata hai?" Mr. Phadke ne kaha, "Wahi to aaj dekhenge. Aur ek mazedaar baat: aisa paani **sehat ke liye bura nahi**, bas ghar ke kaam mein pareshaan karta hai. Chal, kettle ki parat se shuru karte hain."

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Mridu paani (soft water)** | Jis paani mein calcium aur magnesium ke salt **bahut kam** ghule hon. Sabun aasani se jhaag deta hai | Baarish ka paani, distilled paani |
| **Kathor paani (hard water)** | Jis paani mein calcium aur magnesium ke salt **zyada** ghule hon. Sabun ka jhaag mushkil se banta hai | Kai borewell aur kuein ka paani |
| **Scum** | Kathor paani mein sabun se bana **dahi jaisa, na ghulne wala thos**, jo paani ki sataha par aur bartan par chipakta hai | Sink par safed parat |
| **Asthayi kathorta (temporary hardness)** | Calcium aur magnesium ke **hydrogencarbonate** ke karan. **Ubalne se** hat jaati hai | Ca(HCO₃)₂, Mg(HCO₃)₂ |
| **Sthayi kathorta (permanent hardness)** | Calcium aur magnesium ke **chloride aur sulphate** ke karan. **Ubalne se nahi** hatti | CaCl₂, MgSO₄, CaSO₄ |
| **Scale (kettle ki parat)** | Garam karne par banne wala safed thos, jo kettle, geyser aur boiler ki deewar par jamta hai | CaCO₃ |
| **Washing soda** | Sodium carbonate Na₂CO₃, jo kathorta hatane ke kaam aata hai | Na₂CO₃ |
| **Ion-exchange** | Ek resin (ya zeolite) jo paani ke Ca²⁺ aur Mg²⁺ ko le leta hai aur badle mein Na⁺ de deta hai | Water softener |
| **Detergent** | Kapde dhone ka ek cheez jo kathor paani mein bhi scum nahi banata | Washing powder, liquid wash |

## Part A: Mridu aur kathor paani: farq kya hai?

Shuddh paani ke bare mein hamne Topic 1 mein dekha. Par kuein, borewell ya nadi ka paani **bilkul shuddh H₂O nahi** hota. Wo zameen ke neeche aur pahadon ki chattanon se guzarte hue unke andar ke salt **ghola** leta hai (Topic 2 ka solvent wala kaam). Chuna-patthar aur chattanon mein **calcium aur magnesium** ke compounds hote hain, aur paani unhe thoda-thoda ghola leta hai.

Is hisaab se:

- **Mridu paani:** calcium aur magnesium ke salt **bahut kam**. Jaise baarish ka paani ya distilled paani.
- **Kathor paani:** calcium aur magnesium ke salt **zyada**. Jaise kai borewell ya kuein ka paani.

Dhyan do: ye dono baatein **nuksan nahi, sirf pehchaan** hain. Kathor paani zeheela nahi hai, aur aksar peene mein theek hota hai (Part G mein dekhenge). Fark wahan padta hai jahan sabun, kettle ya boiler ka kaam hai.

## Part B: Sabun ki jaanch: kathor paani mein jhaag kyun nahi?

Sabun ek aisa padarth hai jiska ek chhota hissa paani mein ghulkar jhaag aur safai ka kaam karta hai. Sabun ke andar **sodium ke salt** hote hain (jaise sodium stearate).

Jab sabun **Ca²⁺ ya Mg²⁺** se milta hai, to ek **double displacement** hota hai (Chapter 7 wala saathi badalna). Sodium wala sabun calcium ka salt ban jaata hai, jo **paani mein nahi ghulta**. Wahi dahi jaisa **scum** hai:

> sodium wala sabun (ghulta hai) + Ca²⁺ ya Mg²⁺ wala paani → **calcium ya magnesium wala sabun (nahi ghulta, scum)** + sodium wale salt (ghule rehte hain)

Yaani sabun ka sodium wala hissa Ca²⁺ ya Mg²⁺ se saathi badal leta hai. (Sabun ka poora formula lamba hai; wo yaad karna zaroori nahi.)

**Iska matlab:** jab tak paani mein Ca²⁺ aur Mg²⁺ **bache hain**, sabun pehle unse react karke scum banata rahega. Jhaag tab hi aayega jab **saare Ca²⁺ aur Mg²⁺ khatam** ho chuke honge. Tab tak jitna sabun daaloge, wo **bekaar** jaayega. Isliye kathor paani mein **zyada sabun lagta hai**, aur kapde par scum ka dhabba bhi reh jaata hai.

Aur **detergent** (kapde dhone ka powder ya liquid)? Detergent ka calcium aur magnesium wala salt paani mein **ghul jaata hai**, isliye wo kathor paani mein bhi jhaag deta hai aur scum nahi banata (detergent ka formula aage ki class mein).

Ab asli tasveer: do glass, ek mein mridu paani aur ek mein kathor paani, dono mein sabun ki barabar matra, aur dono ko 10 baar hilaaya.

```
      SOFT WATER                        HARD WATER
      (mridu)                           (kathor)

     ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~                        . ~ .
    ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~   <- gadha jhaag       (thoda jhaag)
    ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~
   +-------------+                    +-------------+
   :             :                    :   o   o     :
   :   saaf      :                    :  o  o  o    :  <- dahi jaisa
   :   paani     :                    :  o  o   o   :     scum, paani
   :             :                    :  gadla      :     gadla
   +-------------+                    +-------------+

   Jhaag ki unchai: zyada             Jhaag ki unchai: kam
   Paani: saaf rehta hai              Paani: scum se gadla
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Do glass, same paani ki matra aur sabun ki matra, 10 baar hilane ke baad. Mridu paani (baayein) mein gadha jhaag aur saaf paani; kathor paani (daayein) mein thoda jhaag aur sabun ka **scum** (o) jo paani ko gadla karta hai, kyunki sabun ka bada hissa Ca²⁺ aur Mg²⁺ ke saath scum ban gaya.

### Activity 3.1: Sabun ka jhaag, teen paani mein

> **Activity 3.1 (ghar par adult ke saath).** Teen saaf, same size ki **chhoti bottle ya jar** (dhakkan wali) lo. Har ek mein **100 mL** paani naapke daalo (measuring jug se):

>

>

3. **Bottle B** (ubala hua) mein jhaag **A se zyada** ho sakta hai. Kyun? Ye Part C mein samjhenge.

## Part C: Asthayi kathorta: jo ubalne se chali jaati hai

Pehle **temporary hardness**. Ye paani mein **calcium aur magnesium ke hydrogencarbonate** ke karan hoti hai: **Ca(HCO₃)₂** aur **Mg(HCO₃)₂**. (HCO₃⁻ hydrogencarbonate radical hai, Chapter 3 se.)

Ye compounds **sthir nahi** hote. Garam karne par ye toot jaate hain:

```
   Ca(HCO₃)₂ → CaCO₃ + H₂O + CO₂
   Mg(HCO₃)₂ → MgCO₃ + H₂O + CO₂
```

Jaanch: Ca 1=1, H 2=2, C 2=2 (1 + 1), O 6=6 (3 + 1 + 2); Mg wali bhi isi tarah ✅.

Dhyan do: ye **decomposition** hai (Chapter 7 ka topic 2): ek compound tootkar teen cheezein deta hai. **CaCO₃** paani mein **nahi ghulta**, isliye wo **thos banta hai**, safed parat ki tarah. **CO₂** gas hai jo udd jaati hai. Aur **H₂O** paani mein mil jaata hai.

Ab Mr. Phadke ke kettle ka raaz khul gaya. Jab bhi wo borewell ka paani ubaalte hain, paani mein ghula **Ca(HCO₃)₂** tootkar **CaCO₃** banta hai, jo kettle ki deewar par chipak jaata hai. Roz thoda-thoda jamne se **hafton mein safed khurdari parat** ban jaati hai. Yahi **kettle scale** hai. Geyser, boiler aur chai banane ke bhagone mein bhi yahi hota hai.

```
        PEHLE ubalne se                  BAAD mein (kai hafte)

         dhakkan                           dhakkan
        _________                         _________
       /         \                       /         \
      : paani     :                     : paani     :
      : (Ca(HCO₃)₂:                     : (kam      :
      :  ghula)   :                     :  kathor)  :
      :           :                     :###########:  <- CaCO₃
      :___________:                     :#safed#####:     ki parat
       \_________/                       \#parat###/
         :  aag/                           :  aag/
         v   heater                        v   heater

   Ghule hue Ca²⁺ aur HCO₃⁻       Ubalne par CaCO₃ thos jam gaya
   ghol mein tair rahe hain       aur CO₂ udd gayi
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Kettle ke andar scale ka banna. Ubalne se Ca(HCO₃)₂ → CaCO₃ + H₂O + CO₂. Thos CaCO₃ (#) deewar aur tali par jamta jaata hai, aur CO₂ gas bahar nikal jaati hai. Isi liye **ubala hua paani kam kathor** hota hai, aur Activity 3.1 mein Bottle B mein jhaag zyada aaya.

### Ek chhota hisaab: kettle kitna scale banata hai?

Atomic masses: Ca = 40, C = 12, O = 16, H = 1 (u).

- Ca(HCO₃)₂ = 40 + 2 × (1 + 12 + 3 × 16) = 40 + 2 × 61 = **162 u**
- CaCO₃ = 40 + 12 + 48 = **100 u**
- H₂O = **18 u**, CO₂ = 12 + 32 = **44 u**

Check: 100 + 18 + 44 = **162**. Barabar ✅ (mass sthir hai).

### Example 1. 8.1 g Ca(HCO₃)₂ ubalne par kitna CaCO₃?

162 g Ca(HCO₃)₂ se 100 g CaCO₃ milta hai. To
8.1 g se: 8.1 × 100 ÷ 162 = **5.0 g CaCO₃**.

Jaanch: 8.1 ÷ 162 = 0.05, aur 0.05 × 100 = 5.0 ✅.

### Example 2. Kettle mein roz kitna scale?

Maan lo (sirf hisaab ke liye, ek saral kalpana) ek ghar ke paani mein **0.162 g Ca(HCO₃)₂ har litre** hai, aur roz **2 litre** paani ubala jaata hai. Ek din mein ghula Ca(HCO₃)₂: 0.162 × 2 = **0.324 g**. Isse CaCO₃: 0.324 × 100 ÷ 162 = **0.2 g** roz.

**30 din** mein: 0.2 × 30 = **6 g** scale. Sunne mein kam lag raha hai, par ye deewar par **patli parat** ki tarah jamta hai, aur saalon mein mota ho jaata hai. (Asal mein ye sab ek jagah nahi jamta, kuch paani ke saath bhi nikal jaata hai; yahan sirf maatra ka andaaza hai.)

### Magnesium ka hissa (ek chhoti si note)

Mg(HCO₃)₂ bhi ubalne se **MgCO₃** deta hai. Jab paani lamba waqt tak ubalta rahe, to magnesium ka scale kuch aur roop (hydroxide) mein bhi ja sakta hai, par ye baat aage ki class mein. Hamare liye itna kaafi: **hydrogencarbonate wali kathorta ubalne se jaati hai.**

## Part D: Sthayi kathorta: jo ubalne se nahi jaati

Ab **permanent hardness**. Ye paani mein **calcium aur magnesium ke chloride aur sulphate** ke karan hoti hai: **CaCl₂**, **MgCl₂**, **CaSO₄**, **MgSO₄**.

Ye compounds garam karne par **tootte nahi**. Paani mein ghule rehte hain, chahe kitna bhi ubalo. Isliye **ubalne se ye kathorta nahi jaati.**

Isse ek sawaal: agar kisi paani ko ubalne ke baad bhi sabun ka jhaag kam hi rahe, to? Iska matlab us paani mein **sthayi** kathorta hai, ya **dono** hain: ubalne se **sirf asthayi wala hissa** gaya, **sthayi wala** wahin raha. Yaani ek hi paani mein dono ho sakte hain:

| Kathorta ka type | Karan (compounds) | Ubalne se? |
|---|---|---|
| Asthayi (temporary) | Ca(HCO₃)₂, Mg(HCO₃)₂ | Hat jaati hai |
| Sthayi (permanent) | CaCl₂, MgCl₂, CaSO₄, MgSO₄ | Nahi hatti |

---

## Part E: Kathorta hatane ke tareeke

Ab sawaal: kathor paani ko mridu kaise banaye?

### Tareeka 1: Ubalna (sirf asthayi ke liye)

Ubalne se Ca(HCO₃)₂ aur Mg(HCO₃)₂ toot kar CaCO₃ aur MgCO₃ ban jaate hain, jo neeche jam jaate hain (ya kettle par scale bante hain). Ghar mein ye sabse aasaan tareeka hai, par **sirf asthayi kathorta** ke liye, aur **sthayi** par asar nahi.

### Tareeka 2: Washing soda (dono ke liye)

**Washing soda (Na₂CO₃)** dono tarah ki kathorta hata sakta hai. Wo Ca²⁺ aur Mg²⁺ ko **thos carbonate** bana kar paani se **bahar** kar deta hai. Ye wahi **double displacement aur precipitate** hai jo Chapter 7 ke Topic 3 mein dekha tha:

```
   CaCl₂ + Na₂CO₃ → CaCO₃ + 2NaCl
   MgSO₄ + Na₂CO₃ → MgCO₃ + Na₂SO₄
   Ca(HCO₃)₂ + Na₂CO₃ → CaCO₃ + 2NaHCO₃
```

Jaanch: pehli: Ca 1=1, Cl 2=2, Na 2=2, C 1=1, O 3=3 ✅. Doosri: Mg 1=1, S 1=1, Na 2=2, C 1=1, O 7=7 ✅. Teesri: Ca 1=1, H 2=2, C 3=3, O 9=9, Na 2=2 ✅.

Teeno mein **saathi badle** aur **carbonate (CaCO₃, MgCO₃) thos ban gaya jo paani mein nahi ghulta**: ye precipitate neeche baith jaata hai, aur paani se Ca²⁺ aur Mg²⁺ **chale gaye**. Jo bache (NaCl, Na₂SO₄, NaHCO₃) **sodium** ke salt hain, jo scum nahi banate. Isliye ye paani ab sabun ke liye "mridu" hai.

Is ko ek hi line mein: **Washing soda Ca²⁺ aur Mg²⁺ ko CaCO₃ aur MgCO₃ ke roop mein bahar nikaal deta hai.**

### Example 3. 11.1 g CaCl₂ ko hatane ke liye kitna Na₂CO₃, aur kitna CaCO₃ banega?

Formula masses: CaCl₂ = 40 + 2 × 35.5 = **111 u**; Na₂CO₃ = 2 × 23 + 12 + 48 = **106 u**; CaCO₃ = **100 u**; NaCl = 23 + 35.5 = 58.5 u, yaani 2NaCl = **117 u**.

Pehle mass check: 111 + 106 = **217**; 100 + 117 = **217**. Barabar ✅.

Ab 111 g CaCl₂ ko 106 g Na₂CO₃ chahiye. To **11.1 g** CaCl₂ (jo uska dasvaan hissa hai) ko 106 ÷ 10 = **10.6 g** Na₂CO₃ chahiye, aur 100 ÷ 10 = **10 g CaCO₃** banega.

Jaanch: 11.1 ÷ 111 = 0.1; 0.1 × 106 = 10.6 ✅ aur 0.1 × 100 = 10 ✅.

### Tareeka 3: Ion-exchange (shabdon mein)

Ghar ke **water softener** (jo kuch paani ke filter ke saath aate hain) ek khaas resin ya **zeolite** use karte hain. Paani is resin ke upar se guzarta hai. Resin **Ca²⁺ aur Mg²⁺ ko pakad leta hai** aur badle mein **Na⁺ chhod deta hai**. Charge ginti: ek Ca²⁺ (do plus) ke badle **do Na⁺** (1 + 1 = 2 plus), yaani charge barabar. Sodium ke salt scum nahi banate, isliye paani mridu ho jaata hai. (Detail aage ki class mein.)

### Tareeka 4: Distillation (sabse shuddh)

Chapter 2 mein padha distillation yaad karo: paani ko bhaap banakar phir thanda karke jama karte hain. Ghule salt peeche reh jaate hain, aur jo paani milta hai wo **mridu (lagbhag shuddh)** hota hai. Par ye **mehnga aur dheema** hai, isliye roz ki zarurat ke liye nahi, sirf lab mein, battery ya iron mein bharne ke liye hota hai.

```
   KATHORTA (kathor paani)
        :
        +---- Asthayi (Ca, Mg ke HCO₃⁻ salt)
        :         :
        :         +-- Ubalna (CaCO₃, MgCO₃ jam jaate hain)
        :         +-- Washing soda
        :         +-- Ion-exchange
        :         +-- Distillation
        :
        +---- Sthayi (Ca, Mg ke Cl⁻ aur SO₄²⁻ salt)
                  :
                  +-- Washing soda
                  +-- Ion-exchange
                  +-- Distillation
                  (ubalna kaam nahi karta)
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Kathorta ka vargikaran aur har ek ko hatane ke tareeke. Asthayi kathorta ke liye chaaron tareeke chalte hain; sthayi ke liye ubalna nahi chalta, baaki teen chalte hain.

### Example 4. Kaun sa tareeka kab?

| Paani ki halat | Sahi tareeka | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| Sirf Ca(HCO₃)₂ wala (asthayi) paani, ghar mein | Ubalna (adult ke saath) | Ca(HCO₃)₂ tootkar CaCO₃ ban jaata hai |
| Paani mein CaCl₂ (sthayi) hai | Washing soda ya ion-exchange | Ubalna CaCl₂ ko nahi todta |
| Paani mein MgSO₄ aur Ca(HCO₃)₂ dono hain | Washing soda ya ion-exchange (ya distillation) | Dono tarah ki kathorta ek saath hatani hai |
| Lab ke liye bahut shuddh paani chahiye | Distillation | Saare ghule salt peeche reh jaate hain |

## Part F: Kathor paani ke nuksan

Kathor paani **zeheela nahi** hai, par kai jagah pareshaan karta hai:

1. **Sabun ki barbaadi:** pehle Ca²⁺ aur Mg²⁺ ko khatam karne mein sabun jaata hai (Part B). Kapde par scum ke dhabbe bhi.
2. **Ghar ke bartan mein scale:** kettle, geyser, cooker par safed parat jamti hai. **Scale garmi ka kharab conductor** hota hai, isliye heater ko **zyada der** garam karna padta hai: **bijli ya fuel zyada kharch** hoti hai, aur kettle ya geyser jaldi kharab hota hai.
3. **Industry mein:** boiler mein scale jamne se pipe tang ho jaate hain, garmi theek se paani tak nahi pahunchti, aur boiler bahut garam hokar **kharab ya khatarnak** ho sakta hai. Isliye factories boiler ke liye paani pehle **mridu** karti hain.
4. **Kapda aur rangai ke kaam:** kathor paani mein kapde ka rang theek se nahi chadta, aur kapde kathor ho sakte hain.
5. **Pipe jaam:** nal ki pipe mein scale jamne se paani ka bahav kam ho jaata hai.

### Example 5. Geyser mein scale: ek hisaab

Maan lo ek ghar ke paani mein **0.405 g Ca(HCO₃)₂ har litre** hai aur geyser roz **10 litre** paani garam karta hai. Roz ka Ca(HCO₃)₂: 0.405 × 10 = **4.05 g**. Isse CaCO₃: 4.05 × 100 ÷ 162 = **2.5 g** roz. 20 din mein: 2.5 × 20 = **50 g** scale (kuch paani ke saath bahar bhi nikal jaata hai, par ye andaaza batata hai ki geyser ke andar scale jaldi kyun jamta hai).

Jaanch: 4.05 ÷ 162 = 0.025, aur 0.025 × 100 = 2.5 ✅.

## Part G: Kathor paani ke fayde (haan, hain)

Kathor paani ko "bura" kehna adhoora hoga:

1. **Swaad:** kuch logon ko kathor paani ka swaad pasand aata hai, aur bahut mridu paani unhe "feeka" lagta hai. (Ye pasand ki baat hai; swaad jaanchne ke liye lab ka paani kabhi chakhna nahi.)
2. **Calcium:** peene ke paani se hamein thoda calcium aur magnesium bhi milta hai, jo haddi aur sharir ke liye zaroori mineral hain (par bada hissa khane se milta hai, paani se nahi). Isliye kathor paani peene ke liye aam taur par theek mana jaata hai.

Aur ek baat: bahut mridu paani mein sabun aasani se dhulta nahi (haath der tak chikne lagte hain), isliye wahan thoda sabun hi kaafi hai.

## Part H: Reasoning: socho aur batao

### Example 6. Paani ubalne par jhaag badha: iska matlab?

Ek student ne nal ke paani se sabun ka test kiya, phir usi paani ko ubaal kar thanda karke test kiya. Pehle **kam jhaag**, phir **zyada jhaag**.

- Ubalne se kathorta **kam hui**, yaani **asthayi kathorta** thi, jo Ca(HCO₃)₂ aur Mg(HCO₃)₂ se thi.
- Agar ubalne ke baad bhi jhaag **utna hi kam** rehta, to kathorta **sthayi** hoti (CaCl₂ ya MgSO₄ jaise salt).

### Example 7. Do paani, ek hi nateeja?

Paani P: ubalne ke baad **jhaag bahut badhta hai**. Paani Q: ubalne ke baad **jhaag bilkul nahi badhta**.

- P mein **mostly asthayi** kathorta hai (hydrogencarbonate).
- Q mein **mostly sthayi** kathorta hai (chloride aur sulphate), jo ubalne se nahi gayi.

### Example 8. Kettle ka scale kya batata hai?

Mr. Phadke ka kettle saaf nahi hota. Agar scale **CaCO₃** hai, to iska seedha matlab: paani mein **Ca(HCO₃)₂** (asthayi) tha, kyunki CaCO₃ ubalne se hi banta hai. (Sthayi wale CaCl₂ ya CaSO₄ ubalne par CaCO₃ nahi dete. CaSO₄ ki bahut thodi sakht parat boiler mein bhi jam sakti hai, par ye aage ki baat hai.)

### Example 9. Washing soda ko paani mein dalne ka asar

Kathor paani mein CaCl₂ ghula hai. Usme washing soda daalne par **dudhiya safed** thos banta hai, jo neeche baith jaata hai. Ye thos kya hai? **CaCO₃**: kyunki CaCl₂ + Na₂CO₃ → CaCO₃ + 2NaCl, aur CaCO₃ paani mein nahi ghulta. Upar ka saaf paani ab **mridu** hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Kathor paani zeheela hota hai"**

Nahi. Kathor paani mein calcium aur magnesium ke salt hote hain, jo aam taur par hanikarak nahi. Problem **sabun, kettle aur boiler** ki hai.

**Galti 2: "Ubalne se har tarah ka kathor paani mridu ho jaata hai"**

Ubalne se **sirf asthayi** kathorta jaati hai. **Sthayi** kathorta (chloride aur sulphate wali) **nahi** jaati.

**Galti 3: "Kettle ka scale ek tarah ka zang ya mitti hai"**

Nahi. Wo **CaCO₃** hai: Ca(HCO₃)₂ ke ubalne se bana thos. Zang loha par banta hai, ye alag cheez hai.

**Galti 4: "Washing soda paani ko bhaap bana deta hai" ya "sab ghula deta hai"**

Washing soda Ca²⁺ aur Mg²⁺ ko CaCO₃ aur MgCO₃ **thos** bana kar paani se **bahar** karta hai. Wo ghulte nahi, neeche baithte hain.

**Galti 5: "Sabun ka jhaag nahi bana, matlab sabun kharab hai"**

Aksar sabun kharab nahi, **paani kathor** hota hai.

**Galti 6: Activity ke paani ko peena ya chakhna**

Activity 3.1 ke kisi bhi bottle ka paani **kabhi peena nahi**. Wo sabun mila hua hai, aur distilled paani bhi swaad ya sehat ke liye peene ka nahi hota.

## Quick Recap

- **Mridu paani:** Ca aur Mg ke salt bahut kam; sabun aasani se jhaag deta hai. **Kathor paani:** Ca aur Mg ke salt zyada; sabun ka jhaag mushkil se banta hai.
- Kathor paani mein sabun **scum** banata hai (calcium ya magnesium ka sabun, jo nahi ghulta), isliye sabun barbaad hota hai. **Detergent** scum nahi banata.
- **Asthayi kathorta:** Ca(HCO₃)₂ aur Mg(HCO₃)₂ se. Ubalne se jaati hai: **Ca(HCO₃)₂ → CaCO₃ + H₂O + CO₂**. Yahi **kettle scale** hai.
- **Sthayi kathorta:** chloride aur sulphate (CaCl₂, MgSO₄, CaSO₄) se. Ubalne se **nahi** jaati.
- **Washing soda** dono hatata hai (double displacement + precipitate): **CaCl₂ + Na₂CO₃ → CaCO₃ + 2NaCl**, **MgSO₄ + Na₂CO₃ → MgCO₃ + Na₂SO₄**.
- **Ion-exchange (resin ya zeolite):** Ca²⁺ aur Mg²⁺ ko Na⁺ se badalta hai. **Distillation** sabse shuddh, par mehnga.
- Nuksan: sabun ki barbaadi, kettle aur geyser mein scale (bijli zyada), boiler aur pipe kharab, rangai mein dikkat. Fayde: thoda calcium aur magnesium milta hai, kuch logon ko swaad achha lagta hai.
- Activity mein paani **kabhi peena nahi**; ubalne ka kaam adult karta hai.

### Answers

1. **Kathor paani** mein calcium aur magnesium ke salt **zyada** hote hain, sabun ka jhaag mushkil se banta hai (jaise kai borewell ya kuein ka paani). **Mridu paani** mein ye salt **bahut kam** hote hain, sabun aasani se jhaag deta hai (jaise baarish ka paani ya distilled paani).

2. Sabun ka sodium wala hissa **Ca²⁺ ya Mg²⁺ se milkar** calcium ya magnesium ka sabun banata hai, jo **paani mein nahi ghulta**. Wahi **scum** hai: **calcium ya magnesium ka sabun**. Jab tak saare Ca²⁺ aur Mg²⁺ khatam nahi hote, sabun scum banane mein jaata rehta hai aur jhaag nahi banta.

3. | Baat | Asthayi (temporary) | Sthayi (permanent) |
   |---|---|---|
   | Karan | Ca aur Mg ke hydrogencarbonate | Ca aur Mg ke chloride aur sulphate |
   | Compounds | Ca(HCO₃)₂, Mg(HCO₃)₂ | CaCl₂, MgCl₂, CaSO₄, MgSO₄ |
   | Ubalne ka asar | Kathorta **hat jaati** hai | Kathorta **nahi** hatti |

4. **Mg(HCO₃)₂ → MgCO₃ + H₂O + CO₂.** Ginti: Mg 1=1, H 2=2, C 2=2 (1 + 1), O 6=6 (3 + 1 + 2) ✅. **MgCO₃** thos hai (paani mein nahi ghulta); H₂O paani, CO₂ gas.

5. Mg(HCO₃)₂ = 24 + 2 × 61 = **146 u**. MgCO₃ = 24 + 12 + 48 = **84 u**. Check: 84 + 18 + 44 = 146 ✅. 146 g se 84 g MgCO₃. To 7.3 g se: 7.3 × 84 ÷ 146 = **4.2 g MgCO₃**. Jaanch: 7.3 ÷ 146 = 0.05, aur 0.05 × 84 = 4.2 ✅.

6. (a) **MgSO₄ + Na₂CO₃ → MgCO₃ + Na₂SO₄.** Ginti: Mg 1=1, S 1=1, Na 2=2, C 1=1, O 7=7 ✅. (b) MgSO₄ = 24 + 32 + 64 = **120 u**; Na₂CO₃ = **106 u**; MgCO₃ = **84 u**; Na₂SO₄ = 46 + 32 + 64 = **142 u**. Mass check: 120 + 106 = 226 = 84 + 142 ✅. 2.4 g MgSO₄ ka matlab 2.4 ÷ 120 = 0.02. Na₂CO₃: 0.02 × 106 = **2.12 g**. MgCO₃: 0.02 × 84 = **1.68 g**.

7. Jhaag **same** raha, matlab ubalne se kathorta **nahi** gayi, isliye paani mein **sthayi kathorta** hai (chloride ya sulphate wale salt), kyunki ye ubalne par nahi tootte. (Ya paani mridu hi tha, tab bhi jhaag pehle se achha hota.)

8. **Ca(HCO₃)₂** garam karne par **tootkar** CaCO₃ (thos), H₂O aur CO₂ deta hai, isliye Ca²⁺ thos mein chala gaya aur paani mridu ho gaya. **CaCl₂** garam karne par **nahi tootta**: Ca²⁺ aur Cl⁻ ion ghol mein hi rehte hain, isliye kathorta wahin rehti hai.

9. **Washing soda** aur **ion-exchange** (aur distillation). Home softener mein **ion-exchange** hota hai: paani ek resin ya zeolite se guzarta hai, jo **Ca²⁺ aur Mg²⁺ ko pakad leta hai** aur badle mein **Na⁺** chhodta hai (ek Ca²⁺ ke badle do Na⁺). Sodium ke salt scum nahi banate.

10. Nuksan (koi teen): sabun zyada lagta hai, kapde par scum, kettle ya geyser mein scale (bijli zyada), boiler ya pipe mein scale se nuksan, rangai mein dikkat. Fayda: kuch calcium aur magnesium milta hai (ya kuch logon ko swaad achha lagta hai).

11. (i) Scale **garmi ka kharab conductor** hai, isliye garmi paani tak theek se nahi pahunchti, **fuel zyada kharch** hota hai. (ii) Scale se **pipe tang ya band** ho jaate hain, aur boiler ki deewar bahut garam hokar **kharab ya khatarnak** ho sakti hai.

12. (a) **False** ❌. Kathor paani zeheela nahi hai; wo sirf sabun aur boiler ke liye pareshani hai. (b) **False** ❌. Detergent kathor paani mein **scum nahi** banata. (c) **True** ✅. (d) **False** ❌. Ubalne se **sirf asthayi** kathorta jaati hai. (e) **True** ✅. Distillation mein ghule salt peeche reh jaate hain.

13. **Distilled paani** mein sabse zyada jhaag (kyunki usme Ca²⁺ aur Mg²⁺ nahi). Ubala-thanda paani mein tap se zyada ho sakta hai, agar asthayi kathorta thi. Safety: **adult ke saath** karna; ubalne ka kaam **sirf adult**; paani poori tarah thanda hone par hi use karna; **kisi bhi bottle ka paani peena ya chakhna nahi**; kaam ke baad paani sink mein baha dena, haath dho lena aur bottles ko khane-peene ke liye dobara use na karna.
