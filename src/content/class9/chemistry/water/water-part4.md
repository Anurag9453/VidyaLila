# Topic 4: Paani Aur Crystals: Hydrates, Efflorescence aur Deliquescence

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki paani mein ghule hue calcium aur magnesium ke salts use **kathor** bana dete hain, aur ye salts kabhi kettle ke andar safed parat ban kar jam jaate hain (boil karne par) aur kabhi ghule hi rehte hain. Wahan paani salt ke **saath** tha, ghol ki shakal mein. Ab ek ajeeb sawaal: kya paani kisi **sookhe, thos crystal ke andar** bhi chhupa ho sakta hai? Aur jab hawa mein nami (moisture) ho, to kuch cheezein usse kheench kar kyun geeli ho jaati hain, aur kuch apni nami hawa ko kyun de deti hain? Aaj ke topic mein hum yahi dekhenge.

Is poore topic mein Chapter 4 ke **rounded atomic masses** hi use honge: H 1, C 12, O 16, Na 23, Mg 24, S 32, Cl 35.5, Ca 40, Fe 56, Cu 63.5. Isliye H₂O ka mass 18 u hai. Percent ek ya do decimal tak round kiye gaye hain, aur har jagah likha hai.

## Part A: Crystal ke andar paani: Water of crystallisation

### Crystal sookha dikhta hai, phir bhi paani rakhta hai

Neela thotha (copper sulphate) ke crystal haath mein lo to bilkul sookhe lagte hain: koi geelapan nahi, koi boondein nahi. Phir bhi agar unhe garam kiya jaaye (teacher ke demo mein, aage dekhenge), to unke andar se paani nikalta hai. Ye paani **crystal banate waqt hi andar jakar baith gaya**. Chapter 2 mein humne dekha tha ki garam, ghule hue salt ke ghol ko thanda karne par crystal banta hai. Kuch salts ke crystal banate waqt paani ke molecules salt ke ions ke beech jagah le lete hain aur jaali ke hisse ban jaate hain. Isse **water of crystallisation** kehte hain.

Do baatein yaad rakhne wali:

1. Ye paani crystal ki "geelepan" wali nami nahi hai. Ye crystal ki **banawat ka hissa** hai, aur isliye iski ginti **fixed** hoti hai: blue vitriol mein har CuSO₄ ke saath **5** H₂O, na 4 na 6.
2. Ye paani crystal ke **rang aur shakal** mein bhi bada role nibhata hai. Neela thotha neela isliye hai ki usme paani juda hai; paani nikal do to wo safed ho jaata hai.

```
   Hydrate crystal ke ek tukde ka sketch (bahut saral)

     [CuSO₄] (H₂O)(H₂O)    [CuSO₄] (H₂O)(H₂O)
             (H₂O)(H₂O)(H₂O)       (H₂O)(H₂O)(H₂O)

     [CuSO₄] (H₂O)(H₂O)    [CuSO₄] (H₂O)(H₂O)
             (H₂O)(H₂O)(H₂O)       (H₂O)(H₂O)(H₂O)

   Har [CuSO₄] ke saath 5 (H₂O): isliye CuSO₄·5H₂O
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Neela thotha crystal ka saral sketch: har CuSO₄ ikai ke saath ek fixed ginti, yaani 5, paani ke molecules (H₂O) jaali mein bandhe hue hain. Sketch sirf samjhane ke liye hai; asli crystal ki jaali 3-D hoti hai.

### Hydrate ka formula kaise padhein

Formula mein ek **bindu (·)** salt aur paani ko alag karta hai: **CuSO₄·5H₂O**. Iska matlab: "ek CuSO₄ ke saath 5 H₂O". Bindu ke baad ka 5 sirf H₂O par lagta hai. Jab hum **formula unit mass** nikaalte hain to H₂O ka mass (5 × 18 = 90) bhi jodna padta hai. Yaad rakho: bindu ka matlab "guna" nahi, "saath mein juda" hai.

Jinke formula mein bindu hota hai, wo hydrate hain. Jinme nahi hota, wo **anhydrous** hain.

| Common naam | Rasayanik naam | Formula | Rang |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neela thotha (blue vitriol) | Copper sulphate pentahydrate | CuSO₄·5H₂O | Neela |
| Washing soda | Sodium carbonate decahydrate | Na₂CO₃·10H₂O | Rangheen (safed, paarbhasi) |
| Hara thotha (green vitriol) | Iron(II) sulphate heptahydrate | FeSO₄·7H₂O | Halka hara |
| Epsom salt | Magnesium sulphate heptahydrate | MgSO₄·7H₂O | Rangheen |
| Gypsum | Calcium sulphate dihydrate | CaSO₄·2H₂O | Safed |

(Pichhle topic ke reactions mein humne washing soda ko sirf Na₂CO₃ likha tha. Dukaan par bikne wale washing soda ke crystal ka poora formula **Na₂CO₃·10H₂O** hai; reaction likhte waqt aksar sirf Na₂CO₃ likhte hain.)

## Part B: Hydrate ka formula unit mass aur paani ka percent

Ab Chapter 4 ka percentage composition wala tareeka wapas aata hai. Bas "tatva" ki jagah **paani** ka hissa nikaalna hai.

**Steps:**

1. Anhydrous salt ka formula unit mass nikalo.
2. Paani ka mass: ginti × 18.
3. Dono jodo: ye hydrate ka formula unit mass hai.
4. **Paani ka percent = (paani ka mass ÷ hydrate ka formula unit mass) × 100.**
5. **Jaanch:** salt ka percent aur paani ka percent jodo; 100 aana chahiye.

### Example 1. Neela thotha, CuSO₄·5H₂O

- CuSO₄: Cu 63.5 + S 32 + O 4 × 16 = 64 → 63.5 + 32 + 64 = **159.5**.
- 5H₂O: 5 × 18 = **90**.
- Formula unit mass = 159.5 + 90 = **249.5 u**.
- Paani = 90 ÷ 249.5 × 100 = 36.07 ≈ **36.07%**. Salt = 159.5 ÷ 249.5 × 100 = 63.93%.

Jaanch: 36.07 + 63.93 = 100.00 ✅

### Example 2. Washing soda, Na₂CO₃·10H₂O

- Na₂CO₃: Na 2 × 23 = 46, C 12, O 3 × 16 = 48 → 46 + 12 + 48 = **106**.
- 10H₂O: 10 × 18 = **180**.
- Formula unit mass = 106 + 180 = **286 u**.
- Paani = 180 ÷ 286 × 100 = 62.94 ≈ **62.94%**. Salt = 106 ÷ 286 × 100 = 37.06%.

Jaanch: 62.94 + 37.06 = 100.00 ✅

Dhyan do: washing soda ke crystal mein **aadhe se zyada** (62.94%) mass paani ka hai! Sookhe dikhne wale crystal ka lagbhag 63% hissa to paani hai.

### Example 3. Hara thotha, FeSO₄·7H₂O

- FeSO₄: Fe 56 + S 32 + O 64 = **152**.
- 7H₂O: 7 × 18 = **126**.
- Formula unit mass = 152 + 126 = **278 u**.
- Paani = 126 ÷ 278 × 100 = 45.32 ≈ **45.32%**. Salt = 152 ÷ 278 × 100 = 54.68%.

Jaanch: 45.32 + 54.68 = 100.00 ✅

### Example 4. Epsom salt, MgSO₄·7H₂O aur Gypsum, CaSO₄·2H₂O

- MgSO₄: 24 + 32 + 64 = 120. 7H₂O = 126. Formula unit mass = 120 + 126 = **246 u**. Paani = 126 ÷ 246 × 100 = 51.22 ≈ **51.22%**.
- CaSO₄: 40 + 32 + 64 = 136. 2H₂O = 36. Formula unit mass = 136 + 36 = **172 u**. Paani = 36 ÷ 172 × 100 = 20.93 ≈ **20.93%**.

Jaanch: MgSO₄ ke liye 51.22 + (120 ÷ 246 × 100 = 48.78) = 100.00 ✅; CaSO₄ ke liye 20.93 + (136 ÷ 172 × 100 = 79.07) = 100.00 ✅

Ek baat dekho: FeSO₄·7H₂O aur MgSO₄·7H₂O dono mein 7 H₂O hain, par MgSO₄ halka hai (120 u, FeSO₄ ke 152 u se), isliye usme paani ka percent zyada hai (51.22% aur 45.32%). Paani ki ginti ek jaisi ho to **halka salt** paani ka zyada percent deta hai.

## Part C: Teacher demo: garam karo, paani nikalo, wapas paani daalo

**Sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo). Students is activity mein copper sulphate nahi chhoote, na garam karte hain.** Copper sulphate nigalne par nuksaan karta hai, aur crystal ko garam karna sirf teacher ka kaam hai. Students door se dekhte hain, aur teacher safety glasses pehenta hai.

Mr. Limaye ne school ki lab mein ye demo kai baar dikhaya hai. Maan lo wo ye karte hain:

1. Ek **sookhi** test tube mein thode **neela thotha ke crystal** daalte hain. (Mehul jaisa vidyarthi door se dekhta hai.)
2. Test tube ko holder se pakad kar halki aanch par **dheere-dheere** garam karte hain, aur munh aisi disha mein rakhte hain jahan koi na ho.
3. Neela rang dheere-dheere **safed** hota jaata hai, aur test tube ki thandi upar wali deewar par **paani ki boondein** dikhti hain. Ye wahi paani hai jo crystal ke andar tha: garmi se wo bhaap ban kar nikla aur thandi deewar par wapas boond bana.
4. Test tube thanda hone ke baad, teacher safed powder par **paani ki kuch boondein** daalte hain. Powder phir **neela** ho jaata hai, aur test tube thodi garam bhi mehsoos hoti hai.

Reaction ko hum shabdon mein aise likh sakte hain:

> **CuSO₄·5H₂O (neela) ⇌ CuSO₄ (safed) + 5H₂O**
> (garam karne par aage, paani milane par wapas)

```
        garmi (heat) dene par: paani bhaap ban kar nikalta hai
      ===========================================================>

   NEELA crystal                                         SAFED powder
   CuSO₄·5H₂O                                               CuSO₄
   (hydrated)                                           (anhydrous)

      <===========================================================
        paani ki boondein milane par: paani wapas jud jaata hai
                    (thodi garmi bhi nikalti hai)
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Copper sulphate ka rang-chakra: garmi dene par neela hydrate safed anhydrous powder ban jaata hai (paani nikalta hai); paani milane par safed powder wapas neela ho jaata hai.

### Isliye ye paani ki pehchaan ka test hai

Topic 1 mein humne paani ko pehchanne ka ek test dekha tha. **Safed (anhydrous) copper sulphate** paani ke sampark mein aate hi **neela** ho jaata hai. Agar kisi tarah (jaise Topic 1 mein bataya) kisi liquid par safed powder neela hota hai, to us liquid mein paani maujood hai. Par dhyan: ye test sirf bataata hai ki **paani hai**, ye nahi ki wo **shuddh** paani hai. Ye bhi **sirf teacher ke demo** mein hota hai.

### Example 5. Demo ke numbers se paani ki matra

Maan lo teacher ne **24.95 g** neela thotha ke crystal garam kiye aur thanda hone ke baad safed powder ka mass **15.95 g** nikla. (Ye numbers is lesson ke liye banaye gaye hain.)

- Gaya hua paani = 24.95 − 15.95 = **9.00 g**.
- Paani ka percent = 9.00 ÷ 24.95 × 100 = **36.07%**. Ye Example 1 ke 36.07% se **bilkul milta hai**.

Jaanch: formula se, 24.95 g (yaani 249.5 u ka 0.1 guna mass) mein paani = 0.1 × 90 = **9.00 g** ✅ aur safed CuSO₄ = 0.1 × 159.5 = **15.95 g** ✅

### Example 6. Ulta: formula ka x kaise nikaalein

Upar ke hi demo-numbers se hum "5" ko bhi nikaal sakte hain, maano humein pata nahi tha.

- Paani ka mass = 9.00 g. Mole = 9.00 ÷ 18 = **0.5 mol** (Chapter 4 ka tareeka).
- Safed CuSO₄ ka mass = 15.95 g. Mole = 15.95 ÷ 159.5 = **0.1 mol**.
- Paani ke mole ÷ salt ke mole = 0.5 ÷ 0.1 = **5**. Yaani formula **CuSO₄·5H₂O** ✅

Jaanch: 5 × 18 = 90 aur 159.5 + 90 = 249.5, jo Example 1 se milta hai ✅

### Example 7. Washing soda ke crystal kitne lage?

Kisi kaam ke liye **53 g anhydrous Na₂CO₃** chahiye, par dukaan par sirf washing soda ke **crystal Na₂CO₃·10H₂O** milte hain. Kitne gram crystal lene padenge?

- Na₂CO₃ ka molar mass 106 g/mol. 53 g = 53 ÷ 106 = **0.5 mol**.
- 0.5 mol Na₂CO₃ ke liye 0.5 mol crystal chahiye (har crystal ikai mein ek Na₂CO₃ hota hai). Crystal ka molar mass 286 g/mol.
- Crystal ka mass = 0.5 × 286 = **143 g**.

Jaanch: 143 g mein paani = 143 × 180 ÷ 286 = 90 g, aur 143 − 90 = **53 g** Na₂CO₃ ✅

## Part D: Efflorescence: hawa mein paani chhodne wale crystal

Ab wapas hawa ki taraf. Hawa mein hamesha thoda paani bhaap ke roop mein hota hai. Kabhi zyada (monsoon), kabhi bahut kam (garmi mein sookhi hawa). Hydrate crystal aur hawa ke beech **paani ka len-den** chalta rehta hai.

**Efflorescence** wahan hota hai jahan crystal apna **crystallisation ka paani hawa ko de deta hai**. Washing soda ka kissa lo: ek saaf, paarbhasi crystal ko agar Rajasthan jaisi sookhi garmi mein khuli plate par chhod do, to kuch din baad uski chamak chali jaati hai. Wo **safed, bhurbhura chura** ban jaata hai, kyunki crystal ka bada hissa paani (62.94%, Example 2) hawa mein chala gaya. Crystal ka aakaar tut jaata hai, kyunki paani hi jaali ko tikaye hue tha.

Jab hawa **bahut sookhi** ho, to hydrate crystal apna paani hawa ko "de" deta hai. Isi liye washing soda ke crystal ko band dabbe mein rakhte hain.

Efflorescence ka matlab hai: **kam nami wali hawa mein, hydrate crystal se paani nikalna.** Ye apne aap hota hai; garam karna zaroori nahi.

Efflorescent cheezein: washing soda ke crystal (Na₂CO₃·10H₂O), Glauber's salt (Na₂SO₄·10H₂O).

## Part E: Deliquescence aur hygroscopic: hawa se paani lene wali cheezein

Ab ulta. Kuch cheezein hawa se paani **kheenchti** hain. Do tarah ki:

### Deliquescent: itna kheenchti hain ki khud ghol ban jaati hain

Maan lo ek khuli plate mein **anhydrous calcium chloride** (CaCl₂) ke daane rakhe hain, monsoon mein. Kuch ghanton mein daane geele ho jaate hain, aur phir plate mein **paani jaisa ghol** ban jaata hai. Daano ne hawa se itni nami kheenchi ki wo us nami mein hi **ghul gaye**. Isse **deliquescence** kehte hain. Aise hi: **sodium hydroxide** (NaOH) ke dane, **magnesium chloride** (MgCl₂).

**Safety:** inme se koi bhi cheez students nahi chhoote. Sodium hydroxide bahut khatarnak hai (jala deta hai), aur calcium chloride bhi aankhon aur tvacha ko pareshaan karta hai. Ye bas padhne aur samajhne ki baat hai.

### Hygroscopic: nami kheenchti hain, par ghol nahi banti

Silica gel ka daana nami kheench leta hai par **thos hi rehta hai**: ghol nahi banta. Aise hi **quicklime** (CaO) hawa ki nami kheenchta hai (wo paani se reaction bhi karta hai, aur jalaata hai; ise students nahi chhoote), aur **concentrated sulphuric acid** hawa se itni nami kheenchta hai ki khuli beaker mein rakhne par uski matra badhti hai (kyunki wo paani samaa leta hai).

Ek baat dhyan se: **sabhi deliquescent cheezein hygroscopic hain, par sabhi hygroscopic cheezein deliquescent nahi.** Silica gel hygroscopic hai par deliquescent nahi.

### Concentrated sulphuric acid: sirf naam ke liye

Concentrated sulphuric acid ek aur tarah ka kaam bhi karta hai: wo **dehydrating agent** hai, yaani kisi aur cheez ke andar se paani ke H aur O ke atoms bhi kheench leta hai. Jaise: agar sugar par ye acid dala jaaye, to chini kaali ho jaati hai (carbon), aur garmi bhi nikalti hai. Ye bahut **khatarnak** hai aur **hum ise kabhi activity ke roop mein nahi karte**; ye sirf ek kahani ki tarah yahan bataya gaya hai taaki "dehydrating agent" ka matlab pata chal jaaye. Iska poora vishleshan **aage ki class mein** hoga.

### Teeno ko ek chart mein

```
              Hawa mein paani ke saath kya hota hai?
                    /                        \
     Hawa se paani LETI hain          Hawa ko paani DETI hai
     (nami badhti hai)                (hydrate crystal se)
          /          \                       EFFLORESCENT
   HYGROSCOPIC    DELIQUESCENT          (jaise washing soda ke
   (thos hi       (itna lein ki          crystal: chura ban jaate)
   rehti hai)     ghol ban jaaye)
      silica gel,    anhydrous CaCl₂,
      quicklime,     NaOH, MgCl₂
      conc. H₂SO₄
```

> **Figure 4.3** – Hawa mein paani ke saath teen tarah ka vyavhaar: efflorescent (paani chhodta hai), hygroscopic (paani leta hai, thos hi rehta hai ya samaa leta hai) aur deliquescent (itna leta hai ki ghol ban jaaye). Deliquescent cheezein hygroscopic ki ek zyada tez kism hain.

### Drying agents: kaam ki baat

Hygroscopic cheezon se bahut kaam liya jaata hai:

- **Silica gel ke packet:** joote ke dabbe, bag, camera, dawaai ki shishiyaan. Packet par "DO NOT EAT" isliye likha hota hai ki wo khaane ki cheez nahi hai.
- **Anhydrous CaCl₂:** lab mein kuch gaison ko sookha karne ke liye, aur kuch jagah nami hatane ke packet mein (sirf prashikshit log use karte hain).
- **Quicklime:** purane zamane mein godown mein nami kam karne ke liye, par ab sambhal kar, kyunki ye jalata hai.

### Namak monsoon mein jam kyun jaata hai? (Ek imaandaar baat)

Monsoon mein namak-daan mein namak ka bhurbhura chura jam jaata hai, ya gaanth-gaanth ban jaata hai. Aam kahani ye hai ki "namak nami kheenchta hai". **Asli kahani thodi zyada imaandaar hai**: **shuddh** NaCl nami ko bahut zyada nahi kheenchta. Table salt mein aksar **bahut kam matra mein** magnesium chloride aur calcium chloride jaisi **hygroscopic** ashuddhiyan hoti hain. Wahi hawa se nami kheench kar daano ko aapas mein chipka deti hain. (Shuddh namak bhi bahut zyada nami wali hawa mein thoda geela ho sakta hai. Isiliye kai brand ke namak mein "anti-caking" cheezein milayi jaati hain jo daano ko alag-alag rakhti hain.)

Isse ghar mein ye tarkeeb nikli hai: namak-daan mein kuch **chaawal ke daane** daal dena. Wo namak se pehle nami kheench lete hain, isliye namak thodi der aur sookha rehta hai.

## Part F: Ghar par adult ke saath: ek chhoti nami ki jaanch

**Ghar par adult ke saath.** Ek **band** silica gel ka packet (jo kisi naye dabbe mein aaya ho) lo. **Use kholna nahi, chakhna nahi, aur bacchon aur pets se door rakhna.** Adult ke saath ye karo:

1. Packet ko kitchen ke tarazu (digital scale) par rakh kar uska mass note karo (jaise 5.0 g).
2. Use ek plate mein kisi **khuli, geeli jagah** (jaise baarish ke mausam mein khidki ke paas) ek din ke liye rakho. **Plate ko kisi bhi khaane ki cheez se alag rakhna.**
3. Agle din dobara mass tolo.

Nami wale mausam mein packet ka mass **thoda badh** jaata hai, kyunki daane ne hawa se nami kheench li. Agar mass nahi badha, to hawa bahut sookhi thi, ya tarazu itna sensitive nahi hai. Ye koi chemical-reaction wala test nahi hai; ye sirf dikhata hai ki silica gel ki nami ki taraf kheench hai. **Packet ko dobara dabbe mein na rakhna; kaam ho jaane par adult ke haath se kachre mein daal do, aur haath dho lo.**

## Part G: Kaun si cheez kaun sa vyavhaar karti hai?

Neeche ki table mein har cheez ko behaviour ke hisaab se baant kar dekho.

| Cheez | Khuli hawa mein kya hota hai | Kaun sa naam |
|---|---|---|
| Washing soda ke crystal (sookhi garmi) | Paani chhodte hain, safed chura ban jaate hain | Efflorescent |
| Anhydrous CaCl₂ (monsoon) | Hawa se nami kheench kar ghol ban jaata hai | Deliquescent |
| NaOH ke dane | Hawa se nami kheench kar ghol ban jaate hain | Deliquescent |
| Silica gel | Nami kheenchta hai par thos hi rehta hai | Hygroscopic |
| Quicklime | Nami kheenchta hai | Hygroscopic |
| Concentrated H₂SO₄ | Nami kheenchta hai, matra badhti hai | Hygroscopic |
| Neela thotha (normal hawa) | Aam taur par badlav nahi dikhta | Na efflorescent, na deliquescent (aam hawa mein) |
| Gypsum | Normal hawa mein stable | Stable hydrate |

Is table mein **Reasoning** ki ek kunji hai: pehle dekho ki cheez ka paani **jaa raha hai** (efflorescent) ya **aa raha hai** (hygroscopic ya deliquescent). Agar aa raha hai, to dekho ki ghol ban gaya ya nahi.

---

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Bindu (·) ko guna samajhna**

CuSO₄·5H₂O ka formula unit mass 159.5 + 5 × 18 = 249.5 hai. Agar koi 159.5 × 5 × 18 = 14355 likhe to galat hai; bindu ka matlab "saath mein juda" hai.

**Galti 2: Hydrate ko geela samajhna**

Neela thotha ke crystal sookhe hote hain, phir bhi unme 36.07% paani hai. Crystallisation ka paani aur "geelapan" alag-alag cheezein hain.

**Galti 3: Efflorescence aur deliquescence ko ulta likhna**

Efflorescence: crystal paani **chhodta** hai (chura ban jaata hai). Deliquescence: cheez paani **leti** hai aur ghol ban jaati hai.

**Galti 4: "Har hygroscopic cheez deliquescent hai" maan lena**

Silica gel hygroscopic hai par thos rehta hai. Deliquescent matlab "itna ki ghol ban jaaye".

**Galti 5: Heat karke hydrate ko "jalna" samajhna**

Neela thotha garam karne par **paani chhodta** hai; wo jalta nahi. Safed powder wahi salt hai bas paani ke bina, aur paani milane par wapas neela ho jaata hai.

**Galti 6: Jaanch bhool jaana**

Hydrate ke percent mein salt ka percent aur paani ka percent jodo; total 100 hona chahiye. Agar 99 ya 101 se door aaye to kul mass galat jodha hai.

**Galti 7: Safed copper sulphate ke test ko "paani shuddh hai" samajhna**

Ye test sirf batata hai ki **paani maujood hai**. Shuddh hai ya nahi, ye nahi batata.

## Quick Recap

- **Water of crystallisation** crystal ki jaali ka hissa hai; ginti fixed hoti hai (CuSO₄·5H₂O mein 5). Bindu (·) "saath mein juda" dikhata hai.
- **Formula unit mass:** CuSO₄·5H₂O = 249.5 u (paani 36.07%), Na₂CO₃·10H₂O = 286 u (62.94%), FeSO₄·7H₂O = 278 u (45.32%), MgSO₄·7H₂O = 246 u (51.22%), CaSO₄·2H₂O = 172 u (20.93%).
- **Paani ka percent** = (ginti × 18 ÷ hydrate ka formula unit mass) × 100.
- **Teacher demo:** neela thotha garam karne par paani nikalta hai aur safed anhydrous CuSO₄ bachta hai; paani milane par wapas neela. Safed CuSO₄ paani ka test hai (sirf teacher demo, students nahi chhoote).
- **Efflorescence:** hydrate paani chhodkar chura ban jaata hai (washing soda). **Deliquescence:** cheez hawa se itna paani leti hai ki ghol ban jaaye (anhydrous CaCl₂, NaOH, MgCl₂). **Hygroscopic:** nami kheenchti hai (silica gel, quicklime, conc. H₂SO₄).
- **Drying agents** (silica gel, CaCl₂) cheezon ko sookha rakhte hain. Concentrated H₂SO₄ ka dehydrating kaam sirf naam ke liye.
- Namak monsoon mein jaam hota hai kyunki usme kam matra mein hygroscopic ashuddhiyan hoti hain (aur nami wali hawa ka bhi asar).
- Koi bhi chemical (copper sulphate, NaOH, conc. H₂SO₄, quicklime) students nahi chhoote, na chakhte hain.

## Practice Questions

1. Find the formula unit mass of sodium sulphate decahydrate, Na₂SO₄·10H₂O, and the percentage of water in it. Check that the two percentages add up to 100. (Use Na 23, S 32, O 16, H 1.)

2. A sample of 64.4 g of Na₂SO₄·10H₂O is heated until all its water of crystallisation is gone. How much water is lost and how much anhydrous sodium sulphate is left?

3. A teacher heats 2.19 g of a hydrated calcium chloride, CaCl₂·xH₂O, until constant mass, and 1.08 g of water is lost. Find x. (Ca 40, Cl 35.5.)

4. Compare CaCl₂·2H₂O and CaCl₂·6H₂O. Which has the larger percentage of water, and why? Support your answer with calculations.

5. Why is the water in a crystal of blue vitriol not like the "wetness" of a wet cloth? Give two points of difference.

6. A packet of silica gel is placed in a camera bag. Is it acting as an efflorescent, a deliquescent or a hygroscopic substance? Explain, and say why it is labelled "DO NOT EAT".

7. A pinch of washing soda crystals is kept open in a very dry room for several days, while a few granules of anhydrous calcium chloride are kept open in a very humid room. Describe what happens in each case and name the property.

8. In a school lab a teacher shows white anhydrous copper sulphate turning blue after a few drops of a clear liquid are added. What can you conclude, and what can you not conclude, about the liquid?

9. Why does a family sometimes put a few grains of rice in the salt shaker during the monsoon? Give a correct, honest explanation of why salt cakes at all.

10. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) The dot in CuSO₄·5H₂O means multiplication.
    (b) Silica gel is deliquescent because it keeps absorbing moisture.
    (c) Heating blue copper sulphate crystals is a safe activity for students to do alone at home.
    (d) A deliquescent substance can turn into a solution on its own in humid air.

### Answers

1. Na₂SO₄: 2 × 23 = 46, S = 32, O = 4 × 16 = 64, jod = 142. 10H₂O = 10 × 18 = 180. Formula unit mass = 142 + 180 = **322 u**. Paani = 180 ÷ 322 × 100 = 55.90 ≈ **55.90%**. Salt = 142 ÷ 322 × 100 = 44.10%. Jaanch: 55.90 + 44.10 = 100.00 ✅

2. 64.4 g = 322 u ka 0.2 guna (64.4 ÷ 322 = 0.2). Paani = 0.2 × 180 = **36.0 g**. Anhydrous Na₂SO₄ = 0.2 × 142 = **28.4 g**. Jaanch: 36.0 + 28.4 = 64.4 ✅

3. Paani = 1.08 g, mole = 1.08 ÷ 18 = 0.06 mol. Bacha hua salt = 2.19 − 1.08 = 1.11 g CaCl₂. CaCl₂ ka molar mass = 40 + 2 × 35.5 = 111 g/mol, to mole = 1.11 ÷ 111 = 0.01 mol. Paani ke mole ÷ salt ke mole = 0.06 ÷ 0.01 = **6**. Isliye x = 6, yaani CaCl₂·6H₂O. Jaanch: 111 + 6 × 18 = 219 u, 2.19 g = 219 u ka 0.01 guna ✅

4. CaCl₂·2H₂O: 111 + 36 = 147 u. Paani = 36 ÷ 147 × 100 = **24.49%**. CaCl₂·6H₂O: 111 + 108 = 219 u. Paani = 108 ÷ 219 × 100 = **49.32%**. **CaCl₂·6H₂O** mein paani ka percent zyada hai, kyunki salt ek hi hai (111 u) par usme paani ke 6 molecules juda hain, 2 nahi. Jaanch: 24.49 + (111 ÷ 147 × 100 = 75.51) = 100.00 ✅

5. (i) Blue vitriol ka paani crystal ki jaali ka hissa hai, isliye uski ginti fixed hai (5 H₂O har CuSO₄ ke saath); geele kapde ka paani koi bhi matra mein ho sakta hai. (ii) Crystal sookha dikhta aur chhoone mein sookha lagta hai; paani nikalne ke liye garam karna padta hai, aur nikalne par crystal ka neela rang aur shakal badal jaati hai, jabki geele kapde ko sirf nichod ya sukha kar paani nikaal sakte hain.

6. **Hygroscopic**: wo hawa ki nami ko kheench leta hai par thos hi rehta hai. Ye camera ko nami aur fungus se bachata hai. "DO NOT EAT" isliye likha hai ki wo khaane ki cheez nahi hai; use muh mein daalna nuksaan kar sakta hai.

7. Washing soda ke crystal: sookhi hawa mein wo apna crystallisation ka paani chhod dete hain aur safed chura ban jaate hain. Ye **efflorescence** hai. Anhydrous CaCl₂ ke daane: nami wali hawa se paani kheench kar geele ho jaate hain aur ghol ban jaate hain. Ye **deliquescence** hai.

8. Hum ye maan sakte hain ki liquid mein **paani maujood hai** (safed anhydrous CuSO₄ paani milne par neela ho jaata hai). Hum ye **nahi** keh sakte ki wo liquid **shuddh paani** hai, kyunki doosre ghol mein bhi paani hota hai. Ye test sirf teacher demo mein hota hai.

9. Chaawal ke daane namak se pehle nami kheench lete hain, isliye namak thodi der aur sookha rehta hai. Namak jam isliye jaata hai ki table salt mein kam matra mein MgCl₂ jaisi hygroscopic ashuddhiyan hoti hain, jo nami kheench kar daano ko chipka deti hain; shuddh NaCl nami ko bahut zyada nahi kheenchta.

10. (a) **False** ❌. Bindu ka matlab "saath mein juda" hai, guna nahi. (b) **False** ❌. Silica gel hygroscopic hai par thos hi rehta hai; deliquescent wo hota hai jo ghol ban jaaye. (c) **False** ❌. Copper sulphate nuksaan karta hai aur garam karna sirf teacher ka kaam hai (demo). (d) **True** ✅. Deliquescent cheez hawa se itni nami leti hai ki khud ghol ban jaati hai.
