# Topic 5: Saaf Paani Ka Safar: Purification

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki paani crystals ke andar bhi chhupa hota hai (water of crystallisation), aur kuch padarth hawa ki nami tak kheench lete hain. Topic 2 aur 3 mein ye bhi dikha tha ki paani mein bahut kuch **ghula** hota hai: namak, calcium, magnesium. Ab sawal ye hai ki jo paani nadi, talaab ya borewell se aata hai, wo hamare nal tak **saaf aur surakshit** kaise ban kar pahunchta hai? Aaj hum ek poore city water treatment plant ka safar karenge, ghar ke tareeke dekhenge, aur ek chhota filter bhi banayenge (adult ke saath).

## Plant ke gate par bhoora paani

School ki trip ek city ke water treatment plant par gayi thi. Mr. Kini sabse aage chal rahe the, aur Kritika unke thik peeche, notebook haath mein. Gate ke paas ek chaudi nahar thi, jisme se bhoora, mitti jaisa paani plant ke andar ja raha tha. Kritika ne naak sikodi. "Sir, ye paani to bilkul gandla dikh raha hai. Isse log nahaate bhi nahi honge. Aur ye hi paani hamare nal mein aata hai?"

Mr. Kini muskuraye. "Aaj plant ke aakhir tak chalo, phir poocho." Plant ke andar badi-badi hauj (tanks) thi. Ek jagah paani halka saaf tha, doosri jagah aur saaf, aur aakhir mein kaanch jaisa. Plant ke engineer ne ek glass bhar ke Kritika ko dikhaya: "Ye dekho, bilkul transparent. Par ek baat batao: kya ye paani **peene layak** hai?"

Kritika boli, "Saaf hai, to peene layak hi hoga!" Engineer ne sir hilaya. "Yehi sabse badi galatfehmi hai. Hamari asli jaanch **laboratory** mein hoti hai, kyunki paani ko khatarnak banane wali asli cheez aankhon se dikhne wali gandagi nahi, **aankhon se na dikhne wale germs** hain."

Is topic mein hum dekhenge ki plant ke har step ka kya kaam hai, aur kyun "saaf dikhna" aur "surakshit hona" do alag baatein hain.

## Part A: Water cycle: paani ka apna chakkar

Plant mein paani ka safar samajhne se pehle ek baat: **hamara paani aata kahan se hai?** Chapter 1 mein evaporation aur condensation seekhe the. Wahi do kaam paani ka ek bada chakkar chalate hain. Dhoop se samundar, nadi aur talaab ka paani **evaporate** hokar bhaap banta hai. Bhaap oopar jaakar thanda hota hai aur **condense** hokar chhoti boondon ke roop mein **badal** bana deta hai. Jab boondein bhaari ho jaati hain, to **barish** (ya barf) ban kar gir jaati hain. Ye paani nadi, talaab aur zameen ke andar (borewell, kuan) jama hota hai, aur phir se evaporate hota hai. Ye chakkar kabhi nahi rukta.

```
            DHOOP (sun)
               \  |  /
              ---  O  ---
               /  |  \

        ,--~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~--.
       (   BADAL (condense)   )        <- bhaap thandi hoti hai
        `--~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~--'
           ^  ^  ^        | | |
           |  |  |        v v v
   evaporation (bhaap       BARISH (rain)
   ban kar oopar)           aur barf
           |  |  |        | | |
    ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ v v v ~~~~~~~~~
    ~~ SAMUNDAR / TALAAB ~~       /\    /\
    ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~   /  \  /  \   <- pahaad
    ~~~~~~~~ nadi bahkar ~~~~~~~/    \/    \
    ~~~~~~~ samundar ki ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
    ~~~~~~~~ taraf jaati hai ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
            neeche zameen ke andar: borewell ka paani
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Water cycle: dhoop se evaporation, oopar jaakar condensation (badal), phir barish. Barish ka paani nadi, talaab aur zameen ke neeche jama hota hai, aur chakkar dobara shuru ho jaata hai.

Ek dilchasp baat: evaporation **ek natural distillation** hai (Chapter 2 wali distillation yaad karo). Samundar ka namkeen paani evaporate hota hai, namak neeche rah jaata hai, aur condense hokar jo barish aati hai wo lagbhag **saaf aur namak-rahit** hoti hai. Par girte waqt aur zameen par bahte waqt wo mitti, patte, sewage aur germs apne saath le leti hai. Isiliye nadi ka paani seedha peena surakshit nahi hota.

## Part B: City treatment plant ke step, aur har step ka "kyun"

Bhoora raw water ko saaf, surakshit paani banane ke liye plant kai step lagata hai. **Har step kisi ek khaas cheez ko hataata hai**, aur ek step doosre ke liye zameen taiyar karta hai.

          |     (moti jaali)            |   jaali mein ruk jaate hain
          +-----------------------------+

          +-----------------------------+

          |     (ret aur kankad parat)  |   baareek kan ret mein ruk jaate hain

### Step 1: Screening (jaali se rokna)

Intake par moti jaali ya rod lagi hoti hai. Nadi mein bahte hue **bade tukde**, jaise lakdi ke tukde, plastic ki thailiyan, patte, aur kabhi-kabhi machhli, jaali mein ruk jaate hain. Ye bilkul wahi **sieving** wala idea hai jo Chapter 2 mein dekha tha: jaali ke chhed se chhota nikal jaata hai, bada ruk jaata hai. Agar ye bade tukde na rokein, to aage ke pump aur pipe jam ho jaayein.

### Step 2: Coagulation: fitkari kyun daalte hain?

Screening ke baad bhi paani bhoora hai, kyunki usme **bahut baareek mitti ke kan** tairte hain. Ye itne chhote hain ki **khud neeche baithte hi nahi** (Chapter 2 ke Topic 3 mein colloid aur Tyndall effect yaad karo: bahut chhote kan hawa-paani mein tairte rehte hain). Seedha sand filter mein daalen to wo jaldi band ho jaayega.

Yahan kaam aati hai **fitkari (alum)**. Fitkari paani mein ghul kar **halke, jelly jaise, chipchipe gucche (floc)** banati hai. Ye gucche **baareek kanon ko apne saath chipka lete hain**, aur gucche badhte-badhte itne **bhaari** ho jaate hain ki ab wo neeche baith sakte hain. Isiliye fitkari daalne ke baad paani ko dheere-dheere ghumaya jaata hai, taaki gucche bante rahein.

Ghar mein bhi gande paani mein **fitkari ka tukda** ghumaane ka purana tareeka hai: thodi der mein mitti neeche baithne lagti hai. Par isse bhi paani peene layak nahi banta: germs ab bhi hote hain.

**Topic 4 ka link:** ghar ki fitkari **potash alum, KAl(SO₄)₂·12H₂O**, khud ek **hydrate** hai: 12 water of crystallisation wala. Plants mein aksar uska ek kareebi cousin, aluminium sulphate, use hota hai.

### Step 3: Sedimentation (neeche baithna)

Coagulation ke baad paani ek **bade, shaant tank** mein jaata hai, jahan wo bahut dheere behta hai. Floc ke bhaari gucche tank ke **tale mein baith jaate hain**, aur oopar ka paani kaafi saaf ho jaata hai. Neeche jama kichad (**sludge**) alag nikaal liya jaata hai. Ye Chapter 2 wala **sedimentation aur decantation** hi hai, bas bade scale par. Paani ko tank mein kitni der rukna hai, ye bhi ek hisaab hai (Part D ke examples mein).

### Step 4: Sand filtration (ret se chhanna)

Sedimentation ke baad bhi thoda halka floc aur bahut baareek kan paani mein reh jaate hain. Ab paani **bade filter bed** se guzarta hai: oopar **ret (sand)**, neeche **kankad (gravel)** ki parat. Ret ke daanon ke beech ki jagah bahut patli hoti hai, to baareek kan wahi atak jaate hain. Chapter 2 ka **filtration** hi hai, filter paper ki jagah ret ki parat. Dhire-dhire ret ke upar kichad jam jaata hai, to filter ko ulta paani chala kar saaf kar diya jaata hai.

Is step ke baad paani **dekhne mein bilkul saaf** hota hai. Par kya ab wo peene layak hai? Nahi: **germs abhi bhi hain.**

### Step 5: Chlorination (germs ko maarna)

Germs (bacteria, virus) itne chhote hote hain ki **na aankhon se dikhte hain, na ret mein ruk jaate hain**. Inhe maarne ke liye plant mein paani mein **bahut kam matra mein chlorine** milaya jaata hai. Chlorine germs ko maar deta hai. Matra **bahut soch-samajh kar** rakhi jaati hai: itni ki germs mar jaayein, par paani ka swaad ya sehat kharab na ho. Thoda sa chlorine pipe mein aage tak bhi bacha rehta hai, taaki paani raaste mein dobara germs se na bhar jaaye.

**Zaroori suraksha baat:** chlorine wale chemicals (bleaching powder, chlorine tablet, ghar ka bleach) **bahut khatarnak** ho sakte hain. Plants mein ye **training paaye expert** sambhaalte hain. Bachche inhe **kabhi na chhoo-ein, na kisi cheez mein milaayein, na khud se paani mein daalein.** Ghar mein bleach ko kisi aur safai wale chemical (jaise toilet cleaner) ke saath milana to bahut hi khatarnak zehreeli gas bana sakta hai.

### Step 6: Storage aur supply

Treat hua paani **oonchi tanki** (overhead tank) aur **underground reservoir** mein jama hota hai, aur pipeline se ghar-ghar jaata hai. Raaste mein purani, toot-phooti ya ganda paani ke paas se guzarti pipe se paani dobara ganda ho sakta hai: isiliye ghar ki tanki aur pipe ki safai bhi zaroori hai, aur isliye ghar mein bhi **peene se pehle ek aur suraksha** (boiling ya filter) rakhne ki salaah di jaati hai.

> **Jaanch:** Sedimentation tank ke baad aur sand filter se pehle ke paani mein halka bhoorapan ho sakta hai. Kya ye paani ab peene layak hai? ❌ Nahi. Abhi sand filtration aur chlorination baaki hain (aur germs ka darr bhi).

## Part C: Saaf dikhna aur surakshit hona: do alag baatein

Plant ki kahani ka sabse zaroori sabak:

> **Saaf paani = surakshit paani, ye zaroori nahi.**

- Ek **bilkul transparent** glass paani mein lakhon germs ho sakte hain, jo na dikhte hain, na swaad se pata chalte hain.
- Ek **thoda bhoora** paani, agar ubaal liya jaaye, germs se surakshit ho sakta hai (par mitti peene mein achhi nahi lagti, isliye pehle chhaan lete hain).
- Paani mein **ghuli hui** cheezein (dissolved salts, kuch zehreele chemicals) bhi aankhon se nahi dikhti. Kapde ya ret ke filter inhe nahi rokte (Chapter 2: solution ke kan filter paper se nikal jaate hain).

### Gande paani se hone wali bimariyan

Jab paani mein **germs** (bacteria, virus ya chhote parasite) hon aur log wo paani piyein ya usse khana banayein ya haath dhoyein, to **waterborne diseases** phail sakti hain. Chaar aam naam:

| Bimari | Kis tarah ka asar | Kaise rokein |
|---|---|---|
| **Cholera** | Bahut tez dast aur ulti, sharir mein paani ki kami | Ubala ya treated paani; ORS dena; doctor ko turant dikhana |
| **Typhoid** | Lagataar bukhaar, kamzori, pet dard | Surakshit paani, saaf khana, haath dhona |
| **Dysentery** | Pet dard aur dast (kabhi khoon ke saath) | Surakshit paani, haath dhona, makkhiyon se bachaav |
| **Jaundice (Hepatitis A)** | Aankhon aur peshaab ka peela hona, thakaan | Surakshit paani aur khana, saaf-safai |

Ye sirf **samajhne ki jaankari** hai, kisi bimari ka ilaaj ghar mein khud mat karo: haal bigde to **doctor** ko dikhao. Aur aise paani se roz bachne ka sabse aasaan tareeka hai: **surakshit paani, haath dhona, aur saaf khaana.**

Baarish ke mausam aur baadh ke baad ye bimariyan zyada phailti hain, kyunki sewage aur nadi ka paani mix ho jaata hai aur pipe mein germs ghus jaate hain. Isiliye us waqt ubaal kar paani peene ki salaah zyada di jaati hai.

## Part D: Plant ke numbers (solved examples)

Plant ke paas bahut paani aata hai, aur har step mein hisaab lagta hai. **Neeche ke saare numbers made-up aur rounded hain**, sirf practice ke liye (asli plant alag ho sakte hain).

**Example 1 (settling tank mein paani kitni der rukta hai?):** Ek sedimentation tank ka volume **1800 m³** hai, aur usme paani **600 m³ har ghante** aata hai. Paani ko tank mein kitni der rukna padta hai?

- Rukne ka time = volume ÷ flow = 1800 m³ ÷ 600 m³/h = **3 ghante**

**Jaanch:** 600 m³/h × 3 h = 1800 m³ ✅. Agar flow double (1200 m³/h) kar do, to time aadha ho kar 1.5 ghante ho jaata hai: floc ke baithne ke liye kam time milega, isiliye plant mein flow ko dhyaan se rakhte hain.

**Example 2 (fitkari kitni lagegi?):** Ek chhote plant mein fitkari **25 mg har litre** paani ke hisaab se milayi jaati hai. Aaj **4,00,000 L** paani saaf karna hai. Kitni fitkari lagegi?

- Fitkari = 25 mg/L × 400000 L = 10000000 mg
- 1 kg = 1000 g = 1000000 mg, to 10000000 mg ÷ 1000000 = **10 kg**

**Jaanch:** 10 kg = 10000 g = 10000000 mg ✅ aur 10000000 ÷ 400000 = 25 mg/L ✅.

**Example 3 (chlorine ka hisaab: sirf plant ke liye):** Plant mein chlorine **3 mg har litre** ke hisaab se milaya jaata hai. **5,00,000 L** paani ke liye kitna chlorine chahiye?

- Chlorine = 3 mg/L × 500000 L = 1500000 mg = **1500 g = 1.5 kg**

**Jaanch:** 1500 g ÷ 500000 L = 0.003 g/L = 3 mg/L ✅. Dhyan do: itne bade paani ke liye bhi bas 1.5 kg: yaani **bahut kam matra**. Ise bachche kabhi nahi sambhaalte.

**Example 4 (plant mein paani ka nuksaan):** Ek plant ne ek din mein **12,00,000 L** kaccha paani liya aur **11,40,000 L** treated paani supply kiya (baaki sludge aur filter ki safai mein gaya). Kitna paani gaya, aur kitna percent?

- Nuksaan = 1200000 − 1140000 = **60000 L**
- Percent = 60000 ÷ 1200000 × 100 = **5%**

**Jaanch:** 5% of 1200000 = 0.05 × 1200000 = 60000 ✅.

**Example 5 (germ aur kapde ke chhed):** Ek moti kapde ke jaal ke chhed lagbhag **100 µm** (0.1 mm) ke hain, jabki ek aam bacteria lagbhag **2 µm** ka hota hai (1 µm = 1 mm ka hazaarwa hissa; dono values approximate). Bacteria chhed se kitne guna chhota hai? Kya kapda use rokega?

- Ratio = 100 µm ÷ 2 µm = **50 guna**
- Bacteria chhed se 50 guna chhota hai, to wo **aaraam se nikal jaayega**. Kapda sirf bade tukde rokega, germs nahi.

**Jaanch:** 2 × 50 = 100 ✅. Isiliye "sirf kapde se chhana" paani ko germs-free nahi banata.

**Example 6 (ubaalne mein kitni garmi aur time lagega?):** Ek adult 2 L paani (2 kg) ko 30 °C se 100 °C tak 2000 W ke electric kettle mein garm karta hai. Garmi ka hisaab Physics wale Q = m c ΔT se (c = 4200 J/kg °C). Garmi ka koi nuksaan nahi maano: time kitna lagega?

- Q = 2 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × (100 − 30) °C = 2 × 4200 × 70 = **588000 J**
- Time = Q ÷ P = 588000 J ÷ 2000 J/s = **294 s** = 294 ÷ 60 = **4.9 minute**
- Phir paani ko **rolling boil** (zor se bulbule) par **lagbhag 1 minute** aur ubaalna hai. (Oonche pahaad par aam taur par di jaane wali safety salaah lagbhag 3 minute ki hai, kyunki wahan paani kam temperature par ubalta hai.)

**Jaanch:** 2000 × 294 = 588000 ✅. Ubaalna **sirf adult** kare: bachche kabhi garm paani ya chulhe ko haath na lagayein.

**Example 7 (fitkari ek hydrate hai: Topic 4 ka hisaab):** Potash alum KAl(SO₄)₂·12H₂O ka formula unit mass aur % water nikaalo. (K = 39, Al = 27, S = 32, O = 16, H = 1; H₂O = 18.)

- KAl(SO₄)₂: 39 + 27 + 2 × (32 + 4 × 16) = 39 + 27 + 2 × 96 = 39 + 27 + 192 = **258 u**
- 12H₂O: 12 × 18 = **216 u**
- Kul = 258 + 216 = **474 u**
- % water = 216 ÷ 474 × 100 = **45.57%** (lagbhag)

**Jaanch:** 258 + 216 = 474 ✅ aur 216 ÷ 474 = 0.4557 ✅. Yaani fitkari ke crystal ka lagbhag 45.6% hissa paani hai (Topic 4 ki Epsom salt ki tarah ek hydrate).

## Part E: Ghar ke tareeke aur unki limits

Sabke ghar mein city plant nahi hota. Ghar ke kuch aam tareeke, aur har ek ki limit:

| Tareeka | Kya karta hai | Limit (kya nahi karta) |
|---|---|---|
| **Kapde ya jaali se chhanna** | Bade tukde, kichad rokta hai | Germs aur ghuli cheezein nahi rokta |
| **Candle (ceramic) filter** | Baareek kan aur kai germs rokta hai | Saaf na rakho to khud germs ka ghar ban jaata hai; ghuli cheezein nahi rokta; maker ki salaah se roz saaf karo |
| **Ubaalna (boiling)** | Rolling boil par lagbhag 1 minute rakhne par **germs mar jaate hain** | Mitti, ghuli cheezein aur kai chemicals nahi hataata; kathor paani ka scale (Topic 3) to aur banta hai; sirf adult kare |
| **UV purifier** | Ultraviolet (UV) roshni germs ko maarti hai | Paani pehle saaf hona chahiye (mitti germs par "chhaya" kar deti hai); ghuli cheezein nahi hataata; bijli chahiye |
| **RO (reverse osmosis)** | Ek bahut baareek jhilli (membrane) paani ko nikalne deti hai, aur ghule namak jaisi cheezein rok leti hai | Isme kuch paani bekaar bhi jaata hai; **ye aage ki class mein** poori tarah samjhenge |

Ek ghar ka purifier aksar **kai tareeke milakar** kaam karta hai (jaise pehle filter, phir UV ya RO), kyunki har tareeka sirf **kuch hi cheezein** hatata hai. Ye wahi soch hai jo plant mein dikhi: ek step doosre ka kaam poora karta hai.

**Ek zaroori baat:** koi bhi ghar ka filter ya purifier **100% sab kuch** nahi hataata. Hamesha dekho ki wo **kya** hataata hai aur **kya nahi**.

## Part F: Activity: bottle filter (ghar par adult ke saath)

Ab hum plant ka chhota model banayenge: **layered bottle filter**. **Dhyan se padho: ye filter sirf samajhne ke liye hai. Iska saaf-sa dikhne wala paani PEENE LAYAK BILKUL NAHI hai, usme germs ho sakte hain. Ise kabhi peena nahi hai, kisi ko pilana nahi hai.**

**Ye activity ghar par adult ke saath karein.** Plastic ki bottle **adult hi katega** (bachche chaaku ya kainchi se nahi kaatenge).

**Saaman:** 1 saaf plastic bottle (1 ya 2 L), ret (dhoyi hui), moti ret ya baareek kankad (dhoye hue), chhote kankad, **activated charcoal** (aquarium ki dukaan ya pharmacy se; chulhe ki raakh ya jala hua koyla nahi, aur use pehle paani se dho lo), rui (cotton wool), ek saaf glass ya katori (neeche rakhne ko), aur "ganda paani": ek jug saaf paani mein **bagiche ki mitti ek chammach** ghol do (naali ya sewage ka paani nahi!).

**Kaise banayein (adult ke saath):**

1. Adult bottle ka **neeche ka hissa kaat** de (bottle ko ulta rakhna hai) aur dhakkan ko **hata de** (ya dhakkan mein chhota ched bana de).
2. Bottle ko ulta karke **gardan (neck) neeche** rakho aur neeche saaf glass rakho.
3. Neeche se oopar parat dalo, har parat ke baad halke se dabao: pehle **rui**, phir **charcoal**, phir **baareek ret**, phir **moti ret**, aur sabse oopar **chhote kankad**.
4. Pehle saaf paani se filter ko **dho lo** (jo paani nikle, use phenk do).
5. Ab oopar se **mitti wala ganda paani** dheere-dheere daalo aur neeche nikalte paani ko dekho.

```
         ganda paani (mitti wala), oopar se dheere-dheere
                   \   |   /
                    v  v  v
          +-----------------------+
          |  o  O  o  O  o  O  o  |   <- 1. chhote kankad (pebbles)
          |                       |      (bade tukde rokte hain)
          |  . : . : . : . : . :  |   <- 2. moti ret (coarse sand)
          |                       |
          |  . . . . . . . . . .  |   <- 3. baareek ret (fine sand)
          |                       |      (chhote kan rokti hai)
          |  # # # # # # # # # #  |   <- 4. charcoal
           \  # # # # # # # # #  /       (rang aur gandh kuch kam)
            \ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ /    <- 5. rui (cotton wool)
             \                 /          (upar ke daane neeche na jayein)
               `--.        .--'
                   |  |             <- gardan (neck, dhakkan hata hua)
                   v  v
              +-----------+
              |   ~ ~ ~   |         <- neeche saaf glass:
              |           |            saaf dikhta hai, par PEENE LAYAK NAHI
              +-----------+
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Layered bottle filter (upar se neeche): chhote kankad, moti ret, baareek ret, charcoal, rui, phir gardan aur neeche rakha glass. Nikla paani saaf dikhta hai par peene layak nahi hai.

**Kya dekhoge:**

- Pehle ganda bhoora paani aur neeche nikla paani kaafi **saaf** dikhega. Pehli kuch boondein shayad abhi bhi dhundhli hon: unhe wapas oopar daal kar dobara chhaan sakte ho.
- Layers ka kaam plant jaisa hai: kankad aur moti ret **screening/sedimentation** jaise bade tukde rokti hain, baareek ret **sand filtration** hai, charcoal thoda rang aur gandh kam karta hai, rui ret ke daano ko neeche aane se rokti hai.

**Ek aur chhota test (Chapter 2 wala Tyndall effect):** saaf paani ke glass ke ek taraf se **torch ki roshni** daalo. Agar beam ka raasta dikh raha hai, to paani mein ab bhi bahut baareek kan hain (Tyndall effect). Ye dikhata hai ki filter ne **sab kuch nahi** roka.

**Kya filter NAHI hataata:** germs, ghule hue namak, ghule hue chemicals. Isiliye **is paani ko kabhi nahi peena**, chahe wo kitna bhi saaf dikhe. Use sirf paudhe ko ya safai ke liye use kar sakte ho, aur activity khatam hone par filter ka saaman (ret, charcoal) phenk do ya dhoo kar bagiche mein daal do. Haath saabun se dho lo.

## Part G: "Kaun si vidhi kab?" (reasoning)

Plant aur ghar dono mein asli kaam hai: **pehle dekho ki paani mein kya samasya hai, phir uske hisaab se vidhi chuno.**

| Situation | Asli samasya | Sahi vidhi | Kyun |
|---|---|---|---|
| Baadh ke baad bhoora paani, pina hai | Mitti aur germs | Pehle kapde ya filter se chhaano ya baitha do, phir ubaalo | Chhanna mitti rokta hai, ubaalna germs maarta hai |
| Bilkul saaf kuan ka paani, par aas-paas sewage hai | Aankh se na dikhne wale germs | Ubaalo (ya treated, tested source) | Saaf dikhne se germs ka kuch pata nahi chalta |
| Paani mein ghula hua zyada namak | Ghule hue namak | RO ya distillation | Ghule namak filter se nahi nikalte (Chapter 2) |
| Pahaad trek par stream ka paani, bijli nahi | Germs, mitti | Chhaan kar rolling boil par kuch minute ubaalna, phir thanda karna | Ubaalna sabse seedha, bina bijli ka tareeka |
| Paani bahut halka bhoora, par germs-free chahiye | Baareek kan aur germs | Fitkari + sedimentation, phir ubaalo | Alum kanon ko bhaari banakar baithata hai, ubaalna germs maarta hai |

**Jaanch (galat sochne wali baat):** "Humne charcoal filter se paani saaf kar liya, ab ye surakshit hai." ❌ Nahi. Saaf dikhna surakshit hona nahi hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Saaf paani matlab surakshit paani"**

Galat. Germs aankhon se nahi dikhte. Isiliye plant mein chlorination aur lab jaanch hoti hai.

**Galti 2: "Bottle filter ka paani peene layak hota hai"**

Galat. Bottle filter sirf bade aur baareek kan rokta hai. Germs aur ghule chemicals nahi rokta. Is paani ko peena nahi hai.

**Galti 3: "Ubaalne se paani mein ghule hue sab chemical nikal jaate hain"**

Galat. Ubaalna germs maarta hai, par ghule namak, kathorta (hardness) ya kai chemicals nahi hataata (aur paani ubalte-ubalte thoda aur concentrated bhi ho sakta hai).

**Galti 4: "Fitkari paani ko saaf karti hai matlab germs bhi maar deti hai"**

Nahi. Fitkari sirf baareek kanon ko chipka kar baithati hai. Germs maarne ka kaam chlorination (ya ubaalna, UV) ka hai.

**Galti 5: "Chlorine ya bleach ghar mein bachche bhi mila sakte hain"**

Nahi. Chlorine wale chemicals khatarnak hain. Inhe sirf bade ya trained log sambhaalte hain, aur kabhi kisi dusre safai wale chemical ke saath nahi milaate.

**Galti 6: "Ek hi tareeka sab kuch hata deta hai"**

Nahi. Har tareeke ki limit hai: chhanna bade kan, ubaalna germs, RO ghuli cheezein (zyada). Isiliye plant ka flow kai step ka hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Water cycle:** evaporation, condensation (badal), barish: paani ka chakkar. Barish lagbhag saaf hoti hai, par bahte waqt mitti aur germs le leti hai.
- **City plant ke step:** screening, coagulation (fitkari), sedimentation, sand filtration, chlorination, storage aur supply.
- **Fitkari** baareek kanon ko chipka kar floc banati hai, jo bhaari hokar tank mein baith jaata hai. Ghar ki fitkari **KAl(SO₄)₂·12H₂O** ek hydrate hai (formula unit mass 474 u, lagbhag 45.57% paani).
- **Chlorine** germs maarta hai (kam matra mein, sirf trained log sambhaalte hain). Bachche chlorine wale chemicals kabhi nahi chhoote ya milaate.
- **Saaf paani surakshit ho, ye zaroori nahi:** germs dikhte nahi.
- **Waterborne diseases:** cholera, typhoid, dysentery, jaundice. Bachaav: surakshit paani, haath dhona, saaf khaana.
- **Ghar ke tareeke:** kapda (bade kan), candle filter, **ubaalna** (rolling boil par lagbhag 1 minute, adult), UV, RO (aage ki class mein). Har ki limit hai.
- **Bottle filter:** sirf activity, adult ke saath. Output saaf dikhta hai par **peene layak nahi**.
- Vidhi chuno **samasya** ke hisaab se: bade kan, baareek kan, germs ya ghuli cheezein.

## Practice Questions

1. Explain the water cycle in your own words using the words evaporation, condensation and rain. Why is rainwater cleaner than river water but still not recommended for drinking straight from the ground?

2. Arrange the steps of a city water treatment plant in the correct order: sand filtration, chlorination, screening, sedimentation, coagulation, storage and supply. Write one job for each step.

3. A student says: "I added alum, and now the water is clear, so it is safe." Explain what alum did and why the student is wrong.

4. A sedimentation tank holds 1500 m³ and water flows in at 250 m³ every hour. How long does the water stay in the tank? What happens to this time if the flow becomes 500 m³ every hour, and why does that matter?

5. A small plant uses 30 mg of alum for every litre of water. How much alum (in kg) is needed for 2,50,000 L of water?

6. A town plant uses 2 mg of chlorine per litre of water. How much chlorine (in kg) does it need for 8,00,000 L? Also say why students must never handle or mix chlorine chemicals at home.

7. A plant takes in 8,00,000 L of raw water in a day and supplies 7,52,000 L of treated water. How much water is lost, and what percentage is that?

8. A filter paper (or fine cloth) has pores of about 10 µm. A bacterium is about 0.5 µm. How many times smaller is the bacterium than the pore? Will it be stopped? What does this tell you about "clear water"?

9. An adult heats 3 L of water (3 kg) from 25 °C to 100 °C in a 1500 W kettle. How much heat is needed (c = 4200 J/kg °C), and how long will it take if there is no heat loss? What else must be done for safety after reaching 100 °C?

10. A student built a bottle filter with pebbles, coarse sand, fine sand, charcoal and cotton. In which order, from top to bottom, should these layers be placed? The water coming out looks clear. Can it be drunk? Give two reasons.

11. In a bottle-filter test, 450 mL of muddy water took 6 minutes to pass through. At the same rate, how long will 1.2 L take?

12. For each situation, pick the best method and give one reason: (a) a trekker has only a stream of cloudy water and a stove, (b) a house has hard water that leaves scale in the kettle, (c) a family's water looks muddy after floods.

13. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) Boiling for about a minute kills most germs in water.
    (b) A candle filter removes all dissolved salts.
    (c) Fitkari is a hydrate.
    (d) A bottle filter made at home gives drinking water.

### Answers

1. Dhoop se samundar, nadi ka paani **evaporate** hota hai, oopar thanda hokar **condense** hota hai (badal), aur **barish** ban kar gir jaata hai. Evaporation ek natural distillation hai (namak neeche rah jaata hai), isliye barish lagbhag saaf hoti hai. Par zameen par bahte waqt mitti, patte aur germs mil jaate hain, isliye nadi ka paani seedha peena surakshit nahi.

2. Sahi order: **screening** (bade tukde rokna) → **coagulation** (fitkari se floc banana) → **sedimentation** (floc tank mein baithna) → **sand filtration** (baareek kan ret mein rukna) → **chlorination** (germs maarna) → **storage and supply** (tanki aur pipeline se ghar tak).

3. Alum ne baareek kanon ko chipka kar bhaari floc banaye, jo neeche baith gaye, isliye paani saaf dikha. Par alum **germs nahi maarti**, aur germs aankhon se nahi dikhte, isliye student galat hai: saaf dikhna surakshit hona nahi hai. Aage chlorination ya ubaalna zaroori hai.

4. Time = 1500 ÷ 250 = **6 ghante**. Flow 500 m³/h ho to time = 1500 ÷ 500 = **3 ghante** (aadha). Kam time mein floc ko baithne ka kam mauka milta hai, isliye paani kam saaf nikalega.
   Jaanch: 250 × 6 = 1500 ✅ aur 500 × 3 = 1500 ✅

5. Alum = 30 mg/L × 250000 L = 7500000 mg. 7500000 ÷ 1000000 = **7.5 kg**.
   Jaanch: 7.5 kg = 7500000 mg, aur 7500000 ÷ 250000 = 30 mg/L ✅

6. Chlorine = 2 mg/L × 800000 L = 1600000 mg = 1600 g = **1.6 kg**. Chlorine wale chemicals zehreeli gas bana sakte hain (khaaskar kisi aur safai wale chemical ke saath milane par), aur skin, aankh, saans ko nuksaan karte hain, isliye bachche inhe kabhi na chhoote na milaayein; sirf trained adult sambhaalein.
   Jaanch: 1600 g ÷ 800000 L = 0.002 g/L = 2 mg/L ✅

7. Nuksaan = 800000 − 752000 = **48000 L**. Percent = 48000 ÷ 800000 × 100 = **6%**.
   Jaanch: 6% of 800000 = 0.06 × 800000 = 48000 ✅

8. Ratio = 10 ÷ 0.5 = **20 guna chhota**. Bacteria pore se aaraam se nikal jaayega, to filter paper ya kapda use **nahi rokega**. Isse yehi sabak milta hai ki paani saaf (kan-rahit) dikhne par bhi germs reh sakte hain.
   Jaanch: 0.5 × 20 = 10 ✅

9. Q = m c ΔT = 3 × 4200 × (100 − 25) = 3 × 4200 × 75 = **945000 J**. Time = 945000 ÷ 1500 = **630 s = 10.5 minute**. Suraksha ke liye 100 °C par pahunchne ke baad paani ko rolling boil par lagbhag **1 minute** rakho (pahaad par aam safety salaah lagbhag 3 minute ki hai), aur garm paani sirf adult hi sambhaale.
   Jaanch: 1500 × 630 = 945000 ✅ aur 630 ÷ 60 = 10.5 ✅

10. Oopar se neeche: **pebbles (chhote kankad), coarse sand, fine sand, charcoal, cotton**. Nikla paani **peene layak nahi**, kyunki (1) germs bahut chhote hain aur filter ke chhed se nikal jaate hain, (2) ghule hue chemicals aur namak filter se nahi nikalte; aur saaf dikhna surakshit hona nahi hai.

11. Rate = 450 ÷ 6 = 75 mL/min. 1.2 L = 1200 mL, to time = 1200 ÷ 75 = **16 minute**.
    Jaanch: 75 × 16 = 1200 ✅

12. (a) Pehle kapde se chhaan lo, phir **rolling boil par kuch minute ubaalo** (pahaad par aam safety salaah lagbhag 3 minute), kyunki ubaalna germs maarta hai aur bijli nahi chahiye. (b) Hard paani ke liye **washing soda, ion exchange ya distillation** (Topic 3): ubaalna sirf temporary hardness hatata hai aur scale banata hai. (c) Pehle **chhanna ya fitkari se baithana**, phir **ubaalna**: chhanna mitti hataata hai, ubaalna germs maarta hai.

13. (a) **True** ✅. Rolling boil par lagbhag 1 minute ubaalne se zyaadatar germs mar jaate hain. (b) **False** ❌. Candle filter ghule hue namak nahi rokta. (c) **True** ✅. Potash alum KAl(SO₄)₂·12H₂O mein 12 water of crystallisation hain. (d) **False** ❌. Home bottle filter ka paani peene layak nahi hota, usme germs aur ghule chemicals ho sakte hain.
