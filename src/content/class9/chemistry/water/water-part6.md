# Topic 6: Paani Bachao: Pollution, Conservation aur Chapter Ka Saar

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki paani ko **saaf aur surakshit** banane mein kitni mehnat lagti hai: screening, alum, sand, chlorine, phir pipeline. Ab ek seedha sawaal: jab paani banana itna mehnat ka kaam hai, to hum use **gandha** kyun karte hain aur **barbaad** kyun karte hain? Is topic mein pehle dekhenge ki paani **kaise pradooshit (polluted)** hota hai aur uska asar kya hota hai, phir **kaise bachayein**, aur sabse maze ki baat: bachat ko **ginti** mein badal kar dekhenge. Aakhir mein poore chapter ka saar.

## Society terrace ka tapakta nal

Ravivaar ki subah thi. Zaid apne papa ke dost Mr. Ghatge ke saath society ki terrace par patang dekhne gaya tha. Wahan kapde sukhane ke paas ek nal se **tap, tap, tap** paani tapak raha tha, neeche ek chhoti si geeli patti ban chuki thi.

"Kaka, ye nal kab se tapak raha hai?" Zaid ne poochha.

Mr. Ghatge ne muskurakar jug nikaal li. "Theek hai, scientist sahab. Pehle ye bata ki paani sirf nal se hi nahi bigadta: nadiyon, talaabon aur samundar mein bhi kuch hota hai. Phir hum ginti karenge."

## Part A: Paani kaise bigadta hai?

Pichhle topic mein hum ne paani saaf karna dekha. Ab ulta: **kaun si cheezein paani ko bigaadti hain?** Chhe bade sources dekhte hain.

| Source | Kya hota hai | Asar |
|---|---|---|
| **Sewage (gharelu gandha paani)** | Bina treat kiye naale se nadi mein jaata hai | Germs (cholera, typhoid ke) phailte hain; badbu; bimariyan |
| **Industrial waste (factory ka kachra)** | Kuch factories rasayan aur dhaatuon ke kan paani mein chhodti hain | Paani zehreela, machhliyan aur paudhe marte hain |
| **Fertiliser aur pesticide ka runoff** | Barish khet ki khaad aur dawai bahakar talaab ya nadi mein le jaati hai | Algal bloom, aur pesticide ka jeevon mein jama hona |
| **Plastic aur kachra** | Thaili, bottle, packet nadi aur samundar mein phenke jaate hain | Jaanwar nigal lete hain; naale band hote hain |
| **Tel (oil) ka rissaav** | Jahaaz ya garage se tel paani par aa jaata hai | Tel paani se halka hai (Topic 2: tel paani mein nahi ghulta), sathah par parat ban kar oxygen rok deta hai |
| **Garam paani (thermal pollution)** | Factory ya power plant thanda karne ke baad garam paani nadi mein chhodti hain | Garam paani mein gas kam ghulti hai (Topic 2), to machhli ko oxygen kam milti hai |

Dhyan rakho, ye sab **insaan ki galtiyon** se hota hai, nature se nahi. Aur yaad rakho: Topic 5 mein dekha tha ki **saaf dikhne wala paani surakshit hona zaroori nahi**. Ulta bhi sach hai: kai baar zehreela paani bhi bilkul saaf dikhta hai, isliye hum sirf aankh se bharosa nahi kar sakte.

> **Jaanch:** "Barish ka paani kabhi pradooshit nahi hota." ❌ Nahi. Girte hue bhi aur zameen par bahte hue bhi paani kachra, khaad aur gandagi apne saath bahakar nadi tak le jaata hai.

## Part B: Eutrophication: talaab ka hara hona

Sabse dilchasp pollution wo hai jo **khaad** se hota hai, jo khet ke liye achhi cheez hai. Khaad mein **nitrate** aur **phosphate** hote hain (paudhon ka khaana). Barish ke saath ye bahkar talaab mein pahunch jaate hain. Sewage mein bhi ye hote hain. Ab talaab ke chhote hare paudhe (algae) ko achanak bahut khaana mil jaata hai. Kya hota hai? Dekho.

```
  [1] Khet mein khaad (nitrate, phosphate)
                  |
                  v
  [2] Barish ke saath runoff talaab tak
                  |
                  v
  [3] Talaab mein nitrate aur phosphate badhe
                  |
                  v
  [4] Algae bahut tezi se phaile: algal bloom
      (sathah par hari parat, neeche roshni kam)
                  |
                  v
  [5] Algae mare aur sade: bacteria ne
      paani ki oxygen kharch ki
                  |
                  v
  [6] Dissolved oxygen ghat gayi:
      machhliyan dam ghutne se mari
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Eutrophication ka sequence: khaad ka nitrate aur phosphate runoff se talaab mein pahunchta hai, algae phailte hain (algal bloom), phir wo mar kar sadte hain aur unhe todne wale bacteria paani ki oxygen kharch kar dete hain, jisse machhliyan dam ghutne se marti hain.

Ye sab **plain shabdon mein** aise samjho. Algae ko khaana mila to wo **bahut tezi se badhe**. Jab wo mar gaye, to unhe sadane wale bacteria bahut zyada ho gaye, aur **wo paani mein ghuli oxygen khaane lage**. Dissolved oxygen kam hui to machhliyon ko saans nahi mili. Isi poori ghatna ka naam hai **eutrophication**. Ye shabd sirf naam hai: asli baat hai "khaad ka zyada khaana, algae ka phailna, oxygen ka khatam hona".

Iska chemistry se rishta bhi hai: Topic 2 mein dekha tha ki **gaas ka paani mein ghulna kam hota hai jab paani garam ho**, aur yahan oxygen khatam hone ki wajah alag hai (bacteria ka kharch), par nateeja wahi: **machhli ke liye oxygen kam**.

> **Jaanch:** "Algal bloom ka matlab hai talaab mein oxygen bahut badh jaana." ❌ Nahi. Bloom ke baad algae marte aur sadte hain, aur dissolved oxygen **ghat jaati hai**.

## Part C: Paani bachane ke tareeke (conservation)

Paani ko pradooshan se bachane ke saath-saath **kam vaparna** bhi zaroori hai. Yaad karo Topic 1: Earth ka lagbhag 97% paani khaara (namkeen) hai, aur sirf lagbhag 2.5% meetha, jisme se bhi zyadatar jama hua (baraf). Yaani insaan ke liye jo paani asaani se milta hai, wo bahut thoda hai. (Ye sab lagbhag ke aankde hain.)

Kuch seedhe, **gharelu** tareeke:

1. **Leak thik karwana.** Tapakta nal, toilet ka rissta hua cistern.
2. **Brush karte, shave karte ya bartan dhote waqt nal band rakhna.** Mug ya balti ka use.
3. **Nahaane mein shower ki jagah balti.**
4. **Reuse:** sabzi ya chawal dhoya hua paani paudhon mein dena. (Sirf saada paani: jisme sabun, tel ya detergent na ho.)
5. **Rainwater harvesting:** barish ka paani chhat se ikattha karna.
6. **Drip irrigation:** khet mein paudhe ki jad ke paas boond-boond paani, to paani sookhi mitti mein ya bhaap ban kar bekaar nahi jaata.
7. **Sewage treatment:** gandhe paani ko nadi mein chhodne se pehle saaf karna (screening, sedimentation, bacteria se saaf karna, aur chlorine jaise steps, Topic 5 jaise).
8. **Khaad aur dawai samajhdaari se:** zaroorat se zyada na daalna, taaki runoff mein kam jaaye.

Ab sabse zaroori baat: **hum ek-ek boond ko ginti mein badlenge.**

## Part D: Tapakta nal: naap aur ginti

Zaid ne nal ke neeche measuring jug rakhi.

```
        nal (tap)
          |
          o   <- ek boond har 1 second mein
          |
          v
     +---------+
     |         |   Measuring jug
     |  ~~~~~  |   (mL mein nishaan)
     |  paani  |
     +---------+
```

> **Figure 6.2** – Tapakta nal aur measuring jug: ek boond har ek second mein girti hai, aur jug mein jama paani se hum bata sakte hain ki ek din mein kitna bekaar jaata hai.

**Example 1: Ek din mein kitni boond?**
1 din = 24 ghante = 24 × 60 × 60 = **86400 seconds**.
Ek boond har second mein, to ek din mein **86400 boond**.

**Example 2: Ye kitne mL aur kitne litre hai?**
20 boond = 1 mL, to 86400 ÷ 20 = **4320 mL**.
Litre mein: 1 L = 1000 mL, to 4320 ÷ 1000 = **4.32 L**.

Yaani ek chhota sa tapakta nal **ek din mein 4.32 litre** paani bekaar kar deta hai. Ek balti lagbhag 20 L ki hoti hai, to ek din mein lagbhag 4.32 ÷ 20 = 0.22 balti, yaani balti ka lagbhag paanchva hissa.

**Example 3: Ek mahine (30 din) mein kitna?**
4.32 × 30 = **129.6 L**. Har balti 20 L maanein to 129.6 ÷ 20 = 6.48, yaani lagbhag saadhe chhe balti paani.

**Example 4: Ek saal mein?**
4.32 × 365 = **1576.8 L**, lagbhag 1.58 m³ (1 m³ = 1000 L).

> **Jaanch:** 4.32 L = 4320 mL, aur har mL mein 20 boond, to 4320 × 20 = 86400 boond ✅.

Zaid ne jug mein 1 minute tak paani gira kar bhi dekha: 60 boond = 60 ÷ 20 = **3 mL**. "Itna kam?" wo chonka. Mr. Ghatge ne kaha: "Haan, ek minute mein 3 mL kuch nahi lagta. Isiliye log kehte hain 'chhoti si boond hai'. Par **samay** se guna karo to badi ho jaati hai."

## Part E: Rainwater harvesting: chhat se pit tak

Ab ulti baat: bachat nahi, **jama karna**. Barish mein chhat par jo paani girta hai, wo naale mein bah jaata hai. Agar hum use pipe se ek **pit (gaddhe) ya tank** mein bhej dein to wo store ho jaata hai ya zameen mein utarkar bhoomigat paani (groundwater) badhata hai.

```
          barish ki boondein
         /   /    /    /   /
        v   v    v    v   v
     +---------------------------+
     |        CHHAT (roof)       |   area = 100 m²
     +--+------------------------+
        |    <- dhalaan, nali
        v
      [ pipe ]
        |
     +--v---+     chhanna (filter)
     |  ::  |     pehli barish ka paani
     +--+---+     alag nikaal dete hain
        |
        v
   +---------+
   |  ~~~~~  |   tank ya recharge pit
   | ~~~~~~~ |   (paani ikattha)
   +---------+
```

> **Figure 6.3** – Chhat aur rainwater harvesting pit: chhat par girta paani nali aur pipe se chhanne (filter) ke raste tank ya pit tak pahunchta hai.

Ab ek **kalpnik** udaharan: ek chhat ka kshetrafal **100 m²** hai, saal bhar mein **0.6 m** (60 cm) barish hoti hai, aur hum 80% paani store kar paate hain (baaki bhaap ya bah jaata hai). Ye sab approximate, made-up aankde hain.

**Example 5: Chhat par kitna paani girta hai?**
Aayatan = area × barish ki oonchai = 100 × 0.6 = **60 m³**.

**Example 6: 80% store ho to?**
60 × 80 / 100 = **48 m³**.

**Example 7: Litre mein?**
1 m³ = 1 m × 1 m × 1 m = 100 cm × 100 cm × 100 cm = 10,00,000 cm³ = 10,00,000 mL = **1000 L**.
To 48 m³ = 48 × 1000 = **48000 L**.

**Example 8: Ek parivaar ke liye kitne din ka paani?**
Maan lo parivaar mein **4 log** hain, har ek roz lagbhag **135 L** vaparta hai (sab kaam milakar, lagbhag aankda).
Roz ka vapar = 4 × 135 = **540 L**.
Din = 48000 ÷ 540 = **88.9 din** (lagbhag, yaani kareeb 3 mahine).

Yaani sirf ek chhat ka barish ka paani ek parivaar ko lagbhag 3 mahine chala sakta hai. Aur wahi barish baaki saal bhi bekaar jaati hai agar hum use roke nahi!

> **Jaanch:** 540 L × 88.9 ≈ 48,006 L, jo 48,000 L ke lagbhag barabar hai ✅. Aur agar 100% paani milta to 60,000 L, yaani 111 din se zyada: 80% ke baad bhi bahut hai.

**Note:** Harvest kiya paani peene ke liye tabhi surakshit hai jab Topic 5 jaisa purification kiya jaaye. Chhat par dhool, patte aur pakshiyon ki gandagi hoti hai. Isliye ye paani pehle **kapde-nahane-safai-paudhon** ke kaam aata hai.

## Part F: Rozmarra ke paani ka hisaab (reasoning)

Zaid ne ab apne ghar ka hisaab socha. Neeche ke sawaal sochkar dekho, jawab Hinglish mein samjhaye gaye hain.

**Example 9: Balti aur shower.**
Balti se nahaane mein lagbhag 20 L lagta hai. Shower 15 L har minute chale aur 8 minute nahaao to 15 × 8 = **120 L**. Bachat har din 120 − 20 = **100 L**. 30 din mein 100 × 30 = **3000 L = 3 m³**.

**Example 10: Ghar ka mahine ka vapar.**
4 log × 135 L × 30 din = **16200 L**, yaani **16.2 m³** ek mahine mein. Tapakte nal ke 129.6 L us vapar ka lagbhag 129.6 ÷ 16200 × 100 = **0.8%** hai. Ek nal akela itna: aise 5 nal hon to lagbhag 4%.

**Example 11: Kaun sa kaam pradooshan hai aur kaun sa bekaari?**
- Tapakta nal: **bekaari (waste)**, pradooshan nahi.
- Nadi mein plastic phenkna: **pradooshan**.
- Khet ki khaad ka bahna: **pradooshan** (eutrophication).
- Safai ke baad paani chhat ke paudhon ko dena: **conservation**.

**Example 12: Eutrophication rokne ka ek tareeka.**
Khaad zaroorat se zyada na daalna aur khet ke kinaare ghaas ki patti lagana, taaki runoff mein nitrate aur phosphate kam pahunche.

> **Jaanch:** 3000 L ÷ 1000 = 3 m³ ✅. Aur 4 × 135 × 30 = 540 × 30 = 16200 ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Ek boond se kya hoga"**
Ek boond har second mein 4.32 L roz, yaani 129.6 L har mahine. Chhoti cheez ko samay se gunna karo.

**Galti 2: "Saaf dikhne wala paani zehreela nahi ho sakta"**
Kuch pollutants aur germs dikhte hi nahi (Topic 5). Saaf dikhna surakshit hona nahi hai.

**Galti 3: "Khaad paudhon ke liye hai, to talaab ko nuksaan nahi karegi"**
Khaad ka nitrate aur phosphate talaab mein algae ko bahut badha deta hai, aur eutrophication se machhliyan marti hain.

**Galti 4: "Algae paudhe hain, to oxygen banate hain, to machhli ko fayda hi hoga"**
Din mein thodi oxygen banti hai, par bloom ke baad marne wale algae ko sadane mein bacteria bahut oxygen kharch kar dete hain. Bloom ke baad oxygen **ghatti** hai.

**Galti 5: "1 m³ = 100 L"**
1 m³ = 1000 L. (1 m³ = 10,00,000 cm³ = 10,00,000 mL = 1000 L.)

**Galti 6: "Rainwater peene ke liye seedha surakshit hai"**
Chhat se aaya paani pehle saaf aur purify karna padta hai (Topic 5). Seedha mat piyo.

**Galti 7: "Sabun-detergent wala dhulai ka paani paudhon ko dena theek hai"**
Sirf saada paani (sabzi, chawal dhoya hua) reuse karo. Sabun, detergent ya tel wala paani paudhon ke liye theek nahi.

## Quick Recap

- Paani ko bigaadne wale sources: sewage, industrial waste, khaad aur pesticide runoff, plastic, tel, aur factory ka garam paani.
- Khaad ka nitrate aur phosphate talaab mein algae badhate hain (algal bloom). Marne wale algae ko bacteria sadate hain aur dissolved oxygen kharch karte hain: ye **eutrophication** hai, jisse machhliyan marti hain.
- Bachane ke tareeke: leak thik karna, nal band rakhna, balti ka vapar, saade paani ka reuse, rainwater harvesting, drip irrigation, sewage treatment.
- 1 boond har second = 86400 boond roz = 4320 mL = **4.32 L roz** (20 boond = 1 mL).
- 1 m³ = 1000 L.
- Rainwater harvesting: area × barish × fraction. Udaharan: 100 × 0.6 × 0.8 = 48 m³ = 48000 L, 4 log × 135 L = 540 L roz, to lagbhag **88.9 din**.
- Harvest kiya paani peene se pehle purify karna zaroori hai.

    (a) 1 m³ is equal to 100 L.
    (b) Water that looks clear cannot be polluted.
    (c) Too much fertiliser in a pond can reduce the oxygen available to fish.
    (d) Rainwater collected from a roof can be drunk directly.

10. A garden tap is fixed so that a leak of 1.5 mL every second is stopped. How many litres are saved in one hour, and in a 24-hour day?

### Answers

1. Har 2 second mein 1 boond, to ek din (86400 seconds) mein 86400 ÷ 2 = **43200 boond**. 20 boond = 1 mL, to 43200 ÷ 20 = 2160 mL = **2.16 L** roz. 30 din mein 2.16 × 30 = **64.8 L**. (Pichhle udaharan se aadha, kyunki boond aadhi tezi se girti hai ✅.)

3. (a) 3.5 × 1000 = **3500 L**. (b) 250 ÷ 1000 = **0.25 m³**. (c) 6000 ÷ 1000 = **6 L**.

4. Sahi kram: **(b) → (d) → (e) → (c) → (a)**. Pehle khaad ka runoff talaab mein aata hai, algae tezi se badhte hain, phir marne wale algae ko bacteria sadate hain, isse dissolved oxygen ghatti hai, aur aakhir mein machhliyan marti hain.

5. Tel paani mein nahi ghulta (Topic 2) aur halka hone ke kaaran sathah par parat bana leta hai. **Pehla karan:** ye parat hawa ki oxygen ko paani mein ghulne se rokti hai. **Doosra karan:** tel pakshiyon ke par aur jaanvaron ke sharir par chipak kar unhe nuksaan pahunchata hai, aur sathah ki parat roshni ko bhi paani ke andar jaane se rokti hai.

6. Topic 2 mein dekha tha ki **garam paani mein gaas kam ghulti hai**. Isliye garam paani mein dissolved oxygen kam hoti hai, aur machhli ko saans lene ke liye kam oxygen milti hai.

7. (a) **waste of water** (nal chalu chhodna). (b) **pollution** (plastic). (c) **conservation** (saade paani ka reuse). (d) **pollution** (pesticide runoff). (e) **waste of water** (tapakta nal).

8. Bucket se 15 L. Shower se 12 × 6 = 72 L. Bachat har din 72 − 15 = **57 L** (ek vyakti). Parivaar ke 3 logon ke liye 57 × 3 = 171 L roz. 30 din mein 171 × 30 = 5130 L = 5130 ÷ 1000 = **5.13 m³** ✅.

9. (a) **False** ❌. 1 m³ = **1000 L**. (b) **False** ❌. Kai pollutants aur germs dikhte nahi, saaf paani bhi pradooshit ho sakta hai. (c) **True** ✅. Nitrate aur phosphate algae badhate hain, aur sadne par oxygen ghatti hai. (d) **False** ❌. Chhat ke paani mein dhool aur gandagi hoti hai, use pehle purify karna padta hai (Topic 5).

10. 1.5 mL har second. Ek ghante mein 3600 seconds, to 1.5 × 3600 = 5400 mL = **5.4 L**. Ek din (24 ghante) mein 5.4 × 24 = **129.6 L**. Ye wahi 129.6 hai jo hamare Zaid ke nal ka ek mahine mein bekaar jaata tha: sanyog se barabar ✅.

## Chapter Ka Saar: Water

Ek nazar mein poore chapter ka safar: paani kya hai, wo kya ghulata hai, kathor aur mridu kyun, crystals mein kaise chhipa hai, kaise saaf hota hai, aur kaise bachana hai.

| Topic | Kya seekha | Ek line |
|---|---|---|
| Topic 1 | Paani kya hai: banawat aur gun | Paani H₂O hai (hydrogen aur oxygen ka covalent compound, mass ratio 1 : 8), aur shuddh paani rangheen, gandhheen, swaadheen hota hai, 0 °C par jamta aur 100 °C par ubalta hai. |
| Topic 2 | Paani ek solvent | Paani bahut si cheezein ghulata hai par sab nahi (tel nahi); solubility ka matlab 100 g paani mein ghulne wale gram, aur ye temperature par nirbhar hai. |
| Topic 3 | Kathor aur mridu paani | Calcium aur magnesium ke namak se paani kathor banta hai: temporary hardness ubaalne se jaati hai, permanent hardness washing soda ya ion-exchange se. |
| Topic 4 | Hydrates, efflorescence aur deliquescence | Crystals mein paani ki nishchit ginti (water of crystallisation) hoti hai, kuch namak hawa se paani sokh kar ghul jaate hain, kuch paani chhod kar chura ban jaate hain. |
| Topic 5 | Purification | Shehar ka plant screening, alum, sedimentation, sand filtration aur chlorine se paani saaf karta hai; saaf dikhna surakshit hona nahi hai. |
