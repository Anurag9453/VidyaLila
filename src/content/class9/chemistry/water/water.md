## Pyau ka steel wala glass

Garmi ki dopahar thi. Mr. Rege apne bhanje Atharva ke saath bazaar se laut rahe the. Dhoop aisi ki sadak chamak rahi thi. Chauraahe par ek **pyau** (garmi mein lagne wala muft paani ka stall) dikha. ## Part A: Paani ki banawat

### Do tatva, ek compound

Paani ka formula **H₂O** hai. Iska matlab: paani ke **ek anu** mein **2 hydrogen atoms** aur **1 oxygen atom** jude hote hain. Ye jod **covalent bond** ka hai (Chapter 6): har hydrogen apna ek electron oxygen ke saath baantta hai, aur teeno ke shell pooray ho jaate hain. Hydrogen ka shell 2 se bharta hai aur oxygen ka 8 se.

```
              O
             / \
            /   \
           /     \
          H       H

   O - H bond: electron ka ek joda baanta hua (covalent)
   H - O - H : 2 hydrogen + 1 oxygen = kul 3 atoms
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Paani ka ek anu (H₂O): ek oxygen atom aur do hydrogen atoms, har H ka O ke saath ek covalent bond. Anu seedhi line nahi, mudi hui "V" shape ka hai (is shape ki detail aage ki class mein).

Ek aur zaroori baat: paani **compound** hai, mixture nahi. Agar hydrogen aur oxygen gas ko sirf ek bartan mein milakar rakh dein, to wo mixture hota hai, aur use aap kisi bhi hisaab mein mila sakte ho. Par jab wo rasayanik roop se jud kar paani banate hain, to hisaab **hamesha fixed** hota hai aur gun bilkul naye ho jaate hain. Hydrogen ek jalne wali gas hai, oxygen jalne mein madad karti hai, aur paani jo **dono ki aag bujhata hai**. Ye Chapter 4 ke Law of Constant Proportions ka ek jeeta-jaagta example hai.

### Fixed hisaab: 1 : 8

Chapter 4 mein hum ek anu ka dravyaman nikaalna seekh chuke hain. H ka dravyaman 1 u aur O ka 16 u lete hue:

- H₂O ka dravyaman = 2 × 1 + 16 = **18 u**
- Hydrogen ka hissa = 2 u, oxygen ka hissa = 16 u
- Mass ratio **H : O = 2 : 16 = 1 : 8**

```
    Paani ka 18 g (har dibba = 2 g)

    [ H ][ O ][ O ][ O ][ O ][ O ][ O ][ O ][ O ]
      ^    ^------- 8 dibbe oxygen ke --------^
      1 dibba hydrogen ka

    Kul 9 dibbe:  1 dibba H (2 g)  +  8 dibbe O (16 g)
    Hydrogen = 1 / 9 = 11.11 %     Oxygen = 8 / 9 = 88.89 %
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Paani ka mass 9 barabar hisson mein: 1 hissa hydrogen aur 8 hisse oxygen. Isliye paani ka 11.11% mass hydrogen ka aur 88.89% oxygen ka hota hai, chahe paani ek boond ho ya poori nadi.

Percentage composition recall (Chapter 4 ke tareeke se):

- Hydrogen % = (2 ÷ 18) × 100 = **11.11 %**
- Oxygen % = (16 ÷ 18) × 100 = **88.89 %**
- Jodkar: 11.11 + 88.89 = 100 ✅

Pehli nazar mein ye ajeeb lagta hai: paani ka lagbhag **9 mein se 8 hissa** oxygen ka mass hai, par hamne dekha ki **anu mein 2 H aur sirf 1 O** hai. Dono baatein sahi hain, kyunki **atoms ki ginti** ek cheez hai aur **dravyaman** doosri: ek oxygen atom ek hydrogen atom se 16 guna bhaari hai.

> **Jaanch:** Paani ka 1 : 8 ratio 2 : 16 hi hai. ✅ Kyunki 2 hydrogen atoms ka dravyaman 2 u aur 1 oxygen atom ka 16 u hai.

### Pakki jaankari: electrolysis se saboot

Chapter 7 mein humne paani ko bijli ke zariye todne ka prayog dekha tha (**electrolysis**): 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂. Isse do baatein pakki hoti hain:

1. Paani se **sirf hydrogen aur oxygen** nikalti hain, kuch aur nahi. Yani paani inhi do tatvon ka bana hai.
2. Dono gas ki **volume ka hisaab 2 : 1** milta hai: jitni hydrogen, usse aadhi oxygen.

Volume 2 : 1 hone par bhi **mass 1 : 8** kaise hai? Equation dekho: 2 anu H₂ ka dravyaman = 2 × 2 = 4 u, aur 1 anu O₂ ka dravyaman = 32 u. Ratio = 4 : 32 = **1 : 8** ✅. Yani hydrogen ke anu bahut halke hain, isliye zyada kan hone par bhi uska mass kam hai.

Ye prayog **sirf teacher ke saath school lab mein** hota hai, ghar par bijli aur paani saath nahi laate.

## Part B: Paani ke gun

Paani ko dekhne ke baad ab uski kundli (profile) dekhte hain. Sabse pehle ek table.

| Gun | Paani mein kya | Ek line |
|---|---|---|
| **Rang** | Rangheen (shuddh paani) | Thoda gehra ho to neeli jhalak, par chhote glass mein dikhta nahi |
| **Gandh** | Gandhheen | Nal ke paani ki gandh chlorine ya mitti ki hoti hai, paani ki nahi |
| **Swaad** | Swaadheen (shuddh paani) | Meetha ya khaara swaad ghule hue namak ya khanij ka hai |
| **Pighalne ka taapman** | 0 °C | Barf ka paani banna |
| **Ubalne ka taapman** | 100 °C | Normal dabaav par (samudra tal ke paas) |
| **Ghanatva (density)** | 1 g per mL, yaani 1000 kg per m³, lagbhag 4 °C par | Isi taapman par paani sabse bhaari (Physics ka yaad) |
| **Specific heat** | Bahut zyada: 4200 J per kg per °C | Isliye paani der se garm aur der se thanda hota hai (Physics ka yaad) |
| **Prakriti** | Neutral: na acidic, na basic | Shuddh paani litmus ka rang nahi badalta |
| **Bijli ki chaalkata** | Shuddh paani bijli bahut kam chalata hai | Nal ka paani thodi zyada bijli chalata hai, kyunki usme namak aur khanij ghule hote hain (kaise, ye agle topic mein) |

Physics ke chapter "Heat and Temperature" mein humne ye do baatein poori tarah dekhi hain: paani 4 °C par sabse bhaari hota hai, aur uska specific heat bahut zyada hai ([Topic 2](../../physics/heat-and-temperature/heat-and-temperature-part2.md) aur [Topic 3](../../physics/heat-and-temperature/heat-and-temperature-part3.md)). Yahan hum unhe dobara nahi padhaenge, bas ek line mein yaad rakhenge: **4 °C par sabse zyada ghana, aur garm karne mein bahut zyada garmi maangta hai.**

Do baatein jo nayi hain:

- **"Shuddh" ka matlab:** ye saare gun **bilkul shuddh paani** ke hain. Pyau ya nal ka paani shuddh nahi hota, usme thoda namak, mitti aur gas ghuli hoti hai (isi ki kahani agle topic mein).
- **Neutral:** shuddh paani na acidic hai na basic. Acid aur base ki baat aage ki class mein aayegi, yahan itna hi: neutral matlab beech ka.

### Paani ka mass aur volume

Ghanatva 1 g per mL hai, to **1 mL paani ka mass 1 g** hota hai (lagbhag 4 °C par). Isse bahut aasaan hisaab nikalte hain:

- 1 L = 1000 mL, to **1 L paani = 1000 g = 1 kg** ✅
- Isliye 5 L ki balti ka paani **5 kg** ka hota hai.

> **Jaanch:** Pyau ka matka 20 L ka hai aur uska paani bharkar uthana mushkil kyun hai? ✅ Kyunki 20 L paani 20 kg ka hota hai, jitna ek chhote bachche ka bag.

## Part C: Paani itna zaroori kyun hai

Paani sirf peene ke liye nahi. Ye hamare aas-paas har cheez mein hai.

- **Dharti ka lagbhag 71% hissa paani se dhaka hai**, aur baaki 29% zameen hai: 71 + 29 = 100 ✅.
- **Hamare sharir mein bhi** paani ki badi matra hai, aur khoon, paseena aur saans ka kaam paani se hi chalta hai (Chapter 7 mein respiration ke equation mein bhi paani banta hai).
- **Kheti, bijli, factory, safai, khana banana:** sab paani par tike hain.
- **Paani nahi, to jeevan nahi**, aur dusre grah par jeevan dhoondhte hue sabse pehle paani hi dhoondha jaata hai.

Itna sab hone par bhi ek chaunkane wali baat: Dharti par paani bahut hai, par **peene layak paani bahut kam**.

```
    Dharti ke paani ko 40 barabar dibbon mein baanto (1 dibba = 2.5 %)

    [K][K][K][K][K][K][K][K][K][K]
    [K][K][K][K][K][K][K][K][K][K]
    [K][K][K][K][K][K][K][K][K][K]
    [K][K][K][K][K][K][K][K][K][M]

    K = khaara paani (samundar)    39 dibbe = lagbhag 97 %
    M = meetha paani               1 dibba  = lagbhag 2.5 %
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Dharti ke paani ka hissa (lagbhag, anumaanit): 40 dibbon mein se sirf ek dibba (2.5%) meetha paani hai; baaki saara samundar ka khaara paani hai. (Alag-alag sroton mein aankde thode alag mil sakte hain; yahan bada hissa samajhna hi zaroori hai.)

Lagbhag **97%** paani samundar ka khaara paani hai, jo seedha peene ya kheti ke kaam nahi aata. Lagbhag **2.5%** meetha paani hai, aur uska bhi **zyadatar hissa (lagbhag 70%) barf aur glacier mein jamma hua hai**. Matlab nadi, jheel aur zameen ke neeche ke paani ka hissa dharti ke kul paani ka bahut chhota sa tukda hai. Is hisaab ka ek namoona Part E mein hai.

> **Jaanch:** "Dharti par 71% paani hai, isliye hamein paani ki kami kabhi nahi hogi." ❌ Galat. Zyadatar paani khaara ya barf mein bandh hai; kaam aane layak meetha paani bahut kam hai.

## Part D: Paani ki pehchaan: test for water

Agar koi taral dikhne mein paani jaisa ho, to kaise pakka karein ki ye paani hai? Dikhne se kaam nahi chalta, chemistry ka ek test hai.

**Anhydrous copper sulphate** (nirjal, safed, sukha copper sulphate) paani ke sampark mein aate hi **neela** ho jaata hai. Safed se neele ka badlaav paani ki maujoodgi ka saboot hai.

- Safed (anhydrous) CuSO₄ + paani ke kuch boond → **neela** CuSO₄·5H₂O.

**Safety:** ye **sirf dekhne ke liye (teacher demo)** hai. Copper sulphate nigalne par nuksaan karta hai, isliye bachche ise na chhuen, na munh ke paas laayen, aur kisi ko na chakhein.

Ye test ye batata hai ki "paani maujood hai", par ye nahi batata ki wo paani **shuddh** hai ya nahi. Ye bhi dhyan rahe ki ye safed copper sulphate "paani" ko pakadta hai, jaise doodh mein ya fal ke ras mein bhi paani hota hai aur safed powder neela ho jaata hai. Is hydrate (paani se bhare crystal) ka poora vishay Topic 4 mein aayega.

## Part E: Solved examples

**Example 1: 36 g paani mein kitna hydrogen aur kitna oxygen hai?**
Ratio 1 : 8, kul 9 hisse. 1 hissa = 36 ÷ 9 = 4 g. Hydrogen = 1 × 4 = **4 g**, oxygen = 8 × 4 = **32 g**.
Jaanch: 4 + 32 = 36 ✅ aur 32 ÷ 4 = 8 ✅.

**Example 2: H₂O ka dravyaman aur % composition?**
Dravyaman = 2 × 1 + 16 = **18 u**. H % = 2 ÷ 18 × 100 = **11.11%**, O % = 16 ÷ 18 × 100 = **88.89%**. Jodkar 100 ✅.

**Example 3: 5 g hydrogen se kitna paani banega, aur kitni oxygen lagegi?**
Hydrogen ka 1 hissa 5 g hai, to oxygen 8 hisse = 8 × 5 = **40 g**, paani = 5 + 40 = **45 g**.
Jaanch: 45 ÷ 9 = 5 ✅ (hydrogen) aur 45 ÷ 9 × 8 = 40 ✅.

**Example 4: Electrolysis mein 20 mL hydrogen mili. Oxygen kitni mili hogi?**
Volume ratio H₂ : O₂ = 2 : 1. 20 mL H₂ ke liye 20 ÷ 2 = **10 mL O₂**. (Ye volume ka hisaab hai, mass ka nahi: mass mein oxygen bahut zyada hoti.)

**Example 5: 1000 L dharti ke paani mein kitna meetha aur kitna khaara paani hoga? Kitna barf mein bandh?**
Khaara = 97% of 1000 = **970 L**. Meetha = 2.5% of 1000 = **25 L**. Meethe ka lagbhag 70% barf mein: 70% of 25 = **17.5 L** barf mein, to baaki 25 − 17.5 = **7.5 L** nadi, jheel aur zameen ke neeche ke paani mein (sab anumaanit). Kul 1000 L mein sirf 7.5 L bacha: yahi "kaam aane layak" paani hai.

**Example 6: 20 L ka matka bhara paani. Uska mass?**
1 L = 1 kg, to **20 kg**.

**Example 7: Ek aadmi kehta hai "paani aur hydrogen-oxygen ka mixture ek hi cheez hai." Sahi ya galat?**
Galat. Mixture mein hydrogen aur oxygen jalne wali alag gas hain aur hisaab koi bhi ho sakta hai. Paani compound hai, rasayanik roop se judkar bana, hisaab **fixed 1 : 8** (mass mein), aur uske gun alag hain: paani aag bujhata hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Paani mein hydrogen aur oxygen barabar mass mein hain"**
Nahi. Mass ka ratio **1 : 8** hai. Ginti (atoms) ka ratio 2 : 1 hai.

**Galti 2: "Paani H₂O hai, to usme 2 gram H aur 1 gram O hoga"**
Subscript atoms ki ginti batata hai, gram nahi. 2 H atoms ka mass 2 u aur 1 O atom ka mass 16 u hota hai.

**Galti 3: "Electrolysis mein hydrogen aur oxygen ka volume ratio 1 : 8 aata hai"**
Nahi. Volume ratio **2 : 1** hai; **1 : 8** mass ka ratio hai.

**Galti 4: "Shuddh paani ka swaad meetha hota hai"**
Shuddh paani swaadheen hai. Jo "meethapan" ya "namkeen" swaad aata hai, wo ghule hue khanij aur namak ka hai.

**Galti 5: "Safed copper sulphate ka paani se neela hona dikhata hai ki paani peene layak hai"**
Ye sirf batata hai ki paani **maujood** hai, safe ya shuddh hone ki guarantee nahi.

**Galti 6: "Dharti par 71% paani hai, to peene ka paani bahut hai"**
Nahi: lagbhag 97% khaara hai aur meethe paani ka bhi zyadatar hissa barf mein hai.

## Quick Recap

- Paani **H₂O** hai: hydrogen aur oxygen ka **compound**, covalent bond se bana; ek anu mein 2 H aur 1 O, mudi hui "V" shape.
- Dravyaman 18 u; mass ratio **H : O = 1 : 8**; **11.11% H aur 88.89% O**.
- Electrolysis (2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂) saboot hai ki paani sirf H aur O ka bana hai; gas volume ka ratio 2 : 1, mass ka 1 : 8.
- Shuddh paani rangheen, gandhheen, swaadheen, **neutral**; 0 °C par pighalta, 100 °C par ubalta hai; ghanatva 1 g per mL (4 °C par sabse zyada); specific heat bahut zyada (Physics ke chapter se).
- 1 L paani = 1 kg.
- Dharti ka lagbhag 71% hissa paani hai, par lagbhag 97% khaara; meethe 2.5% ka bhi zyadatar hissa barf mein hai.
- **Test for water:** safed anhydrous copper sulphate paani se neela ho jaata hai (sirf teacher demo).

## Practice Questions

13. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) Water contains hydrogen and oxygen in the mass ratio 2 : 1.
    (b) Pure water is neutral.
    (c) 1 mL of water has a mass of about 1 g near 4 °C.
    (d) Most of the water on Earth is fresh water.

### Answers

1. Formula **H₂O**: ek anu mein **2 hydrogen atoms aur 1 oxygen atom**. In atoms ko **covalent bond** jodta hai (electron baantne se).

2. Dravyaman = 2 × 1 + 16 = **18 u**. H % = 2 ÷ 18 × 100 = **11.11%**. O % = 16 ÷ 18 × 100 = **88.89%**. Jod: 11.11 + 88.89 = 100 ✅.

3. (i) Mixture mein hydrogen aur oxygen kisi bhi hisaab mein ho sakti hain, par paani mein hisaab **fixed 1 : 8** (mass) hai. (ii) Mixture mein dono gas apne gun rakhti hain (hydrogen jalti hai, oxygen jalne mein madad karti hai), par paani ka gun bilkul alag hai, wo aag bujhata hai. Isliye paani compound hai.

4. Kul 9 hisse, 1 hissa = 72 ÷ 9 = 8 g. Hydrogen = **8 g**, oxygen = 8 × 8 = **64 g**. Jaanch: 8 + 64 = 72 ✅.

5. Oxygen 8 hisse = 24 g, to 1 hissa = 24 ÷ 8 = 3 g. Hydrogen = **3 g**, paani = 3 + 24 = **27 g**. Jaanch: 27 ÷ 9 = 3 ✅ aur 3 × 8 = 24 ✅.

6. Oxygen = 8 × 7 = **56 g**. Paani = 7 + 56 = **63 g**. Jaanch: 63 ÷ 9 = 7 ✅.

7. Volume ratio H₂ : O₂ = 2 : 1, to hydrogen = 2 × 36 = **72 mL**. Volume 2 : 1 hai kyunki equation 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂ mein 2 H₂ aur 1 O₂ ke anu nikalte hain; par mass mein 2 H₂ ka dravyaman 4 u aur 1 O₂ ka 32 u hai, ratio 4 : 32 = 1 : 8 ✅. Kyunki hydrogen ke anu bahut halke hain.

8. Shuddh paani: rangheen, gandhheen, swaadheen; 0 °C par pighalta hai, 100 °C par ubalta hai; ghanatva 1 g per mL (1000 kg per m³) lagbhag 4 °C par; neutral hai. Physics chapter mein do baatein detail mein thi: **4 °C par sabse zyada ghanatva** (anomalous expansion) aur **bahut zyada specific heat (4200 J per kg per °C)**.

9. 1 mL = 1 g, to **250 mL = 250 g**. 1.5 L = 1500 mL = **1500 g = 1.5 kg**. 50 L paani = 50 kg, kaafi bhaari hai, kyunki paani ka ghanatva 1 g per mL hai aur volume bada hai.

10. Dharti ka 71% hissa paani se dhaka hai, par usme se lagbhag 97% khaara samundar ka paani hai jo peene ya kheti ke kaam nahi aata. Meetha paani sirf lagbhag 2.5% hai, aur uska bhi zyadatar hissa barf aur glacier mein jama hai. Isliye kaam aane layak paani bahut kam hai, aur kami hoti hai.

11. Khaara = 97% of 4000 = **3880 L**. Meetha = 2.5% of 4000 = **100 L**. Barf mein = 70% of 100 = **70 L**, baaki **30 L** nadi, jheel aur zameen ke neeche. Jaanch: 0.97 × 4000 = 3880 ✅, 0.025 × 4000 = 100 ✅.

12. Safed **anhydrous copper sulphate** daalne par agar wo **neela** ho jaaye to paani maujood hai. Students ko ye khud nahi karna chahiye, kyunki copper sulphate nigalne par nuksaan karta hai; ise **sirf teacher demo** ke roop mein dekhna chahiye. Ye test sirf paani ki maujoodgi dikhata hai, uski shuddhta ya safety nahi: paani mein kitanu ho sakte hain jo dikhte nahi.

13. (a) **False** ❌. Mass ratio H : O = **1 : 8** (2 : 1 atoms ki ginti ka ratio hai). (b) **True** ✅. (c) **True** ✅. (d) **False** ❌. Lagbhag 97% paani khaara hai; meetha sirf lagbhag 2.5%.

Shabaash! Tumne ab jaan liya ki paani **H₂O** hai, 1 : 8 ke fixed hisaab se bana compound, aur uske gun, aur ye bhi ki dharti ka zyadatar paani hamare kaam nahi aata.

**Aage kya?** Pyau ke matke ka paani bhale hi saaf dikhe, usme namak aur khanij ghule hote hain jo humein dikhte nahi. Agle topic mein hum dekhenge ki **paani ek solvent** kaise hai: dal mein namak ghul kar gayab kyun ho jaata hai par tel tairta kyun rehta hai, aur ek glass paani mein kitna kuch ghul sakta hai.
