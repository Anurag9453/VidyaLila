# Topic 2: Degree Measure of an Arc, Minor and Major Arcs, aur Alternate Segments

Topic 1 mein humne Gauri bakri ke khoonte (centre) aur 7 m ki rassi (radius) se circle se dosti ki thi. Centre, radius, diameter, chord jaise shabd seekhe, arc ka basic idea dekha, segment aur sector ki ek jhalak li, aur ye bhi samjha ki koi point circle ke andar hai, us par hai, ya bahar.

Ab hum **arc** ko zoom karke dekhenge. Ek arc kitna "bada" hai, ise kaise naapte hain? Kaunsa arc chhota (minor) hai, kaunsa bada (major)? Aur jab ek chord circle ko do hisson mein kaatti hai, to wo do hisse ek doosre ke liye kya kehlate hain?

## Mele ka giant wheel

Pinky apne papa ke saath mele mein gayi. Wahan ek bada **giant wheel** tha, jisme **12 cabin** barabar doori par lage the.

Pinky cabin number 1 mein baithi. Wheel ghoomna shuru hua. Pinky ne papa se poocha:

"Papa, jab main cabin 4 ki jagah tak pahunchungi, to maine wheel ka kitna hissa ghoom liya?"

Papa ne samjhaya:

"Pura wheel ek chakkar mein **360°** ghoomta hai. 12 cabin barabar doori par hain, to do paas wale cabins ke beech ka angle hai 360° ÷ 12 = **30°**. Cabin 1 se cabin 4 tak 3 gap hain, to tum **3 × 30° = 90°** ghoomogi. Yaani wheel ka ek chauthai hissa."

Pinky ne phir poocha, "Aur cabin 7 tak?"

"6 gap, yaani 6 × 30° = **180°**. Ye theek aadha chakkar hai. Tab tum bilkul upar wale hisse mein hogi, jahan se chadhi thi uske theek saamne."

Pinky ne jo raasta circle ke kinare par tay kiya, wo ek **arc** hai. Aur papa ne us arc ko **degree** mein naapa. Is topic mein hum yahi seekhenge.

## Part A: Arc ko detail mein samjho

Topic 1 mein dekha tha ki circle ke kinare ka koi bhi tukda **arc** hota hai. Ab ek mazedaar baat dekho.

Agar circle par do points **A** aur **B** lo, to wo circle ko **do arcs** mein baant dete hain:

```
            A
         .  *  .
       .    |    .
      .     |     .
     .      O------* B
      .           .
       .         .
         .  *  .
            C
```

- Ek chhota raasta: A se seedha B tak (upar-right wala chhota hissa).
- Ek bada raasta: A se ghoom kar C se hote hue B tak.

Dono hi A se B tak jaate hain, par lambai bahut alag hai.

### Naam kaise likhte hain?

- **Chhota arc (minor arc):** sirf do letters se likhte hain: **arc AB** (ya AB ke upar ek chhota sa curve ka nishaan, ⌒AB).
- **Bada arc (major arc):** do letters se confusion ho jayega, isliye **teesra letter** beech mein daalte hain, jo bade raaste par pada ho: **arc ACB**.

Teesra letter batata hai ki "ghoom kar C se hote hue jao". Isse saaf pata chalta hai ki hum bade arc ki baat kar rahe hain.

> Yaad rakho: **2 letters = minor arc, 3 letters = major arc.**

---

## Part B: Central angle

Arc ke dono siron (A aur B) ko centre **O** se jod do. Do radius ban gaye: **OA** aur **OB**. Inke beech centre par jo angle bana, wo hai **central angle ∠AOB**.

```
              A
             *
            /
           /
          /  ) ∠AOB
         O----------* B
```

Ise kehte hain: "**arc AB centre par ∠AOB subtend karta hai**". "Subtend" ka matlab bas itna hai ki arc ke dono sire centre par ek angle banate hain.

Giant wheel mein, wheel ka centre O tha. Cabin 1 se cabin 4 tak jaane par centre par 90° ka angle bana. Ye central angle hai.

---

## Part C: Arc ka degree measure

Ab sabse zaroori baat:

> **Kisi arc ka degree measure = uske central angle ka measure.**

Agar ∠AOB = 60° hai, to **minor arc AB = 60°**.

Dhyan do: arc ka degree measure uski **lambai (cm mein)** nahi hai. Ye batata hai ki arc poore circle ka **kitna hissa** hai. Poora circle 360° hota hai.

| Central angle | Arc ka measure | Circle ka kitna hissa |
|---|---|---|
| 90° | 90° | ¼ |
| 180° | 180° | ½ |
| 120° | 120° | ⅓ |
| 60° | 60° | ⅙ |
| 360° | 360° | Poora circle |

Ek chhota aur bada circle socho. Dono mein 90° ka arc **chauthai** hissa hi hoga, bhale bade circle mein wo arc **lamba** dikhe. Degree measure "hissa" batata hai, lambai nahi.

## Part D: Minor arc, Semicircle aur Major arc

Arc ke degree measure ke hisaab se teen naam hain:

| Arc | Degree measure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Minor arc** | 180° se **kam** | 40°, 90°, 150° |
| **Semicircle** | **Theek 180°** | Diameter se bana arc |
| **Major arc** | 180° se **zyada** (360° se kam) | 200°, 270°, 330° |

### Major arc = 360° − Minor arc

Do points A aur B se bane dono arcs mil kar **poora circle** banate hain. Isliye:

**Minor arc + Major arc = 360°**

**Major arc ACB = 360° − minor arc AB**

Ye bilkul wahi baat hai jo [Lines and Angles Topic 1](../lines-and-angles/lines-and-angles.md) mein **reflex angle** ke liye dekhi thi: reflex angle = 360° − angle. Major arc ka central angle asal mein ∠AOB ka **reflex angle** hi hai.

```
            A
         .  *  .
       .   /     .            Minor arc AB  → ∠AOB (chhota angle)
      .   /       .           Major arc ACB → reflex ∠AOB (bada angle)
     .   O---------* B
      .           .
       .         .
         .  *  .
            C
```

### Semicircle kab banta hai?

Jab A aur B **diameter ke do sire** hon, to ∠AOB = 180° (seedhi line). Tab dono arcs **180°** ke hote hain, aur dono **semicircle** hain. Isliye kehte hain ki **diameter circle ko do semicircles mein baant deta hai.**

## Part E: Solved examples

**Example 1:** Minor arc AB = 70°. Major arc ACB nikalo.

Major arc = 360° − 70° = **290°**

Jaanch: 70° + 290° = 360° ✅

**Example 2:** ∠AOB = 120° hai. Dono arcs ke measure batao.

- Minor arc AB = 120°
- Major arc ACB = 360° − 120° = **240°**

**Example 3 (giant wheel):** 12 cabin barabar doori par hain.

- Do paas wale cabins ke beech: 360° ÷ 12 = **30°**
- Cabin 1 se cabin 4: 3 gap × 30° = **90°** (minor arc)
- Cabin 1 se cabin 7: 6 gap × 30° = **180°** (semicircle)
- Cabin 1 se cabin 9:
  - Aage ki taraf: 8 gap × 30° = **240°** (major arc)
  - Peeche ki taraf: 4 gap × 30° = **120°** (minor arc)
  - Jaanch: 240° + 120° = 360° ✅

**Example 4 (gol dial):** Ek gol dial par 12 barabar nishaan hain (har do nishaan ke beech 30°). Nishaan 12 se nishaan 5 tak ghadi ki sui ki disha mein jaane wala arc kitna hai?

5 gap × 30° = **150°**. Ye minor arc hai. Ulti disha wala arc = 360° − 150° = **210°** (major arc).

**Example 5:** Minor arc aur major arc ka ratio 1 : 5 hai. Dono nikalo.

Kul hisse = 1 + 5 = 6. Har hissa = 360° ÷ 6 = 60°.
- Minor arc = **60°**
- Major arc = 5 × 60° = **300°**

Jaanch: 60° + 300° = 360° ✅

**Example 6:** Major arc, minor arc ka 3 guna hai. Dono nikalo.

Minor arc = x, major arc = 3x.
x + 3x = 360° → 4x = 360° → x = 90°
- Minor arc = **90°**
- Major arc = **270°**

**Example 7:** Minor arc = x aur major arc = 2x + 30°. x nikalo.

x + 2x + 30° = 360°
3x = 330°
x = **110°**

Minor arc = 110°, major arc = 2(110°) + 30° = 250°.
Jaanch: 110° + 250° = 360° ✅

**Example 8:** Circle par teen points A, B, C hain. ∠AOB = 100° aur ∠BOC = 120° (minor arcs ke central angles, aur AB, BC, CA mil kar poora circle bana rahe hain). Arc CA kitna hai?

Arc CA = 360° − 100° − 120° = **140°**

## Part F: Arc ki lambai (Extra / Preview)

Ye part Class 9 ke main syllabus se thoda aage ki jhalak hai. Iska poora upyog Class 10 mein hoga.

Degree measure hissa batata hai. Agar hume **cm mein lambai** chahiye, to us hisse ko poori circumference se multiply karo:

> **Arc ki lambai = (θ / 360°) × 2πr**

(Yahan θ arc ka degree measure hai aur r radius.)

**Example 1:** r = 7 cm, θ = 90°. (π = 22/7 lo)
Circumference = 2 × 22/7 × 7 = 44 cm
Arc ki lambai = (90/360) × 44 = ¼ × 44 = **11 cm**

**Example 2:** r = 21 cm, θ = 60°.
Circumference = 2 × 22/7 × 21 = 132 cm
Arc ki lambai = (60/360) × 132 = ⅙ × 132 = **22 cm**

**Example 3:** r = 14 cm, semicircle (θ = 180°).
Circumference = 2 × 22/7 × 14 = 88 cm
Semicircle ki lambai = ½ × 88 = **44 cm**

## Part G: Segment aur Alternate segment

Topic 1 mein roti par seedha cut lagakar segment ka naam suna tha. Ab ise poori tarah samjhte hain.

Topic 1 wali roti phir se lo aur chaaku se ek seedhi line mein kaat do (centre se nahi, thoda side se). Roti do tukdon mein bant gayi: ek **chhota tukda**, ek **bada tukda**. Chaaku ki seedhi line ek **chord** hai.

```
            .  *  .
         .           .
       .     BADA      .
      .   (major segment).
     A *-----------------* B      ← chord AB
       .   chhota      .
          (minor seg.)
            .  *  .
```

- **Minor segment:** chord aur **minor arc** ke beech ka hissa (chhota tukda).
- **Major segment:** chord aur **major arc** ke beech ka hissa (bada tukda).

### Alternate segment

Ab is jodi ko ek naya naam do:

> **Ek segment ka "alternate segment" wahi doosra segment hota hai, jo chord ke doosri taraf hai.**

- Minor segment ka alternate segment = **major segment**
- Major segment ka alternate segment = **minor segment**

Yaani chord ke dono taraf ke tukde ek doosre ke **alternate** hain. "Alternate" ka matlab yahan bas "doosra wala" ya "saamne wala" hai.

### Agar chord diameter ho?

Tab dono tukde **barabar** hote hain. Dono **semicircular regions** hain, aur har ek doosre ka alternate segment hai.

### Aage kahan kaam aayega?

**Class 10 mein (aage ki class):** Jab circle ko chhoone wali line (tangent) padhoge, tab "**angle in the alternate segment**" naam ka ek result aata hai. Wahan ye "alternate segment" wala naam hi kaam aata hai. Abhi bas naam aur matlab pakka kar lo.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Arc ke degree measure ko lambai samajhna**
90° ka arc chhote aur bade circle dono mein 90° hi hai, par bade circle mein lamba hoga. Degree measure "hissa" batata hai, cm nahi.

**Galti 2: Major arc ko do letters se likhna**
Arc AB likhoge to sab minor arc samjhenge. Major arc ke liye teesra letter zaroori hai: **arc ACB**.

**Galti 3: Major arc nikalte waqt 180° se ghatana**
Major arc = **360°** − minor arc, 180° − minor arc nahi. 70° ka major arc 290° hai, 110° nahi.

**Galti 4: 180° wale arc ko minor ya major kehna**
Theek 180° wala arc **semicircle** hai. Minor 180° se kam, major 180° se zyada.

**Galti 5: Segment aur sector mein confusion**
Do radius se ghira = **sector**. Chord se ghira = **segment**.

**Galti 6: Alternate segment ko "barabar" segment samajhna**
Alternate ka matlab "chord ke doosri taraf wala" hai. Wo chhota bhi ho sakta hai, bada bhi. Barabar sirf tab hote hain jab chord diameter ho.

**Galti 7: Giant wheel jaise sawaalon mein gap ki jagah cabin ginna**
Cabin 1 se cabin 4 tak **3 gap** hain, 4 nahi. Angle gap se nikalta hai.

## Quick Recap

- Do points A aur B circle ko **do arcs** mein baant dete hain: minor aur major.
- **Minor arc** do letters se (arc AB), **major arc** teen letters se (arc ACB).
- **Central angle** = arc ke siron ko centre se jodne par bana angle.
- **Arc ka degree measure = central angle.**
- **Minor arc < 180°**, **semicircle = 180°**, **major arc > 180°**.
- **Major arc = 360° − minor arc** (bilkul reflex angle jaisa).
- Diameter circle ko do **semicircles** mein baantta hai.
- (Extra) Arc ki lambai = (θ/360°) × 2πr.
- Chord circular region ko **minor segment** aur **major segment** mein baantti hai. Dono ek doosre ke **alternate segment** hain.
- Sector = do radius + arc. Segment = chord + arc.

## Practice Questions

1. If minor arc AB measures 75°, find the measure of major arc ACB.

2. Classify each arc as a minor arc, a semicircle or a major arc:
   (a) 45°  (b) 180°  (c) 200°  (d) 359°

3. A giant wheel has 10 cabins placed at equal distances.
   (a) Find the angle at the centre between two adjacent cabins.
   (b) Find the arc from cabin 1 to cabin 4 (moving forward).
   (c) Find the minor arc between cabin 2 and cabin 9.

4. The minor arc and the major arc of a circle are in the ratio 2 : 7. Find both arcs.

5. Find the length of an arc of a circle of radius 21 cm whose degree measure is 60°. (Use π = 22/7)

6. What is the degree measure of each arc into which a diameter divides a circle? What are such arcs called?

7. If ∠AOB = 140°, where O is the centre, find the reflex angle AOB and the measure of the major arc ACB.

8. State whether each statement is true or false. Give a reason.
   (a) A major arc always measures more than 180°.
   (b) "Arc AB" written with two letters usually means the major arc.
   (c) The alternate segment of the minor segment is the major segment.
   (d) A segment is the region bounded by two radii and an arc.

### Answers

1. Major arc = 360° − 75° = **285°**
   Jaanch: 75° + 285° = 360° ✅

2. (a) 45° < 180°, to **minor arc**
   (b) Theek 180°, to **semicircle**
   (c) 200° > 180°, to **major arc**
   (d) 359° > 180°, to **major arc**

3. (a) 360° ÷ 10 = **36°**
   (b) Cabin 1 se 4 tak 3 gap: 3 × 36° = **108°**
   (c) Cabin 2 se 9 tak aage ki taraf 7 gap = 7 × 36° = 252°. Peeche ki taraf 3 gap = 3 × 36° = 108°. Chhota wala minor arc hai: **108°**.
   Jaanch: 252° + 108° = 360° ✅

4. Kul hisse = 2 + 7 = 9, har hissa = 360° ÷ 9 = 40°.
   Minor arc = 2 × 40° = **80°**, major arc = 7 × 40° = **280°**
   Jaanch: 80° + 280° = 360° ✅

5. Circumference = 2 × 22/7 × 21 = 132 cm
   Arc ki lambai = (60/360) × 132 = ⅙ × 132 = **22 cm**

6. Diameter ke dono sire centre par 180° banate hain, to har arc **180°** ka hai. Inhe **semicircle** kehte hain.

7. Reflex ∠AOB = 360° − 140° = **220°**. Major arc ACB = reflex ∠AOB = **220°**.

8. (a) **True** ✅. Major arc ki definition hi 180° se zyada hai.
   (b) **False** ❌. Do letters se likha arc AB aam taur par **minor arc** hota hai. Major ke liye teesra letter lagta hai.
   (c) **True** ✅. Chord ke doosri taraf wala segment hi alternate segment hai.
   (d) **False** ❌. Do radius aur arc se ghira region **sector** hai. Segment chord aur arc se ghira hota hai.
