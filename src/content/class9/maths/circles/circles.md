# Topic 1: Circle, Circle se jude shabd, aur Point ki Position

Ab tak hum ne seedhi lines, angles, triangles aur quadrilaterals padhe. Sab mein **seedhe kinaare** the. Is chapter mein hum ek aisi shape se milenge jisme ek bhi kona nahi hai, ek bhi seedha kinaara nahi hai, phir bhi wo duniya ki sabse "barabar" shape hai: **circle (vritt)**.

---

## Gauri bakri ki kahani

Gaon mein Meena ke paas ek bakri hai, naam hai **Gauri**. Har subah Meena use maidan mein le jati hai aur ek **khoonta** (lakdi ka khoonta) zameen mein gaad deti hai. Gauri ko **7 metre lambi rassi** se us khoonte se baandh deti hai.

Ab Gauri din bhar ghaas charti hai. Shaam ko Meena dekhti hai ki maidan mein ek **gol nishaan** ban gaya hai:

- Khoonte ke paas ki saari ghaas khatam ho gayi.
- Ek hadd tak ghaas chari hui hai, uske baad ghaas bilkul waisi hi hai.
- Wo hadd ekdam **gol** hai.

Kyun? Kyunki Gauri khoonte se **7 metre se zyada door ja hi nahi sakti.** Jab wo rassi poori kheench kar ghoomti hai, to har jagah khoonte se theek **7 metre** door hoti hai. Aise saare points mil kar ek **circle** banate hain.

Is kahani mein:
- **Khoonta** = circle ka **centre** (kendra)
- **Rassi ki lambai (7 m)** = circle ka **radius** (trijya)
- **Gol hadd** = **circle**
- **Andar ki chari hui ghaas** = circle ka **interior** (andar wala hissa)
- **Bahar ki ghaas** jahan Gauri nahi pahunch sakti = circle ka **exterior** (bahar wala hissa)

Is topic mein hum yahi sab shabd aaram se samjhenge.

## Part A: Circle kya hai?

### Definition

> **Circle** ek plane mein un **saare points** ka samooh hai jo ek **fix point** se **ek fix doori** par hote hain.
> - Fix point ko **centre** kehte hain.
> - Fix doori ko **radius** kehte hain.

Gauri ki kahani mein ye bilkul saaf hai: khoonta fix hai, rassi ki lambai fix hai, aur Gauri jahan jahan rassi poori kheench kar khadi ho sakti hai, wo saare points circle par hain.

### Circle kaise banate hain?

Ghar par ek dhaaga, ek pin aur ek pencil lo.
1. Pin ko kaagaz par gaad do (ye centre hai).
2. Dhaage ka ek sira pin se, doosra pencil se baandho.
3. Dhaaga tight rakh kar pencil ko ghumao.

Jo shape bani wo circle hai. **Compass** bhi bilkul yahi kaam karta hai: ek nok centre par, doosri nok pencil, aur beech ki doori radius.

### Ek zaroori baat: "plane mein"

Agar hum ek fix point se barabar doori wale saare points **hawa mein (3D mein)** lein, to ek **gend (sphere)** banegi, circle nahi. Circle sirf ek **sapaat sataha (plane)** par banta hai.

```
            * * *
         *         *
       *             *
      *       O-------*      O = centre
       *     (r)     *       O se har * ki doori = r
         *         *
            * * *
```

## Part B: Circle se jude shabd

### 1. Radius (trijya)

Centre O se circle ke kisi bhi point P tak ka line segment **OP** radius hai. Uski lambai ko bhi radius kehte hain, aur ise **r** se likhte hain.

- Circle ke **saare radius barabar** hote hain (definition se hi).
- Ek circle mein **anginat (infinitely many) radius** ho sakte hain, kyunki circle par anginat points hain aur har point ko centre se jod sakte hain.

### 2. Chord (jeeva)

Circle ke **kinhi do points** ko jodne wala line segment **chord** kehlata hai.

```
            * * *
         *         *
       A-------------B      AB = chord
      *                *
       *      O       *
         *         *
            * * *
```

Roti par chaaku se ek seedha cut lagao jo ek kinaare se doosre kinaare tak jaye. Wo cut ek chord hai.

### 3. Diameter (vyas)

Jo chord **centre se hokar** guzarti hai, use **diameter** kehte hain.

```
            * * *
         *         *
       *             *
      P-------O-------Q      PQ = diameter (centre O se guzarti hai)
       *             *       OP = OQ = r
         *         *
            * * *
```

Dhyan do: PQ = PO + OQ = r + r = **2r**.

> **Diameter = 2 × radius**, ya **radius = diameter ÷ 2**

- Har diameter ek chord hai.
- Par har chord diameter **nahi** hai (sirf wahi jo centre se guzre).
- Ek circle mein bhi **anginat diameters** hote hain, aur sab **barabar** hote hain (sab 2r).

### Diameter sabse lambi chord kyun hai?

Koi bhi chord AB lo jo centre se **nahi** guzarti. Centre O ko A aur B se jodo. Ab ΔOAB ek triangle hai.

```
            * * *
         *         *
       A-------------B
      *  \         /   *
       *   \     /     *
         *   \ /     *
            * O *
```

[Congruence of Triangles ke Topic 4](../congruence-of-triangles/congruence-of-triangles-part4.md) mein humne triangle inequality prove ki thi: kisi bhi triangle mein do sides ka sum teesri side se bada hota hai. To:

AB < OA + OB = r + r = 2r = diameter

Yaani centre se na guzarne wali har chord diameter se **chhoti** hai. Aur jab chord centre se guzarti hai, tab wo diameter ban jati hai, lambai theek 2r. Isliye:

> **Diameter circle ki sabse lambi chord hai.**

### 4. Arc (chaap)

Circle ka koi bhi **tukda (curve wala hissa)** arc kehlata hai. Agar circle par do points A aur B hain, to wo circle ko **do arcs** mein baant dete hain. Ise arc AB likhte hain (AB ke upar ek chhoti curve ka nishaan, ⌒AB).

Chudi ko socho. Agar wo do jagah se toot jaye, to do tukde milenge. Har tukda ek arc hai.

Kaunsa arc chhota, kaunsa bada, aur arc ko degree mein kaise naapte hain, ye sab **Topic 2** mein aayega.

### 5. Circumference (paridhi)

Circle ke chaaron taraf **ek poora chakkar** lagane mein jitni lambai aati hai, wo **circumference** hai.

> **Circumference = 2πr** (ya **πd**, jahan d = diameter)
> π ki value lagbhag **22/7** ya **3.14** lete hain.

Dhyan rakho:
- **Circle** ek **curve (shape)** hai.
- **Circumference** us curve ki **lambai** hai (ek number, jaise 44 m).

Gauri ki rassi 7 m hai. Uski chari hui hadd ki lambai:
C = 2 × (22/7) × 7 = **44 m**

Agar Meena us hadd par baadh lagaye, to use 44 m taar chahiye.

### 6. Interior, Exterior, aur Circular region

Circle plane ko **teen hisson** mein baant deta hai:

| Hissa | Kya hai | Gauri ki kahani mein |
|---|---|---|
| **Interior** | Circle ke andar ke points | Chari hui ghaas |
| **Circle khud** | Circle par ke points | Gol hadd |
| **Exterior** | Circle ke bahar ke points | Bachi hui ghaas |

**Circle + uska interior** ko milakar **circular region** kehte hain. Roti ka poora hissa (kinaara + andar) ek circular region hai. Sirf kinaara circle hai.

### 7. Segment aur Sector

**Segment:** Ek chord circle ke andar ke hisse ko do tukdon mein kaatti hai. Har tukda (chord + arc ke beech ka hissa) ek **segment** hai.

**Sector:** Do radius aur unke beech ke arc se ghira hua hissa **sector** hai.

```
   SEGMENT                          SECTOR
       * * *                           * * *
    *  ////// *                     *    |    *
   A----------B   <- chord         *     |     *
  *              *                *      O      *
   *            *                  *   /////   *
     *        *                      * ///// *
        * *                             * *
  //// = segment                   //// = O se do radius aur arc ke beech
```

Roti ke example se:
- Roti par ek **seedha cut** (kinaare se kinaare) lagao. Jo tukda alag hua, wo **segment** hai.
- Roti ke **centre se do cut** lagao (jaise cake ka tukda). Jo tukda nikla, wo **sector** hai.

Chhota segment aur bada segment, chhota sector aur bada sector, ye detail **Topic 2** mein aayegi.

### 8. Semicircle (ardhvritt)

Diameter circle ko **do barabar hisson** mein baant deta hai. Har hissa ek **semicircle** hai.

Aadhi roti socho, jo theek beech se kati ho. Wo semicircle ki shakal hai.

### 9. Secant aur Tangent

Ek line aur ek circle ke beech teen tarah ka rishta ho sakta hai:

```
  (a) Koi common point nahi     (b) Secant: 2 points       (c) Tangent: 1 point
                                                           
  ------------------                                            * * *
                                     * * *                   *         *
       * * *                      *         *               *     O     *
    *         *              ----P-----------Q----           *         *
   *     O     *                *     O     *                   * * *
    *         *                  *         *              ---------T---------
       * * *                        * * *
```

- **(a)** Line circle ko chhooti hi nahi.
- **(b) Secant:** Line circle ko **do points** (P aur Q) par kaatti hai. Roti ke aar-paar jaate seekh ki tarah.
- **(c) Tangent:** Line circle ko **sirf ek point** (T) par chhooti hai. Cycle ka pahiya zameen ko sirf ek jagah chhoota hai, wo zameen us pahiye ki tangent jaisi hai.

Abhi ke liye sirf ye naam aur matlab yaad rakho. Tangent ki properties aage ki classes mein padhoge.

### 10. Concentric circles

Ek talaab mein pathar phenko. Paani mein **ek ke baad ek gol lehrein** banti hain, sabka **centre ek hi** jagah (jahan pathar gira). Aise circles jinka **centre same ho par radius alag** ho, unhe **concentric circles** kehte hain.

```
           * * * * *
        *    * * *    *
      *    *   O   *    *       O = dono ka common centre
        *    * * *    *
           * * * * *
```

Dartboard aur pyaz (onion) ka kata hua hissa bhi concentric circles jaisa dikhta hai.

## Part C: Point ki Position (Circle ke respect mein)

Gauri ki kahani mein Meena ne teen jagah ghaas ke gucche rakhe:
- Gucha **X** khoonte se **5 m** door hai.
- Gucha **Y** khoonte se **7 m** door hai.
- Gucha **Z** khoonte se **9 m** door hai.

Rassi 7 m ki hai. Kaunsa gucha Gauri kha sakti hai?
- **X (5 m):** 5 < 7, Gauri aaram se pahunch jayegi. X circle ke **andar** hai.
- **Y (7 m):** 7 = 7, rassi poori kheench kar theek pahunchegi. Y circle ke **upar** hai.
- **Z (9 m):** 9 > 7, rassi chhoti pad jayegi. Z circle ke **bahar** hai.

### Rule

Maan lo circle ka centre O hai aur radius r hai. Kisi point P ki centre se doori **d = OP** hai. To:

| Shart | Point P kahan hai |
|---|---|
| **d < r** | Circle ke **andar (interior)** |
| **d = r** | Circle ke **upar** |
| **d > r** | Circle ke **bahar (exterior)** |

> Yaad rakhne ka tarika: **Doori ko radius se compare karo.** Diameter se nahi!

### Solved examples

**Example 1:** Ek circle ka centre O aur radius 5 cm hai. OP = 3 cm, OQ = 5 cm, OR = 7.5 cm. Har point ki position batao.

- OP = 3 < 5, to **P andar** hai.
- OQ = 5 = 5, to **Q circle par** hai.
- OR = 7.5 > 5, to **R bahar** hai.

**Example 2:** Ek circle ka diameter 12 cm hai. Centre se 7 cm door ek point A hai. A kahan hai?

Pehle radius nikalo: r = 12 ÷ 2 = **6 cm**.
OA = 7 > 6, to **A bahar** hai.

(Agar galti se 7 ko diameter 12 se compare karte, to "andar" bol dete, jo galat hai.)

**Example 3: Coordinates ke saath.** Ek circle ka centre origin O(0, 0) par hai aur radius 5 hai. Points (3, 4), (2, 1), (6, 0), (0, −5) ki position batao.

Kisi point (x, y) ki origin se doori √(x² + y²) hoti hai. Ye Pythagoras theorem se aata hai, jo pichhli classes mein padha tha. [Coordinate Geometry](../coordinate-geometry/coordinate-geometry.md) mein humne points ke coordinates dekhe the.

| Point | Doori d | d aur r = 5 | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| (3, 4) | √(9 + 16) = √25 = 5 | d = r | Circle **par** |
| (2, 1) | √(4 + 1) = √5 ≈ 2.24 | d < r | **Andar** |
| (6, 0) | 6 (x-axis par seedha) | d > r | **Bahar** |
| (0, −5) | 5 (y-axis par seedha) | d = r | Circle **par** |

**Example 4:** Kya centre khud circle par hota hai?

Centre ki apne aap se doori **0** hai. 0 < r, to centre circle ke **andar (interior)** mein hota hai, circle par nahi.

## Part D: Circumference aur Diameter ke sawaal

**Example 5:** Radius 7 cm hai. Diameter aur circumference nikalo.

- Diameter = 2 × 7 = **14 cm**
- Circumference = 2 × (22/7) × 7 = **44 cm**

**Example 6:** Diameter 21 cm hai. Radius aur circumference nikalo.

- Radius = 21 ÷ 2 = **10.5 cm**
- Circumference = π × d = (22/7) × 21 = **66 cm**

**Example 7:** Circumference 88 cm hai. Radius nikalo.

2πr = 88
2 × (22/7) × r = 88
(44/7) × r = 88
r = 88 × 7 ÷ 44 = **14 cm**

**Jaanch:** 2 × (22/7) × 14 = 2 × 22 × 2 = 88 ✅

**Example 8:** Ek circle ki radius 5 cm hai. Kya isme 11 cm lambi chord ho sakti hai?

Sabse lambi chord = diameter = 2 × 5 = 10 cm. 11 > 10, to **nahi**, aisi chord ho hi nahi sakti.

**Example 9:** Ek circle mein kitne radius aur kitne diameter hote hain?

Dono **anginat (infinitely many)**. Circle par anginat points hain. Har point se ek radius banta hai, aur har point se centre ke aar-paar ek diameter.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Radius aur diameter mein confusion**
Diameter = 2 × radius. Agar question mein diameter diya hai aur formula mein r chahiye, to pehle aadha karo. Diameter 14 hai to r = 7, 14 nahi.

**Galti 2: Position check karte waqt diameter se compare karna**
Doori hamesha **radius** se compare hoti hai. Diameter 12, doori 7: r = 6, to point **bahar** hai, andar nahi.

**Galti 3: Har chord ko diameter samajhna**
Sirf wahi chord diameter hai jo **centre se guzre**. Har diameter chord hai, par har chord diameter nahi.

**Galti 4: Circle aur circular region ko ek samajhna**
Circle sirf **kinaara (boundary)** hai. Kinaara + andar ka hissa = circular region. Chudi ek circle jaisi hai, roti ek circular region jaisi.

**Galti 5: Circle aur circumference ko ek samajhna**
Circle ek **shape** hai. Circumference us shape ki **lambai** (ek number) hai.

**Galti 6: Tangent ko do points par kaatne wali line samajhna**
Do points par kaatne wali line **secant** hai. Tangent sirf **ek point** par chhooti hai.

**Galti 7: Centre ko circle par maan lena**
Centre circle ke **andar** hota hai. Circle ke saare points centre se r doori par hain, aur centre ki apni doori 0 hai.

**Galti 8: Arc aur chord ko ek samajhna**
Chord **seedha** line segment hai. Arc circle ka **curve wala** tukda hai. Chudi ka toota tukda arc hai, uske dono siron ko jodne wala seedha dhaaga chord hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Circle:** ek plane mein ek fix point (**centre**) se fix doori (**radius**) wale saare points.
- **Chord:** circle ke do points ko jodne wala segment. **Diameter:** centre se guzarne wali chord, **= 2r**, aur **sabse lambi chord**.
- Ek circle mein **anginat radius aur diameter** hote hain, sab barabar.
- **Arc:** circle ka curve wala tukda (detail Topic 2 mein).
- **Circumference = 2πr = πd.**
- Circle plane ko **interior, circle, exterior** mein baant deta hai. Circle + interior = **circular region**.
- **Segment:** chord aur arc ke beech. **Sector:** do radius aur arc ke beech. **Semicircle:** diameter se bana aadha circle.
- **Secant:** do points par kaatti hai. **Tangent:** ek point par chhooti hai.
- **Concentric circles:** same centre, alag radius.
- **Point ki position:** d < r andar, d = r par, d > r bahar. **Radius se compare karo.**

1. Fill in the blanks:
   (a) The diameter of a circle is ______ times its radius.
   (b) The longest chord of a circle is its ______.
   (c) A point whose distance from the centre equals the radius lies ______ the circle.
   (d) Circles having the same centre but different radii are called ______ circles.
   (e) A line that touches a circle at exactly one point is called a ______.

2. The radius of a circle is 4.2 cm. Find its diameter and circumference. (Take π = 22/7.)

3. The circumference of a circle is 154 cm. Find its radius. (Take π = 22/7.)

4. A circle has centre O and radius 6 cm. Points A, B and C are such that OA = 4 cm, OB = 6 cm and OC = 8.5 cm. State the position of each point with respect to the circle.

5. A circle has its centre at the origin and radius 10 units. State whether each point lies inside, on or outside the circle:
   (a) (6, 8)  (b) (5, 5)  (c) (0, −10)  (d) (11, 0)

6. State whether each statement is true or false. Give a reason.
   (a) Every chord of a circle is a diameter.
   (b) A circle has infinitely many radii.
   (c) A tangent cuts a circle at two points.
   (d) The centre of a circle lies in its interior.
   (e) The line segment joining any two points on a circle is a chord.

7. The radius of a circle is 5 cm. Can it have a chord of length 12 cm? Give a reason.

8. A goat is tied to a peg with a rope 14 m long.
   (a) Find the length of the boundary of the region it can graze. (Take π = 22/7.)
   (b) Can it reach grass placed 10 m from the peg? What about grass placed 15 m from the peg?

9. The diameter of a cycle wheel is 70 cm. How far does the cycle move in one complete revolution of the wheel? How far in 100 revolutions? (Take π = 22/7.)

### Answers

1. (a) **2**  (b) **diameter**  (c) **on**  (d) **concentric**  (e) **tangent**

2. Diameter = 2 × 4.2 = **8.4 cm**.
   Circumference = 2 × (22/7) × 4.2 = (22/7) × 8.4 = 22 × 1.2 = **26.4 cm**.

3. 2 × (22/7) × r = 154, yaani (44/7) × r = 154.
   r = 154 × 7 ÷ 44 = **24.5 cm**.
   Jaanch: (44/7) × 24.5 = 44 × 3.5 = 154 ✅

4. OA = 4 < 6, to **A andar** hai.
   OB = 6 = 6, to **B circle par** hai.
   OC = 8.5 > 6, to **C bahar** hai.

5. (a) √(36 + 64) = √100 = 10 = r, to **circle par**.
   (b) √(25 + 25) = √50 ≈ 7.07 < 10, to **andar**.
   (c) Doori 10 = r (y-axis par), to **circle par**.
   (d) Doori 11 > 10 (x-axis par), to **bahar**.

6. (a) **False** ❌. Sirf centre se guzarne wali chord diameter hai.
   (b) **True** ✅. Circle par anginat points hain, har ek se ek radius.
   (c) **False** ❌. Tangent sirf ek point par chhooti hai. Do points par kaatne wali line secant hai.
   (d) **True** ✅. Centre ki doori 0 hai, jo r se kam hai.
   (e) **True** ✅. Yahi chord ki definition hai (do alag points lene hain).

7. **Nahi.** Sabse lambi chord diameter hoti hai = 2 × 5 = 10 cm. 12 cm > 10 cm, to aisi chord ho hi nahi sakti.

8. (a) Hadd ki lambai = circumference = 2 × (22/7) × 14 = **88 m**.
   (b) 10 m < 14 m, to **haan**, pahunch jayegi (andar hai). 15 m > 14 m, to **nahi**, bahar hai.

9. Ek chakkar mein cycle utna chalti hai jitni pahiye ki circumference hai.
   Circumference = π × d = (22/7) × 70 = **220 cm**.
   100 chakkar mein = 220 × 100 = 22000 cm = **220 m**.
