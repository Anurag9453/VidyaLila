# Topic 2: Bina Formula ke Compound Interest (Saal-dar-Saal Hisaab)

Topic 1 mein humne dekha tha ki compound interest mein byaaj sirf shuru ke paise par nahi lagta, wo **ab tak jude hue byaaj par bhi** lagta hai. Mannat ke ₹10,000 par 10% se 3 saal mein SI ₹3,000 bana aur CI ₹3,310: pehle saal dono barabar, uske baad CI aage nikal gaya. Wahan hum ye bhi samajh chuke hain ki P, r, n aur Amount kya hote hain.

Ab sawaal ye hai ki CI **nikalte kaise hain**? Is topic mein koi formula nahi hoga. Bas ek seedhi si aadat hogi: **ek saal ka hisaab, phir agle saal ka hisaab**, bilkul usi tarah jaise sahkari society ka munshi register mein likhta hai. Isi se pura compound interest samajh aa jayega, aur aage ka formula bhi isi se niklega.

---

## Harpreet ji ki trolley

Punjab ke ek gaon mein Harpreet ji ke paas purana tractor hai, par kheti ka saaman dhone ke liye unhe ek nayi **trolley** chahiye. Trolley ki keemat ₹50,000 hai. Paise unke paas nahi hain, to gaon ki sahkari society se unhone ₹50,000 ka loan liya, **8% saalana byaaj** par. Society ke munshi ji ne ek shart batayi: "Byaaj har saal ke end mein principal mein jod diya jayega. Agle saal ka byaaj is naye, badhe hue paise par lagega."

Harpreet ji ki beti Jasleen Class 9 mein hai. Wo kehti hai, "Papa, ruko, main register jaisi ek table bana deti hoon."

**Pehla saal:**
- Shuru mein loan: ₹50,000
- Byaaj: 8% of ₹50,000 = ₹4,000
- Saal ke end mein society ko banta hai: 50,000 + 4,000 = **₹54,000**

Harpreet ji bole, "Doosre saal ka byaaj bhi ₹4,000 hi hoga na?"

Jasleen ne munshi ji ki shart yaad dilayi: "Nahi papa. Ab society ke register mein principal ₹50,000 nahi, **₹54,000** hai. Byaaj ab ₹54,000 par lagega."

**Doosra saal:**
- Byaaj: 8% of ₹54,000 = ₹4,320
- Saal ke end mein: 54,000 + 4,320 = **₹58,320**

Dekha? Doosre saal ka byaaj ₹4,000 nahi, ₹4,320 hai. Ye ₹320 ka farak wahi "byaaj par byaaj" hai: pehle saal ke ₹4,000 byaaj par bhi 8% lag gaya (8% of 4,000 = ₹320).

Is topic mein hum yahi register-wali aadat seekhenge: **har saal ka principal, byaaj aur amount alag-alag likhna**. Aage isi loan par ek aur sawaal bhi aayega, jab Harpreet ji beech mein kuch paise lauta denge.

---

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Saal ka principal** | Us saal ki shuruaat mein jitna paisa byaaj ke hisaab mein ginta hai | Doosre saal ka principal ₹54,000 |
| **Saal ka byaaj** | Sirf us ek saal ka byaaj: us saal ka principal × r ÷ 100 | ₹4,320 |
| **Saal ke end ka amount** | Us saal ka principal + us saal ka byaaj | ₹58,320 |
| **Agle saal ka principal** | Pichle saal ke end ka amount hi agle saal ka principal ban jata hai | ₹54,000 |
| **Compound interest (CI)** | Aakhri amount − sabse pehla principal | 58,320 − 50,000 = ₹8,320 |
| **Part payment** | Loan ke beech mein, poora hisaab hone se pehle, kuch paisa lauta dena | Pehle saal ke end mein ₹24,000 dena |
| **Baaki rakam (balance)** | Part payment ke baad jitna loan abhi chukana hai | 54,000 − 24,000 = ₹30,000 |

---

## Part A: Saal-dar-Saal ka tareeka

Har saal ke liye bas teen chhote kaam karne hain:

> **Kaam 1:** Byaaj = us saal ka principal × r ÷ 100
> **Kaam 2:** Amount = us saal ka principal + byaaj
> **Kaam 3:** Ye amount hi **agle saal ka principal** ban jata hai. Wapas Kaam 1 par jao.

Jitne saal, utni baar ye teen kaam dohrao. End mein:

> **Compound interest = aakhri amount − sabse pehla principal**

### Table ka dhancha

Jasleen ne ye ek hi dhancha har sawaal ke liye apna liya:

| Saal | Principal (saal ki shuruaat) | Byaaj | Amount (saal ke end) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | P | P × r ÷ 100 | P + byaaj |
| 2 | Pichhle saal ka amount | us par r% | principal + byaaj |
| 3 | Pichhle saal ka amount | us par r% | principal + byaaj |

**Ek zaroori baat:** table mein "Principal" wala column **har saal badalta hai**. Jis din ye column har row mein ek hi number dikhne lage, samajh lo ki aap SI nikal rahe ho, CI nahi.

### Harpreet ji ke loan ki poori table

| Saal | Principal | Byaaj (8%) | Amount |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ₹50,000 | 50,000 × 8 ÷ 100 = ₹4,000 | ₹54,000 |
| 2 | ₹54,000 | 54,000 × 8 ÷ 100 = ₹4,320 | ₹58,320 |

CI (2 saal) = 58,320 − 50,000 = **₹8,320**

**Jaanch:** Byaaj ke dono hisse jodo: 4,000 + 4,320 = 8,320 ✅. Yaani CI = har saal ka byaaj jod do.

Ek chhoti si baat aur: CI nikalne ke do tareeke hain, aur dono se jawab ek hi aana chahiye:
1. aakhri amount − shuruaati principal
2. har saal ka byaaj ka jod

Ek se nikalo, doosre se jaanch lo.

## Part B: 2 saal aur 3 saal ke sawaal

### Example 1: ₹8,000, 5% saalana, 2 saal

| Saal | Principal | Byaaj (5%) | Amount |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ₹8,000 | 8,000 × 5 ÷ 100 = ₹400 | 8,000 + 400 = ₹8,400 |
| 2 | ₹8,400 | 8,400 × 5 ÷ 100 = ₹420 | 8,400 + 420 = ₹8,820 |

- Aakhri amount = **₹8,820**
- CI = 8,820 − 8,000 = **₹820**

**Jaanch:** 400 + 420 = 820 ✅

### Example 2: ₹25,000, 12% saalana, 2 saal

| Saal | Principal | Byaaj (12%) | Amount |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ₹25,000 | 25,000 × 12 ÷ 100 = ₹3,000 | ₹28,000 |
| 2 | ₹28,000 | 28,000 × 12 ÷ 100 = ₹3,360 | ₹31,360 |

- Aakhri amount = **₹31,360**
- CI = 31,360 − 25,000 = **₹6,360**

**Jaanch:** 3,000 + 3,360 = 6,360 ✅

### Example 3: ₹15,000, 8% saalana, 3 saal

Ab teen saal hain, to teen rows.

| Saal | Principal | Byaaj (8%) | Amount |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ₹15,000 | 15,000 × 8 ÷ 100 = ₹1,200 | ₹16,200 |
| 2 | ₹16,200 | 16,200 × 8 ÷ 100 = ₹1,296 | ₹17,496 |
| 3 | ₹17,496 | 17,496 × 8 ÷ 100 = ₹1,399.68 | ₹18,895.68 |

- Aakhri amount = **₹18,895.68**
- CI = 18,895.68 − 15,000 = **₹3,895.68**

**Jaanch:** 1,200 + 1,296 + 1,399.68 = 3,895.68 ✅

Yahan byaaj mein paise (₹1,399.68) aaye hain. Unhe **kaatna nahi hai**, poore number likhne hain. Is baare mein "Aam galtiyan" mein baat karenge.

**Dhyan do:** 3 saal ke liye 3 baar wahi kaam karna pada. 10 saal ka sawaal hota to 10 rows banani padti. Ye thoda lamba hai, par isi se samajh aata hai ki andar hota kya hai.

## Part C: 2 saal mein CI aur SI ka farak

Topic 1 mein hum dekh chuke hain ki CI, SI se bada hota hai. Ab dekhte hain **kitna bada**, aur **kyun**.

### Example 4: ₹8,000, 5%, 2 saal (Example 1 wala sawaal)

- **SI** (har saal principal ₹8,000 hi): 8,000 × 5 ÷ 100 = ₹400 har saal, to 2 saal mein **₹800**
- **CI** (Example 1 se): **₹820**
- **Farak** = 820 − 800 = **₹20**

### Ye ₹20 aaye kahan se?

Dono saalon ka hisaab saath rakh kar dekho:

| Saal | SI wala byaaj | CI wala byaaj |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ₹400 | ₹400 |
| 2 | ₹400 | ₹420 |

Pehle saal mein koi farak nahi. Doosre saal CI mein ₹20 zyada hain. Aur ye ₹20 kya hain? **Pehle saal ke ₹400 byaaj par 5% byaaj**: 400 × 5 ÷ 100 = ₹20. Yaani sirf "byaaj par byaaj" wala hissa.

### Ye hamesha kaam karta hai (sirf 2 saal ke liye)

Pehle saal ka byaaj hai P × r ÷ 100. Doosre saal us byaaj par bhi r% lagta hai:

> Farak = (P × r ÷ 100) × r ÷ 100 = **P × (r ÷ 100) × (r ÷ 100)**

Isse likhte hain **P(r/100)²**. Isme ² ka matlab bas "r/100 ko apne se ek baar guna karo" hai.

**Jaanch 1:** P = 8,000, r = 5. r ÷ 100 = 0.05, 0.05 × 0.05 = 0.0025. 8,000 × 0.0025 = **₹20** ✅ (upar wala farak)

**Jaanch 2:** Example 2 ka sawaal (₹25,000, 12%): 
- SI = 25,000 × 12 ÷ 100 = ₹3,000 har saal, to 2 saal ka **₹6,000**
- CI = **₹6,360** (Example 2 se)
- Farak = 6,360 − 6,000 = **₹360**
- Shortcut: 12 ÷ 100 = 0.12, 0.12 × 0.12 = 0.0144, to 25,000 × 0.0144 = **₹360** ✅

Dono baar farak mil gaya. Ye shortcut tab kaam aata hai jab sirf **2 saal ke SI aur CI ka farak** poochha ho. 3 saal ya usse zyada ke liye ye seedha nahi chalta, wahan table hi bharosemand hai.

## Part D: Ulte sawaal (reasoning se)

Kabhi CI nikalna nahi hota. Kuch cheezein di hoti hain aur principal ya rate dhoondhna hota hai. Yahan formula ki zaroorat nahi, bas ek hi baat yaad rakho: **har saal ka byaaj = us saal ke principal ka r%**.

### Example 5: Pehle saal ka byaaj diya hai, principal dhoondho

Ek loan par 6% saalana compound interest lagta hai. Pehle saal ka byaaj **₹2,400** hai. Principal kya tha?

**Reasoning:** 6% ka matlab hai "har ₹100 par ₹6". Yaani principal ke har 100 ke hisse par 6 byaaj. Pehle saal ka byaaj ₹2,400 hai, aur wo principal ka 6% hai.
- 6 ↔ ₹2,400, to 1 ↔ 2,400 ÷ 6 = ₹400
- 100 ↔ 400 × 100 = **₹40,000**

Principal = **₹40,000**

**Jaanch:** 40,000 × 6 ÷ 100 = ₹2,400 ✅

Chaho to aage badh kar dekh lo, 2 saal ka CI kitna hota:

| Saal | Principal | Byaaj (6%) | Amount |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ₹40,000 | ₹2,400 | ₹42,400 |
| 2 | ₹42,400 | 42,400 × 6 ÷ 100 = ₹2,544 | ₹44,944 |

CI = 44,944 − 40,000 = **₹4,944** (jaanch: 2,400 + 2,544 = 4,944 ✅)

### Example 6: Do saal ke amounts diye hain, rate dhoondho

Ek bachat khate mein compound interest lagta hai. Pehle saal ke end mein amount ₹21,000 tha, aur doosre saal ke end mein ₹22,050. Rate kya hai?

**Reasoning:** Doosre saal ka principal = pehle saal ka amount = ₹21,000. Doosre saal ke end mein amount ₹22,050 hua. Matlab doosre saal ka byaaj:

22,050 − 21,000 = **₹1,050**

Ye ₹1,050 byaaj ₹21,000 par laga hai. Percentage nikalte hain:

> Rate = (A₂ − A₁) ÷ A₁ × 100 = 1,050 ÷ 21,000 × 100

1,050 ÷ 21,000 = 0.05, to 0.05 × 100 = **5%**

**Jaanch:** 21,000 × 5 ÷ 100 = ₹1,050, aur 21,000 + 1,050 = ₹22,050 ✅

Isme A₁ aur A₂ ke chhote number bas "pehle saal ka amount" aur "doosre saal ka amount" ke naam hain. Asli baat ye hai ki **doosre saal ka byaaj, pehle saal ke amount ka kitna percent hai**, wahi rate hai.

Ye bhi dekho: shuruaati principal ₹20,000 tha (₹21,000 isi par 5% se bana: 20,000 × 5 ÷ 100 = ₹1,000, 20,000 + 1,000 = ₹21,000 ✅). Rate nikalne ke liye principal jaanna zaroori hi nahi tha.

## Part E: Loan chukana, beech mein part payment

Wapas Harpreet ji ke ₹50,000 ke loan par chalte hain (8% saalana, har saal ke end mein byaaj principal mein juda hua).

Pehle saal ke end mein fasal bik gayi aur Harpreet ji ke haath mein kuch paisa aaya. Unhone society ko **₹24,000 lauta diye**. Baaki loan doosre saal ke end mein chukana hai. Doosre saal ke end mein society ko kitna dena hoga?

**Saal 1:** (upar wali table se)
- Principal ₹50,000, byaaj ₹4,000, amount **₹54,000**

**Part payment:** Harpreet ji ne ₹24,000 diye, to baaki rakam:
- 54,000 − 24,000 = **₹30,000**

**Saal 2:** Ab society ke register mein principal **₹30,000** hai (₹54,000 nahi), kyunki ₹24,000 chuk gaye.
- Byaaj = 30,000 × 8 ÷ 100 = **₹2,400**
- Amount = 30,000 + 2,400 = **₹32,400**

Table mein:

| Saal | Principal | Byaaj (8%) | Amount | Harpreet ji ne diya | Baaki |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ₹50,000 | ₹4,000 | ₹54,000 | ₹24,000 | ₹30,000 |
| 2 | ₹30,000 | ₹2,400 | ₹32,400 | ₹32,400 | ₹0 |

**Doosre saal ke end mein Harpreet ji ko ₹32,400 dene honge.**

**Kul kitna diya?** 24,000 + 32,400 = **₹56,400**

**Jaanch (kya fayda hua?):** Agar part payment nahi karte, to Part A ke hisaab se doosre saal ke end mein ₹58,320 dene padte. Ab ₹56,400 diye. Bachat: 58,320 − 56,400 = **₹1,920** ✅

Ye bachat kyun hui? Part payment ke baad ₹30,000 par byaaj laga, ₹54,000 par nahi. 54,000 se 30,000 ka farak ₹24,000 hai, aur ₹24,000 par 8% = ₹1,920. Matlab jo paisa jaldi lauta do, uspar byaaj ruk jata hai.

**Tareeka yaad rakhne ke liye:** jab beech mein koi payment ho, to us saal ke end ke amount mein se payment **ghata do**, aur jo bacha wo agle saal ka principal ban jata hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Har saal original principal par hi byaaj lagana (ye SI hai)**

₹8,000 par 5%, 2 saal:
- Galat: 8,000 × 5 ÷ 100 = 400 har saal, to 400 + 400 = ₹800. Ye **SI** hai.
- Sahi: doosre saal ka principal ₹8,400 hai, byaaj ₹420, to CI = 400 + 420 = **₹820**.

Khud se poochho: "Table ke Principal column mein har row mein naya number hai?" Agar nahi, to CI nahi ban raha.

**Galti 2: Bahut jaldi round kar dena**

Example 3 mein teesre saal ka byaaj ₹1,399.68 tha. Agar usse turant ₹1,400 kar do, to CI ₹3,896 aa jayega, jabki sahi ₹3,895.68 hai. Chhota sa farak dikhta hai, par aur lambe sawaalon mein ye farak badhta jata hai, aur jawab ya to galat ho jata hai ya dobara dekhne par match nahi karta.

**Sahi aadat:** beech ke har step mein paise (paise tak) likhte raho. **Sabse end mein** hi round karo, wo bhi tab jab sawaal kahe.

**Galti 3: Amount aur interest ko ek samajh lena**

Example 1 mein: Saal 2 ke end ka **₹8,820** hai **amount** (principal + byaaj). Aur **CI ₹820** hai, jo sirf byaaj ka hissa hai. Agar sawaal "compound interest" poochhe aur aap ₹8,820 likh do, to galat hai. Jawab dene se pehle ek baar padho: "amount" maanga hai ya "interest"?

Aasan niyam:
- **Amount** = principal + interest
- **CI** = amount − principal

**Galti 4: Byaaj galat principal par lagana**

Doosre saal ka principal ₹8,400 likh diya, par byaaj phir bhi ₹8,000 par nikaal diya. Byaaj hamesha **us saal ki shuruaat ke principal** par lagta hai, jo pichhle saal ke end ka amount hai.

**Galti 5: Part payment ke baad purana principal chalate rehna**

Harpreet ji ke sawaal mein ₹24,000 dene ke baad doosre saal ka byaaj ₹54,000 par (₹4,320) laga dena galat hai. Principal ab ₹30,000 hai.

## Quick Recap

- CI nikalne ke liye **saal-dar-saal table** banao: Saal, Principal, Byaaj, Amount.
- Har saal: **byaaj = principal × r ÷ 100**, **amount = principal + byaaj**, aur ye amount hi **agle saal ka principal**.
- **CI = aakhri amount − sabse pehla principal** (ya har saal ke byaaj ka jod).
- Jaanch: (aakhri amount − shuruaati principal) aur (har saal ke byaaj ka jod), dono se CI ek hi aana chahiye.
- **2 saal mein CI − SI = P(r/100)²**. Ye sirf "byaaj par byaaj" wala hissa hai.
- **Ulte sawaal:** pehle saal ka byaaj diya ho to principal = byaaj ÷ r × 100. Do saal ke amounts diye ho to rate = (A₂ − A₁) ÷ A₁ × 100.
- **Part payment:** us saal ke end ke amount mein se payment ghatao, jo bacha wo agle saal ka principal.
- Galtiyan: har saal original principal use karna (SI ban jata hai), jaldi round karna, amount aur interest ko mila dena.

## Practice Questions

1. Find the compound interest on ₹12,000 for 2 years at 5% per annum, with interest added to the principal at the end of each year. Show a year-wise table.

2. Find the amount and the compound interest on ₹5,000 for 3 years at 4% per annum. Do not round off any value until the final answer.

3. For ₹16,000 at 5% per annum for 2 years, find the simple interest and the compound interest. Verify that their difference equals P(r/100)².

4. The interest for the first year on a sum at 9% per annum is ₹1,800. Find the sum. Then find the amount at the end of the second year if the interest is compounded yearly.

5. A sum earns compound interest yearly. Its amount at the end of the first year is ₹33,000 and at the end of the second year is ₹35,640. Find the rate of interest.

6. Mrs. Kaur borrows ₹40,000 at 5% per annum, with interest compounded yearly. At the end of the first year she pays back ₹22,000. How much must she pay at the end of the second year to clear the loan completely? What is the total amount she pays?

7. Ravi says: "The compound interest on ₹20,000 for 2 years at 5% per annum is ₹2,000, because 20,000 × 5 ÷ 100 = 1,000 per year." Find his mistake and give the correct compound interest.

8. State True or False:
   (a) In compound interest, the amount at the end of one year becomes the principal for the next year.
   (b) In a 2-year problem, the interest for the second year is always the same as the interest for the first year.
   (c) For 2 years, CI − SI = P(r/100)².
   (d) The compound interest on a sum is equal to its amount at the end.

### Answers

1. 
   | Saal | Principal | Byaaj (5%) | Amount |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | ₹12,000 | 12,000 × 5 ÷ 100 = ₹600 | ₹12,600 |
   | 2 | ₹12,600 | 12,600 × 5 ÷ 100 = ₹630 | ₹13,230 |

   CI = 13,230 − 12,000 = **₹1,230**
   Jaanch: 600 + 630 = 1,230 ✅

2. 
   | Saal | Principal | Byaaj (4%) | Amount |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | ₹5,000 | 5,000 × 4 ÷ 100 = ₹200 | ₹5,200 |
   | 2 | ₹5,200 | 5,200 × 4 ÷ 100 = ₹208 | ₹5,408 |
   | 3 | ₹5,408 | 5,408 × 4 ÷ 100 = ₹216.32 | ₹5,624.32 |

   Amount = **₹5,624.32**, CI = 5,624.32 − 5,000 = **₹624.32**
   Jaanch: 200 + 208 + 216.32 = 624.32 ✅

3. SI = 16,000 × 5 ÷ 100 = ₹800 har saal, to 2 saal mein **₹1,600**.
   CI: Saal 1 byaaj = ₹800, amount ₹16,800. Saal 2 byaaj = 16,800 × 5 ÷ 100 = ₹840, amount ₹17,640. CI = 17,640 − 16,000 = **₹1,640**.
   Farak = 1,640 − 1,600 = **₹40**.
   Shortcut: 16,000 × 0.05 × 0.05 = 16,000 × 0.0025 = ₹40 ✅

4. Byaaj ₹1,800 ↔ 9%. 9 ↔ ₹1,800, to 1 ↔ ₹200, to 100 ↔ **₹20,000**. Sum = **₹20,000**.
   Jaanch: 20,000 × 9 ÷ 100 = 1,800 ✅
   Saal 1 amount = 20,000 + 1,800 = ₹21,800. Saal 2 byaaj = 21,800 × 9 ÷ 100 = ₹1,962. Amount = 21,800 + 1,962 = **₹23,762**.
   Jaanch: 1,962 ÷ 21,800 × 100 = 9 ✅

5. Doosre saal ka byaaj = 35,640 − 33,000 = ₹2,640.
   Rate = 2,640 ÷ 33,000 × 100 = 0.08 × 100 = **8%**.
   Jaanch: 33,000 × 8 ÷ 100 = 2,640, aur 33,000 + 2,640 = 35,640 ✅

6. Saal 1: byaaj = 40,000 × 5 ÷ 100 = ₹2,000, amount = ₹42,000.
   ₹22,000 chukaye, to baaki = 42,000 − 22,000 = ₹20,000.
   Saal 2: byaaj = 20,000 × 5 ÷ 100 = ₹1,000, amount = ₹21,000.
   Doosre saal ke end mein dena hoga **₹21,000**. Kul diya = 22,000 + 21,000 = **₹43,000**.
   Jaanch: bina part payment ke 2 saal ka amount 42,000 + 2,100 = ₹44,100 hota. Bachat 44,100 − 43,000 = ₹1,100, aur 22,000 par 5% = ₹1,100 ✅

7. Ravi ne har saal original principal ₹20,000 par hi byaaj lagaya, ye to SI hai (₹2,000).
   Sahi: Saal 1 byaaj ₹1,000, amount ₹21,000. Saal 2 byaaj = 21,000 × 5 ÷ 100 = ₹1,050, amount ₹22,050. CI = 22,050 − 20,000 = **₹2,050**.
   Jaanch: farak 2,050 − 2,000 = 50, aur 20,000 × 0.0025 = 50 ✅

8. (a) **True** ✅. Yahi compound interest ka dil hai.
   (b) **False** ❌. Doosre saal ka principal bada hota hai, to byaaj bhi bada hota hai (jaise 400 aur 420).
   (c) **True** ✅. Sirf 2 saal ke liye.
   (d) **False** ❌. Amount = principal + CI. CI sirf byaaj ka hissa hai.
