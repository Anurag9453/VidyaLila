# Topic 1: Simple Interest ka Recap aur Compound Interest kya hai

Aap shayad pehle se jaante ho ki jab hum bank mein paise rakhte hain ya kisi se udhaar lete hain, to **byaaj (interest)** lagta hai. Is topic mein hum us purani baat ko ek baar jaldi se yaad karenge (**Simple Interest**), aur phir ek naye vichaar se milenge, jisme byaaj apne aap par bhi byaaj kamata hai. Isi ko **Compound Interest** kehte hain. Aaj hum sirf **vichaar** samjhenge. Calculation ka tareeka agle topics mein aayega.

---

## Mannat ki gullak ki kahani

Mannat ne do saal se apni gullak (piggy bank) mein paise jodhe the. Diwali ke shagun, birthday ke paise, aur nani ka har mahine ka "chhota sa inaam". Gullak todi to nikle poore **₹10,000**.

Papa ne kaha, "Ghar mein rakhoge to bas itne hi rahenge. Chalo bank mein **Fixed Deposit** karwate hain."

Bank ke manager uncle ne samjhaya, "Beta, aap hamein ₹10,000 do. Hum is paise ko apne paas rakhenge aur use kaam mein lagayenge. Iske badle hum aapko har saal kuch **extra paise** denge. Is extra ko **byaaj** kehte hain. Maan lo hum saal ke ₹100 par ₹10 denge."

Mannat ne turant hisaab lagaya: "Yaani pehle saal ₹10,000 ke ₹1,000 extra. Wah!"

Uncle muskuraye, "Ab asli mazedaar baat sunna. Agle saal bank do tarah se hisaab kar sakta hai.

**Tareeka 1:** Hum tumhe har saal sirf **pehle wale ₹10,000** par byaaj denge. Har saal wahi ₹1,000.

**Tareeka 2:** Pehle saal ka ₹1,000 byaaj bhi tumhare paise mein jod denge. Ab agle saal bank tumhe **₹11,000** par byaaj dega. Yaani jo byaaj mila, wo bhi aage kamayega."

Mannat ki aankhein chamak gayi. "Dusra tareeka to **byaaj par byaaj** ho gaya! Wo zyada acha hai na?"

"Bilkul," uncle ne kaha. "Pehla tareeka **Simple Interest** hai. Dusra **Compound Interest**. Aaj tum dono ka farak samjho. Ye farak lambe waqt mein bahut bada ho jata hai."

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Principal (P)** | Jo asli paisa aap bank mein rakhte ho ya udhaar lete ho | Mannat ke ₹10,000 |
| **Interest (byaaj)** | Paise ko use karne ke badle diya jaane wala extra paisa | Pehle saal ke ₹1,000 |
| **Rate (r % per annum)** | Har ₹100 par ek saal mein kitna byaaj milega | 10% per annum: har ₹100 par ₹10 saal ka |
| **Per annum** | "Har saal". Latin shabd hai, matlab "ek saal mein" | 10% p.a. = 10% har saal |
| **Time (n ya T)** | Kitne saal ke liye paisa rakha ya liya | 3 saal |
| **Amount (A)** | Aakhir mein milne wala poora paisa: **A = P + I** | ₹10,000 + ₹3,000 = ₹13,000 |
| **Simple Interest (SI)** | Byaaj sirf **asli principal** par lagta hai | Har saal wahi ₹1,000 |
| **Compound Interest (CI)** | Byaaj **principal aur ab tak ke jude hue byaaj** par lagta hai | Byaaj par byaaj |

Yahan "I" ka matlab Interest hai. Jab hum seedha "interest" kehte hain, to wo sirf **extra kamaayi** hai, poora paisa nahi. Poora paisa **Amount** hai.

## Part A: Simple Interest ka Recap (pehle ki class ki yaad)

> Ye aap pehle ki class mein padh chuke ho. Hum use sirf taaza kar rahe hain.

Simple Interest mein byaaj **hamesha asli principal** par hi lagta hai, chahe kitne bhi saal beet jayein.

**Formula:**

**SI = (P × R × T) / 100**

Yahan P = principal, R = rate (% per annum), T = time (saalon mein). (Aage ke topics mein rate ko chhote **r** aur saalon ko **n** se likhenge, matlab wahi hai.)

Ye kyun kaam karta hai? R% ka matlab "har ₹100 par ₹R ek saal mein". To ₹P par ek saal ka byaaj = P × R / 100. Aur T saal ke liye, wahi byaaj T baar milega, isliye T se guna.

Aur **Amount = P + SI**.

### Solved examples

**Example 1:** ₹5,000 ko 8% per annum par 3 saal ke liye rakha. SI aur Amount batao.

- SI = (5000 × 8 × 3) / 100 = 1,20,000 / 100 = **₹1,200**
- Amount = 5,000 + 1,200 = **₹6,200**

**Jaanch:** Ek saal ka byaaj 5,000 ka 8% = ₹400. Teen saal ka = 400 × 3 = ₹1,200 ✅

**Example 2:** ₹12,000 par 5% per annum se 2 saal ka SI aur Amount.

- SI = (12000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 1,20,000 / 100 = **₹1,200**
- Amount = 12,000 + 1,200 = **₹13,200**

**Jaanch:** 12,000 ka 5% = ₹600 saal ka. Do saal = ₹1,200 ✅

**Example 3:** ₹2,00,000 par 6% per annum se 4 saal ka SI aur Amount.

- SI = (2,00,000 × 6 × 4) / 100 = 48,00,000 / 100 = **₹48,000**
- Amount = 2,00,000 + 48,000 = **₹2,48,000**

**Jaanch:** 2,00,000 ka 6% = ₹12,000 saal ka. Chaar saal = ₹48,000 ✅

### Simple Interest ki ek khaas baat: har saal ek jaisa

Mannat ke ₹10,000 par 10% SI lagaao, 3 saal ke liye:

| Saal | Principal jis par byaaj laga | Us saal ka byaaj |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ₹10,000 | ₹1,000 |
| 2 | ₹10,000 | ₹1,000 |
| 3 | ₹10,000 | ₹1,000 |
| **Kul** | | **₹3,000** |

Har saal byaaj **barabar** hai, kyunki principal har saal wahi ₹10,000 raha. Isliye graph banao to seedhi line (har saal barabar badhna) banti hai.

**Jaanch:** SI = (10,000 × 10 × 3) / 100 = ₹3,000 ✅. Amount = ₹13,000.

## Part B: Compound Interest ka vichaar

Ab Mannat ke uncle wala **Tareeka 2** dekhte hain.

Compound Interest mein ek **period** (abhi hum maan lete hain ki ek period = ek saal) ke ant mein jo byaaj bana, use **principal mein jod diya jata hai**. Agle saal ka byaaj is **bade hue principal** par lagta hai.

Isko ek chhote nakshe se samjho:

> Saal 1 ka principal → byaaj bana → principal mein jud gaya → **Saal 2 ka principal bada** → ab usse bhi bada byaaj bana → phir jud gaya → **Saal 3 ka principal aur bada** ...

Yahi hai **"byaaj par byaaj"**. Do tarah ki kamaayi ho rahi hai: asli paise par byaaj, aur ab tak ke byaaj par bhi byaaj.

### Side-by-side: ₹10,000, 10% per annum, 3 saal

Ye numbers abhi **dekhne** ke liye hain, taaki farak samajh aaye. Calculation ka tareeka Topic 2 mein dhyan se seekhenge.

| Saal | **Simple Interest** | | **Compound Interest** | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | Principal | Byaaj | Principal | Byaaj |
| 1 | ₹10,000 | ₹1,000 | ₹10,000 | ₹1,000 |
| 2 | ₹10,000 | ₹1,000 | ₹11,000 | ₹1,100 |
| 3 | ₹10,000 | ₹1,000 | ₹12,100 | ₹1,210 |
| **Kul byaaj** | | **₹3,000** | | **₹3,310** |
| **Amount** | | **₹13,000** | | **₹13,310** |

Dhyan se dekho:

- **Pehle saal mein dono barabar hain** (₹1,000). Kyunki pehle saal mein byaaj par byaaj banne ka mauka hi nahi mila.
- **Dusre saal se farak shuru** hota hai. CI mein principal ₹11,000 ho gaya, to byaaj ₹1,100.
- Teesre saal tak CI wala byaaj ₹1,210 ho gaya, aur SI wala ₹1,000 hi raha.
- Teen saal mein CI ne SI se **₹310 zyada** kamaya (3,310 − 3,000).

**Jaanch:** SI ka kul = 1,000 + 1,000 + 1,000 = 3,000 ✅. CI ka kul = 1,000 + 1,100 + 1,210 = 3,310 ✅. Farak = 3,310 − 3,000 = 310 ✅. Amount (CI) = 10,000 + 3,310 = 13,310 ✅

### Ye farak badhta kyun jata hai?

SI mein byaaj har saal **ek jaisa** rehta hai. CI mein byaaj har saal **pichhle saal se bada** hota hai, kyunki principal bada hota ja raha hai. Jaise ek chhoti si snowball ludhakte hue bade se bada hoti jaye, waise paisa bhi badhta jata hai.

## Part C: Asli zindagi mein Compound Interest kahan hota hai?

- **Bank Fixed Deposit (FD):** Mannat wali kahani. Bank aapka byaaj bhi jodta rehta hai, to aap byaaj par byaaj kamate ho.
- **Savings ki growth:** Paisa kuch saal tak bank ya aisi jagah chhod do jahan byaaj judta rahe, to badhne ki raftaar apne aap tez hoti jati hai.
- **Loan:** Jab aap bank se loan lete ho aur samay par nahi chukate, to kai baar baaki bacha hua byaaj bhi principal ki tarah gina jata hai. Isliye loan ko jaldi chukana samajhdari hai.
- **Credit card (halki jhalak):** Agar bill poora samay par nahi bhara, to jo baaki rakam bachti hai us par byaaj lagta hai, aur agle mahine us byaaj par bhi. Isliye credit card ka bill hamesha samay par bharna chahiye.

Dhyan rakho: har bank ya loan ke apne niyam hote hain, aur kai jagah byaaj **saal mein ek baar** ki jagah **chhote chhote period** mein bhi judta hai. Wo sab hum aage ke topics mein dekhenge. Abhi bas ye vichaar pakka karo ki **byaaj judkar aage kamayega**.

## Part D: Chhota rate, lamba waqt: bada fark

CI ki asli taakat lambe waqt mein dikhti hai.

Mannat ke ₹10,000 par 10% per annum:

- **Simple Interest** mein har saal ₹1,000 judta hai. Paisa dugna (₹20,000) hone mein **10 saal** lagenge. (Kyunki 10 saal × ₹1,000 = ₹10,000 byaaj, aur 10,000 + 10,000 = 20,000.)
- **Compound Interest** mein paisa dugna hone mein lagbhag **7 se 8 saal** lagte hain. Yaani **SI se kuch saal pehle**.

> **Dhyan do:** ye "7 se 8 saal" ek **andaaza (approximate)** hai. Ye dikhane ke liye diya hai ki CI tezi se badhta hai. Pakka hisaab hum Topic 3 mein seekhenge, jab hamare paas ek jaldi wala formula aa jayega.

Uske baad bhi har agle 7 se 8 saal mein paisa phir dugna hota jata hai (ye bhi andaaza hai). SI mein har baar ₹10,000 judne mein hamesha 10 saal hi lagte hain. Isiliye samajhdar log kehte hain: **"Paisa jaldi shuru karo, aur use lamba waqt do."**

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "SI aur CI dono mein pehle saal ka byaaj alag hota hai."**
Nahi. Pehle saal mein dono **barabar** hote hain. ₹10,000 par 10% dono mein ₹1,000. Farak dusre saal se shuru hota hai.

**Galti 2: "Interest aur Amount ek hi cheez hain."**
Interest sirf **extra kamaayi** hai (jaise ₹3,310). Amount **poora milne wala paisa** hai (₹13,310). Amount = P + Interest.

**Galti 3: "CI mein rate badh jata hai."**
Nahi. Rate (10%) wahi rehta hai. Badhta hai **principal jis par byaaj lagta hai**.

**Galti 4: "CI ka matlab hamesha SI se bahut zyada."**
Chhote waqt (jaise 1 saal) mein dono **barabar** hote hain. Farak tab dikhta hai jab waqt lamba ho.

**Galti 5: SI mein 100 se bhaag dena bhool jana.**
SI = P × R × T / 100. Example 1 mein 5000 × 8 × 3 = 1,20,000 aata hai, par SI ₹1,200 hai, kyunki 100 se bhaag dena hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Principal (P)** = asli paisa. **Rate (R%)** = har ₹100 par ek saal ka byaaj. **Time (T)** = saal. **Amount = P + Interest**.
- **Per annum** = har saal.
- **Simple Interest:** SI = P × R × T / 100. Byaaj sirf asli principal par lagta hai, aur **har saal ek jaisa** rehta hai.
- **Compound Interest:** har period ke ant mein byaaj principal mein jud jata hai, to agle period ka principal bada hota hai. Yaani **byaaj par byaaj**.
- ₹10,000, 10%, 3 saal: SI = ₹3,000, CI = ₹3,310. Pehle saal dono barabar, uske baad CI aage nikal jata hai.
- CI bank FD, savings aur loans mein hota hai. Credit card ke baaki bill par bhi kuch aisa hi hota hai.
- Chhote rate par bhi lamba waqt CI ko bahut bada bana deta hai (lagbhag 7 se 8 saal mein dugna, ye andaaza hai).

## Practice Questions

1. Find the Simple Interest and the Amount on ₹8,000 for 4 years at 5% per annum.

2. A sum of ₹15,000 is invested at 6% per annum simple interest for 2 years. How much interest is earned each year? What is the total interest?

3. A man earns ₹2,400 as simple interest on ₹20,000 in 3 years. Find the rate of interest per annum.

4. Anaya deposits ₹5,000 at 10% per annum. Without using any formula, write the interest for the first year and the second year (a) under simple interest and (b) if the first year's interest is added to the principal (compound interest). Which is bigger in the second year?

5. State whether each statement is true or false, and give a reason:
   (a) In simple interest, the interest earned is different every year.
   (b) In the first year, simple interest and compound interest on the same sum at the same rate are equal.
   (c) Amount = Principal + Interest.
   (d) In compound interest, the rate of interest changes every year.

6. In your own words, explain what "interest on interest" means. Why does it make compound interest grow faster than simple interest over a long time?

7. At 10% per annum simple interest, how many years will it take for ₹10,000 to earn ₹10,000 as interest (so that the amount becomes ₹20,000)?

8. Give two real-life situations where compound interest is useful, and one situation where it can hurt a person.

### Answers

1. SI = (8,000 × 5 × 4) / 100 = 1,60,000 / 100 = **₹1,600**.
   Amount = 8,000 + 1,600 = **₹9,600**.
   Jaanch: 8,000 ka 5% = ₹400 saal ka. Chaar saal = ₹1,600 ✅

2. Ek saal ka byaaj = 15,000 ka 6% = (15,000 × 6) / 100 = **₹900**.
   Do saal ka kul byaaj = 900 × 2 = **₹1,800**.
   Jaanch: (15,000 × 6 × 2) / 100 = 1,80,000 / 100 = 1,800 ✅

3. SI = P × R × T / 100, isliye R = (SI × 100) / (P × T) = (2,400 × 100) / (20,000 × 3) = 2,40,000 / 60,000 = **4%** per annum.
   Jaanch: (20,000 × 4 × 3) / 100 = 2,40,000 / 100 = 2,400 ✅

4. (a) Simple interest: pehle saal 5,000 ka 10% = **₹500**. Dusre saal bhi **₹500** (principal wahi 5,000 hai).
   (b) Compound: pehle saal **₹500**. Ye jud gaya to principal ho gaya 5,000 + 500 = ₹5,500. Dusre saal byaaj = 5,500 ka 10% = **₹550**.
   Dusre saal mein **compound interest bada** hai (₹550 vs ₹500).
   Jaanch: 5,500 ka 10% = 550 ✅

5. (a) **False ❌.** Simple interest har saal ek jaisa hota hai, kyunki principal wahi rehta hai.
   (b) **True ✅.** Pehle saal mein byaaj par byaaj banne ka mauka nahi hota.
   (c) **True ✅.** Amount poora milne wala paisa hai: principal aur byaaj ko jodkar.
   (d) **False ❌.** Rate wahi rehta hai. Badhta hai principal jis par byaaj lagta hai.

6. "Byaaj par byaaj" ka matlab hai ki jo byaaj pehle mil chuka, wo bhi principal ban kar aage kamayega. Isse har saal principal bada hota hai, to har saal ka byaaj bhi bada hota hai. SI mein byaaj har saal ek jaisa rehta hai. Isliye lambe waqt mein CI ka byaaj SI se kaafi aage nikal jata hai.

7. Har saal ka SI = 10,000 ka 10% = ₹1,000. ₹10,000 byaaj ke liye 10,000 / 1,000 = **10 saal**.
   Jaanch: (10,000 × 10 × 10) / 100 = 10,00,000 / 100 = 10,000 ✅

8. Jahan kaam aata hai: bank Fixed Deposit (paisa badhta hai) aur lambe waqt ki savings. Jahan nuksaan karta hai: samay par na chukaya gaya loan, ya credit card ka baaki bill, kyunki byaaj par byaaj lagta rehta hai aur karza badhta jata hai. (Koi bhi do achhe aur ek nuksaan wala example sahi hai.)
