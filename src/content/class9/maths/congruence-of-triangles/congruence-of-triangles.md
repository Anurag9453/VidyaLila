# Topic 1: Congruent Figures, Congruent Triangles aur CPCT

[Triangles chapter](../triangles/triangles.md) mein humne triangle ke 6 parts (3 sides, 3 angles) dekhe the, aur ye bhi seekha tha ki kaunsi side kis angle ke **saamne** hoti hai. Wahan do vaade bhi adhoore chhode the: isosceles triangle ke base angles barabar kyun hote hain, aur equilateral ke teeno angles barabar kyun hote hain. In dono ka proof is chapter mein aayega.

Par proof tak pahunchne se pehle ek naya shabd seekhna padega: **congruent**. Is topic mein hum dekhenge ki do cheezein "bilkul ek jaisi" kab kehlati hain, do triangles ko "bilkul ek jaisa" kaise likhte hain, aur isse kya fayda hota hai.

---

## Duplicate chaabi ki kahani

Aman ke ghar ki ek hi chaabi thi. Kahin kho na jaye, isliye mummy ne kaha, "Jaa, iski ek duplicate banwa la."

Aman chaabi wale bhaiya ke paas gaya. Bhaiya ne purani chaabi ek machine mein lagayi, ek khali chaabi doosri taraf lagayi, aur machine ne bilkul waise hi daant kaat diye.

Ghar aakar Aman ne nayi chaabi taale mein daali. Taala khul gaya.

Ab socho, nayi chaabi kab kaam karti?
- Agar daanton ka **shape** same ho, par chaabi thodi **badi ya chhoti** ho, to taale mein ghusegi hi nahi.
- Agar size same ho par ek bhi daant ka **shape** alag ho, to taala nahi khulega.

Chaabi tabhi kaam karti hai jab wo **shape mein bhi same ho aur size mein bhi same ho**. Agar aap dono chaabiyan ek ke upar ek rakho, to wo ek doosre ko **poori tarah dhak leti hain**, ek bhi kinara bahar nahi nikalta.

Maths mein aisi do cheezon ko **congruent** kehte hain.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Congruent** | Shape bhi same, size bhi same. Ek ko doosre par rakho to poora dhak le | Ek hi machine se kati do chaabiyan |
| **≅** | "is congruent to" ka chinh | ΔABC ≅ ΔPQR |
| **Superposition** | Ek figure ko uthakar doosre ke upar rakhna, check karne ke liye ki poora dhakta hai ya nahi | Tracing paper par copy karke doosri figure par rakhna |
| **Corresponding parts** | Do congruent figures mein wo parts jo ek doosre ke upar aate hain | ΔABC ≅ ΔPQR mein A aur P |
| **Correspondence** | Kaunsa vertex kis vertex se match karta hai, uski jodi | A ↔ P, B ↔ Q, C ↔ R |
| **CPCT** | Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles (are equal) | Triangles congruent hain, to AB = PQ |

## Part A: Congruent figures

### Kya hota hai congruent?

> Do figures **congruent** hain agar unka **shape bhi same** ho aur **size bhi same** ho.

Check karne ka tarika: **superposition**. Ek figure ko tracing paper par copy karo, aur us copy ko doosri figure ke upar rakho. Agar dono **poori tarah ek doosre ko dhak lein**, to dono congruent hain.

Euclid chapter ke [Topic 1](../euclids-geometry/euclids-geometry.md) mein Axiom 4 dekha tha: "jo cheezein ek doosre se poori tarah mil jaati hain (coincide karti hain), wo barabar hoti hain." Superposition yahi kaam karta hai.

### Roz ki zindagi mein congruent cheezein

- Ek hi saal ke do **₹5 ke sikke**
- Ek hi saanche se bane do **Parle-G biscuit**
- Ek hi notebook ke do **pages**
- Ek hi stencil se bani do **rangoli designs**
- Do **photocopies** ek hi page ki (100% par)

### Kuch figures ko kab congruent kehte hain

**Line segments:** Do line segments congruent hain jab unki **lambai barabar** ho.

```
A ________ B        P ________ Q
    5 cm                5 cm
```

AB = 5 cm aur PQ = 5 cm, to **AB ≅ PQ**. (Ek ko doosre par rakho, poora dhak lega.)

**Angles:** Do angles congruent hain jab unka **naap (measure) barabar** ho. Arms kitni bhi lambi ho, farak nahi padta.

```
     /                        /
    /                        /
   / 40°                    / 40°
  /_______                 /_____________
```

Dono 40° ke hain, to **congruent** hain. Arms ki lambai alag hai, par angle ka size wahi hai.

**Circles:** Do circles congruent hain jab unki **radius barabar** ho.

**Squares:** Do squares congruent hain jab unki **sides barabar** hon.

| Figure | Congruent kab? |
|---|---|
| Line segment | Lambai barabar |
| Angle | Degree barabar |
| Circle | Radius barabar |
| Square | Side barabar |
| Rectangle | Length aur breadth dono barabar |

### Ulta karna (flip) bhi allowed hai

Agar ek figure doosri ki **aaina wali (mirror) copy** ho, to tracing paper ko **palat kar** (flip karke) rakh sakte ho. Agar palatne ke baad poora dhak le, to bhi wo **congruent** hai.

Jaise aapka left haath ka dastana aur right haath ka dastana. Ek ko palat do to doosre jaisa ban jata hai.

### Same shape, alag size: congruent nahi

```
   /\              /\
  /  \            /  \
 /____\          /    \
                /      \
  chhota       /________\
                  bada
```

Dono triangles ka **shape same** hai (jaise ek photo aur uski badi print), par **size alag** hai. Aisi figures ko **similar** kehte hain, congruent nahi. (Similar figures ki poori baat aage ki classes mein aayegi.)

Yaad rakho:
- **Congruent:** shape same + size same
- **Similar:** sirf shape same

Har congruent figure similar bhi hoti hai, par har similar figure congruent nahi hoti.

## Part B: Congruent triangles

### Kya matlab hai?

Do triangles **congruent** hain agar ek ko uthakar doosre ke upar rakho to wo **poori tarah dhak le**. Matlab:
- Teeno **sides** ek doosre ke upar aa jaati hain, aur barabar hain.
- Teeno **angles** ek doosre ke upar aa jaate hain, aur barabar hain.

Yaani do congruent triangles ke **6 parts** (3 sides + 3 angles) ek ke baad ek barabar hote hain.

### Example

```
          A                             P
         /\                            /\
        /  \                          /  \
  4.6  /    \ 5.6               4.6  /    \ 5.6
      /      \                      /      \
     /________\                    /________\
    B    5.2   C                  Q    5.2   R
```

(Sides cm mein, lagbhag. Angles: ∠A = 60°, ∠B = 70°, ∠C = 50°, aur ∠P = 60°, ∠Q = 70°, ∠R = 50°.)

Agar ΔABC ko uthakar ΔPQR par rakhein, to:
- A, P ke upar aata hai
- B, Q ke upar aata hai
- C, R ke upar aata hai

Ise **correspondence** kehte hain, aur likhte hain:

**A ↔ P, B ↔ Q, C ↔ R**

Aur congruence aise likhte hain:

**ΔABC ≅ ΔPQR**

Padhte hain: "triangle ABC is congruent to triangle PQR."

### Order kyun itna zaroori hai?

Ye is topic ki **sabse zaroori** baat hai.

**ΔABC ≅ ΔPQR** likhne ka matlab hai: **pehla letter pehle se, doosra doosre se, teesra teesre se** match karta hai.

```
Δ  A  B  C
   ↕  ↕  ↕
Δ  P  Q  R
```

Isi likhawat se hum seedha 6 barabar parts padh sakte hain, bina figure dekhe.

**Sides** (do do letters ki jodi, same position par):

| ΔABC mein | ΔPQR mein | Kyun? |
|---|---|---|
| AB | PQ | 1st-2nd letter dono taraf |
| BC | QR | 2nd-3rd letter dono taraf |
| CA | RP | 3rd-1st letter dono taraf |

**Angles** (ek ek letter, same position par):

| ΔABC mein | ΔPQR mein |
|---|---|
| ∠A | ∠P |
| ∠B | ∠Q |
| ∠C | ∠R |

To ΔABC ≅ ΔPQR se ye 6 baatein nikalti hain:

**AB = PQ, BC = QR, CA = RP, ∠A = ∠P, ∠B = ∠Q, ∠C = ∠R**

### Galat order likha to kya hoga?

Upar wale figure ke liye koi galti se likh de: **ΔABC ≅ ΔQPR**.

Is likhawat ke hisaab se:
- A ↔ Q, matlab ∠A = ∠Q. Par ∠A = 60° aur ∠Q = 70°. **Galat!**
- AB = QP. Ye to sahi hai (4.6 = 4.6), par
- BC = PR, matlab 5.2 = 5.6. **Galat!**

To ek galat order poori statement ko galat bana deta hai, aur usse nikli saari baatein bhi galat ho jaati hain. Isliye congruence likhte waqt **figure dekh kar sahi jodi (correspondence) pehle tay karo**, phir likho.

### Ek hi baat ke kai sahi roop

Agar ΔABC ≅ ΔPQR sahi hai, to ye bhi sahi hain (kyunki jodiyan wahi rehti hain, bas dono taraf ka order ek saath badla hai):

- ΔBCA ≅ ΔQRP
- ΔCAB ≅ ΔRPQ
- ΔACB ≅ ΔPRQ
- ΔPQR ≅ ΔABC

Rule: **dono taraf ke letters ko ek saath, ek hi tarah se ghumao.** Jodi (A↔P, B↔Q, C↔R) nahi tootni chahiye.

Par **ΔABC ≅ ΔQPR** galat hai, kyunki isme A ki jodi Q se ban gayi.

## Part C: Figure dekh kar congruence statement likhna

### Tarika (3 kadam)

1. **Barabar angles** ki jodi dhundho. (Ya barabar sides ki.)
2. Har jodi ko likho: kaunsa vertex kis vertex se match karta hai.
3. Ek triangle ke letters kisi bhi order mein likho, aur doosre triangle ke letters **usi jodi ke hisaab se** likho.

### Example 1

ΔABC aur ΔXYZ mein diya hai:
∠A = ∠Z, ∠B = ∠X, ∠C = ∠Y,
AB = ZX, BC = XY, CA = YZ.

**Kadam 1-2:** Jodiyan: A ↔ Z, B ↔ X, C ↔ Y.

**Kadam 3:** ΔABC likha, to doosra triangle A, B, C ki jodiyon ke kram mein: Z, X, Y.

**ΔABC ≅ ΔZXY**

**Jaanch:** Sides check karo.
- AB ↔ ZX ✅ (diya hai AB = ZX)
- BC ↔ XY ✅
- CA ↔ YZ ✅

### Example 2 (sides se)

ΔDEF aur ΔLMN mein: DE = MN, EF = NL, FD = LM.

Kaunsa vertex kis se?
- DE aur EF dono mein **E** common hai. MN aur NL dono mein **N** common hai. To **E ↔ N**.
- DE aur FD mein **D** common hai. MN aur LM mein **M** common hai. To **D ↔ M**.
- Bacha F ↔ L.

**ΔDEF ≅ ΔMNL**

**Jaanch:** DE ↔ MN ✅, EF ↔ NL ✅, FD ↔ LM ✅

Trick: **do barabar sides ka common vertex, doosre triangle ki do barabar sides ke common vertex se match karta hai.**

### Example 3 (do triangles ek hi figure mein)

```
        A
       /|\
      / | \
     /  |  \
    /   |   \
   B----D----C
```

Maan lo ΔABD ≅ ΔACD diya hai.

Jodiyan: A ↔ A, B ↔ C, D ↔ D.

Is se ye nikalta hai:
- AB = AC (1st-2nd)
- BD = CD (2nd-3rd)
- AD = AD (3rd-1st, ye to common side hai)
- ∠BAD = ∠CAD (A wale angles)
- ∠ABD = ∠ACD (B ↔ C)
- ∠ADB = ∠ADC (D wale angles)

Dhyan do, jab ek hi vertex dono triangles mein ho (jaise A aur D), to angle ka naam teen letters se likho (∠BAD, ∠CAD), warna confusion hoga ki ∠A kaunsa hai.

## Part D: CPCT

### Kya hai CPCT?

**CPCT** = **C**orresponding **P**arts of **C**ongruent **T**riangles (are equal).

Hindi mein: **agar do triangles congruent hain, to unke corresponding parts barabar hote hain.**

Upar Part B mein humne yahi kiya tha: ΔABC ≅ ΔPQR se AB = PQ, ∠A = ∠P wagairah nikale. Jab hum ye baat **proof mein reason** ki tarah likhte hain, to use **"CPCT"** likhte hain.

### Ye itna kaam ka kyun hai?

Geometry ke bahut saare sawaal aise hote hain: "Prove karo ki ye do sides barabar hain" ya "ye do angles barabar hain."

Iska ek pakka tarika hai:
1. Wo do sides (ya angles) **do triangles** mein dhundho.
2. Prove karo ki wo do triangles **congruent** hain.
3. Phir **CPCT** se bolo ki wo sides (ya angles) barabar hain.

Step 2 (triangles ko congruent prove karna) hum Topic 2 mein seekhenge. Is topic mein hum maan ke chalenge ki congruence **pehle se di hui** hai, aur CPCT use karna seekhenge.

### Example 1: Seedha CPCT

ΔABC ≅ ΔDEF hai. AB = 4 cm, BC = 6 cm, ∠E = 65°. DE, EF aur ∠B nikalo.

Jodiyan: A ↔ D, B ↔ E, C ↔ F.

- DE = AB (CPCT) = **4 cm**
- EF = BC (CPCT) = **6 cm**
- ∠B = ∠E (CPCT) = **65°**

### Example 2: Proof mein CPCT

**Given:** ΔPQR ≅ ΔSTU
**To prove:** QR = TU aur ∠R = ∠U

| Statement | Reason |
|---|---|
| ΔPQR ≅ ΔSTU | Given |
| P ↔ S, Q ↔ T, R ↔ U | Congruence statement ka order |
| QR = TU | CPCT (2nd-3rd letter) |
| ∠R = ∠U | CPCT (3rd letter) |

**Hence proved.**

(Proof ka Given / To prove / Statement-Reason wala format [Euclid chapter ke Topic 4](../euclids-geometry/euclids-geometry-part4.md) mein dekha tha.)

### Example 3: Ek figure mein CPCT

Do line segments AC aur BD ek point O par kat-te hain, aur ΔAOB ≅ ΔCOD diya hai.

```
  A                 B
   \               /
    \             /
     \           /
      \         /
         O
      /         \
     /           \
    /             \
   D               C
```

Diya hai: ΔAOB ≅ ΔCOD. **Prove karo AB = CD.**

| Statement | Reason |
|---|---|
| ΔAOB ≅ ΔCOD | Given |
| A ↔ C, O ↔ O, B ↔ D | Congruence ka order |
| AB = CD | CPCT (1st-3rd letter: A-B aur C-D) |

**Hence proved.**

Aur isi se ∠OAB = ∠OCD bhi nikalta hai (CPCT, A ↔ C).

### Dhyan rakho: CPCT kab use nahi kar sakte

CPCT tabhi lagao jab **triangles ka congruent hona pehle pakka ho**. Agar congruence prove hi nahi hua, aur aapne CPCT laga diya, to proof galat hai. Pehle congruence, phir CPCT.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Order galat likhna**
Figure mein A ↔ P, B ↔ R, C ↔ Q ho, aur aap likho ΔABC ≅ ΔPQR. Galat! Sahi hai **ΔABC ≅ ΔPRQ**. Pehle jodiyan tay karo, phir likho.

**Galti 2: ≅ ki jagah = likhna**
ΔABC = ΔPQR mat likho. "=" numbers ke liye hai (jaise AB = PQ). Figures ke liye **≅** likho.

**Galti 3: Sirf shape same dekh kar congruent bol dena**
Ek chhota aur ek bada triangle, jinka shape same hai, **similar** hain, congruent nahi. Size bhi same hona chahiye.

**Galti 4: Barabar area ko congruent samajhna**
2 cm × 8 cm ka rectangle aur 4 cm × 4 cm ka square, dono ka area 16 cm² hai. Par ek doosre ko dhakte nahi. To **barabar area se congruent nahi hota**. (Euclid chapter ke Topic 1 mein bhi yahi example tha: Axiom 4 ka ulta sach nahi hota.)

Ulta zaroor sach hai: **congruent figures ka area hamesha barabar hota hai.**

**Galti 5: Barabar perimeter ko congruent samajhna**
3, 4, 5 cm wale triangle ka perimeter 12 cm hai. 4, 4, 4 cm wale ka bhi 12 cm. Par dono alag triangles hain, congruent nahi.

**Galti 6: Congruence prove kiye bina CPCT lagana**
CPCT ek nateeja (result) hai, shuruaat nahi. Pehle triangles congruent prove karo (Topic 2), phir CPCT lagao.

**Galti 7: Common vertex wale angle ko ek letter se likhna**
ΔABD aur ΔACD mein ∠A likhne se pata nahi chalta kaunsa angle. ∠BAD aur ∠CAD likho.

## Quick Recap

- **Congruent** = shape same + size same. Check karne ka tarika: **superposition** (ek ko doosre par rakho, poora dhak le).
- Line segments: lambai barabar. Angles: degree barabar. Circles: radius barabar. Squares: side barabar.
- Flip karke dhak le to bhi congruent hai.
- **Similar** = sirf shape same. Har congruent figure similar hai, par har similar figure congruent nahi.
- **ΔABC ≅ ΔPQR** ka matlab: A ↔ P, B ↔ Q, C ↔ R. **Order sabse zaroori hai.**
- Is se 6 barabar parts nikalte hain: AB = PQ, BC = QR, CA = RP, ∠A = ∠P, ∠B = ∠Q, ∠C = ∠R.
- Dono taraf ka order ek saath badal sakte ho (ΔBCA ≅ ΔQRP), par jodi nahi todni.
- **CPCT:** congruent triangles ke corresponding parts barabar hote hain. Proof mein reason ki tarah likhte hain.
- Pehle congruence, phir CPCT.
- Barabar area ya barabar perimeter se congruence nahi aata.

## Practice Questions

1. State whether the figures in each pair are congruent. Give a reason.
   (a) Two circles, each of radius 3 cm
   (b) Two squares of sides 4 cm and 5 cm
   (c) Two line segments, each 7 cm long
   (d) Two angles, each measuring 45°
   (e) Two equilateral triangles, each of side 6 cm

2. If ΔABC ≅ ΔLMN, write all six pairs of equal corresponding parts.

3. ΔPQR ≅ ΔXYZ. If PQ = 6 cm, QR = 8 cm and ∠R = 40°, find XY, YZ and ∠Z.

4. In ΔABC and ΔDEF, AB = EF, BC = FD, CA = DE, ∠A = ∠E, ∠B = ∠F and ∠C = ∠D. Write the correct congruence statement.

5. If ΔABC ≅ ΔPQR, state whether each of the following is true or false:
   (a) ΔBAC ≅ ΔQPR
   (b) ΔABC ≅ ΔQPR
   (c) ΔCBA ≅ ΔRQP

6. State whether each statement is true or false. Give a reason.
   (a) Two congruent figures have equal areas.
   (b) Two figures with equal areas are always congruent.
   (c) Two triangles with equal perimeters are always congruent.
   (d) All equilateral triangles are congruent.

7. Line segments AC and BD intersect at O, and ΔAOB ≅ ΔCOD. If AB = 5 cm and ∠OAB = 50°, find CD and ∠OCD.

8. In the figure, D is a point on BC such that ΔABD ≅ ΔACD. Prove that ∠BAD = ∠CAD (that is, AD bisects ∠BAC), and BD = CD.

```
        A
       /|\
      / | \
     /  |  \
    B---D---C
```

### Answers

1. (a) **Congruent.** Radius barabar hai.
   (b) **Congruent nahi.** Sides barabar nahi hain (shape same hai, size alag, to similar hain).
   (c) **Congruent.** Lambai barabar hai.
   (d) **Congruent.** Degree barabar hai.
   (e) **Congruent.** Equilateral triangle ki saari sides barabar hoti hain, aur dono ki side 6 cm hai, to teeno sides ek doosre se barabar hain aur dono ek doosre ko poora dhak lete hain.

2. Jodiyan: A ↔ L, B ↔ M, C ↔ N.
   **AB = LM, BC = MN, CA = NL, ∠A = ∠L, ∠B = ∠M, ∠C = ∠N**

3. Jodiyan: P ↔ X, Q ↔ Y, R ↔ Z.
   XY = PQ = **6 cm** (CPCT)
   YZ = QR = **8 cm** (CPCT)
   ∠Z = ∠R = **40°** (CPCT)

4. Angles se jodiyan: A ↔ E, B ↔ F, C ↔ D.
   **ΔABC ≅ ΔEFD**
   Jaanch: AB ↔ EF ✅, BC ↔ FD ✅, CA ↔ DE ✅

5. Jodiyan: A ↔ P, B ↔ Q, C ↔ R.
   (a) **True.** B ↔ Q, A ↔ P, C ↔ R, jodiyan wahi hain ✅
   (b) **False.** Isme A ↔ Q ban gaya, jo galat jodi hai ❌
   (c) **True.** C ↔ R, B ↔ Q, A ↔ P, jodiyan wahi hain ✅

6. (a) **True.** Congruent figures ek doosre ko poora dhakti hain, to jagah (area) bhi utni hi gherti hain.
   (b) **False.** 2 cm × 8 cm rectangle aur 4 cm × 4 cm square, dono ka area 16 cm² hai, par congruent nahi.
   (c) **False.** 3, 4, 5 cm aur 4, 4, 4 cm wale triangles, dono ka perimeter 12 cm hai, par congruent nahi.
   (d) **False.** 3 cm side wala aur 5 cm side wala equilateral triangle, dono ka shape same hai, par size alag. Ye similar hain, congruent nahi.

7. Jodiyan: A ↔ C, O ↔ O, B ↔ D.
   CD = AB (CPCT, A-B ↔ C-D) = **5 cm**
   ∠OCD = ∠OAB (CPCT, A ↔ C) = **50°**

8. **Given:** ΔABD ≅ ΔACD
   **To prove:** ∠BAD = ∠CAD aur BD = CD

   | Statement | Reason |
   |---|---|
   | ΔABD ≅ ΔACD | Given |
   | A ↔ A, B ↔ C, D ↔ D | Congruence statement ka order |
   | ∠BAD = ∠CAD | CPCT (A wale angles) |
   | BD = CD | CPCT (2nd-3rd letter: B-D ↔ C-D) |

   **Hence proved.** Kyunki ∠BAD = ∠CAD, AD angle BAC ko do barabar hisson mein baant-ti hai, yaani AD, ∠BAC ki **bisector** hai.
