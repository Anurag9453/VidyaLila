# Topic 2: Angles Banana

Topic 1 mein humne seekha tha ki compass aur ruler se kya-kya ho sakta hai: segment banana, use copy karna, perpendicular bisector kheenchna aur segment ko barabar hisson mein baantna. Wahan sab kuch **lengths** ka khel tha, jaise Omkar ne rassi ka bilkul beech dhoondh kar lantern latkaya tha. Ab hum **angles** ki taraf chalte hain: koi angle kaise banayein jab protractor na ho, kisi angle ko kaise aadha karein, aur kisi angle ki hu-ba-hu copy kaise banayein. Aur sab kuch wahi do auzaar se: compass aur ruler.

---

## Inaya aur Rohan ke kagaz ke sajaavat

Diwali se pehle Inaya aur Rohan kagaz ki sajaavat bana rahe the: rang-birange kagaz ko fold karke sitare, jhaalar aur phoolon ke kone. Inaya ne kaha, "Mujhe is kone ka angle ekdum **60°** chahiye, warna sitara tedha banega."

Rohan ne geometry box kholi. Usme ruler tha, compass tha, par **protractor gum tha**.

"Ab kya karein?" Inaya ne poochha.

Rohan ne socha: "Humein ek aisi shape chahiye jiske teeno angle apne aap barabar hon. Aisi shape to **equilateral triangle** hai! Uske teeno sides barabar, to teeno angles bhi barabar, aur teeno ka jod 180°, to har angle 60°."

Inaya ki aankhein chamak gayi. "Matlab agar main compass se ek equilateral triangle bana loon, to uska ek kona hi 60° hoga!"

Phir Inaya ne ek aur sawaal kiya: "Aur agar mujhe **30°** chahiye?" Rohan ne turant kaha, "60° ko **aadha** kar do." Aur jab Rohan ko **45°** ka kona chahiye tha, to usne pehle 90° banaya aur use aadha kiya.

Is topic mein hum wahi sab seekhenge jo Inaya aur Rohan ne seekha: 60° kaise bante hain, aadha kaise karte hain, aur in dono ke jod-tod se 15°, 75°, 105°, 135° jaise angle kaise bante hain.

## Part A: Protractor se angle banana (chhota sa yaad)

Protractor wala tareeka sabse seedha hai, bas dhyan se padhna zaroori hai. Maan lo humein **70°** ka angle ∠ABC banana hai.

**Kadam 1:** Ruler se ek ray BC kheencho (B shuruaat ka point, C doosra point).

**Kadam 2:** Protractor ka **beech ka chhota nishaan (centre)** point B par rakho, aur protractor ki seedhi **baseline ko ray BC ke upar** rakho.

**Kadam 3:** Dekho ki ray BC kis taraf hai. Protractor par do scale hoti hain: ek **bahar ki (outer)** aur ek **andar ki (inner)**. Jis scale ka **0° ray BC wale kinare par** ho, usi scale ko padhna hai. Agar ray BC dayein taraf hai, to wo scale chuno jiska 0° dayein kinare par hai. Phir 0° se shuru karke **70° tak** ginti karo.

**Kadam 4:** 70° ke nishaan par pencil se ek chhota point A lagao.

**Kadam 5:** Ruler se B aur A ko jodo. Ray BA banti hai.

> **Figure 2.1** – Ray BC horizontal hai aur B par protractor se 70° naapkar point A lagaya gaya hai; ray BA se ∠ABC = 70° bana.

**Jaanch:** Protractor ko dobara B par rakho, 0° BC par, aur dekho ki BA 70° par hi aa rahi hai ✅. Aur agar dono scales mein se galat wali padh li, to 70° ki jagah 110° dikhega (70 + 110 = 180). Isliye pehle dekho ki ray ka 0° kis taraf hai.

Protractor ki ek kamzori hai: uska nishaan thoda mota hota hai, to angle thoda upar-neeche ho sakta hai. Compass ki constructions zyada **sahi** hoti hain. Aage hum protractor sirf **jaanch** ke liye use karenge.

## Part B: Angle ko aadha karna (angle bisector)

Maan lo humein ek angle ∠ABC milta hai (koi bhi, jaise 80°). Hum use do barabar hisson mein baantna chahte hain.

**Kadam 1:** B ko centre lekar, koi bhi radius (maan lo 4 cm) ka ek arc kheencho jo ray BA ko point P par aur ray BC ko point Q par kaate.

**Kadam 2:** P ko centre lekar, **thoda zyada** (PQ ke aadhe se zyada, maan lo 3 cm) radius ka ek arc kheencho, angle ke andar.

**Kadam 3:** Q ko centre lekar, **bilkul wahi radius (3 cm)** ka ek aur arc kheencho jo pichhle arc ko point X par kaate.

**Kadam 4:** Ruler se B aur X ko jodo aur ray BX banao. Ray BX hi ∠ABC ka bisector hai.

> **Figure 2.2** – ∠ABC ki arms BA aur BC par B centre ke arc se points P aur Q bane; P aur Q ke barabar-radius arcs X par mile aur ray BX ne ∠ABC ko ∠ABX aur ∠XBC mein baanta.

### Kyun kaam karta hai?

△BPX aur △BQX ko dekho:
- BP = BQ (dono B centre ke ek hi arc ke radius hain)
- PX = QX (dono arcs ka radius ek jaisa rakha tha)
- BX dono triangles mein **saanjhi** side hai

Teeno sides barabar hain, to **SSS** se △BPX ≅ △BQX. Congruent triangles ke corresponding angles barabar hote hain, isliye ∠PBX = ∠QBX. Ray BX ne angle ko aadha kar diya. (SSS wo criterion hai jo [congruence-of-triangles-part2.md](../congruence-of-triangles/congruence-of-triangles-part2.md) mein padha tha; yahan dobara nahi samjha rahe.)

**Jaanch:** Protractor se ∠ABC naapo (maan lo 80°), phir ∠ABX aur ∠XBC naapo. Dono 40° aane chahiye. 40 + 40 = 80 ✅.

## Part C: 60° banana (Inaya ka sitara)

Ray BC kheencho. Hum ∠QBC = 60° banayenge.

**Kadam 1:** Ruler se ek ray BC kheencho.

**Kadam 2:** B ko centre lekar, **4 cm** radius ka ek arc kheencho jo ray BC ko point P par kaate. Arc ko upar ki taraf kaafi door tak kheencho.

**Kadam 3:** Compass ki chaudai **4 cm hi rehne do**. Ab P ko centre lekar, **4 cm** radius ka ek arc kheencho jo pehle arc ko point Q par kaate.

**Kadam 4:** Ruler se B aur Q ko jodo. Ray BQ banti hai.

> **Figure 2.3** – Ray BC par B centre ka 4 cm arc P par kaatta hai, P centre ka 4 cm arc pehle arc ko Q par kaatta hai; ray BQ se ∠QBC = 60° bana aur △BPQ equilateral hai.

### Kyun 60° hi hota hai?

- BP = BQ = 4 cm (dono B centre ke arc ke radius)
- PQ = 4 cm (P centre ka arc, wahi radius)

△BPQ ke teeno sides barabar hain, yaani **equilateral triangle**. Equilateral triangle ke teeno angles barabar hote hain aur triangle ke teeno angles ka jod 180° hota hai (iske liye [triangles-part2.md](../triangles/triangles-part2.md) dekho). To har angle = 180° ÷ 3 = **60°**. Isliye ∠PBQ = 60°, aur P ray BC par hai, to ∠QBC = 60°.

**Jaanch:** Protractor se ∠QBC naapo: 60° aana chahiye. Aur ruler se BQ aur PQ naapo: dono 4 cm ✅.

## Part D: 30°, 120°, 90° aur 45°

Ab asli maza: ek angle se doosre angle.

### 30° (60° ko aadha karo)

**Kadam 1:** Figure 2.3 jaise: ray BC kheencho, B centre se 4 cm ka arc P par kaato, P centre se 4 cm ka arc Q par kaato.

**Kadam 2:** P ko centre lekar **3 cm** radius ka arc kheencho (angle ke andar).

**Kadam 3:** Q ko centre lekar **wahi 3 cm** radius ka arc kheencho jo pichhle arc ko point X par kaate. (3 cm, PQ = 4 cm ke aadhe, yaani 2 cm, se zyada hai, isliye arcs ek-doosre ko kaatenge.)

**Kadam 4:** B aur X ko jodo. Ray BX banti hai.

> **Figure 2.4** – ∠QBC = 60° ke arcs P aur Q se 3 cm ke barabar-radius arcs X par mile; ray BX ne 60° ko aadha kiya aur ∠XBC = 30° bana.

**Jaanch:** Part B ke hisaab se ray BX bisector hai, to ∠XBC = 60° ÷ 2 = 30° ✅. Protractor bhi 30° dikhayega.

### 120° (do 60° lagatar)

**Kadam 1:** Ray BC kheencho.

**Kadam 2:** B ko centre lekar **4 cm** ka arc kheencho jo BC ko P par kaate (arc ko kaafi lamba rakho, lagbhag aadha gol).

**Kadam 3:** P ko centre lekar **4 cm** ka arc kheencho jo pehle arc ko Q par kaate.

**Kadam 4:** Q ko centre lekar **4 cm** ka arc kheencho jo pehle arc ko R par kaate.

**Kadam 5:** B aur R ko jodo. Ray BR banti hai.

> **Figure 2.5** – B centre ke 4 cm arc par ray BC se P, phir P se Q, phir Q se R lage (har baar 4 cm radius); ray BR se ∠RBC = 120° bana.

**Kyun?** ∠PBQ = 60° aur ∠QBR = 60° (dono equilateral triangles ka kona). Dono saath-saath hain, to ∠RBC = 60° + 60° = **120°**.

### 90° (60° aur 120° ke beech ka bisector)

Ye standard tareeka hai. 60° aur 120°, dono ek saath banate hain, aur unke beech wale angle ko aadha karte hain.

**Kadam 1:** Ray BC kheencho. B centre se **4 cm** ka arc P par kaato.

**Kadam 2:** P centre se **4 cm** ka arc Q par kaato (arc pe ∠QBC = 60°).

**Kadam 3:** Q centre se **4 cm** ka arc R par kaato (∠RBC = 120°).

**Kadam 4:** Q ko centre lekar **3 cm** radius ka arc kheencho. Phir R ko centre lekar **wahi 3 cm** radius ka arc kheencho jo pichhle arc ko point S par kaate.

**Kadam 5:** B aur S ko jodo. Ray BS banti hai.

> **Figure 2.6** – B centre ke 4 cm arc par P, Q, R (ray BC se 0°, 60°, 120°) bane; Q aur R ke 3 cm arcs S par mile aur ray BS ne ∠QBR ko aadha karke ∠SBC = 90° banaya.

**Kyun 90° hi hai?** ∠QBR = 120° − 60° = 60°. Ray BS use aadha karti hai, to ∠QBS = 30°. Ab ∠SBC = ∠QBC + ∠QBS = 60° + 30° = **90°**.

**Jaanch:** Protractor se ∠SBC = 90° aana chahiye. Ya set-square ka seedha kona rakhkar dekho ✅.

### 45° (90° ko aadha karo)

**Kadam 1:** Upar wali 90° construction karo (Kadam 1 se Kadam 5). Ray BS arc ko point T par kaatti hai (BT = 4 cm).

**Kadam 2:** P ko centre lekar **4 cm** radius ka arc kheencho (angle ke andar).

**Kadam 3:** T ko centre lekar **wahi 4 cm** radius ka arc kheencho jo pichhle arc ko point U par kaate. (Ye dono arcs B par bhi milte hain; U wo doosra point hai jo B se door, angle ke andar hai. PT lagbhag 5.7 cm hai, uska aadha lagbhag 2.9 cm, aur 4 cm usse zyada hai.)

**Kadam 4:** B aur U ko jodo. Ray BU banti hai.

> **Figure 2.7** – 90° ki ray BS B centre ke 4 cm arc ko T par kaatti hai; P aur T ke 4 cm arcs U par mile aur ray BU ne ∠TBC ko aadha karke ∠UBC = 45° banaya.

**Kyun?** Ray BU, ∠SBC = 90° ka bisector hai, to ∠UBC = 90° ÷ 2 = **45°**.

**Jaanch:** Protractor se ∠UBC = 45° aana chahiye.

## Part E: Milakar bane angle (composite angles)

Ab tak humare paas 0°, 60°, 90°, 120° aur 180° (seedhi line) ki rays hain. In rays ke **beech** wale angles ko aadha karne se naye angles milte hain. Hum sab ko ek hi B centre ke 4 cm arc par banate hain.

### 15° (30° ko aadha karo)

**Kadam 1:** Ray BC kheencho. B centre se **4 cm** ka arc P par kaato.

**Kadam 2:** P centre se **4 cm** ka arc Q par kaato.

**Kadam 3:** P aur Q ko centre lekar **3 cm** radius ke arcs kheencho jo X par milein. Ray BX (30°) banao. Ray BX pehle arc ko point Y par kaatti hai.

**Kadam 4:** P ko centre lekar **3 cm** radius ka arc kheencho. Y ko centre lekar **wahi 3 cm** radius ka arc kheencho jo pichhle arc ko point Z par kaate. (PY lagbhag 2.1 cm hai, aadha lagbhag 1.05 cm, 3 cm usse bahut zyada hai.)

**Kadam 5:** B aur Z ko jodo. Ray BZ banti hai.

> **Figure 2.8** – 30° ki ray BX B centre ke 4 cm arc ko Y par kaatti hai; P aur Y ke 3 cm arcs Z par mile aur ray BZ se ∠ZBC = 15° bana.

**Kyun?** ∠XBC = 30°, aur BZ use aadha karti hai, to ∠ZBC = 30° ÷ 2 = **15°**.

### 75° (60° + 15°)

Idea: 60° wali ray aur 90° wali ray ke beech 30° ka gap hai (90 − 60 = 30). Use aadha karo to 15°. Phir 60° + 15° = 75°.

**Kadam 1:** Ray BC. B centre se **4 cm** ka arc P par kaato.

**Kadam 2:** P centre se **4 cm** ka arc Q par kaato (60°).

**Kadam 3:** Q centre se **4 cm** ka arc R par kaato (120°).

**Kadam 4:** Q aur R ko centre lekar **3 cm** radius ke arcs kheencho jo S par milein. Ray BS (90°) banao. Ray BS pehle arc ko T par kaatti hai.

**Kadam 5:** Q aur T ko centre lekar **3 cm** radius ke arcs kheencho jo point V par milein.

**Kadam 6:** B aur V ko jodo. Ray BV banti hai.

> **Figure 2.9** – B centre ke 4 cm arc par Q (60°) aur T (90°) bane; Q aur T ke 3 cm arcs V par mile aur ray BV se ∠VBC = 75° bana.

**Kyun?** ∠QBT = 90° − 60° = 30°. BV use aadha karti hai, to ∠QBV = 15°. ∠VBC = ∠QBC + ∠QBV = 60° + 15° = **75°**. (Doosra tareeka: 90° − 15° = 75°, wahi jawab.)

### 105° (90° + 15°)

Idea: 90° wali ray aur 120° wali ray ke beech 30° ka gap hai (120 − 90 = 30). Use aadha karo to 15°. Phir 90° + 15° = 105°.

**Kadam 1 se 4:** Upar 75° wale Kadam 1 se Kadam 4 ki tarah, P, Q, R, S aur T banao (T ray BS aur arc ka sangam hai).

**Kadam 5:** T aur R ko centre lekar **3 cm** radius ke arcs kheencho jo point U par milein.

**Kadam 6:** B aur U ko jodo. Ray BU banti hai.

> **Figure 2.10** – B centre ke 4 cm arc par T (90°) aur R (120°) bane; T aur R ke 3 cm arcs U par mile aur ray BU se ∠UBC = 105° bana.

**Kyun?** ∠TBR = 120° − 90° = 30°. BU use aadha karti hai, to ∠TBU = 15°. ∠UBC = ∠TBC + ∠TBU = 90° + 15° = **105°**.

### 135° (90° + 45°)

Idea: 90° wali ray aur seedhi line (180°) ke beech bhi 90° ka gap hai, use aadha karo to 45°. To 90° + 45° = 135°.

**Kadam 1:** Ek seedhi line DC kheencho aur uske beech mein ek point B lo (C ek taraf, D doosri taraf).

**Kadam 2:** B centre se **4 cm** ka arc kheencho jo BC ko P par aur BD ko N par kaate. Arc ko upar se aadha-gol kheencho.

**Kadam 3:** P centre se **4 cm** ka arc Q par kaato. Phir Q centre se **4 cm** ka arc R par kaato.

**Kadam 4:** Q aur R ko centre lekar **3 cm** ke arcs S par milao. Ray BS (90°) banao. Ray BS arc ko T par kaatti hai.

**Kadam 5:** T aur N ko centre lekar **4 cm** radius ke arcs kheencho jo point W par milein (B ke alawa wala, B se door ka point). (TN lagbhag 5.7 cm hai, aadha lagbhag 2.9 cm, 4 cm usse zyada hai.)

**Kadam 6:** B aur W ko jodo. Ray BW banti hai.

> **Figure 2.11** – Seedhi line DC ke beech B hai; B centre ke 4 cm arc par P, N (line par), T (90° wali ray BS par) bane; T aur N ke 4 cm arcs W par mile aur ray BW se ∠WBC = 135° bana.

**Kyun?** ∠TBN = 180° − 90° = 90°. BW use aadha karti hai, to ∠TBW = 45°. ∠WBC = ∠TBC + ∠TBW = 90° + 45° = **135°**.

## Part F: Kisi angle ki copy banana

Maan lo humein ∠ABC diya hai aur hume ray EF ke upar point E par **bilkul wahi angle** banana hai. Protractor nahi, to compass se.

**Kadam 1:** Diye hue ∠ABC mein B ko centre lekar **3 cm** radius ka arc kheencho jo BA ko P par aur BC ko Q par kaate.

**Kadam 2:** Alag se ek ray EF kheencho.

**Kadam 3:** E ko centre lekar **wahi 3 cm** radius ka arc kheencho jo EF ko R par kaate. Arc ko upar ki taraf kaafi door tak kheencho.

**Kadam 4:** Compass ki nok Q par rakho aur pencil P par. Ye **chord PQ ki lambai** hai. Compass ko bilkul chhue bina (chaudai badle bina) utha lo.

**Kadam 5:** R ko centre lekar **PQ ke barabar** radius ka arc kheencho jo Kadam 3 wale arc ko point S par kaate.

**Kadam 6:** E aur S ko jodo. Ray ES banao.

> **Figure 2.12** – Diye ∠ABC mein B centre ke 3 cm arc se P (BA par) aur Q (BC par) bane; ray EF par E centre ke 3 cm arc se R bana aur R centre ke radius PQ wale arc ne S diya; ray ES se ∠SEF = ∠ABC bana.

### Kyun kaam karta hai?

△BPQ aur △ESR ko dekho:
- BP = ES (dono 3 cm radius)
- BQ = ER (dono 3 cm radius)
- PQ = SR (compass ki chaudai PQ par hi rakhi thi)

Teeno sides barabar, to **SSS** se △BPQ ≅ △ESR. To ∠PBQ = ∠SER, yaani ∠ABC = ∠SEF.

**Jaanch:** Protractor se ∠ABC aur ∠SEF naapo. Dono ka naap ek jaisa aana chahiye ✅.

## Part G: Inaya aur Rohan ka sawaal hal

Ab wapas kagaz ke sitaron par.

- **Inaya ka 60°:** Inaya ne apne kagaz par ray BC kheenchi, compass 4 cm par kholkar B se arc P par kaata, phir P se arc Q par kaata, aur BQ jod diya. △BPQ equilateral tha, to ∠QBC = 60° ban gaya. (Figure 2.3 jaisa.)
- **Inaya ka 30°:** Usne P aur Q se 3 cm ke arcs X par milaye aur BX jod diya. 60° aadha hua, to ∠XBC = 30°. (Figure 2.4 jaisa.)
- **Rohan ka 45°:** Rohan ne 4 cm ke arc par P, Q, R lagaye (0°, 60°, 120°), Q aur R ke bisector se 90° wali ray BS banayi, phir P aur T ke arcs se 90° ko aadha kiya. ∠UBC = 45° ban gaya. (Figure 2.7 jaisa.)

Dono ne paper-fold ki hui sajaavat ke kone ko in rays par kaata, aur sitare bilkul seedhe bane. Protractor ke bina, sirf compass aur ruler se.

## Part H: Aur solved constructions

### Construction 1: 150° banao

150° = 180° − 30°. Yaani 120° aur 180° ke beech ka 60° ka gap aadha karo, to 30°, aur 120° + 30° = 150°.

**Kadam 1:** Seedhi line DC kheencho, beech mein point B lo (C ek taraf, D doosri taraf).

**Kadam 2:** B centre se **4 cm** ka arc kheencho jo BC ko P par aur BD ko N par kaate.

**Kadam 3:** P centre se **4 cm** ka arc Q par kaato. Phir Q centre se **4 cm** ka arc R par kaato (120° wali jagah).

**Kadam 4:** R aur N ko centre lekar **3 cm** radius ke arcs kheencho jo point M par milein. (RN = 4 cm, aadha 2 cm, 3 cm usse zyada.)

**Kadam 5:** B aur M ko jodo. Ray BM banti hai.

> **Figure 2.13** – Seedhi line DC par B centre ke 4 cm arc se P, N, aur P se Q, Q se R lage; R aur N ke 3 cm arcs M par mile aur ray BM se ∠MBC = 150° bana.

**Jaanch:** ∠RBN = 180° − 120° = 60°, aadha 30°, to ∠MBN = 30° aur ∠MBC = 180° − 30° = 150° ✅. Doosra: 120° + 30° = 150° ✅.

### Construction 2: 22½° banao

22½° = 45° ÷ 2.

**Kadam 1:** Ray BC. B centre se **4 cm** ka arc P par kaato.

**Kadam 2:** P centre se **4 cm** arc Q par, Q centre se **4 cm** arc R par kaato.

**Kadam 3:** Q aur R ke **3 cm** arcs S par milao. Ray BS (90°) arc ko T par kaatti hai.

**Kadam 4:** P aur T ke **4 cm** arcs U par milao (B se door wala point). Ray BU (45°) arc ko W par kaatti hai.

**Kadam 5:** P aur W ko centre lekar **3 cm** radius ke arcs kheencho jo point Y par milein. (PW lagbhag 3.1 cm hai, aadha lagbhag 1.5 cm, 3 cm usse zyada.)

**Kadam 6:** B aur Y ko jodo. Ray BY banti hai.

> **Figure 2.14** – B centre ke 4 cm arc par P, T (90°), W (45° wali ray BU par) bane; P aur W ke 3 cm arcs Y par mile aur ray BY se ∠YBC = 22½° bana.

**Jaanch:** 90° ÷ 2 = 45°, 45° ÷ 2 = 22.5°. Aur ulta: 22.5 + 22.5 = 45 ✅.

### Construction 3: 37½° banao

37½° = 75° ÷ 2.

**Kadam 1:** Ray BC. B centre se **4 cm** ka arc P par kaato.

**Kadam 2:** P centre se **4 cm** arc Q par, Q centre se **4 cm** arc R par kaato.

**Kadam 3:** Q aur R ke **3 cm** arcs S par milao. Ray BS (90°) arc ko T par kaatti hai.

**Kadam 4:** Q aur T ke **3 cm** arcs V par milao. Ray BV (75°) arc ko W par kaatti hai.

**Kadam 5:** P aur W ko centre lekar **3 cm** radius ke arcs kheencho jo point Z par milein. (PW lagbhag 4.9 cm hai, aadha lagbhag 2.4 cm, 3 cm usse zyada.)

**Kadam 6:** B aur Z ko jodo. Ray BZ banti hai.

> **Figure 2.15** – B centre ke 4 cm arc par P, Q (60°), T (90°), W (75° wali ray BV par) bane; P aur W ke 3 cm arcs Z par mile aur ray BZ se ∠ZBC = 37½° bana.

**Jaanch:** 60° + 15° = 75°, 75° ÷ 2 = 37.5° ✅.

### Construction 4: 67½° banao

67½° = 45° + 22½°. Yaani 45° wali ray aur 90° wali ray ke beech ka 45° ka gap aadha karo.

**Kadam 1:** Ray BC. B centre se **4 cm** ka arc P par kaato.

**Kadam 2:** P centre se **4 cm** arc Q par, Q centre se **4 cm** arc R par kaato.

**Kadam 3:** Q aur R ke **3 cm** arcs S par milao. Ray BS (90°) arc ko T par kaatti hai.

**Kadam 4:** P aur T ke **4 cm** arcs U par milao (B se door wala point). Ray BU (45°) arc ko W par kaatti hai.

**Kadam 5:** W aur T ko centre lekar **3 cm** radius ke arcs kheencho jo point Y par milein. (WT lagbhag 3.1 cm hai, aadha lagbhag 1.5 cm, 3 cm usse zyada.)

**Kadam 6:** B aur Y ko jodo. Ray BY banti hai.

> **Figure 2.16** – B centre ke 4 cm arc par P, W (45° wali ray BU par), T (90° wali ray BS par) bane; W aur T ke 3 cm arcs Y par mile aur ray BY se ∠YBC = 67½° bana.

**Jaanch:** ∠WBT = 90° − 45° = 45°, aadha 22.5°. ∠YBC = 45° + 22.5° = 67.5° ✅.

### Construction 5: Protractor se 110° banao aur bisector kheencho

**Kadam 1:** Part A ke tareeke se protractor se ∠ABC = 110° banao (B vertex, ray BC par 0° wala scale padhkar 110° par point A lagao).

**Kadam 2:** B ko centre lekar **4 cm** radius ka arc kheencho jo BA ko P par aur BC ko Q par kaate.

**Kadam 3:** P ko centre lekar **4 cm** radius ka arc kheencho (angle ke andar).

**Kadam 4:** Q ko centre lekar **wahi 4 cm** radius ka arc kheencho jo pichhle arc ko point X par kaate (B ke alawa wala, B se door ka point). (PQ lagbhag 6.5 cm hai, aadha lagbhag 3.3 cm, isliye 3 cm kam padta, 4 cm sahi hai.)

**Kadam 5:** B aur X ko jodo. Ray BX banti hai.

> **Figure 2.17** – Protractor se bane ∠ABC = 110° mein B centre ke 4 cm arc se P (BA par) aur Q (BC par) bane; P aur Q ke 4 cm arcs X par mile aur ray BX se ∠ABX = ∠XBC = 55° bana.

**Jaanch:** Protractor se ∠XBC naapo: 55°. ∠ABX bhi 55°. 55 + 55 = 110 ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Compass ki chaudai beech mein badal dena**

60° banate waqt agar B se 4 cm ka arc kheencha aur P se 5 cm ka, to BP = BQ = 4 cm par PQ = 5 cm. Triangle BPQ equilateral nahi raha, to angle 60° nahi aayega. **Jab tak Kadam kehta hai "wahi radius", compass ko mat chhedo.** Compass ko bandh karke dobara kholne se bachiye.

**Galti 2: Arc itna chhota kheencha ki doosre arc ko kaate hi nahi**

Bisector ke Kadam 2 mein agar radius PQ ke aadhe se **chhota** ho, to dono arcs milenge hi nahi. Maan lo PQ = 4 cm: aadha 2 cm. Radius 1.5 cm lene par arcs kabhi nahi milenge. Hamesha **thoda zyada** lo (jaise 3 cm), aur arc ko kaafi lamba kheencho.

**Galti 3: Bisect karte waqt P aur Q se alag-alag radius lena**

P se 3 cm aur Q se 4 cm ke arcs milenge to sahi, par X, P aur Q se barabar doori par nahi hoga (PX ≠ QX). Tab SSS nahi lagega, to BX bisector nahi hoga. **Dono arcs ka radius barabar rakho.**

**Galti 4: Protractor ki galat scale padhna**

Ray dayein taraf hai aur aapne wo scale padh li jiska 0° baayein kinare par hai, to 70° ki jagah 110° dikhega (70 + 110 = 180). Pehle dekho ki **0° ray ke kinare par hai ya nahi**, tabhi ginti shuru karo.

**Galti 5: Arc kaafi door tak na kheenchna**

Agar B ke arc ko sirf thoda sa kheencha, to R ya T tak arc pahunchega hi nahi aur 120° ya 90° ke points nahi mil payenge. Arc ko lagbhag aadha-gol kheencho.

## Quick Recap

- Protractor se angle banate waqt wo scale padho jiska **0° ray par** ho.
- **Angle bisector:** B centre se arc (P aur Q), P aur Q se barabar radius ke arcs (X), ray BX. **SSS** (△BPX ≅ △BQX) se kaam karta hai.
- **60°:** do arcs, dono 4 cm, se equilateral triangle. Har angle 60°.
- **120°:** do 60° lagatar. **30°:** 60° ko aadha. **90°:** 60° aur 120° ke beech ka bisector. **45°:** 90° ko aadha.
- **15°:** 30° ko aadha. **22½°:** 45° ko aadha.
- **75°** = 60° + 15°, **105°** = 90° + 15°, **135°** = 90° + 45°, **150°** = 180° − 30°.
- **Angle ki copy:** arc aur chord PQ ka naap uthao; SSS se angle barabar.
- Galtiyan: compass ki chaudai badalna, arc chhota rakhna, bisect mein alag radius, galat protractor scale.

## Practice Questions

1. Construct an angle of 60° at point B on ray BC using only a compass and a ruler. Then bisect it. What do you expect a protractor to read for each part?

2. Construct an angle of 90° without using a protractor or set-square. Say in one line why your method gives 90°.

3. Construct an angle of 135° and verify using the sum 90° + 45°.

4. Construct an angle of 165°. (Hint: think of 150° and what lies between 150° and 180°.) Write the angle sum that proves it.

5. Using a protractor, draw ∠ABC = 84°. Bisect it with compass and ruler. What should each half measure? Then copy one of the halves at point E on a new ray EF.

6. Inaya draws the 4 cm arc from B to get P, but for the second arc she opens her compass to 5 cm and marks Q from P. Will ∠QBC be 60°? Give a reason.

7. State True or False:
   (a) To bisect an angle, the arcs from P and Q can have any radius, even smaller than half of PQ.
   (b) In an equilateral triangle each angle is 60°.
   (c) 75° can be made by bisecting the angle between the 60° ray and the 90° ray, then adding it to 60°.
   (d) Bisecting 45° gives 22½°.
   (e) A protractor reading of 70° on the wrong scale can show 110°.

### Answers

1. Ray BC kheencho, B centre se 4 cm ka arc P par, P centre se 4 cm ka arc Q par, ray BQ jodo. ∠QBC = 60°. Phir P aur Q se 3 cm ke arcs X par milao, ray BX. Protractor 60° (poora) aur 30° (har aadha) dikhayega. Jaanch: 30 + 30 = 60 ✅

2. Ray BC par P, Q, R lagao (4 cm arcs: 0°, 60°, 120°). Q aur R se 3 cm ke arcs S par milao, ray BS. ∠QBR = 120° − 60° = 60°, BS use aadha karti hai, to ∠QBS = 30° aur ∠SBC = 60° + 30° = 90° ✅.

3. Figure 2.11 jaisa: seedhi line DC, B se 4 cm arc se P, N, Q, R; Q aur R ke 3 cm arcs S par, ray BS (90°), BS arc ko T par kaatti hai; T aur N ke 4 cm arcs W par milao. ∠TBN = 90°, aadha 45°, to ∠WBC = 90° + 45° = 135° ✅.

4. 150° banao (Construction 1: ray BM). Ab 150° aur 180° ke beech ka gap 180° − 150° = 30° hai. M aur N (N, BD par arc ka point) ke arcs se is 30° ko aadha karo (radius 3 cm, kyunki MN lagbhag 2.1 cm hai, aadha lagbhag 1 cm) aur ray BK lo. 30° ÷ 2 = 15°. To ∠KBC = 150° + 15° = **165°**, aur 180° − 15° = 165° ✅.

5. 84° ÷ 2 = **42°** har aadha. Jaanch: 42 + 42 = 84 ✅. Copy: B se 3 cm arc P (BA par), Q (BC par); E se 3 cm arc R (EF par); R se PQ ke barabar radius arc S par; ray ES. Ab ∠SEF = ∠ABX ya jo bhi half copy kiya wo 42° hona chahiye (SSS se).

6. Nahi. BP = BQ = 4 cm par PQ = 5 cm, to △BPQ equilateral nahi hai, isliye ∠QBC 60° nahi hoga. 60° tabhi aata hai jab teeno sides barabar hon, yaani dono arcs ka radius ek hi ho.

7. (a) **False** ❌. Radius PQ ke aadhe se zyada hona chahiye, warna arcs milenge hi nahi. Aur dono radius barabar hone chahiye.
   (b) **True** ✅. Teeno angles barabar, jod 180°, to 60° each.
   (c) **True** ✅. 90° − 60° = 30°, aadha 15°, 60° + 15° = 75°.
   (d) **True** ✅. 45° ÷ 2 = 22.5°.
   (e) **True** ✅. 70° + 110° = 180°, dono scales ek-doosre ka supplement dikhati hain.
