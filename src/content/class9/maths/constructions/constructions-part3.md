# Topic 3: Perpendicular aur Parallel Lines Banana

Topic 2 mein humne sirf compass aur ruler se angle bana liye: Inaya ke sitare wala 60°, phir 90°, 45° aur kisi bhi angle ki copy. Ab us hunar ko ek kaam mein lagate hain: **seedhi lines banana**. Do tarah ki seedhi lines hoti hain. Ek, jo kisi line ko theek 90° par kaate (**perpendicular**). Doosri, jo kabhi milti hi nahi aur hamesha barabar doori par rehti hain (**parallel**). Dono ko banane ke liye hum Topic 1 ka perpendicular bisector aur Topic 2 ki angle ki copy hi dobara use karenge. Koi nayi jaadui cheez nahi aayegi.

## Mall ke basement ki parking

Ek naye mall ke basement mein parking ke "bays" (gaadi khadi karne ke khaane) paint hone hain. Kaam Sudhakar ji ka hai. Wo 20 saal se painter hain. Unke saath ek nayi trainee hai, Lata.

Basement ka farsh khaali hai. Bas beech mein ek lambi **lane line** pehle se painted hai, jis par se gaadiyan guzarengi. Mall ke owner ka naksha saaf hai:

- Har bay ki side lines lane line ke saath **bilkul 90°** banayengi.
- Saare bays ek doosre ke **parallel** honge.
- Har bay **2.5 metre chaudha** hoga.

Lata ne chalk uthayi aur bola, "Sir, aankh se seedhi line kheench dete hain na? Aap itne saal se kar rahe ho."

Sudhakar ji hans diye. "Aankh se kheenchi to ek gaadi thodi tedhi aayegi, doosri thodi aur tedhi. Pehle hi bay mein 5 cm ka farak, aur aakhri bay tak 50 cm ka. Gaadi ka darwaza khulega hi nahi. Mere paas teen cheezein hain: **rassi, chalk aur chalk wala compass.**"

"Chalk wala compass?"

"Ye dekho. Ek rassi, jis par main 1 metre ke gaanth lagata hoon. Ek siri farsh par pakdo, doosre siri par chalk bandho. Rassi tani rakhke ghumao, to farsh par bada sa arc ban jayega. Wahi compass hai, bas bada."

Lata ne socha, "Sir, school mein hum chhota compass use karte hain. Wahi baat?" "Bilkul wahi," Sudhakar ji bole. "Pehle kagaz par chhota naksha banate hain, **1 cm = 1 metre** maan ke. Naksha sahi bana to farsh par wahi kadam dohrao."

To chalo, hum bhi Lata ke saath pehle kagaz par seekhte hain: perpendicular kaise banta hai, parallel kaise banta hai, aur kab "2.5 metre" ka matlab "2.5 cm" ban jata hai. Poora basement Part F mein paint ho jayega.

## Part A: Line ke upar diye hue point par perpendicular

**Sawaal:** Line l par ek point P diya hai. P se l par perpendicular banao.

Sochne wali baat: hum P ko kisi segment ka **beech ka point (midpoint)** bana dete hain. Phir Topic 1 wala **perpendicular bisector** bana dete hain. Bisector midpoint se guzarta hai, to P se bhi guzarega, aur l par 90° par hoga. Ye wahi purani chaal hai.

**Given:** Line l, aur l par ek point P.

**Construction ke kadam**

- **Kadam 1:** Line l kheencho aur uspar point P lo.
- **Kadam 2:** P ko centre lekar 2 cm radius se P ke dono taraf l ko kaatte hue arcs lagao. Bayein taraf wala kaat-point A, daayein taraf wala B. Isse PA = PB = 2 cm, yaani P segment AB ka midpoint ban gaya.
- **Kadam 3:** A ko centre lekar 3 cm radius se l ke upar ek arc lagao. (3 cm, AB ke aadhe yaani 2 cm se **zyada** hai.)
- **Kadam 4:** B ko centre lekar wahi 3 cm radius (compass ki chaudai bilkul na badlo) se l ke upar doosra arc lagao jo pichhle arc ko Q par kaate.
- **Kadam 5:** P aur Q ko ruler se jod kar line PQ kheencho. Chaaho to usse P ke neeche bhi badha do.

> **Figure 3.1** – Line l par point P; P ke dono taraf 2 cm par A aur B; A aur B se 3 cm ke arcs jo upar Q par milte hain; PQ ⊥ l.

**Kyun kaam karta hai?**

Triangles APQ aur BPQ lo:
- AP = BP = 2 cm (Kadam 2)
- AQ = BQ = 3 cm (Kadam 3 aur 4, same radius)
- PQ dono mein saajha hai

To SSS se ΔAPQ ≅ ΔBPQ (SSS ke baare mein [congruence-of-triangles-part2.md](../congruence-of-triangles/congruence-of-triangles-part2.md) mein dekha tha). Congruent triangles ke corresponding angles barabar hote hain, to ∠APQ = ∠BPQ.

Ab A, P, B ek hi seedhi line l par hain, isliye ∠APQ + ∠BPQ = 180°. Dono barabar hain, to har ek = 180° ÷ 2 = **90°**. Yaani PQ ⊥ l. 

**Jaanch:** Set-square ka 90° wala kona P par rakho, ek bhujaa l par. Doosri bhujaa PQ ke saath bilkul chipki honi chahiye. Ya protractor se ∠APQ naapo: 90° aana chahiye ✅

## Part B: Line ke bahar diye hue point se perpendicular

**Sawaal:** Line l diya hai, aur l ke bahar ek point P hai. P se l par perpendicular girao.

Idea: P se ek arc lagao jo l ko do jagah A aur B par kaate. Tab PA = PB. Ab ek aur point dhoondho jo A aur B se barabar doori par ho. Dono point (P aur ek aur) A aur B se barabar doori par hain, to dono perpendicular bisector par honge, aur unhe milane wali line wahi bisector hogi.

**Given:** Line l, aur l ke bahar point P (P, l se 4 cm upar hai).

**Construction ke kadam**

- **Kadam 1:** Line l kheencho aur l ke upar, l se 4 cm door point P lo.
- **Kadam 2:** P ko centre lekar 5 cm radius se ek arc lagao jo l ko do points par kaate: bayein A, daayein B. (Radius P se l ki doori 4 cm se **zyada** hona zaroori hai, tabhi arc l tak pahunchega.)
- **Kadam 3:** A ko centre lekar 4 cm radius se l ke **neeche** (P ki ulti taraf) ek arc lagao. (AB ka aadha 3 cm hai aur 4 cm usse zyada hai.)
- **Kadam 4:** B ko centre lekar wahi 4 cm radius se l ke neeche doosra arc lagao jo pichhle arc ko Q par kaate.
- **Kadam 5:** P aur Q ko jodo. Line PQ, l ko point M par kaategi.

> **Figure 3.2** – Line l, bahar point P (l se 4 cm upar); P ke arc se kate points A aur B; A aur B se 4 cm ke arcs jo l ke neeche Q par milte hain; PQ, l ko M par kaatti hai aur PM ⊥ l.

**Kyun kaam karta hai?**

- PA = PB = 5 cm (Kadam 2), to P, AB ke perpendicular bisector par hai.
- QA = QB = 4 cm (Kadam 3 aur 4), to Q bhi usi perpendicular bisector par hai.
- Do points par se ek hi seedhi line guzarti hai, to line PQ hi AB ka perpendicular bisector hai. Isliye PQ ⊥ l, aur M, AB ka midpoint hai.

SSS ke saath bhi dekh sakte ho: ΔAPQ aur ΔBPQ mein PA = PB, QA = QB aur PQ saajha, to ΔAPQ ≅ ΔBPQ, aur ∠APQ = ∠BPQ. Phir ΔAPM aur ΔBPM mein PA = PB, ∠APM = ∠BPM, PM saajha, to SAS se ∠AMP = ∠BMP. Ye dono angle ek straight line par hain, to har ek 90°.

Q ko l ki **doosri taraf** isliye lete hain: P wali taraf bhi arcs milte, par wo point P ke paas aata hai (yahan l se sirf lagbhag 2.6 cm upar), aur do paas-paas ke points se kheenchi line thodi si galti par zyada tedhi ho jaati hai. Aur agar arcs ka radius bhi 5 cm hota, to P wali taraf ka milan point khud P hi hota, jisse koi line nahi banti. Doosri taraf ka Q, P se door hota hai, to line PQ saaf aur sahi banti hai.

**Numbers ki jaanch:** PM = 4 cm (P, l se 4 cm upar hai). Triangle APM mein AP = 5 cm aur PM = 4 cm, to AM = 3 cm (kyunki 3² + 4² = 5²). Yaani M, A se 3 cm par hai, aur AB = 6 cm ka midpoint hai. ✅

**Jaanch:** Set-square ka ek kinara l par rakho aur doosra kinara PQ se milao. Ya M par ∠PMB protractor se naapo: 90° ✅

## Part C: Diye hue point se parallel line (angle ki copy se)

**Sawaal:** Line l aur uske bahar ek point P diya hai. P se l ke parallel line banao.

Idea: Topic 2 mein angle ki copy banana seekha tha. Yahan bhi wahi. Pehle P aur l ke beech ek transversal kheencho. Phir l par ban rahe angle ko P par **copy** karo, same dishaa mein (corresponding angle). Agar corresponding angles barabar hon to lines parallel hoti hain (ye [lines-and-angles-part3.md](../lines-and-angles/lines-and-angles-part3.md) mein dekha tha).

**Given:** Line l, aur l ke bahar point P.

**Construction ke kadam**

- **Kadam 1:** l par koi point A lo (P ke neeche kahin). P aur A ko jodo aur line AP ko P se aage upar ki taraf thoda badha do. Ye transversal hai.
- **Kadam 2:** A ko centre lekar 2 cm radius se ek arc lagao jo l ko B par (A ke daayein) aur AP ko C par kaate.
- **Kadam 3:** P ko centre lekar wahi 2 cm radius se ek arc lagao. Wo transversal ko P ke upar D par kaate, aur daayein taraf bada sa kheencho.
- **Kadam 4:** Compass ki nok B par rakho, pencil C par. Is lambai BC ko naap lo (isse compass ka radius BC ke barabar ho gaya).
- **Kadam 5:** D ko centre lekar BC ke barabar radius se ek arc lagao jo Kadam 3 wale arc ko transversal ke **daayein** taraf E par kaate.
- **Kadam 6:** P aur E ko jodo aur line ko dono taraf badha do. Ye line m hai.

> **Figure 3.3** – Line l par A; transversal DPA (D, P ke upar); A par 2 cm ke arc se kate B aur C; P par 2 cm ke arc se kate D aur E (DE = BC); line m = PE ∥ l.

**Kyun kaam karta hai?**

Triangles CAB aur DPE mein:
- AC = PD = 2 cm (dono arcs ka radius)
- AB = PE = 2 cm (wahi radius)
- CB = DE (Kadam 4 aur 5)

To SSS se ΔCAB ≅ ΔDPE, aur isliye **∠DPE = ∠CAB**. Ye dono angle transversal DA ke ek hi taraf, ek hi sthaan par (upar-daayein) hain, yaani **corresponding angles** hain. Corresponding angles barabar hon to lines parallel hoti hain, to **m ∥ l**.

**Jaanch:** Protractor se ∠CAB aur ∠DPE dono naapo, barabar aane chahiye. Ya l aur m ke beech ki perpendicular doori 3-4 jagah naapo: har jagah ek hi number aana chahiye ✅

**Alternate angle se bhi ho sakta hai:** Agar ∠CAB ko P par transversal ke **ulti** taraf copy karte, to alternate interior angles barabar hote, aur tab bhi lines parallel aati. Tareeka bilkul wahi, bas arc doosri taraf.

### Extra: Rhombus method (kam kadam, ek hi radius)

Isme angle naapna hi nahi padta. Bas ek hi radius se teen arcs.

**Given:** Line l, aur l ke bahar point P (P, l se 3 cm upar hai).

**Construction ke kadam**

- **Kadam 1:** P ko centre lekar 4 cm radius se ek arc lagao jo l ko A par kaate. (4 cm, P ki l se doori 3 cm se zyada hai.)
- **Kadam 2:** A ko centre lekar wahi 4 cm radius se l par daayein ek arc lagao jo l ko B par kaate. Ab AB = 4 cm.
- **Kadam 3:** P ko centre lekar 4 cm radius se B ki taraf l ke upar ek arc lagao.
- **Kadam 4:** B ko centre lekar 4 cm radius se ek arc lagao jo Kadam 3 ke arc ko C par kaate. (Ye arc A par bhi mil sakta hai, usse alag wala point C hai, jo l ke upar hai.)
- **Kadam 5:** P aur C ko jodo aur line badha do.

> **Figure 3.4** – Line l, bahar point P (l se 3 cm upar); A aur B l par, PA = AB = BC = CP = 4 cm; PABC rhombus bana aur line PC ∥ l.

**Kyun kaam karta hai?** PA = AB = BC = CP = 4 cm, yaani chaaron sides barabar hain: PABC rhombus hai. Diagonal AC jodo. ΔABC aur ΔCPA mein AB = CP, BC = PA aur AC saajha, to SSS se congruent, aur ∠BAC = ∠PCA. Ye alternate angles hain (transversal AC), to PC ∥ AB, yaani PC ∥ l.

**Jaanch:** Set-square ko l par rakho aur P tak khisko: PC ki line P aur C dono se chipki rehni chahiye. Ya PC aur l ke beech ki doori P par aur C par naapo: dono 3 cm ✅

## Part D: Do parallel lines ke beech ki doori

**Doori kya hoti hai?** Do parallel lines ke beech ki doori wo hai jo ek line ke kisi bhi point se doosri line par girayi gayi **perpendicular ki lambai** ho. Tedhi line se naapoge to lambai zyada aayegi, wo asli doori nahi hai. Aur parallel lines ki khoobi ye hai ki ye doori har jagah **ek hi** rehti hai.

**Sawaal:** Line l ke parallel ek line banao jo l se 3 cm door ho.

Idea: l par do points lo. Dono par 3 cm lambi perpendicular uthao. Unke upar wale sire jod do.

**Given:** Line l, aur uspar XY = 5 cm.

**Construction ke kadam**

- **Kadam 1:** Line l kheencho. Uspar XY = 5 cm lo.
- **Kadam 2:** X ko centre lekar 2 cm radius se l ko X ke dono taraf kaato: bayein X1, daayein X2.
- **Kadam 3:** X1 ko centre lekar 3 cm radius se l ke upar ek arc lagao. Phir X2 ko centre lekar wahi 3 cm radius se doosra arc lagao jo pehle ko S par kaate.
- **Kadam 4:** X se S tak ray kheencho aur usse thoda aage badhao. X ko centre lekar 3 cm radius se ek arc lagao jo is ray ko U par kaate. Ab XU ⊥ l aur XU = 3 cm.
- **Kadam 5:** Y ko centre lekar 2 cm radius se l ko Y ke dono taraf kaato: bayein Y1, daayein Y2.
- **Kadam 6:** Y1 ko centre lekar 3 cm radius se l ke upar ek arc lagao. Y2 ko centre lekar 3 cm radius se doosra arc lagao jo pehle ko T par kaate.
- **Kadam 7:** Y se T tak ray kheencho aur thoda badhao. Y ko centre lekar 3 cm radius se ek arc lagao jo is ray ko V par kaate. Ab YV ⊥ l aur YV = 3 cm.
- **Kadam 8:** U aur V ko jodo aur line ko dono taraf badha do. Ye line m hai.

> **Figure 3.5** – Line l par X aur Y (XY = 5 cm); X aur Y par uthaye 3 cm ke perpendiculars XU aur YV (U aur V l ke upar, S aur T beech ke arc-points); line m = UV ∥ l aur l se 3 cm door.

**Kyun kaam karta hai?**

XU ⊥ l aur YV ⊥ l, to dono ke l ke saath angle 90°, 90° hain. Ye corresponding angles barabar hain, to XU ∥ YV. Ab U se Y jodo. ΔXUY aur ΔVYU mein:
- XU = YV = 3 cm
- UY saajha
- ∠XUY = ∠VYU (alternate angles, kyunki XU ∥ YV)

To SAS se ΔXUY ≅ ΔVYU, aur isliye ∠XYU = ∠VUY. Ye alternate angles hain (transversal UY), to **UV ∥ XY**, yaani m ∥ l. Aur l se m tak har perpendicular 3 cm hai, kyunki XU = YV = 3 cm.

**Jaanch:** l par ek teesra point Z lo (X aur Y ke beech). Z par set-square rakh kar perpendicular upar m tak naapo: ZW = 3 cm aana chahiye ✅ Do se zyada jagah naap ke dekho.

## Part E: Perpendicular bisector se circle ka centre (aage ke kaam ka)

*Ye hissa "aage ke kaam ka" hai: circles chapter ([circles.md](../circles/circles.md)) mein dobara kaam aayega.*

**Sawaal:** Do points A aur B se guzarta hua ek circle banao. (Aisa circle jiska radius 6.5 cm ho aur AB = 5 cm.)

Idea: circle ka centre O wo point hota hai jo A aur B se **barabar doori** par ho, kyunki circle par har point centre se radius ke barabar hota hai. Aur jo point A aur B se barabar doori par ho, wo AB ke perpendicular bisector par hota hai (Topic 1). Yaani **centre perpendicular bisector par kahin hota hai.**

**Given:** Segment AB = 5 cm.

**Construction ke kadam**

- **Kadam 1:** Segment AB = 5 cm kheencho.
- **Kadam 2:** A ko centre lekar 4 cm radius se AB ke upar aur neeche arcs lagao. (4 cm, AB ke aadhe 2.5 cm se zyada hai.)
- **Kadam 3:** B ko centre lekar wahi 4 cm radius se upar aur neeche arcs lagao jo pehle arcs ko upar M par aur neeche N par kaatein.
- **Kadam 4:** M aur N ko jodo aur line ko upar ki taraf lamba kheencho. Ye line AB ko D par kaatti hai.
- **Kadam 5:** A ko centre lekar 6.5 cm radius se ek arc lagao jo line MN ko upar O par kaate.
- **Kadam 6:** O ko centre lekar OA (6.5 cm) radius se circle kheencho.

> **Figure 3.6** – Segment AB = 5 cm; arcs ke points M (upar) aur N (neeche) se bana perpendicular bisector MN jo AB ko D par kaatta hai; point O, MN par (AB ke upar), OA = OB = 6.5 cm; O centre wala 6.5 cm radius ka circle A aur B se guzarta hai.

**Kyun kaam karta hai?** O perpendicular bisector par hai, to OA = OB = 6.5 cm. Isliye A aur B, O se 6.5 cm par hain, yaani dono O centre wale 6.5 cm ke circle par hain.

**Numbers ki jaanch:** AD = DB = 2.5 cm, AO = 6.5 cm, to OD² = 6.5² − 2.5² = 42.25 − 6.25 = 36, OD = 6 cm. OD ⊥ AB, to OB² = 2.5² + 6² = 6.25 + 36 = 42.25, OB = 6.5 cm ✅

**Jaanch:** Ruler se OB naapo: 6.5 cm aana chahiye. Aur circle par kahin se bhi A aur B alag-alag dikhne chahiye ✅

**Dhyan do:** Kadam 5 mein O ko MN par kahin bhi le sakte the (D ke upar ya neeche). Har jagah ke O se ek alag circle banta, jo A aur B se guzarta. **A aur B se anginat (bahut saare) circle** guzarte hain. Agar teesra point C bhi diya ho, to sirf ek hi circle hoga. Wo aage circles mein dekhenge.

## Part F: Parking bays (kahani poori)

Ab wapas basement par. Naksha **1 cm = 1 metre** ke scale par hai. Isliye 2.5 metre chaudha bay naksha mein **2.5 cm** hai, aur bay ki 5 metre lambai 5 cm.

**Sawaal:** Lane line l par do bays banao, har bay 2.5 cm (yaani 2.5 m) chaudha aur 5 cm (5 m) gehra. Side lines lane line ke perpendicular aur ek doosri ke parallel.

**Given:** Lane line l (basement ki painted line).

**Construction ke kadam**

- **Kadam 1:** l par point A lo. A se 2.5 cm par B, aur B se 2.5 cm par C (A, B, C ek hi taraf badhte hue) mark karo. Ab AB = BC = 2.5 cm.
- **Kadam 2:** A ko centre lekar 1 cm radius se l ko A1 (bayein) aur A2 (daayein) par kaato.
- **Kadam 3:** A1 ko centre lekar 1.5 cm radius se l ke upar ek arc lagao. A2 ko centre lekar wahi 1.5 cm radius se doosra arc lagao jo pehle ko P par kaate. (1.5 cm, A1A2 ke aadhe yaani 1 cm se zyada hai.)
- **Kadam 4:** A se P tak ray kheencho. A ko centre lekar 5 cm radius se ek arc lagao jo is ray ko D par kaate. Ab AD ⊥ l aur AD = 5 cm.
- **Kadam 5:** B ko centre lekar 1 cm radius se l ko B1 (bayein) aur B2 (daayein) par kaato.
- **Kadam 6:** B1 ko centre lekar 1.5 cm radius se l ke upar ek arc lagao. B2 ko centre lekar 1.5 cm radius se doosra arc lagao jo pehle ko Q par kaate.
- **Kadam 7:** B se Q tak ray kheencho. B ko centre lekar 5 cm radius se ek arc lagao jo is ray ko E par kaate. Ab BE ⊥ l aur BE = 5 cm.
- **Kadam 8:** C ko centre lekar 1 cm radius se l ko C1 (bayein) aur C2 (daayein) par kaato.
- **Kadam 9:** C1 ko centre lekar 1.5 cm radius se l ke upar ek arc lagao. C2 ko centre lekar 1.5 cm radius se doosra arc lagao jo pehle ko R par kaate.
- **Kadam 10:** C se R tak ray kheencho. C ko centre lekar 5 cm radius se ek arc lagao jo is ray ko F par kaate. Ab CF ⊥ l aur CF = 5 cm.
- **Kadam 11:** D, E, F ko jodo (agar bay ke peeche ki line bhi chahiye ho).

> **Figure 3.7** – Lane line l par A, B, C (AB = BC = 2.5 cm); har par 5 cm ke perpendiculars AD, BE, CF; ye teeno parallel hain aur do bays ABED aur BCFE banate hain, har ek 2.5 cm chaudha aur 5 cm gehra.

**Kyun kaam karta hai?**

Har perpendicular Part A wali chaal se bana hai (A1A2 ka perpendicular bisector A se guzarta hai), to AD ⊥ l, BE ⊥ l, CF ⊥ l. Teeno ke l ke saath corresponding angle 90° hain, to teeno aapas mein **parallel** hain. AD = BE = CF = 5 cm aur AB = BC = 2.5 cm, to har bay ek 2.5 cm × 5 cm ka rectangle hai.

**Jaanch:** Naap lo: DE = 2.5 cm aur EF = 2.5 cm aana chahiye (do parallel lines ke beech ki doori har jagah barabar). Set-square se AD, BE, CF par 90° dekho ✅

**Farsh par kaise?** Sudhakar ji ne wahi kadam rassi se kiye. Rassi par gaanth 1 m, 1.5 m, 2.5 m aur 5 m par. Rassi tani rakho, kyunki dheeli rassi mein radius chhota ho jaata hai. Lata ne A par 1 m wale gaanth se arcs lagaye, phir 1.5 m wale gaanth se arcs, aur aakhri mein 5 m wale gaanth se D tak ka point mark kiya. Baaki bays ki lines bhi isi tarah banti gayi.

Pehle bay ke baad Lata ne bola, "Sir, B par jo line bani wo to dono bays ki saajhi hai!"

Sudhakar ji ne sar hilaya. "Isliye **teen** lines mein **do** bays aate hain. Lata, ab main aankh se paint karta to kya hota?"

"Gaadiyan tedhi, sir!" Lata ne hanskar kaha. Aur sach ye hai ki us din ke baad Lata ko "aankh se kheench dete hain" kehna kabhi yaad nahi aaya.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Compass ka radius AB ke aadhe se kam rakhna**

AB = 6 cm ho aur radius sirf 2 cm lo. Dono arcs ki lambai 2 + 2 = 4 cm hi hai, jabki A aur B ke beech 6 cm hai. Arcs kabhi milenge hi nahi, aur Q ya M milega hi nahi. Radius hamesha AB ke aadhe (yahan 3 cm) se **zyada** rakho, jaise 4 cm.

**Galti 2: Dono arcs ke liye alag radius lena**

A se 4 cm aur B se 3 cm radius lo to arcs ek point Q par mil bhi jayenge, par AQ = 4 cm aur BQ = 3 cm hogi. Tab Q, A aur B se **barabar doori** par nahi hai, to Q perpendicular bisector par nahi aaya. PQ tedhi kheenchegi aur 90° nahi banegi. Dono arcs ke beech compass **na kholo na band karo**.

**Galti 3: Parallel line aankh se kheench dena**

Aankh se kheenchi line shuru mein shayad theek lagti hai, par thoda aage jaake l se door ya paas ho jaati hai. Do lines tabhi parallel hain jab unke beech ki perpendicular doori har jagah barabar ho. Hamesha kisi construction se banao aur 2-3 jagah naap ke jaanchon.

**Galti 4: Doori ko tedhi line se naapna**

Do parallel lines ke beech ki doori **perpendicular** se naapte hain, tedhi line se nahi. 3 cm door lines ke beech tedhi naap 4 cm ya usse zyada aa sakti hai. Doori hamesha 90° par.

**Galti 5: Perpendicular bisector aur angle bisector mein confusion**

- **Perpendicular bisector:** ek **segment** (jaise AB) ke **midpoint** par 90° wali line. Ye segment ko do barabar lambai mein baantta hai.
- **Angle bisector:** ek **angle** ko do barabar angles mein baantta hai (jaise 60° ko 30° aur 30° mein).

Ek segment ko baantti hai, doosri angle ko. Dono alag cheezein hain, aur alag tareeke se banti hain.

## Quick Recap

- **Line par diye point P par perpendicular:** P ke dono taraf barabar radius se A aur B kaato, phir A aur B se barabar bade radius ke arcs lagao. Ye wahi perpendicular bisector wali chaal hai, P ko AB ka midpoint banakar.
- **Line ke bahar point P se perpendicular:** P se arc lagao jo l ko A aur B par kaate, A aur B se barabar radius (AB ke aadhe se zyada) ke arcs l ke doosri taraf Q par milao, PQ ⊥ l. Kyun: PA = PB aur QA = QB, to PQ perpendicular bisector hai (SSS se).
- **Parallel line (angle ki copy):** transversal kheencho, l par bane angle ko P par corresponding position mein copy karo. Barabar corresponding (ya alternate) angles = parallel lines.
- **Rhombus method:** ek hi radius se PA, AB, BC aur CP barabar banao, PC ∥ l.
- **Do parallel lines ke beech ki doori:** perpendicular ki lambai, har jagah ek jaisi. d doori par parallel line: l ke do points par d lambe perpendiculars uthao aur unke sire jodo.
- **Circle ka centre:** A aur B se guzarte circle ka centre AB ke perpendicular bisector par hota hai.
- **Parking bays:** perpendiculars ek jaise lambe aur l par 90° ho to saare parallel, aur scale ke hisaab se 2.5 m = 2.5 cm.
- **Galtiyan:** radius AB ke aadhe se kam, dono arcs ka radius alag, parallel line aankh se, doori tedhi line se, perpendicular bisector aur angle bisector mein confusion.

## Practice Questions

1. A point P lies 3.5 cm above a line l. Describe how you would draw a perpendicular from P to l using a compass. What length will the perpendicular be? Why should the first arc have a radius greater than 3.5 cm?

2. Segment AB = 6 cm. A student draws arcs of radius 2 cm from A and from B to find the perpendicular bisector. Why does this fail? Give a radius that works.

3. How many lines can be drawn parallel to a line l at a distance of 4.5 cm from it? Explain.

4. In the compass-and-ruler method for a parallel line through P (Figure 3.3), suppose ∠CAB is measured as 65°. What is ∠DPE? Name the pair of angles and the rule that makes PE ∥ l.

5. In the rhombus method, P is 3 cm above l and the compass radius is 5 cm. Find the distance from the foot of the perpendicular from P to the point A where the first arc cuts l. What is the distance between lines PC and l?

6. A circle passes through two points A and B with AB = 10 cm and has radius 13 cm. How far is its centre from the midpoint of AB, and on which line does the centre lie?

7. A bay drawn at the scale 1 cm = 2 metres is 2.5 cm wide on paper. What is its real width? If the real bay is 5 metres wide, how wide would it be on a drawing with the scale 1 cm = 2 metres?

8. State True or False:
   (a) The perpendicular bisector of AB passes through the midpoint of AB and is perpendicular to AB.
   (b) If the compass radius is less than half of AB, the two arcs can still meet.
   (c) The distance between two parallel lines is measured along any slanting line joining them.
   (d) Every point on the perpendicular bisector of AB is equally far from A and from B.

### Answers

1. P se 3.5 cm se zyada radius (jaise 5 cm) ka arc lagao jo l ko A aur B par kaate. A aur B se barabar radius (AB ke aadhe se zyada) ke arcs l ki doosri taraf Q par milao. PQ jodo, ye l par perpendicular hai. Perpendicular ki lambai **3.5 cm** hogi, kyunki P, l se 3.5 cm upar hai. Arc ka radius 3.5 cm se kam hota to arc l tak pahunchta hi nahi, aur A, B milte hi nahi.

2. Arcs ki lambai 2 + 2 = 4 cm hai, jabki A aur B 6 cm door hain, to arcs mil hi nahi sakte. Koi bhi radius jo 3 cm se zyada ho kaam karega, jaise **4 cm** (4 + 4 = 8, jo 6 se bada hai).

3. **Do.** Ek l ke ek taraf 4.5 cm par, doosri l ke doosri taraf 4.5 cm par. Dono l ke parallel hain.

4. ∠DPE = **65°**. ∠CAB aur ∠DPE **corresponding angles** hain. Barabar corresponding angles ka matlab hai PE ∥ l. (SSS se ΔCAB ≅ ΔDPE hone ki wajah se ye barabar hain.)

5. Foot se A ki doori ek right-angled triangle ka ek paksh hai: hypotenuse PA = 5 cm, ek paksh 3 cm, to doosra paksh = 4 cm (3² + 4² = 25). Doori = **4 cm**. Lines PC aur l ke beech ki doori **3 cm** hai (wahi jo P se l ki doori hai).

6. Centre AB ke perpendicular bisector par hota hai. Midpoint se centre ki doori = x ho to 5² + x² = 13², to x² = 169 − 25 = 144, x = **12 cm**. (Jaanch: 25 + 144 = 169 ✅)

7. 1 cm = 2 m, to 2.5 cm = 2.5 × 2 = **5 metres**. Agar real bay 5 metre chaudha hai, to drawing mein 5 ÷ 2 = **2.5 cm**.

8. (a) **True** ✅. Yahi uski paribhasha hai.
   (b) **False** ❌. Arcs ki kul lambai AB se kam ho jaati hai, to wo milte hi nahi.
   (c) **False** ❌. Doori hamesha perpendicular se naapte hain, tedhi line se nahi.
   (d) **True** ✅. Perpendicular bisector par har point A aur B se barabar doori par hota hai.
