# Topic 4: Triangle Banana (I): SSS, SAS, ASA aur RHS

Ab tak is chapter mein aapne apna **geometry box** chalana seekh liya hai: segment banana aur copy karna, perpendicular bisector, angle banana, aur Topic 3 mein perpendicular aur parallel lines (jinse Sudhakar ji aur Lata ne parking bays banaye the). Ab inhi auzaaron ko jodkar hum **pehli poori figure** banayenge: **triangle**.

Sawaal ye hai ki triangle banane ke liye kitni cheezein batani padti hain, aur kaun si teen cheezein kaafi hain? Is jawab ka asli "kyun" Congruence chapter mein pehle hi aa chuka hai. Yahan hum use compass, ruler aur protractor se **haath se karke** dekhenge.

## Ms. Banerjee aur Faiz ka roof truss

Ms. Banerjee ek architect hain. Ek chhote hall ki chhat ka triangular **roof truss** (lohe ya lakdi ka triangle jaisa dhancha) design karna hai. Unhone apne intern **Faiz** ko bulaya: "Faiz, is truss ko scale par draw karo. **1 cm = 1 m.** Teen naap diye hain, baaki tum sambhalo."

Faiz ne pehla set liya: **3 m, 4 m, 5 m.** Usne compass uthaya, aur kuch der mein ek sundar triangle ban gaya.

Doosra set aaya: **3 m, 4 m, 8 m.** Faiz ne 8 cm ki line kheenchi, 3 cm aur 4 cm ke arc lagaye, par arc **mile hi nahi**. Usne dobara try kiya. Phir bhi nahi.

Faiz ne kaha, "Madam, mera compass kharab hai kya?"

Ms. Banerjee muskuraye: "Compass theek hai. Ye teen numbers hi aise hain ki koi triangle banta nahi. Ye kyun hota hai, aur kaun se teen numbers chalenge, ye aaj hum dekhenge."

Is topic ke end mein hum Faiz ki dono drawings wapas dekhenge.

## Part A: Triangle kab ban sakta hai?

Triangle banana shuru karne se pehle ek sawaal: **kya diye hue teen numbers se triangle banega bhi?**

Faiz ki baat ko sochte hain. Maan lo sides 3, 4 aur 8 hain. 8 cm ki line AB kheencho. A se 3 cm ke arc aur B se 4 cm ke arc lagao. Dono arcs ko milna hoga, tabhi C milega. Par 3 + 4 = 7 hai, aur 7 < 8. Dono "chhoti sides" milkar bhi 8 cm ki doori tak nahi pahunchti, to arc beech mein hi rah jate hain.

> **Figure 4.1** – 8 cm ki line AB, A se 3 cm ka arc aur B se 4 cm ka arc, jo ek doosre ko kaat nahi paate, isliye koi point C nahi milta.

Isse ek niyam nikalta hai (iska "kyun" [Congruence ke Topic 4](../congruence-of-triangles/congruence-of-triangles-part4.md) mein hai):

> **Triangle ki kisi bhi do sides ka jod, teesri side se bada hona chahiye.**

Aur chahiye ki **teeno jodiyon** ke liye sach ho. Practice mein bas dekh lo ki **do chhoti sides ka jod, sabse badi side se bada hai ya nahi.** Wahi sabse mushkil jodi hai.

### Example 1: Kaun sa set chalega?

- **3, 4, 8:** 3 + 4 = 7, aur 7 < 8. ❌ Triangle nahi banega.
- **5, 6, 10:** 5 + 6 = 11, aur 11 > 10. ✅ Triangle banega.
- **3, 4, 7:** 3 + 4 = 7, jod 7 ke barabar hai, bada nahi. Teeno points ek hi line par aa jaate hain, "chapta" triangle banta hai. ❌ Asli triangle nahi.
- **3, 4, 5:** 3 + 4 = 7 > 5, 3 + 5 = 8 > 4, 4 + 5 = 9 > 3. ✅ Triangle banega.

**Jaanch:** 5, 6, 10 mein dono chhoti sides ka jod 11 hai, aur 11 > 10 ✅. Agar 10 ki jagah 11 hota, to 5 + 6 = 11 barabar aa jata, aur triangle nahi banta.

## Part B: SSS (teeno sides di hain)

### Example 2: △ABC banao, AB = 5 cm, BC = 6 cm, CA = 7 cm

**Pehle check:** do chhoti sides 5 + 6 = 11 > 7 ✅. Triangle ban sakta hai.

**Kadam 1:** Ruler se ek line kheencho aur usme 5 cm ka segment **AB** banao (A bayein, B dayein).

**Kadam 2:** Compass ko **6 cm** par set karo (ruler par naapkar). Centre **B** rakho aur AB ke upar ek arc kheencho. (BC = 6 cm)

**Kadam 3:** Compass ko **7 cm** par set karo. Centre **A** rakho aur AB ke upar ek arc kheencho jo Kadam 2 ke arc ko kaate. (CA = 7 cm)

**Kadam 4:** Dono arcs jahan milte hain, us point ko **C** naam do.

**Kadam 5:** Ruler se **AC** aur **BC** jodo. △ABC taiyaar.

> **Figure 4.2** – △ABC jisme AB = 5 cm neeche ka base, BC = 6 cm (B se kheencha arc), CA = 7 cm (A se kheencha arc), aur C dono arcs ka milan bindu.

**Kyun ek hi triangle banta hai?** Compass ka kaam dekho: C ko B se theek 6 cm door hona hai, aur A se theek 7 cm door. Aisa point sirf do jagah ban sakta hai: AB ke upar aur uska mirror-image neeche. Dono triangles bilkul ek jaise (congruent) hain, bas ulte rakhe hain. Yahi **SSS** criterion hai, jisme [Congruence ke Topic 2](../congruence-of-triangles/congruence-of-triangles-part2.md) mein dekha tha ki teen sides barabar ho to triangle ek hi shape ka banta hai.

**Jaanch:** Protractor se naapo. Lagbhag ye angle aane chahiye:

- ∠A (BC ke saamne) ≈ **57.1°**
- ∠B (CA ke saamne) ≈ **78.5°**
- ∠C (AB ke saamne) ≈ **44.4°**

Jod: 57.1 + 78.5 + 44.4 = 180.0 ✅. (Protractor se 1° idhar-udhar ho sakta hai.)

> Dhyan do: bada angle bade side ke saamne hota hai. Sabse lambi side CA = 7 cm ke saamne ∠B hai, jo sabse bada (78.5°) hai.

## Part C: SAS (do sides aur unke beech ka angle)

### Example 3: △PQR banao, PQ = 5 cm, ∠Q = 60°, QR = 4 cm

Dhyan do: ∠Q, PQ aur QR ke **beech** mein hai, isliye ye SAS hai.

**Kadam 1:** Ruler se ek line kheencho aur usme 4 cm ka segment **QR** banao (Q bayein, R dayein).

**Kadam 2:** Compass ko **3 cm** par set karo. Centre **Q** rakho aur ek arc kheencho jo QR ko point **S** par kaate aur QR ke upar tak jaye.

**Kadam 3:** Compass wahi **3 cm** par rakho (badalna nahi). Centre **S** rakho aur ek arc kheencho jo Kadam 2 ke arc ko point **T** par kaate.

**Kadam 4:** Q se T tak ek lambi ray **QT** kheencho. Isse ∠TQR = 60° ban gaya (Topic 2 ka compass method).

**Kadam 5:** Compass ko **5 cm** par set karo. Centre **Q** rakho aur ray QT par ek arc kheencho jo QT ko point **P** par kaate. (PQ = 5 cm)

**Kadam 6:** P aur R ko jodo. △PQR taiyaar.

> **Figure 4.3** – △PQR jisme QR = 4 cm base, ∠Q = 60° (arcs S aur T se bana), PQ = 5 cm, aur P ray QT par hai; PR side dono ko jodti hai.

**Kyun ek hi triangle banta hai?** Ek baar Q par angle 60° aur dono sides (5 aur 4) fix ho gayi, to P aur R ki jagah fix hai. Unhe jodne wali PR ke liye koi choice bachi hi nahi. Yahi **SAS**, [Congruence ke Topic 2](../congruence-of-triangles/congruence-of-triangles-part2.md) mein padha hua.

**Jaanch:** Ruler se PR naapo, lagbhag **4.6 cm** aana chahiye. Protractor se ∠P ≈ **49.1°** aur ∠R ≈ **70.9°**. Jod: 60 + 49.1 + 70.9 = 180 ✅.

## Part D: ASA (do angle aur unke beech ki side)

### Example 4: △XYZ banao, XY = 6 cm, ∠X = 45°, ∠Y = 75°

**Pehle teesra angle:** ∠Z = 180° − 45° − 75° = **60°**. Triangle ke teeno angles ka jod 180° hota hai, aur yahan 45 + 75 = 120 < 180, to triangle ban sakta hai ✅.

**Kadam 1:** Ek line par 6 cm ka segment **XY** banao (X bayein, Y dayein).

**Kadam 2 (X par 45°):** Compass ko **3 cm** par set karo. Centre **X** rakho aur ek arc kheencho jo XY ko point **A** par kaate. Ye arc XY ke upar bhi jana chahiye.

**Kadam 3:** Wahi 3 cm. Centre **A** rakho, arc kheencho jo pichle arc ko **B** par kaate. (∠AXB = 60°)

**Kadam 4:** Wahi 3 cm. Centre **B** rakho, arc kheencho jo pichle arc ko **C** par kaate. (∠AXC = 120°)

**Kadam 5:** Wahi 3 cm. Centre **B** aur phir centre **C** rakhkar do arcs kheencho jo ek doosre ko **D** par kaate (ye arcs X par bhi milte hain; D wo doosra point hai jo X se door hai). Ray **XD** kheencho aur ye X ke arc ko **E** par kaatti hai. (∠AXE = 90°)

**Kadam 6:** Wahi 3 cm. Centre **A** aur phir centre **E** rakhkar do arcs kheencho jo **F** par kaatein (X se door wala point). Ray **XF** kheencho. Isse ∠YXF = 45°.

**Kadam 7 (Y par 75°):** Compass ko **3 cm** par set karo. Centre **Y** rakho, XY ke upar ki taraf arc kheencho jo YX ko point **G** par kaate.

**Kadam 8:** Wahi 3 cm. Centre **G** rakho, arc kheencho jo pichle arc ko **H** par kaate. (∠GYH = 60°)

**Kadam 9:** Wahi 3 cm. Centre **H** rakho, arc kheencho jo pichle arc ko **J** par kaate. (∠GYJ = 120°)

**Kadam 10:** Wahi 3 cm. Centre **H** aur phir centre **J** rakhkar arcs kheencho jo **K** par milen (Y se door wala point). Ray **YK** kheencho, jo Y ke arc ko **L** par kaatti hai. (∠GYL = 90°)

**Kadam 11:** Compass ko **2 cm** par set karo. Centre **H** aur phir centre **L** rakhkar arcs kheencho jo **M** par milen. Ray **YM** kheencho. Isse ∠XYM = 75° (60° aur 90° ke beech ka aadha).

**Kadam 12:** Ray XF aur ray YM jahan milti hain, use **Z** naam do.

(Agar protractor use karna hai: X par 45° aur Y par 75° seedha naap kar rays kheencho. Dhyan rahe, dono rays XY ke **ek hi taraf** honi chahiye.)

> **Figure 4.4** – △XYZ jisme XY = 6 cm base, ∠X = 45° (ray XF), ∠Y = 75° (ray YM), aur Z dono rays ka milan bindu; ∠Z = 60°.

**Kyun ek hi triangle banta hai?** XY ke dono sirey par angle fix ho gaye, to dono rays ki disha fix hai, aur wo sirf ek hi point Z par milti hain. Yahi **ASA** criterion hai ([Congruence Topic 2](../congruence-of-triangles/congruence-of-triangles-part2.md)).

**Jaanch:** Protractor se ∠Z naapo, **60°** aana chahiye. Angle-sum check: 45 + 75 + 60 = 180 ✅. Sides naapo: XZ ≈ **6.7 cm** (∠Y = 75° ke saamne), YZ ≈ **4.9 cm** (∠X = 45° ke saamne).

## Part E: AAS (do angle aur ek aur side)

AAS mein diya hua side, diye gaye do angles ke **beech mein nahi** hota. Trick simple hai: **teesra angle pehle nikal lo**, to ye ASA ban jata hai.

### Example 5: △ABC banao, ∠A = 60°, ∠B = 50°, BC = 6 cm

Yahan BC, ∠A ke saamne hai, ∠A aur ∠B ke beech nahi, isliye ye AAS hai.

**Pehle teesra angle:** ∠C = 180° − 60° − 50° = **70°**.

Ab BC = 6 cm hai, aur uske dono sirey par ∠B = 50° aur ∠C = 70° hain. Yaani ASA!

**Kadam 1:** Ek line par 6 cm ka segment **BC** banao.

**Kadam 2:** Protractor ka centre **B** par rakho, zero **BC** ke saath milao, aur BC ke upar ki taraf **50°** par ek chhota nishan lagao. B se us nishan se hokar ek lambi ray kheencho.

**Kadam 3:** Protractor ka centre **C** par rakho, zero **CB** ke saath milao (ab ulti taraf se padhna hai), BC ke upar ki taraf **70°** par nishan lagao. C se us nishan se hokar ek lambi ray kheencho.

**Kadam 4:** Dono rays jahan milti hain, use **A** naam do.

> **Figure 4.5** – △ABC jisme BC = 6 cm base, ∠B = 50°, ∠C = 70° (A par ∠A = 60° apne aap ban gaya), A dono rays ka milan bindu.

**Kyun ek hi triangle banta hai?** Do angle diye gaye the, teesra angle angle-sum se fix ho gaya. Ab ye ASA hai, aur ASA mein triangle fix hota hai. Isi tarah AAS bhi ek criterion hai ([Congruence Topic 2](../congruence-of-triangles/congruence-of-triangles-part2.md) mein dekho ki ye ASA se kaise nikalta hai).

**Jaanch:** Protractor se ∠A naapo, **60°** aana chahiye ✅. Sides: AC ≈ **5.3 cm** (∠B ke saamne), AB ≈ **6.5 cm** (∠C ke saamne).

**Protractor ki dhyan rakhne wali baat (Topic 2, Part A wali):** Protractor par do scales hoti hain, andar wali aur bahar wali. Zero jis taraf ki line par hai, **usi taraf ki scale** padho.

## Part F: RHS (right triangle: hypotenuse aur ek side)

### Example 6: Right triangle △DEF banao, ∠E = 90°, hypotenuse DF = 5 cm, EF = 3 cm

Teesri side DE kitni hogi? Pythagoras se: DE² = DF² − EF² = 25 − 9 = 16, to **DE = 4 cm**. (Ye 3-4-5 triplet [Pythagoras Theorem](../pythagoras-theorem/pythagoras-theorem.md) se pehchaana hua hai.)

**Kadam 1:** Ek line par 3 cm ka segment **EF** banao (E bayein, F dayein). E se aage bayein taraf line ko badha do.

**Kadam 2 (E par perpendicular, Topic 3):** Compass ko **2 cm** par set karo. Centre **E** rakho, arc kheencho jo EF ko **S** par aur badhai hui line ko **G** par kaate.

**Kadam 3:** Compass ko **3 cm** par set karo. Centre **S** rakho aur EF ke upar ek arc kheencho. Phir centre **G** rakho aur wahi 3 cm se doosra arc kheencho jo pichle arc ko **T** par kaate.

**Kadam 4:** E se T tak ray **ET** kheencho. Isse ET ⟂ EF, yaani ∠TEF = 90°.

**Kadam 5:** Compass ko **5 cm** par set karo. Centre **F** rakho aur ray ET par ek arc kheencho jo ET ko point **D** par kaate. (DF = 5 cm)

**Kadam 6:** D aur F ko jodo. △DEF taiyaar.

> **Figure 4.6** – Right triangle △DEF jisme EF = 3 cm base, ∠E = 90° (perpendicular ET, arcs S, G aur T se bana), hypotenuse DF = 5 cm, aur D ray ET par hai.

**Kyun ek hi triangle banta hai?** Right angle E par hai, 3 cm ki side fix hai, aur D ko F se theek 5 cm door hona hai. Ray ET par aisa point sirf ek hi hai, aur wahi (DE = 4) ban jata hai. Yahi **RHS** hai ([Congruence Topic 2](../congruence-of-triangles/congruence-of-triangles-part2.md), Part G).

**Jaanch:** Ruler se DE naapo, **4 cm** aana chahiye, aur Pythagoras se bhi 3² + 4² = 9 + 16 = 25 = 5² ✅. Angles: ∠D ≈ **36.9°**, ∠F ≈ **53.1°**; 90 + 36.9 + 53.1 = 180 ✅.

## Part G: Khaas triangles

### Example 7: Equilateral triangle, side 5 cm

Teeno sides barabar hain, to ye SSS ka hi simple roop hai.

**Kadam 1:** Ek line par 5 cm ka segment **LM** banao.

**Kadam 2:** Compass ko **5 cm** par set karo. Centre **L** rakho aur LM ke upar ek arc kheencho.

**Kadam 3:** Compass wahi **5 cm** (badalna nahi). Centre **M** rakho aur arc kheencho jo pichle arc ko **N** par kaate.

**Kadam 4:** N ko L aur M se jodo.

> **Figure 4.7** – Equilateral △LMN jisme LM = MN = NL = 5 cm, aur har angle 60°; N dono arcs ka milan bindu.

**Kyun?** N ki doori L se 5 cm aur M se 5 cm hai, aur LM bhi 5 cm hai. Teeno sides barabar hain.

**Jaanch:** Har angle **60°** hona chahiye (60 + 60 + 60 = 180 ✅).

### Example 8: Isosceles triangle, base 6 cm, equal sides 5 cm

Pehle check: 5 + 5 = 10 > 6 ✅.

**Kadam 1:** Ek line par 6 cm ka segment **JK** banao.

**Kadam 2 (perpendicular bisector, Topic 1):** Compass ko **4 cm** par set karo (JK ke aadhe, 3 cm, se zyada). Centre **J** rakho aur JK ke upar aur neeche arc kheencho. Wahi 4 cm, centre **K** rakho aur dono taraf arc kheencho, jo pehle arcs ko **U** (upar) aur **V** (neeche) par kaate.

**Kadam 3:** U aur V ko jodo. Ye line JK ko point **M** par kaatti hai, jo JK ka beech ka point hai (JM = MK = 3 cm).

**Kadam 4:** Compass ko **5 cm** par set karo. Centre **J** rakho aur line UV ke upar ek arc kheencho jo UV ko point **H** par kaate. (HJ = 5 cm)

**Kadam 5:** H ko J aur K se jodo.

> **Figure 4.8** – Isosceles △HJK jisme base JK = 6 cm, HJ = HK = 5 cm, H perpendicular bisector UV par hai, aur M, JK ka beech ka point hai.

**Kyun?** H perpendicular bisector par hai, to H, J aur K se barabar door hai ([Congruence Topic 2 ka SAS wala proof](../congruence-of-triangles/congruence-of-triangles-part2.md)). Isliye HK bhi 5 cm hai.

**Jaanch:** HK naapo, **5 cm** aana chahiye. Ooncha HM = **4 cm** (3² + 4² = 25 = 5² ✅). Base angles ∠J = ∠K ≈ **53.1°** aur ∠H ≈ **73.7°**; 53.1 + 53.1 + 73.7 = 179.9, rounding se lagbhag 180 ✅.

## Part H: SSA kyun kaam nahi karta?

Ab ek sawaal jo dhokha deta hai: **do sides aur ek angle di ho, par angle beech mein na ho** (SSA). Kya triangle fix ho jata hai? [Congruence Topic 2 (Part H: AAA aur SSA)](../congruence-of-triangles/congruence-of-triangles-part2.md) mein dekha tha ki **nahi**. Ab construction se dekhte hain.

### Example 9: AB = 5 cm, ∠B = 40°, AC = 4 cm

Dhyan do: ∠B, side AB aur side AC ke beech mein nahi hai (AC, ∠B ke saamne hai). Ye SSA hai.

**Kadam 1:** Ek line par 5 cm ka segment **AB** banao.

**Kadam 2:** B par protractor se ∠ABX = 40° banao, aur B se ray **BX** kheencho.

**Kadam 3:** Compass ko **4 cm** par set karo. Centre **A** rakho aur ek arc kheencho jo ray BX ko kaate.

**Kadam 4:** Dekho, arc ray BX ko **do jagah** kaat raha hai. Paas wale point ko **C₁** aur door wale ko **C₂** naam do.

**Kadam 5:** AC₁ aur AC₂ jodo.

> **Figure 4.9** – AB = 5 cm base, ray BX par ∠ABX = 40°, A se 4 cm ka arc ray ko do points C₁ aur C₂ par kaatta hai, aur dono △ABC₁ aur △ABC₂ me AB = 5 cm, ∠B = 40°, AC = 4 cm hain.

Dono triangles me diya hua sab kuch milta hai (AB = 5, ∠B = 40°, AC = 4), par ye **alag shape** ke hain:

- △ABC₁ mein BC₁ ≈ **1.4 cm**, aur ∠C₁ ≈ 126.5° (motha).
- △ABC₂ mein BC₂ ≈ **6.2 cm**, aur ∠C₂ ≈ 53.5° (patla).

**Kyun do triangles?** A se ray BX tak ki sabse chhoti doori, yaani A se BX par perpendicular (Topic 3 wala tareeka), naapo: lagbhag 3.2 cm aati hai. Arc ka radius 4 cm hai, jo 3.2 se bada, par AB (5 cm) se chhota hai, isliye arc ray ko dono taraf kaat deta hai. Isliye **SSA** triangle ko fix nahi karta, aur ye criterion nahi hai. Hamesha SAS (angle **beech** mein) ya RHS (jahan ek angle 90° hai) hi use karo.

**Jaanch:** Dono C₁ aur C₂ se A ki doori ruler se naapo: **4 cm** hi aani chahiye ✅.

## Part I: Faiz ki story ka hal

Ab wapas Ms. Banerjee aur Faiz par.

**Truss 3 m, 4 m, 5 m, scale 1 cm = 1 m.** Matlab drawing mein sides 3 cm, 4 cm, 5 cm.

Pehle check: 3 + 4 = 7 > 5 ✅. Ye 3-4-5 hai, to ye right triangle bhi hoga (3² + 4² = 25 = 5²). Faiz ne SSS se banaya:

**Kadam 1:** 4 cm ka segment **AB** (4 m ki side) banao.

**Kadam 2:** Compass ko **3 cm** par set karo. Centre **B** rakho, AB ke upar arc kheencho.

**Kadam 3:** Compass ko **5 cm** par set karo. Centre **A** rakho, AB ke upar arc kheencho jo pichle arc ko **C** par kaate.

**Kadam 4:** AC aur BC jodo.

> **Figure 4.10** – Roof truss △ABC scale 1 cm = 1 m par: AB = 4 cm, BC = 3 cm, CA = 5 cm, aur ∠B = 90°, kyunki 3² + 4² = 5².

**Jaanch:** ∠B protractor se naapo, **90°** aana chahiye ✅. Baaki angle ∠A ≈ **36.9°**, ∠C ≈ **53.1°**.

Ms. Banerjee boli, "Dekha Faiz? Hamari chhat ka frame ek right triangle nikla. Isse beam ko seedha laga sakte hain."

**Aur 3 m, 4 m, 8 m?** 3 + 4 = 7, aur 7 < 8. Do chhoti sides milkar bhi badi side tak nahi pahunchti. Isliye koi truss nahi banta. Faiz ka compass bilkul theek tha, **numbers galat the**. Ms. Banerjee ne kaha, "Drawing shuru karne se pehle sabse pehle ye jod check kar liya karo. Isse time bachta hai."

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Beech mein compass ki radius badal dena**

Angle banate waqt (jaise 60°) agar Kadam 2 mein radius 3 cm tha, aur Kadam 3 mein 2.8 cm ho gaya, to angle 60° nahi aayega. Jahan likha ho "wahi radius", wahan compass ko hilaana mat. Ek hi ishara: compass ke screw ko tight rakho.

**Galti 2: Protractor ki galat scale padhna**

Protractor par do scales hoti hain. Agar zero andar ki scale par hai aur aap bahar ki scale padh lo, to 50° ki jagah 130° mil jata hai. Hamesha dekho: **kaun si taraf zero hai**, aur usi scale se padho. Jaanch: 50° ka angle "chhota" dikhna chahiye, 130° ka "bada".

**Galti 3: Do sides ka jod check karna bhool jana**

3, 4, 8 jaise numbers par construction shuru kar dena. Pehle 5 second ka check: do chhoti sides ka jod, sabse badi se bada hai ya nahi.

**Galti 4: Diye hue sides ka order badal dena**

SAS mein "PQ = 5, ∠Q = 60°, QR = 4" ka matlab hai ki 5 cm wali side aur 4 cm wali side Q par milti hain. Agar aap 5 cm ki side ko R par laga do, to alag triangle banega. Hamesha dekho ki **angle kin do sides ke beech** hai, aur waise hi rakho.

**Galti 5: Arc ko adhoora kheenchna**

SSS mein agar dono arcs chhote kheenche, to ho sakta hai ki wo mile hi nahi, ya milne ka point saaf na dikhe. Arc thode lambe kheencho, taaki kaatne wala point saaf dikhe.

## Quick Recap

- Triangle tab banta hai jab **do chhoti sides ka jod, sabse badi side se bada** ho. 3, 4, 8 nahi banta, 5, 6, 10 banta hai.
- **SSS:** ek side kheencho, uske dono siron se baaki do sides ki radius ke arc lagao. Milan bindu teesra vertex.
- **SAS:** ek side kheencho, uske ek sirey par angle banao, us ray par doosri side ka arc kaato.
- **ASA:** ek side kheencho, uske dono siron par angle banao, rays jahan milen wahi teesra vertex.
- **AAS:** teesra angle (180° − do angle) pehle nikalo, phir ASA banao.
- **RHS:** ek side kheencho, uske sirey par 90° banao, doosre sirey se hypotenuse ke arc se ray kaato.
- **Equilateral** (SSS ka khaas roop) aur **isosceles** (perpendicular bisector se) bhi isi tarah banaye jate hain.
- **SSA** triangle ko fix nahi karta: arc ray ko do jagah kaat sakta hai.
- Har construction ke baad **Jaanch** karo: sides ruler se, angles protractor se, aur angle-sum 180° ka check.

## Practice Questions

1. Which of these sets of lengths (in cm) can be the sides of a triangle? Give a reason for each. (a) 7, 10, 18 (b) 6, 8, 10 (c) 4, 4, 8 (d) 5, 7, 11

2. Construct △ABC with AB = 4 cm, BC = 5 cm and CA = 6 cm. Measure the three angles with a protractor and write what you should expect to get, approximately.

3. Construct △PQR with PQ = 4 cm, ∠Q = 90° and QR = 3 cm. Find the length of PR without measuring, and then check by measuring.

4. Construct △ABC with AB = 7 cm, ∠A = 30° and ∠B = 80°. What is ∠C?

5. Construct △ABC with ∠B = 40°, ∠C = 65° and BC = 5 cm. Which criterion is this, and what is ∠A?

6. Construct a right triangle with hypotenuse 13 cm and one side 5 cm. Find the third side by Pythagoras before you draw it.

7. Construct an isosceles triangle with base 8 cm and equal sides 5 cm. Find its height and its base angles (approximately).

8. Can you draw two different triangles with AB = 6 cm, ∠B = 30° and AC = 4 cm? Explain using the arc from A.

9. State True or False:
   (a) A triangle with sides 5 cm, 5 cm and 10 cm can be drawn.
   (b) In SAS, the angle must lie between the two given sides.
   (c) SSA is a criterion for fixing a triangle.
   (d) Two arcs drawn with changing compass radius can still give an exact 60° angle.

### Answers

1. Do chhoti sides ka jod, sabse badi side se bada hona chahiye.
   (a) 7 + 10 = 17, aur 17 < 18. **Nahi** ❌.
   (b) 6 + 8 = 14 > 10. **Haan** ✅.
   (c) 4 + 4 = 8, jod 8 ke barabar hai, bada nahi. **Nahi** ❌ (chapta triangle).
   (d) 5 + 7 = 12 > 11. **Haan** ✅.

2. Sides 4, 5, 6: 4 + 5 = 9 > 6 ✅. Construction Part B jaisa: 6 cm ka base le sakte ho, ya 4 cm. Lagbhag angles: 6 cm ke saamne ≈ **82.8°**, 4 cm ke saamne ≈ **41.4°**, 5 cm ke saamne ≈ **55.8°**. Jod: 82.8 + 41.4 + 55.8 = 180 ✅.

3. PR² = 4² + 3² = 16 + 9 = 25, to **PR = 5 cm**. Naapne par bhi 5 cm aana chahiye ✅. Ye SAS bhi hai (90° beech mein).

4. ∠C = 180 − 30 − 80 = **70°**. AB = 7 cm kheencho, A par 30° aur B par 80° ki rays kheencho (ASA). Protractor se ∠C naapo: 70° ✅.

5. ∠A = 180 − 40 − 65 = **75°**. Diya hua side BC, ∠B aur ∠C ke beech mein hai, to ye **ASA** hai. (Agar side ek angle ke saamne hoti, to AAS hota.) Jod: 40 + 65 + 75 = 180 ✅.

6. Teesri side² = 13² − 5² = 169 − 25 = 144, to teesri side = **12 cm**. Construction: 5 cm ki side kheencho, uske sirey par 90° banao, doosre sirey se 13 cm ka arc lagao. Naapne par 12 cm ✅ (5² + 12² = 25 + 144 = 169 = 13²).

7. Height = √(5² − 4²) = √9 = **3 cm** (aadha base 4 cm). Base angles: ≈ **36.9°** (protractor se naap lo). Apex angle ≈ 180 − 2 × 36.9 = **106.3°**. Jod: 36.9 + 36.9 + 106.3 = 180.1, rounding se lagbhag 180 ✅.

8. **Haan, do triangles ban sakte hain.** A se ray BX par perpendicular girao (Topic 3) aur naapo: ye sabse chhoti doori 3 cm aati hai. A se 4 cm ke arc ka radius 3 cm se bada hai (aur AB = 6 se chhota), isliye arc ray ko do jagah kaatta hai. Do alag triangles bante hain, ek me BC ≈ 2.6 cm aur doosre me BC ≈ 7.8 cm. Is wajah se SSA se triangle fix nahi hota ([Congruence Topic 2](../congruence-of-triangles/congruence-of-triangles-part2.md)).

9. (a) **False** ❌. 5 + 5 = 10, bada nahi, to triangle nahi banega.
   (b) **True** ✅. Angle beech mein hona hi SAS ki shart hai.
   (c) **False** ❌. SSA se do triangle ban sakte hain (Part H).
   (d) **False** ❌. 60° ke liye compass ki radius poore time ek hi rehni chahiye.
