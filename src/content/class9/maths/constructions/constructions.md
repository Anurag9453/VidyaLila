# Topic 1: Geometry Box aur Basic Constructions

Ab tak geometry mein humne zyaadatar **sochna aur sabit karna** seekha: Lines and Angles mein line segment aur perpendicular dekhe, Circles mein radius aur arc, aur Congruence of Triangles mein SSS aur SAS jaise rules. Is chapter mein hum **haath se banana** seekhenge: sirf ruler aur compass se bilkul sahi figures, aur har construction ke peeche ka "kyun" bhi.

Yahan ek maze ki baat hai: jo hum seekh chuke hain (congruence), wahi aaj hamare constructions ko sahi sabit karega.

## Scout camp ka lantern

Scout camp mein Mr. Pillai ne do ped ke beech ek lambi rassi kas ke bandh di. Ped ke paas rassi ke sire par do khoonte hain: bayein khoonte ko naam do **A**, dayein ko **B**. Rassi seedhi tani hui hai, A se B tak.

Mr. Pillai ne scout Omkar se kaha, "Raat ko is rassi ke **bilkul beech mein** lantern latkana hai. Par measuring tape nahi hai. Mere paas bas ye string ka loop aur ek stick hai. Bata, kaise karega?"

Omkar ne socha: "Aankh se andaaza lagaun? Agar thoda bhi idhar-udhar hua, to lantern ek taraf jhuka rahega." Phir usne ek chaal soochi. String ke ek sire ko khoonte A par pakda, doosre sire par stick bandhi, string ko kas kar tana, aur stick se zameen par **ek gol arc** kheench diya. Phir wahi string, bina chhoti-badi kiye, khoonte B par pakadi, aur B se bhi arc kheencha. Dono arcs rassi ke upar aur neeche ek-ek jagah mile. Un dono milne wali jagahon ko jodne wali seedhi line ne rassi ko jahan kaata, wahi tha rassi ka **ekdum beech**.

Mr. Pillai ne rassi ko modkar dekha, A wala sira B wale sire par rakha, to modi hui jagah wahi thi. Omkar ne usi jagah lantern tang diya.

Kya Omkar ne jaadu kiya? Nahi. Usne ek hi tareeka use kiya, jise geometry mein **perpendicular bisector** kehte hain. Is topic ke end mein hum is rassi wali figure ko poori tarah banayenge, kadam-dar-kadam. Pehle apni geometry box se mil lete hain.

## Part A: Geometry box mein kya-kya hota hai

| Tool | Kis kaam ka |
|---|---|
| **Pencil** | Points aur lines banane ke liye. HB ya 2H jaisi patli, tez nok wali |
| **Ruler (scale)** | Sirf **seedhi line kheenchne** aur **lambai naapne** ke liye. Geometry mein ruler se arc ya angle nahi banate |
| **Compass** | **Arc aur circle** banane ke liye, aur lambai ek jagah se doosri jagah le jaane ke liye |
| **Protractor** | Angle naapne aur banane ke liye (angle ke constructions agle topic mein) |
| **Set-squares (do)** | Teen kone wale tikone: ek 45°-45°-90° ka, ek 30°-60°-90° ka. Seedhi line aur 90° ke liye |
| **Eraser** | Galat ya extra nishaan mitane ke liye. Sahi arcs ko mat mitao, wo hi kaam ke saboot hain |

**Is chapter mein hamara asli jodi hai: ruler (sirf line ke liye) aur compass (sirf arc ke liye).** Protractor aur set-square mostly jaanch ke liye kaam aayenge.

### Compass kaise pakadna hai

- Compass ko **upar ke chhote handle (knob)** se pakdo, **angoothe aur do ungliyon se**. Dono taangon se mat pakdo, warna kholai badal jayegi.
- Nok (needle) ko kagaz par halka sa daba kar rakho taaki wo sarke nahi. Itna bhi mat dabao ki kagaz mein chhed ho jaye.
- Pencil wali taang aur nok wali taang ke sire **ek hi level** par hon, yaani nok se pencil tak seedhi kholai.
- Screw ko tight rakho. Dheela screw ho to arc banate waqt compass chaudi-chhoti ho jata hai.
- Arc ek hi seedhi, halki ghumaav mein banao. Beech mein ruk-ruk kar mat banao.

### Pencil ko tez kaise rakhein

- Ek tez nok wali pencil se line patli banti hai, aur points chhote aur saaf dikhte hain. Mote nishaan mein "asli point kahan hai" ye pata hi nahi chalta.
- Pencil ki nok chheel kar sandpaper par thoda ghisa lo.
- Compass ki lead bhi tez rakho, ek taraf se tirchi ghisi hui.
- Points ko chhote **dot** se mark karo aur turant naam (A, B, C ...) likh do.

## Part B: Diye hue naap ka line segment banana

> **Segment banana:** "AB = 6 cm ka line segment banao."

**Kadam 1:** Ruler se ek seedhi halki line kheencho. Us par bayein sire ki taraf ek dot lagakar usse **A** naam do.

**Kadam 2:** Ruler ka **0 ka nishaan point A par** rakho (ruler ke kinare nahi, 0 par). Ruler ke saath saath **6 cm ke nishaan** par ek dot lagao aur usse **B** naam do.

**Kadam 3:** Ruler se A aur B ko jodo.

> **Figure 1.1** – Line segment AB jiski lambai 6 cm hai, A bayein aur B dayein sire par.

**Jaanch:** Ruler dobara rakho: A par 0 aur B par 6 cm padhna chahiye. ✅

## Part C: Segment ki copy compass se

> **Segment copy karna:** "Diya hua segment AB (maan lo 5 cm) hai. Iske barabar segment CD banao, bina ruler se naape."

Compass se hum **bina number jaane** lambai uthakar kisi aur jagah rakh sakte hain.

**Kadam 1:** Ruler se ek nayi line l kheencho. Us par ek point **C** lo.

**Kadam 2:** Compass ki **nok A par** rakho aur **pencil B par**. Ab compass ki kholai AB ke barabar ho gayi. Isko ab hilana nahi hai.

**Kadam 3:** **Centre C**, **radius AB ke barabar** (jo abhi compass mein hai). Line l ko ek arc se kaato. Jahan arc line l ko kaatta hai, wo point **D** hai.

> **Figure 1.2** – Line l par point C se D tak segment CD, jo diye hue segment AB (5 cm) ke barabar hai. A aur B ek alag segment ke sire hain.

**Jaanch:** Ruler se naapo: AB = 5 cm, CD = 5 cm. Dono barabar. ✅

**Kyun sahi hai?** Arc ke har point ki C se doori radius ke barabar hoti hai (circle ki yahi pehchaan hai, [circles.md](../circles/circles.md)). Radius AB hai, to C se D ki doori AB hi hui.

## Part D: Segment ka perpendicular bisector

Yahi Omkar ka trick tha. Ab usse ruler aur compass ke saath dhang se karte hain.

> **Perpendicular bisector banana:** "AB = 6 cm ka segment diya hai. Iska perpendicular bisector banao."

Sabse pehle segment AB banao (Part B jaise).

**Kadam 1:** Compass ko AB ke **aadhe se thoda zyada** kholo. AB = 6 cm hai, to aadha 3 cm hai. 3 cm se zyada kuch bhi chalega, hum **4 cm** lete hain. Is kholai ko ab badalna nahi hai.

**Kadam 2:** **Centre A**, **radius 4 cm**. AB ke **upar** ek arc aur AB ke **neeche** ek arc kheencho. (Arc itne lambe hon ki line AB ke dono taraf dikhein.)

**Kadam 3:** **Centre B**, **radius wahi 4 cm**. AB ke upar aur neeche arc kheencho, jo Kadam 2 ke arcs ko kaatein. Upar wale milne ke point ko **P** aur neeche wale ko **Q** naam do.

**Kadam 4:** Ruler se P aur Q ko jodo. Ye line PQ segment AB ko jis point par kaatti hai, use **M** naam do.

> **Figure 1.3** – Segment AB = 6 cm; A aur B se 4 cm radius ke arcs AB ke upar P par aur neeche Q par milte hain; perpendicular bisector PQ AB ko midpoint M par ∠AMP = 90° par kaatta hai, AM = MB = 3 cm.

**Jaanch:**
- Ruler se AM aur MB naapo: dono 3 cm. ✅
- Protractor ya set-square ka 90° wala kona M par rakho: ∠AMP = 90°. ✅
- Bonus: PA aur PB naapo, dono barabar (lagbhag 4 cm) aur QA, QB bhi barabar (4 cm). ✅

### "Thoda zyada" kyun? (aadhe se zyada hi kyun)

AB = 6 cm hai, to aadha 3 cm hai.
- Radius **2 cm** (aadhe se kam): A ka arc A se sirf 2 cm door tak jata hai, B ka arc B se sirf 2 cm door tak. Beech mein 2 cm ka gap bach jata hai (6 − 2 − 2 = 2). **Arcs milte hi nahi.**
- Radius **3 cm** (bilkul aadha): arcs sirf point M par chhoote hain, ek hi point milta hai. Line nahi ban sakti.
- Radius **4 cm** (aadhe se zyada): arcs upar aur neeche **do** jagah kaatte hain. P aur Q mil gaye.

Isliye radius hamesha **AB ke aadhe se zyada** rakho.

### Kyun kaam karta hai?

Omkar ke trick ke peeche congruence hai. Dekhte hain.

**Given:** AB ek segment. A aur B se **ek hi radius** ke arcs ne P aur Q diye. PQ ne AB ko M par kaata.

**Sabit karna hai:** AM = MB aur ∠AMP = 90°.

**Proof:**
1. P ke liye: AP = BP (dono ek hi radius ke arc par hain). Isi tarah Q ke liye: AQ = BQ.
2. △APQ aur △BPQ mein:
   - AP = BP (upar bataya)
   - AQ = BQ (upar bataya)
   - PQ = PQ (common)

   Isliye △APQ ≅ △BPQ (SSS). To ∠APQ = ∠BPQ.
3. Ab △APM aur △BPM mein:
   - AP = BP
   - ∠APM = ∠BPM (upar step 2 se, kyunki M line PQ par hai)
   - PM = PM (common)

   Isliye △APM ≅ △BPM (SAS). To **AM = MB** aur ∠AMP = ∠BMP.
4. A, M, B ek line mein hain, to ∠AMP + ∠BMP = 180° (linear pair). Dono barabar hain, to har ek **90°**.

Is tarah M beech ka point hai aur PQ ⊥ AB. SSS aur SAS dobara yaad karne ke liye dekho [congruence-of-triangles-part2.md](../congruence-of-triangles/congruence-of-triangles-part2.md).

**Ek aur nateeja:** line PQ ka **har point** A aur B se barabar door hota hai (jaise P ke liye PA = PB). Isi baat ka Example 4 mein use karenge.

## Part E: Segment ko 2 aur 4 barabar hisson mein baantna

**2 barabar hisse:** bas ek baar perpendicular bisector bana do. M ne AB ko do barabar hisson mein baant diya: AM = MB.

**4 barabar hisse:** bisector ko **dobara** lagao, ab AM aur MB par.

> **Segment ko 4 barabar hisson mein baantna:** "AB = 8 cm ko 4 barabar hisson mein baanto. Har hissa 2 cm hona chahiye."

**Kadam 1:** AB = 8 cm banao. **Centre A**, **radius 5 cm** (aadhe 4 cm se zyada), AB ke upar aur neeche arc. **Centre B**, **radius 5 cm**, upar aur neeche arc. Milne ke points **P** (upar) aur **Q** (neeche). PQ jodo. PQ, AB ko **M** par kaatega. AM = MB = 4 cm.

**Kadam 2 (AM ko bisect karo):** AM = 4 cm, aadha 2 cm. Radius lo **3 cm** (2 cm se zyada). **Centre A**, **radius 3 cm**, AB ke upar aur neeche arc. **Centre M**, **radius 3 cm**, AB ke upar aur neeche arc. Milne ke points **R** (upar) aur **S** (neeche). RS jodo. RS, AM ko **X** par kaatega. AX = XM = 2 cm.

**Kadam 3 (MB ko bisect karo):** MB = 4 cm, aadha 2 cm. Radius **3 cm** hi. **Centre M**, **radius 3 cm**, upar aur neeche arc. **Centre B**, **radius 3 cm**, upar aur neeche arc. Milne ke points **T** (upar) aur **U** (neeche). TU jodo. TU, MB ko **Y** par kaatega. MY = YB = 2 cm.

> **Figure 1.4** – Segment AB = 8 cm, jis par points A, X, M, Y, B is kram mein hain, aur AX = XM = MY = YB = 2 cm. Bisectors PQ (M par), RS (X par) aur TU (Y par) AB ko 90° par kaatte hain.

**Jaanch:** Ruler se A se har point ki doori: AX = 2 cm, AM = 4 cm, AY = 6 cm, AB = 8 cm. Chaaron hisse 2 cm ke. 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 8 ✅

**Dhyan:** Is tareeke se segment ko 2, 4, 8, ... (2 ki powers) hisson mein baant sakte hain. 3 ya 5 barabar hisson ke liye alag tareeka lagega, wo is chapter ka hissa nahi hai.

## Part F: Kahani ka hal, rassi ka beech

Ab Omkar ki figure ko "kadam-dar-kadam" banate hain. Maan lo rassi 8 m lambi hai aur kagaz par hum 1 cm = 1 m lete hain. To AB = 8 cm. String ka loop 6 m ka tha, to kagaz par 6 cm.

**Kadam 1:** Rassi ko segment AB maano (A bayein khoonta, B dayein khoonta). Aadha AB = 4 cm. String 6 cm hai, ye 4 cm se zyada hai, to arcs milenge.

**Kadam 2:** **Centre A**, **radius 6 cm (string ka naap)**, rassi ke upar aur neeche arc.

**Kadam 3:** **Centre B**, **radius wahi 6 cm** (wahi string, bina badle), upar aur neeche arc. Milne ke points: **P** (upar) aur **Q** (neeche).

**Kadam 4:** P aur Q ko jodo. Ye line rassi AB ko **M** par kaatti hai.

> **Figure 1.5** – Rassi AB = 8 cm (kagaz par), A aur B se 6 cm radius ke arcs P aur Q par milte hain, line PQ rassi ko midpoint M par kaatti hai, AM = MB = 4 cm.

**Jaanch (Mr. Pillai ka tareeka):** Rassi ko modkar A ko B par rakho. Modi hui jagah M par aati hai. Kagaz par ruler se: AM = 4 cm aur MB = 4 cm. ✅

Lantern wahin M par tang gaya. Na tape, na andaaza.

## Part G: Solved constructions

### Example 1: Segment ka midpoint nikalo

**Question:** XY = 9 cm ka segment banao aur uska midpoint nikalo.

**Kadam 1:** Ruler se XY = 9 cm banao. Aadha 4.5 cm hai, to radius **5 cm** lo (4.5 se zyada).

**Kadam 2:** **Centre X**, **radius 5 cm**, XY ke upar aur neeche arc.

**Kadam 3:** **Centre Y**, **radius 5 cm**, upar aur neeche arc. Milne ke points **P** (upar) aur **Q** (neeche).

**Kadam 4:** P aur Q ko jodo. PQ, XY ko **M** par kaatti hai. M midpoint hai.

> **Figure 1.6** – Segment XY = 9 cm, jiska midpoint M hai; perpendicular bisector PQ M par XY ko 90° par kaatti hai aur XM = MY = 4.5 cm.

**Jaanch:** Ruler se XM = 4.5 cm aur MY = 4.5 cm. 4.5 + 4.5 = 9 ✅ aur protractor se ∠XMP = 90° ✅

---

### Example 2: Segment par 1.25 cm ka point

**Question:** AB = 5 cm banao. A se 1.25 cm door AB par ek point X dhoondho (bina 1.25 naape).

**Soch:** 5 ÷ 2 = 2.5, 2.5 ÷ 2 = 1.25. Yaani do baar bisect karo.

**Kadam 1:** AB = 5 cm banao. Aadha 2.5 cm hai, radius **3 cm** lo. **Centre A**, **radius 3 cm**, upar aur neeche arc. **Centre B**, **radius 3 cm**, upar aur neeche arc. Milne ke points **P** aur **Q**. PQ jodo, wo AB ko **M** par kaatti hai. AM = 2.5 cm.

**Kadam 2:** AM ka aadha 1.25 cm hai, radius **2 cm** lo (1.25 se zyada). **Centre A**, **radius 2 cm**, upar aur neeche arc. **Centre M**, **radius 2 cm**, upar aur neeche arc. Milne ke points **R** aur **S**. RS jodo, wo AM ko **X** par kaatti hai.

> **Figure 1.7** – Segment AB = 5 cm par midpoint M aur A se pehle chauthai ka point X; AX = 1.25 cm, XM = 1.25 cm, MB = 2.5 cm. Bisector PQ AB ko M par aur bisector RS AB ko X par kaatta hai.

**Jaanch:** Ruler se AX naapo: 1.25 cm (ya 12.5 mm). Aur AB ka chauthai hissa: 5 ÷ 4 = 1.25 ✅

---

### Example 3: Do segments ka jod

**Question:** AB = 4 cm aur CD = 3 cm diye hain. Ek aisa segment PR banao jo AB + CD ke barabar ho, sirf compass se lambai utaarkar.

**Kadam 1:** Ruler se ek line l kheencho. Us par ek point **P** lo.

**Kadam 2:** Compass ko A par nok aur B par pencil rakh kar AB (4 cm) ke barabar kholo. **Centre P**, **radius AB**, line l par arc kaato. Kaatne ke point ko **Q** naam do. PQ = 4 cm.

**Kadam 3:** Ab compass ko C par nok aur D par pencil rakh kar CD (3 cm) ke barabar kholo. **Centre Q**, **radius CD**, line l par Q ke aage (P se door wali taraf) arc kaato. Kaatne ke point ko **R** naam do. QR = 3 cm.

> **Figure 1.8** – Line l par points P, Q, R is kram mein, PQ = AB = 4 cm aur QR = CD = 3 cm, isliye PR = AB + CD = 7 cm.

**Jaanch:** Ruler se PR naapo: 4 + 3 = 7 cm ✅

---

### Example 4: Do gaon se barabar door kuan

**Question:** Do gaon A aur B ke beech doori 6 cm (nakshe par) hai. Ek aisa point W dhoondho jo A aur B dono se **5 cm** door ho.

**Soch:** Jo point A aur B se barabar door hota hai wo perpendicular bisector par hota hai. Hum wahi arcs lagayenge, bas radius 5 cm.

**Kadam 1:** AB = 6 cm banao. Aadha 3 cm hai, aur radius 5 cm usse zyada hai, to arcs milenge.

**Kadam 2:** **Centre A**, **radius 5 cm**, AB ke upar aur neeche arc. **Centre B**, **radius 5 cm**, AB ke upar aur neeche arc. Upar milne ka point **W**, neeche milne ka point **W'**. Dono A aur B se 5 cm door hain.

**Kadam 3:** Ruler se W aur W' ko jodo. Ye line (perpendicular bisector) AB ko **M** par kaatti hai. W ko A aur B se bhi jodo.

> **Figure 1.9** – Segment AB = 6 cm aur AB ke upar point W jo A aur B dono se 5 cm door hai (WA = WB = 5 cm); AB ka midpoint M hai, WM ⊥ AB aur WM = 4 cm. Neeche ka point W' bhi A aur B se 5 cm door hai.

**Jaanch:** Ruler se WA = 5 cm aur WB = 5 cm ✅. WM naapo: 4 cm. (Dekho: AM = 3, WM = 4, WA = 5, ye wahi 3-4-5 wala tikona hai: 3² + 4² = 9 + 16 = 25 = 5² ✅.)

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Radius aadhe se kam lena**

AB = 8 cm ho aur radius 3 cm lo (aadha 4 cm hai). A ka arc A se 3 cm tak aur B ka arc B se 3 cm tak jata hai, 8 − 3 − 3 = 2 cm ka gap bach jata hai. **Arcs kabhi milte nahi.** Hamesha radius AB ke aadhe se zyada rakho.

**Galti 2: Do arcs ke beech compass ki kholai badal dena**

Agar A se 5 cm aur B se 4.5 cm ka arc lagaya, to P aisi jagah banega jahan PA ≠ PB. Phir PQ beech se nahi jayegi, aur M midpoint nahi rahega. Dono arcs ke liye **ek hi radius** hona zaroori hai (proof mein AP = BP isi par tika tha).

**Galti 3: Points ko mark na karna ya naam na dena**

Arcs ke crossing points P, Q ko bina dot lagaye "aankh se" jod diya. Line thodi tedhi ho jati hai. Har crossing par chhota dot lagao aur P, Q, M naam likho.

**Galti 4: Mothi (blunt) pencil**

Mothi pencil se line mote nishaan banati hai. Crossing point ka sahi jagah pata nahi chalta, aur 0.5 cm ka farak bhi aa sakta hai. Pencil ko hamesha tez rakho.

**Galti 5: Ruler se arc ya compass se seedhi line banana**

Ruler sirf seedhi line aur naapne ke liye hai, compass sirf arc ke liye. Ek ka kaam doosre se mat karo.

## Quick Recap

- Geometry box: pencil (tez), ruler (sirf seedhi line aur naap), compass (arc aur lambai copy), protractor, do set-squares, eraser.
- Compass ko upar ke handle se pakdo, nok aur pencil ek level par, screw tight.
- **Segment AB = 6 cm** banane ke liye ruler ka 0 point A par rakho aur 6 cm par B mark karo.
- **Segment copy** karne ke liye: nok A par, pencil B par, phir centre C se usi radius ka arc.
- **Perpendicular bisector:** A aur B se **ek hi radius (AB ke aadhe se zyada)** ke arcs upar aur neeche. Crossing points P, Q ko jodo. PQ, AB ko M par kaatti hai, **AM = MB** aur **∠AMP = 90°**.
- **Kyun:** SSS se △APQ ≅ △BPQ, phir SAS se △APM ≅ △BPM.
- Bisector ko dobara AM aur MB par lagao to segment 4 barabar hisson mein baant jata hai.
- Galtiyan: radius aadhe se kam, beech mein kholai badalna, points mark na karna, mothi pencil.

## Practice Questions

1. Draw a line segment AB = 7.5 cm and construct its perpendicular bisector. State the radius you used and why that radius works. What is AM?

2. Draw a segment PQ = 12 cm and divide it into 4 equal parts using only a compass and ruler. State the radius you used for each bisector and the length of each part.

3. A student tries to bisect AB = 10 cm using a compass opening of 4 cm. Explain why the arcs will not meet.

4. Draw a segment AB = 4.5 cm and a segment CD = 3 cm. Using a compass, construct a segment PR = AB + CD, and another segment ST = AB − CD. Find the length of each by calculation and check with the ruler.

5. In the figure for the perpendicular bisector of AB, P lies on the bisector and PA = 7 cm. What is PB? Give the reason in one line.

6. Construct a point W that is 5 cm from each of two points A and B with AB = 8 cm. How far is W from the midpoint M of AB?

7. State True or False:
   (a) A ruler can be used to draw an arc in geometry constructions.
   (b) If the compass opening is exactly half of AB, the arcs from A and B meet at two points.
   (c) The perpendicular bisector of AB meets AB at 90°.
   (d) It is fine to change the compass opening between the arc from A and the arc from B.

8. Mr. Pillai's rope is 10 m long and Omkar's string loop is 7 m long. Will Omkar's trick work? What if the string were only 4 m long?

### Answers

1. AB = 7.5 cm, aadha = 3.75 cm. Radius **3.75 cm se zyada** chahiye, jaise **4.5 cm**. A aur B se 4.5 cm ke arcs upar aur neeche, crossing points P, Q jodo. AM = 7.5 ÷ 2 = **3.75 cm**.
   Kyun kaam karta hai: radius aadhe se zyada hai to arcs milte hain.
   Jaanch: AM = MB = 3.75 cm aur ∠AMP = 90° ✅

2. PQ = 12 cm. Pehla bisector: aadha = 6 cm, radius **7 cm** (6 se zyada). Isse M milega, PM = MQ = 6 cm. Ab PM aur MQ ko bisect karo: aadha = 3 cm, radius **4 cm** (3 se zyada). Har hissa **3 cm**.
   Jaanch: 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12 ✅

3. AB = 10 cm, aadha 5 cm. Radius 4 cm, 5 cm se kam hai. A ka arc A se 4 cm tak aur B ka arc B se 4 cm tak jata hai, 4 + 4 = 8 cm, 10 cm se kam. Dono arcs ke beech 2 cm ka gap rah jata hai, to arcs milte hi nahi.

4. PR = AB + CD = 4.5 + 3 = **7.5 cm**. ST = AB − CD = 4.5 − 3 = **1.5 cm**.
   PR ke liye: line par P lo, centre P radius AB se Q, phir centre Q radius CD se R (P ki doosri taraf).
   ST ke liye: line par S lo, centre S radius AB se U, phir centre U radius CD se T (U se S ki taraf, yaani wapas). ST = AB − CD = 1.5 cm.
   Jaanch: ruler se PR = 7.5 cm aur ST = 1.5 cm ✅

5. PB = **7 cm**. P perpendicular bisector par hai, aur usske har point ki A aur B se doori barabar hoti hai (PA = PB, SSS wale proof ki wahi baat).

6. AB = 8 cm, aadha 4 cm, radius 5 cm usse zyada hai. Centre A, radius 5 cm aur centre B, radius 5 cm ke arcs ek-doosre ko W par kaatenge. WM: AM = 4 cm, WA = 5 cm, to WM = √(25 − 16) = √9 = **3 cm**.
   Jaanch: ruler se WM = 3 cm ✅

7. (a) **False** ❌. Ruler sirf seedhi line aur naap ke liye hai.
