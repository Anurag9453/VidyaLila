# Topic 1: Introduction to Coordinate Geometry

Ab tak maths mein humne do alag duniya dekhi hain:
- **Algebra**, jahan x, y, equations aur numbers hote hain.
- **Geometry**, jahan points, lines, triangles aur shapes hote hain.

Is chapter mein hum in dono duniyaon ko **jodna** seekhenge. Iska pehla kadam hai: kisi bhi jagah ko **numbers se bata paana**. Isi se shuru karte hain.

---

## Ek makkhi ki kahani

Bahut saal pehle, France mein **René Descartes** naam ke ek bade mathematician the. Kehte hain ki ek din wo bimaar the aur bistar par lete hue chhat ko dekh rahe the. Chhat par ek **makkhi** idhar udhar ghoom rahi thi.

Unke mann mein ek sawal aaya:

> "Main kisi ko **theek theek** kaise bataun ki makkhi is waqt **kahan** baithi hai?"

"Chhat par" kehna kaafi nahi tha, chhat to badi hai. "Kone ke paas" bhi saaf nahi tha, kaunsa kona? Kitna paas?

Phir unhe ek tarkeeb soojhi. Kamre ka ek kona chuno. Wahan se:
- Ek deewar ke saath saath naapo ki makkhi **kitni door** hai.
- Doosri deewar ke saath saath naapo ki makkhi **kitni door** hai.

Jaise: "Kone se **3 foot** is deewar ke saath, aur **2 foot** us deewar ke saath."

Bas! Ab sirf **do numbers (3 aur 2)** se makkhi ki jagah ekdum pakki ho gayi. Koi bhi insaan ye do number sunkar theek usi jagah pahunch sakta hai.

```
   Deewar 2
     |
   2 ft  . . . . . *  <- makkhi
     |             .
     |             .
     +-------------+------- Deewar 1
   Kona          3 ft
```

Descartes ki isi soch se maths ki ek nayi shakha bani: **Coordinate Geometry**.

### Roz ki zindagi mein yahi tarkeeb

Aap ye tarkeeb pehle se use karte ho, bas naam nahi pata tha:

- **Dost ka ghar:** "Main road se **4th gali** mein muddna, phir **6th ghar**." Do numbers, ek pakki jagah.
- **Chess board:** Har khaane ka naam hota hai, jaise **e4**. Ek letter column batata hai, ek number row. Do cheezein, ek pakka khaana.
- **Excel sheet:** Cell **B7** matlab column B aur row 7.
- **Map par jagah:** Latitude aur longitude, do numbers jo duniya ki kisi bhi jagah ko pakka batate hain. GPS isi par chalta hai.

Har jagah ek hi baat: **ek flat jagah par kisi point ko batane ke liye do numbers chahiye.**

## Part A: Coordinate Geometry kya hai?

**Coordinate geometry** maths ki wo shakha hai jisme hum **points, lines aur shapes ko numbers aur equations se** describe karte hain.

Ise ek pul (bridge) ki tarah socho:

```
   ALGEBRA                              GEOMETRY
 (numbers, x, y,      <=== pul ===>     (points, lines,
  equations)        Coordinate Geometry   shapes)
```

Is pul ki wajah se:
- Ek **point** ko hum do numbers mein likh sakte hain.
- Ek **line** ko hum ek equation mein likh sakte hain.
- Shapes ki lambai, area jaisi cheezein numbers se calculate kar sakte hain, bina scale aur protractor ke.

Isiliye ise **Analytical Geometry** bhi kehte hain.

Aaj ye har jagah kaam aata hai: Google Maps, video games mein character ki jagah, mobile screen par aapki ungli kahan touch hui, aur robots ki movement. Sab ke peeche coordinate geometry hai.

## Part B: Cartesian Coordinates aur Descartes

**René Descartes** (1596-1650) France ke ek mathematician aur philosopher the. Unhone sabse pehle ye saaf tarika diya ki geometry ke points ko numbers se kaise likhe.

Unka Latin naam **Cartesius** tha. Isliye unke naam par:
- Is system ko **Cartesian system** kehte hain.
- Points ke numbers ko **Cartesian coordinates** kehte hain.
- Jis flat sataha par ye sab hota hai, use **Cartesian plane** kehte hain.

> **Cartesian coordinates** = wo do numbers jo Descartes ke tarike se kisi point ki jagah batate hain.

## Part C: Coordinate System

### Ek number line se shuru karo

Aap number line jaante ho:

```
  <---+----+----+----+----+----+----+---->
     -3   -2   -1    0    1    2    3
```

Ek number line par koi bhi jagah **ek number** se batayi ja sakti hai. Jaise 2 ka matlab: 0 se 2 kadam right.

Par ek number line sirf **ek seedhi line** hai. Makkhi to poori chhat par kahin bhi ho sakti hai. Ek number kaafi nahi hai.

### Do number lines, ek doosre par seedhi

Descartes ka idea tha: **do number lines lo, aur unhe ek doosre par 90° (perpendicular) rakh do**, aise ki dono ka **0 ek hi jagah** mile.

```
                 y
                 ^
                 |  3
                 |  2
                 |  1
   <---+----+----+----+----+----+---> x
      -3   -2   -1  O  1    2    3
                 | -1
                 | -2
                 | -3
                 v
```

Ye do lines aur unka milne ka point milkar ek **coordinate system** banate hain. Ab plane ki har jagah ko hum in do lines ki madad se naap sakte hain.

> **Coordinate system** = do perpendicular number lines, jo ek hi point (0) par milti hain, aur plane ki har jagah naapne ka aadhar hain.

## Part D: Coordinate Axes aur Origin

Upar wali do lines ke apne naam hain:

| Naam | Kaisi line | Kis disha mein |
|---|---|---|
| **x-axis** | Aadi (horizontal), farsh ki tarah sapaat | Left se right |
| **y-axis** | Khadi (vertical), deewar ki tarah seedhi | Neeche se upar |
| **Origin (O)** | Jahan x-axis aur y-axis ek doosre ko kaatti hain | Dono ka 0 yahin hai |

Dono lines ko milakar **coordinate axes** kehte hain ("axes" = "axis" ka bahuvachan).

```
                 y-axis (khadi)
                 ^
                 |
                 |
   <-------------O-------------> x-axis (aadi)
                 |
                 |
                 v
```

### Yaad rakhne ke chhote tarike

- **x-axis aadi hai:** x ko aise socho jaise wo zameen par leta hua hai.
- **y-axis khadi hai:** "**y** upar ki taraf haath utha ke khada hai."
- **Origin** shabd "origin" yaani **shuruaat** se aaya hai. Saari naap yahin se shuru hoti hai, isliye ise O likhte hain.

### Kuch zaroori baatein

- Dono axes ek doosre par **90°** par hote hain. Isiliye ise **rectangular coordinate system** bhi kehte hain.
- Dono axes **dono taraf bina ant ke** failte hain. Isliye unke siron par teer (arrow) banate hain.
- Dono axes par ek jaisi doori ko ek unit maana jata hai (graph paper ke chhote khaane ki tarah).
- Ye do axes milkar poore plane ko **chaar hisson** mein baant dete hain. Ye hisse kya kehlate hain, wo hum Topic 2 mein dekhenge.

## Part E: Ordered Pair

### Kahani wapas: makkhi

Makkhi ke liye humne kaha tha: "is deewar ke saath **3**, aur us deewar ke saath **2**."

Ab socho kisi ne galti se ulta sun liya: "is deewar ke saath **2**, us ke saath **3**." Kya wo makkhi tak pahunchega? **Nahi!** Wo bilkul alag jagah pahunch jayega.

Yaani do numbers hi kaafi nahi hain, **unka kram (order) bhi pakka hona chahiye**: pehla number kis cheez ke liye, doosra kis cheez ke liye.

Isiliye coordinate geometry mein hum do numbers ko ek **tay kram** mein likhte hain:

> **(x, y)**: pehla number **hamesha x-axis ki disha** ka, doosra **hamesha y-axis ki disha** ka.

Aise do numbers ka joda jisme kram maayne rakhta hai, use **ordered pair** kehte hain.

### Kram kyun zaroori hai?

Gali wale example se:
- "4th gali, 6th ghar" aur "6th gali, 4th ghar" alag alag ghar hain.

Chess se:
- e4 aur 4e ka koi matlab nahi banta. Pehle column, phir row, ye niyam pakka hai.

Isi tarah:

**(3, 2) ≠ (2, 3)**

Ye dono alag points hain.

Kab dono barabar honge? Sirf tab jab dono number same ho. Jaise **(5, 5)** ko palat do to bhi **(5, 5)** hi rahega.

> **(a, b) = (b, a)** sirf tab, jab **a = b** ho.

### Likhne ka tarika

- Hamesha **gol bracket ( )** mein likho.
- Beech mein **comma** lagao.
- Pehle x, phir y.

| Sahi | Galat |
|---|---|
| (3, 2) | 3, 2 (bracket nahi) |
| (-4, 7) | {-4, 7} (galat bracket; ye set ka bracket hai) |
| (0, 5) | (5 0) (comma nahi) |

### Do ordered pairs kab barabar hote hain?

Do ordered pairs **tabhi barabar** hote hain jab:
- **pehla number pehle number ke barabar** ho, **aur**
- **doosra number doosre number ke barabar** ho.

> **(a, b) = (c, d)** ka matlab hai **a = c aur b = d**.

Isse hum unknown numbers nikal sakte hain.

**Example 1:** Agar (x + 1, 3) = (5, y - 2), to x aur y nikalo.

Pehle number barabar: x + 1 = 5, to **x = 4**.
Doosre number barabar: 3 = y - 2, to **y = 5**.

Jaanch: (4 + 1, 3) = (5, 3) aur (5, 5 - 2) = (5, 3). Dono barabar ✅

**Example 2:** Agar (2a, b + 3) = (8, -1), to a aur b nikalo.

2a = 8, to **a = 4**.
b + 3 = -1, to **b = -4**.

Jaanch: (2 × 4, -4 + 3) = (8, -1) ✅

**Example 3:** Agar (x - y, x + y) = (2, 8), to x aur y nikalo.

x - y = 2 ...(1)
x + y = 8 ...(2)

Dono ko jodo: 2x = 10, to **x = 5**.
(2) mein rakho: 5 + y = 8, to **y = 3**.

Jaanch: (5 - 3, 5 + 3) = (2, 8) ✅

## Part F: Convention of Signs (Signs ka niyam)

### Kahani: building ki lift

Socho aap ek mall ki lift mein ho. Ground floor ko **0** maano.
- Upar ke floors: 1, 2, 3, ... (**positive**)
- Neeche basement: B1, B2, ... jinhe hum **-1, -2, ...** keh sakte hain (**negative**)

Ek aur example: **temperature**. 0°C se upar garmi ki taraf **+**, aur 0°C se neeche thand ki taraf **−**. Shimla mein sardi mein -3°C ka matlab hai 0 se 3 degree neeche.

Yaani **0 ek beech ki jagah hai**, aur uske ek taraf **plus**, doosri taraf **minus**. Coordinate axes par bhi bilkul yahi hota hai, aur **origin O hi wo 0 hai**.

### Niyam

| Disha (origin se) | Kaunsa axis | Sign |
|---|---|---|
| **Right** (daayein) | x-axis | **Positive (+)** |
| **Left** (baayein) | x-axis | **Negative (−)** |
| **Upar** | y-axis | **Positive (+)** |
| **Neeche** | y-axis | **Negative (−)** |

```
                    y
                    ^
                    |  + (upar)
                    |
   (left) −  <------O------>  + (right)   x
                    |
                    |  − (neeche)
                    v
```

Ise yaad rakhne ka aasan tarika: **jaise number line par hota hai, waisa hi.** Number line par right taraf bade (positive) numbers hote hain, left taraf negative. y-axis bas ek khadi number line hai, jisme upar positive aur neeche negative.

### Sign ka asal matlab

Sign batata hai **disha**, aur number batata hai **doori**.

- x-axis ki disha mein **+4** ka matlab: origin se **4 unit right**.
- x-axis ki disha mein **-4** ka matlab: origin se **4 unit left**.
- y-axis ki disha mein **+2** ka matlab: origin se **2 unit upar**.
- y-axis ki disha mein **-2** ka matlab: origin se **2 unit neeche**.

Dhyan do: **+4 aur -4 dono ki doori 4 hi hai**, bas disha ulti hai. Jaise ground floor se 2 floor upar aur 2 floor neeche, dono 2 floor door hain.

### Ordered pair mein signs

Ab ordered pair (x, y) ko phir dekho. Har number ka sign batata hai ki **kis taraf** jaana hai:
- **x ka sign:** right (+) ya left (−)
- **y ka sign:** upar (+) ya neeche (−)

Jaise ordered pair (-3, 5) mein:
- -3 kehta hai: x ki disha mein **3 left**.
- 5 kehta hai: y ki disha mein **5 upar**.

Kisi point tak sach mein kaise pahunchte hain aur use graph par kaise lagate hain, wo Topic 2 ka kaam hai. Abhi bas itna samajh lo ki **sign = disha, number = doori**.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: x aur y axis ki disha ulti samajhna**
x-axis **aadi** (left-right) hai, y-axis **khadi** (upar-neeche). "y haath utha ke khada hai" yaad rakho.

**Galti 2: Ordered pair mein kram badal dena**
(3, 2) aur (2, 3) alag hain. Pehla number hamesha x ka, doosra y ka.

**Galti 3: (a, b) = (b, a) hamesha sahi samajhna**
Ye sirf tab sahi hai jab a = b. Jaise (4, 4) palatne par bhi (4, 4), par (4, 1) palatne par (1, 4), jo alag hai.

**Galti 4: Negative ka matlab "chhota" ya "galat" samajhna**
-4 ka matlab "4 left" hai, ye koi galti nahi hai. Sign sirf **disha** batata hai. -4 aur +4 dono origin se 4 unit door hain.

**Galti 5: Neeche aur left ke sign mein confuse hona**
Left = − aur neeche = −. Right = + aur upar = +. Number line jaisa hi socho.

**Galti 6: Ordered pair galat bracket mein likhna**
Ordered pair **( )** mein likhte hain, comma ke saath. { } set ka bracket hai, wo yahan nahi chalega.

**Galti 7: Ordered pairs ki barabari mein sirf ek hissa milana**
(x + 1, 3) = (5, y - 2) mein **dono** cheezein barabar karni hain: x + 1 = 5 **aur** 3 = y - 2. Sirf ek se kaam nahi chalega.

## Quick Recap

- **Coordinate geometry** algebra aur geometry ko jodti hai: points ko numbers se, lines ko equations se.
- Ise **René Descartes** ne diya. Unke Latin naam Cartesius par ise **Cartesian** system kehte hain.
- Flat jagah (plane) par kisi point ko batane ke liye **do numbers** chahiye.
- **Coordinate system** = do **perpendicular number lines** jo **0 par** milti hain.
- **x-axis** aadi (horizontal), **y-axis** khadi (vertical). Dono milte hain **origin O** par.
- **Ordered pair (x, y):** pehla number x ki disha ka, doosra y ki disha ka. **Kram maayne rakhta hai:** (a, b) ≠ (b, a) jab tak a = b na ho.
- **(a, b) = (c, d)** tabhi jab **a = c aur b = d**.
- **Signs ka niyam:** origin se **right +, left −, upar +, neeche −**.
- **Sign = disha, number = doori.**

## Practice Questions

1. Fill in the blanks:
   (a) The horizontal axis is called the __________.
   (b) The vertical axis is called the __________.
   (c) The point where the two axes meet is called the __________.
   (d) The Cartesian system is named after the mathematician __________.

2. Explain in your own words what coordinate geometry is.

3. State whether each statement is true or false. Give a reason.
   (a) The ordered pairs (2, 7) and (7, 2) are the same.
   (b) The ordered pairs (6, 6) and (6, 6) are the same.
   (c) The x-axis and the y-axis are perpendicular to each other.
   (d) A negative number on the x-axis is to the right of the origin.

4. Find x and y if:
   (a) (3x - 1, 2y + 4) = (8, 10)
   (b) (x/2 + 1, y - 3) = (3, -2)
   (c) (x + y, x - y) = (7, 1)

5. Write the sign (+ or −) for each direction, measured from the origin:
   (a) 5 units to the left along the x-axis
   (b) 3 units upwards along the y-axis
   (c) 2 units downwards along the y-axis
   (d) 6 units to the right along the x-axis

6. In the ordered pair (-4, 6), what does each number tell you about direction and distance from the origin?

7. Why do we need two numbers, and not just one, to describe the position of a point on a flat surface? Give one example from daily life.

### Answers

1. (a) **x-axis**  (b) **y-axis**  (c) **origin**  (d) **René Descartes**
2. Coordinate geometry maths ki wo shakha hai jo **geometry ko algebra se jodti hai**. Isme points ki jagah ko numbers (coordinates) se aur lines ko equations se likhte hain.
3. (a) **False.** Ordered pair mein kram maayne rakhta hai, aur 2 ≠ 7, to dono alag hain.
   (b) **True.** Dono numbers same hain, to dono pairs barabar hain.
   (c) **True.** Dono axes 90° par ek doosre ko kaatte hain.
   (d) **False.** x-axis par negative numbers origin ke **left** mein hote hain.
4. (a) 3x - 1 = 8 → 3x = 9 → **x = 3**. 2y + 4 = 10 → 2y = 6 → **y = 3**.
   Jaanch: (9 - 1, 6 + 4) = (8, 10) ✅
   (b) x/2 + 1 = 3 → x/2 = 2 → **x = 4**. y - 3 = -2 → **y = 1**.
   Jaanch: (2 + 1, 1 - 3) = (3, -2) ✅
   (c) x + y = 7 aur x - y = 1. Dono jodo: 2x = 8 → **x = 4**. Phir 4 + y = 7 → **y = 3**.
   Jaanch: (4 + 3, 4 - 3) = (7, 1) ✅
5. (a) **−** (left)  (b) **+** (upar)  (c) **−** (neeche)  (d) **+** (right)
6. -4 batata hai: x ki disha mein origin se **4 unit left** (minus = left). 6 batata hai: y ki disha mein origin se **6 unit upar** (plus = upar).
7. Flat sataha do dishaon mein failti hai (left-right aur upar-neeche), isliye har disha ke liye ek number chahiye. Ek number se sirf ek seedhi line par jagah batayi ja sakti hai. Example: "4th gali, 6th ghar", ya chess ka khaana e4, ya Excel ka cell B7.
