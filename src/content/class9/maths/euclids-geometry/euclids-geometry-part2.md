# Topic 2: Geometry ke kuch zaroori shabd, aur Lines ke Incidence Axioms

Topic 1 mein humne geometry ki kahani dekhi thi: kaise purane zamane mein log zameen naapte the, aur kaise Euclid ne saara gyaan ek kitaab (Elements) mein jod diya. Wahan humne ye bhi seekha ki **axioms aur postulates** wo baatein hain jinhe hum bina proof ke sach maan lete hain, aur Euclid ke 7 axioms aur 5 postulates dekhe.

Par un postulates mein baar baar kuch shabd aate hain: **point, line, seedhi line, surface**. Ab sawal ye hai ki ye shabd asal mein hain kya? Euclid ne inki definition kaise di? Aur kya wo definitions sach mein kaam karti hain? Is topic mein hum yahi dekhenge, aur saath mein lines ke baare mein kuch pakke niyam (incidence axioms) seekhenge.

---

## Chhote bhai ke "kyun?" wali kahani

Socho aapka chhota bhai Chintu aapki geometry ki copy dekh raha hai.

**Chintu:** Didi, ye "point" kya hota hai?
**Aap:** Point wo cheez hai jiska koi **hissa** nahi hota.
**Chintu:** "Hissa" kya hota hai?
**Aap:** Hissa matlab... kisi cheez ka **tukda**.
**Chintu:** "Tukda" kya hota hai?
**Aap:** Tukda matlab... kisi cheez ka chhota **part**.
**Chintu:** Aur "part" kya hota hai?
**Aap:** ...

Dekha? Har shabd ko samjhane ke liye aapko **ek naya shabd** lena padta hai, aur us naye shabd ko samjhane ke liye **ek aur**. Ye silsila kabhi khatam nahi hota.

Is kahani mein is topic ka sabse bada raaz chhupa hai: **kuch shabdon ko bina definition ke hi maanna padta hai.** Chaliye dekhte hain kaise.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Definition** | Kisi cheez ka matlab doosre shabdon se samjhana | "Triangle teen sides wali shape hai" |
| **Undefined term** | Aisa shabd jiski pakki definition nahi di jaati, bas samajh se maan lete hain | Point, line, plane |
| **Line segment** | Line ka wo tukda jiske **do end points** hon | Pencil, scale ka kinara |
| **Ray** | Line ka wo hissa jo **ek point se shuru** ho kar ek taraf **hamesha** chalta rahe | Torch ki roshni, suraj ki kiran |
| **Line** | Dono taraf **bina ruke** chalne wali seedhi rekha | Railway track ko dono taraf hamesha badhte socho |
| **Collinear points** | Aise points jo **ek hi line** par hon | Seedhi line mein khade bachche |
| **Concurrent lines** | Teen ya zyada lines jo **ek hi point** se guzarein | Cycle ke pahiye ki teeliyan (spokes) |
| **Incidence axiom** | Points aur lines ke "kaun kis par padta hai" wale pakke niyam | Do points se sirf ek line guzarti hai |

## Part A: Euclid ki definitions

Euclid ne apni kitaab Elements ki shuruaat kuch **definitions** se ki thi. Unme se kuch ye hain:

1. **Point** wo hai jiska koi hissa nahi hota. *(A point is that which has no part.)*
2. **Line** bina chaudai (breadth) ki lambai hai. *(A line is breadthless length.)*
3. Line ke **siron (ends)** par points hote hain.
4. **Seedhi line (straight line)** wo line hai jo apne upar ke points ke saath **barabar (evenly)** padi ho.
5. **Surface** wo hai jisme sirf **lambai aur chaudai** ho (motai nahi).
6. Surface ke **kinare (edges)** lines hote hain.
7. **Plane surface** wo surface hai jo apne upar ki seedhi lines ke saath **barabar** padi ho.

### In definitions mein problem kya hai?

Chintu wali kahani yaad karo. Euclid ki definitions mein bhi aise shabd hain jinki khud definition chahiye:

| Definition | Kaunse shabd khud samjhane padenge? |
|---|---|
| Point: jiska koi **hissa** nahi | "Hissa" (part) kya hai? |
| Line: **chaudai** ke bina **lambai** | "Chaudai" (breadth) aur "lambai" (length) kya hain? |
| Seedhi line: points ke saath **barabar** padi ho | "Barabar padna" (evenly) kya hai? |
| Surface: sirf **lambai aur chaudai** | Phir wahi: lambai, chaudai kya hain? |

Agar hum "hissa" ki definition dein, to usme koi naya shabd aayega, phir uski definition chahiye... Ye **kabhi na khatam hone wali chain** ban jati hai.

### Hal kya nikala?

Mathematicians ne faisla kiya: **kuch shabdon ko bina definition ke maan lo.** Inhe **undefined terms** kehte hain. Geometry mein teen sabse mukhya undefined terms hain:

> **Point, Line aur Plane**

Hum inki "definition" nahi dete, par inka matlab sab samajhte hain. Jaise aap "neela rang" ki definition nahi de sakte, par neela rang dekhte hi pehchaan lete ho.

## Part B: Teen undefined terms ko mehsoos karo

Definition nahi de sakte, par **tasveer** to bana sakte hain.

### Point

- Bahut nukeeli pencil se copy par lagaya hua **chhota sa dot**.
- Raat ke aasmaan mein door ka **tara**.
- Map par kisi shehar ka **nishaan**.

Dhyan do: asli geometry wale point ka **koi size nahi** hota. Pencil ka dot bhi thoda sa jagah leta hai, par hum use bas samajhne ke liye banate hain. Point ko capital letter se naam dete hain: A, B, P.

### Line

- **Kheencha hua (tight) dhaaga**, jise dono taraf hamesha badhta hua socho.
- Ek seedhi **railway line**, jo dono taraf kabhi khatam nahi hoti (sirf sochne ke liye).

Geometry wali line ki **chaudai nahi** hoti, aur ye **dono taraf bina ruke** chalti rehti hai. Isliye hum kabhi poori line kaagaz par nahi bana sakte, bas uska ek hissa banakar dono taraf **teer** laga dete hain.

### Plane

- **Table ka sapaat (flat) upar wala hissa**, jise chaaron taraf hamesha phaila hua socho.
- **Shant taalab ka paani**.
- Aapki copy ka **panna**.

Plane ki **motai nahi** hoti, aur ye har taraf **bina ruke** phaila hota hai.

## Part C: Line, Line Segment aur Ray

Ye teeno milte julte lagte hain, par inme ek bada farak hai: **inke kitne sire (ends) hain.**

### Line segment

Line ka wo tukda jiske **dono taraf end points** hon.

```
   A                    B
   •--------------------•
```

- Naam: **line segment AB** (ya BA, dono same).
- Likhne ka tarika: AB ke **upar ek seedhi pattee** (—).
- Iski **lambai naap sakte hain**, jaise AB = 5 cm.
- Example: pencil, scale ka kinara, darwaze ki ek side.

### Ray

Ek point se **shuru** ho kar ek taraf **hamesha** chalti rehti hai.

```
   A                    B
   •--------------------•------------------->
```

- Naam: **ray AB**. Pehla letter (A) wo point hai jahan se ray **shuru** hoti hai.
- Likhne ka tarika: AB ke **upar ek taraf teer** (→).
- Iski **lambai nahi naap sakte**, kyunki ek taraf ye khatam hi nahi hoti.
- Example: torch ki roshni, suraj ki kiran.

**Dhyan rakho:** Ray AB aur ray BA **alag** hain.

```
 Ray AB:     A •----------• B ---------->     (A se shuru, B ki taraf)

 Ray BA:  <---------- A •----------• B         (B se shuru, A ki taraf)
```

### Line

**Dono taraf** hamesha chalti rehti hai. Koi end point nahi.

```
  <-------------•--------------------•------------->
                A                    B
```

- Naam: **line AB** (ya BA, dono same). Kabhi kabhi chhote letter se bhi: line l.
- Likhne ka tarika: AB ke **upar dono taraf teer** (↔).
- Iski **lambai nahi naap sakte**.

### Teeno ek nazar mein

| | End points | Lambai naap sakte hain? | Naam ka order matter karta hai? | Likhne ka nishaan |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Line segment AB** | 2 (A aur B) | **Haan** | Nahi (AB = BA) | Upar seedhi pattee (—) |
| **Ray AB** | 1 (sirf A) | Nahi | **Haan** (A shuruaat hai) | Upar ek taraf teer (→) |
| **Line AB** | 0 | Nahi | Nahi | Upar dono taraf teer (↔) |

### Line segment ki lambai

Line segment AB ki lambai ko bas **AB** likhte hain (bina kisi nishaan ke), jaise **AB = 6 cm**.

Agar teen points P, Q, R ek line par hon aur Q beech mein ho:

```
   P          Q                R
   •----------•----------------•
     3 cm           4 cm
```

To **PR = PQ + QR = 3 + 4 = 7 cm**. Poora hissa, dono tukdon ke jod ke barabar hota hai. (Aur dekho, PR = 7 cm apne hisse PQ = 3 cm se bada hai. Topic 1 ka Axiom 5, "poora apne hisse se bada hota hai", yahan number ke saath dikh raha hai.)

Jaanch: 3 + 4 = 7 ✅

## Part D: Collinear aur Non-collinear Points

### Collinear points

Jab teen ya zyada points **ek hi seedhi line** par hon, to unhe **collinear points** kehte hain.

```
   A          B          C          D
   •----------•----------•----------•
```

A, B, C, D collinear hain.

Roz ki zindagi mein:
- Assembly mein **ek seedhi line mein khade bachche**.
- Ek seedhi sadak par lage **bijli ke khambe**.

### Non-collinear points

Jab points **ek line par na hon**, to wo **non-collinear** hain.

```
          Q
          •

   P                R
   •                •
```

P, Q, R non-collinear hain. Inhe jodo to ek **triangle** ban jata hai.

### Ek zaroori baat

**Koi bhi do points hamesha collinear hote hain**, kyunki do points se hamesha ek line guzar sakti hai (ye Part G ke incidence axiom se aata hai). Isliye collinear ya non-collinear ki baat tab dilchasp hoti hai jab **teen ya zyada** points hon.

## Part E: Intersecting, Parallel aur Concurrent Lines

### Intersecting lines

Do lines jo **ek point par milti (kaat-ti)** hain.

```
         \       /
          \     /
           \   /
            \ /
             • O
            / \
           /   \
          /     \
```

O wo point hai jahan dono lines milti hain. Ise **point of intersection** kehte hain.

Roz ki zindagi mein: **kainchi (scissors)** ke do blade, sadak ka **chauraha**, angrezi ka akshar **X**.

### Parallel lines

Do lines jo **ek hi plane** mein hon aur **kabhi na milein**, chahe kitna bhi badhao.

```
  <------------------------------------->   l

  <------------------------------------->   m
```

Line l aur line m parallel hain. Inhe **l ∥ m** likhte hain.

Roz ki zindagi mein: **railway ki do patriyan**, copy ki **ruled lines**, seedhi (ladder) ke do lambe dande.

### Concurrent lines

**Teen ya zyada** lines jo **ek hi point** se guzarein.

```
          \   |   /
           \  |  /
            \ | /
   ----------\|/----------
              • O
             /|\
            / | \
           /  |  \
```

Ye chaar lines O se guzar rahi hain, to ye **concurrent** hain. O ko **point of concurrence** kehte hain.

Roz ki zindagi mein: **cycle ke pahiye ki teeliyan (spokes)** jo beech mein milti hain, **chhatri ki teeliyan**, kate hue **pizza ki lines**.

**Dhyan rakho:** Concurrent kehne ke liye kam se kam **teen** lines chahiye. Do lines milein to bas "intersecting" kehte hain.

---

## Part F: Coplanar points aur lines (chhota sa parichay)

Jo points ya lines **ek hi plane** (ek hi sapaat sataha) par hon, unhe **coplanar** kehte hain.

- Aapki copy ke **ek panne** par bane saare points coplanar hain.
- Classroom ke **farsh** par rakhe saare points coplanar hain.

Ab ek kamre ka **kona** socho. Farsh ke do kone aur chhat ka ek kona: kya ye teeno ek hi farsh par hain? Nahi. Par koi bhi **teen points** hamesha **kisi na kisi** plane par aa hi jaate hain (jaise ek tirchha kaagaz jo teeno ko chhoo le). Isliye **non-coplanar** ki baat **chaar ya zyada** points par hoti hai. Jaise farsh ke teen kone aur chhat ka ek kona: ye chaar points ek plane par nahi hain.

Is class mein bas itna kaafi hai. Aage ki classes mein (3D geometry mein) ye vistaar se aayega.

## Part G: Incidence Axioms on Lines

"Incidence" ka seedha matlab hai: **kaun kis par padta (lie karta) hai.** Ye wo pakke niyam hain jo batate hain ki points aur lines ka aapas mein kya rishta hai. Inhe hum **axioms** ki tarah sach maan lete hain (Topic 1 mein dekha tha ki axiom wo baat hai jise bina proof ke maan lete hain).

(Dhyan do: Topic 1 ke Euclid wale Axiom 1-7 se alag pehchaan ke liye, inhe hum **Incidence Axiom 1-4** kahenge.)

### Incidence Axiom 1: Ek line par anginat (infinitely many) points hote hain.

Ek line par aap chahe kitne bhi points lagao, hamesha **do points ke beech** ek aur point laga sakte ho. Aur line dono taraf chalti hi rehti hai.

```
  <----•----•----•----•----•----•----•----•---->
       A    B    C    D    E    F    G    H   ...
```

Socho ek line ko **zoom** karte jao. A aur B ke beech ek point, uske aur A ke beech ek aur, aur aage... ye kabhi khatam nahi hota.

### Incidence Axiom 2: Ek diye gaye point se anginat lines guzar sakti hain.

Ek point P lo. Usse kitni lines guzar sakti hain? Ek? Do? **Anginat!**

```
            \    |    /
         \   \   |   /   /
          \   \  |  /   /
     ------------•P-----------
          /   /  |  \   \
         /   /   |   \   \
            /    |    \
```

Socho P par ek **pin** gadi hai aur usme ek dhaaga ghumate jao. Har naye angle par ek nayi line milti hai. Isliye ek point se guzarne wali lines ginti mein nahi aatin.

### Incidence Axiom 3: Do alag (distinct) points se ek, aur sirf ek, line guzarti hai.

Do points A aur B lo. Inhe jodne wali **seedhi** line kitni hain? **Sirf ek.**

```
   <---------•--------------------•--------->
             A                    B
```

Ise khud karke dekho: copy par do dots lagao aur scale se seedhi line kheencho. Aap koi doosri seedhi line nahi bana sakte jo dono dots se guzre. Tedhi-medhi line to bahut ban jayengi, par **seedhi** sirf ek.

Yahi wajah hai ki do kile thokkar uspe dhaaga kheencho to dhaaga hamesha ek hi raste par tikta hai.

(Topic 1 mein Euclid ka pehla postulate bhi kuch aisa hi kehta tha: kisi bhi point se kisi doosre point tak ek seedhi line kheenchi ja sakti hai. Ye axiom usme ek baat aur jodta hai: wo line **sirf ek** hi hoti hai.)

### Incidence Axiom 4 (fact): Do alag lines zyada se zyada ek point par milti hain.

Do alag lines ke saath sirf do hi cheezein ho sakti hain:

| Situation | Kitne common points? |
|---|---|
| Lines **milti** hain (intersecting) | **Theek 1** point |
| Lines **kabhi nahi milti** (parallel) | **0** points |

Do alag seedhi lines **do points par nahi mil sakti**. Kyun? Agar do points par milti, to un do points se **do alag lines** guzar rahi hoti, jo Incidence Axiom 3 ke khilaaf hai.

Ye baat asal mein ek **theorem** hai. Iska poora, step by step proof hum **Topic 4 (Theorems)** mein dekhenge. Abhi ke liye ise ek pakki baat ki tarah yaad rakho.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Line aur line segment ko ek samajhna**
Scale se jo kheenchte ho wo asal mein **line segment** hai (do sire hain). Line ke dono taraf teer hote hain, kyunki wo khatam nahi hoti.

**Galti 2: Ray AB aur ray BA ko same samajhna**
Ray AB, **A se shuru** hoti hai aur B ki taraf jaati hai. Ray BA, **B se shuru** hoti hai aur A ki taraf jaati hai. Dono alag hain. (Segment AB aur BA same hote hain.)

**Galti 3: Line ya ray ki lambai likh dena**
"Line AB = 5 cm" galat hai. Lambai sirf **line segment** ki hoti hai.

**Galti 4: Point ka size samajhna**
Copy par dot thoda bada dikhta hai, par geometry wale point ka **koi size nahi** hota. Dot sirf samajhne ke liye hai.

**Galti 5: Do points ko "non-collinear" kehna**
Koi bhi do points **hamesha collinear** hote hain. Collinear/non-collinear ki baat teen ya zyada points par hoti hai.

**Galti 6: Do intersecting lines ko concurrent kehna**
Concurrent ke liye kam se kam **teen** lines chahiye jo ek point se guzarein.

**Galti 7: Parallel ki shart bhool jana**
Parallel lines **ek hi plane** mein honi chahiye aur **kabhi na milein**. Ek line farsh par aur ek chhat par, alag disha mein, bhi kabhi nahi milti, par wo parallel nahi kehlati.

**Galti 8: Do points se do seedhi lines maan lena**
Do alag points se **sirf ek** seedhi line guzarti hai (Incidence Axiom 3).

## Quick Recap

- Euclid ki definitions (point, line, surface...) mein aise shabd hain (**hissa, chaudai, lambai, barabar**) jinki khud definition chahiye. Ye chain kabhi khatam nahi hoti.
- Isliye **point, line aur plane** ko **undefined terms** maana jata hai.
- **Line segment:** 2 end points, lambai naap sakte hain, AB = BA.
- **Ray:** 1 end point (shuruaat), ek taraf hamesha. Ray AB ≠ ray BA.
- **Line:** 0 end points, dono taraf hamesha.
- **Collinear:** ek hi line par. Koi bhi do points hamesha collinear hote hain.
- **Intersecting:** ek point par milti hain. **Parallel:** ek plane mein, kabhi nahi milti (l ∥ m). **Concurrent:** 3 ya zyada lines ek point se.
- **Coplanar:** ek hi plane par.
- **Incidence axioms:**
  1. Ek line par anginat points.
  2. Ek point se anginat lines.
  3. Do alag points se **sirf ek** line.
  4. Do alag lines **zyada se zyada ek** point par milti hain (proof Topic 4 mein).
