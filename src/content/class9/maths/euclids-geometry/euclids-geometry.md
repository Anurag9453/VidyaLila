# Topic 1: History, Axioms aur Postulates

## Ek khet ki kahani, 5000 saal purani

Socho aap aaj se lagbhag 5000 saal pehle Misr (Egypt) mein ek kisaan ho. Aapka khet **Nile nadi** ke kinare hai.

Har saal baarish ke mausam mein Nile mein baadh aati hai. Paani utarta hai to khet ki saari mitti ek jaisi ho jati hai. Aapke khet ki seema (boundary) ke nishaan bhi beh jaate hain. Ab aapka khet kahan khatam hota hai aur padosi ka kahan shuru, kisi ko pata nahi.

Jhagda na ho, isliye raja ke log aate hain. Unke haath mein **gaanth lagi rassi** hoti hai. Wo rassi khinch kar khet ko dobara naapte hain, aur har kisaan ko uska hissa wapas dete hain.

Isi zameen naapne ke kaam se ek naya vishay paida hua: **Geometry**.

- **Geo** = dharti (earth)
- **Metron** = naapna (measure)

Yaani **geometry = dharti ko naapna.**

Par aaj ki geometry sirf khet naapna nahi hai. Is chapter mein hum dekhenge ki kaise ek Greek teacher, **Euclid**, ne geometry ko ek aisi shakal di jo 2000 saal se zyada se school mein padhai ja rahi hai. Uska raaz tha kuch **bahut simple baatein** jinhe sab maan lete hain, aur unhi se baaki sab kuch **sabit (prove)** karna.

## Part A: Geometry ka itihaas (History)

Geometry kisi ek jagah ya ek insaan ne nahi banayi. Duniya ke alag alag hisson mein log apni zaroorat ke hisaab se ise use kar rahe the.

### Misr (Egypt)

Upar ki kahani yahin ki hai. Nile ki baadh ke baad khet dobara naapne padte the. Is kaam ke liye rassi wale naapne waale log hote the, jinhe "**rope-stretchers**" (rassi kheenchne wale) kaha jata hai.

Misr ke log sirf khet nahi naapte the. Wo **area, volume** nikalna jaante the, aur **pyramids** jaise bade nirmaan bhi banate the. Par unka gyaan zyada tar **niyamon ki list** jaisa tha: "aisa karo to ye milega". Ye **kyun** kaam karta hai, ye wo prove nahi karte the.

### Sindhu Ghati Sabhyata (Indus Valley Civilisation)

Lagbhag **3000 BCE** mein Harappa aur Mohenjo-daro jaise shehar bahut sooch samajh kar banaye gaye the:
- Sadkein ek doosre ko **seedhe kone (right angle)** par kaatti thin.
- Naaliyan (drainage) dhaki hui aur ek tarteeb mein thin.
- Eenton (bricks) ki **lambai : chaudai : motai** ka anupaat (ratio) **4 : 2 : 1** hota tha. Isse eenten aapas mein achchhe se jud jaati thin.

Ye sab bina geometry ke samajh ke mumkin nahi tha.

### Sulbasutras (Prachin Bharat)

Lagbhag **800 BCE se 500 BCE** ke beech Bharat mein **Sulbasutras** likhe gaye. "Sulba" ka matlab hai **rassi**. Ye vedic yagyon ki **vedi (altar)** banane ki niyam-pustakein thin.

- Alag alag aakar ki vediyan banani hoti thin, jaise square aur circle.
- Kai baar ek square vedi aur ek circular vedi ka **area barabar** rakhna hota tha.
- Iske liye naap aur aakar ka gehra gyaan chahiye tha.

### Greece (Yunaan)

Greek logon ne geometry ko ek naya mod diya. Unhone poochha: "**Ye sach kyun hai?**"

- **Thales** (640–546 BCE): Inhe pehle insaan maana jata hai jinhone geometry ki ek baat ka **proof** diya. Unhone dikhaya ki ek circle ka diameter use do barabar hisson mein baant deta hai.
- **Pythagoras** (lagbhag 572 BCE): Thales ke shishya maane jaate hain. Inke naam par ek mashhoor theorem hai, jo aap aage padhenge.

### Euclid aur "Elements"

Ab aate hain is chapter ke hero par: **Euclid**.

- Euclid lagbhag **300 BCE** mein Misr ke shehar **Alexandria** mein ek teacher the.
- Unhone apne samay tak ka saara geometry ka gyaan ikattha kiya aur ek kitaab mein tarteeb se likha. Iska naam hai **"Elements"**.
- Elements mein **13 books (hisse)** hain.
- Euclid ko aksar "**Father of Geometry**" (geometry ke pita) kaha jata hai.

### Euclid ka asli kamaal kya tha?

Euclid se pehle bhi log bahut geometry jaante the. Par wo jaankaari bikhri hui thi. Euclid ne ek naya tarika apnaya:

1. Kuch **bahut simple baatein** chuno, jo itni saaf hon ki koi bhi maan le. (Ye axioms aur postulates hain.)
2. Unhe **bina proof** ke sach maano.
3. Baaki **har ek baat** ko inhi se, step by step, **prove** karo.

Ise ek **imaarat (building)** ki tarah socho:

```
          [ Bade theorems ]          <- upar ki manzilein
         [ Chhote theorems ]
        [ Pehle proofs     ]
   ==============================
   [ Axioms + Postulates ]           <- neenv (foundation)
```

Neenv mazboot ho to poori imaarat mazboot rehti hai. Euclid ki geometry ki neenv yahi axioms aur postulates hain. Aaj bhi maths isi tarike se banti hai.

## Part B: Axioms aur Postulates kya hote hain?

### Khel ke niyam wali baat

Socho aap doston ke saath gali cricket khel rahe ho. Khel shuru hone se pehle sab kuch niyam tay kar lete hain:
- "Diwar par seedha lage to out."
- "Nali mein gend gayi to chhakka nahi, sirf do run."

In niyamon ka koi "proof" nahi hota. Sab bas **maan** lete hain, taaki khel chal sake. Aur phir saara khel inhi niyamon par chalta hai.

Maths mein bhi aisa hi hai. Kuch baatein hum **bina proof ke maan** lete hain. Unhe kehte hain **axioms** aur **postulates**.

### Dono mein farak

Euclid ne in maani hui baaton ko do group mein baanta:

| | **Axiom** (common notion) | **Postulate** |
|---|---|---|
| Kya hai? | Bina proof maani hui baat | Bina proof maani hui baat |
| Kahan kaam aati hai? | **Har jagah**: numbers, lambai, wazan, paisa, sab mein | **Sirf geometry** mein |
| Example | "Barabar cheezon mein barabar jodo to jod barabar rehta hai" | "Kisi bhi centre aur radius se circle banaya ja sakta hai" |

Ek line mein:
> **Axiom = sab jagah ka niyam. Postulate = sirf geometry ka niyam.**

Aaj kal ke maths mein dono ko aksar ek hi naam (**axiom**) se bula dete hain. Par Euclid ka farak samajhna is chapter ke liye zaroori hai.

### Ek zaroori baat

Axioms aur postulates ko **prove nahi kiya jata.** Wo shuruaat hain. Unse jo nayi baatein prove ki jaati hain, unhe **theorems** kehte hain. (Theorems ke baare mein Topic 4 mein padhenge.)

## Part C: Euclid ke 7 Axioms

Yahan hum Euclid ke 7 axioms dekhenge. Har ek ko ek ek karke, roz ki zindagi aur numbers ke saath samjhte hain.

### Axiom 1: Jo cheezein ek hi cheez ke barabar hon, wo aapas mein bhi barabar hoti hain.

**Roz ki zindagi:** Ravi ki height Sita ke barabar hai. Sita ki height Aman ke barabar hai. To Ravi aur Aman ki height bhi barabar hai. Inhe saath khada karke naapne ki zaroorat bhi nahi.

**Numbers mein:** Agar **a = c** aur **b = c**, to **a = b**.

**Example:** a + b = 10 hai, aur a + b = c bhi hai. To c = 10. (Dono 10 aur c, "a + b" ke barabar hain.)

### Axiom 2: Agar barabar cheezon mein barabar cheezein jodi jayein, to jod (wholes) barabar hote hain.

**Roz ki zindagi:** Aapke aur aapke bhai ke gullak mein ₹50 - ₹50 hain. Papa dono ko ₹20 - ₹20 aur dete hain. Ab dono ke paas ₹70 - ₹70 hain. Barabar hi rahe.

**Numbers mein:** Agar **x = y**, to **x + 5 = y + 5**.

### Axiom 3: Agar barabar cheezon mein se barabar cheezein ghatayi jayein, to bachi hui cheezein (remainders) barabar hoti hain.

**Roz ki zindagi:** Do doston ke paas 12 - 12 toffee thin. Dono ne 4 - 4 kha li. Ab dono ke paas 8 - 8 bachi. Barabar.

**Numbers mein:** Agar **p + q = r + q**, to dono taraf se q ghatao: **p = r**.

### Axiom 4: Jo cheezein ek doosre ke saath poori tarah mel kha jayein (coincide karein), wo aapas mein barabar hoti hain.

"Coincide" ka matlab: ek ko doosre ke upar rakho, to **bilkul theek fit** ho jaye, koi hissa bahar na nikle.

**Roz ki zindagi:** Do ek jaise playing cards ek ke upar ek rakho. Wo bilkul dhak jaate hain. To unka size barabar hai.

Ye axiom aage chal kar triangles ko compare karne (congruence) mein bahut kaam aata hai.

**Dhyan rakho:** Iska ulta hamesha sach nahi hai. Barabar area wali do shapes zaroori nahi ki ek doosre par fit ho jayein. Ek rectangle 2 cm × 8 cm ka hai (area 16 cm²) aur ek square 4 cm × 4 cm ka (area 16 cm²). Area barabar hai, par ek ke upar doosra rakho to fit nahi hoga.

### Axiom 5: Poora (whole) apne hisse (part) se bada hota hai.

**Roz ki zindagi:** Ek poora pizza uske ek slice se bada hota hai. Agar class mein ladke aur ladkiyan dono hain, to poori class ke bachchon ki ginti sirf ladkon ki ginti se zyada hogi.

**Numbers mein:** Agar **a = b + c**, aur c koi positive cheez hai (jaise lambai ya wazan), to **a > b**.

**Example:** 10 = 7 + 3. Yahan 10 poora hai, 7 uska ek hissa. To 10 > 7.

### Axiom 6: Jo cheezein ek hi cheez ki dugni (double) hon, wo aapas mein barabar hoti hain.

**Roz ki zindagi:** Rohan ke paas Meena se dugne kanche hain. Sohan ke paas bhi Meena se dugne kanche hain. To Rohan aur Sohan ke kanche barabar hain.

**Numbers mein:** Agar **x = 2y** aur **z = 2y**, to **x = z**.

### Axiom 7: Jo cheezein ek hi cheez ki aadhi (halves) hon, wo aapas mein barabar hoti hain.

**Roz ki zindagi:** Ek roti ke do barabar tukde karo. Dono tukde us ek roti ke aadhe hain, to dono tukde barabar hain.

**Numbers mein:** Agar **a = b/2** aur **c = b/2**, to **a = c**.

### Saare axioms ek nazar mein

| # | Axiom (seedhi bhasha mein) | Chhota example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ek hi cheez ke barabar → aapas mein barabar | a = c, b = c → a = b |
| 2 | Barabar + barabar → jod barabar | x = y → x + 5 = y + 5 |
| 3 | Barabar − barabar → bacha hua barabar | p + q = r + q → p = r |
| 4 | Poori tarah fit (coincide) → barabar | Do ek jaise cards |
| 5 | Poora > uska hissa | Pizza > ek slice |
| 6 | Ek hi cheez ke double → barabar | x = 2y, z = 2y → x = z |
| 7 | Ek hi cheez ke aadhe → barabar | a = b/2, c = b/2 → a = c |

## Part D: Euclid ke 5 Postulates

Ab geometry ke khaas niyam. Inme kuch shabd aayenge, jaise point, line aur circle. Abhi ke liye inka roz wala matlab samjho (point = ek bindu, line = seedhi rekha). Inki sahi definitions hum **Topic 2** mein padhenge.

### Postulate 1: Kisi bhi ek point se kisi bhi doosre point tak ek seedhi line kheenchi ja sakti hai.

```
   A •─────────────────────• B
```

**Roz ki zindagi:** Copy par do bindu lagao, A aur B. Scale rakh kar dono ko jod do. Hamesha ho jayega.

(Ek mazedaar sawaal: A aur B se **kitni** lines guzar sakti hain? Ek ya zyada? Iska jawab **Topic 3** mein milega.)

### Postulate 2: Ek seemit (terminated) line ko dono taraf jitna chaho badhaya ja sakta hai.

"Terminated line" yaani jiske dono sire (ends) hon. Euclid kehte hain ki ise aage aur peeche, kitna bhi lamba kar sakte ho.

```
   Pehle:         A •──────• B

   Badhane par:  ←───• A ──────• B ───→
```

**Roz ki zindagi:** Ek seedhi sadak ka ek tukda dekh rahe ho. Sochke dekho ki wo sadak dono taraf aage tak chalti ja sakti hai. Geometry mein koi "deewar" nahi hai jo use rok de.

### Postulate 3: Kisi bhi centre aur kisi bhi radius se ek circle banaya ja sakta hai.

```
           . - ~ ~ - .
        .'             '.
       :        O •───── :     O = centre
        '.     radius  .'
           ' - ~ ~ - '
```

**Roz ki zindagi:** Compass (parkar) ki nok kahin bhi rakho, aur use kitna bhi khol lo. Ek circle ban jayega. Chhota ho ya bada, koi rok nahi.

### Postulate 4: Saare right angles aapas mein barabar hote hain.

Right angle yaani **90°** ka angle, jaise kitaab ka kona, darwaze ka kona, ya **L** akshar.

**Roz ki zindagi:** Aapki copy ka kona, ek bade blackboard ka kona, aur ek building ki deewar aur zameen ke beech ka kona. Ye sab alag size ki cheezon par hain, par **kona (angle) ek hi jaisa** hai.

Ye baat sunne mein bahut seedhi lagti hai, par Euclid ne ise saaf likha, taaki aage ke proofs mein koi shak na rahe.

### Postulate 5: Parallel Postulate (paanchva aur sabse mashhoor)

Poora statement ye hai:

> Agar ek seedhi line, do seedhi lines par is tarah gire ki ek hi taraf ke **andar wale do angles ka jod (sum) do right angles (180°) se kam** ho, to wo do lines, agar dono taraf badhai jayein, to **usi taraf mil jaati hain** jis taraf ye jod 180° se kam hai.

Ye lamba sentence darata hai. Ise tod kar samjho.

**Step 1:** Do lines hain, **l** aur **m**.
**Step 2:** Ek teesri line **n** in dono ko kaatti hai.
**Step 3:** n ke **right side**, l aur m ke **beech** do angles bante hain: **∠1** aur **∠2**.
**Step 4:** Agar **∠1 + ∠2 < 180°**, to l aur m right side mein aage jaakar **mil jayengi**.

```
                 n
                 |
  l  ------------+------------------------.__
                 |  ∠1                        ``--.__
                 |                                   • P   (yahan mil gayi)
                 |  ∠2                        __..--``
  m  ------------+------------------------.--``
                 |
```

**Roz ki zindagi:** Do railway ki patriyan socho. Agar wo bilkul saath saath (parallel) hon, to kabhi nahi milti. Par agar thodi si bhi ek doosre ki taraf jhuki hon, to aage jaakar kahin na kahin mil hi jayengi. Kis taraf? Jis taraf wo jhuki hain. Aur jis taraf wo jhuki hain, usi taraf andar wale angles ka jod 180° se kam hota hai.

**Ek chhota number example:**
- Agar ∠1 = 80° aur ∠2 = 85°, to jod = 165°. Ye 180° se kam hai. To l aur m **right side** mein mil jayengi.
- Agar ∠1 = 90° aur ∠2 = 90°, to jod = 180°, jo 180° se kam nahi hai. Ye postulate yahan "mil jayengi" nahi kehta. Aisi lines parallel hoti hain aur kabhi nahi milti.

### Postulate 5 ka aasan roop: Playfair's Axiom

Paanchva postulate itna lamba hai ki baad mein logon ne ise ek aasan, barabar wale (equivalent) roop mein likha. Ise **Playfair's Axiom** kehte hain:

> Kisi bhi line **l** aur us par **na pade** kisi bhi point **P** ke liye, P se guzarne wali **sirf ek hi line** hoti hai jo l ke **parallel** ho.

```
             P
  m  --------•--------------------   (P se l ke parallel: sirf yahi ek line)

  l  -----------------------------
```

### Paanchve postulate ki khaas kahani (Class 9 se aage ki baat)

Pehle chaar postulates bahut chhote aur seedhe hain. Paanchva lamba aur thoda ulajha hua hai. Isliye 2000 saal tak bade bade mathematicians ne koshish ki ki ise baaki chaar se **prove** kar dein. Koi nahi kar paya.

Aakhir mein samajh aaya ki ise prove kiya hi nahi ja sakta. Aur jab logon ne paanchve postulate ko **badal kar** dekha, to bilkul nayi tarah ki geometry ban gayi, jise **non-Euclidean geometry** kehte hain. Jaise ek globe (gend) ki sataha par geometry alag tarah se kaam karti hai. Ye Class 9 ke syllabus se aage ki baat hai, bas itna jaan lo ki Euclid ka ye ek postulate itihaas mein bahut mashhoor hua.

### Saare postulates ek nazar mein

| # | Postulate (seedhi bhasha mein) |
|---|---|
| 1 | Do points ko ek seedhi line se joda ja sakta hai |
| 2 | Ek line ke tukde ko dono taraf jitna chaho badhaya ja sakta hai |
| 3 | Kisi bhi centre aur radius se circle banaya ja sakta hai |
| 4 | Saare right angles barabar hote hain |
| 5 | Andar ke angles ka jod 180° se kam ho, to lines usi taraf mil jaati hain |

## Part E: Axioms ko kaam mein lagana

Ab dekhte hain ki in axioms se chhote chhote sawaal kaise hal hote hain. Har step ke saath batayenge ki kaunsa axiom laga.

### Example 1

**a + b = 10** aur **a + b = c**. Dikhao ki **c = 10**. Kaunsa axiom?

- 10 aur c, dono "a + b" ke barabar hain.
- Ek hi cheez ke barabar cheezein aapas mein barabar hoti hain.
- To **c = 10**. Ye **Axiom 1** hai.

### Example 2

**x = y** hai. Dikhao ki **x + 7 = y + 7**.

- Barabar cheezon (x aur y) mein barabar cheez (7) jodi.
- To jod barabar: **x + 7 = y + 7**. Ye **Axiom 2** hai.

### Example 3 (seedhi line par points)

Ek seedhi line par chaar points **A, B, C, D** isi kram mein hain, aur **AC = BD** hai. Dikhao ki **AB = CD**.

```
   A •-------• B -------• C -------• D
```

- AC = AB + BC (A se C tak jaane mein pehle AB, phir BC)
- BD = BC + CD (B se D tak jaane mein pehle BC, phir CD)
- AC = BD diya hai, to **AB + BC = BC + CD**.
- Dono taraf se **BC** ghatao (barabar mein se barabar ghataya).
- To **AB = CD**. Ye **Axiom 3** hai.

**Jaanch (numbers se):** Maan lo AB = 2, BC = 3, CD = 2. To AC = 5 aur BD = 5, barabar hain. Aur AB = CD = 2 ✅

(Yahan bas axiom ko kaam karte dekha. Isi sawaal ka poora proof, Given / To prove / Statement-Reason ke saath, Topic 4 mein likhna seekhenge.)

### Example 4

Ek rassi ki lambai **12 m** hai. Usme se **5 m** kaat ke alag kiya. Kya bacha hua hissa poori rassi se chhota hai?

- Poori rassi (12 m) = bacha hissa (7 m) + kata hissa (5 m).
- Poora apne hisse se bada hota hai.
- To 12 > 7, yaani bacha hissa chhota hai. Ye **Axiom 5** hai.

### Example 5

Rohit ki umar Priya ki umar ka **dugna** hai. Aman ki umar bhi Priya ki umar ka **dugna** hai. Rohit aur Aman ki umar ke baare mein kya keh sakte ho?

- Dono ek hi cheez (Priya ki umar) ke double hain.
- To **Rohit aur Aman ki umar barabar** hai. Ye **Axiom 6** hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Axioms ko prove karne ki koshish karna**
Axioms aur postulates **maani hui** baatein hain. Unka proof nahi hota. Unse doosri baatein (theorems) prove hoti hain.

**Galti 2: Axiom aur postulate ko ek hi samajhna**
Euclid ke hisaab se axiom **har jagah** ka niyam hai (numbers, wazan, paisa), aur postulate **sirf geometry** ka. "Saare right angles barabar hain" ek postulate hai, axiom nahi.

**Galti 3: Axiom 4 ko ulta maan lena**
Coincide karne wali cheezein barabar hoti hain. Par barabar area wali har shape coincide nahi karti. 2 × 8 ka rectangle aur 4 × 4 ka square, dono ka area 16 hai, par ek doosre par fit nahi hote.

**Galti 4: Paanchve postulate mein galat side chun lena**
Lines us taraf milti hain jis taraf andar ke angles ka jod **180° se kam** hai, us taraf nahi jahan jod zyada hai.

**Galti 5: Jod 180° ho tab bhi "mil jayengi" bol dena**
Paanchva postulate sirf tab kehta hai ki lines milengi jab jod **180° se kam** ho. Jod theek 180° ho to lines parallel hoti hain aur nahi milti.

**Galti 6: Postulate 1 ka matlab "sirf ek line" samajhna**
Postulate 1 sirf itna kehta hai ki do points ko line se **joda ja sakta hai**. "Kitni lines" wala sawaal alag hai, aur uska jawab Topic 3 mein aayega.

**Galti 7: Euclid ko geometry ka "khojkarta" samajhna**
Geometry Euclid se pehle bhi Misr, Sindhu ghati, Bharat aur Greece mein thi. Euclid ka kamaal ye tha ki unhone use **ek tarteeb mein** likha aur sab kuch kuch maani hui baaton se **prove** kiya.

## Quick Recap

- **Geometry = geo (dharti) + metron (naapna).** Shuruaat zameen naapne se hui.
- Geometry ki jhalak **Misr** (Nile ki baadh ke baad khet naapna), **Sindhu ghati** (eenton ka anupaat 4 : 2 : 1, seedhi sadkein), **Sulbasutras** (yagya ki vediyan) aur **Greece** (Thales, Pythagoras) mein milti hai.
- **Euclid** (lagbhag 300 BCE, Alexandria) ne **Elements** likhi, jisme **13 books** hain. Unhe "Father of Geometry" kehte hain.
- Euclid ka tarika: kuch baatein **bina proof** maano, baaki sab unse **prove** karo.
- **Axiom:** har jagah kaam aane wali maani hui baat. **Postulate:** sirf geometry ki maani hui baat.
- **7 axioms:** ek hi cheez ke barabar → barabar; barabar + barabar; barabar − barabar; coincide → barabar; poora > hissa; ek hi ke double → barabar; ek hi ke aadhe → barabar.
- **5 postulates:** do points ko jodna; line badhana; circle banana; saare right angles barabar; parallel postulate.
- **Paanchva postulate** ka aasan roop **Playfair's axiom** hai: line ke bahar ke point se sirf **ek** parallel line.

4. A, B, C and D are four points on a straight line, in that order, such that AC = BD. Prove that AB = CD. State the axiom you use.

6. A straight line n cuts two lines l and m. The two interior angles on the same side of n are ∠1 and ∠2. Will l and m meet on that side if:
   (a) ∠1 + ∠2 = 170°?
   (b) ∠1 + ∠2 = 180°?
   Give a reason using Euclid's fifth postulate.

7. State whether each statement is true or false. Give a reason.
   (a) Axioms are proved using theorems.
   (b) Euclid's fourth postulate says that all right angles are equal to one another.
   (c) Two figures with equal areas always coincide with each other.
   (d) Geometry was first used in ancient Egypt to re-measure land after the floods of the river Nile.

8. Write Playfair's axiom in your own words. Which of Euclid's postulates is it equivalent to?

### Answers

1. **Euclid** ko Father of Geometry kehte hain. Unki kitaab ka naam **Elements** hai, aur isme **13 books** hain.

2. **Axiom** har jagah kaam aane wali maani hui baat hai (numbers, wazan, paisa sab mein). Example: "Poora apne hisse se bada hota hai."
   **Postulate** sirf geometry ki maani hui baat hai. Example: "Kisi bhi centre aur radius se circle banaya ja sakta hai."

3. (a) **Axiom 1** (ek hi cheez ke barabar → aapas mein barabar)
   (b) **Axiom 2** (barabar mein barabar jodo)
   (c) **Axiom 3** (barabar mein se barabar ghatao)
   (d) **Axiom 5** (poora apne hisse se bada)
   (e) **Axiom 6** (ek hi cheez ke double barabar)
   (f) **Axiom 7** (ek hi cheez ke aadhe barabar)

4. AC = AB + BC aur BD = BC + CD.
   AC = BD diya hai, to AB + BC = BC + CD.
   Dono taraf se BC ghatao: **AB = CD**.
   **Axiom 3** laga (barabar mein se barabar ghatane par bacha hua barabar). (AC = BD ko AB + BC = BC + CD likhne mein Axiom 1 bhi chhupa hai.)
   Jaanch: AB = 2, BC = 3, CD = 2 lo. AC = 5, BD = 5, aur AB = CD ✅

5. Ravi aur Aman ki height **barabar** hai, kyunki dono Sita ki height ke barabar hain. **Axiom 1**.

6. (a) **Haan, milengi.** 170° < 180°, to paanchve postulate ke hisaab se l aur m usi taraf mil jayengi.
   (b) **Nahi milengi.** Jod 180° hai, jo 180° se kam nahi hai, to postulate "milengi" nahi kehta. Aisi lines parallel hoti hain.

7. (a) **False ❌.** Axioms prove nahi kiye jaate. Ulta, theorems axioms se prove hote hain.
   (b) **True ✅.** Ye Euclid ka chautha postulate hai.
   (c) **False ❌.** Jaise 2 × 8 ka rectangle aur 4 × 4 ka square, dono ka area 16 hai, par coincide nahi karte.
   (d) **True ✅.** Nile ki baadh ke baad khet ki seemayein dobara naapni padti thin.

8. **Playfair's axiom:** Agar ek line l hai aur ek point P hai jo l par nahi hai, to P se guzarne wali **sirf ek hi line** hoti hai jo l ke parallel ho. Ye Euclid ke **paanchve postulate (parallel postulate)** ke barabar (equivalent) hai.
