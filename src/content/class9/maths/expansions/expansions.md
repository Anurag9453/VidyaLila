# Topic 1: Expansion kya hai aur Basic Identities

Polynomials chapter mein humne dekha tha ki **terms** kya hote hain aur **like terms** ko kaise jodte hain (jaise 3x + 5x = 8x). Wahan ek chhota sa kaam bhi kiya tha: ek term ko bracket se multiply karna, jaise 2x(x + 3) = 2x² + 6x.

Ab ek kadam aage chalte hain. Kya hoga jab **do brackets** aapas mein multiply hon? Jaise (x + 3)(x + 5), ya (a + b)². Isi ko **expansion** kehte hain. Aur is topic mein hum chaar aise formulas seekhenge jo maths ke sabse zyada kaam aane wale formulas mein se hain.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Expand karna** | Bracket ko multiply karke hatana, aur ek lambi expression likhna | (x + 2)(x + 3) = x² + 5x + 6 |
| **Expansion** | Bracket kholne ke baad jo lambi expression milti hai | x² + 5x + 6 |
| **Binomial** | Do terms wali expression | x + 3, 2a − b |
| **Identity** | Aisi barabari jo variable ki **har** value ke liye sach ho | (a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b² |
| **Equation** | Aisi barabari jo sirf **kuch khaas** values ke liye sach ho | x + 2 = 5 (sirf x = 3 par) |
| **Distributive law** | Bracket ke bahar wali cheez bracket ke har term se multiply hoti hai | a(b + c) = ab + ac |

## Part A: Expand karna kya hai?

### Ek term × bracket (yaad dilana)

Polynomials chapter mein ye kar chuke hain:

3x(2x + 5) = 3x × 2x + 3x × 5 = **6x² + 15x**

Iske peeche ek simple niyam hai, jise **distributive law** kehte hain: bracket ke bahar wala har andar wale term se ek-ek baar milta hai. Jaise ek dost party mein aaye aur sabse haath milaye.

### Bracket × bracket

Ab do brackets: (x + 3)(x + 5).

Socho pehle bracket ke **dono** terms (x aur 3) doosre bracket ke **dono** terms (x aur 5) se haath milate hain. Kul **2 × 2 = 4** haath milenge:

- x × x = x²
- x × 5 = 5x
- 3 × x = 3x
- 3 × 5 = 15

Sab jodo: x² + 5x + 3x + 15. Like terms (5x + 3x) jodo:

**(x + 3)(x + 5) = x² + 8x + 15**

**Jaanch:** x = 2 rakho. Bayaan taraf: (5)(7) = 35. Daayan taraf: 4 + 16 + 15 = 35 ✅

### FOIL wali chhoti trick

Do binomials multiply karte waqt ek order yaad rakh sakte ho, jise **FOIL** kehte hain:

| Akshar | Matlab | (x + 3)(x + 5) mein |
|---|---|---|
| **F** (First) | Dono ke pehle terms | x × x = x² |
| **O** (Outer) | Bahar wale terms | x × 5 = 5x |
| **I** (Inner) | Andar wale terms | 3 × x = 3x |
| **L** (Last) | Dono ke aakhri terms | 3 × 5 = 15 |

Ye sirf ek yaad rakhne ka tareeka hai. Asal niyam wahi hai: **har term ko har term se multiply karo.**

### Sign ka dhyan

(2x − 3)(x + 4):
- 2x × x = 2x²
- 2x × 4 = 8x
- (−3) × x = −3x
- (−3) × 4 = −12

= 2x² + 8x − 3x − 12 = **2x² + 5x − 12**

Minus sign apne term ke saath hi chipka rehta hai, jaise Polynomials chapter mein dekha tha.

### Identity aur equation ka farak

Ye farak samajhna bahut zaroori hai, kyunki is chapter ke saare formulas **identities** hain.

- **Equation:** x + 2 = 5. Ye sirf **x = 3** par sach hai. x = 4 rakho to 6 = 5, jhooth.
- **Identity:** (x + 3)(x + 5) = x² + 8x + 15. Ye x ki **har** value par sach hai.

Ek identity ko kuch values par check karke dekho:

| x | (x + 3)(x + 5) | x² + 8x + 15 | Barabar? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 3 × 5 = 15 | 0 + 0 + 15 = 15 | ✅ |
| 1 | 4 × 6 = 24 | 1 + 8 + 15 = 24 | ✅ |
| −2 | 1 × 3 = 3 | 4 − 16 + 15 = 3 | ✅ |
| 10 | 13 × 15 = 195 | 100 + 80 + 15 = 195 | ✅ |

Har baar barabar. Isiliye ise identity kehte hain. Identity ek "pakka formula" hai jise bina soche kahin bhi use kar sakte ho.

## Part B: Identity 1: (a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²

### Tasveer se samjho

Ek square socho jiski har side **(a + b)** hai. Uska area (a + b)² hai. Ab ise lines kheench kar 4 hisson mein baanto:

```
        a           b
   +-----------+-------+
   |           |       |
 a |    a²     |  ab   |
   |           |       |
   +-----------+-------+
 b |    ab     |  b²   |
   +-----------+-------+
```

- Ek bada square: a × a = **a²**
- Ek chhota square: b × b = **b²**
- Do rectangle: har ek a × b = **ab**, to milkar **2ab**

Kul area = a² + ab + ab + b² = **a² + 2ab + b²**

### Algebra se proof

(a + b)² = (a + b)(a + b)
= a × a + a × b + b × a + b × b
= a² + ab + ab + b²
= **a² + 2ab + b²**

### Yaad rakhne ka tareeka

"**Pehle ka square, dono ka double product, doosre ka square.**"

Ananya ne 102² aise hi nikala tha: 100² + 2(100)(2) + 2².

## Part C: Identity 2: (a − b)² = a² − 2ab + b²

### Tasveer se samjho

Ek bada square lo jiski side **a** hai. Usme se ek side par **b** chaudi patti aur doosri side par bhi **b** chaudi patti kaat do. Jo chhota square bachta hai, uski side **(a − b)** hai.

```
          a − b         b
   +---------------+-------+
   |               |       |
   |               |       |
a−b|   (a − b)²    |       |
   |               |       |
   +---------------+-------+
 b |               |  b²   |
   +---------------+-------+
   <--------- a --------->
```

Bade square a² mein se do patti hatao. Har patti ka area a × b = ab hai. Par dekho, kone wala chhota b² square **dono pattiyon mein gin liya gaya**, yaani do baar kat gaya. Isliye use ek baar wapas jodna padega:

(a − b)² = a² − ab − ab + b² = **a² − 2ab + b²**

### Algebra se proof

(a − b)² = (a − b)(a − b)
= a² − ab − ab + b²
= **a² − 2ab + b²**

Dhyan do: (−b) × (−b) = **+b²**. Minus × minus plus hota hai. Isliye aakhri term hamesha **plus** hai.

### Farak sirf ek sign ka

| Identity | Beech wala term | Aakhri term |
|---|---|---|
| (a + b)² | **+2ab** | +b² |
| (a − b)² | **−2ab** | +b² |

Ananya ka 98²: (100 − 2)² = 10000 − 400 + 4 = **9604**.

## Part D: Identity 3: (a + b)(a − b) = a² − b²

### Algebra se proof

(a + b)(a − b)
= a × a − a × b + b × a − b × b
= a² − ab + ab − b²
= **a² − b²**

Beech ke dono terms (−ab aur +ab) aapas mein **kat gaye**. Isliye ye identity sabse chhoti aur sabse pyaari hai.

### Tasveer se samjho

Ek a × a square lo, aur uske kone se ek b × b ka chhota square kaat do. Bacha hua area **a² − b²** hai. Ye "L" jaisi shakal hai. Ise do rectangles mein kaato:

```
          a − b         b
   +---------------+-------+
   |               |       |
a−b|       P       |   Q   |      upar wali patti = P + Q = a × (a − b)
   |               |       |
   +---------------+-------+
 b |       R       | (kata)       neeche wali patti = R = (a − b) × b
   +---------------+
```

- Upar wali patti (P + Q): lambai **a**, chaudai **(a − b)**
- Neeche wali patti (R): lambai **(a − b)**, chaudai **b**. Ise ghuma do, to iski ek side **b** aur doosri **(a − b)** hai.

Ab R ko ghuma kar upar wali patti ke **bagal mein** lagao, taaki dono ki chaudai (a − b) ek line mein aaye:

```
   +-----------------------+-----------+
   |           a           |     b     |   chaudai (a − b)
   +-----------------------+-----------+
   <------------- a + b -------------->
```

Ek rectangle ban gaya jiski lambai **(a + b)** aur chaudai **(a − b)** hai. Area wahi hai jo L ka tha, isliye (a + b)(a − b) = a² − b².

(Kaagaz kaat ke khud karke dekhna aur bhi mazedaar hai.)

### Mental maths

Ananya ka 103 × 97: dono 100 se 3 door hain, ek upar ek neeche. To (100 + 3)(100 − 3) = 100² − 3² = 10000 − 9 = **9991**.

Trick: jab do numbers ek "gol" number se **barabar door** hon (ek upar, ek neeche), ye identity lagao.

## Part E: Identity 4: (x + a)(x + b) = x² + (a + b)x + ab

### Algebra se proof

(x + a)(x + b)
= x × x + x × b + a × x + a × b
= x² + bx + ax + ab
= **x² + (a + b)x + ab**

### Yaad rakhne ka tareeka

Jab dono brackets mein **pehla term same** ho (x), to:
- pehla term: **x²**
- beech wala: dono numbers ka **jod** × x
- aakhri: dono numbers ka **guna**

### Tasveer

```
          x          b
   +-----------+-------+
   |           |       |
 x |    x²     |  bx   |
   |           |       |
   +-----------+-------+
 a |    ax     |  ab   |
   +-----------+-------+
```

### Negative numbers ke saath

Ye identity tab bhi kaam karti hai jab a ya b negative hon. Bas sign ke saath number rakho.

- (x + 4)(x − 7): yahan a = 4, b = −7.
  Jod: 4 + (−7) = −3. Guna: 4 × (−7) = −28.
  = **x² − 3x − 28**
- (y − 3)(y − 5): a = −3, b = −5.
  Jod: −8. Guna: +15.
  = **y² − 8y + 15**
- (x − 2)(x + 6): a = −2, b = 6.
  Jod: +4. Guna: −12.
  = **x² + 4x − 12**

### Ek baat dhyan do

Identity 1, 2 aur 3 asal mein Identity 4 ke hi khaas roop hain. Jaise (x + 3)(x + 3) mein a = b = 3: x² + 6x + 9, jo (x + 3)² ka formula hi hai.

## Part F: Solved examples

**Example 1:** (2x + 3y)²

Identity 1 mein a = 2x, b = 3y:
= (2x)² + 2(2x)(3y) + (3y)²
= **4x² + 12xy + 9y²**

Dhyan do: (2x)² = 4x², sirf 2x² nahi. Coefficient ka bhi square hota hai.

**Example 2:** (3a − 4b)²

Identity 2 mein a → 3a, b → 4b:
= (3a)² − 2(3a)(4b) + (4b)²
= **9a² − 24ab + 16b²**

**Example 3:** (x/2 + 2/x)²

= (x/2)² + 2(x/2)(2/x) + (2/x)²
= x²/4 + 2 + 4/x²

Beech wala term: 2 × (x/2) × (2/x) = 2 × 1 = **2**. Yahan x aapas mein kat gaya.

= **x²/4 + 2 + 4/x²**

**Example 4:** (5x − 1/2)²

= (5x)² − 2(5x)(1/2) + (1/2)²
= **25x² − 5x + 1/4**

**Example 5:** (2a + 3)(2a − 3)

Identity 3:
= (2a)² − 3²
= **4a² − 9**

**Example 6:** (x + 4)(x − 7)

Identity 4 (upar Part E mein dekha):
= **x² − 3x − 28**

**Jaanch:** x = 1: (5)(−6) = −30. Formula: 1 − 3 − 28 = −30 ✅

**Example 7:** (y − 3)(y − 5)

= **y² − 8y + 15**

**Jaanch:** y = 0: (−3)(−5) = 15. Formula: 0 − 0 + 15 = 15 ✅

**Example 8 (Simplify):** (a + b)² + (a − b)²

= (a² + 2ab + b²) + (a² − 2ab + b²)
= **2a² + 2b²**

2ab aur −2ab kat gaye.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: (a + b)² = a² + b² likh dena**
Ye sabse badi galti hai. Number se check karo: a = 2, b = 3.
- (2 + 3)² = 5² = **25**
- 2² + 3² = 4 + 9 = **13**

25 ≠ 13. Beech wala **2ab = 12** bhool gaye. Square ka tasveer yaad karo: do rectangle bhi hote hain.

**Galti 2: (a − b)² mein aakhri sign minus kar dena**
(a − b)² = a² − 2ab **+** b². Aakhri term plus hai, kyunki (−b)² = +b².

**Galti 3: Coefficient ka square bhool jana**
(3x)² = 3x × 3x = **9x²**, 3x² nahi.
(2x + 5)² = 4x² + 20x + 25. Agar 2x² likha, to galat.

**Galti 4: Beech wale term mein 2 bhool jana**
(x + 4)² = x² + **8**x + 16. 2 × x × 4 = 8x. Sirf 4x likhna galat hai.

**Galti 5: (x + a)(x + b) mein sign galat lena**
(x + 4)(x − 7) mein guna 4 × (−7) = **−28** hai, +28 nahi. Number ko uske sign ke saath uthao.

**Galti 6: a² − b² ko (a − b)² samajhna**
- a² − b² = (a + b)(a − b)
- (a − b)² = a² − 2ab + b²

Dono alag hain. a = 5, b = 3: a² − b² = 16, par (a − b)² = 4.

## Quick Recap

- **Expand karna** = brackets ko multiply karke hatana. Niyam: har term ko har term se multiply karo (distributive law).
- **Identity** har value par sach hoti hai, **equation** sirf kuch values par.
- **Chaar basic identities:**
  - (a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
  - (a − b)² = a² − 2ab + b²
  - (a + b)(a − b) = a² − b²
  - (x + a)(x + b) = x² + (a + b)x + ab
- Har identity ki ek **tasveer** hai: square ya rectangle ko tukdon mein baantna.
- Coefficient ka bhi square hota hai: (3x)² = 9x².
- **Mental maths:** "gol" number ke paas wale numbers ka square ya guna in identities se second mein.
- Sabse badi galti: (a + b)² ≠ a² + b².

## Practice Questions

1. Expand using a suitable identity:
   (a) (x + 5)²
   (b) (2a − 7)²
   (c) (3p + 2q)²
   (d) (x/3 − 3/x)²

2. Expand:
   (a) (4x + 5)(4x − 5)
   (b) (x + 6)(x + 2)
   (c) (y − 4)(y + 9)
   (d) (a − 8)(a − 3)

3. Evaluate using identities (no long multiplication):
   (a) 105²
   (b) 95²
   (c) 101 × 99
   (d) 299²

4. Is (x + 3)² = x² + 9 an identity? Check by putting x = 1.

5. Fill in the blanks:
   (a) (2x + ___)² = 4x² + 20x + 25
   (b) (x − ___)² = x² − 14x + 49

6. Simplify: (a + b)² − (a − b)²

7. Show that (3x + 7)² − 84x = (3x − 7)².

8. Find (x + 2)(x + 3) using an identity, and check your answer by putting x = 10.

### Answers

1. (a) x² + 2(x)(5) + 25 = **x² + 10x + 25**
   (b) (2a)² − 2(2a)(7) + 7² = **4a² − 28a + 49**
   (c) (3p)² + 2(3p)(2q) + (2q)² = **9p² + 12pq + 4q²**
   (d) (x/3)² − 2(x/3)(3/x) + (3/x)² = **x²/9 − 2 + 9/x²** (beech mein x kat gaya, 2 × 1 = 2)

2. (a) (4x)² − 5² = **16x² − 25**
   (b) Jod 8, guna 12: **x² + 8x + 12**
   (c) a = −4, b = 9. Jod 5, guna −36: **y² + 5y − 36**
   (d) a = −8, b = −3. Jod −11, guna +24: **a² − 11a + 24**

3. (a) (100 + 5)² = 10000 + 1000 + 25 = **11025**
   (b) (100 − 5)² = 10000 − 1000 + 25 = **9025**
   (c) (100 + 1)(100 − 1) = 10000 − 1 = **9999**
   (d) (300 − 1)² = 90000 − 600 + 1 = **89401**

4. x = 1 rakho: (1 + 3)² = **16**, par 1² + 9 = **10**. Barabar nahi, isliye ye **identity nahi** hai ❌. Sahi expansion x² + 6x + 9 hai.

5. (a) 4x² = (2x)², aur 2(2x)(?) = 20x se ? = **5**. Jaanch: 5² = 25 ✅
   (b) 2(x)(?) = 14x se ? = **7**. Jaanch: 7² = 49 ✅

6. (a² + 2ab + b²) − (a² − 2ab + b²) = a² + 2ab + b² − a² + 2ab − b² = **4ab**

7. Bayaan taraf: (3x + 7)² − 84x = 9x² + 42x + 49 − 84x = 9x² − 42x + 49.
   Daayan taraf: (3x − 7)² = 9x² − 42x + 49.
   Dono barabar ✅

8. Identity 4: jod 5, guna 6. **x² + 5x + 6**.
   Jaanch x = 10 par: (12)(13) = 156. Formula: 100 + 50 + 6 = 156 ✅
