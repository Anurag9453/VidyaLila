# Topic 1: Introduction, Linear Equations in Two Variables aur unka Solution

Polynomials chapter mein humne **variables** (badalne wale letters), **constants** aur **degree** (sabse badi power) ke baare mein seekha tha. Wahan humne expressions ke saath kaam kiya, jaise 3x + 5. Ab hum ek kadam aage jayenge: **equations** ke saath kaam karenge, yaani wo statements jinme **barabar (=)** ka nishaan hota hai. Aur is baar ek nahi, **do variables** honge.

---

## Ek canteen ki kahani

**Pehla din.** Riya school canteen gayi. Usne **2 notebooks** aur ek **₹3 ki pencil** kharidi. Kul bill aaya **₹11**. Notebook ka daam kya tha?

Maan lo ek notebook ka daam **x** rupaye hai. To:

**2x + 3 = 11**

Ye ek aisi equation hai jisme sirf **ek variable (x)** hai. Iska jawab nikalo:

- 2x = 11 - 3 = 8
- x = **4**

Notebook ₹4 ki thi. Aur dhyan do, **x ka sirf ek hi jawab hai: 4.** Koi aur number rakho to bill ₹11 nahi aayega.

**Doosra din.** Ab Aman canteen gaya. Usne **2 samose** aur **1 chai** li aur **₹30** diye. Par use na samose ka daam pata hai, na chai ka.

Maan lo ek samosa **x** rupaye ka hai aur ek chai **y** rupaye ki. To:

**2x + y = 30**

Ab ye equation **do variables (x aur y)** wali hai. Kya iska ek hi jawab hai? Dekho:

| Samosa (x) | Chai (y) | Bill = 2x + y |
|---|---|---|
| ₹10 | ₹10 | 20 + 10 = ₹30 |
| ₹12 | ₹6 | 24 + 6 = ₹30 |
| ₹5 | ₹20 | 10 + 20 = ₹30 |
| ₹14 | ₹2 | 28 + 2 = ₹30 |

Sab mein bill ₹30 hi aaya! Yaani **kai jawab ho sakte hain.** Sirf is ek jaankari se hum pakka nahi bata sakte ki samosa kitne ka tha. (Pakka jawab paane ke liye ek aur jaankari chahiye hoti hai. Wo baat aage ke chapters mein aayegi.)

Is topic mein hum isi doosre din wali equation ko samjhenge: **linear equation in two variables**, aur uske **solutions**.

## Part A: Ek variable wali linear equation (thoda yaad kar lo)

Ye aapne pichli class mein padha hai, to bas jaldi se yaad kar lete hain.

**Linear equation in one variable** wo equation hai jisme **sirf ek variable** ho aur uski **power 1** ho.

Examples:
- 2x + 3 = 11
- 5y - 7 = 8
- x/2 = 6

Inhe ek **taraazu (balance)** ki tarah socho. Dono palde barabar hain. Aap jo bhi ek taraf karo, wahi doosri taraf bhi karo, to taraazu barabar rahega.

**5y - 7 = 8**
- Dono taraf 7 jodo: 5y = 15
- Dono taraf 5 se divide karo: y = **3**

Jaanch: 5(3) - 7 = 15 - 7 = 8 ✅

### Iska sirf ek hi jawab kyun?

Kyunki sirf ek cheez (y) pata nahi hai, aur uske baare mein ek poori jaankari (equation) mil gayi. Ek anjaan, ek jaankari, to **ek pakka jawab.**

Doosre din ki kahani mein **do anjaan** the (samosa aur chai), par jaankari sirf **ek** thi. Isliye pakka jawab nahi mila, kai jawab mil gaye.

## Part B: Linear Equation in Two Variables

### Definition

> **Linear equation in two variables** wo equation hai jisme **do variables** hon (jaise x aur y), **dono ki power 1** ho, aur dono variables **aapas mein multiply na hon.**

Iska **standard form** hai:

> **ax + by + c = 0**
>
> jahan a, b, c numbers (real numbers) hain, aur **a aur b dono ek saath zero nahi** ho sakte.

- **a** = x ka coefficient
- **b** = y ka coefficient
- **c** = constant term

(Coefficient aur constant term ka matlab humne Polynomials chapter ke shuru mein dekha tha.)

### "Linear" naam kyun?

Polynomials chapter ke Topic 4 mein humne seekha tha ki **degree 1** wale polynomial ko **linear** kehte hain. Yahan bhi wahi baat hai: x aur y dono ki power 1 hai, isliye ye **linear** equation hai.

Is naam ke peeche ek aur mazedaar wajah hai, jo hum agle topic mein dekhenge.

### a, b aur c pehchanna

Pehle equation ko **ax + by + c = 0** ki shakal mein lao, yaani **saare terms left side** mein le jao aur right side par sirf **0** rakho. Phir a, b, c padh lo.

**Example 1:** 2x + 3y = 12
Saare terms left mein: 2x + 3y - 12 = 0
**a = 2, b = 3, c = -12**

Dhyan do, c = **-12** hai, 12 nahi. Right se left aate hi sign badal gaya.

**Example 2:** x - 5y = 0
Ye pehle se standard form mein hai.
**a = 1, b = -5, c = 0**

**Example 3:** y = 3x + 2
Saare terms left mein: y - 3x - 2 = 0. Ise x pehle likh ke: -3x + y - 2 = 0.
**a = -3, b = 1, c = -2**

Chaho to saare terms **right side** mein le jao: 0 = 3x - y + 2, yaani 3x - y + 2 = 0, to a = 3, b = -1, c = 2. **Dono sahi hain**, kyunki poori equation ko -1 se multiply karne par ek doosre mein badal jaate hain. Bas ek baar jo form chuno, usi se a, b, c padho.

**Example 4:** 4x = 7
Yahan y hai hi nahi. Hum ise likh sakte hain: 4x + **0·y** - 7 = 0
**a = 4, b = 0, c = -7**

**Example 5:** y = -2
x nahi hai, to: **0·x** + y + 2 = 0
**a = 0, b = 1, c = 2**

**Example 6 (fraction wala):** x/2 + y/3 = 1
Fractions hatane ke liye dono taraf **6** (2 aur 3 ka LCM) se multiply karo:
3x + 2y = 6
3x + 2y - 6 = 0
**a = 3, b = 2, c = -6**

**Example 7 (bracket wala):** 3(x - y) = 2x + 1
Bracket kholo: 3x - 3y = 2x + 1
Sab left mein: 3x - 3y - 2x - 1 = 0
Like terms jodo: **x - 3y - 1 = 0**
**a = 1, b = -3, c = -1**

Jaanch (koi bhi value daal ke): x = 4, y = 1 lo. Original: 3(4 - 1) = 9, aur 2(4) + 1 = 9. Barabar ✅. Naya form: 4 - 3 - 1 = 0 ✅

### Kaun linear in two variables hai, kaun nahi?

| Equation | Linear in two variables? | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| 2x + 3y = 7 | **Haan** | Do variables, dono ki power 1 |
| 4x = 3y | **Haan** | 4x - 3y = 0, dono ki power 1 |
| x/2 + y/3 = 1 | **Haan** | Fraction coefficient mein hai, power 1 hi hai |
| x² + y = 5 | **Nahi** | x ki power 2 hai |
| xy = 6 | **Nahi** | x aur y aapas mein multiply ho rahe hain. Term xy ki degree 1 + 1 = 2 hai |
| 1/x + y = 2 | **Nahi** | 1/x = x⁻¹, power -1 hai (Polynomials Topic 3 mein dekha tha ki ye polynomial nahi hota) |
| √x + y = 3 | **Nahi** | √x ki power 1/2 hai |
| 0x + 0y + 5 = 0 | **Nahi** | a aur b dono zero hain. Ye to bas "5 = 0" ban gaya, jo galat statement hai |
| x = 3 | **Haan, ek tarah se** | Ise 1·x + 0·y - 3 = 0 likh sakte hain (Part C ke end mein dekhenge) |

### Shabdon se equation banana

Kahani ko maths mein badalna is chapter ka sabse kaam ka hunar hai. Pehle tay karo ki **x kya hai** aur **y kya hai**, phir likho.

**Example 1:** Do numbers ka sum 25 hai.
Pehla number x, doosra y.
**x + y = 25**

**Example 2:** Ek match mein Virat aur Rohit ne milkar 176 runs banaye.
Virat ke runs x, Rohit ke y.
**x + y = 176**

**Example 3:** Ek pen ka daam, ek pencil ke daam ke dugne se ₹5 zyada hai.
Pen ₹x, pencil ₹y.
x = 2y + 5, yaani **x - 2y - 5 = 0**

**Example 4:** Riya ki umar uske bhai ki umar ki 3 guna hai.
Riya ki umar x, bhai ki y.
x = 3y, yaani **x - 3y = 0**

**Example 5:** Ek rectangle ki length x cm aur breadth y cm hai, aur uska perimeter 40 cm hai.
Perimeter = 2(length + breadth)
**2x + 2y = 40**, ya dono taraf 2 se divide karke **x + y = 20**

**Example 6:** Ek number ka 3 guna, doosre number ke 2 gune se 7 zyada hai.
3x = 2y + 7, yaani **3x - 2y - 7 = 0**

## Part C: Solution of a Linear Equation

### Solution kya hota hai?

Canteen ki kahani mein samosa ₹10 aur chai ₹10 rakhne par 2x + y = 30 sach ho gaya. To **x = 10, y = 10** is equation ka ek **solution** hai.

> Linear equation in two variables ka **solution** x aur y ki aisi **jodi** hai, jise rakhne par equation ki **left side aur right side barabar** ho jayein.

### Ordered pair: jodi likhne ka tarika

Har baar "x = 10, y = 10" likhne ki jagah hum chhota likhte hain:

**(10, 10)**

Isse **ordered pair** kehte hain. Niyam:

> **(x, y)**: pehle **x** ki value, phir **y** ki value. Hamesha isi kram mein.

### Order kyun zaroori hai?

Socho aapke paas cinema ka ticket hai: "**Row 5, Seat 3**". Agar aap galti se "Row 3, Seat 5" par baith gaye, to wo kisi aur ki seat hai! Numbers wahi hain, par kram badalne se jagah badal gayi.

Equation ke saath bhi aisa hi hai. **2x + y = 7** mein dekho:

- **(2, 3):** x = 2, y = 3 → 2(2) + 3 = 4 + 3 = 7 ✅ **Solution hai**
- **(3, 2):** x = 3, y = 2 → 2(3) + 2 = 6 + 2 = 8 ❌ **Solution nahi hai**

Same numbers, alag kram, alag nateeja. Isiliye isse **ordered** pair kehte hain.

### Kaise check karein ki koi pair solution hai ya nahi?

1. Pair mein se **pehla number x** ki jagah, **doosra number y** ki jagah rakho.
2. Left side calculate karo.
3. Right side se milao. Barabar ho to solution hai, nahi to nahi.

**Example:** Kaun se pairs **x + 2y = 8** ke solutions hain?

| Pair | x + 2y | = 8? |
|---|---|---|
| (2, 3) | 2 + 6 = 8 | ✅ Haan |
| (4, 2) | 4 + 4 = 8 | ✅ Haan |
| (1, 3) | 1 + 6 = 7 | ❌ Nahi |
| (0, 4) | 0 + 8 = 8 | ✅ Haan |
| (8, 0) | 8 + 0 = 8 | ✅ Haan |
| (-2, 5) | -2 + 10 = 8 | ✅ Haan |

Dekho, negative numbers bhi solution ho sakte hain.

### Solutions khud kaise dhundhein?

Sabse aasan tarika:

1. Equation se **y ko x ke roop mein** likh lo (y akela ek taraf).
2. **x ki koi bhi value chuno.**
3. y ki value nikalo.
4. Ek **table** bana lo.

**Example 1:** Canteen wali equation **2x + y = 30**

Step 1: y = 30 - 2x

| x (chuna) | y = 30 - 2x | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 30 - 10 = 20 | (5, 20) |
| 10 | 30 - 20 = 10 | (10, 10) |
| 12 | 30 - 24 = 6 | (12, 6) |
| 15 | 30 - 30 = 0 | (15, 0) |
| 20 | 30 - 40 = -10 | (20, -10) |

Aakhri wala dekho: (20, -10). Maths ke hisaab se ye bilkul sahi solution hai: 2(20) + (-10) = 40 - 10 = 30 ✅. Par asli zindagi mein chai ka daam -₹10 nahi ho sakta! Isliye jab sawaal kisi kahani se juda ho, to dekhna padta hai ki solution **us situation mein samajh aata hai ya nahi.**

**Example 2:** **3x + 2y = 12**

Step 1: 2y = 12 - 3x, to **y = (12 - 3x)/2**

| x | y = (12 - 3x)/2 | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 12/2 = 6 | (0, 6) |
| 2 | 6/2 = 3 | (2, 3) |
| 4 | 0/2 = 0 | (4, 0) |
| -2 | 18/2 = 9 | (-2, 9) |
| 1 | 9/2 = 4.5 | (1, 9/2) |

Jaanch (1, 9/2): 3(1) + 2(9/2) = 3 + 9 = 12 ✅

**Tip:** x ki aisi values chuno jisse y poora number aaye. Yahan x even (0, 2, 4, -2) rakhne par y poora number aaya. Par fraction wala solution bhi utna hi sahi hai.

### Solutions kitne hote hain?

Table mein aap x ki jagah **koi bhi number** rakh sakte ho: 1, 2, 100, -7, 1/2, 0.35... Aur har x ke liye ek y mil jata hai. Numbers khatam hi nahi hote!

> Isliye **linear equation in two variables ke infinitely many (anant) solutions hote hain.**

Part A se fark yaad rakho:

| | Equation | Solutions kitne? |
|---|---|---|
| Ek variable | 2x + 3 = 11 | **Sirf ek** (x = 4) |
| Do variables | 2x + y = 30 | **Anant (infinitely many)** |

### Sabse aasan do solutions: x = 0 aur y = 0 rakh kar

Aksar sabse jaldi do solutions milte hain jab ek baar **x = 0** rakho aur ek baar **y = 0**.

**Example:** **3x + 2y = 12**
- x = 0 rakho: 2y = 12, y = 6 → **(0, 6)**
- y = 0 rakho: 3x = 12, x = 4 → **(4, 0)**

**Example:** **4x - 3y = 12**
- x = 0: -3y = 12, y = -4 → **(0, -4)**
- y = 0: 4x = 12, x = 3 → **(3, 0)**

Jaanch (0, -4): 4(0) - 3(-4) = 0 + 12 = 12 ✅

Ye do solutions agle topic mein bahut kaam aayenge.

### Jab solution diya ho aur k dhundhna ho

Agar bataya jaye ki koi pair solution hai, to use equation mein rakho. Equation sach honi chahiye, isse anjaan k mil jata hai.

**Example 1:** Agar **(2, 1)** equation **3x + ky = 10** ka solution hai, to k nikalo.

x = 2, y = 1 rakho:
3(2) + k(1) = 10
6 + k = 10
**k = 4**

Jaanch: 3(2) + 4(1) = 6 + 4 = 10 ✅

**Example 2:** Agar **(-1, 3)** equation **kx + 2y = 8** ka solution hai, to k nikalo.

x = -1, y = 3 rakho:
k(-1) + 2(3) = 8
-k + 6 = 8
-k = 2
**k = -2**

Jaanch: (-2)(-1) + 2(3) = 2 + 6 = 8 ✅

**Example 3:** Agar **(k, 2k)** equation **x + y = 12** ka solution hai, to k nikalo.

k + 2k = 12
3k = 12
**k = 4**

To solution (4, 8) hai. Jaanch: 4 + 8 = 12 ✅

### Ek variable wali equation ko do variables mein dekhna

**x = 3** ko dekho. Ek variable ki tarah iska sirf ek jawab hai: x = 3.

Par agar hum ise **do variables** ki duniya mein dekhein, to likh sakte hain:

**1·x + 0·y = 3**

Ab y ki jagah kuch bhi rakho, 0·y hamesha 0 hi hoga. To x = 3 hona chahiye, aur **y kuch bhi ho sakta hai.**

Solutions: **(3, 0), (3, 5), (3, -2), (3, 100)**... anant!

Isi tarah **y = -2** ko 0·x + 1·y = -2 likhte hain. Solutions: **(0, -2), (7, -2), (-1, -2)**... x kuch bhi, y hamesha -2.

Yaani ek hi equation ko **kis nazar se dekh rahe ho** (ek variable ya do variables), uske hisaab se solutions ki ginti badal jati hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Ordered pair mein kram ulta karna**
(x, y) mein **pehle x**. 2x + y = 7 ke liye (2, 3) solution hai, par (3, 2) nahi.

**Galti 2: Sochna ki sirf ek hi solution hai**
2x + y = 30 ka ek solution (10, 10) mil gaya to ruk mat jao. Iske **anant** solutions hain.

**Galti 3: Standard form mein sign galat**
2x + 3y = 12 ko standard form mein laane par **c = -12** hota hai, 12 nahi. Koi term = ke paar jaata hai to sign badalta hai.

**Galti 4: y = 3x + 2 mein b = 1 likh dena**
Agar 3x - y + 2 = 0 likha hai, to **b = -1** hai. Jo form chuna, usi se padho.

**Galti 5: xy = 6 ko linear samajhna**
Isme koi square nahi dikh raha, phir bhi xy ki degree **2** hai (x ki 1 + y ki 1). Isliye ye linear nahi hai.

**Galti 6: a aur b dono zero hone dena**
0x + 0y + 5 = 0 linear equation in two variables nahi hai. Kam se kam ek (a ya b) non-zero hona chahiye.

**Galti 7: Negative value rakhte waqt bracket bhoolna**
x = -2 ho to 3x = 3(**-2**) = -6. Bracket lagao, warna sign gadbad ho jata hai.

**Galti 8: Kahani wale sawaal mein ulta-pulta solution maan lena**
(20, -10) maths mein 2x + y = 30 ka solution hai, par chai ka daam negative nahi ho sakta. Kahani wale sawaal mein dekho ki jawab samajh mein aata hai ya nahi.

## Quick Recap

- **Ek variable** wali linear equation (jaise 2x + 3 = 11) ka **sirf ek** solution hota hai.
- **Linear equation in two variables:** do variables, dono ki power 1, aur aapas mein multiply nahi.
- **Standard form:** **ax + by + c = 0**, jahan a aur b dono ek saath zero nahi.
- a, b, c padhne se pehle **saare terms left side** mein le jao. Sign ka dhyan rakho.
- **Solution** = (x, y) ki aisi jodi jo equation ko sach kar de. Ise **ordered pair** likhte hain, aur **kram maayne rakhta hai.**
- Check karne ke liye: pair rakho, LHS aur RHS milao.
- Solutions dhundhne ke liye: y ko x ke roop mein likho, x chuno, y nikalo, table banao.
- **x = 0** aur **y = 0** rakhkar sabse jaldi do solutions milte hain.
- Linear equation in two variables ke **infinitely many (anant)** solutions hote hain.
- x = 3 jaisi equation ko do variables mein **1·x + 0·y = 3** likh sakte hain. Tab y kuch bhi ho sakta hai.

    (c) Kuch possible jodiyan: x = 12, y = 2 (36 + 4 = 40 ✅), x = 8, y = 8 (24 + 16 = 40 ✅), x = 6, y = 11 (18 + 22 = 40 ✅). Inme se koi bhi do.
