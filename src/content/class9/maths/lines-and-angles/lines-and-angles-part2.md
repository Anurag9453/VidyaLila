# Topic 2: Kuch Angle Relations: Adjacent Angles aur Linear Pair

Topic 1 mein humne ek angle ko akele dekha tha: uski do arms aur ek vertex hoti hai, use degree mein naapte hain, aur naap ke hisaab se wo acute, right, obtuse, straight, reflex ya complete hota hai. Meera ki deewar ghadi se humne ye bhi nikala tha ki 3 baje 90° aur 6 baje 180° hota hai. Ab hum ek kadam aage chalenge. Is topic mein hum **do ya zyada angles ko saath mein** dekhenge: kab unka jod 90° hota hai, kab 180°, aur kab wo ek doosre ke "padosi" kehlate hain.

## Pizza ki kahani

Riya ke ghar pizza aaya. Pizza gol tha, aur dukaan wale ne use **8 barabar slices** mein kaata tha.

Topic 1 mein acute angle ke example mein humne pizza ka slice dekha tha: poora pizza ek **complete angle** (360°) hai, to 8 slice wale pizza ka har slice 360° ÷ 8 = **45°** ka hota hai. Riya ka pizza bhi waisa hi hai. Par is baar hum ek slice ko akele nahi, **kai slices ko saath mein** dekhenge.

Ab mazedaar baatein shuru hui:

- Riya ne **2 slices** uthaye jo saath saath the. Unka angle hua 45° + 45° = **90°**, yaani ek **right angle** (ek kona, jaise kitaab ka kona).
- Uske bhai ne **4 slices** uthaye, ek line se. Unka angle hua 4 × 45° = **180°**, yaani **aadha pizza**. Kinare se kinare tak ek **seedhi line** ban gayi.
- Bache **4 slices** mummy-papa ke liye. Wo bhi 180° the.

Riya ne dekha:
- Do angles jud kar **90°** ban sakte hain.
- Do angles jud kar **180°** ban sakte hain.
- Saath saath wale slices ek **common kinara** share karte hain.

Yahi teen baatein is topic ke teen bade idea hain: **complementary angles**, **supplementary angles**, aur **adjacent angles / linear pair**.

## Part A: Complementary Angles (jod 90°)

**Complementary angles** wo do angles hain jinka **jod 90°** ho.

Yaad rakhne ka tarika: **C**omplementary → **C**orner. Kona yaani right angle, 90°.

### Examples

| Angle 1 | Angle 2 | Jod | Complementary? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30° | 60° | 90° | Haan |
| 45° | 45° | 90° | Haan |
| 20° | 70° | 90° | Haan |
| 50° | 50° | 100° | Nahi |

### Complement kaise nikalein

Kisi angle x ka complement = **90° − x**

- 40° ka complement = 90° − 40° = **50°**
- 63° ka complement = 90° − 63° = **27°**
- 89° ka complement = **1°**

**Dhyan do:** Complement sirf **acute angle** (90° se chhota) ka hi hota hai. 100° ka complement nikalo to 90 − 100 = −10° aata hai, jo angle nahi hai. Isliye obtuse angle ka koi complement nahi hota.

### Do complementary angles saath mein (ek kona baante hue)

Socho ek kamre ka kona (90°) hai, aur usme ek ray OC beech mein hai.

```
  A
  |
  |     C
  |    /
  |   /
  |  /
  | /
  |/________ B
  O
```

∠AOB = 90° hai. Ray OC ne ise do hisson mein baant diya: ∠AOC aur ∠COB.
To **∠AOC + ∠COB = 90°**.

**Example:** ∠AOC = 2x + 6 aur ∠COB = x + 12. x nikalo.

(2x + 6) + (x + 12) = 90
3x + 18 = 90
3x = 72
**x = 24**

∠AOC = 2(24) + 6 = **54°**, ∠COB = 24 + 12 = **36°**

Jaanch: 54° + 36° = 90° ✅

## Part B: Supplementary Angles (jod 180°)

**Supplementary angles** wo do angles hain jinka **jod 180°** ho.

Yaad rakhne ka tarika: **S**upplementary → **S**traight. Seedhi line, 180°.

### Examples

| Angle 1 | Angle 2 | Jod | Supplementary? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 110° | 70° | 180° | Haan |
| 90° | 90° | 180° | Haan |
| 150° | 30° | 180° | Haan |
| 100° | 100° | 200° | Nahi |

### Supplement kaise nikalein

Kisi angle x ka supplement = **180° − x**

- 40° ka supplement = 180° − 40° = **140°**
- 75° ka supplement = **105°**
- 128° ka supplement = **52°**
- 90° ka supplement = **90°** (right angle ka supplement khud right angle hai)

### Ek chhoti si soch

| Agar ek angle ... ho | To uska supplement ... hoga |
|---|---|
| Acute (90° se kam) | Obtuse (90° se zyada) |
| Right (90°) | Right (90°) |
| Obtuse (90° se zyada) | Acute (90° se kam) |

Isliye **do acute angles kabhi supplementary nahi ho sakte** (dono milkar bhi 180° tak nahi pahunchte), aur **do obtuse angles bhi nahi** (dono milkar 180° se aage nikal jaate hain).

## Part C: Complement aur Supplement wale word problems

Yahan ek trick kaam aati hai: **angle ko x maano**, complement ko (90 − x) aur supplement ko (180 − x) likho, phir sawaal ki baat ko equation mein badlo.

**Example 1:** Ek angle apne complement se 20° bada hai. Angle nikalo.

Angle = x, complement = 90 − x
"Angle, complement se 20° bada hai": x = (90 − x) + 20
x = 110 − x
2x = 110
**x = 55°**

Complement = 90 − 55 = 35°
Jaanch: 55 − 35 = 20 ✅, aur 55 + 35 = 90 ✅

**Example 2:** Do supplementary angles **2 : 3** ke ratio mein hain. Dono angles nikalo.

Angles = 2k aur 3k
2k + 3k = 180
5k = 180
k = 36
Angles = 2(36) = **72°** aur 3(36) = **108°**

Jaanch: 72 + 108 = 180 ✅, aur 72 : 108 = 2 : 3 ✅

**Example 3:** Ek angle apne complement ka 4 guna hai. Angle nikalo.

x = 4(90 − x)
x = 360 − 4x
5x = 360
**x = 72°**

Complement = 18°. Jaanch: 4 × 18 = 72 ✅

**Example 4:** Ek angle apne supplement ke barabar hai. Angle nikalo.

x = 180 − x
2x = 180
**x = 90°**

**Example 5:** Kisi angle ka supplement, uske complement ka 3 guna hai. Angle nikalo.

180 − x = 3(90 − x)
180 − x = 270 − 3x
3x − x = 270 − 180
2x = 90
**x = 45°**

Jaanch: supplement = 135°, complement = 45°, aur 3 × 45 = 135 ✅

## Part D: Adjacent Angles (padosi angles)

Pizza mein do saath wale slices yaad karo. Dono ka **centre same** hai, dono ke beech **ek kinara common** hai, aur dono us kinare ke **alag alag taraf** hain. Aise angles ko **adjacent angles** kehte hain. "Adjacent" ka matlab hai **paas wala, padosi**.

### Adjacent hone ki 3 shartein

Do angles adjacent tabhi hain jab **teeno** shartein poori hon:

1. **Common vertex:** dono ka kona (vertex) ek hi ho.
2. **Common arm:** dono ki ek arm same ho.
3. **Non-common arms common arm ke alag alag taraf hon:** yaani dono angles ek doosre ke **upar na chadhein** (overlap na karein).

```
         A
        /
       /     C
      /    /
     /   /
    /  /
   / /
  O/___________ B
```

Yahan ∠AOC aur ∠COB:
- Common vertex: **O** ✅
- Common arm: **OC** ✅
- Non-common arms OA aur OB, OC ke alag alag taraf hain ✅

To **∠AOC aur ∠COB adjacent angles hain**.

Ek aur baat dekho: jab do angles adjacent hote hain, to unka jod bada wala angle ban jata hai.
**∠AOC + ∠COB = ∠AOB**

### Non-examples (ye adjacent nahi hain)

**Non-example 1: Vertex alag hai**

```
     A               D
    /               /
   /               /
  O------ B       P------ E
```

∠AOB aur ∠DPE ka vertex alag hai (O aur P). Adjacent nahi.

**Non-example 2: Vertex same, par common arm nahi**

```
   A         D
    \       /
     \     /
      \   /
       \ /
        O
       / \
      /   \
     B     E
```

∠AOB aur ∠DOE ka vertex O same hai, par koi arm common nahi. Adjacent nahi.

**Non-example 3: Ek angle doosre ke andar (overlap)**

Pehle wale diagram mein ∠AOB aur ∠AOC dekho. Vertex O common hai, arm OA common hai. **Par** C, ∠AOB ke **andar** hai, yaani non-common arms OB aur OC, common arm OA ke **ek hi taraf** hain. ∠AOC, ∠AOB ka hissa hai. Isliye ye **adjacent nahi** hain.

### Quick check table

| Shart | ∠AOC & ∠COB | ∠AOB & ∠AOC |
|---|---|---|
| Common vertex? | Haan (O) | Haan (O) |
| Common arm? | Haan (OC) | Haan (OA) |
| Non-common arms alag taraf? | Haan | **Nahi** (overlap) |
| **Adjacent?** | **Haan** | **Nahi** |

## Part E: Linear Pair

Ab pizza ka **aadha hissa** yaad karo: 4 slices jo milkar seedhi line banate hain. Agar is aadhe pizze ko sirf **do tukdon** mein kaato, to dono tukde:
- adjacent hain (common vertex, common arm),
- aur unki bahar wali arms milkar **ek seedhi line** banati hain.

Aise do angles ko **linear pair** kehte hain.

> **Linear pair:** Do adjacent angles jinki **non-common arms milkar ek seedhi line** banati hain.

```
                C
               /
              /
             /
            /
  A -------O------- B
```

Ray OC, line AB par khadi hai.
- ∠AOC aur ∠COB adjacent hain (common vertex O, common arm OC).
- Non-common arms OA aur OB milkar seedhi line AB banati hain.

To **∠AOC aur ∠COB ek linear pair** hain, aur
**∠AOC + ∠COB = 180°** (kyunki seedhi line ka angle 180° hota hai).

### Linear Pair Axiom

> **Agar ek ray kisi line par khadi ho, to us se bane do adjacent angles ka jod 180° hota hai.**

Ye bilkul wahi baat hai jo upar dekhi. Ise prove nahi karte, **maan lete hain** (Euclid's Geometry chapter mein humne dekha tha ki axiom wo baat hai jo bina proof ke maani jati hai).

**Example:** Ray OC line AB par khadi hai, aur ∠AOC = 65°. ∠BOC nikalo.

Linear Pair Axiom se: ∠AOC + ∠BOC = 180°
65° + ∠BOC = 180°
**∠BOC = 115°**

### Linear Pair Axiom ka converse (ulti baat)

> **Agar do adjacent angles ka jod 180° ho, to unki non-common arms ek seedhi line banati hain.**

Yaani agar aapko pata ho ki do padosi angles milkar 180° hain, to aap pakka keh sakte ho ki bahar wali arms ek line par hain.

**Example:** ∠AOC = 110° aur ∠COB = 70°, aur dono adjacent hain. Kya A, O, B ek seedhi line par hain?

110° + 70° = 180°. Linear Pair Axiom ke converse se, OA aur OB ek seedhi line banate hain. To **haan, A, O, B ek line par hain (collinear hain)**.

Agar ∠COB = 60° hota, to jod 170° hota, aur A, O, B ek seedhi line par **nahi** hote.

### Linear Pair Axiom aur uska converse ek saath

| | Kya diya hai | Kya milta hai |
|---|---|---|
| **Linear Pair Axiom** | Ray ek **line** par khadi hai | Dono angles ka jod **180°** |
| **Linear Pair Axiom ka converse** | Do adjacent angles ka jod **180°** | Bahar wali arms ek **line** banati hain |

Dono ek doosre ke ulte hain. Isiliye kabhi kabhi dono ko milakar bhi bas **Linear Pair Axiom** kaha jata hai.

## Part F: Linear pair aur supplementary mein farak

Ye sabse zyada confuse karne wali baat hai, isliye dhyan se dekho.

- Har **linear pair** ka jod 180° hota hai. To **har linear pair supplementary hota hai.**
- Par **har supplementary pair linear pair nahi hota.** Supplementary angles ka sirf jod 180° hona chahiye, unhe **adjacent hone ki zaroorat nahi.** Wo bilkul alag alag jagah bhi ho sakte hain.

```
  Supplementary, par linear pair NAHI:

        D                         R
       /                         /
      / 120°                    / 60°
     P--------- E              Q--------- S
```

∠DPE = 120° aur ∠RQS = 60°. Jod 180° hai, to **supplementary hain**. Par vertex alag hain (P aur Q), isliye **adjacent nahi**, aur **linear pair bhi nahi**.

| | Jod 180° zaroori? | Adjacent hona zaroori? | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Supplementary** | Haan | **Nahi** | 120° aur 60° alag jagah par |
| **Linear pair** | Haan | **Haan**, aur bahar wali arms ek line par | Line par khadi ray ke do angles |

Ek line mein: **Linear pair ek khaas tarah ka supplementary pair hai, jo padosi bhi hai.**

Isi tarah, **complementary** angles bhi adjacent ho sakte hain (jaise Part A ka kamre ka kona) ya alag alag jagah par bhi.

## Part G: Ek line par kai rays, aur ek point ke chaaron taraf

### Ek seedhi line par kai rays (jod 180°)

Linear pair mein ek ray line par khadi thi. Agar **kai rays** ek hi point se nikal kar line ke **ek taraf** hon, to bhi saare angles milkar **180°** hi banate hain. (Jaise aadha pizza 4 slices mein bhi 180° hi tha.)

```
            D     C
             \   /
        E     \ /
          \    /
           \  /
  A ---------O--------- B
```

**∠AOE + ∠EOD + ∠DOC + ∠COB = 180°**

**Example 1:** Ek line par ek point se nikle teen angles **x, 2x aur 3x** hain. x nikalo.

x + 2x + 3x = 180
6x = 180
**x = 30°**

Angles: **30°, 60°, 90°**. Jaanch: 30 + 60 + 90 = 180 ✅

**Example 2:** Line ke ek taraf teen angles **40°, x aur 65°** hain. x nikalo.

40 + x + 65 = 180
**x = 75°**

### Ek point ke chaaron taraf (jod 360°)

Poora pizza socho. Saare slices milkar **poora ghoom** jaate hain, yaani **360°** (complete angle, Topic 1 mein dekha tha).

> **Ek point ke chaaron taraf bane saare angles ka jod 360° hota hai.**

```
           B
           |
     C     |     A
       \   |   /
         \ | /
  D -------O
         / | \
       /   |   \
     E     |     H
           G
```

(Point O ke chaaron taraf jitne bhi angles bane, sabka jod 360°.)

**Example 3:** Ek point ke chaaron taraf chaar angles **x, 2x, 3x aur 4x** hain. Angles nikalo.

x + 2x + 3x + 4x = 360
10x = 360
x = 36
Angles: **36°, 72°, 108°, 144°**

Jaanch: 36 + 72 + 108 + 144 = 360 ✅

**Example 4:** Ek point ke chaaron taraf angles **100°, 80°, x aur 2x** hain. x nikalo.

100 + 80 + x + 2x = 360
180 + 3x = 360
3x = 180
**x = 60°**, aur 2x = 120°

Jaanch: 100 + 80 + 60 + 120 = 360 ✅

## Part H: Linear pair wale "find x" sawaal

**Example 1:** Do angles **(3x + 10)°** aur **(2x − 5)°** ek linear pair banate hain. x aur dono angles nikalo.

Linear pair ka jod 180°:
(3x + 10) + (2x − 5) = 180
5x + 5 = 180
5x = 175
**x = 35**

Angles: 3(35) + 10 = **115°** aur 2(35) − 5 = **65°**

Jaanch: 115 + 65 = 180 ✅

**Example 2:** Ray OC line AB par khadi hai. ∠AOC = **(2y + 15)°** aur ∠BOC = **(3y − 10)°**. y nikalo.

(2y + 15) + (3y − 10) = 180
5y + 5 = 180
5y = 175
**y = 35**

∠AOC = 85°, ∠BOC = 95°. Jaanch: 85 + 95 = 180 ✅

**Example 3:** Ek linear pair ke angles **4 : 5** ke ratio mein hain. Dono nikalo.

4k + 5k = 180
9k = 180
k = 20
Angles: **80°** aur **100°**

**Example 4:** Ray OC line AB par khadi hai, ∠AOC = 70°. Ray OD, ∠BOC ko bisect karti hai (barabar do hisson mein baantti hai, Topic 1 wala bisector). ∠COD nikalo.

Pehle ∠BOC = 180° − 70° = 110° (linear pair).
OD bisector hai, to ∠COD = 110° ÷ 2 = **55°**

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Complementary aur supplementary ulat dena**
Complementary = 90° (**C**orner), Supplementary = 180° (**S**traight). Dono ke pehle letter se yaad rakho.

**Galti 2: Har supplementary pair ko linear pair maan lena**
120° aur 60° supplementary hain, par agar unka vertex alag hai to wo linear pair **nahi** hain. Linear pair ke liye **adjacent** hona bhi zaroori hai.

**Galti 3: Overlap wale angles ko adjacent kehna**
∠AOB aur ∠AOC (jab C, ∠AOB ke andar ho) ka vertex aur ek arm common hai, phir bhi wo adjacent **nahi** hain, kyunki ek doosre ke upar chadhe hue hain. Teesri shart yaad rakho.

**Galti 4: Obtuse angle ka complement nikalna**
120° ka complement nahi hota (90 − 120 = −30, jo angle nahi hai). Complement sirf acute angle ka hota hai.

**Galti 5: Ek line par angles ka jod 360° le lena**
Ek **seedhi line ke ek taraf** bane angles ka jod **180°** hota hai. **360°** tab, jab angles ek **point ke chaaron taraf** hon.

**Galti 6: x nikal kar ruk jana**
Sawaal mein "angles nikalo" likha ho to x ki value daal kar **dono angles** bhi likho. Sirf x = 35 likhna adhoora answer hai.

**Galti 7: Equation banate waqt complement galat likhna**
"Angle, complement se 20° bada hai" ka matlab x = (90 − x) + 20 hai, x + 20 = 90 − x nahi. Pehle socho: bada kaun hai? Wahi akela ek taraf aayega.

## Quick Recap

- **Complementary angles:** jod **90°**. x ka complement = **90° − x**. Sirf acute angle ka complement hota hai.
- **Supplementary angles:** jod **180°**. x ka supplement = **180° − x**.
- **Adjacent angles:** teen shartein: **common vertex**, **common arm**, aur non-common arms common arm ke **alag alag taraf** (overlap nahi).
- **Linear pair:** adjacent angles jinki non-common arms ek **seedhi line** banayein. Jod **180°**.
- **Linear Pair Axiom:** ray line par khadi ho → do adjacent angles ka jod 180°.
- **Linear Pair Axiom ka converse:** do adjacent angles ka jod 180° → non-common arms ek line banati hain.
- **Har linear pair supplementary hai, par har supplementary pair linear pair nahi.**
- Ek line ke ek taraf saare angles ka jod **180°**. Ek point ke chaaron taraf saare angles ka jod **360°**.
- "Find x" mein angle ko x maano, sahi jod (90°, 180° ya 360°) ke barabar rakho, aur aakhir mein **jaanch** karo.

## Practice Questions

1. Find:
   (a) the complement of 40°
   (b) the complement of 63°
   (c) the supplement of 75°
   (d) the supplement of 128°

2. Find the angle which is equal to its complement.

3. Two supplementary angles differ by 40°. Find the angles.

4. An angle is four times its supplement. Find the angle.

5. The angles (5x − 20)° and (3x + 40)° form a linear pair. Find x and both angles.

6. Three angles 2x, (3x + 10)° and (4x − 10)° lie on one side of a straight line at the same point. Find each angle.

7. Four angles around a point are x, (x + 20)°, (x + 40)° and (x + 60)°. Find each angle.

8. Ray OC stands on line AB such that ∠AOC = 65°. Find ∠BOC. If ray OD bisects ∠BOC, find ∠COD.

9. State whether each statement is true or false. Give a reason.
   (a) Two acute angles can be supplementary.
   (b) Every linear pair is supplementary.
   (c) Two supplementary angles always form a linear pair.
   (d) Two right angles are supplementary.
   (e) Two obtuse angles can be complementary.

### Answers

1. (a) 90 − 40 = **50°**
   (b) 90 − 63 = **27°**
   (c) 180 − 75 = **105°**
   (d) 180 − 128 = **52°**

2. x = 90 − x → 2x = 90 → **x = 45°**

3. Angles x aur x + 40.
   x + (x + 40) = 180 → 2x = 140 → x = 70
   Angles: **70° aur 110°**. Jaanch: 70 + 110 = 180 ✅, 110 − 70 = 40 ✅

4. x = 4(180 − x) → x = 720 − 4x → 5x = 720 → **x = 144°**
   Supplement = 36°. Jaanch: 4 × 36 = 144 ✅

5. (5x − 20) + (3x + 40) = 180 → 8x + 20 = 180 → 8x = 160 → **x = 20**
   Angles: 5(20) − 20 = **80°** aur 3(20) + 40 = **100°**. Jaanch: 80 + 100 = 180 ✅

6. 2x + (3x + 10) + (4x − 10) = 180 → 9x = 180 → x = 20
   Angles: **40°, 70°, 70°**. Jaanch: 40 + 70 + 70 = 180 ✅

7. x + (x + 20) + (x + 40) + (x + 60) = 360 → 4x + 120 = 360 → 4x = 240 → x = 60
   Angles: **60°, 80°, 100°, 120°**. Jaanch: 60 + 80 + 100 + 120 = 360 ✅

8. Linear Pair Axiom se: ∠BOC = 180° − 65° = **115°**
   OD bisector hai, to ∠COD = 115° ÷ 2 = **57.5°**

9. (a) **False** ❌. Do acute angles dono 90° se chhote hain, to unka jod 180° se kam hi rahega.
   (b) **True** ✅. Linear pair ka jod hamesha 180° hota hai.
   (c) **False** ❌. Supplementary angles alag alag jagah bhi ho sakte hain. Linear pair ke liye adjacent hona zaroori hai.
   (d) **True** ✅. 90° + 90° = 180°.
   (e) **False** ❌. Obtuse angle khud 90° se bada hota hai, to do obtuse angles ka jod 90° kabhi nahi ho sakta.
