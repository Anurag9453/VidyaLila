# Topic 1: Basic Terms aur Angles (Types of Angles)

Euclid's Geometry chapter mein humne geometry ki neev rakhi thi: point, line, ray, aur wo axioms jin par sab tika hai. Ab hum us neev par pehli manzil banayenge. Is chapter ka hero hai **angle (kon)**, wo cheez jo tab banti hai jab do rays ek hi point se nikalti hain. Is topic mein hum angle ko pehchanna, uska naam rakhna, naapna, aur uske alag alag types seekhenge.

---

## Deewar ghadi ki kahani

Raat ke 9 baje hain. Meera apne kamre mein padh rahi hai aur baar baar deewar ki ghadi dekh rahi hai.

- **3 baje** dopahar ko usne dekha tha ki ghadi ki dono suiyan ek **"L"** jaisi shakal bana rahi thin.
- **6 baje** shaam ko dono suiyan ek **seedhi line** mein thin, ek upar ek neeche.
- **1 baje** dopahar ko dono suiyan **bilkul paas paas** thin, jaise kainchi thodi si khuli ho.
- Aur **12 baje** dono suiyan **ek ke upar ek** thin.

Meera ne socha: "Har baar dono suiyan ek hi point (ghadi ke beech) se nikal rahi hain, bas unke beech ka **khulaapan** badal raha hai."

Yahi "khulaapan" geometry mein **angle** kehlata hai. Aur aapke aas paas ye har jagah hai:

- Darwaza thoda khola, thoda zyada khola: angle badal gaya.
- Kainchi kholi: dono blades ke beech angle.
- Laptop ki screen kholi: screen aur keyboard ke beech angle.
- Cricket mein batsman ne bat ghumaya: bat ne ek angle banaya.

Is topic ke end tak aap in sab angles ko **naam** de paoge aur **naap** bhi paoge.

## Part B: Angle kya hai?

> **Angle:** Jab **do rays ek hi end point se** shuru hoti hain, to wo ek angle banati hain.

```
            A
           /
          /
         /
        /
       B -------------- C
```

Is figure mein:
- **BA aur BC** do rays hain. Ye angle ki **arms** hain.
- **B** wo point hai jahan se dono rays nikal rahi hain. Ye angle ka **vertex** hai.
- Dono arms ke beech ka khulaapan **angle** hai.

### Angle ka naam kaise rakhein

Angle ko teen letters se likhte hain, aur **vertex wala letter hamesha beech mein** aata hai.

Upar wale figure ka angle:
- **∠ABC** (padhte hain "angle ABC")
- ya **∠CBA** (ye bhi sahi hai)

Dono mein B beech mein hai, isliye dono same angle hain.

**Galat naam:** ∠BAC ya ∠ACB. Inme beech mein B nahi hai, to ye koi aur angle bata rahe hain.

Yaad rakhne ka tarika: **vertex sandwich ki filling hai, beech mein hi aayegi.**

### Kab sirf ek letter (∠B) chalta hai?

Jab vertex par **sirf ek hi angle** bana ho, tab ∠B likhna kaafi hai.

Par agar ek hi vertex se **teen ya zyada rays** nikal rahi hon, to wahan kai angles bante hain. Tab sirf ∠B likhne se confusion hoga.

```
            A
           /
          /      D
         /    .
        /  .
       B -------------- C
```

Yahan B par teen angles hain:
- ∠ABD (A aur D ke beech)
- ∠DBC (D aur C ke beech)
- ∠ABC (A aur C ke beech, poora wala)

Isliye yahan **teen letters zaroori** hain. Kabhi kabhi angle ko andar ek number ya Greek letter se bhi likhte hain, jaise ∠1 ya ∠x.

## Part C: Angle ka andar (interior) aur bahar (exterior)

Ek angle plane ko **teen hisson** mein baant deta hai:

```
                A
     bahar     /
    (exterior)/   andar
             /  (interior)
            /    •P
           B ------------- C
               •Q
              bahar
```

1. **Interior (andar):** dono arms ke **beech** wala hissa. Point P angle ke andar hai.
2. **Exterior (bahar):** baaki saara hissa. Point Q angle ke bahar hai.
3. **Angle khud:** dono arms par pade points. Ye na andar hain, na bahar.

Ise ek khule darwaze se samjho. Darwaza aur deewar ke beech jo jagah bani hai, wo **interior** hai. Darwaze ke peeche aur baaki kamra **exterior** hai.

## Part D: Angle ko naapna (degree aur protractor)

### Degree kya hai

Angles ko **degree** mein naapte hain. Iska chinh hai **°**.

> **Ek poora chakkar = 360°**

Agar ek ray apne vertex par poora ek chakkar ghoom kar wahin wapas aa jaye, to usne **360°** ka angle banaya.

### 360 hi kyun?

Ye ek mazedaar itihaas hai. Bahut purane zamane ke **Babylon** ke log 60 ke hisaab se ginti karte the, aur unhe lagta tha ki saal mein lagbhag 360 din hote hain. 360 ek bahut "friendly" number bhi hai, kyunki ye 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 12, 15, 18, 20, 24, 30, 36, 40, 45, 60, 72, 90, 120, 180 se poora bat jata hai. Isliye chakkar ke tukde karna aasan ho jata hai.

Iska fayda dekho:
- Aadha chakkar = 360 ÷ 2 = **180°**
- Chauthai chakkar = 360 ÷ 4 = **90°**
- Ghadi ke 12 ghante: har ghante ka hissa = 360 ÷ 12 = **30°**
- Ghadi ke 60 minute: har minute ka hissa = 360 ÷ 60 = **6°**

### Protractor se angle naapna

Protractor (chaanda) aapke geometry box ka **D-shape** wala tool hai, jis par 0 se 180 tak nishaan hote hain.

**Steps:**
1. Protractor ka **beech wala chhota nishaan (centre)** angle ke **vertex** par rakho.
2. Protractor ki **seedhi baseline** ko ek arm ke upar seedha rakho.
3. Us arm se **0** se ginti shuru karo, aur dekho ki doosri arm kis number ko chhoo rahi hai.
4. Wahi angle ka maap hai.

**Dhyan rakho:** Protractor par **do scales** hoti hain, ek andar wali aur ek bahar wali. Hamesha **wo scale padho jo pehli arm par 0 se shuru ho rahi hai.** Galat scale padhne par 50° ki jagah 130° padh loge.

Ek jaanch ki trick: agar angle dikhne mein **chhota (patla)** hai to answer 90 se kam hona chahiye, aur **chauda** hai to 90 se zyada. Isse galat scale wali galti turant pakad mein aa jati hai.

## Part E: Angles ke types

Ab sabse zaroori hissa. Angle ka **size** dekh kar uska naam rakhte hain.

### 1. Acute angle (nyoon kon): 0° se bada, 90° se chhota

```
          /
         /
        /
       /______
```

**0° < x < 90°**

Examples:
- Kainchi thodi si khuli ho.
- Ghadi mein **1 baje** (30°) aur **2 baje** (60°).
- Pizza ka ek slice (8 slice wale pizza mein har slice 360 ÷ 8 = 45°).
- Alphabet "**V**" ke beech ka angle.

### 2. Right angle (samkon): theek 90°

```
       |
       |
       |
       |_______
```

**x = 90°**

Right angle ko figure mein ek **chhote square ke nishaan** se dikhate hain.

Examples:
- Kitaab ka kona, darwaze ka kona, deewar aur zameen ka kona.
- Ghadi mein **3 baje** aur **9 baje**.
- Alphabet "**L**".
- Plus (+) ke chaaron kone.

Jab do lines right angle par milti hain, to kehte hain ki wo ek doosre ke **perpendicular (lamb)** hain.

### 3. Obtuse angle (adhik kon): 90° se bada, 180° se chhota

```
  \
   \
    \
     \_________
```

**90° < x < 180°**

Examples:
- Laptop ki screen jo thodi peeche jhuki ho.
- Ghadi mein **4 baje** (120°) aur **5 baje** (150°).
- Aaram kursi (reclining chair) ki peeth aur seat ke beech.

### 4. Straight angle (rijju kon): theek 180°

```
  <---------•--------->
            B
```

**x = 180°**

Dono arms **ek hi seedhi line** mein, ulti dishaon mein. Ye aadha chakkar hai.

Examples:
- Ghadi mein **6 baje**.
- Ek poori khuli kitaab jo table par sapaat padi ho.
- Ek seedhi sadak par khade hokar aage aur peeche dekhna.

### 5. Reflex angle (vruhat kon): 180° se bada, 360° se chhota

```
        /
       /
      /   <- ye chhota wala angle nahi,
     /       baaki bada hissa (bahar ghoom kar)
     ‾‾‾‾‾‾‾
```

**180° < x < 360°**

Reflex angle "**bahar wala bada hissa**" hai. Jab aap kisi angle ke dono arms ke beech **lambe raaste se** ghoom kar jaate ho, to wo reflex angle hai.

Examples:
- Pac-Man ka munh khula ho to uske **sharir** wala hissa ek reflex angle hai.
- Agar ghadi mein 8 baje dono suiyon ke beech ka **bada** hissa naapein to 240°.
- Darwaza ghumate ghumate deewar ke peeche tak le jao (jaise kuch darwaze 270° tak khulte hain).

### 6. Complete angle (sampoorn kon): theek 360°

**x = 360°**

Ek ray ne **poora ek chakkar** laga liya aur wapas wahin aa gayi.

Examples:
- Ghadi ki minute wali sui ka **ek ghante** mein ghoomna.
- Pankhe ki ek blade ka ek poora round.
- Gol ghoom kar wahin wapas aana (jaise "360 degree ghoom gaya").

### (Bonus) Zero angle: theek 0°

Jab dono arms **ek ke upar ek** hon, to angle 0° hai. Jaise ghadi mein **12 baje** dono suiyan.

### Saare types ek nazar mein

| Type | Hindi naam | Range | Ghadi ka example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zero | — | 0° | 12:00 |
| Acute | Nyoon kon | 0° < x < 90° | 1:00 (30°), 2:00 (60°) |
| Right | Samkon | 90° | 3:00, 9:00 |
| Obtuse | Adhik kon | 90° < x < 180° | 4:00 (120°), 5:00 (150°) |
| Straight | Rijju kon | 180° | 6:00 |
| Reflex | Vruhat kon | 180° < x < 360° | 8:00 ka bada hissa (240°) |
| Complete | Sampoorn kon | 360° | Minute sui ka 1 ghanta |

Yaad rakhne ki trick: **90 aur 180 do darwaze hain.** 90 se pehle "acute" (chhota, nukeela), 90 aur 180 ke beech "obtuse" (chauda, motaa), aur 180 ke baad "reflex" (ghoom kar peeche wala).

## Part F: Har angle ka ek reflex saathi

Jab do rays ek vertex se nikalti hain, to asal mein **do angles** bante hain: ek **chhota** (andar wala) aur ek **bada** (bahar se ghoom kar). Dono milkar poora chakkar banate hain.

> **Reflex angle = 360° − (chhota angle)**

| Chhota angle | Reflex saathi |
|---|---|
| 30° | 360° − 30° = **330°** |
| 90° | 360° − 90° = **270°** |
| 110° | 360° − 110° = **250°** |
| 145° | 360° − 145° = **215°** |
| 179° | 360° − 179° = **181°** |

Jaanch: 30 + 330 = 360 ✅, 110 + 250 = 360 ✅

Jab hum sirf "∠ABC" likhte hain, to aam taur par **chhota wala** angle samjha jata hai. Reflex chahiye ho to saaf likhte hain: "**reflex ∠ABC**".

## Part G: Angle bisector (sirf idea)

**Bisect** ka matlab hai **do barabar hisson mein baantna**.

> **Angle bisector:** Wo ray jo kisi angle ko **do barabar angles** mein baant de.

```
            A
           /
          /      D   (BD bisector hai)
         /    .
        /  .
       B -------------- C
```

Agar BD, ∠ABC ka bisector hai, to:
**∠ABD = ∠DBC = ½ ∠ABC**

Examples:
- 60° ka bisector use **30° + 30°** mein baantta hai.
- 90° ka bisector use **45° + 45°** mein baantta hai.
- 140° ka bisector use **70° + 70°** mein baantta hai.

Roz ki zindagi mein: ek roti ko theek beech se kaat do, aur phir us aadhe tukde ko theek beech se. Har kaat bisector jaisa kaam kar raha hai.

(Bisector ko compass se **banana** construction wale chapter mein seekhenge. Abhi bas iska matlab samajhna kaafi hai.)

## Part H: Ghadi ke angles nikalna

Ghadi angles ki practice ke liye sabse badhiya cheez hai. Do pakki baatein yaad rakho:

- **Minute sui** 60 minute mein 360° ghoomti hai, to **1 minute mein 6°**.
- **Ghante ki sui** 12 ghante mein 360° ghoomti hai, to **1 ghante mein 30°**, aur **1 minute mein 0.5°** (30 ÷ 60).

### Poore ghante (whole hours)

Jab ghadi theek ghante par ho (jaise 4:00), to minute sui **12** par hoti hai aur ghante ki sui us ghante ke number par. Har number ke beech **30°** hai.

**Angle = (beech mein kitne number ke gaps) × 30°**

| Samay | Gaps | Angle | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1:00 | 1 | 30° | Acute |
| 2:00 | 2 | 60° | Acute |
| 3:00 | 3 | 90° | Right |
| 4:00 | 4 | 120° | Obtuse |
| 5:00 | 5 | 150° | Obtuse |
| 6:00 | 6 | 180° | Straight |

**7, 8, 9, 10, 11 baje kya?** Tab do raaste hain: chhota aur bada. Aam taur par "suiyon ke beech ka angle" ka matlab **chhota** wala hota hai.

- 8:00 baje: ek taraf se 8 gaps (240°), doosri taraf se 4 gaps (**120°**). Chhota angle **120°**, aur reflex **240°**.
- 9:00 baje: 3 gaps ek taraf se (**90°**).
- 10:00 baje: 2 gaps (**60°**).

### Saadhe (half hours): ek chhota sa twist

Saadhe 3 baje (3:30) socho. Minute sui **6** par hai. Par ghante ki sui **3 par nahi** hai! Aadha ghanta beet gaya hai, to wo **3 aur 4 ke beech** aadhe raaste par hai.

Isse step by step nikalte hain. 12 ko 0° maan kar, ghadi ki disha mein naapo.

**3:30:**
- Minute sui: 30 minute × 6° = **180°** (6 par)
- Ghante ki sui: 3 ghante × 30° + 30 minute × 0.5° = 90° + 15° = **105°**
- Angle = 180° − 105° = **75°** (Acute)

**6:30:**
- Minute sui: **180°**
- Ghante ki sui: 6 × 30° + 15° = 180° + 15° = **195°**
- Angle = 195° − 180° = **15°** (Acute)

Dekho, 6:30 par dono suiyan lagbhag saath hain, bas ghante wali thoda aage nikal gayi hai.

**9:30:**
- Minute sui: **180°**
- Ghante ki sui: 9 × 30° + 15° = 270° + 15° = **285°**
- Angle = 285° − 180° = **105°** (Obtuse)

**12:30:**
- Minute sui: **180°**
- Ghante ki sui: 0° + 15° = **15°**
- Angle = 180° − 15° = **165°** (Obtuse)

Jaanch: 12:30 par minute sui 6 par aur ghante ki sui 12 se thodi aage. Dono lagbhag ulti disha mein, par poori seedhi nahi. 165° (180° se thoda kam) bilkul theek lagta hai ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Naam mein vertex beech mein na rakhna**
Figure mein vertex B hai, to angle ∠ABC ya ∠CBA hai. ∠BAC likhoge to wo A wala angle ho jayega, jo bilkul alag hai.

**Galti 2: Ek vertex par kai angles hon to bhi ∠B likh dena**
Jab B se teen rays nikal rahi hon, to ∠B se pata nahi chalta ki kaunsa angle. Teen letters likho.

**Galti 3: Arms ki lambai se angle ka size judge karna**
Arms lambi kheencho ya chhoti, **angle wahi rehta hai**. Angle sirf khulaapan hai, lambai nahi. Ek chhoti ghadi aur Big Ben, dono mein 3 baje 90° hi hota hai.

**Galti 4: Protractor ki galat scale padhna**
Andar aur bahar wali scales ulti chalti hain. Hamesha wo scale padho jo pehli arm par **0** se shuru ho. Phir aankh se check karo: patla angle 90 se kam, chauda 90 se zyada.

**Galti 5: 90° ko acute ya 180° ko obtuse bolna**
Acute **90 se kam** hai, 90 nahi. Obtuse **180 se kam** hai, 180 nahi. Theek 90 **right** hai aur theek 180 **straight**.

**Galti 6: Reflex angle ko "negative" ya "ulta" angle samajhna**
Reflex angle bhi ek poora positive angle hai (180° se 360° ke beech). Bas wo bahar wale lambe raaste se naapa jata hai.

**Galti 7: Saadhe time par ghante ki sui ko number par hi maan lena**
3:30 par ghante ki sui 3 par nahi, **3 aur 4 ke beech** hai. Isliye angle 90° nahi, **75°** hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Angle** do rays se banta hai jo ek hi point (**vertex**) se nikalti hain. Rays ko **arms** kehte hain.
- Naam rakhte waqt **vertex beech mein**: ∠ABC. Ek hi angle ho to ∠B bhi chalta hai.
- Angle plane ko **interior**, **exterior** aur khud angle mein baant deta hai.
- Angle **degree (°)** mein naapte hain. **Poora chakkar = 360°.** Protractor se naapte waqt sahi scale (0 wali) padho.
- **Types:**
  - Acute: 0° < x < 90°
  - Right: 90°
  - Obtuse: 90° < x < 180°
  - Straight: 180°
  - Reflex: 180° < x < 360°
  - Complete: 360°
- Har angle ka **reflex saathi = 360° − angle**.
- **Angle bisector** angle ko do barabar hisson mein baantta hai.
- **Ghadi:** har ghante ka gap 30°, minute sui 6° per minute, ghante ki sui 0.5° per minute.

## Practice Questions

1. In the figure below, name the vertex and the arms of the angle, and write the angle in two correct ways.

   ```
            P
           /
          /
         Q -------- R
   ```

2. Classify each angle as acute, right, obtuse, straight, reflex or complete:
   (a) 47°  (b) 90°  (c) 135°  (d) 180°  (e) 210°  (f) 360°  (g) 89.5°  (h) 179°

3. Find the reflex angle corresponding to each angle:
   (a) 40°  (b) 125°  (c) 90°

4. A ray BD bisects ∠ABC. If ∠ABC = 84°, find ∠ABD and ∠DBC.

5. Find the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand of a clock at:
   (a) 2:00  (b) 5:00  (c) 10:00  (d) 6:00
   Also classify each angle.

6. Find the angle between the hands of a clock at 3:30 and at 9:30.

7. State whether each statement is true or false. Give a reason.
   (a) An angle of 90° is an acute angle.
   (b) If the arms of an angle are made longer, the angle becomes bigger.
   (c) The reflex angle of 100° is 260°.
   (d) ∠ABC and ∠CBA are the same angle.
   (e) A straight angle is equal to two right angles.

8. Through how many degrees does the minute hand of a clock turn in (a) 15 minutes, (b) 40 minutes, (c) 1 hour?

### Answers

1. Vertex **Q**. Arms **QP** aur **QR**. Angle: **∠PQR** ya **∠RQP** (Q beech mein).

2. (a) 47° → **Acute**
   (b) 90° → **Right**
   (c) 135° → **Obtuse**
   (d) 180° → **Straight**
   (e) 210° → **Reflex**
   (f) 360° → **Complete**
   (g) 89.5° → **Acute** (90 se thoda sa kam, par kam hi hai)
   (h) 179° → **Obtuse** (180 se kam)

3. (a) 360° − 40° = **320°**
   (b) 360° − 125° = **235°**
   (c) 360° − 90° = **270°**

4. Bisector barabar baantta hai: ∠ABD = ∠DBC = 84° ÷ 2 = **42°**.
   Jaanch: 42° + 42° = 84° ✅

5. (a) 2:00 → 2 gaps × 30° = **60°**, Acute
   (b) 5:00 → 5 gaps × 30° = **150°**, Obtuse
   (c) 10:00 → chhota raasta 2 gaps (10 se 12) × 30° = **60°**, Acute
   (d) 6:00 → 6 gaps × 30° = **180°**, Straight

6. **3:30:** Minute sui 180°. Ghante ki sui 3 × 30° + 30 × 0.5° = 90° + 15° = 105°. Angle = 180° − 105° = **75°**.
   **9:30:** Minute sui 180°. Ghante ki sui 9 × 30° + 15° = 285°. Angle = 285° − 180° = **105°**.

7. (a) **False.** Acute angle 90° se **kam** hota hai. 90° right angle hai.
   (b) **False.** Angle sirf arms ke beech ka khulaapan hai, arms ki lambai se nahi badalta.
   (c) **True.** 360° − 100° = 260° ✅
   (d) **True.** Dono mein vertex B beech mein hai, aur arms wahi BA aur BC hain.
   (e) **True.** 2 × 90° = 180° ✅

8. Minute sui 1 minute mein 6° ghoomti hai.
   (a) 15 × 6° = **90°**
   (b) 40 × 6° = **240°**
   (c) 60 × 6° = **360°**
