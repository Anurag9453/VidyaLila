# Topic 4: Right Circular Cylinder

Topic 3 mein humne dibbe jaisi cheezon, yaani **cube aur cuboid**, ka hisaab lagaya tha, jaise Simran ka 20 cm × 10 cm × 5 cm wala gift box (700 cm² kaagaz, 500 toffiyan). Unki deewaron ka area (lateral surface area), poori sataha ka area (total surface area), aur andar kitna saaman aata hai (volume) nikalna seekha. Saath mein ye bhi dekha ki **1 litre = 1000 cm³** aur **1 m³ = 1000 litre** hota hai.

Par ghar mein har cheez dibbe jaisi nahi hoti. Paani ka gilaas, tel ka tin, gas cylinder, paani ki pipe, ye sab **gol** hain. Is topic mein hum inhi gol dibbon, yaani **cylinder**, ka hisaab lagana seekhenge. Achhi baat ye hai ki Topic 3 ka ek idea yahan bhi seedha kaam aayega: **volume = base ka area × height.**

---

## Tin ke label ki kahani

Aarav ki mummy ki dukaan par mattar ke **tin ke dabbe** bikte hain. Ek din label chhapne wala aadmi aaya aur bola, "Har tin par label chipkana hai. Batao, ek label kitna bada kaagaz lega?"

Aarav ne ek tin uthaya aur socha. Label tin ke **chaaron taraf lipta** hota hai, par upar aur neeche ke gol dhakkan par nahi hota.

Usne ek purane tin ka label dhyan se chhuda kar kholkar mez par bichha diya. Hairani ki baat: gol lipta hua label khulte hi ek seedha-saadha **rectangle** ban gaya!

- Rectangle ki **chaudai** = tin ki **height**.
- Rectangle ki **lambai** = tin ke chaaron taraf ek chakkar, yaani tin ke dhakkan ki **circumference**.

Ab label ka area nikalna aasan tha: lambai × chaudai.

Bas yahi is poore topic ka raaz hai: **cylinder ki gol deewar ko khol do, to wo ek rectangle ban jaati hai.**

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Right circular cylinder** | Aisi thos ya khokhli cheez jiske upar aur neeche do barabar circle hon, aur deewar seedhi khadi ho | Gilaas, tin, pipe ka tukda |
| **Base** | Neeche (aur upar) wala gol hissa | Tin ka dhakkan |
| **Radius (r)** | Base ke centre se kinaare tak ki doori | 7 cm |
| **Height (h)** | Neeche wale base se upar wale base tak ki seedhi doori | 10 cm |
| **Axis** | Dono bases ke centres ko jodne wali seedhi line | Tin ke beech se guzarti seekh |
| **Curved surface area (CSA)** | Sirf gol deewar ka area (label wala hissa) | 2πrh |
| **Total surface area (TSA)** | Gol deewar + upar ka circle + neeche ka circle | 2πr(r + h) |
| **Volume** | Cylinder ke andar kitni jagah hai | πr²h |
| **Hollow cylinder** | Andar se khaali cylinder, jaise pipe | Paani ki pipe |

## Part A: Circle ki do baatein yaad kar lo

Cylinder ke dono sire **circle** hote hain. Isliye circle ki do formulas chahiye. Circle ke saare terms [Circles chapter](../circles/circles.md) mein detail mein hain. Yahan sirf do formule:

| Cheez | Formula | Matlab |
|---|---|---|
| **Circumference** (gol kinaare ki lambai) | **2πr** | Circle ke chaaron taraf ek chakkar |
| **Area** (andar ki jagah) | **πr²** | Circle ke andar kitni jagah hai |

### π = 22/7 kyun lete hain?

π asal mein ek irrational number hai (3.14159…), jo kabhi khatam nahi hota. Sawaalon mein hum aam taur par **π = 22/7** lete hain. Iska ek pyaara fayda hai:

> Jab radius **7 ka guna** ho (7, 14, 3.5, 0.7, 21…), to 22/7 ka **7 katt jata hai** aur answer ekdum saaf number aata hai.

Jaise r = 7 cm ho to:
- Circumference = 2 × 22/7 × 7 = **44 cm**
- Area = 22/7 × 7 × 7 = **154 cm²**

Isiliye sawaal banane wale aksar radius 7, 14, 3.5 jaise rakhte hain. Jab sawaal mein π = 3.14 diya ho, tab wahi lena.

---

## Part B: Cylinder kya hota hai?

**Right circular cylinder** wo shape hai jiske:
1. **Do base** hain, dono barabar circle, ek upar ek neeche.
2. Dono bases ek doosre ke **theek upar** hain.
3. **Axis** (dono centres ko jodne wali line) base par **seedhi khadi** (perpendicular) hai. Isliye naam mein "right" hai.

```
          _________
        /           \
       |      •------|---- r (radius)
        \___________/
       |             |
       |             |
       |      |      |   h (height)
       |      | axis |
       |      |      |
        \___________/
```

Agar axis tirchhi ho (jaise jhuka hua tin), to wo "right" cylinder nahi rehta. Is topic mein hum sirf **right circular cylinder** ki baat karenge, aur aage use bas "cylinder" kahenge.

### Asli zindagi mein cylinder

- Paani ka gilaas, steel ka tumbler
- Tel ya mattar ka tin
- Gas cylinder, pani ki tanki (gol wali)
- Road roller ka belan
- Pipe, pencil (bina chhili), mombatti

## Part C: Curved Surface Area (CSA)

Aarav ke label wala experiment yaad karo. Gol deewar ko height ki seedh mein kaat kar khol do:

```
   Lipta hua label              Khula hua label (rectangle)

      _______                 +---------------------------+
     /       \                |                           |
    |  LABEL  |      ===>     |          LABEL            |  h
    |         |               |                           |
     \_______/                +---------------------------+
                                     2πr (ek poora chakkar)
```

- Rectangle ki lambai = base ki circumference = **2πr**
- Rectangle ki chaudai = cylinder ki height = **h**

Rectangle ka area = lambai × chaudai, isliye:

> **CSA = 2πrh**

Ise **lateral surface area** bhi kehte hain. Topic 3 mein cuboid ki chaar deewaron ka area bhi "lateral" kehlaya tha. Wahi idea hai, bas yahan deewar gol hai.

### Example 1: Tin ka label

Ek tin ka radius **3.5 cm** aur height **10 cm** hai. Label ke liye kitna kaagaz chahiye?

Label sirf gol deewar par hai, to CSA chahiye.

CSA = 2πrh = 2 × 22/7 × 3.5 × 10

- 22/7 × 3.5 = 11
- 2 × 11 × 10 = **220 cm²**

Jaanch: Circumference = 2 × 22/7 × 3.5 = 22 cm. Label ka rectangle 22 cm × 10 cm = 220 cm² ✅

---

## Part D: Total Surface Area (TSA)

Agar poora tin **bahar se paint** karna ho, to gol deewar ke saath **upar aur neeche ke dono circle** bhi paint honge.

TSA = CSA + 2 × (ek circle ka area)
= 2πrh + 2πr²

Dono mein **2πr** common hai (Factorisation chapter wala common factor!), to:

> **TSA = 2πr(r + h)**

### Example 2: r = 7 cm, h = 10 cm wala cylinder

**CSA** = 2 × 22/7 × 7 × 10 = 2 × 22 × 10 = **440 cm²**

**Ek circle ka area** = 22/7 × 7 × 7 = **154 cm²**

**TSA** = 440 + 2 × 154 = 440 + 308 = **748 cm²**

Formula se jaanch: 2πr(r + h) = 2 × 22/7 × 7 × (7 + 10) = 44 × 17 = **748 cm²** ✅

---

## Part E: Volume

Topic 3 mein cuboid ka volume aise samjha tha: farsh par 1 cm ke chhote cubes ki ek parat (l × b cubes), aur aisi h parten upar-upar. Yaani **base ka area × height.**

Cylinder ke saath bhi bilkul yahi hota hai. Socho ek-ek rupaye ke **sikkon ka dher**. Har sikka ek patla circle hai. Sikke ek ke upar ek rakhte jao, to ek cylinder ban jata hai.

- Har parat (sikka) ka area = base ka area = **πr²**
- Kitni oonchai tak rakhe = **h**

> **Volume = base ka area × height = πr²h**

### Example 3: Wahi r = 7 cm, h = 10 cm

Volume = 22/7 × 7 × 7 × 10 = 154 × 10 = **1540 cm³**

Litre mein: 1540 ÷ 1000 = **1.54 litre** (kyunki 1 L = 1000 cm³, Topic 3 se)

## Part F: Solved examples

### Example 4: Road roller kitni zameen dabata hai?

Ek road roller ke belan ka **diameter 1.4 m** aur **lambai 2 m** hai. **500 chakkar** mein wo kitni zameen samtal karega?

**Soch:** Ek chakkar mein belan ki **poori gol deewar** ek baar zameen ko chhooti hai. Yaani ek chakkar = ek CSA.

- r = 1.4 ÷ 2 = **0.7 m**, h (belan ki lambai) = **2 m**
- Ek chakkar = CSA = 2 × 22/7 × 0.7 × 2 = 2 × 2.2 × 2 = **8.8 m²**
- 500 chakkar = 8.8 × 500 = **4400 m²**

Dhyan do: yahan cylinder "leta hua" hai, isliye uski **lambai hi h** hai.

### Example 5: Gilaas mein kitna paani?

Ek steel ke gilaas ka andar ka radius **3.5 cm** aur height **14 cm** hai. Usme kitna paani aayega?

Volume = 22/7 × 3.5 × 3.5 × 14
- 22/7 × 14 = 44
- 44 × 3.5 × 3.5 = 44 × 12.25 = **539 cm³**

Litre mein: 539 ÷ 1000 = **0.539 litre** (lagbhag aadha litre).

### Example 6: Gol tanki ki capacity

Ek gol paani ki tanki ka **diameter 2.8 m** aur **height 5 m** hai. Usme kitne litre paani aayega?

- r = 1.4 m
- Volume = 22/7 × 1.4 × 1.4 × 5 = 22 × 0.2 × 1.4 × 5 = **30.8 m³**
- Litre mein: 30.8 × 1000 = **30,800 litre**

(Jaanch: 22/7 × 1.4 = 4.4, phir 4.4 × 1.4 = 6.16, phir 6.16 × 5 = 30.8 ✅)

### Example 7: Khambhe par paint ka kharcha

Ek gol khambhe (pillar) ka **diameter 70 cm** aur **height 4 m** hai. Uski gol sataha par **₹25 per m²** ki dar se paint karwane ka kharcha kitna hoga?

**Soch:** Khambhe ka upar aur neeche ka hissa paint nahi hota (neeche zameen, upar chhat). Sirf **CSA**.

- Pehle units ek jaise karo: r = 70 ÷ 2 = 35 cm = **0.35 m**
- CSA = 2 × 22/7 × 0.35 × 4 = 2 × 1.1 × 4 = **8.8 m²**
- Kharcha = 8.8 × 25 = **₹220**

### Example 8: Khula bartan vs band bartan

Ek bartan ka radius **7 cm** aur height **20 cm** hai. Use banane mein kitni metal sheet lagegi, agar:
(a) bartan **upar se khula** ho
(b) bartan **dono taraf se band** ho

- CSA = 2 × 22/7 × 7 × 20 = **880 cm²**
- Ek circle = 22/7 × 7 × 7 = **154 cm²**

(a) Khula bartan: sirf **neeche ka ek** circle.
Sheet = 880 + 154 = **1034 cm²**

(b) Band bartan: **dono** circle.
Sheet = 880 + 2 × 154 = **1188 cm²**

Jaanch (b): 2πr(r + h) = 44 × 27 = 1188 ✅

### Example 9: Volume se height nikalna

Ek cylinder ka volume **3080 cm³** aur radius **7 cm** hai. Height kitni hai?

- Base ka area = 22/7 × 7 × 7 = **154 cm²**
- Volume = base area × h, to 3080 = 154 × h
- h = 3080 ÷ 154 = **20 cm**

Jaanch: 154 × 20 = 3080 ✅

## Part G: Hollow cylinder (pipe) ki ek jhalak

Paani ki pipe andar se **khokhli** hoti hai. Uske do radius hote hain:
- **Bahar ka radius R**
- **Andar ka radius r**

```
        bahar ka circle (R)
          _________
        /  _______  \
       |  /       \  |
       | |  khaali | |   <- beech ka metal = do circles ke beech ka hissa
       |  \_______/  |
        \___________/
        andar ka circle (r)
```

Pipe mein **kitna metal** laga, ye nikalne ke liye: poore bade cylinder ka volume minus andar ke khaali cylinder ka volume.

> **Metal ka volume = πR²h − πr²h = π(R² − r²)h**

### Example 10

Ek pipe **1 m (100 cm)** lambi hai. Bahar ka radius **3.5 cm** aur andar ka radius **2.8 cm** hai. Usme kitna metal hai?

- R² − r² = 12.25 − 7.84 = **4.41**
- π(R² − r²) = 22/7 × 4.41 = **13.86**
- Volume = 13.86 × 100 = **1386 cm³**

Hollow cylinder ki baaki baatein (andar aur bahar dono ka surface area) abhi chhod dete hain. Abhi bas itna yaad rakho: **bada minus chhota.**

## Saare formule ek jagah

| Kya chahiye | Formula | Kab use karein |
|---|---|---|
| Curved surface area | **2πrh** | Label, paint (sirf deewar), road roller ka ek chakkar |
| Total surface area (band) | **2πr(r + h)** | Dono taraf se band dibba, poora bahar se paint |
| Khula bartan (ek base) | **2πrh + πr²** | Gilaas, balti, upar se khuli tanki |
| Volume | **πr²h** | Kitna paani/tel/saaman aayega |
| Hollow cylinder ka metal | **π(R² − r²)h** | Pipe banane mein laga metal |

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Diameter ko radius samajh lena**
Sawaal mein "diameter 14 cm" likha ho to **r = 7 cm** hai, 14 nahi. Pehla kadam hamesha: diameter mila to aadha karo.

**Galti 2: CSA aur TSA mein confusion**
Label lagana, khambha paint karna, roller ke chakkar: **CSA**. Poora band dabba bahar se dhakna: **TSA**. Pehle socho "kaunse hisse ki baat ho rahi hai", phir formula chuno.

**Galti 3: Khule bartan mein do circle jod dena**
Gilaas ya balti upar se khule hote hain. Inme sirf **ek** base hota hai. 2πr(r + h) lagaoge to ek extra dhakkan gin loge.

**Galti 4: Units mila dena**
Radius cm mein aur height m mein ho, to pehle **dono ek unit mein** lao. Example 7 mein 70 cm ko 0.35 m (radius) banaya tha.

**Galti 5: cm³ ko seedha litre likh dena**
1540 cm³ ka matlab 1540 litre nahi, **1.54 litre** hai. Yaad rakho: 1000 cm³ = 1 litre, aur 1 m³ = 1000 litre.

**Galti 6: Area ko cm³ mein ya volume ko cm² mein likhna**
Area (CSA, TSA) ki unit **cm²** hoti hai. Volume ki unit **cm³** hoti hai. Unit se hi pata chal jata hai ki tum kya nikal rahe ho.

**Galti 7: Road roller mein lambai ko radius samajhna**
Leta hua cylinder ho to uski **lambai hi h** hai. Belan ka radius alag se diya hota hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Right circular cylinder:** do barabar circular base, aur axis base par seedhi khadi.
- Gol deewar kholne par **rectangle** banta hai: lambai 2πr, chaudai h.
- **CSA = 2πrh** (sirf gol deewar)
- **TSA = 2πr(r + h)** (deewar + dono dhakkan)
- **Khula bartan** = 2πrh + πr² (sirf ek base)
- **Volume = πr²h** (base ka area × height, cuboid jaisa hi idea)
- **Hollow cylinder ka metal** = π(R² − r²)h
- **π = 22/7** lene par radius 7 ka guna ho to numbers saaf aate hain.
- Units ka dhyan: 1000 cm³ = 1 L, 1 m³ = 1000 L.

## Practice Questions

1. A cylinder has radius 14 cm and height 20 cm. Find its (a) curved surface area, (b) total surface area, (c) volume. (Take π = 22/7.)

2. A cylindrical pillar has a diameter of 56 cm and a height of 3.5 m. Find the cost of painting its curved surface at ₹12.50 per m².

3. The roller of a road roller has a diameter of 84 cm and a length of 120 cm. Find the area of ground it levels in 500 complete revolutions. Give your answer in m².

4. A closed cylindrical tank of radius 7 m and height 3 m is made from a metal sheet. How much sheet is required?

5. A cylindrical tank has a diameter of 1.4 m and a height of 2 m. How many litres of water can it hold?

6. The volume of a cylindrical jar is 2.31 litres and its radius is 7 cm. Find its height.

7. A metal pipe is 70 cm long. Its outer radius is 4 cm and its inner radius is 3 cm. Find the volume of metal used.

8. The curved surface area of a cylinder is 4400 cm² and its height is 50 cm. Find its radius.

9. State whether each statement is true or false. Give a reason.
   (a) If the radius of a cylinder is doubled (height same), its volume also doubles.
   (b) The volume of a cylinder is equal to the area of its base multiplied by its height.
   (c) To find the sheet needed for an open glass, we use 2πr(r + h).

### Answers

1. r = 14, h = 20.
   (a) CSA = 2 × 22/7 × 14 × 20 = 88 × 20 = **1760 cm²**
   (b) TSA = 2 × 22/7 × 14 × (14 + 20) = 88 × 34 = **2992 cm²**
   (c) Volume = 22/7 × 14 × 14 × 20 = 616 × 20 = **12320 cm³**

2. r = 56 ÷ 2 = 28 cm = 0.28 m, h = 3.5 m.
   CSA = 2 × 22/7 × 0.28 × 3.5 = 2 × 22 × 0.04 × 3.5 = **6.16 m²**
   Kharcha = 6.16 × 12.50 = **₹77**

3. r = 42 cm, h = 120 cm.
   Ek chakkar = CSA = 2 × 22/7 × 42 × 120 = 2 × 22 × 6 × 120 = 31680 cm² = **3.168 m²**
   500 chakkar = 3.168 × 500 = **1584 m²**

4. Band tanki, to TSA.
   TSA = 2 × 22/7 × 7 × (7 + 3) = 44 × 10 = **440 m²**

5. r = 0.7 m, h = 2 m.
   Volume = 22/7 × 0.7 × 0.7 × 2 = 2.2 × 0.7 × 2 = **3.08 m³**
   Litre = 3.08 × 1000 = **3080 litre**

6. 2.31 litre = 2310 cm³. Base area = 22/7 × 7 × 7 = 154 cm².
   h = 2310 ÷ 154 = **15 cm**
   Jaanch: 154 × 15 = 2310 ✅

7. Metal = π(R² − r²)h = 22/7 × (16 − 9) × 70 = 22/7 × 7 × 70 = 22 × 70 = **1540 cm³**

8. 2πrh = 4400, to 2 × 22/7 × r × 50 = 4400.
   (2200/7) × r = 4400, to r = 4400 × 7/2200 = **14 cm**
   Jaanch: 2 × 22/7 × 14 × 50 = 88 × 50 = 4400 ✅

9. (a) **False** ❌. Volume = πr²h mein r ka **square** hai. Radius double karo to volume **4 guna** ho jata hai.
   (b) **True** ✅. Volume = πr² × h = base ka area × height.
   (c) **False** ❌. Khule gilaas mein sirf ek base hota hai, to sheet = 2πrh + πr². 2πr(r + h) dono dhakkan gin leta hai.
