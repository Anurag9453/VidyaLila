# Topic 6: Sphere aur Hemisphere

Pichle topic mein Golu ki birthday party par humne cone dekha: caps ke kaagaz ke liye slant height aur curved surface area (πrl), volume, aur ice-cream wale bhaiya ka khel ki ek hi base aur height wale teen cone milkar ek cylinder bhar dete hain. Usse pehle cylinder, cube aur cuboid aa chuke hain. Ab hamare paas sirf ek shape bachi hai, aur shayad sabse sundar bhi: **gol, bilkul gol, har taraf se ek jaisi.** Isi ko **sphere** kehte hain. Aur agar use beech se kaat do, to milta hai **hemisphere**.

## Mochi chacha aur football ki kahani

Wahi Golu, jiski birthday party Topic 5 mein dekhi thi! Is baar uski football phat gayi. Wo use lekar gali ke kone wale **mochi chacha** ke paas gaya.

Chacha ne football ko ghuma kar dekha aur bole, "Beta, poora naya chamda (leather) chadhana padega. Pehle naapna padega ki **kitna chamda** lagega."

Golu ne poocha, "Chacha, aur andar kitni hawa jaayegi?"

Chacha hans pade. "Wo alag sawaal hai beta. Chamda to **upar ki sataha** (surface) ka hisaab hai, aur hawa **andar ki jagah** (volume) ka."

Ghar aakar Golu ne dekha ki mummy dal ek **katori** mein rakh rahi hain. Katori bhi gol thi, par sirf aadhi. "Ye to aadhi football jaisi hai!" usne socha.

Golu ke teen sawaal hi is topic ki poori kahani hain:
1. Gol cheez ke **upar** kitna chamda lagta hai? (Surface area)
2. Gol cheez ke **andar** kitni jagah hoti hai? (Volume)
3. Aadhi gol cheez (katori) ka kya hisaab hai? (Hemisphere)

## Part A: Sphere kya hai?

[Circles chapter](../circles/circles.md) mein humne circle dekha tha: ek **plane (sapaat kaagaz)** par wo saare points jo centre se barabar doori par hon.

Ab socho, ye niyam **kaagaz se bahar nikal kar** har disha mein lag jaye: upar, neeche, aage, peeche, daayein, baayein. Centre se barabar doori wale saare points milkar ek **gol gend** bana dete hain. Yahi hai **sphere**.

```
   Circle (2D, kaagaz par)          Sphere (3D, hawa mein)

          * * *                            .-"""-.
       *         *                       /         \
      *     O-----*  r                  |     O-----|  r
       *         *                       \         /
          * * *                            '-...-'
     sirf ek line jaisa ghera         poori gol gend (andar se bhari)
```

Fark yaad rakho:
- **Circle** ek sapaat shape hai. Uska **area** hota hai, par **volume nahi**.
- **Sphere** ek thos (3D) shape hai. Uska **surface area** bhi hota hai aur **volume** bhi.

Aur ek baat: sphere ka **koi kona (edge) nahi** hota aur **koi sapaat face nahi** hota. Isiliye football kisi bhi taraf ludhak jaati hai.

## Part B: Sphere ka Surface Area = 4πr²

### Santra (orange) wala experiment

Ye experiment ghar par karke dekho, bahut mazedaar hai:

1. Ek gol santra lo aur beech se kaat kar uska **radius** naap lo (maan lo r).
2. Kaagaz par **usi radius ke 4 circles** bana lo.
3. Ab santre ka chhilka utaaro, aur chhilke ke chhote chhote tukde in circles mein bharo.
4. Dekhoge ki chhilka **lagbhag theek 4 circles** bhar deta hai.

```
   ( O )  ( O )  ( O )  ( O )      <- 4 circles, har ek ka radius r
     |      |      |      |
   chhilke ke tukdon se bhare hue
```

Ek circle ka area πr² hota hai. Chaar circles ka area = 4 × πr². Isliye:

> **Sphere ka Surface Area = 4πr²**

(Iska pakka proof Class 9 se aage ki maths mein aata hai. Abhi ke liye santra wala experiment hi hamara saboot hai.)

### Example 1: r = 7 cm wale sphere ka surface area

SA = 4πr²
= 4 × (22/7) × 7 × 7
= 4 × 22 × 7
= **616 cm²**

### Example 2: Golu ki football par kitna chamda?

Football ka **diameter 21 cm** hai. Pehle radius nikalo:
r = 21/2 = 10.5 cm

SA = 4 × (22/7) × (21/2) × (21/2)
= 4 × (22/7) × (441/4)
= (22/7) × 441
= 22 × 63
= **1386 cm²**

To mochi chacha ko kam se kam **1386 cm²** chamda chahiye (silai aur kinare ke liye thoda extra alag se).

**Jaanch:** 4 × 3.14 × 10.5 × 10.5 = 4 × 3.14 × 110.25 ≈ 1384.7 cm². π = 22/7 lene par 1386 aaya. Dono lagbhag barabar hain ✅

### Example 3: Surface area se radius nikalna

Ek gend ka surface area **154 cm²** hai. Radius kitna hai?

4 × (22/7) × r² = 154
(88/7) × r² = 154
r² = 154 × 7 / 88 = 1078 / 88 = 12.25
r = **3.5 cm**

**Jaanch:** 4 × (22/7) × 3.5 × 3.5 = 4 × 22 × 0.5 × 3.5 = 154 ✅

## Part C: Sphere ka Volume = (4/3)πr³

### Archimedes ki pasandeeda khoj

Bahut pehle, Greece mein **Archimedes** naam ke ek mahaan ganitagya the. Unhe ek khoj itni pasand thi ki unhone kaha tha, "Meri kabr par yahi chitra banana."

Wo khoj ye thi. Ek sphere lo jiska radius r hai. Ab ek aisa cylinder lo jisme ye sphere **bilkul fit** baithe:
- Cylinder ka radius = r
- Cylinder ki height = 2r (kyunki sphere ki poori unchai 2r hai)

```
     ___________
    |  .-"""-.  |
    | /       \ |
    ||    O    ||   height = 2r
    | \       / |
    |  '-...-'  |
    |___________|
       radius r
```

Topic 4 se cylinder ka volume = πr² × h = πr² × 2r = **2πr³**.

Archimedes ne dikhaya ki sphere is cylinder ka theek **2/3 hissa** bharta hai:

Sphere ka volume = (2/3) × 2πr³ = **(4/3)πr³**

> **Sphere ka Volume = (4/3)πr³**

(Iska pakka proof bhi Class 9 se aage aata hai. Ek tareeka ye hai ki sphere ko paani se bhare cylinder mein dubo kar dekho kitna paani bahar girta hai.)

### Ek aur sundar baat: 1 : 2 : 3

Topic 5 mein dekha tha ki cone ka volume = (1/3)πr²h. Agar cone ka radius bhi r ho aur height 2r ho, to:

| Shape (radius r, height 2r) | Volume |
|---|---|
| Cone | (1/3)πr² × 2r = (2/3)πr³ |
| Sphere | (4/3)πr³ |
| Cylinder | 2πr³ = (6/3)πr³ |

To **cone : sphere : cylinder = 2 : 4 : 6 = 1 : 2 : 3**. Ek cone aur ek sphere milkar theek ek cylinder bhar dete hain!

Aur ek surprise: is cylinder ki mudi hui sataha (CSA) = 2πr × 2r = **4πr²**, jo **sphere ke surface area ke barabar** hai. Archimedes ko ye bhi pata tha.

### Example 4: r = 7 cm wale sphere ka volume

V = (4/3) × (22/7) × 7 × 7 × 7
= (4/3) × 22 × 49
= (4 × 1078) / 3
= 4312 / 3
= **1437⅓ cm³** (lagbhag 1437.33 cm³)

### Example 5: Football mein kitni hawa?

r = 21/2 cm

V = (4/3) × (22/7) × (21/2)³
= (4/3) × (22/7) × (9261/8)

Ab katao: 9261 ÷ 7 = 1323, aur 1323 ÷ 3 = 441.
= (4 × 22 × 441) / 8
= (22 × 441) / 2
= 11 × 441
= **4851 cm³**

1000 cm³ = 1 litre, to football mein lagbhag **4.85 litre** hawa hai.

### Example 6: Volume se radius nikalna

Ek thos gend ka volume **38808 cm³** hai. Radius kitna hai?

(4/3) × (22/7) × r³ = 38808
(88/21) × r³ = 38808
r³ = 38808 × 21 / 88
38808 ÷ 88 = 441, to r³ = 441 × 21 = 9261
r = ∛9261 = **21 cm** (kyunki 21 × 21 × 21 = 9261)

**Jaanch:** (4/3) × (22/7) × 9261 = 4 × 22 × 441 = 38808 ✅

## Part D: Radius badalne par kya hota hai?

Topic 3 mein cube ki side double karke dekha tha ki area 4 guna aur volume 8 guna ho jata hai. Sphere ke saath bhi bilkul wahi hota hai:

- Surface area mein r **do baar** (r²) hai.
- Volume mein r **teen baar** (r³) hai.

To agar radius **2 guna** ho jaye:
- Surface area **2 × 2 = 4 guna**
- Volume **2 × 2 × 2 = 8 guna**

### Example 7: r = 7 se r = 14

| | r = 7 cm | r = 14 cm | Kitna guna |
|---|---|---|---|
| Surface area | 616 cm² | 4 × (22/7) × 196 = 2464 cm² | 2464 ÷ 616 = **4** |
| Volume | 4312/3 cm³ | (4/3) × (22/7) × 2744 = 34496/3 cm³ | 34496 ÷ 4312 = **8** |

### Example 8: Gubbara (balloon) phulana

Ek gubbare ka radius **7 cm** se badhkar **14 cm** ho gaya. Pehle aur baad ke surface area ka anupaat (ratio)?

Ratio = 4π(7)² : 4π(14)² = 49 : 196 = **1 : 4**

Dekho, 4π dono taraf kat gaya. Sirf radius ka square bacha: (7/14)² = 1/4.

## Part E: Hemisphere (aadha sphere)

Golu ki katori yaad hai? Sphere ko **centre se hokar** bilkul do barabar hisson mein kaato, to har hissa ek **hemisphere** hai.

```
        .-"""-.
      /         \       <- mudi hui sataha (curved)
     |___________|      <- sapaat gol face (flat circle), radius r
```

Ab dhyan se socho, hemisphere ki **do tarah ki sataha** hoti hai:
1. **Mudi hui sataha (curved):** sphere ki sataha ka aadha = (1/2) × 4πr² = **2πr²**
2. **Sapaat gol face:** ek circle, jiska area = **πr²**

### Hemisphere ke formulas

| Kya | Formula | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| Curved Surface Area (CSA) | **2πr²** | Sphere ki sataha ka aadha |
| Total Surface Area (TSA) | **3πr²** | 2πr² (curved) + πr² (flat circle) |
| Volume | **(2/3)πr³** | Sphere ke volume ka aadha |

### Katori (khuli) vs thos aadhi gend

- **Khuli katori ya gumbad (dome):** Upar ka hissa khula hai, wahan koi sataha nahi. To sirf **CSA = 2πr²** ka hisaab lagta hai.
- **Thos (solid) hemisphere:** Jaise ek gend ko kaat kar uska aadha. Isme gol sataha bhi hai aur neeche ka sapaat gol face bhi. To **TSA = 3πr²**.

### Example 9: Thos hemisphere, r = 7 cm

- CSA = 2 × (22/7) × 49 = 2 × 154 = **308 cm²**
- TSA = 3 × (22/7) × 49 = 3 × 154 = **462 cm²**
- Volume = (2/3) × (22/7) × 343 = (2/3) × 1078 = 2156/3 = **718⅔ cm³**

**Jaanch:** Volume, Example 4 ke sphere (4312/3) ka aadha hona chahiye: 4312/3 ÷ 2 = 2156/3 ✅

### Example 10: Katori mein kitni dal aayegi?

Ek hemispherical katori ka radius **10.5 cm** hai. Usme kitne litre dal aa sakti hai?

V = (2/3) × (22/7) × (21/2)³
= (2/3) × (22/7) × (9261/8)
9261 ÷ 21 = 441, to
= (2 × 22 × 441) / 8
= 19404 / 8
= **2425.5 cm³**

1000 cm³ = 1 litre, to katori mein **2.4255 litre** (lagbhag 2.43 litre) dal aayegi.

**Jaanch:** Example 5 ki football (r = 10.5 cm) ka volume 4851 cm³ tha. Uska aadha = 2425.5 ✅

### Example 11: Katori par kalai (tin-plating) ka kharcha

Ek peetal ki hemispherical katori ka **andar ka diameter 10.5 cm** hai. Andar ki sataha par kalai karwani hai, rate **₹16 per 100 cm²**. Kharcha kitna?

Radius = 10.5 / 2 = 5.25 cm = 21/4 cm

Katori khuli hai, to sirf CSA:
CSA = 2 × (22/7) × (21/4) × (21/4)
= 2 × (22/7) × (441/16)
= (44 × 63) / 16
= 2772 / 16
= **173.25 cm²**

Kharcha = 173.25 × (16/100) = **₹27.72**

## Part F: Pighla kar naya roop (volume wahi rehta hai)

Jab koi dhaatu (metal) ki cheez pighla kar doosri shape mein dhaali jaati hai, to **dhaatu utni hi rehti hai**. Matlab **volume nahi badalta**, bas shape badalti hai.

### Example 12: Gend se cylinder

Ek thos dhaatu ki gend (radius **3 cm**) ko pighla kar ek cylinder banaya gaya jiska radius **2 cm** hai. Cylinder ki height kitni hogi?

Gend ka volume = (4/3)π × 3³ = (4/3)π × 27 = **36π cm³**

Cylinder ka volume = π × 2² × h = **4πh**

Dono barabar:
4πh = 36π
h = **9 cm**

Dekho, π dono taraf kat gaya, to 22/7 lagane ki zaroorat hi nahi padi.

### Example 13: Gend se taar (wire)

Wahi gend (36π cm³) ko ek patli taar mein khincha gaya jiska radius **0.1 cm** hai. Taar kitni lambi banegi?

Taar ek bahut lamba patla cylinder hi hai:
π × (0.1)² × h = 36π
0.01 × h = 36
h = **3600 cm = 36 m**

Ek chhoti si gend se 36 metre lambi taar!

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Surface area aur volume ke formula mix karna**
Surface area = 4πr² (r ka **square**, unit cm²). Volume = (4/3)πr³ (r ka **cube**, unit cm³). Trick: **sataha ke liye 2 power, jagah ke liye 3 power.**

**Galti 2: Diameter ko radius samajh lena**
"Football ka diameter 21 cm" mein r = 21 nahi, **r = 10.5** hai. Agar r = 21 le liya to surface area 4 guna aur volume 8 guna galat aayega.

**Galti 3: Thos hemisphere ki TSA mein sapaat face bhool jana**
Thos aadhi gend ki TSA = 2πr² + πr² = **3πr²**, sirf 2πr² nahi. Par **khuli katori** ke liye sirf 2πr² hi lena hai. Sawaal padh kar dekho ki cheez khuli hai ya thos.

**Galti 4: Hemisphere ki CSA ko πr² samajhna**
Mudi hui sataha sphere ka aadha hai: (1/2) × 4πr² = **2πr²**. πr² to sirf neeche ka sapaat circle hai.

**Galti 5: Units galat likhna**
Area ki unit **cm²** ya **m²**, volume ki **cm³** ya **m³**. Litre mein badalna ho to **1000 cm³ = 1 litre**.

**Galti 6: Radius 2 guna hone par volume bhi 2 guna samajhna**
Radius 2 guna → surface area **4 guna**, volume **8 guna**. Volume mein r teen baar hai.

**Galti 7: Pighlane wale sawaal mein surface area barabar karna**
Pighla kar banane par **volume** barabar hota hai, surface area nahi.

## Quick Recap

- **Sphere:** har point centre se barabar doori (r) par. 3D shape, koi kona ya sapaat face nahi.
- **Sphere ka surface area = 4πr²** (santre ka chhilka 4 circles bharta hai).
- **Sphere ka volume = (4/3)πr³** (sphere apne fit hone wale cylinder ka 2/3 bharta hai).
- **Cone : Sphere : Cylinder = 1 : 2 : 3** (radius r, height 2r wale).
- **Hemisphere:** CSA = 2πr², TSA (thos) = 3πr², Volume = (2/3)πr³.
- **Khuli katori** ke liye sirf CSA, **thos aadhi gend** ke liye TSA.
- Radius 2 guna → surface area 4 guna, volume 8 guna.
- Pighla kar naya roop dene par **volume wahi rehta hai**.
- Diameter diya ho to pehle **radius nikalo**.

## Practice Questions

Use π = 22/7 unless stated otherwise.

1. Find the surface area and the volume of a sphere of radius 3.5 cm.

2. Find the surface area of a sphere of diameter 28 cm.

3. A hemispherical bowl has radius 3.5 cm. How much water (in mL) can it hold?

4. Find the total surface area of a solid hemisphere of radius 21 cm.

5. The radius of a sphere is halved. What happens to (a) its surface area, (b) its volume?

6. The surface area of a sphere is 616 cm². Find its radius and its volume.

7. A solid metal sphere of radius 6 cm is melted and recast into a cylinder of radius 4 cm. Find the height of the cylinder.

8. The inside of a hemispherical dome of radius 7 m is to be painted at ₹20 per m². Find the cost.

9. State whether each statement is true or false.
   (a) The volume of a hemisphere is half the volume of a sphere of the same radius.
   (b) The total surface area of a solid hemisphere is 2πr².
   (c) If the radius of a sphere is doubled, its volume is doubled.
   (d) A sphere has no edges and no flat faces.

### Answers

1. r = 3.5 = 7/2 cm.
   SA = 4 × (22/7) × (7/2)² = 4 × (22/7) × (49/4) = 22 × 7 = **154 cm²**
   V = (4/3) × (22/7) × (343/8) = (4 × 22 × 49) / 24 = 4312/24 = **539/3 cm³ ≈ 179.67 cm³**

2. d = 28, to r = 14 cm.
   SA = 4 × (22/7) × 196 = 4 × 22 × 28 = **2464 cm²**

3. V = (2/3) × (22/7) × (343/8) = (2 × 22 × 49) / 24 = 2156/24 = **539/6 cm³ ≈ 89.83 cm³**
   1 cm³ = 1 mL, to katori mein lagbhag **89.83 mL** paani aayega.

4. Thos hemisphere, to TSA = 3πr² = 3 × (22/7) × 441 = 3 × 1386 = **4158 cm²**

5. Radius aadha, yaani r ki jagah r/2.
   (a) Surface area mein r², to (1/2)² = 1/4. Surface area **chauthai (1/4)** reh jayega.
   (b) Volume mein r³, to (1/2)³ = 1/8. Volume **1/8** reh jayega.

6. 4 × (22/7) × r² = 616 → r² = 616 × 7 / 88 = 49 → **r = 7 cm**
   V = (4/3) × (22/7) × 343 = **4312/3 cm³ = 1437⅓ cm³**

7. Gend ka volume = (4/3)π × 6³ = (4/3)π × 216 = 288π cm³
   Cylinder: π × 4² × h = 16πh
   16πh = 288π → **h = 18 cm**

8. Dome andar se khula hai, to sirf CSA:
   CSA = 2 × (22/7) × 49 = 308 m²
   Kharcha = 308 × 20 = **₹6160**

9. (a) **True** ✅. (2/3)πr³ = (1/2) × (4/3)πr³.
   (b) **False** ❌. Thos hemisphere ki TSA = 2πr² + πr² = 3πr².
   (c) **False** ❌. Volume 2 × 2 × 2 = **8 guna** hota hai.
   (d) **True** ✅. Sphere poori tarah gol hai.
