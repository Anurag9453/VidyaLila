# Topic 8: Uniform Cross-section wale Solids (Prism)

Topic 7 mein humne flat gol shapes ka hisaab seekha: Tara ke gaon ke kuen ka chabootra (ring, 192.5 m² tiles aur 66 m railing), pahiye ke chakkar, aur sprinkler se bheega lawn (sector). Usse pehle Topic 3 aur 4 mein do aise solids dekhe the jinka volume ek jaisi soch se nikla tha: [cuboid](mensuration-part3.md) mein **l × b × h** aur [cylinder](mensuration-part4.md) mein **πr² × h**. Kya ye sirf ittefaq tha? Nahi! Is aakhri topic mein hum dekhenge ki in dono ke peeche ek hi bada idea hai, aur wahi idea har us solid par chalta hai jise "lambai mein kaato to har tukda ek jaisa" mile.

---

## Tara ki tikoni chocolate

Wahi Tara, jiske kuen wale chabootre ka hisaab Topic 7 mein lagaya tha! Is baar uske mama Switzerland se ek lambi si chocolate laaye. Uska aakaar ajeeb tha: upar se dekho to lamba, par saamne se dekho to **tikona (triangle)**.

Tara ne use chaaku se kaatna shuru kiya. Pehla tukda: ek chhota triangle. Doosra tukda: **bilkul waisa hi** triangle. Teesra, chautha, paanchva: sab ek jaise.

Tara ne mummy se poocha, "Ye chocolate kitni hai? Matlab andar kitni chocolate bhari hai?"

Mummy ne kaha, "Ek tukde ka area naap lo, aur dekho chocolate kitni lambi hai. Bas, dono ko guna kar do."

Tara ne naapa:
- Har tukde ka triangle: sides 3 cm, 4 cm aur 5 cm (ek right triangle). Area = ½ × 3 × 4 = **6 cm²**
- Chocolate ki lambai: **20 cm**

Volume = 6 × 20 = **120 cm³**

Tara ko yaad aaya, "Arre, cuboid mein bhi to yahi kiya tha! Neeche ka area (l × b) guna unchai (h)." Bilkul sahi. Jab bhi kisi solid ko lambai ke saath kaatne par **har tukda ek jaisa** nikle, uska volume nikalne ka tarika ek hi hai.

## Part A: Uniform cross-section kya hai?

Ek solid ko **ek hi disha mein** patle-patle tukdon mein kaato, har baar lambai ke seedhe (90° par).

- Agar **har tukda bilkul ek jaisa** nikle (shape aur size dono), to solid ka **uniform cross-section** hai.
- Agar tukde **chhote-bade** hote jayein, to cross-section uniform nahi hai.

### Uniform cross-section wale solids

| Solid | Har tukda kaisa? |
|---|---|
| Eent (cuboid) | Rectangle |
| Bina chheeli hui pencil | Hexagon (chhe kone wala) |
| Steel ki pipe ya [cylinder](mensuration-part4.md) | Circle (ya ring) |
| Tara ki chocolate | Triangle |
| Double roti (bread) ka loaf | Lagbhag ek jaisa slice |
| Ghar ke bahar ki pakki naali | Rectangle ya trapezium |

### Uniform cross-section NAHI hai

| Solid | Kyun nahi? |
|---|---|
| [Cone](mensuration-part5.md) | Upar jaate-jaate circle chhota hota jata hai, aur nok par khatam |
| [Sphere](mensuration-part6.md) | Beech mein sabse bada circle, kinaron par chhota |
| Pyramid | Upar jaate-jaate square chhota hota jata hai |

```
 Uniform (har tukda same)          Uniform nahi (tukde badalte hain)

   ______________                         /\
  /|            /|                       /  \
 / |           / |                      /    \
/__|__________/  |                     /      \
|  |          |  |                    /________\
|  |__________|__|                      cone/pyramid
| /           | /
|/____________|/
   cuboid / prism
```

## Part B: Volume = Cross-section ka area × Lambai

> **Volume = (Cross-section ka area) × (Lambai)**

Ise aise socho: ek tukde ka area A hai. Ab aise bahut saare patle tukde ek ke peeche ek rakhte jao, kul lambai L tak. Jitni lambai, utna volume.

### Ye formula pehle se aapke paas tha!

| Solid | Cross-section | Area (A) | Lambai | Volume = A × L |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cuboid ([Topic 3](mensuration-part3.md)) | Rectangle l × b | lb | h | **lbh** |
| Cube | Square a × a | a² | a | **a³** |
| Cylinder ([Topic 4](mensuration-part4.md)) | Circle radius r | πr² | h | **πr²h** |

Topic 3 mein humne cuboid ko 1 cm ke chhote cubes ki parton (layers) se bhara tha: ek parat mein l × b cubes, aur h parten. Wahi baat yahan hai: **ek parat ka area × kitni parten**.

Isliye cone aur sphere ke formulas alag the (⅓ aur 4/3 wale), kyunki unke tukde ek jaise nahi hote.

## Part C: Surface Area

### Lateral Surface Area (dono sire chhod kar)

Tara ki chocolate ke wrapper ka **lamba hissa** socho (dono tikone sire chhod kar). Wo teen lambi patti (rectangles) se bana hai:
- 3 cm × 20 cm
- 4 cm × 20 cm
- 5 cm × 20 cm

Jodo: (3 + 4 + 5) × 20 = 12 × 20 = **240 cm²**

Dhyan do, 3 + 4 + 5 = 12 cross-section ka **perimeter** hai. To:

> **Lateral Surface Area = (Cross-section ka perimeter) × (Lambai)**

### Total Surface Area (dono sire bhi)

Dono siron par ek-ek triangle aur hai, har ek 6 cm².

> **Total Surface Area = LSA + 2 × (Cross-section ka area)**

Tara ki chocolate: TSA = 240 + 2 × 6 = **252 cm²**

### Purane formulas se jaanch

| Solid | Perimeter × Lambai | Hamara purana LSA |
|---|---|---|
| Cuboid | 2(l + b) × h | 2h(l + b) |
| Cylinder | 2πr × h | 2πrh |

Jaanch: Cuboid ka TSA = 2h(l + b) + 2lb = 2(lb + bh + hl) ✅. Bilkul Topic 3 wala formula.

## Part D: Triangular Prism

Jab cross-section triangle ho, to triangle ka area kisi bhi tarike se nikal sakte ho: ½ × base × height, ya agar teeno sides pata hon to [Topic 1 ka Heron's formula](mensuration.md).

### Example 1: Tara ki chocolate (poora hisaab)

Cross-section: 3, 4, 5 cm wala right triangle. Lambai: 20 cm.

- Cross-section area = ½ × 3 × 4 = 6 cm²
- Volume = 6 × 20 = **120 cm³**
- Perimeter = 3 + 4 + 5 = 12 cm
- LSA = 12 × 20 = **240 cm²**
- TSA = 240 + 2 × 6 = **252 cm²**

Jaanch: Heron se bhi area: s = 6, √(6 × 3 × 2 × 1) = √36 = 6 ✅

### Example 2: Heron wala prism

Ek lakdi ke gutke (block) ka cross-section ek triangle hai jiski sides 13 cm, 14 cm aur 15 cm hain. Gutke ki lambai 10 cm hai.

Ye wahi 13-14-15 wala triangle hai jo Sameer ke traffic island mein aaya tha (Topic 1).

- s = (13 + 14 + 15)/2 = 21
- Area = √(21 × 8 × 7 × 6) = √7056 = **84 cm²**
- Volume = 84 × 10 = **840 cm³**
- Perimeter = 42 cm, LSA = 42 × 10 = **420 cm²**
- TSA = 420 + 2 × 84 = **588 cm²**

Jaanch: 21 × 8 × 7 × 6 = 7056, aur 84 × 84 = 7056 ✅

### Example 3: Lakdiyon ke dher ka cover

Gaon mein jalane ki lakdiyon ke dher ko baarish se bachane ke liye tirpal ka ek cover banana hai. Cover ka aakaar ek **triangular prism** jaisa hai: saamne se dekho to ek isosceles triangle (neeche 6 m chauda, dono slant sides 5 m), aur peeche ki taraf 10 m lamba. **Neeche zameen par tirpal nahi lagega.**

**Triangle ki height:** Base ke aadhe (3 m) aur slant side (5 m) se, height = √(5² − 3²) = √16 = **4 m** (Pythagoras, pichhli classes wala).

**Tirpal kitna lagega?**
- Do slant wali deewarein: 2 × (5 × 10) = 100 m²
- Do tikone sire: 2 × (½ × 6 × 4) = 2 × 12 = 24 m²
- Neeche ki zameen: **nahi** (6 × 10 wala hissa chhod do)
- Kul tirpal = 100 + 24 = **124 m²**

**Andar kitni jagah?** Volume = 12 × 10 = **120 m³**

**Kharcha:** ₹50 per m² ke hisaab se, 124 × 50 = **₹6200**

Dhyan do, yahan "LSA = perimeter × lambai" seedha nahi lagaya, kyunki perimeter mein neeche ki 6 m wali side bhi aati hai, jis par tirpal nahi chahiye. **Sawaal padh kar dekho ki kaunsi deewar chahiye, kaunsi nahi.**

## Part E: Trapezium cross-section (nahar aur swimming pool)

**Trapezium ka area (pichhli classes se yaad):**

> Area = ½ × (dono parallel sides ka jod) × (unke beech ki doori)
> = ½ (a + b) × h

```
        a (upar)
   ______________
   \            /
    \           /   h (gehraai)
     \_________/
       b (neeche)
```

### Example 4: Slope wala swimming pool

Ek swimming pool **20 m lamba** aur **10 m chauda** hai. Ek sire par gehraai **1 m** hai aur doosre sire par **3 m**, aur farsh ek seedhi dhalaan mein neeche jata hai. Pool mein kitna paani aayega?

**Sahi cross-section chuno.** Pool ko side se dekho (20 m wali deewar ki taraf se). Ye ek trapezium hai:
- Parallel sides: 1 m aur 3 m (dono siron ki gehraai)
- Unke beech ki doori: 20 m

Area = ½ × (1 + 3) × 20 = **40 m²**

Ye trapezium poori **chaudai (10 m)** mein ek jaisa hai. To:

Volume = 40 × 10 = **400 m³**

Litre mein: 400 × 1000 = **4,00,000 litre**

Jaanch: Agar pool har jagah average gehraai (1 + 3)/2 = 2 m ka hota, to 20 × 10 × 2 = 400 m³ ✅

### Example 5: Nahar ka paani

Ek nahar (canal) ka cross-section ek trapezium hai: upar 8 m chaudi, neeche 4 m chaudi, aur 2 m gehri. Paani **3 km/h** ki speed se behta hai.

**(a) Ek ghante mein kitna paani?**
- Cross-section area = ½ × (8 + 4) × 2 = **12 m²**
- Ek ghante mein paani 3 km = **3000 m** aage jata hai (units pehle barabar karo!)
- Ek ghante ka volume = 12 × 3000 = **36,000 m³**

Socho, ek ghante mein jitna paani aage gaya, wo ek lamba "prism" hai: cross-section 12 m² aur lambai 3000 m.

**(b) Aadhe ghante mein?** 36,000 ÷ 2 = **18,000 m³**

**(c) Khet ki sinchai:** Ye aadha ghante ka paani khet mein 10 cm (= 0.1 m) gehra bhar diya jaye, to kitne area ki sinchai hogi?

Area = Volume ÷ Gehraai = 18,000 ÷ 0.1 = **1,80,000 m²**

(1 hectare = 10,000 m², to ye 18 hectare hua.)

Jaanch: 1,80,000 × 0.1 = 18,000 ✅

### Example 6: Naali (drain) ka paani

Ghar ke bahar ki pakki naali ka cross-section ek rectangle hai: 50 cm chaudi aur 30 cm gehri (poori bhari hui). Paani 2 km/h se behta hai. Ek minute mein kitne litre paani nikalta hai?

- Pehle sab meter mein: 0.5 m × 0.3 m = **0.15 m²**
- Ek ghante mein: 0.15 × 2000 = **300 m³**
- Ek minute mein: 300 ÷ 60 = **5 m³** = 5 × 1000 = **5000 litre**

Jaanch: 5000 L × 60 = 3,00,000 L = 300 m³ ✅

## Part F: Khokhli (hollow) rectangular pipe (ek jhalak)

Jaise Topic 4 mein gol pipe ka metal = bahar ka area − andar ka area tha, waise hi chaukor pipe mein bhi.

### Example 7

Ek lohe ki chaukor pipe ka bahar ka cross-section 10 cm × 8 cm hai aur andar ka khaali hissa 8 cm × 6 cm. Pipe 2 m lambi hai.

- Metal wala area = 10 × 8 − 8 × 6 = 80 − 48 = **32 cm²**
- Lambai = 2 m = **200 cm** (units barabar!)
- Metal ka volume = 32 × 200 = **6400 cm³**

Agar 1 cm³ lohe ka vajan 7.8 g ho, to pipe ka vajan = 6400 × 7.8 = 49,920 g ≈ **49.92 kg**

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Volume mein perimeter laga dena**
Volume = **area** × lambai. Perimeter × lambai se LSA milta hai, volume nahi. Tara ki chocolate mein 12 × 20 = 240 cm² LSA hai, volume 6 × 20 = 120 cm³ hai.

**Galti 2: TSA mein dono sire bhool jana**
TSA = LSA **+ 2 ×** cross-section area. Sirf LSA likh doge to dono triangle ya trapezium wale sire chhoot jayenge.

**Galti 3: Galat cross-section chunna**
Slope wale pool mein trapezium **side wali deewar** hai (parallel sides 1 m aur 3 m, doori 20 m). Use 10 m (chaudai) se guna karna hai, 20 m se dobara nahi. Pehle socho: kis disha mein kaatne par har tukda ek jaisa hai?

**Galti 4: Speed aur size ki units mix karna**
Nahar ki chaudai meter mein aur speed km/h mein ho, to pehle 3 km/h ko 3000 m per ghanta banao. Warna answer 1000 guna galat aayega.

**Galti 5: Cone ya sphere par ye formula lagana**
Area × lambai sirf **uniform** cross-section par chalta hai. Cone ke liye ⅓ aur sphere ke liye 4/3 wale alag formulas hain (Topic 5 aur 6).

**Galti 6: Sawaal ki deewarein na padhna**
Lakdiyon wale cover mein neeche tirpal nahi tha. "Perimeter × lambai" seedha lagane se pehle dekho ki kaunsi deewar chahiye.

## Quick Recap

- **Uniform cross-section:** lambai ke seedhe kahin se bhi kaato, har tukda ek jaisa.
- **Volume = cross-section ka area × lambai.** Cuboid (lbh) aur cylinder (πr²h) isi ke khaas roop hain.
- **LSA = cross-section ka perimeter × lambai.**
- **TSA = LSA + 2 × cross-section ka area.**
- Triangle cross-section ho to area ½bh se ya Heron se.
- Trapezium cross-section (nahar, pool): area = ½(a + b)h.
- **Behta paani:** ek ghante ka volume = cross-section area × (ek ghante mein tay ki gayi doori). Units pehle barabar karo.
- Cone, sphere aur pyramid uniform cross-section wale **nahi** hain.

## Practice Questions

1. Which of the following solids have a uniform cross-section?
   (a) a brick
   (b) a cone
   (c) an unsharpened hexagonal pencil
   (d) a football
   (e) a long triangular chocolate bar
   (f) a steel pipe

2. A prism has a cross-section of area 15 cm² and a length of 12 cm. Find its volume.

3. A triangular prism has a right-triangle cross-section with legs 6 cm and 8 cm. Its length is 25 cm. Find its volume, lateral surface area and total surface area.

4. The cross-section of a prism is a triangle with sides 5 cm, 5 cm and 6 cm. The prism is 15 cm long. Using Heron's formula, find its volume and total surface area.

5. A swimming pool is 25 m long and 12 m wide. Its depth increases uniformly from 1.2 m at one end to 2.8 m at the other. How many litres of water can it hold?

6. A canal is 6 m wide at the top, 3 m wide at the bottom and 1.5 m deep. Water flows through it at 4 km/h. How much water flows through it in 20 minutes?

7. A tarpaulin cover is shaped like a triangular prism. Its cross-section is an isosceles triangle with base 8 m and equal sides 5 m, and it is 15 m long. There is no tarpaulin on the floor. Find the area of tarpaulin needed and the volume of space inside.

8. State true or false:
   (a) Volume of a prism = perimeter of cross-section × length.
   (b) A cylinder is a solid with a uniform cross-section.
   (c) A cone is a solid with a uniform cross-section.
   (d) Total surface area of a prism = lateral surface area + 2 × area of cross-section.

### Answers

1. **(a), (c), (e), (f).** Cone aur football mein tukde chhote-bade hote hain.

2. Volume = 15 × 12 = **180 cm³**

3. Hypotenuse = √(36 + 64) = 10 cm.
   - Area = ½ × 6 × 8 = 24 cm², Volume = 24 × 25 = **600 cm³**
   - Perimeter = 6 + 8 + 10 = 24 cm, LSA = 24 × 25 = **600 cm²**
   - TSA = 600 + 2 × 24 = **648 cm²**

4. s = (5 + 5 + 6)/2 = 8. Area = √(8 × 3 × 3 × 2) = √144 = 12 cm².
   - Volume = 12 × 15 = **180 cm³**
   - Perimeter = 16 cm, LSA = 16 × 15 = 240 cm²
   - TSA = 240 + 2 × 12 = **264 cm²**

5. Side ka trapezium: ½ × (1.2 + 2.8) × 25 = ½ × 4 × 25 = 50 m².
   Volume = 50 × 12 = 600 m³ = **6,00,000 litre**
   Jaanch: average gehraai 2 m, 25 × 12 × 2 = 600 ✅

6. Cross-section = ½ × (6 + 3) × 1.5 = 6.75 m².
   20 minute mein paani 4000 × (20/60) = 4000/3 m aage jata hai.
   Volume = 6.75 × 4000/3 = 27,000/3 = **9000 m³**

7. Triangle ki height = √(5² − 4²) = √9 = 3 m. Triangle ka area = ½ × 8 × 3 = 12 m².
   - Tirpal = do slant deewarein + do sire = 2 × (5 × 15) + 2 × 12 = 150 + 24 = **174 m²**
   - Andar ki jagah = 12 × 15 = **180 m³**

8. (a) **False** ❌. Perimeter × lambai se LSA milta hai. Volume ke liye area chahiye.
   (b) **True** ✅. Har tukda ek jaisa circle.
   (c) **False** ❌. Upar jaate-jaate circle chhota hota hai.
   (d) **True** ✅.

### Saare formulas ek jagah

**Flat shapes (area aur perimeter)**

| Shape | Area | Perimeter / Lambai |
|---|---|---|
| Triangle (base b, height h) | ½bh | a + b + c |
| Triangle (teeno sides) | √(s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)) | 2s |
| Equilateral triangle (a) | (√3/4)a² | 3a |
| Circle (r) | πr² | 2πr |
| Semicircle (r) | ½πr² | πr + 2r |
| Quadrant (r) | ¼πr² | ½πr + 2r |
| Ring (R bahar, r andar) | π(R² − r²) = π(R + r)(R − r) | — |
| Sector (angle θ) | (θ/360) × πr² | Arc = (θ/360) × 2πr; perimeter = arc + 2r |
| Trapezium | ½(a + b)h | — |

**Solids**

| Shape | CSA / LSA | TSA | Volume |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cuboid (l, b, h) | 2h(l + b) | 2(lb + bh + hl) | lbh |
| Cube (a) | 4a² | 6a² | a³ |
| Cylinder (r, h) | 2πrh | 2πr(r + h) | πr²h |
| Cone (r, h, l) | πrl | πr(l + r) | ⅓πr²h |
| Sphere (r) | 4πr² | 4πr² | (4/3)πr³ |
| Hemisphere (r) | 2πr² | 3πr² | (2/3)πr³ |
| Prism (cross-section A, perimeter P, lambai L) | P × L | P × L + 2A | A × L |
