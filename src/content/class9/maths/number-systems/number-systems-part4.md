# Topic 4: Real Numbers par Operations, nth Root aur Surds

Topic 3 mein humne ek nayi duniya dekhi: **irrational numbers**. Aise numbers jinka decimal kabhi khatam nahi hota aur kabhi repeat bhi nahi hota, jaise √2, √3 aur π. Humne ye bhi dekha ki rational aur irrational milkar **real numbers** banate hain, aur number line ka har point ek real number hai.

Ab sawaal ye hai: in numbers ke saath **kaam** kaise karein? Agar ek rational aur ek irrational ko jodo, to kya milega? √50 jaise number ko chhota kaise likhein? Aur √5 jaisa number number line par kahan baithega? Is topic mein hum yahi seekhenge.

Bunty ne apni dadi ki ek photo ke liye **square frame** banaya, jiski har side **1 foot** thi. Frame ko sundar banane ke liye usne ek kone se doosre kone tak **tirchhi (diagonal) golden ribbon** lagayi. Phir frame ko deewar par latkane ke liye upar **2 foot** ki seedhi dori bandhi.

## Part A: Rational aur irrational ko milana

### Rule 1: Rational + irrational = irrational

**Kyun?** Maan lo 2 + √3 rational hota, jaise koi number r. To √3 = r − 2 hota. Par do rational numbers ka antar hamesha rational hota hai. Iska matlab √3 rational ho jata, jo galat hai (Topic 3 mein dekha ki √3 irrational hai). To 2 + √3 rational ho hi nahi sakta.

Isi tarah:
- **Rational − irrational = irrational** (jaise 5 − √2)
- **Irrational − rational = irrational** (jaise √7 − 1)

### Rule 2: Non-zero rational × irrational = irrational

Isi wajah se 5√2, (1/3)√7, −4π, sab irrational hain.

Same baat bhaag ke liye: **irrational ÷ non-zero rational = irrational**, jaise √3/2.

**"Non-zero" kyun likha?** Kyunki 0 × √2 = 0, aur 0 rational hai. Zero sabko zero bana deta hai, isliye use alag rakhna padta hai.

### Rule 3: Irrational aur irrational: kuch bhi ho sakta hai

Yahan koi pakka rule nahi hai. Dekho:

| Operation | Jawab | Rational ya irrational? |
|---|---|---|
| √2 + (−√2) | 0 | Rational |
| (2 + √3) + (2 − √3) | 4 | Rational |
| √3 + √3 | 2√3 | Irrational |
| √2 × √2 | 2 | Rational |
| √2 × √8 | √16 = 4 | Rational |
| √2 × √3 | √6 | Irrational |
| √12 ÷ √3 | √4 = 2 | Rational |
| π − π | 0 | Rational |

Isliye jab do irrational numbers mil rahe hon, to **pehle hisaab lagao, phir faisla karo.**

### Solved examples: rational hai ya irrational?

**Example 1:** 3 + √5
Rational (3) + irrational (√5) = **irrational**.

**Example 2:** (4 + √7) − √7
Pehle simplify karo: 4 + √7 − √7 = 4. Ye **rational** hai.
(Bina simplify kiye "irrational" bol dena aam galti hai.)

**Example 3:** 6√2 ÷ 3√2
= (6/3) × (√2/√2) = 2 × 1 = 2. **Rational.**

**Example 4:** 2π
Non-zero rational × irrational = **irrational**.

**Example 5:** √5 × √20
= √100 = 10. **Rational.**

**Example 6:** 1/√2
Ye 1 ÷ √2 hai. Agar ye rational hota, to uska ulta √2 bhi rational hota. Par √2 irrational hai, to 1/√2 bhi **irrational** hai.

## Part B: Kisi bhi √x ko number line par dikhana

Topic 3 mein humne √2 aur √3 ko number line par Pythagoras aur compass se dikhaya tha, aur square root spiral bhi banaya tha. Par √9.3 ya √5.7 jaise numbers ke liye wo tareeka (ek ke baad ek triangle) bahut lamba ho jayega. Iske liye ek chhota, mazedaar tareeka hai: **semicircle wala tareeka.**

### Steps (√x dikhane ke liye, x > 0)

1. Ek seedhi line par point A lo. A se **x unit** door point B lagao. Yaani AB = x.
2. B se aage **1 unit** door point C lagao. Yaani BC = 1, aur AC = x + 1.
3. AC ka **midpoint O** nikalo. O ko centre maan kar, OA radius se ek **semicircle** banao.
4. B par AC ke **perpendicular** ek line kheencho. Ye semicircle ko jahan kaate, use **D** kaho.
5. Ab **BD = √x** hai!
6. Number line par dikhane ke liye: B ko **0** maano aur BC wali line ko number line maano. B ko centre aur BD ko radius lekar ek arc banao, jo number line ko **E** par kaate. **E hi √x hai.**

```
           . . . . .
        .             D
      .               | .
     .                |  .
    A----------O------B---C
    |<------ x ------>|<1>|
                   (B = 0)
```

(Sketch sirf idea ke liye hai, scale par nahi.)

### Ye kaam kyun karta hai? (Halki si jhalak)

Iske liye Pythagoras theorem chahiye, jo pichhli classes mein padha tha: right triangle mein (hypotenuse)² = (ek side)² + (doosri side)².

- OD = radius = (x + 1)/2
- OB = AB − AO = x − (x + 1)/2 = **(x − 1)/2**
- Triangle OBD mein angle B = 90°, to:

BD² = OD² − OB²
= ((x + 1)/2)² − ((x − 1)/2)²
= [(x + 1)² − (x − 1)²] / 4
= [x² + 2x + 1 − x² + 2x − 1] / 4
= 4x / 4
= **x**

To BD = √x. Jaadu nahi, seedha hisaab hai!

### Example 1: √5 ko number line par dikhao

- AB = 5, BC = 1, to AC = 6.
- O, AC ka midpoint: AO = 3. Radius = 3.
- OB = 5 − 3 = 2.
- BD² = 3² − 2² = 9 − 4 = 5, to **BD = √5** ✅
- Ab B ko 0 maan kar, BD radius ka arc number line par √5 dikhayega (lagbhag 2.236).

### Example 2: √9.3 ko number line par dikhao

- AB = 9.3, BC = 1, to AC = 10.3.
- Radius = 10.3 / 2 = 5.15.
- OB = 9.3 − 5.15 = 4.15.
- BD² = 5.15² − 4.15² = (5.15 + 4.15)(5.15 − 4.15) = 9.3 × 1 = 9.3 ✅
- To BD = √9.3 (lagbhag 3.05, kyunki 3.05² = 9.3025).

Dekha? Kitna bhi "ajeeb" positive number ho, ye tareeka uska square root number line par dikha deta hai. Isse Topic 3 wali baat aur pakki hoti hai: **har real number ka number line par ek point hai.**

## Part C: nth root

### Square root aur cube root yaad karo

- √9 = 3, kyunki 3 × 3 = 9 (3 ko **2 baar** guna kiya)
- ∛8 = 2, kyunki 2 × 2 × 2 = 8 (2 ko **3 baar** guna kiya)

Ab isi ko aage badhao.

### Definition

Maan lo a ek positive real number hai aur n ek positive integer hai. Agar

**bⁿ = a**, to **b = ⁿ√a**

Yaani **a ka nth root wo number hai jise n baar khud se guna karne par a mile.**

### Teen naam yaad rakho

```
   index
     ↓
     ⁴√81     ← poora: "81 ka fourth root"
      ↑ ↑
radical sign  radicand
```

- **Radical sign:** √ ka nishaan
- **Index:** chhota number (yahan 4). Square root mein index 2 hota hai, par use likhte nahi.
- **Radicand:** andar wala number (yahan 81)

### Examples

| nth root | Kyun? | Jawab |
|---|---|---|
| ∛8 | 2 × 2 × 2 = 8 | **2** |
| ∛27 | 3 × 3 × 3 = 27 | **3** |
| ⁴√81 | 3 × 3 × 3 × 3 = 81 | **3** |
| ⁴√16 | 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 16 | **2** |
| ⁵√32 | 2⁵ = 32 | **2** |
| ⁶√64 | 2⁶ = 64 | **2** |

### Negative number ka root? (Ek chhoti si jhalak)

- **Odd root** (3rd, 5th...) negative number ka bhi mil jata hai: ∛(−8) = −2, kyunki (−2)³ = −8.
- **Even root** (2nd, 4th...) negative number ka **real number nahi hota.** √(−4) ke liye aisa koi real number chahiye jiska square −4 ho, par koi bhi real number square karne par negative nahi aata. Aise numbers ke baare mein aage ki classes mein padhoge.

Is chapter mein hum sirf **positive** numbers ke roots ki baat karenge.

## Part D: Surds

### Surd kya hai?

**Surd** kisi positive rational number ka aisa root hai jo **irrational** ho.

| Number | Surd hai? | Kyun? |
|---|---|---|
| √2 | Haan | 2 rational hai, √2 irrational hai |
| ∛5 | Haan | 5 rational hai, ∛5 irrational hai |
| √50 | Haan | Irrational hai (aage dekhenge: 5√2) |
| √9 | **Nahi** | √9 = 3, jo rational hai |
| ∛27 | **Nahi** | ∛27 = 3, rational |
| √π | **Nahi** | π khud rational nahi hai, to ye surd ki definition mein nahi aata |

**Order of a surd** = uska index.
- √2 ka order **2**
- ∛5 ka order **3**
- ⁴√7 ka order **4**

### Surd ko simplify karna

Idea simple hai: radicand mein se **perfect square** (ya cube root ke liye **perfect cube**) dhoondo, aur use bahar nikalo. Ye Part E ke rule √(ab) = √a × √b par tika hai.

**Example 1:** √50
50 = 25 × 2, aur 25 = 5² perfect square hai.
√50 = √25 × √2 = **5√2**
Jaanch: (5√2)² = 25 × 2 = 50 ✅

**Example 2:** √72
72 = 36 × 2 (36 sabse bada perfect square factor hai).
√72 = √36 × √2 = **6√2**
Jaanch: (6√2)² = 36 × 2 = 72 ✅

**Example 3:** ∛54
54 = 27 × 2, aur 27 = 3³ perfect cube hai.
∛54 = ∛27 × ∛2 = **3∛2**
Jaanch: (3∛2)³ = 27 × 2 = 54 ✅

**Example 4:** √48
48 = 16 × 3.
√48 = **4√3**

**Tip:** Hamesha **sabse bada** perfect square factor lo. Agar √72 = √4 × √18 = 2√18 likh diya, to ruk mat jao, kyunki √18 ko aur simplify kar sakte ho: 2√18 = 2 × 3√2 = 6√2.

### Like aur unlike surds

Polynomials chapter ke "like terms" yaad hain? 3x + 5x = 8x hota hai, par 3x + 5y ko jod nahi sakte. Surds mein bhi bilkul yahi hai.

- **Like surds:** simplify karne ke baad radicand aur order same. Jaise 2√3 aur 5√3.
- **Unlike surds:** radicand ya order alag. Jaise √2 aur √3.

**Like surds ko jod-ghata sakte hain:**
- 2√3 + 5√3 = **7√3**
- 9√5 − 4√5 = **5√5**

**Unlike surds ko nahi:**
- √2 + √3 aise hi rahega. Ye √5 **nahi** hai.

**Example 5:** √12 + √27
Pehle simplify: √12 = 2√3, √27 = 3√3.
Ab dono like surds hain: 2√3 + 3√3 = **5√3**

**Example 6:** √50 − √18
√50 = 5√2, √18 = 3√2.
5√2 − 3√2 = **2√2**

**Example 7:** √8 + √18 + √32
= 2√2 + 3√2 + 4√2 = **9√2**

Dekha? Pehli nazar mein unlike lagne wale surds bhi simplify karne ke baad like ban jate hain. Isliye **pehle simplify, phir jodo.**

### Surds ko compare karna

3√2 bada hai ya 2√3? Seedha dekh ke batana mushkil hai. Trick: **dono ko square kar do.** Positive numbers mein jiska square bada, wahi bada.

**Example 8:** 3√2 vs 2√3
- (3√2)² = 9 × 2 = 18
- (2√3)² = 4 × 3 = 12
- 18 > 12, to **3√2 > 2√3**

**Example 9:** 2√5 vs 3√2
- (2√5)² = 20
- (3√2)² = 18
- To **2√5 > 3√2**

(Agar dono surds ka order alag ho, jaise √5 aur ∛11, to dono ko aisi power par le jao jo dono ko poora hata de. Yahan power 6: (√5)⁶ = 125 aur (∛11)⁶ = 121, to √5 bada hai. Ye ek extra trick hai.)

## Part E: Square roots wali identities

Ye identities aage bahut kaam aayengi, khaas kar Topic 5 mein. Yahan a, b, c, d sab positive real numbers hain.

| # | Identity | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | √(ab) = √a × √b | √36 = √4 × √9 = 2 × 3 = 6 |
| 2 | √(a/b) = √a / √b | √(9/16) = 3/4 |
| 3 | (√a + √b)(√a − √b) = a − b | (√5 + √2)(√5 − √2) = 3 |
| 4 | (a + √b)(a − √b) = a² − b | (5 + √7)(5 − √7) = 18 |
| 5 | (√a + √b)² = a + 2√(ab) + b | (√3 + √2)² = 5 + 2√6 |
| 6 | (√a + √b)(√c + √d) = √(ac) + √(ad) + √(bc) + √(bd) | neeche Example 1 |

Identity 3 aur 4 dhyan se dekho: **root wala hissa poora gayab ho gaya!** Ye Factorisation chapter ka a² − b² = (a + b)(a − b) hi hai, bas a aur b ki jagah roots hain. Ye baat Topic 5 mein jaadu ki tarah kaam aayegi.

### Solved examples

**Example 1:** (3 + √3)(2 + √2)
Har term ko har term se guna karo:
= 3 × 2 + 3 × √2 + √3 × 2 + √3 × √2
= **6 + 3√2 + 2√3 + √6**

**Example 2:** (√5 + √2)²
Identity 5 se:
= 5 + 2√(5 × 2) + 2
= **7 + 2√10**
Jaanch: (√5 + √2)(√5 + √2) = 5 + √10 + √10 + 2 = 7 + 2√10 ✅

**Example 3:** (√11 − √7)(√11 + √7)
Identity 3 se:
= 11 − 7 = **4**
(Do irrational numbers ka guna, aur jawab ek seedha rational number!)

**Example 4:** (5 + √7)(5 − √7)
Identity 4 se:
= 5² − 7 = 25 − 7 = **18**

**Example 5:** (3 − √5)²
= 3² − 2 × 3 × √5 + (√5)²
= 9 − 6√5 + 5
= **14 − 6√5**

**Example 6:** (2√3 + √5)(2√3 − √5)
Identity 3 ki tarah:
= (2√3)² − (√5)²
= 12 − 5 = **7**

**Example 7:** (√2 + √3)(√5 + √7)
Identity 6 se:
= √10 + √14 + √15 + √21
(Inme koi like surds nahi hain, to jawab yahi rahega.)

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: √(a + b) = √a + √b samajhna**
√(9 + 16) = √25 = **5**, par √9 + √16 = 3 + 4 = **7**. Dono alag hain! Root guna aur bhaag par toot-ta hai, **jod aur ghata par nahi.**

**Galti 2: Unlike surds ko jod dena**
√2 + √3 ≠ √5. Check karo: √2 + √3 ≈ 1.414 + 1.732 = 3.146, par √5 ≈ 2.236.

**Galti 3: Simplify kiye bina "unlike" bol dena**
√12 + √27 dekhne mein unlike lagte hain, par simplify karne par 2√3 + 3√3 = 5√3 ban jate hain.

**Galti 4: Do irrational ka jawab hamesha irrational maan lena**
√2 × √8 = 4, jo rational hai. Pehle hisaab lagao.

**Galti 5: (√a + √b)² = a + b likhna**
Beech ka term **2√(ab)** bhool gaye. (√3 + √2)² = 5 + 2√6, sirf 5 nahi.

**Galti 6: Har root ko surd maan lena**
√9 = 3 rational hai, isliye surd nahi hai. Surd sirf tab hai jab root irrational ho.

**Galti 7: Sabse bada perfect square na lena**
√72 = 2√18 par mat ruko. Aage 2√18 = 6√2 tak jao.

**Galti 8: Semicircle wale tareeke mein BC ko 1 lena bhool jana**
√x ke liye AB = x aur BC = **1** hona chahiye. Agar BC galat liya, to BD = √x nahi aayega.

## Quick Recap

- **Rational ± irrational = irrational.** **Non-zero rational × ya ÷ irrational = irrational.**
- **Irrational aur irrational** ka jawab rational bhi ho sakta hai aur irrational bhi. Pehle hisaab lagao.
- **√x number line par:** AB = x, BC = 1, AC par semicircle, B par perpendicular. BD = √x.
- **nth root:** bⁿ = a ho to b = ⁿ√a. Index, radical sign aur radicand.
- **Surd:** positive rational number ka irrational root. Order = index.
- **Simplify:** sabse bada perfect square (ya cube) bahar nikalo. √50 = 5√2.
- **Like surds** ko jod sakte hain (2√3 + 5√3 = 7√3), unlike ko nahi.
- **Compare:** dono ko square karo, jiska square bada wahi bada.
- **Identities:** √(ab) = √a√b, (√a + √b)(√a − √b) = a − b, (a + √b)(a − √b) = a² − b, (√a + √b)² = a + 2√(ab) + b.
- √(a + b) ≠ √a + √b.

3. Simplify:
   (a) √98
   (b) ∛250
   (c) √45 + √20
   (d) 2√12 − √27

4. Which is greater?
   (a) 2√7 or 3√3
   (b) 4√2 or 3√3

   (a) (√7 + √2)(√7 − √2)
   (b) (2 + √3)²
   (c) (√5 + √3)(√2 + 1)
   (d) (3 + √2)(3 − √2)

6. Find the value of:
   (a) ∛125
   (b) ⁴√16
   (c) ⁶√64

7. Describe the steps to locate √6.5 on the number line, and show why the length you get is √6.5.

8. State true or false. Give a reason.
   (a) √(16 + 9) = √16 + √9
   (b) Every surd is an irrational number.
   (c) √9 is a surd.
   (d) The product of two irrational numbers is always irrational.

### Answers

1. (a) **Irrational.** Rational (2) − irrational (√5) = irrational.
   (b) **Rational.** Simplify karne par 3 bachta hai.
   (c) **Rational.** √7 kat jata hai, bachta hai 2/7.
   (d) **Irrational.** Agar 1/√2 rational hota, to √2 bhi rational hota.
   (e) **Irrational.** Non-zero rational (2) × irrational (π).
   (f) **Rational.** √2 × √18 = √36 = 6.

2. Koi bhi sahi example chalega. Jaise:
   (a) √3 aur −√3 (sum 0). Ya 2 + √3 aur 2 − √3 (sum 4).
   (b) √2 aur √8 (product √16 = 4).
   (c) √2 aur √3 (product √6).

3. (a) √98 = √(49 × 2) = **7√2**
   (b) ∛250 = ∛(125 × 2) = **5∛2**
   (c) √45 = 3√5, √20 = 2√5. Total = **5√5**
   (d) 2√12 = 2 × 2√3 = 4√3, √27 = 3√3. 4√3 − 3√3 = **√3**

4. (a) (2√7)² = 28, (3√3)² = 27. 28 > 27, to **2√7 bada hai.**
   (b) (4√2)² = 32, (3√3)² = 27. To **4√2 bada hai.**

5. (a) 7 − 2 = **5**
   (b) 4 + 4√3 + 3 = **7 + 4√3**
   Jaanch: (2 + √3)(2 + √3) = 4 + 2√3 + 2√3 + 3 = 7 + 4√3 ✅
   (c) √5 × √2 + √5 × 1 + √3 × √2 + √3 × 1 = **√10 + √5 + √6 + √3**
   (d) 9 − 2 = **7**

6. (a) 5 × 5 × 5 = 125, to **5**
   (b) 2⁴ = 16, to **2**
   (c) 2⁶ = 64, to **2**

7. Steps:
   - Line par AB = 6.5 lo, aur B se aage BC = 1. To AC = 7.5.
   - AC ka midpoint O. Radius = 7.5 / 2 = 3.75. Semicircle banao.
   - B par perpendicular kheencho, jo semicircle ko D par kaate.
   - OB = 6.5 − 3.75 = 2.75.
   - BD² = 3.75² − 2.75² = (3.75 + 2.75)(3.75 − 2.75) = 6.5 × 1 = 6.5, to **BD = √6.5** ✅
   - B ko 0 maan kar, B centre aur BD radius ka arc number line ko jahan kaate, wahi √6.5 hai.

8. (a) **False** ❌. √25 = 5, par √16 + √9 = 4 + 3 = 7.
   (b) **True** ✅. Surd ki definition hi hai ki wo irrational root ho.
   (c) **False** ❌. √9 = 3, jo rational hai.
   (d) **False** ❌. √2 × √2 = 2, jo rational hai.

**Agle topic mein:** 1/√2 ya 1/(√5 + √2) jaise numbers, jinke **neeche** root baitha hai. Inke saath kaam karna mushkil hota hai. Topic 5 mein hum seekhenge ki Part E ki identity (√a + √b)(√a − √b) = a − b ki madad se denominator se root ko kaise "gayab" karte hain. Isse **Rationalisation** kehte hain.
