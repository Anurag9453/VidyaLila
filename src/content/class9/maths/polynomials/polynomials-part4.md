# Topic 5: Number of Terms in a Polynomial

Pichle topic mein hum ne polynomials ko unki **degree** ke hisaab se naam diye (linear, quadratic, cubic, biquadratic). Ab hum polynomials ko ek **doosre tarike** se naam denge: unme **kitne terms** hain, ye dekhkar. Topic 1 mein hum ne monomial, binomial aur trinomial ka naam ek baar chhoote hue dekha tha. Ab hum inhe poori tarah, saari baariki ke saath samjhenge.

---

## Ice-cream ki kahani

Socho aap ice-cream wale ke paas gaye.

- Aapne **1 scoop** liya (sirf vanilla). Ye hua **ek scoop wala cone.**
- Aapke dost ne **2 scoop** liye (vanilla aur chocolate). Ye hua **do scoop wala cone.**
- Aapki behen ne **3 scoop** liye (vanilla, chocolate aur strawberry). Ye hua **teen scoop wala cone.**

Yahan **har scoop ek term** hai (pichle topics ki tarah, jahan har term + ya - se alag hota tha).

Ab ek mazedaar sawal. Agar aapne **2 scoop vanilla** hi liye, to kya wo do alag flavour hue? Nahi, wo to **ek hi flavour** hai, bas zyada matra mein. Algebra mein bhi yahi hota hai: 3x aur 5x **like terms** hain, aur unhe jodne ke baad ek hi term (8x) bachta hai.

Ye yaad rakhna, kyunki isse aage hum dekhenge ki **terms ginne se pehle like terms jodna** zaroori hai.

---

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Monomial** | Sirf **1 term** wala polynomial | 5x, 7, 3x²y |
| **Binomial** | Sirf **2 terms** wala polynomial | x + 3, 2a - 5b |
| **Trinomial** | Sirf **3 terms** wala polynomial | x² + 2x + 1 |

Naam yaad rakhne ka tarika:
- **Mono** = ek (monorail: ek patri par chalne wali train)
- **Bi** = do (binoculars: do aankhon se dekhne wala yantra)
- **Tri** = teen (tripod: teen taangon wala stand)

---

## Part A: Monomial (1 term)

**Monomial** wo polynomial hai jisme **sirf ek term** ho. Yaani isme koi **+ ya - (jo terms ko alag kare)** nahi hota.

### Examples

| Polynomial | Kyun monomial hai |
|---|---|
| 5x | Sirf ek term |
| -7y² | Sirf ek term (minus sign term ke saath hai) |
| 3x²yz | Kai letters hain, par sab × se jude hain, to ek hi term |
| 9 | Constant bhi ek term hai |
| x/4 | (1/4) × x, ek term |
| πr² | π × r × r, ek term |

### Zaroori baatein

- Monomial mein **sirf ek letter hona zaroori nahi hai.** 3x²yz bhi monomial hai.
- Monomial mein **constant bhi aa sakta hai.** 9 ek monomial hai.
- Monomial ki degree kuch bhi ho sakti hai. 5x (degree 1), 5x² (degree 2), 5x⁹ (degree 9), sab monomial hain.

### Real-life mein monomial

- Square ka perimeter = **4s** (monomial)
- Square ka area = **s²** (monomial)
- Rectangle ka area = **lb** (monomial)
- Circle ka area = **πr²** (monomial)
- n pen ka cost (₹10 each) = **10n** (monomial)

---

## Part B: Binomial (2 terms)

**Binomial** wo polynomial hai jisme **theek do terms** ho.

### Examples

| Polynomial | Terms |
|---|---|
| x + 3 | x, 3 |
| 2a - 5b | 2a, -5b |
| x² - 1 | x², -1 |
| 4x³ + 7x | 4x³, 7x |
| 3xy - 2 | 3xy, -2 |
| y/2 + 6 | y/2, 6 |

### Ek zaroori baat: minus bhi binomial banata hai

Log aksar sochte hain ki "binomial" ka matlab "do cheezein jodna" hai (plus). Par **x - y bhi binomial hai**, kyunki Topic 2 mein humne dekha tha ki term minus ke saath hi aata hai. x - y mein terms hain **x aur -y**, yaani 2 terms.

### Real-life mein binomial

- Rectangle ka perimeter = **2l + 2b** (binomial)
- Auto ka kiraya = **30 + 12x** (binomial, Topic 1 wala)
- 4 pens (₹p each) aur 3 books (₹b each) ka cost = **4p + 3b** (binomial)

---

## Part C: Trinomial (3 terms)

**Trinomial** wo polynomial hai jisme **theek teen terms** ho.

### Examples

| Polynomial | Terms |
|---|---|
| x² + 2x + 1 | x², 2x, 1 |
| a + b + c | a, b, c |
| 3y³ - y + 5 | 3y³, -y, 5 |
| x² - 3xy + 2y² | x², -3xy, 2y² |
| 2p - 5q + 8 | 2p, -5q, 8 |

### Real-life mein trinomial

- Triangle ka perimeter (sides a, b, c) = **a + b + c** (trinomial)
- Topic 2 ka shop bill: 4 notebooks, 3 pens aur 1 bag = **4n + 3p + 250** (trinomial)
- Topic 3 ka khet: **x² + 5x + 6** (trinomial)

### Ek khaas baat

Topic 4 ka **quadratic polynomial ax² + bx + c** ek trinomial ki tarah dikhta hai. Isiliye quadratic aksar trinomial hota hai, par hamesha nahi. Ye hum aage dekhenge.

---

## Part D: 3 se zyada terms

Agar kisi polynomial mein **3 se zyada terms** hon, to uske liye koi alag naam use nahi hota. Hum bas kehte hain "**4 terms wala polynomial**", "**5 terms wala polynomial**" (ya multinomial).

**Example:** x³ + x² + x + 1 mein **4 terms** hain. Ye na monomial hai, na binomial, na trinomial.

---

## Part E: Terms ginne ke 4 niyam

Isme sabse zyada galtiyan hoti hain, isliye dhyan se padho. (Topic 2 mein humne bracket aur fraction ke baare mein dekha tha, yahan hum use polynomials par laga rahe hain.)

### Niyam 1: Pehle like terms jodo, phir terms gino

Ice-cream wali baat yaad karo: 2 scoop vanilla ek hi flavour hai.

**Example:** 3x + 5x + 2
Dekhne mein 3 terms lagte hain (trinomial). Par 3x + 5x = 8x, to
= 8x + 2 → **2 terms, binomial.**

**Example:** 2x² + 3x - 2x² + 5
2x² - 2x² = 0 ho jata hai, to
= 3x + 5 → **2 terms, binomial.**

### Niyam 2: Bracket ho to pehle kholo

**Example:** x(x + 1)
Kholo: x² + x → **2 terms, binomial.**

**Example:** 3(a + b) - 3a
Kholo: 3a + 3b - 3a. 3a - 3a = 0 ho gaya, bacha 3b → **1 term, monomial.**

### Niyam 3: Fraction mein numerator ke terms dekho

- **x/2** → 1 term, monomial.
- **(x + y)/2** = x/2 + y/2 → **2 terms, binomial.**

### Niyam 4: Minus sign term ko alag nahi karta, term ke saath chipka rehta hai

- **a - b** → terms a aur -b → **2 terms, binomial.**
- **x² - 5x + 6** → x², -5x, 6 → **3 terms, trinomial.**

---

## Part F: Do tarike se naam dena (terms aur degree dono)

Topic 4 mein degree ke hisaab se naam diye (linear, quadratic...). Is topic mein terms ke hisaab se (mono, bi, tri). Dono **alag alag baatein** hain, to ek polynomial ko **dono naam** diye ja sakte hain.

Isse aise samjho: ek insaan ko hum uski **height** se bhi bula sakte hain ("lamba") aur **age** se bhi ("bada"). Dono sach hain, bas alag cheez ke baare mein.

**Example:** x² + 2x + 1
- Terms ki ginti: 3 → **trinomial**
- Degree: 2 → **quadratic**
- Poora naam: **quadratic trinomial**

### Table: Degree × Terms

| | Monomial (1 term) | Binomial (2 terms) | Trinomial (3 terms) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Linear (degree 1)** | 5x | 3x + 2 | (ek variable mein nahi ho sakta*) |
| **Quadratic (degree 2)** | 7x² | x² - 4 | x² - 4x + 3 |
| **Cubic (degree 3)** | 2x³ | x³ + 1 | x³ - x + 5 |
| **Biquadratic (degree 4)** | x⁴ | x⁴ - 16 | x⁴ - 5x² + 4 |

*Ek variable (x) mein linear polynomial ax + b mein zyada se zyada 2 terms hote hain (x aur constant), to trinomial nahi ban sakta.

### Kitne terms tak ja sakte hain?

Ek variable mein, **degree n** wale polynomial mein zyada se zyada **n + 1 terms** ho sakte hain (kyunki powers n, n - 1, ..., 1, 0 hote hain).

| Degree | Zyada se zyada terms |
|---|---|
| 1 (linear) | 2 |
| 2 (quadratic) | 3 |
| 3 (cubic) | 4 |
| 4 (biquadratic) | 5 |

Is se ye nikalta hai:
- **Linear** mein 1 ya 2 terms ho sakte hain (monomial ya binomial).
- **Quadratic** mein 1, 2 ya 3 terms ho sakte hain.
- **Cubic** mein 1, 2, 3 ya 4 terms ho sakte hain.

## Part G: Do ya zyada variables wale polynomials

Terms ginne ka tarika bilkul wahi hai, chahe variables ek hon ya zyada.

| Polynomial | Terms | Naam |
|---|---|---|
| 3ab | 1 | Monomial |
| x² - y² | 2 | Binomial |
| x² + xy + y² | 3 | Trinomial |
| 2p²q - 3pq² + 5 | 3 | Trinomial |
| a + b + c + d | 4 | 4 terms wala |

## Part H: Solved examples

Neeche har example mein terms aur degree dono ke hisaab se naam batayenge.

**Example 1:** 7x
- 1 term, **monomial**
- Degree 1, **linear**
- **Linear monomial**

**Example 2:** 3x² + 5
- 2 terms, **binomial**
- Degree 2, **quadratic**
- **Quadratic binomial**

**Example 3:** y³ - y² + 4
- 3 terms, **trinomial**
- Degree 3, **cubic**
- **Cubic trinomial**

**Example 4:** x⁴ - 16
- 2 terms, **binomial**
- Degree 4, **biquadratic**
- **Biquadratic binomial**

**Example 5:** 9
- 1 term, **monomial**
- Degree 0, **constant**
- **Constant monomial**

**Example 6:** 5x + 2x - 3
Pehle like terms jodo: 5x + 2x = 7x, to 7x - 3.
- 2 terms, **binomial**; degree 1, **linear**.
- **Linear binomial**

**Example 7:** x² + 3x - x² + 5x
x² - x² = 0 aur 3x + 5x = 8x, to bacha **8x**.
- 1 term, **monomial**; degree 1, **linear**.
- **Linear monomial** (dekhne mein tha 4 terms, par jodne ke baad 1 term nikla!)

**Example 8:** 3(a + b) - 3a
Kholo: 3a + 3b - 3a = **3b**.
- **Monomial** (linear).

**Example 9:** 2x²y - 3xy² + 5
- 3 terms, **trinomial** (do variables x, y hain).

**Example 10:** Ek **cubic trinomial** likho.
x³ + 2x - 1 (powers 3, 1, 0, to 3 terms hain aur sabse badi power 3 hai).

**Example 11:** Ek **biquadratic binomial** likho.
x⁴ + 1 (ya 2z⁴ - 7z).

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Monomial ka matlab "sirf ek letter" samajhna**
3x²yz monomial hai, kyunki isme **ek hi term** hai. Letters kitne bhi ho sakte hain. Ginti terms ki hoti hai, letters ki nahi.

**Galti 2: Like terms jode bina terms ginna**
3x + 5x + 2 trinomial **nahi** hai. Jodne ke baad 8x + 2 bacha, jo **binomial** hai.

**Galti 3: Trinomial aur cubic mein confuse hona**
Dono mein "3" aata hai, par dono alag cheezein hain:
- **Trinomial** = **3 terms** hain
- **Cubic** = **degree 3** hai

x³ + 1 **cubic** hai (degree 3), par **binomial** hai (2 terms). x² + 2x + 1 **trinomial** hai (3 terms), par **quadratic** hai (degree 2).

**Galti 4: Minus wale ko binomial nahi maanna**
x - 5 bhi binomial hai. Terms hain x aur -5.

**Galti 5: Bracket ke andar ke terms ko pehle se gin lena**
3(a + b) ko dekh ke 2 terms mat bol do. Pehle bracket kholo (3a + 3b), phir gino. Aur kabhi kabhi jodne ke baad ginti badal bhi jati hai (jaise Example 8).

**Galti 6: Monomial ko degree 1 samajhna**
Monomial ki degree kuch bhi ho sakti hai. 5x⁴ monomial hai, aur uski degree 4 hai.

**Galti 7: Degree 3 wale mein hamesha 3 terms samajhna**
Cubic mein 1 se 4 tak terms ho sakte hain. Ginti degree se tay nahi hoti.

---

## Quick Recap

- **Monomial** = 1 term, **Binomial** = 2 terms, **Trinomial** = 3 terms. 3 se zyada ke liye alag naam nahi, bas "4 terms wala" etc.
- **Mono, bi, tri** = ek, do, teen (monorail, binoculars, tripod).
- **Terms ginne ke 4 niyam:**
  1. Pehle like terms jodo.
  2. Bracket ho to pehle kholo.
  3. Fraction mein numerator ke terms alag ho sakte hain.
  4. Minus sign term ke saath chipka rehta hai.
- Ek hi polynomial ke **do naam** ho sakte hain: terms ke hisaab se aur degree ke hisaab se (jaise quadratic trinomial, cubic binomial).
- Ek variable mein degree n wale polynomial mein **zyada se zyada n + 1 terms** ho sakte hain.
- Do ya zyada variables hon to bhi terms ginne ka tarika wahi hai.
- **Trinomial (3 terms)** aur **cubic (degree 3)** ko mix mat karna.

## Practice Questions

1. Name each polynomial according to the number of terms (monomial, binomial, trinomial, or none of these):
   (a) 5x³
   (b) x - 7
   (c) a² + ab + b²
   (d) 12
   (e) 2p - 3q
   (f) 4m²n
   (g) x³ + x² + x + 1

2. Simplify first, and then classify according to the number of terms:
   (a) 4x + 3x
   (b) 2y² + 5y - 2y²
   (c) 6a - 2a + b - b + 3
   (d) 3(x + 2) - 3x

3. Classify each polynomial according to both the number of terms and the degree (for example, "quadratic trinomial"):
   (a) 6x² - 1
   (b) y³
   (c) z⁴ + z² + 1
   (d) 2t - 9

4. Write one example of each:
   (a) a cubic trinomial in x
   (b) a quadratic monomial in y
   (c) a linear binomial in t
   (d) a biquadratic binomial in z

5. State whether each statement is true or false. Give a reason.
   (a) x² - y² is a binomial.
   (b) 5xyz is a monomial.
   (c) Every trinomial is a quadratic polynomial.
   (d) A cubic polynomial in one variable can have at most 4 terms.
   (e) 3x + 5x is a binomial.

6. A rectangle has length l and breadth b. Write its perimeter as a polynomial and name it according to the number of terms.

7. For what value of k is (k - 4)x³ + 2x² + 5
   (a) a binomial?
   (b) a trinomial?

### Answers

1. (a) **Monomial**  (b) **Binomial**  (c) **Trinomial**  (d) **Monomial**  (e) **Binomial**  (f) **Monomial**  (g) **None of these** (4 terms hain)
2. (a) 4x + 3x = 7x → **monomial**
   (b) 2y² - 2y² = 0, bacha 5y → **monomial**
   (c) 6a - 2a = 4a, b - b = 0, bacha 4a + 3 → **binomial**
   (d) 3x + 6 - 3x = 6 → **monomial** (ek constant)
3. (a) **Quadratic binomial**
   (b) **Cubic monomial**
   (c) **Biquadratic trinomial**
   (d) **Linear binomial**
4. Koi bhi sahi example chalega. Jaise:
   (a) **x³ + 2x - 1**  (b) **7y²**  (c) **3t + 5**  (d) **z⁴ - 1**
5. (a) **True.** Terms hain x² aur -y², yaani 2.
   (b) **True.** 5xyz ek hi term hai, chahe isme 3 letters hain.
   (c) **False.** Trinomial ka matlab 3 terms hai, degree koi bhi ho sakti hai (jaise x³ + x + 1 cubic trinomial hai).
   (d) **True.** Degree 3 mein powers 3, 2, 1, 0 ho sakti hain, yaani zyada se zyada 4 terms.
   (e) **False.** 3x + 5x = 8x, jo **monomial** hai.
6. Perimeter = **2l + 2b**. Isme 2 terms hain, to ye **binomial** hai (aur degree 1, to linear binomial).
7. (a) Binomial tab hoga jab x³ wala term gayab ho: k - 4 = 0, to **k = 4**. Tab polynomial 2x² + 5 ban jata hai (2 terms).
   (b) Trinomial tab hoga jab k - 4 ≠ 0, yaani **k ≠ 4**. Tab x³, x² aur constant, teeno terms rehte hain.

---

Agar sab kuch ek saath yaad rakhna mushkil lag raha ho, to bas ye ek line yaad kar lo: **terms ginne se pehle like terms jodo, phir ginti karo: 1 = mono, 2 = bi, 3 = tri.** Aur degree aur terms ki ginti ko kabhi mix mat karna, dono alag cheezein hain.
