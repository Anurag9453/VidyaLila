# Topic 1: Constants, Variables aur Algebraic Expressions

## Ek auto wale ki kahani

Socho aap auto mein baithte ho. Auto wala bolta hai:

"Meter down hote hi **₹30** lagenge, aur uske baad har kilometre ke **₹12**."

Ab sawal ye hai ki aapko kitna dena padega?

- Agar aap 1 km gaye: 30 + 12 × 1 = ₹42
- Agar aap 5 km gaye: 30 + 12 × 5 = ₹90
- Agar aap 10 km gaye: 30 + 12 × 10 = ₹150

Dhyan do, **₹30 har baar wahi raha**, chahe aap 1 km gaye ya 10 km. Par **kilometre badalte rahe**, aur unke saath kiraya bhi badalta raha.

Ab agar hum kilometre ko ek letter **x** bana dein, to kiraya aise likh sakte hain:

**Kiraya = 30 + 12x**

Bas, aapne abhi abhi apni pehli algebraic expression likh di. Is topic mein hum yahi seekhenge: ye 30 kya hai, ye x kya hai, aur ye poori cheez kya kehlati hai.

## Pehle kuch naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Constant** | Wo number jiski value kabhi nahi badalti | 5, -7, 3/4, π |
| **Variable** | Wo letter jiski value badalti rehti hai | x, y, a, t |
| **Algebraic expression** | Numbers aur letters ko +, -, ×, ÷ se jodkar bana hua phrase | 3x + 5, a² - 2b |
| **Term** | Expression ka ek hissa, jo + ya - se alag hota hai | 3x + 5 mein terms hain 3x aur 5 |
| **Coefficient** | Term mein letter ke saath laga hua number | 7x mein 7 |
| **Like terms** | Aise terms jinke letters aur unki powers bilkul same hon | 3x aur 8x |

## Part A: Constant kya hota hai

**Constant** ka matlab hai wo cheez jo **constant rehti hai, yaani badalti nahi.**

Roz ki zindagi se examples:

- Ek hafte mein hamesha **7** din hote hain. Ye kabhi 8 nahi hoga.
- Ek ghante mein **60** minute hote hain.
- Ek triangle ke teeno angles ka sum hamesha **180°** hota hai.
- Ek dozen mein hamesha **12** cheezein hoti hain.

Maths mein bhi aise hi numbers hote hain:

- 5, 100, -3, 0
- Fractions jaise 3/4, -2/7
- Decimals jaise 0.5, 2.75
- Special numbers jaise π (jo lagbhag 3.14 hai) aur √2

Inki value fix hai. 5 hamesha 5 hi rahega, chahe aap use kahin bhi likho.

### Ek zaroori baat

Kabhi kabhi letters bhi constant hote hain. Jaise **ax + b** mein, a aur b aise letters hain jinki value ek question ke andar fix rehti hai (jaise a = 3, b = 5). Unhe bhi constant maana jata hai. Chinta mat karo, abhi ke liye bas itna yaad rakho ki **constant wo hai jo us question mein badalta nahi.**

---

## Part B: Variable kya hota hai

**Variable** ka matlab hai wo cheez jo **vary karti hai, yaani badalti rehti hai.**

Pehle roz ki zindagi dekho:

- Aaj ka **temperature**: subah 22°C, dopahar 35°C, raat 25°C. Badalta rehta hai.
- Petrol ka **rate**: har kuch hafte mein badal sakta hai.
- Aapki **age**: har saal 1 badh jati hai.
- Cricket match mein **score**: har ball ke saath badal sakta hai.

In sab ko hum letter se likh sakte hain. Jaise temperature ko **t**, age ko **a**, score ko **s**.

Letter kyun use karte hain? Do wajah hain:

1. **Value badalti hai**, to har baar naya number likhne se achha ek letter likh do.
2. **Value abhi pata nahi hai**, to uski jagah ek letter rakh dete hain, jaise ek khali dabba.

Aam taur par hum x, y, z, a, b, p, q, t jaise letters use karte hain, par koi bhi letter ho sakta hai.

### Example se samjho

Ek school ki canteen mein samosa ₹10 ka hai. Aap kitne samose kharidte ho wo badalta rehta hai. Maan lo samose ki sankhya **n** hai:

| Samose (n) | Kul kharcha (10 × n) |
|---|---|
| 1 | ₹10 |
| 3 | ₹30 |
| 7 | ₹70 |
| 12 | ₹120 |

Yahan:
- **10** constant hai (rate fix hai)
- **n** variable hai (sankhya badalti hai)
- **10n** batata hai ki n samose ka kharcha kitna hoga

## Part C: Algebraic Expression kya hai

Jab hum **constants aur variables ko +, -, ×, ÷ ki madad se jodte hain**, to jo banta hai use **algebraic expression** kehte hain.

Auto wale ka kiraya **30 + 12x** ek algebraic expression hai. Aur bhi dekho:

| Expression | Ye kahan kaam aata hai |
|---|---|
| 5x | 5 pens, har pen x rupaye ka. Kul kharcha 5x |
| 2l + 2b | Rectangle ka perimeter (l = length, b = breadth) |
| 3a - 7 | Kisi number ka 3 guna, phir usme se 7 ghatao |
| x² + y² | Do numbers ke squares ka sum |
| a/2 | Kisi cheez ka aadha |
| 4m + 3n | 4 mangoes (m rupaye each) aur 3 oranges (n rupaye each) ka kharcha |

### Expression aur Equation mein farak

Ye ek bahut common confusion hai. Is tarah socho:

- **Expression ek phrase** hai, jaise "Ravi ke paas x aam".
- **Equation ek poora sentence** hai, jaise "Ravi ke paas x aam hain, aur wo 10 hain." Isme **barabar (=)** ka chinh hota hai.

| Ye kya hai? | Kya hai |
|---|---|
| 3x + 5 | Expression (koi = nahi) |
| 3x + 5 = 20 | Equation (= hai, aur ek baat keh raha hai) |
| a² - 4b | Expression |
| a² - 4b = 0 | Equation |

Is chapter mein hum mostly expressions ke saath kaam karenge.

## Part D: Terms aur Coefficient

### Terms

Expression ke wo hisse jo **+ ya - se alag hote hain**, unhe **terms** kehte hain.

**Example 1:** 3x + 5
- Term 1: 3x
- Term 2: 5
- Total 2 terms.

**Example 2:** 4x² - 7x + 2
- Term 1: 4x²
- Term 2: -7x (dhyan do, minus sign term ke saath chipka hota hai)
- Term 3: 2
- Total 3 terms.

**Example 3:** 5xy
- Sirf 1 term hai. Yahan x aur y ke beech koi + ya - nahi hai, sirf multiplication hai.

> Yaad rakho: **+ aur - terms ko alag karte hain.** × aur ÷ ek hi term ke andar hote hain.

### Coefficient

Term mein **letter ke saath jo number lagta hai**, use **coefficient** kehte hain. Ye batata hai ki letter kitni baar hai.

- 7x mein x ka coefficient **7** hai (matlab 7 baar x).
- -3y mein y ka coefficient **-3** hai.
- x mein x ka coefficient **1** hai (kyunki x = 1 × x).
- -a mein a ka coefficient **-1** hai.

Aur ek dilchasp baat. Agar term **5xy** hai, to:
- Sirf number dekho to coefficient **5** hai (ise numerical coefficient kehte hain).
- x ka coefficient **5y** hai (kyunki 5xy = 5y × x).
- y ka coefficient **5x** hai.

### Constant term

Jis term mein **koi variable nahi hota**, use **constant term** kehte hain.

4x² - 7x + **2** mein constant term **2** hai.

## Part E: Like Terms aur Unlike Terms

Socho aapke paas ek tokri mein **3 seb** hain, aur dost ne **5 seb** aur de diye. Ab kitne seb hue? **8 seb.** Aasan hai na?

Par agar aapke paas **3 seb** hain aur dost ne **5 kele** diye, to kya aap keh sakte ho "8 seb-kele"? Nahi! Wo alag alag cheezein hain, unhe jodkar ek naya naam nahi diya ja sakta.

Algebra mein bhi yahi hota hai.

**Like terms** wo terms hain jinke **variables aur unki powers bilkul same hon.** Sirf coefficient alag ho sakta hai.

| Terms | Like ya Unlike? | Kyun? |
|---|---|---|
| 3x aur 8x | Like | Dono mein x (power 1) |
| 5y² aur -2y² | Like | Dono mein y² |
| 4ab aur 7ba | Like | ab aur ba same hain (order se farak nahi padta) |
| 3x aur 3x² | Unlike | Ek mein x, doosre mein x² |
| 2x aur 2y | Unlike | Variable alag hain |
| 5xy aur 5x | Unlike | Ek mein x aur y, doosre mein sirf x |
| 6 aur 6x | Unlike | Ek constant hai, doosre mein variable |

### Like terms ko jodna

Like terms ko jodte waqt **sirf coefficients jodte hain, letter wahi rehta hai.**

- 3x + 5x = **8x** (3 seb + 5 seb = 8 seb)
- 9y - 4y = **5y**
- 7a² - 10a² = **-3a²**
- 2xy + 6xy - xy = **7xy**

Unlike terms ko jod nahi sakte:

- 3x + 5y ko aur chhota nahi kar sakte. Ye 3x + 5y hi rahega.
- 2x + 3x² bhi aise hi rahega.

### Simplify karne ka example

**Simplify karo: 5x² + 3x - 2x² + 4x + 1**

Step 1: Like terms ko pehchano aur saath mein likho.
- x² wale: 5x² aur -2x²
- x wale: 3x aur 4x
- constant: 1

Step 2: Har group ko jodo.
- 5x² - 2x² = 3x²
- 3x + 4x = 7x
- 1 akela hai, to wo 1 hi rahega

**Answer: 3x² + 7x + 1**

## Part F: Expression ke prakar (terms ki sankhya ke hisaab se)

| Naam | Terms | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Monomial** | 1 term (mono = ek) | 5x, 7a²b, -4 |
| **Binomial** | 2 terms (bi = do) | x + 3, 2a - 5b |
| **Trinomial** | 3 terms (tri = teen) | x² + 2x + 1, a + b + c |
| **Multinomial** | 3 se zyada terms | x³ + x² + x + 1 |

Naam yaad rakhne ka ek aasan tarika: **bicycle** mein 2 pahiye, **tricycle** mein 3. Wahi "bi" aur "tri" yahan bhi hai.

**Dhyan rakho:** Terms ginte waqt pehle expression ko simplify karo. Jaise 3x + 5x + 2 dikhne mein trinomial lagta hai, par simplify karne par 8x + 2 ban jata hai, jo ek binomial hai.

## Part G: Shabdon se expression banana

Algebra ka sabse bada fayda yahi hai ki hum kahani ko maths mein badal sakte hain. Kuch phrases ke matlab pehle samajh lo:

| Shabd | Maths mein |
|---|---|
| more than, plus, increased by, sum | + |
| less than, minus, decreased by, difference | - |
| of, times, product, twice (2 ×), thrice (3 ×) | × |
| divided by, quotient, half of (÷ 2) | ÷ |

### Examples

1. **"Ek number x ka 5 guna"** → **5x**
2. **"Ek number x se 7 zyada"** → **x + 7**
3. **"Ek number y se 3 kam"** → **y - 3**
4. **"Ek number n ka aadha"** → **n/2**
5. **"Ek number x ke dugne mein 5 jodo"** → **2x + 5**
6. **"Ek number y ke 4 guna mein se 3 ghatao"** → **4y - 3**
7. **"a aur b ke sum ko 2 se multiply karo"** → **2(a + b)**
8. **"Ek number x ka square, aur uska 3 guna"** → **x² + 3x**

### Dhyan dene wali baat: "less than" ka ulta chakkar

"**x se 3 kam**" likha ho to answer hai **x - 3**.
"**3 less than x**" bhi **x - 3** hai.

Par "**3 minus x**" hoga **3 - x**, jo bilkul alag hai. Kam karne wale number ko hamesha **baad mein** likhte hain.

### Real-life examples

- Ek rectangle ki length l aur breadth b hai. Perimeter = **2(l + b)**, Area = **lb**.
- Ek square ki side s hai. Perimeter = **4s**, Area = **s²**.
- Ravi ki age x saal hai. 5 saal baad uski age = **x + 5**. 3 saal pehle thi = **x - 3**. Uske papa uski age se 25 saal bade hain to papa ki age = **x + 25**.
- Ek notebook ₹n ki hai aur pen ₹p ka. 4 notebook aur 6 pen ka kul kharcha = **4n + 6p**.

## Part H: Expression ki value nikalna

Expression mein variable ki jagah koi number rakhne ko **substitute** karna kehte hain. Phir jo answer aata hai wo expression ki **value** hai.

Vapas auto wale par chalte hain. Kiraya = **30 + 12x**.

- x = 10 ho to: 30 + 12(10) = 30 + 120 = **₹150**
- x = 3 ho to: 30 + 12(3) = 30 + 36 = **₹66**

Aur examples:

**Example 1:** x = 2 par **3x + 5** ki value nikalo.
= 3(2) + 5 = 6 + 5 = **11**

**Example 2:** x = -1 par **x² - 4x + 7** ki value nikalo.
= (-1)² - 4(-1) + 7
= 1 + 4 + 7
= **12**

**Example 3:** a = 2, b = -3 par **2a + 3b** ki value nikalo.
= 2(2) + 3(-3) = 4 - 9 = **-5**

**Example 4:** x = 3, y = 2 par **x² + y² - 2xy** ki value nikalo.
= 9 + 4 - 2(3)(2) = 13 - 12 = **1**

> Tip: Negative number rakhte waqt **hamesha bracket lagao.** x = -1 par x² ko (-1)² likho, -1² nahi. Dono ka answer alag hota hai (pehla 1, doosra -1).

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: 2x ko "2 aur x" samajhna**
2x ka matlab 2 × x hai. Agar x = 5 hai to 2x = **10**, 25 nahi.

**Galti 2: Unlike terms ko jodna**
3x + 5 ≠ 8x. Kyunki 3x variable wala term hai aur 5 constant. Check karo: x = 1 par 3x + 5 = 8, par x = 2 par 3x + 5 = 11, jabki 8x = 16. Alag alag aaya, to ye barabar nahi hain.

**Galti 3: x² aur 2x ko ek jaisa samajhna**
- x² = x × x
- 2x = x + x

Agar x = 4: x² = 16, par 2x = 8.

**Galti 4: xy aur x + y ko ek samajhna**
x = 3, y = 4 par xy = 12, par x + y = 7.

**Galti 5: Term ka sign bhool jana**
x² - 5x + 6 mein doosra term **-5x** hai, 5x nahi. Sign hamesha term ke saath jata hai.

**Galti 6: x ka coefficient 0 samajhna**
x likha ho to uska coefficient **1** hota hai, 0 nahi.

## Quick Recap

- **Constant:** wo number jo badalta nahi (5, -3, π).
- **Variable:** wo letter jiski value badalti hai (x, y, a).
- **Algebraic expression:** constants aur variables ka +, -, ×, ÷ se bana phrase (3x + 5). Isme barabar (=) nahi hota.
- **Term:** + ya - se alag hone wala hissa.
- **Coefficient:** term mein letter ke saath laga number.
- **Like terms:** same letters aur same powers wale terms. Inhi ko jod ya ghata sakte hain.
- **Monomial, Binomial, Trinomial:** 1, 2, 3 terms wale expressions.
- **Value nikalne ke liye:** variable ki jagah number rakho (negative ho to bracket lagao).

# Topic 2: Terms of an Algebraic Expression

Topic 1 mein hum ne dekha tha ki term wo hissa hota hai jo **+ ya - se alag** hota hai, aur coefficient wo number hota hai jo letter ke saath laga hota hai. Ab hum in dono ko **ek level aur gehrai se** samjhenge. Is topic mein hum dekhenge ki ek term ke **andar** kya kya hota hai, aur bracket ya fraction wale expressions mein terms kaise ginte hain.

---

## Ek thali ki kahani

Socho aapke saamne ek thali hai. Usme alag alag katori hain: dal, sabzi, chawal aur ek roti.

- **Thali poori expression** hai.
- **Har katori ek term** hai.
- Katoriyan ek doosre se alag hain, bas yahi "alag alag hona" terms ka kaam hai.

Ab ek katori ke andar jhaank ke dekho. Maan lo sabzi ki katori mein aloo, matar aur masale mile hue hain. Wo sab **mil kar ek hi katori** (ek hi term) bana rahe hain. Unhe alag nahi kiya ja sakta.

Isi tarah, ek term ke andar jo cheezein **multiply** hoti hain, unhe us term ke **factors** kehte hain.

Ye ek line yaad rakho:

> **Terms ko + aur - alag karte hain. Factors ko × jodta hai.**

---

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Factor** | Term ke andar jo cheezein aapas mein multiply ho rahi hain | 5xy mein factors 5, x, y |
| **Numerical factor** | Factors mein jo number hai | 5xy mein **5** |
| **Literal factor** | Factors mein jo letters hain (literal = letter wala) | 5xy mein **x aur y** |
| **Numerical coefficient** | Term ka number wala hissa (numerical factor hi) | 5xy mein **5** |
| **Literal coefficient** | Term ka letters wala hissa | 5xy mein **xy** |

---

## Part A: Term ke andar ke factors

Kisi bhi term ko aap **multiplication ki shakal** mein tod sakte ho.

**Example 1:** Term **5xy**
5xy = 5 × x × y
Factors: **5, x, y**

**Example 2:** Term **4x²y**
x² ka matlab x × x hai, to
4x²y = 4 × x × x × y
Factors: **4, x, x, y**

**Example 3:** Term **-7ab²c**
b² = b × b, to
-7ab²c = (-7) × a × b × b × c
Factors: **-7, a, b, b, c**

Dhyan do, minus sign **number ke saath** chipka hota hai. Isliye numerical factor **-7** hai, 7 nahi.

**Example 4:** Term **x**
x = 1 × x
Factors: **1, x**

### Numerical aur Literal factors alag karna

| Term | Numerical factor | Literal factors |
|---|---|---|
| 6a | 6 | a |
| -3pq | -3 | p, q |
| 8x²y | 8 | x, x, y |
| -z | -1 | z |
| (2/5)mn | 2/5 | m, n |
| 9 | 9 | koi nahi |

Aakhri row dekho: constant term 9 mein koi letter nahi hota, isliye uska sirf numerical factor hai.

---

## Part B: Coefficient ko gehrai se samjho

Topic 1 mein hum ne dekha tha ki 5xy mein x ka coefficient 5y hota hai. Ab iska pura rule samajhte hain.

> **Kisi bhi factor ka coefficient = term ke baaki saare factors ka product.**

Yaani us factor ko "hata do", jo bacha wahi uska coefficient hai.

### Term 9x²yz ke saare coefficients

| Kiska coefficient? | Hatao | Jo bacha (coefficient) |
|---|---|---|
| Numerical coefficient | letters | **9** |
| x² ka | 9, y, z | **9yz** |
| y ka | 9, x², z | **9x²z** |
| z ka | 9, x², y | **9x²y** |
| yz ka | 9, x² | **9x²** |
| x²y ka | 9, z | **9z** |

Aap dekh sakte ho ki **ek hi term ke kai coefficient** ho sakte hain, ye depend karta hai ki aap kiske baare mein pooch rahe ho.

### Jab term mein fraction ho

- **(3/5)pq** mein numerical coefficient **3/5** hai.
- **-x²y/3** ko likho -(1/3) × x²y, to numerical coefficient **-1/3** hai.
- **y/4** ko (1/4) × y likh sakte ho, to coefficient **1/4** hai.

### Jab term mein π ho

π bhi ek number hai (lagbhag 3.14). Isliye **2πr** mein numerical coefficient **2π** hai aur literal factor **r** hai. Circle ki circumference isi shakal ki hoti hai, C = 2πr.

---

## Part C: Terms ginna jab bracket ya fraction ho

Yahan sabse zyada galtiyan hoti hain, isliye dhyan se padho.

### Rule 1: Bracket ke andar ke + / - alag term nahi banate

Jab tak bracket **khola nahi gaya**, bracket wala poora hissa ek term maana jata hai.

**Example:** 2(x + y) - 5

Jaise likha hai:
- Term 1: **2(x + y)**
- Term 2: **-5**
- Total **2 terms**.

Par agar bracket khol do to:
2(x + y) - 5 = 2x + 2y - 5
- Terms: 2x, 2y, -5
- Total **3 terms**.

Isliye jab question mein terms ginne ko kaha jaye, to pehle dekho ki expression **simplified form** mein chahiye ya jaisa diya hai waisa. Aam taur par expression ko pehle bracket kholkar likha jata hai.

### Rule 2: Fraction mein numerator ke + / - ko dhyan se dekho

- **x/2 + y/3**: Ye **2 terms** hain (x/2 aur y/3).
- **(x + y)/2**: Jaise likha hai, ye ek hi bada term hai. Par isko likh sakte ho x/2 + y/2, to ye **2 terms** ban jate hain.

### Rule 3: Multiplication ya power ke andar ke terms nahi ginte

- **3x²y** → 1 term (sirf multiplication hai).
- **(x + 1)²** → jaise likha hai ek term hai. Kholne par x² + 2x + 1 banega, jisme 3 terms hain. (Ye kholna hum aage ke topics mein seekhenge.)

### Terms ginne ka quick check

| Expression | Terms | Kitne |
|---|---|---|
| 5x² - 3xy + 2y² - 7 | 5x², -3xy, 2y², -7 | 4 |
| a/2 - b/3 | a/2, -b/3 | 2 |
| 6mn | 6mn | 1 |
| 4p + 3(q - r) | 4p, 3(q - r) | 2 (jaise likha hai) |
| 4p + 3q - 3r | 4p, 3q, -3r | 3 (khulne ke baad) |

## Part D: Term sign ke saath hi aata hai

Ye Topic 1 mein bhi aaya tha, par ye itna zaroori hai ki dobara ek chhote example ke saath dekhte hain.

Expression: **8a - 3b + 5**

| Hum kya bolte hain | Sahi ya galat? |
|---|---|
| Terms hain 8a, 3b, 5 | Galat (b ke aage minus hai) |
| Terms hain 8a, **-3b**, 5 | Sahi |

Isliye b wale term ka coefficient **-3** hoga. Isse ye fark padta hai jab hum value nikalte hain ya like terms jodte hain.

**Example:** 8a - 3b + 5 mein se -3b wale term ko lekar 2b jodo.
-3b + 2b = **-b**

Agar aap galti se +3b le lete, to answer 5b aata, jo galat hai.

## Part E: Terms ko arrange karna

Jab expression mein bahut saare terms ho, to unhe ek kram mein likhna padhne mein aasan karta hai. Do tarike hote hain:

- **Descending order:** x ki sabse badi power pehle, phir kam hoti hui. Ye zyada use hota hai.
- **Ascending order:** x ki sabse chhoti power pehle, phir badhti hui.

**Example:** Expression **5 + 2x³ - x + 4x²**

Pehle har term ko dekho: constant 5, x³ wala 2x³, x wala -x, x² wala 4x².

**Descending order (power ghatti hui):**
2x³ + 4x² - x + 5

**Ascending order (power badhti hui):**
5 - x + 4x² + 2x³

Dhyan rakho ki **term ke saath uska sign bhi jata hai.** -x ko uthate waqt minus sign saath rakhna hai.

## Part F: Real-life mein terms aur factors

Ek shop ka bill dekho:

- 4 notebooks, har ek ₹n ka
- 3 pens, har ek ₹p ka
- 1 school bag, ₹250 ka

Kul bill = **4n + 3p + 250**

| Term | Kya dikha raha hai | Factors |
|---|---|---|
| 4n | Notebooks ka kharcha | 4 (kitni), n (rate) |
| 3p | Pens ka kharcha | 3 (kitne), p (rate) |
| 250 | Bag ka kharcha | sirf number |

Yahan har item ka alag kharcha ek **term** hai, aur har kharche ke andar "kitni cheezein" × "ek ka rate" ek term ke **factors** hain.

Ek aur example. Rectangle ka area aur perimeter jodna ho:
**lb + 2l + 2b**
- Terms: lb, 2l, 2b (3 terms)
- lb ke factors: l aur b
- 2l ke factors: 2 aur l

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Terms aur factors ko mix karna**
3x + 5 mein 3x aur 5 **terms** hain. 3x ke andar 3 aur x **factors** hain. Terms ko + / - alag karte hain, factors ko × jodta hai.

**Galti 2: Minus sign chhod dena**
x² - 4x + 1 mein doosra term -4x hai, 4x nahi.

**Galti 3: Bracket ke andar ke terms ko alag gin lena**
3(a + b) jaise likha hai ek term hai. Bracket kholne ke baad hi 3a aur 3b alag terms bante hain.

**Galti 4: Coefficient sirf number samajhna**
"Coefficient of x in 5xy" ka answer 5 nahi, **5y** hai. Sirf numerical coefficient pooche to hi 5.

**Galti 5: Variable ka coefficient 0 samajhna**
Agar sirf x likha ho to x ka coefficient **1** hota hai. -x ka coefficient **-1** hota hai.

**Galti 6: Constant term mein factor na dhundna**
Constant term 7 ka bhi numerical factor 7 hota hai. Bas isme koi letter factor nahi hota.

---

## Quick Recap

- **Term** ko + aur - alag karte hain. **Factors** ko × jodta hai.
- Term ke andar ka number **numerical factor** (ya numerical coefficient) kehlata hai, aur letters **literal factors**.
- **Kisi factor ka coefficient** = term ke baaki saare factors ka product.
- Sign term ke saath chipka hota hai (-3b ek term hai, 3b nahi).
- Bracket khulne se pehle bracket wala hissa ek term hota hai. Khulne ke baad naye terms bante hain.
- x/2 + y/3 mein 2 terms hain, par (x + y)/2 jaise likha hai ek term hai.
- Terms ko descending ya ascending order mein arrange kar sakte hain (sign ke saath).

## Practice Questions

1. Write the terms of each expression:
   (a) 7a - 3b + 4
   (b) x²y - 2xy² + y³ - 1

2. Write the numerical factor and the literal factors of each term:
   (a) -6pq²
   (b) (2/3)x³y

3. In the term 9x²yz, write:
   (a) the numerical coefficient
   (b) the coefficient of x²
   (c) the coefficient of yz
   (d) the coefficient of x²y

4. How many terms does the expression 2(a + b) - 3 have
   (a) as it is written?
   (b) after removing the bracket?

5. A shopkeeper sells 4 pens at ₹p each, 3 books at ₹b each and one bag for ₹10.
   (a) Write the total cost as an algebraic expression.
   (b) Write its terms.
   (c) What is the coefficient of b?

6. Which of the following are terms of the expression 5x - 2y + 7?
   5x, 2y, -2y, 7, x

7. Arrange the terms of 5 + 2x³ - x + 4x² in descending powers of x.

8. Write the numerical coefficient of each:
   (a) -x²y/3
   (b) 2πr
   (c) -z

### Answers

1. (a) Terms: **7a, -3b, 4**
   (b) Terms: **x²y, -2xy², y³, -1**
2. (a) Numerical factor **-6**, literal factors **p, q, q**
   (b) Numerical factor **2/3**, literal factors **x, x, x, y**
3. (a) **9**  (b) **9yz**  (c) **9x²**  (d) **9z**
4. (a) **2 terms** (2(a + b) aur -3)
   (b) 2a + 2b - 3 ban jata hai, to **3 terms**
5. (a) **4p + 3b + 10**
   (b) Terms: **4p, 3b, 10**
   (c) **3**
6. Terms hain **5x, -2y, 7**. (2y term nahi hai kyunki term mein minus sign saath aata hai, aur x pura term nahi hai.)
7. **2x³ + 4x² - x + 5**
8. (a) **-1/3**  (b) **2π**  (c) **-1**
