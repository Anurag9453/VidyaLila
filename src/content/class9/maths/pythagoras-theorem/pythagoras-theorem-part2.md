# Topic 2: Pythagoras Theorem ka Upyog

Topic 1 mein Nikhil ke 43-inch TV se humne Pythagoras theorem ko samjha, 3-4-5 ki squares wali picture (9 + 16 = 25) dekhi, aur Aditi ke (a + b)² wale bade square se use sach hote hue bhi dekha: right triangle mein **hypotenuse ka square = baaki do sides ke squares ka jod**. Ab tak wo ek "sundar baat" thi. Is topic mein wo ek **kaam ka aujaar (tool)** ban jayegi. Jab bhi right triangle mein do sides pata hon, teesri side nikaalni ho, hum yahi use karenge. Aur dhyan se dekho to duniya mein right triangles har jagah hain: deewar aur zameen, screen, field, seedhi.

## Balwant ji ki seedhi

School mein fire drill chal rahi hai. Fireman Balwant ji ek lambi seedhi (ladder) lekar aaye hain. Unhe building ke **second-floor ki khidki** tak pahunchna hai, jo zameen se **8 metre** ooper hai. Seedhi **10 metre** lambi hai.

Class 9 ki Parul paas khadi dekh rahi hai. Wo poochti hai, "Balwant ji, seedhi ka neeche wala sira deewar se kitni door rakhna hai?"

Balwant ji muskurate hain. "Beta, ye to roz ka kaam hai. Zyada paas rakhi to seedhi seedhi khadi ho jaati hai aur palat sakti hai. Zyada door rakhi to khidki tak pahunchegi hi nahi."

Parul ne zameen par chitra banaya: deewar seedhi khadi, zameen sidhi, aur seedhi tirchhi. Ye ek **right triangle** hai!
- Deewar (8 m) aur zameen (x m): ye do **legs** hain, jo right angle banate hain.
- Seedhi (10 m): ye **hypotenuse** hai, kyunki wo right angle ke saamne hai.

Parul ne Topic 1 wala formula lagaya:

x² + 8² = 10²
x² + 64 = 100
x² = 100 − 64 = 36
x = √36 = **6 metre**

"Seedhi ka paer deewar se 6 metre door rakhna hai," Parul ne kaha. Balwant ji hans pade, "Bilkul sahi. Hum bhi yahi rakhte hain."

**Jaanch:** 6² + 8² = 36 + 64 = 100 = 10² ✅

Is topic mein Parul ki yahi aadat seekhenge: **right triangle pehchano, hypotenuse dhoondho, formula lagao**. Aage ek aisi seedhi ka sawaal bhi aayega jiska paer sarakne lagta hai.

## Part A: Hypotenuse nikalna

Jab **dono legs pata hon** aur hypotenuse chahiye:

> **c² = a² + b²**, isliye **c = √(a² + b²)**

Steps:
1. Dono legs ke square likho.
2. Jodo.
3. **Square root** lo. (Yahi aakhri step log bhoolte hain.)

### Example 1: Legs 9 aur 12

c² = 9² + 12² = 81 + 144 = 225
c = √225 = **15**

**Jaanch:** 9² + 12² = 225 = 15² ✅

### Example 2: Legs 5 aur 5 (surd wala jawab)

c² = 5² + 5² = 25 + 25 = 50
c = √50

50 poora square nahi hai. Par 50 = 25 × 2, aur 25 poora square hai. Surds simplify karne ka tareeka [Number Systems Part 4](../number-systems/number-systems-part4.md) mein dekha tha:

√50 = √25 × √2 = **5√2**

Decimal mein: 5√2 ≈ 5 × 1.414 = **7.07** (lagbhag).

**Jaanch:** (5√2)² = 25 × 2 = 50 = 25 + 25 ✅

### Example 3: Legs 1 aur 2

c² = 1² + 2² = 1 + 4 = 5
c = **√5** ≈ 2.236

√5 aur simplify nahi hota (5 mein koi perfect square factor nahi hai). Aise hi chhod do.

**Jaanch:** (√5)² = 5 = 1 + 4 ✅

---

## Part B: Ek leg nikalna

Ab **hypotenuse aur ek leg pata hai**, doosra leg chahiye. Balwant ji wala sawaal yahi tha.

> **a² + b² = c²** se **a² = c² − b²**, isliye **a = √(c² − b²)**

**Zaroori:** minus **hamesha bade (hypotenuse) ke square mein se** hota hai: c² − b². Ulta nahi.

### Example 4: Hypotenuse 17, ek leg 8

a² = 17² − 8² = 289 − 64 = 225
a = √225 = **15**

**Jaanch:** 15² + 8² = 225 + 64 = 289 = 17² ✅

### Example 5: Hypotenuse 9, ek leg 7 (surd wala jawab)

a² = 9² − 7² = 81 − 49 = 32
a = √32 = √16 × √2 = **4√2** ≈ 4 × 1.414 = **5.66**

**Jaanch:** (4√2)² = 16 × 2 = 32, aur 32 + 49 = 81 = 9² ✅

### Example 6: Hypotenuse 13, ek leg 7

a² = 13² − 7² = 169 − 49 = 120
a = √120 = √4 × √30 = **2√30** ≈ 2 × 5.477 = **10.95**

**Jaanch:** (2√30)² = 4 × 30 = 120, aur 120 + 49 = 169 = 13² ✅

## Part C: Pythagorean triplets

Balwant ji ki seedhi mein 6, 8, 10 aaya, aur Topic 1 mein 3, 4, 5. Ye teeno numbers poore (whole) hain, koi surd nahi. Aise teen numbers ko **Pythagorean triplet** kehte hain.

### Common triplets

| a | b | c | Jaanch |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 4 | 5 | 9 + 16 = 25 ✅ |
| 5 | 12 | 13 | 25 + 144 = 169 ✅ |
| 8 | 15 | 17 | 64 + 225 = 289 ✅ |
| 7 | 24 | 25 | 49 + 576 = 625 ✅ |
| 20 | 21 | 29 | 400 + 441 = 841 ✅ |

Inhe yaad rakhna bahut kaam aata hai: sawaal mein dikhte hi jawab mil jaata hai.

### Multiples bhi triplet hote hain

Kisi triplet ke teeno numbers ko **same number se multiply** kar do, to naya triplet milta hai.

- 3-4-5 ko 2 se: **6-8-10** (36 + 64 = 100 ✅). Yahi Balwant ji ki seedhi thi.
- 3-4-5 ko 3 se: **9-12-15** (81 + 144 = 225 ✅). Example 1 wala.
- 5-12-13 ko 2 se: **10-24-26** (100 + 576 = 676 = 26² ✅)

**Kyun?** Agar a² + b² = c², to (ka)² + (kb)² = k²(a² + b²) = k²c² = (kc)². Yaani triangle ko sirf "bada" kar rahe hain, right angle waisa ka waisa rehta hai.

### Triplets banane ka formula

Koi bhi whole number m lo jo **1 se bada** ho. Tab:

> **(2m, m² − 1, m² + 1)** ek Pythagorean triplet hai.

Check karte hain:

| m | 2m | m² − 1 | m² + 1 | Jaanch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 4 | 3 | 5 | 16 + 9 = 25 ✅ |
| 3 | 6 | 8 | 10 | 36 + 64 = 100 ✅ |
| 4 | 8 | 15 | 17 | 64 + 225 = 289 ✅ |

**Ye hamesha kyun kaam karta hai?** Square karke dekho:
(2m)² + (m² − 1)² = 4m² + m⁴ − 2m² + 1 = m⁴ + 2m² + 1 = (m² + 1)² ✅

Ek aur: m = 5 se (10, 24, 26) aur m = 6 se (12, 35, 37) milte hain. Check: 144 + 1225 = 1369 = 37² ✅

**Ek baat:** ye formula **saare** triplets nahi deta. Jaise 20-21-29 isse seedha nahi milta. Par jitne bhi chaaho, naye triplets bana sakte ho.

## Part D: Rozmarra ke sawaal

Har sawaal mein pehle ek chhota chitra banao, right angle pehchano, hypotenuse kaun si hai wo tay karo, phir formula.

### Seedhi aur deewar

**Example 7:** 13 m ki seedhi deewar se lagi hai, paer deewar se 5 m door hai. Seedhi deewar par kitni ooncha pahunchti hai?

Seedhi hypotenuse hai (13), zameen wala leg 5, deewar wala leg h.
h² = 13² − 5² = 169 − 25 = 144
h = **12 m**

**Jaanch:** 5² + 12² = 25 + 144 = 169 = 13² ✅

**Example 8 (paer sarakne wala sawaal):** 25 m ki seedhi deewar se lagi hai, paer deewar se 7 m door hai. Seedhi ka **ooper wala sira 4 m neeche** sarak jaye, to **paer kitna bahar** sarakega?

*Pehle ki sthiti:* ooncha = √(25² − 7²) = √(625 − 49) = √576 = **24 m**.
*Sarakne ke baad:* ooncha = 24 − 4 = **20 m**.
Naya paer deewar se: √(25² − 20²) = √(625 − 400) = √225 = **15 m**.
Paer sarka = 15 − 7 = **8 m**.

Dhyan do: ooper wala sira 4 m neeche aaya, par paer 8 m bahar gaya. Dono alag-alag right triangles hain (7-24-25 aur 15-20-25), isliye dono ko alag-alag solve karna padta hai.

**Jaanch:** 15² + 20² = 225 + 400 = 625 = 25² ✅ aur 7² + 24² = 49 + 576 = 625 ✅

### TV ya phone screen

TV/phone ki size uska **diagonal** hota hai (inch mein), jaise Topic 1 mein Nikhil ke TV ke 43 inch. Screen ek rectangle hai, to diagonal hypotenuse hai.

**Example 9:** Ek 40 inch ke TV ki screen ki height 24 inch hai. Width kitni hai?
w² = 40² − 24² = 1600 − 576 = 1024
w = √1024 = **32 inch**

**Jaanch:** 32² + 24² = 1024 + 576 = 1600 = 40² ✅ (Ye 8 × (4, 3, 5) ka triplet hai: 32-24-40.)

**Example 10:** Ek phone ki screen 2.5 inch chaudi aur 6 inch lambi hai. Diagonal (phone ki size)?
d² = 2.5² + 6² = 6.25 + 36 = 42.25, d = **6.5 inch** (kyunki 6.5 × 6.5 = 42.25).

### Rectangle ka diagonal

**Example 11:** Ek field 40 m lambi aur 30 m chaudi hai. Ek kone se saamne wale kone tak seedha jaane mein kitna chalna padega?
d² = 40² + 30² = 1600 + 900 = 2500, d = **50 m**
Kinare-kinare chalo to 40 + 30 = 70 m. Seedha chalne se **20 m** ki bachat!

**Example 12:** Ek rectangle ka diagonal 26 cm hai aur ek side 24 cm hai. Doosri side, perimeter, area?
b² = 26² − 24² = 676 − 576 = 100, b = **10 cm**
Perimeter = 2(24 + 10) = **68 cm**, Area = 24 × 10 = **240 cm²**
**Jaanch:** 24² + 10² = 576 + 100 = 676 = 26² ✅

### Uttar phir poorv: seedhe wapas ka raasta

**Example 13:** Ek aadmi ghar se **9 km uttar** chala, phir **12 km poorv**. Ghar wapas seedha jaane ka sabse chhota raasta?
Uttar aur poorv ke beech **90°** ka angle hai, to right triangle banta hai.
d² = 9² + 12² = 81 + 144 = 225, d = **15 km**
(Ghoom ke aane mein 21 km lagta, seedha sirf 15 km.)

**Example 14:** Ek ladki 20 m pashchim chali, phir 21 m dakshin. Seedhi doori?
d² = 400 + 441 = 841, d = **29 m** (20-21-29 triplet)

### Khambe ya ped ka support wire

**Example 15:** Ek khambe ke sire se ek wire zameen par bandhi hai. Wire 15 m lambi hai aur khambe ke neeche se 9 m door bandhi hai. Khamba kitna ooncha hai?
h² = 15² − 9² = 225 − 81 = 144, h = **12 m**
**Jaanch:** 9² + 12² = 81 + 144 = 225 = 15² ✅

**Example 16:** Ek 24 m ooncha khamba hai aur wire 26 m lambi hai. Wire ka neeche wala sira khambe se kitna door bandhna hoga?
x² = 26² − 24² = 676 − 576 = 100, x = **10 m**

### Equilateral triangle ki height

Mensuration mein humne equilateral triangle ki height Pythagoras se hi nikaali thi: [../mensuration/mensuration.md](../mensuration/mensuration.md). Wahi baat yahan phir:

Side a ke triangle mein ooper se neeche ki height base ko aadha (a/2) karti hai. Right triangle: hypotenuse a, ek leg a/2.

h² = a² − (a/2)² = a² − a²/4 = 3a²/4, isliye **h = (√3/2)a**

**Example 17:** Side 10 cm.
h = (√3/2) × 10 = **5√3 ≈ 8.66 cm**
**Jaanch (seedha Pythagoras):** 10² − 5² = 100 − 25 = 75, √75 = √25 × √3 = 5√3 ✅

**Example 18:** Tile equilateral triangle jaisi hai, side 12 cm. Height aur area?
h = (√3/2) × 12 = **6√3 ≈ 10.39 cm**
Area = ½ × 12 × 6√3 = **36√3 cm²**
**Jaanch:** (√3/4) × 12² = (√3/4) × 144 = 36√3 ✅

### Rhombus aur uske diagonals

Rhombus ke diagonals ek doosre ko **90° par aadha-aadha** kaat te hain. Isliye 4 chhote right triangles bante hain, jinka hypotenuse rhombus ki side hai.

**Example 19:** Rhombus ke diagonals 16 cm aur 30 cm hain. Side?
Aadhe diagonals: 8 aur 15.
side² = 8² + 15² = 64 + 225 = 289, side = **17 cm**
Perimeter = 4 × 17 = **68 cm**. Area = ½ × 16 × 30 = **240 cm²**

**Example 20:** Rhombus ki side 13 cm hai aur ek diagonal 24 cm hai. Doosra diagonal?
Aadha diagonal = 12. Doosra aadha: x² = 13² − 12² = 169 − 144 = 25, x = 5. Poora diagonal = **10 cm**.
**Jaanch:** 12² + 5² = 144 + 25 = 169 = 13² ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

### 1. Minus galat order mein

Leg nikalte waqt **bade square mein se chhota** minus karo.
- Galat: Hypotenuse 13, leg 5: 5² − 13² = 25 − 169 = −144. Negative square aa gaya, ye ho hi nahi sakta.
- Sahi: 13² − 5² = 169 − 25 = 144, to leg = **12**

### 2. Square root lena bhool jana

- Galat: Legs 6 aur 8: c² = 36 + 64 = 100, "to c = 100".
- Sahi: c² = 100 to c = √100 = **10**

Jaanch tarika: 100 metre ki seedhi to bahut hi lambi hoti, jabki legs sirf 6 aur 8 the. Hypotenuse legs se lambi hoti hai, par itni nahi.

### 3. Surd ko simplify na karna

- Galat: c = √72 ko wahi chhod dena.
- Sahi: √72 = √36 × √2 = **6√2** ≈ 6 × 1.414 = **8.49**

Jawab sahi hota hai, par simplify karna zaroori hai taaki jawab saaf dikhe.

### 4. Galat side ko hypotenuse maan lena

Triangle ke sides 7, 24 aur 25 hain, aur 7 aur 25 diye hain, 24 poochna hai.
- Galat: 7² + 25² = 49 + 625 = 674. (25 hypotenuse hai, use jodna nahi tha!)
- Sahi: Hypotenuse sabse lambi side hai, right angle ke saamne: **25**. To 24² = 25² − 7² = 625 − 49 = 576, **24**

### 5. Har triangle par formula laga dena

c² = a² + b² sirf **right triangle** ke liye hai. Agar sawaal mein right angle ki baat hi nahi hai, to ye mat lagao. (Ye ulti baat, yaani "agar sides aisi hon to kya triangle right hota hai?", aage dekhenge.)

## Quick Recap

- Hypotenuse: **c = √(a² + b²)** (squares **jodo**, phir root lo).
- Leg: **a = √(c² − b²)** (hypotenuse ke square mein se **minus**, phir root).
- Jawab surd ho sakta hai: √50 = 5√2, √32 = 4√2. Simplify karo.
- Pythagorean triplets: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25, 20-21-29. Inke multiples bhi triplets hain.
- m > 1 ke liye (2m, m² − 1, m² + 1) triplet deta hai (m = 2, 3, 4: 4-3-5, 6-8-10, 8-15-17).
- Seedhi, screen, rectangle ka diagonal, raasta, wire, equilateral triangle ki height (√3/2)a, rhombus: sab right triangles hain.
- Hypotenuse hamesha right angle ke saamne aur sabse lambi side hai.

## Practice Questions

1. The two legs of a right triangle are 24 cm and 7 cm. Find the hypotenuse.
2. The hypotenuse of a right triangle is 29 cm and one leg is 20 cm. Find the other leg.
3. Find the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs 4 cm and 6 cm. Give the answer as a simplified surd.
4. A right triangle has hypotenuse 12 cm and one leg 8 cm. Find the other leg as a simplified surd.
5. A 17 m ladder reaches a window that is 15 m above the ground. How far is the foot of the ladder from the wall?
6. The foot of a 10 m ladder is 6 m from a wall. The foot is pulled 2 m further away from the wall. How far does the top of the ladder slide down?
7. The diagonal of a rectangle is 25 cm and one side is 7 cm. Find the other side and the perimeter.
8. The diagonals of a rhombus are 10 cm and 24 cm. Find its side and perimeter.
9. Find the height of an equilateral triangle of side 14 cm. Give the answer in surd form and approximately.
10. Use the formula (2m, m² − 1, m² + 1) with m = 7 to make a triplet, and check it.
11. Which of these are Pythagorean triplets? (a) 33, 56, 65 (b) 6, 9, 11 (c) 12, 16, 20 (d) 9, 12, 16
12. A girl walks 15 m south and then 8 m east. What is the shortest distance back to her starting point?
13. True or false? (a) c² = a² + b² works for every triangle. (b) Doubling a Pythagorean triplet gives another triplet. (c) The hypotenuse is always the longest side of a right triangle. (d) √(a² + b²) = a + b.

### Answers

1. c² = 24² + 7² = 576 + 49 = 625, c = **25 cm**.
   Jaanch: 7² + 24² = 625 = 25² ✅

2. b² = 29² − 20² = 841 − 400 = 441, b = **21 cm**.
   Jaanch: 20² + 21² = 400 + 441 = 841 ✅

3. c² = 16 + 36 = 52, c = √52 = √4 × √13 = **2√13 cm** ≈ 7.21 cm.
   Jaanch: (2√13)² = 4 × 13 = 52 ✅

4. b² = 144 − 64 = 80, b = √80 = √16 × √5 = **4√5 cm** ≈ 8.94 cm.
   Jaanch: 80 + 64 = 144 ✅

5. x² = 17² − 15² = 289 − 225 = 64, x = **8 m**.
   Jaanch: 8² + 15² = 64 + 225 = 289 ✅

6. Pehle ooncha = √(10² − 6²) = √64 = 8 m. Paer 6 + 2 = 8 m door hone par ooncha = √(10² − 8²) = √36 = 6 m. Top **2 m** neeche sarka.
   Jaanch: 8² + 6² = 100 ✅

7. Doosri side² = 625 − 49 = 576, side = **24 cm**. Perimeter = 2(24 + 7) = **62 cm**.
   Jaanch: 7² + 24² = 625 ✅

8. Aadhe diagonals 5 aur 12. Side² = 25 + 144 = 169, side = **13 cm**. Perimeter = 4 × 13 = **52 cm**.
   Jaanch: 5² + 12² = 13² ✅

9. h = (√3/2) × 14 = **7√3 cm** ≈ 7 × 1.732 = **12.12 cm**.
   Jaanch: 14² − 7² = 196 − 49 = 147 = 49 × 3, √147 = 7√3 ✅

10. m = 7: 2m = 14, m² − 1 = 48, m² + 1 = 50. Triplet **(14, 48, 50)**.
    Jaanch: 14² + 48² = 196 + 2304 = 2500 = 50² ✅ (ye 7-24-25 ka double hai.)

11. (a) 1089 + 3136 = 4225 = 65², **haan** ✅. (b) 36 + 81 = 117, par 11² = 121, **nahi** ❌. (c) 144 + 256 = 400 = 20², **haan** ✅ (4 × 3-4-5). (d) 81 + 144 = 225, par 16² = 256, **nahi** ❌ (sabse lambi side 16 hai, 225 ka root 15 hota).

12. d² = 15² + 8² = 225 + 64 = 289, d = **17 m**.
    Jaanch: 8-15-17 triplet ✅

13. (a) **False** ❌. Sirf right triangle ke liye.
    (b) **True** ✅. k² wali baat se.
    (c) **True** ✅. Hypotenuse sabse lambi hoti hai.
    (d) **False** ❌. Jaise a = 3, b = 4: √25 = 5, par 3 + 4 = 7.
