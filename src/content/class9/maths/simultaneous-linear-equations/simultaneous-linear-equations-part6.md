# Topic 6: Word Problems I: Numbers, Umar, Fractions aur Digits

Ab tak aapne do equations ko solve karne ke saare tareeke seekh liye: substitution, elimination, cross-multiplication, graph se dekhna, aur Topic 5 mein Mystery Box ki chits (2/x + 3/y = 13) jaisi 1/x wali equations, jinhe u = 1/x, v = 1/y rakh kar seedha bana liya tha. Ab tak equations hamesha **tayyar** mili thi. Asli zindagi mein koi equation likh kar nahi deta; sawaal **shabdon mein** aata hai: "do numbers ka jod 50 hai...".

Is topic mein hum wahi seekhenge jo sabse zaroori hai: **shabdon ko equations mein badalna**. Solve karna aap pehle se jaante ho. Yahan naya kaam hai ek sawaal ko padhna, aur usse do equations nikaalna.

## Sonpur mela ka paheli stall

Sonpur ke mela mein ek stall ke upar bada sa board laga tha: **"Paheli suljhao, patang jeet kar le jao!"** Stall Ramdin chacha ka tha. Tejas aur uski chhoti behen Bani wahan pahunche.

Ramdin chacha ne ek parchi padhi: "Do numbers hain. Dono ka **jod 50** hai, aur dono ka **antar 12** hai. Batao, dono number kaun se hain?"

Tejas ne turant guess lagana shuru kiya: "25 aur 25?" Jod 50 hai, par antar 0 hai. "30 aur 20?" Jod 50, antar 10. "32 aur 18?" Jod 50, antar 14. Bani hans padi: "Bhaiya, aap andhere mein teer chala rahe ho."

Bani ne parchi ke peeche pencil se likha:

- bada number = x, chhota number = y
- x + y = 50
- x − y = 12

"Ye to hum Topic 2 mein kar chuke hain. Dono equations jodo: 2x = 62, to x = 31. Phir y = 19." Ramdin chacha ne 31 + 19 = 50 aur 31 − 19 = 12 jaanch kar patang Bani ko thama diya.

Bani ne koi nayi maths nahi lagayi. Usne bas **do kaam** kiye: pehle batayi ki x aur y kya hain, phir har vaakya ko ek equation bana diya. Is topic mein hum yahi aadat pakki karenge.

## Part A: Word problem ke 4 kadam

Har word problem ke liye wahi chaar kadam, chahe sawaal kitna bhi lamba ho:

> **Kadam 1: Variables chuno.** Saaf likho ki x kya hai aur y kya hai ("let x = ...").
> **Kadam 2: Har vaakya ko ek equation bana do.** Do anjaan cheezein hain, to do alag information chahiye, yaani do equations.
> **Kadam 3: Solve karo.** Substitution, elimination ya cross-multiplication, jo aapko aasaan lage.
> **Kadam 4: Jawab ko ASLI shabdon se jaancho.** Apni banayi equation mein nahi, **sawaal ke original vaakyon mein** daal kar dekho.

Kadam 4 sabse zyada chhoda jaata hai, par yahi pakadta hai ki aapne kahin vaakya galat to nahi padha. Agar equation hi galat bani ho, to equation mein check karne par jawab "theek" dikhega, par sawaal ke shabdon mein galat nikalega.

### Phrase se maths: translation table

| Shabdon mein | Maths mein | Dhyan do |
|---|---|---|
| x aur y ka **sum** (jod) | x + y | |
| x aur y ka **difference** (antar) | x − y | Bada − chhota, jo bada ho wo pehle |
| x ka **twice** (do guna) | 2x | |
| x ka **3 guna** | 3x | |
| x se **5 more than** | x + 5 | |
| x se **5 less than** | x − 5 | |
| x, y se **5 zyada** hai | x = y + 5 | "x" wala hissa bada hai |
| **3 more than twice** y | 2y + 3 | Pehle twice, phir 3 jodo |
| x aur y ka **ratio 3 : 4** | x ÷ y = 3 ÷ 4, yaani 4x = 3y | Cross-multiply karke likhna aasaan hai |
| **x saal pehle** ki umar | ab ki umar − x | Aaj se x saal peeche |
| **y saal baad** ki umar | ab ki umar + y | Aaj se y saal aage |
| number ke digits ka **sum** | x + y | Do-digit number ke liye |

**Khaas dhyan do:** "x, y se 5 zyada hai" ka matlab x = y + 5 hai, **y = x + 5 nahi**. Bada wahi hai jise "zyada" kaha gaya. Hamesha ek baar socho: bada kaun hai? Usi ke side mein "+ 5" lagta hai.

## Part B: Numbers ke sawaal

### Example 1: Sum aur difference

Do numbers ka sum 50 aur difference 12 hai. Numbers dhoondho. (Bani wala sawaal.)

**Kadam 1:** Bada number = x, chhota = y.
**Kadam 2:** x + y = 50 ... (1) aur x − y = 12 ... (2)
**Kadam 3:** (1) + (2): 2x = 62, to x = 31. (1) mein daalo: 31 + y = 50, to y = 19.
**Kadam 4 (Jaanch):** Sum: 31 + 19 = 50 ✅. Difference: 31 − 19 = 12 ✅.

Numbers **31 aur 19** hain.

### Example 2: "Ek number doosre se 3 zyada, twice"

Ek number doosre number ke twice se 3 zyada hai. Dono ka sum 54 hai. Numbers dhoondho.

**Kadam 1:** Doosra number = y, pehla number = x.
**Kadam 2:** "Pehla, doosre ke twice se 3 zyada": x = 2y + 3 ... (1). "Sum 54": x + y = 54 ... (2).
**Kadam 3:** (1) ko (2) mein daalo (substitution): (2y + 3) + y = 54, to 3y + 3 = 54, 3y = 51, y = 17. Phir x = 2(17) + 3 = 37.
**Kadam 4 (Jaanch):** 17 ka twice = 34, aur 34 + 3 = 37 ✅. Sum: 37 + 17 = 54 ✅.

Numbers **37 aur 17** hain.

### Example 3: Do alag-alag jod

Pehle number ka 3 guna aur doosre number ko jodo to 40 aata hai. Pehle number aur doosre number ka 3 guna jodo to 24 aata hai. Numbers dhoondho.

**Kadam 1:** Pehla = x, doosra = y.
**Kadam 2:** 3x + y = 40 ... (1) aur x + 3y = 24 ... (2).
**Kadam 3:** (1) se y = 40 − 3x. (2) mein daalo: x + 3(40 − 3x) = 24, to x + 120 − 9x = 24, −8x = −96, x = 12. Phir y = 40 − 36 = 4.
**Kadam 4 (Jaanch):** 3 × 12 + 4 = 40 ✅. 12 + 3 × 4 = 24 ✅.

Numbers **12 aur 4** hain.

## Part C: Umar ke sawaal

Umar ke sawaalon mein ek nayi baat aati hai: **samay badalta hai**. Ek baar ki umar aaj ki hoti hai, aur doosri baar "x saal pehle" ya "y saal baad" ki. Sabse safe tareeka ek chhoti table hai:

| | Aaj | x saal pehle ya y saal baad |
|---|---|---|
| Pehla vyakti | ... | ... |
| Doosra vyakti | ... | ... |

**Zaroori niyam:** "5 saal pehle" ya "6 saal baad" jaisi shart **dono** vyaktiyon par lagti hai. Dono ki umar badalti hai, sirf ek ki nahi.

### Example 4: Aaj ki umar aur ek shart

Aaj Tejas ke pitaji ki umar Tejas se 3 guna hai. 10 saal baad pitaji ki umar Tejas ki umar ki twice hogi. Dono ki aaj ki umar dhoondho.

**Kadam 1:** Pitaji ki aaj ki umar = x, Tejas ki aaj ki umar = y.
**Kadam 2:**
- Aaj: x = 3y ... (1)
- 10 saal baad: pitaji x + 10, Tejas y + 10. Shart: x + 10 = 2(y + 10) ... (2)

**Kadam 3:** (1) ko (2) mein daalo: 3y + 10 = 2y + 20, to y = 10. Phir x = 30.
**Kadam 4 (Jaanch):** Aaj 30 aur 10: 30 = 3 × 10 ✅. 10 saal baad 40 aur 20: 40 = 2 × 20 ✅.

Pitaji **30 saal** ke aur Tejas **10 saal** ka hai.

### Example 5: Pehle bhi, baad mein bhi

5 saal pehle ek pita ki umar apne bete ki umar ki 7 guna thi. 5 saal baad pita ki umar bete ki umar ki 3 guna hogi. Aaj ki umar dhoondho.

**Kadam 1:** Pita ki aaj ki umar = x, bete ki aaj ki umar = y.
**Kadam 2:**

| | Aaj | 5 saal pehle | 5 saal baad |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pita | x | x − 5 | x + 5 |
| Beta | y | y − 5 | y + 5 |

- 5 saal pehle: x − 5 = 7(y − 5) ... (1)
- 5 saal baad: x + 5 = 3(y + 5) ... (2)

**Kadam 3:** (1) kholo: x − 5 = 7y − 35, to x = 7y − 30. (2) kholo: x + 5 = 3y + 15, to x = 3y + 10.
Dono x barabar: 7y − 30 = 3y + 10, to 4y = 40, y = 10. Phir x = 3(10) + 10 = 40.
**Kadam 4 (Jaanch):** Aaj: pita 40, beta 10.
- 5 saal pehle: pita 35, beta 5. 35 = 7 × 5 ✅
- 5 saal baad: pita 45, beta 15. 45 = 3 × 15 ✅

Pita **40 saal** ke aur beta **10 saal** ka hai.

### Example 6: Ratio aur sum

Do behenon ki umar ka ratio 3 : 4 hai. 6 saal baad unki umar ka sum 40 saal hoga. Aaj ki umar dhoondho.

**Kadam 1:** Choti behen = x, badi behen = y.
**Kadam 2:**
- Ratio 3 : 4: x ÷ y = 3 ÷ 4, yaani 4x = 3y ... (1)
- 6 saal baad: (x + 6) + (y + 6) = 40, to x + y = 28 ... (2)

**Kadam 3:** (2) se x = 28 − y. (1) mein daalo: 4(28 − y) = 3y, to 112 − 4y = 3y, 112 = 7y, y = 16. Phir x = 12.
**Kadam 4 (Jaanch):** Aaj 12 aur 16, ratio 12 : 16 = 3 : 4 ✅. 6 saal baad 18 aur 22, sum 18 + 22 = 40 ✅.

Choti behen **12 saal** ki aur badi **16 saal** ki hai.

## Part D: Fractions ke sawaal

Fraction ka matlab hai numerator ÷ denominator. Anjaan fraction ke liye likhte hain:

> **Fraction = x / y**, jahan x numerator aur y denominator.

Jab sawaal kahe "numerator mein 1 jodo", to naya fraction **(x + 1) / y** hota hai. Jab kahe "dono mein 1 jodo", to **(x + 1) / (y + 1)** hota hai.

Fraction wali shart ko solve karne ke liye **cross-multiply** karke fraction hata do.

### Example 7: Dono mein jodo, dono mein ghatao

Ek fraction ke numerator aur denominator dono mein 1 jodne par wo 1/2 ho jata hai. Dono mein se 1 ghatane par wo 1/3 ho jata hai. Fraction dhoondho.

**Kadam 1:** Fraction = x / y.
**Kadam 2:**
- (x + 1) / (y + 1) = 1/2. Cross-multiply: 2(x + 1) = y + 1, to 2x + 2 = y + 1, yaani **y = 2x + 1** ... (1)
- (x − 1) / (y − 1) = 1/3. Cross-multiply: 3(x − 1) = y − 1, to 3x − 3 = y − 1, yaani **y = 3x − 2** ... (2)

**Kadam 3:** Dono y barabar: 2x + 1 = 3x − 2, to x = 3. Phir y = 2(3) + 1 = 7.
**Kadam 4 (Jaanch):** Fraction 3/7.
- Dono mein 1 jodo: 4/8 = 1/2 ✅
- Dono mein se 1 ghatao: 2/6 = 1/3 ✅

Fraction **3/7** hai.

### Example 8: Sum aur ek shart

Ek fraction ke numerator aur denominator ka sum 12 hai. Agar denominator mein 3 jod diya jaye, to fraction 1/2 ho jata hai. Fraction dhoondho.

**Kadam 1:** Fraction = x / y.
**Kadam 2:**
- Sum 12: x + y = 12 ... (1)
- Denominator mein 3 jodo: x / (y + 3) = 1/2, to 2x = y + 3 ... (2)

**Kadam 3:** (2) se y = 2x − 3. (1) mein daalo: x + 2x − 3 = 12, 3x = 15, x = 5. Phir y = 7.
**Kadam 4 (Jaanch):** Fraction 5/7. Sum: 5 + 7 = 12 ✅. Denominator mein 3 jodo: 5/10 = 1/2 ✅.

Fraction **5/7** hai.

## Part E: Do-digit numbers ke sawaal

Yahan sabse zaroori idea hai **place value**. Number 47 ka matlab 4 aur 7 ka "saath likha hona" nahi hai. Iska matlab hai 4 dahaiyan aur 7 ikaiyan: 4 × 10 + 7 = 47.

> Do-digit number jiska **tens digit x** aur **units digit y** ho:
> **Number = 10x + y**
> Digits **reverse** karne par: **10y + x**

Jaanch: 47 mein x = 4, y = 7. 10 × 4 + 7 = 47 ✅. Reverse: 10 × 7 + 4 = 74 ✅.

**Ek chhoti kaam ki baat:** (10x + y) aur (10y + x) ka antar hamesha 9 ka guna hota hai:
(10y + x) − (10x + y) = 9y − 9x = 9(y − x).
Isse "reverse karne par 27 zyada" jaisi shart ko seedha **y − x = 3** bana sakte ho.

### Example 9: Digit sum aur reverse

Ek do-digit number ke digits ka sum 9 hai. Digits reverse karne par naya number original se 27 zyada ho jata hai. Number dhoondho.

**Kadam 1:** Tens digit = x, units digit = y. Number = 10x + y.
**Kadam 2:**
- Digits ka sum: x + y = 9 ... (1)
- Reverse number original se 27 zyada: (10y + x) = (10x + y) + 27. Sab ek side lao: 9y − 9x = 27, 9 se divide karo: **y − x = 3** ... (2)

**Kadam 3:** (1) + (2): 2y = 12, y = 6. Phir x = 3.
**Kadam 4 (Jaanch):** Number 10(3) + 6 = **36**.
- Digits ka sum: 3 + 6 = 9 ✅
- Reverse: 63. 63 − 36 = 27 ✅
- Dono digits 0 se 9 ke beech ke hain ✅

### Example 10: Number, digit sum ka 7 guna

Ek do-digit number apne digits ke sum ka 7 guna hai. Agar digits reverse kar do, to naya number original se 36 kam hota hai. Number dhoondho.

**Kadam 1:** Tens digit = x, units digit = y. Number = 10x + y.
**Kadam 2:**
- Number = 7 × (digit sum): 10x + y = 7(x + y), to 3x = 6y, yaani **x = 2y** ... (1)
- Reverse original se 36 kam: (10y + x) = (10x + y) − 36. Sab ek side lao: 9x − 9y = 36, to **x − y = 4** ... (2)

**Kadam 3:** (1) ko (2) mein daalo: 2y − y = 4, to y = 4. Phir x = 8.
**Kadam 4 (Jaanch):** Number **84**.
- Digit sum 8 + 4 = 12. 7 × 12 = 84 ✅
- Reverse: 48. 84 − 48 = 36 ✅
- Digits 8 aur 4 dono 0 se 9 ke beech ✅

### Example 11: Sum aur reverse, ek aur jodi

Ek do-digit number ke digits ka sum 12 hai. Digits reverse karne par number 36 zyada ho jata hai. Number dhoondho.

**Kadam 1:** Tens digit = x, units digit = y.
**Kadam 2:** x + y = 12 ... (1). Reverse 36 zyada: 9(y − x) = 36, to y − x = 4 ... (2).
**Kadam 3:** (1) + (2): 2y = 16, y = 8, x = 4.
**Kadam 4 (Jaanch):** Number **48**. Digit sum 4 + 8 = 12 ✅. Reverse 84, 84 − 48 = 36 ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: 10x + y ko "xy" likh dena**

Algebra mein "xy" ka matlab **x × y** hota hai, do digits ka number nahi. Number 36 mein x = 3, y = 6 hai:
- Galat: xy = 3 × 6 = 18. Ye 36 nahi hai.
- Sahi: 10x + y = 10 × 3 + 6 = **36** ✅.

Isliye do-digit number hamesha **10x + y** likho, aur reverse **10y + x**.

**Galti 2: "Saal pehle" ya "saal baad" sirf ek vyakti par lagana**

Example 5 mein "5 saal pehle pita ki umar bete ki 7 guna thi". Galat equation: x − 5 = 7y, yaani sirf pita ke saal ghataye, bete ke nahi.
Is galat equation ko doosri (sahi) equation x + 5 = 3(y + 5) ke saath solve karo: x = 7y + 5, to 7y + 5 + 5 = 3y + 15, 4y = 5, y = 1.25 aur x = 13.75. Aise umar sawaal mein achhi nahi lagti. Sahi equation x − 5 = 7(y − 5) mein **dono** ke 5 saal ghate, aur jawab 40 aur 10 aaya.

Aadat: 5 saal pehle ke liye **dono** ki umar mein se 5 ghatao. Table banao to ye galti hoti hi nahi.

**Galti 3: Jawab sensible hai ya nahi, ye na dekhna**

Algebra jo bhi nikaal de, wo sawaal ke hisaab se sahi nahi ho jata. Teen niyam yaad rakho:
- **Umar** positive hogi (0 ya negative nahi).
- Do-digit number ka **har digit 0 se 9** ke beech ka poora number hoga, aur tens digit x kabhi 0 nahi hoga.
- Log ya cheezon ki ginti **poora number** hogi, aadhi nahi.

Do demo dekho:
- Ravi, Sunil se 5 saal bada hai aur dono ki umar ka sum 4 hai. Equations: x = y + 5, x + y = 4. Solve: 2y + 5 = 4, y = −0.5. Umar negative aayi, matlab ye sawaal hi **mumkin nahi** hai. Ya to equations galat bani hain, ya sawaal hi galat diya gaya hai.
- Do-digit number ke digits ka sum 5 hai aur reverse karne par number 40 zyada hota hai. Equations: x + y = 5 aur 9(y − x) = 40, to y − x = 40/9. Ye poora number nahi hai, to digits poore number nahi ban sakte. Aisa koi do-digit number **hai hi nahi**.

Kadam 4 yahi pakadta hai: jawab ko sawaal ke shabdon mein daalo **aur** dekho ki wo sensible hai.

**Galti 4: Antar mein jagah ulti likhna**

"x, y se 5 zyada hai" ko y = x + 5 likh dena galat hai. Bada x hai, to x = y + 5. Jab bhi shak ho, ek chhota example socho: agar y = 10 ho, to x kitna hoga? 15. Yaani x = y + 5 ✅.

## Quick Recap

- Word problem ke **4 kadam**: variables chuno (saaf likho x kya hai), har vaakya ko equation banao, solve karo, ASLI shabdon se jaancho.
- Translation: "sum" ko +, "difference" ko −, "twice" ko 2x, "5 more than x" ko x + 5, "ratio 3 : 4" ko 4x = 3y, "x saal pehle" ko ab ki umar − x, "y saal baad" ko ab ki umar + y.
- **Umar:** table banao, aur "saal pehle/baad" **dono** vyaktiyon par lagao.
- **Fraction:** x/y maan kar shart likho, phir cross-multiply karke fraction hata do.
- **Do-digit number:** 10x + y; reverse 10y + x; reverse ka antar 9 ka guna hota hai (9(y − x)).
- Jawab sensible hona chahiye: umar positive, digit 0 se 9 ke beech.
- Galtiyan: "xy" likhna, "saal pehle" sirf ek par lagana, sensible check bhool jaana.

## Practice Questions

1. The sum of two numbers is 41 and their difference is 9. Find the numbers.

2. One number is 5 less than three times another number. The sum of the two numbers is 63. Find the numbers.

3. A mother is 3 times as old as her daughter. After 12 years, the mother will be twice as old as her daughter. Find their present ages.

4. 4 years ago, an uncle was 5 times as old as his nephew. 4 years from now, the uncle will be 3 times as old as the nephew. Find their present ages.

5. A fraction becomes 9/11 when 2 is added to both its numerator and denominator, and becomes 5/6 when 3 is added to both. Find the fraction.

6. The sum of the digits of a two-digit number is 11. If the digits are reversed, the new number is 27 more than the original. Find the original number.

7. A two-digit number is 4 times the sum of its digits. If the digits are reversed, the new number is 18 more than the original. Find the number.

8. (a) A student writes a two-digit number with tens digit x and units digit y as "xy". Explain what is wrong and write the correct expression.
   (b) The sum of the present ages of two friends is 30 and one is 40 years older than the other. Set up the equations, solve them, and say why the answer shows that the problem cannot be right.

### Answers

1. Bada = x, chhota = y. x + y = 41 aur x − y = 9. Jodne par 2x = 50, x = 25, y = 16.
   Jaanch: 25 + 16 = 41 ✅, 25 − 16 = 9 ✅. Numbers **25 aur 16**.

2. Doosra number = y, pehla = x. x = 3y − 5 aur x + y = 63. Daalo: 3y − 5 + y = 63, 4y = 68, y = 17, x = 3(17) − 5 = 46.
   Jaanch: 3 × 17 = 51, 51 − 5 = 46 ✅. 46 + 17 = 63 ✅. Numbers **46 aur 17**.

3. Maa x, beti y. x = 3y. 12 saal baad x + 12 = 2(y + 12). Daalo: 3y + 12 = 2y + 24, y = 12, x = 36.
   Jaanch: 36 = 3 × 12 ✅. 12 saal baad 48 aur 24, 48 = 2 × 24 ✅. Maa **36 saal** ki, beti **12 saal** ki.

4. Uncle x, nephew y. 4 saal pehle: x − 4 = 5(y − 4), to x = 5y − 16. 4 saal baad: x + 4 = 3(y + 4), to x = 3y + 8. Barabar karo: 5y − 16 = 3y + 8, 2y = 24, y = 12, x = 3(12) + 8 = 44.
   Jaanch: 4 saal pehle 40 aur 8, 40 = 5 × 8 ✅. 4 saal baad 48 aur 16, 48 = 3 × 16 ✅. Uncle **44 saal** ke, nephew **12 saal** ka.

5. Fraction x/y. (x + 2)/(y + 2) = 9/11: 11x + 22 = 9y + 18, to 11x − 9y = −4 ... (1). (x + 3)/(y + 3) = 5/6: 6x + 18 = 5y + 15, to 6x − 5y = −3 ... (2).
   (1) ko 5 se aur (2) ko 9 se guna karo: 55x − 45y = −20 aur 54x − 45y = −27. Ghatane par x = 7. (2) se 42 − 5y = −3, 5y = 45, y = 9.
   Jaanch: (7 + 2)/(9 + 2) = 9/11 ✅. (7 + 3)/(9 + 3) = 10/12 = 5/6 ✅. Fraction **7/9**.

6. Tens x, units y. x + y = 11. Reverse 27 zyada: 9(y − x) = 27, to y − x = 3. Jodne par 2y = 14, y = 7, x = 4.
   Jaanch: number 47. Digit sum 4 + 7 = 11 ✅. Reverse 74, 74 − 47 = 27 ✅. Number **47**.

7. Tens x, units y. 10x + y = 4(x + y), to 6x = 3y, y = 2x. Reverse 18 zyada: 9(y − x) = 18, y − x = 2. Daalo: 2x − x = 2, x = 2, y = 4.
   Jaanch: number 24. Digit sum 6, 4 × 6 = 24 ✅. Reverse 42, 42 − 24 = 18 ✅. Number **24**.

8. (a) "xy" ka algebra mein matlab x × y hota hai, number nahi. Jaise x = 3, y = 6 ho, to xy = 18, par number 36 hai. Sahi: **10x + y**.
   (b) Friends x aur y, x bada. x + y = 30 aur x − y = 40. Jodne par 2x = 70, x = 35, y = −5. Umar negative nahi ho sakti, isliye ye sawaal mumkin nahi hai. Jaanch: x − y = 35 − (−5) = 40 aur x + y = 35 + (−5) = 30 to milte hain, par umar −5 ho hi nahi sakti. Asal baat: jab sum 30 ho, to antar 30 se zyada ho hi nahi sakta.
