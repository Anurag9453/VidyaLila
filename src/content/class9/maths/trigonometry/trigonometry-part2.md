# Topic 2: Sin, Cos aur Tan

Topic 1 mein humne right triangle ki sides ko naam dena seekha: kisi bhi acute angle θ ke liye **hypotenuse** (sabse lambi side), **opposite** (θ ke saamne wali) aur **adjacent** (θ ke bagal wali). Ye bhi dekha ki angle badalte hi opposite aur adjacent aapas mein badal jate hain.

Ab asli maza shuru hota hai. Naam dene se kaam nahi chalta, humein in sides ka **ratio** (ek side ÷ doosri side) banana hai. Teen khaas ratios hain, aur unke naam hain **sin, cos aur tan**. Aur ek bahut kamaal ki baat bhi dekhenge: ye ratios triangle ke **size** par nahi, sirf **angle** par depend karte hain.

---

## Ramp kitni steep hai?

Harleen ke school mein wheelchair ke liye ek ramp banne wala hai. Ramp-builder Mr. Sandhu jagah naap rahe the, aur Harleen unke paas khadi hokar dekh rahi thi.

**Harleen:** Sandhu sir, ramp ko kitna lamba rakhenge?

**Mr. Sandhu:** Ye is par depend karta hai ki hamein zameen se kitna upar jaana hai. Ek ramp ko do cheezein define karti hain: wo kitna **upar uthta hai** (rise) aur kitni **seedhi zameen par aage badhta hai** (run). Dekho, do ramp sochte hain. Pehla ramp 12 m ki run mein sirf 1 m upar uthta hai. Doosra ramp sirf 4 m ki run mein 1 m upar uthta hai.

**Harleen:** To doosra zyada steep hoga, kyunki wo jaldi upar chadh jata hai.

**Mr. Sandhu:** Bilkul. Pehla gentle hai, wheelchair aaram se chadh jayegi. Doosra steep hai, usme to dhakka lagana padega. Ab bataao, "steep" ko ek number mein kaise likhein?

Harleen ne thodi der socha. "Rise ÷ run? Pehle ramp ke liye 1 ÷ 12, doosre ke liye 1 ÷ 4."

**Mr. Sandhu:** Wah! Ab dekho: 1/12 chhota number hai, 1/4 bada number. **Jitna bada ratio, utna steep ramp.** Aur ek aur baat: agar main ek chhota **model ramp** banaun, jo 60 cm ki run mein 5 cm uthta ho, to uska ratio 5 ÷ 60 = 1/12 aata hai. Wahi ratio, wahi steepness. Chhota ho ya bada, agar steepness same hai, to rise ÷ run bhi same.

**Harleen:** To ratio ramp ke size ko nahi, uski **steepness** ko batata hai!

**Mr. Sandhu:** Aur steepness kya hai? Wo ramp aur zameen ke beech ka **angle** hi hai. Is topic ka poora idea yahi hai.

Ramp, zameen aur seedhi khadi deewar (rise) milkar ek **right triangle** banate hain. Rise us angle ki **opposite** side hai, run us angle ki **adjacent** side hai. Yaani Mr. Sandhu ka "rise ÷ run" asal mein **opposite ÷ adjacent** hai. Ye trigonometry ke teen ratios mein se ek hai, jise hum **tan** kehte hain. Baaki do ratios bhi isi tarah bante hain.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Trigonometric ratio** | Right triangle ki do sides ka ratio, kisi ek acute angle ke hisaab se | Opposite ÷ adjacent |
| **sin θ** (sine) | Opposite ÷ hypotenuse | 3-4-5 mein angle A ke liye 3/5 |
| **cos θ** (cosine) | Adjacent ÷ hypotenuse | 3-4-5 mein angle A ke liye 4/5 |
| **tan θ** (tangent) | Opposite ÷ adjacent | 3-4-5 mein angle A ke liye 3/4 |
| **θ (theta)** | Ek angle ka naam, jaise hum "x" ek number ko kehte hain | ∠A = θ |
| **SOH-CAH-TOA** | Teeno ratios yaad rakhne ka ek chhota sa trick | Neeche Part A mein |

## Part A: Teen ratios aur unka yaad rakhne ka trick

Ek right triangle lo, jisme ∠θ acute angle hai. Is angle ke hisaab se sides ke naam Topic 1 se pata hain: hypotenuse, opposite, adjacent. Ab teen ratios:

> **sin θ = opposite ÷ hypotenuse**
> **cos θ = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse**
> **tan θ = opposite ÷ adjacent**

### Yaad rakhne ka trick: SOH-CAH-TOA

Ye ek jaana-pehchana trick hai. Isse teen chhote tukdon mein padho:

- **SOH:** **S**in = **O**pposite ÷ **H**ypotenuse
- **CAH:** **C**os = **A**djacent ÷ **H**ypotenuse
- **TOA:** **T**an = **O**pposite ÷ **A**djacent

Har tukde ka pehla akshar ratio ka naam hai, aur baaki do akshar uski upar-wali aur neeche-wali side hain. Bas ye tukde do-teen baar bol lo: "SOH-CAH-TOA, SOH-CAH-TOA". Phir kabhi nahi bhoologe.

### Ek zaroori baat: ratio hamesha ek angle ke hisaab se

"Opposite" aur "adjacent" us angle par depend karte hain jise tum dekh rahe ho. Isliye sin, cos aur tan hamesha **kisi ek angle ke saath** likhte hain: sin A, cos B, tan θ. Sirf "sin" ka koi matlab nahi hota.

## Part B: 3-4-5 triangle se pehla hisaab

△ABC mein ∠C = 90°, AC = 4, BC = 3, aur AB = 5. (Check: 3² + 4² = 9 + 16 = 25 = 5² ✅, jo Pythagoras se pata hai.)

Hypotenuse AB = 5 hai (sabse lambi side, 90° ke saamne).

### Example 1: Angle A ke liye teeno ratios

Angle A ki **opposite** side BC = 3 hai. Angle A ki **adjacent** side AC = 4 hai.

- sin A = opposite ÷ hypotenuse = **3/5**
- cos A = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse = **4/5**
- tan A = opposite ÷ adjacent = **3/4**

### Example 2: Doosre acute angle B ke liye teeno ratios

Ab hum angle B se dekhte hain. Ab opposite aur adjacent **badal gaye**:

Angle B ki **opposite** side AC = 4 hai. Angle B ki **adjacent** side BC = 3 hai.

- sin B = 4/5
- cos B = 3/5
- tan B = 4/3

Dekho kaisa mazedaar hai: sin A = 3/5 tha aur cos B = 3/5 hai. Aur cos A = 4/5 tha, sin B = 4/5 hai. Ye ulta-pulta kyun hota hai, ye hum isi chapter ke Topic 6 mein poori tarah samjhenge. Abhi bas ye notice karo ki **angle badalte hi ratios badal jate hain**, kyunki opposite aur adjacent badal jate hain.

**Jaanch:** Hypotenuse hamesha 5 hi raha, sirf opposite aur adjacent ne jagah badli. 3/5 aur 4/5 ki jagah dono angles ke liye yahi do numbers aaye ✅

## Part C: Ratio sirf angle par depend karta hai, size par nahi

Ab wo asli idea, jo ramp wale scene mein pehle aaya tha.

Maan lo ek 3-4-5 triangle hai, aur uska ek bada photo (zoom in) lete hain, jisme har side **double** ho gayi. To ye ban gaya **6-8-10 triangle**. Dono ka shape bilkul same hai, bas ek chhota hai aur ek bada, jaise ek hi photo ka chhota aur bada print. Angle A dono mein barabar hai.

Dekhte hain ratios kya aate hain angle A ke liye.

**Chhota triangle (3-4-5):**
- sin A = 3/5
- cos A = 4/5
- tan A = 3/4

**Bada triangle (6-8-10):**
- sin A = 6/10 = **3/5**
- cos A = 8/10 = **4/5**
- tan A = 6/8 = **3/4**

Dono triangles mein **bilkul same ratios** aaye ✅. Jab saari sides double hoti hain, to ratio mein upar aur neeche dono barabar badhte hain, aur wo aapas mein cancel ho jate hain.

Triangle ko triple karo: 9-12-15. tan A = 9/12 = 3/4. Phir wahi. Ek 1.5 times bada triangle: 4.5-6-7.5. tan A = 4.5/6 = 0.75 = 3/4. Phir wahi.

### Ye kyun hota hai? (aasaan samajh)

Do triangles jinke **angles barabar** hain, unka **shape same** hota hai: ek doosre ka chhota ya bada photo hota hai. Photo bada karne par har side utni hi guna (jaise 2 guna) hoti hai, isliye do sides ka ratio nahi badalta. Is baat ka poora, formal proof "similar triangles" naam ke topic mein hota hai, jo **aage ki class mein** aayega. Abhi itna samajhna kaafi hai: **same angle, same shape, same ratio**.

### Ramp ka sawaal solve

Wapas Harleen aur Mr. Sandhu ke ramp par aate hain.

- Mr. Sandhu ka model ramp: rise = 5 cm, run = 60 cm. tan(angle) = 5 ÷ 60 = **1/12**.
- Asli ramp: rise = 0.5 m, run = 6 m. tan(angle) = 0.5 ÷ 6 = **1/12**.

Model chhota hai, asli ramp bada, par ratio same hai kyunki **angle same** hai. Isi wajah se Mr. Sandhu chhota model bana kar pehle check kar sakte hain ki ramp kitna steep hoga, aur phir bade ramp ko usi angle par bana sakte hain.

**Harleen ka ek aur sawaal:** "Sir, agar ek ramp 1 m ki run mein 1 m uthe, to?" 1 ÷ 1 = 1. Ye to bahut steep hai. Aur 1 m run mein 2 m rise ho, to ratio 2 hota hai: wheelchair ke liye to bekaar, par tan ke liye ye bilkul theek number hai. Yaani tan 1 se bada bhi ho sakta hai.

## Part D: Aur examples, alag-alag triangles

### Example 3: 5-12-13 triangle

△PQR mein ∠Q = 90°, PQ = 5, QR = 12, PR = 13. (Check: 25 + 144 = 169 = 13² ✅)

**Angle P ke liye:** opposite = QR = 12, adjacent = PQ = 5, hypotenuse = PR = 13.

- sin P = 12/13
- cos P = 5/13
- tan P = 12/5

**Angle R ke liye:** opposite = PQ = 5, adjacent = QR = 12, hypotenuse = 13.

- sin R = 5/13
- cos R = 12/13
- tan R = 5/12

**Jaanch:** Dono angles ke liye numbers 5, 12 aur 13 hi use hue, bas opposite aur adjacent ne jagah badli ✅

### Example 4: 8-15-17 triangle

△XYZ mein ∠Y = 90°, XY = 15, YZ = 8, XZ = 17. (Check: 64 + 225 = 289 = 17² ✅)

Angle X ki opposite side YZ = 8, adjacent side XY = 15, hypotenuse XZ = 17.

- sin X = 8/17
- cos X = 15/17
- tan X = 8/15

Fraction ko aur simplify nahi kar sakte, kyunki 8 aur 15 ka koi common factor nahi hai (aur 8 aur 17 ka bhi nahi).

### Example 5: Ek side missing hai (pehle Pythagoras, phir ratio)

Ek right triangle mein hypotenuse 26 hai, aur angle θ ki opposite side 10 hai. Adjacent side nahi di gayi. θ ke teeno ratios nikaalo.

**Step 1: Adjacent nikaalo.** Pythagoras (26² = 10² + adjacent²):

adjacent² = 676 − 100 = 576, to adjacent = **24**.

**Step 2: Ratios nikaalo.**

- sin θ = 10/26 = **5/13** (simplify kiya)
- cos θ = 24/26 = **12/13**
- tan θ = 10/24 = **5/12**

Ye wahi ratios hain jo Example 3 mein angle R ke the. Ye triangle 5-12-13 ka hi double (10-24-26) hai, to ye hona hi tha ✅

**Jaanch:** Pythagoras: 10² + 24² = 100 + 576 = 676 = 26² ✅

### Example 6: Jab sides barabar hon (surd aata hai)

Ek right triangle mein opposite = 1 aur adjacent = 1. Hypotenuse kya hai? Pythagoras se:

hypotenuse² = 1² + 1² = 2, to hypotenuse = **√2**.

Ab ratios:

- sin θ = 1/√2
- cos θ = 1/√2
- tan θ = 1/1 = **1**

Yahan sin aur cos mein √2 (surd) aa gaya. Is baat se ghabrao mat. 1/√2 bilkul theek ek number hai (lagbhag 0.707). Isko aur sundar form mein likhna hum abhi nahi karenge.

**Jaanch:** 1² + 1² = 2 = (√2)² ✅

### Example 7: Alag-alag orientation

Ek right triangle mein ∠B = 90°, AB = 7, BC = 24, AC = 25. (Check: 49 + 576 = 625 = 25² ✅) Angle C ke teeno ratios nikaalo.

Angle C ki opposite side AB = 7, adjacent side BC = 24, hypotenuse AC = 25.

- sin C = 7/25
- cos C = 24/25
- tan C = 7/24

Dhyan do: yahan right angle B par hai, A par nahi. Hypotenuse hamesha **right angle ke saamne** wali side hoti hai, chahe triangle kisi bhi tarah rakha ho. Ye yaad rakhna.

### Example 8: Kaun si ramp zyada steep?

Ek ramp 2 m ki rise ke liye 10 m ki run leta hai. Doosra ramp 3 m rise ke liye 18 m ki run leta hai. Kaun zyada steep hai?

- Pehle ramp ka tan = 2/10 = 1/5
- Doosre ramp ka tan = 3/18 = 1/6

1/5 aur 1/6 mein se 1/5 bada hai (kyunki 5 chhota hai, to ek hissa bada). To **pehla ramp zyada steep** hai.

**Jaanch:** 1/5 = 0.2, 1/6 ≈ 0.167, aur 0.2 > 0.167 ✅

## Part E: Kuch chhoti, kaam ki baatein

Ye sirf **observations** hain. In sab ko hum yahan prove nahi kar rahe.

**1. Ratios mein koi unit nahi hota.**
Ratio do lengths ka hota hai, jaise 3 cm ÷ 5 cm. cm cm se cancel ho jata hai. Isliye sin θ = 3/5 likhte hain, "3/5 cm" nahi.

**2. sin θ aur cos θ hamesha 0 aur 1 ke beech aate hain (acute angle ke liye).**
Kyunki hypotenuse right triangle ki sabse lambi side hoti hai. Sin mein opposite ÷ hypotenuse hai, yaani chhoti side ÷ badi side, to jawab 1 se chhota hi aayega. Cos ke liye bhi yahi baat hai. Isliye agar tumhe kabhi "sin θ = 5/4" jaisa answer mile, to samajh jao ki kuch galat hua hai.

**3. tan θ 1 se bada ho sakta hai.**
Tan mein hypotenuse nahi hai: opposite ÷ adjacent. Agar opposite adjacent se lambi ho, to tan 1 se bada aayega. Jaise Example 2 mein tan B = 4/3, ya Example 3 mein tan P = 12/5.

**4. Kya ratio ka size se koi lena-dena nahi?**
Haan, ratio sirf angle par depend karta hai (Part C). Triangle bada karo ya chhota, angle same rakho, to sin, cos, tan same.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: sin θ = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse likh dena**

3-4-5 triangle mein angle A ke liye:
- Galat: sin A = 4/5 (ye adjacent ÷ hypotenuse hai, yaani cos A)
- Sahi: sin A = opposite ÷ hypotenuse = **3/5**

SOH yaad rakho: Sin = Opposite ÷ Hypotenuse.

**Galti 2: Alag angle ke hisaab se opposite lena**

3-4-5 mein ∠A (opposite 3) dekh rahe ho, par tan likhte waqt 4/3 likh diya (jo angle B ka tan hai).
- Pehle tay karo: **kaun sa angle?** Phir us angle ke hisaab se opposite aur adjacent pehchano.
- Angle A ke liye tan A = 3/4. Angle B ke liye tan B = 4/3.

**Galti 3: Fraction simplify karna bhool jana**

6-8-10 triangle mein sin A = 6/10. Ise **3/5** likhna zyada accha hai, aur isse dikhta hai ki ye 3-4-5 triangle jaisa hi hai. Hamesha check karo ki numerator aur denominator mein koi common factor to nahi.

**Galti 4: Hypotenuse ko leg samajh lena**

Ek triangle mein sides 5, 12, 13 hain. Agar tumne tan = 12/13 likh diya, to tumne 13 (hypotenuse) ko adjacent maan liya. Tan mein **hypotenuse nahi aata**. Tan = opposite ÷ adjacent, dono legs. Hypotenuse hamesha sabse lambi side hai (yahan 13).

**Galti 5: Sochna ki triangle bada karne par ratio badal jata hai**

Kuch log sochte hain ki 6-8-10 triangle ka tan 3-4-5 se alag hoga kyunki sides badi hain. Sahi nahi. 6/8 = 3/4. **Same angle, same ratio.** Triangle ka size ratio ko nahi badalta.

## Quick Recap

- Right triangle ke ek acute angle θ ke liye:
  - **sin θ = opposite ÷ hypotenuse**
  - **cos θ = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse**
  - **tan θ = opposite ÷ adjacent**
- Yaad rakhne ka trick: **SOH-CAH-TOA**.
- Ratio hamesha **kisi ek angle** ke hisaab se hota hai. Angle badalne par opposite aur adjacent badal jate hain, to ratio bhi badal jata hai.
- **Ratio sirf angle par depend karta hai, triangle ke size par nahi.** 3-4-5 aur 6-8-10 dono mein tan = 3/4 (same shape, bada ya chhota photo).
- Ramp ki steepness = rise ÷ run = tan(angle). Bada ratio, zyada steep ramp.
- Ek side missing ho to pehle Pythagoras se nikalo, phir ratio.
- sin θ aur cos θ 0 aur 1 ke beech hote hain (acute angle ke liye), kyunki hypotenuse sabse lambi side hai. tan θ 1 se bada ho sakta hai.
- Ratios ka koi unit nahi hota (lengths ka ratio).
- Galtiyan: sin ko adjacent ÷ hypotenuse likhna, galat angle se dekhna, simplify na karna, hypotenuse ko leg samajhna, sochna ki size badalne se ratio badalta hai.

## Practice Questions

1. In △ABC, ∠B = 90°, AB = 5, BC = 12 and AC = 13. Find sin A, cos A and tan A. Then find sin C, cos C and tan C.

2. In a right triangle the hypotenuse is 25 and the side opposite to angle θ is 7. Find the adjacent side, and then sin θ, cos θ and tan θ.

3. The two legs of a right triangle are 9 and 12. Find the hypotenuse. For the angle opposite the side of length 9, find sin, cos and tan, and write each in simplest form.

4. Two right triangles have sides 6, 8, 10 and 9, 12, 15. For the smallest angle in each triangle, find tan. What do you notice, and why does it happen?

5. A ramp A rises 3 m over a horizontal run of 15 m. A ramp B rises 2 m over a horizontal run of 8 m. Which ramp is steeper? Show your working using the ratio rise ÷ run.

6. A student claims that in some right triangle, for an acute angle θ, sin θ = 5/4. Is this possible? Explain using the idea of the hypotenuse.

7. In a right triangle, both legs are 2 units long. Find the hypotenuse, and then sin θ, cos θ and tan θ for one of the acute angles. Leave any square root as it is.

8. State True or False:
   (a) For an acute angle, tan θ can be greater than 1.
   (b) sin θ = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse.
   (c) If you enlarge a right triangle keeping its angles the same, the value of tan θ changes.
   (d) For an acute angle in a right triangle, cos θ is always less than 1.
   (e) The value of sin θ = 3/5 means the opposite side is 3 cm and the hypotenuse is 5 cm only.

### Answers

1. ∠B = 90°, to hypotenuse AC = 13 (check: 25 + 144 = 169 ✅).
   Angle A: opposite = BC = 12, adjacent = AB = 5.
   sin A = **12/13**, cos A = **5/13**, tan A = **12/5**.
   Angle C: opposite = AB = 5, adjacent = BC = 12.
   sin C = **5/13**, cos C = **12/13**, tan C = **5/12**.

2. Adjacent² = 25² − 7² = 625 − 49 = 576, to adjacent = **24**.
   sin θ = **7/25**, cos θ = **24/25**, tan θ = **7/24**.
   Jaanch: 7² + 24² = 49 + 576 = 625 = 25² ✅

3. Hypotenuse² = 9² + 12² = 81 + 144 = 225, to hypotenuse = **15**.
   Angle jiski opposite 9 hai: adjacent = 12.
   sin = 9/15 = **3/5**, cos = 12/15 = **4/5**, tan = 9/12 = **3/4**.

4. Sabse chhota angle sabse chhoti side (6 ya 9) ke saamne hota hai.
   Pehle triangle mein tan = 6/8 = **3/4**. Doosre mein tan = 9/12 = **3/4**.
   Dono barabar hain. Aisa isliye hota hai kyunki dono triangles ka shape same hai (ek doosre ka chhota-bada photo hain), isliye angle same hai, aur ratio sirf angle par depend karta hai, size par nahi.

5. Ramp A: 3/15 = 1/5 = 0.2. Ramp B: 2/8 = 1/4 = 0.25.
   0.25 > 0.2, to **ramp B zyada steep** hai.

6. **Nahi, ye possible nahi hai.** sin θ = opposite ÷ hypotenuse. Hypotenuse right triangle ki sabse lambi side hoti hai, to opposite (jo ek leg hai) hypotenuse se chhoti hogi. Isliye ratio 1 se chhota hi aayega. 5/4 to 1 se bada hai (1.25), to ye ho hi nahi sakta.

7. Hypotenuse² = 2² + 2² = 4 + 4 = 8, to hypotenuse = √8 = **2√2**.
   Kisi bhi ek acute angle ke liye opposite = 2, adjacent = 2.
   sin θ = 2/(2√2) = **1/√2**, cos θ = 2/(2√2) = **1/√2**, tan θ = 2/2 = **1**.
   (Ye wahi ratios hain jo Example 6 mein aaye, kyunki ye usi triangle ka double hai. Same angle, same ratio!)

8. (a) **True** ✅. Jaise tan = 12/5 ya 4/3.
   (b) **False** ❌. sin θ = opposite ÷ hypotenuse. Adjacent ÷ hypotenuse to cos θ hai.
   (c) **False** ❌. Ratio sirf angle par depend karta hai, size par nahi.
   (d) **True** ✅. Cos = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse, aur adjacent leg hypotenuse se chhoti hoti hai.
   (e) **False** ❌. sin θ = 3/5 ka matlab bas ye hai ki opposite aur hypotenuse ka ratio 3:5 hai. Ye 3 cm aur 5 cm ho sakte hain, ya 6 cm aur 10 cm, ya 30 m aur 50 m. Ratio ka koi unit nahi hota.

Sin, cos aur tan ab tumhare paas hain, aur tumhe pata hai ki ye triangle ke size se nahi, sirf angle se tay hote hain. Jab bhi atko, SOH-CAH-TOA bolo aur pehle apna angle pehchano.

**Aage kya?** Ye teen ratios akele nahi aate. Agar har ratio ko ulta (flip) kar dein, to kya milega? Topic 3 mein yahi dekhenge.
