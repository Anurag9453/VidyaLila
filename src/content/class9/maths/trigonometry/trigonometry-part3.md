# Topic 3: Cosec, Sec aur Cot (Ulte Ratios)

Pichhle topic mein humne teen ratio seekhe the: sin θ, cos θ aur tan θ. Har ratio mein do sides ko ek doosre se divide kiya jata tha, aur ye ratio sirf angle par depend karta tha, triangle ke chhote-bade hone par nahi. Ek sawaal abhi bacha hai: jab ek ratio mil gaya, to kya uska **ulta** (flip kiya hua) ratio bhi koi kaam ka hai? Jawab hai haan. Aur ek rasoi se shuru karte hain.

---

## Yuvan ki ulti recipe

Chef Bhavna ki chhoti si bakery hai. Aaj ek khaas dessert banana hai, aur recipe card par likha hai:

> **sugar : flour = 1 : 3**

Matlab 1 hissa sugar aur 3 hisse flour. Bhavna ne Yuvan ko kaha, "100 gram sugar aur 300 gram flour naap lo."

Yuvan jaldi mein tha. Usne card ko ulta padh liya, "flour : sugar = 1 : 3", aur naap liya **100 gram flour aur 300 gram sugar**. Dessert bana to itna meetha ki muh mein ek bite bhi nahi gaya.

Bhavna ne plate dekhi aur hans padi. "Yuvan, tune ratio ulta kar diya. Par ek baat bata dun: ratio ko ulta karna galat nahi hai, bas ye **ek naya ratio** hai. Recipe sugar ki taraf se dekhti hai: sugar 1, flour 3. Ulta ratio flour ki taraf se dekhta hai: flour 3, sugar 1. Kahani wahi hai, bas doosre kone se sunayi gayi."

Yuvan ne poocha, "To chef, mujhe ulta ratio kab use karna chahiye?"

"Jab sawaal ulta ho," Bhavna boli. "Jaise: 'agar flour 600 gram hai to sugar kitna?' Wahan flour ki taraf se sochna aasan hota hai."

Triangle ke teen ratio (sin, cos, tan) mein bhi yahi hota hai. Har ratio ko **ulta kar do** to teen naye ratio mil jaate hain. Unke naam hain **cosec θ, sec θ aur cot θ**. Is topic mein hum yahi teen seekhenge, aur end mein Yuvan ki ulti recipe ko wapas dekhenge.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Reciprocal (ulta)** | Kisi fraction ko flip kar dena: numerator neeche, denominator upar | 3/5 ka ulta 5/3 |
| **cosec θ** | hypotenuse ÷ opposite. sin θ ka ulta | sin θ = 3/5 to cosec θ = 5/3 |
| **sec θ** | hypotenuse ÷ adjacent. cos θ ka ulta | cos θ = 4/5 to sec θ = 5/4 |
| **cot θ** | adjacent ÷ opposite. tan θ ka ulta | tan θ = 3/4 to cot θ = 4/3 |
| **Jodi (pair)** | Ek ratio aur uska ulta, jinka guna 1 hota hai | sin θ aur cosec θ |

## Part A: Teen naye ratio

Topic 1 aur 2 ki tarah ek acute angle θ lo. Uske hisaab se triangle ki sides ke naam hain: opposite, adjacent aur hypotenuse. Ab teen naye ratio:

> **cosec θ = hypotenuse ÷ opposite**
> **sec θ = hypotenuse ÷ adjacent**
> **cot θ = adjacent ÷ opposite**

Aur jo teen purane ratio the:

> sin θ = opposite ÷ hypotenuse, cos θ = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse, tan θ = opposite ÷ adjacent

Dono ko saath rakh kar dekho to saaf dikhta hai ki har naya ratio purane ka **ulta** hai:

> **cosec θ = 1 ÷ sin θ**
> **sec θ = 1 ÷ cos θ**
> **cot θ = 1 ÷ tan θ**

Jaanch: sin θ = opposite ÷ hypotenuse ka ulta hypotenuse ÷ opposite hai, wahi cosec θ. ✅

### Kaun kiske saath? (Jodi yaad rakhne ka tareeka)

Yahan sabse zyada confusion hota hai, to dhyan se padho. "co" wale naam apas mein jodi nahi banate. Cos ki jodi sec hai, cosec nahi.

Ye yaad rakhne ka tareeka hai: **"Jo c se shuru hote hain, unki jodi un se hai jo c se shuru nahi hote."**

| c se shuru NAHI hota | c se shuru hota hai | Jodi |
|---|---|---|
| **s**in | **c**osec | sin ↔ cosec |
| **s**ec | **c**os | cos ↔ sec |
| **t**an | **c**ot | tan ↔ cot |

Dhyan do ki table mein teen "c se shuru hone wale" naam hain (cos, cosec, cot), aur teen "c se shuru na hone wale" (sin, sec, tan). Har jodi mein ek naam c se shuru hota hai aur ek nahi:
- sin (c nahi) ↔ cosec (c hai) ✅
- cos (c hai) ↔ sec (c nahi) ✅
- tan (c nahi) ↔ cot (c hai) ✅

Isliye **cos ki jodi sec hai, cosec nahi**. Cosec ki jodi sin hai. Agar kabhi doubt ho, to hypotenuse dekho: cos mein hypotenuse **neeche** hai (adjacent ÷ hypotenuse), to uske ulta mein hypotenuse **upar** aana chahiye aur neeche adjacent. Wo sec hai.

### Jodi ka guna 1 hota hai

Kisi fraction ko uske ulte se guna karo to 1 aata hai. Jaise 3/5 × 5/3 = 15/15 = 1. Yahi baat har jodi ke liye sach hai:

> sin θ × cosec θ = 1, cos θ × sec θ = 1, tan θ × cot θ = 1

## Part B: Ek triangle ke saare chhe ratio

Ab ek triangle lete hain jo Topic 2 ke 3-4-5 se alag hai: **5-12-13**. Pehle check karo ki ye right triangle hai:

5² + 12² = 25 + 144 = 169 = 13² ✅

Triangle ABC mein ∠B = 90°, AB = 12, BC = 5, AC = 13. Angle ∠A ko dekh rahe hain. ∠A ke hisaab se:
- opposite = BC = 5
- adjacent = AB = 12
- hypotenuse = AC = 13

| Ratio | Sides se | Value (∠A) |
|---|---|---|
| sin A | 5 ÷ 13 | 5/13 |
| cos A | 12 ÷ 13 | 12/13 |
| tan A | 5 ÷ 12 | 5/12 |
| cosec A | 13 ÷ 5 | 13/5 |
| sec A | 13 ÷ 12 | 13/12 |
| cot A | 12 ÷ 5 | 12/5 |

**Jaanch (jodi ka guna):**
- sin A × cosec A = 5/13 × 13/5 = 65/65 = 1 ✅
- cos A × sec A = 12/13 × 13/12 = 156/156 = 1 ✅
- tan A × cot A = 5/12 × 12/5 = 60/60 = 1 ✅

## Part C: tan aur cot, sin aur cos se

Ek kaam ki baat: tan θ ko sin θ aur cos θ se bhi likh sakte hain. Sides ke saath dekho. Maan lo opposite = p, adjacent = b, hypotenuse = h.

sin θ ÷ cos θ = (p ÷ h) ÷ (b ÷ h)

Dono mein "÷ h" hai. Divide karte waqt hypotenuse **cancel** ho jaata hai:

(p ÷ h) ÷ (b ÷ h) = (p ÷ h) × (h ÷ b) = p ÷ b

Aur p ÷ b to tan θ hai. Isliye:

> **tan θ = sin θ ÷ cos θ**
> **cot θ = cos θ ÷ sin θ**

(cot ke liye bhi wahi kaam: (b ÷ h) ÷ (p ÷ h) = b ÷ p.)

**Jaanch, 5-12-13 triangle mein:**
- sin A ÷ cos A = (5/13) ÷ (12/13) = 5/13 × 13/12 = 5/12 = tan A ✅
- cos A ÷ sin A = (12/13) ÷ (5/13) = 12/13 × 13/5 = 12/5 = cot A ✅

## Part D: Solved examples

### Example 1: Sides di hain, saare chhe ratio likho

Triangle ABC mein ∠B = 90°, AB = 8, BC = 15, AC = 17. ∠A ke saare chhe ratio likho.

Pehle jaanch: 8² + 15² = 64 + 225 = 289 = 17² ✅

∠A ke hisaab se: opposite = BC = 15, adjacent = AB = 8, hypotenuse = AC = 17.

- sin A = 15/17
- cos A = 8/17
- tan A = 15/8
- cosec A = 17/15
- sec A = 17/8
- cot A = 8/15

Ye sab fractions pehle se lowest terms mein hain (15 aur 17, 8 aur 17, 15 aur 8 mein koi common factor nahi).

**Jaanch:** sin A × cosec A = 15/17 × 17/15 = 1 ✅, cos A × sec A = 8/17 × 17/8 = 1 ✅, tan A × cot A = 15/8 × 8/15 = 1 ✅

### Example 2: Ek side kam hai, pehle Pythagoras

Ek right triangle mein ∠θ ke opposite wali side 20 hai aur hypotenuse 29 hai. Saare chhe ratio likho.

**Step 1:** adjacent nikalo. adjacent² = 29² − 20² = 841 − 400 = 441, to adjacent = 21.

**Jaanch:** 20² + 21² = 400 + 441 = 841 = 29² ✅

**Step 2:** ratio.
- sin θ = 20/29
- cos θ = 21/29
- tan θ = 20/21
- cosec θ = 29/20
- sec θ = 29/21
- cot θ = 21/20

### Example 3: Lowest terms mein likhna

Ek right triangle mein ∠θ ke opposite wali side 6 hai aur hypotenuse 10 hai. Saare chhe ratio lowest terms mein likho.

**Step 1:** adjacent² = 10² − 6² = 100 − 36 = 64, to adjacent = 8. Jaanch: 6² + 8² = 36 + 64 = 100 ✅

**Step 2:** ratio, aur fir lowest terms.
- sin θ = 6/10 = **3/5**
- cos θ = 8/10 = **4/5**
- tan θ = 6/8 = **3/4**
- cosec θ = 10/6 = **5/3**
- sec θ = 10/8 = **5/4**
- cot θ = 8/6 = **4/3**

**Jaanch:** sin θ × cosec θ = 3/5 × 5/3 = 1 ✅, cos θ × sec θ = 4/5 × 5/4 = 1 ✅, tan θ × cot θ = 3/4 × 4/3 = 1 ✅

Dekho, ye wahi 3-4-5 ka ratio nikla (6-8-10 uska double hai). Matlab bada triangle, par ratio wahi, kyunki ratio sirf angle par depend karte hain.

### Example 4: sin diya hai, cosec turant likho

Agar sin θ = 4/5, to cosec θ kya hai?

cosec θ = 1 ÷ sin θ = 1 ÷ (4/5) = 5/4.

Seedha flip kar do: **cosec θ = 5/4**.

**Jaanch:** 4/5 × 5/4 = 1 ✅

Isi tarah:
- cos θ = 12/13 to sec θ = **13/12**
- tan θ = 7/24 to cot θ = **24/7**
- cot θ = 3/8 to tan θ = **8/3** (ulta dono taraf chalta hai)

Dhyan rahe: yahan humne sirf ek ratio ko flip kiya. Bachi hui sides kaise nikalen, ye aage ka topic hai.

### Example 5: Surd wala case, sides 1, 1, √2

Ek right triangle ki do chhoti sides 1 aur 1 hain. Hypotenuse = √(1² + 1²) = √(1 + 1) = √2.

Jaanch: 1² + 1² = 2 = (√2)² ✅

Ek acute angle θ ke liye: opposite = 1, adjacent = 1, hypotenuse = √2.

- sin θ = 1/√2
- cos θ = 1/√2
- tan θ = 1/1 = 1
- cosec θ = √2/1 = √2
- sec θ = √2/1 = √2
- cot θ = 1/1 = 1

Dekho, yahan ulta karna aur bhi mazedaar hai: sin θ = 1/√2 ka ulta seedha √2 hai, koi mushkil nahi.

**Jaanch:** sin θ × cosec θ = 1/√2 × √2 = √2/√2 = 1 ✅. tan θ × cot θ = 1 × 1 = 1 ✅

Is triangle ka angle kya hai, ye hum Topic 4 mein dekhenge.

### Example 6: tan = sin ÷ cos ko 8-15-17 par check karo

Example 1 ke ∠A ke liye: sin A = 15/17, cos A = 8/17.

sin A ÷ cos A = (15/17) ÷ (8/17) = 15/17 × 17/8 = 15/8 = tan A ✅

cos A ÷ sin A = (8/17) ÷ (15/17) = 8/17 × 17/15 = 8/15 = cot A ✅

### Example 7: Doosra angle, doosre ratio

Example 1 wale triangle (AB = 8, BC = 15, AC = 17, ∠B = 90°) mein ab **∠C** ko dekho. ∠C ke hisaab se sides ke naam badal jaate hain:
- opposite = AB = 8
- adjacent = BC = 15
- hypotenuse = AC = 17

- sin C = 8/17
- cos C = 15/17
- tan C = 8/15
- cosec C = 17/8
- sec C = 17/15
- cot C = 15/8

Dhyan do: ∠A ke liye cosec A = 17/15 tha, aur ∠C ke liye cosec C = 17/8. Alag angle, alag ratio. Isliye pehle hamesha poochho: **main kis angle se dekh raha hoon?**

## Part E: Do observations (sirf dekhna hai)

### Observation 1: sec aur cosec kabhi 1 se chhote nahi hote

Hypotenuse right triangle ki sabse lambi side hoti hai. sec θ = hypotenuse ÷ adjacent aur cosec θ = hypotenuse ÷ opposite, dono mein upar sabse bada number hai. Bada number chhote number se divide hoga to jawab **1 ya usse bada** hi aayega.

Check: ab tak ke saare sec aur cosec dekho: 13/5, 13/12, 17/15, 17/8, 29/20, 29/21, 5/3, 5/4, √2. Sab 1 se bade hain. ✅

Isliye agar koi kahe ki acute angle ke liye sec θ = 3/4, to samajh jao ki kuch galat hai: 3/4 to 1 se chhota hai.

### Observation 2: Angle chhota ho to cot bada ho jata hai

Ek right triangle lo jisme adjacent side 10 hai. Opposite side ko chhota karte jao:

| Opposite | Adjacent | cot θ = adjacent ÷ opposite |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 10 | 2 |
| 2 | 10 | 5 |
| 1 | 10 | 10 |
| 0.5 | 10 | 20 |

Opposite jitni chhoti hoti jaati hai, utna angle θ chhota hota jaata hai (triangle patla ho raha hai), aur cot θ utna bada hota jaata hai. Ye sirf ek jhalak hai, aage ke topics mein iska zyada kaam aayega.

## Wapas Yuvan ki recipe par

Recipe ka ratio tha **sugar : flour = 1 : 3**. Isse fraction ki tarah likho: sugar ÷ flour = 1/3.

Iska ulta hai flour ÷ sugar = 3/1, yaani **3 : 1**. Ye bhi sahi ratio hai, par ye **flour ki taraf se** kahani sunata hai: "flour sugar se 3 guna hai."

Yuvan ne kya galti ki? Usne ulte ratio ko recipe ka ratio maan liya. 1 : 3 ko "flour : sugar" padh liya, jabki wo "sugar : flour" tha. Dono ratio sahi hain, par **kaun sa upar aur kaun sa neeche** ye pata hona chahiye.

Triangle mein bhi yahi hai:
- sin θ = opposite ÷ hypotenuse (opposite upar)
- cosec θ = hypotenuse ÷ opposite (hypotenuse upar)

Dono ek hi story sunate hain, par alag taraf se. Bhavna ne Yuvan ko samjhaya, "Jab ratio padho, to dhyan se dekho kaun sa upar hai."

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: cos ko cosec ke saath jodna**

Naam ke "co" dekh kar lagta hai ki cos aur cosec ek jodi hain, par **cos ki jodi sec hai**. 5-12-13 triangle mein:
- cos A = 12/13, ulta = 13/12 = sec A ✅
- cosec A = 13/5 (ye to sin A = 5/13 ka ulta hai)

Yaad rakho: c se shuru hone wale ki jodi, c se shuru na hone wale se.

**Galti 2: Ulta karna bhool jana**

Sawaal: sin θ = 3/5 hai, cosec θ kya hai?
- Galat: cosec θ = 3/5 (bas wahi likh diya)
- Sahi: cosec θ = 5/3 (flip kiya)

**Galti 3: Sirf numerator ya sirf denominator ko ulta karna**

Pura fraction ek saath flip hota hai. 4/5 ka ulta 5/4 hai.
- Galat: sirf numerator ulta kar ke 1/4 ÷ 5 likh dena, ya sirf denominator ulta kar ke 4 ÷ (1/5) jaisa kuch (dono mein 5/4 nahi milta).
- Sahi: numerator aur denominator dono ki jagah badal do, 5/4.

Jaanch hamesha kar sakte ho: 4/5 × 5/4 = 1 aana chahiye. Agar nahi aaya to flip galat hua.

**Galti 4: Galat angle se dekhna**

Example 7 mein dekha: ∠A aur ∠C ke liye alag ratio the. Agar ∠C ke sawaal mein ∠A ke hisaab se opposite le liya, to saare ratio galat honge. Pehle angle ko mark karo, phir opposite aur adjacent likho.

**Galti 5: 1 ÷ sin θ ko sin⁻¹ θ likhna**

Kabhi-kabhi log 1 ÷ sin θ ko sin⁻¹ θ likh dete hain. **Ye galat hai.** Calculator ya kitaab mein jo sin⁻¹ dikhta hai, uska matlab bilkul alag hota hai (ye aage ki class mein dekhoge). Ulte ratio ko hamesha **cosec θ** likhte hain, ya 1 ÷ sin θ.

## Quick Recap

- **cosec θ = hypotenuse ÷ opposite = 1 ÷ sin θ**
- **sec θ = hypotenuse ÷ adjacent = 1 ÷ cos θ**
- **cot θ = adjacent ÷ opposite = 1 ÷ tan θ**
- **Jodi:** sin ↔ cosec, cos ↔ sec, tan ↔ cot. "c se shuru hone wale ki jodi, c se shuru na hone wale se." Cos ki jodi sec hai, cosec nahi.
- Har jodi ka guna 1 hota hai.
- **tan θ = sin θ ÷ cos θ** aur **cot θ = cos θ ÷ sin θ** (hypotenuse cancel ho jata hai).
- Acute angle ke liye **sec θ aur cosec θ hamesha 1 ya usse bade** hote hain.
- Angle chhota hota jaye to **cot θ bada** hota jata hai.
- Ulta karne ka matlab poora fraction flip karna. Jaanch: jodi ka guna 1 aana chahiye.
- 1 ÷ sin θ ko sin⁻¹ θ nahi likhte.
