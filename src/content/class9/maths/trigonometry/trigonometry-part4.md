# Topic 4: Standard Angles: 0°, 30°, 45°, 60° aur 90°

Ab tak humne right-angled triangle ki teeno sides ke naam seekhe, phir sin, cos, tan bane, aur Topic 3 mein unke reciprocals cosec, sec, cot bhi mil gaye. Yaani ab hamare paas **chhe ratios** hain. Par ek baat abhi baaki hai: ye ratios kisi bhi angle ke liye ek number dete hain, aur wo number aam taur par table ya calculator se milta hai. Kya kuch angles aise hain jinke ratios hum **bina table aur bina calculator ke, bilkul exact** nikaal sakte hain?

Haan, paanch angles aise hain: **0°, 30°, 45°, 60° aur 90°**. Inhe hi standard angles kehte hain. Aur unke ratios nikalne ke liye humein bas Pythagoras theorem aur do chhote triangle chahiye. Pythagoras ki yaad ke liye [pythagoras-theorem.md](../pythagoras-theorem/pythagoras-theorem.md) dekh sakte ho, aur √2, √3 jaise surds ke liye [number-systems-part4.md](../number-systems/number-systems-part4.md) (simplify karna) aur [number-systems-part5.md](../number-systems/number-systems-part5.md) (rationalise karna).

## Set-square ka raaz

Ek stationery factory mein plastic ki bandi sheets machine ke neeche se nikal rahi hain. Factory ke malik Mr. Fonseca ek table ke paas khade hain, jahan worker Leela sheets ko kaat kar do tarah ke set-square bana rahi hai. Ek set-square ke angles 30°, 60°, 90° hain, aur doosre ke 45°, 45°, 90°.

Leela ne kaam rokte hue poochha, "Sir, har geometry box mein bas yahi do set-square kyun hote hain? Koi 37° ya 52° wala kyun nahi banta?"

Mr. Fonseca muskuraye, "Kyunki in do shapes ki sides ek **bahut saaf ratio** mein hoti hain. Inke trigonometric ratios hum **exactly** bata sakte hain, bina table ke, bina calculator ke. 37° ka ratio itna saaf nahi nikalta."

Leela ne kaan khade kiye, "Exactly? Kaise?"

"Chalo," Mr. Fonseca ne ek square sheet uthayi, "pehle ise kaatte hain."

Is topic mein hum wahi karenge, jo Mr. Fonseca ne Leela ko dikhaya: ek square aur ek equilateral triangle se paanchon standard angles ke saare ratios nikalna.

> **Note:** √2 ≈ 1.414 aur √3 ≈ 1.732. Ye sirf andaaza lagane ke liye hain. Hamare answers **exact** honge, yaani √2 ya √3 ke saath hi likhe jayenge. Isliye in values ke liye table ya calculator ki zaroorat nahi padti.

## Part A: 45°-45°-90° wala triangle

Mr. Fonseca ne **1 cm side** ka ek square liya aur use ek **diagonal** par kaat diya. Ab ek right-angled triangle bana:

- Dono legs (square ki sides): 1 aur 1
- Square ke kone ka 90° diagonal ne barabar do hisson mein baant diya, to is triangle ke angles hain **45°, 45°, 90°**
- Hypotenuse (diagonal): Pythagoras se

> hypotenuse² = 1² + 1² = 1 + 1 = 2, to hypotenuse = **√2**

Sides ka ratio: **1 : 1 : √2**.

Ab angle 45° par khade hokar teeno sides pehchaano. Dono legs barabar (1 aur 1) hain, isliye opposite = 1, adjacent = 1, hypotenuse = √2.

- sin 45° = opposite ÷ hypotenuse = **1/√2**
- cos 45° = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse = **1/√2**
- tan 45° = opposite ÷ adjacent = 1/1 = **1**

Ab Topic 3 ke reciprocals (ulta kar do):

- cosec 45° = √2/1 = **√2**
- sec 45° = √2/1 = **√2**
- cot 45° = 1/1 = **1**

**1/√2 ko saaf karna:** Upar ya neeche √ ho to rationalise karte hain (number-systems-part5 wala tareeka). Upar-neeche √2 se multiply karo:

1/√2 = (1 × √2) ÷ (√2 × √2) = √2/2

To **sin 45° = cos 45° = 1/√2 = √2/2**. Dono form sahi hain; aage table mein √2/2 hi likhenge, kyunki isse pattern dikhta hai.

**Jaanch:** √2/2 ≈ 1.414 ÷ 2 = 0.707, aur 1/√2 ≈ 1 ÷ 1.414 = 0.707 ✅. Aur sin 45° = cos 45° isliye, kyunki dono legs barabar hain.

## Part B: 30°-60°-90° wala triangle

Ab doosra set-square. Mr. Fonseca ne ek **equilateral triangle** (teeno sides barabar, teeno angles 60°) liya, **side 2 cm**. Phir ek vertex se samne wali side par **perpendicular** giraya.

Ye perpendicular teen kaam karta hai:

- Base ko barabar do hisson mein baant deta hai: 1 aur 1
- Upar ke 60° ke angle ko do 30° mein baant deta hai
- Ek bada triangle do chhote right-angled triangles mein tod deta hai

Ek chhota triangle dekho. Uski sides:

- Hypotenuse = 2 (equilateral triangle ki ek side)
- Chhoti leg (aadhe base) = 1
- Height (perpendicular) = Pythagoras se:

> height² + 1² = 2², to height² = 4 − 1 = 3, to height = **√3**

Is triangle ke angles hain **30°, 60°, 90°**, aur sides ka ratio: **1 : √3 : 2**.

Yaad rakhne ka tareeka: **sabse chhota angle (30°) ke saamne sabse chhoti side (1)**, aur **sabse bade angle (90°) ke saamne sabse badi side (2)**.

### 30° ke ratios

30° par khade ho to: opposite = 1, adjacent = √3, hypotenuse = 2.

- sin 30° = 1/2
- cos 30° = √3/2
- tan 30° = 1/√3 = (1 × √3) ÷ (√3 × √3) = **√3/3**

### 60° ke ratios

60° par khade ho to: opposite = √3, adjacent = 1, hypotenuse = 2.

- sin 60° = √3/2
- cos 60° = 1/2
- tan 60° = √3/1 = **√3**

### Reciprocals

- cosec 30° = 2/1 = **2**, sec 30° = 2/√3 = **2√3/3**, cot 30° = √3/1 = **√3**
- cosec 60° = 2/√3 = **2√3/3**, sec 60° = 2/1 = **2**, cot 60° = 1/√3 = **√3/3**

**Jaanch:** sin 30° × cosec 30° = 1/2 × 2 = 1 ✅. tan 60° × cot 60° = √3 × (1/√3) = 1 ✅. Aur √3/2 ≈ 1.732 ÷ 2 = 0.866, jo 1 se chhota hai (leg hamesha hypotenuse se chhoti hoti hai) ✅.

Dhyan do: sin 30° = cos 60° aur sin 60° = cos 30°. Ye ek hi triangle ko do kone se dekhne ka nateeja hai (is par Topic 6 mein detail se baat hogi).

## Part C: 0° aur 90° (limit ki soch)

Ab do aise angles ke baare mein sochte hain jinke liye koi asli triangle ban hi nahi sakta (0° ka triangle ek sapaat line hoga!). Isliye yahan hum **ek idea** se kaam chalate hain.

> **Limit ki soch, aage ki class mein poori tarah.** Yahan sirf andaaza lagana hai, pakka proof nahi.

Ek right-angled triangle lo jiski hypotenuse hamesha **10** rahe, aur angle θ ko dheere-dheere chhota karte jao:

| θ | Opposite (lagbhag) | Adjacent (lagbhag) | sin θ | cos θ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30° | 5 | 8.66 | 0.5 | 0.866 |
| 10° | 1.74 | 9.85 | 0.174 | 0.985 |
| 1° | 0.17 | 10.0 | 0.017 | 1.000 |

Dekho kya ho raha hai. Jaise-jaise θ chhota hota hai, triangle **patla aur patla** hota jaata hai:

- Opposite side **chhoti hoti jaati hai**, 0 ki taraf
- Adjacent side **badhti jaati hai** aur hypotenuse ke barabar hone lagti hai

Isliye θ → 0° par:

- **sin 0° = 0 ÷ hypotenuse = 0**
- **cos 0° = hypotenuse ÷ hypotenuse = 1**
- **tan 0° = 0 ÷ adjacent = 0**

Ab ulta: θ ko 90° ki taraf le jao. Triangle phir patla ho jaata hai, par is baar **adjacent side chhoti** hoti jaati hai (0 ki taraf) aur **opposite side hypotenuse ke barabar** hone lagti hai. Isliye θ → 90° par:

- **sin 90° = hypotenuse ÷ hypotenuse = 1**
- **cos 90° = 0 ÷ hypotenuse = 0**
- **tan 90° = opposite ÷ 0**, ye **not defined** hai, kyunki 0 se divide nahi hota

### Kaun-kaun si ratios not defined hain?

Reciprocals ke liye hamesha dekho: kya neeche 0 aa raha hai?

| Ratio | Kaise banta hai | Value |
|---|---|---|
| cosec 0° | 1 ÷ sin 0° = 1 ÷ 0 | **not defined** |
| sec 0° | 1 ÷ cos 0° = 1 ÷ 1 | 1 |
| cot 0° | cos 0° ÷ sin 0° = 1 ÷ 0 | **not defined** |
| cosec 90° | 1 ÷ sin 90° = 1 ÷ 1 | 1 |
| sec 90° | 1 ÷ cos 90° = 1 ÷ 0 | **not defined** |
| cot 90° | cos 90° ÷ sin 90° = 0 ÷ 1 | 0 |

Aur upar tan 90° bhi not defined mil chuka hai. To kul chaar ratios not defined hain: **cosec 0°, cot 0°, sec 90°, tan 90°**. Baaki sab ki value hai.

## Part D: Poori Standard-Angle Table

| θ | sin | cos | tan | cosec | sec | cot |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0°** | 0 | 1 | 0 | not defined | 1 | not defined |
| **30°** | 1/2 | √3/2 | 1/√3 (= √3/3) | 2 | 2/√3 (= 2√3/3) | √3 |
| **45°** | √2/2 (= 1/√2) | √2/2 (= 1/√2) | 1 | √2 | √2 | 1 |
| **60°** | √3/2 | 1/2 | √3 | 2/√3 (= 2√3/3) | 2 | 1/√3 (= √3/3) |
| **90°** | 1 | 0 | not defined | 1 | not defined | 0 |

### Yaad rakhne ka pattern (sin wali row)

sin ki row ko aise likho: 0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90° ke liye

> √0/2, √1/2, √2/2, √3/2, √4/2

Ab har ek ko simplify karo:

- √0/2 = 0
- √1/2 = 1/2
- √2/2 (waise hi rehne do, ye = 1/√2)
- √3/2
- √4/2 = 2/2 = 1

To sin ki row hai: **0, 1/2, √2/2, √3/2, 1**. Table se milao: sin 0° = 0, sin 30° = 1/2, sin 45° = √2/2, sin 60° = √3/2, sin 90° = 1 ✅.

**cos ki row wahi row ulti likhi hui hai:** cos 0° = 1, cos 30° = √3/2, cos 45° = √2/2, cos 60° = 1/2, cos 90° = 0. Yaani sin ki row **left se right** padho, cos ki row **right se left**.

**tan ki row:** tan θ = sin θ ÷ cos θ, aur table mein dono row ek-doosre ke upar-neeche hain, to tan ko turant check kar sakte ho. Jaise tan 30° = (1/2) ÷ (√3/2) = 1/√3 ✅, tan 60° = (√3/2) ÷ (1/2) = √3 ✅.

## Part E: Solved Examples (exact values)

Saare examples table ki values se, bina calculator ke.

### Example 1: sin 30° + cos 60°

sin 30° = 1/2 aur cos 60° = 1/2.

1/2 + 1/2 = **1**

**Jaanch:** decimal mein sin 30° + cos 60° = 0.5 + 0.5 = 1.0 ✅

### Example 2: 2 tan 45° + cos 60° − sin 30°

tan 45° = 1, cos 60° = 1/2, sin 30° = 1/2.

2 × 1 + 1/2 − 1/2 = 2 + 0 = **2**

**Jaanch:** 2 × tan(45°) + cos(60°) − sin(30°) = 2.0 ✅

### Example 3: sin 60° cos 30° + cos 60° sin 30°

sin 60° = √3/2, cos 30° = √3/2, cos 60° = 1/2, sin 30° = 1/2.

Pehla hissa: (√3/2) × (√3/2) = (√3 × √3) ÷ 4 = 3/4
Doosra hissa: (1/2) × (1/2) = 1/4

3/4 + 1/4 = 4/4 = **1**

**Jaanch:** sin(60°)cos(30°) + cos(60°)sin(30°) = 0.75 + 0.25 = 1.0 ✅

### Example 4: 4 sin 60° − 3 tan 30°

sin 60° = √3/2, tan 30° = 1/√3.

4 × (√3/2) = 2√3
3 × (1/√3) = 3/√3 = (3 × √3) ÷ (√3 × √3) = 3√3/3 = √3

2√3 − √3 = **√3** (≈ 1.732)

**Jaanch:** 4 sin(60°) − 3 tan(30°) = 3.4641 − 1.7321 = 1.7320 ≈ √3 ✅

Yahan agar tan 30° ko pehle hi 0.577 likh dete, to jawab 1.732 ke bahut paas aata par exact √3 nahi dikhta. Exact value rakhne se √3 saaf nikal aaya.

### Example 5: cosec 30° + sec 60° − cot 45°

cosec 30° = 2, sec 60° = 2, cot 45° = 1.

2 + 2 − 1 = **3**

**Jaanch:** 1 ÷ sin(30°) + 1 ÷ cos(60°) − 1 ÷ tan(45°) = 2 + 2 − 1 = 3.0 ✅

### Example 6: sin 45° cos 45° + tan 30° tan 60°

sin 45° = cos 45° = √2/2, tan 30° = 1/√3, tan 60° = √3.

Pehla hissa: (√2/2) × (√2/2) = (√2 × √2) ÷ 4 = 2/4 = 1/2
Doosra hissa: (1/√3) × √3 = 1

1/2 + 1 = **3/2**

**Jaanch:** sin(45°)cos(45°) + tan(30°)tan(60°) = 0.5 + 1.0 = 1.5 ✅

### Example 7: Leela ka set-square (factory wala sawaal)

Leela ko ek **30°-60°-90° set-square** banana hai jiski **hypotenuse 20 cm** ho. Dono legs kitni lambi kategi?

30° ke saamne wali (chhoti) leg ke liye:
sin 30° = opposite ÷ hypotenuse, to opposite = hypotenuse × sin 30° = 20 × 1/2 = **10 cm**

60° ke saamne wali (lambi) leg ke liye:
sin 60° = opposite ÷ hypotenuse, to opposite = 20 × √3/2 = **10√3 cm** ≈ 10 × 1.732 = **17.32 cm**

Ye wahi ratio hai: sides 1 : √3 : 2 ko 10 se multiply karo to 10 : 10√3 : 20.

**Jaanch (Pythagoras):** 10² + (10√3)² = 100 + 300 = 400 = 20² ✅

**Bonus:** 45°-45°-90° set-square ki hypotenuse bhi 20 cm ho, to har leg = 20 × sin 45° = 20 × √2/2 = 10√2 ≈ 14.14 cm. Jaanch: (10√2)² + (10√2)² = 200 + 200 = 400 = 20² ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: sin 30° aur sin 60° ko ulta likhna**

sin 30° = **1/2** (chhota angle, chhoti value) aur sin 60° = **√3/2** (bada angle, badi value). 0° se 90° tak sin ki value 0 se 1 tak **badhti** hai, to 30° ki value 60° se chhoti hi hogi. √3/2 ≈ 0.866 aur 1/2 = 0.5: 0.5 chhota hai, to 30° ke saath yahi jaayega.

**Galti 2: tan 30° ko √3 likhna**

Sahi: tan 30° = **1/√3** (≈ 0.577). √3 hai **tan 60°**. Dhyan se dekho: 30° par opposite chhoti side (1) hai, to tan 30° = 1/√3 chhota number hoga. √3 ≈ 1.732 bada hai, to 60° wale ka hai.

**Galti 3: tan 90° ko koi number ya "infinity" maan lena**

tan 90° = opposite ÷ 0, aur 0 se divide nahi hota. Isliye **tan 90° not defined** hai. Isse "∞" likh kar value bana dena ya 1 likh dena galat hai. Yahi baat sec 90°, cosec 0° aur cot 0° par bhi lagti hai.

**Galti 4: sin 0° = 1 likhna**

θ = 0° par opposite side 0 ho jaati hai, to sin 0° = 0 ÷ hypotenuse = **0**. 1 to **cos 0°** hai. Aur sin 90° = 1. Ek line mein yaad rakho: "sin 0 se shuru hota hai aur 1 par khatam; cos 1 se shuru hota hai aur 0 par khatam."

**Galti 5: sin aur cos ki rows ko mix kar dena**

sin ki row **0, 1/2, √2/2, √3/2, 1** hai, cos ki row **1, √3/2, √2/2, 1/2, 0** (ulti). Agar 60° par sin ki jagah 1/2 likh diya to aapne cos ki row padh li.

**Galti 6: jahan exact surd chahiye wahan decimal likh dena**

Example 4 mein sin 60° ko 0.866 aur tan 30° ko 0.577 likhoge to answer 4 × 0.866 − 3 × 0.577 = 1.733 aayega, jo √3 jaisa dikhta hi nahi aur thoda galat bhi hai. Jab sawaal "find the value" kahe to **√ ke saath exact answer** likho. Decimal sirf andaaza lagane ke liye (jaise Example 7 mein 17.32 cm).

## Quick Recap

- **45°-45°-90°:** sides 1 : 1 : √2. sin 45° = cos 45° = 1/√2 = √2/2, tan 45° = 1, cosec 45° = sec 45° = √2, cot 45° = 1.
- **30°-60°-90°:** sides 1 : √3 : 2. sin 30° = 1/2, cos 30° = √3/2, tan 30° = 1/√3; sin 60° = √3/2, cos 60° = 1/2, tan 60° = √3. cosec 30° = 2, sec 30° = 2/√3, cot 30° = √3; cosec 60° = 2/√3, sec 60° = 2, cot 60° = 1/√3.
- **0° aur 90°:** sin 0° = 0, cos 0° = 1, tan 0° = 0; sin 90° = 1, cos 90° = 0, tan 90° not defined. Ye limit ki soch hai, poori tarah aage ki class mein.
- **Not defined:** cosec 0°, cot 0°, sec 90°, tan 90° (jahan 0 se divide karna padta hai).
- **sin row:** √0/2, √1/2, √2/2, √3/2, √4/2 = 0, 1/2, √2/2, √3/2, 1. **cos row:** wahi ulti.
- √2 ≈ 1.414, √3 ≈ 1.732 sirf andaaze ke liye. Answer **exact** likho, to table ya calculator ki zaroorat nahi.
- Galtiyan: sin 30° aur sin 60° ulta, tan 30° = √3, tan 90° ko number maanna, sin 0° = 1, rows mix karna, decimal mein jaldi badal dena.

## Practice Questions

1. An equilateral triangle has side 4 cm. Drop a perpendicular from one vertex to the opposite side. Find the length of this perpendicular, and use it to find sin 60° and cos 60°.

2. Find the exact value of 2 sin 30° + 3 cos 60°.

3. Find the exact value of tan 60° × tan 30° + sin 90° − cos 0°.

4. Find the exact value of cosec 45° × sec 45° − cot 45°.

5. Find the exact value of sin 60° + cos 30° − tan 60°.

6. A 45°-45°-90° set-square has a hypotenuse of 30 cm. Find the length of each leg in exact form and also as a decimal (use √2 ≈ 1.414).

7. A student writes "tan 30° = √3" and another writes "sin 0° = 1". Explain what is wrong in each and give the correct values.

8. State True or False:
   (a) sin 90° = 1
   (b) tan 90° = 1
   (c) sin 45° = cos 45°
   (d) sec 90° = 0
   (e) cosec 0° is not defined

### Answers

1. Equilateral triangle ki side 4 hai, to perpendicular base ko 2 aur 2 mein baantta hai. Chhota right triangle: hypotenuse 4, base 2, to height² = 4² − 2² = 16 − 4 = 12, height = √12 = **2√3 cm** (≈ 3.46 cm).
   60° par: opposite = 2√3, adjacent = 2, hypotenuse = 4.
   sin 60° = 2√3/4 = **√3/2**, cos 60° = 2/4 = **1/2** ✅ (table se match).

2. sin 30° = 1/2, cos 60° = 1/2. 2 × 1/2 + 3 × 1/2 = 1 + 3/2 = **5/2**.
   Jaanch: 2(0.5) + 3(0.5) = 1 + 1.5 = 2.5 ✅

3. tan 60° × tan 30° = √3 × 1/√3 = 1. sin 90° = 1, cos 0° = 1. 1 + 1 − 1 = **1**.
   Jaanch: 1.0 + 1 − 1 = 1.0 ✅

4. cosec 45° = √2, sec 45° = √2, cot 45° = 1. √2 × √2 = 2. 2 − 1 = **1**.
   Jaanch: 1.414 × 1.414 ≈ 2.0, 2.0 − 1 = 1 ✅

5. sin 60° = √3/2, cos 30° = √3/2, tan 60° = √3. √3/2 + √3/2 = √3. √3 − √3 = **0**.
   Jaanch: 0.866 + 0.866 − 1.732 = 0 ✅

6. Leg = hypotenuse × sin 45° = 30 × √2/2 = **15√2 cm** ≈ 15 × 1.414 = **21.21 cm**.
   Jaanch: (15√2)² + (15√2)² = 450 + 450 = 900 = 30² ✅

7. "tan 30° = √3" galat hai: tan 30° = **1/√3** (≈ 0.577). √3 to tan 60° hai.
   "sin 0° = 1" galat hai: θ = 0° par opposite side 0 hai, to sin 0° = **0**. 1 to cos 0° hai (aur sin 90°).

8. (a) **True** ✅. Opposite side hypotenuse ke barabar ho jaati hai.
   (b) **False** ❌. tan 90° not defined hai, kyunki 0 se divide karna padta hai.
   (c) **True** ✅. Dono √2/2 hain.
   (d) **False** ❌. sec 90° = 1 ÷ cos 90° = 1 ÷ 0, jo not defined hai (0 nahi).
   (e) **True** ✅. cosec 0° = 1 ÷ sin 0° = 1 ÷ 0, not defined.
