# Topic 5: Ek Ratio se Baaki Ratios aur Triangle Hal Karna

Pichhle topic mein humne kuch khaas angles (30°, 45°, 60°) ke ratios ek table mein yaad kiye. Wo table "angle di hai, ratio batao" wala kaam karti thi. Ab ulta kaam karte hain: **ek ratio di hai, baaki ratios batao**, aur **ek side aur ek angle di hai, baaki sides batao**. Isme humein kisi nayi table ki zaroorat nahi, bas ek chhota sa triangle aur Pythagoras theorem chahiye.

## Part A: Tareeka (method)

### Ye kaam kyun karta hai?

Ek hi angle A wale kitne bhi right triangles bana lo, chhote ya bade, unki sides alag hongi, par **sides ka ratio wahi rahega**. Isliye agar sin A = 3/5 hai, to hum koi bhi aisa right triangle le sakte hain jisme opposite : hypotenuse = 3 : 5. Sabse aasan wahi hai jisme opposite = 3 aur hypotenuse = 5 ho. Jo bhi sab ratios us triangle se niklenge, wahi angle A ke asli ratios honge.

### Step-by-step layout

| Step | Kya karna hai | sin A = 3/5 mein |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ek right triangle banao, angle A mark karo | △ABC, ∠B = 90°, angle A |
| 2 | Di hui ratio ko "kaun si side ÷ kaun si side" padho | sin = opposite ÷ hypotenuse, to opposite = 3, hypotenuse = 5 |
| 3 | Teesri side Pythagoras se nikalo | adjacent² = 5² − 3² = 25 − 9 = 16, to adjacent = 4 |
| 4 | Ab teeno sides likh lo | opposite 3, adjacent 4, hypotenuse 5 |
| 5 | Chhe ratios padh lo | sin 3/5, cos 4/5, tan 3/4, cosec 5/3, sec 5/4, cot 4/3 |

**Jaanch:** Step 5 mein sin = 3/5 wapas aaya, jo diya hua tha ✅. Aur tan = sin ÷ cos = (3/5) ÷ (4/5) = 3/4 ✅.

**Ek zaroori baat:** Ratio kisi bhi side ke liye 3 aur 5 hi ho sakta hai, par **sin ke liye 5 hamesha hypotenuse hai**, kyunki sin ke neeche hypotenuse hi hota hai. Isliye 5 ko leg mat bana dena.

### Example 1: Puzzle club ka clue (sin A = 3/5)

Upar wali table hi iska hal hai.

| Ratio | Kaise | Value |
|---|---|---|
| sin A | opposite ÷ hypotenuse | 3/5 |
| cos A | adjacent ÷ hypotenuse | 4/5 |
| tan A | opposite ÷ adjacent | 3/4 |
| cosec A | hypotenuse ÷ opposite | 5/3 |
| sec A | hypotenuse ÷ adjacent | 5/4 |
| cot A | adjacent ÷ opposite | 4/3 |

**Jaanch:** Teeno reciprocal pairs ka guna 1 hai: (3/5)(5/3) = 1, (4/5)(5/4) = 1, (3/4)(4/3) = 1 ✅.

### Example 2: tan θ = 5/12

tan = opposite ÷ adjacent, to opposite = 5, adjacent = 12.
Hypotenuse² = 5² + 12² = 25 + 144 = 169, to hypotenuse = **13**.

| sin θ | cos θ | tan θ | cosec θ | sec θ | cot θ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5/13 | 12/13 | 5/12 | 13/5 | 13/12 | 12/5 |

**Jaanch:** (5/13) ÷ (12/13) = 5/12 ✅, aur 13 ka square 169 = 25 + 144 ✅.

### Example 3: cos A = 8/17

cos = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse, to adjacent = 8, hypotenuse = 17.
Opposite² = 17² − 8² = 289 − 64 = 225, to opposite = **15**.

| sin A | cos A | tan A | cosec A | sec A | cot A |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15/17 | 8/17 | 15/8 | 17/15 | 17/8 | 8/15 |

**Jaanch:** 8² + 15² = 64 + 225 = 289 = 17² ✅.

### Example 4: sec θ = 25/7

sec = hypotenuse ÷ adjacent, to hypotenuse = 25, adjacent = 7.
Opposite² = 25² − 7² = 625 − 49 = 576, to opposite = **24**.

| sin θ | cos θ | tan θ | cosec θ | sec θ | cot θ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 24/25 | 7/25 | 24/7 | 25/24 | 25/7 | 7/24 |

**Jaanch:** 7² + 24² = 49 + 576 = 625 = 25² ✅.

Yahan sec di thi, to pehle usko "ulta" karke cos = 7/25 likh lena bhi theek rehta hai. Phir cos wala hi kaam.

### Example 5: Jab surd aa jaye (sin θ = 1/2)

sin = opposite ÷ hypotenuse, to opposite = 1, hypotenuse = 2.
Adjacent² = 2² − 1² = 4 − 1 = 3, to adjacent = **√3**. (Teesri side hamesha poora number nahi hoti. Surds ka kaam [number systems](../number-systems/number-systems-part4.md) mein dekha tha.)

| sin θ | cos θ | tan θ | cosec θ | sec θ | cot θ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1/2 | √3/2 | 1/√3 | 2 | 2/√3 | √3 |

**Jaanch:** 1² + (√3)² = 1 + 3 = 4 = 2² ✅. Ye wahi values hain jo hum Topic 4 mein 30° ke liye dekh chuke hain, to shayad θ = 30° hi hai. Is baat par Part D mein aate hain.

### Example 6: tan A = 4/3, to (sin A + cos A) ÷ (sin A − cos A) kitna?

tan = opposite ÷ adjacent, to opposite = 4, adjacent = 3.
Hypotenuse² = 16 + 9 = 25, to hypotenuse = 5.
sin A = 4/5, cos A = 3/5.

Ab bas daal do:

- sin A + cos A = 4/5 + 3/5 = 7/5
- sin A − cos A = 4/5 − 3/5 = 1/5
- Ratio = (7/5) ÷ (1/5) = (7/5) × 5 = **7**

**Jaanch:** Topic 3 ke tan = sin ÷ cos se bhi dekh sakte hain. Upar-neeche cos A se divide karo to (tan A + 1) ÷ (tan A − 1) = (4/3 + 1) ÷ (4/3 − 1) = (7/3) ÷ (1/3) = 7 ✅. Dono tareeke se 7 aaya.

## Part B: Right triangle hal karna (standard angle ke saath)

Ab ulta nahi, seedha: ek **side** aur ek **standard angle** di hai, baaki sides nikalni hain. Hum Topic 4 ke exact values use karenge: sin 30° = 1/2, cos 30° = √3/2, tan 30° = 1/√3 aur sin 45° = cos 45° = 1/√2, tan 45° = 1, sin 60° = √3/2, cos 60° = 1/2, tan 60° = √3.

**Tareeka:** Jo side di hai aur jo nikalni hai, unko jodne wala ratio chuno. Us ratio ki value likho aur chhoti si equation hal kar lo.

### Example 7: Hypotenuse 12, angle 30°

△ABC mein ∠B = 90°, ∠A = 30°, hypotenuse AC = 12.

- Opposite (BC) nikalni hai: opposite aur hypotenuse ka ratio sin hai.
  sin 30° = BC ÷ 12, to 1/2 = BC ÷ 12, **BC = 6**.
- Adjacent (AB) nikalni hai: adjacent aur hypotenuse ka ratio cos hai.
  cos 30° = AB ÷ 12, to √3/2 = AB ÷ 12, **AB = 12 × √3/2 = 6√3**.

**Jaanch (Pythagoras):** BC² + AB² = 6² + (6√3)² = 36 + 36 × 3 = 36 + 108 = 144 = 12² ✅.

### Example 8: Angle 45°, adjacent 5

△PQR mein ∠Q = 90°, ∠P = 45°, adjacent PQ = 5.

- Opposite QR: tan 45° = QR ÷ 5, to 1 = QR ÷ 5, **QR = 5**.
- Hypotenuse PR: cos 45° = 5 ÷ PR, to 1/√2 = 5 ÷ PR, **PR = 5√2**.

**Jaanch:** 5² + 5² = 25 + 25 = 50 = (5√2)² = 25 × 2 ✅.

Ye sach mein sahi lagta hai: 45° par dono legs barabar hone chahiye (do angle 45°, 45° barabar hain, to un ke samne wali sides bhi barabar). Aur hypotenuse leg se √2 guna lambi.

### Example 9: Angle 60°, opposite 9√3

△XYZ mein ∠Y = 90°, ∠X = 60°, opposite YZ = 9√3.

- Adjacent XY: tan 60° = 9√3 ÷ XY, to √3 = 9√3 ÷ XY, **XY = 9**.
- Hypotenuse XZ: sin 60° = 9√3 ÷ XZ, to √3/2 = 9√3 ÷ XZ, **XZ = 9√3 × 2 ÷ √3 = 18**.

**Jaanch:** 9² + (9√3)² = 81 + 243 = 324 = 18² ✅.

In teeno examples mein ek hi cheez dohrayi: **sahi ratio chuno, equation likho, hal karo, Pythagoras se check karo.**

## Part C: Ratio se angle pata karna

Agar ek ratio ki value kisi **standard value** se match kar jaye, to angle seedha pata chal jata hai. Topic 4 ki table ko ulta padho.

| Ratio di hai | Table se milta hai | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| sin θ = 1/2 | sin 30° = 1/2 | θ = 30° |
| tan θ = √3 | tan 60° = √3 | θ = 60° |
| cos θ = 1/√2 | cos 45° = 1/√2 | θ = 45° |
| tan θ = 1 | tan 45° = 1 | θ = 45° |

Example 5 mein sin θ = 1/2 aaya tha. Wahan humne cos θ = √3/2 aur tan θ = 1/√3 nikala, aur dono 30° wali table se match karte hain. Isliye θ = 30° pakka ho gaya.

**Ek chhota sawaal:** Triangle mein opposite 5 aur hypotenuse 10 hai. θ kitna?
sin θ = 5/10 = 1/2, to **θ = 30°** ✅.

**Dhyan do:** Ye sirf tab kaam karta hai jab ratio hamari table ki value ho. Jaise sin A = 3/5 ka angle humari table mein nahi hai. Wo kitna hota hai, ye nikalne ke liye **table ya calculator** lagta hai. **Baaki angles ke liye table ya calculator lagta hai, jo aage ki class mein.** Abhi hume bas itna chahiye tha ki angle jaane bina bhi saare ratios mil gaye.

## Part D: Sides ki tulna se sin aur cos

Right triangle mein do cheezein hum pehle dekh chuke hain: hypotenuse sabse lambi side hoti hai, aur **bade angle ke saamne lambi side** hoti hai (is baare mein [congruence of triangles](../congruence-of-triangles/congruence-of-triangles-part4.md) mein dekha tha). Ab isse sin aur cos ke baare mein kuch dekhte hain. Ye bas ek observation hai, koi rule yaad nahi karna.

**1. sin aur cos kabhi 1 se bade nahi hote.**
sin A = opposite ÷ hypotenuse aur cos A = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse. Hypotenuse sabse lambi hai, isliye upar wali side (numerator) neeche wali se chhoti hoti hai. Bhaag dene par 1 se kam hi milta hai. Isi tarah cosec aur sec kabhi 1 se kam nahi hote. (tan aur cot 1 se bade bhi ho sakte hain, kyunki wahan dono legs hain.)

**2. Kaun bada: sin ya cos?**
Dono ka denominator same (hypotenuse) hai, to jiska numerator bada, wo ratio bada. Matlab dekhna hai ki opposite lamba hai ya adjacent.

- Agar angle A **45° se bada** hai, to wo bada angle hai, aur uske saamne wali side (opposite) **adjacent se lambi** hogi. To sin A > cos A.
- Agar angle A **45° se chhota** hai, to opposite adjacent se chhoti hogi. To sin A < cos A.
- 45° par dono sides barabar hoti hain, to sin 45° = cos 45°.

**Jaanch (table se):** 30° (45° se chhota): sin 30° = 1/2 = 0.5, cos 30° = √3/2 ≈ 0.87, to sin < cos ✅. 60° (45° se bada): sin 60° = √3/2 ≈ 0.87, cos 60° = 1/2, to sin > cos ✅.

Ab bina kuch nikale: **sin 50° aur cos 50° mein kaun bada?** 50° bada hai 45° se, to **sin 50° > cos 50°**. Kya tum bata sakte ho ki sin 20° aur cos 20° mein kaun bada hai? (cos 20°.)

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Ratio ke hisaab se galat side chunna**

sin A = 3/5 mein 5 ko leg (adjacent) maan lena aur 3 ko opposite: tab hypotenuse² = 3² + 5² = 34. Ye galat hai, kyunki sin ke neeche hypotenuse hota hai.
- Sahi: opposite 3, **hypotenuse 5**, adjacent = 4.

**Galti 2: Simplify na karna**

Example: opposite 6 aur hypotenuse 10 se sin = 6/10 aa gaya. Isko **3/5** likhna chahiye. Kam se kam end mein simplify karo. Surd mein bhi: 2/√3 theek hai, par tan 45° ki jagah 5/5 nahi, **1** likho.

**Galti 3: Flip karte waqt numerator aur denominator ulajhna**

Agar sin A = 3/5 hai, to cosec A = **5/3** hota hai, 3/5 nahi aur na hi 5/4. Aur sec ka matlab cos ka ulta hai, sin ka nahi. Jaanch: reciprocal pair ka guna 1 hona chahiye.

**Galti 4: sin ya cos ko 1 se bada bata dena**

Koi sawaal kahe "sin A = 4/3". Ye ho hi nahi sakta, kyunki hypotenuse sabse lambi side hai aur opposite usse badi nahi ho sakti. Agar apne hal mein sin ya cos 1 se bada aaye, to pakka kahin galti hui hai. (tan 1 se bada ho sakta hai, jaise tan 60° = √3.)

**Galti 5: Hypotenuse ko Pythagoras mein jodna ya ghatana ulta kar dena**

Hypotenuse nikalni ho to **jodo** (legs ke squares), leg nikalni ho to hypotenuse² se **ghatao**. Example 3 mein 17² − 8² kiya tha. Agar 17² + 8² kar dete, to 353 aata, jo perfect square nahi, aur yahi signal hai ki kuch galat hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Ek ratio se baaki:** ratio ke hisaab se do sides chuno, teesri Pythagoras se nikalo, phir chhe ratios padh lo.
- Ye kaam karta hai kyunki **ratio sirf angle par depend karta hai**, triangle ke size par nahi. Koi bhi triangle chalega jisme wahi ratio ho.
- Puzzle club: sin A = 3/5 se cos A = 4/5, tan A = 3/4, cosec A = 5/3, sec A = 5/4, cot A = 4/3.
- Kuch ratios mein teesri side surd aati hai (sin θ = 1/2 se adjacent √3).
- **Triangle hal karna:** side aur standard angle de ho, to sahi ratio chuno, equation hal karo. Pythagoras se check karo.
- **Angle pata karna:** ratio table ki kisi standard value se match kare to angle mil jata hai (sin θ = 1/2 se 30°, tan θ = √3 se 60°). Baaki angles ke liye table ya calculator lagta hai.
- Hypotenuse sabse lambi, to sin aur cos 1 se bade nahi hote. 45° se bade angle ke liye sin > cos, chhote angle ke liye cos > sin.
- Galtiyan: galat side ko hypotenuse banana, simplify na karna, flip mein ulajhna, sin ya cos ko 1 se bada likhna.

## Practice Questions

1. If cos θ = 5/13, find the other five trigonometric ratios of θ.

2. If sin A = 9/41, find cos A and tan A.

3. If cot A = 20/21, find sin A, cos A and tan A.

4. If cosec θ = 2, draw a suitable right triangle, find the other ratios and name the angle θ.

5. If tan A = 3/4, find the value of (2 sin A + 3 cos A) ÷ (4 sin A − cos A).

6. In a right triangle, one acute angle is 60° and the hypotenuse is 14. Find the other two sides. Verify using Pythagoras theorem.

7. In a right triangle, one acute angle is 45° and the side opposite to it is 7√2. Find the other two sides.

8. Find the acute angle θ if: (a) sin θ = √3/2, (b) cos θ = 1/2, (c) tan θ = 1/√3.

9. Without calculating, say which is larger: (a) sin 70° or cos 70°; (b) sin 15° or cos 15°. Give a reason.

10. Samar says, "In my triangle, sin A = 7/5 and cos A = 24/25." Nandini says this cannot be right. Who is correct and why? Also, explain what Samar did wrong if he was told that sin A = 7/25.

11. State True or False:
   (a) If sin A = 3/5, then the hypotenuse of every right triangle with angle A is 5.
   (b) cos A can never be greater than 1.
   (c) In a right triangle, if angle A is less than 45°, then cos A > sin A.
   (d) If tan A = 1, then A = 45°.

### Answers

1. cos θ = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse = 5/13, to adjacent = 5, hypotenuse = 13.
   Opposite² = 169 − 25 = 144, opposite = 12.
   **sin θ = 12/13, tan θ = 12/5, cosec θ = 13/12, sec θ = 13/5, cot θ = 5/12** (cos θ = 5/13).
   Jaanch: 5² + 12² = 25 + 144 = 169 = 13² ✅

2. Opposite = 9, hypotenuse = 41, adjacent² = 41² − 9² = 1681 − 81 = 1600, adjacent = 40.
   **cos A = 40/41, tan A = 9/40.**
   Jaanch: 9² + 40² = 81 + 1600 = 1681 = 41² ✅

3. cot = adjacent ÷ opposite, to adjacent = 20, opposite = 21.
   Hypotenuse² = 400 + 441 = 841, hypotenuse = 29.
   **sin A = 21/29, cos A = 20/29, tan A = 21/20.**
   Jaanch: 20² + 21² = 841 = 29² ✅

4. cosec = hypotenuse ÷ opposite = 2, to hypotenuse = 2, opposite = 1.
   Adjacent² = 4 − 1 = 3, adjacent = √3.
   **sin θ = 1/2, cos θ = √3/2, tan θ = 1/√3, sec θ = 2/√3, cot θ = √3.**
   sin θ = 1/2 standard value hai, isliye **θ = 30°**.
   Jaanch: 1 + 3 = 4 = 2² ✅

5. tan A = 3/4, opposite = 3, adjacent = 4, hypotenuse = 5. sin A = 3/5, cos A = 4/5.
   Numerator = 2 × 3/5 + 3 × 4/5 = 6/5 + 12/5 = 18/5.
   Denominator = 4 × 3/5 − 4/5 = 12/5 − 4/5 = 8/5.
   Ratio = (18/5) ÷ (8/5) = 18/8 = **9/4**.
   Jaanch (tan se): numerator aur denominator ko cos A se divide karo: (2 tan A + 3) ÷ (4 tan A − 1) = (3/2 + 3) ÷ (3 − 1) = (9/2) ÷ 2 = 9/4 ✅

6. Angle 60°, hypotenuse = 14.
   Opposite (60° ke saamne): sin 60° = √3/2, to opposite = 14 × √3/2 = **7√3**.
   Adjacent: cos 60° = 1/2, to adjacent = 14 × 1/2 = **7**.
   Jaanch: 7² + (7√3)² = 49 + 147 = 196 = 14² ✅

7. 45° ke liye dono legs barabar hain, to opposite = adjacent = **7√2**.
   Hypotenuse: sin 45° = 1/√2 = 7√2 ÷ hypotenuse, to hypotenuse = 7√2 × √2 = 7 × 2 = **14**.
   Jaanch: (7√2)² + (7√2)² = 98 + 98 = 196 = 14² ✅

8. (a) sin 60° = √3/2, to **θ = 60°**.
   (b) cos 60° = 1/2, to **θ = 60°**.
   (c) tan 30° = 1/√3, to **θ = 30°**.

9. (a) 70° > 45°, to opposite lambi, **sin 70° > cos 70°**.
   (b) 15° < 45°, to adjacent lambi, **cos 15° > sin 15°**.
   Reason: dono ka denominator hypotenuse hai, to jiska numerator bada, wo ratio bada.

10. **Nandini sahi hai.** sin A = 7/5 > 1 ho hi nahi sakta, kyunki opposite (7) hypotenuse (5) se lambi ho gayi, jabki hypotenuse sabse lambi side hoti hai.
    Agar sin A = 7/25 hai, to opposite = 7, hypotenuse = 25, adjacent² = 625 − 49 = 576, adjacent = 24, to **cos A = 24/25** sahi hai. Matlab Samar ne sin galat likha tha (7/5 ki jagah 7/25), cos sahi tha.
    Jaanch: 7² + 24² = 49 + 576 = 625 = 25² ✅

11. (a) **False** ❌. Sirf ek triangle mein hypotenuse 5 hoti hai, doosre triangles mein 10, 15 ya koi aur ho sakti hai. Ratio wahi 3/5 rehta hai.
    (b) **True** ✅. cos = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse, aur adjacent hypotenuse se chhoti hoti hai.
    (c) **True** ✅. 45° se chhote angle ke saamne chhoti side hoti hai, to sin < cos.
    (d) **True** ✅. tan 45° = 1, aur hamari table mein sirf 45° par tan = 1.
