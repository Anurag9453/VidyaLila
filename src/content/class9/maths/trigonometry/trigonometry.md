# Topic 1: Right Triangle ke Teen Naam

Pichhle chapters mein humne right triangle ko achhe se jaana: ek kona 90° ka, uske saamne sabse lambi bhuja hypotenuse, aur Pythagoras ka rishta ki do chhoti bhujaon ke squares ka jod hypotenuse ke square ke barabar hota hai. Wahan humne triangle ko **bhujaon ki lambai** ke nazariye se dekha tha.

Ab ek naya sawaal hai: triangle ke **kono** ke baare mein kya? Is chapter ka pura khel ek hi baat par tika hai: right triangle ka ek chhota kona (θ) pakdo, aur dekho ki uske hisaab se teeno bhujaon ke **naam** kya hain. Is topic mein sirf yahi naam seekhne hain. Ye naam pakke ho gaye, to aage ka poora chapter aasaan ho jayega.

## Pahaad ki ropeway

Ek pahaadi kasbe mein ek ropeway (cable car) chalti hai. Neeche base station hai, aur upar pahaadi ki choti par hilltop station. Dono ke beech ek lamba, mota cable tana hua hai, jis par trolley chalti hai.

Ropeway ke operator hain Mr. Negi. Aaj unke saath nayi trainee Sia aayi hai, jise pehle din control room ka kaam samjhana hai. Mr. Negi ne Sia ko base station ki chhat par le jaakar upar ki taraf ishara kiya.

"Sia, wo cable dekh rahi ho? Hum tape se na to ye pahaadi naap sakte hain, na ye cable. Phir bhi mujhe pata hai ki hilltop station kitni **oonchai** par hai, aur cable kitna lamba hai. Kaise?"

Sia ne socha, "Sir, shayad koi triangle ka chakkar hai?"

"Bilkul. Dhyan se dekho. Teen cheezein hain:
- **Cable**: base station se seedha hilltop station tak.
- **Vertical rise**: hilltop station seedha neeche zameen tak kitna oopar hai.
- **Horizontal ground distance**: base station se us jagah tak, jo hilltop station ke theek neeche hai, zameen par kitni doori hai.

Ye teeno milkar ek **right triangle** banate hain, kyunki oonchai seedhi khadi hoti hai aur zameen seedhi leti hoti hai. Dono ke beech 90° ka kona hai."

Mr. Negi ne copy par ek chhota sketch banaya. Base station ko A, hilltop station ko C, aur choti ke theek neeche zameen wale bindu ko B naam diya.

```
                 C  (hilltop station)
                /|
               / |
    cable     /  |   vertical rise
   (130 m)   /   |   (50 m)
            /    |
           /θ    |
          A------B
   (base station)
     ground distance 120 m
```

Mr. Negi ne poochha: "Ab ek sawaal. Jab tum **neeche A par** khadi ho aur upar dekh rahi ho, to is triangle mein 'cable' kaunsi bhuja hai?"

Sia: "Sir, AC. Seedhi tirchhi wali."

"Sahi. Ab socho ki tum **upar C par** khadi ho aur neeche dekh rahi ho. Ab cable kaunsi bhuja hai?"

Sia ne ek second socha. "Sir, wahi AC. Wo to wahi hai."

"Wahi to! Cable ka naam kabhi nahi badalta. Wo hypotenuse hai. Par ab ek aur sawaal: jab tum A par khadi ho, to vertical rise BC tumhare kono θ ke **saamne** hai. Aur jab tum C par khadi ho, to vertical rise ka kya ho jayega?"

Sia hichkichayi. "Sir, C par khade hone se vertical rise to meri **bagal** ki bhuja ban jayegi, kyunki C se hi wo shuru hoti hai."

"Shabash. Isi mein poora raaz hai: **tum kis kone par khadi ho, usse bhujaon ke naam badal jaate hain.** Sirf cable ka naam nahi badalta."

Is topic mein hum Sia aur Mr. Negi ke saath yahi sab saaf karenge: naam kya hain, kaise pehchaanein, aur kab badalte hain. Ant mein ropeway ka sawaal wapas kholenge.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Trigonometry** | "Tribhuj ka maap". Greek shabdon tri (teen), gon (kona), metron (maap) se bana | Triangle ke kono aur bhujaon ke rishte ka study |

| **Reference angle** | Wo kona jisse hum bhujaon ko naam de rahe hain | Abhi A, phir C |

## Part A: Hum heights aur doori naapte kyun hain?

Sochiye, Sia ko kuch aise sawaal mile:
- Is ped ki oonchai kitni hai?
- Us mobile tower ki oonchai kitni hai?
- Pahaadi ki choti kitni oonchi hai?
- Nadi ki chaudai kitni hai, jab hum paar jaa hi nahi sakte?

Inme se kisi ko tape lekar naapna mumkin nahi. Ped par tape lekar chadhna khatarnak, tower aur pahaad par to naamumkin. Phir bhi insaan sadiyon se ye sab naapta aa raha hai. Kaise?

**Idea:** zameen par se ek kone ka maap lo (jo naapna aasaan hai), aur ek doori jo naap sakte ho. Phir triangle ke gunon se baaki sab nikal lo. Is poore vigyaan ko hi **trigonometry** kehte hain.

> **tri** = teen, **gon** = kona, **metron** = maap. Trigonometry matlab "tribhuj ka maap".

Is chapter mein hum ye sab karna seekhenge: ped ki oonchai, ladder ki lambai, ropeway ka cable, sab kuch bina chhue. Lekin pehla kadam sabse zaroori hai: **right triangle ke bhujaon ko sahi naam dena**. Wahi naam hamare saare aage ke formulas ki bhasha hoga.

## Part B: Teen bhujaon ke teen naam

Ek right triangle lijiye. Ek kona 90° ka hai. Baaki do kone 90° se chhote (acute) hain. Inme se **ek chhota kona** chuniye aur use θ naam dijiye.

```
        C
       /|
      / |
     /  |
    /   |
   /θ   |
  A-----B
```

Ab triangle ki teeno bhujaon ko naam dete hain.

**1. Hypotenuse (AC):** Right angle (B) ke saamne wali bhuja. Ye hamesha sabse lambi bhuja hoti hai. Ye kone θ par nirbhar nahi karti.

**2. Opposite side, saamne wali (BC):** Wo bhuja jo θ (kone A) ke **saamne** hai, yaani jo kone A ko chhooti hi nahi.

**3. Adjacent side, bagal wali (AB):** Wo bhuja jo θ ko chhooti hai aur jo hypotenuse nahi hai. Ye θ aur right angle, dono ko chhooti hai.

Yaad rakhne ka tareeka: **θ par khade ho jao.**
- Jo bhuja tumhare saamne door baithi hai, wo **opposite**.
- Jo bhuja tumhare theek bagal mein hai (aur right angle tak jaati hai), wo **adjacent**.
- Jo bhuja right angle ke saamne hai, wo **hypotenuse**.

| Naam | Kaise pehchaanein | Upar wale triangle mein (θ kone A par) |
|---|---|---|
| Hypotenuse | Right angle ke saamne, sabse lambi | AC |
| Opposite | θ ke saamne | BC |
| Adjacent | θ ko chhooti hai, hypotenuse nahi | AB |

**Ek aur trick:** pehle hypotenuse pakdo (right angle ke saamne wali bhuja). Phir jo do bhujaayen bachi, unme se jo θ ko chhooti hai, wo adjacent aur jo nahi chhooti, wo opposite.

## Part C: Kona badla, naam badle (hypotenuse nahi badalta)

Ab wahi ropeway wala triangle lete hain. Mr. Negi ke sketch mein △ABC, B par right-angled hai.

- ∠A = θ (base station par)
- ∠C = doosra chhota kona (hilltop station par)
- ∠B = 90°

**A se dekho (θ = ∠A):**

```
                 C
                /|
               / |
              /  |  <- opposite (BC)
  hypotenuse /   |
     (AC)   /    |
           /θ    |
          A------B
        adjacent (AB)
```

| Bhuja | A se dekhne par |
|---|---|
| AC (cable) | hypotenuse |
| BC (vertical rise) | **opposite** |
| AB (ground distance) | **adjacent** |

**C se dekho (∠C ko reference angle maano):**

```
                 C  <- ab yahan khade ho
                /|
               / |
              /  |  adjacent (BC)
  hypotenuse /   |
     (AC)   /    |
           /     |
          A------B
        opposite (AB)
```

| Bhuja | C se dekhne par |
|---|---|
| AC (cable) | hypotenuse (wahi) |
| BC (vertical rise) | **adjacent** (ab ye C ko chhooti hai) |
| AB (ground distance) | **opposite** (ab ye C ke saamne hai) |

Dono table saath rakh kar dekho:

| Bhuja | A se dekho | C se dekho |
|---|---|---|
| AC | hypotenuse | hypotenuse |
| BC | opposite | adjacent |
| AB | adjacent | opposite |

> **Key point:** Kone badalne par **opposite aur adjacent aapas mein badal jaate hain.** **Hypotenuse kabhi nahi badalta**, kyunki wo right angle ke saamne wali bhuja hai, aur right angle apni jagah rehta hai.

## Part D: Alag-alag triangles mein naam pehchaanna

Triangle ke label kuch bhi ho sakte hain, aur use ghuma kar bhi banaya ja sakta hai. Har baar wahi teen sawaal poochho:
1. Right angle kahan hai? Uske saamne hypotenuse.
2. Reference angle kaun sa hai?
3. Jo bhuja us kone ko chhooti hai (aur hypotenuse nahi), wo adjacent. Baaki wo opposite.

### Example 1: △ABC, ∠B = 90°

```
  C
  |\
  | \
  |  \
  B---A
```

Right angle B par, to hypotenuse = **AC**.

- **A se dekho:** opposite = BC, adjacent = AB, hypotenuse = AC.
- **C se dekho:** opposite = AB, adjacent = BC, hypotenuse = AC.

**Jaanch:** A se dekhne par adjacent AB hai, C se dekhne par wahi AB opposite ban gayi, aur BC ulta. Naam swap hue ✅.

### Example 2: △PQR, ∠Q = 90°, triangle ghumaya hua (right angle upar)

```
  P-------Q
   \      |
    \     |
     \    |
      \   |
       \  |
        R
```

Right angle Q par, to hypotenuse = **PR** (wo tirchhi bhuja, jo Q ke saamne hai).

- **P se dekho:** opposite = QR, adjacent = PQ, hypotenuse = PR.
- **R se dekho:** opposite = PQ, adjacent = QR, hypotenuse = PR.

Dhyan do: yahan PQ **upar likhi hui** horizontal bhuja hai, aur P se dekhne par wo **adjacent** hai. Page par upar ya neeche hone se naam nahi badalte.

### Example 3: △XYZ, ∠X = 90°

```
  Y
  |\
  | \
  |  \
  X---Z
```

Right angle X par, to hypotenuse = **YZ**.

- **Y se dekho:** opposite = XZ, adjacent = XY.
- **Z se dekho:** opposite = XY, adjacent = XZ.

Yahan right angle ka label X hai, B nahi. Isliye "B ke saamne wali bhuja hypotenuse" wala rattaa yahan kaam nahi karega. Hamesha **right angle ke saamne wali** bhuja dekho.

### Example 4: △LMN, ∠N = 90°, hypotenuse upar aur right angle neeche

```
  L------------M
    \        /
      \    /
        N
```

Right angle N par, to hypotenuse = **LM** (jo upar horizontal bani hai).

- **L se dekho:** opposite = MN, adjacent = LN, hypotenuse = LM.
- **M se dekho:** opposite = LN, adjacent = MN, hypotenuse = LM.

Yahan hypotenuse **upar horizontal** hai, aur dono baaki bhujaayen tirchhi hain. Phir bhi naam wahi rules se milte hain. "Hypotenuse hamesha tirchhi hoti hai" wali galatfehmi yahan toot jaati hai.

### Example 5: △DEF, ∠D = 90°

```
       E
      /|
     / |
    /  |
   F---D
```

Pehle hypotenuse: right angle D ke saamne = **EF**.

- **E se dekho:** opposite = DF, adjacent = DE.
- **F se dekho:** opposite = DE, adjacent = DF.

**Jaanch:** Har kone ke liye opposite aur adjacent milakar wahi do bhujaayen aati hain jo hypotenuse nahi hain (DE aur DF) ✅.

## Part E: Coordinates aur asli cheezein: ladder aur wall

### Example 6: Coordinates se triangle

Teen bindu lijiye: O(0, 0), P(4, 0), Q(4, 3). Inhe jodne par △OPQ banta hai. O se P tak seedhi lakeer zameen jaisi leti hui, P se Q tak seedhi khadi. To ∠P = 90°.

```
          Q (4,3)
         /|
        / |
       /  |
      /θ  |
     O----P
   (0,0)  (4,0)
```

Right angle P par, to hypotenuse = **OQ**.

θ ko ∠O maanein:
- opposite = **PQ** (θ ke saamne, khadi bhuja)
- adjacent = **OP** (θ ko chhooti, leti hui bhuja)
- hypotenuse = **OQ**

Lambaiyaan: OP = 4, PQ = 3, OQ = ? Pythagoras se: OQ² = 4² + 3² = 16 + 9 = 25, to OQ = **5**.

**Jaanch:** 3² + 4² = 9 + 16 = 25 = 5² ✅.

Ab Q ko reference angle maanein: opposite = **OP** (4), adjacent = **PQ** (3). Naam badle, hypotenuse OQ wahi rahi.

### Example 7: Ladder aur deewar

Ek 13 m lambi ladder deewar se lagi hai. Ladder ka paanv deewar se 5 m door zameen par hai. Zameen par ladder aur zameen ke beech jo kona bana, use θ kehte hain.

```
   |  wall
   |\
   | \  ladder
   |  \  (13 m)
   |   \
   |    \
   |    θ\
   +------\
    5 m (ground)
```

Deewar seedhi khadi hai, zameen leti hui, to deewar aur zameen ke beech 90° ka kona hai. Triangle: ladder = hypotenuse, deewar ki oonchai = ?, zameen par doori = 5 m.

θ zameen par hai (ladder ke paanv par), to:
- hypotenuse = **ladder** (13 m)
- opposite = **deewar par ladder ke upar tak ki oonchai** (θ ke saamne)
- adjacent = **paanv se deewar tak zameen par doori** (5 m)

Oonchai Pythagoras se: oonchai² = 13² − 5² = 169 − 25 = 144, to oonchai = **12 m**.

**Jaanch:** 5² + 12² = 25 + 144 = 169 = 13² ✅.

Dhyan do: agar hum **upar wale kone** (deewar par ladder ka sira) se dekhein, to opposite = zameen par 5 m aur adjacent = deewar ki 12 m wali oonchai ban jati.

### Example 8: △ABC, ∠A = 90°, yaani right angle ka label bhi alag

△ABC mein ∠A = 90° hai, to hypotenuse = **BC**.

- **B se dekho:** opposite = AC, adjacent = AB.
- **C se dekho:** opposite = AB, adjacent = AC.

Yahan dekhne wali baat: pichhle examples mein right angle B par hota tha. Is baar A par hai, to hypotenuse BC ho gayi, AC nahi. Hypotenuse ka naam **label se nahi, right angle ki jagah se** tay hota hai.

## Part F: Do bhujaayen di hon, teesri nikalna (Pythagoras ki yaad)

Naam seekhne ke baad kabhi-kabhi teesri bhuja ki lambai bhi chahiye hoti hai. Iske liye Pythagoras hi kaam aata hai, aur ab wo aur saaf samajh aata hai:

> **(hypotenuse)² = (opposite)² + (adjacent)²**

### Example 9: Do chhoti bhujaayen di hain (3 aur 4)

△ABC, ∠B = 90°, AB = 3, BC = 4. Hypotenuse AC = ?

AC² = 3² + 4² = 9 + 16 = 25, to AC = **5**.

**Jaanch:** 5² = 25 = 9 + 16 ✅.

### Example 10: Hypotenuse aur ek bhuja di hai (13 aur 5)

△PQR, ∠Q = 90°, PR = 13, PQ = 5. QR = ?

Yahan hypotenuse PR hai (right angle Q ke saamne), to:

QR² = PR² − PQ² = 13² − 5² = 169 − 25 = 144, to QR = **12**.

**Jaanch:** 5² + 12² = 25 + 144 = 169 = 13² ✅.

Ab P ko reference angle maano: opposite = QR = 12, adjacent = PQ = 5, hypotenuse = PR = 13.

> 3-4-5 aur 5-12-13 aise right triangles hain jo aage chapter mein baar-baar kaam aayenge, isliye inhe yaad rakhna achha hoga.

## Ropeway ka sawaal wapas

Ab Mr. Negi aur Sia ke ropeway ke sawaal par lautte hain. Mr. Negi ne ye maap bataye: cable AC = 130 m, vertical rise BC = 50 m. Sia se kaha, "Ground distance AB khud nikal lo."

**Pythagoras se:** AB² = 130² − 50² = 16,900 − 2,500 = 14,400, to AB = **120 m**.

**Jaanch:** 120² + 50² = 14,400 + 2,500 = 16,900 = 130² ✅.

Ab teeno bhujaon ke naam, **dono stations se**:

**Base station A par khadi ho (θ = ∠A):**
- Cable AC = 130 m: **hypotenuse**
- Vertical rise BC = 50 m: **opposite** (θ ke saamne)
- Ground distance AB = 120 m: **adjacent** (θ ke bagal mein)

**Hilltop station C par khadi ho (kone ∠C se):**
- Cable AC = 130 m: **hypotenuse** (wahi)
- Vertical rise BC = 50 m: **adjacent**
- Ground distance AB = 120 m: **opposite**

Sia ne table bhar di:

| Bhuja | Lambai | A (base) se | C (hilltop) se |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cable AC | 130 m | hypotenuse | hypotenuse |
| Vertical rise BC | 50 m | opposite | adjacent |
| Ground distance AB | 120 m | adjacent | opposite |

Mr. Negi muskuraye. "Ab tumhe pata hai ki hum kyun kehte hain ki cable ka naam kabhi nahi badalta. Aur ye bhi ki opposite aur adjacent ke naam tab tak adhoore hain jab tak ye na bata do ki kis kone se dekh rahe ho."

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Sabse lambi bhuja ko koi aur naam dena**

Kabhi hypotenuse ko "base" ya "perpendicular" bol dena. Hypotenuse hamesha right angle ke saamne hoti hai aur sabse lambi hoti hai. Ropeway mein cable 130 m hai, jo 120 m aur 50 m dono se lambi hai.

**Galti 2: Opposite aur adjacent ko aapas mein ulta kar dena**

△ABC mein ∠B = 90° hai, A se dekhne par opposite BC hai, AB nahi. Check: opposite wo hai jo kone ko **chhooti nahi**. BC, A ko nahi chhooti (wo B aur C ke beech hai), to wo opposite hai. AB, A ko chhooti hai, to wo adjacent.

**Galti 3: Bhujaon ko page par unki jagah se naam dena**

"Jo bhuja neeche leti hui hai wo hamesha adjacent hai, jo khadi hai wo hamesha opposite." Ye ghalat hai. Example 2 mein PQ upar leti hui thi, aur Example 4 mein dono chhoti bhujaayen tirchhi thin, na khadi, na leti hui. Naam **kone** par nirbhar karte hain, page par jagah par nahi.

Sahi tareeka: pehle reference angle chuno, phir dekho kaun si bhuja use chhooti hai.

**Galti 4: Kone badalne par naam na badalna**

Ropeway mein A se dekhne par BC = opposite tha. Agar C se dekhte waqt bhi BC ko opposite hi bol diya, to galti hai. C se dekhne par BC adjacent ban jati hai. Naam yaad karne se pehle ek baar bol lo: "Main kis kone se dekh raha hoon?"

**Galti 5: Right angle ko reference angle bana lena**

Opposite aur adjacent sirf **chhote (acute) kone** ke liye define karte hain. Right angle (90°) ke saamne wali bhuja to hypotenuse hai, use opposite nahi kehte.

## Quick Recap

- **Trigonometry** = "tribhuj ka maap": tri (teen), gon (kona), metron (maap). Isse hum wo heights aur doori nikalte hain jo tape se nahi naap sakte.
- Right triangle mein ek acute kona chunte hain (θ, A ya C), aur uske hisaab se bhujaon ko naam dete hain.
- **Hypotenuse:** right angle ke saamne, sabse lambi bhuja.
- **Opposite (perpendicular):** reference angle ke saamne wali bhuja.
- **Adjacent (base):** reference angle ko chhooti hui, jo hypotenuse nahi hai.
- Kone badalne par **opposite aur adjacent swap ho jaate hain**. **Hypotenuse nahi badalta.**
- Naam hamesha **kone** se tay hote hain, page par bhuja ki jagah se nahi.
- Do bhujaayen di hon to teesri Pythagoras se: (hypotenuse)² = (opposite)² + (adjacent)². Yaad rakho: 3-4-5 aur 5-12-13.

## Practice Questions

1. In △ABC, ∠B = 90°. For ∠A, name the hypotenuse, the opposite side and the adjacent side. Then do the same for ∠C.

2. In △PQR, ∠R = 90°. Name the hypotenuse. Then, taking P as the reference angle, name the opposite and adjacent sides.

3. In △LMN, ∠M = 90°. Taking ∠N as the reference angle, name the opposite side, the adjacent side and the hypotenuse.

4. The points A(0, 0), B(12, 0) and C(12, 5) are joined to form △ABC.
   (a) Which angle is the right angle?
   (b) Name the hypotenuse and find its length.
   (c) Taking ∠A as θ, name the opposite and adjacent sides and give their lengths.

5. A 25 m ladder stands against a vertical wall with its foot 7 m from the wall on level ground. Taking the angle at the ground as θ:
   (a) Which is the hypotenuse?
   (b) Name the opposite and adjacent sides.
   (c) How high up the wall does the ladder reach?

6. In a right triangle, the two sides meeting at the right angle are 6 cm and 8 cm. Find the hypotenuse. If the angle opposite the 6 cm side is θ, name the 6 cm side and the 8 cm side with respect to θ.

7. Ruchi says: "In every right triangle, the horizontal side is the adjacent side and the vertical side is the opposite side." Find her mistake with an example.

8. State True or False:
   (a) The hypotenuse is always the longest side of a right triangle.
   (b) If a side is "opposite" for ∠A, it is also "opposite" for ∠C.
   (c) The hypotenuse is the same whether we look from ∠A or from ∠C.
   (d) In a right triangle, we can name the opposite and adjacent sides only for the two acute angles.

### Answers

1. Right angle B ke saamne AC hai, to hypotenuse = **AC** (dono kono ke liye).
   - ∠A se: opposite = **BC**, adjacent = **AB**.
   - ∠C se: opposite = **AB**, adjacent = **BC**.
   Jaanch: A aur C ke naam swap hue, hypotenuse wahi rahi ✅.

2. Right angle R par, to hypotenuse = **PQ** (R ke saamne).
   ∠P se: opposite = **QR** (P ko nahi chhooti), adjacent = **PR** (P ko chhooti hai, hypotenuse nahi).
   Jaanch: PQ, QR, PR mein se PR aur PQ, dono P ko chhooti hain, par PQ hypotenuse hai, to adjacent PR ✅.

3. Right angle M par, to hypotenuse = **LN**.
   ∠N se: opposite = **LM** (N ko nahi chhooti), adjacent = **MN** (N ko chhooti hai, hypotenuse nahi).

4. (a) A(0,0) se B(12,0) leti hui hai, B se C(12,5) khadi hai, to **∠B = 90°**.
   (b) Hypotenuse B ke saamne wali bhuja **AC** hai. AC² = 12² + 5² = 144 + 25 = 169, to AC = **13**.
   Jaanch: 13² = 169 ✅.
   (c) ∠A se: opposite = **BC = 5**, adjacent = **AB = 12**.

5. (a) Ladder hypotenuse hai (deewar aur zameen ke 90° ke saamne): **25 m**.
   (b) θ ladder ke paanv par hai. Opposite = **deewar par ladder ke upar tak ki oonchai**. Adjacent = **paanv se deewar tak zameen par doori (7 m)**.
   (c) Oonchai² = 25² − 7² = 625 − 49 = 576, to oonchai = **24 m**.
   Jaanch: 7² + 24² = 49 + 576 = 625 = 25² ✅.

6. Hypotenuse² = 6² + 8² = 36 + 64 = 100, to hypotenuse = **10 cm**.
   θ 6 cm wali bhuja ke saamne hai, to 6 cm wali bhuja θ ki **opposite** side hai. 8 cm wali bhuja θ ko chhooti hai aur hypotenuse nahi, to wo **adjacent** side hai.
   Jaanch: 6² + 8² = 100 = 10² ✅.

7. Ruchi ne bhujaon ko page par unki jagah (leti hui ya khadi) se naam diya, jabki naam kone se tay hote hain. Misaal: Example 2 mein PQ upar leti hui hai, aur P se dekhne par adjacent hai, par R se dekhne par wahi PQ opposite ban jati hai. Bhuja wahi, jagah wahi, par kone ke hisaab se naam badal gaya. Aur agar triangle ghumaya jaye (jaise Example 4 mein), to dono chhoti bhujaayen tirchhi ho sakti hain.

8. (a) **True** ✅. Hypotenuse right angle ke saamne hoti hai aur sabse lambi hoti hai.
   (b) **False** ❌. Jo bhuja ∠A ke saamne hai, wo ∠C ko chhooti hai, to ∠C ke liye wo adjacent hai, opposite nahi.
   (c) **True** ✅. Hypotenuse right angle ke saamne hoti hai, kone A ya C se isme koi farak nahi padta.
   (d) **True** ✅. Right angle ke liye ham opposite aur adjacent nahi bolte, wahan to sidha hypotenuse hi aata hai.
