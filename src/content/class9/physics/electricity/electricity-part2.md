# Topic 2: Electric Current aur Circuit

Topic 1 mein humne dekha ki charge do tarah ka hota hai, aur static electricity mein charge ek jagah **ruka hua** rehta hai: gubbare par, comb par, electroscope ke patron par. Par ghar ki bijli aisi nahi hoti. Tube-light, fan, torch, mobile charger: inme charge ek jagah baitha nahi rehta, wo **taar ke andar chalta rehta hai**. Ek baat pehle se yaad rakho (Topic 1 se): 1 coulomb (C) charge ki ek badi matra hai, aur ek electron ka charge sirf 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C hota hai. **Is poore topic mein: e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C.**

## Part A: Electric current kya hai?

### Charge ka behna

Paani ki pipe ke baare mein socho. Hum kehte hain, "tap se ek minute mein 10 litre paani nikla." Matlab: **1 second mein kitna paani guzra**, ye batata hai ki paani kitni tezi se beh raha hai.

Bijli mein bhi yahi hai. Taar ke kisi bhi ek cross-section (kaat) se jitna **charge 1 second mein guzarta hai**, use **electric current** kehte hain.

> **Current = charge ÷ time**
> **I = Q ÷ t**

- Q = charge, unit coulomb (C)
- t = samay, unit second (s)
- I = current, unit **ampere (A)**

Isse ampere ki definition nikalti hai:

> **1 ampere = 1 coulomb ÷ 1 second**, yaani **1 A = 1 C/s**

Agar kisi taar mein 1 A current hai, to us taar ke kisi bhi point se har second 1 coulomb charge guzarta hai.

### Formula ko ulta-sidha karke

| Kya chahiye? | Formula |
|---|---|
| Current I | I = Q ÷ t |
| Charge Q | Q = I × t |
| Samay t | t = Q ÷ I |

### Ampere ke chhote unit

Roz ki bahut si cheezon mein current 1 A se kaafi kam hota hai, to chhote unit chahiye:

- **1 milliampere (mA) = 10⁻³ A = 0.001 A**   (1000 mA = 1 A)
- **1 microampere (µA) = 10⁻⁶ A = 0.000001 A**   (1000000 µA = 1 A)

Isliye, 250 mA = 250 × 0.001 = 0.25 A. Aur 0.03 A = 0.03 × 1000 = 30 mA.

### Current mein charge kaun le jaata hai?

**Dhatu ke taar mein (metal):** wahan **free electrons** hote hain (Topic 1: conductor mein free electrons hote hain). Jab cell taar se judta hai, ye electrons cell ke **negative sire se positive sire ki taraf** chalte hain.

Par ek ajeeb baat hai: circuit ke diagrams mein hum current ki disha ulti, yaani **cell ke positive sire se negative sire ki taraf** dikhate hain. Ise **conventional current** kehte hain. Wajah itihaas hai: ye convention electron ki khoj se bahut pehle bana liya gaya tha, tab vaigyanik soch rahe the ki positive charge hi chalta hai. Baad mein pata chala ki taar mein electrons chalte hain, par convention nahi badla gaya, kyunki wo hamesha sahi kaam karta hai.

```
        Cell:   +  ─────────────────────────  −

   Conventional current :  + ───────────────►  −
   (diagram mein dikhate hain)

   Electrons ka asli chalna :  + ◄───────────── −
   (dhatu ke taar mein)
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Taar mein electrons negative sire se positive sire ki taraf chalte hain, par conventional current ka arrow positive sire se negative sire ki taraf banate hain: dono arrow ulte hain.

**Liquid mein:** kuch liquids (jaise namak ka paani) mein current ke liye **positive aur negative, dono tarah ke ions** chalte hain. Ye hum Topic 7 mein dekhenge (chemical effect).

## Part B: Electric circuit aur uske parts

Ab Hridaan ke sawal par aate hain: bulb ke jalne ke liye kya **poora** hona chahiye? Jawab: **current ka raasta**.

> **Electric circuit = current ke liye lagataar (continuous) band raasta.**

Raasta kahin se bhi tootega, to current ruk jaayega. Jaise train ki patri kahin se toot jaye to train aage nahi ja sakti.

### Circuit ke parts

1. **Source (cell ya battery):** wo charge ko dhakelta hai, yaani current chalane ki "taakat" deta hai. Ek cell ko ek **battery** kehna theek nahi: kai cell jode jaayein to wo **battery** banti hai. (Daily bol-chaal mein log ek cell ko bhi battery bol dete hain.)
2. **Taar (wires):** conductor ke bane (aam taur par copper), jo current ko raasta dete hain.
3. **Switch:** raasta kholta-band karta hai.
   - Switch **open** (khula): raasta toot gaya, **current nahi**.
   - Switch **closed** (band): raasta poora, **current chalta hai**.
4. **Load:** wo cheez jo current se kaam karti hai: **bulb** (roshni), **buzzer** (awaaz), **motor** (ghoomna).
5. **Measuring instruments:** jaise ammeter (current naapne ke liye). Voltmeter Topic 3 mein aayega.

### Circuit ke symbols

Circuit ka asli photo banane mein bahut samay lagta hai, isliye sab ek jaisa chhota **symbol** use karte hain. Neeche table mein har symbol ASCII mein bana hai aur shabdon mein bhi likha hai.

| Cheez | Symbol | Kaise pehchanein |
|---|---|---|
| Taar (wire) | `─────` | Seedhi lambi line |
| Cell | `─┤├─` | Do khadi lines: **lambi line positive (+) sira**, chhoti line negative (−) sira |
| Battery (kai cell) | `─┤├─┤├─` | Do ya zyada cell ek ke baad ek |
| Switch open (khula) | `─o  o─` | Beech mein gap: raasta toota hua |
| Switch closed (band) | `─o──o─` | Beech mein gap nahi: raasta poora |
| Bulb | `─⊗─` | Gol ke andar cross |
| Resistor | `─/\/\/─` | Zigzag line |
| Rheostat (badalne wala resistor) | `─/\/\/─↑` | Zigzag line, aur ek teer jo sliding contact ko dikhata hai |
| Ammeter | `─(A)─` | Gol ke andar A |
| Voltmeter | `─(V)─` | Gol ke andar V |

Taaron ke crossing aur earth ke symbol ek se zyada line mein bante hain, to wo neeche alag dikhaye hain:

```
  Taar jude hue hain (joint)          Taar jude nahi hain (sirf upar se guzar rahe)

          :                                     :
          :                                     :
   ───────●───────                       ───────:───────
          :                                     :
          :                                     :
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Joint wale crossing par mote bindu (●) hota hai: taar aapas mein jude hain. Bina bindu wale crossing mein taar bas ek-doosre ke upar se nikalte hain, jude nahi hain.

```
        Earth ka symbol

            :
            :
         ───┴───
          ─────
            ───
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Earth ka symbol: ek taar neeche ki taraf jaati hai, aur uske neeche teen line chhoti hoti jaati hain (zameen ke saath connection). Earth ke baare mein Topic 1 mein dekha tha: aage Topic 7 mein ghar ki wiring mein ye kaam aayega.

### Ek simple circuit ka sketch

Ek cell, ek switch, ek bulb aur ek ammeter wala circuit:

```
        +─────────(A)─────────+
        :                      :
        :                      :
      ──┬──  (lambi line: +)  ─⊗─  (bulb)
      ──┴─   (chhoti line: −)  :
        :                      :
        +───────o    o─────────+
              (switch)
```

> **Figure 2.4** – Cell, ammeter, bulb aur switch ek hi band loop mein hain. Cell ki lambi line positive sira hai. Switch band karne par (`o──o`) current cell ke positive sire se nikal kar ammeter, bulb se guzar kar negative sire par waapas aata hai.

Dhyan do: **current cell ke andar se hokar waapas chakkar poora karta hai.** Isiliye ise "circuit" (chakkar) kehte hain.

## Part C: Open, closed aur short circuit

### Open circuit

Raasta kahin se toota hua (switch off, taar kata hua, ya dheela contact): **current nahi chalta**, bulb nahi jalta. Charge cell ke ek sire se doosre tak ja hi nahi sakta.

### Closed circuit

Raasta poora (switch on, sab connection pakke): **current chalta hai**, bulb jalta hai.

### Short circuit

Short circuit mein current ko load se hokar jaane ke bajaye **bahut chhota, seedha raasta** mil jaata hai. Do udaharan:

- Ghar ki wiring mein **live** aur **neutral** taar (Topic 7) aapas mein chhoo jaayein.
- Ek taar seedha cell ke dono sire (+ aur −) ko jod de.

Aise mein current **bahut zyada** ho jaata hai. Itna current taaron ko bahut garm kar deta hai (heating effect: Topic 6), unka plastic pighal sakta hai aur **aag lag sakti hai**. Isliye ghar mein **fuse** lagaya jaata hai: wo zyada current aane par khud pighal kar raasta tod deta hai. Fuse ka poora kaam Topic 6 mein dekhenge. **Cell ya battery ke dono sire kabhi bhi seedhe taar se mat jodna.**

```
   Open circuit           Closed circuit         Short circuit

   +────⊗────+            +────⊗────+          ●────●────⊗────+
   :         :            :         :          :    :         :
 ──┬──       o          ──┬──       :          :  ──┬──       :
 ──┴─         o         ──┴─        :          :  ──┴─        :
   :         :            :         :          :    :         :
   +─────────+            +─────────+          ●────●─────────+
                                               ↑
                                          seedha taar

   switch khula           switch band          seedhe taar ne bulb ko
   bulb nahi jala         bulb jala            bypass kiya: bahut zyada
                                               current, bulb nahi jala
```

> **Figure 2.5** – Open circuit mein raasta toota hai (switch khula). Closed circuit mein raasta poora hai. Short circuit mein ek seedha taar (sabse baayein) cell ke dono sire jod deta hai aur bulb ko bypass kar deta hai. Cell ki lambi line positive sira hai.

## Part D: Torch ki kahani solve karte hain

Mr. Tandon ne Hridaan se poocha tha: "Bulb ke jalne ke liye kya complete hona chahiye?" Ab jawab saaf hai: **current ka raasta**. Torch mein teen cheezein poori honi thi:

1. **Cell sahi taraf lage hon.** Torch mein do cell lagte hain, aur dono ka positive sira doosre ke negative sire se judna chahiye (ek ke baad ek). Ek cell ulta lagne par uski taakat doosre cell ki taakat ko ghata deti hai, aur current chalta hi nahi (is baat ki detail Topic 3 mein "potential difference" ke saath aayegi).
2. **Switch band (closed) ho.** Hridaan ke torch ki patti tedhi thi, to button dabane par bhi switch ki patti doosre metal se nahi lagi: raasta toota raha. Patti theek karne par contact bana, switch closed ho gaya.
3. **Saare taar aur contact pakke hon.** Bulb, cell, spring aur patti: sab aapas mein metal ke contact se jude hon.

Jab teeno poori hui, current ka band raasta bana: **cell ka + sira → switch → bulb ka filament → cell ka − sira**. Bulb jal utha.

Mr. Tandon ne kaha, "Dekha Hridaan? Torch bigdi nahi thi: bas raasta toota hua tha. Jab bhi koi cheez bijli se na chale, sabse pehle dekho: circuit poora hai ya nahi?"

## Part E: Ammeter: current naapne wala instrument

### Ammeter kya hai aur kaise lagta hai

**Ammeter** current naapta hai (unit ampere mein). Iske teen rule yaad rakho:

1. **Ammeter hamesha SERIES mein lagta hai.** Matlab: circuit ka taar **kaat do** aur wahan ammeter laga do, taaki saara current ammeter se hokar guzre. (Jaise pipe mein paani ka meter lagane ke liye pipe kaatna padta hai.)
2. **Ammeter ka resistance bahut kam hota hai.** Isse circuit ka current lagbhag waisa hi rehta hai jaisa ammeter ke bina tha. (Resistance kya hota hai, ye Topic 4 mein.)
3. Ammeter ka **positive terminal** cell ke **positive sire ki taraf** jodna chahiye, aur negative terminal negative sire ki taraf, warna pointer ulti taraf ghoomega.

```
        +─────(A)─────+     Ammeter ka (+) terminal: cell ke (+) ki taraf
        :  (+)    (−)  :
        :              :
      ──┬──           ─⊗─   bulb
      ──┴─             :
        :              :
        +──o──────o────+     switch
```

> **Figure 2.6** – Series mein lagaya hua ammeter. Cell ki lambi line positive sira hai. Ammeter ka positive terminal us taraf hai jahan se cell ka positive sira aa raha hai, aur ammeter bulb ke saath ek hi loop mein hai.

**Zaroori warning:** ammeter ko kabhi **parallel** (cell ke dono sire par seedha) mat lagana. Uska resistance bahut kam hota hai, to ye lagbhag short circuit ban jaata hai: bahut zyada current, ammeter kharab ho jaayega.

### Ammeter ki scale padhna (least count)

Ammeter ki scale mein bhi wahi least count wala niyam lagta hai jo measurement ke chapter mein dekha tha ([measurement-and-units-part3.md](../measurement-and-units/measurement-and-units-part3.md)):

> **Least count (LC) = scale ki puri range ÷ scale ke kul divisions**

Udaharan: 0 se 1 A ki scale, 10 divisions. LC = 1 A ÷ 10 = **0.1 A**. Pointer 6 divisions par ho to reading = 6 × 0.1 A = **0.6 A**.

(Solved example 8 mein 0 se 5 A, 50 divisions wali scale dekhenge.)

## Part F: Current ke asar (effects)

Current dikhta nahi, par uske asar dikhte hain. Chaar mukhya asar:

| Asar | Kya hota hai | Ek udaharan | Kahan padhenge |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Heating effect** | Taar garm hota hai | Electric iron, heater | Topic 6 |
| **Chemical effect** | Liquid mein rasayanik badlaav | Electroplating (kisi cheez par chandi ya nickel ki parat) | Topic 7 |
| **Magnetic effect** | Current ke aas-paas magnet jaisa asar: electromagnet bana sakte hain, aur compass ki needle current ke paas ghoom jaati hai (Oersted ka experiment) | Electromagnet, bell | Agle chapter, Magnetism |
| **Light (roshni)** | Bulb ka filament itna garm hota hai ki chamakne lagta hai | Torch ka bulb | Topic 6 |

Ek taar mein current chale, to ye asar **ek saath** ho sakte hain. Bulb ke filament mein roshni bhi hoti hai aur garmi bhi.

### DC aur AC

- **DC (Direct Current):** current **sirf ek hi disha** mein chalta hai. Cell aur battery DC dete hain: torch isi se chalti hai.
- **AC (Alternating Current):** current ki **disha baar-baar badalti rehti hai**. Hamare ghar mein jo bijli aati hai (mains) wo AC hai: Bharat mein **230 V, 50 Hz**. 50 Hz ka matlab: current ki disha 1 second mein 100 baar badalti hai (50 baar ek taraf, 50 baar doosri taraf).

AC ki poori theory (peak value, rms value) **aage ki class mein** aati hai: abhi bas naam aur ye 50 Hz wali baat yaad rakho.

### Signal ki speed aur electrons ki speed

Ek ajeeb baat: switch dabate hi bulb **turant** jal jaata hai, jabki taar mein electrons ki asli chaal (drift speed) **bahut dheemi** hoti hai (lagbhag kuch millimetre per second ka order, approximate). Aisa kyun?

Isliye ki taar mein electrons pehle se bhare hote hain (jaise paani ki bhari hui pipe mein paani). Switch dabate hi cell ka asar (electric signal) taar ke andar **bahut tezi se** aage badhta hai, aur **taar ke sabhi electrons ek saath** chalne lagte hain, filament ke electrons bhi. Isliye bulb ko electrons ke cell se pahunchne ka intezaar nahi karna padta.

## Part G: Solved examples

**Example 1 (I = Q ÷ t):** Ek taar ke cross-section se 4 s mein 12 C charge guzarta hai. Current?

- Q = 12 C, t = 4 s
- I = Q ÷ t = 12 C ÷ 4 s = **3 A**

**Jaanch:** Q = I × t = 3 A × 4 s = 12 C ✅

**Example 2 (Q = I × t, minute ko second mein):** 0.5 A current 2 minute tak chalta hai. Kitna charge guzra?

- I = 0.5 A, t = 2 minute = 2 × 60 = 120 s (pehle second mein badla)
- Q = I × t = 0.5 A × 120 s = **60 C**

**Jaanch:** I = Q ÷ t = 60 C ÷ 120 s = 0.5 A ✅. (Dhyan: agar 2 minute ko 2 hi rakhte, to Q = 0.5 × 2 = 1 C aata: 60 guna galat.)

**Example 3 (time nikalna):** 1.5 A current se 90 C charge guzarne mein kitna samay lagega?

- t = Q ÷ I = 90 C ÷ 1.5 A = **60 s** = 1 minute

**Jaanch:** Q = I × t = 1.5 A × 60 s = 90 C ✅

**Example 4 (1 A mein electrons per second):** 1 A current mein har second kitne electrons guzarte hain? (e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C)

- 1 A ka matlab: 1 s mein Q = I × t = 1 A × 1 s = 1 C charge.
- Electrons ki sankhya n = Q ÷ e = 1 C ÷ (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C) = **6.25 × 10¹⁸** electrons

**Jaanch:** 6.25 × 10¹⁸ × 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C = 10 × 10⁻¹ C = 1 C ✅. Matlab 1 A mein har second lagbhag 6 billion billion electrons guzarte hain: isiliye hum electrons ko ginte nahi, charge mein baat karte hain.

**Example 5 (mA aur µA ko A mein):** (a) 250 mA ko A mein, (b) 500 µA ko A mein, (c) 0.03 A ko mA mein.

- (a) 250 mA = 250 × 10⁻³ A = **0.25 A**
- (b) 500 µA = 500 × 10⁻⁶ A = **0.0005 A** (= 5 × 10⁻⁴ A)
- (c) 0.03 A = 0.03 × 1000 mA = **30 mA**

**Jaanch:** (a) 0.25 × 1000 = 250 mA ✅. (b) 0.0005 × 1000000 = 500 µA ✅. (c) 30 ÷ 1000 = 0.03 A ✅

**Example 6 (1 ghante mein charge):** Ek taar mein 2 A current 1 ghante tak chalta hai. Kitna charge guzra?

- I = 2 A, t = 1 hour = 1 × 3600 = 3600 s
- Q = I × t = 2 A × 3600 s = **7200 C**

**Jaanch:** I = Q ÷ t = 7200 C ÷ 3600 s = 2 A ✅

**Example 7 (electrons ki sankhya):** 0.8 A current 5 s tak chalta hai. Kitne electrons guzre? (e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C)

- Q = I × t = 0.8 A × 5 s = **4 C**
- n = Q ÷ e = 4 C ÷ (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C) = **2.5 × 10¹⁹** electrons

**Jaanch:** 2.5 × 10¹⁹ × 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C = 4.0 C ✅. (Example 4 se bhi mel: 0.8 A × 5 s = 4 guna 1 A ka 1 s, aur 4 × 6.25 × 10¹⁸ = 2.5 × 10¹⁹ ✅)

**Example 8 (ammeter ki reading):** Ek ammeter ki scale 0 se 5 A hai aur usme 50 divisions hain. Pointer 27 divisions par ruka. Reading kya hai?

- LC = range ÷ divisions = 5 A ÷ 50 = **0.1 A**
- Reading = 27 × 0.1 A = **2.7 A**

**Jaanch:** 2.7 A ÷ 0.1 A = 27 divisions ✅. Aur 2.7 A, 2 A (20 div) aur 3 A (30 div) ke beech mein hai ✅

**Example 9 (components ko pehchano):** Neeche ke components ko source, load, control (switch) ya measuring device mein baanto: (a) cell, (b) buzzer, (c) switch, (d) ammeter, (e) motor, (f) battery.

- (a) cell: **source**
- (b) buzzer: **load**
- (c) switch: **control** (raasta kholta ya band karta hai)
- (d) ammeter: **measuring device**
- (e) motor: **load**
- (f) battery: **source**

**Jaanch:** Source wo jo charge ko dhakelta hai (cell, battery). Load wo jo bijli ko kaam mein badalta hai (buzzer: awaaz, motor: ghoomna) ✅

**Example 10 (circuit ko shabdon aur sketch mein):** Ek cell, ek switch, ek bulb aur ek ammeter ka circuit shabdon mein likho.

Shabdon mein: cell ke positive sire se ek taar ammeter ke positive terminal par, ammeter ke negative terminal se bulb tak, bulb se switch tak, aur switch se wapas cell ke negative sire tak.

```
        +───────(A)───────+
        :                 :
      ──┬──              ─⊗─
      ──┴─                :
        :                 :
        +────o      o─────+
```

> **Figure 2.7** – Same circuit: cell (lambi line positive), ammeter, bulb, aur switch ek hi band loop mein, series mein.

**Jaanch:** Loop mein koi gap nahi (switch band karne par), ammeter series mein hai, aur cell ki lambi line (positive) ammeter ke positive terminal ki taraf hai ✅

**Example 11 (chhoti current mein charge aur electrons):** Ek LED mein 20 mA current 10 s tak chalta hai. Kitna charge guzra, aur kitne electrons? (e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C)

- I = 20 mA = 20 × 10⁻³ A = 0.02 A, t = 10 s
- Q = I × t = 0.02 A × 10 s = **0.2 C**
- n = Q ÷ e = 0.2 C ÷ (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C) = **1.25 × 10¹⁸** electrons

**Jaanch:** 1.25 × 10¹⁸ × 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C = 2.0 × 10⁻¹ = 0.2 C ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Current bulb mein kharch (use up) ho jaata hai"**

Nahi. Jitna current cell se bulb mein jaata hai, **utna hi current bulb se wapas cell mein aata hai.** Charge khatam nahi hota, wo to chakkar lagata rehta hai. Bulb mein jo cheez kharch hoti hai wo **energy** hai (jo roshni aur garmi mein badal jaati hai). Ye hum Topic 3 mein dekhenge.

**Galti 2: Ammeter ko parallel lagana**

Ammeter ka resistance bahut kam hota hai. Parallel lagane par (cell ya bulb ke dono sire par seedha) lagbhag short circuit ban jaata hai, aur ammeter jal sakta hai. Ammeter hamesha **series** mein lagao.

**Galti 3: "Electrons positive se negative ki taraf chalte hain"**

Ulta. Taar mein **electrons negative sire se positive sire** ki taraf chalte hain. **Conventional current** positive se negative ki taraf maana jaata hai (Figure 2.1).

**Galti 4: "Switch sirf positive taar mein hona chahiye"**

Nahi. Circuit ek loop hai: raasta kahin se bhi tootega to current ruk jaayega. Switch positive taar mein ho ya negative taar mein, dono jagah **raasta khol-band** kar sakta hai. (Ghar ki wiring mein switch **live** taar mein lagane ki wajah safety hai: Topic 7.)

**Galti 5: mA aur A mix karna**

250 mA ko 250 A samajhna 1000 guna galat hai. Pehle sab ko A mein badlo: 250 mA = 0.25 A. Phir Q = I × t lagao.

**Galti 6: "Cell charge store karta hai"**

Cell ke andar **chemical energy** store hoti hai. Wo us energy ko electrical energy mein badalta hai aur taar mein pehle se maujood charge ko **dhakelta** hai. Charge to taar mein pehle se hota hai, cell use bas chalata hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Electric current = charge ke behne ki dar: I = Q ÷ t.** Unit **ampere (A)**, 1 A = 1 C/s.
- 1 mA = 10⁻³ A, 1 µA = 10⁻⁶ A.
- Dhatu ke taar mein **free electrons** chalte hain (− se + ki taraf), par **conventional current + se − ki taraf** maana jaata hai: ye convention electron ki khoj se bahut purana hai.
- Liquid mein positive aur negative, dono ions current le jaate hain (Topic 7).
- **Electric circuit = current ke liye lagataar band raasta.** Parts: source (cell, battery), taar, switch, load (bulb, buzzer, motor), measuring instruments.
- Switch open: current nahi. Switch closed: current chalta hai.
- **Short circuit:** load ko bypass karke seedha raasta: bahut zyada current, taar garm, aag ka khatra (fuse: Topic 6).
- **Ammeter:** current naapta hai, **series** mein lagta hai, resistance bahut kam, positive terminal cell ke positive sire ki taraf.
- Ammeter ki reading: LC = range ÷ divisions (0-5 A, 50 div: LC = 0.1 A).
- Current ke asar: heating, chemical, magnetic, light.
- Cell: DC (ek disha). Ghar ki bijli: AC (230 V, 50 Hz, 1 second mein 100 baar disha badalti hai): AC ki theory aage ki class mein.
- Switch dabate hi bulb turant jalta hai (signal tez), electrons ki drift speed bahut dheemi hoti hai (approximate).
- Torch ki kahani: cell sahi taraf, switch closed, saare contact pakke: tab hi circuit poora.
