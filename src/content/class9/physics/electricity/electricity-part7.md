## Part A: Current ka chemical effect

### Kya paani current guzarne deta hai?

Topic 1 aur Topic 2 mein humne dekha tha ki dhaatuein current guzarne deti hain. Par kya liquid bhi guzarne dete hain? Ek simple sochne wala experiment: ek plastic ke cup mein ek battery, ek bulb (ya LED) aur do taar se circuit banao, aur taar ke dono sire cup ke paani mein dalo. (Ye sirf ek ya do cell wali chhoti battery se hi karte hain, ghar ke socket se kabhi nahi.)

- **Distilled paani** (bilkul shuddh paani) mein sire dalo: LED **nahi jalti**. Shuddh paani current ko lagbhag guzarne nahi deta.
- Usi paani mein **namak ya nimbu ka ras (acid)** dal do: LED **jalne lagti hai**.

Matlab shuddh paani ek bahut kamzor conductor hai, par uska ghol (solution) jisme namak, acid ya base mila ho, current guzarne deta hai. Aisi liquid ko **electrolyte** kehte hain.

**Ye kaise hota hai?** Namak paani mein ghulta hai to wo chhote chhote charge wale kanon mein toot jaata hai, jinhe **ions** kehte hain: Na⁺ (positive) aur Cl⁻ (negative). Electrolyte mein current in ions ke chalne se banta hai: **positive ions negative electrode (cathode) ki taraf** jaate hain, aur **negative ions positive electrode (anode) ki taraf**. Bas itna idea abhi kaafi hai; poori kahani aage ki class mein aayegi.

Ye bhi dhyan do: nal, kuein ya nadi ka paani bilkul shuddh nahi hota, usme namak aur minerals ghule hote hain. Isliye wo current guzarne deta hai. Yahi wajah hai ki **geele haath se bijli ke switch ko chhoone se shock lagne ka khatra badh jaata hai** (Part C mein safety dekhenge).

### Paani ka electrolysis

Paani ko current se todne ko **electrolysis of water** kehte hain. Thoda tezaab (acid) mila hua paani ek glass mein lo, usme do electrodes dalo, aur har electrode ke upar paani se bhari test tube ulti kar do.

```
     (cathode wali)         (anode wali)
     test tube              test tube
       ┌───┐                  ┌───┐
       │   │ gas H₂           │   │ gas O₂
       │ H₂│ (2 hissa)        │ O₂│ (1 hissa)
       │   │                  │   │
       └─┬─┘                  └─┬─┘
         │                      │
   ┌─────┼──────────────────────┼─────┐
   │     │  paani + thoda acid  │     │
   │   ──┴──  (electrolyte)   ──┴──   │
   │  cathode (−)           anode (+) │
   └─────┬──────────────────────┬─────┘
         │                      │
         └─────[ battery ]──────┘
               (−)        (+)
```

> **Figure 7.1** – Paani ke electrolysis ka setup: cathode (negative electrode) par hydrogen gas bani, aur anode (positive electrode) par oxygen. Hydrogen ka volume oxygen ke volume se do guna hota hai.

Kuch der baad dono test tubes mein gas jama hone lagti hai:

- **Cathode (−)** par **hydrogen** (H₂) bani.
- **Anode (+)** par **oxygen** (O₂) bani.
- **Hydrogen ka volume oxygen ke volume se do guna** hota hai: **H₂ : O₂ = 2 : 1**. (Kyunki paani ka formula H₂O hai: har oxygen ke saath do hydrogen. Isse zyada abhi nahi.)

Isliye ye ek **chemical change** hai: paani (ek compound) do alag gason mein toot gaya. Sirf garmi ya sirf dhaatu ka chalna nahi, ek naya padaarth bana. Yahi **chemical effect of current** hai.

### Electroplating

Ab Ms. Doshi ka nal. **Electroplating** matlab current ki madad se kisi cheez par ek dhaatu ki bahut patli parat chadha dena.

Maan lo hume ek **pital ke nal par chrome** ya ek chamach par taambe ki parat chadhani hai. Arrangement ye hota hai:

- Jis cheez par parat chadhani hai (nal, chamach), wo **cathode (negative electrode)** banti hai.
- Jis dhaatu ki parat chadhani hai (taamba, chaandi, chrome), uski plate **anode (positive electrode)** banti hai.
- **Electrolyte** us dhaatu ke **namak ka ghol** hota hai. (Taambe ki plating mein copper sulphate ka ghol.)

```
            ┌──────────[ battery ]──────────┐
            │  (+)                     (−)  │
            │                               │
      ┌─────┼───────────────────────────────┼─────┐
      │    ┌┴┐                             ┌┴┐    │
      │    │█│                             │░│    │
      │    │█│   copper sulphate ka ghol   │░│    │
      │    │█│                             │░│    │
      │    └─┘                             └─┘    │
      └───────────────────────────────────────────┘
        anode (+):                   cathode (−):
        taambe ki plate              chamach (jis par
                                     parat chadhani hai)

   Anode ki plate se taamba ghulta hai, chamach par taamba jamta hai.
```

> **Figure 7.2** – Taambe ki electroplating: anode taambe ki plate hai, cathode wo chamach hai jise plate karna hai, aur electrolyte copper sulphate ka ghol hai. Current ke chalne se anode se taamba ghulta hai aur utna hi cathode par jamta jaata hai.

Current chalane par anode se taamba ghulkar ghol mein aata hai, aur ghol ke taambe ke ions cathode par jakar taambe ki parat ban jaate hain. Isliye ghol mein taambe ki matra lagbhag wahi bani rehti hai. (Kitna taamba jamega, iska hisaab aage ki class mein Faraday ke niyamon se aata hai: yahan sirf naam jaan lo.)

**Roz ki zindagi mein electroplating:**

1. **Nal, bathroom ki fittings, car ki bumper aur cycle ke handle par chrome plating:** chamak aur zang (rust) se bachaav ke liye.
2. **Chaandi ya sone ki plating:** sasti dhaatu ke gehne aur bartan par.
3. **Tin plating:** khaane ke dibbon (cans) par tin ki parat, taaki loha khaane se na ghule aur zang na lage.
4. **Galvanisation:** lohe par **zinc** ki parat chadhana, taaki zang (rust) na lage. Pani ke pipe, lohe ki bucket, aur bijli ke khambe galvanised hote hain. (Galvanisation aksar lohe ko pighle hue zinc mein dubokar bhi kiya jaata hai.)
5. **Taambe ki plating:** electronics ke circuit boards par aur chamach par.

Ab Aarit ka sawaal hal ho gaya. Ms. Doshi ne bataya, "Nal ki purani chrome ki parat ghis gayi hai, isliye neeche ka pital dikhne laga aur nal kaala pad gaya. Plating karne wala nal ko saaf karke use **cathode** banata hai, **chrome ki plate anode** banata hai, **chrome ka namak ghol electrolyte** hota hai, aur current guzarta hai. Kuch der mein nal par chrome ki nayi patli parat jam jaati hai aur wo chamak jaata hai."

### Copper ka shuddhikaran aur batteries

- **Copper ka shuddhikaran (purification):** ashuddh taambe ki moti plate ko anode banate hain, shuddh taambe ki patli plate cathode, aur electrolyte copper sulphate. Current se anode ka taamba ghulta hai aur cathode par **shuddh taamba** jamta hai. Isi tarah se bijli ke taar ke liye shuddh copper banta hai. (Sirf naam jaan lo.)

- **Cell aur battery:** jaise current se chemical change ho sakta hai, waise hi **chemical change se current bhi ban sakta hai**. Cell (aur battery, jo kai cells milkar banti hai) mein **chemical energy electrical energy mein badalti hai**.
  - **Primary cell** (jaise torch aur ghadi ka dry cell): ek baar chemical khatam ho gaye to khatam, **dobara charge nahi hota**.
  - **Secondary cell** (jaise car ki lead-acid battery, mobile aur laptop ki lithium-ion battery): **dobara charge ho sakti hai**. Charge karte waqt bijli se chemical change ulta chalta hai, aur chemical energy phir store ho jaati hai.

- **Magnetic effect (sirf naam):** current ka teesra bada asar ye hai ki current wale taar ke aas paas **magnetic field** banti hai. Isi se electric motor, bell aur crane ke electromagnet chalte hain. Ye agle chapter (Magnetism) mein poori tarah aayega.

Is tarah current ke teen asar hue: **heating effect** (Topic 6), **chemical effect** (yahan), aur **magnetic effect** (agla chapter).

## Part B: Ghar ki wiring (sirf samajhne ke liye)

> **Zaroori chetavani:** ye part wiring ka sirf **idea** samjhaata hai. Mains ki bijli (230 V) jaan le sakti hai. Wiring ya kisi bhi switch, socket ya appliance ki repair **sirf licensed electrician** hi kare. Ghar par kuch kholne ya jodne ki koshish kabhi mat karo.

### Teen taar ki service line

Bijli ghar ko 230 V, 50 Hz AC ke roop mein milti hai (Topic 2 mein dekha tha ki India ki mains AC hai). Khambe se ghar tak aane wali **service line mein teen taar** hote hain:

| Taar | Rang (aam taur par) | Kaam |
|---|---|---|
| **Live (phase) wire** | Laal ya bhura | 230 V ka supply laata hai. **Fuse aur switch isi taar mein lagte hain.** |
| **Neutral wire** | Kaala ya neela | Current ki wapsi ka raasta. Iska potential lagbhag earth ke barabar hota hai. |
| **Earth wire** | Hara ya peela-hara | Sirf safety ka taar. Zameen mein dabi dhaatu ki plate se juda hota hai. |

Ye rang **alag alag deshon aur alag samay ki wiring mein alag ho sakte hain**: kai purani wiring mein alag rang hote hain. Isliye rang dekhkar kabhi kisi taar ko chhoone ka faisla mat karo: sirf electrician hi sahi pehchaan sakta hai.

Live aur neutral ke beech ka potential difference **230 V** hota hai. Neutral lagbhag earth ke potential par hota hai, isliye neutral aur earth ke beech lagbhag koi potential difference nahi hota. Isiliye live taar ko chhoona khatarnaak hai: aadmi ka sharir zameen se juda hota hai, to current live taar se uske sharir se hokar zameen mein jaa sakta hai.

### Ghar mein bijli ka raasta

Ghar ke entrance par bijli is order mein aati hai: **service line, main fuse (ya main MCB), energy meter, main switch aur phir distribution board**. Distribution board se alag alag **circuits** nikalte hain:

- **Lighting circuit:** bulb, tube light aur fan ke liye. Isme kam current (aam taur par **5 A rating**) hota hai aur patle taar chalte hain.
- **Power circuit:** bhaari appliances (geyser, AC, refrigerator) ke liye. Isme zyada current (aam taur par **15 A rating**) aata hai, isliye **moti taar** lagti hain.

Taar jitna zyada current le jaata hai, utna moti hona chahiye. Warna wo H = I² R t se zyada garam hogi (Topic 6). Isliye zyada power wale appliance ke liye alag, moti taar wala circuit rakhte hain.

### Sab appliance parallel mein

Topic 5 mein humne dekha tha ki ghar ki wiring **parallel** mein hoti hai. Isiliye:

- Har appliance ko poora **230 V** milta hai.
- Ek appliance band karo ya kharab ho jaaye, to baaki chalte rehte hain: sab **independently** kaam karte hain.

Agar sab series mein hote, to ek bulb ke fuse hone par poora ghar andhera ho jaata, aur har appliance ko 230 V se kam milta.

```
   Live  (laal)   ──[Main Fuse]──[Energy Meter]──[Main Switch]──┬─────────┬──────────┐
                                                                │         │          │
                                                              [Fuse]    [Fuse]    [Switch]
                                                                │         │          │
                                                             [Switch]   [Switch]   [Fuse]
                                                                │         │          │
                                                             (Lamp)     (Fan)    (Socket)──┐
                                                                │         │          │     │
   Neutral (kaala) ─────────────────────────────────────────────┴─────────┴──────────┘     │
                                                                                           │
   Earth  (hara)  ───────────────────────────────────────── (socket ki earth pin) ─────────┘
                          zameen mein dabi dhaatu ki plate se juda
```

> **Figure 7.3** – Ghar ki wiring ka sirf block diagram (sirf samajhne ke liye): live taar mein main fuse, meter, main switch aur har branch mein fuse aur switch hain. Lamp, fan aur socket live aur neutral ke beech **parallel** mein hain. Earth taar sirf socket ki earth pin se juda hai.

### Teen-pin ka plug aur socket

Ghar ke socket aur plug mein **teen pins** hoti hain: ek live ke liye, ek neutral ke liye, aur ek **earth** ke liye. Earth wali pin **upar ki beech wali, lambi aur moti** hoti hai.

```
        ┌──────────────────────┐
        │          ██          │   <- earth pin (sabse lambi aur moti, upar)
        │          ██          │
        │                      │
        │    ●            ●    │   <- live pin aur neutral pin (chhoti, patli)
        └──────────────────────┘
          (plug ko pins ki taraf se dekhne par)
```

> **Figure 7.4** – Teen-pin plug ka naksha: do chhoti pins live aur neutral ki, aur ek lambi aur moti earth pin.

Earth pin **lambi** isliye hoti hai taaki plug lagate waqt **earth ka connection pehle bane aur nikalte waqt aakhir mein toote**. Is tarah appliance ki body kabhi bina earth ke live nahi rahti.

**Earth wire kyun zaroori hai?** Iron, refrigerator, geyser aur washing machine jaise appliance ki **dhaatu ki body** hoti hai. Maan lo kisi fault (kharabi) se live taar body se chhoo gaya. Agar earth wire na ho, to poori body 230 V par aa jaati hai, aur jo aadmi use chhuega, uske sharir se current guzarkar zameen mein chala jaayega: **bijli ka jhatka**.

Agar body earth wire se juri hai, to wahi current **earth wire ke andar se bahut aasaani se zameen mein chala jaata hai** (kyunki earth wire ka resistance bahut kam hota hai). Ye current bahut bada hota hai, isliye **fuse turant pighal jaata hai** aur appliance band ho jaata hai. Body kabhi khatarnaak voltage par nahi rehti, aur chhoone wala aadmi bach jaata hai. (Earthing ka idea [electricity.md](electricity.md) mein bhi tha.)

**Ek zaroori baat:** normal kaam mein current **live aur neutral** se hi chalta hai. **Earth wire normal current nahi le jaati**: wo sirf fault ke waqt kaam aati hai.

### Switch live taar mein kyun?

Switch hamesha **live wire** mein lagta hai. Switch off karne par appliance live taar se kat jaata hai, to wo bilkul "live" nahi rehta. Agar switch neutral mein hota, to off karne par bhi appliance live taar se juda rehta, aur bulb holder ko chhoone par bijli ka jhatka lag sakta tha.

Isi tarah **fuse** bhi live wire mein hota hai: fault hone par fuse jalne par appliance live se alag ho jaata hai.

**Phir se yaad dilaate hain:** ye sab sirf samajhne ke liye hai. Ghar ki wiring ka koi bhi kaam sirf licensed electrician hi karta hai.

## Part C: Bijli ki safety

### Safety ke niyam

1. **Sockets ko overload mat karo:** ek hi socket mein kai heavy appliance mat lagao (jaise geyser aur iron ek hi extension board mein).
2. **Geele haath se ya geele farsh par nange pair se** switch, plug ya kisi appliance ko mat chhuo.
3. **Fuse ke badle moti taar ya zyada rating ka fuse kabhi mat lagao.** Fuse ka kaam hi pighalkar circuit todna hai: moti taar lagane se wo kaam nahi karega aur wiring garam hokar aag laga sakti hai.
4. Kisi bhi repair se pehle **switch off karo aur plug nikalo**. Repair **sirf licensed electrician** karta hai.
5. **Ghisi hui ya katti hui taar aur dheele plug** ka istemaal mat karo.
6. **Chhote bachon** ko sockets se door rakho: socket par **safety cover** lagao.
7. **Patang** bijli ki lines ke paas mat udao, aur **khambe par mat chadho**.
8. Zameen par **girey hue taar** se door raho aur turant bijli ke vibhag ko batao.
9. **Bijli ki aag mein paani kabhi mat daalo.** Pehle supply band karo, phir **dry powder ya CO₂ extinguisher** use karo. (Ye aam salah hai; hamesha pehle apni jaan bachao aur madad bulao.)
10. **Toofan aur bijli kadakne** mein khule maidaan, bade ped ya khambe ke paas mat khade ho (Topic 1 mein lightning ki safety dekhi thi): pakki imaarat ke andar raho.

### Bijli ke jhatke par first aid (aam salah)

- Peedit ko **bilkul mat chhuo** jab tak wo live taar se juda ho: tumhe bhi jhatka lag sakta hai.
- **Sabse pehle main supply (main switch) band karo.**
- Agar switch tak jaana mumkin na ho, to khud sookhi jagah par (jaise sookhi lakdi ya rubber mat par) khade hokar **sookhi lakdi ki lathi** se taar ko ya peedit ko door dhakel do. Koi geeli ya dhaatu ki cheez kabhi mat use karo.
- Turant **doctor ya ambulance ko bulao** aur madad aane tak shaant rakho.

### Aur kuch safety ke upkaran

- **MCB:** fuse jaisa switch jo zyada current par apne aap band ho jaata hai, aur use dobara chalu kiya ja sakta hai.
- **RCD ya ELCB:** ye wo device hai jo tab **trip** hota hai (supply kaat deta hai) jab kuch current **leak hokar earth ki taraf jaata hai**. Ye jhatke se bachane mein madad karta hai. (Sirf naam aur idea.)

## Part D: Bijli ka bill

### Unit kya hai?

Ab Aarit ka pehla sawaal. Ms. Doshi ne samjhaya: "Bill mein jo **unit** likha hai, wo **power ka unit nahi** hai. Wo **energy ka unit** hai: **1 unit = 1 kilowatt-hour (1 kWh)**."

Energy meter ye naapta hai ki ghar mein kitni energy kharch hui:

**E = P × t** (jab P kilowatt (kW) mein aur t ghante (h) mein ho, to E kilowatt-hour (kWh) mein aata hai)

Aur **bill = units × rate per unit**.

Hum yahan **maan lo ₹6 per unit** lete hain (ye sirf ek assumed rate hai, asli rate jagah aur samay ke hisaab se badalta hai). Bill mein fixed charges (meter ka kiraya waghera) bhi alag se lagte hain: yahan hum unhe nahi jodenge.

### Example 1: Geyser ke units

2000 W ka geyser 30 minute chalta hai. Kitni energy kharch hui?

P = 2000 W = 2000 ÷ 1000 kW = 2 kW
t = 30 min = 0.5 h

E = P × t = 2 kW × 0.5 h = **1 kWh = 1 unit**

Jaanch: 2000 W × 1800 s = 3,600,000 J = 3.6 × 10⁶ J = 1 kWh ✅

### Example 2: Us ka kharcha

Example 1 ki 1 unit ka kharcha ₹6 per unit par?

Kharcha = 1 kWh × ₹6/kWh = **₹6**

Jaanch: 1 × 6 = 6 ✅

### Example 3: Ek bulb ka mahine ka kharcha

Ek 60 W bulb roz 5 ghante chalta hai. 30 din mein kitne units aur kitna kharcha (₹6 per unit)?

P = 60 W = 0.06 kW
Ek din: E = 0.06 kW × 5 h = 0.3 kWh
30 din: E = 0.3 kWh × 30 = **9 kWh (9 units)**
Kharcha = 9 kWh × ₹6/kWh = **₹54**

Jaanch: 60 W × 150 h = 9000 Wh = 9 kWh ✅

### Example 4: Ghar ke saare appliances ka mahine ka hisaab

Ek ghar ke appliances aur roz ka istemaal:

| Appliance | Power | Roz ka istemaal | Roz ke units (kWh) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 tube lights (40 W each) | 4 × 40 W = 160 W | 6 h | 0.96 |
| 3 fans (75 W each) | 3 × 75 W = 225 W | 10 h | 2.25 |
| Refrigerator | 150 W | 12 h (aadhe time chalta hai) | 1.80 |
| TV | 100 W | 5 h | 0.50 |
| Geyser | 2000 W | 1 h | 2.00 |
| Washing machine | 500 W | 1 h | 0.50 |
| **Total** |  |  | **8.01** |

Har row ke steps: tube lights: 0.160 kW × 6 h = 0.96 kWh; fans: 0.225 kW × 10 h = 2.25 kWh; refrigerator: 0.150 kW × 12 h = 1.8 kWh; TV: 0.100 kW × 5 h = 0.5 kWh; geyser: 2 kW × 1 h = 2.0 kWh; washing machine: 0.5 kW × 1 h = 0.5 kWh.

Roz ke total units = 0.96 + 2.25 + 1.8 + 0.5 + 2.0 + 0.5 = **8.01 kWh**
30 din ke units = 8.01 kWh × 30 = **240.3 kWh ≈ 240 units**
Bill = 240 units × ₹6/unit = **₹1,440**

Jaanch: 8.01 × 30 = 240.3 ✅ aur 240 × 6 = 1440 ✅

Ye bilkul Ms. Doshi ke bill jaisa nikla: **Units consumed: 240**, amount ₹1,440. Aarit ka pehla sawaal hal.

### Example 5: LED se bachat

Ek 60 W ka purana bulb aur ek 9 W ka LED bulb, dono roz 5 ghante, 30 din tak chalte hain. Kitni bachat hoti hai (₹6 per unit)?

Purana bulb: E₁ = 0.06 kW × 5 h × 30 = **9 kWh**
LED: E₂ = 0.009 kW × 5 h × 30 = **1.35 kWh**
Bachat = 9 kWh − 1.35 kWh = **7.65 kWh**
Paise ki bachat = 7.65 kWh × ₹6/kWh = **₹45.90**

Jaanch: (60 − 9) W = 51 W = 0.051 kW; 0.051 kW × 150 h = 7.65 kWh ✅; 7.65 × 6 = 45.9 ✅

### Example 6: Energy meter ke readings

Pichhle mahine ke ant mein meter ka reading 4580.5 units tha aur is mahine ke ant mein 4820.5 units. Kitne units kharch hue?

Units = 4820.5 kWh − 4580.5 kWh = **240 kWh (240 units)**

Jaanch: 4580.5 + 240 = 4820.5 ✅

```
   Pichhla reading:   4 5 8 0 . 5   kWh
   Is mahine ka:      4 8 2 0 . 5   kWh
                      ───────────
   Farak:                 2 4 0     kWh = 240 units
```

> **Figure 7.5** – Energy meter ke do readings ka farak: is mahine ke reading mein se pichhla reading ghatane par kharch hue units milte hain.

### Example 7: 10 A ke fuse par kitne 60 W ke bulb?

Ghar ke ek circuit mein 10 A ka fuse hai, aur supply 230 V hai. Kitne 60 W ke bulb ek saath jala sakte hain (sirf theory ke hisaab se)?

Maximum power = V × I = 230 V × 10 A = 2300 W
Bulbs ki sankhya = 2300 W ÷ 60 W = 38.33

Poora bulb hi ginte hain, isliye **38 bulb**.

Jaanch: 38 × 60 W = 2280 W ≤ 2300 W ✅ aur 39 × 60 W = 2340 W > 2300 W ❌ (fuse udd jaata).

(Ye sirf theoretical maximum hai. Asal mein itne bulb kisi ghar mein ek circuit par nahi lagaye jaate, aur fuse ko hamesha chhoti margin ke saath chuna jaata hai.)

### Example 8: 1500 W ke AC ka current aur fuse

1500 W ka AC 230 V par chalta hai. Kitna current leta hai, aur kaun sa fuse theek rahega?

I = P ÷ V = 1500 W ÷ 230 V = **6.52 A**

Fuse ki rating normal current se thodi zyada honi chahiye, par bahut zyada nahi. 6.52 A ke liye **10 A ka fuse** theek rahega (5 A ka fuse udd jaata, kyunki 6.52 A > 5 A).

Jaanch: 230 V × 6.52 A = 1499.6 W ≈ 1500 W ✅

### Example 9: Paani ke electrolysis mein gas ka volume

Paani ke electrolysis mein cathode par 20 mL hydrogen jama hui. Anode par kitni oxygen jama hui hogi?

H₂ : O₂ = 2 : 1, to oxygen = 20 mL ÷ 2 = **10 mL**

Jaanch: 20 mL ÷ 10 mL = 2 ✅

### Example 10: Saare appliances ek saath chalein to?

Example 4 ke saare appliances ek saath chalein to ghar ki total power, current aur main fuse?

Total power P = 160 W + 225 W + 150 W + 100 W + 2000 W + 500 W = **3135 W**
Current I = P ÷ V = 3135 W ÷ 230 V = **13.63 A**

Isliye main fuse ya MCB **20 A** ka theek rahega (13.63 A se thoda upar, jo ek standard rating hai). Aise ek saath saare chalna kam hi hota hai, par fuse ko ye halat bhi sambhalni padti hai.

Jaanch: 230 V × 13.63 A = 3134.9 W ≈ 3135 W ✅

### Example 11: 240 units ko joule mein

Ghar ne mahine mein 240 kWh bijli kharch ki. Ye kitne joule hai?

1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J
E = 240 × 3.6 × 10⁶ J = 864 × 10⁶ J = **8.64 × 10⁸ J**

Jaanch: 8.64 × 10⁸ ÷ 3.6 × 10⁶ = 240 ✅

### Example 12: Refrigerator ka mahine ka kharcha

150 W ka refrigerator roz 12 ghante compressor chalata hai. 30 din ka kharcha (₹6 per unit)?

Roz: 0.15 kW × 12 h = 1.8 kWh
30 din: 1.8 kWh × 30 = **54 kWh**
Kharcha = 54 kWh × ₹6/kWh = **₹324**

Jaanch: 150 W × 360 h = 54,000 Wh = 54 kWh ✅

## Part E: Bijli bachane ke tareeke

Ms. Doshi ne Aarit ko samjhaya ki bill kaise kam ho sakta hai:

- **LED bulb aur tube** lagao: wo purane bulb se bahut kam power mein utni hi roshni dete hain (Example 5).
- **Star-rated appliance** kharido: jitne zyada stars, utni kam bijli. (Sirf naam: ye ek rating system hai.)
- **Standby** wale devices (TV, set-top box, charger) ko **switch se off** karo: standby mein bhi thodi bijli chalti rehti hai.
- Kamre se nikalte waqt **light aur fan band** karo.
- Geyser sirf zaroorat ke time chalao, aur AC ka temperature thoda zyada rakho.
- Refrigerator ka darwaza baar baar khula mat chhodo.

### Bijli banti kaise hai? (sirf naam)

Ghar tak bijli power plants se aati hai. Bijli **thermal** (koyla), **hydro** (paani ke bandh), **nuclear**, **solar** aur **wind** (hawa) jaise sources se banti hai. Ye sab **aage ki class mein** dekhenge.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Fuse neutral wire mein hota hai"**
Fuse (aur switch) **live wire** mein hota hai, taaki fault par appliance live se kat jaaye.

**Galti 2: "Earth wire normal current le jaati hai"**
Normal kaam mein current live aur neutral se chalta hai. Earth wire sirf **fault ke waqt** current ko zameen mein bhejti hai.

**Galti 3: "Bill ka unit power ka unit hai"**
Bill ka **1 unit = 1 kWh**, jo **energy** ka unit hai (1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J). Power ka unit watt (W) hai.

**Galti 4: "W aur kWh mix kar dena"**
2000 W ka geyser **power** hai (W ya kW). Wo agar 0.5 ghante chala to **energy** 1 kWh kharch hui. Rating W mein hoti hai, bill kWh mein.

**Galti 5: "Ghar ke saare appliance series mein jude hain"**
Saare appliance **parallel** mein hote hain, taaki har ek ko 230 V mile aur ek band hone se baaki na ruke.

**Galti 6: "Distilled paani bhi achha conductor hai"**
Shuddh (distilled) paani current lagbhag nahi guzarne deta. Namak, acid ya base ghulne par ions banne se paani electrolyte ban jaata hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Chemical effect:** electrolyte (namak, acid ya base ka ghol) current guzarne deta hai, kyunki ions chalte hain. Positive ions cathode (−) ki taraf, negative ions anode (+) ki taraf.
- **Paani ka electrolysis:** cathode par hydrogen, anode par oxygen, **H₂ : O₂ = 2 : 1** volume mein.
- **Electroplating:** jise plate karna hai wo cathode; plating ki dhaatu anode; electrolyte us dhaatu ka namak. Chrome (nal, bumper), tin (dibbe), galvanisation (zinc).
- **Cell:** chemical energy se electrical energy. Primary (dry cell) dobara charge nahi hota; secondary (lead-acid, lithium-ion) hota hai.
- **Magnetic effect:** naam; agle chapter mein.
- **Ghar ki supply:** 230 V, 50 Hz AC. Teen taar: live (fuse aur switch isi mein), neutral, earth (body ko zameen se jodta hai). Rang alag ho sakte hain.
- **Ghar ka circuit:** main fuse, energy meter, main switch, distribution board; lighting (5 A) aur power (15 A) circuit; sab appliance **parallel**.
- **Earth pin** lambi aur moti: pehle jude, baad mein toote.
- **Safety:** overload nahi, geele haath nahi, fuse ke badle moti taar nahi; repair sirf licensed electrician; paani se bijli ki aag nahi; jhatke par pehle supply band karo.
- **Bill:** 1 unit = 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J; E = P × t; bill = units × rate.

|---|---|---|

| Kya | Formula |
