# Topic 1: Electric Charge aur Static Electricity

Pichhle chapter mein humne dekha ki light ek aisi energy hai jise hum **dekhte** hain: wo seedhi chalti hai, mud jaati hai, lens se fokus hoti hai. Ab ek aur energy ki baari, jo har switch ke peeche chhupi hai: **bijli (electricity)**. Pankha, bulb, mobile charger, fridge, metro: sab usi se chalte hain. Par bijli hai kya? Kya wo koi "cheez" hai jo taar mein bhagti hai? Is chapter ke 7 topics mein hum ye step by step samjhenge, aur sabse pehle us chhoti si cheez se shuru karenge jo bijli ki jad hai: **electric charge**.

Is topic mein kuch chalta-bhagta nahi: charge **ek jagah rukha hua** hai, jaise sweater ya balloon par. Isliye ise **static electricity** kehte hain.

---

## Sardi ki shaam, andhera kamra aur chingaari

Sardiyon ki ek sookhi shaam thi. Mr. Bhasin ghar aaye aur apni beti Aarohi ke saath drawing room mein baithe. Pehle ek chhota sa khel hua tha: Aarohi ne ek balloon apne baalon par ragda, aur phir use deewar se laga diya. Balloon deewar se **chipak gaya**, jaise gond lagi ho! Phir Aarohi ne ek plastic ki kangi (comb) apne baalon mein ragdi aur use kaagaz ke chhote tukdon ke paas laayi. Kaagaz ke tukde uchhal kar kangi se chipak gaye.

Aarohi hairaan thi, "Papa, ye kaagaz apne aap kyun uchhal rahe hain? Kangi mein koi magnet hai kya?"

Mr. Bhasin muskuraye, "Magnet to nahi hai beta. Abhi tum aur bhi kuch dekhogi. Chalo, light band karo."

Kamre mein andhera karke Mr. Bhasin ne apna **oon ka sweater (woollen sweater)** jaldi se utaara. Sweater utarte waqt **"chat-chat-chat"** ki halki aawaz aayi, aur andhere mein **chhoti si neeli chingaariyan** (tiny blue sparks) chamak gayin!

Aarohi ghabra kar peeche hat gayi, "Papa! Sweater to **karant maar raha hai!** Ye to bijli hai na? Kahin hume shock to nahi lagega?"

Mr. Bhasin hanse, "Dar mat. Ye wo bijli nahi hai jo ghar ke socket mein hoti hai. Ye **static electricity** hai. Is topic ke end tak tum khud bata paogi ki sweater, balloon, kangi aur aasman ki bijli ek hi kahani ke hisse hain." Is kahani ko hum topic ke beech mein solve karenge.

## Part A: Charge ki shuruaat: ambar se balloon tak

**Do line ki history.** Kaha jaata hai ki lagbhag 2600 saal pehle Greece ke vidwan **Thales** ne dekha ki **ambar (amber, ek pedh ka sakht gond jaisa padarth)** ko oon ya fur se ragdo to wo halke tinke aur pankh kheenchne lagta hai. Ambar ke liye Greek shabd **"elektron"** tha, aur isi se aaj ka shabd **"electron"** aur **"electricity"** bane.

Hum ye sab kuch aaj bhi kar sakte hain. Balloon aur kangi ka khel wahi purana khel hai.

### Do tarah ke charge

Jab hum alag-alag cheezein ragadte hain to unme se kuch cheezein ek doosre ko kheenchti hain, kuch dhakelti hain. Isse pata chalta hai ki charge **do type ke** hote hain. Inhe naam diya **Benjamin Franklin** ne: **positive (+)** aur **negative (−)**.

Ek ahem niyam, jo is poore chapter mein kaam aayega:

> **Same type ke charge (like charges) ek doosre ko dhakelte hain (repel).**
> **Alag type ke charge (unlike charges) ek doosre ko kheenchte hain (attract).**

Aur ek aur baat: **charged cheez ek neutral (bina charge wali) cheez ko bhi kheench leti hai**. Kangi ne kaagaz isi tarah uthaya (ye neutral cheez ke kheenchne wali baat hum Part F mein induction ke saath aur saaf dekhenge).

```
   Like charges: dhakelte hain          Unlike charges: kheenchte hain

     (+) <──────  ──────> (+)              (+) ──────>  <────── (−)

     (−) <──────  ──────> (−)
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Same sign ke charge ek doosre ko door dhakelte hain, alag sign ke charge paas kheenchte hain.

**Aap khud kar sakte ho (bilkul safe):** do ek jaise balloons lo, dono ko apne baalon par ragdo, aur dono ko dhaage se latka kar paas laao. Dono ek doosre se **door bhagenge**, kyunki dono par same type ka (negative) charge aa gaya hai. Ab ek balloon ko haath mein pakad kar baalon par ragdo aur doosre ki jagah apna haath paas laao: balloon haath ki taraf aa jaayega.

## Part B: Charge kahan se aata hai? Atom ki chhoti tasveer

Sab kuch **atoms** se bana hai. (Atom ka apna chapter alag hai, yahan sirf utna jo charge samajhne ke liye chahiye.) Ek atom ki simple tasveer:

- Beech mein ek chhota sa **nucleus**: usme **protons (positive)** aur **neutrons (neutral)** hote hain.
- Nucleus ke chaaron taraf **electrons (negative)** ghoomte hain.

```
              e⁻
        .  .  .  .  .
      .               .
    .      ( p+ n )     .  e⁻
    e⁻     ( n  p+ )      .
      .                 .
        .  .  .  .  .
              e⁻

   p+ = proton (positive),  n = neutron (neutral),  e⁻ = electron (negative)
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Atom ki simple tasveer: beech mein nucleus (proton aur neutron), bahar electron.

Teen baatein samjho:
1. Ek proton ka positive charge aur ek electron ka negative charge **barabar matra** ka hota hai (par sign ulta).
2. Jab kisi cheez mein **protons ki sankhya = electrons ki sankhya**, to dono charge ek doosre ko cancel kar dete hain aur cheez **neutral** hoti hai. **Neutral ka matlab ye nahi ki usme charge hai hi nahi.** Usme positive aur negative charge dono bahut saare hain, bas barabar hain!
3. **Thos cheezon (solids) mein sirf electrons hi ek jagah se doosri jagah ja sakte hain.** Protons nucleus ke andar jakde hue hain, wo nahi hilte. Isliye:
   - jab koi cheez **electrons kho deti hai**, to protons zyada rah jaate hain: wo **positive** ho jaati hai.
   - jab koi cheez **electrons le leti hai**, to electrons zyada ho jaate hain: wo **negative** ho jaati hai.

> Charge naya **paida nahi hota, na khatam hota hai**: sirf ek cheez se doosri cheez mein **electrons transfer** hote hain. Jo ek ne khoya, wahi doosre ne paaya.

## Part C: Ragadne (friction) se charge kaise aata hai?

Jab do **alag-alag padarth** ko aapas mein ragda jaata hai, to ragadne se unke beech electrons ek se doosre mein chale jaate hain. Jis padarth se electrons nikle wo **positive** ho gaya, jisme gaye wo **negative** ho gaya. Dono par **barabar matra** ka, par ulta sign ka charge aata hai.

| Ragda gaya jodi | Positive kaun? | Negative kaun? |
|---|---|---|
| Glass rod aur silk (resham) | Glass rod | Silk |
| Ebonite ya plastic rod aur fur (ya oon) | Fur ya oon | Ebonite ya plastic rod |
| Balloon aur baal | Baal | Balloon |

"Kaun positive hoga" ye **jodi par depend karta hai**. Glass ko silk se ragdo to glass positive hota hai, par ye zaroori nahi ki glass **hamesha** positive hi hoga: kisi doosre padarth ke saath ragdo to wo negative bhi ho sakta hai. Isliye hamesha **jodi** dekho.

### Sweater, balloon aur kangi ka raaz

Ab Aarohi ke teeno sawalon ka jawab:

- **Balloon aur baal:** balloon ne baalon se electrons le liye. Balloon **negative** ho gaya, baal **positive**. Negative balloon deewar ke paas laaya gaya to deewar ke electrons thoda door hat gaye (Part F mein isi tarah ka induction samjhenge) aur balloon ko deewar ki positive taraf kheench mila: wo chipak gaya.
- **Kangi aur kaagaz:** ragadne se kangi par charge aaya, aur wo neutral kaagaz ke tukdon ko kheench leti hai. Kaagaz bahut halka hai, isliye kheenchne ka chhota sa bal bhi use utha deta hai.
- **Oon ka sweater:** sweater utarte waqt wo kapde se ragda jaata hai (aur sookhi sardi mein hawa mein nami kam hoti hai, to charge bahar nahi nikalta). Charge jama ho jaata hai. Jab sweater utarte hain to hawa ke kuch kan ek ek karke charge ko bahar le jaate hain: chingaariyan aur "chat-chat" ki aawaz. Sookhi hawa mein ye ek chhota sa **static discharge** hai.

Aarohi ne poochha tha, "Papa, kya sweater karant maar raha hai?" Jawab: **Nahi.** Isme charge ki matra bahut kam hai (isse ek chhoti si chingaari hi banti hai), aur ye ghar ke socket ki bijli jaisi nahi hai. Itna tez sparks ya karant sirf mains wali bijli mein hota hai, jiske saath kabhi khelna nahi chahiye. Karant (current) kya hai, wo agle topic mein.

## Part D: Charge ki matra: coulomb (C)

Charge ko naapne ka **SI unit coulomb (C)** hai.

Ek electron ka charge bahut chhota hai:

> **e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C**  (electron ke liye −1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C, proton ke liye +1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C)

Isliye **1 C** ke liye bahut saare electrons chahiye:

> 1 C ÷ (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C/electron) = **6.25 × 10¹⁸ electrons**

(yaani lagbhag 6.25 billion billion electrons!) Coulomb bahut bada unit hai, rozmarra ke static electricity ke liye chhote unit kaam aate hain:

| Unit | Matlab | Conversion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 µC (microcoulomb) | 1 coulomb ka dus lakhwaan hissa | 1 µC = 10⁻⁶ C |
| 1 nC (nanocoulomb) | 1 coulomb ka ek arabwaan hissa | 1 nC = 10⁻⁹ C |

Balloon ya kangi par aam taur par kuch nC se kuch µC tak ka charge aata hai.

### Charge hamesha e ka poora guna hota hai: Q = n × e

Electron ya to ek poora hota hai, ya nahi hota: **aadha electron** ham transfer nahi kar sakte. Isliye kisi bhi cheez ka total charge hamesha **e ka poora sankhya guna** hota hai:

> **Q = n × e**
> Q = charge (C), n = electrons ki poori sankhya (kho di ya le li), e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C

(Negative cheez ke liye Q negative likhte hain, positive ke liye positive.) Isse hum solved examples mein bahut saare sawal solve karenge.

## Part E: Conductor aur insulator

Sab cheezein charge ko ek jaise tareeke se nahi chalne deti.

- **Conductor:** isme kuch electrons aasaani se idhar-udhar ja sakte hain, to charge aasaani se behta hai.
- **Insulator:** isme electrons apni jagah par jakde rehte hain, to charge aasaani se nahi behta.

| Conductor | Insulator |
|---|---|
| Copper, aluminium, lohe jaisi saari dhatuein (metals) | Plastic |
| Insaani sharir (human body) | Rubber |
| Nal ka paani (tap water, jisme namak aadi ghule hon) | Glass |
| Graphite (pencil ka lead) | Sookhi lakdi (dry wood) |
| Earth (zameen) | Sookha kapda (dry cloth) |
| | Sookhi hawa (dry air) |
| | Ebonite |

Ek aur category hai: **semiconductors** (jaise silicon aur germanium). Wo conductor aur insulator ke beech ke hote hain. Unka naam sirf yaad rakho: mobile aur computer ki chips unhi se bani hain.

### Hath mein pakda hua metal rod charged kyun nahi rehta?

Agar tum ek **copper rod** haath mein pakad kar oon se ragdo, to wo charged nahi rehta. Par **plastic rod** wahi karke charged ho jaata hai. Kyun?

- Copper conductor hai, aur **insaani sharir bhi conductor** hai. Rod par aaya charge **sharir se hote hue zameen (Earth) mein chala jaata hai**. Isliye rod par charge ruk nahi paata.
- Plastic insulator hai. Uska charge usi jagah par **ruka rehta** hai jahan ragda gaya, aur sharir se hokar nahi behta.

Yaani metal ko charged rakhna ho to use **insulating handle** (plastic ya lakdi) se pakdo.

### Earthing

Agar kisi charged cheez ko taar se **zameen (Earth)** se jod dein, to uska charge zameen mein chala jaata hai: ise **earthing** kehte hain. Earth itni badi hai ki wo kisi bhi charge ko apne mein le sakti hai bina khud badle. Earthing ke kuch roz ke upyog (sirf naam, aage ke topics mein samjhenge):
- badi imaaraton ke liye (bijli girne par),
- petrol ke tanker ki earthing chain (neeche Part I mein),
- bijli ke plug ka **teesra pin** (Topic 7 mein).

## Part F: Charge karne ke teen tareeke

### 1. Friction se (ragadkar)
Hum Part C mein dekh chuke: do alag padarth ko ragdo, electrons ek se doosre mein jaate hain.

### 2. Conduction se (chhukar)
Ek **charged** cheez ko ek **conductor** se **chhuao**. Charge ka kuch hissa conductor mein chala jaata hai, aur conductor par **wahi sign** ka charge aa jaata hai jo charged cheez par tha.
Jaise ek negative rod ko dhatu ke gole se chhuaoge, to gola bhi negative ho jaayega.

### 3. Induction se (bina chhue, sirf paas laakar)
Ek dhatu ke gole ko insulating stand par rakho. Ek charged rod ko gole ke paas laao (chhuao nahi). Gole ke electrons rod ke charge ki wajah se idhar-udhar ho jaate hain. Phir earthing se gole ko **rod ke ulta sign** ka charge de sakte hain. (Ye sirf idea hai; detail aage ki class mein.) Sketch dekho (negative rod ke saath):

```
 Step 1: Neutral gola, insulating       Step 2: Negative rod paas laao.
         stand par.                              Electron door bhagte hain.

        ┌───┐                              rod (−)      ┌───┐
        │ ± │                              (− − −)      │ + │ ← paas wala hissa +
        └─┬─┘                                           │ − │ ← door wala hissa −
        stand                                           └─┬─┘

 Step 3: Gole ko haath ya taar se       Step 4: Pehle earthing hatao,
         earth karo. Door wale                  phir rod hatao.
         electron zameen mein chale gaye.

   rod (−)      ┌───┐                         rod hata diya    ┌───┐
   (− − −)      │ + │                                          │ + │  ← gola ab positive
                └─┬─┘──── taar ──── Earth                      └─┬─┘
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Induction ke chaar kadam: negative rod ke paas laane par gole ke electron door bhagte hain, earthing se wo zameen mein chale jaate hain, aur gole par rod se ulta (positive) charge reh jaata hai.

**Tulna:** conduction mein charge **same sign** ka milta hai aur rod ko chhuna padta hai. Induction mein charge **opposite sign** ka milta hai aur rod **chhuaya nahi** jaata.

## Part G: Gold-leaf electroscope

Kisi cheez par charge hai ya nahi, aur kis sign ka, ye batane ke liye ek simple upkaran hai: **gold-leaf electroscope**.

**Banaawat (construction):**
- Ek **kaanch ke jar (glass jar)** ke andar ek **dhatu ki rod**, jiske upar jar ke bahar ek **dhatu ki ghundi (knob)** hai.
- Rod ke neeche ke sire par **do bahut patle gold ke (ya aluminium ke) patre (leaves)** latke hote hain.
- Jar isliye lagaya jaata hai ki hawa ke jhonke patron ko hila na sakein.

```
            ( ) ← metal ki ghundi (knob)
             │
        ┌────┴────┐
        │    │    │   ← metal ki rod
        │    │    │
        │   ╱ ╲   │   ← do patle gold ke patre (leaves)
        │  ╱   ╲  │
        └─────────┘
          glass jar
```

> **Figure 1.4** – Gold-leaf electroscope: metal ki ghundi aur rod, uske neeche do patle patre, sab ek glass jar ke andar.

**Kaam kaise karta hai (working):** Jab ek charged cheez se **ghundi ko chhuate** hain, to charge rod se hote hue **dono patron** par aa jaata hai (conduction). Sirf **paas laane** par bhi (bina chhue) electroscope ke electron ghundi aur patron ke beech idhar-udhar hote hain aur patron par ek jaisa charge aa jaata hai (induction, Part F). Dono patron par **same sign** ka charge aata hai, to wo ek doosre ko **dhakelte hain** aur **khul jaate hain (diverge)**. Jitna zyada charge, utna zyada khulna.

**Upyog (uses):**
1. **Charge ka pata lagana:** patre khule to charge hai; patre band rahe to charge nahi hai (ya bahut kam hai).
2. **Charge ka sign pata karna.** Pehle electroscope ko **ek jaane-pehchane sign ka charge** do (maan lo positive), patre khul jaate hain. Ab ek anjaan charged cheez ko ghundi ke paas laao:
   - patre **aur zyada khul gaye** to anjaan cheez ka sign **same** hai (positive).
   - patre **kuch band hone lage (kam khule)** to anjaan cheez ka sign **opposite** hai (negative).
   - Dhyan do: patre kam khulna ek **bina charge wali (neutral)** cheez ko paas laane se bhi ho sakta hai. Isliye sign ki pakki pehchaan patron ke **aur zyada khulne** se hoti hai; kam khulne se sirf tab, jab pata ho ki cheez charged hai.

**Seema (limitation):** electroscope se sirf pata chalta hai ki charge **kam hai ya zyada**, aur kaunsa sign hai. Ye charge ko **exact naap nahi** sakta.

## Part H: Aasman ki bijli: lightning

Ab Aarohi ke "chingaari" wale sawaal ka bada bhai: **bijli ka girna (lightning)**.

Badal ke andar barf ke kan aur hawa ke kan bahut tezi se aapas mein ragadte hain. Isse badal ke andar bade paimane par charge alag-alag ho jaata hai: badal ka ek hissa positive, doosra negative. (Ye poori tarah se vaigyanikon ko bhi abhi pakki tarah samajh nahi aaya hai; hum yahan bas mote taur par idea le rahe hain.) Jab charge bahut zyada ho jaata hai, to sookhi hawa (jo insulator hai) bhi use rok nahi paati aur **ek bahut bada chingaari (spark) badal se badal ya badal se zameen** ke beech kood jaata hai. Wahi hai lightning, aur uski gadgadahat hai thunder. **Sweater ki chingaari aur aasman ki bijli ek hi cheez hai: static discharge**, bas pehli bahut chhoti, doosri bahut-bahut badi.

### Lightning se bachne ke aam sujhaav (safety)
- Toofan mein **ghar ya pakke makaan ke andar rahein**.
- **Khule maidan, unche pedh aur paani (talaab, swimming pool, samundar)** se door rahein.
- Toofan ke waqt plug mein laga hua bijli ka upkaran chhune se bachein.

### Lightning conductor

Unchi imaaraton par ek **dhatu ki lambi nok wali rod** lagayi jaati hai, jiska neeche ka sira ek mote taar se **zameen mein gaada** hota hai. Bijli girti hai to wo isi rod se hote hue **seedha zameen mein chali jaati hai** aur imaarat ko nuksan nahi hota. Ye earthing ka hi ek upyog hai.

## Part I: Roz ki zindagi mein static electricity

- **Car ke darwaze ya door knob ka shock:** sardi mein sookhe mausam mein seat se ragadne se sharir par charge jama ho jaata hai. Dhatu ko chhute hi chhota sa jhatka lagta hai. (Ye khatarnak nahi hota; ye wahi chhota sa sweater wala discharge hai.)
- **Kapde ka chipakna:** synthetic kapde ragadne se charge hokar sharir ya ek doosre se chipak jaate hain.
- **TV ya computer screen par dhool:** screen par charge hota hai jo dhool ke kan kheench leta hai.
- **Photocopier:** photocopier mein charge aur kaarbon powder (toner) ka istemal hota hai (sirf naam; detail aage ki class mein).
- **Petrol tanker ki earthing chain:** tanker ka petrol chalte waqt ragad se charge ho sakta hai. Ek chain tanker se zameen tak latki rehti hai taaki charge zameen mein chala jaaye aur chingaari na bane. Warna petrol ki bhaap mein aag lag sakti thi.

## Part J: Solved examples

(Is poore topic mein: e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C.)

**Example 1 (electrons se charge):** 5 × 10¹² electrons ek rod par aa gaye. Rod par charge kitna hai?

- n = 5 × 10¹², e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C
- Magnitude Q = n × e = 5 × 10¹² × 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C = 8 × 10⁻⁷ C
- Electrons liye hain, to charge **negative**: Q = **−8 × 10⁻⁷ C = −0.8 µC** (kyunki 1 µC = 10⁻⁶ C, 8 × 10⁻⁷ ÷ 10⁻⁶ = 0.8)

**Jaanch:** 0.8 µC ÷ 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C/electron = 0.8 × 10⁻⁶ ÷ 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ = 5 × 10¹² ✅

**Example 2 (charge se electrons):** −3.2 × 10⁻⁶ C ke charge mein kitne electrons hain?

- n = Q ÷ e = 3.2 × 10⁻⁶ C ÷ 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C/electron = **2 × 10¹³ electrons** (ye charge negative hai, to ye electrons **zyada** hain)

**Jaanch:** 2 × 10¹³ × 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ = 3.2 × 10⁻⁶ C ✅

**Example 3 (balloon par −2 µC):** Ek balloon ko −2 µC ka charge dene ke liye kitne electrons transfer hue? Kisne diye, kisne liye?

- Q = 2 µC = 2 × 10⁻⁶ C (magnitude)
- n = 2 × 10⁻⁶ C ÷ 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C/electron = **1.25 × 10¹³ electrons**
- Ye electrons baalon se balloon mein gaye: balloon ne **liye**, baalon ne **diye**. Baal par +2 µC charge aaya.

**Jaanch:** 1.25 × 10¹³ × 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ = 2 × 10⁻⁶ C ✅

**Example 4 (electrons khone par net charge):** Ek neutral glass rod silk se ragda gaya aur usne 4 × 10¹² electrons kho diye. Rod par net charge kya hai?

- Rod ne electrons **khoye**, to wo **positive** hai.
- Q = + n × e = 4 × 10¹² × 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C = **+6.4 × 10⁻⁷ C = +0.64 µC**
- Silk par bhi utna hi **−0.64 µC** charge aaya (jo ek ne khoya, wahi doosre ne paaya).

**Jaanch:** 0.64 × 10⁻⁶ ÷ 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ = 4 × 10¹² ✅. Aur dono ka jod: +0.64 µC + (−0.64 µC) = 0 ✅ (charge bacha raha).

**Example 5 (thoda extra: do ek jaise golon ka chhuna):** Do ek jaise (identical) dhatu ke gole hain. Ek par +6 µC charge hai, doosra neutral hai. Dono ko chhuaya aur phir alag kar diya. Ab har ek par kitna charge hai?

- Ek jaise gole charge ko **barabar baant lete hain**.
- Total charge = +6 µC + 0 = +6 µC
- Har gole par = +6 µC ÷ 2 = **+3 µC**

**Jaanch:** +3 µC + 3 µC = +6 µC ✅ (charge ka total wahi raha). Asal mein doosre gole ke electrons pehle gole mein gaye, jisse pehle gole ka +6 µC ghatkar +3 µC ho gaya aur doosra neutral se +3 µC ho gaya.

**Example 6 (conductor ya insulator?):** Is list ko do hisson mein baanto: copper taar, rubber ki chappal, graphite, glass, nal ka paani, plastic ka scale, sookhi lakdi ka ruler, insaani sharir, lohe ki keel, ebonite rod, sookha kapda, aluminium ka vatta.

| Conductor | Insulator |
|---|---|
| Copper taar, graphite, nal ka paani, insaani sharir, lohe ki keel, aluminium ka vatta | Rubber ki chappal, glass, plastic ka scale, sookhi lakdi ka ruler, ebonite rod, sookha kapda |

**Jaanch:** 6 + 6 = 12 cheezein ✅. Dhatuein (copper, lohe, aluminium), graphite, paani aur sharir conductor; baaki sab insulator ✅.

**Example 7 (force ki disha):** Neeche diye gaye jodiyon mein dono cheezein ek doosre ko kheenchengi ya dhakelengi?

| Jodi | Force |
|---|---|
| + aur + | Dhakelengi (repel) |
| + aur − | Kheenchengi (attract) |
| − aur − | Dhakelengi (repel) |
| Charged (+ ya −) aur neutral | Kheenchengi (attract), kyunki neutral cheez ke charge thoda alag ho jaate hain |

**Jaanch:** "Same ko dhakelna, alag ko kheenchna" wala niyam teeno mein sahi hai ✅. Charged cheez neutral ko bhi kheenchti hai: yahi kangi aur kaagaz tha ✅.

**Example 8 (electroscope ka sawal):** Ek electroscope positively charged hai (patre khule hue). Ek charged rod ko ghundi ke paas laane par patre **aur zyada khul gaye**. Rod ka sign kya hai?

- Patre aur zyada khule, yaani divergence **badha**.
- Divergence tab badhta hai jab rod ka sign electroscope ke sign ka **same** ho.
- Electroscope positive hai, to rod bhi **positive** hai.

**Jaanch:** Agar rod negative hota to patre **kam khulte** (kuch band hote). Isliye jawab **positive** ✅.

**Example 9 (kya ye charge ho sakta hai?):** (a) 4.8 × 10⁻¹⁹ C aur (b) 2.4 × 10⁻¹⁹ C: kya kisi cheez par ye charge ho sakte hain?

- (a) n = 4.8 × 10⁻¹⁹ ÷ 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ = **3** (poori sankhya): **ho sakta hai**, 3 electron ka charge.
- (b) n = 2.4 × 10⁻¹⁹ ÷ 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ = **1.5** (poori sankhya nahi): **nahi ho sakta**, kyunki aadha electron nahi hota.

**Jaanch:** 3 × 1.6 = 4.8 ✅; 1.5 × 1.6 = 2.4, par 1.5 poora number nahi ✅.

**Example 10 (nC ko C mein):** Ek kangi par 250 nC ka charge hai. Ye kitne C hai, aur kitne electrons ka charge hai?

- Q = 250 nC = 250 × 10⁻⁹ C = **2.5 × 10⁻⁷ C**
- n = 2.5 × 10⁻⁷ C ÷ 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C/electron = **1.5625 × 10¹² electrons** (lagbhag 1.56 × 10¹²)

**Jaanch:** 1.5625 × 10¹² × 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ = 2.5 × 10⁻⁷ C ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Ragadne mein protons ek cheez se doosri mein jaate hain."**

Nahi. Protons nucleus ke andar jakde hote hain. Thos cheezon mein **sirf electrons** hi hilte hain. Jisne electrons khoye wo positive, jisne liye wo negative.

**Galti 2: "Neutral cheez mein charge hota hi nahi."**

Galat. Neutral cheez mein positive aur negative charge dono **barabar matra** mein hote hain, isliye net charge zero hai. Isi wajah se charged rod neutral kaagaz ko kheench leta hai.

**Galti 3: "Same charge ek doosre ko kheenchte hain."**

Ulta hai. **Like charges repel, unlike charges attract.** (Yaad rakho: "same to same dhakka, alag-alag mein pyaar".)

**Galti 4: "Insaani sharir insulator hai."**

Galat. Insaani sharir ek **conductor** hai (usme namak wala paani hai). Isiliye haath mein pakda dhatu ka rod charge nahi rakh paata: charge sharir se zameen mein chala jaata hai.

**Galti 5: "Static electricity aur socket wali bijli ek hi hai."**

Dono ka ek hi source (charge) hai, par ye alag cheezein hain. Static mein charge **ek jagah ruka hua** hai aur matra bahut kam; socket ki bijli mein charge **taar mein behta** rehta hai aur bahut khatarnak ho sakti hai. Isiliye sweater ki chingaari ko "karant" nahi kehte.

**Galti 6: "Electron ka charge positive hota hai."**

Galat. Electron ka charge **negative** hai: −1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C. Proton ka +1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C. Jo cheez electrons le leti hai, wo **negative** hoti hai, na ki positive.

## Quick Recap

- Bijli ka naam Greek "elektron" (ambar) se aaya. Kaha jaata hai ki Thales ne ambar ko ragad kar tinke uthate dekha.
- Charge do type ke: **positive** aur **negative** (naam Benjamin Franklin ne diye). **Like charges repel, unlike charges attract.** Charged cheez neutral ko bhi kheenchti hai.
- Atom: nucleus (proton +, neutron neutral) aur uske bahar electrons (−). Neutral cheez mein protons = electrons.
- Thos cheezon mein **sirf electrons** hilte hain: electrons khone par **positive**, electrons paane par **negative**.
- Friction se: glass + silk (glass positive), ebonite ya plastic + fur ya oon (ebonite negative), balloon + baal (balloon negative). Sign jodi par depend karta hai.
- SI unit **coulomb (C)**. e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C. 1 C = lagbhag 6.25 × 10¹⁸ electrons. 1 µC = 10⁻⁶ C, 1 nC = 10⁻⁹ C.
- **Q = n × e**: charge hamesha e ka poora guna hota hai.
- **Conductor:** dhatuein, insaani sharir, nal ka paani, graphite, Earth. **Insulator:** plastic, rubber, glass, sookhi lakdi, sookha kapda, sookhi hawa, ebonite. Semiconductor: sirf naam.
- **Earthing:** charged cheez ko zameen se jodne se charge zameen mein chala jaata hai.
- Charge karne ke teen tareeke: **friction, conduction (same sign), induction (opposite sign, earthing ke saath).**
- **Gold-leaf electroscope:** charge aur uska sign pata karta hai (same sign se patre aur khulte hain, opposite se kam khulte hain); exact naap nahi sakta.
- **Lightning** badal ka bahut bada static spark hai. Toofan mein andar raho; unchi imaaraton par lightning conductor lagta hai.
- Static electricity ke udaaharan: door knob ka jhatka, kapde ka chipakna, TV screen par dhool, photocopier, petrol tanker ki earthing chain.
- Kahani: balloon aur kangi se pata chala ki ragadne se charge aata hai. Sweater ki neeli chingaari ek chhota static discharge thi, "karant" nahi.

## Practice Questions

1. A glass rod is rubbed with silk. Which one becomes positive and which becomes negative? Which particles moved, and in which direction?

2. How many electrons are there in a charge of −4.8 × 10⁻⁶ C? (e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C)

3. A plastic comb gains 3 × 10¹² extra electrons while rubbing with hair. Find its charge in coulomb and in µC. Is it positive or negative?

4. A neutral rod loses 2.5 × 10¹² electrons. Find its net charge, with the sign.

5. How many electrons make 1 C of charge? Show the calculation.

6. Can an object have a charge of 5.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C? Can it have 3.2 × 10⁻¹⁹ C? Explain with Q = n e.

7. Two identical metal spheres carry +8 µC and −2 µC. They are touched together and then separated. Find the charge on each sphere. (Hint: add the charges first.)

8. Classify as conductor or insulator: steel spoon, rubber glove, dry wood, graphite, tap water, glass bottle, aluminium foil, plastic pen, ebonite, human body.

9. Two charged balloons are hung near each other and they move apart. What can you say about their charges? What will happen if one balloon is replaced by a rod with the opposite charge?

10. Why does a copper rod held in the hand and rubbed with wool not show charge, while a plastic rod does? Answer in two or three lines.

11. A gold-leaf electroscope is charged negatively (leaves apart). A charged rod is brought near the knob and the leaves close slightly. What is the sign of the rod? What will happen if the rod is brought too close or touched?

12. State True or False:
    (a) A neutral body has no charge at all.
    (b) In rubbing, protons move from one body to the other.
    (c) A charged rod can attract a neutral piece of paper.
    (d) The human body is a good insulator.
    (e) Static electricity and socket electricity are the same thing.

13. Name the three ways of charging a body. Which of them gives the opposite sign of charge to the body being charged, and what is required for it?

14. Explain in three or four lines why the petrol tanker has a chain touching the ground, and why one should avoid standing under a tall tree during lightning.

15. In the story, Aarohi asked whether the sweater was giving an electric shock. In your own words (three or four lines), what is happening when sparks are seen while taking off a sweater in the dark?

### Answers

1. Glass rod **positive**, silk **negative**. **Electrons** moved (glass se silk ki taraf); protons nahi hile.
   Jaanch: glass ne electrons khoye to wo positive, silk ne liye to negative ✅

2. n = 4.8 × 10⁻⁶ C ÷ 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C/electron = **3 × 10¹³ electrons**.
   Jaanch: 3 × 10¹³ × 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ = 4.8 × 10⁻⁶ C ✅

3. Q = 3 × 10¹² × 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C = **4.8 × 10⁻⁷ C = 0.48 µC**. Electrons mile hain, to **negative**: Q = **−4.8 × 10⁻⁷ C = −0.48 µC**.
   Jaanch: 0.48 × 10⁻⁶ ÷ 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ = 3 × 10¹² ✅

4. Electrons khoye, to positive. Q = + 2.5 × 10¹² × 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C = **+4 × 10⁻⁷ C = +0.4 µC**.
   Jaanch: 0.4 × 10⁻⁶ ÷ 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ = 2.5 × 10¹² ✅

5. n = 1 C ÷ 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C/electron = **6.25 × 10¹⁸ electrons**.
   Jaanch: 6.25 × 10¹⁸ × 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ = 1.0 C ✅

6. 5.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ ÷ 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ = 3.5: poori sankhya nahi, to **nahi ho sakta**. 3.2 × 10⁻¹⁹ ÷ 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ = 2: poori sankhya, to **ho sakta hai** (2 electrons ka charge).
   Jaanch: 2 × 1.6 = 3.2 ✅; 3.5 × 1.6 = 5.6 par 3.5 poori sankhya nahi ✅

7. Total = +8 µC + (−2 µC) = +6 µC. Har gole par = +6 µC ÷ 2 = **+3 µC**.
   Jaanch: +3 µC + 3 µC = +6 µC ✅

8. **Conductor:** steel spoon, graphite, tap water, aluminium foil, human body. **Insulator:** rubber glove, dry wood, glass bottle, plastic pen, ebonite.
   Jaanch: 5 + 5 = 10 cheezein ✅

9. Dono balloons **same sign** ke charge se charged hain (dono negative ya dono positive), kyunki wo ek doosre ko dhakel rahe hain. Opposite charge wale rod se wo **kheenchenge** (attract).

10. Copper aur insaani sharir dono conductor hain, to ragadne se aaya charge sharir se hote hue zameen mein chala jaata hai. Plastic insulator hai, to charge usi jagah ruka rehta hai, isliye plastic rod charged rehti hai.

11. Electroscope negative hai aur patre kam khule (band hone lage): divergence kam hua, yaani rod ka sign **opposite**, yaani **positive** hai. Rod ko bahut paas laane ya ghundi se chhuane par patre aur band honge, kyunki rod ka positive charge electroscope ke negative charge ko cancel karne lagta hai. Agar rod ka charge zyada ho, to patre poore band hokar **dobara khul sakte hain**, ab positive charge ke saath.
    Jaanch: opposite sign par divergence ghatta hai ✅

12. (a) **False** ❌. Neutral body mein positive aur negative charge barabar hain, isliye net charge zero hai.
    (b) **False** ❌. Sirf electrons hilte hain.
    (c) **True** ✅. Charged rod neutral kaagaz ko bhi kheench leta hai.
    (d) **False** ❌. Insaani sharir conductor hai.
    (e) **False** ❌. Static mein charge ruka hua aur bahut kam; socket ki bijli mein charge taar mein behta hai aur bahut khatarnak ho sakta hai.

13. **Friction, conduction, induction.** Opposite sign **induction** se milta hai; isme charged rod paas laya jaata hai (chhuaya nahi jaata) aur body ko **earth** karna padta hai.

14. Chalte hue petrol aur tanker ke ragadne se charge jama ho sakta hai, aur ek chingaari petrol ki bhaap mein aag laga sakti hai. Chain charge ko zameen mein bahaa deti hai (earthing). Lightning ke waqt unche pedh ke neeche nahi khade rehna chahiye kyunki wo khule maidan mein sabse unchi cheez hota hai aur bijli usi par gir sakti hai.

15. Sweater utarte waqt ragad se uspar charge jama ho jaata hai. Sookhi hawa insulator hai, to charge ruka rehta hai. Jab hum sweater utaarte hain to charge thoda-thoda hawa mein nikal jaata hai, jisse chhoti si chingaari aur "chat-chat" ki aawaz aati hai. Ye static discharge hai, ghar ke socket ka karant nahi.
