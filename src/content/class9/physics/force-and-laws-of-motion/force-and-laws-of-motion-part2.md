# Topic 2: Balanced aur Unbalanced Forces

Topic 1 mein humne seekha ki force ek **push ya pull** hai, uska unit **newton (N)** hai, aur hum force ko ek **arrow** se dikha sakte hain (arrow jitna lamba, force utna bada). Wahan ek sawaal bhi chhoot gaya tha: Mr. Hegde aur Trisha ke pehle dhakkon se thela hila kyun nahi? Lekin asli zindagi mein kisi cheez par **sirf ek force** shayad hi lagta hai. Kitaab table par rakhi hai to usse kuch neeche kheench raha hai aur kuch upar tham raha hai. Cycle chalate waqt aap pedal se aage dhakelte ho aur hawa aur sadak peeche kheenchte hain. To sawal ye hai: **jab ek hi cheez par kai forces ek saath lagen, to akhir mein hota kya hai?** Aaj hum isi ka jawab dhoondhenge.

## Rassakashi: jab rassi hili hi nahi

School mein inter-house tug-of-war ka din tha: **Ashoka House** versus **Tagore House**. PT teacher **Mr. Dhillon** ne rassi ke beech mein ek laal rumaal bandha aur zameen par ek line kheench di. Unki assistant **Pihu** ke haath mein do spring balance (newton meter) the, jo rassi ke dono sirey se jude the: ek Ashoka ke taraf, ek Tagore ke taraf. Spring balance batata hai ki rassi par kitne newton ka force lag raha hai.

Seeti bajti hai. Dono teams ne poora zor laga diya. Chehre laal ho gaye, pair zameen mein gad gaye. Lekin **rassi ek inch bhi nahi hili**. Rumaal line ke theek upar tika raha.

Pihu ne dono spring balance padhe: **dono par 500 N**. Wo hairaan hokar boli, "Sir, itna zor lag raha hai, phir bhi kuch ho kyun nahi raha? Force lag raha hai to cheez hilni chahiye na!"

Mr. Dhillon muskuraye, "Bilkul sahi sawal hai, Pihu. Bas thoda ruko."

Tabhi Tagore House ke **do aur bachche** line mein aa lage, aur har ek ne rassi par **60 N** ka extra zor lagaya. Pihu ne dobara spring balance dekha: Tagore ki taraf ab **620 N**, Ashoka ki taraf wahi **500 N**. Aur is baar **rumaal dheere-dheere Tagore ki taraf sarakne laga**. Ek baar rassi hili, to wo tez hoti gayi.

Dono baar teams pull kar rahi thi, force dono baar lag raha tha. Phir pehli baar rassi kyun nahi hili, aur doosri baar kyun hili? Is kahani ko hum topic ke beech mein number ke saath solve karenge.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Net force (Resultant force)** | Ek cheez par lag rahe saare forces ka milakar nikla **ek akela asar** | 620 N − 500 N = 120 N |
| **Balanced forces** | Aise forces jinka net force **zero** ho | Rassi par 500 N aur 500 N ulti dishaon mein |
| **Unbalanced forces** | Aise forces jinka net force **zero nahi** ho | Rassi par 620 N aur 500 N |
| **Equilibrium (santulan)** | Wo haalat jisme forces balanced hon | Table par rakhi kitaab |
| **Normal force** | Kisi surface ka cheez ko **seedha upar (surface se bahar) dhakelne** wala force | Table ka kitaab ko upar dhakelna |
| **Disha (direction)** | Force kis taraf lag raha hai | Poorab (East), paschim (West), daayein, baayein |

## Part A: Ek cheez par kai forces: net force

Jab ek cheez par kai forces lagte hain, to cheez **har force ka alag-alag jawab nahi deti**. Wo saare forces ka **milakar** ek hi asar mehsoos karti hai. Is milakar nikle huye force ko **net force** (ya resultant force) kehte hain.

Is topic mein hum sirf wo case dekhenge jisme **saare forces ek hi seedhi line par** hon (jaise rassi ki line, ya sidhi sadak). Tedhe forces ki baat hum aage ki class mein karenge.

### Niyam 1: Ek hi disha ke forces **jodte** hain

Agar do forces ek hi taraf lag rahe hain, to dono ek doosre ki madad karte hain. **Net force = force 1 + force 2**, aur disha wahi rehti hai.

```
Ek hi disha mein:   F1 = 30 N,  F2 = 20 N

   [ Box ]  ======> 30 N
            ====>   20 N

   Net force = 30 N + 20 N = 50 N  (daayein ki taraf)

   [ Box ]  ==========> 50 N
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Ek hi disha ke do forces (30 N aur 20 N) jud kar 50 N ka net force dete hain. (Scale: har "=" ka matlab 5 N, upar ke arrow isi scale par bane hain.)

### Niyam 2: Ulti disha ke forces **ghatate** hain

Agar do forces ulti dishaon mein lag rahe hain, to wo ek doosre se **ladte** hain. **Net force = bada force − chhota force**, aur net force ki disha **bade force wali** hoti hai.

```
Ulti dishaon mein:   600 N daayein,  450 N baayein

   450 N  <=========   [ Rassi ]   ============  600 N
          (9 "=")                   (12 "=")

   Net force = 600 N - 450 N = 150 N  (daayein ki taraf, yani bade force ki taraf)
```

> **Figure 2.2** – 600 N daayein aur 450 N baayein: net force 150 N daayein. (Scale: har "=" ka matlab 50 N.)

### Sign ka tarika (agar aasan lage)

Ek disha ko **+** maan lo aur ulti ko **−**. Phir saare forces ko sign ke saath jodo. Jawab ka sign hi net force ki disha bata dega. Is topic mein hum zyadatar seedhe "jodo" aur "ghatao" se kaam chalayenge, kyunki disha shabdon mein likhna bhi saaf rehta hai.

Example 1 se Example 4 isi niyam ko chalate hain.

**Example 1 (rassakashi, shuruaat):** Tagore aur Ashoka dono 500 N se kheench rahe hain, ulti dishaon mein. Net force?

- Tagore ki taraf: 500 N
- Ashoka ki taraf: 500 N (ulti disha)
- Net force = 500 N − 500 N = **0 N**

**Jaanch:** Dono barabar aur ulte hain, to ek doosre ko poori tarah kaat dete hain: 500 N − 500 N = 0 N ✅

**Example 2 (bada aur chhota force):** Ek rassi par ek team 600 N daayein kheench rahi hai, doosri team 450 N baayein. Net force kitna aur kis taraf?

- Net force = 600 N − 450 N = **150 N**, **daayein** ki taraf (bade force ki taraf).

**Jaanch:** Agar daayein wali team 150 N kam lagati (450 N), to dono barabar ho jaate. Yani 450 N + 150 N = 600 N ✅

**Example 3 (teen forces, ek hi line):** Ek box par teen forces lag rahe hain: **30 N daayein**, **20 N daayein**, aur **40 N baayein**. Net force?

- Daayein wale saare forces ko jodo: 30 N + 20 N = 50 N (daayein)
- Baayein wale: 40 N (baayein)
- Net force = 50 N − 40 N = **10 N, daayein ki taraf**

**Jaanch:** Sign se: (+30) + (+20) + (−40) = +10 N, yaani 10 N daayein ✅

**Example 4 (box, dhakka aur ghisaav):** Ek box ko Pihu **40 N se poorab** (East) ki taraf dhakel rahi hai. Zameen ka **friction 25 N paschim** (West) ki taraf lag raha hai. Net force?

- Poorab: 40 N
- Paschim: 25 N (friction, jiski baat hum Topic 4 mein detail se karenge)
- Net force = 40 N − 25 N = **15 N, poorab ki taraf**

```
  Friction 25 N              Dhakka 40 N
  <===        [ Box ]       =====>

  Net force = 40 N - 25 N = 15 N  ->  poorab
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Box par 40 N dhakka poorab ki taraf aur 25 N friction paschim ki taraf: net force 15 N poorab. (Scale: har "=" ka matlab 8 N, dhakka 5 "=" aur friction 3 "=" ke aas-paas dikhaya hai.)

**Jaanch:** 25 N + 15 N = 40 N, yani friction ke upar 15 N bacha jo box ko aage le jaata hai ✅

## Part B: Balanced forces (net force = 0)

Agar kisi cheez par lag rahe saare forces ka net force **zero** ho, to hum kehte hain ki forces **balanced** hain. Ye cheez **equilibrium** mein hai.

> **Balanced forces cheez ki haalat (state) nahi badalte.**
> - Agar cheez **ruki hui** thi, to **ruki hi rehti hai**.
> - Agar cheez **chal rahi** thi, to **usi speed aur usi disha mein chalti rehti hai** (iske peeche ki poori baat Topic 3 mein).

Dhyan do: balanced forces ka matlab ye **nahi** ki force **gayab** ho gaye. Force abhi bhi lag rahe hain, bas unka milakar asar zero hai.

### Jaldi se dekho: Kitaab table par

Ek kitaab table par rakhi hai. Us par do force lagte hain:

1. **Weight (wazan)**: dharti kitaab ko **neeche** kheenchti hai. Maan lo ye 12 N hai.
2. **Table ka dhakka (normal force)**: table kitaab ko **upar** dhakelta hai. Ise **normal force** kehte hain. Ye bhi 12 N hai.

```
          ^  Table ka dhakka (normal force) = 12 N
          ^
       [ Kitab ]
          v
          v  Weight = 12 N
   ==================  table
```

> **Figure 2.4** – Table par rakhi kitaab: 12 N neeche (weight) aur 12 N upar (normal force). Net force 0, isliye kitaab ruki rehti hai.

**Example 5 (kitaab):** Weight 12 N neeche, normal force 12 N upar. Net force?

- Net force = 12 N − 12 N = **0 N**

Kitaab par forces **balanced** hain, to wo na upar jaati hai, na neeche, **ruki rehti hai**.

**Jaanch:** Agar table ka dhakka 12 N se kam hota (maan lo 5 N), to net force 12 N − 5 N = 7 N neeche hota aur kitaab table ke andar dhans jaati. Wo hota nahi, isliye table ka dhakka bilkul 12 N hi hai ✅

### Seedhi sadak par ek-si speed se chalti car

Ab ek aisi cheez lo jo **chal rahi hai**. Car seedhi sadak par **ek-si speed (constant speed)** se chal rahi hai. Engine car ko **aage** dhakel raha hai, aur hawa aur sadak ka rukaawat wala force (resistance) usse **peeche** kheench raha hai.

**Example 6 (car):** Engine ka force 2000 N aage, aur kul rukaawat 2000 N peeche. Net force?

- Net force = 2000 N − 2000 N = **0 N**

Net force zero hai, isliye car ki speed **na badh rahi hai, na ghat rahi hai**: wo ek-si speed se chalti rehti hai.

**Jaanch:** Agar engine 2000 N se zyada force lagata (maan lo 2300 N), to net force 300 N aage hota aur car tez hoti jaati. Yahan aisa nahi, to forces balanced ✅

Dhyan do: ye wohi baat hai jo Motion chapter ke "uniform velocity = zero acceleration" mein dekhi thi. Ab hum us baat ke peeche ka force wala kaaran dekh rahe hain.

### Ruki cheez par dono taraf barabar dhakka

**Example 7 (almari):** Ek bhaari almari ruki hui hai. Ek taraf se ek bachcha **80 N** se dhakka de raha hai, doosri taraf se doosra bachcha **80 N** se (ulti disha mein). Kya almari hilegi?

- Net force = 80 N − 80 N = **0 N**
- Forces balanced hain, isliye almari **nahi hilegi**, ruki hi rahegi.

**Jaanch:** Dono bachche apna-apna zor laga rahe hain, aur zor barabar hai, to kisi ek ki taraf kuch nahi khisakta: 80 N − 80 N = 0 N ✅

## Part C: Unbalanced forces (net force ≠ 0)

Agar kisi cheez par lag rahe saare forces ka net force **zero nahi** ho, to forces **unbalanced** hain.

> **Unbalanced forces cheez ki haalat badal dete hain.**
> - Ruki cheez **chalne lagti hai**.
> - Chalti cheez **tez ya dheemi** ho jaati hai.
> - Chalti cheez ki **disha badal** jaati hai.
>
> Ye badlav **net force ki disha** mein hota hai.

Matlab: agar net force daayein ki taraf hai, to ruki cheez daayein ki taraf chalna shuru karegi, aur chalti cheez (agar usi disha mein chal rahi ho) daayein ki taraf tez hogi.

### Net force kitna bada hai: asar utna bada

Hum abhi sirf **bhaav (qualitatively)** mein baat karenge:

> **Jitna bada net force, utna bada asar** (ek hi cheez par).

Ek box par 10 N ka net force lage to wo dheere-dheere tez hoga. Wahi box par 100 N ka net force lage to wo bahut jaldi tez ho jaayega. Net force aur asar ke beech ka **exact rishta** (kitna tez hoga, number mein) hum **Topic 6** mein sikhenge.

```
Wahi box, alag net force:

   Case P:  [ Box ] --->          net force chhota = 10 N
   Case Q:  [ Box ] ========>     net force bada   = 100 N   -> asar zyada
```

> **Figure 2.5** – Same box par chhota net force (P) ka asar kam, bade net force (Q) ka asar zyada.

### Disha badalna bhi ek asar hai

Cricket ka ball aapki taraf aa raha hai aur aap bat se use maarte ho. Bat ne ball par force lagaya, aur ball ki **disha badal gayi**. Ye bhi unbalanced force ka asar hai. (Is baat ko hum yahan sirf jaante hain, aur seedhi line ke bahar ke forces ki ginti aage ki class mein hogi.)

### Rassakashi ka jawab number mein

Ab Pihu ke sawal par aate hain.

**Example 8 (kahani, pehla hissa):** Ashoka 500 N, Tagore 500 N, ulti dishaon mein. Rassi par net force?

- Net force = 500 N − 500 N = **0 N**. Forces balanced hain. Rassi **ruki rehti hai**.

Isliye "force lag raha hai to cheez hilni chahiye" wali baat adhoori hai. **Force lag raha hai, par dono force milakar zero ban jaate hain.**

**Example 9 (kahani, doosra hissa):** Tagore ke do aur bachche aaye, har ek ne 60 N lagaya. Tagore ka kul force aur net force?

- Extra force = 2 × 60 N = 120 N
- Tagore ka kul force = 500 N + 120 N = **620 N**
- Ashoka ka force = 500 N (ulti disha mein)
- Net force = 620 N − 500 N = **120 N, Tagore ki taraf**

Net force zero nahi hai, forces **unbalanced** hain, aur rassi **Tagore ki taraf** hilne lagi.

```
  Ashoka (500 N)                       Tagore (620 N)
  <==========   --- laal rumaal ---   ============>
  (10 "=")                              (12 "=")

  Net force = 620 N - 500 N = 120 N  ->  Tagore ki taraf
```

> **Figure 2.6** – Tagore ke extra bachchon ke baad: 620 N ke saamne 500 N. Net force 120 N Tagore ki taraf, rassi usi taraf sarki. (Scale: har "=" ka matlab 50 N; 620 N ko lagbhag 12 "=" se dikhaya hai.)

**Jaanch:** 500 N + 120 N = 620 N ✅. Aur 2 × 60 N = 120 N ✅.

**Pihu ko jawab:** pehle dono taraf 500 N tha, forces balanced the, rassi nahi hili. Ek taraf extra 120 N aaye, forces unbalanced ho gaye, aur rassi net force ki disha mein hilne lagi. Mr. Dhillon ne sir hilaya: "Dekha, force kabhi gaya nahi tha. Bas pehle wo ek-doosre ko kaat rahe the."

## Part D: Arrows se forces padhna

Topic 1 mein dekha tha ki arrow jitna lamba, force utna bada. Ab arrows ko dekhkar net force nikaalna seekhte hain.

**Example 10 (figure padhna):** Ek box par neeche ke teen arrows lage hain. Scale: har "=" ka matlab **10 N**.

```
  A (baayein, 4 "=")       Box        B (daayein, 7 "=")
  <====                  [ BOX ]      =======>

                                      C (daayein, 2 "=")
                                      ==>
```

> **Figure 2.7** – Box par: A = 40 N baayein, B = 70 N daayein, C = 20 N daayein.

- A = 4 × 10 N = 40 N baayein
- B = 7 × 10 N = 70 N daayein
- C = 2 × 10 N = 20 N daayein
- Daayein ka kul force = 70 N + 20 N = 90 N
- Net force = 90 N − 40 N = **50 N, daayein ki taraf**

Box **daayein** ki taraf tez hoga.

**Jaanch:** Sign se: (−40) + (+70) + (+20) = +50 N, yaani 50 N daayein ✅

### Wo force jo sab ko balance kar de

Kabhi-kabhi sawal ulta hota hai: ek cheez ko ruka hua rakhna hai, to aur kaun sa force chahiye? Matlab: **jo net force abhi hai, uska ulta aur barabar force lagana padega.**

**Example 11 (kaun sa force chahiye):** Ek ruki hui cheez par **70 N poorab** aur **30 N poorab** ke force lag rahe hain, aur **50 N paschim** ka bhi ek force hai. Cheez ko ruka rakhne ke liye ek aur force chahiye. Wo kitna aur kis taraf?

- Poorab ke forces: 70 N + 30 N = 100 N
- Paschim ka force: 50 N
- Abhi ka net force = 100 N − 50 N = **50 N poorab**
- Cheez ko balance karne ke liye **50 N paschim** ka force chahiye (ulti disha, barabar maan).

**Jaanch:** Ab paschim mein 50 N + 50 N = 100 N, poorab mein 100 N, net = 100 N − 100 N = 0 N ✅

## Part E: Kya cheez hilegi? Jaldi ka faisla

Jab bhi sawal aaye, ye teen kadam yaad rakho:

1. **Disha chuno** aur har force ko apni taraf (+ ya −) mein likho.
2. **Net force nikalo** (ek hi disha wale jodo, ulti disha wale ghatao).
3. **Faisla karo:** net force zero to haalat nahi badlegi; net force zero nahi to haalat net force ki disha mein badlegi.

| Situation | Net force | Kya hoga? |
|---|---|---|
| 500 N vs 500 N rassi par | 0 N | Rassi ruki rehti hai |
| 620 N vs 500 N rassi par | 120 N Tagore ki taraf | Rassi Tagore ki taraf tez hogi |
| Table par kitaab, 12 N neeche aur 12 N upar | 0 N | Kitaab ruki rehti hai |
| Car, engine 2000 N aage, rukaawat 2000 N peeche | 0 N | Car ek-si speed se chalti rehti hai |
| Box par 40 N poorab, friction 25 N paschim | 15 N poorab | Box poorab ki taraf tez hoga |
| Almari par 80 N dono taraf | 0 N | Almari ruki rehti hai |

**Example 12 (sab ko ek saath):** Ek ruki hui cart ko ek bachcha daayein se **90 N** aur doosra baayein se **90 N** ke force se dhakel raha hai. Phir ek teesra bachcha baayein wali taraf judkar **30 N** ka extra force lagata hai. Pehle aur baad mein net force?

- Pehle: 90 N − 90 N = **0 N**, cart ruki rehti hai.
- Baad mein: baayein ka kul = 90 N + 30 N = 120 N, daayein ka = 90 N. Net force = 120 N − 90 N = **30 N, baayein ki taraf**. Cart baayein ki taraf chalne lagegi.

**Jaanch:** 120 N − 90 N = 30 N ✅. Baayein ka force bada hai, isliye disha baayein ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Rassi nahi hili, matlab koi force nahi lag raha"**

Pihu ki rassi nahi hili, par dono taraf **500 N ka force** lag raha tha. Ruki cheez par bhi force lag sakte hain, bas unka **net force zero** hota hai. Table par rakhi kitaab par bhi do force lagte hain (12 N neeche aur 12 N upar).

**Galti 2: "Cheez chal rahi hai, to uspar unbalanced force hi hoga"**

Ye sahi nahi. Seedhi sadak par ek-si speed se chalti car par forces **balanced** hain (2000 N aage, 2000 N peeche). Ek-si speed se chalne ke liye net force zero chahiye. Iska poora kaaran hum **Topic 3** mein dekhenge.

**Galti 3: Ulti disha ke forces ko jod dena**

500 N daayein aur 300 N baayein ko 500 N + 300 N = 800 N kar dena galat hai. Ulti dishaon mein **ghatate** hain: 500 N − 300 N = **200 N daayein**. Jodna sirf ek hi disha ke forces ke liye hota hai.

**Galti 4: Net force ki disha likhna bhool jaana**

Net force sirf number nahi, **disha bhi** hai. "Net force 150 N hai" adhoora jawab hai. Poora jawab: "150 N, jis taraf bada force hai."

**Galti 5: "Balanced forces matlab force khatam"**

Balanced forces ka matlab ye nahi ki force **gayab** ho gaye. Pihu ke spring balance dono taraf 500 N dikha rahe the. Forces maujood the, bas ek doosre ko kaat rahe the. Isliye cheez par unka **milakar asar** zero hai.

**Galti 6: Net force nikalte waqt unit bhool jaana**

Har jagah **N** likho: 620 N − 500 N = 120 N. Bina unit ke jawab adhoora hai.

## Quick Recap

- Ek cheez par kai forces ek saath lag sakte hain. Unka milakar asar **net force (resultant force)** kehlata hai.
- Ek hi seedhi line par: **ek hi disha** ke forces **jodo**; **ulti disha** ke forces **ghatao**. Net force ki disha **bade force** ki taraf hoti hai.
- **Balanced forces:** net force = 0. Cheez ki haalat nahi badalti: ruki cheez ruki rehti hai, chalti cheez usi speed aur disha mein chalti rehti hai.
- **Unbalanced forces:** net force ≠ 0. Cheez ki speed ya disha badalti hai, **net force ki disha** mein.
- **Jitna bada net force, utna bada asar.** Exact rishta Topic 6 mein.
- Table par rakhi kitaab: weight neeche, **normal force** upar, dono barabar, net 0.
- Balanced forces ka matlab force ka **na hona** nahi, force **maujood hain par ek doosre ko kaat rahe hain.**
- **Kahani ka jawab:** 500 N vs 500 N: net 0, rassi ruki. Tagore ko extra 2 × 60 N = 120 N mile: 620 N vs 500 N, net 120 N Tagore ki taraf, rassi usi taraf hili.

## Practice Questions

1. Two forces act on a box along the same straight line in the same direction: 8 N and 5 N. Find the net force and its direction.

2. A box is pulled with 8 N towards the east and 5 N towards the west along the same line. Find the net force and its direction.

3. Three forces act on a block along one line: 20 N to the right, 35 N to the left and 10 N to the right. Find the net force. Will the block speed up towards the right or the left?

4. A body at rest has forces of 60 N and 25 N acting on it, both towards the east. What extra force (size and direction) must be applied so that the body stays at rest?

5. A crate at rest is pushed with 120 N towards the east, while the floor resists with 120 N towards the west. Find the net force. Will the crate start moving? Explain in one line.

6. In a tug-of-war, Team A pulls with 700 N and Team B pulls with 640 N in the opposite direction. Find the net force on the rope and say in which direction the rope will start to move.

7. A book of weight 5 N lies on a table. What is the upward push of the table (the normal force)? What is the net force on the book?

8. State True or False:
   (a) If a body is at rest, no force is acting on it.
   (b) Balanced forces always give a net force of zero.
   (c) A 30 N force and a 20 N force acting in opposite directions give a net force of 50 N.
   (d) The net force is in the direction of the larger force when two forces act in opposite directions along a line.

9. The same box is acted on by a net force of 10 N in Case A and a net force of 40 N in Case B (in the same direction). In which case will the box change its motion more quickly? Explain in one line, without any formula.

10. Pihu's school holds another tug-of-war. Team X pulls with 540 N and Team Y also pulls with 540 N, and the rope does not move. Then two more students join Team Y, each adding 35 N. Find the new net force on the rope and say which team wins the pull.

### Answers

1. Net force = 8 N + 5 N = **13 N**, usi disha mein jisme dono force lag rahe hain.
   Jaanch: 13 N − 8 N = 5 N ✅

2. Net force = 8 N − 5 N = **3 N**, east ki taraf (bade force ki taraf).
   Jaanch: 5 N + 3 N = 8 N ✅

3. Right ke forces: 20 N + 10 N = 30 N. Left ka force: 35 N. Net force = 35 N − 30 N = **5 N, left ki taraf**. Block **left** ki taraf tez hoga.
   Jaanch: (+20) + (−35) + (+10) = −5 N, yaani 5 N left ✅

4. Maujooda net force = 60 N + 25 N = 85 N east. Ruka rakhne ke liye **85 N west** ka force chahiye.
   Jaanch: West mein 85 N, east mein 85 N, net = 0 N ✅

5. Net force = 120 N − 120 N = **0 N**. Forces balanced hain, isliye crate **nahi** chalega, ruka rahega.
   Jaanch: 120 N − 120 N = 0 N ✅

6. Net force = 700 N − 640 N = **60 N**, Team A ki taraf. Rassi **Team A** ki taraf hilegi.
   Jaanch: 640 N + 60 N = 700 N ✅

7. Kitaab ruki hui hai, to net force zero hai. Table ka upar ka dhakka (normal force) = **5 N**. Net force = 5 N − 5 N = **0 N**.
   Jaanch: 5 N neeche aur 5 N upar, barabar ✅

8. (a) **False** ❌. Force lag sakte hain, par unka net force zero ho sakta hai (jaise table par kitaab).
   (b) **True** ✅. Balanced ki paribhasha hi net force zero hai.
   (c) **False** ❌. Ulti disha mein ghatate hain: 30 N − 20 N = 10 N.
   (d) **True** ✅. Net force bade force ki taraf hota hai.

9. **Case B** mein. Net force bada hai (40 N > 10 N), aur jitna bada net force, utna bada asar. Exact number Topic 6 mein.
