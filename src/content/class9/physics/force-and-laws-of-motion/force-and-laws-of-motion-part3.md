# Topic 3: Newton ka Pehla Law aur Inertia

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki jab kisi cheez par lagne wale saare forces **balanced** hon (net force = 0), to uski gati mein koi badlav nahi aata: ruki hai to ruki rehti hai, chal rahi hai to usi speed aur usi disha mein chalti rehti hai. Aur agar forces **unbalanced** hon (net force ≠ 0), to speed ya disha badal jaati hai. Ab ek aur sawal: **agar koi force lag hi na raha ho, ya net force zero ho, to cheez ke saath kya hoga?** Kya wo ruk jaayegi, ya chalti rahegi? Is topic ka jawab hai **Newton ka Pehla Law**, aur uske saath ek bada shabd: **inertia**.

---

## City bus mein Mr. Naik aur Sana

Sana school se ghar jaane ke liye roz ki tarah city bus mein chadhi. Bus bhari thi, seat nahi mili, to wo handle pakad kar khadi ho gayi. Conductor Mr. Naik tickets kaat rahe the.

Achanak driver ne **zor se brake** maara. Bus ruk gayi, par saare khade yatri ek saath **aage ki taraf** jhuk gaye. Sana ka bag bhi aage jhool gaya.

Mr. Naik muskuraye, "Dhyan se, beta."

Thodi der baad bus ek stop par rukti hui khadi thi. Driver ne **achanak gaadi chalayi**, aur is baar sab yatri **peeche ki taraf** gire. Sana handle na pakadti to gir hi jaati.

Phir bus ek **tez mod** par mudi, aur sab yatri **bahar ki taraf** jhuk gaye, jaise koi unhe mod ke bahar ki taraf kheench raha ho.

Sana ne puchha, "Mr. Naik, ye bus mein har baar hota kyun hai? Kabhi aage, kabhi peeche, kabhi bahar?" Mr. Naik bole, "Iska jawab ek hi shabd mein hai, par us shabd ko samajhne mein thoda time lagega." Ye kahani hum topic ke aakhir mein ek-ek case naam lekar solve karenge.

## Part A: Thoda itihas aur pehla law

### Galileo ka vichaar

Bahut saal pehle log sochte the ki **chalte rehne ke liye force chahiye hi chahiye**: dhakka band, to cheez rukegi. Dhakka band karne par cheezein sach mein ruk bhi jaati hain, to ye baat theek bhi lagti thi.

Italy ke vaigyanik **Galileo** ne ek sochne wala (thought) experiment kiya. Ek gendh ko dhalaan (slope) par chhodo: wo neeche aati hai, phir saamne wali dhalaan par kuch oonchai tak chadhti hai. Ab saamne ki sataah ko **jyada se jyada chikni** karte jao:

```
 Sataah khurdari:   gendh thodi door jaake ruk jaati hai
 Sataah chikni:     gendh aur door tak jaati hai
 Sataah ekdum chikni (sochne wala case):
                    gendh kabhi nahi rukti, chalti hi rehti hai

   Ball                                    (sataah jitni chikni,
     o\                                      utna zyada door tak)
       \________ khurdari ______ x
        \
         \__________ chikni ___________________ x
          \
           \___________ ekdum chikni ________________________ -->
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Sataah jitni chikni hoti jaati hai, gendh utni door tak jaati hai. Bilkul chikni sataah par (jahan koi rukawat nahi) wo chalti hi rehti.

Galileo ne nishkarsh nikala ki gendh ko rokne wali koi **rukawat** hoti hai, na ki chalte rehne ke liye kisi force ki zarurat. Baad mein **Isaac Newton** ne is baat ko saaf shabdon mein likha aur use apne teen laws mein **pehle law** ke roop mein rakha.

### Newton ka Pehla Law

> **Koi bhi cheez ruki rehti hai ya seedhi line mein ek-si speed (uniform motion) se chalti rehti hai, jab tak us par koi unbalanced (net external) force na lage.**

Is law ko do tukdon mein padho:

- **Ruki cheez** ruki rahegi, jab tak net force use na hilaye.
- **Chalti cheez** usi speed aur usi disha mein (seedhi line mein) chalti rahegi, jab tak net force use na badle.

Pichhle topic se jodo: net force zero (balanced forces) ka matlab bhi yehi hai ki gati nahi badlegi. Pehla law bas ye bhi kehta hai ki **chalte rehne ke liye force ki zarurat nahi hai**, force sirf gati **badalne** ke liye chahiye.

**Jaanch (idea ki):** table par rakhi kitaab par gravity neeche kheenchti hai aur table upar dhakelti hai. Dono balanced hain, net force 0, to kitaab ruki rehti hai ✅.

## Part B: Inertia kya hai

Newton ka pehla law batata hai ki cheezein apni gati ki sthiti **badalne se mana** karti hain. Is "mana karne" wali pravritti ko **inertia** kehte hain.

> **Inertia = cheez ki wo pravritti jisse wo apni rest ya uniform motion ki sthiti mein badlav ka virodh karti hai.**

Isi liye Newton ke pehle law ko **inertia ka law** bhi kehte hain.

**Dhyan dein:** inertia koi force nahi hai. Ye cheez ka ek **gun (property)** hai, jaise lambai ya mass. Force "dhakka ya khichav" hota hai aur uska unit newton (N) hota hai. Inertia ka koi alag unit nahi hota: use hum **mass** se naapte hain.

### Inertia mass par depend karta hai

> **Jis cheez ka mass zyada, uska inertia zyada.**

Matlab bhaari cheez ki gati badalna zyada mushkil hai.

- **Loaded truck vs cycle:** khadi cycle ko dhakka dena aasan hai, par khade loaded truck ko hilana bahut mushkil. Truck ka mass zyada hai, to inertia zyada. Chalte hue bhi truck ko rokna cycle se kahin mushkil hai.
- **Khaali vs bhari almari:** khaali almari ko sarka lo, par kitaabon se bhari almari ko hilana mushkil. Bhari almari ka mass zyada hai.

**Numerical sense (formula ki zarurat nahi):** kis cheez ka inertia zyada hai, bas mass dekho.

- Cycle 15 kg, loaded truck 8000 kg: truck ka inertia zyada. Wo cycle se 8000 ÷ 15 ≈ **533 guna** zyada mass wala hai, to uska inertia bhi bahut zyada hai.
- Cricket ball 150 g aur ek halka ball 50 g: pehle wale ka mass 150 ÷ 50 = **3 guna**, to uska inertia zyada.
- Khaali bucket 1 kg, usme 10 kg paani bhara: ab kul mass 1 + 10 = **11 kg**, to inertia 11 guna ho gaya.

**Jaanch:** 8000 ÷ 15 = 533.3 ✅, 150 ÷ 50 = 3 ✅, 1 kg + 10 kg = 11 kg ✅

## Part C: Inertia ke teen roop

### 1. Inertia of rest (ruke rehne ka inertia)

**Ruki hui cheez ruki rehna chahti hai.** Jab tak net force na lage, wo apni jagah par hi rehti hai.

**Example 1: Glass par card aur coin.** Ek glass ke upar ek card rakho, card par ek coin. Card ko ungli se jhatke se tez maaro. Card to door ud jaata hai, par coin **seedha glass mein gir jaata hai**.

```
      coin
     [ o ]            <- coin card par
   ===========        <- card
     \_____/          <- glass (khadi)
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Glass ke upar card aur card par coin. Card ko jhatke se hataane par coin glass mein gir jaata hai.

Kyun? Jhatka sirf card ko laga. Coin ruka hua tha, aur use aage sarkane wala koi khaas force nahi laga, to wo **ruka rehna chahta hai**. Neeche se card hat gaya, ab sirf gravity bachi, to coin seedha neeche glass mein aa gaya.

**Example 2: Carpet ko danda se peetna.** Carpet ko latka kar danda se peeto: carpet ko jhatka lagta hai aur wo hilta hai, par dhool ke kan **ruke rehna chahte hain**. Carpet hat jaata hai, aur dhool peeche chhoot kar neeche gir jaati hai.

**Example 3: Chikni table-cloth par rakha pot.** Bahut chikni table-cloth ko agar bahut tezi se kheencho, to uske upar rakhe bartan apni jagah par hi rehte hain. Cloth turant nikal jaata hai, bartan ko lagne wala force bahut kam aur bahut chhote samay ke liye hota hai, to wo ruka rehta hai. (Ye tabhi hota hai jab cloth chikna ho aur kheencha bahut tez jaaye, ghar par mat aazmana!)

**Example 4: Ped hilana, phal girana.** Ped ki daal ko jhatke se hilao. Daal hilti hai, par phal **ruke rehna chahte hain**, daal unhe saath nahi le jaati, to wo daal se alag hokar neeche gir jaate hain.

**Example 5: Bus achanak chale to yatri peeche girte hain.** Pehle wo yatri ruke hue the, bus chali, par unka sharir **ruka rehna chahta hai**. Paon bus ke floor se bandhe hain, to wo bus ke saath aage chal diye, par upar ka hissa peeche rah gaya.

### 2. Inertia of motion (chalte rehne ka inertia)

**Chalti hui cheez usi speed se chalte rehna chahti hai.** Use rokne ke liye net force chahiye.

**Example 6: Bus achanak ruke to yatri aage jhukte hain.** Chalti bus mein yatri bhi bus ki speed se chal rahe the. Brake lagne par bus ruki, par yatri ka sharir **chalte rehna chahta hai**, to wo aage ki taraf jhuk gaye.

**Example 7: Daud ke lambi kood.** Lambi kood (long jump) wala khiladi pehle daudta hai, phir koodta hai. Daudne se uski gati hoti hai, aur koodne ke baad bhi wo **aage badhte rehna chahta hai**, isse wo zyada door tak jaata hai.

**Example 8: Lawn roller.** Bhaari lawn roller ko ek baar chalu karne ke baad chalate rehna aasan lagta hai, par use rokna mushkil. Mass zyada hai, to inertia zyada.

**Example 9: Brake lagane ke baad bhi gaadi thodi door jaati hai.** Brake lagne ke baad bhi gaadi kuch door chal kar hi rukti hai, kyunki wo chalte rehna chahti hai.

> **Chhota note (gendh chalti train mein):** chalti train mein koi gendh seedhe upar uchhale to wo wapas haath mein hi aati hai. Kyunki gendh bhi train ki hi speed se aage chal rahi thi, aur uski wo gati upar jaate aur neeche aate waqt bhi bani rahti hai (inertia of motion). Is baat ko aur gehraai se aage ki classes mein padhoge.

### 3. Inertia of direction (disha ka inertia)

**Chalti hui cheez usi disha mein seedhi line mein chalte rehna chahti hai.** Disha badalne ke liye bhi net force chahiye.

**Example 10: Mod par bus ya car.** Mod par yatri bahar ki taraf jhukte hain. Bus mod gayi, par yatri ka sharir **seedha hi jaana chahta hai**.

```
        mod ka kendra
              x
            .   .
          .       \
        .   Bus -> \   yatri ka sharir seedha
       .            \  jaana chahta hai
        .       ----> (seedhi line, bahar ki taraf)
          .
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Mod par bus ghoomti hai, par yatri ka sharir seedhi line mein aage jaana chahta hai, isliye wo mod ke bahar ki taraf jhukta hua mehsoos hota hai.

**Example 11: Pathar ghumakar phenkna (sling).** Pathar ko dhaage se baandh kar ghumao aur dhaaga chhod do: pathar **seedhi line** mein, jis disha mein wo us pal chal raha tha, ud jaata hai.

**Example 12: Cycle ya scooter ka sharp turn.** Tez mod par chalaane wale ko andar ki taraf jhukna padta hai, kyunki sharir seedha jaana chahta hai aur gaadi mudti hai.

**Example 13: Paani lagi chhatri ghumana.** Chhatri ko tezi se ghumao to paani ki boondein **seedhi** line mein bahar ki taraf udti hain (ghoomti chhatri se alag hokar).

### Teeno ki tulna

| Prakar | Kaisi cheez | Cheez kya chahti hai | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inertia of rest** | Ruki hui | Ruki rehna | Coin glass mein girta hai, carpet se dhool nikalti hai |
| **Inertia of motion** | Chalti hui | Usi speed se chalte rehna | Bus ruke to yatri aage jhukte hain |
| **Inertia of direction** | Chalti hui, mud rahi | Usi disha mein seedhi line mein jaana | Mod par yatri bahar jhukte hain |

## Part D: Rolling ball aakhir mein ruk kyun jaati hai?

Ek sawal: agar chalti cheez chalte hi rehna chahti hai, to zameen par ludhakti gendh ya cycle aakhir mein **ruk kyun jaati hai**?

Pehle law ke mutabik, gati tab badalti hai jab koi **unbalanced force** lage. Gendh ruki, to uspar zaroor koi unbalanced force laga. Wo force hai **friction (gharshan)**: gendh aur zameen ke beech lagne wala wo force jo gati ko rokta hai. Pehla law galat nahi hota: **gendh apne aap nahi rukti, use ek force rokta hai.**

Agar friction bilkul na ho (jaise Galileo ki bilkul chikni sataah), to gendh kabhi nahi rukegi. Friction kya hai, kaise kaam karta hai aur kab kaam aata hai: ye Topic 4 mein.

## Part E: Mr. Naik aur Sana ka sawal solve karte hain

Ab ek-ek case lekar dekhte hain.

| Bus mein kya hua | Kaun sa inertia | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| Achanak brake lagi, yatri **aage** jhuke | **Motion ka inertia** | Yatri bus ki speed se chal rahe the; bus ruki, par unka sharir chalte rehna chahta hai |
| Bus achanak chali, yatri **peeche** gire | **Rest ka inertia** | Yatri ruke the; paon ke saath neeche ka hissa bus ke saath chala, par upar ka hissa ruka rehna chahta hai |
| Tez mod par yatri **bahar** jhuke | **Disha ka inertia** | Sharir seedhi line mein jaana chahta hai; bus mud gayi |

Aur Sana ne handle kyun pakda? Handle (aur seat, floor) wo **force** deta hai jo yatri ke sharir ki gati ko bus ke saath badalta hai. Bina us force ke sharir apni purani gati aur disha mein hi chalna chahta hai.

```
 Bus chal rahi (-->)       Achanak brake        Achanak start
  o  o  o  (sab seedhe)     o  o  o  (aage)       o  o  o  (peeche)
 [==========]-->           [==========]|         [==========]-->
```

> **Figure 3.4** – Brake par yatri aage jhukte hain (motion ka inertia); achanak start par peeche (rest ka inertia).

Mr. Naik ne Sana se kaha, "Ab samjhi? Koi cheez tumhe aage, peeche ya bahar **kheench nahi rahi**. Tumhara sharir bas apni purani gati badalne se **mana** kar raha hai: yehi inertia hai. Isiliye jab bus rukne wali ho, to handle pakad lo." Sana ne seekha ki **bus mein safety** ka matlab hi inertia ko samajhna hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **"Inertia ek force hai."** Galat. Inertia cheez ka gun hai (mass se naapa jaata hai). Force ka unit N hota hai; inertia ka alag unit nahi hota.
2. **"Bhaari cheez ko hilana ya rokna isliye mushkil hai kyunki uska weight use roke rakhta hai."** Galat. Bilkul chikne floor par bhi (jahan friction na ho) bhaari cheez ki gati badalna mushkil hota hai, kyunki uska **mass** zyada hai, aur mass zyada to inertia zyada. Gati badalne ka virodh inertia karta hai, weight (gravity ka kheenchav) nahi.
3. **"Constant velocity se chalne wali cheez par zaroor koi force lag raha hoga."** Galat. Constant velocity ka matlab net force zero. Force gati ko banaye rakhne ke liye nahi, **badalne** ke liye chahiye.
4. **"Chalte rehne ke liye force chahiye."** Galat. Gendh friction ki wajah se ruki, na ki force ke abhaav se. Bina friction ke wo chalti rehti.
5. **"Bus mein koi mujhe peeche se kheench raha hai."** Galat. Koi alag force aage-peeche nahi kheench raha. Sharir bas apni purani gati badalne se mana kar raha hai: yehi inertia hai.

## Quick Recap

- Galileo ne dikhaya ki chikni sataah par gendh door tak jaati hai: rukawat hati to chalti hi rahegi. Newton ne ise pehle law mein likha.
- **Pehla law:** cheez ruki rehti hai ya seedhi line mein ek-si speed se chalti rehti hai, jab tak unbalanced (net external) force use na badle.
- **Inertia** = rest ya motion ki sthiti badalne ka virodh karne ki pravritti. Ye force nahi, cheez ka gun hai.
- Mass zyada to inertia zyada: loaded truck vs cycle, bhari vs khaali almari.
- Teen roop: **rest** (coin, carpet, phal), **motion** (bus ruke to aage jhukna, lawn roller), **direction** (mod par bahar jhukna).
- Gendh ka rukna friction ki wajah se hai, jo ek unbalanced force hai (Topic 4).
- Bus ke teeno jhatke: brake par aage (motion), start par peeche (rest), mod par bahar (direction).

## Practice Questions

1. State Newton's first law of motion in your own words.
2. What is inertia? Is it a force? Give a reason.
3. Which has greater inertia: a cycle of mass 15 kg or a loaded truck of mass 8000 kg? How many times larger is the truck's mass?
4. A bucket of mass 1 kg is filled with 10 kg of water. What is the total mass, and has its inertia increased or decreased compared with the empty bucket?
5. Identify the kind of inertia (rest, motion or direction) in each case: (a) dust falls out of a carpet when it is beaten with a stick; (b) passengers lean forward when a bus stops suddenly; (c) a car taking a turn makes the passengers lean outward; (d) a coin drops into the glass when the card below it is flicked away.
6. Why do fruits fall from a tree when a branch is shaken?
7. A rolling ball slows down and stops. Does this go against Newton's first law? Explain.
8. A car is moving at a constant velocity on a straight, level road. Is there a net force on it? Explain.
9. Rohit says, "When the bus turned, something pulled me outward." Correct his statement using inertia.
10. Two boxes, one with mass 2 kg and the other with mass 40 kg, are on a smooth floor. Which one is harder to start moving, and which one is harder to stop once moving at the same speed? How many times greater is the mass of the heavier box?

### Answers

1. Agar kisi cheez par unbalanced (net external) force na lage, to ruki cheez ruki rehti hai aur chalti cheez seedhi line mein ek-si speed se chalti rehti hai.

2. Inertia cheez ki wo pravritti hai jisse wo apni rest ya uniform motion ki sthiti mein badlav ka virodh karti hai. Ye **force nahi** hai, kyunki ye cheez ka gun (property) hai jo mass se naapa jaata hai; force dhakka ya khichav hota hai aur uska unit N hai.

3. **Loaded truck** ka inertia zyada hai, kyunki uska mass zyada hai. Mass ka ratio = 8000 kg ÷ 15 kg ≈ **533 guna**.
   Jaanch: 15 × 533.3 ≈ 8000 ✅

4. Total mass = 1 kg + 10 kg = **11 kg**. Mass badha, to inertia **badh gaya** (khaali bucket se 11 guna).
   Jaanch: 11 ÷ 1 = 11 ✅

5. (a) **Rest** ka inertia: dhool ruki rehna chahti hai jabki carpet jhatke se hilta hai. (b) **Motion** ka inertia: yatri chalte rehna chahte hain jabki bus ruki. (c) **Direction** ka inertia: yatri seedhi line mein jaana chahte hain jabki car mudi. (d) **Rest** ka inertia: coin ruka rehna chahta hai, card hat jaata hai.

6. Daal ko jhatka lagta hai to wo hilti hai, par phal **ruke rehna chahte hain** (rest ka inertia). Daal unhe saath nahi le jaati, to wo alag hokar gravity ke karan neeche gir jaate hain.

7. **Nahi.** Gendh apne aap nahi rukti: **friction** naam ka unbalanced force use rokta hai. Pehla law kehta hai ki gati net force se badalti hai, aur yahan friction wo net force hai.

8. **Nahi, net force zero hai.** Constant velocity ka matlab hai ki gati nahi badal rahi, aur pehle law ke mutabik ye tabhi hai jab net force zero ho (engine ka force aur rukawat balanced hain).

9. Rohit galat hai: koi cheez use bahar nahi kheench rahi thi. Bus mud gayi, par uska sharir **disha ke inertia** ke karan seedhi line mein hi jaana chahta tha, isliye wo mod ke bahar ki taraf jhuka hua mehsoos hua.

10. **40 kg** wala box dono kaam mein zyada mushkil hai, kyunki uska mass zyada hai (to inertia zyada: hilana bhi aur rokna bhi). Mass ka ratio = 40 kg ÷ 2 kg = **20 guna**.
    Jaanch: 2 × 20 = 40 ✅

**Aage kya?** Pehla law kehta hai ki chalti cheez chalti hi rehni chahiye. To phir jo kuch bhi chalta hai, rukta kyun hai: ludhakti gendh, band hoti cycle, phislati kitaab? Us **chhupe hue force** ki baat hum Topic 4 mein karenge.
