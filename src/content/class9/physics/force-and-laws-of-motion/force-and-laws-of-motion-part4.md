# Topic 4: Friction: Rok-Tok Karne Wala Force

Pichhle topic mein humne Newton ka Pehla Law dekha: koi cheez ruki ho ya seedhi line mein ek-si speed se chal rahi ho, to wo waisi hi rehti hai jab tak us par **net force** na lage. Wahin ye bhi dekha tha ki ludhakti gend apne aap nahi rukti, use ek force rokta hai, aur us force ka naam bhi aaya tha. Aaj usi force ko poori tarah samjhenge: **friction**.

---

## Polish wale din ka phisalna

School mein aaj floor-polishing ka din tha. Caretaker Mr. Chacko ne subah hi poore corridor ko wax se chamka diya tha. Floor itna chamak raha tha ki usme chhat ki lights ka reflection dikh raha tha.

Lunch ke baad Tia apni dost ke saath corridor se guzri. Jaise hi usne chamakte floor par kadam rakha, uska pair phisal gaya! Wo girte-girte bachi. Aage jo bhi bachche aaye, sab ek-ek karke dheere-dheere, hath failaye, bachte hue chalne lage.

Lekin Tia ne dekha ki darwaze ke paas jahan Mr. Chacko ne ek rubber ka floor mat bichha rakha tha, wahan sab aaram se chal rahe the, bina phisle.

Tia ne Mr. Chacko se poochha, "Uncle, joote to wahi hain, pair wahi hain, floor bhi usi building ka hai. Phir polish wale hisse par phislan aur mat par bilkul nahi, aisa kyun?"

Mr. Chacko muskuraye, "Beta, isme ek chhupa hua force kaam kar raha hai. Aao, dono milkar dhoondhte hain." Is kahani ko hum topic ke ant mein solve karenge.

## Part A: Friction kya hai aur kyun hota hai

Do surfaces jab ek-doosre ko chhoo rahe hon aur ek-doosre par **sarakne** ki koshish karein (ya sarak rahe hon), to unke beech ek force lagta hai jo us sarakne ka **virodh** karta hai. Isi ko **friction** kehte hain.

> **Friction ki disha hamesha us "relative motion" ya "motion ki koshish" ke ulti hoti hai.**

Maan lo ek kitaab mez par daayein sarak rahi hai. Friction **baayein** lagega, yani kitaab ko rokne ki taraf.

```
          kitaab -->  (chal rahi hai daayein)
        +-----------+
  <-----|  KITAAB   |
 friction +----------+
 ======================  mez
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Kitaab daayein sarak rahi hai, friction baayein (ulti disha mein) lagta hai.

### Friction hota kyun hai?

Surface chahe kitna bhi chikna dikhe, **koi bhi surface poori tarah smooth nahi hota.** Microscope se dekho to har surface par chhote-chhote ubhaar aur gaddhe (bumps aur dents) hote hain. Jab do surface ek-doosre par rakhe jaate hain, to inke ubhaar ek-doosre ke gaddhon mein **phans** jaate hain. Sarakne ki koshish karne par ye ubhaar ek-doosre ko rokte hain. Wahi rok friction hai.

Neeche do surfaces ka bada kiya hua (zoomed) sketch hai:

```
   upar wala surface (zoom kiya hua)
    \/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/
    /\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\
   ------------------------------
    ubhaar ek-doosre ke gaddhon mein phanse hain
   ------------------------------
    \/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/
    /\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\
   neeche wala surface (zoom kiya hua)
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Do surfaces ka bada kiya hua rup: dono par tiny ubhaar aur gaddhe hote hain, jo ek-doosre mein phans kar sarakne ka virodh karte hain.

Isi wajah se **jitna rough surface, utna zyada friction**, aur jitna smooth surface, utna kam friction.

Aur yaad rakho: friction ek **force** hai, isliye iska unit bhi force wala hai: **newton (N)**.

## Part B: Friction kin baaton par nirbhar karta hai

### 1. Surfaces ki prakriti (kitne rough ya smooth hain)

Rough surface ke ubhaar bade aur zyada hote hain, to friction zyada. Wahi joote pehen kar sandpaper par chalo to bahut grip milti hai, aur gili sabun lagi tile par phisalte ho. Surface alag, friction alag.

### 2. Surfaces ko kitni zor se dabaya ja raha hai

Jab ek surface doosre par **zyada zor se dabta** hai, to ubhaar aur gehre phanste hain, aur friction badh jaata hai. Isliye **bhaari cheez par friction zyada** hota hai (kyunki wo zameen par zyada zor se dabti hai).

Ek aam anubhav: khaali bore ko zameen par ghaseetna aasan hai, par wahi bora anaaj se bhar jaaye to bahut zor lagta hai.

### 3. Area ke baare mein ek halka sa note

Sochte hain ki bada surface area ho to friction zyada hoga. Par aam sthitiyon mein **friction area par khaas nirbhar nahi karta**; asli baat hai ki surfaces kaise hain aur kitna dab rahe hain. Isse zyada is class mein nahi jaayenge.

## Part C: Friction ke teen roop

Friction har sthiti mein ek jaisa nahi hota. Teen mukhya roop dekho. Ek bhaari **almirah** ko zameen par dhakelne ka example lete hain.

### 1. Static friction: jab cheez chal hi nahi rahi

Tum almirah ko dheere se dhakelte ho. Wo hilta nahi. Kyun? Kyunki zameen ne **utna hi friction** laga diya jitna tumhara push tha, aur dono ek-doosre ko cancel kar rahe hain. Isi ko **static friction** kehte hain: cheez ke chalna shuru karne se pehle.

Mazedaar baat: **static friction apne aap adjust hota hai.** Jitna dhakeloge, utna hi friction lagega, par ek **sabse bada (maximum) limit** tak. Us limit ko **limiting static friction** kehte hain. Jab tumhara push is limit se bada ho jaaye, tab almirah **chal padta hai**.

### 2. Sliding friction: jab cheez sarak rahi hai

Almirah ke chalte hi uska friction thoda **kam** ho jaata hai. Isi ko **sliding friction** (ya kinetic friction) kehte hain. Isiliye almirah ko hilane mein sabse zyada zor lagta hai, par ek baar chal pade to thoda halka lagta hai.

### 3. Rolling friction: jab cheez ludhak rahi hai

Agar almirah ke neeche **rollers** ya pahiye laga do, to wo ludhakne lagta hai. Ludhakne wala friction sabse kam hota hai. Isiliye suitcase pahiye wala hota hai, aur bhaari saaman ko trolley par dhakelna aasan hota hai.

### Teeno ki tulna

> **Aam sthitiyon mein: static friction (maximum) ≥ sliding friction > rolling friction**

```
 friction
 (bada)   static (maximum)  ##########
          sliding           ########
          rolling           ##
 (chhota)
```

> **Figure 4.3** – Teen tarah ke friction ki tulna (sirf andaza dikhane ke liye): static sabse bada, phir sliding, rolling sabse chhota.

| Roop | Kab lagta hai | Almirah wala example | Kitna bada |
|---|---|---|---|
| Static | Cheez abhi chali nahi | Dhakela par hila nahi | Push ke barabar, maximum tak |
| Sliding | Cheez sarak rahi hai | Almirah zameen par sarak raha hai | Static maximum se thoda kam ya barabar |
| Rolling | Cheez ludhak rahi hai | Almirah rollers par | Sabse kam |

## Part D: Solved examples: friction aur net force

Yahan hum Topic 2 ke **net force** ke idea ko friction ke saath jodenge. Yaad rakho: **net force = aage wale forces minus peeche wale forces**. Agar net force **zero** hai, to cheez ka haal waisa hi rehta hai (Topic 3).

**Example 1 (push kam hai):** Ek block zameen par rakha hai. Uska maximum static friction **30 N** hai. Tum use **10 N** se dhakelte ho. Block hilta hai? Friction kitna hai?

- Push = 10 N, jo 30 N ke limit se kam hai, to block **ruka rahega.**
- Static friction apne aap adjust hota hai, to friction = push = **10 N** (ulti disha mein).
- Net force = 10 N − 10 N = **0 N**

**Jaanch:** 10 N ≤ 30 N ✅ (limit se bahar nahi gaye) aur 10 N − 10 N = 0 N ✅ (isliye block ruka hai)

**Example 2 (push thoda badhaya):** Wahi block (maximum static friction 30 N). Ab push **20 N** hai.

- 20 N < 30 N, to block ruka rahega.
- Friction = push = **20 N**
- Net force = 20 N − 20 N = **0 N**

**Jaanch:** friction pehle 10 N tha, ab 20 N hai: dhakke ke saath friction badha ✅ (kyunki ye 30 N ki limit ke andar hai)

> **Dhyan do:** dono examples mein friction alag nikla (10 N, phir 20 N), par block dono baar ruka. Isse pata chalta hai ki **ruki cheez par friction zero nahi hota**, wo push ke barabar hota hai.

```
 push (N)   friction (N)   block
   10          10          ruka
   20          20          ruka
   30          30 (max)    chalne ki kagaar par
   35          25          chal pada
```

> **Figure 4.4** – Block par push badhne ke saath static friction bhi badhta hai (30 N tak), phir block chal padta hai aur sliding friction 25 N lagta hai.

**Example 3 (push limit se bada):** Wahi block. Ab push **35 N** hai. Block chalte hi sliding friction **25 N** ho jaata hai. Net force kitna hai, aur kis disha mein?

- 35 N > 30 N (maximum static friction), to block **chal padta hai.**
- Aage ka force = 35 N, peeche ka force (sliding friction) = 25 N
- Net force = 35 N − 25 N = **10 N** aage ki taraf

**Jaanch:** 25 N + 10 N = 35 N ✅. Net force zero nahi hai, isliye block ki speed badhti jaayegi. (Net force se kitna badlav hota hai, wo Topic 6 mein dekhenge.)

**Example 4 (ek-sa chalna):** Mazdoor ek peti ko zameen par **ek-si speed** se sarka raha hai. Peti par sliding friction **25 N** hai. Mazdoor kitna push laga raha hai?

- Ek-si speed ka matlab: kisi cheez ki speed na badhe, na ghate, yani **net force zero** (Topic 3 ka idea).
- Push = friction = **25 N**
- Net force = 25 N − 25 N = **0 N**

**Jaanch:** 25 N − 25 N = 0 N ✅. Dhyan do: peti chal rahi hai, phir bhi net force zero hai, kyunki forces balanced hain (Topic 2).

**Example 5 (do log milkar):** Do bachche ek bhaari almirah ko ek hi disha mein dhakel rahe hain: ek **12 N** aur doosra **18 N** ke force se. Almirah ka maximum static friction **45 N** hai. Almirah chalega? Friction kitna hoga?

- Kul push = 12 N + 18 N = **30 N** (dono ek hi disha mein, to jod diye)
- 30 N < 45 N, to almirah **nahi chalega.**
- Friction = kul push = **30 N**
- Net force = 30 N − 30 N = **0 N**

**Jaanch:** 30 N ≤ 45 N ✅. Almirah ko chalane ke liye kul push 45 N se thoda zyada chahiye, yaani abhi ke push se 15 N se thoda zyada aur (45 N − 30 N = 15 N) ✅

**Example 6 (push hatane par):** Ek peti 40 N ke push se ek-si speed se sarak rahi hai, aur sliding friction 40 N hai. Achanak push hata diya jaata hai. Ab peti par kaun sa force bacha, aur peti ka kya hoga?

- Pehle: push = friction = 40 N, net force 0 N (ek-si speed).
- Push hatne ke baad: sirf friction bacha, **40 N peeche ki taraf.**
- Net force = 40 N, peeche ki taraf. Ye peti ki chaal ko **kam** karega, aur peti kuch der baad **ruk jaayegi.**

**Jaanch:** Topic 3 mein pucha tha ki chalti gend rukti kyun hai. Yahi jawab hai: friction ne use roka ✅. Agar friction zero hota, to peti ek-si speed se chalti hi jaati.

**Example 7 (zyada daabav, zyada friction):** Ek kitaab mez par rakhi hai aur uska maximum static friction **6 N** hai. Us kitaab ke upar usi jaisi ek aur kitaab rakh do (to mez par dabaav lagbhag dugna ho jaata hai). Is sthiti mein maximum static friction lagbhag dugna, yaani lagbhag **12 N**, ho jaata hai. Ab aap 10 N se dhakelo to?

- Ek kitaab ke liye: 10 N > 6 N, to wo **chal padti.**
- Do kitaabon ke liye: 10 N < 12 N, to wo **nahi chalti**, aur friction = **10 N.**

**Jaanch:** 6 N × 2 = 12 N ✅. Dabaav badha to friction ki limit badhi: ye Part B (2) wali baat hai ✅

## Part E: Friction: ek zaroori burai (necessary evil)

Friction ko log aksar bura bolte hain, par socho: agar duniya se friction **ek din ke liye gayab** ho jaaye to?

- Hum chal nahi paayenge (jaise Tia chamakte floor par phisli thi, wahi har jagah).
- Pen kagaz par likh nahi paayega.
- Gaadi ke brake kaam nahi karenge.
- Dhaage ki ganth khul jaayegi, aur hum cheezein pakad nahi paayenge.

Isliye friction ko **necessary evil** kehte hain: nuksaan bhi, faayda bhi.

### Friction kahan madad karta hai

| Kaam | Friction kaise madad karta hai |
|---|---|
| Chalna | Jooton ke talwe aur zameen ke beech friction pair ko phisalne se rokta hai aur aage badhne mein madad karta hai |
| Brake | Brake pad aur pahiye ke beech friction gaadi ko rokta hai |
| Likhna | Pencil ya pen aur kagaz ke beech friction se nishaan banta hai |
| Tyre ki pakad | Tyre aur sadak ke beech friction gaadi ko phisalne se bachata hai |
| Ganth aur pakad | Rassi ki ganth aur haath mein pakdi cheez friction se tikti hai |
| Keel aur screw | Ye friction ki wajah se deewar mein tike rehte hain |

### Friction kahan nuksaan karta hai

- **Ghisaav (wear and tear):** jooton ke talwe, tyre, machine ke purze ghis kar kharab ho jaate hain.
- **Garmi:** haath ragdo to garam hote hain, aur machine ke purze bhi friction se garam hote hain.
- **Urja ki barbaadi:** friction se jo urja garmi mein badal jaati hai, wo kaam ki nahi rehti. Isliye machine ko chalaane mein zyada urja lagti hai.

## Part F: Friction badhana aur ghatana

### Friction badhane ke tareeke

- **Surface ko rough banana:** jooton ke talwe par grooves, cricket ke bat ki handle par grip.
- **Treads:** tyre aur jooton par khaanchen bani hoti hain taaki zyada grip mile.
- **Phislan wali jagah par ret ya rakh daalna:** barish ya barf mein phislan par ret daalne se friction badhta hai.
- **Dabaav badhana:** brake pad ko zor se dabana.

### Friction ghatane ke tareeke

- **Lubricants (tel, grease):** cycle ki chain aur machine ke purzon mein tel lagana. Tel surfaces ke beech ek patli parat bana deta hai, jisse ubhaar ek-doosre mein kam phanste hain.
- **Polish karna:** surface ko chikna banana (jaise hamara polish wala floor).
- **Ball bearings:** pahiye aur axle ke beech chhoti goliyan hoti hain, jo **sliding ko rolling mein** badal deti hain. Rolling friction sliding se kam hota hai, isliye cheez asaani se ghoomti hai.
- **Streamlined shape:** gaadiyon aur hawaai jahaazon ka aakaar aisa banaya jaata hai ki hawa ka rokna kam ho.
- **Air cushion:** hovercraft jaise vehicle hawa ki ek parat par tikte hain, to surface se friction bahut kam ho jaata hai.

```
   Sliding (zyada)             Ball bearing (kam)
   [  AXLE   ]                 [  AXLE   ]
   ~~~~ rub ~~~~                o o o o   <- chhoti goliyan ludhakti hain
   [  PAHIYA ]                 [  PAHIYA ]
```

> **Figure 4.5** – Seedhe ragadne (sliding) ki jagah chhoti goliyan (ball bearings) lagane se friction kam ho jaata hai, kyunki ab cheez ludhakti hai.

### Hawa aur paani ka friction (fluid friction)

Sirf thos surfaces hi nahi, **hawa aur paani** (jinhe fluids kehte hain) bhi chalti cheez ko rokte hain. Isse **fluid friction** ya **drag** kehte hain. Tez cycle chalate hue hawa ka dabaav mehsoos hota hai, ya paani mein haath tezi se chalane par rukawat milti hai. Parachute isi drag ka istemaal karke girne ki speed ko kam karta hai. Is par abhi sirf itna hi.

Wapas corridor par chaliye. Mr. Chacko aur Tia ne milkar dekha:

- **Polish kiya hua floor:** wax ne surface ko behad chikna bana diya hai. Ubhaar bahut kam hain, to jooton aur floor ke beech friction **bahut kam** hai. Pair ko rokne wala force kamzor hai, isliye pair phisal jaata hai.

Tia ne soch kar kaha, "To chalna bhi friction ki wajah se hi possible hai!" Mr. Chacko hanse, "Bilkul. Aur ye aisa force hai jo hamesha rokta nahi, kabhi-kabhi aage badhne mein bhi madad karta hai. Ye kyun hota hai, ye Topic 7 mein samjhenge."

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Friction hamesha motion ko rokta hi hai."**
Friction **relative motion (ya uski koshish)** ka virodh karta hai. Chalte waqt hamara pair peeche ki taraf zameen ko dhakelta hai, aur zameen par friction hamare pair par **aage ki taraf** lagta hai. Yani yahan friction hamari madad karta hai, rokta nahi. (Ye kaise hota hai, Topic 7 mein dekhenge.)

**Galti 2: "Ruki hui cheez par friction zero hota hai."**
Galat. Example 1 aur 2 mein block ruka tha, par friction 10 N aur 20 N tha. Ruki cheez par bhi **static friction** lag sakta hai.

**Galti 3: "Surface area zyada to friction bahut zyada."**
Nahi. Friction mukhya roop se surfaces ki prakriti aur dabaav par nirbhar karta hai, area par khaas nahi.

**Galti 4: "Friction koi extra force hai jo cheez ko aage dhakelta hai."**
Friction apne aap kisi cheez ko nahi dhakelta. Wo hamesha dono surfaces ke sarakne ka virodh karta hai. (Chalne mein friction aage ki taraf isliye lagta hai kyunki pair peeche ki taraf sarakna chahta hai, aur friction us sarakne ke ulte lagta hai.)

**Galti 5: "Static friction hamesha ek hi value ka hota hai."**
Galat. Static friction push ke saath badalta hai (10 N push par 10 N, 20 N push par 20 N), aur sirf maximum limit tak jaata hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Friction** wo force hai jo do chhoote hue surfaces ke beech relative motion (ya uski koshish) ka virodh karta hai; iski disha motion ke ulti hoti hai.
- Friction isliye hota hai kyunki **koi bhi surface poori tarah smooth nahi hota**: ubhaar aur gaddhe ek-doosre mein phanste hain.
- Friction **surfaces ki prakriti** (roughness) aur **dabaav** (bhaari cheez par zyada) par nirbhar karta hai; area par khaas nahi.
- Teen roop: **static** (cheez chali nahi), **sliding** (sarak rahi hai), **rolling** (ludhak rahi hai). Aam taur par static (maximum) ≥ sliding > rolling.
- Static friction **push ke barabar** adjust hota hai, par ek **maximum limit** tak. Push limit se zyada ho to cheez chal padti hai.
- Friction ek **necessary evil** hai: chalne, brake, likhne, pakad mein madad karta hai; ghisaav, garmi aur urja ki barbaadi karta hai.
- **Badhane ke tareeke:** rough surface, treads, ret. **Ghatane ke tareeke:** lubricant, polish, ball bearings, streamlining, air cushion.
- Hawa aur paani ka friction **fluid friction (drag)** kehlata hai.

## Practice Questions

1. In your own words, define friction. In which direction does it act with respect to the motion of an object?
2. A block has a maximum static friction of 40 N. A person pushes it with 15 N. Does the block move? What is the friction force? What is the net force?
3. The same block (maximum static friction 40 N) is pushed with 50 N and starts sliding. The sliding friction is 32 N. Find the net force on the block and state its direction.
4. A box is moving on a floor at a constant speed. The person pushing it applies 18 N. What is the friction force on the box? Explain why.
5. Two children push an almirah in the same direction with 20 N and 25 N. The maximum static friction on the almirah is 60 N. Will the almirah move? What is the friction force? How much more force is needed to start it moving?
6. Arrange static, sliding and rolling friction from the largest to the smallest in everyday cases. Give one everyday example of each.
7. List two situations where friction is useful and two where it is harmful. For each harmful one, name one way to reduce it.
8. True or false (give a reason): (a) A book on a table is pushed gently but does not move, so there is no friction on it. (b) A heavier object, on the same surface, generally has more friction. (c) Ball bearings reduce friction by changing sliding into rolling.

### Answers

1. Friction wo force hai jo do chhoote hue surfaces ke beech sarakne (ya sarakne ki koshish) ka virodh karta hai. Iski disha motion (ya motion ki koshish) ke **ulti** hoti hai.

2. Push = 15 N, maximum static friction = 40 N, aur 15 N < 40 N, to block **nahi** chalega. Friction = push = **15 N**. Net force = 15 N − 15 N = **0 N**.
   **Jaanch:** 15 N ≤ 40 N ✅

3. Push 50 N > 40 N, to block chal padta hai. Net force = 50 N − 32 N = **18 N**, push ki disha mein (aage ki taraf).
   **Jaanch:** 32 N + 18 N = 50 N ✅

4. Constant speed ka matlab net force zero. To friction = push = **18 N**, push ki ulti disha mein. Kyunki net force zero hai, isliye box ki speed na badhti hai na ghati hai (Topic 3).
   **Jaanch:** 18 N − 18 N = 0 N ✅

5. Kul push = 20 N + 25 N = **45 N**. 45 N < 60 N, to almirah **nahi** chalega. Friction = **45 N**. Chalane ke liye maximum 60 N se zyada chahiye, yani 60 N − 45 N = **15 N** aur (isse thoda zyada).
   **Jaanch:** 45 N + 15 N = 60 N ✅

6. Bade se chhote: **static (maximum) ≥ sliding > rolling.** Static: bhaari almirah dhakela par hila nahi. Sliding: almirah zameen par sarak raha hai. Rolling: almirah rollers ya pahiyon par.

7. Faayda: chalna (pair phisalta nahi), brake (gaadi rukti hai). Nuksaan: machine ke purzon ka ghisna, aur garmi se urja ki barbaadi. Ghatane ke tareeke: lubricant (tel/grease) lagana, ball bearings lagana.

8. (a) **False** ❌: book isliye nahi hili kyunki static friction ne push ko balance kar diya. Ruki cheez par push ho to friction push ke barabar hota hai, zero nahi. (Haan, agar book par koi push hi na ho, tab friction ki zaroorat nahi padti.)
   (b) **True** ✅: bhaari cheez zameen par zyada zor se dabti hai, to ubhaar zyada gehre phanste hain, aur friction zyada hota hai.
   (c) **True** ✅: ball bearings sliding ko rolling mein badal dete hain, aur rolling friction sliding se kam hota hai.
