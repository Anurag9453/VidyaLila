# Topic 5: Momentum: Chalti Cheez ki Takat

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki friction chalti cheez ko dheemi karke rok deta hai. Yani **chalti cheez tabhi rukti hai jab us par koi force lage**. Lekin ek aur baat roz dikhti hai: sab chalti cheezein barabar mushkil se nahi rukti. Ek chalti cycle ko haath se rok lena aasan hai, par chalta hua truck rokna naamumkin. Ek patli si tez ball catch ho jaati hai, par ek dheemi-si lekin bhaari almari ko rokna mushkil. To rukna kitna mushkil hai, ye **do cheezon** par nirbhar karta hai: cheez ka **mass** aur uski **speed**. In dono ko ek hi number mein jodne wali cheez ka naam hai **momentum**.

---

## Kabaddi practice mein Kavish ka imtihaan

Shaam ka kabaddi practice chal raha tha. Coach Mr. Rawat ne young raider Kavish ko bulaya aur bole, "Kavish, aaj ek experiment karte hain. Teen defenders ek-ek karke tumhari taraf aayenge. Tumhe unhe rokna hai."

**Pehla defender:** halka-phulka, 50 kg ka, tez bhaagta hua, takreeban 3 m/s ki speed se. Kavish ne zor lagaya aur usse aaram se rok liya.

**Doosra defender:** bhaari-bharkam, 120 kg ka, bas dheere-dheere chalta hua, takreeban 1.5 m/s ki speed se. Itna dheema ki Kavish ne socha, "Ye to aasan hai." Par jab Kavish ne rokne ki koshish ki, to wo apni jagah se **peeche dhakel diya gaya**. Defender ruka nahi!

**Teesra defender:** wahi 120 kg ka, lekin is baar wo tez bhaaga, 3 m/s ki speed se. Kavish ne haath bhi nahi lagaya, bas side hat gaya.

Mr. Rawat hanste hue bole, "Dekha? Halka par tez wala rukna aasan tha. Bhaari par dheema wala bahut mushkil tha. Aur bhaari par tez wala sabse khatarnaak. Ek defender ko rokna 'mass' se bhi tay nahi hota aur 'speed' se bhi nahi. Dono mil kar tay karte hain. Is 'dono milke' ko number mein kaise likhte hain, aur in teeno defenders ka number kya hoga? Ye hum topic ke beech mein dekhenge."

## Part A: Momentum kya hai aur kaise nikalte hain

Kavish ke teen defenders se ek baat saaf hui: mass badhao to rukna mushkil, speed badhao to bhi rukna mushkil. Isi liye hum dono ko **guna** karte hain:

> **Momentum = mass × velocity**
>
> **p = m × v**

Yahan:
- **p** = momentum
- **m** = mass (kg mein)
- **v** = velocity (m/s mein)

**SI unit:** mass kg mein aur velocity m/s mein, to momentum ka unit hai

> kg × m/s = **kg m/s**

### Momentum ek vector hai

Velocity mein **disha** hoti hai, isliye momentum mein bhi disha hoti hai. **Momentum ki disha wahi hoti hai jo velocity ki.** (Mass kabhi negative nahi hota, to sign velocity se hi aata hai.)

Seedhi line par hum ek disha ko **positive (+)** maan lete hain, jaise daayein, aur ulti disha ko **negative (−)**, jaise baayein. Bilkul waise hi jaise Motion chapter mein velocity ke saath kiya tha.

### Ruki hui cheez ka momentum zero

Agar cheez ruki hui hai, to v = 0, aur

> p = m × 0 = **0 kg m/s**

Chahe truck kitna bhi bhaari ho, agar wo ruka hua hai to uska momentum zero hai. Momentum ke liye **cheez ka chalna** zaroori hai.

### Kya sirf mass ya sirf speed kaafi hai?

Kavish ke teeno defenders ke saath ek aur defender (halka aur dheema, 50 kg ko 1.5 m/s) bhi jodkar chaaron combinations ek table mein rakhte hain:

| Defender | Mass | Speed | Momentum p = m × v |
|---|---|---|---|
| Halka aur dheema | 50 kg | 1.5 m/s | 50 × 1.5 = **75 kg m/s** |
| Halka aur tez (pehla) | 50 kg | 3 m/s | 50 × 3 = **150 kg m/s** |
| Bhaari aur dheema (doosra) | 120 kg | 1.5 m/s | 120 × 1.5 = **180 kg m/s** |
| Bhaari aur tez (teesra) | 120 kg | 3 m/s | 120 × 3 = **360 kg m/s** |

**Jaanch:** 50 kg × 1.5 m/s = 75 kg m/s ✅, 50 kg × 3 m/s = 150 kg m/s ✅, 120 kg × 1.5 m/s = 180 kg m/s ✅, 120 kg × 3 m/s = 360 kg m/s ✅

Ab kahani ka jawab dikhta hai:

- Kavish ne **150 kg m/s** wale pehle defender ko rok liya.
- Doosre defender ka momentum **180 kg m/s** tha, jo pehle se zyada hai (180 > 150), isliye wo nahi ruka.
- Teesre ka momentum **360 kg m/s** tha, jo sabse zyada hai. Wo pehle wale se 360 ÷ 150 = **2.4 guna** zyada, aur sabse halke-dheeme se 360 ÷ 75 = **4.8 guna** zyada tha.

Ek sketch mein dekho ki mass aur speed dono kaise kaam karte hain (lambi arrow = zyada speed, bada box = zyada mass):

```
  Halka, tez:     [ 50 kg ] =======>      p = 150 kg m/s

  Bhaari, dheema: [   120 kg   ] ==>      p = 180 kg m/s

  Bhaari, tez:    [   120 kg   ] =======> p = 360 kg m/s
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Kavish ke teen defenders: box ka size mass dikhata hai, arrow ki lambai speed. Teeno ka momentum alag hai, aur sabse zyada momentum bhaari aur tez wale ka hai.

### Example 1 (momentum nikalna)

Ek truck ka mass 1200 kg hai aur wo 15 m/s se seedha chal raha hai. Uska momentum?

- p = m × v = 1200 kg × 15 m/s = **18000 kg m/s**

**Jaanch:** 18000 kg m/s ÷ 15 m/s = 1200 kg ✅

### Example 2 (do cheezon ki tulna)

Ek cycle aur rider ka mass milake 60 kg hai aur wo 5 m/s se chal rahi hai. Ek scooter aur rider ka mass milake 120 kg hai aur wo 3 m/s se chal raha hai. Kiska momentum zyada hai?

- Cycle: p = 60 kg × 5 m/s = **300 kg m/s**
- Scooter: p = 120 kg × 3 m/s = **360 kg m/s**

Scooter ka momentum zyada hai (360 > 300), chahe cycle zyada speed se chal rahi ho. Scooter ko rokna zyada mushkil hoga.

**Jaanch:** 60 kg × 5 m/s = 300 kg m/s ✅, 120 kg × 3 m/s = 360 kg m/s ✅

### Example 3 (p aur m se velocity nikalna)

Ek 1500 kg ki gaadi ka momentum 3000 kg m/s hai. Uski velocity?

Formula ko ulta karo: **v = p ÷ m**

- v = 3000 kg m/s ÷ 1500 kg = **2 m/s**

**Jaanch:** 1500 kg × 2 m/s = 3000 kg m/s ✅

### Example 4 (halka bullet aur bhaari truck)

Ek bullet ka mass 0.01 kg hai aur wo 400 m/s se ja rahi hai. Ek truck ka mass 5000 kg hai aur wo sirf 5 m/s se (takreeban 18 km/h) ja raha hai. Kiska momentum zyada hai?

- Bullet: p = 0.01 kg × 400 m/s = **4 kg m/s**
- Truck: p = 5000 kg × 5 m/s = **25000 kg m/s**

Truck ka momentum bullet se 25000 ÷ 4 = **6250 guna** zyada hai.

**Jaanch:** 0.01 kg × 400 m/s = 4 kg m/s ✅, 5000 kg × 5 m/s = 25000 kg m/s ✅, 4 kg m/s × 6250 = 25000 kg m/s ✅

Dhyan do: bullet ki speed truck se 400 ÷ 5 = 80 guna zyada hai, par truck ka mass bullet se 5000 ÷ 0.01 = 5,00,000 guna zyada hai. **Mass ka asar yahan speed se kahin bada nikla.** Isliye zaroori nahi ki zyada speed matlab zyada momentum.

## Part B: Disha aur sign (+ aur −)

Seedhi line par ek disha chuno. Hum **daayein = +** lenge.

| Cheez | Mass | Velocity | Momentum |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ball daayein jaa rahi hai | 0.2 kg | +10 m/s | 0.2 × (+10) = **+2 kg m/s** |
| Ball baayein jaa rahi hai | 0.2 kg | −10 m/s | 0.2 × (−10) = **−2 kg m/s** |
| Ball ruki hui | 0.2 kg | 0 m/s | 0.2 × 0 = **0 kg m/s** |

Dono balls ka momentum **barabar maan (magnitude)** ka hai (2 kg m/s), par disha ulti hai, isliye sign ulte hain. Sign **sirf disha** dikhata hai, ye nahi ki momentum chhota ya bada hai. −5 kg m/s, +3 kg m/s se **bada maan** rakhta hai (5 > 3), bas ulti disha mein.

### Momentum ka badlav: m(v − u)

Jab cheez ki velocity badalti hai, to uska momentum bhi badalta hai. Acceleration ki tarah yahan bhi **aakhri minus shuruaati**:

> **Momentum mein badlav = aakhri momentum − shuruaati momentum = mv − mu = m(v − u)**

Yahan u = shuruaati velocity, v = aakhri velocity (Motion chapter wale hi u aur v). Ye bilkul acceleration wale (v − u) jaisa hai, bas ab mass se guna hua.

### Example 5 (gaadi tez hui)

Ek car ka mass 1000 kg hai. Wo seedhi sadak par 10 m/s se 20 m/s ho jaati hai. Momentum ka badlav?

- Shuruaati momentum = 1000 kg × 10 m/s = 10000 kg m/s
- Aakhri momentum = 1000 kg × 20 m/s = 20000 kg m/s
- Badlav = 20000 kg m/s − 10000 kg m/s = **+10000 kg m/s**

Shortcut se: m(v − u) = 1000 kg × (20 m/s − 10 m/s) = 1000 kg × 10 m/s = **10000 kg m/s**

**Jaanch:** 10000 kg m/s + 10000 kg m/s = 20000 kg m/s ✅

### Example 6 (gaadi ruk gayi)

Ek car ka mass 800 kg hai aur wo 15 m/s se chal rahi hai. Brake lagakar wo ruk jaati hai. Momentum ka badlav? (car ki disha positive)

- Shuruaati momentum = 800 kg × 15 m/s = +12000 kg m/s
- Aakhri momentum = 800 kg × 0 m/s = 0 kg m/s
- Badlav = 0 kg m/s − 12000 kg m/s = **−12000 kg m/s**

Negative isliye ki momentum **ghata** (car ki disha mein tha, ab zero ho gaya).

**Jaanch:** 12000 kg m/s + (−12000 kg m/s) = 0 kg m/s ✅

### Ball diwar se takrakar wapas aaye

Ye sabse dilchasp case hai. Maan lo ek ball daayein jaakar diwar se takrati hai aur **utni hi speed se** baayein laut aati hai. Speed wahi hai, par **disha ulti**. To momentum ka badlav zero nahi, balki **do guna** hota hai.

```
        daayein (+)  ---->

   [ball] --------> |  diwar

   takrane se pehle:   u = +4 m/s    p = +2 kg m/s

   [ball] <-------- |  diwar

   takrane ke baad:    v = −4 m/s    p = −2 kg m/s
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Ball diwar se takrakar wapas aati hai. Speed same (4 m/s) par disha ulti, isliye velocity +4 se −4 m/s ho gayi.

### Example 7 (ball ka bounce)

Ek ball ka mass 0.5 kg hai. Wo diwar ki taraf (daayein, +) 4 m/s se aati hai aur utni hi speed se baayein laut aati hai. Momentum ka badlav?

- u = +4 m/s, v = −4 m/s
- Shuruaati momentum = 0.5 kg × (+4 m/s) = +2 kg m/s
- Aakhri momentum = 0.5 kg × (−4 m/s) = −2 kg m/s
- Badlav = −2 kg m/s − (+2 kg m/s) = **−4 kg m/s**

Yani badlav ka **maan 4 kg m/s** hai (= 2mv = 2 × 0.5 × 4), aur sign negative isliye ki badlav baayein ki taraf hai.

**Jaanch:** shuruaat +2 kg m/s, aur badlav −4 kg m/s, to aakhir mein +2 kg m/s + (−4 kg m/s) = −2 kg m/s ✅

Agar ball diwar se chipak kar ruk jaati to badlav sirf 2 kg m/s hota. Wapas uchhalne par **2 guna** (4 kg m/s) hota hai.

### Example 8 (bat se takra kar ball ulti disha mein)

Ek 0.2 kg ki ball baayein 10 m/s se aa rahi hai (baayein = −). Batsman use bat se maarta hai aur wo daayein 15 m/s se jaati hai. Momentum ka badlav?

- u = −10 m/s, v = +15 m/s
- Shuruaati momentum = 0.2 kg × (−10 m/s) = −2 kg m/s
- Aakhri momentum = 0.2 kg × (+15 m/s) = +3 kg m/s
- Badlav = +3 kg m/s − (−2 kg m/s) = **+5 kg m/s**

**Jaanch:** −2 kg m/s + 5 kg m/s = +3 kg m/s ✅

Dhyan do: disha ulti hone par badlav dono ka **jod** hota hai (3 + 2 = 5), farak nahi.

## Part C: Zyada momentum, zyada mushkil rukna

Kavish ke 360 kg m/s wale defender ko rokna isliye mushkil tha ki uska momentum bahut zyada tha. Momentum ko **ghatana** (zero karna) hi rokna hai. Kisi cheez ka momentum jitna zyada hoga, utna hi usse rokne ke liye ya to **zyada force** lagana padega, ya **zyada der tak** force lagani padegi.

Isko ek simple soch se samjho: ek hi 4 kg m/s ka momentum aap kisi ball se **bahut kam time mein** hata do, to bahut zor ka jhatka lagega. Wahi momentum **thoda lamba time** lekar hatao, to jhatka narm lagega. Momentum ka badlav dono mein barabar hai, par time alag hai. (Force aur momentum ke beech ka poora rishta hum agle topic mein dekhenge. Is cheez ka ek naam bhi hai, par wo aage ki class mein aayega.)

Is baat ko jaan lene se hum kai cheezein samajh sakte hain.

### 1. Tez cricket ball catch karna: haath peeche kheenchna

Cricket ball (mass 0.16 kg) 25 m/s se aa rahi hai. Uska momentum = 0.16 kg × 25 m/s = **4 kg m/s** (jaanch: 0.16 × 25 = 4 ✅). Catch karke ruk jaane par ye 4 kg m/s zero hona hai.

Agar fielder haath **akad kar** rakhe, to ball bahut kam time (maan lo 0.01 s) mein ruk jaati hai: haath mein zor ka jhatka lagta hai aur chot lag sakti hai. Agar fielder haath **peeche kheenchte hue** catch kare, to wahi ball zyada time (maan lo 0.2 s) mein rukti hai, yani 0.2 ÷ 0.01 = **20 guna** zyada time. Momentum ka badlav **wahi 4 kg m/s** hai, par jhatka bahut narm ho jaata hai.

```
  Akad kar catch:     ball ===> [haath]        time bahut kam, jhatka bada

  Peeche kheench kar:  ball ===> [haath] --> -->   time zyada, jhatka narm
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Haath peeche kheenchne se ball ko rukne mein zyada time milta hai. Momentum ka badlav wahi rehta hai, par jhatka kam hota hai.

### 2. High jumper ka sand ya foam bed par girna

High jumper jab upar se neeche aata hai, to zameen par pahunchte waqt uska momentum bahut hota hai, jo zero karna hai. Agar wo **sakht zameen** par girta, to bahut kam time mein ruk jata aur gambhir chot lagti. **Foam ya sand ka bed** narm hai: usme dhansne se rukne mein zyada time lagta hai. Momentum ka badlav wahi, par jhatka kam.

### 3. Car ka airbag aur crumple zone

Takkar mein car ke andar baithe insaan ka momentum zero karna padta hai. Agar insaan seedha **steering ya dashboard** se takrata, to rukne mein bahut kam time lagta. **Airbag** phool kar narm gaddi ban jaata hai: insaan thoda zyada time mein rukta hai, jhatka kam. Car ka aage ka hissa bhi **crumple zone** hota hai, jo jaan-boojh kar dab jaane ke liye banaya jaata hai: dabne mein time lagta hai, to takkar ka jhatka insaan tak narm pahunchta hai.

### 4. Bhaari truck ke brake

Ek bhaari truck ka mass bahut zyada hota hai, to chalte waqt uska momentum bhi bahut zyada hota hai. Usse rokne ke liye brake ko **bahut zyada force** lagani padti hai, ya **bahut zyada time** (aur doori) chahiye. Isliye truck ko rokne ke liye door se brake lagana padta hai, aur pahaadi raston par trucks ke liye "emergency escape ramp" (ret ki dhalaan) banayi jaati hai jahan wo dheere-dheere ruk sakein. Isi liye truck ke saamne achanak aana khatarnaak hai.

## Part D: Kavish ke sawaal ka poora jawab

Ab Mr. Rawat ke sawaal par wapas aate hain.

**Example 9 (kahani ke teen defenders)**

- Pehla defender: p = 50 kg × 3 m/s = **150 kg m/s**
- Doosra defender: p = 120 kg × 1.5 m/s = **180 kg m/s**
- Teesra defender: p = 120 kg × 3 m/s = **360 kg m/s**

Kavish 150 kg m/s wale defender ko rok paaya, par 180 kg m/s ya usse zyada momentum wale ko nahi. Isliye kahani mein momentum ka kram: **150 < 180 < 360**, jo bilkul waise hai jaise Kavish ne mehsoos kiya.

**Jaanch:** 50 kg × 3 m/s = 150 kg m/s ✅, 120 kg × 1.5 m/s = 180 kg m/s ✅, 120 kg × 3 m/s = 360 kg m/s ✅. Aur 360 ÷ 180 = 2: teesra defender doosre se **2 guna** momentum rakhta hai, kyunki mass wahi hai aur speed 2 guna hai ✅

**Example 10 (tez par halka, bhaari par dheema, number badal do)**

Maan lo ab ek 40 kg ka defender 4 m/s se aur ek 70 kg ka defender 2 m/s se aaye. Kaun zyada momentum wala?

- 40 kg wala: p = 40 kg × 4 m/s = **160 kg m/s**
- 70 kg wala: p = 70 kg × 2 m/s = **140 kg m/s**

Is baar **halka par tez** wale ka momentum zyada hai (160 > 140). Isliye "bhaari matlab hamesha zyada momentum" kehna galat hai. Dono ko guna karke dekhna padta hai.

**Jaanch:** 40 kg × 4 m/s = 160 kg m/s ✅, 70 kg × 2 m/s = 140 kg m/s ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Mass ki jagah weight use karna**

Momentum mein **mass (kg)** aata hai, **weight** nahi. Weight ek force hai (newton mein), mass nahi. Agar koi kahe "ek insaan ka mass 60 kg hai aur wo 2 m/s se chal raha hai," to p = 60 × 2 = **120 kg m/s**. Weight ke newton wale number ko seedha 2 se guna karna galat hoga, kyunki us se kg m/s nahi, kuch aur unit aayega.

**Galti 2: Disha ya sign bhool jaana**

Baayein jaati 0.2 kg ki ball (10 m/s) ka momentum **−2 kg m/s** hai, +2 nahi. Sign bhool gaye to bounce ka badlav galat aayega.

**Galti 3: kg m/s ko N samajhna**

kg m/s **momentum** ka unit hai. **N (newton)** force ka unit hai. Dono alag cheezein hain. Example 1 ka jawab 18000 kg m/s hai, 18000 N nahi.

**Galti 4: Sochna ki bhaari hamesha zyada momentum**

Example 10 dekho: 70 kg wale ka momentum 140 kg m/s, aur 40 kg wale ka 160 kg m/s tha. Bahut dheeme chalte bhaari insaan ka momentum, tez bhaagte halke insaan se kam bhi ho sakta hai. Mass aur velocity **dono** dekho.

**Galti 5: Bounce mein momentum ka badlav zero samajhna**

Ball ki speed takrane ke pehle aur baad barabar hai, to kuch log kehte hain "badlav zero". Galat. Disha ulti hai, to momentum +2 se −2 kg m/s ho gaya. Badlav = −2 − 2 = **−4 kg m/s**, yani **2mv**, zero nahi.

**Galti 6: u aur v ulta karna**

Badlav = **m(v − u)**, yaani aakhri minus shuruaati. Example 6 mein 12000 − 0 likh dena galat hai: sahi 0 − 12000 = −12000 kg m/s.

## Quick Recap

- **Momentum p = m × v**: mass aur velocity ka guna. Ye batata hai ki chalti cheez ko rokna kitna mushkil hai.
- **SI unit: kg m/s.** Ye N nahi hai.
- Momentum ek **vector** hai: uski disha velocity ki disha hoti hai. Seedhi line par ek disha +, ulti disha −.
- **Ruki cheez ka momentum zero** hota hai (v = 0).
- Zyada momentum ka matlab hai zyada mass **ya** zyada velocity **ya** dono. Sirf bhaari ya sirf tez hona kaafi nahi: dono dekhne padte hain.
- **Momentum mein badlav = m(v − u)** = aakhri momentum − shuruaati momentum.
- Ball diwar se **same speed** par wapas aaye to badlav **2mv** hota hai, zero nahi.
- Zyada momentum rokne ke liye **zyada force** ya **zyada time** chahiye. Isliye catch karte waqt haath peeche kheenchte hain, high jumper bed par girta hai, car mein airbag aur crumple zone hote hain, aur truck ko rokna mushkil hota hai.
- **Kahani ka jawab:** 50 kg × 3 m/s = 150 kg m/s, 120 kg × 1.5 m/s = 180 kg m/s, 120 kg × 3 m/s = 360 kg m/s.

## Practice Questions

1. A truck of mass 2000 kg moves at 12 m/s. Find its momentum.

2. A cricket ball of mass 0.15 kg moves at 30 m/s. Find its momentum.

3. The momentum of a runner is 90 kg m/s and the runner's mass is 45 kg. Find the velocity.

4. A bike of mass 200 kg increases its speed from 5 m/s to 15 m/s along a straight road. Find the change in its momentum.

5. A ball of mass 0.4 kg hits a wall at 5 m/s and bounces back with the same speed. Take the direction towards the wall as positive. Find the initial momentum, the final momentum and the change in momentum.

6. A runner of mass 60 kg runs at 8 m/s. A car of mass 1500 kg creeps forward at 0.5 m/s. Which has the greater momentum? Show the working.

7. A car of mass 900 kg moving at 20 m/s is brought to rest by brakes. Find the change in its momentum. Is it positive or negative if the car's direction is taken as positive? Why?

8. State True or False:
   (a) The momentum of a body at rest is zero.
   (b) The SI unit of momentum is N.
   (c) A heavier body always has more momentum than a lighter one.
   (d) A ball moving to the left has negative momentum if right is taken as positive.

9. Explain in your own words why a fielder pulls his hands back while catching a fast cricket ball. Why does an airbag help in a car crash?

10. At practice, Kavish has to stop one defender of mass 80 kg moving at 2.5 m/s, or another of mass 55 kg moving at 4 m/s. Which defender is harder to stop, and why?

### Answers

1. p = m × v = 2000 kg × 12 m/s = **24000 kg m/s**.
   Jaanch: 24000 kg m/s ÷ 12 m/s = 2000 kg ✅

2. p = 0.15 kg × 30 m/s = **4.5 kg m/s**.
   Jaanch: 4.5 kg m/s ÷ 30 m/s = 0.15 kg ✅

3. v = p ÷ m = 90 kg m/s ÷ 45 kg = **2 m/s**.
   Jaanch: 45 kg × 2 m/s = 90 kg m/s ✅

4. Shuruaati p = 200 kg × 5 m/s = 1000 kg m/s, aakhri p = 200 kg × 15 m/s = 3000 kg m/s. Badlav = 3000 kg m/s − 1000 kg m/s = **2000 kg m/s** (ya m(v − u) = 200 kg × 10 m/s = 2000 kg m/s).
   Jaanch: 1000 kg m/s + 2000 kg m/s = 3000 kg m/s ✅

5. u = +5 m/s, v = −5 m/s. Shuruaati p = 0.4 kg × 5 m/s = **+2 kg m/s**. Aakhri p = 0.4 kg × (−5 m/s) = **−2 kg m/s**. Badlav = −2 kg m/s − 2 kg m/s = **−4 kg m/s** (maan 4 kg m/s = 2mv = 2 × 0.4 kg × 5 m/s).
   Jaanch: 2 kg m/s + (−4 kg m/s) = −2 kg m/s ✅

6. Runner: 60 kg × 8 m/s = **480 kg m/s**. Car: 1500 kg × 0.5 m/s = **750 kg m/s**. **Car** ka momentum zyada hai (750 > 480), kyunki mass bahut zyada hai, chahe wo dheemi hai.
   Jaanch: 60 kg × 8 m/s = 480 kg m/s ✅, 1500 kg × 0.5 m/s = 750 kg m/s ✅

7. Shuruaati p = 900 kg × 20 m/s = 18000 kg m/s, aakhri p = 0 kg m/s. Badlav = 0 kg m/s − 18000 kg m/s = **−18000 kg m/s**. Negative hai kyunki momentum car ki (positive) disha mein ghata hai.
   Jaanch: 18000 kg m/s + (−18000 kg m/s) = 0 kg m/s ✅

8. (a) **True** ✅. v = 0 to p = m × 0 = 0.
   (b) **False** ❌. SI unit kg m/s hai; N force ka unit hai.
   (c) **False** ❌. Dheema bhaari wala, tez halke wale se kam momentum ka ho sakta hai (jaise Example 10 mein).
   (d) **True** ✅. Baayein ki velocity negative, to momentum bhi negative.

9. Catch mein ball ka momentum zero karna hai. Haath peeche kheenchne se ball ko rukne mein **zyada time** milta hai, to jhatka narm hota hai (momentum ka badlav wahi rehta hai). Airbag bhi insaan ko rukne mein zyada time deta hai, aur narm gaddi ki tarah kaam karta hai, isliye chot kam hoti hai.

10. 80 kg wala: p = 80 kg × 2.5 m/s = **200 kg m/s**. 55 kg wala: p = 55 kg × 4 m/s = **220 kg m/s**. **55 kg wala** rokna zyada mushkil hai, kyunki uska momentum zyada hai (220 > 200), chahe wo halka hai. Tez speed ne mass ki kami poori kar di.
    Jaanch: 80 kg × 2.5 m/s = 200 kg m/s ✅, 55 kg × 4 m/s = 220 kg m/s ✅
