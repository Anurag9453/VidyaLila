# Topic 6: Newton ka Doosra Law: F = ma

Pichhle topic mein humne momentum **p = m v** padha, aur ek dilchasp baat dekhi thi: jis cheez ka momentum zyada ho, use rokne ya uska momentum badalne ke liye **ya to bada force chahiye, ya zyada time**. Wo baat abhi sirf "feel" thi. Is topic mein hum use **ek seedhe formula** mein badal denge, jisse bata sakte hain ki kitna force lagane par kisi cheez ki velocity kitni tezi se badlegi. Isi formula ko **Newton ka Doosra Law** kehte hain.

---

## Ms. Lobo, Arhaan aur supermarket ki trolley

Shaam ka time tha. Supermarket mein Ms. Lobo, store assistant, trolleys ki line theek kar rahi thi. Chhota Arhaan apni mummy ke saath aaya tha, par mummy abhi counter par thi aur Arhaan trolley ke paas khada bore ho raha tha.

"Aunty, main ek trolley chala sakta hoon?" usne poocha.

Ms. Lobo muskuraayi. "Ye khaali wali le lo. Dheere se dhakka dena."

Arhaan ne halka sa dhakka diya aur khaali trolley **fatafat tez ho gayi**, aise ki use bhaag kar pakadna pada. Wo hans pada.

Phir Ms. Lobo ne ek trolley ki taraf ishaara kiya, jo atta, chawal, tel aur doodh ke dabbon se bhari thi. "Ab ye wali dhakelo."

Arhaan ne wahi halka dhakka diya. Trolley bas **thodi si khisak** payi. Usne zor lagaya, to wo dheere-dheere chali. "Ye to bahut bhaari hai!"

"Ek aur kaam karo," Ms. Lobo boli. "Khaali trolley ko ab **zor se** dhakelo." Arhaan ne poori taakat se dhakka diya, aur trolley itni tezi se bhaagi ki Ms. Lobo ko haath se rokna pada.

Arhaan ne sir kharoncha. "Matlab dhakka zyada to trolley zyada tez badhti hai, aur trolley bhaari ho to kam tez badhti hai?"

"Bilkul," Ms. Lobo boli. "Aur is baat ko ek bahut **sidhe formula** mein likha ja sakta hai. Do cheezein dekho: tum kitna force lagate ho, aur trolley mein kitna maal (mass) hai. Dono milkar bata dete hain ki trolley ki speed kitni tezi se badhegi." Chalo wo formula banate hain, aur is trolley ko numbers ke saath bhi dekhenge.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Net force (F_net)** | Saare forces ko jodne-ghatane ke baad bacha hua force; wahi acceleration banata hai | 50 N aage, 30 N peeche: net 20 N aage |
| **Newton (N)** | Force ki SI unit: 1 N = 1 kg m/s² | 5 kg ko 2 m/s² dene ke liye 10 N |
| **Acceleration (a)** | Velocity badalne ki dar (Motion chapter); a = (v − u) ÷ t | 2 m/s² |
| **Mass (m)** | Cheez mein kitna maal hai; kg mein napte hain | Khaali trolley: 10 kg |
| **Retardation** | Jab acceleration velocity ki ulti direction mein ho, to a negative likhte hain | −5 m/s² |
| **Momentum mein badlaav** | m (v − u): final minus initial momentum | 0.16 × (0 − 25) kg m/s |

## Part A: Law ka statement

> **Newton ka Doosra Law:** Kisi object ke momentum ke badalne ki **dar (rate of change of momentum)**, us par lage **unbalanced force** ke **seedhe anupaat (directly proportional)** mein hoti hai, aur ye badlaav **force ki direction mein** hota hai.

Chhote shabdon mein: jitna **bada unbalanced force**, utni **tezi se momentum badlega**, aur momentum **usi taraf** badlega jidhar force lag raha hai.

"Directly proportional" ka matlab: force doguna karo to momentum badalne ki dar bhi doguni. Force aadha karo to dar bhi aadhi.

---

## Part B: Formula kaise banta hai

Maan lo m mass ki cheez ki velocity u se badal kar v ho gayi, aur ye badlaav t second mein hua. Pichhle topic ke hisaab se:

- Shuru ka momentum = m u
- End ka momentum = m v
- Momentum mein badlaav = m v − m u = m (v − u)
- Badalne ki dar (rate) = badlaav ÷ time = m (v − u) ÷ t

Law kehta hai ki force is dar ke **seedhe anupaat** mein hai:

**F ∝ m (v − u) ÷ t**

Ab Motion chapter se yaad karein: **a = (v − u) ÷ t** (acceleration ki definition). To dar wali baat ko likh sakte hain:

**F ∝ m × a**

Anupaat (∝) ko barabar (=) banane ke liye ek constant k chahiye:

**F = k m a**

Ab ek chaal: force ki **unit hum aise chunte hain** ki k = 1 ho jaaye. Yaani hum kehte hain ki jo force 1 kg ko 1 m/s² ki acceleration de, use hum **1 unit** (1 newton) kahenge. Tab 1 N = k × 1 kg × 1 m/s², yaani k = 1, aur formula bahut saaf ho jaata hai:

### **F = m a**

Yahan **F = net (unbalanced) force**, **m = mass**, **a = acceleration**.

### Newton (N)

**1 newton (1 N)** wo force hai jo **1 kg** ke mass ko **1 m/s²** ki acceleration de.

**1 N = 1 kg × 1 m/s² = 1 kg m/s²**

Sirf ek "feel" ke liye: 1 N lagbhag utna hota hai jitna force **100 g** ki cheez (jaise ek chhota phal ya chocolate ki badi bar) ko haath par rakhne par neeche ki taraf lagta hai. (Cheez ka ye neeche ka force uska **weight** kehlata hai. Weight ek force hai aur W = m g likha jaata hai, par ye **Gravitation chapter** mein detail se aayega; yahan sirf naam le rahe hain.)

---

## Part C: Teen roop, ek formula

F = m a ko teen tarah se padh sakte hain:

| Kya nikalna hai | Formula | Kab use karein |
|---|---|---|
| Force | **F = m a** | m aur a pata hon |
| Acceleration | **a = F ÷ m** | F aur m pata hon |
| Mass | **m = F ÷ a** | F aur a pata hon |

```
        F = m x a
       /    |    \
      /     |     \
   F = m a  a = F/m  m = F/a
```

> **Figure 6.1** – F = m a se nikalne wale teen roop. Jo cheez nikalni ho use "dhak" lo, baaki do se hisaab ho jaata hai.

### a = F ÷ m ka matlab (trolley wali baat)

- **Mass wahi, force zyada** to acceleration zyada. (Arhaan ne khaali trolley ko zor se dhakela.)
- **Force wahi, mass zyada** to acceleration kam. (Bhari trolley ko halke dhakke se hilana mushkil.)

Direction: **acceleration hamesha net force ki direction mein hoti hai.** Agar tum trolley ko aage dhakelo to wo aage ki taraf tez hoti hai. Agar peeche se rokne ka force lagao to acceleration peeche ki taraf (yaani retardation).

## Part D: Trolley ko numbers se samjhein

Maan lo khaali trolley ka mass **10 kg** hai aur groceries se bhari trolley ka mass **40 kg**. Pehle dono ko **20 N** ke force se dhakelte hain (friction ko abhi chhod dete hain).

- Khaali: a = F ÷ m = 20 N ÷ 10 kg = **2 m/s²**
- Bhari: a = F ÷ m = 20 N ÷ 40 kg = **0.5 m/s²**

Ab force badal kar dekhte hain:

| Trolley | Mass (kg) | Force (N) | a = F ÷ m (m/s²) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Khaali | 10 | 20 | 2 |
| Khaali | 10 | 40 | 4 |
| Bhari | 40 | 20 | 0.5 |
| Bhari | 40 | 40 | 1 |

Table se padhein:

- **Upar ki do line** (mass 10 kg wahi): force 20 N se 40 N (doguna) hua, to a 2 se 4 m/s² (doguna).
- **Neeche ki do line** (mass 40 kg wahi): force doguna to a bhi doguna (0.5 se 1).
- **Pehli aur teesri line** (force 20 N wahi): mass 10 kg se 40 kg (chaar guna) hua, to a chaar guna **kam** (2 se 0.5).

Yahi Arhaan ne mehsoos kiya tha, ab numbers ke saath.

## Part E: F net force hai: friction ke saath

F = m a mein F wo force hai jo **bacha hua (net)** hai, har cheez jodne-ghatane ke baad. Jab friction (Topic 4) bhi kaam kar raha ho to:

**F_net = lagaya hua force − friction** (jab friction gati ke ulta ho)

Aur phir **a = F_net ÷ m**.

```
   friction (30 N)               lagaya hua force (50 N)
   <------------------  [ BOX 20 kg ]  ------------------>

   Net force = 50 N - 30 N = 20 N (aage ki taraf)
```

> **Figure 6.2** – 20 kg ka box 50 N se aage dhakela ja raha hai, friction 30 N peeche ki taraf. Net force 20 N aage.

Real trolley ke saath bhi aisa hi hota hai: Arhaan jo force lagata hai, uska kuch hissa pahiyon ke friction mein "kharch" ho jaata hai. Trolley ko sirf bacha hua hissa tez karta hai.

### Pehle law ka special case

Agar **F_net = 0** ho to F = m a se **a = 0 ÷ m = 0**. Yaani velocity badalti hi nahi: cheez ya to rest mein rahegi, ya same speed se seedhi line mein chalti rahegi. Ye bilkul **Newton ka Pehla Law** hai (Topic 3). Pehla law, doosre law ka ek special case hai.

## Part F: Solved examples

### Example 1: F nikalna (m aur a se)

Ek 5 kg ke dabbe ko 3 m/s² ki acceleration chahiye. Net force kitna chahiye?

Diya hai: m = 5 kg, a = 3 m/s².

F = m a = 5 kg × 3 m/s² = **15 N**

Jaanch: a = F ÷ m = 15 N ÷ 5 kg = 3 m/s² ✅

### Example 2: a nikalna (F aur m se)

12 kg ki ek cheez par 60 N ka net force lagta hai. Acceleration?

a = F ÷ m = 60 N ÷ 12 kg = **5 m/s²**

Jaanch: F = m a = 12 kg × 5 m/s² = 60 N ✅

### Example 3: m nikalna (F aur a se)

Ek cheez par 45 N ka net force lagta hai aur wo 1.5 m/s² se tez hoti hai. Uska mass?

m = F ÷ a = 45 N ÷ 1.5 m/s² = **30 kg**

Jaanch: F = m a = 30 kg × 1.5 m/s² = 45 N ✅

### Example 4: Units ka dhyan (gram ko kg mein)

Ek 500 g ke dabbe par 10 N ka net force lagta hai. Acceleration?

**Pehle units!** 500 g = 500 ÷ 1000 kg = 0.5 kg.

a = F ÷ m = 10 N ÷ 0.5 kg = **20 m/s²**

Jaanch: F = m a = 0.5 kg × 20 m/s² = 10 N ✅

(Agar 500 seedha daal dete: 10 ÷ 500 = 0.02, jo galat hota.)

### Example 5: Friction ke saath net force

Ek 20 kg ke box ko 50 N ke force se dhakela ja raha hai. Floor ka friction 30 N hai (peeche ki taraf). Acceleration?

Diya hai: m = 20 kg, lagaya hua force = 50 N, friction = 30 N.

F_net = 50 N − 30 N = **20 N** (aage)
a = F_net ÷ m = 20 N ÷ 20 kg = **1 m/s²** (aage)

Jaanch: F_net = m a = 20 kg × 1 m/s² = 20 N, aur 50 N − 30 N = 20 N ✅

(Agar friction bhula kar 50 N daalte: a = 50 N ÷ 20 kg = 2.5 m/s². Galat, kyunki acceleration net force se banti hai.)

### Example 6: Equations of motion ke saath

Ek car ka mass 1000 kg hai. Rest se shuru hokar us par 3000 N ka net force 4 s tak lagta hai. Acceleration, final velocity aur tay ki hui doori?

Diya hai: m = 1000 kg, F = 3000 N, u = 0, t = 4 s.

a = F ÷ m = 3000 N ÷ 1000 kg = **3 m/s²**
v = u + a t = 0 + 3 m/s² × 4 s = **12 m/s**
s = u t + ½ a t² = 0 × 4 s + ½ × 3 m/s² × (4 s)² = ½ × 3 × 16 m = **24 m**

Jaanch: v² = u² + 2 a s = 0 + 2 × 3 m/s² × 24 m = 144 m²/s², aur v² = (12 m/s)² = 144 m²/s² ✅

### Example 7: Braking car

Ek 1200 kg ki car 16 m/s se chal rahi hai aur brake lagakar 4 s mein ruk jaati hai. Brake ka force kitna hai?

Diya hai: m = 1200 kg, u = 16 m/s, v = 0, t = 4 s. Shuru ki direction **positive**.

a = (v − u) ÷ t = (0 − 16 m/s) ÷ 4 s = **−4 m/s²**
F = m a = 1200 kg × (−4 m/s²) = **−4800 N**

Negative sign ka matlab: **4800 N ka force car ki gati ke ulti direction mein** hai (rokne wala force).

Jaanch: a = F ÷ m = −4800 N ÷ 1200 kg = −4 m/s² ✅

### Example 8: Ek hi force, do alag masses

Ek hi 12 N ka net force pehle 2 kg ki cheez par, phir 6 kg ki cheez par lagta hai. Dono ki acceleration compare karo.

- 2 kg: a₁ = 12 N ÷ 2 kg = **6 m/s²**
- 6 kg: a₂ = 12 N ÷ 6 kg = **2 m/s²**

Mass 3 guna (2 se 6 kg) hua, to acceleration 3 guni **kam** (6 se 2 m/s²). Jaanch: a₁ ÷ a₂ = 6 ÷ 2 = 3 aur m₂ ÷ m₁ = 6 ÷ 2 = 3 ✅

### Example 9: Arhaan ki trolley

Khaali trolley 10 kg, bhari trolley 40 kg. Arhaan dono ko 20 N se dhakelta hai (friction ko ignore karo). Dono ki acceleration, aur 3 s mein khaali trolley ki speed (rest se)?

- Khaali: a = 20 N ÷ 10 kg = **2 m/s²**
- Bhari: a = 20 N ÷ 40 kg = **0.5 m/s²**

3 s baad khaali trolley: v = u + a t = 0 + 2 m/s² × 3 s = **6 m/s**. Bhari trolley: v = 0 + 0.5 m/s² × 3 s = **1.5 m/s**.

Jaanch: F = m a, khaali: 10 kg × 2 m/s² = 20 N ✅; bhari: 40 kg × 0.5 m/s² = 20 N ✅

Ab Arhaan "zor se" dhakka deta hai, 40 N: khaali ki a = 40 N ÷ 10 kg = **4 m/s²** (doguni), bhari ki a = 40 N ÷ 40 kg = **1 m/s²** (doguni). Table (Part D) se match ✅

### Example 10: Cricket ball rokne ka force (momentum se link)

Pichhle topic wali cricket ball yaad karo: mass 0.16 kg, speed 25 m/s. Fielder haath akad kar catch karta hai aur ball 0.01 s mein ruk jaati hai. Haath par kitna force lagta hai?

Diya hai: m = 0.16 kg, u = 25 m/s, v = 0, t = 0.01 s.

a = (v − u) ÷ t = (0 − 25 m/s) ÷ 0.01 s = **−2500 m/s²** (magnitude 2500 m/s²)
F = m a = 0.16 kg × (−2500 m/s²) = **−400 N** (yaani 400 N ball ki gati ke ulta)

Jaanch (momentum ke roop mein): momentum mein badlaav = m (v − u) = 0.16 kg × (0 − 25 m/s) = −4 kg m/s. F = (−4 kg m/s) ÷ 0.01 s = −400 kg m/s² = −400 N ✅

**Ye pichhle topic ki baat ka hisaab hai:** agar fielder haath peeche kheench kar ball ko **0.2 s** mein roke (zyada time), to a = (0 − 25 m/s) ÷ 0.2 s = −125 m/s² aur F = 0.16 kg × (−125 m/s²) = **−20 N**. Same momentum change, par time 20 guna, to force 20 guna kam. Isi liye achhe fielder haath peeche kheenchte hain.

### Example 11: F = m (v − u) ÷ t seedha use karna

Ek 2 kg ki cheez ki velocity 3 m/s se 11 m/s ho jaati hai 4 s mein. Net force?

**Tareeka 1 (momentum ke roop mein):** F = m (v − u) ÷ t = 2 kg × (11 − 3) m/s ÷ 4 s = 2 × 8 ÷ 4 = **4 N**

**Tareeka 2 (a se):** a = (11 − 3) m/s ÷ 4 s = 2 m/s². F = m a = 2 kg × 2 m/s² = **4 N**

Jaanch: Dono tareeke 4 N dete hain ✅

### Example 12: Friction se rukta hua box

Ek 10 kg ka box floor par 6 m/s se phisalna shuru karta hai. Sirf friction ka 20 N force (ulti direction mein) kaam kar raha hai. Box kitni der mein aur kitni door mein rukega?

Shuru ki direction positive. m = 10 kg, u = 6 m/s, v = 0, F = −20 N.

a = F ÷ m = −20 N ÷ 10 kg = **−2 m/s²**
t = (v − u) ÷ a = (0 − 6 m/s) ÷ (−2 m/s²) = **3 s**
s = u t + ½ a t² = 6 m/s × 3 s + ½ × (−2 m/s²) × (3 s)² = 18 m − 9 m = **9 m**

Jaanch: v² = u² + 2 a s: 0 = (6 m/s)² + 2 × (−2 m/s²) × 9 m = 36 m²/s² − 36 m²/s² = 0 ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Friction hone par lagaya hua force daal dena.** Example 5 mein 50 N daalne se a = 2.5 m/s² aata, jabki sahi net force 20 N aur a = 1 m/s² hai. F = m a mein hamesha **net** force.

2. **Mass aur weight ko ek samajhna.** Mass kg mein hai aur cheez mein maal ki matra hai. Weight ek **force** hai (N mein). 1 kg ka mass aur 1 N ka force alag cheezein hain. (Weight ki baat Gravitation chapter mein.)

3. **Units galat daalna.** 500 g ko seedha 500 daalna, ya jawab ke saath N likhna bhool jaana. Mass hamesha **kg** mein, force **N** mein, a **m/s²** mein.

4. **Retardation ka sign bhoolna.** Example 7 mein a = −4 m/s² aur F = −4800 N hai. Negative ka matlab "gati ke ulta", force ka size 4800 N hi hai. Sign chhod ke "4800 N aage ki taraf" samajhna galat hai.

5. **Sochna ki bhaari cheez hamesha zyada tezi se badhti hai.** Same force par a = F ÷ m, to **bhaari cheez ki acceleration kam** hoti hai (Part D: 0.5 m/s² vs 2 m/s²). Mass zyada hai to a kam.

6. **Rest wali cheez par "velocity zero, to force zero" sochna.** F = m a mein v ki value nahi, **a** (velocity ke badalne ki dar) matter karti hai. Rest se shuru hote hue cheez par bada force ho sakta hai, jaise Example 6 mein u = 0 par F = 3000 N. Force zero tab hota hai jab **a zero** ho, v zero hone se kuch nahi hota.

## Quick Recap

- **Newton ka Doosra Law:** momentum ke badalne ki dar, lage unbalanced force ke seedhe anupaat mein hoti hai, aur force ki direction mein hoti hai.
- Derivation: F ∝ m (v − u) ÷ t = m a, to F = k m a, aur unit aise chunne par k = 1: **F = m a**.
- **1 N = 1 kg m/s²:** wo force jo 1 kg ko 1 m/s² ki acceleration de.
- Teen roop: **F = m a, a = F ÷ m, m = F ÷ a**. Acceleration net force ki direction mein.
- Zyada force to zyada a; zyada mass to kam a (trolley: 2 m/s² vs 0.5 m/s² par 20 N).
- F **net** force hai: friction ke saath F_net = lagaya hua force − friction.
- F_net = 0 to a = 0: Pehla Law doosre law ka special case.
- Mass kg mein, force N mein; weight ek force hai (W = m g, Gravitation chapter mein); 1 N lagbhag 100 g ke weight jitna.
- Same momentum change: zyada time to kam force (cricket ball).

## Practice Questions

1. A force of 30 N acts on a body of mass 6 kg. Find its acceleration.
2. What net force is needed to give a 2.5 kg object an acceleration of 4 m/s²?
3. A net force of 18 N gives an object an acceleration of 3 m/s². Find its mass.
4. A 25 kg box is pushed with 80 N on a floor where friction is 30 N. Find the acceleration.
5. An 800 kg car starts from rest and a net force of 1600 N acts on it for 6 s. Find its final velocity and the distance travelled.
6. A 1500 kg car moving at 30 m/s is stopped in 6 s by the brakes. Find the braking force and state its direction.
7. A 0.2 kg ball moving at 20 m/s is stopped in 0.05 s. Find the force. Then find the force if it is stopped in 0.2 s, and compare.
8. A loaded trolley of mass 50 kg and an empty trolley of mass 10 kg are both pushed with 25 N (ignore friction). Find both accelerations and say which is larger and how many times.
9. A 400 g body is acted on by 2 N. Find the acceleration in m/s². (Be careful with units.)
10. True or false: (a) A body at rest can have a large net force acting on it. (b) A 1 N force is the force that gives 1 g an acceleration of 1 m/s². (c) If the net force on a moving body is zero, its acceleration is zero. (d) A heavier body always has a larger acceleration under the same force.

### Answers

1. a = F ÷ m = 30 N ÷ 6 kg = **5 m/s²**. Jaanch: 6 kg × 5 m/s² = 30 N ✅

2. F = m a = 2.5 kg × 4 m/s² = **10 N**. Jaanch: a = 10 N ÷ 2.5 kg = 4 m/s² ✅

3. m = F ÷ a = 18 N ÷ 3 m/s² = **6 kg**. Jaanch: 6 kg × 3 m/s² = 18 N ✅

4. F_net = 80 N − 30 N = 50 N. a = 50 N ÷ 25 kg = **2 m/s²**. Jaanch: 25 kg × 2 m/s² = 50 N ✅

5. a = 1600 N ÷ 800 kg = 2 m/s². v = 0 + 2 m/s² × 6 s = **12 m/s**. s = ½ × 2 m/s² × (6 s)² = ½ × 2 × 36 m = **36 m**.
   Jaanch: v² = 2 a s = 2 × 2 m/s² × 36 m = 144 m²/s² = (12 m/s)² ✅

6. a = (0 − 30 m/s) ÷ 6 s = −5 m/s². F = 1500 kg × (−5 m/s²) = **−7500 N**, yaani **7500 N car ki gati ke ulti direction mein**. Jaanch: a = −7500 N ÷ 1500 kg = −5 m/s² ✅

7. Pehle case: a = (0 − 20 m/s) ÷ 0.05 s = −400 m/s², F = 0.2 kg × (−400 m/s²) = **−80 N** (80 N ulta). Doosra: a = (0 − 20 m/s) ÷ 0.2 s = −100 m/s², F = 0.2 kg × (−100 m/s²) = **−20 N** (20 N ulta). Time 4 guna to force 4 guna kam.
   Jaanch (momentum se): m (v − u) = 0.2 kg × (−20 m/s) = −4 kg m/s. −4 kg m/s ÷ 0.05 s = −80 N ✅ aur −4 kg m/s ÷ 0.2 s = −20 N ✅

8. Loaded: a = 25 N ÷ 50 kg = **0.5 m/s²**. Empty: a = 25 N ÷ 10 kg = **2.5 m/s²**. Empty trolley ki acceleration 2.5 ÷ 0.5 = **5 guni** zyada, aur mass ka ratio 50 ÷ 10 = 5 ✅

9. 400 g = 0.4 kg. a = 2 N ÷ 0.4 kg = **5 m/s²**. Jaanch: 0.4 kg × 5 m/s² = 2 N ✅

10. (a) **True** ✅. Rest par bhi net force ho sakta hai, tab wo cheez tez hona shuru karti hai (ek dum rest se). (b) **False** ❌. 1 N wo force hai jo **1 kg** ko 1 m/s² de, 1 g ko nahi. (c) **True** ✅. F = 0 to a = 0 (Pehla Law). (d) **False** ❌. Same force par a = F ÷ m, to heavier body ki acceleration **kam** hoti hai.
