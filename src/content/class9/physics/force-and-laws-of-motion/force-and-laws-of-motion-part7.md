# Topic 7: Newton ka Teesra Law: Action aur Reaction

Pichhle topic mein humne F = m a seekha: kisi **ek cheez** par net force lage to uski acceleration kitni hogi. Wahan hum sirf ek cheez ko dekh rahe the aur puchh rahe the, "is par kitna force laga?" Ab ek naya sawal: **ye force aaya kahan se, aur kaun kisko dhakel raha hai?** Jab aap kisi cheez ko dhakelte ho, to kya wo cheez aapko bhi kuch karti hai? Is sawal ka jawab hai Newton ka teesra law.

---

## Swimming pool mein Myra ka pehla din

Myra pehli baar swimming pool aayi thi. Coach Mr. D'Cruz ne use pool ke kinare khada kiya aur kaha, "Myra, aaj aage badhna seekhna hai."

Myra ne socha ki paani mein "aage" jaane ke liye paani ko aage dhakelna padega. Usne hathon se paani ko **aage** dhakela, par wo to bas wahin hilti rahi. Mr. D'Cruz hanse, "Ulta kar rahi ho! Aage jaana hai to paani ko **peeche** dhakelo. Haath aur pair, dono se."

Myra ne is baar hathon se paani ko peeche dhakela, aur wo aage sarak gayi. "Arre, paani ne mujhe aage dhakel diya!" wo chillayi.

Phir Mr. D'Cruz ne use pool mein tairte ek chhote platform par chadhaya. Myra platform se **aage ki taraf kudi**, aur kudte hi usne dekha ki platform **peeche ki taraf** khisak gaya, jaise usne use laat maar di ho.

Myra ne puchha, "Sir, platform ko to maine peeche dhakelne ka socha bhi nahi tha. Wo peeche kyun gaya?" Mr. D'Cruz bole, "Iska jawab Newton ke teesre law mein hai. Kahani ka jawab hum topic ke end mein number ke saath nikalenge."

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Action** | Ek body A ka doosri body B par lagaya gaya force | Myra ka paani ko peeche dhakelna |
| **Reaction** | Body B ka body A par wapas lagaya gaya force: barabar size, ulti disha | Paani ka Myra ko aage dhakelna |
| **Action-reaction pair** | Do bodies ke beech ke do force jo hamesha saath aate hain | Haath-paani ka jodi |
| **Newton ka teesra law** | Har action ka barabar aur ulta reaction hota hai | "To every action there is an equal and opposite reaction" |
| **Different bodies** | Action ek body par lagta hai, reaction doosri body par | Action paani par, reaction Myra par |
| **Normal force** | Kisi surface ka us par rakhi cheez ko seedha upar (surface se bahar) dhakelna | Kursi ka baithe hue insaan ko upar dhakelna |

## Part A: Law kya kehta hai

Newton ka teesra law kehta hai:

> **To every action there is an equal and opposite reaction.**

Ise aasan bhasha mein aise likhte hain:

> **Agar body A, body B par force lagati hai, to body B bhi body A par utna hi bada force, ulti disha mein, lagati hai.**

Yahan teen baatein dhyan se pakadni hain:

1. **Force hamesha do bodies ke beech hota hai.** Akela force nahi hota: jab bhi ek cheez dhakelti ya kheenchti hai, doosri cheez bhi saamne se dhakelti ya kheenchti hai. Dono force milkar ek **jodi (pair)** banate hain.
2. **Dono force barabar bade hote hain** (same size, newton mein) aur **disha ulti** hoti hai.
3. **Dono force ek hi waqt par** aate aur jaate hain. Pehle action, phir reaction: aisa nahi hota.

### Myra ke haath aur paani

```
   Myra ka haath  ---- 30 N peeche ---->  paani
   (paani par action)

   Myra  <---- 30 N aage ----  paani
   (Myra par reaction)

   Disha:   aage  --->
```

> **Figure 7.1** – Myra paani ko 30 N se peeche dhakelti hai (action, paani par). Paani Myra ko 30 N se aage dhakelta hai (reaction, Myra par). Dono force barabar, disha ulti.

Ab Myra ko aage kisne dhakela? **Paani ne.** Hathon ne to paani ko peeche dhakela tha. Isi liye "paani ko peeche dhakelo" ka matlab hai "aage jao". Machhli ki fin, nav ka chappu, aur bacche ka tairna: sab isi trick par chalte hain.

### Ek hi cheez ke do naam

Action aur reaction mein koi "pehla" ya "doosra" nahi hota. Hum bas ek ko "action" bol dete hain, to doosra "reaction" ban jaata hai. Agar Myra paani ko dhakele to paani ka dhakka reaction hai, aur agar hum paani ke dhakke ko action bolein to Myra ka dhakka reaction hai. Dono ek jaise hi zaroori hain.

## Part B: Sabse zaroori baat: dono force alag bodies par lagte hain

Ye is poore topic ki sabse important line hai, to dhyan se padho:

> **Action aur reaction alag-alag bodies par lagte hain. Isliye ye ek-doosre ko cancel nahi karte.**

Cancel hone ka matlab hai jab **ek hi body** par do force lagein, barabar aur ulte (jaise Topic 2 ke balanced forces). Yahan action ek body par lagta hai aur reaction doosri body par. Ek body ko to sirf **apne upar lagne wale** force se matlab hota hai.

### Misaal 1: deewar ko dhakelna

Aap deewar ko apne haath se 80 N se dhakelte ho.

- **Action:** aapka haath deewar par 80 N (deewar ki taraf).
- **Reaction:** deewar aapke haath par 80 N (aapki taraf).

```
   Aap -------> |  deewar
         80 N       (deewar par force, aage)

   Aap <------- |  deewar
         80 N       (aap par force, peeche)
```

> **Figure 7.2** – Deewar ko dhakelte waqt: aapka haath deewar par 80 N lagata hai, aur deewar aapke haath par 80 N lagati hai, ulti disha mein.

Deewar hilti kyun nahi? Kyunki wo neev (foundation) se zameen mein mazbooti se judi hai, aur zameen use apni jagah thaame rakhti hai. Hum yahan bas itna dekhein: **80 N aur 80 N ek-doosre ko cancel nahi kar rahe**, kyunki ek deewar par hai aur doosra aap par.

### Misaal 2: rassi se dabba kheenchna

Ek dabbe ko rassi se kheencha ja raha hai. Rassi mein tension 60 N hai (hum ise Class 9 mein bas "rassi ka kheenchna" samajhte hain).

- Aap rassi ke zariye dabbe par **60 N aage** lagate ho. **Reaction:** dabba rassi ke zariye aap par **60 N peeche** lagata hai.

Dabbe par lagne wale force: aapka 60 N aage, aur ground ka friction (maan lo 20 N peeche). Net force = 60 N − 20 N = 40 N aage. **Dabba isi net force se chalta hai.** Aap par lagne wala 60 N ka reaction dabbe par nahi lag raha, wo to aap par lag raha hai.

Is liye dabbe ki acceleration nikalte waqt hum sirf **dabbe par lage force** gine: 60 N aage aur 20 N peeche. Aapka reaction force (aap par peeche 60 N) gine to galti hogi.

### Yaad rakhne ka tarika

Jab bhi F = m a lagao, pehle ek body chuno aur sirf **us body par lage** force likho. Doosri body par lage force (jaise action ka reaction) us hisaab mein nahi aate.

## Part C: Roz ki zindagi ke action-reaction

### Tairna

Paani ko peeche dhakelo, paani tumhe aage dhakelta hai. (Upar ki kahani.)

### Chalna

Chalte waqt hum paon se zameen ko **peeche** dhakelte hain (action, zameen par). Zameen hum par **aage** ka force lagati hai (reaction, hum par). Isi se hum aage badhte hain. (Topic 4 mein humne isse friction ke roop mein dekha tha: friction ke bina paon bas phisalta rahta.)

### Rocket

Rocket apni gas ko bahut tezi se **neeche** phenkta hai (action, gas par). Gas rocket ko **upar** dhakelti hai (reaction, rocket par). Rocket ko upar jaane ke liye hawa ya zameen ki zaroorat nahi: wo gas se dhakela jaata hai. Isko momentum ki nazar se Topic 8 mein dekhenge.

### Gubbara chhodna

Phoolta hua gubbara chhod do to uski hawa peeche ki taraf nikalti hai (action, hawa par), aur hawa gubbare ko **aage** dhakelti hai (reaction, gubbare par). Gubbara hawa mein idhar-udhar ud jaata hai.

### Bandook ka peechhe lagna

Bandook se goli aage nikalti hai, to bandook **peeche** jhatka khati hai (isse kick ya recoil kehte hain). Bandook goli ko aage dhakelti hai (action), goli bandook ko peeche dhakelti hai (reaction). Recoil ke number Topic 8 mein.

### Nav aur kudna

Jab aap nav se kinare par kudte ho, to aap nav ko peeche dhakelte ho, aur nav aapko aage. Isi liye nav peeche khisak jaati hai. (Myra ka platform bhi yahi tha.)

### Hathoda aur keel

Hathoda keel ko neeche 200 N se thokta hai, to keel bhi hathode ko upar 200 N se dhakelta hai. Isi liye hathoda keel par ruk jaata hai (kabhi thoda uchhal bhi jaata hai).

### Kursi par baithna: ek aam galti

Kursi par baithe insaan par do force lagte hain: neeche **weight** (zameen ka kheenchna) aur upar **kursi ka dhakka (normal force)**. Dono barabar aur ulte hain, to bahut log sochte hain ki ye action-reaction ki jodi hai.

**Ye galat hai (ek aam galti hai, koi baat nahi).** Dono force **ek hi body** (insaan) par lag rahe hain, aur action-reaction **alag bodies** par lagte hain. Insaan ke weight ka asli reaction **insaan ka zameen ko kheenchna** hai (earth–body ki jodi): insaan zameen ko utna hi upar kheenchta hai jitna zameen insaan ko neeche. Ye Gravitation chapter mein detail se aayega. Kursi ka dhakka kiska reaction hai? Insaan ka kursi ko neeche dabana.

## Part D: 8 situations mein action-reaction pehchano

**Example 1:** Neeche har situation ke liye action aur reaction likho.

| Situation | Action (kis par, kisne) | Reaction (kis par, kisne) |
|---|---|---|
| Myra paani ko peeche dhakelti hai | Myra ka haath paani par, peeche | Paani Myra par, aage |
| Paon zameen ko peeche dhakelta hai | Paon zameen par, peeche | Zameen paon par, aage |
| Rocket gas ko neeche phenkta hai | Rocket gas par, neeche | Gas rocket par, upar |
| Bandook goli ko aage dhakelti hai | Bandook goli par, aage | Goli bandook par, peeche |
| Hathoda keel ko neeche thokta hai | Hathoda keel par, neeche | Keel hathode par, upar |
| Nav se kudta insaan nav ko peeche dhakelta hai | Insaan nav par, peeche | Nav insaan par, aage |
| Bat gend ko hit karta hai | Bat gend par, aage | Gend bat par, peeche |
| Kursi par baitha insaan kursi ko dabata hai | Insaan kursi par, neeche | Kursi insaan par, upar |

Dhyan do: har row mein action aur reaction **do alag bodies** par hain, aur har jodi ke do naam hain ("X ne Y par" aur "Y ne X par").

**Jaanch:** har row mein body ke naam ulte ho gaye aur disha ulti hai ✅

**Example 2 (reaction force aur disha):** Ek bachcha deewar ko 50 N ke force se dhakelta hai. Deewar bachche par kitna force lagati hai, aur kis disha mein?

- Action: bachcha deewar par 50 N (deewar ki taraf).
- Reaction (teesre law se): deewar bachche par **50 N**, bachche ki taraf (action se ulta).

**Jaanch:** barabar size (50 N = 50 N), ulti disha, aur alag bodies par ✅

**Example 3 (hathoda aur keel):** Ek hathoda keel par 200 N ke force se lagta hai. Keel hathode par kitna force lagati hai? Dono mein se kis par force zyada lagega?

- Keel hathode par **200 N**, upar ki taraf.
- Dono par force **barabar** hai: 200 N aur 200 N. Zyada kisi par nahi.

**Jaanch:** 200 N = 200 N ✅. Keel lakdi mein ghus jaati hai aur hathoda ruk jaata hai, par iski wajah force ka chhota-bada hona nahi: dono par force barabar hai. Farak in par lage baaki forces (jaise lakdi ki rukawat) aur inke mass ka hai.

## Part E: Barabar force, par alag acceleration

Action aur reaction ke force barabar hote hain, to kya dono bodies ki acceleration bhi barabar hogi? **Nahi.** Kyunki F = m a se **a = F ÷ m**. Force barabar ho par mass alag ho, to acceleration alag hogi.

### Insaan aur platform

**Example 4 (platform se kudna, numbers ke saath):** Ek 60 kg ka insaan 120 kg ke floating platform se kudta hai. Kudte waqt platform aur insaan ek-doosre ko 300 N se dhakelte hain, aur ye force utne hi time ke liye lagta hai. Dono ki acceleration nikalo.

- Insaan par force = 300 N (aage), m = 60 kg.
  a = F ÷ m = 300 N ÷ 60 kg = **5 m/s²** (aage).
- Platform par force = 300 N (peeche), m = 120 kg.
  a = F ÷ m = 300 N ÷ 120 kg = **2.5 m/s²** (peeche).

**Jaanch:** 60 kg × 5 m/s² = 300 N ✅. 120 kg × 2.5 m/s² = 300 N ✅. Platform ki acceleration insaan ki aadhi hai (2.5 ÷ 5 = 0.5), aur platform ka mass insaan se do guna hai (120 ÷ 60 = 2). **Mass jitna zyada, acceleration utni kam.**

### Boat aur kudna

**Example 5:** 50 kg ka ek ladka 100 kg ki boat se kinare ki taraf kudta hai. Dono ek-doosre ko 200 N se dhakelte hain. Dono ki acceleration?

- Ladka: a = 200 N ÷ 50 kg = **4 m/s²** (kinare ki taraf).
- Boat: a = 200 N ÷ 100 kg = **2 m/s²** (kinare se door, ulti disha).

**Jaanch:** 50 kg × 4 m/s² = 200 N ✅, 100 kg × 2 m/s² = 200 N ✅. Boat ka mass do guna hai, to acceleration aadhi hai.

### Do skaters

**Example 6:** Do skaters ek-doosre ko 50 N se dhakelte hain. Pehle skater ka mass 50 kg hai, doosre ka 75 kg. Dono ki acceleration aur disha?

- Pehla: a = 50 N ÷ 50 kg = **1 m/s²**.
- Doosra: a = 50 N ÷ 75 kg = **0.67 m/s²** (lagbhag, 2/3 m/s²).
- Dono ki disha **ulti** hai: pehla ek taraf, doosra doosri taraf.

```
   <---- 1 m/s²   [ 50 kg ]  <-- 50 N | 50 N -->  [ 75 kg ]   0.67 m/s² ---->
```

> **Figure 7.3** – Do skaters ek-doosre ko 50 N se dhakelte hain. Halka skater (50 kg) tez, 1 m/s², aur bhaari skater (75 kg) kam, 0.67 m/s², ulti disha mein.

**Jaanch:** 50 kg × 1 m/s² = 50 N ✅. 75 kg × (2/3) m/s² = 50 N ✅ (0.67 bas gol kiya hua hai). Bhaari skater ka mass 1.5 guna hai (75 ÷ 50), to uski acceleration 1 m/s² ÷ 1.5 ≈ 0.67 m/s² hai.

Ek zaroori baat: bhaari skater ne halke skater par **zyada force nahi** lagaya. Dono par force barabar (50 N) hai. Bas halka skater halka hai, to zyada tezi se bhaaga.

### Do bodies ki kul "gati" (jhalak)

Ye barabar aur ulte force dono bodies ki gati (momentum) mein aise badlav laate hain ki dono bodies ka **total momentum ek jaisa bana rehta hai**. Iski poori baat Topic 8 mein.

## Part F: Ghoda aur gaadi ki paheli

Ek purani paheli: ghoda gaadi ko aage kheenchta hai. Teesre law ke hisaab se gaadi bhi ghode ko utna hi peeche kheenchti hai. Dono force barabar hain. To gaadi chalti kaise hai?

Jawab Part B mein hai: **ghode ke kheenchne wala force gaadi par lagta hai, aur gaadi ke kheenchne wala force ghode par lagta hai.** Alag bodies, to cancel nahi hote. Gaadi ki gati ke liye hume **gaadi par lage net force** dekhna hai.

**Example 7 (ghoda aur gaadi, numbers ke saath):** Ghoda gaadi ko rassi se 500 N ke force se aage kheenchta hai. Gaadi (mass 200 kg) par zameen ka friction 300 N peeche lagta hai. Ghode ka mass 400 kg hai. Ghode ke khur zameen ko peeche dhakelte hain, aur zameen ghode ko aage dhakelti hai. Gaadi ki acceleration kya hai, aur zameen ghode par kitna force lagati hai?

**Step 1: gaadi par lage force (sirf gaadi ko dekho).**

- Aage: ghode ka kheenchna = 500 N.
- Peeche: friction = 300 N.
- Net force = 500 N − 300 N = **200 N** aage.

**Step 2: gaadi ki acceleration.**

- a = F ÷ m = 200 N ÷ 200 kg = **1 m/s²** (aage).

**Step 3: ghode par lage force (ab sirf ghode ko dekho).**

- Peeche: gaadi ka kheenchna = 500 N (ye wahi reaction hai).
- Aage: zameen ka force (khur par friction) = F.
- Ghoda gaadi ke saath chal raha hai, to uski bhi acceleration 1 m/s² hai: net force = 400 kg × 1 m/s² = 400 N.
- F − 500 N = 400 N, to F = **900 N** (aage).

```
   Ghoda par:  <-- 500 N (gaadi)       900 N (zameen) -->     net: 400 N aage
   Gaadi par:  <-- 300 N (friction)    500 N (ghoda)  -->     net: 200 N aage
```

> **Figure 7.4** – Ghode aur gaadi par alag-alag force. Ghode par 900 N (zameen se) aage aur 500 N (gaadi se) peeche: net 400 N. Gaadi par 500 N (ghode se) aage aur 300 N friction peeche: net 200 N. Dono ki acceleration 1 m/s².

**Jaanch:**
- Gaadi: (500 N − 300 N) ÷ 200 kg = 1 m/s² ✅
- Ghoda: (900 N − 500 N) ÷ 400 kg = 1 m/s² ✅
- Dono ko ek system maano (mass 600 kg): bahar se force sirf zameen ka 900 N aage aur friction 300 N peeche: (900 N − 300 N) ÷ 600 kg = 1 m/s² ✅. Ghode aur gaadi ke beech ke 500 N andar ke force hain, wo system ko nahi hilaate.

**Paheli ka jawab:** ghoda isliye aage badhta hai ki **zameen ghode ko aage dhakelti hai** (khur ka friction, jaise chalte waqt hum). Agar zameen bahut phisalan wali ho (barf), to khur phisalenge aur ghoda gaadi ko nahi kheench payega. Gaadi isliye chalti hai ki us par ghode ka aage ka force friction se **zyada** hai.

## Part G: Kahani ka hal

Ab Myra ke platform ki taraf aate hain. Myra ka mass 40 kg hai, platform ka 120 kg. Myra platform ko 120 N se peeche dhakel kar kudti hai, aur ye force 0.5 s ke liye lagta hai.

**Example 8 (kahani):** Myra aur platform ki acceleration, aur 0.5 s baad dono ki velocity (dono shuru mein ruke the)?

- Myra par platform ka force = 120 N (aage). a = 120 N ÷ 40 kg = **3 m/s²** (aage).
- Platform par Myra ka force = 120 N (peeche). a = 120 N ÷ 120 kg = **1 m/s²** (peeche).
- 0.5 s baad velocity (v = a × t): Myra = 3 m/s² × 0.5 s = **1.5 m/s** aage. Platform = 1 m/s² × 0.5 s = **0.5 m/s** peeche.

**Jaanch:** 40 kg × 3 m/s² = 120 N ✅. 120 kg × 1 m/s² = 120 N ✅. 3 m/s² × 0.5 s = 1.5 m/s ✅, 1 m/s² × 0.5 s = 0.5 m/s ✅. Platform ka mass Myra se 3 guna hai, to uski acceleration 1/3 hai (1 ÷ 3).

**Jawab:** platform isliye peeche gaya ki Myra ne use peeche dhakela, aur platform ne Myra ko aage. Dono par force barabar tha (120 N), par platform bhaari tha, to wo kam tez khisaka. Paani mein tairte waqt bhi yehi hota hai: Myra ke haath paani ko peeche dhakelte hain aur paani Myra ko aage.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Action aur reaction cancel ho jaate hain**

Ye sabse badi galti hai. Cancel tab hota hai jab dono force **ek hi body** par lagein. Action aur reaction alag bodies par lagte hain (Part B). Ghode-gaadi ki paheli (Part F) mein 500 N ghode par alag lag raha tha, gaadi par alag.

**Galti 2: Reaction action ke baad aata hai**

Nahi. Dono **ek hi waqt par** aate aur jaate hain. Myra ka haath paani ko dhakelta hai, usi pal paani bhi Myra ko dhakelta hai.

**Galti 3: Bhaari cheez chhoti cheez par zyada force lagati hai**

Nahi. Force **barabar** hote hain. Skaters ke example mein dono par 50 N laga. Bas halke skater ki acceleration zyada thi (1 m/s² vs 0.67 m/s²), kyunki a = F ÷ m.

**Galti 4: Weight aur normal force ko action-reaction samajhna**

Kursi par baithe insaan ke weight (neeche) aur kursi ke dhakke (upar) par dono ek hi body (insaan) par lagte hain, to ye jodi nahi hai. Weight ka asli reaction hai insaan ka zameen ko kheenchna (Part C).

**Galti 5: Force on A aur force on B ko mila dena**

Gaadi ki acceleration nikalte waqt ghode par lage force bhi jod dena galat hai. Pehle body chuno (gaadi), phir sirf **us par lage** force: 500 N aage aur 300 N peeche, net 200 N, a = 1 m/s². Ghode ke 400 N jod diye to galat jawab aayega.

## Quick Recap

- **Newton ka teesra law:** to every action there is an equal and opposite reaction. Body A body B par force lagaye to B bhi A par barabar aur ulta force lagata hai.
- Action aur reaction **do alag bodies** par lagte hain, isliye **cancel nahi hote**. (Sabse zaroori baat.)
- Dono force **ek hi waqt par** aate hain, **barabar size** (N mein) aur **ulti disha** mein.
- Swimming (paani ko peeche dhakelo), chalna (zameen ko peeche dhakelo), rocket, gubbara, bandook ka kick, nav se kudna, hathoda-keel: sab isi law se hote hain.
- Kursi par baithe insaan ke weight aur kursi ke dhakke action-reaction nahi hain (ek hi body par). Weight ki asli jodi earth–body ki hai.
- Force barabar hone par bhi acceleration alag: **a = F ÷ m**. Mass jitna zyada, acceleration utni kam. (60 kg insaan: 5 m/s², 120 kg platform: 2.5 m/s².)
- Ghoda-gaadi: gaadi chalti hai kyunki us par **net force** aage hai (500 − 300 = 200 N), aur ghoda isliye aage badhta hai ki zameen use aage dhakelti hai.
- Ye barabar aur ulte force do bodies ke total momentum ko ek-sa rakhte hain (Topic 8).
- **Kahani ka jawab:** Myra 3 m/s² aage, platform 1 m/s² peeche (120 N dono par).

## Practice Questions

1. A boy pushes a wall with a force of 40 N. What is the force exerted by the wall on the boy? State its size and direction.

2. Name the action-reaction pair in each case: (a) a swimmer pushing the water backward, (b) a rocket throwing its exhaust gases downward, (c) a hammer hitting a nail.

3. Two skaters, of mass 40 kg and 60 kg, push each other with a force of 120 N. Find the acceleration of each skater and state the directions.

4. A 30 kg child jumps off a 90 kg boat. The child and the boat push each other with 180 N. Find the acceleration of each.

5. A horse pulls a cart with a force of 300 N. The friction on the cart is 100 N and the cart's mass is 100 kg. (a) What is the net force on the cart? (b) Find the acceleration of the cart. (c) What force does the cart exert on the horse?

6. State True or False:
   (a) Action and reaction act on the same body.
   (b) The reaction force comes slightly after the action.
   (c) A heavier body exerts a larger force on a lighter body when they push each other.
   (d) A person's weight and the chair's upward push on the person form an action-reaction pair.

7. If action and reaction are equal and opposite, why do they not cancel each other? Explain with an example.

8. You are standing on a skateboard and push a wall. Why do you roll away from the wall?

9. A 50 kg person jumps from a 150 kg platform with a 250 N push. Find the accelerations of both and explain why they are different even though the forces are equal.

### Answers

1. Wall boy par **40 N** lagati hai, boy ki taraf (action se ulti disha).
   Jaanch: 40 N = 40 N, ulti disha, alag bodies ✅

2. (a) Swimmer ka paani ko peeche dhakelna, paani ka swimmer ko aage dhakelna. (b) Rocket ka gas ko neeche dhakelna, gas ka rocket ko upar dhakelna. (c) Hathode ka keel par force (neeche), keel ka hathode par force (upar).

3. 40 kg wala: a = 120 N ÷ 40 kg = **3 m/s²**. 60 kg wala: a = 120 N ÷ 60 kg = **2 m/s²**. Dono ki disha ulti hai.
   Jaanch: 40 kg × 3 m/s² = 120 N ✅, 60 kg × 2 m/s² = 120 N ✅

4. Child: a = 180 N ÷ 30 kg = **6 m/s²**. Boat: a = 180 N ÷ 90 kg = **2 m/s²** (ulti disha).
   Jaanch: 30 kg × 6 m/s² = 180 N ✅, 90 kg × 2 m/s² = 180 N ✅

5. (a) Net force = 300 N − 100 N = **200 N** aage. (b) a = 200 N ÷ 100 kg = **2 m/s²**. (c) Cart horse par **300 N** lagati hai, peeche ki taraf.
   Jaanch: 100 kg × 2 m/s² = 200 N ✅

6. (a) **False** ❌. Alag bodies par lagte hain.
   (b) **False** ❌. Dono ek hi waqt par aate hain.
   (c) **False** ❌. Dono force barabar hote hain.
   (d) **False** ❌. Dono force ek hi body (insaan) par lag rahe hain.

7. Cancel tab hota hai jab do force **ek hi body** par lagein. Action aur reaction alag bodies par lagte hain. Example: aap deewar ko 80 N se dhakelte ho: 80 N deewar par lagta hai, reaction 80 N aap par. Ek hi body par dono nahi, to cancel nahi.

8. Aap wall par force lagate ho, to wall aap par barabar aur ulta force lagati hai. Ye force aap par (skateboard sahit) lagta hai, aur skateboard par friction kam hota hai, to aap wall se door khisak jaate ho.

9. Person: a = 250 N ÷ 50 kg = **5 m/s²**. Platform: a = 250 N ÷ 150 kg = **1.67 m/s²** (lagbhag, 5/3), ulti disha. Force barabar hai par platform ka mass 3 guna hai (150 ÷ 50), to a = F ÷ m ki wajah se uski acceleration 1/3 hai.
   Jaanch: 50 kg × 5 m/s² = 250 N ✅, 150 kg × (5/3) m/s² = 250 N ✅
