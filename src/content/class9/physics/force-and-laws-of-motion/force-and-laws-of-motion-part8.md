# Topic 8: Conservation of Momentum

Pichhle topic mein Newton ka Teesra Law dekha tha: jab do cheezein ek doosre par force lagati hain, to dono forces **barabar aur ulte** hote hain, aur ye **alag alag bodies** par lagte hain. Ab sawaal ye hai ki agar dono bodies ek doosre ko barabar force se, **barabar time** tak dhakel rahi hain, to unke momentum ka kya hota hai? Kya kuch badalta hai, ya kuch ek jaisa bana rehta hai? Ye topic chapter ka aakhri topic hai, aur ye Topic 5 (momentum), Topic 6 (F = m a) aur Topic 7 (action-reaction) ko ek hi jagah jod deta hai. End mein poore chapter ka wrap-up bhi hoga.

## Part A: Law kya kehta hai

**Conservation of Momentum ka law:**

Agar kisi system par bahar se koi unbalanced force nahi lag raha (isolated system), to **interaction se pehle ka total momentum = interaction ke baad ka total momentum.**

**p (pehle) = p (baad mein)**

"Conserve" ka matlab hai "bana rehna". Total badalta nahi. Ek body ka momentum badh sakta hai, doosri ka ghat sakta hai, par **jitna ek ne khoya utna doosre ne paaya**.

### Ye law aata kahan se hai? (Step by step)

Do bodies lo: body 1 (mass m₁) aur body 2 (mass m₂), ek seedhi line mein. Takkar se pehle unki velocities u₁ aur u₂ hain, takkar ke baad v₁ aur v₂.

**Step 1.** Takkar ke dauran body 1 body 2 par force F lagati hai. Newton ke Teesre Law se body 2 body 1 par **barabar aur ulta** force lagati hai: −F.

**Step 2.** Dono forces **same time t** tak lagte hain, kyunki takkar mein dono bodies ek doosre ko tabhi tak chhoti hain jab tak wo saath hain.

**Step 3.** Topic 6 se F = m(v − u) ÷ t, yaani F × t = m(v − u). Force × time = momentum ka change.

- Body 2 ke liye: F × t = m₂(v₂ − u₂)
- Body 1 ke liye: (−F) × t = m₁(v₁ − u₁)

**Step 4.** Dono F × t barabar aur ulte hain, to **momentum ke changes bhi barabar aur ulte** hain:

m₁(v₁ − u₁) = −m₂(v₂ − u₂)

**Step 5.** Bracket kholkar sab ek taraf karo:

m₁v₁ − m₁u₁ = −m₂v₂ + m₂u₂

**m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = m₁v₁ + m₂v₂**

Yaani **pehle ka total momentum = baad ka total momentum.** Ye bina kisi jaadu ke sirf Newton ke Doosre aur Teesre Law se nikla!

```
Takkar se pehle:     m1 --> u1        m2 --> u2
                    [ A ]            [ B ]

Takkar ke dauran:  <-- -F [ A ][ B ] +F -->       (A par peeche, B par aage: barabar,
                                                   ulte force, same time)

Takkar ke baad:      m1 --> v1        m2 --> v2
                    [ A ]            [ B ]
```

> **Figure 8.1** – Takkar ke dauran dono bodies par barabar aur ulte force same time tak lagte hain, isliye ek ka momentum jitna ghatta hai doosre ka utna badhta hai.

### Direction ka sign

Seedhi line mein ek disha chuno. Hum **daayein (right) ko +** aur **baayein (left) ko −** maanenge. Har velocity (aur momentum) ke saath ye sign likhna zaroori hai.

- Right ki taraf 5 m/s: **+5 m/s**
- Left ki taraf 5 m/s: **−5 m/s**

Isolated system tab milta hai jab bahar se koi net force nahi hota. Chikne floor par, ya itne kam time ke takkar mein jahan friction ka asar na ke barabar hota hai, ye law bilkul sahi baithta hai.

## Part B: Takkar ke alag alag tareeke (ek seedhi line mein)

| Situation | Kya hota hai | Likhne ka tareeka |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Chalti body rest wali body se takrati hai | Dono alag ho sakti hain ya chipak sakti hain | m₁u₁ + 0 = m₁v₁ + m₂v₂ |
| 2. Dono ek hi disha mein, peeche wali tez | Peeche wali aage wali ko pakad kar takrati hai | Dono u positive |
| 3. Dono ek doosre ki taraf | Ek ka sign negative | Ek u negative |
| 4. Takkar ke baad chipak jaana | Dono ek hi velocity v se chalti hain | m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = (m₁ + m₂)v |
| 5. Takkar ke baad alag hona (bounce apart) | Dono ki alag velocities | m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = m₁v₁ + m₂v₂ |

Chipakne wale case mein dono ki **combined mass (m₁ + m₂)** ek hi velocity v se chalti hai, isliye baad ka momentum (m₁ + m₂)v likhte hain.

## Part C: Solved examples

### Example 1: Chalti trolley khadi trolley se takrakar chipak jaati hai

Trolley A (m₁ = 2 kg) 6 m/s se chal rahi hai aur khadi trolley B (m₂ = 4 kg) se takrakar chipak jaati hai. Dono ki saath wali velocity?

- Pehle: p = m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = 2 kg × 6 m/s + 4 kg × 0 m/s = 12 kg m/s
- Baad mein: (m₁ + m₂)v = (2 kg + 4 kg) × v = 6 kg × v
- 12 kg m/s = 6 kg × v, to v = 12 kg m/s ÷ 6 kg = **2 m/s** (right ki taraf)

Jaanch: baad ka momentum = 6 kg × 2 m/s = 12 kg m/s = pehle ka momentum ✅

### Example 2: Ek hi disha mein chalti do bodies

5 kg ki body 4 m/s se chal rahi hai aur aage 2 m/s se jaati 3 kg ki body se takrati hai. Takkar ke baad 3 kg wali body 4.5 m/s se chalti hai. 5 kg wali body ki velocity?

- Pehle: 5 kg × 4 m/s + 3 kg × 2 m/s = 20 kg m/s + 6 kg m/s = 26 kg m/s
- Baad mein: 5 kg × v₁ + 3 kg × 4.5 m/s = 5v₁ + 13.5 kg m/s
- 26 kg m/s = 5 kg × v₁ + 13.5 kg m/s, to 5 kg × v₁ = 12.5 kg m/s, v₁ = 12.5 kg m/s ÷ 5 kg = **2.5 m/s**

Jaanch: 5 kg × 2.5 m/s + 13.5 kg m/s = 12.5 kg m/s + 13.5 kg m/s = 26 kg m/s ✅ (dono bodies right ki taraf rahi, sign +)

### Example 3: Do bodies ek doosre ki taraf aate hain aur chipak jaate hain

3 kg ki trolley right ki taraf **+4 m/s** se aur 5 kg ki trolley left ki taraf **−2 m/s** se aati hai. Takkar ke baad dono chipak jaati hain. Combined velocity?

- Pehle: 3 kg × (+4 m/s) + 5 kg × (−2 m/s) = +12 kg m/s − 10 kg m/s = **+2 kg m/s**
- Baad mein: (3 kg + 5 kg) × v = 8 kg × v
- v = 2 kg m/s ÷ 8 kg = **+0.25 m/s** (right ki taraf, kyunki sign +)

Jaanch: 8 kg × 0.25 m/s = 2 kg m/s ✅

Dhyan do: zyada momentum wali body (3 kg × 4 m/s = 12 kg m/s) jeeti, isliye dono ab bahut dheere, right ki taraf chalte hain. Agar sign na lagate aur 12 + 10 = 22 jodte, to galat answer aata.

```
Pehle:     3 kg --> +4 m/s        <-- -2 m/s  5 kg
           [ A ]                              [ B ]
                      takkar
Baad mein:        [ A+B ] --> +0.25 m/s  (8 kg, chipke hue)
```

> **Figure 8.2** – Do trolley ek doosre ki taraf aati hain, takrakar chipak jaati hain aur combined mass right ki taraf bahut dheemi chalti hai.

### Example 4: Takkar ke baad alag ho jaana (bounce apart)

2 kg ki body +5 m/s se khadi 3 kg ki body se takrati hai. Takkar ke baad 2 kg wali body **−1 m/s** se wapas aati hai (left ki taraf). 3 kg wali body ki velocity?

- Pehle: 2 kg × (+5 m/s) + 3 kg × 0 = +10 kg m/s
- Baad mein: 2 kg × (−1 m/s) + 3 kg × v₂ = −2 + 3v₂ kg m/s
- 10 kg m/s = −2 kg m/s + 3 kg × v₂, to 3 kg × v₂ = 12 kg m/s, v₂ = 12 kg m/s ÷ 3 kg = **+4 m/s** (right ki taraf)

Jaanch: −2 kg m/s + 3 kg × 4 m/s = −2 kg m/s + 12 kg m/s = +10 kg m/s ✅

### Example 5: Gun ka recoil

Gun ka mass 4 kg hai aur wo 0.02 kg ki bullet 300 m/s se chalati hai. Gun ki recoil velocity?

Bullet ko right ki taraf (+) maanenge. Pehle gun aur bullet dono rest mein hain, to total momentum = 0.

- Pehle: 0 kg m/s
- Baad mein: m_bullet × v_bullet + m_gun × v_gun = 0.02 kg × 300 m/s + 4 kg × v = 6 kg m/s + 4 kg × v
- 0 = 6 kg m/s + 4 kg × v, to v = −6 kg m/s ÷ 4 kg = **−1.5 m/s**

Yaani gun **1.5 m/s se peeche** (left ki taraf) jhatka khati hai.

Jaanch: 0.02 kg × 300 m/s = +6 kg m/s aur 4 kg × (−1.5 m/s) = −6 kg m/s. Total = 0 ✅

```
Pehle:        [=====GUN=====] (bullet)       sab rest mein, total p = 0

Baad mein:  <-- recoil   [=====GUN=====]  o--> bullet
            -1.5 m/s                          +300 m/s
```

> **Figure 8.3** – Bullet aage jaati hai, gun peeche. Dono ke momentum barabar aur ulte hain, isliye total phir bhi 0 hai.

**Heavy gun kam recoil kyun karti hai?** Bullet ka momentum same 6 kg m/s hai, to gun ka momentum bhi 6 kg m/s (ulti disha mein). Par v = p ÷ m, to mass zyada ho to v kam. Agar gun 8 kg ki hoti: v = 6 kg m/s ÷ 8 kg = **0.75 m/s**, yaani aadha recoil. Isliye bade bandook ya toop bhaari banate hain.

### Example 6: Girl cart se kood rahi hai

60 kg ki khadi cart par 40 kg ki ek ladki khadi hai. Wo 2 m/s se right ki taraf zameen par koodti hai. Cart ki velocity?

- Pehle: sab rest mein, total p = 0
- Baad mein: 40 kg × (+2 m/s) + 60 kg × v = 80 + 60v kg m/s
- 0 = 80 kg m/s + 60 kg × v, to v = −80 kg m/s ÷ 60 kg = **−1.33 m/s** (lagbhag; exact −4/3 m/s, left ki taraf)

Jaanch: 40 kg × 2 m/s = +80 kg m/s aur 60 kg × (−4/3 m/s) = −80 kg m/s, total 0 kg m/s ✅

Isi tarah naav se kinare par koodne par naav peeche hat jaati hai.

### Example 7: Rudra aur Mr. Saini ki dodgem cars (kahani ka hisaab)

Car A (Rudra ki, m = 200 kg) 3 m/s se aati hai aur khadi car B (m = 200 kg) se takrati hai. Takkar ke baad car A 1 m/s se chalti hai (wahi disha mein). Car B ki speed?

Right ko + maanenge.

- Pehle: 200 kg × 3 m/s + 200 kg × 0 = 600 kg m/s
- Baad mein: 200 kg × 1 m/s + 200 kg × v = 200 + 200v kg m/s
- 600 kg m/s = 200 kg m/s + 200 kg × v, to 200 kg × v = 400 kg m/s, v = 400 kg m/s ÷ 200 kg = **2 m/s**

| Cheez | Pehle (kg m/s) | Baad mein (kg m/s) |
|---|---|---|
| Car A | 200 × 3 = 600 | 200 × 1 = 200 |
| Car B | 200 × 0 = 0 | 200 × 2 = 400 |
| **Total** | **600** | **600** |

Jaanch: 600 kg m/s = 600 kg m/s ✅

Rudra ki car ne 400 kg m/s momentum **kho diya** aur khadi car ne wahi 400 kg m/s **paa liya**. Mr. Saini sahi the: takat gayi nahi, sirf transfer hui.

```
Pehle:     [A] --> 3 m/s            [B] khadi
           200 kg                   200 kg

Baad mein:      [A] --> 1 m/s       [B] --> 2 m/s
```

> **Figure 8.4** – Dodgem cars: A ka momentum 600 se 200 kg m/s hua, B ka 0 se 400 kg m/s, total dono taraf 600 kg m/s.

### Example 8: Firecracker ya explosion

3 kg ka ek gola khada hai aur phat kar do tukdon mein tut jaata hai: 2 kg ka tukda right ki taraf +4 m/s se uchhalta hai. Doosre 1 kg ke tukde ki velocity?

Explosion se pehle gola rest mein tha, to total p = 0. Baad mein bhi total 0 hi rehna chahiye.

- 2 kg × (+4 m/s) + 1 kg × v = 0
- 8 kg m/s + 1 kg × v = 0, to v = **−8 m/s** (left ki taraf)

Jaanch: +8 kg m/s + (−8 kg m/s) = 0 ✅

Halka tukda **zyada tez** udta hai, bhaari tukda dheere, aur dono ulti dishaon mein.

### Example 9: Gayab mass nikalna

1.5 kg ka trolley 4 m/s se khade trolley se takrakar chipak jaata hai. Dono saath mein 1 m/s se chalte hain. Khade trolley ka mass?

- Pehle: 1.5 kg × 4 m/s + m × 0 = 6 kg m/s
- Baad mein: (1.5 kg + m) × 1 m/s
- 6 kg m/s = (1.5 kg + m) × 1 m/s, to 1.5 kg + m = 6 kg, aur m = **4.5 kg**

Jaanch: (1.5 + 4.5) kg × 1 m/s = 6 kg m/s ✅

### Example 10: Toop aur gola

500 kg ki toop 5 kg ka gola 100 m/s se chalati hai. Toop ki recoil speed?

- Gole ka momentum = 5 kg × 100 m/s = 500 kg m/s
- Toop ka momentum = 500 kg m/s (ulti disha mein)
- Toop ki speed = 500 kg m/s ÷ 500 kg = **1 m/s** (peeche)

Jaanch: +500 kg m/s − 500 kg m/s = 0 ✅

### Example 11: Naav se aadmi ka koodna

50 kg ka aadmi 150 kg ki khadi naav se 3 m/s se kinare par koodta hai. Naav kitni speed se peeche hatti hai?

- Aadmi ka momentum = 50 kg × 3 m/s = 150 kg m/s
- Naav ka momentum = −150 kg m/s, to v = −150 kg m/s ÷ 150 kg = **−1 m/s**

Naav **1 m/s** se peeche hatti hai.

Jaanch: +150 kg m/s + 150 kg × (−1 m/s) = 0 ✅

### Example 12: Bhaari aur halki body ki takkar

2000 kg ka truck 10 m/s se khade 100 kg ke scooter se takrakar chipak jaata hai. Combined velocity?

- Pehle: 2000 kg × 10 m/s = 20,000 kg m/s
- v = 20,000 kg m/s ÷ (2000 + 100) kg = **9.52 m/s**

Ab ulta: 100 kg ka scooter 10 m/s se khade truck se takrakar chipakta hai:

- Pehle: 100 kg × 10 m/s = 1000 kg m/s
- v = 1000 kg m/s ÷ 2100 kg = **0.48 m/s**

Jaanch: 2100 kg × 9.52 m/s ≈ 20,000 kg m/s ✅ aur 2100 kg × 0.476 m/s ≈ 1000 kg m/s ✅

Bhaari body ka momentum zyada hota hai, isliye wo takkar mein apni raftaar bachaye rakhti hai. Isliye halki gaadi ki velocity bhaari gaadi se takrane par bahut zyada badalti hai, aur usmein baithe logon ko zyada jhatka lagta hai.

## Part D: Aur kuch examples (sirf idea)

- **Balloon aur rocket:** Phoolta hua balloon chhodo to hawa **peeche** nikalti hai aur balloon **aage** udta hai. Hawa ka momentum peeche, to balloon ka momentum aage, aur total shuru ki tarah 0. Rocket mein bhi garam gas peeche jaati hai aur rocket aage. (Iska hisaab aage ki class mein.)
- **Bandook ka kandhe par lagna:** recoil ka jhatka.
- **Naav se kinare par koodna:** naav peeche hatti hai.

### Kab ye law nahi lagta?

Ye law tabhi sahi hai jab **bahar se koi net force na lage**. Agar friction jaisa external force lamba samay tak lage, to total momentum conserve nahi hoga. Maan lo ek ball floor par rolling karti hai. Ball ke saath koi takkar nahi hui, par floor ka friction use dheere dheere rok deta hai. Ball ka momentum kam hota gaya, kyunki ek external force (friction) lagta raha. Dodgem ya trolley ki takkar ke chhote samay mein friction ka asar na ke barabar hota hai, isliye wahan law theek baithta hai.

(Takkar mein energy ka kya hota hai, ye aage Work and Energy chapter mein.)

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Velocity ke sign bhool jaana**
Example 3 mein 3 × 4 + 5 × 2 = 22 jodne se v = 22 ÷ 8 = 2.75 m/s aata hai, jo galat hai. Sahi: 12 kg m/s − 10 kg m/s = 2 kg m/s, v = +0.25 m/s.

**Galti 2: Mass ki jagah weight use karna**
Momentum = m × v, jahan m kg mein **mass** hai. Weight (newton mein, jaise 20 N) ko m ki jagah mat daalo. 2 kg mass hai to m = 2 kg hi likho.

**Galti 3: Speed jodna, momentum nahi**
Example 4 mein speeds 5 aur 0 thi, baad mein 1 aur 4. Speeds jodo to 5 aur 5 aata hai, par ye sirf ittefaq hai; masses (2 kg aur 3 kg) alag hain. Sahi check m × v ka hai: 10 kg m/s aur (−2 + 12) kg m/s = 10 kg m/s. Hamesha **m × v** jodo, speed nahi.

**Galti 4: External force ke saath bhi law lagana**
Lambe samay tak friction lage to total momentum conserve nahi hota (rolling ball).

**Galti 5: Chipakne wali takkar mein velocities alag alag rakhna**
Agar dono chipak gaye, to baad mein **ek hi v** hai aur mass (m₁ + m₂) hai. "m₁v₁ + m₂v₂" mein do alag velocities likhna galat hai.

**Galti 6: Units ki chook**
Momentum kg m/s mein, mass kg mein, velocity m/s mein. Agar mass gram mein hai to pehle kg mein badlo: 20 g = 0.02 kg.

## Quick Recap

- Isolated system (net external force = 0) mein **total momentum pehle = baad mein**.
- Formula: **m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = m₁v₁ + m₂v₂**.
- Ye Newton ke Doosre aur Teesre Law se nikalta hai: dono par barabar, ulte force, same time, to momentum ke change barabar aur ulte.
- Direction: right (+), left (−). Sign likhna zaroori.
- Chipakne par: m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = (m₁ + m₂)v.
- Recoil: bullet ka momentum aage, gun ka utna hi peeche. Bhaari gun kam recoil karti hai.
- Explosion se pehle rest hai to total momentum 0 hi rehta hai.
- Bhaari body takkar mein apni raftaar zyada bachati hai.
- Lamba samay tak external force (friction) ho to law nahi lagta.

5. A toy gun of mass 2 kg fires a 0.01 kg pellet at 500 m/s. Find the recoil velocity of the gun.

6. A 2 kg body moving at +6 m/s hits a stationary 4 kg body and stops. Find the velocity of the 4 kg body.

7. A 6 kg object at rest explodes into two pieces of 4 kg and 2 kg. The 4 kg piece moves at +3 m/s. Find the velocity of the 2 kg piece.

8. A 2 kg trolley moving at 5 m/s hits a stationary trolley and they stick. Their common speed is 2 m/s. Find the mass of the second trolley.

9. Say whether each statement is true or false, giving a reason.
   (a) In a collision on a smooth floor, the total momentum of the two trolleys stays the same.
   (b) In a collision, the momentum of each trolley stays the same.
   (c) A heavier gun, firing the same bullet at the same speed, recoils less.

10. A rolling ball slows down and stops on the floor without hitting anything. Does this break the law of conservation of momentum? Explain.

### Answers

1. Pehle: 4 kg × 3 m/s + 2 kg × 0 = 12 kg m/s. Baad: (4 kg + 2 kg) × v = 6 kg × v. v = 12 kg m/s ÷ 6 kg = **2 m/s**.
   Jaanch: 6 kg × 2 m/s = 12 kg m/s ✅

2. Pehle: 6 kg × (+2 m/s) + 4 kg × (−3 m/s) = 12 kg m/s − 12 kg m/s = 0. Baad: 10 kg × v = 0, to **v = 0** (dono ruk jaate hain).
   Jaanch: 10 kg × 0 m/s = 0 kg m/s ✅

3. Bullet ka momentum = 0.05 kg × 400 m/s = 20 kg m/s. Rifle: 10 kg × v = −20 kg m/s, to **v = −2 m/s** (2 m/s peeche).
   Jaanch: +20 kg m/s + 10 kg × (−2 m/s) = 0 ✅

4. Pehle: 60 kg × 5 m/s + 40 kg × 0 = 300 kg m/s. Baad: (60 kg + 40 kg) × v = 100 kg × v. v = 300 kg m/s ÷ 100 kg = **3 m/s**.
   Jaanch: 100 kg × 3 m/s = 300 kg m/s ✅

5. Pellet: 0.01 kg × 500 m/s = 5 kg m/s. Gun: 2 kg × v = −5 kg m/s, to **v = −2.5 m/s** (2.5 m/s peeche).
   Jaanch: +5 kg m/s + 2 kg × (−2.5 m/s) = 0 ✅

6. Pehle: 2 kg × 6 m/s = 12 kg m/s. Baad: 2 kg × 0 + 4 kg × v = 4 kg × v. v = 12 kg m/s ÷ 4 kg = **+3 m/s**.
   Jaanch: 4 kg × 3 m/s = 12 kg m/s ✅

7. Pehle total 0. 4 kg × 3 m/s + 2 kg × v = 0, yaani 12 kg m/s + 2 kg × v = 0, **v = −6 m/s** (left ki taraf).
   Jaanch: +12 kg m/s − 12 kg m/s = 0 ✅

8. Pehle: 2 kg × 5 m/s = 10 kg m/s. Baad: (2 kg + m) × 2 m/s = 10 kg m/s, to 2 kg + m = 5 kg, **m = 3 kg**.
   Jaanch: (2 kg + 3 kg) × 2 m/s = 10 kg m/s ✅

9. (a) **True** ✅. Dono par barabar, ulte force same time lagte hain, to total momentum same rehta hai.
   (b) **False** ❌. Ek ka momentum ghatta hai aur doosre ka badhta hai; sirf total same hai.
   (c) **True** ✅. Bullet ka momentum same hai, to gun ka momentum bhi same; v = p ÷ m, mass zyada to v kam.

10. **Nahi.** Yahan ek external force, floor ka friction, lamba samay tak lag raha hai. Conservation ka law sirf tab lagta hai jab net external force na ho, isliye ye law ko todta nahi, bas us par lagta hi nahi.

# Chapter Wrap-up: Force aur Laws of Motion

| Topic | Kya seekha | Sabse kaam ki baat |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Force kya hai | Force dhakka ya kheench hai jo cheez ki speed, disha ya shape badal sakta hai; SI unit newton (N) | Force dikhta nahi, uska asar dikhta hai |
| 2. Balanced aur unbalanced forces | Balanced forces ka net force 0; unbalanced forces motion badalte hain | Cheez ki motion net force se badalti hai |
| 3. Newton ka Pehla Law aur inertia | Rest ya uniform motion mein cheez tab tak waisi hi rehti hai jab tak net force na lage; mass inertia ka naap hai | Zyada mass, zyada inertia |
| 4. Friction | Do surfaces ke beech sarakne (ya uski koshish) ka virodh karne wala force; static, sliding, rolling | Aam taur par static (max) ≥ sliding > rolling; friction ek necessary evil hai |
| 5. Momentum | p = m v, ek vector; change in momentum; direction ka sign | Mass aur velocity dono se momentum badhta hai |
| 6. Newton ka Doosra Law | F = m a; F = m(v − u) ÷ t; force ek jaisa ho to halki cheez zyada accelerate hoti hai | Force = momentum badalne ki dar |
| 7. Newton ka Teesra Law | Action aur reaction barabar aur ulte, alag alag bodies par | Dono alag bodies par lagte hain, isliye cancel nahi hote |
| 8. Conservation of Momentum | Isolated system mein total momentum pehle = baad; takkar, recoil, explosion | Sign ke saath m × v jodo, aur friction jaise external force ka dhyan rakho |

### Key definitions aur units

| Quantity | Matlab | SI unit |
|---|---|---|
| Force | Dhakka ya kheench jo motion ya shape badal sakta hai | newton (N) |

| Net force | Cheez par lagne wale sabhi forces ka sign ke saath jod | newton (N) |
| Inertia | Cheez ka apni motion ki halat badalne ka virodh; mass se naapa jata hai | Alag unit nahi (mass se naapte hain, kg) |
| Friction | Surfaces ke beech motion ka virodh karne wala force | newton (N) |
| Momentum | Mass × velocity | kg m/s |
| Recoil | Gun ya toop ka peeche ki taraf jhatka, momentum conservation se | m/s (velocity) |
