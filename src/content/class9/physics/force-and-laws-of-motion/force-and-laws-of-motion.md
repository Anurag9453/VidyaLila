# Topic 1: Force Kya Hai?

Motion (Gati) chapter mein humne dekha ki cheezein **kaise** chalti hain: kitni tez, kis disha mein, kitni acceleration ke saath. Lekin ek sawal baaki reh gaya: **cheez chalti kyun hai, aur kabhi tez, kabhi dheemi ya kabhi mudti kyun hai?** Motion mein dekha kaise chalta hai, ab dekhenge **kyun**. Is kyun ka jawab hai ek chhota sa shabd: **force**.

---

## Dhalaan par atka hua thela

Gaon ki ek gali thi, aur gali ke beech ek halka sa dhalaan (slope) tha. Dhalaan ke neeche sabziyon se bhara ek **haath-thela** (handcart) khada tha: tamatar, lauki, aloo, gobhi. Thele ke maalik **Mr. Hegde** ko us dhalaan ke upar bazaar tak pahunchna tha.

Mr. Hegde ne thele ke peeche haath rakhe aur poori taaqat se **dhakka** diya. Thela ek inch bhi nahi hila. Unhone phir se zor lagaya. Kuch nahi.

Tabhi school se lautti **Trisha** wahan se guzri. Usne bag neeche rakha aur boli, "Uncle, main bhi lagati hoon!" Dono ne milkar dhakka diya. Thele ke pahiye zara sa kasmasaye, par thela ab bhi wahin ka wahin.

Itne mein gali ke teen log aur aa gaye. Ab paanchon ne ek saath dhakka lagaya. Pehle thela **hila**, phir **sarakne laga**, phir uski speed **badhti gayi**, aur dekhte hi dekhte wo dhalaan par **upar chadhne laga**.

Trisha hanspadi, "Uncle, pehle kuch hua hi nahi, ab achanak chal pada! Ye dhakke ka kya chakkar hai?" Is kahani ko hum topic ke ant mein number aur arrows ke saath solve karenge.

---

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Force (bal)** | Kisi cheez par **push (dhakka)** ya **pull (kheenchna)**: do cheezon ke beech ka interaction | Thele ko dhakelna |
| **Newton (N)** | Force ko napne ka SI unit | 1 N, 50 N, 550 N |
| **Spring balance** | Spring wala yantra jisse force napte hain | Sabzi wale ka latkane wala spring taraazu |
| **Magnitude** | Force **kitna bada** hai (number + unit) | 150 N |
| **Direction** | Force **kis taraf** lag raha hai | Dhalaan ke upar ki taraf |
| **Contact force** | Wo force jo **chhoone par** lagta hai | Haath se dhakka, rassi ka kheenchna |
| **Non-contact force** | Wo force jo **bina chhue, door se** lagta hai | Gravity, magnet ka kheenchna |
| **Muscular force** | Hamari muscles se lagne wala force | Thela dhakelna |
| **Tension** | Rassi, taar ya dhaage ke through lagne wala kheenchne wala force | Rassi se baalti kheenchna |
| **Friction (ghisaav)** | Do surfaces ke rubbing se lagne wala rokne wala force (naam abhi sirf jaan lo, aage ek poora topic hai) | Zameen par ghisatta thela |

## Part A: Force kya hai: push ya pull

Seedhi si baat: **force ek push (dhakka) ya ek pull (kheenchna) hota hai.**

- Dhakka dena, ghoonsa marna, kick lagana: **push**.
- Darwaza kheenchna, rassi kheenchna, drawer kholna: **pull**.

Dhyan do: force kabhi **akela** nahi hota. Jab Mr. Hegde thele ko dhakelte hain, to **do cheezein** involve hain: **Mr. Hegde** (jo force laga rahe hain) aur **thela** (jis par force lag raha hai). Isliye kehte hain: force do objects ke beech ka **interaction** (aapas ki kriya) hai.

### Force kya NAHI hai

Force koi **cheez** (object) nahi hai jo kisi ke andar rakhi ho. Force **energy** bhi nahi hai. Force "rakha" nahi jaata, wo bas **tab hota hai jab ek cheez doosri ko push ya pull kar rahi ho**. Jab Mr. Hegde ne haath hata liye, to thele par unka force lagna band ho gaya. Force thele mein "ruka" nahi rehta.

---

## Part B: Force ka unit: newton (N)

Length ko hum metre mein napte hain, time ko second mein. Force ko napne ka SI unit hai **newton**, jise likhte hain **N**.

> **Force ka SI unit = newton (N)**

Newton ka **formal matlab** (1 N exactly kya hota hai) hum **Topic 6** mein seekhenge. Abhi sirf ek **andaza (feel)** le lo:

> Jab tum haath par ek **100 g** ki cheez (jaise ek chhota sa apple ya chocolate ki badi bar) rakhte ho, to wo haath ko neeche ki taraf **lagbhag 1 N** ke force se dabati hai.

Ye sirf ek **feel** hai, exact definition nahi.

| Cheez | Lagbhag kitna force (feel ke liye) |
|---|---|
| Ek chhota apple (100 g) ka neeche ki taraf kheenchav | lagbhag 1 N |
| Ek kitaab (lagbhag 300 g) | lagbhag 3 N |
| Aadhe kilo ki cheeni ka packet | lagbhag 5 N |
| Mr. Hegde ka thele par akela dhakka (is kahani mein) | 150 N |

### N aur kg ko mat milana

**kg** matlab cheez mein **kitna maal** hai (mass). **N** matlab **force** kitna hai. Dono alag cheezein hain. "Mera force 5 kg hai" galat hai, "mera force 5 N hai" sahi hai. Upar ki table mein jo "100 g ka 1 N" feel diya hai, wo ek **tulna** hai, dono ek jaisi cheezein nahi.

## Part C: Spring balance se force napna

Force ko napne ke liye **spring balance** hota hai. Isme ek **spring** hoti hai jisse ek hook jura hota hai. Hook par jitna zyada force lagao, spring utni zyada **khinchti** hai, aur ek pointer scale par reading dikhata hai.

```
      [ ring ]
         !!
   +-----++-----+
   + 0 -- ---- +
   + 1 -- ---- +
   + 2 -- ---- +
   + 3 -- ---- +      <-- pointer yahan, 3 N
   + 4 -- ---- +
   + 5 -- ---- +
   +-----++-----+
         !!   spring andar
       [hook]
         !!
      [ bag ]
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Ek spring balance jiski scale par har 1 N par nishaan hai. Hook se laga bag spring ko kheenchta hai, pointer 3 N par ruka hai.

**Reading kaise padhein:**

1. Pehle dekho ki bina kuch latkaye pointer **0** par hai ya nahi.
2. Hook par cheez latkao ya hook ko kheencho.
3. Pointer jis nishaan par ruke, wahi **force ki reading** hai, unit **N** ke saath.

**Example 1:** Ek spring balance mein har 1 N par nishaan hai aur pointer 4th nishaan par ruka.

- Reading = **4 N**

**Example 2:** Pointer 2nd aur 3rd nishaan ke theek **beech** mein hai (aadhe raaste).

- Reading = 2 N + 0.5 N = **2.5 N**

**Example 3:** Ek bag latkane par pointer 5 N par ruka. Ab doosra, bilkul same bag bhi saath mein latka diya (dono bag milakar).

- Force 2 gunaa ho gaya: 5 N × 2 = **10 N**

**Jaanch:** 5 N + 5 N = 10 N ✅

Spring balance ka asli kaam dekho: jitna zyada force, utni zyada spring khinchti hai. Isi wajah se isse force napne ke liye use kiya ja sakta hai.

## Part D: Force mein sirf "kitna" nahi, "kis taraf" bhi hota hai

Agar tumse koi kahe "dhakka lagao, 100 N ka", to tum poochoge, "Kis taraf?" Kyunki **force ka asar sirf is par nahi ki kitna hai, balki is par bhi ki kis taraf lag raha hai.**

- Thele ko **aage** dhakelo to thela aage jayega.
- Thele ko **peeche** dhakelo to peeche jayega.

Isliye force ke do hisse hote hain:

> **Force = magnitude (kitna) + direction (kis taraf)**

Jo cheez magnitude **aur** direction dono rakhti hai, use **vector** kehte hain. (Motion mein displacement aur velocity bhi vector the.) Force bhi ek **vector** hai. Ye Class 9 level ki baat hai, isse zyada aage ki class mein aayegi.

### Force ko arrow se dikhana

Force ko hum **arrow (teer)** se dikhate hain:

- Arrow ki **nok (tip)** force ki **disha** batati hai.
- Arrow ki **lambai** force ki **magnitude** batati hai: **lamba arrow = bada force**.
- Arrow wahan se shuru karte hain jahan se force lag raha hai.

Isme ek **scale** bhi chuni jaati hai, jaise "1 dabba = 50 N".

```
Scale: 1 dabba [ ] = 50 N

 Thele par force A:   --[ ][ ][ ]-->        (3 dabbe = 150 N, aage)
 Thele par force B:   --[ ]-->              (1 dabba = 50 N, aage)
 Thele par force C:   <--[ ][ ]--           (2 dabbe = 100 N, peeche)
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Teen forces ko arrows se dikhaya hai: A sabse lamba, to sabse bada (150 N); B sabse chhota (50 N); C ka arrow ulti taraf hai, to uski disha peeche ki hai (100 N).

**Example 4:** Scale 1 dabba = 50 N. Ek arrow 4 dabbe lamba hai.

- Force = 4 × 50 N = **200 N**

**Jaanch:** 200 N ÷ 50 N = 4 dabbe ✅

**Example 5:** Arrow P 2 dabbe ka hai aur arrow Q 5 dabbe ka. Kaun sa force bada hai, aur kitna?

- P = 2 × 50 N = 100 N
- Q = 5 × 50 N = 250 N
- Q bada hai, **250 N − 100 N = 150 N** zyada.

**Jaanch:** 100 N + 150 N = 250 N ✅

Is scale ke baare mein yaad rakho: arrow ki lambai ka matlab sirf **usi picture ki scale** ke hisaab se hota hai.

## Part E: Force kya-kya kar sakta hai

Force dikhta nahi, par uska **asar** dikhta hai. Force ye saare kaam kar sakta hai:

| Force ka asar | Example |
|---|---|
| **Ruki cheez ko chalana** (start motion) | Thele ka hilna jab sab ne milkar dhakka diya |
| **Chalti cheez ko rokna** (stop motion) | Goalkeeper ka football ko pakadna |
| **Speed badhana** | Cycle ke paidal par zor lagana |
| **Speed ghatana** | Cycle ke brake lagana |
| **Disha badalna** | Cricket bat se ball ko ghumaana |
| **Shape ya size badalna** | Clay ki gend dabana, spring kheenchna, rubber band kheenchna |

Kuch aur examples dekho:

- **Clay ki gend:** haath se dabao to wo chapti ho jaati hai (shape badli).
- **Spring:** kheencho to lambi, dabao to chhoti (size badla).
- **Rubber band:** kheencho to lambi ho jaati hai, chhodo to wapas.

Ek cheez par ek force **ek ya zyada** asar bhi kar sakta hai. Jaise cricket bat ball ko rokta bhi hai aur nayi disha mein bhejta bhi hai.

---

## Part F: Force ke do pariwar: contact aur non-contact

**Contact forces (chhoone par lagne wale):**

| Force | Matlab | Everyday example |
|---|---|---|
| **Muscular force** | Muscles se lagne wala force | Thela dhakelna, bhaari bag uthana |
| **Friction (ghisaav)** | Do surfaces ke rubbing se lagne wala force | Zameen par ghisatta hua thela (naam ki jaankari, poora topic aage) |
| **Tension** | Rassi/taar ke through kheenchna | Kuen se baalti kheenchna |

**Non-contact forces (bina chhue, door se lagne wale):**

| Force | Matlab | Everyday example |
|---|---|---|
| **Gravitational force (gravity)** | Prithvi ka cheezon ko neeche kheenchna | Seb ka ped se neeche girna |
| **Magnetic force** | Magnet ka lohe ko kheenchna | Fridge par magnet ka chipakna |
| **Electrostatic force** | Charge wali cheezon ke beech ka force | Plastic ka scale baalon par ghisne ke baad kagaz ke tukde kheenchna |

Mr. Hegde ka haath thele ko **chhu raha** tha, to ye **contact** force (muscular force) tha. Lekin thele ko neeche zameen par tikaye rakhne wala gravity ka kheenchav **bina chhue** lag raha tha: wo non-contact force hai.

## Part G: Jab force lage par cheez na hile

Kahani mein ek ajeeb baat dekhi: pehle Mr. Hegde ne dhakka diya, thela nahi hila. Trisha ne bhi dhakka diya, tab bhi lagbhag nahi hila. **To kya force lagne par cheez ka hilna zaroori hai? Nahi.**

Ek force ruki cheez par lag sakta hai aur phir bhi wo cheez **hile bina rahe**. Ye kyun hota hai? Iska jawab agle topic mein hai. Abhi bas itna yaad rakho: **cheez par sirf ek hi force nahi lag raha hota, kai force ek saath lag rahe hote hain.** Ek force ka asar hamesha dikhe, ye zaroori nahi.

---

## Kahani ka hal: Mr. Hegde ka thela

Ab apni kahani ko arrows aur numbers ke saath dekhte hain. Scale: **1 dabba = 50 N**.

| Kaun | Force (dhakka) | Arrow ke dabbe |
|---|---|---|
| Mr. Hegde | 150 N | 3 |
| Trisha | 100 N | 2 |
| Gali ka pehla aadmi | 100 N | 2 |
| Gali ka doosra aadmi | 100 N | 2 |
| Gali ka teesra aadmi | 100 N | 2 |

```
Dhalaan ke upar -->

Mr. Hegde:  -->[ ][ ][ ]-->
Trisha:     -->[ ][ ]-->
Aadmi 1:    -->[ ][ ]-->
Aadmi 2:    -->[ ][ ]-->
Aadmi 3:    -->[ ][ ]-->
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Thele ko dhakelne wale paanchon forces ek hi taraf (dhalaan ke upar) hain. Mr. Hegde ka arrow sabse lamba hai (150 N); baaki sab 100 N ke hain.

Saare dhakke ek hi taraf hain, to sabka kul asar milkar:

- Mr. Hegde + Trisha = 150 N + 100 N = **250 N**
- Teeno aadmi = 100 N + 100 N + 100 N = **300 N**
- Paanchon milkar = 250 N + 300 N = **550 N**

**Jaanch:** arrows ke dabbe: 3 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 11 dabbe, aur 11 × 50 N = 550 N ✅

Is kahani mein force ke saare asar dikh gaye:

1. Jab saare milkar lage, thela **ruka tha se chalne laga** (motion shuru).
2. Phir uski **speed badhti gayi** (speed up).
3. Agar Mr. Hegde dhalaan ke upar pahunch kar thele ko rokte, to force se **motion rukti** (stop).

Aur ek baat: dhakka dene wale logon ke saath thele ke bhaari hone ka bhi asar tha (isko hum aage seekhenge). Abhi bas itna samajh lo ki **zyada force lagao to cheez zyada aasaani se hilti hai**, aur Trisha ka sawal "dhakke ka kya chakkar hai" ka pehla jawab: dhakka ek force hai, aur force hi cheez ko hilata, tez karta ya rokta hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Force ek cheez hai jo kisi ke andar rehti hai."**
Force object nahi hai, aur energy bhi nahi. Wo ek **push ya pull** hai jo ek cheez doosri par lagati hai. Dhakka band, force band.

**Galti 2: "Cheez ko chalte rehne ke liye force zaroori hai."**
Ye baat itni seedhi nahi hai. Cycle chalate waqt paidal rokne par bhi cycle thodi door chalti rehti hai! Iska sahi jawab hum **Topic 3** mein dekhenge. Abhi ke liye is baat par pakka mat ho jana.

**Galti 3: "Force mein sirf kitna hota hai, disha nahi."**
Force mein **disha bhi** hoti hai. 100 N aage aur 100 N peeche ke asar ulte hote hain, bhale hi unki magnitude barabar hai.

**Galti 4: "N aur kg ek hi cheez hain."**
N force ka unit hai, kg mass ka. "5 kg ka force" galat hai.

**Galti 5: "Arrow ki lambai kuch bhi ho sakti hai."**
Arrow ki lambai scale ke hisaab se force ke barabar honi chahiye. Lamba arrow = bada force.

## Quick Recap

- **Force** ek **push ya pull** hai, aur do cheezon ke beech ka interaction hai.
- Force ka SI unit **newton (N)** hai. 100 g ki cheez ka kheenchav **lagbhag 1 N** (sirf feel; exact definition Topic 6 mein).
- Force ko **spring balance** se napte hain.
- Force mein **magnitude aur direction** dono hote hain: ye ek **vector** hai.
- Force ko **arrow** se dikhate hain: **lamba arrow = bada force**, nok = disha.
- Force ye kar sakta hai: cheez ko **chalana, rokna, tez karna, dheema karna, disha badalna, shape/size badalna**.
- **Contact forces:** muscular force, friction, tension. **Non-contact forces:** gravity, magnetic force, electrostatic force.
- Force lagne par cheez ka **hilna zaroori nahi**, kyunki ek cheez par kai forces ek saath lag sakte hain.

## Practice Questions

**Q1.** Say whether each action is a push or a pull: (a) opening a drawer, (b) kicking a football, (c) hauling a bucket out of a well with a rope, (d) pressing a doorbell, (e) pulling a nail out of a wall.

**Q2.** Name the effect of the force in each situation (start motion, stop motion, speed up, slow down, change direction, or change shape/size):
(a) A goalkeeper catches a moving football.
(b) A cyclist pedals harder on a straight road.
(c) A batter hits a ball that was moving toward him, and it goes the other way.
(d) A potter presses a lump of clay.
(e) A rubber band is stretched.
(f) A cyclist applies the brakes.
(g) A stationary toy car is pushed with a finger.
(h) A spring is squeezed between two palms.
(i) A boy kicks a rolling ball sideways.
(j) A sprinter pushes the blocks and starts running.

**Q3.** On a diagram with the scale 1 box = 50 N, force A is drawn 4 boxes long and force B is 7 boxes long. Find the value of each force, and say which is greater and by how much.

**Q4.** A spring balance has marks every 1 N. In three trials the pointer stopped (a) at the 6th mark, (b) halfway between the 1st and the 2nd mark, (c) at the 9th mark. Write each reading with its unit.

**Q5.** Name one contact force and one non-contact force, and give one everyday example of each.

**Q6.** Mr. Hegde pushes a cart with 150 N and Trisha pushes it with 100 N in the same direction. Draw the arrows with the scale 1 box = 50 N. How many boxes long is each? What is the total push?

**Q7.** True or false: (a) A force can be stored inside an object. (b) The unit of force is the kilogram. (c) A longer arrow means a larger force. (d) A force always makes a body move.

**Q8.** A spring balance reads 2 N when one bag is hung from it. What will it read if two identical bags are hung together?

**Q9.** A student says, "The force on the cart is 10 kg." What is wrong with his statement, and what should he say?

**Q10.** A rubber band is pulled and stretched. Name the effect of the force and say whether it is a contact or non-contact force.

### Answers

**A1.** (a) pull, (b) push, (c) pull, (d) push, (e) pull.

**A2.**
(a) Stop motion (ruk gayi).
(b) Speed up (speed badhi).
(c) Change of direction (disha badli; speed bhi badal sakti hai).
(d) Change of shape.
(e) Change of size (aur shape).
(f) Slow down.
(g) Start motion.
(h) Change of size/shape (spring chhoti hui).
(i) Change of direction.
(j) Start motion.

**A3.** A = 4 × 50 N = **200 N**. B = 7 × 50 N = **350 N**. B bada hai, 350 N − 200 N = **150 N** zyada.
**Jaanch:** 200 N + 150 N = 350 N ✅

**A4.** (a) **6 N**. (b) 1 N aur 2 N ke beech, to 1 N + 0.5 N = **1.5 N**. (c) **9 N**.

**A5.** Contact force: friction (zameen par ghisatta hua thela) ya muscular force (dhakka dena). Non-contact force: gravity (seb ka neeche girna) ya magnetic force (fridge par magnet).

**A6.** Mr. Hegde: 150 N ÷ 50 N = **3 boxes**. Trisha: 100 N ÷ 50 N = **2 boxes**. Dono arrows ek hi disha mein. Kul push = 150 N + 100 N = **250 N** (3 + 2 = 5 boxes, 5 × 50 N = 250 N ✅).

**A7.** (a) False ❌ (force push/pull hota hai, store nahi hota). (b) False ❌ (unit newton hai; kg mass ka unit hai). (c) True ✅. (d) False ❌ (ek force lagne par bhi cheez hil nahi sakti, kyunki doosre forces bhi ho sakte hain).

**A8.** Do identical bag matlab 2 gunaa force: 2 N × 2 = **4 N**. **Jaanch:** 2 N + 2 N = 4 N ✅

**A9.** Force ko kg mein nahi napte; kg **mass** ka unit hai. Student ko force newton (N) mein batana chahiye, jaise "10 N".

**A10.** Effect: **shape/size badalna** (rubber band lambi hui). Kheenchne wala haath rubber band ko **chhoo raha** hai, to ye **contact force** (muscular force) hai.
