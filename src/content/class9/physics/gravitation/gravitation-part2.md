# Topic 2: Universal Law of Gravitation

Pichhle topic mein humne seekha ki gravity ek **attractive force** hai jo **koi bhi do cheezon ke beech** lagta hai jinme mass hai, aur Earth hamein apne **centre** ki taraf kheenchti hai. Humne ye bhi dekha ki chhoti cheezon ke beech ye force bahut hi **tiny** hota hai. Ab sawal hai: **kitna** tiny? Aur ye force kin cheezon par nirbhar karta hai: mass par, doori par, ya dono par? Newton ne iska ek seedha formula diya, jise **Universal Law of Gravitation** kehte hain. Aaj hum use samjhenge aur khud number nikalenge.

---

## Science fair mein do bhaari gendein

School ke science fair mein physics ki teacher Ms. Bose aur unka student Parth ek model dikha rahe the. Do bhaari, seese (lead) jaisi gendein, har ek **10 kg** ki, ek-doosre ke paas patli dori se latak rahi thi. Dono ke **centre** ke beech doori sirf **0.2 m** thi.

Parth ne aane-jaane walon se kaha, "Newton ke hisaab se ye dono gendein ek-doosre ko **kheench** rahi hain."

Ek bachhe ne gaur se dekha aur bola, "Par ye to bilkul hil hi nahi rahi!"

Ms. Bose muskuraayin, "Haan, kyunki ye force itna **chhota** hai ki naap-ne ke liye bahut sensitive machine chahiye. Aankhon se kuch nahi dikhega."

Tab Parth ne apni sabse zaroori baat poochhi, "Madam, agar do bhaari gendein bhi ek-doosre ko mushkil se kheench paati hain, to **Earth hamein itni zor se kyun kheenchti hai** ki hum udte nahi, seedhe neeche aate hain?"

Ms. Bose ne kaha, "Bahut accha sawal, Parth. Iska jawab Newton ke formula mein chhupa hai. Is kahani ko hum topic ke beech mein number ke saath solve karenge."

## Part A: Newton ka niyam shabdon mein aur formula mein

Newton ne dekha ki force teen baaton par nirbhar karta hai: dono ke **mass**, aur unke beech ki **doori**. Niyam ye hai:

> **Brahmaand ki har cheez, har doosri cheez ko ek force se kheenchti hai. Ye force dono ke masses ke gunanfal ke **anupaati (proportional)** hota hai aur unke centres ke beech ki doori ke **varg (square)** ke **ulte anupaat (inverse proportion)** mein hota hai.**

Ise formula mein likhte hain:

> **F = G m₁ m₂ ÷ r²**

Har symbol ka matlab:

| Symbol | Matlab | SI unit |
|---|---|---|
| **F** | Gravitational force (dono cheezein ek-doosre ko isi force se kheenchti hain) | newton (N) |
| **G** | Universal gravitational constant | N m²/kg² |
| **m₁** | Pehli cheez ka mass | kg |
| **m₂** | Doosri cheez ka mass | kg |
| **r** | Dono cheezon ke **centres** ke beech doori | m |

Aaram se padho: "m₁ m₂" ka matlab **m₁ × m₂** (dono masses ka gunanfal), aur "r²" ka matlab **r × r**.

**Ye formula aisa kyun?** Teen seedhi baatein:
- Mass zyada, to force zyada: isliye m₁ m₂ **upar** (numerator) mein hai.
- Doori zyada, to force kam: isliye r² **neeche** (denominator) mein hai.
- G ek number hai jo "kitna zyada" aur "kitna kam" ko actual newton mein badalta hai.

### G ka matlab aur uska unit

**G** ki value hai:

> **G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg²**

Ye wahi powers of ten hain jo humne [scientific notation wale topic](../measurement-and-units/measurement-and-units-part2.md) mein dekhe the: 10⁻¹¹ ka matlab hai 0.00000000001, yani bahut hi chhota number.

**Unit kahan se aaya?** Formula ko ulta karke dekho: F = G m₁ m₂ ÷ r². Isse **G = F r² ÷ (m₁ m₂)**. Unit mein: N × m² ÷ (kg × kg) = **N m²/kg²**. Isliye G ke saath ye unit **likhna zaroori** hai.

**"Universal" kyun?** Kyunki G ki value **har jagah, har do cheezon ke liye ek hi hai**: chaahe do gendein hon, Earth aur Moon hon, ya door ke do taare. Na wo cheezon ke naam par badalti hai, na jagah par.

### G itna chhota kyun hai? Ek table se samjho

G chhota hai isliye hum roz ki cheezon ke beech gravity **mehsoos nahi** karte. Dekho, 1 m ki doori par alag masses ke beech force kitna aata hai (F = G m₁ m₂ ÷ r², G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg²):

| Do cheezein | m₁ × m₂ | Force (r = 1 m) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 kg aur 1 kg | 1 kg² | 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N |
| 60 kg aur 70 kg (do log) | 4200 kg² | 2.8 × 10⁻⁷ N |
| 1000 kg aur 1000 kg (do car) | 10⁶ kg² | 6.67 × 10⁻⁵ N |

Ye saare force **bahut chhote** hain. Gravity ko mehsoos karne ke liye kam se kam **ek cheez ka mass bahut bada** chahiye: jaise Earth (6 × 10²⁴ kg). Yehi Parth ke sawal ka jawab hai: Earth ka mass itna bada hai ki use gravity kaafi zor ka milti hai. Ye aage number se pakka karenge.

## Part B: Inverse square: doori aur mass badalne par force kaise badalta hai

Pehle ek **base case** lete hain: do cheezein, force **F**. Ab ek-ek cheez badalte hain aur dekhte hain force kya hota hai. (Koi proof nahi: bas formula mein number daal kar dekhna hai.)

| Kya badla | Formula mein asar | Naya force |
|---|---|---|
| Kuch nahi (base) | F = G m₁ m₂ ÷ r² | **F** |
| Doori 2 guna (2r) | r² ki jagah (2r)² = 4r² | **F ÷ 4** |
| Doori 3 guna (3r) | r² ki jagah (3r)² = 9r² | **F ÷ 9** |
| Doori aadhi (r ÷ 2) | r² ki jagah r² ÷ 4 | **4F** |
| Ek mass 2 guna (2m₁) | numerator 2 guna | **2F** |
| Dono masses 2 guna | numerator 2 × 2 = 4 guna | **4F** |
| m₁ 2 guna aur m₂ 3 guna | numerator 2 × 3 = 6 guna | **6F** |
| Dono masses 2 guna aur doori 2 guna | numerator 4 guna, denominator 4 guna | **F** (koi badlav nahi) |

Ye hi **inverse square** ka asli matlab hai: doori 2 guna karo to force 2 guna **nahi** ghat-ta, balki 2² = 4 guna ghat-ta hai. Doori 3 guna karo to 3² = 9 guna ghat-ta hai.

Ek figure se dekhte hain ki doori badhne par force kaise ghat-ta hai (base force = 1 unit, jab doori = r):

```
 Doori      Force (base force ka hissa)

   r        ################################  1
  2r        ########                          1/4
  3r        ###                               1/9
  4r        ##                                1/16
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Doori r, 2r, 3r, 4r hone par force 1, 1/4, 1/9, 1/16 rah jata hai. Bar ki lambai force dikhati hai.

## Part C: Newton ka teesra niyam yahan bhi: dono par barabar force

Jab Earth seb ko neeche kheenchti hai, to **seb bhi Earth ko upar kheenchta hai**: [action-reaction wale topic](../force-and-laws-of-motion/force-and-laws-of-motion-part7.md) mein dekha tha ki force hamesha **jode** mein aate hain. Gravity mein bhi yahi hota hai:

> Cheez 1 par force (cheez 2 ki taraf) = Cheez 2 par force (cheez 1 ki taraf). **Size barabar, disha ulti.**

Formula mein dekho: F = G m₁ m₂ ÷ r². Isme m₁ aur m₂ ki jagah badal do, to bhi F wahi aata hai. To **dono par F barabar** hi hoga. Bhaari wali cheez halke wale ko zyada zor se **nahi** kheenchti.

**Phir bhi seb neeche girta hai aur Earth hilti kyun nahi?** Kyunki force barabar hai par **acceleration** alag hai. [F = m a wale topic](../force-and-laws-of-motion/force-and-laws-of-motion-part6.md) se a = F ÷ m: wahi force **chhote mass** par bahut bada acceleration deta hai aur **bade mass** par bahut chhota.

```
   Seb par force F (neeche, Earth ki taraf)

                 o  seb
                 |
                 v
        ---------------------
       (       Earth         )
        ---------------------
                 ^
                 |
   Earth par force F (upar, seb ki taraf)
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Seb aur Earth ek-doosre ko barabar size ke force F se kheenchte hain, disha ulti. Seb (kam mass) zyada accelerate hota hai, Earth (bahut zyada mass) lagbhag nahi.

Ye number mein dekhte hain (Example 9 mein).

## Part D: r hamesha centre se centre tak

Formula ka r **do cheezon ke centres ke beech** ki doori hai, na ki unki surface ke beech ki.

- Do chhoti gendein: r = centre se centre (**gap + dono ki radius**).
- **Earth aur koi cheez:** r Earth ke **centre** se naapa jata hai. Earth ki radius **R = 6.4 × 10⁶ m** hai. Agar seb Earth ki surface par hai, to r = R = 6.4 × 10⁶ m. Agar seb surface se 6.4 × 10⁶ m **upar** ho, to r = R + R = 2R.

```
   B  *   seb surface se R upar     (r = 2R)
      |
      |  R
      |
   A  *   seb surface par           (r = R)
      |
      |  R
      |
      +   Earth ka centre
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Seb A surface par ho to r = R (centre se surface tak). Seb B surface se R upar ho to r = R + R = 2R, kyunki r hamesha Earth ke centre se naapa jata hai.

## Part E: Solved examples

Hum G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg² lenge. Har step mein unit likhenge.

### Example 1: 1 kg aur 1 kg, 1 m door

**m₁ = 1 kg, m₂ = 1 kg, r = 1 m.**

- F = G m₁ m₂ ÷ r²
- F = (6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg²) × (1 kg) × (1 kg) ÷ (1 m)²
- F = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m² ÷ 1 m² = **6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N**

Dhyan do: jab m₁ = m₂ = 1 kg aur r = 1 m ho, to F ki value **G ke barabar** aa jati hai. Yahi G ka asli matlab hai: **1 kg aur 1 kg ke beech, 1 m ki doori par jitna force lagta hai, wahi G (ginti mein) hai.**

**Jaanch:** F = G × 1 × 1 ÷ 1 = G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N ✅ (Unit: N m²/kg² × kg² ÷ m² = N ✅)

### Example 2: 10 kg aur 20 kg, 0.5 m door

**m₁ = 10 kg, m₂ = 20 kg, r = 0.5 m.**

- m₁ m₂ = 10 kg × 20 kg = 200 kg²
- r² = (0.5 m)² = 0.25 m²
- F = (6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg²) × (200 kg²) ÷ (0.25 m²)
- F = (6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ × 200 ÷ 0.25) N = (6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ × 800) N
- 6.67 × 800 = 5336, to F = 5336 × 10⁻¹¹ N = **5.336 × 10⁻⁸ N ≈ 5.3 × 10⁻⁸ N**

**Jaanch:** 6.67e-11 × 200 ÷ 0.25 = 5.336e-8 ✅. Ye 1 kg-1 kg wale force (6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N) se kaafi bada hai, jo sahi hai: masses 200 guna aur doori aadhi (force 4 guna), to 200 × 4 = 800 guna: 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ × 800 = 5.336 × 10⁻⁸ ✅

### Example 3: 60 kg aur 70 kg ke do log, 1 m door

**m₁ = 60 kg, m₂ = 70 kg, r = 1 m.**

- m₁ m₂ = 60 kg × 70 kg = 4200 kg²
- r² = (1 m)² = 1 m²
- F = (6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg²) × (4200 kg²) ÷ (1 m²)
- 6.67 × 4200 = 28014, to F = 28014 × 10⁻¹¹ N = 2.8014 × 10⁻⁷ N ≈ **2.8 × 10⁻⁷ N**

**Jaanch:** 6.67e-11 × 4200 = 2.8014e-7 ✅. Ye 0.00000028 N hai: itna kam ki kisi ko pata hi nahi chalta ki dono ek-doosre ko kheench rahe hain.

### Example 4: Earth aur Moon

**M_E = 6 × 10²⁴ kg, M_M = 7.4 × 10²² kg, r = 3.84 × 10⁸ m.**

Pehle gunanfal aur r²:

- M_E × M_M = (6 × 10²⁴ kg) × (7.4 × 10²² kg) = (6 × 7.4) × 10²⁴⁺²² kg² = 44.4 × 10⁴⁶ kg² = **4.44 × 10⁴⁷ kg²**
- r² = (3.84 × 10⁸ m)² = (3.84)² × 10¹⁶ m² = 14.7456 × 10¹⁶ m² = **1.47456 × 10¹⁷ m²**

Ab force:

- F = (6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg²) × (4.44 × 10⁴⁷ kg²) ÷ (1.47456 × 10¹⁷ m²)
- Upar: 6.67 × 4.44 = 29.615, powers: 10⁻¹¹ × 10⁴⁷ = 10³⁶. To upar = 29.615 × 10³⁶ = 2.9615 × 10³⁷
- F = 2.9615 × 10³⁷ ÷ 1.47456 × 10¹⁷ = 2.008 × 10²⁰ N ≈ **2 × 10²⁰ N**

**Jaanch:** Python se: 6.67e-11 × 6e24 × 7.4e22 ÷ (3.84e8)² = 2.008e20 N ✅. Powers of ten ka haath: 10⁻¹¹ × 10⁴⁷ ÷ 10¹⁷ = 10⁻¹¹⁺⁴⁷⁻¹⁷ = 10¹⁹ aur 6.67 × 4.44 ÷ 1.47456 = 20.08, to 20.08 × 10¹⁹ = 2.008 × 10²⁰ ✅

Ye force bahut bada hai (lagbhag 2 × 10²⁰ N), aur isi se Moon Earth ke aas-paas ghoomta rehta hai. (Iski poori baat Topic 6 mein.)

### Example 5: Kahani ka jawab: do gendein

Science fair ki dono gendein: **m₁ = 10 kg, m₂ = 10 kg, r = 0.2 m.**

- m₁ m₂ = 10 kg × 10 kg = 100 kg²
- r² = (0.2 m)² = 0.04 m²
- F = (6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg²) × (100 kg²) ÷ (0.04 m²)
- F = 6.67 × 10⁻⁹ N m² ÷ 0.04 m² = (6.67 ÷ 0.04) × 10⁻⁹ N = 166.75 × 10⁻⁹ N = **1.67 × 10⁻⁷ N**

**Jaanch:** 6.67e-11 × 100 ÷ 0.04 = 1.6675e-7 ✅

Ye sirf 0.000000167 N hai. Isliye gendein hilti hui nahi dikhi.

Ab Parth ke sawal ka jawab. Wahi 10 kg ki ball, **Earth ki surface par**: M_E = 6 × 10²⁴ kg, r = R = 6.4 × 10⁶ m.

- M_E m = (6 × 10²⁴ kg) × (10 kg) = 6 × 10²⁵ kg²
- R² = (6.4 × 10⁶ m)² = 40.96 × 10¹² m² = 4.096 × 10¹³ m²
- F = (6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg²) × (6 × 10²⁵ kg²) ÷ (4.096 × 10¹³ m²)
- Upar: 6.67 × 6 = 40.02, powers: 10⁻¹¹ × 10²⁵ = 10¹⁴, to 40.02 × 10¹⁴ = 4.002 × 10¹⁵
- F = 4.002 × 10¹⁵ ÷ 4.096 × 10¹³ = 0.977 × 10² N = **97.7 N** (lagbhag 98 N)

**Jaanch:** Python: 6.67e-11 × 6e24 × 10 ÷ (6.4e6)² = 97.7 N ✅

Earth ka force 97.7 N, dusri ball ka sirf 1.67 × 10⁻⁷ N: **97.7 ÷ 1.67 × 10⁻⁷ ≈ 5.9 × 10⁸**, yani lagbhag **59 crore guna**. Wajah: Earth ka mass 6 × 10²⁴ kg hai, jo ek 10 kg ki ball se **bahut** bada hai.

Ms. Bose ne Parth se kaha, "Dekha? Gravity har cheez ke beech hai, par jab ek taraf Earth jaisa **bhaari** pind ho tabhi uska asar saaf dikhta hai."

### Example 6: Doori badalne par force (ratio)

Science fair ki dono gendein ka force **1.67 × 10⁻⁷ N** hai jab r = 0.2 m. Agar dono ko door karke r = 0.4 m kar den, to naya force?

Doori **2 guna** hui, to force **¼** hona chahiye (Part B ki table).

- F_naya = 1.6675 × 10⁻⁷ N ÷ 4 = **4.17 × 10⁻⁸ N**

Seedha formula se: F = (6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg²) × (100 kg²) ÷ (0.4 m)² = 6.67 × 10⁻⁹ ÷ 0.16 N = 4.169 × 10⁻⁸ N ✅

**Jaanch:** Agar r = 0.1 m kar den (aadhi doori), to F = 6.67 × 10⁻⁹ ÷ 0.01 = 6.67 × 10⁻⁷ N, jo 1.6675 × 10⁻⁷ N ka **4 guna** hai ✅

### Example 7: Ratio: mass aur doori dono badle

Do cheezon ke beech force F hai. Agar m₁ ko **2 guna**, m₂ ko **3 guna** aur doori ko **2 guna** kar den, to naya force?

- Numerator: 2 × 3 = **6 guna**
- Denominator: (2)² = **4 guna**
- Naya force = F × 6 ÷ 4 = **1.5 F**

**Jaanch:** Maan lo F = 4 N. Naya = 4 × 6 ÷ 4 = 6 N, aur 1.5 × 4 = 6 N ✅

### Example 8: Ek hi Earth, par 2R aur 3R par force

Ek object Earth ke centre se **2R** par hai aur ek hi object doosri jagah centre se **3R** par. Pehli jagah ka force doosri jagah ke force se kitna guna zyada?

- F ∝ 1 ÷ r²
- F₂R ÷ F₃R = (1 ÷ (2R)²) ÷ (1 ÷ (3R)²) = (3R)² ÷ (2R)² = 9R² ÷ 4R² = **9 ÷ 4 = 2.25**

Yani 2R par force 3R ke force se **2.25 guna** zyada hai.

**Jaanch:** Ek 1 kg ka object lo (G M_E = 6.67e-11 × 6e24 = 4.002e14 N m²/kg): F₂R = 4.002e14 ÷ (1.28e7)² = 2.44 N, F₃R = 4.002e14 ÷ (1.92e7)² = 1.09 N, aur 2.44 ÷ 1.09 = 2.25 ✅

Dhyan do: R ka koi value chahiye hi nahi tha: ratio mein R kat gaya.

### Example 9: Seb aur Earth ka acceleration

Ek seb ka mass **0.2 kg** hai aur wo Earth ki surface par hai (M_E = 6 × 10²⁴ kg, R = 6.4 × 10⁶ m).

**Step 1: dono par force**

- F = (6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg²) × (6 × 10²⁴ kg) × (0.2 kg) ÷ (6.4 × 10⁶ m)²
- Upar: 6.67 × 6 × 0.2 = 8.004, powers: 10⁻¹¹ × 10²⁴ = 10¹³, to 8.004 × 10¹³ N m²
- Neeche: 4.096 × 10¹³ m²
- F = 8.004 × 10¹³ ÷ 4.096 × 10¹³ = **1.95 N**

Ye force **seb par** bhi hai (Earth ki taraf) aur **Earth par** bhi hai (seb ki taraf): barabar, 1.95 N.

**Step 2: seb ka acceleration** (a = F ÷ m)

- a_seb = 1.95 N ÷ 0.2 kg = **9.77 m/s² ≈ 9.8 m/s²**

**Step 3: Earth ka acceleration**

- a_Earth = F ÷ M_E = 1.95 N ÷ (6 × 10²⁴ kg) = (1.954 ÷ 6) × 10⁻²⁴ m/s² = **3.3 × 10⁻²⁵ m/s²** (lagbhag)

**Jaanch:** Python: 1.954 ÷ 0.2 = 9.77 ✅; 1.954 ÷ 6e24 = 3.26e-25 ≈ 3.3e-25 ✅. Seb ka acceleration Earth ke acceleration se **3 × 10²⁵ guna** zyada hai (9.77 ÷ 3.26 × 10⁻²⁵ ≈ 3 × 10²⁵), isliye hume sirf seb girta dikhta hai.

(Wahi ≈ 9.8 m/s² jo seb ke liye aaya, Earth ki surface par **har** chhoti cheez ke liye aayega. Is baat par Topic 3 mein aayenge.)

### Example 10: Earth ki surface par 1 kg par force (Topic 3 ka pul)

1 kg ki cheez, Earth ki surface par: m = 1 kg, M_E = 6 × 10²⁴ kg, r = R = 6.4 × 10⁶ m.

- F = G M_E m ÷ R²
- F = (6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg²) × (6 × 10²⁴ kg) × (1 kg) ÷ (6.4 × 10⁶ m)²
- Upar: 6.67 × 6 = 40.02, powers: 10⁻¹¹ × 10²⁴ = 10¹³, to 40.02 × 10¹³ = 4.002 × 10¹⁴ N m²
- Neeche: 4.096 × 10¹³ m²
- F = 4.002 × 10¹⁴ ÷ 4.096 × 10¹³ = 0.977 × 10¹ N = **9.77 N ≈ 9.8 N**

**Jaanch:** 6.67e-11 × 6e24 × 1 ÷ (6.4e6)² = 9.77 N ✅. Example 9 se bhi milta hai: 0.2 kg par 1.95 N, to 1 kg par 1.95 ÷ 0.2 = 9.77 N ✅

Dekho: **1 kg par ~9.8 N, 2 kg par ~19.5 N, 0.2 kg par ~1.95 N**: force mass ke saath badhta hai, aur **force ÷ mass** har baar ek hi number (~9.8) aata hai. Isse hi aage g nikalenge.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Surface ke beech ki doori ko r maan lena**

Do 10 kg ki gendein jinki radius 0.1 m hai, surface se surface **0.1 m** door hain. Centres ke beech doori = 0.1 + 0.1 + 0.1 = **0.3 m** hai, 0.1 m nahi. Galat r = 0.1 se F = 6.67 × 10⁻⁷ N aata, sahi r = 0.3 se F = 6.67 × 10⁻⁹ ÷ 0.09 = 7.4 × 10⁻⁸ N. Dono mein kaafi fark hai.

**Galti 2: r² ki jagah r use karna**

Example 2 mein agar r² = 0.25 ki jagah r = 0.5 se divide karte, to F = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ × 200 ÷ 0.5 = 2.7 × 10⁻⁸ N aata, jo sahi 5.3 × 10⁻⁸ N se aadha hai. Hamesha **r ko square karo**.

**Galti 3: Powers of ten galat jodna**

Example 4 mein 10⁻¹¹ × 10²⁴ × 10²² aata hai. Sahi: 10⁻¹¹⁺²⁴⁺²² = 10³⁵. Galti se 10⁻¹¹ × 10²⁴ ko 10⁻³⁵ ya 10¹³ ki jagah 10⁻¹³ likh dena aam hai. Sahi tareeka: **gunne mein powers jodo, bhaag mein ghatao.** Aur r² mein (10⁸)² = 10¹⁶: power **double** hoti hai.

**Galti 4: G aur g ko ek hi samajhna**

**G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg²** ek **universal constant** hai: har jagah ek-sa, har do cheezon ke liye. **g** (jo Topic 3 mein aayega) Earth ki surface par accelerate hone ka number hai (~9.8 m/s²) aur wo jagah ke saath badal sakta hai. Dono ke naam, value aur unit alag hain.

**Galti 5: Sochna ki bhaari cheez halki ko zyada zor se kheenchti hai**

Example 9 dekho: seb par 1.95 N aur Earth par bhi **1.95 N**. Force barabar hai: **sirf acceleration alag** hai (9.77 m/s² aur 3.3 × 10⁻²⁵ m/s²).

**Galti 6: Unit N m²/kg² bhool jana (ya N likhna)**

G ka unit **N m²/kg²** hai. Agar G ke saath sirf "N" likha ya unit hi nahi likha, to calculation mein units ka check chhoot jata hai, aur final answer ka unit galat nikalta hai. Hamesha unit ke saath likho aur cancel karke dekho ki **N** bacha ya nahi.

## Quick Recap

- **Universal Law of Gravitation:** har object har doosre ko kheenchta hai. Force dono masses ke gunanfal ke **anupaati** hai aur centres ke beech ki doori ke **square ke ulte anupaat** mein hai.
- **F = G m₁ m₂ ÷ r².** F newton mein, m kg mein, r metre mein (centre se centre).
- **G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg²**: universal (har jagah ek hi), aur bahut chhota, isliye roz ki cheezon ke beech gravity mehsoos nahi hoti.
- **Inverse square:** doori 2 guna to force ¼; doori 3 guna to force 1/9; ek mass 2 guna to force 2 guna; m₁ 2 guna aur m₂ 3 guna to force 6 guna.
- **Dono cheezon par force barabar aur ulta** (Newton ka teesra niyam): seb aur Earth ek-doosre ko barabar force se kheenchte hain, par acceleration alag: seb ≈ 9.8 m/s², Earth ≈ 3.3 × 10⁻²⁵ m/s².
- **r centre se centre** naapa jata hai. Earth ke liye Earth ke centre se: surface par r = R = 6.4 × 10⁶ m.
- Gravity ko mehsoos karne ke liye ek cheez ka mass **bahut bada** chahiye (jaise Earth).
- **Kahani ka jawab:** do 10 kg ki gendein, r = 0.2 m: F = 1.67 × 10⁻⁷ N (bahut chhota). 10 kg ki ball par Earth ka force: 97.7 N: lagbhag 59 crore guna.

## Practice Questions

1. State Newton's law of universal gravitation in words and write its formula, naming every symbol with its SI unit.

2. Two lead balls of mass 5 kg and 8 kg have their centres 0.5 m apart. Find the gravitational force between them. (G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg²)

3. The gravitational force between two bodies is 36 N. What will the force become if (a) the distance is doubled, (b) the distance is tripled, (c) the distance is halved, (d) both masses are doubled and the distance is kept the same?

4. If one mass is doubled and the other is tripled, and the distance is also doubled, by what factor does the gravitational force change?

5. Two spherical balls of mass 20 kg each have a radius of 0.2 m. Their surfaces are 0.6 m apart. Find the gravitational force between them. Why must you use the distance between centres?

6. The mass of the Earth is 6 × 10²⁴ kg and the mass of the Sun is 2 × 10³⁰ kg. The distance between them is 1.5 × 10¹¹ m. Find the gravitational force between the Sun and the Earth.

7. An object is at a distance of 4R from the centre of the Earth, where R is the radius of the Earth. Another identical object is on the surface of the Earth. How many times is the force on the second object compared to the first?

8. Find the gravitational force on a 5 kg object at the Earth's surface. (M_E = 6 × 10²⁴ kg, R = 6.4 × 10⁶ m)

9. A 0.5 kg ball and the Earth attract each other. Find the force and the acceleration of the ball. Is the acceleration of the Earth the same as that of the ball? Explain, without calculating.

10. State True or False:
    (a) The gravitational force is stronger on the lighter body than on the heavier one.
    (b) G has the same value everywhere in the universe.
    (c) If the distance between two bodies doubles, the force becomes half.
    (d) The unit of G is N m²/kg².
    (e) G and g are two names for the same quantity.

### Answers

1. **Niyam:** har object har doosre object ko ek force se kheenchta hai jo dono masses ke gunanfal ke anupaati aur centres ke beech ki doori ke square ke ulte anupaat mein hota hai. **F = G m₁ m₂ ÷ r²**, jahan F gravitational force (N), G universal gravitational constant (N m²/kg²), m₁ aur m₂ masses (kg), r centres ke beech doori (m).

2. m₁ m₂ = 5 kg × 8 kg = 40 kg²; r² = (0.5 m)² = 0.25 m².
   F = (6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg²)(40 kg²) ÷ (0.25 m²) = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ × 160 N = 1067.2 × 10⁻¹¹ N = **1.07 × 10⁻⁸ N**.
   Jaanch: 6.67e-11 × 40 ÷ 0.25 = 1.0672e-8 ✅

3. (a) Doori 2 guna: F ÷ 4 = 36 ÷ 4 = **9 N**. (b) Doori 3 guna: F ÷ 9 = 36 ÷ 9 = **4 N**. (c) Doori aadhi: 4F = 4 × 36 = **144 N**. (d) Dono masses 2 guna: 2 × 2 = 4 guna = 36 × 4 = **144 N**.
   Jaanch: (a) 36 ÷ 2² = 9 ✅; (b) 36 ÷ 3² = 4 ✅; (c) 36 ÷ (1/2)² = 144 ✅; (d) 36 × 2 × 2 = 144 ✅

4. Numerator: 2 × 3 = 6 guna. Denominator: 2² = 4 guna. Force = 6 ÷ 4 = **1.5 guna** (ya 3/2).
   Jaanch: F = 4 N maan lo to naya = 4 × 6 ÷ 4 = 6 N = 1.5 × 4 ✅

5. Centre ki doori r = 0.2 m + 0.6 m + 0.2 m = **1.0 m**. m₁ m₂ = 20 kg × 20 kg = 400 kg²; r² = (1.0 m)² = 1 m².
   F = (6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg²)(400 kg²) ÷ (1 m²) = 2668 × 10⁻¹¹ N = **2.67 × 10⁻⁸ N**.
   Kaaran: formula ka r **centres ke beech** ki doori hai, kyunki gravity ko cheez ke centre se aaya hua maana jata hai. Surface ki doori (0.6 m) lene se galat answer aata.
   Jaanch: 6.67e-11 × 400 = 2.668e-8 ✅

6. m₁ m₂ = (6 × 10²⁴ kg)(2 × 10³⁰ kg) = 12 × 10⁵⁴ kg² = 1.2 × 10⁵⁵ kg². r² = (1.5 × 10¹¹ m)² = 2.25 × 10²² m².
   F = (6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg²)(1.2 × 10⁵⁵ kg²) ÷ (2.25 × 10²² m²) = (8.004 × 10⁴⁴ N m²) ÷ (2.25 × 10²² m²) = 3.557 × 10²² N ≈ **3.6 × 10²² N**.
   Jaanch: 6.67e-11 × 6e24 × 2e30 ÷ (1.5e11)² = 3.557e22 ✅

7. F ∝ 1 ÷ r². Surface par r = R, upar r = 4R. F_surface ÷ F_4R = (4R)² ÷ R² = **16**. Surface wala force **16 guna** zyada hai.
   Jaanch: 1 kg ke liye F_surface = 9.77 N, F_4R = 9.77 N ÷ 16 = 0.61 N, aur 9.77 N ÷ 0.61 N = 16 ✅

8. F = G M_E m ÷ R² = (6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg²)(6 × 10²⁴ kg)(5 kg) ÷ (6.4 × 10⁶ m)² = (2.001 × 10¹⁵ N m²) ÷ (4.096 × 10¹³ m²) = **48.9 N** (lagbhag 49 N).
   Jaanch: 5 × 9.77 = 48.85 N ✅ (1 kg par 9.77 N ka 5 guna)

9. F = (6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg²)(6 × 10²⁴ kg)(0.5 kg) ÷ (6.4 × 10⁶ m)² = (2.001 × 10¹⁴ N m²) ÷ (4.096 × 10¹³ m²) = **4.89 N**. Ball ka acceleration a = F ÷ m = 4.89 N ÷ 0.5 kg = **9.77 m/s²**. Nahi, Earth ka acceleration **bahut chhota** hai: force barabar (4.89 N) hai par Earth ka mass bahut bada hai, aur a = F ÷ m.
   Jaanch: 4.89 ÷ 0.5 = 9.78 ≈ 9.77 ✅ (rounding)

10. (a) **False** ❌. Dono par force barabar hota hai (Newton ka teesra niyam).
    (b) **True** ✅. G universal constant hai.
    (c) **False** ❌. Doori double hone par force **¼** hota hai, aadha nahi.
    (d) **True** ✅.
    (e) **False** ❌. G universal constant hai (6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N m²/kg²) aur g Earth par acceleration (~9.8 m/s²) hai.
