# Topic 4: Free Fall ke Equations: Upar aur Neeche

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki vacuum mein har girti cheez, chahe patthar ho ya pankh, **ek hi acceleration g** se girti hai (g ≈ 9.8 m/s², aasani ke liye aksar 10 m/s²). Ab jab acceleration ki value pata hai aur wo constant hai, to Motion chapter ke **teeno equations of motion** seedhe lag sakte hain. Bas har jagah a ki jagah **± g** likhna hai. Is topic mein dekhenge ki girne wali aur upar jaane wali, dono tarah ki cheezon ke sawal kaise hal hote hain.

---

## Mr. Ghosh, Aashi aur terrace ki ball

Building ki terrace par watchman Mr. Ghosh ek kursi par baithe the, haath mein stopwatch. Aashi ek rubber ball lekar aayi.

"Uncle, dekhiye, main ball ko seedha upar fenkti hoon, aur wapas haath mein pakadungi. Aap time note kijiye!"

Aashi ne ball poori taakat se seedha upar uchhali. Ball tezi se upar gayi, dheemi hoti gayi, ek pal ke liye jaise **hawa mein tham** gayi, aur phir neeche aane lagi. Aashi ne use pakad liya. Mr. Ghosh ne stopwatch rokta hua dekha: **4 second**.

Aashi soch mein padh gayi. "Uncle, ball sabse upar ek pal ke liye ruki kyun? Agar ruki thi to us waqt gravity ne kaam karna band kar diya kya? Aur ye gayi kitni oonchi?"

Mr. Ghosh muskuraye. "Ye to **hisaab** se nikal jayega. Bas ek second rukna... pehle formulas ko g ke saath jodte hain."

Is kahani ko hum Example 1 mein number ke saath hal karenge.

## Part A: Convention tay karo, phir a = ± g

Teeno equations wahi hain: **v = u + a t**, **s = u t + ½ a t²**, **v² = u² + 2 a s**. Fark sirf itna hai ki ab **a = g** (ya −g) pehle se pata hai. g ki disha **hamesha neeche** (dharti ki taraf) hoti hai. Ab do tareeke hain, aur **har sawal ke shuru mein** batana hai ki aap kaun sa use kar rahe ho.

| Convention | Positive disha | a ki value | Kab aasaan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Upar positive | Upar | a = −g | Cheez upar fenki gayi ho, ya upar-neeche dono hon |
| 2. Neeche positive | Neeche | a = +g | Cheez sirf neeche ja rahi ho (girayi ya neeche fenki) |

Dono tareeke sahi hain. **Ek hi sawal mein dono mix mat karo.** Convention ke hisaab se u, v, s ke sign bhi tay hote hain: upar positive maanne par upar ki taraf wali velocity (+), neeche ki taraf wali velocity (−), aur jahan se fenka us se neeche wali jagah ka displacement (−).

**Is topic mein g = 10 m/s² liya gaya hai** taaki hisaab aasaan rahe. Jahan 9.8 m/s² liya hai, wahan sawal mein saaf likha hai.

---

## Part B: Rest se girana (u = 0)

Jab cheez ko **chhod diya jaye** (fenka na jaye), to u = 0. Neeche positive maanein, to a = +g, aur teeno equations bahut chhote ho jaate hain:

| Equation | Free fall (rest se) | Matlab |
|---|---|---|
| v = u + a t | **v = g t** | t second baad speed |
| s = u t + ½ a t² | **h = ½ g t²** | t second mein neeche aayi doori |
| v² = u² + 2 a s | **v² = 2 g h** | h doori girne par speed (time ki zaroorat nahi) |

Yahan s ki jagah h likha hai (height jo girna tay hui). Dhyan rahe: h ∝ t², yaani time doguna, to doori **chaar guni**.

## Part C: Upar fenkna (g velocity ko ghatata hai)

Aashi ne ball upar fenki. **Upar positive** maante hain. Ball upar ja rahi hai (v positive), par gravity neeche kheench rahi hai, yaani a = −g = −10 m/s². Matlab har second velocity **10 m/s kam** hoti jaati hai. Maan lo ek ball **30 m/s** se upar fenki gayi (g = 10 m/s²). v = u + a t = 30 − 10 t aur s = 30 t − 5 t² se:

| Time t (s) | Velocity v (m/s) | Height s (m) | Ball kya kar rahi hai |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 30 | 0 | Haath se nikli |
| 1 | 20 | 25 | Upar, dheemi hoti hui |
| 2 | 10 | 40 | Upar, aur dheemi |
| 3 | **0** | **45** | **Sabse upar, ek pal ke liye v = 0** |
| 4 | −10 | 40 | Neeche aa rahi, tez hoti hui |
| 5 | −20 | 25 | Neeche, aur tez |
| 6 | −30 | 0 | Wapas haath mein |

Table ke baad do baatein dekho:

1. **Symmetry:** Jis speed (30 m/s) se fenki, usi speed (30 m/s, par ulti disha mein) se wapas aayi. Kisi bhi height par, jaise 25 m par, upar jaate waqt velocity +20 aur wapas aate waqt −20: speed same. Aur upar jaane mein 3 s lage, neeche aane mein bhi 3 s.
2. **Velocity ka sign badla:** + se 0 se −. Ye badlav har second ek-sa (−10 m/s) hai, yaani **acceleration poore safar mein ek-sa −10 m/s² raha**, top par bhi.

```
 height
   ^
45 :            . . .           <- top: v = 0
   :         .         .
40 :       .             .
   :
25 :     .                 .
   :
 0 +--+--+--+--+--+--+--+--> time (s)
   0  1  2  3  4  5  6
   v: +30 +20 +10  0 -10 -20 -30 (m/s)
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Seedhi upar fenki ball ki height-time curve (u = 30 m/s, g = 10 m/s²). Sabse oonchi point 45 m par, t = 3 s par. Ball ka raasta seedhi line mein hi hai; graph sirf height ko time ke saath dikhata hai.

### Upar fenkne ke teen kaam ke formulas

**Top par v = 0.** Convention: upar positive, a = −g.

- **Top tak time:** v = u + a t, to 0 = u − g t, yaani **t = u ÷ g**.
- **Maximum height:** v² = u² + 2 a s, to 0 = u² − 2 g H, yaani **H = u² ÷ (2g)**.
- **Kul time of flight** (usi level par wapas): up-time + down-time (symmetry se barabar), yaani **T = 2u ÷ g**.

Jaanch u = 30 m/s ke liye: t = 30 ÷ 10 = 3 s ✅ (table mein v = 0 t = 3 par). H = 900 ÷ 20 = 45 m ✅ (table mein 45 m). T = 60 ÷ 10 = 6 s ✅.

## Part D: Top par v = 0, lekin a = g (Motion chapter ka sawaal)

[Motion ke Topic 4](../motion/motion-part4.md) mein ek sawal chhoda tha: ball ko upar fenko to sabse upar kya hoga? Ab jawab milta hai.

Top par **v = 0** hai, par **a = −g ≠ 0**. Acceleration ka matlab hai **velocity badal rahi hai**, na ki velocity kitni hai. Top par ball ki velocity +ve se −ve ban rahi hai, yaani wo us pal bhi badal rahi hai. Part C wali ball (v = 30 − 10 t) ke liye hisaab lagao: t = 2.9 s par v = +1 m/s, t = 3 s par 0, t = 3.1 s par −1 m/s. Har 0.1 s mein 1 m/s kam, wahi 10 m/s². Agar top par acceleration zero hoti, to ball hamesha ke liye wahin hawa mein latki rehti. Gravity ne kaam band nahi kiya: v = 0 sirf **ek pal** ke liye hai.

Aashi ko ye jawab mil gaya: ball ruki isliye lagi ki upar jaana (+) aur neeche aana (−) ke beech velocity ko zero se guzarna hi padta hai.

## Part E: Solved examples

### Example 1: Aashi ki ball (u = 20 m/s upar)

Aashi ne ball **20 m/s** se seedha upar fenki aur wo 4 s baad haath mein aayi. Dekho ki Mr. Ghosh ka 4 s sahi hai ya nahi, aur ball kitni oonchi gayi. (g = 10 m/s²)

**Convention:** upar positive, to u = +20 m/s, a = −10 m/s².

**Top tak time:** v = u + a t, to 0 = 20 m/s − 10 m/s² × t, yaani t = 20 ÷ 10 = **2 s**.

**Maximum height:** v² = u² + 2 a s, to 0 = (20 m/s)² − 2 × 10 m/s² × H, yaani 0 = 400 − 20 H, to **H = 20 m**.

**Kul time:** T = 2 × 2 s = **4 s**. Mr. Ghosh ki stopwatch bilkul sahi.

Jaanch: poori yatra mein s = 0 (wapas wahin). s = u t + ½ a t² = 20 m/s × 4 s + ½ × (−10 m/s²) × (4 s)² = 80 m − 80 m = 0 ✅ Aur top par s = 20 × 2 − ½ × 10 × 4 = 40 − 20 = 20 m ✅

Aashi ka jawab: ball **20 m** oonchi gayi (lagbhag 6 manzil ki building ki oonchai), aur wo upar ruki isliye ki v = 0 ek pal ke liye aati hi hai.

### Example 2: 80 m se girta patthar

Ek patthar 80 m oonchi building se chhoda gaya. Zameen tak kitna time lagega aur kis speed se takrayega? (g = 10 m/s²)

**Convention:** neeche positive, to u = 0, a = +10 m/s², h = 80 m.

h = ½ g t², to 80 m = ½ × 10 m/s² × t² = 5 t², yaani t² = 16 s², **t = 4 s**.

v = g t = 10 m/s² × 4 s = **40 m/s** (neeche ki taraf).

Jaanch: v² = 2 g h = 2 × 10 m/s² × 80 m = 1600 m²/s², aur 40² = 1600 m²/s² ✅

### Example 3: 45 m ki building

Ek stone 45 m oonchi building se chhoda gaya. Time aur speed? (g = 10 m/s²)

**Convention:** neeche positive, u = 0, a = +10 m/s², h = 45 m.

45 m = ½ × 10 m/s² × t² = 5 t², to t² = 9 s², **t = 3 s**. v = 10 m/s² × 3 s = **30 m/s**.

Jaanch: v² = 2 g h = 2 × 10 × 45 = 900 m²/s² = 30² ✅

### Example 4: 2 second ke baad kya hoga?

Ek ball ko rest se chhoda gaya. 2 s baad uski velocity aur is dauran girne wali doori nikalo. (g = 10 m/s²)

**Convention:** neeche positive, u = 0, a = +10 m/s², t = 2 s.

v = g t = 10 m/s² × 2 s = **20 m/s**. h = ½ g t² = ½ × 10 m/s² × (2 s)² = 5 × 4 = **20 m**.

Jaanch: s = ½ (u + v) t = ½ × (0 + 20) m/s × 2 s = 20 m ✅

### Example 5: Top tak time se maximum height

Ek ball seedha upar fenki gayi aur **3 s** mein sabse oonchi point par pahunchi. Fenkne ki speed aur maximum height? (g = 10 m/s²)

**Convention:** upar positive, a = −10 m/s², top par v = 0, t = 3 s.

0 = u − 10 m/s² × 3 s, to **u = 30 m/s**. H = u² ÷ (2g) = 900 m²/s² ÷ 20 m/s² = **45 m**.

Jaanch: s = ½ (u + v) t = ½ × (30 + 0) m/s × 3 s = 45 m ✅ (ye wahi ball hai jo Part C ki table mein thi.)

### Example 6: Ball 15 m/s se upar (g = 9.8 m/s²)

Ek ball 15 m/s se upar fenki gayi. **Is sawal mein g = 9.8 m/s² lo.** Maximum height aur top tak time nikalo.

**Convention:** upar positive, u = +15 m/s, a = −9.8 m/s².

H = u² ÷ (2g) = (15 m/s)² ÷ (2 × 9.8 m/s²) = 225 ÷ 19.6 m = **≈ 11.48 m**.
t = u ÷ g = 15 m/s ÷ 9.8 m/s² = **≈ 1.53 s**.

Jaanch: s = u t + ½ a t² = 15 × 1.53 − ½ × 9.8 × (1.53)² = 22.95 − 11.47 = 11.48 m ✅ (rounding ke andar.)

### Example 7: Chhat se neeche ki taraf fenka

Ek ladka 75 m oonchi chhat se ball ko **10 m/s** se seedha **neeche** fenkta hai. Zameen par kis speed se aur kitne time mein pahunchegi? (g = 10 m/s²)

**Convention:** neeche positive, u = +10 m/s, a = +10 m/s², s = 75 m.

v² = u² + 2 a s = (10 m/s)² + 2 × 10 m/s² × 75 m = 100 + 1500 = 1600 m²/s², to **v = 40 m/s**.
t = (v − u) ÷ a = (40 − 10) m/s ÷ 10 m/s² = **3 s**.

Jaanch: s = u t + ½ a t² = 10 m/s × 3 s + ½ × 10 m/s² × (3 s)² = 30 m + 45 m = 75 m ✅

### Example 8: Chhat se upar fenki ball neeche zameen par

Ek ball 75 m oonchi chhat se **10 m/s** se seedha **upar** fenki gayi. Wo chhat ke bagal se guzar kar neeche zameen par girti hai. Kitne time baad zameen par girti hai, aur kis speed se? (g = 10 m/s²)

**Convention:** upar positive. u = +10 m/s, a = −10 m/s². Zameen **fenkne wali jagah se 75 m neeche** hai, to **s = −75 m** (yahi sabse zaroori sign hai).

s = u t + ½ a t², to −75 = 10 t − 5 t², yaani 5 t² − 10 t − 75 = 0, dono taraf 5 se divide: **t² − 2 t − 15 = 0**.

Factorise: (t − 5)(t + 3) = 0, to t = 5 ya t = −3. Time negative nahi ho sakta, to **t = 5 s**.

Speed: v = u + a t = 10 − 10 × 5 = 10 − 50 = **−40 m/s**, yaani **40 m/s neeche ki taraf**.

Jaanch 1 (t = 5 par): 10 × 5 − 5 × 25 = 50 − 125 = −75 m ✅

Jaanch 2 (v² se): v² = 100 + 2 × (−10) × (−75) = 100 + 1500 = 1600, v = 40 m/s ✅

Jaanch 3 (do hisson mein): **Upar ka hissa:** top tak t₁ = 10 ÷ 10 = 1 s, uthi hui height = 100 ÷ 20 = 5 m. Ab ball chhat se 5 m upar hai, yaani zameen se 75 + 5 = 80 m. **Neeche ka hissa:** 80 m girne ka time = √(2 × 80 ÷ 10) = √16 = 4 s, speed 10 × 4 = 40 m/s. Kul time = 1 + 4 = **5 s** ✅

Ek mazedaar baat: Example 7 mein ball neeche fenki thi aur speed 40 m/s mili. Example 8 mein upar fenki, tab bhi **40 m/s**! Kyunki wapas chhat ke level par aate waqt ball ki speed 10 m/s hoti hai (symmetry), neeche ja kar baaki safar bilkul Example 7 jaisa ho jaata hai. Fark sirf time ka hai (3 s ke badle 5 s).

### Example 9: Kuyen ki gehraai

Ek patthar kuyen mein chhoda gaya aur **3 s** baad pani mein girne ki awaaz (splash) suni gayi. Kuan kitna gehra hai? **Is sawal mein g = 9.8 m/s² lo, aur awaaz ke upar aane ka time ignore karo** (maan lo splash dikhta hai usi pal jab patthar pani ko chhoota hai).

**Convention:** neeche positive, u = 0, a = +9.8 m/s², t = 3 s.

h = ½ g t² = ½ × 9.8 m/s² × (3 s)² = 4.9 × 9 = **44.1 m**.

Jaanch: v = g t = 9.8 × 3 = 29.4 m/s. v² = 2 g h = 2 × 9.8 × 44.1 = 864.36 m²/s², aur 29.4² = 864.36 m²/s² ✅

(Sach mein awaaz ko upar aane mein bhi thoda time lagta hai, to asli depth isse kuch kam hogi. Hum yahan sirf girne ka time maan rahe hain.)

### Example 10: Do patthar, ek chhoda aur ek fenka

80 m oonchi building se ek patthar **chhoda** gaya, aur ek doosra patthar usi pal **30 m/s** se seedha **neeche** fenka gaya. Kaun pehle pahunchega, aur dono kis speed se takrayenge? (g = 10 m/s²)

**Convention:** neeche positive, h = 80 m.

**Patthar A (chhoda):** u = 0. 80 = 5 t², t = **4 s**. v = 10 × 4 = **40 m/s**.

**Patthar B (fenka):** u = 30 m/s. 80 = 30 t + 5 t², yaani t² + 6 t − 16 = 0, to (t + 8)(t − 2) = 0, **t = 2 s**. v = 30 + 10 × 2 = **50 m/s**.

B pehle pahunchega (2 s baad), aur zyada speed se (50 m/s) takrayega.

Jaanch (B ka v² se): v² = 900 + 2 × 10 × 80 = 900 + 1600 = 2500, v = 50 m/s ✅ Aur A ka: v² = 2 × 10 × 80 = 1600, v = 40 m/s ✅

### Example 11: 20 m se girne par speed

Ek cheez 20 m ki oonchai se girti hai. Zameen par speed kya hogi, aur kitna time lagega? (g = 10 m/s²)

**Convention:** neeche positive, u = 0, a = +10 m/s², h = 20 m. Time nahi poochha, to teesra equation.

v² = 2 g h = 2 × 10 m/s² × 20 m = 400 m²/s², to **v = 20 m/s** (= 72 km/h, ek tez chalti car jitni).
t = v ÷ g = 20 ÷ 10 = **2 s**.

Jaanch: h = ½ g t² = 5 × 4 = 20 m ✅

### Example 12 (thoda extra): 1st, 2nd, 3rd second mein doori, ratio 1 : 3 : 5

Rest se girti cheez ke liye h = 5 t² (g = 10 m/s²). Pehle t seconds mein girne wali doori:

| t (s) | h = 5 t² (m) | Us second mein doori (m) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 | 5 − 0 = 5 |
| 2 | 20 | 20 − 5 = 15 |
| 3 | 45 | 45 − 20 = 25 |

Ratio: 5 : 15 : 25 = **1 : 3 : 5**. Yaani pehle second ki doori ke mukable doosre second mein 3 guna aur teesre mein 5 guna doori tay hoti hai (kyunki cheez tez hoti jaati hai). Ye pattern rest se girne par hamesha chalta hai (1 : 3 : 5 : 7 ...).

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Upar jaate waqt g ka sign bhool jana.** Upar positive maanne par a = **−10 m/s²** hai. a = +10 likha, to ball upar jaate waqt aur tez ho jayegi, jo galat hai. Example: u = 20 m/s, a = +10 se H = (0 − 400) ÷ 20 = −20 m milega, negative height ka koi matlab nahi.

2. **"Top par acceleration zero hai" kehna.** Top par sirf **v = 0** hai, a = −g = −10 m/s² pura safar chalta hai (Part D).

3. **Top tak ka time aur kul time mix karna.** Example 1 mein t = u ÷ g = 2 s sirf upar jaane ka time hai. Kul time 4 s hai. Sawal "kitni der mein wapas haath mein aayi" ho to **2u ÷ g** lo.

4. **Ek hi sawal mein g = 9.8 aur g = 10 dono lena.** Ek sawal mein ek value chuno aur wahi poore sawal mein rakho. Warna jawab ke decimal alag aayenge.

5. **Chhat se fenkne par galat s.** Example 8 mein "zameen par girti hai" ka matlab s = **−75 m** (upar positive hone par), s = 0 nahi aur s = +75 bhi nahi. s = 0 sirf tab jab wapas fenkne wali jagah par aaye.

6. **Return speed = throw speed ko har haal mein maan lena.** Ye sirf tab sach hai jab ball **usi level par** wapas aaye. Example 8 mein ball chhat ke level par 10 m/s se aayi, par zameen par pahunchne par 40 m/s thi, kyunki 75 m aur neeche gir chuki thi.

7. **Time ko ulti disha ka maan lena.** t hamesha positive hota hai. Quadratic mein ek root negative aaye (Example 8 mein t = −3) to use chhodo.

## Quick Recap

- Free fall mein a = g = 10 m/s² (ya 9.8 m/s²), disha hamesha neeche. Har sawal ke shuru mein **convention** likho: upar + (a = −g) ya neeche + (a = +g).
- Rest se girana (u = 0): **v = g t, h = ½ g t², v² = 2 g h**.
- Upar fenkne par velocity har second g se kam hoti hai. Top par **v = 0**, **t = u ÷ g**, **H = u² ÷ (2g)**, **T = 2u ÷ g** (usi level par wapas).
- Symmetry: usi level par wapas aane par speed = fenkne ki speed; upar jaane aur neeche aane ka time barabar.
- Top par v = 0 par **a = g ≠ 0**.
- Chhat se fenkne par zameen ka displacement negative (upar + wale convention mein); quadratic ya do hisson mein tod kar hal karo.
- Kuyen wale sawaal mein girne ka time hi lete hain (awaaz ka time ignore).
- Rest se girne par pehle, doosre, teesre second ki doori 1 : 3 : 5.

## Practice Questions

1. A stone is dropped from a height of 125 m. How long does it take to reach the ground and with what speed does it hit? (Take g = 10 m/s².)
2. A ball is thrown vertically upward at 40 m/s. Find the maximum height, the time to reach the top, and the total time of flight. (g = 10 m/s²)
3. A ball thrown straight up reaches its highest point after 1.5 s. With what speed was it thrown and how high did it go? (g = 10 m/s²)
4. A stone dropped into a well hits the water after 4 s. Find the depth of the well, ignoring the time taken by sound. (g = 10 m/s²)
5. A ball is thrown vertically upward at 20 m/s from a 60 m high roof and finally lands on the ground. Taking upward as positive, write s, and find the total time and the speed on landing. (g = 10 m/s²)
6. A stone is thrown vertically downward at 5 m/s from a 30 m high bridge. Find its landing speed and time. Is it faster or slower than a stone simply dropped from the bridge? (g = 10 m/s²)
7. A stone is dropped from a height of 19.6 m. Find the time and the speed on reaching the ground. (Use g = 9.8 m/s².)
8. (Extra) For a freely falling body starting from rest, find the distance covered in the 1st, 2nd and 3rd seconds using g = 9.8 m/s², and show that they are still in the ratio 1 : 3 : 5.
9. True or false: (a) At the highest point of a ball thrown up, its acceleration is zero. (b) A ball thrown up at 20 m/s and caught at the same level has speed 20 m/s when caught. (c) A ball thrown upward from a roof lands on the ground with the same speed as it was thrown with.

### Answers

1. Neeche positive, u = 0, a = +10 m/s², h = 125 m. 125 m = ½ × 10 m/s² × t² = 5 t², t² = 25 s², **t = 5 s**. v = g t = 10 m/s² × 5 s = **50 m/s**.
   Jaanch: v² = 2 g h = 2 × 10 m/s² × 125 m = 2500 m²/s² = (50 m/s)² ✅

2. Upar positive, u = 40 m/s, a = −10 m/s². t = u ÷ g = 40 m/s ÷ 10 m/s² = **4 s**. H = u² ÷ 2g = 1600 m²/s² ÷ 20 m/s² = **80 m**. Kul time = 2 × 4 s = **8 s**.
   Jaanch: s = 40 m/s × 8 s − ½ × 10 m/s² × 64 s² = 320 m − 320 m = 0 ✅ aur 40 m/s × 4 s − 5 m/s² × 16 s² = 160 m − 80 m = 80 m ✅

3. Upar positive, top par v = 0, t = 1.5 s. 0 = u − 10 m/s² × 1.5 s, **u = 15 m/s**. H = u² ÷ 2g = 225 m²/s² ÷ 20 m/s² = **11.25 m**.
   Jaanch: s = ½ (15 + 0) m/s × 1.5 s = 11.25 m ✅

4. Neeche positive, u = 0, t = 4 s. h = ½ × 10 m/s² × (4 s)² = 5 × 16 m = **80 m**.
   Jaanch: v = 10 m/s² × 4 s = 40 m/s, v² = 1600 m²/s² = 2 × 10 m/s² × 80 m ✅

5. Upar positive, u = +20 m/s, a = −10 m/s², **s = −60 m**. −60 m = 20 m/s × t − 5 m/s² × t², to t² − 4 t − 12 = 0 (t seconds mein), (t − 6)(t + 2) = 0, **t = 6 s**. v = 20 m/s − 10 m/s² × 6 s = **−40 m/s** (40 m/s neeche).
   Jaanch: 20 m/s × 6 s − 5 m/s² × 36 s² = 120 m − 180 m = −60 m ✅ Aur v² = 400 m²/s² + 2 × (−10 m/s²) × (−60 m) = 1600 m²/s², v = 40 m/s ✅

6. Neeche positive, u = 5 m/s, a = +10 m/s², s = 30 m. v² = 25 m²/s² + 2 × 10 m/s² × 30 m = 625 m²/s², **v = 25 m/s**. t = (25 − 5) m/s ÷ 10 m/s² = **2 s**. Chhode hue stone ki speed: v² = 2 × 10 m/s² × 30 m = 600 m²/s², v ≈ 24.5 m/s. To fenka hua stone **thoda tez** takrayega (25 m/s > 24.5 m/s) aur pehle pahunchega (dropped stone ka t = 24.5 ÷ 10 ≈ 2.45 s).
   Jaanch: s = 5 m/s × 2 s + ½ × 10 m/s² × (2 s)² = 10 m + 20 m = 30 m ✅

7. Neeche positive, **g = 9.8 m/s²**, u = 0, h = 19.6 m. 19.6 m = ½ × 9.8 m/s² × t² = 4.9 t², t² = 4 s², **t = 2 s**. v = 9.8 m/s² × 2 s = **19.6 m/s**.
   Jaanch: v² = 2 × 9.8 m/s² × 19.6 m = 384.16 m²/s² = (19.6 m/s)² ✅

8. g = 9.8 m/s² se h = ½ × 9.8 m/s² × t² = 4.9 t². 1 s: 4.9 m; 2 s: 19.6 m; 3 s: 44.1 m. Doori: 1st second 4.9 m, 2nd second 19.6 m − 4.9 m = 14.7 m, 3rd second 44.1 m − 19.6 m = 24.5 m. Ratio 4.9 : 14.7 : 24.5 = **1 : 3 : 5** ✅ (g ki value badli, ratio nahi.)

9. (a) **False** ❌. Top par v = 0 par a = −g = −10 m/s² (ya 9.8 m/s²), neeche ki taraf.
   (b) **True** ✅. Same level par symmetry se speed 20 m/s.
   (c) **False** ❌. Zameen roof se neeche hai, to ball aur tez ho chuki hoti hai. Example 8 mein 10 m/s se fenki aur 40 m/s se takrayi.
