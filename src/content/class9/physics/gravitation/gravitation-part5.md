# Topic 5: Mass aur Weight

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki girti hui cheez ki velocity har second mein g jitni badhti hai, aur uske equations se time, speed aur height nikalna seekha. Ab ek aisa sawal jo roz ki zindagi mein aata hai: **jab Earth har cheez ko neeche kheench rahi hai, to us kheenchne ko hum kaise naapein, aur "wazan" ka asli matlab kya hai?** Hum roz bolte hain "mera wazan 55 kilo hai", par physics mein is ek line mein **do alag cheezein** mix ho jaati hain: **mass** aur **weight**.

---

## Gym mein Ravleen ka "kilo"

Ravleen gym mein nayi member thi. Pehle hi din trainer Mr. Khanna ne use digital weighing machine par khada kiya. Screen par aaya: **55.0**.

Mr. Khanna bole, "Ravleen, aapka wazan 55 kilo hai."

Ravleen ne dekha ki gym ki deewar par ek purani **spring balance** bhi latak rahi hai. Usne poochha, "Sir, agar main isme latak jaoon to kya wahan bhi 55 aayega?"

Mr. Khanna hanse, "Nahi, wahan 55 nahi aayega. Spring balance **newton** mein padhti hai, aur number kuch aur hoga."

Ravleen ne turant ek aur sawaal daaga, "Sir, maine padha hai ki Moon par gravity bahut kam hai. Agar main Moon par isi machine par khadi hoon to mere 'kilo' kitne aayenge? Kya main wahan patli ho jaungi?"

Mr. Khanna ne kaha, "Bahut accha sawaal. Iska jawab seedha 'haan' ya 'na' mein nahi hai, kyunki 'kilo' ke peeche do alag cheezein chhupi hain. Chalo, topic ke beech mein dono ko number ke saath solve karte hain."

## Part A: Mass kya hai

Kisi cheez mein **kitna matter (padarth) bhara hai**, usi ko uska **mass** kehte hain. Ek lohe ka gola, ek sooti kapde ka tukda, ek insaan: sab mein matter hai, aur har ek ka apna mass hai.

- **SI unit:** kilogram (**kg**).
- Mass ek **scalar** hai: ye sirf "kitna" batata hai, "kis taraf" nahi.
- Mass **kahin bhi nahi badalta**: Earth par, Moon par, ya kisi aur planet par: wahi rahega. Kyunki cheez ke andar matter utna ka utna hi hai; use kahin le jaane se matter kam ya zyada nahi hota.

**Mass naapna:** mass naapne ke tools (beam balance, electronic balance) cheez ko ek **known mass** se compare karte hain (ye Measurement and Units chapter mein [measurement-and-units-part3.md](../measurement-and-units/measurement-and-units-part3.md) mein dekha tha). Isliye ek tarazu ek jagah par barabar nikle to Moon par bhi barabar hi nikalega (dono pallon par gravity ek jaisi lagti hai).

---

## Part B: Weight kya hai

Topic 1 aur 2 mein dekha tha ki Earth har cheez ko apni taraf kheenchti hai. Is kheenchne wale **force** ko hi **weight** kehte hain.

> **Weight = wo force jisse Earth (ya koi aur pind) cheez ko apne centre ki taraf kheenchta hai.**

Force ka kya hota hai, aur F = m a kya batata hai, ye [force-and-laws-of-motion-part6.md](../force-and-laws-of-motion/force-and-laws-of-motion-part6.md) mein dekha tha. Free fall mein cheez par sirf ye kheenchav lagta hai, aur uski acceleration g hoti hai (Topic 3, [gravitation-part3.md](gravitation-part3.md)). To F = m a mein a ki jagah g rakhne par:

> **W = m × g**

Yahan:
- **W** = weight (newton, N mein)
- **m** = mass (kg mein)
- **g** = us jagah ka free-fall acceleration (m/s² mein)

**Unit ka check:** kg × m/s² = kg m/s² = **N** (newton). Isliye weight ka SI unit **newton** hai.

**Weight ek vector hai:** iska maan (W = m g) bhi hai aur disha bhi: **hamesha us pind ke centre ki taraf** (Earth par neeche, Earth ke centre ki taraf).

```
          ( cheez, mass m )
                [ m ]
                  |
                  |  W = m g
                  v
          neeche, Earth ke centre ki taraf

   ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Earth ki surface ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Cheez ka weight W = m g Earth ke centre ki taraf (neeche) lagta hai. Isliye weight ek vector hai.

### Weight badalta hai, mass nahi

W = m g mein g **jagah ke saath badalta hai** (Topic 3 mein dekha tha: Earth par g ≈ 9.8 m/s², Moon par g ≈ 1.6 m/s²). Isliye:

- jagah badli → g badla → **weight badla**
- cheez wahi hai → matter wahi hai → **mass nahi badla**

## Part C: Mass aur Weight ka farak

| Baat | Mass | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Kya hai | Cheez mein matter ki maatra | Gravity ka force jo cheez par lagta hai |
| Quantity ka type | Scalar | Vector (disha: pind ke centre ki taraf) |
| SI unit | kilogram (kg) | newton (N) |
| Formula | Seedha naapte hain (compare karke) | W = m g |
| Jagah badalne par | **Nahi badalta** | **Badalta hai** (g ke saath) |
| Kabhi zero ho sakta hai? | Nahi | Pind se bahut door bahut chhota ho jaata hai. Free fall mein sirf scale ka reading zero aata hai; weight tab bhi lagta hai |
| Naapne ka tool | Beam balance, electronic balance | Spring balance |

### Spring balance kaise kaam karti hai

Spring balance mein ek spring hoti hai. Jab usme cheez latkate hain, to cheez ka weight spring ko neeche kheenchta hai. Weight jitna zyada, spring utni zyada khichti hai, aur pointer utna neeche jaata hai. Isliye spring balance seedha **weight (force)** naapti hai, aur uska scale **newton** mein hota hai.

```
              O        <- upar hook se tangi hui
              |
          +---+---+
          |   {   |    <- spring
          |   }   |
     0 N -|   {   |
    10 N -|   }   |
    20 N -|  =+=  |    <- pointer (yahan 20 N)
          +---+---+
              |
             [ ]       <- cheez (weight neeche ki taraf)
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Spring balance: cheez ka weight spring ko khichta hai, pointer scale par newton mein weight dikhata hai.

## Part D: Solved examples

Is topic mein jab tak alag se na bataya jaaye: **Earth par g = 9.8 m/s²**, **Moon par g = 1.6 m/s²**. Kuch examples mein aasaan hisaab ke liye g = 10 m/s² lenge: wahan saaf likha hoga, aur **ek example mein ek hi g** use hoga.

**Example 1 (mass se weight):** 60 kg ke ek vyakti ka weight Earth par kitna hoga? (a) g = 10 m/s², (b) g = 9.8 m/s².

- (a) W = m g = 60 kg × 10 m/s² = **600 N**
- (b) W = m g = 60 kg × 9.8 m/s² = **588 N**

**Jaanch:** 588 N ÷ 9.8 m/s² = 60 kg ✅ aur 600 N ÷ 10 m/s² = 60 kg ✅. (g ki value alag li to weight thoda alag aaya, par mass dono mein 60 kg hi hai.)

**Example 2 (weight se mass):** Ek cheez ka weight Earth par 490 N hai. Uska mass? (g = 9.8 m/s²)

W = m g, to **m = W ÷ g**

- m = 490 N ÷ 9.8 m/s² = **50 kg**

**Jaanch:** 50 kg × 9.8 m/s² = 490 N ✅

**Example 3 (Moon par weight):** 60 kg ke vyakti ka weight Moon par kitna hoga? (g_moon = 1.6 m/s²). Uska mass Moon par kitna hoga?

- W_moon = m g_moon = 60 kg × 1.6 m/s² = **96 N**
- Mass = **60 kg** (nahi badla)

**Jaanch:** 96 N ÷ 1.6 m/s² = 60 kg ✅

Earth par (g = 9.8) weight 588 N tha. Ratio:

- W_moon ÷ W_earth = 96 N ÷ 588 N = **0.163**, yaani lagbhag **1/6**

Ye isliye, kyunki g_moon ÷ g_earth = 1.6 ÷ 9.8 = 0.163 ≈ 1/6. Mass wahi hai, to weight ka ratio g ke ratio jaisa hi aayega. (Isliye kaha jaata hai ki Moon par weight Earth ka lagbhag **ek-chhatha** hota hai.)

**Jaanch:** 588 N × 0.163 = 95.8 N ≈ 96 N ✅. (Moon par g = Earth ke g ka 1/6 maan lein to 588 ÷ 6 = 98 N: thoda alag, kyunki 1/6 sirf lagbhag hai.)

**Example 4 (Ravleen ka sawaal, kahani ka jawab):** Weighing machine par Ravleen ka reading 55 "kilo" hai. Uska weight newton mein kitna hai? Moon par kitna hoga? Mass kitna hoga?

Machine ka "55 kilo" asal mein **mass = 55 kg** hai.

Earth par:
- W = m g = 55 kg × 9.8 m/s² = **539 N**
- (aasaan hisaab ke liye g = 10 lein: W = 55 kg × 10 m/s² = **550 N**)

Moon par:
- W_moon = 55 kg × 1.6 m/s² = **88 N**

Mass Moon par bhi: **55 kg**.

**Jaanch:** 539 N ÷ 9.8 m/s² = 55 kg ✅; 88 N ÷ 1.6 m/s² = 55 kg ✅; 88 N ÷ 539 N = 0.163 ≈ 1/6 ✅

**Mr. Khanna ka jawab Ravleen ko:** "Spring balance par Earth par aapka reading 539 N aata (kareeb 550 N). Moon par spring balance sirf 88 N dikhati. Aap patli nahi hui, aapka **mass 55 kg hi hai**. Bas Moon aapko kam zor se kheench rahi hai, isliye aap halka mehsoos karogi aur bahut oonchi kood sakogi."

**Example 5 (doosre planet par):** Ek planet par g = 25 m/s² hai. Wahan 10 kg ke gole ka weight kitna hoga? Earth par (g = 10 m/s² lekar) kitna hota? Ratio?

- Planet par: W = 10 kg × 25 m/s² = **250 N**
- Earth par (g = 10): W = 10 kg × 10 m/s² = **100 N**
- Ratio: 250 N ÷ 100 N = **2.5**

**Jaanch:** 250 N ÷ 25 m/s² = 10 kg ✅. Gole ka mass phir bhi 10 kg hai. Ratio g ke ratio (25 ÷ 10 = 2.5) ke barabar hai ✅

**Example 6 (Moon par weight diya hai):** Ek cheez ka weight Moon par 80 N hai (g_moon = 1.6 m/s²). Uska mass aur Earth par weight (g = 9.8 m/s²)?

- Mass: m = W_moon ÷ g_moon = 80 N ÷ 1.6 m/s² = **50 kg**
- Earth par weight: W_earth = m g = 50 kg × 9.8 m/s² = **490 N**

**Jaanch:** 50 kg × 1.6 m/s² = 80 N ✅; 490 N ÷ 9.8 m/s² = 50 kg ✅. (Mass dono jagah 50 kg: wahi Earth aur Moon dono ka bridge hai.)

**Example 7 (spring balance ka reading):** Ek spring balance par ek cheez latkane se reading 49 N aati hai (Earth par, g = 9.8 m/s²). Cheez ka mass?

- m = W ÷ g = 49 N ÷ 9.8 m/s² = **5 kg**

**Jaanch:** 5 kg × 9.8 m/s² = 49 N ✅

**Example 8 (Newton ke second law ke saath):** 5 kg ka ek patthar free fall (hawa ka pratirodh nahi maanein) kar raha hai. Uske upar net force aur acceleration? (g = 9.8 m/s²)

Free fall mein patthar par sirf ek hi force lag raha hai: **uska weight**. To net force = weight:

- F = W = m g = 5 kg × 9.8 m/s² = **49 N** (neeche ki taraf)

Ab F = m a se:

- a = F ÷ m = 49 N ÷ 5 kg = **9.8 m/s²**

Yaani a = g. Ye wahi baat hai jo Topic 3 mein dekhi thi: free fall mein acceleration g hota hai.

**Jaanch:** m × a = 5 kg × 9.8 m/s² = 49 N ✅

**Example 9 (do cheezon ke weights ki tulna):** Earth par ek cheez ka mass 2 kg aur doosri ka 6 kg hai (g = 9.8 m/s²). Dono ke weights aur unka ratio?

- W₁ = 2 kg × 9.8 m/s² = **19.6 N**
- W₂ = 6 kg × 9.8 m/s² = **58.8 N**
- W₂ ÷ W₁ = 58.8 N ÷ 19.6 N = **3**, aur mass ratio 6 ÷ 2 = **3** bhi

**Jaanch:** Ek hi jagah par g same hai, isliye weights ka ratio masses ke ratio ke barabar hai ✅. Doosri cheez 3 guna bhaari hai to uska weight bhi 3 guna hai.

**Example 10 (kilogram-weight se newton):** Ek vyakti ka weight 686 N hai. "Kilo" mein (yaani kgf mein) kitna hoga? (1 kgf = 9.8 N)

- Weight in kgf = 686 N ÷ 9.8 N/kgf = **70 kgf**, yaani aam bol-chaal mein "70 kilo"

**Jaanch:** 70 kgf × 9.8 N/kgf = 686 N ✅. Ye bilkul Ravleen jaisa hi hai: 55 kgf = 55 × 9.8 = 539 N ✅.

## Part E: "Kilo" aur "kilogram-weight": roz ki boli ka jhol

Ravleen ki machine par "55 kilo" likha tha. Asal mein ye kya hai?

Digital weighing machine ke andar ek **sensor** hota hai jo ye naapta hai ki tum us par **kitne force (weight) se dabti** ho. Phir wo us force ko g se baant kar tumhara **mass** nikalti hai aur screen par kg mein dikha deti hai.

- Force = 539 N
- Machine ye jaanti hai ki g ≈ 9.8 m/s²
- Mass = 539 N ÷ 9.8 m/s² = **55 kg** → screen par aata hai: **55**

Isliye jo hum roz "wazan 55 kilo" kehte hain, wo asal mein **mass** hai, jo machine ne weight se nikala. Ya phir ise **55 kgf** (kilogram-weight) kehte hain: yaani **wo force jo 55 kg ke mass par Earth lagati hai**:

> **1 kgf = 9.8 N**, isliye 55 kgf = 55 × 9.8 N = 539 N

**Roz ki zindagi mein ye mix kyun ho jaata hai?** Kyunki hum sab Earth par hi rehte hain, jahan g lagbhag har jagah ek jaisa (9.8 m/s²) hai. Is liye mass aur weight ek saath badhte hain: zyada mass wali cheez ka weight bhi zyada hota hai. Dukaan par sabzi, sone ki chain, ya khud ko tolna: mass aur weight ka farak kabhi pata hi nahi chalta. Isliye bol-chaal mein humne "weight" shabd ko hi "mass" ke liye bhi bol diya. Physics mein dono ko alag rakhte hain: **mass ko kg mein, weight ko N mein.**

**Dhyan rahe:** agar Ravleen sach mein Moon par us hi bathroom machine par khadi ho jati (jo Earth ke g ke hisaab se banayi gayi hai), to wo machine force ko 9.8 se baant kar dikhati: 88 N ÷ 9.8 m/s² = 8.98 kg, yaani lagbhag **9 "kilo"**. Par ye bas machine ka dhoka hai: Ravleen ka asli mass 55 kg hi rahega. Isiliye Ravleen ke sawaal ka jawab hai: **"kilo" (mass) 55 hi hai; uska weight kam ho jayega.**

## Part F: Weight aur kin jagahon par badalta hai (sirf idea)

Earth par bhi g **bilkul** ek jaisa nahi hota. Sirf idea ke roop mein:

- **Oonchai par** (jaise bahut oonche pahaad ya aeroplane mein): Earth ke centre se doori thodi badhti hai, isliye g **thoda kam** hota hai, aur weight bhi **thoda kam**.
- **Poles aur equator par:** Earth bilkul gol nahi hai, isliye poles par g thoda zyada aur equator par thoda kam hota hai. Weight bhi thoda alag hota hai.
- **Moon, planets par:** g bahut alag hota hai, to weight bahut alag hota hai (Part D mein dekha).

Har jagah mass wahi rehta hai.

### Weightlessness: sirf ek jhalak

Orbit mein ghoomte spaceship ke andar astronaut aur uski weighing machine **dono ek saath free fall** mein hote hain. Machine bhi astronaut jitni hi tezi se girti hai, isliye astronaut machine ko dabata hi nahi, aur machine ka reading **zero** aata hai. Astronaut ko lagta hai ki wo "weightless" hai.

Dhyan do: iska matlab ye nahi ki wahan gravity nahi hai (aur ye bhi nahi ki "hawa nahi hai isliye"). Gravity wahan bhi hai, aur wahi astronaut ko Earth ki taraf kheench rahi hai: bas wo aur uska ghar dono saath girte rehte hain. Iske details **Topic 6 mein thoda aur** dekhenge.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Mera weight 60 kg hai" kehna**

Physics mein kg **mass** ka unit hai. Weight force hai, to uska unit newton hai. Sahi: "Mera mass 60 kg hai, aur weight lagbhag 588 N hai (g = 9.8)." (Bol-chaal mein "60 kilo" chalta hai, par numerical mein N likhna hai.)

**Galti 2: Moon par mass badal jaata hai, aisa maanna**

Nahi. Ravleen ka mass 55 kg Earth par bhi, Moon par bhi. Badalta sirf weight hai: 539 N se 88 N. Jaanch: 88 N ÷ 1.6 m/s² = 55 kg ✅.

**Galti 3: N aur kg ko mix karna**

Agar likh diya "W = 60 kg × 9.8 = 588 kg" to galat hai: jawab **588 N** hai. Hamesha units likhte chalo: kg × m/s² = N.

**Galti 4: Ek step mein g = 9.8, doosre mein g = 10**

Agar weight 55 kg ke liye 550 N (g = 10) nikala aur phir wapas mass 550 ÷ 9.8 = 56.1 kg kar diya, to mass badal gaya, jabki wo 55 kg tha. **Ek question mein ek hi g.** Jaanch: 550 N ÷ 10 m/s² = 55 kg ✅.

**Galti 5: "Space mein weight zero hai kyunki hawa nahi hai"**

Galat kaaran. Orbit mein gravity lagti rehti hai; reading zero isliye aata hai ki cheez aur machine **saath free fall** mein hain. Hawa ka isse koi lena-dena nahi.

**Galti 6: Weight ko scalar samajhna**

Weight ek force hai, to vector hai: uski disha hamesha pind ke centre ki taraf hoti hai (Figure 5.1). Mass scalar hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Mass:** matter ki maatra. SI unit **kg**. Scalar. **Kahin nahi badalta.** Beam / electronic balance se naapte hain (known mass se compare karke).
- **Weight:** wo **force** jisse pind (Earth, Moon...) cheez ko apne centre ki taraf kheenchta hai. **W = m g**. SI unit **newton (N)**. **Vector**, disha centre ki taraf. Spring balance se naapte hain.
- **Weight jagah ke saath badalta hai** (g ke saath), mass nahi.
- Earth par g ≈ 9.8 m/s², Moon par ≈ 1.6 m/s² (lagbhag g/6): Moon par weight Earth ka lagbhag **1/6** hota hai.
- 60 kg ka vyakti: Earth par 600 N (g = 10) ya 588 N (g = 9.8), Moon par 96 N, mass har jagah 60 kg.
- **1 kgf = 9.8 N.** Machine ka "kilo" mass hai jo wo weight se nikalti hai. Roz ki boli mein mass aur weight mix isliye ho jaate hain ki hum sab Earth par hain jahan g lagbhag ek hai.
- Orbit mein / free fall mein scale ka reading zero: kyunki cheez aur scale saath girte hain (gravity gayab nahi hoti). Details Topic 6 mein.
- **Kahani ka jawab:** Ravleen: m = 55 kg; W_earth = 55 × 9.8 = 539 N (g = 10 par 550 N); W_moon = 55 × 1.6 = 88 N; mass Moon par bhi 55 kg.
- Ek question mein ek hi g (9.8 ya 10) lo.

## Practice Questions

1. Find the weight of an 8 kg object on the Earth and on the Moon. (Use g = 9.8 m/s² on the Earth and 1.6 m/s² on the Moon.)

2. The weight of a body on the Earth is 392 N. Find its mass and its weight on the Moon. (g = 9.8 m/s² on the Earth, 1.6 m/s² on the Moon.)

3. A spring balance shows 147 N when an object is hung from it on the Earth. Find the mass of the object. (g = 9.8 m/s²)

4. On a planet, g = 25 m/s². What is the weight of a 4 kg object there? What is its weight on the Earth if g = 10 m/s² there? How many times is the weight on the planet compared to the weight on the Earth?

5. The weight of an object on the Moon is 72 N. Find its mass, and its weight on the Earth. (g_moon = 1.6 m/s², g_earth = 9.8 m/s²)

6. A 2 kg stone is falling freely near the Earth's surface. What is the net force on it, and what is its acceleration? (g = 9.8 m/s²; ignore air resistance.)

7. Two objects of masses 3 kg and 12 kg are on the same table on the Earth. Find the ratio of their weights. Does the ratio depend on the value of g?

8. State True or False:
   (a) The mass of an astronaut becomes zero on the Moon.
   (b) The SI unit of weight is the newton.
   (c) Weight is a scalar quantity.
   (d) A spring balance measures weight directly.

9. A person's weight is 833 N. Express it in kgf and say what his mass is. (1 kgf = 9.8 N, g = 9.8 m/s²)

10. In your own words, explain why a digital weighing machine in a gym shows "55 kg" for Ravleen, and why it would be wrong to say her mass becomes about 9 kg on the Moon even if the machine showed that.

### Answers

1. Earth: W = 8 kg × 9.8 m/s² = **78.4 N**. Moon: W = 8 kg × 1.6 m/s² = **12.8 N**.
   Jaanch: 78.4 N ÷ 9.8 m/s² = 8 kg ✅, 12.8 N ÷ 1.6 m/s² = 8 kg ✅

2. m = W ÷ g = 392 N ÷ 9.8 m/s² = **40 kg**. Moon par W = 40 kg × 1.6 m/s² = **64 N**.
   Jaanch: 40 kg × 9.8 m/s² = 392 N ✅, 64 N ÷ 1.6 m/s² = 40 kg ✅

3. m = 147 N ÷ 9.8 m/s² = **15 kg**.
   Jaanch: 15 kg × 9.8 m/s² = 147 N ✅

4. Planet par W = 4 kg × 25 m/s² = **100 N**. Earth par W = 4 kg × 10 m/s² = **40 N**. Ratio = 100 N ÷ 40 N = **2.5 guna**.
   Jaanch: g ka ratio 25 ÷ 10 = 2.5 ✅

5. m = 72 N ÷ 1.6 m/s² = **45 kg**. Earth par W = 45 kg × 9.8 m/s² = **441 N**.
   Jaanch: 45 kg × 1.6 m/s² = 72 N ✅, 441 N ÷ 9.8 m/s² = 45 kg ✅

6. Net force = weight = m g = 2 kg × 9.8 m/s² = **19.6 N** (neeche ki taraf). a = F ÷ m = 19.6 N ÷ 2 kg = **9.8 m/s²** (= g).
   Jaanch: 2 kg × 9.8 m/s² = 19.6 N ✅

7. W₁ = 3 kg × 9.8 m/s² = 29.4 N, W₂ = 12 kg × 9.8 m/s² = 117.6 N. Ratio W₂ ÷ W₁ = 117.6 N ÷ 29.4 N = **4**, jo masses ke ratio 12 ÷ 3 = 4 ke barabar hai. Ratio g par **nirbhar nahi** karta, kyunki dono par same g hai aur wo ratio mein cut ho jaata hai.
   Jaanch: 29.4 × 4 = 117.6 N ✅

8. (a) **False** ❌. Mass nahi badalta, Moon par bhi wahi rehta hai.
   (b) **True** ✅. Weight force hai, SI unit newton.
   (c) **False** ❌. Weight vector hai (disha centre ki taraf).
   (d) **True** ✅. Spring ki khinchav seedha force (weight) batati hai.

9. kgf = 833 N ÷ 9.8 N/kgf = **85 kgf**. Mass m = 833 N ÷ 9.8 m/s² = **85 kg**.
   Jaanch: 85 kg × 9.8 m/s² = 833 N ✅

10. Machine tumhare weight (force) 539 N ko g = 9.8 m/s² se baant kar mass 55 kg screen par dikhati hai. Moon par weight 88 N hota hai; agar wahi machine (Earth ke g ke hisaab se bani) us par 88 N ÷ 9.8 m/s² ≈ 9 kg dikha de, to wo sirf machine ki galat ginti hai kyunki machine ko sahi g pata nahi. Mass cheez ke andar ke matter ki maatra hai, jo nahi badalti: Ravleen ka mass 55 kg hi rahega.
    Jaanch: 88 ÷ 9.8 = 8.98 ≈ 9 ✅, 88 ÷ 1.6 = 55 kg ✅
