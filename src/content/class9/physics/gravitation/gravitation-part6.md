# Topic 6: Satellites aur Tides ka Parichay

Pichle topic mein humne mass aur weight ka farq samjha: mass cheez mein matter ki matra hai aur kahin nahi badalti, jabki weight W = m g wo gravitational force hai jo jagah ke g ke hisaab se badalta hai (Moon par lagbhag ek-chhathaa). ## Part A: Chaand girta kyun nahi?

### Pehle ek galat soch hata dein

Kai log sochte hain ki Chaand Earth ki gravity se "bahar" hai, ya Earth Chaand ko kheench hi nahi rahi. **Dono baatein galat hain.** Earth Chaand ko bahut zor se kheench rahi hai, aur Chaand bhi Earth ko utne hi zor se kheenchta hai (Topic 2 ka universal law: do masses ek doosre ko barabar force se kheenchte hain). Asli sawaal ye hai ki kheenche jaane ke baad bhi Chaand gir kyun nahi jaata.

Jawaab ek pyaari si baat mein hai: **Chaand gir hi raha hai, har pal. Bas wo itni tez raftaar se saath mein side mein bhi chal raha hai ki girte girte Earth ko "chook jaata" hai.** Is idea ko samajhne ke liye Newton ne ek soch wala experiment (thought experiment) socha tha, jise **Newton ka cannon** kehte hain. Ye sach mein kiya hua experiment nahi, sirf dimaag mein socha hua hai.

### Newton ka cannon (thought experiment)

Kalpana karo ki ek bahut ooche pahaad ki choti par ek toap rakhi hai, aur wo goli seedhi **horizontal** (sidhi, zameen ke samaantar) daagti hai. Hawa ka rukaav bhi nahi maan lo.

**Step 1: kam raftaar.** Goli dheere nikalti hai. Gravity use neeche kheenchti hai (Topic 3 ki free fall), aur goli thodi door jaakar zameen par gir jaati hai.

**Step 2: zyada raftaar.** Goli aur tez nikalti hai. Ab wo zyada door jaakar girti hai, kyunki girne ke dauraan wo side mein zyada aage nikal chuki hoti hai.

**Step 3: bahut zyada raftaar.** Dhyan do, Earth gol hai. Goli jitna aage jaati hai, zameen utna hi neeche ki taraf mud jaati hai. Agar goli itni tez ho ki girte girte zameen ki gol sataah bhi usi dar se neeche mud jaaye, to goli kabhi zameen par pahunchegi hi nahi. Wo girti rahegi, par zameen use hamesha "chhodti" jaayegi.

**Step 4: poora chakkar.** Is haalat mein goli Earth ke chaaro taraf ghoomkar wapas pahaad ki choti ke paas aa jaayegi, aur ghoomti rahegi. Wo ab ek **satellite** ban gayi.

```
                 M  (pahaad ki choti par toap)
                 *----->  .  .  .
                 |'.  '.          .   (3) goli Earth ke chaaro
           .-----+--x----x--.       .     taraf ghoomti rehti hai
         .'     (1)  (2)     '.      .
        /                      \      .
       |         Earth          |      .
        \                      /      .
         '.                  .'      .
           '----------------'      .
              .  .  .  .  .  .  .

   (1) kam raftaar: paas giri    (2) zyada raftaar: aur door giri
   (x = jahan goli zameen par giri)
   (3) bahut zyada raftaar: zameen ki curve ke saath saath girti rehti hai
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Newton ka cannon (thought experiment). Pahaad M ki choti se horizontal daagi goli jitni tez hogi, utni door girti hai. Bahut tez hone par goli girte girte zameen ke curve ko "miss" karti rehti hai aur Earth ke chaaro taraf ghoom jaati hai.

Chaand ke saath yahi ho raha hai. Chaand ko kisi ne daaga nahi, par uske paas bhi Earth ke saath ek bahut badi sideways raftaar hai. Gravity use har pal Earth ki taraf kheenchti hai (yani wo gir raha hai), par uski sideways raftaar ki wajah se wo Earth ko miss karta rehta hai.

### Gravity hi "kendra ki taraf" wala force hai

Motion ke chapter mein humne dekha tha (Topic 7, uniform circular motion) ki jo cheez circle mein chalti hai, uski velocity har pal **tangent** ke saath hoti hai aur uski acceleration **kendra (centre) ki taraf** hoti hai. Aur jahan acceleration hai, wahan kisi ko force lagana padta hai. Dhaage se bandhe patthar mein ye force dhaage ka khichaav tha.

Chaand ke saath koi dhaaga nahi hai. Wahan **Earth ki gravity** wahi kaam karti hai: wo Chaand ko hamesha Earth ke kendra ki taraf kheenchti hai, aur isi se Chaand ka path lagbhag ek gol circle bana rehta hai. Agar achanak gravity band ho jaaye, to bilkul dhaage wale patthar ki tarah Chaand tangent ki disha mein seedha dur ud jaayega. Gravity usse mod-mod kar Earth ke paas rakhti hai.

Ye bhi zaroori hai: Chaand ki raftaar (jaise aage ek example mein nikalenge) lagbhag ek hi maan ki rehti hai, par disha har pal badalti hai. To Topic 7 ki tarah **speed same, par velocity badalti hai**, aur isliye acceleration hai (kendra ki taraf).

## Part B: Satellite kya hota hai

**Satellite wo cheez hai jo kisi bade pind ke chaaro taraf ek fixed path (orbit) mein ghoomti hai.** Do tarah ke hote hain.

**Natural satellite:** Prakriti ne banaya. Chaand Earth ka natural satellite hai. (Doosre planets ke bhi apne chaand hote hain.)

**Artificial satellite:** Insaan ka banaya hua. Isse **rocket** upar le jaata hai, aur sirf upar le jaana kaafi nahi hota: rocket use ek bahut badi **sideways raftaar** bhi deta hai (wahi Newton ke cannon wali tez horizontal raftaar). Is raftaar ke bina satellite seedha neeche gir jaata. Raftaar milne ke baad rocket alag ho jaata hai, aur satellite ko Earth ki gravity hi circle jaise path mein ghumati rehti hai.

### Artificial satellites ke kaam

| Kaam | Kya karta hai | Ek example |
|---|---|---|
| Communication | TV channel, phone call aur internet ke signals door door tak pahunchana | TV channels ka signal bhejne wale communication satellites |
| Weather | Baadalon aur toofaanon ki tasveeren lena, mausam ka andaaza lagana | Cyclone ko samundar par aate dekhna |
| Navigation | Phone ya gaadi ko uski jagah batana (GPS jaise system) | Maps app mein aapki location |
| Remote sensing | Aasmaan se Earth ki tasveeren: fasal, jungle, nadiyan, shehar | Baadh mein kitna ilaaka doob gaya, uska andaaza |

## Part C: Orbits: paas wale aur door wale

Orbit Earth se kitni door hai, isse satellite ki raftaar aur ek chakkar ke time par asar padta hai. Yahan sirf **qualitative** baat (hisaab ke bina, sirf idea) jaanna hai:

- **Earth ke paas (low orbit)** wale satellite **tez** ghoomte hain aur ek chakkar mein **kam time** lagate hain. Aise satellite ka ek chakkar lagbhag **90 minute** (1.5 ghante) mein poora ho jaata hai, yani din mein lagbhag 16 chakkar.
- **Bahut door** wale satellite **dheere** ghoomte hain aur chakkar mein zyada time lagate hain. Chaand bahut door hai (lagbhag 3,84,000 km), aur uska ek chakkar lagbhag **27 din** mein poora hota hai.

Raftaar aur time ka ye rishta kyun aisa hai, uska poora hisaab aage ki class mein aayega. Abhi bas ye pattern yaad rakho: **jitna paas, utna tez aur utna kam time.**

```
        Bahut door wala (dheere, zyada time)
      . - - - - - - - - - - - .
    .'   . - - - - - - - - .   '.
   /   .'    Paas wala       '.   \
  |   /     (tez, kam time)    \   |
  |  |         . - - .          |  |
  |  |        (Earth )          |  |
  |  |         ' - - '          |  |
   \  \                        /  /
    '.  ' - - - - - - - - - '  .'
      ' - - - - - - - - - - - '
```

> **Figure 6.2** – Earth ke chaaro taraf alag alag orbits. Andar ka orbit Earth ke paas hai (satellite tez ghoomta hai, ek chakkar mein kam time), baahar ka orbit door hai (dheere ghoomta hai, zyada time).

### Geostationary satellite (sirf naam)

Ek khaas orbit hai jahan satellite ka ek chakkar poore **24 ghante** mein hota hai, jitna Earth ek baar apni dhuri par ghoomti hai. Aise satellite ko **geostationary** satellite kehte hain. Earth se dekhne par wo aasmaan mein ek hi jagah par tika dikhta hai, isliye ek jagah ke upar bani dish antenna ek hi disha mein lagi rehti hai (jaise ghar ki TV dish). Iska hisaab aage ki class mein.

## Part D: Astronaut tairte kyun hain?

Space station mein astronauts hawa mein tairte hue dikhte hain, aur log sochte hain: "wahan gravity nahi hogi!" Ye **galat** hai.

Space station lagbhag 400 km upar hota hai, jabki Earth ki radius lagbhag 6400 km hai. Itni upar bhi Earth ki gravity bahut kam nahi hui: wahan gravity surface wale maan ka **lagbhag 90%** hoti hai (aage example mein inverse-square law se ye check karenge). Yani gravity wahan bhi bahut zyada hai.

Phir astronaut kyun tairte hain? Kyunki **astronaut aur spacecraft dono saath saath free fall mein hain**. Dono Earth ki taraf gir rahe hain, bilkul wahi Newton ke cannon wale tareeke se (gir bhi rahe hain aur Earth ko miss bhi kar rahe hain). Topic 3 mein dekha tha ki free fall mein sab cheezein ek jaisi acceleration se girti hain. Isliye astronaut apne spacecraft ke floor ko dabate hi nahi: wo aur floor, dono saath saath gir rahe hain.

Isi trah ek lift ki kalpana karo jiski rassi toot jaaye. Lift aur uske andar khada aadmi dono saath saath girte hain. Aadmi ko floor par apna weight "mehsoos" nahi hoga (haan, ye sirf soch hai, asli mein aisa kabhi mat karna!). Wahi baat satellite mein hai.

### Weighing scale zero kyun dikhata hai?

Topic 5 mein dekha tha ki weighing scale asal mein wo force naapta hai jisse hum use dabate hain. Zameen par hum scale ko apne weight ke barabar force se dabate hain, isliye scale hamara weight dikhata hai. Orbit mein astronaut aur scale dono free fall mein hain, to astronaut scale ko dabata hi nahi. Scale ka reading **0** aata hai.

Dhyan do: ye **apparent weightlessness** hai. Gravity ka force astronaut par abhi bhi lag raha hai (aur wahi use gira bhi raha hai), bas wo scale par nahi dikhta. Astronaut ka **mass** bilkul nahi badla.

## Part E: Tides: samundar ka upar-neeche hona

Ab Elisha ke pehle sawaal par aate hain. Samundar ka paani din mein do baar upar chadhta aur do baar utarta hai.

- **High tide:** jab paani kinare par sabse upar tak aata hai.
- **Low tide:** jab paani sabse peeche chala jaata hai aur retai ka bahut hissa khul jaata hai.

Tide koi "lahar" (wave) nahi hai. Lahar to hawa se bani chhoti chhoti hilne wali uthal-puthal hai. Tide **paani ke poore level ka dheere dheere (ghanto mein) upar-neeche hona** hai.

### Chaand ki gravity

Chaand ki gravity Earth ke samundar ke paani ko bhi kheenchti hai. Chaand ki gravity us taraf ke paani par **thodi zyada** hoti hai jo taraf Chaand ki taraf hai, kyunki wo paani Chaand ke **zyada paas** hai (Topic 2: doori kam, to force zyada). Isliye us taraf ka paani thoda upar uth jaata hai, ek **bulge** ban jaata hai, aur wahan **high tide** aati hai. Jaise Earth ghoomti hai, kinare ke shehar kabhi is bulge ke neeche aate hain, kabhi nahi.

Sooraj ki gravity bhi samundar par kaam karti hai, par Sooraj bahut door hai, isliye uska asar Chaand ke asar se **kam** hai (lagbhag aadhe se bhi kam). Dono milkar tide banate hain, aur tide mein Chaand ka role bada hota hai. Isliye "tide sirf Sooraj ki wajah se aati hai" galat hai.

### Do bulge kyun, ek nahi?

Aap soch sakte ho ki bulge sirf Chaand wali taraf hona chahiye. Par asal mein **do bulge** bante hain: ek Chaand ki taraf, aur ek Earth ke **doosri taraf** (Chaand se ulti disha mein). Isi liye din mein do baar high tide aati hai. Ye ulte taraf wala bulge kyun banta hai, iski **poori wajah aage ki class mein** aayegi. Abhi bas itna jaan lo ki Earth ke dono taraf paani ka ubhaar banta hai, aur Earth apni dhuri par ghoomti hai.

```
                        low tide
                  . - - - - - - - - .
            . '       .---------.       ' .
   high    (         (   Earth   )         )    high          Chaand
   tide     ' .       '---------'       . '     tide           ( )
                  ' - - - - - - - - '
                        low tide

 Bahar ka lamba ghera = samundar ka paani. Chaand ki taraf (daayein) aur
 uski ulti taraf (baayein) paani ubhra hua: do bulge, high tide.
 Upar aur neeche ke hisse mein paani kam: low tide.
```

> **Figure 6.3** – Earth ke aas-paas samundar ke paani mein do bulge bante hain: ek Chaand ki taraf, ek uski ulti taraf. Jab Earth ghoomte hue kisi shehar ko bulge ke neeche le jaati hai, wahan high tide hoti hai; bulge ke beech wale ilaake mein low tide hoti hai.

### Din mein do tides: waqt

Ek jagah par ek high tide se agli high tide tak lagbhag **12 ghante 25 minute** lagte hain. Isliye din mein lagbhag do high tides aur do low tides aati hain. Kyunki 12 h 25 min ka do guna 24 h 50 min hota hai, har din ki tide pichle din se lagbhag 50 minute **der se** aati hai. (Aisa isliye ki Chaand bhi Earth ke chaaro taraf ghoom raha hai, par ye bhi aage ki class mein.)

### Spring tide aur neap tide

Tide kabhi bahut badi hoti hai aur kabhi chhoti.

- **Spring tide:** **Poornima** (full moon) aur **amavasya** (new moon) ke din Sooraj, Earth aur Chaand lagbhag **ek seedhi line mein** hote hain. Chaand aur Sooraj dono ka asar ek hi disha mein judta hai, to high tide bahut oonchi aur low tide bahut neeche hoti hai.
- **Neap tide:** **Aadhe Chaand** (half moon, ashtami ke aas paas) ke din Chaand aur Sooraj Earth se lagbhag sahi-kone (90°) par hote hain. Unka asar ek doosre ko thoda kaat deta hai, to tide chhoti hoti hai: high tide kam oonchi, low tide kam neeche.

```
  Spring tide (seedhi line)            Neap tide (sahi-kone par)

   Sooraj ---- Earth ---- Chaand        Chaand
   (ya Chaand ---- Earth ---- Sooraj)      |
                                           |
   Dono ka asar jud jaata hai          Earth ------- Sooraj
   -> bahut badi tide                  Asar ek-doosre ko kaat dete hain
                                       -> chhoti tide
```

> **Figure 6.4** – Spring tide: Sooraj, Earth aur Chaand ek seedhi line mein (poornima ya amavasya). Neap tide: Chaand aur Sooraj Earth se sahi-kone par (aadha Chaand).

### Machhuaare aur tide table

Mr. Pereira jaise machhuaare aur naav chalane wale **tide table** dekhte hain: kisi bandargaah (port) ke liye har din ki high tide aur low tide ka waqt pehle se likha hota hai. Isse wo tay karte hain ki kab naav samundar mein utaarni hai, kab bandargaah mein ghusna safe hai, aur kab kinare ki retai par kaam karna hai.

## Part F: Hisaab ke examples

(Is topic mein jo data diya gaya hai, wo approximate figures hain. Hum unse sirf basic hisaab karenge. π = 3.14.)

### Example 1: Satellite ka time period aur frequency

Ek low-orbit satellite din mein 16 chakkar lagata hai. Uska time period T aur frequency f nikalo.

1 din = 24 h = 24 × 3600 s = 86,400 s
T = 24 h ÷ 16 = **1.5 h** = 1.5 × 3600 s = **5400 s**
f = 1 ÷ T = 1 ÷ 5400 s = **1.85 × 10⁻⁴ Hz** (lagbhag; yani 0.000185 chakkar har second)

Jaanch: 5400 s × 16 chakkar = 86,400 s = 1 din ✅ aur 1.85 × 10⁻⁴ /s × 5400 s = 1.0 chakkar ✅

### Example 2: Ek orbit mein kitni doori?

Satellite ke orbit ki radius r = 6.8 × 10⁶ m hai. Ek chakkar ka time T = 5400 s. Ek chakkar mein satellite kitni doori tay karta hai, aur uski speed kitni hai? (Yahan speed hum gravity se nahi nikaal rahe, bas diya hua data use kar rahe hain.)

Ek chakkar mein doori = circumference = 2πr = 2 × 3.14 × 6.8 × 10⁶ m = **4.27 × 10⁷ m** (lagbhag; exact 4.2704 × 10⁷ m)
Speed v = 2πr ÷ T = 4.2704 × 10⁷ m ÷ 5400 s = **7908 m/s ≈ 7.9 km/s**

Jaanch: 7908 m/s × 5400 s = 4.27 × 10⁷ m ✅. Ye lagbhag 28,500 km/h hai, yani kisi gaadi ya jet se kaafi zyada tez.

### Example 3: Chaand ki speed

Chaand ke orbit ki radius r = 3.84 × 10⁸ m hai aur uska time period T = 27.3 din. Chaand ki speed nikalo.

T = 27.3 din = 27.3 × 24 × 3600 s = 27.3 × 86,400 s = **2.36 × 10⁶ s** (lagbhag; exact 2,358,720 s)
Ek chakkar mein doori = 2πr = 2 × 3.14 × 3.84 × 10⁸ m = **2.41 × 10⁹ m**
v = 2πr ÷ T = 2.4115 × 10⁹ m ÷ 2.3587 × 10⁶ s = **1022 m/s ≈ 1.02 km/s**

Jaanch: 1022 m/s × 2,358,720 s = 2.41 × 10⁹ m ✅

Dekho, Chaand ki speed (1.02 km/s) low-orbit satellite ki speed (7.9 km/s) se bahut kam hai, aur uska time period bhi bahut zyada hai: 27.3 din = 655.2 h, jabki satellite ka 1.5 h. Ye wahi pattern hai: **door wala dheere aur zyada time lekar ghoomta hai.**

### Example 4: Din mein kitni high tides?

Ek bandargaah par do successive high tides ke beech 12 h 25 min hota hai. 24 ghante mein kitni high tides aayengi?

12 h 25 min = 12 × 60 min + 25 min = 745 min
24 h = 24 × 60 min = 1440 min
Tides ki sankhya = 1440 min ÷ 745 min = **1.93** (lagbhag)

Yani ek din (24 h) mein lagbhag do high tides, par poore 2 se thoda kam. Isi se agle din tide pehle din se der se aati hai.

Jaanch: 745 min × 2 = 1490 min = 24 h 50 min, jo 24 h se 50 min zyada hai ✅

### Example 5: Tide ka time-table

Subah 6:00 baje ek high tide aayi. Agli high tide aur uske baad ki high tide kab aayengi? (12 h 25 min ka gap)

Agli high tide = 6:00 + 12 h 25 min = **6:25 shaam (18:25)**
Uske baad = 18:25 + 12 h 25 min = **6:50 subah (agle din)**

Jaanch: 6:00 subah se 6:50 agle subah tak = 24 h 50 min = 2 × 12 h 25 min ✅

### Example 6: Orbit mein gravity kitni?

Earth ki radius R = 6.4 × 10⁶ m hai. Ek satellite Earth ke kendra se r = 6.8 × 10⁶ m par ghoom raha hai. Wahan gravity surface ki gravity ka kitna hissa hai? (Surface par g = 9.8 m/s².)

Topic 2 ka inverse-square law: doori kendra se naapi jaati hai, aur gravity (force ya g) doori ke square ke ulte hisaab se badalti hai.

r ÷ R = 6.8 × 10⁶ m ÷ 6.4 × 10⁶ m = 1.0625
g(orbit) ÷ g(surface) = 1 ÷ (r ÷ R)² = 1 ÷ (1.0625)² = 1 ÷ 1.1289 = **0.886** (yani lagbhag 89%)
g(orbit) = 0.886 × 9.8 m/s² = **8.68 m/s²** (lagbhag)

Jaanch: (6.4 ÷ 6.8)² = (0.9412)² = 0.886 ✅ aur 8.68 ÷ 9.8 = 0.886 ✅

Yani wahan gravity lagbhag 90% hai. Ye wahi "lagbhag 90%" hai jo humne Part D mein kaha tha: gravity gayab nahi hui, sirf thodi kam hui hai. (Agar r = 1.07 R lete, to 1 ÷ 1.07² = 0.873 yani lagbhag 87%, tab bhi lagbhag 90% ke aas-paas.)

### Example 7: Astronaut ka force aur scale

Ek astronaut ka mass 60 kg hai. (a) Zameen par uska weight kitna hai? (b) Example 6 ke orbit par us par gravity ka force kitna hai? (c) Scale kitna dikhayega?

(a) W = m g = 60 kg × 9.8 m/s² = **588 N**
(b) F = m g(orbit) = 60 kg × 8.68 m/s² = **520.8 N** (lagbhag 521 N; yani surface ke 89% ke aas-paas)
(c) Scale ka reading **0 N**, kyunki astronaut aur scale dono free fall mein hain, aur astronaut scale ko dabata hi nahi.

Jaanch: 520.8 N ÷ 588 N = 0.886 ✅ (Example 6 wala hissa). Aur mass 60 kg dono jagah wahi hai.

### Example 8: Doori double ho jaaye to?

Ek jagah Earth ke kendra se doori 2R hai (R = Earth ki radius). Wahan gravity surface ki gravity ka kitna hissa hogi? Surface se wo kitni oonchai par hai?

g(2R) ÷ g(R) = 1 ÷ (2R ÷ R)² = 1 ÷ 2² = **1/4** = 0.25
g(2R) = 9.8 m/s² ÷ 4 = **2.45 m/s²**

Oonchai: kendra se 2R, to surface se oonchai = 2R − R = **R = 6.4 × 10⁶ m** (6400 km)

Jaanch: 0.25 × 9.8 m/s² = 2.45 m/s² ✅. Ye wahi jagah hai jahan Topic 3 (Example 9) mein g = 9.77 m/s² lekar 2.44 m/s² aaya tha; yahan surface ka g = 9.8 m/s² liya, isliye 2.45 m/s². Dhyan do: Topic 2 mein bhi dekha tha ki kendra se naapo, surface se nahi. Is par 60 kg ke aadmi ka force = 60 kg × 2.45 m/s² = 147 N hota (zameen par 588 N tha, yani chauthai: 588 N ÷ 4 = 147 N ✅).

## Part G: Kahani ka hisaab

Elisha ne Mr. Pereira se poochha, "To Chaand roz Earth ke chaaro taraf kitni tez ghoomta hai?"

Mr. Pereira ne bataya ki Chaand ki orbit ki radius lagbhag 3.84 × 10⁸ m hai aur ek chakkar lagbhag 27.3 din mein hota hai. Elisha ne hisaab kiya: Example 3 ke hisaab se Chaand ki speed lagbhag **1.02 km/s** hai.

"Ek second mein 1 km se zyada! Phir bhi girta nahi?"

"Girta hai, beta," Mr. Pereira ne kaha, "par jitna wo ek second mein Earth ki taraf girta hai, utna hi wo side mein aage nikal jaata hai, isliye uska raasta Earth ke chaaro taraf mudta rehta hai. Wo Newton ke cannon wali goli ki tarah hai: gir raha hai, aur Earth ko miss kar raha hai."

"Aur paani?"

"Wahi Chaand, wahi gravity. Chaand ki gravity Earth ke paani ko bhi thoda sa upar kheenchti hai. Isliye Chaand ke saath saath paani ke bulge bante hain. Earth ghoomti hai, to hamare beach par din mein do baar paani chadhta hai, aur do baar utarta hai. Aaj shaam high tide thi, isliye retai par paani upar aaya. Dopahar mein low tide thi, isliye paani peeche tha."

Elisha ne kaha, "Aur agli high tide?" Mr. Pereira ne ghadi dekhi. "Kal subah tak lagbhag 12 ghante 25 minute baad, aur uske baad phir. Dekho, humne tide table mein bhi likha hai." Elisha ne ek baar phir Chaand ko dekha aur muskura di: ab wo sirf ek chamakti gend nahi thi, wo Earth ki taraf girta hua, par kabhi na pahunchne wala ek saathi tha.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Orbit mein gravity nahi hoti"**
Hoti hai. Low orbit mein gravity lagbhag 90% hai (Example 6). Astronaut isliye tairte hain kyunki wo aur spacecraft dono free fall mein hain, gravity ke na hone ki wajah se nahi.

**Galti 2: "Chaand par Earth ki gravity kaam nahi karti"**
Karti hai, aur wahi use circle jaise path mein rakhti hai. Gravity ke bina Chaand tangent ki disha mein seedha chala jaata.

**Galti 3: "Tide sirf Sooraj ki wajah se aati hai"**
Chaand ka asar bada hai (wo Earth ke bahut paas hai), Sooraj ka kam. Dono milkar tide banate hain; spring aur neap tide mein dono ki position ka khel dikhta hai.

**Galti 4: "Satellite isliye tika hai kyunki wo Earth ki gravity se bahar hai"**
Nahi. Satellite gravity ke andar hi hai, aur gravity hi use orbit mein rakhti hai. Wo gir raha hai, par sideways tez raftaar ki wajah se Earth ko miss karta rehta hai.

**Galti 5: "Tide ek wave hai"**
Wave hawa se bani chhoti uthal-puthal hai. Tide paani ke poore level ka ghanto mein upar-neeche hona hai.

**Galti 6: High aur low tide mix karna**
High tide: paani sabse upar, beach par kam retai khuli. Low tide: paani sabse peeche, beach par bahut retai khuli. Elisha ne dopahar mein low tide dekhi thi aur shaam ko high tide.

**Galti 7: "2R par doori ka matlab surface se 2R oonchai"**
Nahi. 2R **kendra** se hai, to surface se oonchai sirf R hai (Example 8).

## Quick Recap

- Chaand Earth ko girta nahi hai kyunki wo **gir bhi raha hai aur saath mein tez sideways bhi chal raha hai**, to Earth ko miss karta rehta hai. Newton ka cannon is idea ka thought experiment hai.
- Earth ki gravity hi wo force hai jo Chaand ko kendra ki taraf kheenchti hai aur path ko lagbhag circle rakhti hai (circular motion ki "kendra ki taraf acceleration").
- **Satellite:** natural (Chaand) ya artificial (rocket se upar, bahut tez sideways raftaar ke saath). Kaam: communication, weather, navigation, remote sensing.
- Paas wale orbit mein satellite tez aur kam time mein ghoomta hai (lagbhag 90 minute); door wale mein dheere aur zyada time (Chaand lagbhag 27 din). **Geostationary** satellite ka chakkar 24 h ka hota hai aur wo ek hi jagah ke upar tika dikhta hai.
- Low orbit mein gravity lagbhag **90%** hoti hai. Astronaut isliye tairte hain kyunki wo aur spacecraft dono **free fall** mein hain. Scale ka reading 0, par gravity aur mass nahi badle.
- **Tides:** Chaand ki gravity (aur kam, Sooraj ki) paani ko upar uthati hai: do bulge, to din mein lagbhag do high aur do low tides (high tides ke beech lagbhag 12 h 25 min).
- **Spring tide:** poornima aur amavasya, Sooraj aur Chaand ek line mein, badi tide. **Neap tide:** aadha Chaand, chhoti tide.
- Machhuaare aur naavik **tide table** dekhte hain.

## Practice Questions

1. A satellite completes 12 orbits in one day. Find its time period in hours and in seconds, and its frequency in Hz (give f to three significant figures).

2. A satellite moves in a circular orbit of radius 7.0 × 10⁶ m and takes 5800 s for one orbit. Find the distance travelled in one orbit and the speed of the satellite in km/s. Take π = 3.14.

3. Using the Moon's speed of 1.02 km/s, find the distance (in km) that the Moon travels in one day. Take 1 day = 86,400 s.

4. At a port, a high tide occurs at 4:00 am. At what times will the next two high tides occur if successive high tides are 12 h 25 min apart? Roughly when will the low tide in between occur (assume it is midway)?

5. Find the value of g at a point whose distance from the centre of the Earth is 3R. Take g = 9.8 m/s² on the surface. How high above the surface is this point?

6. A 75 kg astronaut is in the orbit of Example 6 where g is 8.68 m/s². What is the gravitational force on the astronaut, and what does a weighing scale in the spacecraft read? Why are they different?

7. State whether each statement is true or false, with a reason.
   (a) There is no gravity inside a spacecraft orbiting the Earth.
   (b) The Moon is not attracted by the Earth.
   (c) A satellite in a low orbit takes less time per round than the Moon.
   (d) Tides are the same as waves.

8. Explain in your own words, using the idea of a cannon on a very high mountain, why a satellite does not fall to the Earth.

9. What is the difference between a spring tide and a neap tide? On which days of the lunar month do they occur?

10. Name any two uses of artificial satellites and say what a geostationary satellite means.

### Answers

1. T = 24 h ÷ 12 = **2 h** = 2 × 3600 s = **7200 s**. f = 1 ÷ 7200 s = **1.39 × 10⁻⁴ Hz** (lagbhag).
   Jaanch: 7200 s × 12 = 86,400 s = 1 din ✅

2. Distance = 2πr = 2 × 3.14 × 7.0 × 10⁶ m = **4.396 × 10⁷ m**. Speed = 4.396 × 10⁷ m ÷ 5800 s = 7579 m/s ≈ **7.58 km/s**.
   Jaanch: 7579 m/s × 5800 s = 4.396 × 10⁷ m ✅

3. Distance = speed × time = 1.02 km/s × 86,400 s = **88,128 km** (lagbhag 8.8 × 10⁴ km). (Agar 1022 m/s lo to 88,300 km; dono lagbhag same.)
   Jaanch: 88,128 km ÷ 86,400 s = 1.02 km/s ✅

4. Pehli: 4:00 am + 12 h 25 min = **4:25 pm**. Dusri: 4:25 pm + 12 h 25 min = **4:50 am (agle din)**.
   Beech ki low tide: lagbhag 12 h 25 min ÷ 2 = 6 h 12.5 min baad, yani lagbhag **10:12 am**.
   Jaanch: 4:00 am se 4:50 am agle din = 24 h 50 min = 2 × 12 h 25 min ✅

5. g(3R) = 9.8 m/s² ÷ 3² = 9.8 m/s² ÷ 9 = **1.09 m/s²** (lagbhag). Oonchai = 3R − R = 2R = 2 × 6.4 × 10⁶ m = **1.28 × 10⁷ m** (12,800 km).
   Jaanch: 1.09 m/s² × 9 = 9.81 m/s² ≈ 9.8 m/s² ✅

6. Gravitational force = 75 kg × 8.68 m/s² = **651 N**. Scale ka reading = **0 N**.
   Dono alag isliye ki astronaut aur scale dono free fall mein hain, astronaut scale ko dabata hi nahi. Gravity ka force wahan abhi bhi lag raha hai.
   Jaanch: 651 N ÷ 75 kg = 8.68 m/s² ✅

7. (a) **False** ❌. Gravity wahan lagbhag 90% hai; astronaut free fall mein hain isliye tairte hain.
   (b) **False** ❌. Earth Chaand ko kheenchti hai aur wahi use circular path mein rakhti hai.
   (c) **True** ✅. Low orbit ka time lagbhag 90 minute, Chaand ka lagbhag 27 din.
   (d) **False** ❌. Wave hawa se bani chhoti uthal-puthal hai; tide paani ke poore level ka ghanto mein upar-neeche hona hai, jo mainly Chaand ki gravity se hota hai.

8. Satellite ko rocket bahut tez sideways raftaar deta hai. Gravity use har pal Earth ki taraf kheenchti hai, to wo gir raha hai, par sideways raftaar itni zyada hai ki Earth ki gol sataah bhi usi dar se neeche mud jaati hai. Goli (satellite) Earth ko miss karti rehti hai aur uske chaaro taraf ghoomti hai.

9. Spring tide: Sooraj, Earth, Chaand lagbhag seedhi line mein, dono ka asar judta hai, badi tide. **Poornima aur amavasya** ke din. Neap tide: Chaand aur Sooraj Earth se sahi-kone par, asar ek-doosre ko kaat deta hai, chhoti tide. **Aadhe Chaand** ke din.

10. Do uses: communication (TV, phone signals), weather (cyclone ki tasveeren), navigation, remote sensing (kisi bhi do). Geostationary satellite ka chakkar 24 h mein hota hai, isliye wo Earth se dekhne par ek hi jagah ke upar tika dikhta hai.
