# Topic 1: Gravity Kya Hai?

Pichhle chapter mein Newton ke laws ne humein ek badi baat samjhayi: **force lagta hai, tabhi motion badalti hai**. Rukti cheez tab chalti hai jab koi unbalanced force lage, aur chalti cheez tab mudti ya rukti hai jab koi force use roke ya mode. Ab ek sawal bacha tha: wo kaun sa force hai jo seb ko, aam ko, barish ki boondon ko hamesha neeche hi khinchta hai? Is chapter ka pehla topic usi **ek bahut khaas force** ke baare mein hai: **gravity**.

---

## Aam ke bagh mein Hriday ka sawal

Garmiyon ki dopahar thi. Mr. Yadav aam ke bagh ke watchman the, aur unke paas ek chhota sa bachcha Hriday baitha tha, jo chhuttiyon mein roz bagh dekhne aata tha. Hawa bilkul band thi: ek patta bhi nahi hil raha tha.

Achanak ek pakka aam daal se toota aur **seedha neeche** zameen par aa gira: thap!

Hriday ne aam utha kar dekha aur bola, "Chacha, ek baat batao. Aam hamesha seedha neeche hi kyun girta hai? Hawa nahi chal rahi, phir bhi wo na kabhi side mein gaya, na upar uda. Aisa lagta hai jaise koi use neeche se kheench raha ho. **Kaun kheench raha hai use?**"

Mr. Yadav muskuraye, "Bahut achha sawal pucha beta. Sahi pakda tune: koi hai jo kheench raha hai. Wo dikhta nahi, par har cheez ko kheenchta hai. Chal, aaj usi ko samajhte hain."

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Gravitational force (gravity)** | Do cheezon ke beech ek dusre ko kheenchne wala force, jo unki mass ki wajah se hota hai | Dharti ka aam ko kheenchna |
| **Attractive force** | Wo force jo cheezon ko paas laane ki koshish kare | Gravity |
| **Repulsive force** | Wo force jo cheezon ko door dhakele | Do same magnetic poles ka ek dusre ko dhakelna |
| **Non-contact force** | Wo force jo bina chhue, door se lagta hai | Gravity, magnet ka kheenchna |
| **Mass** | Cheez mein kitna padarth (matter) hai, kg mein naapa jata hai | Aam ki mass 0.4 kg |
| **Kendra (centre)** | Dharti ke bilkul beech ka point | Gravity ka kheenchav isi ki taraf hota hai |

## Part A: Cheezein neeche kyun girti hain

Mr. Yadav ne Hriday se kaha, "Pehle ek chhota sa experiment kar. Ye aam haath mein pakad, phir chhod de."

Hriday ne aam chhoda. Wo neeche gira. Phir usne ek kankar upar uchhala: wo kuch der upar gaya, ruka aur wapas neeche aa gaya.

Mr. Yadav ne poochha, "Kabhi aisa hua ki chhodi hui cheez side mein ya upar chali gayi ho, jab koi usse chhu bhi na raha ho?" Hriday ne sar hilaya, "Nahi chacha, hamesha neeche."

Iska matlab: **zameen har cheez ko apni taraf kheench rahi hai.** Ye kheenchav ek force hai, aur is force ko kehte hain **gravitational force** ya sirf **gravity** (hindi mein **gurutvakarshan**).

> **Gravity = wo kheenchne wala force jo do cheezon ke beech unki mass ki wajah se lagta hai.**

Jab hum "gravity" kehte hain aur koi aur cheez nahi bolte, to aksar matlab hota hai **Dharti ka kheenchav** (jise **gravity of the Earth** bhi kehte hain). Aam ke neeche girne ka karan yehi hai: Dharti aam ko kheench rahi hai.

## Part B: Neeche ka matlab kya hai: kendra ki taraf

Hriday ne ek aur sawal pucha, "Chacha, mere cousin Australia mein rehte hain. Wo to hamare ulte taraf hain, phir bhi wahan aam neeche hi girta hoga, upar nahi. Aisa kaise?"

Mr. Yadav ne mitti mein ek bada gol ghera banaya aur bole, "Yahi to khoobsurat baat hai. Dharti gol hai, aur wo har cheez ko **apne kendra ki taraf** kheenchti hai. Hamare liye 'neeche' ka matlab hai 'Dharti ke kendra ki taraf'. Isliye Australia mein bhi 'neeche' wahi hai: Dharti ke kendra ki taraf."

```
              Hum yahan (India)
                   |
          aam      v
           v   .---------.   v
          ->  /           \  <-
             |    kendra   |
         ->  |      *      |  <-
             |             |
          ->  \           /  <-
           ^   '---------'   ^
                   ^
                   |
          Dharti ki doosri taraf ke log
          (unke liye bhi tir kendra ki taraf)
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Dharti ke charon taraf gravity ke tir kendra (centre) ki taraf hote hain. Isliye Dharti ke kisi bhi hisse mein khade logon ko "neeche" ka matlab kendra ki taraf hi lagta hai.

Seedhi baat: **"neeche" koi fixed disha nahi hai.** Gravity ki disha hamesha Dharti ke kendra ki taraf hoti hai. Hamare ghar se wo neeche lagta hai, Australia mein bhi neeche, aur bilkul ulti taraf wale logon ko bhi neeche.

## Part C: Gravity sirf Dharti ki nahi: har mass wali cheez ki hoti hai

Hriday ne socha, "To kya sirf Dharti hi kheenchti hai?"

Mr. Yadav ne kaha, "Nahi beta. Gravity un **sabhi cheezon** ke beech hoti hai jinki mass hai. Matlab:

- Dharti aam ko kheenchti hai.
- **Aam bhi Dharti ko kheenchta hai.**
- **Chaand** (Moon) Dharti ko kheenchta hai, aur Dharti chaand ko.
- **Sooraj** (Sun) Dharti ko kheenchta hai, aur Dharti sooraj ko.
- Hriday, tu aur main bhi ek dusre ko kheench rahe hain!"

Hriday hans pada, "Chacha, phir hum dono ek dusre ki taraf kheenche kyun nahi jaate?"

### Pen aur kitaab ek dusre ko kheench kyun nahi lete

Mr. Yadav ne kaha, "Sach ye hai ki mez par rakhi **pen aur kitaab bhi ek dusre ko kheenchte hain**. Par gravity ka kheenchav **bahut hi chhota** hota hai, jab tak ki kam se kam ek cheez **bahut badi (bahut zyada mass wali)** na ho, jaise Dharti. Pen aur kitaab ke beech ka kheenchav itna kam hai ki use na hum mehsoos kar sakte hain, na wo pen ko hila sakta hai, kyunki mez ka friction use rok leta hai."

| Cheezein | Kheenchav kaisa? | Humein kyun dikhta hai ya nahi |
|---|---|---|
| Dharti aur aam | Bahut bada asar | Dharti ki mass bahut zyada hai, aam turant gir jata hai |
| Chaand aur Dharti | Bahut bada | Isi ki wajah se chaand Dharti ke chakkar lagata hai (Topic 6 mein) |
| Sooraj aur Dharti | Bahut bada | Dono ki mass bahut zyada hai |
| Pen aur kitaab | Bahut hi chhota | Dono ki mass kam hai, asar dikhta nahi |
| Hriday aur Mr. Yadav | Bahut hi chhota | Dono ki mass Dharti ke mukabley na ke barabar hai |

**Dharti ko aam ka kheenchav kyun nahi dikhta?** Dharti aam ko kheenchti hai, aur **aam bhi Dharti ko utne hi zor se kheenchta hai** (Newton ka Teesra Law yaad karo: action aur reaction barabar). Par Dharti ki mass itni bahut zyada hai ki us ek chhote se kheenchav se Dharti ka zara bhi hilna bhi hume dikhta nahi. Ye "kitna kheenchta hai" ka sawal hum Topic 2 mein number ke saath dekhenge.

## Part D: Gravity ke khaas gun

### 1. Gravity hamesha attractive hai

Gravity sirf **kheenchta** hai, kabhi dhakelta nahi. Aisa koi tarika nahi mila jisse koi cheez gravity se dhakeli jaye. Isliye gravity **hamesha attractive** force hai, kabhi repulsive nahi.

### 2. Gravity bina chhue kaam karti hai

Aam daal se toota, hawa mein tha, kisi ne use chhua nahi, phir bhi Dharti ne use kheench liya. Yaani gravity ek **non-contact force** hai. Force aur Laws of Motion chapter ke [Topic 1](../force-and-laws-of-motion/force-and-laws-of-motion.md) (Part F) mein humne non-contact forces ki list banayi thi: gravity, magnetic force aur electrostatic force. Gravity usi list ka pehla naam hai.

### 3. Gravity har waqt, har jagah lagti hai

Gravity sirf zameen ke paas nahi lagti. Ye pahaad ki choti par bhi lagti hai, hawai jahaaz ki unchai par bhi, aur **antariksh mein bhi**. Doori badhne par ye kam hoti jaati hai, par poori tarah khatam kabhi nahi hoti (ye "kitni kam" wali baat bhi Topic 2 mein aayegi).

### 4. Gravity sab cheezon par lagti hai, bhaari ho ya halki

Dharti sirf bhaari cheezon ko nahi kheenchti. Patta, patthar, aam, kitaab: sab par gravity lagti hai. Kam mass wali cheez par bhi, bas uska asar number mein alag hota hai.

## Part E: Chaand, Sooraj aur Dharti ka rishta

Hriday ne aasman ki taraf dekha. Din mein halka sa chaand bhi dikh raha tha. "Chacha, chaand neeche kyun nahi girta?"

Mr. Yadav ne kaha, "Bahut accha sawal hai, aur iska poora jawab hum is chapter ke aakhri topic (Topic 6) mein dekhenge. Abhi ye samajh le ki chaand ko bhi **Dharti kheench rahi hai**, aur isi kheenchav ki wajah se chaand seedha ud kar door nahi jaata, balki Dharti ke chakkar lagata rehta hai. Aur Dharti ko **Sooraj kheench raha hai**, isliye Dharti Sooraj ke chakkar lagati hai."

Yaani gravity sirf hamare aam gira dene wala force nahi, balki **Chaand, Dharti aur Sooraj ko apni jagah par saath baandh kar rakhne wala force** bhi hai.

## Part F: Gravity aur Newton ke laws

Pichhle chapter ka sabak yahan kaam aata hai. Dekhte hain aam ke saath kya hua:

- Daal par laga aam **rest** mein tha. Daal use pakde thi (daal ka force upar, gravity ka force neeche: balanced).
- Jab aam toota, daal ka force hat gaya. Ab sirf gravity bacha: **unbalanced force**.
- **Newton ka Doosra Law** kehta hai: unbalanced force lage to cheez **accelerate** karti hai. Aam ki speed 0 se badhti gayi: wo neeche ki taraf **accelerate** hua.
- **Newton ka Pehla Law** kehta hai: agar koi unbalanced force na ho, to cheez ki motion nahi badalti. Isliye daal par aam tab tak ruka raha jab tak force balanced tha.

Samajhne ke liye ek chhota hypothetical (kalpanik) number: agar kisi 0.5 kg ki cheez par sirf 2 N ka unbalanced force lage, to a = F ÷ m = 2 N ÷ 0.5 kg = **4 m/s²**. Gravity ka force kis tarah ka acceleration deta hai, ye Topic 3 mein aayega. Abhi bas ye yaad rakho: gravity ek asli force hai, aur wahi niyam is par bhi lagte hain.

**Jaanch:** 4 m/s² × 0.5 kg = 2 N ✅

### Latakti cheez: jab kuch nahi girta

Ek bulb ko chhat se dhaage se latka diya. Bulb **rest** mein hai, gir nahi raha. Kya gravity band ho gayi? Nahi.

```
        ===== chhat =====
              |
              |   <- dhaage ka force (tension), upar
              |
            ( O )  bulb
              |
              v   <- gravity ka force, neeche
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Latke hue bulb par do force: gravity neeche, dhaage ka tension upar. Dono barabar aur ulte hain, isliye bulb rest mein hai.

Bulb par gravity neeche kheench rahi hai, aur dhaaga use upar kheench raha hai. Ye dono force **balanced** hain, isliye net force zero hai aur bulb rukha hua hai. (Is neeche ke force ko hum "bulb ka **weight**" kehte hain: iska poora hisaab Topic 5 mein.)

## Part G: Agar gravity na hoti to? (soch ka experiment)

Mr. Yadav ne kaha, "Maan le ek second ke liye gravity band ho jaaye."

- Aam daal se toot kar neeche nahi girta, **seedha tairta hua hawa mein chala jata**.
- Barish ki boondein neeche nahi aati.
- Hum zameen par tik kar chal nahi paate, halka sa kudne par hi upar uthte chale jaate.
- Dharti ki hawa (atmosphere) bhi bikhar kar chali jaati.

Ek jhalak: **antariksh yatri (astronaut)** space station mein hawa mein tairte dikhte hain. Kya wahan gravity nahi hoti? Ye ek aam galatfehmi hai, jiska sahi jawab hum **weightlessness** ke naam se Topic 5 aur 6 mein dekhenge.

## Part H: Newton aur seb ki kahani (kaha jaata hai)

**Kaha jaata hai** ki ek din Isaac Newton ek seb ke ped ke neeche baithe the, aur ek seb neeche gira. Is se Newton ke dimaag mein ek sawal aaya: "Seb neeche kyun gira? Kya wahi force jo seb ko neeche kheench raha hai, chaand tak bhi pahunchta hai?"

Ye kahani ek **legend (kimvadanti)** hai: kahani ka kitna hissa sach hai, ye pakka nahi kaha ja sakta. Par sachchi baat ye hai ki Newton ne ek bahut bada vichar socha: **jo force seb ko zameen par girata hai, wahi force chaand ko Dharti ke chakkar lagwata hai.** Yaani aasman aur zameen ke liye alag-alag niyam nahi, ek hi niyam hai. Is niyam ko hi **Universal Law of Gravitation** kehte hain, jo hum agle topic mein padhenge.

## Part I: Gravity aur magnetic force ki tulna

Dono bina chhue kaam karte hain, par dono mein bhi farak hai.

| Baat | Gravity | Magnetic force |
|---|---|---|
| Chhue bina kaam karta hai? | Haan, non-contact | Haan, non-contact |
| Kheenchta hai? | Haan, hamesha | Haan (alag poles) |
| Dhakelta hai? | Kabhi nahi | Haan (same poles) |
| Kin cheezon par lagta hai? | **Har cheez jisme mass hai** | Sirf magnet aur kuch dhaatuon (jaise lohe) par |
| Kab dikhta hai? | Jab ek cheez bahut badi ho (jaise Dharti) | Chhote magnet mein bhi dikh jata hai |

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Gravity sirf zameen ke paas lagti hai."**
Sahi: gravity door tak jaati hai. Chaand ko Dharti ki gravity hi baandhe hue hai, jo lagbhag 3,84,000 km door hai. Doori badhne par ye kam hoti hai, par khatam nahi hoti.

**Galti 2: "Gravity hamesha neeche ki taraf hoti hai."**
Sahi: gravity hamesha **Dharti ke kendra ki taraf** hoti hai. Australia ke log hamse ulte lagte hain, par unke liye bhi "neeche" kendra ki taraf hi hai.

**Galti 3: "Antariksh mein gravity nahi hoti."**
Sahi: antariksh mein bhi gravity hoti hai. Isi ki wajah se chaand aur satellite Dharti ke paas bane rehte hain. Space station mein cheezein kyun tairti hain, ye Topic 5 aur 6 mein dekhenge.

**Galti 4: "Bhaari cheezein kheenchi jaati hain, halki nahi."**
Sahi: gravity har mass wali cheez par lagti hai, halki ho ya bhaari. Ek patta bhi Dharti ki taraf kheencha jaata hai, bas hawa ki wajah se dheere girta hai.

**Galti 5: "Aam Dharti ko nahi kheenchta, sirf Dharti aam ko kheenchti hai."**
Sahi: aam bhi Dharti ko kheenchta hai. Par Dharti ki mass bahut zyada hai, isliye uska asar na ke barabar hai. Ye "kitna" Topic 2 mein dekhenge.

**Galti 6: "Cheezein hawa ke dabav (air pressure) ki wajah se neeche tiki hain."**
Sahi: cheezein neeche gravity ki wajah se hain, hawa ke dabav se nahi. Hawa ka dabav kisi cheez ko neeche nahi kheenchta; ye alag cheez hai jise hum agle chapter (Pressure aur Fluids) mein dekhenge.

## Quick Recap

- Cheezein neeche isliye girti hain kyunki Dharti unhe kheench rahi hai: is kheenchav ko **gravity (gravitational force)** kehte hain.
- Gravity kisi bhi do mass wali cheezon ke beech lagti hai: Dharti, Chaand, Sooraj, pen, kitaab, hum sab.
- Dharti har cheez ko apne **kendra ki taraf** kheenchti hai, isliye Dharti ke har hisse mein "neeche" matlab kendra ki taraf.
- Gravity hamesha **attractive** hai, kabhi repulsive nahi, aur ye **non-contact force** hai.
- Pen aur kitaab ke beech gravity bahut chhoti hai: wo tab dikhti hai jab ek cheez bahut badi ho.
- Gravity antariksh mein bhi hoti hai.
- Newton ke laws gravity par bhi lagte hain: akela gravity lage to unbalanced force hai aur cheez accelerate karti hai; latke bulb mein gravity aur tension balanced hain.
- Newton aur seb ki kahani ek legend hai; asli vichar ye hai ki seb aur chaand ko ek hi gravity chalati hai.

## Practice Questions

**Q1.** State whether each statement is true or false.
(a) Gravity acts only on heavy objects.
(b) Gravity can push objects away from each other.
(c) The Earth pulls objects towards its centre.
(d) There is no gravity in outer space.
(e) Gravity acts without touching.
(f) A mango also pulls the Earth.

**Q2.** Write the direction of the gravitational force in each situation.
(a) A ball is thrown straight up and is at its highest point.
(b) A ball is moving upwards just after being thrown.
(c) A man is standing in Australia and drops a cup.
(d) The Moon, as seen from the Earth.
(e) A satellite orbiting the Earth.
(f) A raindrop falling in Delhi.

**Q3.** A lamp hangs at rest from a ceiling by a chain. Name the two forces acting on it, give their directions, and say whether they are balanced.

**Q4.** Why do we not see a pen and a book on a table pulling each other?

**Q5.** Gravity is called a non-contact force. Explain with one example. Name one other non-contact force.

**Q6.** Why can people on the opposite side of the Earth also stand and walk without falling off?

**Q7.** Using Newton's laws, explain why a mango accelerates downwards when it breaks off the tree, but stays still while it is attached.

**Q8.** Give two differences between gravity and magnetic force.

**Q9.** A 0.5 kg object has a net unbalanced force of 6 N acting on it. Find its acceleration. (This question is about F = ma, not gravity formulas.)

**Q10.** A student says, "Things stay on the ground because of air pressure pushing them down." Is this correct? Explain briefly.

### Answers

**A1.**
(a) False ❌. Gravity acts on every object that has mass.
(b) False ❌. Gravity is always attractive.
(c) True ✅.
(d) False ❌. Gravity exists in space too.
(e) True ✅. It is a non-contact force.
(f) True ✅. The effect on the Earth is just too tiny to notice.

**A2.**
(a) Downwards, towards the Earth's centre (gravity still acts at the highest point).
(b) Downwards, towards the Earth's centre. The ball goes up because of the throw, but gravity still pulls it down.
(c) Towards the Earth's centre. For the person in Australia, this is also "down".
(d) The Earth pulls the Moon towards the Earth's centre, i.e. towards the Earth.
(e) Towards the Earth's centre.
(f) Downwards, towards the Earth's centre.

**A3.** Gravity (weight) acts **downwards** and the tension in the chain acts **upwards**. They are **balanced** (equal and opposite), so the net force is zero and the lamp stays at rest.

**A4.** Pen aur kitaab ke beech gravity hoti hai, par dono ki mass bahut kam hai, isliye kheenchav bahut hi chhota hai. Wo na dikhta hai, na pen ko hila sakta hai (table ka friction bhi rokta hai). Gravity tab dikhti hai jab ek cheez Dharti jaisi bahut badi ho.

**A5.** Aam daal se toot kar bina kisi ke chhue Dharti ki taraf girta hai: Dharti ka gravity use door se kheench rahi hai. Ek aur non-contact force: magnetic force (ya electrostatic force).

**A6.** Dharti har cheez ko apne kendra ki taraf kheenchti hai. Dusri taraf ke logon ke liye bhi "neeche" ka matlab kendra ki taraf hi hota hai, isliye wo bhi zameen par tike rehte hain.

**A7.** Daal par laga aam rest mein hai kyunki daal ka upar ka force aur gravity ka neeche ka force balanced hain (Pehla Law). Jab wo toot jata hai, to daal ka force chala jata hai aur sirf gravity bachta hai: unbalanced force. Doosre Law (F = ma) ke hisaab se aam neeche ki taraf accelerate karta hai.

**A8.** (i) Gravity hamesha attract karti hai; magnetic force attract aur repel dono kar sakta hai. (ii) Gravity har mass wali cheez par lagti hai; magnetic force sirf magnets aur kuch dhaatuon (jaise lohe) par.

**A9.** a = F ÷ m = 6 N ÷ 0.5 kg = **12 m/s²**.
**Jaanch:** 12 m/s² × 0.5 kg = 6 N ✅

**A10.** Galat ❌. Cheezein gravity ki wajah se neeche rehti hain. Hawa ka dabav cheezon ko neeche nahi kheenchta, ye alag cheez hai.
