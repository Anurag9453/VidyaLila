# Topic 2: Thermal Expansion: Garm Karne Par Cheezein Kyun Failti Hain

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha tha ki **mercury thermometer** kaam kaise karta hai: bulb garm cheez ke paas aata hai, to andar ka **mercury phailta hai** aur patli naali mein upar chadhta hai. Us waqt humne bas itna maan liya tha ki "garm hone par mercury phailta hai". Ab sawal ye hai: ye phailna **hota kyun hai**? Kya sirf mercury hi phailta hai, ya lohe ki rod, paani, hawa, sab? Aur agar sab phailte hain, to ye hamari zindagi mein kahan kaam aata hai, aur kahan dikkat deta hai? Is topic mein yehi dekhenge.

**Is topic mein sab temperature degree Celsius (°C) mein hain.** Temperature ka **badlaav (rise)** ΔT likhenge, jahan ΔT = final temperature − initial temperature.

---

## Bridge ke jodon ki kahani

Ek lambe steel ke bridge par maintenance engineer **Mr. Menghani** roz ki tarah inspection kar rahe the. Saath mein unki padosi, Class 9 ki **Charvi**, aayi thi, kyunki use apna science project banana tha.

Charvi sadak par chalte chalte achanak ruk gayi. "Uncle, yahan dekhiye! Sadak ke beech mein ek patli si daraar hai, jisme metal ke **kanghe jaise daant** (comb) ek doosre mein fanse hue hain. Aur thodi doori par phir wahi. Ye bridge toota hua hai kya?"

Mr. Menghani hans pade. "Nahi beta, ye bridge ko **jaan-boojh kar** aisa banaya gaya hai. Ye **expansion joints** hain. Aur dekho, aaj garmi ka din hai, to ye daraar kitni patli hai. Maine pichhli sardiyon ki subah ko yahi naapa tha: tab ye kaafi chaudi thi."

Charvi ne socha: "Matlab bridge ke jodon ki chaudai **mausam ke saath badalti hai**? Steel to itna kadak hota hai, wo phailta kaise hai? Aur agar daraar na ho to kya hoga?"

"Bahut achhe sawal," Mr. Menghani bole. "Chalo, pehle ye samjhte hain ki cheezein garm hone par phailti kyun hain. Phir hum bridge ka poora hisaab lagayenge: is bridge ka ek hissa garmi mein kitna lamba ho jaata hai." Is kahani ko hum topic ke beech mein solve karenge.

## Part A: Cheezein phailti kyun hain?

Har cheez bahut chhote-chhote **particles** (kan) se bani hoti hai. Thos cheez mein ye particles apni jagah par tike rehte hain, par bilkul sthir nahi hote: wo apni jagah par **hilte-kaampte (vibrate)** rehte hain, jaise bus mein khade log jhatke se thoda hilte hain.

Jab cheez ko **garm** karte hain, to particles **zyada zor se vibrate** karne lagte hain. Zyada zor se hilne ke liye unhe **thodi zyada jagah** chahiye hoti hai, aur wo ek doosre se **thoda door** ho jaate hain. Har do particles ke beech ka faasla thoda sa badhta hai, aur jab arbon-kharbon particles mein ye ek saath hota hai, to poori cheez ka size badh jaata hai. Isse hum kehte hain **thermal expansion**.

Jab cheez ko **thanda** karte hain, to ulta hota hai: vibration kam hota hai, particles paas aa jaate hain, aur cheez **sikud (contract)** jaati hai.

> **Yaad rakho:** Particles khud bade nahi hote. Bas unke beech ka faasla badhta hai. Ye sirf ek simple tasveer hai; iska gehra hisaab aage ki class mein hoga.

Isliye ek niyam hai: **zyaadatar cheezein garm karne par phailti hain aur thanda karne par sikudti hain.** (Paani ek khaas apwaad hai: uski baat Part F mein karenge.)

## Part B: Thos cheezon mein expansion: teen roop

Ek thos cheez teen tarah se phail sakti hai:

1. **Linear expansion:** length badhti hai (rod, taar, rail ki patri).
2. **Area expansion:** sataha badhti hai (plate, chadar).
3. **Volume expansion:** poora volume badhta hai (lohe ka gola, patthar ka tukda).

Asal mein ye teeno ek saath hote hain: garm hone par cheez **har taraf** thodi si phailti hai. Hum alag alag naam isliye dete hain ki kabhi hamein sirf length se matlab hota hai (taar, patri), kabhi sirf sataha se (plate). Is topic mein hum zyadatar **linear expansion** par dhyan denge.

### Demonstration 1: Ball and ring

Ek dhatu ka gola (ball) aur ek ring lo. Thande hone par gola ring ke andar se **bas nikal jaata hai**. Ab gole ko burner par garm karo. Garm gola **ring mein se nahi nikalta**: wo phail chuka hai aur ring ke chhed se bada ho gaya hai. Gole ko thanda kar do, to wo phir ring mein se nikal jaata hai.

```

        │  <- chain                         │  <- chain
       (●)  chhota gola                   ( ● )  phaila hua gola
    ═══   ═══  <- ring ka chhed         ═══   ═══  <- ring ka chhed

  Gola chhed se nikal jaata hai.    Gola chhed se bada: ring par
```

## Part C: Kitna phailta hai? Teen cheezein tay karti hain

Mr. Menghani ne Charvi se poochha, "Agar tum teen rods ko garm karo, to kaun zyada phailegi? Socho, kaun kaun si cheezein asar karti hain?"

Experiments se teen cheezein nikalti hain:

1. **Original length (L):** jo rod **zyada lambi** hai wo **zyada** phailti hai.
2. **Temperature ka badlaav (ΔT):** jitna **zyada garm** karoge, utna **zyada** phailegi.
3. **Material:** alag alag dhatuyein alag alag matra mein phailti hain (steel kam, aluminium zyada).

Pehli do cheezon ke liye **proportional niyam** hai:

> **ΔL ∝ L × ΔT**
> (Material same ho, to expansion original length ke aur temperature ke badlaav ke **proportional** hoti hai.)

Matlab:
- Rod **do guna lambi** ho, to expansion **do guna**.
- Temperature ka badlaav **teen guna** ho, to expansion **teen guna**.
- Dono ek saath badlein, to dono ke multipliers **guna** hote hain (2 guna lambi aur 3 guna ΔT: 2 × 3 = 6 guna expansion).

### Ek ahem baat: temperature nahi, temperature ka badlaav (ΔT) matter karta hai

Rod 10 °C se 40 °C tak garm ho, ya 60 °C se 90 °C tak, dono mein ΔT = 30 °C hai, to **expansion barabar**. Final temperature kitna bhi bada ho, expansion ΔT se tay hota hai.

### Example 1 (ratio method: length aur ΔT)

Ek steel ki 1 m lambi rod ko 50 °C se garm karne par uski length 0.6 mm badhti hai. Usi steel ki:
(a) 5 m lambi rod ko usi ΔT = 50 °C se garm karne par, expansion?
(b) 1 m rod ko ΔT = 150 °C se garm karne par (ΔT teen guna), expansion?
(c) 5 m rod ko ΔT = 150 °C se garm karne par, expansion?

(Yahan "50 °C garm karna" ka matlab hai ki temperature **50 °C badha**, yaani ΔT = 50 °C.)

- Original: L = 1 m, ΔT = 50 °C, ΔL = 0.6 mm.
- (a) Length 5 guna, ΔT wahi: ΔL = 0.6 mm × 5 = **3 mm**.
- (b) Length wahi, ΔT 3 guna (150 ÷ 50 = 3): ΔL = 0.6 mm × 3 = **1.8 mm**.
- (c) Length 5 guna aur ΔT 3 guna: ΔL = 0.6 mm × 5 × 3 = **9 mm**.

**Jaanch:** (c) ko do kadam mein bhi nikaal sakte hain: pehle 5 m par 50 °C ke liye 3 mm, phir ΔT teen guna: 3 mm × 3 = 9 mm, wahi ✅

### Example 2 (temperature nahi, ΔT matter karta hai)

Ek hi tarah ki do barabar rods hain. Rod A ko 10 °C se 40 °C tak garm kiya, Rod B ko 60 °C se 90 °C tak. Kiski length zyada badhi?

- Rod A: ΔT = 40 °C − 10 °C = 30 °C.
- Rod B: ΔT = 90 °C − 60 °C = 30 °C.
- ΔT dono ka barabar, length aur material barabar, to **dono ki expansion barabar**.

**Jaanch:** Agar kisi ne kaha "Rod B ka temperature zyada hai, to zyada phailegi" to wo galat hai: expansion ka tay karne wala ΔT hai, jo dono mein 30 °C hai ✅

### Material ka asar: coefficient of linear expansion (α)

Ratio method mein hamein "1 m rod, 50 °C par 0.6 mm" jaisa ek jaana-pehchana data chahiye tha. Har baar data dhoondhne ke bajay ek **number α** bana liya gaya, jo kisi material ke liye "ek meter lambi rod ko 1 °C garm karne par kitna fraction phailti hai" batata hai. Iska naam **coefficient of linear expansion** hai. Kuch dhatuyon ke approximate values:

| Material | α (approximately, per °C) |
|---|---|
| Steel | 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ |
| Copper | 1.7 × 10⁻⁵ |
| Aluminium | 2.4 × 10⁻⁵ |

(Ye values lagbhag hain; alag alag kismon ke steel ya aluminium mein thoda farak hota hai.)

Isse ek seedha formula milta hai (Class 10 mein bhi aayega):

> **ΔL = α × L × ΔT**

Yahan L original length (m mein), ΔT temperature ka badlaav (°C mein), α per °C mein, aur ΔL metre (m) mein aata hai. Ye wahi proportional baat hai: α bas "material ka number" hai jo ratio ko ek poora formula bana deta hai.

Jaanch ke liye Example 1 ko dekho: steel ke liye 1 m rod, ΔT = 50 °C:
ΔL = 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ × 1 × 50 = 6 × 10⁻⁴ m = 0.6 mm, wahi jo ratio method mein diya tha ✅

(Tip: α ki value bahut chhoti hai: 0.000012 per °C. Ye batata hai ki steel ke ek meter par 1 °C ke liye sirf 0.000012 m, yaani 0.012 mm, badhta hai.)

### Example 3 (steel ki rail)

Ek steel ki rail 20 m lambi hai. Garmi mein uska temperature 40 °C badh jaata hai. Kitni expansion hogi? (α = 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ per °C)

- L = 20 m, ΔT = 40 °C, α = 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ per °C.
- ΔL = α × L × ΔT = 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ per °C × 20 m × 40 °C
- = 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ × 800 m (kyunki 20 × 40 = 800)
- = 960 × 10⁻⁵ m = 0.0096 m
- 0.0096 m = **9.6 mm** (1 m = 1000 mm)

**Jaanch:** 1.2 × 800 = 960, aur 960 × 10⁻⁵ = 0.0096 m. 0.0096 m = 9.6 mm ✅ (Rail sirf lagbhag 1 cm zyada lambi hui, par agar jagah na ho, to itna sa bhi bahut bada kaam bigaad sakta hai. Aage dekhte hain.)

## Part D: Bridge ka sawaal solve karte hain

Charvi wapas daraar ke paas aayi. "Uncle, ye bridge kitna lamba hai? Kitne hisse hain?"

"Bridge ke har section ki length lagbhag **100 m** hai, aur beech mein ek expansion joint hai," Mr. Menghani ne kaha. "Garmi mein steel ka temperature sardi ke mukable lagbhag **30 °C** zyada hota hai. Is section ki length kitni badhegi?"

### Example 4 (bridge ka section)

Steel ka 100 m ka section, ΔT = 30 °C, α = 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ per °C. Expansion?

- ΔL = α × L × ΔT = 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ per °C × 100 m × 30 °C
- = 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ × 3000 m (kyunki 100 × 30 = 3000)
- = 3.6 × 10⁻² m (1.2 × 3000 = 3600; 3600 × 10⁻⁵ = 0.036)
- = 0.036 m = **3.6 cm**

**Jaanch:** 1.2 × 3000 = 3600, 3600 × 10⁻⁵ = 0.036 m, 0.036 m × 100 = 3.6 cm ✅ Is hisaab se ek section 3.6 cm lamba ho jaata hai, to expansion joint mein kam se kam itni jagah (gap) chhodni padti hai.

Charvi chaunk gayi. "Sirf 3.6 cm? Ye to kuch nahi."

"Nahi beta, bahut hai," Mr. Menghani ne kaha. "Agar gap na ho, to ye 3.6 cm phailne ki koshish karega, par jagah nahi hai. Tab steel bahut zor lagata hai aur bridge ke hisse **tedhe ho sakte hain**, sadak **ubhar** sakti hai, aur jodon par **daraaren** pad sakti hain. Isliye bridge ke beech mein kanghe jaise jod lagaye jaate hain: garmi mein daant aapas mein andar ki taraf aur fans jaate hain, aur sardi mein thoda bahar nikal aate hain. Isliye aaj garmi ke din gap **patli** hai, aur sardi ki subah mein ye **chaudi** hoti hai. Ek hi bridge, mausam ke saath ye gap saans leti rehti hai."

### Example 5 (kitni gap chahiye: temperature ki range)

Ek steel ke bridge ka 50 m ka section hai. Wahan ka temperature sardi mein 5 °C se leke garmi mein 45 °C tak jaata hai. Expansion joint mein kam se kam kitni gap chhodni chahiye? (α = 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ per °C)

- Pehle ΔT nikalte hain: 45 °C − 5 °C = **40 °C**.
- ΔL = α × L × ΔT = 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ per °C × 50 m × 40 °C
- = 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ × 2000 m
- = 2400 × 10⁻⁵ m = 0.024 m = **2.4 cm**

**Jaanch:** 50 × 40 = 2000, 1.2 × 2000 = 2400, 2400 × 10⁻⁵ = 0.024 m = 2.4 cm ✅ Temperature range ka ΔT lena hai (final − initial), na ki 45 °C ya 5 °C akela.

### Example 6 (aluminium ki rod: ΔL aur final length)

2 m lambi aluminium ki rod ka temperature 20 °C se 70 °C tak badhaya gaya. Expansion kitni hui, aur rod ki nayi length kitni hai? (α = 2.4 × 10⁻⁵ per °C)

- ΔT = 70 °C − 20 °C = 50 °C. L = 2 m.
- ΔL = α × L × ΔT = 2.4 × 10⁻⁵ per °C × 2 m × 50 °C
- = 2.4 × 10⁻⁵ × 100 m
- = 2.4 × 10⁻³ m = 0.0024 m = **2.4 mm**
- Final length = original + ΔL = 2 m + 0.0024 m = **2.0024 m**

**Jaanch:** 2 × 50 = 100, 2.4 × 100 = 240, 240 × 10⁻⁵ = 0.0024 m. Final length 2 + 0.0024 = 2.0024 m ✅

### Example 7 (steel vs aluminium: same length, same ΔT)

Steel aur aluminium ki do rods ki length barabar hai, aur dono ka temperature barabar badhaya gaya. Kiski expansion zyada hogi, aur kitni guna?

- L aur ΔT dono rods mein barabar hain, to ΔL ∝ α.
- Steel ka α = 1.2 × 10⁻⁵, aluminium ka α = 2.4 × 10⁻⁵.
- Ratio ΔL(steel) : ΔL(aluminium) = 1.2 : 2.4 = **1 : 2**

Matlab aluminium ki rod, steel ki rod se **do guna** zyada phailti hai.

**Jaanch:** 2.4 ÷ 1.2 = 2 ✅. Alternatively, 1 m rod aur ΔT = 100 °C lekar: steel 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ × 1 × 100 = 0.0012 m, aluminium 2.4 × 10⁻⁵ × 1 × 100 = 0.0024 m, ratio 0.0012 : 0.0024 = 1 : 2 ✅

### Area aur volume expansion: ek jhalak

Plate ki har side lambai ki tarah phailti hai. Agar plate ki har side garm hone par original ka **0.1 %** (yaani 1.001 guna) ho jaaye, to plate ki sataha lagbhag **0.2 %** zyada ho jaati hai. Aur volume (teen directions mein phailna) lagbhag **0.3 %** zyada hota hai. Matlab area ka expansion lagbhag linear expansion ka **do guna** aur volume ka **teen guna** hota hai (ye sirf chhote ΔT ke liye theek hai).

### Example 8 (plate ka area: sirf ratio wala vichaar)

Ek square plate ki har side garm karne par original ka 1.001 guna ho jaati hai. Area kitna guna hua?

- Nayi side = 1.001 × purani side.
- Area = side × side, to naya area = 1.001 × 1.001 × purana area = **1.002001** × purana area.
- Yaani area lagbhag **1.002 guna**, jo 0.2 % ka badhna hai (linear 0.1 % ka lagbhag do guna).

**Jaanch:** 1.001² = 1.002001 ≈ 1.002 ✅ Isse ye bhi samajh aata hai ki chhote expansion ke liye area ka expansion lagbhag linear expansion ka do guna hota hai. Exact calculation ki zaroorat nahi: bas ye ratio wali soch yaad rakho.

## Part E: Bimetallic strip: do dhatu, ek patti

Mr. Menghani ne apne bag se ek chhoti si patti nikaali. "Charvi, ye ek **bimetallic strip** hai. Expansion ka ye ek bahut chalaak upyog hai, ise dhyan se dekho."

**Bimetallic strip** do alag dhatuon ki patliyon ko **rivet** ya welding se jod kar banayi jaati hai. Maan lo ek patti **brass** (peetal) ki hai aur doosri **iron** (lohe) ki. Brass ka α iron se **zyada** hota hai, yaani **garm karne par brass zyada phailta hai**.

Dono patliyan aapas mein jud chuki hain, isliye alag nahi ho sakti. Garm karne par brass **zyada lamba** hona chahta hai, aur iron **kam**. Jo patti zyada phailti hai, wo bend ke **bahar** (bahri side) ki taraf rehti hai, aur jo kam phailti hai wo **andar** (bend ke bheetar). Isliye strip **iron ki taraf** mudti hai: iron andar wali side mein, aur brass bahar wali side mein.

```
   Garm karne se pehle (seedhi):

   ┌─────────────────────────────┐   brass (upar)
   └─────────────────────────────┘
   ┌─────────────────────────────┐   iron (neeche)
   └─────────────────────────────┘

   Garm karne par (brass zyada phaili, strip neeche iron ki taraf mudi):

          brass: bahar wala, lamba kinara
        ╭───────────────────────────╮
      ╭─╯ ╭───────────────────────╮ ╰─╮
    ╭─╯ ╭─╯  iron: andar wala     ╰─╮ ╰─╮
    │   │                           │   │
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Garm karne par bimetallic strip us taraf mud jaati hai jis dhatu ki expansion kam hai (yahan iron ki taraf), kyunki brass zyada lamba hona chahta hai.

Sardi mein ya thanda karne par ulta hota hai: brass zyada sikudta hai, to patti **brass ki taraf** mud jaati hai.

**Upyog:**
- **Thermostat:** Jab temperature set value se upar jaata hai, to patti mud kar circuit ko **tod** deti hai (switch ki tarah), aur heater band ho jaata hai. Thanda hone par patti seedhi ho kar circuit wapas jod deti hai.
- **Electric iron:** Press ka temperature control karne ke liye. Zyada garm hone par patti current band kar deti hai.
- **Fire alarm:** Aag lagne se tapman badhne par patti mud kar alarm ka circuit jod deti hai, aur alarm baj uthta hai.

### Example 9 (patti kis taraf mudegi?)

Ek bimetallic strip mein ek taraf aluminium (α = 2.4 × 10⁻⁵ per °C) aur doosri taraf steel (α = 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ per °C) hai. Strip ko garm karne par wo kis taraf mudegi? Thanda karne par?

- Dono ki L aur ΔT barabar (kyunki dono jude hain), to **zyada α** wali dhatu zyada phailegi: aluminium (2.4 × 10⁻⁵) > steel (1.2 × 10⁻⁵).
- **Garm karne par:** aluminium zyada phailti hai, to wo bend ke **bahar** rehti hai, aur patti **steel ki taraf** mudti hai.
- **Thanda karne par:** aluminium zyada sikudti hai, to patti **aluminium ki taraf** mudti hai.

**Jaanch:** Niyam: garm karne par patti **kam phailne wali dhatu** ki taraf mudti hai. Steel kam phailti hai, to garm karne par steel ki taraf ✅. Thanda karne par ulta: aluminium ki taraf ✅.

## Part F: Solids ke upyog aur savdhaniyan

Expansion kabhi dikkat deta hai, to kabhi hamare kaam aata hai:

**Dikkat se bachne ke tareeke (precautions):**
1. **Rail ki patriyon ke beech gap:** Patriyon ke beech chhoti si gap chhodi jaati hai, taaki garmi mein phail kar wo tedhi na ho jaayein. (Wahi bridge wali baat.)
2. **Concrete ki sadakon mein joints:** Bade slabs ke beech chhoti daraaren chhodi jaati hain.
3. **Bijli ke taar (overhead power lines):** Garmi mein taar phail kar thode **dheele (sag)** ho jaate hain, aur sardi mein sikud kar **tight** ho jaate hain. Isliye taar ko khambon ke beech **thoda dheela** lagaya jaata hai. Agar bilkul tight lagayenge, to sardi mein sikudne par taar **toot sakta hai**.
4. **Mote glass mein garm paani daalna:** Mota glass garm paani se **andar se pehle garm** hota hai, to andar ka hissa phailta hai aur bahar ka abhi thanda hai. Is **unequal (asamaan) expansion** se glass **chatak** sakta hai. **Borosilicate glass** (jaise lab ke beaker, oven ke dishes) ki expansion bahut kam hoti hai, isliye wo aasaani se nahi tootta.

**Faayde ke upyog:**
1. **Cart ke pahiye par lohe ka tyre:** Lohe ke chhalle (tyre) ko pahiye se **chhota** banaya jaata hai, phir garm karke **phailaya** jaata hai, pahiye par chadhaya jaata hai, aur thanda karne par wo sikud kar pahiye par **kas kar baith jaata hai**.
2. **Rivets:** Garm rivet ko plate ke chhed mein daal kar hathode se dabaya jaata hai. Thanda hone par rivet sikudta hai aur plates ko **kas kar jod deta hai**.
3. **Jam hua jar ka lid:** Glass jar ka dhakkan kas ke atak gaya ho, to dhakkan ko **garm paani** mein dalne se metal ka dhakkan phailta hai aur dheela ho jaata hai.
4. **Thermostat, fire alarm, electric iron:** Bimetallic strip (Part E).

### Chhed (hole) wali galatfahmi

Agar ek plate mein chhed ho aur plate ko garm karein, to chhed **chhota** hota hai ya **bada**? Zyadatar log sochte hain ki plate chhed ki taraf phailti hai, isliye chhed chhota hoga. Ye galat hai. **Chhed bhi phailta hai**, bilkul waise jaise us jagah par usi material ka ek tukda hota, jo plate ke saath phailta.

```
   Garm karne se pehle:         Garm karne ke baad:

   ┌─────────────────┐         ┌───────────────────────┐
   │                 │         │                       │
   │     ┌─────┐     │         │      ┌─────────┐      │
   │     │chhed│     │         │      │  chhed  │      │
   │     └─────┘     │         │      └─────────┘      │
   │                 │         │                       │
   └─────────────────┘         └───────────────────────┘
   Plate chhoti, chhed chhota  Plate badi, chhed bhi bada
```

Samajhne ka aasaan tareeka: socho plate ek **photo** hai jise **photocopier** mein 101 % par bada kiya. Kya chhed bhi bada hoga? Haan, wo bhi photo ke saath bada hota hai. Isi liye "ball and ring" experiment mein gole ke saath ring ko bhi garm karo, to ring ka chhed bhi bada ho jaata hai, aur gola phir se nikal jaata hai.

## Part G: Liquids aur Gases mein expansion

### Liquids

**Liquids solids se zyada phailte hain.** (Isi liye mercury thermometer mein chhoti si garmi se mercury ka phailna naali mein saaf dikh jaata hai.)

**Flask aur naali ka experiment (chhota note):** Ek flask mein rangeen paani lekar ek patli naali lagayi. Jab flask ko garm paani mein rakhte hain, to naali mein paani ka level **pehle thoda neeche** girta hai, **phir upar** chadhta hai. Kyun? Kyunki glass (flask) **pehle garm hota hai** aur phail jaata hai, to flask ka volume thoda badh jaata hai aur level girta hai. Phir paani garm hota hai aur glass se **zyada** phailta hai, to level upar chadh jaata hai.

**Alag alag liquids alag tarah phailte hain.** Jaise **alcohol paani se zyada** phailta hai (barabar garm karne par). Isi liye kuch thermometers mein mercury ke bajay (rang diya hua) alcohol hota hai, jo bahut thande ilaakon mein bhi kaam karta hai. (Mercury ka freezing point alcohol se kafi upar hai.)

### Paani ka anokha (anomalous) expansion

Ab ek bahut dilchasp baat. Zyadatar liquids garm karne par phailte hain. Par **paani 0 °C se 4 °C tak garm karne par sikudta hai!** 4 °C ke baad ye aam liquids ki tarah phailna shuru karta hai.

Matlab ek kg paani ka volume **4 °C par sabse kam** hota hai, aur isliye **density sabse zyada** hoti hai (density = mass ÷ volume, jo Pressure aur Fluids chapter mein dekha tha). 4 °C par paani ki density lagbhag **1000 kg/m³** hoti hai.

Niche ke values **lagbhag (approximate)** hain:

| Temperature | Paani ki density (approx.) |
|---|---|
| 0 °C | 999.84 kg/m³ |
| 4 °C | 999.97 kg/m³ |
| 10 °C | 999.70 kg/m³ |

Dekho: 0 °C se 4 °C tak density **badhi** (999.84 → 999.97), yaani volume **ghata**. 4 °C se 10 °C tak density **ghati** (999.97 → 999.70), yaani volume **badha**.

### Example 10 (paani ka volume do temperatures par)

1000 kg paani ka volume (a) 0 °C par, (b) 4 °C par, (c) 10 °C par kitna hai? Upar ki table ki density use karo. Volume = mass ÷ density.

- (a) 0 °C: V = 1000 kg ÷ 999.84 kg/m³ = **1.00016 m³** (lagbhag)
- (b) 4 °C: V = 1000 kg ÷ 999.97 kg/m³ = **1.00003 m³** (lagbhag)
- (c) 10 °C: V = 1000 kg ÷ 999.70 kg/m³ = **1.00030 m³** (lagbhag)

Tulna: 4 °C par volume sabse kam (1.00003 m³). 0 °C par 1.00016 − 1.00003 = 0.00013 m³ zyada (yaani lagbhag 130 cm³ zyada, 1 m³ = 10⁶ cm³ ke hisaab se). 10 °C par 1.00030 − 1.00003 = 0.00027 m³ zyada (lagbhag 270 cm³).

**Jaanch:** 999.97 × 1.00003 = 999.97 + 999.97 × 0.00003 = 999.97 + 0.03 = 1000.00 ✅. Dono taraf 4 °C se door jaane par volume badhta hai ✅.

### Is anokhepan ka natija: sardi mein talaab ki machhliyan kaise bachti hain

Socho sardi aa rahi hai aur ek talaab (pond) ki upar ki satah thandi hawa se thandi hoti ja rahi hai.

1. Upar ka paani thanda hota hai, uski density badhti hai (jab tak 4 °C), to wo **bhaari** ho kar **neeche** dooba jaata hai. Neeche ka **garam** paani upar uthta hai. Ye chakkar chalta rehta hai.
2. Jab poora talaab **4 °C** tak aa jaata hai, to ab upar ka paani 4 °C se aur thanda hone par **halka** (kam dense) ho jaata hai, to wo **upar hi rehta hai**, neeche nahi jaata.
3. Upar ka paani aur thanda ho kar **0 °C par barf** ban jaata hai, aur barf **upar** hi tairti hai (ice paani se kam dense hai: dekho [pressure-and-fluids-part7.md](../pressure-and-fluids/pressure-and-fluids-part7.md)).
4. Barf ki parat ke **neeche** paani **0 °C se 4 °C** ke beech rehta hai: sabse neeche lagbhag 4 °C, aur upar wali taraf 0 °C ke paas. Is paani mein machhliyan **jinda rehti hain**.

```
   Sardi ke din talaab (side view):

   hawa: bahut thandi (jaise -5 °C)
   ─────────────────────────────────────  <- satah
   ░░░░░░░░░░░ barf (0 °C) ░░░░░░░░░░░░░░   barf upar
   ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ paani, lagbhag 1 °C ~ ~ ~ ~
   ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ paani, lagbhag 2 °C ~ ~ ~ ~
   ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ paani, lagbhag 3 °C ~ ~ ~ ~
   ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ paani, lagbhag 4 °C ~ ~ ~ ~   sabse neeche (sabse bhaari)
   ═════════════════════════════════════  talaab ki tali
```

> **Figure 2.5** – Sardi mein talaab ke upar barf ki parat ban jaati hai, aur neeche paani 0 °C se 4 °C ke beech rehta hai, jahan machhliyan jinda rehti hain.

Agar paani **anomalous** na hota, aur sab liquids ki tarah thanda hone par har waqt bhaari hota, to talaab **neeche se upar** ki taraf jam jaata, aur machhliyan nahi bach paatin.

### Gases

**Gases sabse zyada phailti hain**, solids aur liquids dono se zyada.
- Dhoop mein rakha gubbara (balloon) thoda phool jaata hai.
- Ek khali bottle ke munh par balloon lagao, aur bottle ko garm paani mein rakho. Andar ki hawa garm ho kar phailti hai, aur balloon **phool jaata hai**.

> Gas ke expansion ka niyam (kaise aur kitna phailti hai) **aage ki class mein** padhenge. Abhi sirf itna yaad rakho: **gases > liquids > solids** expansion ke maamle mein.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Cheezein sirf phailti hain, kabhi sikudti nahi"**

Nahi. Thanda karne par cheezein **sikudti** hain (**contraction**). Sardi mein bridge ki gap chaudi ho jaati hai, kyunki bridge sikud gaya. Bijli ke taar sardi mein tight ho jaate hain.

**Galti 2: "Jo cheez bhaari hai wo zyada phailegi"**

Nahi. Expansion **length (L)**, **ΔT** aur **material (α)** par depend karta hai, **mass par nahi**. Example 7 mein steel aur aluminium ki barabar L aur ΔT wali rods ke liye aluminium zyada phaila, chahe aluminium steel se halka hota hai.

**Galti 3: "Paani garm karne par hamesha phailta hai"**

Nahi. **0 °C se 4 °C** tak garm karne par paani **sikudta** hai (Example 10: 0 °C par 1.00016 m³ tha, 4 °C par 1.00003 m³). 4 °C ke baad hi wo phailta hai.

**Galti 4: "Plate ko garm karne par chhed chhota ho jaata hai"**

Nahi. **Chhed bhi bada hota hai**, jaise poori plate ko zoom kar diya ho (Figure 2.4).

**Galti 5: "Final temperature zyada hai, to expansion zyada hoga"**

Nahi. Expansion **temperature ke badlaav ΔT** par depend karti hai, final temperature par nahi. Example 2 mein 10 °C se 40 °C aur 60 °C se 90 °C dono mein ΔT = 30 °C tha, to expansion barabar.

## Quick Recap

- **Thermal expansion:** garm karne par cheez ka size badhna. **Contraction:** thanda karne par ghatna. Wajah: garm hone par particles zyada vibrate karte hain aur ek doosre se thoda door ho jaate hain.
- Solids mein teen roop: **linear** (length), **area**, **volume**.
- **ΔL ∝ L × ΔT:** length double to expansion double; ΔT triple to expansion triple. Material ka bhi asar padta hai.
- Expansion **temperature ke badlaav (ΔT)** se tay hoti hai, final temperature se nahi. Mass se koi lena-dena nahi.
- **ΔL = α × L × ΔT.** Steel α ≈ 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ per °C, copper ≈ 1.7 × 10⁻⁵, aluminium ≈ 2.4 × 10⁻⁵.
- Bridge ka 100 m steel section ΔT = 30 °C par 0.036 m = **3.6 cm** badhta hai: isliye expansion joints.
- Steel aur aluminium (barabar L, barabar ΔT): expansion ka ratio **1 : 2**.
- **Bimetallic strip:** garm karne par wo kam phailne wali dhatu ki taraf mudti hai. Upyog: thermostat, electric iron, fire alarm.
- Upyog/savdhaniyan: rail aur bridge mein gap, dheele bijli ke taar, lohe ka tyre, rivets, jam hua lid, mote glass mein garm paani (borosilicate theek rehta hai).
- **Chhed** garm karne par bada hota hai, chhota nahi.
- **Liquids solids se zyada phailte hain.** Alag liquids alag tarah phailte hain.
- **Paani 0 °C se 4 °C tak sikudta hai**, 4 °C par density sabse zyada (lagbhag 1000 kg/m³), uske baad phailta hai. Isliye talaab upar se jamta hai aur machhliyan neeche bach jaati hain.
- **Gases sabse zyada phailti hain** (gubbara dhoop mein, bottle par balloon). Niyam aage ki class mein.

## Practice Questions

1. A steel rod 2 m long expands by 0.5 mm when its temperature rises by 20 °C. Using the proportional idea, find the expansion of (a) a 6 m rod of the same steel for the same rise of 20 °C, (b) the 2 m rod for a rise of 60 °C, (c) the 6 m rod for a rise of 60 °C.

2. Two identical copper rods are heated. Rod P goes from 15 °C to 65 °C and Rod Q from 100 °C to 150 °C. Which one expands more? Give a reason.

3. A steel rail is 12 m long. Find its expansion when its temperature rises by 50 °C. (α = 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ per °C)

4. A copper wire is 10 m long at 20 °C. Find its increase in length and its new length when it is heated to 60 °C. (α = 1.7 × 10⁻⁵ per °C)

5. A bridge section of steel is 80 m long. The temperature varies from 10 °C in winter to 40 °C in summer. What is the minimum gap that should be left in the expansion joint? (α = 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ per °C)

6. A copper rod and an aluminium rod are each 3 m long. Both are heated through 50 °C. Find the expansion of each rod, and the ratio of the expansion of aluminium to that of copper. (Copper α = 1.7 × 10⁻⁵, aluminium α = 2.4 × 10⁻⁵ per °C)

7. A bimetallic strip is made of brass (expands more) and iron (expands less). (a) Towards which metal does it bend when heated? (b) Towards which metal does it bend when cooled? (c) Name two devices that use a bimetallic strip.

8. A metal plate has a circular hole. When the plate is heated, does the hole become smaller, larger or stay the same? Explain in one line.

9. Using the table of densities (0 °C: 999.84 kg/m³, 4 °C: 999.97 kg/m³, approximate), find the volume of 500 kg of water at 0 °C and at 4 °C. At which temperature is the volume smaller?

10. State True or False:
    (a) Substances expand on heating but never contract.
    (b) A heavier rod of the same material and same length expands more than a lighter rod for the same rise in temperature.
    (c) Water contracts when heated from 0 °C to 4 °C.
    (d) Overhead power lines are fitted a little loose so that they do not snap in winter.
    (e) Gases expand less than liquids on heating.

### Answers

1. 2 m rod, 20 °C: 0.5 mm.
   (a) Length 3 guna (6 ÷ 2 = 3), ΔT wahi: 0.5 mm × 3 = **1.5 mm**.
   (b) ΔT 3 guna (60 ÷ 20 = 3), length wahi: 0.5 mm × 3 = **1.5 mm**.
   (c) Dono 3 guna: 0.5 mm × 3 × 3 = **4.5 mm**.
   Jaanch: 1.5 mm × 3 = 4.5 mm ✅

2. Rod P: ΔT = 65 − 15 = 50 °C. Rod Q: ΔT = 150 − 100 = 50 °C. ΔT barabar, material aur length barabar, to **dono ki expansion barabar**. (Temperature ka badlaav matter karta hai, final temperature nahi.)
   Jaanch: 50 °C = 50 °C ✅

3. ΔL = α × L × ΔT = 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ per °C × 12 m × 50 °C = 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ × 600 m = 720 × 10⁻⁵ m = 0.0072 m = **7.2 mm**.
   Jaanch: 12 × 50 = 600, 1.2 × 600 = 720, 720 × 10⁻⁵ = 0.0072 m = 7.2 mm ✅

4. ΔT = 60 − 20 = 40 °C. ΔL = 1.7 × 10⁻⁵ per °C × 10 m × 40 °C = 1.7 × 10⁻⁵ × 400 m = 680 × 10⁻⁵ m = 0.0068 m = **6.8 mm**. Nayi length = 10 m + 0.0068 m = **10.0068 m**.
   Jaanch: 1.7 × 400 = 680, 680 × 10⁻⁵ = 0.0068 ✅

5. ΔT = 40 − 10 = 30 °C. ΔL = 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ per °C × 80 m × 30 °C = 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ × 2400 m = 2880 × 10⁻⁵ m = 0.0288 m = **2.88 cm** (lagbhag 2.9 cm). Kam se kam itni gap chahiye.
   Jaanch: 80 × 30 = 2400, 1.2 × 2400 = 2880, 2880 × 10⁻⁵ = 0.0288 m = 2.88 cm ✅

6. Copper: ΔL = 1.7 × 10⁻⁵ per °C × 3 m × 50 °C = 1.7 × 10⁻⁵ × 150 m = 255 × 10⁻⁵ m = 0.00255 m = **2.55 mm**. Aluminium: ΔL = 2.4 × 10⁻⁵ per °C × 3 m × 50 °C = 2.4 × 10⁻⁵ × 150 m = 360 × 10⁻⁵ m = 0.0036 m = **3.6 mm**. Ratio = 3.6 mm ÷ 2.55 mm ≈ **1.41** (L aur ΔT barabar hain, to ye α ka ratio 2.4 : 1.7 hi hai).
   Jaanch: 2.4 ÷ 1.7 ≈ 1.41 ✅

7. (a) Garm karne par **iron ki taraf** mudti hai (brass bahar, zyada lamba). (b) Thanda karne par **brass ki taraf** mudti hai (brass zyada sikudta hai). (c) Thermostat, electric iron, fire alarm (koi do).

8. Chhed **bada** hota hai, kyunki plate ke saath chhed bhi usi tarah phailta hai, jaise poori tasveer ko bada kar diya ho.

9. V = m ÷ density. 0 °C par: 500 kg ÷ 999.84 kg/m³ = **0.50008 m³**. 4 °C par: 500 kg ÷ 999.97 kg/m³ = **0.50002 m³**. Volume **4 °C par chhota** hai.
   Jaanch: 500 ÷ 999.84 = 0.500080, 500 ÷ 999.97 = 0.500015 ≈ 0.50002, 0.50008 > 0.50002 ✅

10. (a) **False** ❌. Thanda karne par contraction hota hai.
    (b) **False** ❌. Expansion length, ΔT aur material par depend karta hai, mass par nahi.
    (c) **True** ✅. 0 °C se 4 °C tak paani sikudta hai.
    (d) **True** ✅. Sardi mein taar sikudta hai, to dheela hone se wo tootne se bachta hai.
    (e) **False** ❌. Gases liquids se zyada phailti hain.
