# Topic 3: Specific Heat Capacity

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki garm karne par cheezein failti hain: yaani heat dene se unka size badalta hai. Ab ek aur sawal: **kisi cheez ko garm karne ke liye kitni heat chahiye?** Kya 1 kg paani aur 1 kg lohe ko 1 °C garm karne ke liye same heat lagti hai? Ya alag? Aur 2 kg ko garm karna ho to? Is topic mein hum heat, mass aur temperature ke badlaav ke beech ka seedha rishta seekhenge.

Ek baat pehle topic se yaad rakho: ek chhoti cup chai ka **temperature** zyada ho sakta hai, par baalti bhar halke garm paani mein **heat** zyada hoti hai. Us baat ko ab hum number se samjhenge. **Is poore topic mein: c(paani) = 4200 J/kg °C, aur 1 cal = 4.2 J.**

## Part A: Heat capacity, aur specific heat capacity kya hai?

### Heat capacity: poori cheez ke liye

Kisi cheez (body) ka temperature 1 °C badhane ke liye jitni heat chahiye, use us cheez ki **heat capacity (thermal capacity)** kehte hain. Iska unit **J/°C** hai.

Do baatein sochne wali hain:
- Cheez jitni **bhaari (zyada mass)** hogi, utni zyada heat chahiye. 10 kg paani ko 1 °C garm karna 1 kg paani ko garm karne se zyada mehnat hai.
- Cheez ka **material** bhi matter karta hai. Same mass ke paani aur lohe ko 1 °C garm karne ke liye same heat nahi lagti.

Yaani heat capacity **mass par bhi** aur **material par bhi** depend karti hai. Isiliye alag-alag cheezon ko tulna (compare) karne ke liye ek aisi quantity chahiye jo mass par depend na kare. Wahi hai specific heat capacity.

### Specific heat capacity (c): 1 kg ke liye

> **Specific heat capacity (c)** = 1 kg padarth ka temperature 1 °C badhane ke liye jitni heat chahiye.

- SI unit: **J/kg °C** (ya J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹; hum yahan dono ko barabar maante hain, kyunki 1 °C ka badlaav = 1 K ka badlaav).
- Calorie mein bhi likha jata hai: **cal/g °C**. Paani ke liye c = 1 cal/g °C. Aur kyunki 1 cal = 4.2 J, aur 1 g = 0.001 kg:

> 1 cal/g °C = 4.2 J ÷ 0.001 kg °C = **4200 J/kg °C**

Paani ka c = 4200 J/kg °C ka matlab: **1 kg paani ko 1 °C garm karne ke liye 4200 J heat chahiye.** Lohe ka c = 450 J/kg °C ka matlab: 1 kg lohe ko 1 °C garm karne ke liye sirf 450 J chahiye.

### Heat capacity aur specific heat ka rishta

Agar 1 kg ke liye c joule chahiye, to m kg ke liye m × c joule. Yaani:

> **Heat capacity = m × c**   (unit: kg × J/kg °C = J/°C)

Example: 0.5 kg lohe ki heat capacity = 0.5 kg × 450 J/kg °C = **225 J/°C**. Matlab is lohe ke tukde ko 1 °C garm karne ke liye 225 J lagti hai.

### Dono mein farak ek nazar mein

| | Specific heat (c) | Heat capacity (m × c) |
|---|---|---|
| Kiske liye? | 1 kg padarth ke liye | Poori cheez ke liye |
| Unit | J/kg °C | J/°C |
| Mass par depend? | Nahi: sirf material par | Haan: mass aur material dono par |
| Example | Lohe ka c = 450 J/kg °C | 0.5 kg lohe ki = 225 J/°C |

## Part B: Formula Q = m c ΔT, teen chhote kadam mein

Hum formula yaad nahi karenge, banayenge. Paani se shuru karte hain (c = 4200 J/kg °C).

**Kadam 1: Heat mass ke proportional hai (Q ∝ m).** Temperature badlaav same rakho, 1 °C.
- 1 kg paani ko 1 °C: 4200 J
- 2 kg paani ko 1 °C: 2 × 4200 = 8400 J
- 3 kg paani ko 1 °C: 3 × 4200 = 12600 J

Mass double, to heat double.

**Kadam 2: Heat temperature ke badlaav ke proportional hai (Q ∝ ΔT).** Mass same rakho, 2 kg.
- 2 kg ko 1 °C: 8400 J
- 2 kg ko 5 °C: 5 × 8400 = 42000 J

Temperature ka badlaav 5 guna, to heat bhi 5 guna.

**Kadam 3: Heat material par depend karti hai.** Same 1 kg, same 1 °C: paani ko 4200 J chahiye, lohe ko 450 J, mercury ko sirf 140 J. Is material-wali sankhya ko hi hum **c** (specific heat) kehte hain.

Teeno ko jod do: **Q = (mass) × (1 kg, 1 °C ki heat) × (temperature ka badlaav)**

> **Q = m × c × ΔT**
> Heat li ya di gayi (J) = mass (kg) × specific heat (J/kg °C) × temperature ka badlaav (°C)

Yahan **ΔT = final temperature − initial temperature**.

- Garm karte waqt ΔT positive, Q **li gayi** heat.
- Thanda hote waqt final temperature chhota hota hai. Is topic mein hum **di gayi (kho gayi) heat** ka sirf magnitude (matra) nikalenge: bada temperature − chhota temperature lekar. Isliye heat hamesha positive sankhya aayegi, aur hum saaf likhenge ki wo **li gayi** hai ya **di gayi**.

### Formula ko ulta-sidha karke use karna

| Kya chahiye? | Formula |
|---|---|
| Heat Q | Q = m × c × ΔT |
| Temperature ka badlaav ΔT | ΔT = Q ÷ (m × c) |
| Mass m | m = Q ÷ (c × ΔT) |
| Specific heat c | c = Q ÷ (m × ΔT) |

### Ek figure: same heat, alag-alag cheez

1 kg ki alag-alag cheezon ko same 8400 J heat dein, to temperature kitna badhega? (ΔT = Q ÷ (m × c) se: paani 8400 ÷ 4200 = 2 °C, copper 8400 ÷ 400 = 21 °C, mercury 8400 ÷ 140 = 60 °C.)

```
 Same heat 8400 J, har ek ka mass 1 kg: temperature ka badlaav (ΔT)

 Paani    (c = 4200) : ## 2 °C
 Copper   (c = 400)  : ##################### 21 °C
 Mercury  (c = 140)  : ############################################################ 60 °C
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Same heat dene par jis ka specific heat jitna kam, uska temperature utna zyada badhta hai (har # ≈ 1 °C).

## Part D: Aniket ka sawal solve karte hain

Ms. Bhandari ne 0.2 kg tel aur 0.2 kg paani liya. Dono ko burner se **16800 J** heat mili (same flame, same time: dono ko barabar). Kis ka temperature kitna badha?

Formula: ΔT = Q ÷ (m × c)

**Paani:** ΔT = 16800 J ÷ (0.2 kg × 4200 J/kg °C) = 16800 J ÷ 840 J/°C = **20 °C**

**Tel (c = 2000 J/kg °C):** ΔT = 16800 J ÷ (0.2 kg × 2000 J/kg °C) = 16800 J ÷ 400 J/°C = **42 °C**

**Jaanch:** Paani: 0.2 × 4200 × 20 = 16800 J ✅. Tel: 0.2 × 2000 × 42 = 16800 J ✅. Tel ka temperature badlaav 42 ÷ 20 = 2.1 guna, yaani lagbhag **do guna** (4200 aur 2000 ka ratio bhi lagbhag 2 hai) ✅

> Ms. Bhandari boli, "Dekha Aniket? Heat dono ko barabar mili. Par paani ko garm karne ke liye zyada heat chahiye, isliye usne wahi heat leke sirf 20 °C badha. Tel ko kam heat chahiye, to usi heat se wo 42 °C chadh gaya."

Aniket muskuraya, "To burner ne dono ko barabar diya, par paani 'zyada heat maangta hai' aur tel 'kam'. Isiliye tel ka thermometer upar chala gaya."

```
  Same flame, same time, same mass (0.2 kg)

     TEL                      PAANI
   ┌───────┐                ┌───────┐
   │ 42 °C │                │ 20 °C │      Dono ne 16800 J liye
   └───────┘                └───────┘
   ═════════                ═════════
    ^ ^ ^ ^                  ^ ^ ^ ^
   burner 1                 burner 2
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Same heat aur same mass par tel ka temperature lagbhag do guna badhta hai, kyunki uska specific heat paani ke lagbhag aadha hai.

## Part E: Solved examples

**Example 1 (garm karne ki heat):** 2 kg paani ko 20 °C se 70 °C tak garm karne ke liye kitni heat chahiye?

- m = 2 kg, c = 4200 J/kg °C, ΔT = 70 − 20 = 50 °C
- Q = m × c × ΔT = 2 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × 50 °C = 8400 J/°C × 50 °C = **420000 J**

**Jaanch:** 420000 ÷ (2 × 4200) = 420000 ÷ 8400 = 50 °C ✅

**Example 2 (thanda hote waqt di gayi heat):** 500 g lohe ka tukda 150 °C se 50 °C tak thanda hota hai. Wo kitni heat deta hai?

- Pehle g ko kg mein badlo: m = 500 g = 500 ÷ 1000 = 0.5 kg
- c = 450 J/kg °C, ΔT = 150 − 50 = 100 °C
- Q = 0.5 kg × 450 J/kg °C × 100 °C = 225 J/°C × 100 °C = **22500 J** (di gayi)

**Jaanch:** 22500 ÷ (0.5 × 450) = 22500 ÷ 225 = 100 °C ✅. (Dhyan: agar 500 g ko kg mein na badalte, to 500 × 450 × 100 = 22,500,000 J aata: 1000 guna galat.)

**Example 3 (temperature ka badhna):** 1 kg aluminium ko 18000 J heat di gayi. Uska temperature kitna badhega?

- ΔT = Q ÷ (m × c) = 18000 J ÷ (1 kg × 900 J/kg °C) = 18000 J ÷ 900 J/°C = **20 °C**

**Jaanch:** 1 × 900 × 20 = 18000 J ✅

**Example 4 (final temperature):** 3 kg paani ka initial temperature 30 °C hai. Use 252000 J heat di jaati hai. Final temperature kya hoga?

- ΔT = 252000 J ÷ (3 kg × 4200 J/kg °C) = 252000 J ÷ 12600 J/°C = 20 °C
- Final temperature = initial + ΔT = 30 °C + 20 °C = **50 °C**

**Jaanch:** 3 × 4200 × (50 − 30) = 12600 × 20 = 252000 J ✅. (Dhyan: 20 °C badlaav hai, final temperature nahi. Final 50 °C hai.)

**Example 5 (mass nikalna):** Ek lohe ke tukde ko 90000 J heat dene par uska temperature 50 °C badhta hai. Tukde ka mass kitna hai?

- m = Q ÷ (c × ΔT) = 90000 J ÷ (450 J/kg °C × 50 °C) = 90000 J ÷ 22500 J/kg = **4 kg**

**Jaanch:** 4 × 450 × 50 = 90000 J ✅

**Example 6 (experiment se specific heat):** 200 g ki ek dhatu ko 3600 J heat dene par uska temperature 20 °C badhta hai. Uska specific heat kitna hai? Ye kaunsi dhatu ho sakti hai?

- m = 200 g = 0.2 kg
- c = Q ÷ (m × ΔT) = 3600 J ÷ (0.2 kg × 20 °C) = 3600 J ÷ 4 kg °C = **900 J/kg °C**
- Table se 900 J/kg °C **aluminium** ka hai.

**Jaanch:** 0.2 × 900 × 20 = 3600 J ✅

**Example 7 (same heat, barabar mass: paani aur copper):** 1 kg paani aur 1 kg copper ko 8400 J heat di jaati hai. Dono ka temperature kitna badhega? Ratio kya hai?

- Paani: ΔT = 8400 J ÷ (1 kg × 4200 J/kg °C) = **2 °C**
- Copper: ΔT = 8400 J ÷ (1 kg × 400 J/kg °C) = **21 °C**
- Ratio ΔT(paani) : ΔT(copper) = 2 : 21 = (1 ÷ 4200) : (1 ÷ 400) = **400 : 4200** (ulta, c ke ratio se)

**Jaanch:** 1 × 4200 × 2 = 8400 J ✅, 1 × 400 × 21 = 8400 J ✅, aur 400 : 4200 = 2 : 21 ✅. Copper lagbhag 10 guna zyada garm hua.

**Example 8 (heat capacity):** 10 kg paani se bhari baalti (baalti ka mass chhod do) ki heat capacity kitni hai?

- Heat capacity = m × c = 10 kg × 4200 J/kg °C = **42000 J/°C**

**Jaanch:** Matlab 1 °C badhane ke liye 42000 J. 10 °C badhane ke liye 420000 J chahiye, aur 10 × 4200 × 10 = 420000 J ✅. Ye wahi "baalti mein heat zyada" wali baat hai: baalti ki heat capacity bahut badi hai, isliye wo bahut heat rakh sakti hai. Topic 1 ke Aviral wale sawal se milao: maan lo 0.2 kg chai 80 °C se kamre ke 20 °C tak thandi ho, to wo 0.2 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × 60 °C = 50400 J deti hai. 10 kg paani 40 °C se 20 °C tak thanda ho, to 10 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × 20 °C = 840000 J deta hai. Chai zyada garm thi, par baalti ke paas heat lagbhag 17 guna zyada hai.

**Example 9 (copper ka thanda hona):** 300 g ka garm copper ka tukda 100 °C se 30 °C tak thanda hota hai. Di gayi heat?

- m = 300 g = 0.3 kg, ΔT = 100 − 30 = 70 °C
- Q = 0.3 kg × 400 J/kg °C × 70 °C = 120 J/°C × 70 °C = **8400 J** (di gayi)

**Jaanch:** 8400 ÷ (0.3 × 400) = 8400 ÷ 120 = 70 °C ✅

**Example 10 (nahane ka paani):** Ek baalti mein 20 kg paani 25 °C par hai. Use 45 °C tak garm karna hai. (a) Kitni heat chahiye? (b) 2 kW ka heater kitni der lagayega?

(a) ΔT = 45 − 25 = 20 °C
Q = 20 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × 20 °C = 84000 J/°C × 20 °C = **1680000 J = 1680 kJ**

(b) Power ka formula (power wale topic se): **P = Q ÷ t**, to t = Q ÷ P.
P = 2 kW = 2 × 1000 = 2000 W = 2000 J/s
t = 1680000 J ÷ 2000 J/s = **840 s** = 840 ÷ 60 = **14 minute**

**Jaanch:** 2000 J/s × 840 s = 1680000 J ✅. (Maan liya ki heater ki saari heat paani ko mili. Asal mein kuch heat bahar bhi jaati hai, to thoda zyada time lagega.)

**Example 11 (calorie mein):** (a) 4200 J ko calorie mein badlo. (b) 420000 J ko kilocalorie (kcal) mein badlo. 1 cal = 4.2 J, 1 kcal = 1000 cal.

(a) 4200 J ÷ 4.2 J/cal = **1000 cal** (= 1 kcal)
(b) 420000 J ÷ 4.2 J/cal = 100000 cal = 100000 ÷ 1000 = **100 kcal**

**Jaanch:** (a) 1000 cal × 4.2 = 4200 J ✅ (yani 1 kg paani ka 1 °C: 1000 g × 1 cal/g °C × 1 °C = 1000 cal ✅). (b) Example 1 ka 420000 J = 100 kcal: 2000 g × 1 cal/g °C × 50 °C = 100000 cal = 100 kcal ✅

**Example 12 (heat capacity se heat):** 0.2 kg aluminium ko 20 °C se 70 °C tak garm karna hai. Heat?

- ΔT = 70 − 20 = 50 °C
- Q = 0.2 kg × 900 J/kg °C × 50 °C = 180 J/°C × 50 °C = **9000 J**

**Jaanch:** Heat capacity = 0.2 × 900 = 180 J/°C, to 50 °C ke liye 180 × 50 = 9000 J ✅. Dekho: Q = (heat capacity) × ΔT bhi likh sakte hain.

## Part F: Paani ka bada specific heat: roz ki zindagi mein

Paani ko garm karne mein heat zyada lagti hai, aur wo thanda hone par bhi zyada heat chhodta hai. Iske kai asar dikhte hain.

**1. Achha coolant.** Car ke engine ka radiator paani se thanda hota hai. Paani engine se bahut saari heat le leta hai, phir bhi uska temperature zyada nahi badhta. Doosri taraf **hot-water bottle** mein paani isliye bharte hain ki wo thanda hone mein bahut heat deta hai aur der tak garmi deta rehta hai.

**2. Samundar kinare ka mausam sama (moderate) rehta hai.** Din mein dhoop se zameen (reth, mitti) jaldi garm ho jati hai, kyunki uska specific heat kam hai. Samundar ka paani dheere garm hota hai. To din mein zameen samundar se zyada garm hoti hai, aur isliye samundar se thandi hawa zameen ki taraf chalti hai: yahi **sea breeze** hai. Raat ko zameen jaldi thandi hoti hai, par samundar ab bhi garm rehta hai, to garmi aur sardi ke beech ka farak kam rehta hai. (Hawa ke chalne ka poora tareeka aage heat transfer wale topic mein aayega.)

**3. Registan: din mein tez garmi, raat mein thand.** Reth ka specific heat kam hai (lagbhag 830 J/kg °C), to dhoop se wo bahut jaldi garm ho jati hai, aur raat ko utni hi jaldi thandi bhi. Isliye registan mein din mein bahut garmi aur raat mein kaafi thand hoti hai.

**4. Insaani sharir.** Hamare sharir mein lagbhag 60% paani hai. Isliye bahar ka mausam badalne par bhi hamara temperature jaldi nahi badalta, aur hum lagbhag 37 °C par bane rehte hain.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Gram ko kg mein na badalna**

500 g lohe ko 0.5 kg likhna zaroori hai. Q = 500 × 450 × 100 = 22,500,000 J galat hai. Sahi: 0.5 × 450 × 100 = **22500 J**.

**Galti 2: Specific heat aur heat capacity ko ek samajhna**

Specific heat (c) **1 kg** ke liye hai aur material ka gun hai: lohe ka hamesha 450 J/kg °C. Heat capacity **poori cheez** ke liye hai: 0.5 kg lohe ki 225 J/°C, aur 2 kg lohe ki 900 J/°C. Unit dekho: J/kg °C aur J/°C.

**Galti 3: ΔT ki jagah final temperature use karna**

2 kg paani ko 20 °C se 70 °C tak garm karna hai. Galat: Q = 2 × 4200 × 70 = 588000 J. Sahi: ΔT = 70 − 20 = 50 °C, Q = 2 × 4200 × 50 = **420000 J**.

**Galti 4: "Jiska c zyada, wo jaldi garm hota hai"**

Ulta hai. Jiska c zyada hai (jaise paani), use garm karne mein **zyada heat** lagti hai, to wo **dheere** garm hota hai (Example 7: paani 2 °C, copper 21 °C). Jiska c kam hai wo jaldi garm aur jaldi thanda hota hai.

**Galti 5: "Mass badhane se specific heat badhta hai"**

Nahi. c material ka gun hai. 1 g paani ho ya 100 kg paani, c = 4200 J/kg °C hi rahega. Mass badhne se heat capacity badhti hai, c nahi.

**Galti 6: J, kJ aur cal ko mila dena**

1 kJ = 1000 J, 1 kcal = 1000 cal = 4200 J. Example 10 ka 1680000 J = 1680 kJ. Joule aur calorie ek hi quantity ke do unit hain: J ko cal mein badalne ke liye 4.2 se bhaag do, cal ko J mein badalne ke liye 4.2 se guna karo.

## Quick Recap

- **Heat capacity** = poori cheez ka temperature 1 °C badhane ki heat = m × c, unit **J/°C**. Ye mass aur material dono par depend karti hai.
- **Specific heat (c)** = 1 kg ka temperature 1 °C badhane ki heat, unit **J/kg °C** (ya J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹). Ye sirf material (aur state) par depend karti hai, mass par nahi.
- **Q = m × c × ΔT**, jahan ΔT = final − initial. Isse ΔT = Q ÷ (m c), m = Q ÷ (c ΔT), c = Q ÷ (m ΔT).
- Formula teen kadam se bana: Q ∝ m, Q ∝ ΔT, aur Q material par depend karti hai.
- Values: paani 4200, barf 2100, bhaap lagbhag 2000, aluminium 900, reth lagbhag 830, tel lagbhag 2000, lohe 450, copper 400, mercury 140 (J/kg °C).
- Jiska c zyada, wo **dheere** garm aur **dheere** thanda hota hai.
- 1 cal = 4.2 J, 1 cal/g °C = 4200 J/kg °C, 1 kcal = 4200 J.
- Paani ka bada c: car radiator aur hot-water bottle, samundar kinare ka sama mausam, registan ki garm dopahar aur thandi raat, aur insaani sharir (lagbhag 60% paani) ka temperature sthir rehna.
- Kitchen ki kahani: 0.2 kg par 16800 J dene se paani 20 °C aur tel 42 °C badha.

### Answers

1. ΔT = 40 − 10 = 30 °C. Q = 3 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × 30 °C = 12600 J/°C × 30 °C = **378000 J**.
   Jaanch: 378000 ÷ (3 × 4200) = 378000 ÷ 12600 = 30 °C ✅

2. ΔT = 70 − 20 = 50 °C. Q = 2 kg × 400 J/kg °C × 50 °C = 800 J/°C × 50 °C = **40000 J**.
   Jaanch: 40000 ÷ (2 × 400) = 50 °C ✅

3. m = 400 g = 0.4 kg, ΔT = 80 − 30 = 50 °C. Q = 0.4 kg × 900 J/kg °C × 50 °C = 360 J/°C × 50 °C = **18000 J** (di gayi).
   Jaanch: 18000 ÷ (0.4 × 900) = 18000 ÷ 360 = 50 °C ✅

4. m = 250 g = 0.25 kg, ΔT = 60 − 20 = 40 °C. Q = 0.25 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × 40 °C = 1050 J/°C × 40 °C = **42000 J = 42 kJ**.
   Jaanch: 42000 ÷ (0.25 × 4200) = 42000 ÷ 1050 = 40 °C ✅

5. ΔT = 90000 J ÷ (5 kg × 450 J/kg °C) = 90000 J ÷ 2250 J/°C = 40 °C. Final temperature = 20 + 40 = **60 °C**.
   Jaanch: 5 × 450 × (60 − 20) = 2250 × 40 = 90000 J ✅

6. m = Q ÷ (c × ΔT) = 84000 J ÷ (4200 J/kg °C × 10 °C) = 84000 J ÷ 42000 J/kg = **2 kg**.
   Jaanch: 2 × 4200 × 10 = 84000 J ✅

7. c = Q ÷ (m × ΔT) = 7000 J ÷ (0.5 kg × 100 °C) = 7000 J ÷ 50 kg °C = **140 J/kg °C**. Table mein ye **mercury** ka hai.
   Jaanch: 0.5 × 140 × 100 = 7000 J ✅

8. (a) Heat capacity = m × c = 3 kg × 400 J/kg °C = **1200 J/°C**. (b) Q = 1200 J/°C × 15 °C = **18000 J**.
   Jaanch: 3 × 400 × 15 = 18000 J ✅

9. Paani: ΔT = 8400 J ÷ (1 kg × 4200 J/kg °C) = **2 °C**. Barf: ΔT = 8400 J ÷ (1 kg × 2100 J/kg °C) = **4 °C**. Barf zyada tezi se garm hui, kyunki uska specific heat paani ka aadha hai, to usi heat se temperature double badha.
   Jaanch: 1 × 4200 × 2 = 8400 J ✅, 1 × 2100 × 4 = 8400 J ✅

10. Q = 5 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × 20 °C = 21000 J/°C × 20 °C = **420000 J**. P = 1 kW = 1000 W = 1000 J/s, to t = 420000 J ÷ 1000 J/s = **420 s** = 7 minute.
    Jaanch: 1000 J/s × 420 s = 420000 J ✅

11. (a) 8400 J ÷ 4.2 J/cal = **2000 cal = 2 kcal**. (b) 5000 cal × 4.2 J/cal = **21000 J**.
    Jaanch: 2000 × 4.2 = 8400 ✅; 21000 ÷ 4.2 = 5000 ✅

12. (a) **False** ❌. Jiska c zyada hai, use garm karne mein zyada heat lagti hai, to wo dheere garm hota hai.
    (b) **False** ❌. Specific heat material ka gun hai, mass se nahi badalta: 4200 J/kg °C hi rahega. Heat capacity 10 guna hoti hai.
    (c) **True** ✅. Heat capacity = m × c, unit J/°C.
    (d) **True** ✅. Reth ka specific heat (lagbhag 830) paani ke (4200) se kam hai, to wo jaldi garm hoti hai.

13. Paani ka specific heat bahut bada hai (4200 J/kg °C), to wo thoda sa temperature badhne par bhi bahut heat le leta hai. Isliye radiator mein wo engine ki heat bahut lekar use thanda rakhta hai. Hot-water bottle mein wahi paani thanda hone par bahut heat chhodta hai, to der tak sekai (garmi) deta hai.

14. Tel: ΔT = 33600 J ÷ (0.2 kg × 2000 J/kg °C) = 33600 J ÷ 400 J/°C = **84 °C**. Paani: ΔT = 33600 J ÷ (0.2 kg × 4200 J/kg °C) = 33600 J ÷ 840 J/°C = **40 °C**. Tel ka badlaav 84 ÷ 40 = 2.1 guna.
