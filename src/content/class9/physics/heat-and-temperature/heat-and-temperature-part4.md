# Topic 4: Calorimetry aur Principle of Mixtures

Pichhle topic mein humne ek bahut kaam ka formula seekha: **Q = m c ΔT**. Isse pata chalta hai ki kisi cheez ko garam karne par wo kitni heat **leti** hai, ya thanda hone par kitni heat **deti** hai. Wahan hum ek hi cheez ko dekh rahe the. Ab sawal ye hai: jab **do cheezein** (ek garam, ek thandi) aapas mein mil jaayein, to kya hoga? Garam wali kitni heat degi, thandi wali kitni legi, aur dono ka aakhir mein temperature kitna hoga? Is topic mein yehi dekhenge, aur ek aisa tareeka bhi seekhenge jisse kisi cheez ka specific heat **naapa** ja sakta hai.

**Is poore topic mein** c(water) = 4200 J/kg °C, c(aluminium) = 900 J/kg °C, c(iron) = 450 J/kg °C aur c(copper) = 400 J/kg °C (approximate values) liye gaye hain.

## Sardi ki subah, nahane ka paani

Sardiyon ki ek subah thi. Mr. Chauhan ne apni poti Reva ko awaaz di, "Reva, aao, aaj nahane ka paani tum bhi taiyar karna seekho." Reva rajai chhodke bathroom mein aayi.

Geyser ka paani bahut garam tha: **80 °C**, haath dalo to jal jaaye. Nal (tap) ka paani itna thanda tha ki ungliyaan sunn ho gayi: **20 °C**. Mr. Chauhan ne ek bade tub mein pehle **1 balti** (10 kg) geyser ka paani dala, phir nal ka paani **doguna** (20 kg) milaya. Phir haath dalke hilaya aur bole, "Haan, ab nahane layak hai."

Reva ne poocha, "Dadaji, aapne haath se check kiya. Par kya bina haath dale pehle se hi pata chal sakta hai ki **mix hone ke baad temperature kitna hoga**? Agar tub mein paani 30 kg hai aur hum kuch aur ratio lein, tab?"

Mr. Chauhan muskuraaye, "Bilkul pata chal sakta hai, aur wo bhi sirf ek chhote se niyam se: **jitni heat garam paani ne kho di, utni hi thande paani ne le li**. Is kahani ko hum topic ke beech mein solve karenge."

## Part A: Calorimetry aur calorimeter

**Calorimetry** ka matlab hai heat ka **measurement**. Jis bartan mein ye measurement kiya jaata hai use **calorimeter** kehte hain. Isme do hisse hote hain:

- Andar ka vessel, jo **copper ya aluminium** ka hota hai (ye metal heat ko achhi tarah aage dete hain, to paani aur vessel jaldi ek-se temperature par aa jaate hain).
- Bahar ka **insulated jacket** (jaise thermocol ya felt), jis par **lid (dhakkan)** hota hai. Lid mein ek chhota sa **thermometer ka hole** hota hai, aur ek **stirrer** bhi laga hota hai.

```
        thermometer      stirrer
           │               │
     ┌─────┼───────────────┼─────┐  <- lid (dhakkan)
     │     │               │     │
   ╔═╪═════╪═══════════════╪═════╪═╗
   ║ │   ┌─┴───────────────┴─┐   │ ║
   ║ │   │  ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~  │   │ ║  <- insulated
   ║ │   │  ~ paani + cheez ~│   │ ║     jacket
   ║ │   │  ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~  │   │ ║
   ║ │   └───────────────────┘   │ ║
   ║ │   copper/aluminium vessel │ ║
   ╚═╧═══════════════════════════╧═╝
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Calorimeter: metal ka vessel, bahar insulated jacket, lid mein thermometer aur stirrer ke liye jagah.

**Itna ghumaa-phira ke kyun banaya?** Maqsad ek hi hai: **surroundings ko heat ka loss kam se kam rakhna**. Agar heat bahar nikal jaaye ya bahar se andar aa jaaye, to hamara "garam ne jitna khoya utna thande ne paya" wala hisaab bigad jaayega. Jacket aur lid heat ko bahar bhaagne se rokte hain, aur stirrer paani ko hilakar poore paani ka temperature ek-sa kar deta hai.

> **Ek line:** calorimeter ka apna vessel bhi thodi heat leta hai, par is topic mein hum use **ignore** karenge jab tak sawaal mein na likha ho. Isko theek se jodna aage ki class mein "water equivalent" ke naam se aata hai.

## Part B: Principle of calorimetry (principle of mixtures)

Jab **do cheezein, alag temperature par**, mix ho jaayein ya ek doosre ke contact mein aa jaayein, aur surroundings ko koi heat loss na ho, to:

> **Heat lost by hotter body = Heat gained by colder body**

Ye tab tak chalta hai jab tak dono ka temperature **ek ho jaaye**: us final temperature T ko hum **thermal equilibrium** ka temperature kehte hain. Uske baad koi heat idhar se udhar nahi jaati.

**Ye principle kahaan se aaya?** Ye **energy conservation** ka hi niyam hai, jo tumne [work-energy-and-power-part6.md](../work-energy-and-power/work-energy-and-power-part6.md) mein dekha tha: energy na banti hai, na khatam hoti hai, sirf ek jagah se doosri jagah jaati hai. Yahan **heat energy** garam cheez se thandi cheez ko chali jaati hai. Jitni ek jagah se gayi, utni doosri jagah pahunchi.

### Working formula

Maano hotter body: mass m₁, specific heat c₁, shuruaati temperature T₁. Colder body: mass m₂, specific heat c₂, shuruaati temperature T₂ (T₁ > T₂). Final temperature T.

- Garam body ka temperature **T₁ se girkar T** tak aaya, to wo **T₁ − T** (positive number) se thanda hui: heat lost = m₁ c₁ (T₁ − T).
- Thandi body ka temperature **T₂ se badhkar T** tak gaya, to wo **T − T₂** (positive number) se garam hui: heat gained = m₂ c₂ (T − T₂).

Principle kehta hai dono barabar hain:

> **m₁ c₁ (T₁ − T) = m₂ c₂ (T − T₂)**

Yaad rakhne ki trick: dono taraf **ΔT ko positive number** ki tarah likho: garam ke liye "bada − chhota" aur thande ke liye bhi "bada − chhota". Tab kisi sign ki ulajhan nahi hogi.

### Special case: ek hi substance (paani + paani)

Agar dono taraf paani hi hai, to c₁ = c₂ = c, aur wo dono taraf se kat jaata hai:

m₁ (T₁ − T) = m₂ (T − T₂)

m₁ T₁ − m₁ T = m₂ T − m₂ T₂

m₁ T₁ + m₂ T₂ = (m₁ + m₂) T

> **T = (m₁ T₁ + m₂ T₂) ÷ (m₁ + m₂)**

Ye ek **weighted average** hai: jiska mass zyada, uske temperature ka asar final T par utna zyada. Agar dono ke mass **barabar** hain (m₁ = m₂), to T = (T₁ + T₂) ÷ 2, yaani seedha **average**.

### Final temperature hamesha beech mein

Mix hone ke baad T hamesha **T₂ aur T₁ ke beech** hota hai: T₂ < T < T₁. Garam wali cheez thandi hoti hai par thandi wali se zyada thandi nahi ho sakti, aur thandi wali garam hoti hai par garam wali se zyada garam nahi.

```
  T₂ (20 °C)                                    T₁ (80 °C)
   ●────────────────────────────────────────────●
   thanda                    ^                  garam
                    T yahin kahin beech mein
        (zyada mass garam ka: T T₁ ke paas
         zyada mass thande ka: T T₂ ke paas)
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Final temperature T hamesha dono shuruaati temperature ke beech mein hota hai. Jis cheez ka (m × c) bada ho, T uske shuruaati temperature ke paas hota hai.

### Sawal hal karne ka tareeka (5 steps)

1. **Data likho:** har cheez ka m, c, shuruaati temperature (mass kg mein).
2. **Decide karo** kaun heat **khoyega** (garam) aur kaun **payega** (thanda).
3. **Likho:** heat lost = heat gained, yaani m₁ c₁ (T₁ − T) = m₂ c₂ (T − T₂).
4. **Solve karo** T (ya jo bhi anjaan ho) ke liye.
5. **Jaanch karo:** T dono shuruaati temperature ke **beech** mein hai? Agar nahi, to kahin galti hai.

## Part C: Reva ka sawal, hal ho gaya

Dadaji ne **10 kg paani 80 °C** par aur **20 kg paani 20 °C** par mix kiya. Dono paani hain, to formula seedha:

T = (m₁ T₁ + m₂ T₂) ÷ (m₁ + m₂) = (10 kg × 80 °C + 20 kg × 20 °C) ÷ (10 kg + 20 kg)

= (800 + 400) kg °C ÷ 30 kg = 1200 kg °C ÷ 30 kg = **40 °C**

**Jaanch (heat se):**
- Heat lost = m₁ c (T₁ − T) = 10 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × (80 − 40) °C = **1,680,000 J**
- Heat gained = m₂ c (T − T₂) = 20 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × (40 − 20) °C = **1,680,000 J**

Dono barabar hain, aur 40 °C, 20 °C aur 80 °C ke beech mein hai ✅. Ye bhi dekho: 80 se 40 ki doori 40 °C hai aur 40 se 20 ki doori 20 °C. Thande paani ka mass doguna tha, isliye final temperature thande paani ke shuruaati 20 °C ke zyada paas (sirf 20 °C upar) aaya, 2 : 1 ke hisaab se.

> Reva ne kaha, "Dadaji, to ab main bina haath dale bata sakti hoon ki paani 40 °C hoga!" Dadaji hanse, "Aur haath se check karna bhi mat chhodna, bete: sirf sawal ka jawab nahi, nahane ka maza bhi to dekhna hai."

## Part D: Solved examples

**Example 1 (barabar mass, average):** 3 kg paani 90 °C par aur 3 kg paani 30 °C par mix kiye gaye. Final temperature?

- m₁ = m₂ = 3 kg, to T = average = (90 °C + 30 °C) ÷ 2 = 120 °C ÷ 2 = **60 °C**
- Formula se: T = (3 kg × 90 °C + 3 kg × 30 °C) ÷ (3 kg + 3 kg) = (270 + 90) kg °C ÷ 6 kg = 360 kg °C ÷ 6 kg = **60 °C**

**Jaanch:** 90 − 60 = 30 °C aur 60 − 30 = 30 °C, dono ΔT barabar ✅, 60 °C, 30 aur 90 ke beech mein ✅

**Example 2 (alag mass):** 2 kg paani 70 °C par aur 8 kg paani 20 °C par mix kiye gaye. Final temperature?

- T = (2 kg × 70 °C + 8 kg × 20 °C) ÷ (2 kg + 8 kg) = (140 + 160) kg °C ÷ 10 kg = 300 kg °C ÷ 10 kg = **30 °C**

**Jaanch:** Lost = 2 × 4200 × (70 − 30) = 2 × 4200 × 40 = 336,000 J. Gained = 8 × 4200 × (30 − 20) = 8 × 4200 × 10 = 336,000 J ✅. T = 30 °C, 20 aur 70 ke beech mein, aur thande paani ka mass zyada hai to T 20 ke paas hai ✅

**Example 3 (garam paani ka mass nikalna):** Dadaji ko **30 kg paani 40 °C** par chahiye. Unke paas 80 °C ka geyser ka paani aur 20 °C ka nal ka paani hai. Kitna-kitna lein?

Maano garam paani ka mass m₁ kg. Kul 30 kg chahiye, to thande ka mass m₂ = (30 − m₁) kg.

- Heat lost = heat gained: m₁ × c × (80 − 40) = (30 − m₁) × c × (40 − 20)
- c dono taraf se kat gaya: 40 m₁ = 20 (30 − m₁)
- 40 m₁ = 600 − 20 m₁, to 60 m₁ = 600, to **m₁ = 10 kg**
- Thanda paani: m₂ = 30 − 10 = **20 kg**

**Jaanch:** T = (10 kg × 80 °C + 20 kg × 20 °C) ÷ 30 kg = 1200 kg °C ÷ 30 kg = 40 °C ✅ (yahi to Reva ka tub tha)

**Example 4 (iron ka gola paani mein):** 200 g ka iron ka gola 150 °C par hai. Use 400 g paani (20 °C) mein dala gaya. Final temperature? Calorimeter ki heat ignore karo.

Pehle **g ko kg** mein badlo: 200 g = 0.2 kg, 400 g = 0.4 kg.

- Heat lost (iron) = 0.2 kg × 450 J/kg °C × (150 − T) °C = 90 (150 − T) J
- Heat gained (paani) = 0.4 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × (T − 20) °C = 1680 (T − 20) J
- Barabar likho: 90 (150 − T) = 1680 (T − 20)
- Kholo: 13500 − 90 T = 1680 T − 33600
- 13500 + 33600 = 1680 T + 90 T, to 47100 = 1770 T
- T = 47100 ÷ 1770 = 26.61... ≈ **26.6 °C**

**Jaanch:** Lost = 0.2 × 450 × (150 − 26.61) = 90 × 123.39 ≈ 11,105 J. Gained = 0.4 × 4200 × (26.61 − 20) = 1680 × 6.61 ≈ 11,105 J ✅. T = 26.6 °C, 20 aur 150 ke beech mein ✅ (paani ka m × c = 0.4 × 4200 = 1680 J/°C, iron ka sirf 0.2 × 450 = 90 J/°C: paani ki "pakad" bahut zyada hai, isliye T paani ke 20 °C ke bahut paas raha)

**Example 5 (copper ka block paani mein):** 500 g copper ka block 100 °C par hai. Use 1 kg paani (20 °C) mein dala. Final temperature?

- m = 0.5 kg copper, 1 kg paani.
- Heat lost = 0.5 kg × 400 J/kg °C × (100 − T) °C = 200 (100 − T) J
- Heat gained = 1 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × (T − 20) °C = 4200 (T − 20) J
- 200 (100 − T) = 4200 (T − 20), to 20000 − 200 T = 4200 T − 84000
- 104000 = 4400 T, to T = 104000 ÷ 4400 = 23.63... ≈ **23.6 °C**

**Jaanch:** Lost = 0.5 × 400 × (100 − 23.64) = 200 × 76.36 ≈ 15,273 J. Gained = 1 × 4200 × (23.64 − 20) = 4200 × 3.64 ≈ 15,273 J ✅

**Example 6 (do metal mix):** 0.5 kg aluminium ka tukda 100 °C par aur 1 kg iron ka tukda 20 °C par ek doosre ke contact mein rakhe gaye (bahar ko heat loss nahi). Final temperature?

- Heat lost (Al) = 0.5 kg × 900 J/kg °C × (100 − T) °C = 450 (100 − T) J
- Heat gained (Fe) = 1 kg × 450 J/kg °C × (T − 20) °C = 450 (T − 20) J
- 450 (100 − T) = 450 (T − 20), to 100 − T = T − 20, to 120 = 2 T, to **T = 60 °C**

**Jaanch:** Lost = 0.5 × 900 × 40 = 18,000 J. Gained = 1 × 450 × 40 = 18,000 J ✅. Dhyan do: yahan mass barabar nahi the (0.5 kg aur 1 kg), phir bhi T seedha average (60 °C) aaya. Wajah ye ki dono ka **m × c** barabar tha (0.5 × 900 = 450 aur 1 × 450 = 450). "Average" ka asli matlab hai ki dono ka m × c barabar ho.

**Example 7 (chai mein doodh):** 150 g chai 80 °C par hai aur usme 50 g doodh 10 °C par milaya. Dono ko paani maano (c same). Final temperature?

- T = (m₁ T₁ + m₂ T₂) ÷ (m₁ + m₂), yahan g mein hi chalega kyunki ratio hai (dono g mein hain):
- T = (150 g × 80 °C + 50 g × 10 °C) ÷ (150 g + 50 g) = (12000 + 500) g °C ÷ 200 g = 12500 g °C ÷ 200 g = **62.5 °C**

**Jaanch:** Chai ne gawai = 0.15 × 4200 × (80 − 62.5) = 630 × 17.5 = 11,025 J. Doodh ne payi = 0.05 × 4200 × (62.5 − 10) = 210 × 52.5 = 11,025 J ✅

**Example 8 (galat jawab pakadna):** Ek student ne 3 kg paani (90 °C) aur 2 kg paani (40 °C) mix karke jawab nikala: "T = 20 °C" aur doosre ne "T = 95 °C". Sahi jawab kya hai, aur galat jawab turant kaise pakde?

- Sahi: T = (3 kg × 90 °C + 2 kg × 40 °C) ÷ (3 kg + 2 kg) = (270 + 80) kg °C ÷ 5 kg = 350 kg °C ÷ 5 kg = **70 °C**
- 20 °C **40 se bhi kam** hai: thanda paani apne shuruaati 40 °C se bhi zyada thanda kaise ho sakta hai? Aisa nahi hota, **galat**.
- 95 °C **90 se bhi zyada** hai: garam paani apne shuruaati 90 °C se bhi zyada garam kaise ho sakta hai? **Galat**.

**Jaanch:** Sahi T = 70 °C, 40 aur 90 ke beech mein ✅. Lost = 3 × 4200 × 20 = 252,000 J, Gained = 2 × 4200 × 30 = 252,000 J ✅

**Example 9 (heat loss ka ek comment):** Agar Example 3 wale tub mein surroundings ko 10% heat loss ho jaaye, to thande paani ne payi hui heat, garam paani ki khoyi hui heat ka sirf 90% hogi, aur final temperature thoda kam hoga. Hamare sawaalon mein hum loss ko ignore karte hain, calorimeter isi loss ko kam karne ke liye hai.

## Part E: Method of mixtures se specific heat naapna

Ye principle ek kaam ka experiment deta hai: kisi **thos (solid) ka specific heat** naapna.

**Idea:** metal ke tukde ko pehle **boiling paani** mein garam karo (temperature T₁ ≈ 100 °C), phir use jaldi se calorimeter ke **thande paani** mein dalo. Dono ka final temperature T naapo. Phir principle laga do.

Notation: metal ka mass m_m, specific heat c_m (anjaan), shuruaati T₁. Paani ka mass m_w, c_w = 4200 J/kg °C, shuruaati T₂. Final T.

m_m c_m (T₁ − T) = m_w c_w (T − T₂)

> **c_m = m_w c_w (T − T₂) ÷ [m_m (T₁ − T)]**

```
   Step 1                  Step 2                   Step 3
   metal ko ubalte         turant calorimeter       hilao, thermometer
   paani mein 100 °C       ke thande paani mein     se final T padho
   tak garam karo          daalo (lid band)

     ┌──────┐              ┌────────┐ lid              ┌────────┐
     │ ~~~~ │  ──────>     │ metal  │         ──────>  │ T = ?  │
     │ metal│              │ ~~~~~~ │                  │ ~~~~~~ │
     └──┬───┘              └────────┘                  └────────┘
      burner               paani T₂                     paani + metal
```

> **Figure 4.3** – Method of mixtures: garam metal ko thande paani mein daalo, aur final temperature naapkar specific heat nikaalo.

### Worked experiment (sample readings)

Ek student ne aluminium ke tukde ke saath ye experiment kiya (calorimeter ki heat ignore):

| Cheez | Reading |
|---|---|
| Metal ka mass m_m | 100 g = 0.1 kg |
| Metal ka shuruaati temperature T₁ | 100 °C |
| Paani ka mass m_w | 200 g = 0.2 kg |
| Paani ka shuruaati temperature T₂ | 27 °C |
| Final temperature T | 34 °C |

- Paani ne payi: m_w c_w (T − T₂) = 0.2 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × (34 − 27) °C = 0.2 × 4200 × 7 = **5880 J**
- Metal ne gawai: m_m c_m (T₁ − T) = 0.1 kg × c_m × (100 − 34) °C = 6.6 c_m  (kg °C)
- Barabar: 6.6 c_m = 5880, to c_m = 5880 ÷ 6.6 = 890.9 ≈ **891 J/kg °C**

**Standard value** aluminium ka 900 J/kg °C hai. Ab **percentage error** nikaalte hain (jaise [measurement-and-units-part4.md](../measurement-and-units/measurement-and-units-part4.md) mein dekha tha):

- Absolute error = 900 − 891 = 9 J/kg °C
- Percentage error = 9 ÷ 900 × 100 = **1.0%**

**Jaanch:** Agar c_m = 891 lein: 0.1 × 891 × 66 = 5880.6 J ≈ 5880 J ✅. Error sirf 1%, to experiment accha raha.

### Example 10: data se specific heat

100 g ka metal ka tukda 100 °C par hai. Use 200 g paani (20 °C) mein dala, aur final temperature 25 °C aaya. Metal ka specific heat?

- m_m = 0.1 kg, m_w = 0.2 kg, T₁ = 100 °C, T₂ = 20 °C, T = 25 °C
- c_m = m_w c_w (T − T₂) ÷ [m_m (T₁ − T)] = 0.2 × 4200 × (25 − 20) ÷ [0.1 × (100 − 25)]
- = 0.2 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × 5 °C ÷ (0.1 kg × 75 °C) = 4200 J ÷ 7.5 kg °C = **560 J/kg °C**

**Jaanch:** Paani ne payi = 0.2 × 4200 × 5 = 4200 J. Metal ne gawai = 0.1 × 560 × 75 = 4200 J ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Heat lost = heat gained likhna bhool jaana, ya signs ulajhana**

Kuch log ek taraf (T − T₁) aur doosri taraf (T − T₂) likh dete hain aur ek taraf negative aa jaata hai. Sabse safe tareeka: dono taraf **ΔT positive** likho. Example 2 mein 70 − 30 = 40 aur 30 − 20 = 10, dono positive.

**Galti 2: Garam body ke liye (T − T₁) likhna**

Garam body ka temperature gira hai, to uska ΔT = **T₁ − T**. Agar (T − T₁) likhoge to negative number aayega aur equation bigad jaayegi. Hamesha "bada − chhota" likho.

**Galti 3: g aur kg mix karna**

Specific heat J/**kg** °C mein hai, to mass **kg** mein hi lo. Example 4 mein 200 g ko 0.2 kg hi likha tha. Agar iron ke liye 200 le lete (aur paani ke liye 0.4 kg), to equation bigad jaati aur answer bilkul galat aata. (Sirf jab dono mass g mein ho aur c same ho, jaise Example 7, tab ratio ke liye g chal jaata hai.)

**Galti 4: Final temperature ko range se bahar jaane dena**

T hamesha T₂ aur T₁ ke **beech** mein hota hai. Example 8 mein 20 °C aur 95 °C dono is range (40 se 90) ke bahar the, isliye turant galat. Ye check har sawaal ke baad kar lo.

**Galti 5: Maan lena ki final temperature hamesha average hota hai**

Average tab hi hota hai jab **dono ka m × c barabar** ho (jaise same substance ke barabar masses, Example 1, ya Example 6 jahan m × c barabar tha). Example 2 mein masses 2 kg aur 8 kg the, T = 30 °C aaya, average (45 °C) nahi.

**Galti 6: Alag substances mein specific heat bhool jaana**

Agar do alag cheezein hain (iron aur paani, Example 4), to c cut nahi hoga. Iron ke 450 aur paani ke 4200 dono likhne zaroori hain. c sirf tab kat-ta hai jab dono taraf **ek hi substance** ho.

## Quick Recap

- **Calorimetry** = heat ka measurement. **Calorimeter** = metal ka vessel (copper/aluminium) + stirrer, insulated jacket, lid, thermometer hole. Maqsad: surroundings ko heat loss kam karna.
- **Principle of mixtures:** (surroundings ko loss nahi ho to) **heat lost by hotter body = heat gained by colder body**, jab tak dono ek final temperature T par (thermal equilibrium) na aa jaayein. Ye heat ke liye energy conservation hai.
- **Working formula:** m₁ c₁ (T₁ − T) = m₂ c₂ (T − T₂), dono ΔT positive.
- **Same substance:** T = (m₁ T₁ + m₂ T₂) ÷ (m₁ + m₂), weighted average. Barabar mass ho to seedha average.
- **Final T hamesha T₂ aur T₁ ke beech** mein hota hai: ye turant check hai.
- **Steps:** data likho, kaun lose/gain karega decide karo, heat lost = heat gained likho, solve karo, range check karo.
- **Specific heat of solid:** c_m = m_w c_w (T − T₂) ÷ [m_m (T₁ − T)]. Experiment: aluminium ka 891 vs 900 J/kg °C, error 1.0%.
- Story: 10 kg (80 °C) + 20 kg (20 °C) paani = 30 kg paani 40 °C par, heat 1,680,000 J idhar se udhar gayi.
- Calorimeter ki apni heat is topic mein ignore ki gayi hai.

## Practice Questions

1. 4 kg of water at 90 °C is mixed with 6 kg of water at 20 °C. Find the final temperature. Verify by calculating the heat lost and the heat gained. Take c of water = 4200 J/kg °C.

2. 2 kg of water at 60 °C is mixed with 2 kg of water at 30 °C. Find the final temperature. Why is it exactly the average?

3. How much water at 70 °C should be added to 10 kg of water at 20 °C so that the mixture is at 30 °C?

4. A 0.5 kg aluminium block at 100 °C is dropped into 1 kg of water at 20 °C. Find the final temperature (ignore the calorimeter). c(aluminium) = 900 J/kg °C, c(water) = 4200 J/kg °C. Give the answer to one decimal place.

5. A 100 g piece of metal at 100 °C is dropped into 200 g of water at 20 °C. The final temperature is 24 °C. Find the specific heat of the metal. Compare with the value for iron (450 J/kg °C) and find the percentage error, if the metal is iron.

6. 200 g of tea at 70 °C is mixed with 100 g of milk at 10 °C. Treat both as water and find the final temperature.

7. 1 kg of water at 80 °C is mixed with 3 kg of water at 20 °C. Find the final temperature.

8. A 0.3 kg copper piece at 90 °C is placed in contact with a 0.2 kg iron piece at 20 °C. Find the final temperature. c(copper) = 400, c(iron) = 450 J/kg °C.

9. A student mixes 5 kg of water at 50 °C with 5 kg of water at 30 °C and writes the final temperature as 80 °C. Without calculating, how do you know it is wrong? What is the correct answer?

10. State True or False:
    (a) In the principle of mixtures, the heat lost by the hot body is always greater than the heat gained by the cold body.
    (b) The final temperature of a mixture always lies between the two initial temperatures.
    (c) The final temperature is always the simple average of the two initial temperatures.
    (d) A calorimeter has an insulated jacket to reduce the heat loss to the surroundings.

### Answers

1. T = (4 kg × 90 °C + 6 kg × 20 °C) ÷ (4 kg + 6 kg) = (360 + 120) kg °C ÷ 10 kg = 480 kg °C ÷ 10 kg = **48 °C**.
   Lost = 4 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × (90 − 48) °C = 705,600 J. Gained = 6 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × (48 − 20) °C = 705,600 J.
   Jaanch: dono barabar, aur 48 °C, 20 aur 90 ke beech mein ✅

2. T = (2 × 60 + 2 × 30) ÷ (2 + 2) = (120 + 60) ÷ 4 = 180 ÷ 4 = **45 °C**. Masses barabar aur substance same, to formula (T₁ + T₂) ÷ 2 ban jaata hai: (60 + 30) ÷ 2 = 45 °C.
   Jaanch: 60 − 45 = 15 °C aur 45 − 30 = 15 °C ✅

3. Maano garam paani ka mass m kg. Heat lost = heat gained: m × c × (70 − 30) °C = 10 kg × c × (30 − 20) °C. c dono taraf se kat gaya: 40 m = 100, to **m = 2.5 kg**.
   Jaanch: T = (2.5 × 70 + 10 × 20) ÷ 12.5 = (175 + 200) ÷ 12.5 = 375 ÷ 12.5 = 30 °C ✅

4. 0.5 kg × 900 J/kg °C × (100 − T) °C = 1 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × (T − 20) °C, to 450 (100 − T) = 4200 (T − 20), to 45000 − 450 T = 4200 T − 84000, to 129000 = 4650 T, to T = **27.7 °C** (27.74 ...).
   Jaanch: Lost = 0.5 × 900 × (100 − 27.74) = 450 × 72.26 ≈ 32,516 J. Gained = 4200 × (27.74 − 20) = 4200 × 7.74 ≈ 32,516 J ✅

5. m_m = 0.1 kg, m_w = 0.2 kg. c = 0.2 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × (24 − 20) °C ÷ [0.1 kg × (100 − 24) °C] = 3360 J ÷ 7.6 kg °C = **442.1 J/kg °C** (≈ 442). Agar metal iron hai: absolute error = 450 − 442.1 = 7.9 J/kg °C, percentage error = 7.9 ÷ 450 × 100 ≈ **1.8%**.
   Jaanch: Paani ne payi = 0.2 × 4200 × 4 = 3360 J. Metal ne gawai = 0.1 × 442.1 × 76 ≈ 3360 J ✅

6. T = (200 × 70 + 100 × 10) ÷ (200 + 100) = (14000 + 1000) ÷ 300 = 15000 ÷ 300 = **50 °C**.
   Jaanch: Chai ne gawai = 0.2 × 4200 × 20 = 16,800 J. Doodh ne payi = 0.1 × 4200 × 40 = 16,800 J ✅

7. T = (1 × 80 + 3 × 20) ÷ (1 + 3) = (80 + 60) ÷ 4 = 140 ÷ 4 = **35 °C**. (Thande paani ka mass 3 guna hai, isliye T 20 °C ke paas, average 50 °C nahi.)
   Jaanch: Lost = 1 × 4200 × 45 = 189,000 J. Gained = 3 × 4200 × 15 = 189,000 J ✅

8. 0.3 kg × 400 J/kg °C × (90 − T) °C = 0.2 kg × 450 J/kg °C × (T − 20) °C, to 120 (90 − T) = 90 (T − 20), to 10800 − 120 T = 90 T − 1800, to 12600 = 210 T, to **T = 60 °C**.
   Jaanch: Lost = 0.3 × 400 × 30 = 3600 J. Gained = 0.2 × 450 × 40 = 3600 J ✅

9. Dono paani 30 °C aur 50 °C ke hain, to final T in dono ke **beech** mein hona chahiye. 80 °C to 50 °C se bhi zyada hai, to galat. Sahi: barabar masses, to T = (50 + 30) ÷ 2 = **40 °C**.
   Jaanch: 50 − 40 = 10 °C aur 40 − 30 = 10 °C ✅

10. (a) **False** ❌. Principle ke hisaab se dono **barabar** hain (jab heat loss na ho).
    (b) **True** ✅. T₂ < T < T₁.
    (c) **False** ❌. Average tab hi hota hai jab dono ka m × c barabar ho (jaise same substance ke barabar masses).
    (d) **True** ✅. Jacket aur lid heat ko surroundings mein jaane se rokte hain.
