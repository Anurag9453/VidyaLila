# Topic 5: Change of State aur Latent Heat

Pichhle topics mein jab bhi garmi (heat) kisi cheez mein gayi ya usse nikli, uska **temperature badla**: paani garm hua to temperature badha, thanda hua to ghata. Calorimetry mein bhi garam paani ne garmi di aur uska temperature gira, thande paani ne garmi li aur uska temperature chadha. Har jagah **Q = m c ΔT** kaam aaya. Par kya garmi dene par temperature **hamesha** badhta hai? Ab ek aisi situation dekhte hain jahan garmi lagatar mil rahi hai, aur phir bhi thermometer apni jagah se hilta nahi.

**Is poore topic mein ye values li gayi hain:** c(paani) = 4200 J/kg °C, c(barf) = 2100 J/kg °C, c(bhaap) = 2000 J/kg °C, barf ke pighalne ki specific latent heat L_f = 3.36 × 10⁵ J/kg (yaani 336 kJ/kg) aur paani ke bhaap banne ki specific latent heat L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg (yaani 2260 kJ/kg). Ye values **lagbhag (approximate)** hain.

---

## Dhoop mein rakhi barf ki badi silli

Ek chhoti si ice factory mein manager Mr. Dar subah ka stock check kar rahe the. Bahar dhoop mein 50 kg ki barf ki ek badi silli (block) rakhi thi, kyunki use truck mein chadhana tha. Paas mein khadi Nitya, jo factory dekhne aayi thi, barf ko dekh rahi thi. Silli ke andar ek thermometer ghusa hua tha, jo Mr. Dar ne subah laga diya tha.

Dhoop tez thi. Silli se paani ki dhaar bahne lagi, kinare gal gaye, aur silli dheere dheere chhoti hone lagi. Nitya ne thermometer dekha. Wo **0 °C** par hi tha. Das minute baad dekha: phir **0 °C**. Aadha ghanta baad: phir **0 °C**, jabki silli ka kaafi hissa pighal chuka tha.

Nitya ne hairaan hokar poochha, "Sir, dhoop to lagatar garmi de rahi hai. Phir thermometer upar kyun nahi chadh raha? Ye garmi **kahan ja rahi hai**?"

Mr. Dar muskuraaye, "Bahut achha sawal hai Nitya. Iska jawab hi aaj ka topic hai. Pehle teen cheezein samjhte hain: states of matter, state badalna, aur phir wo 'chhupi hui garmi'. Aakhir mein hum is 50 kg ki silli ka poora hisaab bhi karenge." Is kahani ko hum topic ke beech mein solve karenge.

## Part A: Matter ke teen roop

Hamare aas-paas ki har cheez **teen states** mein milti hai: **solid, liquid aur gas**. Ek hi padaarth (jaise paani) teeno roop le sakta hai: barf (solid), paani (liquid) aur bhaap (gas). Fark kya hai? Fark hai uske **particles (chhote kanon)** ke baithne ke tareeke mein. Yahan sirf shabdon mein samjhenge:

- **Solid:** particles apni **fixed jagah** par baithe hain, ek doosre ke bahut paas. Wo sirf apni jagah par thoda kaampte hain, jagah chhodte nahi. Isliye solid ka apna fixed aakaar aur size hota hai.
- **Liquid:** particles ab bhi ek doosre ke **paas** hain, par ek doosre ke upar se **phisal** sakte hain. Isliye liquid bahta hai aur bartan ka aakaar le leta hai.
- **Gas:** particles ek doosre se **bahut door** aur **azaad** hote hain, idhar-udhar tezi se ghoomte hain. Isliye gas poore bartan mein phail jaati hai.

```
   SOLID              LIQUID               GAS

  o o o o o          o  o    o           o        o
  o o o o o           o   o  o                o
  o o o o o          o  o  o  o        o           o
  o o o o o           o  o   o              o    o
                                          o         o
 fixed jagah,        paas-paas,          door-door,
 bas kaampte         phisalte hain       azaad ghoomte
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Solid mein particles fixed jagah par, liquid mein paas par phisalte hue, gas mein door-door aur azaad.

Ek zaroori baat: **particles ke beech ek doosre ko pakde rakhne wala "hold" (aakarshan)** hota hai. Solid mein ye hold sabse mazboot hai, liquid mein kam, aur gas mein lagbhag nahi ke barabar. Aage ye hold hi humari kahani ki chaabi hai.

## Part B: State kaise badalti hai?

Garmi dene ya nikalne se matter ek state se doosri state mein ja sakta hai. In badlaavon ke alag-alag naam hain:

| Badlaav | Naam | Garmi ka kya hota hai? |
|---|---|---|
| Solid → Liquid | **Melting (fusion)** | Garmi li jaati hai |
| Liquid → Solid | **Freezing (solidification)** | Garmi nikalti hai |
| Liquid → Gas | **Vaporisation** (boiling ya evaporation) | Garmi li jaati hai |
| Gas → Liquid | **Condensation** | Garmi nikalti hai |
| Solid → Gas | **Sublimation** | Garmi li jaati hai |
| Gas → Solid | Sublimation ka ulta (jam jaana) | Garmi nikalti hai |

**Sublimation** ke aam example: **kapoor (camphor)**, **naphthalene ki goliyan** (almari mein kapdon ke saath) aur **dry ice** (jama hui carbon dioxide). Ye khule mein rakhne par bina pighle seedhe gas ban kar ud jaate hain, aur isiliye kuch dino mein chhote hote jaate hain.

```
                  sublimation (garmi lo)
         ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
         │                                                     ▼
      SOLID ──── melting ────▶ LIQUID ──── vaporisation ────▶ GAS
    (barf)   ◀─── freezing ──── (paani) ◀── condensation ──── (bhaap)
         ▲                                                     │
         └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                  gas se seedha solid (garmi nikaalo)
```

> **Figure 5.2** – State badalne ke chakkar mein teeno states aur unke beech ke badlaav. Aage jaate hue (solid se gas ki taraf) garmi **li** jaati hai, ulta jaate hue garmi **nikalti** hai.

### Melting point aur boiling point

Har pure padaarth ka solid se liquid banne ka ek fixed temperature hota hai, jise **melting point** kehte hain. Pure barf ke liye ye **0 °C** hai. Isi tarah liquid ke poore ubalne ka fixed temperature **boiling point** hai. Paani ke liye ye **100 °C** hai (normal pressure par). Jo temperature par solid pighalta hai, wahi temperature par liquid jamta bhi hai: pure paani ka freezing point bhi 0 °C hi hai.

## Part C: Latent heat: chhupi hui garmi

Ab Nitya ke sawal par aate hain. Silli ko lagatar garmi mil rahi hai, par thermometer 0 °C par atka hai. Garmi kahan gayi?

Yaad karo: barf mein particles apni fixed jagah par ek doosre ko **mazboot hold** se pakde baithe hain. Jab garmi milti hai to pehle ki tarah (Topic 3 jaisa) particles tez kaampte hain aur temperature badhta hai. Par **0 °C par pahunchne ke baad** kuch aur hota hai: ab milne wali garmi particles ko tez nahi karti, balki us **hold ko dheela karne** mein kharch ho jaati hai, taaki particles apni fixed jagah chhod kar phisalne lagein. Yaani garmi ka kaam ab **state badalna** hai, temperature badhana nahi.

> **Jab tak poori barf paani nahi ban jaati, tab tak temperature 0 °C par hi rehta hai, chahe kitni bhi garmi mile.**

Ye garmi gayab nahi hui: wo paani ke particles ke andar **energy ke roop mein store** ho gayi. Hum ise thermometer se "mehsoos" nahi kar sakte, isiliye ise **latent heat** kehte hain (latent = chhupi hui). Ye ulta bhi hota hai: jab paani jam kar barf banta hai, to wahi chhupi hui garmi **bahar nikalti** hai aur temperature tab bhi 0 °C par hi rehta hai.

Boiling mein bhi yahi hota hai: 100 °C par pahunchne ke baad paani ko garmi milti rehti hai, par temperature 100 °C par hi rukta hai, kyunki garmi particles ko ek doosre se **poori tarah door karne** (liquid se gas banane) mein lag jaati hai. Ye kaam bahut bada hai, isiliye boiling mein garmi bhi bahut zyada lagti hai (aage dekhenge).

**Bahut zaroori:** latent heat sirf tab involve hoti hai jab **state badal rahi ho**. Jab ek hi state mein (sirf barf, ya sirf paani, ya sirf bhaap) temperature badh raha ho, tab latent heat ki baat nahi hoti: tab hum pehle wala **Q = m c ΔT** hi lagate hain.

## Part D: Specific latent heat aur formula Q = m L

Alag-alag padaarthon ko pighlane mein alag-alag garmi lagti hai, aur zyada mass ho to zyada garmi lagti hai. Isliye 1 kg ke liye ek standard value banayi gayi:

- **Specific latent heat of fusion (L_f):** **1 kg** solid ko, uske **melting point par**, bina temperature badle **liquid** mein badalne ke liye zaroori garmi. Barf ke liye **L_f = 3.36 × 10⁵ J/kg** (yaani 336000 J/kg = 336 kJ/kg).
- **Specific latent heat of vaporisation (L_v):** **1 kg** liquid ko, uske **boiling point par**, bina temperature badle **gas** mein badalne ke liye zaroori garmi. Paani ke liye **L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg** (yaani 2260000 J/kg = 2260 kJ/kg).

Unit hai **J/kg**. Ab jab m kg padaarth ki state badalni ho, to:

> **Q = m × L**
> (Q = state badalne mein li ya di gayi garmi, J mein; m = mass, kg mein; L = specific latent heat, J/kg mein)

Is formula mein **ΔT nahi hai**, kyunki state badalte waqt temperature badalta hi nahi.

Ek baat dhyan dene layak: **L_v, L_f se kaafi bada hai.** (2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg) ÷ (3.36 × 10⁵ J/kg) ≈ 6.7, yaani paani ko bhaap banane mein barf ko pighlane se lagbhag **7 guna** zyada garmi lagti hai. Aisa isliye ki liquid se gas banne mein particles ko ek doosre se poori tarah door karna padta hai.

### Calorie waali purani values se milaan

Purane zamane mein ye values **calorie** mein di jaati thi: L_f(barf) = **80 cal/g** aur L_v(paani) = **540 cal/g**. Hum **1 cal = 4.2 J** lete hain:

- 80 cal/g × 4.2 J/cal = **336 J/g** = 336000 J/kg = 3.36 × 10⁵ J/kg ✅
- 540 cal/g × 4.2 J/cal = **2268 J/g** ≈ 2260000 J/kg ≈ 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg ✅

Dono value-sets **match** karte hain (2268 aur 2260 lagbhag barabar hain, kyunki ye approximate values hain).

## Part E: Barf se bhaap tak ka safar: Heating curve

Ab 1 kg barf lo jo **−10 °C** par hai, aur use lagatar garmi do, yahan tak ki wo **110 °C** ki bhaap ban jaaye. Temperature kaise badlega? Ye pura safar paanch segments mein hai.

```
 T (°C)
  110 ┤                                            ╱  E
      │                                          ╱
  100 ┤                          ╱───────────────
      │                        ╱     D (ubalna)
      │                      ╱  C
      │                    ╱
    0 ┤       ╱────────────
      │     ╱    B (pighalna)
  -10 ┤   ╱  A
      │
      └──────────────────────────────────────────────────▶ Q (di gayi garmi)
```

> **Figure 5.3** – 1 kg barf ka heating curve (scale ke hisaab se nahi). A, C, E mein temperature badhta hai (Q = m c ΔT). B (0 °C par) aur D (100 °C par) mein graph **flat** hai: yahan state badal rahi hai (Q = m L).

Har segment ke liye 1 kg ka hisaab:

| Segment | Kya ho raha hai | Formula | Garmi (1 kg ke liye) |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Barf −10 °C se 0 °C tak garm hui | m c_barf ΔT = 1 kg × 2100 J/kg °C × 10 °C | **21000 J** |
| B | Barf 0 °C par pighli (temperature 0 °C) | m L_f = 1 kg × 336000 J/kg | **336000 J** |
| C | Paani 0 °C se 100 °C tak garm hua | m c_paani ΔT = 1 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × 100 °C | **420000 J** |
| D | Paani 100 °C par bhaap bana (temperature 100 °C) | m L_v = 1 kg × 2260000 J/kg | **2260000 J** |
| E | Bhaap 100 °C se 110 °C tak garm hui | m c_bhaap ΔT = 1 kg × 2000 J/kg °C × 10 °C | **20000 J** |

**Kul garmi** = 21000 + 336000 + 420000 + 2260000 + 20000 = **3057000 J** (lagbhag 3.06 × 10⁶ J).

**Jaanch:** 21000 + 336000 = 357000; + 420000 = 777000; + 2260000 = 3037000; + 20000 = **3057000 J** ✅

Heating curve ke readings ko ek table mein bhi dekho (jaise-jaise garmi jodte gaye):

| Ab tak di gayi kul garmi | Temperature | Is waqt kya hai |
|---|---|---|
| 0 J | −10 °C | Thandi barf |
| 21000 J | 0 °C | Barf pighalne wali hai |
| 357000 J | 0 °C | Poori barf pighal gayi, ab paani hai |
| 777000 J | 100 °C | Paani ubalne wala hai |
| 3037000 J | 100 °C | Poora paani bhaap ban gaya |
| 3057000 J | 110 °C | Bhaap 110 °C par |

Dhyan do: **boiling wala segment (D) akela hi kul garmi ka lagbhag 74% hai** (2260000 ÷ 3057000 ≈ 0.74). Aur ye bhi: segments B aur D mein **temperature sach mein flat** rehta hai, bhale hi garmi lagatar di ja rahi ho.

## Part F: Solved examples

**Example 1 (barf pighlana):** 2 kg barf 0 °C par hai. Use poori tarah paani (0 °C) banane mein kitni garmi lagegi?

- State badal rahi hai, temperature nahi: Q = m L_f
- Q = 2 kg × 336000 J/kg = **672000 J**

**Jaanch:** 2 × 336000 = 672000 ✅. Aur 1 kg ke liye 336000 J tha, to 2 kg ke liye double ✅

**Example 2 (paani ko bhaap banana):** 0.5 kg paani 100 °C par hai. Use poori bhaap (100 °C) banane ke liye kitni garmi chahiye?

- Q = m L_v = 0.5 kg × 2260000 J/kg = **1130000 J**

**Jaanch:** 1 kg ke liye 2260000 J, aadhe kg ke liye aadha = 1130000 J ✅

**Example 3 (barf se garm paani tak):** 200 g barf −10 °C par hai. Use 20 °C ke paani mein badalne ke liye kitni garmi chahiye?

Pehle mass ko kg mein badlo: 200 g = **0.2 kg**. Teen segments hain: barf ko 0 °C tak garm karo, pighlao, phir paani ko 20 °C tak garm karo.

- Segment 1 (barf −10 °C → 0 °C): Q₁ = m c_barf ΔT = 0.2 kg × 2100 J/kg °C × 10 °C = **4200 J**
- Segment 2 (0 °C par pighalna): Q₂ = m L_f = 0.2 kg × 336000 J/kg = **67200 J**
- Segment 3 (paani 0 °C → 20 °C): Q₃ = m c_paani ΔT = 0.2 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × 20 °C = **16800 J**
- Kul Q = 4200 J + 67200 J + 16800 J = **88200 J**

**Jaanch:** 4200 + 67200 = 71400; 71400 + 16800 = 88200 ✅. Dhyan do ki sabse bada hissa (67200 J) pighalne wale segment ka hai, jahan temperature nahi badla.

**Example 4 (bhaap se paani, 100 °C par):** 100 g bhaap 100 °C par condense hokar 100 °C ke paani mein badalti hai. Kitni garmi nikalti hai?

100 g = **0.1 kg**. Condensation mein utni hi garmi nikalti hai jitni vaporisation mein lagi thi.

- Q = m L_v = 0.1 kg × 2260000 J/kg = **226000 J** (garmi nikalti hai)

**Jaanch:** 0.1 × 2260000 = 226000 ✅

**Example 5 (bhaap se 20 °C ke paani tak):** 1 kg bhaap 100 °C par thandi hokar 20 °C ka paani banti hai. Kul kitni garmi nikalti hai?

Do segments: pehle bhaap condense hoti hai (100 °C par), phir 100 °C ka paani 20 °C tak thanda hota hai (ΔT = 100 − 20 = 80 °C).

- Condensation: Q₁ = m L_v = 1 kg × 2260000 J/kg = **2260000 J**
- Paani ka thanda hona: Q₂ = m c_paani ΔT = 1 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × 80 °C = **336000 J**
- Kul Q = 2260000 J + 336000 J = **2596000 J**

**Jaanch:** 4200 × 80 = 336000, aur 2260000 + 336000 = 2596000 ✅. Condensation ki garmi hi kul ka lagbhag 87% hai (2260000 ÷ 2596000 ≈ 0.87).

**Example 6 (di gayi garmi se kitni barf pighli):** 0 °C ki barf par heater se 100800 J garmi di gayi, aur wo sab barf ke pighalne mein lagi. Kitni barf pighli?

- Q = m L_f, isliye m = Q ÷ L_f
- m = 100800 J ÷ 336000 J/kg = **0.3 kg** (yaani 300 g)

**Jaanch:** 0.3 kg × 336000 J/kg = 100800 J ✅

**Example 7 (mixture: kitni barf chahiye?):** 0.5 kg paani 30 °C par hai. Use thanda karke 0 °C tak laane ke liye 0 °C ki kitni barf chahiye? (Barf poori pighal jaati hai aur paani 0 °C par hi ruk jaata hai. Garmi ka koi nuksaan nahi.)

Principle of mixtures: paani ki di hui garmi = barf ki li hui garmi.

- Paani ki di hui garmi = m c ΔT = 0.5 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × 30 °C = **63000 J**
- Barf ki li hui garmi (pighalne mein) = m_barf × L_f = m_barf × 336000 J/kg
- Barabar karo: m_barf × 336000 J/kg = 63000 J
- m_barf = 63000 J ÷ 336000 J/kg = **0.1875 kg** (yaani 187.5 g)

**Jaanch:** 0.1875 kg × 336000 J/kg = 63000 J ✅. Aur 0.5 × 4200 × 30 = 63000 ✅. (Barf ko yahan Q = m c ΔT se nahi, **Q = m L_f** se liya, kyunki wo 0 °C par pighal rahi hai.)

**Example 8 (heater kitni der chalega?):** Ek 2000 W ka electric heater 2 kg barf (0 °C) ko pighlata hai. Poori garmi barf ko jaati hai. Kitna time lagega? (Power aur energy ka sambandh [work-energy-and-power-part7.md](../work-energy-and-power/work-energy-and-power-part7.md) mein tha: P = E ÷ t, yaani t = Q ÷ P.)

- Garmi chahiye: Q = m L_f = 2 kg × 336000 J/kg = 672000 J (Example 1 se)
- Heater ki power P = 2000 W = 2000 J/s
- t = Q ÷ P = 672000 J ÷ 2000 J/s = **336 s** (lagbhag 5.6 minute)

**Jaanch:** 2000 J/s × 336 s = 672000 J ✅. 336 s ÷ 60 = 5.6 minute ✅

**Example 9 (measured data se L nikalna):** Ek experiment mein 0.1 kg barf (0 °C par) ko pighlane mein 33600 J garmi lagi. Barf ki specific latent heat of fusion kya hai?

- Q = m L, isliye L = Q ÷ m
- L = 33600 J ÷ 0.1 kg = **336000 J/kg** = 3.36 × 10⁵ J/kg

**Jaanch:** 0.1 kg × 336000 J/kg = 33600 J ✅. Ye wahi value hai jo humne shuru mein li thi.

**Example 10 (pasine ka thanda karne wala asar):** Garmi mein ek insaan ke sharir se 10 g pasina evaporate ho jaata hai. Pasine ke evaporate hone mein sharir se lagbhag kitni garmi nikal jaati hai? (Evaporation ke liye bhi lagbhag 2260 J/g maan lo.)

- m = 10 g
- Q ≈ 10 g × 2260 J/g = **22600 J** (lagbhag)

**Jaanch:** 10 × 2260 = 22600 ✅. Ye garmi sharir se li jaati hai, isiliye pasina sookhne par thanda lagta hai. Itni garmi 1 kg paani ka temperature lagbhag 5.4 °C badha sakti hai (22600 ÷ 4200 ≈ 5.4) ✅.

**Example 11 (bhaap se jalna zyada kharab kyun?):** 1 g bhaap 100 °C par tumhari skin par lagti hai aur 1 g garam paani bhi 100 °C par lagta hai. Dono skin ko garmi dekar (skin ke temperature tak) thande hote hain. Sirf itna dekho: 1 g garam paani 100 °C se 0 °C tak thanda hone mein kitni garmi deta hai, aur 1 g bhaap sirf condense hokar 100 °C ka paani banne mein kitni garmi deti hai?

Yahan seedha J/g mein hisaab karte hain: c_paani = 4.2 J/g °C aur L_v = 2260 J/g.

- 1 g **garam paani** 100 °C → 0 °C: Q = 1 g × 4.2 J/g °C × 100 °C = **420 J**
- 1 g **bhaap** (100 °C) condense hokar 100 °C ka paani bane: Q = 1 g × 2260 J/g = **2260 J**
- 2260 J ÷ 420 J ≈ **5.4 guna**: sirf condensation ki garmi hi paani ke poore 100 °C se 0 °C tak thanda hone ki garmi se lagbhag 5.4 guna hai.

**Jaanch:** 4.2 × 100 = 420, aur 2260 ÷ 420 ≈ 5.38 ✅. Isi wajah se bhaap se jalna garam paani se jalne se kaafi zyada gambheer hota hai (aage Part G mein).

**Example 12 (kahani ka hisaab: 50 kg ki silli):** Mr. Dar ki 50 kg ki barf ki silli 0 °C par hai. (a) Use poori pighalne mein kitni garmi lagegi? (b) Agar dhoop se silli ko lagbhag 5000 W (5000 J/s) ki garmi mil rahi ho, to poori pighalne mein kitna time lagega?

- (a) Q = m L_f = 50 kg × 336000 J/kg = 16800000 J = **1.68 × 10⁷ J**
- (b) t = Q ÷ P = 16800000 J ÷ 5000 J/s = **3360 s** = 3360 ÷ 60 = **56 minute**

**Jaanch:** 50 × 336000 = 16800000 ✅; 5000 × 3360 = 16800000 ✅. Ye poori garmi paani ke particles ke hold ko dheela karne mein gayi, isiliye thermometer 56 minute tak 0 °C par hi raha.

## Part G: Roz ki zindagi mein latent heat

### 1. Bhaap se jalna garam paani se zyada kharab kyun?

100 °C ka paani aur 100 °C ki bhaap ka temperature to same hai, par bhaap ke andar paani ke mukabale **2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg ki extra chhupi hui garmi** hai. Jab bhaap tumhari skin par lagti hai, to wo condense hoti hai aur ye saari garmi skin ko de deti hai. Example 11 ka hisaab: sirf 1 g ke liye, bhaap se **2260 J** (condensation) aur garam paani se **420 J** (100 °C se 0 °C tak thanda hote hue). Isliye bhaap se jalna zyada gambheer hota hai.

### 2. Barf ka thanda asar paani se zyada kyun?

Maan lo tumhare paas **100 g barf 0 °C par** aur **100 g paani 0 °C par** hai. Dono ko ek garam drink mein daalte ho, aur dono ko 10 °C tak pahunchna hai (m = 0.1 kg).

- **0 °C ka paani** drink se sirf garmi leta hai 0 °C se 10 °C tak: Q = 0.1 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × 10 °C = **4200 J**
- **0 °C ki barf** pehle pighalti hai: Q = 0.1 kg × 336000 J/kg = **33600 J**, phir 0 °C se 10 °C tak garm hoti hai: 0.1 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × 10 °C = **4200 J**. Kul = 33600 J + 4200 J = **37800 J**

**Jaanch:** 33600 + 4200 = 37800 ✅. Barf drink se 37800 J le leti hai, jabki wahi temperature ka paani sirf 4200 J. Yaani barf lagbhag **9 guna** zyada thanda karti hai (37800 ÷ 4200 = 9). Isiliye **0 °C ki barf, 0 °C ke paani se behtar drink thanda karti hai**: barf pighalte waqt L_f ki garmi bhi leti hai.

### 3. Evaporation aur boiling: dono ek nahi hain

| Baat | Evaporation | Boiling |
|---|---|---|
| Kab hota hai | **Har temperature** par | Sirf **ek fixed temperature** (boiling point) par |
| Kahan se | Sirf **surface** se | **Poore liquid** mein (andar se bhi bulbule) |
| Raftaar | Dheema | Tez |
| Asar | Bacha hua liquid **thanda** hota hai | Temperature fixed rehta hai (garmi chalti rahe to bhi) |
| Example | Geele kapde sookhna, pasina sookhna | Gas par chadha ubalta paani |

**Evaporation se thandak:** jab surface ke tez chalte particles liquid chhodkar bhaap ban jaate hain, wo apne saath garmi le jaate hain, aur baaki liquid **thanda** ho jaata hai. Isi wajah se:
- **Pasina** sookhne par thanda lagta hai (Example 10: 10 g pasine se lagbhag 22600 J nikal gaya).
- Bukhaar mein **maathe par geele kapde ki patti** rakhte hain: kapde ka paani evaporate hokar garmi kheenchta hai.
- **Matka (mitti ka ghada)** mein paani thanda rehta hai: ghade ki deewar ke chhote chhedon se paani bahar aakar evaporate hota hai aur andar ke paani ko thanda karta hai.

**Evaporation ki raftaar kin cheezon par depend karti hai?** (Sirf naam yaad rakho.)
- **Surface area:** jitna zyada phaila hua, utna tez (thali ka paani katori se jaldi sookhta hai).
- **Temperature:** jitna garam, utna tez.
- **Humidity (hawa mein nami):** hawa mein nami zyada ho to evaporation dheema (barsaat mein kapde der se sookhte hain).
- **Wind (hawa ka chalna):** hawa tez ho to evaporation tez (isiliye kapde khule mein, hawa mein jaldi sookhte hain).

### 4. Pressure ka boiling point par asar (sirf naam)

Paani ka boiling point 100 °C hamesha nahi rehta; ye **pressure** par depend karta hai. Pressure chapter mein ([pressure-and-fluids-part4.md](../pressure-and-fluids/pressure-and-fluids-part4.md)) ek note tha ki "Bhaap ka asar Heat chapter mein". Wo ye hai:
- **Pressure cooker** mein andar ka pressure zyada ho jaata hai, to paani **100 °C se zyada** par ubalta hai. Zyada garam paani mein daal-chawal **jaldi** pakte hain.
- **Hill station (oonche pahad)** par hawa ka pressure kam hota hai, to paani **100 °C se kam** par hi ubalne lagta hai. Is kam temperature par khana **der se** pakta hai.

Poori detail aage ki classes mein seekhoge. Abhi sirf itna yaad rakho: pressure badhe to boiling point badhta hai, pressure ghate to boiling point ghatta hai.

### 5. Ashuddhi (impurity) aur melting point

Pure barf 0 °C par pighalti (aur paani 0 °C par jamta) hai. Agar paani mein **namak** jaisi koi cheez mili ho, to uska freezing point **0 °C se neeche** chala jaata hai. Isiliye sardi mein barf jami sadakon par **namak daala jaata hai**: namak barf ke saath milkar freezing point ko neeche kar deta hai, aur barf 0 °C se kam temperature par bhi pighal jaati hai. (Ice-cream wale bhi barf mein namak milate hain, taaki mixture 0 °C se bhi zyada thanda ban jaaye.)

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Barf pighalte waqt temperature badhta rehta hai"**

Nahi. Pighalte waqt temperature **0 °C par fixed** rehta hai, jab tak poori barf paani na ban jaaye. Garmi state badalne mein lag rahi hai (Example 12 mein 56 minute tak thermometer 0 °C par hi tha).

**Galti 2: Barf se paani banane ke sawal mein latent heat bhool jaana**

Example 3 mein agar sirf Q = m c ΔT jodte (4200 J + 16800 J = 21000 J), to jawab 21000 J aata. Sahi jawab **88200 J** hai, kyunki beech ka pighalne wala 67200 J chhoot gaya tha. Jab bhi sawal mein barf se paani, ya paani se bhaap ban raha ho, to **latent heat zaroor jodo**.

**Galti 3: State badalte waqt Q = m c ΔT lagana**

State badalte waqt ΔT = 0 hota hai, to m c ΔT = 0 aata, jo galat hai (garmi to lag rahi hai!). Us segment mein **Q = m L** lagao. Q = m c ΔT sirf tab, jab temperature sach mein badal raha ho.

**Galti 4: L_f aur L_v ko aapas mein mila dena**

L_f (pighalne ki, 3.36 × 10⁵ J/kg) aur L_v (bhaap banne ki, 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg) alag hain. Pighalne ke sawal mein L_f aur ubalne/bhaap ke sawal mein L_v. Example 2 mein agar L_f le lete to 0.5 × 336000 = 168000 J aata, jo 1130000 J se bahut kam, galat jawab hai.

**Galti 5: g aur kg ko mila dena**

Latent heat J/kg mein hai. 200 g ko **0.2 kg** karna zaroori hai. Agar 200 ko seedha 336000 se multiply kar do, to jawab 1000 guna bada aayega (200 × 336000 = 67200000 J, jabki sahi 67200 J hai).

**Galti 6: "Evaporation aur boiling ek hi cheez hain"**

Nahi. Evaporation har temperature par, sirf surface se, aur dheere hoti hai, aur thandak deti hai. Boiling ek fixed temperature par, poore liquid mein, aur tez hoti hai.

## Quick Recap

- Matter ki teen states: **solid** (fixed jagah, paas), **liquid** (paas par phisalte), **gas** (door aur azaad).
- State badalna: melting (solid→liquid), freezing (liquid→solid), vaporisation (liquid→gas, boiling ya evaporation), condensation (gas→liquid), sublimation (solid→gas seedha: kapoor, naphthalene, dry ice).
- Melting point (barf: 0 °C) aur boiling point (paani: 100 °C) fixed hote hain.
- State badalte waqt **temperature constant** rehta hai, chahe garmi di ya nikaali jaaye. Garmi particles ke hold ko dheela karne mein lagti hai aur **latent heat (chhupi hui garmi)** ke roop mein store hoti hai.
- **Q = m L** (state badalne mein): L_f(barf) = 3.36 × 10⁵ J/kg, L_v(paani) = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg (approximate). Purani values 80 cal/g aur 540 cal/g (1 cal = 4.2 J) inhi se match karti hain.
- Heating curve (1 kg barf −10 °C → bhaap 110 °C): 21000 J + 336000 J + 420000 J + 2260000 J + 20000 J = **3057000 J**. B (0 °C) aur D (100 °C) flat hain.
- Bhaap se jalna zyada gambheer: 1 g bhaap condense hoke 2260 J deti hai, jabki 1 g garam paani 100 °C se 0 °C tak sirf 420 J.
- 0 °C ki barf, 0 °C ke paani se zyada thanda karti hai, kyunki wo L_f ki garmi bhi leti hai.
- Evaporation: har temperature par, surface se, thandak deti hai (pasina, matka). Surface area, temperature, humidity aur wind us par asar karte hain. Boiling: fixed temperature par, poore liquid mein.
- Pressure zyada: boiling point zyada (pressure cooker). Pressure kam: boiling point kam (hill station). Impurity (namak) freezing point ko neeche karti hai.
- Mr. Dar ki 50 kg ki silli ko poori pighalne mein 50 × 3.36 × 10⁵ = 1.68 × 10⁷ J lagti hai: wahi garmi jo thermometer par dikhi nahi.

## Practice Questions

1. Name the change of state in each case: (a) ice becoming water, (b) water vapour turning into water droplets on a cold glass, (c) camphor slowly disappearing in an open box, (d) water turning into ice in a freezer.

2. Calculate the heat needed to melt 3 kg of ice at 0 °C. Use L_f = 3.36 × 10⁵ J/kg.

3. Calculate the heat needed to change 0.4 kg of water at 100 °C into steam at 100 °C. Use L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg.

4. How much heat is needed to change 500 g of ice at 0 °C into water at 30 °C? Use L_f = 3.36 × 10⁵ J/kg and c(water) = 4200 J/kg °C.

5. How much heat is released when 0.2 kg of steam at 100 °C changes into water at 50 °C? Use L_v = 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg and c(water) = 4200 J/kg °C.

6. A heater supplies 168000 J of heat to ice at 0 °C and all of it is used in melting the ice. What mass of ice melts?

7. A 500 W heater is used to melt 0.25 kg of ice at 0 °C. If all the heat goes into the ice, how long will it take? (Time = heat ÷ power.)

8. In an experiment, 0.05 kg of a solid at its melting point needs 15000 J of heat to become liquid at the same temperature. Find its specific latent heat of fusion.

9. How much ice at 0 °C is needed to cool 1 kg of water from 20 °C to 0 °C? Assume no heat is lost to the surroundings.

10. State True or False, and correct the false ones:
    (a) The temperature of melting ice keeps rising as heat is supplied.
    (b) Evaporation takes place only at the boiling point.
    (c) Steam at 100 °C can burn more than boiling water at 100 °C because it gives out latent heat on condensing.
    (d) During the change of state, Q = m c ΔT is used.

### Answers

1. (a) **Melting (fusion)**. (b) **Condensation**. (c) **Sublimation**. (d) **Freezing (solidification)**.

2. Q = m L_f = 3 kg × 336000 J/kg = **1008000 J** (1.008 × 10⁶ J).
   Jaanch: 3 × 336000 = 1008000 ✅

3. Q = m L_v = 0.4 kg × 2260000 J/kg = **904000 J**.
   Jaanch: 0.4 × 2260000 = 904000 ✅

4. 500 g = 0.5 kg. Pehle pighlana: Q₁ = 0.5 kg × 336000 J/kg = 168000 J. Phir 0 °C se 30 °C: Q₂ = 0.5 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × 30 °C = 63000 J. Kul Q = 168000 J + 63000 J = **231000 J**.
   Jaanch: 168000 + 63000 = 231000 ✅

5. Condensation: Q₁ = 0.2 kg × 2260000 J/kg = 452000 J. Phir 100 °C se 50 °C (ΔT = 50 °C): Q₂ = 0.2 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × 50 °C = 42000 J. Kul = 452000 J + 42000 J = **494000 J** (nikalti hai).
   Jaanch: 452000 + 42000 = 494000 ✅

6. m = Q ÷ L_f = 168000 J ÷ 336000 J/kg = **0.5 kg**.
   Jaanch: 0.5 kg × 336000 J/kg = 168000 J ✅

7. Garmi chahiye: Q = 0.25 kg × 336000 J/kg = 84000 J. t = Q ÷ P = 84000 J ÷ 500 J/s = **168 s** (2.8 minute).
   Jaanch: 500 J/s × 168 s = 84000 J ✅

8. L = Q ÷ m = 15000 J ÷ 0.05 kg = **300000 J/kg** (3 × 10⁵ J/kg).
   Jaanch: 0.05 kg × 300000 J/kg = 15000 J ✅

9. Paani ki di hui garmi = 1 kg × 4200 J/kg °C × 20 °C = 84000 J. Barf ki li hui garmi = m × 336000 J/kg. Barabar karo: m = 84000 J ÷ 336000 J/kg = **0.25 kg** (250 g).
   Jaanch: 0.25 kg × 336000 J/kg = 84000 J ✅

10. (a) **False** ❌. Pighalte waqt temperature 0 °C par constant rehta hai. (b) **False** ❌. Evaporation har temperature par hota hai (surface se); boiling fixed temperature par hoti hai. (c) **True** ✅. Bhaap condense hone par 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg ki extra garmi deti hai. (d) **False** ❌. State badalte waqt temperature nahi badalta, isliye **Q = m L** use hota hai, Q = m c ΔT nahi.
