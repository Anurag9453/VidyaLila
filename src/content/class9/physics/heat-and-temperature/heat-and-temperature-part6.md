# Topic 6: Heat Transfer: Conduction, Convection aur Radiation

Pichle topic mein humne dekha ki jab koi cheez garmi leti hai, to uska temperature badhta hai, ya wo pighalti aur ubalti hai (change of state), aur har step par Q = m c ΔT ya Q = m L se hisaab lagta hai. Is chapter ke shuru mein hi hum ye bhi jaan chuke hain ki **heat hamesha garam jagah se thandi jagah ki taraf behti hai** (dekho [heat-and-temperature.md](heat-and-temperature.md)).

Par ek sawaal abhi baaki hai: **wo behti kaise hai?** Chulhe ki aanch se pan garam ho jaata hai, jabki pan ko chulhe ne chhua bhi nahi hota ho. Dhoop mein rakhi cheez bhi garam ho jaati hai, jabki Sun aur Earth ke beech lakhon kilometre ka khaali (empty) space hai. Aur heater ke paas baithne par garmi mehsoos hoti hai, chahe aap heater ko chhoo na rahe ho. Is topic mein hum heat ke **teen raaste** seekhenge: conduction, convection aur radiation. Ye chapter ka aakhri topic hai, to end mein poore chapter ka wrap-up bhi hoga.

## Thermos flask ka raaz

Ms. Bhatia ek thermos flask (vacuum flask) banane wali company mein designer hain. Ek Sunday ko unka padosi ladka Hemant unse milne aaya. Table par ek steel ki flask rakhi thi. Ms. Bhatia ne subah 6 baje usmein garam chai bhari thi. Ab dopahar ke 12 baj rahe the.

Hemant ne ghadi dekhi aur hairaan hokar kaha, "Aunty, chai ko bhare hue 6 ghante ho gaye. Ghar par to cup mein rakhi chai 15-20 minute mein thandi ho jaati hai. Is flask mein aisa kya hai ki ye itni der tak garam rehti hai?"

Ms. Bhatia ne flask ka dhakkan khola aur Hemant ke haath mein ek cup ke andar garam chai daal di. Bhaap nikal rahi thi. "Dekho Hemant, chai ki garmi hamesha bahar bhaagne ki koshish karti hai, kyunki heat garam se thande ki taraf behti hai. Cup mein ye garmi **teen raaston** se bahar nikalti hai. Meri flask ko banate waqt hum teeno raaste band kar dete hain."

"Teen raaste? Kaun se?" Hemant ne poocha.

"Chalo, ek ek karke dekhte hain. Aakhir mein hum flask ko khol kar uske har hisse ka matlab samjhenge, aur tumhara sawaal apne aap hal ho jaayega."

## Part A: Conduction: cheez ke andar garmi ka safar

### Rod ko ek siray se garam karna

Ek lambi dhaatu ki rod lo. Uske upar mom (wax) ki chhoti chhoti boondon se chaar paanch pins chipka do, barabar doori par. Rod ka ek siraa mombatti ya burner se garam karo.

```
  burner          pin 1    pin 2    pin 3    pin 4
    ^               v        v        v        v
 (  aag  ) ====[== o =======o========o========o==]   thanda sira
                  wax      wax      wax      wax

  Pehle pin 1 girta hai, phir pin 2, phir pin 3 ...
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Dhaatu ki rod ka ek sira garam karne par wax pighalti jaati hai, aur pins garam sire ke paas se shuru hokar ek ek karke girte hain. Matlab garmi rod ke andar garam sire se thande sire ki taraf badh rahi hai.

Dekhne wali baat ye hai ki **pehle garam sire ke paas wala pin girta hai, phir agla, phir agla.** Garmi rod mein ek jagah se doosri jagah ki taraf chal rahi hai, par rod ka koi hissa apni jagah se hila nahi.

### Ye kaise hota hai?

Rod ke andar bahut chhote chhote particles hote hain jo apni jagah par kaanpte (vibrate) rehte hain. Garam sire ke particles tez kaanpne lagte hain. Ye tez kaanpte particles apne paas wale thande particles ko dhakka dete hain, aur wo bhi tez kaanpne lagte hain. Phir wo apne agle paas wale ko. Is tarah **garmi particle se particle ko milti jaati hai, aur particles khud apni jagah par hi rehte hain.**

Jaise ek line mein khade bacchon mein se pehla bachcha doosre ko dhakka de, doosra teesre ko: dhakka aage chala jaata hai, par koi bachcha apni jagah chhodkar nahi bhaagta.

**Conduction: ek hi cheez (mainly solid) ke andar, bina particles ke apni jagah chhode, garam sire se thande sire tak heat ka transfer.**

Roz ka example: garam chai mein dala steel ka chamach kuch der mein upar se bhi garam ho jaata hai. Garmi chamach ke andar se hokar upar tak chadh aati hai.

### Conductor aur insulator

Sab cheezein garmi ko ek jaisa raasta nahi deti.

- **Conductor (good conductor):** jisse heat tezi se guzarti hai. Saari dhaatuein (metals) achhi conductor hain.
- **Insulator (bad conductor):** jisse heat bahut dheere guzarti hai.

| Cheez | Heat conduct karti hai? | Roz ka use |
|---|---|---|
| Silver (chaandi) | Bahut achhi | Gehne, kuch special bartan |
| Copper (taamba) | Bahut achhi | Kuch bartanon ka neeche ka hissa, taar |
| Aluminium | Achhi | Cooker, kadhai, tava |
| Iron (lohaa) | Achhi | Tava, lohe ki tray |
| Glass | Kamzor | Khidki, cup |
| Lakdi | Bahut kamzor | Pan ke handle, bench |
| Plastic | Bahut kamzor | Cooker ka handle |
| Thermocole | Bahut kamzor | Ice-cream ke box |
| Oon (wool) | Bahut kamzor | Sweater, shawl |
| Hawa (air) | Bahut kamzor | Sweater ke andar phansi hawa |

Dhyan do: hawa khud ek bahut achhi **insulator** hai (agar wo ek jagah ruki rahe). Isi baat ka istemaal karke hum kai chalaaki ke kaam karte hain.

### Roz ki zindagi mein conductor aur insulator

1. **Bartan:** pan, kadhai, tava dhaatu (aluminium, copper, steel) ke bante hain taaki aag ki garmi andar khaane tak tezi se pahunche. Par unke **handle lakdi ya plastic** ke hote hain, taaki garmi haath tak na pahunche.
2. **Lohe ki tray thandi kyun lagti hai?** Sardiyon mein lohe ki tray aur lakdi ki bench ek hi kamre mein rakhi hain, to dono ka temperature kamre ke barabar hai. Phir bhi haath lagane par lohe ki tray zyada thandi lagti hai. Wajah: iron **conductor** hai: wo hamare haath ki garmi ko tezi se kheench leta hai, aur humein lagta hai "kitna thanda". Lakdi insulator hai: wo garmi dheere dheere kheenchti hai, isliye halki garam lagti hai.
3. **Oon ke kapde:** sardiyon mein sweater pehnne se garmi isliye milti hai ki oon ke reshon (fibres) ke beech bahut saari **hawa phansi** rehti hai, jo ek insulator hai. Wo hamare badan ki garmi ko bahar nahi jaane deti. Isi tarah quilt (razai) mein bhi hawa phansi hoti hai.
4. **Double wall aur thermocole box:** thermocole ke andar ke chhote chhote bulbule mein hawa band hoti hai. Isliye thermocole ke box mein ice-cream ya garam khaana der tak waisa hi rehta hai.
5. **Bijli ke taar** conductor (copper, aluminium) ke bane hote hain, par upar se plastic (insulator) ka cover chadha hota hai, taaki chhoone par current ya garmi haath tak na pahunche. (Bijli ka poora chapter aage ki class mein hai.)

### Liquids aur gases bhi (bahut) kamzor conductor hain

Dhaatu ki rod mein conduction aasaan dikhta hai. Par **liquid aur gas mein conduction bahut kam hota hai**, kyunki unke particles solid ki tarah kas kar jude nahi hote (gas mein to bahut door door hote hain), to wo ek doosre ko garmi ka dhakka theek se aage nahi de paate. (Mercury jaisi liquid dhaatu ek apwaad hai.)

**Ek mazedaar demonstration:** Ek test tube mein paani lo aur uske bilkul neeche barf ka ek tukda dabao (barf ko upar uthne se rokne ke liye wire gauze ya kisi jaali se bandh do, taaki wo tairne na paaye). Ab test tube ko tirchha pakadkar sirf **upar ke paani ko burner se garam karo**.

```
          │     │   (upar se khula)
          │ ° ° │  <- upar ka paani ubal raha hai (bulbule)
  aag ──> │ ~~~ │  <- burner ki aanch sirf upar wale hisse par
          │ ~~~ │
          │ ~~~ │  <- beech ka paani
          │#####│  <- wire gauze (barf ko upar nahi aane deti)
          │ ice │  <- neeche: barf, pighli nahi
          ╰─────╯
```

> **Figure 6.2** – Test tube ka upar ka paani ubalne lagta hai, par neeche rakha barf pighalta nahi. Matlab paani ek bahut kamzor conductor hai: garmi upar se neeche tak nahi pahunchi.

Kuch der baad **upar ka paani ubalne lagega, par neeche ka barf waisa hi rahega.** Agar paani achha conductor hota, to garmi neeche pahunch kar barf pighla deti. Isse saaf hai ki paani bahut kamzor conductor hai. Hawa bhi aisi hi hai.

Phir liquid aur gas mein garmi kaise failti hai? Agle part mein dekhte hain.

## Part B: Convection: garam fluid khud chal kar garmi le jaata hai

**Fluid** wo cheez hai jo behti hai: yaani **liquid aur gas**. In dono mein particles aasaani se idhar udhar ja sakte hain. Isliye inme garmi ek naye tareeke se pahunchti hai.

### Convection ka idea

Paani ke pateele ko neeche se garam karo. Neeche wala paani burner se garmi leta hai. Topic 2 mein dekha tha ki garam hone par liquid **phailta (expand) hai**, yaani wahi paani ki matra ab zyada jagah gherti hai. Phailne se uski **density kam** ho jaati hai (dekho [pressure-and-fluids-part7.md](../pressure-and-fluids/pressure-and-fluids-part7.md) mein density aur floating).

Kam density wala garam paani **halka** ho jaata hai, aur jaise lakdi paani mein upar tairti hai, waise hi wo **upar uth jaata hai**. Upar wala thanda paani zyada density wala hota hai, isliye wo **neeche doob jaata hai** aur garam paani ki jagah le leta hai. Phir wo bhi neeche garam hota hai, halka hokar upar uthta hai, aur ye chakkar chalta rehta hai.

Is ghoomte hue chakkar ko **convection current** kehte hain.

**Convection: liquid ya gas mein, garam hue fluid ke khud ek jagah se doosri jagah jaane se (particles ke saath) garmi ka transfer.**

Conduction aur convection ka sabse bada farak: conduction mein **particles apni jagah par rehte hain**, convection mein **particles khud chalte hain aur garmi saath le jaate hain.**

### Demonstration 1: potassium permanganate ka crystal

Ek gol flask mein paani lo. Uske bilkul neeche ek chhota sa potassium permanganate (KMnO₄, ek gehre baingani rang ka crystal) ka dana ek taraf rakh do. Ab flask ko us dane ke theek neeche halka sa garam karo.

```
        ___________
       /           \
      │  ^  ~~  v   │   garam rang wala paani upar uthta hai
      │  ^      v   │   upar se phir ek taraf neeche aata hai
      │  ^      v   │   thanda paani neeche doobta hai
      │  ^~~~~~ v   │
       \_[KMnO4]___/
            ^
          (aag)
```

> **Figure 6.3** – Flask ko KMnO₄ ke dane ke neeche garam karne par rang wala paani upar uthta hai, upar se ek taraf ghoomkar neeche aata hai, aur phir dobara garam hota hai: convection current ka chakkar saaf dikhta hai.

Rang ki dhaar pehle seedhi upar jaati hai (jahan garam paani uth raha hai), phir upar se ek taraf ghoomkar neeche aati hai (jahan thanda paani doob raha hai). Isse saaf **dikh jaata hai** ki paani ek chakkar mein ghoom raha hai.

### Demonstration 2: mombatti ke upar ka dhuan

Ek jalti mombatti ke paas agarbatti ka dhuan karo. Dhuan **seedha upar ki taraf** jaata hai, kyunki mombatti ke paas ki hawa garam hokar halki ho gayi hai aur upar uth rahi hai. Dhuan us hawa ke saath bah raha hai. Aur bagal se thandi hawa mombatti ki taraf aati hai.

### Demonstration 3: chimney

Chulhe ke upar ya factory mein chimney lagi hoti hai. Chulhe ki garam hawa halki hokar chimney se upar uthti hai aur dhuan ko bahar le jaati hai. Neeche se thandi taazi hawa chulhe tak aati rehti hai, jisse aag ko oxygen milta rehta hai. Chimney jitni oonchi, utni achhi draught.

### Convection ke examples

1. **Pateele mein ubalta paani:** upar bataya hua chakkar. Isiliye ubalte waqt poora paani ek saath garam ho jaata hai, sirf neeche wala nahi.

2. **Room heater ko floor ke paas rakhna:** heater ke paas ki hawa garam hokar upar uthti hai, aur upar wali thandi hawa neeche aati hai. Is chakkar se poora kamra garam ho jaata hai. Agar heater chhat ke paas lagaoge, to garam hawa chhat par hi rahegi aur neeche tak nahi aayegi.

3. **AC ko chhat ke paas lagana:** AC ki thandi hawa zyada density wali hai, isliye wo **neeche doobti hai**. Chhat ke paas lagane se thandi hawa neeche utarti hai, aur kamre ki garam (halki) hawa upar uthkar AC tak pahunchti hai, jahan wo phir thandi hoti hai. Isse poora kamra thanda ho jaata hai. (Heater neeche, AC upar: dono ke liye wahi baat, ulti disha mein.)

4. **Sea breeze (samudri hawa) aur land breeze:** Topic 3 mein dekha tha ki paani ki specific heat bahut zyada hai (4200 J/(kg °C)), aur zameen (ret, mitti) ki bahut kam. Isliye **dhoop mein zameen paani se zyada tezi se garam hoti hai**, aur raat ko zameen paani se zyada tezi se thandi bhi ho jaati hai.

```
  DIN MEIN (sea breeze)               RAAT MEIN (land breeze)

               garam hawa upar uthti   garam hawa upar uthti
                          ^               ^
 samundar  ──────────>  zameen         samundar  <──────────  zameen
 (thanda)   thandi hawa  (garam)       (garam)    thandi hawa  (thandi)
```

> **Figure 6.4** – Din mein zameen zyada garam hoti hai, isliye uske upar ki hawa uthti hai aur samundar se thandi hawa zameen ki taraf aati hai (sea breeze). Raat ko zameen jaldi thandi ho jaati hai, isliye samundar ke upar ki hawa uthti hai aur zameen se hawa samundar ki taraf jaati hai (land breeze).

   - **Din mein:** zameen zyada garam, uske upar ki hawa garam, halki, upar uthti hai. Khaali jagah bharne samundar ki thandi hawa zameen ki taraf aati hai: **sea breeze**.
   - **Raat mein:** zameen jaldi thandi ho jaati hai, par paani abhi bhi (comparatively) garam rehta hai. Ab samundar ke upar ki hawa garam hokar uthti hai, aur zameen ki thandi hawa samundar ki taraf jaati hai: **land breeze**.

5. **Hawa (wind):** bade paimane par yahi baat hai: dhoop se kuch jagah zyada garam hoti hain, wahan ki hawa uthti hai, aur doosri jagah ki thandi hawa uski jagah lene aati hai. Is hawa ki gati ko hum "wind" kehte hain.

6. **Hot-air balloon:** balloon ke andar ki hawa burner se garam hoti hai, phailti hai, aur uski density bahar ki hawa se kam ho jaati hai. Halka hone ke kaaran balloon upar uthta hai, bilkul jaise paani mein kam density wali cheez tairti hai.

7. **Geyser (water heater):** geyser mein heating element neeche hota hai. Garam paani halka hokar upar uthta hai aur upar ke paas se nikalta hai, aur thanda paani neeche se andar aata rehta hai. Isiliye geyser ka garam paani ka pipe upar ki taraf hota hai.

### Ek zaroori niyam

**Convection sirf liquid aur gas mein ho sakta hai.** Solid mein particles apni jagah se hil nahi sakte, isliye wahan convection nahi hota. **Vacuum mein bhi convection nahi ho sakta**, kyunki wahan koi particle hi nahi hai jo garmi ko saath le jaaye.

## Part C: Radiation: bina kisi medium ke garmi

### Sun ki garmi

Sun aur Earth ke beech lagbhag poora **khaali space (vacuum)** hai. Wahan na koi solid hai, na paani, na hawa. Fir bhi Sun ki garmi Earth tak pahunchti hai. To ye garmi:

- **conduction se nahi** aa sakti, kyunki conduction ke liye particles ki chain chahiye.
- **convection se nahi** aa sakti, kyunki convection ke liye liquid ya gas chahiye, jo vacuum mein nahi hai.

To teesra tareeka hai **radiation**: garmi **electromagnetic waves** (infrared ke roop mein) ke saath space mein chali aati hai. In waves ko chalne ke liye kisi medium ki zaroorat **nahi** hoti.

**Radiation: bina kisi medium ke, waves ke roop mein heat ka transfer.**

### Aag ke paas mehsoos hone wali garmi

Aag ke paas baithkar haath ko aag ki **side** mein (bagal mein) le jaao. Tumhe garmi mehsoos hogi. Ab sochte hain, ye garmi kaise aayi?

- **Convection se?** Nahi. Aag ke paas ki garam hawa **upar** uthti hai, bagal mein nahi aati.
- **Conduction se?** Nahi. Hawa bahut kamzor conductor hai.

Isliye bagal mein mehsoos hone wali garmi **radiation** hai: aag se seedhi infrared waves tumhare haath tak aa rahi hain.

**Har garam cheez radiation nikaalti hai:** garam chai, tumhara shareer, bulb ka filament, bhatti, sab. Cheez jitni garam, utni zyada radiation nikaalti hai (iska poora formula aage ki class mein aayega, abhi sirf itna yaad rakho).

### Kaali aur safed surface

Radiation ke saath surface ka **rang aur chamak** bhi bahut matter karte hain.

| Surface | Radiation sokhta hai (absorb) | Radiation nikaalta hai (emit) | Radiation laut-ata hai (reflect) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kaali, khurdari (dark, rough) | Zyada | Zyada | Kam |
| Safed ya chamakdaar (white, shiny) | Kam | Kam | Zyada |

Is baat ke roz ke examples:

1. **Sardiyon mein kaale ya gehre rang ke kapde:** kaala rang dhoop ki garmi zyada sokhta hai, isliye sardi mein achha lagta hai.
2. **Garmiyon mein safed ya halke rang ke kapde:** safed rang zyada dhoop wapas laut deta hai, isliye kam garam lagta hai.
3. **Ghar safed rang se rangna:** garmi mein ghar ki deewarein aur chhat kam garam hoti hain kyunki safed rang dhoop ko wapas reflect kar deta hai.
4. **Solar cooker ki kaali sateh:** solar cooker ke andar ki sateh aur bartan ko kaala rangte hain taaki wo zyada se zyada dhoop ki garmi sokh le.
5. **Chamakdaar teapot:** ek shiny (chamakdaar) teapot **kam radiation nikaalta hai**, isliye uski chai kaali, ghisi hui teapot ke muqable der tak garam rehti hai.
6. **Gaadi ke windscreen ke aage sunshade:** dhoop mein khadi gaadi mein chamakdaar sunshade (reflective sheet) lagate hain jo dhoop ko wapas laut deti hai, taaki gaadi ke andar kam garmi aaye.

**Greenhouse (sirf naam):** kaanch ke ghar (greenhouse) mein dhoop ki radiation andar aa jaati hai, par andar ki garmi kaanch se bahar nahi nikal paati. Isi idea par aage ki class mein "greenhouse effect" padhoge.

## Part D: Teeno tareeke ek table mein

| Baat | Conduction | Convection | Radiation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kis cheez mein hota hai | Mainly solids | Liquids aur gases | Kisi bhi mein, vacuum mein bhi |
| Medium chahiye? | Haan | Haan | **Nahi** |

| Raftaar | Dheemi | Medium (conduction se tez) | Sabse tez (roshni ki raftaar) |

Zyadatar asli situations mein **ek se zyada tareeke saath saath** hote hain. Jaise pan se chai ubalte waqt: pan ke neeche se paani tak **conduction**, paani ke andar **convection**, aur garam pan se thodi radiation.

## Part E: Thermos flask ke andar kya hai?

Ab hum Ms. Bhatia ki flask ko khol kar uske andar dekhte hain.

```
                    ┌─────────┐
                    │ stopper │                 <- cork/plastic (insulator)
     ┌──────────────┤         ├──────────────┐  <- outer casing
     │  ┌───────────┤         ├───────────┐  │  <- bahar ki glass deewar
     │  │ ░░░░░░░░░ │         │ ░░░░░░░░░ │  │
     │  │ ░ ┌───────┴─────────┴───────┐ ░ │  │  <- andar ki glass deewar
     │  │ ░ │                         │ ░ │  │
     │  │ ░ │      garam chai         │ ░ │  │
     │  │ ░ │                         │ ░ │  │
     │  │ ░ └─────────────────────────┘ ░ │  │
     │  │ ░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░ │  │  <- ░ = vacuum
     │  └─────────────────────────────────┘  │
     │        [ support (insulator) ]        │
     └───────────────────────────────────────┘
```

> **Figure 6.5** – Thermos flask ka ek saaf sa chitra: dugni glass ki deewar ke beech vacuum, dono deewaron par chamakdaar (silvered) parat, upar cork ya plastic ka stopper, aur bahar ki casing. Asli flask mein glass ki deewarein ek hi bottle ki tarah juri hoti hain.

Flask ke chaar mukhya hisse hain, aur har hissa ek ya ek se zyada tareeke ko rokta hai.

### 1. Dugni glass ki deewar aur beech mein vacuum

Flask ki andar ki bottle **do deewaron wali glass** ki hoti hai. Dono deewaron ke beech ki **saari hawa bahar nikaal di jaati hai**, yaani wahan vacuum hota hai.

- **Conduction ruk jaata hai:** conduction ke liye particles chahiye. Vacuum mein particle hi nahi hain, to garmi andar ki deewar se bahar ki deewar tak particle se particle nahi ja sakti. (Glass khud bhi ek kamzor conductor hai.)
- **Convection ruk jaata hai:** convection ke liye fluid chahiye. Vacuum mein koi hawa nahi, to garam hawa uthne ka chakkar bhi nahi ban sakta.

### 2. Chamakdaar (silvered) deewarein

Dono deewaron par vacuum ki taraf wali sateh par **chaandi jaisi chamakdaar parat** chadhi hoti hai.

- Vacuum mein se conduction aur convection ruk gaye, par **radiation phir bhi vacuum mein se guzar sakta hai.** Isko rokne ke liye hi chamakdaar parat hai.
- **Andar ki deewar** chai ki garmi (infrared radiation) ko wapas **andar reflect** kar deti hai. Isse garmi bahar nahi nikalti.
- **Bahar ki deewar** bahar ki radiation ko reflect kar deti hai. Isliye flask mein **thandi cheez bhi der tak thandi** rehti hai.

### 3. Cork ya plastic ka stopper

Flask ke muh par cork ya plastic ka dhakkan hota hai, jo ek **kamzor conductor (insulator)** hai. Isse:

- garmi stopper ke raaste conduction se nahi nikalti.
- garam hawa ya bhaap flask ke andar se bahar nahi jaa paati (convection aur evaporation dono ruk jaate hain).

### 4. Outer casing

Bahar ka steel ya plastic ka cover glass ko tootne se bachata hai. Neeche ek insulator ka support (cork ya rubber) hota hai taaki glass seedhe steel se na chhue. Is wajah se garmi conduction se bhi bahar nahi nikalti.

**Thodi si garmi phir bhi nikalti hai**, kyunki koi vacuum bilkul perfect nahi hota, aur stopper aur flask ke muh (neck) ke raaste thodi garmi conduction se jaati hai. Isliye kai ghanton baad chai thandi ho hi jaati hai, par bahut dheere.

## Part H: Solved examples

Hum paani ki specific heat **c = 4200 J/(kg °C)** lenge (Topic 3 jaisa).

### Example 1: Garam cup ki chai ne kitni garmi khoyi?

0.2 kg chai (paani jaisi) 90 °C se thandi hokar 80 °C ho gayi. Kitni heat nikli? (c = 4200 J/(kg °C))

ΔT = 90 °C − 80 °C = 10 °C

Q = m c ΔT = 0.2 kg × 4200 J/(kg °C) × 10 °C = **8400 J**

Jaanch: 0.2 × 4200 = 840, aur 840 × 10 = 8400 ✅ (aur 8400 J ÷ (0.2 kg × 4200) = 10 °C ✅)

### Example 2: Heat kho dene ki average dar (power)

Example 1 ki chai ko 10 °C thandi hone mein 10 minute lage. Heat kho dene ki average dar (W mein)?

t = 10 min = 10 × 60 s = 600 s

Average rate = Q ÷ t = 8400 J ÷ 600 s = **14 W**

Jaanch: 14 W × 600 s = 8400 J ✅

(Power ka matlab Work, Energy aur Power chapter mein dekha tha: 1 W = 1 J/s. Yahan cup har second **14 J** garmi bahar bhej raha tha.)

### Example 3: Kaala aur safed dabba dhoop mein

Do ek jaise dabbe hain, har ek mein 2 kg paani hai. Ek ko kaala rang kiya, doosre ko safed. Dono ko ek hi tarah ki dhoop mein barabar time ke liye rakha. Kaale dabbe ka paani 18 °C garam hua, safed ka 8 °C. Dono ne kitni garmi li? Unka ratio kya hai? (c = 4200 J/(kg °C))

Kaala dabba: Q = m c ΔT = 2 kg × 4200 J/(kg °C) × 18 °C = **151,200 J**

Safed dabba: Q = 2 kg × 4200 J/(kg °C) × 8 °C = **67,200 J**

Ratio = 151,200 J ÷ 67,200 J = **2.25**

Jaanch: 2 × 4200 = 8400, 8400 × 18 = 151,200 ✅ aur 8400 × 8 = 67,200 ✅, 67,200 × 2.25 = 151,200 ✅

Matlab: kaale dabbe ne safed dabbe se **2.25 guna** zyada garmi sokhi. Ye idea kaali sateh ko zyada absorb karne ka asar dikhata hai.

### Example 4: Dhoop se milne wali average dar

Example 3 mein dhoop ka time 1 ghanta (3600 s) tha. Dono dabbon ne average kitni heat power li?

Kaala: P = 151,200 J ÷ 3600 s = **42 W**

Safed: P = 67,200 J ÷ 3600 s = 18.67 W ≈ **18.7 W**

Jaanch: 42 W × 3600 s = 151,200 J ✅, 18.67 W × 3600 s ≈ 67,200 J ✅

### Example 5: Flask ne kitni garmi khoyi?

0.5 kg chai flask mein 2 ghante (7200 s) mein sirf 3 °C thandi hui. Kitni heat nikli aur average dar kitni rahi? (c = 4200 J/(kg °C))

Q = m c ΔT = 0.5 kg × 4200 J/(kg °C) × 3 °C = **6300 J**

t = 2 h = 2 × 3600 s = 7200 s

P = Q ÷ t = 6300 J ÷ 7200 s = **0.875 W**

Jaanch: 0.875 W × 7200 s = 6300 J ✅

### Example 6: Khule cup ki tulna flask se

Wahi 0.5 kg chai ek **khule cup** mein 2 ghante mein 25 °C thandi ho gayi. Heat aur average dar? Flask se ratio?

Q = 0.5 kg × 4200 J/(kg °C) × 25 °C = **52,500 J**

P = 52,500 J ÷ 7200 s = 7.29 W ≈ **7.3 W**

Ratio = 52,500 J ÷ 6300 J = **8.33** (ya 7.29 W ÷ 0.875 W = 8.33)

Jaanch: 0.5 × 4200 = 2100, 2100 × 25 = 52,500 ✅; 52,500 ÷ 7200 = 7.2917 ✅; 6300 × 8.33 ≈ 52,500 ✅

Matlab: khula cup flask ke muqable lagbhag **8.3 guna** zyada garmi kho raha tha. Yahi flask ka kamaal hai.

### Example 7: Flask ki chai ko 25 °C girne mein kitna time lagega?

Example 5 ki average dar (0.875 W) maanke, flask mein 0.5 kg chai ko 25 °C girne mein lagbhag kitna time lagega?

25 °C ki heat = 52,500 J (Example 6)

t = Q ÷ P = 52,500 J ÷ 0.875 W = 60,000 s

60,000 s ÷ 3600 s/h = **16.67 ghante** (lagbhag)

Jaanch: 0.875 W × 60,000 s = 52,500 J ✅. Dusre tareeke se: 3 °C ke liye 2 h, to 25 °C ke liye (25 ÷ 3) × 2 h = 16.67 h ✅

Ye sirf andaaza hai (asal mein jaise jaise chai thandi hoti hai garmi ka bahar nikalna dheere hota jaata hai), par isse saaf hai ki flask ki chai poore din tak garam rah sakti hai, jabki khule cup ki chai kuch ghanton mein hi thandi ho jaati hai.

### Example 8: 12 situations mein kaun sa tareeka?

Neeche har situation mein **main** tareeka likha hai. Tareeka kaise pehchanein: pooche **kya particles apni jagah par hain (conduction), ya fluid chal raha hai (convection), ya beech mein koi medium hi nahi hai (radiation)?**

| Situation | Tareeka | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Steel ke chamach ka upar wala sira garam hona | Conduction | Solid ke andar particle se particle |
| 2. Pateele ke paani ka poora garam hona | Convection | Garam paani uthta hai, thanda doobta hai |
| 3. Sun ki garmi Earth tak aana | Radiation | Beech mein vacuum hai |
| 4. Aag ke bagal mein haath garam hona | Radiation | Hawa kamzor conductor, aur garam hawa upar jaati hai |
| 5. AC ko chhat ke paas lagana | Convection | Thandi hawa neeche doobti hai |
| 6. Pan ke lakdi ke handle ka garam na hona | Conduction rokna (insulator) | Lakdi kamzor conductor hai |
| 7. Sea breeze | Convection | Zameen ki garam hawa uthti hai, samundar ki thandi hawa aati hai |
| 8. Safed kapde garmi mein kam garam lagna | Radiation (reflection) | Safed rang radiation zyada wapas laut ata hai |
| 9. Hot-air balloon ka uthna | Convection (garam hawa halki) | Garam hawa ki density kam |

| 11. Thermos ka vacuum | Conduction aur convection rokna | Vacuum mein koi particle nahi |

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Garmi upar uthti hai"**
Garmi (heat) upar nahi uthti: **garam fluid** upar uthta hai, kyunki wo halka (kam density wala) hota hai. Aur ye sirf convection mein hota hai. Radiation har disha mein jaati hai, aur conduction solid mein kisi bhi disha mein (hot se cold ki taraf).

**Galti 2: "Lohe ki tray ka temperature lakdi se kam hai"**
Dono ka temperature kamre jitna hi hai. Lohe ki tray sirf zyada **tezi se** haath ki garmi kheenchti hai, isliye zyada thandi **lagti** hai. Agar thermometer se naapo to dono ek jaise (kamre ke temperature par) milenge.

**Galti 3: "Radiation ko hawa chahiye"**
Radiation ko kisi medium ki zaroorat nahi. Isi wajah se Sun ki garmi vacuum se guzar kar aati hai.

**Galti 4: "Oon garam hoti hai"**
Oon khud garmi nahi deti: wo hamare shareer ki garmi ko bahar jaane se **rokti** hai, kyunki uske reshon ke beech hawa phansi hoti hai (jo insulator hai). Shareer hi garmi paida karta hai, oon sirf use bachati hai.

**Galti 5: "Safed kapda hamesha thanda karta hai"**
Safed kapda zyada dhoop **wapas laut deta hai**, to dhoop mein kam garam hota hai. Par agar tumhara shareer garmi bahar nikaal raha ho aur kapda badan ke paas ho, to wo thodi garmi rokta bhi hai. Dhoop mein khaas taur par halke rang ke kapde achhe rehte hain.

**Galti 6: Kaale aur safed ko ulta kar dena**
Kaala rang radiation zyada **sokhta hai (aur nikaalta bhi zyada hai)**, safed zyada **laut deta hai**. Isliye solar cooker kaala, par ghar ki chhat (garmi mein) safed.

**Galti 7: Convection ko solid mein maan lena**
Solid mein particles apni jagah chhod hi nahi sakte, isliye wahan sirf conduction hota hai, convection nahi.

## Quick Recap

- **Heat transfer ke 3 tareeke:** conduction, convection, radiation.
- **Conduction:** mainly solids mein; particles apni jagah par rehte hain aur garmi particle se particle ko milti hai, hot se cold ki taraf. Metals (silver, copper, aluminium) good conductors; lakdi, plastic, glass, hawa, oon, thermocole insulators.
- **Liquid aur gas** conduction mein bahut kamzor hain (test tube mein upar ubalta paani, neeche barf).
- **Convection:** liquids aur gases mein; garam fluid phailta hai, uski density kam hoti hai, wo upar uthta hai aur thanda fluid neeche aata hai: **convection current**. Solid ya vacuum mein convection nahi hota.
- Convection ke examples: ubalta paani, room heater (neeche), AC (upar), sea breeze aur land breeze, hawa (wind), hot-air balloon, geyser, chimney.
- **Radiation:** bina medium ke, infrared waves ke roop mein. Sun ki garmi vacuum se aati hai. Har garam cheez radiation nikaalti hai.
- **Kaali, khurdari surface** zyada absorb aur zyada emit karti hai; **safed ya chamakdaar** surface zyada reflect karti hai.
- **Thermos flask:** vacuum (conduction aur convection rokta hai), chamakdaar deewarein (radiation rokti hain), cork stopper aur casing (insulator): teeno raaste band.
- Q = m c ΔT se bataya ja sakta hai ki kitni heat nikli ya sokhi gayi, aur Q ÷ t se average dar (W mein).

1. Mode: **conduction**. Metal ek achha conductor hai, to garmi particle se particle tezi se doosre sire tak pahunchti hai. Lakdi ek insulator hai, isliye garmi bahut dheere guzarti hai aur doosra sira garam nahi hota.

   (c) **Conduction rokna**: plastic insulator hai, garmi handle tak nahi pahunchti.
   (d) **Radiation**: Sun ki garmi vacuum se infrared waves ke roop mein aati hai.
   (e) **Conduction**: iron conductor hai, haath ki garmi tezi se kheench leta hai, isliye zyada thanda lagta hai (dono ka temperature kamre jaisa hi hai).
   (f) **Convection**: balloon ki andar ki garam hawa phailkar halki ho jaati hai, isliye balloon uthta hai.

6. Vacuum mein particle nahi hote, isliye **conduction aur convection** ruk jaate hain. Chamakdaar (silvered) deewarein infrared radiation ko wapas reflect kar deti hain, isliye **radiation** ruk jaata hai.

9. Flask: Q = 0.5 kg × 4200 J/(kg °C) × 2 °C = **4200 J**. Open cup: Q = 0.5 kg × 4200 J/(kg °C) × 15 °C = **31,500 J**. Ratio = 31,500 J ÷ 4200 J = **7.5** guna.
   Jaanch: 4200 × 7.5 = 31,500 ✅ (aur 15 ÷ 2 = 7.5 ✅)

10. (a) **False** ❌. Convection ke liye fluid chahiye, vacuum mein koi particle nahi hota.
    (b) **True** ✅. Radiation electromagnetic waves se hota hai, isliye Sun ki garmi vacuum se aa jaati hai.
    (c) **False** ❌. Hawa ek achhi insulator hai: bahut kamzor conductor.

11. Heater ke paas ki garam hawa halki hokar upar uthti hai aur upar ki thandi hawa neeche aati hai, isse poore kamre mein convection current chalta hai. Isliye heater neeche rakhte hain. Geyser mein garam paani halka hone ke kaaran upar jaata hai, isliye garam paani ka outlet upar ki taraf rakha jaata hai.
