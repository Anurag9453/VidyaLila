# Topic 1: Heat aur Temperature: Farak aur Thermometer

Pichhle chapter ke end mein humne kaha tha ki ab ek nayi tarah ki energy par chalte hain: **"garmi"**. Asal mein ye naya mehmaan nahi hai. Energy chapter mein jab forms ki list banayi thi (kinetic, potential, sound, light, ...), usme **heat** bhi ek form thi. Aur machines ke chapter mein dekha tha ki **friction** ki wajah se machine ko di gayi energy ka kuch hissa **garmi** ban jata hai: jack ka screw ghumane ke baad garm milta hai, cycle ki chain aur engine garm ho jaate hain. To **heat ek form of energy hai**, aur is chapter mein hum is garmi ko samjhenge: ye kya hai, ise napte kaise hain, ye kaise bahti hai, aur cheezon par kya asar karti hai.

Is topic mein sabse pehle do bahut judte-julte shabdon ka farak: **heat** aur **temperature**. Aur phir wo yantra jisse temperature napte hain: **thermometer**.

## Part A: Heat kya hai, temperature kya hai

### Heat: energy jo behti hai

**Heat** ek **form of energy** hai. Ye wahi energy hai jo garm cheez se thandi cheez ki taraf **behti** hai, kyunki dono ke temperature mein farak hota hai.

- Heat energy hai, to uski **SI unit joule (J)** hi hai, wahi jo work aur energy ki thi.
- Ek purani aur khaane-peene mein ab bhi chalti unit hai **calorie (cal)**. Roughly **1 cal = 4.2 J** (zyada sahi value 4.18 J hai; hum 4.2 J lenge).
- **Kilocalorie (kcal)** = 1000 cal. Khane ke packet par jo "Calories" likha hota hai, wo asal mein kilocalorie hoti hai.

Ek zaruri baat: "heat" us energy ko kehte hain jo **ek cheez se doosri cheez ko ja rahi hai**. Cheez ke andar rakhi hui garmi ko hum aam bol-chaal mein "garmi" kehte hain, par physics mein pakka shabd hai "heat energy ka behna". Is chapter mein hum is farak ko zyada ulajhayenge nahi; bas itna dhyan rakho ki heat ek energy hai, koi alag "garm padarth" nahi.

### Temperature: kitna garm, kitna thanda

**Temperature** ek number hai jo batata hai ki koi cheez **kitni garm ya kitni thandi** hai. Ye number do kaam karta hai:

1. Cheez ki "garmahat" ki degree batata hai (jaise 38.5 °C).
2. Ye batata hai ki do cheezein chhune par **heat kis taraf behegi**: hamesha **zyada temperature wali cheez se kam temperature wali cheez ki taraf**.

### Heat aur temperature ka farak: ek tasveer

Ek tasveer socho:

- **Temperature = "kitna garm"** (cheez ki garmahat ka level).
- **Heat = "kitni garmi ka bhandaar" ya "kitni garmi ki energy"**.

Aviral ke sawal par aate hain. **80 °C ki chai ka chhota cup** aur **40 °C ke paani ki badi bucket**.

- Chai ka temperature **zyada** hai (80 °C > 40 °C): wo zyada garm hai.
- Par bucket mein paani **bahut zyada (mass bahut zyada)** hai. Itne saare paani ke andar garmi ki energy ka **bhandaar** chai ke chhote cup se kahin bada hai.

Isko ek aur tarah se samjho. Temperature ek "level" jaisa hai, aur heat "kul matra" jaisa: jaise ek chhote glass mein ek-do ghoont paani aur ek badi tanki mein hazaaron litre, dono ko "bhara hua" kehna alag baat hai. Is baat ko numbers ke saath aage ke topics mein dekhenge (isme cheez ka mass aur uska padarth dono aayenge). Abhi ke liye ek line yaad rakho:

> **Temperature batata hai "kitna garm". Heat batati hai "kitni garmi ki energy". Zyada garm cheez mein zyada heat ho, ye zaroori nahi.**

### Heat sirf garm se thande ki taraf behti hai

Garm chai ka cup mez par rakho. Kuch der baad wo thanda ho jata hai. Heat chai se hawa ki taraf gayi. Aisa kabhi nahi hota ki thandi mez ki heat apne aap garm chai ko aur garm kar de. Isliye:

> **Heat apne aap hamesha higher temperature se lower temperature ki taraf behti hai, ulti taraf kabhi nahi.**

(Frij mein ulti taraf heat bhejne ke liye bijli lagti hai. Wo apne aap nahi hota.)

### Thermal equilibrium: jab behna ruk jaye

Garm chai ko thande steel ke glass mein daalo. Chai se glass ko heat milti hai, to chai thandi aur glass garm hota jata hai. Kuch der baad dono ka temperature **barabar** ho jata hai. Ab dono ke beech temperature ka farak nahi, to **heat ka behna ruk jata hai**. Is sthiti ko **thermal equilibrium** kehte hain.

```
 Shuru mein                    Kuch der baad (thermal equilibrium)

   garm chai                       chai aur glass
   ┌────────┐                      ┌────────┐
   │  80 °C │ ~~ heat ~~>          │ 60 °C  │   heat ka behna band
   └────────┘                      └────────┘
   thanda glass 20 °C              glass bhi 60 °C
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Heat garm chai se thande glass ki taraf behti hai, jab tak dono ka temperature barabar na ho jaye. Figure ke numbers sirf ek example ke liye hain.

Aviral bola, "Ma'am, aapne mera mathha chhukar bhi to check kiya tha. Thermometer kyun chahiye?"

Ms. Thomas ne **teen katori** table par rakhi: ek mein **garm paani**, ek mein **barf wala thanda paani**, aur beech wali mein **lukewarm (gunguna) paani**. Phir bolin, "Daayan haath garm paani mein aur baayan haath thande paani mein ek minute ke liye dalo. Phir **dono haath nikalkar** seedha beech wali katori mein daalo."

Aviral ne kiya aur chaunk gaya: "Arre! Daayan haath (jo garm mein tha) kehta hai ki beech wala paani **thanda** hai. Baayan haath (jo thande mein tha) kehta hai ki wahi paani **garm** hai. Paani to ek hi hai!"

```
  Katori 1        Katori 2          Katori 3
  garm paani      lukewarm paani    thanda paani
  ┌────────┐      ┌────────┐        ┌────────┐
  │~~~~~~~~│      │~~~~~~~~│        │~~~~~~~~│
  └────────┘      └────────┘        └────────┘
  daayan haath                      baayan haath
  yahan 1 min                       yahan 1 min
       └───────> beech wali (lukewarm) katori <───────┘
        daayan haath: "thanda!"   baayan haath: "garm!"
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Same lukewarm paani garm haath ko thanda aur thande haath ko garm lagta hai. Hamari touch ki samajh pehle ki sthiti par depend karti hai.

**Seekh:** hamara sense of touch ek **relative** (tulna wala) sense hai, wo sahi temperature nahi batata. Bukhaar jaisi serious cheez ke liye ya khaane-peene, laboratory ke liye hume ek **bharose ka yantra** chahiye: **thermometer**.

## Part C: Thermometer

### Thermometer kaise kaam karta hai

**Thermometer** temperature napne ka yantra hai. Sabse aam **liquid-in-glass thermometer** ek seedhi si baat par chalta hai: **garm karne par liquid failta hai (expand hota hai)**. Jitna zyada temperature, utna zyada liquid failega, to wo utna upar chadhega. (Ye "failna" Topic 2 mein detail se dekhenge.)

Is thermometer ke teen hisse:

- **Bulb:** neeche ka chhota sa gol hissa, jisme zyaadatar liquid bhara hota hai. Ye cheez se heat leta hai.
- **Stem:** patli, ek-si moti (uniform) **bore** wali kaanch ki nali. Liquid isme upar chadhta hai. Nali ek-si patli honi chahiye, tabhi liquid ki lambai temperature ke barabar badhti hai.
- **Scale:** nali par bani hui degree ki lakeerein.

```
         ┌─ stem (patli, ek-si bore)
         │
  ...    ││   <- scale (°C)
         ││
  20 ──  ││
         ││
  10 ──  ││   <- liquid (mercury ya rangeen alcohol)
         ││
   0 ──  ││
        ┌──┐
        │  │  <- bulb (liquid yahan bhara hota hai)
        └──┘
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Liquid-in-glass thermometer: neeche bulb, upar patli stem, aur saath mein scale. Bulb garm hone par liquid stem mein upar chadhta hai.

### Mercury hi kyun?

Aam thermometer mein ye liquid **mercury (paara)** hota hai, kyunki:

- Mercury **bahut uniform tareeke se** failta hai: temperature ke saath uski lambai lagbhag seedhi chalti hai.
- Ye **kaanch ko geela nahi karta** (glass se chipakta nahi), to upar-neeche ek-saath saaf chalta hai.
- Ye **chamakdaar** hai, to padhna aasan hai.
- Isko bahut chauda range mein use kar sakte hain: ye lagbhag **−39 °C** par jamta hai aur lagbhag **357 °C** par ubalta hai.

Par mercury **zehreela (toxic)** hota hai. Thermometer toote to ise haath se mat chhuo; bade ko bulao. Isliye ab bahut jagah **digital thermometer** aa gaye hain, jo batteries se chalte hain aur mercury-free hote hain. **Bahut thande ilakon** mein, jahan temperature −39 °C se neeche chala jata hai, mercury ki jagah **rangeen alcohol** wale thermometer chalte hain, kyunki alcohol bahut kam temperature par bhi nahi jamta.

### Fixed points aur scale: degree kaise banayi jaati hai

Thermometer par lakeerein banane ke liye do **pakke (fixed) nishaan** chahiye jo har jagah ek hi rahein:

- **Lower fixed point (ice point):** thermometer ko **pighalti barf** mein dalo, mercury jahan ruke, wahan nishaan. Celsius mein ise **0 °C** kehte hain.
- **Upper fixed point (steam point):** thermometer ko **normal atmospheric pressure** par **ubalte paani ki bhaap** mein rakho, mercury jahan ruke, wahan nishaan. Celsius mein ise **100 °C** kehte hain.

In dono nishaanon ke beech ka hissa **fundamental interval** kehlata hai. Isko **100 barabar bhagon** mein baant do, to har bhag **1 °C** ho jata hai. (Celsius ka naam Anders Celsius ke naam par hai.)

### Teen scales: Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin

| Scale | Symbol | Ice point | Steam point | Fundamental interval ke bhag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Celsius** | °C | 0 °C | 100 °C | 100 |
| **Fahrenheit** | °F | 32 °F | 212 °F | 180 (212 − 32) |
| **Kelvin** | K | 273 K | 373 K | 100 (373 − 273) |

Kuch zaruri baatein:

- **Kelvin** temperature ki **SI unit** hai (jo Measurement chapter ke [Part 2](../measurement-and-units/measurement-and-units-part2.md) mein SI ki saat base units mein dekha tha). Isme **degree ka sign (°) nahi** lagta: "300 K" likhte hain, "300 °K" nahi.
- Celsius aur Kelvin mein ek bhag ka size **barabar** hai (dono mein 100 bhag), sirf shuruaat ka point alag hai.
- Fahrenheit mein ek bhag Celsius ke bhag se **chhota** hai (180 bhag aur 100 bhag), isliye ek degree Fahrenheit, ek degree Celsius se chhoti hoti hai.

### Scales ke beech conversion

Teeno scales par ek hi temperature dikhne ke liye ek hi **"kitna aage badhe"** ka anupaat (ratio) barabar hoga. Yaani ice point se jitna upar, fundamental interval ka jo hissa, wo teeno scale par ek-sa hoga:

> (C − 0) / 100 = (F − 32) / 180 = (K − 273) / 100

Isse dono ki conversion nikalti hai:

**Celsius se Fahrenheit:** C/100 = (F − 32)/180, to F − 32 = 180/100 × C = (9/5) × C. Yaani

> **°F = (9/5) × °C + 32**

**Fahrenheit se Celsius:** ulta kar do, **pehle 32 ghatao, phir 5/9 se multiply karo**:

> **°C = (5/9) × (°F − 32)**

**Celsius se Kelvin:** C/100 = (K − 273)/100, yaani

> **K = °C + 273**

(Sahi offset 273.15 hota hai. Class 9 mein hum seedha **273** lenge.)

### Teen scales ek saath

```
   °C           °F            K
  100 ──────── 212 ───────── 373   <- steam point
   :            :             :
   :            :             :
   37 ───────── 98.6 ──────── 310   <- shareer ka normal temperature
   :            :             :
    0 ───────── 32 ────────── 273   <- ice point
```

> **Figure 1.4** – Ek hi temperature ke teen naam: ice point 0 °C = 32 °F = 273 K, steam point 100 °C = 212 °F = 373 K.

## Part D: Solved examples

**Example 1 (°C se °F):** 0 °C, 37 °C, 100 °C, 40 °C aur 25 °C ko °F mein badlo.

Formula: °F = (9/5) × °C + 32

- 0 °C: (9/5) × 0 + 32 = 0 + 32 = **32 °F**
- 37 °C: (9/5) × 37 + 32 = 66.6 + 32 = **98.6 °F**
- 100 °C: (9/5) × 100 + 32 = 180 + 32 = **212 °F**
- 40 °C: (9/5) × 40 + 32 = 72 + 32 = **104 °F**
- 25 °C: (9/5) × 25 + 32 = 45 + 32 = **77 °F**

**Jaanch:** 0 °C = 32 °F aur 100 °C = 212 °F, ye dono fixed points se match karte hain. 37 °C = 98.6 °F, jo normal body temperature hai ✅

**Example 2 (°F se °C):** 212 °F, 98.6 °F, 50 °F aur 14 °F ko °C mein badlo.

Formula: °C = (5/9) × (°F − 32)

- 212 °F: (5/9) × (212 − 32) = (5/9) × 180 = **100 °C**
- 98.6 °F: (5/9) × (98.6 − 32) = (5/9) × 66.6 = **37 °C**
- 50 °F: (5/9) × (50 − 32) = (5/9) × 18 = **10 °C**
- 14 °F: (5/9) × (14 − 32) = (5/9) × (−18) = **−10 °C**

**Jaanch:** −10 °C ko wapas °F mein badlo: (9/5) × (−10) + 32 = −18 + 32 = 14 °F ✅. Aur 10 °C: (9/5) × 10 + 32 = 18 + 32 = 50 °F ✅

**Example 3 (°C se K aur K se °C):** 0 °C, 100 °C ko K mein, aur 300 K, 373 K ko °C mein badlo.

- 0 °C: K = 0 + 273 = **273 K**
- 100 °C: K = 100 + 273 = **373 K**
- 300 K: °C = 300 − 273 = **27 °C**
- 373 K: °C = 373 − 273 = **100 °C**

**Jaanch:** 100 °C = 373 K aur 0 °C = 273 K, dono steam point aur ice point ke Kelvin values se match karte hain ✅

**Example 4 (°C aur °F barabar kab?):** Kis temperature par Celsius aur Fahrenheit ki reading ek hi number hoti hai?

Maan lo wo temperature x hai. To °F = °C = x.

- x = (9/5) × x + 32
- x − (9/5) × x = 32
- (−4/5) × x = 32
- x = 32 × (−5/4) = **−40**

Yaani **−40 °C = −40 °F**.

**Jaanch:** (9/5) × (−40) + 32 = −72 + 32 = −40 ✅

**Example 5 (kharab thermometer):** Ek thermometer galti se ice point par **2 °C** aur steam point par **98 °C** dikhata hai (yaani uske dono nishaan sahi nahi hain, par beech ke 96 bhag barabar hain). Jab wo **50 °C** dikhaye to asli temperature kitna hai? Aur jab **26 °C** dikhaye?

Is thermometer ke fundamental interval mein 98 − 2 = 96 bhag hain, jo asli temperature ke 100 bhag ke barabar hain.

Asli temperature = (reading − 2) / (98 − 2) × 100

- Reading 50 °C: (50 − 2)/(98 − 2) × 100 = (48/96) × 100 = 0.5 × 100 = **50 °C**
- Reading 26 °C: (26 − 2)/(98 − 2) × 100 = (24/96) × 100 = 0.25 × 100 = **25 °C**

**Jaanch:** Reading 2 °C par asli 0 °C aur reading 98 °C par asli 100 °C aata hai, aur 50 aur 26 dono beech mein aate hain: 50 °C wali reading interval ka ek aadha hai (asli 50 °C), 26 °C wali reading interval ka ek chauthai (asli 25 °C) ✅

**Example 6 (mercury ki lambai):** Ek thermometer mein mercury ka dhaaga ice point (0 °C) par **5 cm** aur steam point (100 °C) par **25 cm** lamba hai. **35 °C** par dhaaga kitna lamba hoga?

- 0 °C se 100 °C tak lambai mein badlav = 25 cm − 5 cm = 20 cm
- Har 1 °C par badlav = 20 cm / 100 = 0.2 cm
- 35 °C par badlav = 35 × 0.2 cm = 7 cm
- Lambai = 5 cm + 7 cm = **12 cm**

**Jaanch:** Anupaat se: 35/100 × 20 cm = 7 cm, to 5 cm + 7 cm = 12 cm ✅. Ye bhi dekho: 12 cm 5 cm aur 25 cm ke beech mein hai ✅

**Example 7 (300 K, 300 °C aur 300 °F):** Teeno mein se kaun sabse garm hai? Sabko °C mein badlo.

- 300 K: 300 − 273 = **27 °C**
- 300 °C: seedha **300 °C**
- 300 °F: (5/9) × (300 − 32) = (5/9) × 268 = **148.9 °C** (lagbhag)

Order (garm se thanda): **300 °C > 300 °F (≈ 148.9 °C) > 300 K (27 °C)**

**Jaanch:** 300 K = 27 °C kamre ke temperature jaisa hai ✅. 148.9 °C ko wapas °F mein badlo: (9/5) × 148.9 + 32 = 268 + 32 = 300 °F ✅ (lagbhag)

**Example 8 (clinical thermometer, 39 °C):** Kisi bimar vyakti ka temperature 39 °C hai. Clinical thermometer °F mein kya dikhayega?

- °F = (9/5) × 39 + 32 = 70.2 + 32 = **102.2 °F**

**Jaanch:** Wapas: (5/9) × (102.2 − 32) = (5/9) × 70.2 = 39 °C ✅

**Example 9 (clinical thermometer ki range):** Clinical thermometer ki range 35 °C se 42 °C hoti hai. Ye °F mein kya hai?

- 35 °C: (9/5) × 35 + 32 = 63 + 32 = **95 °F**
- 42 °C: (9/5) × 42 + 32 = 75.6 + 32 = **107.6 °F**

Isliye clinical thermometer par aksar likha hota hai "94 °F se 108 °F" (round karke).

**Jaanch:** (5/9) × (107.6 − 32) = (5/9) × 75.6 = 42 °C ✅

## Part E: Clinical aur laboratory thermometer

**Clinical thermometer** shareer ka temperature napne ke liye hai.

- **Range:** 35 °C se 42 °C (yaani 94 °F se 108 °F), kyunki insaan ka temperature isi range ke andar rehta hai.
- **Normal body temperature:** **37 °C = 98.6 °F** (Example 1 mein check kiya).
- **Kink (constriction):** bulb ke thik upar nali mein ek **tang mod** hota hai. Jab mercury failkar upar chadhta hai to is kink se nikal jata hai, par thanda hone par mercury ka dhaaga kink par se **toot jata hai** aur upar hi ruka rehta hai. Isse jab tum thermometer ko body se nikalte ho, reading **wahi bani rehti hai** aur tum aaram se padh sakte ho.
- **Shake karna:** isi wajah se agle reading se pehle thermometer ko **jhatke se hilakar** mercury neeche karna padta hai.

Ek baat na bhoolo: reading thermometer ko baanh se nikalne ke baad padho, aur **shake karna reading se pehle** hota hai, baad mein nahi.

**Laboratory thermometer** ki range **−10 °C se 110 °C** hoti hai, kyunki laboratory mein paani ke ice point se thoda neeche se lekar steam point se thoda upar tak ke temperature napne hote hain. Iska scale chauda hota hai aur ise **kisi bhi cheez mein dubakar** padha jata hai (clinical ki tarah kink nahi hota).

Doosre thermometer bhi hote hain (sirf naam):

- **Digital thermometer:** sensor aur chhoti screen, mercury nahi.
- **Infrared thermometer:** bina chhue maathe ya kaan se temperature napta hai.
- **Maximum-minimum thermometer:** din bhar ka sabse zyada aur sabse kam temperature record karta hai (mausam vibhag ke kaam aata hai).

## Part F: Aviral ka sawal, ab solve karte hain

**1. Aviral ka bukhaar:** thermometer par **38.5 °C** aaya.

- °F = (9/5) × 38.5 + 32 = 69.3 + 32 = **101.3 °F**
- Normal body temperature 37 °C (98.6 °F) hai. Aviral ka temperature normal se **38.5 °C − 37 °C = 1.5 °C** zyada hai: **bukhaar** hai.

**Jaanch:** (5/9) × (101.3 − 32) = (5/9) × 69.3 = 38.5 °C ✅

**2. "Temperature aakhir napta kya hai?"** Temperature batata hai ki koi cheez **kitni garm ya thandi** hai, aur ye bhi ki dusri cheez se touch hone par heat **kis taraf** behegi.

**3. Chai ka cup ya paani ki bucket?**

- **Temperature:** chai ka cup (80 °C) zyada garm hai, bucket ka paani (40 °C) kam garm hai.
- **Heat energy ka bhandaar:** bucket mein paani bahut zyada hai, to uske andar **garmi ki energy zyada** hai, kyunki mass bahut zyada hai.

To Aviral, ek cheez zyada garm ho sakti hai, par uske andar kul garmi ki energy kam ho sakti hai. **Garm chai** ko haath lagane par tumhe jalan zyada hogi (temperature zyada), par agar tum 80 °C ke cup aur 40 °C ki bucket **dono ko barf par** rakho, to bucket zyada barf pighla degi (kyunki uske paas bhandaar bada hai). Ye baat numbers ke saath aage ke topics mein khulegi.

Ms. Thomas ne Aviral se kaha, "Ab paani peete raho aur aaram karo. Main tumhare ghar par khabar bhej deti hoon."

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Heat aur temperature ek hi cheez hain"**

Nahi. **Heat** ek energy hai (joule mein). **Temperature** garmahat ka level hai (°C, °F, K mein). Chai ka cup 80 °C par hai, par uski heat energy bucket se kam hai.

**Galti 2: "Jo cheez zyada garm hai, uske andar zyada heat hogi"**

Zaruri nahi. Bada talaab 25 °C par bhi bahut saari heat rakhta hai, jabki garm chai ka cup (80 °C) usse bahut kam. Heat sirf temperature par nahi, **mass** par bhi depend karti hai.

**Galti 3: °K likhna**

Kelvin ke saath degree ka sign nahi lagta. **300 K** sahi hai, **300 °K** galat. Celsius aur Fahrenheit mein °C aur °F likhte hain.

**Galti 4: Fahrenheit conversion mein 32 bhool jana**

37 °C ko °F mein badalte waqt kisi ne sirf (9/5) × 37 = 66.6 °F likh diya, to wo galat hai. Sahi: 66.6 + 32 = **98.6 °F**.

**Galti 5: 5/9 ya 32 ka galat order**

°F se °C karte waqt **pehle 32 ghatao, phir 5/9 se multiply karo**. Galat tarika: 98.6 × 5/9 − 32 = 54.8 − 32 = 22.8 °C, jo bilkul bekaar hai. Sahi: (5/9) × (98.6 − 32) = (5/9) × 66.6 = **37 °C**.

Aur °C se °F karte waqt **pehle 9/5 se multiply karo, phir 32 jodo**. Pehle 32 jodke phir multiply karoge to galat aayega.

**Galti 6: Haath se touch karke temperature napna**

Teen katori wale experiment ne dikha diya: wahi lukewarm paani ek haath ko thanda aur doosre ko garm lagta hai. Napne ke liye thermometer hi use karo.

**Galti 7: Clinical thermometer ko reading ke baad shake karna**

Kink ki wajah se mercury apne aap neeche nahi aata. Shake **reading se pehle** karna hota hai, taaki purani reading hat jaye. Reading ke baad shake karoge to wo reading mit jayegi.

## Quick Recap

- **Heat** ek **form of energy** hai, jo temperature ke farak ki wajah se **garm se thande** ki taraf behti hai. SI unit **joule (J)**. Calorie: 1 cal ≈ 4.2 J (4.18 J bhi), 1 kcal = 1000 cal.
- **Temperature** batata hai cheez **kitni garm ya thandi** hai aur heat kis taraf behegi. Ye **number** hai.
- Temperature = "kitna garm". Heat = "kitni garmi ki energy". 80 °C ki chai ke cup ka temperature zyada hai, par 40 °C ki badi bucket mein zyada heat energy hai (mass zyada).
- Heat apne aap sirf higher se lower temperature ki taraf behti hai. Dono cheezon ka temperature barabar hone par **thermal equilibrium**: heat ka behna band.
- Touch ka sense bharose ka nahi (teen katori wala experiment). Temperature napne ke liye **thermometer**.
- **Liquid-in-glass thermometer**: bulb, stem (uniform bore), scale. Liquid garm hone par failta hai. **Mercury** uniform failta hai, glass ko geela nahi karta, chamakdaar hai, −39 °C se 357 °C tak; par zehreela hai. Bahut thande ke liye alcohol; digital bhi common.
- Fixed points: **ice point 0 °C**, **steam point 100 °C** (normal atmospheric pressure par). Celsius mein 100 bhag, Fahrenheit mein 32 °F se 212 °F (180 bhag), Kelvin mein 273 K se 373 K (100 bhag).
- **°F = (9/5) × °C + 32**, **°C = (5/9) × (°F − 32)**, **K = °C + 273**. General: (C − 0)/100 = (F − 32)/180 = (K − 273)/100. Kelvin ke saath degree sign nahi.
- **Clinical thermometer**: 35 °C se 42 °C (94 °F se 108 °F), kink, reading se pehle shake. Normal body 37 °C = 98.6 °F.
- **Laboratory thermometer**: −10 °C se 110 °C. Aur bhi: digital, infrared, maximum-minimum.
- Aviral: 38.5 °C = 101.3 °F: bukhaar.

   (c) 300 K is correctly written as 300 °K.
   (d) Two bodies in thermal equilibrium are at the same temperature.

### Answers

1. °F = (9/5) × 60 + 32 = 108 + 32 = **140 °F**.
   Jaanch: (5/9) × (140 − 32) = (5/9) × 108 = 60 °C ✅

2. °C = (5/9) × (68 − 32) = (5/9) × 36 = **20 °C**.
   Jaanch: (9/5) × 20 + 32 = 36 + 32 = 68 °F ✅

3. K = 27 + 273 = **300 K**. °C = 310 − 273 = **37 °C**.
   Jaanch: 300 − 273 = 27 aur 37 + 273 = 310 ✅

4. Interval mein 95 − 5 = 90 bhag. Asli temperature = (41 − 5)/(95 − 5) × 100 = (36/90) × 100 = **40 °C**.
   Jaanch: 5 aur 95 par asli 0 aur 100 aata hai, aur 41 par 0.4 hissa, to 40 °C ✅

5. 0 °C se 100 °C tak 24 − 4 = 20 cm ka badlav, to 1 °C par 0.2 cm. 60 °C par lambai = 4 cm + 60 × 0.2 cm = 4 + 12 = **16 cm**. Lambai 20 cm ho to badlav = 20 − 4 = 16 cm, to temperature = 16/0.2 = **80 °C**.
   Jaanch: 4 + 80 × 0.2 = 4 + 16 = 20 cm ✅

6. °C = (5/9) × (104 − 32) = (5/9) × 72 = **40 °C**. Normal 37 °C se zyada hai, to **haan, bukhaar** (tez bukhaar) hai.
   Jaanch: (9/5) × 40 + 32 = 72 + 32 = 104 °F ✅

7. 50 K ko °C mein badlo: 50 − 273 = −223 °C. 50 °C = 50 + 273 = 323 K. Isliye **50 °C bahut zyada garm** hai (50 K ≈ −223 °C bahut thanda hai).
   Jaanch: 323 K > 50 K ✅

8. Thermometer ko body se nikalne par mercury thanda hokar sikudta hai, par kink par dhaaga toot jata hai aur upar wala hissa wahin ruka rehta hai. Isse reading padhne ke liye bani rehti hai. Kyunki mercury apne aap neeche nahi aata, agli reading se pehle thermometer ko **jhatke se hilakar** mercury neeche karna padta hai.

9. (a) **False** ❌. Bucket mein mass bahut zyada ho to wo zyada heat rakh sakti hai.
   (b) **False** ❌. Heat apne aap sirf garm se thande ki taraf behti hai.
   (c) **False** ❌. Kelvin ke saath degree sign nahi lagta: 300 K.
   (d) **True** ✅. Thermal equilibrium mein dono ka temperature barabar hota hai.

10. Ek haath garm paani se aaya to usko lukewarm paani thanda lagta hai, doosre haath ko, jo thande paani se aaya, wahi garm lagta hai. Touch ka sense **relative** hai. Sahi tareeka **thermometer** hai.
