# Topic 2: Reflection ke Laws

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki light seedhi line mein chalti hai: isi wajah se shadow bante hain, eclipse hote hain aur pinhole camera kaam karta hai. Par light hamesha khuli jagah mein hi nahi chalti rehti. Kabhi na kabhi wo kisi cheez se takrati hai: deewar, kitaab, paani ki sateh, ya sheeshe se. Tab kya hota hai? Kuch cheezon se light **wapas uchhal** jaati hai. Is topic mein hum is uchhalne ke **do pakke niyam** (laws) seekhenge, aur dekhenge ki ye niyam har surface par kaam karte hain, chahe wo sheeshe jaisi chamakdaar ho ya khurdari deewar.

## Terrace par chamakta hua gola

School ki terrace par dopahar ki tez dhoop thi. Science teacher Mr. Parekh ke haath mein ek chhota sa mirror tha. Paas mein Ziva khadi thi.

Mr. Parekh ne mirror ko dhoop mein is tarah pakda ki dhoop usse takra kar courtyard ke us paar wali classroom ki deewar par gir rahi thi. Deewar par ek chamakta hua gola ban gaya.

Ziva khushi se bol padi, "Sir, wo dekho, deewar par ek chhota sa doosra sooraj!"

Mr. Parekh ne mirror ko zara sa tilt kiya. Gola deewar par ek taraf sarak gaya. Phir doosri taraf tilt kiya, gola doosri taraf nach gaya. Ziva taaliyan bajane lagi.

Phir usne poochha, "Sir, kya main is gole ko seedha us upar wali **khidki** par pahuncha sakti hoon, bilkul sahi nishane par?"

Mr. Parekh muskuraye, "Bilkul, aur wo bhi andaze se nahi, **niyam se**. Light jab mirror se uchhalti hai to wo mann-marzi se kahin nahi jaati. Uska ek pakka niyam hai. Wo niyam samajh liya, to tum gole ko jahan chaho wahan bhej sakogi." Is kahani ko hum topic ke ant mein solve karenge.

## Part A: Reflection kya hai, aur use naapte kaise hain

### Reflection

> **Reflection** = jab light kisi surface par girti hai aur wahan se **wapas lautkar usi medium mein** chali jaati hai, to is ghatna ko reflection kehte hain. Reflection mein light ki direction badal jaati hai, par wo usi medium (jaise hawa) mein rehti hai.

Gend ko deewar par maaro to wo wapas aati hai. Light ke saath bhi kuch aisa hi hota hai, bas light ke uchhalne ka ek **bahut sakht niyam** hai.

### Reflection ko samajhne ke liye ek sketch

Reflection ke baare mein baat karne ke liye hume kuch naam chahiye. Pehle sketch dekho (ye ek mirror ko side se dekhne ki tasveer hai; mirror ek seedhi line ki tarah dikh raha hai).

```
                     N (normal)
                     :
   Incident ray      :      Reflected ray
             \       :       /
              \      :      /
               \     :     /
                \  i : r  /
                 ↘   :   ↗
                  \  :  /
                   \ : /
                    \:/
  ===================O===================  Mirror surface
                     ^
                O = point of incidence
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Reflection ke terms. Incident ray O par girti hai, reflected ray O se wapas jaati hai. Normal N, O par mirror ke perpendicular (90°) hai. Angle i incident ray aur normal ke beech hai, angle r reflected ray aur normal ke beech. (Sketch ka shape sirf samjhane ke liye hai: i aur r ke asli maan alag ho sakte hain.)

Ab ek-ek term ko sahi se pakdo:

- **Incident ray:** wo ray jo surface par aa rahi hai.
- **Reflected ray:** wo ray jo surface se wapas ja rahi hai.
- **Point of incidence (O):** surface ka wo bindu jahan incident ray girti hai aur reflected ray shuru hoti hai.
- **Normal (N):** point O par surface ke **perpendicular** line. Ye ek **kalpanik** (imaginary) line hai: hum use sirf angle naapne ke liye banate hain. Normal surface ke **saath-saath** nahi, balki surface se **seedhi bahar** nikalti hai.
- **Angle of incidence (i):** incident ray aur **normal** ke beech ka angle.
- **Angle of reflection (r):** reflected ray aur **normal** ke beech ka angle.
- **Plane of incidence:** wo flat plane jisme incident ray aur normal dono ho. Socho ek sapaat kaagaz ki sheet, jis par ye dono rays bani hon.

> **Sabse zaroori baat:** i aur r **hamesha normal se** naape jaate hain, mirror ki surface se **nahi**. Ye galti bahut hoti hai, isliye har example mein hum ise dhyaan se dekhenge.

## Part B: Reflection ke do niyam (Laws of Reflection)

Ab hum wo do pakke niyam dekhte hain jo Mr. Parekh ne kaha tha.

> **Law 1:** Angle of incidence = angle of reflection.
> **∠i = ∠r**

> **Law 2:** Incident ray, reflected ray aur point of incidence par normal, **teeno ek hi plane mein** hote hain.

Law 1 bata deta hai ki ray **kitni** ghumegi: jis angle par aayi, usi angle par normal se doosri taraf jaayegi. Law 2 bata deta hai ki ray **kis plane mein** rahegi: wo plane se bahar (upar ya neeche) nahi jaayegi.

Dono niyam har chamakdaar aur har khurdari surface par, har ray ke liye sach hain.

### Activity: laws ko khud check karo

Ye activity school lab ya ghar par ho sakti hai. Zaroorat: ek safed sheet (ya chart paper), ek chhota flat mirror (stand ya clay se seedha khada kiya hua), ek torch jisme ek patli slit (ya laser pointer, jise aankhon mein na dalo), protractor, pencil aur scale.

**Steps:**

1. Safed sheet ko table par bichhao. Sheet par ek seedhi line **AB** kheencho.
2. Mirror ko line AB par **seedha (vertical) khada** karo, taki uski reflecting surface AB line ke saath rahe.
3. AB ke beech ke ek point **O** par protractor ya set-square se **perpendicular line ON** (normal) kheencho.
4. Normal ke ek taraf, O se ek line **OP** kheencho jo ON ke saath 30° ka angle banaye. Ye hamari incident ray ki direction hai.
5. Torch ki beam ko sheet ke bilkul **saath-saath** (table ke level par) P se O ki taraf bhejo. Beam sheet par ek patli chamakti line ki tarah dikhegi. Beam O par mirror se takrakar doosri taraf jaayegi.
6. Reflected beam jis raste se jaa rahi hai, us par sheet par do point **Q1** aur **Q2** pencil se mark karo. Phir mirror hata kar O, Q1, Q2 ko line se jodo. Ye **reflected ray OQ** hai.
7. Protractor se **∠NOP (i)** aur **∠NOQ (r)** naapo.
8. P ki jagah badal kar (angle 20°, 45°, 60°) ye sab dobara karo aur readings table mein likho.

**Sample readings:**

| Observation | Angle of incidence i (°) | Angle of reflection r (°) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20 | 20 |
| 2 | 30 | 31 |
| 3 | 45 | 45 |
| 4 | 60 | 59 |

Har baar **i lagbhag r ke barabar** aata hai. Chhota sa 1° ka farak hamare naapne ki galti (reading error) se hai, niyam mein koi gadbad nahi. Ye Law 1 hai.

```
          P       N       Q
            \     :     /
             \    :    /
              \   :   /
               \  :  /
                \ : /
                 \:/
         A========O========B

  Sheet ke plane mein: PO (incident), ON (normal), OQ (reflected)
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Activity ka top view. Sheet par O par normal ON, incident beam PO aur reflected beam OQ, teeno sheet ke plane mein hain. (Mirror ko sheet par line AB ke upar khada kiya gaya hai.)

**Law 2 ka check:** sheet ko aisi banao ki uska aadha hissa (jis par reflected ray hai) normal ke saath mod kar peeche jhuk sake (jaise ek folded card). Jab sheet seedhi hai, beam Q wali taraf sheet par dikhti hai. Ab us aadhe hisse ko peeche jhuka do: **reflected beam us hisse par nazar nahi aati**, kyunki wo hissa ab us plane mein nahi raha jisme incident ray aur normal hain. Sheet ko dobara seedha karte hi beam wapas dikhne lagti hai. Isse Law 2 pakka ho gaya: teeno ek hi plane mein hain.

## Part C: Do khaas baatein: Path ulta chalna aur seedhi takkar

### 1. Path of light reversible hai

Maan lo ek ray AO se aati hai aur mirror se OB ki taraf uchhalti hai. Ab agar light **B se O ki taraf** bheji jaaye (yaani reflected ray ke raste se ulte), to wo **O se A ki taraf** hi jaayegi, yaani apne purane raste par wapas.

```
               N
        A      :      B
         \     :     /
          \    :    /
           ↘   :   ↗
            \  :  /
             \ : /
  =============O=============  Mirror
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Path reversible hai. A se chali light B pahunchti hai, aur B se chali light A pahunchti hai, dono ka raasta wahi hai. (Sketch mein A aur B normal ke dono taraf barabar angle par hain. Ek arrow A se O aur doosra O se B ki taraf hai; B se chalo to arrows ulte ho jaate hain.)

Ek line mein example: agar torch ki beam mirror se uchhalkar deewar ke point W par gir rahi hai, to W se mirror ki taraf bheji gayi beam usi raste se wapas torch tak pahunchegi.

Light ka ye ulta chalna is wajah se sambhav hai ki Law 1 mein i aur r **barabar** hain: raste ko ulta karo to i aur r bas aapas mein jagah badal lete hain, aur dono barabar hi rehte hain.

### 2. Seedhi takkar (Normal incidence)

Agar incident ray **normal ke saath-saath** mirror par aaye, yaani seedhi 90° par, to angle of incidence **i = 0°** hota hai. Law 1 ke hisaab se **r = 0°** bhi hoga. Reflected ray usi raste par wapas lautti hai jis se aayi thi.

```
          N (normal)
     ↓    :    ↑
     ↓    :    ↑
     ↓    :    ↑
     ↓    :    ↑
     ↓    :    ↑
==========O==========  Mirror
```

> **Figure 2.4** – Normal incidence: i = 0°, r = 0°. Ray apne hi raste se wapas jaati hai. (Sketch mein aane aur jaane wali ray ko samajhne ke liye alag-alag dikhaya hai, asal mein dono ek hi line par hain.)

Yaani **0° ka matlab "takkar hi nahi" nahi hai**: takkar hui hai, par ray ne apna raasta nahi badla (wo seedhi laut aayi).

## Part D: Regular reflection aur diffused reflection

Mirror se to light ke niyam dikh jaate hain. Par deewar ya kitaab ke page se bhi light takrati hai. Phir wahan mirror jaisa chamakta gola kyun nahi banta? Is sawal ka jawab surface mein chhupa hai.

### Regular reflection

Jab surface **bahut smooth aur polished** hota hai (jaise mirror, steel ki plate, shaant paani), to usi taraf se aati **parallel rays** reflection ke baad bhi **parallel** hi rehti hain. Tab ek saaf image banti hai.

```
   \       /   \       /   \       /
    ↘     ↗     ↘     ↗     ↘     ↗
     \   /       \   /       \   /
      \ /         \ /         \ /
=======*===========*===========*========
Smooth surface
```

> **Figure 2.5** – Regular reflection: parallel incident rays ke baad reflected rays bhi parallel hain.

### Diffused (irregular) reflection

Jab surface **khurdari (rough)** hota hai, to usme bahut saare chhote-chhote ubhar aur gaddhe hote hain, jo hamari aankhon ko nahi dikhte. Har ray surface ke **apne chhote tukde** par girti hai, aur har tukde ka normal alag direction mein hota hai. Isliye reflected rays **alag-alag directions mein bikhar jaati hain**.

```
   \       /   \   :       \ \
    ↘     ↗     ↘  ↑        ↘ ↖
     \   /       \ :         \ \
      \ /         \:          \ \
/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\
Khurdari (rough) surface
```

> **Figure 2.6** – Diffused reflection: teeno incident rays parallel hain ("\"), par rough surface ke har tukde ka normal alag hai, isliye reflected rays alag-alag directions mein uchhalti hain: pehli upar-daayen, doosri seedhi upar, teesri upar-baayen. (Sketch bas idea dikhane ke liye hai.)

**Bahut zaroori baat:** diffused reflection mein bhi **har ray reflection ke niyam maanti hai**. Har ray ke liye apne point of incidence par **i = r** hai. Bas fark ye hai ki rough surface ke har point par normal ki direction alag hai, isliye reflected rays alag-alag direction mein jaati hain. **Niyam toot nahi rahe, surface ka shape alag hai.**

### Diffused reflection se hi hume cheezein dikhti hain

Kitaab ka page ya deewar khurdari hai, isliye unse light **har direction mein** bikhar jaati hai. Isi wajah se tum classroom ke kisi bhi kone se board, kitaab ya deewar dekh sakte ho. Agar sab kuch sirf mirror jaisa regular reflection karta, to hume cheezein tabhi dikhti jab hamari aankh reflected ray ki seedh mein hoti.

Ziva ke gole ki wajah bhi yahi hai: **mirror (regular reflection)** ne dhoop ko ek hi direction mein bheja, isliye deewar par ek chamakta gola bana. Agar mirror ki jagah safed kaagaz hota, to dhoop har taraf bikhar jaati aur koi gola nahi banta.

### 8 surfaces: regular ya diffused?

| Surface | Reflection ka type | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| Mirror | Regular | Bahut smooth aur polished |
| Shaant talaab ka paani | Regular | Sateh smooth hai |
| Polished steel ki plate | Regular | Chamakdaar aur smooth |
| Safed kaagaz | Diffused | Zoom karo to sateh khurdari hai |
| Deewar (plaster wali) | Diffused | Khurdari sateh |
| Kapda | Diffused | Dhaage ke ubhar |
| Kachchi sadak | Diffused | Bahut khurdari |
| Lahrate paani ki sateh | Diffused (zyada tar) | Sateh tukdon mein tooti hai, har tukde ka normal alag hai |

## Part E: Angles ka sahi hisaab

Ab hum angle ke teen naam ek saath yaad karte hain. Kisi bhi reflection problem mein inhi se kaam chalta hai.

```
                    N
            \       :
             \      :
              \     :
               \i   :
                \   :
                 \  :
                  \ :
  90° - i          \:
  ==================O==================
```

> **Figure 2.7** – Ray aur mirror ki surface ke beech ka angle (glancing angle) = 90° − i. Normal aur mirror surface ke beech 90° hota hai, isliye ray aur surface ka angle, ray aur normal ke angle ko 90° se ghata kar milta hai.

| Angle | Kaise naapte hain | Sutra |
|---|---|---|
| **Angle of incidence (i)** | Incident ray aur **normal** ke beech | Given ya 90° − (ray aur mirror ke beech ka angle) |
| **Angle of reflection (r)** | Reflected ray aur **normal** ke beech | r = i |
| **Ray aur mirror surface ka angle (glancing angle)** | Ray aur **mirror surface** ke beech | 90° − i |
| **Incident aur reflected ray ke beech ka angle (θ)** | Point O par dono rays ke beech | θ = i + r = **2i** |

Ye **angle θ = 2i** wo angle hai jo point O par incident ray aur reflected ray ke beech banta hai. Dhyaan do: ye ray ke **"mudne" ka angle (deviation) nahi** hai. Ray apni purani seedh se jitna mudti hai, wo **180° − 2i** hota hai, aur kai kitaabein usi ko deviation kehti hain. Is chapter mein hum 2i ko hamesha **"incident aur reflected ray ke beech ka angle θ"** hi kahenge, taaki confusion na ho.

> **Dhyaan se:** sawal mein jab "ray mirror ke saath 30° ka angle banati hai" likha ho, to wo 30° mirror ki **surface** se hai, **normal se nahi**. Isliye i = 90° − 30° = 60°.

## Part F: Solved examples

(Har example mein i, r hamesha **normal se** naape gaye hain.)

### Example 1: i diya hai, baaki nikaalo

Ek ray mirror par i = 35° par girti hai. Find (a) r, (b) rays ke beech ka angle θ, (c) reflected ray aur mirror ke beech ka angle.

- (a) Law 1: r = i = **35°**.
- (b) θ = 2i = 2 × 35° = **70°**.
- (c) Mirror se angle = 90° − r = 90° − 35° = **55°**.

Jaanch: θ = i + r = 35° + 35° = 70° ✅ aur 35° + 55° = 90° (normal aur mirror ke beech) ✅

### Example 2: mirror se angle diya hai

Ek ray mirror ki surface ke saath 30° ka angle banati hai. Find i, r aur θ.

- i = 90° − 30° = **60°** (normal se angle, surface se nahi).
- Law 1: r = **60°**.
- θ = 2i = 2 × 60° = **120°**.

Jaanch: ray aur mirror ke beech 30° aur normal se 60°, 30° + 60° = 90° ✅. Aur θ = 60° + 60° = 120° ✅

### Example 3: rays ke beech ka angle diya hai

Incident ray aur reflected ray ke beech ka angle 80° hai. Find i aur r.

- θ = 2i, to i = θ ÷ 2 = 80° ÷ 2 = **40°**.
- r = i = **40°**.

Jaanch: i + r = 40° + 40° = 80° ✅. (Ziva ke gole mein bhi aisa hi hota: agar dhoop mirror ke normal se 40° par aaye, to reflected ray 40° par doosri taraf jaati, aur dono rays ke beech 80° banta.)

### Example 4: ray normal par girti hai

Ek ray mirror par seedhi (normal ke saath-saath) girti hai. Find i, r aur ray ka raasta.

- Normal ke saath angle 0°, to i = **0°**.
- Law 1: r = **0°**.
- Reflected ray normal ke saath-saath hai, yaani **usi raste par wapas** lautti hai.
- θ = 2i = 2 × 0° = **0°**: dono rays ek hi line par hain.
- Ray aur mirror ke beech ka angle = 90° − 0° = **90°**.

Jaanch: ray ka mirror se angle 90° hi hona chahiye jab wo seedhi girti hai ✅

### Example 5: ray mirror se 25° par

Ek ray mirror ki surface se 25° ka angle banakar girti hai. Find i, r aur θ.

- i = 90° − 25° = **65°**.
- r = **65°**.
- θ = 2 × 65° = **130°**.

Jaanch: 65° + 65° = 130° ✅, aur 25° + 65° = 90° ✅

### Example 6: do rays

Mirror par do rays girti hain: ray 1 i = 20° par, ray 2 i = 50° par. Find har ek ka r, θ aur mirror se angle.

| Ray | i | r | θ = 2i | Mirror se angle = 90° − i |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ray 1 | 20° | 20° | 2 × 20° = 40° | 90° − 20° = 70° |
| Ray 2 | 50° | 50° | 2 × 50° = 100° | 90° − 50° = 40° |

Jaanch: Ray 1: 20° + 20° = 40° ✅. Ray 2: 50° + 50° = 100° ✅. Dono ke liye i + (mirror se angle) = 90° ✅ (20° + 70°, 50° + 40°).

Dhyaan do: **i jitna bada, ray utni mirror ki surface ke nazdeek**, aur rays ke beech ka angle utna bada.

### Example 7: normal se 55° par

Ek ray normal se 55° par aati hai. Reflected ray ki direction batao.

- i = 55°.
- Law 1: r = **55°**.
- Reflected ray normal ke **doosri taraf** (jahan se ray nahi aayi) 55° par jaati hai.
- θ = 2 × 55° = **110°**.
- Mirror se angle = 90° − 55° = **35°**.

Jaanch: 55° + 55° = 110° ✅, aur 55° + 35° = 90° ✅

### Example 8: torch ki beam mirror se 50° par

Ek torch ki beam mirror ki surface se 50° ka angle banakar girti hai. Find i, r aur θ.

- i = 90° − 50° = **40°**.
- r = **40°**.
- θ = 2 × 40° = **80°**.

Jaanch: 40° + 50° = 90° ✅, aur 40° + 40° = 80° ✅

### Example 9: reflected ray se wapas incident ki taraf

Reflected ray mirror ki surface ke saath 70° ka angle banati hai. Find r, i aur θ.

- r = 90° − 70° = **20°**.
- Law 1: i = **20°**.
- θ = 20° + 20° = **40°**.

Jaanch: reflected ray ka normal se angle 20°, surface se 70°, 20° + 70° = 90° ✅. Aur θ = 2 × 20° = 40° ✅

### Example 10: θ diya hai, mirror se angle nikaalo

Incident aur reflected ray ke beech ka angle 110° hai. Find i aur ray ka mirror ki surface se angle.

- i = θ ÷ 2 = 110° ÷ 2 = **55°**.
- Mirror se angle = 90° − 55° = **35°**.

Jaanch: i + r = 55° + 55° = 110° ✅, aur 55° + 35° = 90° ✅

### Example 11 (thoda extra): mirror ghumaane par kya hota hai

*(Ye Class 10 ke saath overlap karta hai.)* Incident ray fixed hai. Shuru mein i = 30°. Ab mirror ko 10° ghumaya jaata hai (is tarah ki i badhta hai), to i = 40° ho jaata hai. Reflected ray kitni ghoomi?

- **Pehle:** i = 30°, to θ = 2 × 30° = **60°**.
- Mirror 10° ghoomta hai, to uska normal bhi 10° ghoomta hai, isliye i = 30° + 10° = **40°**.
- **Baad mein:** θ = 2 × 40° = **80°**.
- Reflected ray ka ghoomna = 80° − 60° = **20°**.

Jaanch: mirror ke 10° ghoomne par reflected ray 20° ghoomi, yaani **mirror ke ghoomne ka dugna** ✅. Ye bahut kaam ki baat hai: **mirror thoda sa tilt karne par reflected ray bahut zyada hil jaati hai**. Isliye Ziva ka gola mirror ke chhote se tilt par deewar par door tak sarak jaata tha.

### Example 12: Ziva ki khidki (kahani ka hal)

Ab Ziva ka sawaal: gole ko khidki par kaise pahunchaye?

Mr. Parekh ne bataya: "Sabse pehle do cheezein dekho: **dhoop kis direction se aa rahi hai** aur **khidki kis direction mein hai**. Mirror ko aise rakho ki uska **normal in dono directions ke theek beech mein (bisect karta hua)** ho."

Kyun? Kyunki Law 1 kehta hai i = r. Normal ke ek taraf incident ray, doosri taraf reflected ray, aur dono normal se **barabar angle** par. To normal ko in dono rays ke theek beech mein hona hi padega.

**Ek chhota sa number example:** maan lo mirror ke point O se dhoop ki direction aur khidki ki direction ke beech ka angle 100° hai. Mirror ko kaise rakhein?

- Normal ko is 100° ko barabar do hisson mein baantna hai: har hissa = 100° ÷ 2 = **50°**.
- Yaani i = **50°** aur r = **50°** hone chahiye.
- Mirror ki surface normal ke perpendicular hogi, to dhoop ki ray aur mirror ki surface ke beech ka angle = 90° − 50° = **40°**. Khidki wali ray aur mirror ki surface ke beech bhi **40°** hoga.

Jaanch: 50° + 50° = 100° ✅, aur 50° + 40° = 90° ✅

Aur agar dhoop normal se 40° par aati ho, to reflected ray normal ke doosri taraf 40° par jaayegi, aur dono rays ke beech 80° banega ✅ (Example 3).

Ziva ne mirror ko haath mein pakadkar thoda-thoda ghumaya (Example 11 yaad karke: bahut chhoti tilt kaafi hai). Khidki ki taraf gola dhire-dhire sarkaaya, aur thodi der baad wo sidha **khidki ke sheeshe par chamak** gaya. Ziva khushi se uchhal padi, "Sir, ye to bilkul math jaisa hai!"

"Math nahi, **physics**," Mr. Parekh hanse, "par haan, ye niyam ka kamaal hai."

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**1. Angle mirror ki surface se naap lena (normal se nahi)**
Sawal mein "ray mirror se 30° ka angle banati hai" ho to galti se i = 30° likh dena galat hai. 
❌ i = 30°. ✅ i = 90° − 30° = 60°. Pehle dekho ki angle kis se diya hai: **normal se ya surface se**.

**2. Ye maanna ki diffused reflection mein reflection ke niyam toot jaate hain**
Rough surface par bhi **har ray** apne point par i = r ko maanti hai. Reflected rays isliye bikhar jaati hain kyunki har point par normal ki direction alag hai.

**3. Incident ray aur reflected ray mein confuse hona**
Incident ray surface ki taraf **aati** hai, reflected ray surface se **jaati** hai. Sketch mein arrow dekhkar hi pehchano ki kaun si kaun si hai. Light kab aati hai, kab jaati hai, ye arrow batata hai.

**4. Ye samajhna ki sirf chamakdaar cheezein light reflect karti hain**
Sab kuch **kuch na kuch light reflect** karta hai. Hume kitaab, deewar, apna dost, sab isliye dikhte hain kyunki wo light ko (diffused reflection se) hamari aankh ki taraf bhejte hain. Chamakdaar surface sirf **regular reflection** karta hai, aur isliye image banti hai.

**5. Normal ko surface ke saath-saath kheench dena**
Normal surface ke **perpendicular (90°)** hota hai, surface ke saath-saath nahi. Aap surface ke saath-saath line banakar angle naapoge to i ka sahi maan nahi milega.

## Quick Recap

- **Reflection:** light ka surface se takra kar usi medium mein wapas uchhalna; direction badal jaati hai.
- Terms: **incident ray, reflected ray, point of incidence, normal** (surface ke perpendicular, imaginary line), **plane of incidence**.
- **i** = incident ray aur **normal** ke beech ka angle. **r** = reflected ray aur **normal** ke beech ka angle. Dono **normal se** naape jaate hain.
- **Law 1:** ∠i = ∠r.
- **Law 2:** incident ray, reflected ray aur normal **ek hi plane** mein hote hain.
- **Path reversible** hai: reflected ray ke raste se light bhejo to wo incident ray ke raste se wapas aati hai.
- **Normal incidence:** i = 0°, r = 0°; ray apne raste par wapas.
- **Regular reflection:** smooth surface, parallel rays parallel hi reflect, saaf image. **Diffused reflection:** rough surface, rays alag-alag direction mein; **phir bhi har ray i = r maanti hai**. Isiliye non-shining cheezein har taraf se dikhti hain.
- Incident aur reflected ray ke beech ka angle **θ = 2i**. Ray aur mirror surface ke beech ka angle = **90° − i**.
- *(Thoda extra)* Mirror θ_m se ghoome (ray fixed) to reflected ray **2θ_m** se ghoomti hai.
- Ray ko kisi target par bhejna ho to mirror ka **normal** incident aur target wali ray ko **bisect** kare.

## Practice Questions

1. A ray of light falls on a plane mirror at an angle of incidence of 48°. Find (a) the angle of reflection, (b) the angle between the incident and reflected rays, (c) the angle between the reflected ray and the mirror surface.
2. A ray of light makes an angle of 20° with the surface of a mirror. Find the angle of incidence, the angle of reflection and the angle between the incident and reflected rays.
3. The angle between an incident ray and the reflected ray is 90°. Find the angle of incidence and the angle that the incident ray makes with the mirror surface.
4. State true or false and give a one-line reason: (a) The angle of incidence is measured between the incident ray and the mirror surface. (b) In diffused reflection the laws of reflection are not followed. (c) For a ray falling along the normal, the angle of reflection is 0°. (d) Only shiny objects reflect light.
5. *(Thoda extra)* A ray falls on a mirror at an angle of incidence of 20°. The mirror is now turned by 15° (the incident ray stays fixed) so that the angle of incidence becomes 35°. Find the angle between the incident and reflected rays before and after turning, and by what angle the reflected ray has turned.
6. Classify as regular or diffused reflection: (a) a steel spoon, (b) a cotton shirt, (c) a still pond, (d) a rough stone wall.
7. A ray goes from point A to the mirror at O and reflects to point B. The angle of incidence is 30°. If light is sent from B to O, along which path will it go and what will be the angle of incidence?
8. A ray falls on a mirror and the reflected ray makes 35° with the mirror surface. Find i, r and the angle between the incident and the reflected ray.
9. Explain in two or three lines why a rough wall can be seen from every corner of a room, while a mirror in sunlight throws a bright patch of light in only one direction.
10. A ray falls on a mirror along the normal. Find i, r, the angle between the incident and reflected rays, and the angle between the ray and the mirror.

### Answers

1. (a) Law 1: r = i = **48°**.
   (b) θ = 2i = 2 × 48° = **96°**.
   (c) 90° − 48° = **42°**.
   Jaanch: 48° + 48° = 96° ✅, 48° + 42° = 90° ✅

2. i = 90° − 20° = **70°**. r = **70°**. θ = 2 × 70° = **140°**.
   Jaanch: 70° + 70° = 140° ✅, 20° + 70° = 90° ✅

3. i = θ ÷ 2 = 90° ÷ 2 = **45°**. Mirror se angle = 90° − 45° = **45°**.
   Jaanch: 45° + 45° = 90° ✅

4. (a) **False** ❌. i normal se naapa jaata hai, surface se nahi.
   (b) **False** ❌. Har ray par i = r lagu hota hai; surface khurdari hone se normal alag-alag ho jaate hain.
   (c) **True** ✅. Normal ke saath-saath girne par i = 0°, to r = 0°.
   (d) **False** ❌. Sab kuch kuch na kuch light reflect karta hai (diffused reflection); isi se hume cheezein dikhti hain.

5. Pehle: θ = 2 × 20° = **40°**. Baad mein: θ = 2 × 35° = **70°**. Reflected ray ka ghoomna = 70° − 40° = **30°**, jo mirror ke ghoomne (15°) ka dugna hai.
   Jaanch: 2 × 15° = 30° ✅

6. (a) Steel spoon: **regular** (smooth aur polished). (b) Cotton shirt: **diffused** (dhaagon ki khurdari sateh). (c) Still pond: **regular** (shaant, smooth sateh). (d) Rough stone wall: **diffused**.

7. Path reversible hai, to light B se O tak jaayegi aur phir **O se A ki taraf** (wahi raasta ulta). Angle of incidence **30°** hi rahega, kyunki i = r aur dono jagah badal lete hain.
   Jaanch: pehle i = r = 30°, ab i = 30° aur r = 30° ✅

8. r = 90° − 35° = **55°**. i = r = **55°**. θ = 2 × 55° = **110°**.
   Jaanch: 55° + 55° = 110° ✅, 55° + 35° = 90° ✅

9. Deewar khurdari hai, isliye diffused reflection se light har direction mein bikharti hai, aur wo kisi bhi kone se dikhti hai. Mirror smooth hai, regular reflection se dhoop ek hi direction mein jaati hai, isliye wo chamakta gola sirf ek jagah dikhta hai (aur wahi aankh us direction mein ho to).

10. Normal ke saath-saath ray: i = **0°**, r = **0°**, θ = 2 × 0° = **0°** (dono rays ek hi line par). Ray aur mirror ke beech ka angle = 90° − 0° = **90°**.
    Jaanch: 0° + 90° = 90° ✅
