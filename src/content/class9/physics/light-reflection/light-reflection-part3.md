# Topic 3: Plane Mirror

Pichhle topic mein humne reflection ke laws seekhe: incident ray, reflected ray aur normal ek hi plane mein hote hain, aur **∠i = ∠r** (dono angles normal se naape jaate hain). Ab in laws ko ek asli cheez par chalate hain: sabse aam mirror, yaani **plane (sapaat, flat) mirror**. Hum dekhenge ki us mein image kaise banti hai, wo kaisi dikhti hai, usse bade-bade kaam kaise liye jaate hain, aur ek ambulance par ulte akshar kyun likhe hote hain.

**Is topic mein:** doori (distance) hamesha mirror se naapi jaayegi, aur saari ray diagrams mein mirror ko "#" se dikhaya gaya hai.

## Part A: Plane mirror aur image kya hoti hai

**Plane mirror** ek sapaat reflecting surface hai: seedha-sapaat sheesha jiske peeche chamakdaar polish (ya silver ki parat) hoti hai. Sapaat hone ka matlab ye hai ki pichhle topic ka reflection ka law har point par bilkul waisa hi kaam karta hai.

Hum jis cheez ko dekhte hain use **object** kehte hain, aur mirror mein jo uski tasveer dikhti hai use **image**. Image do tarah ki hoti hai.

### Real image aur virtual image

> **Real image:** jab reflected rays sach mein ek point par **milti** hain, wahan real image banti hai. Real image ko ek **screen** (parda) par pakda ja sakta hai.
> **Virtual image:** jab reflected rays sach mein nahi milti, par peeche badhane par (extend karne par) kisi point se **aati hui lagti hain**, wahan virtual image banti hai. Ise screen par **nahi pakda ja sakta**.

Plane mirror ki image **virtual** hoti hai. Kyun? Reflected rays to mirror se wapas aapki taraf aati hain. Wo mirror ke **peeche** kahin milti nahi (mirror ke peeche to sheesha aur deewar hai, wahan light jaati hi nahi). Hamari aankh bas itna dekhti hai ki rays peeche ke ek point se aati hui lag rahi hain, isliye hume wahan image dikhti hai. Agar tum mirror ke peeche jaake wahan screen rakho, to us par kuch nahi dikhega.

(Real image kab banti hai, ye hum aage ke topics mein dekhenge.)

## Part B: Plane mirror mein image kaise banti hai

Ek **point object O** lo, jise mirror ke saamne rakha gaya hai. Object se rays har taraf jaati hain. Hum sirf **do rays** lekar chalte hain, kyunki do rays ka milan point hi kaafi hai.

- **Ray 1:** O se mirror par **seedhi (normal ki direction mein)** jaati hai. Normal ke saath angle 0° hai, to ∠r bhi 0° hai, aur ray wapas usi raaste laut aati hai.
- **Ray 2:** O se mirror par tirchi jaati hai. Mirror par jis point par girti hai, wahan normal banao. ∠r = ∠i ke hisaab se wo doosri taraf utne hi angle par reflect hoti hai.

Ab dono reflected rays ko mirror ke **peeche** dotted line se badhao. Wo dono ek point I par milti hain. Aankh ko lagta hai ki rays wahin I se aa rahi hain, isliye **I hi image hai**.

```
                              #/
                              #/
                              #/
      O-----►---------◄-------#/......................I
         \                    #/                 .
             \                #/             .
                 \            #/         .
                     \        #/     .
                         \    #/ .
                             \#/
                           /  #/
                       /      #/
                   /          #/
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Point object O (baayen taraf) se do rays: ray 1 seedhi mirror par jaakar usi raaste wapas aati hai, ray 2 tirchi jaakar ∠i = ∠r ke hisaab se neeche ki taraf reflect hoti hai. "#" mirror hai aur "/" mirror ka peeche ka hissa. Reflected rays ko peeche badhane par (dotted line "....") wo I par milti hain: I virtual image hai, O se mirror ke peeche utni hi doori par.

Is figure mein ek baat dhyan se dekho: I ki mirror se doori wahi hai jo O ki mirror se hai, aur O se I ko jodne wali seedhi line mirror ke saath 90° banati hai. Ray 1 isi line par chal rahi hai. Aage ye hi hum properties mein dekhenge.

## Part C: Plane mirror mein image ki properties

> 1. Image **virtual** hoti hai (screen par nahi aati).
> 2. Image **erect (seedhi)** hoti hai.
> 3. Image ka size object ke **barabar** hota hai.
> 4. Image mirror ke peeche utni hi doori par banti hai jitni doori par object mirror ke saamne hai: **image distance = object distance**.
> 5. Image **laterally inverted** hoti hai (left-right badal jaate hain).
> 6. Object aur image ko jodne wali line mirror ke **perpendicular (90°)** hoti hai.

### Object aur image ke beech ki doori

Ye sabse aam galti ki jagah hai. Agar object mirror se **d** doori par hai, to image mirror ke peeche **d** doori par hai. To object aur image ke beech ki doori:

> **Object se image ki doori = d + d = 2 × d**

```
   Insaan                  Mirror                  Image
     O ◄────── 1.5 m ──────► # ◄────── 1.5 m ──────► I
                             #
     ◄──────────────── 3 m ────────────────►
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Insaan mirror se 1.5 m door hai, to image mirror ke peeche 1.5 m par hai. Insaan aur image ke beech kul doori 1.5 m + 1.5 m = 3 m. Insaan aur image ko jodne wali line mirror ke saath 90° banati hai.

**Jaanch:** 2 × 1.5 m = 3 m ✅

### Chalte hue: speed ka sawaal

Maan lo insaan mirror ki taraf **1 m/s** se chal raha hai. Har second mein uski mirror se doori 1 m kam hoti hai. Image ki mirror se doori bhi object ki doori ke barabar rehti hai, isliye image bhi har second mein mirror ki taraf **1 m** aati hai.

- Mirror ke hisaab se: object ki speed = 1 m/s, image ki speed = 1 m/s.
- Dono ek doosre ki taraf aa rahe hain, to **ek doosre ke hisaab se** unki doori har second mein 1 m + 1 m = 2 m kam hoti hai. Relative speed = **2 m/s**.

Ye is liye bhi sahi hai ki doori = 2 × d, aur d har second mein 1 m ghatta hai, to 2 × d har second mein 2 m ghatta hai.

### Lateral inversion: left aur right ka badalna

Mirror ke saamne apna **daaya (right) haath** uthao. Image ka jo haath uthta dikhta hai, wo image ke **baayen (left)** taraf wala dikhta hai, jaise tumhara apna baayan haath uthaya gaya ho. Yaani image mein left aur right badal jaate hain. Isse **lateral inversion** (side ka ulat-pulat) kehte hain.

Dhyan do: **upar-neeche nahi badalta.** Mirror mein tumhara sir upar hi dikhta hai aur pair neeche. Isliye image **erect** hai, lekin left-right badla hua hai.

Isi wajah se mirror ke saamne likha hua shabd bhi ulta dikhta hai: **akshar daayen se baayen ke bajaye ulte kram (order) mein** dikhte hain, aur har akshar ka shape bhi aadha ulta hota hai (jaise B, N, C, E, L ka shape badal jaata hai, jabki A, M, U jaise symmetric akshar waise hi dikhte hain).

## Part D: Poori height dekhne ke liye mirror kitna bada?

Tum apna poora sir se pair tak ka image dekhna chahte ho. Kya iske liye poori height ka mirror chahiye? Nahi. **Aadhi height ka mirror kaafi hai.**

**Simple argument.** Maan lo aankh lagbhag sir ke top ke baraabar height par hai (baad mein exact check karenge). Sir se aankh tak ki ray mirror par seedhi (normal) jaakar wapas aati hai, isliye mirror ka **upar wala kinara** bas sir ki height par chahiye. Ab pair se aankh tak: pair se chali ray mirror ke ek point par girti hai aur ∠i = ∠r ke hisaab se aankh tak pahunchti hai. Equal angles ki wajah se ye point pair aur aankh ki height ke **theek beech mein** hota hai. Yaani mirror ka **neeche wala kinara** pair aur aankh ki height ke beech mein chahiye, jo (aankh sir ke top par ho to) aadhi height par hota hai. To mirror ki lambai = sir ki height − aadhi height = **aadhi height**.

```
    O ------►-------------◄-------------#   <- mirror ka upar wala kinara (height 8)
    :         \                         #
    :                  \                #
    :                           \       #
    :                                  /#   <- mirror ka neeche wala kinara (height 4)
    :                          /
    :                 /
    :        /
    _   <- pair (height 0)
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Insaan (baayen taraf, sir aur aankh O par). Sir se chali ray seedhi mirror par jaakar wapas aati hai. Pair se chali ray (neeche se) mirror ke us point par girti hai jo height 4 par hai (pair 0 aur aankh 8 ke theek beech) aur ∠i = ∠r se aankh tak pahunchti hai. Mirror sirf height 4 se height 8 tak chahiye: poori height 8 ka aadha.

**Exact check (aankh sir se thodi neeche).** Insaan ki height H = 1.6 m aur aankh ki height E = 1.5 m maano. Sir ki ray ke liye mirror ka point = (H + E) ÷ 2 = (1.6 m + 1.5 m) ÷ 2 = 3.1 m ÷ 2 = 1.55 m. Pair ki ray ke liye point = E ÷ 2 = 1.5 m ÷ 2 = 0.75 m. Mirror ki lambai = 1.55 m − 0.75 m = **0.8 m** = 1.6 m ÷ 2 ✅. Neeche ka kinara pair se 0.75 m ooncha chahiye.

> **Minimum mirror height = (insaan ki height) ÷ 2**

**Zaroori baat:** ye lambai insaan ke mirror se **doori par depend nahi karti.** Tum mirror ke paas khade ho ya door, 1.6 m ke insaan ke liye 0.8 m ka mirror kaafi hai. Wajah: mirror par ray ke girne wale points ki sirf **height** (ooncha-neechai) upar likhe calculation mein aati hai, doori nahi. Haan, mirror ko **sahi height par lagana** zaroori hai: neeche ka kinara aankh ki height ka aadha (jaise 0.75 m) hona chahiye, warna poora image nahi dikhega.

## Part E: Do mirrors se kai images

Do plane mirrors ko ek doosre ke saath angle **θ** par rakho, aur beech mein ek object. Pehle mirror ki image, doosre mirror ke liye ek naya object ban jaati hai, aur uski bhi image banti hai. Is tarah kai images ban jaati hain.

> **Images ki sankhya: n = (360° ÷ θ) − 1**, jab 360° ÷ θ ek **even (jodi) sankhya** ho. Tab object mirrors ke beech kahin bhi ho, jawab yahi aata hai.

(Thoda extra: jab 360° ÷ θ **odd** sankhya ho, jaise θ = 72° ke liye 360° ÷ 72° = 5, tab jawab object ki jagah par depend karta hai. Object **theek beech mein** ho (dono mirrors se barabar angle par, symmetric placement), tab hi 5 − 1 = **4** images banti hain. Object beech se hat kar ho to 5 images banti hain. Hum is topic mein bas even waale aam cases tak rahenge.)

| θ | 360° ÷ θ | n = (360° ÷ θ) − 1 |
|---|---|---|
| 90° | 4 | 3 |
| 60° | 6 | 5 |
| 45° | 8 | 7 |
| 30° | 12 | 11 |
| 72° | 5 (odd) | 4, sirf jab object theek beech mein ho |

```
                        # Mirror M1 (#)
                        #
                        #
            I1          #           O
                        #
                        #
                        #                   Mirror M2 (=)
  ......................+===========================
                        :
                        :
                        :
            I3          :           I2
                        :
                        :
                        :
```

> **Figure 3.4** – Do mirrors M1 aur M2, 90° par. Object O ke saamne M1 ki image I1, M2 ki image I2, aur teesri image I3 (I1 ya I2 ki image). Kul 3 images: n = 360° ÷ 90° − 1 = 3. Dotted ("....", ":") hissa mirrors ka peeche ka virtual hissa hai.

**Parallel mirrors:** jab θ = 0° (mirrors ek doosre ke parallel), 360° ÷ θ ka koi seemit maan nahi, aur images **anginat (infinite)** ban jaati hain. Barber ki dukaan mein aamne-saamne ki deewaron par lage mirrors mein tum ek ke baad ek images ki lambi kataar dekhte ho. Har baar image thodi **dheemi (faint)** hoti jaati hai, kyunki har reflection mein thodi light mirror mein hi kho jaati hai, isliye door ki images dikhna band ho jaati hain.

**Kaleidoscope:** ek khilona (aur kala ka saadhan) jisme do ya teen mirrors ek angle par lage hote hain, aur unke beech rang-birange chhote tukde. Bahut saari images milkar sundar patterns banati hain. Ghumao to pattern badal jaata hai.

## Part F: Periscope: deewar ke upar se dekhna

**Periscope** ek lambi nali hai, jiske upar aur neeche ek-ek plane mirror lagaya jaata hai. Dono mirrors ek doosre ke **parallel** hote hain, aur nali ke saath **45°** ka angle banate hain.

```
                ┌─────────────┐
                │   Mirror 1  │
                │             │
Object  ----►----------\      │
                │      ↓      │
                │      ↓      │
                │      ↓      │
                │      ↓      │
                │      ↓      │
                │      ↓      │
                │      \------------►----- Aankh
                │   Mirror 2  │
                │             │
                └─────────────┘
```

> **Figure 3.5** – Periscope. Object se aati ray upar wale mirror 1 (45° par) se reflect hokar neeche jaati hai, phir neeche wale mirror 2 (45° par) se reflect hokar aankh tak aati hai. Do reflections ke baad bhi image **seedhi (erect)** dikhti hai.

Isse hum deewar, bheed ya kisi rukawat ke **upar se** dekh sakte hain. **Submarine** mein periscope se paani ke upar ka manzar dekha jaata hai. Bunker (surakshit morcha) mein bhi iska upyog hota hai.

## Part H: Solved examples

### Example 1: Object aur image ke beech ki doori

Ek insaan plane mirror se 2 m door khada hai. Uske aur image ke beech ki doori kya hai?

Image mirror ke peeche 2 m par hai.
Doori = 2 m + 2 m = **4 m** (ya 2 × 2 m = 4 m).

Jaanch: 2 × 2 m = 4 m ✅

### Example 2: Insaan 1 m paas aaya

Insaan mirror se 3 m door khada hai. Phir 1 m mirror ki taraf aa jaata hai. Pehle aur baad mein object-image doori nikalo.

Pehle: 2 × 3 m = **6 m**.
Baad mein: mirror se doori = 3 m − 1 m = 2 m, to object-image doori = 2 × 2 m = **4 m**.

Jaanch: doori 6 m − 4 m = 2 m kam hui, jo 2 × 1 m ke barabar hai ✅

### Example 3: Image ki speed aur relative speed

Insaan mirror ki taraf 1 m/s se chal raha hai. Image ki mirror ke hisaab se speed aur insaan ke hisaab se image ki speed nikalo.

Mirror ke hisaab se image ki speed = **1 m/s** (image ki doori object ki doori ke barabar rehti hai).
Insaan ke hisaab se: insaan aur image ek doosre ki taraf aa rahe hain, to relative speed = 1 m/s + 1 m/s = **2 m/s**.

Jaanch: object-image doori 2 × d hai, d har second 1 m ghatta hai, to doori har second 2 × 1 m = 2 m ghatti hai ✅

### Example 4: 1.5 m/s se 2 s tak

Insaan mirror se 5 m door hai aur 1.5 m/s se mirror ki taraf 2 s chalta hai. Object-image doori kitni hui?

Chala hua = 1.5 m/s × 2 s = 3 m.
Nayi doori = 5 m − 3 m = 2 m, to object-image doori = 2 × 2 m = **4 m**.
Shuru mein doori = 2 × 5 m = 10 m.

Jaanch: doori 10 m − 4 m = 6 m ghati ho 2 s mein, yaani 6 m ÷ 2 s = 3 m/s = 2 × 1.5 m/s ✅

### Example 5: 1.6 m ke insaan ke liye mirror

Minimum mirror height = 1.6 m ÷ 2 = **0.8 m**.
Jaanch: (1.6 m + 1.5 m) ÷ 2 − 1.5 m ÷ 2 = 1.55 m − 0.75 m = 0.8 m ✅

### Example 6: 1.8 m ke insaan ke liye mirror

Minimum mirror height = 1.8 m ÷ 2 = **0.9 m**.
Jaanch: aankh 1.7 m par ho to (1.8 m + 1.7 m) ÷ 2 − 1.7 m ÷ 2 = 1.75 m − 0.85 m = 0.9 m ✅. Ye lambai insaan ke mirror se door ya paas hone se nahi badalti.

### Example 7: Images ki sankhya

n = (360° ÷ θ) − 1:

- θ = 90°: 360° ÷ 90° = 4, n = 4 − 1 = **3**
- θ = 60°: 360° ÷ 60° = 6, n = 6 − 1 = **5**
- θ = 45°: 360° ÷ 45° = 8, n = 8 − 1 = **7**
- θ = 30°: 360° ÷ 30° = 12, n = 12 − 1 = **11**

Jaanch: θ jitna chhota, images utni zyada ✅

### Example 8: Angle nikalna

Do mirrors se 5 images banti hain. Unke beech ka angle nikalo.

n = (360° ÷ θ) − 1, to 5 = (360° ÷ θ) − 1, 360° ÷ θ = 6, θ = 360° ÷ 6 = **60°**.

Jaanch: 360° ÷ 60° − 1 = 6 − 1 = 5 ✅

### Example 9: Do parallel mirrors

Mirrors A aur B ek doosre se 4 m door aamne-saamne hain. Object A se 1 m door (B se 3 m door) hai. Pehli images ki object se doori nikalo.

Coordinates lo (m mein): A x = 0 par, B x = 4 par, object x = 1 par.

- A mein image: A se 1 m peeche, x = −1 par. Object se doori = 1 − (−1) = **2 m**.
- B mein image: B se 3 m peeche, x = 4 + 3 = 7 par. Object se doori = 7 − 1 = **6 m**.

```
                  A       B
                  #       #
    I           I # O     #     I   I
                  #       #
   -7          -1 0 1     4     7   9
(x in m)
```

> **Figure 3.6** – Do parallel mirrors A (x = 0) aur B (x = 4), object O x = 1 par. Pehli images x = −1 (A mein) aur x = 7 (B mein). Doosri images x = −7 aur x = 9, aur bhi aage tak images banti jaati hain.

Jaanch: image ki mirror se doori = object ki mirror se doori: A ke liye 1 m = 1 m ✅, B ke liye 3 m = 3 m ✅. Agli images: A mein x = 7 ki image x = −7 par (object se 8 m), B mein x = −1 ki image 2 × 4 − (−1) = 9 par (object se 8 m) ✅.

### Example 10: Mirror mein ghadi (clock)

Ghadi mirror mein 3:00 dikhati hai. Asli samay kya hai?

Asli samay = 12:00 − mirror ka samay = 12:00 − 3:00 = **9:00**.

Jaanch: 9:00 ki ghadi mirror mein left-right badal ke 3:00 jaisi dikhegi ✅

### Example 11: Mirror mein 8:20

Mirror mein ghadi 8:20 dikha rahi hai. Asli samay kya hai?

Asli samay = 11:60 − 8:20 = **3:40**.

Jaanch: 3:40 ko mirror mein dekho: 11:60 − 3:40 = 8:20 ✅

### Example 12: Mirror khiskana

Insaan mirror se 2 m door khada hai. Mirror ko insaan se 1 m aur door khiska diya (insaan wahin hai). Image kitni khiski?

Pehle: image mirror ke peeche 2 m par, insaan se 4 m door.
Baad mein: mirror se doori = 2 m + 1 m = 3 m, to image mirror ke peeche 3 m par, insaan se 2 × 3 m = 6 m door.
Image ki nayi position insaan se 6 m, pehle 4 m, to image **2 m** khiski.

Jaanch: mirror 1 m khiska, image 2 × 1 m = 2 m khiski ✅

### Example 13: AMBULANCE ko mirror mein dekhna

Mirror ke saamne AMBULANCE seedha likha ho. Mirror mein kya dikhega?

Shabd ke 9 akshar hain: A, M, B, U, L, A, N, C, E. Mirror mein akshar **ulte kram mein** dikhte hain: pehle E, phir C, N, A, L, U, B, M, A, aur har akshar left-right se palta hua. Isliye B, L, N, C, E ke shape badal jaate hain, jabki A, M, U waise hi dikhte hain.

Jaanch: akshar 9 hi hain, bas kram ulta hua ✅

---

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Ye sochna ki image object se chhoti ya badi hoti hai.** ❌ Plane mirror mein image ka size object ke **barabar** hota hai, chahe tum paas ho ya door. Door se chhoti dikhne ka kaaran doori hai, mirror nahi.
2. **Ye sochna ki image ko screen par pakda ja sakta hai.** ❌ Plane mirror ki image **virtual** hai, screen par nahi aati.
3. **Lateral inversion ko upside-down samajhna.** ❌ Image **seedhi (erect)** hoti hai, sirf left-right badalte hain. Mirror mein sir upar hi dikhta hai.
4. **Object-image doori ko sirf object ki mirror se doori maan lena.** ❌ Agar object 1.5 m par hai, to object aur image ke beech ki doori 1.5 m nahi, **3 m** hai (2 × 1.5 m).
5. **Ye sochna ki minimum mirror height insaan ki mirror se doori par depend karti hai.** ❌ Wo hamesha insaan ki height ki **aadhi** hai, doori chahe jo ho.
6. **Inclined mirrors mein images ki sankhya ko θ se alag maan lena.** ❌ θ jitna chhota, images utni zyada: n = (360° ÷ θ) − 1. 90° par 3 images, 60° par 5 images.

---

## Quick Recap

- **Plane mirror** = sapaat reflecting surface. Isme ∠i = ∠r har point par chalta hai.
- **Real image:** reflected rays sach mein milti hain, screen par aati hai. **Virtual image:** rays peeche se aati hui lagti hain, screen par nahi aati.
- Plane mirror ki image: **virtual, erect, object ke barabar size, mirror ke peeche utni hi doori par** (image distance = object distance), **laterally inverted**, aur object-image ko jodne wali line mirror ke **perpendicular**.
- Object se image ki doori = **2 × (object ki mirror se doori)**. 1.5 m par 3 m.
- Object 1 m/s se mirror ki taraf chale to image bhi 1 m/s se, aur **relative speed 2 m/s**.
- Poori height dekhne ke liye minimum mirror height = **insaan ki height ÷ 2**, aur ye mirror se doori par depend nahi karti.
- Do mirrors θ angle par: **n = (360° ÷ θ) − 1** (jab 360° ÷ θ even ho; odd ho to sirf object ke theek beech mein hone par). Parallel mirrors mein anginat images.
- **Periscope:** do parallel mirrors, 45° par: rukawat ke upar se dekhna. **Kaleidoscope:** inclined mirrors se patterns.
- Mirror ghadi: asli samay = 11:60 − mirror ka samay.

## Practice Questions

1. A person stands 3.5 m in front of a plane mirror. How far is the image from the mirror, and how far is the image from the person?
2. A boy walks towards a plane mirror at 0.8 m/s. (a) What is the speed of his image relative to the mirror? (b) What is the speed of the image relative to the boy? (c) He starts 4 m from the mirror. After 2 s, how far is he from his image?
3. What is the minimum height of a plane mirror in which a girl 1.5 m tall can see her full image? Does the answer change if she moves 2 m farther from the mirror?
4. Find the number of images formed by two plane mirrors inclined at (a) 20° and (b) 36°.
5. Two plane mirrors form 11 images of an object. What is the angle between them?
6. Two parallel mirrors A and B are 6 m apart. An object is 2 m from mirror A (between the mirrors). Find the distance of the first image in A and the first image in B from the object.
7. A clock in a mirror appears to show 4:25. What is the actual time?
8. State true or false and give a one-line reason: (a) A plane mirror image is smaller if the person stands farther. (b) The image in a plane mirror can be caught on a screen. (c) Lateral inversion means the image is upside down. (d) The minimum mirror height for a full-length image depends on how far the person stands.
9. Why is the word AMBULANCE written in mirror-reversed form on the front of an ambulance? Which letters of this word look the same when reversed left to right?
10. In a periscope, how are the two mirrors placed? Is the final image erect or inverted? Name one place where periscopes are used.

### Answers

1. Image distance from the mirror = **3.5 m** (image distance = object distance). Distance from the person = 3.5 m + 3.5 m = **7 m**.
   Jaanch: 2 × 3.5 m = 7 m ✅

2. (a) **0.8 m/s**. (b) 0.8 m/s + 0.8 m/s = **1.6 m/s**. (c) 2 s mein chala hua = 0.8 m/s × 2 s = 1.6 m. Nayi doori = 4 m − 1.6 m = 2.4 m. Boy aur image ke beech = 2 × 2.4 m = **4.8 m**.
   Jaanch: shuru mein 8 m tha, 8 m − 4.8 m = 3.2 m kam hua, 3.2 m ÷ 2 s = 1.6 m/s ✅

3. Minimum height = 1.5 m ÷ 2 = **0.75 m**. Nahi badalta: mirror se doori par depend nahi karta, chahe wo 2 m aur door ho jaye, 0.75 m hi chahiye.
   Jaanch: aankh 1.4 m par ho to (1.5 m + 1.4 m) ÷ 2 − 1.4 m ÷ 2 = 1.45 m − 0.7 m = 0.75 m ✅

4. (a) 360° ÷ 20° = 18 (even), n = 18 − 1 = **17**. (b) 360° ÷ 36° = 10 (even), n = 10 − 1 = **9**.
   Jaanch: 17 + 1 = 18, 18 × 20° = 360° ✅; 9 + 1 = 10, 10 × 36° = 360° ✅

5. 11 = (360° ÷ θ) − 1, to 360° ÷ θ = 12, θ = 360° ÷ 12 = **30°**.
   Jaanch: 360° ÷ 30° − 1 = 12 − 1 = 11 ✅

6. Coordinates: A x = 0, B x = 6, object x = 2. Image in A: x = −2, object se doori = 2 − (−2) = **4 m**. Image in B: x = 2 × 6 − 2 = 10, object se doori = 10 − 2 = **8 m**.
   Jaanch: 2 × (object ki A se doori 2 m) = 4 m ✅, 2 × (object ki B se doori 4 m) = 8 m ✅

7. Asli samay = 11:60 − 4:25 = **7:35**.
   Jaanch: 11:60 − 7:35 = 4:25 ✅

8. (a) **False** ❌. Image ka size hamesha object ke barabar hai. (b) **False** ❌. Virtual image screen par nahi aati. (c) **False** ❌. Image erect hai, sirf left-right badalte hain. (d) **False** ❌. Wo hamesha insaan ki height ka aadha hai.

9. Mirror (rear-view mirror) lateral inversion karta hai. Agar naam ulta likha ho, to aage wale driver ko mirror mein wo **seedha** dikhta hai aur wo jaldi samajh kar raasta deta hai. AMBULANCE ke akshar **A, M, U** (aur doosra A) left-right ulatne par bhi waise hi dikhte hain; B, L, N, C, E ke shape badal jaate hain.

10. Dono mirrors ek doosre ke **parallel** hote hain aur nali ke saath **45°** par hote hain. Final image **erect** hai. Submarine ya bunker mein upyog hota hai.
