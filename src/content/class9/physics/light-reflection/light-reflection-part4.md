# Topic 4: Spherical Mirrors: Concave aur Convex

Pichhle topic mein humne plane (sapaat) mirror dekha: uski image hamesha virtual, seedhi (erect) aur object ke barabar size ki thi, aur image mirror ke peeche utni hi door thi jitna object aage. Plane mirror ki ek hi "personality" hai: sapaat hone ki wajah se wo hamesha ek jaisa behave karta hai. Ab sawal ye hai ki **agar mirror sapaat na ho, balki thoda sa muda hua (curved) ho, to kya hoga?** Is topic mein hum curved mirrors ke do roop, concave aur convex, unke important points (pole, centre of curvature, focus) aur unki teen-chaar khaas kirnein seekhenge. Image kaise banti hai, wo agle topic mein.

**Is poore topic mein: sirf paraxial kirnein (mirror ke axis ke bahut kareeb waali kirnein) lenge, aur mirror ka aperture chhota maanenge.** Isse saari baatein saaf aur simple rehti hain.

## Dhaba ka chamakta chammach

Highway ke kinare ek dhaba tha. Dhaba ke maalik Mr. Saluja chai chhaan rahe the. Paas hi ek bench par Prisha baithi thi, jo school trip se laut rahi thi aur garam chai ka intezaar kar rahi thi.

Mr. Saluja ne steel ka ek chamakta hua chammach (spoon) Prisha ko thama diya, "Lo beta, chai mein cheeni mila lo."

Prisha ne chammach ko apne chehre ke saamne pakda. Chammach ke **andar wale gol hisse (bowl)** mein usne apna chehra dekha: chehra **chhota** tha aur **ulta (upside down)**! Usne hairaani se chammach ko palta. Ab **peeche wali (bahar ki)** side mein bhi apna chehra dikha: is baar chehra **chhota** tha, par **seedha (upright)**.

Prisha ne poochha, "Saluja uncle, ye kya jaadu hai? Ek hi chammach hai, par ek side mein meri shakal ulti aur doosri side mein seedhi kyun dikhti hai?"

Mr. Saluja hanse, "Bitiya, chammach ki dono side ek jaisi nahi hain. Ek side andar ki taraf dabi hui hai aur doosri bahar ki taraf ubhri hui. Inhein physics mein alag-alag naam milte hain, aur dono ke alag-alag points bhi hote hain. Pehle wo naam aur points samajh lo, phir main tumhe wapas is chammach par le aaunga." Is kahani ko hum topic ke beech mein solve karenge.

## Part A: Spherical mirror kya hai, aur uske do roop

Ek khokhla (hollow) gola socho, jaise ek bada sa football ya ek kaanch ki gend. Ab us gole ko kaat kar ek **chhota sa tukda** nikaal lo, jaise santre ka ek chhilka. Agar is tukde ki **ek side par chamakti parat** (polish ya chandi) laga di jaye, to wo **spherical mirror** ban jaata hai.

> **Spherical mirror** = aisa mirror jiski reflecting surface kisi khokhle gole ka ek hissa ho.

Is tukde ki do side hoti hain: ek andar ki (jo gole ke centre ki taraf hai) aur ek bahar ki. Chamakti parat kis side par hai, isse mirror ka roop badal jaata hai.

### 1. Concave mirror

Jab **andar ki side** chamakti ho, yaani reflecting surface **gole ke centre ki taraf dabi hui** ho:

> **Concave mirror** = reflecting surface andar ki taraf dabi hui (jaise katori ya chammach ka bowl). Isse **converging mirror** bhi kehte hain, kyunki ye parallel kirnein ko ek point par ikattha (converge) karta hai. (Ye aage dekhenge.)

### 2. Convex mirror

Jab **bahar ki side** chamakti ho, yaani reflecting surface **bahar ki taraf ubhri hui** ho:

> **Convex mirror** = reflecting surface bahar ki taraf ubhri hui (jaise chammach ki peeth ya ulti katori). Isse **diverging mirror** bhi kehte hain, kyunki ye parallel kirnein ko failata (diverge) hai.

Pehchanne ka ek aasaan tarika: **concave mein "cave" hai, yaani gufa (cave) jaise andar ki taraf dabi hui.** Convex is se ulta, ubhra hua.

Ab ek-ek shabd:

### 1. Pole (P)

> **Pole (P)** = mirror ki reflecting surface ka beech wala point.

Mirror ki surface par sabse beech ka point. Ye hamesha mirror par hi hota hai. Isse hum **doori naapne ka starting point (origin)** maante hain.

### 2. Centre of curvature (C) aur radius of curvature (R)

> **Centre of curvature (C)** = us khokhle gole ka centre jiska mirror ek hissa hai.
> **Radius of curvature (R)** = us gole ki radius, yaani **pole se centre of curvature tak ki doori: R = PC.**

Dhyan do: **C mirror par nahi hota.** Concave mirror mein C mirror ke **aage** (chamakti side ki taraf) hota hai. Convex mirror mein C mirror ke **peeche** hota hai. Ye bhi yaad rakho ki **P aur C alag-alag points hain**: P mirror par hai, C gole ke centre par.

### 3. Principal axis

> **Principal axis** = pole P aur centre of curvature C se guzarti hui seedhi rekha.

Ye mirror ki surface ko P par seedha (90° par) kaatti hai. Hum is par saari doori naapte hain.

### 4. Aperture

> **Aperture** = mirror ki reflecting surface ki chaudai (width), yaani surface ke ek kinare (A) se doosre kinare (B) tak.

Chhoti katori ka aperture kam, badi katori ka zyada. Figure 4.1 aur 4.2 mein AB aperture hai.

### 5. Principal focus (F) aur focal length (f)

Ab sabse zaroori point. Ek bahut door ki cheez (jaise Sooraj) se aati hui kirnein **principal axis ke parallel** maani jaati hain. Dekho ye kirnein mirror se takra kar kya karti hain.

**Concave mirror ke liye:** axis ke parallel kirnein reflect hone ke baad **ek point par milti hain.** Wo point mirror ke **aage** hota hai. Isse **principal focus F** kehte hain.

```

> **Figure 4.3** – Concave mirror: principal axis ke parallel (aur axis ke kareeb) kirnein reflect hone ke baad F par milti hain. F mirror ke aage hai, aur wahan kirnein **sach mein** milti hain. Isliye ye **real focus** hai.

**Convex mirror ke liye:** axis ke parallel kirnein reflect hone ke baad **phail jaati hain**, kabhi nahi milti. Par agar hum reflected kirnein ko peeche ki taraf (mirror ke andar ki taraf) dotted line se badhayein, to wo **mirror ke peeche ek point par milti hui lagti hain.** Wo point **principal focus F** hai.

> **Focal length (f)** = pole se principal focus tak ki doori: **f = PF.**

**Real aur virtual focus mein farak:**
- **Real focus** (concave): kirnein sach mein wahan milti hain. Wahan screen rakho to bright spot dikhega.
- **Virtual focus** (convex): kirnein sach mein wahan nahi milti, sirf wahan se aati hui **lagti** hain. Wahan screen rakhne par kuch nahi dikhega.

### 6. f aur R ka rishta: f = R ÷ 2

Chhote aperture ke mirror ke liye ek simple aur bahut kaam ka result hai:

> **f = R ÷ 2**   (focus P aur C ke **theek beech mein** hota hai)

Yaani **PF = FC = f** aur **PC = R = 2f.**

Ye result kyun sahi hai, uska ek chhota sa idea (poora proof aage ki class mein): axis ke parallel ek ray mirror ke point M par girti hai. M par normal wahi hai jo M se centre C tak jaati radius hai. Reflection ka law (∠i = ∠r) lagane se F aur C ke beech ka triangle aisa banta hai jismein FC = FM hota hai. Chhote aperture mein M, P ke bahut kareeb hota hai, isliye FM ≈ FP. Is tarah FC ≈ FP, yaani F theek P aur C ke beech mein aata hai.

Numerical check: agar R = 30 cm, to f = 30 cm ÷ 2 = 15 cm. Yaani PF = 15 cm aur FC = 30 cm − 15 cm = 15 cm. Dono barabar hain, yaani F theek beech mein hai. ✅

**Chhota aperture kyun?** Agar mirror bahut chauda ho, to axis se door girne wali kirnein axis ke parallel hone ke bawajood F par theek se nahi milti: wo F ke thoda paas ya thoda door milti hain, aur image dhundhli ho jaati hai. Isliye is chapter mein aur hamesha mirror ke formulas ke liye hum **chhote aperture** wale mirror aur **axis ke kareeb** ki kirnein lete hain. Is dosh ka naam (spherical aberration) aur use theek karne ke tarike **aage ki class mein** padhoge.

## Part D: Concave aur convex ki tulna

| Baat | Concave mirror | Convex mirror |
|---|---|---|
| Aakaar (shape) | Andar ki taraf dabi hui | Bahar ki taraf ubhri hui |
| Reflecting side | Gole ke andar ki side (centre ki taraf) | Gole ki bahar ki side (centre se door) |
| Doosra naam | Converging mirror | Diverging mirror |
| Parallel kirnein reflect hone ke baad | Ek point F par ikatthi hoti hain | Phail jaati hain |
| Focus ka type | Real focus (mirror ke aage) | Virtual focus (mirror ke peeche) |
| C aur F kahan hain | Mirror ke aage | Mirror ke peeche |
| Field of view (kitna area dikhta hai) | Kam | **Zyada**: convex mirror se bada area ek saath dikhta hai |
| Example | Chammach ka bowl, katori ka andar | Chammach ki peeth, steel ki gend ki bahar ki surface |

Convex mirror ka wide field of view hone ka matlab: usme peeche ka bahut bada area ek saath chhote roop mein dikh jaata hai. (Iska istemaal Topic 6 mein.)

## Part E: Thoda extra (Class 10 overlap): New Cartesian sign convention

Mirror ke numbers (f, R, doori) likhte waqt alag-alag direction mein jaane par +/− lagana padta hai, taaki formula sab ke liye ek jaisa chale. Isko **sign convention** kehte hain. Yahan sirf shuruaat:

1. **Pole P ko origin** (shunya, 0) maanenge.
2. **Incident light baayen se daayen** chalti hai, aur principal axis ko x-axis maanenge.
3. **Light ki direction mein naapi gayi doori positive (+)**, **light ki ulti direction mein naapi gayi doori negative (−).**

Concave mirror ka F aur C mirror ke **aage** hain, yaani us taraf jahan se light aati hai. Light ki **ulti** direction mein naapte hain, isliye ye **negative** hain. Convex mirror ka F aur C mirror ke **peeche** hain, yaani light ki **same** direction mein, isliye ye **positive** hain.

| Mirror | R ka sign | f ka sign | F aur C kahan hain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concave | Negative (−) | Negative (−) | Mirror ke aage |
| Convex | Positive (+) | Positive (+) | Mirror ke peeche |

Yaani **concave mirror ki f aur R dono negative, convex mirror ki dono positive.** f = R ÷ 2 ka rishta sign ke saath bhi sahi rehta hai.

## Part F: Activity: concave mirror ki focal length naapna

Hum Sooraj ko bahut door ki cheez maan sakte hain, to uski kirnein lagbhag parallel hoti hain. Is se concave mirror ki focal length ka andaza lag sakta hai.

**Zaroorat:** ek concave mirror (ya steel ki bahut chamakti katori ka andar ka hissa), ek screen (gatta ya safed kagaz), ek meter-scale.

**Tarika:**
1. Mirror ko haath mein ya stand par pakdo aur Sooraj (ya kisi bahut door ki roshan cheez, jaise dhoop mein chamakti window) ki taraf rakho.
2. Mirror ke saamne screen (gatta) ko aage-peeche karo, jab tak screen par ek **bahut chhota, sabse chamakdaar spot** (sharp bright spot) na dikhne lage.
3. Mirror (pole) aur screen ke beech ki doori meter-scale se naapo.

```
                                          )
  ──────────►───────────────────────────────╱
                                      │   ↙   )
                                      │ ╱      )
──────────────────────────────────────*─────────)─────────────────
                                      │ ╲      )
                                      │   ↖   )
  ──────────►───────────────────────────────╲
                                          )
                                screen (card) F par

                                      ◄─ f=15 ──►
```

> **Figure 4.11** – Sooraj ki parallel kirnein concave mirror se reflect hokar screen par F par ek chhote chamakdaar spot mein ikatthi hoti hain. Mirror se spot tak ki doori lagbhag f hai.

**Sample reading:** mirror se spot ki doori = 15 cm.
- f ≈ **15 cm**
- R = 2 × f = 2 × 15 cm = **30 cm**

Jaanch: f = R ÷ 2 = 30 cm ÷ 2 = 15 cm ✅

**Savdhani:** Sooraj ki taraf seedha mat dekho, aur mirror se reflect hui dhoop kisi ki aankhon par mat daalo. Sirf screen par bane spot ko dekho. Spot bahut garm hota hai, isliye usse kagaz ya kapde ko zyada der mat tapne do.

(Ye doori sirf **lagbhag** f hai, kyunki Sooraj bahut door hai par infinite door nahi, aur mirror ka aperture bhi chhota-bada hota hai.)

## Part G: Solved examples

Is poore Part mein mirror chhote aperture wala hai aur f = R ÷ 2 lagega.

### Example 1: R se f nikaalo

Ek concave mirror ki radius of curvature R = 30 cm hai. Focal length kya hai?

- f = R ÷ 2 = 30 cm ÷ 2 = **15 cm**

Jaanch: 2 × f = 2 × 15 cm = 30 cm = R ✅

### Example 2: f se R nikaalo

Ek convex mirror ki focal length 12 cm hai. Uski radius of curvature kya hai?

- R = 2 × f = 2 × 12 cm = **24 cm**

Jaanch: R ÷ 2 = 24 cm ÷ 2 = 12 cm = f ✅

### Example 3: C aur F ki position

Ek concave mirror ki R = 36 cm hai. Pole se F aur C kitni door hain?

- f = R ÷ 2 = 36 cm ÷ 2 = 18 cm
- F, P se **18 cm** door, mirror ke **aage**.
- C, P se R = **36 cm** door, mirror ke **aage**.
- F aur C ke beech ki doori: FC = 36 cm − 18 cm = **18 cm** = PF

 C                F               P
 +────────────────+───────────────)
 ◄──── 18 cm ────►◄──── 18 cm ───►
 ◄───────────── 36 cm ───────────►
```

> **Figure 4.12** – Concave mirror: R = 36 cm. PF = 18 cm, FC = 18 cm, PC = 36 cm. P, F aur C teeno mirror ke aage axis par hain (P mirror par).

Jaanch: PF + FC = 18 cm + 18 cm = 36 cm = PC ✅

Ek convex mirror ki R = 24 cm ho to f = 24 cm ÷ 2 = 12 cm. F aur C, P se **12 cm** aur **24 cm** door hain, dono mirror ke **peeche**.

Jaanch: PF = 12 cm, FC = 24 cm − 12 cm = 12 cm, dono barabar ✅

### Example 4: ray dekh kar mirror ka type pehchano

(a) Ek mirror par axis ke parallel kirnein girti hain, aur reflect hone ke baad wo mirror se 8 cm aage ek point par milti hain. Kaunsa mirror hai, aur f aur R kya hain?

- Kirnein sach mein milti hain, point mirror ke aage hai: **concave mirror** (real focus).
- f = **8 cm**, R = 2 × 8 cm = **16 cm**.

(b) Ek doosre mirror par axis ke parallel kirnein girti hain, aur reflect hone ke baad wo phail jaati hain, jaise mirror ke peeche 6 cm door ek point se aa rahi hon. Kaunsa mirror hai, aur f aur R?

- Kirnein phail rahi hain, aur virtual focus mirror ke peeche hai: **convex mirror**.
- f = **6 cm**, R = 2 × 6 cm = **12 cm**.

Jaanch: (a) 16 cm ÷ 2 = 8 cm ✅   (b) 12 cm ÷ 2 = 6 cm ✅

### Example 5: sun-image activity se f

Ek student ne concave mirror se Sooraj ka sabse chamakdaar spot screen par banaya. Mirror aur screen ke beech ki doori 12 cm thi. f aur R?

- f ≈ **12 cm**
- R = 2 × 12 cm = **24 cm**

Jaanch: 24 cm ÷ 2 = 12 cm ✅

### Example 6 (thoda extra): sign convention mein f aur R

(a) Ek concave mirror ki radius 20 cm hai. Sign convention ke saath R aur f likho.
(b) Ek convex mirror ki radius 40 cm hai. Sign convention ke saath R aur f likho.

(a) Concave mirror ke liye R aur f dono negative hote hain:
- R = **−20 cm**
- f = R ÷ 2 = −20 cm ÷ 2 = **−10 cm**

(b) Convex mirror ke liye R aur f dono positive hote hain:
- R = **+40 cm**
- f = R ÷ 2 = +40 cm ÷ 2 = **+20 cm**

Jaanch: (a) 2 × (−10 cm) = −20 cm = R ✅   (b) 2 × (+20 cm) = +40 cm = R ✅

### Example 7: aperture aur pole pehchano

Neeche ke figure mein ek mirror ko dikhaya gaya hai. A aur B mirror ki surface ke do kinare hain, M mirror ka beech wala point hai, aur Q ek point hai jo M se 20 cm door principal axis par hai. Q mirror ke aage hai. A aur B ke beech ki seedhi doori 4 cm hai, aur Q ko hi mirror ka centre of curvature maanen. (a) Pole kaun sa hai? (b) Aperture kitna hai? (c) R aur f kya hain?

```
                                 A      ▲
                                  )     │
                                   )    │  AB = 4 cm
 Q ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ ─ M)   ┼
                                   )    │
                                  )     │
                                 B      ▼
 ◄─────────── MQ = 20 cm ──────────►
```

> **Figure 4.13** – Ek concave mirror: A aur B kinare hain, M surface ka beech ka point (pole) hai, aur Q (mirror ke aage, axis par) M se 20 cm door hai.

(a) Surface ka beech wala point hi **pole** hai: **M = P**.
(b) Aperture = AB = **4 cm**.
(c) C = Q, isliye R = PC = MQ = **20 cm**, aur f = 20 cm ÷ 2 = **10 cm**.

Jaanch: aperture (4 cm) R (20 cm) se kaafi chhota hai, 4 ÷ 20 = 0.2, yaani chhota aperture hi hai ✅ (isliye f = R ÷ 2 lag sakta hai). Aur 2 × 10 cm = 20 cm = R ✅

### Example 8: teen kirnein ka tracing

Ek concave mirror ki focal length 10 cm hai. Ek ray principal axis ke parallel hai aur axis se 2 cm upar hai.

(a) Reflect hone ke baad wo kahan jaayegi?
(b) Ek doosri ray centre of curvature se guzarti hui mirror par girti hai. Wo kahan jaayegi? C kahan hai?
(c) Teesri ray F se guzar kar mirror par girti hai. Wo kahan jaayegi?

- (a) Axis ke parallel ray reflect hone ke baad **F se guzarti hai**. F pole se **10 cm** door, mirror ke aage hai. To ray principal axis ko P se 10 cm aage kaatti hai.
- (b) R = 2 × f = 2 × 10 cm = 20 cm. C, P se **20 cm** aage hai. C se guzarti ray normal ki tarah girti hai, isliye **usi raste wapas C ki taraf** lautti hai.
- (c) F se guzarti ray reflect hone ke baad **principal axis ke parallel** ho jaati hai.

Jaanch: f = R ÷ 2 = 20 cm ÷ 2 = 10 cm ✅

Ab yahi ray (parallel, axis se 2 cm upar) ek **convex mirror** (f = 10 cm) par aaye to: reflect hone ke baad wo **phail jaayegi**, jaise mirror ke peeche 10 cm door F se aa rahi ho. Reflected ray principal axis ko mirror ke aage kahin nahi kaategi.

Jaanch: convex ka F virtual hai, isliye reflected kirnein sach mein kisi point par nahi milti ✅

### Example 10: PF = FC = f ka istemaal

(a) Ek concave mirror mein pole P se centre of curvature C ki doori 50 cm hai. f aur FC kya hain?
(b) Ek mirror mein PF = 9 cm hai. FC aur PC kya hain?

(a) PC = R = 50 cm, to f = 50 cm ÷ 2 = **25 cm**. FC = PC − PF = 50 cm − 25 cm = **25 cm**.
(b) PF = FC = 9 cm. PC = PF + FC = 9 cm + 9 cm = **18 cm**.

Jaanch: (a) PF = FC = 25 cm ✅   (b) PC = 2 × 9 cm = 18 cm ✅

### Example 11: unit badalna aur ray 4

(a) Ek concave mirror ki radius R = 0.4 m hai. f cm mein kitni hai?
(b) Ek ray concave mirror ke pole P par principal axis se 30° ka angle bana kar girti hai. Reflected ray axis ke saath kitna angle banayegi, aur incident aur reflected ray ke beech kitna angle hoga?

(a) f = R ÷ 2 = 0.4 m ÷ 2 = 0.2 m = 0.2 × 100 cm = **20 cm**.

(b) Pole par principal axis hi normal hai, isliye ∠i = 30° to ∠r = **30°**, axis ke doosri taraf. Incident aur reflected ray ke beech ka angle = ∠i + ∠r = 30° + 30° = **60°**.

Jaanch: (a) 20 cm × 2 = 40 cm = 0.4 m = R ✅   (b) ∠r = ∠i = 30° ✅

## Part H: Prisha ka sawal, poora jawab

Mr. Saluja ne chammach wapas uthaya aur dono side Prisha ko dikhayi.

"Dekho bitiya, tumhara chehra chammach ke **bowl (concave mirror)** mein chhota aur ulta dikha, aur **peeth (convex mirror)** mein chhota aur seedha. Dono mirror ke **shape alag** hain, isliye kirnein reflect hone ke baad alag tarah se jaati hain. Concave mirror kirnein ko ikattha karta hai (converge), convex unhein failata hai (diverge). Isi se image bhi alag banti hai."

Prisha ne poochha, "Par bowl mein chehra ulta kyun dikha? Aur agar main chammach ko chehre ke bahut kareeb le aaun to?"

Mr. Saluja muskuraye, "Ye agle topic ka mazedaar hissa hai. Chehra mirror se kitna door hai, ye ginti mein aata hai: kareeb ho ya door, tab image ka size aur seedha-ulta hona badal jaata hai. Pehle tumne mirror, uske points aur kirnein samajh li hain, ab unse image banana seekhna aasaan hoga."

Prisha ne chammach ko dheere-dheere apne chehre ke kareeb laaya aur phir door le gayi. Image ko badalte dekh kar wo muskurai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**1. Concave aur convex ko aapas mein ulta bol dena.**
- Yaad rakho: concave = andar ki taraf dabi (gufa/cave). Chammach ka bowl concave, peeth convex.

**2. R ko focus se naapna.**
- R = PC, yaani **pole se centre of curvature tak**. Focus se C tak ki doori sirf FC = R ÷ 2 hai.
- Example: R = 30 cm. Focus se C tak 15 cm hai, 30 cm nahi.

**3. Sochna ki f = R.**
- Sahi: f = R ÷ 2. Agar R = 40 cm to f = 20 cm, 40 cm nahi.
- Jaanch: f = 20 cm lene par 2 × 20 cm = 40 cm = R ✅. Agar f = 40 cm maante, to R = 2 × 40 cm = 80 cm banta, jo diya hua R nahi hai ❌.

**4. Convex mirror ka focus mirror ke aage dikhana.**
- Convex mirror ka focus **virtual** hai aur mirror ke **peeche** hota hai. Aage banaoge to wo ek concave mirror jaisa dikhega.

**5. Centre of curvature ko pole samajhna.**
- P mirror ki surface par hai, C gole ka centre hai jo mirror ke aage (concave) ya peeche (convex) hota hai. Dono ke beech ki doori R hai, isliye alag-alag hain.

**6. Sochna ki sign convention mein concave ki focal length positive hoti hai.**
- Concave ka F mirror ke aage, light ki **ulti** direction mein hai, isliye f **negative** hai. Example: R = −20 cm to f = −10 cm.

**7. Bade aperture ke mirror par bhi f = R ÷ 2 laga dena.**
- Ye result chhote aperture ke liye hai. Bade aperture mein axis se door ki kirnein F par theek se nahi milti.

## Quick Recap

- **Spherical mirror** = mirror jiski reflecting surface khokhle gole ka hissa ho.
- **Concave** (converging): andar ki taraf dabi. **Convex** (diverging): bahar ki taraf ubhri.
- **P** = pole (surface ka beech). **C** = centre of curvature. **R = PC.** **Principal axis** = P aur C se guzarti line. **Aperture** = reflecting surface ki chaudai.
- **F** = principal focus, **f = PF** = focal length. Concave ka F real (mirror ke aage), convex ka F virtual (mirror ke peeche).
- **f = R ÷ 2.** PF = FC = f. Ye chhote aperture ke liye. Chhote aperture kyun: baaki kirnein F par theek se nahi milti (spherical aberration, aage ki class mein).
- **Teen-chaar khaas kirnein:** (1) axis ke parallel: F se guzarti ya F se aati lagti hai. (2) F se guzarti (ya F ki taraf jaati): axis ke parallel. (3) C se guzarti (ya C ki taraf jaati): usi raste wapas. (4) Pole par: ∠i = ∠r, axis ke doosri taraf.
- **Sign convention (thoda extra, Class 10 overlap):** pole origin hai, light ki direction mein doori +, ulti mein −. Concave ke f aur R negative, convex ke positive.
- Sun-image activity: mirror se sharp bright spot tak ki doori ≈ f.
- Convex mirror ka field of view concave se zyada hota hai.

(c) A ray falls at the pole at 25° to the axis. What is the angle between the incident and the reflected ray?

### Answers
