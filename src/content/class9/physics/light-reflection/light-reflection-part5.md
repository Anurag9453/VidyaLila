# Topic 5: Spherical Mirrors se Image Banna

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki spherical mirror do tarah ke hote hain: **concave** (andar ki taraf dhansa hua) aur **convex** (bahar ki taraf ubhra hua). Humne unke parts bhi pehchane (pole P, centre of curvature C, principal axis, principal focus F, focal length f = R ÷ 2), aur chaar **special rays** seekhe jinka reflected raasta humein pehle se pata hai. Ant mein ek sawal bacha tha: object ko mirror ke kareeb ya door rakhne par image kaisi banti hai? Is topic mein hum us sawal ka poora jawab denge: ray diagram khud banayenge, har position ki image ki position, size aur nature dekhenge, aur ant mein image ko **number** mein bhi nikalenge.

## Part A: Ray diagram se image kaise banti hai

Pichhle topic ke **chaar special rays** yaad karo (poori baat [light-reflection-part4.md](light-reflection-part4.md) mein hai):

1. Principal axis ke **parallel** ray, reflection ke baad F se guzarti hai (concave) ya F se aati hui lagti hai (convex).
2. **F se guzarne wali** ray, reflection ke baad axis ke parallel ho jaati hai.
3. **C se guzarne wali** ray, mirror par seedhi (perpendicular) girti hai, isliye **usi raaste wapas** laut aati hai.
4. **Pole P par** girne wali ray, axis ke saath barabar angle bana kar reflect hoti hai (angle of incidence = angle of reflection).

### Image banane ka tareeka

Object ko ek seedhi arrow ki tarah maano, jiska **neeche ka sira (foot)** principal axis par hai aur **upar ka sira (top)** axis se upar.

1. Object ke **top** se in chaar rays mein se **koi bhi do** chuno aur unka reflected raasta kheencho.
2. Agar reflected rays **sach mein ek point par milti hain**, to wo point top ki **real image** hai. Agar wo kabhi nahi milti par **peeche badhane par** (dotted line se) milti hui lagti hain, to wo point top ki **virtual image** hai.
3. Object ka foot axis par hai aur axis par girne wali ray mirror se seedha laut aati hai, isliye **image ka foot bhi axis par** hi hota hai. Bas image ke top se axis par seedhi perpendicular line kheench do: wahi poori image hai.

**Rays kheenchne ke niyam:** asli (sach mein jaane wali) rays ko **solid line** se aur peeche badhai gayi (extended) rays ko **dotted line** se dikhate hain. Real image wali rays solid hoti hain, virtual image wali rays peeche dotted hoti hain.

**Sketch kaise padhein:** neeche ke sab sketches mein object left mein hai aur mirror right mein. Concave mirror ko ")" aur convex mirror ko "(" se dikhaya hai. Axis "-" se, aur 1 column ≈ 2.5 cm hai. Heights ko saaf dikhane ke liye badha kar banaya hai, isliye ye sketches sirf **idea** dete hain, naap ke liye nahi hain. Hum concave mirror ke liye **f = 10 cm** aur **C = 20 cm** maanenge (P se naapkar).

## Part E: Thoda extra (Class 10 overlap): Magnification (m)

Image object se kitni badi ya chhoti hai, ye ek number se batate hain:

> **Magnification: m = (image ki unchai) ÷ (object ki unchai) = h′ ÷ h**
> Yahan h object ki unchai hai aur h′ image ki unchai hai (dono cm ya dono m mein). m ka koi unit nahi hota (cm ÷ cm).

**Sign ka niyam (simple):** principal axis ke **upar** ki unchai **positive** aur axis ke **neeche** ki unchai **negative** maanenge. Object axis ke upar khada hai, isliye h hamesha **positive** hoti hai.

- **Real, inverted image** axis ke neeche banti hai, isliye h′ **negative**, aur **m negative**.
- **Virtual, erect image** axis ke upar banti hai, isliye h′ **positive**, aur **m positive**.

**Size kaise pata chale (sign ko chhod kar):**

| m ki value | Matlab |
|---|---|
| Sign chhod kar m ka maan 1 se bada (jaise 2 ya −2) | Image badi (enlarged) |
| Sign chhod kar m ka maan 1 ke barabar (1 ya −1) | Image barabar size |
| Sign chhod kar m ka maan 1 se chhota (jaise 0.5 ya −0.5) | Image chhoti (diminished) |
| m negative | Image ulti (inverted), real |
| m positive | Image seedhi (erect), virtual |

Dhyan do: **negative sign ka size se koi lena-dena nahi hai.** m = −2 ka matlab "ulti aur 2 guna badi" hai, "chhoti" nahi.

### Example 1: Heights se magnification

Ek concave mirror ne 6 cm unchi object ki real, ulti image banayi jiski unchai 3 cm hai. Magnification nikalo.

Object ki unchai h = +6 cm. Image ulti hai (axis ke neeche), isliye h′ = −3 cm.
m = h′ ÷ h = (−3 cm) ÷ (6 cm) = **−0.5**

Matlab image **ulti** (real) hai aur object se **aadhi** size ki hai (diminished).

Jaanch: m ka maan 0.5 < 1 (1 se chhota) to image chhoti ✅, aur h′ = m × h = −0.5 × 6 cm = −3 cm ✅

### Example 2: m se image ki unchai

Ek mirror 4 cm unchi object ki image banata hai jiska magnification m = +1.5 hai. Image ki unchai aur nature batao.

h′ = m × h = (+1.5) × (4 cm) = **+6 cm**

m positive hai to image **seedhi (erect)** aur **virtual** hai. m ka maan 1.5 > 1, isliye image **badi** hai.

Jaanch: h′ ÷ h = 6 cm ÷ 4 cm = 1.5 ✅

## Part F: Thoda extra (Class 10 overlap): Mirror formula

Ray diagram se image ki jagah **lagbhag** pata chalti hai. Agar **exact** jagah chahiye, to ek formula kaam aata hai. Ye Class 10 ka hissa hai, yahan hum ise **natija** ki tarah lenge (derivation nahi) aur uska istemal sikhenge.

### Sign convention (simple)

Pole P ko 0 maano. Light ki disha (left se right) ko **positive** maano. To:

- Mirror ke **saamne** (jahan se light aati hai) naapi gayi doori **negative**.
- Mirror ke **peeche** naapi gayi doori **positive**.
- Principal axis ke **upar** ki unchai positive, **neeche** ki unchai negative.

Isse milte hain ye niyam:

| Cheez | Sign | Wajah |
|---|---|---|
| Real object ki doori u | **Negative** | Object mirror ke saamne hai |
| Real image ki doori v | **Negative** | Real image mirror ke saamne banti hai |
| Virtual image ki doori v | **Positive** | Virtual image mirror ke peeche banti hai |
| Concave mirror ka f (aur R) | **Negative** | F aur C mirror ke saamne hain |
| Convex mirror ka f (aur R) | **Positive** | F aur C mirror ke peeche hain |

### Mirror formula aur magnification

> **1/v + 1/u = 1/f**   aur   **m = h′ ÷ h = −v ÷ u**

Ye dono formulas **concave aur convex dono** mirrors ke liye chalte hain, bas sign sahi lagao. Ise istemal karne ke steps:

1. u, f (aur agar diya ho to v) ko **apne sign ke saath** likho (cm ya m).
2. **1/v = 1/f − 1/u** se 1/v nikalo, phir v.
3. **m = −v ÷ u** se magnification nikalo.
4. v ka sign dekho: **negative** = real, mirror ke saamne; **positive** = virtual, mirror ke peeche. m ka sign dekho: negative = inverted, positive = erect.

### Formula ki Jaanch: Part B ke sketches se milaao (f = −10 cm, concave)

Pichhle sketches mein humne object ko 30, 20, 15, 10 aur 5 cm par rakha tha (aur ek baar bahut door). Formula se v aur m nikalte hain:

| u (cm) | 1/v = 1/f − 1/u (1/cm) | v (cm) | m = −v ÷ u | Matlab |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| −30 | −1/10 + 1/30 = −1/15 | −15 | −0.5 | F aur C ke beech, chhoti, real, ulti (Position 2) |
| −20 | −1/10 + 1/20 = −1/20 | −20 | −1 | C par, barabar, real, ulti (Position 3) |
| −15 | −1/10 + 1/15 = −1/30 | −30 | −2 | C ke peeche, badi, real, ulti (Position 4) |
| −10 | −1/10 + 1/10 = 0 | koi v nahi (infinity) | m nahi nikalta | Rays parallel, image infinity par (Position 5) |
| −5 | −1/10 + 1/5 = +1/10 | +10 | +2 | Mirror ke peeche, badi, virtual, erect (Position 6) |

Bahut door ke object (u bahut bada negative) ke liye 1/u lagbhag 0 hota hai, to 1/v ≈ 1/f aur v ≈ f = −10 cm: image F par (Position 1). Ray diagram aur formula **ek hi kahani** sunate hain.

### Example 3: Concave mirror, object dur

Ek concave mirror ki focal length 10 cm hai. 6 cm ki object mirror se 30 cm door hai. Image ki jagah, nature aur size batao.

Sign ke saath: f = −10 cm, u = −30 cm, h = +6 cm.
1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/(−10 cm) − 1/(−30 cm) = −1/10 cm⁻¹ + 1/30 cm⁻¹ = −3/30 cm⁻¹ + 1/30 cm⁻¹ = −2/30 cm⁻¹ = −1/15 cm⁻¹
v = **−15 cm**

v negative hai, isliye image mirror ke **saamne 15 cm** par **real** hai.
m = −v ÷ u = −(−15 cm) ÷ (−30 cm) = **−0.5**
h′ = m × h = −0.5 × 6 cm = **−3 cm** (ulti, 3 cm ki).

Jaanch: 1/v + 1/u = 1/(−15 cm) + 1/(−30 cm) = −2/30 cm⁻¹ − 1/30 cm⁻¹ = −3/30 cm⁻¹ = −1/10 cm⁻¹ = 1/f ✅. Aur ye Example 1 ke numbers se match karta hai ✅.

### Example 4: Concave mirror, object F ke andar

f = −10 cm wale concave mirror ke saamne 5 cm par object hai. Image kaisi banegi?

Sign ke saath: f = −10 cm, u = −5 cm.
1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/(−10 cm) − 1/(−5 cm) = −1/10 cm⁻¹ + 2/10 cm⁻¹ = +1/10 cm⁻¹
v = **+10 cm**
m = −v ÷ u = −(+10 cm) ÷ (−5 cm) = **+2**

v positive hai: image mirror ke **peeche 10 cm** par, **virtual**. m = +2: **seedhi aur 2 guna badi**.

Jaanch: 1/v + 1/u = 1/(10 cm) + 1/(−5 cm) = 1/10 cm⁻¹ − 2/10 cm⁻¹ = −1/10 cm⁻¹ = 1/f ✅

### Example 5: Convex mirror

Ek convex mirror ki focal length +10 cm hai. 5 cm ki object mirror ke saamne 15 cm par hai. Image batao.

Sign ke saath: f = +10 cm, u = −15 cm, h = +5 cm.
1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/(+10 cm) − 1/(−15 cm) = 1/10 cm⁻¹ + 1/15 cm⁻¹ = 3/30 cm⁻¹ + 2/30 cm⁻¹ = 5/30 cm⁻¹ = 1/6 cm⁻¹
v = **+6 cm**
m = −v ÷ u = −(+6 cm) ÷ (−15 cm) = **+0.4**
h′ = m × h = 0.4 × 5 cm = **+2 cm**

Image mirror ke **peeche 6 cm** par (P aur F = 10 cm ke beech) hai, **virtual, erect aur chhoti** (2 cm).

Jaanch: 1/v + 1/u = 1/(6 cm) + 1/(−15 cm) = 5/30 cm⁻¹ − 2/30 cm⁻¹ = 3/30 cm⁻¹ = 1/10 cm⁻¹ = 1/f ✅. Aur v ka maan P aur F ke beech hai, jaisa Part C ne kaha tha ✅.

### Example 6: Radius se focal length, same size ki image

Ek concave mirror ki radius of curvature R = 40 cm hai. Object ko 40 cm par rakhne par image kahan banegi, aur kaisi?

Concave mirror ke liye R negative: R = −40 cm. f = R ÷ 2 = −40 cm ÷ 2 = **−20 cm**. u = −40 cm.
1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/(−20 cm) − 1/(−40 cm) = −2/40 cm⁻¹ + 1/40 cm⁻¹ = −1/40 cm⁻¹
v = **−40 cm**
m = −v ÷ u = −(−40 cm) ÷ (−40 cm) = **−1**

Image mirror ke saamne 40 cm par, **real, ulti, object ke barabar**. Yahan object C par (R = 40 cm) hi tha, yani Position 3.

Jaanch: 1/(−40 cm) + 1/(−40 cm) = −2/40 cm⁻¹ = −1/20 cm⁻¹ = 1/f ✅

### Example 7: Image ki doori di ho, object ki doori nikalo

Ek concave mirror (f = −20 cm) se real image mirror ke saamne 30 cm par bani hai. Object kahan hai?

Sign ke saath: f = −20 cm, v = −30 cm.
1/u = 1/f − 1/v = 1/(−20 cm) − 1/(−30 cm) = −3/60 cm⁻¹ + 2/60 cm⁻¹ = −1/60 cm⁻¹
u = **−60 cm** (object mirror ke saamne 60 cm par)
m = −v ÷ u = −(−30 cm) ÷ (−60 cm) = **−0.5**

Jaanch: 1/v + 1/u = 1/(−30 cm) + 1/(−60 cm) = −2/60 cm⁻¹ − 1/60 cm⁻¹ = −3/60 cm⁻¹ = −1/20 cm⁻¹ = 1/f ✅. Object C = 40 cm ke peeche hai, image F = 20 cm aur C = 40 cm ke beech: Position 2 se match ✅.

### Example 8: Experiment se focal length

Ek student ne concave mirror ke saamne 30 cm par jalti mombatti rakhi aur screen ko 60 cm par le jaakar saaf image paayi. Focal length batao.

Dono mirror ke saamne hain: u = −30 cm, v = −60 cm.
1/f = 1/v + 1/u = 1/(−60 cm) + 1/(−30 cm) = −1/60 cm⁻¹ − 2/60 cm⁻¹ = −3/60 cm⁻¹ = −1/20 cm⁻¹
f = **−20 cm** (concave, focal length 20 cm). R = 2f = **−40 cm**, yaani radius 40 cm.
m = −v ÷ u = −(−60 cm) ÷ (−30 cm) = **−2** (ulti, 2 guna badi).

Jaanch: 1/v + 1/u = 1/(−60 cm) + 1/(−30 cm) = −1/60 cm⁻¹ − 2/60 cm⁻¹ = −3/60 cm⁻¹ = −1/20 cm⁻¹ = 1/f ✅. Object F = 20 cm aur C = 40 cm ke beech tha, to image C ke peeche badi aur real hoti hai (Position 4) ✅.

### Example 9: Real image barabar size ki: object kahan ho?

Ek concave mirror ki focal length 15 cm hai. Object kahan rakhein ki image real aur object ke barabar size ki bane?

Barabar size ka matlab m = −1. Formula se: m = −v ÷ u = −1, to v = u.
Isko mirror formula mein daalo: 1/u + 1/u = 1/f, to 2/u = 1/f, **u = 2f**.
f = −15 cm to u = 2 × (−15 cm) = **−30 cm**

Matlab object mirror ke saamne 30 cm par (yaani C = R = 2f par) rakho. Tab v = u = −30 cm.

Jaanch: 1/(−30 cm) + 1/(−30 cm) = −2/30 cm⁻¹ = −1/15 cm⁻¹ = 1/f ✅. m = −(−30 cm) ÷ (−30 cm) = −1 ✅. Ye Position 3 ka formula wala roop hai: **object C par to image C par**.

### Example 10: Shaving mirror

Ek shaving mirror concave hai jiski focal length 25 cm hai. Chehra mirror se 10 cm door hai. Image kaisi dikhegi?

Sign ke saath: f = −25 cm, u = −10 cm.
1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/(−25 cm) − 1/(−10 cm) = −2/50 cm⁻¹ + 5/50 cm⁻¹ = +3/50 cm⁻¹
v = 50/3 cm = **+16.67 cm**
m = −v ÷ u = −(+16.67 cm) ÷ (−10 cm) = **+1.67**

Chehra F = 25 cm ke **andar** hai (10 cm < 25 cm), isliye image mirror ke **peeche 16.67 cm** par, **virtual, erect aur 1.67 guna badi** hai. Isi wajah se shaving mirror mein chehra badha hua aur seedha dikhta hai.

Jaanch: 1/v + 1/u = 1/(16.67 cm) + 1/(−10 cm) = 0.06 cm⁻¹ − 0.10 cm⁻¹ = −0.04 cm⁻¹ = 1/(−25 cm) ✅

### Example 11: Gaadi ka convex (rear-view) mirror

Ek gaadi ke rear-view mirror ki focal length +1 m hai. Peeche 5 m door ek truck hai jo 3 m ka hai. Truck ki image kahan banegi aur kitni badi?

Sign ke saath: f = +1 m, u = −5 m, h = +3 m.
1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/(+1 m) − 1/(−5 m) = 1 m⁻¹ + 0.2 m⁻¹ = 1.2 m⁻¹ = 6/5 m⁻¹
v = 5/6 m = **+0.833 m** (mirror ke peeche)
m = −v ÷ u = −(+0.833 m) ÷ (−5 m) = **+0.167** (= 1/6)
h′ = m × h = 0.1667 × 3 m = **+0.5 m**

Image virtual, erect aur object ka sirf chhatha (1/6) hissa hai. Isliye convex mirror mein door ki cheezein chhoti dikhti hain, par driver ko bahut chauda nazara (zyada area) ek saath dikh jaata hai. (Kyun, ye Topic 6 mein.)

Jaanch: 1/v + 1/u = 1/(0.833 m) + 1/(−5 m) = 1.2 m⁻¹ − 0.2 m⁻¹ = 1.0 m⁻¹ = 1/f ✅. Aur v = 0.833 m < f = 1 m, yaani P aur F ke beech ✅.

### Example 12: Table se image ki pehchaan (f = 10 cm, concave)

Ek concave mirror ki focal length 10 cm hai (to C = 20 cm). Neeche diye object distances ke liye Part D ki table se image ka nature batao.

| Object P se | Position | Image |
|---|---|---|
| Bahut door (sooraj) | Infinity | F par, point jaisi, real, inverted |
| 50 cm | C (20 cm) ke peeche | F aur C ke beech, chhoti, real, inverted |
| 20 cm | C par | C par, barabar, real, inverted |
| 14 cm | C aur F ke beech (10 cm se 20 cm) | C ke peeche, badi, real, inverted |
| 10 cm | F par | Infinity par, bahut badi, real, inverted |
| 6 cm | F aur P ke beech (10 cm se kam) | Peeche, badi, virtual, erect |

Jaanch (ek row, formula se): u = −14 cm, f = −10 cm: 1/v = −1/10 cm⁻¹ + 1/14 cm⁻¹ = −7/70 cm⁻¹ + 5/70 cm⁻¹ = −2/70 cm⁻¹, v = −35 cm. Ye C = 20 cm ke peeche hai ✅. m = −(−35 cm) ÷ (−14 cm) = −2.5: ulti aur badi ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Galat special ray chunna.** ❌ Sirf wahi rays lo jinka reflected raasta humein pehle se pata hai (parallel ray, F se guzarne wali, C se guzarne wali, P par girne wali). Koi bhi aur ray kheench doge to uska reflected raasta pata nahi hoga. Aur C se guzarne wali ray ko aage jaate nahi, usi raaste **wapas lautte** dikhana.
2. **Real image ko dotted aur virtual ko solid se dikhana.** ❌ Real image wali rays sach mein milti hain, isliye solid. Virtual image mirror ke peeche rays ko peeche badhane par milti hai, isliye wahan **dotted** lines.
3. **Mirror formula mein sign bhool jaana.** ❌ Real object ke liye u hamesha **negative** hai (u = −30 cm, na ki +30 cm). Concave mirror ka f bhi negative hai. Agar u = +30 aur f = −10 daaloge to v galat aayega: 1/v = −1/10 − 1/30 = −4/30, v = −7.5 cm, jabki sahi jawab −15 cm hai.
4. **f = R likhna.** ❌ f = R ÷ 2. R = 40 cm wale mirror ka f = 20 cm (concave mein −20 cm).
5. **Sochna ki convex mirror real image bana sakta hai.** ❌ Real object ke liye convex mirror hamesha **virtual, erect aur chhoti** image banata hai. Hamesha v positive aata hai.
6. **Sochna ki negative magnification ka matlab "chhoti image" hai.** ❌ m ka **sign** batata hai ki image inverted (negative) hai ya erect (positive). Size ka pata m ke maan (sign chhod kar) se chalta hai: m = −3 ka matlab ulti aur **3 guna badi**, aur m = +0.5 ka matlab seedhi aur aadhi.
7. **Sochna ki object F par ho to image F par banegi.** ❌ Object F par ho to reflected rays parallel nikalti hain, image infinity par banti hai. Image F par tab banti hai jab object **infinity par** ho (Position 1).

## Quick Recap

- Image banane ke liye object ke **top** se **koi do** special rays lo. Jahan reflected rays milen (ya peeche badhane par milti dikhen), wahan top ki image; foot ki image axis par.
- **Real image** = rays sach mein milti hain (solid lines, screen par aati hai). **Virtual image** = rays peeche badhane par milti dikhti hain (dotted lines, screen par nahi).
- **Concave mirror**: object infinity par to image F par (point jaisi). C ke peeche: F-C ke beech, chhoti. C par: C par, barabar. C aur F ke beech: C ke peeche, badi. F par: infinity par, bahut badi. In sab mein image **real aur inverted**. F aur P ke beech: peeche, badi, **virtual aur erect**.
- **Convex mirror**: har position par image mirror ke peeche, P aur F ke beech, chhoti, virtual, erect (infinity par: F par, point jaisi). Convex mirror real image nahi banata.
- **Magnification m = h′ ÷ h**. Axis ke upar ki unchai positive, neeche ki negative. m negative = real aur inverted, m positive = virtual aur erect. sign chhod kar m ka maan 1 se bada to badi, 1 se chhota to chhoti.
- **Thoda extra:** 1/v + 1/u = 1/f aur m = −v ÷ u. u (real object) negative; f negative concave ke liye, positive convex ke liye; v negative = real (saamne), v positive = virtual (peeche).
- Funny mirrors: concave hissa (F ke andar) badi seedhi image deta hai, convex hissa chhoti seedhi image deta hai, aur concave hissa ke saamne C ke peeche jaane par image ulti ho jaati hai.

## Practice Questions

(Questions 2 to 5 and 7 to 10 use magnification and the mirror formula from Parts E and F: thoda extra.)

1. For a concave mirror, state the position, size and nature of the image when the object is placed (a) beyond C, (b) at F, (c) between F and P. Which of these three images can be caught on a screen?
2. A concave mirror has a focal length of 15 cm. A 5 cm tall object is placed 45 cm in front of it. Using the mirror formula, find the position of the image, the magnification and the height of the image. Is it real or virtual?
3. A convex mirror has a focal length of +15 cm. A 6 cm tall object is placed 30 cm in front of it. Find the image distance, the magnification and the image height. Is the image real or virtual, erect or inverted?
4. A concave mirror has a radius of curvature of 30 cm. A 2 cm tall object is placed 10 cm from its pole. Find the position, nature and height of the image.
5. A concave mirror forms a real image on a screen, 60 cm from the mirror, that is 3 times the size of the object. Find the object distance and the focal length of the mirror.
6. State true or false and give a one-line reason: (a) A convex mirror can form a real image of a real object. (b) A real image can be caught on a screen. (c) A magnification of m = −2 means the image is smaller than the object. (d) For a concave mirror, an object at C gives an image at C of the same size.
7. In an experiment, an object is kept 24 cm from a concave mirror and a sharp image is found on a screen 40 cm from the mirror. Find the focal length and the radius of curvature of the mirror.
8. A concave mirror has a focal length of 12 cm. An object is placed 4 cm in front of the mirror. Find the image distance and the magnification, and say whether the image is real or virtual.
9. A convex mirror has a focal length of +20 cm. An object is first at 100 cm and then at 10 cm from the mirror. Find the image distance and the magnification in each case. What do you notice about the image size as the object comes closer?
10. A mirror forms an erect image half the size of an object kept 20 cm in front of it. Find the image distance and the focal length. Is the mirror concave or convex?

### Answers

1. (a) Object C ke peeche: image **F aur C ke beech**, **chhoti**, **real aur inverted**. (b) Object F par: image **infinity par**, **bahut badi**, **real aur inverted**. (c) Object F aur P ke beech: image **mirror ke peeche**, **badi**, **virtual aur erect**. Screen par sirf (a) wali image aa sakti hai: wo real hai aur paas ki ek jagah par banti hai. (b) wali real hai, par infinity par banti hai, isliye screen par nahi aati. (c) wali virtual hai, screen par nahi aa sakti.

2. Sign ke saath: f = −15 cm, u = −45 cm, h = +5 cm.
   1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/(−15 cm) − 1/(−45 cm) = −3/45 cm⁻¹ + 1/45 cm⁻¹ = −2/45 cm⁻¹, to v = −45/2 cm = **−22.5 cm**.
   m = −v ÷ u = −(−22.5 cm) ÷ (−45 cm) = **−0.5**. h′ = m × h = −0.5 × 5 cm = **−2.5 cm**.
   v negative: image **real**, mirror ke saamne 22.5 cm par, ulti (m negative) aur chhoti.
   Jaanch: 1/(−22.5 cm) + 1/(−45 cm) = −2/45 cm⁻¹ − 1/45 cm⁻¹ = −3/45 cm⁻¹ = −1/15 cm⁻¹ = 1/f ✅

3. Sign ke saath: f = +15 cm, u = −30 cm, h = +6 cm.
   1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/(15 cm) + 1/(30 cm) = 2/30 cm⁻¹ + 1/30 cm⁻¹ = 3/30 cm⁻¹ = 1/10 cm⁻¹, to v = **+10 cm**.
   m = −v ÷ u = −(10 cm) ÷ (−30 cm) = **+1/3 ≈ +0.33**. h′ = (1/3) × 6 cm = **+2 cm**.
   Image **virtual** (mirror ke peeche 10 cm, P aur F = 15 cm ke beech), **erect** aur **chhoti**.
   Jaanch: 1/(10 cm) + 1/(−30 cm) = 3/30 cm⁻¹ − 1/30 cm⁻¹ = 2/30 cm⁻¹ = 1/(15 cm) ✅

4. R = 30 cm, to f = R ÷ 2 = 15 cm, concave ke liye f = **−15 cm**. u = −10 cm, h = +2 cm.
   1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/(−15 cm) − 1/(−10 cm) = −2/30 cm⁻¹ + 3/30 cm⁻¹ = +1/30 cm⁻¹, to v = **+30 cm**.
   m = −v ÷ u = −(30 cm) ÷ (−10 cm) = **+3**. h′ = 3 × 2 cm = **+6 cm**.
   Image mirror ke **peeche 30 cm** par, **virtual, erect aur badi** (6 cm). Object F (15 cm) ke andar tha, to ye hona hi tha.
   Jaanch: 1/(30 cm) + 1/(−10 cm) = 1/30 cm⁻¹ − 3/30 cm⁻¹ = −2/30 cm⁻¹ = −1/15 cm⁻¹ = 1/f ✅

5. Real image mirror ke saamne hai: v = −60 cm. "3 guna badi" real image ulti hoti hai, to m = −3.
   m = −v ÷ u: −3 = −(−60 cm) ÷ u = 60 cm ÷ u, to u = 60 cm ÷ (−3) = **−20 cm**.
   1/f = 1/v + 1/u = 1/(−60 cm) + 1/(−20 cm) = −1/60 cm⁻¹ − 3/60 cm⁻¹ = −4/60 cm⁻¹ = −1/15 cm⁻¹, to f = **−15 cm**.
   Object mirror ke saamne 20 cm par hai aur F = 15 cm aur C = 30 cm ke beech hai: Position 4, image badi aur real ✅.
   Jaanch: m = −(−60 cm) ÷ (−20 cm) = −3 ✅

6. (a) **False** ❌. Convex mirror ki reflected rays bikharti hain, kabhi milti nahi: image hamesha virtual hoti hai.
   (b) **True** ✅. Real image mein rays sach mein milti hain, isliye screen par aa jaati hai.
   (c) **False** ❌. m = −2 ka matlab image **ulti aur 2 guna badi** hai. Negative sign sirf ulti-pan batata hai.
   (d) **True** ✅. Object C par ho to image C par, barabar size ki, real aur inverted (m = −1).

7. u = −24 cm, v = −40 cm.
   1/f = 1/v + 1/u = 1/(−40 cm) + 1/(−24 cm) = −3/120 cm⁻¹ − 5/120 cm⁻¹ = −8/120 cm⁻¹ = −1/15 cm⁻¹, to f = **−15 cm** (concave). R = 2f = **−30 cm**, yaani radius **30 cm**.
   Jaanch: 1/(−40 cm) + 1/(−24 cm) = −0.025 cm⁻¹ − 0.04167 cm⁻¹ = −0.06667 cm⁻¹ = −1/15 cm⁻¹ ✅. Object F (15 cm) aur C (30 cm) ke beech 24 cm par hai, aur image C ke peeche (40 cm) hai ✅.

8. f = −12 cm, u = −4 cm.
   1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/(−12 cm) − 1/(−4 cm) = −1/12 cm⁻¹ + 3/12 cm⁻¹ = +2/12 cm⁻¹ = 1/6 cm⁻¹, to v = **+6 cm**.
   m = −v ÷ u = −(6 cm) ÷ (−4 cm) = **+1.5**.
   v positive: image **virtual**, mirror ke peeche 6 cm par, erect aur 1.5 guna badi.
   Jaanch: 1/(6 cm) + 1/(−4 cm) = 2/12 cm⁻¹ − 3/12 cm⁻¹ = −1/12 cm⁻¹ = 1/f ✅

9. f = +20 cm.
   Pehli baar u = −100 cm: 1/v = 1/(20 cm) + 1/(100 cm) = 5/100 cm⁻¹ + 1/100 cm⁻¹ = 6/100 cm⁻¹, to v = 100/6 cm = **+16.67 cm**. m = −(16.67 cm) ÷ (−100 cm) = **+0.167** (= 1/6).
   Doosri baar u = −10 cm: 1/v = 1/(20 cm) + 1/(10 cm) = 1/20 cm⁻¹ + 2/20 cm⁻¹ = 3/20 cm⁻¹, to v = 20/3 cm = **+6.67 cm**. m = −(6.67 cm) ÷ (−10 cm) = **+0.667** (= 2/3).
   Dono baar image virtual, erect, chhoti aur P aur F = 20 cm ke beech. Jaise object kareeb aata hai, image **badi** hoti jaati hai (1/6 se 2/3), par kabhi object se badi nahi hoti.
   Jaanch (pehla): 1/(16.67 cm) + 1/(−100 cm) = 0.06 cm⁻¹ − 0.01 cm⁻¹ = 0.05 cm⁻¹ = 1/20 cm⁻¹ ✅. (Doosra): 1/(6.67 cm) + 1/(−10 cm) = 0.15 cm⁻¹ − 0.10 cm⁻¹ = 0.05 cm⁻¹ = 1/20 cm⁻¹ ✅

10. Erect image to virtual hai, to m = **+0.5**. u = −20 cm. m = −v ÷ u: 0.5 = −v ÷ (−20 cm) = v ÷ 20 cm, to v = **+10 cm**.
    1/f = 1/v + 1/u = 1/(10 cm) + 1/(−20 cm) = 2/20 cm⁻¹ − 1/20 cm⁻¹ = 1/20 cm⁻¹, to f = **+20 cm**.
    f positive hai, aur image chhoti aur erect hai, isliye mirror **convex** hai (concave se kabhi chhoti erect image nahi banti).
    Jaanch: m = −(10 cm) ÷ (−20 cm) = +0.5 ✅, 1/(10 cm) + 1/(−20 cm) = 0.05 cm⁻¹ = 1/20 cm⁻¹ ✅
