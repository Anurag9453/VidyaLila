# Topic 6: Mirrors ke Uses aur Chapter Wrap-up

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki mirror jo image banata hai, wo mirror ke type aur object ki position par nirbhar karti hai. Concave mirror mein object F ke bahar ho to image real aur ulti (inverted) banti hai, aur object F aur P ke beech ho to image virtual, seedhi (erect) aur badi dikhti hai. Convex mirror mein image hamesha virtual, erect aur chhoti (diminished) banti hai, aur wo mirror ke peeche F aur P ke beech dikhti hai. Humne m = h′/h aur mirror formula 1/v + 1/u = 1/f bhi use kiye, aur ant mein ek sawaal chhoda tha: ye sab mirrors hamari zindagi mein kahan kaam aate hain?

Aaj hum wahi sawaal hal karenge. Har jagah mirror ka type **soch samajhkar chuna jaata hai**: koi cheez ko badi dikhana hai, koi ko door tak ek seedhi kiran mein bhejna hai, koi mein ek saath bahut saara dikhana hai. Hum dekhenge ki kaun sa kaam kis mirror se hota hai aur **kyun**. Ye chapter ka aakhri topic hai, to end mein poore chapter ka wrap-up bhi hoga.

## Part A: Plane mirror ke uses

Topic 3 mein dekha tha ki plane mirror mein image virtual, erect aur object ke **barabar size** ki banti hai, aur image utni hi peeche dikhti hai jitna object aage hai. Yahi gun use karne wale kaam ye hain.

| Use | Kyun plane mirror? |
|---|---|
| **Dressing table aur bathroom ka mirror** | Chehra aur kapde apni asli size mein, bina bigde dikhne chahiye |
| **Periscope** | Do plane mirrors 45° par: roshni ko do baar mod dena aur seedhi image dena (submarine, bunker, bheed ke peeche se dekhna) |
| **Kaleidoscope** | Teen mirrors ek doosre se 60° par: ek hi cheez ki kai images ban jaati hain aur sundar pattern dikhta hai |
| **Barber ki dukaan** | Aage ka bada mirror aur peeche ki deewar ka mirror: do plane mirrors ke kaaran customer apne sir ka peeche wala hissa bhi dekh leta hai |
| **Solar cooker ke flat reflectors** | Box-type cooker ke dhakkan mein laga flat sheesha zyada dhoop cooker ke andar bhej deta hai |
| **Kamre ki deewar par bada mirror** | Mirror ke peeche image utni hi door dikhti hai, isliye kamra bada lagta hai |

Solar cooker ke flat reflector mein sheesha roshni ko **ikattha nahi** karta, wo sirf **disha badalkar** zyada roshni andar bhejta hai. Ikattha karne ka kaam concave mirror ka hai: wo hum Part C mein dekhenge.

**Kamre wala trick.** Maan lo ek kamra 4 m gehra hai aur ek deewar par bada plane mirror laga hai. Mirror ke saamne waali deewar ki image mirror ke peeche 4 m par banti hai. Jo aadmi mirror se 2 m par khada hai, use wo image 2 m + 4 m = 6 m door dikhti hai, jabki asli deewar sirf 2 m door hai. Isliye kamra bada lagta hai.

### Periscope kaise kaam karta hai

Periscope ek lambi tube hai jiske dono sire par ek ek plane mirror laga hota hai, dono 45° par aur ek doosre ke parallel.

```
           ┌────────────┐
 object ──>────────╲    │   <- M1 (oopar wala plane mirror, 45° par)
  ki kiran │       ↓    │
           │       ↓    │   tube
           │       ↓    │
           │       ╲────────> aankh ki taraf
           └────────────┘   M2 (neeche wala plane mirror, 45° par)
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Periscope (Topic 3 wala): oopar wale mirror M1 par object ki kiran aati hai, 90° mud kar neeche jaati hai, neeche wale mirror M2 par phir 90° mud kar aankh tak pahunchti hai. Isliye aankh ko object seedha aur apni asli size mein dikhta hai.

Periscope ki khaas baat ye hai ki **image erect aur barabar size ki** milti hai, kyunki plane mirror aisi hi image banata hai.

## Part B: Concave mirror ke uses (roshni ko seedha bhejna)

### Kirnon ka aasan niyam yaad karo

Topic 4 mein humne chaar khaas kirnein dekhi thi. Unmein se do yahan sabse kaam ki hain:

1. **Principal axis ke parallel aati kiran** concave mirror se reflect hokar **F se guzarti hai.**
2. **F se guzarti hui kiran** reflect hokar **principal axis ke parallel** ho jaati hai.

Dono niyam ek doosre ke ulte hain. Pehla niyam solar cooker mein kaam aata hai, doosra headlight mein.

### Headlight, torch, searchlight, spotlight

**Bulb ko mirror ke F par rakho.** Bulb se nikli saari kirnein F se aati hui hain, aur doosre niyam ke hisaab se wo mirror se reflect hokar **principal axis ke parallel** ho jaati hain. Is tarah bulb ki bahut saari roshni ek **parallel beam** mein door tak jaati hai aur bikharti nahi.

```
         reflector (concave)
            ╱────────────────────────>
           ╱  ↖
          │      ↖
          │        B ─────────────────>   B = bulb, F par
          │      ↙
           ╲  ↙
            ╲────────────────────────>
```

> **Figure 6.2** – Headlight ka reflector (side se): reflector ki chamakti, andar dabi side daayen taraf hai, aur bulb B uske F par hai. Bulb se peeche ki taraf nikli kirnein concave reflector se takrakar principal axis ke parallel nikalti hain, to ek parallel beam banta hai jo door tak seedha jaata hai.

Isi tarah **torch**, **searchlight**, **stage ke spotlights** aur **projector** ke lamp mein concave reflector aur uske F par bulb hota hai.

**Ek dhyan ki baat.** Agar bulb F par na ho, to beam parallel nahi rehta. Bulb F se door (bahar) ho to beam kahin ek point par simat kar phir failta hai. Bulb F aur P ke beech ho to beam failta (diverge) hai. Isi liye headlight ke bulb ko adjust karke theek F par fit kiya jaata hai.

### Shaving mirror aur make-up mirror

Jab chehra concave mirror ke **F ke andar** (P aur F ke beech) hota hai, to Topic 5 ke hisaab se image **virtual, erect aur badi** banti hai. Isiliye shaving aur make-up mirror concave hote hain: baal, bindi, kajal ya chhote daagh saaf aur bade dikhte hain.

### Dentist ka mirror aur ENT doctor ka head mirror

- **Dentist ka mirror**: chhota concave mirror hai. Daant mirror ke F ke andar rakha jaata hai, to **daant ki badi, erect image** dikhti hai, aur doctor ko chhote chhote cavity bhi saaf dikhte hain.
- **ENT doctor ka head mirror**: maathe par bandha hua bada concave mirror. Wo lamp ki roshni ko **ikattha karke** kaan, naak ya gale ke chhote se hisse par daalta hai, jisse wo jagah tez roshan ho jaati hai aur doctor use achhi tarah dekh pata hai.

### Solar cooker aur solar furnace

Sooraj bahut door hai, isliye uski kirnein hum tak lagbhag **parallel** pahunchti hain. **Bade concave reflector** par ye parallel kirnein padti hain to pehle niyam ke hisaab se **sabhi F se guzarti hain**: yaani saari dhoop F par ikatthi ho jaati hai. F par rakha hua bartan bahut garam ho jaata hai.

```
   suraj ki kirnein (parallel)           concave reflector (dish)
                                   )
   ────────────────────────────────>)
                                 ↙   )
                         [bartan]     )   <- bartan F par
                                 ↖   )
   ────────────────────────────────>)
                                   )
```

> **Figure 6.3** – Solar cooker: suraj ki parallel kirnein bade concave reflector par padti hain aur F par milti hain. F par rakha bartan sabse zyada garam hota hai.

**Solar furnace** mein reflector bahut bahut bada hota hai (kai chhote mirrors milkar), isliye bahut zyada garmi F par ikatthi hoti hai, itni ki dhaatu bhi pighal sakti hai.

### Reflecting telescope aur satellite dish

- **Reflecting telescope** mein ek bahut bada concave mirror hota hai. Taaron ki roshni itni kamzor hoti hai ki bada mirror usse **zyada ikattha** kar leta hai aur F par taare ki chamakdaar image banti hai.
- **Satellite dish** ka aakar bhi concave hota hai. Wo radio aur TV waves (jo aankh se dikhne wali roshni nahi hain, par roshni ki tarah reflect hoti hain) ko ek jagah ikattha karti hai, jahan receiver lagaya jaata hai. Yahan **wahi shape wala idea** kaam karta hai.

## Part C: Convex mirror ke uses (zyada dikhana)

Convex mirror ki image hamesha **virtual, erect aur diminished (chhoti)** banti hai. Chhoti image ka matlab ye hai ki **bahut saara hissa mirror ke chhote se aakar mein sama jaata hai**, yaani **field of view bahut badi hoti hai.**

```
 PLANE MIRROR (kam hissa dikhta hai)

          ═══════════          <- plane mirror
         ╱╲         ╱╲
        ╱  ╲       ╱  ╲
       ╱    ╲     ╱    ╲
      ╱      ╲   ╱      ╲
     ╱        ╲ ╱        ╲
    ╱        aankh        ╲

 CONVEX MIRROR (zyada hissa dikhta hai)

          ╰─────────╯          <- convex mirror (aankh ki taraf ubhra hua)
  ────────╲         ╱────────
           ╲       ╱
            ╲     ╱
             ╲   ╱
              ╲ ╱
             aankh
```

> **Figure 6.4** – Field of view ka taulna (dono mirror ek jitne chhote). Aankh se mirror ke dono kinaaron tak lines, aur wahan se reflect hokar bahar jaati lines. Bahar wali do lines ke beech ka hissa hi mirror mein dikhta hai. Plane mirror mein ye lines utne hi angle par lautti hain, isliye kam hissa dikhta hai. Convex mirror ke kinaare bahar ki taraf mude hain, isliye lines bahut chaudi failti hain aur bahut bada hissa (chhoti image ke roop mein) dikh jaata hai.

### Vehicle ka rear-view (wing) mirror

Gaadi ke side ya peeche dekhne wale mirror **convex** hote hain, kyunki driver ko peeche aur side ki poori sadak ek saath dikhni chahiye. Plane mirror mein itna kam hissa dikhta hai ki aas paas ki gaadi chhoot sakti hai.

Dhyan do ki gaadi ke convex mirror par aksar likha hota hai: **"Objects in mirror are closer than they appear"**. Matlab: peeche ki gaadi jitni door dikhti hai, asal mein utni door nahi hai, wo zyada paas hai. Wajah: image **chhoti** hai, aur hamara dimaag chhoti cheez ko door samajhta hai. Example 4 mein is baat ko number se dekhenge.

### Security mirrors: dukaan, mod aur parking

Dukaan ke kone mein, parking mein ya pahaad ke mod par bade **convex mirror** lagaye jaate hain. Ek hi mirror mein dukaan ka poora hissa ya mod ke doosri taraf ki sadak dikh jaati hai. Kahani wala bada gol sheesha yahi hai.

### Street lamp ka reflector, ATM aur bus ke mirrors

- **Street lamp ka reflector** (kuch lamps mein): convex jaisa ubhra hua reflector roshni ko **chauda failaata hai**, jisse zyada jagah par ujala padta hai.
- **ATM ke upar chhota mirror** aur **bus ke andar conductor/driver ka mirror**: convex hote hain, taaki peeche ya poori bus ka hissa ek nazar mein dikh jaye.

## Part F: Solved examples

*(Examples 2, 3, 4, 6, 7 aur 8 mein mirror formula hai: thoda extra, Class 10 overlap, Topic 5 Part F wala.)* Hum sign convention New Cartesian wali rakhenge: pole P origin hai, jis taraf se roshni aa rahi hai (object ki taraf) wo disha **negative**, mirror ke peeche ki disha **positive**. Concave mirror ka f aur R **negative**, convex ka **positive**. Formula: 1/v + 1/u = 1/f, aur m = −v/u = h′/h.

### Example 1: Headlight ka reflector

Ek car ki headlight ka concave reflector R = 24 cm ka hai. Bulb pole se kitni door rakhna chahiye ki parallel beam mile?

Radius 24 cm hai, to f = R ÷ 2 = 24 cm ÷ 2 = **12 cm** (yahan sirf doori chahiye, isliye sign nahi lagaya)

Bulb F par rakha jayega, yaani pole se **12 cm** door.

Jaanch: R = 2 × f = 2 × 12 cm = 24 cm ✅

### Example 2: Solar cooker mein bartan kahan rakhein?

Solar cooker ke concave reflector ki focal length f = 40 cm hai. Bartan reflector ke pole se kitni door rakhna chahiye?

Sooraj bahut door hai, to u = −∞ (1/u = 0). Sign convention ke saath f = −40 cm.

1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/(−40 cm) − 0 = −1/40 cm⁻¹

v = **−40 cm** (mirror ke saamne, 40 cm par)

Yaani bartan pole se **40 cm** door, F par hona chahiye.

Jaanch: 1/v + 1/u = −1/40 cm⁻¹ + 0 = −1/40 cm⁻¹ = 1/f ✅

### Example 3: Shaving mirror ki magnification

Ek concave shaving mirror ki focal length f = −30 cm hai. Chehra mirror se 10 cm door hai (u = −10 cm). Image kahan banegi aur kitni badi dikhegi?

1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/(−30 cm) − 1/(−10 cm) = −1/30 cm⁻¹ + 3/30 cm⁻¹ = 2/30 cm⁻¹ = 1/15 cm⁻¹

v = **+15 cm** (mirror ke peeche, virtual)

m = −v/u = −(+15 cm) ÷ (−10 cm) = **+1.5**

Image virtual, erect aur 1.5 guna badi hai.

Jaanch: 1/v + 1/u = 1/(15 cm) + 1/(−10 cm) = 2/30 cm⁻¹ − 3/30 cm⁻¹ = −1/30 cm⁻¹ = 1/f ✅

### Example 4: Rear-view mirror mein gaadi

Ek car ke convex rear-view mirror ki focal length f = +2 m hai. Peeche ki ek gaadi mirror se 4 m door hai (u = −4 m). Image kahan banegi aur kitni badi dikhegi?

1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/(2 m) − 1/(−4 m) = 2/4 m⁻¹ + 1/4 m⁻¹ = 3/4 m⁻¹

v = 4/3 m = **+1.33 m** (mirror ke peeche, virtual)

m = −v/u = −(+1.33 m) ÷ (−4 m) = **+0.333** (yaani 1/3)

Gaadi ki image asli size ki sirf **ek tihai** dikhti hai. Chhoti image ko dimaag door ki cheez samajhta hai, isliye gaadi asal mein jitni door lagti hai usse zyada paas hoti hai.

Jaanch: 1/v + 1/u = 3/4 m⁻¹ − 1/4 m⁻¹ = 2/4 m⁻¹ = 1/(2 m) = 1/f ✅

### Example 5: Field of view (sirf samajhne ke liye)

Ek hi jagah par ek plane mirror aur ek convex mirror, dono ek jaise chhote size ke lagaye hain. Peeche ki sadak par 4 gaadiyaan khadi hain. Kaun sa mirror kitni gaadiyaan dikhayega?

Plane mirror cheezon ko asli size mein dikhata hai, to us sheeshe mein sirf do ya teen gaadiyaan aati hain. Convex mirror cheezon ko chhota dikhata hai (Example 4 mein 1/3), to wahi sheesha chaaro gaadiyaan aur aas paas ka rasta dikha deta hai. Isliye wide view ke liye convex mirror chuna jaata hai.

Kami ye hai ki convex mirror mein gaadiyaan chhoti aur door lagti hain, isliye driver ko doori ka andaza sambhal kar karna padta hai.

### Example 6: Dentist ka mirror

Ek dentist ke concave mirror ki focal length f = −5 cm hai. Daant mirror se 2 cm door hai (u = −2 cm). Image kahan banegi aur kitni badi?

1/v = 1/f − 1/u = −1/5 + 1/2 = −2/10 + 5/10 = 3/10 cm⁻¹

v = 10/3 cm = **+3.33 cm** (mirror ke peeche, virtual)

m = −v/u = −(+3.33 cm) ÷ (−2 cm) = **+1.67**

Daant ki image virtual, erect aur 1.67 guna badi dikhti hai (sahi mayne mein 5/3 guna).

Jaanch: 1/v + 1/u = 3/10 + (−1/2) = 3/10 − 5/10 = −2/10 = −1/5 = 1/f ✅

### Example 7: ENT doctor ka head mirror

Ek head mirror ki R = 50 cm hai (concave). Doctor ka lamp mirror se 100 cm door hai. Lamp ki roshni mirror se kitni door ek chamakdaar point par ikatthi hogi? Aur point lamp ke size ka kitna hoga?

R = −50 cm, f = R ÷ 2 = −25 cm; u = −100 cm

1/v = 1/f − 1/u = −1/25 + 1/100 = −4/100 + 1/100 = −3/100 cm⁻¹

v = −100/3 cm = **−33.3 cm** (mirror ke saamne, real)

m = −v/u = −(−33.3 cm) ÷ (−100 cm) = **−0.333**

Lamp ki chhoti (1/3 size ki), ulti, real image mirror se 33.3 cm par banti hai. Doctor kaan ya gale ko wahin laate hain, to chhoti jagah par tez roshni milti hai.

Jaanch: 1/v + 1/u = −3/100 + (−1/100) = −4/100 = −1/25 = 1/f ✅

### Example 8: Dukaan ka security mirror

Ek dukaan ke kone mein convex security mirror hai jiski R = 3 m. Ek grahak mirror se 6 m door khada hai. Image kahan banegi? Agar grahak 1.5 m lamba hai, to image kitni lambi dikhegi?

R = +3 m, f = R ÷ 2 = +1.5 m; u = −6 m

1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/1.5 + 1/6 = 2/3 + 1/6 = 4/6 + 1/6 = 5/6 m⁻¹

v = 6/5 m = **+1.2 m** (mirror ke peeche, virtual)

m = −v/u = −(+1.2 m) ÷ (−6 m) = **+0.2**

h′ = m × h = 0.2 × 1.5 m = **0.3 m = 30 cm**

Mirror mein grahak ki image sirf 30 cm lambi dikhti hai, lekin dukaan ka poora hissa ek saath dikh jaata hai.

Jaanch: 1/v + 1/u = 5/6 + (−1/6) = 4/6 = 2/3 = 1/1.5 = 1/f ✅

## Part H: Suraksha (safety)

1. **Concave mirror ya magnifying cheez se kabhi bhi seedha Sooraj ko mat dekho.** Aankhon ko gambhir nuksaan ho sakta hai.
2. **Dhoop ko concave mirror se kisi jalne wali cheez (kagaz, kapde, sookhi ghaas) par ikattha mat karo.** Wo aag pakad sakti hai.
3. **Solar cooker ka reflector aur bartan bahut garam hote hain.** Haath ya chehra door rakho, aur dastane ya kapde se hi pakdo.
4. Toote hue sheeshe se haath kat sakta hai, isliye phoota mirror turant hata do.

## Part I: Ray rules ka revision aur sign convention

### Chaar khaas kirnein (concave aur convex mirror)

| Kiran | Concave mirror mein reflect hokar | Convex mirror mein reflect hokar |
|---|---|---|
| Principal axis ke parallel | F se guzarti hai | Aisi dikhti hai jaise F se aa rahi ho (peeche se) |
| F se guzarti (concave) ya F ki taraf jaati (convex) | Principal axis ke parallel ho jaati hai | Principal axis ke parallel ho jaati hai |
| C se guzarti (concave) ya C ki taraf jaati (convex) | Usi raaste wapas aa jaati hai | Usi raaste wapas aa jaati hai |
| Pole P par aati hui | Principal axis ke doosri taraf, barabar angle par (∠r = ∠i) | Principal axis ke doosri taraf, barabar angle par (∠r = ∠i) |

### Thoda extra (Class 10 overlap): New Cartesian sign convention (chhota rup)

- Pole P ko origin maano; principal axis ko x-axis.
- Object hamesha mirror ke baayein rakha jaata hai; roshni baayein se daayein aati hai.
- Doori ko roshni ki disha (daayein) mein **positive**, ulti disha (baayein) mein **negative** maano.
- Object ki doori u **negative** hoti hai. Real image ki doori v **negative**, virtual image ki **positive**.
- Concave mirror ka f aur R **negative**; convex ka **positive**.
- Principal axis ke oopar height **positive**, neeche **negative**. Real image ulti hoti hai (h′ negative, m negative); virtual image seedhi (m positive).

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Headlight mein bulb ko C ya P par rakh dena**
Bulb F par rakha jaata hai, tabhi parallel beam milti hai. Headlight ke R = 24 cm ke liye bulb 12 cm par, 24 cm par nahi.

**Galti 2: Rear-view mirror ko concave samajhna**
Concave mirror ki image door ki cheezon ke liye ulti hoti hai aur view bahut chhota hota hai. Convex ki image hamesha seedhi aur chhoti hoti hai, isliye driver ke liye convex chuna jaata hai.

**Galti 3: "Convex mirror mein cheez chhoti dikhti hai, to wo door ho gayi."**
Image chhoti hai, par cheez wahin hai. Isiliye likha hota hai: "Objects in mirror are closer than they appear."

**Galti 4: Solar cooker ke flat reflector aur concave dish ko ek jaisa samajhna**
Flat sheesha sirf zyada dhoop andar bhejta hai. Concave dish dhoop ko F par **ikattha** karti hai, to zyada garmi milti hai.

**Galti 5: Sign lagana bhool jana**
Concave mirror ke liye f = −30 cm, u = −10 cm likhna hai. f ka sign bhoolkar (f = +30 cm lekar) 1/v = 1/30 cm⁻¹ + 1/10 cm⁻¹ = 4/30 cm⁻¹ likhne par v = 7.5 cm aata hai, jo galat hai. Sahi v = +15 cm.

**Galti 6: Units chhod dena**
cm aur m milakar mat likho. Ek hi unit mein poora hisaab karo (jaise sab cm ya sab m).

## Quick Recap

- **Plane mirror ke uses:** dressing aur bathroom mirror, periscope, kaleidoscope, barber ki dukaan, solar cooker ke flat reflector, kamre ko bada dikhana. Wajah: virtual, erect, barabar size ki image.
- **Concave mirror, source F par:** reflect hokar parallel beam: headlight, torch, searchlight, spotlight.
- **Concave mirror, cheez F ke andar:** badi, erect, virtual image: shaving, make-up, dentist ka mirror, ENT ka head mirror.
- **Concave mirror, door ki roshni:** F par ikattha: solar cooker, solar furnace, reflecting telescope. Satellite dish waves ke liye wahi shape.
- **Convex mirror:** hamesha virtual, erect, diminished image aur chauda field of view: rear-view mirror, security mirror, street lamp reflector, ATM aur bus ke mirrors.
- "Objects in mirror are closer than they appear": image chhoti hoti hai isliye cheez door lagti hai.
- Kahani ka hal: mod ka mirror = convex; headlight = concave, bulb F par; driver ka chhota mirror = convex (chauda view).
- **Suraksha:** Sooraj ko mirror se mat dekho; dhoop ko jalne wali cheez par ikattha mat karo; solar cooker garam hota hai.
- Mirror formula 1/v + 1/u = 1/f, m = −v/u, aur sign convention hamesha lagao.

## Practice Questions

(Questions 5, 6, 8 and 9 use the mirror formula: thoda extra.)

1. Name the type of mirror used in each case and give one reason: (a) a blind-turn mirror on a hill road, (b) a car headlight, (c) a dentist's mirror, (d) a periscope, (e) a solar cooker.

2. Why is the bulb of a headlight placed at the focus of the concave reflector? What would happen if it were placed at the centre of curvature?

3. The reflector of a torch has a radius of curvature of 10 cm. At what distance from the pole should the bulb be placed to get a parallel beam?

4. A solar cooker has a concave reflector of focal length 50 cm. Where should the vessel be placed?

5. A concave shaving mirror has a focal length of 20 cm. A person stands 8 cm from it. Find the position of the image and the magnification. State the nature of the image.

6. A convex mirror of focal length 1 m is fitted on a vehicle. A scooter is 3 m behind the mirror. Find the image distance and the magnification. Is the image bigger or smaller than the scooter?

7. Why do vehicles use convex mirrors as rear-view mirrors and not plane mirrors? Why is "Objects in mirror are closer than they appear" written on them?

8. A dentist uses a concave mirror of focal length 6 cm. The tooth is 3 cm from the mirror. Find the image distance and the magnification.

9. A torch reflector has f = 5 cm. The bulb is moved from F to a point 10 cm from the pole. Find where the image of the bulb forms and say what happens to the beam.

10. A shop is 5 m deep and has a large plane mirror on its back wall. A customer stands 1.5 m from the mirror, facing it. How far does the image of the front wall appear from the customer?

11. Say whether each statement is true or false, with a reason.
    (a) A convex mirror can form a real image of a real object.
    (b) A concave mirror can be used to start a fire using sunlight.
    (c) A flat mirror in a box cooker concentrates sunlight at one point.
    (d) A concave mirror is used as a shaving mirror because it forms an enlarged erect image when the face is within the focal length.

12. Write two safety rules for using concave mirrors and solar cookers.

13. In which of these cases is the object placed between P and F of a concave mirror: a make-up mirror, a torch, a solar cooker, a searchlight? Explain.

### Answers

1. (a) **Convex**: chhoti, erect image aur chauda field of view, to mod ke doosri taraf ki sadak dikh jaati hai. (b) **Concave**: bulb F par, to reflect hokar parallel beam banta hai. (c) **Concave**: daant F ke andar ho to badi, erect, virtual image. (d) **Do plane mirrors**: roshni ko 90° mod kar dono baar, seedhi aur barabar size ki image. (e) **Concave**: parallel dhoop F par ikatthi hoti hai.

2. F se aati kirnein reflect hokar principal axis ke parallel ho jaati hain, to door tak seedha beam milta hai. Bulb C par ho to kirnein C se aati hain, jo seedhi wapas C par laut aati hain, to parallel beam nahi milta aur roshni bikhar jaati hai.

3. R = 10 cm, f = R ÷ 2 = 10 cm ÷ 2 = **5 cm**. Bulb pole se 5 cm par.
   Jaanch: 2 × 5 cm = 10 cm = R ✅

4. Dhoop parallel hai, to vessel F par: **50 cm** door.
   Jaanch: u = −∞, f = −50 cm: 1/v = −1/50 cm⁻¹ − 0 = −1/50 cm⁻¹, v = −50 cm ✅

5. f = −20 cm, u = −8 cm.
   1/v = 1/f − 1/u = −1/20 + 1/8 = −2/40 + 5/40 = 3/40 cm⁻¹, to v = 40/3 cm = **+13.3 cm**.
   m = −v/u = −(13.3 cm) ÷ (−8 cm) = **+1.67**. Image virtual, erect, badi.
   Jaanch: 1/v + 1/u = 3/40 − 5/40 = −2/40 = −1/20 = 1/f ✅

6. f = +1 m, u = −3 m.
   1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/1 + 1/3 = 3/3 + 1/3 = 4/3 m⁻¹, to v = 3/4 m = **+0.75 m**.
   m = −v/u = −(0.75 m) ÷ (−3 m) = **+0.25**. Image scooter se **chhoti** (ek chauthai) hai.
   Jaanch: 1/v + 1/u = 4/3 − 1/3 = 3/3 = 1 = 1/1 = 1/f ✅

7. Convex mirror mein chauda field of view milta hai, plane mirror mein kam. Warning isliye likhi hai kyunki image chhoti hoti hai, aur dimaag chhoti image ko door ki cheez samajhta hai, jabki asli gaadi paas hoti hai.

8. f = −6 cm, u = −3 cm.
   1/v = −1/6 + 1/3 = −1/6 + 2/6 = 1/6 cm⁻¹, to v = **+6 cm**.
   m = −v/u = −(6 cm) ÷ (−3 cm) = **+2**. Image virtual, erect aur 2 guna badi.
   Jaanch: 1/v + 1/u = 1/6 − 1/3 = 1/6 − 2/6 = −1/6 = 1/f ✅

9. f = −5 cm, u = −10 cm.
   1/v = −1/5 + 1/10 = −2/10 + 1/10 = −1/10 cm⁻¹, to v = **−10 cm** (mirror ke saamne, real).
   m = −v/u = −(−10 cm) ÷ (−10 cm) = **−1**. Image bulb jitni badi, ulti, C par.
   Beam parallel nahi rehta: kirnein C par milkar phir failti hain.
   Jaanch: 1/v + 1/u = −1/10 − 1/10 = −2/10 = −1/5 = 1/f ✅

10. Saamne ki deewar mirror se 5 m door hai, to uski image mirror ke peeche 5 m par banti hai. Customer mirror se 1.5 m aage hai, to image customer se 1.5 m + 5 m = **6.5 m** door dikhti hai (jabki asli deewar sirf 5 m − 1.5 m = 3.5 m door hai).
    Jaanch: image distance = object distance = 5 m ✅

11. (a) **False** ❌. Convex mirror mein image hamesha virtual hoti hai.
    (b) **True** ✅. Concave mirror dhoop ko F par ikattha karta hai, jahan kagaz jal sakta hai.
    (c) **False** ❌. Flat mirror sirf zyada dhoop andar bhejta hai, ikattha nahi karta.
    (d) **True** ✅. Chehra F ke andar ho to image badi, erect aur virtual hoti hai.

12. Koi bhi do: Sooraj ko seedha mirror se mat dekho; dhoop ko jalne wali cheez par ikattha mat karo; solar cooker ka reflector aur bartan garam hote hain, to dastane se pakdo.

13. **Make-up mirror**, kyunki chehra F ke andar hota hai (P aur F ke beech) aur badi, erect image milti hai. Torch aur searchlight mein bulb F par hota hai, aur solar cooker mein vessel F par.

### (a) Key definitions

| Shabd | Matlab |
|---|---|
| Light | Energy ka wo roop jo hamein cheezein dekhne deta hai; vacuum mein speed 3 × 10⁸ m/s |
| Ray | Light ke raaste ko dikhane wali seedhi line (teer ke saath) |
| Beam | Kirnon ka samooh (parallel, converging ya diverging) |
| Shadow | Opaque cheez ke peeche wo jagah jahan light nahi pahunchti |
| Umbra | Shadow ka bilkul andhera, saaf hissa |
| Penumbra | Shadow ka aadha andhera, kinare wala hissa |
| Reflection | Light ka kisi surface se takrakar wapas aana |

### (b) Formulas aur relations

| Kya | Formula |
|---|---|
| Reflection ka law | ∠i = ∠r |
| Incident aur reflected ray ke beech ka angle | 2i (i = incidence ka angle; ray ka mudna, yaani deviation, 180° − 2i hai) |
| Plane mirror: image distance | Image distance = object distance |
| Do inclined mirrors (angle θ) se images | n = 360° ÷ θ − 1 (jab 360° ÷ θ even ho) |
| Focal length aur radius of curvature | f = R ÷ 2 |
| Magnification (thoda extra) | m = h′/h = −v/u |
| Mirror formula (thoda extra) | 1/v + 1/u = 1/f |
| Sign convention (thoda extra) | Pole origin; object ki taraf (baayein) negative; mirror ke peeche positive; concave f negative, convex f positive |

### (c) Image ki nature

**Concave mirror**

| Object ki position | Image ki position | Nature | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| Infinity par | F par | Real, inverted | Bahut chhoti (point jaisi) |

| C aur F ke beech | C ke bahar | Real, inverted | Enlarged |
| F par | Infinity par | Real, inverted | Bahut badi |
