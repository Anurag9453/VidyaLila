# Topic 1: Light, Shadow aur Pinhole Camera

Pichhle chapter mein humne dekha ki **sound ko chalne ke liye medium chahiye**: hawa, paani ya koi thos cheez. Vacuum mein bell bajti rahe, tab bhi awaaz nahi aati. Par ek cheez hai jo vacuum mein bhi aa jaati hai: Sun aur Earth ke beech lagbhag 15 crore kilometre ka khaali space hai, phir bhi Sun hamein dikhta hai aur uski garmi bhi mehsoos hoti hai. Ye energy hai **roshni (light)**, wahi energy jisse hum **dekhte** hain. Is topic mein hum jaanenge ki light kya hai, kitni tez chalti hai, seedhi kyun chalti hai, parchhai (shadow) kaise banti hai, aur ek dabbe ke chhote se chhed se poori tasveer kaise ban jaati hai (pinhole camera).

## School ke annual day ka parchhai wala show

School ke annual day ki shaam thi. Stage-hand Mr. Surve ek safed chadar (sheet) tan rahe the. Chadar ke peeche unhone ek lamp (bulb) laga diya. Lamp aur chadar ke beech kaagaz ke puppets (kathputli) chhadi se pakad kar hilaane the. Audience chadar ki doosri taraf baithegi, aur unhe sirf **kaali parchhaiyan** dikhengi.

Hana, jo show mein help kar rahi thi, ek 10 cm lamba kaagaz ka sher hilaane lagi. Usne ek ajeeb baat dekhi:

- Jab usne sher ko **lamp ke paas** kiya, chadar par uski parchhai **bahut badi** ho gayi, par uske kinaare **dhundhle (blurry)** ho gaye.
- Jab usne sher ko **chadar ke paas** laga diya, parchhai **chhoti** ho gayi, par kinaare ekdum **saaf (sharp)**.

Hana ne poochha, "Mr. Surve, parchhai kabhi badi aur dhundhli, kabhi chhoti aur saaf kyun ho jaati hai?"

Mr. Surve muskuraye, "Achha sawaal hai. Iska jawab light ke ek bahut seedhe se niyam mein chhupa hai: **light seedhi line mein chalti hai.** Pehle light ko theek se jaan lo, phir hum wapas is sher par aayenge." Is kahani ko hum topic ke beech mein solve karenge.

## Part A: Light kya hai, aur kaun si cheez kaisi hai

### 1. Light ek energy hai, aur use medium ki zaroorat nahi

**Light** energy ka wo roop hai jo hamari aankh par girti hai to hamein cheezein dikhti hain (dekhne ka ehsaas, sensation of sight). Chaand aur taare door hain, par hum unhe dekh paate hain, kyunki unki taraf se light hamari aankh tak aati hai.

Sound aur light mein sabse bada farak yahi hai:

- **Sound** ek mechanical wave hai: use hawa jaise medium ke kanon ki zaroorat hoti hai, isliye vacuum mein sound nahi chalti ([sound.md](../sound/sound.md) mein dekha tha).
- **Light ko kisi medium ki zaroorat nahi.** Wo vacuum mein bhi chalti hai. Sun ki light khaali space se hokar Earth tak aati hai. Isiliye Moon par khade astronaut ko Sun saaf dikhta hai, par wo apne saathi se bina radio ke baat nahi kar sakta.

Light hawa, paani aur glass jaise transparent medium mein bhi chalti hai, bas wo unke bina bhi chal sakti hai.

### 2. Luminous aur non-luminous cheezein

Kuch cheezein **khud** light banati hain, unhe **luminous** kehte hain. Baaki cheezein khud light nahi banati, unhe **non-luminous** kehte hain.

| Luminous (khud ki light) | Non-luminous (khud ki light nahi) |
|---|---|
| Sun, doosre tare | Chaand, planets |
| Jalta hua bulb, tubelight | Kitaab, kursi, table |
| Candle ya diya ki lau | Mirror, saaf dhaatu ki chadar |
| Jugnu (firefly) | Copy, hum khud |
| On phone ki screen | Off phone ki screen |

Ab ek zaroori sawaal: agar kitaab khud light nahi banati, to hum use dekhte kaise hain? Jawab: **kitaab par lamp ya Sun ki light girti hai, kitaab us light ko har taraf wapas uchhaal deti hai, aur uchhali hui light ka kuch hissa hamari aankh mein aata hai.** Tab hum kitaab ko dekhte hain. Bilkul andhere kamre mein tum kitaab ko nahi dekh paate, kyunki us par girne wali light hi nahi hai.

**Chaand ka example sabse mashhoor hai.** Chaand khud light nahi banata. Wo **Sun ki light ko wapas uchhaalta** hai, isiliye hum use dekh paate hain. Yaani Chaand non-luminous hai, chahe poornima ki raat mein kitna bhi chamakdaar lage. Mirror bhi non-luminous hai: wo bhi bas light ko wapas uchhaalta hai. Ye "uchhalna" hi **reflection** hai, iski poori kahani agle topics mein.

### 3. Transparent, translucent aur opaque

Light jab kisi material par girti hai, to material ke hisaab se teen cheezein ho sakti hain.

| Kism | Light ke saath kya hota hai | Teen examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Transparent** | Lagbhag poori light paar chali jaati hai; paar ki cheez saaf dikhti hai | Saaf glass, saaf paani, hawa |
| **Translucent** | Kuch light paar jaati hai; roshni to aati hai par paar ki cheez dhundhli dikhti hai | Butter paper, tracing paper, frosted glass |
| **Opaque** | Light paar nahi jaati (ya to roki jaati hai ya wapas uchhal jaati hai) | Lakdi (wood), patthar, dhaatu |

Do dhyan dene wali baatein:

- Ye material ki **motai (thickness)** par bhi depend karta hai. Patla kaagaz roshni ka kuch hissa paar jaane deta hai, par moti cardboard nahi. Bahut gehra paani bhi neeche andhera kar deta hai.
- Opaque cheez ke peeche parchhai banti hai, kyunki wo light ko rok leti hai. Ye hum Part D mein dekhenge.

## Part B: Light ki speed

Light bahut, bahut tez chalti hai. **Vacuum mein light ki speed:**

> **c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s** (yaani 300000000 m/s, lagbhag **3 lakh km/s**)

Hawa mein bhi light ki speed lagbhag itni hi hoti hai. (Paani ya glass mein wo thodi kam ho jaati hai: ye agle chapter ki baat hai.)

Sound se milao: sound hawa mein 340 m/s chalti hai. Light sound se 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 340 ≈ 8.8 × 10⁵ guna, yaani lagbhag **9 lakh guna** tez hai.

Speed = doori ÷ samay se **samay = doori ÷ speed**. Is formula se hum kai sawaal solve karenge (Part G mein):

- **Sun se Earth tak:** doori 1.5 × 10¹¹ m (yaani 15 crore km). Light ko lagte hain 1.5 × 10¹¹ m ÷ 3 × 10⁸ m/s = **500 s = 8 minute 20 second**. Yaani abhi tum Sun ko jaisa dekh rahe ho, wo 8 minute 20 second purani tasveer hai.
- **Chaand se Earth tak:** doori 3.84 × 10⁸ m, aur light ko lagte hain lagbhag **1.28 s**.

### Light year: doori ki unit, samay ki nahi

Taare itne door hain ki unki doori ko metre ya km mein likhna bahut mushkil ho jaata hai. Isliye ek badi unit banayi gayi:

> **1 light year (ly)** = wo **doori** jo light **ek saal** mein tay karti hai ≈ **9.46 × 10¹⁵ m**

Naam mein "year" hai, par **light year samay nahi, doori ki unit hai.** (Hum isse Example 5 mein check karenge.) Hamare Sun ke baad sabse paas ke taare ki light hum tak lagbhag 4.2 saal mein pahunchti hai, to wo taara lagbhag 4.2 light year door hai.

## Part C: Light seedhi line mein chalti hai

Ek **uniform transparent medium** mein (jaise hawa ya saaf paani, jiski har jagah ek jaisi hi cheez ho) **light seedhi line mein chalti hai.** Isse **rectilinear propagation** kehte hain (rectilinear = seedhi line mein).

### Iske saboot

1. **Parchhai:** Opaque cheez ke peeche parchhai banti hai, kyunki light seedhi chalti hai aur cheez ke peeche mud kar nahi aa sakti. Agar light mud sakti, to cheez ke peeche bhi roshni pahunch jaati.
2. **Dhuein ya dhool bhare kamre mein roshni ki patti:** Chhed se aati dhoop ya torch ki roshni mein dhool ke kan chamakte hain, aur light ka raasta ek seedhi patti jaisa dikhta hai.
3. **Pipe ka test:** Ek lambi seedhi pipe se tum door rakhe diye ko dekh sakte ho. Par pipe ko modh do, to diya dikhna band ho jaata hai, kyunki light pipe ke modh par deewar se ruk jaati hai.

```
 Seedhi pipe (diya dikhta hai):

   (o)[========================]  * diya
   aankh <----------- light ------------

 Tedhi pipe (diya nahi dikhta):

   (o)==========\
                 \
                  \
                   ==========  * diya
   (light seedhi chalti hai, mod par pipe ki deewar se ruk jaati hai)
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Seedhi pipe mein se diya dikhta hai, tedhi pipe mein se nahi. Isse pata chalta hai ki light seedhi line mein chalti hai.

### Ray aur beam

Light ke raaste ko dikhane ke liye hum ek **seedhi line** kheenchte hain aur uske ek sire par **teer ka nishaan (arrowhead)** lagate hain: isse **ray** (kiran) kehte hain. Teer light ke chalne ki disha batata hai.

Bahut saari rays ke **gucchhe** ko **beam** kehte hain. Beam teen tarah ka ho sakta hai:

- **Parallel beam:** rays ek doosre ke samaantar (jaise Sun ki rays, jo itni door se aati hain ki lagbhag samaantar ho jaati hain).
- **Convergent beam:** rays aage jaakar ek point par milti hain.
- **Divergent beam:** rays ek point se nikalkar failti jaati hain (jaise chhote bulb se nikalti roshni).

```
 Parallel beam:

 ---------------->
 ---------------->
 ---------------->

 Convergent beam (rays P par milti hain):

 -------\
         ------\
 ----------------------> * P
         ------/
 -------/

 Divergent beam (rays S se faili hain):

                  /----->
           /------
 S *--------------------->
           \------
                  \----->
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Teen tarah ke beam: parallel, convergent aur divergent. Har ray ek seedhi line hai jis par teer light ki disha dikhata hai.

## Part D: Shadow (parchhai) kaise banti hai

### 1. Point source: saaf parchhai

Jab ek **opaque** cheez light ko rok leti hai, to uske peeche ek andhera hissa banta hai: wahi **shadow** hai. Shadow ka aakar do cheezon par depend karta hai: **source (lamp) kitna bada hai** aur **cheez lamp se kitni door hai.**

Pehle ek **point source** socho: itna chhota lamp ki use ek bindu jaisa maana ja sake. Usse rays sirf ek point se nikalti hain. Puppet ke kinaare ko chhookar guzarti rays jahan chadar par girti hain, wahi shadow ka sira hota hai. Isliye shadow ka kinaara **saaf (sharp)** hota hai: ya to us jagah light hai, ya bilkul nahi.

```
                                                              │
                                                         -----#  <- shadow ka upar ka sira
                                                --------/     #
                                     ----------/              #
                            --------/                         #
                 -----#----/                                  #
        --------/     #                                       #
  *----/              #                                       #  <- shadow (30 cm)
        --------\     #                                       #
lamp             -----#----\                                  #
                            --------\                         #
                  puppet             ----------\              #
                                                --------\     #
                                                         -----#  <- shadow ka neeche ka sira
                                                              │
                                                          sheet
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Point source se saaf shadow: lamp se puppet ki doori 20 cm, lamp se chadar ki doori 60 cm. 10 cm ke puppet ki shadow 30 cm ki hai. (Sketch ka scale poora sahi nahi hai, par anupaat sahi hai: shadow puppet se 3 guna badi.)

### 2. Shadow ka size: "doori badhne par parchhai chhoti"

Figure 1.3 mein lamp se nikalne wali rays do **tikone (triangles)** banati hain: ek chhota (lamp se puppet tak) aur ek bada (lamp se chadar tak). Dono **ek jaise aakar ke (similar)** hain, isliye unki lambaiyon ka **anupaat (ratio)** barabar hota hai:

> **shadow ki height ÷ puppet ki height = (lamp se chadar ki doori) ÷ (lamp se puppet ki doori)**

Yaani **shadow = puppet ki height × (lamp se chadar) ÷ (lamp se puppet)**.

Ye saaf batata hai: **puppet lamp ke jitna paas, shadow utni badi.** Puppet jaise-jaise lamp se door (chadar ki taraf) jaata hai, shadow chhoti hoti jaati hai, aur jab puppet bilkul chadar se laga ho, to shadow puppet ke barabar hi hoti hai.

### 3. Extended source: umbra aur penumbra

Asli lamp ya bulb **bindu** nahi hota; wo kuch chaudaai wala (extended) source hota hai. Bulb ka har chhota-chhota hissa ek alag point source jaisa kaam karta hai, aur har hissa puppet ki shadow **thodi alag jagah par** banata hai. Ye sab shadows ek doosre ko aanshik roop se dhak leti hain.

Isse chadar par do tarah ke hisse bante hain:

- **Umbra:** beech ka hissa jahan **bulb ke kisi bhi hisse ki** light nahi pahunch paati. Yahan poora andhera hota hai.
- **Penumbra:** umbra ke charo taraf ka halka hissa, jahan bulb ke **kuch** hisson ki light pahunch jaati hai aur kuch ki roki jaati hai. Yahan halka andhera hota hai, aur kinaara dhundhla dikhta hai.

```
                                                              │
                                                           ---│:
                                                     -----/   │:
                                              ------/         │: <- penumbra (halka andhera)
                                       ------/                │:
                                ------/             ----------│#
                          -----/ ------------------/          │#
            ----------#---------/                             │#
  O--------/ -----/   #                                       │#
  O   ------/         #                                       │# <- umbra (poora andhera)
  O--------\ -----\   #                                       │#
            ----------#---------\                             │#
lamp                      -----\ ------------------\          │#
                  puppet        ------\             ----------│#
                                       ------\                │:
                                              ------\         │: <- penumbra (halka andhera)
                                                     -----\   │:
                                                           ---│:
                                                          sheet
```

> **Figure 1.4** – Bade (extended) lamp, puppet aur chadar. "#" umbra hai, ":" penumbra hai. Lamp ke upar aur neeche ke sire se nikalti rays puppet ke kinaaron ko chhookar chadar par alag-alag jagah girti hain.

Ek aur galat samajh se bachna hai: **shadow puppet ki reflection nahi hai.** Shadow wo jagah hai jahan light **pahunchi hi nahi**; reflection tab hota hai jab light kisi surface se **uchhal kar** wapas aati hai. Dono alag cheezein hain, aur reflection ki baat hum agle topics mein karenge.

## Wapas annual day par: kahani ka hal

Ab Hana ka sawaal dobara dekhte hain. Mr. Surve ne samjhaya, "Humara lamp ek bulb hai, point nahi. Isliye sher ki parchhai mein **umbra aur penumbra** dono hain."

**Jab sher lamp ke paas tha** (lamp se sher 20 cm, lamp se chadar 60 cm):

- Shadow = 10 cm × 60 cm ÷ 20 cm = **30 cm**, yaani sher se 3 guna badi.
- Lamp ke itne paas hone se bulb ke alag-alag hisson ki shadows ek doosre se door-door khisak jaati hain, isliye **penumbra chaudi (badi)** ho jaati hai aur parchhai ke kinaare dhundhle dikhte hain.

**Jab sher chadar ke paas gaya** (lamp se sher 50 cm, lamp se chadar wahi 60 cm):

- Shadow = 10 cm × 60 cm ÷ 50 cm = **12 cm**, yaani sher ke aakar jaisi.
- Sher chadar ke itna paas hai ki bulb ke alag-alag hisson ki shadows ek doosre se bahut kam khisakti hain, isliye **penumbra bahut patla** hota hai aur kinaare **saaf** dikhte hain.

Jaanch: 12 cm ÷ 10 cm = 1.2 aur 60 ÷ 50 = 1.2 ✅. Aur 30 cm ÷ 10 cm = 3 aur 60 ÷ 20 = 3 ✅

Mr. Surve ne Hana ko ek tarkeeb di: "Agar tumhe **badi, dramatic** parchhai chahiye (jaise bada sher), to puppet lamp ke paas rakho, par kinaare dhundhle honge. Agar **saaf, tez** parchhai chahiye, to puppet ko chadar ke paas rakho. Aur agar lamp chhota ya door kar do, to penumbra aur patla ho jaayega."

Hana khush hokar boli, "Yaani main shadow ki size aur sharpness dono apne haath se control kar sakti hoon!"

## Part E: Eclipses (grahan)

Eclipse bhi **parchhai ki hi kahani** hai, bas bahut bade scale par.

**Suraj grahan (solar eclipse):** Jab **Chaand, Sun aur Earth ke beech mein** aa jaata hai (amavasya ke din, jab teeno lagbhag ek seedhi line mein hon), to Chaand Sun ki light ko rok leta hai aur **Chaand ki parchhai Earth ke ek chhote hisse par** padti hai.

- Jahan **umbra** padti hai, wahan se **poora suraj grahan** dikhta hai (din mein bhi andhera).
- Jahan **penumbra** padti hai, wahan **aanshik (partial) grahan** dikhta hai: Sun ka sirf kuch hissa dhaka hota hai.

```
                                                            │
  -------------------------->                               │
                                                            │
  -------------------------->                        -------│:
                                      --------------/       │:  <- penumbra: aanshik grahan
  --------------------------> #------\                      │:
                              #       --------------\       │:
  --------------------------> #                      -------│#  <- umbra: poora suraj grahan
                              #       --------------/       │:
  --------------------------> #------/                      │:
                                      --------------\       │:
  -------------------------->                        -------│:
                          Chandrama                         │
  -------------------------->                               │
                                                            │

  Surya ki kirnein                                       Prithvi
```

> **Figure 1.5** – Suraj grahan (scale ke hisaab se nahi): baayen taraf Sun ki kirnein Chaand ki taraf aa rahi hain. Sun bahut bada (extended) source hai, isliye uske upar aur neeche ke kinaaron se aati kirnein Chaand ke kinaaron ko chhookar tirchi lines banati hain (Figure 1.4 jaisa). Chaand Sun aur Earth ke beech mein hai. Umbra Earth ke ek chhote se hisse par padti hai, jahan se poora grahan dikhta hai. Uske charo taraf penumbra ka hissa hai, jahan se aanshik grahan dikhta hai.

**Chandra grahan (lunar eclipse):** Jab **Earth, Sun aur Chaand ke beech mein** aa jaati hai (poornima ki raat, jab teeno lagbhag ek seedhi line mein hon), to Earth ki parchhai Chaand par padti hai. Jab Chaand Earth ki umbra mein aata hai, to wo dhundhla ya halka laal dikhta hai. (Laal rang kyun dikhta hai, ye aage ki class ki baat hai.)

```
  -------------------------->                                       ---  <- penumbra
                                                     --------------/
  -------------------------->         --------------/
                              #-------\
  --------------------------> #        ----------------\
                              #                         --O--\
  --------------------------> #                           O    >         <- umbra (Chaand iske andar)
                              #                         --O--/
  --------------------------> #        ----------------/
                              #-------/
  -------------------------->         --------------\ Chandrama
                           Prithvi                   --------------\
  -------------------------->                                       ---  <- penumbra
```

> **Figure 1.6** – Chandra grahan (scale ke hisaab se nahi): Earth Sun aur Chaand ke beech mein hai, aur Chaand ("O") Earth ki umbra mein aa gaya hai.

Har mahine amavasya aur poornima aati hai, to har mahine grahan kyun nahi lagta? Kyunki Chaand ka raasta Earth ke raaste se thoda jhuka hua hai, isliye zyadatar baar Chaand ki parchhai Earth ke upar ya neeche se nikal jaati hai (aur Earth ki parchhai Chaand ke upar ya neeche se). Teeno ek seedhi line mein kabhi-kabhi hi aate hain.

> **Suraksha ki chetavni (bahut zaroori):** **Suraj grahan ko kabhi seedhe nangi aankhon se na dekho, aur na ordinary chashme, na sunglasses, na X-ray film ke through dekho.** Sun ki tez light aankh ke andar ke parde (retina) ko hamesha ke liye kharab kar sakti hai, aur grahan ke samay Sun ka sirf thoda sa hissa dikhne par bhi wo utna hi khatarnak hai. Surakshit tareeka: **pinhole camera** (agla Part) ya vishesh solar-filter wale certified chashme. **Chandra grahan** ko nangi aankhon se dekhna surakshit hai, kyunki Chaand se aati light bahut halki hoti hai.

## Part F: Pinhole camera

Pinhole camera ek bahut simple dabba hai. Ek band dabbe ki ek deewar mein ek **bahut chhota chhed (pinhole)** kar do, aur uske saamne wali deewar ki jagah ek **translucent screen** (butter paper ya tracing paper) laga do. Chhed ke saamne koi roshan cheez (jaise ped ya jalti hui candle) rakho, to screen par uski ek **tasveer (image)** ban jaati hai.

Ye image **ulti** hoti hai: upar ka hissa neeche aur neeche ka hissa upar (aur left-right bhi badal jaata hai). Aisa kyun? Kyunki light seedhi chalti hai:

- Ped ki **choti** (upar) se nikalne wali ray chhed se guzarti hai aur seedhi chalte hue screen par **neeche** pahunchti hai.
- Ped ki **jad** (neeche) se nikalne wali ray chhed se guzarti hai aur screen par **upar** pahunchti hai (figure mein jad axis par hai, isliye wo seedhi beech mein pahunchti hai).

Yaani rays chhed par **cross** karti hain, aur isliye image ulta ban jaata hai.

```
                          chhed         screen
  #-\                         │             │
  #  ---\                     │             │
  #      ----\                │             │
  #           ----\           │             │
  #                ----\      │             │
  #                     ---\  │             │
  #--------------------------> o-\-----------#
ped                           │  ---\       #
                              │      ----\  #
                              │           --# <- ulta image (neeche ki taraf)
                              │             │
                              │             │
                              │             │
  <--------------------------><------------->
               u                     v
```

> **Figure 1.7** – Pinhole camera: ped ki choti se aane wali ray chhed se guzar kar screen par neeche pahunchti hai, isliye image ulta hai. u = chhed se object ki doori, v = chhed se screen ki doori.

Ye image **real** hoti hai: rays sach mein screen par milti hain, isliye use screen par pakda ja sakta hai.

### Chhed ka size: saaf ya roshan?

- **Chhed chhota** karo: har point se bahut patli beam andar jaati hai, isliye image mein har point ek chhote dhabbe jaisa banta hai: image **saaf (sharp)** hoti hai, par **kam roshan (dim)**, kyunki kam light andar aati hai.
- **Chhed bada** karo: zyada light aati hai, image **zyada roshan** hoti hai, par har point ka dhabba bada ho jaata hai aur dhabbe ek doosre par chadh jaate hain: image **dhundhli (blurry)** ho jaati hai.

Isliye **bada chhed = roshan par dhundhli, chhota chhed = saaf par dim.** (Ye wahi baat hai jo shadow mein dekhi: chhota source, yaani chhota lamp, to saaf kinaara.)

### Image ka size: similar triangles

Figure 1.7 mein chhed ke dono taraf ke tikone similar hain (ek ped aur chhed ka, doosra image aur chhed ka). Isliye:

> **image ki height ÷ object ki height = chhed se screen ki doori ÷ chhed se object ki doori**
> **hᵢ ÷ hₒ = v ÷ u**

Aur **magnification m = hᵢ ÷ hₒ = v ÷ u.** Isse do baatein saaf hoti hain:

- Screen ko chhed se **door** (v zyada) karo, to image **badi** hoti hai (par aur dim).
- Object ko **door** (u zyada) le jao, to image **chhoti** hoti hai.

### Pinhole camera ke upyog

- **Suraj grahan ko surakshit tareeke se dekhna:** Pinhole camera se Sun ki image screen par banti hai. Hum Sun ko seedha dekhne ke bajay screen par uski tasveer (jisme grahan se kata hua Sun saaf dikhta hai) dekhte hain. Isme aankh ko koi khatra nahi.
- **Camera obscura:** Purane zamane ka ek bada kamra ya dabba, jisme ek chhote chhed se bahar ka drishya andar deewar par ulta dikhta tha. Painters aur scientists ise use karte the. Aaj ke camera isi idea se aage badhe hain.

## Part G: Solved examples

Is Part mein light ki speed c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s li gayi hai.

### Example 1: Sun se Earth tak light ka samay

Doori = 1.5 × 10¹¹ m, c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s.

t = doori ÷ speed = 1.5 × 10¹¹ m ÷ 3 × 10⁸ m/s = 0.5 × 10³ s = **500 s**

Minutes mein: 500 ÷ 60 = 8 minute aur 20 second (kyunki 8 × 60 = 480 s, aur 500 − 480 = 20 s). Yaani **8 min 20 s.**

Jaanch: 3 × 10⁸ m/s × 500 s = 1.5 × 10¹¹ m ✅

### Example 2: Chaand se Earth tak light ka samay

Chaand ki doori = 3.84 × 10⁸ m.

t = 3.84 × 10⁸ m ÷ 3 × 10⁸ m/s = **1.28 s**

Jaanch: 3 × 10⁸ m/s × 1.28 s = 3.84 × 10⁸ m ✅

(Yaani hum Chaand ko 1.28 second purane roop mein dekhte hain.)

### Example 3: 6 m ka kamra paar karne mein light ko kitna samay?

Doori = 6 m.

t = 6 m ÷ 3 × 10⁸ m/s = 2 × 10⁻⁸ s (yaani 0.00000002 s, ya 20 nanosecond)

Jaanch: 3 × 10⁸ m/s × 2 × 10⁻⁸ s = 6 m ✅

Itna kam samay ki hum ise mehsoos hi nahi kar sakte: switch dabate hi kamra roshan lagta hai.

### Example 4: 1 microsecond (1 µs) mein light kitni doori tay karti hai?

1 µs = 10⁻⁶ s.

Doori = speed × samay = 3 × 10⁸ m/s × 10⁻⁶ s = **300 m**

Jaanch: 300 m ÷ 3 × 10⁸ m/s = 10⁻⁶ s ✅

### Example 5: Light year kitne metre ka hota hai?

Pehle ek saal mein seconds: 1 saal = 365 din, 1 din = 24 ghante, 1 ghanta = 3600 s.

1 saal = 365 × 24 × 3600 s = 365 × 86400 s = 31 536 000 s ≈ **3.15 × 10⁷ s** (365.25 din lene par 3.156 × 10⁷ s, lagbhag wahi)

Light ek saal mein doori = c × t = 3 × 10⁸ m/s × 3.1536 × 10⁷ s = 9.4608 × 10¹⁵ m ≈ **9.46 × 10¹⁵ m**

Yaani **1 light year ≈ 9.46 × 10¹⁵ m** (lagbhag 9.46 × 10¹² km).

Jaanch: units: (m/s) × s = m, yaani **doori** ✅. Isliye light year samay ki nahi, doori ki unit hai.

### Example 6: Shadow ki size (point lamp)

10 cm ka puppet ek point lamp se 20 cm door hai, aur chadar lamp se 60 cm door hai. Shadow kitni badi hogi?

Shadow ÷ puppet = (lamp se chadar) ÷ (lamp se puppet) = 60 cm ÷ 20 cm = 3

Shadow = 10 cm × 3 = **30 cm**

Ab puppet ko lamp se 40 cm par kar do (chadar wahi, lamp se 60 cm par):

Shadow = 10 cm × (60 cm ÷ 40 cm) = 10 cm × 1.5 = **15 cm**

Jaanch: puppet lamp se door gaya (20 cm se 40 cm), to shadow chhoti hui (30 cm se 15 cm) ✅

### Example 7: Pinhole camera se ped ki image

3 m lamba ped chhed se 6 m door hai. Screen chhed ke peeche 12 cm door hai. Image ki height kitni hogi?

Pehle cm ko m mein badlo: 12 cm = 12 ÷ 100 = 0.12 m.

hᵢ = hₒ × v ÷ u = 3 m × 0.12 m ÷ 6 m = 0.36 m² ÷ 6 m = 0.06 m = 0.06 × 100 cm = **6 cm**

Image ulti hogi.

Jaanch: hᵢ ÷ hₒ = 0.06 m ÷ 3 m = 0.02, aur v ÷ u = 0.12 m ÷ 6 m = 0.02 ✅

### Example 8: Magnification, aur screen door karne par

Example 7 mein magnification m = hᵢ ÷ hₒ = 6 cm ÷ 300 cm = **0.02** (koi unit nahi, kyunki cm ÷ cm).

Ab screen ko 24 cm door kar do (v = 24 cm = 0.24 m):

hᵢ = 3 m × 0.24 m ÷ 6 m = 0.72 m² ÷ 6 m = 0.12 m = **12 cm**, aur m = 12 cm ÷ 300 cm = **0.04**.

Jaanch: v double (12 cm se 24 cm) to image bhi double (6 cm se 12 cm) ✅

### Example 9: Object kitni door hai?

Ek 1.5 m lambe insaan ki image pinhole camera mein 5 cm lambi bani, jab screen chhed se 10 cm door thi. Insaan chhed se kitni door hai?

hᵢ = 5 cm = 0.05 m, hₒ = 1.5 m, v = 10 cm = 0.10 m.

hᵢ ÷ hₒ = v ÷ u, to u = v × hₒ ÷ hᵢ = 0.10 m × 1.5 m ÷ 0.05 m = 0.15 m² ÷ 0.05 m = **3 m**

Jaanch: v ÷ u = 0.10 m ÷ 3 m = 0.0333 aur hᵢ ÷ hₒ = 0.05 m ÷ 1.5 m = 0.0333 ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Chaand ko luminous samajhna.** ❌ Chaand khud light nahi banata. Wo Sun ki light **wapas uchhaalta** hai. Isiliye Chaand non-luminous hai (Example 10).
2. **Ye sochna ki light ko bhi sound ki tarah medium chahiye.** ❌ Light vacuum mein chalti hai: Sun ki light khaali space se Earth tak aati hai. Sound vacuum mein nahi chalti.
3. **Shadow ko object ki reflection samajhna.** ❌ Shadow wo jagah hai jahan light **pahunchi hi nahi** (opaque cheez ne rok li). Reflection tab banta hai jab light uchhal kar wapas aati hai. Dono alag hain.
4. **Ye sochna ki bada pinhole zyada saaf image deta hai.** ❌ Bada chhed zyada roshan par **dhundhli** image deta hai. **Chhota** chhed **saaf** par dim image deta hai.
5. **Light year ko samay ki unit samajhna.** ❌ Light year **doori** hai: 1 ly ≈ 9.46 × 10¹⁵ m (Example 5). Samay ki unit second, ghanta, saal hain.
6. **Suraj grahan ko seedha ya ordinary chashme se dekhna.** ❌ Kabhi nahi! Aankh ko hamesha ke liye nuksaan ho sakta hai. Pinhole camera ya certified solar filter hi surakshit hain.
7. **cm aur m ko mix karna.** Example 7 mein 12 cm ko pehle 0.12 m kiya, tab 3 m × 0.12 m ÷ 6 m nikala. Ek hi calculation mein sab lambaiyan ek hi unit mein likho.

## Quick Recap

- **Light** energy ka wo roop hai jisse hum dekhte hain. Use **medium ki zaroorat nahi**: wo vacuum mein chalti hai (sound ki tarah nahi).
- **Luminous** cheez khud light banati hai (Sun, tare, bulb, candle, jugnu, on phone screen). **Non-luminous** cheez ko hum tabhi dekhte hain jab wo us par padi light ko wapas uchhaalti hai (Chaand, kitaab, mirror).
- **Transparent** (glass), **translucent** (butter paper) aur **opaque** (lakdi): light ko paar jaane dene ke hisaab se.
- Vacuum mein light ki speed **c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s**, hawa mein lagbhag wahi. Samay = doori ÷ speed. Sun se Earth tak light ko **500 s = 8 min 20 s** lagte hain, Chaand se **1.28 s**.
- **Light year** = ek saal mein light ki tay ki gayi **doori** ≈ 9.46 × 10¹⁵ m (samay nahi).
- Light uniform transparent medium mein **seedhi line** mein chalti hai (rectilinear propagation). **Ray** = seedhi line + teer. **Beam** = rays ka gucchha (parallel, convergent, divergent).
- **Shadow** opaque cheez ke peeche banti hai. Point source se **saaf** shadow. Extended source se **umbra** (poora andhera) aur **penumbra** (halka, dhundhla kinaara).
- Shadow = puppet ki height × (lamp se chadar) ÷ (lamp se puppet). Puppet lamp ke paas to shadow badi aur dhundhli, chadar ke paas to chhoti aur saaf.
- **Suraj grahan:** Chaand Sun aur Earth ke beech mein. **Chandra grahan:** Earth Sun aur Chaand ke beech mein. Suraj grahan ko kabhi seedha mat dekho.
- **Pinhole camera:** ulti aur real image, rays chhed par cross karti hain. Chhota chhed = saaf par dim, bada chhed = roshan par dhundhli. **hᵢ ÷ hₒ = v ÷ u = magnification.**
