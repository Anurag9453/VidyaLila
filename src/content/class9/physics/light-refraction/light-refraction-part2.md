# Topic 2: Refractive Index

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki jab light ek medium se doosre medium mein jaati hai to uski ray **mud jaati hai**, kyunki dono medium mein uski speed alag hoti hai. Humne ye bhi dekha ki i aur r ke **sine ka ratio** (sin i ÷ sin r) ek **constant number** hota hai: angle badlo, ratio wahi rehta hai. Par us topic ke ant mein ek sawal adhoora chhoda tha: **ye constant number asal mein kya hai, aur kisi medium ke liye uski value kitni hoti hai?** Is topic mein hum is number ka naam, matlab aur kaam seekhenge. Is number ka naam hai **refractive index**.

---

## Glass factory ke do ek jaise block

Ek glass factory ke quality-check hall mein Mr. Naidu, quality inspector, ek table par kaam kar rahe the. Paas mein Anvesha khadi thi: wo school trip ke saath factory dekhne aayi thi aur sabse zyada sawal wahi poochh rahi thi.

Table par do saaf, chamakte hue glass ke block rakhe the. Ek bhi dust ka daag nahi. Dono ek hi size ke, dono ek jaise paardarshi (transparent).

Mr. Naidu ne ek block uthaya, phir doosra, aur Anvesha ko pakda diya. "Batao, in dono mein koi farak dikhta hai?"

Anvesha ne dono ko ghooma-ghooma kar dekha. "Nahi sir, bilkul ek jaise hain."

Mr. Naidu muskuraye. "Sach ye hai ki inme se ek **ordinary glass** hai aur doosra **flint glass**, jo zyada **dense** (optically ghana) hota hai. Aur main inhe **todhe bina** pehchaan sakta hoon. Bas ek **number** chahiye."

Anvesha ne hairaan hokar poochha, "Number? Kaun sa number, sir?"

"Wahi number jo bata deta hai ki light kisi cheez ke andar kitni **mudti** hai aur kitni **dheemi** ho jaati hai," Mr. Naidu ne kaha. Is kahani ko hum topic ke ant mein solve karenge.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Refractive index (n)** | Ye batata hai ki kisi medium mein light vacuum ke muqable kitni dheemi chalti hai: n = c ÷ v | Paani ka n = 1.33, glass ka n = 1.5 |
| **Absolute refractive index** | Vacuum (ya lagbhag hawa) ke hisaab se refractive index; yehi "n" kehlata hai | n(glass) = 1.5 |
| **Relative refractive index (n₂₁)** | Medium 2 ka refractive index medium 1 ke **hisaab se**: n₂₁ = n₂ ÷ n₁ | Paani se glass mein jaate waqt: 1.5 ÷ 1.33 = 1.128 |

| **v** | Kisi medium mein light ki speed | Glass mein v = 2 × 10⁸ m/s |
| **Optically denser medium** | Jiska n zyada ho; usme light zyada dheemi chalti hai | Glass paani se optically denser hai |
| **Optically rarer medium** | Jiska n kam ho; usme light tez chalti hai | Hawa glass se optically rarer hai |
| **Snell's law** | n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r (Class 10 ke saath overlap) | Light paani se glass mein jaati hai |

## Part A: Refractive index kya hai

### Kya hai ye number

Pichhle topic mein dekha ki glass ya paani ke andar jaate hi light ki speed kam ho jaati hai. Hum is "kitni kam" ko ek number mein likhna chahte hain. Sabse saaf tareeka hai vacuum mein speed ko medium ki speed se bhaag dena:

> **Refractive index (absolute)**
> **n = c ÷ v**
> jahan **c** = vacuum mein light ki speed = 3 × 10⁸ m/s, aur **v** = us medium mein light ki speed.

Ise samajhna aasaan hai. Maan lo kisi medium mein light ki speed vacuum ki **aadhi** hai. To n = c ÷ (c ÷ 2) = **2**. Yaani **n batata hai ki light vacuum ke muqable kitni guna dheemi ho gayi**.

Kuch zaroori baatein:

- **n ka koi unit nahi hota.** Dono speeds m/s mein hain, to m/s ÷ m/s mein unit kat jaata hai. n sirf ek **shuddh number** hai.
- **Vacuum ke liye n = 1** (kyunki v = c). **Baaki har medium mein light vacuum se dheemi hai**, isliye v < c aur **n hamesha 1 se zyada** hota hai.
- **n jitna bada, light utni dheemi, aur medium utna optically denser.**
- Hawa ke liye n = 1.0003, jo itna 1 ke paas hai ki hum use **n = 1.00** le lete hain.

### Alag-alag medium ke refractive index

| Medium | Refractive index n (approximate) |
|---|---|
| Vacuum | 1 (exactly) |
| Hawa | 1.0003 (hum 1.00 lenge) |
| Ice | 1.31 |
| Paani | 1.33 |
| Ethyl alcohol (spirit) | 1.36 |
| Kerosene | 1.44 |
| Fused quartz | 1.46 |
| Turpentine oil | 1.47 |
| Crown glass | 1.52 (kai sawalon mein 1.5 lete hain) |
| Rock salt | 1.54 |
| Flint glass | 1.65 |
| Diamond | 2.42 (kai sawalon mein 2.4 lete hain) |

(Ye values **lagbhag** hain: asli value glass ya liquid ke type par thodi badal sakti hai.)

Is table se ek baat saaf dikhti hai: **diamond ka n sabse zyada hai (lagbhag 2.4)**. Matlab diamond ke andar light bahut dheemi ho jaati hai, aur wo bahut zyada mudti hai.

### Light kitni dheemi chalti hai: v = c ÷ n

Formula ko ulta kar do to speed nikaalna aasaan ho jaata hai:

> **v = c ÷ n**

Paani, glass aur diamond mein light ki speed (c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s):

| Medium | n | v = c ÷ n |
|---|---|---|
| Paani | 1.33 | 3 × 10⁸ m/s ÷ 1.33 = **2.26 × 10⁸ m/s** |
| Glass | 1.5 | 3 × 10⁸ m/s ÷ 1.5 = **2 × 10⁸ m/s** |
| Diamond | 2.4 | 3 × 10⁸ m/s ÷ 2.4 = **1.25 × 10⁸ m/s** |

Jaanch: paani mein 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s × 1.33 = 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s ✅, glass mein 2 × 10⁸ m/s × 1.5 = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ✅, diamond mein 1.25 × 10⁸ m/s × 2.4 = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ✅

Yaani diamond mein light ki speed vacuum ki speed se **aadhe se bhi kam** (1.25 ÷ 3 = 0.42 guna) reh jaati hai.

### Refractive index aur angles

Pichhle topic mein dekha tha ki sin i ÷ sin r constant hota hai. Ab uska naam bhi jaan lo. Jab light **hawa (ya vacuum) se kisi medium mein** jaati hai, to

> **n = sin i ÷ sin r**

yaani wo constant number us medium ka **refractive index** hi hai. (Yahan i hawa mein incident angle hai aur r medium mein refracted angle, dono normal se naape jaate hain.)

Ek chhota sa nateeja: **n jitna bada, r utna chhota** (kyunki sin r = sin i ÷ n), yaani ray normal ki taraf utni zyada mudegi.

## Part B: Light kis medium mein kitni mudti hai

Light ko hawa se paani, glass aur diamond mein **ek hi angle i = 45°** par bhejo. Dekho, r kitna aata hai (sin r = sin 45° ÷ n, sin 45° = 0.7071):

|---|---|---|---|
| Paani | 1.33 | 0.7071 ÷ 1.33 = 0.532 | **32.1°** |
| Glass | 1.5 | 0.7071 ÷ 1.5 = 0.471 | **28.1°** |
| Diamond | 2.4 | 0.7071 ÷ 2.4 = 0.295 | **17.1°** |

### Ek aur dhyaan dene wali baat

n sirf **medium** par depend karta hai, **angle i par nahi.** i = 30° ho ya 60°, hawa se glass mein jaate waqt sin i ÷ sin r hamesha 1.5 hi aayega. Ye pichhle topic ke "constant" wali baat ka naam hai.

## Part C: Relative refractive index

Ab tak humne hamesha hawa (vacuum) se kisi medium mein light jaate dekha. Par light **paani se glass** mein bhi jaa sakti hai, jaise paani se bhare glass ke bartan mein. Tab bhi ye number banta hai, bas ab hum "kis ke hisaab se" wala hissa saaf likhte hain.

Maan lo light **medium 1** se **medium 2** mein jaati hai. Medium 1 mein n = n₁ aur speed v₁ hai; medium 2 mein n = n₂ aur speed v₂.

> **Relative refractive index** (medium 2 ka, medium 1 ke hisaab se)
> **n₂₁ = n₂ ÷ n₁ = v₁ ÷ v₂ = sin i ÷ sin r**
> (Kai jagah ise **₁n₂** bhi likhte hain: matlab wahi, "medium 1 se medium 2 mein jaate waqt".)

Samajhne ka tareeka: **"kitni dheemi hui"**. Medium 1 mein speed v₁ thi, medium 2 mein v₂ ho gayi. To v₁ ÷ v₂ batata hai ki speed kitni guna kam hui. Aur n = c ÷ v se ye wahi n₂ ÷ n₁ hai (c dono jagah kat jaata hai): n₂ ÷ n₁ = (c ÷ v₂) ÷ (c ÷ v₁) = v₁ ÷ v₂.

**Absolute n ek special relative n hai:** jab medium 1 hawa/vacuum hai (n₁ = 1), to n₂₁ = n₂ ÷ 1 = n₂. Isliye hum kehte hain ki absolute refractive index = vacuum ke hisaab se relative index.

### Paani aur glass ka example

Paani n = 1.33, glass n = 1.5. Light **paani se glass** mein jaati hai:

- n(glass wrt water) = n₂ ÷ n₁ = 1.5 ÷ 1.33 = **1.128**
- Speeds se bhi: v(water) = 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s, v(glass) = 2 × 10⁸ m/s, to v₁ ÷ v₂ = 2.26 × 10⁸ ÷ 2 × 10⁸ = **1.13** (lagbhag 1.128; ye dono ek hi cheez hain)

Aur jab light **ulta**, glass se paani mein jaati hai:

- n(water wrt glass) = n(water) ÷ n(glass) = 1.33 ÷ 1.5 = **0.887**

> **Ulta jaane par reciprocal:** **n₁₂ = 1 ÷ n₂₁**
> Jaanch: 1 ÷ 1.128 = 0.887 ✅

Dhyaan do: **relative refractive index 1 se kam bhi ho sakta hai** (0.887 jaisa). Wo tab hota hai jab light **dense se rarer** medium mein jaati hai (glass se paani), kyunki tab speed **badhti** hai. Par **absolute n kabhi 1 se kam nahi hota** (vacuum se tez kuch nahi).

## Part D: Snell's law n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r (thoda extra: Class 10 overlap)

*(Thoda extra: ye hissa Class 10 ke saath overlap karta hai, par dekhne mein simple hai.)*

Relative index ka formula ek sutra mein badla ja sakta hai. n₂₁ = sin i ÷ sin r aur n₂₁ = n₂ ÷ n₁ ko barabar rakho:

sin i ÷ sin r = n₂ ÷ n₁

Cross-multiply karo:

> **n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r**

Isse **Snell's law** kehte hain. Isme i hamesha **medium 1 mein** angle hai, aur r **medium 2 mein**. Dono angle normal se naape jaate hain.

Isse humein ek bahut kaam ki baat milti hai: **jis side ka n bada hai, us side ka sin (aur angle) chhota hoga**: n × sin ka product dono taraf barabar hai.

**Ek jhalak:** hawa se paani mein jaana, n₁ = 1, n₂ = 1.33, i = 40°. Tab sin r = 1 × sin 40° ÷ 1.33 = 0.6428 ÷ 1.33 = 0.4833, jisse **r = 28.9°**. Ray normal ki taraf mud gayi (28.9° < 40°) ✅. Ye dikhata hai ki Snell ka formula wahi hai jo humne pehle dekha, bas ab dono medium kisi bhi tarah ke ho sakte hain.

## Part E: n aur kuch aur cheezein

### 1. n light ke rang (wavelength) par bhi depend karta hai

Ek aur dilchasp baat: **alag-alag rang ki light ke liye ek hi medium ka n thoda alag hota hai.** Jaise glass ka n **neeli** light ke liye **laal** light se thoda zyada hota hai. Isi wajah se safed light prism se guzarti hai to sat rangon mein bikhar jaati hai, jise **dispersion** kehte hain. Wo bahut dilchasp hai: iske baare mein Topic 7 mein padhenge. Abhi table ke n ko "peele-hare rang ki light ke aas-paas" ki value samajh lo.

### 2. n temperature par bhi depend karta hai

**Temperature badalne par n thoda badal jaata hai**: garam hone par liquid ya glass ka n kuch kam ho jaata hai (kyunki medium thoda halka ho jaata hai). Ye farak itna chhota hota hai ki hum sawal mein ise nazar-andaaz karte hain, par lab mein bahut sahi naapna ho to ye dhyaan mein rakhna padta hai.

### 3. Optical density vs mass density

Pichhle topic mein dekha tha ki **optical density** (light ke liye) aur **mass density** (kg/m³ mein, wajan ke liye) do alag cheezein hain. Ek example se saaf karte hain:

| Liquid | Refractive index n | Mass density | Light ki speed v = c ÷ n |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paani | 1.33 | 1000 kg/m³ | 3 × 10⁸ m/s ÷ 1.33 = 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s |
| Kerosene | 1.44 | 800 kg/m³ | 3 × 10⁸ m/s ÷ 1.44 = 2.08 × 10⁸ m/s |

Kerosene ka n zyada hai (1.44 > 1.33), to **kerosene optically denser hai**, aur uske andar light dheemi chalti hai (2.08 × 10⁸ < 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s). Par uska mass density kam hai (800 < 1000 kg/m³), to kerosene paani se **halka** hai (isi liye wo paani par tairta hai). Matlab: **optically denser ka matlab mass mein bhaari hona zaroori nahi.**

### 4. Heere ki chamak (sirf ek jhalak)

Diamond ka n bahut zyada hai (2.42). Isi wajah se uske andar light bahut mudti hai, aur diamond ki kati hui sateh par light baar-baar andar hi laut jaati hai (ye **total internal reflection** hai, jise Topic 4 mein dekhenge). Isi wajah se diamond itna sparkle karta hai.

## Part F: n ko naapte kaise hain (ek simple experiment)

Glass slab ka n lab mein **pins aur protractor** se aasaani se naap sakte hain.

**Zaroorat:** drawing board, safed sheet, rectangular glass slab, 4 pins, protractor, pencil, scale.

**Steps:**

1. Sheet par glass slab rakhkar uski outline kheencho. Slab hata do.
2. Outline ki ek surface par ek point O lo aur wahan **normal NN'** kheencho.
3. Normal se i = 30° ka angle banati ek line AO kheencho. Is line par do pins **P** aur **Q** lagao.
4. Slab ko wapas outline par rakho. Slab ki doosri taraf se P aur Q ki images ko dekho aur do pins **R** aur **S** is tarah lagao ki ye dono pins P, Q ki images ke saath ek seedhi line mein dikhein.
5. Slab hata kar R aur S se line kheencho, jo doosri surface par O' ko chhuti hai. O ko O' se jodo: ye slab ke andar ki **refracted ray OO'** hai.
6. O par normal se OO' ka angle naapo: ye **r** hai.
7. **n = sin i ÷ sin r** nikaalo. i = 45°, 60° ke liye dobara karo aur average lo.

**Sample readings** (glass ka asli n lagbhag 1.5 hai):

| Observation | i | r | sin i | sin r | n = sin i ÷ sin r |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 30° | 19.5° | 0.500 | 0.334 | 1.50 |
| 2 | 45° | 28° | 0.707 | 0.469 | 1.51 |
| 3 | 60° | 35° | 0.866 | 0.574 | 1.51 |

Average n = (1.50 + 1.51 + 1.51) ÷ 3 = **1.51**, yaani lagbhag 1.5 ✅. Dhyaan do: i alag-alag hai, par n lagbhag **wahi aaya**: ye dikhata hai ki n angle par depend nahi karta.

```
                    N
       P            :
         \          :
           Q        :
             \   i  :
               \    :
  =================\O===========================  slab ki pehli surface
                    :\
          GLASS     : \  r
                    :  \
                    :   \
  =======================O'=====================  slab ki doosri surface
                           \
                             R
                               \
                                 S
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Pins ka experiment (upar se dekha hua, sheet par). P aur Q se aati ray glass ke point O par girti hai (angle i), slab ke andar r angle par mudkar O' tak jaati hai, aur wahan se R aur S ki seedh mein bahar nikalti hai. (Sketch bas idea ke liye hai.)

**Liquid ka n** naapne ke liye bhi ek tareeka hota hai: **real depth aur apparent depth** se. Usme n = real depth ÷ apparent depth hota hai. Wo bahut dilchasp hai: iske baare mein Topic 3 mein dekhenge.

## Part G: Solved examples

(Har example mein c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s aur hawa ke liye n = 1.00 liya hai.)

### Example 1: v se n nikaalna

Kisi glass mein light ki speed v = 2 × 10⁸ m/s hai. Uska refractive index nikaalo.

- n = c ÷ v = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ÷ 2 × 10⁸ m/s = **1.5**

Jaanch: v = c ÷ n = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ÷ 1.5 = 2 × 10⁸ m/s ✅

### Example 2: n se v nikaalna

Paani ka n = 1.33. Uske andar light ki speed batao.

- v = c ÷ n = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ÷ 1.33 = **2.26 × 10⁸ m/s**

Jaanch: n = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ÷ 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s = 1.33 ✅

### Example 3: angles se n nikaalna

Hawa se kisi medium mein light i = 60° par girti hai aur r = 35.3° par mud jaati hai. Medium ka n kya hai?

- sin 60° = 0.866, sin 35.3° = 0.578
- n = sin i ÷ sin r = 0.866 ÷ 0.578 = **1.50**

Jaanch: sin r = sin i ÷ n = 0.866 ÷ 1.5 = 0.577, jo sin 35.3° ke barabar hai ✅. Is medium mein v = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ÷ 1.5 = 2 × 10⁸ m/s: ye glass jaisa hai.

### Example 4: n aur i se r nikaalna

Hawa se kerosene (n = 1.44) mein light i = 30° par girti hai. r nikaalo.

- sin r = sin i ÷ n = sin 30° ÷ 1.44 = 0.5 ÷ 1.44 = 0.3472
- r = **20.3°**

Jaanch: r < i (20.3° < 30°): ray normal ki taraf mudi hai, jo hona chahiye (rarer se denser) ✅. Aur sin i ÷ sin r = 0.5 ÷ 0.3472 = 1.44 ✅

### Example 5: glass ka paani ke hisaab se relative index

Glass n = 1.5, paani n = 1.33. Glass ka paani ke hisaab se relative index nikaalo, aur paani ka glass ke hisaab se bhi.

- Glass wrt paani: n(g wrt w) = n(glass) ÷ n(water) = 1.5 ÷ 1.33 = **1.128**
- Paani wrt glass: n(w wrt g) = n(water) ÷ n(glass) = 1.33 ÷ 1.5 = **0.887**

Jaanch: 1.128 × 0.887 = 1.000 ✅. Dono ek-doosre ke reciprocal hain.

### Example 6: alcohol mein light ki speed

Ethyl alcohol (spirit) ka n = 1.36. Light ki speed nikaalo.

- v = c ÷ n = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ÷ 1.36 = **2.21 × 10⁸ m/s**

Jaanch: 2.21 × 10⁸ m/s × 1.36 = 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s ✅. Alcohol mein light paani (2.26 × 10⁸ m/s) se thodi dheemi hai, kyunki alcohol ka n zyada hai ✅

### Example 7: do medium mein speeds ka ratio

Paani (n = 1.33) aur glass (n = 1.5) mein light ki speeds ka ratio v(water) ÷ v(glass) nikaalo.

- v(water) = c ÷ 1.33 aur v(glass) = c ÷ 1.5
- v(water) ÷ v(glass) = (c ÷ 1.33) ÷ (c ÷ 1.5) = 1.5 ÷ 1.33 = **1.128**

Jaanch: 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s ÷ 2 × 10⁸ m/s = 1.13 ✅. Dhyaan do ki c kat gaya: **ratio sirf n se nikal aata hai**, c yaad rakhna zaroori nahi.

### Example 8: dheemi light wala medium

Kisi medium mein light 1.5 × 10⁸ m/s se chalti hai. Uska n kya hai?

- n = c ÷ v = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ÷ 1.5 × 10⁸ m/s = **2.0**

Jaanch: v = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ÷ 2.0 = 1.5 × 10⁸ m/s ✅. Ye medium diamond jaisa dense hai (diamond n = 2.4 ke aas-paas).

### Example 9: glass block ko paar karne mein kitna time

Light ek 3 cm mote glass block (n = 1.5) ko seedhi paar karti hai. Kitna time lagega? Aur utni hi doori hawa mein kitna?

- Thickness d = 3 cm = 0.03 m.
- Glass mein speed v = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ÷ 1.5 = 2 × 10⁸ m/s.
- Time t = d ÷ v = 0.03 m ÷ 2 × 10⁸ m/s = **1.5 × 10⁻¹⁰ s**.
- Hawa mein: t = 0.03 m ÷ 3 × 10⁸ m/s = **1 × 10⁻¹⁰ s**.

Jaanch: glass mein time hawa ke time ka 1.5 ÷ 1 = 1.5 guna hai, yaani n ke barabar ✅. Glass mein light ko 0.5 × 10⁻¹⁰ s zyada lagta hai.

### Example 10: Snell's law, paani se glass (Class 10 overlap)

Light paani (n₁ = 1.33) se glass (n₂ = 1.5) mein i = 30° par jaati hai. r nikaalo.

- n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r
- sin r = n₁ sin i ÷ n₂ = 1.33 × 0.5 ÷ 1.5 = 0.665 ÷ 1.5 = **0.4433**
- r = **26.3°**

Jaanch: r < i (26.3° < 30°), jo sahi hai kyunki glass paani se denser hai ✅. Ulta check: glass se paani mein i = 26.3° par jao, to sin r = 1.5 × 0.4433 ÷ 1.33 = 0.5, yaani r = 30° ✅ (path reversible hai).

### Example 11: 6 medium ka order (optical density ke hisaab se)

Neeche 6 medium mein light ki speed di hai. Unhe **sabse denser se sabse rarer** order mein likho aur har ka n nikaalo.

| Medium | Speed v |
|---|---|
| Paani | 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s |
| Diamond | 1.24 × 10⁸ m/s |
| Hawa | 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s |
| Crown glass | 1.97 × 10⁸ m/s |
| Flint glass | 1.82 × 10⁸ m/s |
| Ice | 2.29 × 10⁸ m/s |

Jitni kam speed, utna zyada n, utna denser. n = c ÷ v:

| Medium | n = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ v |
|---|---|
| Diamond | 3 ÷ 1.24 = **2.42** |
| Flint glass | 3 ÷ 1.82 = **1.65** |
| Crown glass | 3 ÷ 1.97 = **1.52** |
| Paani | 3 ÷ 2.26 = **1.33** |
| Ice | 3 ÷ 2.29 = **1.31** |
| Hawa | 3 ÷ 3.00 = **1.00** |

**Order (denser se rarer):** Diamond > Flint glass > Crown glass > Paani > Ice > Hawa.

Jaanch: speeds ulte order mein badhti hain: 1.24 < 1.82 < 1.97 < 2.26 < 2.29 < 3.00 (×10⁸ m/s) ✅

### Example 12: Mr. Naidu ke block (kahani ka hal)

Ab Anvesha ke sawal par aate hain: "kaun sa number?" Mr. Naidu ne kaha: "**Refractive index.** Ordinary (crown) glass ka n lagbhag 1.52 hota hai, aur flint glass ka lagbhag 1.65. Ye number kisi bhi block ka alag hota hai, chahe wo dikhne mein ek jaise hon."

Ye number naapne ke liye wo ek chhoti light ki beam ko block par i = 45° par girate hain (jaise Part F mein) aur r naapte hain:

- Ordinary glass (n = 1.52): sin r = 0.7071 ÷ 1.52 = 0.465, **r = 27.7°**
- Flint glass (n = 1.65): sin r = 0.7071 ÷ 1.65 = 0.429, **r = 25.4°**

Ek block mein r = 27.7° aaya, doosre mein r = 25.4°. Jis mein **r chhota** aaya (25.4°), wahi **flint glass** hai: kyunki uska n zyada hai, light zyada mudi.

Speeds bhi dekho:

- Ordinary glass: v = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ÷ 1.52 = **1.97 × 10⁸ m/s**
- Flint glass: v = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ÷ 1.65 = **1.82 × 10⁸ m/s**

Jaanch: 1.97 × 10⁸ × 1.52 = 2.99 × 10⁸ ≈ 3 × 10⁸ ✅, 1.82 × 10⁸ × 1.65 = 3.0 × 10⁸ ✅. Flint glass mein light dheemi hai, aur n zyada hai, jo hona chahiye ✅

Anvesha ne block ko tirchhe kone se light mein dekha aur khushi se bol padi, "Matlab bina todhe, bas ek beam ki ek jhalak se pata chal gaya: **jis mein light zyada mudi, wohi flint hai!**"

"Bilkul," Mr. Naidu hanse. "Aur isi wajah se flint glass se bani cheezein zyada chamakti hain."

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**1. n = v ÷ c likh dena (ulta)**
n = c ÷ v hota hai: vacuum ki speed **upar**. ❌ n = v ÷ c = 2 × 10⁸ ÷ 3 × 10⁸ = 0.67 ✅ n = c ÷ v = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 2 × 10⁸ = 1.5. Pehchaan: absolute n kabhi 1 se kam nahi aa sakta; agar 1 se kam aaya, to formula ulta ho gaya.

**2. Ye maanna ki n, 1 se kam bhi ho sakta hai**
Kisi bhi medium ke liye **absolute** n ≥ 1 hota hai. Light vacuum se tez kisi medium mein nahi chalti. (Relative index kam ho sakta hai, jaise 0.887.)

**3. Ye maanna ki bade n wale medium mein light tez chalti hai**
Ulta hai. n = c ÷ v, to n bada matlab v chhota. **Diamond (n = 2.4) mein light 1.25 × 10⁸ m/s se chalti hai, paani (n = 1.33) mein 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s se.**

**4. Absolute aur relative index mix kar dena**
Paani se glass mein jaati light ke liye 1.5 ya 1.33 nahi, **1.5 ÷ 1.33 = 1.128** use hota hai (relative index). Absolute index tab hota hai jab ek side hawa/vacuum ho.

**5. Ye samajhna ki n angle of incidence par depend karta hai**
Part F ke readings dekho: i = 30°, 45°, 60°, par n lagbhag 1.5 hi aaya. n medium ka (aur rang tatha temperature ka) gun hai, angle ka nahi.

**6. Optical density aur mass density ko ek samajhna**
Kerosene ka n paani se zyada hai (optically denser), par uska mass density kam hai (800 < 1000 kg/m³). Dono alag cheezein hain.

## Quick Recap

- **Refractive index n = c ÷ v**: vacuum mein light ki speed ÷ medium mein light ki speed. **Koi unit nahi.**
- Vacuum ka n = 1; baaki har medium ka **absolute n > 1**. Hawa ka n = 1.0003, jise hum 1.00 lete hain.
- **n bada = light dheemi = medium optically denser.** v = c ÷ n.
- Hawa se medium mein jaate waqt **n = sin i ÷ sin r**. n bada ho to r chhota, yaani ray normal ki taraf zyada mudti hai.
- Speeds: paani 2.26 × 10⁸, glass (n = 1.5) 2 × 10⁸, diamond (n = 2.4) 1.25 × 10⁸ m/s.
- **Relative index**: n₂₁ = n₂ ÷ n₁ = v₁ ÷ v₂ = sin i ÷ sin r (medium 1 se medium 2 mein). **n₁₂ = 1 ÷ n₂₁**. Glass wrt paani = 1.128, paani wrt glass = 0.887.
- **Snell's law** *(thoda extra, Class 10 overlap)*: **n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r**.
- n **rang (wavelength)** par thoda depend karta hai (Topic 7: dispersion) aur **temperature** par bhi; **angle par nahi**.
- **Optical density aur mass density alag hain**: kerosene (n = 1.44, 800 kg/m³) optically denser par paani (n = 1.33, 1000 kg/m³) se halka.
- Glass slab ka n pins aur protractor se naap sakte hain (n = sin i ÷ sin r). Liquid ka n real/apparent depth se (Topic 3).
- Diamond ka bada n uski chamak ka ek kaaran hai (Topic 4: total internal reflection).

## Practice Questions

1. The speed of light in a medium is 2.5 × 10⁸ m/s. Find its refractive index. (c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s)
2. The refractive index of turpentine oil is 1.47. Find the speed of light in it.
3. A ray of light goes from air into flint glass (n = 1.65) with an angle of incidence of 50°. Find the angle of refraction. (sin 50° = 0.766)
4. Light goes from air to a medium. When i = 60°, r = 30°. Find the refractive index of the medium and the speed of light in it.
5. Find the refractive index of flint glass (n = 1.65) with respect to crown glass (n = 1.52). Also find the refractive index of crown glass with respect to flint glass.
6. Find the time taken by light to travel 1 m in water (n = 1.33) and the time taken to travel 1 m in air. By how much is the time in water more?
7. State true or false and give a one-line reason: (a) n = v ÷ c. (b) The absolute refractive index of a medium can be 0.8. (c) Light travels fastest in the medium with the largest n. (d) The refractive index of a medium depends on the angle of incidence.
8. Light travels from water (n = 1.33) into glass (n = 1.5) with i = 45°. Find r using n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r. (Class 10 overlap)
9. In a table, medium A has n = 1.31, B has n = 1.46, C has n = 1.00 and D has n = 2.42. Arrange them in order of increasing optical density and say in which medium light is slowest.
10. Turpentine oil has n = 1.47 and a density of about 870 kg/m³. Water has n = 1.33 and a density of 1000 kg/m³. Which is optically denser and which is lighter? What does this show?
11. Two blocks look identical. A ray falling at 45° on the first block refracts at 28.1° and on the second block at 17.1°. Find the n of each block and say which block is likely diamond. (sin 28.1° = 0.471, sin 17.1° = 0.294)
12. Explain in two or three lines why n has no unit and why the absolute n is never less than 1.

### Answers

1. n = c ÷ v = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ÷ 2.5 × 10⁸ m/s = **1.2**.
   Jaanch: v = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 1.2 = 2.5 × 10⁸ m/s ✅

2. v = c ÷ n = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ÷ 1.47 = **2.04 × 10⁸ m/s**.
   Jaanch: 2.04 × 10⁸ × 1.47 = 3.0 × 10⁸ ✅

3. sin r = sin i ÷ n = 0.766 ÷ 1.65 = 0.464, to r = **27.7°**.
   Jaanch: r < i ho gaya (normal ki taraf mudi) ✅, aur 0.766 ÷ 0.464 = 1.65 ✅

4. sin 60° = 0.866, sin 30° = 0.5. n = 0.866 ÷ 0.5 = **1.73**. v = c ÷ n = 3 × 10⁸ m/s ÷ 1.73 = **1.73 × 10⁸ m/s**.
   Jaanch: 1.73 × 10⁸ × 1.73 = 3.0 × 10⁸ ✅ (yahan n aur v ke maan ek jaise aaye kyunki 3 ÷ 1.73 = 1.73)

5. Flint wrt crown = 1.65 ÷ 1.52 = **1.086** (lagbhag 1.09). Crown wrt flint = 1.52 ÷ 1.65 = **0.921** (lagbhag 0.92).
   Jaanch: 1.086 × 0.921 = 1.000 ✅

6. Paani mein: v = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 1.33 = 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s, t = 1 m ÷ 2.26 × 10⁸ m/s = **4.43 × 10⁻⁹ s**. Hawa mein: t = 1 m ÷ 3 × 10⁸ m/s = **3.33 × 10⁻⁹ s**. Farak = 4.43 × 10⁻⁹ − 3.33 × 10⁻⁹ = **1.10 × 10⁻⁹ s** zyada.
   Jaanch: 4.43 ÷ 3.33 = 1.33 = n ✅

7. (a) **False** ❌. n = c ÷ v hota hai (ulta nahi).
   (b) **False** ❌. Absolute n ≥ 1 hota hai, kyunki koi medium vacuum se tez nahi hota.
   (c) **False** ❌. n jitna bada, light utni **dheemi** chalti hai (v = c ÷ n).
   (d) **False** ❌. n medium (aur rang, temperature) par depend karta hai; angle par nahi.

8. sin 45° = 0.7071. sin r = n₁ sin i ÷ n₂ = 1.33 × 0.7071 ÷ 1.5 = 0.9405 ÷ 1.5 = 0.627, to r = **38.8°**.
   Jaanch: r < i (38.8° < 45°), glass denser hai ✅. Ulta: 1.5 × 0.627 ÷ 1.33 = 0.707, yaani 45° ✅

9. Optical density badhne ke order mein: **C (1.00) < A (1.31) < B (1.46) < D (2.42)**. Light **D mein sabse dheemi** hai (n sabse zyada), v = 3 × 10⁸ ÷ 2.42 = 1.24 × 10⁸ m/s.
   Jaanch: n ke saath v ghatti hai: 3.0 > 2.29 > 2.05 > 1.24 (×10⁸ m/s) ✅

10. Turpentine ka n zyada hai (1.47 > 1.33), to **turpentine optically denser** hai. Uska mass density kam hai (870 < 1000 kg/m³), to wo paani se **halka** hai. Isse dikhta hai ki optical density aur mass density do alag cheezein hain.

11. Pehle block: n = sin 45° ÷ sin 28.1° = 0.707 ÷ 0.471 = **1.50**. Doosre block: n = 0.707 ÷ 0.294 = **2.40**. **Doosra block diamond** hoga (n = 2.4).
    Jaanch: 0.707 ÷ 1.5 = 0.471 ✅ aur 0.707 ÷ 2.4 = 0.295 ✅

12. n = c ÷ v mein dono speeds m/s mein hain, to unit kat jaate hain, isliye n ek shuddh number hai. Light vacuum mein sabse tez chalti hai (v ≤ c), to c ÷ v ≥ 1 hota hai: absolute n kabhi 1 se kam nahi hota.

Ab tumhare paas refractive index ka pakka tool hai: **n = c ÷ v, aur n jitna bada, light utni dheemi aur utni zyada mudti hai**. Jab bhi do medium dikhein, pehle dono ka n dekho: wahin se aadha jawab mil jaayega.

**Aage kya?** Ab hum ek **glass slab** ki baat karenge. Topic 3 mein dekhenge ki ek glass slab ke andar se guzarti hui light ka kya hota hai: **lateral shift**. Aur paani mein rakhi cheez uthi hui kyun dikhti hai, aur uski gehraai kitni dikhti hai (apparent depth).
