# Topic 3: Glass Slab, Lateral Shift aur Apparent Depth

Pichhle topic mein humne light ki raftaar se refractive index nikaala: **n = c/v**. Humne ye bhi dekha ki do medium ke beech i aur r ka rishta **n = sin i / sin r** hai, yaani Snell ka niyam. Ab tak hum ek hi surface par refraction dekh rahe the: hawa se paani, ya hawa se glass. Par asli zindagi mein light aksar **dono taraf** se guzarti hai: pehle ek surface se andar jaati hai, phir doosre surface se bahar nikalti hai. Khidki ka sheesha, glass ka gilaas, aquarium, kaanch ki plate: sab aisi hi cheezein hain. Is topic mein hum dekhenge ki glass ke ek **block** (slab) se guzarne ke baad light ke saath kya hota hai, aur paani ya glass ke andar rakhi cheezein apni asli jagah se **upar uthi hui** kyun dikhti hain.

---

## Glass ki dukaan mein ek sideways line

Ek glass ki dukaan mein Zainab apne papa ke saath aayi thi. Dukaan ke malik Mr. Shenoy kaanch ke tukde aur plates saaf kar rahe the. Counter par ek **moti, saaf, aayat jaisi** glass ki slab rakhi thi, aur uske neeche ek safed kaagaz par pencil se ek seedhi lambi line kheenchi hui thi.

Zainab ne slab ko dhyaan se dekha. Phir usne apna sir thoda jhukaya aur slab ki **moti kinaar** (side ki taraf se) se kaagaz ki taraf dekha. Wo chaunk gayi.

"Mr. Shenoy, ye dekhiye! Pencil ki line toot gayi hai! Jo hissa slab ke bahar hai wo ek jagah hai, aur slab ke andar se dikhne wala hissa **side mein khisak** gaya hai. Line seedhi nahi dikh rahi!"

Mr. Shenoy muskuraye aur bole, "Line toot nahi, beta. Light ne slab ke andar jaate aur nikalte waqt do baar **dhakka khaya**, aur isi wajah se wo thodi **side mein sarak** gayi. Isko kehte hain lateral shift. Is shift ko hum ek number se naap bhi sakte hain." Is kahani ko hum topic ke ant mein solve karenge.

## Part A: Glass slab se guzarti hui ray

Glass slab ek aisa block hai jiske do aamne-saamne ke surface (face) **ek-doosre ke parallel** hote hain. Is par hawa ek taraf bhi hai aur doosri taraf bhi.

Ek ray ko slab par girne do. Dekhte hain wo kaise chalti hai:

1. **Pehle surface par (hawa se glass):** light ek rarer medium se denser medium mein ja rahi hai (Topic 1 mein dekha tha). Isliye ray **normal ki taraf mud jaati hai**: r < i.
2. **Slab ke andar:** ray ek **seedhi line** mein chalti hai (ek hi medium hai, to koi mudna nahi).
3. **Doosre surface par (glass se hawa):** ab light denser se rarer medium mein ja rahi hai. Isliye ray **normal se door** mud jaati hai.

```
                  N1
                    :
                    :
  Incident ray\     :
               \    :
                \i  :
                 \  :
                  \ :
  Hawa (air)       \:
  ──────────────────A───────────────────────────────────  Face 1
                    :\
  Glass             :\   :
                    : \  :
                    :  \r:
                    :r \ :
                    :   \:.
                    :   \: .
  ───────────────────────B──.───────────────────────────  Face 2
                         :\  .
  Hawa (air)             : \  .
                         :e \  .
                         :   \  .
                         :    \ d
                         :     \
                         :      \
                        N2       \
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Glass slab se ray ka raasta. A par ray hawa se glass mein jaati hai aur normal N1 ki taraf mudti hai (angle of incidence i, angle of refraction r). Slab ke andar ray seedhi chalti hai. B par ray glass se hawa mein jaati hai aur normal N2 se door mudti hai (angle of emergence e). Dotted line incident ray ka seedha extension hai. Emergent ray usi ke **parallel** hai, par thodi side mein khiski hui: dono ke beech ki perpendicular doori **d** lateral shift hai. (Sketch ka angle sirf samjhane ke liye hai.)

### Emergent ray, incident ray ke parallel kyun hoti hai

Dono surface parallel hain, to unke normal N1 aur N2 bhi **ek-doosre ke parallel** hain. Ray A se B tak jaati hai, to wo in dono parallel normals ko katati hai. Do parallel lines ko ek line kaate to **alternate angles barabar** hote hain. Isliye:

> pehle surface par andar wala angle r = doosre surface par andar wala angle r (dono barabar)

Ab doosre surface par dekho: ray glass se hawa mein jaati hai, aur andar ka angle r hai. Topic 2 ka niyam: n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r. Pehle surface par: 1 × sin i = n × sin r. Doosre surface par: n × sin r = 1 × sin e. Dono jagah n × sin r wahi hai, to

> **sin i = sin e, yaani e = i**

Yaani **angle of emergence = angle of incidence**. Isko **path ki reversibility** se bhi samajh sakte ho: doosre surface par ray ka kaam bilkul waisa hai jaise pehle surface par ray ko ulta chalaya ho. Pehle surface par hawa se glass mein i se r hua tha. Ulta chalo to glass se hawa mein r se i hota hai. Isliye e = i.

Jab emergent ray ka angle (normal ke saath) wahi hai jo incident ray ka tha, to dono ray **parallel** hain. Ray ki **direction** wahi hai, bas ray thodi **side mein khisak** gayi hai.

> **Yaad rakhne wali baat:** slab ray ko **modti nahi**. Wo sirf ray ko **side mein khisakati** hai. Direction wahi, raasta thoda alag.

### Lateral shift: kis par kitna nirbhar hai

**Lateral shift (d)** = incident ray (extended) aur emergent ray ke beech ki **perpendicular doori**.

Fig 3.1 mein d ko ray ke saath-saath nahi, balki dono parallel rays ke beech **seedhe 90° par** naapte hain:

```
          \         .
           \       / .
            \     /   .
             d   /     .
              \ /       .
               \         .
                \         .
                 \         .
    Emergent ray          Incident ray ka extension
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Lateral shift d, dono parallel rays ke beech ki **perpendicular** doori hai (slash wali chhoti line dono ke beech 90° par hai). Slab ke surface ke saath-saath ya ray ke saath-saath naapna galat hai.

Lateral shift teen cheezon par nirbhar karta hai:

| Kya badla | Lateral shift ka kya hua | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| Slab ki **moti t badhi** | **Badhta** hai | Ray glass ke andar zyada lambi doori chalti hai, to zyada side mein khisakti hai |
| **Angle of incidence i badha** | **Badhta** hai | Zyada tirchi ray par i aur r ka farak badhta hai |
| **Refractive index n badha** | **Badhta** hai | Zyada n par ray normal ke aur paas mud jaati hai, farak aur bada |

Aur **i = 0°** par (ray normal ke saath-saath girti hai) koi modna nahi hota, ray seedhi guzar jaati hai. Lateral shift **0** hota hai.

## Part B: Activity: pins se lateral shift naapo

Zaroorat: ek rectangular glass slab, ek safed sheet (drawing board par), 4 pins (P1, P2, P3, P4), pencil, scale aur protractor.

**Steps:**

1. Sheet par slab rakho aur uska outline pencil se kheench lo. Surface ke naam AB (pehla) aur CD (doosra) rakho. Slab hata do.
2. AB par ek point **O** lo aur wahan **normal** kheencho. Normal ke saath 30° ka ek angle kheencho. Ye incident ray ki direction hai.
3. Is line par do pins **P1** aur **P2** seedhi khadi karo (kam se kam 5 cm ki doori par).
4. Slab ko outline par wapas rakho. Ab doosri taraf (CD ke baahar) se **glass ke andar se** P1 aur P2 ki images dekho. Aankh ko aisi jagah rakho ki dono pins ki images **ek hi seedh mein** dikhein.
5. Is seedh mein do pins **P3** aur **P4** khadi karo, taki P3 aur P4 aankh ko P1 aur P2 ki images ke saath ek line mein dikhein.
6. Slab aur pins hata do. Pin ke nishaan (holes) ko jodkar line **P3P4** kheencho. Ye line CD ko point **Q** par katti hai. Ye **emergent ray** hai.
7. O ko Q se jodo: ye slab ke andar ka raasta hai.
8. P1P2 line ko seedha aage kheench kar dotted extension banao. Phir **perpendicular doori d** naapo, jo dotted incident line aur emergent line P3P4 ke beech hai. Protractor se **i** aur **e** bhi naapo.
9. i = 45° aur i = 60° ke liye ye sab dobara karo.

## Part C: Lateral shift ka formula (thoda extra: Class 10 overlap)

*(Ye Class 10 ke saath overlap karta hai. Hum isko derive nahi karenge, sirf ek tayyar result ki tarah use karenge.)*

Agar slab ki moti **t**, angle of incidence **i**, aur slab ke andar angle of refraction **r** ho, to lateral shift

> **d = t × sin(i − r) ÷ cos r**

Yahan r ko pehle Snell ke niyam se nikaalna hota hai: **sin r = sin i ÷ n**.

Is formula mein bhi wahi teen baatein dikhti hain:
- **i = 0°** par r bhi 0° hota hai, to sin(i − r) = sin 0° = 0 aur **d = 0**.
- d seedha **t** ke saath badhta hai (t double kare to d double).
- n badhne par r chhota hota hai aur i − r badhta hai, to d badhta hai.

(Formula mein i − r wo angle hai jitna ray pehle surface par apni seedh se mud gayi. Jitna zyada i − r, utna zyada side mein shift.)

Ab kuch numbers dekhte hain (n = 1.5 lete hain).

| i | sin r = sin i ÷ 1.5 | r | i − r |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30° | 0.5 ÷ 1.5 = 0.3333 | 19.47° | 10.53° |
| 45° | 0.7071 ÷ 1.5 = 0.4714 | 28.13° | 16.87° |
| 60° | 0.8660 ÷ 1.5 = 0.5774 | 35.26° | 24.74° |

### Zainab ki line: kahani ka hal

Zainab ki slab ki moti t = 6 cm thi. Maan lo wo kaagaz ko aise dekh rahi thi ki slab ke andar ray ka angle of incidence i = 60° tha, aur glass ke liye n = 1.5.

- sin r = sin 60° ÷ 1.5 = 0.8660 ÷ 1.5 = 0.5774, to **r = 35.26°**.
- i − r = 60° − 35.26° = 24.74°.
- sin 24.74° = 0.4184 aur cos 35.26° = 0.8165.
- d = t × sin(i − r) ÷ cos r = 6 cm × 0.4184 ÷ 0.8165 = **3.07 cm**.

Jaanch: 6 cm × 0.4184 = 2.51 cm, aur 2.51 cm ÷ 0.8165 = 3.07 cm ✅. Shift t = 6 cm se chhota hai, jaisa hona chahiye ✅.

Yaani slab ke andar se dikhne wali line ke hisse ko light ne lagbhag **3 cm side mein** khisaka diya. Isliye Zainab ko wo hissa ek taraf khisaka hua dikha. Mr. Shenoy ka "dhakka khakar sideways" bilkul sahi tha: do surfaces par do dhakke, aur ray ke raste mein 3 cm ka shift. Line toot nahi, bas light ka raasta shift hua. (Slab ke bahar jo hissa bina glass ke dikh raha tha, uski light seedhi aayi, usme koi shift nahi hua.)

## Part D: Apparent depth: paani ke andar ki cheez upar kyun dikhti hai

Topic 1 mein pool ka tala upar uthta dikha tha. Ab uska number nikaalte hain.

Jab hum **seedha upar se (ya lagbhag seedha)** kisi denser medium (paani, glass) mein rakhi cheez ko dekhte hain, to wo apni asli jagah se **upar** dikhti hai. Wajah: cheez se aane wali rays surface par denser se rarer medium mein jaati hain, to **normal se door mud jaati hain**. Hamari aankh in rays ko peeche ki taraf seedhi extend karke dekhti hai, aur extend karne par wo rays **cheez se thodi upar** milti hain. Wahin hamein cheez ki image dikhti hai.

```
              :             Aankh ki taraf
              :             /
              :           /
              :         /
  Hawa        :       /
              :     /
  ────────────:───P───────────────────────────────Paani ki sateh
              : ./
  Paani       I /
              :/
              O
```

> **Figure 3.4** – Cheez O paani mein hai. O se chali ray surface par P par normal se door mudkar aankh ki taraf jaati hai. Aankh ko ray seedhi extend karne par dotted line dikhti hai, jo O ke thik upar wali line par I par milti hai. Isliye aankh ko cheez O ki jagah **I** par dikhti hai: surface ke zyada paas. (Sketch mein farak badha-chadha ke dikhaya hai.)

### Ek tayyar result

Agar cheez ki **real depth h** ho (surface se cheez tak) aur use **seedha upar se** dekhein, to dikhne wali depth **apparent depth h′** hoti hai, aur

> **real depth ÷ apparent depth = n**,   yaani   **h′ = h ÷ n**

(Ye result sirf **seedhe upar se (ya lagbhag seedhe)** dekhne par hai, kyunki tab ray ka angle chhota hota hai. Derive nahi karenge; Class 10 mein isko Snell ke niyam se nikaalte hain. Humare liye ye ek tayyar niyam hai.)

n > 1 hota hai, to **h′ hamesha h se chhota** hota hai: cheez surface ke paas dikhti hai.

**Raised amount** (kitna upar dikhi) = h − h′.

### Example 1: Talaab ki dikhne wali gehraai

Ek pond ki asli gehraai 3 m hai (paani ke liye n = 1.33). Seedha upar se dekhne par gehraai kitni lagegi?

- h′ = h ÷ n = 3 m ÷ 1.33 = **2.26 m**.

Jaanch: 2.26 m × 1.33 = 3.01 m ≈ 3 m ✅ (aur h′ < h, jaisa hona chahiye ✅).

### Example 2: Pool ka tala kitna upar dikha (Topic 1 ka sawaal)

Topic 1 ke swimming pool mein Tanishq ko tala paas dikh raha tha. Maan lo uski asli gehraai h = 2 m hai. Seedha upar se kitna gehra dikhega, aur tala kitna upar uthta dikhega?

- h′ = 2 m ÷ 1.33 = **1.5 m** (lagbhag).
- Raised = h − h′ = 2 m − 1.5 m = **0.5 m**.

Jaanch: 1.5 m × 1.33 = 2.0 m ✅. Yaani tala 0.5 m (50 cm) upar uthta dikhta hai. Isiliye pool andar se **kam gehra** lagta hai aur dekhne wale ko lagta hai ki paani kam gehra hai: ye bahut khatarnaak bhi ho sakta hai.

### Example 3: Tank

Ek tank mein paani ki gehraai 0.4 m hai. Seedha upar se dekhne par gehraai?

- h′ = 0.4 m ÷ 1.33 = **0.30 m** (30 cm).

Jaanch: 0.30 m × 1.33 = 0.40 m ✅.

### Example 4: Tel ka beaker

Ek beaker mein 12 cm gehra tel hai (n = 1.47). Dikhne wali gehraai?

- h′ = 12 cm ÷ 1.47 = **8.16 cm**.

Jaanch: 8.16 cm × 1.47 = 12.0 cm ✅.

### Example 5: Asli depth, dikhne wali se

Ek machhli seedha upar se dekhne par paani mein 4 m gehri dikhti hai. Asli gehraai?

- h = h′ × n = 4 m × 1.33 = **5.32 m**.

Jaanch: h′ = 5.32 m ÷ 1.33 = 4.0 m ✅.

### Ek galti jo bahut hoti hai

Kuch log h′ = h × n likh dete hain. **Ye galat hai.** n 1 se bada hota hai, to h × n karne par h′ asli depth se **zyada** aa jaayegi, jabki cheez to **upar uthi hui dikhti** hai. Yaad rakho: **dikhne wali depth = asli depth ÷ n**.

## Part E: Glass slab ke neeche ki cheez: apparent shift

Ek coin ko glass ke slab ke neeche rakhkar seedha upar se dekho. Coin thoda **upar utha hua** dikhta hai. Wahi h′ = h ÷ n yahan bhi laagu hota hai, aur slab ki moti t hi coin ki depth hai (coin slab ke neeche surface par hai).

- Dikhne wali depth = t ÷ n.
- **Apparent shift** = t − t ÷ n = **t × (1 − 1/n)** = t × (n − 1) ÷ n.

```
                Aankh (seedha upar se)
                       :
   ═══════════════════════════════════  Upar ki sateh
                       :
        Glass          :
                       :
                      I  (dikhne wali jagah, upar uthi hui)
                       :
                       :
   ═══════════════════════════════════  Neeche ki sateh (shabd yahan chhapa hai)
                      O
```

> **Figure 3.5** – Slab ke neeche chhapa shabd O hai. Seedha upar se dekhne par hamein wo I par dikhta hai, yaani asli jagah se upar utha hua. (Sketch mein shift badha ke dikhaya hai.)

### Example 6: Chhapa shabd, glass block ke neeche

Ek printed shabd 3 cm moti glass slab (n = 1.5) ke neeche hai. Seedha upar se dekhne par wo kitna upar utha dikhega?

- Apparent depth = 3 cm ÷ 1.5 = 2 cm.
- Apparent shift = t − t/n = 3 cm − 2 cm = **1 cm**.
- Ya seedha: 3 cm × (1 − 1/1.5) = 3 cm × (1 − 0.6667) = 3 cm × 0.3333 = **1 cm**.

Jaanch: dono tareeke 1 cm dete hain ✅. Isi tarah **6 cm moti slab** (n = 1.5) ke liye shift 6 cm × (1 − 1/1.5) = 6 cm × 0.3333 = **2 cm** hota hai: moti slab, zyada shift.

### Coin, beaker ke paani mein

Ek coin beaker ke neeche rakho aur use paani mein seedha upar se dekho: wo asli se upar utha dikhta hai. Agar coin pe paani 6 cm hai, to wo 6 cm ÷ 1.33 = 4.5 cm gehra dikhega.

### Real aur apparent depth se refractive index naapna

Kisi liquid ka refractive index naapne ke liye **n = real depth ÷ apparent depth** ka use hota hai.

Lab mein (ek **travelling microscope** aur ek chhote nishaan ke saath; travelling microscope ek aisa microscope hai jise scale par upar-neeche khiskaakar uski position padhi ja sakti hai):

1. Khaali beaker ke neeche ek chhota nishaan (pin ya dot) rakho. Microscope ko upar se uss nishaan par focus karo aur uski reading R₁ lo.
2. Beaker mein liquid dalo. Nishaan ab upar utha dikhta hai, isliye microscope ko uthakar dobara focus karo, reading R₂ lo.
3. Microscope ko liquid ki sateh (surface) par focus karo (sateh par thoda powder chhidak kar) aur reading R₃ lo.
4. **Real depth** = R₃ − R₁. **Apparent depth** = R₃ − R₂. Phir n = real ÷ apparent.

**Sample readings:** real depth = 12 cm, apparent depth = 9 cm.

### Example 7: n nikaalo

- n = real depth ÷ apparent depth = 12 cm ÷ 9 cm = **1.33**.

Jaanch: h′ = h ÷ n = 12 cm ÷ 1.33 = 9.02 cm ≈ 9 cm ✅. Aur 1.33 paani ka refractive index hai ✅, yaani liquid paani hai.

Ek aur example: ek liquid ki real depth 18 cm aur apparent depth 12 cm hai. n = 18 cm ÷ 12 cm = **1.5**. Jaanch: 18 cm ÷ 1.5 = 12 cm ✅.

## Part F: Aquarium aur patli khidki ka sheesha

### Aquarium ki machhli

Aquarium mein machhli hamesha **glass ke thoda paas** dikhti hai. Agar machhli glass se 40 cm andar hai, to wo 40 cm ÷ 1.33 = **30 cm** par dikhegi. Ye apparent depth hai (yahan side se, lagbhag seedha dekhne par).

Agar aquarium **golakar (curved)** ho, to machhli **badi** bhi dikhti hai. Wajah: paani se bhara curved glass ek tarah se cheez ko **bada karke (magnify)** dikhata hai. Ye ek alag baat hai (iska raaz agle topics mein khulega), par yaad rakho ki gol bowl ki machhli sach mein dikhti hui se chhoti hoti hai.

### Patli khidki ka sheesha

Ghar ki khidki ke sheeshe ki moti sirf kuch mm hoti hai. Maan lo t = 4 mm = 0.4 cm, i = 45°, n = 1.5. Pehle r = 28.13° (jaisa upar nikala).

- d = 0.4 cm × sin(45° − 28.13°) ÷ cos 28.13° = 0.4 cm × 0.2903 ÷ 0.8819 = **0.13 cm** = **1.3 mm**.

Jaanch: 0.4 cm × 0.3292 = 0.13 cm ✅. Itna chhota shift **hamari aankh ko pata hi nahi chalta**, isliye khidki ke sheeshe se bahar ki cheezein bilkul apni jagah par dikhti hain. Moti slab hone par hi shift dikhta hai (jaise Zainab ke 6 cm wale mein).

## Part G: Solved examples (lateral shift)

(Yahan n = 1.5 aur i, r hamesha normal se naape gaye hain.)

### Example 8: Angle of emergence

Ek ray glass slab (n = 1.5, dono taraf hawa) par i = 40° par girti hai. Find r (slab ke andar) aur angle of emergence e.

- sin r = sin 40° ÷ 1.5 = 0.6428 ÷ 1.5 = 0.4285, to **r = 25.37°**.
- Doosre surface par ray glass se hawa mein: e = i = **40°**.

Jaanch: n × sin r = 1.5 × sin 25.37° = 1.5 × 0.4285 = 0.6428 = sin 40° ✅ (e = 40° dono taraf).

### Example 9: Teen angles par r

n = 1.5 par r nikaalo: (a) i = 30°, (b) i = 45°, (c) i = 60°.

- (a) sin r = 0.5 ÷ 1.5 = 0.3333, **r = 19.47°**.
- (b) sin r = 0.7071 ÷ 1.5 = 0.4714, **r = 28.13°**.
- (c) sin r = 0.8660 ÷ 1.5 = 0.5774, **r = 35.26°**.

Jaanch: har baar r < i ✅ (glass denser hai, ray normal ki taraf mudi). Aur 1.5 × sin 19.47° = 1.5 × 0.3333 = 0.5 = sin 30° ✅.

### Example 10: Lateral shift teen angles par

Slab ki moti t = 5 cm, n = 1.5. Lateral shift nikaalo: i = 30°, 45°, 60°.

- **i = 30°:** r = 19.47°, i − r = 10.53°. d = 5 cm × sin 10.53° ÷ cos 19.47° = 5 cm × 0.1827 ÷ 0.9428 = **0.97 cm**.
- **i = 45°:** r = 28.13°, i − r = 16.87°. d = 5 cm × 0.2903 ÷ 0.8819 = **1.65 cm**.
- **i = 60°:** r = 35.26°, i − r = 24.74°. d = 5 cm × 0.4184 ÷ 0.8165 = **2.56 cm**.

Jaanch: i badhta gaya aur d bhi 0.97 → 1.65 → 2.56 cm badhta gaya ✅ (Part A ka "zyada angle, zyada shift"). Har d, t = 5 cm se chhota hai ✅.

### Example 11: Moti slab, t = 4 cm, i = 45°

t = 4 cm, i = 45°, n = 1.5. Find d.

- r = 28.13°, i − r = 16.87°.
- d = 4 cm × sin 16.87° ÷ cos 28.13° = 4 cm × 0.2903 ÷ 0.8819 = **1.32 cm**.

Jaanch: Example 10 mein t = 5 cm par d = 1.65 cm tha; 4 cm × 1.65 ÷ 5 = 1.32 cm ✅ (d seedha t ke saath badhta hai).

### Example 12: Slab ki moti, shift se

Ek slab (n = 1.5) par ray i = 45° par girti hai aur lateral shift d = 2 cm milta hai. Slab ki moti t kitni hai?

- r = 28.13°. sin(45° − 28.13°) = 0.2903, cos 28.13° = 0.8819, to sin(i − r) ÷ cos r = 0.2903 ÷ 0.8819 = 0.3292.
- d = t × 0.3292, to t = d ÷ 0.3292 = 2 cm ÷ 0.3292 = **6.08 cm**.

Jaanch: t = 6.08 cm par d = 6.08 cm × 0.3292 = 2.00 cm ✅.

### Example 13 (thoda extra): Do parat, ek coin

Ek coin ke upar pehle 10 cm paani (n = 1.33), aur uske upar 5 cm tel (n = 1.5) hai. Seedha upar se dekhne par coin kitni gehraai par dikhega?

- Paani ki parat ki dikhne wali moti = 10 cm ÷ 1.33 = 7.52 cm.
- Tel ki parat ki dikhne wali moti = 5 cm ÷ 1.5 = 3.33 cm.
- Kul apparent depth = 7.52 cm + 3.33 cm = **10.85 cm** (asli 15 cm).

Jaanch: 10.85 cm < 15 cm ✅, aur har parat ke liye alag-alag ÷ n kiya ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**1. Ye maanna ki emergent ray bhi mud jaati hai**
Slab ke parallel surface hone par emergent ray **incident ray ke parallel** hoti hai, mudi hui nahi. ❌ "ray ki direction badal gayi". ✅ "ray sirf side mein khisaki."

**2. Lateral shift ray ke saath-saath naapna**
d ko ray ke saath-saath ya slab ke surface ke saath-saath naapna galat hai. d hamesha incident (extended) aur emergent ray ke beech **perpendicular** doori hai (Fig 3.2).

**3. h′ = h ÷ n ko tirchhe dekhne par bhi laga dena**
Ye result sirf **seedhe upar se (ya lagbhag seedhe)** dekhne par theek hai. Bahut tirchhe dekhne par ye sahi nahi rehta.

**4. Formula ko ulta likhna: h′ = h × n**
Sahi hai h′ = h ÷ n. Dikhne wali depth hamesha asli se **kam**.

**5. Ye sochna ki slab rang ya rays ki ginti badal deti hai**
Slab sirf raasta shift karta hai. Light ka rang wahi rehta hai aur ray ki ginti (number) bhi wahi rehti hai.

**6. e = i ko har cheez par laga dena**
e = i tabhi hota hai jab slab ke **do surface parallel** hon aur **dono taraf same medium** (jaise hawa) ho. Alag medium ya parallel na hone par e ≠ i ho sakta hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Glass slab:** pehle surface (hawa se glass) par ray **normal ki taraf** mudti hai, andar seedhi chalti hai, doosre surface (glass se hawa) par **normal se door** mudti hai.
- Parallel surface ke liye **e = i** (alternate angles aur reversibility). Emergent ray incident ray ke **parallel** hai, bas **side mein khisaki** hui.
- **Lateral shift d** = incident (extended) aur emergent ray ke beech ki **perpendicular** doori. t badhne, i badhne, n badhne par d **badhta** hai; i = 0° par d = 0.
- *(Thoda extra)* **d = t × sin(i − r) ÷ cos r**. Jaise t = 6 cm, i = 60°, n = 1.5 par d = 3.07 cm. Patli khidki ke sheeshe mein d bahut chhota (lagbhag 1.3 mm).
- **Apparent depth:** seedhe upar se dekhne par **h′ = h ÷ n** (real ÷ apparent = n). Pool 2 m gehra, dikhta 1.5 m; tala 0.5 m upar.
- Slab ke neeche ki cheez ka **apparent shift = t × (1 − 1/n)**. 3 cm slab (n = 1.5) mein 1 cm.
- n = **real depth ÷ apparent depth** se liquid ka refractive index naap sakte hain (12 cm ÷ 9 cm = 1.33).
- *(Thoda extra)* Do parat ho to har parat ke liye alag-alag t ÷ n karke jodo (10.85 cm).
- Machhli aquarium mein paas dikhti hai; gol bowl mein badi bhi dikhti hai.
