# Topic 4: Total Internal Reflection

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki jab light glass slab ke andar se bahar hawa mein nikalti hai, to wo **normal se door** mud jaati hai (kyunki wo ghane medium se patle medium mein ja rahi hai). Ab ek sawal socho. Agar wo mudna itna zyada ho jaaye ki ray bahar nikal hi na paaye, to? Matlab, ray ko normal se door itna mudna pade ki wo surface ke bilkul saath-saath pahunch jaaye, ya usse bhi aage? Tab kya hoga? Jawab bada interesting hai: tab light bahar jaati hi nahi, **poori ki poori andar hi wapas laut aati hai**. Is topic mein hum isi ghatna ko samjhenge, aur dekhenge ki internet ke cable se lekar diamond ki chamak tak, isi ek idea ka kamaal hai.

## Hair jaisa patla taar aur Kabira ka sawaal

Shaam ko Kabira ke ghar broadband ka naya connection lagane Mr. Rawal aaye. Wo internet technician the. Unke bag mein ek chhoti si reel thi, jis par ek bahut patla, baal jitna patla, sheeshe jaisa taar lipta hua tha.

Kabira kitaab chhod kar paas aa gayi. "Uncle, ye kya hai? Ye to bilkul dhaage jaisa dikhta hai."

"Ye **optical fibre** hai, beta. Ye glass (ya plastic) ka bana hai. Aur isme se tumhara internet aayega, light ke roop mein."

"Light? Is baal jaise taar mein se?" Kabira ko yakeen nahi hua.

Mr. Rawal ne taar ka ek tukda kaata aur uska ek sira apni torch ke saamne rakh diya. Phir unhone taar ko ghumakar ek **gol loop** bana diya, jaise koi rassi ka chhalla. Kabira ne taar ka doosra sira dekha. Wahan ek chamakta hua bindu jagmaga raha tha: torch ki light taar ke tede-mede raaste se ghoom kar doosre sire se nikal rahi thi!

Kabira ne apni bhauhein sikodi. "Par uncle, light to seedhi line mein chalti hai na? Ye mudi kaise? Aur taar to sheeshe ka hai, glass to transparent hota hai. Light taar ki **side se bahar kyun nahi nikal jaati**? Wo to chaaron taraf se leak ho jaani chahiye thi."

Mr. Rawal muskuraye. "Bahut sahi sawaal poochha tumne. Iska jawab ek bahut khoobsurat niyam mein chhupa hai, jisse light sheeshe ke andar **kaid** ho jaati hai. Pehle wo niyam samajh lo, phir main tumhein bataunga." Is kahani ko hum topic ke ant mein solve karenge.

## Part A: Light ke andar kaid hone ki kahani, ek-ek angle karke

### Shuru mein wahi purana niyam

Topic 1 aur 3 mein hum dekh chuke hain: jab light **ghane (denser)** medium se **patle (rarer)** medium mein jaati hai (jaise glass ya paani se hawa mein), to wo **normal se door** mudti hai. Matlab angle of refraction (r), angle of incidence (i) se hamesha **bada** hota hai.

Ab ek experiment ki kalpana karo. Glass ke andar se ek ray glass-hawa wali surface par girti hai. Hum i ko dheere-dheere badhate hain:

**Case 1: i chhota hai (i < C).** Ray ka zyada hissa hawa mein nikal jaata hai aur normal se door mud jaata hai. Saath mein **bahut halka sa hissa** surface se reflect hokar glass mein wapas bhi aata hai (har surface par thodi reflection hoti hi hai).

**Case 2: i badhate-badhate ek khaas angle par pahunchna.** Jaise-jaise i badhta hai, refracted ray normal se aur door mudti jaati hai, aur surface ke nazdeek aati jaati hai. Ek khaas i par refracted ray **surface ke bilkul saath-saath** chalne lagti hai. Yaani angle of refraction **r = 90°** ho jaata hai. Is khaas angle of incidence ko **critical angle (C)** kehte hain.

**Case 3: i, C se bhi bada (i > C).** Ab refracted ray ko 90° se bhi zyada mudna padta, jo sambhav hi nahi hai. Isliye **koi refracted ray banti hi nahi**. Poori ki poori light surface se reflect hokar wapas glass mein laut aati hai. Aur ye reflection **reflection ke wahi do niyam** maanti hai jo humne pichhle chapter mein padhe the (i = r, aur sab ek plane mein). Ise **Total Internal Reflection (TIR)** kehte hain: *total* kyunki poori light wapas aati hai, *internal* kyunki wo andar ke medium mein hi rehti hai.

```
 Case 1: i < C             Case 2: i = C             Case 3: i > C

         N                         N                         N
         :     ↗ refracted         :                         :
         :   /                     :                         :
 Hawa    : /                Hawa   :                  Hawa   :
 ========O=========         ======O==→→→→→→→→         ========O=========
 Glass  /:\                 Glass /:\                 Glass  /:\
      ↗  :  ↘                   ↗  :  ↘                   ↗  :  ↘
 incident  weak              incident  reflected      incident  poori light
           reflected                                             reflect
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Teen cases (glass mein se hawa ki taraf jaati ray). Case 1: i < C, ray ka zyada hissa hawa mein jaata hai (normal se door mudkar), thoda sa hissa reflect hota hai. Case 2: i = C, refracted ray boundary ke saath-saath chalti hai (r = 90°). Case 3: i > C, hawa mein kuch nahi jaata, poori light reflect hokar glass mein laut aati hai. (Sketch ka shape sirf samjhane ke liye hai.)

### Critical angle ki paribhasha

> **Critical angle (C)** = ghane medium mein wo **angle of incidence**, jis par rarer medium mein refracted ray boundary ke saath-saath chalti hai (**angle of refraction = 90°**).

Dhyaan do: critical angle **ghane medium ke andar** ka incidence angle hai. 90° wala angle (refraction ka) to bas us khaas halat ki pehchaan hai.

### TIR ki do shartein

TIR tabhi hota hai jab **dono** shartein ek saath poori hon:

1. Light **ghane (denser) medium se patle (rarer) medium** ki taraf ja rahi ho (jaise glass se hawa, ya paani se hawa).
2. Ghane medium mein **angle of incidence, critical angle se bada** ho (i > C).

Agar inme se ek bhi shart toot gayi, to TIR nahi hoga.

> **Ek chhoti si baat:** jab i bilkul C ke barabar hota hai, to refracted ray boundary par "sarakti" hai. Sach mein hum i = C ko TIR ki **seema (border)** kehte hain: usse thoda sa bhi bada i ho jaaye to TIR pakka ho jaata hai.

## Part B: Critical angle ka formula (thoda extra)

*(Ye Class 10 ke saath overlap karta hai.)*

Hum Snell ka niyam pehle se jaante hain: **n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r**. Ghane medium (n) se hawa (n = 1.00) mein jaati ray ke liye, i = C par r = 90°. To:

n × sin C = 1.00 × sin 90°

sin 90° = 1, isliye:

> **sin C = 1/n**

Yaani **jitna bada refractive index, utna chhota critical angle.** Is formula se kisi bhi material ka n jaan kar uska C nikaala ja sakta hai (aur C jaan kar n bhi). Ab aam materials ke liye maan dekhte hain (Python se verify kiye hue):

| Material (hawa ke saath) | Refractive index (n) | sin C = 1/n | Critical angle C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paani | 1.33 | 0.752 | 48.75° (lagbhag 49°) |
| Crown glass | 1.52 | 0.658 | 41.1° (lagbhag 41°) |
| Aam glass | 1.5 | 0.667 | 41.8° (lagbhag 42°) |
| Diamond | 2.42 | 0.413 | 24.4° |

Kuch baatein jo is table se nikalti hain:

- Har ghane medium ka apna critical angle hota hai. **Critical angle sabhi media ke liye ek jaisa nahi hota.** (Aur alag-alag colour ke light ke liye n thoda-thoda alag hota hai, isliye C bhi thoda badal jaata hai. Ye baat hum Topic 7 mein samjhenge.)
- **Diamond ka C bahut chhota (24.4°)** hai. Matlab diamond ke andar light ko TIR ke liye bas 24.4° se bada angle chahiye, jo bahut aasani se mil jaata hai.

### Diamond itna chamakta kyun hai?

Diamond ko is tarah kaata jaata hai ki usme bahut saari chhoti-chhoti flat sateh (faces) bane hon. Diamond ke andar jaati light baar-baar in faces par C = 24.4° se bade angle par girti hai, aur har baar **TIR** ho jaata hai. Light diamond ke andar kai baar uchhalti hai, aur phir ek ya do faces se bahar nikalti hai. Isliye diamond andar se jagmagata hua dikhta hai. Wahi cheez kaat kar banaye gaye **glass ke chandelier** ke tukdon mein hoti hai: unmein bhi light TIR se andar uchhalti hai aur chamak dikhti hai, bas glass ka C (≈ 42°) diamond se bada hai, isliye utni chamak nahi aati.

## Part C: Rozmarra ki zindagi aur technology mein TIR

### 1. Optical fibre

Optical fibre kaise bana hota hai? Beech mein glass ya plastic ka bahut patla **core** (jiska refractive index zyada hai), aur uske chaaron taraf ek parat **cladding** (jiska refractive index kam hai). Core ka n zyada hai, cladding ka kam, isliye core "ghana" hai aur cladding "patla" medium.

Light ek sire se core mein ghusti hai, aur core-cladding ki deewar par bahut **bade angle (C se zyada)** par girti hai. Ghane se patle medium mein jaati light, C se bade angle par? **TIR!** Wo wapas core mein laut aati hai, phir doosri deewar par takrati hai, phir TIR... aur isi tarah **zig-zag** karti hui fibre ke doosre sire tak pahunch jaati hai. Har baar poori light wapas aati hai, isliye door tak jaane par bhi **bahut kam light khoti hai.**

```
 =====================================================  Cladding (n kam)
    /\      /\      /\      /\      /\
   /  \    /  \    /  \    /  \    /  \     Core (n zyada)
 →/    \  /    \  /    \  /    \  /    \→
 /      \/      \/      \/      \/      \
 =====================================================  Cladding (n kam)
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Optical fibre ke andar light ka zig-zag safar. Har baar core-cladding ki deewar par angle of incidence C se bada hota hai, isliye TIR hota hai aur light core ke andar hi rehti hai. (Sketch mein fibre bahut moti dikhayi hai: asli fibre baal jitni patli hoti hai.)

**Kahani ka hal.** Ab Kabira ke sawaal ka jawab saaf hai. Mr. Rawal ne bataya: "Dekho, light taar ki side wali deewar par bahut tirchhe (bade) angle par girti hai. Ye angle critical angle se bada hai, isliye wo bahar nikalti hi nahi: **TIR** ho jaata hai aur wo wapas taar ke andar laut aati hai. Taar ko loop mein modo, tab bhi light deewar se baar-baar uchhalti hui aage badhti rehti hai. Light taar ke andar kaid hai, isliye leak nahi hoti."

Kabira ne taar ko aur zor se ghumaya. "To loop ko kitna bhi gol kar doon, light nikal jaayegi?" "Nahi, bas **bahut tez mod** (bahut chhota bend) mat banana. Agar taar itna zyada mud jaaye ki deewar par angle C se chhota ho jaaye, to wahan se light bahar nikal jaayegi (aur taar toot bhi sakta hai)." Isliye fibre cable ko ek had se zyada nahi modte.

Optical fibre ke kaam:

- **Internet cables, telephone aur cable TV:** shehron aur mahasagaron ke beech data light ke roop mein jaata hai.
- **Endoscope (medicine):** ek patli, lachili nali jisme ek camera aur light ke fibres hote hain. Doctor ise sharir ke andar bhej kar peth ya gale ke andar dekh sakte hain, bina bada operation kiye.

Copper tar ke muqable fibre ke fayde (sirf naam samajh lo): ek hi fibre me **bahut zyada signals** ek saath bheje ja sakte hain, aur ye **bijli ke shor (electric noise)** se prabhavit nahi hota.

### 2. Total reflecting prism

Ek **right-angled glass prism** (jiske angle 45°, 45° aur 90° hon) bahut kaam ka hai. Glass (n = 1.5) ka C ≈ 42° hai, aur 45° isse **bada** hai. Isliye agar light prism ke andar 45° par kisi sateh par girti hai, to TIR ho jaata hai. Is prism se light ko **90°** ya **180°** modi ja sakti hai.

**90° ka mod:** ray ek chhoti (short) face par seedhi girti hai (to wo bina mude andar chali jaati hai). Wo lambi face (hypotenuse) par 45° par girti hai, TIR hota hai aur ray 90° mud kar doosri chhoti face se seedhi bahar nikal jaati hai.

**180° ka mod:** ray lambi face (hypotenuse) par seedhi girti hai, aur andar jakar dono chhoti faces par baari-baari 45° par girti hai. Dono baar TIR hota hai, aur ray ulte raaste par, apni original ray ke **parallel** wapas nikalti hai.

```
   90° ka mod                       180° ka mod

      C
      │\                                   /\
      │ \                                 /  \
      │  \                               /    \
←←←←←←│   *                             /      \
      │   ↑\                           *→→→→→→→→*
      │   ↑ \                         /↑        ↓\
      │   ↑  \                       / ↑        ↓ \
      A───────B                     /  ↑        ↓  \
          ↑                        ──────────────────
                                       ↑        ↓
```

> **Figure 4.3** – Total reflecting prism. Baayen: ray chhoti face se seedhi andar jaati hai, lambi face (CB) par 45° par TIR se mudti hai aur 90° ghoom kar doosri chhoti face (AC) se bahar aati hai. Daayen: ray lambi face (neeche wali) se seedhi andar jaati hai, dono chhoti faces par 45° par TIR hota hai, aur ray ulti disha mein (180° mudkar), apni pehli ray ke parallel wapas nikalti hai. (Sketch ka shape sirf samjhane ke liye hai.)

Isse **periscope** (submarine mein paani ke upar ka sab dekhne wala yantra) aur **binoculars** mein light mod kar rakhne ke liye ye prism use hota hai. **Cycle aur gaadiyon ke reflectors** (peeche laga laal reflector) mein bhi aise hi chhote prism jaise design hote hain: peeche se aati headlight wapas usi taraf laut aati hai, isliye driver ko wo chamakta dikhta hai. Sadak ke beech ki **cat's eyes** (chamakne wali goliyaan) bhi isi idea par bani hain.

**Prism mirror se behtar kyun?** Silvered mirror mein har reflection par thodi light kho jaati hai (mirror ki silver ki parat thodi light soakh leti hai), aur silver parat samay ke saath **tarnish** (kaali) ho jaati hai. TIR mein **poori light** wapas aati hai, aur isme koi parat hai hi nahi jo kharab ho. Isliye periscope aur binoculars ke liye prism behtar hai.

### 3. Mirage: garam sadak par "paani"

Garmi ki dopahar mein hum ne kabhi na kabhi ye dekha hoga: door sadak par aisa lagta hai jaise paani bhara ho, par paas jaane par sadak sookhi nikalti hai. Ye **mirage** hai. Registan (desert) mein ye aur bhi zyada hota hai.

Wajah: dhoop se sadak ke bilkul paas ki hawa bahut **garam** ho jaati hai, aur upar ki parton ki hawa thandi hoti hai. Garam hawa **patli** hoti hai, isliye optically rarer hai (uska refractive index thoda kam hota hai), aur thandi hawa optically denser hai. Matlab sadak ke paas se upar ki taraf jaate hue n badhta hai.

Aasmaan ki taraf se aati light jab neeche ki taraf aati hai, to wo har nayi, thodi garam (rarer) parat mein jaate hue **normal se door** mudti jaati hai (yaani neeche jaate-jaate aur zyada tirchhi hoti jaati hai). Ek jagah par wo itni tirchhi ho jaati hai ki us parat par angle of incidence C se bada ho jaata hai. Tab wahan **TIR** hota hai, aur ray **upar ki taraf** laut jaati hai.

Gaadi chalate hue driver ki aankh tak ye ray upar se aati hui pahunchti hai. Aankh ko ray ke raste ka pata nahi hota, wo bas ray ki aakhri disha dekhti hai: usse lagta hai ki roshni **sadak ke saath** neeche se aa rahi hai. Aasmaan ki tasveer (neela rang, aur door ki cheezein) sadak par ulti hui dikhti hai, bilkul jaise paani ki sateh mein dikhti hai. Isliye driver ko "paani ka talab" dikhta hai. Wo asal mein **aasmaan ki image** hai.

```
 S (aasmaan se aati ray)
 \
  \                          E (driver ki aankh)
 --\------------------------/--------  Thandi hawa (n zyada)
    \                      /
 ----\------------------/------------  Thodi garam hawa
      \_              _/
 --------\___________/---------------  Sabse garam hawa (n kam)
 ===========  Sadak  ==================
```

> **Figure 4.4** – Mirage. Aasmaan se aati ray garam (rarer) parton mein jaate hue normal se door mudti jaati hai, phir ek parat par TIR se upar ki taraf laut jaati hai aur driver ki aankh E tak pahunchti hai. Driver ko wo ray sadak ki taraf se aati dikhti hai: wahan "paani" dikhta hai (asal mein aasmaan ki image). (Ray ke mudne ka nazaara sirf samjhane ke liye hai.)

Mirage koi **dimaag ka dhokha nahi** hai. Ye **asli refraction (aur TIR)** ka asar hai, isliye camera bhi mirage ki tasveer kheench leta hai. Dhokha sirf itna hai ki hum us image ko paani maan lete hain.

### 4. Aur kuch jagah jahan TIR dikhta hai

- **Paani mein hawa ka bubble:** paani ke andar ka hawa ka bubble chandi jaisa chamakta dikhta hai. Paani se bubble ki sateh par jab light kai jagah C se bade angle par girti hai, to TIR hota hai aur wo wapas hamari taraf aati hai.
- **Paani mein dubi hui test tube:** agar khaali test tube ko paani mein tirchha karke daba kar side se dekho, to wo chandi jaisi chamakdaar dikhti hai. Wajah wahi: paani se test tube ke andar ki hawa ki sateh par TIR.
- **Paani ki dhaar mein light:** ek bartan ke chhed se nikalti paani ki dhaar mein agar light (ya laser) bheji jaaye, to light dhaar ke andar-andar uski curve ke saath mud jaati hai (fibre ki tarah). Ye ek mashhoor demonstration hai: dhaar light ko "pakad" leti hai.

## Part D: Solved examples

(Light ki speed c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s lete hain. Saari values Python se verify ki gayi hain.)

### Example 1: Teen materials ka critical angle

Paani (n = 1.33), aam glass (n = 1.5) aur diamond (n = 2.42) ka hawa ke saath critical angle nikalo. *(Ye formula Class 10 ke saath overlap karta hai.)*

- Paani: sin C = 1/1.33 = 0.752, to C = **48.75°** (lagbhag 49°).
- Glass: sin C = 1/1.5 = 0.667, to C = **41.8°** (lagbhag 42°).
- Diamond: sin C = 1/2.42 = 0.413, to C = **24.4°**.

Jaanch: n jitna bada, C utna chhota: 1.33 < 1.5 < 2.42 aur 48.75° > 41.8° > 24.4° ✅

### Example 2: Aise materials jinka n alag hai (thoda extra)

*(Class 10 overlap.)* Ek material ka n = 1.41 hai, doosre ka n = 2.0. Dono ke C nikalo.

- n = 1.41: sin C = 1/1.41 = 0.709, to C = **45.2°**.
- n = 2.0: sin C = 1/2.0 = 0.5, to C = **30°**.

Jaanch: sin 30° = 0.5 ✅ (n = 1/sin C = 1/0.5 = 2.0 ✅), aur sin 45.2° ≈ 0.709 ✅

### Example 3: Critical angle se refractive index

*(Thoda extra.)* Ek material ka critical angle 30° hai. Uska n nikalo. Phir ek aur ka C = 45° ho to uska n.

- n = 1/sin C.
- C = 30°: n = 1/sin 30° = 1/0.5 = **2.0**.
- C = 45°: n = 1/sin 45° = 1/0.7071 = **1.414** (lagbhag √2).

Jaanch: sin⁻¹(1/2.0) = 30° ✅ aur sin⁻¹(1/1.414) = 45° ✅

### Example 4: n se light ki speed (Topic 2 se link)

Topic 2 mein humne n = c/v dekha tha, yaani v = c/n. Diamond mein light ki speed nikalo (n = 2.42), aur batao C ka isse kya rishta hai.

- v = c/n = (3 × 10⁸ m/s) ÷ 2.42 = **1.24 × 10⁸ m/s** (lagbhag).
- Aam glass (n = 1.5) mein v = (3 × 10⁸ m/s) ÷ 1.5 = 2.0 × 10⁸ m/s.

Jaanch: diamond mein speed glass se kam hai (1.24 × 10⁸ m/s < 2.0 × 10⁸ m/s) kyunki n zyada hai ✅. Light jitni **dheemi** ho jaaye medium mein, utna chhota C: diamond mein light bahut dheemi hai, isliye C bhi sabse chhota hai ✅.

### Example 5: Glass-hawa boundary par TIR hoga ya nahi?

Glass (n = 1.5, C = 41.8°) ke andar se ek ray glass-hawa boundary par i = 30°, 42° aur 50° par girti hai. Har case mein batao TIR hoga ya nahi.

- **i = 30°:** 30° < 41.8°, to **TIR nahi hoga.** Ray hawa mein nikal jaayegi (Example 6 mein dekhenge: r = 48.6°), saath mein thoda sa reflection hoga.
- **i = 42°:** 42° > 41.8°, to **TIR hoga** (bas thoda sa upar). Ye critical angle ke bahut paas ka case hai.
- **i = 50°:** 50° > 41.8°, to **TIR pakka hoga.**

Jaanch: i = 42° par sin r = 1.5 × sin 42° = 1.5 × 0.669 = 1.004, jo 1 se bada hai. sin r kabhi 1 se bada nahi ho sakta, matlab refracted ray ban hi nahi sakti ✅. i = 50° par 1.5 × 0.766 = 1.149 > 1 ✅. Aur i = 30° par 1.5 × 0.5 = 0.75 < 1 ✅ (ray ban sakti hai).

### Example 6: i = 30° par glass se hawa mein refraction

Glass (n = 1.5) se hawa mein ray i = 30° par girti hai. Angle of refraction nikalo.

- n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r.
- 1.5 × sin 30° = 1.00 × sin r.
- 1.5 × 0.5 = 0.75 = sin r.
- r = sin⁻¹(0.75) = **48.6°**.

Jaanch: r (48.6°) > i (30°), jo ghane se patle medium mein jaate hue hona chahiye ✅. Aur 1.00 × sin 48.6° = 0.75 = 1.5 × 0.5 ✅. Ye bhi dekho: 48.6° ke baad bhi 90° se bahut kam hai, isliye ray aaram se nikal jaati hai.

### Example 7: Right-angled prism (hawa mein): kya TIR hoga?

Ek right-angled prism (45°, 45°, 90°, n = 1.5) hawa mein hai. Ek ray prism ki ek chhoti face par seedhi girti hai, aur andar jaakar lambi face (hypotenuse) par pahunchti hai. TIR hoga?

- Chhoti face par ray seedhi girti hai (i = 0°), to wo **bina mude** andar jaati hai.
- Prism ke angles 45°-45°-90° hone ki wajah se wo lambi face par **45°** ke angle par girti hai (normal se naapa hua).
- C = 41.8° (glass-hawa). Compare: 45° > 41.8°.
- Light glass (ghane) se hawa (rarer) ki taraf ja rahi hai, aur i > C. Dono shartein poori hain.

**TIR hoga**, aur ray 90° mud jaayegi (Figure 4.3, baayen).

Jaanch: sin r = 1.5 × sin 45° = 1.5 × 0.7071 = 1.061 > 1, to refracted ray ban hi nahi sakti ✅

### Example 8: Wahi prism paani ke andar: kya ab bhi TIR hoga?

Wahi prism (n = 1.5) ab paani (n = 1.33) mein dubo diya gaya hai. Ek ray pehle ki tarah lambi face par 45° par girti hai. Kya TIR hoga?

- Ab boundary glass aur **paani** ke beech hai, hawa nahi.
- Part B wali baat yahan bhi chalegi, bas hawa (n = 1.00) ki jagah paani (n₂ = 1.33) hai: n₁ sin C = n₂ sin 90°, yaani sin C = n₂/n₁ = 1.33/1.5 = 0.887.
- C = sin⁻¹(0.887) = **62.5°**.
- 45° < 62.5°, to **TIR nahi hoga.** Ray paani mein nikal jaayegi.

Jaanch: sin r = (1.5/1.33) × sin 45° = 1.128 × 0.7071 = 0.7975 < 1, to r = sin⁻¹(0.7975) = 52.9°. Ray ban sakti hai aur paani mein nikal jaati hai ✅. **Interesting baat:** wahi prism jo hawa mein light ko 90° mod deta hai, paani ke andar aise kaam nahi karta. Kyunki TIR sirf prism ke n par nahi, **dono media ke n** par nirbhar karta hai.

### Example 9: Glass-paani boundary par critical angle (thoda extra)

*(Class 10 overlap.)* Glass (n = 1.5) se paani (n = 1.33) ki taraf jaati ray ke liye critical angle nikalo.

- Ghane (n₁ = 1.5) se patle (n₂ = 1.33) medium mein: sin C = n₂/n₁.
- sin C = 1.33 ÷ 1.5 = 0.8867.
- C = sin⁻¹(0.8867) = **62.46°**.

Jaanch: n₂ = 1.00 (hawa) rakhne par sin C = 1/1.5 = 0.667 aur C = 41.8°: wahi pichhla formula wapas mil gaya ✅. Ye bhi dekho: C = 62.46° > 41.8°, kyunki glass-paani ke n mein farak kam hai, isliye TIR ke liye zyada tirchha angle chahiye ✅.

### Example 10: Paani ke andar bulb ki roshni ka gol ghera (thoda extra)

*(Ye Class 10 se bhi thoda aage ka hai: sirf shauk ke liye.)* Ek chhota bulb talaab ki sateh se h = 2 m neeche hai. Paani ki sateh par light ka kitna bada gol ghera dikhega? (n = 1.33.)

- Bulb ki jo ray sateh par C se chhote angle par girti hai, wo bahar nikal jaati hai. C se bade angle waali rays TIR se wapas neeche laut jaati hain.
- Isliye sateh par roshni ke ghere ka kinara wo ray banati hai jo C par girti hai. C = sin⁻¹(1/1.33) = 48.75°.
- Bulb se seedhe upar ki line aur ray ke beech ka angle C hai. Right triangle mein: tan C = r/h, to r = h × tan C.
- r = 2 m × tan 48.75° = 2 m × 1.140 = **2.28 m**.

Jaanch: ghera bulb ke theek upar centre mein banta hai, aur uske bahar waali sateh se bulb ki light nahi nikalti (wahan i > C hai, to TIR hota hai) ✅. Ye bhi sahi hai ki paani ka n kam hota to C bada hota, aur ghera bhi bada hota (tan C bada hota).

### Example 11: Optical fibre ki core-cladding boundary (thoda extra)

*(Class 10 overlap.)* Ek fibre ke core ka n = 1.50 aur cladding ka n = 1.45 hai. Core-cladding boundary ka critical angle nikalo.

- Core (ghana) se cladding (patla) ki taraf: sin C = n₂/n₁ = 1.45 ÷ 1.50 = 0.9667.
- C = sin⁻¹(0.9667) = **75.2°**.
- Matlab core mein ray ko deewar par 75.2° se bade angle par girna hai, yaani fibre ke axis ke lagbhag saath-saath (axis se 90° − 75.2° = 14.8° se kam angle par) chalna hai.

Jaanch: sin 75.2° = 0.9667 ✅. Isliye hi fibre mein light ko ek sire par sahi disha mein daalna padta hai, aur fibre ko bahut tez nahi modte: nahi to angle C se chhota ho jaata hai aur light bahar nikal jaati hai.

### Example 12: Kahani ka hal: Kabira ke sawaal ke saath ek number

Mr. Rawal ne Kabira se kaha: "Maan lo fibre ke core ka glass n = 1.5 hai aur bahar hawa hai (bina cladding ke). Taar ke andar ek ray side ki deewar par 60° ke angle par girti hai. Bata, wo bahar jaayegi?"

- Ghana medium (glass) se patla (hawa), C = 41.8°.
- i = 60° > 41.8°, aur light ghane se patle mein ja rahi hai.
- Dono shartein poori hain, **TIR hoga.** Ray bahar nahi jaayegi.

"Aur agar ray 30° par girti?" Kabira ne puchha. "To wo i < C hai, aur wo ray kuch bahar nikal jaati. Isliye fibre mein hamesha **bahut tirchhi** rays hi chalti hain: ek laser jaise patli beam fibre ke axis ke lagbhag saath-saath daali jaati hai."

Jaanch: sin r = 1.5 × sin 60° = 1.5 × 0.866 = 1.299 > 1, to refracted ray ban hi nahi sakti ✅ (60° par TIR). Aur 30° par 1.5 × 0.5 = 0.75 < 1 ✅ (refraction hoga).

Kabira ne taar ko haath mein lekar dekha, aur kaha, "Uncle, ab samajh aaya: light ko taar ke andar kaid kar diya gaya hai, isliye wo gol loop mein bhi aaram se doosre sire tak pahunch jaati hai." Mr. Rawal ne hanskar kaha, "Ab tum bhi technician ban sakti ho."

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**1. Ye maanna ki TIR rarer se denser medium mein jaate hue bhi hota hai**
TIR ki pehli shart hai: light **ghane se patle** medium mein jaa rahi ho. Hawa se glass mein jaati ray hamesha refract hoti hai (normal ke paas mudti hai). Wahan critical angle hota hi nahi.
❌ Hawa se glass mein 80° par TIR. ✅ Hawa se glass mein ray hamesha andar jaati hai: sin r = sin 80° ÷ 1.5 = 0.657, r = 41.0°.

**2. Ye maanna ki i > C hote hi TIR ho jaata hai, medium chahe jo ho**
Do shartein ek saath chahiye: ghane se patle medium mein jaana **aur** i > C. Sirf bada angle kaafi nahi hai.

**3. Critical angle ko angle of refraction se mila dena**
C ek angle of **incidence** hai (ghane medium ke andar). Us par angle of refraction 90° hota hai. Ye do alag angles hain: C ≈ 42° (glass ke liye), aur r = 90° (us khaas halat mein).

**4. Ye maanna ki critical angle sab media ya sab colours ke liye ek jaisa hai**
C = sin⁻¹(1/n): n alag, to C alag. Paani ka 48.75°, glass ka 41.8°, diamond ka 24.4°. Alag colours ke liye n bhi thoda alag hota hai, isliye C bhi.

**5. Ye maanna ki mirage dimaag ka dhokha hai**
Mirage **asli refraction aur TIR** se banta hai. Camera bhi use kheench leta hai. Dhokha bas itna hai ki hum aasmaan ki image ko paani samajh lete hain.

**6. TIR ko "aam reflection" samajh lena**
Aam reflection (jaise glass ki surface par) har boundary par **thodi** hoti hai, par baaki light refract hoti hai. TIR mein **100% light** reflect hoti hai aur kuch bhi refract nahi hota. Aur TIR sirf ghane se patle medium mein hota hai.

## Quick Recap

- Jab light **ghane se patle** medium mein jaati hai, to wo normal se door mudti hai (r > i).
- i badhate-badhate ek angle par r = 90° ho jaata hai: refracted ray boundary ke saath-saath. Ye **critical angle (C)** hai.
- Agar **i > C**, to refraction nahi hota: poori light wapas ghane medium mein reflect ho jaati hai. Ye **Total Internal Reflection (TIR)** hai. Reflection ke wahi do niyam lagte hain.
- **TIR ki do shartein:** (1) light ghane se patle medium mein jaye, (2) i > C.
- *(Thoda extra)* **sin C = 1/n** (hawa ke saath). Do media ke beech: sin C = n₂/n₁ (ghane se patle mein). n bada, to C chhota.
- Maan: paani C = 48.75° (≈ 49°), glass C = 41.8° (≈ 42°), diamond C = 24.4°.
- **Diamond ka chhota C** ki wajah se andar baar-baar TIR hota hai: wahi uski chamak hai. Cut glass (chandelier) mein bhi TIR se chamak aati hai.
- **Optical fibre:** core (n zyada) plus cladding (n kam). Light core ki deewar par C se bade angle par girti hai, to baar-baar TIR hota hai aur light door tak jaati hai. Internet, telephone, cable TV, endoscope. Fayde: kam loss, bahut saare signals, electric noise se bachaav.
- **Total reflecting prism** (45°-45°-90°, C ≈ 42° < 45°): light ko 90° ya 180° modta hai. Periscope, binoculars, reflectors, cat's eyes. Mirror se behtar: koi light loss nahi, tarnishing nahi.
- **Mirage:** garam sadak ke paas ki hawa rarer hai, aasmaan ki light upar ki taraf TIR se mud jaati hai, aur driver ko paani jaisa dikhta hai (aasmaan ki image). Ye asli refraction hai, dimaag ka dhokha nahi.
- Aur jagah: paani mein bubble ya test tube chandi jaisi chamakti hai, paani ki dhaar mein light mud jaati hai.
- Same prism paani mein dubne par (C = 62.5°) 45° par TIR nahi karta: TIR dono media ke n par nirbhar hai.

## Practice Questions

1. Calculate the critical angle for a medium of refractive index 1.25 when light goes from that medium into air. *(Class 10 overlap.)*
2. The critical angle of a transparent material is 40°. Find its refractive index. *(Class 10 overlap.)*
3. State true or false and give a one-line reason: (a) TIR can happen when light goes from air to glass. (b) TIR can happen when light goes from water to air at an angle of incidence of 30°. (c) The critical angle of diamond is smaller than the critical angle of glass. (d) In TIR the whole light is reflected back and none is refracted.
4. A ray in glass (n = 1.5) falls on the glass-air surface at an angle of incidence of 35°. Will it undergo TIR? If not, find the angle of refraction.
5. Explain, in two or three lines, why a diamond sparkles more than a piece of ordinary glass.
6. Why does an air bubble in water, or an empty test tube dipped in water and seen from the side, look silvery?
7. Describe how an optical fibre keeps light inside it even when it is bent. Name two uses of optical fibres.
8. A right-angled glass prism (n = 1.5) is in air. A ray falls on its long face (hypotenuse) at an angle of incidence of 45° from inside the glass. Will TIR occur? Give a reason using the critical angle.
9. *(Thoda extra.)* A small light source is at a depth of 3 m below the surface of water (n = 1.33). Find the radius of the circular patch of light on the surface through which the light can come out.
10. Explain in your own words why a driver sees a "pool of water" on a hot road. Is it only a trick of the mind?
11. Why is a total reflecting prism preferred over a silvered mirror in a periscope?
12. *(Thoda extra.)* Light travels in glass of n = 1.5. Find the critical angle for the glass-water boundary (n of water = 1.33). Is it larger or smaller than the critical angle for the glass-air boundary?

### Answers

1. sin C = 1/n = 1/1.25 = 0.8, to C = **53.1°**.
   Jaanch: sin 53.1° ≈ 0.8 ✅

2. n = 1/sin C = 1/sin 40° = 1/0.6428 = **1.556** (lagbhag 1.56).
   Jaanch: sin⁻¹(1/1.556) = 40° ✅

3. (a) **False** ❌. Hawa se glass mein jaati light rarer se denser mein ja rahi hai, wahan TIR nahi hota.
   (b) **False** ❌. Paani se hawa ke liye C = 48.75°, aur 30° < 48.75°, to refraction hoga.
   (c) **True** ✅. Diamond ka C = 24.4° aur glass ka C = 41.8°.
   (d) **True** ✅. TIR mein poori light reflect hoti hai, refracted ray hoti hi nahi.

4. C = 41.8° (glass-air). i = 35° < 41.8°, to **TIR nahi hoga**.
   n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r: 1.5 × sin 35° = 1.00 × sin r. 1.5 × 0.5736 = 0.8604 = sin r, to r = **59.4°**.
   Jaanch: r (59.4°) > i (35°), ghane se patle medium ke liye sahi ✅

5. Diamond ka refractive index (2.42) bahut zyada hai, to uska critical angle bahut chhota (24.4°) hai. Isliye diamond ke andar light baar-baar C se bade angle par girti hai aur har baar TIR hota hai. Light andar kai baar uchhalti hai aur kuch faces se nikalti hai, isliye diamond jagmagata hai. Ordinary glass ka C ≈ 42° bada hai, to utni zyada TIR nahi hoti.

6. Paani se bubble (ya test tube ke andar ki hawa) ki sateh par light kai jagah C se bade angle par girti hai (ghane se patle medium mein). Wahan TIR hota hai aur poori light wapas hamari aankh ki taraf aati hai, isliye wo chandi jaisa chamakta hai.

7. Fibre mein beech ka core (n zyada) aur uske chaaron taraf cladding (n kam) hoti hai. Light core-cladding ki deewar par C se bade angle par girti hai, to har baar TIR hota hai aur light zig-zag karte hue doosre sire tak pahunchti hai, mod par bhi (agar mod bahut tez na ho). Uses: internet / telephone / cable TV cables, aur endoscope (doctor ke liye).

8. Glass-air ka C = 41.8°. Ray ka i = 45° > 41.8°, aur light glass (ghane) se hawa (patle) mein ja rahi hai. Dono shartein poori hain, to **TIR hoga**.
   Jaanch: 1.5 × sin 45° = 1.5 × 0.7071 = 1.061 > 1 ✅

9. C = sin⁻¹(1/1.33) = 48.75°. r = h × tan C = 3 m × tan 48.75° = 3 m × 1.140 = **3.42 m**.
   Jaanch: pichhle example mein h = 2 m par r = 2.28 m tha, ab h 1.5 guna hai to r bhi 1.5 guna: 2.28 × 1.5 = 3.42 m ✅

10. Garam sadak ke paas ki hawa rarer hoti hai (n kam) aur upar ki hawa denser. Aasmaan se aati light neeche aate hue normal se door mudti jaati hai, aur ek parat par angle C se bada ho jaata hai, to TIR hota hai aur ray upar laut jaati hai. Driver ki aankh ko ray neeche sadak se aati dikhti hai, aur aasmaan ki image sadak par paani jaisi dikhti hai. Ye dimaag ka dhokha nahi hai, asli refraction aur TIR ka asar hai (camera bhi iski tasveer le leta hai).

11. TIR mein poori light reflect hoti hai, to koi loss nahi hota. Prism par silver ki parat nahi hoti, isliye tarnish bhi nahi hota. Silvered mirror mein har reflection par thodi light kho jaati hai aur silver samay ke saath kaali ho jaati hai.

12. sin C = n₂/n₁ = 1.33/1.5 = 0.8867, to C = **62.46°**. Glass-air ka C 41.8° hai, to ye **bada** hai.
   Jaanch: dono media ke n mein farak kam hai, isliye TIR ke liye zyada tirchha angle chahiye ✅
