# Topic 5: Lenses: Convex aur Concave

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki glass ke andar light kabhi-kabhi poori tarah wapas laut jaati hai (total internal reflection), aur isi se prism, optical fibre aur mirage ke raaz khule. Wahan glass ke tukde ke **sapaat (flat) faces** the. Ab sawal ye hai: **agar glass ka tukda sapaat na ho, balki gol-gol muda hua (curved) ho, to light ke saath kya hoga?** Kya wo kirnein ko ek jagah ikattha kar sakta hai? Ya unhein phaila sakta hai? Is topic mein hum curved glass ke do roop, **convex lens** aur **concave lens**, unke important points (optical centre, foci, focal length) aur teen khaas kirnein seekhenge. Image kaise banti hai, wo agle topic mein.

**Is poore topic mein: sirf patle (thin) lens lenge, aur lens ka aperture chhota maanenge, aur kirnein axis ke kareeb waali lenge.** Isse saari baatein saaf aur simple rehti hain.

## Mr. Pinto ka chhota sa sheesha

Gali ke mod par Mr. Pinto ki ghadi-marammat (watch repair) ki dukaan thi. Wo apni aankh ke aage ek chhota sa gol kaanch (loupe) lagakar ek ghadi ke andar ke baareek gear dekh rahe the. Paas hi unka chhota padosi Yuvaan khada tha, jo apne dada ji ka chashma theek karwane aaya tha.

"Uncle, aap itne chhote pure kaise dekh lete ho?" Yuvaan ne poochha.

Mr. Pinto ne loupe uski aankh ke paas laga di. Yuvaan hairaan reh gaya: ghadi ke gears **bahut bade** dikh rahe the, jaise kisi machine ke pahiye!

Phir Yuvaan ne apne dada ji ka chashma uthaya (dada ji door ka to dekh lete hain, par paas ka padhne ke liye unhein chashma lagana padta hai). Us chashme ke kaanch se akshar bhi bade dikhe, bilkul loupe jaise. Phir usne apne dost ka chashma (jo door ka saaf dekhne ke liye lagata hai) aankh ke aage kiya: ab to har cheez **chhoti** ho gayi!

Yuvaan ne sar khujaya, "Uncle, kaanch to kaanch hi hai. Phir ek kaanch cheezein bada kyun dikhata hai aur doosra chhota?"

Mr. Pinto muskuraye, "Dono kaanch ki **shape** alag hai, beta. Ek beech mein se moti, doosri beech mein se patli. Pehle in shapes ke naam aur unke points samajh lo, phir main tumhe wapas isi sawal par laaunga." Is kahani ko hum topic ke beech mein solve karenge.

## Part A: Lens kya hai, aur uske do roop

Topic 3 mein humne sapaat glass slab dekha tha. Ab ek aisa glass ka tukda socho jiski **ek ya dono surfaces gole (spherical) ka hissa** hon, jaise kisi sheeshe ki gend ka chhilka. Aise paardarshi (transparent) tukde ko **lens** kehte hain.

> **Lens** = kaanch ya plastic jaisi transparent cheez ka tukda, jo do surfaces se ghira ho, aur jinmein se kam se kam ek surface spherical ho.

Hum sirf **patli (thin) lens** lenge, yaani lens ki motai uski chaudai (aperture) ke muqable bahut kam ho. (Moti lens aage ki class mein.)

### 1. Convex lens

> **Convex lens** = beech mein **moti** aur kinaron par **patli** lens. Ye parallel kirnein ko **ikattha (converge)** karti hai, isliye isse **converging lens** bhi kehte hain.

### 2. Concave lens

> **Concave lens** = beech mein **patli** aur kinaron par **moti** lens. Ye parallel kirnein ko **phailati (diverge)** hai, isliye isse **diverging lens** bhi kehte hain.

```
        Convex lens               Concave lens

           ╭╮                     ┌──────┐
          (  )                      )  (
         (    )                     )  (
         (    )                     )  (
          (  )                      )  (
           ╰╯                     └──────┘
   beech mein moti,           beech mein patli,
   kinare patle               kinare moti
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Convex lens (beech se moti) aur concave lens (beech se patli) ka side se dikhne wala roop (cross-section).

Dono lens ke aur bhi roop hote hain, jaise:

| Convex lens ke roop | Concave lens ke roop |
|---|---|
| Double convex (dono surface bahar ki taraf ubhre) | Double concave (dono surface andar ki taraf dabe) |
| Plano-convex (ek surface sapaat, ek ubhra hua) | Plano-concave (ek surface sapaat, ek dabi hui) |
| Concavo-convex (ek dabi, ek ubhri, par beech mein moti) | Convexo-concave (ek ubhri, ek dabi, par beech mein patli) |

Hum in sab ko sirf **naam se** jaanenge. Kaam sab ka ek jaisa hai: convex jaisi lens kirnein ikattha karti hai, concave jaisi phailati hai.

**Pehchanne ka aasaan tarika:** lens ko haath mein lekar ungli se mehsoos karo. Agar **beech ka hissa moti hai** to convex, agar **beech ka hissa patla hai (kinare moti)** to concave.

### 3. Lens ko "chhote prisms ka set" samjho

Prism ek aisa glass ka tukda hota hai jiske do sapaat face ek kone par milte hain. Prism light ko apne **moti hisse (base) ki taraf** modta hai. Ab socho ki lens chhote-chhote prisms ko jodkar bani hai.

- **Convex lens** = do prisms jo **base se base** milakar rakhe hain. Upar waale prism ki ray neeche (base ki taraf) mudti hai, neeche waale ki ray upar. Dono axis ki taraf mudti hain, isliye kirnein **ikatthi** hoti hain.
- **Concave lens** = do prisms jo **apex se apex** (patle kinare se patle kinare) milakar rakhe hain. Upar waale prism ki ray upar (apne base ki taraf) mudti hai, neeche waale ki neeche. Isliye kirnein **phail jaati** hain.

```
   Convex lens (base se base)         Concave lens (apex se apex)

            apex                         base
             /\                        ──────────
            /  \                        \      /
           /    \                        \    /
        ──────────  base                  \  /
        ──────────  base                   \/   apex
           \    /                          /\   apex
            \  /                          /  \
             \/                          /    \
            apex                      ──────────
                                         base

   kirnein base ki taraf mudti hain,   kirnein base ki taraf mudti hain,
   yaani axis ki taraf: ikatthi        yaani axis se door: phaili hui
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Convex lens do prisms ko base se base jodne jaisi hai, aur concave lens apex se apex jodne jaisi. Prism ray ko apne base ki taraf modta hai.

Ye sirf ek aasaan idea hai (asli lens ki surface prism ki tarah sapaat nahi, curved hoti hai, aur beech ki ray bilkul nahi mudti, kyunki wahan dono surface lagbhag samanantar hote hain). Par isse dikhta hai ki convex lens kirnein ikattha aur concave phailati kyun hai.

## Part B: Lens ke important points aur lines

Prakash (light) hamesha **baayen se daayen** chalta hua maanenge. Lens ek khadi (vertical) rekha ki tarah dikhayenge: **convex lens** ke upar aur neeche ke sire par **bahar ki taraf** teer ki nok (▲ upar, ▼ neeche), aur **concave lens** ke sire par **andar ki taraf** teer ki nok (▼ upar, ▲ neeche). Ye lens ki standard drawing hai.

**Convex lens ke parts:**

```
                                 ▲ A
                                 │
                                 │
                                 │
                                 │  aperture AB
                                 │
─────────+───────────+───────────┼───────────+───────────+────────
        C₁          F₁           │O         F₂          C₂
                                 │
                     ◄───────────┼───────────►
                        f = OF₁  │  f = OF₂
                                 │
                                 ▼ B
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Convex lens. O = optical centre, principal axis lens ke beech se guzarti hai. F₁ aur F₂ = dono principal foci (O se barabar doori par, ek-ek har taraf). C₁ aur C₂ = dono centres of curvature (sketch mein hum inhein F se doguni doori par dikhate hain). f = OF₁ = OF₂. AB = aperture.

**Concave lens ke parts:**

```
                                 ▼ A
                                 │
                                 │
                                 │
                                 │  aperture AB
                                 │
─────────+───────────+───────────┼───────────+───────────+────────
        C₁          F₁           │O         F₂          C₂
                                 │
                     ◄───────────┼───────────►
                        f = OF₁  │  f = OF₂
                                 │
                                 ▲ B
```

> **Figure 5.4** – Concave lens. Yahan bhi F₁ aur F₂ O se barabar doori par hain, aur C₁, C₂ sketch mein 2f par dikhaye hain. Fark ye hai ki F₁ **virtual** hai (aage Part C dekho).

Ab ek-ek shabd:

### 1. Optical centre (O)

> **Optical centre (O)** = lens ka beech wala point.

**O se guzarti ray bina mude seedhi nikal jaati hai** (patli lens ke liye). Isse hum **doori naapne ka starting point (origin)** maante hain.

### 2. Principal axis

> **Principal axis** = lens ke dono centres of curvature (C₁ aur C₂) aur optical centre O se guzarti seedhi rekha.

Ye lens ko seedha (90° par) kaatti hai.

### 3. Centres of curvature (C₁ aur C₂)

Lens ki **do** surfaces hain, aur har surface kisi gole ka hissa hai. Isliye lens ke **do centres of curvature** hote hain, ek-ek har taraf. Jab dono surface ek jaise hon (jaise double convex), to dono C, O se barabar doori par hote hain. Surface ka radius R ho to C, O se R doori par hota hai. Ye asli doori lens ki shape aur glass par depend karti hai, aur 2f ke barabar ho, ye zaroori nahi. Phir bhi sketch banane ke liye hum ek aasaan **drawing convention** lete hain: axis par O se 2f doori wale point ko **2F** kehte hain, aur sketch mein C ko wahin dikha dete hain. Yaad rakho: ye sirf drawing ki suvidha hai. Image banane mein (Topic 6) kaam **2F** wale point ka hai, aur wahan hum 2F₁, 2F₂ hi likhenge.

### 4. Principal foci (F₁ aur F₂)

Ek lens ke **do foci** hote hain, **ek lens ke har taraf**. Patli lens ke liye (jab lens ke dono taraf same medium, jaise hawa, ho), dono foci optical centre se **barabar doori** par hote hain.

> **Pehla focus F₁:** lens ke us taraf ka focus jahan se light aa rahi hai.
> **Doosra focus F₂:** lens ke doosri taraf ka focus (jahan light jaati hai).

### 5. Focal length (f)

> **Focal length (f)** = optical centre O se principal focus tak ki doori: **f = OF₁ = OF₂.**

### 6. Aperture

> **Aperture** = lens ki chaudai (width), yaani lens ke ek kinare (A) se doosre kinare (B) tak ki doori.

Chhoti lens ka aperture kam, badi lens ka zyada. Figure 5.3 aur 5.4 mein AB aperture hai.

## Part C: Focus: real aur virtual

Ek bahut door ki cheez (jaise Sooraj) se aati hui kirnein **principal axis ke parallel** maani jaati hain. Dekho ye kirnein lens se guzar kar kya karti hain.

**Convex lens ke liye:** axis ke parallel kirnein lens se refract hokar **ek point par milti hain**. Wo point lens ke doosri taraf hota hai. Ye **F₂** hai.

```
                                 ▲
                                 │
                                 │
 ─────────────►──────────────────│─╲
                                 │  ╲──↘
                                 │      ╲─╲
─────────+───────────+───────────┼─────────╲─+───────────+────────
        C₁          F₁           │O     ╱─╱ F₂          C₂
                                 │  ╱──↗
 ─────────────►──────────────────│─╱
                                 │
                                 │
                                 ▼
```

> **Figure 5.5** – Convex lens: principal axis ke parallel kirnein refract hone ke baad F₂ par milti hain. Wahan kirnein **sach mein** milti hain, isliye ye **real focus** hai. (Ye Ray 1 ka sketch bhi hai.)

**Concave lens ke liye:** axis ke parallel kirnein refract hone ke baad **phail jaati hain**, kabhi nahi milti. Par agar hum refract hui kirnein ko peeche (light ki aane ki taraf) dotted line se badhayein, to wo **lens ke usi taraf ek point (F₁) se aati hui lagti hain** jahan se light aa rahi hai.

```
                                 ▼          ╱─
                                 │      ╱──╱
                                 │  ╱──↗
 ─────────────►──────────────────│─╱
                           ····  │
                        ···      │
─────────+───────────+───────────┼───────────+───────────+────────
        C₁          F₁  ···      │O         F₂          C₂
                           ····  │
 ─────────────►──────────────────│─╲
                                 │  ╲──↘
                                 │      ╲──╲
                                 ▲          ╲─
```

> **Figure 5.6** – Concave lens: principal axis ke parallel kirnein refract hone ke baad phail jaati hain, jaise wo lens ke aage ke point F₁ se aa rahi hon. Dotted line (·····) kirnein ko peeche badhane ke liye hai. Wahan kirnein sach mein nahi milti, isliye ye **virtual focus** hai. (Ye Ray 1 ka sketch bhi hai.)

**Real aur virtual focus mein farak:**
- **Real focus** (convex lens): kirnein sach mein wahan milti hain. Wahan screen rakho to bright spot dikhega.
- **Virtual focus** (concave lens): kirnein sirf wahan se aati hui **lagti** hain. Wahan screen rakhne par kuch nahi dikhega.

(Mirrors mein bhi aisa hi tha: concave mirror ka focus real, convex mirror ka virtual. Dhyan do: **lens mein convex ka focus real hai**, mirror mein concave ka. Dono ka kaam "converging" hi hai: convex lens aur concave mirror dono kirnein ikattha karte hain. Par ek lens hai, ek mirror: ye mat milana.)

## Part E: Convex aur concave ki tulna

| Baat | Convex lens | Concave lens |
|---|---|---|
| Aakaar (shape) | Beech mein moti, kinare patle | Beech mein patli, kinare moti |
| Prism ki tarah | Do prisms base se base | Do prisms apex se apex |
| Doosra naam | Converging lens | Diverging lens |
| Parallel kirnein refract hone ke baad | Ek point F₂ par ikatthi hoti hain | Phail jaati hain |
| Focus ka type | Real focus | Virtual focus |
| f ka sign (thoda extra) | Positive (+) | Negative (−) |
| Uses (kuch) | Magnifying glass, loupe, camera, dada ji ke chashme | Door dekhne wale chashme, darwaze ka peephole |

### Lens aur mirror mein farak

| Baat | Lens | Mirror |
|---|---|---|
| Kya karta hai | Light ko **guzarne deta hai** (transmit) aur refract karta hai | Light ko **wapas bhejta hai** (reflect) |
| Kaun sa law | Refraction | Reflection |
| Focus kitne | **Do** (har taraf ek) | **Ek** |
| Converging kaun | Convex lens | Concave mirror |
| Diverging kaun | Concave lens | Convex mirror |

### Kaun si lens zyada "taqatwar"?

Do convex lens lo, ek ki focal length 10 cm aur doosri ki 25 cm. Dono par Sooraj ki parallel kirnein girti hain.

- 10 cm waali lens kirnein **lens ke bahut kareeb** (10 cm par) ikattha kar deti hai.
- 25 cm waali lens kirnein **door** (25 cm par) ikattha karti hai.

Pehli lens kirnein ko **zyada modti hai** (zyada tez bend karti hai). Isliye:

> **Jis lens ki focal length chhoti ho, wo kirnein ko zyada modti hai, yaani zyada taqatwar (stronger) hai.**

Is taqat ko number mein kaise batate hain (power aur dioptre), wo Topic 6 mein.

## Part F: Thoda extra (Class 10 overlap): sign convention

Mirror ki tarah lens ke numbers (f, doori) likhte waqt bhi +/− lagana padta hai. Yahan wahi **New Cartesian sign convention** hai:

1. **Optical centre O ko origin** (shunya, 0) maanenge.
2. **Incident light baayen se daayen** chalti hai, aur principal axis ko x-axis maanenge.
3. **Light ki direction mein naapi gayi doori positive (+)**, **ulti direction mein negative (−)**.

Convex lens ka F₂ light ki **same** direction mein (lens ke peeche) hota hai, isliye uska f **positive** hai. Concave lens ka virtual focus F₁ light ki **ulti** direction mein (lens ke aage) hota hai, isliye uska f **negative** hai.

| Lens | f ka sign | Focus kahan |
|---|---|---|
| Convex | Positive (+) | Real focus, lens ke doosri taraf (F₂) |
| Concave | Negative (−) | Virtual focus, lens ke usi taraf jahan se light aa rahi hai (F₁) |

**Dhyan do: mirror ka sign ulta hai.** Concave **mirror** ki f negative thi, par convex **lens** ki f **positive** hai. Concave lens ki f negative hai, aur convex mirror ki f positive thi. Isliye mirror ka sign yahan mat lagao.

## Part G: Activity: convex lens ki focal length naapna

Sooraj bahut door hai, to uski kirnein lagbhag parallel hoti hain. Is se convex lens ki focal length ka andaza lag sakta hai.

**Zaroorat:** ek convex lens (magnifying glass), ek safed kagaz ya gatta, ek meter-scale.

**Tarika:**
1. Lens ko dhoop mein pakdo, Sooraj ki taraf.
2. Lens ke peeche kagaz ko aage-peeche karo, jab tak kagaz par ek **bahut chhota, sabse chamakdaar spot** (sharp bright spot) na dikhne lage.
3. Lens aur kagaz ke beech ki doori meter-scale se naapo.

```
                                 ▲        screen
                                 │            ║
                                 │            ║
 ─────────────►──────────────────│─╲          ║
                                 │  ╲──↘      ║
                                 │      ╲─╲   ║
─────────+───────────+───────────┼─────────╲─●║──────────+────────
        C₁          F₁           │O     ╱─╱ F₂║         C₂
                                 │  ╱──↗      ║
 ─────────────►──────────────────│─╱          ║
                                 │            ║
                                 │            ║
                                 ▼
```

> **Figure 5.11** – Sooraj ki parallel kirnein convex lens se refract hokar screen (kagaz) par F₂ par ek chhote chamakdaar spot mein ikatthi hoti hain. Lens se spot tak ki doori lagbhag f hai.

**Sample reading:** lens se spot ki doori = 15 cm.
- f ≈ **15 cm**
- Dono foci ke beech ki doori = F₁F₂ = 2 × 15 cm = **30 cm**

Jaanch: F₂ lens ke peeche 15 cm par aur F₁ lens ke aage 15 cm par hai, to F₁ se F₂ tak 15 cm + 15 cm = 30 cm ✅

**Savdhani:** Sooraj ki taraf seedha mat dekho, aur spot ko kisi ki aankh ya haath par mat daalo. Spot bahut garm hota hai, kagaz jal bhi sakta hai. Isliye kagaz ko spot par zyada der mat rakho, aur kisi sukhi cheez (patte, kapde) ke paas ye activity mat karo.

**Burning glass:** isi wajah se bade convex lens ko **burning glass** kehte hain: wo Sooraj ki kirnein ek chhote spot par ikattha kar deta hai, jahan itni garmi hoti hai ki kagaz ya sukhe patte jal sakte hain. Isliye lens ko dhoop mein khali jagah par kabhi mat chhodo, aur bachchon ko kisi bade adult ke saath hi ye activity karni chahiye.

(Ye doori sirf **lagbhag** f hai, kyunki Sooraj bahut door hai par infinite door nahi.)

## Part H: Aage ki class mein, jaanne ke liye: lens alag medium mein

Ab tak lens hawa mein thi. Agar glass ki convex lens ko kisi aise medium (jaise kisi ghane tel ya liquid) mein rakhein jiska refractive index **glass se zyada** ho, to wo kirnein ikattha nahi karti, balki **phailati hai**, yaani diverging lens ki tarah behave karti hai! Aur hawa ka bubble paani ke andar convex shape ka hone par bhi phailata hai.

Yaani lens ka convex ya concave hona uski **shape** par hi nahi, balki lens ke **material aur uske charon taraf ke medium** ke refractive index par bhi depend karta hai. Is class mein hum hamesha **hawa mein** lens lenge.

## Part I: Solved examples

Is poore Part mein lens patli hai, hawa mein hai, aur kirnein axis ke kareeb hain.

### Example 1: sun-image se f

Ek student ne convex lens se Sooraj ka sabse chamakdaar spot screen par banaya. Lens aur screen ke beech ki doori 12 cm thi. f aur 2f kya hain?

- f ≈ **12 cm**
- 2f = 2 × 12 cm = **24 cm**

Jaanch: 24 cm ÷ 2 = 12 cm = f ✅

### Example 2: F aur 2F ki position

Ek convex lens ki f = 10 cm hai. Optical centre se F₁, F₂ aur dono 2F (jahan sketch mein C₁, C₂ dikhate hain) kitni door hain?

- F₂, O se **10 cm** door, lens ke doosri taraf. F₁, O se **10 cm** door, pehli taraf.
- 2F₂ (= C₂), O se 2 × 10 cm = **20 cm** door, doosri taraf. 2F₁ (= C₁), O se **20 cm** door, pehli taraf.
- F₁ se F₂ tak ki doori = 10 cm + 10 cm = **20 cm**.
- C₁ se C₂ tak = 20 cm + 20 cm = **40 cm**.

```
 C₁          F₁          O          F₂          C₂
 +───────────+───────────┼───────────+───────────+
 ◄─ 10 cm ──►◄─ 10 cm ──►◄─ 10 cm ──►◄─ 10 cm ──►
```

> **Figure 5.12** – f = 10 cm: lens ke har taraf F, O se 10 cm par, aur 2F (C) 20 cm par. (Dono taraf ek jaisi doori.)

Jaanch: 10 cm + 10 cm = 20 cm = F₁F₂ ✅. Aur C₁O = 20 cm = 2 × 10 cm ✅

### Example 3: f ka sign

Neeche di hui lens ki focal length sign convention ke saath likho:

(a) Ek convex lens, focal length 20 cm.
(b) Ek concave lens, focal length 15 cm.
(c) Ek convex lens, focal length 0.25 m.
(d) Ek concave lens, focal length 0.5 m.

- (a) Convex: f = **+20 cm**
- (b) Concave: f = **−15 cm**
- (c) f = 0.25 m = 0.25 × 100 cm = 25 cm, convex: f = **+25 cm**
- (d) f = 0.5 m = 0.5 × 100 cm = 50 cm, concave: f = **−50 cm**

Jaanch: convex ki f hamesha +, concave ki hamesha −. (c) mein 25 cm ÷ 100 = 0.25 m ✅, (d) mein 50 cm ÷ 100 = 0.5 m ✅

### Example 4: kirnein dekh kar lens ka type pehchano

(a) Ek lens par axis ke parallel kirnein girti hain, aur refract hone ke baad wo lens se 30 cm peeche ek point par milti hain. Kaunsi lens hai, aur f kya hai?

- Kirnein sach mein milti hain, point lens ke peeche hai: **convex lens**, real focus.
- f = **+30 cm**

(b) Ek doosri lens par axis ke parallel kirnein girti hain, aur refract hone ke baad wo phail jaati hain, jaise lens ke aage (jahan se light aa rahi hai) 18 cm door ek point se aa rahi hon. Kaunsi lens hai, aur f?

- Kirnein phail rahi hain, aur virtual focus usi taraf hai jahan se light aa rahi hai: **concave lens**.
- f = **−18 cm** (magnitude 18 cm)

(c) Kaun si lens kagaz ko jala sakti hai?

- Wahi jo kirnein sach mein ek point par ikattha kare: **convex lens**.

Jaanch: (a) f positive, convex ✅ (b) f negative, concave ✅

### Example 5: teen kirnein, distances ke saath

Ek convex lens ki f = 10 cm hai. Ek ray axis ke parallel hai aur axis se 3 cm upar hai.

(a) Refract hone ke baad wo kahan jaayegi?
(b) Ek doosri ray F₁ se guzar kar lens par girti hai. Wo kahan jaayegi? F₁ kahan hai?
(c) Teesri ray O se guzarti hai. Wo kahan jaayegi?

- (a) Axis ke parallel ray refract hone ke baad **F₂ se guzarti hai**. F₂, O se **10 cm** door, lens ke doosri taraf. Ray axis ko O se 10 cm aage kaatti hai.
- (b) F₁, O se **10 cm** door, lens ki pehli taraf (jahan se light aa rahi hai). F₁ se guzarti ray refract hokar **principal axis ke parallel** ho jaati hai.
- (c) O se guzarti ray **bina mude seedhi** nikal jaati hai.

Ab wahi parallel ray ek **concave lens** (f = −10 cm, yaani magnitude 10 cm) par aaye to: refract hone ke baad wo **phail jaayegi**, jaise lens ke aage 10 cm door F₁ se aa rahi ho. Ye ray axis ko kahin nahi kaategi (kirnein sach mein nahi milti). Doosri ray, jo concave lens ke doosri taraf 10 cm door F₂ ki taraf jaa rahi ho, refract hokar axis ke parallel ho jaayegi. O se guzarti ray seedhi nikal jaayegi.

Jaanch: convex ka F₂ real hai, isliye kirnein sach mein milti hain ✅ (convex), concave ka F virtual hai, isliye nahi milti ✅

### Example 6: lens alag medium mein (sirf jaanne ke liye)

Glass ki ek convex lens hawa mein 20 cm focal length ke saath kirnein ikattha karti hai. Ab use ek aise liquid mein dubo diya jaye jiska refractive index glass se **zyada** hai. Wo kya karegi?

- Hawa mein light hawa (rarer) se glass (denser) mein jaati hai, aur normal ki taraf mudti hai. Is liquid mein light liquid (denser) se glass (rarer) mein jaati hai, to wo normal se **door** mudti hai, yaani ulti taraf. Isliye convex shape ki lens kirnein ikattha nahi karti, balki **phailati** hai: wo **diverging lens** ki tarah kaam karti hai.

Hawa mein convex lens converge karti hai, par uska ye behaviour **medium par depend** karta hai. Calculation aage ki class mein.

Jaanch: sirf concept, koi number nahi. Hawa mein glass ki convex lens converging hi hoti hai ✅

### Example 7: 10 roz ki cheezein sort karo

Neeche 10 cheezein hain. Har ek ko convex lens, concave lens ya kuch aur (jaise convex ki tarah behave karne waali) mein daalo.

| Cheez | Type | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Magnifying glass | Convex | Beech mein moti, cheez bada dikhati hai |
| 2. Camera ka lens | Convex (converging lens ya lenses ka set) | Kirnein ikattha karke film ya sensor par laata hai |
| 3. Door dekhne wale (myopia) chashme ka glass | Concave | Beech mein patla, kirnein phailata hai |
| 4. Patte par paani ki boond | Convex jaisi | Upar se ubhri hui, bade dikhne wali, chhoti convex lens ki tarah |
| 5. Kaanch ki goli (marble) | Convex jaisi | Gol, bahut chhoti focal length wali convex lens ki tarah |
| 6. Paani se bhara bulb | Convex jaisi | Paani ka gola kirnein ikattha karta hai |
| 7. Darwaze ka peephole | Concave | Bahar ka bada area chhote roop mein dikhata hai |
| 8. Aankh ka lens | Convex | Beech mein moti, kirnein retina par ikattha karta hai |
| 9. Projector ka lens | Convex | Kirnein ikattha karke screen par bada image banata hai |
| 10. Ghadi-saaz (watch repairer) ka hand lens (loupe) | Convex | Chhote gears ko bada dikhata hai |

Yahan sirf **shape pehchanni** hai. In lens ke kaam ki baat Topic 6 mein.

### Example 8: foci ki ginti aur F₁F₂

(a) Ek lens ke kitne foci hote hain?
(b) Ek convex lens ki f = 8 cm hai. Dono foci ke beech ki doori kya hai? Dono foci O se kitni door hain?

- (a) **Do**: ek lens ke har taraf ek (F₁ aur F₂).
- (b) F₁ aur F₂ dono O se **8 cm** door hain (ek-ek har taraf). F₁F₂ = 8 cm + 8 cm = **16 cm**.

Jaanch: F₁F₂ = 2f = 2 × 8 cm = 16 cm ✅

### Example 9: kaun si lens zyada taqatwar

Teen convex lens hain: A (f = 10 cm), B (f = 25 cm), C (f = 40 cm). Kaun sab se zyada kirnein modti hai? Kaun sab se kam? Inhein zyada se kam taqat ke order mein likho.

- Jiski focal length sab se chhoti, wo sab se taqatwar: A (10 cm).
- Order: **A (10 cm) → B (25 cm) → C (40 cm)** (zyada se kam).
- A aur C ki focal length mein fark = 40 cm − 10 cm = **30 cm**.

Jaanch: 10 cm < 25 cm < 40 cm ✅. 40 cm − 10 cm = 30 cm ✅

### Example 10: ek spot, do jawab

Ek student ne magnifying glass se Sooraj ka spot paper par banaya. Spot sabse chhota tab tha jab paper lens se 0.15 m door tha. (a) f cm mein kitni hai? (b) Dono foci ke beech ki doori?

- (a) f = 0.15 m = 0.15 × 100 cm = **15 cm**
- (b) F₁F₂ = 2 × 15 cm = **30 cm**

Jaanch: 15 cm ÷ 100 = 0.15 m ✅, 15 cm + 15 cm = 30 cm ✅

## Part J: Yuvaan ka sawal, poora jawab

Mr. Pinto ne loupe wapas uthaya aur Yuvaan ko samjhaya.

"Dekho beta, mere loupe aur dada ji ke chashme ke glass **convex lens** hain. Dost ke door ke chashme ka glass **concave lens** hai. Dono ki **shape alag** hai, isliye kirnein alag tarah se refract hoti hain. Convex lens kirnein ikattha karti hai (converge), concave unhein phailati hai (diverge). Isi se image bhi alag banti hai: convex se cheez (jaise ghadi ke gears) bade dikhte hain, concave se hamesha chhote."

Yuvaan ne poochha, "Par kya convex lens hamesha cheez ko bada hi dikhata hai? Aur kitna bada?"

Mr. Pinto hanse, "Ye agle topic ka mazedaar hissa hai. Convex lens bada hamesha nahi dikhata: cheez lens se kitni door hai, ye ginti mein aata hai. Bahut kareeb (f ke andar) rakho to bada aur seedha, thoda door (f aur 2f ke beech) rakho to bada par ulta, aur 2f se bhi door rakho to chhota aur ulta. Concave lens mein ye jhanjhat nahi: wo hamesha chhota hi dikhata hai. Aur aankh ki baat sab se baad mein, jab hum aankh ke andar ke lens ko samjhenge."

Yuvaan ne loupe ko ghadi ke kareeb laaya, phir door. Gears bade se chhote hote dekh kar wo muskuraya.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**1. Sochna ki lens ka ek hi focus hota hai.**
- Lens ke **do** foci hote hain, har taraf ek (F₁ aur F₂), aur patli lens mein dono O se barabar doori par hote hain. Example: f = 8 cm to F₁F₂ = 16 cm, 8 cm nahi.

**2. Optical centre aur focus ko ek samajhna.**
- O lens ka **beech ka point** hai (lens par), F lens se f doori par axis ka point hai. O se ray seedhi nikal jaati hai, F par kirnein milti hain.

**3. Sochna ki convex lens hamesha cheez ko bada dikhati hai.**
- Ye cheez ki lens se doori par depend karta hai (Topic 6). Loupe ke kareeb rakhi cheez hi bada dikhti hai, door ka nazara ulta aur chhota bhi ho sakta hai.

**4. Mirror ka sign lens par lagana.**
- Concave **mirror** ki f negative thi, par ye yaad rakhke convex **lens** ki f bhi negative likh dena galat hai: **convex lens ki f positive** hai. Concave lens ki f negative hai. Mirror ka sign lens par mat lagao.

**5. Convex lens ko concave mirror samajhna.**
- Dono kirnein ikattha karte hain, par lens light ko **guzarne deta hai** (refraction), mirror use **wapas bhejta hai** (reflection). Lens ke dono taraf focus hain, mirror ka ek hi.

**6. Sochna ki O se guzarti ray bhi modti hai.**
- Patli lens mein O se guzarti ray **bina mude** seedhi nikalti hai, kyunki O ke paas lens ki dono surfaces lagbhag samanantar hoti hain.

**7. Real aur virtual focus ko ulta bolna.**
- Convex lens ka focus **real** (kirnein sach mein milti hain), concave lens ka **virtual** (sirf lagti hain).

## Quick Recap

- **Lens** = transparent tukda jo do surfaces se ghira ho, jisme kam se kam ek spherical ho.
- **Convex** (converging): beech mein moti. **Concave** (diverging): beech mein patli.
- Lens ko chhote prisms ka set samjho: convex = do prisms base se base, concave = do prisms apex se apex.
- **O** = optical centre. **Principal axis** = lens ke centres of curvature aur O se guzarti line. **C₁, C₂** = centres of curvature. **F₁, F₂** = do principal foci (O se barabar doori par). **f = OF.** **Aperture** = lens ki chaudai.
- Convex lens ka focus **real**, concave lens ka **virtual**.
- **Teen khaas kirnein:** (1) axis ke parallel: F₂ se guzarti (convex) ya F₁ se aati lagti (concave). (2) F₁ se guzarti (convex) ya F₂ ki taraf jaati (concave): axis ke parallel. (3) O se guzarti: seedhi nikal jaati hai.
- **Sign convention (thoda extra, Class 10 overlap):** convex lens ki f positive, concave lens ki f negative (mirror se ulta).
- **Sun-image activity:** lens se sharp bright spot tak ki doori ≈ f. Spot garm hota hai: savdhani rakho (burning glass).
- Chhoti focal length wali lens zyada taqatwar (zyada tez modti) hai (power Topic 6 mein).
- Lens kirnein guzarne deta hai, mirror wapas bhejta hai.
- Medium badalne par lens ka behaviour badal sakta hai (aage ki class mein).
