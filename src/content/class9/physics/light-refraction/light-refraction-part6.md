# Topic 6: Lens se Image Banna, Lens Formula aur Power

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki lens do tarah ke hote hain: **convex** (beech se mote, light ko ikattha karne wale) aur **concave** (beech se patle, light ko bikherne wale). Humne unke parts pehchane (optical centre O, principal axis, do foci F₁ aur F₂, focal length f) aur **teen special rays** seekhe: axis ke parallel ray (convex mein F₂ se guzarti hai), F₁ se guzarne wali ray (refraction ke baad axis ke parallel) aur O se guzarne wali ray (bina mude seedhi nikal jaati hai). Ant mein ek sawal bacha tha: object ko lens ke kareeb ya door rakhne par image kaisi banti hai? Is topic mein hum ray diagram khud banayenge, har position ki image dekhenge, aur ant mein image ko **number** mein bhi nikalenge: lens formula se, aur lens ki "taakat" (power) se bhi.

## School ke AV room ka projector

Ek din school ke AV room mein Mr. Dastur ne projector chalaya. Anushka sabse aage baithi thi. Projector ke andar ek **chhoti si slide** lagi thi: Taj Mahal ki photo, sirf ek chhote se dibbe jitni badi. Phir bhi saamne ki badi safed screen par wahi photo **bahut badi** dikh rahi thi, poori deewar jitni.

Magar ek gadbad thi. Pehle Mr. Dastur ne slide holder mein seedhi rakhi to Taj Mahal screen par **ulta** dikha: gumbad neeche aur neev upar! Mr. Dastur ne haste hue slide nikali, **ulti karke** holder mein lagayi, aur ab screen par Taj Mahal bilkul seedha dikhne laga.

Phir bhi picture dhundhli (blurry) thi. Mr. Dastur ne projector ke lens ko bas **thoda sa** aage-peeche sarkaya, aur photo ek dum saaf (sharp) ho gayi.

Anushka ne haath utha kar poocha, "Sir, itni chhoti slide itni badi picture kaise ban gayi? Aur slide ulti kyun lagani padi? Aur lens ko sirf zara sa hilane se picture saaf kaise ho gayi?"

Mr. Dastur muskuraye. "Ye teeno sawal ek hi cheez se jude hain: **convex lens ki image kahan banti hai, ye object ki jagah par tikha hai**. Pehle ye seekhte hain ki image banti kaise hai, phir hum projector ka raaz kholenge." Is kahani ko hum topic ke beech mein solve karenge.

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |

## Part A: Ray diagram se image kaise banti hai

Image banane ka tareeka bilkul wahi hai jo mirror mein tha ([light-reflection-part5.md](../light-reflection/light-reflection-part5.md) mein poori baat hai). Bas rays ab **lens ke aar-paar** jaati hain. Teen special rays [light-refraction-part5.md](light-refraction-part5.md) se yaad rakho:

1. Axis ke **parallel** ray, lens se guzarne ke baad **F₂ se guzarti hai** (convex lens mein).
2. **F₁ se guzarne wali** ray, lens ke baad axis ke **parallel** ho jaati hai.
3. **O se guzarne wali** ray **seedhi nikal jaati hai** (bina mude).

Object ko ek seedhi arrow maano jiska foot axis par hai. Object ke **top** se in teeno mein se **koi do** rays chuno, aur dekho ki lens ke baad wo kahan milti hain. Jahan **sach mein milti hain**, wahan top ki **real image** banti hai. Jahan sirf **peeche badhane par** (dotted line) milti dikhti hain, wahan **virtual image** banti hai. Image ka foot axis par hi hota hai, to top se axis par perpendicular girao: wahi poori image hai.

**Sketch kaise padhein:** object left mein hai, light left se right ko jaati hai. Convex lens ko "()" se dikhaya hai (concave ko ")(" se). Axis "-" se, aur lagbhag 1 column ≈ 1 cm hai. Heights ko saaf dikhane ke liye badha kar banaya hai, isliye sketches sirf **idea** dete hain, naap ke liye nahi. Hum convex lens ke liye **f = 10 cm** maanenge, to **F₁ lens ke left mein 10 cm par**, **F₂ right mein 10 cm par**, **2F₁ left mein 20 cm par** aur **2F₂ right mein 20 cm par** hain. Solid lines asli rays hain, dotted lines peeche badhai gayi (virtual) rays.

## Part E: Thoda extra (Class 10 overlap): Magnification (m)

Image object se kitni badi ya chhoti hai, ye ek number se batate hain:

> **Magnification: m = (image ki unchai) ÷ (object ki unchai) = h′ ÷ h**
> Yahan h object ki unchai hai aur h′ image ki unchai hai (dono cm mein ya dono m mein). m ka koi unit nahi hota (cm ÷ cm).

**Sign ka niyam (simple):** principal axis ke **upar** ki unchai **positive**, **neeche** ki unchai **negative**. Object axis ke upar hai, isliye h hamesha **positive** hai.

- **Real, inverted image** axis ke neeche banti hai, isliye h′ **negative**, aur **m negative**.
- **Virtual, erect image** axis ke upar banti hai, isliye h′ **positive**, aur **m positive**.

| m ki value | Matlab |
|---|---|
| Sign chhod kar m ka maan 1 se bada (jaise 2 ya −2) | Image badi (enlarged) |
| Sign chhod kar m ka maan 1 ke barabar (1 ya −1) | Image barabar size |
| Sign chhod kar m ka maan 1 se chhota (jaise 0.5 ya −0.5) | Image chhoti (diminished) |
| m negative | Image ulti (inverted), real |
| m positive | Image seedhi (erect), virtual |

Dhyan do: **negative sign ka size se koi lena-dena nahi hai.** m = −2 ka matlab "ulti aur 2 guna badi" hai, "chhoti" nahi.

### Example 2: Heights se magnification

Ek convex lens 1.5 cm unchi object ki real, ulti image banata hai jiski unchai 3 cm hai. Magnification nikalo.

Object ki unchai h = +1.5 cm. Image ulti hai (axis ke neeche), isliye h′ = −3 cm.
m = h′ ÷ h = (−3 cm) ÷ (+1.5 cm) = **−2**

Matlab image **ulti** (real) aur object se **2 guna badi** hai.

Jaanch: m ka maan 2 > 1, to image badi ✅, aur h′ = m × h = −2 × 1.5 cm = −3 cm ✅

## Part F: Thoda extra (Class 10 overlap): Lens formula

Ray diagram se image ki jagah **lagbhag** pata chalti hai. **Exact** jagah chahiye to formula kaam aata hai. Ye Class 10 ka hissa hai, isliye hum ise **natija** ki tarah lenge (derivation nahi) aur istemal sikhenge.

### Sign convention (simple)

Optical centre O ko 0 maano. Light ki disha (left se right) ko **positive** maano. To:

- Light jis taraf se aa rahi hai (object wali taraf), wahan naapi gayi doori **negative**.
- Lens ki doosri taraf naapi gayi doori **positive**.
- Principal axis ke **upar** ki unchai positive, **neeche** ki negative.

| Cheez | Sign | Wajah |
|---|---|---|
| Real object ki doori u | **Negative** | Object light wali (left) taraf hai |
| Real image ki doori v | **Positive** | Real image lens ki doosri taraf banti hai |
| Virtual image ki doori v | **Negative** | Virtual image object ki hi taraf banti hai |
| Convex lens ka f | **Positive** | F₂ doosri taraf hai |
| Concave lens ka f | **Negative** | Virtual focus F₁ object wali taraf hai |

### Lens formula aur magnification

> **1/v − 1/u = 1/f**   aur   **m = h′ ÷ h = v ÷ u**

Ye dono formulas **convex aur concave dono** lenses ke liye chalte hain, bas sign sahi lagao. Steps:

1. u, f (aur agar diya ho to v) ko **apne sign ke saath** likho (cm ya m).
2. **1/v = 1/f + 1/u** se 1/v nikalo, phir v.
3. **m = v ÷ u** se magnification nikalo.
4. v ka sign dekho: **positive** = real, lens ki doosri taraf; **negative** = virtual, object ki taraf. m ka sign dekho: negative = inverted, positive = erect.

**Dhyan se:** lens formula **1/v − 1/u = 1/f** hai aur **m = v ÷ u** (koi minus nahi). Mirror mein formula **1/v + 1/u = 1/f** aur **m = −v ÷ u** tha. Dono ko mat milana.

### Formula ki Jaanch: Part B ke sketches se milaao (f = +10 cm, convex)

| u (cm) | 1/v = 1/f + 1/u (1/cm) | v (cm) | m = v ÷ u | Matlab |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| −30 | 1/10 − 1/30 = 2/30 = 1/15 | +15 | −0.5 | F₂ aur 2F₂ ke beech, chhoti, real, inverted (Position 2) |
| −20 | 1/10 − 1/20 = 1/20 | +20 | −1 | 2F₂ par, barabar, real, inverted (Position 3) |
| −15 | 1/10 − 1/15 = 1/30 | +30 | −2 | 2F₂ ke peeche, badi, real, inverted (Position 4) |
| −10 | 1/10 − 1/10 = 0 | koi v nahi (infinity) | m nahi nikalta | Rays parallel, image infinity par (Position 5) |
| −5 | 1/10 − 1/5 = −1/10 | −10 | +2 | Object ki taraf, badi, virtual, erect (Position 6) |

Bahut door ke object (u bahut bada negative) ke liye 1/u lagbhag 0 hota hai, to 1/v ≈ 1/f aur v ≈ f = +10 cm: image F₂ par (Position 1). Ray diagram aur formula **ek hi kahani** sunate hain.

### Example 3: Convex lens, object 2F₁ ke peeche

Ek convex lens ki focal length 12 cm hai. 6 cm unchi object lens se 48 cm door hai. Image ki jagah, nature aur size batao.

Sign ke saath: f = +12 cm, u = −48 cm, h = +6 cm.
1/v = 1/f + 1/u = 1/(+12 cm) + 1/(−48 cm) = 4/48 cm⁻¹ − 1/48 cm⁻¹ = 3/48 cm⁻¹ = 1/16 cm⁻¹
v = **+16 cm**

v positive hai, isliye image lens ki doosri taraf **16 cm** par **real** hai (F₂ = 12 cm aur 2F₂ = 24 cm ke beech).
m = v ÷ u = (+16 cm) ÷ (−48 cm) = **−1/3 ≈ −0.33**
h′ = m × h = −1/3 × 6 cm = **−2 cm** (ulti, 2 cm ki).

Jaanch: 1/v − 1/u = 1/(16 cm) − 1/(−48 cm) = 3/48 cm⁻¹ + 1/48 cm⁻¹ = 4/48 cm⁻¹ = 1/12 cm⁻¹ = 1/f ✅

### Example 4: Convex lens, object 2F₁ par

f = +10 cm wale convex lens ke saamne 20 cm par object hai. Image kahan banegi?

Sign ke saath: f = +10 cm, u = −20 cm.
1/v = 1/f + 1/u = 1/(10 cm) − 1/(20 cm) = 2/20 cm⁻¹ − 1/20 cm⁻¹ = 1/20 cm⁻¹
v = **+20 cm**
m = v ÷ u = (+20 cm) ÷ (−20 cm) = **−1**

Image lens ke doosri taraf 20 cm par (2F₂ par), **real, ulti aur object ke barabar**.

Jaanch: 1/v − 1/u = 1/(20 cm) + 1/(20 cm) = 2/20 cm⁻¹ = 1/10 cm⁻¹ = 1/f ✅

### Example 5: Convex lens, object F₁ aur 2F₁ ke beech (projector wali position)

f = +10 cm wale convex lens se 15 cm par ek 2 cm unchi slide hai. Image batao.

Sign ke saath: f = +10 cm, u = −15 cm, h = +2 cm.
1/v = 1/f + 1/u = 1/(10 cm) − 1/(15 cm) = 3/30 cm⁻¹ − 2/30 cm⁻¹ = 1/30 cm⁻¹
v = **+30 cm**
m = v ÷ u = (+30 cm) ÷ (−15 cm) = **−2**
h′ = m × h = −2 × 2 cm = **−4 cm**

Image lens ke doosri taraf 30 cm par (2F₂ = 20 cm ke peeche), **real, ulti aur 2 guna badi** (4 cm). Yahi projector hai.

Jaanch: 1/v − 1/u = 1/(30 cm) + 1/(15 cm) = 1/30 cm⁻¹ + 2/30 cm⁻¹ = 3/30 cm⁻¹ = 1/10 cm⁻¹ = 1/f ✅

### Example 6: Convex lens, object F₁ ke andar (magnifying glass)

f = +15 cm wale convex lens se 10 cm par ek 2 cm unchi object hai. Image batao.

Sign ke saath: f = +15 cm, u = −10 cm, h = +2 cm.
1/v = 1/f + 1/u = 1/(15 cm) − 1/(10 cm) = 2/30 cm⁻¹ − 3/30 cm⁻¹ = −1/30 cm⁻¹
v = **−30 cm**
m = v ÷ u = (−30 cm) ÷ (−10 cm) = **+3**
h′ = m × h = 3 × 2 cm = **+6 cm**

v negative hai: image **object ki hi taraf, lens se 30 cm par**, **virtual**. m = +3: **seedhi aur 3 guna badi**. Yahi magnifying glass hai.

Jaanch: 1/v − 1/u = 1/(−30 cm) − 1/(−10 cm) = −1/30 cm⁻¹ + 3/30 cm⁻¹ = 2/30 cm⁻¹ = 1/15 cm⁻¹ = 1/f ✅

### Example 7: Concave lens

Ek concave lens ki focal length 10 cm hai. 6 cm unchi object lens se 20 cm door hai. Image batao.

Concave lens ke liye f negative: f = −10 cm, u = −20 cm, h = +6 cm.
1/v = 1/f + 1/u = 1/(−10 cm) + 1/(−20 cm) = −2/20 cm⁻¹ − 1/20 cm⁻¹ = −3/20 cm⁻¹
v = −20/3 cm = **−6.67 cm**
m = v ÷ u = (−6.67 cm) ÷ (−20 cm) = **+0.333** (= 1/3)
h′ = m × h = 0.333 × 6 cm = **+2 cm**

v negative: image object ki hi taraf, lens se 6.67 cm par (O aur F₁ = 10 cm ke beech), **virtual, erect aur ek-tihayi** (2 cm). Part D ne yahi kaha tha.

Jaanch: 1/v − 1/u = 1/(−6.67 cm) − 1/(−20 cm) = −0.15 cm⁻¹ + 0.05 cm⁻¹ = −0.10 cm⁻¹ = 1/(−10 cm) = 1/f ✅

### Example 8: Experiment se focal length

Ek student ne convex lens ke saamne 25 cm par jalti mombatti rakhi aur screen ko lens se 50 cm par le jaakar saaf image paayi. Focal length batao.

Image screen par aayi, to real hai: v = +50 cm. Object saamne: u = −25 cm.
1/f = 1/v − 1/u = 1/(+50 cm) − 1/(−25 cm) = 1/50 cm⁻¹ + 2/50 cm⁻¹ = 3/50 cm⁻¹
f = 50/3 cm = **+16.67 cm**
m = v ÷ u = (+50 cm) ÷ (−25 cm) = **−2** (ulti, 2 guna badi).

Jaanch: F₁ = 16.67 cm aur 2F₁ = 33.3 cm hain. Object 25 cm par F₁ aur 2F₁ ke beech hai, to image 2F₂ (33.3 cm) ke peeche (50 cm par), badi aur real honi chahiye: Position 4 ✅. Aur 1/(50 cm) − 1/(−25 cm) = 0.02 cm⁻¹ + 0.04 cm⁻¹ = 0.06 cm⁻¹ = 1/(16.67 cm) ✅

### Example 9: Object 40 cm par, f = 15 cm

Ek convex lens ki focal length 15 cm hai. Object 40 cm par hai. Image batao.

Sign ke saath: f = +15 cm, u = −40 cm.
1/v = 1/f + 1/u = 1/(15 cm) − 1/(40 cm) = 8/120 cm⁻¹ − 3/120 cm⁻¹ = 5/120 cm⁻¹ = 1/24 cm⁻¹
v = **+24 cm**
m = v ÷ u = (+24 cm) ÷ (−40 cm) = **−0.6**

Image lens ke doosri taraf 24 cm par, **real, ulti aur chhoti** (0.6 guna). Object 2F₁ = 30 cm ke peeche tha, to image F₂ = 15 cm aur 2F₂ = 30 cm ke beech (24 cm par) honi chahiye ✅

Jaanch: 1/v − 1/u = 1/(24 cm) + 1/(40 cm) = 5/120 cm⁻¹ + 3/120 cm⁻¹ = 8/120 cm⁻¹ = 1/15 cm⁻¹ = 1/f ✅

### Example 10: Barabar size ki real image: object kahan ho?

Ek convex lens ki focal length 12 cm hai. Object kahan rakhein ki image real aur object ke barabar size ki bane?

Barabar size ki real image ka matlab m = −1. Formula se m = v ÷ u = −1, to v = −u.
Isko lens formula mein daalo: 1/v − 1/u = 1/(−u) − 1/u = −2/u = 1/f, to **u = −2f**.
f = +12 cm to u = −2 × 12 cm = **−24 cm**

Object lens se 24 cm par (yaani 2F₁ par). Tab v = −u = **+24 cm**.

Jaanch: 1/v − 1/u = 1/(24 cm) + 1/(24 cm) = 2/24 cm⁻¹ = 1/12 cm⁻¹ = 1/f ✅. m = (+24 cm) ÷ (−24 cm) = −1 ✅. Ye Position 3 ka formula wala roop hai: **object 2F₁ par to image 2F₂ par**.

### Example 11: Projector ki kahani, numbers mein

Mr. Dastur ke projector ke lens ki focal length 10 cm hai. Screen lens se 3 m (300 cm) door hai. Slide 3 cm unchi hai. Slide ko lens se kitni doori par rakhna padega, aur screen par picture kitni badi hogi?

Real image screen par: v = +300 cm. f = +10 cm. u nikalna hai.
1/u = 1/v − 1/f = 1/(300 cm) − 1/(10 cm) = 1/300 cm⁻¹ − 30/300 cm⁻¹ = −29/300 cm⁻¹
u = −300/29 cm = **−10.34 cm** (slide lens se 10.34 cm par)
m = v ÷ u = (+300 cm) ÷ (−300/29 cm) = **−29**
h′ = m × h = −29 × 3 cm = **−87 cm**

Screen par picture **87 cm unchi, ulti**. Slide F₁ = 10 cm ke thik bahar (10.34 cm par) hai, F₁ aur 2F₁ = 20 cm ke beech: Position 4 ✅.

**Lens hilane ka raaz:** agar slide ko lens se 10.5 cm par kar dein (sirf 0.16 cm, yaani lagbhag 1.6 mm door), to
1/v = 1/f + 1/u = 1/(10 cm) − 1/(10.5 cm) = 21/210 cm⁻¹ − 20/210 cm⁻¹ = 1/210 cm⁻¹, to v = **+210 cm**.

Matlab slide ko sirf lagbhag 1.6 mm sarkane se image 3 m se 2.1 m par aa jaati hai, aur screen (3 m) par picture **dhundhli** ho jaati hai. Isi wajah se Mr. Dastur ne lens ko zara sa hilaya aur picture ek dum saaf ho gayi.

Jaanch: 1/v − 1/u = 1/(300 cm) + 29/(300 cm) = 30/300 cm⁻¹ = 1/10 cm⁻¹ = 1/f ✅

## Part G: Thoda extra (Class 10 overlap): Lens ki Power

Kuch lens light ko bahut tezi se modte hain (f chhota), kuch kam (f bada). Is "modne ki taakat" ko **power** kehte hain:

> **P = 1 ÷ f**   (f **metre** mein)
> Power ki unit **dioptre (D)** hai. **1 D** = 1 m focal length wale lens ki power.

- **Convex lens** ka f positive, to power **positive**.
- **Concave lens** ka f negative, to power **negative**.
- f jitna chhota, power utni zyada: chhota focal length matlab tez modne wala lens.

**Dhyan se:** f hamesha **metre** mein badlo. Cm mein 1/f nikaloge to galat jawab aayega.

### Example 12: f se power

(a) f = +50 cm wale lens ki power. (b) f = −25 cm wale lens ki power. (c) f = +20 cm.

(a) f = +50 cm = +0.5 m. P = 1 ÷ f = 1 ÷ (+0.5 m) = **+2 D**
(b) f = −25 cm = −0.25 m. P = 1 ÷ (−0.25 m) = **−4 D** (concave lens)
(c) f = +20 cm = +0.2 m. P = 1 ÷ (+0.2 m) = **+5 D**

Jaanch: f = 1 ÷ P: 1 ÷ 2 = 0.5 m ✅, 1 ÷ (−4) = −0.25 m ✅, 1 ÷ 5 = 0.2 m ✅. Aur (c) ka lens (20 cm) (a) wale (50 cm) se tez modta hai, power bhi zyada (+5 D > +2 D) ✅

### Example 13: Power se f

Ek lens ki power +2.5 D hai. Uski focal length batao. Kaun sa lens hai?

f = 1 ÷ P = 1 ÷ (+2.5 D) = +0.4 m = **+40 cm**. f positive hai, to **convex lens**.

Jaanch: P = 1 ÷ f = 1 ÷ (0.4 m) = 2.5 D ✅

**Opticians ki parchi:** chashme ki parchi par likha "−2.5 D" matlab ek **concave lens** jiski power −2.5 D hai, yaani f = 1 ÷ (−2.5 D) = −0.4 m = −40 cm. "+1.5 D" matlab convex lens. Kis aankh ki takleef mein kaun sa lens lagta hai, ye hum Topic 7 mein dekhenge.

### Thoda extra: do lens ek-doosre se chipke hon to

Agar do patle lens ek-doosre se **chipka kar** rakhe jaayein, to unki powers **jud** jaati hain: **P = P₁ + P₂**.

### Example 14: Do lens chipke hue

Ek +2 D ka convex lens aur ek −0.5 D ka concave lens chipka kar rakhe hain. Milkar power aur focal length batao.

P = P₁ + P₂ = (+2 D) + (−0.5 D) = **+1.5 D**
f = 1 ÷ P = 1 ÷ (+1.5 D) = 0.667 m = **+66.7 cm**

Jaanch: P = 1 ÷ f = 1 ÷ (0.667 m) = 1.5 D ✅. Convex lens ki power zyada thi, to milkar bhi convex.

(Isse zyada, lens ke combinations aage ki class mein.)

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Lens ke liye mirror formula lagana.** ❌ Lens ka formula **1/v − 1/u = 1/f** hai, mirror ka 1/v + 1/u = 1/f. Convex lens, u = −30 cm, f = +10 cm: sahi: 1/v = 1/10 − 1/30 = 2/30, v = +15 cm. Mirror wala formula lagaoge to 1/v = 1/10 + 1/30 = 4/30 aayega, v = 7.5 cm, jo galat hai.
2. **Lens ke liye m = −v ÷ u likhna.** ❌ Lens ke liye **m = v ÷ u** hai (minus nahi). u = −30 cm, v = +15 cm: m = 15 ÷ (−30) = −0.5 ✅. Galat formula se +0.5 aata, yaani seedhi image, jo real image ke liye ho hi nahi sakta.
3. **u aur f ke sign bhool jaana.** ❌ Real object ke liye u hamesha **negative** (u = −30 cm). Convex lens ka f **positive** aur concave lens ka f **negative**. Agar u = +30 cm daaloge to v galat aayega.
4. **Sochna ki concave lens real image bana sakta hai.** ❌ Real object ke liye concave lens hamesha **virtual, erect aur chhoti** image banata hai (v negative aata hai).
5. **Image lens ki galat taraf banana.** ❌ Real image **doosri taraf** (v positive). Virtual image **object wali taraf** (v negative), dotted lines se.
6. **Power mein cm aur m milana.** ❌ P = 1/f mein f **metre** mein. f = 50 cm ke liye 1/50 = 0.02 nahi, sahi: f = 0.5 m, P = +2 D.
7. **Sochna ki lens ki image hamesha ulti hoti hai.** ❌ Convex lens ki image tab ulti (real) hoti hai jab object F₁ ke bahar ho. F₁ ke andar rakho (magnifying glass) to image **seedhi (erect) aur virtual** banti hai. Concave lens ki image to hamesha seedhi hoti hai.
8. **Negative magnification ko "chhoti image" samajhna.** ❌ m = −2 ka matlab ulti aur **2 guna badi**. Sign sirf ulti-pan batata hai.

## Quick Recap

- Image banane ke liye object ke **top** se teen special rays mein se **koi do** lo (axis ke parallel, F₁ se guzarne wali, O se guzarne wali). Jahan lens ke baad rays milen wahan real image; peeche badhane par milen to virtual image.
- **Convex lens** (f = 10 cm): object infinity par to image F₂ par (point jaisi). 2F₁ ke peeche: F₂ aur 2F₂ ke beech, chhoti. 2F₁ par: 2F₂ par, barabar. F₁ aur 2F₁ ke beech: 2F₂ ke peeche, badi (projector). F₁ par: infinity par, bahut badi. In sab mein image **real aur inverted**. F₁ aur O ke beech: object ki taraf, badi, **virtual aur erect** (magnifying glass).
- **Concave lens**: har position par image object ki taraf, O aur F₁ ke beech, chhoti, virtual, erect (infinity par F₁ par). Real image kabhi nahi.
- Real image screen par aa sakti hai, virtual nahi.
- Projector mein slide F₁ aur 2F₁ ke beech hoti hai, image badi, real aur ulti: isliye slide ulti lagate hain.
- **Thoda extra:** m = h′ ÷ h = v ÷ u (m negative = real, inverted; m positive = virtual, erect). **1/v − 1/u = 1/f**. u (real object) negative; v positive = real (doosri taraf), v negative = virtual (object ki taraf); f positive convex, negative concave.
- **Power P = 1/f** (f metre mein), unit dioptre (D). Convex +, concave −. Chipke hue lenses ki power jud jaati hai: P = P₁ + P₂.
- Lens ke upyog: magnifying glass, camera, projector, searchlight, chashma, aankh; microscope aur telescope aage ki class mein.

Ab tum kisi bhi convex ya concave lens ke liye ray diagram bana sakte ho, image ki position, size aur nature pehchan sakte ho, aur lens formula se exact number bhi nikaal sakte ho. Ek baat hamesha yaad rakhna: lens ka formula **1/v − 1/u = 1/f** hai aur real object ke liye u negative hota hai. Anushka ab jaanti hai ki projector ki badi, seedhi picture ke peeche ek convex lens, ek ulti slide aur thodi si doori ka khel hai.

**Aage kya?** Ab ek **teen-kone ka glass prism** aur hamari apni **aankh**, jo khud ek lens hai. Topic 7 mein dekhenge ki prism safed light ko rangon mein kaise todta hai, aur aankh ke andar ka lens kaise kaam karta hai.
