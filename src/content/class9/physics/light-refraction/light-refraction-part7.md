## Part A: Glass prism

### Prism kya hai?

Topic 3 mein humne rectangular glass slab dekha tha, jiske aamne-saamne ke sateh parallel the. **Prism** wo glass ka transparent solid hai jiske **do aadhaar teen-kone** hain aur **teen aayatakar sateh** (faces) hain. Jab hum prism ko side se dekhte hain to wo ek triangle jaisa dikhta hai. Jin do faces se roshni andar jaati aur bahar aati hai, unke beech ka kon **prism ka angle (A)** kehlata hai. Aam prism mein A = 60° hota hai.

### Prism mein ray ka safar

Prism ki khaas baat ye hai ki uske do faces **parallel nahi, jhuke hue (inclined)** hain. Is wajah se ray do baar mudti hai, aur dono baar **base ki taraf**:

1. Pehle face par hawa se glass mein jaate hue: ray **normal ki taraf** mudti hai (rarer se denser), isliye ray base ki taraf jhuk jaati hai.
2. Doosre face par glass se hawa mein nikalte hue: ray **normal se door** mudti hai (denser se rarer). Face jhuka hua hai, isliye ye bending bhi **base ki taraf** hi hoti hai.

```
                        A
                       / \
                      /   \
                     /     \
                    /       \
  ─────────────────●──────   \
                  /       ────●─
                 /             \────
                /               \   ────────>
                _________________
                      BASE
```

> **Figure 7.1** – Prism se ray ka guzarna (schematic): incident ray pehle face par aati hai (incidence ka angle i), glass mein r₁ ke saath mudti hai, doosre face par r₂ angle banati hai aur e ke angle se bahar nikalti hai. Dono baar ray BASE ki taraf mudti hai, isliye emergent ray apni shuruati raah se neeche (base ki taraf) jhuk jaati hai. Incident ray aur emergent ray ke beech ka kon **angle of deviation δ** hai.

**Angle of deviation (δ)** = incident ray aur emergent ray ke beech ka kon. Ray hamesha **base ki taraf** mudti hai, kabhi apex (upar wala kon) ki taraf nahi.

### Prism aur slab mein fark

| Baat | Glass slab (Topic 3) | Prism |
|---|---|---|
| Aamne-saamne ke faces | Parallel | Jhuke hue (inclined) |
| Emergent ray | Incident ray ke parallel (bas thodi side mein khisak jaati hai: lateral shift) | Incident ray ke parallel nahi: base ki taraf mudi hui |
| Total mudna | Koi nahi (deviation zero) | Deviation δ hota hai |

Prism ka koi seedha formula hum yahan nahi seekhenge (wo aage ki class mein aayega). Bas ek chhota sa result jaan lo, **thoda extra** ke taur par: jab incidence angle i aur emergent angle e ho aur prism ka angle A ho, to

**δ = i + e − A**

### Example 1: Deviation nikalna (thoda extra)

Ek prism ka angle A = 60° hai. Ray i = 45° par aati hai aur e = 45° par nikalti hai. Deviation?

δ = i + e − A = 45° + 45° − 60° = 90° − 60° = **30°**

Jaanch: i + e = 90°, aur A = 60° ghatane par 30° bachta hai. Yaani ray apni raah se 30° base ki taraf mud gayi ✅

## Part B: Dispersion, yaani safed roshni ke andar ke rang

### Newton ka experiment

Sir Isaac Newton ne ek andhere kamre ke parde mein ek chhota chhed kiya, taaki dhoop ki patli kiran andar aaye. Us kiran ke raaste mein unhone ek glass prism rakha. Prism ke doosri taraf screen par unhein ek **rangeen patti** dikhi: ek chhote safed dhabbe ki jagah ek lambi patti. Is patti ko **spectrum** kehte hain, aur safed roshni ke rangon mein bant jaane ko **dispersion** kehte hain.

```
                    /\                             SCREEN
                   /  \                            ┌─────────┐
  safed           /    \───────────────────────>   │ laal    │  <- sabse kam mudi
  dhoop  ──────>●       \──────────────────────>   │ narangi │
                /        \─────────────────────>   │ peela   │
               /          \────────────────────>   │ hara    │
              /____________\───────────────────>   │ neela   │
                                                   │ jamuni  │
                                                   │ baingani│  <- sabse zyada mudi
                                                   └─────────┘
```

> **Figure 7.2** – Newton ka experiment: safed kiran prism se guzar kar saat rangon mein bant jaati hai. Laal rang sabse kam mudta hai, baingani (violet) sabse zyada. Screen par ye rang VIBGYOR ke kram mein dikhte hain.

Rangon ka kram yaad rakhne ka trick: **VIBGYOR**

**V**iolet (baingani), **I**ndigo (jamuni), **B**lue (neela), **G**reen (hara), **Y**ellow (peela), **O**range (narangi), **R**ed (laal).

### Rang alag alag kyun mudte hain?

Topic 2 mein humne dekha tha ki refractive index n = c ÷ v hota hai, yaani glass mein roshni ki speed jitni kam, n utna zyada. Ek ajeeb baat: **vacuum mein sabhi rangon ki speed ek hi hoti hai** (c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s), par **glass mein alag alag rangon ki speed thodi alag hoti hai**. Violet roshni glass mein laal se **zyada dheemi** chalti hai. Isliye ek hi glass ka refractive index violet ke liye laal se **thoda zyada** hota hai.

Jis rang ke liye n zyada, wo normal ki taraf zyada mudta hai (Topic 1), aur prism mein zyada deviate hota hai. Isliye **violet sabse zyada** aur **red sabse kam** mudta hai. (Ye kyun hota hai, iska poora kaaran aage ki class mein.)

### Example 2: Laal aur baingani ke angles

Ek glass ke liye refractive index laal roshni ke liye n_red = 1.514 aur baingani ke liye n_violet = 1.532 hai. Dono rang prism ke pehle face par i = 45° par girte hain. Glass ke andar refraction angle r kitna hoga? (sin 45° = 0.7071)

Snell ka niyam (Topic 1): sin i ÷ sin r = n, to sin r = sin i ÷ n.

Laal: sin r = 0.7071 ÷ 1.514 = 0.4670, to r_red = **27.8°**
Baingani: sin r = 0.7071 ÷ 1.532 = 0.4616, to r_violet = **27.5°**

Jaanch: sin 27.8° ≈ 0.4665 aur 0.4665 × 1.514 ≈ 0.706 ≈ sin 45° ✅. sin 27.5° ≈ 0.4617 aur 0.4617 × 1.532 ≈ 0.707 ✅

Matlab: r_violet < r_red. Baingani ki ray normal ki taraf **zyada** mudi (r chhota), isliye wo prism ke andar laal se alag raaste par chali. Dono ke beech ka farak sirf 27.8° − 27.5° = **0.3°** ka lagta hai, par prism ke do faces par aur bahar jaakar ye farak bada ho jaata hai aur screen par poori rangeen patti ban jaati hai.

### Rang wapas safed: Newton ka doosra experiment

Kya prism rang paida karta hai ya sirf alag karta hai? Newton ne ek **doosra, ulta prism** pehle ke saamne rakha. Dekha ki alag hue saat rang phir ek hokar **safed roshni** ban gaye.

```
   safed      prism 1         prism 2 (ulta)       safed
   roshni      /\             ______                roshni
   ─────>●    /  \  ═══ rang ═══\    /  ═════════════>
              /____\               \  /
                                    \/
```

> **Figure 7.3** – Pehla prism safed roshni ko rangon mein todta hai, doosra (ulta rakha hua) prism un rangon ko wapas milakar safed roshni bana deta hai. Isse saabit hota hai ki prism rang paida nahi karta, wo safed roshni ke andar ke rangon ko alag karta hai.

Isse Newton ne nateeja nikala: **safed roshni saat rangon ka mel hai.**

### Example 3: Rang aur unka kram (quiz table)

| Rang | Kram (V se R tak) | Mudna (deviation) | Glass mein refractive index |
|---|---|---|---|
| Violet (baingani) | 1 | Sabse zyada | Sabse zyada |
| Indigo (jamuni) | 2 | Zyada | Zyada |
| Blue (neela) | 3 | Thoda zyada | Thoda zyada |
| Green (hara) | 4 | Beech ka | Beech ka |
| Yellow (peela) | 5 | Thoda kam | Thoda kam |
| Orange (narangi) | 6 | Kam | Kam |
| Red (laal) | 7 | Sabse kam | Sabse kam |

Saaf hai: rangon ki ginti **7** hai, aur sabse zyada mudne wala **violet** hai.

### Cheezein rangeen kyun dikhti hain? (sirf naam)

Ek laal gulab laal isliye dikhta hai kyunki wo safed roshni mein se **laal rang ko reflect** karta hai aur baaki rang **absorb** kar leta hai. Hara patta hara rang reflect karta hai. Safed cheez sab rang reflect karti hai, aur kali cheez lagbhag sab absorb kar leti hai. Roshni ke **primary rang** (red, green, blue) ka zikr aage ki class mein hoga.

## Part C: Indradhanush (rainbow)

Ab Dr. Hosur ne Ruhi ko indradhanush ka raaz samjhaya. **Baarish ki har boond ek chhote prism ki tarah kaam karti hai.**

Dhoop ki ek kiran boond mein ghusti hai to teen cheezein hoti hain:

1. **Refraction aur dispersion:** hawa se paani mein jaate waqt kiran mudti hai, aur safed roshni alag alag rangon mein bant jaati hai.
2. **Internal reflection:** rang boond ke peeche wale sateh se takrakar andar hi wapas lautte hain.
3. **Refraction:** boond se bahar nikalte waqt kiran phir mudti hai, aur rang aur zyada alag ho jaate hain.

```
                                · · · · ·
     dhoop (safed)          · ·           · ·
                       1  ·                   ·
   ──────────────────────●───                  ·     1 = andar jaate hue refraction
                        ·    ──────             ·        aur dispersion
                       ·           ──────        ·
                      ·    boond         ──────   ·  2 = peeche ki sateh se
                      ·   (paani)              ───●2     internal reflection
                      ·                  ──────   ·
                       ·           ──────        ·
                      3 ·    ──────             ·    3 = bahar nikalte hue
                       ──●───                  ·         refraction
                   ────╱╱ ·                   ·
               ────  ╱      · ·           · ·
          ─────   ╱╱╱           · · · · ·
      ────       ╱
  baingani    ╱╱╱
            laal
```

> **Figure 7.4** – Baarish ki ek boond: dhoop ki kiran point 1 par boond mein jaati hai (refraction aur dispersion), point 2 par peeche ki sateh se internal reflection hota hai, aur point 3 par bahar nikalte waqt phir refraction hota hai. Rang neeche ki taraf, dekhne wale ki or, nikalte hain: laal zyada tirchha neeche jaata hai, isliye dekhne wale ko laal oonchi boondon se aata hai aur indradhanush ke **bahar** ki kinaare par dikhta hai; baingani andar. (Schematic chitra: angles aur do rangon ka farak badha-chadha kar dikhaya hai; boond ke andar rangon ka alag hona nahi dikhaya.)

### Indradhanush dekhne ki shartein

- **Sooraj aapki peeth ke peeche** hona chahiye.
- **Boondein aapke saamne** honi chahiye: baarish, fuhaar, jharne ya waterfall ki bauchhar, ya garden ki hose ka spray.
- Isliye indradhanush tab dikhta hai jab ek taraf baarish ho aur doosri taraf se dhoop nikli ho.

Indradhanush ek **arc (kamaan)** ki tarah dikhta hai, jisme **laal rang bahar ki kinaare** par aur **baingani rang andar ki kinaare** par hota hai. Har boond se aapki aankh tak sirf ek rang pahunchta hai, aur alag alag oonchai ki boondein alag alag rang bhejti hain. Laal rang ki kiran **anti-solar point** (sooraj ke theek ulte point) se lagbhag **42°** par aati hai, aur baingani lagbhag **40°** par (ye **approximate** angles hain). Isliye laal bahar aur baingani andar dikhta hai.

**Double rainbow:** kabhi kabhi ek halka aur doosra indradhanush upar dikhta hai. Is doosre indradhanush mein roshni boond ke andar **do baar** reflect hoti hai. Wo pehle se **halka** hota hai aur uske **rangon ka kram ulta** hota hai: laal andar ki taraf, baingani bahar ki taraf. Ruhi ne aasmaan mein yahi dekha tha.

## Part D: Aasmaan mein roshni ke kuch aur khel

### Taaron ka jhilmilana

Taare door hain, aur unki roshni hum tak aate waqt **hawa ki alag alag parton se refract** hoti hai. Hawa ki ye parte ek jaisi nahi rehtin: garam aur thandi hawa ka density badalta rehta hai, to refractive index bhi thoda thoda badalta hai. Isliye taare ki roshni ki raah thodi thodi hilti rehti hai, aur taara **jhilmilata** (twinkle karta) dikhta hai. Grah (planets) bahut paas hain, aur wo ek bade bindu jaise hain, isliye wo aam taur par jhilmilate nahi.

### Sooraj jaldi dikhna aur der se doobna

Atmospheric refraction ke karan sooraj asli sunrise se lagbhag **2 minute pehle** hi dikhne lagta hai aur asli sunset ke lagbhag **2 minute baad** tak dikhta rehta hai (**approximate**). Yaani din ki lambai lagbhag 4 minute badh jaati hai.

### Aasmaan neela kyun aur sunrise laal kyun?

Roshni jab hawa ke kano (molecules) se takrati hai to wo alag alag disha mein **bikhar** jaati hai. Isse **scattering** kehte hain. Chhoti **wavelength** wale rang (neela aur baingani) zyada bikhrte hain aur laal kam. Dopahar mein aasmaan ke har hisse se neela rang bikhr kar hamari aankh tak aata hai, isliye **aasmaan neela dikhta hai**. Sunrise aur sunset ke waqt sooraj ki roshni ko **lambi hawa ki parat** guzarni padti hai. Raaste mein neela rang bikhr kar nikal jaata hai, aur laal-narangi rang hum tak pahunchta hai, isliye **sooraj laal dikhta hai**. (Ye idea hai, iska formula, jise Rayleigh ka niyam kehte hain, aage ki class mein aayega.)

## Part E: Human eye

Ab Dr. Hosur ne kaha, "Ruhi, tumhari aankh ek chhote camera jaisi hai. Dekho kaise."

> **Figure 7.5** – Aankh ke hisse (schematic): saamne cornea, uske peeche aqueous humour, iris aur pupil, eye lens (ciliary muscles se juda hua), vitreous humour, aur peeche retina. Retina se optic nerve dimaag tak jaati hai; jahan nerve retina se nikalti hai wahan blind spot hai.

### Aankh ke hisse aur unka kaam

| Hissa | Kaam |
|---|---|
| **Cornea** | Saamne ka transparent parda. Roshni ki **sabse zyada bending** yahin hoti hai. |
| **Aqueous humour** | Cornea ke peeche ka paani jaisa taral. |
| **Iris** | Rangeen peshiyon ka chhalla. Aankh ka rang (kaali, bhuri) iska hota hai. |
| **Pupil** | Iris ke beech ka chhed. Tez roshni mein **chhota** aur halki roshni mein **bada** ho jaata hai. |
| **Eye lens** | Lachila **convex lens**. Cornea ke baad bachi hui thodi bending karke image ko retina par **baariki se focus** karta hai (fine-tuning). |
| **Ciliary muscles** | Eye lens ke aaspaas ki peshiyaan jo lens ki aakriti badalti hain. |
| **Vitreous humour** | Lens aur retina ke beech ka jelly jaisa taral. |
| **Retina** | Peeche ki roshni-sanvedi **screen**. Ismein **rods** (halki roshni mein dekhne wale, kala-safed) aur **cones** (tez roshni mein dekhne wale, rang pehchanne wale) hote hain. |
| **Optic nerve** | Retina ke signals ko **dimaag** tak le jaati hai. |
| **Blind spot** | Retina ka wo point jahan optic nerve nikalti hai. Yahan koi rods ya cones nahi hote, isliye yahan image dikhta nahi. |

### Aankh mein image kaise banta hai?

Roshni cornea se guzar kar (jahan sabse zyada bending hoti hai) pupil se hoti hui eye lens tak aati hai. Lens us roshni ko retina par focus karta hai. Retina par bana image **real, ulta (inverted) aur chhota (diminished)** hota hai. Retina ke rods aur cones use electric signals mein badalte hain, optic nerve use dimaag tak pahunchati hai, aur **dimaag use seedha (upright) kar deta hai**. Isliye hum cheezein seedhi dekhte hain.

### Power of accommodation

Aankh door ki aur paas ki, dono cheezein saaf kaise dekhti hai? Eye lens ki **focal length badal kar**. Is kshamata ko **power of accommodation** kehte hain.

- **Paas ki cheez dekhte waqt:** ciliary muscles **sikudti (contract)** hain, lens **mota** ho jaata hai, uski focal length **kam** ho jaati hai, aur image retina par banta hai.
- **Door ki cheez dekhte waqt:** ciliary muscles **dheeli (relax)** hoti hain, lens **patla** ho jaata hai, focal length **zyada** ho jaati hai.

Isi liye der tak bahut paas ki cheez padhne se aankh thak jaati hai: muscles lagataar sikudi rehti hain.

- **Near point:** normal aankh ke liye lagbhag **25 cm**. Isse paas ki cheez saaf nahi dikhti.
- **Far point:** normal aankh ke liye **anant (infinity)**.
- **Range of vision:** near point se far point tak ki doori. Normal aankh ke liye lagbhag 25 cm se infinity tak.

### Persistence of vision aur do aankhein

Kisi cheez ko dekhne ke baad uska image aankh mein lagbhag **1/16 s** tak tika rehta hai, chahe cheez hat jaye. Ise **persistence of vision** kehte hain. Isi wajah se cinema aur animation chalte hain: ek ke baad ek tasveerein tezi se dikhayi jaayein to hamein harkat dikhti hai.

**Do aankhein** ek hi cheez ko thode alag kon se dekhti hain, aur dimaag dono tasveeron ko milakar **teen-aayami (3D)** ehsaas, yaani gehrayi aur doori ka andaaza, deta hai.

## Part F: Aankh ke dosh aur unke ilaaj

Kabhi kabhi eye lens ya eyeball ka aakar thoda bigad jaata hai aur image **retina par theek se nahi banta**. Ye **defects of vision** kehlate hain.

### 1. Myopia (near-sightedness)

- **Kya hota hai:** door ki cheezein dhundhli, paas ki saaf.
- **Kyun:** eyeball bahut lamba hai, ya eye lens bahut mota (zyada power wala) hai. Door ki cheez ka image **retina ke saamne** ban jaata hai.
- **Ilaaj:** **concave lens** (diverging) ka chashma, jo kiranon ko thoda faila kar image ko peeche retina par le jaata hai.

### 3. Aur kuch dosh (sirf naam)

- **Presbyopia:** budhape mein ciliary muscles kamzor ho jaati hain aur lens kam lachila ho jaata hai, to accommodation ki kshamata ghat jaati hai: paas bhi dhundhla aur kabhi kabhi door bhi. Ilaaj: **bifocal lenses** (upar door ke liye, neeche paas ke liye).
- **Cataract:** eye lens **dhundhla (cloudy)** ho jaata hai. Ilaaj: **surgery**, jisme dhundhle lens ki jagah naya artificial lens lagaya jaata hai.
- **Astigmatism:** cornea ya lens ki aakriti ek jaisi gol nahi hoti, isliye ek disha ki lines saaf aur doosri disha ki dhundhli dikhti hain. Ilaaj special lens se.

## Part G: Solved examples (corrective lenses aur numerical)

*(Thoda extra: Class 10 overlap.)* Hum power ke liye P = 1 ÷ f (f metre mein) aur lens formula 1/v − 1/u = 1/f lenge. Sign convention wahi hai jo Topic 6 mein tha: concave lens ka f negative aur convex lens ka f positive hota hai.

### Example 4: Myopia ke liye lens (far point 80 cm)

Ruhi ka far point 80 cm hai. Use kaisa lens chahiye jo door ki cheez ko dekhne layak bana de?

Lens ko ek anant door ki cheez (u = ∞) ka **virtual image** 80 cm par (aankh ke far point par) banana hai.

u = ∞, v = −80 cm (virtual image, lens ke usi taraf)

1/f = 1/v − 1/u = 1/(−80 cm) − 1/∞ = −1/80 cm⁻¹ − 0 = −1/80 cm⁻¹

f = **−80 cm** = −0.80 m

P = 1 ÷ f = 1 ÷ (−0.80 m) = **−1.25 D**

Jaanch: f = 1 ÷ P = 1 ÷ (−1.25 D) = −0.8 m = −80 cm ✅. Minus sign saaf kehta hai ki lens **concave** hai.

### Example 5: Far point 2 m

Ek vyakti ka far point 2 m hai. Lens ki power?

u = ∞, v = −2 m

1/f = 1/v − 1/u = 1/(−2 m) − 0 = −0.5 m⁻¹, to f = −2 m

P = 1 ÷ (−2 m) = **−0.5 D**

Jaanch: f = 1 ÷ (−0.5 D) = −2 m ✅

Dhyan do: far point jitna paas, power utni zyada (Example 4 mein −1.25 D, yahan sirf −0.5 D).

### Example 6: Hypermetropia ke liye lens (near point 50 cm)

Ek vyakti ka near point 50 cm hai (normal 25 cm ki jagah). Use kitni power ka lens chahiye jisse wo 25 cm par rakhi kitaab padh sake?

Lens ko 25 cm par rakhi cheez ka **virtual image 50 cm par** (near point par) banana hai.

u = −25 cm, v = −50 cm

1/f = 1/v − 1/u = 1/(−50 cm) − 1/(−25 cm) = −1/50 cm⁻¹ + 1/25 cm⁻¹ = −1/50 cm⁻¹ + 2/50 cm⁻¹ = **1/50 cm⁻¹**

f = **+50 cm** = +0.5 m

P = 1 ÷ 0.5 m = **+2 D**

Jaanch (lens formula mein wapas): 1/v − 1/u = 1/(−50) − 1/(−25) = −0.02 + 0.04 = 0.02 cm⁻¹ = 1/50 cm⁻¹ ✅ aur f = +50 cm, plus sign yaani **convex lens** ✅

### Example 7: Range of vision

Ruhi ka near point 25 cm aur far point 80 cm hai. Uski range of vision?

Range = 25 cm se 80 cm tak, yaani lagbhag 80 cm − 25 cm = **55 cm** ki chaudaai.

Jaanch: 25 cm + 55 cm = 80 cm ✅. Normal aankh ki range 25 cm se infinity hoti hai, jo bahut badi hai.

### Example 8: Eye lens ki focal length aur power

Retina eye lens se lagbhag 2 cm door hoti hai. Door ki cheez ke liye eye lens ki focal length lagbhag kitni hogi aur power kitni?

Door ki cheez (u ≈ ∞) ka image retina par banta hai, to image distance v = 2 cm = 0.02 m. Isliye f ≈ 2 cm.

P = 1 ÷ f = 1 ÷ 0.02 m = **50 D** (lagbhag)

Jaanch: f = 1 ÷ 50 D = 0.02 m = 2 cm ✅

Ye ek **approximate** figure hai, aur ye pura cornea aur eye lens ki milli hui power hai (cornea zyada hissa deta hai).

### Example 9: Dispersion ka farak (laal aur baingani)

Example 2 dubara, par ab i = 30° lekar. n_red = 1.514, n_violet = 1.532. sin 30° = 0.5.

sin r_red = 0.5 ÷ 1.514 = 0.3303, to r_red = **19.3°**
sin r_violet = 0.5 ÷ 1.532 = 0.3264, to r_violet = **19.0°**

Jaanch: sin 19.3° ≈ 0.3305 aur 0.3305 × 1.514 ≈ 0.500 ✅. sin 19.0° ≈ 0.3256 aur 0.3256 × 1.532 ≈ 0.499 ≈ 0.5 ✅

Phir bhi r_violet < r_red: baingani zyada mudti hai.

### Example 10: Persistence of vision aur film

Ek film 24 frames (tasveerein) per second se chalti hai. Ek tasveer parde par kitni der rehti hai? Aur kya ye 1/16 s se kam hai?

t = 1 ÷ 24 s = **0.0417 s**

1/16 s = 0.0625 s

Jaanch: 24 × 0.0417 s = 1.0008 s ≈ 1 s ✅. Kyunki 0.0417 s < 0.0625 s, ek tasveer ka asar aankh mein agli tasveer aane tak tika rehta hai, isliye hamein alag alag tasveerein nahi, saaf harkat dikhti hai.

## Part H: Aankhon ki dekhbhaal aur aankh daan

### Aankhon ko surakshit rakhne ke tareeke

1. **Kam roshni mein na padho.** Bahut halki roshni mein aankh par zyada zor padta hai.
2. **Screen se breaks lo.** Ek aam salaah **20-20-20 rule** hai: har 20 minute baad, kisi **20 feet** (lagbhag 6 m) door ki cheez ko **20 second** tak dekho. (Ye ek **general advice** hai, koi kadi seema nahi.)
3. **Khaane mein vitamin A** wali cheezein lo: gajar, palak, papita, doodh, ande. Vitamin A ki kami se raat ko dekhne mein dikkat ho sakti hai.
4. **Regular eye check-up** karwao, khaaskar jab bhi door ya paas ka dekhna kam ho.
5. **Dhool aur tez roshni** se bachao: dhool mein chashma pehno, kabhi sooraj ko seedha na dekho, aur aankh ko gande haathon se na masalo.

### Aankh daan (donation)

Hamare desh mein bahut se log cornea kharab hone ya cataract jaise kaaranon se dekh nahi paate. Kisi ki **maut ke baad aankhein daan karna** (aankhein daan karna) un logon ko roshni de sakta hai, jinka cornea kharab ho gaya ho. Cornea ke transplant se unki dunia phir se rangeen ho sakti hai. Ye ek bahut bada punya ka kaam hai, aur iske liye ek chhota sa faisla aur parivaar ko bata dena kaafi hota hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Prism rang paida karta hai."**
Nahi. Prism ke andar ke rang safed roshni mein pehle se maujood the. Doosra ulta prism un rangon ko wapas milakar safed bana deta hai.

**Galti 2: "Laal rang sabse zyada mudta hai."**
Ulta hai: laal sabse **kam** aur baingani sabse **zyada** mudta hai.

**Galti 3: "Glass mein sab rangon ki speed barabar hoti hai."**
Vacuum mein barabar hoti hai. Glass mein baingani ki speed laal se thodi kam hoti hai, isliye uska refractive index zyada hota hai.

**Galti 4: "Indradhanush mein laal andar aur baingani bahar hoti hai."**
Pehle (primary) indradhanush mein **laal bahar** aur **baingani andar** hoti hai. Doosre (halke) indradhanush mein kram ulta hota hai.

**Galti 5: "Retina par image seedha banta hai."**
Retina par image **ulta** banta hai. Dimaag use seedha kar deta hai.

**Galti 6: "Myopia mein convex lens lagta hai."**
Nahi. Myopia mein **concave** lens (negative power), aur hypermetropia mein **convex** lens (positive power) lagta hai.

**Galti 7: "Near point 25 cm ka matlab aankh 25 cm se door kuch nahi dekh sakti."**
Near point **sabse paas** ki saaf doori hai. Normal aankh door tak (infinity tak) dekh sakti hai.

**Galti 8: "Power nikalte waqt f ko cm mein hi rakhna."**
P = 1 ÷ f mein f **metre** mein hona chahiye. f = −80 cm = −0.8 m, to P = −1.25 D (−0.0125 D nahi).

## Quick Recap

- **Prism:** do triangle aadhaar aur teen aayatakar faces. Ray do baar **base ki taraf** mudti hai. Emergent ray incident ray ke parallel nahi hoti. Deviation δ = incident aur emergent ray ke beech ka kon. (Thoda extra: δ = i + e − A.)
- **Dispersion:** safed roshni ka saat rangon mein bantna. Spectrum ka kram **VIBGYOR**. **Violet sabse zyada mudta hai, red sabse kam**, kyunki glass ka n violet ke liye zyada hai (violet glass mein zyada dheemi).
- Doosra ulta prism rangon ko milakar wapas **safed roshni** bana deta hai.
- **Rainbow:** baarish ki boond mein refraction, dispersion, internal reflection aur phir refraction. Sooraj peeth ke peeche aur boondein saamne. Laal bahar, baingani andar. Double rainbow halka aur ulte kram mein.
- **Aasmaan mein:** taare **atmospheric refraction** se jhilmilate hain. Sooraj lagbhag 2 minute pehle dikhta aur der se doobta hai. **Scattering** se aasmaan neela aur sunrise-sunset laal dikhte hain.
- **Aankh:** cornea (sabse zyada bending), iris, pupil, eye lens (convex), ciliary muscles, retina (rods aur cones), optic nerve, blind spot. Retina par image real, ulta aur chhota.
- **Accommodation:** paas ke liye lens mota (f kam), door ke liye patla (f zyada). Near point lagbhag 25 cm, far point infinity.
- **Myopia:** door dhundhla, image retina ke saamne, **concave lens**. **Hypermetropia:** paas dhundhla, image retina ke peeche, **convex lens**. Presbyopia (bifocal), cataract (surgery), astigmatism: sirf naam.
- Aankhon ki dekhbhaal: roshni, screen breaks (20-20-20), vitamin A, check-up, aankh daan.
