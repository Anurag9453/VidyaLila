# Topic 1: Refraction Kya Hai?

Pichhle chapter mein humne dekha ki light jab kisi surface par girti hai, to uska ek hissa **wapas uchhal** jaata hai, aur wo uchhalna i = r ke pakke niyam se hota hai. Par ek sawaal tab adhoora reh gaya tha. Mirror to light ko wapas bhej deta hai, lekin paani ya sheeshe ka kya? Unke andar to hum aar-paar dekh sakte hain. Jab light **hawa se paani ya sheeshe ke andar ghus jaati hai**, to us light ka kya hota hai? Kya wo seedhi chalti rehti hai? Is chapter ka pehla topic yahi puchta hai, aur jawab thoda hairaan karne wala hai: light andar jaate hi **mud jaati hai**. Is mudne ko **refraction** kehte hain.

## Swimming pool ka jhoothaa paani?

Shaam ka waqt tha. Society ke swimming pool ke **shallow end** (kam gehre kinare) par lifeguard Mr. Fabian apni kursi se nazar rakhe hue the. Pool ke kinare baithe Tanishq ne paani mein paer latka rakhe the. Wo bahut der se kuch ghoor raha tha.

Achanak wo bola, "Fabian uncle, ye pool mujhse jhooth bol raha hai!"

Mr. Fabian hans pade, "Pool aur jhooth? Kaise?"

"Dekhiye na," Tanishq ne paani ki taraf ishara kiya, "neeche ki tiles mujhe bilkul paas dikh rahi hain, lagta hai ek-do feet ki hi gehraai hai. Par kal jab main yahin utra tha to paer neeche tak pahunchne mein der lagi thi, gehraai kaafi zyada thi. Aur wo dekhiye, mere paer! Paani ke andar wo chhote dikh rahe hain, aur jahan paani ki sateh hai wahan se aise **tede** lag rahe hain jaise kisi ne tod diye hon. Mere paer to tode nahi hain. To kya pool mujhe bewakoof bana raha hai?"

Mr. Fabian muskuraye, "Pool jhooth nahi bol raha, Tanishq. **Light** tumhe ek chaal dikha rahi hai. Jab paani ke neeche se aati light hawa mein nikalti hai to wo apna raasta **mod** leti hai, aur tumhari aankh ko lagta hai ki cheez kahin aur rakhi hai. Isko hi refraction kehte hain. Chalo, pehle ye samajhte hain ki light mud kyun jaati hai, phir hum tumhare pool ka raaz khol denge." Is kahani ko hum topic ke ant mein solve karenge.

## Part A: Refraction kya hai, aur light mud kyun jaati hai

### Light ki raftaar sab jagah ek jaisi nahi hoti

Light ki raftaar sabse zyada **hawa (aur khali space)** mein hoti hai. Jab wahi light paani mein jaati hai to wo **dheemi** ho jaati hai, aur sheeshe mein jaati hai to aur bhi dheemi. Matlab har medium light ko thoda-bahut **rokta** hai, kam ya zyada.

Aur yahi hai refraction ki asli wajah. Ek analogy se samjho (ye sirf idea samjhane ke liye hai): socho ek gaadi pakki sadak se tirchi hokar kichad mein ghusti hai. Pehle uska ek pahiya kichad mein pahunchta hai aur dheema ho jaata hai, jabki doosra pahiya abhi sadak par tez chal raha hai. To gaadi ek taraf **mud** jaati hai. Light ke saath bhi kuch aisa hi hota hai: jab wo tirchi hokar naye medium mein jaati hai, to **raftaar badalne ki wajah se** uski direction badal jaati hai.

> **Refraction** = jab light kisi transparent medium se doosre transparent medium mein **tirchi (oblique) girti hai**, to boundary par uski **direction badal jaati hai**. Is mudne ka **kaaran** hai ki naye medium mein light ki **raftaar badal jaati hai**.

Do baatein pakki yaad rakho:

- Direction tabhi badalti hai jab light **tirchi** aaye. (Seedhi aane par kya hota hai, wo Part C mein dekhenge.)
- Light ki **raftaar** to hamesha badalti hai, chahe wo seedhi aaye ya tirchi. Mudna sirf tirchi hone par hota hai.

*(Ek idea, jo sirf jaanne ke liye hai: naye medium mein light ka **rang** (frequency) wahi rehta hai, sirf raftaar aur direction badalti hai. Isliye paani ke andar neeli light neeli hi rehti hai.)*

### Refraction ko samajhne ke liye ek sketch

Pehle sketch dekho (hawa upar hai, sheesha neeche; boundary ek seedhi line hai).

```
                      N (normal)
                      :
    incident ray      :     reflected ray (halki)
               \      :      /
                \     :     /
                 \    :    /
                  ↘   :   ↗
                   \  :  /
              i     \ : /  i
                     \:/
======================O=================================  boundary
                      :\
                      :\
                      : \
                      : ↘ r
                      :  \    refracted ray
                      :   \
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Hawa se sheeshe mein jaati ray. O point of incidence hai aur N normal (boundary ke perpendicular). Incident ray O par girti hai. Ek chhota hissa wapas uchhal kar **hawa mein hi** reflected ray ban jaata hai (halka dikhaya hai, wo reflection ka kaam hai). Baaki light **sheeshe ke andar** jaati hai aur wahi **refracted ray** hai. i incident ray aur normal ke beech ka angle hai, r refracted ray aur normal ke beech ka angle.

Ek-ek term pakdo:

- **Incident ray:** boundary par aati ray.
- **Refracted ray:** doosre medium ke **andar** chalne wali ray. Ye **doosre medium mein** hoti hai.
- **Normal:** point O par boundary ke perpendicular ek kalpanik line, jo boundary ke dono taraf jaati hai.
- **i:** incident ray aur normal ke beech ka angle.
- **r:** refracted ray aur normal ke beech ka angle.

> **Sabse zaroori baat:** i aur r **hamesha normal se** naape jaate hain, boundary (surface) se nahi. Reflection mein bhi yahi niyam tha.

> **Reflected aur refracted mein fark:** reflected ray **usi medium mein wapas** aati hai (jahan se incident ray aayi thi). Refracted ray **doosre medium ke andar** jaati hai. Sketch mein dono ko isi tarah alag pehchano.

## Part B: Light kis taraf mudti hai? Teen simple rules

Ab asli sawaal: mudti kis taraf hai? Normal ki taraf ya normal se door? Jawab is par depend karta hai ki light **kis tarah ke medium se kis tarah ke medium** mein ja rahi hai.

### Optically denser aur rarer: ek dhyaan dene wali baat

Jis medium mein light **zyada dheemi** ho jaati hai, use **optically denser** (optically zyada dense) kehte hain. Jis mein light zyada tez chalti hai, wo **optically rarer** hai. Hawa rarer hai, paani usse denser, aur sheesha paani se bhi zyada denser.

> **Warning:** **optical density** ka matlab hai ki medium light ko **kitna dheema** karta hai. Ye **mass density** (kitna bhaari, kitna ghana) se alag cheez hai. Misaal: **kerosene tel paani se halka hai** (paani par tairta hai), par kerosene **optically paani se denser** hai, kyunki usme light paani se zyada dheemi chalti hai. Isliye "halka hai to optically rarer hoga" kabhi mat soch lena.

### Rule 1: rarer se denser medium mein: normal ki taraf (towards the normal)

Jab light **rarer** medium se **denser** medium mein jaati hai (jaise hawa se paani ya sheeshe mein), to wo **normal ki taraf mud jaati hai**. Matlab angle of refraction chhota ho jaata hai: **r < i**.

```
                      N
                      :
          HAWA (rarer)
               \      :
                \     :
                 \    :
                  ↘   :
                   \  :
                i   \ :
                     \:
======================O=================================
                      :\
                      :\
                      : \
                      : ↘ r
                      :  \    refracted ray
                      :   \
                      :   \  SHEESHA (denser)
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Rarer (hawa) se denser (sheesha) mein: ray normal ki taraf mudti hai, isliye r < i. (Sketch mein incident ray ko normal se kaafi door aur refracted ray ko normal ke kaafi nazdeek dikhaya hai.)

Kyun? Kyunki naye medium mein light dheemi ho jaati hai, aur wo apni ray ko normal ki taraf jhuka leti hai.

### Rule 2: denser se rarer medium mein: normal se door (away from the normal)

Jab light **denser** medium se **rarer** medium mein jaati hai (jaise sheeshe ya paani se hawa mein), to wo **normal se door mud jaati hai**. Matlab angle of refraction **bada** ho jaata hai: **r > i**.

```
                      N
                      :
          HAWA        :              ↗  (hawa mein nikli ray)
          (rarer)     :          ↗
                      :  r   ↗
                      :  ↗
======================O=================================
                     ↗:
                    ↗ :
                   ↗  :
                  ↗ i :
                 ↗    :   SHEESHA (denser)
                ↗     :
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Denser (sheesha) se rarer (hawa) mein: ray normal se door mudti hai, isliye r > i. Sheeshe ke andar ray normal ke paas hai (chhota angle i), aur hawa mein nikal kar boundary ki taraf jhuk jaati hai (bada angle r). (Sketch ka shape sirf samjhane ke liye hai.)

### Rule 3: normal ke saath-saath girne par bilkul nahi mudti

Agar ray **normal ke saath-saath** (boundary par seedhi, 90° par) girti hai, to **i = 0°** hota hai aur **r = 0°** bhi. Ray **seedhi nikal jaati hai, mudti nahi**. Gaadi wali analogy se samjho: agar gaadi seedhi (sadak ke perpendicular) kichad mein jaaye, to dono pahiye **ek saath** kichad mein ghuste hain, ek ke pehle dheema hone ka sawaal hi nahi, isliye gaadi mudti nahi. Bas dheemi ho jaati hai. Light ke saath bhi yahi hota hai.

```
                      N
                      :
                      ↓      HAWA
                      ↓
                      ↓
======================O=================================
                      ↓
                      ↓      SHEESHA
                      ↓
                      :
```

> **Figure 1.4** – Normal ke saath-saath girne par i = 0° aur r = 0°. Ray seedhi chali jaati hai, mudti nahi (par naye medium mein uski raftaar badal jaati hai). Dono taraf ki ray aur normal ek hi line par hain.

### Teeno rules ek jagah

| Light kahan se kahan | Bending | Angles |
|---|---|---|
| Rarer se denser | Normal ki taraf (towards the normal) | r < i |
| Denser se rarer | Normal se door (away from the normal) | r > i |
| Normal ke saath-saath (kisi bhi medium mein) | Bilkul nahi mudti | i = r = 0° |

## Part C: Refraction ke niyam (Laws of Refraction)

### Law 1

> **Law 1:** Incident ray, refracted ray aur point of incidence par normal, **teeno ek hi plane mein** hote hain.

Ye reflection ke Law 2 jaisa hi hai: ray apne plane se bahar nahi jaati.

### Law 2: Snell ka niyam (Snell's law)

> **Law 2 (Snell's law):** kisi diye gaye **rang ki light** aur kisi diye gaye **do media ke jode** ke liye, **sin i ÷ sin r = ek constant** (har baar ek hi number).

Matlab: i badalte jaao, r bhi badlega, par **sin i ÷ sin r** ka jawab har baar wahi aayega. Is constant ko kehte hain **doosre medium ka pehle medium ke respect mein refractive index**. Hum ise **n** likhenge.

**sin i ÷ sin r = n**

*(Thoda extra: ye Class 10 ke saath overlap karta hai. Is topic mein hum is niyam ko sirf seedha use karenge. n ka matlab aur uski poori kahani agle topic mein aayegi.)*

Sine kya hota hai, iski chinta mat karo. Hum ye standard values istemaal karenge (calculator ki zaroorat bahut kam):

| Angle | 30° | 45° | 60° |
|---|---|---|---|
| sin | 1/2 = 0.5 | 0.707 | 0.866 |

Baaki angles ke sine hum sawal mein khud de denge.

Ek chhoti si baat samajh lo: **n jitna bada, mudna utna zyada** (i fixed ho to r utna chhota). Hawa se sheeshe mein jaane par hawa se paani mein jaane se zyada mudti hai, kyunki sheesha paani se zyada denser hai.

### Light ka raasta ulta chalna (reversibility)

Refraction mein bhi **light ka raasta reversible hai**: agar ray ko refracted ray ke raste se ulta bheja jaaye, to wo incident ray ke raste se wapas nikalti hai.

## Part D: Roz ke jeevan mein refraction

### 1. Paani ke glass mein pencil ya straw tuta-sa dikhta hai

Ek glass mein paani lo aur usme pencil ya straw tirchi rakho. Side se dekho: paani ki sateh par wo **tuti hui ya mudi hui** dikhti hai, aur paani ke andar wala hissa **motaa aur upar uthaa hua** lagta hai. Pencil tuti nahi hai. Paani ke andar wale hissa se aati light jab paani se hawa mein nikalti hai to wo **normal se door mud jaati hai**, isliye wo hissa apni asli jagah se thoda **upar** dikhta hai.

### 2. Katori mein sikka (Activity)

**Zaroorat:** ek opaque (jisme aar-paar na dikhe) katori ya cup, ek sikka, paani, ek dost.

**Steps:**

1. Sikka katori ke bilkul **tale** mein rakho.
2. Katori ko table par rakho aur khud peeche hat jao, jab tak sikka katori ke kinare (rim) ke peeche chhup na jaaye. Ab tum sikka nahi dekh paate.
3. Apni jagah aur sir ki position **bilkul mat badalna**. Dost se kaho ki dheere-dheere katori mein paani daale (sikke ko na hilaye).
4. Jaise-jaise paani badhega, **sikka wapas dikhne lagega**, jabki tum apni jagah se hile bhi nahi.

**Kyun?** Paani aane se sikke se aati light ki ray paani ki sateh par **normal se door** mud jaati hai aur katori ke kinare ke upar se nikal kar tumhari aankh tak pahunchne lagti hai. Tumhari aankh us ray ko seedhi line mein peeche ki taraf maanti hai, isliye sikka **upar uthaa hua** dikhta hai. Pehle paani nahi tha, ray seedhi kinare se takra jaati thi.

### 3. Pool ya talaab ka tala (bottom) uthaa hua dikhta hai

Yahi hai Tanishq ka jhooth: **paani ki gehraai asli se kam dikhti hai.** Is ka ray-sketch dekho. Tale ke ek bindu P se kai rays nikalti hain. Hum bas ek ray dekh lete hain.

```
                          N
                          :       E (aankh)
                          :     /
  HAWA                    :   ↗
                          : /
==========================A===================== sateh
                        ./:
                      . / :
  PAANI             P' ↗  :
                    : /   :
                    :/    :
                    P     :
                          :
```

> **Figure 1.5** – Pool ke tale ka bindu P (asli jagah). Ray P se chalkar sateh par A par pahunchti hai, wahan **denser (paani) se rarer (hawa)** mein jaati hai, isliye **normal se door** mud jaati hai aur aankh E tak jaati hai. Aankh ko lagta hai ki ray seedhi line mein peeche se aa rahi hai: wo ray ko peeche ki taraf badhati hai (dotted line) aur P' par pahunchti hai. Isliye aankh ko cheez P' par dikhti hai, jo P se **upar** (sateh ke nazdeek) hai. P' ko **image** ya **apparent position** kehte hain.

Isliye pool, talaab ya tank ki **dikhne wali gehraai (apparent depth)** asli gehraai se **kam** hoti hai. Ye idea hai. Kitna kam dikhta hai, uska number aur hisaab Topic 3 mein aayega.

### 4. Machhli bank se dekho to wahan nahi hoti jahan dikhti hai

Pichhle point ka ye hi roop hai. Paani mein machhli ek bindu P ki tarah hai. Bank par khade aadmi ko wo P' par (asli se thodi **upar**) dikhti hai. Isliye **teer ya bhale (spear) se machhli pakadne wala** seedha dikhti hui machhli par nishana nahi lagata, wo **dikhti hui machhli se thoda neeche** nishana lagata hai. Wahi asli machhli ki jagah hoti hai. Agar seedha dikhti jagah par maare to nishana **upar se nikal jaayega**.

### 5. Kuch aur jagah jahan refraction chhupa hai (sirf naam)

- **Tare jhilmil karte hain (twinkling):** hawa ki parten kahin garam, kahin thandi hoti hain aur lagatar hilti rehti hain, isliye har parat ka optical density thoda alag hai. Tare ki light un parton se guzarte hue thodi-thodi mud kar hilti rehti hai, aur tara timtimata (jhilmilata) hai.
- **Garam sadak par hawa jhilmilati hai:** garmi mein sadak ke upar garam hawa upar uthti hai, aur uski optical density har pal badalti hai. Usme se guzarti light mud kar hilti hai, isliye door ki cheezein kaanpti hui dikhti hain.
- **Sooraj ka jaldi nikalna aur der se doobna (atmospheric refraction):** hum sooraj ko **asli sunrise se lagbhag 2 minute pehle** dekh lete hain, aur sunset ke baad bhi **lagbhag 2 minute tak** dekhte rehte hain (ye samay approximate hai). Wajah: sooraj ki light jab bahar se dharti ki hawa mein aati hai, to hawa ki parten neeche ki taraf aur zyada dense hoti jaati hain, aur light thoda-thoda **mudti** jaati hai. Isliye sooraj hume actual position se thoda upar dikhta hai, jabki asli mein wo abhi kshitij (horizon) se neeche hota hai.
- **Mirage (maru-marichika):** garam sadak ya registaan mein door paani jaisa dikhna bhi hawa ki alag-alag density wali parton mein hone wale light ke mudne se hi hota hai. (Ise yahan sirf naam se jaana hai.)

## Part E: Solved examples

(Har example mein i aur r **normal se** naape gaye hain. Sines ki values: sin 30° = 0.5, sin 45° = 0.707, sin 60° = 0.866.)

### Example 1: bending ki direction (8 jode)

Har jode ke liye batao ki light normal ki taraf mudegi ya normal se door.

| Light kahan se kahan | Rarer ya denser | Bending |
|---|---|---|
| Hawa se sheeshe mein | Rarer se denser | **Towards** the normal (r < i) |
| Sheeshe se hawa mein | Denser se rarer | **Away from** the normal (r > i) |
| Hawa se paani mein | Rarer se denser | **Towards** the normal |
| Paani se hawa mein | Denser se rarer | **Away from** the normal |
| Paani se sheeshe mein (sheesha paani se denser hai) | Rarer se denser | **Towards** the normal |
| Sheeshe se paani mein | Denser se rarer | **Away from** the normal |
| Paani se kerosene mein (kerosene optically paani se denser hai) | Rarer se denser | **Towards** the normal |
| Kerosene se paani mein | Denser se rarer | **Away from** the normal |

Jaanch: har jode mein jo medium naye mein jaa raha hai usse **denser** hai to towards, **rarer** hai to away ✅. Dhyaan do: "paani" kabhi rarer hota hai, kabhi denser. Ye is par depend karta hai ki **saamne doosra medium kaunsa hai**: hawa ke saamne paani denser hai, par sheeshe ke saamne paani rarer hai. Hamesha **do media ki aapas mein tulna** karo.

### Example 2: hawa se sheeshe mein (i = 30°, n = 1.5)

Hawa se sheeshe mein jaati ray ka angle of incidence i = 30° hai. Sheeshe ke liye n = 1.5. Angle of refraction r nikaalo.

- Snell's law: sin i ÷ sin r = n, to sin r = sin i ÷ n.
- sin i = sin 30° = 0.5.
- sin r = 0.5 ÷ 1.5 = **0.3333**.
- r = wo angle jiska sine 0.3333 ho, **r ≈ 19.47°**.

Jaanch: r (19.47°) < i (30°), jaisa Rule 1 kehta hai (rarer se denser: towards the normal) ✅. Aur sin 19.47° ≈ 0.3333, to 0.5 ÷ 0.3333 = 1.5 ✅.

### Example 3: hawa se paani mein (i = 45°, n = 1.33)

Hawa se paani mein ray i = 45° par girti hai. Paani ke liye n = 1.33. r nikaalo.

- sin i = sin 45° = 0.7071 (hum 0.707 bhi le sakte hain).
- sin r = 0.7071 ÷ 1.33 = **0.5317**.
- r ≈ **32.1°**.

Jaanch: r (32.1°) < i (45°) ✅ (towards the normal). Aur sin 32.1° ≈ 0.5317, 0.7071 ÷ 0.5317 ≈ 1.33 ✅.

### Example 4: hawa se sheeshe mein (i = 60°, n = 1.5)

Hawa se sheeshe mein i = 60°, n = 1.5. r nikaalo.

- sin i = sin 60° = 0.866.
- sin r = 0.866 ÷ 1.5 = **0.5773** (0.5774).
- r ≈ **35.26°**.

Jaanch: r (35.26°) < i (60°) ✅. Aur 0.866 ÷ sin 35.26° = 0.866 ÷ 0.5774 = 1.5 ✅.

Dhyaan do: Example 2, 3, 4 mein **i badha to r bhi badha**, par **r hamesha i se chhota** raha.

### Example 5: ulta raasta (sheeshe se hawa mein)

Example 2 mein sheeshe ke andar ray normal se 19.47° par chal rahi thi. Agar yahi ray ulte raste se sheeshe se hawa mein nikle (ray ka sheeshe ke andar normal ke saath angle 19.47° hai), to hawa mein angle kitna hoga?

- Reversibility: ray apne **hi raste se wapas** jaayegi, to hawa mein angle **30°** hoga.
- Snell se check karke dekho. Sheeshe se hawa mein jaate waqt: sin (hawa wala angle) ÷ sin (sheeshe wala angle) = 1.5 (jo angle rarer medium mein hai wo bada hota hai).
- sin (hawa wala angle) = 1.5 × sin 19.47° = 1.5 × 0.3333 = **0.5**.
- Hawa wala angle = **30°**.

Jaanch: 1.5 × 0.3333 = 0.5 aur sin 30° = 0.5 ✅. Aur 30° > 19.47° (denser se rarer mein away from the normal) ✅. *(Class 10 overlap: jo angle rarer medium mein hota hai uska sine, denser wale angle ke sine ka n guna hota hai.)*

### Example 6: normal ke saath-saath ray

Hawa se paani mein ek ray normal ke saath-saath girti hai. Find i, r aur ray ka raasta.

- Normal ke saath-saath girti hai, to i = **0°**.
- sin i = sin 0° = 0, to sin r = 0 ÷ 1.33 = 0, aur r = **0°**.
- Ray **seedhi chali jaati hai**, mudti nahi.

Jaanch: i = r = 0° ✅. (Paani mein ray ki **raftaar** zaroor kam ho gayi, par direction wahi rahi.)

### Example 7: ray kitni mudi? (deviation = i − r)

Incident ray ki seedh se ray ne kitna mod liya? Rarer se denser mein jaate waqt ye **i − r** hota hai (deviation, mudne ka angle). Example 2, 3 aur 4 ke liye nikaalo.

| Example | i | r | Deviation = i − r |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 (hawa se sheesha) | 30° | 19.47° | 30° − 19.47° = **10.53°** |
| 3 (hawa se paani) | 45° | 32.1° | 45° − 32.1° = **12.9°** |
| 4 (hawa se sheesha) | 60° | 35.26° | 60° − 35.26° = **24.74°** |

Jaanch: Example 2 mein 19.47° + 10.53° = 30° ✅, Example 3 mein 32.1° + 12.9° = 45° ✅, Example 4 mein 35.26° + 24.74° = 60° ✅.

Dhyaan do: i jitna bada, light utna zyada mudti hai (deviation 10.53° se 24.74° tak badha). Aur normal par (i = 0°) deviation 0° hai, jo Example 6 mein dekha.

### Example 8: naapi hui angles se refractive index

Ek student ne hawa se kaanch (glass) mein jaati ray ke liye naapa: i = 40° aur r = 25°. n nikaalo. (Sine values: sin 40° = 0.6428, sin 25° = 0.4226.)

- n = sin i ÷ sin r = 0.6428 ÷ 0.4226.
- n = **1.52** (lagbhag).

Jaanch: n × sin r = 1.52 × 0.4226 = 0.6424 ≈ 0.6428 ✅ (chhota sa fark rounding ka hai). Aur n ≈ 1.5, jo sheeshe ke liye ek aam value hai.

### Example 9: Tanishq ka sawaal: pool jhooth bolta hai? (kahani ka hal)

Ab Tanishq ke pool ka raaz. Mr. Fabian ne Figure 1.5 banaya aur samjhaya:

**Tiles ka paas dikhna:** tiles ke bindu P se chali ray paani se hawa mein aati hai (denser se rarer), to **normal se door** mudti hai. Tanishq ki aankh ko wo ray seedhi line mein peeche se aati lagti hai, aur wo us seedh ko peeche badha kar tiles ko P' par dekhta hai, jo asli P se **upar** hai. Isliye **tiles paas, yaani pool kam gehra dikhta hai**. Asli gehraai zyada hai. Mr. Fabian ne kaha, "Isliye dikhne wali gehraai par bharosa mat karna: **paani asli mein dikhne se zyada gehra hota hai.**"

**Tanishq ke paer:** paani ke andar wale hissa ke har bindu se aati rays bhi isi tarah sateh par normal se door mudti hain, isliye wo har bindu apni asli jagah se **upar uthaa hua** dikhta hai. Paani ke andar wala paer apni asli jagah se upar utha aur chhota dikhta hai, jabki paani ke upar wala hissa (jo bina refraction ke seedha dikhta hai) apni jagah par hai. Dono ke milne wali jagah (paani ki sateh) par paer **tuta-sa, tedha** lagta hai.

**Nishkarsh:** pool jhooth nahi bol raha. **Light apna raasta modti hai**, aur humari aankh ray ko hamesha seedhi line mein peeche ki taraf maanti hai. Isliye cheez apni asli jagah se thodi hat kar dikhti hai.

Tanishq hans pada, "Matlab pool nahi, **light** chaalbaaz hai!" Mr. Fabian bole, "Chaalbaaz nahi, bas raftaar badalne ka natija hai. Aur ek baat yaad rakhna: pool kam gehra dikhe, tab bhi bina gehraai pooche kabhi kood mat padna."

### Example 10: kaunsa case kya hai? (classification)

Neeche diye gaye har case mein batao ki refraction kya kar raha hai.

| Case | Light kahan se kahan | Kya hota hai |
|---|---|---|
| Glass mein pencil tuti dikhti hai | Paani se hawa | Rays normal se door mudti hain, andar ka hissa upar uthaa dikhta hai |
| Katori mein sikka paani daalne par dikhne lagta hai | Paani se hawa | Ray normal se door mudkar kinare ke upar se nikalti hai |
| Pool ka tala uthaa dikhta hai | Paani se hawa | Tala asli se paas (kam gehra) dikhta hai |
| Machhli asli jagah se upar dikhti hai | Paani se hawa | Isliye nishana dikhti machhli se neeche lagate hain |
| Sooraj sunrise se pehle dikh jaata hai | Khali space se hawa (zyada dense hoti) mein | Light thodi mud kar upar dikhaati hai (atmospheric refraction) |
| Tare jhilmilate hain | Alag-alag dense hawa ki parten | Ray thodi-thodi mud kar hilti hai |
| Garam sadak par hawa kaanpti dikhti hai | Alag-alag dense hawa ki parten | Light lagatar thodi mud kar hilti hai |
| Mirage (sirf naam) | Alag-alag dense hawa ki parten | Garam hawa ki parton mein light mud kar upar uthti hai, isliye door paani jaisa dikhta hai |

Jaanch: pehle char mein light **paani se hawa** mein gayi, to normal se door mudi aur cheez upar uthi dikhi ✅. Baaki mein medium (hawa) ki density lagatar badal rahi hai, isliye mudna bhi lagatar badal raha hai ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**1. Angle boundary (surface) se naap lena**
Sawal mein "ray surface ke saath 30° ka angle banati hai" ho to i = 30° likh dena galat hai.
❌ i = 30°. ✅ i = 90° − 30° = 60°. i aur r hamesha **normal se** naape jaate hain.

**2. Ye maanna ki ray har case mein normal ki taraf mudti hai**
Sirf **denser medium mein jaate waqt** (rarer se denser) ray normal ki taraf mudti hai. **Denser se rarer** mein ray normal se **door** mudti hai.

**3. Optical density ko mass density samajhna**
Kerosene paani se halka hai, phir bhi optically paani se **denser** hai. Optical density ka matlab hai ki medium light ko kitna dheema karta hai.

**4. Ye samajhna ki normal par aane wali ray bhi mudti hai**
Jab i = 0° ho, r = 0° hota hai: ray seedhi nikal jaati hai. Raftaar badalti hai, direction nahi.

**5. Ye maanna ki naye medium mein light ka rang (frequency) badal jaata hai**
Naye medium mein light ka sirf **raftaar** (aur tirchi hone par direction) badalti hai, rang wahi rehta hai. (Idea.)

**6. Reflected ray aur refracted ray mein confuse hona**
Reflected ray **usi medium mein wapas** aati hai. Refracted ray **doosre medium ke andar** jaati hai. Sketch mein ye dekho ki ray boundary ke kis taraf hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Refraction:** light ka ek transparent medium se doosre mein **tirchi** jaate waqt apni **direction badal lena**. Wajah: naye medium mein light ki **raftaar badal jaati hai**.
- Terms: **boundary, incident ray, refracted ray, normal, angle of incidence i, angle of refraction r** (dono **normal se** naape jaate hain).
- **Rarer se denser:** normal ki taraf (r < i). **Denser se rarer:** normal se door (r > i). **Normal ke saath-saath:** koi bending nahi (i = r = 0°), par raftaar badalti hai.
- **Optical density** = medium light ko kitna dheema karta hai. **Mass density se alag**: kerosene paani se halka par optically denser hai.
- **Laws of refraction:** (1) incident ray, refracted ray aur normal **ek hi plane mein**. (2) **Snell's law:** sin i ÷ sin r = constant n (diye gaye rang aur do media ke liye). *(Class 10 overlap.)*
- Refraction mein bhi light ka **raasta reversible** hai.
- Dikhne wali jagah asli se alag hoti hai: **pool/talaab/katori ka tala upar uthaa dikhta hai (apparent depth kam)**, **pencil paani mein tuti dikhti hai**, **machhli asli se upar dikhti hai** (isliye nishana neeche).
- Tare ka jhilmilana, garam sadak par hawa ka kaanpna, sooraj ka lagbhag 2 minute pehle dikhna (approximate): sab **hawa mein hone wale refraction** ke naam hain.
- Naye medium mein light ka rang (frequency) nahi badalta. (Idea.)

## Practice Questions

1. State whether the ray bends towards the normal or away from the normal in each case: (a) air to water, (b) glass to air, (c) water to glass (glass is optically denser than water), (d) kerosene to water (kerosene is optically denser than water).
2. A ray goes from air into water at an angle of incidence of 30°. For water n = 1.33. Find sin r and the angle of refraction r. (Use sin 22° = 0.3746 and sin 23° = 0.3907.)
3. A ray goes from air into water at an angle of incidence of 60°. For water n = 1.33. Find sin r. (sin 40° = 0.643, sin 41° = 0.656: say which whole-degree value r is closest to.)
4. In an experiment with a glass block, a student measures i = 30° (in air) and r = 20° (in glass). Given sin 20° = 0.342, find n. Round to two decimals.
5. A ray inside glass reaches the boundary with air making an angle of 30° with the normal. For glass n = 1.5. Find the angle with the normal in air. (sin 48.6° = 0.75.)
6. State true or false and give a one-line reason: (a) Kerosene is lighter than water, so it must be optically rarer than water. (b) A ray falling along the normal on a glass surface does not bend. (c) The angle of incidence is measured from the surface of the boundary. (d) Light always bends towards the normal when it enters a new medium. (e) The colour of light changes when it enters water.
7. Explain in two or three lines why the bottom of a swimming pool looks closer to the surface than it really is.
8. A spear-fisher standing on the bank sees a fish in the water. Should he aim at the fish he sees, above it or below it? Explain in one or two lines.
9. A ray in air makes 45° with the normal and enters water, where it makes 32.1° with the normal. If the ray now goes backward from water into air along the same path, at what angle with the normal will it leave in air? Which property of light tells you this?
10. Classify as "bending due to refraction by a medium change" or "no bending": (a) a straw in a glass of water, (b) a torch beam going along the normal into a water tank, (c) a coin that becomes visible when water is poured into a cup, (d) a ray of light in a room full of air (same temperature), (e) twinkling of a star.

### Answers

1. (a) Hawa (rarer) se paani (denser) mein: **towards the normal**.
   (b) Sheesha (denser) se hawa (rarer) mein: **away from the normal**.
   (c) Paani se sheeshe mein (sheesha denser): rarer se denser, **towards the normal**.
   (d) Kerosene se paani mein (kerosene denser): denser se rarer, **away from the normal**.

2. sin r = sin i ÷ n = 0.5 ÷ 1.33 = **0.3759**. 0.3759 sin 22° (0.3746) ke bahut nazdeek hai, to r ≈ **22.1°** (lagbhag 22°).
   Jaanch: 0.5 ÷ 0.3759 = 1.33 ✅, aur r (22.1°) < i (30°) ✅.

3. sin r = sin 60° ÷ 1.33 = 0.866 ÷ 1.33 = **0.651**. Ye sin 40° (0.643) aur sin 41° (0.656) ke beech hai, aur 0.656 ke zyada nazdeek hai (0.651 aur 0.656 ka fark 0.005, 0.651 aur 0.643 ka fark 0.008). To r ≈ **41°** ke closest (asli value 40.6°).
   Jaanch: 0.866 ÷ 0.651 = 1.33 ✅, aur r (40.6°) < i (60°) ✅.

4. n = sin i ÷ sin r = sin 30° ÷ sin 20° = 0.5 ÷ 0.342 = **1.46**.
   Jaanch: 1.46 × 0.342 = 0.4993 ≈ 0.5 ✅.

5. Sheeshe se hawa mein jaate waqt: sin (hawa wala angle) = n × sin (sheeshe wala angle) = 1.5 × sin 30° = 1.5 × 0.5 = **0.75**. To hawa mein angle = **48.6°** (given sin 48.6° = 0.75).
   Jaanch: 48.6° > 30° (denser se rarer: away from the normal) ✅. Aur 1.5 × 0.5 = 0.75 ✅.

6. (a) **False** ❌. Optical density mass density se alag hai: kerosene halka hai par paani se optically denser hai.
   (b) **True** ✅. i = 0° hai to r = 0°, ray seedhi nikal jaati hai (sirf raftaar badalti hai).
   (c) **False** ❌. i **normal se** naapa jaata hai, surface se nahi.
   (d) **False** ❌. Sirf **denser** medium mein jaane par normal ki taraf mudti hai; rarer medium mein jaane par normal se door.
   (e) **False** ❌. Rang (frequency) wahi rehta hai; sirf raftaar aur direction badalti hai.

7. Tale se aati rays paani se hawa mein nikalte waqt **normal se door** mud jaati hain. Aankh ray ko seedhi line mein peeche ki taraf badhati hai, to tale ka bindu apni asli jagah se **upar (sateh ke paas)** dikhta hai. Isliye pool asli se kam gehra lagta hai.

8. **Dikhti hui machhli se neeche** nishana lagana chahiye. Machhli se aati rays paani se hawa mein jaate waqt normal se door mudti hain, to machhli asli jagah se **upar** dikhti hai. Asli machhli dikhti hui jagah se **neeche** hoti hai.

9. Light ka raasta reversible hai, to ray wahi raasta ulta chalegi aur hawa mein normal ke saath **45°** par nikalegi. **Property:** light ke raaste ka reversible hona (path of light is reversible).
   Jaanch: 1.33 × sin 32.1° = 1.33 × 0.5314 = 0.7067 ≈ sin 45° (0.7071) ✅.

10. (a) **Bending due to refraction** (paani se hawa mein, medium badla). (b) **No bending** (normal ke saath-saath i = 0°). (c) **Bending due to refraction** (paani se hawa mein ray kinare ke upar se nikalti hai). (d) **No bending** (ek hi medium, ek hi density: light seedhi chalti hai). (e) **Bending due to refraction** (hawa ki alag-alag density wali parton mein, lagatar badalti bending).

Ab tumhare paas refraction ka pakka tool hai: **light jab ek medium se doosre mein tirchi jaati hai to uski raftaar badalti hai aur wo mud jaati hai: rarer se denser mein normal ki taraf, denser se rarer mein normal se door, aur normal ke saath-saath girne par seedhi.** Jab bhi light ek medium se doosre mein jaaye, pehle normal kheencho, angle normal se naapo, aur socho ki light dheemi ho rahi hai ya tez.

**Aage kya?** Ye bending kitni hoti hai, ise naapne ke liye ek number: **refractive index**. Topic 2 mein dekhenge ki kisi medium ka ye number kaise nikalta hai, aur kyun hira sheeshe se zyada mudta hai.
