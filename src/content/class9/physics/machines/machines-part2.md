# Topic 2: Mechanical Advantage, Velocity Ratio aur Efficiency

Pichhle topic mein humne seekha ki **machine** ek aisi yukti hai jo force ka size ya disha badalti hai, ya kaam ki raftaar badalti hai. Humne **effort** (jo force hum lagate hain) aur **load** (jis cheez ko hilana hai) ke naam bhi jaane, aur ye bhi dekha ki koi machine force multiplier hoti hai, koi speed multiplier, aur koi sirf disha badalti hai. Par ek sawal baaki reh gaya: "kitni badi madad" mili? Kisi machine ko doosri se behtar kaise kehenge? Is topic mein hum machine ko **number** dena seekhenge: mechanical advantage, velocity ratio aur efficiency.

**Is poore topic mein g = 10 m/s² liya gaya hai.** Saath hi, jahan force ki baat ho wahan hum newton (N) hi use karenge. (Ek note: dukaan wale aksar load ko kgf mein bolte hain, jaise "200 kgf". 1 kgf lagbhag 10 N hota hai. Hum is topic mein har jagah N hi likhenge.)

## Highway ke tyre shop mein Mr. Gulati aur Reyansh

Highway par ek tyre shop tha. Reyansh apne papa ki van ke saath wahan ruka tha, kyunki van ka tyre badalna tha. Tyre mechanic Mr. Gulati ne van ke neeche ek **screw jack** lagaya. Van load se bhari thi, kai quintal ki, aur Reyansh ko laga ki ise to crane hi uthayegi.

Par Mr. Gulati ne jack ka lamba haanda pakda aur use **gol-gol ghumana** shuru kiya: ek chakkar, do chakkar, das chakkar, aur bhi. Haath ko bas thoda sa zor lag raha tha. Dheere dheere van **thodi si** upar uthi, bas itni ki tyre zameen se alag ho jaaye.

Reyansh ne gaur kiya aur bola, "Uncle, aap haanda itna lamba ghumate ho, itna lamba, aur van to sirf zara sa upar gayi! Itni mehnat ka fayda kya?"

Mr. Gulati hanse. "Beta, fayda hi to ye hai. Mere haath mein itna dum nahi ki kai quintal ki van seedhe utha doon. Par haanda lamba ghumane se **zor chhota** lagta hai. Jitna zor bachta hai, utna hi lamba ghumana padta hai. Ye machine ka sauda hai: **bada load, chhota effort, par haath ka lamba safar.**"

"To kya iska koi hisaab hai? Kitna zor bachta hai, kitna lamba ghumana padta hai?"

"Hai na. Aur wo hisaab hi aaj ka topic hai. Do number hain: ek force ka, ek doori ka. Phir ek teesra number batata hai ki machine kitni imaandaar hai." Is kahani ko hum topic ke beech mein number ke saath solve karenge.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Mechanical Advantage (MA)** | Load aur effort ka ratio: load ÷ effort. Batata hai ki machine ne effort ko kitni baar "badi" kar diya | Effort 100 N se load 2000 N uthe, to MA = 20 |
| **Velocity Ratio (VR)** | Effort ne jo doori tay ki ÷ load ne jo doori tay ki | Effort 12 m chala, load 0.5 m utha: VR = 24 |
| **Efficiency (η)** | Machine se mila kaam ÷ machine ko diya gaya kaam, percent mein | 1200 J diya, 1000 J mila: 83.3% |
| **Work input** | Effort ne machine ko diya hua kaam: effort × effort ki doori | 100 N × 12 m = 1200 J |
| **Work output** | Machine ne load par kiya hua kaam: load × load ki doori | 2000 N × 0.5 m = 1000 J |
| **Ideal machine** | Kalpana ki machine jisme friction bilkul nahi aur uske apne hisson ka weight bhi nahi | MA = VR aur η = 100% |
| **Real machine** | Asli machine, jisme friction hota hai | MA < VR aur η < 100% |

## Part A: Mechanical Advantage (MA): force par kitni madad

Machine ka sabse pehla kaam force mein madad hai. Us madad ko number dene ke liye hum load aur effort ko aamne-saamne rakhte hain:

> **MA = Load ÷ Effort**

Ye ek **ratio** hai: N ÷ N, to units kat jaati hain. Isliye **MA ki koi unit nahi hoti.**

- Agar 100 N ke effort se 2000 N ka load uthe, to MA = 2000 N ÷ 100 N = **20**. Matlab machine ne mere zor ko 20 guna bana diya.

MA ka size machine ke baare mein kya batata hai:

| MA | Kya hota hai | Machine ka naam (Topic 1 se) |
|---|---|---|
| MA > 1 | Load effort se bada: kam force se bhaari cheez hili | **Force multiplier** |
| MA = 1 | Load aur effort barabar: sirf disha badli | **Direction badalne wali machine** |
| MA < 1 | Load effort se chhota: zyada force lagaya, par kaam tezi se hua | **Speed multiplier** |

Dhyan do: MA hamesha **force** ke baare mein hai. Isme doori ka koi lena-dena nahi.

## Part B: Velocity Ratio (VR): doori par kitna kharcha

Ab Reyansh ke sawal ka doosra hissa: haanda itna lamba kyun ghumana padta hai? Iska number hai velocity ratio:

> **VR = Effort ne jo doori tay ki ÷ Load ne jo doori tay ki**

Ye bhi doori ÷ doori hai, to **VR ki bhi koi unit nahi hoti.** Dono doori **ek hi unit** (dono metre mein, ya dono cm mein) honi chahiye.

- Agar effort 12 m chale aur load sirf 0.5 m upar uthe, to VR = 12 m ÷ 0.5 m = **24**.

VR batata hai ki machine "doori ka kitna sauda" karti hai: VR = 24 ka matlab, load ko 1 m hilane ke liye effort ko 24 m chalna padega.

Screw jack ki figure dekho. Haanda bahut lamba gol ghoomta hai, van sirf thodi si uthti hai:

```
        ┌───────────────────────┐
        │     VAN  (load L)     │   ↑ van ka safar: bahut kam
        └───────────┬───────────┘
                    │
                  ┌─┴─┐
                  │ ~ │   screw (ghumaavdaar)
   haanda ════════┤ ~ │
   (effort E)     │ ~ │
                  └───┘
 ════════════════════════════════════ zameen

   haanda gol-gol ghoomta hai: haath ka safar bahut lamba
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Screw jack van ke neeche lagta hai. Effort (haanda) gol-gol ghoom kar bahut lamba safar tay karta hai, load (van) bahut kam upar uthta hai. Isliye VR bada hota hai.

## Part C: MA, VR aur kaam ka rishta (efficiency)

Ab dono ko jod ke ek bahut zaroori cheez nikalte hain. Pehle yaad karo ki [Work, Energy aur Power chapter](../work-energy-and-power/work-energy-and-power.md) mein **work = force × displacement** tha, aur unit joule (J).

Machine ke do sire hain:

- **Work input** = effort ne diya hua kaam = **Effort × effort ki doori**
- **Work output** = machine ne load par kiya hua kaam = **Load × load ki doori**

Efficiency ek simple sawal ka jawab hai: **jitna kaam diya, usme se kitna mila?**

> **η = (Work output ÷ Work input) × 100%**

Ab isi ko MA aur VR mein badalte hain, step by step:

**Step 1:** η = (Work output ÷ Work input) × 100

**Step 2:** Work output = Load × load ki doori, Work input = Effort × effort ki doori. To
η = (Load × load ki doori) ÷ (Effort × effort ki doori) × 100

**Step 3:** Isse do hisson mein tod do:
η = (Load ÷ Effort) × (load ki doori ÷ effort ki doori) × 100

**Step 4:** Pehla hissa Load ÷ Effort = **MA**. Doosra hissa (load ki doori ÷ effort ki doori) VR ka **ulta** hai, yaani 1 ÷ VR.

**Step 5:** To
> **η = (MA ÷ VR) × 100%**

Is formula ka matlab: machine ki efficiency utni hi hogi jitna MA, VR ke saamne bacha. Agar MA = VR, to MA ÷ VR = 1 aur η = 100%.

### Ideal aur real machine

- **Ideal machine** (kalpana ki machine): isme friction bilkul nahi hota, aur uske apne hisson ka weight bhi nahi hota. Ismein effort ka diya hua poora kaam load ko mil jaata hai: work output = work input. To η = **100%**, aur MA = **VR**.
- **Real machine** (asli machine): ismein friction hota hai. Effort ka kuch kaam friction se ladne mein kharch ho jaata hai. To work output < work input. Isliye **MA < VR** hota hai, aur **η < 100%** hota hai.

Friction ka ye kharch kahan jaata hai? Wo **heat** ban jaata hai: machine garm ho jaati hai (jack ke screw ko chalane ke baad haath lagaoge to thoda garam milega). Ye energy kaam ki cheez mein nahi lagti, par gayab bhi nahi hoti: [work-energy-and-power-part6.md](../work-energy-and-power/work-energy-and-power-part6.md) mein dekha tha ki energy heat mein badal jaati hai, nasht nahi hoti.

### Machine kya nahi kar sakti: kaam badhana

Machine ka matlab "jaadu" nahi hai. Koi bhi machine **work output ko work input se zyada nahi kar sakti**. Ideal machine mein barabar, real machine mein kam (friction ki wajah se).

Isi se Reyansh ke sawal ka jawab nikalta hai. Agar load ka kaam (Load × load ki doori) aur effort ka kaam (Effort × effort ki doori) lagbhag barabar rakhna hai, aur load bada hai effort chhota, to effort ki doori **zyada** honi chahiye:

> Chhota effort × lambi doori ≈ Bada load × chhoti doori

Yehi Mr. Gulati ne kaha tha: **zor bachta hai, kaam nahi.** Force multiplier machine "force" bachati hai, par **effort ko zyada doori tak chalna padta hai.** Jitna zor ka fayda, utni hi lambi doori ka kharcha.

Ab figure mein dono kaam ek saath dekho (har ■ block 100 J ka maan lo):

```
 Work input  (effort ne diya) : ■■■■■■■■■■■■   1200 J
 Work output (load ko mila)   : ■■■■■■■■■■     1000 J
 Kho gaya (friction se heat)  : ■■               200 J
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Real machine mein work input se work output kam hota hai. Beech ka fark friction ke karan heat ban jaata hai. Ye numbers Mr. Gulati ke jack ke hain (Example 9 mein nikaalenge).

### Efficiency badhane wale factors

Real machine ko ideal ke paas laane ke do tareeke:

1. **Friction kam karo:** machine ke ghoomne wale hisson mein **tel ya grease (lubrication)** lagao. Isse friction kam hota hai aur efficiency badhti hai. Isiliye Mr. Gulati jack ke screw par grease lagate hain.
2. **Chalne wale hisson ka weight kam karo:** halke (light) parts use karo, taaki machine ke apne hisson ko uthane mein effort bekaar na jaaye.

Phir bhi kisi real machine mein friction poori tarah zero nahi hota, to **real machine ki efficiency hamesha 100% se kam** hoti hai.

## Part D: Solved examples

**Example 1 (MA nikalna):** Ek machine 150 N ke effort se 600 N ka load uthati hai. MA?

- MA = Load ÷ Effort = 600 N ÷ 150 N = **4**

**Jaanch:** 150 N × 4 = 600 N ✅. MA > 1, to ye force multiplier hai.

**Example 2 (load se effort):** Ek machine ka MA 5 hai. 800 N ka load uthane ke liye kitna effort lagega?

- MA = Load ÷ Effort, to Effort = Load ÷ MA
- Effort = 800 N ÷ 5 = **160 N**

**Jaanch:** 800 N ÷ 160 N = 5 ✅

**Example 3 (MA aur effort se load):** Ek machine ka MA 6 hai. 40 N ka effort lagane par kitna load uthega?

- Load = MA × Effort = 6 × 40 N = **240 N**

**Jaanch:** 240 N ÷ 40 N = 6 ✅

**Example 4 (VR, cm ko m mein badal ke):** Ek machine mein effort 2 m chalta hai aur load 25 cm uthta hai. VR?

- Pehle ek hi unit karo: 25 cm = 0.25 m.
- VR = Effort ki doori ÷ load ki doori = 2 m ÷ 0.25 m = **8**

**Jaanch:** 0.25 m × 8 = 2 m ✅. (Agar galti se 2 ÷ 25 kar dete, to 0.08 aata, jo galat hai.)

**Example 5 (MA, VR aur efficiency, ek hi machine):** Ek machine mein 150 N ke effort se 900 N ka load uthta hai. Effort 12 m chalta hai, load 1.5 m uthta hai. MA, VR aur efficiency nikalo.

- MA = 900 N ÷ 150 N = **6**
- VR = 12 m ÷ 1.5 m = **8**
- η = (MA ÷ VR) × 100 = (6 ÷ 8) × 100 = **75%**

**Jaanch:** Work input = 150 N × 12 m = 1800 J. Work output = 900 N × 1.5 m = 1350 J. η = 1350 J ÷ 1800 J × 100 = 75% ✅ (dono tareeke ek hi jawab dete hain).

**Example 6 (work input, work output aur kho gayi energy):** Ek machine par 80 N ka effort 5 m chalta hai aur 300 N ka load 1 m uthta hai. Work input, work output, kho gayi energy aur efficiency?

- Work input = 80 N × 5 m = **400 J**
- Work output = 300 N × 1 m = **300 J**
- Kho gayi energy (friction se heat) = 400 J − 300 J = **100 J**
- η = (300 J ÷ 400 J) × 100 = **75%**

**Jaanch:** MA = 300 ÷ 80 = 3.75, VR = 5 ÷ 1 = 5, aur (3.75 ÷ 5) × 100 = 75% ✅

**Example 7 (ideal machine):** Ek ideal machine ka VR 4 hai. Effort 50 N hai. Kitna load uthega? Agar effort 2 m chale, to load kitna uthega?

- Ideal machine mein MA = VR = 4.
- Load = MA × Effort = 4 × 50 N = **200 N**
- Load ki doori = effort ki doori ÷ VR = 2 m ÷ 4 = **0.5 m**

**Jaanch:** Work input = 50 N × 2 m = 100 J. Work output = 200 N × 0.5 m = 100 J. Ideal machine hai, to barabar ✅

**Example 8 (80% efficiency wali machine):** Ek machine ki efficiency 80% hai aur VR = 5. Effort 100 N lagaya jaaye to kitna load uthega?

- η = (MA ÷ VR) × 100, to MA = (η ÷ 100) × VR = 0.8 × 5 = **4**
- Load = MA × Effort = 4 × 100 N = **400 N**

**Jaanch:** Ideal machine hoti to MA = 5 aur load 500 N hota. Ye real machine hai, to load 400 N, yaani 500 N ka 80% ✅

**Example 9 (Mr. Gulati ka jack, kahani solve):** Mr. Gulati ke haath ka effort 100 N hai, jo haanda ghuma kar 12 m ka safar tay karta hai. Isse van ka 2000 N ka (uska jack par aaya hua) load 0.5 m upar uthta hai. MA, VR, efficiency, work input, work output aur kho gayi energy?

- MA = 2000 N ÷ 100 N = **20**
- VR = 12 m ÷ 0.5 m = **24**
- η = (20 ÷ 24) × 100 = **83.3%**
- Work input = 100 N × 12 m = **1200 J**
- Work output = 2000 N × 0.5 m = **1000 J**
- Kho gayi energy = 1200 J − 1000 J = **200 J**

**Jaanch:** 1000 ÷ 1200 × 100 = 83.3% ✅, aur MA (20) < VR (24), real machine ke hisaab se ✅.

Reyansh ke sawal ka jawab ab number mein hai: **MA = 20** ka matlab Mr. Gulati ko 2000 N ke load ke liye sirf 100 N lagana pada (force 20 guna kam). Par **VR = 24** ka matlab haath ko 12 m chalna pada, jabki van sirf 0.5 m (50 cm) uthi. Mr. Gulati ne force bachaya, kaam nahi: unhone 1200 J diye aur van ko 1000 J mile.

**Example 10 (do machines ko compare karna):** Machine A mein 100 N ke effort se 500 N ka load uthta hai, effort 10 m chalta hai aur load 1.5 m uthta hai. Machine B mein 100 N ke effort se 400 N ka load uthta hai, effort 6 m chalta hai aur load 1.2 m uthta hai. Kiska MA zyada hai? Kaun zyada efficient hai?

- Machine A: MA = 500 N ÷ 100 N = **5**. Work input = 100 N × 10 m = 1000 J. Work output = 500 N × 1.5 m = 750 J. η = 750 J ÷ 1000 J × 100 = **75%**
- Machine B: MA = 400 N ÷ 100 N = **4**. Work input = 100 N × 6 m = 600 J. Work output = 400 N × 1.2 m = 480 J. η = 480 J ÷ 600 J × 100 = **80%**

**Jaanch:** VR dekho. A: 10 ÷ 1.5 = 6.67, to (5 ÷ 6.67) × 100 = 75% ✅. B: 6 ÷ 1.2 = 5, to (4 ÷ 5) × 100 = 80% ✅.

Nateeja: **A ka MA bada hai, par B zyada efficient hai.** MA bada hona aur efficient hona alag baatein hain.

**Example 11 (effort ki doori nikalna):** Ek machine ka MA 6 hai aur efficiency 75% hai. Agar load 0.5 m uthaana hai, to effort ko kitna chalna padega?

- η = (MA ÷ VR) × 100, to VR = MA ÷ (η ÷ 100) = 6 ÷ 0.75 = **8**
- Effort ki doori = VR × load ki doori = 8 × 0.5 m = **4 m**

**Jaanch:** VR = 4 m ÷ 0.5 m = 8 ✅, aur (6 ÷ 8) × 100 = 75% ✅

**Example 12 (energy ka loss J mein):** Ek machine ko 2500 J ka kaam diya gaya aur uski efficiency 60% hai. Output kitna mila aur kitna loss hua? Agar ye machine 300 N ke load ko uthati hai, to load kitna upar uthega?

- Work output = 60% of 2500 J = 0.6 × 2500 J = **1500 J**
- Loss = 2500 J − 1500 J = **1000 J** (heat ban gaya)
- Load ki doori = Work output ÷ Load = 1500 J ÷ 300 N = **5 m**

**Jaanch:** 300 N × 5 m = 1500 J ✅, aur 1500 ÷ 2500 × 100 = 60% ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: MA aur VR ko ek hi cheez samajhna**

MA **force** ke baare mein hai (load ÷ effort), VR **doori** ke baare mein (effort ki doori ÷ load ki doori). Example 9 mein MA = 20 aur VR = 24: dono alag number hain aur alag cheez batate hain.

**Galti 2: "MA, VR se bada ho sakta hai"**

Nahi. Ideal machine mein MA = VR aur real machine mein MA < VR. MA ÷ VR > 1 ka matlab efficiency 100% se zyada, jo possible nahi. Agar tumhare calculation mein MA > VR aaye, to kahin galti hui hai.

**Galti 3: "Efficiency 100% se zyada ho sakti hai"**

Nahi. Efficiency 100% se zyada ka matlab hota ki machine ne **kaam banaya**: jitna diya usse zyada mila. Energy na banti hai na nasht hoti hai. Real machine ki efficiency hamesha 100% se kam hai.

**Galti 4: MA mein effort ko load se divide kar dena**

MA = **Load ÷ Effort**, ulta nahi. Example 1 mein 150 ÷ 600 = 0.25 nikalta, jo force multiplier ke bajaye speed multiplier jaisa dikhta, jabki machine force multiplier hai. Hamesha "load upar, effort neeche" yaad rakho.

**Galti 5: cm ko m mein badalna bhool jaana**

Example 4 mein load 25 cm tha aur effort 2 m. Agar seedha 2 ÷ 25 = 0.08 kar dete, to VR galat aata. Hamesha dono doori ek unit mein karo: 25 cm = 0.25 m, tab VR = 8.

**Galti 6: "MA bada hai, to kaam bach gaya"**

Bada MA **force** bachata hai, **kaam nahi.** Example 9 mein MA = 20, phir bhi 1200 J ka kaam dena pada, aur sirf 1000 J mila. Force kam laga, par doori itni lambi chalni padi ki kaam bachna to door, 200 J heat mein gayab ho gaya.

## Quick Recap

- **MA = Load ÷ Effort.** Ye ratio hai, koi unit nahi. MA > 1: force multiplier. MA = 1: sirf disha badalti hai. MA < 1: speed multiplier.
- **VR = Effort ki doori ÷ Load ki doori.** Ye bhi ratio hai, koi unit nahi. Dono doori ek unit mein.
- **Work input = Effort × effort ki doori.** **Work output = Load × load ki doori.**
- **η = (Work output ÷ Work input) × 100% = (MA ÷ VR) × 100%.**
- **Ideal machine** (no friction, no weight of parts): MA = VR, η = 100%. **Real machine:** MA < VR, η < 100%.
- Efficiency kabhi 100% se zyada nahi. Kho gayi energy friction se **heat** ban jaati hai.
- Koi machine **kaam nahi badhati**: work output ≤ work input. Force multiplier ke liye effort ko **zyada doori** chalna padta hai.
- Efficiency badhane ke liye: **lubrication** (friction kam) aur **halke parts** (apna weight kam).
- Mr. Gulati ka jack: effort 100 N, 12 m; load 2000 N, 0.5 m. MA = 20, VR = 24, η = 83.3%, loss = 200 J.

## Practice Questions

1. A machine lifts a load of 450 N with an effort of 90 N. Find its mechanical advantage. Is it a force multiplier?

2. A machine has MA = 8. What effort is needed to lift a load of 200 N?

3. In a machine, the effort moves through 6 m while the load moves through 1.5 m. Find the velocity ratio. If the effort moved 3 m instead, how far would the load move?

4. An effort of 60 N moves through 5 m and raises a load of 200 N through 1 m. Find (a) the work input, (b) the work output, (c) the efficiency, (d) the MA and VR of the machine.

5. An ideal machine has VR = 6. What load can it lift with an effort of 45 N?

6. A machine of efficiency 90% has VR = 10. What load can it lift with an effort of 50 N?

7. In a machine, an effort of 125 N moves through 2 m and lifts a load of 1000 N through 0.2 m. Find the work input, the work output, the energy lost as heat, and the efficiency.

8. State True or False:
   (a) MA and VR both have the unit newton.
   (b) In a real machine, MA is greater than VR.
   (c) A machine with a large MA saves work.
   (d) The efficiency of a real machine can never be 100%.

9. Machine A is given 400 J and delivers 300 J. Machine B is given 500 J and delivers 400 J. Find the efficiency of each and say which is better. How much energy is lost as heat in each?

10. A machine has VR = 12 and an efficiency of 75%. (a) If the load rises 0.25 m, how far does the effort move? (b) Find its MA. (c) What load can it lift with an effort of 100 N?

### Answers

1. MA = Load ÷ Effort = 450 N ÷ 90 N = **5**. MA > 1, to ye **force multiplier** hai.
   Jaanch: 90 N × 5 = 450 N ✅

2. Effort = Load ÷ MA = 200 N ÷ 8 = **25 N**.
   Jaanch: 200 N ÷ 25 N = 8 ✅

3. VR = 6 m ÷ 1.5 m = **4**. Agar effort 3 m chale, to load ki doori = 3 m ÷ 4 = **0.75 m**.
   Jaanch: 1.5 m × 4 = 6 m ✅, aur 0.75 m × 4 = 3 m ✅

4. (a) Work input = 60 N × 5 m = **300 J**. (b) Work output = 200 N × 1 m = **200 J**. (c) η = 200 J ÷ 300 J × 100 = **66.7%**. (d) MA = 200 N ÷ 60 N = **3.33**, VR = 5 m ÷ 1 m = **5**.
   Jaanch: (MA ÷ VR) × 100 = (3.33 ÷ 5) × 100 = 66.7% ✅

5. Ideal machine: MA = VR = 6. Load = 6 × 45 N = **270 N**.
   Jaanch: Agar effort 6 m chale, work input = 45 N × 6 m = 270 J, aur load 1 m uthe, work output = 270 N × 1 m = 270 J ✅

6. MA = 0.9 × 10 = **9**. Load = 9 × 50 N = **450 N**.
   Jaanch: 450 N ÷ 50 N = 9, aur (9 ÷ 10) × 100 = 90% ✅

7. Work input = 125 N × 2 m = **250 J**. Work output = 1000 N × 0.2 m = **200 J**. Heat mein gayi energy = 250 J − 200 J = **50 J**. η = 200 J ÷ 250 J × 100 = **80%**.
   Jaanch: MA = 1000 ÷ 125 = 8, VR = 2 ÷ 0.2 = 10, (8 ÷ 10) × 100 = 80% ✅

8. (a) **False** ❌. MA aur VR dono ratio hain, unki koi unit nahi.
   (b) **False** ❌. Real machine mein MA < VR hota hai.
   (c) **False** ❌. Bada MA force bachata hai, kaam nahi.
   (d) **True** ✅. Friction hamesha rehta hai, to efficiency 100% se kam.

9. A: 300 J ÷ 400 J × 100 = **75%**. B: 400 J ÷ 500 J × 100 = **80%**. **B zyada efficient** hai. Heat mein loss: A mein 400 J − 300 J = **100 J**, B mein 500 J − 400 J = **100 J**. (Loss dono mein barabar hai, par B ko diye gaye kaam ka zyada hissa mila, isliye B behtar hai.)
   Jaanch: 0.75 × 400 = 300 ✅, 0.8 × 500 = 400 ✅

10. (a) Effort ki doori = VR × load ki doori = 12 × 0.25 m = **3 m**. (b) MA = 0.75 × 12 = **9**. (c) Load = 9 × 100 N = **900 N**.
    Jaanch: Work input = 100 N × 3 m = 300 J. Work output = 900 N × 0.25 m = 225 J. 225 ÷ 300 × 100 = 75% ✅

**Aage kya?** Ab sawal ye hai ki asli machines mein ye MA aur VR aate kahan se hain? Agle topic mein hum milenge **sabse purani aur sabse aasaan machine: lever** se. Ek simple si chhad, ek tikne ki jagah (pivot), aur ek naya idea: force ka "ghumane wala asar" (moment).
