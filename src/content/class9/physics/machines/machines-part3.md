# Topic 3: Lever: Moment of Force aur Principle of Moments

Pichhle topic mein humne machines ko naapne ke teen paimane seekhe: **mechanical advantage** (MA = load ÷ effort), **velocity ratio** (VR = effort ka distance ÷ load ka distance) aur **efficiency** (η = MA ÷ VR × 100%). Ek ideal machine mein MA = VR hota hai. Ab sawal ye hai: in teeno ko **kisi asli machine par** kaise lagayein? Shuruaat sabse purani aur sabse saaf machine se karte hain: **lever**. Ek seedhi chhad (bar), ek tikne ki jagah, bas. Is topic mein dekhenge ki ye chhad itna kaam kaise karti hai, aur **moment of force** aur **principle of moments** kya hote hain.

**Is poore topic mein g = 10 m/s² liya gaya hai**, to 1 kg ka weight 10 N hota hai. Plank, chhad ya beam ka apna weight hum **ignore** karenge, jab tak sawal mein alag se na bataya ho.

## Part A: Lever kya hai aur uske hisse

**Lever** ek sakht chhad hai jo ek fixed point ke around **ghoom sakti hai**. Us fixed point ko **fulcrum** (F) kehte hain. Chhad par do force lagte hain:

- **Effort (E):** jo force hum lagate hain.
- **Load (L):** jo force hume jeetna hai (aksar kisi cheez ka weight).

Fulcrum se in dono forces ki **perpendicular doori** ko **arm** kehte hain: **effort arm** aur **load arm**. Perpendicular ka matlab hai force ki line se **seedhe 90° par** naapi gayi doori.

Seedhi, simple figure (fulcrum ke ek taraf load, doosri taraf effort):

```
   effort arm            load arm
 <─────────────>   <──────────>
 ↓ E                           ↓ L
 ═══════════════════════════════
                 ^
                 F (fulcrum)
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Lever ke hisse: beam "=" se, fulcrum "^" se dikhaya gaya hai. Effort aur load ki fulcrum se doori ko effort arm aur load arm kehte hain.

Is figure mein fulcrum beech mein hai. Aage dekhenge ki fulcrum, load aur effort ki **jagah badalne** se lever ke teen alag roop (classes) ban jaate hain.

## Part B: Moment of force (ghumane ka asar)

Kabhi band darwaze ko **kabze (hinge) ke paas** dhakela hai? Bahut mushkil lagta hai. Wahi darwaza **handle** par dhakelo, to halka sa dhakka kaafi hai. Force wahi, par asar alag. Kyun? Kyunki ghumane ka asar sirf force par nahi, **force kahan lagaya gaya** (hinge se kitni door) us par bhi depend karta hai.

Isi ghumane ke asar ko naapne ke liye physics mein **moment of force** use karte hain:

> **Moment of force = force × fulcrum (pivot) se force ki line tak ki perpendicular doori**
> **M = F × d**

- Unit: **N m** (newton metre). Likhne mein ye joule jaisa lagta hai (N × m), par yahan ye ghumane ka asar hai, work nahi.
- Moment ka ek **sense** (ghumane ki disha) bhi hota hai: ya to **clockwise** (ghadi ki soi ki disha) ya **anticlockwise** (ulti).

Moment **do cheezon** par depend karta hai: force kitna bada hai aur pivot se kitni door laga hai. Isi liye lambi spanner se kam force mein nut khul jaata hai.

### Perpendicular doori hi gini jaati hai

Agar force chhad par **tirchha (slanted)** lag raha ho, to chhad ke saath naapi hui doori nahi, force ki line se pivot tak ki **seedhi (90° wali) doori** li jaati hai.

```
 O ═════════════════════ P
  ╲                   ╱     F (tirchha force, P par)
   ╲ d             ╱
    ╲           ·
     ╲       ·        (F ki line, aage badhayi hui)
      ╲   ·
       N  (yahan 90° ka kona)
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Chhad OP ke sire P par tirchha force F lagta hai. F ki line ko aage badhao aur pivot O se us line par seedhi (90° wali) line ON daalo: ye doori d hi arm hai. d chhad ki lambai OP se **chhoti** hoti hai, isliye OP ko arm mat samjho.

Is baat ko yahin tak rakhte hain. Class 9 mein hum zyaadatar wo cases lenge jahan force chhad ke seedhe 90° par lagta hai, aur arm bas chhad par naapi hui doori hoti hai.

### Clockwise aur anticlockwise

Fulcrum ke **dayein** taraf neeche dabane wala force chhad ko **clockwise** ghumana chahta hai. Fulcrum ke **bayein** taraf neeche dabane wala force **anticlockwise** ghumana chahta hai. Dono taraf ke moments ek doosre se **ulta** kaam karte hain.

## Part C: Principle of moments

Jab koi chhad **balance mein** ho (na ghoom rahi ho), to:

> **Clockwise moments ka jod = anticlockwise moments ka jod** (same pivot ke around)

Ise **principle of moments** kehte hain.

Lever mein aksar ek taraf sirf load hota hai aur doosri taraf sirf effort. To balance ke liye:

> **Load × load arm = Effort × effort arm**
> **L × l = E × e**

Is se seedha **MA** nikalta hai:

- MA = load ÷ effort = (e ÷ l) kyunki L × l = E × e se L ÷ E = e ÷ l
- **MA = effort arm ÷ load arm**

Ab Topic 2 ka **velocity ratio** yaad karo: VR = effort ka distance ÷ load ka distance. Jab lever ghoomta hai, effort wala sira **bada chaap** (arc) banata hai aur load wala sira **chhota**. Ye distances apni-apni arms ke **barabar ratio** mein hote hain. Isliye ek **ideal lever** (friction aur weight ko ignore karke) mein:

> **VR = effort arm ÷ load arm = MA**

Ye wahi baat hai jo Topic 2 mein kahi thi: **ideal machine mein MA = VR**.

### Lambi effort arm = zyaada MA

MA = effort arm ÷ load arm hai, to effort arm jitna **lamba**, MA utna **zyaada**. Isliye:
- **Crowbar** ko haath se **door** pakda jata hai (lamba effort arm).
- Lambi **spanner** se nut kholna aasaan hai.

Price? Effort ko **zyaada doori** tak chalana padta hai (VR bada), jaisa Topic 2 mein dekha tha.

### Lever ka apna weight

Lever, beam ya plank ka apna weight yahan **ignore** kiya jata hai, jab tak sawal mein bataya na jaye. Asli life mein agar bhaari plank ka beech pivot par na ho, to uska weight bhi ek moment banata hai, par hum yahan ye case nahi lenge.

## Part D: Lever ke teen class

Lever mein **fulcrum (F), load (L) aur effort (E)** ki **kramik jagah** teen tarah se ho sakti hai. Isi se teen **class** bante hain.

### First class lever: fulcrum beech mein

Order: **E ... F ... L** (fulcrum beech mein).

```
   E ↓                       ↓ L
 ═══════════════════════════════
                ^
                F
```

> **Figure 3.3** – First class lever: fulcrum effort aur load ke beech mein hai.

- MA **1 se zyaada, 1 ke barabar ya 1 se kam**, kuch bhi ho sakta hai, kyunki ye effort arm aur load arm ki lambai par depend karta hai (fulcrum ko load ke paas rakhoge to MA bada, effort ke paas rakhoge to chhota).
- Examples: see-saw, scissors (kainchi), crowbar, pliers, beam balance (taraazu), claw hammer se keel nikalna, hand pump ka handle.

### Second class lever: load beech mein

Order: **F ... L ... E** (load fulcrum aur effort ke beech mein).

```
                 ↑ E
 ═══════════════════════════════
 ^         ↓ L
 F
```

> **Figure 3.4** – Second class lever: load fulcrum aur effort ke beech mein hai. Effort arm hamesha load arm se lamba hota hai.

- Effort arm **hamesha** load arm se lamba hai, isliye **MA hamesha 1 se zyaada**. Kam effort se bhaari load uthta hai.
- Examples: wheelbarrow (thela), nutcracker (akhrot todne ka), bottle opener, darwaza (hinge fulcrum, darwaze ki rukawat beech mein, handle par effort), lemon squeezer, nail cutter.

### Third class lever: effort beech mein

Order: **F ... E ... L** (effort fulcrum aur load ke beech mein).

```
                 ↑ E            ↓ L
 ═══════════════════════════════
 ^
 F
```

> **Figure 3.5** – Third class lever: effort fulcrum aur load ke beech mein hai. Effort arm load arm se chhota hota hai.

- Effort arm **hamesha** load arm se chhota hai, isliye **MA hamesha 1 se kam**. Yani effort load se **zyaada** lagana padta hai.
- Phir bhi ye bekaar nahi: ye lever **speed aur range** (load ko zyaada doori tak, tezi se hilana) deta hai. Yaani ye **speed multiplier** hai.
- Examples: tongs (chimta), tweezers (chimti), fishing rod, jhaadu (broom), kalayi (forearm) se kuch uthana, cricket bat ko ghumana.

### Teeno ek table mein

| Class | Order (F, L, E) | MA | Kaam | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First | F beech mein: E ... F ... L | Kuch bhi (arms par depend) | Force multiplier, ya direction change | See-saw, scissors, crowbar, pliers, beam balance, claw hammer |
| Second | L beech mein: F ... L ... E | Hamesha 1 se zyaada | Force multiplier | Wheelbarrow, nutcracker, bottle opener, darwaza, lemon squeezer |
| Third | E beech mein: F ... E ... L | Hamesha 1 se kam | Speed multiplier (range deta hai) | Tongs, tweezers, fishing rod, jhaadu, forearm |

**Yaad karne ka tarika:** jo **beech mein** ho, wo class ka naam batata hai: **F** beech mein = first, **L** beech mein = second, **E** beech mein = third. (F, L, E: 1, 2, 3, ye order yaad rakho.)

## Part E: Solved examples

**Example 1 (moment nikalna):** Ek spanner par 40 N ka force nut se 25 cm door, seedhe 90° par lagta hai. Moment kitna?

- Doori ko metre mein badlo: 25 cm = 25 ÷ 100 = 0.25 m
- Moment M = F × d = 40 N × 0.25 m = **10 N m**

**Jaanch:** 40 × 0.25 = 10 ✅. Agar wahi 40 N ko 50 cm (0.5 m) par lagate to moment 40 × 0.5 = 20 N m hota: doori double, moment double ✅

**Example 2 (effort nikalna):** Ek lever mein load 600 N hai, jo fulcrum se 0.2 m par hai. Effort fulcrum se 1.2 m par lagta hai. Effort aur MA batao.

- Principle of moments: L × l = E × e
- 600 N × 0.2 m = E × 1.2 m
- 120 N m = E × 1.2 m
- E = 120 N m ÷ 1.2 m = **100 N**
- MA = L ÷ E = 600 N ÷ 100 N = **6**

**Jaanch:** MA = effort arm ÷ load arm = 1.2 m ÷ 0.2 m = 6 ✅ (dono tarike se 6 aaya).

**Example 3 (effort arm ki lambai):** Ek crowbar se 900 N ka load uthana hai. Load arm 0.1 m hai, aur hum 150 N ka effort laga sakte hain. Effort arm kitna lamba hona chahiye?

- L × l = E × e
- 900 N × 0.1 m = 150 N × e
- 90 N m = 150 N × e
- e = 90 N m ÷ 150 N = **0.6 m**
- MA = 900 N ÷ 150 N = 6

**Jaanch:** 150 N × 0.6 m = 90 N m, aur 900 N × 0.1 m = 90 N m: dono barabar ✅. MA = 0.6 m ÷ 0.1 m = 6 ✅

**Example 4 (metre rule par do masses):** Ek 100 cm ki metre rule uske 50 cm wale nishan par pivot ki gayi hai. 50 g ka mass 35 cm wale nishan par latka hai. Doosri taraf 20 g ka mass kis nishan par latkayein ki rule balance ho? (Rule ka weight ignore karo.)

- Weights: 50 g = 0.05 kg, to W₁ = 0.05 kg × 10 m/s² = **0.5 N**. 20 g = 0.02 kg, to W₂ = 0.02 kg × 10 m/s² = **0.2 N**.
- 50 g pivot se 50 cm − 35 cm = 15 cm = **0.15 m** door hai.
- Anticlockwise moment (bayein taraf) = 0.5 N × 0.15 m = **0.075 N m**
- Doosri taraf: 0.2 N × d = 0.075 N m, to d = 0.075 N m ÷ 0.2 N = **0.375 m = 37.5 cm**
- Ye pivot (50 cm) se 37.5 cm dayein, to nishan = 50 + 37.5 = **87.5 cm**

**Jaanch:** 0.2 N × 0.375 m = 0.075 N m, aur 0.5 N × 0.15 m = 0.075 N m ✅. Sidhe grams se bhi dekho: 50 g × 15 cm = 750 g cm, 20 g × 37.5 cm = 750 g cm ✅ (dono taraf ke units same hone chahiye, tabhi ye shortcut chalta hai).

**Example 5 (Maya ka see-saw):** Ab kahani wapas. Park ka see-saw 4 m lamba hai, beech mein fulcrum. Papa ka mass **80 kg** hai, wo fulcrum se **0.5 m** par baithe hain. Bachche ka mass **20 kg** hai. Bachcha fulcrum se kitni door baithe ki see-saw balance ho?

- Papa ka weight = 80 kg × 10 m/s² = **800 N**
- Bachche ka weight = 20 kg × 10 m/s² = **200 N**
- Papa ka moment (ek taraf) = 800 N × 0.5 m = **400 N m**
- Bachche ka moment (doosri taraf) = 200 N × d
- Balance ke liye: 200 N × d = 400 N m
- d = 400 N m ÷ 200 N = **2 m**

```
 bachcha (200 N)                 papa (800 N)
      ↓                              ↓
      ═════════════════════════════════════════════════
      :<───────── 2 m ───────>:<0.5m>:
                              ^
                           fulcrum
```

> **Figure 3.6** – Park ka see-saw: bachcha 2 m par aur papa 0.5 m par, aur dono ke moment 400 N m ke barabar hain.

**Jaanch:** 800 N × 0.5 m = 400 N m aur 200 N × 2 m = 400 N m ✅. 4 m wale see-saw ka sira 2 m par hi hai, to bachcha bilkul sire par baitha hai, jaisa kahani mein tha ✅.

Mr. Rastogi ne Maya se kaha, "Dekha? Papa 4 guna bhaari hain, par bachcha 4 guna **door** baitha hai. Weight × doori dono taraf barabar, isliye balance."

**Example 6 (beam par do loads, unknown weight):** Ek halki beam (jiska apna weight ignore hai) ke fulcrum se 0.4 m bayein taraf 60 N ka weight latka hai. Fulcrum se 0.8 m dayein taraf kitna weight latkayein ki beam balance rahe? Fulcrum ko kitna upar ka force (support) dena padega?

- Bayein taraf (anticlockwise) moment = 60 N × 0.4 m = **24 N m**
- Dayein taraf (clockwise) moment = W × 0.8 m
- Principle of moments: W × 0.8 m = 24 N m, to W = 24 N m ÷ 0.8 m = **30 N**
- Pivot ko dono weights ko saath saath sambhalna hai: support force = 60 N + 30 N = **90 N** (upar ki taraf).

```
     60 N                               30 N
      ↓                                   ↓
 ═══════════════════════════════════════
  :<─ 0.4 m ─>:<──────── 0.8 m ───────>
              ^
         pivot (support 90 N)
```

> **Figure 3.7** – Beam par do weights: dono ke moment (24 N m) barabar, aur pivot ka support force total weight (90 N) ke barabar.

**Jaanch:** 60 × 0.4 = 24 aur 30 × 0.8 = 24 ✅. Beam ko neeche ki taraf total force 60 + 30 = 90 N hai, to support 90 N hi chahiye ✅.

**Example 7 (kai logon ka see-saw, net moment):** Ek see-saw par fulcrum ke bayein taraf do bachche baithe hain: 30 kg wala fulcrum se 2 m par, 40 kg wala 1 m par. Dayein taraf 50 kg wala 1.5 m par baitha hai. Kaun si taraf neeche jayegi? Balance karne ke liye 50 kg wale ko kahan baithna padega?

- Weights: 30 kg = 300 N, 40 kg = 400 N, 50 kg = 500 N.
- Bayein (anticlockwise) moments: 300 N × 2 m = 600 N m, aur 400 N × 1 m = 400 N m. Jod = 600 + 400 = **1000 N m**
- Dayein (clockwise) moment: 500 N × 1.5 m = **750 N m**
- Net moment = 1000 N m − 750 N m = **250 N m anticlockwise**
- Anticlockwise bada hai, to **bayein taraf neeche jayegi**.
- Balance ke liye 500 N × d = 1000 N m, to d = 1000 N m ÷ 500 N = **2 m**

**Jaanch:** 500 × 2 = 1000 ✅ aur pehle wala 500 × 1.5 = 750, 1000 − 750 = 250 ✅. Balance par dono taraf 1000 N m ✅. **Net moment** ka sense batata hai ki lever kis taraf ghoomega.

**Example 8 (wheelbarrow: second class):** Ek wheelbarrow mein 500 N ka load pahiya (fulcrum) se 0.5 m par rakha hai. Handles pahiya se 1.5 m par hain. Effort aur MA?

- L × l = E × e
- 500 N × 0.5 m = E × 1.5 m
- 250 N m = E × 1.5 m
- E = 250 N m ÷ 1.5 m = **166.7 N** (lagbhag)
- MA = 500 N ÷ 166.7 N = **3**

**Jaanch:** MA = e ÷ l = 1.5 m ÷ 0.5 m = 3 ✅. Second class lever mein effort arm load arm se lamba hai, to MA > 1 ✅.

**Example 9 (forearm: third class):** Insaan ki kalayi (forearm) mein kohni (elbow) fulcrum hai. Bicep muscle ka effort kohni se 4 cm par lagta hai, aur haath mein 40 N ka load kohni se 30 cm par hai. Effort kitna lagega? MA?

- Doori m mein: 4 cm = 0.04 m, 30 cm = 0.30 m
- L × l = E × e
- 40 N × 0.30 m = E × 0.04 m
- 12 N m = E × 0.04 m
- E = 12 N m ÷ 0.04 m = **300 N**
- MA = 40 N ÷ 300 N = **0.133** (lagbhag)

**Jaanch:** MA = e ÷ l = 4 cm ÷ 30 cm = 0.133 ✅. MA 1 se kam hai (third class), to effort load se bahut zyaada hai: 300 N > 40 N ✅. Lekin kya faayda? Muscle sirf thoda sa sikudti hai, aur haath kai guna zyaada door tak ghoom jata hai: **range aur speed** ka faayda.

**Example 10 (kainchi ka MA):** Ek kainchi mein effort arm (haath se pivot tak) 10 cm hai aur load arm (kapde ke cutting point se pivot tak) 2 cm hai. MA? Agar kapde ko katne ke liye 20 N ka force chahiye, to effort kitna?

- MA = e ÷ l = 10 cm ÷ 2 cm = **5** (cm ka ratio hai, to cm to m badalna zaroori nahi)
- MA = L ÷ E, to E = L ÷ MA = 20 N ÷ 5 = **4 N**

**Jaanch:** L × l = 20 N × 0.02 m = 0.4 N m, aur E × e = 4 N × 0.10 m = 0.4 N m ✅.

**Example 11 (10 auzaaron ki class):** Neeche diye gaye auzaaron ko class mein baantho:

| Auzaar | Fulcrum kahan | Load kahan | Effort kahan | Class | MA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| See-saw | Beech | Ek sira | Doosra sira | First | Arms par depend |
| Kainchi | Pivot screw | Cutting point | Haath | First | Aam taur par > 1 |
| Crowbar (beech mein tika) | Tikne ka point | Patthar ke paas | Lamba haath wala sira | First | > 1 |
| Pliers | Pivot | Jaw ke paas | Haath | First | Aam taur par > 1 |
| Beam balance | Beech ka knife edge | Ek pallu | Doosra pallu | First | 1 (barabar arms) |
| Claw hammer (keel nikalna) | Sir ka wo hissa jo lakdi par tika hai | Keel | Haath | First | > 1 |
| Wheelbarrow | Pahiya | Beech mein load | Handles | Second | > 1 |
| Nutcracker | Hinge | Akhrot beech mein | Haath | Second | > 1 |
| Bottle opener | Cap ke upar ka door wala kinara | Cap ka paas wala kinara (jahan opener phansa hai) | Haath | Second | > 1 |
| Tongs (chimta) | Pivot | Pakda hua angaara | Beech mein haath | Third | < 1 |

Har auzaar ki class pehchanne ke liye dekho ki **kaun beech mein hai**. Nutcracker ke liye numbers: effort arm 12 cm, load arm 3 cm, to MA = 12 cm ÷ 3 cm = **4**. Tongs ke liye: effort arm 8 cm, load arm 20 cm, to MA = 8 cm ÷ 20 cm = **0.4**.

**Jaanch:** Nutcracker: 12 ÷ 3 = 4 > 1, effort arm lamba ✅. Tongs: 8 ÷ 20 = 0.4 < 1, effort arm chhota ✅. Beam balance: dono arms barabar, to MA = 1 ✅.

**Example 12 (ideal lever: MA = VR):** Ek ideal lever mein effort arm 1.2 m aur load arm 0.2 m hai (Example 2 wala lever). Jab effort wala sira 30 cm neeche jata hai, to load kitna uthta hai? VR aur MA?

- VR = effort arm ÷ load arm = 1.2 m ÷ 0.2 m = **6**
- Load ka distance = effort ka distance ÷ VR = 30 cm ÷ 6 = **5 cm**
- MA (Example 2 se) = 6

**Jaanch:** VR = MA = 6 ✅, jo ideal machine ka niyam hai. 100 N effort 30 cm chala, to effort ka kaam = 100 N × 0.30 m = 30 J; 600 N load 5 cm = 0.05 m uthta hai, to 600 N × 0.05 m = 30 J ✅. Dono barabar nikle: ideal machine mein work input = work output, jaisa Topic 2 mein dekha tha (iski poori wajah Topic 6 mein).

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Force tirchha ho to chhad ke saath naapi doori le lena**

Moment mein sirf **perpendicular doori** (pivot se force ki line tak ki seedhi 90° wali doori) ginti hai. Chhad par naapi hui lambai nahi (Figure 3.2).

**Galti 2: MA = effort arm ÷ load arm mein arms ulta kar dena**

MA = **effort arm ÷ load arm**. Example 2: 1.2 ÷ 0.2 = 6 hai, ulta 0.2 ÷ 1.2 = 0.167 nahi. Ek aasaan jaanch: crowbar jaisi lamba-effort-wali lever mein MA bada hona chahiye. Agar answer 1 se chhota aaya aur lever aisi hai, to arms ulte ho gaye.

**Galti 3: cm ko m mein badalna bhool jaana**

Example 1 mein 40 N × 25 cm = 1000 N cm hota. Ise "1000 N m" likhna galat hai. Sahi: 25 cm = 0.25 m, to 40 × 0.25 = **10 N m**. N m chahiye to doori hamesha m mein lo. (Do arms ka ratio lena ho, to dono cm mein chalte hain, jaise Example 10.)

**Galti 4: Third class lever ko bekaar samajhna**

Forearm aur tongs ka MA 1 se kam hai (Example 9: 0.133), par ye **speed aur range** dete hain. Muscle thoda sa sikudti hai, to haath door tak aur tezi se ghoomta hai. Isliye ye **speed multiplier** hain: MA < 1 matlab "force nuksaan", par "doori aur speed faayda".

**Galti 5: Moment aur force ko ek hi cheez samajhna**

Force newton (N) mein hai, moment N m mein. 800 N ka force aur 400 N m ka moment alag cheezein hain. Dekho Example 5: papa ka force 800 N, par moment 800 N × 0.5 m = 400 N m. Bachche ka force bahut chhota (200 N), par moment (200 N × 2 m = 400 N m) papa ke barabar. Balance **moments** ka hota hai, forces ka nahi.

**Galti 6: grams aur newton mix kar dena**

50 g ko seedha "50 N" mat likho. 50 g = 0.05 kg, weight = 0.05 kg × 10 m/s² = **0.5 N**. Example 4 ki Jaanch mein dekha: grams aur cm mein dono taraf ke moments barabar ho sakte hain, par wo sirf barabari dekhne ke liye hai. Jab moment N m mein maanga ho, to hamesha pehle g → kg → N karo, aur cm → m.

## Quick Recap

- **Lever:** ek sakht chhad jo ek fixed point **fulcrum** ke around ghoom sakti hai. Isme **effort (E)** aur **load (L)** lagte hain.
- **Effort arm / load arm:** fulcrum se effort / load ki line tak ki **perpendicular** doori.
- **Moment of force = force × perpendicular doori** (N m). Clockwise aur anticlockwise, dono sense ke moments hote hain.
- **Principle of moments:** balance mein **clockwise moments ka jod = anticlockwise moments ka jod**.
- **Balanced lever:** L × l = E × e, to **MA = L ÷ E = effort arm ÷ load arm**. Ideal lever mein **MA = VR**.
- Lambi effort arm = zyaada MA (crowbar, spanner), par effort ko zyaada doori chalna padta hai.
- **Teen class:** First (F beech mein): MA kuch bhi; Second (L beech mein): MA hamesha > 1; Third (E beech mein): MA hamesha < 1, par speed aur range milti hai.
- **Maya ka see-saw:** 80 kg papa 0.5 m par (800 N × 0.5 m = 400 N m) aur 20 kg bachcha 2 m par (200 N × 2 m = 400 N m): balance.
- Beam, plank aur lever ka apna weight tab tak ignore, jab tak sawal mein bataya na jaye. g = 10 m/s².
- Net moment ka sense batata hai ki lever kis taraf ghoomega (Example 7: 250 N m anticlockwise).

## Practice Questions

1. A force of 30 N acts at right angles to a door at a distance of 40 cm from the hinge. Find its moment about the hinge in N m. If the same force acts at 20 cm from the hinge, what is the moment?

2. A lever has a load of 360 N at 0.25 m from the fulcrum, and the effort acts at 1.5 m from the fulcrum on the other side. Find (a) the effort needed, (b) the mechanical advantage.

3. A crowbar must lift a 1200 N load. The load arm is 20 cm and the effort available is 150 N. Find (a) the length of the effort arm in metres, (b) the MA of the crowbar.

4. A metre rule is pivoted at its 50 cm mark. A 60 g mass hangs at the 20 cm mark. At which mark should a 40 g mass hang so that the rule balances? Take g = 10 m/s² and ignore the weight of the rule.

5. On a see-saw, a child of mass 25 kg sits 2.4 m from the fulcrum. At what distance on the other side must a child of mass 30 kg sit to balance it? Take g = 10 m/s².

6. Classify each tool as a first, second or third class lever: scissors, tongs, wheelbarrow, claw hammer (removing a nail), tweezers, fishing rod, bottle opener, beam balance.

7. In a wheelbarrow, the load of 600 N is 0.6 m from the wheel axle, and the handles are 1.8 m from the axle. Find the effort and the MA. Why is its MA always more than 1?

8. In a third class lever, a load of 20 N is at 0.8 m from the fulcrum and the effort is applied at 0.2 m from the fulcrum. Find the effort and the MA. Why is such a lever still useful?

9. A light beam is pivoted at its centre. On the left, weights of 20 N at 0.5 m and 30 N at 0.2 m from the pivot are hung. On the right, a 40 N weight hangs at 0.5 m from the pivot. Find the net moment and say which side goes down.

10. State True or False:
    (a) Moment of force is measured in newtons.
    (b) A third class lever always has a mechanical advantage less than 1.
    (c) For a lever to balance, the load must equal the effort.
    (d) A longer effort arm gives a larger MA.
    (e) For a slanted force, the arm is measured along the beam.

11. A 50 N force turns a spanner at 20 cm from the nut. How much force would be needed at 40 cm to produce the same moment? Explain in one line why a longer spanner helps.

### Answers

1. 40 cm = 0.4 m. Moment = 30 N × 0.4 m = **12 N m**. 20 cm = 0.2 m: moment = 30 N × 0.2 m = **6 N m**.
   Jaanch: 30 × 0.4 = 12 ✅, 30 × 0.2 = 6 ✅. Doori aadhi, to moment aadha ✅.

2. (a) L × l = E × e: 360 N × 0.25 m = E × 1.5 m, to 90 N m = E × 1.5 m, E = 90 N m ÷ 1.5 m = **60 N**. (b) MA = 360 N ÷ 60 N = **6**.
   Jaanch: MA = 1.5 m ÷ 0.25 m = 6 ✅.

3. (a) Load arm = 20 cm = 0.2 m. L × l = 1200 N × 0.2 m = 240 N m. E × e = 240 N m, to e = 240 N m ÷ 150 N = **1.6 m**. (b) MA = 1200 N ÷ 150 N = **8**.
   Jaanch: e ÷ l = 1.6 m ÷ 0.2 m = 8 ✅.

4. 60 g = 0.06 kg, weight = 0.06 kg × 10 m/s² = 0.6 N. 40 g = 0.04 kg, weight = 0.4 N. 60 g pivot se 50 − 20 = 30 cm = 0.3 m door hai. Moment = 0.6 N × 0.3 m = 0.18 N m. Doosri taraf: 0.4 N × d = 0.18 N m, to d = 0.18 N m ÷ 0.4 N = 0.45 m = 45 cm. Nishan = 50 + 45 = **95 cm**.
   Jaanch: 0.4 × 0.45 = 0.18 ✅; 60 g × 30 cm = 1800 g cm, 40 g × 45 cm = 1800 g cm ✅.

5. Weights: 25 kg = 250 N, 30 kg = 300 N. Moment of first child = 250 N × 2.4 m = 600 N m. Doosra: 300 N × d = 600 N m, to d = 600 N m ÷ 300 N = **2 m**.
   Jaanch: 300 × 2 = 600 ✅. Bhaari bachcha kam door baithta hai ✅.

6. Scissors: **first**. Tongs: **third**. Wheelbarrow: **second**. Claw hammer (nail removal): **first**. Tweezers: **third**. Fishing rod: **third**. Bottle opener: **second**. Beam balance: **first**.
   Jaanch: pehchaan ka tarika: beech mein F = first, L = second, E = third ✅.

7. L × l = 600 N × 0.6 m = 360 N m. E × 1.8 m = 360 N m, to E = 360 N m ÷ 1.8 m = **200 N**. MA = 600 N ÷ 200 N = **3**. Wheelbarrow second class hai: load fulcrum (pahiya) aur effort (handles) ke beech mein hota hai, isliye effort arm (1.8 m) hamesha load arm (0.6 m) se lamba hai, to MA > 1.
   Jaanch: 1.8 ÷ 0.6 = 3 ✅.

8. L × l = 20 N × 0.8 m = 16 N m. E × 0.2 m = 16 N m, to E = 16 N m ÷ 0.2 m = **80 N**. MA = 20 N ÷ 80 N = **0.25**. Ye lever bekaar nahi: effort chhoti si doori chalta hai aur load bahut zyaada doori (aur tezi se) tak chalta hai, yaani ye **speed multiplier** hai.
   Jaanch: e ÷ l = 0.2 ÷ 0.8 = 0.25 ✅.

9. Left (anticlockwise): 20 N × 0.5 m = 10 N m, aur 30 N × 0.2 m = 6 N m, jod = 16 N m. Right (clockwise): 40 N × 0.5 m = 20 N m. Net moment = 20 − 16 = **4 N m clockwise**, to **right side neeche** jayegi.
   Jaanch: 10 + 6 = 16 ✅, 20 − 16 = 4 ✅.

10. (a) **False** ❌. Moment N m mein naapa jata hai; newton force ki unit hai.
    (b) **True** ✅. Third class mein effort arm load arm se chhota hai, to MA = effort arm ÷ load arm < 1.
    (c) **False** ❌. Moments barabar hone chahiye (L × l = E × e), force nahi. Arms alag hon to L aur E alag hote hain.
    (d) **True** ✅. MA = effort arm ÷ load arm, to effort arm badhne se MA badhta hai.
    (e) **False** ❌. Slanted force mein arm force ki line tak ki seedhi (perpendicular) doori hoti hai.

11. Moment = 50 N × 0.2 m = 10 N m. 40 cm = 0.4 m par F × 0.4 m = 10 N m, to F = 10 N m ÷ 0.4 m = **25 N**. Lambi spanner mein effort arm lamba hai, to wahi moment kam force se mil jata hai.
    Jaanch: 25 × 0.4 = 10 ✅.
