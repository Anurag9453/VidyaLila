# Topic 4: Pulley

Pichhle topic mein humne lever dekha: ek **bar** aur ek **pivot (fulcrum)**. Bar ko pivot ke upar ghumaya aur chhota effort lagakar bhaari load utha liya. Ab ek bilkul alag shakal ki machine dekhte hain: bar aur pivot ki jagah ek **pahiya (wheel)** aur ek **rassi (rope)**. Is machine ka naam hai **pulley**. Ye kuein se paani kheenchne se lekar bade cranes tak har jagah milti hai. Topic 2 ke teen shabd (MA, VR aur efficiency) yahan bhi wahi kaam karenge, to unhe ek baar yaad kar lena.

**Is poore topic mein g = 10 m/s² liya gaya hai**, to 1 kg ka weight 10 N hota hai.

## Part A: Pulley kya hai

**Pulley** ek **grooved wheel** hota hai, yaani aisa pahiya jiske kinare par ek naali (groove) bani hoti hai taaki rassi phisle nahi. Ye pahiya apni **axle** par bina atke ghoomta rehta hai. Rassi (ya string) isi naali mein se guzarti hai: ek sire par load, doosre sire par hum.

Rassi pulley par se guzarte waqt **tension** (kheenchav) ek jaisa rakhti hai: agar friction na ho, to rassi ke har hisse mein wahi force hota hai. Pulley ke chaar roop hote hain, aur har ek ka alag kaam hai:

1. Single fixed pulley
2. Single movable pulley
3. Fixed aur movable ki combination
4. Block and tackle

Ab ek ek karke dekhte hain.

---

## Part B: Single fixed pulley

**Fixed pulley** kisi support (jaise flagpole ya chhat ki beam) se judi hoti hai. Rassi ke ek sire par load, doosre sire par effort. Jab hum effort neeche ki taraf kheenchte hain, to load upar jaata hai.

```
      ══════════════════════  support (pole ya chhat)
               ║
            ╭──┴──╮
      ┌─────┤  ○  ├─────┐
      ║     ╰─────╯     ║
      ║                 ║
      ║                 ║
   Effort F ↓        [ LOAD W ] ↑
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Single fixed pulley: rassi neeche kheenchne par load upar jaata hai. Pulley apni jagah par hi ghoomti hai.

### Fixed pulley kya karti hai aur kya nahi

- Load W ko sambhalne wali rassi mein jitna tension hai, utna hi effort F dena padta hai. Ideal (friction-free) pulley mein **F = W**.
- Isliye **ideal MA = W ÷ F = 1**.
- Load jitna upar jaata hai, effort wali rassi utni hi neeche aati hai. To **VR = effort distance ÷ load distance = 1**.
- Fixed pulley ka kaam sirf itna hai: **effort ki disha badal dena**. Upar uthana ho to neeche kheencho. Ye bahut kaam ka hai, kyunki neeche kheenchne mein hum apna **weight** bhi istemal kar sakte hain, aur zyada aasani se kheench sakte hain.

Yahi Kiaan ke pehle sawal ka jawab hai: **rassi neeche isliye kheenchni padi kyunki fixed pulley sirf disha badalti hai.** Jhanda upar jaana tha, aur khade-khade upar kheenchne se neeche kheenchna kahin zyada aasan hai.

### Friction aur real pulley

Asli pulley mein axle par thoda friction hota hai, aur rassi bhi thodi akad (stiff) hoti hai. Isliye load ko uthane ke liye effort load se **thoda zyada** lagta hai: F > W. To real **MA = W ÷ F < 1** aati hai. Fixed pulley ka MA 1 se **kam** hi rehta hai, kabhi zyada nahi.

## Part C: Single movable pulley

**Movable pulley** load ke saath hi upar-neeche chalti hai. Rassi ka ek sira support se **bandha** hota hai, rassi pulley ke neeche se guzarti hai aur doosra sira upar ki taraf kheencha jaata hai. Load pulley ke hook se latka hota hai.

```
     ═════════╗               Effort F ↑
    (support) ║ ①                      ║ ②
              ║                        ║
              ║                        ║
              ╰─────────( ○ )──────────╯  <- movable pulley
                          ║
                      [ LOAD W ]
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Single movable pulley: load ko rassi ke **do hisse** (① aur ②) sambhalte hain.

### Do segments, dugna faayda

Movable pulley ko do rassi-hisse (segments) upar kheench rahe hain: ek wo jo support se bandha hai (①), aur doosra jise hum kheench rahe hain (②). Rassi ke dono hisson mein tension ek jaisa hai. Agar tension T hai, to:

> 2 × T = W, yaani **T = W ÷ 2**

Effort F bhi wahi tension hai, to ideal case mein **F = W ÷ 2**. Isliye **ideal MA = W ÷ F = 2**.

Principle shabdon mein: **"2 rope segments load ko share karte hain, to har ek ko sirf load ÷ 2 sambhalna padta hai."**

### Effort dugni door chalta hai

Agar load 1 m upar jaana hai, to pulley ke dono taraf ki rassi 1 m chhoti honi chahiye: ① aur ② dono se 1 m rassi ghatni chahiye. Magar ① to support se bandha hai, wo apni jagah se hil nahi sakta. Isliye **poori ki poori 2 m rassi ② wale hisse se aani padegi**. To effort 2 m door chalta hai, jabki load sirf 1 m upar jaata hai.

> **VR = effort distance ÷ load distance = 2 m ÷ 1 m = 2**

Ideal machine mein **MA = VR = 2**. Is tarah ek ideal movable pulley effort ko aadha kar deti hai, magar uski keemat hai: effort ko **dugni door** chalna padta hai (Topic 2 wali baat: zor bachta hai, kaam nahi).

### Real movable pulley

Asli movable pulley mein do baatein dhyan rakhni padti hain:
- Pulley khud **bhaari** hoti hai: uska weight bhi rassi ke dono hisse mil kar uthate hain.
- Axle par thoda **friction** bhi hota hai.

Dono ki wajah se real effort ideal se zyada lagta hai, to **real MA < 2**, yaani 2 se kam. Neeche numerical example mein dekhenge.

## Part D: Fixed aur movable ki combination

Movable pulley ka faayda hai (MA = 2), par ek dikkat hai: effort **upar ki taraf** kheenchna padta hai, jo aksar thakane wala aur asuvidha wala hota hai. Hal ye hai: rassi ko ek **fixed pulley** ke upar se guzaar do, taaki effort **neeche ki taraf** lage.

```
     ═════╦═══════════════╦════════   support
          ║               ║
          ║             ( A )           A = fixed pulley
          ║             ║   ║
          ║ ①         ② ║   ║ ③
          ║             ║   ▼ Effort F (neeche)
          ║             ║
          ╰─────( B )───╯
                  ║
             [ LOAD W ]                 B = movable pulley
```

> **Figure 4.3** – Fixed (A) aur movable (B) pulley ki combination: movable pulley ko rassi ke **do hisse** (① aur ②) sambhalte hain, aur effort ③ neeche ki taraf lagta hai. ③ ko count nahi karte.

Is system mein:
- Segment ① aur ② movable pulley B se jude hain: **2 supporting segments**.
- Segment ③ sirf hamare haath tak aata hai aur B ko nahi sambhalta, isliye ise **count nahi karte**.
- Isliye **ideal MA = 2** aur **ideal VR = 2**.

Faayda: MA movable wala hi (2), aur effort ki disha **convenient** (neeche).

## Part E: Block and tackle

Jab load bahut bhaari ho, to ek se zyada pulleys ek hi rassi mein lagakar bahut bada MA paaya ja sakta hai. Ise **block and tackle** kehte hain.

Ek **upar ka fixed block** (jisme kai pulleys hote hain) support se judta hai, aur ek **neeche ka movable block** load ke saath chalta hai. Ek lambi rassi dono blocks ke pulleys par baari-baari se lipti hoti hai.

```
   ═══════════════╦═══════════════   support
            ╔═════╩═════════╗
            ║   ○     ○     ║   upar ka block (fixed): 2 pulleys
            ╚═╤══╤══╤══╤══╤═╝
              ║  ║  ║  ║  ║
              ①  ②  ③  ④  ║     <- 4 supporting segments
              ║  ║  ║  ║  ▼ Effort F (ye sira count nahi hota)
            ╔═╧══╧══╧══╧═╗
            ║  ○     ○   ║      neeche ka block (movable): 2 pulleys
            ╚═════╦══════╝
                  ║
              [ LOAD W ]
```

> **Figure 4.4** – Block and tackle (n = 4): movable block ko rassi ke **4 hisse** (① se ④) sambhalte hain. Effort wala sira upar ke block se neeche aata hai, isliye gina nahi jaata.

### Principle

> **"n rope segments load ko share karte hain, to har segment ko sirf load ÷ n sambhalna padta hai."**

Isliye ideal case mein:

> **F = W ÷ n, ideal MA = n, ideal VR = n**

Jahan **n = movable block (aur load) ko sambhalne wale rope segments ki sankhya**.

- Agar load 1 m upar jaata hai, to rassi ke n hisson mein se har ek ko 1 m chhota hona hai: kul rassi **n × 1 m** effort ki taraf se aani chahiye. To effort **n guna door** chalta hai.
- n = 3 ke liye ideal MA = 3, n = 4 ke liye ideal MA = 4, aur isi tarah aage.

**Segments kaise gino:** seedha rule hai ki **movable block ko jitne rope-hisse upar kheench rahe hain, utne ginte hain.** Jo sira upar ke fixed block se neeche haath tak aata hai (jaise Figure 4.3 mein ③), wo movable block ko nahi kheenchta, isliye use nahi ginte. Haan, agar effort wala sira khud movable block ki taraf se upar kheencha jaa raha ho (jaise Figure 4.2 mein ②), to wo hissa gina jaata hai.

## Part F: Chaar roop ek table mein

| Type | Effort ki disha | Ideal MA | Ideal VR |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single fixed pulley | Neeche (disha badalti hai) | 1 | 1 |
| Single movable pulley | Upar | 2 | 2 |
| Fixed + movable combination | Neeche | 2 | 2 |
| Block and tackle (n segments) | Aam taur par neeche | n | n |

Real machine mein har jagah **MA < VR**, kyunki friction aur pulley ka weight kuch effort kha jaate hain. **Efficiency η = MA ÷ VR × 100%** (Topic 2).

## Part G: Solved examples

**Example 1 (fixed pulley, ideal):** Ek ideal fixed pulley se 200 N ka load uthana hai. Effort kitna lagega, aur MA kitna hoga?

- Fixed pulley mein F = W (ideal).
- F = **200 N**
- MA = W ÷ F = 200 N ÷ 200 N = **1**

**Jaanch:** Ideal fixed pulley mein MA = VR = 1 hota hai, jo hamare MA se milta hai ✅

**Example 2 (fixed pulley, friction ke saath):** Ek real fixed pulley se 200 N load uthane ke liye 250 N effort lagana padta hai. MA aur efficiency batao.

- MA = W ÷ F = 200 N ÷ 250 N = **0.8**
- VR = 1 (fixed pulley)
- η = MA ÷ VR × 100% = 0.8 ÷ 1 × 100% = **80%**

**Jaanch:** MA = 0.8 < 1, jo real fixed pulley ke liye sahi hai. Aur 200 ÷ 250 = 0.8 ✅

**Example 3 (movable pulley, ideal):** Ek ideal single movable pulley se 400 N ka load uthana hai. Effort kitna lagega?

- Ideal MA = 2, to F = W ÷ 2.
- F = 400 N ÷ 2 = **200 N**

**Jaanch:** MA = W ÷ F = 400 N ÷ 200 N = 2, jo VR = 2 ke barabar hai ✅

**Example 4 (effort ne kitni door rassi kheenchi):** Ek single movable pulley se load ko 1.5 m upar uthana hai. Effort wali rassi kitni door kheenchni padegi?

- VR = 2, to effort distance = VR × load distance.
- Effort distance = 2 × 1.5 m = **3 m**

**Jaanch:** VR = 3 m ÷ 1.5 m = 2 ✅

**Example 5 (pulley ka weight bhi ginna ho):** 400 N ka load ek movable pulley se uthana hai. Pulley ka apna weight 20 N hai. Friction ko ignore karte hue, effort kitna lagega? (Maan lo load aur pulley dono ko rassi ke 2 hisse sambhalte hain.)

- Rassi ko kul bhaar uthana hai = load + pulley ka weight = 400 N + 20 N = 420 N.
- Do hisse share karte hain: F = 420 N ÷ 2 = **210 N**
- Ye ideal 200 N (Example 3) se zyada hai.
- Real MA = W ÷ F = 400 N ÷ 210 N = **1.90** (lagbhag)
- η = MA ÷ VR × 100% = 1.90 ÷ 2 × 100% = **95.2%**

**Jaanch:** 210 N × 2 = 420 N ✅. MA 2 se kam aayi, jaisa real pulley mein hona chahiye ✅. Seedhe naap kar: 400 ÷ 210 = 1.9048, aur 1.9048 ÷ 2 = 0.9524, to 95.2% ✅

**Example 6 (block and tackle, n = 4, ideal):** Ek ideal block and tackle mein movable block ko 4 rassi-hisse sambhalte hain. 800 N ka load uthana hai. Effort aur MA batao.

- Ideal MA = n = 4.
- F = W ÷ n = 800 N ÷ 4 = **200 N**
- MA = 800 N ÷ 200 N = **4**

**Jaanch:** 200 N × 4 = 800 N ✅

**Example 7 (n = 3 ginna):** Ek ideal block and tackle se 900 N ka load 300 N effort se uth jaata hai. Movable block ko kitne rope-segments sambhal rahe hain?

- n = ideal MA = W ÷ F = 900 N ÷ 300 N = **3 segments**
- (Agar load 600 N aur effort 150 N hota, to n = 600 ÷ 150 = 4.)

**Jaanch:** 900 N ÷ 3 = 300 N ✅

**Example 8 (real effort, efficiency 80%):** Example 6 wale block and tackle (n = 4, VR = 4) ki efficiency 80% hai. 800 N load ke liye real effort kitna lagega?

- MA = η × VR = 0.80 × 4 = **3.2**
- Effort F = W ÷ MA = 800 N ÷ 3.2 = **250 N**

**Jaanch:** 250 N × 3.2 = 800 N ✅. Ye ideal 200 N se 50 N zyada hai ✅

**Example 9 (movable pulley ka work input aur output):** Ek single movable pulley (VR = 2) se 400 N ka load 1 m upar uthaya jaata hai, yaani effort rassi ko 2 m kheenchna padta hai. (a) Ideal case mein effort 200 N hota hai. (b) Real case mein effort 220 N lagta hai. Dono mein work input, work output aur efficiency nikalo.

- Work output (load par) = W × load distance = 400 N × 1 m = **400 J**

**(a) Ideal:**
- Work input = F × effort distance = 200 N × 2 m = **400 J**
- Efficiency = 400 J ÷ 400 J × 100% = **100%**

**(b) Real:**
- Work input = 220 N × 2 m = **440 J**
- Efficiency = work output ÷ work input × 100% = 400 J ÷ 440 J × 100% = **90.9%** (lagbhag)
- Kuch bhi muft nahi: 440 J − 400 J = **40 J** extra input friction aur pulley ke weight ko harane mein gaya.

**Jaanch:** MA = 400 N ÷ 220 N = 1.818; η = MA ÷ VR × 100% = 1.818 ÷ 2 × 100% = 90.9%, jo work wale tareeke se match karta hai ✅

**Example 10 (measure kiye hue data se efficiency):** Ek block and tackle se 600 N load ko 0.5 m upar uthane ke liye 250 N ka effort lagaya gaya, aur effort wali rassi 1.5 m kheenchni padi. Efficiency nikalo.

- Work output = 600 N × 0.5 m = **300 J**
- Work input = 250 N × 1.5 m = **375 J**
- η = 300 J ÷ 375 J × 100% = **80%**
- Dusra tareeka: MA = 600 N ÷ 250 N = 2.4, VR = 1.5 m ÷ 0.5 m = 3, to η = 2.4 ÷ 3 × 100% = 80%.

**Jaanch:** Dono tareeke se 80% aaya, aur VR = 3 se pata chalta hai ki ye n = 3 wala system hai ✅

**Example 11 (support par force, simple baat):** 400 N ka load ek ideal single movable pulley se latka hua hai (Figure 4.2), aur effort 200 N laga hua hai. Rassi ka bandha hua sira support par kitna force lagata hai?

- Rassi ke dono hisson mein tension 200 N hai.
- Bandhe hue sire par support ko rassi **200 N** se kheench rahi hai, yaani movable pulley ka **aadha load** support sambhalta hai. Baaki aadha (200 N) effort sambhalta hai.
- Dono ka jod = 200 N + 200 N = 400 N = load.

**Jaanch:** 200 N + 200 N = 400 N ✅

## Part H: Kahani ab numbers ke saath

### 1. Jhanda (fixed pulley)

Maan lo jhande aur uski rassi ka milakar weight 20 N hai, aur use 6 m ki unchai tak chadhana hai. Pulley mein friction hone se Kiaan ko 25 N ka effort lagana padta hai.

- Effort distance = load distance (VR = 1) = **6 m**
- MA = 20 N ÷ 25 N = **0.8**
- Work input = 25 N × 6 m = **150 J**
- Work output = 20 N × 6 m = **120 J**
- η = 120 J ÷ 150 J × 100% = **80%**

**Jaanch:** MA ÷ VR = 0.8 ÷ 1 = 0.8, yaani 80% ✅

Kiaan ne dekha ki effort (25 N) jhande ke weight (20 N) se bhi **zyada** laga. "Sir, matlab pulley ne meri taaqat bachayi nahi!" Mr. Bajwa ne kaha, "Bilkul. Pulley ne sirf **disha** badli: tumne neeche kheencha, jhanda upar gaya. Taaqat bachane ka kaam fixed pulley karti hi nahi."

### 2. Speaker (fixed + movable, double pulley)

Phir stage par 300 N (30 kg) ka speaker 1.2 m ki unchai tak chadhana tha. Mr. Bajwa ne ek **double pulley** system lagaya: ek fixed pulley upar, ek movable pulley speaker ke saath (Figure 4.3). Movable block ko 2 rope-segments sambhalte hain, to VR = 2.

**Ideal case:**
- F = W ÷ 2 = 300 N ÷ 2 = **150 N**

**Real case, efficiency 88%:**
- MA = η × VR = 0.88 × 2 = **1.76**
- Effort F = W ÷ MA = 300 N ÷ 1.76 = 170.45 N, yaani lagbhag **170 N**
- Effort distance = VR × load distance = 2 × 1.2 m = **2.4 m**
- Work input = 170 N × 2.4 m = **408 J**
- Work output = 300 N × 1.2 m = **360 J**
- η = 360 J ÷ 408 J × 100% = **88.2%** (lagbhag 88%)

**Jaanch:** 170 N × 1.76 = 299.2 N, lagbhag 300 N ✅. Ideal 150 N, real lagbhag 170 N: real hamesha zyada ✅

Kiaan aur Mr. Bajwa ne mil kar rassi neeche kheenchi. Speaker upar chadh gaya, aur Kiaan bola, "Pehle jhande ke liye maine 25 N lagaya jo jhande ke weight se zyada tha. Ab 300 N ka speaker sirf 170 N ke effort se chadh gaya! Fixed se disha mili, movable se taaqat!" Mr. Bajwa hans pade, "Bas yehi hai pulleys ka khel."

## Part I: Pulley kahan kaam aati hai

- **Kuein (well):** bucket ko neeche kheenchkar upar laana: fixed pulley.
- **Flagpole:** jhanda upar chadhane ke liye: fixed pulley.
- **Construction cranes:** bhaari saman ya bricks ko upar uthane ke liye block and tackle: bahut chhote effort se bada load.
- **Curtain rails:** pardon ko khinchne aur khulne ke liye chhoti pulleys.
- **Elevators (lifts):** lift ki cabin ko rassi (steel cable) aur pulleys se upar-neeche kiya jaata hai.
- **Sailing boats:** baadbaan (sail) ko upar chadhane aur kasne ke liye rassiyon aur pulleys ka use hota hai.

Har jagah wahi do kaam: **disha badalna** (fixed pulley) aur **effort ghataana** (movable pulley ya block and tackle).

---

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Fixed pulley effort ghatati hai"**

Nahi. Example 1 mein 200 N ke load ke liye effort 200 N hi laga, aur Example 2 mein real pulley mein to 250 N laga. Fixed pulley sirf **disha** badalti hai; MA = 1 (ya real mein 1 se kam).

**Galti 2: Supporting segments ginte waqt effort wala sira bhi gin lena**

Figure 4.3 mein segment ③ sirf hamare haath tak aata hai aur movable pulley ko nahi sambhalta. Wahan n = 2 hai, 3 nahi. Ginne ka rule: **sirf wo segments jo movable block ko kheench rahe hain.**

**Galti 3: Real machine se ideal MA ki ummeed karna**

Block and tackle (n = 4) ka ideal MA 4 hai, par 80% efficiency par real MA sirf 3.2 hai. 800 N load ke liye ideal effort 200 N tha (Example 6), par real effort 250 N laga (Example 8). Real MA hamesha ideal se kam hota hai.

**Galti 4: Ye bhool jaana ki effort n guna door chalta hai**

Movable pulley se 400 N ko sirf 200 N effort se uthaya, par load 1.5 m uthane ke liye rassi 3 m kheenchni padi (Example 4). Agar rassi 1.5 m hi kheenchi, to load sirf 0.75 m uthega. Force ki bachat distance mein chukani padti hai.

**Galti 5: Movable pulley ka apna weight ignore kar dena**

Example 5 mein 400 N load aur 20 N pulley ke liye effort 200 N nahi, 210 N laga. Pulley ka weight bhi rassi ko uthana padta hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Pulley** = grooved wheel jo axle par ghoomta hai, aur uske upar se rassi guzarti hai.
- **Single fixed pulley:** sirf **disha badalti hai**. Ideal MA = 1, VR = 1, F = W. Friction ke saath real MA < 1.
- **Single movable pulley:** load ko **2 rope segments** sambhalte hain. Ideal MA = 2, VR = 2, F = W ÷ 2. Effort load se **2 guna door** chalta hai. Real MA < 2 (friction aur pulley ka weight).
- **Fixed + movable:** MA = 2, aur effort **neeche** ki taraf lagta hai (convenient).
- **Block and tackle:** movable block ko n rope-segments sambhalte hain, to ideal MA = VR = n, F = W ÷ n. Effort n guna door chalta hai.
- **Principle:** n rope segments load ko share karte hain, to har ek ko load ÷ n sambhalna padta hai.
- **Segments ginna:** sirf wo jo movable block ko kheench rahe hain.
- **Real machine:** MA = η × VR. Jaise η = 80%, VR = 4 par MA = 3.2, aur 800 N load ke liye effort 250 N.
- **Kahani:** jhanda (fixed pulley): 20 N jhande ke liye 25 N effort, η = 80%. Speaker (double pulley): 300 N ke liye ideal 150 N, real lagbhag 170 N, η = 88%.
- **Uses:** kuein, flagpole, cranes, curtain rails, elevators, sailing boats.

## Practice Questions

1. An ideal single fixed pulley is used to lift a 350 N load. (a) What effort is needed? (b) What are its MA and VR?

2. An ideal single movable pulley is used to lift a 600 N load by 2 m. Find (a) the effort needed, (b) the distance moved by the effort.

3. An ideal block and tackle has 5 rope segments supporting the movable block. (a) What effort lifts a 1000 N load? (b) If its efficiency is 80%, what is the real MA and the real effort for the same load?

4. A student says, "A fixed pulley makes lifting easier because it reduces the force." Is the student right? What does a fixed pulley really do, and why is it still useful?

5. A block and tackle lifts a 500 N load through 2 m using an effort of 160 N. The effort moves 8 m. Find (a) the work input, (b) the work output, (c) the MA, (d) the VR, (e) the efficiency (to one decimal place).

6. An ideal block and tackle lifts a 1200 N load with a 300 N effort. How many rope segments support the movable block? If the real effort needed is 375 N, find the efficiency.

7. A movable pulley weighs 30 N and lifts a 570 N load. Ignoring friction, find the effort needed. Compare it with the effort needed if the pulley's weight is ignored.

8. State True or False:
   (a) The ideal MA of a single fixed pulley is 2.
   (b) In a single movable pulley, the effort moves twice as far as the load.
   (c) A real machine can have an MA greater than its VR.
   (d) In a block and tackle, you count all the rope segments, including the one on which you pull downward.

9. A fixed and a movable pulley are combined (as in Figure 4.3) to lift a 240 N load by 1.5 m. Ignoring friction and the weight of the pulleys, find (a) the effort, (b) the distance the effort rope is pulled, (c) the direction of the effort.

10. Name three places where pulleys are used. For each, say whether the pulley mainly changes the direction of the force or reduces the effort.

### Answers

1. (a) Ideal fixed pulley: F = W = **350 N**. (b) MA = W ÷ F = 350 N ÷ 350 N = **1**, VR = **1**.
   Jaanch: ideal machine mein MA = VR, dono 1 ✅

2. (a) Ideal MA = 2, to F = 600 N ÷ 2 = **300 N**. (b) VR = 2, to effort distance = 2 × 2 m = **4 m**.
   Jaanch: work input = 300 N × 4 m = 1200 J, work output = 600 N × 2 m = 1200 J ✅

3. (a) n = 5, to F = 1000 N ÷ 5 = **200 N**. (b) Real MA = η × VR = 0.80 × 5 = **4**, real effort = 1000 N ÷ 4 = **250 N**.
   Jaanch: 250 N × 4 = 1000 N ✅

4. Student **galat** hai. Fixed pulley force ko **kam nahi karti** (ideal MA = 1, F = W). Wo sirf **disha badalti hai**. Phir bhi kaam ki hai, kyunki neeche kheenchna upar kheenchne se aasan hai (hum apna weight use kar sakte hain), jaise jhanda chadhana ya kuein se paani nikalna.

5. (a) Work input = 160 N × 8 m = **1280 J**. (b) Work output = 500 N × 2 m = **1000 J**. (c) MA = 500 N ÷ 160 N = **3.125**. (d) VR = 8 m ÷ 2 m = **4**. (e) η = 1000 J ÷ 1280 J × 100% = **78.1%**.
   Jaanch: MA ÷ VR = 3.125 ÷ 4 = 0.78125, yaani 78.1% ✅

6. n = W ÷ F = 1200 N ÷ 300 N = **4 segments**. Real MA = 1200 N ÷ 375 N = 3.2, VR = 4, to η = 3.2 ÷ 4 × 100% = **80%**.
   Jaanch: 0.80 × 4 = 3.2, aur 1200 ÷ 3.2 = 375 N ✅

7. Pulley ka weight ginte hue: kul bhaar = 570 N + 30 N = 600 N, to F = 600 N ÷ 2 = **300 N**. Weight ignore karte to F = 570 N ÷ 2 = **285 N**. Pulley ka weight ginne par effort **15 N zyada** laga.
   Jaanch: 300 N × 2 = 600 N, aur 285 N × 2 = 570 N ✅

8. (a) **False** ❌. Fixed pulley ka ideal MA 1 hota hai.
   (b) **True** ✅. VR = 2.
   (c) **False** ❌. Real machine mein MA hamesha VR se kam hota hai (efficiency 100% se kam).
   (d) **False** ❌. Sirf wo segments ginte hain jo movable block ko kheench rahe hain.

9. n = 2, ideal. (a) F = 240 N ÷ 2 = **120 N**. (b) Effort distance = 2 × 1.5 m = **3 m**. (c) Effort **neeche** ki taraf lagta hai, kyunki rassi fixed pulley par se guzarti hai.
   Jaanch: work input = 120 N × 3 m = 360 J, work output = 240 N × 1.5 m = 360 J ✅
