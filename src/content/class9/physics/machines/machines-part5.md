# Topic 5: Inclined Plane, Wedge, Screw aur Wheel-and-Axle

Pichhle topic mein humne pulley dekhi: rassi aur chakke ka jod, jisme rassi ke kitne tukde load ko sambhal rahe hain, wahi ideal MA bata deta tha. Par simple machines ka parivaar sirf rassi aur chakke tak nahi rukta. Jab tum kisi ramp par chadhte ho, kisi bolt ko ghumate ho, ya darwaze ka handle ghumate ho, to tum bina jaane ek machine hi chala rahe ho. Is topic mein hum chaar aur simple machines dekhenge: **inclined plane (dhalaan), wedge (fanna), screw aur wheel-and-axle**. Chaaron mein wahi purana sawal rahega: kitna effort, kitna load, aur MA kitna.

**Is poore topic mein g = 10 m/s² liya gaya hai**, to 1 kg ka weight 10 N hota hai. Saare MA aur VR **ideal (bina friction ke)** liye gaye hain, jab tak alag se na bola jaye.

## Part A: Inclined plane (dhalaan)

### Dhalaan kya karti hai

**Inclined plane** ek **tirchhi sapaat surface** hai jiska ek sira neeche aur doosra sira ooncha hota hai. Load ko seedha **h** unchai tak uthane ke bajaye hum use **l** lambai ki dhalaan par chadhate hain. Dhalaan lambi aur halki hogi to rasta lamba, par force kam.

```
                              ╱│ ← truck ka floor (ooncha sira)
                            ╱  │
               l (plank)  ╱    │
                        ╱      │ h
                      ╱        │
                    ╱          │
 ─────────────────╱────────────┴─────────────
  zameen
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Plank ki lambai l hai (slanting) aur truck ke floor ki seedhi unchai h hai.

### Ideal MA = l ÷ h

Ideal (friction-free) dhalaan mein **work input = work output**. Hum effort ko dhalaan ke saath-saath l tak chalate hain, aur load seedhe h tak upar jaata hai.

- Work input = effort × l
- Work output = load × h

Dono barabar:

> **effort × l = load × h**

Dono taraf ko effort × h se baantne par:

> **MA = load ÷ effort = l ÷ h**

Aur effort l distance chalta hai jabki load h distance upar jaata hai, to **VR = l ÷ h** bhi hai. Isliye ideal incline mein **MA = VR = l ÷ h**.

Ek zaruri baat: yahan **l = dhalaan ki lambai** (slanting) aur **h = seedhi unchai** hai. Dono ko mat mila dena. Is topic mein hum kisi angle ka istemal nahi karenge, sirf l aur h.

### Ab Zeenat ka sawal solve karte hain

**Plank 1 (chhoti, steep):** l = 2 m, h = 1 m, load = 1200 N.

- MA = l ÷ h = 2 m ÷ 1 m = **2**
- Effort = load ÷ MA = 1200 N ÷ 2 = **600 N**

**Plank 2 (lambi, gentle):** l = 4 m, h = 1 m, load = 1200 N.

- MA = 4 m ÷ 1 m = **4**
- Effort = 1200 N ÷ 4 = **300 N**

Ab **work** dekhte hain:

- Plank 1: effort × l = 600 N × 2 m = **1200 J**
- Plank 2: effort × l = 300 N × 4 m = **1200 J**
- Seedha uthane par: load × h = 1200 N × 1 m = **1200 J**

**Jaanch:** teeno jagah 1200 J ✅. Lambi plank ne **zor aadha kar diya, par rasta double ho gaya**. Kaam wahi ka wahi raha (ideal case mein).

> Mr. Shaikh ne kaha, "Dekha Zeenat? Lambi plank se zor kam hua, kaam nahi. Jo force mein bachaya, wo doori mein chuka diya."

Topic 1 mein Mr. Sathe ke 1000 N ke drum ke liye kaha tha ki ramp par "kaafi kam effort" lagta hai. Agar unki ramp bhi l = 4 m, h = 1 m ki ho, to ideal effort = 1000 N ÷ 4 = **250 N**, yaani seedha uthane ka chautha hissa.

### Real incline: friction aata hai

Asli plank par friction hota hai, isliye drum ko dhakelne mein **ideal se zyada** effort lagta hai. Tab **work output < work input**, aur machine ki **efficiency (η) 100% se kam** hoti hai. Efficiency ka formula Topic 2 mein dekha tha: **η = (MA ÷ VR) × 100%**.

### Dhalaan kahan-kahan milti hai

- **Ramp** (truck ka, godown ka, flyover ka)
- Park ki **slide** (children's slide)
- Pahadi **sadak**, jo seedhi chadhne ke bajaye **hairpin bends** (U-shaped mod) leti hui lambi ghoomti hai: lambi sadak, halki dhalaan
- **Seedhiyan (stairs)**: ye bhi ek tarah ki dhalaan hain jinmein dhalaan ko kadmon mein kaat diya gaya hai
- **Wheelchair ka ramp**: gentle ramp jisse kam effort mein chadha ja sake

## Part B: Wedge (fanna)

**Wedge** do inclined planes ko **peeth se peeth jodkar** bana hota hai. Ye kisi cheez ke beech mein **ghus kar use baant** deta hai. Hum wedge ko dhakelte hain (effort), aur wo cheez ko dono taraf kholta hai (load, side ka force).

```
          effort (hathoda)
               ↓
          ┌─────────┐   ← motai t (upar ka chauda sira)
           ╲       ╱
            ╲     ╱   ← l = slope ki lambai
             ╲   ╱
              ╲ ╱
               V   ← nuka (neeche)
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Wedge ke do slanting side dono taraf ki dhalaan hain (har ek ki lambai l), aur upar ki motai t hai.

Ideal wedge ke liye:

> **MA = slope ki lambai l ÷ motai t**

Jitna patla aur nukila wedge, utna bada MA.

**Wedge ke examples:** chaaku, kulhadi (axe), chisel, keel (nail), darwaze ke neeche lagne wala **doorstop**, aur kheti mein **plough** (hal ka nukila hissa jo mitti ko cheerta hai).

**Ek zaroori baat:** wedge bhi ek inclined plane hi hai (balki do). Bas fark ye hai ki dhalaan par load chadhane ke bajaye, yahan dhalaan khud cheez ke andar ghusti hai.

## Part C: Screw

**Screw** ek **cylinder par lipti hui inclined plane** hai. Agar tum ek tirchhe kate hue kagaz (triangle) ko pencil par lapeto, to uski slanting dhar pencil par ghumavdaar line bana degi: wahi **thread** hai.

### Pitch

Do paas-paas ke threads ke beech ki doori **pitch (p)** kehlati hai. Screw ko ek **poora chakkar** ghumao, to wo apni **pitch ke barabar** doori aage (ya peeche) badhta hai.

```
   ┌────────────────────────┐
   │ ╱  ╱  ╱  ╱  ╱  ╱  ╱  ╱ │   ← threads (screw ka body)
   └────────────────────────┘
     ←p→
     do paas-paas ke threads ke beech ki doori = pitch p
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Pitch p: ek poore chakkar mein screw itna hi aage badhta hai.

### Screw ka MA

Screw ka effort ek **circle** mein ghumta hai (jaise spanner ya handle ka sira), aur load pitch ke barabar upar uthta hai. **Screw jack** ka ideal MA (given result):

> **MA = (effort ke circle ki circumference) ÷ pitch = 2π r ÷ p**

Yahan r = handle ki lambai, aur 2π r handle ke sire dwara banaye gaye circle ki circumference hai. Is topic mein hum π = 3.14 lenge.

Yahi **Topic 2 ki jack ki kahani** ka raaz hai: chhota effort ek bada circle banata hai, aur load bahut chhoti si pitch jitna upar uthta hai. Isliye MA bahut bada ho jaata hai.

**Screw ke examples:** bolt, jar ka lid, bottle ka cap, screw jack, vice, drill bit.

## Part D: Wheel-and-axle

**Wheel-and-axle** mein ek **bada wheel (radius R)** ek **chhote axle (radius r)** se jama hota hai, aur dono saath ghumte hain. **Effort wheel ke rim par** lagta hai, **load axle par**.

```
           effort (rim par)
                v
          ╭───────────╮
         ╱   ╭─────╮   ╲
        │    │axle │    │     R = wheel ka radius
        │    │  r  │    │     r = axle ka radius
         ╲   ╰─────╯   ╱
          ╰───────────╯
                │
              load (axle ki rassi se)
```

> **Figure 5.4** – Bada wheel R, chhota axle r, effort rim par, load axle par.

Ek poore chakkar mein effort wheel ke rim par 2π R doori chalta hai, aur load axle ki 2π r doori chalta hai. To:

> **ideal MA = VR = R ÷ r**

Jitna bada wheel aur jitna patla axle, utna bada MA.

**Wheel-and-axle ke examples:** darwaze ka knob, steering wheel, screwdriver (handle bada, blade chhota; isliye Topic 1 mein ise force multiplier kaha tha), **windlass** (kuein ka handle), water-tap ka handle, aur cycle ka pedal aur wheel.

### Lever se rishta

Wheel-and-axle ko **lagatar ghumta hua lever** samajh sakte ho: wheel ka radius effort ki arm hai aur axle ka radius load ki arm.

## Part E: Chaaron machines ek table mein

| Machine | Ideal MA | VR | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inclined plane | l ÷ h | l ÷ h | Ramp, slide, hill road, stairs |
| Wedge | l ÷ t | l ÷ t | Chaaku, kulhadi, chisel, keel, plough |
| Screw (jack) | 2π r ÷ p | 2π r ÷ p | Bolt, jar lid, screw jack, vice |
| Wheel-and-axle | R ÷ r | R ÷ r | Door knob, steering wheel, windlass |

Ideal machine mein **MA = VR** hota hai. Asli machine mein friction ki wajah se **MA < VR**, aur efficiency η = (MA ÷ VR) × 100% hoti hai.

## Part F: Solved examples

**Example 1 (incline ka effort aur MA):** Ek ramp ki lambai 6 m aur unchai 1.5 m hai. 800 N ka load ideal case mein chadhana hai. MA aur effort?

- MA = l ÷ h = 6 m ÷ 1.5 m = **4**
- Effort = load ÷ MA = 800 N ÷ 4 = **200 N**

**Jaanch:** effort × l = 200 N × 6 m = 1200 J, aur load × h = 800 N × 1.5 m = 1200 J ✅

**Example 2 (kitni lambi dhalaan chahiye):** 900 N ka load 1.5 m ooncha uthana hai, aur effort sirf 300 N lagana hai (ideal). Dhalaan ki lambai?

- MA = load ÷ effort = 900 N ÷ 300 N = 3
- l = MA × h = 3 × 1.5 m = **4.5 m**

**Jaanch:** effort × l = 300 N × 4.5 m = 1350 J, load × h = 900 N × 1.5 m = 1350 J ✅

**Example 3 (work ideal aur real):** Ek incline l = 5 m, h = 1 m hai. 500 N ka load chadhane mein **125 N** ka effort laga. MA, VR, efficiency, ideal effort aur lost energy?

- MA = load ÷ effort = 500 N ÷ 125 N = **4**
- VR = l ÷ h = 5 m ÷ 1 m = **5**
- η = (MA ÷ VR) × 100% = (4 ÷ 5) × 100% = **80%**
- Ideal effort = load ÷ VR = 500 N ÷ 5 = **100 N**
- Work input = 125 N × 5 m = 625 J, work output = 500 N × 1 m = 500 J
- Lost energy = 625 J − 500 J = **125 J** (friction mein gaya)

**Jaanch:** output ÷ input = 500 J ÷ 625 J = 0.8 = 80% ✅ (MA ÷ VR wale se milta hai).

**Example 4 (wedge ka MA):** Ek wedge ki slope ki lambai 10 cm aur motai 2 cm hai. MA?

- MA = l ÷ t = 10 cm ÷ 2 cm = **5**

**Jaanch:** dono lambai ek hi unit (cm) mein hain, to ratio unit-free 5 aaya ✅

**Example 5 (kulhadi):** Ek kulhadi ke blade ki slope 12 cm aur motai 3 cm hai. Lakdi ko cheerne ke liye 50 N ka effort lagta hai. Ideal load (side ka force)?

- MA = 12 cm ÷ 3 cm = 4
- Load = MA × effort = 4 × 50 N = **200 N**

**Jaanch:** load ÷ effort = 200 N ÷ 50 N = 4 ✅

**Example 6 (screw jack ka MA):** Ek screw jack ke handle ki lambai 40 cm hai aur pitch 5 mm. MA?

- r = 40 cm = 0.4 m, p = 5 mm = 0.005 m (**pehle dono ko metre mein**)
- Circumference = 2π r = 2 × 3.14 × 0.4 m = 2.512 m
- MA = 2.512 m ÷ 0.005 m = 502.4 ≈ **502**

**Jaanch:** 502.4 × 0.005 = 2.512 ✅

**Example 7 (screw jack ka effort):** Example 6 wale jack se 10000 N ka load uthana hai. Ideal effort?

- Effort = load ÷ MA = 10000 N ÷ 502.4 ≈ **19.9 N** (lagbhag 20 N)

**Jaanch:** ek poore chakkar mein effort ka kaam = 19.9 N × 2.512 m ≈ 50 J, aur load ka kaam = 10000 N × 0.005 m = 50 J ✅. Isi wajah se sirf lagbhag 20 N ke effort se 10000 N (lagbhag 1000 kg) ka load uth jaata hai.

**Example 8 (wheel-and-axle MA aur effort):** Wheel ka radius 30 cm, axle ka 5 cm. 600 N ka load. MA aur ideal effort?

- MA = R ÷ r = 30 cm ÷ 5 cm = **6**
- Effort = 600 N ÷ 6 = **100 N**

**Jaanch:** 100 N × 6 = 600 N ✅

**Example 9 (windlass):** Windlass ke handle ka radius 40 cm aur drum ka radius 10 cm hai. 200 N ki balti uthani hai. MA aur effort?

- MA = R ÷ r = 40 cm ÷ 10 cm = **4**
- Effort = 200 N ÷ 4 = **50 N**

**Jaanch:** 50 N × 4 = 200 N ✅

**Example 10 (wheelchair ke do ramps):** Ek raste mein 0.3 m ki unchai chadhni hai. Wheelchair aur vyakti ka total weight 900 N. Ramp A ki lambai 1.5 m, ramp B ki 3.6 m. Dono mein ideal effort?

- Ramp A: MA = 1.5 m ÷ 0.3 m = 5, effort = 900 N ÷ 5 = **180 N**
- Ramp B: MA = 3.6 m ÷ 0.3 m = 12, effort = 900 N ÷ 12 = **75 N**

**Jaanch:** A: 180 N × 1.5 m = 270 J, B: 75 N × 3.6 m = 270 J, aur 900 N × 0.3 m = 270 J ✅. Ramp B mein zor kam, par lambai zyada. Wheelchair ke liye B behtar, bas jagah chahiye.

**Example 11 (wheel-and-axle ki efficiency):** Ek wheel-and-axle mein R = 20 cm aur r = 4 cm hai. 400 N ka load uthane ke liye 100 N ka effort laga. VR, MA aur efficiency?

- VR = R ÷ r = 20 cm ÷ 4 cm = **5**
- MA = 400 N ÷ 100 N = **4**
- η = (4 ÷ 5) × 100% = **80%**

**Jaanch:** Agar load 0.1 m upar jaye, to effort 5 × 0.1 m = 0.5 m chalta hai. Work input = 100 N × 0.5 m = 50 J, output = 400 N × 0.1 m = 40 J, 40 ÷ 50 = 80% ✅

**Example 12 (hill road):** Ek pahadi sadak 2000 m lambi hai aur 200 m ooncha chadhati hai. 20000 N ki gaadi ke liye ideal force?

- MA = 2000 m ÷ 200 m = 10
- Force = 20000 N ÷ 10 = **2000 N**

**Jaanch:** 2000 N × 2000 m = 4000000 J, aur 20000 N × 200 m = 4000000 J ✅. Sadak lambi ghoom kar banayi jaati hai (hairpin bends), isi liye.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Gentle ramp se kaam kam ho jata hai"**

Nahi. Zeenat ke sawal ka jawab: plank 2 m ho ya 4 m, ideal work 1200 J hi hai. Force kam hota hai, par distance badh jaati hai.

**Galti 2: l aur h ulat dena**

MA = l ÷ h (slant length ÷ seedhi unchai). Agar ulta kar diya, to MA 1 se bhi kam aayega, jo ramp ke liye ajeeb lagta hai. Example 1 mein MA = 6 ÷ 1.5 = 4 hai, 1.5 ÷ 6 = 0.25 nahi.

**Galti 3: Wheel-and-axle mein R aur r mila dena**

MA = **R ÷ r** (bada ÷ chhota). Agar effort axle par aur load wheel par ho, to MA 1 se kam ho jaata hai. Hamesha dekho ki effort kahan lagta hai.

**Galti 4: mm aur cm ko metre mein nahi badalna**

Example 6 mein r = 0.4 m aur p = 0.005 m liya. Agar 40 cm aur 5 mm direct bhar dete, to 2 × 3.14 × 40 ÷ 5 = 50.24 aata, jo galat hai.

**Galti 5: "Wedge inclined plane nahi hai"**

Wedge do inclined planes ka jod hi hai, peeth se peeth jodkar.

**Galti 6: Screw ka MA sirf pitch se nikalna**

Screw ka MA **handle ki lambai aur pitch, dono** par depend karta hai: MA = 2π r ÷ p. Handle jitna lamba, ya pitch jitni chhoti, MA utna bada.

## Quick Recap

- **Inclined plane:** tirchhi surface; ideal MA = VR = l ÷ h. Lambi dhalaan se effort kam, rasta zyada.
- Work input = effort × l = load × h = work output (ideal). **Kaam wahi**, force aur doori ka trade-off.
- Real incline mein friction se η < 100%. Example 3: η = 80%, lost energy = 125 J.
- **Wedge:** do inclined planes peeth se peeth; ideal MA = l ÷ t (chaaku, kulhadi, keel, plough).
- **Screw:** cylinder par lipti hui inclined plane; pitch = ek chakkar mein aage badhne ki doori; screw jack ka MA = 2π r ÷ p.
- **Wheel-and-axle:** ideal MA = VR = R ÷ r; lagatar ghumta hua lever.
- Hamesha cm aur mm ko m mein badal kar hi MA nikalo.

## Practice Questions

1. A ramp is 8 m long and 2 m high. Find the ideal MA and the effort needed to push an 800 N load up it.

2. A 600 N load must be raised through 1.2 m using an ideal incline with an effort of only 150 N. What length of incline is needed?

3. An incline has l = 6 m and h = 1.5 m. A load of 840 N is raised using an effort of 280 N. Find the MA, VR, efficiency and the energy lost.

4. A wedge has a slope length of 15 cm and a thickness of 3 cm. Find its ideal MA. What load does it exert on the wood if the effort is 80 N?

5. A screw jack has a handle of length 0.5 m and a pitch of 4 mm. Find its ideal MA, and the effort needed to lift a load of 7850 N. Take π = 3.14.

6. A wheel-and-axle has R = 50 cm and r = 10 cm. Find its ideal MA, and the effort needed for a 750 N load.

7. A windlass has a handle radius of 60 cm and a drum radius of 15 cm. Find the effort needed to lift a 240 N bucket (ideal).

8. State True or False:
   (a) A gentler ramp reduces the work needed to lift a load.
   (b) A wedge is made of two inclined planes placed back to back.
   (c) A screw with a smaller pitch has a larger MA, if the handle length is the same.
   (d) For a wheel-and-axle, ideal MA = r ÷ R.

9. A wheel-and-axle with R = 25 cm and r = 5 cm lifts a 300 N load with an effort of 75 N. Find its VR, MA and efficiency.

10. Why do mountain roads take hairpin bends instead of going straight up the slope? Answer in terms of force and distance.

### Answers

1. MA = l ÷ h = 8 m ÷ 2 m = **4**. Effort = 800 N ÷ 4 = **200 N**.
   Jaanch: 200 × 8 = 1600 J, 800 × 2 = 1600 J ✅

2. MA = 600 N ÷ 150 N = 4. l = MA × h = 4 × 1.2 m = **4.8 m**.
   Jaanch: 150 × 4.8 = 720 J, 600 × 1.2 = 720 J ✅

3. MA = 840 N ÷ 280 N = **3**. VR = 6 m ÷ 1.5 m = **4**. η = (3 ÷ 4) × 100% = **75%**. Work input = 280 N × 6 m = 1680 J, output = 840 N × 1.5 m = 1260 J, energy lost = 1680 J − 1260 J = **420 J**.
   Jaanch: 1260 J ÷ 1680 J = 0.75 = 75% ✅

4. MA = 15 cm ÷ 3 cm = **5**. Load = 5 × 80 N = **400 N**.
   Jaanch: 400 ÷ 80 = 5 ✅

5. Circumference = 2 × 3.14 × 0.5 m = 3.14 m. p = 4 mm = 0.004 m. MA = 3.14 ÷ 0.004 = **785**. Effort = 7850 N ÷ 785 = **10 N**.
   Jaanch: 10 × 785 = 7850 ✅

6. MA = 50 cm ÷ 10 cm = **5**. Effort = 750 N ÷ 5 = **150 N**.
   Jaanch: 150 × 5 = 750 ✅

7. MA = 60 cm ÷ 15 cm = 4. Effort = 240 N ÷ 4 = **60 N**.
   Jaanch: 60 × 4 = 240 ✅

8. (a) **False** ❌. Gentler ramp se effort kam hota hai, par work (ideal) wahi rehta hai.
   (b) **True** ✅. Wedge do inclined planes peeth se peeth jodkar banta hai.
   (c) **True** ✅. MA = 2π r ÷ p, p chhota to MA bada.
   (d) **False** ❌. Ideal MA = R ÷ r.

9. VR = 25 cm ÷ 5 cm = **5**. MA = 300 N ÷ 75 N = **4**. η = (4 ÷ 5) × 100% = **80%**.
   Jaanch: Load 0.1 m uthe to effort 0.5 m chalta hai: input = 75 × 0.5 = 37.5 J, output = 300 × 0.1 = 30 J, 30 ÷ 37.5 = 80% ✅

10. Seedhi chadhai mein slope bahut steep hoti hai aur gaadi ko bahut zyada force chahiye. Hairpin bends se sadak lambi ho jaati hai, to slope halki ho jaati hai aur force kam lagta hai. Ideal mein work wahi rehta hai: force kam, distance zyada.
