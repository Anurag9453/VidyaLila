# Topic 6: Principle of Machines aur Real Life ki Machines

Pichhle topics mein humne saari simple machines dekhi: lever, pulley, inclined plane, wedge, screw aur wheel-and-axle. Har machine ne ek hi kaam kiya: thodi si effort se bada load sambhala, ya effort ki disha badal di, ya raftaar badha di. Har baar humne MA, VR aur efficiency nikali.

Ab ek bada sawaal: itni alag alag machines, par kya sab ke peeche koi **ek hi niyam** hai? Haan! Aur wo niyam hum pehle hi jaante hain: **energy conservation** (Work, Energy aur Power chapter). Is topic mein hum us niyam se sabhi machines ko ek saath samjhenge, dekhenge ki real machines mein energy kahan "kho" jaati hai, aur ye bhi dekhenge ki jab do ya teen machines saath judti hain (jaise cycle ya crane) to unki MA kaise jud kar badhti hai. Ye chapter ka aakhri topic hai, to end mein poore chapter ka wrap-up bhi hoga.

Is topic mein jahan zaroorat ho, hum **g = 10 m/s²** lenge (asli value 9.8 m/s² ke kareeb hai, par hisaab aasaan ho jaata hai).

## Cycle repair shop mein Mr. Wadia aur Ojas

Ojas ki cycle ki chain baar baar utar rahi thi, to wo use Mr. Wadia ki cycle repair shop par le gaya. Mr. Wadia ne 20 saal se cycles theek ki hain. Unhone chain chadhai, gears par thoda tel lagaya, aur cycle ko ek round chalakar dekha.

Ojas ki cycle mein gears the: pedal ke paas ek aur peeche ke pahiye par kai chhote bade gear (sprocket). Ojas ne poocha, "Uncle, ye gears kis kaam ke hain?"

Mr. Wadia ne kaha, "Beta, jab steep dhalaan (slope) aaye, to **choti gear** lagana. Aur jab sapaat road mile, to **badi gear**."

Ojas ne socha aur phir muskuraya, "To uncle, agar main sabse choti gear laga loon, to kya dhalaan par cycle bina kisi mehnat ke upar chadh jayegi? Koi na koi gear to aisi hogi jo mujhe bilkul free mein upar le jaye?"

Mr. Wadia hans pade. "Arre beta, itni achhi baat poochi hai. Free mein? Chal, aaj tujhe bataata hoon ki gear mehnat **kam** to kar deti hai, par **khatam** kabhi nahi karti. Aur ye sirf cycle ki nahi, duniya ki har machine ki kahani hai."

## Part A: Principle of machines (energy conservation se)

### Kaam kabhi free nahi milta

Work, Energy aur Power chapter ke Topic 6 mein humne dekha tha ki energy na banti hai, na khatam hoti hai, sirf ek roop se doosre roop mein badalti hai, aur friction energy ko heat mein badal deta hai. Machine par bhi yahi niyam laagu hota hai:

**Work output = Work input − energy jo friction se heat ban gayi**

Machine mein hum effort laga kar kaam **dete** hain (work input = effort × effort distance), aur machine load par kaam **karti** hai (work output = load × load distance).

### Ideal machine: friction zero

Agar friction bilkul na ho (ideal machine), to koi energy heat nahi banegi, aur:

**Work output = Work input**

**Load × load distance = Effort × effort distance**

Isi ko **principle of machines** kehte hain.

```
   Effort ───►  [   MACHINE   ]  ───►  Load par kaam
   (input)        friction se            (output)
   E × d_E        heat nikli             L × d_L
                      │
                      ▼
               (energy lost)

   Real:   output = input − energy lost
   Ideal:  output = input
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Machine energy ko ek taraf se leti hai aur doosri taraf load par kaam karti hai. Real machine mein kuch energy friction se heat ban kar nikal jaati hai, ideal machine mein kuch bhi nahi.

### Isse kya nikalta hai: gain aur loss ka sauda

Load × load distance = Effort × effort distance ko dhyan se dekho. Dono taraf ka gunaa-fal barabar hona chahiye. To:

- Agar effort **chhoti** chahiye, to effort distance **badi** rakhni padegi. (Force multiplier: **force mein faayda, distance mein nuksaan**.)
- Agar effort distance **chhoti** chahiye (cheez ko tez ya zyada door bhejna), to effort **badi** lagegi. (Speed multiplier: **speed mein faayda, force mein nuksaan**.)

Isse Topic 2 ki MA aur VR ka rishta bhi samajh aata hai: ideal machine mein MA = VR, kyunki load ÷ effort = effort distance ÷ load distance.

### "No free lunch"

Koi bhi machine **jitna kaam input mein milta hai, usse zyada output mein nahi de sakti**. Machine sirf kaam ko "badalti" hai: kam force par zyada distance, ya zyada force par kam distance. Isi wajah se aisi machine jo bina energy diye hamesha chalti rahe (perpetual motion machine) **kabhi nahi ban sakti**: wo energy conservation ke khilaaf hai.

### Har simple machine par ek hi principle

| Machine | Principle ka roop (ideal) |
|---|---|
| Lever | Load × load arm = effort × effort arm |
| Pulley (n rope wali) | Effort distance = n × load distance, isliye effort × n = load |
| Inclined plane | Effort × l = Load × h |
| Screw | Effort × 2π r = Load × pitch (ek chakkar mein) |
| Wheel-and-axle | Effort × R = Load × r |

Jaanch (ek example se, lever): load 60 N, load arm 0.2 m, effort arm 0.6 m. Effort = (60 N × 0.2 m) ÷ 0.6 m = 12 N m ÷ 0.6 m = **20 N**. Jaanch: 20 N × 0.6 m = 12 N m aur 60 N × 0.2 m = 12 N m ✅

## Part B: Real machines: friction aur energy ka nuksaan

### Heat aur sound ke roop mein loss

Ideal machine sirf kitaab mein hoti hai. Asli machine mein hamesha friction hota hai: chain ke links aapas mein ragadte hain, axle apne support mein ragadta hai, rope pulley par ragadti hai. Iska nateeja:

- Kuch energy **heat** ban jaati hai (chain, bearing ya motor garm ho jaate hain).
- Kuch energy **sound** ban jaati hai (chain ki khat-khat, gear ki awaaz).
- Isliye **work output < work input**, yaani efficiency 100% se kam.

Machine ke kuch hisse (pulley ka block, lever ka bhaar) ka **apna weight** bhi hota hai, aur use bhi uthana padta hai. Ye bhi input energy ka ek hissa kha leta hai.

### Friction kam kaise karein

| Tareeka | Kaise madad karta hai | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Oiling / grease | Ragadne wali satahon ke beech patli parat bana kar friction kam | Cycle chain par tel |
| Ball bearings | Sliding ki jagah rolling, jo kam friction deta hai | Pahiye ka hub, fan |
| Polishing | Sataho ko chikna banana | Pulley ki groove, gear ke daant |
| Streamlining | Hawa ka rukawat kam karne ke liye smooth aakaar | Cycle racer ka jhuka hua dhaancha, car ka shape |
| Halke material | Machine ke apne parts ka weight kam | Racing cycle ka aluminium frame |

Ye sab input energy ko bachakar output ke kareeb laate hain, par friction **bilkul zero** kabhi nahi hota.

### Roz ki machines ki efficiency (lagbhag, sirf andaza)

Neeche ki values **approximate** hain. Alag model, umar, aur halat par alag ho sakti hain. Yahan sirf feel lene ke liye hain.

| Machine | Efficiency (lagbhag) |
|---|---|
| Cycle chain drive (achhi tarah tel lagi hui) | 90% se 98% |
| Electric motor | 80% se 95% |
| Car ka petrol engine | 25% se 30% |
| Insaan ka sharir (kaam machine ke roop mein) | 20% se 25% |
| LED bulb aur filament bulb | LED zyada efficient, filament bulb mein zyadatar energy heat banti hai |

Dekho, chain kaafi achhi machine hai, par petrol engine kaafi energy heat mein bahaata hai. Isliye engine aksar bahut garm ho jaata hai.

## Part C: Cycle ke gears aur Ojas ka sawaal

### Cycle ki gears kya karti hain

Cycle mein pedal ke paas ek bada gear (chainring) hota hai, aur peeche pahiye par kai gears (sprockets) jinmein se tum ek chunte ho. Chain pedal ki gati ko peeche ke pahiye tak pahunchati hai.

Yahan **sirf soch** (qualitative), koi formula nahi:

- **Chhota rear sprocket** (kam daante): ek pedal ke chakkar mein pahiya zyada ghoomta hai, cycle **zyada distance** jaati hai. Par pedal par **zyada force** lagani padti hai. Ye **speed multiplier** jaisa kaam hai. Sapaat road par yahi theek hai.
- **Bada rear sprocket** (zyada daante): ek pedal ke chakkar mein pahiya kam ghoomta hai, cycle **kam distance** jaati hai. Par pedal par **kam force** lagti hai. Ye **force multiplier** jaisa kaam hai. Dhalaan par yahi chahiye.

```
        Chhota sprocket          Bada sprocket
        (sapaat road)            (steep slope)
           ___                      _____
          / o \                    /     \
          \___/                   (   o   )
                                   \_____/

   1 pedal chakkar:            1 pedal chakkar:
   cycle zyada door            cycle kam door
   pedal par zyada force       pedal par kam force
```

> **Figure 6.2** – Cycle ke peeche ke do sprockets. Chhota sprocket zyada distance deta hai par zyada force maangta hai (speed multiplier). Bada sprocket kam distance deta hai par kam force maangta hai (force multiplier).

**Ek naming ki dikkat:** cycle shop mein "choti gear" ya "low gear" ka matlab hota hai **peeche ka bada sprocket**, kyunki use hum "pehli gear" (gear number 1, sabse halki) kehte hain. Isliye Mr. Wadia ne dhalaan par "choti gear" bola: matlab bada sprocket, jo force kam kar deta hai. Aur sapaat road par "badi gear" yaani chhota sprocket, jo zyada raftaar deta hai.

### Ojas ke sawaal ka jawab

Ojas ne poocha tha, "Kya koi gear dhalaan par bina mehnat ke upar le jayegi?" Mr. Wadia ka jawab:

Dhalaan par chadhne mein cycle aur Ojas ko ek height par uthana hota hai. Isme gravity ke khilaaf kaam hota hai, aur wo utna hi rahega, chaahe koi bhi gear lagao. Choti gear (bada sprocket) sirf ye karti hai ki:

- Har pedal chakkar par force **kam** lagti hai,
- Par cycle ko dhalaan par upar pahunchane ke liye pedal ke **zyada chakkar** lagane padte hain.

Yaani force mein faayda, distance mein nuksaan. **Kaam ki kul matra kam nahi hoti.** Free mein kuch nahi milta. Isi wajah se "choti gear" mein dhalaan chadhna aasaan lagta hai (thaka nahi deta), par dheere dheere aur zyada pedalling karni padti hai.

## Part D: Combination of machines (kai machines ek saath)

Real life mein kai cheezein do ya zyada simple machines ka mel hoti hain.

| Cheez | Kaun kaun si machines |
|---|---|
| Wheelbarrow | Lever (poora dhaancha, jismein pahiya fulcrum hai) aur wheel |
| Cycle | Pahiye aur pedal (wheel-and-axle), brake (lever), gear aur chain |
| Crane | Pulley (rope aur block) aur lever (boom) |
| Scissors | Do levers jo ek fulcrum par jude hain |
| Screw jack | Screw aur lever (lamba handle) |

### Compound MA = individual MAs ka gunaa-fal (product)

Jab ek machine ka output doosri machine ka input ban jaata hai (series mein), to **kul MA = pehli MA × doosri MA**. Jodna (add karna) galat hai.

Kyun? Maan lo lever MA 4 hai: 1 N effort se 4 N force milti hai. Wo 4 N force pulley ko mili, jiski MA 3 hai: to 4 N ki force se 4 × 3 = 12 N load sambhal jayega. Yaani 1 N effort se 12 N, kul MA = **4 × 3 = 12**, na ki 4 + 3 = 7.

```
   Effort                    Lever            Pulley
   (E)  ───────►  [ MA = 4 ] ───────► [ MA = 3 ] ───────►  Load (L)
                  1 N  ──►  4 N        4 N  ──►  12 N

   Kul MA = 4 × 3 = 12   (jodna nahi, guna karna)
```

> **Figure 6.3** – Lever (MA 4) ka output pulley (MA 3) ka input ban jaata hai. Kul MA 12 hota hai.

### Combination ki efficiency

Har machine apni energy kuch heat mein kho deti hai. Isliye combination mein loss jodte jaate hain: **overall efficiency kam ho jaati hai**, aur real MA ideal MA se kam rehta hai. Zyada machines jodne par friction ke naye points bhi judte jaate hain.

### Insaan ka sharir bhi levers ka mel hai

Hamare sharir mein bhi levers hain, sirf naam yaad rakhne hain:

- **Haath ki kohni (elbow):** hath mod kar kuch uthate hain, to bicep muscle effort deti hai, kohni fulcrum hai.
- **Sir ka ghoomna ya jhukna:** gardan ki haddi fulcrum ka kaam karti hai.
- **Pair ke paanv ki ungliyon par khade hona:** paanv ki ungli fulcrum hai aur pindli muscle effort lagati hai.

## Part E: Solved examples

### Example 1: Work input, work output aur energy loss

Ek machine mein 40 N ki effort 3 m tak chalti hai aur 90 N ka load 1 m uthta hai. Work input, work output aur lost energy nikalo.

Work input = effort × effort distance = 40 N × 3 m = **120 J**

Work output = load × load distance = 90 N × 1 m = **90 J**

Energy lost = input − output = 120 J − 90 J = **30 J**

Jaanch: 90 J + 30 J = 120 J ✅ (energy conserved)

### Example 2: Loads aur distances se efficiency

Ek lever mein 25 N effort 0.8 m chalti hai, aur 60 N ka load 0.3 m uthta hai. Efficiency?

Work input = 25 N × 0.8 m = **20 J**
Work output = 60 N × 0.3 m = **18 J**

Efficiency η = (work output ÷ work input) × 100% = (18 J ÷ 20 J) × 100% = 0.9 × 100% = **90%**

Jaanch (MA ÷ VR se): MA = 60 N ÷ 25 N = 2.4. VR = 0.8 m ÷ 0.3 m = 2.667. η = (2.4 ÷ 2.667) × 100% = 0.9 × 100% = 90% ✅

### Example 3: 75% efficiency, 800 J input

Ek machine 75% efficient hai aur 800 J ka input milta hai. Work output aur lost energy?

Work output = 75% of 800 J = 0.75 × 800 J = **600 J**

Energy lost = 800 J − 600 J = **200 J**

Jaanch: 600 J ÷ 800 J × 100% = 75% ✅, aur 600 J + 200 J = 800 J ✅

### Example 4: Lever (MA 4) aur pulley (MA 3), ideal

1000 N ka load uthana hai. Lever ki MA 4 hai aur uska output pulley ko chalata hai, jiski MA 3 hai. Ideal case mein effort kitni lagegi?

Kul MA = 4 × 3 = **12**

Effort = load ÷ MA = 1000 N ÷ 12 = **83.3 N** (lagbhag)

Jaanch: 83.3 N × 12 = 999.6 N ≈ 1000 N ✅

### Example 5: Wahi combination, overall efficiency 90%

Example 4 ki combination real hai, overall efficiency 90%. Ab effort kitni?

Ideal MA = 12. Real MA = efficiency × ideal MA (kyunki η = MA ÷ VR aur ideal MA = VR) = 0.9 × 12 = **10.8**

Effort = 1000 N ÷ 10.8 = **92.6 N** (lagbhag)

Jaanch: 92.6 N × 10.8 = 1000.08 N ≈ 1000 N ✅. Ideal 83.3 N se zyada effort lagi, jo sahi hai, kyunki friction ke loss ki bharpai ke liye zyada effort chahiye.

### Example 6: Ramp aur pulley series mein

Ek godown mein 600 N ka load ramp par chadhana hai. Ramp ki lambai l = 4 m hai aur height h = 1 m. Load rope se pulley block system (MA 3) ke zariye khincha jaata hai. Ideal effort kitni?

Ramp ki MA = l ÷ h = 4 m ÷ 1 m = **4**
Pulley ki MA = **3**
Kul MA = 4 × 3 = **12**

Effort = 600 N ÷ 12 = **50 N**

Jaanch (work se): load 1 m upar uthta hai (ramp ki height), to work output = 600 N × 1 m = 600 J. Effort ko ramp par 4 m aur pulley ki wajah se 3 guna rope khinchni padti hai: effort distance = 4 m × 3 = 12 m, aur work input = 50 N × 12 m = 600 J ✅

### Example 7: Cycle ka sprocket: distance aur force ki tulna

Ojas ke cycle ke ek bade sprocket par ek pedal chakkar mein cycle 3 m aage badhti hai. Doosre sprocket mein daante 2 guna kam hain, to ek pedal chakkar mein pahiya 2 guna zyada ghoomta hai.

Bade sprocket par: ek pedal chakkar mein cycle ka distance = **3 m**.
Chhote sprocket (2 guna kam daante) par: cycle ka distance = 2 × 3 m = **6 m**.

Principle of machines (ideal) kehta hai ki ek pedal chakkar mein input work barabar hai, to doosri taraf kaam bhi barabar: jab distance **doguna** (3 m se 6 m), to pahiye ke zameen par dhakelne ki force **aadhi** ho jaati hai. Ya ulta: agar chhote sprocket par road par wahi force banaye rakhni ho (dhalaan chadhte waqt), to pedal par **2 guna force** lagani padegi.

Matlab: **distance dugni hone par force aadhi (ya force dugni karni padegi)**: kuch bhi free nahi.

### Example 8: Energy loss percentage

Ek car ka engine 50,000 J petrol ki energy leta hai aur lagbhag 30% ko pahiyon ke kaam mein badalta hai. Kitni energy useful hai aur kitni heat aur sound mein gayi? Loss kitna percent hai?

Useful energy = 30% of 50,000 J = 0.3 × 50,000 J = **15,000 J**

Lost energy = 50,000 J − 15,000 J = **35,000 J**

Loss % = (35,000 J ÷ 50,000 J) × 100% = **70%**

Jaanch: 15,000 J + 35,000 J = 50,000 J ✅, aur 30% + 70% = 100% ✅

### Example 9: Ek machine jo MA = 5 aur VR = 4 batati hai

Ek company ka dawa hai: "Humari machine ki MA 5 hai aur VR 4." Kya ye mumkin hai?

Efficiency η = (MA ÷ VR) × 100% = (5 ÷ 4) × 100% = **125%**

Iska matlab hai ki output input se **zyada** hai, jo energy conservation ke khilaaf hai. Agar load 100 N lete hain to effort = 100 N ÷ 5 = 20 N, aur load distance 1 m ho to effort distance = 4 × 1 m = 4 m. Work input = 20 N × 4 m = 80 J, par work output = 100 N × 1 m = 100 J. Output (100 J) > input (80 J). Ye "free" 20 J kahan se aaye?

Isliye dawa **galat** hai. Real machine mein hamesha **MA < VR** (aur ideal mein MA = VR). MA kabhi VR se bada nahi ho sakta.

Jaanch: kisi bhi machine ke liye η ≤ 100%, yaani MA ≤ VR. Yahan 5 > 4 ❌

### Example 10: Screw jack, ideal aur real

Ek screw jack se 3000 N ka load uthana hai. Handle ki lambai (radius) r = 0.5 m, screw ka pitch = 4 mm = 0.004 m. (π = 3.14 lo.)

**Ideal effort** (friction zero):

Effort × 2π r = Load × pitch

Effort = (3000 N × 0.004 m) ÷ (2 × 3.14 × 0.5 m) = 12 J ÷ 3.14 m = **3.82 N** (lagbhag)

Jaanch: 3.82 N × 3.14 m = 11.99 J ≈ 12 J ✅

**Real effort:** screw jack mein friction bahut hota hai, aur efficiency lagbhag 20% maan lo.

Real effort = ideal effort ÷ efficiency = 3.82 N ÷ 0.20 = **19.1 N** (lagbhag)

Jaanch (MA se): ideal MA = VR = 2π r ÷ pitch = 3.14 m ÷ 0.004 m = 785. Real MA = 0.2 × 785 = 157. Effort = 3000 N ÷ 157 = **19.1 N** ✅

Dekho: ideal mein 3.82 N, par friction ki wajah se real mein 19.1 N lagti hai, yaani 5 guna. Phir bhi 3000 N ka load 19 N se utha lena jaadu jaisa hai. Par is ke liye handle ko bahut chakkar ghumane padte hain.

### Example 11: Ojas dhalaan par: kaam kam nahi hota

Ojas aur cycle ka total mass 60 kg hai. Wo 5 m oonchi dhalaan par chadhta hai. (g = 10 m/s²) Minimum kitna kaam (gravity ke khilaaf) karna hi padega? Agar chain ki efficiency 96% ho (aur kuch aur loss ignore karein), to pedal se kitna kaam dena padega?

Gravity ke khilaaf kaam = m g h = 60 kg × 10 m/s² × 5 m = **3000 J**

Chain 96% efficient hai: pedal se kaam = 3000 J ÷ 0.96 = **3125 J**

Loss = 3125 J − 3000 J = **125 J**

Jaanch: 96% of 3125 J = 3000 J ✅

Koi bhi gear lagao, ye 3000 J (aur loss) dena hi padega. Choti gear sirf is kaam ko **zyada pedal chakkar** par faila deti hai, taaki har chakkar mein force kam lage.

### Example 12: Insaan ke sharir ki efficiency

Ek vyakti cycle chalate waqt apne sharir se kul 2000 J energy kharch karta hai (khane se mili energy). Sharir ki efficiency lagbhag 20% maanein. Useful kaam aur heat kitni?

Useful kaam = 20% of 2000 J = 0.2 × 2000 J = **400 J**

Baaki heat = 2000 J − 400 J = **1600 J**

Jaanch: 400 J + 1600 J = 2000 J ✅. Isiliye cycle chalate waqt garmi lagti hai aur paseena aata hai.

## Part F: Sawaal hal karne ki strategy

Koi bhi machines ka sawaal dekho to ye teen kadam lo:

1. **Pehle pata karo kaunsi machine hai:** lever, pulley, incline, screw, wheel-and-axle, ya combination.
2. **Phir likho kya diya hai aur kya poochha hai:** load, effort, load distance, effort distance, ya energy.
3. **Phir socho: MA, VR ya efficiency?**
   - Load aur effort di hain: **MA = load ÷ effort**.
   - Distances di hain: **VR = effort distance ÷ load distance**.
   - Input aur output ka kaam di hai: **η = (output ÷ input) × 100%**.
   - Ideal machine hai: **MA = VR, η = 100%**.
   - Combination hai: **MAs ko guna** karo.
4. Har step par **units** likho (N, m, J), aur ant mein sochkar dekho ki answer (jaise efficiency) 100% se zyada to nahi aa gayi.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Machine input se zyada output de sakti hai"**
Ye galat hai. Example 9 mein 5 aur 4 wali MA aur VR se efficiency 125% aati hai, jo asambhav hai. Hamesha output ≤ input.

**Galti 2: "Badi (high) gear lagane se dhalaan par effort bachti hai"**
Ulta hai. Dhalaan par **choti gear (low gear, bada sprocket)** chahiye, jo force kam karti hai. Sapaat road par badi gear (chhota sprocket) tez chalne ke kaam aati hai, par zyada force maangti hai.

**Galti 3: Efficiency aur MA ko ek hi maan lena**
Efficiency ek ratio hai (output ÷ input, percent mein), jabki MA load ÷ effort hai. Ek machine ki MA 10 ho sakti hai aur efficiency sirf 70%. Dono alag cheezein hain: η = MA ÷ VR × 100%.

**Galti 4: Combination mein MAs ko jodna**
Lever MA 4 aur pulley MA 3 ka kul MA 4 + 3 = 7 nahi hota. Sahi: 4 × 3 = **12** (Example 4).

**Galti 5: Real machine mein friction bhool jaana**
Real machine mein effort ideal effort se zyada lagti hai. Example 10 mein ideal 3.82 N tha par real 19.1 N. Agar sirf ideal answer likh do to wo adhoora hoga.

## Quick Recap

- **Principle of machines:** ideal machine mein work output = work input, yaani load × load distance = effort × effort distance.
- Real machine mein **output = input − energy lost** (friction se heat aur sound).
- **Force mein faayda hota hai to distance mein nuksaan**, aur speed mein faayda hota hai to force mein nuksaan. **Free lunch nahi** milta: perpetual motion machine nahi ban sakti.
- Friction kam karne ke tareeke: oiling, ball bearings, polishing, streamlining, halke parts.
- Roz ki machines ki efficiency (lagbhag): chain 90 se 98%, electric motor 80 se 95%, petrol engine 25 se 30%, insaan 20 se 25%.
- Cycle gears: chhota sprocket zyada distance, zyada force (speed multiplier). Bada sprocket kam distance, kam force (force multiplier). Dhalaan par low gear.
- Combination mein **kul MA = individual MAs ka product**. Efficiency combination mein kam hoti jaati hai.
- Real machine mein **MA < VR**, aur MA kabhi VR se bada nahi ho sakta.
- Strategy: machine pehchano, diya hua aur poochha hua likho, phir MA, VR ya efficiency chuno.

## Practice Questions

1. In a machine, a 50 N effort moves 2 m and an 80 N load moves 1 m. Find the work input, the work output, the energy lost and the efficiency.

2. A machine of 60% efficiency receives 500 J of work input. Find the work output and the energy lost.

3. A lever of MA 5 drives a pulley system of MA 2. Find the combined ideal MA. What ideal effort lifts a 600 N load?

4. The same combination as in Question 3 has an overall efficiency of 80%. Find the real MA and the effort needed to lift the 600 N load.

5. A company claims that a machine needs an effort of 20 N moving 3 m to lift a 70 N load by 1 m. Is the claim possible? Explain using work input and work output.

6. A screw jack has a handle of length 0.4 m and a pitch of 5 mm. Find the ideal effort needed to lift a 2000 N load. Take π = 3.14. If the efficiency is 25%, find the real effort.

7. A pulley system (MA 4) is used together with a lever (MA 2) to lift a 400 N load. Find the ideal effort.

8. A person's body takes in 2400 J of energy and has an efficiency of 25% as a work machine. Find the useful work done and the energy turned into heat.

9. State whether each statement is true or false, with a reason.
   (a) A machine with MA greater than its VR is possible.
   (b) A low gear on a cycle helps while climbing a steep slope.
   (c) If a lever of MA 3 drives a lever of MA 2, the combined MA is 5.

10. Ojas says, "If I use the lowest gear on a hill, I will do no work at all." Explain in your own words why he is wrong, and what the gear actually does.

### Answers

1. Work input = 50 N × 2 m = **100 J**. Work output = 80 N × 1 m = **80 J**. Energy lost = 100 J − 80 J = **20 J**. Efficiency = (80 J ÷ 100 J) × 100% = **80%**.
   Jaanch: 80 J + 20 J = 100 J ✅

2. Work output = 60% of 500 J = 0.6 × 500 J = **300 J**. Energy lost = 500 J − 300 J = **200 J**.
   Jaanch: 300 J ÷ 500 J × 100% = 60% ✅

3. Combined ideal MA = 5 × 2 = **10**. Effort = 600 N ÷ 10 = **60 N**.
   Jaanch: 60 N × 10 = 600 N ✅

4. Real MA = 0.8 × 10 = **8**. Effort = 600 N ÷ 8 = **75 N**.
   Jaanch: 75 N × 8 = 600 N ✅ (ideal 60 N se zyada, jo sahi hai)

5. Work input = 20 N × 3 m = 60 J. Work output = 70 N × 1 m = 70 J. Output (70 J) input (60 J) se zyada hai, to claim **possible nahi** hai: machine 10 J "free" nahi de sakti.
   Jaanch: efficiency = 70 ÷ 60 × 100% = 116.7%, jo 100% se zyada hai ❌

6. Ideal effort = (2000 N × 0.005 m) ÷ (2 × 3.14 × 0.4 m) = 10 J ÷ 2.512 m = **3.98 N** (lagbhag 4 N). Real effort = 3.98 N ÷ 0.25 = **15.9 N** (lagbhag).
   Jaanch: 3.98 N × 2.512 m = 10.0 J ✅. Ideal MA = 2.512 ÷ 0.005 = 502.4, real MA = 0.25 × 502.4 = 125.6, aur 2000 ÷ 125.6 = 15.9 N ✅

7. Combined MA = 4 × 2 = 8. Ideal effort = 400 N ÷ 8 = **50 N**.
   Jaanch: 50 N × 8 = 400 N ✅

8. Useful work = 25% of 2400 J = 0.25 × 2400 J = **600 J**. Heat = 2400 J − 600 J = **1800 J**.
   Jaanch: 600 J + 1800 J = 2400 J ✅

9. (a) **False** ❌. MA = η × VR aur η 100% se zyada nahi ho sakti, to MA ≤ VR.
   (b) **True** ✅. Low gear (bada rear sprocket) pedal par kam force lagwati hai, par zyada pedal chakkar chahiye.
   (c) **False** ❌. MAs ko guna karte hain: 3 × 2 = 6, jodte nahi.

10. Hill par cycle aur Ojas ko upar uthana padta hai, jismein gravity ke khilaaf kaam m g h ke barabar hota hai. Ye kaam kisi bhi gear se kam nahi hota. Low gear (bada rear sprocket) sirf pedal par force kam kar deti hai, par uski keemat ye hai ki cycle ko upar tak pahunchane ke liye pedal ke zyada chakkar lagane padte hain: force mein faayda, distance mein nuksaan. Total kaam wahi rehta hai (aur friction ki wajah se thoda zyada).

| Kya | Formula |

| Efficiency | η = (work output ÷ work input) × 100% = (MA ÷ VR) × 100% |
