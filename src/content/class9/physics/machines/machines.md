# Topic 1: Machine Kya Hai?

Pichhle chapter mein humne seekha ki **work** = force × displacement, aur ki kaam karne ke liye **energy** lagti hai. Last mein ek baat kahi thi: "kaam wohi, bas force kam." Matlab kaam ki maatra wahi rehti hai, lekin hum use **kis tarah** karte hain, ye badal sakte hain. Kabhi kam force lagakar, kabhi force ki disha badalkar, kabhi zyada tez kaam karke.

Jo cheez ye badlaav karwati hai, use physics mein kehte hain **machine**. Is chapter mein hum machines ko samjhenge: kya hoti hain, kitna fayda deti hain, aur kaun-kaun si simple machines hoti hain. Is topic mein bas pehchaan: machine kya hai aur wo hamari madad kaise karti hai.

**Is poore chapter mein g = 10 m/s² liya gaya hai**, to 1 kg ka weight (gravity ka force) 10 N hota hai.

## Part A: Machine kya hai?

> **Machine** wo device hai jisse hum force ki **maatra (magnitude)** ya **disha (direction)** badal sakte hain, ya kaam ki **chaal** badal sakte hain, taaki kaam aasaan ho jaye.

Dhyan do: "aasaan" ka matlab ye nahi ki kaam kam ho gaya. Kaam (work) ki baat hum Part D mein dekhenge. Abhi itna samjho ki machine ek **madadgaar** hai, jaadu nahi.

Har machine mein do cheezein hamesha hoti hain:

- **Effort**: wo force jo hum lagate hain. (Haath ka dabaav, rassi ka kheenchna.)
- **Load**: wo cheez jise hume haraana ya uthana hai. Load sirf weight nahi, koi bhi rukawat ho sakti hai: dhakkan ki pakad, akhrot ke chhilke ka vajan, screw ko lakdi mein ghusne se rokne wali rukawat.

Bahut se logon ko lagta hai ki "load" matlab sirf saman ka bojh. Nahi. **Load = jo hume haraana hai.**

Tum effort lagate ho, machine us effort ko ek alag roop mein load tak pahunchati hai. Bas.

## Part B: Machine madad kaise karti hai? Teen tarike (aur ek chhota chautha)

### Tarika 1: Force multiplier (chhota effort, bada load)

Yahan machine tumhara **chhota effort** leti hai aur use load par **bade force** ki tarah lagati hai. Isse bada load kam effort se haar jaata hai.

Examples:
- **Crowbar**: ek bhaari patthar ke neeche chhoti si rod ghusakar halka dabane se patthar sarak jaata hai.
- **Nutcracker**: haath ka halka dabaav akhrot ka sakht chhilka tod deta hai.
- **Screw jack**: ghumane ke chhote-chhote effort se poori car upar uth jaati hai.

### Tarika 2: Speed multiplier (chhoti hilchal, badi hilchal)

Yahan machine ka kaam force kam karna nahi hai. Yahan effort ka **chhota sa hilna load ko kaafi door tak** hila deta hai. Isme **effort bada** lagana padta hai, lekin kaam **tezi se** hota hai.

Examples:
- **Chimta (tongs)**: tum beech mein thoda sa dabate ho aur chimte ka munh kaafi zyada hilta hai. Effort zyada lagta hai, par load ki hilchal badi hoti hai.
- **Fishing rod**: haath ko thoda hilao to rod ka sira bahut door tak jaata hai.
- **Cycle ka higher gear**: pedal ek baar ghumao to pahiya kaafi aage nikal jaata hai. (Sirf naam abhi.)

### Tarika 3: Force ki disha badalna

Kabhi machine force kam ya zyada nahi karti, **sirf disha badal deti hai.** Ye bhi bahut kaam ki baat hai.

Examples:
- **Flagpole ki rassi**: tum rassi neeche kheenchte ho aur jhanda upar jaata hai. Neeche kheenchna aasaan hai, kyunki tum apne body weight ka istemaal kar sakte ho.
- **Kuein ki pulley**: rassi neeche kheench kar bhari baalti upar uthaayi jaati hai.

### Tarika 4: Position ki suvidha (chhota sa tarika)

Kai baar machine ka fayda ye hota hai ki tum **jis jagah aur jis tarah** aasaani se khade ho sako, us tarah kaam kar sako. Jaise wheelbarrow mein tum zameen par khade rehkar hi bhaari saman aage dhakel lete ho, use uthaana nahi padta.

Pehle do tarike ek figure mein (dhyan do: sirf hilchal ka farak dikhaya hai):

```
 Force multiplier (crowbar)            Speed multiplier (chimta)

 effort (chhota) ──> neeche ko          effort (bada) ──> andar ko
          :                                      :
   ───────:─────────────────             ┌───────:───────┐
   load ^ : fulcrum                      fulcrum          load (tip)
   (bada) :                                       tip bahut zyada hilta hai
   effort ko zyada chalna padta hai      effort kam chalta hai, load zyada
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Force multiplier mein chhota effort bade load ko haraata hai (par effort ko zyada chalna padta hai), jabki speed multiplier mein effort bada lagta hai par load kaafi door tak hilta hai.

## Part C: Simple machines aur complex machines

Duniya bhar ki saari machines chhe simple machines ke jod se bani hain. Ye chhe hain:

1. **Lever** (Topic 3)
2. **Pulley** (Topic 4)
3. **Inclined plane** (ramp, dhalaan) (Topic 5)
4. **Wedge** (Topic 5)
5. **Screw** (Topic 5)
6. **Wheel-and-axle** (Topic 5)

Jab kai simple machines saath jud jaati hain, to **complex machine** (milli-juli machine) ban jaati hai:

- **Cycle**: pedal (wheel-and-axle), brake (lever), chain aur gear (wheel-and-axle) sab mile hue hain.
- **Scissors (kainchi)**: do lever, jinme se har ek mein ek wedge jaisi dhaar hai. (Sirf naam abhi.)
- **Car jack**: screw aur lever ka jod.
- **Wheelbarrow**: ek pahiya (wheel-and-axle) aur uska dhancha (lever).

Mr. Sathe ke toolbox ki saari cheezein inhi mein se hain. Isliye wo sab machines hain, chahe unmein koi motor ya bijli na ho.

## Part D: Machine extra kaam nahi deti

Ye bahut zaroori baat hai, aur bahut logon ko lagta hai ki machine "free ka kaam" deti hai. Aisa nahi hai.

> Machine tumhare diye hue kaam se **zyada kaam nahi** karti. Wo sirf effort ka **roop** badalti hai: kam force, ya alag disha, ya zyada chaal.

Ye baat poori tarah Topic 6 mein samjhenge, jahan hum dekhenge ki kaam ki maatra ka kya hota hai. Abhi bas itna yaad rakho: **machine se kaam aasaan hota hai, extra kaam nahi milta.**

## Part F: Mr. Sathe aur Dhara, toolbox ka hisaab

Ab Dhara ne ek-ek cheez uthayi aur Mr. Sathe se classify karwai.

**1. Bottle opener.**
- Effort: haath ka halka jhatka. Load: dhakkan ki pakad.
- Opener ek chhote force ko bade force mein badal kar dhakkan khol deta hai. **Force multiplier.**

**2. Chimta (tongs).**
- Effort: ungliyon ka dabaav. Load: garam angaara.
- Ungliyon ka dabna chhoti hilchal hai, par chimte ka munh (tip) kaafi zyada hilta hai. Effort bada lagta hai, par load ki hilchal badi aur tez hoti hai. **Speed multiplier.**

**3. Nutcracker.**
- Effort: haath ka dabaav. Load: akhrot ka sakht chhilka.
- Nutcracker ke dono haath lambe hain aur akhrot jod ke paas hai. Chhota effort bada force ban jaata hai. **Force multiplier.**

**4. Screwdriver.**
- Effort: haath se handle ko ghumane ka force. Load: screw ko lakdi mein ghusne se rokne wali rukawat.
- Handle mota hai aur nok patli, to chhota effort bade turning force mein badal jaata hai. **Force multiplier.**

**5. Ramp (dhalaan).**
- Mr. Sathe ke paas 100 kg ka drum hai, to uska weight = 100 kg × 10 m/s² = **1000 N**.
- Seedha upar uthane ke liye 1000 N ka effort chahiye. Ramp par ludhkaane mein usse kaafi kam effort lagta hai (kitna kam, ye Topic 5 mein).
- Effort: drum ko dhakelne ka force. Load: drum ka weight. **Force multiplier.**

Dhara boli, "To Mr. Sathe, sab machine hain! Bina bijli ke bhi."

Mr. Sathe muskuraye. "Haan Dhara. Aur ye sab inhi tarikon se kaam karti hain: ya to **force ko kam** karke, ya **disha** badalkar, ya **chaal** badalkar. Machine ki pehchaan bijli se nahi, ye batane se hoti hai ki wo kaam kaise aasaan karti hai."

**Jaanch:** 100 kg × 10 m/s² = 1000 N ✅. Paanchon cheezon mein effort aur load alag-alag pehchane gaye ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Machine wahi hai jo bijli ya petrol se chale"**

Nahi. Bottle opener, chimta, ramp jaisi cheezein bina bijli ke chalti hain, phir bhi machines hain. Machine ka matlab hai **kaam aasaan karne wala device**, motor wala nahi.

**Galti 2: "Machine extra energy ya extra kaam deti hai"**

Nahi. Machine tumhare diye hue kaam se zyada kaam nahi karti, wo sirf effort ka roop badalti hai (Part D). Poori baat Topic 6 mein.

**Galti 3: "Force multiplier distance bhi multiply karta hai"**

Ulta hota hai. Jab machine force kam karti hai, to effort ko **zyada doori** tak chalna padta hai. Jaise ramp mein drum ko seedha 1 m uthane ke bajay lambi dhalaan par kaafi door tak dhakelna padta hai. Ye baat Topic 2 mein number ke saath dekhenge.

**Galti 4: Effort aur load ko mila dena**

Effort wo force hai jo **hum** lagate hain. Load wo hai jo **hume haraana** hai. Ek tarika: "mera haath kya kar raha hai? Wo effort. Aur cheez kya roke baithi hai? Wo load."

**Galti 5: "Jo machine force kam kare, wo time bhi bachayegi"**

Zaroori nahi. Ramp par drum dhakelne mein effort kam lagta hai, par raasta lamba hone se **zyada time** bhi lag sakta hai. Force kam hona aur time kam hona alag baatein hain. (Speed multiplier mein ulta hota hai: time bachta hai par effort badhta hai.)

## Quick Recap

- **Machine** wo device hai jo force ki maatra ya disha, ya kaam ki chaal badalkar kaam aasaan karta hai. Bijli ya fuel zaroori nahi.
- **Effort** = jo force hum lagate hain. **Load** = jo hume haraana hai (weight ya koi bhi rukawat).
- **Fulcrum**, **effort arm** aur **load arm** ke naam abhi sirf jaan lo (detail Topic 3 mein).
- Machine ke teen mukhya fayde: **force multiplier** (chhota effort, bada load), **speed multiplier** (chhoti hilchal se badi hilchal, effort zyada), **direction badalna** (flagpole, kuein ki pulley). Chautha chhota fayda: position ki suvidha (wheelbarrow).
- **Simple machines chhe hain:** lever, pulley, inclined plane, wedge, screw, wheel-and-axle. Inke jod se **complex machines** (cycle, scissors, car jack, wheelbarrow) bante hain.
- Machine **extra kaam nahi deti**: sirf effort ka roop badalti hai (Topic 6 mein poori tarah).
- Mr. Sathe ke toolbox mein: bottle opener, nutcracker, screwdriver aur ramp **force multiplier**, aur chimta **speed multiplier**.
- Force multiplier mein effort ko zyada doori chalna padta hai: force kam, doori zyada (Topic 2).

## Practice Questions

1. Is a bicycle a machine? Is it a simple machine or a complex machine? Explain in one or two lines.

2. Dhara says, "A machine must run on electricity or fuel." Mr. Sathe says she is wrong. Give two examples of machines that need neither.

3. Name the effort and the load in each case:
   (a) a nutcracker cracking a walnut
   (b) a crowbar lifting a heavy stone
   (c) a rope being pulled down to raise a flag
   (d) a screwdriver turning a screw into wood

4. Classify each tool as a force multiplier, a speed multiplier, or a direction changer: (a) tongs, (b) bottle opener, (c) fishing rod, (d) pulley at a well, (e) screw jack.

5. Write the names of the six simple machines.

6. State True or False:
   (a) A machine gives out more work than the work we put in.
   (b) A force multiplier makes the effort move through a larger distance than the load moves.
   (c) A flagpole rope pulley changes the direction of the force.
   (d) A machine that reduces the force always saves time.

7. A drum has a mass of 50 kg. Find its weight in newtons. Take g = 10 m/s². Is this weight the effort or the load when we try to roll it up a ramp?

8. A fishing rod is a speed multiplier. What do we gain and what do we give up when we use it?

9. Dhara's friend says, "A wheelbarrow is a simple machine." Is she right? Explain.

10. A shopkeeper says, "My nutcracker gives me extra energy because it cracks the nut so easily." What is wrong with this statement?

### Answers

1. Haan, cycle ek machine hai. Ye ek **complex machine** hai, kyunki isme kai simple machines jude hain: pedal aur gear (wheel-and-axle), brake (lever) wagairah.

2. Bottle opener aur chimta (ya ramp, nutcracker): inme na bijli lagti hai na fuel, phir bhi ye force ki maatra ya chaal badalkar kaam aasaan karti hain. Isliye machine ka matlab "kaam aasaan karne wala device" hai, motor wala nahi.

3. (a) Effort = haath ka dabaav. Load = akhrot ke chhilke ki sakhti.
   (b) Effort = haath ka force. Load = patthar ka weight.
   (c) Effort = rassi ko neeche kheenchne ka force. Load = jhande ka weight.
   (d) Effort = haath se handle ko ghumane ka force. Load = screw ko lakdi mein ghusne se rokne wali rukawat.

4. (a) Tongs: **speed multiplier**. (b) Bottle opener: **force multiplier**. (c) Fishing rod: **speed multiplier**. (d) Kuein ki pulley: **direction changer**. (e) Screw jack: **force multiplier**.

5. Lever, pulley, inclined plane, wedge, screw, wheel-and-axle.

6. (a) **False** ❌. Machine extra kaam nahi deti, sirf effort ka roop badalti hai.
   (b) **True** ✅. Force kam hota hai to effort ko zyada doori chalna padta hai (Topic 2 mein number ke saath).
   (c) **True** ✅. Rassi neeche kheenchte hain, jhanda upar jaata hai.
   (d) **False** ❌. Ramp par effort kam lagta hai par raasta lamba hone se zyada time lag sakta hai.

7. Weight = 50 kg × 10 m/s² = **500 N**. Ramp par drum ko upar chadhane ki koshish mein ye weight **load** hai (jise hume haraana hai), effort wo force hai jo hum dhakelne mein lagate hain.
   Jaanch: 50 × 10 = 500 ✅

8. Hume faayda milta hai ki haath ki chhoti hilchal se rod ka sira kaafi door tak jaata hai (tezi/range). Hum ye khote hain ki effort bada lagana padta hai (force kam nahi hota, badhta hai).

9. Nahi, ye **complex machine** hai. Isme pahiya (wheel-and-axle) aur dhancha (lever) dono jude hain, kyunki ye kai simple machines ka jod hai.

10. Machine energy nahi deti. Akhrot tabhi tootta hai kyunki nutcracker ne tumhare chhote effort ko bade force mein badal diya, par kaam (work) wahi rehta hai jo tumne diya. Nutcracker se aasaani hui, extra energy nahi mili.
