# Topic 2: Magnetic Field aur Field Lines

Pichhle topic mein humne ek baat note ki thi: magnet ka force **door se** bhi kaam karta hai. Kaagaz ke upar rakhe paper clip ko neeche ke magnet se, chhue bina, khinchte dekha tha. Par ye "door se" asal mein kitni door tak? Magnet ke ek taraf kitna, doosri taraf kitna? Kya is asar ko hum **dekh** sakte hain, ya kam se kam **naksha (map)** bana sakte hain? Is topic mein hum magnet ke aas-paas ke us ilaake ko ek naam denge, usse ek naksha banayenge, aur phir dekhenge ki ye naksha ek **compass** ke baare mein aur poori **Prithvi (Earth)** ke baare mein kya batata hai.

## Jahaaz ki deck par compass

Ek shaam ek bada jahaaz (ship) samundar mein chal raha tha. Deck par buzurg captain Mr. Deshpande kursi par baithe the, aur unke saath ek chhota ladka Chirag tha, jo pehli baar jahaaz par aaya tha.

Captain ke saamne ek chhota sa gol compass laga hua tha, jiske andar ek patli needle thi. Jahaaz ne dheere se mod liya. Chirag ne dekha ki jahaaz ghooma, deck ghoomi, compass ka dabba bhi ghooma, par **needle apni jagah, usi disha mein tiki rahi.**

Chirag hairaan hua, "Captain saab, ye needle ko kaun pakde hue hai? Jahaaz kitna bhi ghoome, ye hamesha ek hi taraf kyun dikhati hai? Kisi ne isse dhaaga to bandha nahi hai!"

Mr. Deshpande muskuraye, "Dhaaga nahi hai beta, ek **force** hai jo dikhta nahi."

Chirag ki nazar captain ke bade steel ke toolbox par padi. Usne poocha, "Aur agar main ye bhaari steel ka toolbox compass ke bahut paas rakh doon, to kya needle tab bhi waise hi chalegi?"

"Bahut accha sawal," Mr. Deshpande bole. "Pehle ye samajh lo ki magnet ke aas-paas ka force kaise 'failta' hai. Phir tumhare dono sawalon ka jawab apne aap mil jayega." Is kahani ko hum Part D mein solve karenge.

## Part A: Magnetic field kya hai?

### Force ka ilaaka

Magnet ko lohe ki pin ke paas laao: pin khinchti hai. Magnet ko thoda door karo: pin ab bhi khinchti hai, par kamzor. Aur door karo: ek jagah aati hai jahan se pin ko kuch pata hi nahi chalta. Yaani magnet ke **aas-paas ek ilaaka** hai jahan uska asar hota hai, aur us ilaake ke bahar nahi hota.

> **Magnetic field** = magnet ke aas-paas ka wo ilaaka jahan uska magnetic force mehsoos kiya ja sakta hai: yaani wahan koi magnetic material (jaise lohe ki pin) ya doosra magnet rakhne par us par force lagta hai.

"Field" shabd ko kheti ke "khet" ki tarah socho: khet ek ilaaka hai. Magnetic field bhi ek ilaaka hai, bas wo aankhon se nahi dikhta.

### Field kahan taakatwar, kahan kamzor?

Pichhle topic mein dekha tha ki lohe ki pins magnet ke **poles** par sabse zyada chipakti hain, beech mein kam. Yahi field ki taakat batata hai:

- Magnet ke **poles ke paas** field sabse **taakatwar (strong)** hota hai.
- Magnet se jaise-jaise **door** jaate hain, field **kamzor (weak)** hota jata hai.

### Iron filings se field ko dekhna

Ek experiment karte hain. Ek bar magnet ke upar ek safed kaagaz rakho. Kaagaz par lohe ka baareek burada (iron filings) halke se chhidko, aur kaagaz ko ungli se halke-halke thapthapao (tap karo).

Tapping se filings thodi hil kar free ho jati hain, aur magnet ke force se wo khud ko ek khaas pattern mein jama leti hain: **ek pole se doosre pole tak jati hui curved lines**. Poles ke paas filings ghani aur ek jagah jama hoti hain, door jaate-jaate kam.

```

            ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~
         ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~
      ~ ~ ~                         ~ ~ ~
    ~ ~ ~  ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~  ~ ~ ~

  ~ ~  ~ ~    [N═════════════════S]    ~ ~  ~ ~
   ~ ~  ~ ~                           ~ ~  ~ ~
    ~ ~ ~  ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~  ~ ~ ~
```

Dhyan do: filings **lines ki tarah** jami hain, par ye lines asal mein kaagaz par likhi nahi hain. Filings sirf us field ki disha dikha rahi hain jo pehle se wahan maujood tha. Ye lines hi aage "field lines" kahlayengi.

## Part B: Field lines (force ki lines)

Iron filings ka pattern har baar naye sire se chhidakna padta hai. Isliye scientists ne ek aasan tareeka nikala: field ko **lines** se dikhana. Inhe **magnetic field lines** (ya **lines of force**) kehte hain.

> **Field lines** = kalpanik (imaginary) lines jo magnetic field ki **disha** aur uski **taakat** dikhati hain.

Ye lines sach-much koi dhaaga ya taar nahi hain: ye sirf ek **naksha** hain, jaise map par latitude-longitude ki lines sach mein zameen par nahi khinchi hotin.

### Field lines ke chaar gun (properties)

**Gun 1: Bahar N se S ki taraf, aur andar S se N ki taraf.** Magnet ke **bahar** field lines **north pole se nikalti hain aur south pole mein ghusti hain.** Magnet ke **andar** wo S se N ki taraf chalti hain. Isliye field lines **band loop** (closed loop) banati hain: kahin shuru ya khatam nahi hotin, ghoom ke wapas aa jati hain.

**Gun 2: Kisi bhi bindu par field ki disha, us bindu par line ke tangent ke saath hoti hai.** Kisi bindu par field ki disha wahi hai jis taraf wahan ek free **north pole** chalega (ya compass needle ka **N end** point karega). Ye disha line par arrowhead se dikhayi jati hai. Curved line par kisi bindu par "tangent" matlab us bindu par line ko chhookar jati seedhi line, usi taraf jis taraf arrow hai.

**Gun 3: Field lines kabhi ek doosre ko cross nahi karti.** Agar do lines kisi bindu par cross karti, to us bindu par field ki do alag disha hoti: yaani compass needle ke paas do alag disha jaane ka option hota. Par needle ek hi taraf jaati hai: ek bindu par field ki sirf **ek** disha hoti hai.

**Gun 4: Jahan field zyada taakatwar, wahan lines paas-paas; jahan kamzor, wahan door-door.** Poles ke paas lines ghani (paas-paas) hoti hain, door jaate-jaate failti jati hain.

### Bar magnet ka field

Neeche bar magnet ke field ki tasveer hai. Magnet ke **bahar** har line N se nikalti hai, ghoomti hai aur S mein ghusti hai. Poles ke paas lines paas-paas hain, magnet se door door-door. (Ye ek sketch hai: lines ki ginti pakki nahi, bas pattern dekhna hai.)

```
                          ---->----
                       ~---       ----
                     ~~~             ~~~
                    ~~        -        ~~
                    (    ----->-----    ~
                    (  ^~-         -~v  )
                    ^ ~~  ---->----  ~~ v
                    ~~(~---       ----~~~
      ---------<-----[N═══════════════S]-------<-----
                    ~~(~---       ----~~~
                    v ~~  ---->----  ~~ ^
                    (  v~-         -~^  )
                    (    ----->-----    ~
                    ~~        ~        ~~
                     ~~~             ~~~
                       ~---       ----
                          ---->----
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Bar magnet ka magnetic field: bahar lines N se S ki taraf (arrowheads), poles ke paas ghani aur door jaakar door-door.

Ek sachchi baat aur: field lines magnet ke **andar bhi** chalti hain, S se N ki taraf, aur isi se ek band loop poora hota hai.

```
                    ----->-----
                 ----         ---~
                ~~               ~~
               ~~                 ~~
              ^~                   v~
              (                     )
             [N<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<<S]
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Ek field line ka band loop: bahar N se S, andar S se N (andar ke "<" arrowheads).

### Horseshoe magnet ka field

Horseshoe (U-shape) magnet mein dono pole paas-paas hote hain. Dono arms ke beech field lines lagbhag **seedhi aur ek doosre ke samanantar (parallel)** hoti hain, yaani wahan field lagbhag ek jaisa (uniform) hai.

```
         .~~~~~~~~~~~>~~~~~~~~~~~.
        /                         \
       N                           S
     ┌───┐                       ┌───┐
     │   │   ────────────>       │   │
     │   │   ────────────>       │   │
     │   │   ────────────>       │   │
     │   │                       │   │
     │   └───────────────────────┘   │
     └───────────────────────────────┘
```

> **Figure 2.4** – Horseshoe magnet: arms ke beech lines lagbhag seedhi, parallel aur barabar doori par (uniform field), N se S ki taraf.

### Do magnets ke beech ka field

**Unlike poles saamne (N ke saamne S):** magnets ek doosre ko khinchte hain. Lines seedhi N se S tak jaati hain, beech mein seedhi aur kinaron par bulge karti hui.

```
                            ---->----
                         ~---       ----
                       ~~~             ~~~
                      ~^ ------->------- v~
                      (~--             ---~
        [S═════════N] ---------->--------[S═════════N]
                      (~--             ---~
                      ~v ------->------- ^~
                       ~~~             ~~~
                         ~---       ----
                            ---->----
```

> **Figure 2.5** – Unlike poles (N ke saamne S): lines pehle magnet ke N se doosre ke S tak jaati hain, yaani attraction.

**Like poles saamne (N ke saamne N):** magnets ek doosre ko dhakelte hain. Lines ek doosre se **door mud jaati hain**, aur dono poles ke beech ek jagah aati hai jahan field zero hota hai. Is bindu ko **neutral point** kehte hain (bas naam jaan lo, X se dikhaya hai).

```

    ~~-           ~~(                       )~~           -~~
    ~~------<------~~                       ~~------>------~~
     ~~-         -~~                         ~~-         -~~
       -----<-----                             ----->-----
```

> **Figure 2.6** – Like poles (N ke saamne N): lines ek doosre se door mud jati hain (repulsion); beech ka X neutral point hai.

## Part C: Compass se field ki lines banana

Bina iron filings ke bhi field line bana sakte hain, ek chhote compass ki madad se.

**Kadam:**
1. Kaagaz par bar magnet rakho (N ek taraf, S doosri taraf) aur uske kinaare pencil se mark karo.
2. Chhota compass magnet ke N pole ke paas rakho. Needle ke **dono sire** par pencil se do dot lagao.
3. Compass ko uthao aur is tarah rakho ki uski needle ka **S sira** pichhle dot par aa jaye. Phir needle ke naye sire par dot lagao.
4. Ye dohrate raho jab tak compass magnet ke S pole tak na pahunch jaye.
5. Sab dots ko smooth curve se jodo: ye **ek field line** hai. Compass needle ka N sira jis taraf point karta tha, wahi arrow ki disha hai (N se S ki taraf).
6. Naye shuruaati dot se aur lines banao.

```
                 o   o   o   o
             o                   o
          o                         o
         o                           o
        ^                             v
      [N═════════════════════════════S]
```

> **Figure 2.7** – Compass se banayi gayi field line: har "o" pencil ka ek dot hai; dots jodne par N se S tak curved line milti hai.

## Part D: Magnetic compass, aur captain ka jawab

### Compass kaise bana hai

> **Magnetic compass** = ek chhoti magnetised needle, jo apne **beech (centre) par pivot** par tiki hoti hai aur **aaraam se ghoom sakti hai**. Ye ek dabbe (case) mein hoti hai jis par disha ke nishaan (N, E, S, W) bane hote hain.

Needle khud ek chhota magnet hai. Isliye wo jis magnetic field mein rakhi ho, uski disha mein khud ko seedha kar leti hai: **needle ka N sira field ki disha mein point karta hai.** Khali jagah (kisi magnet ke bina) mein ye N sira Prithvi ke **geographic north** ki taraf rehta hai. Isi liye sailor (jahaaz ke), trekker (pahaad par chalne wale) aur pilot (hawai jahaaz ke) compass se disha pata karte hain.

```
                   N
              .---------.
            .'     ^     '.
       W   (       ║       )   E
            '.     ●     .'
              '-.  ║  .-'
                 '---'
                   S
```

> **Figure 2.8** – Compass: needle beech ke pivot (●) par tiki hai; upar wala sira (N end) north ki taraf point karta hai, dabbe par N, E, S, W likhe hain.

### Captain ke dono sawal solve

**Sawal 1: Jahaaz ghoome to needle kyun nahi ghoomti?**

Prithvi ke aas-paas ek **magnetic field** hai (Part E mein dekhenge). Compass needle us field ki disha mein tiki rehti hai. Jahaaz aur compass ka dabba ghoomte hain, par needle ko field ki disha pakde rehti hai, isliye needle usi taraf point karti hai. Chirag ne jo "dhaaga" socha tha, wo asal mein Prithvi ka **magnetic field** hai.

**Sawal 2: Steel ka toolbox paas rakhne par?**

Needle wahi disha batati hai jo us jagah ke **kul** field ki hoti hai. Steel (lohe jaisa) magnetic material hai: compass ka chhota magnet use khinchta hai, aur needle toolbox ki taraf **ghoom jati hai (deflect hoti hai)**, north se hat kar. Agar toolbox khud magnet ban chuka ho, ya paas mein koi magnet ya bijli ka taar ho, to bhi needle bhatak jati hai. Isiliye jahaaz par compass ko steel ki bhaari cheezon, magnets aur bijli ke taaron se door rakha jata hai.

Mr. Deshpande bole, "Dekha Chirag? Compass ek imaandaar dost hai, par uske paas dusre magnet ya steel rakh do to wo ghalat disha dikhane lagta hai." Chirag ne turant toolbox compass se door khiska diya.

## Part E: Prithvi ek bada magnet hai

Compass ki needle ka N sira hamesha north ki taraf kyun jata hai? Iska jawab ye hai: **Prithvi khud ek bahut bada magnet ki tarah vyavhaar karti hai.** Maano ki Prithvi ke andar ek bahut bada bar magnet rakha ho, aur uska magnetic field poori Prithvi ke aas-paas phailta hai.

```
                      Geographic North
                             :
                     .-~~~~~~:~~~~~-.
                  .-'         :  S    '-.
        ^        /            : ║       \        ^
        ^       (             :║         )       ^
        ^       (             ║:         )       ^
        ^       (            ║ :         )       ^
        ^        \          N  :        /        ^
                  '-.          :      .-'
                     '-~~~~~~~~:~~~~-'
                             :
                      Geographic South
```

> **Figure 2.9** – Prithvi ke andar ka kalpanik bar magnet (axis se lagbhag 10° jhuka hua, ye angle approximate hai). Bahar ke arrows (^) field ki disha dikhate hain: zameen ke paas south se north ki taraf.

### Magnetic north ek magnetic SOUTH pole hai

Ek dilchasp baat. Compass needle ka **N sira** Prithvi ke north ki taraf jata hai. Ye "N sira" ek north pole hai. Aur hum jaante hain ki **unlike poles attract karte hain** (north pole south pole ko khinchta hai). Isliye needle ka N sira Prithvi ke us pole ki taraf kheencha ja raha hai jo ek **magnetic SOUTH pole** hai.

Yaani Prithvi ke andar ke kalpanik bar magnet ka jo sira **geographic north ke paas** hai, wo asal mein us magnet ka **S pole** hai. (Naam "magnetic north pole" isliye rakha gaya ki wo north disha mein hai, par bar magnet ke hisaab se wo S pole hai.)

### Magnetic poles aur geographic poles alag hain

- **Geographic poles:** Prithvi ke ghoomne ke axis ke sire.
- **Magnetic poles:** Prithvi ke magnetic field ke sire. Ye geographic poles ke **paas** hain, par **bilkul wahan nahi**. Kalpanik bar magnet ka axis ghoomne ke axis se lagbhag 10° jhuka hua hai (approximate).
- Magnetic poles **ek jagah par fixed nahi** hain: ye dheere-dheere khiskte (wander) rehte hain.

### Declination aur dip (sirf naam)

- **Declination:** kisi jagah par **geographic north aur magnetic north ke beech ka kon (angle)**. Compass ka N sira magnetic north ki taraf jata hai, isliye wo geographic north se thoda alag dikhata hai. Declination alag jagahon par alag hota hai.
- **Dip:** Prithvi ka field zameen ke saath ek khaas kon par jhukta hai: ise dip kehte hain. Ye abhi sirf naam jaan lo. (Aage ki class mein.)

### Prithvi ka field aata kahan se hai? (aage ki class mein)

Scientists ke mutabik Prithvi ke andar, **bahari core mein pighle hue lohe (molten iron) ki gati** se ye field banta hai. Ye "aage ki class mein" seekhenge.

### Prithvi ke field ke kaam

- **Disha dhoondhna (navigation):** compass isi field ki wajah se kaam karta hai.
- **Jaanwar:** kaha jata hai ki kai pravasi (migrating) pakshi aur samudri kachhue (turtles) lambe safar mein is field se raasta pehchante hain. Scientists ko lagta hai ki ye sahi hai, par poori tarah pakka nahi hai.
- **Aurora:** Suraj se aane wale charged particles Prithvi ke field ki wajah se poles ke paas khinch jate hain, aur wahan aasmaan mein rangeen roshni (aurora) banti hai. (Ek line ki baat, bas jaan lo.)

### Magnetic shielding

Kabhi kisi nazuk instrument ko bahar ke magnetic field se bachana hota hai. Iske liye us instrument ke charon taraf **naram lohe (soft iron) ka chhalla (ring)** rakhte hain. Field lines lohe ke andar se guzarti hain, aur chhalle ke andar ki jagah lagbhag field-free rehti hai. Ise **magnetic shielding** kehte hain.

## Part F: Solved examples

**Example 1 (compass ki disha, 6 jagah):** Neeche bar magnet ke aas-paas 6 jagahon par chhota compass rakha hai. Har jagah needle ke **N sire** ki disha batao. (Bar magnet ka N bayein taraf hai.)

```
           5      1
           ·      ·

     3 ·  [N═════════════S] · 4

                  ·      ·
                  2      6
```

> **Figure 2.10** – Bar magnet ke aas-paas compass ki 6 jagah (1 aur 2 beech ke upar aur neeche, 3 N ke bayein, 4 S ke daayein, 5 N ke upar, 6 S ke neeche).

Rule: needle ka N sira field ki disha mein point karta hai, aur bahar field N se S ki taraf jaati hai.

- **Jagah 1 (beech ke upar):** magnet ke N se dhakka (daayein), S ki taraf khinchav (daayein): needle **daayein (→)**.
- **Jagah 2 (beech ke neeche):** wahi: **daayein (→)**.
- **Jagah 3 (N ke bayein, axis par):** N se door dhakka: **bayein (←)**.
- **Jagah 4 (S ke daayein, axis par):** S ki taraf khinchav, yaani **bayein (←)**.
- **Jagah 5 (N ke thik upar):** N se door: **upar (↑)** (lagbhag).
- **Jagah 6 (S ke thik neeche):** S ki taraf: **upar (↑)** (lagbhag).

**Jaanch:** Jagah 1, 2: field line magnet ke beech N se S ki taraf, yaani → ✅. Jagah 3: line N se bayein nikalti hai ✅. Jagah 4: line S mein daayein se ghusti hai, yaani ← ✅

**Example 2 (do magnets ke beech field ki disha):** Do bar magnets seedhi line mein hain, beech mein thodi jagah. Bayein magnet ko L, daayein ko R kaho. Beech ke bindu par compass ka N sira kis taraf jayega?

- **(i) N-S** (L ka daayein sira N, R ka bayein sira S): field N se S tak: **daayein (→)**.
- **(ii) S-N** (L ka daayein sira S, R ka bayein sira N): field R ke N se L ke S tak: **bayein (←)**.
- **(iii) N-N:** beech ke bindu par field **zero (neutral point)**; usse bayein (L ke N ke paas) field L ke N se door, yaani **daayein (→)**; usse daayein (R ke N ke paas) **bayein (←)** (dono taraf ki lines N se door, neutral point ki taraf aakar mud jati hain).
- **(iv) S-S:** beech mein neutral point; usse bayein (L ke S ke paas) field L ke S ki taraf, yaani **bayein (←)**; usse daayein **daayein (→)** (lines dono S ki taraf jati hain).

**Jaanch:** Unlike arrangement (i, ii) mein field N se S ki taraf ✅. Like arrangement (iii, iv) mein beech ka bindu field-free ✅

**Example 3 (kahan field zyada?):** Bindu P bar magnet ke N pole ke bahut paas hai, jahan lines paas-paas hain. Bindu Q magnet se door hai, jahan lines door-door hain. Kahan field zyada hai?

- Jahan lines paas-paas, wahan field zyada. **P par field zyada** hai.

**Jaanch:** Poles ke paas lohe ki pins sabse zyada chipakti hain: wahan field sabse zyada ✅

**Example 4 (lines ginna):** Ek jaisi chhoti window P (N pole ke paas) aur Q (door) par rakhi hai. Kitni lines guzarti hain? Kahan field zyada?

```
 Window P (pole ke paas)          Window Q (magnet se door)
 ┌─────────────────┐              ┌─────────────────┐
 │ ───────────>    │              │                 │
 │ ───────────>    │              │ ───────────>    │
 │ ───────────>    │              │                 │
 │ ───────────>    │              │ ───────────>    │
 │ ───────────>    │              │                 │
 └─────────────────┘              └─────────────────┘
```

> **Figure 2.11** – Same size ki window mein P par 5 lines, Q par 2 lines.

P par 5 lines, Q par 2 lines. P par field **zyada**. Anuman: 5 ÷ 2 = 2.5, yaani P par field Q se lagbhag **2.5 guna** (sirf sketch ka anuman).

**Jaanch:** 5 ÷ 2 = 2.5 ✅ aur 2 × 2.5 = 5 ✅

**Example 5 (sketch poora karo):** Bar magnet ke upar 3 lines (a, b, c) bani hain, par arrowheads nahi lage. Kahan, kis taraf lagenge?

```
                   ------a------
                 ~~-           --~
                ~~       b       ~~
                (   -----------   ~
               (  ~~-         -~~  )
               (~~~  ----c----  ~~))
                ~(~---       ----~~
                [N═══════════════S]
```

> **Figure 2.12** – Teen lines bina arrowhead ke; N bayein, S daayein.

Teeno lines **N se S ki taraf**: upar a, b, c ke beech mein arrow **bayein se daayein (→)**. Bayein side par arrow **upar (^)**, daayein side par arrow **neeche (v)**.

**Jaanch:** Bahar har line N (bayein) se S (daayein) ki taraf ✅

**Example 6 (steel toolbox ke paas compass):** Compass ke bahut paas bada steel ka box rakha hai. Needle kya karegi?

- Steel magnetic material hai: needle ka chhota magnet use khinchta hai, to needle **toolbox ki taraf ghoom jati hai**, north se hat kar.
- Agar box khud ek **magnet** ho: uska nazdeeki pole **S** ho to needle ka **N sira** us ki taraf; nazdeeki pole **N** ho to needle ka **S sira** us ki taraf point karega.

**Jaanch:** Unlike poles attract: needle ka N sira box ke S ko, S sira box ke N ko khinchta hai ✅

**Example 7 (horseshoe ke beech compass):** Horseshoe magnet ke bayein arm par N, daayein par S hai. Beech mein rakhe compass ka N sira kis taraf?

- Field N se S ki taraf: needle ka N sira **daayein (→)**, S arm ki taraf.

**Jaanch:** Needle ka N sira S pole ki taraf khincha jata hai ✅

**Example 8 (Prithvi ka sawal):** Compass needle ka N sira north ki taraf kyun jata hai? Prithvi ke north ki taraf ka magnetic pole kaisa hai?

- N sira ek north pole hai. Unlike poles khinchte hain, isliye wo Prithvi ke ek **magnetic S pole** ki taraf khincha ja raha hai.
- Isliye Prithvi ke north ke paas ka magnetic pole, kalpanik bar magnet ka **S pole** hai (naam magnetic "north" hai, par bar magnet ke hisaab se wo S hai).

**Jaanch:** N-end north ki taraf aur N ko S khinchta hai, to north ke paas S pole ✅

**Example 9 (declination ke saath):** Sign rule (sirf is example ke liye): declination 3° **East** matlab magnetic north, geographic north se 3° east mein hai. Compass angle magnetic north se naapta hai (clockwise). Ek jagah ka true bearing 90° (east) hai. Compass kitna padhega?

- East wali disha magnetic north se 90° − 3° = **87°** par hai: compass **87°** dikhayega.
- Declination 3° **West** hota, to compass 90° + 3° = **93°**.
- Dono padhne ka antar: 93° − 87° = **6°** (= 2 × 3°).

**Jaanch:** East ke liye true = compass + 3° = 87° + 3° = 90° ✅. West ke liye true = compass − 3° = 93° − 3° = 90° ✅

**Example 10 (jahaaz ka heading):** Jahaaz ka compass 150° padhta hai. Declination 3° West. True heading?

- West: true = compass − 3° = 150° − 3° = **147°**.
- East hota: true = 150° + 3° = **153°**.

**Jaanch:** West: 147° + 3° = 150° ✅. East: 153° − 3° = 150° ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Field lines asal mein dikhne wali lines hain."** Nahi. Wo kalpanik hain; iron filings sirf unka pattern dikhati hain.

**Galti 2: "Field lines ek doosre ko cross kar sakti hain."** Nahi. Cross karne par ek bindu par do disha hoti, jabki ek bindu par field ki ek hi disha hoti hai.

**Galti 3: "Lines sirf magnet ke bahar chalti hain."** Nahi. Andar bhi chalti hain, S se N ki taraf, aur band loop banati hain.

**Galti 4: "Bahar arrow S se N ki taraf hota hai."** Nahi. Bahar arrow **N se S** ki taraf hota hai.

**Galti 5: "Compass ka N sira Prithvi ke north pole ko 'N, N ko khinche' ki wajah se point karta hai."** Nahi. Like poles dhakelte hain. Prithvi ke north ke paas ka magnetic pole asal mein ek **magnetic S pole** hai.

**Galti 6: "Prithvi ke magnetic aur geographic poles ek hi hain."** Nahi. Paas hain, par bilkul wahi nahi, aur magnetic poles khiskte rehte hain.

## Quick Recap

- **Magnetic field** = magnet ke aas-paas ka ilaaka jahan uska force mehsoos hota hai; poles ke paas sabse zyada, door jaakar kam.
- **Field lines** kalpanik hain; bahar N se S, andar S se N, band loop.
- Kisi bindu par field ki disha line ke **tangent** ke saath (free N pole ya compass ke N sire ki disha).
- Lines **kabhi cross nahi** karti; jahan field zyada, wahan paas-paas.
- Horseshoe ke arms ke beech field lagbhag uniform; unlike poles ke beech N se S; like poles par lines door mud jati hain, neutral point par field zero.
- **Compass** = pivot par tiki chhoti magnetised needle; N sira geographic north ki taraf; steel, magnet ya current paas ho to bhatakti hai.
- **Prithvi ek bada magnet** (lagbhag 10° jhuka kalpanik bar magnet); north ke paas magnetic **S** pole; magnetic poles khiskte hain; declination aur dip sirf naam.
- Prithvi ka field: navigation, shayad pakshi aur kachhue, aurora; magnetic shielding naram lohe ke chhalle se.

### Answers

   Jaanch: 3 × 2 = 6 ✅

   Jaanch: 63° − 3° = 60° ✅
