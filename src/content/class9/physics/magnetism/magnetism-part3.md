# Topic 3: Magnetisation aur Demagnetisation

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki har magnet ke aas-paas ek **magnetic field** hoti hai, jise field lines se dikhate hain: bahar N se S ki taraf, band loops mein, aur jahan field zyada tez wahan lines paas-paas. Ab ek natural sawal uthta hai: **ek sadharan lohe ki cheez ko magnet kaise banaya jaata hai, aur ek magnet kamzor kaise ho jaata hai?** Is topic mein hum magnet banane (magnetisation) aur magnetism khone (demagnetisation) dono ke tareeke dekhenge, aur ek "tiny magnets" wali simple tasveer se unka "kyun" samjhenge.

## School lab ki ek sui aur ek gira hua magnet

School ki physics lab mein Ms. Pradhan ne Rajveer ko ek steel ki silai wali sui (sewing needle) di aur boli, "Pehle isse chhote pins ke paas le jao. Dekho kya hota hai."

Rajveer ne sui pins ke paas rakhi. Ek bhi pin nahi uthi. "Ma'am, ye to sirf ek sadharan sui hai."

Ms. Pradhan ne ek bar magnet uthaya. "Ab is magnet ke **ek hi sire (N pole)** se sui ko ek end se doosre end tak ek hi disha mein ragdo. Har baar magnet ko sui se door utha kar wapas shuru wale end par laao. Aise kam se kam 30-40 baar karo."

Rajveer ne dhairya se wahi kiya. Phir jab usne sui pins ke paas rakhi, to **sui ne kai chhote pins utha liye.** Wo chakit reh gaya.

Usi lab mein ek purana magnet pada tha, jo Rajveer se pichhle hafte kai baar gir chuka tha, aur ek din heater ke paas bhi bhool gaya tha. Ms. Pradhan ne hasi mein kaha, "Is magnet se bhi pins uthao." Pehle ye magnet 15 pins uthata tha. Ab usne bas 4 pins uthaye.

Rajveer ne soch kar poochha, "Ma'am, sui mein magnetism **kahan se aayi**? Aur is purane magnet ki magnetism **kahan chali gayi**?" Ye dono sawal hum topic ke beech mein solve karenge.

## Part A: Magnetic aur non-magnetic material, aur "tiny magnets" ki tasveer

### Kaun magnet ban sakta hai?

Har cheez magnet nahi ban sakti.

- **Magnetic materials:** lohe (iron), steel, nickel, cobalt, aur inke kuch mishran (alloys) jaise alnico. Inhe magnet kheenchta hai, aur ye khud magnet ban sakte hain.
- **Non-magnetic materials:** copper, aluminium, brass, plastic, lakdi, kaanch, kagaz. Inhe magnet nahi kheenchta, aur ye magnet nahi ban sakte.

Isliye ek **copper ke taar ko magnet nahi banaya ja sakta**, chaahe use kitna bhi ragdo. (Isse hum Example 7 mein dobara dekhenge.)

### Tiny magnets: sui mein magnetism kahan se aayi?

Ek simple tasveer imagine karo (ye sirf ek soch hai, poori sachchai nahi): **lohe ya steel ka har tukda, chaahe kitna bhi chhota ho, andar se ginti na ho sakne wale chhote-chhote magnets (tiny magnets) se bana hua hai.** Har tiny magnet ke apne N aur S hain. Vigyan mein inhe **magnetic domains** kehte hain: aage ki class mein inki detail aayegi. Abhi bas naam aur tasveer.

- **Unmagnetised piece mein** ye tiny magnets **har disha mein** ghoomte rehte hain. Kuch idhar, kuch udhar. Isliye ek ka asar doosre ke asar ko **kaat deta hai (cancel)**, aur poore tukde ka koi asar bahar nahi dikhta.
- **Magnetised piece mein** ye tiny magnets **ek hi disha mein line up** ho jaate hain. Sab ke asar jud jaate hain, aur poora tukda ek magnet ban jaata hai: ek sire par N, doosre par S.

```
  Unmagnetised tukda                   Magnetised tukda
  (tiny magnets har disha mein)        (sab ek hi disha mein)

   ↗   ↙   ↑   ←   ↘                    S  → → → → → → →  N
   ↓   →   ↖   ↗   ↙                       → → → → → → →
   ←   ↘   ↑   ↙   →                       → → → → → → →
   ↖   ↓   ↗   ←   ↘                       → → → → → → →

   Asar ek-doosre ko kaat dete hain     Asar jud jaate hain: ek N, ek S
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Har tiny magnet ek chhota sa teer (→) hai; teer ki nok uska N sira hai. Alag-alag disha mein hone par poora tukda magnet nahi, sab ek disha mein aane par magnet.

Ab Rajveer ke pehle sawal ka jawab: **sui mein magnetism bahar se "beh kar" nahi aayi.** Sui ke andar tiny magnets pehle se the. Bas wo aadhe-adhure, har disha mein the. Magnet se baar-baar ek hi disha mein ragadne se wo tiny magnets ek hi disha mein **line up** ho gaye.

**Magnetisation** = kisi magnetic material ke tiny magnets ko ek disha mein line up karke use magnet banana.

### Ek tukde ko tod do, to?

Topic 1 mein dekha tha ki magnet ke poles alag nahi ho sakte. Ab iska "kyun" bhi samajh aata hai. Bar magnet ko beech se tod do: har tukde ke andar bhi tiny magnets pehle jaise hi line up the. Isliye **har tukda khud ek poora magnet ban jaata hai**, apne N aur S ke saath.

```
  Poora magnet:        S [→ → → → → → → →] N

  Beech se toda:       S [→ → → →] N     S [→ → → →] N
                           tukda 1             tukda 2

  Phir se toda:        S[→ →]N  S[→ →]N  S[→ →]N  S[→ →]N
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Magnet ko jitna bhi todo, har tukde ke apne N aur S bante hain, kyunki har tukde ke andar tiny magnets pehle jaise hi line up hain.

## Part B: Magnetisation ke tareeke

### Tareeka 1: Single touch method

Is tareeke mein ek steel ki chhad (ya sui) ko ek **strong magnet ke ek hi pole** se ek hi disha mein ragadte hain.

Kadam:
1. Steel ki chhad ko mez par rakho aur uske dono sire nishaan kar lo (A aur B).
2. Magnet ka **ek hi pole** (maan lo N pole) chhad ke sire A par rakho.
3. Use dheere se, thoda dabake, **A se B tak** ragdo.
4. B par pahunch kar magnet ko chhad se **kaafi door utha lo** (seedha upar ek chaap banate hue).
5. Phir A par wapas laao (hawa mein, chhad ko chhue bina) aur kadam 3 se 30-40 baar dohrao.

**Pole ka rule:** *Jis sire par ragadna khatam hota hai (jahan se magnet hatta hai), us sire par us pole se **ulta** pole banta hai jis pole se ragda gaya tha.* Yaani N pole se ragdo to akhri sire par **S pole**; S pole se ragdo to akhri sire par **N pole**. Doosra sira (jahan se shuru kiya) apne aap ulta pole ban jaata hai.

```
        S
        ║   magnet (N pole neeche, har baar A se shuru)
        N  →  →  →  →  →  →  ↗  (B par uthao)
   ┌──────────────────────────┐
   │ A                      B │   steel ki chhad
   └──────────────────────────┘
      N                      S
   (shuru ka sira)       (akhri sira)
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Single touch: magnet ke N pole ko A se B tak ek hi disha mein ragdo. Akhri sira B, S pole banta hai, aur A sira N pole.

**Zaroori baatein:**
- Ragadna **hamesha ek hi disha mein** hona chahiye. Aage-peechhe ragadne se tiny magnets ek taraf line up hote hi doosri taraf ghum jaate hain: **magnetise nahi, demagnetise** hota hai.
- Magnet ko wapas laate waqt **door utha lo**, chhad par ghasitte hue wapas mat laao.
- Strong magnet use karo: kamzor magnet se kam asar hota hai.

### Tareeka 2: Double touch (divided touch) method

Ye tareeka single touch se zyada achha hai. Isme **do bar magnets** lete hain, aur chhad ke beech mein dono ke **ulte (unlike) poles** rakhte hain: ek ka N pole aur doosre ka S pole. Dono magnets ke beech chhota sa gap rakhte hain (lakdi ka tukda beech mein rakh sakte ho).

Kadam:
1. Dono magnets ko chhad ke beech mein rakho: ek ka N pole aur doosre ka S pole.
2. N pole wale magnet ko **dayein (right) sire** tak ragdo, aur S pole wale ko **bayein (left) sire** tak. Dono ek saath, ulti disha mein.
3. Dono siron par pahunch kar dono magnets ko door utha lo, aur wapas beech mein laao.
4. Ye 30-40 baar karo.

**Pole:** rule wahi hai: jis sire par N pole ka ragadna khatam hua, wahan **S pole**; jis sire par S pole ka ragadna khatam hua, wahan **N pole**.

```
     S pole wala magnet ←  ·  → N pole wala magnet
              (dono beech se shuru)

   ┌───────────────────────────────────┐
   │ A                               B │   steel ki chhad
   └───────────────────────────────────┘
     N                                 S
   (S pole wale magnet ka           (N pole wale magnet ka
    ragadna yahan khatam)            ragadna yahan khatam)
```

> **Figure 3.4** – Double touch: beech se dono taraf ragdo. N pole wala magnet dayein sire B par khatam hota hai, to B par S pole; S pole wala magnet bayein sire A par khatam hota hai, to A par N pole.

Ye tareeka isliye achha hai ki dono taraf se ek saath tiny magnets line up hote hain, to chhad zyada tezi se aur mazboot magnet banti hai.

### Tareeka 3: Electrical method (sabse tez aur sabse achha)

Steel ki chhad ko ek **coil (solenoid)**, yaani taar ke kai lipte hue gheron, ke andar rakhte hain, aur coil mein se **direct current (DC)** bhejte hain. Chhad kuch hi second mein achhi tarah magnetised ho jaati hai, aur ye tareeka sabse tez aur sabse achha mana jaata hai, kyunki isse bahut mazboot magnet bante hain.

```
       ┌── cell ──┐
       │          │
   ┌───┴──────────┴───┐
   │ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○ ○  │   coil ke andar steel ki chhad
   │ [N═════════S]    │   (poles current ki disha se tay hote hain:
   └──────────────────┘    baayein sire se dekhne par current anticlockwise
                           ghoomta hai, isliye baayan sira N)
```

> **Figure 3.5** – Coil ke andar rakhi steel ki chhad DC current se magnet ban jaati hai. Current ki disha ulti karo to N aur S bhi ulte ho jaate hain. (Kaun sa sira N hoga, iska poora right-hand rule Topic 4 mein.)

**Poles kaise tay hote hain** aur current ke magnetic effect ka poora rule hum **Topic 4** mein seekhenge. Yahan bas itna yaad rakho: coil mein DC current ki disha se chhad ke poles tay hote hain.

> **Safety:** Cell aur coil wali koi bhi activity sirf **kuch second** ke liye karo. Zyada der tak karne par cell aur taar garm ho jaate hain aur **jala sakte hain**. **Ghar ki bijli (mains)** se aisa koi experiment kabhi mat karo: wo jaan ke liye khatarnak hai. Experiment sirf teacher ki nigrani mein karo.

### Tareeka 4: Earth ke field se magnetisation

Earth khud ek bada magnet hai (Topic 2). Agar steel ya lohe ki chhad ko **North-South disha mein** rakhkar usse hathode se thoka jaye, to wo **kamzor magnet** ban jaati hai. Hathode ke jhatke tiny magnets ko thoda hilate hain, aur Earth ka field unhe ek hi disha mein line up karne mein thodi madad kar deta hai. Kaha jaata hai ki lohe ki chhadein aur pipes, jo bahut samay se North-South disha mein padi rehti hain, kamzor magnet ban jaati hain. Ye tareeka kamzor hai, isliye hum ise sirf naam se jaante hain.

### Magnetised sui ka test

Sui sach mein magnet ban gayi hai ya nahi, ye kaise pata chalega? Do tareeke:

1. **Pole test (repulsion):** Ek jaane-pehchane magnet (jiska N pole pata ho) ka N pole sui ke ek sire ke paas laao. Agar wo sira **dhakka dekar door hota hai (repel)**, to wo sira bhi N pole hai aur sui magnet hai. **Repulsion hi pakka test hai**, kyunki attraction to bina magnet wali lohe ki cheez ke saath bhi hota hai (Topic 1).
2. **Hanging test:** Sui ko dhaage se beech mein latka do (ya paani mein tairti cork par rakh do). Agar wo har baar **North-South** disha mein rukti hai, to wo magnet hai, aur jo sira North ki taraf rukta hai wo uska N pole hai. (Compass ki tarah, Topic 2.)

## Part C: Temporary aur permanent magnets

Sab magnetic materials ek jaise nahi hote. Kuch aasani se magnet bante hain, par jaldi khote bhi hain; kuch magnet banne mein mushkil karte hain, par phir lambe samay tak banaye rakhte hain.

- **Soft iron** (naram lohe): aasani se magnet banta hai, aur magnetising ka asar hatate hi (jaise magnet ko door karte hi) lagbhag turant apna magnetism kho deta hai. Isliye isse **temporary magnet** kehte hain. Electromagnet ka core isi se banta hai (Topic 5).
- **Steel aur alloys (alnico, ticonal):** inhe magnetise karna thoda mushkil hai, par ek baar ho jaye to magnetism **bahut samay tak** rehta hai. Isliye inse **permanent magnets** banate hain: bar magnets, compass ki sui, speaker ke magnets.

| | Soft iron | Steel / alnico / ticonal |
|---|---|---|
| Magnetise karna | Bahut aasan | Thoda mushkil |
| Magnetism rakhna | Lagbhag turant kho deta hai | Bahut samay tak rakhta hai |
| Kis tarah ka magnet | Temporary | Permanent |
| Kahan use | Electromagnet ka core, electric bell, keeper | Bar magnet, compass sui, speaker magnet |

**Yaad rakhne ka tareeka:** *Jo aasani se aata hai, wo aasani se jaata hai. Jo mushkil se aata hai, wo mushkil se jaata hai.*

## Part D: Demagnetisation: magnetism kaise khoti hai

Ab Rajveer ka doosra sawal: magnet ki magnetism kahan chali gayi? Jawab: **kahin nahi gayi.** Bas tiny magnets ki line-up bigad gayi: wo phir se alag-alag disha mein ghoom gaye. Ye **demagnetisation** hai. Iske kai tareeke hain.

**1. Garm karne se.** Magnet ko tez garm karo to garmi ke kaaran tiny magnets itna hilne lagte hain ki unki line-up toot jaati hai. Ek khaas temperature par magnet apna magnetism **poori tarah** kho deta hai. Is temperature ko **Curie temperature** kehte hain (sirf naam, iska formula aage ki class mein). **Lohe ke liye ye lagbhag 770 °C hai** (approximate value). Is se kam garm karne par bhi magnet kamzor ho jaata hai.

**2. Baar-baar hathode se thokne ya girane se.** Har jhatke se kuch tiny magnets apni disha se hil jaate hain. Baar-baar girane se line-up kharab hoti jaati hai, aur magnet kamzor ho jaata hai. Isliye magnet ko gira mat karo.

**3. Alternating current (AC) se (thoda extra, Class 10 overlap).** Magnet ko ek coil ke andar rakhkar usmein AC bhejte hain, aur coil ko dheere-dheere door karte hain ya current ghatate hain. AC ki disha baar-baar badalti hai, to tiny magnets ki line-up bhi bikhar jaati hai. Ye tareeka **udyogon (industries) mein** magnets ko demagnetise karne ke liye kaam aata hai. (AC ka poora topic Class 10 mein.)

**4. Galat tareeke se rakhne se (self-demagnetisation).** Ye sabse dhire hone wali wajah hai. Akele rakhe bar magnet ke N aur S pole ek-doosre ke field ke **ulte** (opposing) hote hain, aur ye ulta field khud magnet ke tiny magnets ko dheere-dheere bigadta rehta hai. Isliye magnets ko **keepers** ke saath rakhte hain (neeche).

**5. Strong ulte field ke paas.** Agar magnet ko ek strong, ulti disha wale magnetic field ke paas rakha jaye, to wo kamzor ho sakta hai. Ye baat sirf naam se jaanni hai. (Topic 2 wali magnetic shielding magnet ko kamzor nahi karti: wo sirf andar rakhi cheez ko bahar ke field se bachati hai.)

### Keeper kyun lagate hain?

Do bar magnets ko ek-doosre ke saath, **ulte poles saath-saath** karke, beech mein lakdi ki patti rakhkar, aur dono siron par **soft iron ke keepers** lagakar rakhte hain.

```
     keeper                               keeper
   (soft iron)                          (soft iron)
     ┃  [N═══════════════════S]  ┃
     ┃                           ┃
     ┃  [S═══════════════════N]  ┃
```

> **Figure 3.6** – Do bar magnets, ulte poles saath-saath, aur dono siron par soft iron ke keepers. Field lines ek magnet se nikal kar keeper ke andar se guzarti hain aur doosre magnet mein jaakar loop poora karti hain: loop lagbhag andar hi band ho jaata hai.

Topic 2 mein dekha tha ki field lines band loops hoti hain, aur Topic 1 mein ki poles ek-doosre ko kheenchte hain. Keeper soft iron ka hota hai, to aasani se magnetise ho kar **field lines ko apne andar se guzaar deta hai**. Is tarah magnet ka field **band loop** mein andar hi andar poora ho jaata hai. Bahar ka ulta field kam ho jaata hai, aur magnet apni taakat **zyada samay tak** banaye rakhta hai. Isliye **keeper bekaar nahi, kaam ki cheez hai.**

Ab Rajveer ke dono sawal solve:
- **Sui:** magnet ke baar-baar ek disha mein ragadne se tiny magnets line up ho gaye, isliye sui magnet ban gayi.
- **Gira aur garm hua magnet:** girne ke jhatkon aur heater ki garmi ne tiny magnets ki line-up bigaad di, isliye magnet kamzor ho gaya.

Ms. Pradhan boli, "Dekha Rajveer? Magnetism na kahin se aayi, na kahin gayi. Bas tiny magnets ek disha mein aaye ya bikhar gaye."

## Part E: Magnets ki dekhbhaal, aur roz ke magnets

### Magnets ki dekhbhaal (care of magnets)

- Magnet ko **gira mat**, aur hathode se mat thoko.
- Magnet ko **garm cheezon** (heater, aag) se door rakho.
- Bar magnets ko **keepers** ke saath, ulte poles saath-saath karke rakho.
- Do magnets ko jod kar rakhna ho to ulte poles saath-saath rakho.
- Strong ulte magnetic field ke paas mat rakho.

Ek aur baat: **magnet cheezein uthane se ghistha nahi.** Magnet baar-baar pins ya keel uthane se kamzor nahi hota. Ye sirf upar ki wajahon (garmi, jhatke, galat rakhna) se kamzor hota hai.

### Roz ki cheezon mein magnets

Aaj ki kai cheezein magnetism par chalti hain, aur strong magnet ke paas aane se kharab ho sakti hain:

- **Credit/debit card ki magnetic patti:** strong magnet se ragad kar uska data mit sakta hai.
- **Hard disk:** andar magnetic material par data likha hota hai. Strong magnet ke paas rakhne par data kharab ho sakta hai.
- **Purana audio/video tape:** usmein bhi magnetic coating hoti hai, jo magnet ke paas kharab ho sakti hai.

Isliye in cheezon ko bade magnets (jaise speaker ke) se door rakho.

### Temporary aur permanent magnets ke uses

| Magnet | Material | Kahan use hota hai |
|---|---|---|
| Temporary | Soft iron | Electromagnet ka core (Topic 5), electric bell ka armature, keeper |
| Permanent | Steel | Compass ki sui, chhote bar magnets |
| Permanent | Alnico, ticonal | Speaker aur microphone ke magnets, mazboot magnets |

## Part F: Solved examples

**Example 1 (single touch: 6 cases).** Ek steel ki sui ke sire hain A (aankh wala, left) aur B (nok wala, right). Rule: **jis sire par ragadna khatam hua, us par ragadne wale pole se ulta pole banta hai.** Neeche 6 cases ke poles likho.

| Case | Ragadne wala pole | Disha | Akhri sira | Akhri sire ka pole | Shuru ka sira ka pole |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | N | A se B | B | **S** | A: **N** |
| 2 | S | A se B | B | **N** | A: **S** |
| 3 | N | B se A | A | **S** | B: **N** |
| 4 | S | B se A | A | **N** | B: **S** |
| 5 | N | aankh se nok | nok (B) | **S** | aankh (A): **N** |
| 6 | S | nok se aankh | aankh (A) | **N** | nok (B): **S** |

Case 1 ki tasveer:

```
        S
        ║   magnet (N pole neeche)
        N  →  →  →  →  →  →  ↗
   ┌───────────────────────────┐
   │ A                       B │   steel ki sui
   └───────────────────────────┘
      N                       S
```

> **Figure 3.7** – Case 1: N pole se A se B ki taraf ragdo. Akhri sira B par S pole, aur A par N pole.

**Jaanch:** Har case mein dono siron par **ulte** poles hain (kabhi N-N ya S-S nahi), aur akhri sire par hamesha ragadne wale pole se ulta ✅. Case 1 aur case 3 mein ragadne wala N hi hai par disha ulti hai, to poles bhi ulte ✅. Case 5 mein aankh-nok A-B jaisa hi hai, to case 1 jaisa hi jawab ✅.

**Example 2 (double touch).** Ek steel ki chhad ke beech mein ek bar magnet ka N pole aur doosre ka S pole rakhe hain. N pole dayein sire B tak, S pole bayein sire A tak ragda gaya. Dono siron ke poles kya honge?

- B par N pole ka ragadna khatam hua, to B par **S pole**.
- A par S pole ka ragadna khatam hua, to A par **N pole**.

**Jaanch:** Dono siron par ulte poles ✅ (B: S, A: N). Aur Figure 3.4 se bhi milta hai ✅.

**Example 3 (12 materials: temporary, permanent ya non-magnetic).** Har ek ko batao:

| Material | Jawab | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| Soft iron ki keel | Temporary | Aasani se magnet banti aur khoti hai |
| Steel ki sui | Permanent | Mushkil se banti, der tak rehti |
| Alnico ki chhad | Permanent | Mazboot permanent magnet |
| Copper ka taar | Non-magnetic | Magnetic material hi nahi |
| Soft iron ka keeper | Temporary | Aasan magnetising, aasan khona |
| Compass ki sui | Permanent | Steel se bani |
| Ticonal ka speaker magnet | Permanent | Alloy |
| Electromagnet ka core | Temporary | Soft iron |
| Plastic ka scale | Non-magnetic | Plastic |
| Brass ki chaabi | Non-magnetic | Brass magnetic nahi |
| Steel ka screwdriver | Permanent (steel) | Steel hai |
| Aluminium ka foil | Non-magnetic | Aluminium magnetic nahi |

**Jaanch:** 12 cheezein: 3 temporary (keel, keeper, core), 5 permanent (sui, alnico, compass sui, ticonal, screwdriver), 4 non-magnetic (copper, plastic, brass, aluminium). 3 + 5 + 4 = 12 ✅

**Example 4 (pins ka hisaab).** Rajveer ka magnet pehle 15 pins uthata tha. 10 baar girane ke baad 10 pins uthaye, phir heater ke paas garm hone ke baad 4 pins. Kitni taakat (strength) gayi? (Pins ki sankhya ko magnet ki taakat ka paimana maan lo.)

| Haalat | Pins | Taakat ghati (15 se) |
|---|---|---|
| Shuru mein | 15 | 0% |
| 10 baar girane ke baad | 10 | (15 − 10) ÷ 15 × 100 = 5 ÷ 15 × 100 = **33.3%** |
| Heater ke baad | 4 | (15 − 4) ÷ 15 × 100 = 11 ÷ 15 × 100 = **73.3%** |

**Jaanch:** 33.3% of 15 = 0.333 × 15 = 5 pins gaye, to 10 bache ✅. 73.3% of 15 = 0.733 × 15 = 11 pins gaye, to 4 bache ✅. Girne se lagbhag 33% aur garmi se lagbhag 73% taakat gayi. Dono milakar magnet ko bahut kamzor kar dete hain.

**Example 5 (kaam ke hisaab se material).** Neeche ke kaam ke liye soft iron ya steel/alnico batao:

- Electromagnet ka core: **soft iron** (current band hote hi magnetism chali jaye, taaki electromagnet aasani se on-off ho).
- Compass ki sui: **steel** (magnetism lambe samay tak rahe, warna compass kharab).
- Electric bell ka armature (jo hathode ko hilata hai): **soft iron** (baar-baar turant magnet banna aur khona chahiye).
- Speaker ka magnet: **alnico/ticonal** (mazboot aur permanent).
- Keeper: **soft iron**.

**Jaanch:** "Baar-baar on-off" wale kaam (core, armature) mein temporary chahiye ✅. "Hamesha rakhna" wale kaam (compass, speaker) mein permanent chahiye ✅.

**Example 6 (keeper soft iron ka kyun, steel ka kyun nahi?).** Keeper ka kaam hai magnet ki field lines ko apne andar se guzaarna. Soft iron aasani se magnetise hota hai, to wo field lines ko achhi tarah guzaar deta hai. Steel mushkil se magnetise hota hai, aur ek baar ho jaye to **khud ek permanent magnet** ban jaata hai. Tab wo sirf field ka rasta nahi rehta, apna alag magnetism bhi rakhta hai, jo gadbad kar sakta hai. Soft iron keeper sirf tab magnet banta hai jab asli magnet paas ho, aur door karte hi apna magnetism kho deta hai: wo bas rasta banata hai.

**Jaanch:** Keeper ka kaam sirf field ka rasta banana hai, khud permanent magnet banna nahi: soft iron sahi ✅.

**Example 7 (reasoning).**
(a) *Kya magnet ek brass ke tukde ko kheench sakta hai?* **Nahi**, kyunki brass non-magnetic hai.
(b) *Kya copper ke taar ko magnet banaya ja sakta hai?* **Nahi.** Copper magnetic material nahi hai, to ye koi bhi tareeke se (ragadna, coil mein rakhna) magnet nahi banta.

**Jaanch:** Table mein copper aur brass dono non-magnetic material mein hain ✅.

**Example 8 (magnet aur lohe ki chhad mein antar).** Do dikhne mein ek jaisi chhadein hain: ek magnet, ek sadharan lohe ki. Kaise pehchanoge?

Ek jaane-pehchane magnet ka N pole **ek chhad ke ek sire** ke paas laao. Agar wo sira **dhakka dekar hat jaaye (repel)**, to chhad magnet hai. Agar wo sirf **kheenchi jaaye**, to ye pakka nahi: sadharan lohe ki chhad bhi kheenchi jaati hai. Isliye **repulsion hi sabse pakka test hai.** Agar kisi bhi sire par repulsion kabhi nahi mile, to chhad sadharan lohe ki hai.

**Jaanch:** Magnet ka N sira jab N se aaya to repel, S se aaya to attract. Sadharan lohe hamesha sirf attract ✅.

**Example 9 (tukde karne par poles).** 6 cm ki ek magnetised sui ko 3 barabar tukdon (2 cm, 2 cm, 2 cm) mein kaata gaya. (a) Kitne magnets bane? (b) Kul kitne poles hain?

- (a) Har tukda khud ek poora magnet hai, to **3 magnets**.
- (b) Har magnet ke 2 poles (N aur S), to kul 3 × 2 = **6 poles** (3 N aur 3 S).

**Jaanch:** N poles aur S poles ki sankhya barabar hai: 3 aur 3 ✅. Tukdon ki lambai ka jod 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 cm ✅. Pole akele alag nahi hue: har tukde mein N aur S dono hain.

**Example 10 (galat tareeka pehchano).** Rajveer ne ek sui ko magnet ke N pole se pehle A se B, phir B se A (aage-peechhe), 40 baar ragda. Sui magnet bani ya nahi?

Is tarah ragadne se ek baar tiny magnets ek taraf line up hote hain, aur doosri baar ulti taraf. Dono asar ek-doosre ko kaat dete hain, to sui **magnet nahi banti** (ya bahut kamzor banti hai). Sahi tareeka: **hamesha ek hi disha mein ragdo**, aur wapas laate waqt magnet ko sui se door utha lo.

**Jaanch:** 20 baar A se B aur 20 baar B se A, to ek disha ka asar +20 aur doosri ka −20 maano: kul 20 − 20 = 0 ✅ (sirf ek moti si ginti hai).

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Aage-peechhe dono disha mein ragadne se bhi magnetise hota hai."**
Nahi. Isse ulta hota hai: ek disha ka asar doosri disha se kat jaata hai (Example 10). Ragadna **hamesha ek hi disha mein** hona chahiye.

**Galti 2: "Magnet ki magnetism sui mein beh kar chali jaati hai."**
Nahi. Kuch bhi "beh kar" nahi aata. Sui ke tiny magnets pehle se andar the, bas wo har disha mein the. Ragadne se wo ek disha mein line up ho gaye. Isliye magnet apni taakat khoe bina bhi doosre ko magnet bana sakta hai.

**Galti 3: "Steel soft iron ki tarah aasani se magnetism kho deta hai."**
Nahi. Steel mushkil se magnetise hota hai aur mushkil se hi khota hai. Soft iron aasani se khota hai. Isliye permanent magnets steel ya alnico se bante hain.

**Galti 4: "Garm karne ya hathode se thokne se magnet mazboot hota hai."**
Ulta hai. Garmi aur jhatke tiny magnets ki line-up bigaadte hain, to magnet kamzor hota hai (Example 4: 33% aur 73% taakat gayi).

**Galti 5: "Keeper bekaar hai."**
Nahi. Keeper field lines ko andar band loop banane deta hai aur magnet ko zyada samay tak mazboot rakhta hai (Figure 3.6).

**Galti 6: "Magnet baar-baar cheezein uthane se ghis kar kamzor ho jaata hai."**
Nahi. Cheezein uthane se magnet kamzor nahi hota. Wo sirf garmi, jhatkon, galat tareeke se rakhne, ya strong ulte field se kamzor hota hai.

## Quick Recap

- Magnetic material (lohe, steel, nickel, cobalt) ko **tiny magnets** (magnetic domains) ka bana soch sakte hain. Unmagnetised mein ye **har disha mein** hote hain aur asar cancel ho jaata hai; magnetised mein **ek hi disha mein line up** hote hain.
- **Magnetisation** = magnetic material ko magnet banana. Tareeke: **single touch** (ek pole se, ek hi disha mein, 30-40 baar), **double touch** (do ulte poles beech se dono siron tak), **electrical** (coil mein DC current: sabse tez aur sabse achha), **Earth ke field se** (North-South dishaa mein hathode se thokna, kamzor).
- **Pole rule:** jis sire par ragadna khatam hota hai, us par ragadne wale pole se **ulta** pole banta hai.
- Sui ka test: **repulsion** (pakka test) aur **hanging test** (North-South mein rukna).
- **Temporary magnet:** soft iron, aasani se banta aur khota hai (electromagnet ka core, bell, keeper). **Permanent magnet:** steel, alnico, ticonal, mushkil se banta, der tak rehta hai (bar magnet, compass sui, speaker).
- **Demagnetisation:** garm karne se (Curie temperature, lohe ke liye lagbhag 770 °C), hathode se thokne ya girane se, AC se (udyogon mein), galat rakhne se (self-demagnetisation), strong ulte field se.
- **Keepers** soft iron ke hote hain: field lines ko andar band loop banate hain, magnet mazboot rehta hai.
- Bar magnet ko todne par har tukda poora magnet banta hai: poles alag nahi hote, kyunki har tukde mein tiny magnets line up hi hote hain.
- Credit card, hard disk aur purane tape strong magnet ke paas kharab ho sakte hain.
- Kahani: sui ke tiny magnets ragadne se line up hue, aur Rajveer ka magnet girne aur garmi se bikhar gaya: 15 pins se 10 (33%) aur phir 4 (73%).
