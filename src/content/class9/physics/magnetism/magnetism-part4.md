# Topic 4: Electric Current ka Magnetic Effect

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki magnetism ek magnet se aati hai, aur use banaya, kamzor kiya aur khatam bhi kiya ja sakta hai. Us topic ke end mein ek bada sawal chhoda tha: **kya bina kisi magnet ke bhi magnetism paida ho sakti hai?** Is topic mein hum dekhenge ki haan: sirf ek **bahta hua current** bhi apne aas-paas magnetic field bana deta hai. Yaani ab magnetism ka source ek magnet nahi, ek taar hai jisme bijli beh rahi hai. Is topic mein hum ye bhi seekhenge ki current ki direction se field ki direction kaise pata karte hain, aur taar ko lapet kar ek aisa coil (solenoid) kaise banta hai jo bilkul bar magnet jaisa kaam karta hai.

## Part A: Oersted ka experiment

### Set-up

Is experiment mein hum kuch chhoti cheezein lenge: ek ya do cell, ek chhoti taar, ek switch (ya taar ka sira cell se chhoona) aur ek compass.

1. Compass ko table par rakho. Sui north-south direction mein ruk jayegi (magnetism-part2.md mein dekha tha ki Earth ka magnetic field compass ko is direction mein rakhta hai).
2. Taar ko compass ki sui ke **upar**, uske **parallel** (north-south direction mein) rakho.
3. Taar ko cell se jodo (switch on).

```
        taar (north-south direction mein, sui ke upar)
   N  ──────────────────────────────  S
       ←  current ki direction  ─┐
        ┌──────┐                 │
        │ sui  │ (compass)      cell, switch
        └──────┘                 │
   (taar sui ke upar, parallel)  ┘
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Oersted ka set-up: taar compass ki sui ke upar aur parallel rakhi hai, aur cell se juda hai.

### Kya dikhta hai?

| Kya kiya | Kya hua |
|---|---|
| Switch on (current beh raha hai) | Sui apni north-south direction se hatkar **deflect** hui |
| Current ki direction ulti ki (cell ke siron ki taar badal di) | Sui **doosri taraf** deflect hui |
| Switch off (current band) | Sui **wapas** north-south mein aa gayi |

### Conclusion

> **Current-wale taar ke aas-paas magnetic field hota hai.** Yaani electric current ka ek **magnetic effect** hota hai. Jab tak current beh raha hai, field hai. Current band, to field bhi band.

**Safety:** Is experiment mein **sirf ek ya do cell**, ek chhoti taar aur ek compass use karo. Taar ko cell se sirf **kuch second** ke liye jodo, kyunki cell ko bina bulb ke seedhe patli ya moti taar se jodne par (short circuit) bahut current beh sakta hai, jisse taar aur cell dono garm ho jaate hain aur jal bhi sakte hain. **Kabhi bhi ghar ki bijli (mains) se ye experiment mat karo.** Ye bahut khatarnak hai.

### Ek aur dhyan dene wali baat

Sui ko taar ke chhoone ki zaroorat nahi hai. Taar sui ke thoda upar bhi ho (bina chhuye), tab bhi sui ghoomti hai. Field **hawa mein door tak** failta hai, isiliye sui deflect hoti hai: wo taar ka touch nahi, taar ka **field** mehsoos karti hai.

## Part B: Seedhe taar ke aas-paas ka field

### Field gol (circular) hota hai

Seedhe lambe taar ke aas-paas field kaisa dikhta hai? Isko dekhne ke liye ek card lo, usme chhed karke taar ko seedha (card ke perpendicular) nikalo, card par iron filings chhidko aur thoda tap karo, jab current beh raha ho. Filings **gol circles** banati hain, jinka centre taar hai.

> Taar ke aas-paas field lines **concentric circles** hain, taar ke perpendicular plane mein. Field lines taar ke **saath-saath seedhi** line nahi hain, **taar ke around ghoomti** hain.

### Taar page ke andar ya bahar

Maan lo taar page ko seedha cheerti hai (page ke perpendicular):
- **⊙** matlab current page se **bahar** (aapki taraf) aa raha hai.
- **⊗** matlab current page ke **andar** ja raha hai (aapse door).

Dono cases ko alag-alag sketch mein dekhte hain (arrows field ki direction dikhate hain):

```
          ←←←←
        ↙      ↖
       ↓   ⊙    ↑        Anticlockwise: upar se baayein, neeche se daayein
        ↘      ↗
          →→→→
```

> **Figure 4.2** – ⊙ (current page se bahar): field circle **anticlockwise** hai (upar se baayein, baayein se neeche, neeche se daayein, daayein se upar).

```
          →→→→
        ↗      ↘
       ↑   ⊗    ↓        Clockwise: upar se daayein, daayein se neeche
        ↖      ↙
          ←←←←
```

> **Figure 4.3** – ⊗ (current page ke andar): field circle **clockwise** hai.

### Doori ke saath field

- Taar ke **paas** field **zyada** strong hota hai (circles ek-doosre ke paas, filings zyada saaf jamti hain).
- Taar se **door** jaane par field **kam** hota jaata hai (circles ek-doosre se door).
- **Current zyada**, to field zyada.

Yaani field taar ke paas strong, aur "doori ke saath kam" hota jaata hai. (Iska exact formula aage ki class mein hai: abhi sirf ye baat samjho.)

## Part C: Field ki direction: right-hand thumb rule

Field ki direction kaise pata karein? Iske liye ek bahut aasaan niyam hai.

> **Right-hand thumb rule (Maxwell ka right-hand grip rule):** Seedhe taar ko apne **daayein haath** mein is tarah pakdo ki **angootha current ki direction** mein ho. Tab **mudi hui ungliyan** field ki direction dikhati hain.

**Dhyan:** current ki direction hamesha **conventional direction** (positive se negative) maani jaati hai, jaisa electricity-part2.md mein dekha tha.

### Taar upar ya neeche

Maan lo taar seedhi khadi hai (vertical) aur aap use **upar se** dekh rahe ho:

```
   Current UPAR ki taraf                Current NEECHE ki taraf
   (aapki taraf aata hua)               (aapse door jata hua)

        ↑  (angootha upar)                   ↓  (angootha neeche)
        │                                    │
   upar se dekhne par:                  upar se dekhne par:
   field ANTICLOCKWISE (⟲)              field CLOCKWISE (⟳)
```

> **Figure 4.4** – Current upar: upar se dekhne par field anticlockwise. Current neeche: upar se dekhne par field clockwise.

### Rule ko check karna

Current page se bahar (⊙), yaani current aapki taraf aa raha hai. Daayein haath ka angootha aapki taraf. Ab ungliyon ko mudo: wo **anticlockwise** ghoomti dikhti hain. Yahi Figure 4.2 hai. Current andar (⊗) ho, angootha aapse door, ungliyan **clockwise**: Figure 4.3.

### Compass se direction ki jaanch

Oersted ke experiment mein compass ki sui field ki direction batati hai. **Sui ka North pole** field ki direction mein hat-ta hai (magnetism-part2.md: field ki direction wahi hai jis taraf compass ka N pole point karta hai).

Taar ke **upar** rakhi compass aur taar ke **neeche** rakhi compass ke field ki direction **ulti** hoti hai (circle ka ek hissa upar jaata hai, to doosra neeche ulta jaata hai). Isliye taar ke upar aur neeche rakhi compass alag-alag taraf deflect hoti hain.

## Part D: Gol chhalla (circular loop) ka field

Agar seedhe taar ko mod kar gol chhalla (loop) bana dein, to uska field kaisa hoga?

- Taar ke paas har jagah field wahi circles hain, par ab sab circles **loop ke andar (centre ke paas)** same direction mein aate hain.
- Isliye loop ke **centre** ke paas field **sabse strong** hota hai aur loop ki **axis** ki direction mein hota hai.
- Centre se door jaane par field kam hota jaata hai.

```
         taar (side se dekha)
        ⊗  ⊗  ⊗         Upar wale hisse mein current andar (⊗)
      (                 )
     (        ←←←       )    Loop ke andar field lines
      (      ←←←←       )    ek hi taraf (yahan baayein) gujarti hain
        ⊙  ⊙  ⊙         Neeche wale hisse mein current bahar (⊙)
```

> **Figure 4.5** – Gol loop ke andar field lines ek hi direction mein gujarti hain, isliye centre par field strong hai.

### Loop ke do chehre: N aur S

Gol loop ka ek **chehra (face) N pole** jaisa aur doosra **chehra S pole** jaisa hota hai (jaise ek bahut patla bar magnet).

> **Clock rule:** Loop ke ek chehre ko saamne se dekho. Agar current us chehre ki taraf se dekhne par **anticlockwise** hai, to wo chehra **North pole** hai. Agar current **clockwise** hai, to wo chehra **South pole** hai.

```
   Is chehre se dekha:                  Is chehre se dekha:

        ┌──←──┐                              ┌──→──┐
        ↓     ↑                              ↑     ↓
        └──→──┘                              └──←──┘
   current ANTICLOCKWISE                  current CLOCKWISE
   to ye chehra  N                        to ye chehra  S
```

> **Figure 4.6** – Clock rule: anticlockwise current = North chehra, clockwise current = South chehra.

**Dhyan:** Ek hi loop ke doosre chehre se dekhne par wahi current ulta (opposite) ghoomta dikhta hai. Isliye ek chehra N aur doosra S hota hai.

### Bahut saare turns: coil

Agar taar ke **n turns** hain (ek ke upar ek, same current), to **sab turns ke field add hote hain**, isliye field ek turn ke muqable lagbhag **n guna** zyada hota hai. 50 turns ka coil ek loop se lagbhag 50 guna strong field deta hai.

## Part E: Solenoid (thoda extra, Class 10 overlap)

> **Solenoid** insulated (parat chadhi) taar ka lamba cylinder jaisa coil hai, jisme bahut saare gol turns **paas-paas** lapete gaye hote hain.

```
    A                                       B
    (((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((
     B sire se dekho: current ANTICLOCKWISE ghoomta hai
     A sire se dekho: wahi current CLOCKWISE dikhta hai
    (((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((
```

> **Figure 4.7** – Solenoid: taar ke bahut saare turns paas-paas. B sire se dekhne par current anticlockwise hai, isliye (Part E ke rule se) B sira N aur A sira S banta hai, jaise Figure 4.8 mein daayan sira N hai.

### Solenoid ka field

Solenoid ka field **bar magnet ke field jaisa** dikhta hai:

```
          ╭────────────────←────────────────╮
          │       ╭────────←────────╮       │
          ↓       ↓                 ↑       ↑
    ──→───────    S(((((((((((((((((N   ──→──
                   ──→ ──→ ──→ ──→
          ↑       ↑  (andar: S se N)↓       ↓
          │       ╰────────←────────╯       │
          ╰────────────────←────────────────╯
       (bahar: N end se nikalkar S end tak)
```

> **Figure 4.8** – Solenoid ka field: andar seedhi, strong aur parallel lines (S end se N end ki taraf); bahar bar magnet jaisi lines jo N end se nikalkar S end tak jaati hain. Kul milakar closed loops banti hain.


| Jagah | Field kaisa hai |
|---|---|
| **Solenoid ke bahar** | Bilkul bar magnet jaisa: field lines **N end se nikalti hain** aur **S end mein ghusti hain** |
| **Solenoid ke andar** | Field lines **seedhi, ek-doosre ke parallel** (axis ke parallel), aur field **strong aur lagbhag uniform** (har jagah ek jaisa) |
| **Closed loops** | Bahar ki lines andar se judkar poora **band loop** banati hain (magnetism-part2.md: field lines closed loops hoti hain) |


### Solenoid ke poles: right-hand rule for the coil

> **Right-hand rule for solenoid (coil ke liye):** Solenoid ko apne **daayein haath** mein pakdo, **ungliyan current ki direction mein mudi hui** ho. Tab **angootha North pole** ki taraf point karega (wo sira jahan se field lines bahar nikalti hain).

**Clock rule (har sire par):** Solenoid ke ek sire ko saamne se dekho:
- current **anticlockwise** dikhe, to wo sira **North pole**.
- current **clockwise** dikhe, to wo sira **South pole**.

Dono rule hamesha **ek hi jawab** dete hain. Hum Part G ke examples mein ye check karenge.

## Part F: Solenoid ki strength kin cheezon par depend karti hai (thoda extra, Class 10 overlap)

| Factor | Kya hota hai |
|---|---|
| **1. Current** | Current zyada, to field zyada strong |
| **2. Turns ki sankhya (lambai ke hisaab se)** | Same lambai mein turns zyada (zyada paas-paas), to field zyada strong |
| **3. Soft iron core** | Andar naram lohe ki chhad rakhne par field **bahut zyada** badh jaata hai (Topic 5: electromagnet) |

Is topic mein hum sirf ye "zyada ya kam" wali baat seekhte hain. Exact formulas aage ki class mein aate hain.

### Current ki direction ulti karne par

Current ulta karne par field ki **strength** wahi rehti hai, par field aur poles ki **direction ulti** ho jaati hai (N pole wala sira ab S ban jaata hai, aur S wala sira N).

### Bar magnet aur solenoid ki tulna

| Baat | Bar magnet | Solenoid |
|---|---|---|
| Bahar ka field | N se S ki taraf | Bilkul waisa hi (N se S ki taraf) |
| Poles | Hamesha ek N, ek S | Ek N, ek S (current se tay hote hain) |
| Strength badal sakte hain? | Nahi, tay hoti hai | **Haan:** current ya turns badhakar/ghatakar |
| Switch off kar sakte hain? | Nahi, magnet hamesha rehta hai | **Haan:** current band, magnetism band |
| Poles ulte kar sakte hain? | Nahi (sirf magnet ko ghumao) | **Haan:** current ulta karo |

## Part G: Solved examples

**Example 1 (taar ke field ki direction: 6 cases):** Right-hand thumb rule se field ki direction batao.

| Case | Taar mein current | Kahan se dekhein | Field ki direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Seedhe **upar** (khada taar) | Upar se | **Anticlockwise** |
| 2 | Seedhe **neeche** (khada taar) | Upar se | **Clockwise** |
| 3 | Page se **bahar** (⊙) | Aap (page ke saamne) | **Anticlockwise** |
| 4 | Page ke **andar** (⊗) | Aap | **Clockwise** |
| 5 | Taar mein current **baayein se daayein**, taar page ke plane mein | Taar ke **upar** wali jagah | Field page se **bahar** (⊙), aur taar ke neeche page ke **andar** (⊗) |
| 6 | Taar mein current **daayein se baayein** | Taar ke upar wali jagah | Field page ke **andar** (⊗), aur taar ke neeche page se **bahar** (⊙) |

Rule: angootha current ki taraf, ungliyan field ki taraf. Case 5: angootha daayein ki taraf, ungliyan taar ke upar se aapki taraf (bahar) aur neeche se aapse door (andar) mudti hain. Case 6 bilkul ulta.

**Jaanch:** Case 1 aur 2 mein current ulta karne par direction anticlockwise se clockwise ho gayi ✅. Case 3 aur 4 mein bhi yahi hua ✅. Case 5 aur 6 mein bhi ulta ✅.

**Example 2 (compass ki deflection):** Taar compass ki sui ke **upar** north ki taraf current le jaa raha hai (south se north ki taraf). Sui ka N pole kis taraf deflect hoga? Ab current ulta karo.

- Taar upar, current north ki taraf. Right-hand rule: angootha north ki taraf, ungliyan taar ke upar se **east** ki taraf aur taar ke **neeche** se **west** ki taraf mudti hain.
- Sui taar ke **neeche** hai, to wahan field **west** ki taraf hai. Sui ka N pole **west** ki taraf deflect hoga.
- Current ulta (south ki taraf): field neeche **east** ki taraf. Sui ka N pole ab **east** ki taraf deflect hoga.

**Jaanch:** Current ulta karne par deflection ulti ho gayi (west se east) ✅. Agar compass taar ke **upar** hoti (taar neeche), to wahan field ulta hota, deflection bhi ulti hoti ✅.

**Example 3 (solenoid ke poles: 6 cases):** Solenoid ki axis ke dono sire hain. Ek sire (jahan se dekha gaya) par current ki direction di gayi hai. Right-hand rule aur clock rule dono se poles batao.

| Case | Kahan se dekha | Wahan current | Us sire ka pole (clock rule) | Doosre sire ka pole | Right-hand grip rule: angootha kis taraf? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Baayein sire se (axis horizontal) | Anticlockwise | **N** | **S** | Baayein sire ki taraf |
| 2 | Baayein sire se | Clockwise | **S** | **N** | Daayein sire ki taraf |
| 3 | Daayein sire se | Anticlockwise | **N** | **S** | Daayein sire ki taraf |
| 4 | Daayein sire se | Clockwise | **S** | **N** | Baayein sire ki taraf |
| 5 | Upar wale sire se (axis khadi) | Anticlockwise | **N** (upar) | **S** (neeche) | Upar ki taraf |
| 6 | Upar wale sire se (axis khadi) | Clockwise | **S** (upar) | **N** (neeche) | Neeche ki taraf |

Doosre sire par wahi current ulti direction mein dikhta hai (anticlockwise ka ulta clockwise), isliye doosra pole ulta aata hai.

**Jaanch:** Har case mein clock rule aur right-hand grip rule dono se **ek hi N pole** mila ✅. Aur har solenoid mein ek N aur ek S hi aaya ✅.

**Example 4 (current badhane ka asar):** Ek solenoid mein 0.5 A current se field ek certain strength ka hai. Current ko double (1.0 A) kar diya. Field kaisa hoga?

- Current 0.5 A se 1.0 A: 1.0 A ÷ 0.5 A = **2 guna**.
- Baaki sab same (turns, lambai), to field lagbhag **2 guna** strong hoga (current badhne par field badhta hai: ye proportional wali simple soch hai).

**Jaanch:** 0.5 A × 2 = 1.0 A ✅. Current aur field dono 2 guna ✅. (Yaad rakho: ye ek aam samajh hai. Iron core jaise factors ke saath strict proportion nahi rehta.)

**Example 5 (turns double karne ka asar):** Ek 10 cm lambe solenoid par 100 turns hain. Use usi 10 cm lambai mein 200 turns kar diya (turns zyada paas-paas). Field ka kya hoga?

- Pehle: turns per cm = 100 turns ÷ 10 cm = **10 turns/cm**.
- Baad mein: 200 turns ÷ 10 cm = **20 turns/cm**.
- Turns per cm 2 guna, to (current same) field lagbhag **2 guna**.

**Jaanch:** 20 turns/cm ÷ 10 turns/cm = 2 ✅. Lambai wahi rakhi, tabhi ye soch sahi hai. Agar lambai bhi double kar dein aur turns double kar dein, to turns per cm wahi rahenge (200 turns ÷ 20 cm = 10 turns/cm) aur field badhega nahi.

**Example 6 (kul turns):** 10 cm lambe solenoid mein 20 turns per cm hain. Kul turns kitne hain?

- Kul turns = (turns per cm) × (lambai cm mein) = 20 turns/cm × 10 cm = **200 turns**.

**Jaanch:** 200 turns ÷ 10 cm = 20 turns/cm ✅.

**Example 7 (taar ki lambai):** 50 turns ka coil banana hai, har turn ki radius 2 cm hai. Kitni taar chahiye?

- Ek turn ki lambai = circle ka perimeter = 2πr, r = 2 cm = 0.02 m.
- 50 turns: 2 × 3.14 × 0.02 m × 50 = 0.1256 m × 50 = **6.28 m**.

**Jaanch:** Ek turn 0.1256 m, 50 turns 0.1256 m × 50 = 6.28 m ✅. 6.28 m ÷ 50 = 0.1256 m ✅ (cm mein bhi: 2 × 3.14 × 2 cm = 12.56 cm, 12.56 cm × 50 = 628 cm = 6.28 m ✅).

**Example 8 (coil mein current):** 3 V ki battery ek 6 Ω ke coil se judi hai. Current kitna beh raha hai?

- Ohm ka law (electricity-part4.md): I = V ÷ R = 3 V ÷ 6 Ω = **0.5 A**.

**Jaanch:** V = I × R = 0.5 A × 6 Ω = 3 V ✅.

**Example 9 (zyada resistance matlab zyada field? Nahi):** Wahi 3 V battery ab 12 Ω ke coil se judi hai. Current aur field ka kya hoga?

- I = 3 V ÷ 12 Ω = **0.25 A**.
- Pehle 0.5 A tha, ab 0.25 A: aadha. Field (baaki sab same ho, to) **kam** hoga.

**Jaanch:** 0.25 A × 12 Ω = 3 V ✅. Aur 0.5 A ÷ 0.25 A = 2 ✅. Field resistance se seedha nahi, **current** se depend karta hai. Resistance badha to current ghata, isliye field ghata.

**Example 10 (kaun sa switch off ho sakta hai?):** Ek bar magnet aur ek solenoid mein se kaun sa switch off kiya ja sakta hai? Kaun sa poles ulta kar sakta hai?

- **Solenoid.** Current band karo, field band. Current ulta karo, N aur S pole ulte.
- Bar magnet ka magnetism band nahi kiya ja sakta (jab tak demagnetise na karein, Topic 3).

**Jaanch:** Table (Part F) se milaya ✅.

**Example 11 (do parallel taar):** Do seedhe parallel taar paas-paas rakhe hain aur dono mein current **ek hi direction** mein hai. Kya hoga?

- Har taar ke field mein doosra taar hai. Ye dono taar ek-doosre ko **attract** (aakarshit) karte hain.
- (Ye taar par force wali baat hai: iska poora detail aage ki class mein aata hai. Hum yahan sirf natija jaante hain.)

**Jaanch:** Ulti direction ke currents ke liye ye taar ek-doosre ko door dhakelte hain (sirf naam ke liye yaad rakho; ye force wali baat Topic 6 mein aayegi).

**Example 12 (bina label wale solenoid ke poles compass se):** Ek solenoid ke dono sire par label nahi hai. Compass ko ek sire ke paas laate hain, aur uski sui ka **N end us solenoid ke ek sire ki taraf** point karta hai. Wo sira kaun sa pole hai?

- Compass ki sui ka N pole **magnet ke S pole** ki taraf attract hota hai (opposite poles attract, magnetism.md).
- To jis sire ki taraf sui ka N end point kare, wo sira **S pole** hai. Aur jis sire se sui ka N end door hata (repel hua), wo sira **N pole** hai.

**Jaanch:** Compass ko dono sire par le jaakar dekho: dono ka jawab ulta aana chahiye. Aur right-hand rule se bhi ek hi jawab ✅.

### Right-hand rule application sheet

| Kya chahiye | Kaunsa rule | Kaise |
|---|---|---|
| Seedhe taar ke aas-paas field ki direction | Right-hand thumb rule | Angootha current ki taraf, ungliyan field ki taraf |
| Solenoid ya loop ka N pole | Right-hand rule for coil | Ungliyan current ki taraf, angootha N pole ki taraf |
| Loop ke chehre ka pole | Clock rule | Anticlockwise: N, clockwise: S |

Mr. Kohli ne Tvisha se poocha, "Sui ghoomi kyun?"

Tvisha: "Sir, taar mein current tha, to taar ke aas-paas ek **magnetic field** ban gaya. Wo field **taar ke around gol circles** mein hai. Sui north-south mein thi, par taar ke field ki direction taar ke perpendicular hai. To sui ko ek naya push mila aur wo ghoom gayi."

Mr. Kohli: "Bilkul. Aur jab current ulta kiya, to field ulta hua, isliye sui doosri taraf gayi. Aur switch off hone par field khatam, to sui wapas Earth ke field mein aa gayi."

Tvisha muskurayi, "Sir, ye to bahut mazedaar hai!"

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Taar mein current na ho, tab bhi magnetic field hota hai"**

Nahi. Taar mein current na ho to uska koi magnetic field nahi hota. Switch off, field off, compass wapas normal.

**Galti 2: "Field lines taar ke saath seedhi lines hain"**

Nahi. Seedhe taar ke aas-paas field lines **taar ke around gol circles** hain, taar ke perpendicular plane mein.

**Galti 3: Right-hand aur left-hand rule mix karna**

Field ki direction ke liye **right-hand** rule use hota hai (is topic mein). Left-hand rule (Fleming ka) **force** ki direction ke liye hai: wo Topic 6 mein aayega.

**Galti 4: "Solenoid ke poles nahi hote"**

Hote hain. Solenoid ke ek sire par N aur doosre par S pole hota hai, bilkul bar magnet jaisa.

**Galti 5: "Compass ki sui sirf taar ko chhune par ghoomti hai"**

Nahi. Field taar ke aas-paas hawa mein bhi hai. Sui bina touch kiye hi ghoomti hai.

**Galti 6: "Current ulta karne se field ki strength badal jaati hai"**

Nahi. Current ulta karne se field ki **strength** wahi rehti hai, sirf **direction aur poles ulte** ho jaate hain.

**Galti 7: "Zyada resistance wala coil hamesha zyada strong field deta hai"**

Nahi. Field **current** par depend karta hai. Same battery se zyada resistance matlab kam current (Example 9), to field kam hota hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Current ka magnetic effect:** current-wale taar ke aas-paas magnetic field hota hai (Oersted ka experiment). Current ulta, to deflection ulti. Current band, to field band.
- Seedhe taar ke aas-paas field lines **concentric circles** hain. Taar ke paas strong, door kam. Current zyada, to field zyada.
- **Right-hand thumb rule:** angootha current ki taraf, ungliyan field ki taraf. Current page se bahar (⊙): **anticlockwise**; andar (⊗): **clockwise**.
- Gol loop ke centre par field strong. **Clock rule:** anticlockwise = N chehra, clockwise = S chehra.
- n turns ka coil: field lagbhag n guna.
- **Solenoid:** bahar bar magnet jaisa field (N se S), andar strong, uniform, axis ke parallel.
- Solenoid ke poles: daayein haath ki ungliyan current ki taraf, angootha **N pole** ki taraf.
- Strength: current badhao, turns per length badhao, soft iron core daalo (Topic 5).
- Solenoid ko switch off kiya ja sakta hai, strength badli ja sakti hai, poles current ulta karke badle ja sakte hain.
- Solved numbers: 10 cm par 20 turns/cm = 200 turns; 50 turns, r = 2 cm: 6.28 m taar; 3 V aur 6 Ω: 0.5 A.
- **Electromagnetism:** bijli aur magnetism ek hi sikke ke do pehlu.

## Practice Questions

2. Why must the Oersted experiment be done with only one or two cells and for only a few seconds? Why should it never be done with mains electricity?

9. A solenoid is 15 cm long and has 30 turns per cm. How many turns does it have in all? What will happen to the field if the same 450 turns are wound in 5 cm?

12. List the differences between a bar magnet and a solenoid in a table (at least three points).

13. State True or False:
    (a) A wire with no current has a magnetic field around it.
    (b) The field lines around a straight current-carrying wire are circles.
    (c) Reversing the current reverses the poles of a solenoid.
    (d) The compass needle deflects only if it touches the wire.

14. Name three ways to make the magnetic field of a solenoid stronger.

### Answers

1. Oersted ke experiment ne dikhaya ki **current-wale taar ke aas-paas magnetic field hota hai.** Current on karne par compass ki sui apni direction se hat gayi (deflect hui). Current ulta karne par wo doosri taraf deflect hui. Current band karne par sui wapas aa gayi.

2. Cell ko seedhe taar se jodne par bahut current beh sakta hai (short circuit), jisse taar aur cell garm ho jaate hain aur jal bhi sakte hain. Isliye sirf ek-do cell aur kuch second ke liye. **Mains bahut khatarnak hai**, isse jhatka lag sakta hai aur jaan ko khatra hai.

3. Lines taar ke around **concentric circles** hain. Current page ke andar (⊗) ho, to right-hand rule se direction **clockwise**.

4. Current neeche, angootha neeche. Upar se dekhne par field **clockwise**.

5. **Opposite** directions mein. Taar ke upar aur neeche field ki direction ulti hoti hai (circle ka ek hissa upar ek taraf, neeche ulta), isliye sui alag-alag taraf deflect hoti hain.

6. Taar se door jaane par field **kam** hota jaata hai. Current double karne par field zyada strong (lagbhag double) ho jaata hai.

7. Anticlockwise current = **North** chehra, clockwise current = **South** chehra. Is case mein front face **North** pole hai.

8. Left end par clockwise = **S pole**. Right-hand rule se bhi: ungliyan current ki taraf, angootha **right end** ki taraf, to right end **N**. Right end se dekhne par current anticlockwise dikhta hai, clock rule se bhi right end **N** ✅. To left end **S**, right end **N**.

9. Kul turns = 30 turns/cm × 15 cm = **450 turns**. Agar wahi 450 turns 5 cm mein wind kiye jaayein, to turns per cm = 450 turns ÷ 5 cm = **90 turns/cm** (pehle ke 30 ka teen guna), to field lagbhag **3 guna** ho jayega (current same).
   Jaanch: 450 turns ÷ 15 cm = 30 turns/cm ✅, 90 ÷ 30 = 3 ✅

10. Ek turn = 2 × 22/7 × 0.035 m = 0.22 m. 40 turns = 0.22 m × 40 = **8.8 m**.
    Jaanch: 8.8 m ÷ 40 = 0.22 m ✅

11. I = V ÷ R = 6 V ÷ 4 Ω = **1.5 A**. 8 Ω ke saath I = 6 V ÷ 8 Ω = **0.75 A**. Current aadha ho gaya, to field **kam** (lagbhag aadha) ho jaata hai.
    Jaanch: 1.5 A × 4 Ω = 6 V ✅, 0.75 A × 8 Ω = 6 V ✅

12. Bar magnet ki strength tay hoti hai, solenoid ki strength current aur turns se badal sakte hain. Bar magnet ko switch off nahi kar sakte, solenoid ko current band karke kar sakte hain. Bar magnet ke poles ulte nahi ho sakte, solenoid ke current ulta karne par ho jaate hain. Dono ka bahar ka field N se S ki taraf jaata hai.

13. (a) **False** ❌. Bina current ke taar ke aas-paas koi magnetic field nahi hota.
    (b) **True** ✅. Taar ke around gol circles.
    (c) **True** ✅. Current ulta, to N aur S ulte.
    (d) **False** ❌. Field hawa mein hai, sui bina touch kiye ghoomti hai.

14. (1) Current badhana, (2) turns ki sankhya (lambai ke hisaab se) badhana, (3) andar soft iron core rakhna.
