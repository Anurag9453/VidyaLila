# Topic 5: Electromagnet aur Uske Uses

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki jab wire ke coil (solenoid) mein se current guzarta hai, to wo ek bar magnet ki tarah behave karta hai: uska ek sira N, doosra S, aur right-hand rule se hum pole pehchan sakte hain. Us topic ke aakhir mein ek idea chhoda tha: solenoid ke andar lohe ki chhad rakh do. Ab is topic mein hum wahi karenge, aur dekhenge ki isse ek aisa **taqatwar, on-off hone wala magnet** banta hai jo duniya bhar mein kaam aa raha hai: kabadi ki crane se le kar bell, speaker aur hospital ki MRI machine tak.

## Kabadkhane ki crane

Ek bade kabadkhane (scrapyard) mein Mr. Bhullar crane chalate the. Crane ke hook par ek bada gol, kala sa "magnet" latka tha. Unka chhota padosi Daksh ek din chhutti mein unke saath aa gaya aur door khade hokar dekhne laga.

Mr. Bhullar ne switch dabaya. Magnet neeche gaya, purani car ke dhaanchon (car bodies) ke dher par tika, aur jab upar utha to poora dher, kai purani gaadiyon ke dhaanche, uske saath chipak kar hawa mein utha gaya. Crane ghoomi, truck ke upar pahunchi, aur Mr. Bhullar ne switch dobara dabaya. **Chhapaak!** Saara dher ek saath truck mein gir pada.

Daksh hairaan reh gaya. "Uncle, ye to magnet hai na? Magnet ka to kaam hai chipakna. Ye kabhi chipakta hai aur kabhi chhod deta hai? Ek aam magnet ko to maine kabhi 'chhodte' nahi dekha!"

Mr. Bhullar muskuraye, "Ye aam magnet hai hi nahi, beta. Ye bijli se chalne wala magnet hai. Isko banane ka tareeka tum abhi mere saath seekh lo, phir tumhara sawal khud solve ho jayega." Is kahani ko hum topic ke aakhir mein solve karenge.

## Part A: Electromagnet kya hai?

### Coil + soft iron core

Topic 4 mein humne dekha ki sirf coil (khaali solenoid) mein current dene par bhi uska magnetic field banta hai. Magnetic field ka hona thik hai, par wo kamzor hota hai: ek khaali coil se aap shayad ek-do pin bhi mushkil se utha paoge.

Ab coil ke andar **soft iron ki chhad ya nail** rakh do. Dekhne mein itna hi badlaav hai, par asar bahut bada hai:

- Jaise hi current chalta hai, coil ka field lohe ke andar pahunchta hai. Topic 3 mein dekha tha ki lohe ke andar chhote-chhote magnet (domains) hote hain jo aam taur par idhar-udhar muh kiye rehte hain. **Coil ka field un sab ko ek hi disha mein sidha (line up) kar deta hai.** Ab lohe ka tukda khud ek taqatwar magnet ban jaata hai, aur uska apna field coil ke field ke saath jud jaata hai.
- Isliye **electromagnet ka field khaali coil ke field se kai guna zyada** hota hai.
- Jab current band karte hain, to coil ka field khatam ho jaata hai. **Soft iron** apna magnetism lagbhag saara kho deta hai (Topic 3: soft iron temporary magnet hai), to chhad se pin gir jaate hain.

> **Electromagnet = insulated wire ka coil + soft iron ka core + current ka source (cell ya battery) + switch.**
> Current chalu: taqatwar magnet. Current band: (lagbhag) koi magnet nahi.

### Banawat (construction)

```
                    soft iron ki nail (core)
          ┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
    ┌─────┤ ((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((((( ├─────┐
    │     └─────────────────────────────────────┘     │
    │              insulated wire ke turns            │
    │                                                 │
    o                                                ─┴─
     \  switch                                       ─┬─  cell (battery)
    o                                                 │
    │                                                 │
    └──────────────────── tar (wire) ─────────────────┘
        (switch band: current chalu, nail magnet banti hai)
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Electromagnet: soft iron ki nail par lipta insulated tar, ek switch aur ek cell. Tar ke dono sire cell se jude hain.

Ek aur aam shape **C-shaped ya horseshoe (ghode ki naal)** wala soft iron ka core hai. Isme core ko mod kar C ya U shape diya jaata hai, aur uski **dono baajuon (limbs) par alag-alag coil** lapete jaate hain (dono coil is tarah jude hote hain ki ek limb N bane aur doosra S).

```
      coil 1                   coil 2
     @@@@@@@@                 @@@@@@@@
   ┌═══════════╗           ╔═══════════┐
   ║                                   ║
   ║        soft iron ka core          ║
   ║         (U ya C shape)            ║
   ║                                   ║
   ╚═══╗                           ╔═══╝
       ║                           ║
       N                           S
     (pole)                      (pole)
     └──── chhota gap, dono pole saath kheenchte hain ────┘
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Horseshoe electromagnet: do coil, dono pole paas-paas. Lohe ka tukda dono poles ke beech aa jaye to dono saath kheenchte hain.

Horseshoe shape ka fayda: **dono poles paas-paas** aate hain, isliye dono saath mil kar lohe ke tukde ko kheenchte hain. Alag-alag door rakhe do poles utne asardaar nahi hote.

### Poles ka pata kaise lagayein

Topic 4 ka **right-hand rule** wahi hai: ungliyaan coil mein **current ki disha** mein mudo, to angootha us taraf point karega jo **N pole** hai. Ya fir coil ke ek sire ko saamne se dekho: wahan current **anticlockwise** (ghadi ki ulti disha) dikhe to wo sira **N**, aur **clockwise** (ghadi ki disha) dikhe to wo sira **S**.

## Part B: Activity: nail se pin uthana

**Chahiye:** ek lohe ki nail (jis par rust na ho, jitni lambi mile), insulated (enamelled) copper wire, 1.5 V ke cell (ek ya do), cell holder, aur kuch chhote pin ya paper clips (lohe ke).

**Safety (zaroori):**
- Circuit **sirf kuch second** ke liye jodo, kyunki tar aur cell **garm** ho jaate hain. Pins utha kar gino aur turant circuit tod do.
- **Kabhi mains bijli (ghar ki bijli) ya sockets istemal mat karna.** Sirf cell ya choti battery. Kisi bade adult ke bina koi bhi experiment mat karo.
- Car battery bilkul nahi.

**Kaise karna hai:**
1. Wire ko nail par paas-paas, ek hi disha mein lapeto (jaise 10 turns).
2. Wire ke dono sire cell se jodo. Nail ko pins ke dher ke paas le jao. Pin chipak jayenge. Ginti likho.
3. Circuit tod do (cell se ek sira hata do). Pin gir jayenge.
4. Ab turns 20 karo aur phir ginti likho. Fir cells 2 karo (cells ko is tarah jodo ki current badhe) aur dobara ginti likho.

**Ek namune ke readings:**

| Turns | Cells | Pins utthe |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 1 | 4 |
| 20 | 1 | 9 |
| 20 | 2 | 18 |

**Observation:** Turns badhaye to pins badhe (4 se 9). Cells badhaye (yaani current badha) to bhi pins badhe (9 se 18). **Zyada turns aur zyada current, to magnet zyada taqatwar.** Circuit tootte hi pin gir gaye: magnet "off" ho gaya.

## Part C: Electromagnet kin baaton par depend karta hai? (thoda extra, Class 10 overlap)

Electromagnet ki taqat badhane ke chaar tareeke:

1. **Turns ki sankhya badhao.** Har turn ka apna chhota sa field hota hai, aur turns jitne zyada, sab ke field milkar utne bade.
2. **Current badhao.** Zyada cells (series mein) lagao, ya circuit ka resistance kam karo, to current badhta hai. (Dhyan: current badhane par tar garm bhi zyada hota hai, Part F dekho.)
3. **Soft iron core ek achhe size ka rakho.** Core ka hona zaroori hai, aur wo coil ke hisaab se sahi lambai-moti ka ho. Lohe ke bina bhi field hota hai, par kaafi kamzor.
4. **Poles ke beech ka gap kam karo.** Horseshoe shape isiliye acchi hai: dono poles paas, dono ki khinchaav saath.

```
 Pins utthe (namune ke readings):

 10 turns, 1 cell : ####                4 pins
 20 turns, 1 cell : #########           9 pins
 20 turns, 2 cells: ##################  18 pins
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Turns aur current badhane par uthne wale pins ki sankhya badhti hai (har # = 1 pin).

## Part D: Electromagnet bana permanent magnet se behtar kyun?

| Baat | Permanent magnet | Electromagnet |
|---|---|---|
| Taqat badalna | Taqat lagbhag tay rehti hai | Current ya turns badal kar badal sakte hain |
| On-off | Hamesha "on" rehta hai | Switch se on-off hota hai |
| Poles ulatna | Poles tay hote hain | Current ulta karo to N aur S badal jaate hain |
| Bahut taqatwar banana | Mushkil | Bahut bada aur taqatwar ban sakta hai (tonnes tak uthata hai) |
| Chalane ka kharch | Kuch nahi | Lagataar bijli chahiye |

**Kamiyaan (disadvantages):**
- Use chalu rakhne ke liye **lagataar bijli (energy)** chahiye.
- Coil mein current se **heat** paida hoti hai (heating effect: [electricity-part6.md](../electricity/electricity-part6.md)), jisse tar aur cell garm hote hain. Isliye bade electromagnet ko thanda karna padta hai ya zyada der chalu nahi rakhte.

## Part E: Solved examples

**Example 1 (current ki disha se poles):** Ek nail par coil lipta hai. Har case mein batao ki nail ka daayan (right) sira N hai ya S, aur baayan (left) sira kya hai. (Dekhne wala us sire ki taraf muh karke dekh raha hai jiska zikr hai.)

| Case | Kahan se dekha | Current ki disha | Wo sira | Doosra sira |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (a) | Daayein sire se | Anticlockwise | N | S |
| (b) | Daayein sire se | Clockwise | S | N |
| (c) | Baayein sire se | Anticlockwise | N | S |
| (d) | Baayein sire se | Clockwise | S | N |

Matlab: (a) daayan sira N, baayan S. (b) daayan sira S, baayan N. (c) baayan sira N, daayan S. (d) baayan sira S, daayan N.

**Jaanch:** Anticlockwise = N (right-hand rule: ungliyaan current ki disha mein mudo, angootha N ki taraf). Har case mein ek sira N aur ek S, kabhi dono N nahi ✅

**Example 2 (current ulta karne ka asar):** Cell ke terminals badal diye gaye. Electromagnet ka kya hoga?

Current ki disha ulti hui, to jo sira pehle anticlockwise dikhta tha wo ab clockwise dikhega (aur ulta). **N aur S pole aapas mein badal jaate hain.** Par current ki matra wahi hai (cell wahi, turns wahi), to magnet ki **taqat same** rahegi. Pin utne hi utthenge.

**Jaanch:** Current ka direction badla, size nahi. 0.5 A ulta karo to bhi 0.5 A hi hai, to field ki taqat wahi, sirf N-S ulte ✅

**Example 3 (readings ka tulna):** Part B ke readings se: (a) turns double (10 se 20, 1 cell) karne par pins ka ratio. (b) current double (1 se 2 cells, 20 turns) karne par ratio. (c) dono ek saath double karne par.

- (a) 9 pins ÷ 4 pins = **2.25**
- (b) 18 pins ÷ 9 pins = **2**
- (c) 18 pins ÷ 4 pins = **4.5**

**Jaanch:** 2.25 × 4 pins = 9 pins ✅, 2 × 9 pins = 18 pins ✅, 4.5 × 4 pins = 18 pins ✅. Matlab turns double karne par pins **lagbhag** double, current double karne par pins **lagbhag** double (ek-do pins ka farak experiment ki galtiyon se aata hai). Dono double karne par lagbhag 4 guna. Yani taqat turns aur current ke **lagbhag proportional** hai.

**Example 4 (kitne turns lapetenge):** Ek insulated wire ki moti (diameter) 0.5 mm hai. Nail par 6 cm lambai tak ek parat mein wire lapetna hai. Turns kitne aayenge? Aur tar lagbhag kitna lamba lagega agar nail ka diameter 5 mm hai?

- 1 cm = 10 mm, to 1 cm mein 10 mm ÷ 0.5 mm = **20 turns/cm**
- 6 cm mein 6 cm × 20 turns/cm = **120 turns**
- Ek turn ki lambai = circumference = π × d = 3.14 × 0.5 cm = 1.57 cm. 120 turns = 120 × 1.57 cm = 188.4 cm, yaani **lagbhag 1.9 m** tar.

**Jaanch:** 120 turns × 0.5 mm = 60 mm = 6 cm ✅. (Tar ki apni motai ko lagbhag chhod diya, isliye "lagbhag".)

**Example 5 (coil ka current):** Ek coil ka resistance 12 Ω hai, aur use 6 V ki battery se jodha gaya hai. Current kitna hoga?

Ohm ka rule: I = V ÷ R = 6 V ÷ 12 Ω = **0.5 A**

**Jaanch:** 0.5 A × 12 Ω = 6 V ✅. Agar sirf 4.5 V (teen 1.5 V ke cell) lagayein: I = 4.5 V ÷ 12 Ω = 0.375 A, yaani kam current, to magnet bhi kamzor (Example 3 ki tarah) ✅

**Example 6 (coil mein heat):** Example 5 ke coil (0.5 A, 12 Ω) mein 1 minute mein kitni heat paida hogi? 10 minute mein?

Heat H = I² × R × t
- 1 minute = 60 s: H = (0.5 A)² × 12 Ω × 60 s = 0.25 A² × 12 Ω × 60 s = **180 J**
- 10 minute = 600 s: H = 0.25 A² × 12 Ω × 600 s = **1800 J**

**Jaanch:** 0.25 A² × 12 Ω = 3 W (power), 3 W × 60 s = 180 J ✅, 3 W × 600 s = 1800 J ✅. Ye heat tar aur lohe ko garm karti hai, isiliye electromagnet ko zyada der chalu nahi rakhte. Aadha current (0.25 A) ho to heat sirf (0.25 A)² × 12 Ω × 60 s = 45 J, yaani chaar guna kam (180 J ÷ 45 J = 4 ✅).

**Example 7 (crane kitna bal sambhale):** Ek car ke dhaanche ka mass 800 kg hai. Crane ke magnet ko kam se kam kitna bal lagana hoga use uthane ke liye? Agar ek baar mein 5 aise dhaanche uthaye jayein? (g = 10 m/s²)

- Ek dhaancha: weight = m × g = 800 kg × 10 m/s² = **8000 N**. Magnet ka bal **kam se kam 8000 N** hona chahiye (practically thoda zyada, kyunki hilne ya jhatke se bal lagta hai).
- 5 dhaanche: mass = 5 × 800 kg = 4000 kg, weight = 4000 kg × 10 m/s² = **40000 N**.

**Jaanch:** 8000 N × 5 = 40000 N ✅. (Weight kya hai: [gravitation-part5.md](../gravitation/gravitation-part5.md).) Aam magnet itna bal nahi laga sakta, par bada electromagnet laga sakta hai.

**Example 8 (electric bell kaise rukta aur chalta hai):** Electric bell ka kaunsa hissa circuit tod deta hai, aur hammer baar-baar kyun hilta rehta hai?

(Bell ki poori banawat Part F mein hai.) Jab current coil mein chalta hai, to coil electromagnet ban jaata hai aur soft iron ki armature ko kheench leta hai. Armature ke saath hammer gong par lagta hai, aur usi pal **contact screw se contact toot jaata hai**, to current ruk jaata hai. Current ruka to electromagnet ka magnetism khatam (soft iron!), aur **spring strip armature ko wapas kheench leti hai**. Wapas aate hi contact dobara jud jaata hai, current chalu, aur chakra phir shuru. Ye ek second mein kai baar (bahut tezi se) hota hai, isliye hammer lagataar hilta aur gong bajta rehta hai.

**Jaanch:** Agar core steel ka hota to magnetism na jaata aur armature wapas nahi aati (Example 10) ✅. Hammer ko haath se hilane ki zarurat nahi.

**Example 9 (relay: do alag circuit):** Ek relay ke coil ko 6 V ke chhote circuit (bahut kam current) se chalaya gaya, aur relay ke switch (contacts) se ek 12 V ki battery aur ek bada motor jodha gaya, jo bahut zyada current leta hai (jaise car ka starter motor). Ye kaise kaam karta hai aur fayda kya hai?

```
  Circuit 1 (chhota, 6 V, kam current)     Circuit 2 (bada, 12 V, zyada current)

  ─┤├─ cell ── switch ── coil               motor ── relay ka switch
                         @@@@                         (contacts)
                         ═════ soft iron core            │
                           │ kheenchta hai               │
                           └──────── armature ───────────┘
```

> **Figure 5.4** – Relay ka block diagram: chhote circuit ka coil armature ko kheenchta hai, aur armature doosre circuit ka switch band kar deta hai. Do circuit aapas mein alag hain.

Chhote circuit ka switch dabaya: coil electromagnet bana, armature ko kheencha, aur doosre circuit ke contacts band ho gaye, to motor chal pada. Switch chhoda: coil magnet nahi raha, armature spring se wapas, contacts khul gaye, motor ruk gaya.

**Fayda:** Dono circuit **alag** hain. Haath sirf 6 V wale chhote, kam current wale switch ko chhoota hai, aur motor ka bada current patle taar aur chhote switch se nahi guzarta, alag circuit mein rehta hai. Ye bas samajhne ke liye diagram hai; ghar ki bijli (mains) ke saath kuch jodne ka koi experiment kabhi mat karna.

**Jaanch:** Circuit 1 ka voltage 6 V, circuit 2 ka 12 V: dono circuit alag-alag, beech mein sirf armature ka magnetic kheenchav ✅

**Example 10 (core ka material):** Crane ke electromagnet mein soft iron ka core kyun lagta hai, steel ka kyun nahi?

- **Soft iron:** aasani se magnetise hota hai (current aaya, magnet taqatwar), aur current band hote hi lagbhag saara magnetism kho deta hai. Lohe ka dher chhoot jaata hai.
- **Steel:** magnetise hone mein mushkil, aur ek baar magnetise hua to **magnetism rakh leta hai** (permanent magnet jaisa). Current band karne par bhi core lohe ko pakde rakhega, to crane lohe ko chhod nahi paayegi.

**Jaanch:** Crane ko "on" bhi chahiye aur "off" bhi. "Off" sirf soft iron deta hai ✅. Steel wahan kaam aata hai jahan magnetism hamesha chahiye (permanent magnet, Topic 3).

## Part F: Electromagnet ke uses

### 1. Kabadkhane ki crane aur lohe ke karkhaane

Crane ke hook par bada electromagnet. Switch on: current chala, soft iron core taqatwar magnet bana, aur purani gaadiyon ka lohe ka dher chipak kar uth gaya. Switch off: current band, magnetism khatam, dher truck mein gir gaya. Aise hi steel plants mein garm lohe ke tukde aur factories mein bhaari lohe ki chaadar (iron sheets) uthane ke liye, aur shipyards mein bhi magnetic cranes use hoti hain.

### 2. Electric bell

```
   ┌──── push-button ────┐
   │                     │
  ─┴─  cell          ┌───┴───────────────┐
  ─┬─                │ COIL  @@@@@@@@    │  electromagnet
   │                 │ (soft iron core)  │
   │                 └───┬───────────────┘
   │                     │ tar    ↑ coil armature ko kheenchta hai
   │   spring strip ═════╧══ ARMATURE (soft iron) ══ hammer ──► GONG
   │                              ●  contact point (armature par)
   │                              ●  CONTACT SCREW
   └──────────────────────────────┘
```

> **Figure 5.5** – Electric bell: push-button, coil (electromagnet), soft iron ki armature jis par hammer laga hai, spring strip, contact screw aur gong. Current ka raasta: cell, push-button, coil, spring strip, armature, contact point, contact screw, aur wapas cell. Coil armature ko upar kheenchti hai to contact point screw se alag ho jaata hai.

Kaam karne ka tareeka, kadam-dar-kadam:
1. Push-button dabaya: current cell, coil, armature aur contact screw se hota hua chalta hai.
2. Coil **electromagnet** ban gaya aur soft iron ki **armature ko kheench liya**.
3. Armature ke saath **hammer gong par laga**: tang!
4. Armature kheenchte hi **contact screw se alag ho gayi**, circuit toot gaya, current ruk gaya.
5. Magnetism khatam (soft iron), to **spring strip ne armature ko wapas kheench liya**.
6. Wapas aate hi contact dobara jud gaya: kadam 2 se phir shuru.

Ye chakra bahut tezi se baar-baar hota hai, isliye jab tak **push-button dabaa hai**, bell **lagataar bajti rehti hai**. Button chhoda, to chakra ruk gaya.

### 3. Electric relay
Part E ka Example 9: chhota current bade current wale circuit ko on-off karta hai. Car ke starter, circuit breakers aur bahut se automatic switches mein relay hota hai.

### 4. Telegraph aur purane telephone receiver
Purane telegraph mein electromagnet ek patti ko kheench kar "tak-tak" ki awaaz karta tha (dots aur dashes). Purane telephone receivers mein bhi electromagnet ek patli patti (diaphragm) ko hilata tha.

### 5. Loudspeaker aur headphones
In mein ek **coil ek permanent magnet ke paas** hota hai. Coil mein awaaz ke hisaab se badalta current jaata hai, to coil magnet ke saamne aage-peeche hilta hai, aur usse jude paper ya diaphragm ko hilata hai. Hawa hilne se **sound** banti hai ([sound.md](../sound/sound.md)). (Kyun hilta hai, uska kaaran, yaani magnetic field mein current wale taar par force, Topic 6 mein aayega; abhi sirf itna jaano.)

### 6. Motors aur generators
Electric fan, mixer, metro ki motor: sab mein electromagnet. Bijli banane wale generators mein bhi. In dono ki baat Topic 6 mein.

### 7. Magnetic separator
Khanon (mines) aur recycling plants mein conveyor belt ke upar ya aage bada electromagnet lagta hai. Kachre mein se **lohe ke tukde** (Topic 1: lohe par magnet ka asar hota hai, plastic, kaanch, aluminium par nahi) magnet se chipak kar alag ho jaate hain.

### 8. MRI machine
Hospital ki MRI machine mein bahut taqatwar **superconducting electromagnets** hote hain, jinke coil bahut thande kiye jaate hain taaki unka resistance lagbhag zero ho jaaye aur current bahut zyada chale bina zyada heat ke.

### 9. Magnetic levitation (maglev) trains
Kuch trains electromagnets ke bal se track ke upar thodi uthi (float) rehti hain, to pahiyon ka ghisaav nahi hota aur wo bahut tez chalti hain.

### 10. Aur bhi
Doorbell, circuit breaker (zyada current aane par electromagnet switch tod deta hai), door ke electromagnetic lock, fire-door ke **door-holder** (aam din mein electromagnet darwaaza khula pakde rakhta hai; aag ka alarm bajte hi current band, to darwaaza apne aap band), aur computer ke **hard disk** mein bhi electromagnet data likhta hai.

## Part G: Daksh ka sawal solve karte hain

Daksh ne dobara poocha, "Par uncle, magnet chhod kaise deta hai?"

Mr. Bhullar ne samjhaya, "Crane ka magnet ek bada coil hai jis ke andar soft iron ka core hai. **Switch dabaya: current chalta hai, core magnet ban jaata hai** aur lohe ke dher ko pakad leta hai. **Switch dobara dabaya: current band, core ka magnetism lagbhag khatam**, to dher apne bhaar (weight) se neeche gir jaata hai."

Daksh bola, "To isliye ye aam magnet nahi, ye to 'jab marzi ho, tab' wala magnet hai!"

"Bilkul. Aur isme **steel nahi, soft iron** lagta hai, warna current band karne par bhi magnetism rehta aur dher kabhi nahi girta. Ek car ke dhaanche (800 kg) ka weight hi 8000 N hai, aur jab dher uthta hai tab magnet ko usse bhi zyada bal chahiye." Daksh ne dher ko truck mein girte hue dekha aur taali baja di.

(Topic 1 mein Mr. Barua ke kabaadi yard ka crane magnet bhi asal mein aisa hi electromagnet tha. Tab humne sirf ye dekha tha ki wo kya uthata hai; ab ye bhi pata hai ki wo dher ko chhodta kaise hai.)

## Part H: Surakshit rehna

- Coil ke saath **car battery ya ghar ki bijli (mains) kabhi mat jodo**: bahut zyada current, tar jal sakta hai, bijli ka jhatka ya aag lag sakti hai. Experiment mein sirf 1.5 V ke cell.
- Taqatwar electromagnet ke paas **pacemaker** wale mareez ko nahi jaana chahiye (doctor ki salah se hi). Hospital ki MRI mein dhaatu ki cheezein nahi le jaate.
- **Credit/debit card, phone, ghadi** taqatwar magnet ke paas mat rakho, unka data ya kaam kharab ho sakta hai.
- Taar aur cell jab garm lage, to turant circuit tod do.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Electromagnet ek aisa magnet hai jo current band hone par bhi magnetism rakhta hai."**
Nahi. Core soft iron ka hai, jo current band hote hi lagbhag saara magnetism kho deta hai. Magnetism rakhne wala steel hota hai, soft iron nahi.

**Galti 2: "Crane mein steel ka core behtar hoga."**
Ulta. Steel magnetism rakh leta, to crane dher ko chhod nahi paati (Example 10).

**Galti 3: Bina insulation ka nanga tar lapetna.**
Nanga tar lapetne par paas-paas turns aapas mein chhoo kar **short-circuit** ho jaate hain: current coil ke chakkar lagaye bina seedha chala jaata hai, to field kamzor. Isliye tar insulated (enamelled) hona chahiye.

**Galti 4: "Bina current ke bada coil bhi magnet hota hai."**
Nahi. Current ke bina coil (aur soft iron core) magnet nahi hai. Magnet tab banta hai jab current chalta hai.

**Galti 5: "Bell ka hammer haath se hilana padta hai."**
Nahi. Bell ka hammer apne aap hilta hai: electromagnet armature ko kheenchta hai, contact tootta hai, spring wapas laati hai (Example 8). Push-button sirf pehla current chalu karta hai.

**Galti 6: "Electromagnet ke poles ulte nahi ho sakte."**
Ho sakte hain. Current ulta karo, to N aur S badal jaate hain (Example 2). Yahi permanent magnet se bada fark hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Electromagnet** = insulated wire ka coil + **soft iron core** + cell/battery + switch. Core current chalne par taqatwar magnet banta hai (iske chhote magnet line up ho jaate hain) aur current band hote hi lagbhag saara magnetism kho deta hai.
- Electromagnet ka field khaali coil ke field se **kai guna zyada** hota hai.
- Poles **right-hand rule** se: ungliyaan current ki disha mein, angootha N ki taraf. Anticlockwise sira N, clockwise sira S. Current ulta karne par poles ulte, strength same.
- Activity (namune ke readings): 10 turns/1 cell: 4 pins, 20 turns/1 cell: 9 pins, 20 turns/2 cells: 18 pins. Ratio 9/4 = 2.25 aur 18/9 = 2: **lagbhag proportional**. Safety: circuit sirf kuch second, sirf cell, mains/socket kabhi nahi.
- Strength badhane ke tareeke: **zyada turns, zyada current, soft iron core sahi size ka, poles ke beech kam gap (horseshoe)**.
- Faayde: taqat badal sakte hain, on-off, poles ulte, bahut taqatwar. Kamiyaan: lagataar bijli chahiye, garm hota hai (I²Rt: 0.5² × 12 × 60 = 180 J ek minute mein).
- Uses: crane (car dhaancha 800 kg = 8000 N), electric bell, relay, telegraph aur purane telephone, loudspeaker aur headphones, motors aur generators, magnetic separators, MRI, maglev trains, circuit breakers, doorbell, locks, door-holders, hard disk.
- Bell ka chakra: current, coil magnet, armature khinchi, hammer gong par, contact toota, current band, magnetism gaya, spring ne armature wapas ki, contact jud gaya, phir se.
- Safety: car battery ya mains kabhi nahi; pacemaker, cards aur phone ko taqatwar magnet se door rakho.

## Practice Questions

1. What is an electromagnet? Name its main parts.

2. Why is the core of an electromagnet made of soft iron and not steel? Answer in two or three lines.

3. A coil is wound on an iron nail. Looking at the right end, the current flows clockwise. Which pole is formed at the right end and which at the left end? Does the strength change if the cell is reversed?

4. In an experiment with a nail coil, 10 turns with 1 cell lift 5 pins, and 20 turns with 1 cell lift 11 pins. Find the ratio of the pins and state what you conclude.

5. Name four ways to make an electromagnet stronger.

6. A coil has resistance 15 Ω and is connected to a 6 V battery. Find the current. Then find the heat produced in 2 minutes.

7. A wire of thickness 0.5 mm (including insulation) is wound in a single layer along 4 cm of an iron rod. How many turns are there?

8. A crane lifts a car body of mass 1200 kg. Find its weight, taking g = 10 m/s². What is the minimum force the magnet must exert?

9. Describe in steps how an electric bell keeps ringing as long as the button is pressed. Which part breaks the circuit?

10. A relay uses a small 6 V circuit to switch on a large motor that runs from a 12 V battery and draws a very large current. What is the advantage of keeping the two circuits separate?

11. State True or False:
    (a) An electromagnet keeps its magnetism for a long time after the current is switched off, if its core is soft iron.
    (b) Reversing the current in an electromagnet reverses its poles.
    (c) A bare (uninsulated) wire is fine for winding an electromagnet.
    (d) A magnetic separator can pull out iron pieces from a pile of plastic and glass waste.

12. Give three advantages and two disadvantages of an electromagnet compared with a permanent magnet.

13. Why should the circuit of the nail electromagnet be connected only for a few seconds, and why should we never use mains electricity or a socket for it?

14. A coil carries 0.4 A and has resistance 10 Ω. Find the heat produced in 30 s. If the current is doubled, how many times will the heat become?

### Answers

1. Electromagnet ek **insulated wire ka coil** hai jiske andar **soft iron ka core** hota hai, aur wo cell ya battery se jaise hi current paata hai, taqatwar magnet ban jaata hai. Main parts: coil, soft iron core, cell/battery, switch.

2. Soft iron aasani se magnetise hota hai aur current band hote hi lagbhag saara magnetism kho deta hai, to magnet "on" aur "off" ho sakta hai. Steel apna magnetism rakh leta hai, to current band karne par bhi magnet "off" nahi hota.

3. Clockwise dekha jaane wala (right) sira **S** hai, to left sira **N**. Cell ulta karne par poles ulte ho jaate hain (right N, left S), par strength **same** rehti hai kyunki current ki matra wahi hai.
   Jaanch: clockwise = S, anticlockwise = N ✅

4. Ratio = 11 pins ÷ 5 pins = **2.2**. Turns double karne par pins lagbhag double, yani strength turns ke lagbhag proportional.
   Jaanch: 2.2 × 5 pins = 11 pins ✅

5. (i) Turns badhao. (ii) Current badhao (zyada cells, kam resistance). (iii) Sahi size ka soft iron core lagao. (iv) Poles ke beech gap kam karo (horseshoe shape).

6. I = V ÷ R = 6 V ÷ 15 Ω = **0.4 A**. t = 2 × 60 s = 120 s. H = I² × R × t = (0.4 A)² × 15 Ω × 120 s = 0.16 A² × 15 Ω × 120 s = 2.4 W × 120 s = **288 J**.
   Jaanch: 0.4 A × 15 Ω = 6 V ✅; 0.16 A² × 15 Ω = 2.4 W, 2.4 W × 120 s = 288 J ✅

7. 0.5 mm = 0.05 cm. 1 cm mein 1 cm ÷ 0.05 cm = 20 turns. 4 cm mein 4 cm × 20 turns/cm = **80 turns**.
   Jaanch: 80 × 0.5 mm = 40 mm = 4 cm ✅

8. Weight = m × g = 1200 kg × 10 m/s² = **12000 N**. Magnet ko **kam se kam 12000 N** bal lagana hoga.
   Jaanch: 12000 N ÷ 10 m/s² = 1200 kg ✅

9. (1) Button dabao, current coil mein. (2) Coil electromagnet bana, armature ko kheencha. (3) Hammer gong par laga. (4) Armature ke hatte hi **contact screw** par contact toot gaya, circuit toot gaya. (5) Magnetism gaya, spring ne armature wapas laayi. (6) Contact dobara juda, chakra phir shuru. **Contact screw** (contact) circuit todta hai.

10. Dono circuit alag hain: haath sirf 6 V wale chhote, kam current wale switch ko chhoota hai, aur motor ka bahut bada current apne alag circuit mein rehta hai. Isliye chhota switch aur patle taar garm hokar kharab nahi hote, aur bade circuit ko door se, surakshit tareeke se on-off kiya ja sakta hai.

11. (a) **False** ❌. Soft iron current band hote hi lagbhag saara magnetism kho deta hai.
    (b) **True** ✅. Current ulta karne par N aur S badal jaate hain.
    (c) **False** ❌. Bina insulation ke turns short-circuit ho jaate hain.
    (d) **True** ✅. Plastic aur glass par magnet ka asar nahi hota, iron par hota hai.

12. Faayde: strength badal sakte hain, switch se on-off, poles ulte, bahut taqatwar ban sakta hai (koi bhi teen). Kamiyaan: lagataar bijli chahiye, aur coil garm hota hai.

13. Tar aur cell current se garm hote hain, aur zyada der chalu rakhne par garmi se kharabi ya jalan ho sakti hai. Mains (230 V) ya socket se bahut zyada voltage aur current hota hai: bijli ka jhatka aur aag ka khatra, isliye sirf 1.5 V ke cell.

14. H = (0.4 A)² × 10 Ω × 30 s = 0.16 A² × 10 Ω × 30 s = **48 J**. Current double (0.8 A) karne par H = (0.8 A)² × 10 Ω × 30 s = 0.64 A² × 10 Ω × 30 s = 192 J, yani **4 guna**.
    Jaanch: 192 J ÷ 48 J = 4 ✅
