## Part A: Magnet se current: Faraday ka prayog

### Ek bahut puraani khoj

Oersted ne dikhaya tha ki current magnetism banata hai. Uske kuch saal baad, **Michael Faraday** naam ke vaigyanik ne ulta socha: agar current magnet jaisa asar karta hai, to shayad magnet bhi current paida kar sake. Kaha jaata hai ki 1831 mein unhone ye khoj ki, aur isko **electromagnetic induction** naam diya gaya. Aaj ke bijli-ghar isi khoj par chalte hain.

### Activity: coil aur magnet

**Samaan:** taar ki ek chhoti coil (jis par taar ke kai pher lapete ho), ek sensitive galvanometer (centre-zero wala) ya ek compass, aur ek bar magnet.

**Suraksha:** is activity mein koi mains ki bijli (ghar ka socket) nahi lagti. Sirf chhoti coil, magnet aur galvanometer. Kabhi bhi socket ya ghar ki bijli se ye prayog mat karna.

Coil ke dono sire galvanometer se jod do. Ab magnet ko coil ke saath ye karo:

```
        coil (kai pher)
        ((((((((((
   ┌────((((((((((────┐
   │                   │
   │                  ─(G)─  galvanometer
   │                   │
   └───────────────────┘

   [N═════S]  ───>  coil ke andar dhakelo
   magnet
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Prayog ka set-up: taar ki coil galvanometer se judi hai, aur bar magnet ko coil ke andar dhakela ya bahar khincha ja raha hai. Circuit mein koi battery nahi hai.

**Kya dikhta hai?**

1. Magnet ko coil ke **andar dhakelo**: galvanometer ki needle **ek taraf** hilti hai.
2. Magnet ko coil se **bahar khincho**: needle **doosri taraf** hilti hai.
3. Magnet ko coil ke andar **rok do (sthir)**: needle **beech (zero)** par aa jaati hai, koi deflection nahi.
4. Magnet ko **tezi se** hilao: needle **zyada** hilti hai.
5. Magnet ko rokkar **coil ko** magnet ki taraf hilao: wahi nateeja milta hai. Matlab sirf **relative motion** matter karti hai.
6. Coil ke **pher (turns) badhao**, ya **zyada tagda magnet** lo: needle aur zyada hilti hai.

Dekho: circuit mein koi battery nahi thi, phir bhi current bana. Aur wo tabhi bana jab magnet (ya coil) hil raha tha.

### Nateeja

**Jab bhi band (closed) coil ke aas paas ka magnetic field badalta hai (magnet aur coil ke beech relative motion hota hai), coil mein current paida hota hai.** Ise **induced current** kehte hain, aur isko chalane wale "dabaav" (potential difference) ko **induced EMF** kehte hain. Ye hi **electromagnetic induction** hai.

Teen zaroori baatein:

- **Sirf badalne par:** field ya magnet sthir hai to koi current nahi, chahe magnet coil ke andar hi kyun na ho.
- **Disha:** halchal ulti karo to induced current ki disha bhi ulti ho jaati hai (needle doosri taraf hilti hai). Iska poora niyam, jise **Lenz ka niyam** kehte hain, aage ki class mein aayega. Abhi sirf naam jaan lo.
- **Kitna current:** zyada tez halchal, zyada pher aur zyada tagda magnet, to zyada induced current. (Iska formula aage ki class mein; abhi sirf ye teen factor yaad rakho.)

**Closed coil** isliye likha hai ki induced EMF to coil ke khule hone par bhi banta hai, par current tabhi behta hai jab circuit band ho.

Ab Sahana ke sawaal ka jawab dhoondhte hain. Mr. Pawar ne dynamo ka dabba khola.

```
       dynamo (cycle ka)

   pahiye se judi         coil (sthir)
   chhoti magnet          ((((((((((
   [N═S] ghoomti hai ──>   ((((((((((  ───> light
```

> **Figure 6.2** – Cycle ki dynamo ka saar: pahiya ghumata hai to ek chhoti magnet coil ke paas ghoomti hai, coil mein current bante hain aur light jalti hai.

Dabbe ke andar ek **chhoti si magnet** aur ek **coil** thi. Jab pahiya ghoomta hai to tyre se laga hissa magnet ko coil ke paas ghumata hai. Magnet ghoomti hai to coil ke aas paas ka magnetic field **lagatar badalta rehta hai**, yaani relative motion hai. Isliye coil mein induced current bante hain, jo light ke bulb se guzar kar use jalate hain.

| Tez pedal par light zyada chamki | Magnet tez ghoomi, field tezi se badla, induced current zyada |

Sahana ne kaha, "Uncle, matlab jab tak main pedal maarti hoon, mere pair ki mehnat bijli ban jaati hai!" Mr. Pawar ne sar hilaya. "Bilkul. Ye bijli bani nahi hai kahin se: tumhari mehnat (mechanical energy) ne use banaya hai."

## Part C: AC generator

### Dynamo ka bada bhai

Dynamo ek chhota **generator** hai. Bijli-ghar ke bade generators bhi isi idea par chalte hain. **Generator wo machine hai jo mechanical energy (ghoomne wali energy) ko electrical energy mein badalti hai.** Ye bijli **banati nahi**, sirf energy ka roop badalti hai.

### Simple AC generator (thoda extra, Class 10 overlap)

Ek **rectangular coil** ko ek **strong magnet ke N aur S poles ke beech** ghumao. Coil ghoomti hai to uske aas paas ka field lagatar badalta rehta hai, aur coil mein induced current bante hain.

```
                  brush           brush
                  ┌─┐             ┌─┐
   external ──────┘ │             │ └────── external
   circuit    (bulb)└─ ring 1   ring 2 ─┘   circuit
                      ║           ║
        ┌─────────────╬═══ axle ══╬─────────────┐
        │             ║           ║             │
   [N]  │     ┌───────╨───────────╨───────┐     │  [S]
   [N]  │     │    rectangular coil       │ ←→  │  [S]
   [N]  │     └───────────────────────────┘     │  [S]
        └───────────────────────────────────────┘
          magnet ke poles ke beech coil ghoomti hai
```

> **Figure 6.3** – AC generator ka saaf sa chitra: N aur S poles ke beech rectangular coil axle par ghoomti hai; coil ke sire do slip rings se jude hain, aur do brushes ring ko chhookar current ko bahar ke circuit (bulb) tak le jaate hain. Axle ko paani, bhaap ya hawa ghumati hai.

Chitra ke hisse:

- **Coil (rotor):** ghoomne wali rectangular coil.
- **Magnet:** strong magnet ke do poles, jinke beech coil ghoomti hai.
- **Axle:** wo dandi jo coil ko ghumati hai.
- **Slip rings:** do ring jo coil ke saath ghoomte hain. Har ring coil ke ek sire se juda hai.
- **Brushes:** do sthir (na ghoomne wale) sampark jo rings ko chhoote rehte hain aur current ko bahar ke circuit tak le jaate hain.

### Current ka behna: AC kyun?

Jaise jaise coil ghoomti hai, wo kabhi field ko ek taraf se kaatti hai aur kabhi doosri taraf se. Isliye induced current ki disha **badalti rehti hai**. Coil ke **har ek poore chakkar mein current apni disha 2 baar ulti karta hai**. Aisa current **alternating current (AC)** kehlata hai. Ye wahi hai jo hamare ghar ke sockets mein aata hai.

**India mein ghar ki bijli 50 Hz AC hai.** 50 Hz ka matlab **1 second mein 50 cycle**. Ek cycle mein current ek baar ek disha mein aur ek baar ulti disha mein behta hai. Yaani 1 second mein current **100 baar** disha badalta hai (aage solved example mein hisaab hai). Ye tezi itni hai ki bulb mein hume jhilmil nahi dikhti.

### DC generator (sirf naam)

Agar slip rings ki jagah **split-ring commutator** (ek ring jo do aadhe hisson mein kati ho) lagaaye jaayein, to bahar ke circuit mein current ek hi disha mein jaata hai: **DC generator**. Iski bareeki aage ki class mein; abhi sirf naam yaad rakho.

### Axle ko ghumata kaun hai?

Generator ko ghumane ke liye kisi ko dhakka dena padta hai. Bijli-ghar mein ye kaam alag alag tareeke se hota hai:

| Bijli-ghar | Axle kisse ghumta hai |
|---|---|
| Hydro (paani, dam) | Oonchai se girta paani turbine ko ghumata hai |
| Thermal aur nuclear | Koyle ya nuclear fuel se bhaap banti hai, bhaap turbine ghumati hai |
| Wind (hawa ki chakki) | Hawa blades ko ghumati hai |

Is energy transformation chain ka pehla hissa humne [work-energy-and-power-part3.md](../work-energy-and-power/work-energy-and-power-part3.md) mein dekha tha. Dam par chain ye hai: **paani (oonchai par, potential energy) → girta paani (kinetic energy) → turbine ghoomta hai → generator → electrical energy**. Hand-cranked radio aur hand-crank wali torch mein bhi chhota generator hota hai: haath se handle ghumao to bijli banti hai.

## Part D: Electric motor: ulta kaam

### Current wale taar par force

Ab ek ulta sawaal: generator mein ghumne se bijli bani. To kya bijli se ghumna bhi mil sakta hai? Haan! Ye hi **electric motor** hai.

**Observation:** ek taar ko horseshoe magnet ke poles ke beech latka do aur usmein current chalao. Taar **hilta hai (jhoolta hai)**. Yaani **magnetic field mein rakhe current wale taar par force lagta hai.** (Topic 4 mein humne is force ka sirf naam liya tha. Ab dekhte hain.)

```
          [N]  (upar wala pole)
           :
           :  field upar se neeche (N se S)
           v
   ←────── ⊗     taar: current page ke andar (⊗)
   force   :     taar par force: left (←), field aur current dono se 90° par
           v
          [S]  (neeche wala pole)
```

> **Figure 6.4** – Magnet ke poles ke beech ka current wala taar: field, current aur force (taar ki chaal) teeno ek doosre se 90° (right angle) par hain.

Teen cheezein ek doosre se **90° par** hoti hain: **field ki disha, current ki disha, aur force (chaal) ki disha.**

### Fleming ka left-hand rule (thoda extra: Class 10 overlap)

Force ki disha nikalne ka aasaan tareeka hai. **Apne left hand ka angootha (thumb), tarjani (forefinger) aur beech ki ungli (middle finger) ek doosre se 90° par failao:**

- **Forefinger** = **field** ki disha (N se S)
- **Middle finger** = **current** ki disha
- **Thumb** = **force (chaal)** ki disha

Dhyan do: ye **left hand** hai. Topic 4 ke rules (thumb rule) right hand ke the, aur wo field ki disha batate the. Ye wala left hand ka niyam **force** batata hai.

Convention (sirf disha batane ke liye): page par **right = →, left = ←, up = ↑, down = ↓**, aur page ke andar jaata current (ya field) **⊗**, page se bahar aata **⊙** (Topic 4 wale nishaan).

### Ulta karne par

Agar **current ulta** kar do, to force ulta ho jaata hai. Agar **field ulta** kar do (magnet palat do), to bhi force ulta ho jaata hai. Agar dono ulta kar do, to force pehle jaisa hi rehta hai.

### DC motor (thoda extra, Class 10 overlap)

Ek rectangular coil ko magnet ke poles ke beech rakho aur usmein current bhejo. Coil ki **do aamne-saamne ki bhujaon** mein current **ulti dishaon** mein behta hai. Isliye un par force bhi **ulti dishaon** mein lagta hai: ek bhuja upar, doosri neeche. Isse coil ghoomne lagti hai.

```
                                     ↑ force upar
     [N]      ⊗ ═══════ coil ═══════ ⊙      [S]
              ↓ force neeche
          field: N se S ki taraf (→ → → → → → →)

   (coil ko side se dekha hai: ⊗ wali bhuja mein current page ke andar,
    ⊙ wali bhuja mein page se bahar)
   coil ke sire ── split ring (commutator) ── brushes ── battery
```

> **Figure 6.5** – DC motor ka saar: coil ki do bhujaon par ulte force lagte hain (⊗ wali par neeche, ⊙ wali par upar) to coil ghoomti hai; split ring commutator har aadhe chakkar ke baad coil mein current ki disha ulti kar deta hai.

Coil aadha chakkar ghoom jaaye to bina kuch kiye coil ki bhujaon par force ulte ho jaate (aur coil wapas ruk jaati). Isliye **split-ring commutator** lagaya jaata hai: wo har aadhe chakkar par coil ke current ki disha ulti kar deta hai, taaki coil **hamesha ek hi disha mein ghoomti rahe**. (Iski design ki bareeki aage ki class mein.)

**Motor ki speed** badhti hai jab current zyada ho, field zyada tagda ho, ya coil mein pher zyada ho (sirf naam; formula nahi).

### Motor ke uses

Pankha, mixer-grinder, washing machine, paani ka pump, electric gaadiyaan (EV), khilone, drill machine, sab mein motor hota hai. Aas paas ghar mein dekho: lagbhag har ghoomne wali machine mein motor hai.

### Motor aur generator mein farak

| Baat | Motor | Generator |
|---|---|---|
| Energy conversion | Electrical → mechanical | Mechanical → electrical |
| Input | Electric current | Ghoomna (mechanical energy) |
| Output | Ghoomna | Electric current |
| Niyam | Fleming ka left-hand rule (force ki disha) | Fleming ka right-hand rule (induced current ki disha, sirf naam) |
| Example | Pankha | Dynamo, bijli-ghar |

Dono ek doosre ke **ulte** hain, aur dono mein coil, magnet aur ghoomna hota hai. Par dono ek hi machine nahi hain.

### EMI ke aur kaam (sirf naam)

- **Transformer:** AC ka voltage badalta hai (badhata: step-up, ghatata: step-down). Aapke phone charger mein 230 V ko ghatakar lagbhag 5 V banane wala hissa ([electricity-part3.md](../electricity/electricity-part3.md)) isi idea par chalta hai. Iski bareeki aage ki class mein.
- **Induction cooker:** badalta field bartan mein current banakar use garam karta hai.
- **Metal detector:** dhaatu aane par coil ka field badalta hai, aur beep karta hai.
- **Wireless charger:** coil se coil mein bina taar ke energy jaati hai.
- **Credit/debit card reader:** card ki magnetic patti reader ki coil ke aage se guzarti hai to coil mein chhota current banta hai.

### Example 1: Activity table: needle ka behaviour

### Example 2: AC ka frequency, period aur disha badalna

Ghar ki bijli 50 Hz hai. Ek cycle ka time (period) kitna? Current ek second mein kitni baar disha badalta hai? Ek minute mein?

Period T = 1 ÷ frequency = 1 ÷ 50 Hz = **0.02 s**

Ek cycle mein current 2 baar disha badalta hai, to 1 second mein 50 × 2 = **100 baar**.

1 minute = 60 s, to 1 minute mein 100 × 60 = **6,000 baar**.

Jaanch: 0.02 s × 50 = 1 s ✅; 100 × 60 = 6,000 ✅

### Example 3: Generator ki rpm

Ek generator ki coil ek poore chakkar mein ek cycle banati hai. 50 Hz AC ke liye coil ko kitne rotations per minute (rpm) par ghumna hoga?

Coil ko 1 second mein 50 chakkar lagane hain.

1 minute (60 s) mein chakkar = 50 × 60 = **3,000 rpm**

Jaanch: 3,000 ÷ 60 = 50 chakkar per second ✅

### Example 4: Fleming ke left-hand rule se force ki disha

Convention: page par right (→), left (←), up (↑), down (↓); page ke andar ⊗, page se bahar ⊙. Har case mein left hand ki teeno ungliyan ek doosre se 90° par rakho.

**(a)** Field right (→), current page ke andar (⊗).
Left hand se: forefinger right ko, middle finger page ke andar ko; thumb **neeche** ko jaata hai.
**Force: neeche (↓).** (Yahi Figure 6.5 ki ⊗ wali bhuja hai.)

**(b)** Field page ke andar (⊗), current right (→).
Forefinger page ke andar, middle finger right ko; thumb **upar** aata hai.
**Force: upar (↑).**

**(c)** Field page ke andar (⊗), current upar (↑).
Forefinger page ke andar, middle finger upar ko; thumb **left** ko jaata hai.
**Force: left (←).**

**(d)** Field page se bahar (⊙), current right (→).
Forefinger page se bahar (apni taraf), middle finger right ko; thumb **neeche** jaata hai.
**Force: neeche (↓).**

Jaanch: Case (b) aur (d) ko compare karo: current wahi (right) hai, par field ulta hai (andar se bahar), aur force bhi ulta ho gaya (upar se neeche) ✅. Yahi "ek cheez ulti, to force ulta" wala niyam hai. Aur har case mein force, field aur current dono se 90° par hai ✅.

### Example 5: Motor ki efficiency

Ek motor 100 W bijli leta hai aur 80 W ki mechanical power deta hai. Efficiency?

Efficiency = (output ÷ input) × 100 = (80 W ÷ 100 W) × 100 = **80%**

Bachi hui 20 W garmi aur awaaz mein jaati hai (Machines chapter ki efficiency wali baat: [machines-part2.md](../machines/machines-part2.md)).

Jaanch: 80% of 100 W = 80 W ✅

### Example 6: Dam se bijli

Ek dam mein 100 kg paani 50 m neeche girta hai. g = 10 m/s² lo. Paani ki energy kitni? Agar usmein se 80% electrical energy ban jaaye to kitni bijli bani?

Energy = m g h = 100 kg × 10 m/s² × 50 m = **50,000 J**

Electrical energy = 80% of 50,000 J = 0.8 × 50,000 = **40,000 J**

Jaanch: 100 kg × 10 m/s² = 1,000 N aur 1,000 N × 50 m = 50,000 J ✅; 40,000 J ÷ 50,000 J = 0.8 ✅. Bachi 10,000 J garmi aur awaaz mein gayi.

### Example 7: Dynamo lamp ka glow: sochkar batao

(a) Cycle khadi hai aur pahiya ruka hua hai: light kyun nahi jalti? Pahiya ruka, to magnet ruki, field badalna band, to induced current nahi.
(b) Sahana zyada tez pedal maare: light zyada kyun chamakti? Magnet tez ghoomti hai, field tezi se badalta hai, induced current zyada.
(c) Light jalane wali energy kahan se aayi? Sahana ki mehnat se: mechanical energy hi electrical energy mein badli.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Magnet coil ke andar rakha ho to current banega."**
Nahi. Sthir magnet se koi current nahi. Field **badalna** chahiye (relative motion), tabhi current banta hai.

**Galti 2: "Coil ko sthir magnet ke paas rakhne se current aayega."**
Nahi. Dono sthir hain, to kuch nahi badal raha, current nahi.

**Galti 3: Left aur right hand ke rules mix karna.**
Fleming ka **left-hand rule** current wale taar par force (motor) batata hai. Topic 4 ke right-hand rules field ki disha batate hain, aur generator ke current ki disha ke liye Fleming ka right-hand rule hai (sirf naam).

**Galti 4: "Generator bijli banata hai."**
Generator energy **banata nahi**: mechanical energy ko electrical energy mein badalta hai. Sahana ki mehnat hi bijli banti hai. (Energy ka conservation aapko Work, Energy aur Power chapter mein mil chuka hai.)

**Galti 5: "AC aur DC ek hi hain."**
DC hamesha ek hi disha mein behta hai. AC baar baar disha badalta hai (50 Hz AC mein 1 second mein 100 baar).

**Galti 6: "Motor aur generator ek hi machine hain, ek hi kaam karti hain."**
Dono ke energy conversion **ulte** hain: motor electrical → mechanical, generator mechanical → electrical.

**Galti 7: "Tez pedal sirf voltage badhata hai, ya sirf frequency."**
Tez pedal karne par field tezi se badalta hai, to induced EMF zyada banta hai aur AC ka frequency bhi zyada hota hai. Light ki chamak in dono par nirbhar karti hai. (Abhi sirf itna samajhna kaafi hai ki tez pedal, zyada chamak.)

## Quick Recap

- **Electromagnetic induction:** closed coil ke aas paas magnetic field badalne (magnet aur coil ke beech relative motion) par coil mein current paida hota hai. Khoj Michael Faraday ki (1831, kaha jaata hai).
- Sthir magnet se current nahi. Halchal ulti to current ulta (Lenz ka niyam: sirf naam).
- Zyada current: tez halchal, zyada pher, zyada tagda magnet.
- **Dynamo/generator:** mechanical energy → electrical energy. Coil poles ke beech ghoomti hai; slip rings aur brushes current ko bahar le jaate hain.
- Ghumne wali coil se **AC** banta hai: har chakkar mein current 2 baar ulta. India ki bijli 50 Hz: 1 second mein 100 baar disha badalna.
- Generator ko paani, bhaap ya hawa ghumati hai.
- **Motor:** electrical → mechanical. Magnetic field mein current wale taar par force. **Fleming ka left-hand rule** (Class 10 overlap): forefinger field, middle finger current, thumb force.
- Current ya field ulta to force ulta. Commutator coil ko ek hi disha mein ghumata rakhta hai.
- Transformer, induction cooker, metal detector, wireless charger, card reader EMI par chalte hain (sirf naam).
- Generators aur transformers ko kabhi kholna nahi; pacemaker ke paas magnet nahi.

   (b) Field upward, current to the right.
   (c) Field out of the page, current upward.
   (d) Field into the page, current to the left.

    (a) A generator creates energy.
    (b) A motor and a generator convert energy in opposite directions.

    (d) Moving a coil towards a still magnet can induce a current.

12. For each of these, name the principle used: electric bell, transformer, mixer-grinder, hydro power station, scrap-yard crane, metal detector.

### Answers

1. **Nahi**. Magnet coil ke andar sthir hai, to magnetic field badal nahi raha. Current tabhi banta hai jab field badal raha ho (relative motion ho).

2. (i) Magnet ko zyada tezi se hilana. (ii) Coil mein pher (turns) badhana. (iii) Zyada tagda magnet lena.

3. (a) Needle **left** ki taraf hilegi (ulti halchal, ulti disha). (b) Needle **zyada** hilegi. (c) Needle **right** ki taraf hilegi, jaise magnet dhakelne par hilti thi, kyunki sirf relative motion matter karti hai.

4. Pedal marne par pahiya dynamo ki magnet ko ghumata hai. Magnet ke ghoomne se coil ke aas paas ka field badalta hai, to induced current bana aur light jali. Pedal band to magnet ruki, field badalna band, current band, light band. Tez pedal par field tezi se badalta hai, current zyada, light zyada chamkili.

5. T = 1 ÷ 50 Hz = **0.02 s**. Ek cycle mein 2 baar, to 1 second mein 50 × 2 = **100 baar**. 1 minute mein 100 × 60 = **6,000 baar**.
   Jaanch: 0.02 s × 50 = 1 s ✅

6. 50 chakkar per second × 60 = **3,000 rpm**.
   Jaanch: 3,000 ÷ 60 = 50 ✅

7. Efficiency = (300 W ÷ 400 W) × 100 = **75%**. Bachi 100 W garmi aur awaaz mein jaati hai.
   Jaanch: 0.75 × 400 W = 300 W ✅

8. (a) Forefinger left, middle finger page ke andar: thumb **upar (↑)**. (Example 4(a) se field ulta, to force bhi ulta.)
   (b) Forefinger upar, middle finger right: thumb **page se bahar (⊙)**.
   (c) Forefinger page se bahar, middle finger upar: thumb **right (→)**.
   (d) Forefinger page ke andar, middle finger left: thumb **neeche (↓)**. (Example 4(b) se current ulta, to force bhi ulta.)
   Jaanch: har case mein force, field aur current dono se 90° par hai ✅

9. Energy = m g h = 200 kg × 10 m/s² × 30 m = 60,000 J. Electrical = 50% of 60,000 J = **30,000 J**.
   Jaanch: 200 kg × 10 m/s² = 2,000 N aur 2,000 N × 30 m = 60,000 J ✅; 60,000 J ÷ 2 = 30,000 J ✅

10. (a) **False** ❌. Generator mechanical energy ko electrical energy mein badalta hai, energy banata nahi.

    (c) **False** ❌. AC baar baar disha badalta hai (50 Hz mein 1 second mein 100 baar).
    (d) **True** ✅. Sirf relative motion matter karti hai, to coil ko hilane par bhi current banta hai.

11. (i) AC generator mechanical energy ko electrical energy mein badalta hai, DC motor electrical ko mechanical mein. (ii) Generator mein slip rings (bahar ke circuit mein AC), motor mein split-ring commutator (coil ko ek hi disha mein ghumata rakhne ke liye). (Aur generator right-hand rule ke saath jaata hai, motor left-hand rule ke saath.)
