# Topic 1: Magnet aur Magnetic Poles

Pichhle chapter mein humne electric current ko samjha: tar mein bahte charge, bulb ka jalna, heater ka garm hona. Ab Physics ke is aakhri chapter mein ek aisi cheez aati hai jo pehli nazar mein bijli se bilkul alag lagti hai, par asal mein uski sagi saheli hai: **magnet (chumbak)**. Aage ke topics mein dekhenge ki current magnet jaisa asar dikha sakta hai, aur magnet current bana sakta hai. Par pehle is topic mein sirf magnet ko achhe se jaan lete hain: wo kya hai, kya khinchta hai, aur uske do "chhor" (poles) kya karte hain.

Ek chhoti si baat shuru mein hi: **magnets ke saath khelna maze ka hai, par ye ek general caution yaad rakho.** Magnet ko mobile phone, credit/debit card, ghadi, TV ya pacemaker (dil ki dhadkan ko niyantrit karne wali chhoti device) ke paas mat rakho. Is topic ke sab experiments chhote, safe magnets aur iron ke chhote tukdon se hain. Bijli ke plug ya socket se koi experiment nahi karna hai.

## Kabaadi ke yard mein jaadu

Mr. Barua ka kabaadi (scrap) yard shahar ke kinare par tha. Ek Sunday Lavanya apne papa ke saath wahan gayi, kyunki use purani cheezein dekhna bahut pasand tha. Wahan ek bada crane tha jiske hook se ek rassi se **ek bahut bada gol magnet** latka hua tha.

Mr. Barua ne crane chalaya. Magnet dheere-dheere kabaadi ke dher par utra. Dher mein lohe ki keelein, bolt, purane tin ke dabbe, taambe ka tar, aluminium ke can, plastic ki botlein aur kaanch ke tukde, sab mile-jule pade the. Magnet ne dher ko chhua, aur "tak-tak-tak" karte hue **keelein, bolt aur tin ke dabbe uchhal kar magnet se chipak gaye**. Magnet upar utha to wo sab uske saath upar aa gaye. Par **taambe ka tar, aluminium ke can, plastic aur kaanch dher mein hi pade rahe**, jaise magnet ne unhe dekha hi nahi.

Lavanya aankhein phaad kar boli, "Barua uncle, ye magnet kaise jaanta hai ki kaun si cheez uthani hai aur kaun si chhodni hai? Ye to sab kuch kyun nahi utha leta? Kya ye dekh kar chunta hai?"

Mr. Barua muskuraye, "Dekh kar nahi, beta. Ye cheezon ke **material** se chunta hai. Chalo, pehle magnet ke baare mein thoda jaan lo, phir main tumhe iska raaz bataunga." Is kahani ko hum topic ke beech mein solve karenge.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Magnet (chumbak)** | Wo cheez jo iron jaisi cheezon ko apni taraf khinchti hai | Bar magnet, fridge magnet |
| **Magnetic material** | Wo material jo magnet se khinchta hai | Iron, steel, nickel, cobalt |
| **Non-magnetic material** | Wo material jo magnet se (aam taur par) nahi khinchta | Copper, plastic, glass, wood |
| **Pole (dhruv)** | Magnet ke wo do hisse (sire ke paas) jahan khinchav sabse zyada hota hai | Bar magnet ke N aur S sire |
| **North pole (N)** | Wo sira jo azaadi se latakne par uttar (north) ki taraf rukta hai (north-seeking pole) | Bar magnet ka N sira |
| **South pole (S)** | Wo sira jo dakshin (south) ki taraf rukta hai (south-seeking pole) | Bar magnet ka S sira |
| **Directive property** | Azaadi se latakte magnet ka lagbhag north-south mein rukna | Dhaage se latka bar magnet |
| **Attraction aur repulsion** | Khinchav (paas aana) aur dhakka (door jaana) | Unlike poles khinchte hain, like poles dhakelte hain |
| **Keeper** | Magnet ke siron par rakha naram lohe ka tukda, jo magnet ko kamzor hone se bachata hai | Bar magnet ke pair ke siron par iron ki patti |

## Part A: Magnet ki chhoti si kahani, aur uske kisam

### Itihaas do line mein

Kaha jaata hai ki bahut purane zamane mein Magnes naam ka ek charwaha (shepherd) tha. Ek din pahaad par chalte hue uske joote ki lohe ki keelein aur lathi ka lohe ka sira ek khaas patthar se chipakne lage. Isi patthar se **magnet** shabd aaya, aisa maana jaata hai. Ye bhi kaha jaata hai ki Chin aur Bharat mein purane samay mein log isi patthar ka tukda (jise **lodestone** kehte hain) latka kar disha pata karte the, yaani wo pehla **compass** tha.

### Natural magnet aur artificial magnet

- **Natural magnet:** prakriti mein mila hua patthar jo khud magnet hai. Isse **lodestone** kehte hain. Ye **magnetite** naam ke iron ke ore (khanij) ka ek roop hai. Isse hi purane zamane ke log pehchaante the.
- **Artificial magnet (banaya hua magnet):** jo hum insaan **iron, steel, nickel, cobalt** jaise materials aur unke mishran (alloys) se banate hain. Aaj ke zyadatar magnets artificial hain. Ek achha alloy hai **alnico** (aluminium + nickel + cobalt, aur thoda iron). Ye bahut strong magnet banata hai. Ye alloys kaise magnet bante hain, wo Topic 3 mein.

### Magnets ke aakaar (shapes)

Zarurat ke hisaab se magnets alag-alag shape mein banaye jaate hain:

```
 1. Bar magnet (sidhi chhad)  :   [ N ══════════════ S ]

 2. Horseshoe magnet (naal)   :      ╭═════════╮
                                    ═╯         ╰═
                                    N           S

 3. U-shaped magnet           :     N           S
                                    ║           ║
                                    ║           ║
                                    ╚═══════════╝

 4. Cylindrical magnet        :   [ N ═══ S ]   (moti, chhoti belan)

 5. Disc magnet (chakti)      :   (  N face  )  ek taraf N, doosri taraf S

 6. Ring magnet (chhalla)     :   ( ◯ )  beech mein chhed, ek face N, doosri S

 7. Magnetic needle (compass) :   ◄ N ═══ S ►   (patli nukili suyi)

 8. Fridge magnet (rubber)    :   [▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒]  patli, mudne wali patti
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Magnets ke kuch aam aakaar. Har magnet ke N aur S do sire (poles) hote hain.

- **Bar magnet:** sabse aam, school lab mein dikhta hai.
- **Horseshoe aur U-shaped:** dono ke sire paas-paas hote hain, to dono poles ek hi cheez ko ek saath utha sakte hain, isliye strong khinchav milta hai.
- **Cylindrical, disc, ring:** khilone, speaker aur machines mein kaam aate hain.
- **Magnetic needle (compass ki suyi):** ek chhota, halka magnet, jo beech mein tika hota hai taaki aaram se ghoom sake.
- **Fridge magnet:** rubber mein magnet ka powder milakar bani **flexible (mudne wali) patti**. Ye fridge ke steel ke darwaze par chipak jaati hai.

## Part B: Kaun khinchta hai, kaun nahi?

### Magnetic aur non-magnetic materials

| Kisam | Materials | Magnet se kya hota hai? |
|---|---|---|
| **Magnetic materials** | Iron (lohe), steel, nickel, cobalt | Khinchte hain (tez khinchav) |
| **Non-magnetic materials** | Copper (taamba), aluminium, brass (peetal), plastic, glass (kaanch), wood (lakdi), paper, rubber | Aam taur par nahi khinchte |

Ek chhoti si baat: **aluminium** jaisi kuch cheezein magnet se **bahut kamzor** (bahut hi zyada kamzor) asar dikhati hain, itna ki hum roz ke kaam mein unhe non-magnetic hi maan lete hain. Ek aur note: kuch khaas kism ke stainless steel ke bartan magnet se kam ya bilkul nahi chipakte. Hum yahan **aam steel** (jaise paper clip, aur tin ke dabbe) ki baat kar rahe hain.

### Kahani solve karte hain

Ab Lavanya ka sawal. Mr. Barua ne samjhaya, "Magnet ko cheezein dekh kar chunna nahi aata. Wo sirf **magnetic materials** ko khinchta hai. Meri dher mein **keelein aur bolt iron ke** hain. **Tin ke dabbe** asal mein andar se steel ke hote hain jis par tin ki patli parat chadhi hoti hai. Isliye wo bhi khinch gaye. Par **taamba, aluminium, plastic aur kaanch** non-magnetic hain. Magnet unke liye 'andha' hai."

Lavanya boli, "Matlab magnet ne kuch chuna nahi: iron-steel wali cheezein khud chipak gayi!" Barua uncle haske bole, "Bilkul. Isi se kabaadi ki sorting aasaan ho jaati hai: ek magnet se iron-steel alag, baaki alag."

## Part C: Magnet ke gun (properties)

### Gun 1: Magnetic materials ko khinchta hai

Ye aap dekh chuke ho. Magnet iron, steel, nickel aur cobalt ko apni taraf khinchta hai.

### Gun 2: Khinchav sabse zyada siron par hota hai (poles)

**Experiment 1 (iron filings).** Ek mez par bar magnet rakho. Uske upar iron filings (lohe ka bhura chura, jo lab mein milta hai) chhidko. Dekhoge ki zyadatar filings **do siron ke paas** chipak jaati hain, aur beech ke hisse mein bahut kam.

```
   filings     ░▒▓▓▓▒░                           ░▒▓▓▓▒░    filings
              ░▓▓▓▓▓▓░  [ N ═══════════ S ]    ░▓▓▓▓▓▓░
   (zyada)     ░▒▓▓▓▒░     beech mein kam         ░▒▓▓▓▒░     (zyada)
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Bar magnet par filings: siron (poles) ke paas bahut, beech mein bahut kam.

Magnet ke wo do hisse jahan khinchav sabse zyada hota hai, **poles** kehlate hain. Dhyan rahe: poles bilkul aakhri point par nahi, **siron ke paas, thoda andar ke hisse mein** hote hain. Beech ke hisse mein asar bahut kam hota hai.

### Gun 3: Azaad latakne par north-south rukta hai (directive property)

**Experiment 2.** Ek bar magnet ko beech se dhaage se is tarah latkao ki wo kisi cheez se na chhue aur aaram se ghoom sake. (Ya use cork ke tukde par paani ke bartan mein tairao.) Thodi der ghoomne ke baad wo **lagbhag north-south disha** mein rukega. Baar-baar ghumao, har baar wahi disha.

```
               Uttar (North)
                    ▲
                    N
                    ║
   dhaaga ────────► ║   latka hua bar magnet
                    ║
                    S
                    ▼
               Dakshin (South)
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Azaadi se latka bar magnet: ek sira uttar ki taraf, doosra dakshin ki taraf rukta hai.

- Jo sira **uttar (north)** ki taraf rukta hai, use **north-seeking pole (N)** kehte hain.
- Jo sira **dakshin (south)** ki taraf rukta hai, use **south-seeking pole (S)** kehte hain.

Magnet ke is "dishaa bataane" ke gun ko **directive property** kehte hain. Isi par compass kaam karta hai. Poles hamesha **jode (pairs) mein** hote hain: ek N, ek S.

### Gun 4: Samaan poles dhakelte hain, alag poles khinchte hain

**Experiment 3.** Do bar magnets lo. Ek ko dhaage se latka do (ruk jane do). Doosre ko haath mein pakad kar uske sire ko latke magnet ke sire ke paas lao.

```
  Alag poles (N-S): KHINCHAV (attract)    [ N ══════ S ]   [ N ══════ S ]
                                                       ◄──►
                                                  S aur N paas aate hain

  Samaan poles (N-N): DHAKKA (repel)      [ S ══════ N ]   [ N ══════ S ]
                                                       ◄──►  dono door bhaagte hain
```

> **Figure 1.4** – Unlike poles khinchte hain, like poles dhakelte hain.

**Niyam (rule):**

> **Like poles repel (N-N, S-S dhakelte hain). Unlike poles attract (N-S khinchte hain).**

### Magnet ki pakki pehchaan: repulsion hi sahi test hai

Sochiye, ek chhad ka sira magnet ke N sire ko **khinch** raha hai. Kya wo chhad zarur magnet hai? **Nahi.** Kyunki magnet to **saada iron ke tukde ko bhi khinchta hai** (iron magnetic material hai). To sirf khinchav dekh kar hum nahi keh sakte ki wo chhad magnet hai.

Par agar kisi chhad ka sira magnet ke sire ko **dhakel** raha hai, to wo **pakka magnet hai**. Kyunki iron ka saada tukda kabhi dhakelta nahi, wo sirf khinchta hai.

> **Pakka test: repulsion (dhakka).** Khinchav dono ho sakta hai (magnet ya iron), dhakka sirf magnet se.

### Gun 5: Poles alag nahi ho sakte

**Experiment 4 (soch kar dekho).** Ek bar magnet ko beech se tod do. Aap sochoge: ek tukde mein sirf N aur doosre mein sirf S. Par **aisa nahi hota.** Har tukda **poora magnet** ban jaata hai, apne **N aur S dono ke saath**. Phir se todo, to chhote tukde bhi poore magnet. Chhoti se chhoti tukri tak yahi hota hai.

```
 Poora magnet :   [ N ══════════════════ S ]

 Ek bar toota :   [ N ═════ S ] [ N ═════ S ]

 Teen bar toota (chaar tukde):
                  [ N ══ S ] [ N ══ S ] [ N ══ S ] [ N ══ S ]
```

> **Figure 1.5** – Magnet ko jitna bhi todo, har tukde mein N aur S dono bante hain.

Kisi ek akele pole (sirf N ya sirf S) ko **magnetic monopole** kehte hain. Aaj tak pakka koi monopole nahi mila. Isiliye hum kehte hain: **magnet ke poles hamesha jode mein hote hain.**

### Gun 6: Bina chhue bhi asar (non-contact force), aur doori badhne par kamzor

Magnet iron ko **chhue bina** bhi khinch leta hai. Ye ek **non-contact force** hai (jaise gravity, jo bina chhue kheenchti hai; dekho [force-and-laws-of-motion.md](../force-and-laws-of-motion/force-and-laws-of-motion.md) ke contact aur non-contact force). Ye force hawa, kaagaz, lakdi aur kaanch ke aar-paar bhi kaam karta hai.

**Experiment 5 (paper clip aur kaagaz).** Ek kaagaz par paper clip rakho. Kaagaz ke neeche se magnet ghumao. Paper clip ghoomta hai, jaise koi bhoot use dhakel raha ho.

```
         ┌─┐
         └─┘   ◄── paper clip (kaagaz ke upar)
   ─────────────────────  kaagaz ki sheet
       [ N ═══ S ]   ◄── magnet (kaagaz ke neeche)
```

> **Figure 1.6** – Magnet kaagaz ke aar-paar paper clip ko khinch leta hai: chhue bina.

Ab kaagaz ki sheets badhate jao (1, 2, 3, ... sheet). Jaise-jaise magnet aur paper clip ke beech ki doori badhti hai, **khinchav kamzor** hota jaata hai, aur ek doori ke baad clip nahi khinchta. Yaani **magnetic force doori badhne par ghat jaata hai.**

### Compass aur poles (chhoti si jhalak)

Compass ki suyi bhi ek chhota magnet hai. Uska **N sira** bar magnet ke **S pole** ki taraf khinchta hai, aur **N pole** se dhakelta hai (unlike attract, like repel). Is idea ko Topic 2 mein aur gehraai se dekhenge.

### Magnet ki taakat: kitni pins uthayi?

Magnet kitna taakatwar hai, ise **uthayi gayi pins ya paper clips ki ginti** se napa ja sakta hai. Jo magnet jyada pins uthaye, utna zyada taakatwar (ya us jagah par utna zyada khinchav).

**Experiment 6.** Ek bar magnet ke alag-alag jagah par pins ka dher laga kar dekho kitni pins chipakti hain. Jaise: **N sire par 12 pins, S sire par 11 pins, beech mein sirf 2 pins.** Matlab magnet sabse taakatwar **poles (siron)** par hai, aur beech mein sabse kamzor.

```
 Bar magnet par pins (har # = 1 pin)

 N sira (0 cm)   : ############ 12
 beech (6 cm)    : ##  2
 S sira (12 cm)  : ########### 11
```

> **Figure 1.7** – Uthayi gayi pins ki ginti dikhati hai ki khinchav poles par zyada, beech mein kam.

Siron par kul 12 + 11 = 23 pins uthi, jabki beech mein 2. Yaani N sire par beech se 12 ÷ 2 = 6 guna zyada pins chipki.

### Magnets ke kaam (uses)

- **Fridge ka darwaza:** darwaze mein rubber magnet ki patti usse band rakhti hai.
- **Compass:** disha pata karne ke liye (jahaz, trekking).
- **Speaker aur microphone:** inme magnet awaaz banane/pakadne mein madad karta hai.
- **Electric motor aur generator:** inme magnet zaroori hain (Topic 6).
- **Magnetic stripe aur lock:** ATM/credit card ki patti, aur kuch tarah ke lock.
- **Almari ka door latch:** chhota magnet darwaza band rakhta hai.
- **Khilone:** magnetic building blocks, fishing games.
- **Magnetic separator (industry):** kabaadi, anaaj, sand mein se iron alag karne ke liye. Mr. Barua ka crane bhi yahi karta hai.
- **Recycling:** steel ke dabbe aur plastic/aluminium ko alag karne ke liye.
- **MRI machine:** hospital mein shareer ke andar ki tasveer lene wali machine; ismein bahut strong magnet hote hain.
- **Magnetic levitation (maglev) train:** magnets ki madad se patri se thodi upar uthi hui chalti hai.
- **Hard disk:** computer ke purane tarah ke storage mein magnetism se data likha jaata hai.

### Magnets ki dekhbhaal

- Magnets ko **jodon mein** rakho: **N ke paas S** (opposite poles saath-saath), aur siron par **naram lohe ka tukda (keeper)** laga do.
- **Garam mat karo, hathoda mat maaro, gira mat do.** Isse magnet kamzor ho sakta hai.
- **Phone, credit card, ghadi, TV aur pacemaker** se door rakho.

```
 Do bar magnets ka pair (alag poles saath):

   ┌──────────────────────────────────┐
   │  [ N ═════════ S ]               │  ◄── keeper (naram iron), dono siron par
   │  [ S ═════════ N ]               │
   └──────────────────────────────────┘

 Horseshoe magnet:

      ╭═════════╮
     ═╯         ╰═
     N           S
     ╚═══════════╝   ◄── ek keeper (siron ke upar ek patti)
```

> **Figure 1.8** – Magnet ko jode mein, opposite poles ke saath, aur keeper ke saath rakha jaata hai.

Keeper magnet ko uski taakat banaye rakhne mein madad karta hai. Kaise, wo Topic 3 mein.

## Part D: Solved examples

### Example 1: 15 cheezein: magnetic ya non-magnetic?

Kabaadi ke dher ki 15 cheezein: (1) iron ki keel, (2) taambe ka tar, (3) steel ka paper clip, (4) aluminium ka can, (5) plastic ki kangi, (6) kaanch ki goli, (7) lakdi ki ruler, (8) iron ka bolt, (9) brass ki chaabi, (10) rubber band, (11) nickel ka tar, (12) kaagaz, (13) tin ka dabba (andar steel), (14) steel ke scissors ka blade, (15) sooti kapda.

- **Magnetic (6):** iron ki keel, steel ka paper clip, iron ka bolt, nickel ka tar, tin ka dabba, steel ke scissors ka blade.
- **Non-magnetic (9):** taambe ka tar, aluminium ka can, plastic ki kangi, kaanch ki goli, lakdi ki ruler, brass ki chaabi, rubber band, kaagaz, sooti kapda.

Jaanch: 6 + 9 = 15 cheezein ✅

### Example 2: 8 pairs: khinchav ya dhakka?

| No. | Pair | Kya hoga? |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | N aur N | Dhakka (repel) |
| 2 | N aur S | Khinchav (attract) |
| 3 | S aur S | Dhakka (repel) |
| 4 | S aur N | Khinchav (attract) |
| 5 | N aur N | Dhakka (repel) |
| 6 | S aur N | Khinchav (attract) |
| 7 | S aur S | Dhakka (repel) |
| 8 | N aur S | Khinchav (attract) |

Rule: samaan (like) dhakelte hain, alag (unlike) khinchte hain. Dhakka: pair 1, 3, 5, 7 (4 pairs). Khinchav: pair 2, 4, 6, 8 (4 pairs).
Jaanch: 4 + 4 = 8 pairs ✅

### Example 3: 12 cm ka magnet, 3 barabar tukde

Ek 12 cm lamba bar magnet ko 3 barabar tukdon mein kaata gaya. Kitne magnets bane? Har ka size? Kul kitne poles?

- Har tukde ki lambai = 12 cm ÷ 3 = **4 cm.**
- Har tukda ek poora magnet hai, apne N aur S ke saath. Magnets = **3**.
- Kul poles = 3 magnets × 2 poles = **6 poles** (3 N aur 3 S).

Jaanch: 3 × 4 cm = 12 cm ✅ aur 3 × 2 = 6 poles ✅ (Kaatne ke liye 2 cut lagte hain, jabki magnets 3 hain.)

### Example 4: Compass ka sawal

Ek compass ki suyi ka N sira ek bar magnet ke sire A ki taraf khinchta hai, aur sire B se door bhaagta hai. A aur B kaun se poles hain?

- Suyi ka N sira **S pole** ki taraf khinchta hai, to **A = S pole**.
- Suyi ka N sira **N pole** se dhakelta hai (like poles), to **B = N pole**.

Jaanch: A (S) aur B (N) alag-alag hain, jaise bar magnet mein hona chahiye ✅

### Example 5: Pins ki table

12 cm lambe bar magnet ke ek sire (0 cm) se naapte hue alag-alag jagah chipki pins:

| Jagah (0 cm se) | Pins |
|---|---|
| 0 cm | 12 |
| 2 cm | 7 |
| 4 cm | 3 |
| 6 cm | 2 |
| 8 cm | 3 |
| 10 cm | 6 |
| 12 cm | 11 |

- **Poles kahan hain?** Sabse zyada pins siron par (0 cm par 12, 12 cm par 11). To poles **dono siron ke paas** hain.
- **Sabse kamzor jagah:** 6 cm (beech), 2 pins.
- Sire (0 cm) aur beech ka antar = 12 − 2 = **10 pins** zyada. Anupaat: 12 ÷ 2 = **6 guna**.

Jaanch: sabse zyada pins siron par (12 aur 11), sabse kam beech mein (2), aur 4 cm aur 8 cm par barabar 3 pins ✅

### Example 6: Dono sire dono siron ko khinche, ye kya hai?

Do ek jaisi dikhne wali chhad P aur Q hain. P ka ek sira Q ke **dono siron** ko khinchta hai. Kya dono magnet ho sakti hain?

- Agar dono magnet hoti, to P ka ek sira Q ke ek sire ko khinchta aur **doosre sire ko dhakelta** (kyunki Q ke dono sire N aur S hote hain).
- Yahan dono siron par khinchav hi hai, dhakka kahin nahi. Isliye **Q magnet nahi hai**, wo sirf **iron (ya steel) ka tukda** hai.
- **P magnet hai** (kyunki iron ka tukda iron ko nahi khinchta).

Jaanch: P magnet, Q iron: P iron ko dono siron par khinchta hai ✅ Aur agar kahin dhakka dikhta, to Q bhi magnet hota.

### Example 7: Ek ko ulta kar do

Do bar magnets M1 aur M2 aise rakhe hain ki M1 ka S sira M2 ke N sire ke saamne hai. Phir M2 ko ghuma kar ulta kar diya. Kya badla?

```
 Pehle  :   [ N ══ S ]  ◄──►  [ N ══ S ]    S saamne N ke: KHINCHAV
               M1                M2

 Baad mein: [ N ══ S ]  ◄──►  [ S ══ N ]    S saamne S ke: DHAKKA
               M1                M2 (ulta)
```

> **Figure 1.9** – M2 ko ghumane se N-S ki jagah S-S saamne aa gaye: khinchav dhakke mein badal gaya.

- Pehle: M1 ka S aur M2 ka N: **unlike**, to **khinchav**.
- M2 ko ulta karne par uska S sira saamne aaya: M1 ka S aur M2 ka S: **like**, to **dhakka**.

Jaanch: kul 2 situations: ek mein attract, ek mein repel ✅

### Example 8: Dhaage se latka magnet

Ek bar magnet dhaage se latka hai aur ruk gaya hai. Aap us disha ki taraf munh karke khade ho jahan se suraj nikalta hai (east). Magnet ka sira A aapke **baayen haath** ki taraf hai. Sira A kaun sa pole hai?

- East ki taraf munh karo to **north aapke baayen haath par** hota hai.
- Sira A baayen haath ki taraf, yaani north ki taraf ruka hai. To **A = N pole (north-seeking pole)**, aur doosra sira S pole.

Jaanch: east ki taraf munh karke baayen taraf north hota hai, aur A wahin hai ✅

### Example 9: Kabaadi ki sorting

Yard ke ek dher mein 20 cheezein thi: 8 iron ke items, 5 taambe ke taar, 4 aluminium ke can aur 3 plastic ki cheezein. Magnet ne kitni uthayi, kitni chhodi?

- Uthayi (magnetic): 8 iron items.
- Chhodi (non-magnetic): 5 + 4 + 3 = **12**.

Jaanch: 8 + 12 = 20 ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **"Magnet sab dhaatuon (metals) ko khinchta hai."**
   Galat. Magnet sirf iron, steel, nickel, cobalt jaise magnetic materials ko khinchta hai. Copper, aluminium, brass dhaatu hain, par magnet unhe nahi khinchta (kahani ke taambe ke tar aur aluminium ke can).

2. **"Magnet ko tod do to ek tukde mein sirf N aur doosre mein sirf S hoga."**
   Galat. Har tukde mein N aur S dono aate hain. 12 cm magnet ke 3 tukde = 3 magnets aur 6 poles.

3. **"Poles bilkul aakhri point par hote hain."**
   Galat. Poles siron ke **paas** hote hain, ek chhote hisse mein. Filings ka experiment dekho: wo sire ke aas-paas ke hisse mein chipakti hain, sirf ek bindu par nahi.

4. **"Agar chhad khinchti hai, to wo pakka magnet hai."**
   Galat. Iron ka tukda bhi khinchta hai. **Pakka test dhakka (repulsion)** hai.

5. **"Magnet ko kaam karne ke liye chhuna padta hai."**
   Galat. Magnet kaagaz, lakdi, hawa ke aar-paar bhi, **bina chhue** khinchta hai (non-contact force). Bas doori badhne par kamzor hota hai.

6. **"Magnet ka North pole Earth ke north ko khinchta hai, kyunki N-N ek doosre ko khinchte hain."**
   Galat. N-N to dhakelte hain! Magnet ka **north-seeking pole** north disha ki taraf rukta hai. Isse ye samajh aata hai ki Earth ke uttar mein kisi **magnetic south** jaisi cheez ka asar hai. Iska raaz Topic 2 mein.

## Quick Recap

- Magnet iron jaisi cheezein khinchta hai. **Natural magnet:** lodestone (magnetite). **Artificial magnet:** iron, steel, nickel, cobalt, alnico se bane.
- Magnet ke aakaar: bar, horseshoe, U-shaped, cylindrical, disc, ring, compass needle, fridge (flexible rubber) magnet.
- **Magnetic:** iron, steel, nickel, cobalt. **Non-magnetic:** copper, aluminium (bahut kamzor asar), brass, plastic, glass, wood, paper, rubber.
- Khinchav sabse zyada **siron (poles)** par, beech mein kam.
- Azaadi se latka magnet north-south mein rukta hai (**directive property**): north ki taraf wala sira **N**, south ki taraf wala **S**.
- **Like poles repel, unlike poles attract.** Pakka test repulsion hai.
- Magnet ko todo to har tukda poora magnet (N aur S dono); akela pole (monopole) nahi milta.
- Magnetic force **bina chhue** kaam karta hai aur doori badhne par ghatta hai.
- Magnet ki taakat pins ya paper clips ki ginti se: siron par zyada, beech mein kam.
- Dekhbhaal: jode mein, opposite poles saath, keeper lagao; garm, hathoda, gira dena nahi; phone, card, ghadi, TV, pacemaker se door.

## Practice Questions

1. Classify as magnetic or non-magnetic: steel spoon (ordinary steel), copper coin-shaped disc, glass tumbler, iron nail, plastic ruler, nickel strip, aluminium foil, wooden pencil.

2. A 10 cm bar magnet is cut into 5 equal pieces. How long is each piece? How many magnets and how many poles are there now?

3. A bar magnet lifts 14 pins at one end, 13 pins at the other end, 1 pin at the centre and 6 pins at 2 cm from an end. (a) Where are the poles? (b) How many times more pins does the 14-pin end lift than the centre? (c) How many pins in total at the two ends?

4. Say "attract" or "repel" for these six pairs of poles: N-S, S-S, N-N, S-N, S-S, N-S. How many pairs attract and how many repel?

5. One end of a rod X attracts the N pole of a magnet and also attracts the S pole of the same magnet. Is X a magnet? Explain.

6. A rod Y repels the N pole of a magnet. What can you say about Y? Explain.

7. A bar magnet is hanging freely on a thread. It rests with the end A pointing south. Which pole is A?

8. The N end of a compass needle is pulled towards one end of a bar magnet and pushed away from the other end. Which end of the bar magnet is the N pole?

9. Why is repulsion, and not attraction, the sure test for a magnet?

10. A scrap heap has 30 items: 10 iron bolts, 8 steel tins, 7 copper wires, 3 aluminium cans and 2 glass bottles. How many items does a magnetic crane pick, and how many does it leave?

11. A magnet is kept below a wooden table and a paper clip on the table moves. Does the magnet need to touch the clip? What happens if the table is made much thicker? Explain.

12. Two bar magnets are to be stored together. Describe how you should place them, and what to put at the ends, and why.

13. A 20 cm magnet is broken into two unequal pieces, 8 cm and 12 cm. How many poles does each piece have, and how many in total?

14. State True or False:
    (a) A magnet attracts all metals.
    (b) The north-seeking pole of a freely hanging magnet points towards the geographic north.
    (c) If you break a magnet, you get a separate N pole and a separate S pole.
    (d) A magnet can pull a paper clip through a sheet of paper.

15. Why should a magnet not be kept near a mobile phone or a credit card? Answer in one or two lines.

### Answers

1. **Magnetic:** steel spoon (ordinary steel), iron nail, nickel strip. **Non-magnetic:** copper disc, glass tumbler, plastic ruler, aluminium foil, wooden pencil. Jaanch: 3 + 5 = 8 items ✅

2. Har piece = 10 cm ÷ 5 = **2 cm**. Magnets = **5**. Poles = 5 × 2 = **10 poles**.
   Jaanch: 5 × 2 cm = 10 cm ✅

3. (a) Poles **dono siron ke paas** hain (14 aur 13 pins), kyunki wahin sabse zyada pins chipki. (b) 14 ÷ 1 = **14 guna**. (c) 14 + 13 = **27 pins**.
   Jaanch: 14 × 1 pin = 14 pins ✅

4. N-S attract, S-S repel, N-N repel, S-N attract, S-S repel, N-S attract. **Attract: 3 pairs, repel: 3 pairs.** Jaanch: 3 + 3 = 6 ✅

5. X ka wo sira magnet ke N aur S **dono** ko khinchta hai. Agar X magnet hota, to uska sira magnet ke ek pole ko khinchta aur doosre ko **dhakelta**. Dhakka kahin nahi hai, to X **magnet nahi hai**, wo sirf iron ya steel ka tukda hai (magnetic material).

6. **Y magnet hai**, aur uska wo sira (jo dhakel raha hai) **N pole** hai (like poles repel). Kyunki saada iron kabhi dhakelta nahi, sirf khinchta hai.

7. Sira A south ki taraf hai, to **A = S pole** (south-seeking pole). Doosra sira N.

8. Compass ka N sira **S pole** ki taraf khinchta hai. Isliye jis sire ki taraf wo khinchta hai wo **S pole** hai, aur jis sire se dhakelta hai wo **N pole** hai. Bar magnet ka **N pole = wo sira jisse needle ka N door bhaaga.**

9. Khinchav iron ke saade tukde se bhi mil jaata hai, to wo pakka saboot nahi. Par dhakka sirf magnet ke poles se aata hai: **like poles** hi dhakelte hain. Isliye repulsion pakka test hai.

10. Magnetic items: 10 bolts + 8 steel tins = **18** uthaye. Non-magnetic: 7 + 3 + 2 = **12** chhode.
    Jaanch: 18 + 12 = 30 ✅

11. Magnet ko clip ko **chhuna nahi padta**; magnetic force lakdi ke aar-paar bhi kaam karta hai (non-contact force). Agar table bahut moti kar di jaaye, to doori badh jaayegi, force kamzor hoga, aur clip shayad na hile.

12. Dono magnets ko saath-saath rakho, **ek ka N doosre ke S ke paas** (opposite poles saath). Dono siron par **naram lohe ke keepers** (patti) laga do. Isse magnet apni taakat banaye rakhta hai (Topic 3 mein detail).

13. Har tukda poora magnet hai: **8 cm wale mein 2 poles**, **12 cm wale mein 2 poles**. Kul **4 poles**.
    Jaanch: 2 magnets × 2 = 4 ✅

14. (a) **False** ❌. Sirf magnetic materials (iron, steel, nickel, cobalt). (b) **True** ✅. (c) **False** ❌. Har tukde mein N aur S dono. (d) **True** ✅. Magnet kaagaz ke aar-paar bhi kaam karta hai.

15. Magnet ka asar phone ya card ki patti par laga hua magnetic data bigaad (kharab kar) sakta hai, aur phone ke kuch hisson ko prabhavit kar sakta hai. Isliye door rakho.
