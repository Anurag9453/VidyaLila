# Topic 5: Density aur Volume Naapna

Pichle topic mein humne dekha ki koi bhi naap perfect nahi hota: error hota hai, accuracy aur precision alag cheezein hain, percentage error se pata chalta hai ki galti kitni badi hai, aur significant figures batate hain ki answer mein kitne digits bharosemand hain. Ab tak hum length, mass aur time jaisi cheezein naap rahe the, aur unke SI units aur instruments bhi jaan chuke hain.

Ab hum in sab ko ek saath kaam par lagayenge. Mass aur volume ko naapkar ek nayi quantity banayenge, **density**, aur dekhenge ki ek tedhe-medhe patthar ka volume bhi paani ki madad se kaise nikalte hain. Ye chapter ka aakhri topic hai, to end mein poore chapter ka wrap-up bhi hoga.

## Do paperweight, ek jaisa size, alag wazan

Thodi der baad Mr. Reddy ek ajeeb sa, tedha-medha patthar Neel ko dete hain. "Isse bhi kuch banana hai. Bata, iska volume kaise nikalega? Isme na length hai, na breadth!" Neel sochta reh jaata hai. Dono ka jawab hum aage paayenge.

## Part A: Density kya hoti hai

### Kitna ghana?

Do cheezein socho: ek bada sa sponge aur ek utne hi size ka lohe ka tukda. Size (volume) same, par lohe mein mass bahut zyada hai. Kyunki lohe mein ek chhoti si jagah mein bahut saara maal thoosa hua hai. Isi "kitna maal ek unit jagah mein packed hai" ko density kehte hain:

**Density = Mass ÷ Volume**

Symbol mein ρ (rho) likhte hain: **ρ = m ÷ V**

- Agar mass ko kg mein aur volume ko m³ mein lein, to density kg/m³ aayegi. Ye **SI unit** hai (Topic 2 mein dekha tha).
- Agar mass g mein aur volume cm³ mein ho, to density g/cm³ aayegi. Lab mein ye aksar kaam aati hai.
- Dono ka rishta: **1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³**.

Ek aur baat: density ek **property of the material** hai. Lohe ka bada tukda ho ya chhota, density same rehti hai (iske baare mein Aam galtiyan mein aur baat karenge).

### Kuch aam cheezon ki density

| Padarth | Density (kg/m³), lagbhag | Density (g/cm³), lagbhag |
|---|---|---|
| Hawa (air) | 1.2 | 0.0012 |
| Wood (lakdi) | 600 (lakdi ke type ke hisaab se badalti hai) | 0.6 |
| Paani (water) | 1000 | 1.0 |
| Aluminium | 2700 | 2.7 |
| Iron (lohe) | 7800 | 7.8 |
| Copper (taamba) | 8900 | 8.9 |
| Mercury (paara) | 13600 | 13.6 |

Ye sab values **approximate** hain; temperature aur purity ke saath thodi badalti hain. Dhyan do: paani ki 1000 kg/m³ yaad rakhne layak hai, kyunki baaki sabko usse compare karte hain.

### Example 1: Lohe ke block ki density

Ek iron block ka mass 390 g aur volume 50 cm³ hai. Density?

ρ = m ÷ V = 390 g ÷ 50 cm³ = **7.8 g/cm³**

SI mein: 7.8 g/cm³ × 1000 = **7800 kg/m³**

Jaanch: 7.8 g/cm³ × 50 cm³ = 390 g ✅ (aur table mein iron ≈ 7800 kg/m³ hai)

### Example 2: Volume se mass nikalna

Ek aluminium ke piece ka volume 0.002 m³ hai. Aluminium ki density 2700 kg/m³ hai. Mass?

m = ρ × V = 2700 kg/m³ × 0.002 m³ = **5.4 kg**

Jaanch: 5.4 kg ÷ 0.002 m³ = 2700 kg/m³ ✅

### Example 3: Mass se volume nikalna

Copper ka 17.8 kg ka tukda hai. Copper ki density 8900 kg/m³ hai. Volume?

V = m ÷ ρ = 17.8 kg ÷ 8900 kg/m³ = **0.002 m³**

cm³ mein: 0.002 m³ × 1,000,000 = **2000 cm³** (kyunki 1 m³ = 10,00,000 cm³)

Jaanch: 8900 kg/m³ × 0.002 m³ = 17.8 kg ✅

### Example 4: Units badalna

(a) Mercury ki density 13.6 g/cm³ hai. kg/m³ mein?

13.6 g/cm³ × 1000 = **13600 kg/m³**

(b) Lakdi ki density 600 kg/m³ hai. g/cm³ mein?

600 kg/m³ ÷ 1000 = **0.6 g/cm³**

Jaanch: 0.6 g/cm³ × 1000 = 600 kg/m³ ✅

### Example 5: Do cheezon ki tulna

Cheez A ka mass 270 g aur volume 100 cm³ hai. Cheez B ka mass bhi 270 g hai, par volume 300 cm³ hai. Kaunsi zyada ghani hai?

ρ(A) = 270 g ÷ 100 cm³ = **2.7 g/cm³**
ρ(B) = 270 g ÷ 300 cm³ = **0.9 g/cm³**

A zyada ghani hai (2.7 > 0.9). Dono ka mass same tha, par B ne usi mass ko 3 guna zyada jagah mein phaila rakha hai.

Jaanch: 2.7 ÷ 0.9 = 3, aur volume ka ratio 300 ÷ 100 = 3 ✅

## Part B: Volume kaise naapte hain

Density nikalne ke liye mass to balance se mil jaata hai. Volume ke liye cheez ki shape dekhni padti hai.

### 1. Regular solids: formula se

| Shape | Volume |
|---|---|
| Cuboid | length × breadth × height |
| Cube (side a) | a³ |
| Cylinder (radius r, height h) | πr²h (π = 22/7 ya 3.14) |
| Sphere (gola) | (4/3)πr³ (sirf naam aur formula, ise yaad rakhna hi kaafi hai) |

Length, breadth, height scale ya vernier caliper se naapte hain (Topic 3), aur unke units same rakhte hain.

### Example 6: Cuboid ka volume aur density

Ek iron cuboid 10 cm × 5 cm × 4 cm ka hai aur mass 1560 g hai.

V = 10 cm × 5 cm × 4 cm = **200 cm³**
ρ = 1560 g ÷ 200 cm³ = **7.8 g/cm³** = **7800 kg/m³**

Jaanch: 7.8 × 200 = 1560 g ✅

### Example 7: Cylinder ka volume aur density

Ek metal cylinder ki radius 3.5 cm aur height 10 cm hai. Mass 3080 g hai. π = 22/7 lo.

V = πr²h = (22/7) × 3.5 cm × 3.5 cm × 10 cm = (22/7) × 12.25 cm² × 10 cm = 38.5 cm² × 10 cm = **385 cm³**
ρ = 3080 g ÷ 385 cm³ = **8 g/cm³** = **8000 kg/m³**

Jaanch: 8 g/cm³ × 385 cm³ = 3080 g ✅

### 2. Liquid ka volume: measuring cylinder

Cylinder par mL (millilitre) ke nishaan bane hote hain. **1 mL = 1 cm³** (aur 1 L = 1000 mL = 1000 cm³).

Paani cylinder ki deewar ke paas thoda upar chadh jaata hai, to sateh beech mein neeche ki taraf jhuki dikhti hai. Is jhuki sateh ko **meniscus** kehte hain. Reading hamesha **meniscus ke sabse neeche ke point** se, **aankh ko usi level par rakhkar** leni hoti hai.

```
        aankh yahan (meniscus ke level par)
              o  ------------------->
   |      |
   |\    /|   <- paani deewar ke paas upar
   | \__/ |   <- meniscus ka sabse neeche ka point
   |------|--- 40 mL   <- yahan se padho
   |      |
   |------|--- 30 mL
   |______|
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Measuring cylinder mein reading: aankh ko meniscus ke level par rakho aur uske neeche ke point se padho. Upar se dekhoge ya neeche se, to reading galat aayegi (parallax error).

Is figure mein reading **40 mL** hai.

### 3. Irregular solid: displacement method

Tedhe-medhe patthar ke liye formula nahi chalta. Yahan ek simple idea kaam aata hai: **koi bhi thos cheez jitni jagah gherti hai, paani ko utni hi jagah se hata deti hai.**

Steps:
1. Measuring cylinder mein itna paani lo jisme patthar poora doob jaaye. Level padho: V₁.
2. Patthar ko dhaage se baandhkar dheere se paani mein chhodo, taaki wo poora doob jaaye aur paani bahar na chhalke.
3. Naya level padho: V₂.
4. Patthar ka volume = V₂ − V₁.

```
   Pehle              Baad mein
   |      |           |      |
   |      |           |  ~~~ |  <- V2 = 68 mL
   |~~~~~ | <- V1     |~~~~~ |
   | 50   |   =50 mL  | (@)  |  <- patthar doob gaya
   |______|           |______|
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Displacement method: patthar doobne se paani ka level V₁ = 50 mL se badhkar V₂ = 68 mL ho gaya. Level ka badhna hi patthar ka volume hai.

Agar cylinder ke andar jaane layak size ka na ho, to **overflow can (eureka can)** use karte hain. Can ko nozzle tak paani se bharte hain, patthar dubate hain, aur nozzle se jo paani bahar giray use measuring cylinder mein pakadkar naap lete hain. Wo paani ka volume hi patthar ka volume hai.

Dhyan rahe: ye tareeka un cheezon ke liye hai jo **paani mein doob jaati hain**. Jo cheezein tairti hain (floating), unka alag mamla hai, wo **Fluids chapter mein** dekhenge.

### Example 8: Patthar ka volume aur density

Paani ka level 50 mL se badhkar 68 mL ho gaya. Patthar ka mass 140.4 g hai.

Volume = V₂ − V₁ = 68 mL − 50 mL = **18 mL = 18 cm³** (1 mL = 1 cm³)
ρ = 140.4 g ÷ 18 cm³ = **7.8 g/cm³** = **7800 kg/m³**

Jaanch: 7.8 g/cm³ × 18 cm³ = 140.4 g ✅ (ye iron jaisi density hai)

## Part C: Lab mein solid ki density naapna

**Chahiye:** common balance, measuring cylinder, paani, dhaaga, aur solid ka tukda (maano aluminium ka).

**Steps:**
1. Balance se solid ka mass naapo (zero error check karke, Topic 3).
2. Cylinder mein paani lo, V₁ padho (meniscus ke neeche se, aankh level par).
3. Solid ko dhaage se baandhkar dheere se doobao, V₂ padho.
4. Volume V = V₂ − V₁.
5. Density ρ = m ÷ V nikalo.
6. Kam se kam 3 baar dohrao aur average lo (random error kam karne ke liye, Topic 4).

**Sample readings:** aluminium ka tukda, mass m = 54.2 g.

| Trial | V₁ (mL) | V₂ (mL) | V = V₂ − V₁ (cm³) | ρ = m ÷ V (g/cm³) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 40 | 60 | 20 | 2.71 |
| 2 | 30 | 51 | 21 | 2.58 |
| 3 | 25 | 45 | 20 | 2.71 |

Average density = (2.71 + 2.58 + 2.71) ÷ 3 = 8.00 ÷ 3 = **2.67 g/cm³** (lagbhag)

Standard (diya hua) value 2.70 g/cm³ hai.

Percentage error = (measured aur standard ka farq ÷ standard) × 100
= (0.03 g/cm³ ÷ 2.70 g/cm³) × 100 ≈ **1.1%**

Jaanch: 0.03 ÷ 2.70 = 0.0111, yaani 1.11% ✅ (kaafi accha result)

Cylinder ki least count 1 mL hai, to V ka error lagbhag ±1 cm³ hota hai, jo 20 cm³ ka 5% hai. Isliye trials mein thoda farq aana normal hai. Topic 3 aur 4 ki wahi baat: bada volume lo ya average lo, to error ka asar kam hota hai.

## Part D: Kahani ka hisaab

**Paperweights.** Dono cube 5 cm side ke hain.

V = a³ = 5 cm × 5 cm × 5 cm = **125 cm³** (dono ka)

Neel ne balance par rakha: patthar wale ka mass 337.5 g aur plaster wale ka 100 g.

ρ(patthar) = 337.5 g ÷ 125 cm³ = **2.7 g/cm³** = 2700 kg/m³
ρ(plaster) = 100 g ÷ 125 cm³ = **0.8 g/cm³** = 800 kg/m³

Ab Neel samjha: size same, par patthar wale mein 125 cm³ mein 337.5 g maal hai, jabki plaster wale mein sirf 100 g (andar ki khokhli jagah ki wajah se bhi). Isliye ek bhaari, doosra halka.

Jaanch: 2.7 × 125 = 337.5 g ✅ aur 0.8 × 125 = 100 g ✅

**Tedha patthar.** Neel ne cylinder mein 60 mL paani liya. Patthar dubane par level 95 mL hua. Patthar ka mass 94.5 g mila.

V = 95 mL − 60 mL = 35 mL = **35 cm³**
ρ = 94.5 g ÷ 35 cm³ = **2.7 g/cm³**

Ye wahi 2.7 g/cm³ hai jo pehle paperweight ki thi. Mr. Reddy ne kaha, "Bilkul sahi. Ye usi tarah ka patthar hai, ab isse bhi paperweight bana sakte hain."

Jaanch: 2.7 × 35 = 94.5 g ✅

Mr. Reddy ne ek aur baat bataayi: "Kyun ek chiz paani mein doob jaati hai aur doosri tairti hai, ye density se juda hai. Par wo poori kahani **Fluids chapter mein** milegi. Wahin relative density ka naam bhi aayega."

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: g ko m³ ke saath use karna (bina badle)**
Iron block: 390 g aur 50 cm³ = 0.00005 m³. 390 ÷ 0.00005 = 78,00,000 g/m³. Ye SI unit nahi hai, aur dekhne mein ajeeb lagta hai. Ya to g ke saath cm³ rakho (7.8 g/cm³), ya kg ke saath m³ (0.39 kg ÷ 0.00005 m³ = 7800 kg/m³).

**Galti 2: mL aur L ko mix karna**
1 L = 1000 mL = 1000 cm³. Agar 2 L paani ko 2 mL maan lo, to volume 1000 guna kam aa jayega. Hamesha unit check karo.

**Galti 3: Meniscus ko upar se padhna**
Aankh upar ho to reading zyada ya kam dikhti hai. Sahi tareeka: aankh meniscus ke level par, aur neeche ke point se padho (Figure 5.1).

**Galti 4: Sochna ki bade piece ki density zyada hogi**
Lohe ka 390 g ka tukda 50 cm³ ka hai, ρ = 7.8 g/cm³. Uska aadha: 195 g aur 25 cm³, ρ = 195 ÷ 25 = **7.8 g/cm³**. Mass aur volume dono aadhe hue, ratio wahi raha. Density size par nahi, **material** par depend karti hai.

**Galti 5: Galat unit likhna (kg/m² ya kg)**
Density ka unit kg/m³ hai. kg/m² to area par mass hota hai, aur sirf kg to mass ka unit hai, density ka nahi. Ek simple check: density = mass ÷ volume, to unit = kg ÷ m³ = kg/m³.

## Quick Recap

- **Density = Mass ÷ Volume** (ρ = m ÷ V): ek unit volume mein kitna mass hai.
- SI unit **kg/m³**; lab mein **g/cm³**. **1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³**.
- Paani ki density 1000 kg/m³ (1 g/cm³); iron lagbhag 7800, aluminium 2700, copper 8900, mercury 13600, wood 600 (varies), air 1.2 kg/m³ (approx).
- Density material ki property hai: sample chhota ho ya bada, same rehti hai.
- **Regular solid:** formula (cuboid lbh, cube a³, cylinder πr²h).
- **Liquid:** measuring cylinder, meniscus ke neeche ke point se, aankh level par. **1 mL = 1 cm³**.
- **Irregular solid (jo doobta hai):** displacement method, V = V₂ − V₁. Tairne wali cheezein aur relative density: Fluids chapter mein.
- Lab mein density nikalkar standard value se percentage error nikaal sakte hain.

## Practice Questions

1. A stone has a mass of 600 g and a volume of 250 cm³. Find its density in g/cm³ and in kg/m³.

2. A water tank is 2 m long, 1.5 m wide and 1 m deep and is completely full. If the density of water is 1000 kg/m³, find the mass of the water.

3. A wooden log has a mass of 30 kg. If the density of the wood is 600 kg/m³, find its volume in m³ and in cm³.

4. Convert (a) 0.9 g/cm³ to kg/m³ and (b) 2700 kg/m³ to g/cm³.

5. Mercury has a density of 13600 kg/m³. Find the mass of 500 cm³ of mercury in kg.

6. A solid is lowered into a measuring cylinder. The water level rises from 35 mL to 47 mL and the mass of the solid is 96 g. Find its volume and density.

7. A cylinder has radius 7 cm and height 10 cm. It is made of aluminium (density 2.7 g/cm³). Find its mass in g and in kg. Take π = 22/7.

8. A student measures the density of a copper piece as 8.7 g/cm³. The standard value is 8.9 g/cm³. Find the percentage error (round to one decimal place).

9. An iron block of density 7800 kg/m³ is cut into two equal halves. What is the density of each half? Give a reason.

10. A student reads the volume of water in a measuring cylinder by keeping the eye above the meniscus. Why is this wrong, and how should it be done? Also, a block has a mass of 2 kg and a volume of 500 cm³. Find its density in g/cm³.

### Answers

1. ρ = 600 g ÷ 250 cm³ = **2.4 g/cm³**. SI mein: 2.4 g/cm³ × 1000 = **2400 kg/m³**.
   Jaanch: 2.4 g/cm³ × 250 cm³ = 600 g ✅

2. V = 2 m × 1.5 m × 1 m = 3 m³. m = ρ × V = 1000 kg/m³ × 3 m³ = **3000 kg**.
   Jaanch: 3000 kg ÷ 3 m³ = 1000 kg/m³ ✅

3. V = m ÷ ρ = 30 kg ÷ 600 kg/m³ = **0.05 m³**. cm³ mein: 0.05 × 10,00,000 = **50,000 cm³**.
   Jaanch: 600 × 0.05 = 30 kg ✅

4. (a) 0.9 g/cm³ × 1000 = **900 kg/m³**. (b) 2700 kg/m³ ÷ 1000 = **2.7 g/cm³**.
   Jaanch: 2.7 g/cm³ × 1000 = 2700 kg/m³ ✅

5. 500 cm³ = 500 ÷ 10,00,000 m³ = 0.0005 m³. m = 13600 kg/m³ × 0.0005 m³ = **6.8 kg**.
   Jaanch: 6.8 ÷ 0.0005 = 13600 kg/m³ ✅

6. V = 47 mL − 35 mL = 12 mL = **12 cm³**. ρ = 96 g ÷ 12 cm³ = **8 g/cm³** (= 8000 kg/m³).
   Jaanch: 8 × 12 = 96 g ✅

7. V = (22/7) × 7 cm × 7 cm × 10 cm = 22 × 7 × 10 cm³ = 1540 cm³. m = 2.7 g/cm³ × 1540 cm³ = **4158 g = 4.158 kg**.
   Jaanch: 4158 ÷ 1540 = 2.7 g/cm³ ✅

8. Percentage error = (8.9 − 8.7) g/cm³ ÷ 8.9 g/cm³ × 100 = 0.2 ÷ 8.9 × 100 = 2.247..., to **2.2%**.
   Jaanch: 8.9 g/cm³ × 0.022 = 0.196 g/cm³ ≈ 0.2 g/cm³ ✅

9. **7800 kg/m³** hi rahegi. Aadha karne par mass aur volume dono aadhe ho jaate hain, to ratio wahi rehta hai. Density material ki property hai.

10. Upar se dekhne par parallax error aata hai aur reading galat padh jaati hai. Sahi tareeka: aankh ko meniscus ke level par rakho aur uske neeche ke point se padho.
    Density: 2 kg = 2000 g, ρ = 2000 g ÷ 500 cm³ = **4 g/cm³** (= 4000 kg/m³).
    Jaanch: 4 × 500 = 2000 g ✅
