# Topic 1: Physical Quantity aur Unit: Naapna Zaroori Kyun Hai

Ye Physics ka pehla chapter hai, to yahan koi pichhla topic nahi hai. Bas ek sawaal hai jo Physics ki shuruaat hai: **hum cheezon ko naapte kyun hain, aur naapne ke liye sirf ek number kaafi kyun nahi hota?** Is topic mein hum yahi samjhenge. Naapne ke tareeke, SI units aur instruments aage ke topics mein aayenge.

---

## Mr. Bhatt ke "5 haath"

Bazaar mein Mr. Bhatt ki kapde ki purani dukaan hai. Wo cloth naapne ke liye meter-scale nahi, apna **haath** use karte hain: kohni se lekar ungliyon ki nok tak ki lambai. Kapda kheench kar bolte hain, "Ek haath, do haath, teen haath..."

Ek din Class 9 ki student Tisha apni maa ke liye kapda lene aayi. Usne bola, "Uncle, 5 haath de dijiye." Mr. Bhatt ne apne haath se 5 baar naapa aur kaat diya.

Ghar aakar Tisha ne dekha ki kapda chhota lag raha hai. Usne apne haath se naapa. Uska haath Mr. Bhatt ke haath se chhota nikla. Kal wo dukaan par gayi aur bola, "Uncle, aapka haath aur mera haath alag hai. Phir '5 haath' ka matlab kiska 5 haath?"

Mr. Bhatt hanse, "Beta, baat to sahi hai. Mere 5 haath aur tumhare 5 haath ek jaise nahi hain."

Dono ne scale se naapa (maan lo):
- Mr. Bhatt ka 1 haath = 48 cm
- Tisha ka 1 haath = 42 cm

Ab dekho:
- Mr. Bhatt ke 5 haath = 5 × 48 cm = **240 cm**
- Tisha ke 5 haath = 5 × 42 cm = **210 cm**

Same "5 haath", par kapde mein **30 cm** ka farak (240 cm − 210 cm = 30 cm). Tisha ko 30 cm kam kapda mila!

Tisha ne socha, "Number 5 to dono ke paas ek hi hai. Gadbad number mein nahi, **haath** mein hai. Haath har insaan ka alag hai. Agar sab ek hi fixed lambai ko 'ek unit' maan lein, to ye jhagda khatam ho jayega."

Yahin se Physics shuru hoti hai: **naapna, aur sabke liye ek jaisa naapna.**

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Observation** | Dekhna, mehsoos karna ya notice karna | "Aaj garmi hai" |
| **Measurement (naap)** | Kisi cheez ko number aur unit ke saath batana | 240 cm |
| **Physical quantity** | Wo cheez jise hum naap sakte hain (number + unit se) | Lambai, mass, time, temperature |
| **Unit** | Naapne ka ek tay maapdand, jisse hum number ko tulna karte hain | cm, kg, s |
| **Standard unit** | Aisi unit jo sab logon ke liye same ho, kisi ek insaan par depend na kare | Meter (is par aage Topic 2 mein baat hogi) |
| **Magnitude** | Kisi quantity ka size, yaani kitni hai: number aur unit milakar | 210 cm |

## Part A: Observation aur Measurement mein farak

Hum roz bahut kuch dekhte aur mehsoos karte hain: chai garam hai, gaana accha hai, bag bhaari hai. Ye sab **observations** hain. Lekin Physics sirf dekhne par nahi rukti. Wo poochti hai: **"Kitna garam? Kitna bhaari?"**

Dono ka farak:

- "Chai garam hai" ek observation hai. Garam kitna, ye pata nahi.
- "Chai ka temperature 70 °C hai" ek **measurement** hai. Ab kisi bhi insaan ko poori baat samajh aati hai.

Kuch cheezein hum **naap** sakte hain, aur kuch nahi:

| Naap sakte hain | Naap nahi sakte |
|---|---|
| Lambai (length) | Khushi (happiness) |
| Mass (cheez mein kitna dravya) | Sundarta (beauty) |
| Time (samay) | Swaad (taste) |
| Temperature | Dosti (friendship) |

Ye farak kyun? Lambai ke liye hum scale rakh kar ek **number** de sakte hain, aur do log alag-alag naapein to bhi (theek tarah se naapne par) number lagbhag ek aayega. Par "ye gaana kitna accha hai?" par har insaan ka jawab alag hoga. Koi 7 bolega, koi 10, aur koi kuch nahi. Isliye khushi ya swaad ko Physics mein measure nahi karte.

> **Physical quantity** = aisi cheez jise hum **number aur unit** ke saath naap sakte hain, aur jo sab ke liye ek jaisi ho.

## Part B: Number ke saath unit kyun zaroori hai

Tisha ki kahani se ek baat saaf hai: sirf "5" kuch nahi batata.

- "Kapda 5 hai": 5 kya? Meter? Haath? Inch?
- "Kapda 5 haath hai": ab unit pata hai, par haath har insaan ka alag hai.
- "Kapda 210 cm hai": number bhi hai, unit bhi hai, aur unit sabke liye same hai. Ab koi confusion nahi.

> **Ek measurement = number + unit**

| Statement | Number | Unit | Poora? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Table ki lambai 120 cm hai | 120 | cm | Haan |
| Mera mass 45 kg hai | 45 | kg | Haan |
| Race 12 s mein poori hui | 12 | s | Haan |
| Paani ka temperature 30 hai | 30 | ? | Nahi, unit gayab |

Dono hone chahiye. Sirf number ho to batana adhoora, aur sirf unit ho ("cm") to bhi adhoora.

### Example 1: Number aur unit pehchano

Statement: "Tisha ki haath ki lambai 42 cm hai."
- Number = **42**
- Unit = **cm**
- Physical quantity = **lambai (length)**

**Jaanch:** 42 aur cm dono mil kar 42 cm bane, jisse Tisha ka haath kisi ko bhi samajh aa jata hai ✅

### Example 2: Teen haath, do log

Mr. Bhatt (1 haath = 48 cm) aur Tisha (1 haath = 42 cm) dono 3 haath kapda naapte hain.
- Mr. Bhatt: 3 × 48 cm = **144 cm**
- Tisha: 3 × 42 cm = **126 cm**
- Farak = 144 cm − 126 cm = **18 cm**

Number (3) ek hi hai, par lambai alag. Isliye "3 haath" kehna galat nahi hai, par ye kisi bharose layak naap nahi hai.

**Jaanch:** 126 cm + 18 cm = 144 cm ✅

### Example 3: Ek cheez, ek hi lambai

Agar dono ye kahein ki "kapda 210 cm hai", to dono ke paas kapde ki lambai **same** hogi, chahe naapne wala koi bhi ho. Ab Tisha ko Mr. Bhatt par bharosa karne ki zaroorat nahi, wo scale par khud dekh sakti hai.

## Part C: Purani units kyun fail hui

Purane zamane mein log har jagah apne sharir se naapte the:

| Purani unit | Kaise naapte the |
|---|---|
| Haath | Kohni se ungli ki nok tak |
| Qadam | Ek qadam ki doori |
| Bitta | Anguthe aur chhoti ungli ke beech ka faasla |
| Muththi | Ek muththi mein jitna aa jaye (anaaj naapne ke liye) |
| Anguli | Ek ungli ki motai |

Ye tareeke asaan the, kyunki sharir hamesha saath hota hai. Par inme ek badi kami hai: **ye insaan-insaan par badalte hain.**

- Bachche ka haath chhota, bade ka haath bada.
- Lamba insaan bada qadam leta hai, chhota insaan chhota.
- Kisi ki muththi bhari, kisi ki halki.

Jab kapde ya anaaj ka len-den ho, to ek jaisa naap hona zaroori hai. Warna jhagde honge, aur kisi ko nuksaan hoga. Isliye ek **common, fixed, standard unit** ki zaroorat padi, yaani aisi unit jo:

1. har insaan ke liye ek jaisi ho,
2. kabhi badle nahi,
3. jise sab log maanne ko taiyaar hon.

Kai jagah, alag-alag samay par, log ek jaisi unit banane ki koshish karte rahe. Aage chalkar duniya bhar ke scientists ne milkar **ek hi system** par sahmati banayi, jise **SI system** kehte hain. Wo kya hai aur usme kaun si units hain, ye hum agle topic mein dekhenge.

## Part D: Physical aur non-physical quantity

Ab aap khud apni samajh jaanchte hain. Neeche 10 cheezein hain. Pehle khud socho: kaunsi **physical quantity** hai (naap sakte ho, number + unit ke saath) aur kaunsi nahi?

1. Table ki lambai
2. Ek bachche ka mass
3. Ek khel ka time
4. Kamre ka temperature
5. Kisi film ka "accha" hona
6. Ek phool ki sundarta
7. Dosti ki gehrai
8. Kisi pen ki lambai
9. Biryani ka swaad
10. Ek gilaas paani ka mass

**Jawab:**

| Number | Physical quantity? | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Haan | Scale se naap sakte hain (jaise 150 cm) |
| 2 | Haan | Tarazu se naap sakte hain (jaise 40 kg) |
| 3 | Haan | Ghadi se naap sakte hain (jaise 90 s) |
| 4 | Haan | Thermometer se naap sakte hain (jaise 28 °C) |
| 5 | Nahi | Har insaan ki raay alag |
| 6 | Nahi | Sundarta ka koi sabke liye same number nahi |
| 7 | Nahi | Mehsoos hoti hai, naapi nahi jaati |
| 8 | Haan | Lambai, jaise 14 cm |
| 9 | Nahi | Swaad par sabki raay alag |
| 10 | Haan | Mass, jaise 250 g |

Dhyan do: "film accha lagi" ek observation hai, par "film 150 minute ki hai" ek **time ka measurement** hai. Ek hi cheez ke kuch pehlu naap sakte hain aur kuch nahi.

### Ek halka sa hint (sirf jhalak)

Mass, time aur temperature ke liye **number + unit** kaafi hai: "5 kg", "10 s", "30 °C". Par kuch cheezon mein **disha (direction) bhi chahiye**, jaise displacement. Wo Motion chapter mein aayega. Abhi aap itna jaan lo ki har physical quantity ka poora naap = number + unit hota hai.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Number likhna, unit bhool jana**

- Galat: "Meri pencil ki lambai 15 hai."
- Sahi: "Meri pencil ki lambai **15 cm** hai."

Bina unit ke 15 kuch bhi ho sakta hai: 15 cm, 15 m, 15 inch. Physics mein har answer ke saath unit likhna zaroori hai.

**Galti 2: Feelings ko physical quantity samajhna**

- Galat: "Mere dost ki khushi 8 hai, to khushi ek physical quantity hai."
- Sahi: Khushi, sundarta ya swaad ke liye koi common unit nahi hai, aur har insaan ka jawab alag aata hai. Isliye ye physical quantity nahi hain.

**Galti 3: "Alag unit, matlab alag lambai"**

Tisha ne kapda 210 cm bola. Agar koi kahe ki "2.1 m", to kya ye alag kapda hai? Nahi. Dono ek hi lambai hain, bas likhne ka tareeka alag hai (jaise ek hi cheez ko do alag bhashaon mein naam dena). Unit badalne se asli lambai nahi badalti. **Kaise badalte hain, ye Topic 2 mein seekhenge.**

Ye bhi dhyan rakho: Mr. Bhatt ke "5 haath" aur Tisha ke "5 haath" mein unit ka **naam** ek hai (haath), par unit ki **asli lambai** alag hai (48 cm aur 42 cm). Naam ek ho to bhi lambai alag ho sakti hai, jab tak unit standard na ho.

**Galti 4: Observation ko measurement samajhna**

"Aaj bahut garmi hai" measurement nahi hai. "Aaj 40 °C hai" measurement hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Observation** dekhna hai, **measurement** number + unit ke saath naapna hai.
- **Physical quantity** wo hai jise hum naap sakte hain: lambai, mass, time, temperature.
- Khushi, sundarta, swaad jaisi cheezein physical quantity nahi hain.
- **Measurement = number + unit.** Dono zaroori hain.
- Haath, qadam, bitta, muththi, anguli jaisi purani units insaan-insaan par badalti hain, isliye bharose layak nahi.
- Is liye **ek fixed, common, standard unit** chahiye, jise sab maanein. Duniya bhar ne milkar **SI system** apnaya (agle topic mein).
- Kuch quantities mein disha bhi chahiye, jaise displacement (Motion chapter mein).

## Practice Questions

1. Mr. Bhatt's haath is 48 cm and Tisha's haath is 42 cm. If both measure 10 haath of cloth, find the length each gets. What is the difference?

2. Write the number and the unit in each statement:
   (a) The length of the room is 4 m.
   (b) The mass of the bag is 6 kg.
   (c) The race took 15 s.
   (d) The temperature of the water is 60 °C.

3. Which of these statements is missing a unit? Rewrite it correctly by choosing a sensible unit.
   (a) The pencil is 18 cm long.
   (b) The baby weighs 7.
   (c) The film lasts 2 hours.

4. Sort these into physical quantities and non-physical ones: length of a road, loudness of someone's laughter (as "funny"), time taken to cook rice, the beauty of a rangoli, mass of a mango, temperature of tea, how much you miss your friend.

5. A student says, "The cloth is 5 haath long, so the length is fully described." Explain why this is not a good measurement, using the example of two people with different haath sizes.

6. State True or False:
   (a) A measurement needs both a number and a unit.
   (b) "The tea is hot" is a measurement.
   (c) Length is a physical quantity.
   (d) Two persons using "haath" will always get the same length of cloth.

7. Why was a common standard unit needed when people traded cloth and grain? Answer in 2-3 lines.

### Answers

1. Mr. Bhatt: 10 × 48 cm = **480 cm**. Tisha: 10 × 42 cm = **420 cm**. Farak = 480 cm − 420 cm = **60 cm**.
   Jaanch: 420 cm + 60 cm = 480 cm ✅

2. (a) Number **4**, unit **m**. (b) Number **6**, unit **kg**. (c) Number **15**, unit **s**. (d) Number **60**, unit **°C**.

3. (b) mein unit gayab hai. "The baby weighs 7" mein pata nahi 7 kya hai. Sahi: "The baby weighs **7 kg**." (a) mein 18 cm aur (c) mein 2 hours (time ki unit) theek hain.

4. **Physical quantities:** length of a road, time taken to cook rice, mass of a mango, temperature of tea.
   **Non-physical:** loudness of laughter as "funny" (ye raay hai), beauty of a rangoli, how much you miss your friend.

5. "5 haath" mein number to hai par unit ki lambai har insaan ki alag hai. Mr. Bhatt ke 5 haath 240 cm, aur Tisha ke 5 haath 210 cm. Farak 30 cm ka hai. Isliye lambai poori tarah describe nahi hui. Standard unit jaise cm mein likhna hoga.

6. (a) **True** ✅. (b) **False** ❌. Isme temperature ka number aur unit nahi hai, ye bas observation hai. (c) **True** ✅. (d) **False** ❌. Har insaan ka haath alag hai, to length alag aayegi (jaise 240 cm aur 210 cm).

7. Jab log kapda ya anaaj bechte-kharidte the, to har insaan ki apni unit hoti thi (haath, muththi), jo alag-alag size ki thi. Isse jhagde aur nuksaan hote the. Ek common standard unit se sabko ek jaisa naap milta hai aur bharosa banta hai.
