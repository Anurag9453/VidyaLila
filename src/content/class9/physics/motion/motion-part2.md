# Topic 2: Distance aur Displacement

Topic 1 mein humne seekha ki koi cheez chal rahi hai ya ruki hui hai, ye reference point ke hisaab se tay hota hai, aur position ko number line par likha ja sakta hai. Ab agla sawaal: jab cheez **chal rahi hai**, to hum "kitna chali" ko kaise naapein? Ye sawaal jitna seedha lagta hai, utna hai nahi, kyunki "kitna chala" ke **do alag matlab** ho sakte hain. Isi topic mein hum dono ko alag karna seekhenge: **distance** aur **displacement**.

## Mr. Joshi ka dak ka round

Gaon ke dak-babu (postman) Mr. Joshi roz subah post office se nikalte hain. Aaj unka round ye tha:

- Post office se **temple**: seedha **400 m east** (poorab) ki taraf.
- Temple se **school**: wahan se seedha **300 m north** (uttar) ki taraf.
- School mein mail uthayi, aur phir **usi sadak se wapas** school se temple, temple se post office.

Class 9 ki Ritika school ke gate par khadi thi. Usne Mr. Joshi ko wapas jaate dekha, to pooch liya, "Uncle, aaj aap kul kitna chale?"

Mr. Joshi ne ginti ki, "Beta, 400 aur 300 aur wapas 300 aur 400: poore **1400 m**."

Ritika ne ek aur sawaal pooch diya, "Aur ab aap post office se kitni door hain?"

Mr. Joshi hanse, "Arre, abhi to main post office pahunch gaya hoon. Door kahan, **zero metre**!"

Ritika ne socha: "Uncle 1400 m chale, phir bhi shuruaat se 0 m door? Dono baatein sach kaise ho sakti hain?" Dono sach hain, kyunki ye **do alag cheezein** hain. Is kahani ko hum end mein poora solve karenge. Pehle shabd samajh lete hain.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |

## Part A: Distance (kitna chale)

**Distance** = cheez ne path par jitna **poora** safar kiya, uski total length.

Seedhi si baatein:
- Distance ko **raaste ki length** ki tarah socho, jaise odometer ya meter-reading. Jitna chale, utna judta jaata hai.
- Distance ka koi direction nahi hota. "1400 m" kehna kaafi hai.
- Distance **kabhi negative nahi** hota. Ya to zero (jab hile hi nahi) ya positive.
- Distance **kabhi ghat-ta nahi**. Wapas mudne par bhi raaste ki length judti hi jaati hai, kam nahi hoti.
- Distance ek **scalar** hai.
- SI unit: **metre (m)**. Lambi doori ke liye km bhi chalta hai (1 km = 1000 m).

Mr. Joshi ke round mein jaise-jaise wo aage badhe, distance badhta gaya: 400 m, phir 700 m, phir 1000 m, phir 1400 m. Wapas aane par bhi ye ghata nahi.

---

## Part B: Displacement (shuruaat se ab kahan)

**Displacement** = initial position se final position tak ki **seedhi, sabse chhoti doori**, aur **kis direction mein**.

Yaani displacement ko ye parwah nahi ki beech mein cheez ne kaunsa raasta liya. Wo sirf **shuru aur end** dekhta hai.

- Displacement ka magnitude = shuru aur end point ko jodne wali **seedhi line** ki length.
- Direction = **shuru se end ki taraf**.
- Displacement ek **vector** hai: ise bataane ke liye magnitude aur direction dono chahiye. "500 m" adhoora hai; "500 m, north-east ki taraf" poora hai.
- Displacement **zero** bhi ho sakta hai (agar cheez wahin wapas aa jaye jahan se chali thi).
- Seedhi line (ek hi line) par direction ko **+ aur −** sign se dikhate hain.

### Seedhi line par + aur − sign

Number line par ek direction ko hum **+** maan lete hain, to ulti direction **−** ho jaati hai. Jaise east ko + maan lo to west −; ya upar ko + maan lo to neeche −.

```
  west (-)                                        east (+)
  <------+-----+-----+-----+-----+-----+-----+------>
        -3    -2    -1     0    +1    +2    +3  ... (m)
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Seedhi line par direction: east ki taraf wale displacement + aur west ki taraf wale − sign se likhte hain.

Matlab "−4 m" ka arth hai "4 m, ulti direction mein (west ki taraf)". Ye negative sign **doori kam hone** ka nahi, **direction ulti hone** ka sign hai.

> **Dhyan do:** sign wala displacement sirf seedhi line ke liye hai. Class 9 mein tedhe raaste ka displacement hum Pythagoras se **magnitude** nikalte hain aur direction shabdon mein likhte hain ("north-east ki taraf").

## Part C: Mr. Joshi ka round, dono ko saath rakh kar

Pythagoras theorem ki yaad: seedhi dono sides ke squares ka jod = hypotenuse ka square (iske liye [pythagoras-theorem.md](../../maths/pythagoras-theorem/pythagoras-theorem.md) dekho).

```
                      School (S)
                        *
                       /|
                      / |
          seedha     /  | 300 m (north)
          raasta    /   |
         500 m     /    |
                  /     |
   Post office   *------*  Temple (T)
       (P)       400 m (east)
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Mr. Joshi ka path: P se T tak 400 m east, T se S tak 300 m north. P se S tak seedhi line (shortest doori) 500 m hai.

### School tak (aadha safar)

- Distance = 400 m + 300 m = **700 m**
- Displacement (P se S tak seedhi line) = √(400² + 300²) = √(160000 + 90000) = √250000 = **500 m**, north-east ki taraf

**Jaanch:** 500² = 250000 aur 400² + 300² = 160000 + 90000 = 250000 ✅. Aur 500 m < 700 m, yaani displacement distance se chhota nikla.

### Poora round (wapas post office)

- Distance = 400 + 300 + 300 + 400 = **1400 m**
- Displacement = initial aur final position **ek hi** (post office), to seedhi doori **0 m**

**Jaanch:** distance ke chaaron tukde: 400 + 300 = 700, aur wapsi mein bhi 300 + 400 = 700, 700 + 700 = 1400 ✅. Shuru aur end ek hi jagah, to displacement = 0 ✅.

Ab Ritika ke sawaal ka jawab saaf hai: Mr. Joshi **1400 m chale** (distance) lekin shuruaat se unka **displacement 0 m** hai. Dono baatein sach hain kyunki dono alag cheezein naapti hain: ek raaste ki length, doosri shuru se end ki seedhi doori.

## Part D: Solved examples

### Example 1: 6 m east, phir 2 m west

Ek bachcha seedhi gali mein 6 m east ki taraf chala, phir ruk kar 2 m west ki taraf wapas aaya. Distance aur displacement nikalo.

East ko + maano.

- Distance = 6 m + 2 m = **8 m**
- Displacement = (+6 m) + (−2 m) = **+4 m**, yaani **4 m east ki taraf**

**Jaanch:** Shuru se 6 m east gaya, 2 m wapas aaya, to shuru se 6 − 2 = 4 m east par hai ✅. Aur 4 m < 8 m ✅.

### Example 2: Ghar se dukaan aur wapas

Ritika ghar se dukaan tak 150 m gayi aur wahi raaste se wapas ghar aa gayi.

- Distance = 150 m + 150 m = **300 m**
- Displacement = **0 m** (shuru aur end dono ghar)

**Jaanch:** (+150 m) + (−150 m) = 0 m ✅

Dhyan do: distance zero nahi, par displacement zero. Ye bilkul possible hai.

### Example 3: Gol track par aadha chakkar

Ek gol track ki radius **r = 14 m** hai. Ek bachcha point A se chalna shuru karke **aadha chakkar** laga kar theek saamne wale (diametrically opposite) point B par pahunchta hai. π = 22/7 lo.

```
          .  ~  ~  ~  .
       ~                 ~
      ~                   ~
     A *-------------------* B
      ~         O           ~
       ~                   ~
          .  ~  ~  ~  .

   A se B tak gol raaste (aadha chakkar): distance = pi x r
   A se B tak seedhi line (diameter):     displacement = 2r
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Gol track par aadha chakkar: raasta aadhe circle jitna lamba (πr), seedhi doori diameter (2r) ke barabar.

Aadhe circle ki length = (poore circle ki circumference) ÷ 2 = (2πr) ÷ 2 = πr.

- Distance = πr = (22/7) × 14 m = 22 × 2 = **44 m**
- Displacement = A se B ki seedhi doori = diameter = 2r = 2 × 14 m = **28 m**, A se B ki taraf

**Jaanch:** 28 m < 44 m ✅. Aur 2r = 28 m: radius 14 m ka double ✅.

(Agar π = 3.14 lo to distance = 3.14 × 14 = 43.96 m, jo lagbhag 44 m hi hai ✅.)

### Example 4: Usi track par poora chakkar

Wahi bachcha (r = 14 m) ab **poora ek chakkar** laga kar wapas A par aa jaata hai.

- Distance = poore circle ki length = 2πr = 2 × (22/7) × 14 m = **88 m**
- Displacement = shuru aur end ek hi point A, to **0 m**

**Jaanch:** poora chakkar = aadhe chakkar ka double = 2 × 44 m = 88 m ✅. Displacement 0 m ✅.

### Example 5: Mr. Joshi ka round, har padaav par

Mr. Joshi jab wapasi mein **temple** par pahunche, tab distance aur displacement (post office se) kya the?

- Distance = 400 m (P se T) + 300 m (T se S) + 300 m (S se T) = **1000 m**
- Wo temple par hain, jo post office se 400 m east mein hai, to displacement = **400 m east ki taraf**

**Jaanch:** shuru se temple tak seedhi doori 400 m hai (Figure 2.2 mein P se T ki line) ✅. Aur 400 m < 1000 m ✅.

Dekho, ek hi round mein alag-alag jagah par distance har baar badha, par displacement pehle 400 m, phir 500 m, phir wapas 400 m, aur end mein 0 ho gaya. **Displacement badh bhi sakta hai aur ghat bhi sakta hai; distance sirf badhta hai.**

### Example 6: Seedhi sadak par bus

Ek bus seedhi sadak par 60 km aage gayi (maano east ki taraf), phir wahan se 20 km peeche aayi. Distance aur displacement nikalo.

East ko + maano.

- Distance = 60 km + 20 km = **80 km**
- Displacement = (+60 km) + (−20 km) = **+40 km**, yaani **40 km east ki taraf**

**Jaanch:** shuruaat se bus 60 − 20 = 40 km aage hai ✅. Metre mein 40 km = 40 × 1000 = 40000 m, aur 80 km = 80000 m. 40000 < 80000 ✅.

### Example 7: Gend 10 m se giri aur 4 m uchhli

Ek gend 10 m ki unchai se seedhi neeche zameen par giri, takraai, aur wapas upar **4 m** tak uchhli. Distance aur displacement nikalo (shuruaat ka point: 10 m ki unchai).

Upar ko + maano.

- Distance = 10 m (neeche) + 4 m (upar) = **14 m**
- Final position = zameen se 4 m upar. Shuru 10 m par tha. Displacement = 4 m − 10 m = **−6 m**, yaani **6 m neeche ki taraf**

**Jaanch:** gend shuru ke point se 10 − 4 = 6 m neeche hai ✅. Distance 14 m, displacement ka magnitude 6 m: 6 < 14 ✅.

### Example 8: Number line par negative displacement

Ek chhote gaon ki sadak ko number line maano jisme east +. Ek cyclist **+5 m** wali jagah se chali aur **−3 m** wali jagah par pahunchi (seedha chali, mudi nahi). Distance aur displacement kya hain?

- Displacement = final − initial = (−3 m) − (+5 m) = **−8 m**, yaani **8 m west ki taraf**
- Distance = 8 m (seedhi chali, mudi nahi, to raaste ki length wahi 8 m)

**Jaanch:** +5 se 0 tak 5 m, 0 se −3 tak 3 m, kul 5 + 3 = 8 m ✅. Seedhi line mein ek hi direction mein chalne par distance aur displacement ka magnitude barabar nikla ✅.

### Example 9: 30 m north phir 40 m east

Ek bachcha khule maidan mein 30 m north chala, phir mud kar 40 m east chala. Distance aur displacement nikalo.

- Distance = 30 m + 40 m = **70 m**
- Displacement (shuru se end ki seedhi doori), Pythagoras se: √(30² + 40²) = √(900 + 1600) = √2500 = **50 m**, north-east ki taraf

**Jaanch:** 50² = 2500 aur 30² + 40² = 900 + 1600 = 2500 ✅. 50 m < 70 m ✅. Ye 3-4-5 wala triple hai (30-40-50 = 10 × (3-4-5)).

### Example 10: 400 m ki athletic track

Ek athletic track ek chakkar mein 400 m lamba hai. Ek dhaavak track par chakkar lagata hai.

- **Ek chakkar:** distance = **400 m**, displacement = **0 m** (start line par wapas).
- **3 chakkar:** distance = 3 × 400 m = **1200 m**, displacement = **0 m** (agar start line par hi ruke).

**Jaanch:** har poore chakkar ke baad shuru aur end ek hi point, to displacement 0 m ✅. Distance har chakkar ke saath 400 m badhta hai: 400, 800, 1200 ✅.

## Part E: Distance aur displacement kab barabar hote hain?

Examples dekh kar ek pattern dikhta hai:

> Distance aur displacement ka magnitude **sirf tab barabar** hote hain jab cheez **seedhi line mein, ek hi direction mein, bina mude** chale (Example 8).

Baaki sab situations mein displacement ka magnitude **distance se chhota** hota hai:

| Situation | Distance | Displacement (magnitude) |
|---|---|---|
| Seedhi line, mudna nahi (Example 8) | 8 m | 8 m |
| Seedhi line, thoda wapas aana (Example 1) | 8 m | 4 m |
| L-shape ka raasta (Example 9) | 70 m | 50 m |
| Aadha chakkar (Example 3) | 44 m | 28 m |
| Poora round (Example 4) | 88 m | 0 m |

Isliye ek niyam hamesha sach hai:

> **Displacement ka magnitude ≤ distance** (kabhi bada nahi hota).

Kyun? Do point ke beech **sabse chhota raasta seedhi line** hoti hai. Koi bhi tedha raasta usse lamba hi hoga, ya sabse achha seedhi line jitna.

## Scalar vs Vector

Ab hum ye farak ek bade idea mein dekh lete hain.

- **Scalar:** sirf magnitude (kitna) kaafi. Direction ka koi matlab nahi. Examples: **mass** (5 kg), **time** (10 s), **distance** (1400 m), **speed** (aage ke topic mein), temperature.
- **Vector:** magnitude **aur** direction dono. Examples: **displacement** (500 m north-east), **velocity** (aage ke topic mein), **force** (10 N neeche ki taraf).

Socho: "mass 5 kg, north ki taraf" kehna bekaar hai, mass ka direction hota hi nahi. Lekin "gaon 5 km" kehna adhoora hai agar raasta batana ho, kyunki "5 km kis taraf?" ka jawab chahiye. Isliye displacement **vector** hai.

> Vectors ko jodne ke tareeke (jaise components wala jod) bahut aage ki class ka topic hain. Class 9 mein hum bas itna karte hain: seedhi line par + / − sign, ya tedhe path ke liye Pythagoras se shortest doori.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Raaste ki length ko displacement bol dena**

Mr. Joshi ke round mein "1400 m" poochha jaye to usse displacement mat likho. 1400 m raaste ki length hai, yaani **distance**. Poore round ka displacement to 0 m hai.

**Galti 2: Displacement bina direction ke likhna**

"Displacement 500 m" adhoora hai. Poora jawab: "500 m, north-east ki taraf" (ya seedhi line par "+500 m" / "−500 m"). Bina direction ke ye vector nahi raha.

**Galti 3: Sochna ki agar distance zero nahi to displacement zero nahi ho sakta**

Example 2 aur Example 4 dekho: distance 300 m aur 88 m hai, phir bhi displacement 0 m. Agar shuru aur end ek hi point ho, displacement hamesha 0 hota hai, chahe kitna bhi chale ho.

**Galti 4: Displacement ke tukde seedha jod dena**

Example 9 mein 30 m aur 40 m ko jod kar "displacement 70 m" likh dena galat hai. 70 m raaste ki length (distance) hai. Displacement shuru se end ki **seedhi doori** hai, jo Pythagoras se **50 m** nikli. Yaad rakho: displacement 70 m se zyada ho hi nahi sakta, kyunki displacement ≤ distance.

**Galti 5: Units bhool jana**

"Distance = 1400" likhna adhoora hai. Hamesha unit likho: **1400 m**. Aur km aur m ko ek jawab mein mat milao: pehle ek unit mein convert karo (1 km = 1000 m).

**Galti 6: Negative sign ko "kam doori" samajh lena**

−6 m ka matlab "6 m **ulti direction** mein", doori ka magnitude to 6 m hi hai. Negative displacement ka arth direction hai, "kam" nahi. Distance negative kabhi nahi hota.

## Quick Recap

- **Distance** = path ki total length. Scalar. Hamesha ≥ 0. Kabhi ghat-ta nahi. Unit: m.
- **Displacement** = initial se final position tak ki seedhi (shortest) doori, direction ke saath. Vector. Positive, negative ya zero ho sakta hai. Unit: m.
- Seedhi line par direction ko **+ aur −** sign se likhte hain.
- Tedhe (L-shape) raaste ka displacement Pythagoras se: √(a² + b²).
- Gol track par **aadha chakkar**: distance πr, displacement 2r. **Poora chakkar**: distance 2πr, displacement 0.
- **Poora round trip:** displacement 0, distance 0 nahi.
- **Displacement ka magnitude ≤ distance.** Barabar sirf tab, jab seedhi line mein, ek hi direction mein, bina mude chalo.
- Mr. Joshi: distance 1400 m, displacement 0 m (poora round); school tak distance 700 m, displacement 500 m.
- **Scalar:** sirf magnitude (mass, time, distance). **Vector:** magnitude + direction (displacement, force).
- Galtiyan: distance ko displacement kehna, direction bhool jana, tukde seedha jod dena, unit bhoolna.

## Practice Questions

1. Define distance and displacement. Give two differences between them.

2. A boy walks 8 m east and then 3 m west along a straight lane. Taking east as positive, find the distance and the displacement.

3. Ritika walks from her house to the school, 600 m away along a straight road, and then comes back 200 m towards her house and stops. Find her distance and displacement (magnitude and direction).

4. A runner completes 2 laps of a 400 m track and stops at the starting line. Find the total distance and the displacement.

5. A circular park has a radius of 7 m. A child starts at a point and walks half a round along the boundary. Find the distance and the displacement. (Use π = 22/7.) What are they for a full round?

6. A man walks 12 m north and then 5 m east. Find the distance he walks and his displacement (magnitude and direction).

7. A ball is thrown vertically up from the ground, rises 5 m, and then falls back and is caught at the ground. Find the distance and displacement. Taking upward as positive, write the displacement with its sign.

8. Mr. Joshi walks from the post office to the temple (400 m east), then to the school (300 m north of the temple). Find his distance and displacement for this part. Then find them for the whole trip if he comes back by the same road.

9. Can the displacement of an object be larger than the distance it travelled? Can the distance be zero while displacement is non-zero? Explain.

10. State True or False:
    (a) Distance can be negative.
    (b) Displacement can be zero even when distance is not zero.
    (c) Displacement is a scalar quantity.
    (d) Distance and displacement have the same magnitude for motion in a straight line in one direction.
    (e) Mass is a vector quantity.

### Answers

1. **Distance** = path ki poori length (scalar, ≥ 0, kabhi ghat-ta nahi). **Displacement** = shuru se end ki seedhi doori direction ke saath (vector, + / − / 0 ho sakta hai). Farak: (i) distance scalar, displacement vector; (ii) distance kabhi negative nahi, displacement negative ya zero ho sakta hai; (iii) displacement magnitude ≤ distance.

2. East +. Distance = 8 + 3 = **11 m**. Displacement = (+8) + (−3) = **+5 m**, yaani 5 m east ki taraf.
   Jaanch: 8 − 3 = 5 ✅

3. Distance = 600 + 200 = **800 m**. Displacement: school ki direction ko + maano, to +600 m − 200 m = **+400 m**, yaani ghar se 400 m, school ki taraf.
   Jaanch: ghar se 600 m door tha, 200 m wapas aaya, 600 − 200 = 400 ✅

4. Distance = 2 × 400 = **800 m**. Displacement = **0 m** (start line par hi ruka).

5. Aadha chakkar: distance = πr = (22/7) × 7 = **22 m**. Displacement = 2r = 2 × 7 = **14 m** (shuru ke point se opposite point ki taraf).
   Poora chakkar: distance = 2πr = 2 × 22 = **44 m**, displacement = **0 m**.
   Jaanch: 14 m < 22 m ✅; 44 = 2 × 22 ✅

6. Distance = 12 + 5 = **17 m**. Displacement = √(12² + 5²) = √(144 + 25) = √169 = **13 m**, north-east ki taraf (north aur east ke beech).
   Jaanch: 13² = 169 = 144 + 25 ✅; 13 < 17 ✅

7. Distance = 5 m (upar) + 5 m (neeche) = **10 m**. Shuru aur end dono zameen par, to displacement = **0 m** (sign: +5 + (−5) = 0).
   Jaanch: +5 m + (−5 m) = 0 m ✅

8. School tak: distance = 400 + 300 = **700 m**; displacement = √(400² + 300²) = √250000 = **500 m**, north-east ki taraf. Poori trip (wapsi ke saath): distance = 400 + 300 + 300 + 400 = **1400 m**; displacement = **0 m**.
   Jaanch: 500² = 250000 = 160000 + 90000 ✅

9. Nahi. Displacement ka magnitude hamesha distance se chhota ya barabar hota hai, kyunki do point ke beech seedhi line sabse chhota raasta hai. Distance zero ka matlab cheez hili hi nahi, to displacement bhi zero hoga. Isliye "distance zero aur displacement non-zero" possible nahi hai.

10. (a) **False** ❌. Distance kabhi negative nahi hota.
    (b) **True** ✅. Jaise poora round trip.
    (c) **False** ❌. Displacement vector hai.
    (d) **True** ✅. Seedhi line, ek direction, bina mude.
    (e) **False** ❌. Mass scalar hai.
