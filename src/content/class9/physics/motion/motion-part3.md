# Topic 3: Speed aur Velocity

Pichhle topic mein humne seekha ki "kitna chale" (distance, sirf ek number) aur "shuruaat se ab tak seedhi doori kis taraf" (displacement, number ke saath direction) alag-alag cheezein hain. Ab ek natural sawaal uthta hai: **wo doori kitni jaldi tay hui?** Ek hi 100 m ko koi 10 second mein dauda, koi 100 second mein chala. Doori same, par "gati" bahut alag. Is topic mein hum is "jaldi" ko number dena seekhenge: **speed** aur **velocity**.

---

## Speed gun ka 108

Ek cricket academy mein subah ka practice chal raha tha. Naya tez gendbaaz **Aadi** apna ek zordaar delivery phenk kar hanpte hue wapas aaya. Coach **Mr. Bedi** ke haath mein speed gun thi, aur uski screen par likha tha: **108 km/h**.

Aadi ne screen dekhi aur bola, "Sir, 108 km/h to bahut zyada lagta hai, par mujhe ye feel nahi aa raha. Ye meter per second mein kitna hua? Aur pitch to sirf 20 m lambi hai. Gend ko wo 20 m tay karne mein kitna time lagta hoga? Kya 108 km/h wahi cheez hai jo poori pitch ka average speed hota?"

Mr. Bedi muskuraye. "Ek saath teen sawaal! Chalo ek-ek karke dekhte hain. Is kahani ko hum topic ke beech mein solve karenge. Pehle ye samajh lo ki speed ka matlab hota kya hai."

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed** | Kitni doori kitne time mein tay hui: distance ÷ time. Sirf number (scalar) | 30 m/s |

## Part A: Speed: "ek second mein kitni doori"

Speed ka seedha matlab hai: **ek second (ya ek ghante) mein kitni doori tay hoti hai.**

> **Speed = distance ÷ time**

- Distance sirf ek number hai (scalar), isliye speed bhi **scalar** hai: usme koi direction nahi.
- **SI unit:** metre per second, likhte hain **m/s** (kyunki metre ÷ second).
- Roz ki zindagi mein **km/h** (kilometre per hour) bhi bahut chalta hai. Gaadiyon ke board par yahi likha hota hai.

Jab hum kehte hain "speed 5 m/s", to matlab: **har second mein 5 metre**. Pehle second mein 5 m, doosre mein 5 m aur aage bhi aise hi (agar speed same rahe).

### km/h aur m/s ke beech conversion

Is conversion ka tareeka [measurement-and-units-part2.md](../measurement-and-units/measurement-and-units-part2.md) mein dekha tha. Yahan bas speed ke liye kaam ka nateeja:

- 1 km = 1000 m aur 1 h = 3600 s
- To **1 km/h = 1000 m ÷ 3600 s = 5/18 m/s** (lagbhag 0.278 m/s)

Isliye:

> **km/h se m/s:** 5/18 se guna karo (ya 3.6 se bhaag do)
> **m/s se km/h:** 18/5 se guna karo (ya 3.6 se guna karo)

**Example 1: Aadi ki speed gun (conversion wala hissa)**

Speed gun ne dikhaya 108 km/h. m/s mein?

- 108 km/h × 5/18 = 108 ÷ 18 × 5 = 6 × 5 = **30 m/s**

**Jaanch:** 30 m/s × 3.6 = 108 km/h ✅. Matlab Aadi ki gend **har second mein 30 metre** chalti hai.

Mr. Bedi ne kaha, "Dekha? 108 sunne mein bada lagta hai, par m/s mein 30 hai. Dono ek hi speed ke do naam hain, bas alag unit."

### Uniform speed aur variable speed

- **Uniform speed:** har barabar time mein barabar doori. Jaise ek cyclist jo har second 5 m chal raha hai.
- **Variable speed:** kabhi zyada, kabhi kam. Jaise traffic mein chalti bus: kabhi tez, kabhi dheemi, kabhi ruk gayi.

Aam zindagi mein lagbhag har yatra variable speed ki hoti hai. Isiliye "average speed" ki zaroorat padti hai (Part B).

### Speed ke teen basic sawaal

Speed = distance ÷ time se teen cheezein nikalti hain. Ek formula triangle jaisa sochna:

```
        distance
       -----------
        speed x time

   speed    = distance / time
   time     = distance / speed
   distance = speed x time
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Speed, distance aur time ka rishta: koi bhi do pata hon to teesra nikal sakte hain.

**Example 2: Speed nikalna**

Ek cyclist 900 m ka rasta 3 minute mein tay karta hai. Speed m/s mein?

- Time = 3 min = 3 × 60 = 180 s
- Speed = 900 m ÷ 180 s = **5 m/s**

**Jaanch:** 5 m/s × 180 s = 900 m ✅. Km/h mein: 5 × 3.6 = 18 km/h.

**Example 3: Time nikalna**

Ek car highway par 25 m/s ki constant speed se chal rahi hai. 2 km ka rasta kitne time mein tay hoga?

- Distance = 2 km = 2000 m
- Time = distance ÷ speed = 2000 m ÷ 25 m/s = **80 s**

**Jaanch:** 25 m/s × 80 s = 2000 m ✅. 80 s = 1 min 20 s.

**Example 4: Distance nikalna**

Ek aadmi 1.4 m/s ki speed se 15 minute tak chalta hai. Kitni doori tay hui?

- Time = 15 min = 15 × 60 = 900 s
- Distance = 1.4 m/s × 900 s = **1260 m** (= 1.26 km)

**Jaanch:** 1260 m ÷ 900 s = 1.4 m/s ✅

**Example 5: km/h aur m/s ke do aur conversions**

(a) 72 km/h m/s mein? (b) 15 m/s km/h mein?

- (a) 72 × 5/18 = 72 ÷ 18 × 5 = 4 × 5 = **20 m/s**
- (b) 15 × 18/5 = 15 ÷ 5 × 18 = 3 × 18 = **54 km/h**

**Jaanch:** (a) 20 m/s × 3.6 = 72 km/h ✅ (b) 54 ÷ 3.6 = 15 m/s ✅

### Kuch speeds jo "feel" dilaati hain (lagbhag values)

Ye sab **approximate** hain, aur situation ke hisaab se badalte hain:

| Cheez | Lagbhag speed (m/s) | Lagbhag speed (km/h) |
|---|---|---|
| Aaram se chalta insaan | 1.4 | 5 |
| Cycle chalata insaan | 5 | 18 |
| Highway par car | 25 | 90 |
| Aadi ki delivery | 30 | 108 |
| Hawa mein awaaz (sound) | 340 | lagbhag 1224 |
| Roshni (light) | 3 × 10⁸ | lagbhag 1.08 × 10⁹ |

Roshni itni tez hai ki ek second mein dharti ke saat se zyada chakkar laga le. Is feel se samajh aata hai ki Aadi ki 30 m/s tez zaroor hai, par bijli-si tez nahi.

## Part B: Average speed: poori yatra ki ek speed

Real yatra mein speed badalti rehti hai. To poori yatra ko ek number mein kaise bataye? Hum poori yatra ko aise maante hain jaise **ek hi constant speed se** tay hui ho. Wo speed hai **average speed**.

> **Average speed = total distance ÷ total time**

**Dhyan se:** ye "do speeds ka average" nahi hai. Ye **total distance ÷ total time** hai, aur total time mein **ruke hue samay (halt) bhi shamil** hote hain.

**Example 6: Jaana aur aana (pehli galti pakadne wala sawaal)**

Ek gaadi 60 km jaati hai 30 km/h ki speed se, aur wapas 60 km aati hai 60 km/h ki speed se. Poori yatra ki average speed?

- Jaane ka time = 60 km ÷ 30 km/h = 2 h
- Aane ka time = 60 km ÷ 60 km/h = 1 h
- Total distance = 60 km + 60 km = 120 km
- Total time = 2 h + 1 h = 3 h
- Average speed = 120 km ÷ 3 h = **40 km/h**

Aksar log seedha (30 + 60) ÷ 2 = 45 km/h likh dete hain. **Wo galat hai.** Kyun? Kyunki gaadi ne **kam speed par zyada time** bitaya (2 h) aur zyada speed par kam time (1 h). Zyada time kam speed ko zyada weight deta hai, isliye average 45 nahi, 40 aata hai.

**Jaanch:** 40 km/h × 3 h = 120 km ✅ (total distance wahi hai)

**Example 7: Walk plus run**

Aadi warm-up mein pehle 600 m **1 m/s** se chalta hai, phir 400 m **4 m/s** se daudta hai. Average speed?

- Chalne ka time = 600 m ÷ 1 m/s = 600 s
- Daudne ka time = 400 m ÷ 4 m/s = 100 s
- Total distance = 600 + 400 = 1000 m
- Total time = 600 + 100 = 700 s
- Average speed = 1000 m ÷ 700 s = **1.43 m/s** (lagbhag)

Do speeds ka seedha average (1 + 4) ÷ 2 = 2.5 m/s hota, jo galat hai. Aadi ne zyada time chalne mein bitaya, isliye average 1 m/s ke paas hi raha.

**Jaanch:** 1.43 m/s × 700 s ≈ 1000 m ✅

**Example 8: Beech mein ruka hua bus (halt wala sawaal)**

Ek bus pehle 90 km 1.5 h mein tay karti hai. Phir chai ke liye 30 min rukti hai. Phir aakhri 60 km 1 h mein tay karti hai. Average speed?

- Total distance = 90 + 60 = 150 km
- Total time = 1.5 h + 0.5 h (halt) + 1 h = **3 h**
- Average speed = 150 km ÷ 3 h = **50 km/h**

Agar halt ka time bhool jao: 150 ÷ 2.5 = 60 km/h, jo galat hai. Yatra ka time wo hai jo ghadi ne poori yatra mein dikhaya, ruka hua samay bhi usme hai.

**Jaanch:** 50 km/h × 3 h = 150 km ✅

## Part C: Velocity: speed jisme direction bhi hai

Ab Topic 2 ka displacement kaam aata hai.

> **Velocity = displacement ÷ time**

- Displacement vector hai (number + direction), isliye velocity bhi **vector** hai.
- **Unit wahi** hai: m/s (ya km/h). Farak bas ye ki velocity ke saath **direction** bhi batate hain, jaise "5 m/s east ki taraf".
- Seedhi line mein ek direction ko **positive (+)** aur ulti direction ko **negative (−)** maan lete hain. To velocity **positive ya negative** dono ho sakti hai. Negative ka matlab sirf itna ki wo ulti direction mein hai, "kam" nahi.

**Example 9: Negative velocity**

Maan lo east ko + liya. Ek ladki west ki taraf 10 m 5 s mein jaati hai. Uski velocity?

- Displacement = 10 m west = **−10 m**
- Velocity = −10 m ÷ 5 s = **−2 m/s** (matlab 2 m/s west ki taraf)
- Uski speed = 10 m ÷ 5 s = **2 m/s** (speed kabhi negative nahi hoti)

**Jaanch:** −2 m/s × 5 s = −10 m ✅

### Uniform velocity aur variable velocity

- **Uniform velocity:** barabar time mein barabar displacement, aur **direction bhi na badle**.
- **Variable velocity:** speed badle, ya direction badle, ya dono.

Is hisaab se ek car jo 60 km/h ki constant speed se mod (turn) leti hai, uski speed same hai, par velocity badal rahi hai (kyunki direction badal rahi hai).

### Average velocity

> **Average velocity = total displacement ÷ total time**

**Example 10: Aage-peeche ka round trip**

Ek seedhi sadak par Aadi 40 m east ki taraf 50 s mein chalta hai, phir wahi 40 m west ki taraf 30 s mein wapas aata hai, aur shuruaati jagah par pahunch jaata hai.

```
   start              40 m east
   O----------------------------->  (50 s)
   O<-----------------------------  (30 s, wapas)
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Seedhi line par aage 40 m aur wapas 40 m: Aadi wahin pahunch gaya jahan se chala tha.

- Total distance = 40 m + 40 m = 80 m
- Total time = 50 s + 30 s = 80 s
- **Average speed** = 80 m ÷ 80 s = **1 m/s**
- Total displacement = **0 m** (shuruaat aur ant ek hi jagah)
- **Average velocity** = 0 m ÷ 80 s = **0 m/s**

Matlab poori yatra mein Aadi chala bhi, speed 1 m/s bhi thi, par average velocity zero hai. Ye bilkul Topic 2 ke postman jaisa hai: round trip mein distance 1400 m, par displacement 0. Isliye **round trip mein average speed zero nahi hoti, par average velocity zero hoti hai.**

**Example 11: Aage-peeche, par poori wapasi nahi**

Ek seedhi line par ek ladka east ki taraf 60 m 40 s mein jaata hai, phir west ki taraf 20 m 20 s mein wapas aata hai. Average speed aur average velocity?

- Total distance = 60 + 20 = 80 m
- Displacement = 60 m east − 20 m west = **40 m east**
- Total time = 40 + 20 = 60 s
- Average speed = 80 m ÷ 60 s = **1.33 m/s** (lagbhag)
- Average velocity = 40 m ÷ 60 s = **0.67 m/s east ki taraf** (lagbhag)

**Jaanch:** average speed (1.33) average velocity ki magnitude (0.67) se zyada hai, kyunki distance (80 m) displacement (40 m) se zyada hai ✅

### Speed aur velocity: farak ek nazar mein

| Pehlu | Speed | Velocity |
|---|---|---|
| Formula | distance ÷ time | displacement ÷ time |
| Type | Scalar (sirf number) | Vector (number aur direction) |
| Negative ho sakti hai? | Nahi | Haan (direction ke hisaab se) |
| Round trip mein | Zero nahi | Average velocity zero |
| Unit | m/s | m/s |

### Kab speed aur velocity ki magnitude barabar hoti hai?

Jab object **seedhi line mein, ek hi direction mein chale, bina wapas mude** (Example 2 wali cycle ya Example 3 wali car). Tab distance = displacement, to speed aur velocity ki magnitude barabar hai. Agar object mud jaye ya wapas aaye, to distance zyada hota hai aur displacement kam, to average speed ≥ average velocity ki magnitude.

## Part D: Speedometer aur odometer

Car ke dashboard par do meter hote hain:

- **Speedometer:** abhi ki speed dikhata hai (jaise 60 km/h). Ye **speed** hai, direction nahi batata.
- **Odometer:** ab tak tay hui **total doori (distance)** dikhata hai (jaise 23 450 km). Ye distance hai, displacement nahi. Car ghar se nikal kar wapas aa jaye, odometer phir bhi badhta hai.

Dono kaam alag hain: ek "kitni tez" batata hai, doosra "kitna chale" batata hai.

## Part E: Aadi ki kahani ka jawab

Wapas academy chalte hain. Mr. Bedi ne Example 1 mein dekha ki 108 km/h = 30 m/s.

**Example 12: Pitch par gend ka time**

Pitch 20 m lambi hai. Agar gend ki speed 30 m/s hi rahe, to 20 m kitne time mein?

- Time = distance ÷ speed = 20 m ÷ 30 m/s = **0.67 s** (lagbhag, do decimal tak)

**Jaanch:** 30 m/s × 0.67 s = 20.1 m ≈ 20 m ✅

Yani batsman ke paas sochne ke liye ek second se bhi kam. Isiliye tez gend khelna mushkil hota hai.

**Aur Aadi ka doosra sawaal: 108 km/h aur average speed ek hi cheez hai?**

Mr. Bedi ne samjhaya, "Speed gun gend ke haath se chhootne ke thik baad ki speed dikhati hai. Hum yahan **saral banane** ke liye maan rahe hain ki gend poori pitch par wahi 30 m/s se gayi. Par asliyat mein hawa gend ko thoda rokti hai aur pitch par uchhalne se bhi gend ki speed kam ho jaati hai."

Maan lo stopwatch se naapa gaya ki 20 m tay karne mein asal mein 0.72 s lage.

- Average speed = 20 m ÷ 0.72 s = **27.8 m/s** (lagbhag)
- Km/h mein: 27.78 m/s × 3.6 = **100 km/h** (lagbhag)

**Jaanch:** 27.78 m/s × 0.72 s = 20 m ✅

To speed gun ka 108 km/h gend ke chhootne ke time ki speed thi, aur poori pitch ka **average speed** thoda kam, lagbhag 100 km/h, nikla. Dono alag cheezein hain: ek "us pal ki speed", doosra "poori doori ÷ poora time". Ye "us pal ki speed" wali baat kitni gehri hai, wo aage ki class mein; abhi bas itna yaad rakho ki average speed poori yatra ka hota hai. Aur ye bhi dhyan rahe: speed gun ne **speed** dikhayi, direction nahi, to wo speed hai, velocity nahi.

## Part F: Ek mushkil sawaal: train aur platform

**Example 13: Train ka platform cross karna**

200 m lambi train 90 km/h ki constant speed se 300 m lambe platform ko poora cross karti hai. Kitna time lagega?

Train "platform cross kar chuki" tab maani jaati hai jab uska **aakhri dabba** bhi platform se bahar ho jaye. Isliye train ko platform ki length **plus** apni length, dono tay karni padti hai.

```
   Shuru:    [=== train 200 m ===]|---- platform 300 m ----|

   Ant:                           |---- platform 300 m ----|[=== train ===]
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Train ka engine platform ke shuru par aaya aur aakhri dabba platform ke ant se nikla: total doori = train ki length + platform ki length.

- Total distance = 200 m + 300 m = **500 m**
- Speed = 90 km/h × 5/18 = 90 ÷ 18 × 5 = **25 m/s**
- Time = 500 m ÷ 25 m/s = **20 s**

**Jaanch:** 25 m/s × 20 s = 500 m ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Do speeds ka seedha average**

30 km/h aur 60 km/h ke liye (30 + 60) ÷ 2 = 45 likhna galat hai jab doori barabar ho. Sahi: total distance ÷ total time = 120 ÷ 3 = 40 km/h (Example 6).

**Galti 2: km/h aur m/s ko mix karna**

Distance 500 m aur speed 90 km/h ho to seedha 500 ÷ 90 mat karo. Pehle ek hi unit mein lao: 90 km/h = 25 m/s, phir 500 ÷ 25 = 20 s.

**Galti 3: Velocity ke liye distance lena**

Velocity mein **displacement** aata hai. Round trip mein distance 80 m tha, par displacement 0, to average velocity 0 (Example 10), 1 m/s nahi.

**Galti 4: Halt ko total time mein na jodna**

Bus ke 30 min ke halt ko bhool kar 150 ÷ 2.5 = 60 km/h likha, sahi 50 km/h hai (Example 8).

**Galti 5: Sochna ki velocity negative nahi ho sakti**

Negative velocity ka matlab sirf ulti direction hai. −2 m/s ka matlab 2 m/s west ki taraf, jab east positive ho. Speed hamesha positive ya zero hoti hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Speed = distance ÷ time**; scalar; SI unit **m/s**. "Ek second mein kitni doori."
- **1 km/h = 5/18 m/s**; km/h se m/s ke liye 3.6 se bhaag, m/s se km/h ke liye 3.6 se guna. Jaise 108 km/h = 30 m/s.
- **Uniform speed:** barabar time mein barabar doori. **Variable speed:** speed badalti rehti hai.
- **Average speed = total distance ÷ total time** (halt bhi time mein). Ye do speeds ka seedha average nahi hai: 60 km at 30 km/h aur 60 km at 60 km/h ka average 40 km/h.
- **Velocity = displacement ÷ time**; vector; seedhi line mein + ya − ho sakti hai.
- **Average velocity = total displacement ÷ total time.** Round trip mein 0, par average speed 0 nahi.
- Speed aur velocity ki magnitude tab barabar jab seedhi line mein bina wapas mude chalein.
- **Speedometer** speed dikhata hai, **odometer** ab tak tay hui doori.
- Approximate speeds: chalna 1.4 m/s, cycle 5 m/s, car 25 m/s, awaaz 340 m/s, roshni 3 × 10⁸ m/s.
- **Kahani ka jawab:** 108 km/h = 30 m/s; 20 m ka time = 0.67 s; poori pitch ka average speed thoda kam (lagbhag 100 km/h agar 0.72 s lage).

## Practice Questions

1. A cyclist covers 1.8 km in 6 minutes. Find her speed in m/s and in km/h.

2. Convert (a) 54 km/h into m/s and (b) 8 m/s into km/h.

3. A bus moves at a constant 20 m/s. How much time will it take to cover 3 km? Give the answer in seconds and in minutes.

4. Sound travels at about 340 m/s in air. How far does it travel in 4 s?

5. A car travels 100 km at 50 km/h and then another 100 km at 100 km/h. Find the average speed for the whole journey. Why is it not 75 km/h?

6. A student walks 1.2 km in 20 min, rests for 10 min, and then walks 0.6 km in 10 min. Find the average speed for the whole trip in km/h and in m/s.

7. On a straight track, a runner goes 100 m east in 20 s and then 40 m west in 10 s. Find (a) the average speed and (b) the average velocity for the whole 30 s.

8. A 150 m long train crosses a 450 m long bridge at 72 km/h. How long does it take to cross the bridge completely?

9. A runner completes one full lap of a 400 m circular track in 80 s, ending where she started. Find her average speed and her average velocity.

10. State True or False:
    (a) Speed can be negative.
    (b) Velocity can be negative.
    (c) Average velocity of a round trip is zero.
    (d) For equal distances at 20 km/h and 30 km/h, the average speed is 25 km/h.
    (e) The speedometer of a car shows the total distance it has covered.

### Answers

1. Distance = 1.8 km = 1800 m, time = 6 min = 360 s.
   Speed = 1800 m ÷ 360 s = **5 m/s**. Km/h mein: 5 × 3.6 = **18 km/h**.
   Jaanch: 5 m/s × 360 s = 1800 m ✅

2. (a) 54 × 5/18 = 54 ÷ 18 × 5 = 3 × 5 = **15 m/s**.
   (b) 8 × 18/5 = 8 × 3.6 = **28.8 km/h**.
   Jaanch: 15 × 3.6 = 54 ✅ aur 28.8 ÷ 3.6 = 8 ✅

3. Distance = 3 km = 3000 m. Time = 3000 m ÷ 20 m/s = **150 s** = **2.5 min**.
   Jaanch: 20 m/s × 150 s = 3000 m ✅

4. Distance = 340 m/s × 4 s = **1360 m** (lagbhag 1.36 km).
   Jaanch: 1360 ÷ 4 = 340 m/s ✅

5. Time 1 = 100 km ÷ 50 km/h = 2 h. Time 2 = 100 km ÷ 100 km/h = 1 h.
   Total distance = 200 km, total time = 3 h.
   Average speed = 200 ÷ 3 = **66.7 km/h** (lagbhag).
   75 isliye nahi: gaadi ne kam speed par zyada time (2 h) bitaya, to average 50 ke paas rehta hai.
   Jaanch: 66.7 km/h × 3 h ≈ 200 km ✅

6. Total distance = 1.2 + 0.6 = 1.8 km. Total time = 20 + 10 + 10 = 40 min = 2/3 h (rest bhi shamil).
   Average speed = 1.8 ÷ (2/3) = **2.7 km/h**. m/s mein: 2.7 ÷ 3.6 = **0.75 m/s**.
   Jaanch: 1800 m ÷ 2400 s = 0.75 m/s ✅

7. (a) Total distance = 100 + 40 = 140 m, total time = 20 + 10 = 30 s. Average speed = 140 ÷ 30 = **4.67 m/s** (lagbhag).
   (b) Displacement = 100 m east − 40 m west = 60 m east. Average velocity = 60 ÷ 30 = **2 m/s east ki taraf**.
   Jaanch: 2 m/s × 30 s = 60 m ✅

8. Total distance = 150 + 450 = 600 m. Speed = 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s.
   Time = 600 ÷ 20 = **30 s**.
   Jaanch: 20 m/s × 30 s = 600 m ✅

9. Distance = 400 m, time = 80 s: average speed = 400 ÷ 80 = **5 m/s**.
   Wo wahin pahunchi jahan se chali thi, to displacement = 0 aur average velocity = 0 ÷ 80 = **0 m/s**.
   Jaanch: speed 5 m/s ≠ 0, par velocity 0 ✅

10. (a) **False** ❌. Speed distance se banti hai, wo negative nahi hoti.
    (b) **True** ✅. Negative velocity ulti direction batati hai.
    (c) **True** ✅. Round trip mein displacement 0 hota hai.
    (d) **False** ❌. Maan lo har taraf d km: time = d/20 + d/30 = d/12 h, total distance 2d, to average = 2d ÷ (d/12) = **24 km/h**, 25 nahi.
    (e) **False** ❌. Speedometer abhi ki speed dikhata hai; total distance odometer dikhata hai.
