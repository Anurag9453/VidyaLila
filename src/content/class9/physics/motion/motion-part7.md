# Topic 7: Uniform Circular Motion

Ab tak humne jitni bhi motion dekhi, wo ek seedhi line mein thi: gaadi sadak par seedhi chali, gend seedhi gir gayi. Pichle topic mein humne uniform acceleration ke teen equations (v = u + at, s = ut + ½at², v² = u² + 2as) seekhe, aur wo sab seedhi line ke liye the. Par duniya mein bahut kuch seedha nahi chalta: pankha ghoomta hai, ghadi ki sui ghoomti hai, giant wheel ghoomta hai.

Is aakhri topic mein hum dekhenge ki jab cheez **gol ghumti hai** to kya alag hota hai. Sabse majedaar baat ye hai ki speed same rehne par bhi motion "accelerated" ho sakti hai. Ye chapter ka aakhri topic hai, to end mein poore chapter ka wrap-up bhi hoga.

## Kumhaar ka chaak aur rang-birangi mitti

Mr. Prajapati kumhaar hain. Unke chaak (potter's wheel) par mitti ka ek gola ghoom raha hai, aur wo us par ek sundar sa diya bana rahe hain. Bachhi Diya paas baithkar dekh rahi hai.

## Part A: Circular motion kya hai

Jab koi cheez aise chale ki uska path ek **circle** ho, to use **circular motion** kehte hain (Topic 1 mein rotational aur circular motion ka naam aaya tha). Aas-paas bahut examples hain:

- Kumhaar ka chaak, aur us par rakhi koi bhi boond
- Dhaage se bandha patthar, jise haath se goal ghumaya jaaye
- Ceiling fan ke blade ka sira (tip)
- Round-about par ghoomti hui car
- Ghadi ki sui (second, minute, hour hand)
- Chaand jo Earth ke chaaro taraf ghoomta hai (sirf naam, ye aage ki class mein)

### Uniform circular motion

Agar cheez circle par is tarah chale ki uski **speed har pal same** rahe, to use **uniform circular motion** kehte hain. "Uniform" ka matlab yahan speed ka same rehna hai, jaise Topic 3 mein uniform speed dekhi thi.

### Speed same, to velocity bhi same?

Yahan sabse zaroori baat aati hai. Topic 3 mein dekha tha ki **velocity = speed + direction**. Chaak par ghoomti boond ki speed (maano 1 m/s) har pal same hai. Par uski **disha har pal badalti hai**. To uski **velocity badalti rehti hai**, bhale hi speed na badle.

Aur Topic 4 mein dekha tha ki jab bhi velocity badalti hai (magnitude mein ya direction mein), to acceleration hoti hai. Isliye:

**Uniform circular motion accelerated motion hai**, bhale hi speed constant ho.

Is acceleration ki disha hamesha **circle ke kendra (centre) ki taraf** hoti hai. (Ise nikaalna aage ki class mein aayega; abhi sirf itna yaad rakho ki acceleration kendra ki taraf hai.) Aur jahan acceleration hai, wahan kisi cheez ko force bhi lagani padti hai, jo cheez ko circle mein rakhti hai. Dhaage se bandhe patthar mein ye force dhaage ki khichaav se aati hai, aur ye force bhi kendra ki taraf lagta hai. **Force chapter mein** hum isse theek se samjhenge.

### Velocity hamesha tangent ke saath

Circle ke kisi bhi point par cheez ki velocity ki disha us point par **tangent** ke saath hoti hai. Tangent matlab: wo seedhi line jo circle ko us point par sirf chhoo kar nikal jaaye (cut na kare).

Ek figure banate hain, jisme boond anticlockwise ghoom rahi hai:

```
                 B
        <--------*
               .   .
           .           .
   C      .       O       .      A
      v  *                 *  ^
           .           .
               .   .
                 *-------->
                 D
```

> **Figure 7.1** – Circle par ghoomti cheez (anticlockwise). Point A par velocity upar ki taraf, B par baayein, C par neeche ki taraf, D par daayein. Har point par velocity us point ke tangent ke saath hai, aur disha har pal badal rahi hai. Speed har jagah same hai.

Figure mein dekho: A, B, C, D chaaron jagah speed same hai, par arrow ki disha alag alag hai. Isi liye velocity badal rahi hai.

## Part B: Time period aur frequency

Chaak ko ghoomte dekhkar do sawaal natural hain: ek chakkar mein kitna time lagta hai? Aur ek second mein kitne chakkar lagte hain?

### Time period (T)

**Ek poora chakkar lagane mein jitna time lage, use time period (T) kehte hain.** Unit: **second (s)**.

### Frequency (f)

**Ek second mein jitne chakkar lagein, use frequency (f) kehte hain.** Unit: **hertz (Hz)**, aur **1 Hz = 1/s** (yaani ek chakkar har second).

### Dono ka rishta

Agar ek chakkar mein T seconds lagte hain, to ek second mein 1 ÷ T chakkar lagenge. Isliye:

**f = 1 ÷ T** aur **T = 1 ÷ f**

Jaise ek chakkar 0.5 s mein hota hai, to ek second mein 1 ÷ 0.5 s = 2 chakkar: f = 2 Hz.

### rpm aur Hz

Machine aur motor aksar **rpm** (rounds per minute) mein likhe jaate hain. 1 minute = 60 s, isliye:

**f (Hz) = rpm ÷ 60**   aur   **rpm = f (Hz) × 60**

Yaani **60 rpm = 1 Hz**.

### Example 1: Period aur frequency

Ek wheel 1 minute mein 120 chakkar lagata hai. T aur f nikalo.

1 minute = 60 s
f = 120 chakkar ÷ 60 s = **2 Hz**
T = 1 ÷ f = 1 ÷ 2 Hz = **0.5 s**

Jaanch: 0.5 s × 120 chakkar = 60 s = 1 minute ✅

### Example 2: rpm se Hz aur Hz se rpm

(a) Ek pankha 300 rpm par ghoomta hai. f aur T?
f = 300 ÷ 60 = **5 Hz**, T = 1 ÷ 5 Hz = **0.2 s**

(b) Ek motor 2 Hz par ghoomti hai. rpm?
rpm = 2 Hz × 60 = **120 rpm**

Jaanch: 120 rpm ÷ 60 = 2 Hz ✅ aur 5 Hz × 0.2 s = 1 ✅

## Part C: Circular motion mein speed

### Ek chakkar mein kitni doori?

Ek poora chakkar lagane mein cheez circle ki **circumference** ke barabar doori tay karti hai: 2πr (r = radius). Ye poora chakkar T seconds mein hota hai. To speed = doori ÷ time se:

**v = 2πr ÷ T**

Aur kyunki 1 ÷ T = f, ye aise bhi likha jaata hai:

**v = 2πr × f**

Yahan π = 22/7 ya 3.14 lete hain. Units: r metre mein, T second mein, to v **m/s** mein aayegi.

```
         . - - - .
      .'           '.
     /               \
    (        O--------*   r (centre se boond tak)
     \               /
      '.           .'
         ' - - - '

   Ek chakkar mein tay doori = circumference = 2πr
```

> **Figure 7.2** – Radius r wale circle ki circumference 2πr hoti hai. Ek chakkar mein cheez itni hi doori chalti hai, aur T seconds lagte hain.

### Example 3: Patthar ki speed

Dhaage se bandha patthar 0.5 m radius ke circle mein ghoomta hai. Ek chakkar mein T = 2 s lagte hain. Speed? (π = 3.14)

v = 2πr ÷ T = (2 × 3.14 × 0.5 m) ÷ 2 s = 3.14 m ÷ 2 s = **1.57 m/s**

Jaanch: 1.57 m/s × 2 s = 3.14 m = 2π × 0.5 m ✅

### Example 4: Pankhe ke blade ke sire ki speed

Ek ceiling fan 300 rpm par ghoomta hai aur blade ke sire (tip) ki centre se doori 0.6 m hai. Tip ki speed? (π = 3.14)

f = 300 ÷ 60 = 5 Hz
v = 2πr f = 2 × 3.14 × 0.6 m × 5 Hz = 3.768 m × 5 /s = **18.84 m/s**

Jaanch: T = 1 ÷ 5 = 0.2 s, to v = 3.768 m ÷ 0.2 s = 18.84 m/s ✅ (lagbhag 68 km/h, kaafi tez, isi liye ghoomte pankhe ko kabhi chhoona nahi chahiye)

### Example 5: Ghadi ke second hand ki speed

Ek ghadi ke second hand ki lambai 7 cm hai. Uske sire ki speed? (π = 22/7)

Second hand ek chakkar 60 s mein lagata hai, T = 60 s. r = 7 cm = 0.07 m.

v = 2πr ÷ T = (2 × 22/7 × 0.07 m) ÷ 60 s = 0.44 m ÷ 60 s = **0.00733 m/s** (lagbhag 0.0073 m/s, yaani 0.73 cm/s)

Jaanch: 0.00733 m/s × 60 s = 0.44 m = 2π × 0.07 m ✅

### Example 6: Race track

Ek gol race track ki radius 100 m hai. Ek gaadi ek lap 100 s mein poora karti hai. Speed? (π = 3.14)

v = 2πr ÷ T = (2 × 3.14 × 100 m) ÷ 100 s = 628 m ÷ 100 s = **6.28 m/s**

Jaanch: 6.28 m/s × 100 s = 628 m ✅

### Example 7: Diameter di ho to

Ek merry-go-round ka diameter 6 m hai aur wo 12 s mein ek chakkar lagata hai. Kinare par baithe bachche ki speed? (π = 3.14)

Dhyan do: diameter diya hai, radius nahi. r = 6 m ÷ 2 = 3 m.

v = 2πr ÷ T = (2 × 3.14 × 3 m) ÷ 12 s = 18.84 m ÷ 12 s = **1.57 m/s**

Jaanch: 1.57 m/s × 12 s = 18.84 m = 2π × 3 m ✅

### Example 8: Frequency se alag cheez nikalna

Ek stone 0.5 m radius ke circle mein 3.14 m/s ki speed se ghoom raha hai. f aur T? (π = 3.14)

v = 2πr f, to f = v ÷ (2πr) = 3.14 m/s ÷ (2 × 3.14 × 0.5 m) = 3.14 m/s ÷ 3.14 m = **1 Hz**
T = 1 ÷ f = **1 s**

Jaanch: v = 2 × 3.14 × 0.5 m × 1 Hz = 3.14 m/s ✅

## Part D: Distance aur displacement circle par

Topic 2 mein dekha tha ki distance path ki lambai hai (scalar), aur displacement shuru aur ant point ke beech ki seedhi line hai (vector). Circle par ye farq bahut saaf dikhta hai.

### Example 9: Aadha chakkar aur poora chakkar

Race track (r = 100 m, T = 100 s, π = 3.14) par gaadi ek point se shuru karti hai.

**Aadha chakkar** (50 s mein):
- Distance = aadhi circumference = πr = 3.14 × 100 m = **314 m**
- Displacement = diameter = 2r = 2 × 100 m = **200 m** (shuru ke point se theek saamne wale point ki taraf seedhi line)
- Average speed = 314 m ÷ 50 s = **6.28 m/s**
- Average velocity = 200 m ÷ 50 s = **4 m/s** (displacement ki disha mein)

**Poora chakkar** (100 s mein):
- Distance = 2πr = 2 × 3.14 × 100 m = **628 m**
- Displacement = **0 m** (gaadi wahin wapas aa gayi jahan se chali thi)
- Average speed = 628 m ÷ 100 s = **6.28 m/s**
- Average velocity = 0 m ÷ 100 s = **0 m/s**

Jaanch: 314 m ÷ 50 s = 6.28 ✅, 200 ÷ 50 = 4 ✅, 628 ÷ 100 = 6.28 ✅

Dhyan do: poore chakkar mein average velocity 0 hai, par gaadi har pal 6.28 m/s se chal rahi thi. Average velocity displacement se aati hai, aur displacement 0 hai.

## Part E: Chhootne par cheez kahan jaati hai?

Dhaage se bandha patthar ghoom raha hai. Dhaaga cheez ko kendra ki taraf kheench raha hai (wahi force jiski baat upar hui), aur isi se patthar circle mein rehta hai. Ab agar dhaaga **achanak chhoot jaaye ya kat jaaye**, to kendra ki taraf khichne wala force khatam ho jaata hai. Cheez ko ab kuch circle mein nahi rakh raha.

To patthar wahi karega jo wo us pal kar raha tha: apni **us waqt ki velocity** ki disha mein seedha jaayega. Aur wo disha **tangent** ki hoti hai.

```
              P (patthar yahan se chhoota)
              *------------------------>  seedhi line (tangent)
        . - '   ' - .
      .'             '.
     /                 \
    (         O         )
     \                 /
      '.             .'
        ' - . _ . - '
```

> **Figure 7.3** – Patthar clockwise ghoom raha hai. Jis point P par dhaaga chhoota, wahan se patthar circle ke tangent ke saath seedha nikal jaata hai. Wo kendra se door seedha radius ki line par nahi jaata, aur wapas circle mein bhi nahi mudta.

(Asal mein patthar gravity ki wajah se thoda neeche bhi girta hai, par abhi sirf horizontal disha dekh rahe hain.)

Isi tarah kuch cheezein jo tangent par udti hain: gile kapde ghoomte hue washing machine ke drum se paani ki boondein, hammer-throw mein chhoota hua hammer, aur tez mudti cycle ke tyres se uchhalti keechad ke chheente.

## Part F: Kahani ka hisaab

Chaak 60 rpm par ghoom raha hai. Diya ne laal boond centre se 0.15 m door lagayi hai. (π = 3.14)

**Frequency aur time period:**
f = 60 ÷ 60 = **1 Hz**
T = 1 ÷ 1 Hz = **1 s**

**Boond ki speed:**
v = 2πr ÷ T = (2 × 3.14 × 0.15 m) ÷ 1 s = 0.942 m ÷ 1 s = **0.942 m/s ≈ 0.94 m/s**

Jaanch: 0.942 m/s × 1 s = 0.942 m = 2π × 0.15 m ✅

Mr. Prajapati ne kaha, "Dekha Diya? Boond ek second mein ek poora chakkar lagati hai aur lagbhag 0.94 m chalti hai. Speed same hai, par har pal disha badalti hai, to velocity badalti hai. Iske liye hi kendra ki taraf force chahiye."

Diya ne poochha, "Aur agar boond chhoot jaaye to?" Mr. Prajapati ne kaha, "Jis point par boond hai, wahan chaak ke kinare par ek seedhi line kheencho jo kinare ko sirf chhooti ho. Usi line par boond seedha udegi, tangent par. Isi liye mitti ke kaam ke waqt hum chaak ko dheere ghumate hain, nahi to boond bahar ud jaati hai."

Diya ne dekha: ab wo samajh gayi ki "same raftaar" aur "same velocity" mein kitna bada farq hota hai.

## Part G: Linear aur circular motion ki tulna

| Baat | Linear (seedhi line) motion | Uniform circular motion |
|---|---|---|
| Path | Seedhi line | Circle |
| Speed | Same ya badalti | Same (uniform) |
| Velocity ki disha | Same (agar line na badle) | Har pal badalti hai (tangent ke saath) |
| Acceleration (constant speed par) | 0 | Hai, kendra ki taraf |
| Ek poore round ka displacement | Alag point par pahunch gaye, to non-zero | 0 |
| Speed ka formula | v = doori ÷ time | v = 2πr ÷ T = 2πr f |
| Chhodne par | Wahi seedhi disha mein jaata rahega | Tangent ki disha mein seedha jaata hai |

---

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Speed constant hai, to acceleration 0 hogi"**
Seedhi line mein ye sahi hai. Circle mein velocity ki disha badalti hai, isliye velocity badalti hai aur acceleration hoti hai (kendra ki taraf). Boond ki speed 0.94 m/s same thi, phir bhi accelerated motion thi.

**Galti 2: Diameter ko radius ki jagah daal dena**
Merry-go-round ka diameter 6 m tha. Agar 2π × 6 m use karte to 37.68 m aata aur v = 37.68 ÷ 12 = 3.14 m/s milti, jo **asli 1.57 m/s ka double** hai. Pehle radius = diameter ÷ 2 = 3 m nikalo.

**Galti 3: rpm ko seedha Hz ki tarah use karna**
Pankha 300 rpm hai. Agar f = 300 Hz maan lo to speed 60 guna zyada aayegi. Sahi: f = 300 ÷ 60 = 5 Hz. Hamesha rpm ko 60 se bhaag do.

**Galti 4: Chhootne par cheez ko radially bahar jaata hua batana**
Cheez kendra se seedha door (radius ki line par) nahi jaati. Wo **tangent** ki disha mein jaati hai, yaani radius ke 90° (perpendicular) par. Figure 7.3 dekho.

**Galti 5: Poore chakkar mein displacement ko 2πr batana**
2πr to **distance** hai. Poore chakkar mein shuru aur ant ka point same hai, to displacement **0** hai, aur average velocity bhi 0.

## Quick Recap

- **Circular motion:** path circle ho. **Uniform circular motion:** speed har pal same.
- Velocity hamesha **tangent** ke saath hoti hai, to uski disha har pal badalti hai. Isliye uniform circular motion **accelerated** hai; acceleration kendra ki taraf (aur ise rakhne ke liye force bhi kendra ki taraf, Force chapter mein).
- **Time period T:** ek chakkar ka time (s). **Frequency f:** ek second mein chakkar (Hz = 1/s). **f = 1 ÷ T**.
- **60 rpm = 1 Hz**, f (Hz) = rpm ÷ 60.
- **v = 2πr ÷ T = 2πr f** (r radius hai, diameter nahi). π = 22/7 ya 3.14.
- Aadhe chakkar mein distance πr aur displacement 2r. Poore chakkar mein distance 2πr aur displacement 0.
- Chhootne par cheez **tangent** ki disha mein seedhi jaati hai.

## Practice Questions

1. A wheel makes 180 rounds in one minute. Find its frequency in Hz and its time period in seconds (give T to two decimal places).

2. A toy on a turntable takes 0.25 s for one round. Find its frequency in Hz and in rpm.

3. A stone tied to a string is whirled in a circle of radius 0.4 m at a frequency of 2 Hz. Find the speed of the stone. Take π = 3.14.

4. The tip of a fan blade is 0.5 m from the centre. The fan runs at 600 rpm. Find the speed of the tip. Take π = 3.14.

5. The minute hand of a wall clock is 14 cm long. Find the speed of its tip in cm/s (π = 22/7, give the answer to 4 decimal places).

6. A circular track has a radius of 50 m. A runner starts at one point and runs half a round and then a full round. Find the distance and displacement in both cases. Take π = 3.14.

7. A merry-go-round has a diameter of 4 m and takes 5 s for one round. Find the speed of a child sitting on its edge. Take π = 3.14.

8. A car goes round a roundabout of radius 10 m at a constant speed of 6.28 m/s. Find the time it takes for one round. Take π = 3.14.

9. State whether each statement is true or false, with a reason.
   (a) A body moving in a circle at constant speed has zero acceleration.
   (b) A stone released from a whirling string moves along the radius, away from the centre.
   (c) After one complete round, the displacement is zero but the distance is not.

10. Explain in your own words why the velocity of a clay drop on a wheel keeps changing even though its speed is constant. In which direction does it move if it is released?

### Answers

1. f = 180 rounds ÷ 60 s = **3 Hz**. T = 1 ÷ 3 Hz = 0.3333... s ≈ **0.33 s**.
   Jaanch: 0.33 s × 180 rounds = 59.4 s ≈ 60 s ✅

2. f = 1 ÷ T = 1 ÷ 0.25 s = **4 Hz**. rpm = 4 × 60 = **240 rpm**.
   Jaanch: 240 ÷ 60 = 4 Hz ✅

3. v = 2πr f = 2 × 3.14 × 0.4 m × 2 Hz = 2.512 m × 2 /s = **5.024 m/s** (lagbhag 5.02 m/s).
   Jaanch: T = 1 ÷ 2 = 0.5 s, v = 2.512 m ÷ 0.5 s = 5.024 m/s ✅

4. f = 600 ÷ 60 = 10 Hz. v = 2 × 3.14 × 0.5 m × 10 Hz = 3.14 m × 10 /s = **31.4 m/s**.
   Jaanch: T = 0.1 s, v = 3.14 m ÷ 0.1 s = 31.4 m/s ✅

5. T = 60 min = 3600 s. v = 2πr ÷ T = (2 × 22/7 × 14 cm) ÷ 3600 s = 88 cm ÷ 3600 s = **0.0244 cm/s** (lagbhag).
   Jaanch: 0.0244 cm/s × 3600 s = 87.84 cm ≈ 88 cm ✅

6. Half round: distance = πr = 3.14 × 50 m = **157 m**; displacement = 2r = 2 × 50 m = **100 m**.
   Full round: distance = 2πr = 2 × 3.14 × 50 m = **314 m**; displacement = **0 m**.
   Jaanch: 157 × 2 = 314 ✅

7. r = 4 m ÷ 2 = 2 m. v = (2 × 3.14 × 2 m) ÷ 5 s = 12.56 m ÷ 5 s = **2.512 m/s** (lagbhag 2.51 m/s).
   Jaanch: 2.512 m/s × 5 s = 12.56 m = 2π × 2 m ✅

8. T = 2πr ÷ v = (2 × 3.14 × 10 m) ÷ 6.28 m/s = 62.8 m ÷ 6.28 m/s = **10 s**.
   Jaanch: 6.28 m/s × 10 s = 62.8 m ✅

9. (a) **False** ❌. Speed same hai par velocity ki disha badalti hai, isliye acceleration hai (kendra ki taraf).
   (b) **False** ❌. Cheez tangent ki disha mein jaati hai, radius ki line par nahi.
   (c) **True** ✅. Displacement = 0 (shuru aur ant point same), distance = 2πr jo 0 nahi hai.

10. Velocity mein speed aur direction dono aate hain. Boond ki speed same rehti hai, par circle par har point par uski disha (tangent) alag hoti hai, isliye velocity badalti rehti hai. Chhootne par wo us point ke **tangent** ki disha mein seedhi jaati hai.

| Time period (T) | Ek chakkar ka time | second (s) |
| Frequency (f) | Ek second mein chakkar | hertz (Hz = 1/s) |
