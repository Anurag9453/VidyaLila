# Topic 1: Rest aur Motion: Kaun Chal Raha Hai?

Pichhle chapter mein humne seekha ki cheezein **naapi kaise jaati hain**: metre, second, kilogram jaise units, ruler aur stopwatch jaise tools, aur galtiyon ka hisaab. Ab in tools ka asli use shuru hota hai. Is chapter mein hum dekhenge ki cheezein **chalti kaise hain**, aur unki chaal ko metre aur second ki bhasha mein kaise bataya jaata hai. Pehla sawaal sabse seedha hai, aur thoda tricky bhi: **kaun chal raha hai, aur kaun ruka hua hai?**

---

## Metro mein Tanishka ka sawaal

Mr. Kapoor apni bhanji Tanishka ko metro se naani ke ghar le ja rahe the. Dono train ke ek dibbe mein khidki ke paas baithe the. Tanishka ke bagal wali seat par ek aur yatri baithe the.

Train abhi platform par khadi thi. Tanishka khidki se platform ke khambhe aur chai ke stall ko dekh rahi thi. Phir ek halki si ghanti bajee aur train chal padi.

Tanishka ek dum bol padi, "Mama, dekhiye! Platform peeche ki taraf bhaag raha hai!"

Mr. Kapoor muskuraye. "Platform to apni jagah par hai, beta. Train aage chal rahi hai."

Tanishka ne sir hilaya, par phir uski nazar apne bagal wale yatri par gayi. "Mama, ek aur baat. Ye uncle to bilkul ruke hue lag rahe hain. Na aage jaa rahe, na peeche. Lekin agar train chal rahi hai, to wo bhi to chal rahe honge na?"

Mr. Kapoor ne kaha, "Bahut accha sawaal hai. Platform par khade guard se poochho to wo kahega ki tum, main aur ye uncle teeno tez chal rahe hain. Tumhe lagta hai ki uncle ruke hain. Ab bolo, **sach kya hai?** Uncle chal rahe hain ya ruke hain?"

Tanishka confuse ho gayi. Is kahani ko hum topic ke end mein poora solve karenge. Pehle "chalna" aur "ruka hona" ka asli matlab samajhte hain.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Motion (gati)** | Jab kisi cheez ki position samay ke saath badalti hai | Chalti hui metro |
| **Rest (sthirta)** | Jab kisi cheez ki position samay ke saath nahi badalti | Platform par khada khamba |
| **Reference point (origin)** | Wo cheez ya jagah jisse hum position naapte hain aur jisse tulna karte hain | Bus stop, platform |
| **Position** | Origin se ek seedhi line par kitni doori par, aur kis taraf | Bus stop se +100 m |
| **Relative** | Kisi doosri cheez ke hisaab se, uske sandarbh mein | Platform ke relative train chal rahi hai |
| **Linear motion** | Seedhi line mein chalna | Seedhi sadak par chalti car |
| **Circular motion** | Gol raste par chalna | Gol chakkar lagata dhawak |
| **Rotational motion** | Apni hi dhuri (axis) par ghoomna | Ghoomta pankha |
| **Oscillatory motion** | Ek hi raste par idhar-udhar aana-jaana | Jhoola |
| **Uniform motion** | Barabar samay mein barabar doori tay karna | Har second mein 10 m |
| **Non-uniform motion** | Barabar samay mein alag-alag doori tay karna | Kabhi 5 m, kabhi 12 m |

## Part A: Motion aur rest kya hain

### Motion ki pehchaan

Ek simple niyam yaad rakho:

> Agar kisi cheez ki **position samay ke saath badal rahi hai**, to wo **motion** mein hai.
> Agar position **samay ke saath nahi badal rahi**, to wo **rest** par hai.

Lekin "position badal rahi hai" kis ke hisaab se? Position hamesha kisi **doosri cheez ke hisaab se** batayi jaati hai. Us doosri cheez ko **reference point** (ya origin) kehte hain. Isi wajah se pehla sawaal hamesha ye hona chahiye: **"Kis ke relative?"**

### Platform aur train

Platform ke khambe ko reference maan lo.
- Train ki position khambe se badal rahi hai: train **motion** mein hai.
- Khamba apne aap mein wahin ka wahin hai: khambe ki position khambe se nahi badal rahi, wo **rest** par hai.

### Rest aur motion relative hote hain

Ab Tanishka ki seat ko reference maan lo.
- Platform ka khamba seat se door hota ja raha hai (aur peeche ki taraf). Seat ke relative khamba **motion** mein hai.
- Bagal wale uncle seat ke bagal mein hi hain, aur wahi doori bani hui hai. Seat ke relative uncle **rest** par hain.

Yaani ek hi cheez ek reference ke hisaab se motion mein ho sakti hai aur doosre ke hisaab se rest par. Isi ko kehte hain ki **rest aur motion relative hote hain**. "Poori tarah ruka hua" ya "poori tarah chalta hua" jaisi koi cheez nahi hoti, jab tak ye na bataya jaaye ki kis ke relative. (Aage ki class mein is reference ko hi **frame of reference** kehte hain.)

### Aur kuch jaane-pehchane udaharan

| Situation | Kis ke relative | Motion ya rest |
|---|---|---|
| Bus mein baitha yatri | Bus ki seat | Rest |
| Wahi yatri | Sadak ke kinare ka ped | Motion |
| Chalti bus se dikhta ped | Bus ka yatri | Motion (peeche jaata hua lagta hai) |
| Wahi ped | Zameen | Rest |
| Chalti bus mein hath mein pakda hua bag | Bus ka driver | Rest |
| Platform par khada guard | Chalti train ka yatri | Motion (peeche jaata hua lagta hai) |

Dhyan do ki ped ko hum "ruka hua" bolte hain kyunki hum aam taur par **zameen** ko reference maan lete hain. Jab kuch bataya nahi jaata, to reference zameen hi hota hai.

## Part B: Position batana: origin aur number line

Position batana ek address batane jaisa hai. "Wo bus stop se aage hai" adhoori baat hai, kitna aage? Aur kis taraf? Seedhi line par ye kaam bahut aasaan hai: ek **number line** bana lo.

1. Ek jagah ko **origin** (zero) chuno.
2. Origin ki ek taraf ko **positive** (+) maano, doosri taraf ko **negative** (−).
3. Doori metre mein naapo.

Maan lo ek seedhi sadak par ye cheezein hain. Bus stop ko origin chuna, aur daayin taraf ko positive.

```
  -60 m        0          +40 m              +100 m
    S          B             L                  D
 ---+----------+-------------+------------------+--->  (+ taraf)
  School     Bus stop     Lamp-post           Dukaan
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Seedhi sadak par number line: origin bus stop (B) par hai. School 60 m baayin taraf (negative), lamp-post 40 m aur dukaan 100 m daayin taraf (positive).

Positions (bus stop ke relative):
- Bus stop: **0 m**
- Lamp-post: **+40 m**
- Dukaan: **+100 m**
- School: **−60 m** (negative ka matlab: origin ki baayin taraf)

### Origin badalne se position badalti hai

Ab lamp-post ko origin maan lo (daayin taraf phir bhi positive). Har cheez ki nayi position = purani position − 40 m.

- Bus stop: 0 − 40 = **−40 m**
- Lamp-post: 40 − 40 = **0 m**
- Dukaan: 100 − 40 = **+60 m**
- School: −60 − 40 = **−100 m**

**Jaanch:** Dukaan aur school ke beech ki doori dono origins mein ek hi honi chahiye. Pehle origin se: 100 − (−60) = 160 m. Doosre origin se: 60 − (−100) = 160 m ✅

Sabak: **cheez wahi, sadak wahi, par origin badla to position ka number badal gaya.** Isliye position batate waqt hamesha origin batao: "bus stop se +100 m".

## Part C: Motion ke prakar (raste ke hisaab se)

Cheezein alag-alag tareeke se chalti hain. Raste ke hisaab se motion ke kuch aam prakar ye hain:

| Prakar | Pehchaan | Udaharan |
|---|---|---|
| **Linear (seedhi line mein)** | Raasta seedhi line | Seedhi sadak par chalti car, gira hua patthar |
| **Circular (gol raste par)** | Kisi centre ke chaaron taraf gol raasta | Stadium mein gol track par daudta athlete |
| **Rotational (apni dhuri par)** | Cheez khud ghoomti hai, jagah wahin rehti hai | Ghoomta pankha, lattu |
| **Oscillatory / vibratory (idhar-udhar)** | Ek hi raste par aage-peeche baar-baar | Jhoola, ghadi ka pendulum |
| **Random (anirdharit)** | Koi tay raasta ya pattern nahi | Bheed mein ghoomta bachcha, udti hui titli |

(Rotational mein ghoomti cheez ka har hissa gol chakkar lagata hai, par cheez poori ek jagah tiki rehti hai. Circular mein cheez ek jagah se doosri jagah gol raste par jaati hai.)

### Chalo sort karte hain

| Udaharan | Prakar |
|---|---|
| Seedhi sadak par chalti bike | Linear |
| Ghoomta hua ceiling fan | Rotational |
| Park ka jhoola | Oscillatory |
| Gol track par daudta dhawak | Circular |
| Seedhi pitch par phenki gayi cricket ball (seedhi line mein) | Linear |
| Deewar ghadi ka pendulum | Oscillatory |
| Mela mein bheed mein idhar-udhar ghoomta bachcha | Random |
| Giri hui patang jo hawa mein ladkhadati hai | Random |
| Giant wheel par baitha insaan (wheel ke centre ke chaaron taraf) | Circular |
| Gitar ka taar chhedne par | Oscillatory (vibratory) |

Dhyan do: ek hi cheez mein ek se zyada motion bhi ho sakte hain. Chalte hue cycle ka pahiya apni dhuri par ghoomta hai (rotational), aur saath mein cycle ke saath aage seedhi line mein bhi jaata hai (linear).

## Part D: Uniform aur non-uniform motion

Ab ek car ko dekhte hain jo seedhi sadak par chal rahi hai. Har **1 second** mein hum uski doori note karte hain.

| Samay ka interval | Car A ne tay ki doori | Car B ne tay ki doori |
|---|---|---|
| 0 se 1 s | 10 m | 5 m |
| 1 se 2 s | 10 m | 12 m |
| 2 se 3 s | 10 m | 8 m |
| 3 se 4 s | 10 m | 15 m |

- **Car A:** har 1 s mein 10 m. Barabar samay mein **barabar doori**. Ye **uniform motion** hai.
- **Car B:** kabhi 5 m, kabhi 12 m, kabhi 8 m, kabhi 15 m. Barabar samay mein **alag-alag doori**. Ye **non-uniform motion** hai.

Ab 4 s ke baad kul doori dekhte hain, jahan se chali thi wahan se:

| Samay | Car A ki kul doori | Car B ki kul doori |
|---|---|---|
| 1 s | 10 m | 5 m |
| 2 s | 20 m | 17 m |
| 3 s | 30 m | 25 m |
| 4 s | 40 m | 40 m |

**Jaanch:** Car A: 10 + 10 + 10 + 10 = 40 m ✅. Car B: 5 + 12 + 8 + 15 = 40 m ✅.

Dono 4 s ke end mein 40 m par pahunchi, par raaste mein unka tarika alag tha. Car A ek jaisi chali, Car B kabhi dheere, kabhi tez. Aur ek baat: "kitni tez chal rahi hai" is baat ko ek number mein kaise bataya jaaye, uski baat agle topics mein.

Ye bhi dhyan rakho: yahan uniform ka matlab sirf itna hai ki **barabar samay mein barabar doori**.

Roz ki zindagi mein poori tarah uniform motion bahut kam milta hai. Ghadi ki second wali suyi lagbhag uniform hai. Auto traffic mein chalta hai to non-uniform hota hai: rukta hai, chalta hai, tez hota hai.

## Part E: Ek halki si jhalak: scalar aur vector

Kuch cheezein sirf ek number (aur unit) se poori bata di jaati hain. Jaise "ek bottle mein 2 litre paani" ya "class mein 40 bachche": iske baad kuch poochne ki zaroorat nahi. Lekin kuch cheezein ek number se adhoori rehti hain, unke saath **disha (direction)** bhi batani padti hai. Jaise "main bus stop se 100 m door hoon" adhoori baat hai, kyunki 100 m daayin taraf ya baayin taraf? Isi liye humne Part B mein + aur − ka use kiya. Iska poora treatment Topic 2 mein hoga.

## Metro ki kahani solve karte hain

Ab Tanishka ke sawaal par wapas aate hain. Platform ke ek khambe ko reference maan kar train ki position note ki, jis jagah Tanishka ki seat train ke start hone par thi wo 0 m hai, aur train ki disha ko positive maana.

| Samay | Tanishka ki seat ki position (platform ke relative) | Bagal wale yatri ki seat ki position (platform ke relative) | Yatri ki position Tanishka ke relative |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 s | 0 m | 0.5 m | 0.5 m |
| 2 s | 3 m | 3.5 m | 0.5 m |
| 4 s | 10 m | 10.5 m | 0.5 m |

**Platform ke relative:** Tanishka ki position 0, 3, 10 m: badal rahi hai. Bagal wale yatri ki 0.5, 3.5, 10.5 m: wo bhi badal rahi hai. Dono **motion** mein hain. Ye wahi hai jo guard ko dikhta hai.

**Tanishka ke relative:** bagal wale yatri ki position 0.5 m, 0.5 m, 0.5 m: **nahi badal rahi**. Isliye Tanishka ke hisaab se wo **rest** par hain.

**Jaanch:** 3.5 − 3 = 0.5 m ✅ aur 10.5 − 10 = 0.5 m ✅.

Aur Tanishka ko platform peeche kyun dikha? Tanishka ko reference maane to platform ki position uski seat ke relative badal rahi hai, to platform uske hisaab se **motion** mein hai (peeche ki taraf). Dono baatein sach hain:

- Platform ke relative: train aur uske andar ke sab log motion mein.
- Train ke andar baithe yatri ke relative: platform motion mein, aur bagal ka yatri rest par.

Mr. Kapoor ne Tanishka se kaha, "Beta, **sach ek hi nahi hai, ye depend karta hai ki tum kis ke hisaab se dekh rahi ho.**" Aur Tanishka ka sawaal "uncle chal rahe hain ya ruke hain?" ka sahi jawab hai: **platform ke relative chal rahe hain, mere relative ruke hain.**

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**1. Rest ko absolute (sabke liye same) maan lena.**
"Ped ruka hua hai" kehna adhoora hai. Zameen ke relative ruka hua hai, par chalti bus ke yatri ke relative wo peeche jaata hua dikhta hai. ❌ "Ped hamesha rest par hota hai." ✅ "Zameen ke relative ped rest par hai."

**2. Reference point bhool jaana.**
"Meri position 50 m hai" bina origin ke bekaar hai. Hamesha likho: "Bus stop se +50 m."

**3. Circular motion aur oscillation ko ek samajhna.**
Circular motion mein cheez gol raste par jaati hai (ek hi taraf ghoomte hue wapas shuru ke point par aati hai). Oscillation mein cheez ek hi raste par idhar-udhar aati-jaati hai (jaise jhoola). Dono baar-baar dohrate hain, par raasta alag hai.

**4. Uniform motion ko kuch aur samajh lena.**
Is topic mein uniform ka matlab sirf **barabar samay mein barabar doori** hai. Agar car pehle second 10 m aur doosre second 12 m chale, to wo uniform nahi hai, chahe "dono second mein bahut tez" ho.

**5. Rotational aur circular ko mila dena.**
Ghoomta pankha rotational hai (wo khud ghoom raha hai, jagah wahin hai). Gol track par daudta athlete circular hai (wo ek jagah se doosri jagah jaata hai).

## Quick Recap

- Position samay ke saath badle to **motion**, na badle to **rest**.
- Position hamesha kisi **reference point (origin)** ke relative batayi jaati hai.
- **Rest aur motion relative hote hain**: ek hi cheez ek ke hisaab se chal sakti hai, doosre ke hisaab se ruki.
- Seedhi line par position number line se batate hain: origin zero, ek taraf +, doosri taraf −.
- Origin badalne par position ka number badal jaata hai, par do cheezon ke beech ki doori nahi badalti.
- Raste ke hisaab se motion: **linear, circular, rotational, oscillatory (vibratory), random**.
- **Uniform motion:** barabar samay mein barabar doori. **Non-uniform:** barabar samay mein alag-alag doori.
- Kuch cheezein sirf number se, kuch number aur disha dono se batayi jaati hain (Topic 2 mein).

## Practice Questions

1. A passenger is sitting inside a moving bus. State whether she is at rest or in motion relative to (a) the bus driver, (b) a tree on the roadside, (c) another passenger sitting in the last row.
2. Mohit is sitting in a train that is moving. He drops a pen on the floor of the train. Relative to Mohit, is the pen at rest or in motion before it is dropped? Relative to the platform?
3. On a straight road, a bus stop is taken as the origin and the right side is positive. A temple is at +80 m and a tea stall is at −30 m. (a) Give the position of each relative to the bus stop. (b) How far apart are the temple and the tea stall (the gap between their positions)?
4. In Question 3, the temple is now chosen as the new origin (right side still positive). Find the new positions of the bus stop and the tea stall. Check that the gap between the positions of the bus stop and the tea stall is the same in both cases.
5. Classify each as linear, circular, rotational, oscillatory or random: (a) a child on a swing, (b) a spinning top, (c) a car on a straight highway, (d) a bee flying around flowers, (e) the Moon going around the Earth (shape of path only), (f) a vibrating guitar string.
6. A bike covers 12 m in the first second, 12 m in the second second, 12 m in the third second and 12 m in the fourth second. Another bike covers 8 m, 14 m, 6 m and 20 m in the same four seconds. Which bike is in uniform motion? How far has each travelled in 4 s?
7. True or false: (a) A pole on the ground is at rest for everyone, in every situation. (b) A ceiling fan is in rotational motion. (c) Circular motion and oscillatory motion are the same. Give a reason for each.
8. A girl walks across a classroom carrying a bag. Name one object that is at rest relative to the floor but in motion relative to the girl, and one object that is at rest relative to the girl but in motion relative to the floor.

### Answers

1. (a) **Rest**: driver bhi bus ke saath hai, uski position driver se nahi badal rahi. (b) **Motion**: ped se doori badal rahi hai. (c) **Rest**: dono bus ke saath ek jaise chal rahe hain, beech ki doori wahi rehti hai.
2. Girane se pehle pen Mohit ke relative **rest** par hai (hath mein, uske saath). Platform ke relative **motion** mein hai (train ke saath aage jaa raha hai).
3. (a) Temple: **+80 m**, tea stall: **−30 m**. (b) Doori = 80 − (−30) = 80 + 30 = **110 m**.
4. Nayi position = purani − 80 m. Bus stop: 0 − 80 = **−80 m**. Tea stall: −30 − 80 = **−110 m**. Jaanch: bus stop aur tea stall ke beech pehle 0 − (−30) = 30 m, ab −80 − (−110) = 30 m ✅ (doori wahi rahi).
5. (a) Oscillatory, (b) Rotational, (c) Linear, (d) Random, (e) Circular, (f) Oscillatory (vibratory).
6. Pehli bike **uniform** hai (har second 12 m). Doosri non-uniform hai. Pehli ne 12 + 12 + 12 + 12 = **48 m**, doosri ne 8 + 14 + 6 + 20 = **48 m** tay kiye. Doori barabar hai, par motion ka tarika alag hai.
7. (a) **False** ❌: pole zameen ke relative rest par hai, par chalti bus ke yatri ke relative motion mein hai. (b) **True** ✅: pankha apni dhuri par ghoomta hai. (c) **False** ❌: circular mein cheez gol raste par chalti hai, oscillation mein ek hi raste par idhar-udhar aati-jaati hai.
8. Floor ke relative rest, ladki ke relative motion: almirah (ya board). Ladki ke relative rest, floor ke relative motion: uske haath ka bag. Har baar reference batana zaroori hai.
