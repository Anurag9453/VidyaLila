# Topic 2: Pressure in Liquids

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki **thrust** wo force hai jo surface ke **perpendicular** lagta hai, aur **pressure = force ÷ area**, yaani **P = F ÷ A**, jiska SI unit **pascal (Pa)** hai. Wahan force ek thos cheez (jaise eent) se aa raha tha. Ab ek naya sawal: **paani, tel jaise liquids bhi to bhaari hote hain**, aur wo bahne wale hain. Kya wo bhi apne container ki deewaron aur tali par force lagate hain? Agar haan, to kitna pressure lagate hain, aur wo kin cheezon par nirbhar karta hai? Topic 1 mein dekha tha ki liquids aur gases ko milakar **fluids** kehte hain. Is topic mein hum liquids ka pressure samjhenge.

## Part A: Liquid deewaron aur tali par pressure lagata hai

Ek thos cheez (jaise eent) sirf **neeche** ki taraf, apne neeche wali surface par, apna weight lagati hai. Lekin **liquid** bahne wala hota hai. Bottle ka paani sirf **tali** par hi nahi, **side ki deewaron** par bhi dabav dalta hai. Ye hum bottle ke chhedon se dekh chuke hain: agar paani deewar ko dabata na hota, to chhed se bahar kyun dhakela jata?

### Teen chhedon wali bottle

```
        free surface (paani ki upar ki sataha)
      ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
      │                      │
      │                      o--> (1) upar wala chhed: jet paas gira
      │                      │
      │                      │
      │                      o----> (2) beech wala chhed: jet thoda door
      │                      │
      │                      │
      │                      │
      │                      o--------> (3) neeche wala chhed: jet sabse door
      │______________________│
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Teen chhedon wali bottle: jaise-jaise chhed neeche (zyada depth par) hai, paani ka jet utna hi zyada door tak jata hai.

Is se hame teen baatein pata chali:

1. Liquid **tali par** pressure lagata hai.
2. Liquid **side ki deewaron par** bhi pressure lagata hai (isi se jet bahar nikla).
3. **Zyada depth par pressure zyada hota hai** (isi liye neeche wala jet sabse tez aur sabse door gaya).

### Depth badhne par pressure kyun badhta hai?

Kisi point ke **upar** jitna zyada paani hoga, utna zyada **weight** us point ko dabayega. Surface ke paas upar ka paani bahut kam hai, to pressure kam. Gehrayi par upar ek lambe paani ke column ka weight hai, to pressure zyada.

Ye bilkul waisa hi hai jaise ek ke upar ek rakhi hui eenton mein sabse neeche wali eent par sabse zyada weight hota hai.

### Ek point par pressure sab disha mein barabar

Liquid mein kisi bhi **ek point** par pressure **har disha mein barabar** hota hai: upar, neeche, side mein, tirchha, sab taraf. Isliye ek gotakhor (diver) ko ek hi depth par pressure sir par bhi utna hi lagta hai jitna kandhon aur pairon par. Dishayein badalne se pressure nahi badalta, **depth badalne se badalta hai.**

```
          Ek hi depth par ek point P:

                  ^
                  │
            <---- P ---->        (teeno tarah ke teer barabar pressure ke)
                 /│\
                  │
                  v
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Liquid mein ek point P par pressure har disha (upar, neeche, side, tirchha) mein barabar hota hai.

## Part B: P = h ρ g ka formula nikalte hain

Ab ek simple derivation dekhte hain ki depth **h** par pressure kitna hota hai. Hum ek **seedha, kalpanik (imaginary) paani ka column** sochte hain, jiski:

- **Base area** = A
- **Height** = h (surface se us depth tak)
- Liquid ki **density** = ρ

```
        free surface
      ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
      │                   │
      │    paani ka       │  ^
      │    column         │  │
      │    (area A)       │  h
      │                   │  │
      │...................│  v
      │    yahan pressure │
      │___________________│
```

> **Figure 2.3** – h height aur A base area wale paani ke column ka weight, us base par pressure banata hai.

**Step 1: column ka volume**

Volume = base area × height = **A × h**

**Step 2: column ka mass**

Mass = density × volume = **ρ × A × h**  (yaani A h ρ)

**Step 3: column ka weight (jo base par force lagata hai)**

Weight = m g = **(A h ρ) g**

Ye weight hi base par neeche ki taraf force hai: **F = A h ρ g**

**Step 4: pressure = force ÷ area**

P = F ÷ A = (A h ρ g) ÷ A

Upar aur neeche **A kat gaya**:

> **P = h ρ g**

### Units ki jaanch

- h ka unit: **m**
- ρ ka unit: **kg/m³**
- g ka unit: **m/s²**

P = h ρ g = m × kg/m³ × m/s² = kg ÷ (m × s²) = (kg·m/s²) ÷ m² = **N/m² = Pa** ✅

Is class mein hum **g = 10 m/s²** lenge (hisab aasan karne ke liye), aur paani ki density **1000 kg/m³**. Jahan g = 9.8 m/s² lenge, wahan saaf likhenge.

### Ek zaroori baat: ye pressure sirf liquid ka hai

Is formula se jo pressure aaya wo **sirf liquid ke weight** ki wajah se hai (isse **gauge pressure** bhi kehte hain). Asli mein paani ki surface ke upar **hawa** bhi hai, jo apna alag pressure (atmospheric pressure) jodti hai. Uski baat hum **Topic 4** mein karenge. Is topic ke sawalon mein, jab tak kuch aur na likha ho, hum sirf liquid ka pressure nikalenge.

## Part C: Pressure kis cheez par nirbhar karta hai (aur kis par nahi)

Formula P = h ρ g dekhiye. Isme sirf teen cheezein hain:

| Cheez | Pressure par asar |
|---|---|
| **Depth (h)** | h double to pressure double |
| **Liquid ki density (ρ)** | Bhaari liquid (jaise mercury) to pressure zyada |
| **g** | Ek hi jagah par ek jaisi |

Aur **kisi aur cheez ka nahi**: container ki **shape**, **chaudai (width)**, ya paani ki **kul matra (amount)** ka kuch asar nahi hota.

### Teen alag shape ke vessel

```
   Vessel 1         Vessel 2           Vessel 3
   (seedha)         (chaura upar)      (patla upar)

   │       │       │             │         │   │
   │       │       │             │        /     \
   │       │        \           /        /       \
   │       │         \         /        │         │
   │_______│          \_______/         │_________│

   (teeno mein paani ki height h barabar hai)
```

> **Figure 2.4** – Teen alag shape ke vessel, jinme paani ki height (depth) h barabar hai. Teeno ki tali par pressure barabar hoga.

Teeno mein paani ki height barabar hai. Kisi mein paani zyada hai, kisi mein kam, lekin **tali par pressure teeno mein barabar hai**, kyunki P = h ρ g mein h, ρ, g barabar hain. Is baat ko hairani wali baat (**hydrostatic paradox**) kehte hain: chaude vessel mein zyada paani hai, phir bhi pressure zyada nahi.

Isliye yaad rakho: **tali par pressure vessel mein rakhe paani ki matra se nahi, sirf vertical depth h se tay hota hai.** (Chaude vessel mein tirchhi deewarein paani ka kuch weight sambhal leti hain, aur patle-upar wale mein deewarein paani ko neeche dabati hain; dono ka asar milakar tali par pressure wahi rehta hai jo seedhe column ka hota.)

### Same level par pressure barabar

Ek hi liquid mein, **same horizontal level par** (same depth par) saare points par pressure barabar hota hai. Agar do points ka pressure alag hota, to liquid kam pressure ki taraf bahne lagta.

Isi liye jab liquid **neeche se jude hue vessels** (communicating vessels) mein dala jata hai, to **sab mein liquid ka level barabar** ho jata hai, chahe vessel ki shape alag-alag ho. Isse kehte hain "**liquid apna level khud dhoondhta hai**".

```
   ~~~         ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~         ~~~
   │ │           │          │           │ │
   │ │           │          │           │ │
   │ │___________│          │___________│ │
   │________________________________________│
        teeno nalion mein level barabar
```

> **Figure 2.5** – Communicating vessels: neeche se jude vessels mein liquid ka level barabar hota hai.

Aapne apne ghar mein bhi dekha hoga: **chaaynak (kettle)** mein uski tooti (spout) mein paani ka level, chaaynak ke andar ke level ke barabar hota hai. Ye communicating vessels hi hain. Isi tarah **chhat ki tanki aur ghar ke nal** jude hote hain, aur nal tak paani chadhta hai.

## Part D: Solved examples

(Is poore part mein **g = 10 m/s²** aur paani ki density **1000 kg/m³** lete hain. Pressure sirf liquid ka hai.)

### Example 1: 5 m gehre paani mein pressure

Ek talaab mein surface se **5 m neeche** pressure?

- P = h ρ g = 5 m × 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s²
- P = 5 × 1000 × 10 = **50000 Pa** (yaani 5 × 10⁴ Pa)

**Jaanch:** 5 m × 1000 kg/m³ = 5000 kg/m², 5000 kg/m² × 10 m/s² = 50000 N/m² = 50000 Pa ✅

### Example 2: 20 m gehre paani mein pressure

- P = 20 m × 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² = **200000 Pa** (yaani 2 × 10⁵ Pa)

**Jaanch:** Depth 5 m se 4 guna (5 × 4 = 20) hai, to pressure bhi 4 guna: 50000 Pa × 4 = 200000 Pa ✅

### Example 3: Do depths ke beech pressure ka antar

Ek tank mein paani hai. Surface se **3 m** par aur **8 m** par pressure ka antar kitna hai?

- P₃ = 3 m × 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² = 30000 Pa
- P₈ = 8 m × 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² = 80000 Pa
- Antar = 80000 Pa − 30000 Pa = **50000 Pa**

**Jaanch (seedha tareeka):** depth mein antar = 8 m − 3 m = 5 m, to antar = 5 m × 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² = 50000 Pa ✅. Dhyan do: antar sirf **depth ke antar** par nirbhar karta hai.

### Example 4: Mercury ke column ka pressure

0.76 m (76 cm) ooncha mercury ka column. Mercury ki density = **13600 kg/m³**. Neeche ka pressure?

**g = 10 m/s² se:**
- P = h ρ g = 0.76 m × 13600 kg/m³ × 10 m/s²
- P = 13600 × 10 × 0.76 = **103360 Pa**

**g = 9.8 m/s² se (zyada sahi):**
- P = 0.76 m × 13600 kg/m³ × 9.8 m/s² = **101293 Pa** (lagbhag 1.01 × 10⁵ Pa)

**Jaanch:** 13600 × 9.8 = 133280, 133280 × 0.76 = 101292.8 ≈ 101293 Pa ✅. Ye value kuch jaani-pehchani lag rahi hai? Sirf 76 cm mercury ka column lagbhag 1 × 10⁵ Pa ka pressure deta hai, jo ek bahut jaani-pehchani value ke bilkul kareeb hai. Iski kahani Topic 4 mein (wahan g = 9.8 m/s² lekar yahi 101293 Pa use hoga; g = 10 m/s² se 103360 Pa, yaani lagbhag 1.03 × 10⁵ Pa, aata hai).

### Example 5: Tank ki tali par force

Ek tank ki tali ka area **2 m²** hai, aur usme **3 m** gehra paani bhara hai. Tali par pressure aur total force?

- Pressure: P = 3 m × 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² = **30000 Pa**
- Force: F = P × A = 30000 Pa × 2 m² = **60000 N**

**Jaanch:** Seedha tank hai, to paani ka volume = 2 m² × 3 m = 6 m³, mass = 6 m³ × 1000 kg/m³ = 6000 kg, weight = 6000 kg × 10 m/s² = 60000 N ✅ (force = paani ka weight).

Dhyan do: **pressure** (30000 Pa) aur **force** (60000 N) alag cheezein hain. Pressure per m² ka force hai, force poore area par kul force hai.

### Example 6: Kis depth par kitna pressure

Paani mein kis depth par pressure **150000 Pa** hoga?

- P = h ρ g, to **h = P ÷ (ρ g)**
- h = 150000 Pa ÷ (1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s²) = 150000 ÷ 10000 = **15 m**

**Jaanch:** 15 m × 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² = 150000 Pa ✅

### Example 7: Paani aur tel, same depth par

Paani (1000 kg/m³) aur tel (800 kg/m³) dono mein surface se **4 m** neeche pressure? Kisme zyada?

- Paani: P = 4 m × 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² = **40000 Pa**
- Tel: P = 4 m × 800 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² = **32000 Pa**
- Antar = 40000 Pa − 32000 Pa = 8000 Pa

Paani mein pressure zyada hai, kyunki paani **bhaari (zyada density)** hai.

**Jaanch:** 32000 ÷ 40000 = 0.8 = 800 ÷ 1000 (densities ka ratio) ✅

### Example 8: Submarine ki khidki par force

Ek submarine ki chhoti khidki ka area **0.02 m²** hai. Wo paani mein **40 m** neeche hai. Khidki par pressure aur force? (Hawa ka pressure ignore karo.)

- Pressure: P = 40 m × 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² = **400000 Pa** (4 × 10⁵ Pa)
- Force: F = P × A = 400000 Pa × 0.02 m² = **8000 N**

**Jaanch:** 8000 N ≈ 800 kg ke weight ke barabar, sirf ek chhoti si khidki par. Isliye submarine ki khidki bahut mazboot aur chhoti banti hai ✅ (400000 × 0.02 = 8000).

### Example 9: Units badalne ka khayal (cm aur g/cm³)

Ek liquid ki density **1.2 g/cm³** hai aur use **50 cm** gehra bhara hai. Tali par pressure?

- Pehle SI mein badlo: 1.2 g/cm³ = **1200 kg/m³** (kyunki 1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³), aur 50 cm = **0.5 m**
- P = 0.5 m × 1200 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² = **6000 Pa**

**Jaanch:** Agar 50 aur 1.2 seedha laga dete: 50 × 1.2 × 10 = 600, jo galat unit mein hoti. Sahi SI mein 0.5 × 1200 × 10 = 6000 Pa ✅

## Part E: Dam, gotakhor, submarine aur machhli

### Dam ki deewar neeche se moti kyun?

P = h ρ g ke hisaab se, paani **jitna gehra, utna zyada pressure**. Dam ke **neeche** pressure sabse zyada hota hai, to deewar ko wahan sabse zyada force jhelna padta hai. Isliye engineers deewar ko neeche **moti** aur upar **patli** banate hain, taaki wo neeche ke bade pressure ko jhel sake aur tootne na paye.

```
       upar: patli deewar, kam pressure
        │\
        │ \
        │  \       <--- paani ka pressure
        │   \           (neeche zyada)
        │    \
        │     \
        │      \
        │_______\
      neeche: moti deewar, zyada pressure
```

> **Figure 2.6** – Dam ki deewar: paani ki gehrayi ke saath pressure badhta hai, isliye deewar neeche moti aur upar patli banayi jaati hai.

### Gotakhor aur submarine

Jaise-jaise gotakhor ya submarine zyada gehre jaate hain, unpar paani ka pressure badhta jaata hai (h badhta hai). Isliye **gehre samundar** mein jaane ke liye ek khaas mazboot kapde ya submarine chahiye hota hai, jo is bade pressure ko jhel sake. Samundar mein 1000 m gehrayi par (samundar ke paani ki density lagbhag 1030 kg/m³ lene par) P = 1000 m × 1030 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² = 10300000 Pa, yaani lagbhag 1.03 × 10⁷ Pa, jo 10 m gehre paani ke pressure (1 × 10⁵ Pa) se kai guna zyada hai.

### Gehre samundar ki machhli

Bahut gehre samundar mein rehne wali machhliyon ka sharir is bade pressure ke hisaab se **khaas tarah ka** hota hai. Unke sharir ke andar ka pressure bahar ke pressure ke jaisa hi hota hai, isliye wo kuchalti nahi. Agar wohi machhli upar surface par laayi jaye to uska sharir kam pressure ki wajah se kharab ho sakta hai. (Sirf ek jhalak: aage ki class mein is par gehrayi se baat hogi.)

## Part F: Kahani ka jawab: Omar ka sawal solve karte hain

### Dam mein pressure

Maan lo dam mein **30 m** gehra paani hai. Dam ki tali (sabse neeche) par pressure?

- P = 30 m × 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² = **300000 Pa** (3 × 10⁵ Pa)

Aur **10 m** ki depth par (jahan deewar patli ho sakti hai):

- P = 10 m × 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² = **100000 Pa** (1 × 10⁵ Pa)

Neeche ka pressure upar wale (10 m par) se **3 guna** hai: 300000 ÷ 100000 = 3. Deewar neeche se 3 guna zyada pressure jhelti hai, isliye usko neeche se moti banana hi padega.

Neeche ke **1 m²** hisse par paani ka force: F = P × A = 300000 Pa × 1 m² = **300000 N**, jo lagbhag **30000 kg (30 tonne)** ke weight ke barabar hai. Ab Omar samajh gaya ki cement kam nahi, zaroorat ke hisaab se lagta hai.

**Jaanch:** 300000 N ÷ 10 m/s² = 30000 kg ✅

### Bottle ke jet

Ms. Chandra ki bottle ke teen chhed surface se **5 cm, 10 cm aur 15 cm** neeche the:

| Chhed | Depth h | Pressure P = h ρ g |
|---|---|---|
| Upar wala | 0.05 m | 0.05 × 1000 × 10 = 500 Pa |
| Beech wala | 0.10 m | 0.10 × 1000 × 10 = 1000 Pa |
| Neeche wala | 0.15 m | 0.15 × 1000 × 10 = 1500 Pa |

Neeche wale chhed par pressure sabse zyada (1500 Pa), isliye paani ko wahan se sabse zor se bahar dhakela jata hai aur jet sabse door girta hai. Wohi baat dam par lagu hoti hai: jahan pressure zyada, wahan zyada mazbooti chahiye. Omar ka sawal solve ho gaya.

**Jaanch:** depth 5 → 10 → 15 cm (1 : 2 : 3), pressure 500 → 1000 → 1500 Pa (1 : 2 : 3), yaani pressure depth ke saath seedha badhta hai ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**1. Container ki lambayi ya tirchhi length ko depth maan lena.**
Depth hamesha **surface se point tak seedhi vertical doori** hoti hai. Tirchhe pipe ki lambayi nahi.
- Galat: pipe 10 m lamba hai (tirchha), to h = 10 m. ❌
- Sahi: vertical gehrayi (maan lo 6 m) li jayegi, h = 6 m. ✅

**2. Height ki jagah volume ya paani ki matra le lena.**
Formula mein h hai, volume ya amount nahi. 1 litre aur 100 litre paani agar ek hi depth ke ho to pressure barabar hoga.

**3. Sochna ki chaude vessel ki tali par pressure zyada hoga.**
Pressure sirf h, ρ, g par nirbhar hai. Same height ke teen vessel (Figure 2.4) mein tali par pressure barabar hai.

**4. cm ko m aur g/cm³ ko kg/m³ mein na badalna.**
- Galat: h = 50 cm, ρ = 1 g/cm³, P = 50 × 1 × 10 = 500 (galat unit). ❌
- Sahi: h = 0.5 m, ρ = 1000 kg/m³, P = 0.5 × 1000 × 10 = 5000 Pa. ✅

**5. Pressure aur force ko ek hi samajhna.**
P = h ρ g **pressure** (Pa) deta hai. **Force** nikalne ke liye alag se **F = P × A** karna padta hai (N mein). Example 5 mein 30000 Pa pressure tha, aur force 60000 N.

## Quick Recap

- Liquid **tali** par aur **side ki deewaron** par pressure lagata hai.
- Liquid mein **depth badhne par pressure badhta hai**.
- Ek point par pressure **har disha mein barabar** hota hai.
- Derivation: column ka weight = (A h ρ) g, to **P = (A h ρ g) ÷ A = h ρ g**. Unit: m × kg/m³ × m/s² = Pa.
- Pressure sirf **h, ρ, g** par nirbhar hai: container ki shape ya paani ki matra par **nahi** (teen alag shape ke vessel, same height, same pressure).
- **Same liquid mein same level par pressure barabar**: communicating vessels mein liquid ka level barabar ho jata hai.
- Tali par total force = **P × A**. Pressure aur force alag cheezein hain.
- Ye pressure sirf liquid ka hai; upar ki hawa ka pressure Topic 4 mein.
- Zyada density ki liquid (mercury 13600 kg/m³) mein same depth par zyada pressure; tel (800 kg/m³) mein kam.
- Dam neeche se moti kyunki neeche pressure zyada hai.

## Practice Questions

1. Find the pressure due to water at a depth of 12 m. (Take g = 10 m/s² and density of water = 1000 kg/m³.)
2. Find the pressure due to oil of density 800 kg/m³ at a depth of 6 m. (g = 10 m/s²)
3. Find the pressure at the bottom of a mercury column 5 cm high. (Density of mercury = 13600 kg/m³, g = 10 m/s². Convert cm to m.)
4. At what depth in water is the pressure due to the water equal to 250000 Pa? (g = 10 m/s²)
5. A tank has a base of area 4 m² and holds water to a height of 2.5 m. Find (a) the pressure on the base, (b) the force on the base. (g = 10 m/s²)
6. Two points in a lake are at depths of 0.2 m and 0.6 m below the surface. What is the difference in pressure between them? (g = 10 m/s²)
7. A liquid of density 0.9 g/cm³ fills a container to a depth of 30 cm. Find the pressure at the bottom. (g = 10 m/s²)
8. A diver's window has area 0.01 m² and is 50 m below the water surface. Find the force on the window due to water. (Ignore the air above the water. g = 10 m/s²)
9. Three vessels of different shapes are filled with the same liquid to the same height. Is the pressure at the bottom the same or different? Give a reason.
10. Why is a dam wall made much thicker at the bottom than at the top?

### Answers

**1.** P = h ρ g = 12 m × 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² = **120000 Pa**. (Jaanch: 12 × 10000 = 120000 ✅)

**2.** P = 6 m × 800 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² = 6 × 8000 = **48000 Pa**. ✅

**3.** 5 cm = 0.05 m. P = 0.05 m × 13600 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² = **6800 Pa**. (Jaanch: 13600 × 10 = 136000, 136000 × 0.05 = 6800 ✅)

**4.** h = P ÷ (ρ g) = 250000 Pa ÷ (1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s²) = 250000 ÷ 10000 = **25 m**. (Jaanch: 25 × 10000 = 250000 ✅)

**5.** (a) P = 2.5 m × 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² = **25000 Pa**. (b) F = P × A = 25000 Pa × 4 m² = **100000 N**. (Jaanch: volume = 4 × 2.5 = 10 m³, mass = 10000 kg, weight = 100000 N ✅)

**6.** Depth ka antar = 0.6 m − 0.2 m = 0.4 m. ΔP = 0.4 m × 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² = **4000 Pa**. (Jaanch: 0.6 m par 6000 Pa, 0.2 m par 2000 Pa, antar 4000 Pa ✅)

**7.** 0.9 g/cm³ = 900 kg/m³, 30 cm = 0.3 m. P = 0.3 m × 900 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² = **2700 Pa**. ✅

**8.** P = 50 m × 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² = 500000 Pa. F = P × A = 500000 Pa × 0.01 m² = **5000 N**. ✅

**9.** **Same** hoga. Pressure P = h ρ g sirf height (depth), density aur g par nirbhar hai, vessel ki shape ya paani ki matra par nahi. Teeno mein h, ρ, g barabar hain.

**10.** Paani ki gehrayi badhne se pressure badhta hai (P = h ρ g). Dam ke neeche pressure sabse zyada hota hai, isliye wahan deewar ko zyada force jhelna padta hai. Isliye wo neeche se moti banayi jaati hai.
