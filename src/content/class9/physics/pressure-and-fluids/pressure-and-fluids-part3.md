# Topic 3: Pascal's Law aur Hydraulic Machines

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki liquid ke andar **gehrai (depth) badhne par pressure badhta hai**, aur ek hi level par har jagah pressure **barabar** hota hai (P = h ρ g). Wahan pressure ka kaaran liquid ka **apna bojh** tha. Ab ek naya sawal: agar hum liquid ko **bahar se dabayein**, to us dabav ka kya hoga? Wo sirf dabane wali jagah par rahega, ya poore liquid mein phail jayega? Is sawal ka jawab hai **Pascal's law**, aur isi par bani hain **hydraulic machines**, jo bhaari-bhaari cheezein bas halke dhakke se utha deti hain.

Is topic mein jahan g ki zaroorat ho, hum **g = 10 m/s²** lenge.

---

## Service station ki hydraulic lift

Ek Sunday subah Tanmay apne papa ki car ki servicing ke liye service station aaya. Wahan mechanic Mr. Sandeep ne use ek kone mein khada kar diya, "Beta, yahin se dekho, line ke andar mat aana."

Car ka wazan lagbhag **ek tonne** (1000 kg) tha. Ek mechanic ne car ko lift ke platform par chadhaya. Phir usne ek chhote se lever ko haath se halke se **dheere-dheere** neeche-upar karna shuru kiya. Tanmay ki aankhein badi ho gayin: **poori car dheere-dheere upar uthne lagi**, bilkul aaram se, jaise koi ungli se khilona utha raha ho!

Tanmay ne Mr. Sandeep se poocha, "Uncle, ye to kamaal hai! Ek tonne ki car aur mechanic bhaiya sirf halka sa push de rahe hain. Itni badi car itne chhote push se kaise uth gayi?"

Mr. Sandeep muskuraye, "Isme koi jaadu nahi hai, beta. Isme **liquid ka ek kamaal ka niyam** chhupa hai. Chalo, pehle wo niyam samajhte hain, phir hum isi car ka hisaab number ke saath lagayenge."

## Part A: Pascal's law

Topic 1 mein hum jaan chuke hain ki **pressure = force ÷ area** (P = F ÷ A), aur uska SI unit **pascal (Pa)** hai, jahan 1 Pa = 1 N/m².

Ab maan lo ek band bartan mein liquid bhara hai, aur hum uspar bahar se force lagakar dabate hain. **Pascal's law** kehta hai:

> **Band (enclosed) fluid par lagaya gaya pressure fluid ke har hisse tak aur bartan ki deewaron tak barabar aur bina ghate (undiminished) pahunchta hai.**

Seedhi bhasha mein: liquid ko ek jagah se dabao, to wo dabav **sab taraf**, **sab jagah**, **barabar** phail jata hai. Na wo sirf neeche jata hai, na sirf us jagah rukta hai.

Dhyan do ki yahan "extra pressure" ki baat ho rahi hai jo bahar se dabane par mila. Liquid ke apne bojh wala pressure (h ρ g) to pehle se hai hi, wo gehrai ke hisaab se alag-alag hota hai. Is topic mein hum bahar se lagaye gaye pressure ko dekhenge.

### Teen aasan demonstration

**1) Chhed wali band bottle aur piston (syringe)**

Ek plastic ki bottle lo jisme **paani bhara ho**. Uske charo taraf alag-alag jagah par chhote-chhote **chhed** karo. Bottle ke munh par ek piston (ya syringe) tight fit karo, taaki paani bahar na nikle. Ab piston ko dheere se dabao.

```
          piston dabao (force)
                │
                v
           +---------+
           │  [___]  │   <- piston
        ---+         +---
   <== o   │  paani  │   o ==>
   <== o   │         │   o ==>
        ---+         +---
           │    o    │
           +----v----+
                v  (neeche bhi jet)
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Band bottle mein piston dabane par har chhed se paani ki jet nikalti hai, aur har jet lagbhag **barabar tezi** se, chahe chhed upar ho, neeche ho ya side mein. Matlab dabav har taraf barabar pahuncha.

**2) Toothpaste ka tube**

Tube ko beech se dabaoge to paste **sirf munh se nahi**, agar tube mein kahin chhota chhed ho to wahan se bhi nikalne lagegi. Tube ko jahan dabao, dabav poore paste mein phail jata hai. (Paste ek gaadha fluid jaisa hai, isliye ye ek acchi roz ki misaal hai.)

**3) Chhed wala ghubbara (balloon)**

Ek ghubbare mein paani bharo aur uske upar kai jagah **sui se bahut chhote chhed** karo. Ghubbare ko dabaoge to **sabhi chhedon se paani ki dhaarein barabar** nikalti dikhengi. Sui ke chhed bahut chhote hain isliye ghubbara ek **band system** jaisa hi kaam karta hai.

```
         .-"""""""-.
        /  o  o  o  \      <- chhed
   ==> │  o  paani o  │ <==
        \  o  o  o  /
         '-._____.-'
            ^ ^ ^
          haath se dabao
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Paani bhare ghubbare ko dabane par har chhed se paani ki jet nikalti hai. Dabav har taraf barabar pahuncha.

Ye teeno dekhne ke baad Pascal's law ka matlab saaf hai: **fluid ko ek jagah se dabaya, to dabav har jagah pahunch gaya.**

### Liquid kyun, gas kyun nahi?

Hydraulic machines mein hum **liquid** (aam taur par tel) use karte hain, hawa nahi. Wajah:

- **Liquid lagbhag incompressible hota hai.** Usko dabane par uska volume (lagbhag) nahi ghatta, isliye jo dabav lagaya wo seedha aage pahunch jata hai.
- **Gas (hawa) compressible hoti hai.** Hawa ko dabao to wo **sikud jati hai** (volume ghat jata hai), aur lagaya hua force kaafi had tak "sikudne" mein kharch ho jata hai, aage kam pahunchta hai. Isliye hawa se machine bhari cheez ko pakka nahi utha sakti.

Isiliye aapne suna hoga: brake mein agar **hawa ka bubble** chala jaye to brake "spongy" ho jata hai, pedal dabane par jaise sponge dab raha ho. Wo bubble dab jata hai, aur dabav aage tak poora nahi pahunchta.

## Part B: Hydraulic lift ka kaam karne ka tarika

Ab Mr. Sandeep ki lift par aate hain. Do cylinders ko neeche se ek pipe se jod do aur dono mein tel bhar do. Ek cylinder **patla** hai, jisme chhota piston hai, **area a**. Doosra **mota** hai, jisme bada piston hai, **area A**.

```
        F1 (halka push)                    F2 (bada force, upar)
          │                                       ^
          v                                       │
     +-----------+                      +------------------------+
     │   chhota  │                      │       bada piston      │
     │  piston a │                      │        (area A)        │
     +---+   +---+                      +---+                +---+
         │   │                              │                │
         │   │       tel bhara hai          │                │
         │   +------------------------------+                │
         +---------------------------------------------------+
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Hydraulic lift: do cylinders ek pipe se jude hain aur tel se bhare hain. Chhote piston (area a) par chhota force F₁ lagta hai, bade piston (area A) par bada force F₂ milta hai.

**Kaise?**

1. Chhote piston par force F₁ lagao. Tel par pressure bana:

   > **P = F₁ ÷ a**

2. **Pascal's law:** ye pressure tel ke har hisse mein barabar pahunchta hai. To bade piston ke neeche bhi **wahi pressure P** hai.

3. Bade piston par is pressure se force banta hai:

   > **F₂ = P × A**

4. Pressure barabar hai, isliye:

   > **F₁ ÷ a = F₂ ÷ A**

   Dono taraf ka P ek hai. Isse hume mila:

   > **F₂ = F₁ × (A ÷ a)**

Yani force **A ÷ a guna** ho gaya. Agar bade piston ka area chhote se 100 guna hai, to force bhi **100 guna** milega. Isiliye hydraulic lift ko **force multiplier** kehte hain.

**Sabse zaroori baat:** pressure dono pistons par **barabar** hai. Force alag hai kyunki area alag hai. (F = P × area, area zyada to force zyada.)

### Kya kuch free mein mil gaya? Nahi!

Zara rukiye. Kya ye "free ka force" hai? Nahi. Ek tarah ka **saudaa** hota hai:

- Chhota piston ko **lamba rasta (distance)** chalna padta hai.
- Bada piston **bahut thoda** upar uthta hai.

Kyunki dono taraf **liquid ki matra barabar** hi idhar se udhar jati hai. Chhote cylinder mein liquid ki lambi si patli column neeche gayi, wahi liquid bade cylinder mein chaudi si moti, par bahut chhoti height ki column bani. Isliye:

> **a × d₁ = A × d₂**   (d₁ = chhote piston ka distance, d₂ = bade piston ka distance)

Yani **jitna force guna badhta hai, utna hi distance guna ghat jata hai.** Force aur distance ka gunaa dono taraf barabar rehta hai (F₁ d₁ = F₂ d₂), is liye **machine se koi extra energy ya work nahi milta**; ye sirf force ko bada karti hai aur distance ko chhota. Work aur energy ki poori baat hum **Work aur Energy chapter mein** karenge, abhi sirf ek number se jaanch lenge.

## Part C: Kahani ka hisaab

Ab Tanmay ke sawal ka number se jawab dete hain.

**Example 1 (kahani ki lift):** Car ka mass 1000 kg hai. Chhote piston ka area a = 10 cm², bade piston ka area A = 1000 cm². Chhote piston par kitna force chahiye? g = 10 m/s² lo.

**Step 1: Car ka weight (bade piston par force F₂)**

- F₂ = m × g = 1000 kg × 10 m/s² = **10000 N**

**Step 2: Area ka ratio**

- A ÷ a = 1000 cm² ÷ 10 cm² = **100** (dono area ek hi unit cm² mein hain, isliye cm² ka cancel ho gaya)

**Step 3: Chhote piston par force**

- F₁ = F₂ ÷ (A ÷ a) = 10000 N ÷ 100 = **100 N**

Yani sirf **100 N** ka push (lagbhag 10 kg wale bojh ka weight) lagana hai.

**Jaanch (pressure barabar?):**
- Chhote piston par: P = 100 N ÷ 10 cm² = 100 N ÷ 0.001 m² = 100000 Pa
- Bade piston par: P = 10000 N ÷ 1000 cm² = 10000 N ÷ 0.1 m² = 100000 Pa

Dono barabar: **1 × 10⁵ Pa** ✅

**Ab distance:** car ko 1 cm upar uthana hai, to chhota piston kitna chalega?
- a × d₁ = A × d₂ ⇒ d₁ = (A ÷ a) × d₂ = 100 × 1 cm = **100 cm = 1 m**

**Jaanch (work barabar?):**
- Chhote piston par: F₁ × d₁ = 100 N × 1 m = **100 N·m**
- Bade piston par: F₂ × d₂ = 10000 N × 0.01 m = **100 N·m** ✅

Matlab mechanic bhaiya ne **halka push** diya, par us push ko **lamba** (1 m) chalna pada, tab jakar car sirf **1 cm** uthi. Isiliye wo lever ko baar-baar dheere-dheere pump karte rahe. Tanmay ka sawal hal: **chhota force + lamba rasta = bada force + chhota rasta.**

## Part D: Hydraulic machines ke upyog

Yahi ek niyam kai jagah kaam aata hai:

| Machine | Ek line mein kaam |
|---|---|
| **Hydraulic lift** | Car ya bhaari saman ko uthana (service station, garage mein) |
| **Hydraulic brakes** | Pedal ka halka dabav tel ke raste chaaron pahiyon ke brake tak pahunchakar gaadi rok deta hai |
| **Hydraulic jack** | Bus, truck ya car ka ek pahiya uthakar tyre badalne ke kaam aata hai |
| **Dentist ki chair** | Pedal ya button se kursi ko upar-neeche aur jhukane ke liye |
| **Hydraulic press** | Bahut bade force se dhaatu ki chaadar dabana, aakaar dena, ya rui ke gathhe kasna |

### Hydraulic brake ka naksha

```
   pedal ka force
        │
        v
   +--------+                         +--------------+
   │ master │=========== tel =========│ pahiye ka    │--> brake pad
   │ piston │    (pipe se pahunchta)  │ bada piston  │    pahiye ko roke
   +--------+                         +--------------+
```

> **Figure 3.4** – Hydraulic brake: pedal se dabaya gaya chhota piston tel mein pressure banata hai, ye pressure pipe se pahiye ke piston tak pahunchta hai (Pascal's law), aur wahan bada force banakar pahiye ko rokta hai.

## Part E: Solved examples

(Jahan zaroorat ho, **1 cm² = 0.0001 m²** aur **1 cm = 0.01 m** lo. Ratio nikalte waqt dono area ek hi unit mein hone chahiye.)

**Example 2 (output force nikalna):** Chhote piston ka area 5 cm², bade piston ka area 500 cm². Chhote piston par 50 N force lagaya. Bade piston par force?

- A ÷ a = 500 cm² ÷ 5 cm² = 100
- F₂ = F₁ × (A ÷ a) = 50 N × 100 = **5000 N**

**Jaanch:** F₁ ÷ a = 50 N ÷ 5 cm² = 10 N/cm². F₂ ÷ A = 5000 N ÷ 500 cm² = 10 N/cm² ✅ (pressure barabar)

**Example 3 (input force nikalna):** Ek lift ko 8000 N ka bojh uthana hai. Chhote piston ka area 20 cm² aur bade piston ka area 800 cm² hai. Chhote piston par kitna force lagana padega?

- A ÷ a = 800 cm² ÷ 20 cm² = 40
- F₁ = F₂ ÷ (A ÷ a) = 8000 N ÷ 40 = **200 N**

**Jaanch:** F₁ × (A ÷ a) = 200 N × 40 = 8000 N ✅

**Example 4 (piston ka area nikalna):** Chhote piston par 200 N force lagane se bade piston par 20000 N force chahiye. Chhote piston ka area 4 cm² hai. Bade piston ka area kitna hona chahiye?

- Force ka ratio = F₂ ÷ F₁ = 20000 N ÷ 200 N = 100
- A = a × (F₂ ÷ F₁) = 4 cm² × 100 = **400 cm²**

**Jaanch:** F₂ = F₁ × (A ÷ a) = 200 N × (400 cm² ÷ 4 cm²) = 200 N × 100 = 20000 N ✅

**Example 5 (radius se area ka ratio):** Ek hydraulic press ke chhote piston ki radius 1 cm aur bade piston ki radius 10 cm hai. Chhote piston par 30 N force lagaya. Bade piston par force?

Area A = π r². Dono ke liye π barabar hai, isliye ratio mein cancel ho jata hai:

- A ÷ a = π (10 cm)² ÷ π (1 cm)² = (10)² ÷ (1)² = 100 ÷ 1 = **100**

(Dhyan: radius 10 guna hai, par **area 10 guna nahi, 100 guna** hai, kyunki area radius ka square hota hai.)

- F₂ = F₁ × (A ÷ a) = 30 N × 100 = **3000 N**

**Jaanch:** π lekar dekhein (π = 3.14): a = 3.14 × 1² = 3.14 cm², A = 3.14 × 10² = 314 cm². A ÷ a = 314 ÷ 3.14 = 100 ✅

**Example 6 (dono pistons ka distance):** Chhote piston ka area 10 cm², bade piston ka area 500 cm² hai. Chhote piston ko 25 cm neeche dabaya gaya. Bada piston kitna upar uthega?

Dono taraf liquid ki matra barabar: a × d₁ = A × d₂

- 10 cm² × 25 cm = 250 cm³ liquid chhote cylinder se gaya
- d₂ = (a × d₁) ÷ A = 250 cm³ ÷ 500 cm² = **0.5 cm**

**Jaanch:** A × d₂ = 500 cm² × 0.5 cm = 250 cm³ = a × d₁ ✅

**Example 7 (piston ke neeche pressure):** Ek chhote piston ka area 20 cm² hai aur uspar 200 N force lagaya gaya. Tel mein pressure kitna bana?

Pehle area ko m² mein badlo:

- a = 20 cm² = 20 × 0.0001 m² = 0.002 m²

Ab:

- P = F ÷ a = 200 N ÷ 0.002 m² = **100000 Pa** (= 1 × 10⁵ Pa)

**Jaanch:** P × a = 100000 Pa × 0.002 m² = 200 N ✅

(Agar cm² mein hi chhod dete to 200 N ÷ 20 cm² = 10 N/cm² milta, jo sahi hai par Pa nahi hai. Pa ke liye m² zaroori hai.)

**Example 8 (hydraulic brake):** Ek car ke brake mein pedal se master piston par 60 N ka force pahunchta hai. Master piston ka area 2 cm² hai. Pahiye ke piston ka area 8 cm² hai. (a) Tel mein pressure? (b) Pahiye ke piston par force?

- Master piston ka area: a = 2 cm² = 2 × 0.0001 m² = 0.0002 m²
- Pahiye ke piston ka area: A = 8 cm² = 0.0008 m²

(a) P = F ÷ a = 60 N ÷ 0.0002 m² = **300000 Pa** (= 3 × 10⁵ Pa)

(b) Pascal's law se pahiye ke piston par bhi wahi pressure hai:

- F = P × A = 300000 Pa × 0.0008 m² = **240 N**

**Jaanch:** Area ratio = 8 ÷ 2 = 4, aur force = 60 N × 4 = 240 N ✅

**Example 9 (dono pistons ke pressure ki tulna):** Chhote piston (area 2 cm²) par 20 N aur bade piston (area 200 cm²) par 2000 N force hai. Dono jagah pressure kitna hai? Kya ye barabar hain?

- Chhota piston: a = 2 cm² = 0.0002 m². P₁ = 20 N ÷ 0.0002 m² = **100000 Pa**
- Bada piston: A = 200 cm² = 0.02 m². P₂ = 2000 N ÷ 0.02 m² = **100000 Pa**

P₁ = P₂ ✅. **Pressure barabar hai, force alag.** Bade piston par force 2000 ÷ 20 = 100 guna hai kyunki area bhi 200 ÷ 2 = 100 guna hai.

**Jaanch:** 100000 Pa × 0.0002 m² = 20 N ✅ aur 100000 Pa × 0.02 m² = 2000 N ✅

**Example 10 (work ki jaanch):** Ek hydraulic machine ka a = 5 cm², A = 125 cm² hai. Chhote piston par 40 N force lagaya aur use 50 cm neeche dabaya. Bade piston par force aur distance? Dono taraf F × d barabar hai kya?

- A ÷ a = 125 cm² ÷ 5 cm² = 25
- F₂ = 40 N × 25 = **1000 N**
- d₂ = d₁ ÷ 25 = 50 cm ÷ 25 = **2 cm**

Ab F × d, distance ko metre mein badal kar (50 cm = 0.5 m, 2 cm = 0.02 m):

- Chhote piston: F₁ × d₁ = 40 N × 0.5 m = **20 N·m**
- Bade piston: F₂ × d₂ = 1000 N × 0.02 m = **20 N·m** ✅

Dono barabar: machine ne force 25 guna kiya, par distance 25 guna ghata diya. Kuch free nahi mila.

**Example 11 (radii se input force):** Ek lift mein chhote piston ki radius 2 cm aur bade piston ki radius 14 cm hai. 4900 N ka bojh uthana hai. Chhote piston par kitna force chahiye?

- A ÷ a = (14 cm)² ÷ (2 cm)² = 196 cm² ÷ 4 cm² = **49**
- F₁ = F₂ ÷ (A ÷ a) = 4900 N ÷ 49 = **100 N**

**Jaanch:** F₁ × 49 = 100 N × 49 = 4900 N ✅ (Agar galti se radius ka ratio 7 le lete to F₁ = 700 N aata, jo galat hai.)

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Hydraulic lift se extra energy ya work milta hai."**

Nahi. Force 100 guna hota hai to distance 100 guna ghat jata hai. Example 1 mein F₁ d₁ = 100 N × 1 m = 100 N·m, aur F₂ d₂ = 10000 N × 0.01 m = 100 N·m: barabar. Machine sirf force ko **badal** deti hai, energy paida nahi karti.

**Galti 2: Radius ki jagah diameter use karna (ya ek mein radius, ek mein diameter)**

Area = π r² mein **radius** aata hai. Agar piston ka diameter 4 cm diya ho to radius 2 cm hai: a = 3.14 × 2² = **12.56 cm²**. Agar 4 ko hi radius maan liya to 3.14 × 4² = 50.24 cm² aayega, jo **4 guna** galat hai. Aur ratio mein agar ek piston ka radius aur doosre ka diameter le liya, to jawab bilkul galat hoga.

**Galti 3: Area ka ratio radius ke ratio jaisa le lena**

Radius 1 cm aur 10 cm ho to area ka ratio **100** hai, 10 nahi (Example 5). Ratio mein **square** karna na bhoolo.

**Galti 4: cm² ko m² mein badle bina Pa nikalna**

P = F ÷ A mein Pa tabhi aata hai jab A m² mein ho. 1 cm² = 0.0001 m². Example 7 mein 20 cm² = 0.002 m², tab P = 100000 Pa. Agar 200 ÷ 20 = 10 ko hi Pa likh diya to galat. (Ratio A ÷ a nikalte waqt dono cm² mein ho to koi dikkat nahi.)

**Galti 5: "Bade piston par pressure zyada hota hai."**

Nahi. **Pressure dono par barabar** hai (Pascal's law). **Force** bade piston par zyada hai kyunki uska **area** zyada hai. Example 9 mein dono jagah 100000 Pa tha.

**Galti 6: Pascal's law ko khule (open) system ya hawa bhare / leak wale system par laga dena**

Pascal's law **band** fluid ke liye hai. Agar pipe mein leak hai to tel bahar nikal jayega aur dabav ghat jayega. Agar hawa ka bubble hai to wo compress hoga (hawa compressible hai) aur force poora aage nahi pahunchega. Isiliye brake ya lift mein bubble ya leak khatarnak hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Pascal's law:** band fluid par lagaya gaya pressure fluid ke har hisse aur bartan ki deewaron tak **barabar aur bina ghate** pahunchta hai.
- Chhed wali bottle, toothpaste tube aur chhed wala ghubbara isi ke demonstration hain.
- **Liquid lagbhag incompressible** hai, isliye hydraulic machines mein use hota hai. **Gas compressible** hai, isliye nahi.
- **Hydraulic lift:** F₁ ÷ a = F₂ ÷ A, yani **F₂ = F₁ × (A ÷ a)**. Chhote force se bada force milta hai: force multiplier.
- Pressure dono pistons par **barabar**, force alag.
- Distance ulta chhota: **a × d₁ = A × d₂**. **F₁ d₁ = F₂ d₂**, koi extra energy nahi (Work chapter mein aur).
- Area ka ratio radius ke **square** ka ratio hai. Pa ke liye area **m²** mein lo (1 cm² = 0.0001 m²).
- **Upyog:** hydraulic lift, brakes, jack, dentist ki chair, hydraulic press.
- **Kahani ka jawab:** F₂ = 10000 N, A ÷ a = 100, F₁ = **100 N**; chhota piston 100 cm chalta hai to car sirf 1 cm uthti hai.

### Answers

1. A ÷ a = 400 cm² ÷ 4 cm² = 100. F₂ = 25 N × 100 = **2500 N**.
   Jaanch: 2500 N ÷ 100 = 25 N ✅

2. A ÷ a = 1000 cm² ÷ 10 cm² = 100. F₁ = 6000 N ÷ 100 = **60 N**.
   Jaanch: 60 N × 100 = 6000 N ✅

3. Area ratio = (6 cm)² ÷ (2 cm)² = 36 ÷ 4 = **9** (radius ratio 3 hai, par area ratio 9). F₂ = 50 N × 9 = **450 N**.
   Jaanch: 450 N ÷ 9 = 50 N ✅

4. (a) a = 5 cm² = 0.0005 m². P = 100 N ÷ 0.0005 m² = **200000 Pa** (2 × 10⁵ Pa).
   (b) A = 250 cm² = 0.025 m². F = P × A = 200000 Pa × 0.025 m² = **5000 N**.
   Jaanch: area ratio 250 ÷ 5 = 50, aur 100 N × 50 = 5000 N ✅

5. a × d₁ = A × d₂ ⇒ d₂ = (2 cm² × 40 cm) ÷ 80 cm² = 80 cm³ ÷ 80 cm² = **1 cm**. Area ratio = 80 ÷ 2 = 40, to F₂ = 10 N × 40 = 400 N.
   F₁ d₁ = 10 N × 0.4 m = 4 N·m. F₂ d₂ = 400 N × 0.01 m = 4 N·m ✅ Barabar hain.

6. a = 3 cm² = 0.0003 m². P = 90 N ÷ 0.0003 m² = **300000 Pa**. Wheel piston: A = 12 cm² = 0.0012 m². F = 300000 Pa × 0.0012 m² = **360 N**.
   Jaanch: area ratio 12 ÷ 3 = 4, 90 N × 4 = 360 N ✅

7. (a) **False** ❌. Work kabhi zyada nahi milta: force badhta hai to distance ghat jata hai (F₁ d₁ = F₂ d₂).
   (b) **False** ❌. Pressure dono par barabar hai; bade piston par **force** zyada hai.
   (c) **True** ✅. Incompressible hone se dabav seedha aage pahunchta hai.
   (d) **True** ✅. Pascal ki definition yehi hai: ek newton per metre square.

8. Hawa compressible hoti hai. Pedal dabane par bubble sikud jata hai, to pedal ka kaafi dabav bubble ko dabane mein hi kharch ho jata hai aur poora pressure pahiye ke piston tak nahi pahunchta. Isliye pedal neeche tak dabta hai par brake kamzor lagta hai (spongy).

9. F₂ = m g = 1500 kg × 10 m/s² = 15000 N. F₁ = 15000 N ÷ 100 = **150 N**.
   Jaanch: 150 N × 100 = 15000 N ✅

10. **Aadha sahi.** Force to 50 guna ho jata hai, par "thoda dabane se bahut upar" galat hai. a × d₁ = A × d₂ se d₂ = d₁ ÷ 50: bada piston, chhote piston ke muqabley sirf **1/50** distance upar uthta hai. Maan lo chhote piston ko 50 cm dabaya, to bada piston sirf 1 cm uthega. Force 50 guna hai par distance 50 guna kam, to energy/work barabar rehta hai.
    Jaanch: 50 × 1 = 1 × 50 = 50 (force × distance dono taraf barabar) ✅
