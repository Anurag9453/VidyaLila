# Topic 4: Atmospheric Pressure

Pichhle topic mein dekha ki **liquid** band jagah mein pressure ko har taraf barabar pahunchata hai, aur isi se hydraulic machines kaam karti hain. Par liquid hi akela fluid nahi hai: **hawa (air) bhi ek fluid hai**. Wo bhi behti hai aur dabaav bhi deti hai. To ek naya sawal: hum jis hawa ke samundar ke andar rehte hain, kya wo hum par pressure daal rahi hai? Aur agar haan, to hume mehsoos kyun nahi hota? Is topic mein yehi dekhenge.

---

## Bathroom ka suction hook

Naira ke ghar ke bathroom mein tauliya rakhne ki jagah nahi thi. Plumber Mr. Fazal aaye to unke thaile mein ek rubber ka **suction hook** tha: ek chhota gol katora jaisa cup, jiske beech mein hook laga tha aur kinare par ek chhoti si tab (patti) thi.

Mr. Fazal ne cup ko geeli tile par rakha aur hatheli se zor se dabaya. Naira ne dekha ki cup chipak gaya. "Na glue, na screw," usne hairaan hokar kaha. Phir usne apna geela tauliya hook par latka diya. Hook tauliye ka poora wazan sambhal raha tha.

Tabhi Mr. Fazal ne cup ki chhoti si tab ko ungli se halka sa kheencha. **Phat!** Ek halki si sau-sau ki awaaz aayi aur hook tauliye samet neeche gir pada.

Naira ne poochha, "Chacha, ye hook itni der kisne pakad ke rakha tha? Aur tab kheenchte hi achanak kya ho gaya?"

Mr. Fazal muskuraye, "Beta, isme koi glue nahi tha. Isse **hawa** ne pakad ke rakha tha. Is kahani ko hum topic ke beech mein number ke saath solve karenge."

## Part A: Hawa ka wazan aur atmospheric pressure

### Hawa mein wazan hota hai

Hawa dikhti nahi, isliye lagta hai usme wazan nahi. Par hawa bhi gas ke chhote-chhote kanon (molecules) se bani hai, aur Prithvi unhe neeche kheenchti hai. To **hawa mein wazan hota hai**. Hamari Prithvi ke upar kai kilometre oonchi hawa ki parat hai, jise **atmosphere** kehte hain. Ye saari hawa apne wazan se neeche ki cheezon par dabti hai. Isi dabaav ko **atmospheric pressure** kehte hain.

Socho ek kitaabon ki almari: sabse neeche wali kitaab par upar ki saari kitaabon ka wazan hai. Hum bhi hawa ki is "almari" ke sabse neeche hain.

**Sea level par atmospheric pressure lagbhag:**

> **1 atm = 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa** (lagbhag 101 kPa)

Is topic mein hum **P(atm) = 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa ≈ 101300 Pa** lenge.

### Ye pressure kitna bada hai?

1 Pa matlab 1 N har 1 m² par. To 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa matlab har **1 m²** par lagbhag **1,01,300 N** ka bal. Itna bal lagbhag 10 tonne ke wazan jaisa hai. Fir bhi hume kuchh mehsoos kyun nahi hota?

### Hume ye dabaav mehsoos kyun nahi hota?

Do wajah:

1. **Hamare sharir ke andar bhi pressure hai.** Hamare khoon aur sharir ke hisson ke andar ka pressure bahar ke atmospheric pressure ke lagbhag barabar hai. Andar aur bahar ka pressure balance ho jaata hai, to koi net dabaav mehsoos nahi hota. Hum is dabaav ke aadi hain, jaise machhli paani ke dabaav mein rehti hai.
2. **Pressure har disha mein lagta hai.** Jaise paani mein pressure Topic 2 aur 3 mein har taraf lagta dekha tha, waise hi hawa ka pressure bhi upar, neeche, aage, peeche, tirche: **har taraf** barabar hota hai. Isliye hawa hume kisi ek taraf nahi dhakelti.

Ek aur dhyan rakhne wali baat: atmospheric pressure sirf **neeche ki taraf** nahi dabata. Ek khuli khidki par bahar ki hawa andar ki taraf aur andar ki hawa bahar ki taraf barabar dabaati hai.

## Part B: Atmospheric pressure ke simple pradarshan

### 1. Glass, paani aur card

Ek glass ko kinare tak paani se bharo. Upar ek moti, samtal card (ya plastic ka patla card) rakho, jisme koi ched na ho. Hatheli se card ko halka dabaye hue glass ko palat do, phir hatheli hata do.

```
      (glass ulta)
     +------------+
     │   paani    │
     │   paani    │
     │   paani    │
     +============+   <-- card
          ^ ^ ^
          │ │ │       hawa upar ki taraf dabati hai
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Ulte glass ka card nahi girta. Card ke neeche se bahar ki hawa upar ki taraf lagbhag 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa dabaati hai, jabki glass ke andar paani ka dabaav card par neeche ki taraf bahut kam hai.

Kyun nahi girta card? Glass mein paani ki oonchai sirf kuch cm hai, to us paani ka card par pressure P = h ρ g bahut chhota hai. Maan lo h = 10 cm = 0.10 m:

P = 0.10 m × 1000 kg/m³ × 9.8 m/s² = **980 Pa**

Jabki neeche se hawa lagbhag **1.013 × 10⁵ Pa** se upar dhakel rahi hai. 980 Pa bahut kam hai, to card tika rehta hai. (Ye tabhi hota hai jab glass ke andar hawa ka bulbula na ho, aur card poora geela ho taki hawa andar na ghuse.)

### 2. Suction hook (hamari kahani)

Hook ka rubber cup pehle hatheli se dabaya gaya. Dabane par cup ke andar ki **hawa bahar nikal gayi**. Ab cup ke andar ki hawa kam hai, to **andar ka pressure kam** ho gaya. Par cup ke **bahar** ki hawa ka pressure wahi purana (1 atm) hai. Bahar ka pressure zyada aur andar ka kam: iska matlab hai ki bahar ki hawa cup ko tile par **dabaati** hai.

```
        hook
         │
    +----+----+
    │  cup    │  <-- rubber cup
 ---+  (kam   +---     wall tile
    │ hawa)   │
    +---------+
   ->  bahar ka pressure (zyada)  <-
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Suction cup ke andar hawa kam hai, to andar ka pressure kam hai. Bahar ki hawa ka zyada pressure cup ko tile par dabaye rakhta hai.

Jab Mr. Fazal ne **tab** kheenchi, to cup ke kinare se thodi hawa andar chali gayi. Andar ka pressure badhkar bahar ke barabar ho gaya. Ab dono taraf ka pressure barabar hai, to koi net dabaav nahi bacha, aur hook tauliye ke wazan se **gir gaya**. Yaani **hook ko chipakaye rakhne wali cheez hawa ka bahar wala dabaav tha**.

### 3. Straw se peena

Straw se juice peete waqt hum kehte hain "main juice **kheench** raha hoon". Par asal mein kya hota hai?

- Munh se hawa kheenchne par straw aur munh ke andar ka pressure **kam** ho jaata hai.
- Glass mein juice ki khuli sataha par **atmospheric pressure** lag raha hai, jo andar ke kam pressure se zyada hai.
- Bahar ka zyada pressure juice ko straw mein **upar dhakelta** hai.

To juice ko kheencha nahi gaya, **atmosphere ne dhakela hai**. Agar glass band ho aur hawa andar na pahunche, ya straw mein chhed ho (hawa andar aa jaaye), to juice upar nahi aata.

### Aur kuchh jagah jahan yehi hota hai

- **Syringe:** plunger ko peeche kheenchne par syringe ke andar jagah badi aur pressure kam ho jaata hai. Bahar ka atmospheric pressure dawai ko neeche se andar dhakel deta hai.
- **Dropper:** rubber bulb ko dabakar chhodte hain, to andar ka pressure kam hota hai aur atmosphere liquid ko dropper mein bhar deta hai.
- **Rubber plunger (sink saaf karne wala):** dabane se andar ki hawa nikalti hai, aur bahar ka pressure plunger ko chipka deta hai.
- **Magdeburg hemispheres:** 1650 ke aaspaas Otto von Guericke ne do khokhle dhatu ke aadhe gole jodkar andar se hawa nikali, aur phir ghodon ki do teams bhi unhe alag nahi kar saki, kyunki bahar ka atmospheric pressure unhe dabaye hue tha.
- **Water pump (hand pump):** piston upar karne par andar pressure kam hota hai aur atmospheric pressure paani ko neeche se upar dhakel deta hai. (Ek aam hand pump kuchh metre se zyada gehraai se paani nahi utha sakta, iska karan Example 5 mein dekhenge.)

## Part C: Mercury barometer (Torricelli ka prayog)

Atmospheric pressure napne ke liye Italian vaigyanik **Torricelli** ne ek tareeka nikala.

### Bana kaise hai

1. Ek lagbhag **1 m lambi** kaanch ki nali lo, jiska ek sira band ho.
2. Use poori tarah **mercury** se bharo.
3. Ungli se khula sira band karke nali ko ulta karo aur khula sira **mercury ke ek bartan (trough)** mein dubo do, phir ungli hata do.

Mercury kuchh neeche utarta hai, par poora nahi. Wo ek jagah **ruk jaata hai**: trough ke mercury ki sataha se lagbhag **76 cm (760 mm)** upar.

```
        band sira
          +---+  --
          │   │   ^  yahan vacuum
          │   │   v  (lagbhag)
   --     │===│  <-- mercury ki oopari sataha
    ^     │ M │
 76 │     │ E │
 cm │     │ R │      atmosphere trough ki
    v   v │ C │ v    khuli sataha ko neeche dabati hai
   --  +--+---+--+  <-- trough ki sataha
       │ mercury │
       +---------+
```

> **Figure 4.3** – Mercury barometer: nali mein mercury ka column trough ki sataha se lagbhag 76 cm oonchai par rukta hai. Oopar ki khali jagah (lagbhag) vacuum hai.

Nali ke upar ki khali jagah mein **lagbhag vacuum** hai (bahut kam mercury ki vapour hoti hai). Matlab oopar se column par koi hawa ka dabaav nahi.

### Column kyun ruka rehta hai?

Trough ki sataha par jo hawa dabaati hai, wo mercury ko nali ke andar dhakel rahi hai. Nali ke andar column ka apna wazan neeche dabata hai. Jab dono barabar ho jaate hain, column ruk jaata hai.

Trough ki sataha ke **level par**:

> **Column ka pressure = atmospheric pressure**

Ab P = h ρ g (Topic 2 se). Mercury ki density ρ = 13600 kg/m³, g = **9.8 m/s²** (is poore topic mein), h = 0.76 m:

> P(atm) = h ρ g = 0.76 m × 13600 kg/m³ × 9.8 m/s² = **101293 Pa ≈ 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa**

(Topic 2 ke Example 4 mein yahi 76 cm ka mercury column g = 10 m/s² se 103360 Pa aaya tha. Yahan g = 9.8 m/s² liya hai, isliye 101293 Pa.)

Yehi sea level ka 1 atm hai. Isliye:

> **1 atm = 76 cm of Hg = 760 mm of Hg = 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa**

### Pressure ki alag-alag units

- **mm of Hg** (ya cm of Hg): pressure ko mercury ke column ki oonchai se batate hain.
- **torr:** 1 torr = 1 mm of Hg. To 760 torr = 1 atm.
- **Pa:** 1 mm of Hg = 0.001 m × 13600 kg/m³ × 9.8 m/s² = 133.28 Pa, yaani lagbhag **133.3 Pa**.

Jaanch: 760 × 133.3 Pa = 1,01,308 Pa ≈ 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa ✅

### Kya column ki oonchai nali ki chaudai ya tirchapan par nirbhar hai?

**Nahi.** Oonchai sirf **vertical (seedhi) oonchai** hai: trough ki sataha se column ki oopari sataha tak.

- **Nali moti ho ya patli:** pressure = h ρ g mein sirf h, ρ aur g hain, area nahi. To oonchai wahi 76 cm rahegi.
- **Nali ko tirchha karo:** column ki **lambai** badh jaayegi, par vertical oonchai wahi 76 cm rahegi.

### Paani ka barometer kyun nahi?

Paani ki density sirf 1000 kg/m³ hai, mercury se bahut kam. To wahi 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa ko sambhalne ke liye paani ka column bahut oonchaa chahiye: lagbhag **10.3 m** (Example 5 mein nikalenge). Itni lambi nali rakhna aur use bharna mushkil hai. Mercury bhaari hai (13600 kg/m³), isliye uska column sirf 76 cm ka hota hai.

### Aneroid barometer

**Aneroid barometer** mein mercury nahi hota. Isme ek patla, band, lagbhag khali dhatu ka dabba hota hai. Pressure badhne par dabba thoda dab jaata hai aur ghatne par thoda phail jaata hai. Is chhote badlav ko lever aur pointer dial par dikha dete hain. Ye halka, chhota, aur kahin le jaane mein aasan hai. **Mausam (weather) ki bhavishyavani** mein aur **altimeter** (oonchai napne ka yantra) mein yehi kaam aata hai.

### Oonchai ke saath pressure

Jaise-jaise hum oonchai par jaate hain, hamare upar hawa ki parat patli hoti jaati hai, to pressure **ghatta** hai. Aur oonchai par hawa bhi patli hoti hai. Takriban (approximately), **5.5 km ki oonchai par pressure sea level ka lagbhag aadha** ho jaata hai. Altimeter isi ghatte pressure ko dekhkar oonchai batata hai (formula hum yahan nahi sikhenge).

## Part D: Solved examples

Is Part mein **g = 9.8 m/s²**, mercury ki density **13600 kg/m³**, paani ki **1000 kg/m³** aur **P(atm) = 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa ≈ 101300 Pa** lenge. Ek hi sawaal mein kabhi g = 9.8 aur kabhi 10 mat milana.

### Example 1: 76 cm of Hg ko Pa mein badlo

h = 76 cm = 0.76 m, ρ = 13600 kg/m³, g = 9.8 m/s².

- P = h ρ g = 0.76 m × 13600 kg/m³ × 9.8 m/s²
- 0.76 × 13600 = 10336 kg/m², aur 10336 kg/m² × 9.8 m/s² = 101292.8 Pa
- **P ≈ 101293 Pa ≈ 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa**

**Jaanch:** 101293 Pa ÷ 133.3 Pa per mm of Hg = 759.9 mm ≈ 760 mm = 76 cm ✅

### Example 2: 1 m² par atmosphere ka bal

A = 1 m², P = 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa.

- F = P × A = 1.013 × 10⁵ N/m² × 1 m² = **1.013 × 10⁵ N** ≈ 10⁵ N

**Jaanch:** F ÷ A = 1.013 × 10⁵ N ÷ 1 m² = 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa ✅

### Example 3: Table top par bal, aur table toot kyun nahi jaati

Table top: 0.5 m × 0.4 m. Area A = 0.5 m × 0.4 m = 0.2 m².

- F = P × A = 1.013 × 10⁵ N/m² × 0.2 m² = **20260 N ≈ 2 × 10⁴ N**

Itna bal lagbhag 2000 kg ke wazan jaisa hai (20260 ÷ 9.8 = 2067 kg). Phir table pichak kyun nahi jaati? Kyunki **hawa table ke neeche se bhi upar ki taraf utna hi dabaati hai.** Upar wala 20260 N neeche ki taraf aur neeche wala 20260 N upar ki taraf: dono barabar, **net bal = 20260 − 20260 = 0 N**.

**Jaanch:** 20260 N ÷ 0.2 m² = 101300 Pa ✅

### Example 4: Hatheli par atmosphere ka bal

Hatheli ya sir ke upar ke hisse ka area A = 0.01 m².

- F = P × A = 1.013 × 10⁵ N/m² × 0.01 m² = **1013 N** (lagbhag 10³ N)

Ye lagbhag 103 kg ke wazan jaisa bal hai (1013 N ÷ 9.8 m/s² = 103 kg). Hum ise mehsoos nahi karte kyunki hamare hath ke neeche ki or aur sharir ke andar se bhi barabar pressure usko balance kar raha hai.

**Jaanch:** 1013 N ÷ 0.01 m² = 1,01,300 Pa ✅

### Example 5: Paani ka kitna oonchaa column sambhal sakta hai atmosphere?

Wohi 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa ko paani ke column se barabar rakho: P = h ρ g.

- h = P ÷ (ρ g) = 101300 Pa ÷ (1000 kg/m³ × 9.8 m/s²)
- h = 101300 ÷ 9800 = **10.34 m ≈ 10.3 m**

**Jaanch:** h ρ g = 10.34 m × 1000 kg/m³ × 9.8 m/s² = 101332 Pa ≈ 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa ✅

Isliye paani ka barometer bahut lamba hota, aur isliye ek aam hand pump jo sirf atmosphere ke dhakke se paani uthata hai, zyada se zyada lagbhag 10 m tak hi utha sakta hai.

### Example 6: Barometer ki reading 74 cm of Hg ho

h = 74 cm = 0.74 m.

- P = h ρ g = 0.74 m × 13600 kg/m³ × 9.8 m/s² = 98627 Pa ≈ **9.86 × 10⁴ Pa**
- atm mein: P ÷ 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa = 98627 ÷ 101300 = **0.974 atm**

Dusre tareeke se: 74 cm ÷ 76 cm = 0.974 atm.

**Jaanch:** 0.974 atm × 101300 Pa/atm = 98666 Pa, jo 98627 Pa ke bahut paas hai (antar rounding ka hai) ✅

### Example 7: Tel (oil) ka barometer, ρ = 800 kg/m³

Maan lo hum mercury ki jagah ek tel (density 800 kg/m³) ka barometer banaye (aur uski vapour ko ignore karein).

- h = P ÷ (ρ g) = 101300 Pa ÷ (800 kg/m³ × 9.8 m/s²)
- h = 101300 ÷ 7840 = **12.92 m ≈ 12.9 m**

**Jaanch:** 12.92 m × 800 kg/m³ × 9.8 m/s² = 101293 Pa ≈ 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa ✅

Tel mercury se bahut halka hai, to uska column bahut oonchaa (12.9 m) aata hai. Halki liquid = lamba column.

### Example 8: Kahani ka jawab: suction cup ka bal

Hook ke cup ka area A = 20 cm². Cm² ko m² mein badlo: 1 cm² = 10⁻⁴ m², to A = 20 × 10⁻⁴ m² = 2 × 10⁻³ m².

Bahar ka pressure P(out) = 1.0 × 10⁵ Pa. Cup ke andar ka pressure aadha: P(in) = 0.5 × 10⁵ Pa.

- Net dabaav = P(out) − P(in) = 1.0 × 10⁵ Pa − 0.5 × 10⁵ Pa = 0.5 × 10⁵ Pa = 5 × 10⁴ Pa
- Net bal F = (P(out) − P(in)) × A = 5 × 10⁴ N/m² × 2 × 10⁻³ m² = **100 N**

**Jaanch:** 100 N ÷ 9.8 m/s² ≈ 10.2 kg. Matlab cup lagbhag 10 kg tak ka wazan tile par rok sakta hai (jab tak cup aur tile ke beech se hawa leak na ho) ✅. Aur 100 N ÷ 2 × 10⁻³ m² = 5 × 10⁴ Pa ✅

Tab kheenchne par P(in) badhkar P(out) ke barabar ho gaya, to net dabaav 0 Pa, net bal = 0 N, aur hook gir gaya. Kahani solve!

### Example 9: Paani mein 10 m ki gehraai par gauge aur absolute pressure

Paani ke andar 10 m par:

- **Gauge pressure** (sirf paani ka) = h ρ g = 10 m × 1000 kg/m³ × 9.8 m/s² = **98000 Pa = 9.8 × 10⁴ Pa**
- **Absolute pressure** = atmospheric + gauge = 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa + 9.8 × 10⁴ Pa = 1.013 × 10⁵ + 0.98 × 10⁵ = **1.993 × 10⁵ Pa**

atm mein: 1.993 × 10⁵ ÷ 1.013 × 10⁵ = 1.97 atm ≈ **2 atm**.

**Jaanch:** 101300 Pa + 98000 Pa = 199300 Pa ✅

Yaani 10 m paani mein ek gotakhor par lagbhag 2 atm ka pressure hota hai: ek atm hawa ka, ek atm 10 m paani ka (ye Example 5 se match karta hai: 10.3 m paani ≈ 1 atm).

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Sucking" se kheench (pull) lagta hai.**
Straw se peete waqt hum juice ko kheenchte nahi. Hum sirf munh ke andar pressure kam karte hain, aur bahar ka atmospheric pressure juice ko upar **dhakelta** hai. Pull nahi, push.

**Galti 2: Atmosphere sirf neeche ki taraf dabaata hai.**
Nahi. Pressure har taraf lagta hai: upar, neeche, aage, peeche. Ulte glass ke card ko hawa **upar** ki taraf dhakel rahi thi.

**Galti 3: Barometer ki oonchai nali ki chaudai ya tirchhapan par nirbhar hai.**
Nahi. Sirf **vertical oonchai** matter karti hai. Moti nali ho, patli ho, ya tirchhi ho, trough ki sataha se oonchai wahi 76 cm hogi.

**Galti 4: 760 mm ko 76 m samajh lena.**
760 mm = 76 cm = 0.76 m, **76 m nahi**. 1 m = 1000 mm, to 760 mm = 0.76 m. Units hamesha badlo pehle: P = 0.76 m × 13600 × 9.8, aur 76 × 13600 × 9.8 nahi.

**Galti 5: Hawa mein wazan nahi hota.**
Hota hai. Ek kamre ki hawa ka wazan bhi kai kilogram hota hai. Atmospheric pressure isi hawa ke wazan se aata hai.

**Galti 6: Ek hi sawaal mein g = 9.8 aur g = 10 dono lena.**
Ek value chuno aur poore sawaal mein wahi use karo. Is topic mein g = 9.8 m/s².

**Galti 7: Table toot jaani chahiye kyunki 20000 N lag raha hai.**
Table ke neeche se bhi utna hi pressure upar ki taraf lagta hai. Net bal 0 N hai.

## Quick Recap

- Hawa ek fluid hai aur uska wazan hota hai. Hawa ki parat (atmosphere) ka wazan **atmospheric pressure** deta hai.
- **1 atm = 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa (lagbhag 101 kPa) = 76 cm of Hg = 760 mm of Hg.**
- Hume ye pressure mehsoos nahi hota kyunki sharir ke andar ka pressure usse balance karta hai, aur pressure **har disha mein** lagta hai.
- Suction cup, straw, syringe, dropper, plunger, hand pump, Magdeburg hemispheres: sab mein andar ka pressure kam hota hai aur **bahar ka atmospheric pressure dhakelta hai**.
- **Mercury barometer** (Torricelli): 1 m lambi nali, mercury se bhari, trough mein ulti. Column lagbhag 76 cm par ruk jaata hai, oopar lagbhag vacuum. P = h ρ g = 0.76 × 13600 × 9.8 = 101293 Pa.
- Column ki **vertical** oonchai nali ki chaudai aur tirchhepan par nirbhar nahi hoti.
- Paani ka barometer lagbhag 10.3 m ka hota, isliye mercury (bhaari) use hota hai.
- 1 mm of Hg = 1 torr ≈ 133.3 Pa.
- **Aneroid barometer** mausam ki bhavishyavani aur altimeter mein. Oonchai badhne par pressure ghatta hai (approximately 5.5 km par aadha).
- Oonchai par saans lene mein dikkat, naak se khoon aana, aur pahadon par khana der se pakna: ye sab kam pressure ke asar hain (bhaap ka asar Heat chapter mein). Kam pressure aksar kharab mausam ka sanket hota hai.
- Absolute pressure = atmospheric + gauge. Paani mein 10 m par lagbhag 2 atm.
- **Kahani ka jawab:** cup ke andar ka pressure kam, bahar ka 1 atm; net bal = (P(out) − P(in)) × A = 100 N. Tab kheenchne se andar hawa aayi, pressure barabar hua, hook gir gaya.

## Practice Questions

1. Convert a pressure of 70 cm of Hg into pascals. Use ρ(Hg) = 13600 kg/m³ and g = 9.8 m/s².

2. Find the force exerted by the atmosphere on a door of size 2 m × 1 m. Why does the door not get pushed in or out when it is closed? Use P = 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa.

3. A barometer shows 38 cm of Hg on a mountain. Find the pressure in pascals, and express it as a fraction of 1 atm.

4. A suction hook has a cup of area 50 cm². The outside pressure is 1.0 × 10⁵ Pa and the pressure inside the cup is 0.2 × 10⁵ Pa. Find the net force pressing the cup to the wall. (Convert the area to m² first.)

5. A liquid of density 1200 kg/m³ is used to make a barometer. How tall would the column be at 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa? Use g = 9.8 m/s².

6. Find the gauge pressure and the absolute pressure at a depth of 5 m in a lake. (ρ = 1000 kg/m³, g = 9.8 m/s².)

7. In a mercury barometer, the tube is tilted so that mercury fills 95 cm of the tube's length. What is the vertical height of the mercury column above the trough, if the atmospheric pressure is 76 cm of Hg? Explain.

8. Explain in your own words why juice rises up a straw. Who is pushing the juice up?

9. State True or False:
   (a) Air has no weight.
   (b) Atmospheric pressure acts only downwards.
   (c) A wider barometer tube gives a larger column height.
   (d) 760 mm of Hg is about 1 atm.

10. Why is mercury preferred over water for a barometer? Support your answer with a calculation of the height of the water column at 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa.

### Answers

1. h = 70 cm = 0.70 m. P = h ρ g = 0.70 m × 13600 kg/m³ × 9.8 m/s² = **93296 Pa ≈ 9.33 × 10⁴ Pa**.
   Jaanch: 93296 Pa ÷ 133.3 Pa per mm of Hg = 699.9 mm = 70 cm ✅

2. A = 2 m × 1 m = 2 m². F = P × A = 1.013 × 10⁵ N/m² × 2 m² = **2.026 × 10⁵ N = 2,02,600 N**. Door nahi hilta kyunki hawa door ke dono taraf barabar dabaati hai, to net bal 0 N hai.
   Jaanch: 202600 N ÷ 2 m² = 101300 Pa ✅

3. h = 38 cm = 0.38 m. P = 0.38 m × 13600 kg/m³ × 9.8 m/s² = **50646 Pa ≈ 5.06 × 10⁴ Pa**. Fraction: 38 ÷ 76 = **0.5**, yaani aadha atm.
   Jaanch: 0.5 × 101300 Pa = 50650 Pa ≈ 50646 Pa ✅

4. A = 50 cm² = 50 × 10⁻⁴ m² = 5 × 10⁻³ m². Net dabaav = 1.0 × 10⁵ − 0.2 × 10⁵ = 0.8 × 10⁵ Pa = 8 × 10⁴ Pa. F = 8 × 10⁴ N/m² × 5 × 10⁻³ m² = **400 N**.
   Jaanch: 400 N ÷ 5 × 10⁻³ m² = 8 × 10⁴ Pa ✅

5. h = P ÷ (ρ g) = 101300 Pa ÷ (1200 kg/m³ × 9.8 m/s²) = 101300 ÷ 11760 = **8.61 m**.
   Jaanch: 8.61 m × 1200 kg/m³ × 9.8 m/s² = 101254 Pa ≈ 1.013 × 10⁵ Pa ✅

6. Gauge pressure = h ρ g = 5 m × 1000 kg/m³ × 9.8 m/s² = **49000 Pa**. Absolute = 101300 Pa + 49000 Pa = **150300 Pa ≈ 1.50 × 10⁵ Pa** (lagbhag 1.5 atm).
   Jaanch: 150300 ÷ 101300 = 1.48 atm ✅

7. Vertical height = **76 cm**. Column ki lambai 95 cm hai (tirchhi), par pressure sirf vertical oonchai se tay hota hai (P = h ρ g mein h vertical). Tirchhapan se sirf lambai badli, oonchai nahi.
   Jaanch: 95 cm lambai ≥ 76 cm vertical oonchai (tirchhi nali mein lambai hamesha zyada hoti hai) ✅

8. Straw se hawa kheenchne par munh aur straw ke andar ka pressure kam ho jaata hai. Glass mein juice ki khuli sataha par atmospheric pressure bacha rehta hai. Ye zyada bahari pressure juice ko straw mein **upar dhakelta hai**. Juice ko **atmosphere** dhakel raha hai, hum nahi.

9. (a) **False** ❌. Hawa mein wazan hota hai, isi se atmospheric pressure aata hai.
   (b) **False** ❌. Pressure har disha mein lagta hai.
   (c) **False** ❌. Oonchai nali ki chaudai par nirbhar nahi karti.
   (d) **True** ✅. 760 mm of Hg = 1 atm.

10. Mercury ka column: h = 101300 ÷ (13600 × 9.8) = 101300 ÷ 133280 = 0.76 m. Paani ka column: h = 101300 ÷ (1000 × 9.8) = **10.34 m**. Paani ka column 13 guna se zyada lamba (10.34 ÷ 0.76 = 13.6) hota, isliye mercury (bhaari liquid) se chhoti nali bhi kaam kar jaati hai.
    Jaanch: 0.76 × 13.6 = 10.34 m ✅
