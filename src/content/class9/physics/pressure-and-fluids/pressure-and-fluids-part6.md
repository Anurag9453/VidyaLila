# Topic 6: Archimedes' Principle aur Relative Density

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki jab koi cheez kisi liquid (ya gas) mein dooba hoti hai, to liquid use **upar ki taraf dhakelta** hai. Is dhakke ko **upthrust** kehte hain. Humne ek dooba hua cube lekar, uske neeche aur upar ke pressure ka fark nikala, aur formula paaya:

> **Upthrust = ρ_fluid × g × V_immersed**

Humne ye bhi dekha ki spring balance mein cheez ka **apparent weight = weight − upthrust** hota hai, aur ye bhi ki upthrust ke kaaran cheez halki "lagti" hai. Ab ek sawal: agar upthrust ki value nikalne ke liye humesha cube jaisi seedhi shape chahiye, to **tedhi-medhi cheez** ka upthrust kaise nikalenge? Is sawal ka ek bahut seedha jawab ek purane vaigyanik ne diya. Wo jawab hi aaj ka topic hai.

## Sunar Mr. Verghese aur Anika ki chain

Anika apni mummy ke saath sunar Mr. Verghese ki dukaan par aayi thi. Mummy ne kuch din pehle wahin se ek bhaari sone ki chain kharidi thi, aur aaj uski safayi karwane aayi thi. Mummy kaam ke baare mein baat kar rahi thi, aur Anika chain ko dekhti rahi.

"Uncle," Anika ne puchha, "ye chain asli sone ki hai na? Kya pata, usme kuch aur milaya ho?"

Mr. Verghese muskuraye. "Achha sawal hai, beta. Sunar ke paas isko jaanchne ke kai tareeke hote hain. Ek bahut purana tareeka kisi vaigyanik ki kahani se jodta hai. **Kaha jaata hai** ki bahut saal pehle Yunaan ke ek vidwan **Archimedes** the. Wahan ke raja ne ek sone ka mukut banwaya tha, aur use shak tha ki sunar ne usme chandi mila di hai. Raja ne Archimedes se kaha ki mukut ko bina todhe jaanch kar batao."

"Phir?" Anika ne puchha.

"Kahani aage kehti hai ki Archimedes soch mein dooba tha, aur ek din nahane ke tub mein utarte hi usne dekha ki paani chhalak kar bahar gira. Kaha jaata hai ki use achanak jawab sujh gaya aur wo khushi se 'Eureka! Eureka!' (yaani 'mil gaya!') chillata hua bhaaga. Ye kahani bahut mashhoor hai, par **sunee-sunayi hai**: ye sab sach mein aise hi hua tha, iska pakka saboot nahi hai. Magar uske peeche jo vigyan hai, wo bilkul pakka hai, aur hum use kaaghaz-pen se aur ek chhote prayog se check kar sakte hain."

Mr. Verghese ne ek spring balance nikala, chain use latka kar tola, phir chain ko paani ke glass mein dubakar dobara tola. Dono padhne mein **kuch fark** tha. "Is fark mein hi chain ka raaz chhupa hai," unhone kaha. Is kahani ka jawab hum topic ke beech mein number ke saath nikalenge.

## Part A: Archimedes' principle

Topic 5 mein hum cube ke dono pressure ka fark nikalkar upthrust tak pahunche the. Ab usi natije ko ek **principle** ki tarah kehte hain:

> **Archimedes' principle:** Jab koi cheez kisi fluid mein **poori ya aadhi (partly)** dooba hoti hai, to us par **upar ki taraf** ek upthrust lagta hai, jo us cheez dwara **hataye gaye (displaced) fluid ke weight** ke barabar hota hai.
>
> **Upthrust = displaced fluid ka weight = ρ_fluid × V_displaced × g**

Ye wahi formula hai jo Topic 5 mein aaya tha. Dekhte hain kaise:

- Displaced fluid ka **volume** = cheez ka **dooba hua** volume (V).
- Us fluid ka **mass** = density × volume = ρ_fluid × V.
- Us mass ka **weight** = mass × g = ρ_fluid × V × g.

Yani "upthrust = ρ_fluid g V" aur "upthrust = displaced fluid ka weight" **ek hi baat** hain. Fayda ye hai ki ab cheez ki shape koi bhi ho: usse sirf ye dekhna hai ki **kitna fluid hata**.

Is topic mein hum **g = 10 m/s²** lenge (hisaab aasan rakhne ke liye), aur **paani ki density 1000 kg/m³ = 1 g/cm³** maanenge.

### Do zaroori baatein

1. **Volume wo lena hai jo fluid mein dooba hai.** Poori dooba cheez ke liye puri cheez ka volume, aadhi dooba ke liye aadhe ka volume.
2. **Density fluid ki leni hai**, cheez ki nahi. Cheez bhaari ho ya halki, upthrust sirf do cheezon par nirbhar hai: **fluid ki density** aur **kitna volume dooba**.

### Eureka can wala prayog

Archimedes' principle ko hum apne haath se check kar sakte hain.

**Kya chahiye:** ek eureka can (overflow can), ek patthar jisme dhaaga bandha ho, ek spring balance, ek khaali beaker, aur ek measuring cylinder.

**Kaise karte hain:**

1. Eureka can ko paani se itna bharo ki paani nali ke muh tak aa jaye aur tapakna band ho jaye.
2. Patthar ko spring balance se latka kar hawa mein tolo. Maan lo **weight in air = 3.0 N**.
3. Khaali beaker ko nali ke neeche rakho.
4. Patthar ko dheere se paani mein poora dubao (balance se latka rakhte hue). Jitna paani patthar ne hataya, wo nali se beaker mein aa girega.
5. Ab balance ka padhna dekho: **weight in water = 2.0 N**.
6. Beaker ke paani ko measuring cylinder mein daal kar volume napo.

```
        spring balance
            +---+
            │   │   <- padhna: 2.0 N
            +---+
              │
              │  dhaaga
        │     │     │
        │~~~~~│~~~~~│===\      <- paani nali ke muh tak
        │     │     │    \
        │     O     │     \
        │  patthar  │    +-v-+
        │  (poora   │    │~~~│  <- beaker: displaced paani
        │   dooba)  │    +---+
        +-----------+
          eureka can
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Eureka can: dooba hua patthar apne volume jitna paani nali se bahar bhej deta hai. Wo paani beaker mein jama hota hai, aur spring balance ka padhna kam ho jaata hai.

**Sample readings:**

| Cheez | Value |
|---|---|
| Patthar ka weight hawa mein | 3.0 N |
| Patthar ka weight paani mein | 2.0 N |
| Weight ka loss = 3.0 N − 2.0 N | 1.0 N |
| Beaker mein aaya paani | 100 mL |

Ab check karte hain ki principle sach hai ya nahi. Displaced paani ka weight nikalo:

- 100 mL = 100 cm³ = 100 × 10⁻⁶ m³ = 1 × 10⁻⁴ m³
- Mass = ρ × V = 1000 kg/m³ × 1 × 10⁻⁴ m³ = 0.1 kg
- Weight = m × g = 0.1 kg × 10 m/s² = **1.0 N**

**Jaanch:** Weight ka loss = 1.0 N aur displaced paani ka weight = 1.0 N. Dono barabar hain. ✅ Yani **loss of weight = upthrust = displaced paani ka weight**, bilkul Archimedes' principle ki tarah.

Dhyan do: spring balance ka padhna kam isliye hua ki patthar par upar ki taraf 1.0 N ka upthrust laga. Apparent weight = 3.0 N − 1.0 N = 2.0 N, jaisa Topic 5 mein bataya tha.

### Solved examples (upthrust)

**Example 1 (alag-alag liquids mein upthrust):** Ek chhoti cheez ka volume 5 × 10⁻⁵ m³ hai. Wo poori dooba hui hai. Upthrust nikalo: (a) paani mein (ρ = 1000 kg/m³), (b) kerosene mein (ρ = 800 kg/m³), (c) mercury mein (ρ = 13600 kg/m³). Lo g = 10 m/s².

Formula: upthrust = ρ × g × V

- (a) Paani: 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² × 5 × 10⁻⁵ m³ = **0.5 N**
- (b) Kerosene: 800 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² × 5 × 10⁻⁵ m³ = 0.4 N, yaani **0.4 N**
- (c) Mercury: 13600 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² × 5 × 10⁻⁵ m³ = **6.8 N**

**Jaanch:** Volume wahi hai, to upthrust density ke saath badhta hai. Paani se mercury ki density 13.6 guna hai, aur 0.5 N × 13.6 = 6.8 N ✅. Kerosene paani se halka hai, aur 0.4 < 0.5 ✅.

**Example 2 (patthar ka weight paani mein):** Ek patthar ka mass 0.6 kg aur volume 2.5 × 10⁻⁴ m³ hai. Wo paani mein poora dooba hai. Spring balance kya dikhayega?

- Weight in air = m × g = 0.6 kg × 10 m/s² = **6 N**
- Upthrust = ρ g V = 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² × 2.5 × 10⁻⁴ m³ = **2.5 N**
- Weight in water = 6 N − 2.5 N = **3.5 N**

**Jaanch:** 3.5 N + 2.5 N = 6 N ✅. Paani mein weight hawa wale weight se kam hai ✅.

**Example 3 (loss se displaced paani ka weight):** Ek cheez ka weight hawa mein 4.5 N aur paani mein 3.7 N hai. Displaced paani ka weight, mass aur volume nikalo.

- Loss of weight = 4.5 N − 3.7 N = **0.8 N**
- Archimedes ke hisaab se, displaced paani ka weight = **0.8 N**
- Displaced paani ka mass = W ÷ g = 0.8 N ÷ 10 m/s² = **0.08 kg** (80 g)
- Displaced paani ka volume = m ÷ ρ = 0.08 kg ÷ 1000 kg/m³ = 8 × 10⁻⁵ m³ = **80 cm³**

**Jaanch:** 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² × 8 × 10⁻⁵ m³ = 0.8 N ✅

**Example 4 (loss se cheez ka volume):** Ek tedhe-medhe patthar ka weight hawa mein 2.0 N hai, aur paani mein poora dooba kar tolne par 1.5 N aata hai. Patthar ka volume kya hai?

Yaha patthar ki shape se koi matlab nahi, bas loss dekhna hai.

- Loss = 2.0 N − 1.5 N = 0.5 N = upthrust
- Upthrust = ρ g V, to V = upthrust ÷ (ρ g) = 0.5 N ÷ (1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s²) = 0.5 ÷ 10000 = **5 × 10⁻⁵ m³** = **50 cm³**

**Jaanch:** 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² × 5 × 10⁻⁵ m³ = 0.5 N ✅. Yaani **irregular cheez ka volume** napne ka ye ek tareeka hai: loss of weight nikalo, aur ρ g se bhaag do (patthar ka volume measuring cylinder se bhi nap sakte the, par ye tareeka bhi chalta hai).

**Example 5 (aadha dooba block):** Ek lohe ka block 10 cm × 10 cm × 5 cm ka hai. Lohe ki density 7800 kg/m³. Block ko spring balance se latka kar aadha paani mein dubaya gaya. Balance kya dikhayega?

Pehle sab SI unit mein:

- Block ka volume = 0.10 m × 0.10 m × 0.05 m = 5 × 10⁻⁴ m³
- Mass = ρ × V = 7800 kg/m³ × 5 × 10⁻⁴ m³ = 3.9 kg
- Weight in air = 3.9 kg × 10 m/s² = **39 N**
- **Dooba hua volume** = aadha = 2.5 × 10⁻⁴ m³
- Upthrust = 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² × 2.5 × 10⁻⁴ m³ = **2.5 N**
- Balance ka padhna = 39 N − 2.5 N = **36.5 N**

**Jaanch:** Agar block poora dooba hota, to upthrust 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² × 5 × 10⁻⁴ m³ = 5 N hota. Aadhe ke liye 5 N ka aadha = 2.5 N ✅. Padhna 36.5 N, jo poore dooba hone par aane wale 34 N se zyada hai ✅.

**Example 6 (paani aur namkeen paani mein upthrust):** Ek cheez ka volume 2 × 10⁻⁴ m³ aur weight hawa mein 5 N hai. Ise (a) saaf paani (1000 kg/m³) aur (b) namkeen samudri paani (1030 kg/m³) mein poora dubaya. Dono mein upthrust aur balance ka padhna nikalo.

- (a) Upthrust = 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² × 2 × 10⁻⁴ m³ = **2.0 N**. Padhna = 5 − 2.0 = **3.0 N**
- (b) Upthrust = 1030 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² × 2 × 10⁻⁴ m³ = **2.06 N**. Padhna = 5 − 2.06 = **2.94 N**

**Jaanch:** Namkeen paani ki density zyada hai (1030 > 1000), to upthrust bhi zyada: 2.06 > 2.0 ✅. Fark = 0.06 N, aur 2.06 N / 2.0 N = 1.03, jo 1030 / 1000 ke barabar hai ✅. Isi liye samudra mein tairna nadi se thoda aasan lagta hai.

## Part B: Relative density (RD)

Hum ab tak "density" kg/m³ mein likhte rahe. Lekin kabhi kabhi hume sirf ye jaanna hota hai ki koi padarth **paani se kitna guna bhaari** hai. Iske liye relative density banayi gayi.

> **Relative density (RD) = padarth ki density ÷ paani ki density (4 °C par)**

Paani ki density 4 °C par 1000 kg/m³ hoti hai. Dono density ka **unit ek hi** hai (kg/m³ ÷ kg/m³), isliye unit **kat jaata hai**: **RD ka koi unit nahi hota.** Ye sirf ek **number (ratio)** hai.

**Matlab:** RD = 7.8 ka matlab hai "ye padarth same volume ke paani se 7.8 guna bhaari hai".

| Padarth | Density (kg/m³) | RD |
|---|---|---|
| Paani | 1000 | 1 |
| Lakdi (aam tor par) | 600 (kareeb) | 0.6 (kareeb) |
| Aluminium | 2700 | 2.7 |
| Lohe | 7800 | 7.8 |
| Mercury | 13600 | 13.6 |
| Sona | 19300 | 19.3 |

**Density aur RD ka rishta:**

> **Density (kg/m³) = RD × 1000 kg/m³**

**Example 7 (RD se density):** Aluminium ki RD 2.7 hai. Uski density kya hai? Aur 2 × 10⁻³ m³ aluminium ka mass?

- Density = RD × 1000 kg/m³ = 2.7 × 1000 kg/m³ = **2700 kg/m³**
- Mass = ρ × V = 2700 kg/m³ × 2 × 10⁻³ m³ = **5.4 kg**

**Jaanch:** 5.4 kg ÷ 2 × 10⁻³ m³ = 2700 kg/m³ ✅, aur 2700 ÷ 1000 = 2.7 ✅.

Dhyan do ki RD ki cheez ye hai ki **lakdi ki RD 0.6** hai, jo 1 se kam hai, aur lohe ki 7.8, jo 1 se zyada hai. Ye 1 se kam ya zyada hona kisi badi baat ki taraf ishara karta hai. Wo baat hum Topic 7 mein dekhenge. Abhi sirf ye note kar lo.

## Part C: Archimedes' principle se dooba hone wale solid ki RD

Ab asli kaam: ek aisi **thos cheez** ki RD kaise nikalein jo paani mein doob jaati hai (jaise sona, lohe ka tukda, chain)? Dekho, Archimedes' principle hume seedha tareeka deta hai. Bas do baar tolna hai: **hawa mein aur paani mein.**

### Derivation

Maan lo solid ka volume V hai, aur uski density ρ hai. Paani ki density ρ_w hai.

- **Weight in air** = ρ × V × g
- Solid poora dooba hai, to hataye gaye paani ka volume bhi V hai.
- **Loss of weight** = displaced paani ka weight = ρ_w × V × g

Dono ko bhaag dete hain:

> weight in air ÷ loss of weight = (ρ V g) ÷ (ρ_w V g) = ρ ÷ ρ_w = **RD**

V aur g dono **kat gaye**, isliye cheez ka volume pehle se jaanne ki bhi zaroorat nahi.

> **RD = weight in air ÷ (weight in air − weight in water) = weight in air ÷ loss of weight**

Dhyan rakho: **upar** hawa wala weight hai aur **neeche** loss. Ulta bhaagne par RD galat aayegi.

### Sunar ka tarika: chain ki jaanch

Ab Mr. Verghese ki kahani par wapas aate hain. Unhone chain ko spring balance se latkaya:

```
   HAWA MEIN                       PAANI MEIN

    +---+                           +---+
    │   │  1.93 N                   │   │  1.83 N
    +---+                           +---+
      │                               │
      │                          │    │    │
    chain                        │~~~~│~~~~│
   (hawa mein)                   │  chain  │
                                 │ (dooba) │
                                 +---------+
                                    glass
```

> **Figure 6.2** – Chain ko pehle hawa mein (1.93 N) aur phir paani mein dubakar (1.83 N) tola gaya. Dono padhne ka fark upthrust hai.

**Example 8 (chain ki RD):** Weight in air = 1.93 N, weight in water = 1.83 N. RD nikalo, aur batao ki chain asli sone ki hai ya nahi (sone ki RD = 19.3).

- Loss of weight = 1.93 N − 1.83 N = **0.10 N**
- RD = weight in air ÷ loss = 1.93 N ÷ 0.10 N = **19.3**
- Density = 19.3 × 1000 kg/m³ = **19300 kg/m³**

19.3 bilkul sone ki RD jaisi hai. Isliye chain **asli sone ki hai.**

**Jaanch:** Chain ka volume = loss ÷ (ρ g) = 0.10 ÷ (1000 × 10) = 1 × 10⁻⁵ m³. Chain ka mass = 1.93 N ÷ 10 m/s² = 0.193 kg. Density = 0.193 kg ÷ 1 × 10⁻⁵ m³ = 19300 kg/m³ ✅ (sone ki density).

Ab dusri chain sochte hain, jisme sone ke saath sasti dhatu mili ho. Maan lo uska weight in air 1.50 N aur weight in water 1.40 N aaya.

**Example 9 (milawati chain):** Loss = 1.50 N − 1.40 N = 0.10 N. RD = 1.50 ÷ 0.10 = **15**. Density = 15 × 1000 = **15000 kg/m³**.

15 sone ki RD (19.3) se **kam** hai, yaani is chain mein koi halki dhatu mili hai: chain **pure sone ki nahi** hai.

**Jaanch:** Agar ye 1.50 N ki chain pure sone ki hoti, to uska loss = 1.50 ÷ 19.3 ≈ 0.078 N aata. Hamare reading mein loss 0.10 N tha, jo zyada hai. Zyada loss yaani zyada volume, yaani ye chain usi weight ke liye "halki dhatu" se bani hai ✅.

Ye hai Mr. Verghese ka jawab. Anika ki mummy ki chain ka RD 19.3 aaya tha, to wo asli nikli.

### Sunar ke tareeke ki ek zaroori shart

Ye tareeka tab kaam karta hai jab solid paani mein **doob jaye** aur **paani mein ghule nahi** (jaise sone, lohe, patthar). Jo cheez paani mein tairne lagti hai (jaise lakdi), us par ye tareeka seedha kaam nahi karta. Tairti cheez ki density uske doobe hue hisse se kaise pata chalti hai, wo Topic 7 mein.

## Part D: Liquid ki RD (jaanne ke liye, optional)

Ye part chhota hai. Isme hum dekhte hain ki **kisi liquid ki RD** bhi spring balance se nikal sakte hain.

Ek solid lo jo dono liquid (paani aur wo liquid) mein doob jaaye. Use **teen baar** tolo: hawa mein, paani mein, aur us liquid mein. Solid ka volume V har baar wahi hai.

- Paani mein loss = ρ_w × V × g
- Liquid mein loss = ρ_l × V × g

Dono ko bhaag do:

> loss in liquid ÷ loss in water = ρ_l ÷ ρ_w = **RD of liquid**

**Example 10 (liquid ki RD):** Ek solid ka weight hawa mein 5.0 N, paani mein 3.0 N, aur ek tel mein 3.4 N hai. Tel ki RD aur density nikalo.

- Loss in water = 5.0 N − 3.0 N = **2.0 N**
- Loss in tel = 5.0 N − 3.4 N = **1.6 N**
- RD of tel = loss in tel ÷ loss in water = 1.6 N ÷ 2.0 N = **0.8**
- Density = 0.8 × 1000 = **800 kg/m³**

**Jaanch:** Solid ka volume = 2.0 N ÷ (1000 × 10) = 2 × 10⁻⁴ m³. Tel mein upthrust = 800 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² × 2 × 10⁻⁴ m³ = 1.6 N ✅, jo loss in tel se match karta hai.

## Part E: Kuch aur uses (naam hi jaano)

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Aadhi dooba cheez ke liye poora volume lena**

Example 5 mein block ka volume 5 × 10⁻⁴ m³ hai, par wo sirf aadha dooba hai. Agar poora volume le liya to upthrust 5 N aa jayega, jabki sahi 2.5 N hai. **Sirf dooba hua volume** lo.

**Galti 2: RD ko density samajhna (ya uske saath unit likhna)**

Lohe ki RD 7.8 hai, 7.8 kg/m³ nahi. Density 7800 kg/m³ hai. RD ek ratio hai, isliye uska koi unit nahi.

**Galti 3: Mass aur weight mein gadbad**

Spring balance **weight** (N) batata hai, mass (kg) nahi. Is topic mein hum sab jagah weight N mein le rahe hain. Agar kisi sawal mein mass kg mein diya ho, to pehle weight = m × g se N banao. Aam bolchal mein "300 gram" ka matlab mass hai, weight nahi.

**Galti 4: Ulta bhaag dena**

Sahi: RD = weight **in air** ÷ loss. Agar weight in water ÷ weight in air kar diya (jaise chain mein 1.83 ÷ 1.93 ≈ 0.95), to RD 0.95 aa jayegi, jo sone ke liye bilkul galat hai. Sahi 19.3 tha.

**Galti 5: Paani ki density bhool jaana**

Upthrust ya volume nikalte waqt ρ_w = 1000 kg/m³ lagana mat bhoolo. Bina ρ ke "upthrust = g × V" likhne par jawab 1000 guna chhota ho jayega.

**Galti 6: Kahani ko pakka itihaas samajhna**

Archimedes ki mukut aur "Eureka" wali kahani **kaha jaata hai** wali kahani hai; iska pakka saboot nahi. Pakka ye hai ki **Archimedes' principle** sach hai, aur hum use prayog se check kar chuke hain.

## Quick Recap

- **Archimedes' principle:** poori ya aadhi dooba cheez par upar ki taraf upthrust = displaced fluid ka weight = **ρ_fluid × V_displaced × g**.
- Ye Topic 5 ke ρ g V ka hi principle roop hai. Shape koi bhi ho, bas **dooba hua volume** aur **fluid ki density** dekho.
- **Eureka can prayog:** loss of weight (3.0 − 2.0 = 1.0 N) = displaced paani ka weight (0.1 kg × 10 m/s² = 1.0 N).
- **Relative density = padarth ki density ÷ paani ki density.** Koi unit nahi. **Density = RD × 1000 kg/m³.**
- **Doobne wale solid ki RD = weight in air ÷ (weight in air − weight in water).** Weight in air upar, loss neeche.
- **Liquid ki RD = loss in liquid ÷ loss in water** (wahi solid dono mein).
- **Kahani ka jawab:** chain: loss = 1.93 − 1.83 = 0.10 N, RD = 19.3 = sone ki RD, to chain asli. 1.50 N / 1.40 N wali chain ki RD = 15, jo kam hai, to wo milawati.
- Hydrometer aur lactometer ki baat Topic 7 mein.

## Practice Questions

1. A cube of side 5 cm is completely immersed in water. Find the volume of water it displaces and the upthrust on it. (Take g = 10 m/s², density of water = 1000 kg/m³.)

2. A stone weighs 8.0 N in air and 5.5 N when fully immersed in water. Find (a) the loss of weight, (b) the volume of the stone, and (c) the relative density of the stone.

3. A solid of volume 3 × 10⁻⁴ m³ is completely immersed in kerosene of density 800 kg/m³. Find the upthrust. What would the upthrust be if it were in water?

4. A silver piece weighs 1.05 N in air and 0.95 N in water. Find its relative density and its density in kg/m³.

5. The relative density of aluminium is 2.7. Find its density in kg/m³ and the mass of 4 × 10⁻³ m³ of it.

6. A solid weighs 6.0 N in air, 4.0 N in water and 4.6 N in a liquid X. Find the relative density and the density of liquid X.

7. A cylinder of volume 6 × 10⁻⁴ m³ is held half-immersed in water. Find the upthrust. A student used the full volume and got 6 N. What was his mistake?

8. State True or False:
   (a) The upthrust on an immersed body is equal to the weight of the fluid it displaces.
   (b) Relative density is measured in kg/m³.
   (c) For a solid that sinks, RD = weight in water ÷ weight in air.
   (d) The story of Archimedes and the king's crown is a fully proven historical event.

9. A chain weighs 0.965 N in air and 0.915 N in water. Is it pure gold (RD 19.3)? Another chain weighs 1.20 N in air and 1.12 N in water. Is it pure gold?

10. In an eureka can experiment, 250 mL of water overflows when an object is completely dipped. (a) Find the weight of this water. (b) If the object weighs 6.0 N in air, what will the spring balance read in water?

### Answers

1. Volume = 0.05 m × 0.05 m × 0.05 m = 1.25 × 10⁻⁴ m³ (= 125 cm³), yahi displaced paani ka volume hai. Upthrust = ρ g V = 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² × 1.25 × 10⁻⁴ m³ = **1.25 N**.
   Jaanch: displaced paani ka mass = 1000 kg/m³ × 1.25 × 10⁻⁴ m³ = 0.125 kg, weight = 0.125 kg × 10 m/s² = 1.25 N ✅

2. (a) Loss = 8.0 N − 5.5 N = **2.5 N**.
   (b) V = 2.5 N ÷ (1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s²) = **2.5 × 10⁻⁴ m³** (250 cm³).
   (c) RD = 8.0 N ÷ 2.5 N = **3.2**.
   Jaanch: 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² × 2.5 × 10⁻⁴ m³ = 2.5 N ✅; density = 3.2 × 1000 = 3200 kg/m³, mass = 0.8 kg, 0.8 ÷ 2.5 × 10⁻⁴ = 3200 ✅

3. Kerosene: 800 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² × 3 × 10⁻⁴ m³ = **2.4 N**. Paani mein: 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² × 3 × 10⁻⁴ m³ = **3.0 N**.
   Jaanch: 2.4 ÷ 3.0 = 0.8 = 800 ÷ 1000 ✅

4. Loss = 1.05 − 0.95 = 0.10 N. RD = 1.05 ÷ 0.10 = **10.5**. Density = 10.5 × 1000 = **10500 kg/m³**.
   Jaanch: 0.10 × 10.5 = 1.05 ✅

5. Density = 2.7 × 1000 = **2700 kg/m³**. Mass of 4 × 10⁻³ m³ = 2700 × 4 × 10⁻³ = **10.8 kg**.
   Jaanch: 10.8 ÷ 4 × 10⁻³ = 2700 ✅

6. Loss in water = 6.0 − 4.0 = 2.0 N. Loss in X = 6.0 − 4.6 = 1.4 N. RD of X = 1.4 ÷ 2.0 = **0.7**, density = **700 kg/m³**.
   Jaanch: V = 2.0 ÷ 10000 = 2 × 10⁻⁴ m³; 700 × 10 × 2 × 10⁻⁴ = 1.4 N ✅

7. Dooba hua volume = 3 × 10⁻⁴ m³. Upthrust = 1000 kg/m³ × 10 m/s² × 3 × 10⁻⁴ m³ = **3 N**. Student ne poora volume liya, jabki cylinder sirf aadha dooba hai. Sahi jawab 3 N hai, 6 N nahi.
   Jaanch: 6 N ka aadha = 3 N ✅

8. (a) **True** ✅. Yahi Archimedes' principle hai.
   (b) **False** ❌. RD ek ratio hai, uska koi unit nahi.
   (c) **False** ❌. RD = weight in air ÷ loss of weight.
   (d) **False** ❌. Wo ek mashhoor kahani hai ("kaha jaata hai"); iska pakka saboot nahi hai. Principle zaroor sach hai.

9. Pehli chain: loss = 0.965 − 0.915 = 0.05 N, RD = 0.965 ÷ 0.05 = **19.3**: pure gold. Doosri chain: loss = 1.20 − 1.12 = 0.08 N, RD = 1.20 ÷ 0.08 = **15**, jo 19.3 se kam hai: **pure gold nahi**.
   Jaanch: 0.05 × 19.3 = 0.965 ✅; 0.08 × 15 = 1.20 ✅

10. (a) 250 mL = 250 cm³ = 2.5 × 10⁻⁴ m³. Mass = 1000 × 2.5 × 10⁻⁴ = 0.25 kg. Weight = 0.25 kg × 10 m/s² = **2.5 N**.
    (b) Upthrust = displaced paani ka weight = 2.5 N. Balance ka padhna = 6.0 − 2.5 = **3.5 N**.
    Jaanch: 3.5 + 2.5 = 6.0 N ✅

---

Ab aap Anika ko bata sakte ho ki chain ka raaz kaise khulta hai: do baar tolo, loss nikalo, aur weight in air ko loss se bhaag do. Agar RD 19.3 aaye, to chain sone ki hai. Aur ye sab Archimedes' principle ki wajah se hota hai.

**Aage kya?** Ab tak hum dooba hui cheezon ke upthrust ki baat kar rahe the.
