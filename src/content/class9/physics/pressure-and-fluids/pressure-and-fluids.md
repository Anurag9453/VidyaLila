# Topic 1: Thrust aur Pressure

Gravitation chapter mein humne dekha ki Earth har cheez ko neeche kheenchti hai, aur us kheenchav ko hum **weight** kehte hain (Topic 5 mein weight = m × g). Ab ek naya sawal: jab koi cheez kisi sateh (surface) par rakhi hoti hai, to wo us sateh ko **dabati** hai. Ye dabav kitna "chubhta" hai, ye sirf weight par nahi, **kis jagah par** dabaya ja raha hai us par bhi depend karta hai. Is chapter ka naam hai **Pressure aur Fluids**, aur pehle topic mein hum seekhenge ki **force** aur **pressure** mein kya farak hai.

## Hussain ji ka tez chaaku aur Anaya ka sawal

Anaya chhutti mein apne ghar ki kitchen mein baithi thi. Cook Mr. Hussain salad bana rahe the. Unhone ek **tez dhaar wala chaaku** liya aur tamatar par halka sa dabaya: tamatar "khach" se do tukde ho gaya, bina zor lagaye.

Anaya ne hairaan hokar poochha, "Hussain uncle, dono baar aapne haath se dhakka to lagbhag ek-sa hi diya, phir ek chaaku se tamatar kat gaya aur doosre se pichak gaya. Aisa kyun?"

Mr. Hussain muskuraye, "Dhakka (force) ek-sa ho sakta hai, beta, par asli khel is baat ka hai ki wo dhakka **kitni chhoti jagah** par laga hai. Chalo, iska hisaab hum topic ke beech mein number ke saath karte hain."

## Part A: Fluid, thrust aur pressure kya hain

### Fluid kise kehte hain

Jo cheezein **beh sakti hain** (jinka apna fixed aakaar nahi hota aur wo bartan ka aakaar le leti hain), unhe **fluid** kehte hain. Fluid do tarah ke hote hain:

- **Liquid:** paani, doodh, tel, petrol.
- **Gas:** hawa, oxygen, LPG.

Is chapter mein hum dekhenge ki fluids kaise force lagate hain aur un par kaun se force lagte hain. Aaj ki shuruaat solid se karte hain, kyunki wahi sabse aasan hai.

### Thrust: sateh par seedha (perpendicular) force

Jab koi force kisi sateh par **seedha 90° par** (right angle par) lagta hai, to use **thrust** kehte hain. Thrust ek force hi hai, isliye iska unit bhi **newton (N)** hai.

```
        thrust (haath ka dabav)
          vvv
   +-----------+
   ¦   TAMATAR ¦
 ==+===========+==  <- chopping board (sateh)
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Chaaku ya haath tamatar ko board par seedha (90° par) dabata hai. Ye seedha dabav hi thrust hai.

Ek baat yaad rakho: agar force sateh par **tirchha** lage, to sirf uska wo hissa jo sateh ke **perpendicular** hai, thrust kehlata hai. Class 9 mein hum zyadatar wahi cases lenge jahan force seedha sateh par lagta hai.

### Solid ka weight kab thrust banta hai

Jab koi solid cheez **seedhi (horizontal) sateh par rakhi** ho, to wo us sateh par apna **weight** hi thrust ke roop mein lagati hai. Weight ka formula (Gravitation Topic 5, [gravitation-part5.md](../gravitation/gravitation-part5.md)) hum jaante hain:

> **F = W = m × g**

Is chapter mein hum aasaan hisaab ke liye **g = 10 m/s²** lenge (jahan alag hoga wahan bata denge).

Jaise 2 kg ki cheez ka weight = 2 kg × 10 m/s² = **20 N**.

### Pressure: thrust per unit area

Thrust akela poori kahani nahi batata. Kahani ye hai ki wo thrust **kitne area** par fail raha hai. Isiliye hum **pressure** banate hain:

> **Pressure = Thrust ÷ Area**
>
> **P = F ÷ A**

**SI unit:** F ka unit N hai, A ka unit m², to

> P ka unit = N ÷ m² = **N/m²**, jise **pascal (Pa)** kehte hain.
>
> **1 Pa = 1 N/m²**

(Ye naam Blaise Pascal ke samman mein hai.)

**Matlab kya hai:** 1 Pa ka matlab hai ki **har 1 m² par 1 N ka thrust** lag raha hai. 1 Pa bahut chhota pressure hai, isliye bade unit use karte hain:

| Unit | Kitna Pa | Kab use hota hai |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Pa | 1 N/m² | Bahut halke dabav |
| 1 kPa | 1000 Pa | Roz ke dabav (jaise insaan ka khade hona) |
| 1 bar | 10⁵ Pa = 100 kPa | Hawa aur gaadi ke tyre jaise bade dabav |

### Area ko hamesha m² mein badlo

Pressure Pa mein tabhi aayega jab F newton mein ho aur A **m²** mein ho. Isliye cm² aur mm² ko pehle m² mein badalna zaroori hai:

> 1 cm = 10⁻² m, to **1 cm² = (10⁻² m)² = 10⁻⁴ m²**
>
> 1 mm = 10⁻³ m, to **1 mm² = (10⁻³ m)² = 10⁻⁶ m²**

Ye yaad rakhna: cm² se m² mein jaate waqt **100 se nahi, 10,000 se** bhaag dete hain.

## Part B: Same force, alag pressure: area ka kamaal

Pressure ka formula P = F ÷ A bahut kuch batata hai:

- **Area chhota** to pressure **bada** (force ek-sa ho tab bhi).
- **Area bada** to pressure **chhota**.

Ye bilkul waise hai jaise ek chocolate bar ko 2 logon mein bantna ya 20 logon mein: jitne zyada log, utna kam hissa har ek ko.

### Pressure kam ya zyada kaise kiya ja sakta hai

| Kya badla | Force F | Area A | Pressure P = F ÷ A |
|---|---|---|---|
| Force badhaya, area wahi | Badha | Wahi | **Badha** |
| Force ghataya, area wahi | Ghata | Wahi | **Ghata** |
| Force wahi, area ghataya | Wahi | Ghata | **Badha** |
| Force wahi, area badhaya | Wahi | Badha | **Ghata** |

### Jab hume pressure BADHANA ho: area chhota karo

- **Tez chaaku, kainchi, kulhaadi (axe):** dhaar bahut patli hoti hai, area bahut kam, isliye halke dhakke se bhi bahut zyada pressure banta hai aur cheez kat jaati hai.
- **Keel (nail), pin, sui, drawing pin:** nok ka area bahut kam hota hai, isliye wo lakdi ya board mein aasani se ghus jaati hai.
- **Ladki ki high heel:** heel ka area bahut chhota, isliye zameen par bahut zyada pressure.

### Jab hume pressure GHATANA ho: area bada karo

- **School bag ke chaude (wide) straps:** kandhe par bag ka weight badi jagah par fail jaata hai, to kandha kam dukhta hai.
- **Tractor ke chaude tyre:** tractor kheton ki naram mitti mein dhansta nahi.
- **Bulldozer / tank ki chain tracks:** poora weight lambi chain par fail jaata hai.
- **Camel (oont) ke chaude pair:** registan ki naram ret mein nahi dhanste.
- **Snowshoes aur skis:** barf par chalte waqt insaan barf mein nahi dhansta kyunki area bada ho jaata hai.
- **Flat joote:** high heel ke mukable pressure bahut kam.

```
   Neeche se dekho (area):          Same weight W:

   Nok wali keel:   .               P bahut zyada
   Keel ka sir:     [====]          P bahut kam
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Keel ki nok bahut chhoti jagah par force deti hai (bahut zyada pressure), jabki sir badi jagah par (kam pressure). Isiliye hathode se sir par maarte hain aur nok lakdi mein ghusti hai.

**Ek zaroori baat:** chaude pair wale object ka weight kam nahi ho jaata. Camel ya tractor ka **weight (force) wahi rehta hai**, bas wo **badi jagah par fail** jaata hai, isliye **pressure kam** hota hai.

## Part C: Solved examples

Is Part mein g = 10 m/s² lenge.

### Example 1: Kahani wala sawaal (tez chaaku vs kund chaaku)

Mr. Hussain ne dono chaakuon par **20 N** ka thrust lagaya. Tez chaaku ki dhaar **0.5 mm × 10 cm** hai aur kund chaaku ki **2 mm × 10 cm**.

**Tez chaaku ka area (A₁):**
- 0.5 mm = 0.5 × 10⁻³ m = 5 × 10⁻⁴ m
- 10 cm = 10 × 10⁻² m = 0.1 m
- A₁ = 5 × 10⁻⁴ m × 0.1 m = **5 × 10⁻⁵ m²**

**Tez chaaku ka pressure:**
- P₁ = F ÷ A₁ = 20 N ÷ (5 × 10⁻⁵ m²) = **4 × 10⁵ Pa** (= 400 kPa)

**Kund chaaku ka area (A₂):**
- 2 mm = 2 × 10⁻³ m
- A₂ = 2 × 10⁻³ m × 0.1 m = **2 × 10⁻⁴ m²**

**Kund chaaku ka pressure:**
- P₂ = 20 N ÷ (2 × 10⁻⁴ m²) = **10⁵ Pa** (= 100 kPa)

**Tulna:** P₁ ÷ P₂ = 4 × 10⁵ ÷ 10⁵ = **4**. Tez chaaku ka pressure **4 guna** hai. Dhaar ka area 4 guna kam hai (A₂ ÷ A₁ = 2 × 10⁻⁴ ÷ 5 × 10⁻⁵ = 4).

**Jaanch:** P₁ × A₁ = 4 × 10⁵ Pa × 5 × 10⁻⁵ m² = 20 N ✅ aur P₂ × A₂ = 10⁵ Pa × 2 × 10⁻⁴ m² = 20 N ✅

Yani Anaya ke sawaal ka jawab: force dono mein 20 N hi tha, par tez chaaku ne wahi force 4 guna chhoti jagah par lagaya, isliye pressure 4 guna zyada aur tamatar kat gaya. Kund chaaku ko tamatar kaatne ke liye jitna pressure chahiye, wo dene ke liye 4 guna zyada force lagana padta, aur uske pehle hi tamatar pichak jaata.

### Example 2: Pressure nikalna (F aur A diye hain)

Ek mez par 100 N ka thrust 0.5 m² area par lag raha hai. Pressure kitna hai?

- P = F ÷ A = 100 N ÷ 0.5 m² = **200 Pa**

**Jaanch:** 200 Pa × 0.5 m² = 100 N ✅

### Example 3: Force nikalna (P aur A diye hain)

Ek sateh par 5000 Pa ka pressure hai aur area 0.02 m² hai. Thrust kitna hai?

- Formula se: F = P × A
- F = 5000 Pa × 0.02 m² = 5000 N/m² × 0.02 m² = **100 N**

**Jaanch:** P = 100 N ÷ 0.02 m² = 5000 Pa ✅

### Example 4: Area nikalna (F aur P diye hain)

Ek 600 N ke thrust se 15000 Pa ka pressure banta hai. Area kitna hai?

- A = F ÷ P = 600 N ÷ 15000 Pa = 600 N ÷ 15000 N/m² = **0.04 m²**

**Jaanch:** 600 N ÷ 0.04 m² = 15000 Pa ✅

### Example 5: 60 kg ka insaan, do pair vs ek pair

Ek insaan ka mass 60 kg hai. Dono pairon ka kul area 0.04 m² hai. Dono pairon par khade hone par aur sirf ek pair par khade hone par pressure nikalo.

**Weight (thrust):**
- F = m × g = 60 kg × 10 m/s² = **600 N**

**Dono pairon par:**
- A = 0.04 m²
- P = 600 N ÷ 0.04 m² = **15000 Pa** (= 15 kPa)

**Ek pair par:** ek pair ka area = 0.04 m² ÷ 2 = 0.02 m².
- P = 600 N ÷ 0.02 m² = **30000 Pa** (= 30 kPa)

**Jaanch:** 15000 Pa × 0.04 m² = 600 N ✅ aur 30000 Pa × 0.02 m² = 600 N ✅

Weight (600 N) dono baar wahi hai, par ek pair par khade hone par area aadha hua, to pressure **doguna** ho gaya.

### Example 6: Eent (brick) ke teen faces

Ek eent ka size 20 cm × 10 cm × 5 cm hai aur mass 2 kg hai. Use teen alag tarah se table par rakhne par pressure nikalo.

**Weight:** F = 2 kg × 10 m/s² = **20 N** (har tarah rakhne par wahi).

```
          20 cm
     +--------------+
    /              /|
   /     10 cm    / | 5 cm
  +--------------+  |
  ¦              ¦ /
  +--------------+/
```

> **Figure 1.3** – 20 cm × 10 cm × 5 cm ki eent. Badi sateh (20 × 10) par rakho to area sabse zyada, aur khadi (10 × 5) rakho to area sabse kam.

**Face 1: lete hue (sabse badi sateh), 20 cm × 10 cm**
- A = 20 cm × 10 cm = 200 cm² = 200 × 10⁻⁴ m² = **0.02 m²**
- P = 20 N ÷ 0.02 m² = **1000 Pa**

**Face 2: side par, 20 cm × 5 cm**
- A = 20 cm × 5 cm = 100 cm² = 100 × 10⁻⁴ m² = **0.01 m²**
- P = 20 N ÷ 0.01 m² = **2000 Pa**

**Face 3: khadi (end par), 10 cm × 5 cm**
- A = 10 cm × 5 cm = 50 cm² = 50 × 10⁻⁴ m² = **0.005 m²**
- P = 20 N ÷ 0.005 m² = **4000 Pa**

| Rakhne ka tareeka | Area (m²) | Pressure (Pa) |
|---|---|---|
| Lete hue (20 × 10) | 0.02 | 1000 |
| Side par (20 × 5) | 0.01 | 2000 |
| Khadi (10 × 5) | 0.005 | 4000 |

**Jaanch:** 1000 × 0.02 = 20 N ✅, 2000 × 0.01 = 20 N ✅, 4000 × 0.005 = 20 N ✅. Sabse chhota face (khadi eent) par pressure sabse zyada (4000 Pa), sabse bade face par sabse kam (1000 Pa). Force teeno mein wahi 20 N hai.

### Example 7: Keel ki nok aur sir

Ek keel ko 50 N ke thrust se thoka ja raha hai. Nok ka area 1 mm² hai aur sir ka area 1 cm² hai. Dono jagah pressure nikalo.

**Nok par:**
- A = 1 mm² = 1 × 10⁻⁶ m²
- P = 50 N ÷ (1 × 10⁻⁶ m²) = **5 × 10⁷ Pa** (= 50,000 kPa)

**Sir par:**
- A = 1 cm² = 1 × 10⁻⁴ m²
- P = 50 N ÷ (1 × 10⁻⁴ m²) = **5 × 10⁵ Pa** (= 500 kPa)

**Tulna:** (5 × 10⁷) ÷ (5 × 10⁵) = **100**. Nok par pressure sir ke mukable **100 guna** hai, kyunki area 100 guna kam hai (1 cm² = 100 mm²).

**Jaanch:** 5 × 10⁷ Pa × 10⁻⁶ m² = 50 N ✅ aur 5 × 10⁵ Pa × 10⁻⁴ m² = 50 N ✅

### Example 8: Haathi vs high heel wali ladki

Ek haathi ka mass 4000 kg hai aur uske chaar pairon ka kul area 0.4 m² hai. Ek ladki ka mass 40 kg hai aur uski do heels ka kul area 2 cm² hai (har heel 1 cm²). Kiska pressure zyada hai?

**Haathi:**
- F = 4000 kg × 10 m/s² = 40000 N
- P = 40000 N ÷ 0.4 m² = **1 × 10⁵ Pa**

**Ladki (dono heels par poora weight maan kar):**
- F = 40 kg × 10 m/s² = 400 N
- A = 2 cm² = 2 × 10⁻⁴ m²
- P = 400 N ÷ (2 × 10⁻⁴ m²) = **2 × 10⁶ Pa**

**Tulna:** (2 × 10⁶) ÷ (1 × 10⁵) = **20**. Ladki ki heels ka pressure haathi ke pairon se **20 guna** zyada hai, jabki haathi ka weight ladki se 100 guna zyada hai (40000 ÷ 400 = 100)!

**Jaanch:** 10⁵ Pa × 0.4 m² = 40000 N ✅ aur 2 × 10⁶ Pa × 2 × 10⁻⁴ m² = 400 N ✅

### Example 9: cm² ko m² mein badalna (aur kPa)

Ek 30 N ka thrust 15 cm² par lag raha hai. Pressure Pa aur kPa mein nikalo.

- A = 15 cm² = 15 × 10⁻⁴ m² = **0.0015 m²**
- P = 30 N ÷ 0.0015 m² = **20000 Pa**
- 20000 Pa ÷ 1000 = **20 kPa**

**Jaanch:** 20000 Pa × 0.0015 m² = 30 N ✅

**Ulta bhi dekho:** 2.5 kPa = 2.5 × 1000 Pa = **2500 Pa**, aur 36000 Pa = 36000 ÷ 1000 = **36 kPa**. Aur 1 bar = 10⁵ Pa = **100 kPa**.

### Example 10: School bag ke straps

Ek bag ka mass 6 kg hai. Patle straps ka kul contact area 4 cm² hai, aur chaude straps ka 40 cm². Dono mein kandhon par pressure nikalo (g = 10 m/s²).

- F = 6 kg × 10 m/s² = **60 N**

**Patle straps:**
- A = 4 cm² = 4 × 10⁻⁴ m²
- P = 60 N ÷ (4 × 10⁻⁴ m²) = **1.5 × 10⁵ Pa** (= 150 kPa)

**Chaude straps:**
- A = 40 cm² = 40 × 10⁻⁴ m² = 4 × 10⁻³ m²
- P = 60 N ÷ (4 × 10⁻³ m²) = **1.5 × 10⁴ Pa** (= 15 kPa)

**Jaanch:** 1.5 × 10⁵ × 4 × 10⁻⁴ = 60 N ✅ aur 1.5 × 10⁴ × 4 × 10⁻³ = 60 N ✅

Area 10 guna to pressure **10 guna kam**. Bag ka weight (60 N) dono mein wahi hai.

## Part D: Aage ki jhalak: fluid bhi pressure lagate hain

Ab tak humne solid ka pressure dekha. Par **fluids** (liquid aur gas) bhi pressure lagate hain, aur wo apne **bartan ki deewaron** par lagate hain. Gubbare mein hawa uski deewar ko bahar ki taraf dabati hai, aur paani ki tanki ki deewar par paani dabav daalta hai. Fluids ka pressure kaise kaam karta hai, ye hum aage ke topics mein dekhenge. Abhi bas ye yaad rakho ki **pressure sirf solid ki cheez nahi hai**.

---

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Force aur pressure ko ek hi samajhna.**
Force (thrust) ka unit N hai, pressure ka unit Pa (N/m²) hai. Force kul dabav batata hai, pressure batata hai ki **har m² par** kitna dabav hai. Ek hi force alag-alag areas par alag pressure banata hai.

**Galti 2: Area cm² mein rakhkar answer Pa mein likhna.**
Misaal: 30 N ÷ 15 cm² = 2 **sahi nahi hai** (ye "2 N/cm²" hoga, Pa nahi). Sahi: 15 cm² = 0.0015 m², P = 30 N ÷ 0.0015 m² = **20000 Pa**.

**Galti 3: cm² ko m² mein badalte waqt 100 se bhaag dena.**
1 cm = 10⁻² m, to 1 cm² = 10⁻⁴ m² (10,000 se bhaag). Agar 100 se bhaage: 200 cm² ko 2 m² likh doge, jabki sahi 0.02 m² hai, aur pressure 100 guna galat aayega.

**Galti 4: Sochna ki pressure sirf force par depend karta hai.**
Pressure = F ÷ A. Haathi ka force bahut zyada hai par uske pair chaude hain; ladki ki heel ka force kam hai par area bahut chhota hai, isliye uska pressure zyada nikla (Example 8).

**Galti 5: Sochna ki chaude object ka force kam hota hai.**
Tractor ke chaude tyre ya camel ke chaude pair se force (weight) kam nahi hota. Force **wahi** rehta hai; bas wo badi jagah par fail jaata hai, isliye **pressure kam** hota hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Fluid** = liquid aur gas (jo beh sakte hain).
- **Thrust** = sateh par seedha (perpendicular) lagne wala force. Unit: **N**.
- Horizontal sateh par rakhe solid ka thrust uska **weight** hota hai: F = m × g.
- **Pressure = thrust ÷ area**: **P = F ÷ A**.
- SI unit: **pascal (Pa)**, 1 Pa = 1 N/m². 1 kPa = 1000 Pa, 1 bar = 10⁵ Pa.
- Area ko hamesha **m²** mein badlo: 1 cm² = 10⁻⁴ m², 1 mm² = 10⁻⁶ m².
- Area **chhota** to pressure **bada** (chaaku, keel, kulhaadi); area **bada** to pressure **chhota** (bag ke straps, tractor tyre, camel ke pair, snowshoes).
- Force wahi rehta hai, sirf area badalne se pressure badal jaata hai.
- Fluids bhi apne bartan ki deewaron par pressure lagate hain (aage dekhenge).

## Practice Questions

1. What is the difference between thrust and pressure? State the SI unit of each.
2. A force of 250 N acts at right angles on an area of 0.5 m². Find the pressure.
3. A pressure of 8000 Pa acts on a surface of area 0.03 m². Find the thrust.
4. A force of 900 N produces a pressure of 18000 Pa. Find the area on which it acts.
5. A boy of mass 50 kg stands on both feet, with total contact area 0.05 m². Find the pressure he exerts on the floor (g = 10 m/s²). What happens to the pressure if he stands on one foot (area 0.025 m²)?
6. A wooden block of mass 5 kg has dimensions 50 cm × 20 cm × 10 cm. Find the greatest and the least pressure it can exert on a table (g = 10 m/s²).
7. A force of 12 N acts on an area of 6 cm². Find the pressure in pascal and in kPa.
8. A sharp needle tip has area 0.2 mm². Find the pressure when it is pressed with a force of 4 N.
9. Why do we use wide straps on a heavy school bag, and why is a nail pointed at one end?
10. State true or false, with a reason: (a) A camel's wide feet reduce its weight. (b) 1 kPa = 1000 Pa. (c) If area is doubled and force stays the same, pressure becomes half. (d) 1 cm² = 10⁻² m².

### Answers

1. **Thrust** sateh par seedha lagne wala force hai (SI unit **N**). **Pressure** har unit area par lagne wala thrust hai, P = F ÷ A (SI unit **Pa = N/m²**).

2. P = 250 N ÷ 0.5 m² = **500 Pa**.
   Jaanch: 500 Pa × 0.5 m² = 250 N ✅

3. F = P × A = 8000 Pa × 0.03 m² = **240 N**.
   Jaanch: 240 N ÷ 0.03 m² = 8000 Pa ✅

4. A = F ÷ P = 900 N ÷ 18000 Pa = **0.05 m²**.
   Jaanch: 900 N ÷ 0.05 m² = 18000 Pa ✅

5. F = 50 kg × 10 m/s² = 500 N. Dono pair: P = 500 N ÷ 0.05 m² = **10000 Pa** (10 kPa). Ek pair: P = 500 N ÷ 0.025 m² = **20000 Pa**, yani pressure **doguna** ho jaata hai kyunki area aadha ho gaya.
   Jaanch: 10000 × 0.05 = 500 N ✅, 20000 × 0.025 = 500 N ✅

6. F = 5 kg × 10 m/s² = 50 N.
   Sabse bada face: 50 cm × 20 cm = 1000 cm² = 0.1 m² (pressure sabse kam): P = 50 N ÷ 0.1 m² = **500 Pa** (least).
   Sabse chhota face: 20 cm × 10 cm = 200 cm² = 0.02 m² (pressure sabse zyada): P = 50 N ÷ 0.02 m² = **2500 Pa** (greatest).
   Jaanch: 500 × 0.1 = 50 N ✅, 2500 × 0.02 = 50 N ✅

7. A = 6 cm² = 6 × 10⁻⁴ m². P = 12 N ÷ (6 × 10⁻⁴ m²) = **20000 Pa** = **20 kPa**.
   Jaanch: 20000 × 6 × 10⁻⁴ = 12 N ✅

8. A = 0.2 mm² = 0.2 × 10⁻⁶ m² = 2 × 10⁻⁷ m². P = 4 N ÷ (2 × 10⁻⁷ m²) = **2 × 10⁷ Pa**.
   Jaanch: 2 × 10⁷ × 2 × 10⁻⁷ = 4 N ✅

9. Chaude straps se bag ka weight badi jagah par fail jaata hai, area bada hone se pressure kam hota hai aur kandha kam dukhta hai. Keel ki nok ka area bahut chhota hota hai, isliye wahi force bahut zyada pressure banata hai aur nok lakdi mein ghus jaati hai.

10. (a) **False** ❌. Weight wahi rehta hai; chaude pair se sirf pressure kam hota hai.
    (b) **True** ✅. kilo ka matlab 1000.
    (c) **True** ✅. P = F ÷ A, A doguna to P aadha.
    (d) **False** ❌. 1 cm² = 10⁻⁴ m² (kyunki length ko 10⁻² se multiply karke square karte hain).
