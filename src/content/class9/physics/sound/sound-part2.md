# Topic 2: Sound Waves: Longitudinal aur Transverse

Topic 1 mein humne dekha ki sound medium (jaise hawa) ke bina nahi chalti, aur ye bhi dekha ki hawa ke particles vibration ko **layer by layer aage pass** karte hain, khud source se kaan tak nahi aate. Topic 1 ka aakhri sawal tha: **sound kaise phailti hai?** Jawab hai: **tarang (wave)** ki tarah. Is topic mein hum dekhenge ki wave kya hoti hai, uske do type kaun se hain, aur sound in mein se kaunsi hai. Aage ke saare topics isi par tikte hain.

## Part A: Wave kya hoti hai?

Pichhle topic ka drum yaad karo. Drum par haath maarne se uski jhilli hilti hai, uske paas ki hawa hilti hai, phir us se aage ki hawa hilti hai, aur is tarah hilna door tak chala jaata hai. Isi chalte hue hilne ko **wave** kehte hain.

> **Wave** = ek disturbance jo medium mein ek jagah se doosri jagah **aage badhta hai** aur apne saath **energy** le jaata hai, par **matter (medium ke particles) ko saath nahi le jaata**.

Medium ke particles bas apni **mean position** ke aas-paas hilte hain (oscillate karte hain). Aage-peeche ya upar-neeche hilna kaisa hota hai, ye tum [motion.md](../motion/motion.md) mein dekh chuke ho. Yahan bas itna samajhna hai ki particle apni jagah ke paas hi hilta rehta hai, aur energy aage nikal jaati hai.

Do aam example:

- **Pond ki ripple.** Paani mein kankad phenko. Gol ripples bahar ki taraf phailte hain. Par agar paani par ek patta tair raha hai, to wo ripple ke saath bahar nahi bahta. Wo bas **upar-neeche hilta rehta hai** aur lagbhag usi jagah rehta hai. Ripple (energy) door chali gayi, paani aur patta nahi gaye.
- **Stadium ki "Mexican wave".** Cricket match mein log baari-baari se khade hote hain aur baith jaate hain. Poore stadium mein ek "lehar" ghoomti dikhti hai. Par koi bhi insaan apni seat se doosri seat par nahi gaya. Har insaan bas apni jagah khada hua aur baith gaya. Chalti hui cheez **disturbance** hai, log nahi.

> **Yaad rakho:** wave **energy** transfer karti hai, **matter** nahi.

## Part B: Transverse wave: "side mein hilna"

Ab Ms. Dalal ka doosra tareeka dekho: sira side mein jhatkana.

Rope ka ek sira haath mein pakdo aur doosra sira kisi khambe se baandho. Haath ko ek baar **upar-neeche** hilao. Rope par ek ubhaar (hump) banta hai jo khambe ki taraf **aage** chalta hai. Ab rope ke ek chhote hisse (ek particle) par nazar rakho. Wo bas **upar jaata hai aur neeche aata hai**. Wave aage ja rahi hai, par particle wave ki disha ke **right angle (perpendicular)** par hil raha hai.

> **Transverse wave:** jisme medium ke particles wave ke chalne ki disha ke **perpendicular (90°)** vibrate karte hain.

```
 Wave ki disha  ────────────────►

            crest                   crest
           .─────.                 .─────.
 ────────'         '─────.     .──'         '───────
                          '───'
                         trough

 Rope ke ek particle ki harkat:   ↕  (sirf upar-neeche)
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Transverse wave: wave aage (daayein) ja rahi hai, par rope ka har particle sirf upar-neeche hilta hai. Ooncha hissa crest hai, neecha hissa trough.

Transverse wave mein do khaas hisse dikhte hain:
- **Crest:** sabse ooncha hissa (pahaad ki choti jaisa).
- **Trough:** sabse neecha hissa (ghaati jaisa).

Transverse waves ke examples:
- Rope ya string par chalti wave (jaise guitar ki tar).
- Paani ki surface par ripples. Dhyan: paani ki surface ki waves thodi complicated hoti hain (particle asal mein gol-gol ghoomte hain), isliye hum unhe **lagbhag transverse** kehte hain.
- **Light waves** bhi transverse hoti hain (isse aage ki class mein padhoge).

## Part C: Longitudinal wave: "aage-peeche hilna"

Ab Ms. Dalal ka pehla tareeka: spring ko uski lambai ki disha mein dhakelna aur kheenchna.

Sira aage dhakelne par samne ke coils paas-paas aa jaate hain: ek bheed wala hissa banta hai, jise **compression** kehte hain. Phir sira peeche kheenchne par wahan ke coils door-door ho jaate hain: ek khula hua hissa banta hai, jise **rarefaction** kehte hain. Baar-baar dhakelo aur kheencho, to compression aur rarefaction **ek ke baad ek** spring par chalte hue jaate hain.

Ab spring ke ek coil par nazar rakho. Wo bas **aage-peeche** hilta hai, aur wo bhi wahi disha jis disha mein wave chal rahi hai. Coil apni jagah se door nahi jaata.

> **Longitudinal wave:** jisme medium ke particles wave ke chalne ki disha ke **saath saath (parallel)** vibrate karte hain.

```
 Haath ──►   Wave ki disha  ──────────────────────────►

  C         R         C         R         C
  ooooo     o     o     ooooo     o     o     ooooo
  ooooo     o     o     ooooo     o     o     ooooo
```

> **Figure 2.2** – Slinky ya spring par longitudinal wave: C jagah par coils bheed mein hain (compression), R jagah par door-door hain (rarefaction). Har coil sirf aage-peeche hilta hai.

Dono ke beech ke terms yaad rakhne ka tareeka:

| | Particles kaise hain? | Pressure aur density |
|---|---|---|
| **Compression** | Bheed mein, paas-paas | **Zyada** |
| **Rarefaction** | Phaile hue, door-door | **Kam** |

Jaise mele ki bheed mein jagah kam hoti hai aur dabaav (pressure) zyada, aur khuli jagah mein pressure kam. "Compression = compress hona = dab jana = high pressure."

## Part D: Sound hawa mein longitudinal wave hai

Ab Ishvi ke sawal ki taraf badhte hain. Kisi vibrating cheez ko lo: tuning fork ki prong ya loudspeaker ka cone.

1. Jab prong (ya cone) **aage** badhta hai, wo apne saamne ki hawa ko dhakelta hai. Wahan ke particles paas-paas aa jaate hain: **compression** banta hai.
2. Jab prong **peeche** hatta hai, saamne ki hawa mein jagah khali ho jaati hai. Particles phail kar door-door ho jaate hain: **rarefaction** banta hai.
3. Ye aage-peeche hona baar-baar hota hai, isliye hawa mein **compression, rarefaction, compression, rarefaction...** ek ke baad ek aage badhte jaate hain.

Hawa ka har particle bas apni jagah ke paas **aage-peeche** hilta hai, wahi disha jis mein sound chal rahi hai. Isliye sound hawa mein **longitudinal wave** hai.

```
 Vibrating
 source
 ──────►
 (prong ya cone)

              C       R       C       R       C  
            ooooo   o   o   ooooo   o   o   ooooo
            ooooo   o   o   ooooo   o   o   ooooo
            ooooo   o   o   ooooo   o   o   ooooo

 Sound ki disha  ──────────────────────────────────►

 Pressure (graph)
 Zyada       ***             ***             ***
           **   **         **   **         **   *
 Normal   *       *       *       *       *
                   **   **         **   **
 Kam                 ***             ***
          (C par peak, R par gaddha)
```

> **Figure 2.3** – Upar: hawa ke particles (dots). C par dots paas-paas hain (compression), R par door-door (rarefaction). Neeche: pressure ka graph. Compression par pressure normal se zyada (graph ki choti), rarefaction par normal se kam (graph ka gaddha).

Graph ko seedhe padho: jahan dots ki bheed hai wahan graph upar jaata hai (zyada pressure), jahan dots door-door hain wahan graph neeche jaata hai (kam pressure). Bich ki seedhi line normal (sahi hawa wala) pressure hai.

**Sound ko medium kyun chahiye?** Longitudinal wave banane ke liye particles **compress** hone chahiye aur phir phail jaane chahiye. Vacuum mein particles hi nahi hain, to kuch compress hone ko hai hi nahi. Isliye vacuum mein sound nahi chalti (Topic 1 ka wahi naata).

**Solids, liquids aur gases mein sound:**
- **Liquids aur gases** mein sound sirf longitudinal wave hoti hai.
- **Solids** mein sound longitudinal bhi ho sakti hai aur transverse bhi (ye aage ki class mein dekhoge).

Ek line mein: sound ek **mechanical wave** hai, yaani use chalne ke liye medium chahiye. Light mechanical nahi hai, usse medium ki zaroorat nahi, isliye wo vacuum mein bhi chalti hai.

## Part E: Transverse aur longitudinal ek nazar mein

| Baat | Transverse wave | Longitudinal wave |
|---|---|---|
| Particles kaise hilte hain? | Wave ki disha ke **perpendicular** | Wave ki disha ke **parallel** |
| Khaas hisse | **Crest** (ooncha) aur **trough** (neecha) | **Compression** (bheed) aur **rarefaction** (phailav) |
| Medium | Rope, string ya paani jaise medium mein chalti hai (light ko medium nahi chahiye) | Hamesha medium chahiye (solid, liquid ya gas) |
| Examples | Rope par wave, guitar ki tar, paani ke ripples (lagbhag), light | Slinky ka push-pull, hawa mein sound, explosion ki pressure wave |

## Part G: Solved examples

**Example 1 (figure mein gino):** Figure 2.3 mein upar ke particles dekho. Kitne compressions aur kitne rarefactions hain? Pehle compression se aakhri compression tak kitne poore compression-rarefaction chakkar hain?

- Compressions (C): **3**
- Rarefactions (R): **2**
- Pehla C, phir R, phir C, phir R, phir C. Har "C se agle C tak" ek poora chakkar hai. 3 C ke beech 2 gap hain, to **2 poore chakkar**.

**Jaanch:** Sequence C R C R C mein C aur R baari-baari se aate hain, aur 3 C ke beech mein 2 R hain ✅. 2 C-C gap = 2 chakkar ✅

**Example 2 (loudspeaker cone ki harkat):** Ek loudspeaker ka cone ye harkat karta hai: aage, peeche, aage, peeche, aage. Saamne ki hawa mein kya banta hai?

- Cone **aage** gaya: compression (C)
- Cone **peeche** aaya: rarefaction (R)
- Cone aage, peeche, aage, peeche, aage = **C, R, C, R, C**

Jaanch: aage ki 3 harkatein = 3 compressions, peeche ki 2 harkatein = 2 rarefactions, aur total 3 + 2 = 5 harkatein ✅

**Example 3 (ek chakkar mein kitna time):** Cone ek second mein 50 baar aage-peeche hota hai (50 poore vibrations). Ek poore chakkar (ek compression aur ek rarefaction) mein kitna time lagta hai?

- 50 chakkar = 1 s
- 1 chakkar = 1 s ÷ 50 = **0.02 s**

**Jaanch:** 50 × 0.02 s = 1 s ✅

(Ek chakkar ke time ko **time period** kehte hain, aur ek second ke chakkar ko **frequency**. Dono ko Topic 3 mein theek se seekhenge.)

**Example 4 (energy kitni door gayi, particle kitna):** Hawa mein sound 340 m/s ki speed se chalti hai (isse Topic 4 mein seekhenge). 2 s mein sound ki energy kitni door pahunchegi? Aur hawa ka ek particle?

- Energy ka safar: distance = speed × time = 340 m/s × 2 s = **680 m**
- Hawa ka ek particle: wo bas apni jagah ke paas aage-peeche hilta hai, aur wo bhi **bahut kam** (kuch micrometres jaisa, yaani meter ke chhote se hisse jitna). Wo 680 m door nahi jaata.

**Jaanch:** 340 × 2 = 680 m ✅. Yahi Topic 1 ki baat hai: energy door gayi, particle nahi. Isliye hum bhi tabhi sunte hain jab sound ki wave ki energy kaan tak aati hai, hawa ke particles nahi.

**Example 5 (10 waves ko type mein baantna):** Har wave ko transverse ya longitudinal likho.

| No. | Wave | Type | Kyun? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hawa mein sound | **Longitudinal** | Hawa ke particles sound ki disha mein aage-peeche hilte hain |
| 2 | Rope par wave | **Transverse** | Rope ke particles upar-neeche hilte hain, wave aage jaati hai |
| 3 | Slinky ka push-pull | **Longitudinal** | Coils lambai ki disha mein hilte hain |
| 4 | Light | **Transverse** | Light ki waves transverse hoti hain (aage ki class mein) |
| 5 | Paani ka ripple | **Lagbhag transverse** | Surface upar-neeche hoti hai, par poori tasveer thodi complicated hai |
| 6 | Guitar ki tar ki wave | **Transverse** | Tar ke particles tar ke perpendicular hilte hain |
| 7 | Tuning fork se hawa mein wave | **Longitudinal** | Ye hawa mein sound hai (compression-rarefaction) |
| 8 | Haath se hilayi hui rope ki wave | **Transverse** | Haath upar-neeche, wave aage |
| 9 | Stadium ki Mexican wave | **Transverse** | Log upar-neeche (khade-baithe), wave side mein chalti hai |
| 10 | Explosion ki pressure wave | **Longitudinal** | Hawa mein pressure ke bheed aur phailav ki wave |

**Jaanch:** Transverse: 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9 = 6. Longitudinal: 1, 3, 7, 10 = 4. 6 + 4 = 10 ✅. Sound se jude (1, 7, 10) sab longitudinal hain ✅

**Example 6 (tuning fork ki prong):** Ek tuning fork ki prong daayein se baayein aur baayein se daayein hilti hai. Jab prong daayein ki taraf jaati hai, to uske daayein ki hawa mein kya banta hai? Jab baayein aati hai?

- Daayein jaane par: prong daayein ki hawa ko dhakelti hai, wahan **compression** banta hai.
- Baayein aane par: daayein ki hawa mein jagah khali hoti hai, wahan **rarefaction** banta hai.

**Jaanch:** "aage dhakela = compression, peeche hata = rarefaction" Example 2 ke jaisa hi niyam hai ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Hawa ke particles source se kaan tak safar karte hain"**

Nahi. Particles apni jagah ke paas hi aage-peeche hilte hain. Sirf disturbance (energy) chalta hai. Example 4: energy 680 m gayi, par particle bahut kam hila.

**Galti 2: "Sound transverse wave hai"**

Nahi. Hawa mein sound **longitudinal** hai: particles wave ki disha mein hilte hain, aur compression-rarefaction bante hain. Crest-trough waali shape transverse waves ki hai (light, rope).

**Galti 3: Compression aur rarefaction ulta karna**

Compression = bheed = **high** pressure aur density. Rarefaction = khula, phailav = **low** pressure aur density. Yaad rakho: compression = compress = dab gaya = zyada pressure.

**Galti 4: "Wave matter ko saath le jaati hai"**

Nahi. Wave sirf energy le jaati hai. Patta pond ki ripple ke saath bahar nahi jaata, sirf upar-neeche hilta hai. Stadium wave mein koi insaan apni seat nahi chhodta.

**Galti 5: "Wave ko chalne ke liye medium ko saath chalna padta hai"**

Nahi. Medium apni jagah rehta hai aur sirf hilta hai. Wave medium ke andar se guzarti hai, medium wave ke saath nahi chalta.

## Quick Recap

- **Wave** = disturbance jo medium mein aage badhta hai aur **energy** le jaata hai, par **matter nahi**. Particles sirf apni mean position ke paas oscillate karte hain.
- Examples: pond ki ripple (patta sirf upar-neeche), stadium ki Mexican wave.
- **Transverse wave:** particles wave ki disha ke **perpendicular** hilte hain. Isme **crest** aur **trough** hote hain. Examples: rope ki wave, paani ke ripples (lagbhag), light.
- **Longitudinal wave:** particles wave ki disha ke **parallel** hilte hain. Isme **compression** (bheed: high pressure, density) aur **rarefaction** (phailav: low pressure, density) hote hain. Example: slinky push-pull.
- **Hawa mein sound longitudinal** hai: source (prong ya cone) aage dhakelta hai (compression), peeche hatta hai (rarefaction), baar-baar.
- Sound ko medium isliye chahiye ki longitudinal wave mein particles ko compress hona hota hai; vacuum mein particles nahi hain.
- Liquids aur gases mein sound sirf longitudinal. Solids mein dono (aage ki class mein).
- Sound mechanical wave hai (medium chahiye); light nahi.
- Kahani: Ishvi ka slinky push-pull wali wave longitudinal thi, aur sound usi jaisi hai.

### Answers

1. Wave ek disturbance hai jo medium mein aage badhta hai. Wo **sirf energy** le jaati hai, **matter (particles) nahi**. Particles apni jagah ke paas hi hilte hain.

2. Patta ripple ke saath bahar nahi jaata, wo sirf **upar-neeche** hilta rehta hai. Isse pata chalta hai ki wave energy aage le jaati hai, par paani ya patta (matter) nahi.

3. Transverse: particles wave ki disha ke perpendicular vibrate karte hain (example: rope par wave). Longitudinal: particles wave ki disha ke parallel vibrate karte hain (example: hawa mein sound).

4. (a) → (ii), (b) → (iv), (c) → (iii), (d) → (i).
   Jaanch: compression = bheed = high pressure ✅, rarefaction = phailav = low pressure ✅, crest = peak ✅, trough = neecha point ✅

5. Cone ki harkat: aage, peeche, aage, peeche, aage, peeche. Aage = compression, peeche = rarefaction. Sequence: **C, R, C, R, C, R**. Total **3 compressions aur 3 rarefactions**.
   Jaanch: 6 harkatein = 3 + 3 ✅

6. 20 vibrations = 1 s, to 1 vibration = 1 s ÷ 20 = **0.05 s**.
   Jaanch: 20 × 0.05 s = 1 s ✅

7. Distance = speed × time = 340 m/s × 3 s = **1020 m**. Air ka particle itni door nahi jaata. Wo bas apni jagah ke paas bahut kam (micrometre jaisa) aage-peeche hilta hai. Energy chalti hai, particle nahi.
   Jaanch: 340 × 3 = 1020 m ✅

8. Vacuum mein koi particle nahi hota. Sound longitudinal wave hai jisme particles ko compress (compression) aur phir phailna (rarefaction) padta hai. Particles hi nahi hain, to compression-rarefaction ban hi nahi sakte, isliye sound nahi chalti.

9. (a) **Longitudinal**, (b) **Transverse**, (c) **Longitudinal**, (d) **Transverse**, (e) **Longitudinal**.
   Jaanch: sound se jude (a, c ka push-pull, e) sab longitudinal hain, rope aur light transverse hain ✅

10. (a) **False** ❌. Longitudinal mein particles wave ki disha ke **parallel** hilte hain; perpendicular transverse mein hota hai.
    (b) **True** ✅. Compression mein particles bheed mein hain, pressure zyada.
    (c) **False** ❌. Particles apni jagah ke paas hilte hain, sirf energy kaan tak aati hai.
    (d) **True** ✅. Sound ko chalne ke liye medium chahiye.

11. Sequence C R C R C R C. 4 compressions ke beech 3 gap hain, to **3 complete cycles**.
    Jaanch: 4 C aur 3 R baari-baari se aate hain ✅, aur 4 − 1 = 3 ✅

12. Nahi, ye wave sound jaisi nahi hai. Side mein jhatke se coils wave ki disha ke perpendicular hilte hain, yaani wo **transverse** wave hai, jisme crest aur trough bante hain. Sound longitudinal hai, jisme compression aur rarefaction bante hain, jaise slinky ke push-pull mein.
