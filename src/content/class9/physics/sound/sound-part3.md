# Topic 3: Wave ke Parameters: Frequency, Wavelength, Amplitude aur v = f λ

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki hawa mein sound ek wave ki tarah chalti hai: kahin hawa ke kan paas-paas aa jaate hain (compression), kahin door-door ho jaate hain (rarefaction), aur ye pattern aage badhta jaata hai. Wave energy ko aage le jaati hai, hawa ke kan khud aage nahi bhaagte. Ab sawal ye hai ki is wave ko **numbers mein kaise bataya jaye**? Do sounds ko alag-alag kaise pehchana jaye? Is topic mein hum wave ke teen number seekhenge, aur ek chhota sa formula **v = f λ** khud banayenge.

**Is poore topic mein: sound ki speed hawa mein v = 340 m/s maanenge.** (Alag-alag medium mein speed kyun alag hoti hai, wo agle topic mein.)

## Music room ki do taarein

School ke music room mein guitar teacher Mr. Alvares ek guitar le kar baithe the. Paas mein Kyra khadi thi, jo guitar seekhne aayi thi.

Mr. Alvares ne guitar ki sabse patli taar ko ungli se chhedha. Ek **patli, unchi aawaaz** nikli: "teeeen". Phir unhone sabse moti taar chhedi. Ab ek **moti, neechi aawaaz** nikli: "dhoooon".

Kyra ne kaha, "Sir, dono taarein hilti hain, par aawaaz alag kyun hai?"

Mr. Alvares muskuraye, "Ab dekho." Unhone moti taar ko pehle halke se chhedha, phir zor se kheencha. "Zor se chhedne par aawaaz **zyada tez (loud)** ho gayi, par aawaaz ki unchai-neechai wahi rahi. To ek taar ko chhedne par bhi do cheezein badal sakti hain: kitni loud hai, aur kitni unchi hai."

Kyra ne poochha, "To ye sab hota kaise hai?"

Mr. Alvares bole, "Taar ki vibration se hawa mein ek wave banti hai. **Teen number batate hain ki wo wave kaisi hai.** Pehle wo teen number samjho, phir main tumhe wapas in taaron par le aaunga." Is kahani ko hum topic ke beech mein solve karenge.

## Naye shabd

| Shabd | Matlab (simple bhasha mein) | Example |
|---|---|---|

## Part A: Wave ke teen number

Hawa mein sound ki wave ko ek graph ki tarah dekhna aasaan hai. Ek jagah ka kan beech wali (mean) position ke aas-paas aage-peeche hilta hai. Hum is hilne (displacement) ko, ya us jagah ke pressure ke badlaav ko, ek simple wave jaise graph mein dikha sakte hain.

Pehle **distance ke saath** graph dekho: ek hi pal ki "tasveer", jisme alag-alag jagah par kya hai.

```
 y (displacement ya pressure ka badlaav)
 +A ┤      * *                      * *
    │    *     *                  *     *
  0 ┼──*─────────*──────────────*─────────*──→ x (distance, m)
    │              *          *
 -A ┤                * *   * *
    │
    │       ├─────────── λ ──────────┤
    │   (ek wave ki lambai: peak se agle peak tak)
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Distance ke saath wave ka graph. Pressure ke graph mein ooncha point (peak) = compression aur neecha point (trough) = rarefaction. Do lagataar peaks ke beech ki doori = wavelength λ. Beech wali rekha (0) se peak tak ki unchai = amplitude A.

Ab **samay ke saath** graph dekho: ek hi jagah ka kan, samay ke saath kaise hil raha hai.

```
 y (displacement)
 +A ┤      * *                      * *
    │    *     *                  *     *
  0 ┼──*─────────*──────────────*─────────*──→ t (samay, s)
    │              *          *
 -A ┤                * *   * *
    │
    │       ├─────────── T ──────────┤
    │   (ek poori vibration mein lagne wala samay)
```

> **Figure 3.2** – Samay ke saath graph. Aakar wahi hai, par ab do lagataar peaks ke beech ka **samay** time period T hai.

Dono graph dikhne mein ek jaise hain, par ek mein x-axis par **doori** hai aur doosre mein **samay**. Isiliye pehle mein "ek wave ki lambai" λ aur doosre mein "ek vibration ka samay" T milta hai.

### 1. Amplitude (A): wave kitni "badi" hai

> **Amplitude (A)** = kan ka apni mean (beech wali) position se maximum displacement. Sound ke liye ye pressure ke maximum badlaav ke roop mein bhi bataya jaata hai.

- Displacement ke liye unit **metre (m)** hai, pressure ke badlaav ke liye **pascal (Pa)**.
- Amplitude jitna bada, sound utni **loud** (zyada tez). Amplitude chhota, sound dheemi.
- Sound mein ye displacement bahut chhota hota hai: aam baat-cheet mein kan sirf mm ke bhi bahut chhote hisse jitna hilte hain.

Mr. Alvares ne moti taar ko zor se chheda to taar ka amplitude zyada hua, hawa mein pressure ke badlaav bhi zyada hue, aur sound loud sunayi di. (Loudness ki poori kahani Topic 5 mein.)

### 2. Wavelength (λ): ek wave kitni lambi hai

> **Wavelength (λ, "lambda")** = do lagataar compressions ke beech ki doori. Ye do lagataar rarefactions ke beech ki doori ke barabar bhi hai. (Transverse wave mein do lagataar crests ya troughs ke beech ki doori.)

- SI unit: **metre (m)**. Chhoti wavelength ko cm ya mm mein bhi likhte hain.

```
 Hawa ke kan (har ":" ek kan hai)

  : : :     :     :     : : :     :     :     : : :
    C          R          C          R          C
    ◄───────── λ ─────────►

 (C = compression: kan paas-paas, R = rarefaction: kan door-door)
```

> **Figure 3.3** – Hawa mein do lagataar compressions ke beech ki doori wavelength λ hai.

### 3. Frequency (f) aur time period (T): kitni jaldi-jaldi vibrations

Motion wale chapter mein hum time period aur frequency dekh chuke hain. Wahi baat yahan:

> **Frequency (f)** = ek jagah par 1 second mein hone wali poori vibrations (oscillations) ki sankhya. SI unit: **hertz (Hz)**, aur 1 Hz = 1 vibration per second = 1 s⁻¹.
> **Time period (T)** = ek poori vibration mein lagne wala samay. Unit: **second (s)**.

Dono ek doosre ke ulte hain:

> **f = 1 ÷ T**   aur   **T = 1 ÷ f**

Bade frequency ke liye prefixes: **1 kHz = 1000 Hz = 10³ Hz**, **1 MHz = 10⁶ Hz**. Chhote samay ke liye: **1 ms = 10⁻³ s**.

Agar kisi jagah ka kan 1 second mein 440 baar poori vibration karta hai, to f = 440 Hz aur T = 1 ÷ 440 s.

## Part B: Wave ki speed, aur v = f λ khud banao

Wave ki speed ka matlab hai: wave ka koi bhi point (jaise ek compression) **1 second mein kitni doori tay karta hai**. Unit m/s.

Ab ek chhoti si soch. Ek source (jaise taar) hawa mein compressions bhej raha hai. **Ek poori vibration mein, yaani ek time period T mein, ek poori wave banti hai.** Is dauraan pehla compression aage badh jaata hai, aur agla compression usi jagah ban jaata hai jahan se pehle wala chala tha. Pehla compression ab agle compression se **ek wavelength λ** aage hai.

Yaani:
- **Samay T mein wave ne doori λ tay ki.**

Speed = doori ÷ samay, isliye:

> **v = λ ÷ T**   (unit: m ÷ s = m/s)

Ab T ki jagah 1 ÷ f likho (kyunki T = 1 ÷ f):

> **v = λ ÷ (1 ÷ f) = f × λ**

Ye hamara formula hai:

> **v = f × λ**
> Wave ki speed (m/s) = frequency (Hz) × wavelength (m)

**Units ka check:** Hz = 1 ÷ s, to Hz × m = (1 ÷ s) × m = m ÷ s = **m/s** ✅

Is formula se teen rishte nikalte hain:

| Kya chahiye? | Formula | Units |
|---|---|---|
| Speed v | v = f × λ | Hz × m = m/s |
| Wavelength λ | λ = v ÷ f | (m/s) ÷ Hz = m |
| Frequency f | f = v ÷ λ | (m/s) ÷ m = Hz |

### Speed medium par depend karti hai, frequency par nahi

Ek zaroori baat: **kisi diye gaye medium (aur temperature) mein sound ki speed fixed hoti hai.** Hawa mein ye 340 m/s hai, chahe sound dheemi ho ya loud, unchi ho ya neechi. Wave ki speed **frequency par depend nahi karti**, aur **loud sound tez nahi chalti** (loudness amplitude se judi hai, speed se nahi).

Agar v fixed hai aur f badhe, to v = f × λ barabar rehne ke liye **λ ko ghatna padega**:

> **λ = v ÷ f**   (v fixed hone par f badhne se λ chhoti hoti hai)

Yaani unchi frequency ki sound ki wavelength chhoti hoti hai, aur neechi frequency ki sound ki wavelength lambi.

### Table 1: Hawa mein alag-alag frequency (v = 340 m/s)

| Sound | f (Hz) | λ = v ÷ f |
|---|---|---|
| Neechi awaaz | 100 | 340 ÷ 100 = **3.4 m** |
| Beech ka sur (middle note) | 440 | 340 ÷ 440 ≈ **0.77 m** |
| Unchi awaaz | 4000 | 340 ÷ 4000 = 0.085 m = **8.5 cm** |

```
 Hawa mein wavelength (har # ≈ 0.1 m)

 100 Hz   : ################################## 3.4 m
 440 Hz   : ######## 0.77 m
 4000 Hz  : # 0.085 m
```

> **Figure 3.4** – Hawa mein frequency badhne par wavelength tezi se ghatti hai (v = 340 m/s fixed).

### Table 2: Same frequency, alag medium

Ek 440 Hz ka tuning fork (Topic 1 wali U-shape ki dhaatu ki chhadi, jo ek fixed frequency par hilti hai) ek baar hawa mein aur ek baar paani mein bajaya gaya. Hawa mein v = 340 m/s aur paani mein v = 1500 m/s maano. (Ye alag speeds kyun hoti hain, wo Topic 4 mein.)

| Medium | v (m/s) | f (Hz) | λ = v ÷ f |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hawa | 340 | 440 | 340 ÷ 440 ≈ **0.77 m** |
| Paani | 1500 | 440 | 1500 ÷ 440 ≈ **3.41 m** |

Dekho: **frequency nahi badli, wavelength badal gayi.** Aisa kyun?

Frequency to **source** tay karta hai: tuning fork 1 second mein 440 baar hilta hai, to wo medium ko 1 second mein 440 baar dhakelta hai, chahe medium kuch bhi ho. Medium badalne par wave ki speed badalti hai, aur f fixed hai, isliye v = f × λ ke hisaab se **λ badalti hai**.

### Sirf idea: amplitude, loudness, frequency aur pitch

- **Amplitude badhao** (zor se chhedo): sound **loud** hoti hai. Frequency wahi rehti hai, isliye unchai-neechai (pitch) nahi badalti.
- **Frequency badhao**: sound ki **pitch unchi** hoti hai (patli, unchi awaaz).

Dono ki poori kahani Topic 5 mein aayegi. Abhi bas itna yaad rakho.

## Wapas music room mein: kahani ka hal

Mr. Alvares ne Kyra se kaha, "Patli taar chhoti aur halki hoti hai, isliye wo **jaldi-jaldi** hilti hai. Moti taar bhaari hoti hai, wo **dheere-dheere** hilti hai."

Maan lo patli taar ki frequency **880 Hz** hai aur moti taar ki **110 Hz**. Hawa mein v = 340 m/s:

- Patli taar: λ = v ÷ f = 340 m/s ÷ 880 Hz = **0.386 m**
- Moti taar: λ = v ÷ f = 340 m/s ÷ 110 Hz = **3.09 m**

Jaanch: 880 Hz × 0.386 m = 339.7 m/s ≈ 340 m/s ✅, aur 110 Hz × 3.09 m = 339.9 m/s ≈ 340 m/s ✅ (chhota sa farak rounding ki wajah se hai).

Mr. Alvares bole, "Dekha Kyra? Patli taar: **zyada frequency, chhoti wavelength, unchi pitch**. Moti taar: **kam frequency, lambi wavelength, neechi pitch**. Aur jab maine zor se chheda, to **amplitude** badha, frequency wahi rahi, isliye sirf aawaaz loud hui."

Kyra bahut khush hui: "Yaani ek hi taar se bhi teeno numbers samajh aa gaye: A, f aur λ!"

## Part C: Solved examples

Is topic mein har jagah hawa mein v = 340 m/s (jab tak kuch aur na likha ho).

### Example 1: f aur T

Ek source 2 s mein 50 oscillations karta hai. Uski frequency aur time period nikalo.

f = oscillations ÷ samay = 50 ÷ 2 s = **25 Hz**
T = 1 ÷ f = 1 ÷ 25 Hz = **0.04 s**

Jaanch: 25 Hz × 0.04 s = 1 ✅ (f × T = 1 hona chahiye)

### Example 2: f aur λ se speed

Ek 256 Hz ke sur ki wavelength 1.33 m hai. Wave ki speed kitni hai?

v = f × λ = 256 Hz × 1.33 m = 340.48 m/s ≈ **340.5 m/s**

Jaanch: 340.48 ÷ 256 = 1.33 m ✅, aur ye hawa mein 340 m/s ke bahut nazdeek hai ✅

### Example 3: v aur f se wavelength

680 Hz ki sound hawa mein jaa rahi hai. Wavelength kitni hai?

λ = v ÷ f = 340 m/s ÷ 680 Hz = **0.5 m**

Jaanch: 680 Hz × 0.5 m = 340 m/s ✅

### Example 4: v aur λ se frequency

Hawa mein ek sound ki wavelength 0.25 m hai. Frequency kitni hai?

f = v ÷ λ = 340 m/s ÷ 0.25 m = **1360 Hz** (= 1.36 kHz)

Jaanch: 1360 Hz × 0.25 m = 340 m/s ✅

### Example 5: 20 Hz aur 20000 Hz ki wavelength

Hawa mein 20 Hz aur 20000 Hz (= 20 kHz) ki sound ki wavelength nikalo.

20 Hz: λ = 340 m/s ÷ 20 Hz = **17 m**
20000 Hz: λ = 340 m/s ÷ 20000 Hz = 0.017 m = **1.7 cm** (kyunki 0.017 m × 100 = 1.7 cm)

Jaanch: 20 Hz × 17 m = 340 ✅, 20000 Hz × 0.017 m = 340 ✅

(Ye 20 Hz se 20000 Hz wala hissa insaan ke sunne ki range hai. Iski poori baat Topic 6 mein.)

### Example 6: Kisi doori mein kitni waves

170 Hz ki ek sound ki wavelength 2 m hai. 100 m ki doori mein kitni poori waves aayengi?

Waves ki sankhya = kul doori ÷ ek wave ki lambai = 100 m ÷ 2 m = **50 waves**

Jaanch: 50 × 2 m = 100 m ✅. Aur v = f × λ = 170 Hz × 2 m = 340 m/s, jo hawa ki speed se match karta hai ✅

### Example 7: 512 Hz ke tuning fork ka time period

T = 1 ÷ f = 1 ÷ 512 Hz = 0.001953 s ≈ 0.00195 s = **1.95 ms** (kyunki 1 s = 1000 ms, to 0.001953 × 1000 = 1.953 ms)

Jaanch: 512 Hz × 0.001953 s = 1.0 ✅

### Example 8: Machhar ke pankhon ka time period

Ek machhar ke pankh 1 second mein 500 baar hilte hain (f = 500 Hz). Time period kitna hai?

T = 1 ÷ f = 1 ÷ 500 Hz = 0.002 s = **2 ms**

Jaanch: 500 Hz × 0.002 s = 1 ✅

### Example 9: 1.7 km ki doori kitne samay mein?

850 Hz ki sound ko 1.7 km door tak pahunchne mein kitna samay lagega?

Pehle km ko m mein badlo: 1.7 km = 1.7 × 1000 = 1700 m.
Samay = doori ÷ speed = 1700 m ÷ 340 m/s = **5 s**

Dhyan do: samay nikalne mein frequency (850 Hz) ki zaroorat hi nahi padi, kyunki speed medium par depend karti hai, frequency par nahi.

Jaanch: 340 m/s × 5 s = 1700 m ✅

### Example 10: Paani mein wave

Paani mein sound ki speed 1500 m/s hai. 3000 Hz ki sound ki wavelength nikalo.

λ = v ÷ f = 1500 m/s ÷ 3000 Hz = **0.5 m**

Jaanch: 3000 Hz × 0.5 m = 1500 m/s ✅

### Example 11: Ratio wala sawaal

Hawa mein ek sound 200 Hz ki hai. Agar frequency double kar di jaye (medium wahi), to wavelength par kya asar padega?

Pehle: λ₁ = 340 m/s ÷ 200 Hz = 1.7 m
Frequency double: f₂ = 2 × 200 Hz = 400 Hz, to λ₂ = 340 m/s ÷ 400 Hz = 0.85 m

λ₂ ÷ λ₁ = 0.85 ÷ 1.7 = 0.5, yaani wavelength **aadhi** ho gayi.

Jaanch: v fixed hai, isliye λ ∝ 1 ÷ f: f double to λ aadhi ✅. Ye har frequency ke liye sach hai.

### Example 12: Data table se amplitude, T, f aur λ padhna

Ek jagah ke kan ki displacement samay ke saath is table mein hai:

| t (ms) | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| y (mm) | 0 | 2 | 0 | −2 | 0 | 2 | 0 | −2 | 0 |

- **Amplitude:** y ka sabse bada maan 2 mm (aur sabse chhota −2 mm), to A = **2 mm** (beech wali 0 position se maximum doori).
- **Time period:** y = 2 mm pehli baar t = 1 ms par hai aur agli baar t = 5 ms par. Beech ka samay T = 5 − 1 = **4 ms** = 4 × 10⁻³ s = 0.004 s.
- **Frequency:** f = 1 ÷ T = 1 ÷ 0.004 s = **250 Hz**.
- **Hawa mein wavelength:** λ = v ÷ f = 340 m/s ÷ 250 Hz = **1.36 m**.

Jaanch: 250 Hz × 0.004 s = 1 ✅, aur 250 Hz × 1.36 m = 340 m/s ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **Wavelength ko amplitude samajhna.** ❌ Wavelength **lambai** hai (ek compression se agle compression tak), amplitude **unchai** (beech se peak tak). Figure 3.1 mein dono alag nishaan hain. Dono ka unit metre ho sakta hai, par naap alag hai.
2. **Ye sochna ki frequency badhne par speed badhti hai.** ❌ Hawa mein speed 340 m/s hi rehti hai. Frequency badhti hai to **wavelength ghatti hai**.
3. **Ye sochna ki naye medium mein jaane par frequency badal jaati hai.** ❌ Frequency source tay karta hai aur wo same rehti hai. Wavelength aur speed badalti hain. (Tuning fork 440 Hz: hawa mein 0.77 m, paani mein 3.41 m.)
4. **f aur T ko mix karna.** f = 1 ÷ T, **f × T = 1**. Agar f badi hai to T chhota hoga. Example: 500 Hz ka T = 0.002 s, 500 s nahi.
5. **Units convert karna bhoolna.** 3 kHz = **3000 Hz**, 1.7 cm = **0.017 m**, 1.7 km = **1700 m**. v = f × λ mein hamesha Hz aur m hi daalo.
6. **Ye sochna ki loud sound tez chalti hai.** ❌ Loudness amplitude se judi hai. Speed medium par depend karti hai. Zor se bajao ya dheere se, awaaz utni hi der mein pahunchegi.

## Quick Recap

- **Amplitude (A)** = beech wali position se maximum displacement (ya pressure ka maximum badlaav). Unit m (ya Pa). Ye sound ki loudness se judi hai.
- **Wavelength (λ)** = do lagataar compressions (ya rarefactions) ke beech ki doori. SI unit m.
- **Frequency (f)** = 1 second mein poori vibrations. SI unit Hz (1 Hz = 1 s⁻¹, 1 kHz = 1000 Hz).
- **Time period (T)** = ek vibration ka samay (s). **f = 1 ÷ T**.
- Ek time period T mein wave ek wavelength λ tay karti hai, isliye **v = λ ÷ T = f × λ**. Units: m/s = Hz × m.
- Hawa mein v = 340 m/s: **λ = v ÷ f**, aur f = v ÷ λ.
- Speed medium par depend karti hai, frequency par nahi. Is wajah se frequency badhne par wavelength ghatti hai.
- Naye medium mein jaane par **f wahi rehti hai**, v aur λ badalte hain.
- Idea: amplitude se loudness, frequency se pitch (poori kahani Topic 5 mein).

## Practice Questions

(Take the speed of sound in air as 340 m/s and in water as 1500 m/s.)

1. A source vibrates 2000 times in one second. Find its frequency and its time period (in ms).
2. A vibrating body has a time period of 0.005 s. Find its frequency, and the wavelength of the sound it produces in air.
3. Find the wavelength in air of a 5 kHz sound. Give the answer in metres and in centimetres.
4. A sound wave in water has a wavelength of 0.75 m. What is its frequency?
5. A tuning fork of frequency 256 Hz vibrates for 10 s. (a) How many complete waves does it send out? (b) What distance does the first wave front travel in air in this time? (c) Check that (number of waves) × (wavelength) gives the same distance.
6. In a distance graph of a sound wave in air, two consecutive compressions are at x = 0.4 m and x = 1.2 m. Find the wavelength, the frequency and the time period.
7. A tuning fork of 256 Hz is struck first in air and then in water. What happens to (a) its frequency, (b) its wavelength? Find the wavelength in each medium.
8. Frequency of a sound is tripled in the same medium. By what factor does the wavelength change?
9. State true or false and give a one-line reason: (a) A louder sound travels faster in air. (b) The frequency of a wave changes when it moves from air to water. (c) The amplitude and the wavelength of a wave are the same thing. (d) f × T = 1.
10. From this data of a vibrating point, find the amplitude, the time period and the frequency: t = 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2 ms gives y = 0, 3, 0, −3, 0 mm (one full oscillation in 2 ms).

### Answers

1. f = **2000 Hz** (= 2 kHz). T = 1 ÷ 2000 Hz = 0.0005 s = **0.5 ms**.
   Jaanch: 2000 Hz × 0.0005 s = 1 ✅

2. f = 1 ÷ T = 1 ÷ 0.005 s = **200 Hz**. λ = v ÷ f = 340 m/s ÷ 200 Hz = **1.7 m**.
   Jaanch: 200 Hz × 1.7 m = 340 m/s ✅

3. 5 kHz = 5000 Hz. λ = 340 m/s ÷ 5000 Hz = **0.068 m** = 0.068 × 100 = **6.8 cm**.
   Jaanch: 5000 Hz × 0.068 m = 340 m/s ✅

4. f = v ÷ λ = 1500 m/s ÷ 0.75 m = **2000 Hz**.
   Jaanch: 2000 Hz × 0.75 m = 1500 m/s ✅

5. (a) 256 Hz = 256 waves per second, to 10 s mein 256 × 10 = **2560 waves**.
   (b) Doori = v × t = 340 m/s × 10 s = **3400 m**.
   (c) λ = 340 ÷ 256 = 1.328 m. Waves × λ = 2560 × 1.328125 m = 3400 m ✅ (exact: 2560 × 340 ÷ 256 = 10 × 340 = 3400).

6. λ = 1.2 m − 0.4 m = **0.8 m**. f = v ÷ λ = 340 m/s ÷ 0.8 m = **425 Hz**. T = 1 ÷ 425 Hz = 0.00235 s ≈ **2.35 ms**.
   Jaanch: 425 Hz × 0.8 m = 340 m/s ✅

7. (a) Frequency **nahi badalti**: 256 Hz hi rehti hai, kyunki source use tay karta hai.
   (b) Wavelength badalti hai. Hawa mein: 340 ÷ 256 = **1.33 m**. Paani mein: 1500 ÷ 256 ≈ **5.86 m**.
   Jaanch: 256 Hz × 5.86 m = 1500 m/s (lagbhag) ✅

8. λ = v ÷ f. f ko 3 guna karne par λ **ek-tihai (1/3)** reh jaati hai.
   Example: 100 Hz ke liye 3.4 m, 300 Hz ke liye 340 ÷ 300 = 1.13 m, aur 3.4 ÷ 3 = 1.13 ✅

9. (a) **False** ❌. Speed medium par depend karti hai, loudness (amplitude) par nahi.
   (b) **False** ❌. Frequency source se tay hoti hai; wavelength badalti hai.
   (c) **False** ❌. Wavelength lambai hai (ek wave ki), amplitude beech se maximum displacement hai.
   (d) **True** ✅. Kyunki f = 1 ÷ T.

10. Amplitude = **3 mm** (maximum y). Ek poori oscillation 2 ms mein hoti hai, to T = **2 ms** = 0.002 s. f = 1 ÷ 0.002 s = **500 Hz**.
    Jaanch: 500 Hz × 0.002 s = 1 ✅
