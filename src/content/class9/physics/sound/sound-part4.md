# Topic 4: Sound ki Speed, Echo aur Reverberation

Pichhle topic mein humne v = f λ seekha: wave ki speed, frequency aur wavelength ko jodti hai. Ek baat bhi dekhi thi ki **speed medium decide karta hai**, aur frequency source decide karta hai. Ab sawal ye hai ki wo speed asal mein hai kitni? Alag-alag medium mein kitni badalti hai? Aur jab sound kisi deewar ya pahaad se takrakar wapas aati hai, to kya hota hai? Is topic mein hum sound ki speed, uske badalne ke karan, echo (goonj) aur reverberation (hall mein awaaz ka ruk-ruk kar goonjna) samjhenge.

Speed ka formula purana hai: **speed = distance ÷ time** (motion wale chapter se). Hum use hi sound par lagayenge: **v = d ÷ t**, yaani d = v × t. **Is poore topic mein, jab tak kuch aur na likha ho, hawa mein sound ki speed v = 340 m/s lenge.**

## Pahaad ki goonj

Mr. Karma ek trekking guide the. Ek din wo Zia aur uske group ko pahaad par le gaye. Raaste mein ek bahut gehri, chaudi ghati (valley) aayi. Kinare par khade hokar neeche dekho to bas dhundh aur door saamne ki pathreeli deewar dikhti thi.

Mr. Karma ne muskurakar kaha, "Zia, zor se 'hello' bolo."

Zia ne apni poori taakat se chillaya, "Hello!" Kuch der kuch nahi hua. Phir saamne ki deewar se awaaz wapas aayi: "...hello..." Zia ki aankhein badi ho gayi. "Sir, ye kaun bola? Mere jaisi hi awaaz thi!"

"Wo teri hi awaaz thi," Mr. Karma hanse. "Saamne ki chattan se takrakar wapas aayi hai. Ise **echo** kehte hain. Tune dhyan diya, awaaz wapas aane mein lagbhag **2.5 second** lage?"

Zia ne socha, "To sir, saamne ki deewar kitni door hai? Kya hum ye bina napey pata kar sakte hain?"

Mr. Karma ne kaha, "Bilkul. Aur shaam ko jab baarish aayegi, to bijli ki chamak aur gadgadahat se bhi hum kuch aisa hi nikalenge." Is kahani ko hum topic ke beech aur ant mein solve karenge.

## Part A: Sound ki speed, bijli aur gadgadahat

### Sound bahut tez hai, par roshni se bahut dheemi

Sound ki speed hawa mein lagbhag 340 m/s hai. Ye kaafi tez lagti hai: 340 m/s = 340 × 3600 ÷ 1000 = 1224 km/h. Par roshni (light) iske saamne bahut tez hai: lagbhag 3 × 10⁸ m/s (30 crore m/s). Yani roshni ek second mein lagbhag 3 lakh km chalti hai. (Roshni ke baare mein aage ke chapters mein padhenge.)

Isiliye jab baadal mein bijli chamakti hai, to **chamak hume lagbhag turant dikhti hai, aur gadgadahat (thunder) baad mein sunayi deti hai**, jabki dono ek hi jagah, ek hi pal mein bane the. Bijli 2 km door ho, to roshni ko wahan se aane mein sirf 2000 m ÷ (3 × 10⁸ m/s) ≈ 0.0000068 s lagta hai, yani itna kam ki hum ise zero hi maan sakte hain. Sound ko wahi 2 km aane mein kai second lagte hain.

### Zia ka storm wala sawal

Shaam ko jab tez aandhi-toofan aaya, to Mr. Karma aur Zia ek shed ke neeche ruk gaye. Achanak bijli chamki. Mr. Karma ne kaha, "Ginti shuru karo!" Zia ne ginna shuru kiya: ek, do, teen... 6 second baad gadgadahat ki awaaz aayi.

Mr. Karma bole, "Ab bijli ki doori nikalo. Roshni to turant aa gayi, to sound ka lagne wala time hi poora gap hai."

- v = 340 m/s, t = 6 s
- d = v × t = 340 m/s × 6 s = **2040 m** (lagbhag 2 km)

**Jaanch:** d ÷ v = 2040 m ÷ 340 m/s = 6 s ✅

> **Anguthe ka niyam (rule of thumb):** 340 m/s ke lagbhag 1000 m ÷ 3 s hota hai. Isliye **har 3 second ka gap = lagbhag 1 km**. 6 second ka gap = 2 km, 9 second ka gap = 3 km. Jaanch: 340 × 3 = 1020 m ≈ 1 km ✅

Zia ne socha, "Matlab agar chamak aur gadgadahat ke beech ka time kam hota jaye, to toofan paas aa raha hai!" Mr. Karma ne sir hilaya, "Bilkul sahi."

## Part B: Sound ki speed kin cheezon par depend karti hai?

### 1. Medium par

Pichhle topics mein humne dekha tha ki sound ko chalne ke liye medium chahiye. Ab dekhte hain ki alag-alag medium mein uski speed alag kyun hai. Neeche ke numbers **lagbhag (approximate)** hain:

| Medium | Speed of sound (m/s), lagbhag |
|---|---|
| Hawa, 0 °C par | 331 |
| Hawa, 20 °C par | 343 |
| Hydrogen gas | 1270 |
| Paani (fresh water) | 1480 se 1500 |
| Samundar ka paani (sea water) | 1530 |
| Barf (ice) | 3200 |
| Lohe (iron) | 5100 |
| Steel | 5900 |
| Kaanch (glass) | 5640 |
| Aluminium | 6400 |

Table se ek saaf pattern dikhta hai: **aam taur par sound sabse tez solid mein, phir liquid mein, aur sabse dheemi gas mein chalti hai.**

```
 Sound ki speed (lagbhag, har # ≈ 250 m/s)

 Hawa (20 °C)  : # 343 m/s
 Paani         : ###### 1500 m/s
 Lohe          : #################### 5100 m/s
 Aluminium     : ########################## 6400 m/s
```

> **Figure 4.1** – Alag-alag medium mein sound ki speed (lagbhag): hawa mein sabse kam, solid mein sabse zyada.

**Aisa kyun?** Sound ek particle se doosre particle tak vibration pass karti hai. Solid mein particles ek-dusre ke bahut paas aur kaske hue (tightly connected) hote hain, to ek particle ko hilne par wo apne padosi ko turant dhakka de deta hai: vibration jaldi aage badhta hai. Liquid mein particles thode door aur dheele jude hote hain, to vibration thoda dheere chalta hai. Gas mein particles bahut door-door hote hain, aur ek particle ko doosre tak pahunchne mein zyada samay lagta hai, isliye sound sabse dheemi chalti hai.

Ek rozmarra ka example: railway track par kaan lagakar door aati train ki awaaz pehle track mein se sunayi deti hai (lohe mein tez), aur hawa mein se baad mein. (Dhyan rahe: ye sirf dikhane ke liye hai, asal mein track par kaan lagana khatarnaak hai.)

### 2. Temperature par

Ek hi medium mein bhi speed temperature ke saath badalti hai. Hawa garm ho to sound **zyada tez** chalti hai. Table mein dekho: 0 °C par 331 m/s aur 20 °C par 343 m/s. Yani 20 °C badhne par 12 m/s zyada, matlab har 1 °C par lagbhag **0.6 m/s** zyada. Isse ek anguthe ka niyam (approximation) nikalta hai:

> **v ≈ 331 + 0.6 T**   (v hawa mein sound ki speed m/s mein, T temperature °C mein)

Jaanch: T = 20 °C par v = 331 + 0.6 × 20 = 331 + 12 = 343 m/s ✅. Ye ek **lagbhag** wala formula hai, aur hum ise sirf hawa ke liye aur ek di hui approximation ke roop mein use karenge.

### 3. Jin cheezon par speed depend nahi karti

Ek hi medium (aur ek hi temperature) mein sound ki speed **awaaz ke tez ya dheere hone par, uski frequency (pitch) par, ya amplitude par depend nahi karti.** Yani zor se chillane wali awaaz aur dheere se bolne wali awaaz, patli awaaz aur mote sur ki awaaz: sab same speed se chalti hain. (Agar frequency badalti hai to v = f λ ke hisaab se wavelength badal jati hai, speed nahi.) Hawa mein nami (humidity) zyada ho to sound thodi si zyada tez chalti hai, par is chapter mein hum bas itna jaanenge.

## Part C: Alag-alag medium aur temperature ke solved examples

**Example 1 (hawa, paani aur lohe mein samay):** Ek sound ko 1.7 km door jaana hai. Hawa mein (340 m/s), paani mein (1500 m/s) aur lohe mein (5100 m/s) kitna time lagega?

- d = 1.7 km = 1.7 × 1000 = 1700 m
- Hawa: t = d ÷ v = 1700 m ÷ 340 m/s = **5 s**
- Paani: t = 1700 m ÷ 1500 m/s = **1.13 s** (lagbhag)
- Lohe: t = 1700 m ÷ 5100 m/s = **0.33 s** (lagbhag)

**Jaanch:** 340 × 5 = 1700 m ✅. 1500 × 1.133 = 1700 m ✅. 5100 × 0.333 = 1700 m ✅. Hawa mein sabse zyada samay, lohe mein sabse kam.

**Example 2 (bandook ki awaaz):** Ek shikari ne bandook chalayi. Koi vyakti uske 1.02 km door hai. Use goli ki awaaz kitni der baad sunayi degi? (v = 340 m/s)

- d = 1.02 km = 1020 m
- t = 1020 m ÷ 340 m/s = **3 s**

**Jaanch:** 340 m/s × 3 s = 1020 m ✅

**Example 3 (jahaaz ka horn):** Ek jahaaz ne horn bajaya. 3.4 km door doosra jahaaz paani ke upar khada hai. Hawa ke raaste awaaz use kitni der mein sunayi degi? (v = 340 m/s)

- d = 3.4 km = 3400 m
- t = 3400 m ÷ 340 m/s = **10 s**

**Jaanch:** 340 × 10 = 3400 m ✅

**Example 4 (30 °C par speed):** Hawa ka temperature 30 °C hai. v ≈ 331 + 0.6 T se sound ki lagbhag speed nikalo.

- v ≈ 331 + 0.6 × 30 = 331 + 18 = **349 m/s** (lagbhag)

**Jaanch:** 20 °C wali speed 343 m/s se 10 °C zyada garmi par 0.6 × 10 = 6 m/s zyada: 343 + 6 = 349 m/s ✅

**Example 5 (garm din mein samay):** Example 4 ki hawa mein (30 °C, 349 m/s) 1.745 km door ek khadaan (quarry) mein dhamaka hua. Awaaz kitni der mein aayegi?

- d = 1.745 km = 1745 m
- t = 1745 m ÷ 349 m/s = **5 s**

**Jaanch:** 349 m/s × 5 s = 1745 m ✅. Agar 340 m/s lete to 1745 ÷ 340 = 5.13 s aata: thoda sa fark, kyunki garmi mein sound tez hoti hai. Isliye jab sawal temperature batata hai, to wahi speed use karo.

## Part D: Reflection aur Echo

### Sound bhi wapas lautti hai

Gend ko deewar par maaro to wo wapas aati hai. Waise hi **sound jab kisi kathor (hard) aur chikne (smooth) surface se takrati hai, to wapas lautti hai.** Ise sound ka **reflection** kehte hain. Chattan, pakki deewar, bade hall ki deewarein aisi surface hain. Ghati ki chattan se Zia ki awaaz isi tarah wapas aayi.

Jo reflected sound hume original awaaz ke baad, alag aur saaf sunayi de, wo **echo (goonj)** hai.

### Echo sunne ki shart

Hamare kaan mein koi bhi awaaz sunne ke baad uska asar lagbhag **0.1 second** tak bana rehta hai (persistence of hearing). Agar doosri awaaz 0.1 s ke andar aa jaye, to dono mil jaati hain aur alag-alag nahi sunayi deti. Isliye echo sunne ke liye **reflected sound original sound ke kam se kam 0.1 s baad kaan tak pahunchni chahiye.**

Sawal: deewar kam se kam kitni door honi chahiye? Maan lo hum deewar ke saamne khade hain aur ek awaaz nikalte hain.

- Awaaz deewar tak jaati hai aur wapas aati hai. Yani **kul doori = 2 × d** (d = deewar tak ki doori).
- Is kul doori ko tay karne mein sound ko kam se kam 0.1 s chahiye.
- 2d = v × t = 340 m/s × 0.1 s = 34 m
- d = 34 m ÷ 2 = **17 m**

**Jaanch:** 17 m aana + 17 m jaana = 34 m. 34 m ÷ 340 m/s = 0.1 s ✅

Yani echo sunne ke liye reflecting surface source se **kam se kam lagbhag 17 m** door honi chahiye. 20 °C par v = 343 m/s lene se d = 343 × 0.1 ÷ 2 = 17.15 m aata hai, yaani yahan bhi lagbhag 17 m hi. Isiliye chhote kamre mein hum chillate hain to echo nahi sunayi deta (deewar 17 m se kam door hai), par khule maidan, bade hall ya pahaad ke saamne sunayi deta hai.

```
  Source (Zia)                                   Deewar / chattan
     :                                                :
     :  ────────── awaaz jaati hai (d) ───────────>   :
     :                                                :
     :  <───────── echo wapas aata hai (d) ─────────   :
     :                                                :
     ├────────────────── d ───────────────────────────┤
              Kul doori = d + d = 2d
```

> **Figure 4.2** – Echo mein sound deewar tak jaati hai aur wapas aati hai, isliye sound ki chali hui kul doori 2d hoti hai.

### Deewar ki doori nikalne ka rule

Echo aane mein jo time t lagta hai, usme sound ne **aana aur jaana**, dono kiye. To:

> **2d = v × t,  isliye  d = v × t ÷ 2**

Yahi wo "÷ 2" hai jise log sabse zyada bhoolte hain. Yaad rakho: stopwatch ka time pura **round trip** ka hai, deewar tak ek taraf ka nahi.

### Zia ka sawal solve karte hain

Zia ki awaaz ka echo 2.5 s baad aaya. Ghati ki saamne wali deewar kitni door hai?

- v = 340 m/s, t = 2.5 s
- Sound ne chali hui kul doori = v × t = 340 m/s × 2.5 s = 850 m
- Deewar tak ki doori d = 850 m ÷ 2 = **425 m**

**Jaanch:** 425 m jaana + 425 m aana = 850 m. 850 m ÷ 340 m/s = 2.5 s ✅. Aur 425 m > 17 m, to echo alag sunayi dena theek hai ✅

Mr. Karma ne kaha, "Dekha Zia? Deewar 425 m door hai, yani lagbhag aadha km. Bina kisi measuring tape ke!"

## Part E: Echo ke solved examples

**Example 6 (kam se kam doori):** Hawa mein v = 340 m/s hai. Echo sunne ke liye reflecting surface kam se kam kitni door honi chahiye?

- Kul doori = v × t = 340 m/s × 0.1 s = 34 m
- d = 34 m ÷ 2 = **17 m**

**Jaanch:** 2 × 17 m = 34 m, aur 34 m ÷ 340 m/s = 0.1 s ✅

**Example 7 (pahaad ki doori, 1.5 s):** Ek aadmi ne ghati mein chillaya aur 1.5 s baad echo suna. Pahaad kitni door hai? (v = 340 m/s)

- Kul doori = 340 m/s × 1.5 s = 510 m
- d = 510 m ÷ 2 = **255 m**

**Jaanch:** 255 m × 2 = 510 m, 510 m ÷ 340 m/s = 1.5 s ✅

**Example 8 (echo 3 s baad):** Ek ladki ne pahaad ke saamne taali bajayi aur 3 s baad echo suna. Pahaad kitni door hai? (v = 340 m/s)

- Kul doori = 340 m/s × 3 s = 1020 m
- d = 1020 m ÷ 2 = **510 m**

**Jaanch:** 510 × 2 = 1020 m, 1020 ÷ 340 = 3 s ✅. Dhyan do: Example 7 ka time 1.5 s tha aur doori 255 m; yahan time double (3 s) hai, to doori bhi double (510 m) ✅

**Example 9 (bandook aur deewar):** Ek aadmi bandook chalata hai aur 2 s baad echo sunta hai. Deewar kitni door hai? (v = 340 m/s)

- Kul doori = 340 m/s × 2 s = 680 m
- d = 680 m ÷ 2 = **340 m**

**Jaanch:** 340 × 2 = 680 m, 680 ÷ 340 = 2 s ✅. Dhyan do: sound ne 340 m (1 s) jaane mein aur 340 m (1 s) aane mein lagaya.

**Example 10 (echo se sound ki speed):** Ek student deewar se 100 m door khade hokar taali bajata hai aur 0.6 s baad echo sunta hai. Sound ki speed kitni hai?

- Kul doori = 2 × d = 2 × 100 m = 200 m
- v = kul doori ÷ time = 200 m ÷ 0.6 s = **333.3 m/s** (lagbhag)

**Jaanch:** 333.3 m/s × 0.6 s = 200 m ✅. Ye value hawa ki lagbhag 331 se 343 m/s ke beech mein hai: sensible (thoda thanda din ho sakta hai).

**Example 11 (do echo):** Ek aadmi ek maidan mein khada hai. Door ek pahaad hai aur paas ek imarat (building). Usne taali bajayi to 1 s baad pehla echo aur 3 s baad doosra echo suna. Dono ki doori aur do echo ke beech ka time batao. (v = 340 m/s)

- Pehla echo (imarat se): d₁ = 340 m/s × 1 s ÷ 2 = **170 m**
- Doosra echo (pahaad se): d₂ = 340 m/s × 3 s ÷ 2 = **510 m**
- Do echo ke beech ka time = 3 s − 1 s = **2 s**

**Jaanch:** 170 × 2 = 340 m, 340 ÷ 340 = 1 s ✅. 510 × 2 = 1020 m, 1020 ÷ 340 = 3 s ✅. Zyada door ka surface zyada der baad echo deta hai, isliye imarat pehle, pahaad baad mein ✅.

```
  Aadmi              Imarat                  Pahaad
    :                  :                       :
    :<───── 170 m ────>:                       :
    :<───────────────── 510 m ────────────────>:

   Pehla echo: 1 s      Doosra echo: 3 s
```

> **Figure 4.3** – Do alag-alag doori par surfaces hon to do alag echo sunayi dete hain: nazdeek wala pehle aur door wala baad mein.

**Multiple echoes (kai echo):** Agar do samne-samne ki pahaadiyan (parallel cliffs) hon, ya ghati ki deewarein kai taraf ho, to awaaz kai baar reflect hoti hai aur kai echo sunayi dete hain. Isiliye pahaadon mein "hello" bolne par kabhi-kabhi ek ke baad ek kai goonj sunayi deti hain.

**Example 12 (garm din mein echo):** Hawa ka temperature 30 °C hai (v ≈ 349 m/s). Ek shout ka echo 2 s baad aaya. Deewar kitni door hai?

- v ≈ 331 + 0.6 × 30 = 349 m/s
- Kul doori = 349 m/s × 2 s = 698 m
- d = 698 m ÷ 2 = **349 m**

**Jaanch:** 349 × 2 = 698 m, 698 ÷ 349 = 2 s ✅. Agar 340 m/s lete to 340 m aata, thoda sa alag: isliye jab temperature diya ho to us hisaab ki speed lo.

## Part F: Reverberation

### Bade hall mein awaaz kyun gunjti hai?

Ek bada hall, mandir ya khali auditorium sochiye. Wahan koi taali bajaye ya bole, to awaaz deewaron, chhat aur farsh se baar-baar reflect hoti hai. Har reflection ke baad thodi si awaaz kaan tak aati hai. Is wajah se **source ke band hone ke baad bhi awaaz kuch der tak sunayi deti rehti hai, gunjti rehti hai.** Isi ko **reverberation** kehte hain.

```
    ┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │  \         /           \          /    │   chhat se reflection
    │   \       /             \        /     │
    │    \     /      (S)      \      /      │   S = awaaz ka source
    │     \   /     /   :   \   \    /       │
    │      \ /     /    :    \   \  /        │
    │       X     <     (K)    >    X        │   K = sunne wala (kaan)
    └────────────────────────────────────────┘
        deewaron aur farsh se bhi baar-baar reflection
```

> **Figure 4.4** – Hall mein awaaz kai raaston se, alag-alag samay par baar-baar kaan tak pahunchti hai, isliye gunj bani rehti hai (reverberation).

### Echo aur reverberation mein farak

- **Echo:** door ki surface (17 m ya usse zyada) se **ek saaf, alag** repeat sunayi deti hai.
- **Reverberation:** chhote ya band space mein **kai reflections itne jaldi-jaldi** aate hain ki wo ek-dusre mein mil jaate hain aur ek lambi gunj ban jaati hai. Alag-alag repeat nahi sunayi dete.

### Zyada reverberation ka nuksaan aur uska upay

Agar hall mein reverberation zyada ho, to ek shabd ki gunj agle shabd se mil jaati hai aur **bhasha (speech) samajhna mushkil ho jaata hai**. Ise kam karne ke upay:

- **Sound absorb karne wali cheezein lagana:** parde (curtains), carpet, gadde-dar kursiyan, soft furnishing, chhed-wale (perforated) board. Ye sound ko sokh leti hain, wapas reflect nahi karti. Hall mein baithe log (audience) bhi sound sokhte hain.
- **Chhat ko curved banana** aur **stage ke peeche soundboard (sounding board) lagana:** ye speaker ki awaaz ko ek hi disha mein audience ki taraf bhejte hain, jisse awaaz door tak saaf pahunchti hai.

Isiliye khaali bade hall mein bolne par jyada gunjti hai, par log bhar jaane par aur parde laga hone par awaaz saaf ho jaati hai.

**Example 13 (hall, sirf samajhne ke liye):** Ek khaali hall 17 m × 12 m ka hai, pakki deewaron aur sakht farsh ke saath. Ek aadmi bolta hai. Hall itna chhota hai ki deewarein 17 m se kam ya bas usi ke aaspaas hain, to reflected sound 0.1 s ke aaspaas ya usse bhi pehle pahunch jaati hai. Upar se chhat, farsh aur chaaron deewaron se kai reflections thode-thode samay ke fark se aate hain. Isliye alag-alag echo nahi sunayi dete, balki sab mil kar ek **gunj (reverberation)** banate hain. Parde, carpet aur audience baith jayein to ye gunj kam ho jaati hai.

## Part G: Sound ke reflection ke upyog

- **Megaphone, horn aur bugle:** Inki shankhu (cone) jaisi nali awaaz ko failne se rokti hai aur reflect karke ek hi disha mein door tak bhejti hai.

- **Concert halls aur auditorium:** Curved chhat aur soundboard awaaz ko audience tak achchhe se pahunchate hain.
- **Chamgadad aur dolphin:** Ye andhere ya gande paani mein echo ki madad se raasta dhoondte hain aur shikar pakadte hain (echolocation). Iska vistaar Topic 6 mein.
- **Samundar ki gehrayi napna (SONAR):** Jahaaz se awaaz neeche bheji jaati hai aur samundar ke tal se echo wapas aata hai. Ye bhi Topic 6 mein detail se aayega.
- **Echo method se speed napna:** Jaise Example 10 mein humne dekha.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Echo ke sawal mein doori ko aadha na karna**

Echo 2.5 s baad aaya. Galat: d = 340 × 2.5 = 850 m. Sahi: sound ne aana-jaana dono kiya, to d = 340 × 2.5 ÷ 2 = **425 m**.

**Galti 2: "Zor ki awaaz zyada tez chalti hai"**

Nahi. Ek hi medium aur temperature mein sound ki speed awaaz ke tez ya dheere hone par depend nahi karti. Zor se ki gayi awaaz bas zyada door tak sunayi deti hai, par uski speed wahi rehti hai.

**Galti 3: "Jis medium mein particles kam bhare hon, wahan sound tez chalti hai"**

Ulta hai. Hawa mein particles door-door hote hain aur sound sabse dheemi chalti hai (340 m/s). Lohe mein particles paas-paas aur kaske hote hain aur sound sabse tez chalti hai (5100 m/s). Andaza lagane ki jagah table ki values dekho.

**Galti 4: Alag temperature par ek hi speed use karna**

Agar sawal mein temperature diya hai (jaise 30 °C), to v ≈ 331 + 0.6 T se uski speed nikalo (349 m/s). Agar kuch nahi diya, to is topic mein 340 m/s lo.

**Galti 5: Echo aur reverberation ko ek samajhna**

Echo ek **alag, saaf** repeat hai jo door ki surface se aati hai (kam se kam 0.1 s baad). Reverberation mein **kai reflections itne paas-paas** aate hain ki wo mil jaate hain aur gunj ban jaati hai: ye hall jaisi band jagah mein hota hai.

**Galti 6: "Echo kisi bhi doori par sunayi deta hai"**

Nahi. Surface source se kam se kam lagbhag 17 m door honi chahiye (v = 340 m/s). 10 m ki deewar ke saath sound ko aana-jaana 20 m chalna padta hai, 20 m ÷ 340 m/s = 0.059 s ≈ 0.06 s, jo 0.1 s se kam hai: echo alag nahi sunayi dega.

## Quick Recap

- Sound ki speed hawa mein lagbhag **340 m/s** (v = d ÷ t). Roshni (3 × 10⁸ m/s) se bahut dheemi, isiliye bijli pehle dikhti hai aur gadgadahat baad mein sunayi deti hai.
- **Bijli ki doori ≈ 340 m/s × gap (s).** 3 second ka gap ≈ 1 km. Storm story: 6 s gap to 2040 m.
- Speed **medium** par depend karti hai: aam taur par **solid > liquid > gas** (lagbhag: hawa 340, paani 1500, lohe 5100 m/s), kyunki solid mein particles paas-paas aur kaske hote hain.
- **Temperature badhne par** sound ki speed badhti hai: lagbhag v ≈ 331 + 0.6 T (hawa mein, T °C mein). 0 °C par 331 m/s aur 20 °C par 343 m/s.
- Ek hi medium mein speed **loudness, frequency (pitch) ya amplitude par depend nahi karti**. Nami (humidity) zyada ho to speed thodi si badhti hai.
- **Echo:** kathor, chikni surface se reflected sound jo alag, saaf sunayi de. Shart: reflected sound original ke **kam se kam 0.1 s baad** pahunche (persistence of hearing), yani surface **lagbhag 17 m ya usse zyada** door ho (340 m/s par).
- **d = v × t ÷ 2**: kyunki sound aana-jaana dono karti hai. Story: 2.5 s baad echo to deewar 425 m door.
- **Multiple echoes:** kai surface (ghati, parallel cliffs) se kai echo.
- **Reverberation:** band ya bade hall mein baar-baar reflection se source band hone ke baad bhi awaaz ka gunjna. Zyada ho to speech unclear. Upay: parde, carpet, gadde, perforated board, audience, curved chhat, soundboard.
- Reflection ke upyog: megaphone/horn, stethoscope, concert hall ki chhat, chamgadad aur dolphin ki echolocation, SONAR (Topic 6), echo se speed napna.

## Practice Questions

1. A thunder is heard 4 s after a lightning flash is seen. How far away is the lightning? (Take the speed of sound in air as 340 m/s and ignore the time taken by light.)

2. A sound has to travel 2.55 km. Find the time taken (a) in air (340 m/s) and (b) in water (1500 m/s).

3. A man shouts near a cliff and hears the echo after 4 s. How far is the cliff? (v = 340 m/s)

4. A girl stands 8 m from a tall wall and claps. Will she hear a separate echo? Show with a calculation. (v = 340 m/s)

5. Using v ≈ 331 + 0.6 T, find the approximate speed of sound in air at 25 °C. How far does the sound travel in 5 s at this temperature?

6. At a temperature of 40 °C, the speed of sound in air is about 331 + 0.6 × 40 = 355 m/s. Find the minimum distance of a wall from a source for a separate echo to be heard at this temperature.

7. A student stands 85 m from a wall and claps. She hears the echo after 0.5 s. Find the speed of sound.

8. A person hears two echoes of a clap, one after 0.5 s and another after 1.5 s. If v = 340 m/s, find the distance of each reflecting surface and the time between the two echoes.

9. State True or False:
   (a) Louder sound travels faster than a softer sound in the same medium.
   (b) Sound generally travels faster in a solid than in a gas.
   (c) The sound of a clap in a hall with curtains and carpets reverberates more than in a bare hall.
   (d) To hear an echo, the reflected sound must reach the ear at least about 0.1 s after the original sound.

10. In a storm, the gap between a flash and the thunder is 15 s. How far is the storm (in m and in km)? Check it with the "3 s is about 1 km" rule.

11. What is reverberation? How is it different from an echo? Name two ways to reduce reverberation in a hall.

12. The speed of sound in iron is 5100 m/s. How long does a hammer-tap sound take to travel through a 2.55 km long iron rail? How long would it take through air at 340 m/s?

13. In the valley story, suppose Zia had heard her echo after 3.5 s instead of 2.5 s. How far would the opposite wall be? (v = 340 m/s)

### Answers

1. d = v × t = 340 m/s × 4 s = **1360 m** (lagbhag 1.36 km).
   Jaanch: 1360 m ÷ 340 m/s = 4 s ✅

2. d = 2.55 km = 2550 m. (a) Hawa mein: t = 2550 m ÷ 340 m/s = **7.5 s**. (b) Paani mein: t = 2550 m ÷ 1500 m/s = **1.7 s**.
   Jaanch: 340 × 7.5 = 2550 m ✅; 1500 × 1.7 = 2550 m ✅

3. d = v × t ÷ 2 = 340 m/s × 4 s ÷ 2 = 1360 m ÷ 2 = **680 m**.
   Jaanch: 680 × 2 = 1360 m, 1360 ÷ 340 = 4 s ✅

4. Sound ko aana-jaana 2 × 8 m = 16 m chalna hai. t = 16 m ÷ 340 m/s = **0.047 s** (lagbhag). Ye 0.1 s se kam hai, isliye **alag echo nahi sunayi dega**. (Awaaz original ke saath mil jaati hai.)
   Jaanch: Kam se kam doori 17 m honi chahiye, aur 8 m < 17 m ✅

5. v ≈ 331 + 0.6 × 25 = 331 + 15 = **346 m/s** (lagbhag). 5 s mein doori = 346 m/s × 5 s = **1730 m**.
   Jaanch: 1730 ÷ 346 = 5 s ✅

6. Kul doori = 355 m/s × 0.1 s = 35.5 m, to d = 35.5 m ÷ 2 = **17.75 m** (lagbhag 17.8 m).
   Jaanch: 17.75 × 2 = 35.5 m, 35.5 ÷ 355 = 0.1 s ✅. Garmi mein thodi zyada doori chahiye, par fark chhota hai.

7. Kul doori = 2 × 85 m = 170 m. v = 170 m ÷ 0.5 s = **340 m/s**.
   Jaanch: 340 m/s × 0.5 s = 170 m ✅

8. Pehla surface: d₁ = 340 m/s × 0.5 s ÷ 2 = **85 m**. Doosra: d₂ = 340 m/s × 1.5 s ÷ 2 = **255 m**. Do echo ke beech ka time = 1.5 s − 0.5 s = **1 s**.
   Jaanch: 85 × 2 ÷ 340 = 0.5 s ✅; 255 × 2 ÷ 340 = 1.5 s ✅

9. (a) **False** ❌. Ek hi medium mein sound ki speed loudness par depend nahi karti.
   (b) **True** ✅. Aam taur par solid > liquid > gas.
   (c) **False** ❌. Parde aur carpet sound sokhte hain, to reverberation kam hoti hai, zyada nahi.
   (d) **True** ✅. Persistence of hearing lagbhag 0.1 s hai.

10. d = 340 m/s × 15 s = **5100 m = 5.1 km**. Rule: 15 ÷ 3 = 5 km.
    Jaanch: 5100 ÷ 340 = 15 s ✅, aur 5.1 km ≈ 5 km ✅

11. Reverberation: hall jaisi band jagah mein deewaron, chhat aur farsh se baar-baar reflection ki wajah se source band hone ke baad bhi awaaz ka gunjte rehna. Echo ek door ki surface se aayi alag, saaf repeat hoti hai, jabki reverberation mein kai reflections mil jaate hain. Kam karne ke tareeke: parde, carpet, gadde-dar kursiyan, perforated board lagana (ya curved chhat aur soundboard banana).

12. d = 2.55 km = 2550 m. Lohe mein: t = 2550 m ÷ 5100 m/s = **0.5 s**. Hawa mein: t = 2550 m ÷ 340 m/s = **7.5 s**.
    Jaanch: 5100 × 0.5 = 2550 m ✅; 340 × 7.5 = 2550 m ✅

13. d = 340 m/s × 3.5 s ÷ 2 = 1190 m ÷ 2 = **595 m**.
    Jaanch: 595 × 2 = 1190 m, 1190 ÷ 340 = 3.5 s ✅
