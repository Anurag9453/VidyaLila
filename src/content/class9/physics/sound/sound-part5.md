# Topic 5: Sound ki Characteristics: Loudness, Pitch aur Quality

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki sound hawa mein lagbhag 340 m/s ki speed se chalti hai, aur ye speed medium aur temperature par depend karti hai: awaaz tez ho ya dheemi, patli ho ya moti, speed wahi rehti hai. Par ek baat abhi baaki hai. Sab awaazein ek jaisi nahi lagti: kuch tez, kuch patli, kuch mitthi. **Dhol ki awaaz aur bansuri ki awaaz mein farak kya hai? Ek chhote bachche ki awaaz aur uske papa ki awaaz mein farak kya hai?** Is topic mein hum sound ki teen characteristics seekhenge: **loudness, pitch aur quality**, aur dekhenge ki hamara kaan inhi teen cheezon se har awaaz ko pehchanta hai.

Prior knowledge: amplitude A (vibration ka maximum displacement), frequency f (1 second mein kitne vibration, unit Hz) aur wavelength λ (ek poore wave ki lambai) ke baare mein [sound-part3.md](sound-part3.md) mein padha tha. Hum unhi ka use karenge. **Is poore topic mein: hawa mein sound ki speed v = 340 m/s.**

## Radio station ka studio: bansuri aur violin

Ek radio station ke sound studio mein sound engineer Mr. Pandey apne mixing desk par baithe the. Unki padosi ki beti Nysa aaj pehli baar studio dekhne aayi thi. Studio ki deewaron par moti, narm foam ki panels lagi thi, aur beech mein do music artists the: ek bansuri (flute) wala aur ek violin wala.

Mr. Pandey ne Nysa se kaha, "Nysa, ab aankhein band karo. Main in dono se ek hi note bajane ko kehta hoon, ek ke baad ek. Dono ko main ek jaisi tezi par set kar raha hoon: na ek zyada zor se, na doosra kam."

Nysa ne aankhein band kar li. Pehle ek awaaz aayi, phir doosri. Dono ka sur (note) bilkul ek hi tha, aur dono utni hi tez thi. Phir bhi Nysa turant bol padi, "Pehli bansuri thi, doosri violin!"

Mr. Pandey muskuraye, "Bilkul sahi! Par ek baat batao: jab sur bhi same tha aur awaaz ki tezi bhi same thi, to tumne pehchana kaise?"

Nysa soch mein pad gayi, "Pata nahi Uncle, bas **alag lag rahi thi**: bansuri thodi 'halki aur saaf', aur violin 'bhari aur kuch khurdari'."

Mr. Pandey bole, "Yahi to hai. Kaan kisi bhi awaaz ko **teen cheezon** se tolta hai. Do cheezein dono mein same thi, isliye teesri ne tumhe bata diya. Chalo, teeno ko ek-ek karke samajhte hain." Is kahani ko hum topic ke ant mein solve karenge.

## Part A: Loudness (awaaz ki tezi)

### Loudness kya hai?

Jab koi sound hamare kaan tak aati hai, to hamein lagta hai ki wo **tez (loud)** hai ya **dheemi (soft)**. Is ehsaas ko **loudness** kehte hain. Ye kaan aur dimaag ka ehsaas hai, ek tarah ka "mehsoos karna".

### Loudness amplitude par depend karti hai

Sitar ki taar ko halke se chhedo, to dheemi awaaz aati hai. Taar ko zor se kheencho, to awaaz tez aati hai. Dhol ko halke se thapthapao to dheemi, zor se maaro to tez awaaz. Kyun?

- Zor se maarne par taar (ya dhol ki jhilli) **zyada door tak** hilti hai, yaani uska **amplitude bada** hota hai.
- Bade amplitude ka matlab **zyada energy**. Zyada energy wali vibration hawa ko zyada zor se dhakelti hai, aur hamare kaan ko zyada tez awaaz sunai deti hai.

> **Loudness mukhya roop se vibration ke amplitude par depend karti hai: amplitude jitna bada, awaaz utni tez.**

```
 Halki awaaz: chhota amplitude

     *****           *****           *****
   **-----***-----***-----***-----***-----***-----**
             *****           *****           *****

 Tez awaaz: bada amplitude (wahi frequency)

      ***             ***             ***
     *   *           *   *           *   *
    *     *         *     *         *     *
   *-------*-------*-------*-------*-------*-------*
            *     *         *     *         *     *
             *   *           *   *           *   *
              ***             ***             ***
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Dono waves ki wavelength (aur isliye frequency) barabar hai, par neeche wale ka amplitude bada hai: wo zyada tez awaaz hai.

### Loudness aur kin cheezon par depend karti hai?

1. **Amplitude:** sabse mukhya karan (upar dekha).
2. **Source se doori:** sound jaise-jaise door jaati hai, uski energy zyada area mein phailti jaati hai, to door baithe vyakti ko wahi awaaz dheemi sunai deti hai. Isiliye door ke dhol ki awaaz dheemi lagti hai.
3. **Vibrate hone wali surface ka area:** badi surface zyada hawa ko hilati hai. Isiliye chhote speaker se bada speaker aksar zyada tez awaaz deta hai, aur sitar ki taar ke neeche lagi lakdi ki body (jo vibrate karti hai) awaaz ko tez kar deti hai.
4. **Sunne wale ke kaan ki sensitivity:** kuch logon ke kaan zyada sensitive hote hain, kuch ke kam. Ek hi awaaz do logon ko alag tezi ki lag sakti hai.

### Loudness aur intensity mein farak

- **Intensity:** 1 second mein 1 m² area se guzarne wali sound ki energy. Ye ek **physical quantity** hai, jo instrument se naapi ja sakti hai. (Iska formula hum nahi padhenge: wo aage ki class mein aayega.)
- **Loudness:** is intensity ka hamare kaan par **ehsaas**. Ye insaan ke kaan par bhi depend karti hai.

Intensity badhne par loudness badhti hai, par dono ek hi cheez nahi hain: ek physical hai, ek hamara ehsaas.

### Loudness ka unit: decibel (dB)

Loudness **decibel (dB)** mein naapi jaati hai. Yahan sirf ye table dekhni hai: ye lagbhag (approximate) values hain, har jagah thodi alag ho sakti hain. (Decibel ke andar ka hisaab aage ki class mein aayega, aur iske asar Topic 7 mein dekhenge.)

| Awaaz | Lagbhag loudness (dB) |
|---|---|
| Sabse halki sunai dene wali awaaz | 0 dB |
| Fusfusahat (whisper) | 20 dB |
| Shaant kamra | 40 se 50 dB |
| Normal baat-cheet | 60 dB |
| Bhaari traffic | 80 dB |
| Tez factory ya power tool (drill) | 90 se 100 dB |
| Rock concert, ya kaan ke paas siren | 110 se 120 dB |
| Jet engine ke bahut paas (tez dard) | 140 dB |

```

## Part B: Pitch (awaaz ki oonchaai)

### Pitch kya hai?

Kuch awaazein **patli, oonchi (shrill)** lagti hain, jaise chidiya ki chahchahat ya seeti. Kuch awaazein **bhaari, neechi (flat, deep)** lagti hain, jaise sher ki dahaad. Is ehsaas ko **pitch** kehte hain.

### Pitch frequency par depend karti hai

> **Frequency jitni zyada, pitch utni oonchi (shriller). Frequency jitni kam, pitch utni neechi.**

Roz ki zindagi ke example:
- **Mosquito ki bhinbhinahat** oonchi pitch ki hai (uske pankh bahut tezi se hilte hain), aur **madhumakkhi (bee) ki gunjan** neechi pitch ki hai.
- **Chidiya ki chahchahat** oonchi, **sher ki dahaad** neechi.
- **Mahila ya bachche ki awaaz** aam taur par oonchi pitch ki hoti hai, aur **aadmi ki awaaz** neechi. Iska karan ye hai ki unke vocal cords (gale ke andar ki awaaz banane wali jhilliyan) alag tarah se vibrate karte hain: bachchon aur mahilaon ke vocal cords aam taur par chhote aur patle hote hain, to unki frequency zyada hoti hai.

Topic 3 ki guitar wali baat yaad karo: **patli taar ki frequency zyada hoti hai, to uski pitch oonchi**, aur **moti taar ki frequency kam, to pitch neechi**.

```
 Neechi pitch: kam frequency (lambi wavelength)

        *******                         *******
     ***       ***                   ***       ***
   **-------------***-------------***-------------**
                     ***       ***
                        *******

 Oonchi pitch: zyada frequency (chhoti wavelength)

      ***             ***             ***
    **   **         **   **         **   **
   *-------*-------*-------*-------*-------*-------*
            **   **         **   **         **   **
              ***             ***             ***
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Dono ka amplitude barabar hai (isliye loudness barabar), par neeche wale ki frequency double hai, to uski pitch oonchi hai.

### Taar ki pitch kin cheezon se badalti hai?

Guitar, sitar ya violin ki taar ki frequency teen cheezon se badalti hai (yahan sirf seedhi baat, formula nahi):

| Badlaav | Frequency | Pitch |
|---|---|---|
| Taar **chhoti** karo | badhti hai | oonchi |
| Taar **patli** lo | badhti hai | oonchi |
| Taar ko **zyada kaso (tight)** | badhti hai | oonchi |

Yaani **chhoti, patli aur kasi hui taar tezi se vibrate karti hai** aur oonchi pitch deti hai. Isiliye sitar ya guitar ki tuning karte waqt khoonti (peg) ghumakar taar ko kasa ya dheela kiya jaata hai.

### Pitch aur amplitude: do alag cheezein (tuning fork wala dikhawa)

Ek tuning fork lo jiski frequency 512 Hz hai. Pehle use halke se maaro, phir zor se maaro.

- Halke se maarne par amplitude **chhota**: awaaz **dheemi**.
- Zor se maarne par amplitude **bada**: awaaz **tez**.

Par dono baar fork 1 second mein 512 baar hi vibrate karta hai, yaani **frequency same: 512 Hz**. To dono awaazon ki **pitch bilkul same** hai. Tezi badli (amplitude se), sur nahi badla (frequency se).

> **Pitch frequency par depend karti hai, amplitude par nahi.**

### Sur (notes) ki baat: sirf naam aur ratio

Sangeet mein 8 sur ek **octave** banate hain. Hamare yahan inhe **Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni (Sa)** kehte hain, aur western music mein **Do Re Mi Fa Sol La Ti (Do)**. Ye har sur ki ek frequency hoti hai, aur sur ek ke baad ek **badhti pitch** ke hote hain. Hum music theory nahi padh rahe: bas ye dekhna hai ki sur frequency ki **ek seedhi list** hain. C major scale ke notes ki frequency ek doosre se **lagbhag** in ratio mein hoti hai:

| Sur (hindi / western) | Frequency ratio (lagbhag) | Frequency agar Sa = 262 Hz (lagbhag) |
|---|---|---|
| Sa / Do | 1 | 262 Hz |
| Re / Re | 9/8 | 295 Hz |
| Ga / Mi | 5/4 | 328 Hz |
| Ma / Fa | 4/3 | 349 Hz |
| Pa / Sol | 3/2 | 393 Hz |
| Dha / La | 5/3 | 437 Hz |
| Ni / Ti | 15/8 | 491 Hz |
| Sa (upar wala) / Do (upar wala) | 2 | 524 Hz |

Do sur yaad rakhne layak hain:

| Note | Frequency (lagbhag) |
|---|---|
| Middle C (beech ka Sa / Do) | 262 Hz |
| A (La): tuning ka standard | 440 Hz |

Upar ki table mein La 437 Hz aaya, jo 440 Hz ke bahut paas hai. Farak isliye hai ki ye ratio wali list aur aaj ki standard tuning thodi si alag hoti hain, isliye table ki saari frequencies **lagbhag** hain. Aur dhyan do: **upar wala Sa ka frequency neeche wale Sa se theek double (ratio 2)** hota hai: isi ko **octave** kehte hain. Isiliye ek hi sur neeche aur upar wale octave mein "same sur" jaisa lagta hai, bas ek oonchi pitch ka.

## Part C: Quality (timbre)

### Same loudness, same pitch, phir bhi alag

Studio ki kahani yaad karo. Bansuri aur violin ne **same note** (same frequency, to same pitch) bajaya, aur dono ki **loudness bhi same** thi. Phir bhi awaazein alag lagi. Is teesre gun ko **quality (timbre)** kehte hain.

> **Quality (timbre) wo characteristic hai jisse hum same loudness aur same pitch wali do awaazon mein farak pehchan lete hain.**

### Quality kahan se aati hai? Waveform se

Jab koi instrument ek note bajata hai, to wo sirf ek hi vibration nahi karta. Uski **mukhya vibration (fundamental)** ke saath-saath **kuch extra, kamzor vibrations (overtones)** bhi hote hain. Alag instrument ke overtones alag hote hain. Mukhya vibration aur overtones jud kar ek **naya wave shape (waveform)** banate hain. Waveform ka aakaar alag hone se awaaz ki quality alag ho jaati hai. (Overtones ki gehri padhai aage ki class mein: abhi bas ye idea.)

```

### Roz ki zindagi mein quality

- **Phone par dost ki awaaz pehchan lena:** aap bina naam bataye pehchan lete ho ki "ye Sparsh hai". Kyunki har insaan ki awaaz ki quality (waveform) alag hoti hai. Do log same sur mein, same tezi se bolein, to bhi unki awaaz alag lagti hai.
- **Piano aur sitar:** same note bajane par bhi alag.
- **Dhol, tabla aur harmonium:** teeno ko aankhein band karke pehchan lete hain.

### Musical sound aur noise

- **Musical sound:** sunne mein achhi lagti hai. Iski waveform **regular** hoti hai: ek ke baad ek **ek jaisi dohrati hui**. Jaise sitar, tabla, bansuri.
- **Noise:** isme **irregular, random vibrations** hote hain, koi tarteeb nahi. Aksar pareshan karti hai. Jaise hathode ki thok-thok, machine ka shor, bhaari traffic ki cheekh-pukaar.

(Noise aur uske nuksaan Topic 7 mein.)

### Teeno characteristics ek nazar mein

| | Loudness | Pitch | Quality (timbre) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kya hai? | Awaaz kitni tez ya dheemi | Awaaz kitni oonchi ya neechi | Awaaz ka "rang" ya "swad" |
| Depend karti hai | Mukhya roop se amplitude par | Frequency par | Waveform (overtones) par |
| Unit | dB (decibel) | Hz (frequency ka unit) | Koi unit nahi |
| Example | Dhol ko zor se bajao: loudness zyada | Patli taar: oonchi pitch | Bansuri vs violin: alag quality |

## Part E: Solved examples

**Example 1 (octave ka ratio):** Ek note 440 Hz ka hai aur uska octave 880 Hz ka. Dono ki frequency ka ratio nikalo.

- Ratio = 880 Hz ÷ 440 Hz = **2**

**Jaanch:** 440 Hz × 2 = 880 Hz ✅. Octave ka matlab hi double frequency hai.

**Example 2 (kiski pitch zyada?):** Do taaron ki frequency 256 Hz aur 320 Hz hai. Kiski pitch oonchi hai? Ratio aur farak bhi nikalo.

- Zyada frequency = oonchi pitch, to **320 Hz wali taar** ki pitch oonchi hai.
- Ratio = 320 Hz ÷ 256 Hz = **1.25** (yaani 5 : 4)
- Farak = 320 Hz − 256 Hz = **64 Hz**

**Jaanch:** 256 Hz × 1.25 = 320 Hz ✅. (Ye ratio 5/4 wahi hai jo Sa aur Ga ke beech ka tha.)

**Example 3 (Middle C ki wavelength):** Hawa mein v = 340 m/s. Middle C (262 Hz) ki wavelength kitni hai? Uske octave (524 Hz) ki wavelength kitni hai?

Formula (Topic 3): v = f × λ, to **λ = v ÷ f**

- 262 Hz: λ = 340 m/s ÷ 262 Hz = **1.298 m ≈ 1.30 m**
- 524 Hz: λ = 340 m/s ÷ 524 Hz = **0.649 m ≈ 0.65 m**

**Jaanch:** 1.298 m × 262 Hz = 340.1 m/s ≈ 340 m/s ✅. 0.649 m × 524 Hz = 340.1 m/s ≈ 340 m/s ✅. Aur 1.298 m ÷ 0.649 m = 2, yaani frequency double hui to wavelength aadhi. Speed (340 m/s) same rahi: pitch badalne se speed nahi badalti (Topic 4).

**Example 4 (amplitude double karne ka asar):** 440 Hz ka tuning fork halke se maara gaya. Phir usi fork ko aise maara ki amplitude double ho gaya. Pitch aur loudness par kya asar hua?

- Fork ki frequency uski banawat se tay hai: **440 Hz hi rahi**. To pitch **nahi badli**.
- Amplitude bada hua, to vibration mein zyada energy aayi, to awaaz **zyada tez (louder)** ho gayi.

**Jaanch:** Pitch frequency par depend karti hai (440 Hz → 440 Hz, koi badlaav nahi) ✅. Loudness amplitude par depend karti hai (amplitude double → zyada loud) ✅.

**Example 5 (table padhna aur order karna):** Niche di gayi awaazon ko kam se zyada loudness ke order mein likho: (a) shaant kamra, (b) rock concert, (c) fusfusahat, (d) bhaari traffic, (e) normal baat-cheet.

Table se lagbhag values: (a) 40 se 50 dB (lo 45 dB), (b) 110 se 120 dB (lo 115 dB), (c) 20 dB, (d) 80 dB, (e) 60 dB.

Order (kam se zyada): fusfusahat 20 dB, shaant kamra 45 dB, normal baat-cheet 60 dB, bhaari traffic 80 dB, rock concert 115 dB. Yaani **(c), (a), (e), (d), (b)**.

**Jaanch:** 20 < 45 < 60 < 80 < 115 ✅. Aur sabse zyada wali (b) 120 dB ke aas-paas hai, jo kaan ko nuksaan pahuncha sakti hai.

**Example 6 (vibrations ki ginti):** 512 Hz ka tuning fork 3 second mein kitni baar vibrate karega?

- 512 Hz = 512 vibrations per second
- Vibrations = frequency × time = 512 vibrations/s × 3 s = **1536 vibrations**

**Jaanch:** 1536 ÷ 3 s = 512 per second = 512 Hz ✅. Ek vibration ka time period T = 1 ÷ 512 Hz = **0.00195 s** (lagbhag 0.002 s), aur 1536 × 0.00195 s = 3.0 s ✅.

**Example 7 (kaunsi characteristic badli?):** Niche ki har situation mein batao ki loudness, pitch ya quality mein se kya badla.

| # | Situation | Kya badla? | Kyun? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Taar ko pehle se zyada zor se pluck karna | Loudness | Amplitude bada hua |
| 2 | Taar ko chhota kar dena | Pitch (oonchi hui) | Chhoti taar ki frequency zyada |
| 3 | Same note piano aur sitar par | Quality | Waveform alag |
| 4 | Radio ka volume knob ghumana | Loudness | Amplitude (aur intensity) badli |
| 5 | Aadmi ki awaaz vs mahila ki awaaz | Pitch (aam taur par) | Mahila ki frequency zyada |
| 6 | Fusfusahat (whisper) | Loudness (bahut kam) | Amplitude bahut chhota |
| 7 | Door se aati dhol ki awaaz | Loudness (kam) | Door jaane par energy phailti hai. Pitch wahi rehti hai |
| 8 | Ek hi gaana do alag singers se | Quality | Sur wahi, par waveform alag |
| 9 | Taar ko kasna (tight karna) | Pitch (oonchi hui) | Frequency badhi |
| 10 | Bansuri vs seeti (whistle), same note aur same loudness par | Quality | Waveform alag |

**Jaanch:** Row 2: taar chhoti karne par frequency badhti hai, aur zyada frequency ka matlab oonchi pitch ✅. Row 10: frequency same hai (to pitch same) aur loudness bhi same, to farak sirf waveform ka bacha, yaani quality ✅.

**Example 8 (cheezein dhyan se):** Do awaazein hain: A ki frequency 300 Hz aur amplitude 2 cm, B ki frequency 600 Hz aur amplitude 1 cm. Kiski pitch oonchi hai? Loudness kiski zyada hogi?

- Pitch: 600 Hz > 300 Hz, to **B ki pitch oonchi**.
- Loudness: amplitude zyada wali, to **A zyada loud** (baaki cheezein jaise doori, same maan kar).

**Jaanch:** Pitch ka sawal frequency se tay hota hai (B), loudness ka amplitude se (A). Dono alag cheezein hain ✅.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Loudness aur pitch ko ek maan lena**

"Tez awaaz matlab oonchi pitch." Galat. Dhol ki awaaz bahut tez ho sakti hai par uski pitch neechi hoti hai. Mosquito ki awaaz bahut dheemi hoti hai par uski pitch oonchi. Loudness amplitude se, pitch frequency se.

**Galti 2: "Tez awaaz ki frequency zyada hoti hai"**

Nahi. Fork ko zor se maaro to awaaz tez hoti hai, par frequency wahi 512 Hz rehti hai (Part B). Frequency tab badalti hai jab fork (ya taar) ka size, mota-patla pan ya kasaav badle.

**Galti 3: Pitch ko speed se jodna**

"Oonchi pitch ki awaaz tez chalti hai." Galat. Hawa mein sound ki speed lagbhag 340 m/s hi rehti hai (Topic 4). v = f λ mein f badhne par λ ghat jaata hai, v wahi rehta hai (Example 3).

**Galti 4: Quality ka matlab "music achha hai"**

Quality yahan **timbre** hai: awaaz ka "rang". Ye achhi ya buri nahi hoti. Har instrument aur har insaan ki apni quality hoti hai.

**Galti 5: Decibel ko pitch ka unit samajhna**

dB **loudness** ka unit hai. Pitch ya frequency Hz mein naapi jaati hai.

**Galti 6: Amplitude badhne se pitch badhti hai, aisa sochna**

Amplitude badhane par sirf **loudness** badhti hai. Pitch frequency par depend karti hai aur wahi rehti hai (Example 4).

## Quick Recap

- Sound ki teen characteristics: **loudness, pitch, quality**.
- **Loudness** = awaaz ki tezi ka ehsaas. Mukhya roop se **amplitude** par depend karti hai (bada amplitude = zyada energy = zyada tez). Doori badhne par kam hoti hai (energy phailti hai); surface ke area aur kaan ki sensitivity par bhi depend karti hai.
- Loudness ka unit **decibel (dB)**: 0 dB sabse halki, 20 dB whisper, 60 dB baat-cheet, 80 dB traffic, 110 se 120 dB concert, 140 dB jet engine ke paas (sab approximate). Lagbhag 120 dB se kaan mein dard shuru ho sakta hai, aur aisi awaaz kaan ko nuksaan pahuncha sakti hai.
- **Intensity** physical quantity hai (energy per second per unit area); **loudness** uska ehsaas hai.
- **Pitch** = oonchi ya neechi hone ka ehsaas. **Frequency** par depend karti hai: zyada frequency = oonchi pitch.
- Taar jitni **chhoti, patli, aur kasi hui**, utni oonchi pitch.
- Pitch amplitude par depend nahi karti: tuning fork ko halke ya zor se maaro, pitch same.
- Middle C lagbhag 262 Hz, A lagbhag 440 Hz. **Octave** = double frequency (ratio 2).
- **Quality (timbre)** = same loudness aur same pitch wali do awaazon mein farak karne wala gun. Ye **waveform** (overtones) par depend karti hai.
- Musical sound = regular waveform; noise = irregular, random vibrations.
- Studio ki kahani: bansuri aur violin ka note aur loudness same, par quality alag.

## Practice Questions

1. Name the three characteristics of a musical sound. For each, write the physical quantity (amplitude, frequency or waveform) on which it mainly depends.

2. A tuning fork of frequency 480 Hz is struck. How many vibrations does it make in 5 seconds?

3. Two notes have frequencies 262 Hz and 524 Hz. (a) Find the ratio of their frequencies. (b) Which note has the higher pitch? (c) What do we call the interval between them?

4. Find the wavelength in air of a note of frequency 320 Hz. (Speed of sound in air = 340 m/s.)

5. A sound wave in air has a wavelength of 0.4 m. Find its frequency. (Speed of sound = 340 m/s.)

6. A drummer hits a drum first gently and then hard. (a) What changes: loudness, pitch or quality? (b) What stays the same? Give a reason.

7. The sounds of a siren near the ear, a whisper, a normal conversation and heavy traffic have approximate loudness values of 115 dB, 20 dB, 60 dB and 80 dB (in some order). Arrange the four sounds from the softest to the loudest.

8. Which has a higher pitch: a mosquito's whine or a bee's hum? A man's voice or a child's voice? Give a reason in one line.

9. State what happens to the pitch of a guitar string if it is (a) made shorter, (b) tightened, (c) replaced with a thicker string of the same length and tension.

10. Two instruments play the same note with the same loudness, yet you can tell them apart. Which characteristic helps you? What causes it?

11. State True or False:
    (a) A loud sound always has a high pitch.
    (b) Doubling the amplitude doubles the frequency.
    (c) Decibel is a unit of loudness.
    (d) The speed of sound in air is higher for a high-pitched sound than for a low-pitched one.
    (e) Noise has regular, repeating vibrations.

12. The middle C is about 262 Hz. Using the ratio 3/2, find the approximate frequency of the note Pa (Sol) in the same octave.

13. A sound becomes softer as you walk away from the source. Which characteristic changes and why does this happen? Does the pitch change?

14. Identify the characteristic that changes in each case: (a) turning the volume knob of a radio down, (b) tightening a violin string, (c) a sitar and a tabla playing the same pitch.

### Answers

1. **Loudness** mukhya roop se **amplitude** par, **pitch** **frequency** par, aur **quality (timbre)** **waveform** (overtones) par depend karti hai.

2. Vibrations = f × t = 480 vibrations/s × 5 s = **2400 vibrations**.
   Jaanch: 2400 ÷ 5 s = 480 Hz ✅

3. (a) Ratio = 524 Hz ÷ 262 Hz = **2** (ya 1 : 2). (b) **524 Hz wale** note ki pitch zyada hai. (c) Is interval ko **octave** kehte hain.
   Jaanch: 262 Hz × 2 = 524 Hz ✅

4. λ = v ÷ f = 340 m/s ÷ 320 Hz = **1.06 m** (lagbhag; 1.0625 m).
   Jaanch: 1.0625 m × 320 Hz = 340 m/s ✅

5. f = v ÷ λ = 340 m/s ÷ 0.4 m = **850 Hz**.
   Jaanch: 0.4 m × 850 Hz = 340 m/s ✅

6. (a) **Loudness** badli: zor se maarne par amplitude bada, to awaaz tez hui. (b) **Pitch** wahi rahi, kyunki drum ki jhilli ki frequency uski banawat aur kasaav se tay hoti hai, amplitude se nahi. Quality bhi lagbhag same rahegi (drum wahi hai).

7. Softest se loudest: **whisper (20 dB), normal conversation (60 dB), heavy traffic (80 dB), siren near the ear (115 dB)**.
   Jaanch: 20 < 60 < 80 < 115 ✅

8. **Mosquito** ki bhinbhinahat ki pitch zyada hai (uske par bahut tezi se hilte hain, to frequency zyada). **Bachche** ki awaaz ki pitch aam taur par aadmi ki awaaz se zyada hoti hai (chhote aur patle vocal cords, zyada frequency).

9. (a) Chhoti karne par frequency badhti hai, to pitch **oonchi**. (b) Kasne par frequency badhti hai, to pitch **oonchi**. (c) Moti taar ki frequency kam hoti hai, to pitch **neechi**.

10. **Quality (timbre)** se. Iska karan hai **waveform ka alag aakaar**, jo instrument ke extra, kamzor vibrations (overtones) se banta hai.

11. (a) **False** ❌. Loudness amplitude se aur pitch frequency se tay hoti hai: dhol tez hota hai par uski pitch neechi hoti hai.
    (b) **False** ❌. Amplitude badhne par sirf loudness badhti hai, frequency nahi badalti.
    (c) **True** ✅.
    (d) **False** ❌. Hawa mein dono ki speed lagbhag 340 m/s hi hoti hai: speed pitch par depend nahi karti.
    (e) **False** ❌. Noise mein irregular, random vibrations hote hain; regular vibrations musical sound mein hote hain.

12. f(Pa) = 262 Hz × 3/2 = 262 × 1.5 = **393 Hz** (lagbhag).
    Jaanch: 393 ÷ 262 = 1.5 ✅

13. **Loudness** kam hoti hai, kyunki door jaane par sound ki energy zyada area mein phail jaati hai, to kaan ke paas kam energy pahunchti hai. **Pitch nahi badalti**, kyunki frequency wahi rehti hai.

14. (a) **Loudness** (amplitude kam hua). (b) **Pitch** (frequency badhi, to oonchi). (c) **Quality (timbre)**: pitch wahi, par waveform alag.
