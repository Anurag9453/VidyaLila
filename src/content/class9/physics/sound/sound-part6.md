# Topic 6: Audible Range, Ultrasound, Infrasound aur SONAR

Pichhle topic mein humne sound ki teen characteristics dekhi: **loudness** (amplitude se), **pitch** (frequency se) aur **quality** (waveform se). Us topic ke ant mein ek sawal chhoda tha: kuch awaazein itni oonchi ya itni neechi hoti hain ki hum sun hi nahi paate. Yaani sound ki pitch ki bhi ek **seema** hoti hai. Is topic mein hum dekhenge ki hamare kaan kis frequency se kis frequency tak sunte hain, uske upar aur neeche kaun si awaazein hoti hain, aur unse kaise kaam liye jaate hain: bachche ki photo lena, samundar ki gehrai naapna, aur andhere mein udte chamgadad ka rasta dhoondhna.

**Is poore topic mein: hawa mein sound ki speed 340 m/s, aur samundar ke paani mein lagbhag 1500 m/s** (dono approximate values hain).

Hospital ke sonography room mein Dr. Sandhya apni machine ke saamne baithi thi. Bed par Jivika ki mummy leti thi. Jivika, jo ek pal ke liye mummy ke saath andar aa gayi thi, dar ke maare ek kone mein khadi thi.

## Part A: Audible range: hum kitna sunte hain?

### Hamare kaan ki seema

Frequency ka matlab tha: ek second mein kitne vibrations (Topic 3). Insaani kaan har frequency ki sound nahi sun sakta. Ek **swasth insaan ka kaan lagbhag 20 Hz se 20000 Hz (20 kHz) tak** ki sound sun sakta hai. Ise **audible range** kehte hain.

- **20 Hz se kam** frequency ki sound: **infrasound** (infrasonic). Hum ise nahi sun paate.
- **20 Hz se 20 kHz**: **audible sound**, jo hum sunte hain.
- **20 kHz se zyada** frequency ki sound: **ultrasound** (ultrasonic). Ise bhi hum nahi sun paate.

Dhyan do: infrasound aur ultrasound dono **sound hi hain**. Wahi vibrations, wahi medium mein chalne wali waves. Bas unki frequency hamare kaan ke sunne ke daayre se bahar hai. Kisi cheez ka hamein sunai na dena use "sound nahi" nahi bana deta.

### Umar ke saath badalta hai

Ye 20 Hz se 20 kHz ek aam, swasth, jawan insaan (aur bachchon) ke liye hai. **Umar badhne par ooncha frequency sunne ki shakti kam hoti jati hai.** Bachche 20 kHz tak sun lete hain, par bade log aksar 15 kHz ya usse kam tak hi sun pate hain. Isi liye kabhi-kabhi bachche ek bahut tez, patli si awaaz sun lete hain jise bade log sun hi nahi paate.

### Jaanwaron ki range alag hoti hai

Har prani ki sunne ki range alag hoti hai. Neeche di gayi values **lagbhag (approximate)** hain, alag-alag source mein thodi alag milti hain:

| Prani | Lagbhag kitni ooncha frequency sun sakta hai |
|---|---|
| Insaan | lagbhag 20 kHz tak |
| Kutta (dog) | lagbhag 45 kHz tak |
| Billi (cat) | lagbhag 64 kHz tak |
| Chamgadad (bat) | lagbhag 100 se 110 kHz tak |
| Dolphin | lagbhag 100 se 150 kHz tak |

Isiliye ek **dog whistle** jo insaan ko lagbhag sunai nahi deti, kutta use aaram se sun leta hai: wo ultrasound hai. Doosri taraf **haathi (elephant) aur whale** infrasound (bahut neechi frequency) sunte aur nikalte hain.

```
 Frequency ki number line (log jaisi, andaaza, scale nahi)

 Infrasound            Audible sound               Ultrasound
 (20 Hz se kam)        (20 Hz se 20 kHz)           (20 kHz se zyada)
 ◄────────────────────┬───────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────►
                     20 Hz                       20 kHz
 haathi, whale        insaan (20 Hz - 20 kHz)      kutta  : lagbhag 45 kHz tak
 bhukamp ki lehrein   bachche zyada upar tak       billi  : lagbhag 64 kHz tak
 (ye sunai nahi dete) bade log kam upar tak        chamgadad : lagbhag 100 kHz tak
                                                   dolphin: lagbhag 100 - 150 kHz
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Frequency ki number line: 20 Hz se neeche infrasound, 20 Hz se 20 kHz tak audible range, aur 20 kHz se upar ultrasound. Prani ki range ke aankde approximate hain.

## Part B: Infrasound: bahut neechi frequency

Infrasound ki frequency 20 Hz se kam hoti hai. Iske kuch source:

- **Bhukamp (earthquake)** aur **volcano ka visfot**: zameen ke andar ki halchal infrasound paida karti hai.
- **Haathi aur whale**: ye prani infrasound se bahut door baithe saathiyon ko "bulate" hain. Haathi kai kilometre door tak ek doosre se baat kar lete hain, aur whale to samundar mein aur bhi door tak.
- **Badi machines** (bade engine, turbine, factories ki heavy machinery) aur **bijli ki garaj (thunder)** bhi infrasound nikalte hain.

**Ek "kaha jaata hai" wali baat:** kuch logon ka kehna hai ki bhukamp ke **main jhatke se pehle** kuch jaanwar bechain ho jaate hain, kyunki wo shayad us infrasound ko mehsoos kar lete hain. Par ye sirf kaha jaata hai: ye pakka sabit nahi hua hai, isliye ise bhukamp ki bhavishyavani nahi maana ja sakta.

### Infrasound door tak kyun jaata hai?

Neechi frequency ka matlab **lambi wavelength**. v = f λ (Topic 3) se: λ = v ÷ f. Hawa mein v = 340 m/s, to 10 Hz ki infrasound ki wavelength:

> λ = 340 m/s ÷ 10 Hz = **34 m**

Itni lambi wavelength wali waves ko aam rukawatein (imaarat, ped, chhoti pahadiyaan) zyada rok nahi paatin: wo unke aas-paas se mud kar aage badh jaati hain, aur unki energy dheere-dheere hi kam hoti hai. Isi karan infrasound **bahut door tak** safar kar leta hai. (Ye "mud kar" wali baat bas ek jhalak hai: aage ki class mein diffraction mein padhoge.)

## Part C: Ultrasound: bahut oonchi frequency

20 kHz se upar ki sound ultrasound hai. Aam taur par medical aur industry mein 1 MHz se 10 MHz tak ke ultrasound kaam aate hain. Iske **gun (properties)**:

1. **High frequency, chhoti wavelength.** 40 kHz ke ultrasound ki hawa mein wavelength: λ = 340 m/s ÷ 40000 Hz = 0.0085 m = **8.5 mm**.
2. **Seedhi line mein, ek patli beam ki tarah chalta hai.** Chhoti wavelength ki wajah se ye idhar-udhar faila nahi, torch ki patli roshni ki tarah jaata hai. Aap ise ek disha mein bhej sakte ho.
3. **Reflect bahut achhe se hota hai.** Chhoti cheezein bhi ise wapas bhej deti hain (echo banta hai). Jab tak medium ek sa rehta hai, wave seedhi chalti hai; do alag medium (jaise muscle aur haddi, ya paani aur sea-bed) ki seema par wave ka kuch hissa wapas lautta hai.
4. **Zyada energy le jaa sakta hai** (isliye tez ultrasound se cheezein hil bhi sakti hain).
5. **Kaise banta hai?** Ek khaas kism ke **crystal** (quartz) se, jo bijli lagane par bahut tezi se kaampta hai. Wahi kaampna ultrasound hai. (Is crystal ki physics aage ki class mein: abhi bas "ek kism ka crystal jo bijli se kaampta hai" yaad rakhna.)

### Ek zaroori baat: speed wahi, frequency alag

**Ultrasound hawa mein audible sound se tez nahi chalta.** Kisi ek medium mein sound ki speed lagbhag wahi rehti hai: hawa mein dono lagbhag 340 m/s se chalte hain. Farak sirf frequency (aur isliye wavelength) ka hai. v = f λ mein v wahi rehta hai, f bada to λ chhota.

## Part D: Ultrasound ke upyog (uses)

### 1. Medical: sharir ke andar jhaankna

Ye wahi hai jo Jivika ne screen par dekha. **Ultrasound scan (sonography)** se doctor:
- pet mein pal raha **bachcha (baby in the womb)**,
- **dil (heart)** ki halchal (isse **echocardiography** kehte hain),
- **liver** aur **kidney** jaise ang

dekh sakte hain.

Kaise? Probe sharir mein ultrasound ki chhoti-chhoti pulse bhejti hai. Ye pulse tissue se guzarti hai. Jahan do alag tissues ki seema aati hai (jaise kidney aur uske aas-paas ka hissa), wahan pulse ka thoda hissa **echo banke wapas** aa jaata hai. Probe ye echo pakadti hai, aur machine har echo ke aane ka time napti hai, aur doori nikalti hai (wahi d = v t / 2 jo Topic 4 mein tha). Ye saari echoes mil kar screen par ek grey tasveer ban jaati hain.

Ye **surakshit** hai: ultrasound mein **X-ray jaisi radiation nahi** hoti (X-ray ek tarah ki high-energy radiation hai jisse zyada baar kaam lena theek nahi). Ultrasound sirf sound waves hain. Isliye pregnancy mein bachche ki scan ke liye bhi iska upyog hota hai.

Medical mein aur bhi: **ultrasonic physiotherapy** (dard wali jagah par ultrasound se sekai) aur **kidney stone** ko todna (iski technique **lithotripsy** kehlati hai, jisme tez ultrasound jaisi waves stone ko chhote tukdon mein tod deti hain taaki wo sharir se nikal jaye).

### 2. Industrial upyog

- **Nazuk cheezon ki safai.** Jewellery, ghadi ke chhote purze aur electronic circuit boards ko ek tank mein rakha jaata hai jisme saaf karne wala liquid hota hai. Tank mein ultrasound bheja jaata hai. Ultrasound ke tez vibrations cheez ke kone-konon mein jami **dhool aur mail ko hila kar chhuda dete hain**, bina cheez ko ghisse.
- **Dhaatu ke blocks mein crack aur kamiyaan dhoondhna.** Ek **ultrasonic flaw detector** dhatu ke block mein ek pulse bhejta hai. Agar block andar se thos aur theek hai, to pulse sirf doosre sire se echo ke roop mein wapas aati hai. Par agar andar crack ya hawa ka bubble hai, to wahan se **ek extra echo** aa jaata hai. Echo ke time se pata chalta hai ki crack kitni gehrai par hai. (Is baat se hum ek example karenge.)

### 3. SONAR: paani ke neeche doori naapna

**SONAR** ka poora naam hai **SOund Navigation And Ranging**. Isme ek **transmitter** ultrasound ki pulse paani mein bhejta hai. Pulse paani mein chalti hai, kisi cheez (sea-bed, submarine, machhliyon ka jhund, jahaz) se takrati hai, aur **echo banke wapas** aati hai. Ek **detector** use pakadta hai. Pulse bhejne aur echo aane ke beech ka time napa jaata hai.

Sound ki speed **samundar ke paani mein lagbhag 1500 m/s** hai (hawa wali 340 m/s nahi!). Isliye doori:

> **d = v t / 2**   (v = 1500 m/s, t = bhejne se echo milne tak ka time)

Aadha kyun? Kyunki t mein pulse **jaane aur wapas aane**, dono ka time hai: doori do baar tay ki gayi. (Bilkul Topic 4 ke echo wale rule jaisa.)

```
   Jahaz (SONAR)
   ┌──────────┐ 
   │ T    D   │   T = transmitter (pulse bheje)
   └──┬────┬──┘   D = detector (echo pakde)
      │ ↓  ↑ │
      │ ↓  ↑ │    pulse neeche jaati hai (↓)
      │ ↓  ↑ │    aur echo ban kar upar aati hai (↑)
  ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~ ~  samundar ka paani  (v = 1500 m/s)
      │ ↓  ↑ │
      │ ↓  ↑ │         d = v t / 2
  ════╧══════╧═════════════════   sea-bed ya submarine
```

> **Figure 6.2** – SONAR: transmitter se pulse neeche jaati hai, sea-bed se takra kar echo ban kar detector tak wapas aati hai. Total time t mein jaana aur aana dono shamil hai, isliye doori d = v t / 2.

SONAR se kya-kya pata chalta hai:
- samundar ki **gehrai (depth)**,
- **sea-bed ki bantar (shape)**: kahan ghaati aur kahan pahadi hai,
- **submarine**, **dooba hua jahaz (shipwreck)** aur **machhliyon ke jhund (shoal)** ki jagah.

**Audible sound ki jagah ultrasound kyun?**
- Ultrasound ki **patli beam** hoti hai, isliye wo sahi disha mein jaati hai aur idhar-udhar nahi failti.
- Uski **wavelength chhoti** hoti hai, isliye wo **chhoti cheezon** se bhi achhe se reflect hoti hai.
- Ye **insaanon ko pareshaan nahi karta**: jahaz par aur samundar ke kinare tez awaaz sunai nahi deti.

**Radar aur SONAR alag hain:** radar **radio waves** (ek kism ki electromagnetic wave, sound nahi) use karta hai aur hawa mein kaam karta hai. SONAR **sound (ultrasound)** use karta hai aur paani mein kaam karta hai.

### 4. Jaanwar: nature ka apna SONAR

**Chamgadad (bat)** raat ko andhere mein udta hai, par kabhi deewar se nahi takraata aur keede (insects) pakad leta hai. Wo apne munh se **ultrasound ki chhoti squeaks** (lagbhag 50 se 100 kHz) nikalta hai. Ye squeaks keede ya deewar se takra kar echo banti hain, aur bat apne kaanon se echo sun leta hai. Echo ke time se use pata chalta hai ki cheez kitni door hai. Is tareeke ko **echolocation** kehte hain. **Dolphin** aur **porpoise** bhi paani ke andar isi tarah apna raasta dhoondhte aur shikaar pakadte hain.

## Part E: Jivika ka sawal solve karte hain

Dr. Sandhya ne Jivika ko samjhaya, "Dekho beta. Is probe se lagbhag **3 MHz** ka ultrasound nikalta hai, yaani ek second mein 3 × 10⁶ vibrations. Hamare kaan sirf 20 kHz tak sunte hain, isliye hamein ye awaaz sunai nahi deti. Par **sunai na dena aur sound na hona** ek baat nahi hai. Sound hai, aur wo sharir ke andar jaa rahi hai."

"Sharir ke andar ultrasound ki speed lagbhag **1540 m/s** hoti hai (sharir ka tissue ek tarah ka paani-jaisa hota hai)." Isliye uski wavelength:

> λ = v ÷ f = 1540 m/s ÷ (3 × 10⁶ Hz) = 0.000513 m ≈ **0.5 mm**

"Itni chhoti wavelength ki wajah se ye sharir ke andar ki **chhoti-chhoti cheezon** se bhi reflect ho jaati hai, aur ek patli beam ki tarah chalti hai. Jahan do tissues ki seema aati hai, wahan se echo wapas aati hai. Probe us echo ko pakadti hai. Machine har echo ke aane ka time napti hai. Jaise agar echo 65 µs (microsecond) baad aaye to:"

> d = v t / 2 = 1540 m/s × 65 × 10⁻⁶ s ÷ 2 = 0.1001 m ÷ 2 ≈ 0.05 m = **5 cm**

"Yaani wo seema probe se lagbhag 5 cm andar hai. Machine hazaaron echoes ke liye ye hisaab karti hai, aur har seema ko screen par uski sahi jagah par ek chamakta dhabba bana deti hai. Aur jab probe ghumti hai, to nayi echoes se tasveer badalti rehti hai: isiliye tasveer hilti hui dikhti hai."

> **Jaanch:** 1540 × 65 × 10⁻⁶ = 0.1001 m; aadha karne par 0.05005 m ≈ 5 cm ✅

Jivika ne kaha, "Matlab hum us awaaz ko sun nahi sakte, par uske **echoes se tasveer bana sakte hain**. Bilkul chamgadad jaisa!"

Dr. Sandhya muskuraayi, "Bilkul. Aur gel isliye lagaya, kyunki probe aur skin ke beech agar hawa ki patli parat rahe to ultrasound andar jaa hi nahi paayega. Gel ultrasound ke liye ek seedha raasta bana deta hai."

```
        probe (ek patli ultrasound beam bhejti hai)
          │
          │ ↓ pulse           ↑ echo
          │
   ────── skin (upar gel) ──────────────
          │ ↓                 ↑ (pehli seema se echo)
      muscle
   ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄│┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄ pehli seema
          │ ↓                 ↑ (doosri seema se echo)
      kidney
   ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄│┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄ doosri seema
```

> **Figure 6.3** – Sonography: har seema se aayi echo ka time alag hota hai. Echo ke time se doori nikalte hain aur machine un sab ko jod kar screen par tasveer banati hai.

## Part F: Solved examples

Is part mein: hawa mein v = 340 m/s, samundar mein v = 1500 m/s (jab tak kuch aur na diya ho).

**Example 1 (classify karo):** Neeche di gayi frequencies ko infrasound, audible ya ultrasound batao: 10 Hz, 50 Hz, 15 kHz, 25 kHz, 100 kHz.

Audible range: 20 Hz se 20 kHz (= 20000 Hz).

- 10 Hz: 10 Hz < 20 Hz, to **infrasound**.
- 50 Hz: 20 Hz < 50 Hz < 20000 Hz, to **audible**.
- 15 kHz = 15000 Hz: 20 Hz < 15000 Hz < 20000 Hz, to **audible**.
- 25 kHz = 25000 Hz: 25000 Hz > 20000 Hz, to **ultrasound**.
- 100 kHz = 100000 Hz: 100000 Hz > 20000 Hz, to **ultrasound**.

**Jaanch:** Saari frequencies ko Hz mein badla aur 20 Hz aur 20000 Hz se milaya: 10 (neeche), 50 (beech mein), 15000 (beech mein), 25000 (upar), 100000 (upar) ✅

**Example 2 (wavelength, hawa mein):** 20 Hz, 20 kHz, 40 kHz aur 10 Hz ki sound ki wavelength nikalo. λ = v ÷ f.

- 20 Hz: λ = 340 m/s ÷ 20 Hz = **17 m**
- 20 kHz = 20000 Hz: λ = 340 m/s ÷ 20000 Hz = 0.017 m = **17 mm**
- 40 kHz = 40000 Hz: λ = 340 m/s ÷ 40000 Hz = 0.0085 m = **8.5 mm**
- 10 Hz: λ = 340 m/s ÷ 10 Hz = **34 m**

**Jaanch:** f × λ wapas: 20 Hz × 17 m = 340 m/s ✅; 20000 Hz × 0.017 m = 340 m/s ✅; 40000 Hz × 0.0085 m = 340 m/s ✅; 10 Hz × 34 m = 340 m/s ✅. Ye bhi dekho: infrasound ki wavelength metre mein, ultrasound ki millimetre mein. Frequency jitni zyada, wavelength utni chhoti.

**Example 3 (samundar ki gehrai):** SONAR se ek jahaz neeche ultrasound bhejta hai. Echo (a) 1.2 s, (b) 4 s, (c) 0.5 s baad milta hai. Samundar ki gehrai?

d = v t / 2, v = 1500 m/s.

- (a) d = 1500 m/s × 1.2 s ÷ 2 = 1800 m ÷ 2 = **900 m**
- (b) d = 1500 m/s × 4 s ÷ 2 = 6000 m ÷ 2 = **3000 m**
- (c) d = 1500 m/s × 0.5 s ÷ 2 = 750 m ÷ 2 = **375 m**

**Jaanch:** Pulse ne jaane aur aane mein 2d chala: (a) 2 × 900 m = 1800 m = 1500 m/s × 1.2 s ✅; (b) 2 × 3000 = 6000 = 1500 × 4 ✅; (c) 2 × 375 = 750 = 1500 × 0.5 ✅

**Example 4 (submarine tak echo ka time):** Ek submarine SONAR se 4.5 km door hai. Echo kitni der baad milega?

- d = 4.5 km = 4.5 × 1000 = 4500 m
- t = 2d ÷ v = 2 × 4500 m ÷ 1500 m/s = 9000 m ÷ 1500 m/s = **6 s**

**Jaanch:** d = v t / 2 = 1500 m/s × 6 s ÷ 2 = 9000 m ÷ 2 = 4500 m = 4.5 km ✅

**Example 5 (machhliyon ka jhund):** Echo 0.2 s baad aata hai. Jhund kitni door hai?

- d = v t / 2 = 1500 m/s × 0.2 s ÷ 2 = 300 m ÷ 2 = **150 m**

**Jaanch:** 2 × 150 m = 300 m = 1500 m/s × 0.2 s ✅

**Example 6 (chamgadad aur keeda):** Ek bat ka ultrasound ek keede tak jaa kar wapas aata hai. Keeda 3.4 m door hai, hawa mein v = 340 m/s. Kitni der lagi?

- Total doori = 2 × 3.4 m = 6.8 m
- t = 6.8 m ÷ 340 m/s = **0.02 s** (= 20 ms)

**Jaanch:** d = v t / 2 = 340 m/s × 0.02 s ÷ 2 = 6.8 m ÷ 2 = 3.4 m ✅

**Example 7 (bat ki squeak ki wavelength):** Bat ki squeak ki frequency 50 kHz hai. Hawa mein uski wavelength?

- f = 50 kHz = 50000 Hz
- λ = v ÷ f = 340 m/s ÷ 50000 Hz = 0.0068 m = **6.8 mm**

**Jaanch:** 50000 Hz × 0.0068 m = 340 m/s ✅. Itni chhoti wavelength se keeda (jo kuch mm ka hota hai) bhi achhe se echo deta hai.

**Example 8 (ek second mein kitne vibrations):** 2 MHz ke ultrasound mein 1 s mein kitne vibrations hote hain? Ek vibration ka time kitna hai?

- 2 MHz = 2 × 10⁶ Hz, yaani 1 s mein **2 × 10⁶** (20 lakh) vibrations.
- T = 1 ÷ f = 1 ÷ (2 × 10⁶ Hz) = 5 × 10⁻⁷ s = 0.5 µs

**Jaanch:** f × T = 2 × 10⁶ Hz × 5 × 10⁻⁷ s = 1 ✅

**Example 9 (dhatu mein crack):** Ek metal block mein ultrasound ki speed 5000 m/s hai. Flaw detector ko sire se extra echo 20 µs baad milta hai. Crack kitni gehrai par hai?

- t = 20 µs = 20 × 10⁻⁶ s
- d = v t / 2 = 5000 m/s × 20 × 10⁻⁶ s ÷ 2 = 0.1 m ÷ 2 = **0.05 m = 5 cm**

**Jaanch:** 2 × 0.05 m = 0.1 m = 5000 m/s × 20 × 10⁻⁶ s = 0.1 m ✅

**Example 10 (samundar mein speed napna):** Ek jahaz neeche 750 m door sea-bed tak ultrasound bhejta hai, aur echo 1 s baad aata hai. Samundar mein sound ki speed kitni hai?

- Total doori = 2 × 750 m = 1500 m
- v = (2d) ÷ t = 1500 m ÷ 1 s = **1500 m/s**

**Jaanch:** d = v t / 2 = 1500 m/s × 1 s ÷ 2 = 750 m ✅. Ye hawa ki speed (340 m/s) se lagbhag 4.4 guna zyada hai: 1500 ÷ 340 ≈ 4.4.

**Example 11 (Jivika wali scan):** Sonography ki probe 3 MHz ka ultrasound bhejti hai. Tissue mein v = 1540 m/s. Wavelength?

- f = 3 MHz = 3 × 10⁶ Hz
- λ = v ÷ f = 1540 m/s ÷ (3 × 10⁶ Hz) = 5.13 × 10⁻⁴ m ≈ 0.5 mm

**Jaanch:** 3 × 10⁶ Hz × 5.13 × 10⁻⁴ m = 1539 m/s ≈ 1540 m/s ✅

## Part G: Surakshit upyog

Ultrasound aur infrasound dono ka **bahut zyada tez (high intensity)** hona nuksaan kar sakta hai. Bahut tez ultrasound tissue ko garm kar ya tod sakta hai (isi se lithotripsy mein stone tootta hai), aur bahut tez infrasound insaan ko bechaini ya chakkar jaisa mehsoos karwa sakta hai. Isiliye medical scan mein ultrasound ki intensity bahut kam rakhi jaati hai aur sirf zaroorat par aur training wale doctor hi scan karte hain.

---

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Ultrasound sound nahi hai, kyunki sunai nahi deta"**

Sunai dena ya na dena hamare kaan ki seema par depend karta hai. Ultrasound bhi vibrations se banti, medium mein chalti sound wave hai: bas uski frequency 20 kHz se zyada hai. Isi liye bat, dog aur dolphin use sun lete hain.

**Galti 2: "Ultrasound audible sound se tez chalta hai"**

Nahi. Ek hi medium mein dono ki speed lagbhag same hai (hawa mein 340 m/s). Ultrasound ki **frequency** zyada hai aur **wavelength** chhoti, speed nahi badalti. v = f λ mein f badhta hai to λ ghatta hai.

**Galti 3: SONAR mein doori ko aadha na karna**

Echo ka time t aane aur jaane dono ka hai. Agar t = 4 s ho, to d = 1500 × 4 = 6000 m galat hai. Sahi: d = 1500 × 4 ÷ 2 = **3000 m**.

**Galti 4: SONAR aur radar ko ek samajhna**

SONAR **ultrasound (sound)** use karta hai, aksar paani ke neeche. Radar **radio waves** use karta hai (jo sound nahi hain), hawa mein, jaise aasman mein jahaz dhoondhne ke liye.

**Galti 5: "Ultrasound scan bhi X-ray ki tarah radiation deti hai"**

Nahi. Ultrasound sirf sound waves hain, jisme X-ray jaisi radiation nahi hoti. Isiliye bachche ki scan mein ise surakshit mana jaata hai.

**Galti 6: Samundar mein bhi 340 m/s use karna**

Samundar ke paani mein sound ki speed lagbhag **1500 m/s** hai. Agar 340 m/s lete ho to echo ke 4 s ke liye d = 340 × 4 ÷ 2 = 680 m aata hai, jo sahi 3000 m se bahut kam hai.

## Quick Recap

- Insaani kaan ka **audible range: lagbhag 20 Hz se 20000 Hz (20 kHz)**. 20 Hz se kam = **infrasound**, 20 kHz se zyada = **ultrasound**. Dono sound hi hain, bas hum sun nahi paate.
- Umar badhne par ooncha frequency sunne ki shakti ghatti hai. Kutta lagbhag 45 kHz, billi lagbhag 64 kHz, bat lagbhag 100 kHz, dolphin lagbhag 100 se 150 kHz tak sunte hain (approximate). Haathi aur whale infrasound use karte hain.
- **Infrasound**: bhukamp, volcano, haathi-whale, badi machines, thunder. Lambi wavelength (10 Hz mein 34 m), isliye door tak jaati hai.
- **Ultrasound**: high frequency, chhoti wavelength (40 kHz mein 8.5 mm), patli seedhi beam, achhi reflection, zyada energy. Ek khaas crystal se banti hai, jo bijli se kaampta hai.
- Ultrasound ki speed hawa mein audible sound jitni hi hai.
- **Upyog**: sonography (bachcha, dil, liver, kidney), physiotherapy, kidney stone todna; nazuk cheezon ki safai; dhatu mein crack dhoondhna; SONAR; bat aur dolphin ka echolocation.
- **SONAR** = SOund Navigation And Ranging: d = v t / 2, samundar mein v ≈ 1500 m/s.
- Radar radio waves use karta hai, SONAR sound.
- Bahut tez ultrasound ya infrasound nuksaan kar sakta hai.
- Hospital ki kahani: 3 MHz ke ultrasound ki tissue mein wavelength lagbhag 0.5 mm; echoes se tasveer banti hai.

## Practice Questions

1. Classify each as infrasound, audible or ultrasound: 5 Hz, 18 kHz, 30 kHz, 2 MHz.

2. Find the wavelength of a 25 kHz ultrasound in air. Give the answer in mm. (v = 340 m/s)

3. Find the wavelength of a 5 Hz infrasound in air. Why can infrasound travel very long distances?

4. A ship sends an ultrasonic pulse towards the sea-bed and receives the echo after 2 s. Find the depth of the sea. (v = 1500 m/s)

5. The sea at a place is 1200 m deep. How long after sending a SONAR pulse will the echo be received? (v = 1500 m/s)

6. A bat emits a 100 kHz squeak. (a) Find its wavelength in air. (b) An insect is 1.7 m from the bat. After how much time does the bat hear the echo? (v = 340 m/s)

7. An ultrasound of frequency 5 MHz is used in a scan. How many vibrations occur in 1 s? Find the time of one vibration (period) in microseconds.

8. A dolphin produces a 120 kHz sound in sea water. Find its wavelength. (v = 1500 m/s)

9. An ultrasonic flaw detector is used on a steel block, in which the speed of ultrasound is 5000 m/s. An extra echo from a flaw arrives after 30 µs. How deep is the flaw?

10. An elephant makes a call of 15 Hz. (a) Is it audible to humans? (b) Find its wavelength in air. (v = 340 m/s)

11. State True or False:
    (a) Ultrasound travels faster than audible sound in air.
    (b) SONAR uses radio waves.
    (c) Ultrasound scans use X-rays.
    (d) Older people generally cannot hear high frequencies as well as children.

12. Give three reasons why ultrasound, and not audible sound, is used in SONAR.

13. A student finds the depth of the sea from an echo time of 4 s using 340 m/s and gets 680 m. What mistake has he made? Find the correct depth.

14. In the hospital story, how does the probe form a picture of the kidney even though the ultrasound cannot be heard? Answer in three or four lines.

### Answers

1. 5 Hz < 20 Hz: **infrasound**. 18 kHz = 18000 Hz, between 20 Hz and 20000 Hz: **audible**. 30 kHz = 30000 Hz > 20000 Hz: **ultrasound**. 2 MHz = 2 × 10⁶ Hz: **ultrasound**.
   Jaanch: 5 (neeche), 18000 (beech mein), 30000 (upar), 2000000 (upar) ✅

2. f = 25 kHz = 25000 Hz. λ = 340 m/s ÷ 25000 Hz = 0.0136 m = **13.6 mm**.
   Jaanch: 25000 Hz × 0.0136 m = 340 m/s ✅

3. λ = 340 m/s ÷ 5 Hz = **68 m**. Infrasound ki wavelength bahut lambi hai, isliye rukawatein use zyada nahi rokti aur uski energy dheere-dheere kam hoti hai: wo bahut door tak jaati hai.
   Jaanch: 5 Hz × 68 m = 340 m/s ✅

4. d = v t / 2 = 1500 m/s × 2 s ÷ 2 = 3000 m ÷ 2 = **1500 m**.
   Jaanch: 2 × 1500 m = 3000 m = 1500 m/s × 2 s ✅

5. t = 2d ÷ v = 2 × 1200 m ÷ 1500 m/s = 2400 m ÷ 1500 m/s = **1.6 s**.
   Jaanch: d = 1500 m/s × 1.6 s ÷ 2 = 2400 m ÷ 2 = 1200 m ✅

6. (a) f = 100 kHz = 100000 Hz. λ = 340 m/s ÷ 100000 Hz = 0.0034 m = **3.4 mm**.
   (b) t = 2 × 1.7 m ÷ 340 m/s = 3.4 m ÷ 340 m/s = **0.01 s** (= 10 ms).
   Jaanch: 100000 × 0.0034 = 340 ✅; d = 340 × 0.01 ÷ 2 = 1.7 m ✅

7. f = 5 MHz = 5 × 10⁶ Hz, to 1 s mein **5 × 10⁶** vibrations. T = 1 ÷ f = 1 ÷ (5 × 10⁶ Hz) = 2 × 10⁻⁷ s = **0.2 µs**.
   Jaanch: 5 × 10⁶ × 2 × 10⁻⁷ = 1 ✅

8. f = 120 kHz = 120000 Hz. λ = 1500 m/s ÷ 120000 Hz = 0.0125 m = **12.5 mm**.
   Jaanch: 120000 Hz × 0.0125 m = 1500 m/s ✅

9. t = 30 µs = 30 × 10⁻⁶ s. d = v t / 2 = 5000 m/s × 30 × 10⁻⁶ s ÷ 2 = 0.15 m ÷ 2 = 0.075 m = **7.5 cm**.
   Jaanch: 2 × 0.075 m = 0.15 m = 5000 m/s × 30 × 10⁻⁶ s ✅

10. (a) 15 Hz < 20 Hz, yaani infrasound: **audible nahi hai**. (b) λ = 340 m/s ÷ 15 Hz = 22.67 m ≈ **22.7 m**.
    Jaanch: 15 Hz × 22.67 m = 340 m/s ✅

11. (a) **False** ❌. Ek hi medium mein dono ki speed lagbhag same hai; farak frequency ka hai.
    (b) **False** ❌. SONAR ultrasound (sound) use karta hai; radar radio waves use karta hai.
    (c) **False** ❌. Ultrasound scan sound waves se hoti hai, X-rays se nahi.
    (d) **True** ✅. Umar ke saath ooncha frequency sunne ki shakti kam hoti hai.

12. (i) Ultrasound ki patli beam hoti hai, to wo ek hi disha mein sahi nishane par jaati hai. (ii) Chhoti wavelength ke karan wo chhoti cheezon se bhi reflect hoti hai. (iii) Insaan ise sun nahi sakte, to jahaz par tez awaaz se pareshani nahi hoti.

13. Usne samundar mein 340 m/s (hawa ki speed) use ki. Samundar mein speed lagbhag 1500 m/s hai. Sahi: d = 1500 m/s × 4 s ÷ 2 = **3000 m**.
    Jaanch: 2 × 3000 m = 6000 m = 1500 m/s × 4 s ✅

14. Probe ultrasound ki pulse sharir mein bhejti hai. Jahan do tissues ki seema aati hai, wahan se echo wapas aata hai. Probe echo pakadti hai aur machine har echo ke time se d = v t / 2 se doori nikalti hai. Saari doorion ko jod kar screen par tasveer banti hai. Awaaz sunai nahi deti, par uske echoes ki jaankari se tasveer ban jaati hai.
