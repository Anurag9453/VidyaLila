# Topic 7: Human Ear aur Noise Pollution

Pichle topic mein humne dekha ki sound ki duniya hamare sunne se kaafi badi hai. Insaan lagbhag 20 Hz se 20 kHz tak ki awaaz sun sakta hai (audible range). Usse zyada frequency wali ultrasound ko hum sun nahi paate, par chamgaadar, dolphin aur SONAR jaisi machine use sunti hain aur mapti hain. Usse kam frequency wali infrasound bhi hamare kaanon ke liye nahi hai.

Ab ek sawaal bacha hai: **hum sunte kaise hain?** Hawa mein chalti hui sound wave hamare sir ke andar ek electric message mein kaise badalti hai, jise dimaag "awaaz" samajhta hai? Aur agar kaan itna nazuk yantra hai, to bahut tez shor (noise) se uska kya hota hai? Ye chapter ka aakhri topic hai, to end mein poore chapter ka wrap-up bhi hoga.

## Part A: Kaan ke teen hisse

Hamara kaan sirf wo flap nahi hai jo sir ke dono taraf dikhta hai. Wo to kaan ka ek chhota sa hissa hai. Poora kaan **teen hisson** mein baanta jaata hai:

1. **Baahari kaan (outer ear)**
2. **Beech ka kaan (middle ear)**
3. **Andaruni kaan (inner ear)**

Sound wave ko in teeno se guzarna padta hai, tab jaakar hum sun pate hain.

```
  BAAHARI KAAN       BEECH KA KAAN                ANDARUNI KAAN
  (outer ear)        (middle ear)                 (inner ear)

                                                  semicircular canals
                                                  (balance)
                         hammer  anvil  stirrup         │
    pinna                  [H] ── [A] ── [S] ──> oval window
   ( ))) ══ ear canal ══>│                              │
                      eardrum                       cochlea (@)
                         │                              │
                  Eustachian tube                 auditory nerve
                  (gale ki taraf)                       │
                                                        v
                                                    DIMAAG
```

> **Figure 7.1** – Kaan ka ek saaf sa chitra: pinna aur ear canal baahari kaan hain; eardrum, hammer, anvil aur stirrup beech ke kaan mein hain; oval window ke baad cochlea, semicircular canals aur auditory nerve andaruni kaan hain. Eustachian tube beech ke kaan ko gale se jodti hai.

### Hissa 1: Baahari kaan

- **Pinna (kaan ka flap)** ek chhote funnel ki tarah kaam karta hai: wo aas paas ki sound waves ko ikattha karta hai aur andar ki taraf bhejta hai.
- Wave phir **ear canal (auditory canal)** mein jaati hai, jo ek chhoti naali hai, lagbhag 2.5 cm lambi.
- Ear canal ke ant mein **eardrum (tympanic membrane)** hai: ek patli, tani hui jhilli. Jab sound wave ki hawa ke compression aur rarefaction eardrum par takrate hain, to eardrum **usi frequency par** kaanpne lagti hai. Bilkul dhol ki khaal ki tarah, jise dhakka lage to wo kaanpti hai.

### Hissa 2: Beech ka kaan

Eardrum ke peeche ek chhoti hawa se bhari jagah hai, jismein **teen chhoti haddiyaan** hain. Ye sharir ki sabse chhoti haddiyaan hain:

1. **Hammer (malleus)**: eardrum se judi hoti hai.
2. **Anvil (incus)**: beech mein.
3. **Stirrup (stapes)**: ant mein, inner ear ke **oval window** se judi.

Ye teeno haddiyaan ek chain ki tarah eardrum ke vibrations ko aage **pahunchati hain aur unhein badhaati (amplify) bhi hain**, taaki andar ke taral (fluid) mein vibrations ban sakein. (Taral mein vibrations paida karna hawa se mushkil hota hai, isliye ye badhaav zaroori hai.)

**Eustachian tube:** beech ke kaan se gale tak jaati ek patli naali. Iska kaam eardrum ke **dono taraf** ke hawa ke pressure ko barabar rakhna hai. Bahar ka pressure badle (jaise oonchai par chadhne par), to ye naali beech ke kaan ke andar ka pressure bhi barabar kar deti hai.

Plane mein ya lift mein tezi se upar-neeche jaate waqt hamare kaan **"pop"** karte hain, kyunki bahar ka pressure achaanak badal jaata hai aur eardrum ke dono taraf ka pressure ek pal ke liye alag ho jaata hai. Nigalne ya jamhai lene se Eustachian tube khulti hai, pressure barabar ho jaata hai aur "pop" ke saath kaan khul jaata hai. (Atmospheric pressure kya hai aur oonchai ke saath kaise badalta hai, ye [pressure-and-fluids-part4.md](../pressure-and-fluids/pressure-and-fluids-part4.md) mein dekha tha.)

### Hissa 3: Andaruni kaan

- **Cochlea**: ghonghe (snail) jaisi kundli wali naali, jo **taral (fluid)** se bhari hai. Stirrup oval window ko dhakelta hai, to cochlea ke andar ka taral hilne lagta hai.
- Cochlea ki deewar par bahut saari **hair cells** (chhoti baal jaisi koshikayein) hoti hain. Taral ke hilne se ye hair cells bhi hilti hain, aur **hair cells vibrations ko electric signals mein badal deti hain.**
- Ye electric signals **auditory nerve** se hote hue **dimaag (brain)** tak jaate hain. Dimaag in signals ko samajh kar batata hai: "ye awaaz hai, aur wo bhi aisi."
- **Semicircular canals** bhi andaruni kaan mein hi hain, par ye sunne ke liye nahi: ye sharir ka **balance** banaye rakhne mein madad karti hain. (Isliye kaan ki kuch bimariyon mein chakkar aate hain.)

### Sound ka poora safar: ek chain mein

```
  sound waves
       │
       v
     pinna                 ┐
       │                   │  baahari kaan
       v                   │
   ear canal               │
       │                   ┘
       v
    eardrum                ┐
       │                   │  beech ka kaan
       v                   │
 hammer → anvil → stirrup  ┘
       │
       v
 oval window → cochlea     ┐  andaruni kaan
       │                   │
       v                   │
 auditory nerve            ┘
       │
       v
     brain
```

> **Figure 7.2** – Sound ka safar: sound waves → pinna → ear canal → eardrum → teen haddiyaan (hammer, anvil, stirrup) → cochlea → auditory nerve → brain.

Chhota roop: **sound waves → pinna → ear canal → eardrum → teen haddiyaan → cochlea → auditory nerve → brain.**

### Mechanical se electrical

Is safar ki ek khaas baat dekho. Pinna se lekar cochlea ke taral tak sab kuch **mechanical** hai: hawa ke vibrations, eardrum ke vibrations, haddiyon ke vibrations, taral ke vibrations. Hair cells par pahunchkar ye **electrical** signal mein badal jaata hai, aur nerve us electric signal ko dimaag tak le jaati hai. Yaani **mechanical vibrations pehle electrical signals banti hain, tab dimaag unhein sound samajhta hai.**

### Har hisse ka kaam: ek table mein

| Hissa | Kaan ka kaun sa bhaag | Kaam |
|---|---|---|
| Pinna | Baahari kaan | Sound waves ikattha karke ear canal mein bhejna |
| Ear canal | Baahari kaan | Waves ko eardrum tak le jaana |
| Eardrum | Baahari aur beech ke kaan ke beech | Sound waves se kaanpna |
| Hammer, anvil, stirrup | Beech ka kaan | Vibrations ko aage badhana aur badhaana (amplify karna) |
| Eustachian tube | Beech ka kaan | Eardrum ke dono taraf ka pressure barabar karna |
| Cochlea | Andaruni kaan | Taral ke vibrations se hair cells ko hilana |
| Hair cells | Andaruni kaan | Vibrations ko electric signals mein badalna |
| Auditory nerve | Andaruni kaan se dimaag tak | Signals ko dimaag tak pahunchana |
| Semicircular canals | Andaruni kaan | Sharir ka balance (sunne mein nahi) |

### Hum awaaz ki disha kaise jaante hain?

Hamare **do kaan** hain, sir ke dono taraf. Agar awaaz ek taraf se aa rahi hai, to wo us taraf wale kaan tak **thodi pehle** pahunchti hai, aur doosre kaan tak thodi der se (wahan tak jaane mein sound ko kuch zyada doori tay karni padti hai, aur sir bhi beech mein rukaawat banata hai). Dimaag is bahut chhote time ke farak (aur dono kaanon mein awaaz ki halki ki alag taakat) ko pakadkar andaaza laga leta hai ki awaaz kis disha se aa rahi hai. Isliye ek kaan band karke disha pehchanna mushkil ho jaata hai. (Iska ek numerical example neeche hai.)

## Part B: Kaan ki dekhbhaal aur sunne ki kami

### Kaan ko surakshit rakhne ke tareeke

1. **Kaan mein koi cheez na daalo**: pin, pencil, matchstick, ya zor se ear-buds. Eardrum bahut patli hai aur chot lag sakti hai. Kaan ka mail (wax) ek suraksha parat hai; zaroorat ho to doctor se saaf karwao.
2. **Bahut tez awaaz se door raho**, jaise pataakhe phodte waqt paas khade rehna ya loudspeaker ke bilkul saamne baithna.
3. **Earphones ya headphones ka volume kam rakho aur lambe samay tak lagataar mat suno.** Ek aam salaah ek **"60-60 rule"** hai: volume zyada se zyada lagbhag 60% rakho, aur ek baar mein lagbhag 60 minute se zyada na suno, phir kaanon ko aaraam do. (Ye ek **general advice** hai, koi kadi seema nahi; device aur earphone ke hisaab se asli awaaz alag hoti hai.)
4. **Ear plugs** lagao jab shor se bachna mushkil ho (jaise factory, construction site ya bade concert mein).
5. **Kaan mein dard, sunai kam dena ya "seeti" jaisi awaaz** (tinnitus) mehsoos ho to doctor ko dikhao.

### Sunne ki kami aur hearing aid

Kabhi kabhi bimaari, chot, umar ke saath ya bahut zyada shor ki wajah se kaan ka koi hissa theek se kaam nahi karta, aur insaan kam sunta hai (**hearing loss**). Aise logon ke liye **hearing aid** hoti hai: ek chhoti si machine jo kaan ke paas ya andar lagti hai. Wo awaaz ko pakadkar **badha** deti hai (amplify karti hai) aur kaan mein bhejti hai, jisse insaan zyada saaf sun sake. Doctor jaanch ke baad hi hearing aid ka sahi prakar batate hain.

## Part C: Noise pollution

**Noise pollution: anchaahi, bahut zyada ya nuksaan karne wali awaaz.**

Sangeet kitna bhi achha ho, agar wo bahut tez ho ya aisi jagah ho jahan koi sunna na chahta ho, to wo noise ban jaata hai.

### Noise ke strot (sources)

- Gaadiyon ke **horn** aur traffic ka lagataar shor
- **Aircraft** (hawaai jahaaz) aur railway
- **Factories** ki machines
- **Construction** (drilling, hammer, concrete mixer)
- Shaadi aur tyohaar mein **loudspeakers** aur DJ
- **Pataakhe (crackers)**
- Bahut tez **music** (ghar mein bhi aur earphones par bhi)
- **Generators**

### Noise ke asar

Neeche sirf wo asar hain jo aam taur par maane jaate hain:

1. **Sunne ki kami** (lambe samay tak tez shor se kaan ki hair cells ko nuksaan)
2. **Sir dard**
3. **Tension, chidchidapan aur gussa** (stress)
4. **Neend na aana**
5. **High blood pressure** (lambe samay tak bahut shor mein rehne par)
6. **Dhyan lagane aur padhne mein mushkil**, khaaskar bachchon ke liye
7. **Janwaron par bhi asar**: pakshi aur jaanwar bahut tez shor se ghabra jaate hain, aur kuch shor wali jagahon se door chale jaate hain.

### Awaaz ka level (dB) aur uska asar

Awaaz ki taakat (loudness) **decibel (dB)** mein naapi jaati hai. dB ke hisaab mein bahut chhoti badhat bhi bahut zyada awaaz ka matlab hoti hai, isliye 10 dB ki badhat sunne mein bahut tez lagti hai. (Ye Topic 5 mein dekha tha. Yahan hum koi calculation nahi karenge.)

| Awaaz ka strot | Lagbhag level (dB) | Asar (general guidance) |
|---|---|---|
| Fusfusahat (whisper) | lagbhag 20 dB | Bahut shaant: koi nuksaan nahi |
| Aam baatcheet | lagbhag 60 dB | Aaram se sun sakte hain: safe |
| Heavy traffic | lagbhag 80 dB | Bahut der rehne par thakaan aur chidchidapan |
| Lambe samay tak (ghanton) 85 dB ya usse zyada | 85 dB aur upar | Sunne ko nuksaan pahunch sakta hai |
| Rock concert | lagbhag 110 se 120 dB | Kuch hi minuton mein kaan ko nuksaan ho sakta hai |
| Bahut tez dhamaka, jet engine ke paas | lagbhag 120 dB | Kaan mein dard ho sakta hai |
| Pain threshold | lagbhag 140 dB | Tez dard; turant nuksaan ka khatra |

**Dhyan do:** ye sab **lagbhag (approximate)** aur **general guidance** hain. Asal mein nuksaan awaaz ke level aur **kitni der** sunne par nirbhar karta hai: jitna tez, utni kam der mein nuksaan.

### Noise ko kam karne ke upaay

1. **Engine band karo** jab gaadi ruki ho (jaise red light par).
2. **Volume kam rakho**: TV, music system aur loudspeaker.
3. **Bina zaroorat horn mat bajao.** Hospital aur school ke paas **"No horn" zone** hona chahiye.
4. **Sadak ke kinare ped lagao**: ped aur patte awaaz ka kuch hissa sokh lete hain.
5. **Sound-absorbing deewarein** aur **double-glazed windows** (do sheeshe wali khidki) lagao: dono ke beech ki hawa ki parat awaaz ko kam karti hai.
6. **Soundproofing**: kamre ki deewaron par moti parat (curtains, foam, sound absorbers) lagana.
7. **Kanoon**: kai jagah kanoon hain jo raat ko loudspeakers ko rokte ya seemit karte hain.
8. **Traffic management**: vehicles ki gati niyantrit karna, bheed kam karna.
9. **Silent zones**: hospitals aur schools ke paas ka ilaaka "silent zone" banaya jaata hai.

## Part E: Solved examples

Hum sound ki speed **340 m/s** lenge.

### Example 1: Sadak se classroom tak awaaz ko kitna time lagta hai?

Ek truck ka horn sadak par baj raha hai, aur classroom us se **30 m** door hai. Awaaz ko classroom tak pahunchne mein kitna time lagega?

d = 30 m, v = 340 m/s

t = d ÷ v = 30 m ÷ 340 m/s = **0.088 s** (lagbhag)

Jaanch: 340 m/s × 0.088 s = 29.92 m ≈ 30 m ✅

Matlab: awaaz lagbhag turant pahunch jaati hai: ek second ke dasve hisse (0.1 s) se bhi kam mein.

### Example 2: Ear canal mein vibrations ko kitna time lagta hai?

Ear canal ki lambai 2.5 cm hai. Maan lo awaaz ear canal mein bhi 340 m/s se chalti hai. Pinna se eardrum tak jaane mein kitna time lagega?

d = 2.5 cm = 2.5 ÷ 100 m = 0.025 m

t = d ÷ v = 0.025 m ÷ 340 m/s = **7.4 × 10⁻⁵ s** (lagbhag)

Jaanch: 340 m/s × 7.4 × 10⁻⁵ s = 0.02516 m ≈ 0.025 m ✅

Matlab: ye samay sirf 0.000074 s hai, itna chhota ki hum use kabhi mehsoos hi nahi kar sakte.

### Example 3: 2000 Hz ki awaaz ki wavelength

Ek awaaz ki frequency 2000 Hz hai. Hawa mein uski wavelength kitni hogi? (v = f λ)

λ = v ÷ f = 340 m/s ÷ 2000 Hz = **0.17 m = 17 cm**

Jaanch: f × λ = 2000 Hz × 0.17 m = 340 m/s ✅

Matlab: 2000 Hz ki awaaz ki ek poori wave sirf 17 cm lambi hoti hai.

### Example 4: Dono kaanon tak awaaz ke pahunchne mein farak

Maan lo kisi ke dono kaanon ke beech ki doori lagbhag 20 cm hai, aur awaaz bilkul ek taraf se aa rahi hai, jisse sound ko doosre kaan tak 20 cm zyada jaana padta hai. Doosre kaan tak wo kitni der baad pahunchegi? (v = 340 m/s)

d = 20 cm = 0.20 m

t = d ÷ v = 0.20 m ÷ 340 m/s = **5.9 × 10⁻⁴ s** (lagbhag 0.0006 s)

Jaanch: 340 m/s × 5.9 × 10⁻⁴ s = 0.2006 m ≈ 0.20 m ✅

Matlab: farak second ke hazaarve hisse se bhi chhota hai, phir bhi hamara dimaag use pakad leta hai. Isi se hum jaante hain ki awaaz kis taraf se aa rahi hai. (Ye ek andaza hai: asal sir ki shape ke karan farak thoda alag hota hai.)

### Example 5: Eardrum 4 second mein kitni baar kaanpti hai?

Ek 500 Hz ki note 4 second tak baji. Eardrum ne kitni vibrations ki?

f = 500 Hz = 500 vibrations per second, t = 4 s

Vibrations = f × t = 500 vibrations/s × 4 s = **2000 vibrations**

Jaanch: Time period T = 1 ÷ f = 1 ÷ 500 Hz = 0.002 s. 2000 vibrations × 0.002 s = 4 s ✅

### Example 6: Doori badhne par awaaz ka asar (qualitative)

Ek loudspeaker se 10 m ki doori par awaaz tez lagti hai. Agar tum **20 m** door chale jaao (doori double), to awaaz ka kya hoga?

Awaaz door jaate jaate **fail jaati hai** aur uski energy badi jagah mein bant jaati hai. Isliye 20 m par awaaz 10 m ke mukable **aur kam** (zyada halki) hogi.

Matlab: shor se bachne ke liye **doori badhana** ek seedha upaay hai. Isi liye school ki classrooms ko sadak se door karna madad karta hai. (Kitni kam hogi, iska hisaab aage ki class mein.)

### Example 7: Din bhar ke shor ka hisaab (qualitative)

Neeche teen log hain. Har ek ke liye batao: unke kaan ke liye ye stithi kitni surakshit hai?

| Vyakti | Kahan hai | Lagbhag level | Kitni der |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Library | lagbhag 40 dB | Poora din |
| B | Heavy traffic ke paas dukaan | lagbhag 80 dB | 8 ghante |
| C | Factory ki machine ke paas | lagbhag 90 dB | 8 ghante, bina ear plugs |

- **A:** 40 dB bahut shaant hai: **safe**.
- **B:** 80 dB, 85 dB ki seema se thoda neeche hai, par poora din rehna thakaan aur chidchidapan deta hai. Mostly **safe se thoda zyada pareshani**: dhyan rakhna chahiye.
- **C:** 90 dB, 85 dB se zyada hai aur 8 ghante tak: **unsafe**. Ear plugs lagane chahiye.

Matlab: nuksaan **level** aur **time** dono par nirbhar karta hai.

### Example 8: Ear ke hisse ko sahi kram mein lagao

Neeche kaan ke hisse galat kram mein diye hain: **cochlea, pinna, brain, eardrum, ear canal, auditory nerve, hammer-anvil-stirrup.** Unhein sound ke safar ke sahi kram mein likho.

Sahi kram:
1. **Pinna**: sound ikattha karta hai
2. **Ear canal**: waves ko andar le jaati hai
3. **Eardrum**: kaanpti hai
4. **Hammer, anvil, stirrup**: vibrations aage badhati hain aur badhaati hain
5. **Cochlea**: taral aur hair cells se electric signals banata hai
6. **Auditory nerve**: signals ko le jaati hai
7. **Brain**: signals ko "awaaz" samajhta hai

Jaanch: baahari kaan (1, 2, 3) → beech ka kaan (3 se 4 tak) → andaruni kaan (5, 6) → dimaag (7). Mechanical (1 se 5 ke taral tak) phir electrical (hair cells se aage) ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Kaan sirf wo hai jo bahar dikhta hai."**
Bahar dikhne wala pinna kaan ka sirf ek chhota hissa hai. Asli sunne ka kaam beech aur andaruni kaan mein hota hai.

**Galti 2: "Eardrum hi awaaz ko dimaag tak bhejti hai."**
Eardrum sirf kaanpti hai. Awaaz ko electric signal mein badalne ka kaam cochlea ke hair cells karte hain, aur nerve use dimaag tak le jaati hai.

**Galti 3: "Semicircular canals sunne mein madad karte hain."**
Nahi, ye sharir ke balance ke liye hain.

**Galti 4: "Kaan saaf karne ke liye pin ya pencil daalna theek hai."**
Bilkul nahi. Isse eardrum ko chot lag sakti hai. Kaan mein koi cheez nahi daalni chahiye.

**Galti 5: "Music kitna bhi tez ho, kaan ko nuksaan nahi karta."**
Tez music bhi noise ki tarah nuksaan kar sakta hai. Level aur kitni der sunte ho, dono mayne rakhte hain.

**Galti 6: "60-60 rule ek kanoon hai."**
Ye sirf ek aam salaah hai, taaki kaanon ko aaraam mile.

**Galti 7: "Ped lagane se shor poori tarah band ho jaata hai."**
Ped shor ka sirf **kuch hissa** sokhte hain. Poori tarah band nahi karte, isliye doosre upaay bhi saath mein chahiye.

## Quick Recap

- **Kaan ke teen hisse:** baahari (pinna, ear canal, eardrum), beech ka (hammer, anvil, stirrup, Eustachian tube) aur andaruni (cochlea, semicircular canals, auditory nerve).
- **Sound ka safar:** sound waves → pinna → ear canal → eardrum → hammer, anvil, stirrup → cochlea → auditory nerve → brain.
- Pinna se cochlea ke taral tak **mechanical** vibrations hain; hair cells unhein **electric signals** mein badalti hain, nerve dimaag tak le jaati hai.
- **Eustachian tube** eardrum ke dono taraf ka pressure barabar karti hai (plane ya lift mein kaan "pop" hona).
- **Semicircular canals** balance ke liye hain.
- Do kaan hone se hum awaaz ki **disha** pehchante hain: ek kaan tak awaaz thodi pehle pahunchti hai.
- **Kaan ki dekhbhaal:** kaan mein cheez na daalo, bahut tez awaaz se bacho, earphone ka volume aur time kam rakho (60-60 salaah), ear plugs, doctor ko dikhana. Kam sunne par **hearing aid**.
- **Noise pollution:** anchaahi, zyada ya nuksaandeh awaaz. Strot: horn, aircraft, factory, construction, loudspeaker, pataakhe, generator. Asar: sunne ki kami, sir dard, stress, neend, high blood pressure, padhai mein dhyan, janwaron par asar.
- Lagbhag 85 dB ya usse upar ka shor ghanton tak nuksaan kar sakta hai; lagbhag 120 dB par dard ho sakta hai (general guidance).
- **Upaay:** engine band karna, volume kam, no horn zone, ped lagana, sound-absorbing walls, double glass, soundproofing, kanoon, traffic management, silent zones.

### Answers

1. **Baahari kaan** (pinna, ear canal, eardrum), **beech ka kaan** (hammer, anvil, stirrup, Eustachian tube), **andaruni kaan** (cochlea, semicircular canals, auditory nerve). Koi bhi do naam kaafi hain.

2. **Pinna → ear canal → eardrum → hammer-anvil-stirrup → cochlea → auditory nerve → brain.**

3. Eardrum sound wave ke aane par us frequency par kaanpti hai, aur beech ke kaan ki haddiyon ko vibrations deti hai. Ye ek patli, kasi hui (tani hui) jhilli hai, isliye ise stretched membrane kehte hain (dhol ki khaal jaisi).

4. Ye teeno haddiyaan eardrum ke vibrations ko aage inner ear tak pahunchati hain aur unhein **badhaati (amplify karti)** hain. Naam: **hammer (malleus), anvil (incus), stirrup (stapes).**

5. Tezi se upar-neeche jaane par bahar ka pressure achaanak badalta hai, aur eardrum ke dono taraf ka pressure thodi der ke liye alag ho jaata hai. **Eustachian tube** dono taraf ka pressure barabar karti hai, aur tab kaan "pop" karta hai.

6. **Cochlea ki hair cells** mechanical vibrations ko electric signals mein badalti hain. **Auditory nerve** signal ko brain tak le jaati hai.

7. Semicircular canals sharir ka **balance** banaye rakhne mein madad karti hain. (Ye sunne ke liye nahi hain.)

8. Awaaz ek taraf se aati hai to us taraf wale kaan tak thodi pehle pahunchti hai aur doosre tak thodi der se. Dimaag is chhote se time ke farak ko pakadkar disha ka andaaza laga leta hai.

9. λ = v ÷ f = 340 m/s ÷ 1000 Hz = 0.34 m = **34 cm**.
   Jaanch: 1000 Hz × 0.34 m = 340 m/s ✅

10. t = 0.025 m ÷ 340 m/s = **7.4 × 10⁻⁵ s** (lagbhag).
    Jaanch: 340 × 7.4 × 10⁻⁵ = 0.02516 ≈ 0.025 m ✅

11. Vibrations = 250 Hz × 6 s = **1500**. T = 1 ÷ 250 Hz = **0.004 s**.
    Jaanch: 1500 × 0.004 s = 6 s ✅

12. t = 51 m ÷ 340 m/s = **0.15 s**.
    Jaanch: 340 m/s × 0.15 s = 51 m ✅

13. Noise pollution: anchaahi, zyada ya nuksaandeh awaaz. Strot (koi chaar): traffic ke horn, aircraft, factory, construction, loudspeaker, pataakhe, generator. Asar (koi chaar): sunne ki kami, sir dard, stress ya chidchidapan, neend na aana, high blood pressure, padhai mein dhyan na lagna.

14. Koi chaar: classrooms ko sadak se door karna, hari patti (green belt) lagana, double glass ki khidkiyaan, mote parde, no horn zone aur speed limit, traffic ko dheere karwana.

15. (a) **False** ❌. 85 dB aur usse upar ka shor ghanton tak kaan ko nuksaan pahuncha sakta hai.
    (b) **True** ✅. Ped aur patte shor ka kuch hissa sokhte hain.
    (c) **True** ✅. Ye ek general salaah hai (60% volume, 60 minute tak).
    (d) **False** ❌. Cochlea andaruni kaan (inner ear) mein hai.

16. 95 dB, 85 dB se zyada hai aur 8 ghante rozana: **safe nahi hai**, kaan ko nuksaan ho sakta hai. Do upaay: ear plugs (ya ear muffs) lagana, aur beech beech mein shor se door aaraam ke breaks lena (ya machine ko soundproof karna).

17. Eardrum bahut patli aur nazuk hoti hai, aur pin ya pencil se chhoot sakti hai ya chot kha sakti hai, jisse sunne ko nuksaan ho sakta hai. Kaan ka wax bhi use bachata hai.
