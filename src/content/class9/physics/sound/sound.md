# Topic 1: Sound Kya Hai: Vibration aur Medium

Pichhle chapter mein humne dekha ki **energy kai roop mein hoti hai**, aur heat unmein se ek roop hai: wo garm cheez se thandi cheez ki taraf jaati hai. Us chapter ke end mein ek baat kahi thi: ek aur energy hai jo waves ki tarah safar karti hai aur jise hum **sun** sakte hain. Wahi hai **awaaz (sound)**.

Sound bhi energy ka ek roop hai. Isi energy ke kaan tak pahunchne par hume sunai deta hai. Is topic mein hum teen sawaalon ke jawab dhundhenge: **sound banti kaise hai? Wo safar kaise karti hai? Aur kya wo bina kisi cheez ke bhi safar kar sakti hai?**

**Is poore chapter mein hum hawa mein sound ki speed 340 m/s lenge.** (Asal mein ye 0 °C par lagbhag 331 m/s aur 20 °C par lagbhag 343 m/s hoti hai; 340 m/s ek aasaan beech ki value hai.) Is speed ka poora hisaab Topic 4 mein aayega. Yahan hum ise sirf kuch chhote examples mein istemaal karenge.

## Part A: Sound ek energy hai

Sound koi cheez (matter) nahi hai jise hum haath mein pakad sakein: wo **energy ka ek roop** hai. Jab hum dholak par haath maarte hain, to haath ki energy dholak ki khaal ko hilati hai, aur phir wahi energy sound ban kar kaan tak pahunchti hai. Kaan use pakadta hai aur dimaag ko "sunai diya" ka message bhejta hai.

> **Sound** = energy ka wo roop jo hamare kaan mein **sunne ka ehsaas** (sensation of hearing) paida karta hai.

Dhyan do: sound khud koi "pani jaisi" cheez nahi jo bahti ho. Wo energy hai jo ek jagah se doosri jagah **pahunchti** hai.

## Part B: Sound kaise banti hai? Vibration se

### Vibration ya oscillation kya hota hai?

Motion chapter mein humne **oscillatory motion** padhi thi: jaise jhoola, jo ek beech ki jagah ke aas-paas aage-peechhe jaata hai. Sound ke liye bhi wahi motion kaam aati hai, bas bahut tezi se.

> **Vibration (oscillation)** = kisi cheez ka ek **mean position** ke aas-paas **to-and-fro** (aage-peechhe, ya upar-neeche) hilna.

Ek vibration ya ek poori oscillation tab poori hoti hai jab cheez beech se ek taraf jaaye, wapas beech se hoti hui doosri taraf jaaye, aur phir wapas beech mein aa jaaye.

### Mool baat

> **Har sound kisi vibrate karti cheez se paida hoti hai.** Jab vibration ruk jaati hai, sound bhi ruk jaati hai.

Ab isse apni aankhon aur haathon se check karte hain. Ghar mein ye sab kar sakte ho.

### Demonstrations: vibration ko dekho aur mehsoos karo

1. **Tuning fork aur paani.** Ek tuning fork (U-shape ki dhaatu ki chhadi) ko rubber pad par maaro. Wo "tiiiin" ki awaaz degi. Ab uski bhujaa ko paani ki katori ke upar ya chhune ke liye laao: paani **chhapaak se uchhalta** hai. Matlab bhujaa tezi se hil rahi hai. Ya phir tuning fork ko ek latakti hui **ping-pong ball** se chhuao: ball ek dum door uchhal jaati hai.
2. **Ghitar ki taar ya ruler.** Ghitar ki taar ko kheench kar chhodo: wo **dhundhli aur moti** dikhti hai (kyunki bahut tezi se hil rahi hai) aur awaaz aati hai. Ek ruler ko mez ke kinare se nikal kar uska ek sira dabao aur doosra sira kheench kar chhodo: wo upar-neeche hota hai aur "thhrrr" ki awaaz aati hai.
3. **Gale par haath.** Apni ungliyaan gale (neck) par rakho aur "aaaa" bolo. **Halki si jhanjhanahat** mehsoos hogi. Ye hamare **vocal cords** ka vibration hai. Chup ho jao to jhanjhanahat bhi band.
4. **Rubber band.** Ek rubber band ko kheench kar chhodo: wo hilti hai aur "tungg" ki awaaz karti hai.
5. **Ghanti.** Ghanti bajane ke baad haath se chhoo kar dekho: halki kapkapi mehsoos hogi. Haath se pakad lo to awaaz **turant band**.
6. **Dholak (chawal ke dane).** Hamari kahani wala experiment. Isse hum thodi der mein solve karenge.
7. **Speaker par balloon.** Ek speaker par hawa se bhara balloon rakho aur gaana chalao. Balloon par haath rakho: **jhanjhanahat** aayegi. Speaker ka cone vibrate kar raha hai, aur wahi balloon ko hila raha hai.

```
  Tuning fork aur ping-pong ball

          │   <- dhaaga
          │
   ║     ║○   <- ping-pong ball (bhujaa ko chhoote hi door uchhal jaati hai)
   ║     ║
   ║     ║    <- tuning fork ki do bhujaayein (tezi se hil rahi hain)
   ╚══╦══╝
      ║       <- handle
      ║
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Vibrate karti tuning fork ball ko chhute hi ball door uchhal jaati hai: ye batata hai ki fork sach mein hil rahi hai.

### Sound ke alag-alag source

Har sound ka koi na koi vibrate karti cheez (source) hoti hai:

| Source | Kya vibrate karta hai? |
|---|---|
| Insaan ki awaaz | Gale ke **vocal cords** |
| Bansuri (flute) | Munh se phoonkne par bansuri ke **andar ka air column** |
| Ghitar, sitar, veena | Kasi hui **taar (string)** |
| Dholak, tabla, dhol | Kasi hui **khaal (membrane)** |
| Ghanti, thali | Poora **dhaatu ka tukda** |
| Machhar ki bhin-bhin | Machhar ke **pankh**, lagbhag 500 baar har second hilte hain (sirf jaankari ke liye) |
| Madhumakkhi ki bhann-bhann | Madhumakkhi ke **pankh** |

Ek mazedaar baat: **sound sirf badi ya zor ki cheezon se paida nahi hoti.** Machhar ke chhote-chhote pankh bhi vibrate karke sound paida karte hain, bas wo halki hoti hai.

## Part C: Sound ko safar ke liye medium chahiye

### Sound akeli nahi chalti

Kisi bhi source se paida hui sound ko hum tabhi sun paate hain jab wo kisi **material medium** (jaise hawa, paani ya lohe) se hoti hui kaan tak pahunche.

> **Medium** = wo padarth (solid, liquid ya gas) jisme se sound guzarti hai.

Sound **teeno** mein safar kar sakti hai: **solid, liquid aur gas**. Par agar koi bhi padarth na ho, yaani **vacuum**, to sound **safar nahi kar sakti.**

### Bell jar ka experiment

Ye ek mashhoor demonstration hai jo science museums aur labs mein dikhaya jaata hai.

Ek kaanch ke bade jaar (**bell jar**) ke andar ek **electric bell** latkai jaati hai. Bell bajti rehti hai, aur bahar se hum uski awaaz saaf sunte hain, kyunki bell ke andar ki hawa sound ko jaar ki deewar tak pahunchati hai.

Ab ek **vacuum pump** se jaar ke andar ki hawa dheere-dheere bahar nikalte hain.

- Jaise-jaise hawa kam hoti hai, **awaaz kam sunai deti hai.**
- Jab lagbhag saari hawa nikal jaati hai, to awaaz **bahut hi dheemi** ho jaati hai, aur lagbhag sunai nahi deti.
- Dhyan do: bell ka hammer ab bhi **ghoom raha hai aur gong ko maar raha hai**: bell vibrate ho rahi hai! Par sound ko kaan tak le jaane wali hawa nahi hai.
- Hawa wapas jaar mein jaane do, to awaaz phir se sunai dene lagti hai.

```
        ┌───────────────┐
        │ Bell jar      │
        │   ┌──────┐    │      ← vacuum pump
        │   │ BELL │    │ ════════════╗
        │   └──────┘    │             ║
        │ (hawa nikal   │         ┌───╨────┐
        │  chuki hai)   │         │  PUMP  │
        └───────┬───────┘         └────────┘
         ═══════╧════════ (base plate)
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Bell jar ke andar electric bell bajti rehti hai. Pump se hawa nikalne par awaaz kam hoti jaati hai, jo batata hai ki sound ko safar ke liye medium (hawa) chahiye.

Dhyan rahe: hum **poora** vacuum aasani se nahi bana paate, isliye awaaz poori tarah gayab nahi hoti: wo bahut kam ho jaati hai. Isi se hum is natije par pahunchte hain ki **hawa (medium) nahi hogi to sound nahi sunai degi**.

### Chaand par radio se baat kyun?

Chaand (Moon) par hawa lagbhag nahi hoti: wahan vacuum jaisi sthiti hai. Isliye wahan **do astronauts ek doosre ke paas khade ho kar bhi seedha baat nahi kar sakte**: unki awaaz ko safar ke liye hawa hi nahi milti. Isliye wo apne **radio** (walkie-talkie jaise) se baat karte hain. Radio **electromagnetic waves** istemaal karta hai, jo vacuum mein bhi chalti hain. (Ye waves kya hoti hain, wo aage ki class mein.)

### Sound ki speed medium par depend karti hai (ek jhalak)

Alag-alag medium mein sound ki speed alag hoti hai:

> **Sound sabse tez solids mein, usse kam liquids mein, aur sabse dheemi gases (jaise hawa) mein chalti hai.**

Iski table aur numbers Topic 4 mein aayenge. Yahan bas ye baat yaad rakho.

## Part D: Alag-alag medium mein sound

### Solid mein sound

- **Railway track.** Door se train aa rahi ho, to pataliyon par **kaan laga kar** train ki awaaz hawa se **pehle** sunai de jaati hai. (Ye sirf jaankari ke liye hai: sach mein kabhi aisa mat karo, ye khatarnak ho sakta hai.)
- **Lakdi ki mez.** Mez ke ek kinare par dheere se thapthapao, aur doosre kinare par **kaan mez se laga do**. Awaaz zyada **saaf** sunai degi, jabki hawa se wo halki lagti hai.

### Liquid mein sound

- **Swimmer.** Paani ke andar tairte hue agar koi pathar kisi doosri jagah paani mein girta hai, to tairne wale ko uski awaaz saaf sunai deti hai. Matlab paani bhi sound ko le jaata hai.

### Gas mein sound

Roz ki zyada-tar awaazein: bolna, gaana, horn: **hawa** (gas) se hi hum tak aati hain.

## Part E: Sound hum tak kaise pahunchti hai?

Maan lo dholak ki khaal vibrate kar rahi hai. Wo apne paas ki **hawa ke particles ko dhakka** deti hai. Ye particles apne aage wale particles ko dhakka dete hain, wo apne aage wale ko, aur is tarah **layer dar layer** vibration aage badhta jaata hai, jab tak wo hamare kaan tak na pahunch jaaye.

```
  Source        Layer 1      Layer 2      Layer 3       Kaan
 (dholak)  →   particles →  particles →  particles  →   (ear)
  vibrate       dhakka       dhakka       dhakka       sunai diya!
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Source ka vibration paas ke particles ko, phir unke aage ke particles ko dhakka deta hai: vibration (energy) kaan tak pahunchta hai.

**Bahut zaroori baat:** hawa ke particles khud dholak se chal kar hamare kaan tak **nahi aate**. Har particle apni jagah ke aas-paas thoda aage-peechhe hilta hai, bas. Jo cheez aage badhti hai wo hai **vibration (energy)**, particle nahi.

Ise aise socho: stadium mein "Mexican wave" mein log khade ho kar baith jaate hain: wave poore stadium mein ghoomti hai, par koi bhi insaan apni seat chhod kar nahi bhaagta. Isi tarah sound ki "wave" ka poora tareeka (ye wave kaisi hoti hai) hum **Topic 2** mein samjhenge.

## Part F: Sound aur light mein farak

| Baat | Sound | Light |
|---|---|---|

## Part G: Sangeet aur shor

Sab awaazein sunne mein achhi nahi lagti.

- **Music (sangeet)**: sukoon dene wali, sundar, taal mein bandhi hui awaaz: jaise dholak, bansuri.
- **Noise (shor)**: bekaar, pareshaan karne wali awaaz: jaise tez horn ya drilling machine.

Ye farak is baat par bhi depend karta hai ki sunne wale ko kaisa lagta hai. Dono ke peechhe vibration hi hota hai. Is baare mein aage Topic 5 aur Topic 7 mein dekhenge.

## Part H: Ahaan ka raaz solve karte hain

Ab Ahaan ke sawaal par wapas aate hain.

**Mr. Kanojia ne dholak ki khaal par maara.** Khaal **tezi se upar-neeche vibrate** karne lagi. Is vibration ki wajah se:
1. Khaal ke upar rakhe chawal ke dane **dhakka kha kar uchhalne** lage: isi se wo naachte dikhe.
2. Wahi vibrate karti khaal paas ki **hawa ke particles** ko dhakka deti rahi, aur layer dar layer vibration Ahaan ke kaan tak pahunch gaya: isi se awaaz **sunai di**.

**Jab Mr. Kanojia ne hatheli khaal par rakhi**, unhone khaal ko **vibrate karne se rok diya.** Vibration ruka, to na to dane hile, aur na hi koi sound paida hui.

```
   Dholak par maara gaya          Hatheli rakh di
   ────────────────────           ───────────────
      o  o   o                       o o o
   ═══════════════════            ═══════════════
    khaal vibrate (↑↓)             khaal shaant
    dane uchhalte hain             dane aaram se rakhe
    awaaz aati hai                 koi awaaz nahi
```

> **Figure 1.4** – Vibrate karti dholak ki khaal par dane uchhalte hain aur awaaz aati hai. Hatheli rakhne par vibration ruk jaata hai, to awaaz bhi ruk jaati hai.

Ahaan ne kaha, "Matlab awaaz aur dano ka uchhalna ek hi wajah se hote hain: **khaal ka vibration**!"

Mr. Kanojia ne sar hilaya, "Bilkul. **Vibration nahi, to sound nahi.**" Ahaan ne ek aur sawaal kiya, "Agar hum ye dholak chaand par bajayein to?" Mr. Kanojia hanse, "Dane to wahan bhi naachenge, kyunki khaal wahan bhi vibrate karegi. Par **sunai kuch nahi dega**, kyunki wahan hawa nahi hai!"

## Part I: Solved examples

**Example 1 (vibrations ginna):** Ek ruler 3 second mein 12 baar upar-neeche vibrate karta hai. Wo 1 second mein kitni baar vibrate karta hai?

- 1 second mein vibrations = 12 vibrations ÷ 3 s = **4 vibrations per second**

**Jaanch:** 4 vibrations/s × 3 s = 12 vibrations ✅. (Ye "kitni baar har second" wali ginti ek bahut kaam ki cheez hai. Isse **frequency** kehte hain: ye Topic 3 mein detail mein aayegi.)

**Example 2 (sound ka distance):** Hawa mein sound 340 m/s ki speed se chalti hai. 5 second mein wo kitni door jaayegi?

- Distance = speed × time = 340 m/s × 5 s = **1700 m**

**Jaanch:** 1700 m ÷ 340 m/s = 5 s ✅

**Example 3 (hall paar karne ka time):** Ek 68 m lambe hall ke ek kinare par kisi ne taali bajayi. Sound ko doosre kinare tak pahunchne mein kitna time lagega? (Speed = 340 m/s)

- Time = distance ÷ speed = 68 m ÷ 340 m/s = **0.2 s**

**Jaanch:** 340 m/s × 0.2 s = 68 m ✅

**Example 4 (paani mein girte pathar ki awaaz):** Ek talaab ke kinare Ahaan ne ek pathar paani mein phenka. Maan lo wo uske kaan se 34 m door tha, aur awaaz hawa se aayi. Awaaz sunai dene mein kitna time laga? (Speed = 340 m/s)

- Time = 34 m ÷ 340 m/s = **0.1 s**

**Jaanch:** 340 m/s × 0.1 s = 34 m ✅. (Itna kam time ki hum use lagbhag turant hi hua samajhte hain.)

**Example 5 (kya ye sound hogi?):** Ek rassi ko kheench kar chhoda to wo hilti hui dikhi aur halki si awaaz aayi. Awaaz ka source kya tha?

- Source rassi ka **vibration** hai: rassi mean position ke aas-paas aage-peechhe hil rahi hai.

**Jaanch:** Agar rassi ko pakad lo to hilna ruk jaayega aur awaaz bhi ruk jaayegi ✅

**Example 6 (madhumakkhi ke pankh):** Ek madhumakkhi ke pankh 3 second mein 600 baar vibrate karte hain. Ek second mein wo kitni baar vibrate karte hain?

- 600 ÷ 3 s = **200 vibrations per second**

**Jaanch:** 200 × 3 = 600 ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Sound vacuum mein bhi chalti hai"**

Nahi. Sound ko safar ke liye **material medium** chahiye. Chaand par hawa nahi hai, isliye wahan awaaz nahi sunai deti. Bell jar experiment is baat ka saboot hai.

**Galti 2: "Sound koi cheez hai jo paani ki tarah behti hai"**

Sound koi padarth nahi, **energy** ka ek roop hai. Wo bahti nahi, balki vibration ke roop mein **aage badhti** hai.

**Galti 3: "Sound sirf zor ki cheezon se paida hoti hai"**

Nahi. Har vibrate karti cheez sound paida karti hai, chahe wo machhar ke chhote pankh hi kyun na ho. Bas wo halki hoti hai.

**Galti 4: "Hawa ke particles source se kaan tak chal kar aate hain"**

Nahi. Har particle apni jagah ke aas-paas thoda hilta hai aur agle particle ko dhakka deta hai. **Vibration (energy)** aage jaata hai, particle nahi.

**Galti 5: "Sound hawa mein paani se zyada tez chalti hai"**

Ulta hai. Sound **solids mein sabse tez, liquids mein usse kam, aur gases mein sabse dheemi** chalti hai. (Numbers Topic 4 mein.)

## Quick Recap

- **Sound** energy ka wo roop hai jo kaan mein sunne ka ehsaas paida karta hai.
- Sound **vibrate karti cheez** se paida hoti hai. **Vibration** = mean position ke aas-paas to-and-fro motion. Vibration ruka, to sound ruki.
- **Demonstrations:** tuning fork aur paani ya ping-pong ball, ghitar ki taar ya ruler, gale par haath, rubber band, ghanti, dholak par chawal ke dane, speaker par balloon.
- **Sources:** vocal cords, bansuri ka air column, taarein, khaal (drum), machhar aur madhumakkhi ke pankh.
- Sound ko safar ke liye **material medium** chahiye (solid, liquid ya gas). **Vacuum** mein wo nahi chalti: **bell jar** experiment.
- Chaand par astronaut **radio** (electromagnetic waves) se baat karte hain.
- Speed: **solid > liquid > gas** (table Topic 4 mein).
- Sound source se kaan tak **layer dar layer vibration** ke roop mein pahunchti hai: particle khud source se kaan tak nahi aate.
- **Light ko medium nahi chahiye** aur wo bahut tez hai: bijli pehle dikhti hai, garaj baad mein sunai deti hai.
- **Music** sukoon dene wali, **noise** pareshaan karne wali sound hai.
- Kahani: dholak ki khaal vibrate kare to dane uchhalte hain aur awaaz aati hai; hatheli rakhte hi dono band.
