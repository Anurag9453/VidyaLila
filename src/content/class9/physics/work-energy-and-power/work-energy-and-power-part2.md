# Topic 2: Positive, Negative aur Zero Work

Pichhle topic mein humne seekha ki physics mein **work** tab hota hai jab (1) cheez par koi **force** lage aur (2) cheez **displace** ho, wo bhi force ki disha mein. Tab **W = F × s**, jahan F newton (N) mein, s metre (m) mein, aur W **joule (J)** mein. Wahan humne force aur displacement ko ek hi line mein, ek hi taraf liya tha. Ab sawal ye hai: agar force displacement ki **ulti** taraf ho? Ya **aade (tirchhe 90° par)** ho? Kya tab bhi work hoga? Aur uska sign kya hoga? Is topic mein yehi dekhenge.

**Is poore topic mein g = 10 m/s² liya gaya hai**, to 1 kg ka weight (gravity ka force) 10 N hota hai.

## Part A: Teen cases, teen sign

Work ka sign is baat par depend karta hai ki **jis force ke work ki baat kar rahe ho, wo displacement ki disha ke hisaab se kis taraf hai.** Teen hi cases hote hain.

### Case 1: Force aur displacement ek hi disha mein: positive work

Force cheez ko usi taraf dhakel raha hai jis taraf wo ja rahi hai. Force **madad** kar raha hai.

> **W = + F × s**

Examples:
- Tum trolley ko aage dhakelte ho aur trolley aage jaati hai.
- Gravity ek girte hue patthar par: gravity neeche kheenchti hai, patthar bhi neeche ja raha hai.
- Bucket ko kuein se upar kheenchte waqt rassi ka (haath ka) force upar, bucket bhi upar.

### Case 2: Force displacement ki ulti disha mein: negative work

Force cheez ki chaal ke **khilaaf** lag raha hai.

> **W = − F × s**

Examples:
- Farsh par phisalte block par **friction**: block aage jaa raha hai, friction peeche ko.
- Upar jaati gend par **gravity**: gend upar, gravity neeche.
- Car par **brake** ka force: car aage, brake ka force peeche.
- Load ko **neeche utarte waqt** haath ka force: haath upar ko force lagata hai, load neeche ja raha hai. To haath ka work **negative**, aur gravity ka work **positive** (gravity neeche, load bhi neeche).

### Case 3: Force displacement se 90° par: zero work

Agar force cheez ki chaal ke **seedhe aade (right angle)** par hai, to wo force cheez ko na aage dhakelta hai na peeche rokta hai. Uska work **zero** hai.

Examples:
- **Level sadak par chalti car par gravity**: gravity seedhi neeche, car seedhi aage. 90° ka angle, work 0.
- **Waiter ek tray ko horizontally le jaa raha hai**: tray ko sambhalne ka force upar ko, waiter aage ko chal raha hai. Work 0.
- **Block ke neeche ka normal force**: table block ko seedhi upar dhakelta hai, block table par aage sarakta hai. Work 0.
- **Rassi se ghumayi gayi gend**: [motion-part7.md](../motion/motion-part7.md) mein dekha tha ki circular motion mein centre ki taraf wala force chaal se seedhe aade (90° par) hota hai. Rassi ka tension is force ka kaam karta hai, to uska work 0.

### Case 3 ka jodidaar: displacement zero hai to bhi work zero

Pichhle topic ka niyam yaad karo: **displacement nahi, to work nahi.** Chahe force kitna bhi bada ho.
- Barbell ko sir ke upar pakde rakhna (barbell ek jagah ruka hai).
- Diwar ko poori taaqat se dhakelna (diwar hili hi nahi).

Dono mein force lag raha hai, par s = 0 hai, to W = F × 0 = **0 J**.

### Teeno cases ek table mein

Angle yahan sirf "force ki disha aur chaal ki disha ke beech ka kona" hai. Is kone se koi formula nahi banega, sirf teen khaas values yaad rakho.

| Case | Force aur chaal | Angle between them | Work ka sign | Formula |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Force →, chaal → (ek hi taraf) | 0° | Positive | W = + F × s |
| 2 | Force ←, chaal → (ulti taraf) | 180° | Negative | W = − F × s |
| 3 | Force ↑, chaal → (seedhe aade) | 90° | Zero | W = 0 |
| 4 | Koi bhi force, chaal hi nahi (s = 0) | Koi angle nahi | Zero | W = F × 0 = 0 |

Teeno cases ko ek figure mein dekho (neeche box ka "chaal" hamesha daayein ko hai):

```
 Case 1: positive             Case 2: negative            Case 3: zero

     F ──────>                    <────── F                     ^ F
   ┌───┐ ──────> chaal          ┌───┐ ──────> chaal         ┌───┐ ──────> chaal
   └───┘                        └───┘                       └───┘
 angle 0°                     angle 180°                  angle 90°
 W = + F s                    W = − F s                   W = 0
```

> **Figure 2.1** – Force aur chaal ka angle 0° ho to work positive, 180° ho to negative, aur 90° ho to zero.

### Negative work ka matlab "kaam nahi hua" nahi hai

"Negative" sunke lagta hai ki kuch hua hi nahi. Ye galat hai. Negative work ka matlab hai ki **force ne cheez ki chaal ke khilaaf kaam kiya**: wo force cheez ki chaal se kuch **chheen raha** hai. Aage ke topics mein hum dekhenge ki wo "kuch" kya hai. Abhi itna teaser: jis cheez par negative work hota hai wo **dheemi** hone lagti hai, aur jis par positive work hota hai wo **tez** hone lagti hai. Bas itna yaad rakho, calculation hum baad mein karenge.

### Sign force ke hisaab se badalta hai

Ek hi cheez par alag-alag forces ke work ke sign alag ho sakte hain. Jab bag ko haath se upar uthaya jata hai: haath ka force upar hai aur bag bhi upar ja raha hai, to **haath ka work positive**. Par usi bag par **gravity** neeche kheench rahi hai, to **gravity ka work negative**. Isliye hamesha poochho: **"kis force ka work?"**

Aur ek hi force kisi ek cheez par positive work kar sakta hai aur kisi doosri cheez par negative. Jaise ek aadmi crate ko aage dhakelta hai: aadmi ka force **crate par** aage ko, crate bhi aage, to **positive work**. Par newton ke niyam ke hisaab se crate bhi aadmi par utna hi force **peeche ko** lagata hai, aur aadmi aage chal raha hai: to crate ka force **aadmi par negative work** karta hai. (Ye action-reaction wali baat Force chapter mein dekh chuke ho, yahan sirf iska sign dekhna hai.)

## Part B: Solved examples

**Example 1 (gravity, patthar gir raha hai):** 2 kg ka patthar 5 m neeche girta hai. Gravity ka work?

- Gravity ka force F = m × g = 2 kg × 10 m/s² = 20 N, neeche ki taraf.
- Displacement s = 5 m, neeche ki taraf. Force aur displacement **ek hi disha** mein, to work **positive**.
- W = + F × s = + 20 N × 5 m = **+100 J**

**Jaanch:** 20 N × 5 m = 100 J, aur dono neeche ko hain, to sign + ✅

**Example 2 (gravity, wahi patthar upar jaa raha hai):** Wahi 2 kg ka patthar ab 5 m **upar** jaata hai. Gravity ka work?

- F = 2 kg × 10 m/s² = 20 N, neeche. Displacement 5 m, upar. **Ulti disha** hai, to work **negative**.
- W = − F × s = − 20 N × 5 m = **−100 J**

**Jaanch:** Example 1 se magnitude wahi 100 J, bas disha ulti, to sign ulta ✅

**Example 3 (friction):** Ek box 4 m tak farsh par phisalta hai. Friction ka force 10 N hai. Friction ka work?

- Friction box ki chaal ke **ulta** lagta hai, to work negative.
- W = − F × s = − 10 N × 4 m = **−40 J**

**Jaanch:** 10 N × 4 m = 40 J, ulti disha, to −40 J ✅

**Example 4 (bag haath se uthaya: haath aur gravity dono):** 3 kg ka bag 2 m ki unchai tak **constant speed** se (ek-si chaal se) uthaya jata hai. Haath ka work, gravity ka work aur dono ko jodkar kitna?

Constant speed ka matlab hai ki bag ko na tez ho raha hai na dheema, to haath ka force bag ke weight ke **barabar** hota hai.

- Gravity ka force = 3 kg × 10 m/s² = 30 N, neeche. Haath ka force bhi = 30 N, upar.
- Haath ka work = + F × s = + 30 N × 2 m = **+60 J**  (force upar, displacement upar)
- Gravity ka work = − F × s = − 30 N × 2 m = **−60 J**  (force neeche, displacement upar)
- Dono ka jod = + 60 J + (− 60 J) = **0 J**

**Jaanch:** 30 N × 2 m = 60 J, aur +60 − 60 = 0 ✅. Bag ki chaal badli hi nahi, aur dono forces ke work ka jod zero aaya, ye baat saath-saath chalti hai (iska poora matlab agle topics mein khulega).

**Example 5 (car ke brake):** Ek car par brake ka force 2000 N hai aur wo 10 m mein ruk jaati hai. Brake ka work?

- Brake ka force car ki chaal ke **ulta** hai, to negative.
- W = − F × s = − 2000 N × 10 m = **−20000 J**

**Jaanch:** 2000 N × 10 m = 20000 J, ulti disha, to −20000 J ✅

**Example 6 (coolie, level platform):** Ek coolie sir par 20 kg ka saman rakhe platform par 50 m seedha chalta hai. Uske sir ka load par force aur uska work?

- Load ko sambhalne ke liye sir ka force load ke weight ke barabar, **seedhe upar**: F = 20 kg × 10 m/s² = 200 N.
- Chaal **horizontal** (aage ki taraf) hai, force vertical (upar). Dono **90° par**, to work **zero**.
- W = **0 J**

**Jaanch:** Sir ke force ka koi hissa chaal ki disha mein nahi hai. Agar (galat tarah se) 200 N × 50 m = 10000 J nikalte to wo galat hota, kyunki force aur displacement ek hi line mein nahi hain ✅

**Example 7 (rassi ghumati gend):** Rassi se bandha patthar ek circle mein ghoomta hai, aur rassi ka tension 15 N hai. Patthar ek poora chakkar lagata hai. Rassi ka work?

- Har pal par tension centre ki taraf, patthar ki chaal circle ki tangent (seedhe aade) par: **90° ka angle** har pal.
- Har chhote hisse mein work 0, to poore chakkar mein bhi **W = 0 J**.
- (Dhyan do: yahan W = 0 ki wajah har pal ka 90° wala angle hai. Sirf "patthar wapas wahin aa gaya" kehna kaafi nahi hota.)

**Jaanch:** Tension 15 N bada hai, par 90° par hone se uska work 0 J hi rehta hai ✅

**Example 8 (aadmi aur crate):** Ek aadmi 80 N ke force se crate ko aage dhakelta hai, aur dono saath-saath 3 m aage jaate hain. Aadmi ka crate par work aur crate ka aadmi par work?

- Aadmi ka crate par force aage, crate bhi aage: W = + 80 N × 3 m = **+240 J**
- Crate ka aadmi par force peeche (80 N), aadmi aage 3 m: W = − 80 N × 3 m = **−240 J**

**Jaanch:** Magnitude dono ka 240 J, disha ke hisaab se sign ulte ✅

**Example 9 (trolley dhakelna):** Ek bachhe ne 50 N ke force se trolley ko 6 m aage dhakela. Uska work?

- Force aur displacement ek hi disha mein, to positive.
- W = + 50 N × 6 m = **+300 J**

**Jaanch:** 50 × 6 = 300, sign + ✅

## Part C: Inaaya ka barbell, ab numbers ke saath

Barbell: m = 40 kg, to weight = 40 kg × 10 m/s² = **400 N**. Unchai h = 1.5 m.

**Stage 1: Barbell ko upar uthana (slowly, ek-si chaal se).** Lifting force 400 N upar, displacement 1.5 m upar.

- Lifting force ka work = + 400 N × 1.5 m = **+600 J**
- Gravity ka work = − 400 N × 1.5 m = **−600 J**

**Stage 2: Barbell ko sir ke upar 10 s pakde rakhna.** Inaaya 400 N ka force laga rahi hai, par displacement 0 m hai.

- Work = 400 N × 0 m = **0 J**

Chahe haath kaanp rahe ho aur paseena aa raha ho, physics ke hisaab se **koi work nahi**. (Muscle andar se thak rahi hai, kyunki wo lagatar kheench-dheel kar rahi hai, par barbell ko displace nahi kar rahi.)

**Stage 3: Barbell ko 1.5 m neeche dheere se laana.** Barbell neeche ja raha hai, lifting force abhi bhi upar (400 N) hai, kyunki barbell ko girne se rokna hai.

- Lifting force ka work = − 400 N × 1.5 m = **−600 J** (force upar, displacement neeche)
- Gravity ka work = + 400 N × 1.5 m = **+600 J** (force neeche, displacement neeche)

| Stage | Displacement | Lifting force ka work | Gravity ka work |
|---|---|---|---|
| Upar uthana | 1.5 m upar | + 600 J | − 600 J |
| Pakde rakhna (10 s) | 0 m | 0 J | 0 J |
| Neeche laana | 1.5 m neeche | − 600 J | + 600 J |

**Jaanch:** 400 N × 1.5 m = 600 J ✅. Har stage mein dono forces ke work ka jod 0 J hai (+600 − 600, 0 + 0, −600 + 600) ✅.

> Ms. Kaur ne kaha, "Dekha Inaaya? Upar uthate waqt tumhara work positive, pakde rakhte waqt zero, aur neeche laate waqt negative. Physics yehi hisaab rakhti hai."

Ab saaf ho gaya ki **thakaan ya mehnat** ka feel aur **physics ka work** ek cheez nahi hain.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: "Negative work" ka matlab "work nahi hua" ya "kisi negative aadmi ne kiya"**

Nahi. Negative work ka matlab hai ki force **chaal ke khilaaf** hai. Brake ka work −20000 J hai: brake ne kaam kiya, aur car ko roka. "Kaam nahi hua" to 0 J ka matlab hota hai.

**Galti 2: "Friction kuch kar raha hai, isliye uska work positive hoga"**

Friction hamesha chalti cheez ke **ulta** lagta hai, to uska work negative hota hai. Example 3: −40 J, +40 J nahi.

**Galti 3: Sign ko dekhna lekin ye bhulna ki kis force ka work pooch rahe ho**

Example 4 mein bag uthate waqt haath ka work +60 J aur gravity ka −60 J tha: ek hi bag, alag-alag signs. Hamesha pehle likho: "kis force ka work?"

**Galti 4: Kul (jod) work zero hai to "kisi ne work nahi kiya" kehna**

Example 4 mein dono ka jod 0 J tha, par haath ka work +60 J aur gravity ka −60 J asli the. Jod zero hone se alag-alag forces ke work gayab nahi ho jate.

**Galti 5: Bada force hai to bada work hoga**

Coolie ka sir 200 N ka force laga raha tha, par wo force chaal se 90° par tha, to work **0 J**. Force bada ho par seedhe aade ho, to work zero.

## Quick Recap

- **Case 1:** force aur displacement ek hi disha mein (angle 0°): **positive work**, W = + F × s.
- **Case 2:** force displacement ki ulti disha mein (angle 180°): **negative work**, W = − F × s.
- **Case 3:** force displacement se 90° par: **zero work**. Displacement zero ho to bhi **zero work** (barbell pakde rakhna, diwar dhakelna).
- Level sadak par gravity, tray ke saath chalna, normal force, aur circle mein tension: sab 90° wale cases hain, to work 0.
- Sign **force ke hisaab se** badalta hai: bag uthate waqt haath +, gravity −. Neeche laate waqt haath −, gravity +.
- Negative work kaam ka na hona nahi hai: force chaal ke khilaaf hai. (Teaser: positive work se cheez tez, negative se dheemi hoti hai.)
- Inaaya ka barbell (40 kg, 1.5 m, g = 10 m/s²): uthana +600 J, pakde rakhna 0 J, neeche laana −600 J (haath ka force), gravity ka +600 J.
- Work ki unit joule (J) hi rehti hai, sirf sign badalta hai.

2. The same 5 kg stone is thrown upward and rises 4 m. Find the work done by gravity. Why is the sign different from Question 1?

3. A block slides 6 m on a rough floor. The friction force is 8 N. Find the work done by friction.

6. A porter carries a 30 kg load on his head and walks 80 m along a level platform. What is the work done by the force of his head on the load? Explain in one line.

8. State True or False:
   (a) Negative work means no work is done.
   (b) A person pushing a wall for ten minutes does zero work on the wall.
   (c) Friction on a sliding block does positive work.
   (d) A large force at 90° to the motion does a large amount of work.

9. A 20 kg bucket is lowered slowly into a well through 5 m. Find the work done by (a) the rope (lifting force), (b) gravity. Take g = 10 m/s².

### Answers

1. F = m × g = 5 kg × 10 m/s² = 50 N, neeche. Displacement 4 m neeche, ek hi disha: W = + 50 N × 4 m = **+200 J**.
   Jaanch: 50 × 4 = 200 ✅

2. Force 50 N neeche, displacement 4 m upar: ulti disha, to W = − 50 N × 4 m = **−200 J**. Sign isliye alag hai ki Q1 mein force aur displacement ek hi disha mein the, Q2 mein ulti disha mein.
   Jaanch: magnitude 200 J dono mein ✅

3. Friction ulta lagta hai: W = − 8 N × 6 m = **−48 J**.
   Jaanch: 8 × 6 = 48 ✅

4. Weight = 2 kg × 10 m/s² = 20 N. (a) Lifting force 20 N upar, displacement 3 m upar: W = + 20 N × 3 m = **+60 J**. (b) Gravity: W = − 20 N × 3 m = **−60 J**. (c) Jod = 60 + (−60) = **0 J**.
   Jaanch: 20 × 3 = 60 ✅

5. Brake ka force ulta hai: W = − 1500 N × 20 m = **−30000 J**.
   Jaanch: 1500 × 20 = 30000 ✅

6. Force = 30 kg × 10 m/s² = 300 N, seedhe upar; chaal horizontal hai, to angle 90°. **W = 0 J**. Force aur displacement ek doosre ke seedhe aade hain.
   Jaanch: 300 N × 80 m = 24000 J nahi lena (force aur chaal ek line mein nahi) ✅

7. Tension har pal chaal se 90° par hai, to work **0 J**, 25 N bada hone par bhi.
   Jaanch: har chhote hisse mein force chaal se 90° par, to har hisse ka work 0 J, aur jod bhi 0 J ✅

8. (a) **False** ❌. Negative work ka matlab force chaal ke khilaaf hai; no work to 0 J hai.

10. Displacement 0 m, to W = 400 N × 0 m = **0 J**. Phir bhi thakaan isliye hoti hai kyunki uski muscles andar se lagatar kheench-dheel kar rahi hain; ye physics ke "work" (force × displacement) se alag baat hai.
    Jaanch: 40 kg × 10 = 400 N, 400 × 0 = 0 ✅
