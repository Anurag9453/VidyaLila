# Topic 3: Energy Aur Uske Roop

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki work **positive, negative ya zero** ho sakta hai. Jab force aur displacement ek hi disha mein ho, to work positive hota hai, aur wo kuch **de deta hai** us cheez ko jis par work hua. Ab sawal ye hai: wo "kuch" aakhir hai kya? Cheez ko kya mil raha hai, aur jab koi cheez work karti hai to wo apne andar se kya kharch karti hai? Is "kuch" ka naam hai **energy**.

---

## Power cut ki shaam

Shaam ko achanak bijli chali gayi. Mr. Bansal ke ghar mein andhera ho gaya. Unki beti Hiya ne kaha, "Papa, kuch dikh nahi raha!"

Mr. Bansal ne mez se ek **mombatti** nikali aur jala di. Kamre mein peeli roshni phail gayi. Phir unhone ek **battery wali torch** on ki, aur uski safed kiran deewar par chamak gayi. Ab raat ka khana banana tha, to unhone **gas stove** jalaya aur uski neeli lau par patila rakh diya.

Hiya sab dekh rahi thi. Wo boli, "Papa, mombatti roshni aur garmi de rahi hai, torch roshni de rahi hai, aur stove garmi de raha hai. Ye teeno kuch na kuch **kar** rahe hain. Par inme **kya kharch ho raha hai?** Mombatti chhoti hoti ja rahi hai, torch ki battery kuch dino mein khatam ho jaati hai, aur cylinder bhi ek din khali ho jaata hai!"

Mr. Bansal muskuraye, "Bahut achha sawal hai Hiya. Is sawal ka jawab hai **energy**. Chalo, topic ke ant mein hum teeno ka jawab number ke saath nikalenge."

## Part A: Energy kya hai?

Topic 1 mein humne dekha tha ki jab force lagakar cheez ko hilate hain, to **work** hota hai. Ab sochiye: kaun work kar sakta hai? Ek thaka hua aadmi bhaari bojh nahi utha sakta, jabki taaqatwar pehalwan utha leta hai. Chalti hui cricket ball stumps ko gira deti hai, par table par rakhi hui ball nahi giraati. Pani ke upar bandha hua dam turbine ghuma deta hai.

In sabme ek baat common hai: kisi ke paas work karne ki **kshamta** hai, kisi ke paas nahi. Isi kshamta ko **energy** kehte hain.

> **Energy = work karne ki kshamta (ability).**
> Jis cheez mein energy hai, wo work kar sakti hai.

### Energy ka unit: joule

Work ka SI unit joule (J) hai. Energy bhi joule mein hi napi jaati hai. Iska ek achha karan hai: **work aur energy ek hi cheez ke do naam jaise hain**: energy ek tarah ka "bank balance" hai, aur work us balance ka lena-dena. Dono ko napne ka paimana alag hota to hisaab mushkil hota. Isliye:

> **Energy ka SI unit = joule (J)**, wahi jo work ka hai.

Bade numbers ke liye **kilojoule** (kJ) use hota hai:

> 1 kJ = 1000 J

### Energy ek scalar hai

Energy ki sirf **maatra** hoti hai, koi disha nahi. "50 J energy" kehna kaafi hai, "50 J uttar ki taraf" kehne ka koi matlab nahi. Isliye energy ek **scalar** hai.

### Energy force nahi hai, cheez bhi nahi hai

Energy koi dhakka (force) nahi hai, aur na hi koi cheez jise haath mein pakda ja sake. Wo ek **kshamta** hai, jo kisi cheez ke andar ya uski sthiti mein hoti hai. Hum energy ko seedha dekh nahi sakte: hum sirf dekhte hain ki cheez **kya kar rahi hai** (gaadi chal rahi hai, bulb chamak raha hai).

## Part B: Energy ke roop

Energy ek hi cheez hai, par wo alag-alag **roop** (forms) mein milti hai. Neeche har roop ka ek roz ka example hai.

| Energy ka roop | Kya hai | Roz ka example |
|---|---|---|
| **Mechanical energy** | Cheez ki gati (kinetic) ya uski sthiti/position (potential) se mili energy | Chalti hui cycle; ooncha rakha hua matka |
| **Heat (thermal) energy** | Garam cheez ki energy | Chai ka garam paani, stove ki lau |
| **Light energy** | Roshni ki energy | Suraj, bulb, mombatti ki roshni |
| **Sound energy** | Aawaz ki energy | Dhol ki aawaz, speaker |
| **Electrical energy** | Bijli ke taaron mein behti charge ki energy | Bijli se chalta pankha |
| **Chemical energy** | Khane, fuel ya battery ke andar chhipi energy | Roti, LPG gas, torch ka cell |
| **Nuclear energy** | Parmanu (atom) ke andar ki energy | Suraj ki energy ka source, nuclear power plant |
| **Magnetic energy** | Chumbak (magnet) ki energy | Magnet ka lohe ki cheez ko kheench lena |

Mechanical energy ke do hisse hain: **kinetic energy** (chalti hui cheez ki energy) aur **potential energy** (position ya sthiti ki wajah se energy). Inki poori kahani agle do topics mein hogi (Topic 4 aur Topic 5). Abhi bas naam yaad rakhiye.

Nuclear aur magnetic energy ke baare mein aage ki classes mein padhoge. Abhi bas ye jaan lo ki ye bhi energy ke roop hain.

> **Light aur sound bhi energy hain.** Dhoop se kapde sookhte hain (light/heat work karti hai), aur bahut tez dhamaake se khidki ke sheeshe kaanp jaate hain (sound ne sheeshe ko hilaya, yani work kiya). Jo work kar sake, wo energy hai.

> Ek naam aur: **heat** energy hai, aur **temperature** (garmi ka napa hua level) alag cheez hai. Dono ka antar aage ki class mein poori tarah samjhenge. Yahan itna yaad rakho ki dono ek nahi hain.

## Part C: Energy ek roop se doosre mein badalti hai

Sabse dilchasp baat ye hai ki energy ek roop se doosre roop mein **badal** sakti hai. Isko **energy transformation** kehte hain. Mombatti mein chemical energy (mom ke andar) heat aur light ban jaati hai. Torch mein chemical energy pehle electrical aur phir light ban jaati hai.

Hum badlav ko **tir (arrow) ki chain** se likhte hain: pehla roop → doosra roop.

### 10 jaani-pehchani cheezein aur unke energy badlav

| Number | Device ya process | Energy badlav (chain) | Samjhaao |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Electric bulb** | Electrical → light + heat | Bijli se roshni milti hai, par bulb garam bhi ho jaata hai |
| 2 | **Pankha (fan)** | Electrical → kinetic | Bijli se blades ghoomte hain |
| 3 | **Battery torch** | Chemical → electrical → light | Cell ki chemical energy bijli banti hai, bijli bulb ko jalati hai |
| 4 | **Mobile phone ka speaker** | Electrical → sound | Bijli ke signal se speaker hilta hai aur aawaz banti hai |
| 5 | **Hathoda jo nail par lagta hai** | Kinetic → heat + sound | Chalte hathode ki energy se thak ki aawaz aati hai aur nail thoda garam ho jaata hai |
| 6 | **Solar cell** | Light → electrical | Dhoop se bijli banti hai |
| 7 | **Hydroelectric power plant (bandh se bijli)** | Potential → kinetic → electrical | Oonchai par rakha paani gir kar turbine ghumata hai, turbine bijli banata hai |
| 8 | **Insaani sharir** | Chemical (khana) → kinetic + heat | Khane ki energy se hum chalte-daudte hain aur sharir garam rehta hai |
| 9 | **Kheenchi hui rubber band** | Elastic potential → kinetic | Chhodte hi rubber band jhatke se sikud kar uchhalti hai |
| 10 | **Jalti mombatti** | Chemical → heat + light | Mom jalti hai, garmi aur roshni milti hai |

Is table se ek pattern dikhta hai: **jab bhi kuch hota hai (kuch chalta hai, jalta hai, garam hota hai, aawaz karta hai), kahin na kahin energy ek roop se doosre mein badal rahi hoti hai.**

Ek chhota sa ASCII chitra torch ka:

```
  Cell            Taar            Bulb
 ┌─────┐       ┌───────┐       ┌───────┐
 │ chem│ ────> │ bijli │ ────> │ roshni│
 └─────┘       └───────┘       └───────┘
 chemical      electrical         light
```

> **Figure 3.1** – Battery torch mein energy ki chain: cell ki chemical energy → electrical energy → light energy.

(Asal mein bulb thoda garam bhi hota hai, yani kuch energy heat ban kar bhi nikal jaati hai, jaise bulb wali row mein likha hai.)

### Energy "kam" nahi hoti, roop badalti hai

Hiya ne puchha tha, "Mombatti chhoti hoti ja rahi hai, to kya energy kharch ho gayi?" Sach ye hai ki energy **na banti hai, na khatam hoti hai: sirf roop badalti hai.** Mombatti ki chemical energy heat aur light ban kar kamre mein phail gayi, gayab nahi hui. Ye bahut bada niyam hai: **energy ke sanrakshan (conservation) ka niyam**. Is par poori baat **Topic 6** mein karenge.

## Part D: Work aur energy ka rishta

Dekho kitni baar "work" aur "energy" ek hi saans mein aaye. Inka seedha rishta hai:

> **Work, energy ka transfer (ek jagah se doosri jagah jaana) hai.**

Do baatein, abhi sirf shabdon mein (formula aage ke topics mein):

1. **Jab kisi cheez par work kiya jaata hai, uski energy badhti hai.**
   Misaal: Hiya ne ek kitab farsh se utha kar shelf par rakh di. Hiya ne kitab par work kiya, aur kitab ki energy badh gayi (ab wo oonchai par hai).
2. **Jab koi cheez work karti hai, uski energy ghat jaati hai.**
   Misaal: shelf se kitab neeche gir kar farsh par rakhi ek chhoti gaadi ko dhakel deti hai: ab kitab ne work kiya aur uski energy ghati.

Yani work aur energy ki **ek hi ikai (joule)** hai, kyunki work asal mein energy ke lena-dena ka naam hai.

### Energy ko napte kaise hain?

Energy ko napne ka sabse seedha tareeka hai **work ko napna**. Agar kisi cheez ko X joule work karke kisi sthiti mein laya gaya, to uski energy X joule badh gayi. Is topic mein hum isi tareeke se numbers nikalenge.

### Sources of energy (energy ke strot)

Hamein energy kahan se milti hai? Jaanch-padtal ke baad pata chalta hai ki lagbhag sabki jad **Suraj (Sun)** hai:

- **Fossil fuels** (koyla, petrol, diesel) ancient paudhon-janwaron se bane, jinhone Suraj se energy li thi.
- **Hawa (wind)** Suraj ki garmi se zameen ke alag-alag hisson ke garam hone se chalti hai.
- **Khana** paudhe Suraj ki roshni se banate hain, aur hum paudhe ya unhe khane wale janwar khate hain.
- **Solar cell** seedhe Suraj se bijli banate hain.

Strot do tarah ke hote hain, abhi sirf naam jaan lo:
- **Renewable** (naveekaraneey): jo khatam nahi hote, jaise Suraj, hawa, behta paani.
- **Non-renewable** (ghatne wale): jo ek baar kharch ho jaaye to dobara jaldi nahi banta, jaise koyla, petrol, LPG.

Inki poori kahani Class 10 aur aage ki classes mein hai.

## Part F: Solved examples

### Example 1: Kisi cheez ko oopar uthane mein kitni energy deni padti hai?

Ek 4 kg ki cheez ko 3 m oopar uthana hai. Itni energy us cheez ko dene ke liye kitna work karna hoga? (g = 10 m/s² lo.)

Topic 1 se: work = force × displacement. Cheez ko dheere-dheere uthane ke liye force = cheez ka weight.

- Weight = m × g = 4 kg × 10 m/s² = **40 N**
- Work W = F × s = 40 N × 3 m = **120 J**

Jo work kiya, wahi energy cheez ko mili. Yani us cheez ki energy **120 J** badh gayi.

**Jaanch:** 40 N × 3 m = 120 N·m = 120 J ✅. Aur 120 J ÷ 3 m = 40 N ✅.

### Example 2: Khane ki energy vs seedhiyan chadhne ka work

Ek snack ke packet par likha hai **400 kJ**. Ek 50 kg ka insaan 3 m oonchi seedhiyan chadhta hai. Kitni baar aisa chadhne ke liye wo 400 kJ kaafi hai? (g = 10 m/s²; maan lo snack ki saari energy kaam aa jaaye. Asal mein sharir ka kaafi hissa garmi ban jaata hai.)

Pehle ek baar chadhne mein work:
- Weight = 50 kg × 10 m/s² = **500 N**
- Work = 500 N × 3 m = **1500 J**

Snack ki energy joule mein:
- 400 kJ = 400 × 1000 J = **400 000 J**

Baar ginti:
- 400 000 J ÷ 1500 J = **266.67 ≈ 267 baar**

**Jaanch:** 267 × 1500 J = 400 500 J ≈ 400 000 J ✅ (aur 266 × 1500 = 399 000 J, jo 400 000 se thoda kam hai, isliye lagbhag 267).

Matlab chhote se snack mein itni energy hoti hai ki 3 m ki seedhi 267 baar chadh sakte ho!

### Example 3: Energy ke badlav ki chain likho

Neeche ke devices ki energy chain likho:

(a) **Electric iron (press)**: Electrical → heat
(b) **Dhol bajta hua**: Kinetic (haath ki gati) → sound
(c) **Cell phone charge hote waqt**: Electrical → chemical (battery mein jama)
(d) **Pedal-wali cycle ki dynamo wali lamp**: Kinetic → electrical → light

Ye sab chain batati hain ki energy ek roop se doosre roop mein gayi, aur kisi mein bhi gayab nahi hui.

### Example 4: kJ, J aur kcal ke badlav

(a) 0.5 kJ ko joule mein badlo.
   - 0.5 kJ = 0.5 × 1000 J = **500 J**

(b) Ek laddoo ki energy 180 kcal hai. Isko kJ mein badlo (1 kcal ≈ 4.2 kJ).
   - 180 kcal × 4.2 kJ/kcal = **756 kJ**

(c) Ek khane ki cheez ki energy 840 kJ hai. Kcal mein kitni?
   - 840 kJ ÷ 4.2 kJ/kcal = **200 kcal**

**Jaanch:** (a) 500 J ÷ 1000 = 0.5 kJ ✅. (b) 756 ÷ 4.2 = 180 ✅. (c) 200 × 4.2 = 840 ✅.

### Example 5: 60 W ka pankha 10 second mein kitni energy use karta hai?

Ek 60 W ka pankha har second mein **60 J** electrical energy use karta hai (W ka matlab "joule per second": power ke baare mein Topic 7).

- 10 s mein energy = 60 J/s × 10 s = **600 J**

**Jaanch:** 600 J ÷ 10 s = 60 J/s ✅. Aur 1 second mein 60 J, to 10 second mein 10 guna = 600 J ✅.

### Example 6: 100 W ka bulb 5 second mein

Bulb har second mein 100 J lega, to 5 s mein:
- 100 J/s × 5 s = **500 J**

**Jaanch:** 500 J ÷ 5 s = 100 J/s ✅.

### Example 7: 12 situations, kaunsi energy?

| Situation | Mukhya energy (roop) |
|---|---|
| 1. Chalti hui cycle | Kinetic (mechanical) |
| 2. Khichi hui kamaan (dhanush) | Elastic potential (mechanical) |
| 3. Garam chai | Heat |
| 4. Tube light jalti hui | Light (aur thodi heat) |
| 5. Radio se aati aawaz | Sound |
| 6. Pankhe ke taaron mein behti bijli | Electrical |
| 7. LPG cylinder ki gas | Chemical |
| 8. Tower par chadha hua pani ka tank (pani oonchai par) | Gravitational potential (mechanical) |
| 9. Suraj ki dhoop | Light aur heat |
| 10. Chumbak jo lohe ki keel ko kheench raha hai | Magnetic |
| 11. Dhamaake ki tez aawaz | Sound |
| 12. Nuclear power plant ka fuel (uranium) | Nuclear |

### Example 8: Kahani ka hal

Hiya ka sawal tha: mombatti, torch aur stove mein kya kharch ho raha hai? Hiya aur Mr. Bansal ne milkar chain likhi:

- **Mombatti:** Chemical (mom) → heat + light. Mom ki chemical energy garmi aur roshni banti hai.
- **Torch:** Chemical (cell) → electrical → light. Cell ki chemical energy bijli banti hai, aur bijli bulb ko jalati hai. (Thodi heat bhi banti hai.)
- **Gas stove:** Chemical (LPG) → heat + light. Gas ki chemical energy garmi aur lau ki roshni ban gayi.

Hiya boli, "Yani teeno mein **chemical energy** ek roop se doosre mein badal rahi hai." Mr. Bansal ne kaha, "Bilkul. Aur energy gayab nahi ho rahi, bas roop badal rahi hai: usi ko aage ke topic mein dekhenge."

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **"Energy ek force hai ya koi cheez hai."**
   Galat. Energy kshamta hai, force nahi. Force newton (N) mein, energy joule (J) mein napi jaati hai.

2. **"Energy 'kharch' ho kar khatam ho jaati hai."**
   Aam bolchaal mein hum kehte hain "bijli kharch hui", par asal mein energy sirf **roop badalti hai**. Mombatti ki chemical energy gayab nahi hui, wo heat aur light ban gayi. (Topic 6 mein poora.)

3. **Heat aur temperature ko ek samajhna.**
   Dono alag hain. Heat energy hai, temperature batata hai ki cheez kitni garam hai. Dono ka antar aage ki class mein.

4. **"Light aur sound energy nahi hain."**
   Galat. Dono energy ke roop hain: dhoop se kapde sookhte hain, tez aawaz se sheeshe kaanp jaate hain.

5. **"Energy ka unit watt hai."**
   Galat. Energy ka SI unit **joule (J)** hai. Watt (W) alag cheez hai: wo power ka unit hai (Topic 7).
   Jaanch: 60 W pankha 10 s mein 600 **J** energy use karta hai: energy joule mein, power watt mein ✅.

## Quick Recap

- **Energy = work karne ki kshamta.** Jis cheez mein energy hai, wo work kar sakti hai.
- SI unit **joule (J)**, wahi jo work ka hai. 1 kJ = 1000 J.
- Energy ek **scalar** hai (sirf maatra, disha nahi). Wo force ya cheez nahi hai.
- Roop: mechanical (kinetic + potential), heat, light, sound, electrical, chemical, nuclear, magnetic.
- Energy ek roop se doosre mein badal sakti hai (energy transformation), jaise bulb: electrical → light + heat.
- Energy na banti hai na khatam hoti hai, sirf roop badalti hai (Topic 6 mein poora).
- Work energy ka transfer hai: work kiye jaane par cheez ki energy badhti hai, aur work karne par ghat-ti hai.
- Energy napne ka tareeka: work nikalna (joule).
- Zyaadatar energy ka strot **Suraj** hai. Strot renewable ya non-renewable ho sakte hain.
- 1 kcal ≈ 4.2 kJ (lagbhag).
