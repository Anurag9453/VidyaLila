# Topic 5: Potential Energy

Pichhle topic mein humne **kinetic energy** dekhi: wo energy jo cheez ko uski **gati (motion)** ki wajah se milti hai. Chalti gaadi, udta ball, behta paani: sabme KE hoti hai. Lekin ek sawal baaki hai: kya ek **ruki hui** cheez mein bhi kaam karne ki taaqat ho sakti hai? Haan, ho sakti hai. Is topic mein hum dekhenge **position ki energy**: yaani cheez ke **kahan rakhe hone** se milne wali energy. Ise **potential energy** kehte hain.

Is poore topic mein hum **g = 10 m/s²** lenge (aasan arithmetic ke liye). Jahan 9.8 lenge, wahan saaf likh denge. g aur weight ki baat [gravitation-part5.md](../gravitation/gravitation-part5.md) mein ho chuki hai, aur free fall ki baat [gravitation-part4.md](../gravitation/gravitation-part4.md) mein.

## Terrace par tanki ka sawal

Shaam ko Tejal apartment ki terrace par kabootar ko daana dene aayi thi. Wahi caretaker Mr. Mishra ek bade **overhead tank** ke paas khade the. Neeche ek motor "ghurrr..." karke chal rahi thi aur pipe se paani **upar tank mein** chadha rahi thi.

Tejal ne pooch liya, "Mishra uncle, ye motor itni der se kya kar rahi hai?"

Mr. Mishra bole, "Beta, neeche ki tanki se paani uthakar yahan, **12 metre ooncha**, is tank mein bhar rahi hai. Kaafi mehnat ka kaam hai."

Kuch der baad motor band ho gayi. Tank bhar gaya. Ab paani tank mein **chupchaap baitha** tha: na hilta, na behta. Tejal ne tank ko ghoor kar dekha, "Ab to isme kuch ho hi nahi raha."

Tabhi neeche kisi ne **tap khola**. Mr. Mishra ne kaha, "Suno." Neeche se paani ki **zor se "sssshhh"** ki awaaz aayi: paani tez dhaar mein nikal raha tha. Tejal hairan ho gayi, "Uncle, tank mein to paani **hila bhi nahi** tha. Phir ye force aur ye tezi aayi kahan se?"

Mr. Mishra muskuraye, "Yahi to sawal hai, Tejal. Paani ne tank mein baithe-baithe ek cheez **jama** kar li thi: ek 'chupi hui taaqat'. Chal, ise number ke saath samajhte hain." Is kahani ko hum topic ke beech mein solve karenge.

## Part A: Potential energy kya hoti hai

Pichhle topics mein humne dekha tha ki **energy = kaam karne ki kshamta (capacity)**. Kinetic energy wo hai jo **chalne** se aati hai. Ab socho: ek bhaari gamla chhat ki muni (parapet) par rakha hai. Wo ruka hua hai, KE zero hai. Par agar wo gir jaye to neeche rakhi cheez ko tod sakta hai, yaani **kaam kar sakta hai**. To uske paas koi na koi energy zaroor hai.

> **Potential energy (PE) = wo energy jo kisi cheez ke paas uski position (jagah) ya condition (haalat) ki wajah se hoti hai.**

"Potential" ka matlab hai **"sambhavna"**: abhi wo energy dikh nahi rahi, par mauka milte hi kaam mein badal sakti hai. Ye teen tarah ki aam misaalein hain:

| Tarah | Position ya condition | Misaal |
|---|---|---|
| **Gravitational PE** | Cheez zameen se **upar** rakhi hai | Tank ka paani, chhat par gamla, dam ka paani |
| **Elastic PE** | Cheez **kheenchi ya dabayi** gayi hai | Kamaan (bow) ki kheenchi doori, dabi hui spring, khichi hui catapult |
| **Chemical PE** | Cheez ke **andar** rasayanik roop mein energy jama hai | Petrol, LPG, khana, battery |

Elastic aur chemical PE ke baare mein hum yahan sirf naam aur misaal le rahe hain: inka detail aage ki padhai mein. Is topic ka **sitara** hai gravitational PE.

## Part B: Gravitational PE ka formula (m g h)

Ab Tejal ka sawal number mein badlte hain. Maan lo ek vastu ko **mass m** ke saath **height h** tak upar uthana hai.

**Step 1.** Vastu ko upar uthane ke liye hamein **gravity ke khilaf** force lagana padta hai. Agar hum use **ek-si speed** se (dheere-dheere, bina tez kiye) uthayein, to hamara force vastu ke **weight** ke barabar hota hai (Topic 1 aur 2 mein dekha tha ki uthane wala force aur displacement dono upar ki taraf hain, to kaam positive hai).

> Weight = m × g

**Step 2.** Kaam = force × displacement (Topic 1). Yahan force = m g aur displacement = h:

> W = F × s = (m g) × h

**Step 3.** Ye kaam vastu mein **jama** ho jata hai, jaise gullak mein paise. Isi jama kaam ko hum **gravitational potential energy** kehte hain:

> **PE = m × g × h**

**Unit check:** m kg mein, g m/s² mein, h m mein:

> kg × m/s² × m = (kg × m/s²) × m = N × m = **J** ✅

Yani PE ka unit bhi **joule (J)** hai, kaam wala hi unit.

**Seedhi baat:** PE teen cheezon par nirbhar karti hai:
- **m** (mass): jitni bhaari cheez, utni zyada PE.
- **h** (height): jitna oopar, utni zyada PE.
- **g** (gravity): jahan gravity zyada, wahan utni zyada PE (Moon par kam).

Ek chhota sketch dekhte hain:

```
        ┌───────┐
        │  m    │   <-- vastu, mass m
        └───────┘
           :   ^
           :   :  weight = m g (neeche ki taraf)
        h  :   :  hamara force = m g (upar ki taraf)
           :   :
   --------:---------------------  reference level (PE = 0)
```

> **Figure 5.1** – Vastu ko reference level se h oonchai tak ek-si speed se uthane ke liye force m g lagta hai. Kiya gaya kaam W = m g h hi PE ban jata hai.

## Part C: Reference level: "kahan se naapein?"

Ek zaroori sawal: "height" naapein to **kahan se**? Zameen se, table se, ya kamre ke farsh se? Jawab: **jahan se aap chahein**. Us level ko hum **reference level (zero level)** kehte hain aur wahan PE = 0 maan lete hain.

Ek bahut kaam ki baat: **PE ki value reference level ke saath badal sakti hai, lekin do jagah ki PE ka antar (difference) kabhi nahi badalta.** Aur isi antar ke barabar kaam hum us vastu ko ek jagah se doosri jagah le jaane mein karte hain. Isliye physics mein hamesha **antar** (difference) hi sachmuch zaroori hota hai.

Neeche sketch mein ek kitaab hai jo table se 0.7 m upar aur zameen se 1.5 m upar hai:

```
                 ┌────────┐
                 │ kitaab │  <-- 1.5 m (zameen se), 0.7 m (table se)
                 └────────┘
            ┌──────────────────┐  table ka upar wala surface: zameen se 0.8 m
            └──┬────────────┬──┘
               │            │
               │            │
   ======================================  zameen (farsh)
```

> **Figure 5.2** – Kitaab ki PE zameen se napo to height 1.5 m, table se napo to 0.7 m. Table ka surface zameen se 0.8 m upar hai (0.8 + 0.7 = 1.5).

## Part D: Solved examples (m, g, h se PE)

**Example 1 (PE nikalna):** 3 kg ki ek gend ko zameen se 5 m ki oonchai par rakha hai. Zameen reference level hai. Uski PE?

- m = 3 kg, g = 10 m/s², h = 5 m
- PE = m × g × h = 3 kg × 10 m/s² × 5 m = 30 N × 5 m = **150 J**

**Jaanch:** 150 J ÷ (10 m/s² × 5 m) = 150 ÷ 50 = 3 kg ✅

**Example 2 (h nikalna):** 4 kg ki vastu ki PE 600 J hai (zameen se). Wo kitni oonchai par hai?

- PE = m g h, to h = PE ÷ (m × g)
- h = 600 J ÷ (4 kg × 10 m/s²) = 600 J ÷ 40 N = **15 m**

**Jaanch:** 4 kg × 10 m/s² × 15 m = 40 N × 15 m = 600 J ✅

**Example 3 (m nikalna):** Ek vastu 6 m ki oonchai par hai aur uski PE 900 J hai. Uska mass?

- m = PE ÷ (g × h) = 900 J ÷ (10 m/s² × 6 m) = 900 J ÷ 60 m²/s² = **15 kg**

**Jaanch:** 15 kg × 10 m/s² × 6 m = 150 N × 6 m = 900 J ✅

**Example 4 (reference level badalna):** 2 kg ki ek kitaab zameen se 1.5 m oonchai par hai. Table ka surface zameen se 0.8 m ooncha hai (yaani kitaab table se 0.7 m upar hai). Kitaab ki PE (a) zameen ke hisaab se, (b) table ke hisaab se? Aur table se kitaab tak uthane mein kitna kaam hua?

- (a) Zameen reference: PE = 2 kg × 10 m/s² × 1.5 m = 20 N × 1.5 m = **30 J**
- (b) Table reference: PE = 2 kg × 10 m/s² × 0.7 m = 20 N × 0.7 m = **14 J**
- Table ke surface ki PE zameen se: 20 N × 0.8 m = 16 J. Antar = 30 J − 16 J = **14 J**.
- Table se kitaab tak uthane mein kaam W = m g h = 20 N × 0.7 m = **14 J**.

**Jaanch:** zameen ke hisaab se 30 J aur table ke hisaab se 14 J **alag** hain, par table se kitaab tak ka antar 30 − 16 = 14 J = kiya gaya kaam ✅. Reference level badla, par kaam wahi raha.

> Ek line: agar vastu reference level se **neeche** ho (jaise zameen se 2 m gehre gaddhe mein 3 kg: PE = 3 × 10 × (−2) = −60 J), to PE **negative** aati hai. Isme ghabrane ki baat nahi, bas reference ke neeche hone ka matlab hai.

**Example 5 (seedhi chadhne wale ka kaam):** 60 kg ka ek aadmi 3 m oonchi seedhiyan (staircase) chadhta hai. Gravity ke khilaf usne kitna kaam kiya?

- Weight = 60 kg × 10 m/s² = 600 N
- W = m g h = 600 N × 3 m = **1800 J**
- Ye usse mili PE ke barabar hai: PE = **1800 J** (neeche ki manzil ke hisaab se).

**Jaanch:** 1800 J ÷ 3 m = 600 N = 60 kg × 10 ✅

**Example 6 (height double hone par PE):** 5 kg ki vastu (a) 100 m, (b) 200 m oonchai par. PE?

- (a) PE = 5 kg × 10 m/s² × 100 m = 50 N × 100 m = **5000 J**
- (b) PE = 5 kg × 10 m/s² × 200 m = 50 N × 200 m = **10000 J**

**Jaanch:** 10000 ÷ 5000 = 2, aur 200 ÷ 100 = 2 ✅. Height double to PE double.

**Example 7 (20 eent):** Ek mazdoor 20 eenton ko, har eent 2 kg ki, 3 m oonchi chhat par pahunchata hai. Eenton ne kitni PE paayi?

- Kul mass = 20 × 2 kg = 40 kg
- PE = m g h = 40 kg × 10 m/s² × 3 m = 400 N × 3 m = **1200 J**

**Jaanch:** ek eent ke liye 2 × 10 × 3 = 60 J, aur 20 × 60 J = 1200 J ✅

**Example 8 (imaarat ka gend):** 0.5 kg ki gend 20 m oonchi imaarat ki chhat par hai. (a) Zameen ke hisaab se PE? (b) Imaarat ki aadhi oonchai (beech) par hone par PE?

- (a) h = 20 m: PE = 0.5 kg × 10 m/s² × 20 m = 5 N × 20 m = **100 J**
- (b) h = 10 m: PE = 0.5 kg × 10 m/s² × 10 m = 5 N × 10 m = **50 J**

**Jaanch:** beech ki height aadhi hai, to PE bhi aadhi: 100 ÷ 2 = 50 J ✅

**Example 9 (do manzilon ka antar):** Ek imaarat mein ek manzil se doosri manzil tak ki oonchai 3 m hai. 80 kg ka ek aadmi neeche ki manzil se ek upar ki manzil par jata hai. Uski PE kitni badhi?

- Weight = 80 kg × 10 m/s² = 800 N
- PE ka antar = m g h = 800 N × 3 m = **2400 J**

**Jaanch:** 2400 J ÷ 800 N = 3 m ✅. Ye antar is par nirbhar nahi ki aap zameen se napo ya pehli manzil se.

## Part E: Alag jagah, alag g: Moon par PE

PE mein **g** bhi hai, isliye gravity badle to PE bhi badlegi. Chand par g lagbhag **1.6 m/s²** hai (Earth par hamne 10 liya hai).

**Example 10 (Earth aur Moon ka muqabla):** 10 kg ki vastu ko 2 m upar uthana hai. Earth par aur Moon par kitni PE milegi?

- Earth (g = 10 m/s²): PE = 10 kg × 10 m/s² × 2 m = 100 N × 2 m = **200 J**
- Moon (g = 1.6 m/s²): PE = 10 kg × 1.6 m/s² × 2 m = 16 N × 2 m = **32 J**

**Jaanch:** 32 ÷ 200 = 0.16 = 1.6 ÷ 10 ✅. Moon par uthana aasan hai: kaam kam lagta hai, to PE bhi kam.

## Part F: PE raaste par nirbhar nahi karti

Maan lo ek 10 kg ka crate 2 m oonchai tak pahunchana hai. Ek tarika: seedha **lift** se uthao. Doosra: **5 m lambi ramp** (dhalaan) par kheencho. Teesra: seedhiyon se le jao.

```
                              ┌──┐
                              └──┘  <-- crate (2 m upar)
                             / │
                   ramp     /  │
                   (5 m)   /   │ 2 m
                          /    │
   ======================/═════┴==========  zameen
```

> **Figure 5.3** – Seedhe lift, 5 m lambi ramp ya seedhiyan: teeno raaste crate ko zameen se 2 m oonchai par pahunchate hain. PE sirf **seedhi oonchai (vertical height)** par nirbhar karti hai, raaste ki lambai par nahi.

**Example 11:** 10 kg ke crate ki PE, 2 m oonchai par (g = 10 m/s²), friction ko chhod kar:

- Seedha lift ya ramp ya seedhiyan, teeno ke liye h = **2 m** (vertical height)
- PE = 10 kg × 10 m/s² × 2 m = 100 N × 2 m = **200 J**

**Jaanch:** agar galti se ramp ki lambai 5 m ko "h" le lete, to 100 N × 5 m = 500 J aa jata ✅ (ye galat hota). Sahi h hamesha **reference level se seedhi oonchai** hai.

Ramp par kheenchna **aasan** lagta hai (kam force), par ramp lambi hoti hai. PE ka antar fir bhi wahi 200 J rehta hai. Friction ki baat hum yahan chhod rahe hain.

## Part G: Tejal ka sawal solve karte hain

Ab wapas terrace par. Tank ka paani **12 m** oonchai par hai (tap ki jagah ke hisaab se). Maan lo tank mein **1000 kg** paani (1000 litre) hai. g = 10 m/s².

- PE = m g h = 1000 kg × 10 m/s² × 12 m = 10000 N × 12 m = **120000 J** (yaani 120 kJ)

**Jaanch:** 120000 J ÷ (10 m/s² × 12 m) = 120000 ÷ 120 = 1000 kg ✅

**Motor ne kya kiya:** Motor ne paani ko 12 m upar uthane mein jo kaam kiya (gravity ke khilaf), wo **W = m g h = 120000 J** ke barabar hai (friction ko chhod kar). Yahi kaam paani mein **PE bankar jama** ho gaya. Tank mein paani ruka hua tha, par uske paas 120000 J ki "chupi hui taaqat" thi.

**Tap khulne par:** Jab neeche tap khola, paani neeche aane laga. Neeche aate-aate uski PE ghatti gayi aur use **gati** mili: yaani PE **KE mein badal gayi**. Isi KE ki wajah se paani zor se "sssshhh" karta hua nikla. Mr. Mishra ne Tejal se kaha, "Tank mein paani ne taaqat jama ki thi: wohi taaqat ab tez dhaar ban gayi."

Is badlav ko hum agle topic mein poori tarah dekhenge. Abhi ek chhoti si **jhalak** (sirf preview, Topic 6 mein detail):

**Example 12 (girte hue PE gawana, KE paana, sirf preview):** 2 kg ka patthar 5 m oonchai se girta hai (air resistance nahi). Free fall mein neeche pahunchne par v² = 2 g h (gravitation wale topic mein dekha tha). PE ka kitna ghata? KE kitna badha?

- PE ka ghatna = m g h = 2 kg × 10 m/s² × 5 m = 20 N × 5 m = **100 J**
- v² = 2 g h = 2 × 10 m/s² × 5 m = 100 m²/s², to v = **10 m/s**
- KE = ½ m v² = ½ × 2 kg × (10 m/s)² = ½ × 2 kg × 100 m²/s² = **100 J**

**Jaanch:** PE ka ghata hua 100 J = KE ka badha hua 100 J ✅. Ye barabari **sirf ek jhalak** hai: Topic 6 mein hum ise **energy conservation** ke naam se samjhenge.

## Part H: Dam ka paani: PE ka bada pairaman

Ek bade **dam** mein lakhon-karodon kilogram paani oonchai par ruka hota hai. Is paani ki PE bahut zyada hoti hai. Jab paani pipe se neeche girta hai, to uski PE KE mein badalti hai, aur wo KE **turbine** ko ghumati hai, jisse bijli banti hai. Ise **hydroelectric power** kehte hain. Tank wali baat ka hi bada roop: oonchai par paani = jama hui PE.

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Reference level bhool jana**

"PE kitni hai?" kehna adhoora hai. Hamesha batao **kahan se** naap rahe ho. Example 4 mein kitaab ki PE zameen se 30 J thi aur table se 14 J. Dono sahi hain, par reference alag hai.

**Galti 2: Galat height lena (ramp ki lambai)**

Ramp ya seedhi ki **lambai** h nahi hoti. h hai reference level se **seedhi oonchai**. Example 11 mein ramp 5 m lambi thi, par h = 2 m tha. 5 m lete to 500 J aata, jo galat hai.

**Galti 3: Mass ko force samajhna (g bhool jana)**

Kg mass hai, force nahi. 3 kg ko 5 m par PE = 3 × 5 = 15 likhna galat hai. Sahi: 3 kg × 10 m/s² × 5 m = 150 J. g ke bina unit joule nahi banta.

**Galti 4: Sochna ki PE raaste par nirbhar karti hai**

Seedhe lift, ramp ya seedhiyan: agar vertical height wahi hai, to PE ka antar wahi hai (Example 11, friction chhod kar).

**Galti 5: PE hamesha positive hoti hai, aisa maan lena**

Reference level se **neeche** ki cheez ki PE negative aati hai (Example 4 ke niche wali line). Zaroori nahi ki hamesha positive ho.

**Galti 6: g = 10 aur g = 9.8 ko mix karna**

Ek hi sawal mein ek hi g lo. 3 kg, 5 m: g = 10 se 150 J, g = 9.8 se 147 J. Dono alag-alag sawalon mein theek hain, par ek hi sawal mein mix karne se jawab gadbad ho jata hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Potential energy (PE)** = cheez ke paas uski **position ya condition** ki wajah se jama energy.
- **Gravitational PE = m × g × h**, unit **joule (J)**: kg × m/s² × m = J.
- Ye wahi kaam hai jo cheez ko gravity ke khilaf (ek-si speed se) h oonchai tak uthane mein kiya jata hai (force = m g).
- PE **m, g aur h** par nirbhar hai: height double to PE double. Moon par g = 1.6 m/s², to wahi uthayi kam PE deti hai (10 kg, 2 m: Earth par 200 J, Moon par 32 J).
- PE **reference level** se naapi jaati hai. Alag reference par PE ki value badalti hai, par **do jagah ke beech PE ka antar (aur uthane ka kaam) wahi rehta hai**.
- h = **seedhi (vertical) oonchai**; ramp ki lambai nahi. PE **raaste par nirbhar nahi** karti (friction chhod kar).
- Reference ke neeche PE negative ho sakti hai.
- **Elastic PE** (khichi spring, kamaan, catapult) aur **chemical PE** (petrol, khana, battery): abhi sirf naam, detail baad mein.
- **Kahani ka jawab:** tank ka paani: PE = 1000 × 10 × 12 = 120000 J, jitna motor ne kaam kiya. Tap khulne par ye PE KE mein badalti hai, isliye paani zor se nikalta hai.

## Practice Questions

2. An object has a potential energy of 500 J at a height of 2 m. Find its mass (g = 10 m/s²).

6. A 6 kg object is lifted 5 m. Find the PE gained on the Earth (g = 10 m/s²) and on the Moon (g = 1.6 m/s²).

8. A 30 kg box is pulled up a ramp 6 m long that rises 2 m vertically. Find its PE gain. Is 6 m or 2 m used as h? Why?

9. A 4 kg object is at 10 m and then at 20 m. Find both PE values and say how they compare.

10. State True or False:
    (a) PE depends on the path taken to reach a height (ignore friction).
    (b) PE of a body can be negative if it is below the reference level.
    (c) PE = m × h (the g is not needed).
    (d) A stretched rubber band has potential energy.

11. A 1 kg stone falls from a height of 20 m. Find the PE lost, and, using v² = 2 g h, the KE it has just before touching the ground. What do you notice? (g = 10 m/s², air resistance ignored)

   Jaanch: 25 × 10 × 2 = 500 J ✅

3. h = PE ÷ (m g) = 240 J ÷ (2 kg × 10 m/s²) = 240 ÷ 20 = **12 m**.
   Jaanch: 2 × 10 × 12 = 240 J ✅

4. Kul height h = 12 × 0.25 m = 3 m. Weight = 45 kg × 10 m/s² = 450 N. W = 450 N × 3 m = **1350 J**. PE gain bhi **1350 J**.
   Jaanch: 1350 J ÷ 450 N = 3 m ✅

5. PE = 2000 kg × 10 m/s² × 9 m = 20000 N × 9 m = **180000 J (180 kJ)**. Motor ka kaam bhi **180000 J** (friction chhod kar).
   Jaanch: 180000 ÷ (2000 × 10) = 9 m ✅

6. Earth: 6 kg × 10 m/s² × 5 m = 60 N × 5 m = **300 J**. Moon: 6 kg × 1.6 m/s² × 5 m = 9.6 N × 5 m = **48 J**.
   Jaanch: 48 ÷ 300 = 0.16 = 1.6 ÷ 10 ✅

7. (a) Floor: 1.5 kg × 10 m/s² × 2 m = 15 N × 2 m = **30 J**. (b) Table se height = 2 − 0.5 = 1.5 m: PE = 15 N × 1.5 m = **22.5 J**. Table se shelf tak uthane ka kaam = **22.5 J**.
   Jaanch: table ki PE (floor ke hisaab se) = 15 × 0.5 = 7.5 J, aur 30 − 7.5 = 22.5 J ✅

8. **h = 2 m** (vertical oonchai), 6 m ramp ki lambai hai. PE = 30 kg × 10 m/s² × 2 m = 300 N × 2 m = **600 J**.
   Jaanch: 6 m lete to 1800 J aata, jo galat hai: PE sirf vertical height par nirbhar hai ✅

9. 10 m par: 4 kg × 10 m/s² × 10 m = **400 J**. 20 m par: 4 kg × 10 m/s² × 20 m = **800 J**. Height double hui to PE bhi **double** (800 ÷ 400 = 2).
   Jaanch: 800 ÷ 400 = 2 = 20 ÷ 10 ✅

10. (a) **False** ❌. PE sirf vertical height par nirbhar hai, raaste par nahi.
    (b) **True** ✅. Reference ke neeche h negative maanne se PE negative aati hai.
    (c) **False** ❌. PE = m g h: g bhi chahiye, tabhi unit joule banta hai.
    (d) **True** ✅. Kheenchi hui rubber band mein elastic PE hoti hai.

11. PE lost = 1 kg × 10 m/s² × 20 m = 10 N × 20 m = **200 J**. v² = 2 × 10 m/s² × 20 m = 400 m²/s², v = 20 m/s. KE = ½ × 1 kg × (20 m/s)² = ½ × 400 = **200 J**. Dono barabar hain: ye Topic 6 ka preview hai.
    Jaanch: 200 J = 200 J ✅
