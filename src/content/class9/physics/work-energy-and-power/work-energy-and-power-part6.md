# Topic 6: Conservation of Energy

Pichhle topic mein humne dekha ki oonchai par rakhi cheez ke paas **position ki wajah se** energy hoti hai: PE = m g h. Aur ek hint bhi mila tha: jab cheez girti hai to uski PE kam hoti hai aur speed badhti hai, yaani PE **Kinetic Energy (KE)** mein badalti jaati hai. Ab sawaal ye hai: jitni PE gayi, kya bilkul utni hi KE mili? Kya koi energy beech mein gayab to nahi ho jaati? Is topic mein physics ka sabse bada niyam seekhenge: **energy ka hisaab hamesha barabar baithta hai.**

Is poore topic mein hum **g = 10 m/s²** lenge (aasaan arithmetic ke liye). Free fall ka v² = 2 g h pehle se aata hai: dekhein [gravitation-part4.md](../gravitation/gravitation-part4.md).

## Mr. Joaquim, Sneha aur roller coaster ki pehli pahaadi

Amusement park mein roller coaster ke saamne lambi line thi. Roller coaster ke operator Mr. Joaquim lever ke paas khade the. Line mein khadi Sneha ne dekha ki coaster ki **pehli pahaadi** sabse oonchi hai, aur cars ek chain ke sahare dheere-dheere khinchkar wahan tak jaati hain. Chain ki "tak-tak-tak" awaaz door tak aati thi.

Phir upar pahunchte hi chain chhoot jaati hai, aur uske baad car mein **koi engine nahi**. Phir bhi car dhadhdhadati hui neeche aati, dip se tezi se nikalti, aur agli pahaadi par chadh jaati. Sneha ne yeh kai baar dekha. Ek khaas baat usne note ki: **har agli pahaadi pichhli se chhoti thi.**

Sneha ne Mr. Joaquim se poochha, "Uncle, jab engine hai hi nahi, to car agli pahaadi par chadhti kaise hai? Aur ye pahaadiyan hamesha chhoti kyun hoti hain? Aakhri wali to sabse chhoti hai. Pehli ke barabar oonchi kyun nahi bana dete?"

Mr. Joaquim hans pade. "Bahut achha sawaal. Ek line mein jawab: **chain ne jo energy pehli pahaadi par car ko di, bas wahi poore safar ka 'bank balance' hai.** Car us balance ko kabhi PE mein badalti hai, kabhi KE mein, par balance ko khud nahi badha sakti." Sneha ne bhaunhein uthayi, "Balance? Aur kharch kahan hota hai?" Mr. Joaquim bole, "Ye sab hum is topic ke end mein hisaab laga kar dekhenge. Pehle niyam samajhte hain."

## Part A: Conservation of Energy ka niyam

**Law of Conservation of Energy:**

> Energy ko **na banaya ja sakta hai, na nasht kiya ja sakta hai**. Use sirf **ek roop se doosre roop mein badla ja sakta hai**. Kisi isolated system ki **kul energy hamesha constant** rehti hai.

Do baatein dhyan se:

1. "Kul energy" ka matlab **saari** forms: KE, PE, heat, sound, light, sab milakar.
2. Kisi ek roop (jaise KE) ki energy kam ho sakti hai, par tab wo kisi aur roop mein dikhegi. Kul jod nahi badalta.

Mechanical energy ka naam hai **KE + PE**. Agar koi friction ya air resistance na ho (ya wo itne kam hon ki ignore kar sakein), to:

**KE + PE = constant**

Matlab ek roop jitna ghatta hai, doosra utna hi badhta hai. Real zindagi mein friction aur hawa hoti hain, jiske baare mein Part D mein baat karenge.

## Part B: Free fall mein conservation

Ek **2 kg ka patthar 20 m ki oonchai se** chhoda gaya (hawa ignore, g = 10 m/s²). Hum jaanna chahte hain ki girte waqt har oonchai par PE, KE aur speed kya hai.

- Oonchai h par PE = m g h = 2 kg × 10 m/s² × h = 20 × h (J).
- Utni doori gir chuka (20 − h) m. Free fall se speed v = √(2 g (20 − h)).
- KE = ½ m v².

| Oonchai h (m) | PE = m g h (J) | KE (J) | KE + PE (J) | Speed v (m/s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | 400 | 0 | 400 | 0 |
| 15 | 300 | 100 | 400 | 10 |
| 10 | 200 | 200 | 400 | 14.1 |
| 5 | 100 | 300 | 400 | 17.3 |
| 0 | 0 | 400 | 400 | 20 |

Table kaise bana? Do rows hum haath se bhar ke dekhte hain.

**h = 15 m:** gira = 5 m. v = √(2 × 10 m/s² × 5 m) = √100 = **10 m/s**. KE = ½ × 2 kg × (10 m/s)² = ½ × 2 × 100 = **100 J**. PE = 2 × 10 × 15 = **300 J**. Jod = 400 J.

**h = 10 m:** gira = 10 m. v = √(2 × 10 × 10) = √200 = **14.1 m/s**. KE = ½ × 2 × (√200)² = 1 × 200 = **200 J**. PE = 2 × 10 × 10 = **200 J**. Jod = 400 J.

Teen baatein table se saaf dikhti hain:

- **Sabse upar (h = 20 m):** saari energy **PE** hai (400 J), KE zero (patthar abhi chhoda hai, speed 0).
- **Sabse neeche (h = 0):** saari energy **KE** hai (400 J), PE zero.
- **Beech mein (h = 10 m, aadhi oonchai):** PE aur KE **barabar** hain (200 J aur 200 J).

Aur har row mein **KE + PE = 400 J**, hamesha. Yehi conservation hai. Isse Topic 5 ke end mein jo preview tha, wo bhi poora hota hai: 2 kg ka patthar 5 m gira (20 m ki oonchai se 15 m tak) to PE ne **100 J** khoyi (400 − 300) aur KE ne **100 J** paayi (0 → 100). Barabar!

Ek chhota "energy bar" sketch dekhein (har # = 100 J):

```
 h (m)   PE              KE
  20     ####            .
  15     ###             #
  10     ##              ##
   5     #               ###
   0     .               ####
        (PE ghatti)     (KE badhti)
        Har row mein # ki kul sankhya = 4
```

> **Figure 6.1** – Girte patthar ki energy bars: PE ke # ghatte hain, KE ke # badhte hain, par har oonchai par kul 4 (yaani 400 J) rehta hai.

## Part C: Free fall ka proof (step by step)

Table ke numbers sirf ek example hain. Ab hum **kisi bhi** mass aur **kisi bhi** oonchai ke liye saabit karte hain ki KE + PE constant hai.

Maan lo m kg ka ek body ground se **h** oonchai par rest se chhoda gaya. Kuch der baad wo **x** meter neeche gir chuka hai. Ab uski oonchai (ground se) hai **h − x**.

**Step 1 (speed):** Free fall mein rest se shuru, to v² = 2 g x (gravitation wale v² = u² + 2 g s se, u = 0).

**Step 2 (KE):**
KE = ½ m v² = ½ m (2 g x) = **m g x**

**Step 3 (PE):**
PE = m g × (ground se oonchai) = **m g (h − x)**

**Step 4 (jodo):**
KE + PE = m g x + m g (h − x) = m g x + m g h − m g x = **m g h**

Dekha? x kat gaya. Jod m g h hai, jo x par depend nahi karta. Matlab girne ke kisi bhi pal par (x kuch bhi ho) **KE + PE = m g h = shuru ki PE**. Yehi constant hai.

Table check: m = 2 kg, h = 20 m: m g h = 2 × 10 × 20 = 400 J ✅ (table mein har row ka jod 400 J tha).

Ek aur baat: v² = 2 g x se hume mass nahi dikhta. Isliye speed **mass par depend nahi karti**. Ye hum Example 8 mein dekhenge.

## Part D: Aur jagahon par conservation

### 1. Simple pendulum

Ek chhoti gend (bob) dhaage se latki hai. Use ek taraf kheench kar chhodo to wo jhoolti hai.

```
          O   (point of suspension)
         /:\
        / : \
       /  :  \
      /   :   \
     A    :    B     <- A aur B: same oonchai h
      ' . : . '
          C          <- C: sabse neeche (lowest point)

   A, B : saari energy PE   (KE = 0)
   C    : saari energy KE   (PE = 0)
```

> **Figure 6.2** – Simple pendulum: dono extreme positions A aur B par saari energy PE hai, sabse neeche point C par saari energy KE hai. Hawa aur friction na ho to bob doosri taraf **usi oonchai** tak jaata hai.

- **Extreme position A par:** bob ek pal ke liye ruka hai, v = 0, to KE = 0, PE sabse zyada (reference level: C).
- **Neeche aate hue:** PE kam, KE zyada.
- **Lowest point C par:** PE = 0, KE sabse zyada, speed sabse zyada.
- **Doosri taraf chadhte hue:** KE phir PE mein badalti hai, aur bob B par wahi oonchai h tak pahunchta hai, jahan se A par chhoda gaya tha. (Hawa aur friction ho to bob thoda kam oonchai tak jaata hai, aur dheere-dheere jhoola ruk jaata hai.)

**Bob ki speed lowest point par:** maan lo bob ko lowest point se **0.2 m** oonchai par chhoda gaya. Saari PE neeche KE ban jaati hai:

m g h = ½ m v²
v² = 2 g h (m dono taraf kat gaya)
v = √(2 × 10 m/s² × 0.2 m) = √4 m²/s² = **2 m/s**

Jaanch: KE = ½ m v², PE = m g h = m × 10 × 0.2 = 2 m (J). KE = ½ m × 4 = 2 m (J) ✅ (mass kuch bhi ho, barabar).

### 2. Upar phenki gayi gend (KE → PE)

Gend ko upar phenko to wo upar jaate hue **dheemi** hoti jaati hai: KE ghat rahi hai, PE badh rahi hai. Sabse oonche point par ek pal ke liye v = 0: saari KE, PE ban chuki. Phir wapas girte hue PE → KE. Girte waqt har oonchai par wahi speed (opposite direction mein) jo upar jaate waqt thi, yaani poora safar free fall ka mirror hai.

### 3. Roller coaster (hamari kahani)

Pehli pahaadi par chain ne car ko oonchai tak khincha: car ko **PE** mil gayi. Ab neeche aate hue PE → KE, dip ke bottom par speed sabse zyada. Agli pahaadi chadhte hue KE → PE, speed ghatti hai. Ye ek chhota sketch:

```
   Pehli pahaadi
   (chain yahan
   tak khinchti hai)
       /\
      /  \             /\  Doosri pahaadi
     /    \           /  \ (chhoti)
    /      \         /    \
   /        \_______/      \_____
  Start        Dip            Dip
```

> **Figure 6.3** – Roller coaster: pehli pahaadi sabse oonchi (yahan chain energy deti hai). Uske baad har pahaadi pichhli se chhoti, kyunki thodi energy friction aur hawa mein heat/sound ban jaati hai.

### 4. Slide par bachcha (friction wala case)

Slide par bachcha neeche aata hai. Frictionless ho to speed v = √(2 g h) hoti. Par slide ki surface aur kapdon ke beech **friction** hai. Friction ke kaaran PE ka kuch hissa **heat** (surface thodi garam ho jaati hai) aur thodi **sound** ban jaata hai. Isliye neeche pahunchne par KE kam bachti hai, **speed hisaab se kam** aati hai. Energy nasht nahi hui: KE + heat + sound mila kar shuru ki PE ke barabar hai (Example 7).

### 5. Bouncing ball

Ball ko oonchai se girao: wo zameen par takraati hai aur ek bar upar uchhalti hai, par **pehle se kam oonchai** tak. Har bounce par oonchai kam hoti jaati hai aur aakhir mein ball ruk jaata hai. Kya energy khatam ho gayi? Nahi. Zameen se takrate waqt ball aur zameen thode dab-pichak jaate hain, kuch energy **heat** ban jaati hai aur "thap" ki **sound** nikalti hai. Kul energy (ball ki + heat + sound) wahi hai.

### 6. Bijli banane wale plant

- **Hydro power plant:** oonchai par dam mein ruka paani PE rakhta hai. Neeche girte hue PE → KE; paani turbine ghumata hai (KE → turbine ki rotational energy), generator se **bijli**.
- **Windmill (pawan chakki):** chalti hawa ki KE blades ghumati hai, generator mein bijli banti hai.

Dono jagah energy **banayi nahi gayi**, sirf ek roop se doosre mein pahunchayi gayi.

### 7. Energy transformation chain, phir se

Pichhle topics mein chains dekhi thi. Ab har step ko conservation ke hisaab se dekhte hain. **Hathoda** jo oonchai se girke keel par padta hai:

**PE (oonchai par) → KE (girte hue) → heat + sound + keel ka dhasna (impact par)**

Impact par hathode ki KE zero ho jaati hai, par energy kahan gayi? Keel thodi garam hui, "thak" ki awaaz hui, aur keel lakdi mein dhans gayi. Kul energy wahi.

### Perpetual motion machine

Real zindagi mein friction aur hawa har machine ki thodi energy heat bana dete hain, aur bahar se energy diye bina koi machine hamesha nahi chal sakti: isliye **perpetual motion machine possible nahi hai**.

### Roller coaster ka sawaal hal

Ab Sneha ke sawal ka jawab:

1. **Kul energy chain deti hai.** Pehli pahaadi ki chadhai tak chain ne jitni PE di (m g h), car ko poore safar ke liye utni hi "mechanical energy" mili. Uske baad koi engine nahi.
2. **Har pahaadi par thodi energy kam hoti hai.** Car ke wheels aur track ke beech friction, aur car aur hawa ke beech air resistance ke kaaran kuch mechanical energy heat aur sound mein badalti rehti hai (track garam hota hai, aur car ke chalne ki awaaz aati hai). Kul energy wahi rehti hai, par **mechanical energy (KE + PE) ghatti jaati hai.**
3. **Isliye agli pahaadi chhoti.** Doosri pahaadi par car sirf utni hi oonchai chadh sakti hai jitni PE bachi hui mechanical energy se ban sake. Agar doosri pahaadi pehli jitni (ya zyada) oonchi hoti, to car uske top tak pahunchti hi nahi, aur beech mein ruk jaati.
4. **Chain ko poori energy dene ka kaam pehle hill par hi karna hai.** Isliye pehli pahaadi sabse oonchi rakhi jaati hai taaki car ke paas poore track ke liye (friction ke nuksan ke baad bhi) kaafi energy bachi rahe.

Mr. Joaquim ne Sneha se kaha, "Dekho, bank balance wali baat: chain ne balance daala, friction aur hawa har pahaadi par thoda 'service charge' kaatte hain, aur balance KE aur PE mein ghoomta rehta hai. Total paisa (energy) nahi ghata, bas ek hissa heat aur sound ke 'account' mein chala gaya." Sneha ne sar hilaya, "To aakhri pahaadi isliye sabse chhoti, kyunki wahan tak balance sabse kam bacha hota hai!"

## Part E: Solved examples

### Example 1: Girne ke baad speed (energy se)

Ek patthar **5 m** ki oonchai se gira. Zameen par pahunchne ki speed (hawa ignore) nikalo.

Energy conservation: shuru ki PE = ant ki KE.
m g h = ½ m v², to v² = 2 g h (m kat gaya).
v = √(2 × 10 m/s² × 5 m) = √100 m²/s² = **10 m/s**

Jaanch (equation of motion se): v² = u² + 2 g s = 0 + 2 × 10 × 5 = 100, to v = 10 m/s ✅ (dono tareeke se same).

### Example 2: Almari se girti gend ki speed

Ek gend **3.2 m** oonchi almari se gir kar farsh par kitni speed se takrayegi?

v = √(2 g h) = √(2 × 10 m/s² × 3.2 m) = √64 m²/s² = **8 m/s**

Jaanch: v² = 2 g s = 2 × 10 m/s² × 3.2 m = 64 m²/s², aur (8 m/s)² = 64 m²/s² ✅

### Example 3: Kis oonchai par KE = PE?

2 kg ka patthar 20 m se gira. Kis oonchai par KE aur PE barabar hongi?

Kul energy = m g h = 2 × 10 × 20 = 400 J. KE = PE ka matlab har ek = 400 ÷ 2 = 200 J.
PE = m g h' = 200 J, to 2 × 10 × h' = 200, to h' = 200 ÷ 20 = **10 m** (aadhi oonchai).

Jaanch: is oonchai par v = √(2 × 10 × (20 − 10)) = √(200 m²/s²) = 14.1 m/s, KE = ½ × 2 × 200 = 200 J ✅ aur PE = 200 J ✅

### Example 4: Upar phenki gend

0.5 kg ki ek gend **10 m/s** se seedhi upar phenki gayi. Jis pal wo **3 m** oonchai par hai, uski PE, KE aur speed nikalo.

Phenkte waqt: KE = ½ × 0.5 kg × (10 m/s)² = ½ × 0.5 × 100 = **25 J**, PE = 0 (reference level hamara haath/ground). Kul ME = 25 J.

3 m par:
- PE = m g h = 0.5 kg × 10 m/s² × 3 m = **15 J**
- KE = kul − PE = 25 J − 15 J = **10 J**
- Speed: KE = ½ m v² to v² = 2 × KE ÷ m = 2 × 10 J ÷ 0.5 kg = 40 m²/s², v = √40 = **6.32 m/s**

Jaanch (equation se): v² = u² − 2 g h = 100 − 2 × 10 × 3 = 100 − 60 = 40 m²/s² ✅ (v = 6.32 m/s).

### Example 5: Upar phenki gend ki maximum height

Ek gend **15 m/s** se seedhi upar phenki gayi. Wo kitni oonchai tak jayegi? (Gravitation mein [gravitation-part4.md](../gravitation/gravitation-part4.md) ka H = u² ÷ (2 g) yaad karo.)

Sabse oonche point par v = 0, to saari KE → PE. Gend ka mass kuch bhi lo, jaise m:
½ m u² = m g H, to H = u² ÷ (2 g).
H = (15 m/s)² ÷ (2 × 10 m/s²) = 225 m²/s² ÷ 20 m/s² = **11.25 m**

Jaanch (numbers se, m = 0.5 kg lekar): KE = ½ × 0.5 kg × (15 m/s)² = 56.25 J. PE = 0.5 kg × 10 m/s² × 11.25 m = 56.25 J ✅

### Example 6: Pendulum bob ki speed

Ek lambe pendulum ke bob ko lowest point se **0.8 m** oonchai par chhoda gaya. Lowest point par speed?

v = √(2 g h) = √(2 × 10 m/s² × 0.8 m) = √16 m²/s² = **4 m/s**

Jaanch: 2 g h = 2 × 10 m/s² × 0.8 m = 16 m²/s², aur (4 m/s)² = 16 m²/s² ✅

### Example 7: Roller coaster car (frictionless maan kar)

500 kg ki ek car **40 m** ooncha pahaadi ke top se (rest se) chhodi gayi. (a) Dip ke bottom par speed? (b) Agli **25 m** ooncha pahaadi ke top par speed?

Reference level: dip ka bottom. Top par kul energy = m g h = 500 kg × 10 m/s² × 40 m = **200 000 J** (= 200 kJ).

**(a) Bottom par:**
v = √(2 g h) = √(2 × 10 × 40) = √800 = **28.3 m/s**
(m = 500 kg yahan kat gaya, isse fark nahi padta.)

**(b) 25 m wali pahaadi ke top par:** car 40 m se 25 m par aayi, yaani **15 m neeche** (oonchai ka farak).
v = √(2 g × 15 m) = √(2 × 10 × 15) = √300 = **17.3 m/s**

Jaanch (energy se): 25 m par PE = 500 × 10 × 25 = 125 000 J. KE = ½ × 500 × 300 = 75 000 J. Jod = 125 000 + 75 000 = 200 000 J ✅ (shuru ke barabar).

### Example 8: Mass ka asar nahi (bhaari vs halka)

Do patthar, **1 kg** aur **10 kg**, dono **20 m** se gire. Dono ki speed ground par? Dono ki KE?

Dono ke liye v = √(2 g h) = √(2 × 10 × 20) = √400 = **20 m/s** (mass formula mein hai hi nahi).
- 1 kg ki KE = ½ × 1 × 400 = **200 J**
- 10 kg ki KE = ½ × 10 × 400 = **2000 J**

Bhaari patthar ke paas PE (aur KE) 10 guna zyada hai (2000 J vs 200 J), par **speed same** hai. "Zyada PE hai to zyada tez girega" galat soch hai: zyada PE ko **zyada mass** ko bhi chalana padta hai, isliye speed same rehti hai.

### Example 9: Friction wala case

10 kg ka ek box **5 m** ooncha slope se phisalta hai aur neeche **8 m/s** par pahunchta hai. Kitni energy heat (aur sound) mein gayi?

Top par PE = m g h = 10 kg × 10 m/s² × 5 m = **500 J** (KE = 0).
Bottom par KE = ½ m v² = ½ × 10 kg × (8 m/s)² = ½ × 10 × 64 = **320 J** (PE = 0).

Frictionless hota to bottom par KE = 500 J hoti (speed v = √(2 × 10 × 5) = 10 m/s). Par sirf 320 J mili.

Heat + sound mein gayi energy = 500 J − 320 J = **180 J**.
Percentage = 180 ÷ 500 × 100 = **36%**.

Jaanch: KE + heat/sound = 320 J + 180 J = 500 J = shuru ki PE ✅ (Kul energy conserve hui.)

### Example 10: Bouncing ball

Ek ball 2 m ki oonchai se gira aur pehli bounce ke baad **80%** oonchai tak, yaani 1.6 m tak uchha. Ball ki energy ka kitna percent heat/sound mein gaya?

Ball ki PE oonchai ke saath chalti hai (m g h, m aur g constant): energy ∝ oonchai.
Shuru: h₁ = 2 m. Bounce ke baad: h₂ = 1.6 m (80% of 2 m).
Energy ka ratio = 1.6 ÷ 2 = 0.8, yaani **80%** energy bachi.
Energy jo gayi = 100% − 80% = **20%**.

Numbers se (m = 0.5 kg lekar): PE₁ = 0.5 × 10 × 2 = 10 J. PE₂ = 0.5 × 10 × 1.6 = 8 J. Gayi hui = 10 − 8 = **2 J** = 2 ÷ 10 = 20% ✅. Ye 2 J heat aur sound mein badli.

### Example 11: Tower se girta patthar (PE ki kami = KE ki badhat)

2 kg ka patthar **45 m** ooncha tower se chhoda gaya. **2 s** baad (aur zameen par pahunchne par) uski KE aur PE dekhein. (Free fall: gira hua x = ½ g t², v = g t.)

**2 s par:** x = ½ × 10 × 2² = 20 m. v = 10 × 2 = 20 m/s.
- KE = ½ × 2 kg × (20 m/s)² = **400 J**
- PE ki kami = m g x = 2 × 10 × 20 = **400 J**
- Ab ground se oonchai = 45 − 20 = 25 m, PE = 2 × 10 × 25 = **500 J**

Jod = KE + PE = 400 + 500 = **900 J**.

**Zameen par:** v = √(2 × 10 × 45) = √900 = **30 m/s**. KE = ½ × 2 × 900 = **900 J**, PE = 0. Jod = 900 J.

Shuru ki PE = 2 × 10 × 45 = 900 J.

Jaanch: 2 s par jod = 900 J ✅, ground par jod = 900 J ✅. PE ki kami (400 J) = KE ki badhat (400 J) ✅

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

1. **"Friction hone par energy nasht ho jaati hai."** Galat. Friction mein mechanical energy **heat aur sound** ban jaati hai. Example 9 mein 180 J nasht nahi hui, heat/sound ban gayi (320 + 180 = 500 J).

2. **Friction ke saath bhi KE + PE = constant lagana.** Ye niyam sirf tab chalta hai jab friction/air resistance na ho. Friction ho to kul energy constant hai, par **KE + PE alag se ghatega**. Example 9 mein 500 J se ghatke 320 J hua.

3. **Speed aur KE ko ek hi samajh lena.** Speed 2 guni ho to KE **4 guni** hoti hai (KE ∝ v²). Example: v = 10 m/s par KE = ½ × 2 × 100 = 100 J, v = 20 m/s par KE = ½ × 2 × 400 = 400 J (4 guni).

4. **Galat oonchai lena (galat reference level).** Example 7(b) mein pahaadi 25 m ooncha thi, par car 40 m se sirf **15 m** neeche aayi. Speed nikalte waqt √(2 g × 15) lena hai, √(2 g × 25) nahi. Hamesha dekho ki shuruaat aur ant ki **oonchai ka farak** kitna hai.

5. **"Bhaari cheez zyada tez girti hai kyunki uski PE zyada hai."** Galat. v = √(2 g h) mein mass hai hi nahi. 1 kg aur 10 kg dono 20 m se girne par 20 m/s se takrate hain (Example 8).

6. **Square root lena bhool jaana.** Example 2 mein v² = 64 m²/s². Yahin ruk gaye to "64 m/s" likh denge, jo galat hai. Sahi: v = √64 = 8 m/s.

7. **Heat ya sound ko "kho gayi energy" kehna.** Heat aur sound bhi energy ke roop hain, inhe kul energy mein jodna hai.

## Quick Recap

- **Law of Conservation of Energy:** energy na banti hai, na nasht hoti hai; sirf ek roop se doosre mein badalti hai. Isolated system ki kul energy constant.
- **Mechanical energy = KE + PE.** Friction/air resistance na ho to ye constant rehti hai.
- Free fall mein: upar saari energy PE, neeche saari KE, aadhi oonchai par barabar. Proof: KE = m g x, PE = m g (h − x), jod = m g h.
- Girne par speed v = √(2 g h), jo mass par depend nahi karti.
- Pendulum: extreme par saari PE, lowest point par saari KE; bob usi oonchai tak doosri taraf jaata hai (ideal case).
- Real zindagi mein friction aur hawa mechanical energy ko heat aur sound mein badalti hain (slide, bouncing ball, roller coaster). Kul energy phir bhi wahi rehti hai.
- Isliye roller coaster ki agli pahaadiyan chhoti hoti hain, aur perpetual motion machine possible nahi.
- Hydro plant aur windmill energy ka roop badalte hain, energy banate nahi.

## Practice Questions

1. A 4 kg object is dropped from a height of 10 m. Using g = 10 m/s², find its PE at the top, its speed just before hitting the ground, and its KE at the ground. Also find its KE and PE when it is at 5 m above the ground.
2. A pendulum bob is released from a height of 0.45 m above its lowest point. Find its speed at the lowest point.
3. A 0.2 kg ball is thrown vertically upward at 12 m/s. Find (a) the maximum height it reaches, and (b) its KE, PE and speed when it is 4 m above the point of throw.
4. A 400 kg roller-coaster car is released from rest at the top of a 30 m hill. Ignoring friction, find its speed at the bottom, and its speed at the top of the next 10 m hill.
5. A 25 kg child slides down a slide of height 2 m and reaches the bottom at 5 m/s. How much energy became heat and sound? What percent of the starting PE is that?
6. A ball dropped from 2 m rises to only 1.5 m after the first bounce. What percent of its energy was lost? Where did that energy go?
7. Two stones, one of 1 kg and one of 5 kg, are dropped from the same height. Compare (a) their speeds just before hitting the ground, (b) their KE there.
8. A stone is dropped from 20 m and then another from 80 m. By what factor is the final speed of the second stone greater than the first? (Use v = √(2 g h).)
9. True or false: (a) The total energy of an isolated system keeps decreasing when there is friction. (b) At the highest point of a ball thrown upward, its mechanical energy is all PE. (c) A heavier body falling freely from a given height reaches the ground with a larger speed than a lighter one. (d) A perpetual motion machine is possible if friction is made very small.

### Answers

1. m = 4 kg, h = 10 m. PE = m g h = 4 kg × 10 m/s² × 10 m = **400 J**. v = √(2 × 10 m/s² × 10 m) = √(200 m²/s²) = **14.1 m/s**. KE at ground = ½ × 4 kg × 200 m²/s² = **400 J** (= PE at top). At 5 m: PE = 4 kg × 10 m/s² × 5 m = **200 J**, KE = 400 J − 200 J = **200 J**.
   Jaanch: KE + PE = 200 + 200 = 400 J ✅

2. v = √(2 g h) = √(2 × 10 × 0.45) = √9 = **3 m/s**.
   Jaanch: 2 × 10 × 0.45 = 9, aur 3² = 9 ✅

3. (a) KE at start = ½ × 0.2 × 144 = 14.4 J. At the top all PE: 0.2 × 10 × H = 14.4, to 2 H = 14.4, **H = 7.2 m**. Jaanch: H = u² ÷ 2g = 144 ÷ 20 = 7.2 m ✅
   (b) At 4 m: PE = 0.2 × 10 × 4 = **8 J**. KE = 14.4 − 8 = **6.4 J**. v = √(2 × 6.4 ÷ 0.2) = √64 = **8 m/s**.
   Jaanch: v² = u² − 2 g h = 144 − 80 = 64 ✅

4. Bottom: v = √(2 × 10 × 30) = √600 = **24.5 m/s**. Next hill 10 m: car 30 − 10 = 20 m neeche, v = √(2 × 10 × 20) = √400 = **20 m/s**.
   Jaanch: 10 m par PE = 400 × 10 × 10 = 40 000 J, KE = ½ × 400 × 400 = 80 000 J, jod = 120 000 J = 400 × 10 × 30 ✅

5. PE = 25 kg × 10 m/s² × 2 m = **500 J**. KE = ½ × 25 kg × (5 m/s)² = **312.5 J**. Heat + sound = 500 J − 312.5 J = **187.5 J**. Percent = 187.5 ÷ 500 × 100 = **37.5%**.
   Jaanch: 312.5 + 187.5 = 500 J ✅

6. Energy ∝ height. Bachi hui = 1.5 ÷ 2 = 0.75 = 75%. Lost = **25%**. Ye energy zameen se takrane par **heat aur sound** mein gayi (nasht nahi hui).
   Jaanch (m = 1 kg): PE₁ = 20 J, PE₂ = 15 J, lost 5 J = 5 ÷ 20 = 25% ✅

7. (a) Dono ki speed **barabar**, v = √(2 g h), mass formula mein nahi. (b) KE = ½ m v², v² same, to KE ka ratio = mass ka ratio: 5 kg ki KE 1 kg ki **5 guni** hai. Jaanch (h = 20 m, v² = 400): 1 kg: 200 J, 5 kg: 1000 J, 1000 ÷ 200 = 5 ✅

8. v₁ = √(2 × 10 × 20) = √400 = 20 m/s. v₂ = √(2 × 10 × 80) = √1600 = 40 m/s. Factor = 40 ÷ 20 = **2 guna**. (Oonchai 4 guni, speed sirf 2 guni, kyunki v ∝ √h.)

9. (a) **False** ❌. Kul energy constant rehti hai; sirf mechanical energy ghatti hai (heat/sound ban jaati hai).
   (b) **True** ✅. Top par v = 0, to KE = 0, saari ME PE hai.
   (c) **False** ❌. Speed mass par depend nahi karti (hawa ignore).
   (d) **False** ❌. Friction bilkul zero nahi ho sakta, aur bahar se energy diye bina machine hamesha nahi chalti.
