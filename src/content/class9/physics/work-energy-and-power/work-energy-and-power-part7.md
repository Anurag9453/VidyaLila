# Topic 7: Power: Kaam Kitni Jaldi Hua?

Pichle topic mein humne conservation of energy dekha: energy kabhi banti ya khatam nahi hoti, sirf ek roop se doosre roop mein badalti hai. Free fall mein PE kam hoti gayi aur KE badhti gayi, aur total (KE + PE) wahi raha. Friction ke saath kuch energy heat ban gayi, par hisaab phir bhi poora mila.

Par ek sawaal abhi baaki hai: **wahi energy badalne mein kitna time laga?** Ek patthar dheere dheere neeche rakho ya seedha gira do, ek hi jagah se ek hi jagah tak: PE ka change same hai. Bijli ka ek chhota heater paani ko ghanton mein garm kare, ya bada heater minuton mein: total energy lagbhag same, par **raftaar** alag. Is topic mein hum us raftaar ko naapna seekhenge. Isse hum ye bhi samjhenge ki bijli ke bill par "unit" kya hota hai. Ye chapter ka aakhri topic hai, to end mein poore chapter ka wrap-up bhi hoga.

Is topic mein jahan zaroorat ho, hum **g = 10 m/s²** lenge (asli value 9.8 m/s² ke kareeb hai, par hisaab aasaan ho jaata hai).

## Sports day ki seedhiyan

School ki sports day thi. Ground ke kinare ek seedhiyon wali stand thi, jis par 20 seedhiyan (steps) thi, aur har seedhi 0.2 m ooncha thi. Sports teacher Ms. Pathan ne ek chhota sa match rakha: do students ek ek karke usi stair par upar chadhenge. Pehla student **aaram se chalkar** chadha. Doosra student **daudkar** chadha.

Dono ka mass same tha, 50 kg, aur dono ek hi seedhi ke upar tak pahunche. Par chalne wala student 20 second mein pahuncha, aur daudne wala sirf 8 second mein. Daudne wala upar pahunchkar zor zor se haanf raha tha, jabki chalne wala bas halka sa saans le raha tha.

Armaan ye sab dekh raha tha. Usne Ms. Pathan se poocha, "Madam, dono ne to ek hi seedhi chadhi, ek hi mass ke saath, ek hi height tak. Phir daudne wala itna zyada thak kyun gaya? Dono mein difference kya hai?"

Ms. Pathan muskuraayin. "Bahut achha sawaal, Armaan. Dono ne **kaam** to same kiya, par ek ne wo kaam **jaldi** kiya. Is jaldi ke naap ko hi physics mein **power** kehte hain."

Chalo, dekhte hain.

## Part A: Power kya hai

### Do students, same kaam

Pehle dekhte hain ki sach mein dono ne same kaam kiya ya nahi. Topic 1 mein dekha tha ki kaam = force × displacement. Upar chadhte waqt gravity ke khilaaf kaam hota hai. Topic 5 se hum jaante hain ki height h par PE = m g h, aur kaam jitna kiya utni hi PE badhi.

```
                              ________   <- upar (4 m)
                         ____│
                    ____│          Har seedhi: 0.2 m ooncha
               ____│               20 seedhiyan
          ____│                    Kul height = 20 × 0.2 m = 4 m
     ____│
 ___│
 :  neeche (0 m)
```

> **Figure 7.1** – 20 seedhiyon wali stair, har seedhi 0.2 m ooncha. Chalne wala aur daudne wala dono student 4 m ooncha chadhte hain, isliye dono ki PE (aur dono ka kiya hua kaam) barabar badhta hai.

Kul height = 20 × 0.2 m = **4 m**.

Kaam (gravity ke khilaaf) = m g h = 50 kg × 10 m/s² × 4 m = **2000 J**

Jaanch: weight = 50 kg × 10 m/s² = 500 N, aur 500 N × 4 m = 2000 J ✅

Dono students ne **2000 J** ka kaam kiya. Ab fark sirf time ka hai.

### Power ki definition

**Power = ek second mein kiya gaya kaam.** Yaani kaam ko time se bhaag do:

**P = W ÷ t**

Yahan W kaam (J mein), t time (s mein), aur P power hai. Topic 3 mein dekha tha ki energy aur kaam ka unit same (joule) hai, kyunki kaam energy ka transfer hi hai. Isliye power ko **energy transfer hone ki dar** bhi keh sakte hain: P = E ÷ t.

### Watt: power ka unit

P = W ÷ t mein W joule mein aur t second mein hai, to power ka unit hua **joule per second (J/s)**. Isi ko **watt (W)** kehte hain.

**1 W = 1 J/s**

Yaani 1 watt ki power ka matlab: har second mein 1 joule kaam (ya 1 joule energy ka transfer). Topic 3 ke teaser ko yaad karo: **100 W ka bulb har second 100 J** electrical energy use karta hai (aur roshni aur heat mein badalta hai).

Bade kaam ke liye bade units:
- **1 kilowatt (kW) = 1000 W**
- **1 megawatt (MW) = 1,000,000 W = 10⁶ W** (sirf naam: power station jaisi bade pamaane ki cheezon ke liye)

Power ek **scalar** hai: ismein sirf size aati hai, disha nahi.

### Kahani ka hisaab

**Chalne wala student:** W = 2000 J, t = 20 s

P = W ÷ t = 2000 J ÷ 20 s = **100 W**

**Daudne wala student:** W = 2000 J, t = 8 s

P = W ÷ t = 2000 J ÷ 8 s = **250 W**

Jaanch: 100 W × 20 s = 2000 J ✅ aur 250 W × 8 s = 2000 J ✅

```
 Kaam: 2000 J (dono ka)

 Chalne wala  :##################### 20 s  ->  P = 100 W
 Daudne wala  :######## 8 s          ->  P = 250 W

 Same kaam, kam time  =  zyada power
```

> **Figure 7.2** – Dono students ne 2000 J kaam kiya. Chalne wale ko 20 s lage (100 W), daudne wale ko sirf 8 s (250 W). Time kam, to power zyada.

Ms. Pathan ne Armaan ko samjhaya, "Dekha? Kaam same (2000 J) hai, par daudne wale ki power 250 W thi, chalne wale ki sirf 100 W. Daudne wale ka body ek second mein 2.5 guna zyada kaam kar raha tha. Isiliye dil tez dhadka, saans phooli, aur wo zyada thaka. Jaldi kaam karne ke liye body ko tez raftaar se energy deni padti hai."

Armaan ne kaha, "To power zyada hone ka matlab ye nahi ki kaam zyada hua, balki ye ki kaam **tez** hua!" Bilkul sahi.

## Part B: Average power

Asli zindagi mein hum hamesha ek hi raftaar se kaam nahi karte. Beech beech mein ruk bhi jaate hain. Aise mein poore time ka **average power** nikaalte hain:

**Average power = total kaam ÷ total time**

Maan lo Armaan ne bhi (mass 50 kg) wahi seedhi chadhi: pehle 8 s daudkar poori seedhi chadhi (2000 J), phir upar 12 s rukkar saans li (kaam 0 J).

Total kaam = 2000 J, total time = 8 s + 12 s = 20 s

Average power = 2000 J ÷ 20 s = **100 W**

Jaanch: 100 W × 20 s = 2000 J ✅

Dhyan do: daudte waqt uski power 250 W thi, par poore 20 s ka average sirf 100 W nikla. Average power batati hai ki poore time mein kaam ki ausat dar kya rahi.

### Horsepower (hp)

Engine aur motor ki power aksar **horsepower (hp)** mein likhi hoti hai. Ye ek purana unit hai:

**1 hp ≈ 746 W** (lagbhag; hum isse approximate maante hain)

Isliye hp se W mein jaane ke liye 746 se **guna** karo, aur W se hp mein jaane ke liye 746 se **bhaag** do.

## Part D: Kilowatt-hour: bijli ka commercial unit

### Joule bahut chhota hai

100 W ka bulb sirf 1 ghante mein = 100 W × 3600 s = 360,000 J energy leta hai. Ghar ke saare appliances ke mahine bhar ka hisaab joule mein likhein to 8 se 9 digit ke bade bade numbers aayenge. Isliye bijli company energy ke liye ek bada unit use karti hai: **kilowatt-hour (kWh)**.

### Definition

**1 kWh = wo energy jo 1 kW ki power wala appliance 1 ghante tak chalne par use karta hai.**

1 kWh = 1 kW × 1 h = 1000 W × 3600 s = **3,600,000 J = 3.6 × 10⁶ J**

Jaanch: 1000 × 3600 = 3,600,000 ✅

Bijli ke bill par jise **"1 unit"** kehte hain, wo asal mein **1 kWh** hi hai.

**Zaroori baat:** kWh **power ka nahi, energy ka unit** hai. Kyunki power × time = energy. Watt power ka unit hai, kilowatt-hour energy ka.

### Energy nikaalne ka formula

**E = P × t**

(Ye P = W ÷ t ka hi ulta roop hai.) Agar P kW mein ho aur t ghanton (h) mein, to E seedha **kWh** mein aati hai. Agar P W mein aur t s mein ho, to E **J** mein aati hai.

## Part E: Solved examples

### Example 1: Power nikalna (W aur t se)

Ek motor 4800 J ka kaam 12 s mein karti hai. Power?

P = W ÷ t = 4800 J ÷ 12 s = **400 W**

Jaanch: 400 W × 12 s = 4800 J ✅

### Example 2: Kaam nikalna (P aur t se)

Ek 40 W ki tube light 45 s jalti hai. Kitni energy use hui?

W = P × t = 40 W × 45 s = **1800 J**

Jaanch: 1800 J ÷ 45 s = 40 W ✅

### Example 3: Time nikalna (W aur P se)

Ek 250 W ki motor 6000 J ka kaam karne mein kitna time legi?

t = W ÷ P = 6000 J ÷ 250 W = **24 s**

Jaanch: 250 W × 24 s = 6000 J ✅

### Example 4: Pump ki power

Ek pump 600 kg paani ko 10 m ooncha 60 s mein uthata hai. Power? (g = 10 m/s²)

Kaam = m g h = 600 kg × 10 m/s² × 10 m = **60,000 J**

P = W ÷ t = 60,000 J ÷ 60 s = **1000 W = 1 kW**

Jaanch: 1000 W × 60 s = 60,000 J ✅

### Example 5: Crane ki power

Ek crane 1000 kg ke load ko 10 m ooncha 20 s mein uthati hai. Power? (g = 10 m/s²)

Kaam = m g h = 1000 kg × 10 m/s² × 10 m = **100,000 J**

P = 100,000 J ÷ 20 s = **5000 W = 5 kW**

Jaanch: 5000 W × 20 s = 100,000 J ✅

### Example 6: hp se W aur W se hp

(a) 2 hp ki motor ki power W mein?

P = 2 × 746 W = **1492 W** (lagbhag, ≈ 1.49 kW)

(b) 3730 W ka engine kitne hp ka hua?

P = 3730 W ÷ 746 W/hp = **5 hp**

Jaanch: 5 × 746 = 3730 ✅ aur 1492 ÷ 746 = 2 ✅

### Example 7: Ek ladka seedhi daudkar chadhta hai

35 kg ka ek ladka 4 m oonchi seedhi 5 s mein daudkar chadhta hai. Power? (g = 10 m/s²)

Kaam = m g h = 35 kg × 10 m/s² × 4 m = **1400 J**

P = 1400 J ÷ 5 s = **280 W**

Jaanch: 280 W × 5 s = 1400 J ✅

### Example 8: 60 kg ka insaan 6 m ooncha building par

60 kg ka ek insaan 6 m ooncha building par 30 s mein chadhta hai. Power? (g = 10 m/s²)

Kaam = 60 kg × 10 m/s² × 6 m = **3600 J**

P = 3600 J ÷ 30 s = **120 W**

Jaanch: 120 W × 30 s = 3600 J ✅

### Example 9: Appliances ki energy kWh mein

Teen appliances ka hisaab. Dhyan rakho: **P ko kW mein badlo, t ghanton mein rakho.**

| Appliance | Power | Time | Energy E = P × t |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pankha | 60 W = 0.06 kW | 10 h | 0.06 kW × 10 h = **0.6 kWh** |
| AC | 1500 W = 1.5 kW | 8 h | 1.5 kW × 8 h = **12 kWh** |
| Tube light | 40 W = 0.04 kW | 5 h | 0.04 kW × 5 h = **0.2 kWh** |

Jaanch (W aur h se): 60 × 10 = 600 Wh = 0.6 kWh ✅, 1500 × 8 = 12,000 Wh = 12 kWh ✅, 40 × 5 = 200 Wh = 0.2 kWh ✅

### Example 10: Energy ka kharch (tariff)

**Maan lo ₹6 per unit** bijli ka rate hai (ye sirf hisaab ke liye maana hua rate hai, asli rate jagah aur samay ke hisaab se badalta hai). Example 9 ke teeno appliances ka kharch:

| Appliance | Energy | Kharch (× ₹6) |
|---|---|---|
| Pankha | 0.6 kWh | 0.6 × 6 = **₹3.60** |
| AC | 12 kWh | 12 × 6 = **₹72** |
| Tube light | 0.2 kWh | 0.2 × 6 = **₹1.20** |

Jaanch: 3.60 + 72 + 1.20 = ₹76.80, aur total energy 0.6 + 12 + 0.2 = 12.8 kWh, 12.8 × 6 = ₹76.80 ✅

Dekha: AC ka 8 ghante ka kharch bahut zyada hai, kyunki uski power zyada hai.

### Example 11: Ek chhote ghar ka monthly bill

Ek chhote ghar mein 5 tarah ke appliances hain. Mahina 30 din ka hai. **Maan lo ₹6 per unit.**

Fridge ek compressor se thoda thoda chalta hai, isliye hum maan lete hain ki wo din mein kul 8 ghante asal mein chalta hai.

| Appliance | Power | Roz ka time | Roz ki energy | 30 din ki energy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 tube lights (40 W each) | 80 W | 5 h | 0.08 kW × 5 h = 0.4 kWh | 0.4 × 30 = **12 kWh** |
| 2 pankhe (60 W each) | 120 W | 10 h | 0.12 kW × 10 h = 1.2 kWh | 1.2 × 30 = **36 kWh** |
| Fridge | 200 W | 8 h | 0.2 kW × 8 h = 1.6 kWh | 1.6 × 30 = **48 kWh** |
| TV | 100 W | 4 h | 0.1 kW × 4 h = 0.4 kWh | 0.4 × 30 = **12 kWh** |
| Washing machine | 500 W | 0.5 h | 0.5 kW × 0.5 h = 0.25 kWh | 0.25 × 30 = **7.5 kWh** |

Total energy = 12 + 36 + 48 + 12 + 7.5 = **115.5 kWh** = 115.5 units

Kharch = 115.5 × ₹6 = **₹693**

Jaanch: 12 + 36 = 48, 48 + 48 = 96, 96 + 12 = 108, 108 + 7.5 = 115.5 ✅, aur 115.5 × 6 = 693 ✅

### Example 12: Bulb badal kar LED lagaya

Ek 100 W bulb ko 15 W ki LED se badal diya. Dono roz 5 ghante, 30 din chalte hain. Kitni energy bachi? (₹6 per unit maanke bachat bhi nikalo.)

100 W bulb: E = 0.1 kW × 5 h × 30 = **15 kWh**
15 W LED: E = 0.015 kW × 5 h × 30 = **2.25 kWh**

Bachat = 15 kWh − 2.25 kWh = **12.75 kWh**

Paison mein bachat = 12.75 × ₹6 = **₹76.50**

Jaanch: 0.1 × 5 × 30 = 15 ✅, 0.015 × 5 × 30 = 2.25 ✅, 15 − 2.25 = 12.75 ✅, 12.75 × 6 = 76.5 ✅

### Example 13: kWh ko joule mein

2 kWh kitne joule ke barabar hai?

2 kWh = 2 × 3,600,000 J = **7,200,000 J = 7.2 × 10⁶ J**

Jaanch: 7,200,000 ÷ 3,600,000 = 2 ✅

## Part F: Halka extra (aage ki class ki jhalak)

*Ye hissa sirf ek jhalak hai, abhi isse yaad karne ki zaroorat nahi.*

### Power = force × speed (sirf ek line)

Jab koi force F cheez ko constant speed v se kheeche, to power = F × v hoti hai. Iska poora derivation **aage ki class mein poori tarah** aayega. Abhi ek chhota example sirf application ke roop mein:

Ek lift ki motor 500 kg ke load ko constant speed 2 m/s se upar uthati hai. Load ka weight = m g = 500 kg × 10 m/s² = 5000 N, to motor ko itna force lagana padta hai.

P = F × v = 5000 N × 2 m/s = **10,000 W = 10 kW**

Jaanch (Part A wale tareeke se): 1 s mein load 2 m upar jaata hai, kaam = 5000 N × 2 m = 10,000 J, to P = 10,000 J ÷ 1 s = 10,000 W ✅

### Efficiency: ek simple ratio

Koi bhi machine jitni energy leti hai, wo poori useful kaam mein nahi badalti, kuch heat aur awaaz mein nikal jaati hai (Topic 6 mein friction aur heat dekha tha).

**Efficiency = useful energy (output) ÷ total energy lagayi gayi (input)**, aur ise aksar percent mein likhte hain.

Example: Ek motor 1000 J electrical energy leti hai aur 800 J useful kaam deti hai.

Efficiency = 800 J ÷ 1000 J = 0.8 = **80%**

Jaanch: 80% of 1000 J = 800 J ✅ (baaki 200 J heat aur awaaz mein gayi, aur 800 + 200 = 1000 J, energy conserved ✅)

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**Galti 1: Power, work aur energy ko ek hi samajhna**
Joule (J) kaam aur energy ka unit hai. Watt (W) power ka unit hai. Power kaam ki **dar** hai, kaam khud nahi. 2000 J "kaam" hai, 100 W "power" hai.

**Galti 2: kilowatt-hour (kWh) ko power ka unit maanna**
kW power hai (kaam ki dar). kWh = kW × h = energy hai. Bill par jo "unit" aata hai wo energy (kWh) hai, power nahi.

**Galti 3: Minute aur ghante ko seedha daal dena**
Ek 500 W ki motor 2 minute chali. Agar E = 500 × 2 = 1000 J likha to galat. Sahi: 2 min = 120 s, E = 500 W × 120 s = **60,000 J**.

**Galti 4: W aur kW mein gadbad**
1500 W ke AC ko 8 h chalaya. Agar 1500 × 8 = 12,000 kWh likha to galat (ye 12,000 Wh hai). Sahi: 1.5 kW × 8 h = **12 kWh**.

**Galti 5: "Zyada power matlab zyada kaam"**
Ye zaroori nahi. Zyada power ka matlab hai kaam **tez** hota hai. Dono students ne 2000 J kiya, par daudne wale ki power 250 W thi aur chalne wale ki 100 W. Kaam same, power alag.

**Galti 6: hp aur W ko ulta convert karna**
1 hp ≈ 746 W, isliye hp se W mein jaane ke liye **guna** karo. 2 hp = 2 × 746 = 1492 W. Agar 2 ÷ 746 = 0.00268 W likha to galat: 2 hp ki motor ki power itni chhoti ho hi nahi sakti.

## Quick Recap

- **Power = kaam ÷ time = energy transfer ÷ time.** P = W ÷ t.
- **Unit:** watt, 1 W = 1 J/s. 1 kW = 1000 W; 1 MW = 10⁶ W. **1 hp ≈ 746 W.**
- Power **scalar** hai. Same kaam kam time mein ho to power zyada hoti hai.
- **Average power = total kaam ÷ total time.**
- **E = P × t.** P kW mein aur t ghanton mein ho to E kWh mein aati hai.
- **1 kWh = 1000 W × 3600 s = 3.6 × 10⁶ J.** Bill par 1 unit = 1 kWh. kWh **energy** ka unit hai.
- Jaldi kaam karne ke liye zyada power chahiye, par kaam ki matra power se nahi, force aur displacement se aati hai.
- Halka extra: P = F × v (aage ki class mein poori tarah); efficiency = output ÷ input.

## Practice Questions

1. A girl of mass 40 kg climbs a hill of height 5 m in 10 s. Find the work done against gravity and her power. Take g = 10 m/s².

2. A motor of 750 W runs for 4 minutes. How much energy does it use, in joules?

3. A 300 W heater has to supply 1200 J of energy. How much time will it take?

4. A water pump is rated 3 hp. What is its power in watts? (Take 1 hp = 746 W.)

5. A 2 kW heater runs for 3 hours every day. Find the energy used per day in kWh. If one unit costs ₹6 (assumed), what is the cost per day?

6. In a classroom there are 10 tube lights of 40 W each. They stay on for 6 hours. Find the energy used in kWh.

7. Machine A lifts a 100 kg load by 5 m in 10 s. Machine B lifts the same load by the same height in 5 s. Find the power of each machine. Take g = 10 m/s².

8. A motor takes 2000 J of electrical energy and gives 1500 J of useful work. Find its efficiency in percent and the energy lost as heat and sound.

9. State whether each statement is true or false, with a reason.
   (a) A kilowatt-hour is a unit of power.
   (b) A machine with more power always does more work.
   (c) A 2 kW appliance running for 30 minutes uses 1 kWh of energy.

10. Two students carry the same bag up the same staircase. One walks and the other runs. Explain in your own words which one does more work and which one has more power.

### Answers

1. Kaam = m g h = 40 kg × 10 m/s² × 5 m = **2000 J**. Power = 2000 J ÷ 10 s = **200 W**.
   Jaanch: 200 W × 10 s = 2000 J ✅

2. 4 min = 4 × 60 = 240 s. E = P × t = 750 W × 240 s = **180,000 J** (1.8 × 10⁵ J).
   Jaanch: 180,000 J ÷ 240 s = 750 W ✅

3. t = W ÷ P = 1200 J ÷ 300 W = **4 s**.
   Jaanch: 300 W × 4 s = 1200 J ✅

4. P = 3 × 746 W = **2238 W** (lagbhag, ≈ 2.24 kW).
   Jaanch: 2238 ÷ 746 = 3 ✅

5. E = 2 kW × 3 h = **6 kWh**. Cost = 6 × ₹6 = **₹36 per day**.
   Jaanch: 6 kWh ÷ 3 h = 2 kW ✅, 36 ÷ 6 = 6 units ✅

6. Total power = 10 × 40 W = 400 W = 0.4 kW. E = 0.4 kW × 6 h = **2.4 kWh**.
   Jaanch: 400 W × 6 h = 2400 Wh = 2.4 kWh ✅

7. Kaam (dono ka) = 100 kg × 10 m/s² × 5 m = 5000 J.
   Machine A: P = 5000 J ÷ 10 s = **500 W**. Machine B: P = 5000 J ÷ 5 s = **1000 W**.
   Machine B ki power double hai, kyunki kaam same par time aadha.
   Jaanch: 500 × 10 = 5000 ✅, 1000 × 5 = 5000 ✅

8. Efficiency = 1500 J ÷ 2000 J = 0.75 = **75%**. Lost energy = 2000 J − 1500 J = **500 J**.
   Jaanch: 75% of 2000 J = 1500 J ✅, 1500 + 500 = 2000 ✅

9. (a) **False** ❌. kWh energy ka unit hai (kW × h). Power ka unit W ya kW hai.
   (b) **False** ❌. Zyada power ka matlab kaam tez hota hai. Kaam ki matra time par bhi nirbhar hai: kam power wali machine zyada der chale to zyada kaam kar sakti hai.
   (c) **True** ✅. E = 2 kW × 0.5 h = 1 kWh (30 min = 0.5 h).

10. Dono ne same bag same height tak uthaya, to m g h wahi hai, isliye **kaam dono ka barabar** hai. Par daudne wale ne wo kaam kam time mein kiya, to uski **power zyada** hai (P = W ÷ t). Isiliye daudne wala zyada thakta hai.

| 2. Positive, negative aur zero work | Force aur displacement ki disha ke hisaab se kaam positive, negative ya zero hota hai | Disha ka rishta kaam ka sign tay karta hai |
| 3. Energy aur uske roop | Energy kaam karne ki kshamta hai; unit joule (kaam wala hi); energy ke alag alag roop (mechanical, heat, light, sound, electrical, etc.) | Energy ek roop se doosre mein badalti hai |
| 4. Kinetic energy | Chalti cheez ki energy; KE = ½ m v²; kaam aur KE ka rishta | Speed doguni to KE chaar guni |
| 5. Potential energy | Position ya height ki wajah se stored energy; PE = m g h | Jitna ooncha, utni zyada PE |
| 6. Conservation of energy | Free fall mein KE + PE constant rehta hai; friction energy ko heat mein badal deta hai | Energy na banti hai na khatam hoti hai, sirf badalti hai |
| 7. Power | P = W ÷ t, unit watt (1 W = 1 J/s); average power; kWh (1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J, bill ka 1 unit); E = P × t | Power kaam ki raftaar hai, kaam ki matra nahi |

### Key definitions aur units

| Quantity | Matlab | SI unit |
|---|---|---|
| Work | Force × force ki disha mein displacement | joule (J) |
| Joule | 1 N force se 1 m displacement mein kiya gaya kaam | J = N m |
| Energy | Kaam karne ki kshamta | joule (J) |
| Kinetic energy (KE) | Gati ki wajah se energy | joule (J) |
| Potential energy (PE) | Position ya height ki wajah se stored energy | joule (J) |
| Power | Kaam (ya energy transfer) ki dar | watt (W = J/s) |
| Watt | 1 joule har second | W |
| Kilowatt-hour (kWh) | 1 kW ki power ke 1 ghante mein kharch energy (bill ka 1 unit) | 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J |
| Horsepower (hp) | Power ka purana unit | 1 hp ≈ 746 W |

### Formulae

| Kya | Formula |
|---|---|
| Work | W = F × s |
| Kinetic energy | KE = ½ m v² |
| Potential energy (height h par) | PE = m g h |
| Conservation of energy (free fall) | KE + PE = constant |
| Work-energy relation | W = ΔKE (kaam = KE mein badlav) |
| Power | P = W ÷ t |
| Energy (power aur time se) | E = P × t |
| kWh aur joule | 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J |
| hp aur watt | 1 hp ≈ 746 W |

### Kaam ke teen cases

| Case | Force aur displacement ki disha | Kaam | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Positive work | Force aur displacement ek hi disha mein | W > 0 (positive) | Cart ko aage dhakelna jab wo aage jaye |
| Negative work | Force displacement ke ulti disha mein | W < 0 (negative) | Friction se chalti cheez ka rukna |
| Zero work | Displacement 0 hai, ya force displacement ke 90° par hai | W = 0 | Deewar ko dhakelna jab wo na hile; bag leke seedha chalna |
