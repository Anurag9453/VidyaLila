# Topic 1: Work (Kaam) Kya Hai?

Pichhle chapters mein humne dekha ki force kya hota hai, force se cheezein kaise hilti hain, ruki hui cheez kaise chalti hai, aur dabaav tatha fluids mein force kya kamaal karta hai. Ab ek naya sawal: jab force kisi cheez ko **sach mein hila deta hai**, to usne kitna **kaam** kiya? Aur wo kaam kitni **jaldi** hua? Is chapter mein hum "kaam" (work), "urja" (energy) aur "shakti" (power) ko samjhenge. Shuruaat sabse pehle shabd, **work**, se.

---

## Building site par Mr. Gowda aur Varsha

Ek nayi building ka kaam chal raha tha. Mason **Mr. Gowda** deewar par plaster kar rahe the. Neeche, sand ke bore par baithi thi unki padosi ki beti **Varsha**. Kal uska science ka test tha, to wo kitaab kholkar poori jaan se padh rahi thi: maathe par pasina, aankhein kitaab par, dimaag puri tarah thaka hua.

Saamne ek mazdoor bhaiya lakdi ki seedhi (ladder) se **eenton ko upar** le ja rahe the. Ek ek eent uthate, seedhi chadhte, upar pahunchate, aur phir neeche aate. Unki saans phool rahi thi.

Varsha ne thak kar kitaab band ki aur boli, "Gowda uncle, aaj to hum dono ne kitni mehnat ki! Bhaiya ne eenton ka kaam kiya, maine padhai ka kaam kiya. Dono ne bahut kaam kiya na?"

Mr. Gowda muskuraye. "Roz ki zindagi mein tumhari baat sahi hai, beta. Lekin **physics mein** in dono mein se sirf **ek ne kaam kiya hai**."

Varsha hairaan: "Sirf ek ne? Maine to itna dimaag lagaya!" Kisne kiya aur kisne nahi, aur kyun? Is kahani ko hum topic ke beech mein number ke saath solve karenge.

## Part A: Roz ka "kaam" aur physics ka "work"

Hindi mein "kaam" bahut badi cheez hai: padhna, office jaana, thakna, sochna, ghar saaf karna, sab kaam hai. Physics ka **work** ek chhota aur **bilkul saaf** shabd hai. Physics mein work tabhi hota hai jab **dono** baatein sach hon:

> **Shart 1:** cheez par **force** lag raha ho.
> **Shart 2:** cheez **force ki disha mein hile** (uska displacement ho).

Agar ek bhi shart toot jaye, to physics ke hisaab se **work = 0**.

Isliye mehnat (thakaan) aur work alag cheezein hain. Thakaan hamare sharir ke andar ki baat hai; work cheez ke hilne ki baat hai.

Ek chhota sa sketch dekho: ek log dabba dhakel raha hai.

```
        F (dhakka) ──────►
      ┌────────────┐
  o   │   dabba    │ ──────► s (dabba itna khisaka)
 /│\──┤            │
 / \  └────────────┘
 ═══════════════════════════════════════  farsh
      ◄─────────── s ───────────►
```

> **Figure 1.1** – Dabba ko force F se dhakela gaya aur wo usi disha mein s doori tak khisaka. Force bhi hai aur displacement bhi hai (force ki disha mein): to work hua.

Ab ek dusra sketch: ek aadmi deewar ko poori taakat se dhakel raha hai.

```
        F ──────►
  o   ╔═════════╗
 /│\──╢  deewar ║    deewar hili hi nahi: s = 0
 / \  ╚═════════╝
 ═══════════════════
```

> **Figure 1.2** – Aadmi deewar ko zor se dhakel raha hai (force hai), lekin deewar hilti nahi (displacement s = 0). Isliye physics mein work = 0.

## Part B: Work ka formula aur joule

Jab force aur displacement **ek hi disha mein** hon, to:

> **Work = Force × Displacement (force ki disha mein)**
>
> **W = F × s**

Yahan:
- **W** = work (joule, J)
- **F** = force (newton, N)
- **s** = displacement force ki disha mein (metre, m)

Is formula ko teen tarah se padh sakte ho:
- W = F × s
- F = W ÷ s
- s = W ÷ F

### Joule (J): work ka SI unit

Force ka unit newton (N) hai, doori ka unit metre (m). To work ka unit hua N × m, jise hum ek naam dete hain: **joule (J)**.

> **1 J = 1 N × 1 m = 1 N m**
>
> **1 joule wo work hai jo tab hota hai jab 1 N ka force kisi cheez ko apni hi disha mein 1 m hila de.**

Yaad rakho: **J aur N m ek hi unit hain**, bas ek ka naam alag hai.

Ek andaaza: ek chhoti si seb (apple) ka weight lagbhag 1 N hota hai. Use seedha 1 m upar uthane mein lagbhag **1 J** work hota hai.

### Bade units

| Unit | Matlab | Joule mein |
|---|---|---|
| 1 kilojoule (kJ) | 1000 J | 10³ J |
| 1 megajoule (MJ) | 10 lakh J | 10⁶ J |

Jaise 1 km = 1000 m, waise hi 1 kJ = 1000 J. Aur 1 MJ = 1000 kJ.

### Work ek scalar hai

Work mein sirf **kitna** hota hai, wo bataya jata hai: jaise "100 J". "100 J uttar ki taraf" nahi bolte. Isliye **work scalar** hai. (Force aur displacement mein disha hoti hai, par unka gunaa jo work banata hai uska sirf maan bataya jata hai.)

### Mass nahi, force chahiye

Kisi cheez ko seedha upar uthane ke liye jitna force lagana padta hai, wo uske **weight** ke barabar hota hai:

> **Weight = F = m × g**

Is chapter mein hum **g = 10 m/s²** lenge (aasaan hisaab ke liye; asli maan lagbhag 9.8 m/s² hai). Jaise 2 kg ki eent ka weight = 2 kg × 10 m/s² = **20 N**.

Agar cheez ko h metre upar uthayein, to uthane wale ne **gravity ke khilaaf** jo work kiya:

> **W = F × s = (m × g) × h**

(Is formula ka gehra matlab hum aage Topic 5 mein dekhenge. Abhi ise sirf "weight ke barabar force × uthayi gayi unchai" samjho.)

Ek sketch: bucket kuein se upar kheencha ja raha hai.

```
        rassi
          │  F = m g (upar ki taraf)
          ▲
        ┌─┴─┐   ▲
        │bkt│   │ h (upar uthne ki doori)
        └───┘   ▼
   ════ kuein ═════
```

> **Figure 1.3** – Bucket ko seedhe upar uthane ke liye rassi ka force F = m g (bucket ke weight ke barabar) upar ki taraf lagta hai, aur bucket h doori upar jaata hai: force aur displacement dono upar, to W = m g h.

## Part C: Kab work hua, kab nahi?

Neeche 8 sthitiyan hain. Har ek mein dono shartein check karo.

| Sthiti | Force hai? | Cheez force ki disha mein hili? | Physics work? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Deewar ko zor se dhakelna | Haan | Nahi (s = 0) | **Nahi** (W = 0) |
| 2. Heavy suitcase ko haath mein pakde khade rehna | Haan (upar ki taraf, weight ke barabar) | Nahi (s = 0) | **Nahi** (W = 0) |
| 3. Bag haath mein lekar seedhi sapaat sadak par chalna | Haan (upar ki taraf, bag ko sambhalne wala) | Nahi, bag aage chala jabki force upar tha | **Is force se work nahi** (poori baat Topic 2 mein) |
| 4. Kursi par baithkar padhai karna | Koi aisa force nahi jo kuch hilaye | Nahi | **Nahi** (W = 0) |
| 5. Mazdoor ka bhaari saaman lekar seedhiyan chadhna | Haan (upar ki taraf) | Haan (upar uthta gaya) | **Haan**: gravity ke khilaaf work |
| 6. Bucket ko kuein se upar kheenchna | Haan (rassi ka force upar) | Haan (bucket upar gaya) | **Haan**: work = m g h |
| 7. Cart ko rassi se kheench kar aage le jaana | Haan (aage) | Haan (aage) | **Haan**: work = F × s |
| 8. Patthar ka zameen par phisalte hue ruk jaana | Haan (friction ka force) | Patthar friction ke saath bhi hila, par friction ulte rukh mein tha | **Friction ka work hota hai** (disha ki baat Topic 2 mein) |

Teen cheezein dhyan se dekho:
- Situation 1 aur 2: **bahut zyada force**, par **hila nahi** to work zero.
- Situation 3 aur 8: yahan force aur motion ki **disha alag** hai. Abhi sirf itna jaano ki force ki disha mein cheez nahi hili: poori baat agle topic mein.
- Situation 4: padhne mein dimaag thakta hai, par physics mein kisi cheez par force lagakar use hilaya hi nahi, isliye work nahi.

## Part D: Solved examples

Is part mein **g = 10 m/s²** hai.

### Example 1: W nikalna (F aur s diye hain)

Ek bachcha 20 N ka force lagakar cart ko force ki disha mein 5 m khisakata hai. Work kitna hua?

- F = 20 N, s = 5 m
- W = F × s = 20 N × 5 m = **100 N m = 100 J**

**Jaanch:** 100 J ÷ 5 m = 20 N ✅

### Example 2: F nikalna

Ek force ne 6 m ki doori tak cheez ko apni disha mein hilakar 150 J work kiya. Force kitna tha?

- W = 150 J, s = 6 m
- F = W ÷ s = 150 J ÷ 6 m = 150 N m ÷ 6 m = **25 N**

**Jaanch:** 25 N × 6 m = 150 J ✅

### Example 3: s nikalna

40 N ka force ek cheez ko apni disha mein hilakar 240 J work karta hai. Cheez kitni door gayi?

- W = 240 J, F = 40 N
- s = W ÷ F = 240 J ÷ 40 N = 240 N m ÷ 40 N = **6 m**

**Jaanch:** 40 N × 6 m = 240 J ✅

### Example 4: 5 kg ka bag 2 m upar uthana

Pehle force (weight) nikalo, phir work.

- m = 5 kg, g = 10 m/s², h = 2 m
- F = m × g = 5 kg × 10 m/s² = 50 N
- W = F × h = 50 N × 2 m = **100 J**

**Jaanch:** W = m g h = 5 kg × 10 m/s² × 2 m = 100 J ✅

### Example 5: Trolley dhakelna

30 N ke force se trolley ko seedhi line mein 12 m dhakela gaya. Work kitna?

- F = 30 N, s = 12 m
- W = 30 N × 12 m = **360 J**

**Jaanch:** 360 J ÷ 12 m = 30 N ✅

### Example 6: 55 kg ka ladka 24 seedhiyan chadhta hai

Har seedhi (step) 0.25 m ooncha hai. Gravity ke khilaaf work kitna hua?

- Kul unchai h = 24 × 0.25 m = 6 m
- Weight F = m g = 55 kg × 10 m/s² = 550 N
- W = F × h = 550 N × 6 m = **3300 J = 3.3 kJ**

**Jaanch:** 55 kg × 10 m/s² × 6 m = 3300 J, aur 3300 J ÷ 1000 = 3.3 kJ ✅

### Example 7: Cart kheenchna aur kJ mein badalna

Ek aadmi cart ko rassi se 80 N ke force se force ki disha mein 25 m kheenchta hai.

- W = F × s = 80 N × 25 m = **2000 J**
- kJ mein: 2000 J ÷ 1000 = **2 kJ**

Kuch aur conversions:
- 2.5 kJ = 2.5 × 1000 = **2500 J**
- 4500 J = 4500 ÷ 1000 = **4.5 kJ**
- 3 MJ = 3 × 1 000 000 = **3 000 000 J**

**Jaanch:** 2 kJ × 1000 = 2000 J ✅, 2500 J ÷ 1000 = 2.5 kJ ✅, 4.5 kJ × 1000 = 4500 J ✅

### Example 8: Friction ke khilaaf box ghaseetna

Ek box ko farsh par 5 m ghaseetna hai. Friction ka force 40 N hai. Box ko ek-saman chalane ke liye hamein friction ke barabar force lagana padta hai, yaani 40 N.

- F = 40 N, s = 5 m
- W = F × s = 40 N × 5 m = **200 J**

**Jaanch:** 200 J ÷ 5 m = 40 N ✅

### Example 9: Do logon ke work ki tulna

Person A 6 kg ki cheez ko 5 m upar uthata hai. Person B 15 kg ki cheez ko 1.5 m upar uthata hai. Kisne zyada work kiya?

- A: F = 6 × 10 = 60 N, W = 60 N × 5 m = **300 J**
- B: F = 15 × 10 = 150 N, W = 150 N × 1.5 m = **225 J**
- 300 J > 225 J, to **A ne zyada work kiya**, jabki B ka force zyada tha (150 N > 60 N).

**Jaanch:** A: 6 × 10 × 5 = 300 J ✅. B: 15 × 10 × 1.5 = 225 J ✅

Seekh: work force aur displacement **dono** par depend karta hai. Sirf bhaari force se zyada work nahi hota.

### Example 10: Jab cheez hili hi nahi

Ek aadmi deewar ko 500 N ke force se dhakelta hai. Deewar 0 m hilti hai. Work kitna?

- F = 500 N, s = 0 m
- W = 500 N × 0 m = **0 J**

**Jaanch:** koi bhi number × 0 = 0 ✅

## Kahani ka jawab: kisne work kiya?

Ab Mr. Gowda aur Varsha ki baat solve karte hain. Maan lo ek eent ka mass 2 kg hai aur seedhi ke upar tak ki unchai 3 m hai.

**Mazdoor bhaiya (1 eent):**
- F = m g = 2 kg × 10 m/s² = 20 N (upar ki taraf)
- s = 3 m (upar ki taraf, force ki disha mein)
- W = F × s = 20 N × 3 m = **60 J**

**Jaanch:** 2 × 10 × 3 = 60 J ✅ aur 60 J ÷ 3 m = 20 N ✅

Agar bhaiya ek saath 10 eent le jaate: mass = 10 × 2 kg = 20 kg, F = 20 × 10 = 200 N, W = 200 N × 3 m = **600 J**. Yaani 10 guna eenten, 10 guna work (60 × 10 = 600 ✅).

**Varsha (padhai):**
- Kitaab ya body ko force ki disha mein koi displacement nahi hua. Dimaag ki mehnat se koi cheez force lagakar hili nahi.
- W = **0 J**

Mr. Gowda ne kaha, "Dekho Varsha, bhaiya ne eent par upar ka force lagaya aur eent upar gayi: dono shartein poori. Tumne bahut mehnat ki, thakaan hui, par physics ke hisaab se kisi cheez par force lagakar use hilaya nahi. Isliye tumhara work 0 J hai, par tumhari padhai ki value kam nahi hui!" Varsha hans padi, "To mehnat aur work alag-alag cheezein hain!"

## Part E: Work ka asar: speed badalti hai

Jab work kisi cheez par hota hai, to us cheez mein kuch badalta zaroor hai. Agar farsh par rakha trolley ruka hua tha aur aapne use dhakela, to wo chalne laga: **uski speed badh gayi**. Yaani work ke baad cheez ki speed badal sakti hai. Ye badlav kitna hota hai aur uska kya naam hai, ye hum aage dekhenge. Aap soch rakhna: **work ka asar kinetic energy par** hota hai, ye Topic 4 mein samjhenge.

---

## Aam galtiyan (bachke rehna)

**1. "Bhaari cheez ko pakde rehna bhi work hai."**
Suitcase ko haath mein pakad kar khade rehne mein force (weight ke barabar) hai par displacement 0 hai. W = F × 0 = 0 J. Haath thakta hai, par physics mein work nahi hota. (Sharir ke andar muscles andar se kaam karte rehte hain, par suitcase par work nahi hota.)

**2. "Jitni zyada mehnat, utna zyada work."**
Mehnat aur work alag hain. Varsha ne bahut mehnat ki par W = 0. Work ke liye cheez ka **hilna** zaroori hai.

**3. Force ki jagah mass use karna.**
5 kg ko 2 m uthane par W = 5 × 2 = 10 J likhna **galat** hai. Pehle force: F = 5 × 10 = 50 N. Phir W = 50 N × 2 m = **100 J**.

**4. Rasta nahi, force ki disha mein displacement.**
Agar koi cheez ghoom-phirkar wapas wahi aa jaye to uska displacement 0 hai. Is par aage ki class mein aur baat karenge; yahan yaad rakho ki W = F × s mein s force ki disha mein hilne ki doori hai, rasta kitna bhi lamba ho.

**5. N m aur J ko alag samajhna.**
1 N m = 1 J. Ye ek hi unit ke do naam hain. "200 N m" aur "200 J" ek hi cheez hain.

**6. g bhool jana.**
Cheez uthane ka force m g hota hai. "m × h" karke chhod dena galat hai. Hamesha pehle weight = m g nikalo.

---

## Quick Recap

- **Physics mein work** tab hota hai jab (1) cheez par force lage aur (2) cheez force ki disha mein hile.
- **W = F × s**, F newton mein, s metre mein, W joule mein.
- **1 J = 1 N × 1 m = 1 N m**. 1 kJ = 1000 J, 1 MJ = 10 lakh J.
- **Work scalar hai**: sirf maan hota hai.
- Cheez ko h unchai tak uthane mein gravity ke khilaaf **W = m g h** (g = 10 m/s²).
- Cheez nahi hili ya force ki disha mein nahi hili to is force ka work = 0 (poori baat agle topic mein).
- Thakaan aur mehnat kaam (work) ke barabar nahi hain.
- Work ka ek asar cheez ki speed badalna hai (Topic 4 mein).
- **Kahani:** eent (2 kg, 3 m): 60 J. Padhai: 0 J.

## Practice Questions

1. A force of 15 N moves a box through 8 m in the direction of the force. How much work is done?

2. A bucket of water of mass 12 kg is pulled up a well through 5 m. Find the work done against gravity (g = 10 m/s²).

3. A force does 900 J of work in moving a cart 15 m in its own direction. Find the force.

4. A force of 250 N does 2 kJ of work. How far does the object move in the direction of the force?

5. A girl of mass 40 kg climbs 15 steps, each 0.2 m high. How much work does she do against gravity? Give the answer in J and in kJ.

6. State True or False:
   (a) Holding a heavy bag without moving means work is being done on the bag.
   (b) 1 J = 1 N m.
   (c) Work is a vector quantity.
   (d) If you push a wall with 100 N and the wall does not move, the work done on the wall is 0 J.

7. A box of mass 8 kg is dragged 6 m along a floor against a friction force of 25 N. Find the work done by the person (the mass is given only to confuse you).

8. Person A lifts a 3 kg brick through 4 m. Person B lifts a 6 kg brick through 2 m. Who does more work, or is it equal? Show the working.

9. Convert: (a) 0.75 kJ into J, (b) 5400 J into kJ, (c) 2 MJ into kJ.

10. A labourer carries 12 bricks of 2 kg each up a ladder, 3 m high. Find the work he does against gravity. How much work (in joules) does a student sitting and studying for the same time do on any object?

### Answers

1. W = F × s = 15 N × 8 m = **120 J**.
   Jaanch: 120 J ÷ 8 m = 15 N ✅

2. F = m g = 12 kg × 10 m/s² = 120 N. W = 120 N × 5 m = **600 J**.
   Jaanch: 12 × 10 × 5 = 600 J ✅

3. F = W ÷ s = 900 J ÷ 15 m = **60 N**.
   Jaanch: 60 N × 15 m = 900 J ✅

4. W = 2 kJ = 2000 J. s = W ÷ F = 2000 J ÷ 250 N = **8 m**.
   Jaanch: 250 N × 8 m = 2000 J ✅

5. Unchai h = 15 × 0.2 m = 3 m. F = 40 kg × 10 m/s² = 400 N. W = 400 N × 3 m = **1200 J = 1.2 kJ**.
   Jaanch: 40 × 10 × 3 = 1200 J, 1200 ÷ 1000 = 1.2 kJ ✅

6. (a) **False** ❌. Force hai par displacement 0, to W = 0.
   (b) **True** ✅. Newton × metre = joule.
   (c) **False** ❌. Work scalar hai.
   (d) **True** ✅. Deewar hili nahi, s = 0, to W = 100 N × 0 m = 0 J.

7. Friction ke khilaaf force F = 25 N (mass 8 kg ki zaroorat nahi). W = 25 N × 6 m = **150 J**.
   Jaanch: 150 J ÷ 6 m = 25 N ✅

8. A: F = 3 × 10 = 30 N, W = 30 N × 4 m = 120 J. B: F = 6 × 10 = 60 N, W = 60 N × 2 m = 120 J. Dono ka work **barabar** hai (120 J = 120 J): B ka force double hai par doori aadhi hai.
   Jaanch: 3 × 10 × 4 = 120 ✅, 6 × 10 × 2 = 120 ✅

9. (a) 0.75 kJ = 0.75 × 1000 = **750 J**. (b) 5400 J = 5400 ÷ 1000 = **5.4 kJ**. (c) 2 MJ = 2 × 1000 = **2000 kJ**.
   Jaanch: 750 ÷ 1000 = 0.75 ✅, 5.4 × 1000 = 5400 ✅, 2000 kJ × 1000 = 2 000 000 J = 2 MJ ✅

10. Mass = 12 × 2 kg = 24 kg. F = 24 × 10 = 240 N. W = 240 N × 3 m = **720 J**. Padhai karte student ne kisi cheez ko force ki disha mein hilaya nahi, to uska work **0 J** hai.
    Jaanch: 12 × 20 N × 3 m = 720 J ✅
